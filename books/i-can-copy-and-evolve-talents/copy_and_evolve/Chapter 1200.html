His footsteps echoed through corridors that had widened, the cramped catacombs giving way to vast chambers carved from obsidian and bone. The walls here weren’t just stone—they were organic. Pulsing. Breathing.

The passage opened into a chamber so vast the ceiling disappeared into darkness. The floor was a lattice of bone—ancient ribs the size of buildings, vertebrae that formed platforms, skulls that served as pillars. Everything calcified. Everything consumed. Everything digested.

At the center, perched on a throne of calcified agony, sat Maw’Thoraxis.

Eight meters of chitinous nightmare. Humanoid in the way a mantis is humanoid—recognizably bipedal but fundamentally wrong. Black armor plates overlapping like living metal. Gray-white underbelly segmented and deceptively vulnerable. The elongated blade-skull with no eyes, only those massive jaws that split four ways, revealing spiral rows of serrated teeth.

Its clawed fingers—six on each hand, each claw as long and curved as a stiletto—drummed against the throne’s arm. The sound echoed wrong, arriving before it was made.

Northern felt the weight of its attention. Not sight. Not hearing. Pure predatory awareness.

NEW.

The sensation slammed into Northern’s mind. Not words—compressed meaning transmitted through the domain itself. The same method the Broodmother had used. But this one... the voice cut sharper, gleamed with intelligence.

’I can’t be surprised that these monsters are talking. They lived for hundreds of years and probably consumed millions of humans...’

[You’re using Echo Summoning]

The Matriarch’s mandibles clicked—awkward, searching for speech.

"Fight... kill... protect... Master..."

IMPOSSIBLE. A BELIAL. HERE? IN THIS... DOMAIN?
