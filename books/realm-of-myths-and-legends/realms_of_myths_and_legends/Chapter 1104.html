"Huh?" Peaceful Chaos felt the world around him slow down as his eyes landed on Izroth's right hand.

From his perspective, that hand was moving so painfully slow that he had enough time to launch tens of attacks while it was still in motion. However, the strange thing was the foreboding sense that crept into Peaceful Chaos' mind from his experience as a core player. His body warned him to instinctively retreat. But, he pushed this feeling down as he believed that it was only a matter of time until he broke through whatever defensive measures Izroth put into place.

Besides, how could Izroth ever hit him with such laughable agility? Plus, Peaceful Chaos figured that worst case scenario, he could easily take another attack from Izroth.

Azalea was someone who consumed a Five Cycles Pill and possessed the Sight and Hearing Enhancement effect from the Queen of Phantoms. "What is this terrifying feeling?" Azalea muttered with furrowed brows.

The sound of the thunder alone was so oppressive that it was challenging for one to even breathe properly, let alone move around freely.

Izroth set his sights on the skies above. He had witnessed this phenomenon before back during the second team's selection at the Sky Palace.

'It's not as overbearing as it was back then, but this pressure... Without a doubt, it's the heavenly lightning. How troublesome.'

The heavenly lightning appeared when one was going too far against the laws of the world, such as when Sage merged two of the Main Upper Eight Domains.

In this case, due to the Hygrin's Great Seal used by Peaceful Chaos and Izroth's Unbounded One skill, he had obtained a power that was far beyond what someone below the firmament should possess. As a result, the world itself in the form of the heavenly lightning moved to eliminate him.

'It's a shame, but with my current strength, it's not possible to face a force of nature like the heavenly lightning head-on. Still, it's a good thing I held back my strength. I thought something like this might

occur.'

Izroth restrained the power of his strike to around 10% because he knew the consequences of those who used too great of power without the means to control it. Nevertheless, it appeared that it was not enough to stop the heavenly lightning from showing up.

Izroth did not know much about the heavenly lightning in RML; however, if it was anything like that existed in the Seven Realms, then relying on raw physical strength or skills alone was useless. One might be able to buy some time that way, but it would only be a matter of time until the heavenly lightning struck down with enough power to wipe them out.

'It is not yet time for me to face that place. First, I must build enough strength so that I'm not merely a powerless target when I set foot there. Although it's not at the same level as the heavenly lightning back at the Sky Palace, it seems I've still managed to gather quite the audience.'

Thanks to his Energy Vision Sense, Izroth could sense the gazes of numerous existences. Without a doubt, these existences were all in the legendary realm or belonged to some

powerful organization.

The heavenly lightning in the skies started to grow restless. One could tell that it was getting ready to strike down at any moment!

Then, without warning, something unusual happened. The heavenly lightning began to calm down as the dark clouds started to recede. The overwhelming pressure also subsided.

'Fortunately, there's quite a simple way out this time.'

In the palm of Izroth's hand was an exquisite ring.

[System Alert: You have unequipped the <<Ring of the Ancient Colossus>>!

The moment Izroth removed the Ring of the Ancient Colossus, his Might stat disappeared and the unfathomable power that surged through him vanished without a trace. With that power, Izroth was confident that no one below the firmament would be his match. However, in the end, it was a power that did not belong to him and would only lead to his demise. He was not so short-sighted as to be blinded by momentary power.

Not long after Izroth unequipped the Ring of the Ancient Colossus, the heavenly lightning crackled one last time before fading away. The dark clouds that lingered over the Kifa Karsts also dispersed as things returned to normal.

As for the gazes, they left one by one after the heavenly lightning went away; however, a few of them seemed to linger around. But, Izroth chose to ignore these gazes since getting rid of them would be more trouble than it was worth.

Izroth did not know precisely how Hygrin's Great Seal worked, but if it could bypass his Unbounded One skill, he figured that it was not a genuine sealing magic item. Of course, he tried to activate his Flickering Steps and any other movement skills to add in his massive speed decrease when Peaceful Chaos first activated the item. But, seeing as how it was all useless, Izroth deduced that the stats could not be changed for the item's effect duration. If so, there was a simple solution to avoiding the wrath of the heavenly lightning, and that was to remove the source of the Might stat, the Ring of the Ancient Colossus. Fortunately, the Might stat was tied to the Ring of the Ancient Colossus. If it had instead been something like his attack stat, Izroth would not have been able to use such a method to fend off the heavenly lightning.
