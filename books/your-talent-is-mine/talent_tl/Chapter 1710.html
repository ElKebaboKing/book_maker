Chapter 1709: Cultivation Breakthrough, Initial Stage Legendary Lord Ninth Step!

Source Sea.
The end of the Spacetime Staircase.
Ye Tian was the first to walk out, followed by one Eternal Realm expert after another stepping into the Source Sea.
Boom!!!!
The auras of the Eternal Realm experts layered together, causing the origin of the Source Sea to tremble.
Soon, 3,000 Eternal Realm experts officially arrived at the Source Sea.
At this moment, they observed their surroundings and realized that this was truly another Eternal Sea, and its location was somewhere they had never heard of.
This was a new Eternal Sea.
Immediately.
These Eternal Realm experts became excited. If they were stationed here, many rare resources might be born here in the future, and they would have a chance to obtain some. Once the Source Sea developed, it would be much easier to obtain resources here than in the Boundless Sea.
Spacetime Territory.
Ye Tian brought the 3,000 Eternal Realm experts here, immediately drawing the attention of the Lord Alliance.
Especially Lord Bing Han and the others; seeing so many Eternal Realm experts at once, with the lowest cultivation being Ninth Step Immortal Stage Lords, they were instantly dumbfounded.
Shortly after Ye Tian settled the guard army, Lord Bing Han and four other Legendary Lords came to find him.
Blood Bone Territory.
Lord Bing Han stood before Ye Tian, inquiring about the guard army.
"Lord Wujin, those Eternal Realm experts you brought aren't from the Source Sea or the Spacetime Territory, are they?" Lord Bing Han asked.
"Indeed, they are not!" Ye Tian did not hide it and said directly, "They come from the Boundless Sea and are now a legion under my command."
"What? A legion under your command!!!"
Lord Bing Han and the others could not believe it. So many Legendary Lords and Ninth Step Immortal Stage Lords—this power was many times greater than that of the Source Sea. Dispatching any few Legendary Lords would be enough to sweep through the Source Sea.
It was unknown how many ages it would take for the Source Sea to give birth to so many Legendary Lords; it would definitely be an incredibly long time.
Yet now, so many Legendary Lords and Ninth Step Immortal Stage Lords were actually a legion under Ye Tian's command.
How could they dare to imagine this!
"Lord Wujin, how do you have so many Legendary Lord subordinates?" Lord Sheng Feng asked curiously.
Ye Tian smiled and said, "I joined the Zhenwu Sacred Land of the Boundless Sea, which is the mysterious power behind the Zhenwu Legend Tower. After joining, I became a Guard Commander of the Zhenwu Sacred Land, possessing the qualification to recruit Eternal Realm experts. These Eternal Realm experts voluntarily joined my guard army. Furthermore, the Source Sea has already become my guarding area, so the threat of the Qin Family is no longer an issue."
Learning that the threat of the Qin Family was gone, Lord Bing Han and the others breathed a sigh of relief.
After chatting for a while, Lord Bing Han requested, "Lord Wujin, I want to go to the Boundless Sea. Since you could go to the Boundless Sea so quickly and even bring these Eternal Realm experts over, there must be a shortcut. I wish to use this passage, and I hope Lord Wujin will grant this!"
The other Legendary Lords also spoke up; they also wanted to go to the Boundless Sea.
"Do you truly want to go to the Boundless Sea?" Ye Tian asked.
"Yes!" Lord Bing Han smiled and said, "Lord Wujin, we are only sending clones. Even if they truly perish, it is no big deal. If we don't go to the Boundless Sea, it will be difficult for us to continue increasing our strength, and it might even be hard to step into the Initial Stage Lord level."
Ye Tian nodded and agreed, saying, "Very well. I will let you pass through the Spacetime Staircase, and you will reach the Boundless Sea very quickly. However, after you enter the Boundless Sea, you must not reveal the spacetime coordinates or the situation of the Source Sea. Do you understand?"
"We understand!"
Lord Bing Han and the other four Legendary Lords responded one after another.
Subsequently, Ye Tian informed them of the location of the Spacetime Staircase. The five Legendary Lords each sent a clone onto the Spacetime Staircase, and they soon disappeared, having already entered the Boundless Sea.
……
In the vast void area, the Legendary Lords of the Qin Family were still on their way. They had already passed through the Dark Barrier and were heading toward the Purple Aurora Domain. However, crossing the Purple Aurora Domain would take a long time, so the Qin Family members could not reach the Source Sea in the short term.
Ye Tian no longer worried about the threat of the Qin Family. Now that he didn't have to worry about anything, he began to cultivate diligently.
Not only that, but he also dispatched a duplicated body to stay in the Zhenwu Sacred Land long-term.
In the Zhenwu Sacred Land, he could browse many precious inheritance classics. Although the cultivation paths of the Boundless Sea and the Source Sea were somewhat different, at the level of Legendary Lords, they were mostly the same. Much of the knowledge here was a supreme treasure to him.
Thus, Ye Tian continued to learn, which would be of great benefit to his future cultivation.
Additionally.
Ye Tian could use the precious lands of the Zhenwu Sacred Land to enhance his will and improve the fusion of his will, body, and Innate Eternal Rules. Combined with the assistance of the Hunyuan Token, Ye Tian's cultivation speed at the Initial Stage level was many times faster than before.
At this rate, it would not take an excessively long time for Ye Tian to step into the Perfected Stage Legendary Lord level.
One Eternal Year after another passed.
Unknowingly, a long period of time went by...
On this day, Ye Tian finally broke through, advancing from the Seventh Step of the Initial Stage Legendary Lord to the Eighth Step. His entire body had generated 80% Eternal Original Power, and his strength was significantly greater than before.
Furthermore, Ye Tian further refined his Legend-grade Eternal Techniques.
In the past, he had obtained too few inheritances, and his insights into Legend-grade Eternal Techniques relied entirely on his own understanding, making some aspects imperfect. But now, having obtained a large number of inheritances from the Zhenwu Sacred Land, he naturally had a brand-new understanding of Legend-grade Eternal Techniques, thus perfecting them step by step.
Not only that, but he also frequently wandered around various parts of the Boundless Sea with the goal of duplicating a large number of talents.
In the Boundless Sea, there were still quite a few Immortal Ancestral Talents. If he duplicated them in large quantities, they could be fused into Legendary Ancestral Talents.
Thus, as time passed, Ye Tian gathered many Legendary Ancestral Talents. This was a massive opportunity for him, and his own potential would be greatly enhanced.
As for whether there were Legend-grade Ancestral Talents above Legendary Ancestral Talents, Ye Tian was not quite sure.
Because he had never seen a Legend-grade Ancestral Talent, and even those Legendary Lords did not possess Legendary Ancestral Talents!
And as for what kind of talents Legendary Stage Lords possessed, he was also not very clear. He had not seen a single Legendary Stage Lord to this day; such Legendary Stage Lords were extremely mysterious and would not easily show themselves.
In the blink of an eye, another long period of time passed. Ye Tian's cultivation advanced further; his will, body, and Innate Eternal Rules had already fused by 90%. This could almost be considered the peak of an Initial Stage Lord.
If he took another step and his body perfectly fused with his will and Innate Eternal Rules, that would be 100% fusion, which meant stepping into the Perfected Stage Legendary Lord realm.
However, there was a massive shackle between the Initial Stage and the Perfected Stage, which many Initial Stage Legendary Lords found difficult to cross.
But as a Heaven-tier Sacred Son, Ye Tian's treatment in the Zhenwu Sacred Land was excellent. He could enter many precious lands and even consult some Half-step Legendary Stage Lords.
Additionally, the Zhenwu Sacred Land had places specifically designed to help break this shackle, though the price to pay was high. However, he could use them.
Therefore, for Ye Tian, breaking the shackle and advancing to a Perfected Stage Legendary Lord was truly not difficult at all.
Of course, Ye Tian had not yet reached the point of charging toward the Perfected Stage Legendary Lord level, so there was no need to rush for now.
On this day.
In the void area outside the Spacetime Territory of the Source Sea, a large number of Legendary Lords appeared.
They were the Legendary Lords of the Qin Family!
The Qin Family had arrived!
