Chapter 1731: Lord Wushuang Becomes a Legendary Realm Lord!

Zhenwu Sacred Ground.
Legendary Realm Lord Longgu, Legendary Realm Lord Tengjiu, Legendary Realm Lord Huanghe, Legendary Realm Lord Hemu, and Legendary Realm Lord Zhenguang.
These five Legendary Realm Lords appeared to protect Lord Wushuang.
Although the Zhenwu Sacred Ground had more than five Legendary Realm Lords, it was not necessary for all of them to come and protect her.
Moreover, it was not certain that any Legendary Realm Lords would come to cause trouble while Lord Wushuang was impacting the Legendary Realm Lord rank.
"Wushuang, are you ready?"
Lord Longgu asked.
"I am ready!" Lord Wushuang said.
"Then let us begin!"
Lord Longgu and the others entered the Void Sea with Lord Wushuang.
The Void Sea represented space, while the River of Time represented time.
In reality, the Void Sea and the River of Time existed in the same place, just in two different regions. Some people group the Void Sea and the River of Time together, referring to them as the Spacetime River, but in the eyes of Legendary Realm Lords, the two were still separate.
However, at the end of the River of Time and the end of the Void Sea, the two converged. If a Peak Legendary Realm Lord wanted to impact the Eternal Controller rank, they would need to go to the end of the River of Time and the Void Sea to do so.
For example, the treasure land Ye Tian previously entered deep within the Time Eternal Gold vein was a treasure land formed where the Void Sea and the River of Time converged.
A Half-step Legendary Realm Lord impacting the Legendary Realm Lord rank would inevitably enter either the Void Sea or the River of Time. Since Lord Wushuang controlled space, she would impact the Legendary Realm Lord rank within the Void Sea.
Splash, splash, splash!!!
The five Legendary Realm Lords and Lord Wushuang all entered the Void Sea. Surrounded by endless spatial power, an endless spatial pressure surged toward them. Without the strength of a Legendary Realm Lord or mastery over space, even a Half-step Legendary Realm Lord would find it difficult to stay here for long, just as a Half-step Legendary Realm Lord could not stay in the River of Time for very long.
At this time.
The five Legendary Realm Lords protected this area, allowing Lord Wushuang to impact her breakthrough with peace of mind.
"Wushuang, break through with confidence. We will not let other Legendary Realm Lords disturb you," Lord Longgu said.
"Mhm!"
With five Legendary Realm Lords protecting her, Lord Wushuang was incredibly steady and did not worry about anything.
Soon, she began to impact the Legendary Realm Lord rank.
By controlling space, she could also control the power here, using the power of the Void Sea to break the shackles in the dark, thereby becoming a Spatial Incarnation and completely merging herself with space.
Only in this way could she ascend to the rank of Legendary Realm Lord.
Without entering space, it was impossible to break the shackles or ascend to the rank of Legendary Realm Lord.
Boom!!!!
Lord Wushuang took control of the space here, and an infinite amount of spatial power flooded into her body. Her physical body gradually merged with space, impacting the shackles in the dark.
The five Legendary Realm Lords were not worried for the moment, because even if an enemy came to interrupt the breakthrough, they would not come now. Interrupting now would be meaningless; she could simply try to break through again next time.
The best time to truly disrupt it was the moment Lord Wushuang completely merged with the spatial power and became a Spatial Incarnation. If she were interrupted then, Lord Wushuang would suffer a backlash from the Void Sea. While she might not perish, once she suffered a backlash, it would be difficult for her to impact the Legendary Realm Lord rank again in the future.
Time slowly passed, and Lord Wushuang drew closer and closer to becoming a Spatial Incarnation. The aura on her body also grew stronger and stronger.
At this time, the five Legendary Realm Lords began to grow solemn, constantly scanning the surroundings for fear that a large number of Legendary Realm Lords would invade.
After another period of time, Lord Wushuang reached the most critical moment.
Boom!
Lord Wushuang instantly absorbed an infinite amount of space. Her body spatialized, and the aura of a Legendary Realm Lord began to permeate from her.
Right at this moment, an arrow pierced through the air, ignoring the power of the Void Sea.
"It is Lord Guixing's Guixing Arrow!"
The expressions of the five Legendary Realm Lords changed.
Just as the Guixing Arrow was about to ignore everything and strike Lord Wushuang's true body, Lord Zhenguang sacrificed an Eternal Treasure—the Wuguang Shield!
With a bang!
The Wuguang Shield deflected the Guixing Arrow, stopping Lord Guixing's sneak attack.
In the next instant, Lord Wushuang completely broke through, becoming a Legendary Realm Lord as a powerful aura spread out.
Whoosh!
The Guixing Arrow disappeared, and Lord Guixing's voice drifted over: "Humph, consider yourselves lucky!"
The five Legendary Realm Lords did not give chase, as it would be very difficult to actually kill Lord Guixing.
"Zhenguang, thanks to your Wuguang Shield. If not for its Eternal property of being able to block anything, it would have been truly difficult for us to stop Lord Guixing's Guixing Arrow," Lord Longgu said.
Lord Zhenguang said, "Back then, Lord Guixing used the Guixing Arrow to shoot and kill a Legendary Realm Lord of our Zhenwu Sacred Ground. That is why I went to great lengths to obtain the Wuguang Shield. Otherwise, while we could avoid the Guixing Arrow ourselves, trying to block it would be difficult. Only a Peak Legendary Realm Lord can block the Guixing Arrow."
Peak Legendary Realm!
This was the Peak stage of the Legendary Realm Lord rank. The Zhenwu Sacred Ground did not have such a Legendary Realm Lord, and in the entire Boundless Sea, there was only one such Legendary Realm Lord.
If the Zhenwu Sacred Ground possessed a Peak Legendary Realm Lord, they would be an almost Invincible existence among Legendary Realm Lords. Who would dare provoke the Zhenwu Sacred Ground?
Unfortunately, it was too difficult for a Peak Legendary Realm Lord to be born. In the entire Void Region and the many Eternal Seas, there were not many Peak Legendary Realm Lords.
At this time, Lord Wushuang had also basically stabilized her realm initially.
"Congratulations to Lord Wushuang for ascending to the rank of Legendary Realm Lord."
"Congratulations to Lord Wushuang for ascending to the rank of Legendary Realm Lord."
"Congratulations to Lord Wushuang for ascending to the rank of Legendary Realm Lord."
"Hahahaha, our Zhenwu Sacred Ground has gained another Legendary Realm Lord. Our strength has grown again!"
The five Legendary Realm Lords said excitedly.
Lord Wushuang also said gratefully, "Thank you five for your protection. Otherwise, I fear my breakthrough would have been interrupted by Lord Guixing."
"It was only right. Come, let us return and hold a grand banquet for you, so the entire Boundless Sea knows that our Zhenwu Sacred Ground has given birth to a Legendary Realm Lord," Lord Longgu said.
Afterward.
They returned to the Zhenwu Sacred Ground and announced the news.
Immediately, countless members of the Zhenwu Sacred Ground were incredibly excited, while many forces in the Boundless Sea were also shocked.
It had been a very long time since a Legendary Realm Lord had been born in the Boundless Sea, but now one had emerged.
"Lord Wushuang has broken through to the Legendary Realm Lord rank. That was fast!"
After Ye Tian learned of this, he remarked with a sigh.
Once she became a Legendary Realm Lord, Lord Wushuang's status and position in the Zhenwu Sacred Ground were completely different. She was now on equal footing with Lord Longgu and the others, belonging to the highest tier of the Zhenwu Sacred Ground.
"I should also work hard and strive to break through to the Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord rank as soon as possible."
Ye Tian murmured.
