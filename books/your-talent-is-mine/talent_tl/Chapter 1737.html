Chapter 1736: Indestructible Dragon Rock Fire!

Soon, the auction ended.
Ye Tian had already obtained a spot, so he only needed to go back and wait patiently.
He waited for about several dozen days in Earth time; in the Boundless Sea, such a short time was nothing at all.
"A void region near the Jinsha Sea."
Ye Tian looked at the information sent by the Time Merchant Guild and prepared to head to the Jinsha Sea immediately.
The Boundless Sea had a space-time staircase leading to the Jinsha Sea, and the True Void Sacred Ground had one as well.
Thus, Ye Tian quickly reached the Jinsha Sea via the space-time staircase of the True Void Sacred Ground.
Afterward.
He left the Jinsha Sea and shuttled through the void region for a long time before arriving at this Legendary Realm Lord's tomb.
Soon, more and more Transcendence Realm Lords arrived here one after another.
Here, Ye Tian also saw several Transcendence Realm Lords from the True Void Sacred Ground, and they greeted him.
"Endless Lord, you've come too!"
The Transcendence Realm Lords from the True Void Sacred Ground greeted him.
These Transcendence Realm Lords were Jinbei Lord, Huoyan Lord, Yunzhong Lord, and Muyi Lord.
Ye Tian responded to each of the four lords.
Then.
Muyi Lord said, "This ruin is the tomb of the Legendary Realm Lord, Yang Lie Lord. Yang Lie Lord had a bad temper. Before he fell, he likely made his territory extremely dangerous. If Transcendence Realm Lords enter, many will probably die. I suggest we go in a bit later and let them scout the way first."
"I also think we should wait. It won't be easy to take the good things inside. Being the first to rush in doesn't necessarily mean getting benefits; it's very likely you'll lose an avatar," Jinbei Lord said.
"We can go in later."
Ye Tian also agreed.
He wasn't in a hurry; he didn't think other Transcendence Realm Lords could take the Time River Pearl just by being the first to enter.
At this time.
The entrance to the ruin was still sealed by a Legendary Realm Lord from the Time Merchant Guild.
Once almost everyone had arrived, the Legendary Realm Lord from the Time Merchant Guild unsealed it.
"Everyone, I wish you great opportunities in this tomb ruin!"
After saying that, the Legendary Realm Lord vanished into the long river of time and disappeared.
"Go in!"
One by one, the Transcendence Realm Lords rushed into the dark tomb entrance.
This tomb was located in a special void, manifesting only as this entrance. As for what was inside the tomb, they were unclear.
However, many Transcendence Realm Lords were very bold and rushed straight in.
After these Transcendence Realm Lords broke into the tomb, they immediately entered a sea of fire.
"Not good, it's Indestructible Dragon Rock Fire! Damn it, Yang Lie Lord actually collected so much Indestructible Dragon Rock Fire to form an Indestructible Dragon Rock Fire Formation. I can't block it!"
Almost in an instant, this Transcendence Realm Lord was turned to ashes.
Not just this Transcendence Realm Lord, but nearly ninety percent of the first batch of Transcendence Realm Lords who rushed in couldn't withstand it and were instantly turned to ashes. Only a few top-tier Transcendence Realm Lords managed to charge out of the Indestructible Dragon Rock Fire sea.
……
"Someone is coming out!"
Ye Tian said.
A few Transcendence Realm Lords were seen rushing out of the tomb with heavily injured bodies, some still carrying Indestructible Dragon Rock Fire on them.
"Indestructible Dragon Rock Fire?"
Ye Tian recognized this flame; it was one of the most terrifying types of fire in the void region.
Indestructible Dragon Rock Fire could burn even Transcendence Realm Lords to ashes; only Half-step Legendary Realm Lords could directly withstand it.
By now.
Many Transcendence Realm Lords knew the situation inside.
The Indestructible Dragon Rock Fire formed a sea of flames; once they entered the tomb, they would inevitably suffer its burning.
If their body strength wasn't enough, they simply couldn't withstand the burning of the Indestructible Dragon Rock Fire and thus couldn't go deeper into the tomb.
It could be said that they encountered a major problem at the very first trial of entering the tomb.
"It's over. My body isn't strong enough; I can't resist the Indestructible Dragon Rock Fire at all."
"Unless I find an Eternal Supreme Treasure or a special secret treasure that can resist the Indestructible Dragon Rock Fire, I won't be able to cross the sea of Indestructible Dragon Rock Fire."
"I wasted so many Time Coins buying a spot. If I had known, I wouldn't have spent this money in vain."
"The Time Merchant Guild is too black-hearted. They knew we couldn't make it through, yet they still sold us spots."
"The Time Merchant Guild's reputation is good, but you expect them to be kind-hearted? Forget it!"
At this time, many Transcendence Realm Lords chickened out and didn't dare to enter, otherwise, they would lose an avatar.
Crucially, some Domain Realm Legendary Lords had also purchased spots this time, and they were all in despair now. Even many Transcendence Realm Lords who had condensed Legendary Bodies couldn't withstand the Indestructible Dragon Rock Fire; were they going there just to die?
"Not good, the Indestructible Dragon Rock Fire counters my Legendary Body!"
Muyi Lord said with a bitter smile.
"Regardless, since we're here, we have to try," Jinbei Lord said.
At this moment, several Transcendence Realm Lords looked at Ye Tian.
"Endless Lord, do you still insist on going in?"
Yunzhong Lord asked.
"Of course I'm going in!" Ye Tian said. "I'm still very confident in my body strength."
The Transcendence Realm Lords had heard of Ye Tian's deeds and knew he wasn't an ordinary Half-step Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord, so they didn't try to persuade him otherwise.
"Let's go in together!"
Jinbei Lord suggested.
"Alright!"
The five of them rushed into the tomb entrance together.
Whoosh!
The world shifted, and they had already entered the sea of Indestructible Dragon Rock Fire.
Boom!!!!
Endless Indestructible Dragon Rock Fire burned their bodies, and Muyi Lord was the first to be unable to hold on.
"I can't do it, I have to get out, otherwise this avatar will surely die."
After saying that, Muyi Lord fled directly.
Three of the Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords could still hold on, but the most relaxed one was Ye Tian.
Ye Tian had crossed the Grey Mist Fire Sea within the Grey Mist Statue and completed the remodeling of his body particles. The sturdiness of his body particles was terrifying, comparable to the Half-step Legendary Realm.
Therefore, even though the Indestructible Dragon Rock Fire was terrifying, it could do nothing to Ye Tian.
The other three Transcendence Realm Lords struggled to hold on, and seeing Ye Tian so relaxed, they were instantly dumbfounded.
"Endless Lord, are you not afraid of the Indestructible Dragon Rock Fire?"
Yunzhong Lord asked.
"My body is somewhat special, so I'm not afraid of the Indestructible Dragon Rock Fire,"
Ye Tian said with a smile.
Hearing this, the three Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords were instantly filled with envy.
Immediately.
They tried to cross the Indestructible Dragon Rock Fire sea at their fastest speed.
Finally, just as the three Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords were about to give in, they made it through, but each was heavily injured and would likely need to recover for a long time.
Meanwhile, Ye Tian had almost no injuries at all.
