Chapter 1723: Purple Polar Light Beast, Legendary Ancestral Talent Level...

Chapter 1723: Purple Polar Light Beast, Legendary Ancestral Talent Level Time Talent!

Ye Tian continued to comprehend this blood tissue, while his other copy bodies were also in secluded cultivation. For instance, one was comprehending that treasure map, which was helpful for cultivation at the Domain Realm Legendary Lord level.
The following years were very peaceful, with no major events occurring.
Ye Tian remained in secluded cultivation, and the Time-Space Domain added many more Immortal Realm Lords, but such minor matters no longer caught Ye Tian's attention.
100,000 Eternal Years!
500,000 Eternal Years!
One million Eternal Years!
Ten million Eternal Years!
Over such a long period, the living beings in the Source Sea Time-Space Domain had changed generation after generation; only those in the Eternal Realm existed eternally.
Although many beings in the Source Sea had eternal lifespans, as their numbers exploded, various wars and killings still occurred, like one great tribulation after another.
Ye Tian did not stop them, allowing everything to follow the self-development of the Source Sea.
However, the Human Race grew increasingly powerful, their footsteps reaching every corner of the Source Sea and even appearing in countless time-spaces within the Time-Space Domain.
......
"Ten million Eternal Years have passed, and my cultivation has improved significantly," Ye Tian murmured.
Now, his Will Rule Domain was much stronger than before, not far from the Extreme of the Will Rule Domain.
Furthermore, his Will had also improved greatly during these Eternal Years, reaching the late stage of the Fifth-Tier Will.
In terms of the Eternal Dao, Ye Tian's progress was also immense.
He had comprehended ten types of Eternal Dao, all of which he had raised to the Perfected stage to consolidate his foundation.
Additionally, his Gray Mist Eternal Dao had recently reached the Perfected stage as well.
Currently, although Ye Tian had not reached the Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord level, his true strength was not inferior to some powerful Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords.
"Once I reach the Extreme of the Domain Realm Legendary Lord level, I will have to start preparing to charge toward the Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord level," Ye Tian thought to himself.
The True Void Sacred Ground also provided two opportunities for Domain Realm Legendary Lords: one targeted the Will Rule Domain and the other targeted the Eternal Body. However, these two opportunities only increased the chances slightly and could not completely guarantee a successful breakthrough.
But for ordinary forces, such opportunities would not exist at all.
Ye Tian originally intended to cultivate quietly to the Extreme of the Domain Realm Legendary Lord level and then rely on the opportunities given by the True Void Sacred Ground to charge toward the Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord level. Regardless of whether he could succeed, he had to try.
However, on this day, a piece of news reached Ye Tian's ears that piqued his interest.
A special creature had appeared in the Purple Polar Light Domain.
The Purple Polar Light Domain and the Dark Barrier were the two major obstacles on the path from the Source Sea to the Boundless Sea. The Purple Polar Light Domain only made traveling and identifying directions difficult; it was almost devoid of danger and had never contained any living beings.
But this time, an Immortal Realm Lord from the Source Sea had encountered something in the Purple Polar Light Domain, and one of his clones had been killed by a special purple creature.
If it were just once, it might have been a coincidence—another creature wandering into the Purple Polar Light Domain.
But subsequently, a Legendary Lord also encountered that special purple creature in the Purple Polar Light Domain and was similarly killed instantly.
Now, the Purple Polar Light Domain had attracted the attention of many Legendary Lords, and even many Legendary Lords from the Boundless Sea had arrived, including Domain Realm Legendary Lords.
......
Within the Lord Alliance.
Many Immortal Realm Lords and Legendary Lords were present, and Ye Tian had also arrived, sitting at the very top.
"What has been happening in the Purple Polar Light Domain lately?" Ye Tian asked.
At this time, Lord Bing Han said, "Some special purple creatures have appeared in the Purple Polar Light Domain. They are likely a special race that migrated from other regions. Their intelligence is not very high, but their strength is formidable. More importantly, they possess extremely powerful Time abilities that even we Legendary Lords find difficult to resist. For example, this creature possesses a Time Stop ability. One of my clones previously encountered a purple creature and was directly frozen in time before being killed."
"Even Lord Bing Han's clone was killed?" Ye Tian was somewhat surprised.
Lord Bing Han was now an Initial Realm Lord, and his strength was not weak. If he couldn't even resist the opponent's Time ability and was directly frozen in time, it meant the gap in strength between the two sides was enormous.
"Low intelligence, terrifying Time abilities!"
At this moment, Ye Tian thought of a possibility—
Talents!
Those purple creatures likely possessed a Time Talent, and the level of that Time Talent must be extremely high—at least at the Legendary Ancestral Talent level. Otherwise, it could not possibly possess such power.
A Legendary Ancestral Talent level Time Talent, combined with the purple creature's own strength being comparable to a Perfected Realm Legendary Lord or a Domain Realm Legendary Lord, could indeed instantly freeze the time where Lord Bing Han was located.
·········Seeking Flowers··· ·····
"If it really is a Legendary Ancestral Talent level Time Talent, then it would be of immense help to me!" Ye Tian was incredibly excited.
If he copied a large number of Legendary Ancestral Talent level Time Talents, wouldn't he have a chance to fuse them into a Mythic Grade Time Ancestral Talent?
On this day.
Ye Tian dispatched a copy body to the Purple Polar Light Domain.
Ye Tian's speed was very fast, and before long, he arrived at the Purple Polar Light Domain.
......
Within the void region, a stretch of purple formed a horizon; that was the Purple Polar Light Domain, a territory that seemed endless.
*Whoosh!*
Ye Tian entered the Purple Polar Light Domain and immediately felt intense pressure and the erosion of power. Only Eternal Realm Lords above the Immortal Realm could move freely here. If a slightly weaker Immortal Realm being entered, they would be as helpless as an ordinary person.
..... ..... ...
For many living beings, this was undoubtedly a true forbidden land.
Furthermore, as far as the eye could see, everything was a purple light domain; there was nothing else, and no resources whatsoever.
*Swish!*
Ye Tian used his Void Step to constantly shuttle through the area, while his Copy Talent enveloped the surrounding region to search for that purple creature.
Time passed slowly. During this period, Ye Tian did detect other Legendary Lords, but he did not interact with them, choosing instead to avoid them.
After a long time, Ye Tian finally perceived the existence of a special creature.
Race: Purple Polar Light Beast
Cultivation Talent: Eternal Grade
Strength: Immortal Ancestral Talent
Speed: Immortal Ancestral Talent
Defense: Immortal Ancestral Talent
Time: Legendary Ancestral Talent
......
"It really is a Legendary Ancestral Talent level Time Talent!"
Ye Tian immediately copied the opponent's Time Talent.
Soon, the Time Legendary Ancestral Talent was successfully copied.
"Judging by the aura of this Purple Polar Light Beast, it should be equivalent to a top-tier Perfected Realm Legendary Lord. An Initial Realm Legendary Lord is indeed far from being its match," Ye Tian noted secretly.
He did not kill this Purple Polar Light Beast but instead followed it silently, completely concealing his aura and using his Copy Talent to track it.
He wanted to use this Purple Polar Light Beast to find others of its kind, so he wouldn't have to search for them slowly himself.
