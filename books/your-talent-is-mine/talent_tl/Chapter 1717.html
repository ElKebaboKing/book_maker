Chapter 1716: Gray Mist Deceptive Bridge, Gray Mist Eternal Dao Entry!

Ye Tian and the others were not very clear about what was happening outside the Gray Mist Stone Statue.
At this moment, the group quickly arrived at the Gray Mist Bridge.
Some Half-step Fabled Lords of the Zhenwu Sacred Land had indeed heard of the Gray Mist Stone Statue, but they had never entered it.
After all, every time the Gray Mist Stone Statue appeared, there were only one hundred slots. This number was far too small; by the time you heard the news, you wouldn't be able to make it in time.
Only those Fabled Lords who had lived through too many long years and experienced multiple appearances of the Gray Mist Stone Statue had certainly been there before.
At this time, many Legendary Lords were quite excited because the Gray Mist Stone Statue was also an incredibly massive opportunity for them. If they were lucky, it would be enough to cause a huge change in their fate.
Swish! Swish! Swish!!!
One by one, the Legendary Lords rushed onto the bridge. They were not afraid of danger; basically, the Legendary Lords who dared to come here had sent clones. Even if they perished, it would only be the loss of a single clone.
Of course, they also put up various defenses. After all, once they perished, it would be equivalent to missing out on the opportunities here.
However.
Soon, these Legendary Lords returned one by one to the entrance of the bridge.
Immediately, they were dumbfounded.
At this time, Wushuang Lord spoke up: "This bridge is called the Gray Mist Deceptive Bridge. It is not something that can be crossed by luck alone. According to rumors, to pass the Gray Mist Deceptive Bridge, one must start from the Innate Eternal Rules. If one can comprehend the Eternal Dao on the Gray Mist Deceptive Bridge, that would be even better. However, the Gray Mist Eternal Dao is extremely difficult to comprehend. Without enough talent, one simply cannot comprehend it. Once you cross the Gray Mist Deceptive Bridge, you can obtain a reward, but as for what that reward is, I am not very clear."
"So that's how it is. Thank you, Wushuang Lord!"
The Legendary Lords expressed their gratitude.
Swish! Swish! Swish!!!
The Half-step Fabled Lords of the Zhenwu Sacred Land rushed onto the Gray Mist Deceptive Bridge one by one and did not come out for a long time. Clearly, the strength of the Half-step Fabled Realm was still powerful enough, and their mastery of the Innate Eternal Rules far surpassed these Domain Realm Legendary Lords and Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords.
Thus, the Gray Mist Deceptive Bridge could not stop these Half-step Fabled Lords at all.
In reality, Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords could basically cross the Gray Mist Deceptive Bridge as well, but Domain Realm Legendary Lords needed a certain amount of time for comprehension before they could pass this Gray Mist Deceptive Bridge.
As for Binghan Lord and Shengfeng Lord, they actually had no possibility of crossing the Gray Mist Deceptive Bridge. That was why Longgu Lord had said giving two slots to them was a waste, but this was Ye Tian's promise.
Furthermore, there were benefits to Binghan Lord and Shengfeng Lord staying here; it was enough for them to comprehend some things, which would be of great help to their strength.
Ye Tian did not pay attention to the progress of the others; he continued to silently comprehend on the Gray Mist Deceptive Bridge.
Time passed, and in the blink of an eye, over a hundred thousand Eternal Years had gone by.
On this day.
"Hahaha!!! Hahaha!"
Ye Tian laughed inwardly. He had finally comprehended the Gray Mist Eternal Dao.
It had to be said that this Eternal Dao was indeed very difficult. Had it not been for his various attributes of Legendary Ancestral Talents, combined with the special environment here, he truly would not have been able to comprehend the Gray Mist Eternal Dao.
The Gray Mist Eternal Dao involved too many Innate Eternal Rules. Its power and potential were many times that of other Eternal Daos, and it could serve as Ye Tian's primary Eternal Dao in the future.
Not only had his Gray Mist Eternal Dao reached the Entry stage, but his Hunyuan Eternal Dao and Wansheng Eternal Dao were also at the Major stage. This harvest alone was already very significant.
Moreover, with the Gray Mist Eternal Dao at the Entry stage, Ye Tian was no longer interfered with by the Gray Mist Deceptive Bridge. He could easily cross it.
Thus, Ye Tian walked upon the Gray Mist Deceptive Bridge. He never returned to the entrance again but kept walking like this. After a long time, he finally reached the end of the Gray Mist Deceptive Bridge.
Taking a step forward, the Gray Mist Deceptive Bridge vanished.
Whoosh!
An object fell into Ye Tian's hand; this was the reward for crossing the Gray Mist Deceptive Bridge.
It was a set of battle armor.
"An Eternal Supreme Treasure? No, it doesn't have the aura of an Eternal Supreme Treasure, yet it possesses the aura of the Gray Mist Eternal Dao. This thing should be an Eternal Secret Treasure that is comparable to an Eternal Supreme Treasure and is compatible with the Gray Mist Eternal Dao."
Ye Tian infused the power of the Gray Mist Eternal Dao into it and quickly refined it.
Whoosh!
The battle armor was donned on Ye Tian's body, fitting him perfectly.
At this moment, Ye Tian felt his strength increase significantly, and his defense had also been amplified many times over.
More importantly, his Gray Mist Eternal Dao also received a boost.
"Gray Mist Battle Armor!"
That was the name of the armor.
From a value perspective, this thing was comparable to an Eternal Supreme Treasure, and for Ye Tian, it was much more precious than an ordinary Eternal Supreme Treasure.
"To think there is such a reward just for crossing the Gray Mist Deceptive Bridge. The rewards are too generous!"
Ye Tian murmured.
No wonder the Zhenwu Sacred Land attached such importance to the Gray Mist Stone Statue, with a Fabled Lord personally coming to guard it. The rewards were indeed very generous, and for Legendary Lords, this place was a heaven-sent opportunity. As long as they crossed the Gray Mist Deceptive Bridge, even if they only obtained one reward, it would be worth it.
It was conceivable that after emerging from the Gray Mist Stone Statue, the strength of the Zhenwu Sacred Land would increase considerably.
Ye Tian continued to fly forward and soon saw the figures of some Legendary Lords.
Among these people were a few Domain Realm Legendary Lords and several Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords, but he did not see any Half-step Fabled Lords. Clearly, the Half-step Fabled Lords had already gone to the next level.
"Endless Lord!"
"Endless Lord!"
"Endless Lord!"
When these Legendary Lords saw Ye Tian, they were immediately shocked.
Ye Tian's cultivation was only at the Perfected Realm Legendary Lord stage, yet he had crossed the Gray Mist Deceptive Bridge so quickly. This shocked them too much.
Even some Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords had spent quite some time to pass the Gray Mist Deceptive Bridge, and currently, there were still some Domain Realm Legendary Lords who had yet to cross it.
One must know that the Domain Realm Legendary Lords qualified to obtain a slot to come to the Gray Mist Stone Statue were not ordinary Domain Realm Legendary Lords; they were truly top-tier existences among them!
Of course, they did not dwell too much on their shock. They still needed to break through here before they could continue forward.
This was a river, a long river composed of gray mist, flowing through this area. It was impossible to bypass; one had to fly across this Gray Mist Long River.
"Lords, what is the situation here?"
Ye Tian consulted them.
At this time, a Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord said: "This is the Gray Mist Long River. One must fly across it. If one fails to fly across, they will fall into the Gray Mist Long River. Unlike the Gray Mist Deceptive Bridge, this place is extremely dangerous. Once you fall into the Gray Mist Long River, even a Domain Realm Legendary Lord must pay a heavy price to break free. Therefore, every attempt comes with a great cost."
Because of this, these Legendary Lords did not dare to attempt it recklessly.
