Chapter 1745: Invading the Ghost Star Pavilion

"The purpose of this operation is to invade the Ghost Star Pavilion," Lord Longgu said.

"Invading the Ghost Star Pavilion... as expected."

Ye Tian had already guessed that the Zhenwu Holy Land was going to move against the Ghost Star Pavilion. After all, Lord Wushuang had made it so obvious; he wasn't a fool and could naturally guess it, though he hadn't looked into it deeply.

At this moment, Lord Longgu continued, "The Ghost Star Pavilion is not strong, with only two Legendary Realm Lords: Lord Guixing and Lord Fanxing. However, behind the Ghost Star Pavilion is an alliance—the Myriad Seas Alliance of the Boundless Sea. The Myriad Seas Alliance has a total of twenty-one Legendary Realm Lords. Compared to the entire Myriad Gods Alliance, our Zhenwu Holy Land is naturally no match. But this time, good news has arrived: internal conflicts have arisen within the Myriad Gods Alliance, making it impossible for them to unite. Furthermore, our Zhenwu Holy Land has given birth to two new Legendary Realm Lords, so we are prepared to launch an attack on the Ghost Star Pavilion. Even if we cannot kill the Ghost Star Pavilion Master, we must make him lose one or two avatars, heavily damaging or even wiping out the Ghost Star Pavilion."

Subsequently, Lord Longgu began to assign tasks.

The Ghost Star Pavilion had many industries, so they had to be struck comprehensively. Additionally, to prevent certain forces from the Myriad Seas Alliance from providing support, people had to be sent to defend.

The task of the Legendary Realm Lords was to hold off the Legendary Realm Lords of the Myriad Seas Alliance and to surround and kill Lord Guixing.

This time, every Legendary Realm Lord of the Zhenwu Holy Land had sent at least one avatar, and some had sent all their avatars except for their main body.

One could only imagine that, counting by the number of bodies of the Legendary Realm Lords, this was almost a massive battle between dozens of Legendary Realm Lords.

Soon.

Ye Tian was also assigned a task.

"Attack the Ghost Star Pavilion headquarters."

This was Ye Tian's task.

At that time, the Legendary Realm Lords of the Ghost Star Pavilion would be hunted by the Legendary Realm Lords of the Zhenwu Holy Land or drawn into the Void Sea or the River of Time to fight. When that happened, the Ghost Star Pavilion headquarters would have no Legendary Realm Lords. The Half-step Legendary Realm Lords of the Zhenwu Holy Land would lead a large number of Transcendence Realm Lords to invade the Ghost Star Pavilion headquarters.

And Ye Tian was one of those Transcendence Realm Lords.

By destroying the Ghost Star Pavilion headquarters and killing the many powerhouses of the Ghost Star Pavilion, even if the two Legendary Realm Lords managed to survive by luck, the Ghost Star Pavilion would effectively be destroyed.

"Lord Longgu, when do we act?" Lord Wumeng asked.

"To prevent information from leaking, we act now," Lord Longgu said.

...

The Ghost Star Pavilion was located on Ghost Star Boundless Island in the Boundless Sea, which was entirely the Ghost Star Pavilion's territory.

Moreover, the Ghost Star Pavilion had branches everywhere in the Boundless Sea.

At this time.

Lord Guixing was in seclusion at the Ghost Star Pavilion headquarters.

Suddenly.

The River of Time surged as a Legendary Realm Lord came through it to attack him.

"Ghost Star Pavilion, activate the array!"

Lord Guixing activated the Ghost Star Pavilion's grand array, and then his body moved, entering the River of Time to clash with an incoming hand.

Boom!!!

The waters of the River of Time stopped for an instant. Then, a figure appeared before Lord Guixing.

"Zhenwu Holy Land, Lord Baiheng," Lord Guixing said coldly.

Although this was a newly promoted Lord, Lord Baiheng had been nearly Invincible when he was at the Half-step Legendary Realm. Even though he had just promoted to a Legendary Realm Lord, his strength was not at all inferior to his own.

Swish! Swish! Swish!

One after another, Legendary Realm Lords appeared, all of them from the Zhenwu Holy Land.

At the same time.

Lord Guixing's expression changed. He discovered that his other avatars were also being targeted.

As a Legendary Realm Lord, he had quite a few avatars—one main body and three major avatars.

Now, all of them were being targeted.

Lord Fanxing was in the same situation; all of his bodies were being watched.

This was clearly not a probe, but a real attack.

"Call for help!"

Lord Guixing immediately requested aid from the Myriad Seas Alliance, stating that the Zhenwu Holy Land was attacking the Ghost Star Pavilion.

As for how many Legendary Realm Lords would come to help, he couldn't be sure, but there would definitely be some.

Otherwise, once the Ghost Star Pavilion was destroyed, the strength of the Myriad Seas Alliance would be weakened, and the other Legendary Realm Lords likely wouldn't sit by and watch that happen.

"What does your Zhenwu Holy Land want to do?"

Lord Guixing was panicked.

There was no other way; surrounded by so many Legendary Realm Lords, he absolutely could not win. One mistake and he would fall.

It would be fine if an avatar fell, but if the main body fell, he would truly be dead.

And right now, his avatars were tied down, and his main body was surrounded by so many Legendary Realm Lords. It was truly dangerous.

The slaughter began!

The support from the Myriad Seas Alliance was also fast, with Legendary Realm Lords coming to help. However, due to internal conflicts, not all the Legendary Realm Lords came to assist, which gave the Zhenwu Holy Land an opportunity.

On the other side.

The Ghost Star Pavilion headquarters was also under attack. Lord Wumeng and other Half-step Legendary Realm Lords led a large number of Transcendence Realm Lords to attack the headquarters.

Legendary Realm Lords had the battlefield of Legendary Realm Lords, while they, these Legendary Lords, had the battlefield of Legendary Lords.

Soon.

Ye Tian also faced a Transcendence Realm Lord. The opponent was just an ordinary Transcendence Realm Lord, and upon clashing, he was directly crushed by Ye Tian.

With Legendary-level Eternal Paths and top-tier Legendary Techniques, Ye Tian directly beat that Transcendence Realm Lord until he had no power to resist, killing him within a few moves.

In the Ghost Star Pavilion headquarters, many Legendary Lords were there in their main bodies, not avatars, because the main body was very safe in the Ghost Star Pavilion. But this time, it was dangerous, and thus many Legendary Lords' main bodies fell, causing their avatars in the Boundless Sea and other Eternal Seas to directly perish.

Ye Tian killed one Legendary Lord of the Ghost Star Pavilion after another. His purpose for entering this place was only one: to find Void Sea Grains.

And the most likely place for the Ghost Star Pavilion to store Void Sea Grains was either within Lord Guixing's Void Sphere or in the treasury.

The Ghost Star Pavilion did not have just one treasury, but many.

Therefore, finding the Void Sea Grains would not be easy.

Abruptly.

Ye Tian noticed a Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord being escorted by several Half-step Legendary Realm Lords, attempting to break through the encirclement.

He noticed that the Eternal Treasure in that Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord's hand was a longsword. The longsword emitted an incredibly sharp aura, as if it could tear through space and pierce into the Void Sea at will.

On the tip of this Eternal Treasure sword, Ye Tian sensed the aura of Void Sea Grains.

"This fellow's Eternal Treasure has a Void Sea Grain fused into it. Who is he to Lord Guixing?"

Ye Tian was surprised.

But soon, through the other Legendary Lords of the Zhenwu Holy Land, he learned the identity of this Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord.

He was a bloodline descendant and direct disciple of Lord Guixing. He possessed the strength of an Invincible Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord within the Ghost Star Pavilion and was named Lord Suixing.

"It seems I don't need to go look in the Ghost Star Pavilion's treasuries. By killing this Lord Suixing and seizing his Eternal Treasure, I can obtain a Void Sea Grain," Ye Tian thought to himself.
