Chapter 1714: A Gray Mist Bridge!

Source Sea, at the Gray Mist Statue.
Ye Tian looked at the Gray Mist Statue before him, his curiosity toward it growing even further.
According to Wushuang Lord, the marks on the Gray Mist Statue were left by an Eternal Controller, and Eternal Controllers could cross timelines and even attack the future across time and space.
It could even be said that an Eternal Controller could appear here at any moment.
"The Eternal Controller must have engaged in a brief clash over this Gray Mist Statue back then, even entering its interior. In the end, they might have obtained what they wanted or failed to find what they needed, and thus abandoned this Gray Mist Statue!" Ye Tian speculated.
Regardless, the fact that this Gray Mist Statue could attract the attention of an Eternal Controller was enough to prove its extraordinary nature. Furthermore, even just the marks of an Eternal Controller were enough for a Legendary Lord to comprehend for countless ages.
"First, I will create a duplicate body to stay here and comprehend the Eternal Controller's attack marks, while another duplicate body enters the Gray Mist Statue's interior!"
Ye Tian decided.
*Whoosh!*
A duplicate body was soon created and began its comprehension there, while the other duplicate body prepared to enter the Gray Mist Statue. This way, even if one duplicate body perished, he could at least continue to create new ones to keep exploring.
Otherwise, if he only had one duplicate body and it perished, Ye Tian would need to re-enter the time and space where the Gray Mist Statue was located.
*Boom!!!!*
Ye Tian deployed various defensive techniques before rushing into the crack of the Gray Mist Statue, entering its interior.
……
*Buzz!!!!*
Violent forces turned into beams of light and blasted toward Ye Tian.
These forces were enough to heavily injure an ordinary Legendary Lord, and if one went deeper, even an ordinary Legendary Lord would perish.
However, when these attacks hit Ye Tian, they only caused ripples and could not cause him any harm.
Ye Tian stepped deeper and deeper into the Gray Mist Statue.
Soon.
Ye Tian saw a bridge. This bridge seemed to be composed entirely of a special energy, stretching across the depths of the endless void.
Ye Tian felt this bridge was a bit eerie, so he prepared to bypass it from another direction.
However, something strange happened.
No matter which direction he traveled, he would be moved to the entrance of the bridge by a mysterious energy.
In other words, he had to cross this bridge to move forward; otherwise, there was no way to continue.
This bridge was a mandatory path!
"It seems I must cross this bridge!"
Ye Tian muttered.
He stepped onto the bridge, and instantly, the bridge became covered in gray mist, obscuring all directions.
Thus, he continued forward, and after walking for an unknown amount of time, he actually returned to the entrance of the bridge.
"Strange, I was clearly walking forward, yet I walked back to the entrance!"
Ye Tian was extremely puzzled.
Next, he tried many times, but it was still the same as before, ending up at the bridge's entrance.
"I'll use countless incarnations to look in every direction."
Immediately, Ye Tian's body split into countless incarnations. These incarnations swarmed onto the bridge, but they were soon annihilated one by one.
The method of finding the way with incarnations simply didn't work; the mysterious power contained in this bridge directly annihilated his incarnations.
"If incarnations don't work, then I'll let the duplicate body investigate time and again. I don't believe I can't cross it!"
Ye Tian thought.
But next, whether he shuttled through space, flew at extreme speeds, or used other means, he couldn't cross the bridge and would always return to the entrance.
Moreover, once he entered the bridge, his perception could not exceed the bridge's range, and his Copy Talent could only sense the talents of living beings, which was useless on this bridge.
After trying many times, Ye Tian stopped this unplanned behavior and began to calm down.
"This bridge being built here must have a purpose. There should be a special way to cross it, and since I haven't mastered that way yet, I can't cross it no matter what?"
Thinking this way, Ye Tian roughly understood and began to look for that method.
Then, he walked onto the bridge and was immediately in the gray mist again...
This time, he didn't continue forward but began to comprehend within the gray mist.
Time passed slowly.
At first, Ye Tian was very confused, but gradually, he gained some insight.
"I understand. This gray mist area has the effect of interfering with rules. Every mist particle contains various Innate Eternal Rules. The endless gray mist is a grand formation created by countless Innate Eternal Rules. Being in this grand formation, one is constantly interfered with. To cross this bridge, one must reach a certain level in their understanding of Innate Eternal Rules and be able to see through the rules here. Only then can one avoid being interfered with by the gray mist area and reach the other end of the bridge."
Ye Tian realized.
In other words, his comprehension of Innate Eternal Rules was not deep enough, making it difficult to break through this bridge.
Then, Ye Tian was overjoyed.
The mist in this gray mist area is constantly churning, which also represents the constant changes of various Innate Eternal Rules. Comprehending the changes of the Innate Eternal Rules here would help Ye Tian understand those Eternal Daos.
In fact, there was also a very powerful Eternal Dao here—the Gray Mist Eternal Dao.
The Gray Mist Eternal Dao involved the Innate Eternal Rules of Space, Time, Darkness, Water, and so on. It was undoubtedly an incredibly powerful Eternal Dao.

Of course, the difficulty of comprehending the Gray Mist Eternal Dao was also immense.
For Ye Tian, using this place to comprehend other Eternal Daos was undoubtedly the best way. He could use this bridge to verify his progress at any time, allowing his Eternal Daos to reach a higher tier.
And once he crossed this bridge, he would likely encounter a true opportunity.
"I'll stay here and comprehend!"
Ye Tian decided.
The main Eternal Daos he comprehended were the Myriad Life Eternal Dao and the Hunyuan Eternal Dao. He wanted to cultivate one of them to the Major stage or even the Perfected stage as soon as possible.
And now, the Hunyuan Eternal Dao had barely reached the Minor stage, and it was still very far from the Major stage.
In the blink of an eye, tens of thousands of Eternal Years passed.
