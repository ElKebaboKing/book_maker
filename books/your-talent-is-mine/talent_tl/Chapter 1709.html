Chapter 1708: Garrison Legion!

Heaven-tier Holy Son level!
Although Ye Tian did not know much about the Zhenwu Sacred Land, he could tell from the Silver Moon Lord's reaction that this was definitely a very high status and identity rank.
At this time, Wushuang Lord spoke again, "Boundless Lord, you can choose from the positions available to Heaven-tier Holy Son level members. There is no rush to choose; you can take your time after you understand the situation of the Zhenwu Sacred Land. You and the Silver Moon Lord should first choose a residence."
"Yes!"
Ye Tian and the Silver Moon Lord replied.
Subsequently, Ye Tian found a mountain range somewhere on Zhenwu Island and built his own residence, where he would cultivate in seclusion from now on.
There were many precious lands in the Zhenwu Sacred Land, such as those for improving will. There were many such places here, and one only needed to pay a very low price to cultivate there. As a Heaven-tier Holy Son level member, Ye Tian could even use those precious lands for free.
In the following period, Ye Tian focused on understanding the Zhenwu Sacred Land and its various positions.
Almost everyone in the Zhenwu Sacred Land was a cultivator above the Eternal Realm. At this stage, their paths were almost fixed, so it was impossible to take a master or anything of the sort. Therefore, the Zhenwu Sacred Land was not primarily composed of disciples but was divided into various positions.
For example, Eternal Realm cultivators who guarded the Zhenwu Archipelago were called Island Patrol Envoys. While the status of an Island Patrol Envoy was low, the status of the Island Patrol Commander who managed them was not.
As a top-tier genius being cultivated at the Heaven-tier Holy Son level, Ye Tian had many positions to choose from. Naturally, he would not choose a low position and wanted to select from the highest ones available to him.
Currently, there were three highest positions Ye Tian could choose from.
Punishment Commander, Garrison Commander, and Zhenwu Island Master!
The Punishment Commander was responsible for punishing and pursuing members of the Zhenwu Sacred Land who committed offenses. It was a very dangerous position and easily offended others.
After all, if some Zhenwu Sacred Land disciples committed a crime and you arrested them, you would offend the high-level members of the Zhenwu Sacred Land behind them. Perhaps one day, you might even offend a Legendary Realm Lord.
Thus, while the position of Punishment Commander seemed high, it was actually a major pitfall.
The Zhenwu Island Master was a leisurely post. There were many Boundless Islands in the Zhenwu Archipelago, and one could choose an island to serve as its master, managing the island's affairs and receiving a certain percentage of the proceeds, with no real danger.
However, the position of master of an ordinary Boundless Island had little value and could not provide many benefits.
As for the Garrison Commander, one needed to guard a certain area. In the garrisoned territory, they were the highest commander and represented the face of the Zhenwu Sacred Land. General forces would not dare to offend a Garrison Commander, but during times of war, the Garrison Commander was almost the most unfortunate position.
Ye Tian thought about it and decided to choose the position of Garrison Commander.
After all, the Punishment Commander was a pitfall, and the Zhenwu Island Master was too leisurely and currently of no help to him. If he chose to be a Garrison Commander, he could apply to garrison the Source Sea, and the Zhenwu Sacred Land would definitely be happy to develop the Source Sea as a growth area.
Once the application was approved, Ye Tian could not only become a Garrison Commander but also completely control the Source Sea. Any good things in the Source Sea would belong to him.
"It just depends on whether the Zhenwu Sacred Land agrees!"
Ye Tian murmured.
Immediately, he submitted the application and waited for the Zhenwu Sacred Land's response.
Time passed slowly, and one day, the Zhenwu Sacred Land replied to Ye Tian.
"It passed!"
Ye Tian was very happy when he saw this message.
Afterward, Ye Tian went to collect the Garrison Commander's seal. This seal was no ordinary seal; although it was not an Eternal Supreme Treasure, it was an Eternal Rare Treasure second only to an Eternal Supreme Treasure. It was even infused with legendary-level power, making it comparable to an Eternal Supreme Treasure to a certain extent.
In addition to this garrison seal, Ye Tian also received some resources and equipment.
Furthermore, he had the right to recruit a legion under his command.
After all, as a Garrison Commander, it would be too embarrassing to be alone.
Therefore, he could recruit some Eternal Realm cultivators as his legion to guard the Source Sea.
Of course, he could also recruit a small number.
"The Source Sea only has a few Legendary Realm Lords, and the number of Eternal Realm cultivators is not very large. So, I can only recruit some Eternal Realm cultivators from the Boundless Sea. At the very least, they must be Immortal Realm Legendary Lords, and the minimum requirement is a Nine-step Immortal Realm Legendary Lord. I'll recruit a batch of Legendary Lords and then return to the Source Sea through the Space-Time Staircase."
Ye Tian planned.
Next, in his capacity as a Zhenwu Sacred Land Garrison Commander, he released recruitment information to the Zhenwu Archipelago. Although the Eternal Realm cultivators who joined the Garrison Legion were not direct members of the Zhenwu Sacred Land, they were considered peripheral members.
For those unable to join the Zhenwu Sacred Land directly, choosing to join a Garrison Legion was a good option.
Many Eternal Realm cultivators immediately came to sign up, but Ye Tian rejected any whose cultivation did not reach the level of a Nine-step Immortal Realm Legendary Lord.
In a short time, Ye Tian recruited many experts above the level of Nine-step Immortal Realm Legendary Lord. There were even over a hundred Legendary Lords willing to join Ye Tian's Garrison Legion, and nine Initial Realm Lords also pledged their allegiance to him.
After a selection process, Ye Tian finally confirmed the roster.
He recruited a total of 3,000 Eternal Realm cultivators and appointed the nine Initial Realm Lords as the Nine Great Garrison Envoys. This was his personal certification, but even so, such a position was very high to many Eternal Realm cultivators in the outside world and could represent the Zhenwu Sacred Land to some extent.
The Garrison Legion was not allowed to enter the Zhenwu Sacred Land, so these 3,000 Eternal Realm experts gathered on a certain island in the Zhenwu Archipelago, waiting for Ye Tian's arrival.
When Ye Tian appeared, the 3,000 Eternal Realm cultivators bowed respectfully and said, "Greetings, Commander!"
Ye Tian nodded in satisfaction and then said, "From today onward, you are the Garrison Legion under my command. The place we are going to guard this time is not in the Boundless Sea, but in a new Eternal Sea. In a moment, we will travel to that Eternal Sea through the Space-Time Staircase. I need each of you to swear an oath not to reveal the location of that Eternal Sea. After all, the location of that Eternal Sea has not yet been leaked, so we should try not to let the outside world know."
"Yes!"
These Eternal Realm cultivators all nodded and began to swear their oaths. Although the binding nature of an oath was not absolute and did not require a great price, having an oath could at least provide some restraint. Moreover, this oath used the Will Origin as the object of the oath; if it were truly violated, the Will Origin would be severely damaged. Even with secret techniques for protection, many Eternal Realm cultivators would not dare to violate it.
After the 3,000 Eternal Realm cultivators had sworn their oaths, Ye Tian led them to the Zhenwu Sacred Land's Space-Time Staircase Hall in the Zhenwu Archipelago.
Here, Ye Tian established a Space-Time Staircase connecting to the Source Sea. This Space-Time Staircase would be protected by the Space-Time Staircase Hall, but a certain fee had to be paid, and the Space-Time Staircase Hall would not allow others to pass through this Space-Time Staircase.
Soon, the construction of the Space-Time Staircase was completed, and Ye Tian led the 3,000 Eternal Realm cultivators onto the Space-Time Staircase.
