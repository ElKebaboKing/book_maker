Under Ethan's strength, the wooden wall burst open like a door ripped off its hinges. In the next second, as the entire wall collapsed outward, it revealed a long cliff behind it.

More than ten meters below the cliff was a rushing river whose depths couldn't even be seen!

It seemed that if any Trainer became trapped in the hallway and tried escaping through the window, this unexpected trap would send them plunging straight into the raging waters below.

Haunter looked confused.

"Huh? How did you know there was a trap here?"

"Because before me," Ethan replied, "there was already a very good example to learn from."

As he spoke, Ethan walked over to the opposite wall and pushed hard. With the turning of a hidden panel, a battle arena concealed behind it was revealed.

The moment he stepped through the hidden doorway, sharp whistling sounds suddenly filled the air as more than a dozen shuriken flew straight toward him at incredible speed!

The ninja stars sliced past him, several barely grazing his ears. The force of the wind they created even stirred his hair!

Thunk! Thunk! Thunk!!

Ethan stood calmly in place. When the surroundings finally fell silent again, he turned around and saw that the shuriken embedded in the hidden door had perfectly outlined the shape of a human figure!

A true master of silhouette throwing. What incredible aim!

At that moment, a woman with a long green ponytail descended from above and landed directly in front of Ethan.

She wore a pink ninja outfit that exposed her thighs, and her entrance was as dramatic as possible.

"Born in darkness, living in darkness—such is the fate of a ninja. Female ninja Aya has arrived!"

Putting aside her flashy entrance, Ethan's very first impression of her was simple:

Seductive. Extremely seductive.

Compared to Janine earlier, she was also noticeably more beautiful.

But dressed like that… wasn't it inconvenient for stealth assassinations?

Could she be the type of ninja who specialized in seduction?

"You managed to defeat Janine and pass through all those traps to reach this place. That's impressive," Aya said while applauding lightly. "And to remain expressionless even in front of my shuriken… you're quite an extraordinary man."

Ethan maintained a cold expression.

Ridiculous. Was he supposed to admit that he simply hadn't reacted in time!?

"After going through all this trouble," Ethan said, "I thought I'd finally get to meet the Gym Leader."

Aya smiled.

"If you want to see my brother, you'll have to defeat me first. But since you already defeated Janine, the number of Pokémon you're allowed to use has now become two!"

As she spoke, she blew a whistle, and a fuzzy Pokémon with fly-like compound eyes leaped beside her.

Pokédex:

"Beep. Venonat, the Insect Pokémon. A Bug and Poison-type Pokémon. Although its appearance may not seem very friendly, it is actually quite gentle. Its radar-like eyes are made up of many tiny eyes, allowing it to never miss any prey, even in complete darkness."

Since the opponent was Venonat…

Ethan's fingers brushed across the Poké Balls at his waist before finally choosing a Pokémon with a favorable type matchup.

"Come out, Charmeleon!"

A flash of blue light appeared, and Charmeleon emerged onto the battlefield.

The moment it appeared and saw Venonat, it raised its head and roared proudly, its eyes blazing with fighting spirit!

As Ethan slowly walked to the Trainer's platform, he carefully examined the battlefield.

The setup here was exactly identical to the arena where he had battled Janine earlier. If he hadn't been certain he had climbed upstairs, he would've thought he was still in the same room!

Once both sides were ready, Ethan launched the first attack immediately.

"Charmeleon, use Flamethrower!"

Charmeleon inhaled deeply, its stomach swelling before it opened its mouth and unleashed a torrent of flames filled with scorching heat straight toward Venonat!

Facing the incoming blaze, Venonat simply didn't possess enough speed to dodge and was forced to endure the attack head-on.

Fire-type attacks were super effective against Bug-types!

Just as Ethan intended to press the advantage again, Aya suddenly shouted,

"Venonat, use Disable!"

Strange waves of light radiated from Venonat's body and struck Charmeleon.

Although the move's name sounded like it immobilized opponents, its actual effect was different—it sealed away the last move the opponent had used!

That meant Charmeleon's Flamethrower, despite being used only once, had immediately been temporarily disabled!

"Good! Venonat, follow up with Stun Spore!"

"Tch." Ethan clicked his tongue. Knowing it couldn't be avoided, he shouted,

"Charmeleon, use Smokescreen!"

Thick black smoke and Stun Spore simultaneously engulfed Charmeleon.

However, in Venonat's eyes, the dense smoke was practically meaningless. Even hidden within the darkness, Charmeleon's position remained perfectly visible thanks to Venonat's radar-like vision.

Aya immediately gave another command.

"Venonat, use Leech Life!"

Venonat rushed into the smoke and opened its mouth, sinking its small fangs viciously into Charmeleon!

Outside the smoke, although Ethan couldn't see what was happening inside, he still shouted,

"Charmeleon, use Dragon Rage!"

Inside the smoke, Charmeleon had just been bitten. Even though it couldn't see clearly either, the moment Venonat released its bite and tried to retreat, Charmeleon grabbed hold of its body and unleashed a shockwave from its mouth directly at Venonat!

Boom!

Hit by Dragon Rage, Venonat was blasted out of the smoke and slammed onto the wooden floor outside.

At the same time, the smoke surrounding Charmeleon was blown away by the impact and quickly dissipated.

"Perfect chance! Charmeleon, use Fire Fang!"

At Ethan's command, flames ignited around Charmeleon's jaws as it charged toward Venonat.

But before it could get very far, its movements suddenly slowed dramatically before its body stiffened completely!

The paralysis had finally taken effect, temporarily preventing it from moving!

Seeing this, Aya hurriedly shouted,

"Now's our chance! Venonat, use Confusion!"

Psychic energy burst forth from Venonat's antennae, but Ethan quickly ordered Charmeleon to counter it with another Dragon Rage, canceling out the attack.

"Looks like we've reached a stalemate," Aya said with a smile.

Without waiting for Ethan's response, she continued,

"Venonat, keep attacking and see how much longer it can endure! Use Leech Life!"

Venonat once again opened its jaws and rushed toward Charmeleon.

But just as it reached close range, Ethan suddenly shouted:

"Charmeleon, use Flamethrower!!"

"What!? Flamethrower!?" Aya froze for a second before her expression suddenly changed drastically. "Oh no! Venonat, dodge quickly!!"

Flames gathered inside Charmeleon's mouth once more before erupting outward, engulfing Venonat completely!

After struggling for a while, Venonat finally collapsed.

Ethan let out a long breath of relief.

"Miss Aya, this was your mistake. I think you got so focused on the battle that you forgot Disable's duration had already ended."

(End of Chapter)
