As the seawater gradually receded, the people looked upon the city now occupied by Tentacool. Whether they wanted to or not, they had no choice but to make a final stand to protect this land.

Once everyone retreated to safer ground, they simultaneously released their Pokémon. While resisting the Tentacool assault, they also began launching counterattacks.

Seeing this, Brock took out a Poké Ball and said to Ethan and the others, "Let's help too!"

"But…" Ash hesitated. "How are we supposed to fight something that huge?"

"Don't think about it too much!" Ethan also released his Pokémon at that moment. "Let's deal with the smaller Tentacool first! Kirlia, Gyarados, Jolteon, Ivysaur, and Wartortle, you're up too!"

At this point, nobody had any other choice. Everyone threw themselves into battle.

Ash released Pikachu, Pidgeotto, Bulbasaur, and Squirtle. Brock sent out Zubat. Only Misty still wanted to try calming the chaos through persuasion.

"It's useless." Seeing Misty about to step forward, Ethan quickly grabbed her. "As long as Tentacruel's anger hasn't subsided, it won't listen to any explanations from humans. We need to calm it down first before we can communicate with it!"

If it had been anyone else, Misty would definitely have argued back. But facing Ethan, she simply did not want to argue with him at all. Her attitude immediately softened, and she obediently nodded.

"Alright then, I'll help too. Go, Staryu, Starmie, and Goldeen!"

As more and more Trainers joined the battle with their Pokémon, the fight officially erupted. Battles broke out across nearly every corner of the city.

With such a massive chaotic conflict, it was obviously impossible to issue precise commands to every Pokémon. People could only let them fight freely, relying on their instincts and prior battle experience.

However, the number of Tentacool was simply overwhelming. Combined with the giant Tentacruel, the human side still failed to gain any real advantage even after fighting for a long time.

Seeing the battle deadlocked while the city suffered increasingly severe destruction, Ethan understood that perhaps it was finally time for him to do something.

After a brief hesitation, he quietly turned to Misty and said, "I'll leave my Pokémon to you for now. I need to leave for a little while."

"Huh?" Misty asked in confusion. "Where are you going?"

Ethan raised a finger to his lips, signaling her to stay quiet. Without another word, he slipped away from the crowd alone and headed behind the television tower.

Only after reaching an unnoticed corner did Ethan crouch down and pull out the Gengar mask from his backpack.

Haunter emerged from his shadow. Clever as always, it had already guessed Ethan's intentions.

"You're really planning to do this?"

Ethan asked back, "What? Are you trying to stop me?"

"How could that be?" Haunter let out its trademark sinister laugh. "I love excitement the most. How could something this interesting happen without me?"

"Good. Then I'll leave my back to you."

Ethan put on the mask. Then he noticed a black raincoat tangled in a nearby bush, apparently washed there from somewhere unknown. He walked over and draped it around himself like a cloak.

Perfect. Completely covered up. Even someone familiar with him would never recognize his identity so easily now.

After preparing everything, he released Zapdos.

The moment Zapdos appeared, it spread its wings and let out a piercing cry. Even after being transferred into a better Poké Ball, the Legendary Pokémon still disliked staying inside such cramped, enclosed spaces.

"Zapdos, this opponent is a real giant, and it's incredibly strong. I'm counting on you."

Zapdos stared at Ethan for several seconds. It did not refuse, but reluctantly nodded.

"Great." Ethan pointed toward the top of the television tower and said, "Then carry me up there first."

!!!

Hearing that command, Zapdos widened its eyes in disbelief.

It let out an irritated cry, clearly warning Ethan not to push his luck! It was not some ordinary bird Pokémon meant for humans to casually ride around!

"Hey now, don't look at me like that." Ethan spread his hands helplessly. "Kirlia isn't here, so I can't use Teleport to get up there. Besides, the view from the top is perfect for directing the battle!"

Zapdos turned its head toward Haunter.

"That won't work either." Ethan quickly shook his head. "Haunter's always beside me. People could easily recognize me through it, so I'll have to trouble you this time."

After saying that, Ethan ignored whether Zapdos agreed or not and directly wrapped his arms around its neck before climbing onto its back.

Zapdos twisted violently, trying to throw Ethan off. However, Ethan held on tightly. No matter how hard Zapdos struggled, it simply could not shake him loose.

"Come on, don't be so stingy." Ethan softly tried calming it down. "We shouldn't waste any more time. This city desperately needs our help, so let's get moving already."

Zapdos sighed helplessly and finally spread its wings, carrying Ethan toward the top of the television tower.

Only after truly riding Zapdos did Ethan realize that, much like Jolteon's fur, its feathers were surprisingly stiff and prickly. Not only was it uncomfortable to sit on, but if he paid attention, he could even smell the faint scent of electricity flowing through its body.

The sea breeze swept through Ethan's hair.

Zapdos flew at incredible speed. After only a few powerful flaps of its wings, it had already reached the rooftop platform at the top of the tower.

Ethan released his grip and jumped down smoothly.

Pointing toward the giant Tentacruel in the distance, he said, "Look over there. That Pokémon is Tentacruel, and it's your opponent this time. Don't let its massive size fool you. It's only a Water- and Poison-type. In terms of type advantage, we completely dominate it!"

It was also Zapdos's first time seeing such an enormous creature. Its eyes immediately burned with fighting spirit. With a deafening cry, it shot directly toward Tentacruel!

Meanwhile, the battle below remained extremely intense. Although the Trainers' Pokémon were individually stronger, there were simply too many Tentacool. Most Pokémon were being swarmed by several Tentacool at once.

Just as the people were beginning to feel increasingly overwhelmed, someone suddenly noticed something above them. Looking up, they saw a streak of golden lightning slicing across the sky!

"Look! It's Zapdos!!"

Someone shouted, and all the nearby Trainers quickly looked upward. When they clearly saw that it truly was the Legendary Pokémon Zapdos, every face became filled with shock and disbelief!
