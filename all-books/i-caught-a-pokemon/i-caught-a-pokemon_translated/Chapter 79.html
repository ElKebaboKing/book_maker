Charmeleon pounded its chest confidently, wearing an expression that clearly said, "Leave it to me!"

After Ethan returned to the table, Haunter floated over curiously.

"Why hand this task over to Charmeleon? Wouldn't it be better and more effective for you to take care of Dratini yourself instead of leaving it to another Pokémon?"

"Of course I'll still help from the sidelines," Ethan said with a smile. "But at the very least, both of their names contain the word 'dragon.' Compared to the others, they'll definitely communicate more easily!"

Seeing that Haunter still looked confused, Ethan continued,

"Alright, the truth is that Charmeleon's personality is pretty rough around the edges. Even if I deliberately remind it, it probably won't notice Dratini's subtle emotional changes anyway. But precisely because it acts so naturally and carelessly, it can slowly influence Dratini's personality over time!"

"For example, I once knew a guy with the surname Uzumaki. Through sheer persistence and his refusal to give up no matter how coldly he was treated, he eventually became best friends with a mentally troubled Uchiha kid."

"So I firmly believe that if anyone can truly change Dratini, it's definitely Charmeleon!"

After leaving Fuchsia City, Ethan continued southward. Following a night camping outdoors, he arrived at the southern coastline of the Kanto continent.

Continuing along the shore, he soon found a small unnamed fishing town by the sea.

Seeing several fishermen unloading their boats at the dock after returning from the ocean, Ethan walked over and politely asked,

"Sorry to bother you, but do you know where the Seafoam Islands are located?"

"The Seafoam Islands?" An old fisherman with a weathered face took a few puffs from his pipe while squinting at Ethan. "They're along one of the sea routes leading to Cinnabar Island, pretty far from here. But let me ask you something, young man—what are you planning to do there?"

Ethan looked toward the distant ocean and smiled.

"I heard there's a rare Pokémon there. I want to try catching it."

"A rare Pokémon? You mean Articuno?" The old fisherman exhaled a thick cloud of smoke before kindly advising him, "That's just a rumor people pass around. I've been fishing these waters my whole life and never once seen any Articuno on those islands! Every year plenty of people head there chasing legends, but after all that effort, don't they all just return empty-handed in the end?"

"Yes, yes… I'm just trying my luck. Even if I don't find Articuno, it's fine…"

Perhaps because of old age, the fisherman enjoyed rambling to anyone willing to listen, whether they were strangers or not. Ethan patiently endured the lecture for quite some time before finally learning the approximate location of the Seafoam Islands.

After marking the location on his phone map, he thanked the fisherman, left the town, and rode Gyarados toward the deep sea.

At that moment, just as his phone signal was about to disappear, a message suddenly popped up in his chat app.

The sender was a beautiful girl with long flaxen curls. In her profile picture, she was holding onto a man's arm with both hands. Though her expression looked slightly shy, she was still smiling happily toward the camera.

Staring at the girl's profile picture, Ethan's thoughts became chaotic.

After hesitating for a long time, his finger seemed frozen over the screen, unable to open the message.

"Who's this cute girl?"

The sudden voice beside his ear startled Ethan so badly that he nearly dropped his phone into the sea.

"Haunter! Don't suddenly speak up like that!"

"What do you mean suddenly!?" Haunter widened its eyes in disbelief. "I've been calling you for ages! You were the one staring blankly like a wooden statue! Your soul practically flew away!"

"How is that possible?" Ethan coughed awkwardly and quickly denied it. "I just zoned out for a second. It's not as exaggerated as you're making it sound!"

"Oh? Really?" Haunter teased with a mischievous grin. "Then who exactly is this girl? And that arm she's holding onto… that's yours, isn't it?"

"She…" Ethan clearly didn't want to elaborate and vaguely replied, "She's just a girl who lived next door when I stayed in the Kalos region. We used to play together a lot, so we became pretty close. That's all."

"That's all? Really? Then why are you too scared to even open her message?"

"Well…" Ethan hesitated for a moment. "Some things happened in the past, and now… I'm not really sure how to face her anymore."

Some things happened? Too afraid to face her?

Instantly, Haunter's imagination ran wild.

However, noticing the awkwardness in Ethan's tone, Haunter—despite being extremely curious—considerately chose not to continue asking and instead changed the subject.

"By the way, how did you originally become a Trainer? Why have you never talked about anything from before you came to Kanto? Even when I ask Kirlia, it refuses to reveal a single thing!"

Ethan shook his head.

"It's complicated. There's no way to explain it properly in just a few words. Maybe someday in the future, I'll tell you."

By now, Gyarados had already traveled far out to sea. Seeing no one nearby, Ethan switched back to riding Zapdos.

Above the clouds, Zapdos spread its wings and flew westward with the wind. Two hours later, they finally reached the target area.

However, trying to locate two small islands in the middle of the endless ocean was far more difficult than expected.

Patting Zapdos's neck feathers, Ethan asked,

"Hey, since you legendary birds have been fighting each other for so many years, do Zapdos, Articuno, and Moltres have some kind of special sensing ability? Something that can help us accurately locate Articuno?"

Zapdos completely ignored the meaningless nonsense, but it still turned its head and used its eagle-like eyesight to help Ethan locate the Seafoam Islands.

The Seafoam Islands, as their name suggested, consisted of two islands with tall barren mountains rising at their centers. Because the mountains looked nearly identical—like twins—the islands became known as the Seafoam Islands.

And since there were no other islands nearby, the two stood out sharply in the middle of the ocean.

After circling above them several times without noticing anything suspicious, Ethan thought for a moment before finally deciding to have Zapdos land on the larger island.

A fierce sea wind howled through the mountains, and even the rocks beneath his feet seemed to radiate a chilling coldness.

Far from human civilization, the two islands were usually extremely quiet.

However, for some reason, although patches of vegetation existed, they were sparse. Most of the terrain consisted of exposed stone, making the islands appear incredibly desolate.

Even more troublesome, perhaps because they were frequently disturbed by visitors—or because the local Pokémon had a strong territorial instinct—from the moment Ethan set foot on the island, every wild Pokémon he encountered attacked him without hesitation!

(End of Chapter)
