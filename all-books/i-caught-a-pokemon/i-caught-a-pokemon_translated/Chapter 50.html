Ethan did not know much about perfume, but as long as he listened to a professional's recommendations, he was confident he could not go wrong.

At that moment, Erika took out a sample bottle of the perfume intended for Sabrina. Rolling up her sleeve slightly, she sprayed a small amount onto her wrist.

"Here, smell it and see if you like the fragrance."

Glancing at her fair and smooth arm, Ethan leaned closer and gently inhaled. Sure enough, he caught a pleasant scent—fresh and elegant without being overpowering.

But besides the perfume itself, there was also a faint feminine fragrance that made his heartbeat quicken slightly.

The scent was completely different from Sabrina's, subtly reflecting the stark contrast between the two girls' personalities.

Erika smiled softly.

"This perfume is exclusive to our shop. You won't find it anywhere else. It's one of our unique custom blends."

Clearing his throat lightly, Ethan said,

"Alright then, I'll take these four perfumes. Please wrap them up for me."

After paying, Ethan bid farewell to Erika and left the perfume shop. He originally wanted to contact Looker, but no matter how many times he called, the phone never connected.

Strange. Hadn't Looker said he wanted to meet after Ethan arrived in Celadon City?

Still, remembering Looker's mysterious line of work, Ethan did not dwell on it too much.

Maybe he was carrying out some sort of mission.

And since Erika, the Gym Leader, was currently at the perfume shop, there was no point in heading to the Celadon Gym now anyway.

After thinking for a moment, Ethan turned and headed toward the Celadon Game Corner.

Since he had nothing else to do, he might as well spend some time there.

The Celadon Game Corner was located in the southeastern part of the city. Once Ethan saw it in person, he realized the scale was far larger than what appeared in the games.

Not only were there hotels and restaurants, but even a movie theater and multiple dance halls!

Inside the Game Corner, crowds bustled constantly in and out. There were groups of young people everywhere, as well as many middle-aged customers. Looking around inside, Ethan saw claw machines, arcade games, slot machines, dance machines, balloon shooting games, and pachinko machines of every kind imaginable.

The entire place was lively and noisy.

At the counter, Ethan exchanged some money for over a dozen game tokens. Just as he was about to head toward the claw machines, something at a nearby stall suddenly caught his attention.

The stall owner was an ordinary middle-aged man, but the products he sold were rows upon rows of Pokémon masks in various designs.

"Boss, how much is that mask?"

Ethan pointed toward a half-face Gengar mask.

The upper half looked exactly like Gengar's large dark-purple face and could cover most of the wearer's features, while the lower portion was missing, leaving the wearer's mouth exposed.

"You have good taste, customer," the stall owner said proudly. "This mask is the treasure of my shop! Not only is it extremely durable, but the eye sections are made of glass, so your vision won't be obstructed at all. Normally it sells for 300, but since it's a special day, I'll let you have it for 250."

"That number…"

Ethan frowned slightly before bargaining.

"How about 100?"

The stall owner immediately put on a pained expression.

"You must be joking, customer. 250 is already very cheap."

Ethan simply shook his head, turned around, and started walking away without hesitation.

Sure enough, the stall owner quickly rushed forward and grabbed him back.

"Alright, alright! I can tell we're fated to meet. How about this—you add a little more, and I'll sell it to you for 150?"

Ethan secretly laughed inside.

This trick really did work every single time.

However, outwardly, he still looked completely unmoved. He merely took out a single hundred-dollar bill.

Seeing this, the stall owner sighed dramatically, but his hands moved lightning-fast as he snatched the bill and shoved the mask into Ethan's hands.

His speed was so quick that Ethan could not help wondering whether even 100 had still been too high of a price…

After leaving the stall, Ethan carefully stored the Gengar mask inside his backpack.

Ever since revealing Zapdos at Silph Company, Ethan had clearly realized that showing his real face too openly would attract unnecessary trouble.

This mask, although simple, could effectively conceal his identity and greatly reduce the chance of exposure.

Taking out some game tokens, Ethan inserted them into a claw machine. However, while he was completely focused on the game, several pairs of eyes outside the Game Corner simultaneously locked onto him.

"Hey, hey… are we really going to attack him?"

James hid inside a nearby phone booth and whispered nervously. Beside him, as always, were Jessie and Meowth.

"Of course we are. It's the Boss's orders." Jessie nodded firmly. "And didn't we abandon the twerp and his Pikachu just to rush back to Celadon City for this guy?"

"But…" James hesitated. "He's actually a really good person. Didn't he help us escape from the bottom of the ocean last time? Wouldn't this make us totally ungrateful?"

"Idiot! We're villains! How can you say something like that!?" Meowth leaped up and scratched James hard across the face.

"Ahhh! My face!"

James collapsed dramatically before quickly climbing back up again.

"But the place he's in right now is the entrance to our secret base! Are we really going to make a move here?"

"Of course not!" As the true leader of the trio, Jessie made the final decision. "We'll wait until he leaves the Game Corner and then find another chance to attack!"

After enjoying himself thoroughly at the Game Corner and winning two plush dolls as prizes, Ethan was just about to leave when he suddenly received a message.

The message was from Looker.

However, aside from a location pin, there was not a single word attached.

What was this supposed to mean? Was Looker asking to meet there?

Remaining cautious, Ethan followed the location all the way toward the edge of the city before finally stopping near several warehouses beside a freight train station.

The area was completely silent, without a single person in sight. Other than the occasional cargo train rumbling past, there was no other sound.

"Haunter, scout the nearby area. Kirlia, stay alert."

Ethan spoke quietly before putting on the Gengar mask he had just purchased.

Who would have thought that a mask bought only moments ago would become useful so quickly?

Not long afterward, Haunter returned and shook its head at Ethan.

"There's no ambush here. But inside the warehouse ahead, there's someone badly injured!"

Someone badly injured?

Ethan froze for a moment. Knowing Haunter had never met Looker and would not recognize him, Ethan nodded and quickly followed its directions toward a damaged warehouse.

The warehouse door was already broken open, hanging loosely on its hinges.

What shocked Ethan even more was the trail of blood stretching from outside the iron door all the way into the warehouse interior.

The bloodstains were horrifyingly conspicuous, and the thick metallic smell in the air seemed to silently tell a grim story.
