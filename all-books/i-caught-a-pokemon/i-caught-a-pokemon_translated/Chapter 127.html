The three of them continued chatting casually, and when Ethan noticed that Chloe seemed to have taken a liking to Eevee, the conversation naturally shifted from all sorts of topics to Erika's Eevee.

Ethan asked, "Considering your Gym specializes in Grass-type Pokémon, I suppose its evolution will be Leafeon, right?"

Erika nodded.

"Of course. But for it to evolve, I still need a Leaf Stone. A few days ago, our Gym held a Trainer Appreciation Event in accordance with the Pokémon League's request, so we used up all of our Leaf Stones. My Eevee will have to wait a little longer before it can evolve."

A Leaf Stone?

"What a coincidence!" Ethan exclaimed.

He rummaged through his backpack and pulled out the Leaf Stone he had obtained from the Eevee Brothers in Stone Town. Without the slightest hesitation, he handed it to Erika.

"Here. You can have this Leaf Stone."

Erika looked at it in surprise.

"Hm? How do you have something like this?"

Ethan shrugged.

"Just a souvenir from my travels. I don't have any use for it, so I'd rather your Eevee benefit from it."

"Oh my, thank you so much."

Erika accepted it without any hesitation.

"However, Eevee is still a little inexperienced. I'd like to wait until it gains more experience before choosing the right moment to use this Leaf Stone."

Ethan waved dismissively.

"That's fine. Since I've already given it to you, when you evolve Eevee is entirely your decision."

As he spoke, he glanced at the time. Without realizing it, they had rested for more than an hour. Their energy had mostly recovered, and it was time to continue their journey.

"Well, it's getting late. We still need to reach Viridian City before nightfall. Thanks for the wonderful meal. See you next time!"

He and Chloe stood up to say goodbye, prompting Erika to quickly rise as well.

"I'm truly sorry," Erika said apologetically. "The Gym and the perfume shop have been keeping me extremely busy, so I won't be able to cheer for you in person. But I'll definitely watch all of your matches through the television broadcast."

"It's alright. Don't worry about it."

In truth, after traveling alone for so long, Ethan had grown accustomed to it. Perhaps it was simply his personality—even if nobody came to cheer for him, it wouldn't affect his mindset in the slightest.

Everyone had their own life to live, and he had no right to expect others to change theirs for his sake.

Erika placed both hands gently in front of her chest and bowed gracefully.

"Then I wish you the greatest success in battle. May you achieve outstanding results."

Smiling, Ethan and Chloe returned the bow in the same traditional manner.

After they had walked quite a distance, Chloe glanced back and noticed that Erika was still standing there, quietly watching them leave. She quickly turned her head forward again.

Miss Erika is still watching us...?

Although she could no longer make out Erika's expression from this distance, Chloe could almost feel the gentle warmth of her gaze.

Ethan has really met so many interesting people during his journey...

Leaving the open fields behind, they continued onward until they reached Sunshine Town.

Traveling farther west would bring them directly to the outskirts of Viridian City, and once they entered the city, it would mean they were finally very close to the Indigo Plateau.

Before they could get there, however, a wide river blocked their path.

The place was completely deserted—there were no boats on the river and no bridges spanning across it. The only way to cross was with the help of a Pokémon.

Ethan called out his Gyarados, and together with a cautious Chloe, they climbed onto its back and safely crossed the river.

On the opposite bank stretched an endless, dense forest.

It was none other than the famous Viridian Forest of the Kanto region.

The sun was already sinking toward the horizon. Ethan hadn't planned on entering the forest so close to nightfall, but neither did he want to camp outdoors. With little choice, they quickened their pace, hoping to reach Viridian City before darkness completely fell.

Just as before, Bug-type Pokémon could be seen everywhere throughout the forest.

Caterpie and Weedle frequently peeked out from behind bushes and tree trunks, frightening Chloe at every turn.

"Why are there bugs everywhere in this forest?"

Unable to hide her fear, Chloe hurried a few steps forward and instinctively grabbed the corner of Ethan's jacket.

Truthfully, she wasn't alone.

A fear of bugs was common among many girls—even Misty couldn't stand Bug-type Pokémon.

Just as Ethan was about to reassure her, a voice suddenly called out from the distance.

"Hey, you over there! You look strangely familiar!"

Hearing the voice, Ethan turned around.

Under the shade of a large tree stood a young man dressed like a samurai, leaning casually against the trunk as he stared at them with a cool expression.

"Ah, it's you!"

That distinctive outfit made Ethan recognize him immediately.

He hadn't expected that after so much time had passed, he would run into the Samurai again in this very forest.

The Samurai crossed his arms and said coolly,

"I never thought you'd set foot in this forest again. So... how about another battle?"

Ethan looked him over once more.

Although the young man's appearance hadn't changed much, his expression had become noticeably steadier. It was obvious he had grown stronger through training.

Still, Ethan couldn't help admiring his perseverance.

To have stayed here for such a long time without ever leaving the forest...

That was honestly impressive.

Has he decided to make this forest his permanent home?

Seeing Ethan remain silent, the Samurai mistakenly thought he was being looked down upon.

"Hmph! Last time you told me to teach those Trainers from Pallet Town a lesson. I have to admit, they were all incredibly strong. I lost badly to every single one of them. But I've improved tremendously since then! Not only have my original Pokémon evolved, I've also caught many new partners!"

"Oh? Is that so?"

Ethan smiled.

"But you aren't the only one who's grown stronger. It's been a long time—I've improved as well."

Beside him, Chloe looked utterly confused.

"Those Trainers from Pallet Town?"

A question mark practically appeared over her head.

Why had Ethan suddenly become a Trainer from Pallet Town?

Noticing her puzzled expression, Ethan leaned close and quietly whispered the story of how he had tricked the Samurai back then.

Chloe burst into giggles.

"Ethan, you're so mean."

Meanwhile, on the other side, the Samurai happened to glance at Chloe.

Seeing her beautiful smile and hearing her cheerful laughter, his face unexpectedly turned bright red.

Perhaps sensing Ethan's amused gaze, he quickly tried to hide his embarrassment by throwing out a Poké Ball.

"Go! Butterfree!"

Since his opponent had already sent out a Pokémon, it was obvious that a battle was unavoidable today.

Ethan looked up at the darkening sky and sighed.

"You're really persistent."

He took out one of his own Poké Balls.

"Looks like I have no choice. I'm counting on you—go, Charizard!"

A flash of blue light erupted as the Poké Ball opened, and a majestic dragon-like Pokémon with a pair of powerful wings appeared on the battlefield.
