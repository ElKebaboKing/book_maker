At that moment, the giant screen displayed the information for Ethan Vale and Damian. Beneath their portraits were three icons representing the number of Pokémon each Trainer had remaining.

The battle format was 3-on-3. The first Trainer to lose all three Pokémon would be eliminated from the tournament.

A referee stood in the center of the battlefield. Seeing that both sides were ready, he raised the two flags in his hands and shouted,

"Both Trainers, please send out your Pokémon!"

Ethan cleared his mind and reached for the Poké Ball he had already selected.

Across the field, Damian grinned confidently as he gripped one of his own.

As both Trainers released their Pokémon simultaneously, two flashes of blue light appeared.

Standing opposite Ethan's Gardevoir was a Pokémon with a round, furry body, a pig-like nose, powerful limbs, and a monkey-like appearance—

Primeape.

A Primeape...

As a Fighting-type Pokémon, Primeape was at a severe disadvantage against Gardevoir's Psychic and Fairy typing.

The moment Gardevoir appeared, many spectators—especially those unfamiliar with Pokémon from other regions—couldn't help but admire its graceful appearance.

Countless cameras immediately focused on it.

Even Damian, with his limited experience, had never seen a Pokémon like this before.

"Hey! What kind of Pokémon is that?"

Faced with the unknown, Damian frowned deeply and shouted across the field.

Ethan ignored him completely.

With thousands of spectators watching, Damian didn't have the nerve to embarrass himself by pulling out his Pokédex and scanning Gardevoir.

Unable to identify it, he could only judge from its appearance.

But after only a few seconds, those crimson eyes alone made him instinctively feel danger.

The starting bell rang.

The battle officially began.

"Gardevoir, use Psychic!"

"Primeape, rush in and crush it!"

Both Trainers gave their commands simultaneously.

Primeape had barely leaped forward before an invisible psychic force slammed into it like an invisible truck.

It was hurled violently to the ground.

Because of the type disadvantage, the attack dealt devastating damage.

Damian's expression immediately changed.

Damn it... So it really is a Psychic-type!

What a terrible matchup right from the start!

After landing the first attack, Gardevoir gave Primeape no chance to recover.

It continued bombarding its opponent with overwhelming psychic power.

Wave after wave of Psychic attacks pinned Primeape down, causing it to howl in pain.

Damian grew increasingly anxious.

"Primeape! Fight back already!!"

Struggling desperately, Primeape clawed wildly at the ground.

By chance, it grabbed a fist-sized rock.

Damian's eyes lit up as though he'd found a lifeline.

"Use Fling!"

Primeape immediately hurled the rock toward Gardevoir.

To block the projectile, Gardevoir finally released its Psychic hold.

Seizing the opportunity, Primeape sprang to its feet and charged forward.

But before it could get close—

The very same rock suddenly reversed direction at incredible speed and shot straight back toward Primeape's face!

Fortunately, Primeape instinctively tilted its head at the last moment.

Instead of striking its forehead, the rock merely grazed its cheek before flying past.

"Primeape!" Damian shouted as the distance closed. "Use Seismic Toss!"

Primeape stretched out both arms, preparing to grab Gardevoir and leap into the air—

But Gardevoir vanished.

With a flash of light, it used Teleport, instantly reappearing several meters away.

The next second—

Psychic struck again, blasting Primeape back onto the ground.

"Damn it!!"

Knowing the type matchup was hopeless, Damian decided to switch Pokémon.

Grinding his teeth, he reached for another Poké Ball—

But Ethan had already anticipated his intentions.

"Gardevoir, stop him!"

A red beam shot from Damian's Poké Ball, attempting to recall Primeape.

However, before the beam reached it, Primeape was lifted high into the air by Gardevoir's Psychic power.

Although Ethan had only just spoken, the deep bond between Trainer and Pokémon allowed Gardevoir to react almost instantly.

The recall beam passed harmlessly beneath Primeape without touching it.

"Damn it!"

Damian cursed again and immediately fired another recall beam.

This time—

Boom!

Gardevoir slammed Primeape straight into the ground with tremendous force.

Unable to withstand any more punishment, Primeape collapsed unconscious.

Only then was Damian finally able to recall it.

Thunderous applause and cheers erupted throughout the stadium.

The loudest voices, naturally, came from the biker gang, Chloe, Misty, Ash, Brock, and the others cheering from the stands.

While spectators certainly enjoyed close battles, they also respected overwhelming victories like this one.

And after only a single battle, Ethan's handsome appearance—combined with Gardevoir's elegance and overwhelming strength—had already earned him plenty of new fans among the audience.

On the Trainer's platform, Damian's face turned bright red.

He had never been known for having a good temper.

Unable to contain himself, he cursed under his breath.

Before the tournament, he had confidently bragged to his friends that he would at least reach the semifinals—or even compete for the championship.

Yet now, in the very first preliminary round, he had encountered an opponent this powerful.

For Ethan, however, it had been an exceptionally easy battle.

The entire match had been completely one-sided.

From beginning to end, Primeape hadn't caused Gardevoir the slightest bit of trouble.

Just then, the announcer's voice echoed across the battlefield.

"Oh my! Damian's first Pokémon has already been defeated! But don't count him out just yet! Let's see what Pokémon he'll send out next in an attempt to turn this battle around!"

With little time to think, Damian clenched his jaw and threw another Poké Ball.

"Go, Marowak!"

A Ground-type this time?

Ethan calmly recalled Gardevoir before sliding his finger across the Poké Balls at his waist.

"It's your turn now—Venusaur!"

His strategy was simple.

While aiming to win every battle, he also wanted each of his Pokémon to gain experience battling on the tournament stage.

After all, performing before tens of thousands of spectators naturally made even his Pokémon nervous.

By rotating them into battle, they could gradually adapt to the atmosphere, overcome their tension, and prepare themselves for the increasingly difficult matches ahead.

With both Pokémon ready, the announcer's excited voice rang out once more.

"Everything is set for Round Two! Will Damian stage an incredible comeback... or will Ethan Vale continue his overwhelming dominance?"

( End of Chapter )
