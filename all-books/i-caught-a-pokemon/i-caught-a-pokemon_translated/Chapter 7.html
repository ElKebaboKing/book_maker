"You really do have a bold mouth…"

With just a single sentence, Ethan Vale had already left a deep impression on the Cerulean Beauties!

Daisy smiled faintly.

"Whether it's being trainers or performing water ballet, both are part of our profession. Alright then, come with us."

Following the three sisters, Ethan Vale walked through a corridor and arrived at the edge of the swimming pool.

At this moment, all the spectators who had come to watch the performance had already left, leaving nobody behind to disturb them.

Just then, Violet and Lily tossed several floating platforms into the pool. With such a simple setup, the battlefield was complete.

Honestly… it felt rather improvised.

But then again…

Ethan Vale carefully scanned the floating platforms several times and did not dare lower his guard in the slightest.

After all, this was a Water-type Gym. Water surrounded the entire battlefield, and since he had no Water-type Pokémon of his own, his Pokémon's movement space was extremely limited. Every move would require caution.

"Alright." Violet clapped her hands and smiled. "Now we can battle!"

Without another word, Ethan Vale walked to the trainer's platform on the opposite side, facing the three sisters across the pool.

Daisy's voice soon echoed over the water.

"The rules here are simple. Each of us will use one Pokémon. If you defeat all three of us, your Gym challenge will be considered successful!"

Violet crossed her arms with an amused expression.

"So then, who would you like to challenge first?"

It could be said that Ethan Vale's earlier remark had ignited a small competitive spirit within them.

His gaze swept across their faces one by one, though it revealed nothing.

"Anyone is fine. You can decide yourselves."

"You've got quite the confidence." As the youngest sister, Lily volunteered immediately, stepping forward while taking out a Poké Ball. "In that case, let me test you first! Go, Goldeen!"

As she opened the Poké Ball, a flash of blue light appeared, releasing a white fish Pokémon with a horn on its head into the pool.

Information about it immediately surfaced in Ethan Vale's mind.

Goldeen was a pure Water-type Pokémon. Its dorsal and tail fins were highly developed, allowing it to swim at speeds of up to five knots. Because its elegant tail resembled a flowing dress, it was also known as the Queen of the Water!

Glancing at the Pokémon beside him, Ethan Vale sent out his own first combatant.

"Eevee, you're up."

Hearing Ethan Vale's command, Eevee cried out excitedly before leaping gracefully from the poolside onto one of the floating platforms.

From a type perspective, neither Pokémon held a natural advantage over the other.

Victory would therefore depend entirely on strength and strategy. However, with the battlefield favoring Water-types, defeating Goldeen would certainly not be easy for Eevee.

Everything depended on Eevee's performance—and Ethan Vale's commands.

As the eldest sister, Daisy acted as referee. Once both Pokémon were ready, she loudly announced:

"The Cerulean Gym challenge battle begins now!"

The moment her words fell, Lily gave her command.

"Goldeen, use Peck!"

With Lily's order, the battle officially began!

Goldeen immediately dove beneath the water, disappearing from sight. A few seconds later, ripples spread across the surface as Goldeen suddenly burst upward from the pool, charging toward Eevee as though it could fly!

Its speed was undeniably impressive.

Ethan Vale's eyes narrowed slightly as he calmly ordered:

"Eevee, dodge it!"

Relying on its excellent agility, Eevee twisted aside just before impact, easily avoiding the attack.

Seeing this, Ethan Vale immediately followed up.

"Use Tackle!"

At the instant the two Pokémon crossed paths, Eevee leaped into the air and slammed fiercely toward Goldeen.

Unfortunately, just as Eevee was about to connect, Goldeen twisted its body as well and narrowly avoided the attack before splashing back into the water and vanishing once more.

The first exchange ended without either side gaining an advantage.

Still, Lily now realized that the man standing opposite her would not be easy to deal with.

Eevee landed steadily on another floating platform. However, because of Goldeen's splash into the water, droplets soaked parts of its fur, leaving it damp.

Eevee shook itself vigorously, but failed to fully dry its coat.

Watching everything carefully, Lily smiled.

"Very good! Goldeen, keep it up and force it into the water!"

Goldeen repeated its previous tactic, once again launching a Horn Attack from the water.

This time, however, the added weight from the water on Eevee's fur greatly affected its speed. Facing Goldeen's second charge, Eevee barely managed to evade the attack at the very last second.

Seeing this, Ethan Vale quietly let out a breath of relief.

After all, given Eevee's current strength, if it were struck directly and accidentally knocked into the water, the situation would become extremely dangerous.

Looks like I'll need another strategy…

As he thought, Ethan Vale's eyes swept repeatedly across the floating platforms. Noticing that two of them had already drifted together, a plan slowly formed in his mind.

"Eevee, jump onto that platform!"

Eevee glanced back at Ethan Vale in confusion but still obeyed and jumped over.

Naturally, Lily had no idea what he intended to do, but she still laughed confidently.

"No matter what you try now, it won't help! Goldeen, use Horn Attack!"

Goldeen burst from the water once more, charging directly toward Eevee with the horn atop its head.

"Eevee, move backward!"

Ethan Vale's command rang out at that precise moment.

Hearing the order, Eevee immediately retreated several steps toward the far side of the platform. The sudden shift in weight caused the floating platform to tilt upward!

And at that exact moment, Goldeen struck it head-on!

Even worse for Goldeen, its sharp horn became lodged directly into the raised platform, leaving its entire body suspended helplessly in the air!

"What!?" Lily cried out in shock, while even Daisy and Violet widened their eyes in disbelief.

None of them had expected such a tactic!

Ethan Vale smiled lightly.

A fish out of water, no matter how fierce it once was, only had one fate left.

Without water, and now trapped against the platform, what could Goldeen possibly do?

"Eevee, finish it!"

Hearing Ethan Vale's command, Lily panicked and immediately shouted:

"Goldeen, get back into the water quickly!"

However, no matter how much Goldeen twisted and struggled, its horn remained firmly stuck.

"Damn it." Seeing Eevee approaching closer and closer, Lily hurriedly ordered, "Goldeen, use Supersonic!"

Visible sound waves erupted from Goldeen's mouth. Unfortunately, because it was facing away from Eevee, although the attack successfully caused brief confusion, the overall situation remained unchanged once Eevee recovered.

"It's over."

As if proving Ethan Vale's words true, Eevee slammed forward with Tackle, striking heavily and finally knocking Goldeen out of battle.

"You're pretty good…"

Lily sighed helplessly, recalling Goldeen into its Poké Ball before silently stepping aside.

As she withdrew, Violet took her place at the trainer's platform.

"You've definitely got some skill." Looking at Ethan Vale, she took out her Poké Ball and released her own Pokémon. "But the next one won't be so easy to handle. Go, Krabby!"

As her words fell, a crab-like Pokémon appeared within the pool.

At first glance, its enormous pincers raised high above its body were especially eye-catching!

However, upon closer inspection, its red-and-white body and blank-looking eyes made it appear somewhat foolish, almost as though it wasn't particularly intelligent.

Because Ethan Vale knew the species well, information immediately surfaced in his mind.

Krabby was a freshwater crab Pokémon, and its massive pincers were undoubtedly its greatest weapons!

Occasionally, its claws could even fall off during battle, though they would quickly regenerate. When danger approached, it could also spit bubbles around its body to make itself appear larger.

So it sacrifices mobility for close-range combat power…

After thinking briefly, Ethan Vale recalled Eevee.

"Come back, Eevee. You did great."

Once Eevee returned to his side, Ethan Vale gently rubbed its head in praise before taking out Bulbasaur's Poké Ball.

"Now it's your turn, Bulbasaur."

As he opened the Poké Ball, Bulbasaur appeared upon the floating platform in a flash of blue light.

"Bulba! Bulba!"

The moment it appeared, Bulbasaur locked its gaze firmly onto Krabby in the water, its fighting spirit surging high!

From a type perspective, Grass-type Bulbasaur undoubtedly held an advantage over the Water-type Krabby.

But the same issue still remained.

On this battlefield surrounded by water, movement space was heavily restricted. Maintaining balance and avoiding falling into the pool would become the key factor in deciding victory!

(End of Chapter)
