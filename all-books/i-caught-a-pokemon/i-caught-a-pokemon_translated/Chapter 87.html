Ethan Vale stood up and moved toward the divider in the middle of the hot spring, making sure the others couldn't see his chat history.

Leaning back against the wooden partition, he looked up at Brock and suddenly asked, "After traveling for so long, have you ever liked a girl?"

"Of course I have." Though confused by the sudden topic change, Brock answered matter-of-factly, "Whether it's Officer Jenny or Nurse Joy, as long as they're beautiful older girls, I like them all."

"And what about you, Ash?"

Ash, being the embodiment of a battle-obsessed straight man, immediately shook his head.

"I'm going to become a Pokémon Master! I don't need any girl I like!"

"Oh? Really?" Misty's gleeful voice drifted over from the other side of the hot spring. "Then I wonder who it was that blushed when we passed by Pokémon Tech and saw the picture of that girl named Giselle, and even said she was cute?"

"A-ah…" Ash froze awkwardly and scratched his cheek. "Did I really say something like that?"

Everyone laughed, and Ethan turned back to Brock.

"Then have you ever met a girl who likes you?"

"Uh…" Brock hesitated. "Nope. Why are you asking?"

"Nothing." Ethan smiled while fiddling with his phone. "Just asking casually."

Brock: ???

Even though Ethan hadn't explained anything, Brock still felt strangely offended by his tone.

So the person Ethan had been chatting with all this time was a girl who liked him?

Unbelievable! Just because someone likes him, he's acting this smug!?

That's infuriating!!

Splash…

At that moment, the sound of water came from behind Ethan.

Ripples spread through the hot spring, and in the next second, Misty swam to the other side of the divider, leaning back against the same spot with only a thin curtain separating them.

Ethan could even faintly feel the warmth radiating from her body through the gap.

"By the way…" Misty said softly, "what do you think Mr. Blaine's riddle really means?"

Ethan immediately understood what she was thinking and smiled.

"You're smart enough that you've probably already figured it out, haven't you?"

"'Floods above, blazing fire below'…" Misty hesitated. "I think it probably refers to this hot spring. Maybe the Gym battlefield is underneath it. I just still can't figure out where the mechanism is hidden."

In truth, Ethan only vaguely remembered the Cinnabar Gym from the anime. As for the specific details, he'd forgotten them long ago, so naturally he couldn't recall where the hidden switch actually was.

Even so, he wasn't worried in the slightest. In fact, he was perfectly happy leaving this sort of thing to Misty and the others.

Soaking comfortably in the warm water, Ethan nearly fell asleep.

But after a short moment of peace, Misty's anxious shout suddenly rang out behind him.

"Togepi! Come back!"

Ethan instantly snapped awake, though since he didn't know what had happened, he could only ask, "Misty, what's wrong?"

"It's Togepi being naughty!" Misty replied. "It climbed onto the faucet at the hot spring outlet. It's standing way too high—it could fall!"

On the other side of the divider, Togepi was hopping happily atop a Gyarados-shaped water spout.

Misty stood up, about to catch Togepi, but as it continued bouncing, the faucet suddenly sank slightly, as if triggering some hidden mechanism!

Click…

The next second, a massive rock beside the hot spring slowly shifted aside, revealing a pitch-black tunnel hidden behind it!

Unfortunately, the moving rock also smashed through the wooden divider in the spring.

The partition instantly collapsed, and Ethan and Misty found themselves staring directly at each other.

At that moment, Misty was wrapped only in a towel, exposing much of her fair skin. The sudden accident made her face turn bright red.

With a startled cry, she hurriedly crouched down into the water.

Fortunately, Ethan had been standing in the middle, and Ash and Brock were completely distracted by the hidden tunnel. By the time they turned toward Misty after hearing her scream, all they saw was the top half of her head sticking out of the water.

"Huh?" Ash looked puzzled. "Why's your face so red, Misty? You look like a boiled shrimp. But seriously, you're way too timid—it was just the divider falling over!"

Misty first glanced timidly at Ethan, then glared fiercely at Ash.

"What's it to you!? Mind your own business!"

"Alright, enough." Ethan quickly interrupted them. "That tunnel is probably the secret passage leading to the real Gym. Looks like we really have Togepi to thank this time. Who would've thought that while we were just relaxing in the hot spring, it would accidentally trigger the hidden switch?"

The group returned to the changing room one after another and changed back into their normal clothes.

A few minutes later, they regrouped beside the hot spring.

After exchanging glances, they entered the tunnel.

The staircase beneath their feet stretched downward, seemingly leading deep into the volcano itself.

After walking dozens of meters, the temperature inside the cave grew hotter and hotter. Thankfully, candles burning along the walls provided enough light to keep the place from feeling too dark or oppressive.

Eventually, they arrived before a tightly sealed iron door.

Without thinking, Ash immediately grabbed the handle—only to jerk his hand back the instant he touched it.

"Hot! Hot! Hot!!"

Ethan raised his foot and kicked the iron door open with the sole of his shoe.

But before they could clearly see what lay beyond, a thick cloud of white steam billowed toward them.

At the same time, a strong sulfur smell assaulted their noses.

As the steam slowly dispersed into the flowing air, an astonishing battlefield appeared before them.

It was a simple platform suspended above molten lava by thick iron chains, and beneath the platform bubbled scorching magma that constantly churned and burst.

The heat was so intense that sweat soaked through their clothes after standing there for only a short while.

Bang! Bang! Bang! Bang!

At that moment, rows of spotlights suddenly switched on.

Far across the battlefield stood Blaine, calmly watching them from the opposite side.

"Well now," Blaine chuckled, "I never expected you'd actually find this place. Looks like today's destined to become a blazing-hot battle!"

"Two battles, actually," Ethan corrected. "Both of us came here to challenge you, Mr. Blaine."

"It doesn't matter how many challengers there are," Blaine replied with a grin. "So then—which one of you wants to face me first in a three-on-three battle?"

Ethan glanced at Ash, and seeing no objection from him, Ash immediately stepped forward.

"Of course it's me!"

Behind him, Misty quickly reminded him,

"Then do your best, Ash! But don't forget what you said earlier—we're all waiting to see you win the Volcano Badge entirely through your own strength!"

( End of Chapter )
