Seeing that the battlefield had become much smaller, Pyro burst into laughter.

"Haha! Now your speed advantage can't be used anymore! Flareon, do it again—use Flamethrower!"

Flareon opened its mouth and unleashed another stream of blazing fire.

Faced with the roaring flames, Jolteon instinctively stepped backward. But now it was surrounded by fire from both the front and the rear, leaving it with nowhere to dodge.

Seeing this, Ethan shouted,

"Jolteon, get to the other side of the pond!"

Hearing Ethan's command, Jolteon leaped into the pond. Paddling rapidly with all four legs, it quickly swam to the opposite bank.

Although it ended up completely soaked, it successfully escaped the sea of flames.

Turning back, it looked at Flareon.

Now the two battling Pokémon occupied separate sections of the battlefield, and with rocky ground beneath its feet, Jolteon no longer had to worry about the flames spreading toward it.

Flareon, on the other hand, was still standing on a patch of grass. If it wanted to attack Jolteon again, it would first have to cross to the opposite side.

Even worse, the fire beneath it continued to spread. If it didn't move soon, it would eventually be caught in its own flames.

"Tch..." Pyro sighed helplessly.

As a Fire-type Pokémon, Flareon wasn't as afraid of water as Charmander, but it still disliked getting wet.

Crossing through the pond would soak its fur, and if Jolteon attacked with an Electric-type move while it was in the water, the damage would undoubtedly be even greater.

Noticing the hesitation on Pyro's face, Ethan deliberately provoked him.

"What? Afraid to come over?"

"As if I would be!"

Pyro's fiery personality couldn't tolerate such taunting.

Compared to sitting back and waiting, he much preferred taking the offensive.

Just as he was about to give an order, he paused for a moment and came up with another idea.

"Flareon, charge straight through the flames!"

Hearing the command, even Ethan couldn't help but admire Pyro's decisiveness.

Because of its Fire typing, the flames would only deal half damage to Flareon. Instead of crossing the pond, running through the fire was indeed the better choice.

Enduring the scorching flames, Flareon finally burst through the blazing inferno.

But waiting for it on the other side was a fully prepared Jolteon.

"Jolteon, use Discharge!"

A surge of electricity struck Flareon head-on.

But Jolteon's assault wasn't over.

Closing the distance in an instant, it opened its jaws and retaliated with Thunder Fang, biting deeply into Flareon.

"Flareon!!"

Flareon winced in pain.

It tried to turn around and bite Jolteon back, but because of their positioning, it couldn't reach its opponent no matter how hard it tried.

The situation had completely reversed.

Pyro shouted anxiously,

"Flareon, don't give up! Fight back!"

Driven by pain, Flareon opened its mouth and unleashed Fire Spin, surrounding both itself and Jolteon in a vortex of flames.

This self-destructive strategy made Ethan frown.

He knew the battle couldn't drag on any longer.

The continuous flames would keep inflicting burn damage on any Pokémon trapped inside, and the longer the fight lasted, the worse it would be for Jolteon.

"Jolteon, use Double Kick!"

Jolteon released its bite, spun around, and lashed out with both hind legs.

Its kicks came so quickly they were almost invisible.

The left kick struck Flareon's head.

A split second later, the right kick slammed into its chest.

After taking consecutive blows to its weak points, Flareon's vision darkened.

It collapsed stiffly onto the ground.

Seeing that it was no longer moving, Rainer announced,

"Flareon is unable to battle! The winner is Jolteon!"

Clap! Clap! Clap!

Warm applause echoed around the battlefield.

After defeating both Vaporeon and Flareon in succession, everyone present gained an even greater respect for Ethan's strength.

Although the battle had ended, Jolteon was covered in injuries and looked ready to collapse at any moment.

Ethan quickly released Wartortle and had it extinguish the flames on Jolteon with Water Gun.

He then took out a Burn Heal spray and carefully treated Flareon's burns.

Meanwhile, the other Trainers used their Water-type Pokémon to put out the remaining flames across the battlefield.

Once everything had settled down, Rainer, Mikey, and Pyro each approached Ethan carrying a small box.

When they opened the boxes, they revealed a variety of Evolution Stones.

Rainer smiled.

"As a reward for defeating us, choose whichever stones you like and take them with you."

"Huh?" Ethan blinked in surprise.

"I thought this was just a friendly battle. There's a prize too?"

"Of course," Mikey replied casually.

"Anyone who defeats us gets to take home some of our town's specialty. That's been our tradition for years."

"All right."

Since the hosts insisted, refusing any further would only seem rude.

Ethan looked over the stones.

Fire Stones, Water Stones, Thunder Stones, Leaf Stones...

Their symbols and shapes made each type easy to identify.

Truthfully, Ethan didn't have much need for Evolution Stones.

While they were valuable, not every Pokémon evolved through them.

Among his own Pokémon, only Jolteon had ever evolved using a Thunder Stone.

Still, since he had the opportunity, he figured he might as well choose one.

After all, no one knew what the future might hold.

After a moment's thought, he picked up a Leaf Stone.

"A Leaf Stone?" Rainer raised an eyebrow.

"That's an unusual choice. But is one enough? Since you defeated both Mikey and Pyro, you're allowed to take another one. Don't worry—we have plenty of these."

To be honest, Ethan didn't really know what else to choose.

As he considered his options, his eyes suddenly landed on an ordinary gray stone lying quietly in the corner.

"What's this?"

Rainer glanced at it.

"Oh, that's an Everstone. It's nothing special—we just threw it in there to fill the box."

So this is an Everstone...

Compared to the common Evolution Stones, this was Ethan's first time seeing one.

"It's actually pretty rare. Then I'll take an Everstone as my second choice."

"Huh?" Rainer asked in confusion.

"What would you want an Everstone for? If you use it on a Pokémon, it'll never evolve."

"Isn't that even better?" Ethan said with a grin.

"If someone ever gets on my bad side, I'll just throw an Everstone at their Pokémon and make sure it can never evolve for the rest of its life!"

"..."

The three brothers looked at each other before bursting into laughter.

"Haha! That's a great idea! All right then—the Everstone is yours!"
