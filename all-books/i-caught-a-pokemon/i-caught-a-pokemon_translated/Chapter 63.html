Ethan's ability to "fly" was naturally all thanks to Haunter.

With Haunter beside him, there was no need to worry about accidents at all. He could mess around in the sky however he wanted!

As Zapdos let out a furious cry, dark clouds suddenly spread across the city skyline. Thick storm clouds piled together until the heavens resembled the black bottom of a giant cauldron, plunging the sky into darkness.

Below the television tower, Ash exchanged a glance with Pikachu before looking up at Zapdos, which shone beneath the storm clouds like a blazing sun.

"That move is…"

"That's right, it's Thunder," Brock said knowledgeably with a nod. "It's one of the most powerful Electric-type moves. And during rainy weather, opponents can barely dodge it at all!"

Once the power of lightning reached its peak, Zapdos screeched again, and an enormous bolt of lightning descended with blinding speed, striking the giant Tentacruel directly!

This time, the giant Tentacruel could no longer endure it and collapsed to the ground!

Perhaps it had exhausted all its energy, or maybe it had simply lost consciousness, but its body quickly shrank back down until it returned to its normal size.

Cheers erupted throughout the crowd. Without the giant Tentacruel commanding them, the remaining Tentacool quickly lost momentum and were soon driven back out of the city.

Zapdos glided through the air for several circles before flying back toward the television tower. Ethan seized the opportunity, leaped onto its back, and under the watchful eyes of the crowd, the man and bird quickly disappeared behind the tower.

Not long afterward, Ethan removed his mask, blended back into the crowd, and returned beneath the television tower as though nothing had happened.

By then, everything was already over.

This jellyfish uprising had caused damages worth tens of billions. Countless buildings had collapsed, and immeasurable property had been destroyed!

After such a catastrophe, Madam Kins' businesses were completely ruined, and her reputation had fallen to rock bottom. She would likely never continue investing in Yo Town Harbor again. As her dream crumbled apart, nobody knew what lessons people would ultimately learn from this painful disaster.

Ash and Brock had been too focused on the battle earlier to notice Ethan's disappearance. Only Misty seemed to suspect something. She smiled brightly at him but wisely chose not to expose him.

As Ethan recalled his Pokémon into their Poké Balls and prepared to leave with the others, the injured Horsea from earlier suddenly approached Misty.

During the chaotic battle, Misty had not only bandaged its wounds but had also protected it personally. Because of that, Horsea had come to acknowledge her, and after the incident willingly chose to become her Pokémon.

After such an exhausting ordeal, the group set up camp outside the city around noon and shared lunch together.

It was also the first time Ethan had ever tasted Brock's cooking.

"Wow, this is incredible!" Ethan gave Brock a thumbs-up in admiration. "Compared to your cooking, the food I make is honestly an insult to my own stomach! Looks like if I ever get the chance in the future, I need to travel with someone who's actually good at cooking!"

Ethan was not exaggerating. No matter how ordinary the ingredients looked, once Brock cooked them, they became unbelievably delicious. Brock also cooked with great care and attention. Not only did Ethan praise him nonstop, but even his Pokémon clearly wanted more after finishing their meals!

"It's not that good," Brock said embarrassedly.

He rummaged through his backpack, pulled out some paper and a pen, and wrote down several recipes before handing them to Ethan.

"These are a few recipes I use. The ingredients are all pretty common, but if you follow my methods, they'll definitely taste amazing!"

"Thank you so much!"

Ethan carefully stored the recipes away.

Meanwhile, while they enjoyed their lunch, videos of Zapdos battling the giant Tentacruel had already spread across the internet. The clips quickly attracted massive attention, with their popularity skyrocketing and even spreading to other regions!

People discussed the mysterious masked man endlessly, all trying to guess his true identity.

As for Zapdos's majestic appearance and overwhelming power, countless people became obsessed with it!

After a short rest, although Misty felt extremely reluctant, she knew it was time to say goodbye once again.

As Ethan waved farewell to the group, he handed Misty the bottle of perfume he had bought for her in Celadon City.

"Take it. I bought this for you in Celadon City. I hope you like it."

Misty's face immediately flushed red as she shyly accepted the gift box, though she did not open it on the spot.

Truthfully, no matter what was inside—even if it had only been a single piece of candy—she would have treasured it dearly.

Watching Ethan's gradually fading figure, Misty's eyes became misty as she softly whispered in a tiny voice,

"Ethan… big brother… let's meet again next time."

Ash's ears twitched.

"Huh? Ethan big brother? Why are you calling him that?"

"Idiot!"

Realizing Ash had overheard her, Misty blushed furiously and grabbed his ear.

"He's older than us! Calling him 'big brother' out of basic politeness is perfectly normal!"

"Oh, I see!"

Ash clearly failed to understand a girl's feelings, but after hearing her explanation, he grinned.

"Then I'll call him Ethan big brother too! It actually sounds really friendly!"

After leaving Yo Town Harbor, Ethan continued northward and several hours later arrived once more at Maiden's Peak.

Even though many days had passed since his last visit, he felt no particular nostalgia about returning.

Passing through the familiar streets, he headed directly toward the Maiden Shrine.

At that moment, the elderly priest they had briefly met before was sweeping the courtyard. Although the summer festival had long since ended, Maiden's Peak remained a popular tourist destination, and visitors still came every day.

Ethan walked up to the old priest and explained why he had come.

"What? You want to help spread the story of Maiden's Peak more effectively!?"

The energetic priest nodded repeatedly.

"That's wonderful! Thank you very much!"

When he learned that Ethan planned to use manga to help younger generations learn about the story, the priest may not have fully understood it, but he still treated the matter with great importance.

"This matter may have originated over two thousand years ago, but it now concerns the entire town. I can't make the decision alone. How about this—you wait here for a while. I'll go notify the mayor immediately. Once he arrives, you can explain everything to him personally."

"Huh? You need to notify the mayor too?"

Ethan froze in surprise.

Originally, his only intention had been to help the Maiden Ghost better convey her feelings and, at the same time, reassure Haunter. He had assumed the shrine would simply print the manga and distribute it to tourists.

He never expected that the town mayor himself would become involved!
