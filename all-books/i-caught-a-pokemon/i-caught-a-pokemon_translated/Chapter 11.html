Tamer, Damian

Hearing the voice, Ethan Vale turned around to look at him.

"My challenge?"

"That's right." The young man straightened his collar confidently and smiled. "My name is Damian, the powerful trainer known as the 'Beast Tamer'! Ever since I began my training journey, I've never been defeated. So, what do you say? Do you dare battle me?"

The Beast Tamer Damian?

Glancing at the Sandshrew beside his feet, Ethan Vale immediately understood who he was.

"I don't mind," Ethan Vale replied casually. Looking around into the distance, he then asked, "But where are we supposed to battle? This entire area is just city streets. There's no battlefield nearby."

Thanks to how widespread and popular Pokémon battling had become under the Pokémon League system, battlefields were everywhere throughout the world—similar to outdoor basketball courts from Ethan Vale's previous life. In parks, schools, riversides, and shopping districts, one could often find all kinds of simple battle arenas.

Unfortunately, the area Ethan Vale currently stood in was rather far from any public battlefield.

"Of course not here." Damian pointed toward the direction of Vermilion City. "I built a private Gym out in the wilderness not far ahead. There's a battlefield there. Follow me."

Without giving Ethan Vale any opportunity to refuse, Damian immediately turned and walked away.

Shrugging helplessly toward his Pokémon companions, Ethan Vale chose to follow after him.

The two walked one after another, leaving Cerulean City behind. After another ten minutes or so, Ethan Vale finally saw a simple campsite built from fences and tents.

Outside the camp stood a massive signboard that read:

Unofficial Pokémon League Gym — Currently 92 Consecutive Wins

From Damian's confident expression and the bold statement on the signboard, it was obvious that he was an extremely self-assured trainer.

And someone who really wanted attention.

Still, combined with his broad build, thick raised eyebrows, and rough-looking face, the longer one looked at him, the more strangely goofy he seemed.

Noticing Ethan Vale staring at the signboard, Damian proudly declared:

"My goal is to reach 100 consecutive victories. Once I achieve that, I'll begin traveling everywhere, challenging Gyms and collecting badges!"

"That actually sounds pretty impressive." Ethan Vale shrugged and offered a compliment, though there was very little admiration visible on his face.

The two entered the camp and arrived at Damian's so-called private Gym.

Through the open entrance of the tent, Ethan Vale immediately spotted several Pokémon training independently inside.

Butterfree and Beedrill repeatedly collided in midair, seemingly practicing combat drills, while on the ground, three Rattata wearing training weights appeared to be undergoing endurance exercises.

"So? Pretty great Gym, right?" Damian walked to the trainer's platform and said proudly, "As long as I beat you, I'll achieve my 93rd consecutive victory and move one step closer to my goal!"

"That's certainly worth celebrating." Ethan Vale stepped onto the opposite side and replied seriously, "I can tell you're definitely a strong opponent. But ever since I started traveling, I haven't lost a single battle either."

Just as he said, ever since obtaining Ralts, Ethan Vale had been winning continuously.

Both of them were on winning streaks—so neither had any reason to fear the other.

Beneath their feet was a hardened dirt battlefield. While completely unremarkable, this type of terrain was the most common wild battle arena in the world.

"Really? Then it seems someone's record is destined to be broken today!" Damian spread his hands helplessly as though Ethan Vale was impossible to deal with. "Alright, enough talking. The rules are simple—each of us uses one Pokémon. Whoever remains standing at the end wins!"

Ethan Vale nodded.

"Understood."

Pulling out a whip from behind him, Damian snapped it forward and shouted:

"Sandshrew, show them our power!"

At his command, the Sandshrew beside him slowly walked onto the battlefield.

From its appearance, aside from standing upright on two legs, Sandshrew looked fairly similar to a pangolin from Earth. Overall, however, it appeared rounder, with yellowish-brown skin covered in hard, dry brick-like patterns.

Ethan Vale took out his Pokédex and scanned it.

"Beep. Sandshrew, the Mouse Pokémon. A Ground-type Pokémon that dislikes water. It digs deep burrows in dry areas to live in. When danger approaches, it curls into a ball to protect itself."

In terms of typing, both Bulbasaur and Squirtle held a natural advantage over the Ground-type Sandshrew.

Although Ethan Vale could no longer remember every detail clearly, he did recall that this Sandshrew had once defeated Ash and even Team Rocket in the anime. Because of that, he dared not underestimate it.

After thinking carefully for a moment, Ethan Vale waved his hand and immediately sent out his ace Pokémon—Kirlia.

Looking at the elegant, maiden-like Kirlia, Damian exclaimed in surprise:

"Your Pokémon is definitely unique. But it doesn't matter who you use—the result will still be the same! Let's go, Sandshrew! Use Rollout!"

Sandshrew sprinted rapidly forward on all fours. After building enough momentum, it curled its body into a perfect sphere and rolled toward Kirlia at incredible speed.

"Kirlia, use Double Team!"

Kirlia instantly split into dozens of afterimages. Because Sandshrew's speed was too great to stop in time, it rolled directly through its original trajectory before finally turning around and realizing it had merely passed through a fake illusion.

"Kirlia, use Confusion!"

Kirlia raised its hand, and psychic energy instantly surrounded Sandshrew. Yet just at that moment, accompanied by the crack of Damian's whip, Sandshrew suddenly curled tightly into a ball!

Confusion lifted Sandshrew high into the air before slamming it violently back down.

When the dust settled, however, Sandshrew stretched its body, shook its head, and climbed back onto its feet, clearly having suffered very little damage.

"Not bad at all." Ethan Vale couldn't help praising it. "To instantly use Defense Curl to guard against attacks at the perfect moment… I have to admit, you've trained this Sandshrew extremely well."

Damian curled his lip proudly.

"Hmph. That little bit of pain means nothing! My Sandshrew has gone through intense training—it's even overcome its fear of water!"

"Oh?" Ethan Vale raised an eyebrow in surprise. "A Ground-type Pokémon overcoming its Water weakness? That really is impressive."

In truth, after their brief exchange earlier, Ethan Vale had already noticed something.

Not only was this Sandshrew exceptionally strong, but the communication between it and Damian was incredibly fast and efficient.

Most of the time, Damian only needed to crack his whip once. Without speaking at all, Sandshrew could understand his intentions through the sound alone, adjusting its attacks accordingly and catching opponents off guard.

Those two factors were exactly why they had achieved more than ninety consecutive victories.

But now, they had encountered an equally matched combination in Ethan Vale and Kirlia.

Ethan Vale and Kirlia also shared flawless coordination. Thanks to the red horns on its head, Kirlia could rapidly sense Ethan Vale's emotions. And as a Psychic-type Pokémon, it could even faintly perceive some of the thoughts in his mind.

(End of Chapter)
