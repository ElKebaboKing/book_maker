Looking at the boy, Ethan reached toward his belt and, in the next instant, threw out several Poké Balls at once.

As blue light flashed, Charmeleon, Wartortle, Jolteon, and Ivysaur appeared alongside Kirlia, completely surrounding the boy.

The boy looked at Ethan's intimidating Pokémon, then down at his own Farfetch'd. The expression on his face could only be described as priceless.

"Aren't you supposed to battle according to the rules!?"

"Of course," Ethan replied matter-of-factly. "These are my rules."

He shrugged.

"I already told you—I'm not a good person. I don't believe in sticking rigidly to conventions. When it comes to dealing with bad guys, my style has always been simple: overwhelm the weak with strength and win through numbers."

"..."

The boy was speechless.

"You're shameless."

"Thank you for the compliment."

Not wanting to waste any more time, Ethan waved his hand.

"Get them."

The Pokémon rushed forward.

Farfetch'd was quickly defeated, and Ivysaur wrapped the boy's wrists with its Vine Whip, leaving him completely unable to escape.

Ethan then immediately called the police.

The boy panicked.

He begged desperately, but when Ethan ignored him entirely, he gave up and began shouting curses instead.

Finding him too noisy, Ethan picked up a stone and shoved it into the boy's mouth before sealing it with a strip of tape.

That finally silenced him.

A short while later, Officer Jenny arrived on a motorcycle.

With a sharp brake, she stopped dramatically in front of Ethan, kicking up a cloud of exhaust.

"Who reported the incident?" she asked coolly.

"Cough, cough…"

Waving away the smoke, Ethan raised his hand.

"That would be me."

After hearing the explanation, Officer Jenny glared at the boy in anger.

"Kenta! I can't believe you've gone right back to your old ways. This time, no amount of pleading will help you. You're coming to the station with me!"

"Mmph! Mmph!"

The boy—Kenta—struggled frantically.

Officer Jenny didn't bother removing the tape. Instead, she slapped a pair of cold handcuffs onto his wrists and stuffed him into the vehicle.

Watching the motorcycle disappear into the distance, Ethan felt nothing in particular.

To him, this had merely been a brief incident in life—a small problem solved along the way and hardly worth remembering.

Before leaving, however, Officer Jenny had casually provided a useful piece of information.

Apparently, near the outskirts of Fuchsia City, a massive bridge was being built across the sea, connecting directly to Sunny Town on the opposite shore.

Taking that route would save a tremendous amount of time.

Moreover, Sunny Town was very close to Celadon City and Saffron City, making it the perfect opportunity to retrieve the Silph Scope that Sabrina had left for him.

Since the route was convenient and challenging Viridian Gym was not urgent, Ethan immediately changed his destination once again and set out for Saffron City.

Passing through bustling Fuchsia City and walking among shopping centers and towering buildings, Ethan eventually reached the northern coastline.

Stretching across the ocean before him was a bridge so vast that its end could not be seen.

Supported by hundreds of concrete pillars, the colossal structure extended for nearly twenty kilometers.

Traveling on foot would take until nightfall to reach the other side.

Arriving at the entrance, Ethan saw several barriers blocking the way.

Although the bridge had not officially been completed, only motor vehicles were prohibited. Cyclists could still travel freely across it.

As Ethan approached, a security guard leaned out of the gatehouse.

"Oh? Sir, are you planning to cross the bridge?"

When Ethan nodded, the guard smiled apologetically.

"I'm afraid that won't be possible. The bridge is too long, so pedestrians aren't allowed to cross. If you want to get to the other side, you'll need a bicycle."

Ethan vaguely remembered this rule.

Still unwilling to give up, he asked,

"Really? There's no way to make an exception?"

"Sorry."

The guard gave a helpless smile.

"Rules are rules. My hands are tied."

Ethan sighed.

Just as he was considering riding Gyarados across the sea instead, he noticed a group of young men and women approaching.

Wearing sunglasses, sporting dyed hair, and dressed like delinquents, they rode heavily modified bicycles at full speed.

Some waved flags while others shouted loudly.

The entire group resembled a flock of noisy crows.

"Ugh."

The security guard sighed.

"There they are again—the bicycle biker gang. Making noise every single day. They're such a headache."

Even though Ethan vaguely remembered this part of the story, he still couldn't help commenting,

"Wait... bicycle biker gangs actually exist? That's kind of lacking in prestige."

"Well, they're just kids."

The guard shrugged.

"Not many seventeen- or eighteen-year-olds can afford expensive motorcycles. Though at this rate, it's probably only a matter of time before they graduate into an actual motorcycle gang."

Looking closer, Ethan realized the guard was right.

Most of them appeared to be around seventeen or eighteen years old.

"If they cause so much trouble, why doesn't anyone stop them?"

"Because there's nothing we can really do."

The guard shook his head.

"They're loud, sure, but all they do is ride bicycles back and forth across the bridge. Technically they're not damaging anything."

He sighed again.

"And they're all local kids. They're afraid of the police, but if we actually arrested them for disturbing public order, their parents would probably surround the police station the next day."

"That does sound troublesome."

As they spoke, the group arrived.

Seeing their bicycles, Ethan suddenly got an idea.

He stepped forward and blocked their path.

The cyclists stopped immediately, frowning.

"Hey! Who are you?"

"Why are you blocking us?"

Ethan took out a Poké Ball and smiled.

"I need a bicycle to get across the bridge. How about a battle?"

He pointed toward the bicycles.

"If I win, lend me one."

A young man with a bright red flame-shaped pompadour—clearly their leader—removed his sunglasses.

"Hah!? Someone actually wants to challenge us?"

He laughed mockingly.

"You've got some nerve. Haven't you ever heard of us?"

"Sorry."

Ethan shrugged.

"I'm afraid I haven't. So, are you going to battle or not?"

"Of course we are!"

The young man looked Ethan up and down before jerking a thumb over his shoulder toward a crude battle arena in the distance.

"We're the ones who challenge other people, not the other way around!"

A grin spread across his face.

"If you're really not afraid of getting crushed, then follow us over there!"

( End of Chapter )
