"Zapdos!? It really is Zapdos!?"

"Why is a Legendary Pokémon here!?"

People shouted in disbelief. To them, Zapdos's sudden appearance was even more eye-catching than the gigantic Tentacruel itself!

Just as some people were about to abandon the fight against the Tentacool and chase after Zapdos instead, they suddenly saw it fly at incredible speed toward the giant Tentacruel and launch an attack!

"Huh!? It's attacking that giant Tentacruel!?"

Everyone froze for a moment before finally realizing something.

"Does that Zapdos already have a Trainer!?"

Following the direction Zapdos had flown from, the crowd searched around and eventually discovered a man standing atop the television tower, wearing a mask and draped in a black cloak.

Wrapped up so completely, nobody could determine the man's appearance or age. Due to the distance, all they could see beneath the oddly amusing Gengar mask was his mouth moving as if he were giving commands.

On the rooftop, Ethan stood with one foot on the edge of the concrete ledge and glanced nervously downward.

"Hey, Haunter… I think I might actually be afraid of heights. Otherwise, why are my legs shaking this badly…?"

From Ethan's shadow, only Haunter's eyes were visible. It sighed helplessly.

"If you're afraid of heights, then step back a little! Or better yet, stop looking down and pay more attention to Zapdos's battle!"

"Nope!" Ethan replied seriously. "Only by facing fear directly can you truly overcome it. But speaking of which, if I accidentally fall, you'll save me, right? Hey, don't suddenly go silent! Say something!"

Haunter floated out from the shadow, pretending to scratch its ear.

"Honestly, I never realized you had such a talkative side before. Don't worry, I'll definitely catch you when you're exactly 0.01 meters above the ground so you can personally experience the terrifying thrill of the Haunter-brand free-fall ride!"

Perhaps Haunter's dark humor helped ease Ethan's nerves. He took a quick look at his other Pokémon and saw them battling countless Tentacool under Misty's command.

"Well done, Misty. Let my Pokémon farm some more experience points!"

Ethan silently praised her. Just as he had predicted earlier, countless people were staring in his direction, and some were even recording both him and Zapdos on their phones!

Good thing I prepared ahead of time. Otherwise, my identity would definitely have been exposed in front of everyone.

In the distance, Zapdos circled around the giant Tentacruel while streams of electricity crackled from its wings, striking Tentacruel repeatedly.

Tentacruel naturally refused to back down. The red jewel on its head continuously fired laser-like beams toward Zapdos.

Perhaps due to its giant transformation, Tentacruel's resistance had increased tremendously. Even after enduring so many electric attacks, it still looked relatively unharmed and showed no signs of retreating!

Seeing this, Ethan shouted, "Zapdos! Stop using Thunder Shock! Use Thunderbolt instead!"

Whether his voice truly carried through the wind to Zapdos's ears or not, the next second Zapdos suddenly unleashed its power. Countless dazzling bolts of electricity exploded outward, carrying overwhelming electrical force as they spread in every direction with indiscriminate destruction!

This time, the giant Tentacruel finally let out a painful cry. Meanwhile, the electricity traveled through the seawater beneath it, striking countless Tentacool nearby.

Immediately, Tentacool after Tentacool flipped upside down from the shock and drifted helplessly across the waves, completely unconscious.

At that moment, one of Tentacruel's tentacles suddenly lifted Meowth out from somewhere unknown.

Using its poison to control Meowth's nervous system, Tentacruel borrowed Meowth's mouth to convey its anger to humanity.

"We are Tentacruel and Tentacool! You humans destroyed our home, so we will destroy your city in revenge! You will experience the sorrow of losing your home as well!"

As it spoke, its gaze suddenly shifted toward Zapdos.

"And you! You are a Pokémon just like us, so why are you siding with humans!?"

Unfortunately, with Zapdos's prideful nature, its only response was another blast of electricity!

The giant Tentacruel became furious beyond measure. Dozens of tentacles whipped wildly through the air, yet none could even touch a single feather on Zapdos's body.

Realizing it could not harm Zapdos directly, Tentacruel suddenly remembered Ethan, who had been commanding the battle earlier. Its massive body immediately began moving toward the television tower.

Along the way, any Pokémon that dared block its path was mercilessly crushed beneath it. Seeing this, Ethan knew he absolutely could not allow it to get any closer.

"Zapdos, stop it! Use Drill Peck!"

Zapdos began gliding through the air, building up speed while spinning rapidly. Its long beak glowed brightly as it drilled straight toward the giant Tentacruel!

Although Tentacruel wildly swung its tentacles in defense, it still failed to stop Zapdos's attack. As the glowing beak slammed violently into its body, the giant Tentacruel let out an enormous scream of agony!

WOOOAAAHHH!!!

That cry seemed to shake heaven and earth alike, fully revealing Zapdos's power to every Trainer in the city.

"S-So powerful!!"

"So this is the strength of a Legendary Pokémon!?"

People stared blankly at the battle between the two Pokémon. Compared to them, their own Pokémon battles felt like children's games. Even Ash and Brock watched with faces full of admiration.

After all, what Trainer could resist the allure of a Legendary Pokémon?

Yet despite suffering such a devastating hit, the giant Tentacruel still did not collapse. Once it got close enough, it suddenly fired a beam of light directly toward the top of the television tower.

After the flash faded, Ethan felt the ground beneath him tremble violently. The section he stood on suddenly collapsed.

The next second, along with countless shattered bricks and debris, Ethan's body helplessly plummeted toward the ground. But at the critical moment, Zapdos swooped down at incredible speed and caught him securely.

"Hah…" Ethan let out a long breath. "For a second there, I thought my heart had stopped. This Tentacruel actually knows to capture the king before the thieves. Pretty impressive, honestly! But anyway, thanks a lot, Zapdos. You acted so unwilling earlier, but you still came to save me."

"..."

After a brief silence, for one fleeting moment, Zapdos genuinely considered dropping him again.

At that moment, several more beams of light swept toward them. Zapdos hurriedly maneuvered to dodge them, but carrying Ethan greatly reduced its speed. Several attacks barely missed them by inches.

"Damn it, this won't work." Ethan crouched on Zapdos's back and said, "Use that move!"

Zapdos lightly nodded.

Then, to everyone's shock, Ethan suddenly leapt backward and voluntarily jumped off Zapdos's back!

Under the stunned gazes of the crowd, it looked as though he had invisible wings. Riding the wind itself, he flew gracefully through the air before landing once again atop the television tower!

This time, the people could no longer suppress their emotions and cried out in astonishment.

"My God! How did he do that!?"
