"So next, are we going to dive into the water?" Ash asked.

Brock gently stirred the seawater with his hand and said worriedly, "But if we dive down and swim for a long time only to find a dead end—or a sealed hatch we can't open—we'll be in serious trouble."

"In a situation like this, of course we rely on Water-type Pokémon!" Misty said as she took out a Poké Ball and released a Goldeen into the water. "Goldeen, go check below and find a route we can use to escape!"

Goldeen nodded before diving beneath the surface, disappearing in the blink of an eye.

However, even after a long while, it still had not returned.

"So it really won't work either...?"

Misty frowned. Just as she was about to dive down personally to investigate, ripples suddenly appeared on the water's surface.

A moment later, as the water parted, the first thing that emerged was a long-haired head that looked exactly like a drowned ghost!

Because everyone had been concentrating so intensely, they were all startled. Misty let out a scream and immediately hid behind Ethan, clutching tightly to the corner of his clothes while trembling.

Compared to the girls, Ethan was much calmer. Forcing himself to stay composed, he waited for the figure to rise further before realizing it was actually Team Rocket's trio stacked on top of one another, being pushed upward by Goldeen from beneath the water.

"Don't worry, everyone. It's just Team Rocket."

Ethan gently patted Misty's shoulder, and after confirming that it really was Team Rocket, she finally let out a sigh of relief.

"What the heck!? Why were they underwater?"

Jessie, James, and Meowth lay sprawled across the floor, coughing up mouthfuls of seawater. Just moments ago they had looked half-dead with rolled-back eyes, but the instant they heard Misty's voice, they suddenly sprang back to life.

Jessie shouted, "Talk about enemies crossing paths! Why is it you people again!?"

For a moment, the atmosphere became tense. Both sides immediately pulled out Poké Balls. Jessie and James released Ekans and Koffing, while Ash and Brock sent out Squirtle and Geodude.

"Hey, hey!" Misty quickly stepped between them to stop things from escalating. "Get this straight first—my Goldeen just saved your lives! And look around you! This is not the time to fight! If we don't figure out a way to escape soon, we're all going to die here!"

Even so, Ash, Brock, and the Team Rocket trio continued glaring fiercely at each other, unwilling to back down.

At that moment, James suddenly noticed Ethan standing nearby and pointed at him in surprise.

"Ah! It's you! Thanks again for earlier. Otherwise, I really would've been tricked by that Magikarp salesman!"

"Idiot! We're supposed to be villains! Why are you thanking people!?" Jessie smacked James hard on the head before turning toward Ethan suspiciously. "But wait... who is this guy anyway?"

James's face reddened, and he did not dare explain the truth. If Jessie found out he had almost spent both of their advance salaries on a Magikarp that only flopped around uselessly, she would probably kill him herself.

Ethan stepped forward and tried to calm everyone down.

"Let's just call a truce for now. Misty's right—we really don't have much time left."

With Ethan mediating, both sides finally recalled their Pokémon.

After asking what had happened, Ethan and the others learned that Team Rocket had also failed to escape the S.S. Anne in time. When the ship flipped over, they panicked and broke through part of the hull in an attempt to escape, causing seawater to flood inside and sweep them away in the current.

If Goldeen had not happened to pass by earlier, the three of them would probably already have become corpses at the bottom of the sea.

"Hmph, fine then." Jessie placed her hands on her hips arrogantly. "But don't get the wrong idea! This is only a temporary truce! It doesn't mean we'll spare you in the future!"

"By the way..." Ethan asked cautiously, "you don't have any Water-type Pokémon, do you? We're about to escape through the sea, so are you really in a position to act this proud?"

"Uh..."

The expressions of the Team Rocket trio changed instantly as they finally realized the problem. Their attitudes immediately shifted completely, and they all bowed apologetically.

"Ah! Sorry! We were wrong!"

Ethan smiled and shook his head. No matter how heavy the atmosphere became, whenever those three were around, things somehow always turned absurdly comedic and lively again.

Now that the Team Rocket issue had been settled, they could finally discuss a proper plan.

Brock rubbed his chin thoughtfully and asked, "So how exactly are we supposed to get out of here?"

"Leave it to me!" Misty proudly puffed out her chest. "I once built a model of the S.S. Anne, so I know its structure very well. Since the lower route is completely blocked off, we'll have to go upward instead. I remember that the hull beneath the engine room is relatively thin. If we break a hole there, we should be able to escape!"

Ash and the others nodded in agreement, but Ethan was the only one who objected.

"We can't break through the bottom of the ship!"

"Huh?" Misty asked in confusion. "Why not?"

Ethan explained, "If we break through the hull, the seawater will rush downward from above. The pressure will become extremely intense, and swimming against that current will be very difficult. Also, once the ship is breached, the pressure balance will collapse and seawater will rapidly flood the entire vessel. If we fail to escape successfully through the hull, we probably won't have enough time left to try another method."

Misty suddenly understood. "I see... Then do you have another idea?"

Ethan continued, "Since the lower sections are already flooded anyway, why not let the Water-type Pokémon break through one of the submerged walls below and lead us out together? That way, the water pressure we face directly should be much smaller."

"But if we do that..." Misty hesitated, "it'll definitely take longer than escaping upward. I'm worried we won't be able to hold our breath that long."

"Don't worry about that. I looked outside through the window earlier. Although the ship has sunk, the seabed in this area is relatively shallow, so we're actually not too far from the surface."

As he spoke, Ethan pointed toward the seawater at their feet.

"And besides, look at that."

Everyone looked down and saw several plastic bottles floating in the water.

The drinks inside had already been consumed, leaving the bottles filled with air, and somehow they had drifted here.

Ethan explained, "If we hold the bottles in our mouths, even taking just one extra breath could help us last several more minutes than normal. And since the water here isn't very deep, we don't need to worry about the sea pressure crushing the bottles."

If anyone else had raised objections, Misty might have insisted on her own plan. But since it was Ethan speaking, she immediately became his biggest supporter.

As for the others, they exchanged uncertain glances, unable to make up their minds. Still, since Misty supported Ethan's idea, there was nothing left to argue about.

"Alright then," Ash said firmly. "Let's do it this way!"

(End of Chapter)
