After recalling all of his Pokémon except Kirlia, Ethan gently woke Misty and Ash.

Ash stretched lazily, apparently having slept quite well.

"Is it already morning?"

Misty rubbed her eyes drowsily.

"I think I just had a dream. In the dream, we were attacked by a ghost."

Ethan gave a bitter smile.

"That wasn't a dream. It really happened. And Brock has disappeared too."

"What!?" Ash and Misty were instantly shocked awake. "He got taken away!?"

Thinking back to the related events from the anime, Ethan nodded.

"But I don't think he's in any life-threatening danger. Still, no matter what, we should find him as quickly as possible."

The three cautiously looked toward the wooden cabin in front of them and slowly walked inside, only to discover it was completely empty. The only thing inside was the damp, rotten smell of mildew, making it impossible to tell how many years the cabin had stood there.

Their footsteps creaked eerily against the wooden floorboards. Misty peeked out nervously from behind Ethan, fearfully glancing around.

"Huh? Brock isn't here?"

After thinking carefully for a moment, Ethan turned to the other two.

"Come on. Let's head back to the Maiden's Peak Shrine!"

By the time they returned to Maiden's Peak town, it was already midnight.

Stars filled the night sky. The noise and excitement of the festival had long since faded, leaving the entire town strangely quiet. The cold air seeped through their clothes, causing everyone to shiver involuntarily.

All the festival-goers had already dispersed, leaving only garbage scattered across the streets to be cleaned up at dawn.

When they arrived at the Maiden's Shrine and pushed open the doors, they saw Brock lying unconscious on the floor.

But aside from him, Gastly was nowhere to be seen.

In truth, Ethan understood that with Gastly's bizarre movements and elusive nature, actually finding it would be extremely difficult.

"Brock, wake up!"

Ash vigorously shook Brock. Only after being shaken for quite a while did Brock finally weakly regain consciousness. Even then, he continued mumbling softly:

"I really want to see her…"

Ethan quickly crouched down to examine him.

"He has dark circles under his eyes, his clothes are disheveled, his精神 is weak, and he's noticeably thinner than before. His stamina and life energy were probably drained excessively!"

Compared to simple-minded Ash, Misty, being a girl, was slightly more mature mentally. Although she did not fully understand such matters, she could vaguely grasp part of what Ethan meant.

Her face flushed red.

"Was Gastly the one who did this?"

"No." Ethan shook his head. "I think it was the real ghost that absorbed his energy. Gastly was probably just helping her."

"The real ghost?" Misty exclaimed in surprise. "You mean that woman actually exists?"

"Of course," Ethan answered firmly. "Otherwise, Gastly wouldn't have brought Brock to a place like this."

Misty asked again, "Then why would she do something like that?"

After thinking for a moment, Ethan guessed,

"She's existed for two thousand years. Even ghosts need energy to maintain their existence. That's probably why, during every summer festival, she absorbs the life force of men like this."

Seeing Misty about to continue questioning him, Ethan quickly interrupted.

"Alright, enough questions. Let's hurry and bring Brock back to the Pokémon Center."

Inside the Pokémon Center, after Nurse Joy examined Brock and confirmed that he was simply suffering from extreme exhaustion, everyone finally relaxed.

However, no matter how much they questioned him about what had happened after Gastly took him away, Brock only looked confused, seemingly unable to remember anything at all.

After laying Brock down on a bed in the dormitory and asking Ash to take good care of him, Ethan left the Pokémon Center alone.

Even though Gastly was extremely troublesome, Ethan had already made up his mind to capture it.

As a wild Gastly that had likely lived for countless years, its abilities were naturally extraordinary. More importantly, just like Team Rocket's Meowth, it could actually speak human language!

That alone made it incredibly valuable. If he captured Gastly, communicating with his Pokémon in the future would become far easier, no longer relying solely on guesswork and mutual understanding.

But where could Gastly be right now?

It was already two in the morning, and the night was at its darkest.

After looking around for a while, Ethan finally decided to trust his instincts and head toward the Maiden's Rock cliff by the seaside to try his luck.

Following the mountain path out of town, he soon arrived at his destination. But just like before, aside from the crashing waves and the salty sea breeze in the air, there was nothing there.

"Gastly, are you here!?"

Ethan shouted loudly, but his voice only echoed into the distance without receiving any response.

"So you really aren't here?" Ethan muttered to himself as he walked toward Maiden's Rock. "Then I guess it won't matter even if I destroy this rock now, right?"

"Hey, hey!" A voice suddenly came from behind him. "I can't pretend I didn't hear that!"

Smiling, Ethan turned around and saw Gastly floating there.

"Gastly, let's battle!"

"Oh?" Gastly's expression became serious. "You want to capture me?"

Ethan took a Poké Ball from his waist and enlarged it in his hand.

"Of course. I'm aiming for the Indigo League and searching for companions. Your abilities would definitely become a great asset to me!"

As he spoke, Ethan threw the Poké Ball. After a flash of red light, a fully bristled Pokémon appeared in front of Gastly.

It was Jolteon!

"What? So it's Jolteon." Gastly sounded somewhat surprised, but more than anything, relieved.

Ethan asked,

"What's wrong? Are you surprised to see Jolteon?"

Gastly replied,

"I thought you were going to use Zapdos against me. Honestly, if I had to fight a Legendary Pokémon like that, even I wouldn't have much chance of winning."

Ethan chuckled inwardly.

As if I don't want to.

Do you think I'd tell you I still can't properly command it!?

"Relax," Ethan said coolly with his arms crossed. "Even if Jolteon loses, I still won't use Zapdos against you! Enough talking—let's battle! Jolteon, use Thunder Shock!"

Crackling electricity flickered across Jolteon's body before gathering into a bolt of lightning that shot toward Gastly.

But the moment Jolteon attacked, a black sphere of energy condensed beside Gastly and collided with the electricity.

It was the Ghost-type move Shadow Ball!

However, Thunder Shock's power was clearly inferior to Shadow Ball's. The dark sphere pierced through the electric attack and continued flying toward Jolteon.

"Jolteon, dodge it quickly! Then use Pin Missile!"

With its incredible speed, Jolteon easily avoided the incoming Shadow Ball. It leapt to the side, and the fur across its body stiffened like steel needles as wave after wave of sharp spikes shot toward Gastly!
