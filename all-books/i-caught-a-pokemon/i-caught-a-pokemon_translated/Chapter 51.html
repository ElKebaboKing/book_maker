Ethan walked into the warehouse and found Looker half-lying on the ground behind several cargo crates deep inside.

His condition could only be described as miserable.

Not only was he seriously injured, but even through the bandages wrapped around his abdomen, fresh blood continued seeping out, staining his suit red.

When Looker first saw the mask on Ethan's face, he was visibly startled. But after noticing Kirlia standing nearby, he quickly calmed down, and a faint smile finally appeared on his pale and exhausted face.

"You… finally came… cough cough…"

Ethan immediately took out some Potion spray and applied it to his wounds.

As a qualified trainer, Ethan always carried various medicines in his backpack, including Potions, Antidotes, Burn Heals, and Paralyze Heals, precisely so he would not be helpless in situations like this.

"How did you get hurt this badly?"

Ignoring the blood staining his hands, Ethan frowned and took out fresh bandages to rewrap Looker's lower abdomen.

Even though this was the first time he had ever seen so much blood, at such a life-or-death moment, he forced himself to endure the overwhelming smell of blood and his discomfort while carefully finishing the bandaging.

"I got ambushed… caught completely off guard." Looker's lips had already turned pale. Enduring the pain, he released his two Pokémon as well. "Those two are injured too… I'll have to trouble you again."

His Croagunk and Growlithe were both in terrible condition. Not only were they covered in wounds, but they had also both been poisoned.

Taking out Antidotes, Ethan carefully fed the medicine to them. After bandaging their injuries as well, he returned them to their Poké Balls.

These medicines could only serve as emergency treatment. If they truly wanted to recover, they would still need proper professional care. Compared to outside, the Poké Balls were much safer and would allow them to rest without worsening their injuries.

Taking out a water bottle, Ethan helped Looker drink a little water. Once his complexion improved slightly, Ethan asked,

"Who attacked you this badly? Team Rocket?"

"Who else could it be?" After the treatment, Looker looked much better, or at least he had stopped bleeding. "But unlike ordinary grunts, the ones who attacked me this time were Team Rocket's elite squad. They call themselves the Matori Matrix. They're an extremely troublesome group. I barely managed to shake them off."

The Matori Matrix!?

Ethan thought for a moment before shaking his head.

He vaguely remembered hearing the name before, but the details were long forgotten.

"I should take you to a Pokémon Center first."

"No!" Looker said weakly. "Those people are still searching for me everywhere, and Team Rocket has informants all over the city. The moment I show myself, they'll find me."

"Then what are you planning to do?" Ethan asked.

Looker glanced behind him as though he had already planned an escape route.

"Behind here is the freight train station. I calculated it earlier—there's a train passing through roughly every ten minutes or so. I'm planning to use one to leave the city first."

After pondering briefly, Ethan still voiced his concern.

"I think it would be safer to let your colleagues come pick you up, or at least contact the police."

"Forget it." Looker forced a bitter smile. "Team Rocket has operated in this city for years. Who knows whether there are spies among the police? It's too risky. Besides, I already contacted headquarters earlier. Even if they send someone, it'll still take time."

Although Ethan still felt uneasy, since Looker had already made up his mind, there was little more he could say.

Supporting Looker, Ethan prepared to leave the warehouse. But suddenly, accompanied by a woman's arrogant laughter, two people jumped through the window!

"Ohohohoho!" Jessie looked incredibly smug. "Hey kid! We've followed you for so long, and we finally found our chance!"

"Jessie!?" Ethan instantly became alert. "Instead of bothering Ash, why are you targeting me?"

"Obviously because the Boss ordered us to eliminate you! Don't think wearing a mask means we won't recognize you! Go, Ekans!"

As Jessie spoke, she threw out her Poké Ball. Yet after waiting several seconds, James still had not joined in.

Enraged, Jessie grabbed him by the collar and shook him violently.

"Idiot! Stop hesitating and hurry up already!"

James sighed helplessly and reluctantly released his Pokémon.

"Go… Koffing…"

Seeing the large red "R" symbols on their chests, Looker shook his head helplessly.

"So even you've been targeted by them now…"

Ethan also sighed internally. Although he had long prepared himself for conflict with Team Rocket, he had not expected things to escalate this quickly.

Jessie and James had crossed paths with Ethan before, but they had never actually fought him, so naturally they had no understanding of his true strength.

Unfortunately, Ethan had no time to waste on them right now. He immediately threw out two Poké Balls from his waist.

"Charmander, Squirtle! I'm leaving this place to you two—we're going ahead!"

"You think you're getting away?" Meowth suddenly appeared from nowhere and blocked Ethan's path. "Take this—Fury Swipes!"

Leaping forward, Meowth slashed toward Ethan's face with gleaming claws!

"Nice one, Meowth!" Jessie cried out excitedly.

However, before her words had even fully left her mouth, Meowth suddenly stopped midair as if it had slammed into an invisible wall. Then it was blasted backward like a cannonball, crashing through several wooden crates!

Kirlia slowly lowered its arm, staring coldly at Meowth.

"Damn it!" Jessie clenched her fists and shouted, "Ekans! Koffing! Stop standing around and attack already!"

The two Pokémon immediately launched their attacks, but in the next instant, Charmander and Squirtle blocked them with Ember and Water Gun!

Taking advantage of the opportunity, Ethan quickly escaped through the warehouse's back entrance with Looker and arrived beside the railway tracks.

Just as he was about to set Looker down nearby, a rocket suddenly flew toward them at incredible speed!

In that split second, as ordinary humans, neither Ethan nor Looker could react in time.

Yet at the critical moment, a strange gust of wind suddenly wrapped around their bodies and hurled them sideways, allowing them to narrowly evade the attack.

The explosion echoed throughout the entire freight station. Shattered rocks and metal fragments scattered everywhere, only to be blocked completely by that mysterious wind.

"Oh? You actually dodged?" A deep voice sounded from a nearby rooftop. "How exactly did you manage that?"

Ethan looked up and saw five men and women standing atop the roof. They wore black Team Rocket uniforms along with berets, staring down at them coldly.

Seeing that these Team Rocket members carried an aura completely different from ordinary grunts, Ethan asked in a low voice,

"Who are you people!?"

"Fair enough. We should introduce ourselves properly, otherwise you won't even know who killed you before reaching the afterlife!" The leader was a burly man with an eyepatch over his right eye. He grinned viciously. "My name is Kane, and we are Team Rocket Executive Matori's elite squad—the Matori Matrix!"

(End of Chapter)
