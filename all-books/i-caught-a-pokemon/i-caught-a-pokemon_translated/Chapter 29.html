"Damn it!" Handsome slammed his fist against the railing of the corridor. "That bastard still managed to escape!"

Before Ethan could speak, Handsome explained on his own, "That guy, Lambda, is the high-ranking Team Rocket executive I've been tracking this whole time. I thought I'd finally capture him today and cut off one of Team Rocket's major assets, but in the end, it still all fell apart!"

"You already did an amazing job by stopping this hijacking," Ethan comforted him before suddenly changing the subject. "But now isn't the time to blame yourself. The top priority is dealing with the ordinary Team Rocket grunts downstairs!"

"You're right." Handsome nodded seriously. "Then let's move quickly!"

A faint breeze swept across the sea, and the once-calm ocean began to churn with waves. Looking into the distance, they could already see the sky changing color as enormous dark storm clouds slowly approached the S.S. Anne.

Inside the first-floor hall, the passengers soon managed to defeat Team Rocket through sheer teamwork.

The Team Rocket grunts scattered and fled in all directions, disappearing in the blink of an eye.

Handsome could only watch helplessly as they escaped.

After all, there was no way he could arrest so many Team Rocket members by himself. So after exchanging contact information with Ethan and giving him his phone number, he quietly left as well.

At that moment, a massive wave slammed into the ship, causing the entire vessel to shake violently. Caught off guard, the passengers lost their footing and fell backward.

Ethan reacted quickly and grabbed onto a nearby table to steady himself. Before he could even look up, however, a petite figure crashed straight into his arms.

It was Misty!

Ethan quickly wrapped an arm around her waist to keep her from falling and asked with concern, "Are you alright?"

Blushing slightly, Misty looked up at him and gently shook her head.

As waves battered the S.S. Anne, the ship swayed violently from side to side. Under such terrible weather conditions, the stabilization system was practically useless. Panicked passengers rushed toward the windows to look outside, only to find that the sky had become as dark as night itself. The only things visible were flashes of lightning and roaring thunder.

"It's a storm!"

Someone shouted, instantly sending the crowd into panic.

Helping Misty back to her feet, Ethan sighed. "With weather this severe, there's no way this ship can return safely."

At the other side of the hall, the captain—who had disappeared since the chaos began—was suddenly dragged out by the passengers. Though panic was written across his face, he still tried to sound calm.

"...By the time I realized it, the ship was already surrounded by the storm. But please don't worry! The S.S. Anne has been through countless voyages and is extremely large. I guarantee it absolutely will not sink!"

The moment he finished speaking, another gigantic wave smashed into the ship. Everyone was thrown to the floor again. But when they got back up, they saw that the captain had already run to the end of the emergency passageway and climbed into a lifeboat!

"Everyone!" the captain shouted from the lifeboat, repeating himself, "I guarantee this ship absolutely won't sink! Please remain calm and stay where you are!"

But after seeing the captain himself boarding a lifeboat, nobody believed him anymore. The passengers scrambled toward the lifeboats in desperation.

Women screamed, men shouted, children cried—the entire scene instantly descended into complete chaos.

"We need to leave too," Ethan said, motioning to Misty and Brock. Just as he was about to move, he realized someone was missing. "Huh? Where's Ash?"

Misty and Brock hurriedly looked around and finally spotted him beside a machine in the corner.

"There he is!"

When Ethan and the others reached Ash, they found him exchanging Pokémon with the businessman at the transfer machine.

So he finally figured it out...

As the businessman operated the machine, he noticed Ethan and looked surprised. "Oh, it's you!? In such a short time, your Ralts has already evolved into Kirlia. It must be much stronger now than before, right?"

Ethan nodded politely without saying much.

At that moment, Ash took back Butterfree's Poké Ball. But before he could hold it firmly, the S.S. Anne lurched violently once more. His hand slipped, and the Poké Ball rolled away into a nearby corridor.

"Butterfree!"

Ash shouted as he chased after it. Worried about him, Misty and Brock followed into the corridor as well.

Ethan glanced back at the lifeboats. If he wanted to escape safely, now was definitely the best opportunity.

But realistically speaking, with waves this massive, even the lifeboats might not survive safely.

Shaking his head, he chose not to leave alone. Instead, he followed Misty and Brock into the corridor.

After running dozens of steps forward, he finally caught up to Ash. Just as Ash picked up the Poké Ball and was about to stand, an unprecedented force suddenly struck the S.S. Anne.

The next second, Ethan felt the world spin violently as his body was thrown into the wall.

The impact hurt badly. After a long moment, Ethan finally stood up with Kirlia's support, only to discover that the entire ship had completely flipped upside down.

And he could clearly feel the ship continuously sinking. Amid the cracking noises around them, seawater was rapidly pouring into the vessel.

So it finally sank...

Beside him, Ash and the others were also completely disoriented from the fall. Ethan quickly helped wake them up one by one, and once they understood the situation, all of their faces changed dramatically.

Another tremor shook the ship, and the S.S. Anne seemed to settle completely at the bottom of the ocean.

The corridor lights still illuminated the cabin, meaning the ship's power system had not yet fully failed.

"What do we do now?" Ash pressed himself against a ship window, his face filled with shock. "The entire ship has sunk to the bottom of the sea!"

"Calm down," Ethan reassured him. "We need to find a way out immediately. We have to escape before this place is completely flooded!"

Fortunately, because there was still a large amount of trapped air inside the ship, water pressure prevented the seawater from flooding every part of the vessel immediately, giving them some extra time.

The group hurried down the corridor. Unfortunately, because the ship had flipped over, what used to be the ceiling had become the floor, and many paths were now blocked. Combined with the rising water, moving forward became increasingly difficult.

And when they finally reached the staircase, they stopped nervously.

The entire stairwell ahead had already been completely submerged underwater.
