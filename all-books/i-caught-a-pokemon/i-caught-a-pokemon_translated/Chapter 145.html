After meeting up with Chloe and the others, the group headed to the Ice Field to watch Ash's match.

As the tournament progressed and the number of matches decreased, attendance at each arena continued to rise.

Starting from the third round, the Grass, Rock, Water, and Ice Fields were all packed with spectators.

After finally finding a few empty seats, Ethan and the others had barely sat down before Ash's battle began.

To everyone's surprise, however, Ash lost the first round almost immediately.

His Squirtle seemed out of form—or perhaps the freezing conditions of the Ice Field had affected its movements. It was completely overwhelmed by Justin's Pokémon and barely managed to fight back.

Fortunately, Ash quickly adjusted his strategy.

His Kingler defeated two Pokémon in succession before Pikachu finished off Justin's Arcanine, securing the overall victory.

Overall, Ash had displayed superior skill.

Compared to the previous round, his performance was much steadier.

When Ash left the battlefield and met Ethan, the two exchanged smiles.

Having both advanced to the next round, there was no need for words.

Everything was understood.

To celebrate, Ethan decided not to eat at the Players' Village cafeteria that afternoon.

Instead, he took everyone to an upscale restaurant for a lavish meal.

After lunch, they returned toward the residential area.

But while they were still some distance away, they noticed a large crowd gathered outside Ethan's villa.

Before anyone could understand what was happening, the moment the reporters spotted Ethan, they surged toward him like sharks smelling blood.

"Ethan! I'm a reporter from Saffron City TV! Could I ask you a few questions?"

"I'm from Celadon TV! Ethan, how do you feel after defeating one of the tournament favorites?"

"Ethan! I'm from Vermilion TV..."

"Ethan! Ethan!"

The noisy crowd completely pushed Ash and the others aside.

Watching the scene unfold, everyone looked utterly confused.

Looking at Ash—who wasn't receiving even the slightest attention—Brock scratched his head in bewilderment.

"What's going on? Why are so many reporters suddenly trying to interview Ethan?"

Because they had spent the entire day watching battles in person, they were actually the last ones to learn what had happened.

They had no idea that the Rock Field match had shattered viewership records.

Nor did they realize that Ethan had suddenly become the hottest topic of the tournament, overshadowing every other contestant.

These reporters, relying on years of professional instinct, all wanted to be the first to secure an exclusive interview.

"I'm sorry, everyone."

The constant camera flashes were nearly blinding him.

Facing the sea of microphones thrust toward his face, Ethan still couldn't understand why they had all suddenly come looking for him.

Even so, he politely declined.

"I appreciate your interest, but I won't be accepting any interviews at this time."

The reporter from Saffron City looked genuinely surprised.

"Huh? Why would you refuse?"

Ethan answered calmly,

"Until I achieve even better results in this tournament, I don't want to make any comments about myself or any of my opponents."

"I hope you can understand."

He understood a simple truth—

The higher someone was praised, the harder they could fall.

Besides, he had never cared about fame or vanity.

Otherwise, when he had defeated the Giant Tentacruel, he wouldn't have worn a mask to hide his identity.

The reporters clearly weren't satisfied.

Having interviewed countless public figures over the years, they weren't about to give up because of a few polite words.

Just as they prepared to continue pressing Ethan...

More than a dozen intimidating bikers suddenly strode over.

Without saying another word, they physically separated the reporters from Ethan.

"Move aside!"

"Didn't you hear what our boss said? He isn't giving interviews!"

"Stop bothering him and let him get some rest!"

The reporters could usually act aggressively toward celebrities or ordinary people.

But faced with a group of fierce-looking bikers, they muttered a few complaints before reluctantly retreating.

After unlocking the door, Ethan entered the villa and invited Misa and Masha inside.

Only then did they explain what had happened.

"I never expected to receive this much attention..."

"This was completely unexpected."

To Ethan, it had simply been another ordinary battle.

Aside from Serena Yuto being more popular—and somewhat stronger—than his previous opponents, the match hadn't felt any different.

In fact...

If he had known the television ratings would become so outrageous, he might have intentionally dropped a couple of rounds just to avoid attracting so much attention.

After all, becoming famous too early would only encourage the remaining contestants to study his team and prepare specifically to counter him.

Outside the villa, the bikers stood guard, refusing to let anyone approach.

They were genuinely thrilled by Ethan's growing popularity.

After all...

The more famous Ethan became, the wiser their decision to recognize him as their boss appeared.

While everyone was chatting inside, one of the biker members suddenly rushed through the front door.

"Boss! Another reporter wants to see you!"

Before Ethan could answer, Masha slammed the table.

"What the heck?!"

"Didn't I tell you guys not to let any reporters come near here?"

"They're all supposed to be chased away!"

The young biker hesitated.

"But..."

"She said she'd already made an appointment with the boss."

"She claims she's here because of that appointment."

"Huh?"

Masha froze and immediately looked toward Ethan.

"An appointment?"

Ethan frowned.

He couldn't remember making plans with any reporter.

But after a moment's thought...

He immediately realized who it had to be.

Alexa.

After all, she was the only reporter he'd ever actually spoken with.

The fact that even Alexa had returned made Ethan realize just how significant the aftermath of today's battle had become.

After thinking it over...

He knew he couldn't keep avoiding the media forever.

If he revealed absolutely nothing, the reporters would continue following his every move.

And the bikers couldn't guard him forever.

"Let her come in."

The biker nodded and left.

A short while later...

Alexa entered the villa.

This time she had brought only her Heliolisk assistant.

Her Gogoat was simply too large and had been left outside.

"My, my."

Alexa smiled as soon as she saw Ethan.

"It's surprisingly difficult to meet you now."

"You even have people guarding your door."

Ethan shook his head with a smile.

"You're joking, Miss Alexa."

"They're just my friends."

"They were helping me drive away those troublesome reporters."

"Oh?"

Alexa smiled knowingly.

"When you say 'troublesome reporters'..."

"I assume you're talking about my colleagues."

"I wonder..."

"Does that include me?"

"Of course not."

Ethan quickly shook his head.

"How could I possibly dislike such a beautiful reporter?"

Alexa's smile immediately grew wider.

She had never expected the young trainer in front of her to tease her so naturally.

Meanwhile...

Standing nearby, Chloe and Misty both silently curled their lips.

Hmph.

Sweet-talking other women right in front of us...

Typical man.
