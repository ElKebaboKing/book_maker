"The three starters?" Professor Cerise chuckled. "That's quite an interesting term."

"It's just a nickname I casually came up with to make them easier to refer to," Ethan Vale said with a wave of his hand. Then he asked, "Professor Cerise, do you know where I might find wild starter Pokémon nearby?"

"As for the starters, Charmander can usually be encountered near volcanoes or lava caves, while Squirtle tends to appear along remote coastlines or isolated islands. Bulbasaur, on the other hand, should be found within dense forests." At this point, Parker suddenly seemed to remember something. "Have you ever heard of the Mysterious Garden?"

"The Mysterious Garden?" Hearing the name, Ethan Vale felt it sounded strangely familiar.

Beside him, Professor Cerise explained:

"According to legend, the Mysterious Garden is a place where Bulbasaur gather together to hold celebrations for their evolution festival. However, because very few people have ever been there, most consider it nothing more than a mythical place."

Hearing that, Ethan Vale suddenly remembered the related storyline. Back in the original Pokémon anime, hadn't Ash once entered the Mysterious Garden while searching for his Bulbasaur?

Still…

Feeling somewhat puzzled, Ethan Vale asked, "But is this even the season for their festival right now?"

"I'm not entirely sure about that." Professor Cerise thought for a moment before adding, "However, we can confirm that the Mysterious Garden truly exists. And even if it isn't festival season, I imagine many Bulbasaur still live and gather around that area."

"I see." Ethan Vale nodded. "Then do you have any clues? Or rather… how exactly am I supposed to find it?"

Professor Cerise operated his computer and brought up a map of the area surrounding Vermilion City on a large screen.

Pointing to one particular region, he said:

"Although I don't know the exact location of the Mysterious Garden, during our previous observations we discovered a large number of wild Bulbasaur activities deep within the forest north of Vermilion City. Perhaps you could try your luck there."

"I understand." Ethan Vale carefully memorized the marked location before sincerely thanking him. "You've truly helped me a great deal this time, Professor Cerise. Thank you very much!"

…

Walking down the laboratory steps alongside Chloe, Ethan Vale glanced back and saw Professor Cerise's Yamper running out of the laboratory as well, wagging its tail happily while following close behind Chloe.

Seeing this, Ethan Vale couldn't help but smile.

"It seems to really like you."

"Does it?" Chloe looked down at Yamper, though her tone remained unchanged. "I don't really notice it. It clings to my parents all the time at home too, so I think it treats everyone the same way."

After this brief interaction, Ethan Vale realized Chloe was not a particularly talkative person. Although still underage, she was calm and composed, giving off the impression of someone mature and well-behaved.

Crouching down, Ethan Vale gently rubbed Yamper's forehead. The Pokémon immediately narrowed its eyes in comfort and happily stuck out its long tongue toward him.

So it enjoys being scratched… In that sense, it really isn't much different from a normal dog.

"Alright, no need to escort me any farther. Chloe, I suppose we'll meet again if fate allows!"

After waving goodbye to Chloe, Ethan Vale did not immediately set off to search for the Mysterious Garden. Instead, he first visited an outdoor supply store in Vermilion City, where he purchased a baseball cap, sleeping bag, water bottle, binoculars, portable food, and simple cooking equipment, packing everything into a large backpack.

Since he was heading into the wilderness, proper preparation was essential. Otherwise, if night fell before he could reach a town, he would have to rely entirely on these supplies to camp outdoors.

…

Leaving Vermilion City behind, Ethan Vale and Ralts traveled northward for quite some time. Only after leaving the urban district and crossing over a small hill did they finally arrive within a dense forest.

Compared to the surrounding woods, the vegetation here was noticeably thicker and more vigorous.

As soon as they entered the forest, the phone signal quickly disappeared due to the distance from the city. With navigation no longer working, Ethan Vale could only rely on his instincts while making his way through the wilderness.

Everywhere he looked was green.

Only when surrounded by nature like this could one truly feel how small they were.

Although Ethan Vale had entered forests many times before, this was his first time exploring such a vast sea of trees.

The surroundings were eerily quiet. Fortunately, Ralts remained beside him, preventing the loneliness from becoming overwhelming.

As they continued deeper into the forest, Ethan Vale realized just how primitive this place truly was.

Towering trees with dense branches filled every direction. At first there had still been narrow paths left behind by travelers, but as they ventured farther inward, even those paths gradually disappeared. Eventually, Ralts had to use Confusion to move aside branches and vines before they could continue forward.

The forest remained silent.

There were no Caterpie, Weedle, Oddish, or Bellsprout anywhere in sight—not even one of the common Pokémon typically found in forests.

Yet the stranger this became, the more convinced Ethan Vale was that he had come to the right place.

After all, only when entering the territory of a dominant Pokémon group—where territorial instincts drove away all outsiders—would such a phenomenon occur.

Within this maze-like forest, they continued onward, stopping and walking intermittently. More than two hours later, around sunset, Ethan Vale finally began hearing the cries of numerous Pokémon somewhere ahead.

However, the path before him was blocked by thick vines.

The vines formed something resembling a giant wall. Not only were they incredibly sturdy, but they were also covered in sharp thorns, preventing any creature from approaching.

This definitely wasn't natural growth.

Which meant the Mysterious Garden was probably hidden behind this vine wall!

Exchanging a glance with Ralts, Ethan Vale saw the Pokémon nod knowingly before activating its psychic powers. An invisible burst of energy shot from its hand and struck the vines, instantly opening a small gap in the barrier.

Though narrow, it was enough for them to pass through.

Using both hands and feet, Ethan Vale squeezed through the opening before carefully lightening his footsteps and continuing onward.

Then, as he pushed aside a cluster of branches, his vision suddenly widened.

Deep within the endless dense forest, there was actually a massive basin!

Far in the center stood a gigantic withered tree, while countless Bulbasaur played happily around it.

The Mysterious Garden.

Though hidden deep in the heart of the forest and protected by a wall of vines, Ethan Vale had finally managed to find it through sheer luck.

Then again, considering the enormous size of the basin, even with the vine wall protecting it, anyone determined enough would eventually discover it.

It was simply that very few people ever made it this far.

Stopping in place, Ethan Vale gazed at the peaceful habitat before him. Rather than recklessly intruding and disturbing the Bulbasaur, he instead took out his binoculars and carefully observed the area beneath the fading glow of the sunset.

After scanning the basin several times, he focused on one particular Bulbasaur.

Bulbasaur was a quadrupedal Pokémon resembling a green frog. Besides its crimson eyes and dark green markings, its most distinctive feature was naturally the bulb-shaped plant growing on its back.

The Bulbasaur still had not noticed Ethan Vale's presence. However, because there were simply too many of them, it was easy to become overwhelmed. Furthermore, it was difficult to judge their personalities based solely on appearance.

After observing for a while, Ethan Vale finally locked his attention onto a Bulbasaur that had just wandered away from the basin.

Perhaps because nobody had intruded upon this place in a very long time, the Bulbasaur appeared completely relaxed. Occasionally, it used Vine Whip to pluck berries from nearby trees and casually ate them as it moved farther away from its group.

Seeing the opportunity, Ethan Vale no longer hesitated. Quietly moving forward while making as little noise as possible, he carefully approached it.

Choosing this particular Bulbasaur was actually the safest strategy.

If he ventured too deeply into the habitat, it might trigger panic and chaos among the Bulbasaur colony. And if the entire group attacked together, the situation would become far more trouble than it was worth.

The Bulbasaur remained entirely unaware of the approaching danger.

However, after getting close, Ethan Vale did not choose to ambush it. Instead, he stepped directly in front of it.

Hearing movement and suddenly seeing a human standing before it, the Bulbasaur was initially startled.

But almost immediately, it bent its limbs and assumed a battle stance!

Two Vine Whips suddenly extended from the bulb on its back, wildly lashing through the air as if attempting to intimidate Ethan Vale.

Meeting its gaze, Ethan Vale waved his hand, signaling Ralts to step forward.

The moment Bulbasaur saw Ralts, it seemed to instantly realize what was happening. Crying out "Bulba! Bulba!" repeatedly, its eyes immediately sharpened with battle intent!
