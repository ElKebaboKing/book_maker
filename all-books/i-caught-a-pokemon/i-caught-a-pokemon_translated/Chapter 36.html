Facing the incoming attack, that strange gust of wind blew once again, scattering all of the Pin Missiles before they could reach their target.

Ethan asked curiously,

"What kind of move is that?"

"Heh heh~" Gastly laughed proudly. "Over the long years I've lived, I didn't just learn how to speak human language. I've mastered plenty of abilities like this too!"

As if showing off, Gastly suddenly transformed itself into a Jolteon!

"I mean…" Ethan could not help complaining, "Isn't that ridiculously overpowered!? And isn't Transform supposed to be Ditto's ability? Why can you do it too?"

Gastly glanced at Kirlia. Perhaps because of the way Kirlia had protected its trainer earlier, Gastly had developed a slightly better impression of Ethan.

Canceling the transformation, it explained,

"This is actually just Hypnosis. My Hypnosis has already reached the point where it can affect reality itself without even putting people to sleep!"

Ethan frowned.

Could it really be that powerful!?

The sensation of the wind brushing across his face felt incredibly real and vivid. No matter how he looked at it, it did not seem like something Hypnosis alone should be capable of.

But this Gastly was clearly far from ordinary. It could not be judged by normal standards.

"Jolteon, use Thunder Fang!"

Electricity crackled between Jolteon's sharp teeth as it lunged toward Gastly at incredible speed.

However, just as Jolteon's attack was about to hit, Gastly's body suddenly dispersed like smoke blown away by the wind and vanished completely!

"Hehehe!"

When Gastly reappeared, it had somehow already moved beside Jolteon. With a mischievous grin, it stretched out its long tongue and gave Jolteon a heavy lick.

Jolteon shuddered violently. Just as it tried to leap away, paralysis suddenly spread through its body, leaving it unable to move.

"Ahh, the taste of electricity."

Laughing loudly, Gastly formed another Shadow Ball and slammed it directly into Jolteon.

The attack instantly knocked Jolteon unconscious.

A one-hit knockout?

Was the difference in level really that huge...?

Recalling Jolteon, Ethan lowered his head and looked toward Kirlia.

"You're up next."

Kirlia nodded and stepped forward to face Gastly.

"So now you're trying to wear me down with a relay battle?" Gastly asked. "But you do realize Psychic-type Pokémon can't defeat me, right?"

"Of course I know that." Ethan suddenly pulled out several more Poké Balls from behind his waist and tossed them all onto the battlefield. "That's why I never planned on letting Kirlia fight alone. Everyone, go together!"

Ivysaur, Squirtle, and Charmander all appeared beside Kirlia.

"What the heck? So you're just going to gang up on me?" Gastly pouted, though its tone showed no concern whatsoever.

"Well, there's no helping it," Ethan replied with a smile. "After watching your battle with Jolteon, I truly understand how strong you are. If I fought you normally, there's no way I could ever capture you!"

Hearing that, Gastly fell silent for a moment before asking:

"Why are you so obsessed with capturing powerful Pokémon? Is participating in the Pokémon League really that important to you?"

Ethan thought for a moment before answering.

"How should I put it... Life is filled with joy, anger, sorrow, and happiness. The Pokémon League isn't everything in life, but it's both my dream and something I genuinely want to pursue. What about you? Don't you have any goals you want to chase?"

"Dreams? Goals?" Gastly muttered softly.

As a Pokémon that loved pranks, it had spent countless years simply amusing itself and enjoying life. It had never truly considered such abstract ideas before.

Seeing Gastly deep in thought, Ethan's eyes suddenly lit up.

An opening!

"Kirlia, use Magical Leaf!"

At Ethan's command, glowing green leaves spiraled toward Gastly.

"Hey, hey! Attacking while I'm distracted is way too shameless!"

Gastly hurriedly dodged the attack, but before it even finished speaking, a Water Gun suddenly shot at it from the side.

"That's not shameless—that's acting decisively!" Ethan shouted. "Charmander, use Ember!"

The Water Gun grazed Gastly's body. Just as it stabilized itself, a shower of flames rushed toward it.

"How is that any different!?"

Gastly vanished once again, but the moment it reappeared, it was unexpectedly struck by Razor Leaf.

Being hit by several attacks in succession left Gastly looking rather disheveled, and it finally decided to stop holding back.

Wave after wave of light spread from Gastly's body, enveloping all the Pokémon present.

Ethan's heart sank. He currently had no way to deal with this move.

All he could do was grit his teeth and shout:

"Charmander, use Smokescreen!"

A huge cloud of black smoke burst from Charmander's mouth, instantly covering the battlefield.

"So you're trying to use Smokescreen to block my vision and reduce the accuracy of Hypnosis?" Gastly laughed wickedly. "Nice idea, but you're still far too naive!"

Gastly focused intensely. In the next second, the waves of light became visibly faster and denser, shifting from a targeted attack into a wide-area assault.

In just moments, all of Ethan's Pokémon fell asleep.

At this point, Ethan truly had no solution left.

Gastly slowly floated upward, staring down at the thick smoke beneath it as it launched its final attack.

Pink streams of energy began flowing out from Ethan's sleeping Pokémon and into Gastly's body. As Gastly's eyes widened sharply, all of Ethan's Pokémon collapsed to the ground as though struck mentally.

Dream Eater had activated!

Dream Eater—a Psychic-type move that devours dreams to inflict heavy damage while restoring half of the damage dealt as HP.

As the black smoke gradually faded away, Ethan looked at his Pokémon lying unconscious on the ground and let out a deep sigh.

This Gastly was vastly stronger than any other of its kind. Even when facing Zapdos before, Ethan had never felt as powerless as he did now.

One by one, he recalled his defeated Pokémon into their Poké Balls.

"I lost."

Gastly slowly floated over to Ethan and carefully examined him.

"No. You haven't lost yet."

Its gaze shifted toward the Poké Ball containing Zapdos as it smiled.

"Don't you still have a Legendary Pokémon? So how can this be called a loss? Besides, I've used up a lot of energy too. Since you refuse to use Zapdos against me, why don't we just call this battle a draw instead?"

"Huh?" Ethan froze, completely surprised that Gastly would say something like that.

Gastly continued:

"Your personality suits my taste quite well. Although I don't really have any dreams right now, I do want to leave this place and travel around the world. And honestly, winning the Pokémon League and becoming Champion sounds like a pretty good goal."

Ethan looked at Gastly, joy surging through his heart.

"What are you saying?"

Gastly spread its mouth into a huge grin.

"What I mean is, I already acknowledge you. Now take out your Poké Ball. I'm willing to become your Pokémon and help you achieve your dream!"

(End of Chapter)
