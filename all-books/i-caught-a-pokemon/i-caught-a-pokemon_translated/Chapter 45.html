"However, your luck ends here."

As Lance spoke, the Team Rocket members all released their Pokémon at once, instantly filling the corridor from end to end.

Ethan and Sabrina stood back-to-back, pressed tightly against each other, not daring to relax for even a moment.

"What do we do now? Retreat?" Sabrina asked quietly.

"Of course not." Ethan shook his head and pulled a Poké Ball from his waist before tossing it into the air. "We've already come this far. There's no way we're turning back halfway through!"

A flash of blue light appeared.

The next second, a Pokémon with a wingspan nearly three meters wide appeared before everyone. Its golden body crackled with electricity from head to toe.

"Zapdos!?"

"Wow, it's my first time seeing one in person!"

The Team Rocket members cried out in shock.

Even Sabrina could not help turning around, her eyes filled with astonishment as she looked at Zapdos.

After all, Legendary Pokémon like this were unbelievably rare. Most ordinary people would never even see one in their entire lives, much less capture or battle alongside one!

"Don't panic!" Seeing his subordinates begin to lose composure, Lance shouted loudly to calm them. "So what if he has Zapdos? Attack together!"

At the same time, Ethan shouted,

"Zapdos! Use Thunderbolt!!"

Zapdos originally had no intention of obeying Ethan's command.

However, with attacks coming from both the front and rear, it had no choice but to protect itself.

Letting out a furious cry, Zapdos unleashed a massive indiscriminate electrical attack in every direction.

Blinding electricity exploded outward from Zapdos like a storm.

Not only did it completely overwhelm the other Pokémon's attacks, but it continued spreading until most of the corridor was engulfed in crackling lightning.

The instant Zapdos released Thunderbolt, Kirlia grabbed Ethan and Sabrina by the hands and teleported them to the opposite end of the hallway, allowing them to avoid the devastating attack.

The lightning was so bright it almost blinded them.

Amid the crackling electricity came the screams and cries of the Team Rocket members.

By the time the light faded, the corridor was littered with unconscious Team Rocket grunts and defeated Pokémon.

"Damn it!"

Lance turned toward Ethan with an extremely dark expression.

Because he had been standing far behind the others, he had narrowly escaped being struck by Thunderbolt.

Grinding his teeth, he released his own Pokémon.

"As long as I'm standing here, don't think for a second that you're leaving this place!"

Just like Lambda aboard the S.S. Anne, Lance's Pokémon were also Zubat and Weezing.

More footsteps echoed continuously as additional Team Rocket members rushed up from the stairwell and gathered behind Lance.

"Tch." Ethan sighed. "They just keep coming. What a pain."

At that moment, Sabrina stepped forward and stood in front of Ethan, leaving him with only her alluring back view.

"Alright. Leave this place to me. Go do what you need to do."

"…"

Ethan hesitated briefly.

But he knew the situation was urgent. This was no time for pointless hesitation or politeness.

Besides, Sabrina's abilities were exceptional. There was no need to worry too much about her safety.

"Alright. I'll leave Zapdos and Gastly here to help you. Be careful."

Without waiting for Sabrina to agree, Ethan immediately took Kirlia and rushed straight toward the executive office without obstruction.

"Damn it! Stop right there!"

Seeing Ethan leave, Lance tried to pursue him.

But after only taking a few steps, Sabrina calmly tossed out a Poké Ball and blocked his path.

Ethan pushed open the office doors and immediately saw an elderly man collapsed on the sofa.

Aside from him, the office was completely empty.

Huh?

Giovanni isn't here?

Thinking back to the helicopter that had just departed, Ethan already understood what had happened.

Without wasting time, he quickly stepped forward.

Judging from the old man's bearing and clothing, he was obviously not part of Team Rocket.

"Are you alright?"

The elderly man slowly opened his eyes.

After seeing Ethan, he finally let out a sigh of relief.

"You police officers arrived far too late…"

"Sorry." Ethan shook his head. "I'm not a police officer. I was only asked to investigate the situation here."

"What!?" The old man widened his eyes in disbelief. "You're not police, yet you still managed to reach this place? Incredible! Then all those Team Rocket members outside…"

"That's right." Ethan paused, allowing the old man to hear the ongoing screams echoing from the corridor outside. "They're still out there, so we should leave immediately."

Just as Ethan helped the old man to his feet and prepared to have Kirlia use Teleport to escape—

A woman's voice suddenly echoed through the room.

"I knew that useless fool Lance couldn't handle things properly. To think he still let rats sneak in here. What a waste of the Boss's trust."

Ethan spun around instantly.

Only then did he realize that standing silently in the corner of the office was a woman wearing Team Rocket's white executive uniform.

She had crimson hair and matching red eyebrows.

Her appearance had been so silent and eerie that she had seemed almost like a ghost.

"Who are you?" Ethan asked cautiously.

"Allow me to introduce myself." The woman's expression appeared arrogant and vicious. "My name is Athena. I'm both a research scientist and, just like that incompetent man outside, one of Team Rocket's executives."

"Another executive?" Ethan sized her up carefully. "And you're a researcher too?"

Athena smiled and nodded.

"My primary field of research involves special sound waves capable of forcibly evolving Pokémon. I originally came here today hoping to see the legendary Master Ball for myself. Unfortunately, this stubborn old man refuses to talk."

Seeing the malicious look in her eyes, Ethan quietly raised his guard while smiling casually.

"Since you're a researcher, getting into a fight would damage your refined image. How about this instead? Let's pretend we never saw each other and allow us to leave safely."

"You certainly are smooth with words." Athena smiled without warmth. "Mind telling me your identity?"

"That wouldn't be possible." Ethan shrugged. "My mother once warned me never to tell my name to scary-looking older women who seem unpleasant."

Though the two appeared to be joking, every word between them carried sharp hostility.

Neither side was willing to yield.

"…"

Athena's expression darkened completely.

She was clearly angry now.

"I see. In that case, I have even less reason to let you leave."

As she spoke, she pulled out two Poké Balls and released her Pokémon.

One was a massive cobra-like Pokémon with a coiled body—Arbok.

The other was a pitch-black crow-like Pokémon whose head feathers resembled a wizard's hat, while its tail looked like a broom—Murkrow.

At this point, Ethan stopped wasting words as well.

He immediately threw out several Poké Balls of his own.

Blue light flashed one after another.

The next moment, Ivysaur, Squirtle, Charmander, and Jolteon all appeared inside the office.

For a moment, the atmosphere became razor-sharp with tension.

A chaotic battle now seemed completely unavoidable.

(End of Chapter)
