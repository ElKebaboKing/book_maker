Although Mewtwo had been struck by the attack, its body remained completely spotless—not even a speck of dirt or a scratch could be seen.

Looking at Mew, its eyes burned with fighting spirit.

"You are powerful. You are worthy of my full strength."

Glancing at everyone else, Mewtwo unleashed its psychic power once more. As another elevator platform rose from below, more than a dozen cloned Pokémon, each marked with distinctive stripes, emerged.

"The reason humans created us was to make us stronger. Yet they never imagined that one day they would suffer the consequences!"

It raised its arm.

"Go! My clones! Let these humans—and their servants—feel the fury of lives that have been manipulated and toyed with!"

Inspired by Mewtwo's command, the cloned Pokémon charged forward.

Ash and the others had never intended to fight, but with no choice but to defend themselves, they ordered their Pokémon into battle.

A chaotic melee instantly erupted.

Ethan also released his Pokémon, sending them to fight alongside the others against the clones.

However, the moment the battle began, Ethan realized that these cloned Pokémon were far stronger than ordinary members of their species.

Although they were greatly outnumbered, each clone could easily fight two opponents at once without falling behind against Pokémon that had been carefully trained for years.

Flames, torrents of water, and bolts of electricity filled the battlefield.

Roars and cries echoed throughout the castle as Pokémon clashed across every corner.

As time passed and everyone's stamina gradually wore down, neither side managed to gain the upper hand.

Meanwhile, Mewtwo had grown tired of Mew's incredible agility.

It had also grown tired of the stalemate.

"Now... witness my true power!"

Determined to end the battle once and for all, Mewtwo gathered all of its remaining strength into one final attack.

Seeing this, Mew had no choice but to respond in kind.

Two enormous spheres of energy formed before Mewtwo and Mew.

Once they reached maximum power, both Pokémon fired at the same time.

But at that very moment...

To everyone's astonishment, Ash suddenly rushed between them.

"Stop! Please don't fight anymore!"

The attacks had already been launched.

There was no stopping them now.

A blue sphere and a pink sphere slammed directly into Ash.

BOOM!

A blinding flash engulfed the battlefield.

Without even letting out a scream, Ash's eyes widened before he collapsed to the ground...

His body instantly transformed into solid stone.

The sudden tragedy caught everyone completely off guard.

No one had time to react.

All they could do was watch helplessly as Ash fell.

"Ash!!"

Ethan sprinted over and grabbed Ash's shoulders, trying to shake him awake.

Receiving no response, he immediately checked for signs of life.

There was no pulse.

No breathing.

He'd... been petrified.

"What a fool."

Mewtwo looked down at Ash coldly.

"He actually tried to stop our battle. How utterly foolish."

"Pika! Pika!!"

Seeing Ash lying motionless, Pikachu seemed to realize what had happened.

In desperation, it repeatedly unleashed electric shocks onto Ash's body, hoping the electricity would somehow break the petrification.

Crackle!

Forced back by the powerful current, Ethan retreated several steps.

Pikachu's desperate cries echoed through the battlefield.

The heartbreaking scene drew everyone's attention.

Humans.

Pokémon.

Even Mewtwo and the cloned Pokémon.

One after another, they stopped fighting and silently watched.

After shocking Ash again and again without success, Pikachu finally collapsed from exhaustion.

Lying across Ash's stone body, it burst into tears.

The kind-hearted Pokémon witnessing the scene realized that someone had sacrificed his own life simply to stop the fighting.

Moved beyond words, they too began to cry.

Then...

Something miraculous happened.

The Pokémon's tears glowed with tiny, star-like lights.

Countless sparkling droplets floated into the sky before gathering together and descending into Ash's body.

At that very moment...

The raging storm surrounding New Island suddenly vanished.

A brilliant pillar of white light descended from the heavens, enveloping Ash.

The mysterious power revived him instantly, breaking the petrification and restoring him to life.

"W-What!?"

Even after witnessing countless miracles, everyone stood frozen in disbelief.

Only Ethan seemed to notice something unusual.

He immediately looked toward the sky.

There, he caught sight of a magnificent bird Pokémon soaring into the distance.

Its rainbow-colored wings proclaimed its extraordinary existence.

Ho-Oh...?

Legend said that Ho-Oh possessed the power to restore life.

It must have appeared just in time and saved Ash.

Yet...

Aside from Ethan, no one else seemed to notice Ho-Oh's presence.

But why had Ho-Oh appeared here?

Was the old rumor actually true—that it secretly followed Ash wherever he went?

Or perhaps, like Mew, it had simply sensed Mewtwo's presence and come to investigate?

Whatever the reason, Ash's miraculous revival filled everyone with relief.

Pokémon all around the battlefield cried out joyfully.

More importantly...

Witnessing this miracle, combined with Ash's willingness to sacrifice himself for Pokémon, deeply shook Mewtwo's heart.

Floating silently in place, Mewtwo said nothing.

But countless thoughts surged through its mind.

Taking the opportunity, Ethan stepped forward.

"Mewtwo."

"No matter how this battle ends, it has no real meaning."

"Whether you're the original or a clone... every living being has the right to live freely."

"Existence itself gives life meaning."

"You're all living creatures."

"There's no reason to fight simply to prove who's stronger."

After a long silence, Mewtwo finally spoke.

"What you say... makes sense."

"I understand now."

"Compared to life itself... strength is ultimately nothing more than a meaningless farce."

With that, it turned toward Mew.

"Whether born naturally or created through cloning, we're all Pokémon."

"Each of us has our own life."

"No one has the right to force their own beliefs upon another."

"Perhaps... none of this..."

"...none of this absurd drama should be remembered."

Mew gently nodded.

Seeing that Mew agreed, the two legendary Pokémon activated their psychic powers together.

Soft blue particles began drifting down from the sky.

The glowing lights covered the entire island.

While Misty and the others admired the beautiful scene, Ethan looked upward.

He knew these glowing particles were the psychic energy Mewtwo would use to erase everyone's memories.

Just as he was about to ask Gardevoir to shield him with Psychic, he suddenly noticed something strange.

The blue lights deliberately flowed around him...

Landing only on everyone else.

Before he could wonder why, Mewtwo slowly descended until it stood before him.

"I still have something I wish to ask you."

Looking directly into Ethan's eyes, Mewtwo spoke seriously.

"When I searched your memories, I saw that you knew many things before they happened."

"I want to know..."

"...why?"
