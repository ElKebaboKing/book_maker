"Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to the second-round advancement match at the Indigo Plateau's Water Field!"

After a moment of silence, the sharp, energetic voice of the announcer echoed across the Water Field.

"Entering first on the Red side is the handsome and charismatic Ethan Vale! In his previous match, he claimed an overwhelming victory. Will he be able to carry that same momentum into today's battle?"

As thunderous applause swept through the stadium, Ethan emerged from the trainer's tunnel, waving to the cheering crowd.

In the spectator stands, the biker gang waved flags and shouted themselves hoarse, drawing curious glances from everyone nearby.

After Ethan's match two days earlier, the audience had already become familiar with this rowdy group and knew they were cheering for the handsome young trainer before them.

Still, the stark contrast between Ethan's refined appearance and the rough-looking bikers—who constantly shouted "Boss!"—sparked endless speculation.

Some people had already imagined wild stories such as:

"A reformed biker gang leader becomes a Pokémon Trainer!"

"The son of an underworld boss competes in the Pokémon League!"

Once the applause subsided, the announcer continued.

"Entering on the Green side is Saizo! He fought a difficult battle to earn his first-round victory. Facing such a formidable opponent today, can he overcome the challenge?"

Amid polite applause from the audience—and loud boos from the biker gang—Saizo stepped onto his command platform, facing Ethan from across the battlefield.

Seeing that both Trainers were ready, the referee raised his flags.

"Both Trainers, please send out your first Pokémon!"

Ethan enlarged the Poké Ball at his waist and released his choice.

"Come out, Gyarados!"

Across from him, Saizo also threw his Poké Ball.

"Go, Poliwhirl!"

As the evolved form of Poliwag, Poliwhirl was a bipedal Pokémon with a round blue body. Its smooth, slippery skin gleamed under the lights, its white glove-like hands were raised in readiness, and the black counterclockwise spiral on its white belly immediately caught everyone's attention.

The announcer smiled.

"It looks like both Trainers have chosen Water-type Pokémon! With neither side holding a type advantage, this battle will come down entirely to strength and skill!"

However, the instant Gyarados appeared, its blood-red eyes and terrifying aura caused Poliwhirl to instinctively stumble backward several steps.

"Oh?" the announcer exclaimed. "Ethan Vale's Gyarados has an incredible presence! Its Intimidate Ability has already shaken Poliwhirl before the battle has even begun!"

"Poliwhirl, pull yourself together!" Saizo shouted. "The battle hasn't even started yet! Don't give up already!"

His encouragement had only a limited effect. Unable to remain patient, Saizo immediately ordered the first attack.

"Poliwhirl, use Hypnosis!"

Poliwhirl forced itself to stay calm as concentric waves of hypnotic energy spread from its eyes toward Gyarados.

"Gyarados," Ethan ordered calmly, "dive underwater!"

Just before Hypnosis could connect, Gyarados plunged from its platform into the pool below.

The hypnotic waves struck only the surface of the water, accomplishing nothing.

"Don't think hiding underwater will save you!" Saizo yelled. "Poliwhirl, get into the water too!"

Poliwhirl leaped toward the pool.

But before it could land—

BOOM!

The water suddenly exploded upward.

Gyarados burst from beneath the surface and slammed directly into Poliwhirl in midair.

"Good," Ethan nodded. "Now bite down and don't let go!"

Despite Poliwhirl's slippery body, the enormous difference in size allowed Gyarados to twist its head and clamp its massive jaws tightly around the round-bodied Pokémon.

Its fangs tightened relentlessly.

Poliwhirl cried out in pain, struggling desperately but unable to escape.

"Poliwhirl! Quick! Use Hypnosis again!" Saizo shouted anxiously.

Poliwhirl's eyes began glowing once more, attempting to cast Hypnosis from inside Gyarados's jaws.

However, Gyarados shook its head violently while tightening its bite.

The constant pain made it impossible for Poliwhirl to maintain the concentration required for the move.

Saizo had no choice but to change tactics.

"Poliwhirl, use Water Gun!"

A powerful stream of water blasted directly into Gyarados's mouth.

Although the same-type attack dealt only half damage, the inside of Gyarados's mouth was much softer than its scales.

The pain forced Gyarados to instinctively loosen its bite.

Poliwhirl immediately escaped from the dragon's jaws.

But before Saizo could celebrate, Ethan's voice rang out again.

"Gyarados, use Twister!"

Gyarados churned violently across the water's surface.

The swirling currents rapidly formed into a massive waterspout that engulfed Poliwhirl.

WHOOSH!!

The Twister grew larger and larger until it covered nearly half the battlefield.

The powerful winds even reached the audience, blowing across the spectators' faces.

"Wow!" the announcer shouted excitedly. "What an incredible tornado! Ethan Vale's Gyarados is unbelievably powerful!"

Inside the Twister, Poliwhirl spun uncontrollably, suffering continuous damage.

When the winds finally subsided, it had been carried high into the air.

"Now!" Ethan's eyes flashed. "Gyarados, finish it with Aqua Tail!"

Water wrapped around Gyarados's tail before it whipped upward with explosive force toward the completely helpless Poliwhirl.

SMACK!

The crisp impact echoed throughout the entire stadium.

Poliwhirl spun through the air like a rubber ball before being launched straight out of the battlefield.

BANG!

It slammed into the arena wall before slowly sliding to the ground.

The referee hurried over, checked Poliwhirl, then immediately raised the flag.

"Poliwhirl is out of bounds and unable to battle!"

The crowd erupted into thunderous cheers.

Gyarados's overwhelming dominance and the gigantic Twister had thrilled the audience.

Compared to ordinary exchanges, this was exactly the kind of spectacular battle everyone had come to see.

Saizo let out a defeated sigh, his eyelids twitching.

Although he had lost only his first Pokémon, the overwhelming gap in strength made both his heart and his mind realize the truth.

His Indigo League journey might very well end in this round.

Even so, having come this far, there was no way he could simply give up.

After a brief moment of thought, he recalled Poliwhirl and sent out his second Pokémon.

( End of Chapter )
