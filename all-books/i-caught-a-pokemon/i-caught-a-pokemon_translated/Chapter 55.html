"Don't worry," Ethan said seriously. "As soon as I finish challenging the Celadon Gym, I'll leave the city immediately."

"I believe in your strength. Then let me wish you success in your challenge." Looker paused before changing the subject. "However, there's something else I need to tell you. Once our preparations are complete, we'll form a special operations team to lead the assault on Team Rocket's base. At that time, we may invite you to join as well."

"Me?" Ethan was briefly surprised before quickly agreeing. "Of course. Since Team Rocket has already made me a target, there's no way I can just stand by and do nothing. But who else will be part of this special operations team?"

"Well…" Looker thought for a moment. "Anyone who joins will naturally be extremely strong. We'll do our best to invite the Elite Four, and we'll also contact outstanding Gym Leaders through the Pokémon League."

Ethan nodded inwardly.

If they truly managed to assemble a lineup like that, they could practically sweep through Team Rocket's base without resistance.

After hanging up the call, Ethan followed the main road until he arrived at the Celadon Gym.

The gym's exterior resembled a large sports arena commonly found in schools. Its roof was earthy yellow, while the surrounding walls were silver-gray. It stood within an enormous garden in the center of the city.

As Celadon City's official Gym, Erika specialized in Grass-type Pokémon. Anyone who defeated her would earn the Rainbow Badge.

After entering the building, Ethan first arrived at a spacious lobby where several beautiful young women in shorts were working. Upon seeing him, a blue-haired girl walked over.

"How may I help you?"

Once Ethan explained his purpose, she led him deeper into the gym. After leaving the lobby, what first came into view were rows of greenhouses separated by glass walls.

The greenhouses were lush and overflowing with vibrant vegetation, filled with flourishing trees, bushes, and flowers. Compared to a battle-oriented Gym, the place resembled more of an indoor botanical garden.

Speaking of which, the perfumes Ethan had purchased yesterday were produced here. The entire staff consisted of young women who not only worked for the Gym, but also managed the perfume shop as a side business.

Along the way, many Grass-type Pokémon ran about freely. Under the care of the staff members, they played and frolicked happily.

At the very end, they arrived at an elegant room.

Through the window, Ethan could see Erika kneeling gracefully on a tatami mat. While trimming flowers in a vase with scissors, she calmly explained something to several women seated before her.

Seeing Ethan staring intently, the blue-haired girl beside him explained,

"Miss Erika isn't just the Gym Leader—she's also a famous ikebana master. Many people come every week to learn flower arranging from her. Sir, do you also enjoy ikebana?"

"In a way, yes," Ethan nodded. "Flower arranging is pleasing to the eye and calming to the mind. It's an excellent way to regulate one's emotions."

Not long afterward, the lesson ended.

When Erika stepped out of the room and saw Ethan, she immediately recognized him.

"You're the person from the perfume shop yesterday?"

"That's right." Ethan smiled. "I didn't want to disturb you before, but this time I've come specifically to challenge you, Miss Erika."

Now that he observed her more carefully, Ethan realized that Erika truly was very beautiful.

She appeared to be around eighteen or nineteen years old, petite in stature, and always dressed in a kimono regardless of whether she was working or relaxing. She clearly embodied the image of a traditional Japanese lady, with an elegant and refined grace in every movement.

"I see. Please follow me."

The three of them retraced their path until they reached a fork in the corridor and turned toward the battlefield.

Upon seeing the many Grass-type Pokémon again, Ethan could not help but remark,

"Oddish, Bellsprout, Exeggcute, Weepinbell, Victreebel, Vileplume… You really have an impressive variety of Grass-type Pokémon here."

Erika smiled gently.

"I've loved nature and Grass-type Pokémon ever since I was little, so I've raised quite a few of them. The perfumes in our shop are also made by extracting ingredients from the leaves of these Pokémon."

When they arrived at the battlefield, the two stood on opposite sides.

Despite specializing in Grass-types, the arena contained none of the usual obstacles that might favor her Pokémon. Instead, it was an entirely open and ordinary battlefield.

However, one detail stood out.

Directly above the field was a skylight, and with the sun shining brightly overhead, warm sunlight poured down and bathed the entire arena.

The blue-haired girl stepped forward to serve as referee.

"The rules for this Gym battle are three Pokémon each. If you defeat all of Miss Erika's Pokémon, you win the match!"

As she spoke, Erika sent out her first Pokémon.

It was a Pokémon completely covered in tangled vines. It had no visible arms, only a pair of feet that looked as though they were wearing red boots. Aside from its large eyes peeking through the vines, nothing else could be seen.

Pokédex: "Tangela, the Vine Pokémon. A Grass-type Pokémon. Its entire body is covered in constantly wriggling blue vines, hiding its true appearance. It walks with a swaying motion and enjoys wrapping its vines around nearby objects. Even if its vines are damaged, they quickly grow back."

Ethan took out a Poké Ball and threw it onto the field.

Against a pure Grass-type Pokémon like this, using type advantage was the obvious choice.

"Go, Charmander!"

Seeing the flames blazing brightly on Charmander's tail, Erika gently shook her head.

As a Gym Leader specializing in Grass-types, she had no alternative Pokémon that could effectively counter Charmander. Unless there was a massive difference in strength, the outcome of this battle was practically decided already.

"The match begins!"

The moment the blue-haired referee announced the start, both Pokémon attacked almost simultaneously.

"Tangela, use Stun Spore!"

"Charmander, use Ember!"

Sparkling powder drifted toward Charmander, but the instant Ember collided with it, the flames suddenly surged violently, burning away most of the powder.

Did some sort of chemical reaction just occur?

The fire continued surging toward Tangela. However, perhaps due to its Chlorophyll ability, Tangela's speed doubled under the intense sunlight, allowing it to narrowly evade the attack just before the flames reached it.

Still, Erika's strategy had been sound.

Knowing she was at a type disadvantage, she had intended to paralyze Charmander first before adjusting her tactics afterward.

Unfortunately for her, the plan had failed.

(End of Chapter)
