"Ralts, use Confusion!"

At Ethan Vale's command, Bulbasaur's body was suddenly enveloped in light. Struck by an invisible force, it was lifted straight into the air!

After overcoming its initial panic, Bulbasaur refused to surrender despite continuously taking damage. Enduring the pain, it lashed its Vine Whips fiercely toward Ralts even while unable to move its body freely.

Naturally, Ethan Vale had no intention of allowing Ralts to take the attack head-on.

"Use Teleport!"

Through the red horn on its head, Ralts could keenly sense Ethan Vale's emotions. Combined with their long experience battling together, Ralts had already used Teleport before Ethan Vale had even finished speaking, instantly disappearing from its original position.

Smack! Smack!

The Vine Whips slammed into the ground, sending countless blades of grass flying everywhere!

When Ralts reappeared, it had moved from Bulbasaur's front to directly behind it.

With a wave of its hands, Bulbasaur's body was hurled sideways.

Bang!

It crashed heavily into a tree trunk before slowly falling back down.

It hurt terribly.

And against this relentless series of attacks, Bulbasaur had virtually no way to retaliate.

Letting out a pained cry, the bulb on Bulbasaur's back trembled before launching a seed roughly the size of a peach pit straight toward Ralts.

Although it was his first time seeing it in person, Ethan Vale recognized the move instantly.

Leech Seed—a technique capable of draining an opponent's stamina!

"Ralts, dodge it!"

At his command, Ralts used Teleport once again to avoid the attack.

But the moment Ralts reappeared, Bulbasaur suddenly charged forward, slamming its body directly toward it.

I see!

Looks like it knows how to predict movements too!

However, this time Ethan Vale did not even need to give another command. Bulbasaur's body was suddenly surrounded by glowing psychic energy once again. In the next second, it was blasted backward at an even greater speed than before, crashing violently into the same large tree!

Bang!

The impact was so powerful that it left a visible dent in the trunk itself!

This time, Bulbasaur could no longer endure it and completely lost consciousness.

Seeing this, Ethan Vale calmly took a Poké Ball from his waist and threw it toward Bulbasaur.

A red light enveloped Bulbasaur and drew it into the Poké Ball. The ball shook slightly for a moment before, after a soft click, the button dimmed.

Success!

A flash of joy appeared in Ethan Vale's eyes.

Overall, the battle had been fairly easy and not particularly difficult.

Only now did the tension in his heart finally disappear.

Although the fight itself seemed simple, this was still his very first time capturing a wild Pokémon, so naturally he felt excited.

In the distance, several Bulbasaur had already been attracted by the sounds of battle and were beginning to look in this direction.

Before they could approach, Ethan Vale quickly picked up the Poké Ball and retreated back to where he had come from.

However, he did not leave immediately. Instead, he took out his phone and snapped several photos of the Mysterious Garden before finally departing in satisfaction.

Following the same route back, Ethan Vale's mood was completely different from when he first arrived.

Even his footsteps seemed lighter than before.

Several hours later, by the time he finally emerged from the forest, the moon was already hanging high overhead beneath a sky filled with stars.

So much time had passed during the journey there and back…

Bathed in moonlight, Ethan Vale spread out his sleeping bag while Ralts gathered several branches nearby. Once the campfire was lit, the blazing flames not only drove away the surrounding chill but also brought warmth and brightness to the night.

Taking out a set of cooking utensils from his backpack, Ethan Vale set up a small cooking rack before heating several canned instant meals, preparing a warm and simple dinner for himself and his two Pokémon.

The delicious aroma spread through the air alongside the gentle breeze.

Although he was far from a skilled cook, heating canned food was hardly difficult.

Once everything was ready, Ethan Vale finally opened the Poké Ball and released Bulbasaur.

After several hours of rest, Bulbasaur had already recovered much of its stamina. However, upon seeing Ethan Vale, it instinctively stepped backward, its eyes still filled with caution.

"Does the current environment still feel unfamiliar to you?" Ethan Vale smiled gently before speaking softly. "Bulbasaur, I'm sorry about earlier, but welcome to our little family. From now on, let's take good care of each other."

As he spoke, he placed a plate of food in front of Bulbasaur.

The enticing aroma drifted directly beneath its nose.

Bulbasaur sniffed instinctively, and saliva immediately began forming in its mouth.

It was hardly surprising. Wild Pokémon rarely ever experienced the smell of human food.

Practically unable to resist, Bulbasaur stared intently at the steaming vegetable soup before it.

Ethan Vale smiled silently at the sight.

Anyone who had tried canned food knew that regardless of whether it actually tasted amazing, the smell alone was undeniably tempting.

Perhaps because it could no longer resist its hunger, or perhaps because it saw Ralts elegantly eating nearby, Bulbasaur hesitated for a long time before finally lowering its guard and stepping forward to eat enthusiastically.

…

Early the next morning, after breakfast, Ethan Vale followed the route back to Vermilion City and returned once more to Cerise Laboratory.

When everyone—including Chloe—saw the Bulbasaur beside Ethan Vale, they were all visibly astonished.

Especially Parker, who immediately asked excitedly:

"Bulbasaur? You actually caught one? Was it really inside the Mysterious Garden?"

Ethan Vale took out his phone and transferred all the photos he had taken of the Mysterious Garden to Professor Cerise.

Professor Cerise opened the images on the computer, and soon several heads gathered around the screen in amazement.

"This valley is absolutely beautiful!"

"So many Bulbasaur and their evolved forms… So this really is the Mysterious Garden?"

"Look at that giant tree—it must have existed for thousands of years!"

After carefully reviewing the photos, Professor Cerise adjusted his glasses and turned toward Ethan Vale.

"It seems another person has now discovered the Mysterious Garden. And this also confirms our previous theories—the garden truly is hidden within that forest. Judging from your photographs, the area has remained completely untouched, which is undoubtedly the ideal environment for Bulbasaur to live and reproduce."

After finishing, he smiled again.

"However, I believe this secret should not be spread around. On the contrary, it would be best if it remained hidden forever."

"I feel the same way." Ethan Vale nodded in agreement. "Although I'm a trainer and I caught a Pokémon there, from the Pokémon's perspective, this hidden habitat really should remain peaceful and undisturbed by too many people."

Hearing Ethan Vale say such things made Professor Cerise regard him in a completely new light.

Since everyone shared the same opinion, they naturally stopped discussing the Mysterious Garden any further.

At that moment, Parker lowered his head and looked toward Bulbasaur.

"This Bulbasaur looks incredibly sturdy. I imagine it must be quite strong too, right?"

Although Parker was merely a researcher, he owned a Magnemite named Francois and enjoyed watching Pokémon battles in his spare time. Among everyone at the laboratory, he was the one most interested in Pokémon strength.

"Yes." Ethan Vale crouched down and gently rubbed Bulbasaur's forehead. "This Bulbasaur really is impressive. I had to put in quite a bit of effort to catch it. In fact, on our way back to Vermilion City this morning, we were challenged by a Youngster. Bulbasaur fought two opponents by itself and defeated both Diglett and Marowak one after another. Thanks to those battles, Bulbasaur and I have also become much closer."

"Oh?"

For a moment, Parker honestly didn't know whom he should praise more.

Was it Bulbasaur for being so strong right after being captured?

Or Ethan Vale for commanding it so skillfully?

At that moment, Professor Cerise also stepped forward toward Ethan Vale.

Looking at him seriously, he said:

"Mr. Ethan Vale, there is actually something I would like to ask of you as well."

Seeing Professor Cerise so formal, Ethan Vale looked somewhat puzzled.

"There's no need to be so polite, Professor. Please just say it directly. As long as it's within my abilities, I'll gladly help."

"It's nothing that serious." Professor Cerise smiled. "After what happened earlier, I've realized that you truly are different. Not only can you discover secrets that ordinary people would overlook, but you're also calm, thoughtful, and considerate toward Pokémon. Perhaps you genuinely possess extraordinary talent. So… would you be willing to become a Special Research Fellow for our laboratory?"

"A Special Research Fellow?"

Assuming Ethan Vale didn't understand the position, Professor Cerise explained further.

"During your travels, you'll encounter, interact with, and communicate with all kinds of Pokémon across this planet. Together with us, you'll deepen humanity's understanding of Pokémon and uncover hidden secrets scattered throughout the world! Well? Doesn't that sound exciting?"

Well…

Ethan Vale paused, not feeling that there was anything wrong with the proposal.

Still…

He clearly remembered that in the anime, both Ash and Goh had once served as Special Research Fellows for Cerise Laboratory.

And now, had he somehow gotten there before them?
