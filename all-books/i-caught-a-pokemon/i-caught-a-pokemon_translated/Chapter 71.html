Chloe was just about to speak when Ethan's phone suddenly rang. She could only stop herself, crouch down with her chin resting on her hands, and watch Ethan with a cheerful smile while waiting for him to finish the call.

Ethan glanced at the screen.

It was Sabrina calling.

"Hello?" As soon as the call connected, Ethan skipped the pleasantries and went straight to the point. "How did things go?"

On the other end of the line, Sabrina's voice remained as cool and calm as ever.

"I already returned the Silph Scope to Mr. Silph. He expressed his sincere gratitude that you were able to recover it."

Ethan waited for more, but seeing Sabrina suddenly stop speaking, he asked in surprise:

"Huh? That's it?"

Sabrina laughed softly and asked in return,

"What? You sound disappointed."

"Of course I am," Ethan admitted openly. "After all, I recovered it from Team Rocket. I originally thought he'd reward me with another Master Ball or something."

"A Master Ball?" Sabrina sounded amused. "What exactly do you think a Master Ball is? Some ordinary Poké Ball sold in piles at convenience stores?"

"Well, obviously not," Ethan replied. "But men are greedy creatures. We always have unrealistic fantasies about things we can't obtain."

For some reason, Ethan's words seemed to cause Sabrina to misunderstand something. She fell silent briefly before slowly continuing:

"Alright, enough teasing you. Actually, Mr. Silph knows you're continuing your journey, so he had the Silph Scope modified. Now it can be installed onto your mask."

Mask? What mask?

Chloe's ears immediately perked up.

"Huh? He actually gave me the Silph Scope!? Wait… you actually know how to joke around now!?" Ethan exclaimed in surprise. Then, noticing Chloe secretly listening in, he quickly turned sideways and lowered his voice. "Ahem, anyway, thank you. And thank Mr. Silph for me too. Just keep it with you for now. When I have time to visit Saffron City, you can give it to me then."

That afternoon, after returning to the laboratory, Ethan still didn't rest. Instead, he busied himself helping Professor Cerise and the others with various tasks.

Besides Professor Cerise, Parker and Ren also taught him many things, such as writing observation notes, using simple instruments, and analyzing the essence of various phenomena. They truly treated him as part of the laboratory staff.

Taking advantage of the opportunity, Ethan studied seriously and humbly learned from the two of them, absorbing as much Pokémon-related knowledge as he possibly could.

…

In the blink of an eye, Monday arrived. Once Chloe returned to school, Ethan once again set out on a new journey.

This time, his destination was Fuchsia City, home of the Fuchsia Gym!

The straight-line distance between Fuchsia City and Vermilion City was not actually very far. However, because a large sea separated them, there were only two possible ways to get there.

The first option was traveling by land—heading east from Vermilion City, crossing deep mountains outside the city, then circling along the coastline southwestward until finally reaching Fuchsia City.

But this route would require traveling over ten times farther while also being extremely time-consuming and exhausting. Clearly, it was not worth the trouble.

The second option was much simpler: taking a ship directly across the sea.

However, after thinking for a while, Ethan decided to try a method he had never experienced before!

"Come out, Gyarados!"

As the Poké Ball opened, a long-bodied Pokémon with a dragon-like head appeared in the ocean.

Ethan leapt forward and landed steadily on Gyarados's back.

Ever since arriving in this world, he had always wanted to experience riding a Water-type Pokémon across the sea. Now that the perfect opportunity had finally appeared, one of his wishes was at last fulfilled!

Locking onto the direction, Ethan let out an excited cheer as Gyarados surged forward with most of its body above the water, rapidly swimming toward the opposite shore.

The endless ocean, blue skies, and white clouds made people feel refreshed and relaxed. Even the salty sea breeze blowing against his body felt especially pleasant.

Combined with the occasional passing ships and the Pokémon that occasionally leapt from the sea, this otherwise ordinary trip became filled with little surprises and moments of joy.

Yet after enough time passed, even the most beautiful scenery gradually became dull and monotonous.

Ethan yawned. Just as boredom began creeping in, a violent gust of wind suddenly swept over him. Looking into the distance, he saw a fierce storm rapidly approaching from the horizon!

Seriously? Why do I always run into this kind of weather whenever I go out to sea!?

Sighing inwardly, Ethan quickly instructed Gyarados to change course. However, the storm was moving far too quickly. In only a few minutes, it had already reached them.

The violent sea wind made it nearly impossible to keep his eyes open, while the pouring rain instantly soaked his clothes!

Just as Ethan was debating whether he should switch to Zapdos and escape through the air instead, massive waves crashed directly into him!

Before the power of nature, both humans and Pokémon seemed unbelievably small.

Gyarados's body was completely beyond his control, constantly rocking among the raging waves!

One of Ethan's arms tightly wrapped around Gyarados's neck while the other reached toward the Ultra Ball at his waist. But at that exact moment, several Pokémon suddenly flew toward him from the distant sky!

Their chubby yellow bodies stood out brilliantly even beneath the dark storm clouds, seemingly unaffected by the storm itself.

Dragonite!!

Before Ethan could understand what they intended to do, one of the Dragonite flew directly in front of him, grabbed him firmly with both claws, and carried him high into the sky without another word!

Ethan looked utterly confused.

What was going on? Had they mistaken him for a shipwreck victim?

Seeing himself being carried farther and farther away, he quickly took out his Poké Ball and recalled Gyarados. However, since he sensed no hostility from these Dragonite, he simply allowed them to carry him away from the storm.

The Dragonite beat their wings continuously. After flying far away from the tempest, they unexpectedly plunged straight into another storm!

Before Ethan could even react in surprise, they had already passed through it, and the scenery before him suddenly opened up completely!

From high above, Ethan looked downward and saw that within the ring-shaped storms there was a lush, isolated island hidden from the outside world!

The sight instantly reminded him of one place—

Dragonite Island!

After the Dragonite landed on the island, Ethan stepped onto the soft beach and looked up, only to find the Dragonite surrounding him, all staring proudly at him.

Their expressions looked exactly like children who had just done a good deed and desperately wanted praise!

"Uh… alright then." Ethan shook his head and cleared his throat before saying sincerely, "Even though I didn't actually need rescuing, I still have to thank you for taking me away from that storm…"

(End of Chapter)
