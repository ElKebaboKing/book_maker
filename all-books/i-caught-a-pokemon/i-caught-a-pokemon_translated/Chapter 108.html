After parting ways with Erika, Ethan soon ran into Misty and her companions once again.

Misty said, "When I was on Cinnabar Island, I got a call from my sisters. They told me I absolutely had to return to the Cerulean Gym. Also, my Horsea hasn't been itself lately—it's been looking listless for some reason. I thought I'd bring it back here for a while and let it live in a larger pool to see if it recovers."

As she spoke, Misty's eyes kept drifting toward Ethan.

Now dressed in his brand-new outfit, Ethan looked even more handsome than before. The sight of him made the girl, who had already developed feelings for him, feel her heart race.

Especially standing beside Ash and Brock—two people who paid almost no attention to their appearance—Ethan seemed even more striking by comparison.

How should she describe the feeling?

Misty thought to herself that it was like comparing a Fearow to a Ho-Oh. Though both were bird Pokémon with somewhat similar appearances, one glance was enough to reveal just how extraordinary Ho-Oh truly was.

The group chatted casually for a while.

Since they had met by chance and Ethan had nothing pressing to do, he accepted Misty's enthusiastic invitation and accompanied them back into Cerulean City.

Talking and laughing along the way, they eventually arrived at the Cerulean Gym.

Looking at the Gym where she had grown up—a place so familiar to her—Misty couldn't help but sigh with emotion.

"I really missed this place. Ever since I left on my journey, it feels like it's been forever since I came back."

Just as they were about to enter, Ethan noticed a billboard standing nearby.

A huge poster covered its surface. It featured many Water-type Pokémon, but the most eye-catching image was that of a beautiful girl dressed like a mermaid, wearing a seashell swimsuit.

Although only half of her delicate face was visible in profile, her graceful figure alone was enough to captivate anyone who looked at it.

Reading the words on the poster, Ethan asked curiously,

"A Magnificent Underwater Ballet Show?"

"It looks like they've introduced a brand-new performance."

"A performance?"

The others gathered around.

The moment Brock saw the mermaid girl, he was instantly mesmerized.

"Wow... she's gorgeous! Looks like we came back at just the right time. We're really in for a treat!"

Ethan then noticed a line of smaller text beneath the title and read it aloud.

"Performed by the famous underwater ballet artist who has triumphantly returned to Cerulean City."

"Huh? Misty... are they talking about you?"

When he pointed it out, everyone looked more carefully.

The girl on the poster really did resemble Misty. Looking closely, it was clearly what she looked like with her hair let down.

"There's no way," Misty said, shaking her head.

"I don't even know how to perform underwater ballet. It must be a guest performer my sisters invited."

Passing through the automatic doors, the group entered the Gym.

The large hall was completely empty.

It was unusually quiet, as though they had arrived on a rest day. The Gym wasn't open to the public, and no performance was currently taking place.

"I'm home!"

Misty called happily.

The very next second, her three sisters—Daisy, Violet, and Lily—hurried out to greet her.

"You're finally back!"

Lily rushed over and grabbed Misty's hands.

"What took you so long? Didn't we tell you to come back as quickly as possible?"

Before Misty could answer, Violet added suspiciously,

"Don't tell me you wandered off somewhere again? You almost made us miss something really important!"

"Alright, that's enough," Daisy interrupted, stopping the other two.

Although she sounded calmer, it was obvious she was deeply relieved that Misty had arrived in time.

"You're later than we'd hoped, but fortunately... you just barely made it."

The three sisters talked over one another nonstop, giving Misty no chance to get a word in.

Completely confused, Misty finally spoke once they quieted down.

"What are you all talking about? What exactly did I make it back in time for?

"Oh, right! Before that—I brought some guests with me."

Only then did the sisters finally notice Ethan and the others.

"Oh! It's you guys!"

"How have you been lately?"

"Misty hasn't caused you too much trouble, has she?"

After greeting Ash and Brock, the three sisters turned toward Ethan.

The moment they got a clear look at his face, their eyes lit up.

Like bees spotting flowers, they all hurried over to him.

Lily pretended to pout.

"Oh, I remember you! You're the one who completely defeated us back then!"

"That's right!"

"So... are you traveling with Misty now?"

"Don't tell us you've set your sights on our little sister?"

Faced with the combined assault of all three sisters, Ethan felt his head beginning to ache.

He quickly shook his head.

"Today's meeting was purely a coincidence. Although we're not traveling together, Misty and I are indeed very good friends."

Hearing Ethan say that made Misty secretly happy.

However, seeing all three of her sisters crowding around him immediately made her jealous.

Puffing out her cheeks, she hurried over and forcefully pushed them aside.

"Alright, alright!"

She looked at her sisters.

"So why did you call me back so urgently?"

Reminded of the real reason, the three sisters immediately became serious again.

"Hurry, Misty! There's no time left! You need to start practicing right away!"

"Huh?"

Misty blinked in confusion.

"What exactly am I supposed to practice?"

"What else?"

Violet pointed toward the poster on the wall.

"The underwater ballet, of course!"

"W-What!?"

Misty cried out in shock.

"You're seriously expecting me to perform in the show!?"

"Of course we are," Lily answered as though it were the most natural thing in the world.

"We've already decided—the role of the Mermaid Princess in the next underwater ballet performance belongs to you!"

"What!?"

Misty stared at them in disbelief.

"Why would you decide something like that without even asking me first?

"And besides, my dream is to become a Water Pokémon Master! How could I possibly go perform in a ballet show?

"No! I refuse! Absolutely not!"

Seeing how determined she was, Violet was about to argue back when Daisy gently stopped her.

"Alright, Misty. Calm down first.

"We can't leave our guests standing around like this.

"Let's bring everyone inside to rest before we continue."

Following Daisy, the group entered the lounge and took seats around a long table.

After Daisy served everyone tea, Ethan and the others finally learned the full story behind everything from the three sisters.
