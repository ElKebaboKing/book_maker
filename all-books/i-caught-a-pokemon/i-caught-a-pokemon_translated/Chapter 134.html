Misa finally noticed Ash and the others.

"Oh! I remember you guys!" she exclaimed in realization. "You're those kids from before, aren't you? So you know Boss Ethan too?"

"That's right, it's us. Thanks again for escorting us across the bridge back then," Brock nodded. Then, looking puzzled, he asked, "But why do you call him 'Boss'?"

Remembering the promise she had made with Ethan, Misa didn't reveal what had actually happened, even though these were his friends.

She shrugged, glanced at them, and joked, "Because after he defeated us, we were completely won over by his incredible strength. That's why we willingly acknowledge Boss Ethan as our leader!"

"I see..."

Ash and the others were far too innocent to doubt her story and accepted it without question.

Misa turned back toward Ethan and clasped her hands together.

"Boss, please do your best! I believe no matter who your opponent is, you'll never lose!"

Masha nodded enthusiastically.

"Exactly! We'll always be your most loyal followers! Please show everyone how amazing you are!"

"You guys..." Ethan sighed with a helpless expression. "You're making me sound invincible. Don't put me on such a high pedestal! Anyone who qualifies for this tournament is incredibly strong. Never underestimate your opponents."

Misa and the others simply grinned.

Although Ethan said that, they had personally witnessed him defeat several Team Rocket Executives and had even seen him command both Zapdos and Articuno. Their confidence in him was absolute.

Not far away, seeing the crowd gathered here, a rather unusual news crew approached, assuming something important was happening.

Holding the microphone was a stylish young woman with an elegant appearance and graceful figure. Her distinctive hairstyle immediately struck Ethan as strangely familiar.

The unusual part of the crew, however, was her companions.

A Gogoat carried all of the filming equipment like a cameraman, while a small Helioptile acted as her assistant.

Walking up to the group, the reporter smiled.

"Hello. We're reporters from Lumiose Press. Would you mind answering a few interview questions?"

Before anyone else could respond, Brock's eyes lit up.

"Ah! Beautiful miss, please interview me instead! No matter how many questions you ask, I'll answer every single one perfectly!"

Afraid such an embarrassing scene might actually be broadcast, Misty quickly smacked Brock on the head, knocking him unconscious before dragging him behind the group.

"Sorry about that," Misty said awkwardly with a smile. "That's just how he is. Please don't mind him."

Then she remembered something.

"But... Lumiose Press? I've never heard of it. Is it a TV station from another city?"

The reporter smiled.

"Lumiose City is one of the major cities in the Kalos Region, far across the sea, so it's only natural you haven't heard of it. We're not a television station, but our publishing company is quite influential back home."

"The Kalos Region?" Chloe, Misty, and Ash all looked surprised before simultaneously turning toward Ethan.

"Isn't that where you're from?"

Ethan nodded.

"You could say that. It's where I became a Pokémon Trainer and first began chasing my dream."

A trainer from Kalos crossing the ocean to challenge the Indigo League?

Thanks to her professional instincts, the reporter immediately realized she had found an excellent story. Once broadcast in Kalos, it would undoubtedly attract considerable attention.

She extended her hand toward Ethan.

"Allow me to introduce myself. My name is Alexa, a special correspondent for Lumiose Press. If possible, I'd like to conduct an exclusive interview with you."

Alexa... So it really is her.

Ethan instantly remembered her.

Not only was she a reporter, but she was also the older sister of Viola, the Gym Leader of Santalune City.

"Interview me?"

After thinking for a moment, Ethan politely shook his head.

"I'm sorry, but I'll have to decline for now. I haven't accomplished anything yet, and I don't want to make bold promises before the tournament starts or raise unnecessary expectations. If you'd still like to interview me after I've made it through the preliminary rounds, then I'd be happy to talk."

Alexa blinked in surprise, finding herself viewing Ethan in a new light.

Most people jumped at opportunities for publicity, especially before a major tournament. She had never expected someone to refuse such exposure.

"Very well," she smiled. "Then I wish you the best of luck in the preliminary rounds. Once you've advanced, we'll talk about that exclusive interview again."

"Ladies and gentlemen! The second match of the Grass Field is about to begin!"

The announcer's energetic voice echoed across the auxiliary battlefield of the Indigo Stadium through the loudspeakers.

"Our sincere thanks to the competitors from the first match for delivering such an exciting battle! And now, let's welcome the two Trainers competing in the second Grass Field match!"

Although this was only the preliminary round—and one of four matches taking place simultaneously—the smaller stadium was still packed with enthusiastic spectators.

As the two Trainers emerged from opposite entrances, thunderous cheers and applause erupted throughout the arena.

Among them, one especially loud group stood out.

Curious spectators turned to look and saw more than a dozen bikers waving banners, flags, and cheering at the top of their lungs.

Their over-the-top enthusiasm made everyone both amused and curious about exactly who they were supporting.

Stepping through the tunnel, Ethan ignored the deafening noise around him as he calmly walked alone onto the Trainer's platform.

Across the field, his opponent stood waiting.

Their eyes met briefly.

No sparks flew between them.

After silently sizing each other up, both turned their attention back to the battlefield.

The announcer raised his voice.

"Representing the Red Side, please welcome Damian!"

Damian waved confidently to the audience, earning loud cheers from the crowd.

Only the biker gang responded with a chorus of loud boos.

Once the cheers subsided, the announcer continued,

"And representing the Green Side, please welcome Ethan Vale!"

Ethan raised a hand in greeting.

The audience answered with warm applause, but no one was louder than the bikers.

"Boss! Give 'em hell!"

"Show that guy what real strength looks like!"

Their enthusiastic shouting echoed throughout the arena, drawing so much attention that the television cameras repeatedly focused on them.

Following the voices, Ethan quickly spotted their location.

He could only smile helplessly before giving them a small nod of acknowledgment.

Then his gaze shifted beside them.

There sat Chloe, Misty, Ash, and Brock, lined up together in the front row, all watching him with anxious anticipation.
