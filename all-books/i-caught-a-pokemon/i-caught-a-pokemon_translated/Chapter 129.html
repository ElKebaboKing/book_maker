The moment Charizard appeared, its overwhelming presence alone gave it the upper hand over Butterfree.

It landed beside Ethan, exhaling a burst of flames as it fixed its gaze firmly on its opponents.

During the final stretch of training before the Indigo Plateau Conference, all of Ethan's Pokémon—except Dratini and Haunter, who had yet to evolve—had already reached their final evolutionary forms.

Watching the two Pokémon prepare to battle, Chloe looked around the dense forest with concern.

"Brother Ethan, are you sure it's okay to use Charizard here? The forest is so dense... won't it start a wildfire?"

Ethan nodded gently.

"That's a valid concern. Don't worry—I'll be careful."

Just as both sides were about to begin, over a dozen shuriken suddenly shot out from the shadows in a connected barrage, flying straight toward Ethan.

Fortunately, they weren't especially fast. Sensing the danger, Ethan quickly leaped aside, narrowly dodging them.

Whoosh! Whoosh! Whoosh!

The shuriken flew past him and embedded themselves diagonally into the grassy ground where he had been standing moments before.

"Who's there!?" Ethan called toward the dense forest.

The next second, a beautiful woman dressed in a purple ninja outfit stepped out from behind a large tree.

It was none other than Aya, the younger sister of Fuchsia Gym Leader Koga.

The moment Ethan saw her, he immediately remembered everything.

Uh-oh...

"Not bad." Aya looked him over with a cold smile. "If I'd known your reflexes were this good, I would've aimed for your head."

Ethan laughed awkwardly.

"What a coincidence, Miss Aya. What brings you all the way here? Training?"

The reason he had avoided the shuriken was simple. After traveling for so long, not only had his Pokémon grown stronger, but his own physical abilities had improved as well.

He glanced at the shuriken on the ground.

None of them were sharpened, meaning that even if they had hit him, they would've only left bruises rather than causing any serious injury.

Even Haunter, hiding in his shadow, and Gardevoir beside him hadn't made a move. They had clearly recognized Aya beforehand and sensed no real hostility from her.

"Training?" Aya snorted angrily. "Would I come all the way out here just for training? Stop playing dumb! I finally caught the liar who wasted so much of my time!"

"Liar?" Chloe blinked in surprise.

Her imagination immediately ran wild, picturing a dramatic romance straight out of the novels her classmates often shared—a story of betrayal and heartbreak.

Ethan hurriedly waved his hands.

"Hey! Miss Aya, don't say things like that! People are going to misunderstand!"

Chloe was burning with curiosity.

What exactly did Brother Ethan lie to her about?

Thankfully, Aya answered the question herself.

"Misunderstand? You promised you'd battle me and then completely disappeared! I waited for you at the Gym for so long before my brother finally told me you'd already returned to Kanto!"

Ethan scratched his head sheepishly.

Back then, he'd only promised to battle her later so he could get away quickly and continue toward the Seafoam Islands.

He had never expected her to remember it this long.

"I... I'm sorry. I honestly forgot."

"Enough excuses." Aya waved him off impatiently. "We'll settle both old and new scores right here. I'll make up for my defeat at the Gym!"

Before Ethan could answer, the long-forgotten Samurai youth stepped forward.

"Hey! Miss, I was here first. I don't know what grudge you have against him, but if anyone battles him first, it should be me!"

"No chance!" Aya shot him an annoyed look. "I spent ages waiting here to catch him. Of course I battle him first! Besides, kid, if you keep getting in my way, I don't mind defeating you too!"

"What did you say!?"

The Samurai youth angrily drew his sword and pointed it at Aya.

Instead of fighting Ethan, the two of them began arguing with each other.

After several heated exchanges, they finally realized the decision rested with Ethan and turned toward him simultaneously.

"So?" Aya asked. "Who are you battling first?"

"..."

Ethan hesitated.

Somehow, the difficult choice had landed on him.

Looking from one to the other, it was obvious neither would back down.

Finally, he gritted his teeth.

"In that case... both of you can come at me together."

"What!?" Aya stared in surprise.

"You want to fight both of us at once?"

When Ethan nodded, even the Samurai youth frowned.

"You've got some nerve looking down on us like this."

"For once, we actually agree," Aya said with a cold glance toward him before turning back to Ethan. "You're awfully confident. I hope you won't regret it after you lose! Go, Venomoth!"

She threw a Poké Ball, releasing a large moth Pokémon.

Pokédex:

"Venomoth, the Poison Moth Pokémon. A Bug/Poison-type and the evolved form of Venonat. The powder covering its wings contains different toxins depending on the color. It is nocturnal and becomes active after dark, often feeding on insects attracted to city lights."

Looking at the butterfly and moth before him, Ethan rubbed his chin.

A butterfly and a moth...

Interesting combination.

After a brief moment of thought, he released his second Pokémon.

"Your turn, Blastoise!"

A flash of blue light burst forth, and Blastoise landed heavily on the ground.

Even with the thick grass cushioning the impact, its massive body still caused the earth to tremble slightly.

"Charizard, Flamethrower! Blastoise, Water Gun!"

Handling both Pokémon at once, Ethan issued two commands simultaneously.

On the opposite side, Aya and the Samurai youth responded.

"Butterfree, Stun Spore!" the Samurai shouted.

"Venomoth, Stun Spore!" Aya called.

"...!?"

The moment the words left their mouths, they realized they had ordered the exact same move.

They glanced awkwardly at each other before Aya quickly looked away.

It wasn't surprising.

Since they had never fought together before, they had no teamwork whatsoever.

Besides, both Butterfree and Venomoth excelled at using powders such as Poison Powder, Stun Spore, and Sleep Powder, making overlapping commands almost inevitable.

Clouds of yellow powder filled the air but were immediately scattered by Charizard's flames and Blastoise's jet of water.

Butterfree and Venomoth hurriedly took flight to avoid the attacks.

As soon as they steadied themselves, Aya shouted again.

"Venomoth, use Psybeam!"

The strange beam struck Blastoise, dealing damage and, by sheer luck, leaving it confused.

Blastoise stood there uneasily.

Before Aya could celebrate, Butterfree's Confusion followed immediately afterward.

Although it dealt damage as well, it also knocked Blastoise out of its confused state.

Aya couldn't take it anymore.

"Hey!!" she yelled at the Samurai. "Do you even know how to work with a partner? Stop sabotaging me!"

The Samurai glanced away guiltily.

Knowing he'd made a mistake, he wisely kept quiet.

Aya took a deep breath.

"Listen carefully. From now on, each of us focuses on one target. Don't make the same mistake again!"

After a brief adjustment, they began fighting separately.

But by then, Charizard and Blastoise had already seized complete control of the battle.

Unlike his opponents, Ethan never had to worry about coordinating with another trainer.

Even Chloe, the only person who might distract him, quietly stood behind him without making a sound, afraid of interrupting his concentration.

The battle raged on.

Flames and water blasts flew across the battlefield.

Though Butterfree and Venomoth were agile, they continued taking damage.

At one point, the combination of fire and water even filled the area with thick steam.

Butterfree accidentally flew straight into Charizard's Flamethrower, suffering a burn.

Before it could recover, Blastoise's Water Gun struck it head-on.

The sudden burst of steam surrounded its body.

Hiss—!

The burn, the impact, and the steam combined into devastating damage.

Unable to endure any longer, Butterfree's vision went black.

It lost consciousness and dropped straight to the ground.

"Nicely done."

Ethan nodded in approval.

If he remembered correctly, this Butterfree had originally been the Samurai youth's Caterpie.

Although it had evolved, the boy had spent so much time training exclusively inside Viridian Forest that it had never learned many advanced techniques.

Compared to him, Aya's experience and her Venomoth made her the more dangerous opponent.

Now that only one enemy remained, Ethan calmly had Charizard and Blastoise focus all their attacks on Venomoth.

Against such an overwhelming two-on-one assault, Venomoth had little chance.

Before long, Charizard's Dragon Rage slammed it into the ground, knocking it unconscious.

Ethan let out a quiet sigh of relief.

Thanks to his previous experience commanding multiple Pokémon against powerful opponents, this double battle had actually felt rather easy.

Part of it was the difference in strength.

The other part was that Aya and the Samurai youth constantly interfered with one another, wasting valuable opportunities.

Unwilling to accept defeat, both trainers immediately threw another Poké Ball, releasing their second Pokémon.

"...Don't tell me you're planning to keep sending out Pokémon one after another?" Ethan muttered.

Before either of them could answer, the nearby bushes suddenly rustled.

A small, round pink figure bounced out.

It had huge eyes, chubby cheeks, and an irresistibly adorable appearance.

There was no mistaking it.

It was a Jigglypuff.

J-Jigglypuff!?

Ethan's eyelid twitched.

His gaze immediately fell to the microphone in its tiny hand.

Oh no... It's that pink demon!

There might be countless Jigglypuff in the world...

But only this microphone-carrying Jigglypuff inspired absolute terror.

Why is it here!?

Jigglypuff looked around.

Seeing such a large audience, it apparently decided this was the perfect venue for a concert.

Slowly, it raised its microphone.

Seeing this, Ethan shouted in panic,

"Oh no! Whatever you do, don't let it start singing!"

Aya and the Samurai youth had never seen this particular Jigglypuff before.

Although they heard Ethan's warning, they looked utterly confused.

"A wild Jigglypuff? It's so adorable!"

Aya, especially, was completely captivated by its cute appearance.

Instead of being cautious, she even reached for a Poké Ball, intending to catch it.

Jigglypuff paid no attention to anyone.

It only wanted to show off its beautiful singing voice.

"Jiggly... Jiggly... Jiggly... Puff... ♪"

Its gentle melody echoed through the microphone, spreading clearly across the forest.

As soon as it began singing, everyone's eyelids grew heavier and heavier.

Their consciousness gradually faded.

"Jiggly... Puff... Jiggly... ♪"

Jigglypuff thought its performance was magnificent.

Yet after waiting for applause that never came, it finally realized something.

Every single human and Pokémon had fallen asleep.

"Puff!!"

Pouting angrily, Jigglypuff jumped onto Aya's chest.

Then it pulled out the marker hidden inside its microphone and began happily doodling all over everyone's faces.

When Ethan finally woke up, it was already the next morning.

The crisp morning air brushed against his exposed skin, raising goosebumps.

Looking around, he saw Pokémon sprawled everywhere.

As for Jigglypuff, it had long since disappeared.

His eyes swept over everyone.

Each face was covered with thick black marker scribbles.

Seeing the scene, Ethan could only smile bitterly.

Even without a mirror, he knew his own face had undoubtedly suffered the exact same fate.
