Facing the ferocious Gyarados, Blaine naturally had no intention of backing down.

"Rapidash, use Stomp!"

Gyarados's tail became wrapped in rushing water as it viciously swung toward Rapidash. At the same time, Rapidash raised its front legs high into the air, preparing to stomp down onto Gyarados.

However, the moment the two Pokémon collided, before Rapidash's hooves could even come down, its body was already struck by the overwhelming force of Aqua Tail.

Bang!

Like a ball being swatted away, Rapidash was violently flung more than ten meters across the battlefield!

Water beats Fire—it's super effective!

Shaking its head, Rapidash struggled back to its feet, and Blaine immediately shouted,

"Rapidash, use Flame Charge!"

The flames covering Rapidash's body instantly surged upward until it was completely engulfed in fire, turning into a blazing fireball!

Then it kicked off with incredible speed and charged straight at Gyarados once more!

Since Flame Charge was a contact move, Ethan knew physical collision was unavoidable.

Rather than dodge, he calmly chose to meet force with force.

"One more time! Aqua Tail!!"

The two Pokémon slammed into each other, causing water and fire to erupt into clouds of steaming vapor.

Yet in the end, Aqua Tail's power proved superior once again, injuring Rapidash and knocking it backward several steps.

Blaine frowned slightly.

After two exchanges, Rapidash had taken heavy damage while Gyarados remained dominant. He had already realized just how troublesome Ethan's Gyarados truly was.

But unfortunately, there wasn't much he could do about it.

After all, Rapidash specialized primarily in close-range combat. Against an immensely powerful Water-type Pokémon like Gyarados, it had encountered its absolute worst matchup.

Seeing Blaine fall silent, Ethan naturally had no idea how complicated his thoughts were.

All he knew was that his attacks were working, so he pressed the advantage immediately.

"Gyarados, don't give it a chance to breathe!"

Facing the savage-looking Gyarados, Blaine quickly ordered,

"Rapidash, use Flame Wheel!"

More than a dozen small fireballs appeared around Rapidash, spinning rapidly around its body.

But just as it prepared to charge Gyarados again, it suddenly met Gyarados's massive, fang-filled maw.

Still affected by Intimidate, Rapidash instinctively hesitated in fear. Its hooves faltered, and its body shifted uneasily.

A perfect opportunity!

Ethan's eyes lit up as he instantly seized the fleeting opening.

"Gyarados! Let's tear this place apart!!"

At Ethan's command, Gyarados let out a thunderous roar. Its eyes turned blood-red as all reason vanished, replaced only by violent destruction and pure savagery!

In the next second, its head, body, and tail all became weapons.

Without any technique whatsoever, relying solely on overwhelming brute force, Gyarados unleashed all its power upon Rapidash and the battlefield itself!

Boom! Boom! Boom! Boom!!!

Ten strikes… twenty strikes… thirty strikes…

Under Gyarados's relentless assault, not only did the suspended battlefield shake violently, but even the chains supporting it rattled loudly, as if they might snap apart at any moment!

Dust and shattered debris filled the air, completely obscuring the battlefield from view.

Only after Gyarados finally calmed down and the dust settled did everyone clearly see the result.

Rapidash lay unconscious inside a massive crater, covered in injuries and completely unable to continue battling…

"Yes!!"

Before Ethan could even react, Misty had already jumped up excitedly.

Judging by her expression, she seemed even happier than if she had won the battle herself!

Nearby, Brock and Ash also applauded enthusiastically.

"Come back, Rapidash."

Blaine gently shook his head and recalled his Pokémon.

After securing the first victory, Ethan finally relaxed slightly.

After all, Blaine's effortless defeat of Ash earlier had made Ethan take his strength far more seriously.

But now that Gyarados had defeated Rapidash, much of the pressure weighing on him disappeared, easing his tension considerably.

"Well done," Blaine said while looking at Ethan and releasing another Poké Ball. "Compared to that brat from earlier, you're definitely much harder to deal with. Go, Ninetales!"

The dazzlingly elegant Ninetales returned to the battlefield.

Pokédex: "Ninetales, the Fox Pokémon. A Fire-type and the evolved form of Vulpix. Highly intelligent but extremely vengeful. According to legend, it was born from the fusion of nine sacred sages and possesses golden fur along with nine beautiful tails. It is said to live for one thousand years."

Only a thousand years?

Compared to my Wartortle's lifespan, that's nothing!

Even after the previous battle, Ethan had no intention of switching Pokémon.

"Gyarados, keep it up!"

"ROOOAR!!"

The moment the battle began, Blaine used the same strategy that had defeated Squirtle earlier.

"Ninetales, use Fire Spin!"

"So it's that move again!" Ethan naturally didn't dare underestimate it. "Gyarados, use Whirlpool!"

The flaming vortex and the spiraling water current—two moves with similar effects but opposite elements—shot toward one another and collided head-on.

This time, however, water claimed victory.

Not only did Whirlpool extinguish the flames, but it also struck Ninetales directly.

"Ninetales!" Seeing his drenched Pokémon, Blaine quickly shouted, "Use Confuse Ray!"

A brilliantly colored beam shot from Ninetales's mouth.

"Gyarados, dodge it!"

Although Ethan reacted immediately, Gyarados's speed still wasn't enough.

Confuse Ray struck perfectly.

When the light faded, Gyarados stood there blankly, already trapped in confusion.

"Excellent!" Blaine said. "Ninetales, use Quick Attack!"

Moving at incredible speed, Ninetales slammed directly into Gyarados.

Though it dealt damage, the impact also snapped Gyarados out of its confusion!

"Gyarados, Aqua Tail!"

As Gyarados swung its water-covered tail toward Ninetales, the fox Pokémon hurriedly leaped away, barely avoiding the attack.

Meanwhile, seeing his strategy succeed, Blaine attempted the same tactic again.

"Let's do it one more time—Confuse Ray!"

Again!?

Ethan frowned deeply as he rapidly searched for a solution.

Then his eyes suddenly landed on the thick iron chains suspending the battlefield.

His eyes lit up instantly.

"Gyarados! Break the chain beside you!!"

Although confused, Gyarados obediently swung its Aqua Tail and smashed through the chain in a single blow.

Bang! Bang!

As the chains snapped apart, the battlefield suddenly tilted sharply toward Gyarados's side.

At almost the exact same moment, Ninetales's Confuse Ray barely grazed past Gyarados's horn and missed entirely!

"What!?"

Blaine was shocked.

But before he could react, Ninetales had already begun sliding down the tilted battlefield straight toward Gyarados!

"Now!" Ethan shouted. "Bite it!"

Although Gyarados also slid backward, it used its immense strength to lunge forward violently.

Opening its massive jaws wide, it clamped down directly onto Ninetales!

Its fangs sank deeply into Ninetales's body.

Ninetales cried out miserably and struggled desperately, yet it still couldn't escape from Gyarados's terrifying maw filled with razor-sharp teeth!

( End of Chapter )
