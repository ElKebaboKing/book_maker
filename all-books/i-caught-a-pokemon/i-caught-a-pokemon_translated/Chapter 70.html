Blue psychic energy enveloped Ethan, protecting his mind and consciousness.

And it was precisely because of Kirlia's psychic power that Ethan finally broke free from Mewtwo's influence and could no longer hear its telepathic voice.

Though he secretly breathed a sigh of relief, when he looked at Mewtwo again, his eyes were filled with vigilance and caution. In that brief instant just now, he had no idea how many of his secrets and private memories Mewtwo had managed to glimpse!

In the battlefield below, Mewtwo—who had previously been overwhelming everyone without a single opponent able to withstand it—suddenly stopped moving.

Its body floated motionlessly in midair, completely still, while only its thick tail continued swaying restlessly, as though it had fallen into extreme agitation.

This sudden change caused Lance, Agatha, Handsome Guy, Nanu, and the others to frown deeply. However, since none of them knew what Mewtwo was doing, no one dared issue attack commands rashly. Their Pokémon, having received no instructions, also stopped fighting and began panting heavily.

Giovanni narrowed his eyes.

This situation with Mewtwo had already happened once before, back in the laboratory where it had been created. At the time, it had questioned its own existence, unable to understand the meaning of life and being alive. As a result, it had revealed its violent side and used apocalyptic power to destroy everything on the island!

And now, this scene once again reminded Giovanni of that hellish sea of flames.

"What are you doing? Defeat them!" Giovanni's voice echoed throughout the hall. "Don't forget—you must learn to control your power. Otherwise, all you will bring to this world is destruction!"

Yet Mewtwo remained completely unmoved.

Seeing this, Giovanni immediately sensed something was wrong. He glanced at his four subordinates before slowly walking toward the elevator nearby.

"Giovanni, where are you going!?" Handsome Guy shouted.

Giovanni ignored him. Taking out a keycard, he swiped it across the control panel and opened the tightly sealed elevator doors.

"Damn it!" Handsome Guy clenched his fists and turned toward a nearby SWAT officer. "Where does that elevator lead? Is the exit guarded?"

The officer hesitated before replying, "We tried cutting the power earlier, but that elevator seems to run independently. Without a keycard, we can't control it at all!"

"Damn it! We can't let him escape!" Handsome Guy shouted. Then he yelled to his Pokémon, "Croagunk! Growlithe! Stop them!"

Just as the two Pokémon were about to move, Mewtwo suddenly erupted with dazzling blue light!

A violent and irresistible force exploded outward, generating a terrifying pressure wave that blasted in every direction. Everyone instinctively narrowed their eyes before being hurled to the ground by the shockwave.

When they finally recovered, they saw Mewtwo flying upward, smashing through several layers of ceiling before vanishing completely!

Its armor scattered across the floor, yet no one had managed to glimpse its true appearance.

"It left!?"

Lance looked up at the massive hole in the ceiling, unable to understand why this mysterious Pokémon had suddenly abandoned its Trainer and fled in the middle of battle.

"Giovanni!"

Handsome Guy shouted and quickly looked ahead, only to discover Giovanni and the executives had already entered the elevator.

The heavy doors slowly closed, revealing only Giovanni's slightly gloomy face.

"This is bad! After them!"

Knowing there was no way to stop the elevator, Handsome Guy immediately turned and rushed back toward the stairs with the SWAT team in pursuit.

Ethan climbed to his feet and helped Sabrina and Erika up one after another.

"That Pokémon was unbelievably strong," Erika said with lingering fear. "What exactly does it look like beneath that armor? Is it some newly discovered Legendary Pokémon?"

"I don't know. It's my first time seeing a Pokémon like that too," Sabrina replied.

Then, remembering Ethan's strange reaction earlier, she looked at him with faint concern in her eyes.

"What happened to you just now?"

Ethan remained silent for a moment before simply shaking his head without explaining further.

Some secrets were destined to rot away in silence, never to be revealed to anyone.

Giovanni's escape meant this operation could be considered both a success and a failure.

The success was that they had captured a long-established Team Rocket base, obtained a huge amount of data, and arrested many Team Rocket members, dealing the organization massive losses.

But the failure was equally obvious—they had failed to capture even a single high-ranking executive.

…

After continuous battles and being dragged into the Team Rocket incident, both Ethan's body and mind were exhausted. So after saying goodbye to everyone and returning to Vermilion City once again, he decisively gave himself a short vacation to rest for two days.

"Charmeleon, use Flamethrower! Jolteon, dodge quickly! Then use Thunder Fang!"

Inside Cerise Park, Ethan lounged lazily in a reclining chair while simultaneously commanding two Pokémon battling each other.

Meanwhile, Chloe sat nearby, gently brushing Kirlia's hair with a wooden comb. Seeing Ethan acting so relaxed, she couldn't help but laugh softly.

"You're way too lazy. If you command them like that, there's no way they'll ever decide a winner!"

"This is called me fighting myself. The goal is just to train them," Ethan said before sighing lightly. "But I can't help it. I'm the only Trainer here. Even if I wanted someone else to help train with me, there's nobody around."

Chloe happened to be there simply because it was the weekend and she had free time to come help.

"Weren't you always saying that traveling is what helps Pokémon grow stronger? Why are you suddenly resting now?"

Ethan looked up at the sky and stretched lazily.

"I'm only human after all. Resting is just to better prepare myself for the next journey."

"Oh, right!" At the mention of traveling, Chloe suddenly remembered something. "Let me show you something interesting!"

She stood up, walked beside Ethan, took out her phone, and opened a video.

When Ethan leaned over to look, it was footage of the day he used Zapdos to battle the giant Tentacruel at Porta Vista Harbor!

Instantly, he became fully alert and watched carefully.

To be fair, although the video quality was somewhat blurry, with the raincoat and mask covering him from head to toe, nobody could identify him at all.

And after being edited with dramatic filters, the battle looked even more spectacular!

Chloe continued, "This video's been really popular these past few days. The boys in our class are practically obsessed with it. Since you're a Trainer too, I figured you'd probably like it."

"Of course!" Ethan quickly agreed. "Just look at that Zapdos! Its Electric-type moves are incredibly powerful. You can tell immediately how amazing it is!"

The Ultra Ball at his waist shook slightly, clearly showing that Zapdos was very pleased by Ethan's flattery.

Chloe nodded.

"It really is amazing. But honestly, that Trainer's outfit is pretty ugly."

"…Ugly!?"

Ethan's face darkened as the corners of his mouth twitched.

"Sure, the outfit might be a little strange, but it's not ugly, right? Look—the black cloak together with the Gengar mask gives off a mysterious vibe, doesn't it?"
