Zapdos let out a sharp cry and spread its wings wide. Electricity crackled violently around its body, and with a single Thunderbolt, it completely neutralized all incoming attacks.

Ethan Vale could not help but praise it.

"As expected of a Legendary Pokémon. Even while facing multiple opponents alone, you can still handle everything so effortlessly!"

However, perhaps finding him too noisy, Zapdos suddenly fired a bolt of electricity directly at him.

Startled, Ethan Vale quickly ducked behind the doorway, barely escaping the fate of being roasted alive.

Poking his head out again, he shouted:

"Kirlia, find a way to restrict its movement!"

Kirlia raised both hands, surrounding Zapdos with invisible psychic power. But perhaps because the gap in level, strength, and base stats was simply too great, Zapdos only struggled briefly before breaking free of the restraint.

After gliding beneath the skylight in a circle, Zapdos suddenly began spinning rapidly. Its sharp beak glowed brightly as it charged straight toward Ivysaur.

"Not good—it's Drill Peck! Charmander, use Smokescreen!"

Drill Peck was a Flying-type move that not only had a type advantage against Grass-types, but considering Zapdos's strength, a direct hit on Ivysaur would easily knock it out instantly.

Hearing the command, Charmander opened its mouth and released a thick cloud of black smoke, covering the room and hiding everyone's positions.

Zapdos lost sight of its target.

The next second, its attack veered off course and slammed heavily into the ground instead. The impact blasted a huge crater into the floor, and even the entire power chamber shook violently.

Missing its target, Zapdos soared upward once more. But with electricity still flashing around its body, it glowed inside the smoke like a bright lightbulb, making it impossible to miss.

Suddenly, a stream of water and a burst of flames pierced through the smoke from opposite directions, striking Zapdos simultaneously.

Zapdos cried out in pain before violently flapping its wings, dispersing the entire Smokescreen.

At that exact moment, two vines shot upward from below and wrapped tightly around Zapdos's wings.

"Ivysaur! Nice job!" Ethan Vale's eyes lit up. "Pull it down!"

Ivysaur strained with all its strength, trying to drag Zapdos to the ground. But Zapdos beat its wings furiously, forcing the two sides into a stalemate midair.

"Jolteon, use Quick Attack!"

Jolteon's speed suddenly surged. It sprinted across the walls with several rapid steps before leaping high into the air and slamming directly into Zapdos.

Although Quick Attack contained the word "Quick," it was actually a Normal-type move. Still, after being struck, Zapdos lost control of its balance for a brief moment, allowing Ivysaur to yank it down from the air.

"Now's our chance!" Ethan Vale shouted. "Everyone attack together!"

All of his Pokémon launched their attacks simultaneously, and once again several moves struck Zapdos head-on.

This time, after taking the full force of the attacks, Zapdos let out a painful cry.

Yet as expected of a Legendary Pokémon, even after enduring such concentrated attacks, it still refused to fall. Electricity instantly covered its body as it unleashed another Thunderbolt!

Wild, violent currents of electricity exploded throughout the power chamber, indiscriminately attacking everything nearby.

For a moment, not only did Ethan Vale's Pokémon scatter to avoid the attacks, but even the power plant's machinery suffered massive damage.

When Ethan Vale peeked out from behind the doorway again, explosions, smoke, sparks, and broken machines had become the dominant scenery inside the chamber.

His eyes quickly scanned the battlefield to check on his Pokémon.

Aside from Jolteon, all of them had suffered varying degrees of injury and looked battered. Fortunately, none had collapsed yet. Even Squirtle had used Withdraw at the last moment to protect itself.

Zapdos soared high above them, glaring down at the tiny Pokémon it viewed as insignificant. Flames of rage seemed to burn within its eyes.

After suffering such humiliation, it was now truly furious.

With a deafening screech, dark storm clouds suddenly gathered above the power plant through the shattered skylight.

Within mere seconds, thick clouds covered the entire sky, plunging everything into darkness.

Lightning flashed continuously within the clouds, as though a terrifying thunderstorm was brewing overhead.

Swallowing nervously, Ethan Vale was just about to issue another command when his phone suddenly rang.

Without thinking, he answered immediately. The moment he activated speaker mode, Professor Cerise's anxious voice came through the phone.

"Ethan Vale, are you alright? I think I just heard explosions! And now the entire area—including the laboratory—has lost power! What exactly is happening over there?"

Looking up at the storm clouds, Ethan Vale gave a bitter smile.

"Sorry, Professor. Things got a little out of hand here. But no matter how this ends... I think it'll all be over very soon!"

Just as he finished speaking, an enormous bolt of lightning crashed down from the sky, piercing through the skylight and engulfing the entire power chamber.

Thunder.

An Electric-type move with devastating power and massive range, capable of engulfing all enemies within its area. Even if the damage failed to defeat the target, it still had a chance of inflicting paralysis.

At that moment, all the Pokémon inside the power chamber had nowhere to escape.

But at the critical instant—

A small figure suddenly leapt into the air and charged directly toward the incoming lightning bolt.

It was Jolteon!!

Before Ethan Vale could even shout, the deafening roar of thunder exploded throughout the room.

The lightning struck Jolteon directly, releasing a blinding flash so intense it nearly burned Ethan Vale's eyes.

When everything finally settled, Ethan Vale hurriedly looked toward Jolteon.

To his utter shock, Jolteon remained standing. Though breathing heavily, it had suffered absolutely no damage from the lightning!

After staring blankly for a moment, Ethan Vale suddenly remembered Jolteon's Ability.

Volt Absorb: Pokémon with this Ability are unaffected by Electric-type moves. When hit by an Electric-type attack, they take no damage and instead recover a small amount of health.

"That's amazing, Jolteon!"

Meanwhile, high above, Zapdos had clearly not expected such a result either.

During the brief moment it paused in surprise, Kirlia used Confusion while Ivysaur lashed out with its vines, trapping Zapdos simultaneously.

"Don't give it another chance to fight back!" Ethan Vale shouted. "Everyone, finish it now!"

A colorful barrage of attacks struck Zapdos all at once.

This time, it could no longer endure the damage and finally crashed down from the sky!

Now was the chance!

Ethan Vale immediately pulled out a Poké Ball and threw it.

The Poké Ball traced a perfect arc through the air before striking Zapdos directly. A flash of red light engulfed the Legendary Pokémon as it was pulled inside the ball—

But in the very next second, Zapdos burst back out again!

It still had strength left!?

"Everyone, keep pushing! Attack again!"

Several more attacks struck Zapdos. It let out a mournful cry, yet with the last of its strength, it unleashed another Thunderbolt!

BZZZZZT!!

After the blinding flash faded, Ethan Vale's Pokémon finally collapsed one after another, unable to withstand the damage any longer.

Only Jolteon remained standing.

"Damn it!" Knowing this was the decisive moment and that giving up now was impossible, Ethan Vale gritted his teeth and shouted:

"Jolteon, Quick Attack!!"

Jolteon accelerated instantly, moving like a shooting star as it slammed violently into Zapdos!

This time, Zapdos could no longer resist.

With one final weak cry, it collapsed onto the ground.

Ethan Vale's heart was pounding so hard it felt ready to burst from his chest.

He pulled out another Poké Ball and threw it at Zapdos.

A flash of red light swallowed the Legendary Pokémon once again.

The Poké Ball shook violently once... twice... three times...

Then, several seconds later—

Click.

The button dimmed.

Slowly picking up the red-and-white Poké Ball, Ethan Vale stared at it blankly in disbelief.

I actually captured Zapdos...!?
