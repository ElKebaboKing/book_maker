The heavy rain lasted only a short while before finally coming to an end.

Once the skies cleared, Ethan invited Ash and the others to a nearby restaurant for a lavish lunch, treating them to a proper meal after their long journey of rough travel and living off the road.

"This ish sho delishious!"

Ash held a fork in his left hand and chopsticks in his right, stuffing his mouth so full of food that he could barely speak. His eating manners were as exaggerated as ever.

"I haven't eaten anything this tasty in a long time!"

As he spoke, his gaze shifted between Pikachu, who was devouring food like a whirlwind, and Gardevoir, who was gracefully eating with all the elegance of a refined lady.

Suddenly, he remembered something and quickly swallowed the food in his mouth.

"Oh, right, Ethan!"

"We've known each other for so long, but we've never battled!"

"How about having a match with me after this?"

Before Ethan could answer, Brock became interested as well.

"Oh?"

"You two are going to battle?"

"That should be exciting!"

"Exciting?" Misty rolled her eyes.

Whether emotionally or logically, she had already chosen to stand firmly on Ethan's side.

"With your current skill level, there's no way you can beat Ethan."

"Come on, Misty." Ethan waved his hand, stopping her.

"You shouldn't say that."

"In Pokémon battles, there's never such a thing as a guaranteed victory."

"A lot depends on the Pokémon's condition and how both Trainers perform during the battle."

"You never truly know who's going to win until it's over."

Ash continued staring at him expectantly.

Seeing that, Ethan agreed without hesitation.

"Sure."

"I'd love to."

"I've always believed in your strength."

"And besides, a good battle after a meal is perfect for helping digestion."

"Awesome!"

Ash's eyes lit up with excitement.

"Then let's go right away!"

"Oh, by the way, is there a battlefield nearby?"

Ethan chuckled.

"Why are you in such a hurry?"

"The nearest battlefield..."

He thought for a moment.

"Would probably be the backyard of Professor Cerise's Laboratory."

"There's a simple practice field there."

"Hardly anyone ever uses it, so it's perfect for a battle."

After finishing their meal, Ethan paid the bill and led everyone across the city to the rear grounds of Professor Cerise's Laboratory.

The moment they arrived, Ash's attention was immediately captured by the enormous glass-domed Cerise Park nearby.

"Wow!"

"What a huge greenhouse park!"

"There's even a river inside!"

"This is Professor Cerise's Laboratory," Ethan explained, pointing around the area as he introduced the different facilities.

Then he smiled apologetically.

"Unfortunately, I'm only staying here temporarily."

"So it wouldn't really be appropriate for me to invite everyone inside for a tour."

"That's okay."

"We're happy just looking from outside."

Fortunately, Ash and the others didn't mind.

After chatting around the park for a while, everyone's attention quickly returned to the upcoming battle.

The battlefield had gone unused for so long that it was completely overgrown with grass.

Instead of clearing it away, Ethan and Ash simply treated it as a Grass-type battlefield.

Standing on opposite sides of the field, Ethan said,

"I'm not very good at making complicated battle rules."

"So how about a simple one-on-one match?"

"One battle."

"Winner takes all."

"What do you think?"

Ash had no objections.

He nodded before looking down beside him.

"Go, Pikachu!"

"Pika!"

Pikachu cried energetically and ran to the center of the battlefield, its tail standing proudly upright, just as excited as its Trainer.

Ethan stroked his chin thoughtfully.

As expected...

He chose Pikachu, the partner he trusts most.

After a brief moment of thought, he gently waved his hand.

Understanding immediately, Gardevoir calmly walked onto the battlefield.

These two Pokémon were each Trainer's very first partner.

They carried extraordinary meaning for both of them.

Although Gardevoir had already reached its final evolution...

Just as Ethan had once said...

There was no such thing as a truly weak Pokémon.

Nor was there any absolute peak of strength.

Every Pokémon possessed tremendous hidden potential.

The strong could lose.

The weak could overcome the strong.

The world was unpredictable.

Even a tiny Wishiwashi could create miracles.

Just then, the commotion outside attracted someone's attention from inside the laboratory.

Footsteps approached.

A moment later, Chloe slowly walked over.

When she reached them and saw Ethan, she smiled softly.

"Ethan."

"Did your friends come to visit?"

Ethan waved to her in greeting before introducing both sides.

"These are friends I met during my journey."

"They're all trustworthy companions."

Ash smiled brightly and introduced himself first.

"Hi!"

"I'm Ash from Pallet Town!"

"My dream is to become a Pokémon Master!"

"Pika Pi!"

"Idiot."

"You don't need to introduce your dream every single time."

Brock sighed helplessly before smiling at Chloe.

"Hello."

"I'm Brock."

"I'm Ash's traveling companion."

Misty also smiled.

"My name is Misty."

"It's nice to meet you."

Chloe's personality was naturally quiet.

She wasn't someone who usually initiated conversations with strangers.

However, after hearing that they were Ethan's friends, she politely nodded.

"Hello."

"My name is Chloe Cerise."

Knowing her personality well, Ethan continued the introduction for her.

"Chloe is still in school."

"Her father is the well-known Professor Cerise."

"This laboratory behind us, along with Cerise Park next to it, were both established by him."

"A famous Professor?" Ash blinked.

"Like Professor Oak?"

Ethan laughed.

"Not quite as famous or knowledgeable as Professor Oak."

"But among Pokémon Professors, he's definitely an outstanding researcher."

Chloe quietly stood beside Ethan, silently listening to the conversation.

However...

For some unknown reason...

The moment Chloe's eyes met Misty's...

Both girls seemed to sense something.

Almost instinctively, they both glanced at Ethan...

Then immediately noticed each other's gaze.

Could this girl also...

Although it was only their first meeting...

Both of them had already keenly realized...

That the other seemed to harbor special feelings for Ethan as well.

This is bad...

Both girls' hearts tightened.

Even in the warmth of early summer...

They suddenly felt an icy chill.

Their expressions subtly changed.

It was as if invisible lightning flashed from their eyes, colliding in midair.

Neither was willing to back down.

Meanwhile...

Ethan, the completely oblivious male lead of this unfolding romantic drama, noticed none of it.

His entire focus was already fixed on the battle that was about to begin.
