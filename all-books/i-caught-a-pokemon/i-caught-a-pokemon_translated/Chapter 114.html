Inside the Cerulean Gym's lounge, after all the audience had left, Ethan Vale and the others returned, collapsing into their chairs without caring about appearances. Every one of them was mentally and physically exhausted.

"You all worked so hard!" Sakura said with a smile as she handed everyone a bottle of water. "Did you see the audience's reaction? They absolutely loved the performance! It completely exceeded our expectations. Especially Ethan and Ayame's kiss at the end—it was the perfect finishing touch to the entire play!"

Please stop talking about it, Sakura...

Misty felt like crying.

She desperately wanted to ask:

What am I supposed to do when the person I like kisses my second eldest sister right in front of me?

Noticing something was wrong, Sakura walked over and gently patted Misty's head.

"I'm sorry I chose not to rescue you back then. Are you alright? You weren't hurt, were you?"

Misty glanced at Ethan, forced a smile, and nodded calmly.

"I'm fine. Really... I'm okay. Don't worry about me, Sis..."

Meanwhile, Ayame smiled mischievously at Ethan when the conversation returned to the kiss.

"Sigh, you really took advantage of me back there. So? Were my lips soft?"

Pfft!

Instantly, Misty felt as if a knife had pierced straight through her heart.

Please... stop talking...

Ethan shrugged.

"If anyone took advantage, it goes both ways. You know, I suffered a loss too."

After all, it had only been acting, forced by the circumstances. The fact that they could joke about it so casually only proved that neither of them was taking the kiss seriously.

"So it was a free kiss, huh?" Ayame teased with a grin. "Judging by your reaction, this definitely wasn't your first time kissing a girl."

Pffft!!

Misty immediately choked on the water she had just drunk.

It felt as though a second knife had stabbed straight into her heart.

After saying goodbye to Ash and the others once again, Ethan returned to Vermilion City.

Now that he had collected all eight Gym Badges, he officially qualified to compete in the Indigo League.

There were still more than two weeks before registration for the Indigo League began. Although he no longer needed to travel from Gym to Gym, he couldn't afford to relax. He had to prepare himself thoroughly for the upcoming tournament.

Everyone at the Cerise Laboratory was delighted to see Ethan return. Chloe, especially, came to visit him immediately after school as soon as she heard the news.

"Wow!"

She circled Gardevoir several times, admiring the Pokémon that had grown even taller than herself.

"So this is Ralts' final evolution? She's so beautiful!"

She then picked up Ethan's Badge Case and carefully examined each of the eight shining Gym Badges one by one.

"You like these badges too?" Ethan asked with a smile. "Maybe you should challenge the Gyms yourself someday."

Chloe shook her head.

"Pokémon battles are exciting to watch, but they don't really suit me. Oh, and I already talked to Dad. When the Indigo League starts, we'll all come watch your matches!"

Over the next several days, Ethan's life settled back into a peaceful routine.

He searched online for different training methods and also took advice from Professor Cerise. Based on each of his Pokémon's individual strengths, he created several specialized training programs.

Since Zapdos and Articuno couldn't be used in official League battles, he only trained them alongside Haunter whenever no one else was around, gradually increasing their strength step by step.

Meanwhile, Gardevoir, Venusaur, Gyarados, and Jolteon formed the core of his regular battle team.

Because both Gardevoir and Venusaur had evolved only recently, Ethan, with Chloe acting as his temporary assistant, spent time helping them fully master their new abilities. At the same time, all four Pokémon engaged in high-intensity battles every day until they were completely exhausted, pushing their potential to its limits.

Next came Charmeleon and Wartortle.

Since neither had evolved yet, Ethan often took them to the beaches and forests surrounding Vermilion City while the others trained. There, they constantly challenged Trainers and battled every wild Pokémon they encountered to gain more experience.

As for Dratini, Ethan had absolutely no intention of using it during the Indigo League.

Instead, he continued focusing on improving its timid personality. Most of the time, it simply acted as the group's mascot, happily playing with Chloe's Yamper.

Every day was filled with training after training.

Whenever he had free time, he chatted through his messaging app with several girls he had gotten to know.

His days were both fulfilling and satisfying.

Several days later, a Dragonite carrying a messenger bag suddenly flew into Cerise Park and landed in front of Ethan.

After carefully observing him for a few moments, seemingly confirming his identity, Dragonite reached into its bag and handed him an envelope.

"Oh? This is for me?"

Ethan blinked in surprise.

Ever since arriving in this world, everyone he knew either had his phone number or was connected to him through messaging apps. It seemed impossible that someone would suddenly send such a rare Pokémon to personally deliver a letter without any prior notice.

Although he had no idea who the sender was, Dragonite didn't appear hostile, nor did it seem like some childish prank.

After thinking it over briefly, Ethan accepted the envelope.

When he opened it, he found a small handheld device inside.

The moment he picked it up, however, the device activated automatically, as though it had recognized his identity.

A woman's holographic image appeared above his palm.

She wore a hat, her body wrapped in a thick cloak resembling a nun's habit, with only part of her face visible.

She bowed politely before speaking.

"Please forgive my sudden invitation. My master will soon be hosting a gathering for the world's strongest Pokémon Trainers. As one of our specially selected guests, you are cordially invited to attend."

"The event will be held at the Pokémon Castle on New Island."

"May I ask whether you will accept this invitation?"

At Dragonite's gesture, Ethan reopened the envelope.

Only then did he discover that there was also a postcard hidden inside.

Taking it out, he found only two simple options printed on it:

YES and NO.

The woman continued,

"Please indicate your answer. I must add that this is a once-in-a-lifetime gathering of elite Trainers, and we sincerely hope you will attend."

This storyline...

Ethan instantly recognized it.

This was the plot of the movie Mewtwo Strikes Back.

After a brief moment of thought, he picked up a pen and placed a checkmark beside YES.

Handing the postcard back to Dragonite, he watched it fly away until it disappeared into the distance.

Then he quietly fell into deep thought.

Ever since Mewtwo had read his memories inside Team Rocket's base, an uneasy feeling had lingered in his heart.

Had Mewtwo already discovered that he was someone who had crossed over from another world?

And now Mewtwo had personally designated him as a special guest and invited him to this gathering.

What exactly was Mewtwo planning?

Still, Ethan wasn't someone who backed away from uncertainty.

Since the invitation had already arrived, no matter what awaited him, he had to go and see it for himself.
