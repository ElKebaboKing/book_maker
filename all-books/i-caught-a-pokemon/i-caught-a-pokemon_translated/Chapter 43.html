However, no matter how hard he shook his teammate, the other grunt showed no signs of waking up.

Alarmed, he was just about to shout when he suddenly sensed movement behind him.

He hurriedly turned around—only to see a pitch-black, round face pressed right in front of his own.

Before he could even scream, a thick slobbery tongue had already licked across his face.

Instantly, the world spun around him.

Just like his companion, he collapsed unconscious onto the floor.

"Keheheh…" Gastly cackled wickedly. "Have a nice nightmare."

After dealing with the two Team Rocket grunts, Gastly looked around at the surveillance equipment. A Shadow Ball formed beside it and slammed directly into the main control system.

In the next second, sparks crackled violently, and every monitor screen went dark.

"That should do it."

Looking at its handiwork, Gastly nodded in satisfaction.

Just as it was about to disappear, the communicator nearby suddenly crackled to life.

"Control room? I thought I heard something just now. Did something happen over there?"

Gastly's body shifted and transformed until it perfectly resembled the Team Rocket grunt from earlier.

Picking up the communicator, it answered using the exact same voice and tone.

"Report: everything is normal here. I accidentally bumped into a chair just now—that was the noise you heard."

"I see." The person on the other end did not suspect a thing. "Continue monitoring."

Inside the office on the fifteenth floor, Ethan peeked into the hallway once more.

When he noticed the red light on the surveillance camera had gone out, he immediately understood that Gastly had succeeded.

He gestured to Sabrina, and the two quickly headed toward the stairwell.

Naturally, under these circumstances, neither of them dared use the elevators.

If they were discovered inside one, there would be nowhere left to escape.

Unfortunately, things immediately went wrong.

Just as they were about to reach the stairs, a chaotic set of footsteps suddenly echoed from inside.

Ethan's heart tightened.

The footsteps sounded extremely close, as though they might encounter each other at any moment.

And since they had already walked quite far, there was no time to retreat back to the previous office.

Before Ethan could even react, Sabrina suddenly grabbed his sleeve and pulled him into a nearby room.

The instant they shut the door, they heard several people emerge from the stairwell and arrive on their floor.

The two exchanged glances.

Both immediately understood that they were still far from the top floor and absolutely could not expose themselves here.

Simultaneously, they turned to search for a hiding place.

The room appeared to be a recreational lounge for Silph Co. employees.

Several billiard tables and sofas were scattered around the room, while decorative plants sat in the corners. Along the far wall stood a row of tall lockers.

They hurried toward the lockers and began pulling at the doors.

However, several in a row were locked.

Just as Sabrina was about to use Psychic to force one open, Ethan finally managed to pull open a locker large enough to barely fit both of them.

Hearing the approaching footsteps growing closer and closer, Sabrina did not hesitate any further and immediately climbed inside.

Ethan quickly returned Kirlia to its Poké Ball and squeezed into the locker as well.

Even though the locker was fairly large, fitting two people inside was still extremely cramped.

Ethan and Sabrina were pressed tightly together, unavoidable physical contact between them. They were so close that they could feel each other's breathing against their faces.

Yet neither of them had time to think about such things.

The moment the recreation room door opened, several Team Rocket grunts slowly entered.

One of them said,

"Search carefully again. Don't leave anyone behind!"

"Boss," another grunt complained, "haven't we already searched the entire building once? Why do we have to do it again?"

"Idiot," the squad leader snapped. "This company is huge, and those researchers are slippery. There could still be people hiding somewhere. Our operation cannot fail, so keep your eyes open and search properly!"

The footsteps gradually approached.

One Team Rocket grunt stopped in front of the lockers and reached for a handle.

But when he pulled, the locker did not budge.

He shook it hard, confirmed it would not open, then moved on to the next locker.

Several more proved to be locked as well.

When he finally reached Ethan's locker, Ethan's expression darkened slightly.

He had already prepared himself to burst out fighting the moment the door opened.

However, when the grunt pulled on the locker door, it remained completely motionless—just like the others.

"Why are all these lockers locked?" the grunt muttered quietly.

Only then did Ethan realize what had happened.

Turning toward Sabrina, he understood that she had secretly used Psychic to hold the locker shut.

From the other side of the room, the squad leader asked,

"Still can't open them? Huh? They're all locked? Forget it then. Let's move on to the next room."

"Huh?" one grunt groaned. "Boss, can we rest for a bit? We've been searching floor after floor nonstop—I'm exhausted."

"Seriously, let us take a short break," several others quickly agreed.

After a brief pause, the squad leader finally nodded.

"Fine. We'll rest here for five minutes. After five minutes, we continue searching."

The grunts immediately cheered and collapsed onto the sofas.

Inside the dim locker, Ethan and Sabrina remained completely silent.

The cramped space had poor ventilation.

As time passed, the air gradually became stuffy while the temperature slowly rose.

Meanwhile, in the recreation room, the Team Rocket members relaxed during their rare break. With their minds temporarily wandering, they casually began chatting.

"Boss, what exactly is the purpose of this huge operation anyway?"

As low-ranking grunts, they knew very little about the mission details themselves.

Unable to suppress their curiosity, they hoped to learn something from their leader.

At first, the squad leader seemed unwilling to say anything.

But after repeated questioning from everyone, he finally relented.

"Alright, alright. I can't tell you too much, but I'll reveal a little."

He coughed lightly before continuing,

"Besides this company's assets, our real objective is actually a special Poké Ball they've been secretly developing—the Master Ball!"

"The Master Ball!?" one grunt asked blankly. "What's that?"

"It's the most valuable Poké Ball imaginable." Even the squad leader showed a trace of longing when mentioning it. "According to rumors, no matter what kind of Pokémon it is, as long as you use this ball, you can capture it with a 100% success rate—even without battling or weakening it first!"

"That amazing!?"

The Team Rocket grunts were all stunned.

"Of course." The squad leader laughed loudly. "Otherwise why would Team Rocket go through so much trouble to seize this place? If we really obtain the Master Ball, then capturing powerful and rare Pokémon will no longer be just a dream!"
