"Bulbasaur, use Vine Whip!"

Bulbasaur shook the dust off its body and extended two vines from the bulb on its back, lashing heavily toward Geodude.

After taking several direct hits, Geodude showed a pained expression. Seeing it at a disadvantage, Brock quickly shouted:

"At this rate, this won't end well! Geodude, close the distance and use Rollout!"

Geodude pulled in its fists and rapidly spun its body, transforming itself into a rolling stone sphere that charged toward Bulbasaur at high speed.

Trying to force close combat and avoid Bulbasaur's Razor Leaf attacks?

Ethan Vale immediately responded:

"Don't let it succeed! Use Vine Whip to restrain it!"

However, Geodude's Rollout speed was simply too fast. After dodging several Vine Whip strikes, it still managed to slam directly into Bulbasaur.

Unfortunately for Brock, Rollout was a Rock-type move, and after the collision, it caused only limited damage to Bulbasaur.

The moment Rollout stopped, Bulbasaur seized the opportunity. Its vines wrapped around Geodude, lifting it high into the air and restraining it completely.

"It's time to finish this! Bulbasaur, use Razor Leaf!"

At such close range, Geodude was struck head-on by the full force of Razor Leaf.

This time, it completely lost the ability to battle.

As it fell to the ground, it was like a stone sinking into the ocean—unable to make another ripple.

Brock recalled Geodude, his expression growing serious.

From the previous battle alone, it was obvious that Ethan Vale possessed both superior strategy and a type advantage.

"From what I've seen so far, you're definitely a very tactical Trainer," Brock admitted with a nod. "But if you want the Boulder Badge, you'll still have to defeat this one. Come out, Onix!"

Seeing Onix return to the battlefield, Ethan Vale recalled Bulbasaur and sent out Squirtle instead.

"I'm counting on you, Squirtle."

"Squirtle! Squirtle!"

Perhaps having learned from the earlier battle, Brock immediately commanded as soon as the match began:

"Onix, use Rock Tomb!"

Onix raised its massive body and opened its mouth wide. Instantly, huge rocks formed overhead before crashing down toward Squirtle.

"Squirtle, use Withdraw!"

At Ethan Vale's command, Squirtle quickly retracted its head and limbs into its shell.

A second later, the rocks smashed down—only to bounce harmlessly off Squirtle's sturdy shell.

"What a perfect chance!"

A sharp glint flashed in Brock's eyes as he shouted:

"Onix, now use Wrap!"

Taking advantage of Squirtle still being hidden inside its shell, Onix rushed forward and wrapped its massive tail around Squirtle, just as it had done to Pikachu earlier.

It squeezed tightly, producing frightening cracking noises as pressure pressed against the shell.

In the spectator stands, Misty's heart tightened nervously.

Ash also widened his eyes, wanting to see how Ethan Vale would respond to the same move that had defeated Pikachu.

Inside the Trainer's area, Ethan Vale calmly ordered:

"Squirtle, use Rapid Spin!"

Squirtle's shell began vibrating violently. The spinning intensified, grinding and colliding against Onix's body before accelerating into a spinning top-like rotation that forcefully blasted Onix away.

Rapid Spin—a Normal-type physical move—not only damages the opponent but can also free the user from restraints and effects like Leech Seed.

Taking advantage of Onix being staggered backward, Ethan Vale immediately continued:

"Squirtle, use Water Gun!"

Brock's brief moment of shock caused him to miss the perfect timing to issue another command.

By the time he reacted and attempted to order Onix to counterattack, it was already too late.

Facing the powerful stream of water, Onix instinctively tried to dodge, but its massive size made evasion impossible.

The Water Gun struck directly.

Water is super effective against Rock- and Ground-types!

Hearing Onix roar in pain, Brock quickly shouted:

"Onix, use Dig!"

If not seen with one's own eyes, it would've been hard to believe such a gigantic Pokémon could burrow underground so skillfully using the stone horn atop its head.

The battlefield fell silent.

Squirtle carefully scanned the area, cautiously watching for any movement.

Just as it turned around, the ground beneath its feet suddenly split open, and the enormous Onix burst upward.

But Ethan Vale had already anticipated this.

He knew Dig's purpose was to hide underground while waiting for the perfect ambush opportunity. Seeing Onix emerge exactly where he expected, Ethan Vale shouted loudly:

"Squirtle, use Bubble!"

A large stream of bubbles burst from Squirtle's mouth and rapidly expanded.

Not only did the bubbles obstruct Onix's vision, but they also continuously dealt Water-type damage.

Extending his right arm forward, Ethan Vale declared:

"Now's our chance! Let's finish this! Squirtle, use Water Gun!"

Squirtle looked up fearlessly at the gigantic Pokémon towering over it—more than ten times its size.

Opening its mouth wide, it unleashed a powerful Water Gun directly onto Onix.

With a thunderous crash as it collapsed to the ground, Onix was finally defeated!

Clap! Clap! Clap!

Applause echoed through the Gym.

After recalling the unconscious Onix, Brock walked over to Ethan Vale and sincerely said:

"You're truly incredible. I completely accept this defeat."

"Not at all." Ethan Vale quickly waved his hands modestly. "I only had the advantage because of type matchups. If I'd used other Pokémon, I definitely wouldn't have won so easily."

In truth, Brock's strength wasn't especially outstanding compared to many other Gym Leaders.

Because both of his parents had abandoned the family, Brock had been forced to care for his nine younger siblings while still underage. Between cooking, cleaning, and taking care of them every day, he simply didn't have enough time to properly train his Pokémon.

Though he was still much stronger than ordinary Trainers, he wasn't overwhelmingly difficult to defeat.

At that moment, Brock walked to the side, took a badge from a small box, and handed it to Ethan Vale.

"This is the Boulder Badge. As a reward for your victory, I present it to you."

The Boulder Badge resembled a cut diamond.

Though dark gray in color, it still reflected brilliant light beneath the Gym lamps.

The second badge of the Indigo League was finally his!

After placing the badge into his badge case, Ethan Vale praised both of his Pokémon before turning around to find Ash and Misty.

However, Ash and Pikachu had already disappeared, leaving only Misty waiting there alone.

"Huh? Where's Ash?"

Misty shrugged helplessly.

"Watching you win so easily must've really shaken him up. Without saying a word, he suddenly ran out of the Gym. No idea where he went."

Shaking his head, Ethan Vale replied:

"Forget it for now. Knowing his personality, he's probably secretly doing some special training somewhere again. Even if nobody goes after him, I'm sure he'll pull himself together soon enough and come back to challenge this Gym again."

(End of Chapter)
