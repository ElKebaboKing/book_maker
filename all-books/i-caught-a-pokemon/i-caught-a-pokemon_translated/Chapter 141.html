The next morning, Ethan Vale and his group arrived at the draw hall once again.

Compared to the second round, the number of contestants had been cut in half again. Only 64 trainers remained. Although the draw hall felt much quieter, the first two rounds had already eliminated the weaker competitors. From the third round onward, nearly everyone left was an elite trainer, and every opponent would be far more challenging.

The staff member who had helped them previously happened to be assisting someone else, so Ethan and the others didn't disturb her. Instead, they walked over to another empty counter.

After two rounds, they were already very familiar with the drawing process. After greeting the staff member and pressing the button, the results quickly appeared.

Ash was assigned to the Ice Field, where he would face a trainer named Shawn. Judging from Shawn's previous matches, he was a capable trainer, and defeating him certainly wouldn't be easy.

As for Ethan's draw...

The Rock Field appeared first, followed immediately by the portrait of a beautiful young woman.

"Huh... that's...?" Misty blinked in surprise before turning toward Ash with a teasing smile.

"Ethan, remember when I told you Ash once admitted there was a girl he thought was really cute during our journey? That's her! If I remember correctly, her name is Serena Yuto, right?"

Ash's face instantly turned bright red.

Seriously... why couldn't everyone just forget that embarrassing memory!?

Serena Yuto...

Ethan rubbed his chin thoughtfully.

So she's the top student from Pokémon Tech—the one everyone ranked alongside Gary as a championship favorite.

From her photo alone, she was undeniably adorable. No wonder Ash had developed a crush on her. Combined with her elegant demeanor, she clearly looked like a young lady from a prestigious family.

As Brock once joked, give her another eight years, and she'd undoubtedly grow into a graceful beauty.

Seeing Ash so embarrassed, Ethan laughed.

"Haha, so you've been hiding your true type all along, Ash. You like girls like her?"

Ash's face grew even redder as he frantically shook his head.

"I don't! That's not true! Stop making things up!!"

Just then, perhaps because she had heard someone mention her name, a cute girl in the distance turned around and looked in their direction.

After spotting Ash and the others, she paused briefly as if recalling something before walking over with several boys dressed in school uniforms.

"So it really is you."

She folded her arms and looked at them thoughtfully.

"If I remember correctly, your names are Ash, Brock, and Misty, right?"

The girl was naturally Serena Yuto.

None of them had expected her to appear just as they were talking about her, making the situation rather awkward.

"That's right, it's us," Misty replied with an awkward cough. Trying to change the subject, she asked,

"So you made it to the third round too? That's strange... where's Justin? Didn't he enter the tournament?"

The Justin Misty referred to was the boy they had met at Pokémon Tech, who had constantly been put through intense training by his classmates.

"Justin was eliminated in the very first round."

Serena Yuto shook her head in disappointment.

"Even though he qualified through the academy just like I did, he's simply too weak."

Then she looked back at them.

"I heard someone mention my name just now. What were you talking about?"

Before Misty could answer, Serena Yuto noticed her own portrait displayed on the large screen.

"Oh?"

Her gaze shifted to Ethan.

"So you're my next opponent?"

"That's right," Ethan nodded. "Please go easy on me, Miss Serena."

Serena Yuto proudly lifted her chin.

"A battlefield is no place for mercy. As the academy's star student, there's no way I'd hold back during a battle."

Then she smiled confidently.

"But don't worry. Since you're already an adult, I doubt you'll cry after you lose."

Ethan fell silent.

I was only being polite...

He hadn't expected Serena Yuto to be this confident. She spoke as though his defeat was already guaranteed.

Someone actually dared to insult Ethan right in front of them?

Although Misty had once lost to Serena Yuto herself, she couldn't tolerate hearing Ethan belittled. She immediately stepped forward, ready to argue.

Before she could say anything, Ethan gently stopped her.

As they left the draw hall, Misty was still visibly upset.

Seeing that, Ethan smiled and shook his head.

"Why argue with someone like that? The best answer is to defeat her in battle. Once she's beaten, that'll be far more convincing than words."

"Welcome, everyone, to the third-round match of the Indigo League's Rock Field!"

The announcer's energetic voice echoed across the stadium.

"Representing the Red Corner is the prestigious Pokémon Tech star student, Serena Yuto!"

"She's the academy's brightest star! Not only has she completely dominated students in her own year, but even many of her teachers have failed to defeat her. She's undoubtedly one of the tournament's strongest competitors!"

"Representing the Green Corner is Ethan Vale, who has advanced through the first two rounds with overwhelming victories in every match!"

"His performances have proven that he's equally outstanding. But today, only one trainer can move on! Who will claim victory and advance to the next round? Let's find out!"

As Ethan and Serena Yuto entered the battlefield, wave after wave of cheers erupted throughout the stadium.

However, Serena Yuto's popularity was clearly greater. Even with the biker gang enthusiastically cheering for Ethan, her supporters easily overwhelmed them in sheer volume.

From the sound of it, most of her fans were male.

From another perspective, if Ethan hadn't been competing himself, even he might have rooted for such an adorable girl.

The Rock Field consisted of hardened earth scattered with dozens of massive boulders and hundreds of smaller rocks, giving the battlefield an earthy yellow appearance.

When the referee signaled both trainers to send out their Pokémon, Ethan thought for a moment before releasing Blastoise.

Across the field, Serena Yuto surprised everyone by sending out Onix.

The announcer immediately commented,

"Oh? Serena Yuto has chosen the Rock- and Ground-type Onix to take advantage of the battlefield, while Ethan appears to have anticipated exactly that by sending out the Water-type Blastoise!"

"From a type matchup perspective, Serena Yuto seems to be at a considerable disadvantage!"

The announcer didn't need to point it out—Serena Yuto already knew the matchup wasn't in her favor.

Yet despite the disadvantage, she remained perfectly calm.

Back when Ash and the others had first visited Pokémon Tech, she had defeated Misty's Starmie with Golem, despite being at a type disadvantage herself.

When it came to overcoming unfavorable matchups, she was every bit as skilled as Ash.
