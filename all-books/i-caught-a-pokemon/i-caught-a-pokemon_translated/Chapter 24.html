Dragging his exhausted body along, Ethan Vale arrived at the Pokémon Center in Vermilion City.

After handing his Pokémon over to Nurse Joy, he finally returned to the Cerise Research Institute once their treatment was complete.

Along the way, chaos could be seen everywhere.

Because of the explosion at the power plant, just as Professor Cerise had mentioned on the phone earlier, a large-scale blackout had struck the area surrounding the research institute. Transportation, restaurants, and even work operations had all been severely affected.

Ethan even saw Officer Jenny standing in the middle of the road with a Growlithe, directing traffic.

Fortunately, important facilities such as the Cerise Research Institute and the Pokémon Center all had backup power supplies, so they were still functioning normally for the time being.

After meeting Professor Cerise, Ethan briefly explained everything that had happened. When the professor heard that Ethan had encountered Zapdos, he was so shocked that he stood up from his chair.

"You actually encountered the legendary Pokémon Zapdos!?"

Seeing Ethan nod, Professor Cerise quickly calmed himself and continued,

"So the frequent power outages at the institute were caused by it after all. But since there was an explosion, I doubt that Zapdos will appear there again. As for the power room, I'll contact the electric company immediately and have them send people to repair the facility and replace the generators."

"Actually…" Ethan scratched the back of his head awkwardly. "I also happened to catch Zapdos."

"What!?" Professor Cerise nearly choked on his own saliva. After coughing twice, he stared at Ethan in utter disbelief. "You caught it!?"

"Yes." Ethan took out the Poké Ball containing Zapdos and waved it in front of the professor to prove he was telling the truth. "But to avoid unnecessary trouble, I hope you can help me keep this a secret."

Taking a deep breath, Professor Cerise steadied himself and nodded. He naturally understood that this matter could not be publicized.

"Don't worry. Until you choose to reveal it yourself, I'll keep it completely confidential."

At that moment, Ethan cautiously asked,

"Professor, once a trainer carries more than six Pokémon, any newly caught Pokémon gets automatically transferred to the laboratory. Is there any way to change that setting?"

Hearing his question, Professor Cerise smiled.

"What's wrong? Aren't six Pokémon enough for you?"

"In normal circumstances, they are," Ethan said while gesturing toward the Poké Ball in his hand. "But in situations like this, I can't exactly leave Zapdos somewhere else. Since it occupies one of my active slots, it really limits my regular battle lineup."

Professor Cerise suddenly understood.

"So you don't want to use Zapdos as one of your standard battle Pokémon?"

"Exactly," Ethan explained. "For me, Pokémon like Kirlia and Ivysaur are my visible cards. But a Legendary Pokémon like Zapdos attracts too much attention, so it's more like a hidden trump card. Having both visible strength and concealed strength makes it easier to catch opponents off guard."

"I see now. That's actually a very cautious and mature way of thinking."

Professor Cerise stretched out his hand.

"Give me your Pokédex."

After Ethan handed it over, Professor Cerise connected it to his computer and made several adjustments before returning it.

"All done. I've modified your permissions and increased your Poké Ball carrying limit from six to twelve. This way, besides your normal six Poké Balls, you won't have to worry about newly caught rare Pokémon being transferred away automatically."

That afternoon, at Cerise Park—

Ethan opened the Poké Ball and released Zapdos.

Since the park was not yet open to the public, there was no need to worry about anyone seeing Zapdos and causing a commotion.

Zapdos flapped its wings and stared down arrogantly at Ethan.

Ethan smiled.

"I know you're unhappy about being defeated by weaker Pokémon, especially after being outnumbered. But that's fine. I believe that with time, you'll eventually accept me."

Zapdos lifted its head proudly, clearly full of disdain.

Ethan paid no attention to its attitude.

"It doesn't matter if you ignore me for now. But as my Pokémon, you should at least be prepared for this. Since you're so powerful, you can help train the others!"

After saying that, Ethan ignored whether Zapdos agreed or not and shouted to his Pokémon,

"Alright everyone, attack together!"

With a furious screech, brilliant electric light suddenly exploded throughout Cerise Park.

After resting for a day, Ethan packed his backpack, gathered his Pokémon, and left the Cerise Research Institute, preparing to head toward the next Gym city.

But just as he was about to leave, he suddenly heard someone calling his name from behind.

Turning around, he saw Ash, Misty, and Brock together!

So fate had truly arranged things exactly like in the anime, allowing the three of them to travel together as companions.

"You guys actually made it to Vermilion City this quickly?" Ethan smiled. "So next, you're planning to challenge the Vermilion Gym?"

Ash rubbed his nose proudly and unzipped his jacket, showing Ethan the three Gym Badges attached inside.

"The Boulder Badge, Cascade Badge, and Thunder Badge?" Ethan said in surprise. "You managed to collect three badges in just a few days? That's incredible speed."

"It's nothing special!" Ash laughed loudly. "My goal is to become a Pokémon Master! Getting three badges is easy!"

"Oh, give me a break." Misty frowned from the side. "That only happened because we went easy on you during our matches and practically handed you those badges out of sympathy."

Brock nodded seriously.

"Exactly. People should stay humble."

Having his bragging immediately exposed by his companions, Ash looked embarrassed, but quickly laughed it off without caring too much.

At that moment, Misty walked up to Ethan with her hands behind her back and greeted him.

However, perhaps without even realizing it herself, her tone carried less of her usual stubbornness and more shyness and excitement.

Ethan smiled brightly.

"It's only been two days, but Misty, you seem even prettier than before."

Meanwhile, Brock extended his right hand toward Ethan.

"We never properly introduced ourselves. My name is Brock. A few days ago, I stepped down as the Gym Leader and started traveling together with Ash and Misty. Right now, I'm working hard toward becoming a Pokémon Breeder."

"That's a wonderful goal," Ethan said as he shook Brock's hand. "Though personally, I think becoming a Pokémon Doctor would suit you really well too."

Then Ethan suddenly changed the subject and asked,

"But forgive me for asking… are you really only fifteen years old? Honestly, you look older than me ."
