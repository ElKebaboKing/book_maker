Ethan quickly adjusted his sitting position.

To break the awkward atmosphere, he cleared his throat and changed the subject.

"What a coincidence. You came to the hot spring too? I thought you'd go with Brock to look for Ash."

Misty stirred the water gently and shook her head.

"Why would I look for him? Even if nobody goes after him, he'll come back on his own sooner or later."

Then she turned toward Ethan and asked:

"Now that you've earned the Crimson Badge, are you leaving Cinnabar Island tomorrow?"

Ethan nodded.

"Yeah. I've already obtained my seventh badge. There's not much reason to stay here anymore. It's time to continue my journey."

Misty hesitated.

"Actually, I..."

As if sensing what she wanted to say, Ethan interrupted her.

"To be honest, there are times when I envy you guys."

"Huh?" Misty looked surprised. "Why?"

"Traveling as a group of three. Exploring together, camping together, sharing adventures together... just thinking about it sounds fun."

He scooped up another ladle of hot spring water and poured it over Wartortle.

"Unlike me. I'm always traveling alone. Sometimes I want someone to talk to, but there isn't anyone around."

Nearby, Haunter rolled its eyes.

No one to talk to?

Then what am I?

Hah. Men...

"How about traveling with us then?" Misty blurted out.

Then, gathering her courage, she added:

"Or... I could travel with you instead. Two people traveling together would definitely be more fun than being alone."

Ethan glanced at her and smiled apologetically.

"Let's not do that."

"If you leave with me, what will happen to Ash and Brock?"

"Those two have enough problems already—getting lost, chasing girls, being careless, oversleeping, and plenty more. Without you keeping them in line, do you really think they'll be okay?"

"That's..."

Thinking about all the years they had spent traveling together, Misty couldn't help worrying.

If she suddenly left, Ash and Brock might never even make it to the Indigo League.

Seeing her hesitation, Ethan continued:

"Besides, why rush?"

"If you really want to travel with me, there's no need to do it right now. Once your journey with Ash and Brock is over, we'll have plenty of time."

"That makes sense."

Relieved by Ethan's promise, Misty seemed to put her worries aside.

"Alright then. We'll travel together next time."

"Of course."

Ethan smiled.

"As long as you still want to come with me by then, we'll set off on a journey without any planning at all."

For a brief moment, his vision overlapped with a memory.

His hand unconsciously rose, intending to pat Misty's head.

Then he suddenly snapped back to reality.

Although she also called him "Brother Ethan," she wasn't that girl from Kalos.

Whew...

Habit really is a scary thing...

"A journey without planning?" Misty covered her mouth and laughed softly. "I'm really looking forward to that day."

Just then, a small, round, pink Pokémon hopped into the courtyard.

Before Ethan could get a good look at it, Misty cried out loudly:

"Ah! It's that Jigglypuff!"

That Jigglypuff!?

Ethan focused his gaze.

The pink Pokémon's face looked soft and bouncy like a balloon, with large innocent eyes that made it appear incredibly cute.

What drew the most attention, however, was the wireless microphone it carried.

After a brief moment of confusion, Ethan immediately understood why Misty looked so terrified.

If there was any wild Pokémon from the original Pokémon anime that had left a lasting impression on him, it was undoubtedly this pink devil—Jigglypuff.

Its extraordinary sleep-inducing singing ability could put anyone who heard its song to sleep, whether human or Pokémon.

But why was it here!?

Before he could think further, Jigglypuff noticed the large gathering of Pokémon and apparently decided it would make the perfect audience for a small concert.

Without hesitation, it raised its microphone.

Seeing this, Misty's face changed dramatically.

"No! Don't sing!"

Unfortunately, Jigglypuff completely ignored her warning.

"Puff-puff-puffri puff-puff-puffri..."

The moment the first note left its mouth, Ethan felt his eyelids growing heavy.

His thoughts drifted away, completely beyond his control.

Relying on sheer willpower, he managed to stay awake for a few more seconds.

Yet as he reached his limit, he saw his Pokémon sprawled across the hot spring, all fast asleep.

As for Misty, she toppled forward and landed against his chest.

At the same time, because her body had relaxed completely, the towel wrapped around her began slipping downward.

Before Ethan could react, his consciousness finally faded away.

He had no idea how much time had passed when he slowly awakened to the sound of rustling.

The moon hung high overhead, illuminating the night alongside countless stars.

Looking around, he saw that all of his Pokémon were still asleep.

Nearby, Misty had her back turned toward him and was hurriedly rewrapping her towel.

As for the culprit, Jigglypuff was nowhere to be seen.

When Ethan tried to move, he discovered that soaking in the hot spring for so long had left his body weak and sore.

His limbs felt swollen and sluggish.

Fortunately, he had awakened now.

If he had remained asleep much longer, he probably would have been completely soaked through and half-cooked.

Hearing movement behind her, Misty slowly turned around with a blush on her face.

Ethan expected the atmosphere to become awkward.

Instead, the moment they noticed the doodles drawn all over each other's faces, they both burst into knowing smiles.

Early the next morning, Ethan bid farewell to Ash, Blaine, and the others.

After repacking his gear, he set out toward the Kanto mainland.

Following a night's rest, both Ethan and his Pokémon had fully recovered.

The only lingering effect was a slight backache from maintaining the same position in the hot spring all night.

Leaving Cinnabar Town behind, he traveled another dozen kilometers before reaching Cinnabar Island's northern coast.

He summoned Gyarados and departed along another sea route.

Riding the waves and flying with the wind.

Ethan's original plan was to travel by water to Pallet Town and then continue to Viridian City to challenge the Viridian Gym.

However, while avoiding a large whirlpool, he drifted off course.

When he finally came ashore, he discovered that he had somehow arrived near Fuchsia City instead.

Still, it didn't matter much.

In terms of straight-line distance, Fuchsia City wasn't particularly far from Viridian City.

At worst, it would simply require a bit of extra travel.

Just as he entered a forest, set down his backpack, and prepared to rest before continuing onward, a sudden voice caught his attention.

(End of Chapter)
