"Bulbasaur, use Vine Whip!"

At Ethan Vale's command, two Vine Whips suddenly shot out from the bulb on Bulbasaur's back, lashing fiercely toward Eevee.

However, this Eevee clearly excelled in speed. Its four legs moved swiftly, allowing it to dodge the attacks with ease.

Once it safely avoided the strikes, it even glanced at Bulbasaur and let out a provocative cry!

The taunt immediately infuriated Bulbasaur.

"Bulba! Bulba!"

Bulbasaur's eyes sharpened, and it almost instinctively charged forward.

But just as it moved, Ethan Vale quickly stopped it.

"Bulbasaur, don't let its Growl affect you. Continue attacking with Vine Whip!"

This was the greatest difference between a wild Pokémon and one that belonged to a trainer.

Before being captured, Bulbasaur would have rushed in recklessly after such provocation. But now, things were clearly different.

Following Ethan Vale's instructions, Bulbasaur's two Vine Whips launched an endless barrage of attacks without pause.

Seeing this, Ethan Vale nodded in satisfaction before calmly saying:

"Good. Maintain the attack rhythm and wear down its stamina!"

At first, Eevee still appeared relaxed, seemingly intending to rely on its speed to avoid the attacks.

However, as its stamina steadily declined, Bulbasaur finally seized an opening and landed a heavy strike with Vine Whip!

Smack!

Eevee cried out in pain before suddenly charging forward with all its strength, slamming directly toward Bulbasaur.

"So you've switched to a direct approach?" Ethan Vale smiled faintly before commanding, "Bulbasaur, don't be afraid of it. Use Tackle!"

Following the order, Bulbasaur sprinted forward as well, crashing head-on toward Eevee.

In the next second, the two Pokémon collided violently. With a loud impact, both were thrown backward in opposite directions.

The collision clearly injured both of them heavily. They stumbled backward several steps before collapsing onto the ground.

Fortunately, neither had lost the ability to battle. After struggling for a moment, both Pokémon slowly stood up again.

However, thanks to its superior stamina, Bulbasaur recovered first and shook away the dizziness clouding its mind.

Seeing Eevee still standing there, Bulbasaur did not even wait for Ethan Vale's command. It immediately extended its Vine Whips once again and struck repeatedly.

Eevee had no strength left to dodge. After taking several direct hits from Vine Whip, it finally lost the ability to battle.

Seeing this, Ethan Vale decisively threw a Poké Ball and captured it with ease.

Excellent.

Looking at the Poké Ball resting quietly on the grass, Ethan Vale felt very satisfied with the battle.

This also meant Bulbasaur had truly begun transforming from a wild Pokémon into a trainer's Pokémon.

Of course, most importantly, he had successfully captured his third Pokémon!

…

Cerulean City Pokémon Center.

After two days of traveling, Ethan Vale had finally arrived.

After resting for a night and eating the free breakfast provided by the Pokémon Center cafeteria, Ethan Vale brought along his three Pokémon and headed straight toward the Cerulean Gym!

Through continuous training and traveling together, both Bulbasaur and Eevee had fully adapted to their new identities.

And through playing and training together, Ethan Vale's bond with them had grown much deeper.

At the very least, they had completely acknowledged Ethan Vale as their trainer and were now fully willing to follow his commands.

Just after leaving the Pokémon Center and stepping onto the street, Ethan Vale noticed something posted on a nearby bulletin board.

Upon closer inspection, he realized it was actually a wanted poster!

And judging from the blurry photos, the criminals being sought were clearly Jessie and James of Team Rocket!

Below the images was a line written in large bold letters:

"Pokémon thieves have recently been spotted nearby. Please remain vigilant!"

Seeing that sentence, Ethan Vale couldn't help but laugh softly.

No matter what Team Rocket had done in the anime or what backgrounds they came from, in the eyes of the police, they were ultimately nothing more than Pokémon thieves.

Still…

Ethan Vale actually found himself somewhat looking forward to meeting them sooner rather than later.

…

The appearance of the Cerulean Gym resembled a massive circus tent. Beneath its colorful dome hung an enormous sign featuring a Dewgong, making the building exceptionally eye-catching even from a distance!

According to Nurse Joy at the Pokémon Center, the current Gym Leader was not Misty yet, but rather her three older sisters—Daisy, Violet, and Lily—the famous Cerulean Beauties, also known as the Water Flowers of Cerulean City.

As for those three…

Their battle strength was honestly difficult to describe.

Following the street without even needing directions, Ethan Vale soon arrived at the official Gym.

However, a huge crowd had already gathered outside the entrance. After asking around briefly, he learned that today was the Cerulean Beauties' synchronized water ballet performance, and these people had all gathered here specifically to enjoy the show.

They claimed to love ballet, but clearly they were all here to admire long beautiful legs!

Men really do remain boys until the day they die.

But this also meant…

If Ethan Vale wanted to challenge the Gym, he would have to wait until after the performance ended.

Glancing around, Ethan Vale realized there was not much else worth exploring in Cerulean City. As a medium-sized coastal city, its scenery was fairly similar to many other places.

Since he had nothing better to do anyway, he simply bought a ticket and entered the Gym together with the crowd to watch the performance.

Ahem. Of course, he definitely wasn't there to look at legs. He was only passing the time.

Waiting. Standing in line.

After walking through a long corridor with the crowd, Ethan Vale finally entered the Gym.

The first thing he saw was an enormous swimming pool!

This was not only the stage for today's performance, but also the Gym's actual battle arena.

As if the timing had been perfectly arranged, Ethan Vale had barely found his seat and prepared to sit down when, in an instant, all the lights inside the hall suddenly dimmed.

At the same time, a female announcer's voice echoed clearly through the loudspeakers into every corner of the venue.

"Ladies and gentlemen, thank you all for waiting! The synchronized aquatic ballet performance by our city's famous Cerulean Beauties is about to begin!"

As the announcer finished speaking, all the spotlights turned on simultaneously and focused upon the diving platform beside the pool.

Standing there were three beautiful women in swimsuits, smiling and waving toward the audience!

The Cerulean Beauties had officially appeared!

The audience instantly erupted into thunderous applause and excited cheers. Clearly, the three sisters were genuinely popular.

Ethan Vale joined the crowd in clapping as well, completely immersing himself in the atmosphere of a spectator.

He had to admit—they really were beautiful.

Especially their long, flawless legs, which attracted nearly everyone's attention.

Far too eye-catching.

As the music began playing, the atmosphere instantly transformed, signaling the official start of the performance.

In the next moment, the three sisters simultaneously dove into the water and began their elegant aquatic dance.

The performance was mesmerizing, completely drawing the audience into its rhythm. As the music continuously shifted and flowed, Ethan Vale lost track of time entirely.

Only when the sisters climbed back out of the pool at the end and waved farewell to the audience once more did Ethan Vale suddenly realize that more than an hour had already passed!

It was honestly unbelievable…

Beautiful things truly did make people reluctant to leave.

Even after the show ended, the surrounding audience still seemed unsatisfied and continued praising the performance enthusiastically.

"Daisy is absolutely gorgeous and so gentle—she's the perfect woman!"

"Nonsense! Violet is obviously the prettiest! Ah, her blue hair and dark red eyes have completely captured my heart!"

"Hmph, none of you understand true beauty! Lily is clearly the cutest one among them!"

Listening to the arguments around him, Ethan Vale chuckled softly.

Now that the performance was over, it was finally his turn.

Leaving through a nearby staircase, he headed underground and soon found Daisy and her sisters inside the aquarium section.

At the moment, the three sisters were chatting and laughing together, clearly very pleased with today's performance.

"Did you see the audience's reactions? Today's performance was a huge success!"

"Exactly! Looks like all our recent special training really paid off!"

Seeing that the sisters were about to disappear deeper into the aquarium, Ethan Vale quickly walked over and called out to them.

"Excuse me, Miss Daisy, please wait a moment!"

Hearing his voice, the three sisters stopped and turned around, all showing surprised expressions when they saw Ethan Vale.

"Sir, the performance has already ended. If you'd like to watch again, you'll have to come back tomorrow."

Ethan Vale shook his head.

"Ah, no, actually I'm—"

Before he could finish speaking, Violet interrupted him.

"Could you be a reporter? If so, I'm sorry, but interviews require scheduling through our manager first."

"A reporter?" Ethan Vale shook his head again. "No, I'm not a reporter. I just came here to—"

"Not a reporter?" Lily crossed her arms and smiled. "Then you must want our autographs?"

How troublesome…

These three didn't even give him a chance to speak!

Seeing Ethan Vale's speechless expression, only Daisy seemed to realize something. Raising an eyebrow, she finally asked:

"Sir… could it be that you're here to challenge the Gym?"

"Of course." Ethan Vale nodded matter-of-factly before replying, "Isn't that supposed to be your actual profession?"
