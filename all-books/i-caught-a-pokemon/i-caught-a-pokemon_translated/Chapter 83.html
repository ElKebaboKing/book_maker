Articuno and Jolteon both nodded. Only Kirlia and Haunter looked at Ethan with concern.

"You need to be careful too," Haunter warned. "Don't get caught in the middle of the battle!"

"Don't worry, I'll be careful." Ethan patted Kirlia on the shoulder before releasing Gyarados and Wartortle as well. "I'll have these two act as guards and reserve forces, ready to support at any moment. With them protecting me, there won't be any danger. So from here on, fight with everything you've got!"

"Wartortle! Wartortle!"

Wartortle nodded seriously at Haunter, its expression saying, Leave this place to me!

"Good. Then let's go!"

Haunter grinned excitedly and charged toward Articuno together with the others.

High above, Articuno gazed coldly at the group of ill-intentioned challengers. It flapped its wings, unleashing a fierce gust of wind, seemingly trying to intimidate these not-yet-fully-evolved Pokémon into retreating.

"What an overwhelming presence…" Ethan shouted to them, "Don't back down! Kirlia, use Confusion! Jolteon, use Thunder Shock! Charmeleon, Flamethrower!"

Three different attacks shot toward Articuno simultaneously. Facing such coordinated pressure, Articuno didn't dare underestimate them. It immediately summoned a violent gale, completely dispersing the incoming attacks.

But at that very moment, a Shadow Ball suddenly struck its back without warning!

Articuno cried out in pain and quickly turned around, only to discover that Haunter had silently appeared behind it and launched a sneak attack.

Enraged, Articuno fired countless Ice Shards toward Haunter, yet Haunter burst into eerie laughter as its body vanished directly into the shadows!

The ice spikes pierced nothing but empty air before crashing into the wall behind.

"Hehehehehe…"

Haunter's sinister laughter echoed around Articuno. Before it could determine the direction of the sound, another Shadow Ball slammed into its body!

This completely infuriated Articuno. It spread its wings and released layers of fine snow in every direction at once.

It was trying to force Haunter out with indiscriminate attacks!

However, beneath it, a stream of scorching fire suddenly surged upward. Unable to dodge in time, Articuno was scorched across most of its body!

"Kyaaaah!"

With so many enemies attacking from every direction, Articuno simply couldn't defend against them all. The slightest lapse in attention meant another attack would strike from somewhere unseen.

After suffering continuous damage, its already-limited stamina was nearly depleted. Its eyes sharpened as it spread its wings and flew straight toward the stone ceiling overhead.

"Huh? Is it trying to run away?" Haunter asked in surprise.

"No!" Ethan stared intently at Articuno's movements and immediately realized what it was doing. "It's preparing another ultimate move! Everyone, be ready!"

Judging from the buildup alone, this attack was definitely not Blizzard like before.

"Don't let it finish! Attack now!"

The three Pokémon on the ground hurriedly launched attacks, but the distance was simply too great. Their moves couldn't reach it!

Just as Haunter was about to rush upward, Articuno's attack was completed.

A mist-like mass of azure freezing energy condensed together before descending toward the ground.

Ethan's expression changed dramatically.

"Oh no—it's Sheer Cold! Everyone, dodge!!"

Just like Fissure, Sheer Cold was a one-hit knockout move! As long as the target wasn't an Ice-type and lacked immunity to Ice attacks, the damage was enough to instantly knock them out!

In that split second, the freezing mist had already arrived.

Faced with such terrifying power, Ethan's Pokémon each reacted in their own way. Haunter vanished into the shadows completely. Kirlia used Teleport to escape to safety. Even Jolteon managed to flee the attack zone with its incredible speed.

Only Charmeleon failed to escape in time and was struck directly by Sheer Cold.

The icy mist touched Charmeleon's body and instantly transformed into massive frozen spikes, completely encasing it in ice!

Charmeleon didn't even have time to scream before the extreme cold froze it unconscious. When the ice shattered apart, its body fell stiffly onto the ground.

Ethan immediately recalled Charmeleon with a Poké Ball and shouted:

"Haunter! We can't let it use that move a second time! Think of something!"

"Got it!"

Haunter finally became serious.

Its eyes began glowing as rows upon rows of icicles throughout the cavern were lifted by invisible force and hurled toward Articuno!

At the same time, the lake water outside the island spiraled upward into towering water tornadoes that surged toward Articuno as well.

The attacks came endlessly. Although the water tornadoes moved too slowly to hit their target, at least a few icicles managed to strike Articuno.

Unfortunately, the damage was far from impressive.

"Hey!" Ethan shouted. "Don't use Ice-type attacks! They barely affect it!"

"I know!" Haunter replied helplessly. "But there's nothing else here except ice and water! I can influence and manipulate what already exists, but I can't create something out of nothing!"

"Then use that move!" Ethan yelled. "Hypnosis!"

Haunter nodded. Waves of psychic energy spread outward from its body and passed through Articuno.

Articuno's eyelids gradually grew heavier, its thoughts becoming sluggish. Within only a few breaths, it lost focus entirely and plummeted from the sky!

And just before Articuno hit the ground, Haunter unleashed its final move.

Dream Eater activated!

Boom!

The sleeping Articuno suffered damage from Dream Eater and then crashed heavily onto the icy ground. Under the double impact, the damage completely overwhelmed it, and it finally lost consciousness.

Just like Confusion, Hypnosis truly was an incredibly useful move.

Or rather… Ethan's Haunter was simply far too powerful!

After confirming it was safe, Ethan slowly stepped forward until he stood before Articuno.

Its condition was pitiful. The beautiful feathers it once possessed were now messy and battered.

Without hesitation, Ethan took an Ultra Ball from his backpack and threw it toward Articuno.

A flash of red light enveloped the legendary bird as it was drawn inside the Ultra Ball.

The ball shook gently once…

Then, with a soft click, it became completely still.

Seeing this, Ethan finally let out the breath he had been holding.

Excellent.

His second Legendary Pokémon… was finally his.
