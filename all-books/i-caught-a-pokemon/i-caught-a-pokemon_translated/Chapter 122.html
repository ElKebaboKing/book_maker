To be honest, Mewtwo's change in attitude happened so quickly that even Ethan was surprised.

But it also proved something else.

Although Mewtwo was stubborn and obsessive, it was never an inherently evil Pokémon. Hatred had merely clouded its judgment. Once it witnessed the goodness that still existed in the world, its convictions naturally began to waver.

After thinking for a moment, Ethan asked in return,

"What exactly do you want to know?"

After a brief silence, Mewtwo replied,

"I want to know who you really are."

"Why do you know my name?"

"And why do you possess so much knowledge about the future?"

Are you the Pokémon version of 'Twenty Questions'?

Ethan couldn't help complaining inwardly.

Still, the fact that Mewtwo was asking these questions proved that, during their previous encounter, it hadn't been able to read all of Ethan's memories.

So...

How should he bluff—no, explain everything?

His mind raced.

Instead of answering immediately, Ethan glanced toward Ash, Misty, Brock, and the others.

At that moment, including all of the Pokémon, they looked absent-minded.

Under the influence of the glowing blue particles, their memories of everything that had happened on the island were slowly disappearing.

The particles continued drifting beyond the island with the wind, spreading farther and farther.

After a moment's thought, Ethan smiled.

"I'll answer your questions."

"But before that... I think we should send Ash and the others safely back to the mainland first."

...

Whoosh!

Violent winds swept across Vermilion Harbor.

Towering waves crashed against the lighthouse before exploding onto the docks.

Inside the passenger terminal, the building was still packed with Trainers and their Pokémon.

The salty smell of the sea mixed with rainwater and the various scents of Pokémon filled the enclosed space with a strange, indescribable odor.

When Ash slowly opened his eyes, he was surprised to find himself leaning against a chair.

Rubbing his eyes, he looked around.

Ethan, Misty, and Brock were all sitting nearby.

"Huh?"

"When did I fall asleep?"

His thoughts were a complete mess.

He couldn't remember anything that had happened before.

Everything in front of him felt strangely familiar.

It was as if yesterday had repeated itself...

As though time had looped and everything had happened all over again.

Just then, Misty and Brock yawned and slowly woke up as well.

"This is weird."

"Why are we here?"

Faced with their confusion, Ethan casually answered,

"Did you all sleep so deeply that you forgot?"

"We came inside to take shelter from the storm."

Hearing that explanation, Ash and the others all felt something was off.

Yet none of them could explain exactly what.

Misty frowned.

"But... this is Vermilion City, isn't it?"

"What brought us here in the first place?"

She desperately tried to remember.

It felt as though a piece of her memory had been completely cut away.

Nothing came back.

"And Ethan..."

"Why are you here too?"

Ethan smiled.

"Didn't you tell me you'd come visit me in Vermilion after collecting all eight Gym Badges?"

"So I came to the harbor to meet you."

"What?"

"You forgot everything after taking a nap in those chairs?"

Was that really what happened?

Just as Misty was about to ask another question, a commotion suddenly broke out among the crowd ahead.

Many Trainers and travelers had gathered around Officer Jenny and Harbor Administrator Poija.

Looking more closely, Ethan also spotted Claire, Hayden, and Logan—the three Trainers who had fought alongside them on New Island.

Facing dozens of anxious faces, Officer Jenny raised her voice.

"I'm sorry, everyone."

"Due to the storm, all ferry services have been temporarily suspended."

"Please wait patiently. As soon as the weather improves, the harbor will reopen."

"Officer Jenny is right."

Another woman's voice came from behind the crowd.

As everyone stepped aside, the newcomer turned out to be Nurse Joy from the Pokémon Center.

"But please don't worry," Nurse Joy said with her usual professional smile.

"No matter how long the storm lasts, the Pokémon Center will continue providing free meals and accommodation for everyone."

Even though Nurse Joy had supposedly been missing for quite some time...

Neither Officer Jenny nor anyone else seemed surprised by her sudden return.

Ethan looked toward the wall.

The missing-person poster that had once hung there...

Had completely vanished.

Thanks to the combined powers of Mewtwo and Mew, not only had everyone's memories of New Island been erased...

Even every trace connected to those events had been rewritten.

It was as though nothing had ever happened.

Mew... Mewtwo...

Their names truly suited them.

They possessed powers as mysterious and dreamlike as legends claimed.

Aside from them...

Only Ethan and his Pokémon still remembered everything that had happened on New Island.

Just then, Ash suddenly noticed something.

"Hey!"

"Look! What's that Pokémon?"

Everyone hurried over to the terminal's giant windows and looked toward the sky where Ash was pointing.

Far above the clouds, a strange Pokémon shot across the heavens at incredible speed before disappearing into the thick cloud cover.

The others couldn't make out what it was.

But Ethan recognized it instantly.

It was Mewtwo...

The very Pokémon that had just returned them safely to the mainland.

Before leaving, Ethan had spoken with Mewtwo for quite some time.

Although he still kept some secrets, he answered many of Mewtwo's questions.

As for why he possessed knowledge of future events...

Ethan honestly admitted that he originally came from another world.

Interestingly...

Mewtwo accepted the truth surprisingly quickly.

Ghost, on the other hand, was completely dumbfounded.

Its jaw dropped so wide that it looked as though an entire basketball could fit inside its mouth.

The sight was so ridiculous that Ethan almost laughed.

Perhaps because of Ethan's honesty...

Mewtwo finally acknowledged him.

Not only did all of its hostility disappear...

It also felt a strange sense of kinship toward Ethan, another being who originally didn't belong to this world.

After their long conversation, Mewtwo had even developed genuine goodwill toward him.

Before sending Ethan back, it had solemnly told him,

"If you ever face difficulties in the future..."

"You may ask me for help."

Earning Mewtwo's friendship was certainly something to celebrate.

However...

Compared to Mewtwo, Mew fascinated Ethan even more.

Not only was Mew unbelievably adorable...

It was also incredibly powerful.

Unfortunately, after Mew and Mewtwo finished casting their memory-erasing ability together, Ethan had been just about to take out his Master Ball and try catching Mew...

When Mew suddenly disappeared without a trace.

It simply flew away.

Left with no other choice, Ethan had to give up.

Although missing the opportunity to catch Mew was disappointing...

Every journey had its gains and losses.

And Ethan firmly believed...

That somewhere along his future travels...

He and Mew would meet again.

( End of Chapter )
