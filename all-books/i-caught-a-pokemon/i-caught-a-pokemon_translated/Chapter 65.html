When Ethan walked into the lounge, several people were already seated inside.

The first person sitting at the front was a stern-looking handsome man. He had flame-like spiky hair, wore a black cape, and possessed an exceptionally sharp gaze.

Beside him, a Dragonite was lazily curled up on the floor, dozing.

Next, sitting in the corner, was an elderly woman wearing a purple robe and leaning on a cane. Her hair was nearly gray-yellow, and she looked extremely difficult to approach. From her aura alone, anyone unfamiliar with her would absolutely mistake her for some wicked old witch!

Then there was an older man with gray-white hair and enormous dark circles under his eyes. Judging by his appearance, he looked even more exhausted than Handsome Guy.

The man wore an open-collared police uniform on his upper body, but oddly enough, he had slippers on his feet. Despite his age, he gave off an extremely carefree and laid-back feeling.

And finally, the last person was none other than Celadon Gym Leader Erika!

Including Ethan, Sabrina, and Handsome Guy, the members of the special operations team were now fully assembled!

For a moment, everyone's attention focused entirely on Ethan.

"Why are you still wearing a mask?" the older man curled his lips. "What are you, some undercover operative carrying out a secret mission?"

Off to the side, Erika also looked at Ethan suspiciously. Somehow, his figure seemed strangely familiar to her.

However, since she had only met Ethan a few times, she did not identify him based solely on his silhouette.

That was, until Sabrina walked past her.

A faint fragrance drifted past Erika's nose. She lightly sniffed the scent and immediately recognized it as the unique perfume from her own shop!

But Sabrina definitely had not been wearing perfume when she left earlier.

And with Sabrina's status, if she had personally visited the shop to buy perfume, Erika's staff would certainly have recognized her!

Being someone with a delicate and observant mind, Erika pieced things together almost instantly and quickly figured out Ethan's identity as well.

So it's him!

But immediately afterward, another question troubled her.

What exactly was the relationship between these two? Why would he give Sabrina perfume? And even more unbelievable—Sabrina had actually accepted it!!

At this moment, Handsome Guy walked to the tactical board and faced the group.

"Our final member has arrived. Let me first introduce every—"

As he spoke, he glanced once more at Ethan's Gengar mask. His expression stiffened, and he sighed helplessly.

"Forget it. You should just introduce yourself."

Ethan cleared his throat.

"Hello everyone. My na— ahem, codename is Player. I specialize in Ghost-type Pokémon. Please take care of me."

Codename!? Player!?

What kind of nonsense was that!?

Handsome Guy's face darkened with exasperation, though he did not expose Ethan and instead introduced the others before him.

"The two on the left are members of the Kanto Elite Four: Ghost-type Elite Four Agatha, and Dragon-type Elite Four Lance, who is also a Pokémon investigator."

"This old bag of bones still being called 'Miss'… even I feel a little embarrassed hearing that."

Agatha replied calmly before nodding at Ethan.

"Young man, wearing a Gengar mask and claiming to specialize in Ghost-types—you're quite unusual. Young people these days are becoming less and less fond of Pokémon that carry such eerie and deathly energy."

Ethan calmly accepted Agatha's praise.

Although he only possessed one Ghost-type Pokémon, compared to his other Pokémon, Haunter was by far his strongest partner. So saying he specialized in Ghost-types was not entirely inaccurate.

Lance also stepped forward and shook Ethan's hand.

"Hello, I'm Lance. Since you were able to come here, your strength must have already earned Officer Handsome Guy's recognition. On behalf of the entire special operations team, welcome aboard."

See? That's how someone with real experience speaks!

Handsome Guy then looked toward the two women on the right.

"There's no need to introduce Miss Sabrina since you already know her, and I believe you and Miss Erika have also met before, so I'll skip the introductions there as well."

At last, only the exhausted-looking older man remained.

"This is Mr. Nanu, my former superior. He specializes in Dark-type Pokémon and is also one of the island kahunas of the Alola region!"

"Oh, those are just meaningless little titles, nothing worth bragging about." Nanu casually waved at Ethan before sighing lazily. "Honestly, let's just finish this mission quickly so I can retire early and go back to Alola to enjoy my old age."

"Mr. Nanu," Handsome Guy laughed awkwardly, "please don't say that. You're still quite young, so why are you always thinking about retirement?"

Just as Nanu was about to respond, the lounge door suddenly opened, and a bald, muscular man dressed in a formal suit walked in.

The moment he appeared, Nanu immediately straightened up and became serious.

"Is everyone here?" the bald man asked.

"Sir!" Handsome Guy saluted respectfully. "The seven members of the special operations team are all present!"

The man nodded and addressed the group.

"I am the commander-in-chief of this operation. My codename is Zero. Thank you all for taking time out of your busy schedules to come here and help eliminate Rocket Team, this troublesome criminal organization threatening not only Kanto, but the entire world."

Nanu began clapping first, followed by scattered applause throughout the lounge.

Once the applause died down, Zero flipped the tactical board and continued.

"This operation will be divided into two forces. The main force, composed of International Police special tactical units, will launch the first assault on the base's main entrance with support from armored vehicles and helicopters, drawing Rocket Team's attention."

"As for your special operations squad, once the battle begins, you will secretly infiltrate the base through the underground entrance beneath Celadon Game Corner. Working together from the inside and outside, you will eliminate and arrest everyone inside in one decisive strike."

Lance asked, "What about Giovanni?"

"Don't worry." Zero glanced at him. "The other two Elite Four members, Bruno and Lorelei, have already arrived in Viridian City. Once the assault begins, they will launch a simultaneous attack on the Viridian Gym."

The arrangement seemed flawless, and no one voiced any objections.

"How unbelievable," Agatha sighed with emotion. "Even now, I still can't believe that the man known as Giovanni of the Earth—that calm and composed man—is actually the true leader behind Rocket Team!"

"Whether we like it or not, the facts remain the facts." Zero sneered coldly. "Behind every successful man often lies an enormous secret. No matter how perfect someone appears, sooner or later, the cracks will show. No one is an exception."

(End of Chapter)
