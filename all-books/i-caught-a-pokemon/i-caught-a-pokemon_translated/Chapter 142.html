As soon as the referee declared the match began, Ethan Vale and Serena Yuto issued their commands almost simultaneously.

"Blastoise, use Water Gun!"

"Onix, burrow underground!"

The twin cannons on Blastoise's back fired powerful streams of water toward Onix.

However, by the time the Water Gun reached its target, Onix had already disappeared beneath the ground.

The attack crashed into a massive boulder instead, splashing water in every direction and leaving a large puddle behind, without even grazing Onix's tail.

A faint rumble echoed beneath the battlefield.

Even from the trainer's platform, Ethan could feel the subtle vibrations through the ground, sensing Onix rapidly tunneling beneath the rocks.

Unfortunately, the constant roar of the crowd made it impossible for him to pinpoint Onix's exact location.

"Blastoise, get on top of that largest boulder!"

Blastoise quickly located the tallest rock in the arena. Despite its heavy body, it bounded across the field with several powerful jumps before landing firmly on top.

From there, it had a clear view of the battlefield. Even if Onix launched an attack from underground, the extra height would give Blastoise enough time to react.

Seeing this, Serena Yuto gently shook her head.

She had originally intended to launch a surprise attack, but that plan was no longer possible.

"Not a bad response," she admitted before smiling confidently.

"But by doing that, you've trapped yourself instead. Onix, use Stone Edge!"

Sharp stone spikes suddenly burst from beneath the earth.

They tore through the soil, shattered rocks, and shot toward Blastoise at incredible speed.

Even on flat ground, Stone Edge was difficult to dodge. Standing alone atop a giant boulder made escaping even harder.

Fortunately, Ethan had already anticipated this. He had practiced against this very situation many times before.

"Blastoise, use Withdraw!"

Blastoise instantly pulled its head and limbs into its shell.

Just as Stone Edge reached it, the massive boulder beneath its feet exploded apart.

Boom!

Blastoise was launched high into the air.

Protected by its incredibly sturdy shell, it suffered only minor impact damage.

Serena Yuto frowned slightly.

If it had been almost any other Pokémon, that attack would have dealt devastating damage.

Unlike her, Ethan remained completely at ease.

Although he had used Withdraw countless times before, it remained one of the most effective ways to counter powerful physical attacks like Stone Edge.

And unlike before, Onix had unknowingly exposed its position during the attack.

Time for our counterattack!

As Blastoise reached the peak of its flight, Ethan pointed toward the ground.

"Right there! Rapid Spin!"

Blastoise spun rapidly through the air like a massive spinning wheel before crashing straight down onto the section of ground where Onix was hiding.

Although Onix immediately tried to move away, tunneling underground was still slower than Blastoise's descending Rapid Spin.

BOOM!!

The tremendous impact shook the entire battlefield.

Underground, Onix became completely disoriented by the violent shockwave.

Unable to endure it any longer, it burst out from beneath the earth.

"Now's our chance!" Ethan shouted immediately.

"Blastoise, use Water Gun!"

The twin cannons on Blastoise's back rapidly gathered energy.

By the time Onix recovered from its dizziness, the powerful streams of water had already blasted directly into its body.

"ROOOAR!!"

Against the Rock- and Ground-type Onix, the Water Gun dealt quadruple damage.

It was devastatingly effective.

Seeing Onix's stamina nearly depleted, Serena Yuto quickly shouted,

"Onix, get out of the way!"

But after the first Water Gun, the battlefield had already been soaked.

The once-solid ground of dirt and broken stones had turned into thick mud.

As Onix's massive body tried to move, it immediately began sinking, the softened earth trapping it like a swamp.

"W-What!?"

Not only were the spectators stunned—even Serena Yuto herself had never expected such a situation.

Watching her lose her composure, Ethan smiled.

"I suppose this is the kind of situation they can't teach you in school."

Serena Yuto clenched her fists but said nothing.

In truth, every battle at Pokémon Tech took place on carefully maintained battlefields.

Students followed strategies exactly as their instructors taught them.

A situation like this had never happened before.

Seeing Onix still struggling helplessly, Ethan calmly gave the finishing command.

"That's enough. Blastoise... finish it!"

Blastoise aimed its cannons once more.

Another powerful Water Gun blasted straight into Onix, knocking the giant Pokémon to the ground.

This time, it completely lost the ability to battle.

"Oh, what a shame!" the announcer exclaimed, his voice echoing throughout the stadium.

"Although Onix possesses incredible physical durability, its greatest strength was completely meaningless against a super-effective special attack!"

Cheers erupted throughout the arena.

While Serena Yuto's supporters lowered their heads in disappointment, Ethan's fans celebrated enthusiastically.

The announcer quickly tried to comfort the losing side.

"But don't be discouraged! Without the severe type disadvantage, this battle surely would have been much closer!"

"Now then, let's see what Pokémon Serena Yuto will send out next to turn this match around!"

On the battlefield, Serena Yuto let out a quiet sigh.

She had never imagined her Onix would be defeated so easily.

Back at the academy, she had always excelled at using Ground-type Pokémon.

Even when facing unfavorable type matchups, she routinely defeated her classmates with ease.

But the harsh reality of the Indigo League had finally taught this flower raised in a greenhouse a difficult lesson.

Only now did she truly realize that the handsome trainer standing across from her was nothing like her classmates, her teachers, or the opponents she had defeated in the previous two rounds.

After a brief moment of frustration, the confidence she had built over many years quickly returned.

"I underestimated you in that first battle," she admitted honestly.

"I actually believed I could defeat a trainer as strong as you despite the type disadvantage."

She recalled Onix and calmly took out her second Poké Ball.

"But this next battle will be different."

"With this partner by my side... I won't lose!"

( End of Chapter )
