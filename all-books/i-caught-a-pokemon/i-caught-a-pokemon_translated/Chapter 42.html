Sabrina calmly asked, "So, where exactly are we entering from?"

Ethan looked up at the surveillance cameras surrounding the Silph Co. building. After glancing around, he pointed toward another nearby commercial office tower.

"That building is only about half the height of Silph Co. If we enter Silph Co. from its rooftop, we can avoid the Team Rocket members occupying the lower floors."

Sabrina did not object.

Following Ethan, she crossed the street and blended into a crowd of office workers as they entered the neighboring building.

The two took the elevator to the top floor. At the stairway leading upward, Sabrina used her psychic powers to twist apart the door lock, allowing them to reach the rooftop without obstruction.

Psychic powers really were incredibly powerful and convenient.

A chilly wind blew across Ethan as he stood by the edge railing and looked ahead.

The endless traffic below now appeared tiny from this height, while the Silph Co. building across from them still seemed quite far away.

"What's wrong?" Sabrina asked softly. "Are you afraid?"

"Not exactly afraid," Ethan admitted honestly without pretending otherwise. "But my legs are definitely shaking a little…"

Still, he looked determined.

"At a time like this, there's no way I can back down."

As he spoke, his gaze searched the opposite building until he quickly found a shattered window left behind during Team Rocket's invasion.

It was a large floor-to-ceiling window designed to provide excellent lighting, though it had long since been broken. Behind it was an office room that now sat completely empty after the chaos.

"We'll enter from there."

Seeing Sabrina nod lightly, Ethan signaled to Kirlia.

Kirlia immediately stepped beside him and wrapped its arms around his waist.

Teleport activated.

In the next instant, Ethan vanished.

When he and Kirlia reappeared, they were already standing inside that office room.

Turning back toward Sabrina, Ethan watched as her body floated effortlessly through the air, flying directly over from the rooftop across the way.

Alright then.

Psychic powers really are unfairly useful.

Once reunited, Ethan lowered his voice and called softly,

"Gastly."

Gastly floated out from Ethan's shadow.

"What is it?"

But before Ethan could speak, Sabrina suddenly covered her mouth in rare surprise.

"It can actually talk?"

"What's so strange about that?" Gastly glanced sideways at Sabrina. "It's just human language. Little girl, you really haven't seen much of the world yet!"

"Alright, stop showing off," Ethan interrupted. "Go scout the nearby area first. Make sure to locate Team Rocket's positions clearly."

Watching Gastly phase through the wall and disappear, Sabrina turned toward Ethan again as though seeing him in a completely new light.

"A Pokémon trainer, connected to the International Police, and even owning a talking Gastly… We're around the same age, yet your experiences seem far more extraordinary than mine."

"That still doesn't compare to you." Despite the dangerous environment, Ethan remained surprisingly relaxed. "You have psychic powers and became a Gym Leader. That's something truly enviable."

Perhaps because she had already heard countless compliments in her life, Sabrina paid little attention to his praise.

Instead, she asked,

"Do you know what Team Rocket's actual goal is?"

Ethan shrugged.

"No idea."

The events involving Silph Co. in the games had happened too long ago for him to remember clearly.

Still, he was certain the company president was somehow involved.

At that moment, Ethan checked his phone.

Just like aboard the S.S. Anne, the signal had been completely blocked.

Before long, Gastly returned.

"Most of Team Rocket's members are concentrated on the upper and lower floors. The middle floors actually have very few guards."

"What about the employees inside the building?"

Gastly replied, "They've all been gathered into several rooms and are being guarded separately. But from what I can tell, Team Rocket doesn't seem interested in hurting them, so they're safe for now."

Sabrina looked toward Ethan.

"What do you plan to do next?"

"With this many people, there's no way the two of us can rescue everyone," Ethan said after thinking briefly. "I think our best option is to locate the president's office and get him out safely. As long as we ensure his escape, whatever Team Rocket is planning will fail."

Sabrina nodded.

"That certainly sounds dangerous. But don't worry—since I accepted this request, I'll help you all the way."

"That's actually very reassuring to hear."

Taking a deep breath, Ethan peeked into the hallway before quickly pulling back again.

"There's a security camera right outside. If we walk out like this, we'll immediately be spotted. Before moving, we need to deal with the surveillance system first."

Turning toward Gastly, Ethan instructed,

"We'll split up now. Head to the monitoring room and see if you can disable the entire surveillance system. After that, meet us on the top floor."

"Keheheh." Gastly grinned widely. "Leave it to me. Causing destruction is both my favorite thing and my specialty!"

As soon as it finished speaking, its body dissolved into the room's shadows and vanished.

Silph Co. – First Floor Surveillance Room

At that moment, two Team Rocket grunts were intently staring at the surveillance monitors, afraid of missing even the slightest detail.

Before today's operation began, both their boss and their superior officers had strictly ordered everyone to remain on full alert without the slightest negligence.

And as ordinary members with great ambitions within Team Rocket, they naturally executed those orders perfectly.

"Although Team Rocket was an evil organization constantly involved in criminal acts, its internal benefits and treatment of members were actually excellent."

Not only was it massive in scale with many large multinational companies under its control, but it also provided strong employee benefits, paid salaries on time, supplied equipment and Pokémon resources, and regularly held social gatherings, tea parties, and morale-boosting events.

That was precisely why their organizational unity was so strong.

Suddenly, a frivolous male voice came through the communicator.

"Hey, how's everything going over there?"

One of the grunts immediately replied respectfully,

"Reporting to Mr. Lance! No abnormalities have been detected outside the building!"

The voice on the communicator asked again,

"What about inside the tower?"

The grunt swept his eyes across all the surveillance monitors.

"No abnormalities detected on any floor either!"

"Very good," the man praised casually before continuing, "Remain vigilant. If anything happens, report it immediately."

"Yes, sir!"

Setting the communicator aside, the grunt wiped sweat from his forehead and joked to himself,

"Honestly, no matter how many times I talk to these executives, they always scare the life out of me…"

Yet after speaking, his companion gave no response whatsoever.

Feeling puzzled, he quickly turned around.

To his shock, the other grunt was slumped motionless over the control console—asleep.

"Hey, are you kidding me!?" He hurried over and shoved him hard. "You're seriously sleeping at a time like this? You've got some nerve!"
