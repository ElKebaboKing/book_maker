After giving Ethan a long, deep look, Aya took out a Poké Ball and recalled Venonat.

"Good work, Venonat!"

However, judging from her expression, she was clearly unwilling to accept her defeat.

At that moment, a smoke bomb suddenly rolled into the battlefield. With a loud explosion, thick smoke spread everywhere, and when it finally dispersed, a man appeared in the center of the arena together with Janine.

The man looked to be around forty years old. His dark blue-black hair stood upright like spikes, and he wore a black ninja outfit with a red cape draped behind him. His expression was extremely stern.

"Brother!"

The moment Aya saw him, she immediately dropped to one knee.

Brother? Then this must be Koga!

Koga said calmly,

"Aya, both you and your Venonat still have a long way to go in your training."

Hearing that, Aya lowered her head in shame.

"I was too careless just now. If I hadn't forgotten the duration of Disable, I definitely would've—"

"That's enough." Koga interrupted her. "A loss is a loss. Don't make excuses for it anymore."

As he spoke, he turned toward Ethan.

"That was a truly splendid battle. I am Koga, the Gym Leader of this Fuchsia Gym."

Ethan nodded.

"It's an honor to meet you. My name is Ethan Vale, and I came specifically to challenge you, Mr. Koga!"

Aya and Janine both stepped aside, leaving the battlefield to the two of them.

Facing Koga, Ethan naturally didn't dare underestimate the man. After Lance, Agatha, and the others retired, Koga had once risen to become one of the Elite Four of both Kanto and Johto—a truly powerful Poison-type master!

Even though this was only a standard Gym battle and Koga certainly wouldn't use his ace Pokémon, even his ordinary Pokémon would undoubtedly be extremely formidable.

Koga said,

"The rules are the same as before. As long as you defeat one of my Pokémon, your challenge will be considered successful. However, since you already defeated Janine and Aya, the number of Pokémon you're allowed to use has increased to three."

Three against one?

Ethan nodded to show he understood.

Walking to the opposite side of the field, Koga casually threw out a Poké Ball.

When the blue light faded, Ethan hadn't even managed to clearly see what Pokémon had appeared before an unbearable stench slammed into his face!

Ugh—

That smell was far too familiar. Don't tell him it was another Skuntank!?

Fortunately, once he adapted slightly and looked carefully, he discovered it was actually a Muk!

Pokédex:

"Beep. Muk, the Sludge Pokémon. A Poison-type Pokémon and the evolved form of Grimer. Its body is entirely made of toxic sludge and emits an incredibly foul odor. It can easily squeeze through iron doors, sewer covers, and other narrow openings. It enjoys living in filthy places such as sewers and garbage dumps. The liquid secreted from its body gives off an overpowering stench—even a single drop can pollute an entire swimming pool."

Pinching his nose, Ethan quickly considered his strategy.

Since he was battling someone nearly at Elite Four level, and such an opportunity was incredibly rare—not to mention he had a three-on-one advantage—he wanted his Pokémon to gain as much combat experience as possible.

With that in mind, he once again sent out Jolteon.

"Jolteon, you're up again!"

As the Poké Ball opened, Jolteon reappeared, still carrying injuries from its earlier battle against Janine.

However, this time Ethan wasn't aiming solely for victory. He simply wanted Jolteon to fight freely and gain experience.

Once the match began, the two Pokémon tangled in fierce combat. After a brief rest earlier, Jolteon initially managed to rely on its incredible speed and electric attacks to give Muk quite a bit of trouble.

But as its stamina gradually declined, Muk eventually found an opening and defeated it.

Ethan was very satisfied with Jolteon's performance. After recalling it, he sent out Charmeleon.

"Charmeleon, your turn!"

Across the field, Koga had already seen through Ethan's intentions, and a trace of admiration appeared in his eyes instead.

"You're training your Pokémon while simultaneously wearing down Muk's stamina. Quite a crafty man."

Ethan laughed awkwardly.

"Mr. Koga's Pokémon are all extremely strong. Battling against a master like you is an incredibly valuable opportunity!"

On the battlefield, flames erupted wildly from Charmeleon's mouth. Although they dealt considerable damage to Muk, Charmeleon eventually ran out of stamina and was defeated by a Body Slam from Muk, losing its ability to battle as well.

After recalling Charmeleon, Ethan sent out his final Pokémon.

And this time, he finally became serious.

"Go, Wartortle!"

The evolved form of the little water turtle appeared on the battlefield and immediately turned back toward Ethan with a dependable smile, as if telling him not to worry.

"Wartortle! Wartortle!"

Although the two Pokémon didn't have a direct type advantage over each other, Muk had already consumed a great deal of stamina. Against it, Wartortle held a significant advantage.

"Confidence is a good thing, but you still need to be careful!" Ethan said with a grin. "Go, Wartortle! Use Water Gun!"

A powerful stream of water blasted from Wartortle's mouth and struck Muk first.

Taking the attack head-on, Koga shouted,

"Muk, use Sludge!"

Muk swung its arm forward, separating a large clump of sludge from its body and hurling it toward Wartortle.

"Wartortle! Withdraw into your shell!"

Facing the purple gas-emitting sludge, Wartortle naturally didn't dare take it lightly. It quickly pulled in its head and limbs, hiding completely inside its shell.

The sludge splattered against the shell with loud smacking sounds, but thanks to Wartortle's quick reaction, it didn't suffer much damage.

"Muk, use Body Slam!"

Before Wartortle could emerge again, Muk suddenly leaped high into the air. Its body spread wide before crashing heavily onto Wartortle, wrapping its soft body entirely around the shell!

This attack not only squeezed Wartortle's shell with immense pressure, but also completely restricted its movement!

Outside the battlefield, a satisfied smile appeared on Aya's face, as though she had finally gotten her revenge.

Muk had already defeated Charmeleon earlier, which helped vent her frustration, and now seeing Wartortle being suppressed made her hope even more that her brother would defeat Ethan completely.

But her expectations were destined to fail.

"Wartortle, use Rapid Spin!"

At Ethan's command, the shell beneath Muk began vibrating violently before slowly spinning. Even though Muk struggled desperately to pin Wartortle down, the shell spun faster and faster until Muk completely lost control and was launched into the air!

At the same time, Wartortle spun rapidly several times in midair before extending its limbs and landing perfectly.

As Muk was still airborne and hadn't yet fallen back down, Ethan immediately followed up:

"Perfect chance! Use Aqua Tail!"

Wartortle's tail became wrapped in flowing water, glowing with a blue aura. As it leaped high into the air and twisted its body, it smashed its tail fiercely into Muk!

(End of Chapter)
