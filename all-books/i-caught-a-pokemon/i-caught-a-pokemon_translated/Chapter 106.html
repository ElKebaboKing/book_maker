Viridian—the eternal shade of evergreen.

Setting out from Saffron City, Ethan Vale, now dressed in his brand-new gear, followed a quiet forest path until he arrived at the city surrounded by dense woodlands—Viridian City.

Although the city itself was not particularly large, it served as a major transportation hub and one of western Kanto's most important economic centers. More importantly, it was the mandatory gateway for trainers traveling to the Indigo Plateau to participate in the Pokémon League.

However, because of its close proximity to the Viridian Forest, Bug-type Pokémon frequently wandered into the city and caused trouble for its residents.

As Giovanni's long-time base of operations, Team Rocket's influence here had naturally run deep. While walking through the streets, Ethan had been somewhat worried that lingering Team Rocket members might ambush him. Fortunately, after seeing police officers patrolling nearly every street corner, he finally relaxed.

Apparently, Looker and the International Police understood the situation well. The level of investigation here was second only to Celadon City.

After crossing most of the city, Ethan finally arrived at the Viridian Gym.

Perhaps because so much land was occupied by the surrounding forest, the Gym itself consisted of only a two-story building. Though smaller than many other Gyms, it still looked dignified and imposing. Had it not been for the familiar Pokémon League emblem atop the building, Ethan might have mistaken it for some sort of government embassy.

Its architectural style truly reflected Giovanni's cold, earth-like authority.

Even before Agatha became the acting Gym Leader, the Viridian Gym had already been considered the most difficult Gym to challenge.

It wasn't necessarily because Giovanni's strength was overwhelming, but because the Gym spent most of its time closed to challengers, leaving countless trainers disappointed.

Now that Agatha had taken over, her tremendous strength and calculating personality had made Viridian Gym an even more insurmountable obstacle for inexperienced trainers.

Entering the Gym, Ethan explained his purpose to the staff and was promptly escorted to Agatha.

After exchanging greetings, the oldest member of the Elite Four felt that Ethan's voice sounded strangely familiar, yet she failed to recognize him at first.

Only after Haunter revealed itself did realization dawn on her.

"So it's you."

Agatha smiled in surprise.

"I truly thought your real identity might be some undercover agent. I never expected you to simply be a League trainer traveling around challenging Gyms."

Scratching his head, Ethan laughed awkwardly.

"Being a Pokémon Trainer is my real profession. As for that operation... I was merely a five-star model citizen doing what any righteous person should."

"What an interesting young man."

Returning to the matter at hand, Agatha continued,

"I already have a good understanding of your abilities. There's no need for a standard three-on-three Gym battle."

"Let's settle everything with a single match."

"Huh?"

Ethan blinked.

"You're an Elite Four member. Isn't that format a little unfair to me? If my Pokémon loses, I won't even get a chance to make a comeback. I'll lose the Gym Challenge outright."

"You're absolutely right."

Agatha wore a sly grin.

"That's exactly why you'll need to show me your courage and skill."

"But don't worry. This is only an ordinary Gym battle. I won't be using my ace."

Since she'd already decided, Ethan could only agree.

The two entered the battlefield and took their places at opposite Trainer's Boxes.

Viridian Gym's battlefield had been built inside a grand hall designed in a classical Western style. Murals, stone carvings, towering pillars, and beautifully painted ceilings decorated every corner. Beneath the dim lighting, the entire hall felt mysterious and almost eerie.

Since it had originally been a Ground-type Gym, Agatha had made no effort to renovate the battlefield itself. The arena beneath Ethan's feet remained a simple field of compacted earth.

Agatha made the first move.

"Come out, Misdreavus!"

A burst of light flashed from the Poké Ball.

Floating into the air appeared a ghostly Pokémon with a dark purple body, glowing crimson eyes, bead-like ornaments around its neck, and wisps of hair that flickered like ghostly flames.

Pokédex:

"Misdreavus, the Screech Pokémon. A Ghost-type Pokémon. It loves playing pranks, often crying out at night with sounds resembling human wails to frighten people. The red beads around its neck absorb fear, which it uses as nourishment."

Facing a pure Ghost-type Pokémon, Ethan realized that, aside from Gardevoir, none of his Pokémon were placed at any major disadvantage.

After a brief moment of thought, his finger stopped on one Poké Ball.

"This battle is yours. Go, Ivysaur!"

A flash of blue light revealed Ivysaur.

One floated in the air while the other stood firmly on the ground. Though separated by height, both Pokémon stared at each other with fierce determination.

"An Ivysaur?"

Agatha raised an eyebrow.

"I thought you'd send out your Haunter."

"Ace against ace, spear against spear."

Ethan smiled.

"Since you're holding back your strongest Pokémon, there's no reason for me to send out mine."

"And don't underestimate my Ivysaur."

"It's much stronger than it looks."

Agatha snorted.

"I hope you won't regret that decision after you lose."

Once both Pokémon were ready, the referee shouted,

"Begin!"

Both Trainers issued commands simultaneously.

"Ivysaur, use Razor Leaf!"

"Misdreavus, use Shadow Ball!"

Countless razor-sharp leaves burst from Ivysaur's flower bud and sliced toward Misdreavus, colliding head-on with the Shadow Ball.

Although Shadow Ball possessed greater raw power, the relentless stream of Razor Leaves eventually neutralized it.

Seeing her first attack fail, Agatha immediately commanded,

"Misdreavus, use Psybeam!"

So she intended to confuse Ivysaur first before attacking freely afterward.

Ethan's mind raced.

"Ivysaur, use Leech Seed!"

The flower bud trembled as a seed shot forward at incredible speed.

At the very same moment, Psybeam struck Ivysaur.

Both Pokémon were hit.

Fortunately, Psybeam failed to confuse Ivysaur.

Meanwhile, the Leech Seed rapidly sprouted upon Misdreavus, wrapping vines tightly around its body.

Not only did it restrict Misdreavus's movement, but it also steadily drained its stamina.

"Excellent!"

Ethan immediately followed up.

"Use Vine Whip!"

Two long vines lashed out from Ivysaur's flower bud, striking Misdreavus heavily.

Unable to dodge while restrained, Misdreavus could only endure the attack.

Agatha frowned deeply.

"Misdreavus! Break free at once!"

Following her command, Misdreavus fired another Shadow Ball directly at the vines binding it.

With a loud explosion, it ended up looking rather battered, but finally succeeded in freeing itself.

( End of Chapter )
