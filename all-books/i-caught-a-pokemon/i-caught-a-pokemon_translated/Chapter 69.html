"Giovanni!?"

Everyone was shocked and immediately tensed up, preparing for battle.

"What's going on?" Ethan quietly asked Handsome Guy. "Why is he here? Didn't you already confirm he was at the Viridian Gym and send Bruno and Lorelei after him?"

Handsome Guy hesitated.

"I'm not sure either. Giovanni's whereabouts have always been unpredictable. Maybe Bruno and Lorelei went after a false lead. But since he's shown himself here, that saves us a lot of trouble—we can arrest him directly!"

Standing behind Giovanni were the four Rocket Executives: Apollo, Athena, Lance, and Lambda.

Despite being cornered, not a trace of panic could be seen on any of their faces.

Handsome Guy stepped forward several paces and stared directly at Giovanni.

"Giovanni! You are suspected of kidnapping, destruction, and multiple acts of terrorism. Under international security law, I am officially placing you under arrest!"

Giovanni glanced at him indifferently.

"The exposure of this base was only a matter of time. I'm not surprised you found this place. However, your speed was indeed several years faster than I expected. It seems the International Police are not entirely a bunch of useless fools after all."

"Enough talking!" Handsome Guy said coldly. "The destruction of Team Rocket is inevitable! At this point, are you going to surrender quietly, or continue resisting to the bitter end?"

Hearing that, Giovanni slowly rose from the sofa.

"You occupied one of my bases, and now you think victory is already assured?"

"What do you mean by that?" Handsome Guy pointed behind himself. "So many people have gathered here today, and Mr. Lance and Ms. Agatha are both present. Do you still think you can escape?"

Looking at Giovanni, Lance's expression carried a trace of sorrow.

"Mr. Giovanni, I once believed you were an honorable man. It seems I truly misjudged you."

"Young Giovanni…" Agatha sighed as well. "Even though we've known each other for many years, I never imagined you were hiding such an identity."

"Humans are often blinded by the surface of what they believe, unable to see the true nature of things," Giovanni said calmly, his tone as cold and emotionless as ever. "How much have any of you ever truly understood me?"

"Enough nonsense!" Handsome Guy shouted. "Giovanni! The strongest Trainer in Kanto and the strongest Pokémon are all gathered here today. If you know what's good for you, surrender now!"

"The strongest?" Giovanni sneered. "What a joke. A group of ignorant fools who know nothing of the vastness of this world. Today, I'll show you what the truly strongest Pokémon looks like."

As Giovanni finished speaking, a thin red light suddenly appeared deep within the dark corridor behind him.

Then, before everyone's eyes, a strange armored Pokémon wearing a helmet slowly floated out from the darkness using psychic power.

It was a humanoid Pokémon. Most of its body was concealed beneath armor, making its true appearance impossible to discern. Only its silver-white legs, shaped almost like a cat's, and its thick purple tail were clearly visible.

"What kind of Pokémon is that!?"

Everyone exclaimed in shock. Even Lance and Agatha, both highly experienced, looked stunned.

Only Ethan showed no surprise upon seeing the Pokémon.

At this point in time, it was indeed still under Giovanni's control.

"You think a single Pokémon can stop all of us?" Handsome Guy waved his hand forward and immediately released his Croagunk and Growlithe.

The two Pokémon leapt forward simultaneously, launching countless Poison Stings and a stream of Flamethrower directly at the armored Pokémon!

However, just as the attacks reached it, the armored Pokémon raised one arm. In the next second, the poison needles and flames became enveloped in blue light and froze in midair!

With a slight motion of its hand, the blue light surged forward again, hurling the attacks back at Croagunk and Growlithe and smashing both Pokémon violently into the wall behind them!

Bang! Bang!

After two loud crashes, both Pokémon collapsed onto the floor.

Everyone was horrified.

With only a single move, it had effortlessly subdued two Pokémon. After displaying such terrifying strength, nobody dared underestimate the armored Pokémon anymore!

Lance gave Dragonite a nod, while Agatha released her Gengar. The two Pokémon attacked from opposite sides simultaneously!

A Shadow Ball formed in Gengar's claws before being hurled toward the armored Pokémon, while Dragonite beat its wings and charged straight at it.

Yet the instant the attacks were about to connect, the armored Pokémon suddenly vanished.

When it reappeared, it extended both hands at once. Dragonite and Gengar were instantly wrapped in blue light and slammed heavily into the ground.

"As expected, it's incredibly powerful," Nanu sighed before saying to everyone, "Don't just stand there. Let's all attack together!"

Erika and Sabrina both nodded and commanded their Pokémon into battle. Once Handsome Guy's Croagunk and Growlithe climbed back to their feet, they also rejoined the assault alongside everyone else's Pokémon.

But as more and more Pokémon entered the fight and attacks flew wildly in every direction, the entire fourth underground floor began trembling slightly.

Haunter was just about to join the battle when it noticed Ethan's calm expression. Scratching its head, it asked:

"Huh? Aren't you going to let me help too?"

Ethan hesitated slightly.

Of course they needed help—but Giovanni and the four executives behind him all looked as though they were simply watching a show. None of them attempted to flee or release additional Pokémon into battle.

Did they truly have absolute confidence in Mewtwo's strength?

As Ethan pondered this, a strange and genderless voice suddenly echoed within his mind.

'You know my name is Mewtwo? Do you know me?'

Whose voice was that?

Ethan froze briefly before realizing someone was communicating with him telepathically!

And judging by the tone, it was undoubtedly Mewtwo itself. Yet when Ethan looked up at it, Mewtwo was still fighting as though its attention wasn't on him at all.

'So you really do know my name. Who are you?'

Before Ethan could even answer within his thoughts, a sudden feeling of dread surged through him.

In the next instant, his memories began rapidly flipping through his mind, as though someone were reading through his entire life!

Not good!!

"Kirlia!!!"

Ethan suddenly shouted, startling Erika, Sabrina, and even Haunter beside him. But he had no time to care about that. Struggling, he stretched his hand toward Kirlia.

Kirlia immediately realized the urgency of the situation. Using Teleport, it instantly appeared beside Ethan and reached out with hands wrapped in psychic energy, tightly grasping his own!
