At this moment, Haunter floated quietly in place, its eyes fixed on Ethan as well. Its body was in a faintly transparent state, not fully materialized.

However, through the goggles, its figure appeared perfectly clear—even the normally invisible portions of its body could be seen completely!

The moment Ethan removed the goggles, Haunter returned to its usual appearance.

"Huh? Why are you staring at me like that?" Haunter asked curiously.

"Your body becomes completely visible through these goggles."

Haunter was astonished.

"What? There are really glasses that amazing?"

Ethan nodded.

"If I'm not mistaken, this should be the Silph Scope."

"The Silph Scope? What's that?"

Seeing Erika and Sabrina approach as well, Ethan explained:

"It's a high-tech product manufactured by Silph Co. Its purpose is to let people see things invisible to the naked eye—for example, revealing hidden Ghost-type Pokémon, detecting enemies concealed within smokescreens, and even seeing psychic energy fluctuations in the air."

"It's that incredible!?" Erika exclaimed.

Ethan turned toward Sabrina.

"This was probably one of the items Rocket Team stole from Silph Co. during the attack on Silph Tower."

Sabrina looked around at the surrounding equipment.

"In that case, were they trying to analyze its technology and mass-produce it?"

Just as Ethan was about to reply, the laboratory door suddenly opened once more, and several people rushed in from outside!

Wearing berets and black uniforms, they were none other than the members of the Matori Matrix who had clashed with Ethan before! Their leader was the burly one-eyed man—Kane!

"You filthy rats! So you've already made it this far!"

The moment Kane saw Handsome Guy and Ethan, fury burned in his eyes.

"So it's you two again!? This time, without those two idiots helping you, I'll make sure you die here!!"

At the sight of these people, Handsome Guy's still-healing wounds began throbbing painfully again.

"Kane! Last time, I was seriously injured because of your ambush! But this time, I'll personally bring you to justice and wipe Rocket Team out for good!"

As the saying goes, enemies become especially hateful upon meeting face-to-face. The instant both sides finished exchanging threats, their Pokémon lunged forward and began battling fiercely!

Ethan stored the Silph Scope inside his backpack and was about to send Haunter into battle when Handsome Guy suddenly stopped him.

"We can't all get tied down here. Go to the other sections immediately—we absolutely cannot allow them to keep destroying data!"

"You still want to split up?" Ethan frowned. "If we keep dividing our forces, we'll lose our advantage."

Personally, he preferred keeping the four of them together, concentrating their strength into a single overwhelming force.

However, Handsome Guy's decision was not necessarily wrong either. After all, their different identities naturally caused them to prioritize different things.

Ethan's main concern was everyone's safety. Since they had already infiltrated this far, it was better to stay together and avoid unnecessary sacrifices from being defeated separately.

But as an International Police officer, Handsome Guy valued mission completion and efficiency above personal safety. To headquarters, every piece of information in this base was incredibly valuable.

"Don't worry." Handsome Guy forced a confident grin. "Once I deal with these guys, I'll come meet up with you."

"Alright then. We'll leave this place to you!"

Just as Ethan was about to leave, Kane roared furiously.

"You think you're leaving!? None of you are getting out of here! Aggron, use Take Down!!"

Aggron charged forward with heavy footsteps, smashing toward Ethan's group, only to be halted by Sabrina's telekinesis.

"I'll stay and help him as well." Sabrina turned to Ethan. "You two go ahead. Leave this place to us."

The scene instantly reminded Ethan of the similar situation back at Silph Tower. Exchanging a glance with Erika, the two no longer hesitated and immediately escaped through the back entrance.

After passing through a long connecting corridor, they arrived at the core section of the base. There, they discovered an elevator leading farther downward.

Ethan pressed the buttons twice, but the elevator showed no signs of activating.

Pointing at the card slot on the control panel, Erika said,

"Looks like it requires a keycard to operate. Let's find another route."

Continuing onward, they passed through several rooms before eventually entering the base cafeteria.

The two abruptly stopped—not because they didn't want to continue, but because seated behind rows of dining tables were a man and a woman calmly eating as if they had been waiting there all along.

The woman was Athena, the same Rocket Executive Ethan had fought at Silph Tower.

And beside her sat another man wearing the same white Rocket Team uniform.

He had short teal-blue hair and a handsome, composed face.

After elegantly wiping the corner of his mouth with a napkin, he slowly raised his head, his sharp gaze settling onto Ethan.

"This is as far as you go," the man said coldly. "I won't allow you to take another step forward."

The instant he spoke, the wall beside Ethan and Erika suddenly exploded apart. A massive Pokémon resembling the monster Godzilla burst through the wall and slammed directly into Erika!

Her body was sent flying backward, but Ethan reacted quickly and caught her in time.

A trace of blood appeared at the corner of her lips as she stared at the Pokémon with a grave expression.

"It's Tyranitar!"

Carefully setting Erika down behind him, Ethan already had his suspicions, but he still asked:

"Who are you?"

The man and Athena rose from their seats and slowly approached.

"You're Ethan, correct? My name is Apollo. Just like Lambda, Lance, and Athena—the ones you defeated before—I am one of Rocket Team's Four Executives."

So it really is him!

Ethan glanced back at Erika and gave a bitter smile.

"I originally thought everything would go smoothly with the International Police backing us up, but it looks like we still have to fight these people head-on. Can you still battle?"

"I'm fine."

Erika slowly stood up and waved her hand, releasing two Pokémon at once.

One was the Gloom that had previously battled Ethan, while the other was a Pokémon resembling a walking pineapple tree—Exeggutor!

Athena stared coldly at Ethan.

"Take off that ridiculous mask. Did you really think I wouldn't recognize you just because you're wearing it?"

As she spoke, she also released her Pokémon—Arbok and Murkrow!

Knowing a fierce battle was now unavoidable, Ethan no longer hesitated and threw several Poké Balls.

In an instant, Kirlia, Jolteon, Charmeleon, Wartortle, and Ivysaur appeared before him.

"ROOOAR!!"

The atmosphere between both sides became incredibly tense. Then, with Tyranitar's furious roar, all the Pokémon launched their attacks simultaneously at the opposing side!
