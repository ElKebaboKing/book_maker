"Now you've got nowhere left to run!" Ethan shouted as he let out a long breath. "Gyarados, let's finish this! Use Hydro Pump!"

A massive torrent of water formed inside Gyarados's mouth before blasting forward like a high-caliber cannon shell, slamming directly into Ninetales.

Because its body was trapped in Gyarados's jaws, Ninetales had no way to dodge and could only endure the full force of the powerful Water-type attack!

At last, it could no longer hold on. Following Rapidash's fate, it collapsed beneath Gyarados, defeated.

However, the Hydro Pump's remaining force did not stop there. After knocking out Ninetales, it continued forward and, by sheer coincidence, snapped another chain!

The already unstable battlefield suddenly dropped like an elevator that had lost all support!

BOOM!!!

With a thunderous crash, the entire platform slammed onto the magma below. Not only did it create a gigantic wave of lava, but the battlefield itself shattered into several pieces!

"This is bad!"

Ethan and Blaine hurriedly pulled out their Poké Balls and recalled their Pokémon.

At the same time, scorching heat waves surged toward the shore. Seeing Ethan still standing there, Misty ignored her own safety and grabbed his arm, dragging him back to a safe area.

WHOOSH!!

The splashing magma rained down like fiery droplets throughout the underground cavern. By the time everything finally calmed and the lava slowly receded, the hidden battlefield beneath the hot spring inn had already been reduced to ruins.

Patting Misty's hand reassuringly, Ethan turned toward Blaine.

"Mr. Blaine, I'm really sorry!" Ethan apologized sincerely. "I accidentally destroyed your battlefield."

"It's fine. A Gym can always be rebuilt. What matters is that none of you were hurt," Blaine replied casually. "Though it looks like the final match will need a different venue!"

After leaving the underground passage and returning to the inn, the group rested briefly before following Blaine toward the volcano.

They walked along a winding mountain trail that climbed higher and higher until they finally arrived at the crater of Cinnabar Volcano!

Looking down at the bubbling lava beneath them, Brock asked worriedly, "Is it really safe to battle here? What if the volcano erupts?"

"No need to worry," Blaine laughed. "I'm not only a Fire-type Pokémon expert, but also a volcano expert. While this volcano has been somewhat active lately, it won't erupt anytime soon."

Hearing his reassurance, Ethan stepped closer to inspect the replacement battlefield.

Inside the circular crater stood dozens of stone pillars, all similar in shape. Although they appeared naturally formed, Ethan had never seen such a strange volcanic crater before, whether online or in the news.

And beneath those stone pillars rolled the steaming lava below.

"My final Pokémon will be Magmar," Blaine declared as he sent out his strongest Pokémon. Then he changed the subject slightly and asked, "But I hope that Gyarados from earlier isn't your strongest Pokémon. Otherwise, now that I understand your battle style, you won't stand much of a chance."

"You don't need to worry about that," Ethan replied. "Ever since Gyarados evolved, this was actually its first Gym battle. Even these results surprised me."

"…"

Blaine looked down at the two Poké Balls on his belt and froze for a moment before muttering quietly, "So that overwhelming Gyarados performance was only its first Gym battle…"

Not noticing the strange look on Blaine's face, Ethan smiled.

"But honestly, this is the first time I've met a Gym Leader who worries about whether the challenger is strong enough."

Blaine sighed, his voice carrying the weight of his personal philosophy.

"Life itself is a riddle—an endless cycle of asking and answering questions. Every time people solve a new riddle, they grow stronger from it. Pokémon battles are much the same. No matter how high the wall before you may be, as long as you overcome it, you'll become even stronger! That's why, as long as we live, we must continue creating new riddles and constantly challenge Pokémon battles!"

"Just as expected from Mr. Blaine. I've learned something valuable," Ethan nodded.

Without wasting any more time, he sent out his second Pokémon.

"Go, Wartortle!"

As the blue light flashed, Wartortle appeared atop one of the stone pillars inside the volcano.

"Wartortle! Wartortle!"

"A Wartortle?" Blaine frowned. "I thought you were about to send out something stronger. I didn't expect it to only be a Wartortle that hasn't even fully evolved yet. I don't understand—why would you choose it?"

"An opportunity like this is rare," Ethan replied casually while putting away the Poké Ball. "Of course I want it to gain battle experience. Maybe Wartortle isn't Magmar's match, but I still have one more Pokémon afterward. Even if it loses, at least it can wear Magmar down first."

"I see!" Blaine burst into laughter. "Looks like you're the one truly living by my philosophy!"

Once both Pokémon were ready, the two trainers issued their commands almost simultaneously.

"Wartortle, use Water Gun!"

"Magmar, use Fire Punch!"

A stream of water shot from Wartortle's mouth straight toward Magmar. But Magmar endured the damage head-on and leaped rapidly across the stone pillars until it closed the distance with Wartortle.

The same strategy had worked extremely well against Pikachu earlier. Clearly, Blaine intended to recreate that advantage despite the unfavorable type matchup.

Unfortunately for him, Ethan was not Ash, and Wartortle was not Pikachu.

Ethan naturally wasn't about to let him succeed.

"Wartortle, Withdraw!"

As Wartortle pulled its body into its shell, Magmar suddenly had no clear target to strike and could only pound repeatedly on the shell with its flaming fists.

Blaine frowned and shouted, "Magmar, throw it into the lava!"

!?!?

Ethan's eyes widened in shock. Was that even allowed!?

Even though Wartortle was a Water-type Pokémon, if it were thrown into lava, forget about peacefully retiring in old age—it would probably be burned to ashes instantly!

Seeing Magmar reaching out as if preparing to lift Wartortle, Ethan hurriedly shouted:

"Wartortle, Rapid Spin!!"

Wartortle spun violently faster and faster, forcing Magmar backward.

At the same time, the shell launched high into the air, tracing a perfect arc as it spun toward Magmar.

"Magmar, use Flamethrower!"

Magmar unleashed a massive burst of flames toward Wartortle. Yet despite being engulfed in fire, Wartortle still smashed directly into Magmar's face!

(End of Chapter)
