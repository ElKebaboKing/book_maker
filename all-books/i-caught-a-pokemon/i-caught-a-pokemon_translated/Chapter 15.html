Early in the morning, after a full night of rest, Ethan Vale felt refreshed and energized. Just as he was about to leave the Pokémon Center, he suddenly noticed a boy with a Pikachu and an orange-haired girl arguing about something in front of a poster on the wall.

The boy wore a baseball cap, a T-shirt beneath a blue jacket, and although he looked a little silly, he was full of spirit and overflowing with youthful energy.

The orange-haired girl wore her hair in a side ponytail. She was beautiful and dressed in shorts and a fitted yellow tank top that exposed her midriff, slender arms, and long fair legs. She looked equally vibrant and energetic.

With just one glance, Ethan Vale immediately recognized who they were.

Wasn't this Ash and Misty?

And the yellow mouse beside Ash—

Wasn't that the legendary Pikachu that could somehow go toe-to-toe with anyone… the mighty "Pikagod"?

Seeing the two of them carrying backpacks and looking travel-worn, Ethan Vale couldn't help but sigh inwardly.

He hadn't expected them to meet so quickly and already arrive in Pewter City.

Their speed was incredible…

At that moment, Misty pointed at the poster and said:

"Challenging the Pokémon League Tournament isn't as easy as you think. At the very least, you need to defeat eight Gym Leaders and collect their badges before you're qualified to enter."

"That's no big deal for me." Ash puffed up confidently, his tone filled with the fearless courage of a rookie trainer. "Once we leave here, I'm heading straight to challenge the Pewter Gym and show you our true strength!"

Hearing their conversation, Ethan Vale walked over and interrupted them.

"You're Ash, right?"

Ash turned around and realized the speaker was an unfamiliar young man.

"That's right, I'm Ash. Who are you?"

After giving him a long look, Ethan Vale replied:

"My name is Ethan Vale. A few days ago, when I visited Pallet Town, I happened to hear Professor Oak mention you. I didn't expect we'd actually meet here today."

"You know Professor Oak too?" Ash smiled brightly. "This is my partner Pikachu, and she's—"

Before Ash could finish, Misty interrupted proudly:

"My name is Misty, the world's number one beautiful girl!"

The world's number one beautiful girl?

Ethan Vale's eyes unconsciously glanced at her chest.

Misty definitely had the potential to grow into a beauty, but her body clearly hadn't fully matured yet. If she wanted to become the world's number one beauty, she still had a long way to go.

Meanwhile, Misty was also carefully observing Ethan Vale. When she noticed his handsome appearance and the Kirlia following behind him, she suddenly remembered something.

"Wait a second—you're the Trainer with the Kirlia who defeated my sisters!?"

Ethan Vale deliberately played dumb.

"Oh? Your sisters are?"

Hearing this, Misty immediately closed her mouth, clearly unwilling to reveal that she came from the Cerulean Gym.

At that moment, a middle-aged man wearing a tall red hat and sporting a thick beard walked over.

"Are you all planning to challenge Brock, the Pewter Gym Leader?"

Ash nodded confidently.

"Of course! As soon as we leave the Pokémon Center, I'm heading straight to the Pewter Gym to beat him!"

Unexpectedly, the middle-aged man burst into loud laughter.

"Hahahahaha! You?"

His tone was filled with mockery. Before Ash could even respond, the man laughed again and walked out of the Pokémon Center.

After watching him leave through the doors, Ethan Vale asked curiously:

"Who was that? He sounded like he really looked down on you."

Misty explained:

"His name is Flint. When we first arrived here, he was selling stones nearby."

Flint? The stone-selling guy?

Ethan Vale froze for a moment before immediately realizing—

Wasn't that Brock's father who had been away from home for years!?

Watching the man's departing figure, Ash angrily declared:

"Hmph! That guy seriously underestimated me! Fine, I'll head to the Gym right now and defeat Brock to prove him wrong!"

"Well, that's convenient." Ethan Vale shrugged with a smile. "I was planning to challenge the Pewter Gym too. Why don't we go together?"

Pewter City.

The city's name came from the color of stone, and it had supposedly been famous for producing rocks since ancient times. However, aside from stones and fossils, it really had no other unique characteristics. Overall, it was a quiet northern city.

Mountains and farmland surrounded the entire city, while a wide river split it in half. The Pewter Gym was located in the city's northwest district, directly across the street from another famous landmark—the Pewter Museum of Science.

The group soon arrived at the Pewter Gym.

From a distance, the Gym looked as though it had been built entirely from massive stacked boulders and steel. But once they approached closer, they realized it was mostly just decoration.

Those giant rocks were merely thin stone panels crafted to look authentic.

After walking around the decorative entrance, they finally discovered that the back of the Gym looked no different from an ordinary warehouse.

"What the heck," Misty muttered unhappily. "I thought the entire Gym was made of stone. Turns out only this tiny front section is real!"

Pushing open the Gym doors, they entered a surprisingly dark interior despite it still being daytime outside.

The group walked deeper inside until suddenly a spotlight switched on, illuminating a corner of the Gym where a man wearing a green vest sat cross-legged.

"Who are you people?"

The man's voice was calm and steady, almost like a monk who had reached enlightenment.

"You're Brock, the Gym Leader, right?" Ash quickly stepped forward. "I'm Ash from Pallet Town, and I'm here to challenge you!"

Ethan Vale also spoke up.

"My name is Ethan Vale. I'm here to challenge the Gym as well."

"As the Gym Leader, I don't mind how many challengers come," Brock slowly stood up. "However, each Trainer may only use two Pokémon. Which one of you wants to battle first?"

Only now did Ethan Vale finally get a proper look at Brock.

His spiky hair, overly serious face, and perpetually squinting eyes gave him an appearance far older than his actual age. Even when staring carefully, it was difficult to tell whether his eyes were open or closed.

After glancing at Ethan Vale, Ash eagerly stepped forward.

"Of course I'm going first. Bring it on!"

Seeing Ash so enthusiastic, Ethan Vale had no intention of competing with him for the first battle. Instead, he led his Pokémon toward the spectator seats.

"Ash, good luck!" Misty waved encouragingly before walking over to sit beside Ethan Vale.

Looking toward Ash, Brock snapped his fingers.

Instantly, all the lights in the Gym switched on, illuminating every corner. At the same time, compartments beneath the spectator stands opened, and two battlefields made of stone and dirt slowly rose from below before locking together into one complete arena.

As a specialist in Rock- and Ground-type Pokémon, Brock's battlefield naturally matched his style.

Seeing Ash step onto the field with Pikachu, Brock leaped gracefully to the opposite Trainer's platform.

Ash pointed forward dramatically and shouted:

"Go, Pikachu!"

"So young…" Brock smiled faintly before throwing out his own Poké Ball.

"Come out, my Pokémon—Onix!"

(End of Chapter)
