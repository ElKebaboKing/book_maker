"Manga?"

Ethan raised an eyebrow. He had never considered taking the story in that direction before.

Yoshino Cerise replied,

"Of course. Compared to plain text and illustrations, manga can present the excitement of a story much more directly, and younger audiences tend to enjoy it more."

"Wouldn't that be too troublesome?"

Yoshino's voice was full of anticipation as she laughed softly.

"It is a bit troublesome, but the structure and framework of the story are already finished. All that's left is bringing it to life visually. Besides, I'm usually free at home anyway. If I can create a manga like this, it would also fulfill one of my dreams."

Since she had already put it that way, Ethan had no reason to refuse.

The two discussed several details before finally ending the call.

Because his plans had changed, Ethan sent Misty a message telling them not to wait for him. After challenging the Saffron Gym, they could continue onward without him.

Over the next two days, Ethan remained in Maiden's Peak, gathering reference material locally while staying in contact with Yoshino Cerise and providing her with additional background information.

On the third day, after receiving a call from her informing him that she had already completed over twenty pages of draft work, Ethan finally set out and returned to Vermilion City.

Professor Cerise's home was a typical Japanese-style detached house located not far from the laboratory.

Since they already knew Ethan would be visiting, Professor Cerise had specifically stayed home instead of going to work at the lab.

And because it happened to be Saturday, the entire family of four—including Chloe and her younger brother Parker—came outside together to greet him.

Upon seeing Ethan, Yamper immediately ran circles around him excitedly with its tail wagging nonstop.

Looking at the perfectly assembled family, Ethan could not help sweating slightly.

Why does this suddenly feel like I'm visiting my future in-laws for the first time empty-handed…?

As the host, Professor Cerise spoke first.

"Welcome, welcome! Please come in."

Ethan nodded politely. His gaze briefly swept over Parker before stopping on the lady of the house.

"It's a pleasure to meet you. I'm Ethan. Sorry for intruding."

Yoshino Cerise wore round red-framed glasses. Her appearance resembled Chloe somewhat, though her face was rounder and more mature. Her cherry-pink hair was styled into a side braid that rested over her left shoulder.

As for Parker Cerise, he resembled Professor Cerise more closely.

The little boy carried a cool and composed demeanor typical of a classic young prodigy. Even though he was several years younger than Chloe, he seemed surprisingly mature and reliable—perhaps even more dependable than his sister.

After somewhat awkwardly changing into indoor slippers, Ethan entered the house and arrived at the living room.

Yoshino Cerise took out a transparent folder and removed the twenty-plus manga pages inside.

"For now, I've only drawn these pages. I estimate it'll take another one hundred ninety-five pages to fully adapt the rest of the story. However, since the compositions aren't too complicated, it may go even faster."

The precision of her estimate made it obvious how seriously she was taking the project.

As Ethan carefully flipped through the pages, he sighed in admiration.

"Your artwork is incredible, ma'am. It's truly a shame you never became a professional manga artist."

For a full-time housewife, having her abilities genuinely recognized naturally made her extremely happy.

"Your story is excellent too," Yoshino Cerise said with a smile behind her hand. She already had an extremely favorable impression of Ethan. "Only a wonderful story paired with excellent artwork can become truly popular."

"That's true," Chloe agreed. "I was the very first reader of the story. After reading it carefully, I honestly found it really captivating."

"I was second, and Dad was third." Parker rested both hands behind his head casually.

However, perhaps it was just his imagination, but Parker suddenly felt like a round face inside Ethan's shadow had just stuck out its tongue and made a goofy face at him!

He rubbed his eyes and leaned closer to inspect it carefully.

The next second, a black ball-like figure suddenly popped out and startled him badly.

"WAAAH!!"

Parker jumped high into the air and tumbled behind the sofa.

"Hehehe!" Gastly appeared fully, grinning mischievously. "What a scaredy-cat!"

"So this is the Gastly mentioned in the story?" Professor Cerise helped his son up while looking at Gastly with immense curiosity.

"That's right." Ethan stopped Gastly from teasing further before nodding. "In a sense, it's actually the true protagonist of the story."

After climbing back up, Parker showed no fear despite falling earlier. Instead, he circled around Gastly curiously, clearly fascinated by how it could hide within shadows.

At noon, after repeatedly insisting that he stay, the Cerise family invited Ethan to have lunch with them.

The group chatted and laughed together, and the dining table quickly filled with a warm and cheerful atmosphere.

When Ethan showed them the photos he had taken beneath the sea during the sinking of the S.S. Anne, all four of them cried out in shock.

Professor Cerise widened his eyes in disbelief.

"The news about the S.S. Anne sinking caused an enormous uproar across Kanto a few days ago. I never imagined you not only experienced the incident personally, but actually sank to the bottom of the sea along with the ship and still managed to escape alive!"

Even Chloe stared at Ethan as though she were seeing him in an entirely new light.

Meanwhile, Parker's eyes sparkled with admiration.

"Big Brother Ethan, you're sooo cool!!"

…

After finalizing a completion schedule with Yoshino Cerise, Ethan once again set off on his journey toward Saffron City.

Saffron—the shining golden color of brilliance.

This time, aside from Kirlia accompanying him openly and Gastly hiding within his shadow, Ethan kept all his other Pokémon inside their Poké Balls.

Nothing noteworthy happened along the journey.

By the time he finally arrived at this massive metropolitan city in the Kanto region, Ash and his group had already departed for another Gym city.

However, according to Misty, they had encountered enormous difficulties while challenging the Saffron Gym.

After failing their first attempt and suffering quite a bit afterward, they were forced to travel to Lavender Town's Pokémon Tower. Only after obtaining the help of a Haunter there were they finally able to earn the Gym Badge successfully.

Because of this, they had also wasted two extra days, meaning the distance between them and Ethan was still not very large.

Saffron City was filled with towering skyscrapers everywhere.

Not only was it the central commercial hub of the Kanto region, but it also possessed the largest infrastructure in the entire region. It even housed a magnetic levitation train station connecting Kanto and Johto.

Every day, countless trains traveled between Saffron City and Goldenrod City at speeds reaching up to 550 kilometers per hour, bringing tremendous economic prosperity and vitality to both metropolitan areas.

Passing through the bustling streets, Ethan headed directly toward the Saffron Gym.

Looking up, the building resembled countless white umbrellas pressed closely together, giving it a very distinctive design.

Still, in Ethan's opinion, the appearance was somewhat too monotonous compared to the other Gyms he had seen.

As the official Gym Leader of Saffron City, Sabrina specialized in Psychic-type Pokémon and was notoriously difficult to deal with. However, defeating her would earn challengers the Marsh Badge.

Passing through the automatic doors, Ethan glanced around the Gym interior.

The main hall was completely empty, with no one there to greet him.

After walking forward a short distance, he looked through the windows of a nearby room and saw a group of people dressed in white lab coats seated around tables.

Each of them held a spoon in hand while staring at it intensely, seemingly practicing some sort of psychic ability training.
