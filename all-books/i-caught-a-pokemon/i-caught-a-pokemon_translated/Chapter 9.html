"Ralts, use Confusion!"

Hearing the command, Ralts was just about to use Confusion when Seel suddenly dove into the water at incredible speed.

The thick ice covering the surface prevented Ralts from clearly tracking Seel's position. It glanced around cautiously, but from a blind spot in Ralts' vision, Seel suddenly burst through the ice and slammed forward headfirst!

However, after months spent together with Ethan Vale and countless battles fought side by side, Ralts quickly reacted after a brief moment of surprise and used Teleport once again to evade the attack.

"Seel, use Ice Shard!"

Seel opened its mouth again and formed several sharp chunks of ice before firing them toward the newly reappeared Ralts.

A violent explosion erupted as shattered ice and broken platform fragments scattered everywhere, engulfing Ralts completely.

Yet once the dust settled, Daisy and her sisters were shocked to discover that although Ralts had been struck by Ice Shard, it had not only endured the attack but appeared to have taken very little damage!

"Huh? That's strange!" Daisy exclaimed in surprise. "Psychic- and Fairy-type Ralts shouldn't have any type advantage or disadvantage against Water-type Seel. And Ice Shard is an Ice-type move, so why did it deal so little damage?"

"Miss Daisy," Ethan Vale said with a smile, "I assume your Seel's Ability is Thick Fat?"

The Thick Fat Ability reduced damage taken from Ice- and Fire-type moves by half.

Although confused by the sudden mention of Abilities, Daisy still nodded.

"That's right… but what does that have to do with anything?"

Ethan Vale shrugged.

"My Ralts' Ability is Trace, which means it can completely copy Seel's Thick Fat Ability. That's why your Ice-type attacks aren't very effective against it."

"So that's it!" Daisy finally realized the truth and smiled bitterly. "So at the very start of the battle, it had already copied Seel's Ability?"

"Exactly."

Seeing Ethan Vale confirm it, Daisy sighed helplessly.

"I have to admit, that's quite a rare Ability. You really caught me off guard. But I'm not giving up yet!"

"Seel, use Aqua Jet!"

At the exact same moment Daisy gave her command, Ethan Vale also spoke.

"Ralts, use Magical Leaf!"

Countless glowing leaves suddenly materialized before Ralts. Spinning and compressing together, they shot rapidly toward Seel and collided directly with the incoming Aqua Jet, triggering a violent explosion.

However, once the mist cleared, it became obvious that Magical Leaf had not only neutralized Aqua Jet but had even injured Seel in the process!

After taking the hit, Seel was forced to fall onto the frozen platform.

Grass beats Water—super effective!

Daisy's expression immediately tightened.

This opponent was simply too difficult to handle!

Still, no matter how difficult things became, the battle had to continue.

"Ralts, use Confusion!"

"Seel, hurry back into the water!"

Both trainers gave commands simultaneously. However, after learning from the previous exchange, Ralts acted first this time. Focusing its mind completely, it enveloped Seel in psychic energy before it could move, lifting it directly into the air.

Under the pressure of Confusion, Seel cried out painfully. No matter how hard it struggled, it could not escape the psychic restraints.

Once Seel had been lifted high enough, Ralts swung its arm downward, hurling Seel violently toward the frozen platform below.

Boom!

After the tremendous crash, Seel didn't even have time to cry out before following Goldeen and Krabby into defeat, completely losing the ability to battle.

"We've lost…"

Recalling the unconscious Seel into its Poké Ball, Daisy exchanged glances with her sisters before slowly walking toward Ethan Vale.

However, before Ethan Vale could recall Ralts, its body was suddenly enveloped in a bright light. Several seconds later, Ralts disappeared—and in its place stood a brand-new form!

Kirlia!

Seeing this, Violet immediately covered her mouth in surprise.

"Huh? It evolved!?"

Kirlia resembled a graceful dancer dressed in white, almost as though it were wearing a ballet skirt. Its legs were slender, and unlike Ralts, its hair no longer completely covered its eyes. Instead, soft bangs rested near its shoulders, revealing its crimson eyes. Even the two red horns on its head had shifted positions, now resembling elegant hair clips.

How should one describe it?

It was as though a little girl had grown into a beautiful young maiden.

The Pokédex, still in Ethan Vale's hand, responded at the perfect moment.

"Beep. Kirlia, the Emotion Pokémon. A Psychic- and Fairy-type Pokémon. When amplifying its psychic power, the horns on its head distort the surrounding space into illusions. It possesses the ability to sensitively detect its trainer's emotions. When happy, it begins dancing, and its trainer's cheerful emotions become the source of its psychic power."

Kirlia clearly wasn't fully accustomed to its new body yet. After spinning around several times awkwardly, it finally returned to Ethan Vale's side.

"Kirlia, your new appearance is beautiful."

Kirlia looked slightly shy beneath Ethan Vale's praise. Only after he gently patted its head did it affectionately rub its cheek against his hand.

At that moment, Daisy walked over holding a blue badge.

The badge resembled a light-blue water droplet, sparkling like a gemstone beneath the sunlight.

"Congratulations, Trainer. This is your reward for defeating us—and the badge you rightfully earned."

After exchanging a few polite words, Ethan Vale accepted the Cascade Badge and placed it into his badge case.

Looking at the single lonely badge inside the case, Violet pouted and sighed dramatically.

"What the heck… We actually became the very first Gym Leaders to lose to you. That really stings."

Ethan Vale merely smiled without responding.

Compared to protecting the Gym, the three sisters clearly devoted most of their attention toward water ballet performances. Their Pokémon training had long been neglected, making them among the weakest Gym Leaders overall.

Still, everyone had their own ambitions. Hopefully Misty would take over the Gym soon.

"Well then, I'll be taking my leave."

Waving goodbye, Ethan Vale departed alongside his Pokémon.

Only after he had fully left the Gym and disappeared from sight did an orange-haired girl with a side ponytail enter the battlefield.

"Seriously, I've been looking everywhere for you three! So this is where you were. Huh? Why do all of you look so depressed?"

Hearing the girl's question, Violet dramatically cried out:

"Misty, we were deeply wounded by a man just now!"

"Hurt!?" Misty froze in confusion. But after Daisy explained what had happened, she immediately puffed up in annoyance.

"And here I thought someone dumped you or something! Turns out you just failed to defend the Gym!"

"Hahaha… well, it's basically the same thing!"

Even Daisy seemed to seriously consider it.

"Now that I think about it, that man from earlier looked very gentle, and he was really strong too. He might actually make quite a good boyfriend."

"I seriously can't deal with you three." Misty puffed out her cheeks dismissively. "If I had been here earlier, there's no way I would've let him take our Cascade Badge! And you actually got swept three-to-zero? That's seriously embarrassing!"

Hearing that, Lily wagged a finger.

"Don't talk so confidently. That man with the Kirlia was genuinely strong. Even with your abilities, you definitely wouldn't have beaten him."

"Don't underestimate me!" Misty exploded like an angry little hedgehog. "Hmph! I've already decided! The day after tomorrow, I'm leaving on a journey. Until I become a truly great trainer, I'm not coming back!"
