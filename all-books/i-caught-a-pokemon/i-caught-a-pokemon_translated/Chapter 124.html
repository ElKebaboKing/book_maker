Figures darted across the battlefield, attacking and counterattacking in rapid succession.

On the simple battle field beside Cerise Park, one tall Pokémon and one small Pokémon were locked in an intense clash.

From their condition alone, it was obvious that this Pokémon battle had reached its final stage.

"Pikachu, use Thunderbolt!"

At Ash's command, Pikachu's body erupted with dazzling bolts of electricity. More than a dozen brilliant arcs shot outward, spreading across the battlefield in unpredictable paths with loud crackling sounds.

Facing the fierce Electric-type attack, Gardevoir used Teleport, instantly appearing at the edge of the battlefield.

The moment it dodged the attack, its eyes glowed blue.

Using Psychic, it struck Pikachu with overwhelming force, slamming it heavily into the ground.

Thud!

Pikachu struggled to get back up, but after only a few attempts, it finally collapsed from exhaustion.

Serving as the referee, Brock raised his right hand.

"Pikachu is unable to battle. The winner is Gardevoir!"

"Wow! Gardevoir, you're amazing!"

As the battle ended, Misty couldn't help cheering loudly.

Chloe also applauded.

No matter how intense their silent rivalry had been just moments ago, both girls were genuinely happy for Ethan's victory.

However, Misty's enthusiastic cheering made Ash feel rather awkward.

"Come on, Misty."

"We've been traveling together for so long."

"Even if you don't cheer for me, do you really have to look that happy?"

Misty simply stuck out her tongue.

"Bleh!"

"I like cheering for Ethan."

"What are you going to do about it?"

Ignoring their bickering, Ethan walked over and gently patted Gardevoir on the shoulder.

From the very beginning, Pikachu had been in excellent form.

Its agile footwork had caused Gardevoir plenty of trouble during the early exchanges.

However, Psychic-type Pokémon naturally held a strong advantage, and Gardevoir was performing at its best today.

After several rounds of fierce combat, Gardevoir ultimately claimed the victory.

No longer paying attention to Misty, Ash picked up Pikachu and turned to Ethan.

"Ethan, your Gardevoir is incredible."

"I lost this battle."

Although he admitted defeat, there wasn't the slightest hint of frustration or discouragement on his face.

Deep down, Ash had always known Ethan was much stronger than he was.

In fact, even before the battle began, he had already prepared himself for the possibility of losing.

"Your Pikachu is outstanding too," Ethan said encouragingly.

"At least among every Pikachu I've ever seen, none are stronger than yours."

"And honestly, I only won by a narrow margin."

"If we battle again next time..."

"I might be the one who loses."

After the exhilarating battle, Ethan brought everyone to the same dessert shop he had previously visited with Chloe.

They ordered several of the shop's signature sundaes.

As they enjoyed the delicious ice cream, they chatted casually until the sun dipped below the horizon and the evening sky was painted gold.

Only then did Ash and the others finally stand up to say goodbye.

Perhaps because they had already experienced so many meetings and farewells, Misty had grown used to them.

Still...

Before leaving, she glanced at Chloe with a complicated expression and quietly sighed.

Only then did she wave at Ethan.

"Well then, Ethan..."

"We're heading off."

Watching them, Ethan asked,

"So what's your plan from here?"

Ash had clearly already made up his mind.

"Tonight we'll stay at the Pokémon Center."

"Tomorrow morning we'll head back to Pallet Town."

"Hehe..."

"After battling you just now, I realized how much I still need to improve."

"Once I get home, I'm going to train my Pokémon even harder."

Hearing that, Ethan extended his right hand.

"Then let's both give it our best."

"There isn't much time left before the Indigo Conference."

"Let's work hard together until then."

Ash firmly shook Ethan's hand and nodded.

"No problem!"

"Let's meet again at the Indigo Conference!"

"Hehe!"

"Next time, Pikachu definitely won't lose!"

Watching them disappear into the distance, Ethan and Chloe returned to Professor Cerise's Laboratory.

Just as Ethan had said, the Indigo Conference was drawing ever closer.

There was no time to waste.

Fortunately, his training plan had already been prepared in advance.

Over the coming days, he intended to stay almost entirely inside Cerise Park, focusing solely on training his Pokémon.

Unless something truly important happened, he had no intention of leaving.

Before that, however...

Ethan carefully organized every photograph he had taken throughout his journey and added written notes beside each one.

Some memories gradually faded with time.

Eventually, they disappeared altogether.

Only photographs and written records could preserve those beautiful moments forever.

He believed that one day, when he grew old...

Looking back through those albums...

Every page would become another treasured memory of the incredible journey he had lived.

The Indigo Conference earned its name because it was held at the Indigo Stadium on the Indigo Plateau.

As the official Pokémon League tournament and the largest sporting event in the Kanto region, it became the center of everyone's attention each year.

The entire region focused on this single grand competition.

Even sporting events, esports tournaments, concerts, and other major entertainment programs deliberately adjusted their schedules to avoid conflicting with it.

Its overwhelming popularity made it the hottest topic everywhere—

Schools.

Companies.

Street corners.

Restaurants.

Gatherings among friends.

Even full-time homemakers would chat with their neighbors about the latest Indigo Conference news while shopping for groceries.

Through the relentless coverage of reporters, along with television broadcasts and the internet, news of the tournament spread far beyond Kanto, attracting widespread attention across other regions as well.

Unlike Pokémon Leagues in other regions, however, the Indigo Conference allowed competitors to qualify through multiple paths.

Besides collecting all eight Gym Badges, Trainers could also qualify by graduating from an accredited Pokémon Academy or passing the official Pokémon League certification examination.

Time flowed onward like a great river.

Quietly.

Relentlessly.

Before anyone realized it...

Only a few days remained before the opening ceremony.

At last, Ethan emerged from his period of intensive training.

After countless days of exhausting practice and constant adjustments, both he and his Pokémon had completed every preparation they possibly could.

He wasn't overflowing with confidence.

But he knew one thing for certain—

They had given everything they had.

No matter what results awaited them at the Indigo Conference...

They would have no regrets.

After cleaning himself up and washing away the dust of training, Ethan packed his belongings once again.

He then went to bid Professor Cerise and everyone at the laboratory a sincere farewell.

He had stayed in Kanto for a long time.

He had also imposed upon the Cerise family for many months.

Once the Indigo Conference was over and his goal had been fulfilled...

It would be time to leave Kanto...

And begin a new journey in another region.
