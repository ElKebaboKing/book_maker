With a flash of red light, a Pokémon with short limbs, tightly shut eyes like Brock's, and a reddish-brown flower blooming on its head appeared on the battlefield.

Its appearance resembled a giant tropical rafflesia flower. The flower's pistil was surrounded by four spherical petals, while beneath the massive bloom was a blue, bulb-like body.

Pokédex: "Gloom, the Weed Pokémon. A Grass and Poison-type. The evolved form of Oddish. It constantly drools a sweet-smelling saliva that carries an extremely foul odor. However, when it is relaxed, it will not release its stench. Only when nervous or sensing danger does it emit the odor to drive away enemies."

A strong smell spread from around Gloom. Even standing in the trainer's area, Ethan could clearly smell it from a distance.

Looks like it has the Stench Ability too, just like that Skuntank from yesterday…

Looking at Charmeleon, perhaps because it had already experienced battling against such opponents before, although it was clearly nauseated by the smell, it still stood its ground stubbornly, refusing to take even a single step back.

Knowing the longer the battle dragged on, the worse the situation would become, Ethan decided to strike first.

"Charmeleon, let's attack first!"

Charmeleon opened its mouth and unleashed a stream of fire far larger, hotter, and more powerful than Ember!

"So it has already learned Flamethrower?" Erika waved her hand and called out, "Gloom, dodge quickly!"

Unfortunately, without Chlorophyll boosting its speed, Gloom was far too slow. No matter how much it tried to evade, it was ultimately struck by the blazing flames.

Its body caught fire, and once the flames faded, Erika immediately commanded,

"Gloom, use Sleep Powder!"

Whenever an opponent opened with Sleep Powder, Ethan knew it was usually followed by a devastating attack, so he did not dare lower his guard.

"Charmeleon, use Smokescreen again!"

The previous Smokescreen from the earlier battle had already dissipated, so Charmeleon quickly spewed out black smoke once more, surrounding itself completely.

At the same time, clusters of powder drifted out from Gloom's flower petals, gradually spreading across the battlefield before seeping into the smoke.

Before the smoke fully thickened, Erika could vaguely make out Charmeleon's silhouette standing motionless inside it, apparently having fallen asleep.

"Excellent! Gloom, use Growth!"

A faint green glow enveloped Gloom's body. Not only did its size increase slightly, but under the bright sunlight, the power of its attacks also rose dramatically.

In the trainer's area, Ethan understood clearly that once Charmeleon fell asleep, without being struck by an outside force, it would be almost impossible for it to wake up quickly on its own. Therefore, he chose not to issue any commands and simply watched quietly, waiting for what would come next.

He had already done all he could.

Now it was time to see his opponent's play.

After holding back for so long, Erika finally revealed her trump card.

"Gloom, use Solar Beam!"

At Erika's command, Gloom began absorbing sunlight. Countless particles of light rapidly gathered and condensed at the center of its flower, eventually forming a glowing sphere!

And the moment the energy reached its peak, the beam fired straight toward Charmeleon!

A brilliant ray shot forward, instantly tearing through the layers of smoke before striking Charmeleon directly!

Solar Beam — a powerful advanced Grass-type move capable of dealing massive damage. Under normal circumstances, it required time to charge by gathering sunlight first. But under today's exceptionally bright conditions, its charging time had been drastically shortened!

Charmeleon took the full hit and stumbled before crashing to the ground.

Although Grass-type attacks only dealt half damage against Fire-types, with the boost from Growth, this single strike nearly knocked Charmeleon out completely!

Charmeleon grimaced in pain as it struggled to stand back up.

Both its stamina and endurance had reached their absolute limits, but fortunately, the impact of Solar Beam had also broken its sleep status!

It roared loudly, triggering its Blaze Ability. Without waiting for Ethan's command, it charged straight toward Gloom entirely on instinct!

"What incredible tenacity!" Erika praised instinctively before shaking her head. "But with so little strength left, what can you possibly do now? Gloom, use Poison Powder!"

Gloom shook its flower, releasing clouds of sickly green powder that instantly formed a barrier in front of it.

Erika's intention had been to force Charmeleon to change direction. Yet to her surprise, Charmeleon completely ignored the poison. It rushed directly into the cloud, then suddenly leapt high into the air before the poison could fully take effect.

Flying forward, flames ignited between its teeth as it bit down fiercely onto Gloom's body!

In that split second, Gloom let out a painful scream!

Fire Fang activated!

It was super effective!

"What!?"

Erika was shocked. Just as she was about to command Gloom to break free, Charmeleon's body suddenly swayed.

The Poison Powder had drained the last of its remaining stamina, causing it to collapse unconscious onto the ground.

Erika let out a breath of relief. But just as the blue-haired referee was about to announce the result, something unexpected happened!

Gloom's body suddenly glowed red. It trembled violently as though suffering from severe burns.

"Oh no, it's the burn effect!"

Erika cried out instinctively. Yet the moment the words left her mouth, Gloom collapsed as well, losing its ability to battle.

"This…"

Erika and the blue-haired referee exchanged stunned looks. Several seconds passed before the referee finally recovered and raised her hand high.

"Both Pokémon are unable to battle! The challenger wins!"

Taking out a Poké Ball, Ethan quietly recalled Charmeleon.

This Gym battle had truly pushed it to its limits.

And the battle just now had also shown Ethan the immense potential Charmeleon possessed. If properly trained in the future, it would undoubtedly become one of his strongest partners.

"Congratulations. You defeated me and earned the Rainbow Badge." Erika walked over to Ethan and applauded gently. "I have to say, your Charmeleon was truly impressive. Especially its unyielding spirit at the end—it really opened my eyes."

"I was surprised by its performance too." Ethan shook his head with a smile. "I'll continue training it so it can become even stronger."

Erika said nothing further. She took out a badge from her kimono and handed it to Ethan.

Great. The fifth Badge was finally his!

Looking closely, the badge resembled a blooming flower, with petals colored after the rainbow itself, making it uniquely beautiful.

After placing it carefully into his Badge case, Ethan thanked her.

"Thank you for the battle, Miss Erika. I hope we'll meet again someday."

(End of Chapter)
