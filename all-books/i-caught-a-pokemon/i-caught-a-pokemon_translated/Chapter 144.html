"First came Psybeam, followed by Draining Kiss, and then finished with Magical Leaf—what an incredible combo! Mr. Mime is down! Faced with Gardevoir's relentless offensive, it simply couldn't hold on any longer!"

The announcer spoke rapidly, clearly delivering every detail of the battle to the audience.

After more than a dozen intense exchanges, Gardevoir had ultimately proven to be the stronger Pokémon, securing the victory.

Looking at the defeated Mr. Mime lying before it, Gardevoir merely took a few deep breaths. Aside from a handful of minor injuries, it was still in excellent condition.

The spectators erupted into applause, amazed by the spectacular battle.

To Serena Yuto, however, the cheers sounded like blows to the heart.

Her face turned pale, and even her fingers trembled slightly.

This is bad...

She had never imagined that even the Pokémon she trusted the most—her strongest partner, Mr. Mime—would lose.

Ever since receiving her very first Pokémon and enrolling at the Pokémon Research School, everything had gone smoothly for her.

Never had she been pushed into such a desperate situation.

For a moment, her mind went completely blank.

Only after the referee reminded her did she snap back to reality and return the unconscious Mr. Mime to its Poké Ball.

What do I do now?

After a long hesitation, Serena Yuto finally released her third Pokémon.

A flash of blue light appeared, and a Scyther landed before Ethan.

Seeing that it wasn't another rare Pokémon, the audience looked somewhat disappointed, and even the announcer couldn't hide the drop in excitement.

"Ah... it's a Scyther! Well, while it may not be as eye-catching as that Shiny Mr. Mime, Serena Yuto must have great confidence in its strength if she's chosen it as her final Pokémon!"

On the opposite side, after briefly evaluating Scyther, Ethan followed the strategy he had planned beforehand.

He recalled Gardevoir and sent out Jolteon.

The moment the battle began, Ethan immediately noticed that although Scyther was quite capable, it was still noticeably weaker than Mr. Mime.

So that's it... She's already run out of trump cards.

Lightning crackled through the battlefield while razor-sharp blades flashed through the air.

As the fight progressed, Jolteon's Electric-type attacks proved devastating against the Flying-type Scyther.

After being struck several times, Scyther's stamina quickly reached its limit.

At that point, the outcome of the battle was no longer in doubt.

Although Serena Yuto continued directing Scyther to fight until the very end, the gap in strength combined with the unfavorable type matchup soon led to its defeat.

Scyther convulsed a few times before collapsing unconscious after being overwhelmed by Jolteon's electricity.

When the referee officially announced the result, even the announcer couldn't hide his amazement.

"My goodness! Another clean 3–0 victory! Even against tournament favorite Serena Yuto, Ethan has once again displayed overwhelming dominance! This is absolutely unbelievable! At this point, I honestly don't know who can stop his incredible winning streak!"

I... I actually lost...

Standing in the middle of the battlefield, the proud young lady from a prestigious family felt her eyes redden as tears slowly welled up.

Before the match, she had confidently declared that Ethan would be the one left in tears.

Instead...

She was the one crying afterward.

Students from the Pokémon Research School undoubtedly possessed superior theoretical knowledge.

However, unlike trainers who had fought their way through Gym after Gym, they lacked real battlefield experience.

Only after stepping onto the League stage did they realize that classroom knowledge alone simply wasn't enough for battles of this intensity.

Seeing her standing there with tears in her eyes, Ethan wasn't sure whether he should comfort someone he had only just met.

After thinking for a moment, he spoke carefully.

"Serena Yuto... I understand how you're feeling."

"But if you truly want to become stronger, staying comfortably at school as an honor student simply isn't enough."

When she looked up at him, Ethan continued,

"Go out and travel."

"Broaden your horizons."

"Meet stronger trainers."

"Experience different ways of thinking."

"Only by constantly battling powerful opponents and learning from the world will you continue to improve and become far stronger than you are today."

"Thank you... for your advice."

Serena Yuto took a deep breath and nodded.

"I lost this battle fair and square."

"But I won't give up so easily."

"Just as you said, I'll leave school and begin my own journey."

"And when I've grown stronger... I'll come back and challenge you again!"

"No problem."

"I'll accept your challenge anytime."

Ethan paid little attention to her declaration.

After all, she wasn't the first person who had vowed revenge against him.

Little John...

Aya...

There were already plenty of people chasing after him.

In a way, having rivals constantly pursuing him also motivated Ethan to keep improving under pressure.

With that thought, he stopped paying attention to Serena Yuto.

He waved to the cheering audience and television cameras, recalled Jolteon, and calmly walked toward the players' tunnel.

By defeating Serena Yuto, he had officially advanced to the Top 32.

For Ethan, every step forward was making history, and that alone was worth celebrating.

The crowd continued cheering.

Meanwhile, inside the Indigo Stadium's broadcast control center...

John, the director of the live broadcast, kept his eyes fixed on the real-time ratings.

Although viewership had declined slightly after the previous peak, countless new viewers had stayed to watch the entire match on television and online.

Watching Ethan secure yet another overwhelming victory, John thought for a moment before making a decision.

"Hey!"

He turned toward one of his staff members.

"Starting today, raise this trainer's priority."

"As much as possible, arrange for him to face opponents with high popularity."

"Whenever possible, schedule all of his matches during prime-time broadcasts."

"Increase promotional coverage."

"And assign more cameras to whichever field he's battling on."

"Understood?"

The staff member froze.

"Uh... but both the battlefields and opponents are supposed to be determined randomly by the tournament computers."

"Unless we manually manipulate the system from the control room, we can't guarantee those results."

"Idiot!"

John shot him an annoyed glare.

"Of course I know that!"

"So stop standing there and make the necessary adjustments!"

"Do I have to repeat myself?"

The staff member hurriedly began operating the computers.

Standing quietly behind them, League President Charles Goodshow watched everything unfold.

For once...

He said nothing.

The Pokémon League was an enormous organization, with every department fulfilling its own role.

But everyone ultimately shared the same goal—

To host the greatest tournament possible.

Goodshow wasn't an inflexible man.

He understood better than anyone that, no matter where or when, behind-the-scenes manipulation would always exist.

Having an outstanding competitor like Ethan would only improve both the quality of the tournament and its television ratings.

From the League's perspective, the benefits far outweighed the drawbacks.

Because of that...

He was perfectly willing to let this little bit of backstage manipulation continue.

After all, if it benefited the future of the Pokémon League...

Why should he interfere?

( End of Chapter )
