Rolling her eyes at Ash, Misty said,

"Even though he won the battle just now, he traded his Butterfree for the other trainer's Raticate."

"A trade?" Ethan was briefly stunned. After recalling this part of the storyline, he could not help shaking his head. "Isn't Butterfree your partner? Why would you trade it away? Besides, you're not trying to complete a Pokédex, and Raticate isn't some rare Pokémon. There's really no reason to trade for it."

"But…" Ash looked extremely conflicted, unable to hide the disappointment in his voice. "Brock agreed to the trade too."

"That's because he saw the other guy's female companion and completely lost focus," Misty said helplessly. "There's no way he was actually listening to what you were saying."

Since things had already reached this point, Ethan did not try persuading Ash to immediately trade Butterfree back.

Everyone grows gradually, and this was something Ash needed to experience himself. Besides, he had agreed to the trade willingly. If he regretted it later, he would have to come to that realization on his own.

"By the way," Misty asked after taking another bite of cake, "where did you go just now?"

"I was just wandering around nearby." Ethan glanced at the several pieces of cake in her hands and smiled. "Although you're still growing, you probably shouldn't eat too many sweets. It's very easy to gain weight, you know."

Hearing that, Misty glanced at Ethan. For some reason, she seemed to think of something. Although reluctant, she slowly put down the cake in her hand.

The ship's horn sounded once again.

At this point, the S.S. Anne had already been sailing for a long time. Outside the windows, there was no longer any sign of land or other ships—only endless blue ocean in every direction.

Sitting beside Misty, Ethan had only taken a few bites of food when suddenly, with a loud bang, all the doors to the hall slammed shut. At the same time, a group of sinister-looking people appeared out of nowhere.

These people wore black hats and black uniforms marked with the letter "R." Their faces were partially covered, and each of them carried a strange machine equipped with several suction tubes as they surrounded the passengers.

Just as everyone stood there confused and at a loss, the lights in the hall suddenly dimmed.

Then, a man and a woman dressed in white uniforms leapt onto a platform made of stacked tables. Amid wild laughter, a spotlight shone down on them.

"To protect the world from devastation!"

"To unite all peoples within our nation!"

"To denounce the evils of truth and love!

"To extend our reach to the stars

"The cute and charming villains!"

"I'm Jessie!"

"And I'm James!"

"We are Team Rocket, blasting through the galaxy!"

"A white hole! A white future is waiting for us!"

"That's right, meow~" A standing Meowth finally leapt between the two of them.

The iconic Team Rocket trio that appeared throughout the entire Pokémon anime had finally made their grand entrance!

Jessie, with her long, thick magenta hair, placed her hands on her hips and shouted loudly to the passengers, "Today, every single Pokémon here will belong to Team Rocket!"

"That's right!" James crossed his arms and shouted just as loudly. "And if anyone dares to resist, don't blame us for not showing mercy!"

Before the passengers could even react, the Team Rocket grunts dressed in black activated the machines on their backs. Instantly, powerful currents of air swirled through the hall. Using the machines' overwhelming suction force, more than half of the Poké Balls held in people's hands or attached to their belts were sucked directly into the devices.

The crowd immediately erupted into chaos. Meanwhile, because Ethan, Ash, and Misty were standing farther away, they had not yet been affected by the suction.

So Team Rocket really was attacking the passengers, just like in the anime...

After the brief moment of panic, the passengers naturally refused to surrender without a fight. One after another, they released their Pokémon, and under their trainers' commands, the Pokémon rose up to resist, clashing fiercely against Team Rocket's Pokémon.

Most of the Pokémon used by the Team Rocket grunts were Poison-types, with Ekans, Koffing, and Zubat being the most common among them.

As for the passengers' Pokémon, their varieties were much more diverse. However, despite the wide range of species, most were still unevolved Pokémon such as Rattata, Pidgey, and Bellsprout.

Ash, Misty, and Brock also joined the battle at this time.

A fierce fight erupted throughout the grand hall. Explosions and bursts of fire quickly turned the once-luxurious interior into a complete mess.

But overall, unless Team Rocket had some other trick prepared, even without Ash and the others stepping in, their defeat was only a matter of time.

Could a plan they had prepared for so long really be this simple?

Ethan calmly observed everything. Suddenly, he noticed that on the second-floor balcony corridor, the man codenamed Handsome was battling another man dressed in a Team Rocket uniform. The man had a sinister appearance and a purple mohawk hairstyle.

A thought flashed through Ethan's mind. Without even having time to notify Misty and the others, he immediately ran toward the staircase and rushed up to the second-floor corridor.

Handsome's two Pokémon were Croagunk, a standing frog Pokémon with an annoyingly smug expression, and Growlithe, which had tiger-like stripes and a mane resembling that of a lion.

As for the sinister man, his Pokémon were a Zubat with no visible eyes and a huge gaping mouth, along with a Weezing constantly emitting poisonous gas.

The four Pokémon were locked in an intense battle. The moment Handsome saw Ethan arrive, joy instantly appeared on his face, while the sinister man's expression became even uglier.

"Ethan!" Handsome shouted before pointing at the man and saying loudly, "That guy is Lambda, one of Team Rocket's Four Executives and the field commander of this hijacking! If we capture him, we can truly stop this entire incident!"

"Damn you! Stop interfering!" Lambda roared angrily. "Whether you're International Police or some random trainers, anyone who dares stand in the way of Team Rocket's plans will pay the price!"

So he was the true commander behind this operation. No wonder the Team Rocket grunts downstairs had become as disorganized as scattered sand after being tied down here.

"You should give up already," Ethan said coldly. "Take a look downstairs. Your plan has completely fallen apart. Your subordinates are already fleeing in every direction. Defeat is inevitable! Kirlia, use Psychic!"

The next second, Lambda's Zubat looked as though an invisible hand had seized it by the throat before violently slamming it into the ground.

With Ethan joining the fight, the evenly matched battle instantly tilted in their favor.

Seeing this, no matter how unwilling he was to accept it, Lambda could only swallow the bitter taste of defeat.

"Don't celebrate too early—we'll be back!" After glaring fiercely at Handsome and Ethan, Lambda shouted, "Weezing, use Smokescreen!"

A massive cloud of black smoke burst from Weezing's body, instantly engulfing Lambda.

"This is bad!" Handsome cried out in alarm. But when he covered his nose and rushed into the smoke, Lambda and his Pokémon had already vanished without a trace.

(End of Chapter)
