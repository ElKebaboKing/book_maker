"You really have the nerve to say that!" The young man in the yellow pants was stunned for a moment before looking at Ethan's Jolteon with a look of understanding. "So you like Jolteon too? I never thought I'd finally meet someone who shares my taste! Ah, right—I forgot to introduce myself. My name is Rainer, and I'm the owner of this estate. These two are my younger brothers, Mikey and Pyro."

The blue-haired, blue-clad Mikey and the red-haired, red-clad Pyro each extended a hand.

"Nice to meet you," Ethan said as he shook their hands one by one. "My name is Ethan Vale. It's a pleasure to meet all of you."

A closer look revealed that the three brothers not only wore pants matching the colors of their Eeveelutions, but even their hairstyles and hair colors closely resembled them.

They really do love Eevee with every fiber of their being.

"So you're saying your Jolteon is the strongest?" Pyro, whose fiery personality matched his appearance, challenged eagerly. "That's a pretty bold claim. In that case, why don't we settle it with a battle?"

Before Ethan could respond, another brother stepped forward.

"I'm the older brother, so I should go first," Mikey declared before turning to Ethan. "Even though my Vaporeon is at a type disadvantage, I won't lose!"

"I don't mind," Ethan replied as he glanced around. Many of the visiting Trainers in the spacious courtyard had already turned their attention toward them. "But are we battling right now? I don't see a battlefield here."

"Of course not here." Rainer hurriedly stepped between them. "There's a multi-terrain battlefield behind the estate that's perfect for battles. But there's no rush. Let's let our guest come inside and rest first."

"You're right," Mikey agreed. "Keeping a guest standing at the front gate definitely isn't proper hospitality."

Following the three brothers, Ethan entered the manor.

Inside, the magnificent hall glittered with luxury.

Dozens of Trainers stood around a long buffet table, enjoying delicious food while chatting with one another in a warm and relaxed atmosphere.

Many of them had their Pokémon by their sides. Some behaved obediently, while others ran around playfully, happily interacting with each other throughout the hall.

Once inside, Rainer turned to Ethan.

"We still have a few things to take care of. We'll arrange a room for you shortly. Please relax for a while in the meantime."

Watching them leave, Ethan helped himself to some food.

Just as he was about to look around, he noticed Rainer and his brothers walking through the courtyard carrying Evolution Stones.

Whenever they met a Trainer hoping to evolve a Pokémon, they generously used the appropriate stone to help complete the evolution.

Seeing how freely they gave away such valuable items, Ethan could only admire them.

Noticing Ethan watching them so intently, a nearby Trainer sighed with admiration.

"Rainer, Mikey, and Pyro are truly wonderful people. Thanks to their selfless generosity, countless Trainers come here every week hoping to evolve their Pokémon. Even my Poliwrath only evolved because Mikey gave me a Water Stone."

Ethan nodded.

"They really are remarkable."

Honestly, if he were in their shoes, he knew he could never be that generous.

Before long, the three brothers returned.

This time, however, they were accompanied by a young boy.

He looked to be about five or six years old and held an Eevee in his arms.

Unlike his older brothers, his clothes were perfectly ordinary, making him seem completely out of place beside them.

Rainer introduced him.

"This is our youngest brother, Terry, along with his Eevee. When he heard we were having a battle, he insisted on coming to watch."

"I did not!" Terry protested, his face turning red. "You were the ones who said I should watch an Eevee battle before deciding which evolution I want!"

Terry...

That name sounds awfully familiar.

Seeing Ethan hesitate, Rainer asked curiously,

"What's wrong?"

"Nothing."

Ethan smiled.

"I just happened to know another boy named Terry when I was younger. His surname was Yagami, and he also traveled with a reliable partner through countless hardships and battles."

"Really?" Terry asked hopefully. "Could you introduce me to him?"

"I'm afraid that's impossible."

Ethan shook his head.

"I only knew him when I was a child. After we grew up, I never saw him again."

"I see..."

Terry sighed, unable to hide his disappointment.

"What a shame."

As they talked, they arrived at the backyard.

Sure enough, a multi-terrain battlefield lay within the garden.

It combined a pond, rocky terrain, and grassy fields into a single battlefield. Compared to ordinary arenas, it offered far more variables and allowed Pokémon to fully demonstrate their abilities.

Hearing that a battle was about to begin, the surrounding Trainers eagerly gathered around.

Because the three brothers were so well-liked, nearly everyone cheered for them.

Rainer stepped into the center of the field to act as referee.

"This battle has one special rule—only Eeveelutions may be used. Are both sides ready?"

Ethan took his place in the Trainer's box.

With a wave of his hand, Jolteon eagerly sprinted onto the battlefield.

"So," Ethan asked, "which one of you is going first?"

Mikey and Pyro exchanged glances.

"As the older brother," Mikey said matter-of-factly, "I'll go first."

Although clearly dissatisfied, Pyro reluctantly stepped aside.

Vaporeon leaped gracefully onto the battlefield, facing Jolteon.

Though both had evolved from Eevee, their appearances were completely different.

Vaporeon's sleek body was a beautiful aqua blue. Its ears resembled fish fins, a white frill encircled its neck, and its split tail swayed gently behind it like the tail of a graceful mermaid.

Once both Pokémon were ready, Rainer raised his hand.

"Begin!"

"Vaporeon, use Water Gun!"

A powerful stream of water shot from Vaporeon's mouth.

At the same time, Ethan shouted,

"Jolteon, use Thunder Shock!"

Crackling electricity struck the Water Gun.

Since electricity wasn't a physical attack, it couldn't block the stream of water. Instead, it traveled directly through the water toward Vaporeon.

Water Gun and Thunder Shock struck their targets almost simultaneously.

However, thanks to the type advantage, Vaporeon suffered far greater damage.

"Jolteon, use Quick Attack!"

"Vaporeon, use Quick Attack too!"

The same command echoed across the battlefield as both Pokémon charged toward each other.

Despite using the same move, Jolteon was noticeably faster.

Before Vaporeon had even crossed half the field, Jolteon seized the perfect opportunity and slammed into it with tremendous force.

Splash!

Vaporeon was knocked flying and crashed straight into the pond.

Then, to everyone's surprise, it silently sank beneath the surface and disappeared completely.
