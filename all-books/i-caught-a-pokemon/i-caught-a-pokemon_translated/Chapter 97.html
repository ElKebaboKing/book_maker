Following Ethan Vale's gaze, Masha and the others beside him finally got a clear look at the four Rocket Executives.

"Who are you people!?" Masha shouted. "How dare you attack the Fuchsia Biker Gang!? Are you tired of living!?"

Lance glanced at him dismissively.

"The Fuchsia Biker Gang? What kind of joke organization is that? Team Rocket is conducting business here. All irrelevant bystanders should leave immediately!"

"Team Rocket!?"

The moment he heard the name, Masha's confidence instantly shrank.

Turning toward Misa, he whispered,

"Hey, Sis, they said they're from Team Rocket. Does that mean they belong to the same organization as Big Sis Chain Musashi and Big Brother Little-Wheels Kojiro?"

Misa nodded.

"Probably."

"So what do we do?" Masha asked.

"Obviously, we hide."

Misa discreetly gestured toward Ethan.

"They're a criminal organization! Didn't you hear them? They're here to cause trouble for that guy. We shouldn't get involved. Let's get out of here while we still can!"

"But..." Masha hesitated. "This is our territory. And they destroyed our bicycle. That debt—"

"Idiot!"

Misa smacked him hard on the forehead and dragged him away.

"Who cares about that right now!? Can't you see how dangerous they look!? Let's go!"

Ethan paid no attention to the siblings' argument.

Instead, he calmly observed the four executives.

"It's rare to see all four Rocket Executives gathered in one place. What is this, a sightseeing tour?"

"You managed to survive that attack," Apollo said coldly. "Looks like your luck is pretty good."

The four executives jumped down from the bridge supports and landed smoothly on the bridge.

"We came here today to avenge the destruction of our base."

"Avenge yourselves on me?"

Ethan spread his hands.

"If you're looking for revenge, shouldn't you be targeting the International Police or the Elite Four? They were the ones who organized that operation. I was just a minor participant."

Operation.

Elite Four.

International Police.

Destruction of a base.

The casually spoken words left Masha and the others utterly stunned.

They exchanged glances.

This was on a completely different level from their petty biker gang antics.

Just who exactly was this man?

Why would four Team Rocket Executives personally come seeking revenge against him?

The flames of curiosity burned brightly within them.

Instead of leaving, they quietly hid farther back, eager to hear even more shocking revelations.

Athena crossed her arms and sneered.

"We'll deal with them too. In fact, we already have. You're simply one of many targets."

"Oh?"

Ethan frowned.

"So nobody is going to escape your reach?"

"Exactly!"

Athena laughed maniacally.

"But you're special. You've interfered with our plans time and time again. Even Boss Giovanni personally ordered that you be eliminated before you can fully grow."

At that moment, Ethan understood.

A battle was unavoidable.

If he wanted to escape, the ocean was behind him. He could summon Zapdos and flee across the sea.

But these people had clearly come prepared.

They would pursue him relentlessly.

And if Giovanni himself had issued the order, they would never stop unless they were defeated badly enough to make them reconsider.

Then I'll defeat them here.

His eyes sharpened.

Slowly, his hand moved toward his belt.

Apollo smirked.

"Not planning to run?"

"Run?"

Ethan shook his head.

"Where would I run? You'd just keep chasing me anyway."

"Haha! At least you're not stupid."

Lambda chuckled.

"In that case, I can finally repay the 'kindness' you showed me aboard the S.S. Anne!"

With that, the executives released their Pokémon.

The next moment, Magmar, Houndoom, Arbok, Murkrow, Golbat, and Weezing appeared simultaneously, completely blocking the path ahead.

Just as Ethan reached for a Poké Ball, he remembered something.

His eyes briefly flicked toward Masha and the others, who were still secretly watching from a distance.

At this point... it doesn't matter anymore.

He swung his arm and released his own Pokémon.

Gardevoir, Gyarados, Jolteon, Charmeleon, Wartortle, and Ivysaur appeared and immediately faced the enemy forces.

Apollo slowly scanned Ethan's team.

"You're not using that Zapdos of yours? Or your Haunter?"

A powerful roar echoed through the air.

A massive shadow leapt down and landed before Apollo.

It was his Tyranitar—the very Pokémon that had launched the earlier ambush.

Ethan pulled a mask from his backpack and placed it over his face.

Then he reached toward two Ultra Balls at his waist.

After a brief pause, he took out only one.

"You outnumber me. There's no reason for me to hold back."

"I'm going all out."

Throwing the Ultra Ball high into the air, Ethan released its contents.

A brilliant flash erupted.

Zapdos spread its wings and soared into the sky.

Behind Ethan, Masha and the others stared with mouths hanging open.

"S-Sis!"

Masha stammered.

"I'm not seeing things, am I!? Isn't that the trainer who's been all over the internet lately!?"

"I think it really is him…"

Misa's eyes widened.

"I never imagined that the guy we just battled would actually be the Masked Trainer!"

At this point, words were meaningless.

Neither side needed any more conversation.

Nobody knew who attacked first.

But amidst furious roars, the battle exploded into chaos.

Psychic.

Water Gun.

Hydro Pump.

Flamethrower.

Razor Leaf.

Thunder Shock.

Attacks flew everywhere.

On the opposing side, Bite, Poison Sting, Fire Punch, Air Cutter, and Sludge Bomb were unleashed without mercy.

The battlefield instantly became a whirlwind of flashing attacks and colliding Pokémon.

Tyranitar tried to charge toward Ethan, but Gyarados—who never backed down from powerful opponents—intercepted it.

Magmar attempted to bully Ivysaur but quickly found itself targeted by Wartortle instead.

Murkrow swooped down toward Gardevoir, only to be blindsided by Jolteon's Quick Attack and knocked from the sky.

With so many Pokémon fighting at once, detailed command and control was no longer realistic.

Thanks to Haunter's assistance, Ethan narrowly avoided a Poison Sting that came from somewhere within the chaos.

Before he could even steady himself, a familiar voice suddenly echoed up from the ocean beneath the bridge.

( End of Chapter)
