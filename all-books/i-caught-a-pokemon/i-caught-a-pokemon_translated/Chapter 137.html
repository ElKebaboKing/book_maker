"I don't care if he's a magician or not," Ash declared as he took a big bite of his ice cream. "I'm going to beat him and advance to the next round!"

At that moment, the television mounted on the wall continued broadcasting matches from the other battlefields.

The announcer's voice suddenly rose in excitement, drawing everyone's attention to the screen.

On the Grass Field, Gary Oak had just defeated his opponent 3–0 with absolute ease.

As one of the tournament favorites, Gary was now surrounded by reporters from several television stations, giving a post-match interview.

"Gary Oak of Pallet Town has delivered a truly unforgettable victory," the female reporter in a pink business suit announced to the audience before holding the microphone toward him. "Gary, how do you feel after your first match?"

"The first match is always important, so I'm happy to have won," Gary replied confidently with his arms crossed. "Of course, everything went exactly as I expected. Advancing to the next round has certainly put my mind at ease."

Hearing those words, Ash instinctively clenched his fists.

Ethan has already advanced... and now even Gary has made it through?

Then I absolutely can't afford to lose!

At noon, perhaps motivated by what he had just seen, Ash headed to the Pokémon Center with Misty and Brock to rethink his strategy and switch out his battle team.

Meanwhile, Ethan, Chloe, and the biker gang arrived at the Water Field early to claim good seats.

Cola, popcorn, and cheering sticks were essential. Sitting in the stands beside Chloe, Ethan absentmindedly finished nearly half a bucket of popcorn before the match had even begun.

Suddenly, his phone vibrated.

Taking it out, he realized several new messages had appeared in his chat app.

The newest one was from Sabrina.

She had been busy defending her Gym while Ethan was battling Damian. By the time her Gym challenge had ended, Ethan's match was already over, so she had only been able to watch the replay before sending her congratulations.

Scrolling further, he found messages from Erika, Mr. Silver, Looker, and many other friends he had met throughout his journey.

The earliest message had actually arrived before the referee had even officially declared Ethan the winner—right after Damian's third Pokémon had fallen.

Judging by the adorable profile picture, it was from the girl back in the Kalos region.

Considering the time difference, it should already be late at night over there.

For someone who always went to bed early, staying up just to watch his match couldn't have been easy.

Ethan quickly typed a reply.

Beside him, Chloe secretly glanced at his phone, but Ethan typed so quickly that she couldn't make out a single word.

As time slowly passed, the spectators waited patiently until Ash and Toby finally entered the battlefield just before the match began.

Compared to Toby's confident entrance, Ash's steps were noticeably stiff. Whether from excitement or nervousness, even he wasn't entirely sure.

Unlike the Grass Field, the Water Field consisted of a large circular pool surrounded by five small battle platforms rising above the water.

When the battle began, just like in the anime, Toby sent out Exeggutor, while Ash chose his undersized Krabby.

Both Trainers had taken advantage of the battlefield.

Krabby naturally benefited from the Water Field, while Exeggutor's Grass typing allowed it to resist Water-type attacks.

It was a smart strategic choice on both sides.

Looking around, Chloe couldn't help but exclaim,

"Wow, there seem to be a lot more spectators here than during your match!"

"There are," Ethan nodded. "Probably about one-third more."

There were indeed far more people watching this battle than there had been for his own.

The reason was simple—Toby's reputation.

During the preliminary rounds, Trainers like Ethan and Ash, who had little public recognition, mainly attracted support from friends and family.

But famous competitors like Toby and Gary naturally drew much larger crowds.

However, as the battle progressed, everyone began to realize something wasn't quite right.

Although Exeggutor's Psychic attacks caused Krabby plenty of trouble early on—and Ash even considered switching Pokémon—Krabby eventually seized an opportunity, rushed in from the water, and defeated Exeggutor with a relentless combination attack.

Ash wasn't the only one surprised.

The audience erupted into excited discussion.

No one had expected the well-known Toby to lose the first battle against a kid.

As if that weren't shocking enough, after defeating Exeggutor, Krabby evolved into Kingler!

For Toby, losing to a child was embarrassing enough.

The unexpected Evolution only made things worse.

Still believing Ash had simply gotten lucky, Toby stubbornly sent out Seadra.

The result was exactly the same.

Ash claimed another victory.

Now Toby could no longer remain calm, and even the audience had completely shifted their support toward Ash, cheering loudly for him.

"That kid is incredible!"

"Isn't he from Pallet Town too? I heard Gary Oak, one of the championship favorites, comes from the same place."

For the third battle, Ash kept Kingler on the field.

Riding the momentum, Kingler defeated Toby's Golbat, securing the match.

"The battle is over!" the announcer shouted in disbelief. "Ash has won! Incredible! Without switching Pokémon even once, he defeated all three of Toby's Pokémon using only a single Kingler!"

Just as the announcer had said, defeating three Pokémon with only one had left the audience thoroughly amazed.

When the referee officially announced the result, Toby dropped to his knees, his face completely pale.

After losing to a child, he would never again carry the same confidence he had shown before the tournament.

Ethan, Chloe, and the biker gang all banged their cheering sticks together.

The rhythmic bang! bang! bang! echoed throughout the stadium.

Ash's victory also meant that both Ethan and Ash had successfully advanced from the original 256 competitors into the next round.

Two days later, the remaining 128 Trainers would compete again, with half of them being eliminated.

The tournament's ruthless format forced every contestant to fight with everything they had for a chance to continue.

After leaving the stadium, Ethan and the others met up with Ash, Misty, and Brock for dinner.

After the meal, they didn't watch any more matches.

With so many participants, the Indigo Conference schedule was packed to the brim.

Although four battlefields operated simultaneously, each one still had to host 32 matches before the next round's competitors could be determined.

There were simply too many battles for anyone to watch them all with the limited time and energy they had.

( End of Chapter)
