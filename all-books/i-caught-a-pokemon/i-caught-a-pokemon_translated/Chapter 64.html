When a young mayor wearing gold-rimmed glasses and looking refined and scholarly arrived at the shrine, Ethan once again explained the matter to him.

The mayor listened carefully while flipping through the manga. Then he slowly raised his head, and a sharp gleam reflected from his glasses!

As a mayor who had originally risen through the ranks as a businessman, he had immediately sensed the business opportunity hidden behind all of this.

Maiden's Peak relied on the legend of the maiden to attract many tourists every year during this season, but aside from that, the town had little else that truly stood out.

Especially with the famous Blue Surf City nearby, as well as the newly rising Yo Town Harbor, Maiden's Peak paled in comparison to those locations.

If this manga spread far and wide, allowing more young people to learn about and fall in love with the town, it could attract even more tourists. Then, just like the larger cities, they could host stage plays, anime and manga conventions, and sell merchandise, all of which would undoubtedly increase the town's popularity.

"I heard you plan to donate this manga to the shrine?"

"That's right." Ethan nodded. Although the project had cost him a great deal of time and effort, he had never intended to profit from it.

However, to his surprise, after hearing this, the mayor proposed a different idea.

"Creating the story and drawing the manga must have taken a tremendous amount of effort. How about this? I'd like to pay a sum of money to purchase all the rights to it. What do you think?"

"Huh? Buy it?" Ethan blinked in surprise. "But this manga itself was adapted from Maiden's Peak's legend. I don't exactly own the story itself. Besides, whether it'll even become popular is still unknown."

"That part is not a problem," the mayor replied calmly, clearly unconcerned about possible setbacks. "With the combined efforts of the entire town of Maiden's Peak, I believe a little promotion will achieve excellent results."

Ethan naturally understood the mayor's true intention. By buying the rights now at a relatively low price, the town could avoid any future disputes over profits no matter how successful the project became.

Still, receiving a sum of money was an unexpected bonus for Ethan, so there was no reason for him to refuse.

To show his sincerity, the mayor ultimately decided to pay 200,000 Poké Dollars and directly purchased all the rights to the manga. Ethan's nearly depleted savings instantly became plentiful again.

After signing the contract, Ethan left the shrine and immediately transferred 100,000 Poké Dollars to Chloe, asking her to pass it on to her mother.

Not long afterward, Yoshino Cerise called him. At first, she absolutely refused to accept the money, but after Ethan repeatedly persuaded her, she finally agreed.

And with that, the Maiden's Peak matter had finally reached its proper conclusion!

As the manga continued to be printed and distributed in the future, Haunter would never again need to worry about the Maiden Ghost's story being forgotten. It could now wholeheartedly continue traveling with Ethan without regrets!

On the outskirts of Celadon City.

Wearing his Gengar mask and black cloak, Ethan slowly approached a heavily guarded temporary camp.

The officers on guard immediately noticed the suspiciously dressed figure, drew their guns, and surrounded him.

"Stop right there! Who are you!?"

Ethan remained perfectly calm.

"Handsome Guy asked me to come. Please notify him."

The officers eyed him suspiciously. One of them made a phone call to verify his identity, and after a short wait, a somewhat exhausted-looking Handsome Guy walked out from inside.

"Huh? Why are you wearing that mask again? Hurry up and take it off."

Ethan shook his head and flatly refused.

"No way. I still remember the lesson Rocket Team taught me last time. From now on, whenever I'm involved in something like this, wearing a mask is much safer."

"But you're already on their blacklist. Even if you hide yourself now, isn't it already too late?" Handsome Guy smiled helplessly. Seeing Ethan remain unmoved, he stopped insisting. "Honestly, I don't know what to do with you anymore. Fine, come inside."

Inside the camp were armed special police officers everywhere, accompanied by groups of Growlithe. In one corner, Ethan even spotted rows of armored vehicles and helicopters!

"So many weapons and vehicles…" Ethan remarked. "Looks like you're absolutely determined to succeed this time."

"Of course." Handsome Guy nodded. "We've already confirmed the exact location of the Rocket Team base. Besides the underground entrance through the Game Corner, we also discovered another route outside the city!"

After only a few sentences, however, Handsome Guy had already begun breathing heavily. Ethan could not help asking,

"How are your injuries?"

Handsome Guy waved dismissively.

"Nothing serious. But the doctors told me to rest and avoid strenuous activity. If it weren't for how urgent this operation is, they never would've allowed me to leave the hospital."

At moments like this, the medical technology of this world truly seemed astonishing. Whether human or Pokémon, no matter how severe the injuries suffered in the wild, as long as Nurse Joy treated them at a Pokémon Center, they would almost certainly be lively and healthy again by the next day!

"Then why are you still participating in this mission?" Ethan asked curiously. "Surely the International Police doesn't only have you working yourself to death?"

"Of course not." Handsome Guy gestured proudly around him. "See all these officers nearby? They were directly transferred here from headquarters. Even my superior is here right now, personally overseeing all operations from the command center ahead!"

Just as Ethan was about to respond, he suddenly spotted a familiar figure in the distance.

A perfectly curved figure, long black hair, and black stockings that naturally drew attention wherever she went. Upon her delicate face sat a pair of cool, emotionless eyes quietly fixed on him.

It was Sabrina!

Ethan truly had not expected her to participate in this operation as well.

But after thinking about it, it made perfect sense. Since the International Police was cooperating with the Pokémon League, Sabrina—being located in Saffron City, so close to Celadon City—would naturally be one of the first people considered.

Ethan quickly walked over and greeted her.

"When we parted last time, you said we would meet again soon. I never imagined that only a few days later, we'd already be fighting side by side once more."

Sabrina's gaze seemed to pierce straight through Ethan's mask and look directly into his eyes.

"I guessed you would come, so I waited here in advance. Looks like my intuition was correct."

Hearing those words, Handsome Guy subconsciously took a step backward!

What was going on here!?

That cold and aloof psychic beauty was actually speaking to someone in such a tone!?

In truth, Handsome Guy and Sabrina had only met a few times. During the Silph Co. incident, the only reason he had managed to invite her to help was thanks to coordination from the Pokémon Association.

Even so, he understood Sabrina's personality extremely well. If their relationship had merely been ordinary, there was no way she would personally come out to greet someone!

So either the two of them had forged a deep bond and mutual understanding through battle… or something else had happened between them!

"Oh, right…"

Ethan opened his backpack, took out the final perfume gift box, and handed it to Sabrina.

"This is a gift I bought for you. Consider it a thank-you for what happened last time. Please accept it."
