When the light finally faded, what appeared before Ethan Vale was Eevee's new evolved form—a Jolteon with yellow fur and sharp lightning-like spikes covering its body.

Jolteon had slender limbs and small paws, with a ring of white fur around its neck. Every spike on its body stood sharply upright like a lightning rod. Just by looking at it, one could practically feel the vibrant energy and electricity flowing within it.

Pushing aside the other curious Pokémon crowding around, Ethan Vale quickly took out his Pokédex to check Jolteon's information.

Pokédex: "Jolteon, the Lightning Pokémon. Electric-type. Evolves from Eevee through exposure to a Thunder Stone. When startled, all the fur on its body stands upright like needles to intimidate enemies. It excels at Electric-type moves and can absorb negatively charged ions from the atmosphere. Sometimes it releases high-voltage electricity exceeding 10,000 volts. Due to its incredible speed, some places even use it to guard other Pokémon."

"This is great." Ethan Vale gently stroked Jolteon's sharp fur and nodded in satisfaction. "Jolteon, your new form suits you perfectly."

The next morning.

Leaving the Cerise Laboratory, Ethan Vale followed a winding mountain road into the deep mountains bordering Vermilion City.

After walking for quite some time, the terrain gradually rose higher. Buildings became increasingly scarce, replaced by dense towering trees and rows of wind turbines spinning slowly along the mountainsides.

Nestled among the mountains stood an old-fashioned power plant built from red brick and cement.

From the outside, the facility appeared heavily weathered. Moss covered the cracks in the walls, and several windows had shattered long ago. Yet from the electric currents occasionally flashing through the power lines and the steady humming of the machinery inside, it was clear the plant had not been completely abandoned. Workers still occasionally came to perform minimal maintenance.

Looking around, aside from the rustling wind, the area was completely silent. There were no strange disturbances or signs of wild Pokémon activity nearby.

As Ethan Vale approached the power plant, he noticed the main entrance was locked shut with a rust-covered padlock hanging from the door.

However, one nearby window was half-open, with broken glass scattered beneath it.

After checking to make sure nobody else was around, he climbed in through the window.

Inside, the power plant was built like a maze.

Just like outside, it was eerily quiet. Under the dim glow of orange emergency lights, the interior felt especially gloomy.

Taking out his flashlight, Ethan Vale removed the tape covering it. Once the beam illuminated the surroundings, the true layout of the power plant became visible.

His footsteps echoed softly through the hallways as he carefully searched room after room, but after opening several doors, he found nothing unusual.

"Have any of you noticed something strange?"

At Ethan Vale's question, Kirlia and the other Pokémon all shook their heads gently.

Nothing?

Could this really just be aging equipment malfunctioning?

Rubbing his chin thoughtfully, Ethan Vale said:

"Well, since we're already here, let's split up and keep searching. Maybe we'll find something."

However, just as he finished speaking, he suddenly heard a cry coming from deep within the power plant.

The sound was brief and sharp. Though it lasted only an instant, Ethan Vale caught it immediately.

That sound...

He listened carefully, but no second cry followed.

Turning toward his Pokémon, he noticed they were all nodding as well. Clearly, they had heard it too.

"It came from over there. Let's move!"

Leading his Pokémon, Ethan Vale hurried through the winding corridors, turning corner after corner until they finally arrived outside a partially open door.

Through the crack, sunlight could be seen pouring through glass panels in the ceiling, illuminating the entire room. The loud humming of generators instantly told Ethan Vale what this place was.

The Main Power Chamber.

But what shocked him even more was the enormous golden bird Pokémon inside the room, perched atop a machine while continuously absorbing electricity from the generators.

The moment Ethan Vale saw its familiar appearance and majestic form, even after seeing countless Pokémon before, his heart still trembled.

It really was the Legendary Pokémon—Zapdos!!

Zapdos was an extremely rare Legendary Pokémon. Its body was bright golden-yellow, with massive wings and tail feathers covered in sharp, jagged spikes. Beneath its fierce eyes extended a long orange beak. Because it possessed the power to control lightning and alter the weather, people often referred to it as the God of Thunder.

What should he do now?

Ethan Vale turned back to glance at his Pokémon. Seeing them all watching him, waiting for his command, he slowly clenched his fist.

A once-in-a-lifetime opportunity.

Since fate had allowed him to encounter Zapdos, no matter what, he wanted to try capturing it!

But with his current team, capturing Zapdos would be incredibly difficult.

Zapdos was both Electric- and Flying-type, and Ethan Vale currently had no Pokémon with a type advantage against it. On the contrary, Zapdos could deal double damage to Squirtle.

Still, no matter how difficult the challenge, Ethan Vale had no intention of letting this chance slip away.

"Kirlia, Ivysaur, Jolteon, Squirtle, and Charmander—this battle is up to all of you! Our opponent this time is an extremely powerful Pokémon. None of you can defeat it alone, so don't hold anything back. Attack together and bring it down!"

All the Pokémon cried out in unison.

The next second, the doors to the power chamber burst open. Jolteon charged in first, followed closely by the others as they surrounded Zapdos from every direction.

Inside the chamber, Zapdos had been peacefully restoring its energy when its feeding was interrupted. Instantly furious, it became even more irritated upon realizing the intruders were merely a group of unevolved low-level Pokémon.

With an angry screech, it spread its wings wide.

A scorching wave of electricity erupted outward.

It was attempting to use its overwhelming pressure to drive the invaders out of its territory!

At that moment, Ethan Vale stepped into the power chamber and shouted:

"Zapdos! Wager your freedom and battle us with everything you've got!"

Zapdos gazed down arrogantly at Ethan Vale, clearly not taking him seriously at all.

"In that case," Ethan Vale said as he casually tossed a Poké Ball in his hand, "I'll take that as agreement!"

"Kirlia, use Magical Leaf! Ivysaur, use Razor Leaf! Charmander, use Ember! Squirtle, use Water Gun! Everyone attack together!"

Following Ethan Vale's command, attacks of every kind instantly bombarded Zapdos from all directions.

And thus, the fierce battle to capture Zapdos officially began!
