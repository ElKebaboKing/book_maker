"Venusaur, use Razor Leaf!!"

At Ethan's command, countless sharp leaves shot out from Venusaur's flower bud, striking the Graveler standing before them.

Slash! Slash! Slash! Slash! Slash!

Unable to withstand the continuous barrage, the wild Pokémon was blasted backward and rolled straight down the mountainside into the forest below.

Letting out a long sigh, Ethan counted silently. Including this one, it was already the sixth wild Pokémon that had attacked him since he arrived on the island.

Golbat, Geodude, Tentacool, Slowpoke, Cloyster, Graveler…

Who knew what would appear next?

Although the difference in strength allowed his Pokémon to easily defeat these wild attackers, continuing to fight nonstop was obviously not a long-term solution.

After thinking for a moment, Ethan decided to release all of his Pokémon at once to avoid further ambushes.

Blue flashes appeared one after another as Jolteon, Wartortle, and the others emerged, surrounding Ethan protectively.

This way, even if another wild Pokémon wanted to attack him, seeing so many powerful guards gathered together would surely make it reconsider before acting recklessly.

Continuing forward, Ethan ventured deeper into the island until he arrived at a cliffside. Crossing a natural stone bridge formed by jagged rocks and squeezing through a narrow crevice, he eventually entered a massive rocky maze.

And just as expected, the effect was immediate.

Along the way, no wild Pokémon dared foolishly attack after seeing such a large group of Pokémon traveling together.

The maze was surrounded by steep, smooth stone walls. Not only were there countless branching paths, but most of them also looked nearly identical. At a glance, there was almost no visible difference between them. After merely changing directions a few times and taking several wrong turns, Ethan was already starting to lose his sense of direction.

Looking around, Haunter asked in confusion,

"Is Articuno really on this island? We've already traveled so far, but there's still no sign of it!"

"If it were that easy to find, it wouldn't still be here waiting for us." After walking for so long, Ethan felt somewhat tired. He took out his water bottle and drank before continuing, "Actually, when people think about bird Pokémon, they instinctively assume they nest in trees. But if we want to find Articuno, we need to go downward instead!"

Whether in the games or the anime, beneath the Seafoam Islands there truly existed a vast underground world of flowing water and ice.

"Downward!?" Haunter suddenly understood. "You mean there's an entirely different world underneath this island!?"

Ethan nodded.

"Otherwise, why would cold air be seeping out from beneath our feet? Somewhere below us must be a freezing environment suitable for Articuno to live in!"

Then he turned to Haunter and Zapdos.

"I'm counting on you two. Fly up high and see if there's any entrance nearby leading underground!"

"No problem! Leave it to us!"

Haunter nodded and, together with Zapdos, flew off in different directions.

Watching them leave, Ethan could not help but sigh. If only he had a Pokémon capable of Dig right now, they could simply tunnel straight downward instead of going through so much trouble.

After resting briefly, Ethan continued navigating the maze with the rest of his Pokémon.

Actually, there was one foolproof way to get through a maze like this: simply keep following either the left or right wall in a single direction, and eventually you would find an exit.

That was exactly the method Ethan was currently using.

Along the way, he discovered traces of old campsites and battle marks left behind by previous visitors. It seemed the old fisherman had been telling the truth—many Trainers had indeed traveled to these islands in search of Articuno!

After years of exposure to wind and rain, however, the abandoned clothes and tents left behind had become tattered and rotten. Some crumbled into dust the moment he touched them.

After traveling for some time longer, Haunter and Zapdos finally returned and landed beside Ethan.

"No luck!" Haunter sighed. "From above, everything looks exactly the same—just endless barren rocks everywhere. My eyes were practically spinning, but I still couldn't find any cave entrance!"

"It's probably hidden somewhere concealed." After thinking carefully, Ethan decided to change strategies. "How about this? Since searching from above isn't working, let's work backward from below instead. Haunter, could you go underground and locate the caves first? Then try finding an exit leading up to the surface."

"Now that sounds like a solid idea. Wait for my good news!" Haunter's eyes lit up excitedly as it sank straight through the rocky ground beneath them.

This time took far less time than before.

Not long afterward, when Haunter returned again, it finally brought back good news.

"You were right!" The thrill of exploration clearly excited it. "There really are multiple underground cave layers beneath the island! And at the very bottom, there's actually a massive frozen chamber made entirely of ice!"

"Did you see Articuno?" Ethan immediately asked.

"No, I was worried about alerting it, so I didn't go any deeper. But I did find a tunnel that connects directly to the underground caves!"

"Excellent! Lead the way!"

Haunter once again sank underground, while Ethan climbed onto Zapdos and took to the skies.

Not long later, somewhere within the maze, a Shadow Ball suddenly shot upward into the air.

"There! Zapdos, let's go!"

Flapping its wings, Zapdos slowly descended. Only then did Ethan realize just how well hidden the cave entrance actually was.

Tall weeds grew along both sides of the opening, while a massive rock partially covered the entrance overhead, blocking out sunlight. Forget spotting it from the air—even standing directly in front of it, Ethan might not have noticed the cave without paying close attention!

Just then, a chilling gust of wind blew out from deep underground. The icy cold instantly slipped through his clothes and struck his body.

Unable to stop himself from shivering, Ethan tightened his collar, took out a flashlight, and had Charmeleon lead the way as he and his Pokémon entered the cave.

At first, the warm sunlight from outside mixed with the freezing cave air, making the ground incredibly slippery. Every single step had to be taken with extreme caution.

But as they ventured deeper, the ground gradually became dry again while the temperature continued to drop. Soon, even their breath turned into visible white mist in the air!
