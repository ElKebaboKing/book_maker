Opening the door, Ethan Vale and Chloe Cerise stepped into the luxurious private lodge.

The accommodation was spacious, bright, and immaculately clean. Not a speck of dust could be found anywhere. With six or seven rooms spread across two floors, it could comfortably accommodate more than a dozen people.

However, with only Ethan and Chloe staying there, the large house felt rather quiet.

Each of them picked a room they liked. Although Chloe felt a little shy about sharing a house with a grown man, she said nothing. Instead, after setting down her backpack, she wandered from room to room, curiously exploring every corner.

"Alright, that's enough sightseeing," Ethan said with a smile, reaching out to stop her. "We'll be staying here for several days, so we'll have plenty of time to look around and enjoy the scenery."

He rubbed his empty stomach and chuckled.

"It's already this late, and I'm starving. Let's head to the dining hall and find something to eat."

Chloe nodded in agreement.

Ever since they had started traveling together, she hadn't enjoyed a proper meal either. Just thinking about delicious food made her swallow unconsciously.

Before leaving, she took out her phone and sent text messages to Professor Cerise and her mother, letting them know she had arrived safely.

Not far from the lodge stood the dining hall. Unfortunately, it was lunchtime, and by the time they entered, it was already packed with League contestants.

The room buzzed with conversations between Trainers and their Pokémon.

Looking around, Ethan saw more than a dozen serving counters. Long buffet tables beneath the glass displays were filled with an impressive variety of dishes.

The food looked delicious, and the aroma alone was enough to make anyone hungry.

Carrying trays piled high with food, Ethan and Chloe found a quiet table by the window in a corner and sat down.

As Ethan casually glanced around, he unexpectedly spotted several familiar faces.

The most eye-catching among them was Gary Oak, who was surrounded by several beautiful girls.

Gary leaned back comfortably in his chair with crossed legs, leisurely sipping a cup of coffee. Yet despite his relaxed appearance, his wandering gaze was clearly evaluating the strength of every Trainer in the room.

Their eyes met.

Gary raised his coffee cup slightly and gave Ethan the sort of confident, charismatic smile that seemed straight out of a romance drama.

Ethan simply nodded in greeting.

They weren't particularly close, so there was no need to walk over and chat.

Shifting his gaze again, Ethan noticed three other familiar faces—Claire ("Candy"), Hayden, and Logan, the Trainers they had met on New Island.

The three of them were scattered throughout the restaurant.

Since Mewtwo had erased everyone's memories, they no longer recognized one another, nor did they remember Ethan.

Putting the matter aside, Ethan and Chloe continued eating and chatting.

Only after he was completely full did Ethan set down his knife and fork, lean back in his chair, and stretch comfortably.

Just then, a man's voice sounded from behind him.

"I was wondering whether I'd run into you here. Turns out I found you almost immediately."

Turning around, Ethan saw the familiar green-haired, broad-built young man.

It was A.J., the Beast Trainer.

"So you're participating in the League too," Ethan said with a smile.

"Of course." A.J. grinned confidently. "After losing to you last time, I built up another one hundred consecutive victories. Then I began traveling across the region and eventually earned all eight Gym Badges."

"A hundred straight wins—that's impressive," Ethan nodded. "After all this time, I imagine your Sandshrew has evolved and become much stronger."

"Naturally!"

A.J. tossed a Poké Ball up and down in his hand before glancing at Ethan's Gardevoir.

"Just like your Pokémon evolved into this form. Hmph... I hope we meet in the tournament. This time, I'll defeat you fair and square."

As a former opponent, Ethan genuinely respected A.J.'s strength.

"That would definitely make for an exciting battle. I'm looking forward to facing you again."

A.J. waved goodbye and turned to leave.

After taking a few steps, he suddenly stopped and looked back.

"Oh, right. I should give you a heads-up."

"This tournament has several favorites to win the championship."

"The biggest names are Professor Oak's grandson and an honor student from the Pokémon Academy."

He paused briefly before continuing.

"There's also another contestant named Toby, from Jungler Town."

"I haven't actually seen him battle, but he keeps bragging about how incredibly strong he is. Maybe he's exaggerating—but either way, if you end up facing any of those people, you'd better be careful."

"Got it." Ethan nodded. "Thanks for the information. I'll keep that in mind."

The following day, the Pokémon League held its traditional Torch Relay.

More than a dozen contestants took turns carrying the torch, passing it every few hundred meters until it finally reached Indigo Stadium.

Ethan chose not to participate.

Instead, he stood among the spectators, quietly taking in the festive atmosphere.

During the event, he also spotted Ash, Misty, and Brock.

Dressed in matching athletic outfits, the three of them were participating in the relay.

Their every movement was broadcast live, giving millions of viewers an early glimpse of the competitors.

Of course, wherever Ash went, Team Rocket was never far behind.

Sure enough, Jessie, James, and Meowth attempted to steal the torch, causing a brief commotion.

Fortunately, the crisis was resolved, and the torch was safely delivered into the hands of the Pokémon League President—Charles Goodshow.

From his appearance, President Goodshow was a short elderly man with thick hair. Even on such an important occasion, he wore a baseball cap backward and dressed quite casually, clearly not the rigid, formal type.

In reality, Goodshow was known for his humorous personality. As chairman of the League Executive Committee responsible for the Sacred Flame and the overall organizer of the Pokémon League Conference, he enjoyed tremendous respect throughout the Pokémon world.

Only then did Ethan learn from the crowd that the flame inside the torch had been lit using the fire of Moltres.

Moltres...

Ethan rubbed his chin thoughtfully.

So far, he had already captured two of the Legendary Birds.

Only Moltres remained.

Once he completed the trio, he could ride one today, another tomorrow, and switch between them whenever he felt like it.
