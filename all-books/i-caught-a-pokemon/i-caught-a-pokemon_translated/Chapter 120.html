Seeing Ethan, Ash was naturally surprised. He shouted,

"Ethan! Quick, help me catch that Poké Ball! Pikachu is trapped inside!"

Even Pikachu—who almost never stayed inside a Poké Ball—had been captured by one of those strange black Poké Balls.

With a wave of his hand, Ethan signaled to Gardevoir. Understanding immediately, she used Psychic to force the Poké Ball open, releasing Pikachu.

"Don't panic. It wasn't just Pikachu—I rescued everyone else's Pokémon too."

"Thank you so much!" Ash hugged Pikachu tightly before running over to Charizard and Squirtle. Seeing that they were both unharmed, he finally breathed a sigh of relief. "Is everyone okay?"

Ethan asked, "What's happening upstairs?"

At the mention of it, Ash looked both frustrated and dejected.

"We lost. Those cloned Pokémon were way too strong. And Mewtwo went too far—it used those weird Poké Balls to steal all our Pokémon!"

Ethan nodded.

"That was its plan from the beginning. It invited us to this island for exactly that reason. But I've already destroyed the equipment here, so its plan is about to fall apart."

Only then did Ash notice the wreckage surrounding them. His eyes widened.

"So that's where you disappeared to! But Ethan... how did you know all this equipment was hidden down here?"

"Well..."

For a moment, Ethan didn't know how to explain it, so he simply changed the subject.

"There's no time for that now. Let's get upstairs and help the others."

Leading all the rescued Pokémon, Ethan and Ash hurried out of the laboratory and rushed back upstairs.

When they returned to the battlefield behind the great hall, they found Misty, Brock, and the others confronting Mewtwo.

But there wasn't a single Pokémon left beside them.

"Humans," Mewtwo said calmly, sounding almost merciful, "I have no intention of taking your lives. Leave this island."

It paused before continuing coldly,

"Of course, if you wish to return safely to the mainland, you'll have to cross that storm on your own."

Misty and the others glared at Mewtwo through clenched teeth.

Its words sounded generous, but without a ship, how could ordinary humans possibly cross such a terrifying sea?

More importantly, as Pokémon Trainers, how could they abandon their Pokémon and flee in disgrace?

Just then, they heard hurried footsteps behind them.

Turning around, they saw Ethan and Ash rushing toward them.

Behind them were all the Pokémon that had been captured moments earlier.

"They're back!"

Mewtwo frowned.

"What happened?"

This unexpected development had completely caught it off guard.

Its gaze shifted between Ash and Ethan before finally settling on Ethan.

"Did you do this?"

Ethan nodded.

"I planned to deal with you last," Mewtwo said, "yet in such a short time, you've caused me far more trouble than I expected."

Ethan smiled faintly.

"No plan survives reality. No matter how carefully you prepare, there's always something that slips beyond your control."

Perhaps because he was furious over everything Mewtwo had done, Ash suddenly rushed forward and threw a punch at Mewtwo.

Naturally, Mewtwo had no intention of letting a foolish human land a hit.

Its eyes glowed blue, and before Ash's fist could connect, powerful psychic energy surrounded him, launching him high into the air.

Ash flew dozens of meters.

Just as he was about to crash into a distant stone wall, a pink psychic bubble suddenly appeared behind him, gently stopping his momentum.

A tiny pink Pokémon that looked like a kitten floated into view.

Its appearance instantly shocked everyone present.

Brock immediately recognized it.

"T-That's... M-Mew!?"

"Mew?" Mewtwo carefully examined the little Pokémon.

"So you're Mew... the original template used to create me?"

Completely ignoring everyone's astonishment, Mew simply floated through the air in playful circles, paying no attention to anyone.

At that moment, Ethan raised his Pokédex toward it.

Pokédex:

"Mew, the New Species Pokémon. A Psychic-type Mythical Pokémon with extraordinarily high intelligence. Although widely believed to be extinct, it is still regarded as a Mythical Pokémon. Because it can become invisible at will, it can approach humans without ever being noticed. Its DNA is said to contain the genetic information of every Pokémon, allowing it to learn every move."

Mew's appearance seemed to strike a nerve within Mewtwo.

Gathering psychic energy into its hand, it formed a glowing sphere and fired it at Mew.

Mew merely glanced at the attack before effortlessly dodging it.

When the first attack missed, Mewtwo launched more than a dozen energy spheres in rapid succession.

Mew danced gracefully through the air, weaving around every attack with ease.

The missed blasts crashed into the castle behind it, erupting in violent explosions.

Staring intently at Mew, Mewtwo declared,

"Mew—the rarest Pokémon in the world. I may have been created from your DNA, but I am the strongest! I am the true original! The only one worthy of existing... is me!"

With that, Mewtwo shot into the sky until it was face-to-face with Mew.

The two Pokémon floated opposite each other.

One radiated overwhelming killing intent.

The other lazily swayed its tail, looking completely harmless.

After waiting for a response and receiving none, Mewtwo attacked.

The two Pokémon streaked across the sky in an instant, chasing each other at incredible speed.

Within moments, they had circled the entire island several times.

"Why won't you fight back?" Mewtwo shouted angrily.

"Are you running because you're afraid of me?"

Mew remained completely indifferent.

Or rather...

The two of them simply weren't on the same wavelength.

Feeling increasingly ignored, Mewtwo pressed both hands together, creating an enormous sphere of psychic energy before launching it straight at Mew.

The massive blast struck Mew directly, sending it soaring into the clouds.

A moment later, the explosion blossomed into a gigantic fireball high in the sky.

Everyone's hearts tightened.

But in the very next second...

An identical energy sphere burst out of the clouds and slammed directly into Mewtwo.

BOOM!

Mewtwo was blasted straight into the ground.

When the dust settled, blue psychic energy surrounded its body once more, and it slowly floated back into the air...

Completely unharmed.

( End of Chapter )
