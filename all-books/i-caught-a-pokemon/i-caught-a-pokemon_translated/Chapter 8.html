However…

Ethan Vale glanced upward toward the ceiling. This battle wasn't entirely without advantages for him.

Through the bright glass dome overhead, the sunlight could clearly be seen shining gently into the arena.

Another sunny day…

"Let's begin!" Seeing that both Pokémon were ready, Violet loudly gave the command before Daisy could even speak. "Krabby, use Water Gun!"

A powerful stream of water burst from Krabby's mouth and shot directly toward Bulbasaur!

"Bulbasaur, dodge it and use Vine Whip!"

Following Ethan Vale's order, Bulbasaur leaped sideways to evade the attack while two Vine Whips extended from the bulb on its back, lashing toward Krabby.

But in the next second, Krabby dove beneath the water, and the Vine Whips struck only the surface, splashing water everywhere.

The first exchange once again ended without either side gaining an advantage.

No matter how focused the Cerulean sisters were on water ballet, their battle skills were certainly not easy to underestimate!

Still, seeing the battle reach a stalemate, Violet suddenly smiled.

"Well, with one Pokémon in the water and the other on land, it's hard to decide a winner like this. Looks like we'll need a different approach. Krabby, climb onto that platform!"

"Oh?" Hearing the command, Ethan Vale raised an eyebrow in surprise. "Giving up your home-field advantage to fight Bulbasaur directly? Are you sure that's a good idea?"

Violet lifted her chin confidently.

"Don't worry. Unlike Lily's Goldeen, my Krabby is still very strong even on land! Krabby, use Leer!"

Krabby rapidly paddled with its legs before climbing sideways onto Bulbasaur's floating platform. Its dull-looking eyes suddenly glowed red as it stared directly at Bulbasaur.

Trying to weaken Bulbasaur through fear?

Ethan Vale calmly responded:

"Bulbasaur, don't let it affect you. Use Growth!"

A faint green glow immediately surrounded Bulbasaur's body.

Growth was a move that increased the user's offensive power. And because today's weather was sunny with abundant sunlight, the move's effect became even stronger!

Seeing the opportunity, Violet's eyes lit up.

"Krabby, hurry and use Water Gun!"

Because there was no time to dodge, the Water Gun struck Bulbasaur directly. Although Leer had lowered Bulbasaur's defenses, Water-type attacks naturally dealt reduced damage against Grass-types, so the hit failed to inflict significant harm.

"Bulbasaur, now it's our turn to strike back! Use Tackle!"

Knowing how powerful the attack would become after Growth's boost, Violet did not dare act carelessly.

"Krabby, Harden quickly!"

As Violet gave the order, Krabby's body flashed with a metallic sheen before Bulbasaur could reach it. Even visually, it was obvious that its shell had become tougher and smoother.

Bang!

Krabby took the Tackle head-on and staggered backward a step.

Fortunately, it did not appear seriously injured and still had enough strength left to counterattack!

"Now, Krabby, use Vice Grip!"

At Violet's command, Krabby thrust its large claw forward and tightly clamped onto Bulbasaur's neck.

Because Vice Grip was a Normal-type move, it was not weakened against Bulbasaur's Grass- and Poison-typing. Combined with Leer's effect, the attack dealt considerable damage, causing Bulbasaur to cry out instinctively in pain.

However, Ethan Vale had no intention of sitting back helplessly.

"Use Vine Whip!"

The Vine Whips rose high from Bulbasaur's bulb before crashing down heavily. Every strike inflicted major damage upon Krabby.

Smack! Smack!

After enduring several painful hits, Krabby could no longer withstand the pain and was forced to retreat, releasing Bulbasaur and backing away.

Bulbasaur had regained its freedom!

And now, the tide of battle had reversed.

"Now then, it's our turn!"

Narrowing his eyes, Ethan Vale commanded:

"Use Leech Seed!"

A tiny seed instantly shot out from Bulbasaur's bulb and attached itself to Krabby's body at incredible speed. In the blink of an eye, vines rapidly sprouted from the seed and tightly wrapped around Krabby!

This time, Krabby became the one unable to move!

Realizing the danger, Violet attempted one final resistance.

"Krabby, hurry and get back into the water!"

Without question, that was the best possible solution.

Unfortunately…

Trying to escape now?

Too late.

Ethan Vale smiled lightly before issuing the finishing command.

"Bulbasaur, finish it with Vine Whip!"

The two Vine Whips struck rapidly, lashing heavily across Krabby's body before it could return to the water. Not only did the Grass-type attack deal super effective damage, but the Leech Seed was also glowing faintly green while draining away Krabby's remaining stamina.

This time, Krabby could no longer endure it and collapsed onto the floating platform.

After staring blankly for a moment, Violet sighed.

"I lost too…"

As she spoke, she withdrew Krabby back into its Poké Ball.

Ethan Vale had now won two consecutive battles.

Only Daisy remained.

Watching both of her younger sisters lose caused Daisy's expression to grow serious.

Slowly stepping onto the trainer's platform, she carefully observed Ethan Vale.

"You're truly strong—and calm as well. It's obvious that you're an excellent trainer. But we won't surrender so easily! Come out, my Pokémon—Seel!"

As Daisy opened her Poké Ball, a grayish-white Pokémon resembling a seal appeared within the pool.

At first glance, the Pokémon had a small horn protruding from its head and upward-curving tusks near its mouth. Sticking out its tongue with its split tail behind it, it looked fairly similar to ordinary seals from Ethan Vale's previous life.

Because he wasn't overly familiar with it, Ethan Vale took out his Pokédex and scanned Seel.

The Pokédex quickly responded.

"Beep. Seel, the Sea Lion Pokémon. A Water-type Pokémon that usually lives among icebergs. It uses the sharp horn on its head to break through ice while swimming. Its thick hide allows it to move freely even in temperatures as low as minus forty degrees."

Unlike Goldeen or Krabby, this Seel possessed both mobility and close-range combat ability, making it much harder to deal with.

"Come back, Bulbasaur."

Ethan Vale recalled Bulbasaur and sent out his final Pokémon—his ace.

"Ralts, I'm counting on you!"

Hearing its trainer's voice, Ralts let out a soft cry before instantly teleporting onto the floating platform.

As it stared at Seel, a faint light shimmered beneath the bangs covering its eyes, while subtle changes quietly began appearing across its body.

"So it's called Ralts?" Looking at Ralts' adorable appearance, Daisy instantly took a liking to it and couldn't stop observing it carefully. "I noticed it earlier too. It's absolutely adorable. Is it a Pokémon from another region?"

"That's right. Ralts isn't native to Kanto." Ethan Vale nodded before kindly warning her, "Please be careful, Miss Daisy. It's a Psychic- and Fairy-type Pokémon, which makes it rather difficult to deal with."

"What a gentle and handsome man you are." Daisy smiled sweetly before continuing, "But I won't go easy on you because of that! Seel, use Aqua Jet!"

Aqua Jet coated Seel's body in water before launching it forward like a projectile. Compared to Water Gun, it was a much more powerful physical Water-type attack. The fact that Seel could use such a move clearly showed that its level was quite high.

However, landing that kind of attack against Ralts would still be difficult!

"Ralts, dodge it!"

Ethan Vale spoke softly, and in the next instant, Ralts vanished from its original position and reappeared on another floating platform.

The attack had been avoided with ease!

"Psychic Pokémon really are troublesome."

Daisy shook her head helplessly before quickly smiling again.

"In that case, let's restrict its movements first. Seel, use Icy Wind!"

A fierce freezing wind burst from Seel's mouth, engulfing both Ralts and the surrounding battlefield. The attack was so powerful that the arena's temperature instantly dropped below freezing, even causing thick layers of ice to form across the water's surface!

Although Ralts once again used Teleport to avoid the area attack, the freezing environment still greatly reduced its speed.
