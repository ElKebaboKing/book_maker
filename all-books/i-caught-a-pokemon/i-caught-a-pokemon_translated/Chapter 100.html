"My name is Ethan Vale. Just as I said earlier, I'm a Pokémon Trainer."

"Boss Ethan! Please accept us!"

After being pestered relentlessly, Ethan finally gave in.

In his view, there was a good chance he would never return to Fuchsia City anyway. Before long, these people would probably forget about him.

Agreeing now was simply the easiest way to stop them from bothering him.

"I'm not going to be anyone's boss," Ethan said. "But we can get along as friends. If you run into any trouble in the future, you can come find me."

"Boss Ethan is way too kind!" Masha exclaimed excitedly. "But we've already made up our minds! From now on, wherever Boss Ethan leads, we'll follow!"

Follow my lead?

Ethan shook his head.

As long as they don't sell me out or drag me down in the future, I'll already be grateful.

After spending considerable effort dealing with them, Ethan mounted a new bicycle. This time, accompanied by Masha and the others, he continued toward Sunshine Town.

Along the way, they chatted nonstop around him, enthusiastically sharing stories from their past.

Only then did Ethan realize that this so-called Fuchsia Biker Gang was really nothing more than a loose group of troublemakers.

Smoking, running red lights, littering, bothering people, holding unauthorized gatherings, making excessive noise, and letting their Pokémon battle wherever they pleased...

None of it sounded like the work of serious criminals.

In fact, the worst thing they had ever done was spray graffiti on walls.

Truthfully, these people had all grown up together.

The reason they formed a biker gang in the first place was because school and work had piled tremendous pressure on them. Under Misa and Masha's leadership, they simply spent their free time riding bicycles around to vent their frustrations.

After learning this, Ethan's impression of them improved significantly.

Whatever they might become in the future, at least right now they were ordinary people trying to enjoy life in their own way.

An hour later, amid laughter and noise, Ethan finally reached the far end of the bridge.

"Boss, please don't forget us!"

"Remember to come back and visit!"

"Mm, next time for sure."

After saying goodbye to the tearful biker gang, Ethan entered the town's Pokémon Center to restore his Pokémon's strength before continuing toward Saffron City.

When he found some privacy, he also called Sabrina and Erika.

Just as Looker had said, neither of them had been attacked.

Still, to be safe, Ethan urged them to remain vigilant.

White clouds drifted lazily across the sky, while bird Pokémon occasionally flew overhead.

As he crossed a vast prairie, Ethan witnessed a rare sight.

Hundreds of Tauros raced across the horizon beneath the setting sun, resembling the great animal migrations he had once seen on television.

After taking several photos, he continued traveling until dusk, eventually arriving at a small town called Stone Town.

Nestled at the foot of the mountains, it had originally prospered through mining. However, because Evolution Stones were occasionally discovered there, most people preferred its nickname—Stone Town.

The town wasn't large and had few tall buildings. Most structures were only two or three stories high.

Ethan intended to find a place to stay, but after searching for quite some time, he couldn't find a Pokémon Center.

Only after asking around did he learn that the town's population was too small for the Pokémon League to justify building one.

With no other option, he headed for the town's only inn.

Unfortunately, when he attempted to check in, he received unexpected news.

"What? It's fully booked?"

"I'm very sorry," the innkeeper said helplessly. "Just a few minutes ago, another guest took the last available room."

Seeing Ethan's disappointed expression, the owner added,

"Hey, you're a Trainer, right?"

When Ethan nodded, the innkeeper pointed toward a manor not far away.

"There's an Evolution Party being held at that estate. The owner is extremely hospitable. Not only do they provide free food, but they often let Trainers like you stay overnight. Why not try your luck there?"

"Well, I don't have much choice."

To avoid sleeping outdoors, Ethan headed toward the manor.

The moment he entered the courtyard, both he and Gardevoir attracted the attention of the hosts.

Three young men dressed in matching athletic outfits approached him.

One of them wore a sleeveless shirt with yellow pants, and his hair resembled a lightning bolt.

"Another Trainer?" he said with a smile. "Did you come here seeking Pokémon evolution too?"

"Huh? No."

Ethan explained his situation.

"If possible, I'd like to stay here for one night. Of course, I can pay."

The second man, who had blue hair and blue pants, waved his hand.

"No problem. As long as you're a Trainer, you're always welcome here."

At that moment, Ethan noticed that each brother was accompanied by one of Eevee's evolved forms.

Jolteon, Vaporeon, and Flareon.

The three classic Eeveelutions stood together before him.

Looking at them and then at the brothers themselves, Ethan finally remembered the anime episode connected to them.

So I've arrived here, huh?

He smiled.

"What a coincidence. I have an Eevee too."

"Oh?"

His words immediately captured all three brothers' attention.

"You have an Eevee too?" asked the brother in yellow pants. "Then which evolution did you choose? Jolteon? Flareon? Vaporeon? Or did you keep it in its original form like our youngest brother?"

Before Ethan could answer, the three immediately began arguing among themselves.

"Jolteon is obviously the strongest!"

"Nonsense! Flareon is clearly the best!"

"Your Flareon can't even beat my Vaporeon, so how can it be the strongest?"

Though they were brothers, none of them would back down when it came to their Pokémon.

After several moments of bickering, they all turned toward Ethan.

"You tell us! Which Eevee evolution is the strongest?"

"The strongest?"

Ethan thought for a moment before taking out a Poké Ball.

"I don't think any of them are inherently stronger than the others. Every Eeveelution has its own strengths. Besides, there are many other evolutions too, such as Umbreon and Leafeon."

He released his Pokémon.

"But if you're forcing me to choose…"

As Jolteon appeared beside him, Ethan grinned.

"Then compared to yours, my Jolteon is definitely the strongest!"

( End of Chapter )
