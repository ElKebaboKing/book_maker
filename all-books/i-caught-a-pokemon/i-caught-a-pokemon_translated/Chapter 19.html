Inside Cerise Park, Ethan Vale and Chloe Cerise walked side by side, leisurely strolling through the grounds.

The environment here was exceptionally beautiful. Functionally, it was quite similar to the forest behind Professor Oak's Laboratory, both serving as places where Trainers could leave extra Pokémon in a natural habitat.

Flowing water sources, large patches of berry trees, abundant sunlight…

After walking around for a while, Ethan Vale realized the park possessed a complete ecosystem. Without human interference, it could fully sustain itself naturally.

As they walked, Ethan Vale couldn't help but sigh in admiration.

"For a project of this scale, Professor Cerise must've put an incredible amount of effort into it."

Chloe covered her mouth with a smile.

"The park was only fully completed yesterday. Dad actually said the first thing he wanted was for you—as a Trainer—to come in and point out any flaws."

Ethan Vale shrugged.

"That really does sound like Professor Cerise. Very thorough."

Beside them, accompanied by Yamper's barking, Bulbasaur, Squirtle, Eevee, and Charmander chased each other around happily. They clearly loved the environment here.

Only Kirlia stayed quietly beside Ethan Vale, never leaving his side.

Seeing this, Ethan Vale smiled and called out:

"Alright, Bulbasaur, stop fooling around so much. Save your energy—we've got important business later."

"Important business?" Chloe asked curiously. "What kind of business?"

"Obviously we're going to challenge the Vermilion Gym." Ethan Vale spoke in a tempting tone. "So, want to come watch? Real Pokémon battles are far more exciting and intense than anything you see on television."

Real Pokémon battles?

Chloe lowered her head, clearly tempted. After hesitating for a moment, she gently nodded.

Vermilion—the orange-yellow color of fallen leaves at dusk.

The Vermilion Gym was located on the western coast of Vermilion City. Its overall color scheme was yellow and white, and from the outside, it resembled a giant inverted trapezoid, somewhat similar to a modern sports stadium.

After entering the Gym, someone immediately came to receive them. Once Ethan Vale explained his purpose, the staff member directly led him to the battle arena.

"Please wait a moment. Gym Leader Lt. Surge will arrive shortly."

After the staff member left, Ethan Vale looked around.

The battlefield itself was a standard battle arena, consisting of a plain dirt-yellow field with no additional terrain features.

Around the battlefield were over a dozen rows of elevated spectator seats on all four sides, much like a sports arena. By Ethan Vale's estimate, if filled completely, it could easily hold nearly a thousand people.

Since it was her first time visiting a place like this, the formal and grand atmosphere of the arena made Chloe slightly nervous.

"Relax." Ethan Vale lightly patted her shoulder. "There's nothing to worry about. Just sit back and enjoy the match."

"I understand." After steadying her breathing, Chloe softly added, "Good luck."

Watching Chloe walk toward the spectator area, Ethan Vale turned around just as several people emerged from the opposite hallway.

The man leading them had sharp blond spiky hair, a large muscular build, and wore a green military-style tank top with camouflage pants. Sunglasses covered his eyes, and military dog tags hung around his neck.

Just from his aura alone, it was obvious this was an extremely fierce and decisive man.

And he was none other than the Vermilion Gym Leader—Lt. Surge.

According to rumors, Lt. Surge had once served as an Air Force Major, piloting specially designed aircraft powered by Electric-type Pokémon during wartime. Only after retiring from military service and traveling around did he eventually become the Vermilion Gym Leader.

From his reputation alone, it was also clear that Lt. Surge battled aggressively and ruthlessly. Against weaker challengers, he often left their Pokémon severely injured.

Standing at the Trainer's platform, Lt. Surge spoke in a booming voice:

"So another overconfident challenger has come crawling into my Vermilion Gym!"

Ethan Vale remained completely unfazed by the trash talk. Taking a few steps forward, he calmly said:

"To earn the Thunder Badge, Gym Leader Lt. Surge, I formally challenge you."

"If you want the badge, beat me first!" Lt. Surge crossed his arms arrogantly. "Every day people come here to challenge me, but they're all way too weak. In my eyes, they're nothing but babies who haven't even grown up yet! So tell me—are you another baby too?"

Hearing Lt. Surge's strange accent and tone, Ethan Vale couldn't help frowning slightly.

He truly hadn't expected Lt. Surge to be this arrogant.

"What are the battle rules here?"

Lt. Surge pulled two Poké Balls from his vest using his fingers.

"The rules are simple. No time limit. Defeat these two Pokémon of mine, and your challenge is successful."

As he spoke, he tossed out one of the Poké Balls.

A flash of blue light appeared, and a Pokémon resembling Pikachu—but larger, with a longer tail and darker coloring—appeared on the battlefield.

So it's Raichu…

Pokédex: "Raichu, the Mouse Pokémon. Electric-type. The evolved form of Pikachu. Its electrical attacks can exceed 100,000 volts, enough to knock out opponents dozens of times larger than itself. The more electricity it stores, the more aggressive its personality becomes."

"So, baby," Lt. Surge sneered, "what Pokémon are you using?"

Ethan Vale glanced behind him before replying:

"Kirlia, I'm counting on you."

Kirlia gently nodded and slowly stepped onto the battlefield.

"So it's Kirlia." Being from another region, Lt. Surge naturally recognized it. Even so, he still curled his lip dismissively.

"None of the Pokémon behind you are fully evolved. They're all way too weak. Looks like you're a baby among babies!"

"You're wrong about that," Ethan Vale replied firmly. "In this world, there are no weak Pokémon. The only ones who are truly weak are the Trainers themselves."

"Oh?" Lt. Surge's expression stiffened slightly. "Now that statement earns a little respect from me."

But immediately after, he shouted loudly:

"Still, words alone mean nothing! Raichu, let's go! Use Mega Punch!"

The moment Raichu charged forward, the battle officially began.

Seeing Raichu already making its move, Ethan Vale called out:

"Kirlia, use Magical Leaf!"

Glowing green Magical Leaves formed around Kirlia and rapidly shot toward the charging Raichu.

Seeing this, Lt. Surge didn't order Raichu to dodge. Instead, he changed tactics and roared:

"Raichu, use Thunder Shock!"

Brilliant electricity burst from Raichu's body and collided with Magical Leaf in midair.

With a violent explosion, the Magical Leaves pierced through the electric blast and struck Raichu directly.
