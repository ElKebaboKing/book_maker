After crossing the long suspension bridge, Ethan arrived at the tightly shut front gate of the Fuchsia Gym.

Just as he was about to knock on the door ring, the two wooden doors suddenly swung open on their own without any wind!

But when he looked into the courtyard, there was nobody behind the gate—no staff members coming out to greet him, nor any Pokémon in sight.

Had he triggered some kind of mechanism?

"Hello? Is anyone here!?" Ethan called out loudly.

His voice echoed through the courtyard, but even after waiting for quite some time, there was still no response.

The surroundings were deathly silent, creating an eerie atmosphere, as though every corner carried a hidden killing intent!

"So mysterious… is this really what a ninja's home is like?" Haunter instantly became excited. As a Pokémon that loved pranks more than anything, places like this ranked alongside haunted houses and graveyards among its favorites.

"Maybe this is part of the test as well."

No longer hesitating, Ethan stepped into the Gym. Following a gravel pathway, he arrived at the entrance hall of the main building.

But unexpectedly, the Fuchsia Gym immediately gave him a harsh welcome the moment he stepped onto the entrance floor!

The wooden flooring suddenly rotated, tilting sharply from flat to steep and instantly turning into a smooth, slanted slope!

Fortunately, Ethan had already known this place was filled with traps. In that split second, he reacted quickly and grabbed onto a nearby pillar.

Looking down, he noticed that behind the rotating floor panel, what had once been a neat stone pathway had become worn and uneven over time. Clearly, countless careless Trainers had fallen victim to this trap before him.

To be safe, he opened a Poké Ball and released Kirlia. With Kirlia's psychic powers around, they would be able to handle most dangers.

After steadying himself, Ethan stepped back into the entrance hall.

Passing around a folding screen painted with bamboo, he entered a dimly lit hallway. However, when he reached the end, he found that the path split into two directions—left and right!

The two paths looked completely ordinary, with nothing unusual about them. But in this mysterious ninja residence, who knew how many traps awaited him? Every step required caution.

After a brief moment of thought, Ethan followed his instincts and chose the left path.

His shoes creaked against the wooden floorboards.

After taking another dozen steps, the ground beneath him suddenly vanished as the floor collapsed into a hidden pitfall!

Yet instead of falling in, Ethan was surrounded by psychic energy and gently floated through the air before safely landing on solid ground.

"Well done, Kirlia!"

Patting Kirlia on the head, Ethan approached the edge of the pit and looked down. The bottom was dark, but there were no spikes or deadly traps inside. The walls, however, were smooth and steep. While the fall wasn't enough to injure someone, anyone who fell in would be unable to climb back out.

It seemed Koga's original intention for this trap was merely to teach careless Trainers a small lesson. Quite thoughtful, really!

After crossing the trap, Ethan continued onward. He soon noticed that the floor was no longer level and had begun gradually sloping upward.

Just as he was about to ask Haunter to scout ahead, faint footsteps suddenly echoed from the other side of the hallway wall!

Then came a click as another mechanism activated. The ceiling split open, and a massive smooth boulder dropped from above, rolling directly toward Ethan with the help of the incline!

The boulder was almost as large as the hallway itself. Seeing the enormous stone barreling toward them with overwhelming momentum, Kirlia quickly used Psychic to stop it while Ethan retreated behind the pitfall to safety.

Once Kirlia released its hold, the boulder continued rolling forward while Kirlia used Teleport to return to Ethan's side.

Boom!

With a heavy thud, the boulder crashed into the pit below.

If it had been anyone else without psychic powers or an incredibly strong Pokémon, that boulder alone would have been enough to flatten a challenger!

"This trap is a little… too dangerous, isn't it?"

Before he could finish marveling at it, the footsteps behind the wall sounded again, rapidly moving farther away as though someone was fleeing.

Seeing this, Ethan quickly signaled for Haunter to pursue them.

Haunter slipped into the wall. A short while later, eerie cackling laughter echoed from the other side, followed immediately by the shrill scream of a girl!

"Ahhh!!"

The next second, part of the wall suddenly rotated open, and a girl stumbled out from within.

Her appearance was fairly ordinary. She wore a typical black ninja bodysuit along with a crimson-pink cape. Her purple hair was tied into a massive ponytail.

Meeting Ethan's gaze, the girl awkwardly smiled and coughed lightly.

"Since you discovered me, it seems my training is still far from complete."

At first glance, Ethan didn't recognize her and politely asked,

"Sorry for barging into the Gym. I came here to challenge Gym Leader Koga. May I ask if he's here?"

Crossing her arms, the girl replied,

"If you want to challenge my father, you'll have to defeat me first!"

"Your father? Then that means…"

"That's right. My name is Janine, and I'm his daughter!" Janine looked Ethan up and down before gesturing for him to follow her. "Alright, come with me. I'll take you to the battlefield."

The two of them moved deeper into the Gym, bypassing several more traps before finally arriving at a simple battle arena.

The battlefield was located inside a spacious room and was extremely minimalistic. But most unbelievable of all, the floor was entirely made of wooden planks!

Since it was wood, Fire-, Ground-, and Rock-type attacks could easily destroy the battlefield. Was it really okay to battle here?

Standing on opposite Trainer platforms, Ethan and Janine faced each other.

Pointing toward a door behind her, Janine said seriously,

"As long as you defeat me, you'll be allowed to continue through that door."

Ethan shrugged casually.

"Alright then. If that's the Gym's rule, I accept. So what are the battle rules?"

"One-on-one. Whoever's Pokémon falls first loses!"

As she spoke, Janine casually threw a Poké Ball.

"Come out, Crobat!"

A purple bat-like Pokémon with two pairs of wings—one large and one small—appeared on the battlefield.

Pokédex:

"Crobat, the Bat Pokémon. A Poison- and Flying-type. The final evolution of Zubat. They usually inhabit dark caves and emit ultrasonic waves inaudible to humans. Although they appear fierce, they are actually quite gentle. As long as their daytime sleep isn't disturbed, they rarely become angry."

Ethan turned to Haunter and asked,

"You've never fought in a Gym battle before. Want to give it a try?"

(End of Chapter)
