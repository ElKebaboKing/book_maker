Ethan Vale gently shook Chloe Cerise awake.

"Hey, Chloe, it's morning. Wake up!"

"Huh? It's already morning?" Chloe rubbed her sleepy eyes. "That's strange... Why am I sleeping here? I clearly remember... watching your battle with them yesterday."

The moment she fully woke up, she saw Ethan's face.

"Ethan? Why did you draw all over your face? You're already an adult—why would you do something so childish?"

"...Childish, my foot!"

Ethan pulled out a mirror and handed it to her.

"Take a look. It's not just me—you've got drawings on your face too."

"What!?"

The moment Chloe saw her reflection, she cried out in surprise. She quickly pulled a pack of wet wipes from her backpack and frantically wiped the doodles off her face.

"What happened?"

With a helpless smile, Ethan explained everything about the mischievous Jigglypuff. Afterwards, he woke each of his Pokémon one by one before turning his attention to Aya and the Samurai Boy.

The two of them were still sleeping soundly.

Not wanting to get caught up in another challenge, Ethan simply ignored them. He recalled all of his Pokémon, called Chloe over, and quietly left without making a sound.

Not long after they departed, Aya slowly woke up.

She sat upright, dazed for a moment before suddenly remembering what had happened yesterday.

Looking around, she found no sign of Ethan. Instead, her eyes landed on her Venomoth and the Samurai Boy.

Both of their faces were covered in ridiculous doodles.

She froze.

Then, as if realizing something, she hurriedly took out a mirror and looked at herself.

Her usually alluring face had been completely vandalized.

Two thick black circles surrounded her eyes, and a huge black nose had been drawn on her face, making her resemble a clumsy panda.

Could... Ethan have done this!?

He always looked so serious. Who would have imagined he had such a mischievous side?

And drawing all over someone else's face...

What kind of strange hobby is that!?

A chilly breeze swept through the forest.

Already furious, Aya suddenly felt a cool draft across her chest.

She looked down and discovered that her collar had somehow become disheveled.

Could it be...

Besides drawing on my face... did he do something inappropriate while I was asleep!?

Although the last thing she remembered was hearing Jigglypuff's song, deep down she simply refused to believe that a tiny Jigglypuff possessed such overwhelming power.

That scoundrel... actually took advantage of me!?

Grinding her teeth, Aya knew that after witnessing Ethan's strength yesterday, she wasn't capable of defeating him yet.

It seemed she would have to find a place to devote herself to training.

Straightening her clothes, she silently swore:

Ethan Vale! Just you wait, you bastard!

One day I'll make you repay everything—with interest!

I absolutely won't forgive you!!

"Achoo!"

As Ethan and Chloe continued westward, they had barely left Viridian Forest when Ethan suddenly sneezed.

Chloe looked over with concern.

"What's wrong? Did sleeping on the ground give you a cold?"

Rubbing his nose, Ethan shook his head.

"My health is far too good to catch a cold. Someone must be thinking about me."

"You're so full of yourself."

Chloe covered her mouth and giggled.

"Who would randomly be thinking about you?"

After passing through Viridian City, they finally reached the Indigo Plateau before noon.

Although it was called a plateau, its elevation wasn't particularly high. It only appeared elevated because of the surrounding basin.

The climate was pleasant, making it an ideal place to live.

However, because of its remote location, only a small town had been built there.

After passing through the outskirts and entering town, Ethan recalled the information he had researched online before coming.

Normally, the permanent population was relatively small.

Without towering skyscrapers, life here moved at a relaxed pace, and the beautiful scenery and fresh air made it an excellent place for retirees.

Today, however, was completely different.

The streets were overflowing with visitors arriving from every corner of the region.

"Wow... there are so many people..."

Chloe couldn't help but marvel.

Afraid of being separated by the crowd, she stayed close behind Ethan.

Walking through town, Ethan could already feel the excitement in the air.

Banners, advertisements, and conversations everywhere revolved around the upcoming Indigo League Conference.

Only during this time each year did the normally peaceful town become so vibrant.

Following the enthusiastic crowds, the two of them easily found the Athletes' Village prepared for League competitors without even needing a map.

Although it was called a village, it actually consisted of several large buildings and over a hundred two-story cottages built beside a scenic lake.

The surroundings were beautiful.

Restaurants, fitness centers, cinemas, a Pokémon Center, and every other facility Trainers might need were all conveniently available.

When they entered the reception hall, a female staff member immediately approached them.

"Welcome. Are you here to participate in the League Conference?"

After Ethan nodded, she smiled politely.

"Then may I please see your credentials?"

Ethan handed over his Pokédex, along with his badge case containing all eight Gym Badges.

After verifying both his identity and the authenticity of his badges, she entered his information into the computer.

"Welcome, Mr. Ethan Vale. You are now officially registered as a competitor in this year's Indigo League Conference."

She returned everything to him along with a thin handbook.

"This is the official tournament rulebook. It contains the battle regulations and all other important information regarding the League. Please keep it somewhere safe."

She continued,

"The opening ceremony will be held the day after tomorrow. However, tomorrow there will be a Torch Relay. If you're interested, you may register to participate."

Seeing that Ethan hadn't made a decision yet, she continued checking his information.

"Now, let me confirm your accommodations. You only have one companion, correct?"

Glancing at Chloe, Ethan nodded.

"Yes. Just the two of us."

After entering the information into the computer, she handed him two keys.

Since there were only two of them, they were assigned an entire two-story cottage.

Following the building numbers through the residential area, they quickly located their assigned accommodation.

The League treated its competitors extremely well.

Not only were all meals provided free of charge, but every competitor also received a private cottage.

Standing outside the front door and looking into the distance, Ethan and Chloe could just make out the magnificent Indigo Stadium—the grand arena where the entire world's attention would soon be focused.
