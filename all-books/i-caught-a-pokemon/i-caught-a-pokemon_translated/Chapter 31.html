As the Poké Balls opened, Misty released her Goldeen and Starmie, while Ethan and Ash each sent out their Squirtle. The four Pokémon dove underwater together and simultaneously used Tackle against a nearby ship window.

With repeated impacts, the window gradually cracked before finally shattering into a large hole wide enough for two people to pass through side by side.

And because the opening was already completely submerged underwater, no additional seawater rushed in, nor did it affect the air pressure inside the ship.

After the Pokémon returned to the group, Brock took several clotheslines from his backpack and tied everyone securely to the Pokémon.

"Hey, hey!" Jessie cried anxiously when she saw that everyone else had a Pokémon assigned to them. "What about us!? We don't have any Water-type Pokémon!"

At that moment, Ethan released his Magikarp.

In the anime, Team Rocket had escaped the sunken ship thanks to this same Magikarp. Unexpectedly, even after changing owners, it still could not escape that fate.

James lowered his head and stared at the lively Magikarp, immediately recognizing it.

"Ah! Isn't that the Magikarp from that shady salesman!?"

Ethan nodded lightly. "That's right. I bought it for 500."

"That scumbag!" James clenched his fists angrily. "After talking it up so much, it really was only worth 500 bucks!"

"Alright," Ethan said to Team Rocket, "this Magikarp will take you out of here. But let me warn you first—it looks pretty dumb, so it might not understand human language very well. If something goes wrong halfway through, I'm not taking responsibility."

"That's right," Misty added. "And don't even think about pulling any tricks. Otherwise, we'll leave you behind and let you fend for yourselves!"

"Don't worry!" the Team Rocket trio hurriedly shook their heads. They looked weak, pitiful, and utterly lacking confidence. "In a life-or-death situation like this, how could we possibly cause trouble!?"

"That's right, meow~"

Even so, Ethan pointed at Meowth and said, "Just in case, Meowth, you're coming with me."

Meowth froze. "Huh? Why meow!?"

"Idiot, obviously because you're the hostage," Ethan replied. Then he turned toward Jessie and James. "Once we get back to the surface, if you want Meowth back, then take good care of my Magikarp. Otherwise, I'll just use him to settle the debt."

Hearing that he had suddenly become a hostage, Meowth stared at Ethan in horror, while Jessie muttered quietly, "Why does this guy seem even more like a villain than us!?"

After everything had been arranged, Ethan recalled Kirlia into its Poké Ball and tied Meowth securely around his waist.

Brock then picked up several empty plastic bottles and handed them to everyone.

"I don't know whether this method will really work, but it's better to be safe than sorry. Everyone should take one."

At that moment, Ethan took out his phone and snapped a few pictures around them. Although the situation was unfortunate, it was still an experience most people would only encounter once in a lifetime—an incredibly rare memory.

Afterward, he sealed his phone, Pokédex, and wallet inside a waterproof plastic pouch. Once he tightly sealed the bag, he wrapped it thoroughly in a raincoat before stuffing it into his backpack.

Finally, he put on his diving goggles and took out his underwater respirator.

Although Ethan could swim, he was not especially skilled at it. He could neither swim very fast nor open his eyes underwater like the others.

This underwater respirator was essentially just a portable mini oxygen tank. Under normal conditions, the compressed air inside could provide about three minutes of oxygen, though Ethan had no idea how long it would actually last at this depth.

Meanwhile, everyone else held empty bottles in their mouths. The sight looked extremely ridiculous, and as they glanced at one another, their eyes were filled with amusement.

Seeing that everyone was ready, Ethan placed the respirator in his mouth and gave an "OK" hand signal before diving underwater together with the others.

Below the surface, the ocean currents were relatively calm. With Squirtle pulling him along, Ethan barely needed to exert any effort as his body was dragged rapidly through the water.

They swam another dozen meters down the corridor before finally spotting the shattered window the Pokémon had broken earlier.

One by one, they exited the ship, then angled upward toward the ocean surface.

Sunlight filtered through the seawater, leaving behind blurry rays of light. Visibility around them was poor. Beyond the few meters surrounding the group, everything else was pitch black.

And beneath the faint glow, Ethan could clearly see Misty's pale legs kicking back and forth right in front of him.

She really looks like a mermaid...

At that moment, the bottles in everyone's mouths gradually became deformed due to the pressure of the seawater, the force of the currents, and their own breathing. Left with no choice, they each took a deep breath before discarding the bottles one after another.

After swimming upward several more meters, Ethan glanced behind him and suddenly noticed that somehow Jessie's and James's ropes had become tangled together. Their bodies were spinning continuously, creating resistance against Magikarp's movement!

Seeing this, Ethan quickly tugged on the rope tied around his waist. Squirtle looked back, understood Ethan's hand signal, and immediately swam back toward Jessie and James.

Ethan reached out with both hands, trying to untangle the ropes, but they had become wound together too tightly. No matter how hard he pulled, he could not separate them.

Just then, a figure darted beside him—it was Misty. Seeing the others falling behind, she had also come to help.

Even with the two of them working together, they still could not untangle the ropes. In the end, Meowth finally lost patience and used his sharp claws to cut the rope around James's waist, freeing them from the mess.

Unfortunately, during the struggle, Jessie accidentally kicked Misty in the stomach.

A sharp pain shot through Misty's body, and instinctively she opened her mouth. Instantly, streams of bubbles escaped from her lips.

Realizing Misty had lost her air, Ethan immediately removed the underwater respirator from his own mouth and handed it to her.

Without hesitation, Misty grabbed it, placed it into her mouth, and took a deep breath.

That single action quite literally saved her life.

After returning the respirator to Ethan, Misty gave him a thumbs-up, signaling that she was alright.

Ethan nodded, waved toward Squirtle, and together with Misty grabbed one of James's arms each. With the help of the two Pokémon, they once again swam upward.

After the earlier accident, the rest of the journey went smoothly. The light above them grew brighter and brighter, and finally, after swimming upward for another dozen meters, they burst through the ocean surface and completely escaped danger.

(End of Chapter)
