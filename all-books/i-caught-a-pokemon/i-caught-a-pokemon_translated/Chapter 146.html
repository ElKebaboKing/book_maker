After exchanging a few pleasantries, only Ethan Vale and Alexa remained in the living room.

Ash and the others had already returned to their own dormitory, while Chloe Cerise voluntarily went upstairs to give them some privacy. Meanwhile, Misa, Masha, and the others stood guard outside to keep other reporters from interrupting.

Ethan sat calmly on the sofa. Once Alexa finished adjusting her equipment and setting up the lights, the exclusive interview officially began.

"Mr. Ethan, could you first give us a brief introduction about yourself?"

After Ethan nodded, Alexa held a voice recorder toward him.

"My name is Ethan Vale. I'm twenty years old and come from Asame Town in the Kalos region. I've just completed my training journey through the Kanto region and am currently challenging the Indigo League."

Alexa took out a notebook and continued asking questions according to the list she had prepared.

Ethan answered each one with ease. None of the questions were difficult or overly personal, making it clear she had thoroughly prepared before coming.

Alexa then asked,

"Since entering the tournament, you've won every battle with remarkable ease. Do you have any special secret to your success?"

Ethan shook his head.

"There's really no secret. I simply treat every battle seriously and make proper use of type advantages. I also haven't encountered any exceptionally strong opponents yet, so things have gone fairly smoothly."

"What inspired you to become a Pokémon Trainer?"

After thinking for a moment, Ethan replied,

"There are many reasons. Ever since I was little, I've loved Pokémon. But more than anything, there's one person I must sincerely thank."

"Oh?" Alexa asked curiously. "Who is it? Your parents? A friend? Or perhaps... your girlfriend?"

"None of those."

Ethan smiled.

"It's Champion Diantha."

"Diantha?"

Alexa's eyes immediately lit up with interest.

"And why would you thank her?"

"She was the one who encouraged me to become a Trainer. In fact, she even gave me my first Pokémon. I also trained under her for a while, learning how to care for Pokémon and how to battle. Without everything she taught me, I don't think I would have come this far."

Alexa's eyes sparkled.

This was huge news!

She had never imagined Ethan actually knew Diantha personally—or that they shared such a close connection.

As one of Kalos's biggest celebrities, every move Diantha made attracted enormous attention. Once this story was published, it would undoubtedly cause a sensation.

She had already thought of the headline.

"The Man Chosen by Diantha Travels Across the Sea to Conquer the Indigo League!"

Filled with excitement, she quickly asked several more related questions.

When the interview finally ended, she pulled Ethan to his feet.

"Come on! There's just one last thing. We need to take some photos!"

"We still have to take pictures?"

"Of course."

Alexa took out her camera and guided Ethan in front of a plain white wall.

"This is an exclusive interview. Besides the video and article, your photos are just as important. You have a great appearance. Once these pictures are published, I'm sure plenty of girls will become your fans."

"It doesn't really matter to me."

Ethan waved his hand before asking,

"By the way, where will this interview be published?"

"Naturally, it'll be sent back to Kalos for publication," Alexa replied with a smile. "Besides the online video, it'll also appear in our newspaper and magazine."

After adjusting the focus, Alexa first took several solo portraits of Ethan.

Satisfied with the results, she then asked him to release some of his Pokémon.

Since the living room wasn't large enough, Ethan didn't send out Gyarados, Blastoise, or Charizard. Instead, he released Gardevoir, Haunter, and Jolteon, posing with them under Alexa's guidance for several group photos.

As expected of an all-around journalist, Alexa's photography skills were excellent. Even with only a few simple poses, she managed to make Ethan look exceptionally handsome.

The next morning, Ethan, Ash, and the others arrived at the drawing hall for the fourth time.

This time, they returned to the very first registration desk they had used.

"Welcome back."

The cheerful staff member once again congratulated them on advancing.

After exchanging a few polite words with her, Ash eagerly stepped forward to draw his next opponent.

Since this would be the final Preliminary Round, everyone had already guessed what battlefield they would receive. The only remaining mystery was the identity of their opponent.

Sure enough, Ash drew the Grass Field, and his opponent turned out to be a Trainer named Shawn, from Maiden's Peak Town.

The moment Brock saw Shawn's photo, he was instantly captivated by her beauty.

After admiring the picture for a while, he eagerly explained,

"Did you know? Maiden's Peak Town is famous! It wasn't originally called that, but because it's been known since ancient times for producing beautiful girls, people gradually started calling it Maiden's Peak Town."

"I see..."

Misty rolled her eyes.

"I really don't get you. You're a Gym Leader, yet you seem to know far more gossip than actual Pokémon facts."

After Ash finished his draw, it was Ethan's turn.

He pressed the button.

As the spinning light slowly came to a stop, the Ice Field appeared on the screen.

At the same time, another portrait appeared beside it.

Gary Oak.

"It... it's Gary!?"

Everyone stared in surprise.

Although they all knew that familiar faces would eventually have to battle one another, actually seeing the result still caught them completely off guard.

What none of them knew was that Ethan's opponent had already been predetermined by John. No matter when he pressed the button, the outcome would always have been the same.

Footsteps echoed throughout the hall.

Then a familiar voice sounded from behind them.

"Looks like I've finally found an opponent worthy of me."

Everyone turned around.

Standing there was Gary Oak.

Behind him followed more than a dozen cheerful young women dressed in matching cheerleader uniforms, waving colorful pom-poms as they supported him.

Compared to Gary's polished cheerleading squad, the tattooed bikers surrounding Ethan looked completely outclassed.

Looking directly at Ethan, Gary smiled confidently.

"I still remember the first time we met outside Saffron City. Back then, you wanted to battle me, but I turned you down. It seems fate had other plans. This battle was inevitable after all."

He grinned.

"Now we'll finally find out... which one of us is truly stronger."
