In the end, Team Rocket retreated.

They left with remarkably calm expressions—if one ignored the gritted teeth and dark looks on every face.

They had come with great expectations and left in complete defeat.

Looking around the battlefield, the brief but intense clash had left the bridge heavily damaged. The roadway was riddled with craters, and parts of the structure exposed the steel and concrete underneath. Fortunately, the bridge had not collapsed.

Still, its completion date was undoubtedly going to be delayed.

After recalling all of his Pokémon except Haunter and Gardevoir, Ethan Vale took out his phone and called Looker.

As soon as the call connected, he briefly explained what had happened.

"They weren't lying. This really was a retaliation operation," Looker sighed from the other end. "And you're not the only target. Several International Police facilities have been attacked as well. Even Lance and Agatha were ambushed yesterday."

Ethan immediately asked,

"What about Sabrina and Erika?"

"Hey, hey, hey!" Looker grumbled unhappily. "We've known each other for quite a while now. You call me, and the first thing you do isn't even ask how I'm doing?"

"Ah, sorry, sorry!" Ethan replied quickly. "Then were you attacked?"

"Of course not!"

"..."

Ethan felt as though something was stuck in his throat.

Rolling his eyes, he said,

"Then why are you wasting my time? Can you be serious for once at a moment like this?"

The reason they could joke around like this was simple. After working together so many times, they had gone from strangers to genuine friends.

"Haha!" Looker laughed. "Don't worry. Since Gym Leaders stay in cities, where there are plenty of people and police nearby, Team Rocket hasn't targeted them. Assassinations there would be too risky."

Hearing that, Ethan relaxed somewhat.

Still, he planned to call them later just to make sure.

Then Looker added,

"You seem awfully concerned about those two. You've gotten pretty close to them without saying a word. Don't tell me you have ulterior motives?"

"What business is that of yours?" Ethan snorted. "You're the one who dragged them into the conflict with Team Rocket in the first place. If they got hurt because of that, anyone would feel guilty."

The two continued chatting about various things.

Just before Ethan was about to hang up, he suddenly remembered something.

"Oh, right. The bridge got damaged during the battle. Wouldn't it be bad if I just left?"

"Huh? How badly damaged is it?"

Looker's tone immediately became miserable.

"Sigh... Even though the International Police is an international organization, the Kanto branch doesn't get much funding. Looks like we'll be tightening our belts again."

After complaining for a while, he finally returned to the topic.

"Don't worry about it. Just leave. I'll send people to handle the aftermath."

After ending the call, Ethan heard approaching footsteps.

Turning around, he found Masha and the other biker gang members cautiously walking toward him.

Masha rubbed his hands together, wearing an expression that was both flattering and eager.

"You are absolutely incredible!"

You?

These opportunistic guys had wanted nothing more than to stay far away earlier. Now even their way of addressing him had changed.

After some thought, Ethan decided there was no longer any need to hide his identity.

They had already seen his face, after all.

Removing his mask, he hesitated before saying,

"About who I really am—"

Masha immediately nodded.

"Don't worry! We absolutely won't reveal your identity! Nothing that happened today will ever leak out!"

"That's right!"

The others nodded frantically and pounded their chests.

"We'll protect your secret!"

Their promise was certainly welcome.

Though Ethan wasn't entirely sure how trustworthy these people actually were.

He waved his hand.

"Stop calling me 'sir.' It sounds strange. As for the bicycle, Team Rocket destroyed it earlier, so I can't return it."

"Boss, please don't worry about that!" Masha replied instantly. "It's just a bicycle! Even if it's destroyed, it's no big deal! If you want, we'll give you all the others too!"

"B-Boss!?"

"Of course!" Masha's eyes sparkled. "To be honest, after watching the video of your battle against the giant Tentacruel, we already thought you were unbelievably cool! And today, seeing Zapdos and Articuno with our own eyes completely blew us away!"

Not only Masha, but even the normally cool Misa had turned into a full-fledged fangirl.

"Masha's right!"

She nodded vigorously.

"A Trainer who owns two Legendary Pokémon like that is unquestionably powerful! Our admiration for you stretches across our lives just like this bridge! Right now, all I feel is pure respect!"

The other bikers shouted in unison.

"Please accept us, Boss!!"

Ethan felt a headache coming on.

The contrast between their current behavior and their earlier attitudes was so extreme that he almost thought they'd been possessed.

"I appreciate you lending me a bicycle," Ethan said, "but I have no interest in taking followers."

With that, he turned and prepared to leave.

Then he heard a series of thuds behind him.

Looking back, he saw every biker kneeling with their hands clasped together, looking pitiful.

"Boss!"

Ethan sighed.

"Sorry, but I'm not running a gang. Besides, didn't you already make Jessie and James your boss and boss lady?"

Misa shook her head.

"When we were younger, those two were local legends. They were always doing outrageous things and attracting attention, so we called them that out of habit. But that's ancient history now."

"Exactly," Masha agreed. "We're not their followers. And now that they've joined Team Rocket, we definitely don't want anything to do with them!"

Ethan still shook his head.

"I'm just a Pokémon Trainer. What would I even do with a bunch of followers?"

"What's wrong with that?" Misa hurriedly said. "If you enter the Pokémon League, we'll be your loyal fan club! If you fight Team Rocket, we'll gladly fight alongside you!"

Sigh...

These people were unbelievably persistent.

And they clearly possessed the kind of determination that wouldn't stop until they got what they wanted.

Then again, biker gangs were basically groups of idle troublemakers.

Naturally, they admired people stronger than themselves.

Seeing Ethan beginning to waver, Masha quickly asked,

"Then, Boss... could you at least tell us your name?"

( End of Chapter )
