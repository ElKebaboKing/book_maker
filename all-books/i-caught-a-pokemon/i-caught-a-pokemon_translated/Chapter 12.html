Heading to Professor Oak's Laboratory

"Kirlia, use Magical Leaf!"

Glowing green Magical Leaves formed in Kirlia's hands and continuously flew toward Sandshrew.

Seeing this, Damian flicked his whip with a sharp crack, and upon hearing the sound, Sandshrew rapidly dug into the ground with its claws, avoiding the attack entirely.

Having lost its target, Kirlia stood cautiously in place, carefully observing the ground beneath its feet.

After waiting a moment, a hole suddenly appeared beside it, and without warning, Sandshrew burst out from underground and charged directly at Kirlia!

Fortunately, Kirlia had remained vigilant. The instant the earth split open, it used Teleport and narrowly avoided the attack.

"You think that's the end of our offense?" Damian snapped his whip once again and shouted loudly, "Sandshrew, use our ultimate move—Fissure!"

Immediately, Sandshrew, whose previous attack had missed, leaped high into the air. Its body radiated an intense yellow-brown glow as it spun rapidly downward headfirst with its right claw extended toward the ground.

The next second, accompanied by a tremendous cracking sound, the battlefield split apart violently from the point where Sandshrew's claw struck the earth!

Massive fissures spread across the entire arena like endless abysses, tearing the ground apart amid terrifying tremors.

Fissure—a Ground-type one-hit knockout move.

If a Pokémon accidentally fell into the cracks created by the attack, the crushing force inside could inflict devastating damage capable of knocking out nearly any Pokémon instantly.

"Kirlia! Get out of there!" Ethan Vale shouted.

Kirlia used Teleport more than a dozen times in rapid succession—disappearing, reappearing, vanishing again, and searching desperately for safe footing. Only after expending over half its stamina did it finally escape the epicenter and retreat safely into a corner.

"What!?"

This outcome completely exceeded Damian's expectations.

Although Fissure had low accuracy, aside from Flying-type Pokémon, no Pokémon had ever successfully avoided this large-scale attack before, despite the many times he had used it.

Seeing that Fissure had stopped and Kirlia remained unharmed, Ethan Vale quietly let out a sigh of relief.

Thankfully, he had the foresight not to send out Bulbasaur or Squirtle. With their movement speed, they would have definitely lost immediately against Fissure.

After unleashing such tremendous power, however, Sandshrew itself was affected by the recoil and fell into a brief moment of sluggishness.

Seizing the opportunity, Ethan Vale decisively ordered:

"Kirlia, use Magical Leaf!"

Kirlia raised both hands, and before Sandshrew could react, countless Magical Leaves surged forward like a raging storm.

Grass beats Ground — it was super effective!

Sandshrew's body swayed repeatedly before its eyes finally rolled back as it collapsed onto the battlefield.

"Sandshrew!!"

Damian rushed over and caught Sandshrew in his arms. Even now, he still couldn't believe he had lost.

He had already achieved ninety-two consecutive victories. Just eight more wins, and he could have left to challenge official Gyms!

Damn it…

Seeing his victory, Ethan Vale applauded sincerely.

"That was an excellent battle. Your Sandshrew is incredibly strong—the strongest opponent I've faced since beginning my journey!"

After returning Sandshrew to its Poké Ball, Damian remained silent. When he finally stood up again, the confidence on his face had completely disappeared.

"I lost. I truly didn't expect anyone to defeat my Sandshrew." Damian let out a heavy sigh, but soon regained his determination. "But even so, I won't abandon my dream. Starting today, I'll begin again from zero and continue defending this Gym until I achieve one hundred consecutive victories. And after that, I'll find you again and challenge you once more!"

"I'll look forward to that day." Ethan Vale extended his hand sincerely. "It's because of strong opponents like you that I'm able to keep improving and continue moving forward."

After parting ways with Damian, Ethan Vale originally planned to continue toward Pewter City and challenge the Pewter Gym.

However, a sudden phone call from Professor Cerise brought him back to Vermilion City.

Inside the Cerise Laboratory, Professor Cerise apologized with an embarrassed expression.

"I'm really sorry for calling you back from so far away. There's an important matter that urgently requires your help."

"No need to apologize, Professor." Ethan Vale waved it off casually. "I was already near Vermilion City anyway, so it wasn't far at all."

At that moment, Ren stepped forward carrying a small box and handed it to Ethan Vale.

"What's this?" Ethan Vale asked curiously.

Professor Cerise explained:

"Inside is a prototype precision instrument. Since it hasn't completed development yet, it's considered confidential scientific equipment, so we can't send it through normal delivery services. Unfortunately, because of our work, none of us can leave the laboratory right now. That's why we can only ask someone we trust to deliver it to Professor Oak's Laboratory and hand it directly to Professor Oak."

Ethan Vale nodded seriously.

"I understand. As a special research investigator for the Cerise Laboratory, I'll make sure it reaches Professor Oak safely and intact!"

Leaving Vermilion City behind, Ethan Vale traveled quickly, bypassing the famous Viridian Forest. By nightfall, he had already arrived in Viridian City.

Since it was already late, he planned to rest overnight at the Pokémon Center before heading to Pallet Town the next morning.

Before entering the Pokémon Center, however, he first visited a nearby shop to restock canned food and buy several Poké Balls in case of emergencies.

While checking out, he unexpectedly received a call from Chloe Cerise.

"Hello, Chloe? What's up?"

On the other side of the phone, Chloe said:

"Didn't you message this morning saying you were returning to Vermilion City? I wanted to see Kirlia's new appearance. It must've become even prettier, right?"

"Ah, sorry about that." Ethan Vale glanced at the clock hanging on the wall. "But after arriving at the laboratory this morning, I immediately left again. I'm already in Viridian City now."

"Viridian City?" Chloe exclaimed in surprise. "How did you get there so quickly?"

"I had something important to do, so I needed to visit Professor Oak's Laboratory. Anyway, it's already late, and I still have things to take care of. You should get some rest too."

After hanging up the phone, Ethan Vale was about to leave the store when one of the shop employees suddenly called out to him.

"Excuse me, sir, please wait a moment."

Ethan Vale looked at him in confusion.

"Is something wrong?"

The employee took out a package from behind the counter.

"Sorry, I accidentally overheard your conversation just now. Are you heading to Professor Oak's Laboratory in Pallet Town?"

After Ethan Vale nodded, the employee smiled happily.

"Ah, that's wonderful! This package was ordered by Professor Oak several days ago, but we've been far too busy to deliver it. Would you mind bringing it along with you on your way there?"

(End of Chapter)
