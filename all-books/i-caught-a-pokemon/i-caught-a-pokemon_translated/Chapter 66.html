Celadon City.

At 9:00 AM, two black sedans pulled up one after another outside the Celadon Game Corner.

Traffic flowed endlessly through the streets, and pedestrians hurried along with their own business, barely sparing the suddenly parked cars a second glance.

"Is it time yet?" asked a young man wearing a Gengar mask in a low voice from the passenger seat.

The driver was a somewhat frail-looking man. He glanced at the time on the dashboard and nodded.

"Right on schedule."

"Should we move?"

"No. We still need to wait for the signal. Rocket Team has informants everywhere around here, so everyone stay alert."

"Understood."

"Hey, hey!" Erika listened to their conversation with a darkened expression. Helplessly, she said, "Could you two please use a normal tone of voice? Don't make it sound like we're undercover agents exchanging secret codes!"

"Ah, sorry!" Handsome Guy scratched his head awkwardly. "It's mainly because Ethan's mask makes him look way too suspicious. In this atmosphere, I just naturally got into character."

Ethan glanced at Handsome Guy and spoke seriously.

"Officer Handsome Guy, please don't say such things."

"Hm? What's wrong?" Handsome Guy froze.

Why did he suddenly become so serious?

Was it because I said he didn't look like a good person?

Ethan declared word by word:

"During the operation, please do not call me by my name. Refer to me only by my codename—Player!"

"I…" Handsome Guy felt a surge of frustration rise into his chest. "I thought you were about to say something important, but you were just obsessing over your codename!?"

"Of course." Ethan nodded matter-of-factly. "It sounds much more professional that way!"

In the back seat, Erika covered her mouth and laughed softly, while even Sabrina's eyes carried a trace of amusement.

At that moment, a sudden explosion echoed from outside the city. It was followed by a rapid series of sounds like firecrackers going off nonstop. Though distant, the gunfire and artillery could still be heard clearly.

Pedestrians on the street all turned toward the outskirts of the city, but with skyscrapers blocking the view, they could see nothing.

"That's the signal, right?" Ethan asked.

Handsome Guy nodded and checked his watch.

"Alright. Time's up. Begin the operation!"

With Handsome Guy's order, everyone stepped out of the car simultaneously. Behind them, Lance, Agatha, and Nanu also emerged from the second sedan.

With solemn expressions, the group strode directly into the Game Corner.

Inside, numerous customers were absorbed in various arcade games. Upon seeing this intimidating group of uninvited guests storm in, they all cast surprised looks their way.

Ignoring the crowd, Ethan and the others walked into a hallway. When they reached a staircase leading to the second floor, instead of going upstairs, Handsome Guy began feeling around the wall. Soon, hidden behind a poster, he found a concealed switch.

The moment he pressed it, the floor suddenly split open, revealing a staircase leading underground!

At long last, Rocket Team's secret base, hidden for many years, was finally exposed to the world!

Two Rocket Team members disguised as store employees hurried over after hearing the commotion. The instant they saw the hidden entrance exposed, their expressions changed drastically.

"This is bad! Intruders got in from this side too!"

But before they could raise the alarm, Lance and Nanu rushed forward, grabbed them by the throat, and knocked them unconscious with effortless punches.

After casually tossing them into a corner, the group descended into the base, only to discover just how enormous the place truly was. This was far beyond some small-time hideout.

The entire underground facility was brightly lit and filled with advanced technology. Red alarm lights flashed nonstop along the walls, though not because of Ethan's group.

Rather, the International Police special forces were currently launching a full-scale assault on another entrance outside Celadon City, which had triggered the alarms and drawn the majority of Rocket Team's personnel toward the frontlines.

"Excellent. Everything's proceeding smoothly," Handsome Guy said. "Next, we follow the original plan and split up!"

Everyone nodded, releasing their Pokémon before dividing into two groups and rushing down opposite hallways.

Although they still lacked a complete layout of the base, basic reasoning suggested the most important areas would be the control room, research lab, archive room, armory, and power station. To prevent Rocket Team from destroying crucial data, they had to secure those locations first.

The teams had already been assigned earlier during the car ride. Ethan, Handsome Guy, Erika, and Sabrina advanced together, overwhelming every Rocket Team member they encountered along the way.

However, the deeper they went, the fiercer the resistance became.

Not only were Rocket Team grunts armed with firearms, but groups of Pokémon also suddenly emerged from behind them to launch sneak attacks!

"Damn it! There are way too many of them!" Handsome Guy barely dodged an Acid Spray attack. Looking at the wall melting from the corrosive poison, he wiped cold sweat from his forehead.

"Not only are there a lot of them, but they're much tougher than the ordinary Rocket Team grunts outside!"

As he spoke, Ethan directed Haunter to use Shadow Ball, knocking out several Pokémon and finally clearing this wave of enemies.

"Look over here!" Erika suddenly pointed at a massive security door. "There are computers and all sorts of machines behind it. This looks like the base's research lab!"

Hearing her words, Handsome Guy stepped up to the reinforced door. After unsuccessfully attempting to open it, he peered through the thick specialized glass.

"You're right. This definitely is where they develop their technology and equipment! But this door's way too thick—we can't get it open!"

"Leave it to me."

The usually silent Sabrina stepped forward. Her eyes began glowing faintly, and under the force of her psychic power, the seemingly indestructible blast-proof door was forcibly pried open!

Inside the research lab, everything had already fallen into chaos. Papers littered the floor everywhere, along with shattered coffee mugs.

It was obvious that once the special forces attacked the main entrance, the researchers had fled in panic without leaving themselves any opportunity to clean up.

"Damn it! Were we still too late!?" Handsome Guy gritted his teeth as he rushed to a computer terminal, attempting to recover any important files that might have been left behind.

Meanwhile, Ethan wandered around the laboratory for a bit. He noticed that nearly all the machines were still running, proving just how hurried and desperate the researchers' evacuation had been.

Just as he turned around, a strangely designed pair of goggles suddenly caught his eye.

What is this thing?

Picking them up, he noticed that the entire device was white, while the lenses emitted a faint blue glow.

Curious, Ethan put the goggles on and looked around the room.

Surprisingly, they had neither telescopic functions nor microscopic or x-ray capabilities. Everything appeared exactly as it normally should.

Just as he was wondering what their purpose could possibly be, Ethan turned his head toward Haunter—

And suddenly, something astonishing appeared before his eyes!
