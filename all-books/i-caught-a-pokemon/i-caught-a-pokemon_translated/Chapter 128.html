The moment Charizard appeared, its imposing presence immediately overshadowed Butterfree.

It landed beside Ethan, releasing a burst of flames from its mouth as it fixed its sharp gaze on its opponent.

During the final stretch of training before the Indigo League, nearly all of Ethan's Pokémon had reached their final evolutionary forms. Only Dratini and Haunter had yet to evolve.

Seeing both Pokémon ready for battle, Chloe looked around uneasily and asked,

"Ethan, is it really okay to use Charizard here? This forest is so dense... won't it start a wildfire?"

Ethan nodded.

"That's definitely possible. Don't worry—I'll be careful."

Just as both sides finished preparing and were about to begin, more than a dozen shuriken suddenly shot out from the shadows in rapid succession, flying straight toward Ethan.

Fortunately, they weren't especially fast.

Sensing the danger, Ethan quickly dodged to the side.

Whoosh! Whoosh! Whoosh!

The shuriken sliced past him and embedded themselves into the patch of grass where he had been standing only a moment before.

"Who's there?!"

Ethan shouted toward the dense forest.

The next second, a striking young woman dressed in a purple ninja outfit stepped out from behind a large tree.

Huh? Why is she here?

She was none other than Aya, the younger sister of Koga, the Fuchsia Gym Leader.

The instant Ethan saw her, he remembered something.

...Uh-oh.

"Nice reflexes," Aya sneered as she sized him up. "If I'd known you were this agile, I would've aimed for your head instead."

Faced with her sarcasm, Ethan forced an awkward smile.

"Miss Aya, what a coincidence. What brings you all the way out here? Training?"

The reason he had managed to dodge the shuriken was simple.

After traveling for so long, not only had his Pokémon grown stronger, but his own physical abilities had improved considerably as well.

He glanced at the shuriken stuck in the ground.

They weren't sharpened.

Even if they had hit him, they would have left nothing worse than bruises.

In fact, Haunter lurking in his shadow and Gardevoir standing beside him had clearly recognized Aya beforehand. Since they sensed no hostility, neither of them had attempted to intervene.

"Training?" Aya's anger instantly flared.

"Hmph! If I were training, would I come all the way out here? Stop pretending! I've finally caught the liar who deceived me after wasting so much time!"

Caught...? Lied...?

Hearing those words, Chloe instinctively turned her head.

Her imagination immediately created the image of a dramatic romance straight out of one of the love novels her classmates passed around.

It sounded exactly like the scene where a heartless man abandoned a girl.

Ethan immediately frowned.

"Hey, hey, Miss Aya! Be careful how you phrase that! People are going to get the wrong idea!"

Meanwhile, Chloe's curiosity was overwhelming.

What exactly did Ethan lie to her about...?

Embarrassed to ask a complete stranger directly, she remained silent.

Fortunately, Aya answered the question herself.

"A misunderstanding?" Aya snorted.

"You promised you'd battle me! And then you completely forgot about it! I waited for you at the Gym for so long before my brother finally told me you'd already returned to mainland Kanto!"

Ethan broke into a cold sweat.

Back then, he had only promised to battle Aya after finishing his business so he could get away quickly and continue toward the Seafoam Islands.

He had never intended to keep that promise.

After leaving the Gym, the matter had completely slipped his mind.

He never expected Aya to take that defeat so seriously.

Now that she had tracked him all the way here, the situation was undeniably awkward.

Rubbing the back of his head, Ethan admitted,

"Sorry... I completely forgot."

"Enough excuses."

Aya waved him off impatiently.

"We'll settle both old and new scores today! I'm making up for my loss at the Gym right here and now!"

"Hey!"

The long-forgotten Samurai finally spoke up.

"I don't know what kind of grudge you have against him, miss, but I challenged him first! If anyone gets to battle him, it should be me!"

"No chance."

Aya shot him a glance.

"I spent all this effort waiting along his route just to catch him. Obviously I battle him first. You little brat should get lost before I decide to deal with you too."

"What did you say?!"

The Samurai angrily drew his katana and pointed it at her.

Before Ethan could say anything, the two of them were already arguing face-to-face.

After exchanging a few heated words, they finally realized that the decision rested with Ethan.

Both turned toward him simultaneously.

Aya asked,

"So? Who are you battling first?"

"..."

Ethan hesitated.

After all that arguing, they had simply thrown the problem back at him.

Looking from one to the other, he could tell neither would leave without getting what they wanted.

Eventually, he sighed and made his decision.

"If that's the case..."

"...Then both of you can come at me together."

"What?!"

Aya stared at him in surprise.

"You want to fight both of us at the same time?"

Ethan nodded.

The Samurai looked equally shocked.

"So you've started looking down on us that much?"

"Looks like we actually agree on something for once."

Aya glanced at the Samurai before locking eyes with Ethan again.

"Hmph. Those are some bold words. Aren't you worried you'll embarrass yourself? Still... I suppose this solves the problem. Just don't regret it after you lose."

She threw a Poké Ball.

"Come out, Venomoth!"

A flash of light burst forth, and a large moth-like Pokémon appeared on the battlefield.

Pokédex:

"Venomoth, the Poison Moth Pokémon. A Bug/Poison-type and the evolved form of Venonat. The powder covering its wings carries different kinds of toxins depending on its color. It is nocturnal and usually becomes active at night, preferring to prey on small insects attracted to streetlights."

Ethan looked at the two opposing Pokémon and stroked his chin.

A butterfly... and a moth.

Interesting combination.

After a moment's thought, he released his second Pokémon.

"Go, Blastoise!"

A burst of blue light flashed.

With a heavy boom, Blastoise landed firmly on the ground.

Despite the thick grass beneath it, its massive body still caused the earth to tremble slightly.
