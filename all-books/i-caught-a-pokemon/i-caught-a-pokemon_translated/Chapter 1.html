Hooooonk—!

Across the calm azure sea, a medium-sized passenger ship slowly approached the harbor as its long whistle echoed through the air.

Although the coastal city ahead was bustling and prosperous, with the docks especially lively, the passengers aboard the ship had no interest in admiring the scenery. Instead, all of their attention was focused on the deck.

A Pokémon battle was reaching its climax!

The two Pokémon facing off were Ralts and the purple mouse Pokémon Rattata. Their trainers were a handsome young man around twenty years old and a middle-aged man dressed in a formal suit.

The fierce battle had rapidly drained Rattata's stamina. Seeing that it had already fallen into a disadvantage yet still couldn't even touch Ralts, the middle-aged man grew anxious and shouted:

"Rattata, get close to it quickly, then use Tackle!"

Hearing the command, Rattata seized the opportunity. Its four limbs suddenly accelerated as it dashed forward, charging straight toward Ralts and slamming its head forward fiercely.

But just as it was about to hit, Ralts suddenly used Double Team, creating several illusions of itself!

Unable to stop in time, Rattata rushed straight through one of the afterimages.

Among the surrounding spectators were many tourists knowledgeable about Pokémon, so most immediately recognized the move. It was the Normal-type technique that many Pokémon could learn—Double Team!

At the edge of the battlefield, watching Rattata stand there in confusion, completely at a loss, Ethan Vale realized that after dragging things out for so long, it was finally time to end the battle.

"Ralts, use Confusion!"

The moment his words fell, every one of Ralts' illusions simultaneously raised an arm and pointed toward Rattata. In the next second, a strange glow suddenly enveloped the small Pokémon, lifting it straight into the air!

No matter how desperately it struggled, it could not break free from Confusion's restraint. Then, with a wave of Ralts' arm, Rattata's body was hurled backward.

Bang!

Its body smashed heavily into the nearby railing.

The impact was so powerful that everyone could clearly see the metal railing bend out of shape!

This time, Rattata could no longer endure it. Its eyes rolled upward as it collapsed helplessly onto the ground.

The crew member acting as referee bent down for a quick look, then shook his head. Raising his right hand, he loudly announced:

"Rattata is unable to battle! Ralts wins!"

The surrounding passengers applauded the exciting battle, while secretly marveling at the strength Ralts had displayed.

One particularly fiery young woman even whistled teasingly at Ethan Vale several times.

"That was amazing!"

"Wow, that makes seven consecutive wins for him, right?"

"Exactly! And that Ralts barely took any damage the entire time. It's seriously impressive!"

Praise filled the air around him, but Ethan Vale remained calm and composed. He walked over to Ralts, affectionately patting its head before approaching the middle-aged man.

The two shook hands lightly. After taking another look at Ralts, the man couldn't help but praise him sincerely.

"Your Ralts has been raised incredibly well! I completely accept this loss!"

"Your Rattata was excellent too," Ethan Vale replied kindly to the defeated trainer, returning the courtesy with some polite praise of his own. "Hopefully we'll have the chance to meet again someday."

The middle-aged man laughed and shook his head before stepping aside.

As he left the battlefield, the surrounding tourists exchanged glances. Several trainers were already eager to step forward and challenge the handsome young man themselves.

However, just as a few of them began competing to enter the battle area, Ethan Vale suddenly stepped back.

"Sorry, everyone. I'd love to keep battling, but it looks like we've already arrived at our destination. It's time for us to disembark."

Hearing this, the passengers hurriedly turned around, only to realize that while they had been absorbed in the battle, the ship had already docked!

…

Warm sunlight bathed the city beneath the sky as waves continuously crashed against the long seawall under the sea's endless push.

Looking out across the horizon, all one could see was endless blue, where sea and sky blended into one, making the entire world seem like a giant mirror.

The moist, salty sea breeze felt indescribably comfortable against the skin.

This was one of the Kanto region's most important ports, a southern city glowing beneath the orange light of the setting sun—Vermilion City!

As Ethan Vale stepped out of the ship and set foot upon this land that felt both familiar and unfamiliar, he could no longer suppress the joy in his heart.

Ash Ketchum, Misty, Brock, Pikachu, Professor Oak, the Kanto region, the Indigo League…

Ever since childhood in his previous life, this place had carried far too many memories for him. Never had he imagined that one day, he would truly arrive here himself!

Thinking about it, although several months had already passed since he came to this Pokémon world, every time he recalled it, it still felt unreal.

At his feet, sensing its trainer's emotions, Ralts let out a soft cry.

Looking down at it, Ethan Vale smiled lightly.

"Alright. Even though we've finally arrived in Kanto, before challenging the Indigo League, we should first head to the Pokémon Center and register for the tournament."

Leaving the harbor, Ethan Vale took out his phone and opened the map, following the roads toward the Pokémon Center in the city center.

Along the way, trainers constantly passed by with their Pokémon. Across the entire world, Pokémon had already become deeply integrated into everyday life—whether in entertainment, dining, deliveries, or public security. They were indispensable companions to humanity.

If Pokémon suddenly disappeared one day, that would be what truly felt wrong.

Still, when people saw the Ralts walking beside Ethan Vale, many cast curious glances their way.

It couldn't be helped. For the people of this region, Ralts was simply too rare!

Its green hair resembled half a cabbage, covering most of its face, while its white body looked as though it were wearing oversized trousers.

As a Pokémon not native to the Kanto region, Ralts naturally attracted plenty of attention. Even in other regions, it was considered relatively uncommon, and not many trainers possessed one.

Ignoring the stares around him, Ethan Vale entered the city center and walked through several intersections before finally spotting the Pokémon Center from afar.

When he stepped into the lobby, he immediately saw a pink-haired Nurse Joy in her uniform organizing documents behind the front desk. Upon noticing Ethan Vale, she greeted him with a warm professional smile.

"Hello, welcome to the Pokémon Center. How may I help you today?"

"Hello, Nurse Joy." Looking at her, Ethan Vale's memories briefly drifted before he quickly came back to himself and smiled. "I'm here to register for the Indigo League challenge."

"Certainly, I understand." Nurse Joy pointed toward a computer screen nearby. "Please place your Pokédex here for identification first."

Ethan Vale nodded. He took his Pokédex from inside his coat and placed it against the computer.

The next second, his information appeared on the screen, while an electronic synthesized male voice echoed from the machine.

"Ethan Vale, from Asame Town in the Kalos region, has completed Indigo League registration procedures. Current badge count: 0. We wish you success in your battles."

"The Kalos region?" Nurse Joy looked at Ethan Vale in surprise before sighing with emotion. "That's certainly a very distant place."

"Yes," Ethan Vale replied softly, his words carrying a double meaning. "Being able to come to this land truly wasn't easy."

Just as the two were speaking, a round and chubby Chansey suddenly walked over from the side.

Holding a box printed with the Indigo League emblem, it waddled step by step until it stood before Ethan Vale.

Nurse Joy made a welcoming gesture and smiled.

"For trainers who complete their registration, we provide a badge case. This one is yours, please accept it."

Ethan Vale lowered his head and glanced at it.

At first glance, it looked like nothing more than an ordinary box, with nothing particularly special or valuable about it.

After thanking her, he accepted the case before asking another question.

"By the way, Nurse Joy, my Pokédex still only contains Kalos region data. Is it possible to upgrade it to include the Kanto Pokédex as well?"

"I'm very sorry…" Nurse Joy hesitated for a moment before shaking her head. "We can't modify Pokédex data here. If you'd like to expand it, you could try visiting Professor Cerise's Laboratory here in the city, or Professor Oak's Laboratory in Pallet Town to the west. I'm sure both professors would be more than happy to help you!"
