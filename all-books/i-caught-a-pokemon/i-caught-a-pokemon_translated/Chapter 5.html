It was noon, right during the school lunch break.

Inside a seaside dessert café in Vermilion City, Ethan Vale sat across from Chloe alongside his two Pokémon. In front of each of them was a giant sundae of different flavors and colors.

As the café's signature item, the Super Sundae was not only incredibly delicious but also ice-cold and refreshing. One bite sent a chilling sensation rushing straight through the back of the head after passing the taste buds!

Even while pressing a hand against her forehead from the cold, Chloe still couldn't help but praise it.

"No matter how many times I try it, the Super Sundae here is always unforgettable!"

Perhaps because of the sugar, she seemed noticeably more relaxed and cheerful than before.

The reason the two of them were sitting here together traced back to around ten minutes earlier.

After eating lunch at Cerise Laboratory, Ethan Vale had been wandering around the city with his two Pokémon, intending to enjoy the scenery of the coastal town.

When he happened to pass by Chloe's school during lunch break, he invited her to come rest here for a while.

And thus, the current scene came to be.

After only two days of acquaintance, although brief, the two had already become fairly familiar with each other. Sitting together no longer felt awkward or uncomfortable.

Especially after Ethan Vale shared several amusing stories from his travels, the distance between them had shortened even further.

After taking a few bites, the conversation once again shifted toward Pokémon. Chloe asked curiously:

"Mr. Ethan Vale, what are your plans next?"

"Plans?" Listening to the soft music playing nearby, Ethan Vale stretched lazily and glanced out the window. "Although I've become a Special Research Fellow, it's mostly just a title. Professor Cerise hasn't assigned me any specific tasks, so besides collecting the three starters, I also plan to challenge Gyms and earn badges in preparation for eventually competing in the Indigo League."

"Challenge Gyms?" Chloe clearly didn't know much about Pokémon battles, so she asked curiously, "Does that mean you're going to challenge the Vermilion Gym?"

"The Vermilion Gym is still a bit too difficult for me right now." Ethan Vale shook his head patiently. "The Gym Leader there, Lt. Surge, specializes in Electric-type Pokémon. Even though Electric-types don't have an advantage against Grass-types, I still want to first challenge the Cerulean Gym and Pewter Gym in order to sharpen my skills. But before heading there, I need to catch one more Pokémon."

Chloe nodded as if she only half understood.

"So this is what people mean by expanding your lineup?"

"Exactly. It's kind of like progressing through levels in a game." Ethan Vale smiled. "You gather strength first, then gradually challenge more difficult opponents step by step. That's what makes the journey interesting."

Outside the café window, students wearing matching school uniforms occasionally passed by along the street.

Just as the two were speaking, a slightly dark-skinned boy around Chloe's age happened to notice her through the open window. Raising an eyebrow curiously, he walked closer.

And the moment he looked inside, he spotted Ethan Vale and immediately frowned slightly.

"Chloe, who's this guy?"

The boy suddenly spoke while his gaze repeatedly shifted between Chloe and Ethan Vale, as though trying to figure something out from the atmosphere between them.

"Goh? What are you doing here?"

Chloe also seemed surprised to see him, though she didn't think much of it and quickly introduced them.

"This is Mr. Ethan Vale. He's currently a Special Research Fellow at my dad's laboratory. Mr. Ethan Vale, this is Goh. He's my classmate and childhood friend."

In truth, Ethan Vale had recognized Goh the instant he saw him.

There was no helping it—he was simply too famous.

Especially since his appearance and temperament were exactly the same as in the anime!

"Nice to meet you," Ethan Vale greeted him politely with a nod.

Seeing Ethan Vale greet him, Goh quickly nodded in return.

"Nice to meet you too. I'm Goh."

However, looking at Ethan Vale's handsome appearance and seeing him sitting face-to-face with Chloe, Goh suddenly felt strangely uncomfortable.

Even he himself couldn't explain why, but the feeling inexplicably irritated him.

At that moment, he suddenly noticed Ralts sitting nearby, and his attention was instantly drawn toward it.

"What Pokémon is that?" he asked in surprise.

"This is my Ralts," Ethan Vale casually replied. Noticing that Goh seemed eager to approach it, he added, "Don't let its appearance fool you. It's a Psychic- and Fairy-type Pokémon, and it doesn't really like strangers getting too close, so… you'd better keep a little distance."

However, Goh clearly didn't take Ethan Vale's warning seriously. Looking at Ralts' adorable appearance, he only seemed to like it more and more.

"It's so cute! I'm definitely going to catch a Ralts myself someday!"

Hearing that, Ethan Vale suddenly asked:

"Someday? So you want to become a Pokémon trainer too?"

"I don't want to become a Pokémon trainer." Goh shook his head. "My dream is to collect every Pokémon in the world—including Mew!"

Just as expected.

Ethan Vale was not surprised by the answer at all.

"But if that's your goal, then as your collection keeps growing, won't those Pokémon simply become collectibles to you? Aside from increasing the number you own, what purpose would they serve?"

"Well…" Goh hesitated slightly, clearly having never considered the question before. After thinking for a moment, however, he shrugged carelessly. "It doesn't really matter. My goal is to catch them all. As for everything else… I'll think about that later."

"Then we're definitely very different." Ethan Vale shook his head helplessly. "We trainers also constantly catch Pokémon, but our goal is to become stronger together, climb to greater heights, and travel the world alongside them. From that perspective, your approach to capturing Pokémon feels a bit too self-centered."

Beside them, Chloe stared blankly at her sundae after hearing Ethan Vale's words.

Traveling together with Pokémon…

It was something she had never imagined before.

But somehow, it sounded rather nice.

…

Leaving Vermilion City behind, Ethan Vale traveled northward along the broad roads.

At his side, Ralts and Bulbasaur walked together with him, one on each side.

The reason he hadn't kept them inside Poké Balls was actually inspired by Ash.

Traveling alongside Pokémon on foot not only strengthened their abilities, but also deepened the bond between them. And with them accompanying him, the journey no longer felt lonely.

As for their destination…

Naturally, it was the first Gym Ethan Vale intended to challenge—the Cerulean Gym!

Judging from the map, however, reaching Cerulean City would not be easy.

Between Vermilion City and Cerulean City stood the largest and most prosperous super metropolis in all of Kanto—Saffron City.

Saffron City was filled with towering skyscrapers and was famous even outside Kanto. Combined with its enormous size, crossing through it would undoubtedly waste a great deal of time.

And speaking of Saffron City, it also contained two Gyms.

One belonged to Sabrina at the Saffron Gym, while the other belonged to Brawly at the Fighting Dojo.

However, Ethan Vale knew very well that his current strength was insufficient to challenge either of them. Rather than recklessly entering Saffron City, he instead chose to go around it and continue directly toward Cerulean City.

Walking and stopping along the way while enjoying the scenery, the journey was somewhat exhausting but also rewarding.

After spending most of the day traveling, he finally managed to bypass Saffron City.

Then, while crossing a grassy plain, a Pokémon resembling a fox suddenly caught his attention.

Most of its fur was brown, with a fluffy tail, short slender legs, brown eyes, a tiny black nose, and long rabbit-like ears that made it appear exceptionally adorable.

A wild Eevee!

Ethan Vale recognized it immediately and quickly aimed his Pokédex toward it.

The Pokédex soon responded.

"Beep. Eevee, the Evolution Pokémon. A Normal-type Pokémon capable of evolving into many different forms."

It was only a brief explanation, without even any detailed information.

But Ethan Vale didn't mind.

Along the journey so far, he had encountered many wild Pokémon without any desire to catch them.

But the moment he saw this Eevee, everything changed.

Since he had encountered it, there was no way he could let the opportunity slip away!

"Bulbasaur, this one's up to you!"

At Ethan Vale's command, Bulbasaur cried out before charging toward Eevee with determined steps and assuming a battle stance.

"Bulba! Bulba!"

It shouted provocatively at Eevee, and from its tone, it was obvious that Bulbasaur was openly challenging it.

Yet instead of fleeing, Eevee became equally excited and cried out in response before rushing forward to attack first!

"Oh?" Ethan Vale's eyes lit up at the sight. "Interesting. You're not afraid of humans, you're willing to attack proactively, and your tail is even raised in excitement. Looks like you've got quite a battle-loving personality!"

(End of Chapter)
