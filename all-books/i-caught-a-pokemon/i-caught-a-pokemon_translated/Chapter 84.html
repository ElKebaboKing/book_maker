Ethan's Pokémon slowly gathered around him, carefully surrounding him protectively.

Picking up the Ultra Ball containing Articuno, Ethan looked once more at the frozen sanctuary. After the brutal battle, its former beauty was gone, replaced only by devastation and ruin.

He sighed softly.

"Although it's a shame things turned out like this, maybe after some time, another Articuno will come here to nest. I'm sure in a few years, this place will return to how it once was."

After collecting himself, Ethan no longer lingered now that his goal had been achieved. He turned and left the area.

Using Teleport, Kirlia brought him outside the lake island. Instead of returning the way they came, Ethan followed a new route after Haunter scouted an exit. Climbing partway up a steep rock wall and flying for quite some time afterward, the surroundings gradually brightened until they finally emerged from the underground world through another narrow crevice.

Refreshing sea wind brushed against him. When the weary Ethan once again saw the blue sky and vast ocean, he realized he was already standing halfway up the smaller island of the Seafoam Islands.

Although the Seafoam Islands appeared to be two separate islands surrounded by ocean water, beneath the sea they were actually connected by underground passages. Just like their name suggested—twins, inseparable from one another.

Leaving the Seafoam Islands behind, Ethan traveled westward across the sea. After several hours, he finally arrived at Cinnabar Island.

"Cinnabar" referred to a blazing crimson red, passionate like fire itself.

Located at the southernmost point of the Kanto region, it was also the largest island in the southern seas. Dense forests covered the island, and at its center stood a massive active volcano. However, due to recent tectonic activity, no one knew exactly when it might erupt.

And naturally, Ethan had come here for one reason only—

To challenge the Cinnabar Gym and obtain the Volcano Badge!

Beside the fine golden beach stood Cinnabar Town, a coastal resort town equally famous for tourism.

Night had already fallen when Ethan entered the town. The very first thing he did was head straight to the Pokémon Center to heal his injured Pokémon.

Inside the Pokémon Center, after accepting the Poké Balls and seeing the two Legendary Pokémon inside them, the slightly tanned Nurse Joy covered her mouth in surprise.

"These are…!"

Yet her excellent professional composure allowed her to recover after only a brief moment of shock. She simply smiled politely at Ethan and silently walked into the treatment room.

Not long afterward, after the short recovery session ended, Ethan rented a room and enjoyed a comfortable hot bath, finally driving the lingering cold from his body. Only then did he finally have time to deal with Zapdos and Articuno.

After thinking for a moment, he released Zapdos first.

A flash of blue light appeared as Zapdos materialized in the room. However, having lost the battle, its usual arrogance was gone, replaced with visible discouragement.

Seeing this, Ethan patted its shoulder and comforted it patiently.

"Win or lose, that's just part of life. In my opinion, you were already amazing. It's just that Articuno was stronger. Don't let a temporary defeat weigh on your mind."

"And besides, think about it from another angle. Because there's such a powerful opponent, you now have a goal to strive toward. Keep becoming stronger, and one day, when you've gained enough power, you can challenge it again and defeat it completely!"

After several bowls' worth of motivational "soul soup," Zapdos's mood finally improved considerably.

Seeing that it was mostly recovered emotionally, Ethan recalled Zapdos and released Articuno instead.

The beautiful blue-feathered Articuno appeared gracefully in the room. Merely stretching its wings caused the room's temperature to drop by several degrees!

The refreshing chill felt just like turning on an air conditioner during a scorching summer day.

"Articuno, welcome to the family! My name is Ethan. Let's get along well from now on!"

Articuno showed no resentment about being captured.

Although it had fought against many opponents alone, the battle had been intense and satisfying. It first defeated Zapdos, then later lost to the incredibly powerful Haunter. As far as it was concerned, the outcome was acceptable.

After introducing Articuno to the rest of his Pokémon, Ethan changed into clean clothes and headed downstairs to the lobby.

But just as he was about to head for dinner, a cheerful girl's voice suddenly called out behind him.

"Big Brother Ethan!"

Ethan turned around and saw Misty, Ash, and Brock.

However…

That long-forgotten form of address—Big Brother Ethan—made his mind momentarily drift away. He suddenly recalled the beautiful girl back in Vaniville Town in the Kalos region, the one who always loved clinging to his side.

Shaking away the thought, Ethan smiled.

"What a coincidence. I didn't expect to run into you guys here."

"Exactly!" Misty smiled brightly. "We just arrived on the island too. We're planning to rest for the night before accompanying Ash to challenge the Cinnabar Gym tomorrow!"

At that moment, Ash and Brock greeted Ethan enthusiastically, and even Pikachu happily cried out toward him.

"Looks like our goals are the same again," Ethan said with a nod.

Then his eyes landed on the Pokémon in Misty's arms.

It was small and adorable, with the lower half of its body still encased inside a patterned eggshell, while only the upper half—shaped like a tiny yellow star—peeked out.

"Huh? You got Togepi?"

"That's right!" Misty proudly lifted Togepi high into the air and spun around happily. "We found an Egg while passing through the Valley of the Giant Pokémon, and it hatched from there!"

Seeing how happy she was, Ethan sincerely smiled for her.

"That's an incredibly rare Pokémon too. Make sure to raise it well."

As they talked, the four of them headed into the restaurant together and shared dinner.

Amid laughter and lively conversation, they exchanged stories from their travels and shared all kinds of interesting experiences with Ethan.

"Heh heh." Ash proudly opened his jacket, revealing the six Gym Badges inside. "Big Brother Ethan, now we have the same number of badges! How about a competition to see who gets the next one first?"

Seeing his smug expression, Ethan laughed.

"What's the point of competing over that? Besides, Blaine—the Cinnabar Gym Leader—is an extremely formidable opponent. Don't underestimate him."

Whenever the topic turned to battles, Ash seemed like a completely different person.

"My Pokémon are super strong! No matter who the opponent is, it'll be fine!"

"Oh? Really?" Misty rolled her eyes meaningfully and began counting on her fingers as she exposed him mercilessly.

"First, there's the Boulder Badge and the Cascade Badge. Brock and I practically gave those to you out of pity."

Ash's expression instantly stiffened.

"Then there's Sabrina's Saffron Gym. You got completely destroyed there, remember? If it weren't for that Haunter you brought back from Pokémon Tower making her laugh, you never would've won the Marsh Badge without even battling!"

Ash could only force an awkward laugh before Misty continued relentlessly.

"And as for your battle against Erika, if Team Rocket hadn't started a fire in the Gym, and if you hadn't rescued her Gloom from the flames, who knows whether she would've even given you the badge!"

"When you really think about it, the only Gyms you truly defeated with your own strength were Vermilion Gym and Fuchsia Gym—despite all the trouble you went through!"
