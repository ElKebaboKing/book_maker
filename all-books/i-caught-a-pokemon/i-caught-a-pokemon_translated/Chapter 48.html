"What do you mean by that?" Ethan froze for a moment.

"What I mean is, if I want to evolve, I can do it anytime and anywhere!!"

As soon as Gastly finished speaking, its body was suddenly enveloped in light. When the glow faded, a completely new form of Pokémon appeared before Ethan.

Ethan was utterly shocked.

Without any warning at all, it had actually evolved into Haunter!?

Compared to its previous form, Haunter's round body had changed shape dramatically. The purple mist surrounding it had disappeared, three spike-like protrusions had appeared on each side of its head, and most notably, it now possessed a pair of floating claws completely detached from its body.

Ethan quickly took out his Pokédex.

The Pokédex spoke:

"Beep. Haunter, the Gas Pokémon. A Ghost- and Poison-type Pokémon and the evolved form of Gastly. It usually lives in haunted houses and graveyards. Compared to Gastly, Haunter possesses much greater power. It hides in darkness while searching for prey. If it licks someone with its gaseous tongue, even their soul may be taken away."

"Hey, you just evolved whenever you felt like it? How did you even do that?"

Ethan asked instinctively, though after thinking it through carefully, Haunter's evolution was both unexpected and perfectly reasonable.

After all, before meeting him, Gastly had already lived for who knew how many years. Under normal circumstances, it should have evolved into Haunter long ago.

The only reason it had remained unevolved until now was probably because it simply did not want to evolve.

"What's so surprising about that?" Haunter repeatedly opened and clenched its new claws while getting used to them, speaking casually. "Actually, I could even continue evolving into my third form right now if I wanted to!"

"Stop, stop, stop!" Ethan hurriedly stopped it. "Evolving too quickly isn't a good thing. You should spend more time adapting to this new body first."

At that moment, Ethan shifted his attention toward Magikarp.

"But putting that aside, even though I'm happy you evolved, we still haven't solved our original problem. Hey, Haunter, hurry up and see if you can communicate with Magikarp!"

"You want me to communicate with it!?"

Haunter first looked stunned, then floated above Magikarp and stared at it eye-to-eye. Its mouth moved slightly, but after a long while, it silently floated back.

"No good. This guy's head feels completely empty. I honestly have no idea what it's thinking!"

Then, seeing Ethan frowning silently, Haunter tried analyzing the situation.

"But since you said it's already close to evolving yet still stuck at the critical point, then maybe it just needs a little more experience or stimulation. I think if it battles a bit, it should evolve very quickly."

Experience? Stimulation?

Ethan's eyes suddenly lit up as inspiration struck him.

Seeing Ethan's expression, Haunter looked shocked.

"No way… you actually want it to battle? But this guy only knows Splash! It literally can't deal damage! Against other Pokémon, it'll just get beaten up!"

"That's not a problem for me!" Ethan clearly already had a plan. Looking toward his Pokémon, he instructed,

"Everyone, spread out and search the area for wild Pokémon!"

They were currently beside a bend in the river near the roadside, with a small forest located nearby.

The Pokémon split up to search, and before long, Kirlia discovered a Bug-type Pokémon sleeping against a tree trunk deep in the forest—a Weedle.

Kirlia let out a soft cry. When Ethan arrived and saw the Pokémon, he nodded in satisfaction.

"A Weedle, huh? Perfect. Everyone, keep an eye on it and don't let it escape!"

Although confused, the Pokémon obediently surrounded Weedle from all sides, cutting off every possible escape route.

Once everything was prepared, Ethan turned to Charmander.

"Use Ember!"

Charmander tiptoed forward, inhaled deeply, and spat a stream of sparks at the sleeping Weedle, instantly engulfing it.

Fire-type moves were super effective against Bug-types!

"Heh-heh. Magikarp, now let me help you evolve!"

Seeing that Weedle was nearly unable to battle, Ethan picked up Magikarp, walked over to Weedle, and raised Magikarp high above his head.

"Go, Magikarp! Show it our combo move—Idiot Fish Smash!!"

With a powerful downward motion, Ethan slammed Magikarp's bony, scale-covered body directly onto Weedle.

Bang!

The two Pokémon collided heavily, producing a dull impact sound.

When Ethan noticed Weedle was somehow still conscious, he raised an eyebrow.

"Huh? Still not down? You're tougher than I expected! But no problem—Magikarp! Let's do it again!"

As he spoke, Ethan lifted Magikarp high once more and smashed it down again!

This time, Weedle could no longer endure it and fainted completely.

At the same time, Magikarp's body suddenly became enveloped in brilliant white light—exactly as Ethan had hoped!

Perfect! Operation complete!

Accompanied by a dragon-like roar that shot into the sky, a massive Gyarados appeared before Ethan.

Its body was roughly six to seven meters long. Its back was blue while its underside was pale yellow. It possessed a three-pronged horn atop its head, long whiskers extending from its face, and a gigantic gaping mouth. Its eyes seemed permanently filled with rage.

The Pokédex announced:

"Beep. Gyarados, the Atrocious Pokémon. A Water- and Flying-type Pokémon evolved from Magikarp. It possesses an extremely violent temperament. Its scales are as hard as steel, and its fangs can crush rocks. Once it goes on a rampage, it will destroy everything in sight. It is an extraordinarily savage Pokémon."

Although Gyarados still looked a little foolish after evolving, the transformation was undeniably a complete qualitative leap.

However, perhaps due to its natural instincts, the first thing it did after evolving was not familiarize itself with its body—but launch indiscriminate attacks at everything nearby!

"Hey, hey, there's definitely something wrong with this guy's brain!" Haunter flew into the air to dodge Gyarados's wild attacks and clicked its tongue at Ethan. "Could it be that you smashed it too hard earlier and broke its head?"

"Idiot, what nonsense are you talking about!?" Ethan hurriedly retreated as well, leaving Gyarados's attack range. "Didn't you hear what the Pokédex said? Its personality is naturally this violent!"

"So what now?" Haunter asked. "Wait for it to calm down by itself?"

"Of course not! We worked so hard to evolve it—how could we just ignore it now?" Ethan waved his hand forward and ordered loudly,

"Alright, everyone, stop standing around! Teach this disobedient guy a lesson! Since it dares attack its own trainer and companions, it's time to make it understand exactly who the boss is here!"

(End of Chapter)
