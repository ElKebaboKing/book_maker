After taking such a powerful Water-type blow, Muk slammed heavily onto the floor with a loud smack. The impact was so strong that even the wooden floor cracked apart, spreading fractures in every direction!

Koga shouted,

"Muk, use Poison Gas!"

Muk struggled back to its feet and shook its dizzy head. A cloud of foul-smelling green gas sprayed from its mouth, rapidly enveloping Wartortle.

Ethan immediately called out again,

"Rapid Spin!"

At Ethan's command, Wartortle pulled its limbs and head back into its shell and began spinning at high speed. In the next moment, the rotating shell became like a giant electric fan, creating a powerful whirlwind that completely blew away the Poison Gas!

"An excellent countermeasure," Koga praised despite being the opponent.

But Wartortle's attack didn't end there. It bounced high into the air and smashed down toward Muk.

Seeing this, Koga quickly shouted,

"Block it!"

Muk spread both arms wide, preparing to receive the attack head-on. As its body flashed with light, its previously soft form suddenly became as hard as concrete.

When the two Pokémon collided, Muk let out a deep roar. Although it managed to stop Wartortle, the recoil damage hurt Muk as well.

After battling three Pokémon in a row, the accumulated damage had already pushed Muk to its limit.

Even so, Koga refused to give up his final struggle!

"Muk! Use Sludge!"

A huge mass of sludge shot out from Muk's mouth. The toxic mud flowed through the openings in Wartortle's shell, rendering its defensive posture completely useless.

Unable to endure it, Wartortle finally stuck its head out again. Its face was slowly turning purple—it had clearly been poisoned!

Unfortunately for Koga, the battle had already reached its final stage.

At Ethan's command of "Water Gun attack!", Wartortle blasted a thick stream of water from its mouth, finally defeating Muk in one decisive strike.

Seeing Muk collapse limply onto the ground, Janine, acting as referee, raised her right hand.

"Muk is unable to battle! The winner is Wartortle!"

Ethan walked over to Wartortle and was about to treat the poison when Koga unexpectedly tossed him a bottle first.

"Use my special antidote instead."

A special antidote?

"Thank you," Ethan said gratefully as he accepted the medicine and fed it to Wartortle.

The normally slow detoxification process was dramatically shortened by the antidote. Almost the instant Wartortle swallowed it, its condition visibly improved!

"What incredible potency!!"

Seeing Ethan's astonishment, Janine proudly explained,

"Of course! My father is a Poison-type master. Not only is he skilled with poison, but he's also an expert at curing it! The antidotes he creates can perfectly neutralize almost any Pokémon poison. And after taking them, Pokémon won't have to worry about being poisoned again for quite some time!"

That amazing!?

Hearing that, Ethan immediately became interested.

"Well… Mr. Koga, could I perhaps…"

Koga smiled slightly, already understanding Ethan's intentions before he even finished speaking. He reached into his robe, pulled out two more antidotes, and tossed them toward Ethan.

"Take them. I hope these antidotes will help you in the future."

Originally, Ethan had intended to ask whether he could buy them, but he never expected Koga to simply give him two for free!

At that moment, Aya walked over carrying a heart-shaped Badge glowing with a faint pink light.

"Take good care of it. This is the Badge of our Fuchsia Gym."

Her tone was somewhat stiff. Deep down, she still blamed herself.

She knew exactly how powerful her brother truly was. If Venonat hadn't lost to Charmeleon earlier and weakened Muk's stamina, perhaps Koga would never have lost this Gym battle at all.

After saying farewell to Koga, Ethan turned and left the Fuchsia Gym. However, just as he stepped outside the estate gates, he suddenly heard a woman's voice behind him.

Turning around, he saw it was Aya!

"Miss Aya, what's wrong?"

Aya blocked Ethan's path and said seriously,

"Battle me again!"

"Huh?" Ethan blinked in confusion. "Another battle? Why?"

"Obviously for revenge! You don't think defeating me was going to be that easy, do you!?" Aya said while waving three Poké Balls in front of him. "Inside the Gym, the rules restricted me from using my full strength. This time, let's have a full three-on-three battle!"

"Sorry." Ethan shook his head. "My Pokémon are all injured right now, and I'm heading to the Pokémon Center for treatment. How about another day?"

Aya frowned slightly and pointed toward Haunter and Kirlia.

"What about those two?"

"Those two are still very weak and can't battle yet," Ethan casually lied while rejecting her. "Besides, I have something extremely important I need to take care of. I really don't have time for another battle."

Seeing how sincere Ethan sounded, Aya thought for a moment before nodding.

"Fine. Another day, then! Remember your words. Don't even think about running away, or I'll hunt you down to the ends of the earth!"

After returning to Fuchsia City, Ethan first went to the Pokémon Center to restore his Pokémon's strength. Afterwards, he brought everyone to have lunch there.

Inside the cafeteria, Ethan was eating french fries when he suddenly noticed Dratini curled up alone in a corner, stuffing food into its mouth nonstop.

Watching it, Ethan began thinking about how he could change its personality and help it truly integrate into the group.

His eyes swept across his Pokémon one by one. The moment they landed on Charmeleon, an idea immediately formed in his mind.

"Charmeleon, come over here for a second!"

With a face full of confusion, Charmeleon followed Ethan over to Dratini.

"Ahem." Ethan cleared his throat, interrupting the intensely focused Dratini. Once it looked up at him in confusion, Ethan introduced them.

"Let me formally introduce you. The Pokémon beside me is Charmeleon. Although you've already met before, you probably don't really know each other yet. But starting today, he'll be your big brother, and you'll be his little brother. I hope the two of you can support each other and grow stronger together!"

???

Ethan's speech sounded grand and official, yet incredibly sudden.

Not only was Dratini confused, but even Charmeleon wore a giant question mark on its face.

Seeing this, Ethan immediately pulled Charmeleon close and quietly whispered to it,

"Listen, this little guy is both a foodie and emotionally traumatized, so it really needs your guidance. No matter what method you use, you absolutely have to help it return to normal. You understand?"

(End of Chapter)
