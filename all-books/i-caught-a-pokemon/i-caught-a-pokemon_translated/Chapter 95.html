At a battle field near the coast, Ethan and the young man stood in their respective Trainer zones.

Around them, more than a dozen strangely dressed bicycle gang members stood scattered about, all wearing mocking expressions.

"Go, Masha! Show him what you can do!"

"Teach him a lesson!"

Whistles, cheers, and shouting blended together. Despite there being only a dozen people, they somehow created an incredibly noisy atmosphere.

Masha grinned confidently and threw out a Poké Ball.

"Come out, Golem!"

A Pokémon with a turtle-like head, gray-brown claws, and a body completely covered in rock appeared on the battlefield.

Pokédex:

"Golem, the Megaton Pokémon. A Rock- and Ground-type Pokémon and the final evolution of Geodude. Its body is as hard as stone and can withstand even explosions without taking damage. Each time it sheds its skin, its body grows larger."

Ethan casually selected a Poké Ball from his belt.

"I'm not going to bully you by using a Pokémon that completely counters yours, so I just picked one at random. Go, Kirlia!"

When Kirlia appeared, the inexperienced gang members—who had rarely seen Pokémon from outside their local area—stared in amazement.

Among them, a girl with dark green hair, an exposed midriff, and a leather mini-skirt let out an audible gasp.

"Wow! So it's called Kirlia? What a beautiful Pokémon!"

Masha pointed dramatically.

"Stop acting so confident! Just because you've got a Pokémon we've never seen doesn't mean you can beat me! Golem, use Rollout!"

Golem tucked in its limbs and curled into a giant boulder before rolling toward Kirlia at high speed.

The moment Ethan gave a mental command, Kirlia raised its hands.

Psychic energy enveloped Golem and lifted it high into the air.

"Tch!" Masha gritted his teeth. "A Psychic-type..."

Kirlia swung its arms downward.

Boom!

Golem crashed heavily into the ground.

However, as a Rock- and Ground-type Pokémon with a thick rocky exterior, it did not suffer much damage.

Unable to get close, Masha changed tactics.

"Golem, use Rock Slide!"

Large rocks materialized overhead and came crashing down toward Kirlia.

Yet Kirlia simply used Teleport once and effortlessly avoided the entire attack.

Before Golem could launch another move, Psychic energy wrapped around it again.

It was lifted into the air and slammed down a second time.

Crash!

A large crater appeared in the battlefield.

Just as Golem struggled to climb out, Kirlia teleported behind it.

Green glowing leaves formed around Kirlia and surged forward.

"Golem, harden yourself!"

Masha shouted in panic.

Unfortunately, the attack landed directly.

Magical Leaf!

Grass beats Ground!

Grass beats Rock!

It's super effective!

Golem collapsed.

"..."

Silence instantly fell over the area.

The bicycle gang members had never imagined that this man would barely say a word while his Pokémon effortlessly ended the battle.

"Masha..."

After a long pause, they opened their mouths as if wanting to comfort him, but none of them knew what to say.

At that moment, the green-haired girl stepped forward.

"You're really strong."

She looked directly at Ethan.

"But beating him isn't enough. If you want our recognition, you'll have to defeat me too!"

"Ohhh!!"

The crowd erupted into cheers.

"As expected of Big Sis Misa!"

"She's so reliable!"

"Big Sis Misa, avenge Masha! Show this guy what happens when he messes with us!"

As Masha recalled Golem, Misa walked forward.

"Big Sis..."

he whispered.

"This guy really is strong. You'd better be careful."

So they were siblings.

Masha and Misa.

Ethan had never noticed that detail while watching the anime years ago.

Now that he saw her up close, he realized that despite her delinquent image, Misa was actually quite pretty.

Misa nodded before facing Ethan.

"How about we raise the stakes?"

She pointed at Kirlia.

"If I win, give me that Kirlia."

"That's impossible."

Ethan refused immediately.

"I never gamble with my Pokémon."

"What a shame."

Misa clicked her tongue but did not push the issue.

Instead, she released her own Pokémon.

"Come out, Cloyster!"

A Pokémon protected by a double shell, sporting a horn and a sinister grin, appeared before Ethan.

Pokédex:

"Cloyster, the Bivalve Pokémon. A Water- and Ice-type Pokémon and the evolved form of Shellder. Its extremely hard shell can withstand most attacks. It protects itself with its shell while firing spikes at opponents. Once its shell is completely closed, even tremendous force cannot pry it open."

Since he did not have a type disadvantage, Ethan saw no reason to switch Pokémon.

"Kirlia, use Psychic!"

Kirlia attacked immediately.

Psychic energy wrapped around Cloyster.

However, Cloyster instantly snapped its shell shut.

Unlike Golem, which balanced offense and defense, Cloyster specialized entirely in defense.

It would patiently wait until its opponent relaxed before striking with a deadly counterattack.

Misa smirked.

"Go ahead. Let's see how you break through my defense."

Kirlia swung its arms.

Boom!

Cloyster slammed into the ground.

Despite the tremendous impact, its shell remained completely intact.

Its double-layered shell even reduced the damage caused by the collision.

Ethan frowned.

"Use Magical Leaf!"

The leaves formed once again around Kirlia and swept toward Cloyster.

But the attack still accomplished very little.

"Now!"

Misa's eyes lit up.

"Cloyster, use Aurora Beam!"

Cloyster's shell snapped open.

A freezing beam of icy energy shot straight toward Kirlia.

Fortunately, Kirlia had been prepared.

With a flash of light, it used Teleport and appeared elsewhere.

Seeing the attack miss, Cloyster immediately closed its shell again.

"What an annoying opponent..."

The battle entered a stalemate.

Ethan furrowed his brow as he searched for a solution.

Meanwhile, the gang members continued their taunts.

"What's wrong now?"

"Can't do anything against Big Sis Misa's Cloyster, can you?"

( End of Chapter ).
