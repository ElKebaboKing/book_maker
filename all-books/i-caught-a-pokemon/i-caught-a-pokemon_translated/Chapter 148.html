Bulbasaur had already defeated two Pokémon in a row and was fighting with incredible momentum.

At first glance, the battle seemed to be tilting heavily in Ash's favor.

However, judging from the unwavering confidence of Shawn's supporters, it was obvious that she still had one final trump card in reserve.

Sure enough, despite losing the first two rounds, Shawn showed no signs of panic. Calmly recalling Scyther, she quietly took out her third Poké Ball.

Everyone in the stadium—including the announcer, the audience, Brock, and Misty—expected her final Pokémon to be a powerful ace with an impressive appearance.

Instead, when the Poké Ball opened, a Bellsprout appeared.

The entire stadium erupted.

The audience exploded with disbelief, and astonished gasps echoed throughout the arena.

Because of its thin, fragile-looking body, Bellsprout was generally regarded as a weak Pokémon. Few people ever associated it with strength.

No one expected that after surviving three rounds of the tournament, someone would still be using a Bellsprout.

Ash, in particular, immediately underestimated it.

He was convinced he had already secured victory.

But once the battle began, the seemingly frail Bellsprout gave everyone a lesson they would never forget.

As Bulbasaur charged forward with Tackle, Bellsprout skillfully redirected its momentum and executed a perfect shoulder throw, hurling Bulbasaur across the battlefield.

The move caused little actual damage, but it left everyone stunned.

"Oh my!" the announcer exclaimed. "What an incredible display! Not only is this Bellsprout surprisingly strong, but it also seems to have mastered the art of using an opponent's strength against them!"

Bulbasaur staggered to its feet, dizzy from the throw.

Determined to prove it was a fluke, it rushed forward once again.

This time, Bellsprout grabbed one of Bulbasaur's front legs, spun it around like a giant windmill several times, then flung it even farther away.

"Incredible!"

As the announcer continued praising the performance, the audience began looking at Bellsprout with entirely new respect.

Meanwhile, Shawn's supporters cheered even louder, as though they had expected this outcome all along.

Even Shawn herself could no longer maintain her graceful composure and burst into hearty laughter.

As for Ethan, he barely remembered Shawn or her Bellsprout from the original events. Even if it had given Ash plenty of trouble, he knew Ash would ultimately find a way to win.

The battle continued.

Taking advantage of Bulbasaur's fall, Bellsprout rushed forward and tossed it around like a rag doll before finally throwing it out of bounds.

Boom!

Bulbasaur crashed heavily against the steps beneath Ash's platform.

After the referee declared Bulbasaur unable to battle, Ash immediately sent out his most trusted partner—

Pikachu.

Unfortunately, even Pikachu couldn't turn the tide.

As the fourth battle began, Pikachu unleashed Thunderbolt, sending electricity across nearly half the battlefield.

Yet Bellsprout weaved through the electric currents with astonishing footwork, avoiding every attack.

Even more unbelievable, it rooted its feet firmly into the ground and endured a direct Thunderbolt without suffering any real damage.

Left with no other option after his ranged attacks failed, Ash ordered Pikachu into close combat.

But under Shawn's careful training, Bellsprout possessed extraordinary martial arts skills.

After only a few exchanges, it landed a swift heel kick that knocked Pikachu to the ground.

Seeing the result, Misty stared in disbelief.

"No way... even Pikachu lost?"

"That's not surprising," Ethan replied, shaking his head.

"Bellsprout is a Grass-type Pokémon, so Electric-type attacks only deal half damage. Besides, Pikachu's performance has always been inconsistent. Its ceiling is incredibly high, but its floor is just as low. Losing to a weaker opponent isn't unusual."

Fortunately, despite suddenly losing two battles in a row, Ash didn't panic.

At the critical moment, he forced himself to calm down and released his final Pokémon.

A Muk appeared in the center of the battlefield.

The audience was once again dumbfounded.

Another unconventional Pokémon?

What nobody expected was that Muk would become the perfect counter to Bellsprout.

The physical strength Shawn had painstakingly trained into Bellsprout suddenly became completely useless.

No matter how many punches or kicks Bellsprout delivered, they had absolutely no effect.

Muk's soft, gelatinous body absorbed every bit of the impact like a giant sponge.

After launching dozens of attacks, Bellsprout was dripping with sweat.

Yet the foul-smelling blob before it remained completely unmoved.

"What!?"

Shawn frowned deeply.

Since raising Bellsprout, she had never encountered an opponent like this.

Eventually, perhaps growing tired of being endlessly poked and kicked, Muk finally acted.

Following Ash's command, it used Body Slam, leaping forward and completely engulfing Bellsprout beneath its massive body, squeezing the air out of it until the battle came to an end.

"That was close."

Even Ethan couldn't help letting out a relieved sigh.

It wasn't that Muk was necessarily stronger than Bellsprout.

Rather, Muk's unique body completely nullified Bellsprout's greatest strength—its close-range martial arts.

If Ash hadn't had the rare flash of inspiration to bring Muk along beforehand, he might not have won this battle.

However...

Ethan rubbed his chin thoughtfully.

Shawn's training methods were genuinely worth studying.

If that kind of close-combat training were applied to an actual Fighting-type Pokémon, the results would probably be even more terrifying.

It seemed he should definitely save a recording of this battle and study it carefully in the future.

When Ash returned from the battlefield, he was beaming with joy.

Not only had he won, but he had also reunited with his mother and Professor Oak after so long, leaving him smiling from ear to ear.

To celebrate, Delia suggested borrowing the kitchen in the dining hall and personally preparing a hearty homemade lunch so Ash could enjoy the taste of home.

However, Ethan wasn't particularly close to either Delia or Professor Oak. With his own match still ahead, he also didn't want to intrude on their family time.

He politely declined their kind invitation and left with Chloe.

After all, his next opponent was Gary.

Spending time with Professor Oak right before battling his grandson would inevitably make things feel a little awkward.

( End of Chapter )
