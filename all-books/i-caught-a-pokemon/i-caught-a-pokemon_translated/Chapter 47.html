Inside the Pokémon Center in Saffron City,

Ethan was eating dinner while talking on the phone with Handsome.

On the other end of the line, Handsome praised him enthusiastically.

"You're incredible! I only asked you to investigate the situation, yet you somehow solved the entire incident along the way!"

"It wasn't really solved…" Ethan took a large sip of fruit juice. "After their leader left early, Team Rocket lost their command structure. Even so, they still resisted for quite a while and almost succeeded. Then, at the final moment, they made the smart decision to retreat without leaving behind even a single injured member."

"Alright, don't focus too much on those details." Handsome's tone remained upbeat. "Most people couldn't have accomplished what you did. So, how about considering joining the International Police? I'm very optimistic about your potential."

"Forget it." Ethan rejected the offer immediately. "My goal is to win the Pokémon League Championship. I don't want to spend every day dealing with criminals."

Then he leaned back lazily in his chair and asked,

"So how are things on your side? Did you find Team Rocket's base yet?"

"No." Handsome answered very straightforwardly. "Celadon City is far too large. I honestly don't know where to start searching. We did find one clue before, but the trail quickly went cold. By the way, your next destination is here too, right? Once you arrive, let me treat you to a meal as thanks."

"Sounds good. I'll contact you after I get there."

Although he said that, Ethan was actually thinking about something else.

Team Rocket's base…

He hesitated for a moment but ultimately decided against saying anything.

Knowing too much ahead of time was not always a good thing.

The more he spoke, the easier it would be to expose something suspicious or attract unwanted attention.

After resting overnight in Saffron City, Ethan set out toward the next Gym the following morning.

According to the map, the nearest Gym city was Celadon City.

Since he had not contacted Misty, he had no idea whether Ash had successfully earned his fifth Badge yet.

The distance between the two cities was not particularly far.

Even so, by noon, Ethan had only traveled a little over halfway there.

Along the journey, he passed through plains and forests.

By coincidence, a gentle breeze swept across the land as flocks of Butterfree fluttered across the sky like a sea of dancing butterflies.

After taking several photos to preserve the rare sight, Ethan was just about to find a place to rest when a red convertible sports car suddenly roared past him from behind before quickly reversing back.

Once the dust raised by the car settled, Ethan turned and saw that five people were sitting inside.

Aside from the handsome red-brown-haired boy in the passenger seat, everyone else—including the driver—were beautiful young women.

The boy wore oversized sunglasses as he carefully sized Ethan up before speaking.

"I remember you. Back when you came to Professor Oak's Laboratory to deliver something to my grandfather, I saw you from a distance."

Naturally, this boy was Professor Oak's grandson and Ash's very first major rival—Gary.

"So you're Gary?" Ethan asked knowingly. "A few days ago, when I was chatting with Ash, he mentioned you. He said you weren't just his childhood friend, but also the rival he acknowledged the most."

"Really?" Gary did not seem particularly concerned about it. "That guy Ash can actually say some decent things sometimes. By the way, judging from the Pokémon beside you, you must be the trainer who told that samurai guy in Viridian Forest to teach trainers from Pallet Town a good lesson, right?"

Remembering that incident, Ethan laughed.

"That was me. Why? Did he give you a hard time?"

"Thanks to you, I got attacked by him the moment I entered the forest." Gary curled his lips slightly. "And he also said you were an incredibly strong trainer. I wonder if that's actually true."

"That depends on who you compare me to." Ethan did not deny it. "At the very least, I still haven't lost a single battle since I started traveling."

"Hah, what's so impressive about that? I've never lost either!" Gary clearly could not tell whether Ethan truly had the skill to back up his words or was simply bragging. After thinking for a moment, he asked, "So how many Badges do you have now?"

"Four." Ethan pointed ahead afterward. "But I'm heading to Celadon Gym right now, so I'll be getting my fifth very soon."

"What? Only four Badges?" Gary sounded openly arrogant. "That's pretty slow. Looks like you and Ash are about the same level after all. I already have seven Badges, you know. I'm on my way to Viridian City now to get the last one."

So he judged strength based purely on the number of Badges…

Honestly, this kid really was unbelievably arrogant.

But it made sense.

Gary had only recently begun his journey. His confidence was sky-high.

As Professor Oak's grandson, he naturally believed he held advantages others did not.

And since he had not yet experienced the crushing defeats that awaited him in future Pokémon League tournaments, he naturally looked down on most other trainers.

Thinking of that, Ethan took out a Poké Ball, enlarged it in his hand, and spun it casually around his finger.

"In that case, how about battling me?"

"A battle?" Gary shrugged. "Sorry, but I don't have time to waste fooling around here. If you really want to face me, then make it into the Indigo League and meet me there."

With that, Gary waved his hand.

The sports car immediately accelerated away and disappeared down the road.

"Heh-heh." Gastly floated out from Ethan's shadow and mocked itself jokingly. "Looks like we got underestimated."

Watching Gary's departing figure, Ethan shook his head.

Young, wealthy, driving a sports car, and surrounded by beautiful girls…

Honestly, it was hard not to feel a little envious.

Shaking off the thought, Ethan set down his backpack and had Kirlia gather firewood to prepare lunch.

At the same time, he released all of his Pokémon except Zapdos and Magikarp.

After traveling with him for so long, the Pokémon no longer needed Ethan's instructions to understand what they should do.

Squirtle carried a kettle to the river to fetch water.

Charmander lit the firewood.

Meanwhile, Ivysaur led the other Pokémon into the nearby forest to gather berries.

Everything proceeded in an orderly and harmonious manner.

After everyone finished eating heartily, Ethan took out Magikarp's Poké Ball.

After thinking for a while, he walked to the riverside and released it.

A flash of blue light appeared as Magikarp emerged into the river, happily swimming around.

After observing it more carefully, Ethan realized that he really had been tricked by that Magikarp salesman back then.

This was obviously a male Magikarp.

How could it possibly lay eggs!?

Still, counting the time that had passed since he obtained this Pokémon, it should have evolved long ago under normal circumstances.

Yet even now, there were still no signs of evolution whatsoever.

So was it really missing James's famous "final kick" from the anime, which prevented it from evolving all this time?

"Hey, Gastly." Ethan turned toward Gastly and asked, "In a situation like this, is there any way to make it evolve quickly?"

"Evolution?" Gastly let out a mischievous laugh. "What? Is evolution really such a difficult thing?"

(End of Chapter)
