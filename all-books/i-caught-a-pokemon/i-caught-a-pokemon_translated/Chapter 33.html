Ethan and the others joined the crowd, slowly moving forward together with the festival visitors.

The streets were lined with rows of stalls selling all kinds of delicious snacks and little trinkets. Noticing that Ash seemed interested, Ethan bought several snacks and shared them with everyone.

Meanwhile, when he saw Misty pause in front of an accessory stand only to silently walk away a moment later, Ethan stepped over to the stall himself. After carefully choosing for a while, he bought a hair clip and gave it to her.

A smile immediately appeared on Misty's face. Although she did not say much, the joy in her expression made it obvious that she truly loved the gift.

Just like every year, the festival was lively and bustling.

After walking a little farther, they even saw a beautifully dressed woman performing an exotic dance amid music and the loud cheers of the crowd.

However, unlike Ethan, Ash, and Misty, who were all enjoying themselves, Brock seemed completely distracted. Walking behind the group with his head lowered, his face was filled with longing. Clearly, he still could not stop thinking about the woman he had seen at the dock earlier, and he continued muttering to himself under his breath.

"Please wait a moment!"

An elderly yet powerful voice suddenly rang out behind them.

Turning around, Ethan saw a tiny old woman with white hair, less than one meter tall, wearing a purple robe and leaning on a cane.

The old woman had an unusually large mouth and dry, wrinkled skin. Most striking of all were her sharp, piercing eyes.

Brock had still been immersed in thoughts of the beautiful woman, but the instant he turned around and saw the old lady's appearance, he was so startled that he fell flat onto the ground.

"Wah! A shriveled-up river goblin just appeared!"

"Who are you calling a goblin!?" the old woman snapped angrily. Then her gaze swept back and forth between Ethan and Brock as she spoke mysteriously, "Listen carefully—you two had better beware of beautiful women tonight, or you're definitely going to suffer bad luck!"

"What kind of thing is that to say!?" Misty puffed out her cheeks. Sneaking a quick glance at Ethan, she spoke somewhat bashfully, "How could I possibly bring him bad luck?"

"Idiot!" the old woman shouted. "I said beautiful women—not a little girl like you who still smells like milk!"

"What!? Did you just call me a little girl!?" Misty instantly exploded in anger. Clearly, those words had struck a sensitive nerve.

Beside her, Brock fell into deep thought after hearing the old woman's warning.

Only Ethan knew that this "old woman" was actually a disguise created by Gastly. Since it had merely taken an interest in them, Ethan simply smiled and did not take her words too seriously.

But Misty had no intention of letting this strange old woman ruin their rare chance to enjoy the festival.

"What nonsense! Alright, let's stop paying attention to her and leave already."

As she spoke, she pushed Ethan forward with one hand while dragging Brock away by the ear with the other.

The final destination of the festival was a shrine located at the far end of the town.

When Ethan and the others arrived there with the crowd, they saw an elderly priest strike a ceremonial bell and drum.

"Everyone, please quiet down! The treasure of Maiden's Peak Shrine is about to be unveiled!"

Beside the priest stood a large hanging scroll, though it was currently covered by a white cloth, preventing anyone from seeing what was painted beneath it.

As the crowd gathered around the shrine, the priest continued:

"This painting is a secret treasure that has been passed down in Maiden's Peak Shrine for two thousand years. It is only displayed once each year during the summer festival. Now then, dear guests, please admire it carefully."

With that, he pulled away the white cloth, fully revealing the painting to the crowd.

The painting depicted a woman facing forward with her hands clasped before her chest as though praying.

Although the artwork was entirely black and white, lacking any other colors, people could still clearly feel both the woman's beauty and the deep sorrow surrounding her.

Beside Ethan, Brock was utterly stunned because the beautiful woman in the portrait was the exact same woman he had seen earlier that day at the small dock!

The priest's narration echoed through the crowd like the voice of a storyteller.

"Two thousand years ago, there was a young couple deeply in love. But because of war, the man was forced to enlist and sail away with the army. Before departing, he promised the woman that once the war was won, he would return triumphantly to her side.

"So the woman waited for many years. But in the end, the man never came back. Endless waiting eventually transformed her into stone, leaving her standing upon the cliff where she remains to this very day, still waiting for the return of her beloved."

It truly was a sorrowful legend, and after hearing it, the crowd's mood inevitably became subdued.

At that moment, Brock suddenly asked in a dazed voice, almost as though he had fallen under a spell:

"Then... where exactly is that rock now!?"

"Huh?" the priest blinked in surprise. "You mean you don't know about Maiden's Rock?"

Night gradually blanketed the land.

On a seaside cliff stood a lone wooden cabin.

After climbing the rugged mountain path and circling around the cabin, Ethan and the others immediately saw a stone statue of a woman resting upon a cliffside rock extending out from the shore.

No one knew how many years had passed, but the statue now seemed almost fused together with the cliff itself.

Waves crashed endlessly against the rocks below. In the distance, the lights of Maiden's Peak glittered brilliantly. Even from so far away, they could still vaguely hear the joyous sounds of the festival celebration drifting through the night air.

Yet this place itself seemed filled with loneliness, just like the legend.

Looking at Maiden's Rock, Misty quietly asked, "Do you think that rock really was once the woman from the story?"

"Who knows," Ethan replied with a shake of his head. "At the very least, I don't see any traces of artificial carving."

It was already ten o'clock at night. Dark clouds slowly covered the moon, and a chilly breeze swept past them.

Relying on instinct, Ethan felt as though something was about to happen.

He took out two Poké Balls, and with flashes of blue light, Charmander and Jolteon appeared beside him.

The flame on Charmander's tail and the occasional sparks flickering across Jolteon's fur not only illuminated the area around them but also gave Ethan a reassuring sense of security.

"There's really nothing much to see here," Misty said. "Let's head back to town."

Compared to the lively festival streets, she naturally had no desire to remain in such a gloomy place.

However, just as she turned around, she suddenly noticed that the door of the small cabin behind them was slowly creaking open.

Creeeak...

At first, she thought the wind had blown the door open.

But in the very next second, a glowing blurry figure suddenly floated out from inside the cabin.

"Ahhh! It's a ghost!!"
