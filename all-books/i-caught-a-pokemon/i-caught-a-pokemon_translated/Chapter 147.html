After delivering his challenge, Gary left with his cheerleading squad, looking thoroughly pleased with himself.

He still hadn't matured and remained the same arrogant rich kid as ever.

Even though both he and Ethan had advanced to the fourth round, Gary didn't think much of Ethan. On the contrary, he was deeply dissatisfied that Ethan had gained such overwhelming popularity after the previous round.

"He only defeated a weak girl."

"If it had been me, I would've ended the battle even more decisively!"

"I'm Professor Oak's grandson. I'm also one of the tournament favorites. Why is he getting all the attention instead of me?"

"Hmph! I'll teach him a lesson later and show everyone who's truly superior!"

Watching Gary walk away, Chloe—who was meeting him for the first time—couldn't help forming a terrible first impression. Seeing how openly he looked down on and provoked Ethan left her quite displeased.

After the draw concluded, it was time for the final round of the preliminary tournament.

Ash's match was scheduled for 11:00 a.m., while Ethan's match would begin at 3:00 p.m.

Three o'clock wasn't exactly prime time, but there was no helping it. With only thirty-two competitors remaining, the schedule had been reduced to sixteen matches, with only four battles held on each battlefield. There simply wasn't room to push the matches any later.

After leaving the drawing hall, they found a place to rest.

When the time came, Ash headed to the competitors' waiting room, while Ethan and the others made their way to the Grass Field spectator stands.

The stadium was once again packed to capacity. Fortunately, the biker gang had reserved seats for them in advance.

No sooner had they sat down than they unexpectedly spotted two familiar faces nearby.

A silver-haired elderly man wearing a white lab coat sat beside a beautiful woman.

"Huh? Professor Oak!? And Aunt Delia!? What are you doing here?"

Even with his eyes nearly closed as always, Brock noticed them immediately.

Professor Oak was equally surprised to see Brock and Misty.

"Oh! It's you two!"

Delia smiled warmly.

"Hello. Thank you for looking after Ash during this journey. He's caused you all quite a bit of trouble."

"It's no trouble at all," Brock replied with a smile. "We're teammates, after all. But when Ash talked to you yesterday, weren't you both still in Pallet Town? Why did you suddenly come all the way here to cheer him on?"

Professor Oak chuckled.

"Watching from home is certainly comfortable, but nothing compares to experiencing the atmosphere in person."

At that moment, Professor Oak looked past Brock and noticed Ethan.

Although it had only been a brief meeting before, and his memory wasn't quite what it used to be, Ethan's recent tournament performances had reminded him who the young Trainer was.

"You're Ethan Vale, aren't you? I've watched your matches. Defeating so many opponents is truly impressive."

Ethan greeted him politely before his eyes wandered between Professor Oak and Delia.

It was his first time meeting Ash's mother in person.

Still, he couldn't help wondering why such an ordinary housewife spent so much time around one of the world's leading Pokémon Professors.

They didn't exactly seem to have many common interests.

Why didn't Professor Oak ever refuse?

And since Delia had come all the way here, why hadn't she told Ash beforehand?

Was she worried about putting too much pressure on him?

Shaking those strange thoughts from his mind, Ethan introduced the girl standing beside him.

"This is Chloe Cerise, Professor Cerise's daughter."

"Oh?"

Professor Oak looked at Chloe in surprise.

"I remember attending your one-month celebration when you were a baby. I even held you in my arms back then. Time really does fly—you've grown into such a fine young lady."

Chloe politely nodded.

Her mother had never mentioned any of that, and naturally, she had no memory of it herself.

Professor Oak enthusiastically continued chatting, even sharing several amusing stories about Professor Cerise's school days.

Unfortunately, while those stories fascinated him as a fellow researcher, Chloe found them rather uninteresting.

Just then, Ash's match began, and everyone quickly shifted their attention to the battlefield.

Ash entered as the Green-side Trainer, while his opponent, Shawn from Maiden's Peak Town, took the field as the Red-side Trainer.

Unlike Ash's ordinary entrance, Shawn's arrival was nothing short of spectacular.

A long red carpet was rolled out from the competitors' tunnel.

Moments later, a young woman dressed in elegant traditional crimson attire gracefully walked onto the field while attendants scattered flower petals before her.

Her dignified and graceful appearance instantly captivated nearly every man in the stadium—including Brock.

Meanwhile, one section of the audience erupted into coordinated cheers.

A large group of young men and women beat drums and clashed cymbals rhythmically while chanting at the top of their lungs,

"Victory for Miss Shawn!"

"Victory for Miss Shawn!"

Their synchronized cheers rose above the already deafening crowd, completely overwhelming Ash's supporters.

Seeing the spectacle, Misty murmured,

"Wow... that's incredible. She must be another really popular competitor. Should we cheer for Ash too?"

Brock shook his head.

"Forget it. We'd only waste our voices. There are so few of us that no one would hear us anyway."

On the battlefield, once the referee signaled the start of the match, Ash sent out Bulbasaur, while Shawn chose Beedrill.

From a type standpoint, the Bug/Poison-type Beedrill held the advantage over the Grass/Poison-type Bulbasaur.

However, right from the start, Ash once again demonstrated why he excelled at overcoming unfavorable matchups.

Bulbasaur trapped the airborne Beedrill with Leech Seed, then charged forward with Tackle, knocking it out.

Oddly enough, although Shawn had just lost her first Pokémon, her supporters showed no disappointment whatsoever.

Instead, they cheered even louder, repeatedly shouting,

"One more!"

Though puzzled by their reaction, the battle continued.

Shawn silently recalled Beedrill before sending out Scyther.

Its strength was immediately apparent.

After using Double Team, two illusionary copies moved across the battlefield alongside the real Scyther, making it almost impossible to distinguish the original.

Thanks to that extraordinary technique, Scyther pushed Bulbasaur to the brink of defeat.

But in the end, Bulbasaur lashed out with Vine Whip, striking every image simultaneously. By eliminating the fakes, it successfully located the real Scyther and defeated it.

( End of Chapter )
