"I'm Poika, the harbor administrator of Vermilion Port."

The blue-haired woman stepped forward. Perhaps because she had spent years managing the harbor and dealing with troublesome crowds of passengers, there was a hint of impatience in her expression as she addressed everyone.

"If you want to know what the sea is like, then go listen to the sound of the waves yourselves." Folding her arms, she clearly had no intention of explaining further. "Even though I grew up by the sea, I've never seen a storm as violent as today's!"

"Is it really that serious?"

Poika nodded expressionlessly.

"And the place you're trying to reach—New Island—is right at the center of this storm. In weather like this, I absolutely will not reopen the harbor and let any ship risk sailing out."

Seeing how firm she was, a slightly overweight Trainer in the crowd said dismissively,

"That's no problem. My Pokémon is an excellent swimmer. If ships can't sail, I'll just ride my Pokémon across the sea!"

His words were echoed by a few others, but most of the Trainers didn't share his confidence.

"No, you mustn't head out recklessly!" Officer Jenny quickly stepped in to stop them. "Putting aside how dangerous it is to sail in weather like this, if your Pokémon get injured, the Pokémon Center can't even treat them right now!"

"Oh? Why is that?"

Officer Jenny pointed toward a missing-person notice posted nearby and sighed.

"For some unknown reason, the local Nurse Joy suddenly disappeared. We've been searching for her for an entire week, but we still haven't found a single clue."

Hearing this, many people were shocked, and quite a few immediately abandoned the idea.

"Maybe there's no need to rush..." someone muttered. "Why don't we just wait until the storm passes before heading to New Island?"

Although many people backed out, it did nothing to change Ethan's or Ash's minds.

Ethan took out his phone and checked the coordinates they had just received.

New Island had originally been nothing more than a deserted island in the middle of the sea. At some point, however, Mewtwo had transformed it into its own domain.

Because it was so far away, reaching it required considerable time and effort.

Normally, Trainers would first take a ship into the open sea, then disembark once they were close enough and use other means to reach the island.

But now, with no ships available, they would have to rely entirely on themselves.

By this point, including the overweight Trainer, several people had already rushed out of the terminal.

Some released Pokémon like Pidgeot or Fearow, risking lightning strikes as they flew through the storm.

Others rode Water-type Pokémon such as Dewgong, heading straight into the sea.

Within minutes, more than a dozen Trainers had departed. Seeing this, Officer Jenny hurried after them, but it was already too late to stop them.

Inside the terminal, Ethan exchanged glances with Ash and the others.

They nodded in unison before running outside as well.

The world beyond the terminal had become as dark as night. Torrential rain lashed down so fiercely that it was nearly impossible to keep their eyes open, while flashes of lightning provided only brief moments of illumination.

When they reached the pier and looked out over the raging ocean, they all hesitated.

Brock scratched his head.

"What should we do? None of us has a Pokémon that can carry us across the sea."

"My Charizard can fly," Ash said helplessly, "but it refuses to carry us."

Just as Ethan was about to send out Gyarados to transport everyone, a wooden boat rowed over from the distance.

A man and a woman dressed as Viking sailors stood aboard it. Their faces were hidden beneath masks, making them impossible to recognize.

The most eye-catching feature of the modest boat was its figurehead—a helmeted statue that resembled a young girl.

"You all seem to be in trouble," the female sailor said with a teasing smile as she held her oar. "Well? Since this is a special situation, we don't mind giving you a free ride."

"Really? Thank you so much!" Ash gratefully jumped aboard first.

Brock and Misty followed without the slightest suspicion.

Although none of the three noticed, Ethan recognized at a glance that the two "sailors" were none other than Team Rocket's trio in disguise.

He didn't know what they were planning, but if it meant conserving Gyarados's stamina, he certainly wasn't going to object.

Still...

Could such a flimsy boat really survive this storm?

As Jessie and James rowed, the boat slowly left the harbor.

Buffeted by fierce winds and towering waves, it tossed about like a drifting leaf, looking ready to capsize at any moment.

Yet thanks to the pair's surprisingly skillful coordination, it somehow continued pushing deeper into the storm despite rocking violently and seeming ready to fall apart.

Whoooosh!

The journey remained surprisingly smooth until an enormous wave suddenly surged toward them!

In the blink of an eye, it crashed into the boat.

Ethan barely had time to take a deep breath before instinctively grabbing the side of the vessel.

Everything went black as seawater swallowed him whole.

Fortunately, a few seconds later, once the wave had passed, everyone found themselves still sitting safely inside the boat.

However, the wave had washed away Team Rocket's disguises, revealing their true identities to Ash and the others.

"You guys!?" Brock frowned. "Seriously, why do you always show up wherever we go?"

Jessie and James struggled to their feet and burst into laughter.

"Since you're asking so sincerely—"

"Then we'll graciously answer—"

As usual, they were about to launch into their motto when another gigantic wave came crashing toward them!

"Ahhh!"

Jessie had barely let out a scream before the wave slammed into them.

This time, the force was overwhelming.

The wooden boat instantly shattered into pieces, and everyone was swept away by the raging sea.

Dragged beneath the surface, Ethan was sinking rapidly when, before he could react, someone grabbed his hand.

It was Misty.

She immediately released her Starmie, which carried both of them back to the surface.

When the wave finally subsided, they saw Ash and Brock surfacing as well, helped by Squirtle.

Looking around, however, they realized that Team Rocket was nowhere to be seen.

No one had time to worry about them.

Taking advantage of the brief lull, Ethan immediately released Gyarados.

"Everyone, climb on quickly! And remember—if another giant wave hits us, don't let go this time!"

( End of Chapter )
