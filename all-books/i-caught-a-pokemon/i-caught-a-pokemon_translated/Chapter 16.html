The moment Onix appeared on the battlefield, its enormous body and the cold, disdainful gaze it gave toward smaller Pokémon instantly startled Pikachu.

"Pika! Pika!"

Pikachu quickly ran back to Ash, lifted up his jacket, and tried pressing the buttons on his other Poké Balls, clearly attempting to swap itself out for another Pokémon!

"Hey, hey!" Ash grabbed Pikachu and lifted it up in frustration. "Pikachu! Stop trying to switch with Pidgeotto and get back out there to battle!"

Under Ash's pushing and urging, Pikachu reluctantly returned to the battlefield.

Using an Electric-type against a Ground-type…

From the spectator stands, Ethan Vale smiled silently.

Not understanding type matchups and battling recklessly was both Ash's greatest weakness and his greatest strength. It was precisely because of this mindset that he was often able to defeat powerful opponents with unpredictable and creative strategies.

But right now, Ash was still far too inexperienced…

Shaking his head, Ethan Vale pulled out his Pokédex and pointed it at Pikachu.

Ash's Pikachu looked no different from ordinary Pikachu.

Its fur was a smooth golden yellow, with long ears, two red electric sacs on its cheeks, and a lightning bolt-shaped tail. Its bright black eyes made it look especially adorable.

Pokédex: "Pikachu, the Mouse Pokémon. Electric-type. Also known as the Electric Mouse Pokémon. The electric sacs in its cheeks store electricity. When angered, it releases the stored power all at once to unleash electric attacks."

Ethan Vale then aimed the Pokédex at Onix.

Onix had a massive rocky horn on its head, and its towering body was composed of huge gray boulders linked together like a giant serpent. It looked incredibly intimidating and powerful.

Pokédex: "Onix, the Rock Snake Pokémon. Rock- and Ground-type. Its body is composed of massive boulders linked together like a giant snake. It tunnels underground while swallowing dirt, moving at speeds of up to 80 kilometers per hour."

With Brock's command, the battle officially began.

"Onix! Use Tackle!"

Hearing the order, Onix's gigantic body lunged forward, smashing toward Pikachu with its massive head.

Pikachu jumped and scrambled around in panic, intimidated by Onix's terrifying presence. It focused only on escaping and completely forgot to counterattack.

Soon, Onix seized the opportunity and wrapped Pikachu tightly with its tail.

"Excellent! Onix, use Bind!"

Seeing Pikachu trapped helplessly in Onix's crushing grip, struggling painfully, Ash anxiously shouted:

"Pikachu, use Thunder Shock!"

At Ash's command, sparks burst from Pikachu's cheeks before a powerful electric blast exploded outward.

Yet despite the attack landing directly on Onix, it caused absolutely no damage.

Across the field, Brock crossed his arms and said calmly:

"As a Trainer, your methods are completely misguided. Did you really think a simple Electric attack could defeat my Onix? That's far too naive."

"Darn it!"

Ash clenched his fists tightly through gritted teeth. Seeing Pikachu suffering more and more while remaining unable to escape, he finally shouted helplessly:

"Please stop! I surrender this match!"

After Brock nodded, Onix finally loosened its tail. Pikachu dropped to the ground, where Ash immediately rushed onto the field and scooped it into his arms.

"Are you okay, Pikachu?"

Seeing the battle end, Ethan Vale slowly walked down from the spectator stands, unsure how to comfort him.

Still, knowing Ash's personality, he would probably recover quickly enough.

Walking onto the Trainer's platform, Ethan Vale looked at Brock and said:

"Alright, the appetizer's over. Now it's my turn."

Looking at Ethan Vale, Brock recalled Onix and took out another Poké Ball.

"You seem completely different from that previous challenger."

"Right now, he's still immature and lacking experience," Ethan Vale said with a smile. "But after enough growth and training, I believe he'll eventually become a Pokémon Master admired by everyone."

Ash, who had just returned to the spectator stands while carrying Pikachu, suddenly turned around when he heard those words.

It was the first time someone had openly acknowledged his potential, and his eyes couldn't help becoming slightly moist.

This person… really believes in me that much?

"Come out, Geodude!"

At that moment, Brock threw his Poké Ball. A flash of blue light appeared, and a round rocky Pokémon with massive muscular arms materialized before Ethan Vale.

Pokédex: "Geodude, the Rock Pokémon. Rock- and Ground-type. It usually lives in mountainous regions, burying half its body underground while observing passing hikers. Because it resembles an ordinary rock, many people accidentally step on it and trip."

Turning around, Ethan Vale's eyes moved between Squirtle and Bulbasaur before finally settling on Bulbasaur.

"Bulbasaur, this battle is yours!"

"Bulba! Bulba!"

Whether it was Water-type Squirtle or Grass-type Bulbasaur, both held a double type advantage against Brock's Rock- and Ground-type Pokémon, meaning either one could deal quadruple damage.

No matter which one he chose, this battle already looked heavily one-sided.

Up in the spectator stands, Misty gently touched Pikachu's forehead before teasing Ash:

"Looks like Ethan Vale understands type advantages a lot better than you do. Unlike a certain idiot, he actually knows how to battle like a real Trainer."

Surprisingly, Ash didn't argue back.

Instead, he carefully watched Ethan Vale's every movement.

Perhaps Ethan Vale's earlier encouragement had made Ash begin taking him much more seriously.

Once Bulbasaur and Geodude were both in position, the match officially started.

"Geodude, use Tackle!"

Ethan Vale immediately countered:

"Bulbasaur, let's show them our new move—Razor Leaf!"

Before Geodude could even get close, countless glowing green leaves suddenly formed around Bulbasaur and shot toward Geodude at high speed.

"Geodude, dodge it quickly!" Brock shouted.

However, Razor Leaf was simply too fast.

Even though Brock gave the command in time, the attack still struck Geodude directly.

It's super effective!

After the explosion and smoke cleared, Geodude wore a pained expression.

Seeing this, Brock immediately ordered:

"Geodude, use Rock Throw!"

Geodude thrust both arms upward, and instantly several massive rocks materialized beneath the Gym ceiling before crashing down toward Bulbasaur.

The attack covered too wide an area.

Knowing Bulbasaur lacked the speed to dodge, Ethan Vale decisively commanded:

"Bulbasaur, aim at the rocks and use Razor Leaf!"

Green light gathered once more as Razor Leaf fired upward, smashing apart most of the falling rocks.

However, many fragments still rained down onto Bulbasaur's body.

Yet despite being struck by the debris, Bulbasaur calmly remained standing in place as though the damage was hardly worth mentioning.

(End of Chapter)
