After printing copies of the script and distributing them to everyone, it was finally time for the intense rehearsal session.

Everyone participating in the performance—both people and Pokémon—gathered beside the swimming pool, memorizing their lines and practicing their movements and stage positions.

"Misty, your swimming needs to be more graceful! Really imagine that you're a mermaid. And remember—don't let your body look too stiff!"

"Mr. Ethan, try to put a little more emotion into your performance. Your tone should sound more like that of a prince!"

Daisy's voice echoed throughout the Gym through a loudspeaker. Although she seemed especially strict with Ethan and Misty, it couldn't be helped. After all, they were the lead actors, and the quality of their performances would directly determine how successful the play would be.

After Ethan's revisions, the performance still included ballet elements, but it now resembled a stage play much more than a traditional ballet.

The story went like this:

In the peaceful underwater kingdom, the Mermaid Princess lives a carefree life until a prince accidentally falls into the sea, disrupting the tranquility. After spending time together, the two gradually fall in love.

However, a jealous witch interferes, doing everything she can to prevent this romance between two different races. At the same time, a ruthless pirate raids the prince's kingdom.

With the help of the prince's loyal maid, a faithful sailor, a crafty merchant, and a group of kind-hearted Pokémon, the prince and the mermaid confront the sea monster Gyarados summoned by the witch. In the end, they defeat the monster, overcome both the pirate and the witch, save the kingdom, and restore peace.

Finally, as the curtain slowly falls, the prince and the Mermaid Princess embrace one another in a happy ending.

After dinner at the Gym, everyone continued rehearsing until midnight.

For more than eight hours, they repeated every scene over and over until everything finally looked polished, with almost no mistakes left.

"I'm exhausted..."

Ethan wiped the sweat from his forehead.

It wasn't just him—even supporting actors like Ash and Brock had become completely dizzy from memorizing lines and stage positions.

"Alright, everyone."

Acting as the temporary director, Daisy clapped her hands and finally called it a day.

"You all did wonderfully today, but make sure you get plenty of rest. You'll need your energy for tomorrow's performance."

She then turned to Ethan, Ash, and Brock.

"Thank you all so much for your hard work. I've already prepared rooms for you. Please follow me."

Following her deeper into the Gym, the three eventually stopped in front of several guest rooms.

Smiling warmly, Daisy said,

"These will be your rooms tonight. I won't disturb you any longer. Sweet dreams, everyone."

After saying goodbye to Daisy, Ethan looked at the room door and quietly sighed in relief.

Thank goodness... They're private rooms.

At least tonight I won't have to listen to Ash grinding his teeth or Brock snoring.

Once Daisy left, Ethan entered his room.

His eyelids were already growing heavy. After washing up, he was just about to climb into bed when he heard a gentle knock on the door.

Suppressing another yawn, he opened it.

Standing outside was Misty.

"What's wrong?" Ethan glanced at the time. "Is something the matter?"

"Were you about to sleep?" Misty hesitated before asking softly. "I can't seem to fall asleep... Can I come in and sit for a while?"

"Of course."

Ethan stepped aside and invited her in.

The two sat on the bed and chatted casually for a while.

Only then did Ethan learn that after returning home for the first time in so long, combined with tomorrow's performance, Misty had become so excited that she couldn't sleep.

After Ethan comforted her for a while, her mood gradually relaxed.

Seeing him yawning repeatedly, she finally smiled and stood up to leave.

"Thank you, Ethan. I feel much better now."

The truth was, her insomnia wasn't only because of tomorrow's performance.

At first, she had strongly resisted appearing in the show.

But after Ethan joined the cast—and especially because the script now included romantic scenes between them—she had completely changed her mind and was actually looking forward to it.

Before leaving, she turned back toward him with sparkling eyes.

"I really hope everything goes smoothly tomorrow... I hope nothing goes wrong."

After a good night's sleep, everyone felt refreshed.

Although the performance wasn't scheduled to begin until 11:20 a.m., by 10:00 a.m., spectators had already started entering the Gym.

"Wow! It's been so long since we've had this many people!" Lily exclaimed happily.

"It looks like changing the show really was the right decision!"

Although most of the audience consisted of parents bringing their children, seeing a long line forming outside the Gym made her smile from ear to ear.

Except for Daisy and her sisters, everyone else was performing on stage for the very first time.

As the audience continued to grow, they couldn't help feeling nervous.

Backstage, Daisy encouraged them.

"Just relax. Pretend the audience isn't even there. As long as you completely immerse yourselves in the performance, you won't be affected."

Everyone changed into their costumes, and after a final round of preparations, their finished appearances were revealed.

First was Ethan.

His age and natural temperament suited the role perfectly. He looked energetic, handsome, and every bit like a prince. Everyone had always believed he'd perform the role exceptionally well.

Next came Misty in her Mermaid Princess costume.

Compared to her usual tomboyish appearance, wearing her hair down made her look noticeably more mature and beautiful.

Then came Brock, Daisy, Violet, and Lily, all of whom looked excellent in their respective costumes.

Finally...

Everyone turned to look at Ash.

Wearing a long wig and heavy makeup, he admittedly looked a little awkward...

But surprisingly, he really did resemble a young girl.

Pikachu circled around him, looking him up and down before collapsing onto the floor in laughter.

"Pika! Pika!"

"Oh, come on, Pikachu! Stop laughing!"

Ash scratched his head awkwardly.

Fortunately, after dressing as a girl so many times during his travels, he wasn't nearly as embarrassed as he once would have been.

Meanwhile, while everyone busied themselves backstage...

Three pairs of scheming eyes were already watching them.

Above the ceiling, peering through a narrow crack, a woman's voice whispered,

"Did you hear everything they said?"

"Of course," a man's voice replied.

"A ballet performance... I actually kind of want to watch it."

"Idiot! Now's not the time for that!"

Another raspy, gender-neutral voice suddenly became louder before quickly lowering again.

"Look at all these Water-type Pokémon gathered together! This is a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity! We absolutely can't let it slip away. We're going to steal every last one of them!"

The owners of those voices were none other than the ever-persistent Team Rocket trio.

"But... steal them how?" James asked in confusion.

"There are so many people—and all these spectators. If we make a move now, we'll be discovered immediately."

"You fool!" Meowth glared at him before grinning slyly.

"Since it's a stage performance, we can join the show openly! Then, while we're on stage, we'll steal the Pokémon right in front of everyone. By the time anyone realizes what's happening, they won't be able to stop us without ruining the performance!"

"Brilliant!"

James nodded enthusiastically.

"That's actually a fantastic idea! I didn't know that clever brain of yours was capable of coming up with something like this!"

Hearing that, Jessie's eyes lit up with excitement.

"So that means... we get to perform too!?"

She clasped her hands together happily.

"Wonderful! I've always dreamed of performing on a stage. My dream is finally going to come true!"

( End of Chapter )
