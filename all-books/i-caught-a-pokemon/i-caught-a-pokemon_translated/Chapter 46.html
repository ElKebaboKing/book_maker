Protecting the elderly man behind him, Ethan decided to strike first.

"Everyone, attack together!"

All of his Pokémon launched their moves simultaneously.

In an instant, colorful attacks flew everywhere as they bombarded Murkrow and Arbok.

While commanding her Pokémon to dodge, Athena sneered,

"So you like using numbers to overwhelm your opponents? Your style really is different from ordinary trainers."

Ethan did not bother responding to her mockery.

After all, this was not an official trainer battle. There was no need to follow any battle rules.

However, Athena's Pokémon were undeniably powerful.

Even while being surrounded and attacked from all sides, they still managed to find openings to counterattack.

"Murkrow, use Wing Attack! Arbok, use Acid Spray!"

Naturally, Ethan had no intention of backing down.

"Charmander, use Ember! Squirtle, use Water Gun!"

The attacks collided head-on and exploded violently.

But Ethan still held the numerical advantage.

"Jolteon, use Thunder Shock! Ivysaur, use Razor Leaf!"

Unable to dodge in time, Murkrow and Arbok were both struck by the attacks, letting out painful cries.

"Tch!"

Athena gritted her teeth.

In the end, she had underestimated the man standing before her—and even more so, the number of Pokémon he possessed.

"Good!" Seeing his attacks land successfully, Ethan pressed the advantage. "Kirlia, use Psychic and hold them in place!"

Arbok, which had been dodging attacks moments earlier, suddenly froze under Psychic's invisible restraint.

Seizing the opportunity, Ethan's Pokémon immediately focused their attacks on it, bombarding it with multiple moves at once.

After taking repeated hits, Athena finally realized the situation was turning against her.

No matter how unwilling she was, the circumstances forced her to make the most rational decision possible.

"Damn it, we're retreating!"

As soon as she finished speaking, she recalled Arbok into its Poké Ball.

Then, after taking a running start, she leapt directly out of the shattered office window—from more than twenty floors above the ground!

The next instant, Ethan saw a black blur streak past him.

Murkrow swooped forward, grabbed Athena by the arm, and carried her far away from Silph Co.

Only after confirming Athena had truly left did Ethan finally let out a long breath.

Looking at the devastated office around him, he turned apologetically toward the elderly man.

"Sorry for turning your office into this."

The old man smiled kindly.

"It's alright. Compared to staying alive, this amount of damage means nothing."

Unexpectedly, Athena's retreat seemed to trigger a chain reaction.

The sounds of battle in the corridor had completely disappeared.

There were no more voices from the Team Rocket members.

When Ethan opened the office door, he came face-to-face with Sabrina.

"Are you alright?" Ethan asked with concern while glancing past her shoulder toward the corridor behind her.

"I'm fine." Sabrina shook her head. "But for some reason, while we were fighting, they suddenly all retreated."

At this moment, aside from the damage left behind by the battle, there was no trace of Team Rocket anywhere in the corridor.

Even the unconscious Team Rocket members had all been taken away.

"Maybe their plan was disrupted and kidnapping this gentleman became impossible, so they decided to withdraw."

As he spoke, Ethan recalled the rest of his Pokémon, leaving only Kirlia and Gastly outside.

The old man's gaze silently shifted between Ethan's face and the Poké Ball containing Zapdos before he subtly nodded.

With sincere gratitude, he said,

"Thank you both so much. Without your help today, not only would this company have been completely destroyed, but the entire world might have suffered a catastrophe."

He was not exaggerating.

If the Master Ball truly fell into Team Rocket's hands and entered mass production, it would undoubtedly cause devastating damage to the world's ecological balance.

"We only did what we could," Ethan replied.

Then he turned to Sabrina.

"There are still many researchers being held elsewhere in the building. I think we should call the police and let them handle the rest."

Sabrina nodded and went downstairs to deal with the remaining matters.

Meanwhile, Ethan sent Gastly to scout the area and ensure no hidden dangers remained nearby.

After everything was finally settled, the old man invited Ethan back into the office once more.

"My apologies. I haven't properly introduced myself yet," the elderly man said. "My name is Silph, and I am the president of this company."

Ethan had already guessed as much.

"Nice to meet you, Mr. Silph. I'm Ethan, a Pokémon Trainer."

Silph invited Ethan to sit on the heavily damaged sofa and studied him silently for quite some time, as though finally making up his mind about something.

He slowly walked toward one wall and activated a hidden switch, revealing a highly advanced safe concealed behind it.

After scanning his fingerprint, he opened the safe and removed a single Poké Ball from within.

This Poké Ball looked completely different from ordinary Poké Balls or Great Balls.

Its lower half was standard white, while the upper half was blue-purple.

On both sides were pink semicircles resembling fly eyes, and in the very center was a large letter "M."

Walking over to Ethan, Silph handed him the Poké Ball solemnly.

"As a token of gratitude, I would like to give you this Master Ball."

"This gift is far too valuable." Ethan quickly stood up.

To him, although the Master Ball was incredibly precious, he still preferred capturing Pokémon he liked through battle.

"Please accept it." Silph let out a long sigh. "Only a few Master Balls have been produced so far, yet they already caused such chaos. If the research is ever fully completed, there's no telling what kind of disaster it might bring."

"Then why not seal it away forever?" Ethan asked.

Silph shook his head as though he had seen through everything.

"No matter where treasure is hidden, greedy people will always come searching for it. It would be better for you to decide what to do with it."

Seeing how firm Silph's attitude was, Ethan thought for a moment before finally accepting the heavy gift with both hands.

After Sabrina returned, Ethan decided to quietly leave before the police arrived.

After all, neither Zapdos nor the Master Ball was something he wanted exposed to the public.

The two bid farewell to Silph and took the elevator out of the building.

As they watched the setting sun slowly descend beyond the skyline, both of them felt as though an entire lifetime had passed, even though only a few hours had actually gone by.

At that moment, Sabrina spoke softly.

"Well then, now that the mission is complete, I should return to my Gym."

Ethan turned to look at her.

Though he wanted to say words of gratitude, in the end, he simply extended his right hand.

"I hope we'll meet again someday."

Sabrina nodded and gently clasped his hand with her soft fingers.

"My intuition tells me that we definitely will."

(End of Chapter)
