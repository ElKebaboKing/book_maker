Silph Co.!?

Wasn't this the company headquarters occupied by Team Rocket in the Pokémon games!?

Ethan observed the building more carefully.

Everything on the first floor appeared perfectly normal. Security guards were patrolling back and forth, and even the receptionist at the front desk sat there lazily yawning in boredom.

Yet somehow, the entire place gave him a strange feeling.

At that moment, a police car sped past Ethan with its siren blaring. However, instead of stopping at Silph Co., it continued farther down the road.

Ethan looked into the distance and listened carefully.

Several streets away, some sort of disturbance seemed to be taking place.

Strange…

How had the entire city changed so much just from the time he spent battling at the Gym?

It felt as though something major was happening.

Walking into a deserted alleyway, Ethan climbed onto a higher rooftop and pulled a pair of binoculars from his backpack, looking toward the upper floors of Silph Co.

The smoke there had mostly dissipated.

Through the windows, he could see several people dressed in black uniforms emblazoned with large red "R" symbols forcing a group of researchers toward another area while using fire extinguishers to put out scattered flames.

It really was Team Rocket!

After thinking briefly, Ethan found a public phone booth and called the police.

The phone rang for a long time before someone finally answered.

However, after Ethan explained the situation, the response he received left him utterly shocked.

"What? You already sent officers to Silph Co. and found nothing unusual?"

"That's right!" the person on the other end replied impatiently. "We've already gone there twice, and everything was completely normal. Right now Team Rocket is causing disturbances all across the city. We're overwhelmed already and don't have extra manpower to deal with pranks like yours!"

"Wait, this isn't a prank, I—"

Halfway through speaking, Ethan sighed and hung up the phone.

Now he completely understood Team Rocket's plan.

First, Team Rocket themselves had anonymously reported suspicious activity at Silph Co., drawing the police there.

But when the police arrived, everything appeared perfectly normal, so naturally they dismissed it as a prank and returned to the station.

Then, Team Rocket dispatched outer members across the city to create chaos and divert police attention while their main force began taking over the building.

Even if researchers inside the building later managed to call the police for help, the officers arriving again would simply be dealt with by the disguised Team Rocket members stationed in the lobby.

Because of the earlier false alarm, the police would not investigate too carefully and would assume it was merely another prank call.

At that point, Team Rocket could safely seize the entire Silph Co. building!

What an ingenious strategy.

However, since Ethan had discovered it, there was no way he could simply ignore Team Rocket's actions.

With that thought, he took several photos with his phone and sent them to Handsome.

Unfortunately, the pictures had been taken slightly too late. They did not capture Team Rocket members or the kidnapped researchers—only lingering smoke and traces of fire remained visible.

Handsome quickly called him back.

"So it really is a major Team Rocket operation?" Handsome sounded excited, yet also regretful and frustrated. "Damn it! I'm currently in Celadon City investigating a Team Rocket base. Even if I leave immediately, I probably won't make it in time."

"It definitely is a major operation," Ethan replied slowly. "And judging by the situation, they've probably already taken complete control of the building."

Handsome asked,

"Did you call the police?"

"Of course."

"What did they say? Are they responding?"

Ethan gave a bitter smile and explained everything he had just heard, along with his deductions.

"I see…" Handsome quickly understood the situation. "Looks like this was carefully planned from the beginning. But why attack Silph Co. specifically?"

Silph Co. was one of the most famous manufacturers and distributors in the Pokémon world.

Many important products—including Poké Balls themselves—were produced by the company.

Yet with Team Rocket's wealth, they should not need Poké Balls at all.

And once the incident became public, it would certainly trigger a major police counterattack.

No matter how Handsome thought about it, he still could not understand Team Rocket's true objective.

"Who cares why?" Ethan said casually. "Even if Team Rocket went there just to celebrate the company president's birthday, we still can't let them succeed."

"You're right." Handsome laughed before suddenly changing his tone. "But your photos don't actually show Team Rocket members. Even if I submit them to headquarters and coordinate with the police, they probably won't trust the report."

Ethan blinked.

"Your status in the International Police is really that low? You don't even have enough authority to convince them?"

"Ahem… let's not discuss that right now…" Handsome coughed awkwardly from the other side of the phone. "The urgent priority is figuring out Team Rocket's true objective. Could you help me with that?"

Although Ethan already intended to help, he still replied,

"Hey, aren't you an International Police officer? Asking an ordinary civilian like me to get involved in something this dangerous doesn't seem very appropriate."

"You're not ordinary at all!" Handsome praised immediately. "From what happened aboard the S.S. Anne, it's obvious you're a talented trainer with a strong sense of justice. And I won't leave you to handle this alone. I'll immediately contact the Pokémon League through some personal connections and have them send someone to assist you!"

Someone from the Pokémon League?

They handle situations like this too?

After hanging up, Ethan stood in the shadows and continued observing Silph Co.

A short while later, a young woman around his age approached from behind.

Turning around, Ethan was stunned.

It was Sabrina!?

"Why are you here?"

Seeing Ethan also surprised Sabrina slightly.

"The Pokémon League contacted me earlier," she explained. "They said an International Police officer needed assistance. I honestly didn't expect it to be you."

"I'm not an International Police officer," Ethan quickly waved his hand. "I'm just helping someone out. Did they explain what happened?"

Sabrina shook her head.

"They only said the matter was urgent and asked me to come here for assistance and follow instructions. So I hurried over immediately."

Having Sabrina's help would definitely be a tremendous advantage.

So Ethan explained the entire situation to her once again.

"So all we need to do is infiltrate the building and determine what's happening inside?" Sabrina turned toward Silph Co. and asked, "Then how are we supposed to get in?"

"There's no way we can sneak in through the first floor anymore." Ethan had clearly already formulated a plan. "Originally, I intended to have Kirlia use Psychic to lift me into the building through the air while Gastly scouted around inside. But now that you're here…"

He smiled.

"We'll go in together."

(End of Chapter)
