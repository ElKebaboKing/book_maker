Seeing this, Ethan instinctively moved closer to Charmeleon, hoping the flame on its tail would provide at least a little warmth.

After passing through the narrow tunnel, the space around him suddenly opened up into an enormous cavern, as though half the mountain had been hollowed out. Above them, sunlight streamed through cracks in the ceiling, making the place far brighter than he had imagined.

Looking around, the cave system stretched in every direction, with countless passages leading into the unknown.

The signs of battle here were obvious as well. Judging from the shattered stone walls and broken pillars, who knew how many Trainers had managed to pass through the maze and reach this place over the years?

"Follow me."

Led by Haunter, Ethan continued deeper inside, turning left and right through the maze-like caverns until they finally arrived before a tunnel barely one meter wide.

The cave itself was inhabited by countless Pokémon. Along the way, pairs of glowing red eyes continuously lit up in the darkness—wild Pokémon silently staring at him with obvious hostility.

Yet upon seeing such a large group of intruders together, none dared attack.

Beside the tunnel entrance were claw marks dug into the stone. Ethan crouched down and touched the scattered rocks and dirt nearby. The tunnel had long since dried and hardened. It clearly wasn't created through battle, but rather dug casually by Pokémon such as Sandshrew while moving underground.

Peering inside, the tunnel descended straight downward into complete darkness. Even shining his flashlight into it, Ethan could not see the bottom at all!

Haunter grinned.

"Don't underestimate this little tunnel. They may appear everywhere underground, but this one's a shortcut! If we go down here, we can pass through several cave layers straight to the deepest area!"

Since Haunter had already investigated it thoroughly, Ethan naturally did not hesitate. He recalled the rest of his Pokémon temporarily while Haunter used Ominous Wind to gently surround him. Together, Trainer and Pokémon slowly descended into the tunnel.

The cramped passage was so narrow Ethan could barely stretch his limbs, but with Haunter protecting him, he felt no fear for his safety.

Layers of stone and dirt rushed past before his eyes. In such a short time, they had already passed through at least four underground levels.

Eventually they reached the bottom and arrived in a vast underground chamber—but aside from rocks, there was absolutely nothing here.

Based on the depth alone, Ethan realized they were already below sea level. For ordinary Trainers, reaching this place without incredible luck or immense effort would have been nearly impossible.

Although the temperature here was even colder, there was still no sign of ice nearby, meaning this was not yet their destination.

"So where do we go next?" Ethan asked.

"Even farther down." Haunter grinned widely. "But from here on, we'll be taking a very special route!"

A special route?

Continuing to follow Haunter through narrow rocky crevices, Ethan soon heard the thunderous roar of rushing water. Before long, they arrived at the edge of a massive waterfall.

Haunter glanced at him and smiled.

"Well, we're here!"

CRASHHH!!

The deafening sound of the waterfall nearly drowned out Haunter's voice entirely. Ethan had to shout at the top of his lungs:

"This is the special route you were talking about!?"

Seeing Haunter nod, Ethan could not help smacking his lips. Walking to the cliff's edge and looking downward, he saw the enormous waterfall crashing violently into the depths below.

"Well, I guess there's no other option besides this 'water route.'"

Letting out a sigh, Ethan released Kirlia. Since he could now see solid ground below, Kirlia used Teleport to bring him directly to the deepest level of the Seafoam Islands!

Within the massive underground cavern, giant icicles hung from the ceiling while thick layers of ice covered the ground beneath their feet. Standing there felt as though they had entered a frozen world sealed in eternal winter!

And because the countless ice crystals reflected the surrounding light, the place actually appeared brighter than the caves above.

But in exchange came bone-piercing cold.

Exhaling a frosty breath, Ethan quickly released Charmeleon and hugged it tightly. At that moment, the burning tail flame was the only thing keeping him alive.

"Charmeleon, I'm only now realizing how incredibly wise it was to catch you back then!"

Charmeleon looked helpless and even tried to push him away.

At that moment, Haunter floated beside him and pointed toward the distance.

"Look over there!"

Ethan raised his head. Not far ahead, the waterfall flowing down from above had gathered into a small lake. And in the middle of that lake stood a tiny island!

On the island grew a dense forest of crystal-clear ice pillars. From afar, it looked like some magical frozen sanctuary straight out of a fantasy world—the Ice Temple itself!

"If Articuno is hiding anywhere, it has to be there!"

Staring at the island, Ethan casually snapped a few photos before crouching down and touching the lake water. The cold was so intense it felt like his fingers were about to freeze off.

"Damn, it's freezing!?"

Ethan hurriedly dried his fingers against Charmeleon's body and turned to Kirlia.

"I really don't want to soak my clothes. Use Teleport again and take us across."

Kirlia nodded softly and placed its hands on Ethan and Charmeleon. In the next instant, Ethan felt a flash before his eyes—and suddenly they were standing on the island itself!

But before he could even look around properly, a freezing shockwave burst from the center of the island and swept directly past him!

Fortunately, Kirlia reacted instantly, protecting Ethan with Psychic power and shielding him from the icy blast. Even so, every breath he took afterward felt like torture on his lungs!

So this was Articuno's intimidation!?

Ethan's teeth chattered violently from the cold as he hurriedly pulled out a bottle of antidote medicine from his backpack and gulped it down.

A burning warmth spread from his stomach through his entire body, almost like drinking a bottle of strong liquor, gradually driving away the freezing cold inside him.

When Haunter floated beside him, Ethan smiled.

"I really should've prepared thicker clothes ahead of time. But it looks like we definitely came to the right place. Articuno must be in there!"

"So what are we waiting for?" Haunter cackled. "Since it already attacked you, hurry up and beat it!"

"That really is a reassuring thing to hear." Ethan tossed several Poké Balls into the air, releasing all of his Pokémon. "But before that, as always, we should test the waters first."

Perhaps Haunter's arrogant words had finally angered the master of this frozen sanctuary.

A sharp, resonating cry suddenly echoed throughout the Ice Temple.

Then, from within the forest of ice pillars, a massive blue bird Pokémon flew out, staring directly at them with cold, piercing eyes.

And it was none other than the legendary Pokémon countless Trainers dreamed of obtaining—

Articuno!
