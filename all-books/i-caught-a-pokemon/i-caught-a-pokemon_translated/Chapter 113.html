Gyarados let out a mighty roar and lunged at the octopus robot.

The robot refused to back down. Several of its tentacles wrapped tightly around Gyarados as the two clashed violently beneath the water.

The fight looked so real—brutal and intense—that the audience's blood began to boil with excitement.

"Wow! That fight scene looks so realistic! It's like they're actually battling!"

"Look! Part of the robot's armor has been bitten off! Are they really destroying the prop instead of reusing it?"

"The armor isn't the point!" another spectator exclaimed, pointing upward. "They even blew a huge hole in the Gym's ceiling just for this performance! Is that really okay?"

Compared to Gyarados, the octopus robot was far too fragile.

After enduring Gyarados' relentless bites, it was soon covered in damage.

Inside the cockpit, red warning lights flashed wildly.

Meowth gripped the control lever with all his strength, sweat pouring down his forehead.

"That Gyarados is way too strong! The machine has reached its limit—we can't keep this up!"

"Then let's retreat!" James said, already satisfied with their results. "Even though we didn't get much stage time—or Pikachu—we caught plenty of Pokémon! The Boss is definitely going to praise us!"

"We're leaving already?" Musashi was the only one who looked disappointed. "I even wore a ballet dress! I wanted to show everyone my dancing!"

"You idiot!" Meowth snapped. "If you don't want to fail like always, leaving now is the smartest thing we can do!"

Under Meowth's control, the octopus robot leapt high out of the water, grabbed the hole in the ceiling with its tentacles, and escaped through it.

Even now, the audience still believed everything was part of the show.

None of them realized that Team Rocket had just committed an actual robbery right before their eyes.

"This is bad!" Brock shouted. "They're getting away!"

Sakura looked equally panicked.

"They took Misty too! What do we do?"

What should they do?

Ayame and Daisy exchanged anxious glances.

Should they stop the performance?

But if they did, the very show they had organized to restore the Gym's popularity would become a complete disaster.

The Gym's reputation would suffer even more.

Yet without the heroine, continuing the play seemed impossible.

Still...

Compared to all of that, Misty's safety—and the Pokémon's safety—was obviously far more important.

Just as Ethan was about to announce the performance's cancellation and go rescue Misty, Ash's voice suddenly came through the earpiece.

"Just keep performing! Leave Misty and the Pokémon to me!"

With that, he ran off with Pikachu without looking back.

"..."

Ethan hesitated.

Ash had certainly become stronger, but knowing how crafty Team Rocket could be, Ethan wasn't entirely comfortable leaving everything to him.

Then Brock's voice sounded through the headset.

"Ash can't handle this alone. I'll go help him too. Leave the rest of the performance to you."

Having traveled with Ash for so long, Brock knew better than anyone how reckless and careless his friend could be.

He simply couldn't trust Ash to face Team Rocket alone.

"Then I'm counting on both of you."

Sakura let out a relieved sigh.

Splitting into two groups seemed like the best solution available.

As the two hurried away, Ayame asked worriedly,

"Will they really be alright?"

"We have no choice but to trust them."

Sakura forced a smile.

"Compared to them, our problem is even more serious."

While the audience was still applauding Team Rocket's dramatic exit, the cast finally had a brief moment to think.

"Sis... what do we do now?"

"At this point," Sakura decided without hesitation, "we can only keep going."

"Ayame, you'll replace Misty as the heroine for now."

"Everyone, listen carefully and improvise!"

"Me?" Ayame stared in surprise.

"But I'm playing the witch!"

"It can't be helped."

Sakura frowned.

"We're short on actors, and the story has already been ruined. We'll have to rewrite it on the spot."

"I understand!"

Ayame and Daisy both nodded firmly.

Despite years of stage experience, neither of them had ever encountered a situation like this.

Even so, they gritted their teeth and stepped back onto the stage.

Inside the broadcasting room, Sakura quickly pieced together a brand-new storyline.

She began narrating:

"The unexpected villains kidnapped the Mermaid Princess. Left with no other choice, the maid and the sailor set off to rescue her. Burning with vengeance, the prince reluctantly sought the witch's help to defeat the pirates who had invaded his homeland..."

The prince teams up with the witch!?

The audience nearly got whiplash from the sudden twist.

Yet strangely enough...

They were even more excited to see what happened next.

Through the earpieces, Sakura continuously gave instructions.

Meanwhile, Ethan and the others moved like puppets on strings, relying entirely on one another's improvisation.

Truthfully...

Even Ethan himself had no idea what he was acting anymore.

Yet somehow...

The audience absolutely loved it.

Finally, with the combined help of the witch and Gyarados, the prince defeated the pirates and reclaimed his kingdom.

"Excellent!"

By this point, Sakura had finally relaxed a little.

Still, she reminded everyone,

"We're almost at the ending! Don't lose focus now!"

Just then, Brock's voice finally returned through the headset.

"Miss Sakura! We rescued Misty and all the Pokémon! Everyone's safe! We're rushing back now! We'll be there in about ten minutes!"

"Ten minutes?"

Sakura glanced at the clock.

"It's too late."

Turning to Ayame, she said,

"Don't wait for them. Let's finish the play."

She immediately continued narrating.

"...After defeating the pirates, the prince and the witch finally lived happily ever after."

The play entered its final scene.

Peace had returned.

The kingdom was restored.

Originally...

This was supposed to be the moment when the prince embraced the Mermaid Princess.

But now...

Just as Misty and the others came rushing back into the Gym, breathing heavily...

She looked toward the stage.

There—

Ethan and Ayame were already embracing one another.

Because the story had changed so drastically, a simple hug no longer felt like a satisfying ending.

It seemed as though something was missing.

Suddenly, inspiration struck Sakura.

"You two..."

"Kiss!"

"Kiss!?"

Both Ethan and Ayame were stunned.

But from the audience's perspective...

The two were already gazing deeply into each other's eyes.

"Kiss!"

"Kiss!"

"Kiss!"

The audience enthusiastically chanted in unison, completely swept up in the emotion of the performance.

Ethan hesitated before lowering his head toward Ayame.

He glanced at her.

She quietly nodded.

There's no other choice...

As Ethan slowly leaned closer...

Ayame shyly closed her eyes.

The curtain and the pool slowly descended.

Before everyone's eyes—

Their lips met.

No...!!

Watching from backstage, silent tears rolled down Misty's cheeks.

That was supposed to be my moment...

The romantic scene I'd been looking forward to...

Why... why did it end up like this...?

Unlike Misty's heartbreak, the audience rose to their feet in thunderous applause.

No one wanted to leave.

"That was incredible!!"

"I know! I've never seen a plot twist like that before!"

"Especially the ending! I never expected the prince to end up with the witch! I want to watch it all over again!
