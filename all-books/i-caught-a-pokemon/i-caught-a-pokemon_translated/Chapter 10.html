Far out at sea beyond Cerulean City, a fishing boat slowly sailed across the ocean.

The sea breeze brushed gently against them as Ethan Vale and his three Pokémon leaned against the deck, enjoying a rare moment of relaxation.

Warm sunlight poured over their bodies, making them feel pleasantly lazy and unwilling to move.

Of course…

If the ship hadn't constantly reeked of fish, the trip would probably have been much more enjoyable.

But there was no helping it. Life rarely turned out perfectly.

After more than an hour of sailing, the fishing boat finally stopped near the edge of a small island.

The weathered captain stepped out from the cabin and shouted toward Ethan Vale on the deck.

"Kid, this is the place you were looking for. You can get off here now. We still need to sail south into deeper waters. We'll come back tonight to pick you up. Until then… good luck!"

"Thanks a lot for the help."

After thanking him, Ethan Vale and his Pokémon jumped off the boat.

Only after watching the fishing boat sail into the distance did he turn around and examine the island.

The island was surrounded by dense green forests, and at its center stood a solitary mountain. Shrubs grew across the mountain in neat patterns, making it resemble the shell of a giant turtle from afar.

As for why Ethan Vale had come here…

This was Turtle Island.

According to rumors, the island did not exist on any map. Only the Pokémon living here and the most experienced fishermen knew its exact location.

And just as its name implied, the island was home to a Blastoise along with a large group of its unevolved forms—Wartortle and Squirtle.

That was right. Ethan Vale had specifically come here to capture one of these Pokémon!

In fact, he had spent a long time searching the harbor and asking countless people before finally finding someone willing to take him out to sea in search of this island.

However, his target was not the Blastoise.

Crossing the beach and entering the forest, Ethan Vale quickly discovered a group of playful Squirtle and Wartortle.

The Pokémon gathered together carefree and relaxed, completely at ease in their environment.

So this really is Turtle Island. They're everywhere…

Taking out his phone, Ethan Vale snapped several photos nearby. He originally intended to send them to Professor Cerise, but after noticing there was no signal whatsoever, he had no choice but to give up for now.

Forget it. I'll send them later.

Moving a little closer to the group, Ethan Vale observed them quietly for a while before finally stepping forward and standing in front of them.

The Squirtle and Wartortle were startled by the sudden appearance of a human, but none of them fled. Instead, they simply stood there and watched curiously.

It was obvious that despite living far from the mainland, these Pokémon were not afraid of humans.

In fact, they were curiously observing him as well!

"Hello, everyone." Ethan Vale smiled warmly as he greeted them. "As you can see, I'm a trainer. I'm currently traveling around searching for companions and becoming stronger so I can challenge the League one day. Is there anyone here willing to leave this island and travel with me?"

In this wonderful world, nearly all Pokémon could understand at least some human language, allowing Ethan Vale's words to reach them clearly.

Unfortunately, as a human, he still couldn't understand Pokémon speech.

On the other side, after hearing Ethan Vale's words, the Squirtle and Wartortle exchanged glances but showed no reaction.

Only several seconds later did one Squirtle finally step forward, perhaps out of curiosity—or simple mischievousness.

"Squirtle! Squirtle!"

"You've got courage." Ethan Vale laughed. "Since you're interested, let's battle. If I win, you'll become my partner."

Perhaps sensing an upcoming battle, the surrounding Pokémon voluntarily cleared an open space.

Not a single one stepped forward to stop it.

It was almost as if they were already accustomed to scenes like this.

Ethan Vale also walked forward with his Pokémon until he stood opposite the Squirtle.

Before the battle began, however, he took out his Pokédex and scanned the blue turtle Pokémon in front of him.

The Pokédex immediately responded.

"Beep. Squirtle, the Tiny Turtle Pokémon. A Water-type Pokémon skilled at Water-type attacks. Its sturdy shell protects it from danger, and it can unleash powerful Water Gun attacks against enemies."

Good. Just as I remembered.

The reason Ethan Vale did this was partly to gather information and partly to confirm that the Pokémon in this world truly matched his memories.

So far, everything remained exactly the same.

Glancing toward his side, Ethan Vale waved his hand toward Bulbasaur.

"Bulbasaur, this battle is yours!"

"Bulba! Bulba!"

Hearing the command, Bulbasaur stepped forward and stood opposite Squirtle.

The two Pokémon stared at one another from a distance. No words were needed as fighting spirit slowly surfaced in both of their eyes.

Seeing both Pokémon prepared, Ethan Vale decisively gave the order.

"Bulbasaur, use Vine Whip!"

At his command, two vines shot out from Bulbasaur's bulb and lashed toward Squirtle.

Yet Squirtle remained completely calm. Just before the attack landed, it suddenly retracted its head and limbs entirely into its shell.

Smack! Smack!

Although Vine Whip was a Grass-type move, Squirtle's durable shell successfully blocked the attack. The only visible effect was a slight vibration of the shell.

As expected, withdrawing into the shell was the greatest defensive weapon of turtle Pokémon!

The moment Squirtle emerged again, a stream of Water Gun shot from its mouth directly toward Bulbasaur.

However, just like Bulbasaur's earlier attack, the Water Gun was far too easy to predict.

"Bulbasaur, dodge!"

Ethan Vale quickly ordered.

Unfortunately, just as Bulbasaur moved to evade, Squirtle suddenly turned its head, causing the stream of water to follow Bulbasaur's movements. Although Bulbasaur dodged at full speed, half its body was still drenched by the moving Water Gun.

There was no helping it—Bulbasaur simply lacked the speed advantage.

At the same time, it also proved that this Squirtle clearly knew how to battle effectively!

"Bulbasaur, use Leech Seed!"

A seed shot rapidly from Bulbasaur's bulb.

Yet just as it reached Squirtle, the little turtle spat out a massive amount of bubbles from its mouth, completely neutralizing the attack!

Seeing this, Ethan Vale couldn't help praising it.

"For a wild Pokémon, you really are impressive."

"Squirtle! Squirtle!"

Hearing the compliment from the human trainer, Squirtle proudly puffed out its chest.

Although paired with its round and slightly comical head, the result looked more amusing than intimidating.

Shaking his head, Ethan Vale pointed forward and became slightly more serious.

"Bulbasaur, continue using Vine Whip!"

The vines lashed forward once more, and just like before, Squirtle immediately withdrew back into its shell.

However, this time Ethan Vale's attack did not stop there.

Under his command, the vines no longer retreated. Instead, they wrapped tightly around Squirtle's shell layer after layer.

"Squirtle!?"

Squirtle was completely shocked. It tried poking its head out, but every opening around the shell had already been sealed shut. No matter how much it struggled, it could not move.

"This is what you call trapping yourself in your own cocoon." Ethan Vale smiled faintly before ordering, "Now lift it up and let it experience the power of a giant swing!"

Following Ethan Vale's command, Bulbasaur lifted Squirtle high into the air with its vines before smashing it heavily against the ground.

Then came a second slam. Then a third.

Even though Squirtle was protected by its shell…

After five or six brutal impacts, the shell finally stopped responding altogether.

With a wave of Ethan Vale's hand, Bulbasaur finally released the vines.

The shell rolled several times before stopping on the sand.

Seeing no movement whatsoever from inside the shell, Ethan Vale threw a Poké Ball toward it.

The Poké Ball traced a perfect arc through the air before arriving above Squirtle.

A beam of red light shot out, enveloping Squirtle and drawing it inside.

Click.

The Poké Ball landed upon the sand.

Perhaps because Squirtle had been completely knocked dizzy, it no longer possessed any strength to resist. The Poké Ball didn't even shake once!

Click.

As the button locked shut, it officially signaled a successful capture.

Excellent. My fourth Pokémon is secured!

However, before Ethan Vale could celebrate, an enormous roar suddenly echoed from the distance.

At the same time, the ground itself began trembling.

Judging by the sound alone, some incredibly powerful Pokémon was rapidly approaching.

Ethan Vale raised an eyebrow.

Looks like the battle earlier finally alerted the Blastoise ruling this island.

Naturally, a human intruding into its territory would make it extremely unhappy.

Still, that was fine. Ethan Vale had already achieved his goal.

The only problem was…

Checking the time, Ethan Vale realized only half an hour had passed since arriving on the island.

At this rate, unless he wanted to fight that Blastoise, he would need to find a place to hide until the fishing boat returned.

Well, fine. Consider it a way to pass the time.

With that thought, he picked up the Poké Ball and grinned toward his Pokémon.

"Bulbasaur, come back. Time to retreat—we're going to play hide-and-seek with that big guy for a while!"

…

Time passed, and the sun and moon changed places.

By the time Ethan Vale left the sea and stepped back onto land, he was once again standing within the streets of Cerulean City.

After resting overnight at the Pokémon Center, Ethan Vale set out once again on his journey toward Pewter City, now accompanied by four Pokémon.

During his free time, he also sent all the photos he had taken on Turtle Island to Professor Cerise.

As a special field investigator for the Cerise Laboratory, documenting the island's ecosystem could certainly be considered fulfilling his duties perfectly.

Of course, he could have chosen not to bother, but since he was already traveling and exploring, taking photos simply added more memories to his journey.

Walking along the wide streets toward the city exit, Ethan Vale was just about to leave Cerulean City when a green-haired young man carrying a backpack—someone around Ethan Vale's age—suddenly stepped forward and blocked his path.

"You there in front! Would you accept my challenge?!"

(End of Chapter)
