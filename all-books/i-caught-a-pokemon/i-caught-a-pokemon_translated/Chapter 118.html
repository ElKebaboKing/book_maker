Although Mewtwo did not speak aloud, a calm, gender-neutral voice suddenly echoed directly inside everyone's minds.

"My rules are mine to decide. I don't need humans telling me what to do!"

Brock frowned.

"That voice... Telepathy!?"

As Mewtwo finished speaking, it casually raised one arm and unleashed its psychic power.

In the next instant, Hayden was enveloped in telekinetic energy. With a pained expression, he was lifted high into the air. Then, with another effortless wave of Mewtwo's hand, Hayden's body was tossed aside like a piece of trash and crashed into the nearby pool.

The moment they met, Mewtwo had established its dominance with overwhelming force, instantly intimidating everyone present.

Glancing at Gardevoir, Ethan quietly whispered,

"We still don't know why this guy invited me. Stay alert at all times."

Beside him, Ash and the others pointed their Pokédexes at Mewtwo, trying to scan it. However, regardless of whose Pokédex—or which model—they used, no data appeared.

"Is that really a Pokémon?" someone muttered. "Or... some kind of alien life-form?"

"You actually attacked me!?"

By now, Hayden had climbed out of the pool. Gritting his teeth, he glared at Mewtwo before shouting,

"Go, Gyarados! Hyper Beam!"

Like Ethan, Hayden also owned a Gyarados among his six Pokémon.

At its Trainer's command, Gyarados lunged toward Mewtwo. Opening its jaws wide, it gathered a brilliant sphere of energy before unleashing a massive Hyper Beam straight at its target.

However, just as the beam reached Mewtwo, it calmly raised its left hand and formed a shimmering psychic barrier.

Not only did the barrier effortlessly block the attack—it reflected it.

The rebounded Hyper Beam slammed directly into Gyarados.

Hit by its own attack, Gyarados was blasted backward and followed its Trainer into the pool.

"W-What!?"

Hayden was horrified. He hurried back to check on Gyarados, while everyone else, witnessing Mewtwo's terrifying power, no longer dared to act recklessly.

"Very well. You're no longer of any use."

Amid the tense silence, Mewtwo waved its hand once more.

Nurse Joy suddenly collapsed to the floor like a puppet with its strings cut.

Brock immediately rushed over and caught her.

"Are you alright?"

Joy slowly opened her eyes and looked around in confusion. Her expression regained its warmth and humanity, and the nun-like hood slipped from her head, revealing her trademark red hair.

"Where... am I? Why am I here?"

"To care for me," Mewtwo answered calmly. "Because of your profession, you possess extensive knowledge of Pokémon physiology. I brought you here from the Pokémon Center, and thanks to your assistance, my experiments progressed smoothly."

"I... helped you?"

Joy rubbed her forehead.

"But... why can't I remember any of it?"

"With my power," Mewtwo replied, "I can freely manipulate the memories and thoughts of humans."

"I once wished to live alongside humanity... but I was disappointed."

"Humans are inferior beings compared to Pokémon. If such weak yet excessively arrogant creatures continue ruling this planet, it will inevitably be destroyed."

Logan snorted.

"So your solution is to let Pokémon rule the world instead?"

Unexpectedly, Mewtwo shook its head.

"Pokémon are no better."

"This planet has always been ruled by humans. Pokémon who have grown accustomed to living for humans are equally unqualified to inherit it."

Hearing those words, Pikachu leaped forward and angrily cried out at Mewtwo.

"You claim you're with your Trainer because you love him?"

Mewtwo stared coldly at Pikachu.

"How shameful. The very existence of such a belief is a mistake."

Its eyes flashed blue.

A powerful psychic force blasted Pikachu away.

Ash caught Pikachu in his arms, and the two were hurled backward for over ten meters before crashing to the ground.

"Only weak Pokémon depend on humans."

At this point, Mewtwo's intentions had become perfectly clear.

Including Claire and the other two Trainers, everyone finally realized there was no "world's strongest Pokémon Trainer."

It had all been a lie to lure them here.

"Enough of your nonsense!"

Logan sneered.

"A Pokémon is still just a Pokémon. No matter how rare you are, I can catch you! Go, Rhyhorn!"

Rhyhorn charged toward Mewtwo with thunderous footsteps.

But before it could even reach its opponent, Mewtwo seized it with telekinesis and violently hurled it back.

Rhyhorn crashed beside Logan, smashing the massive table cleanly in half.

The overwhelming display of power finally made everyone understand the enormous gap between themselves and Mewtwo.

"Give up."

"From the moment I was born, I have been stronger than every Pokémon on this planet."

Yet instead of discouraging Ash, Mewtwo's words only ignited his fighting spirit.

"You'll never know unless you try!"

Mewtwo looked at him calmly.

"So... you truly wish to challenge me?"

Ash nodded firmly.

"More than anything."

Mewtwo activated its psychic power once again.

This time, however, no one was harmed.

After several seconds, a section of the floor slid open, and three Pokémon rose from below on a large platform.

They were a Charizard, a Blastoise, and a Venusaur.

However, unlike ordinary members of their species, strange markings covered their bodies.

Mewtwo spoke calmly.

"They are Pokémon created through my cloning technology."

"Cloning!?"

While everyone was still reeling in shock, the walls of the hall slowly retracted, revealing an enormous open-air battlefield behind them.

"Since you wish to challenge me..."

"Then let us battle."

"Using the exact same species of Pokémon, we'll discover whose command is truly superior."

Provoked by Mewtwo's challenge, the Trainers naturally refused to back down.

Logan sent out Venusaur.

Claire released Blastoise.

Ash opened his Poké Ball and called out Charizard.

One after another, they left the hall and headed toward the battlefield.

None of them noticed that Ethan deliberately slowed his pace until he was walking at the very back.

After everyone had gone ahead, he did not follow them.

Instead, he suddenly turned around, slipped back through the entrance, and quietly entered the castle's corridor.

The long hallway was silent and empty as Ethan hurried forward, with Gardevoir remaining faithfully at his side.

At that moment, Haunter emerged from Ethan's shadow and asked curiously,

"At a time like this, why are you sneaking away?"

"Aren't you interested in watching their battle?"

( End of Chapter )
