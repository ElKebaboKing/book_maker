As the performance drew closer, Sakura took the narration script and left backstage for the broadcasting room.

Besides the actors' performances, the narration was the key element that connected each scene and kept the story moving forward. It was one of the most important parts of the entire production.

She placed the script in front of the microphone on the desk before leaving the room again to take care of other preparations.

The moment she was gone, however, the ventilation duct above quietly opened.

The three members of Team Rocket climbed down in secret.

Musashi walked over to the desk, picked up the narration script, and read through it with a wicked grin.

"Tch! A cliché story like this? Who would enjoy something so ordinary? Let me improve it a little!"

Picking up a marker, she began scribbling all over the script, adding her own "creative" revisions.

Just as she was about to make more changes, footsteps echoed from outside.

Startled, the trio hurried back into the ventilation shaft.

The broadcasting room fell silent once more, as though nothing had ever happened.

When Sakura returned, she naturally had no idea that someone had boldly altered her narration script in just a few short minutes.

Without even glancing at the papers on the desk, she opened a storage cabinet, took out several headsets, and left the room again.

Back in the dressing room, she handed one to everyone.

"These are our new waterproof earpieces. Wear them so you won't forget your lines if you get nervous. If anything goes wrong during the performance, I'll be able to remind you through these."

Ethan Vale accepted the headset and clipped it onto his right ear.

It was compact and nearly matched his skin tone. Unless someone looked closely, they wouldn't even notice he was wearing it. It also fit securely enough that even vigorous movements wouldn't knock it loose.

"Is everyone ready?"

Except for the Cerulean sisters, everyone nodded with serious expressions.

"Don't be so nervous." Sakura clenched her fist encouragingly. "Alright, it's almost time. Let's all do our best!"

Time slowly ticked away.

By the poolside, the audience seating—which could hold nearly a thousand people—was already completely full.

Quiet conversations filled the air as everyone eagerly awaited the new performance.

When the scheduled time finally arrived, Sakura's voice echoed throughout the Gym.

"Thank you all for waiting. The Cerulean Sisters' Water Ballet Theater presents... 'The Mermaid Princess and the Prince'!"

The lights instantly dimmed.

Under the spotlight, the massive swimming pool slowly rose upward until it resembled a giant aquarium displayed before the audience.

Gasps of amazement spread through the crowd.

The unique stage had already won everyone's attention.

Meanwhile, Sakura calmly began reading the narration.

"Deep beneath the calm and peaceful sea, the Mermaid Princess and the Pokémon lived together in happiness..."

As her narration continued, Misty gracefully dove from a high platform into the water, elegantly swimming several laps before joyfully playing with the Pokémon.

Soon it was Ethan's turn to enter.

While fighting pirates, the prince was knocked into the sea.

Fortunately, Misty discovered him in time and carried him safely to the surface.

The two drifted together on a floating barrel, sharing a brief conversation.

It was during this encounter that they began developing feelings for one another.

Despite having no acting experience whatsoever, both performers exceeded everyone's expectations.

The story progressed smoothly.

"You two are doing great!" Sakura praised them through the earpieces. "You don't look like beginners at all! Brock, get ready. You're up next."

Accompanied by music that alternated between gentle melodies and tense rhythms, the play continued.

Ash, Brock, and the others each made their entrances.

Everything was unfolding exactly as planned.

Occasional applause came from the audience.

When he found a spare moment, Ethan secretly glanced toward the crowd.

The reaction isn't bad at all.

Their initial nervousness gradually disappeared, replaced by growing confidence.

As the story reached its climax, the evil witch unleashed the sea monster—Gyarados.

According to the original script, after Sakura's narration, Ethan and the others were supposed to join forces to defeat the giant beast.

Inside the broadcasting room, Sakura focused on the script as she read aloud.

"...The gigantic sea monster opened its enormous jaws, preparing to swallow the prince whole. But at this critical moment... an even more evil enemy suddenly descended from the sky and... huh?"

An even more evil enemy?

The moment the words left her mouth, Sakura realized something was terribly wrong.

The earlier pages had been completely normal.

But this section was covered with handwritten edits everywhere.

Someone changed the script!?

Her thoughts raced.

But the performance was already live.

She couldn't simply stop reading.

After only the briefest hesitation, she forced herself to continue.

"An even more evil enemy suddenly descended from the sky... defeating both the prince and the princess... before capturing every Pokémon..."

What?

On stage, Ethan and the others were completely dumbfounded.

What kind of plot twist is this?

This was never in rehearsal!

The instant Sakura finished reading, loud, arrogant laughter echoed throughout the Gym.

BOOM!

The ceiling exploded open.

A gigantic octopus-shaped robot crashed down from above, landing heavily in the pool.

Huge waves splashed across the front rows of the audience.

The sudden chaos stunned not only Ethan and the others but even the Pokémon.

Only the unsuspecting audience remained excited, believing this was an ingenious new part of the performance.

Thunderous applause erupted.

"Hahaha!" Musashi's voice rang out through the robot's loudspeaker. "Heroes always arrive at the end—but now the lovable and charming villains have appeared! We'll defeat you fake heroes and purify these waters!"

The familiar voice instantly shocked everyone.

Team Rocket!?

"Why are you here?!" Brock shouted.

"Because we're the most important villains in this story, of course!" Musashi laughed loudly behind the robot's glass canopy. "How could a performance like this possibly be complete without us? Go, Meowth! Let's capture every one of these Pokémon!"

Several of the robot's tentacles suddenly transformed into enormous vacuum nozzles, sucking up massive amounts of water.

The powerful suction immediately pulled smaller Pokémon like Goldeen and Horsea into the machine.

"Horsea!!"

Misty cried out in panic.

She swam forward to rescue it, but the powerful current wrapped around her as well.

Before anyone could react, she too was sucked inside the octopus machine.

Not good!

Ethan's heart sank.

He immediately turned toward his own Pokémon.

Ignoring the performance entirely, he silently signaled Gyarados with his eyes—

Destroy that machine.

( End of Chapter )
