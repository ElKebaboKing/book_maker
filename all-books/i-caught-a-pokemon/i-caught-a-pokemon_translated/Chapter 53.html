"Who are you people!? How dare you block our path!?" Kane shouted furiously.

"You're the ones getting in our way!" Jessie's voice came from inside the giant Meowth robot. "We're trying to deal with that brat right now, so if you know what's good for you, get out of here!"

As she spoke, the robot's upper body began spinning rapidly. Its enormous mechanical cat claws whirled like giant bladed fans, generating a powerful hurricane!

Watching the machine, Ethan could not help secretly giving a thumbs-up.

He honestly had no idea where they had managed to get equipment like this in such a short amount of time.

"Damn it!" The violent winds blew the hats off the Matori Matrix members, and while they were distracted, the freight train sped away along the nearby tracks right before their eyes.

Kane roared angrily,

"You dare sabotage the operation!? I'll report everything to the Boss exactly as it happened!"

"What nonsense are you talking about!?" James looked down at Kane through the robot's glass canopy. "We're carrying out our mission here! Since when have we sabotaged anything?"

At this point, no matter how furious Kane was, there was nothing he could do.

He could only settle for making Ethan the top priority once again.

However, when he turned his attention back toward Ethan, he discovered that after the intense fighting, their Pokémon were already falling into a disadvantageous position!

"Squirtle, use Water Gun! Ivysaur, use Razor Leaf!"

The first breakthrough came from Squirtle and Ivysaur.

This Water-and-Grass-type combination was devastating against the Ground-and-Rock-type Rhyperior. Every attack they landed dealt quadruple damage!

Although Rhyperior was already in its second evolutionary stage, its enormous bulk made it too slow to avoid the relentless assault, and eventually it collapsed from exhaustion.

"Tch!" One of the Matori Matrix members clicked his tongue in frustration and recalled it with a Poké Ball.

"Don't let your guard down!" Kane shouted loudly to encourage his teammates. "He's only one person! There's no way we can lose to him!"

But before he even finished speaking, another Pokémon fell.

As a Poison-and-Fighting-type Pokémon, Toxicroak lasted only a few rounds before Kirlia used its psychic powers to completely overwhelm it, pinning it down with absolutely no chance to fight back!

These two Pokémon had been defeated quickly due to unfavorable type matchups.

However, the remaining three Pokémon were far more troublesome.

First was Persian.

As a Normal-type Pokémon, it had no major strengths in terms of typing, but aside from Fighting-type attacks, no other types could deal super-effective damage to it. Moreover, despite looking like a rather plump cat, it possessed astonishing speed completely inconsistent with its appearance.

Among Ethan's Pokémon, only Jolteon could keep up with it.

The two Pokémon chased and tangled with each other continuously, eventually disappearing somewhere far from the battlefield!

Next came Skuntank.

This Poison-and-Dark-type Pokémon was equally troublesome because none of Ethan's Pokémon could inflict extra damage against it. Combined with its Stench ability, every attack it launched carried an unbearable foul odor that made it nearly impossible for other Pokémon to approach!

Finally, there was Kane's Aggron.

This Pokémon was genuinely powerful. Although it was at a type disadvantage against Gyarados, it had already gained the upper hand.

"Aggron! Use Iron Tail!"

Following Kane's command, Aggron swung its massive tail using the last remnants of its composure and slammed it heavily against Gyarados's head!

Gyarados let out a painful roar as its enormous body crashed to the ground.

Just as Kane was about to command Aggron to press the advantage, the giant Meowth robot happened to walk directly in front of it.

"Watch my Heavy Stomp!" Meowth grinned wickedly as he controlled the robot's legs, repeatedly lifting and slamming them into the ground, creating continuous shockwave attacks.

However, instead of finishing off Gyarados, those shockwaves dealt massive damage to the Steel-and-Rock-type Aggron, knocking it flat onto the ground!

"Idiots! What are you doing!? Stop right now!" Kane shouted anxiously, rushing forward to stop them, only to be knocked over himself by the vibrations.

"We're helping you, you know!" Jessie covered her mouth and giggled. "You shouldn't be so ungrateful."

"What a perfect opportunity!"

Seeing Aggron collapse, Ethan's eyes lit up.

He immediately recalled Gyarados with a Poké Ball while shouting toward Ivysaur and Squirtle, who had rushed over for support.

"Attack now!"

The next second, a Water Gun and a barrage of Razor Leaves struck Aggron head-on!

Meanwhile, after succeeding once, Meowth continued piloting the giant robot with heavy stomping steps toward Skuntank.

Most of Skuntank's attacks were close-range moves, and with Kirlia's assistance, Charmander was barely managing to hold the line.

"Skuntank, use Bite!"

At the loud command of one of the Matori Matrix members, Skuntank suddenly accelerated, opened its jaws wide, and viciously lunged toward Charmander.

But at that exact moment, the Meowth robot suddenly stepped between the two Pokémon.

Unable to stop in time, Skuntank smashed its head directly into the robot.

Bang!!

The collision was extremely heavy.

Not only was Skuntank left dizzy and disoriented, but its proud foul odor could not penetrate into the robot at all!

"Hehehehehe…" Team Rocket's trio grinned proudly.

At this point, everyone present finally realized their true intentions.

Although Ethan found it strange and somewhat confusing, he did not pay it much attention.

After all, both groups were enemies in his eyes. Whoever stood in his way had to be defeated!

Meanwhile, the members of the Matori Matrix also realized that Jessie and James had been deliberately sabotaging them at every turn.

Frowning deeply, they gathered around Kane.

"Captain, what should we do?"

"We'll retreat for now." Kane stared coldly at Ethan as he spoke in a low voice. "Compared to that International Police officer, this guy's strength is genuinely terrifying. If this fight continues, we won't win."

"But…" One of the members glanced hatefully at the giant Meowth robot, unwilling to accept the situation. "What about those two?"

"We absolutely cannot let this go!" Kane said viciously. "We're going back to report everything to Miss Matori. We'll make sure the Boss punishes them severely!"

"Yes, sir!"

The members quickly recalled their Pokémon and retreated together with Kane, disappearing into the distance.

"They actually left?" Watching them flee in disgrace, Jessie's mood instantly improved considerably.

However, she still had not forgotten the mission and immediately turned her attention back toward Ethan.

"Now that those annoying people are gone, it's finally time for you to witness our true power!"

As she spoke, the giant Meowth robot charged straight toward Ethan with massive stomping steps.

(End of Chapter)
