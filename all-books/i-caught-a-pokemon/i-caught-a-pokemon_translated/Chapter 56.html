"Charmander, keep using Ember!"

Since he had the type advantage, Ethan had no reason to switch tactics. He simply intended to overwhelm Tangela with continuous Fire-type attacks.

"Tangela, use Bind!" Erika called out loudly.

While constantly dodging the flames, Tangela suddenly made a sharp turn. Seizing an opening, the vines covering its body rapidly shot forward and tightly wrapped around Charmander!

Was she trying to close the distance and use close-range combat despite the type disadvantage?

But for Ethan, this was exactly what he wanted!

Under the effects of Chlorophyll, Tangela's speed was incredibly fast. Ethan had originally been wondering how to finish the battle while conserving the most stamina possible. Now that Tangela had practically delivered itself to him, there was no way he would miss the opportunity.

"Charmander, let your Ember travel along its vines!"

Tightly restrained by the vines, Charmander endured the crushing pressure with a painful expression. Upon hearing Ethan's command, it opened its mouth and spat out a burst of Ember. Using the vines as a conduit, the flames raced directly toward Tangela's main body!

"This is bad!" Erika cried anxiously. "Tangela, let go immediately!!"

But it was already too late.

The fire rapidly spread along the vines and engulfed Tangela in an instant!

The next second, Tangela's entire body burst into flames, making it look like a moving fireball!

"Nicely done!" Ethan calmly commanded. "Charmander, close the distance now!"

By the time Tangela finally freed itself from the flames, it realized an orange figure had already appeared right in front of it!

Before it could react, another Ember blasted out at Ethan's command, swallowing Tangela in flames once more!

Fire against Grass — it was super effective!

Seeing the severe burns covering Tangela's body, Erika gently shook her head and recalled it before sending out her second Pokémon.

This Pokémon resembled a giant bell with a huge mouth. Its yellow body had large leaves extending from either side, and its round black eyes gave it a somewhat vacant appearance.

Pokédex: "Weepinbell, the Flycatcher Pokémon. A Grass and Poison-type. The evolved form of Bellsprout. Though it resembles a plant, Weepinbell mainly feeds on Bug-type Pokémon. Once it discovers prey, it fires sharp leaves and paralyzes its target with poisonous powder before completely dissolving it with acid."

Although Charmander had taken some light damage earlier, Ethan had no intention of switching Pokémon.

And perhaps because of her earlier failure, Erika chose to take the offensive this time.

"Weepinbell, use Razor Leaf!"

Sharp leaves continuously shot out from Weepinbell's leafy stems. Ethan waved his hand, and Charmander answered with Ember.

Boom!!

The two attacks collided and canceled each other out in midair. Yet the moment the leaves and flames disappeared, Weepinbell suddenly charged silently toward Charmander.

"So fast? Don't tell me your Weepinbell also has Chlorophyll?" Ethan asked in surprise.

"You guessed correctly." Erika glanced at the Kirlia beside Ethan and smiled calmly. "Your Pokémon have the type advantage, but today's abundant sunlight helps balance the scales a little."

Oh?

That tone… and that slightly mischievous smile…

Was she subtly complaining that I only relied on type advantages?

Returning his attention to the battlefield, Ethan had already begun to figure out her strategy.

The moment Weepinbell closed in on Charmander, Ethan shouted,

"Charmander, use Smokescreen!"

A cloud of black smoke burst from Charmander's mouth. But just as the smoke enveloped it, Weepinbell suddenly sprayed a stream of thick yellow liquid toward the area where Charmander was hiding!

It was Acid — a Poison-type move that dealt damage while lowering the target's Special Defense!

Judging from the seamless coordination between Erika and Weepinbell, they had likely used this tactic countless times in previous Gym battles.

Although Charmander dodged in advance and had the cover of Smokescreen, it was still splashed by some of the acid.

A painful cry rang out from within the smoke, but Charmander quickly steadied itself again.

The smoke rapidly spread until it covered nearly half the battlefield. Weepinbell remained outside the smoke, but Charmander had completely vanished from sight.

The atmosphere suddenly changed.

The battlefield fell eerily silent, so quiet that even a pin drop could be heard. Yet even after waiting for some time, Charmander still did not reappear.

Erika glanced at Ethan. Seeing his calm expression, she immediately realized something was wrong.

Was this tactic meant to stall until Acid's effects weakened?

Thinking this through, Erika no longer hesitated.

"Weepinbell, use Razor Leaf!"

Weepinbell unleashed another barrage of leaves into the smoke. The swift spinning blades generated gusts of wind that quickly dispersed the Smokescreen.

"Now!!"

Ethan shouted the moment he saw the opening.

Charmander burst out from the opposite side while enduring the damage from Razor Leaf and directly fired an Ember at Weepinbell!

Because Weepinbell was still in the middle of using Razor Leaf, it was stuck in recovery and unable to dodge, forcing it to take the full hit head-on!

The flames engulfed Weepinbell!

It was super effective!

But Charmander's assault was not over yet.

It rushed straight toward Weepinbell, extending its claws and slashing repeatedly. After clawing at it more than a dozen times, it immediately followed up with Dragon Rage, releasing a shockwave at point-blank range that blasted Weepinbell to the ground.

Seeing Weepinbell collapse unconscious, the blue-haired referee raised her hand and loudly declared,

"Weepinbell is unable to battle!"

Erika silently recalled it and was just about to send out her final Pokémon when Charmander's body suddenly became wrapped in glowing light.

"Oh? It's evolving?"

After traveling with Ethan for so long, Charmander had finally accumulated enough experience to evolve!

Soon, as the light faded, the newly evolved Charmeleon appeared in its place.

Compared to Charmander, Charmeleon looked far fiercer. Its skin had darkened, its claws had grown longer and sharper, its eyes appeared more intense, and a horn-like protrusion had emerged on the back of its head.

Pokédex: "Charmeleon, the Flame Pokémon. A Fire-type. The evolved form of Charmander. It excels at using Flamethrower and can master many Fire-type moves. The power of its attacks is closely tied to its fighting spirit. When facing a strong opponent, it becomes extremely excited, breathing bluish-white flames while the fire on its tail burns even more fiercely."

The newly evolved Charmeleon let out a mighty roar toward the sky, radiating overwhelming fighting spirit!

Its evolution also meant that all three of Ethan's starter Pokémon had finally reached their second-stage forms!

Erika sighed softly.

Her Pokémon had already been at a type disadvantage, and now her opponent had suddenly evolved, making things even more difficult. The final battle would undoubtedly be even tougher.

Even though she disliked seeing her Pokémon get hurt, her pride as a Gym Leader still drove her to send out her final Pokémon.

"Go, my most trusted partner — Gloom!"

(End of Chapter)
