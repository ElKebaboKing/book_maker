Although Raichu was hit by Magical Leaf, since Grass-type attacks had no type advantage against Electric-types, it didn't take too much damage.

Still, the exchange caught Ethan Vale's attention.

Instead of relying on speed to dodge, Lt. Surge had chosen to counterattack head-on with another move?

At first glance, there seemed to be nothing wrong with it, but judging from the result, had he underestimated Magical Leaf's power?

Since the attack had proven effective, Ethan Vale naturally had no intention of changing tactics.

"Kirlia, let's do it again! Use Magical Leaf!"

As Magical Leaf began forming once more, Lt. Surge no longer ordered Raichu to use Thunder Shock. Instead, he shouted:

"Raichu, dodge it!"

However, Magical Leaf's firing speed was simply too fast. Even though Raichu moved early, Kirlia controlled the leaves' trajectory, causing them to chase after Raichu like a barrage of bullets before striking its body repeatedly.

Rat-tat-tat!

The explosions stirred up thick smoke that blocked everyone's vision. When the smoke finally cleared, Raichu was covered in fresh wounds. It shook its head and continued charging toward Kirlia.

"Raichu, use Mega Punch!"

Raichu stood upright on its hind legs, both fists glowing blue as it unleashed a barrage of punches toward Kirlia.

Mega Punch: A Normal-type physical attack in which the user strikes the opponent with maximum power, dealing heavy damage if it lands.

"Kirlia, use Double Team!"

Under Ethan Vale's command, Kirlia instantly split into over a dozen afterimages. Unable to identify the real one, Raichu could only wildly punch apart several fake illusions.

Lt. Surge frowned and shouted:

"Raichu, wipe them all out! Use Thunderbolt!!"

Hearing the command, Raichu abandoned its punches and gathered power throughout its body. Electricity crackled violently across its fur as dozens of thick electrical currents spread outward in every direction.

Crackling sounds echoed endlessly through the arena. The light was so blinding that even Ethan Vale had to shield his eyes with his arm.

When everything finally settled, Ethan Vale immediately looked up.

All the illusions had disappeared, leaving only Kirlia standing calmly in the distance, silently watching Raichu.

At the very last moment, it had used Teleport to evade the dangerous area attack.

"Good!" Ethan Vale finally relaxed. "Kirlia, use Confusion!"

Kirlia raised both hands.

Without warning, Raichu was lifted into the air before being slammed violently back onto the battlefield.

"Damn it!" Lt. Surge roared. "Raichu, get up! Close the distance!"

Raichu struggled back to its feet and unleashed the last of its strength as it rapidly charged toward Kirlia.

At that moment, Ethan Vale suddenly remembered Raichu's weakness.

In the anime, during Ash's second challenge against Vermilion Gym, he had discovered that Raichu evolved too early and never learned speed-related techniques. Pikachu had eventually used Agility and Quick Attack to outmaneuver and defeat it.

By then, Raichu had already reached Kirlia.

"Raichu, use Body Slam!"

Following Lt. Surge's command, Raichu leapt high into the air, spread out its body, and crashed downward toward Kirlia.

Although there were many ways to dodge the attack, Ethan Vale chose the simplest and most direct one.

"Kirlia, use Confusion!"

The next second, Raichu—still falling at high speed—suddenly stopped in midair as if it had slammed into an invisible wall.

Then it was lifted once more, like some invisible giant had grabbed it by the tail, swinging it violently through the air before hurling it aside.

Raichu crashed heavily onto the ground again.

This time, however, its eyes rolled back, and it failed to stand up.

The referee in the spectator area raised his flag and loudly announced:

"Raichu is unable to battle! Kirlia wins!"

Upon hearing Kirlia's victory announced, Chloe excitedly clapped from the stands.

Just as Ethan Vale had said earlier, watching a battle live truly was far more exciting than watching it on television.

And emotionally, since she was cheering for Ethan Vale, her feelings rose and fell along with the battle, making the experience even more thrilling.

Recalling Raichu, Lt. Surge nodded approvingly at Ethan Vale.

"The fact that you defeated my Raichu proves you're not some ordinary baby. But if you want my badge, you'll still have to beat this next one!"

With that, he threw out his second Poké Ball.

A flash of blue light appeared, and a strange yellow-and-black Pokémon emerged onto the battlefield.

The Pokémon stood upright on two legs, its yellow body covered in black stripes. A black lightning-bolt pattern marked its chest, while two short fangs curled outward from its mouth. Beside its pointed ears were two round organs used to store and release electricity.

Pokédex: "Electabuzz, the Electric Pokémon. Electric-type. The evolved form of Elekid. A very rare Pokémon. Electricity constantly flows across its body. Because it enjoys consuming electrical power, it often causes widespread blackouts in cities."

Ethan Vale recalled Kirlia and sent out Bulbasaur.

"Bulbasaur, the second round is yours!"

"Bulba! Bulba!"

Seeing Bulbasaur ready itself, Lt. Surge shouted:

"The real game is only starting now! Go, Electabuzz!"

Electabuzz strode toward Bulbasaur with powerful steps, clearly full of fighting spirit.

Ethan Vale gave the command:

"Use Razor Leaf!"

Countless glowing green leaves spun like blades as they shot toward Electabuzz.

Just like before, Lt. Surge responded immediately:

"Electabuzz, use Shock Wave!"

Two dazzling streams of electricity fired from the tips of Electabuzz's antennae, converging together and colliding directly with Razor Leaf.

This time, however, the two attacks canceled each other out completely, with neither side gaining an advantage.

Watching Lt. Surge continue using the same strategy, Ethan Vale finally understood.

Perhaps because of the Trainer's personality, just like Raichu earlier, this Electabuzz had also abandoned speed-focused techniques in favor of overwhelming offensive versatility.

As Electabuzz closed in, Ethan Vale gave another command:

"Bulbasaur, use Vine Whip!"

Two vines shot upward into the air before lashing fiercely toward Electabuzz.

Yet Electabuzz didn't dodge at all.

It charged straight through the attacks, enduring the Vine Whip strikes until it reached Bulbasaur at close range.

"Now!" Lt. Surge shouted. "Use Thunder Punch!"

The next second, electricity erupted around Electabuzz's fists as it swung both punches directly into Bulbasaur.

But Ethan Vale wasn't particularly worried.

From a type perspective, although Grass-types held no advantage against Electric-types, Electric-type attacks against Grass-types were resisted, dealing only half damage.

That was precisely why, back at Cerise Park, Ethan Vale had told Bulbasaur to conserve its stamina.

Because from beginning to end, the true ace for challenging Vermilion Gym had always been Bulbasaur.

(End of Chapter)
