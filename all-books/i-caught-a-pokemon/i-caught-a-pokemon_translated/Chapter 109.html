Daisy looked dejected, and the expressions on her two sisters' faces were no better.

"Maybe it's because our performances have become too repetitive," Daisy sighed. "Fewer and fewer people have been coming to watch. These past few days, the audience seats have been practically empty. There wasn't even anyone cheering."

As they explained the situation, everyone else grew more somber. Even Misty hadn't expected things at home to have become so serious.

Off to the side, Ethan stroked his chin thoughtfully.

Although the situation was certainly unfortunate, he had seen Daisy and her sisters perform before. Combined with the experience he had gained working behind the scenes after arriving in Kalos, it didn't take him long to identify the problem.

"The reason things turned out this way," Ethan said, "is probably because you've misjudged the direction of your performances."

Everyone immediately turned to look at him.

Daisy and her sisters stared at him in surprise.

"Huh?" Daisy asked. "Why do you say that?"

Ethan replied calmly,

"The last time I visited the Cerulean Gym, before battling you three, I watched your performance. How should I put it... it was entertaining, but it lacked variety. Honestly, after the show ended, when I tried to remember it later..."

He rubbed his chin.

"...the only thing I could clearly remember was a few pairs of long legs dancing across the water."

At his words, Misty shot him an annoyed look, while Daisy, Violet, and Lily exchanged awkward glances.

"Well..."

They had always been extremely confident in their performances. More importantly, they genuinely enjoyed showcasing their beauty through underwater ballet.

Hearing that the only impression Ethan had retained was their legs was undeniably hurtful.

Yet, whether they wanted to admit it or not, the steadily declining audience numbers had shattered their illusion.

Violet asked,

"What exactly do you mean by the 'direction' of our performances?"

"I mean your target audience," Ethan explained.

"From what I've observed, nearly everyone who comes here is an adult man. Their goal is simple—they come to admire your dancing. It's certainly pleasing to watch, but after seeing the same thing over and over again, people naturally grow tired of it and stop coming."

His reasoning was clear and logical.

No one felt as though he was speaking carelessly.

Beside him, Lily's eyes lit up with excitement.

"See? That's exactly what I was saying before!"

Daisy nodded as well.

"That's why we decided to bring Misty back and let her play the Mermaid Princess. She's the one who can save the Cerulean Gym!"

"No!"

Misty immediately refused.

"There's no way I can do something like that!"

"But..." Daisy said helplessly, "you saw it yourself. We've already put up the posters, and after people heard there would be a brand-new show tomorrow, every ticket sold out."

"Tomorrow!?"

Misty slammed her hand on the table and jumped to her feet.

"You're telling me you expect me to perform tomorrow without even asking me first? You've got to be kidding!"

"Relax, relax."

Violet pushed Misty gently back into her seat before tossing a bound stack of papers onto the table.

"This is the script we prepared for you. Just follow the lines written here. Even for someone with zero acting experience like you, it'll be easy."

Misty shook her head so vigorously it resembled a spinning rattle drum.

Seeing that she still refused, Daisy pleaded softly,

"Misty... please. I'm begging you. Help us this one time."

Misty hesitated.

Ever since they were children, Daisy had always been the gentlest sister and had treated her the best.

Now that Daisy was personally asking, Misty found it impossible to reject her outright.

Even Ash and Brock joined in.

"You should do it, Misty," Ash encouraged.

Brock nodded.

"The tickets have already been sold. At this point, canceling the performance isn't really an option."

With so many people persuading her, Misty's resolve began to crumble.

Seeing this, Violet and Lily gratefully turned toward Ash and Brock.

"You two are wonderful!"

"Misty is so lucky to have friends like you!"

By now, Misty was completely at a loss.

She instinctively glanced toward Ethan.

Seeing him quietly nod in agreement, she finally let out a helpless sigh.

"...Alright."

"I'll do it."

The moment she agreed, Lily secretly flashed Violet an OK sign, wearing a triumphant smile.

"I told you it'd work! Misty could never refuse a request from Big Sis Daisy!"

Ignoring her, Violet immediately urged,

"Alright, alright! Take a look at the script. If everything's fine, we'll start rehearsing right away!"

Rolling her eyes, Misty reluctantly accepted the script.

Although she had agreed, she still wasn't enthusiastic.

Flipping through the pages, she discovered it was nothing more than a cliché story about a prince defeating a villain to rescue a princess.

Could a story like that really restore the Cerulean Gym's reputation?

Suddenly, she remembered something and turned toward Ethan.

"Oh, right!"

"Ethan, I remember you once told me that you wrote a really popular story, didn't you? Why don't you help us rewrite this script?"

The story Misty referred to was naturally the manga adapted from the Maiden's Peak legend.

After Ethan sold the rights to the Maiden's Peak management, the manga became an instant success, selling extremely well and even gaining popularity in neighboring cities.

He had only mentioned it casually while chatting with Misty during their visit to the hot springs on Cinnabar Island.

He hadn't expected her to remember it.

"I don't know how to rewrite scripts," Ethan replied, shaking his head.

"And besides, although I wrote the original story for the Maiden's Peak manga, the biggest credit for its success belongs to the artist and the publisher."

"The Maiden's Peak manga!?"

"Seriously?"

Hearing this, Violet became instantly excited.

"My sisters and I read it just a few days ago! The writing was so delicate that we thought it had to be written by a female author. We never imagined it was your work! The story about the girl waiting faithfully for her lover was incredibly touching!"

Daisy clasped her hands together.

"Please... if that's the case, could you at least take a look at our script?"

Ethan hesitated.

"Writing stories and adapting scripts are completely different things. I've never done anything like this before. I really don't think I'm qualified."

"Oh, don't be so modest."

The three sisters didn't seem concerned at all.

"If your story could become such a successful manga, then the original story itself must have been excellent."

"Besides, no matter what, you're definitely better than we are."

"Just give it a try."

He hadn't expected to get dragged into this, but since Misty had already agreed, Ethan finally gave in after the sisters' repeated persuasion.

"Alright."

"If none of you mind, then I'll give it a shot."

"But let me make one thing clear first."

"Please don't expect too much from me."

(End of Chapter )
