That night, the device delivered by Dragonite suddenly received an update.

This time, however, it only informed Ethan that he must arrive at Vermilion Harbor by the morning of the day after tomorrow, where he would then receive the exact location of New Island.

Lying in bed inside the dormitory at the Cerise Laboratory, Ethan couldn't help feeling somewhat anxious about his upcoming encounter with Mewtwo.

He carefully tried to recall the events of Mewtwo Strikes Back, but the movie had been so long ago that he only vaguely remembered the story revolving around cloning. Most of the finer details had long since faded from his memory.

One thing, however, he knew for certain.

Once he arrived on the island, a fierce battle was inevitable.

The thought made him frown.

What exactly does Mewtwo want from me?

Is this invitation friendly... or hostile?

The more he thought about it, the more uneasy he became.

He reached into his backpack and took out the Master Ball, casually tossing it into the air and catching it over and over.

To be honest, back when he played the Pokémon games, after clearing the main story he had always loved catching incredibly powerful and stylish Pokémon like Mewtwo.

But now he was living in a world where Pokémon were real beings with emotions and personalities.

Especially as the number of his companions continued to grow, he had become much more selective about who he wanted on his team.

Unless it was a Pokémon he genuinely liked, he would never try to capture it casually.

Still...

If something truly dangerous happened after arriving on the island and he had no other choice...

The Master Ball would become his final trump card against Mewtwo.

After a night's rest, Ethan left the Cerise Laboratory early the next morning and arrived at Vermilion Harbor.

The busy harbor was already crowded with Trainers.

Like him, they had all received Mewtwo's mysterious invitation and were waiting for the next update, eager to attend the gathering of the world's strongest Trainers.

Without warning, however, the previously clear blue sky suddenly became covered by thick storm clouds.

Within moments, violent winds swept across the harbor.

Then came a torrential downpour.

Crash—!

Rain poured from the heavens as though buckets of water had been dumped over the entire city.

Lightning flashed.

Thunder roared.

The ocean churned violently as enormous waves slammed against the breakwater, some reaching heights of more than ten meters.

With no other choice, everyone rushed into the harbor's passenger terminal to shelter from the sudden storm.

"What a crazy storm!"

"Seriously! It was sunny just a minute ago! How did it suddenly become like this?"

The spacious terminal quickly filled with passengers and Trainers waiting to board ships.

Although everyone had managed to get inside in time, nearly all of them had been soaked by the rain.

Only Ethan remained completely dry.

Haunter had quietly created a protective barrier around him, shielding him from every drop of rain.

The salty scent of seawater filled every corner of the building.

Pokémon everywhere had become restless, forcing their Trainers to calm them down again and again.

Everyone discussed the bizarre weather.

Only Ethan knew the truth.

This storm had been created by Mewtwo's psychic power.

Its purpose...

...was to test every Trainer who had accepted the invitation.

After finding an empty seat, Ethan had barely sat down when he saw Ash, Misty, and Brock rushing through the entrance.

The three of them were completely drenched.

"Over here!"

Waving them over, Ethan pulled a pack of tissues from his backpack and handed them over.

After greeting him, Ash wiped himself dry while proudly saying to Misty and Brock,

"See? I told you Ethan would be here! I knew he must've received that invitation too!"

Misty rolled her eyes.

"Well, obviously. Ethan is way stronger than you are. If you got an invitation, there's no way he wouldn't."

"Alright, alright," Ethan interrupted with a laugh. "You two can stop arguing."

He then turned to Ash.

"So you're here... That means you've finally collected all eight Gym Badges?"

Ash nodded proudly.

"Yep! After we parted ways, I challenged the Viridian Gym. That old lady, Agatha, was unbelievably strong! I had to challenge the Gym twice before I finally earned the Earth Badge."

Ethan shrugged.

"Well, she is one of the Elite Four. Even the Pokémon she uses in Gym battles are naturally much stronger than those of ordinary Gym Leaders."

"What!?"

Ash, Misty, and Brock all stared in shock.

"That scary old lady is one of the Elite Four?"

"That's right," Ethan replied. "She's only serving as the temporary Gym Leader. Once the League appoints a permanent replacement, she'll return to her Elite Four duties."

"What? She never told us that," Brock said, shaking his head.

"Although she didn't reveal her identity," he continued, "something interesting happened."

"When we mentioned you during our conversation, and she learned that Ash knew you, her attitude changed completely."

"She even gave Ash... some special treatment."

"Special treatment?"

Ethan blinked.

"What kind of treatment?"

Misty covered her mouth, trying not to laugh.

"The kind where she made his life miserable."

"What was supposed to be a normal three-on-three Gym battle suddenly became much harder after Agatha heard Ash was a Trainer you personally recognized."

"So she deliberately sent out her main Gengar to test his strength!"

"..."

Ethan fell completely silent.

So that was the "special treatment."

"And then?" he finally asked.

Misty burst into laughter.

"What else? Ash lost all three battles!"

"He was so depressed he couldn't sleep that entire night."

"The next day, Agatha finally switched to different Pokémon, and only then did he manage to earn his Badge."

Ethan stared blankly.

He had never imagined that simply mentioning his name would actually make things harder for Ash.

"I'm sorry, Ash."

He sighed sympathetically.

"I never thought my reputation would end up causing you trouble."

"But don't be discouraged."

"Getting personally trained by an Elite Four member isn't exactly a bad experience."

Just as they were chatting happily together, the invitation device suddenly updated with New Island's coordinates.

At the same time, a commotion erupted in one corner of the terminal.

Turning toward the noise, they saw a crowd of Trainers surrounding Officer Jenny and a harbor administrator.

"What!?"

"The ferry service has been suspended?"

"So we can't sail at all?"

"How can this happen?"

"We finally received the invitation! We can't miss this opportunity!"

As the crowd grew louder, Officer Jenny quickly raised both hands, trying to calm everyone.

"Please remain calm!"

"The person beside me is the harbor's administrator."

"She'll now explain the situation to everyone."
