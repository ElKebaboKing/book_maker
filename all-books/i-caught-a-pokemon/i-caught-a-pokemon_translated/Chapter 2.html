After leaving the Pokémon Center, Ethan Vale did not make the long trip to Pallet Town to seek out Professor Oak. Instead, he headed directly to Cerise Laboratory in Vermilion City.

Cerise Laboratory was located on the outskirts of the city and occupied a vast area. Beyond the main gate was a large courtyard, while the primary building itself was an impressive three-story mansion complete with an attic. Rumor had it that the estate had originally belonged to an aristocrat, only to be purchased by Professor Cerise at a high price half a year ago and renovated into a research laboratory.

Passing through the gates and walking along the path, Ethan Vale eventually climbed the steps toward the entrance. Just as he was about to ring the doorbell, he suddenly heard light footsteps behind him.

Turning around, he saw a young girl standing not far away, curiously observing him.

She wore a white dress resembling a school uniform. Her appearance was delicate and beautiful, with long wine-red hair tied into a single ponytail hanging casually behind her head. Her large eyes were exceptionally clear, giving her an innocent and adorable aura.

Ethan Vale recognized her immediately.

She was Chloe Cerise, the childhood friend of Goh from the seventh Pokémon anime series, Pokémon Journeys, and also Professor Cerise's daughter.

Seeing Ethan Vale standing in front of the laboratory, Chloe tilted her head slightly.

After examining him for a moment, she softly asked, "Excuse me, do you need something?"

"Hello." Ethan Vale smiled politely and explained his purpose. "I have something I'd like Professor Cerise to help me with. Is he currently inside the laboratory?"

"You're looking for my dad?" Chloe hesitated briefly before nodding. "He's inside. Come in with me."

Opening the front door of Cerise Laboratory, Chloe led Ethan Vale through the entrance hall and into a room resembling a reception lounge.

Looking around, Ethan Vale only now realized just how astonishingly large the building truly was. It resembled a miniature castle, and from the layout, decorations, and furnishings, it was clearly designed in the style of a traditional English manor.

Professor Cerise certainly had excellent taste.

Pointing toward the sofa in the reception room, Chloe said, "Please wait here for a moment. My dad is still working, so I'll go let him know."

"Thank you."

Ethan Vale nodded. After Chloe left, however, he did not sit down. Instead, he slowly walked toward the window.

Through the glass, he could see a dense forest flourishing with spring greenery. Beyond it, farther in the distance, lay the bustling city of Vermilion City. If he calmed himself and listened carefully, he could even faintly hear the sound of ocean waves.

The environment really was elegant.

Even after arriving here and standing in this place personally, Ethan Vale still found the whole experience somewhat unreal.

He stood there absentmindedly for a while. Before long, Chloe returned, accompanied by a middle-aged man with the same hair color as hers.

The man appeared to be around thirty-seven or thirty-eight years old. He wore thick-framed glasses and a warm smile, dressed in a white research coat. From his appearance alone, one could immediately tell he was a scholarly researcher.

In Ethan Vale's memory, Professor Cerise was not only Professor Oak's junior colleague, but also one of the Pokémon Professors of the Kanto region. Even in his youth, he had already been recognized as a genius with an exceptionally bright future ahead of him.

Despite not being very old, he had also been entrusted by the Pokémon League with the authority to distribute starter Pokémon and Pokédexes to rookie trainers. In other words, he was one of the few true authorities in the entire Kanto region.

Just as Professor Cerise was about to speak, his attention was suddenly drawn to the Ralts standing beside Ethan Vale.

"A Ralts!? Now that's quite a rare sight!"

The moment he finished speaking, a short Pokémon came running in from outside. Upon spotting Chloe, it immediately rushed to her side and affectionately rubbed against her leg.

Ethan Vale lowered his gaze and saw a Pokémon resembling a small puppy. Its body was tan-colored, with a fluffy patch of yellow fur around its neck. It had bright sparkling eyes and a lightning bolt-shaped tail, making it look extremely energetic.

So it was a Yamper…

At that moment, Yamper also noticed Ralts.

Its large eyes filled with curiosity as it cautiously approached, but when Ralts' hand suddenly began glowing with psychic energy, Yamper became frightened and hurried back beside Professor Cerise.

Professor Cerise crouched down and gently patted its head to calm it before looking back at Ethan Vale.

"This is my family's Pokémon. It's always been a bit overly energetic. Did it startle you just now?"

"Not at all." Ethan Vale lightly shook his head. "If I remember correctly, Pokédex records mention that Yamper is an extremely energetic Pokémon, so it's naturally difficult for it to stay still."

"Oh?" Professor Cerise looked surprised and adjusted his glasses. "You know about it?"

"Well, I am a Pokémon trainer, so I know at least a little about many different Pokémon." Ethan Vale smiled. "Although I only know the basics. I'm aware that it's an Electric-type Pokémon from the Galar region. If I'm not mistaken, the fur around its neck is where it stores electricity, right?"

How was that "not knowing much"!? He clearly knew quite a lot!

"You're very knowledgeable, and your mannerisms are quite refined as well. It seems you're no ordinary trainer." Professor Cerise seemed impressed by Ethan Vale's knowledge and skipped unnecessary formalities. "Chloe mentioned you needed my help with something?"

"I apologize for interrupting your work, Professor." Ethan Vale took out his Pokédex. "My name is Ethan Vale, and I'm from the Kalos region. I just arrived in Kanto today, and I was hoping you could upgrade the data on my Pokédex so it can function as a universal Pokédex covering all regions around the world."

Taking the Pokédex, Professor Cerise examined it carefully.

"Hmm, it certainly does differ somewhat from the style used in Kanto, but that's no problem. It's a simple task. Come with me."

Ethan Vale had not expected things to proceed so smoothly, though it also showed just how approachable Professor Cerise truly was.

Following him, Ethan Vale left the reception room and entered the laboratory's work area. Besides Professor Cerise, there were also two assistants helping with the research work: one man and one woman.

The female assistant was a beautiful woman with long dark-blue wavy hair named Ren. The male assistant wore glasses and had messy hair. Though he appeared rather ordinary at first glance, he was clearly quite talented. His name was Parker.

Both assistants were very easygoing people, and under Professor Cerise's introduction, they greeted Ethan Vale warmly.

"Hello, I'm Professor Cerise's assistant, Parker!"

"And I'm Ren!"

Ethan Vale shook hands with both of them and greeted them politely.

"Nice to meet you both. I'm Ethan Vale, a Pokémon trainer. Sorry for troubling all of you today."

Ren waved her hand with a gentle smile.

"It's no problem at all. The laboratory hasn't officially opened yet, so we aren't actually very busy. Things are still pretty relaxed around here."

"Exactly." Parker nodded enthusiastically in agreement. "There are only a few of us working here right now, so it gets lonely sometimes. Your visit today actually brings a bit of life to the laboratory!"

While they chatted, Professor Cerise placed the Pokédex onto a machine and operated it for several minutes before finally handing it back to Ethan Vale.

"There, the upgrade is complete. So, are you planning to begin challenging the Indigo League next?"

"That's right." Ethan Vale nodded. "But at the moment, Ralts is my only Pokémon. If I want to challenge Gyms, I'll need to catch a few more Pokémon to strengthen my team."

The moment the topic shifted to Pokémon, Parker immediately became excited.

"There are tons of Pokémon in the Kanto region! So, do you already have any targets in mind?"

"I do have a few in mind already." Without hiding anything, Ethan Vale answered honestly. "I definitely want to catch the three Kanto starters—Charmander, Squirtle, and Bulbasaur. As for the others… I suppose that'll depend on fate."

(End of Chapter)
