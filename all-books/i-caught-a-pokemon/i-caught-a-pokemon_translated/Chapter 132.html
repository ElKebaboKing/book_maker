That evening, Ethan Vale packed some food and invited Ash, Misty, and Brock to his lodge for a small dinner gathering.

Perhaps because the tournament was only a day away, Ash was feeling nervous. Or perhaps because it was only Chloe Cerise's second time meeting everyone else, she was still a little reserved. Either way, the gathering was quieter than expected. Fortunately, Haunter and Yamper kept the atmosphere lively, preventing any awkward silences.

During their conversation, Ethan could clearly sense Ash's anxiety about the upcoming competition.

Ash barely touched his food. Watching Ethan laugh and chat so naturally, he could only feel envious.

I really wish I could be as calm as he is...

Late that night, Ash returned to his own lodge.

However, after lying in bed and tossing and turning for what felt like forever, he simply couldn't fall asleep.

Looking through the window at the star-filled sky, he quietly put on his jacket, careful not to wake Pikachu, and slipped outside alone.

Compared to the bustling daytime atmosphere, the Athletes' Village was now completely silent. Not a single person could be seen on the streets, though lights still glowed inside many of the lodges, accompanied by faint sounds of laughter.

"Can't sleep either?"

Ash looked up at the familiar voice.

On the roof sat Ethan and his Gardevoir, quietly admiring the moonlight.

The silver moonlight bathed them both, giving them an almost mysterious appearance.

Ash nodded.

"Yeah... I just can't fall asleep. But you're still awake too."

As he spoke, he noticed Ethan was holding his phone. The screen was still lit, displaying a chat window, though Ash had no idea who he had been talking to.

Ethan locked his phone, stood up, and gently floated down to the ground with Gardevoir's Psychic.

"Of course," Ethan replied. "This is my first League tournament too. How could I possibly sleep on such an important night? I'm just not showing it as obviously as you are."

Ash sighed.

"Yeah... My head is completely filled with Pokémon battles right now. I honestly wish I could fight someone immediately."

You're not nervous... you're just overflowing with energy and don't know where to put it, Ethan thought.

After a brief silence, Ethan suddenly asked,

"Want to go see tomorrow's battlefield?"

Ash blinked.

"You mean... Indigo Stadium?"

"Right now?" he asked in surprise. "Can we even get in?"

Ethan shrugged.

"Who knows? We've got nothing better to do. Maybe seeing the stadium beforehand will help calm your nerves."

"That makes sense."

Ash was never one to hesitate.

Once they had made up their minds, the two of them walked toward Indigo Stadium.

The stadium wasn't far from the Athletes' Village.

About fifteen minutes later, they arrived.

As a massive open-air arena, the stadium wasn't completely closed off, allowing them to enter freely through one of the entrances.

Compared to the grand stadiums in some other regions, Indigo Stadium was slightly less impressive due to the era in which it had been built.

Even so, its capacity of fifty thousand spectators and its enormous central display screen made it every bit as exciting as any other major venue.

Standing side by side in the grandstands, Ethan and Ash looked down at the battlefield below.

"So..." Ash murmured, "this is where we'll be battling tomorrow."

Ethan nodded.

"That's right."

"Are you ready?"

"Starting tomorrow, every seat here will be filled. Everything we've worked for—our sweat, our effort—will be displayed before all those people."

Ash clenched his fists.

His nervousness gradually faded, replaced by burning excitement.

"I get it!"

His eyes shone with determination.

"I'm going to battle my own way!"

Remembering Ash's future tournament record, Ethan let out a quiet sigh.

"Do your best, kid."

"If you win, you'll earn glory and recognition."

"If you lose..."

"We'll quietly walk away."

"Spectators are forgetful. Very few people remember those who lose."

"Only the strongest receive respect and admiration."

As Ethan's eyes wandered across the stadium, he unexpectedly noticed President Charles Goodshow.

The short elderly man was standing alone on a distant platform, silently watching Ethan and Ash.

Goodshow had originally intended to leave quietly, but after realizing he had been noticed, he smiled and walked over.

Chuckling, he said,

"What a surprise."

"At a time like this, nearly every contestant is resting in the Athletes' Village."

"Yet you two came here to scout tomorrow's battlefield."

"Good evening, President Goodshow," Ethan greeted politely before smiling.

"We wanted to rest too and prepare ourselves for tomorrow."

"But honestly... we were too nervous."

"So we thought we'd come here and get used to the atmosphere in advance."

Goodshow nodded approvingly.

"That's a good attitude."

"Remember this."

"Sometimes, your attitude determines everything."

"It also determines just how far you'll go on this path."

At 8:00 a.m. the following morning, an endless series of firecrackers echoed throughout the city.

A gigantic Poké Ball suddenly opened high above the stadium, releasing dozens of Pidgey that circled gracefully through the sky over Indigo Stadium.

The spectator stands were already packed with fans who had traveled from all over the region.

Cheers erupted continuously, loud enough to be heard from several kilometers away.

Hundreds of television cameras broadcast the spectacular scene live to viewers across the world.

Once the excitement settled slightly, the male announcer's voice echoed through the stadium speakers.

"At last..."

"The long-awaited opening ceremony of the Pokémon League has officially begun!"

"Outstanding Pokémon Trainers and their partners will battle here over the coming days."

"For those who emerge victorious, they'll earn not only honor and glory, but also overcome one of the greatest trials on the road toward becoming a Pokémon Master!"

Several beautiful young women carrying signboards entered the stadium first.

Behind them marched the competitors in orderly groups.

Including Ethan and Ash, every contestant walked proudly onto the field.

The moment the audience saw them, thunderous cheers erupted throughout the stadium.

The announcer continued enthusiastically,

"And now, our competitors have officially entered the arena!"

"Every single one of them has earned at least eight Gym Badges, proving themselves to be exceptional Trainers!"

"Each competitor is a formidable opponent!"

"But in the end..."

"Who will become this year's champion?"

As the contestants reached the center of the field and lined up facing the podium, the final female torchbearer ran into the stadium.

She climbed the long staircase and ignited the great ceremonial flame atop the Sacred Flame cauldron.

Then President Charles Goodshow stepped onto the stage.

After delivering a brief yet humorous opening speech, he officially declared the Indigo League Conference open.
