The Master Ball?

So that really was their objective!

At that moment, Sabrina shifted awkwardly a few times.

She had been maintaining the same posture inside the cramped locker for too long, and her body had inevitably become stiff.

However, when she moved slightly to ease the discomfort, her body pressed even closer against Ethan's.

Feeling several soft points of contact, Ethan's heartbeat instantly quickened, and even Sabrina's breathing became noticeably heavier.

Fortunately, the Team Rocket members only rested briefly before leaving the recreation room.

Once they could no longer hear any footsteps outside, the two hurriedly climbed out of the locker.

After being trapped inside for so long, Ethan took a deep breath of fresh air.

Perhaps it was only his imagination, but being pressed so closely against Sabrina seemed to have left a faint trace of her fragrance lingering on him.

To avoid awkwardness, Ethan deliberately avoided looking at Sabrina's expression. Instead, he walked to the door and carefully peeked outside.

A pitch-black round face was staring directly back at him.

Ethan nearly jumped in fright before recognizing it as Gastly.

"Why are you standing out there!?"

"Well, you two were taking way too long," Gastly floated into the room while suspiciously glancing back and forth between Ethan and Sabrina. "Strange… what exactly were you doing in there? Why is her face all red?"

"Hey, don't start talking nonsense!" Ethan hurriedly tried to cover Gastly's mouth, only for his hand to pass straight through its body. "We were just hiding from Team Rocket's search!"

"Relax, Team Rocket already left." Gastly snickered mischievously, clearly treating Ethan's explanation as a cover-up. "Since everything's fine now, let's hurry up and move."

Leaving the recreation room, the two entered the stairwell and continued upward.

Unfortunately, there were simply too many Team Rocket members throughout the building.

Not long afterward, they ran directly into several grunts coming down the stairs.

"Who are you!?"

Seeing Ethan and Sabrina dressed nothing like Silph Co. researchers, one of the grunts shouted loudly.

However, before any of them could react, they were instantly enveloped by Psychic energy and transformed by Sabrina into tiny dolls.

This was the same technique she had once used against Brock and Misty during Ash's Gym challenge. Those affected had absolutely no ability to resist and could only be manipulated helplessly.

Although they had dealt with those few grunts, the earlier shout still alerted nearby Team Rocket members.

Many grunts who heard the commotion immediately rushed toward the area.

"So much for things going smoothly like we planned!"

The two sprinted upward through the stairwell while more and more Team Rocket members pursued them from below.

"There are intruders!"

"What happened!? Didn't the control room notice them!?"

"Damn it, how did they get inside!? Notify the executives immediately!"

After running up several more floors, another group of Team Rocket members suddenly appeared ahead and blocked their path completely.

Now they were trapped.

Enemies behind them.

Enemies in front of them.

The route had been completely sealed off.

Seeing this, Ethan decisively pulled out a Poké Ball and released Kirlia.

"Gastly, use Hypnosis! Kirlia, use Psychic!"

The two Pokémon attacked simultaneously.

Before the Team Rocket members even had time to release their own Pokémon, they collapsed one after another like bowling pins hitting the floor.

Taking advantage of the opening, Ethan and Sabrina immediately continued running upward.

Silph Co. – Executive Office, Top Floor

As Team Rocket's supreme boss, Giovanni stood inside the office wearing a perfectly pressed black suit.

His neatly combed hair and imposing expression radiated overwhelming pressure as he looked down coldly at the elderly man before him.

The old man sat calmly on the sofa.

Although already advanced in years, he remained full of spirit. Even in such a dangerous situation, there was not the slightest panic on his face.

"Old man," Giovanni said slowly in a deep, oppressive voice, "for the sake of your employees' lives, you'd better tell me where you've hidden the Master Ball."

"You should give up already." The elderly man remained completely composed. "A Poké Ball that dangerous could never be handed over to people like you. Though I truly didn't expect the infamous leader of Team Rocket to personally lead the takeover of my company today. That certainly is surprising."

Giovanni remained entirely unmoved by the old man's sarcasm.

In truth, after thoroughly searching the building without finding the Master Ball, this old man had become his final hope.

And because of the man's age, Giovanni was unwilling to use physical force against him.

At that moment, a Team Rocket grunt stepped beside Giovanni and whispered several words into his ear.

"Intruders?" Giovanni shot the grunt a cold glance filled with authority. "Why are you only discovering this now?"

Cold sweat instantly formed on the grunt's forehead.

"Th-that's because they first broke into the control room, knocked out our men, and destroyed the equipment, so…"

Giovanni had not expected such a carefully crafted plan to encounter unexpected complications.

"Who are these intruders?"

The grunt hurriedly answered,

"There are two intruders total. One has already been identified as Sabrina, the Saffron Gym Leader! The other is a young man around her age, but his identity is still unknown!"

"Sabrina…"

A flash of killing intent appeared briefly in Giovanni's eyes.

Ordinary intruders were one thing.

But as fellow official Gym Leaders under the Pokémon League, Giovanni had naturally met Sabrina several times before.

His identity as the Viridian Gym Leader was an excellent cover.

If Sabrina discovered who he truly was, it would create serious trouble for him.

Thinking of this, Giovanni turned toward a Team Rocket executive who had been silently leaning against the wall.

"Lance. I'll leave things here to you. Don't let them leave alive."

The executive named Lance wore the standard black Team Rocket cap and had short teal-colored hair.

Upon receiving the order, he bowed elegantly and replied in a frivolous tone,

"Please rest assured, sir. Everything will be over shortly."

By the time Ethan and Sabrina arrived at the top floor, breathing heavily from exhaustion, the loud roar of helicopter blades suddenly echoed outside.

A helicopter rapidly lifted away from Silph Co. and disappeared into the distant sky.

The two entered the hallway and locked the stairwell door behind them.

Just as they were about to relax slightly, applause suddenly echoed ahead of them.

Clapping his hands slowly, Lance walked toward the pair with a smile.

"To make it all the way here through Team Rocket's defenses… I have to admit, you two are quite impressive."

Around Lance, numerous Team Rocket members closed in from every direction.

At the same time, behind Ethan and Sabrina, the Team Rocket members chasing them from below finally smashed through the stairwell door and flooded into the corridor.
