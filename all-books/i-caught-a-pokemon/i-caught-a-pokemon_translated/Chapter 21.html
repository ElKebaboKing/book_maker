"Bulbasaur, use Sleep Powder!"

After taking several punches, it seized an opening and shook the bulb on its back. In an instant, countless glowing spores sprayed out. The powder drifted through the air on its own, covering nearly half the battlefield within seconds.

Lt. Surge's expression changed immediately.

"Electabuzz, get out of there!"

But Electabuzz was simply too close. Caught completely off guard, it was covered head to toe in Sleep Powder. A powerful wave of drowsiness overwhelmed it almost instantly. Unable to resist, its body staggered before collapsing onto the ground, fast asleep.

"Electabuzz! Wake up right now!!"

Despite Lt. Surge shouting repeatedly from the sidelines, Electabuzz slept far too deeply. No matter how urgently he yelled, his voice could not reach its consciousness.

"It's time to end this," Ethan Vale said as he gave Bulbasaur the final command. "Use Razor Leaf!"

Bulbasaur walked up in front of the sleeping Electabuzz and looked down at it from above. Razor Leaf formed overhead before firing with perfect accuracy, striking Electabuzz continuously.

This time, the attack dealt full damage!

Before Electabuzz could even wake up from the attack, it passed directly from sleep into unconsciousness.

A flash of blue light appeared as Lt. Surge withdrew Electabuzz into its Poké Ball.

At that very moment, however, a brilliant light suddenly enveloped Bulbasaur.

Several seconds later, a brand-new form appeared on the battlefield.

Bulbasaur had evolved into Ivysaur!

For Ethan Vale, this was truly an unexpected delight.

Compared to Bulbasaur, Ivysaur's appearance had not changed drastically. Its body color had become slightly darker, while four large green leaves resembling banana leaves had sprouted around the bulb on its back.

Pokédex: "Ivysaur, the Seed Pokémon. Grass- and Poison-type. Evolved form of Bulbasaur. The more sunlight it absorbs, the more energetic it becomes. Once the bud on its back absorbs enough nutrients, it will bloom into a giant flower."

"Congratulations," Lt. Surge slowly walked toward Ethan Vale. Although he had lost, there was no frustration on his face. "Not only did you defeat me, but your Pokémon evolved as well. You've gone from being a Baby to becoming quite the impressive Boy!"

Chloe also walked down from the spectator stands and came to Ethan Vale's side. Judging from her expression, she seemed even happier than Ethan Vale himself. However, with others around, she said nothing. Instead, she crouched down and gently stroked Kirlia and Ivysaur to express her joy.

Lt. Surge took a Badge from his pocket and handed it to Ethan Vale.

"The Thunder Badge. This is the reward you've earned. Take it."

The Badge resembled an orange sun radiating yellow light.

Ethan Vale accepted the Badge and turned to leave with Chloe. However, just as they reached the entrance, Lt. Surge suddenly called out to him again.

"Wait a second. Take this too."

He pulled a sparkling little stone from his pocket and tossed it toward Ethan Vale.

The stone traced a perfect arc through the air, but just before reaching Ethan Vale, it suddenly stopped midair.

Kirlia had caught it using Confusion!

As a Pokémon fiercely loyal to its Trainer, there was no way it would allow some strange object to approach Ethan Vale carelessly.

Ethan Vale carefully examined the stone.

Its appearance resembled a brilliant green gemstone, and through its crystal-clear surface, a distinct lightning-bolt symbol could be seen inside.

A Thunder Stone!?

Ethan Vale looked at Lt. Surge in confusion, unsure of his intentions.

"Go ahead and take it," Lt. Surge laughed. "As a Trainer I recognize, consider this Thunder Stone my personal gift to you."

Ethan Vale froze for a moment before breaking into a wide smile.

"Thanks! Then I won't hold back!"

As he extended his hand, the Thunder Stone gently floated down into his palm.

By now, the sky outside had already grown completely dark.

After leaving Vermilion Gym, Ethan Vale and Chloe walked back toward the Cerise Laboratory along the brightly lit streets.

Inside the lab, Professor Cerise was still working in front of a computer. When he heard Ethan Vale had successfully challenged Vermilion Gym, he was delighted. And when he learned Chloe had gone to the Gym as well, his mouth dropped open in surprise.

"I never imagined there'd come a day when Chloe would enjoy Pokémon battles too."

"Only after seeing one in person did I realize Pokémon battles are actually really interesting."

As Chloe spoke, she subconsciously glanced at Ethan Vale. If not for his invitation, she probably would never have voluntarily gone to watch a Pokémon battle.

Seeing how late it had become outside, Ethan Vale yawned and was about to excuse himself and return to his dorm room when Professor Cerise suddenly stopped him.

"Oh right, Ethan Vale. If it's convenient, I'd like you to check out the nearby power plant tomorrow."

"The power plant?" Ethan Vale blinked in confusion. "What happened there?"

Professor Cerise explained:

"The electricity for our laboratory comes from an abandoned power plant on the outskirts of the city. Although it's been vacant for many years, its electrical systems have continued operating normally. But recently, for some reason, the voltage has become extremely unstable, causing constant power outages and interrupting our research. I suspect some Electric-type Pokémon may have wandered inside."

"An abandoned... power plant..." Ethan Vale nodded thoughtfully. "No problem. Leave it to me."

Late that night, inside the Cerise Laboratory dormitory.

After taking a quick hot shower, Ethan Vale lay shirtless on the soft bed.

The bright lights reflected off the blue-green minimalist wallpaper, giving the room a warm and cozy atmosphere. Even more impressive was how fully furnished it was. The room contained everything from wardrobes, bookshelves, and desks to a private bathroom. There was even a comfortable sofa nearby.

His Pokémon were all playing around the room.

Picking up the Thunder Stone Lt. Surge had given him earlier that day, Ethan Vale thought carefully for a moment before beckoning toward Eevee.

"Eevee, have you ever thought about what you want to evolve into?"

Eevee tilted its head innocently as it looked at him.

Playing with the Thunder Stone in his hand, Ethan Vale continued:

"Your personality is a little impatient, but your speed is incredible. I think you'd be perfect as a Jolteon, where you could truly make use of your strengths. But if there's another evolution you want instead, I'll respect your choice."

Eevee let out a soft cry and stared at the Thunder Stone thoughtfully.

After pausing for a moment, it slowly approached and gently pressed its forehead against the stone.

The next second, accompanied by the buzzing hum of electricity, a dazzling burst of light suddenly illuminated the entire dorm room!

(End of Chapter)
