After reappearing, Kadabra attacked again. Another Psychic struck Kirlia, lifting it into the air. However, once Kirlia used Psychic to resist as well, it only struggled briefly before breaking free from the restraint.

"Kirlia, use Magical Leaf!"

Magical Leaf shot toward Kadabra once more. But just like before, Kadabra used Teleport again to evade the attack.

This time, however, Ethan had already prepared for it.

With a thought in his mind, though he never spoke aloud, Kirlia instantly understood his intentions. The moment Kadabra reappeared, Kirlia immediately unleashed Psychic against it.

Caught completely off guard, Kadabra's body trembled violently. Although it quickly used Psychic to break free, it still suffered slight damage.

At this point, the two Pokémon were essentially evenly matched.

Sabrina said calmly,

"Even though you personally don't possess psychic powers, you can still communicate mentally with your Pokémon. That surprises me. It seems you've raised your Pokémon exceptionally well."

"You and your Kadabra are the truly impressive ones," Ethan returned the compliment. "When it comes to psychic power alone, Kirlia is clearly no match for it."

The corners of Sabrina's lips lifted ever so slightly, though her expression itself did not visibly change.

Even someone like her still felt pleased when praised directly by another trainer.

Even so, she still showed absolutely no mercy.

Kadabra's eyes suddenly glowed red as rings of strange waves shot outward, enveloping Kirlia.

It was Psybeam—a Psychic-type move capable of dealing damage while also potentially confusing the opponent!

"Kirlia, use Teleport!"

Kirlia's body instantly vanished, avoiding the Psybeam entirely.

Ethan's thoughts raced rapidly.

Since both Pokémon shared the same typing, relying solely on Psychic-type attacks would never determine the victor.

Then suddenly, he came up with an idea.

"Kirlia, use Psychic!"

"You're still planning to use Psychic?" Sabrina said dismissively. "Did you already forget what you said earlier? Kirlia's psychic power is inferior to my Kadabra's!"

As she spoke, she transmitted commands to Kadabra through telepathy.

Kadabra used Psychic as well, intending to overpower Kirlia head-on again.

However, just as Kadabra's Psychic launched—

It realized the attack coming toward it was not Psychic at all, but Magical Leaf instead!

"What!?"

Not only Kadabra, but even Sabrina herself looked momentarily stunned.

"Ah…" Ethan also pretended to look surprised. "Kirlia, why aren't you following my commands?"

The invisible Psychic force collided with the continuous stream of Magical Leaves.

In the end, Magical Leaf overwhelmed the Psychic attack and slammed directly into Kadabra.

As a Grass-type move, it no longer suffered from Psychic-type resistance and finally dealt proper damage instead of merely half damage.

Kadabra cried out in pain and dropped to one knee.

Sabrina stared at Ethan in surprise.

"You actually used false commands to mislead your opponent?"

"No, no!" Ethan immediately shook his head in denial.

However, seeing Sabrina slightly narrow her eyes, it was obvious she did not believe him at all.

Ethan coughed awkwardly before explaining again.

"Alright, fine. Actually, this is my special trainer technique. I call it saying one thing while meaning another… or maybe contradiction tactics works too. But if possible, I'd prefer you call it—Tactical Deception!"

Tactical deception?

Sabrina sneered inwardly and ignored him.

The next second, golden light suddenly emerged from Kadabra's body as the scratches left by Magical Leaf rapidly healed at visible speed.

Not good—it's Recover!

"Kirlia, use Psychic!" Ethan shouted immediately.

Before he even finished speaking, Kirlia had already raised both hands and unleashed Psychic at Kadabra.

The attack blasted Kadabra backward onto the ground. Although it did not cause major damage, it interrupted Recover and knocked Kadabra's spoon out of its hand.

"Now!" Ethan's eyes lit up as he realized the opportunity could not be missed. "Use Magical Leaf!"

The spinning Magical Leaves formed a miniature green tornado around Kadabra.

Originally, Kadabra could have escaped instantly with Teleport.

However, it refused to abandon its spoon.

As a result, it endured the full force of Magical Leaf while desperately searching through the swirling leaves to retrieve it.

When the attack finally faded, Kadabra was already panting heavily and covered in injuries.

"Perfect!" Ethan shouted. "Finish it—use Disarming Voice!"

Kirlia let out an enchanting cry.

Immediately afterward, waves of pink sound rippled outward and passed directly through Kadabra's body.

Disarming Voice had replaced Double Team as Kirlia's Fairy-type move. Once it hit, it would inevitably inflict mental damage!

After taking the attack, Kadabra's body swayed back and forth before finally collapsing onto the ground, completely unable to continue battling.

Seeing this, Sabrina's father let out a soft sigh, raised his right hand, and loudly announced:

"Kadabra is unable to battle. Kirlia wins!"

Sabrina recalled Kadabra into its Poké Ball and slowly walked toward Ethan.

Although she had lost the match, her expression remained unchanged. She still looked as cold and composed as ever, showing no obvious emotional reaction.

"You've won. This is the reward you deserve."

As she spoke, she waved her hand.

A Badge floated over from the side and slowly drifted into Ethan's hand.

Naturally, it was the Marsh Badge.

The Marsh Badge resembled a round golden button. True to its name, it gleamed brilliantly like real gold.

After placing it into his Badge case, Ethan now possessed four Gym Badges.

That meant he had defeated four Gym Leaders and was now halfway toward the eight Badges required to enter the Indigo League.

Waving goodbye to Sabrina, Ethan left the Saffron Gym alongside Kirlia, leaving behind only a cool and composed silhouette.

After leaving the Gym, he headed directly toward a nearby Pokémon supply store.

Ever since capturing Zapdos, Ethan had started feeling that ordinary Poké Balls were no longer reliable enough for capturing stronger Pokémon.

So he purchased several Great Balls just in case.

Afterward, he also bought a large number of Pokémon pouches and packed them into his backpack.

Once everything was prepared, he was just about to head toward the Pokémon Center to restore his Pokémon's stamina—

When a sudden flash of fire caught his attention.

He looked up toward a nearby skyscraper.

Through one of the windows around the upper floors, bursts of smoke and sparks suddenly exploded outward, as though some kind of Pokémon battle was taking place inside.

Pokémon battles can happen inside office buildings too?

Ethan shook his head and was about to continue walking when he suddenly froze.

Turning around, he stared at the name of the towering building.

—Silph Co.

(End of Chapter)
