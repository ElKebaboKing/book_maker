On the ocean surface, Ash and Brock, who had surfaced before Ethan, had already found several floating planks from the wreckage. They pieced them together to form a simple floating platform.

The moment they saw Ethan and the others emerge from the water, they quickly reached out and pulled them aboard.

"We're finally saved!"

James lay flat on the wooden planks, completely soaked, staring up at the blue sky and white clouds as though he had just walked along the edge of the River Styx itself.

Ethan also removed his diving goggles, untied the ropes, released Meowth, and recalled Magikarp back into its Poké Ball.

Under the warm sunlight, the sea around them was completely calm. Debris from the S.S. Anne floated everywhere, yet aside from their group, there was not another person in sight.

Using his narrow eyes to scan the surroundings, Brock sighed disappointedly.

"Looks like all the people who escaped in the lifeboats have already left this area."

"So what do we do now?" Ash asked. But as soon as he spoke, he noticed Misty kneeling with her back turned toward everyone, her cheeks faintly flushed red.

"That's strange, Misty. What's wrong with you?"

Misty quickly stood up and shook her head.

"It's nothing. I just swallowed some seawater earlier, so I'm feeling a little uncomfortable."

She tried forcing herself to calm down, but her gaze kept drifting back and forth between the respirator in Ethan's hand and his lips. As a result, her face only became even redder.

Walking over to Ethan, she dropped her usual energetic and prideful attitude and spoke softly.

"Thank you for earlier."

Naturally, Ethan understood what she meant.

But the situation had been so urgent at the time that he had not had the luxury of thinking about anything else. Saving her life had obviously come first.

"You don't need to dwell on it," Ethan coughed awkwardly, though even he felt like he was trying too hard to sound casual. Deciding not to continue the topic, he turned toward Ash instead. "Ash, send out your Pidgeotto and have it scout the surrounding area. See if there are any passing ships or nearby islands."

Ash released Pidgeotto and gave it the command. The bird Pokémon circled above the group twice as though locking onto a direction before flapping its wings and flying into the distance.

Not long afterward, Pidgeotto returned. It seemed to have found something and landed on Ash's shoulder, gesturing repeatedly with its wings.

As the only Pokémon capable of speaking human language, Meowth naturally became the translator.

"It says there's a harbor town not too far ahead!"

The moment they heard the word "harbor," everyone's spirits lifted. Directing their Water-type Pokémon, the group began swimming toward the town...

Maiden's Peak—a harbor town located between Vermilion City and Saffron City.

Although the town itself was relatively small, it was extremely prosperous. Not only did it have a large population, but thanks to its naturally excellent harbor and strategic location along major trade routes, many ships passed through every day, making the place lively and bustling.

The moment Ethan and the others stepped onto the soft sandy beach after reaching the harbor, the Team Rocket trio instantly regained their usual energy.

Somehow, they had already obtained two roses from who-knows-where and stood back-to-back dramatically.

Gently smelling the fragrance of the roses, Jessie snorted proudly.

"Now that we've made it here, it's time for us to say goodbye!"

"That's right!" James declared stylishly with the other rose clenched between his teeth. "Although we appreciate you saving us, next time we meet, we definitely won't go easy on you!"

"That's right, meow~"

With a burst of exaggerated laughter, the three of them turned and ran toward a giant rock by the shore before disappearing from sight.

"I seriously can't deal with those people..." Misty sighed. "But honestly, I don't even have the energy to care right now. Being soaked like this feels awful. I just want to take a nice hot bath and then sleep peacefully."

In truth, it was not just Misty. Even Ethan was completely exhausted after everything they had gone through.

"Then let's hurry to the Pokémon Center and get some rest."

Adjusting his backpack, Ethan was about to move forward when suddenly a strange feeling rose within him.

Turning toward a small dock in the distance, he saw a young woman quietly standing there.

She wore a white dress and had long pinkish-purple hair. As the breeze brushed past her, a strand of hair drifted aside, revealing a delicate and beautiful profile.

Yet for some reason, a faint sadness lingered around her.

Beside Ethan, Brock seemed to have noticed her as well. His eyes instantly transformed into hearts as he let out an excited cry.

"What a beautiful woman!"

Hearing him, Ash and Misty both turned to look into the distance, but saw absolutely nothing.

"What are you two looking at?" Misty asked curiously. "There's nothing there at all."

Ethan blinked, and in that brief instant, the woman had completely vanished.

Continuing onward, the group eventually arrived at the Pokémon Center. After checking in, Ethan also learned from Nurse Joy exactly where they had ended up.

Maiden's Peak?

The beautiful woman's spirit... and Gastly...

Recalling the events from the anime, Ethan could not help smiling. Somehow, they had unknowingly arrived here.

"Oh, by the way," Nurse Joy said cheerfully, "you all arrived at the perfect time! The town is currently holding its summer festival. There'll be a huge parade, a bonfire party, and lots of delicious food stalls. If you can, you definitely shouldn't miss it~"

Naturally, Misty, as a girl, and Ash, with his youthful enthusiasm, were both excited by the idea. Unfortunately, after everything they had endured, everyone's stamina had already reached its limit.

After agreeing on a time to regroup later, they each returned to their rooms to rest.

As a public facility that provided free lodging and meals, the accommodations at Pokémon Centers varied depending on the region.

For example, because Maiden's Peak was not a large town, its Pokémon Center was also fairly small. The boys' dormitory was simply a clean little room furnished with two sets of bunk beds.

But to Ethan, the pristine white bedsheets seemed to possess an irresistible magical pull, making him want nothing more than to collapse onto them immediately.

Even so, he forced himself to stay awake long enough to take a quick shower and toss his clothes into the dryer before finally collapsing onto the bed and falling instantly asleep.

The sun gradually drifted westward. Ethan slept all the way until dusk before finally sitting up groggily.

Seeing that Ash and Brock were still fast asleep and snoring loudly, he shook them awake before knocking on Misty's door. After regrouping, the four of them left the Pokémon Center together.

By now, the entire town had become immersed in the joyful atmosphere of the festival.

Countless people dressed in beautiful festive clothing followed behind elaborate parade floats as they marched through the streets. Amid the laughter and celebration, whether they were townsfolk or travelers like Ethan and the others, everyone's mood naturally became much happier as well.
