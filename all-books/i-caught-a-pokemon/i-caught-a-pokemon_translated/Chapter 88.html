Ash was clearly brimming with confidence and even turned back to give everyone a thumbs-up.

Seeing him about to rush forward so recklessly, Ethan couldn't help but remind him,

"Ash, Mr. Blaine specializes in Fire-type Pokémon and also has the home-field advantage. Don't underestimate him."

"I know!"

On the other side, Blaine had already released his first Pokémon.

It was a pale yellow, fox-like Pokémon—Ninetales!

Just as its name suggested, Ninetales swayed its nine smooth, flowing tails gracefully behind it. Its creamy golden fur, crimson eyes, and elegant figure gave it an exceptionally noble and refined appearance.

Facing Ninetales, Ash sent out Squirtle.

However, once the battle began, the result shocked everyone.

After only a few exchanges, during a clash between Fire Spin and Water Gun, Squirtle was struck directly by the swirling flames, suffering repeated burn damage before collapsing to the ground in disbelief, completely unable to battle.

Not only was Ash stunned, but even Ethan frowned deeply at the sight.

To defeat Squirtle so easily despite the type disadvantage… that was truly impressive.

"Darn it!"

Though unwilling to accept it, Ash had no choice but to recall Squirtle and send out his second Pokémon.

As the Poké Ball opened, the Pokémon that appeared before everyone was a winged, long-necked Pokémon with blazing flames burning at the tip of its tail—Charizard!

Seeing it, Ethan instinctively raised an eyebrow.

So in such a short amount of time, Ash's Charmander had already evolved into Charizard? That was some incredibly rapid progress.

At that moment, Blaine also recalled Ninetales and released his second Pokémon.

"Then my second battler will be this guy! Go, Rhydon!"

In the next second, a heavily armored Pokémon resembling a dinosaur, with a sharp drill horn on its forehead, appeared on the battlefield.

But unexpectedly, when Charizard saw Rhydon, it showed absolutely no fighting spirit at all.

It yawned lazily, casually flew out of the battlefield, and sprawled atop a giant boulder nearby!

"Charizard! What are you doing!?" Ash cried out in shock. "Please, I'm begging you—get down here and battle!"

But no matter how Ash shouted, Charizard remained completely unmoved.

"If you can't command your Pokémon, then clearly it doesn't recognize you as its trainer," Blaine said calmly. "And since Charizard has left the battlefield, I'll count this match as a forfeit."

"H-how could this happen…"

Before the battle started, Ash had never imagined things would turn out like this.

Left with no choice, he could only place all his hopes on Pikachu.

"Go, Pikachu!"

However, sending an Electric-type Pikachu against Rhydon, a Ground- and Rock-type Pokémon, was obviously a terrible matchup.

Sure enough, once the battle began, although Pikachu initially managed to dodge Rhydon's attacks with agile movements, its Electric-type attacks were completely ineffective.

Yet just when everyone thought defeat was inevitable, what happened next once again demonstrated how extraordinary the duo of Ash and Pikachu truly was.

Despite being at a disadvantage, Pikachu refused to give up. Encouraged by Ash's constant cheering, it ultimately defeated Rhydon using Quick Attack and Agility!

"Outstanding!" Blaine applauded while recalling Rhydon. "Your Pikachu is really something. You've truly impressed me!"

Finally letting out a sigh of relief, Ash regained his spirits and shouted,

"Then what Pokémon are you going to use next!?"

"Heh heh." Blaine smiled mysteriously. "The hottest flames in the world… magma within magma… what is it that moves inside?"

"Ahhh!" Ash clawed at his hair in frustration. "Please, not another riddle at a time like this!"

Seeing nobody appreciating his joke, Blaine sighed disappointedly and finally shouted,

"Go, Magmar!"

As magma burst upward, a Pokémon leaped out from the molten lava itself, steaming intensely as it landed magnificently at the center of the battlefield.

Once the battle began, Pikachu immediately fell into a disadvantageous position.

Not only was it struck by Fire Punch, but in the end, it was finished off by a powerful Fire Blast, collapsing to the ground and ending the battle.

As expected, the older the ginger, the spicier it became.

Although Blaine was already advanced in age, his Pokémon were still not to be underestimated.

Worried about Pikachu, Ash jumped down from the raised stone pillar and silently carried Pikachu in his arms.

Gone was his earlier fighting spirit. His face was now filled with disappointment.

Seeing him like this, even Misty refrained from teasing him and instead comforted him gently.

"Go back and train some more," Blaine said mercilessly regarding Charizard's earlier behavior. "A trainer who can't command their own Pokémon isn't qualified yet."

After speaking, he ignored Ash's ashamed expression and turned to Ethan instead.

"Well then? After seeing the strength of my Pokémon, do you still want to challenge me?"

"Of course." Ethan nodded seriously. "Since I came all the way here, how could I possibly back down? Though, Mr. Blaine, your Pokémon just finished battling. For fairness' sake, would you like them to rest first?"

"There's no need at all!" Blaine laughed loudly. "My Pokémon are blazing infernos of burning spirit! There's absolutely no need for you to worry about them!"

That confident, huh?

Seeing that Ethan had no intention of retreating, Blaine took the initiative and threw out another Poké Ball.

But when the blue light faded, it wasn't any of the three Pokémon from before.

Instead, it was a Rapidash, its mane, tail, and hooves all engulfed in blazing flames!

Pokédex: "Rapidash, the Fire Horse Pokémon. A Fire-type and the evolved form of Ponyta. Whenever it sees something moving quickly, it instinctively wants to race it. The faster it runs, the more fiercely the flames on its body burn."

Rapidash, huh…

After a brief moment of thought, Ethan sent out his own Pokémon.

Accompanied by a fierce roar, Gyarados made a dramatic entrance!

The moment Rapidash heard the roar and saw Gyarados's terrifying eyes, it instinctively stepped backward several paces.

Affected by Gyarados's Intimidate ability, Rapidash became fearful before the battle had even begun. Without exchanging a single move, it had already lost some of its momentum.

At that moment, Misty quietly walked over to Ethan and softly encouraged him,

"Ethan, Mr. Blaine isn't easy to deal with. You need to be careful."

Turning back, Ethan gave her a gentle smile and nodded lightly.

"Don't worry. I will."

Refocusing on the battlefield, Ethan decisively gave the attack order.

"Gyarados, let's go! Use Aqua Tail!"

( End of Chapter )
