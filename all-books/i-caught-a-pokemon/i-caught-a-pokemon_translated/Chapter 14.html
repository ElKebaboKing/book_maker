Northwest of Pewter City, beside an unnamed volcano—

After leaving Viridian Forest, Ethan Vale had wandered around this enormous volcano for two entire days.

Originally, he had planned to challenge the Pewter Gym directly. However, after hearing rumors that Charmander had been spotted nearby, he immediately changed priorities and made capturing a Charmander his top objective.

The reason for his urgency was not only because Charizard, Charmander's final evolution, possessed tremendous power, but also because its design was incredibly cool. Ever since his previous life, Ethan Vale had absolutely loved Charizard.

Unfortunately, aside from encountering Diglett, Zubat, Geodude, and Cubone, he had searched most of the volcano without finding even a trace of Charmander.

As the sky gradually darkened once again and night was about to fall, Ethan Vale had just set down his backpack and prepared to camp when Eevee—who had gone ahead to scout the area—suddenly came rushing back in a panic.

The moment it saw Ethan Vale, Eevee hurried over and cried out anxiously.

Although Ethan Vale couldn't understand the exact meaning behind the cries, remembering the task he had assigned Eevee instantly excited him.

"You found Charmander!?"

Eevee nodded repeatedly before turning around and signaling for Ethan Vale to follow.

Without wasting another second, Ethan Vale grabbed his backpack and followed Eevee through the vast rocky terrain. Eventually, within a narrow mountain crevice, he discovered dozens of glowing lights.

Those lights were especially noticeable in the darkness. When he got closer, he finally saw the truth—

It was a tribe composed of Charmander and Charmeleon. Looking upward, he could even vaguely see several Charizard soaring through the clouds above.

And those glowing lights…

Were the flames burning at the tips of their tails—the flames of life itself.

After so much effort, he had finally found Charmander!

However, the next challenge was obvious:

How could he capture one without alerting the Charmeleon and Charizard nearby?

After all, while Charmander were relatively gentle among their evolutionary family, Charmeleon and Charizard would obviously never allow Ethan Vale to openly capture one of their young.

Hidden within the dense trees, Ethan Vale carefully observed his surroundings. Then, when his eyes accidentally fell upon his backpack beside him, inspiration suddenly struck.

Opening the bag, he pulled out a flashlight and tore several small strips of tape, covering most of the lens while leaving only a tiny circular opening exposed.

Next, he took out a can of food, opened it, and placed it behind a large rock big enough to hide behind.

After finishing his preparations, he gestured toward his Pokémon and signaled them to hide nearby.

"This time we're short on time, so forget proper battle rules. Once we lure the Charmander over here, everyone attack together and finish the battle before the Charizard notice us. Understood?"

"Squirtle! Squirtle!"

Seeing all his Pokémon nod in agreement, Ethan Vale quietly returned alone to the tree where he had been hiding and continued observing the Charmander tribe carefully.

Charmander resembled small carnivorous dinosaurs. Their bodies were orange, while their bellies and the underside of their tails were cream-colored. Their most distinctive feature, naturally, was the flame burning at the tip of their tails.

That flame reflected both their physical condition and emotional state.

When energetic, the flame burned intensely.

When exhausted, the flame weakened.

When happy, it flickered gently.

When angry, it blazed violently.

Legend said that if the flame on a Charmander's tail ever went out, its life would end as well. However, as long as it remained healthy, the tail flame would continue burning strongly even if its entire body became soaked.

At the moment, the Charmander were still playing leisurely, completely unaware of Ethan Vale's presence.

After observing for a long while, Ethan Vale finally fixed his eyes on one particular Charmander.

This Charmander was slightly unusual.

Although it played and roughhoused alongside the others, it seemed somewhat clumsy and careless. Sometimes it used too much strength and failed to control itself properly, constantly annoying its companions and earning angry roars in return.

Looking at its slightly silly expression, Ethan Vale made up his mind.

Perfect. You're the one.

Taking out the flashlight, he switched it on and aimed directly at the Charmander.

The thin beam of light flashed several times across the Charmander's face. Since the light didn't spread outward, the other Charmander failed to notice anything unusual.

At first, the Charmander simply blinked and turned its head away from the irritating light.

But when the beam continued following its face wherever it moved, the Charmander's curiosity was finally piqued.

A giant question mark practically appeared above its head as it looked around in confusion before slowly walking toward Ethan Vale's hiding place.

Seeing the bait take effect, Ethan Vale quietly retreated behind the large rock.

Guided by the flashlight beam, the Charmander eventually arrived at the clearing. But just as it realized the light had vanished, it discovered instead a strange, delicious-smelling food placed on the ground.

It sniffed eagerly and cautiously stepped forward twice.

Then—

"Now!"

At Ethan Vale's signal, several figures burst out simultaneously from all directions!

Before Charmander could even react, it was instantly struck by Water Gun, Confusion, Vine Whip, and a Headbutt all at once.

In a flash, the poor Charmander collapsed unconscious, its vision going black.

"Perfect!"

Seeing Charmander fall, Ethan Vale rushed out immediately, grabbed a Poké Ball, and threw it.

A red light flashed as Charmander was pulled into the Poké Ball.

The ball didn't even shake once before the capture succeeded instantly.

Staring at the Poké Ball, Squirtle suddenly froze slightly as an uncomfortable memory surfaced in its mind.

For some reason…

This capture method felt strangely familiar.

"What are you spacing out for?"

Ethan Vale lightly smacked Squirtle on the head. Even in the darkness of night, the smile on his face looked incredibly bright.

"We've successfully captured it. Before the big guys notice us, let's get out of here!"

Late that night, at the Pokémon Center in Pewter City—

Inside the empty cafeteria, Ethan Vale leaned lazily against a chair with one leg crossed over the other, casually drinking vegetable juice with a relaxed expression.

At long last, he had finally achieved one of his goals—

He had collected all three Kanto starters.

Nearby, five Pokémon sat together.

Four of them were happily eating.

Only the newly captured Charmander sat there blankly, staring at its unfamiliar surroundings in complete confusion.

Its memory felt fragmented, almost as if it had merely taken a nap and woken up to find the entire world completely changed.

It didn't even understand why this human had suddenly become its Trainer.

Or why these Pokémon had suddenly become its companions.

Seeing Charmander sitting there in a daze, Ethan Vale gently pushed some food toward it.

"Eat a little more. What's wrong? Still feeling confused?"

He smiled lightly.

"Don't worry. After today, I'm sure you'll get used to everything soon enough."

( End of Chapter )
