"Haunter, use Shadow Ball!"

Beneath the open skies of Cerise Park, Haunter and Zapdos chased and clashed with one another. Electric- and Ghost-type attacks flew everywhere, and after a Shadow Ball struck Zapdos directly, both Pokémon had exhausted more than half their stamina. At last, the training session finally came to an end.

The two Pokémon landed back on the ground, both panting heavily. Judging from the battle, as long as Haunter did not use Hypnosis, the two were evenly matched.

And after being with Ethan for so long, through constant training and adjustment, Zapdos had finally begun to lower its pride slightly before him.

Though it still did not obey him completely, commanding it was no longer a major issue.

Nearby, Ivysaur lazily basked in the sunlight. Wartortle floated on its back in the water, Charmeleon lay atop a rock lazily swinging its tail, while Jolteon endlessly pestered it beside the shore.

At that moment, Kirlia suddenly gave a soft cry. Ethan's heart stirred slightly, and he immediately took out a Poké Ball and recalled Zapdos. Haunter also slipped quietly back into Ethan's shadow.

A few seconds later, two figures entered Cerise Park.

It was Chloe and Goh.

Picking up a brush from beside him, Ethan walked to the pool and called Wartortle over. While scrubbing the gaps in its shell, he casually asked,

"What brings you two here?"

With her hands behind her back, Chloe walked up behind Ethan, her large eyes watching him as she spoke cheerfully,

"I heard from my dad that you came back to the lab yesterday, so after school I came specifically to see Kirlia. As for Goh, he insisted on tagging along, and I couldn't stop him."

Kirlia gracefully bent its body like a dancer and gave Chloe an elegant bow.

Only then did Chloe turn toward it and smile warmly in return.

Meanwhile, Goh strolled around nearby with his hands behind his head. Seeing that the enormous Cerise Park only contained a handful of Pokémon, he laughed.

"You've been traveling this long and only caught these few Pokémon? If it were me, I definitely would've caught dozens already!"

The first time he met Ethan, seeing him with such a rare Pokémon had made him think Ethan must be incredibly impressive. But now that he thought about it… maybe he wasn't all that special after all.

Yet the moment those words left his mouth, a massive dark face suddenly burst out from Ethan's shadow and appeared right in front of him.

"WAAAH!!"

Goh jumped back several steps in fright, even letting out a scream more fitting for a girl.

But it wasn't over yet.

From the pool emerged a gigantic Pokémon with a gaping maw and a terrifying expression. Gyarados burst from the water and glared viciously at him!

Already shaken, Goh broke into a cold sweat. His footing slipped, and with a splash, he fell straight into the pool!

After choking on a few mouthfuls of water and flailing around, he finally managed to climb back out.

Too afraid to make eye contact with Gyarados again, he stared at Haunter in disbelief.

"There are Pokémon that hide inside shadows too!? Hey! Don't suddenly pop out and scare people like that!!"

"Heh heh!" Haunter mocked him. "Kid, your courage is way too weak! And you still want to become a Pokémon Trainer? You'd better start thinking about a different future!"

"Whoa, it talked!" Goh pointed at Haunter, completely stunned.

"Tch, what a country bumpkin reaction." Too lazy to bother with him further, Haunter slowly sank back into Ethan's shadow.

At that moment, Ethan walked over to his backpack and took out a beautifully wrapped small box before handing it to Chloe.

"This is a gift I bought for you in Celadon City."

"What is it?"

Chloe opened the box excitedly and found a bottle of perfume inside.

"It smells amazing!" She sprayed a little onto her wrist, and a faint fragrance immediately drifted into everyone's noses. "A few days ago, the girls in my class were talking about perfumes from Celadon City. They said the perfumes made there are among the most famous and best in the entire Kanto region!"

Seeing that Chloe genuinely liked it, Ethan finally felt relieved.

But beside them, watching Chloe's delighted expression, Goh suddenly felt uncomfortable for some reason.

With a dismissive expression, he muttered,

"Perfume is way too early for someone your age. It doesn't suit you at all."

"What kind of outdated thinking is that!?" Chloe shot him a glare. "Perfume isn't something only adult women can use! It helps people relax, stay calm, refresh their minds, and even heal emotionally. It's not just about smelling good!"

"Uh…"

Goh was instantly speechless, unable to understand why Chloe felt so strongly about perfume.

"Oh, right!" Chloe suddenly remembered something and turned back to Ethan. "My mom already finished the manga. She said if you have time, you should head over as soon as possible."

"Oh? That fast?"

Ethan was a little surprised.

However, instead of immediately heading to the Cerise residence, he first left his Pokémon in the park temporarily and returned to the laboratory to find Professor Cerise.

"Professor, could you transfer Zapdos from this Poké Ball into this Great Ball for me?"

Looking at the two Poké Balls placed before him, Professor Cerise adjusted his glasses in surprise.

"Oh? Why do you want to do that?"

Ethan sighed helplessly.

"Zapdos has finally become somewhat obedient, but it's still bothered by the fact that it was captured in a regular Poké Ball. Changing it into a Great Ball and giving it a different environment might help calm it down a little."

"I see." Professor Cerise nodded knowingly. "No problem. Leave it to me!"

At the Cerise family home—

Since Professor Cerise was still busy working, only Ethan, Chloe, and Yoshino Cerise were present this time.

Even Chloe's younger brother, Parker, was still outside playing with friends and had not returned home yet.

Seated beside the tea table, Ethan first took a sip of the tea Chloe handed him before presenting the gift he had bought for Yoshino Cerise.

"Aunt Yoshino, you've worked so hard these past few days! Please accept this small gift as a token of my gratitude!"

"Oh my, you really shouldn't have spent money on me."

Yoshino Cerise initially wanted to refuse, but with Chloe enthusiastically helping from the side, she eventually accepted the gift.

Only after seeing her accept it did Ethan finally turn his full attention toward the beautifully bound manga volume resting nearby.

Yoshino Cerise was truly dedicated. Not only had she worked tirelessly to finish the artwork, but she had even professionally compiled the entire story into a complete volume herself.

Opening the manga, Ethan skimmed through the earlier portions he had already seen before continuing with the rest of the story.

Once he finished reading everything, he nodded in satisfaction.

As a woman, Yoshino Cerise had portrayed the emotions within the story with delicate nuance and subtlety, perfectly matching the atmosphere and emotional depth the story was meant to convey.

"It's absolutely fantastic. I'm sure once this manga gets released, it'll become very popular!"
