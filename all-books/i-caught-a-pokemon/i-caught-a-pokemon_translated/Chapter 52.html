"You're the Matori Matrix!?"

As Ethan spoke, he glanced back at Looker. Seeing him nod lightly, Ethan immediately realized the situation had become even more troublesome.

"Using rockets like that… you people really have no sense of fair play."

"We're villains. Completing the mission is our top priority." Kane crossed his arms and grinned at Looker. "Still, I really didn't expect you to be hiding here. Originally, we were only following those two idiots to see what sneaky thing they were up to. Who knew we'd end up with such an unexpected bonus?"

"Hey! Who are you calling idiots!?" Behind them, James, Jessie, and Meowth came scrambling out of the warehouse together with their Pokémon, having heard Kane's words.

At the moment, however, the two of them looked absolutely miserable. Not only were they drenched from head to toe, but parts of their hair had even been singed black.

Right behind them, Kirlia, Charmander, and Squirtle hurried out of the warehouse in pursuit.

Jessie pointed at Kane and shouted,

"Ah! I remember you people! Aren't you the underlings of that watermelon-head glasses woman!? Damn it, you were spying on us!"

"Watermelon-head glasses woman?" Kane's expression darkened instantly. "You've got quite a lot of nerve referring to Miss Matori like that."

"Enough nonsense!" Jessie shouted. "This guy is our target! You're not allowed to interfere!"

"Oh? Your target?" Kane's gaze lingered on Ethan's mask for several seconds before realization dawned on him. "I see now. So you're Ethan Vale. Excellent. Since both of them are gathered here together, that saves us the trouble of searching. We can wipe them all out in one go!"

"What did you say!?" James cried out in disbelief. "You're actually going to disobey the Boss and steal our prey!?"

"Idiot." Kane took out a Poké Ball and threw it onto the ground. "As long as the problem gets solved, the Boss won't care who completed the mission! Go, Aggron!"

A flash of blue light appeared, and a massive Pokémon emerged onto the battlefield.

It had three sharp horns on its head, a steel-like tail, and a body completely covered in heavy armor.

The moment Aggron appeared, it let out a deafening roar toward the sky!

Meanwhile, the remaining four members of the Matori Matrix each released their own Pokémon one after another.

"Come out, Toxicroak!"

"Persian, show yourself!"

"You're up, Skuntank!"

"Crush them, Rhyperior!"

As all five Pokémon stepped forward together, they gradually surrounded Ethan and Looker.

Fight… or flee?

After only a moment's thought, Ethan instantly made his decision.

Without holding anything back, he released every single one of his Pokémon at once.

Blue flashes lit up one after another, and when Zapdos and Gyarados appeared simultaneously, their overwhelming presence was enough to completely rival the Matori Matrix.

Gyarados threw back its head and roared furiously, its crimson eyes locking directly onto Aggron!

"Zapdos!?" Looker shouted in shock.

Not only him—even Jessie and James jumped in fright.

The two of them had received orders to eliminate Ethan, but that horrible woman Matori had never once informed them that Ethan possessed a Legendary Pokémon like this!

"That damned watermelon-head glasses woman is absolutely awful!"

Jessie cursed furiously. Within Team Rocket, many people already knew she and Matori despised each other and had been at odds for a very long time.

James quietly asked,

"So… what do we do now?"

"We absolutely cannot let those guys succeed!" Jessie gritted her teeth. "If they complete the mission, we'll completely lose our value in front of the Boss. And in front of that woman, we'll never be able to hold our heads high again!"

"Huh? You want to stop them? Isn't that a bad idea?" James hesitated. "No matter what, they're still fellow Team Rocket members. If the Boss finds out, he definitely won't forgive us."

"Idiot, who said we're going against them?" Meowth grinned slyly. "We're simply following orders too. It's just that while attacking that brat, we accidentally got them caught up in it!"

Jessie and James' eyes both lit up.

"As expected of you, Meowth! You really are the most devious one here!"

After reaching their decision, the trio quickly vanished from sight.

At that moment, the battlefield had already descended into complete chaos.

Seeing their trainer surrounded, Kirlia, Charmander, and Squirtle no longer had time to care about Team Rocket's trio and immediately charged into battle as well.

Gyarados lunged directly at Aggron.

Toxicroak actively engaged Ivysaur.

Persian used its speed advantage to constantly harass Charmander.

Skuntank battled Jolteon.

As for Rhyperior, it fought both Squirtle and Kirlia at the same time!

Meanwhile, Zapdos had become entangled with heavy gunfire and artillery attacks from the five enemy trainers, temporarily unable to break free.

With Haunter still hidden in the shadows as backup, Ethan felt no panic whatsoever and calmly gave orders:

"Kirlia, go deal with Toxicroak! Ivysaur, team up with Squirtle against Rhyperior! Jolteon, stop wasting time with Skuntank and go help against Persian! Charmander, I'll leave Skuntank to you for now—just keep it occupied!"

After this series of tactical adjustments, the chaotic battlefield gradually became more manageable.

At the very least, in terms of type matchups, his Pokémon were no longer being suppressed.

Taking a moment, Ethan glanced toward Gyarados and Aggron.

Biting, roaring, smashing—

The two Pokémon had both gone completely berserk. Without any commands from their trainers, the only thing in their minds was tearing each other apart!

However, Gyarados had only just evolved. Lacking experience and still somewhat immature, it was clearly at a disadvantage. Without outside interference, defeat was only a matter of time.

Now Ethan finally understood why Looker had ended up in such terrible condition.

The Pokémon used by the Matori Matrix were genuinely far beyond what he could handle alone.

At that moment, accompanied by the thunderous sound of an approaching train, a freight train billowing black smoke came speeding toward them from the distance.

Knowing the opportunity could not be missed, Ethan quietly said,

"Haunter, I'm leaving Mr. Looker to you!"

"I got it." Haunter cackled wickedly as it emerged from Ethan's shadow and slipped into Looker's shadow instead.

Immediately afterward, Looker's body slowly floated into the air, drifting toward the freight train before landing steadily atop one of the train cars.

Over on the other side, Kane had been closely monitoring the situation the entire time.

The moment he saw Looker board the train, his expression changed drastically.

"Damn it! He's escaping!"

The members of the Matori Matrix were instantly alarmed and prepared to abandon their fight with Zapdos to rush toward the train.

But before they could move, darkness suddenly fell over their vision.

Then, a gigantic Meowth-shaped robot jumped down directly in front of them!

(End of Chapter)
