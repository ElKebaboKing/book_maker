Hearing her say that, Ethan Vale became genuinely curious about what kind of Pokémon Serena Yuto was about to send out.

On the other side of the field, Serena Yuto clearly had no intention of wasting any time. Without another word, she opened her Poké Ball.

A flash of blue light appeared, and a Pokémon resembling a circus mime stepped onto the battlefield.

A wave of astonished gasps erupted from the audience.

The moment Ethan saw its comical appearance, he immediately recognized it.

It was a Mr. Mime!

Even in the Kanto region, Mr. Mime was considered a rare Pokémon. Ethan hadn't expected Serena Yuto to own one.

More importantly, unlike an ordinary Mr. Mime, this one's body had a distinct green tint.

It was a Shiny Mr. Mime.

"Wow!! This is unbelievable!" the announcer exclaimed excitedly. "Mr. Mime is already an uncommon Pokémon, but this is the first time I've ever seen a Shiny Mr. Mime!"

As a humanoid Pokémon, Mr. Mime possessed oversized hands with suction cups on each of its ten fingers. Its movements resembled those of a professional mime performer, and many researchers even believed its unusually large hands had evolved specifically for pantomime performances.

Outside of battle, Mr. Mime was also famous for being an excellent household helper, making it especially popular among homemakers.

As for this rare Shiny Pokémon, Ethan couldn't tell whether Serena Yuto had caught it herself through extraordinary luck or whether her influential family had obtained it especially for her.

Either way, with a Shiny Pokémon appearing on the field alongside Serena Yuto's popularity, this match already had more than enough excitement to attract attention.

It was easy to imagine that, despite being only a preliminary-round battle, the number of viewers would soar dramatically.

In the stands, seeing everyone's excitement, Chloe turned to Brock.

"Mr. Mime... is it a particularly powerful Pokémon?"

Brock nodded, his bronze-colored face becoming serious.

"Whether it's powerful depends on the trainer using it. But Mr. Mime is a Psychic- and Fairy-type Pokémon. Those two types are mysterious and difficult to deal with, making it much trickier than most Pokémon."

Then he smiled.

"Actually, before we arrived at the Indigo Plateau, we met another Mr. Mime. We didn't catch it, but it decided to stay at Ash's house instead. Aunt Delia even gave it the nickname Mimey."

Chloe blinked.

"Aunt Delia?"

"Ash's mom," Brock explained.

Pressing his hands together beside his face, he seemed to radiate pink sparkles.

"Aunt Delia is such a gentle and caring person. And she's incredibly beautiful too!"

After witnessing Brock's lovestruck behavior several times already, Chloe had long grown used to it.

Once he returned to normal, she continued,

"So... are Shiny Pokémon actually stronger than normal ones?"

Brock thought for a moment.

"Well... as far as moves are concerned, there's really no difference. As for their other abilities... honestly, I've never had the chance to study one, so I don't really know."

"I see..."

Clutching the adorable plush toy Ethan had bought for her the previous night, Chloe looked toward the battlefield with concern.

Seeing her worried expression, Brock chuckled.

"You don't have to worry so much."

"We all know how strong Ethan is. Even if that Pokémon is both rare and Shiny, so what? That doesn't necessarily mean it can defeat him."

Back on the battlefield, Ethan recalled Blastoise.

After thinking for a moment, he chose his next Pokémon.

"Come on out... Gardevoir!"

Since his opponent was a Psychic- and Fairy-type, he decided to fight with equal typing.

"Oooh!" the announcer shouted excitedly.

"A showdown between two Psychic-type Pokémon! This is going to be an incredible battle! Who will emerge victorious? Let's find out!"

Meanwhile, behind the Indigo Stadium...

Listening to the deafening cheers echoing throughout the venue, Indigo League President Charles Goodshow slowly walked into the stadium's broadcast control center.

Inside, numerous staff members were busy working.

A bearded supervisor wearing black-rimmed glasses immediately stood up upon seeing him.

"President Goodshow! What brings you here?"

Goodshow smiled and waved casually.

"John, how many times have I told you? Don't make such a fuss over me. I was just taking a walk."

Looking around the room, he noticed the massive central screen broadcasting the Rock Field battle between Ethan and Serena Yuto.

The other three battlefields had all been reduced to small side screens.

"How are today's ratings?"

Unlike Goodshow, who oversaw the tournament as a whole, John, the broadcast director for the Indigo League Conference, paid constant attention to television ratings.

"The other matches are performing about as expected."

"But this Rock Field battle keeps climbing."

"It just surpassed a 20% live audience rating."

"Oh?"

Goodshow raised an eyebrow in surprise.

"Those are numbers we usually only see during the main tournament."

He then focused on the match itself.

On the screen, Gardevoir and the Shiny Mr. Mime were locked in an intense Psychic battle.

Although the two Pokémon exchanged attacks evenly, neither gained a type advantage against the other.

To casual viewers, the battle appeared evenly matched.

But with Goodshow's experienced eye, he could clearly see that Gardevoir was gradually taking control.

Looking at one of the trainers, he suddenly recognized him.

Isn't that one of the young trainers I met that night at the stadium?

So it's him.

Goodshow nodded approvingly.

"I've heard that this trainer hasn't lost a single battle since the tournament began."

"It looks like we'll have to keep a close eye on him from now on."

"I already am!" John replied excitedly.

"This match has everything—a popular female trainer, a rare Shiny Pokémon, two attractive competitors, and excellent battles."

"The ratings have shattered every previous preliminary-round record."

"In fact, no preliminary match in the history of the Indigo League has ever attracted this many viewers!"

"And most importantly..."

"This is still only the preliminary rounds."

"I can't even imagine how much attention the winner will receive once we reach the main tournament... the semifinals... or even the finals."

"Really?"

Even Goodshow seemed pleasantly surprised.

"That sounds like wonderful news—not just for the trainers, but for the League as well."
