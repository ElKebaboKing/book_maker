Thud! Thud! Thud!!

Just then, Ivysaur unleashed another barrage of Razor Leaf, sending sharp leaves flying toward Misdreavus once again.

The attack struck home, causing Misdreavus to cry out in pain. Its once-cute expression twisted into something far more sinister.

Agatha raised her voice.

"Misdreavus, use Perish Song!"

Perish Song!?

The moment Ethan heard the move's name, his heart tightened.

Although Perish Song was only a Normal-type move, it was one of the most dangerous techniques in existence. Once successfully cast, it placed a mark upon both Pokémon. After only a few turns, the mark would activate, causing both combatants to faint instantly—regardless of how much stamina they still had.

It was as though a timed bomb had been attached to them. Once the countdown reached zero, there was no escape.

Much like Destiny Bond, it was the perfect move for dragging an opponent down together when backed into a corner.

"Ivysaur! Stop it before it finishes!"

Ivysaur lashed Misdreavus repeatedly with Vine Whip, but it was already too late.

Misdreavus opened its mouth, releasing a series of black musical notes infused with eerie energy. Like a song heralding death itself, the haunting melody echoed throughout the battlefield.

The next moment, glowing energy marks appeared on both Pokémon.

Even standing far away, Ethan felt an inexplicable chill after hearing the melody.

It was as though an invisible hand had reached into his chest and tightened around his heart.

"There's no need to be so nervous."

Agatha glanced at him calmly.

"This move only affects Pokémon. It poses no danger to humans."

"I know."

Ethan nodded.

He wasn't worried about himself.

He was worried about Ivysaur.

He understood the move well.

The simplest way to remove Perish Song's effect was to recall the Pokémon into its Poké Ball, severing its connection to the battlefield.

But in a one-on-one Gym battle, recalling his Pokémon would count as surrendering the match.

There was only one option left.

Defeat Misdreavus before Perish Song took effect.

"Ivysaur, give it everything you've got! Use Seed Bomb!"

Ivysaur's flower bud trembled violently as numerous hard seeds shot into the air in graceful arcs before raining down upon Misdreavus.

Misdreavus quickly flew upward in an attempt to dodge—

—but suddenly, two Vine Whips shot out, wrapping around it and dragging it back down!

The Seed Bombs exploded one after another upon its body, scattering bursts of green energy across the battlefield.

Although Misdreavus staggered heavily, it still refused to fall.

"This is bad!"

Realizing time was almost up, Ethan shouted,

"Just a little more! Finish it!"

Perhaps sensing Ethan's desperate determination, Ivysaur responded with its own hidden potential.

Its body suddenly began glowing with brilliant white light.

It was evolving...

Within moments, the light faded.

Standing where Ivysaur had once been was its fully evolved form—

Venusaur!

Its massive body now supported a fully bloomed flower upon its back.

There wasn't even time to admire the transformation.

Venusaur immediately unleashed another Razor Leaf.

Empowered by evolution, the attack's strength had increased dramatically.

The storm of razor-sharp leaves struck Misdreavus head-on—

—and knocked it out instantly.

Thud!

As Misdreavus collapsed, the Perish Song mark was just about to activate.

Without waiting for the referee's announcement, Ethan immediately recalled Venusaur into its Poké Ball.

The referee glanced toward Agatha.

Seeing her nod without objecting to the technicality, he raised his hand.

"Misdreavus is unable to battle! The challenger wins!"

Ethan finally let out a long sigh of relief before releasing Venusaur once again.

Just as he had expected, the moment Venusaur entered its Poké Ball, its connection to Misdreavus was broken, causing the Perish Song effect to disappear completely.

Looking at Venusaur now, Ethan couldn't help but smile.

Its strength had increased tremendously.

Though...

It certainly wasn't as adorable as before.

In fact, compared to Ivysaur, it looked considerably less cute.

Still, it was his Pokémon.

He would never dislike it.

Instead, he felt nothing but pride.

Patting the massive Pokémon—now nearly two meters tall including the giant flower on its back—Ethan smiled warmly.

"Venusaur, I never imagined you'd evolve at such a critical moment."

"That was incredible."

Venusaur rubbed its large face affectionately against Ethan's hand and happily cried out.

However, it still wasn't accustomed to its dramatically increased size.

Using a bit too much strength, it nearly knocked Ethan over.

At that moment, Agatha approached, holding a badge shaped like a stalk of asparagus.

She handed it to Ethan.

"Congratulations on earning the Earth Badge."

"But I have to admit, although I lost this battle, your victory certainly didn't come easily."

"What choice did I have?"

Ethan rolled his eyes helplessly.

"I could've won much more comfortably if you hadn't changed the battle format at the last minute and used a move that was basically mutual destruction."

"Hahaha!"

Agatha burst into laughter.

"That's life."

"Things rarely go exactly as we wish."

"Young man, you've impressed me."

"If you compete in the Pokémon League, I truly believe you have a chance to become Champion."

"When that day comes, if you still want a truly fair battle..."

"...you're always welcome to challenge me again."

"Challenge an Elite Four member?"

Ethan nodded thoughtfully.

"That certainly sounds appealing."

"But before that, I'll take it one step at a time."

"My first goal is reaching the League finals."

"Very well said."

Agatha looked at him approvingly.

"At your age, being this talented while remaining humble is an exceptionally rare quality."

Since there was still plenty of time before the Pokémon League began, Ethan decided to return to Vermilion City and rest for a few days after leaving Viridian City.

However, while passing through Cerulean City, he unexpectedly encountered Misty, Ash, and Brock once again.

The three looked dusty from traveling and were just about to enter the city when they spotted Ethan.

After exchanging greetings, Ethan looked toward Ash.

"So you're only getting back now?"

"Well? Did you earn the Volcano Badge from Blaine?"

"Of course!"

Ash rubbed his nose proudly before opening his jacket to reveal his seventh Gym Badge.

"My Charizard finally listened to me! After an intense battle, it defeated Blaine's Magmar!"

"Only seven badges, and you're already showing off?"

Misty teased mercilessly.

Then she immediately turned to Ethan, her attitude changing completely.

"Ethan... I bet you've already earned your eighth badge, haven't you?"

"I have."

Ethan opened his Badge Case, showing them all eight Gym Badges.

"I just came back from the Viridian Gym."

"Now it's time to prepare for the Pokémon League."

"But speaking of which..."

He looked at the three of them curiously.

"If you're supposed to be challenging Gyms, why have you come back to Cerulean City?"
