Riding atop Gyarados, they surged through one massive wave after another with unstoppable determination, heading straight for New Island.

Ethan sat on Gyarados's neck, while Misty sat behind him, wrapping both arms tightly around his waist. Ash and Brock were left near Gyarados's tail, desperately clinging to its scales to avoid being swallowed by the raging sea.

No one knew how much time had passed.

After climbing over one final gigantic wave, the roaring wind and torrential rain suddenly disappeared.

The sea became completely calm.

Looking around carefully, they realized they had unknowingly entered the eye of the storm.

Behind them, enormous waves still crashed endlessly, but inside the eye, surrounded by the violent circular storm, everything was perfectly peaceful, untouched by the rain.

The place gave Ethan a strange sense of déjà vu.

It reminded him of Dragonite Island.

After everything they had endured, everyone was exhausted. They stretched their stiff limbs, trying to relieve their aching muscles.

At that moment, Misty suddenly pointed ahead.

"Look over there!"

Following her finger, they saw a lonely island standing in the middle of the sea.

Night had already fallen, and under the moonlight, a gloomy castle with towering spires stood prominently on the island.

"We finally made it..." Brock let out a long sigh. "After all that effort... this should be New Island, right?"

"It is."

Ethan checked the coordinates on his phone and nodded before gently patting Gyarados, directing it toward the island.

After traveling a few more nautical miles, they finally reached New Island.

Only then did they discover a harbor built inside a cave beneath the castle.

As Gyarados approached the dock, a woman carrying a lantern was already waiting for them.

Judging by her appearance, she was the same woman who had appeared through the communication device.

"Welcome. Please present your invitation."

After everyone stepped ashore, the woman spoke calmly.

Her movements were unnaturally stiff, and even when she talked, her expression never changed.

"Invitation?" Ash blinked before quickly taking out a device identical to Ethan's. "Do you mean this?"

"That's correct."

She nodded before looking at Ethan's device as well.

"You are indeed among our invited Trainers. Please follow me."

Ever since they landed, Brock had been carefully observing the woman.

After looking at her for a few moments, he suddenly exclaimed,

"Ah! It's really you! You're the Nurse Joy who disappeared from Vermilion City, aren't you!?"

"What are you talking about?"

The woman slowly shook her head.

"I have lived and worked in this castle since I was born. I am not the Nurse Joy you're referring to."

Truthfully, only Brock could notice the subtle differences between different Nurse Joys.

If Ethan hadn't already known who she really was, there was no way he could have identified her simply from her ordinary face, which lacked any distinctive features or obvious signs of age.

Just as in the original story, this Nurse Joy had been brought here by Mewtwo and placed under hypnosis, causing her to completely forget her past.

She continued,

"Please come with me. Besides yourselves, several Trainers have already arrived."

Following her through a narrow cave passage, they entered the castle.

Out of habit, Ethan took out his phone and snapped a few pictures.

Although Nurse Joy noticed, she made no attempt to stop him.

They climbed a long staircase before arriving at a uniquely designed doorway.

Throughout the journey, Ethan found the castle strangely unusual.

Unlike ordinary castles built from stone and wood, neither the walls nor the floor looked like they belonged on this planet.

Instead, everything possessed a distinctly futuristic, almost extraterrestrial appearance.

The massive doors slowly opened.

Beyond them was a spacious hall.

Aside from a spiral staircase leading upward, the hall was almost empty. At its center stood only a long table.

Seated on either side of it were three Trainers, each accompanied by their Pokémon.

Nurse Joy gestured toward them.

"Those are the Trainers who arrived before you."

Misty looked around in surprise.

"That's strange. So many people headed out into the storm. Why are there only three people here?"

"Those who couldn't pass through the storm were never destined to reach this place."

Only then did Ash and the others realize that the violent storm had actually been a test.

Nurse Joy stepped aside and politely gestured toward the table.

"Now, please release your Pokémon and take a seat while you wait."

Ash, Misty, and Brock followed her instructions, releasing all of their Pokémon.

Ethan, however, only released Gardevoir, while Haunter remained hidden in the shadows, protecting him discreetly from the darkness.

As they approached the table, the three Trainers examined them.

One of them, a man dressed in red, lazily remarked,

"What? More people? I thought only a handful of us would make it here."

"Don't say that."

The only girl among the three smiled warmly.

"I think it's much more interesting with more people."

She introduced herself politely.

"My name is Claire, though you can also call me Candy."

"I'm Hayden," said the last Trainer—the slightly overweight man Ethan had seen earlier at the harbor.

"I'm Ash," Ash replied. "These two are my traveling companions, Brock and Misty."

"I'm Ethan."

Finally, everyone's attention turned to the red-clothed Trainer.

Seeing them all staring at him, he sighed reluctantly.

"Fine. My name is Logan. Nice to meet you."

Taking the opportunity, Ethan studied their Pokémon.

Venusaur. Blastoise. Gyarados. Ninetales. Golduck.

None of them were duplicates.

Each Pokémon had clearly been raised exceptionally well, and judging from their presence alone, they were undoubtedly powerful.

Just as everyone was about to continue talking, the lights throughout the hall suddenly dimmed.

As confusion spread among the group, Nurse Joy announced,

"Everyone, thank you for waiting."

"The strongest Pokémon Trainer... is about to make an appearance."

As though sensing an overwhelming presence, every Pokémon in the hall became visibly restless.

A brilliant beam of light suddenly illuminated the staircase.

A figure slowly descended from above.

When it finally landed on the floor, everyone stared in complete shock.

It was a Pokémon none of them had ever seen before.

In fact, aside from Ethan, every Trainer present was seeing Mewtwo for the very first time.

"W-What... is that?"

Ignoring their astonishment, Nurse Joy calmly announced,

"That's right."

"This is the strongest Pokémon Trainer... and also the strongest Pokémon—Mewtwo."

The lights returned to normal.

Mewtwo stood silently before them, calmly observing every person in the room.

"So this is your so-called strongest Trainer!?"

Hayden suddenly slammed both hands onto the table and shot to his feet.

"A Pokémon commanding other Pokémon? Don't be ridiculous!"

"How could something so absurd possibly exist in this world!?"
