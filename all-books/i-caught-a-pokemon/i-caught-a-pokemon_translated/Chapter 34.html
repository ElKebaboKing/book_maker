Hearing Misty's scream, Ethan and the others hurriedly turned around and saw the so-called ghost as well.

The ghost looked exactly like the woman in the portrait. Floating in midair, her body was transparent and blurry as she slowly drifted toward them.

"I've missed you so much."

The soft voice drifted from the ghost's mouth. Ethan was not particularly affected, but Brock beside him was completely captivated by her beautiful appearance, utterly mesmerized.

"I've missed you too..." Brock muttered dreamily.

Looking at Ethan and Brock, the ghost softly said, "I've been waiting for you to return."

As soon as her words fell, a strange gust of wind suddenly swept through the area. In the next moment, Ethan and Brock's bodies were lifted uncontrollably into the air and pulled toward her.

The suction force was incredibly strong. Ethan instinctively reached out, trying to grab onto something, but there was nothing nearby he could use for support. Just as he was being dragged closer and closer, a pair of delicate hands suddenly grabbed him.

Naturally, it was Misty.

Seeing Ethan being pulled away, she no longer cared about being afraid of ghosts.

"You! Where exactly are you trying to take them!?"

Misty tightly gripped one of Ethan's arms and pulled backward with all her strength. Although she was only a girl and lacked great physical strength, no matter how fiercely the strange wind blew, she refused to let go.

Ethan already knew this ghost was actually Gastly in disguise. Thanks to Misty buying him some time, he immediately shouted to his Pokémon:

"Charmander, use Ember! Jolteon, use Thunder Shock!"

A burst of flames and a flash of electricity shot toward the ghost almost simultaneously.

After being attacked, the ghost naturally could no longer maintain control of the strange wind. While dodging the attacks, the suction vanished, causing Ethan and Brock to fall from the air.

Misty instinctively stepped forward, trying to catch Ethan, but he ended up landing directly on top of her instead. Brock, meanwhile, was far less fortunate and crashed heavily onto the ground.

Fortunately, the painful fall snapped Brock back to his senses temporarily.

"What happened?" he asked blankly while looking around. Ever since arriving at Maiden's Peak, his mind had been hazy the entire time, almost as though he had truly been bewitched.

On the other side, the ghost's tone suddenly changed after being interrupted.

"You annoying pests! Stop getting in my way!!"

As soon as the words fell, a swarm of skeletons suddenly appeared, shrieking as they flew over everyone's heads.

"Ahhh! What are those things!?" Seeing such a horrifying sight, Misty could not help burying herself against Ethan's chest.

Ethan stood up and realized everyone seemed to have lost their courage. He immediately shouted loudly:

"Don't be afraid! It's just a Pokémon!"

As he spoke, he took out his Pokédex and pointed it at the ghost.

Pokédex: "Gastly, the Gas Pokémon. A Ghost- and Poison-type Pokémon born from poisonous gases. It excels at hypnosis and loves playing pranks. It usually hides its form and can pass through any solid object, including walls."

"Gastly!?"

Upon hearing it was merely a Pokémon, both Ash and Misty stared at it in shock.

"Hehehe, since you figured it out, I guess there's no helping it now!"

The woman suddenly grinned widely. Her body slowly transformed, first turning into the old fortune-teller woman in the purple cloak before finally becoming a black sphere surrounded by purple gas.

"Sometimes I'm the legendary beautiful woman, sometimes I'm a mysterious fortune teller! But my true form is—Gastly!!"

The black sphere was both Gastly's body and its face. Aside from its sharp eyes, it had only a large mouth with two short fangs exposed.

"So it really is a Pokémon!?" Ash exclaimed excitedly. "Alright! Then let's battle with Pokémon! Pikachu, go!"

"Pika, pika!"

"You want to battle?" Gastly grinned wickedly. "I'm actually in quite a hurry right now, so I'll just take care of all of you at once!"

As its voice echoed out, waves of energy spread from Gastly's body toward everyone.

Ethan instantly recognized the move.

"Oh no! It's Hypnosis!"

Before anyone could react, the waves of Hypnosis passed through them all. An overwhelming wave of drowsiness immediately surged over everyone, their eyelids growing heavier and heavier until they collapsed onto the ground one after another.

"Well, that was easy enough." Gastly looked around proudly after seeing the three humans and all of their Pokémon completely asleep. "Alright, now it's time to get down to business!"

The strange wind appeared once more, lifting Brock's body into the air.

But just as Gastly turned its attention toward Ethan, a flash of blue light suddenly burst from Ethan's waist, and a Pokémon appeared beside him.

With green hair and a body resembling a young girl, it was Kirlia!

"Oh?" Gastly sized Kirlia up curiously. "You actually came out of your Poké Ball on your own. What? Are you trying to protect your trainer?"

It then shook its head.

"But unfortunately for you, Psychic-types are at a disadvantage against Ghost-types. There's no way you can defeat me. Everything you do will just be pointless."

Kirlia naturally understood the unfavorable type matchup, so instead of attacking directly, it used Psychic to open all of Ethan's Poké Balls at once.

Blue lights flashed one after another, and all of Ethan's Pokémon appeared—including Zapdos.

Gastly's gaze swept across Kirlia, Zapdos, Ivysaur, and Squirtle before pausing for quite a while on the constantly flopping Magikarp.

"Four against one, huh?" Gastly laughed. "I didn't expect this trainer to even own Zapdos. He's definitely no ordinary trainer."

Zapdos proudly raised its head. Part of it was disdain toward Gastly, while another part had clearly been triggered by Gastly's words.

As for Gastly, perhaps intimidated by Zapdos's imposing aura, it finally realized the situation had become rather troublesome.

After hesitating for a moment, it sighed.

"Forget it. Even though taking only one person makes things a little less efficient, since all of you care so much about your trainer, I'll spare him and just take this one instead."

With that, Gastly floated away while carrying Brock, disappearing into the night without another glance.

When Ethan finally woke up, he realized he was lying on the ground. Right in front of him was Kirlia's worried face.

"When did you come out?"

After gently rubbing Kirlia's hair, Ethan sat up and discovered that Ash, Misty, Charmander, Jolteon, and Pikachu were all sleeping soundly nearby.

Only Brock was missing.

Meanwhile, on the other side, he also spotted the rest of his Pokémon—including Zapdos.

Even Zapdos came out!?

After thinking for a moment, Ethan quickly pieced together what had happened.
