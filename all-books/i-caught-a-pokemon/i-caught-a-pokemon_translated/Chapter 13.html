Professor Oak's Laboratory was located in Pallet Town, a beautiful place with picturesque scenery.

That afternoon, when Ethan Vale knocked on the door of Professor Oak's Laboratory, the kind old man—with his gray-white short hair and his habit of occasionally reciting Pokémon senryu poems—welcomed him with a warm and generous smile.

"Professor Oak, sorry to trouble you," Ethan Vale introduced himself. "I'm Ethan Vale from the Cerise Laboratory in Vermilion City. Professor Cerise entrusted me to deliver something to you."

Professor Oak nodded.

"I already heard from Cerise that you would be coming. Still, I didn't expect you to travel from Vermilion City to here in just two days. That's impressively fast."

"Since it was Professor Cerise's request, I naturally didn't dare delay," Ethan Vale replied.

He first handed over the box containing the prototype device, then took another package out of his backpack—the one given to him by the employee at the Viridian City shop.

Seeing the package, Professor Oak's eyes lit up.

"You even brought this package as well? That's a huge help! Tomorrow is the departure day for the new batch of trainers. Without the Poké Balls inside, I truly wouldn't know what to do."

Ethan Vale casually remarked, "This time of year must always be very busy for you, Professor. It sounds exhausting."

"That's right, that's right." Professor Oak spread his hands helplessly. "When you get old, even if you refuse to admit it, your body still can't keep up anymore. But this year, my disappointing grandson will also be setting out on his journey. I'm actually quite curious about what kind of person he'll become."

"Your disappointing grandson?"

Ethan Vale asked, "Are you talking about Gary?"

"Oh?" Professor Oak looked surprised. "You know Gary?"

"Not personally." Ethan Vale quickly waved his hands. "I only heard Professor Cerise mention him once in passing."

Even so, Ethan Vale could barely contain his excitement.

Gary was leaving on his journey tomorrow?

That meant Ash would also soon begin his adventure with Pikachu!

Just thinking about eventually meeting them made Ethan Vale excited. At the same time, he knew he needed to keep improving quickly himself. Otherwise, considering how rapidly they grew, he might soon be surpassed and left far behind.

After politely declining Professor Oak's invitation to stay for lunch, Ethan Vale once again set off toward Pewter City.

After passing through Viridian City, since he was no longer in a rush, Ethan Vale chose to head straight into Viridian Forest this time.

Viridian Forest stretched between Viridian City and Pewter City. Countless varieties of trees filled the forest, forming a natural maze. Even with a map, every year countless people still lost their way within this enormous forest where sunlight barely penetrated.

The most common Pokémon here were Bug-types and Flying-types.

Because of the great variety of Bug Pokémon living here, many people jokingly referred to the forest as "Bug Catchers' Paradise."

After entering the forest, Ethan Vale relied on instinct as he moved through the wilderness. Along the way, he defeated several wild Pokémon that suddenly attacked him and also encountered Pidgeotto and Butterfree—Pokémon he had always liked in the past.

After brief hesitation, however, he still chose not to capture them.

Meanwhile, his four Pokémon also gained valuable experience inside the forest. Each of them improved to some extent through the constant battles and training.

After walking for quite some time, the bushes ahead suddenly began to shake violently.

Just as Ethan Vale thought another wild Pokémon was about to ambush him, a young samurai dressed in armor, carrying a bug-catching net on his back and a sword at his waist, suddenly jumped out of the bushes.

"Tch, hearing movement, I thought it was some rare Pokémon. Turns out it's just a trainer."

The samurai boy sized up Ethan Vale's Pokémon before asking directly:

"So, how about a battle?"

Ethan Vale blinked in surprise.

"Huh? That direct?"

"Of course!" the samurai boy declared proudly. "As Pokémon Trainers, the moment our eyes meet, the battle has already begun!"

Without waiting for Ethan Vale's agreement, he immediately threw out a Poké Ball.

"Go, Pinsir!"

The Poké Ball opened, and a fierce-looking Pokémon with two enormous horns—resembling a stag beetle—appeared before Ethan Vale.

Pokédex:

"Pinsir, the Stag Beetle Pokémon. Bug-type. It possesses two powerful horns. Once it clamps onto prey, it never lets go unless it tears the target apart."

"What a troublesome guy…"

Ethan Vale sighed and looked over his four Pokémon before deciding:

"Squirtle, I'm counting on you."

In terms of typing, both the Grass-type Bulbasaur and the Psychic-type Kirlia would be at a disadvantage against the Bug-type Pinsir. Only Eevee and Squirtle had neutral matchups.

"Squirtle! Squirtle!"

Squirtle jumped into the center of the battlefield, its eyes filled with fighting spirit.

However, just as Ethan Vale expected an evenly matched battle, Squirtle only needed a few moves to defeat Pinsir cleanly and decisively.

"What!?" the samurai boy cried out in disbelief. "My Pinsir lost that quickly!?"

"There's nothing strange about it," Ethan Vale replied with a sigh. "It's simply because your Pinsir's level is too low. If you get the chance, you should really train it more."

The samurai boy's face flushed red as he clenched his fists.

"Don't get cocky yet! Take this—my second Pokémon! Go, Caterpie!"

A flash of red light appeared as he released his second Pokémon.

Ah, so it's Rayquaza's baby form—wait, no, it's Caterpie.

Caterpie was a classic Bug-type Pokémon. Its back was green while its underside was white, with its most noticeable feature being the red Y-shaped antenna on its head.

Pokédex:

"Caterpie, the Worm Pokémon. Bug-type. It uses the suction cups on its feet to climb trees and feed on its favorite leaves."

Too lazy to switch Pokémon, Ethan Vale kept Squirtle on the field and once again won easily after only a few attacks.

Seeing the samurai boy's unwilling expression, Ethan Vale was about to leave when he suddenly seemed to remember something.

He stopped, turned around, and said:

"Oh right, I came from Pallet Town. If you're still dissatisfied, then hurry and get stronger so you can properly teach those rookie trainers coming after me a lesson."

The samurai boy muttered:

"Pallet Town? Rookie trainers?"

"That's right." Ethan Vale nodded lightly, a mischievous smile spreading across his face. "The trainers from Pallet Town are all really strong. No matter who you run into, I doubt you'll be able to beat them."

"Damn it! Don't get too full of yourself!"

The samurai boy's eyes burned with determination as he shouted toward Ethan Vale's disappearing figure:

"Remember my words! No matter how strong the trainers from Pallet Town are, next time I'll definitely show all of you what I can do!!"

(End of Chapter)
