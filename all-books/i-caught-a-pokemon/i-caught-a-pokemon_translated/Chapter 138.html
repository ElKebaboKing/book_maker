The battles on the first day continued until around 10:00 PM, and the second day's matches followed the same pattern. It wasn't until late that evening that another 128 contestants were eliminated.

The once-bustling Athletes' Village suddenly felt much quieter as many disappointed Trainers—and their companions—packed up and left.

But joy and sorrow were never shared equally.

Early on the third morning, the remaining competitors once again gathered eagerly at the matchup drawing hall.

Ethan and Ash walked to the same registration counter as before and were greeted by the cheerful staff member they had met previously.

Since they had already spoken once, the atmosphere was much more relaxed this time.

"Congratulations to both of you," she said with a warm smile. "You two are really impressive. So many well-known Trainers were eliminated, yet you both made it through the first round with ease!"

"It's no big deal," Ash replied confidently as he adjusted his cap. "We're really strong! We'll keep beating our opponents and advancing. At the very least... hmm... we'll make it to the Top 16!"

"What confidence!" the staff member laughed. "Then I wish you both the best of luck!"

After glancing at the increasingly impatient line behind them, she quickly returned to business.

"Now that the first round is over, both your battlefields and opponents need to be redrawn. So... which of you wants to go first?"

"Me!"

True to his personality, Ash eagerly stepped forward first.

He slammed the button, and when the roulette finally stopped, he was assigned to the Rock Battlefield. His opponent was a Trainer he had never seen before.

Seeing that he had avoided facing Ethan or Gary so early in the tournament, Ash secretly breathed a sigh of relief.

Although he wasn't afraid of challenging them, he didn't want to meet his friends this early in the competition.

Next, it was Ethan's turn.

He stepped forward and calmly pressed the button.

The roulette gradually slowed before finally stopping on the Water Battlefield.

At the same time, his next opponent appeared on the screen.

The man wore the attire of a wandering swordsman, with a long sword hanging from his waist. More than a Pokémon Trainer, he looked like a master swordsman who wielded the wind itself.

The moment Misty saw his portrait, she blinked in surprise.

"Huh?"

Ethan turned toward her.

"What is it?"

"That guy..." Misty frowned at the screen. "Isn't he that unlucky idiot we met before arriving at the Indigo Plateau?"

How do you people know everyone...?

Suppressing the urge to complain, Ethan asked,

"An unlucky idiot? Who exactly is he?"

Misty thought hard but couldn't remember his name.

"I think he's called... um..."

"His name is Saizo," Brock answered. "When he first arrived at the Indigo Plateau, Team Rocket trapped him with a fruit trap and stole all of his Gym Badges. By the time we found him, he had challenged Ash using Ash's badges as the wager. Of course, Pikachu defeated him, and even his own Pokémon looked down on him afterward. In the end, we had to work pretty hard to help him recover all of his badges."

Getting all your badges stolen by Team Rocket?

No wonder Misty called him an idiot.

Now that the matchups had been decided, Ethan and Ash thanked the staff member and left the drawing hall.

Unfortunately, both of their battles had been scheduled for the same time, meaning they wouldn't be able to watch or cheer for each other's matches.

When they reached the fork in the road leading toward their respective stadiums, Ethan extended his hand.

"Good luck. I hope we both defeat our opponents and advance to the next round. Honestly... I'd love to face you in the finals."

"I will," Ash replied firmly, shaking his hand. "Whether it's you or Gary, you're both people I want to defeat!"

Ash turned to leave with Misty and Brock.

Watching his back, Ethan hesitated for a moment before calling out.

"Hey, Ash!"

Ash looked back.

"What is it?"

After a brief silence, Ethan spoke seriously.

"If you want to go further in this tournament, I think you should spend some time talking with Charizard. If what happened at the Cinnabar Gym happens again—if it refuses to obey you—your Indigo League journey could end right there."

Ash scratched his cheek awkwardly.

"I've already tried talking to it lots of times... but it just won't listen."

Seeing how serious Ethan was, and because he had long respected Ethan's judgment, Ash eventually nodded.

"Alright. After today's match, I'll try talking to Charizard again."

After parting ways with Ash and the others, Ethan and Chloe arrived at the waiting room for the Water Battlefield.

The Pokémon League had prepared a private waiting room for every competing Trainer, giving them a quiet place to relax, adjust their condition, and plan their strategy before battle.

With no one to disturb him, Ethan placed a cool towel over his face and leaned back on the sofa, closing his eyes as he considered which Pokémon he would use.

While there was still some time before the match, Chloe took out her phone and quickly watched recordings of Saizo's previous battle.

"From what I can tell..." she said after watching several clips, "...he doesn't seem especially strong. In his last match, he only managed to defeat his opponent after it came down to his final Pokémon."

"Is that so?"

Still lying lazily on the sofa without opening his eyes, Ethan replied casually,

"Maybe his opponent was unusually strong... or maybe he just wasn't in top condition. By the way, which Pokémon did he use?"

Chloe picked up a notebook beside her and checked her notes.

"Let's see... Pidgeot, Nidoking, and Marowak. Brock also mentioned earlier that Marowak is Saizo's most trusted partner—his ace. But I don't know if he'll use the same team again this time."

Another Marowak?

Marowak certainly seemed to be making plenty of appearances in this Indigo League.

Just then, the stadium announcement echoed through the speakers.

"Contestant Ethan, Contestant Ethan, please proceed to the battlefield after hearing this announcement and prepare for your match."

Ethan removed the towel, patted his cheeks to wake himself up, and stood.

"Whether he uses those Pokémon or not, we'll find out soon enough. Either way, I'll be giving it everything I've got."

He straightened his clothes and headed toward the door.

Before leaving, he looked back at Chloe with a confident smile.

"Alright, don't worry so much. You should head to the stands too. Masha and Misa have already saved you a seat."

He grinned.

"Now... just sit back and enjoy the show."
