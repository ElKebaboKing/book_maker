Ethan looked closely and immediately felt that the man in the suit looked familiar. After thinking for a moment, he realized that this was the same man he had battled on the ship when he first arrived in the Kanto region!

What a coincidence.

Back then, the purple mouse Pokémon that fought Kirlia had been a Rattata. Now, it had evolved into the large brown rodent Pokémon—Raticate.

"Raticate, use Jump Kick!"

"Starmie, use Tackle!"

The two Pokémon collided head-on. Although it looked evenly matched at first glance, Raticate clearly had the upper hand. As soon as they separated, the suited man gave another command.

"Raticate, use Hyper Fang!"

Raticate dashed forward and sank its massive front teeth into Starmie. As it bit down harder and harder, the gemstone at the center of Starmie's body suddenly cracked, and the Pokémon collapsed backward onto the floor.

The crowd erupted into applause, cheering enthusiastically for the victor.

Seeing the young trainer hug his defeated Pokémon, the suited man still maintained a gentlemanly attitude despite winning.

"It was only a small battle," he said gracefully. "You could just say the goddess of victory favored me a little more today. Still, you should train your Starmie harder in the future."

At that moment, a woman in a red strapless evening dress walked over and linked arms with the suited man.

Victorious in both battle and romance, it was obvious this man thoroughly enjoyed his lifestyle.

"Well then," he announced proudly, "is there any other trainer who wants to challenge my Raticate?"

Before Ethan could even react, a blur shot past him.

Ash had already rushed to the center of the crowd.

"I'm fired up now! Let my Butterfree battle your Raticate!"

Shaking his head, Ethan greeted Misty and Brock before deciding not to stay and watch the battle. Instead, he walked off with Kirlia toward another part of the ship.

Before boarding, Ethan had already recalled his other Pokémon into their Poké Balls, leaving only Kirlia by his side, since he expected the cruise to be crowded and noisy.

As he wandered past the various stalls, Ethan suddenly noticed a young man dressed like a waiter talking with an older man who was selling Magikarp.

The young man had handsome features and shoulder-length blue-gray hair parted down the middle, covering his ears. His overall temperament gave off the impression of a kindhearted but gullible person.

"Let me tell you a secret," the Magikarp salesman whispered slyly, wearing a white headband and an expression full of cunning. "This Magikarp can produce gold!"

"Gold?" the young man repeated blankly.

"That's right, that's right!" the salesman covered his mouth dramatically as he spoke. "A single Magikarp can lay one thousand eggs. Then those one thousand Magikarp each lay another thousand eggs. One thousand times one thousand is one million! And if those million lay eggs too, just think about it—that's an astronomical number!"

The blue-haired man seemed completely dizzy from the explanation.

"T-That sounds incredible…"

Seeing him begin to waver, the Magikarp salesman pressed on aggressively.

"This Magikarp only costs 10,000 dollars! If it lays eggs just three times, you'll earn hundreds of millions!"

"Only three times!?" the blue-haired man exclaimed in shock. "Wouldn't that make me a billionaire almost immediately!?"

"That's exactly why I said it produces gold!" the salesman grinned smugly, using the tone of someone offering an unbelievable bargain. "So what do you say? Since it's a special sale today, I'll include the breeding kit, care kit, and elite growth kit too. Altogether, only 30,000 dollars!"

This scene! This exact setup!

Wasn't this the legendary infamous scene with James from Team Rocket and the smooth-talking Magikarp salesman!?

Ethan could hardly believe his eyes. He had actually encountered this iconic moment in person!

Still, he really couldn't understand it. James came from a wealthy family, so how could he still be tempted by money so easily?

Seeing James pull out his wallet as if he were about to buy the Magikarp, Ethan coughed lightly and stepped forward.

"Boss, how about you sell this Magikarp to me for 500 dollars?"

"What did you say!?" The Magikarp salesman waved his hand impatiently. "Get lost! This is a gold-producing Magikarp! How could I possibly sell it for only 500 dollars!?"

"Produces gold?" Ethan rubbed his chin thoughtfully while looking at the Magikarp flopping around in the tank. "But it looks no different from the Magikarp found in rivers. Boss, are you sure you're not mistaken? This kind of common Pokémon can be found practically everywhere—in lakes, rivers, or the sea. All you need is a fishing rod to catch one. Besides me, who would actually buy it?"

James trembled slightly, as though suddenly waking up from a trance.

"Common Magikarp? Nobody would buy it?"

Seeing James begin to doubt himself, the salesman hurriedly explained.

"H-Hey now, don't listen to his nonsense! My Magikarp is completely different from all the others! Gold! It produces gold!"

"Oh really?" Ethan shrugged. "Then why aren't you already a millionaire yourself? Why are you still standing here selling Magikarp?"

That completely snapped James back to reality.

"You scoundrel! You were trying to scam me!"

He angrily kicked the fish tank before nodding gratefully to Ethan and storming off furiously.

Seeing this, Ethan clasped his hands behind his back and turned to leave as well, but the Magikarp salesman quickly blocked his path.

"Hey! We have no grudge against each other, so why did you ruin my business!?"

Ethan tilted his head slightly and replied calmly,

"Ah, sorry about that. But I have a very good impression of that guy, so I really couldn't bear to watch him get scammed. You should just wait for your next destined customer."

After speaking, Ethan tried to walk around him, but the salesman blocked him once again.

"You're not getting away that easily! Since you ruined my sale, then you buy the Magikarp yourself!"

Perhaps afraid Ethan might leave, the salesman reached out to grab Ethan's sleeve.

But Kirlia immediately stepped forward. Light slowly gathered in its eyes as it stared coldly at the salesman.

Its posture made it clear—if the man's hand moved even one inch closer to Ethan, Kirlia would unleash a Psychic attack instantly.

The salesman jumped in fright and quickly pulled his arm back.

Ignoring him, Ethan glanced once more at the Magikarp.

Even without James kicking it later, judging by experience, this Magikarp was probably already close to evolving. If he bought it, he would likely obtain a Gyarados soon enough, which actually made it a pretty worthwhile deal.

"You know what? Fine. Sell it to me for 500 dollars."

Ethan wasn't naming that price randomly.

In many places, Magikarp really did sell for about that much, which perfectly matched the normal market value.

"500 dollars?" The salesman frowned deeply in dissatisfaction. "I could've sold it for 30,000 just a moment ago!"

But seeing Ethan remain completely unmoved, the two of them stared at each other for quite a while. Eventually, the salesman rolled his eyes around helplessly before sighing heavily.

"Fine, fine. 500 it is. I'll just take the loss and sell it to you."

( End of Chapter )
