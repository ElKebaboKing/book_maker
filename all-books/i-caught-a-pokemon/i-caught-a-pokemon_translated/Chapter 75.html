"Forget it." Haunter clearly looked uninterested and shook its head in refusal. "It's too weak. It's completely no match for me."

"Too weak!? You actually dare say my Crobat is weak!?"

Janine was instantly provoked by those words and shouted angrily,

"You talking Haunter over there! Sure, you're unique, but don't underestimate my Pokémon! Do you dare come up here and battle it!?"

Haunter remained completely unmoved. It even made a silly face at Janine, sticking out its tongue.

"Bleh! I'm not going up there. What are you gonna do about it!? Blehh!"

Seeing Janine nearly explode with anger, Ethan hurriedly stepped in to smooth things over.

"Ah, sorry about that. My Haunter's always like this, Miss Janine. Please don't take it personally."

Knowing Haunter had no intention of battling, Ethan glanced at Crobat's pig-like face for a moment before tossing out a Poké Ball.

"Jolteon, this battle's yours!"

From Ethan's memory, Crobat possessed incredible speed, and among all his Pokémon, only Jolteon could currently keep up with it.

Moreover, Electric-type attacks naturally counter Flying-types, making Jolteon an excellent choice for this battle.

On the opposite side, Janine frowned tightly when she saw Jolteon appear. She had honestly thought Ethan would send out that annoying Haunter!

"Jolteon, huh? Fine. No matter who the opponent is, my Crobat will teach anyone who dares underestimate us a lesson!"

Once both Pokémon were ready, Janine launched the first attack immediately.

"Crobat, use Air Slash!"

With a powerful flap of one pair of wings, several crescent-shaped blades of compressed air sliced downward toward Jolteon.

"Jolteon, dodge it!"

At Ethan's command, Jolteon used its nimble footwork to perfectly evade the attack before shaking its fur and launching a counterattack.

A barrage of Pin Missiles shot toward Crobat like steel needles!

Crobat beat its wings and skillfully avoided the sweeping barrage as well.

After this brief exchange, both Trainers realized something at the same time: with both Pokémon possessing such incredible speed, ranged attacks alone would never reliably land.

Understanding this, Janine immediately changed tactics.

"Crobat, use Cross Poison!!"

Crobat circled twice in the air before diving downward. Its clawed front wings glowed with an eerie green light as it slashed toward Jolteon.

Facing the aggressive attack, Ethan chose not to retreat.

"Jolteon, shock it!"

By then, Crobat had already closed the distance. Electricity surged across Jolteon's body as it unleashed a burst of electricity directly into Crobat.

Despite the pain, Crobat endured the attack and viciously slashed Jolteon with its poisonous claws!

Although the exchange looked evenly matched at first glance, Electric-type attacks were super effective against Flying-types, meaning Crobat had taken far more damage!

Once Crobat flew back into the air, Janine continued her assault.

"Crobat! Use Poison Fang!"

Ethan swung his hand forward and responded immediately.

"Jolteon, don't lose to it! Use Thunder Fang!"

One Pokémon's mouth dripped with poison while the other crackled with electricity. The moment they both opened their jaws and collided, the battle devolved into pure instinct!

Biting. Tearing. More biting!

As Crobat's massive jaws clamped down and Jolteon growled fiercely, the two Pokémon clawed and grappled with one another, sinking their fangs repeatedly into each other's bodies!

In only a few breaths, Crobat's body became covered in wounds, while Jolteon also suffered several injuries.

But then something unexpected happened—

Both Pokémon triggered status conditions simultaneously!

First was Jolteon. Poison spread rapidly through its body, instantly coursing everywhere until even its face turned faintly green!

Then came Crobat. Electricity surged through its body, leaving it paralyzed and completely unable to move!

Seeing the opportunity, Ethan immediately shouted,

"Jolteon, finish it with one last attack! Use Discharge!!"

Jolteon's needle-like fur stood straight up as massive electrical energy gathered within its body before exploding outward in a blinding burst of electricity!

Bzzzzzt!!

This time, Crobat took the full force of the attack and collapsed instantly, completely losing the ability to battle!

"I lost…"

Janine sighed dejectedly and recalled Crobat into its Poké Ball.

Ethan nodded to her before taking out an antidote and kneeling beside Jolteon to feed it the medicine.

"You did great."

He gently stroked Jolteon's forehead. Once its complexion improved and the poison seemed to fade away, he also returned it to its Poké Ball.

"Well then, just like we agreed."

Janine stepped aside and cleared the path. "Since you defeated me, you may pass through this door and continue onward."

Without waiting for Ethan to reply, she suddenly vanished through the doorway in a swift ninja-like movement.

"Seriously… why does challenging this Gym have to be so troublesome?"

Even while complaining, Ethan still brought Haunter and Kirlia with him and passed through the wooden door.

Behind it was a staircase. When he climbed up to the second floor, he discovered another long hallway stretching ahead of him.

Sunlight streamed in through small side windows, brightly illuminating the corridor.

"Even though it looks safe," Haunter floated around in a circle and clicked its tongue, "I'm guessing there are still traps hidden here somewhere. You'd better stay careful."

Ethan walked forward for more than ten steps, yet nothing happened. But the calmer things remained, the more cautious he became.

Sure enough, after walking several more meters, his arm suddenly bumped into what felt like an invisible wall of air!

The transparent barrier completely blocked the path ahead. Though invisible, the sunlight reflected faint white glimmers from its surface.

Ethan carefully felt along the wall with his hand. The surface was icy cold and smooth, and when he knocked lightly against it, a dull thud-thud echoed back.

It was clearly a thick transparent glass wall!

Haunter floated through the wall repeatedly. For a ghost with no physical body, the barrier may as well not have existed.

"So?" Haunter asked. "Want me to smash it?"

Ethan shook his head. Suddenly thinking of something, he turned and retraced his steps.

But when he reached the staircase entrance, he discovered that another invisible barrier had somehow appeared there as well!

Now both the front and rear exits were sealed shut, trapping him completely inside the hallway!

Yet instead of panicking, Ethan suddenly smiled.

He vaguely remembered that back when he watched the anime, Ash had encountered this exact trap while challenging the Fuchsia Gym.

And as for what came next after the trap…

Ethan slowly walked over to the wooden wall beside the corridor windows, raised his right leg, and kicked it forward with all his strength!

(End of Chapter)
