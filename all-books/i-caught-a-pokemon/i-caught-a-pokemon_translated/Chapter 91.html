After taking that hit, Magmar's already fierce-looking face became even more twisted with anger.

Although the attack had damaged Magmar, Wartortle had also been burned by the flames and was forced to extend its head out of its shell.

"There's an opening!" Blaine's eyes lit up. "Magmar, use Fire Punch!"

Magmar's flaming fists rained down on Wartortle like a storm. As if seeking revenge for the earlier strike to its face, Magmar put even more power than usual into every punch.

"Wartortle! Bubble Beam!"

A stream of bubbles burst from Wartortle's mouth, rapidly expanding as they collided with the incoming Fire Punches.

Bang! Boom! Bang! Boom!

Both Pokémon suffered damage from the exchange. When they finally retreated to separate stone pillars, both were breathing heavily.

However, because it continued suffering from its burn, Wartortle appeared to be in far greater pain.

"Magmar, use Fire Blast!"

A blazing ball of fire erupted from Magmar's mouth. It grew larger and larger until it formed a gigantic fiery character-shaped blast and surged toward Wartortle.

With nowhere to dodge, Ethan could only meet fire with force.

Unfortunately, Wartortle had not yet learned any stronger Water-type techniques. It could only fire another Water Gun to clash against Fire Blast!

Sssss—

Water and fire collided, instantly creating clouds of steam.

Despite having the type advantage, Water Gun lacked the power to extinguish Fire Blast. Instead, the flames overwhelmed the water attack and continued sweeping toward Wartortle!

When the flames finally subsided, Wartortle could no longer endure any further and collapsed atop the stone pillar.

After finally winning a round, Blaine let out a long sigh of relief.

"Looks like I've managed to even the score."

Ethan nodded in praise.

"Your Magmar is exceptionally well trained. My Wartortle really wasn't a match for it."

Even though he had lost a Pokémon, Ethan remained completely unfazed.

After all, Magmar had taken several attacks and had already lost at least half its stamina.

But now the question was—which Pokémon should he send out next?

His eyes swept across the volcano crater.

The battlefield offered very little room to maneuver. It was unsuitable for conventional combat and especially unfavorable for a massive Pokémon like Gyarados.

After some thought, Ethan grabbed the first Poké Ball at his waist.

"The rest is up to you, Kirlia!"

A flash of blue light appeared, and Kirlia entered the battlefield.

Once both Pokémon were ready, the battle began.

What happened next, however, surprised everyone present.

The battlefield, which should have restricted movement, actually became a disadvantage for Magmar thanks to Kirlia's Teleport.

Using its completely unpredictable movement patterns, Kirlia blinked from pillar to pillar, effortlessly avoiding Magmar's fire attacks.

Magmar never found an opportunity to strike.

In the end, Kirlia used Psychic attacks to defeat Magmar quickly and decisively.

"Amazing," Blaine said helplessly as he recalled Magmar and walked over to Ethan. "Especially that final battle. I expected a hard-fought match between evenly matched opponents, but my Magmar was reduced to nothing more than a punching bag."

Truthfully, even Ethan hadn't expected such an easy victory.

"Mr. Blaine, you can't blame me for that. Kirlia is a Psychic-type. In terrain like this, it's extremely difficult to even touch it."

Shaking his head, Blaine pulled a badge shaped like a flame from his pocket and tossed it toward Ethan.

"I'm getting old. Maybe I'm not what I used to be. Take it—this is proof of your victory."

Ethan caught the badge firmly in his hand.

Unlike Ethan's excitement, however, Ash stared at the badge with a deeply disappointed expression.

His hand unconsciously tightened around Charizard's Poké Ball.

"If only my Charizard would listen to me like that..."

Before they knew it, evening had arrived.

At Blaine's invitation, Ethan and the others remained at the inn as guests.

During dinner, Ash remained unusually quiet and depressed. Without saying a word, he left the room early.

"Huh?" Blaine looked at his departing figure curiously. "Where's he going?"

"Don't worry about him," Misty said casually, clearly unsurprised. "He's probably off doing some kind of special training again. Every time he loses a battle, he gets depressed for a while, but he always bounces back."

"Exactly," Ethan agreed. "In fact, I wouldn't be surprised if he challenges you again first thing tomorrow morning. And when he does, his mindset will be completely different from today."

"Oh?" Blaine took a sip of sake with interest. "In that case, I'm looking forward to it."

Then he turned to Ethan.

"By the way, my hot springs are excellent for treating burns. Instead of going to a Pokémon Center later, why don't you bring Wartortle and let it soak in the springs?"

After dinner, Ethan followed Blaine's suggestion and brought Wartortle back to the hot spring.

This time, however, he was alone.

Brock had gone out searching for Ash, worried about him, while Misty had disappeared somewhere shortly after dinner.

"Since this is such a rare opportunity, everyone should come enjoy the hot spring too."

Ethan opened several Poké Balls and released all his Pokémon except for the two legendary birds.

In an instant, the hot spring became completely crowded.

Especially after Gyarados settled into the water, the water level rose noticeably, leaving only a small space beside Ethan.

Naturally, each Pokémon reacted differently to the hot spring.

Kirlia soaked peacefully in the water like someone enjoying a wellness retreat, with only its head sticking out.

Dratini curled up in a corner like a dead fish, exposing its soft white belly.

Ivysaur paddled leisurely through the water.

Gyarados was simply too large to move.

Jolteon seemed afraid of getting its fur wet and refused to enter the water at all.

As for Charmeleon, which was naturally afraid of water, it hugged its tail protectively and kept its distance from the spring, only daring to dip its toes into the water.

Only Haunter seemed truly delighted, splashing hot spring water onto everyone else and thoroughly enjoying itself.

Scooping up a wooden ladle full of hot spring water, Ethan carefully poured it over Wartortle's shell.

"You worked really hard in that battle..."

At that moment, gentle footsteps approached from outside the spring.

Turning his head, Ethan saw Misty slowly walking over, wrapped in a bath towel.

Because Togepi had accidentally activated the hidden mechanism earlier that day and broken the partition separating the baths, it had not yet been repaired. What had once been separate baths had effectively become a mixed bath.

Since Ethan's Pokémon occupied most of the available space, Misty blushed slightly at the sight but acted as though nothing was wrong.

She stepped into the water and sat down beside him.

Unfortunately, at the exact moment she sat down, Ethan's hand accidentally brushed against her smooth white thigh.

Soft. Warm. Silky.

"Uh..."

For a moment, Ethan froze.

And suddenly, the atmosphere became rather awkward.

(End of Chapter)
