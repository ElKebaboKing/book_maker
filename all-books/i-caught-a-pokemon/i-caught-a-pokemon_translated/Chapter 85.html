Ash's momentum instantly weakened.

Honestly, throughout all of his adventures, the only person who could mercilessly roast him like this was Misty—the slightly spoiled and headstrong girl who proudly called herself the most beautiful maiden in the universe.

"Hmph! Don't underestimate me!" Ash protested unwillingly. "Fine then! Tomorrow I'll show all of you how I'm going to earn the Volcano Badge with my own strength!"

"Really?" Misty grinned mischievously after seeing how easily he took the bait. "Then we'll be looking forward to it!"

Late at night, Ethan returned to the room, but no matter how much he tossed and turned, he simply couldn't fall asleep.

On the other two beds, Ash and Brock were sleeping soundly, their snores rising and falling like thunder. It sounded as though someone was sawing wood right beside Ethan's ears with a giant steel saw!

Unable to sleep, Ethan thought for a long time before finally taking out his phone. Logging into his messaging app, he opened the unread message he had been avoiding.

Pink Dream: Big Brother Ethan, has everything been going smoothly?

The familiar form of address remained unchanged.

Although the message was short and calm, after spending several months together, Ethan knew her personality all too well. And naturally, he understood that regarding that matter, she clearly still hadn't let it go.

Looking at her profile picture, Ethan could still remember how the seemingly strong girl had cried her eyes out after he rejected her.

Every time he remembered it, it felt as though an icy knife pierced straight into his heart.

Letting out a soft sigh, Ethan finally began typing.

Everything Is Bubbles: Things have been alright. I've caught a few more Pokémon, and Ralts has even evolved into Kirlia.

Perhaps feeling that his tone was too stiff, he typed another message.

Everything Is Bubbles: Sorry for not contacting you for so long. How have you been lately?

After sending the messages, Ethan stared blankly at the chat window.

Their profile pictures were actually cropped from the same photo. Unlike the girl's picture, however, Ethan's profile focused on himself. In the photo, his handsome face wore a faint smile, while beside him, a pair of fair delicate arms tightly hugged his arm.

Originally, Ethan thought it was already too late and that she had gone to sleep. Just as he was about to put away his phone, only a few seconds later, a reply suddenly arrived.

Adjusting his position on the bed, Ethan opened the message.

Pink Dream: Same as always. Mom still makes me train with Rhyhorn every single day. [Crying Loudly JPG] It's really exhausting.

Although she didn't say it directly, Ethan could still sense the girl's excitement through her words.

For a moment, however, he didn't know how to reply. So instead, he changed the subject and casually chatted with her about recent events in his life. Only after more than ten minutes, when drowsiness finally overtook him, did he end the conversation himself.

The next morning, when the sun was already high in the sky, Ethan and the others finally left the Pokémon Center and truly experienced the bustling resort town famous for its hot springs.

Tourists from across the mainland filled the streets. Nearly every shop had people lingering outside.

Most of the goods being sold were related to hot springs—traditional yukata robes, hot spring eggs, and similar souvenirs.

Following the directions on their phones, they walked through Cinnabar Town and eventually reached the street at the foot of the volcano where the Gym should have been located.

However, when they arrived, they discovered that what should have been the Gym was actually… a hot spring inn!

"Huh?" Misty asked curiously. "A hot spring inn? Did the Gym move somewhere else?"

Hearing her voice, an old man wearing red clothes stepped out from the inn. He had a long mustache beneath his nose, sunglasses on his face, and a hairstyle resembling a member of The Beatles.

Before anyone could speak, the old man suddenly asked:

"Your head is cold, but your body is warm. What is it?"

"Huh? A riddle?" Ash blinked before eagerly answering, "It's someone sticking their head into a refrigerator while wrapping themselves in an electric blanket for a nap!"

The bizarre logic made the old man frown immediately.

"Hey, hey! What kind of answer is that!? If you don't know, don't just say random things!"

Misty glanced behind the old man confidently before replying:

"I think the answer is an outdoor hot spring."

"Correct, correct!" the old man said happily. "What a clever little girl! Alright then, here's the next riddle. Between cake, eggs, and rice—which one do dogs like the most?"

Brock thought for a moment. Since it was a riddle, the answer obviously couldn't be too simple.

"Rice?" he guessed cautiously.

"Nope!" Misty shook her head. "I think it's cake!"

"Come on," Ash said while shrugging. "Only you like cake that much, Misty. Sweet things make you fat—dogs wouldn't like them like you do!"

Ignoring Ash getting beaten up by Misty, Ethan had already figured out the old man's identity. Smiling slightly, he answered:

"I think the answer is eggs."

"Oh?" The old man looked at him with interest. "And why is that?"

"Because people always say they're 'picking bones out of eggs.'"

"Hahahahaha! Correct!" the old man burst into laughter, clearly pleased with himself. "Well? Aren't my riddles interesting?"

Ash, Misty, and Brock all sighed helplessly. Honestly, none of them understood what was so funny about it.

Only Ethan suddenly became interested.

"It's only polite to return the favor. Since that's the case, let me ask a riddle too."

"Well now, that's rare!" The old man's eyes lit up. "Usually I'm the one testing others. No one has ever challenged me before! Go ahead then!"

Clearing his throat, Ethan asked:

"If two people fall into a pit at the same time, and the dead person is called 'dead man,' then what is the living person called?"

"…Called what?"

The old man, Misty, and the others guessed many answers, but Ethan rejected every single one.

Finally, Misty gave up and asked curiously:

"So what's the answer?"

"Of course it's 'Help!'"

After saying that, Ethan burst out laughing by himself. But as he laughed, he noticed the four people staring at him speechlessly, causing his laughter to awkwardly fade away.

"Huh?" Ethan asked in disbelief. "Don't tell me you guys didn't get the joke?"

"The joke?"

Ash, Misty, and Brock all looked confused. They genuinely didn't understand how it was any different from the old man's riddles.

Was it really that funny?

Even the old man sighed.

"Kid, I thought my riddles were already boring enough. I never expected your jokes to be even more boring than mine."

"Bo… boring!?!?"

Ethan felt as though he had just taken ten thousand points of emotional damage.

"Fine! Then I'll do another one! Listen carefully—what's something people know is fake, yet they're still willing to spend money buying it?"

Before anyone could answer, Ethan pointed directly at the old man's hair and laughed loudly:

"A wig, obviously! Hahahaha—"

Misty: …

Ash: …

Brock: …

"Sigh…" The old man walked over and patted Ethan on the shoulder.

"Young man, I truly admire your cold humor. Starting today, I'm willing to recognize you as my greatest rival in the world of riddles."

"Huh!?" Ethan froze before angrily slapping away the old man's hand. "Who the heck said I wanted to compete with you in cold jokes!?"
