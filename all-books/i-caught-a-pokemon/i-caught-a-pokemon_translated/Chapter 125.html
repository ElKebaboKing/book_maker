Inside the Cerise Laboratory, Ethan handed three gifts he had prepared during his free time to the people standing before him.

"Professor Cerise, Ren, and Parker, thank you so much for taking care of me during this time. These are just small tokens of my appreciation. Please accept them."

Having known each other for quite a while, Professor Cerise and the others didn't bother with unnecessary formalities. Smiling, they gladly accepted Ethan's gifts.

Professor Cerise smiled warmly.

"Time really does fly. Before we knew it, you've already traveled across Kanto for so long, and now you're about to challenge the Indigo Conference."

"It really has gone by quickly," Ethan said with a nostalgic sigh. "I still remember my first visit to the laboratory—the feeling of being completely unfamiliar with everything and a little nervous."

"I remember it clearly too," Professor Cerise chuckled. "I also remember that easygoing and handsome young man who first walked through our doors."

He paused before continuing.

"But the Indigo Conference is Kanto's biggest annual tournament. Since you've made it this far, give it everything you've got. Only by fighting with all your strength can you leave without regrets."

"I feel the same way," Ethan nodded. "No matter how strong my opponents are, I'll treat every battle seriously and cautiously."

After a brief pause, he added,

"Professor, even if I don't get many chances to return here in the future, as a Special Research Fellow of the Cerise Laboratory, wherever I go, if I encounter any unusual Pokémon-related incidents, I'll be sure to report them to you."

Hearing those words filled Professor Cerise with satisfaction.

"Those reports are secondary. Your safety should always come first. Don't force yourself into dangerous situations. But no matter what happens in the future, you'll always be a member of the Cerise Laboratory. This place will always be your home, and you're welcome back anytime."

Then he smiled apologetically.

"Although this is such an important occasion, I'm afraid Ren, Parker, and I have been extremely busy with work lately. We probably won't be able to attend the Indigo Conference to watch your matches in person."

Ethan quickly waved his hand.

"It's alright. Your research contributes not only to humanity but also to Pokémon—and even to the world itself. Your work should always come first. Please don't sacrifice valuable time because of me."

Just then, Chloe walked in through the doorway.

She had changed into a sporty outfit and was carrying a fully packed medium-sized backpack.

Compared to her usual school uniform, she looked less reserved and much more energetic and youthful.

"Huh?" Ethan blinked in surprise. "You're dressed like that... are you going somewhere?"

Professor Cerise smiled.

"Although we can't accompany you to the Indigo Plateau, Chloe can go with you instead. Consider it our way of making up for not being able to attend ourselves."

"Chloe is coming too?" Ethan asked in surprise. "But what about school? Doesn't she still have classes?"

Chloe covered her mouth with a soft smile.

"It's alright. My mother already helped me apply for leave."

Professor Cerise nodded.

"I think it's a good opportunity for her to get out more. Chloe has always been a little too quiet. I was worried she'd end up becoming nothing but a bookworm. To my surprise, this time she actually wanted to go herself and asked if she could watch your matches in person."

"So my wife and I discussed it, and we both agreed it was a good idea. Instead of spending every day buried in schoolwork, seeing more of the outside world will broaden her horizons, and that can only benefit her future studies."

Oh?

Seeing Chloe's slightly shy expression and the way she avoided his gaze, Ethan couldn't help feeling surprised.

She asked to come on her own?

The Chloe he knew in the past would never have willingly traveled so far from home.

Fortunately, Professor Cerise and his wife were incredibly open-minded parents. Otherwise, how could they possibly feel comfortable letting their daughter travel with an adult man?

Ethan spoke seriously.

"Professor, please don't worry. I promise I'll take good care of Chloe."

Looking at his daughter, fully prepared for the journey, Professor Cerise couldn't help feeling sentimental.

Any father would find it difficult to watch his daughter leave home.

Before they departed, he called Chloe aside and gave her a few more reminders.

Since he wasn't speaking quietly, Ethan unintentionally overheard parts of the conversation.

Most of it consisted of ordinary fatherly advice—remember to eat on time, don't overwork yourself, don't push yourself too hard, and similar concerns.

While the father and daughter were talking, Ren and Parker simultaneously extended their hands toward Ethan.

They exchanged glances before Ren spoke first.

"Although I don't know much about Pokémon battles, I sincerely wish you a smooth journey and great success."

Parker glanced discreetly at Professor Cerise before lowering his voice.

"Make sure you achieve a great result. Even if work keeps me busy, I'll secretly tune into the livestream and cheer for you."

Ethan shook hands with each of them.

"Thanks. You two should take care of yourselves too. Make sure you get enough rest and don't overwork yourselves."

After spending so much time together, he had grown attached to this place.

Saying goodbye truly wasn't easy.

Just as Professor Cerise and Chloe returned and everyone was exchanging their farewells, the sound of barking echoed through the laboratory.

Yamper suddenly came running over.

Perhaps sensing that Chloe was leaving on a long journey, Yamper circled around her feet, refusing to leave her side.

Professor Cerise crouched down and gently patted Yamper's head.

"That's right. Since you're setting out on a journey, how could you travel without a Pokémon by your side? Since it doesn't want to leave you, why not let Yamper travel with you?"

"Huh? Bring Yamper with me?"

Chloe hesitated before glancing at Ethan.

Seeing him nod gently, she thought for a moment before agreeing.

"Alright."

Hearing that it could come along, Yamper barked excitedly, its tail wagging so fast that it looked like a tiny electric fan.

Once everything was ready, Chloe slowly walked over to stand beside Ethan.

Professor Cerise waved at them.

"Chloe is in your hands now."

Ethan nodded firmly.

Then he and Chloe bowed respectfully toward Professor Cerise and the others.

"We're heading out now!"

After leaving the Cerise Laboratory, Ethan and Chloe departed Vermilion City and traveled north, arriving in Saffron City before nightfall.

After spending the night there, they continued west toward Celadon City.

It was Chloe's first time leaving home on a real journey.

Having grown up in a large city, everything she saw along the way filled her with excitement and curiosity.

Everything looked new and fascinating.

And with such a cute and beautiful girl traveling beside him, even Ethan found his mood becoming lighter.

Between their laughter and conversations, the road itself no longer felt dull.

It also made Ethan realize something.

Traveling with companions truly was far more enjoyable than traveling alone.
