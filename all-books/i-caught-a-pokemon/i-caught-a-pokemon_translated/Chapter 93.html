Ethan turned his head and saw a bush suddenly part. A Pokémon holding a leek strolled out with an exaggerated swagger.

Its duck-like appearance and the distinctive stalk in its hand made Ethan recognize it immediately.

A Farfetch'd.

The Farfetch'd marched proudly forward, seemingly humming an unknown tune, and casually walked right up to Ethan.

Hmm? Not afraid of people?

Ethan glanced around but saw no sign of any other humans nearby.

A wild Farfetch'd?

Seeing that Ethan remained where he was, the Farfetch'd paced back and forth in front of him, apparently trying to attract his attention.

However, no matter how hard it tried, it simply did not interest Ethan very much.

Just like Butterfree, Beedrill, Psyduck, and Persian, he liked the species well enough, but never felt any need to catch one.

So when it came to this Farfetch'd, he neither particularly liked nor disliked it.

As he watched, Ethan recalled a conversation he'd had with Misty and the others during their recent reunion. They had mentioned encountering a thief who used a Farfetch'd to trick and rob people during their travels.

Combined with this Farfetch'd's suspicious behavior, its identity was already obvious.

A thought flashed through Ethan's mind.

He decided to play along and see what kind of trick they were trying to pull.

"Oh! A Farfetch'd!"

Pretending to be excited, he pulled out a Poké Ball.

"Are you trying to get me to catch you?"

The Farfetch'd immediately assumed Ethan had fallen for the bait. It cried out a few times, suddenly accelerated, and darted into the nearby grass. After weaving past several large trees, it vanished from sight.

Feigning panic, Ethan hurried after it.

But when he rounded the trees, the Farfetch'd was nowhere to be seen.

"Sigh…"

He put on an exaggerated expression of disappointment.

"Aw, I lost it..."

The moment he finished speaking, the Farfetch'd popped its head out from the distance.

Its performance was so over-the-top that it practically had the words "I'm over here! Come catch me!" written across its face.

Ethan sneered inwardly and ignored it.

Instead, he returned to where he had been resting.

Sure enough, a ponytailed teenage boy with a crafty appearance was reaching for Ethan's backpack.

Ethan stared at him coldly.

"So after hiding all this time, you finally couldn't resist making your move?"

"What!?"

The boy jumped in shock.

He stared at Ethan in disbelief, his hand frozen in midair.

"Weren't you chasing the Farfetch'd!?"

"Of course that was a trick," Ethan replied calmly. "Otherwise, how would you have exposed yourself?"

Truthfully, Ethan was quite pleased with his impromptu performance.

If he ever gave up being a Pokémon Trainer and returned to Kalos to work again, maybe he could even move from behind the scenes onto the screen and land a minor acting role.

At that moment, hurried footsteps sounded behind him.

The Farfetch'd had realized something was wrong and rushed back, standing protectively in front of the boy.

Ethan's gaze swept over the pair.

"I heard from Misty that she once encountered a thief who used a Farfetch'd to scam and rob people. After battling you, she thought you'd turned over a new leaf."

He shook his head.

"Who would have thought you'd go right back to your old ways? Looks like Officer Jenny made a mistake by not arresting you back then."

"Misty?"

The boy's face changed instantly.

Of course he remembered Misty, Ash, and Brock.

He also remembered all the grand promises he had made about turning his life around after being caught.

Now that his scheme had been exposed, he immediately grabbed the backpack and turned to run.

But to his shock, the backpack suddenly seemed to grow wings.

It flew right out of his hands.

The boy stared in disbelief as the backpack floated back toward Ethan and landed neatly in his hands.

"H-How is that possible!?"

Ethan rolled his eyes.

"You didn't think I'd leave my backpack here completely unprotected, did you?"

As he spoke, Gengar slowly materialized beside him.

Grinning broadly, it gave the boy a terrifying smile.

"Hello there, Mr. Thief."

"Damn it!"

The boy cursed and bolted.

"He's trying to escape," Gengar chuckled.

"I know."

Ethan calmly took out a Poké Ball.

"Kirlia, I'm counting on you."

Kirlia appeared in a flash of light.

With a quick use of Teleport, it instantly appeared in front of the boy and blocked his escape route.

"Ah, I'm really sorry!"

The boy turned around and clasped his hands together.

"I was wrong! Really wrong! Please let me go!"

"If apologies solved everything, we wouldn't need the police," Ethan replied.

"If it had been someone else, they would've fallen for your trick and lost everything. At that point, no amount of apologies would matter."

He shook his head.

"I'm not exactly a saint, but I won't allow someone to take advantage of me. No matter how smoothly you talk, you're going to face the consequences this time."

"I had no choice!"

The boy desperately tried to play the sympathy card.

"My Farfetch'd is too weak. This is the only way we can survive. We steal because we have no other option!"

"Stop making excuses."

Ethan dismantled the lie immediately.

"In this day and age, anyone willing to work can make a living. Maybe I'm not a particularly good person, but I won't tolerate being targeted."

His expression hardened.

"And this time, no matter what you say, you're going to pay for what you tried to do."

Seeing that Ethan would not budge, the boy finally dropped the act.

Ever since discovering the true capabilities of his Pokémon, he had become increasingly confident in his thefts.

"You want to catch me?"

His voice turned sharp.

"If you're going to push me this hard, then let's see whether you actually have the ability!"

"So you want to settle this with Pokémon?"

Ethan nodded.

"That works for me. The only question is: do you want to battle under normal rules, or under my rules?"

"My rules?"

The boy blinked.

"What's the difference?"

Ethan shrugged.

"It's still a fight either way. Not much difference at all."

"Hmph!"

The boy snorted impatiently.

"Enough with the nonsense! Bring it on!"

He pointed dramatically at Ethan.

"I'll show you just how strong my Farfetch'd really is!"

( End of Chapter )
