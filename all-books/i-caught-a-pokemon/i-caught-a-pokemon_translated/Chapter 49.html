Ethan's Pokémon launched attack after attack against Gyarados, and when Jolteon's final burst of electricity struck it with quadruple effectiveness, Gyarados could no longer endure the damage and collapsed stiffly onto the ground.

"Now you should finally calm down."

According to legend, the reason Gyarados was so violent was because its brain structure underwent a complete reorganization during evolution, causing its temperament to drastically change.

Only after becoming familiar with or acknowledging its trainer would it truly settle down.

Ethan recalled hearing once that stroking the horn on a Gyarados's head could make it gentler, so he slowly walked forward and gently rubbed its hard horn with his right hand.

Sure enough, the resistance in Gyarados's eyes gradually faded and was slowly replaced by acceptance.

After resting briefly and recovering some stamina, Ethan reorganized his backpack and once again continued toward Celadon City.

However, what he did not know was that inside a secret room beneath the Viridian Gym, a conspiracy targeting him had already begun.

Inside the dimly lit room, Giovanni sat calmly on a sofa. Beside his feet lay a large cat-like Pokémon with a red gem embedded in its forehead.

As knocking sounded at the door, a woman with a bob haircut and glasses entered the room.

Giovanni glanced at her indifferently.

"So, Matori, have you finished the investigation?"

Matori handed him a file, and attached to the upper-right corner was a blurry photograph of Ethan!

"It's all been investigated clearly," she replied efficiently, just as befitted a capable and meticulous secretary. "This person's name is Ethan Vale, age twenty. Half a month ago, he arrived in Kanto from the Kalos region and has been traveling around challenging various Gyms ever since. According to Lambda's testimony, he was involved with the International Police during the S.S. Anne incident, and now, together with the Silph Company incident, he has interfered with our operations twice already."

Giovanni flipped through the documents. Inside was general information regarding all of Ethan's Pokémon, including Zapdos and Haunter.

"Where is he now?" Giovanni asked.

Matori adjusted her glasses.

"Based on his route, after leaving Saffron City, his next destination should be Celadon Gym in Celadon City."

"Celadon City?" Giovanni frowned slightly. "Isn't that International Police agent also investigating our base there?"

Matori nodded.

"That's correct. He's been lingering around Celadon City for several days already. Although we successfully shook him off, it seems he still hasn't given up searching."

"We can't let them meet again." Giovanni's tone was as casual as crushing an ant. "Send someone to eliminate that trainer. Which of our operatives are currently near Celadon City?"

Matori thought for a moment.

"Other than the personnel guarding the base, all of the executives have already been dispatched to the Johto region, while the remaining members are scattered across other cities. At the moment, only Jessie and James are still wandering around near Celadon City."

"Then send them." Giovanni made his decision immediately. However, after noticing Matori's hesitation, he narrowed his eyes slightly. "What? Is there a problem?"

"There is…" Matori carefully chose her words. "With Jessie and James's abilities, they're not exactly reassuring. Why not deploy my elite squad—the Matori Matrix—instead?"

"It doesn't matter." Giovanni slowly lit a cigar. "Even if they fail to eliminate him, delaying him for a while is enough. During that time, your squad can first deal with the rat hiding in Celadon City."

Celadon—the color of dreamlike rainbows.

As the second-largest city in Kanto after Saffron City, Celadon was most famous for its internationally renowned university, its massive shopping malls found throughout the city, and the famous Celadon Game Corner.

As Ethan walked along the streets leading toward the Gym, he suddenly caught the scent of a rich fragrance.

The aroma smelled like a blend of countless perfumes mixed together, drifting gently through the breeze and filling the entire street.

Following the scent, Ethan looked ahead and spotted a large perfume shop nestled between several towering buildings.

An idea crossed his mind, and he walked toward it.

"Welcome."

Inside the store, several fashionably dressed female clerks were chatting behind the counter. When they saw Ethan enter, they all smiled teasingly.

"Are you here to buy perfume?"

Ethan nodded, but as he looked at the dazzling variety of perfumes on display, he immediately felt overwhelmed and had no idea where to begin.

One of the girls stepped closer and looked him up and down with a grin.

"Is it for your girlfriend?"

"No, I don't have a girlfriend. I just want to buy a few bottles as gifts for some friends."

"Friends?" Perhaps because they joked with one another so often, the clerks clearly did not believe him. They exchanged mischievous glances and laughed.

"Probably not just friends, right? Are you secretly dating several girls at the same time?"

"Uh…"

Before Ethan could respond, a gentle yet slightly authoritative voice suddenly came from behind him.

"You girls shouldn't joke with customers like that."

Hearing the voice, the female clerks immediately became more disciplined.

Ethan turned around and saw a beautiful young woman walking slowly toward him. She wore a kimono, had a red ribbon tied in her hair, and shoulder-length dark hair framing her elegant face.

"Manager, why did you come over?"

The young woman replied,

"Another shipment of perfume just arrived at the warehouse. Hurry and organize it. I'll handle things here."

After speaking, she walked up to Ethan and bowed lightly.

"I apologize. They're used to teasing each other and sometimes don't know where to draw the line. Please don't mind them."

"Not at all."

Ethan waved his hand dismissively, not bothered in the slightest.

At that moment, however, he had already recognized her identity.

She was none other than the Celadon Gym Leader—Erika!

Walking over to one of the shelves, Erika asked,

"So then, what kind of perfume are you looking for?"

After hesitating briefly, Ethan described Chloe, Misty, and Sabrina's personalities and temperaments to Erika. Then, remembering how much Yoshino had helped him, he also asked Erika to recommend a perfume suitable for her as well.

"I see." Erika nodded thoughtfully before walking toward another section. "Actually, all of the perfumes in our shop are quite good, but based on your descriptions, I would recommend these."

As she spoke, she pointed toward several bottles displayed prominently at the center of the shelf.

"These are our signature floral perfumes. The two on the left are among our most popular—one suits lively girls better, while the other is more suitable for girls with calmer personalities. As for the two on the right, their scents are lighter but longer-lasting, which matches your request very well."

(End of Chapter)
