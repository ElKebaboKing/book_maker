After spending a night in Stone Town and saying goodbye to the warm and hospitable Rainer family, Ethan set off once again.

The rest of the journey passed without incident, and he soon arrived in Saffron City.

Inside the Saffron Gym, Ethan met Sabrina's father for the second time. As before, the middle-aged man wore a baseball cap and sported a thick beard.

However, compared to his previous Gym Challenge, returning here now gave Ethan an entirely different feeling.

"You're here to see Sabrina?"

After learning Ethan's purpose, Sabrina's father looked at him with obvious suspicion and caution.

Before Ethan could answer, the man continued,

"So, was Sabrina helping you fight Team Rocket some time ago?"

The tone was stiff and cold, carrying more than a hint of scrutiny.

For some reason, Ethan felt immense pressure under the man's gaze.

Keeping a straight face, he replied,

"She wasn't helping me. She was assisting the International Police. It had nothing to do with me."

Seeing that the older man still wore a stern expression, Ethan quickly added,

"Actually, Sabrina and I have only met twice. We're not that close. I only came this time because I need to ask her for a favor..."

"Oh..."

Sabrina's father drew out the word thoughtfully.

Whether he truly believed Ethan or not was hard to tell, but his attitude noticeably softened.

Perhaps hearing Ethan say they "weren't that close" eased his mind.

He sighed.

"Those International Police people are always ordering others around. And the Pokémon League isn't any better. How could they send a young girl like Sabrina on such a dangerous mission?"

"Exactly... Uncle, you're absolutely right."

Although he agreed outwardly, Ethan secretly smiled to himself.

The man's overflowing fatherly love was certainly touching.

But while Sabrina was indeed a young woman, she was anything but an ordinary person.

With her psychic powers alone, she could toy with almost any opponent.

Anyone foolish enough to underestimate her would pay dearly for it.

The Saffron Gym hadn't changed much since Ethan's last visit.

Perhaps it was simply the lighting, but the atmosphere no longer felt as oppressive.

Passing the first room, he once again saw the group of people studying psychic powers.

After all this time, he wondered if any of them had actually improved.

After walking for quite a while, the man led Ethan back to the very battlefield where he had once challenged the Gym.

Sabrina sat calmly in her chair, quietly watching him.

"I foresaw your arrival."

It was the exact same sentence she had spoken the first time they met.

To Ethan's surprise, she had repeated it word for word.

Even so, after everything they had experienced together, her state of mind had clearly changed.

A gentle smile rested on her face now, and compared to her former coldness, there was unmistakable warmth in her expression.

"Sabrina, this time I came—"

Before he could finish, Sabrina gently raised her hand to interrupt him.

She then turned toward her father.

"What is it?" the man asked, raising an eyebrow.

Sabrina said nothing.

Silence filled the room.

Yet as her father, he understood her meaning almost immediately.

"You want me to leave!?"

His eyes widened in disbelief.

He then turned and shot Ethan a fierce glare.

"Kid! You chase me away the moment you arrive, and this is what you meant by saying you weren't close to Sabrina!?"

Ethan could only laugh awkwardly.

"Uh... well... um... haha..."

Although the man looked thoroughly displeased, he didn't dare disobey Sabrina under her increasingly intense gaze.

With a dissatisfied snort, he reluctantly left the battle hall.

Once his footsteps faded away, Sabrina moved a finger slightly.

The doors closed automatically.

With the outside world shut off, silence returned to the room.

After studying Ethan for a moment, Sabrina smiled softly.

"Now it's fine. You can continue."

She really has grown up...

Although Ethan felt a little guilty toward Sabrina's father, he had to admit that without the older man acting as a third wheel, he felt much more relaxed.

Walking over to Sabrina while casually looking around the room, he said,

"I actually came to see you... and to pick up the Silph Scope."

"Did you really come just to see me?"

Sabrina asked teasingly.

Her face remained as calm as ever, but hidden beneath her long black hair, her ears had quietly turned crimson.

"Oh, come on..."

Ethan pressed a hand to his forehead helplessly.

"I even brought you a gift, and you're still doubting me? That's really hurtful."

"A gift?"

Ethan opened his backpack and took out a bracelet made from many small, polished red gemstones.

Under the lights, the tiny stones shimmered with a soft glow, making the bracelet look exceptionally beautiful.

"What is it?"

Ethan smiled.

"I bought it as a souvenir on Cinnabar Island. It's carved from agate. They say the stones absorbed minerals after soaking in the hot springs for years. Supposedly, women who wear it feel more refreshed, and their skin gradually becomes healthier."

No woman disliked becoming more beautiful—not even Sabrina.

She accepted the bracelet and examined it carefully.

"Why are you giving me this?"

Ethan shrugged.

"Because I wanted to. That's why I bought it. If I had to give a reason... let's just say it's a thank-you gift."

"Didn't you already thank me before? With that bottle of perfume?"

"One thank-you isn't enough."

Ethan smiled.

"Besides, perfume gets used up. But a bracelet like this can stay with you forever. If it's well cared for, it could even become a family heirloom."

Sabrina genuinely liked the gift.

She slipped the bracelet onto her wrist.

Against her fair skin, the crimson gemstones looked especially elegant.

Although she didn't say it aloud, receiving a gift from Ethan made her genuinely happy.

"Very well. I'll accept it."

She paused before continuing.

"But I should tell you—the Silph Scope isn't with me anymore."

"Huh?"

Ethan blinked in surprise.

"When we spoke on the phone, didn't you say you were keeping it for me? So why..."

Sabrina smiled gently.

"Installing the Silph Scope isn't as simple as you might think. It has to fit perfectly, look natural, and then be properly calibrated. Mr. Silph said that once you arrived, you'd need to visit Silph Co. in person so their researchers could install it for you."

"I see!"

Ethan nodded in understanding.

"If their specialists are handling it, that's definitely for the best."
