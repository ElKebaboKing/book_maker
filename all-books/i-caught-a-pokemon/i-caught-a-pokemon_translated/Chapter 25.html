"Ah, well…" Brock shook his head and laughed bitterly. He never expected that the first thing they would talk about after meeting again would be who looked older! Still, he understood Ethan was only joking as a way to get closer, so he spread his hands helplessly.

"Maybe it's because I have nine younger siblings to take care of, so I've overworked myself."

"Oh right," Ash suddenly asked, "Do you want to come with us to the harbor?"

Ethan looked puzzled.

"What are you going to the harbor for?"

"Someone in downtown Vermilion City gave us tickets to a luxury cruise ship," Misty explained. "The person said a huge trainer banquet is being held there by a major financial group. Lots of people will attend, it'll be super fun, and everything is completely free!"

"There's really such a good deal?" Ethan frowned suspiciously. "Why does this sound like some kind of scam?"

Seeing Ethan's disbelief, Ash pulled a ticket out of his pocket.

"It's real! Look, this is the ticket for the cruise ship. We already saw tons of people heading toward the harbor on our way here."

"Even so…" Ethan looked at the ticket and asked, "But there's only one ticket. How are the three of you planning to get on board?"

Ash opened the ticket and pointed at the word Group printed on it.

"Hehe, it's a group ticket! As many people as we want can come along. If you want to go too, then join us!"

Ethan carefully examined the ticket. Printed on the front was the image of a luxurious cruise ship, and beside it were several large words—

S.S. Anne

The S.S. Anne?

Ethan froze for a moment. Wasn't this the famous sinking ship from the anime!?

Not noticing Ethan's hesitation, Ash continued excitedly,

"We'll get to battle and interact with trainers from all over the world, and we'll probably see lots of Pokémon we've never seen before. It's going to be awesome!"

"Exactly!" Brock nodded enthusiastically, already imagining the scene in his mind. "And there'll definitely be lots of delicious food and beautiful older ladies!"

Even Misty's eyes sparkled with anticipation.

"A relaxing cruise vacation, lying on the deck under the sun… just thinking about it sounds amazing."

While the three of them were daydreaming, Ethan was thinking about something entirely different.

If he remembered correctly, shortly after the S.S. Anne set sail, it would be attacked by Team Rocket and later caught in a violent storm, eventually sinking beneath the ocean after being battered by massive waves.

A luxury cruise ship of that scale sinking into the sea… the financial losses alone would probably amount to billions.

But after thinking it over, Ethan realized that even if he tried to warn everyone in advance, it probably wouldn't change the ship's fate.

Since Team Rocket had already infiltrated the S.S. Anne, they must have prepared thoroughly. Without evidence, interfering recklessly would not only fail to attract the captain's attention, but might even alert Team Rocket instead. And if he acted too obviously, he could even draw the attention of the organizer behind the banquet—

Giovanni, the true boss of Team Rocket.

As for the storm...

Although Ethan didn't know much about maritime affairs, a cruise ship of this scale would definitely monitor weather reports before departure.

Since the captain still allowed the ship to sail, that meant either Team Rocket secretly altered the ship's course, or the massive storm capable of sinking the vessel had formed suddenly.

"Hm? Ethan, what are you thinking about?" Misty asked curiously after noticing his long silence. "So? Are you coming with us or not?"

Ethan nodded.

"Of course I'm going. I'd like to see it for myself too."

The group arrived at Vermilion Harbor, and the moment they entered, they saw countless ships docked along the coastline.

But among them all, the largest and most luxurious was undoubtedly the S.S. Anne.

Towering more than ten stories high, the massive vessel was the undeniable centerpiece of the harbor.

The S.S. Anne was a world-famous luxury cruise liner. It was said to dock at Vermilion City once every year before continuing its voyage around the world.

Just as Ash had said earlier, many trainers with their Pokémon were already lining up at the docks, checking tickets and boarding.

And the closer they got, the more overwhelming the ship became.

"Wow, it's huge!" Misty looked up at the towering cruise ship and let out an amazed cry. "We're really going out to sea on that thing?"

However, just as Ash and the others were about to board, Ethan suddenly stopped them.

"Wait a second. I need to buy something first."

After speaking, he walked into a nearby diving equipment store. After browsing for quite a while, he finally purchased a pair of diving goggles and a simple breathing apparatus.

So expensive...

After buying those two items, the savings he had accumulated from working part-time jobs were reduced to less than half.

Watching Ethan pay for the items and carefully place them into his backpack with a serious expression, Ash asked curiously,

"Why are you buying those?"

"I'm not very good at swimming," Ethan replied casually. "These might come in handy someday."

Ethan chose not to tell them the truth.

After all, none of it had happened yet. Besides, considering how blessed these three were by fate itself, they would always find a way out of danger somehow. Even if the S.S. Anne really sank, they would definitely survive.

They lined up, checked their tickets, and boarded the ship.

After walking up the gangway and across the deck, the group entered a magnificent grand hall.

The hall was spacious and dazzling, illuminated by golden decorations and countless crystal chandeliers. It was already filled with all kinds of guests, while long tables covered with luxurious food lined the red carpet, available for everyone to enjoy freely.

Ethan walked into the crowd and looked around.

Whether they were old friends or strangers meeting for the first time, everyone chatted enthusiastically about their journeys and their Pokémon. Smiles filled every face, and it was obvious that everyone was thoroughly enjoying the cruise.

Along both sides of the hall, merchants were selling all sorts of goods.

From earrings and accessories, to figurines and giant Pokémon plushies, every kind of item imaginable could be found there. Conversations, shouting vendors, and bargaining voices blended together into an extremely lively atmosphere.

But the biggest attraction in the hall was undoubtedly the Pokémon battle currently taking place in the center, surrounded by layers of spectators.

The battle had been organized spontaneously by the passengers.

One side was a middle-aged man in a suit, while the other was a young trainer wearing a green shirt.

Their Pokémon were—

A Rattata and a Starmie.

( End of Chapter )
