"Ah! Marowak has been knocked down! Can it get back on its feet and stage an incredible comeback!?"

In the center of the battlefield, after being relentlessly bombarded by a barrage of Grass-type attacks, Marowak lay battered and bruised on the grassy field.

It struggled to stand, but before it could recover, a sweeping Razor Leaf struck it head-on, knocking it out.

"What a shame! Damian has suffered another defeat! Meanwhile, Ethan Vale has gained a commanding advantage and is now at match point!"

Overall, the battle had been remarkably short. As the announcer's energetic voice echoed throughout the stadium, the spectators could clearly see that Damian had been driven into a corner. Unless he revealed a much stronger Pokémon, he was destined to lose 3–0.

Grinding his teeth, Damian recalled Marowak and reluctantly took out his final Poké Ball.

"Hmph! I was planning to save this Pokémon as my secret weapon until the semifinals—or even the finals. Looks like I'll have to reveal it early!"

Hearing that, Ethan became slightly more serious. Under everyone's expectant gaze, a flash of blue light revealed Damian's final Pokémon.

Its fiery red fur and enormous fluffy tail immediately caught everyone's attention.

It was a Flareon.

"...I see," Ethan said after a brief silence. "So this is your strongest Pokémon?"

"You finally understand," Damian snorted, clearly annoyed by Ethan's disappointed tone. "Don't underestimate it. I know you're friends with the people who took my Charmander. Let me tell you something—the reason I abandoned Charmander in the first place was because I had already obtained this Flareon! In terms of strength, it won't lose to your Pokémon!"

Since it was his opponent's ace, Ethan focused completely.

He recalled Venusaur and sent out his third Pokémon instead.

"You're up, Blastoise."

To guarantee his advancement, Ethan intended to approach every battle seriously, always taking advantage of favorable type matchups whenever possible.

The referee announced the start of the battle.

Flareon immediately burst into action, darting around the field with boundless energy.

Its performance was indeed much stronger than Damian's previous two Pokémon.

However, fire and water were natural opposites.

Despite putting up a determined fight, Flareon's flames were gradually extinguished by Blastoise's relentless Water-type attacks. Eventually, it collapsed onto the battlefield just like the others.

"What? That was so easy," Ash said from the stands, letting out a sigh of relief after watching Ethan win so effortlessly. "Maybe this tournament isn't as difficult as I thought."

Misty rolled her eyes.

"Don't get the wrong idea. Ethan made it look easy because he's much stronger, while Damian just isn't at the same level. That's why it only seems easy."

"Brock's right too," Brock added with a nod. "Ash, don't underestimate the competition."

On the trainer's platform, Damian stood frozen in disbelief.

I... didn't even win a single battle!?

I'm supposed to be strong enough to make it much farther than this!

Unable to accept reality, he pinched his own thigh.

The sharp pain confirmed what he already knew.

He had been completely and utterly defeated.

The referee raised his right hand high.

"All of the Red side's Pokémon have been defeated! The winner of this match is the Green side!"

The announcer followed immediately.

"Congratulations to Ethan Vale for advancing with an overwhelming victory! Let's give him a huge round of applause!"

Amid thunderous cheers from the crowd, Ethan waved to the audience, recalled his Pokémon, and calmly walked back through the players' tunnel.

After the Match

Later, inside a nearby ice cream café outside the stadium, Ethan, Chloe Cerise, Ash, and the others gathered together, along with the biker gang, enjoying a relaxing afternoon.

They occupied several tables, and Ethan bought cold drinks for everyone to thank them for coming to cheer him on.

Kane burst into laughter while talking to his sister, nearly spraying shaved ice all over her.

"That guy Damian was a complete pushover! Ethan beat him 3–0 without breaking a sweat. Honestly, I think even I could've beaten him!"

His voice had become noticeably hoarse from shouting so loudly during the match.

Unfortunately, that careless remark immediately caused trouble.

Outside the café, Damian happened to arrive with several of his friends.

Still furious after losing, he had come to cool off with a cold drink.

The moment he stepped inside, he heard Kane's comment.

"Who was just talking trash!?"

Furious, Damian stormed toward Kane.

But after getting a closer look at Kane and Misa, both dressed like bikers, he instinctively took a cautious step backward.

"Oh?" Kane sneered. "Isn't this the guy our boss crushed? What, still unhappy about losing? Want to settle it with a fight?"

Damian glanced nervously at Kane's leather jacket and towering pompadour hairstyle before looking toward Ethan's table.

Seeing mostly children and women sitting there, his confidence slowly returned.

With several friends backing him up, he decided he couldn't afford to lose face.

"You only won because you got lucky!" Damian shouted arrogantly. "If it had been a full 6-on-6 battle, I would've destroyed you! And now you're insulting me? Want me to knock your teeth out!?"

Before he could finish speaking—

Scrape!

Several nearby tables were pushed back at once.

More than a dozen bikers stood up simultaneously, glaring at Damian with intimidating expressions.

Not only did they vastly outnumber him, but their fierce appearance alone was enough to make anyone nervous.

"I..."

Damian's face turned pale.

He had rushed into the café without paying attention to its layout. He had assumed only Kane and Misa were there.

He never realized the tall wooden shelves separating the seating area had hidden the rest of the biker gang from view.

One of Damian's friends whispered nervously,

"Uh... Damian... this doesn't look good. Let's just leave."

Damian swallowed hard.

He knew he had spoken far too boldly.

"I-I just remembered... I forgot to lock my bicycle outside! Y-You just wait!"

Leaving behind one last empty threat, he and his friends fled the café in embarrassment.

"What was that?" Misty muttered quietly. "Are those guys professional comedians?"

To Ethan and the others, it was nothing more than a brief interruption, and no one paid it much attention.

They continued enjoying their desserts until it was almost time for Ash's match.

Turning toward Ash, Ethan smiled.

"Now it's your turn. Your opponent may have quite a reputation, but I believe you'll win."

Beside him, Chloe thought for a moment before offering a helpful reminder.

"I remember that trainer named Toby is also a magician. I've seen him on the news several times. You'd better be careful."

( End of Chapter )
