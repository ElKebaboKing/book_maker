The Dratini was completely absorbed in eating and didn't seem to pay any attention to Ethan's words.

In truth, quite a long time had already passed since its mother left. Although it still occasionally thought about her, the emotional wounds were slowly healing, and it was no longer as heartbroken as it had been at the beginning.

After licking the can completely clean, it looked back at Ethan as though still wanting more.

Seeing this, Ethan quickly pulled out a second can and placed it in front of the Dratini.

"Go ahead, eat as much as you want."

This time, the Dratini was slightly braver. It carefully and cautiously crept toward Ethan before carrying away the second can.

Watching it devour the food like a whirlwind and polish the second can spotless as well, Ethan couldn't help secretly marveling.

Such a tiny body, yet such an enormous appetite—it almost rivaled two Wartortle combined!

At that moment, Haunter glanced up at the sky and said to Ethan,

"It's getting late. We've already stayed here for quite a while. Shouldn't we be leaving soon?"

Ethan nodded and gathered his Pokémon together. Just as he was about to leave aboard Zapdos, the lonely Dratini stared at his back for a long while. Seeing him preparing to leave, it hurriedly slithered after him and cried out softly a few times.

"Huh?" Haunter said in surprise. "It wants to come with us!"

"Come with us?"

Ethan froze for a moment, genuinely astonished.

As the only pseudo-legendary Pokémon from the first generation, he had always loved Dragonite.

Even before setting foot on this island, he had often thought about how he absolutely wanted to catch a Dragonite someday. But after arriving here and seeing these Pokémon living such peaceful and carefree lives, he had been unwilling to disrupt the island's tranquility and never once considered capturing any of them.

Yet to his complete surprise, this lonely little Dratini actually wanted to leave with him voluntarily. It truly was an unexpected delight!

"Hey, Haunter," Ethan asked, "you don't think it only wants to follow me because it wants more canned food, do you?"

"At least you're somewhat self-aware," Haunter rolled its eyes. "It's lived here its whole life, so it's probably sick of eating berries by now. In its eyes, your backpack is probably an endless treasure chest full of different flavors of canned food."

Well, even if the method was a little unusual, Ethan was naturally delighted that he could obtain such a precious Pokémon with nothing more than canned food.

Turning toward the nearby Dragonite, he saw that not only did they show no objections, but they even wore relieved and pleased expressions.

Ethan nodded toward them, then crouched down and took an empty Poké Ball from his backpack, holding it out toward the Dratini.

"Have you thought this through?" Ethan asked seriously. "If you leave this place with me, you might not be able to come back here for a very, very long time."

The Dratini licked its lips, seemingly not listening to a word Ethan said. Instead, it apparently mistook the Poké Ball for something edible and slowly moved closer.

The instant its nose touched the button, a flash of red light enveloped it, and the Dratini was immediately pulled into the Poké Ball!

The Poké Ball shook lightly, then with a soft click, it became completely still.

Sigh… what an absolute glutton.

Ethan shook his head helplessly, but regardless of the circumstances, after traveling for so long, he had finally caught his ninth Pokémon!

Riding on Zapdos's back, Ethan flew through the ring-shaped storm and left Dragonite Island behind.

Once his phone regained signal, he checked his location and continued toward Fuchsia City.

The air high in the sky was extremely cold, and flying against the wind made it almost difficult to breathe. Ethan quickly patted Zapdos on the back, signaling it to lower its altitude, and only then did he finally feel better.

The ocean sped rapidly beneath them.

With Zapdos's incredible speed, they flew for a very long distance. Once they reached the coastal waters, Ethan switched back to Gyarados to avoid attracting attention, riding across the sea until they reached the shore.

Fuchsia—a brilliant and dazzling crimson-pink hue.

As one of the major cities in southwestern Kanto, besides the Fuchsia Gym, the city's most famous landmark was the Safari Zone located not far away.

There, people only needed to pay an entrance fee of 500 yen to receive thirty Safari Balls, allowing them to legally capture Pokémon within the massive natural habitat.

Since Trainers didn't need to battle and only had to throw Safari Balls, rocks, or bait to catch Pokémon, many ordinary people traveled from far away every year just to obtain their very first Pokémon there.

However, for Ethan, there was nothing left there worth catching anymore. So when he passed by the Safari Zone, he merely glanced at it casually before continuing directly toward Fuchsia City.

But after entering the bustling urban district filled with towering buildings, he discovered through navigation that the Fuchsia Gym, unlike most other Gyms, wasn't located within the city at all. Instead, it was hidden deep within the mountains outside the city.

Having no choice, Ethan restocked on water and food at a nearby shop before setting out toward the mountains once again.

Unfortunately, the path leading to the Fuchsia Gym was extremely rugged. Most of it consisted of uneven mountain trails, and at times he even had to wade through streams and cross narrow wooden bridges, making every step increasingly exhausting.

Strange cries echoed throughout the dense forest, and who knew what Pokémon might be secretly observing them from the shadows.

Seeing Ethan gradually slow down, Haunter suggested,

"How about it? Want me to use the wind to carry you forward?"

"Forget it." Ethan shook his head. "Even though these roads are tiring, it's not entirely a bad thing. I'll just treat it as physical training."

"I seriously don't understand," Haunter muttered while looking around. "Why build a Gym in such a remote place? Gyms exist for Trainers to challenge and earn Badges from, yet this one's hidden so deep in the mountains. By the time challengers arrive, they'll probably collapse from exhaustion before they even battle!"

"Well…" Ethan adjusted his backpack as he walked and explained, "The Fuchsia Gym Leader is Koga. Not only is he a Poison-type specialist, but he's also a ninja—apparently a descendant of the Iga ninja clan. That's why he trains deep in the mountains. So this journey can also be considered part of the challenge for Trainers."

After another ten or so minutes of hiking, they finally reached a high slope. Looking down from above, they could finally see a massive multi-level courtyard built along a distant cliffside!

The architecture looked ancient yet elegant, and all the buildings within the courtyard were connected by covered hallways, forming a huge interconnected complex.

Looking carefully toward the entrance gate, Ethan could clearly see the four large characters written on the plaque:

Fuchsia Gym!
