Leaving the Pewter Gym together, Ethan Vale pointed toward the Pewter Museum of Science across the street.

"The museum here is really famous. You could say it's one of Pewter City's biggest attractions. Want to go take a look together?"

"Sure." Since she had nothing else to do anyway, Misty agreed without hesitation.

Crossing the street, the magnificent museum soon came into view. After entering, Ethan Vale bought two tickets, and together they stepped into the first-floor lobby.

The museum was divided into two main areas: exhibitions and scientific research.

Unfortunately, the research section was not open to the public, so visitors could only explore the exhibition halls.

The exhibition area itself was divided into two floors.

The first floor focused on fossils. Display cases throughout the hall showcased rare ancient Pokémon fossils, with the most notable exhibits being the skeleton fossils of Aerodactyl and Kabutops.

However, Misty clearly wasn't interested in fossils at all. She merely glanced at each display before quickly moving on.

"I seriously don't understand why people would display such creepy things…"

The two then headed upstairs.

Unlike the first floor, the second floor's theme was space exploration.

The exhibits included historical artifacts, meteorites collected from the moon's surface, and even a model of a space shuttle.

As Ethan Vale and Misty stopped in front of the model, an elderly man nearby began speaking on his own:

"Ever since humanity first landed on the moon, decades have passed in the blink of an eye. I've heard there are even plans to travel to the distant red planet next. Whether those stories are true or not, anything related to space always excites me."

Exchanging smiles with each other, the two leisurely wandered around the museum a few more times before eventually leaving and finding a nearby restaurant.

Though the restaurant wasn't particularly luxurious, both the appetizers and desserts were surprisingly satisfying.

During the meal, Misty once again recounted how she and Ash first met—as well as the tragic fate of her bicycle.

"You really should've seen how miserable it looked," Misty complained, still clearly annoyed. "I had only just bought that bike, and after riding it for one day, he turned it into charcoal!"

"So until he pays you back, you're just going to keep following him around?" Ethan Vale asked with a smile.

"That's part of the reason." Misty laughed. "But my real goal is to become a Water-type Pokémon Master, and traveling around is the fastest way to achieve that!"

Ethan Vale smiled and chatted along with her.

Although he had already seen these events in the anime before, hearing Misty personally tell the story gave it a completely different feeling.

By evening, when they returned to the Pokémon Center, Ash still hadn't shown up.

Misty couldn't help asking:

"Do you think Ash might've already left Pewter City?"

"That's impossible." Ethan Vale shook his head. "Unless he defeats Brock, he'll never leave Pewter City. Earliest tonight, latest tomorrow morning, he'll definitely show up again to challenge the Gym."

And just as Ethan Vale predicted, after spending the night resting, he and Misty encountered a completely exhausted—but highly energized—Ash while eating breakfast in the cafeteria the next morning.

"Ash!" Misty waved toward him. "Where did you run off to yesterday?"

"Sorry for worrying you guys."

Ash dropped heavily into a chair at the table and stuffed food into his mouth.

"Yesterday, I met Mr. Flint outside the Gym. Pikachu and I went through a special charging training session with him."

"Mr. Flint? Charging training?" Misty blinked in confusion. "But even so, there's no way an Electric-type Pikachu can beat Brock."

"You'll never know unless you try!" Ash replied, completely filled with determination. "Besides, today's Pikachu is totally different from yesterday!"

"Oh really?" Misty replied skeptically. "Well then, I'm looking forward to seeing it."

Using a napkin to wipe the corner of his mouth, Ethan Vale suddenly spoke up:

"Ash, good luck. Unfortunately, I won't be able to witness that moment myself."

"Why not?" Ash asked curiously.

Instead of answering directly, Ethan Vale countered:

"Your goal is to compete in the Indigo League, right?"

After Ash nodded, Ethan Vale glanced toward his Pokémon and said seriously:

"In that case, we're rivals now. And I also have my own path to follow. It's about time I head toward the next Gym city."

"Huh? You're leaving already?" Misty looked reluctant.

Although she hadn't known Ethan Vale for very long, spending time with him had felt incredibly easy and enjoyable.

And after just one day together, she could already sense how gentle, considerate, and humorous he was.

Ethan Vale smiled warmly.

"Don't look so sad. Kanto may be huge, but our goals are the same. We're bound to meet again sooner or later. Maybe in the next city—or even at the next Gym."

After returning to Vermilion City, Ethan Vale headed straight for the Cerise Research Institute.

"You've worked really hard this trip," Professor Cerise said gratefully upon seeing him. "After you delivered the prototype, Professor Oak immediately called me. He praised you endlessly over the phone—I was honestly getting embarrassed listening to it."

At that moment, Chloe Cerise walked in from outside carrying her school bag.

Seeing Eevee, Squirtle, and Charmander, she looked shocked.

"So you've already caught this many Pokémon?"

"Yeah. I've already collected all three Kanto starters. Next, I just need one more Pokémon to complete my full team."

As he spoke, Ethan Vale gestured toward the Pokémon that hadn't met Chloe before.

"This is Chloe. Go say hello to her."

After gently patting each of them on the forehead, Chloe's eyes lit up when she saw Kirlia. She walked over and crouched down in front of it.

"So this is Kirlia's new form? It's so adorable!"

Hearing Chloe's praise, Kirlia politely bowed toward her.

Its obedient and graceful behavior made Chloe cover her mouth in surprise.

Watching Chloe happily interacting with the Pokémon, Professor Cerise suddenly spoke up:

"Oh right, Ethan Vale. I arranged a dorm room for you here at the institute. That way you won't need to stay at Pokémon Centers anymore. Also, Cerise Park has finally been completed, so from now on, your Pokémon can stay there whenever you want."

"Cerise Park?" Ethan Vale blinked in surprise.

Professor Cerise pointed out the window.

"Look, right over there."

Following the direction of his finger, Ethan Vale saw a gigantic dome-shaped structure made entirely of reinforced glass standing behind the Cerise Research Institute.

The building resembled a massive transparent shield.

Inside the dome were flowing streams, ponds, hills, valleys, and even a dense forest.
