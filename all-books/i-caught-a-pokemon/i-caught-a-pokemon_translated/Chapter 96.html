Shellder's defense was practically flawless, without a single opening.

At this moment, it was like an impossibly tough canned meal. Without the right tool to pry it open, figuring out how to crack its shell had become the biggest challenge.

As Ethan Vale pondered a solution, his eyes accidentally landed on one of the biker gang members. Hanging from the young man's waist was a golden chain that swayed gently with his movements.

In an instant, inspiration struck.

Well... I'm out of ideas anyway. Might as well try this.

"Kirlia, use Psychic and lift it into the air!"

Hearing Ethan's command, Misa laughed.

"You're trying that again? Don't forget—even if you slam Cloyster into the ground with Psychic, it won't take any damage!"

Ignoring her, Ethan watched as Cloyster floated high into the air.

"Good. Now spin it around!"

"What!?"

As Kirlia rotated her fingers, Cloyster's body began spinning under the influence of Psychic.

It looked like a giant windmill whirling through the sky.

Ten rotations.

Twenty.

Fifty.

One hundred...

By the time the biker gang members below were feeling dizzy just from watching, Cloyster was finally smashed back down to the ground.

BOOM!

When it landed, it looked completely unchanged from the outside.

Still, Misa called out cautiously.

"Cloyster... are you okay?"

The next second, Cloyster suddenly opened its shell and began vomiting violently.

Yellow and green fluids mixed together, splattering across the ground. A light breeze carried the foul smell everywhere.

Its mind was completely disoriented. The world seemed upside down, spinning endlessly.

"I can't believe that actually worked."

Even Ethan looked slightly surprised.

"Don't give it a chance to recover. Kirlia, use Magical Leaf!"

Seeing the leaves gather once more, Misa shouted:

"Cloyster! Close your shell!"

Unfortunately, Cloyster either couldn't hear her or simply lacked the strength to respond.

Misa could only watch helplessly as her Pokémon was struck.

Thud! Thud! Thud!

The Magical Leaves hit one after another.

Perhaps because it had spun so many times, even the pain failed to snap Cloyster out of its dizziness.

Then a second wave of Magical Leaf struck.

This time, Cloyster finally reached its limit and fainted.

Ethan shrugged.

"Looks like I'm the winner after all."

Just as he finished speaking, a brilliant white light suddenly enveloped Kirlia.

"Is that... evolution?"

This Pokémon had been with Ethan longer than any other. After so many journeys and intense battles, it had first evolved from Ralts into Kirlia.

And now...

It was finally evolving into Gardevoir!

As the light gradually faded, a graceful Pokémon emerged.

Its green body resembled an elegant humanoid figure dressed in a flowing white evening gown. Its slightly curled hair framed a mature and dignified appearance.

The transformation was striking.

The bright young girl-like Kirlia had become a beautiful, mature lady radiating elegance.

Most noticeably, the two red horn-like fins that had once protruded from its head had moved to its chest and back.

Pokédex:

"Gardevoir, the Embrace Pokémon. A Psychic- and Fairy-type Pokémon and the evolved form of Kirlia. Compared to its previous form, Gardevoir can sense the emotions of humans and Pokémon more clearly. Some are even said to glimpse the future. Its psychic powers can distort space or create black holes, and it will risk its own life to protect its Trainer."

"Wow!"

Seeing Gardevoir, Misa's eyes sparkled.

"She's even more beautiful than before!"

Gardevoir quietly walked to Ethan's side.

Out of habit, Ethan raised a hand to pat her head.

Then he paused.

Her height had increased dramatically.

From eighty centimeters, she had nearly doubled in size and now stood at shoulder height beside him.

Looking at her now, Gardevoir felt less like a Pokémon and more like a gentle, loyal girlfriend walking at his side.

"Congratulations, Gardevoir."

Ethan smiled warmly.

"You've finally reached your third stage. But don't get too proud. Our challenges are only just beginning."

At that moment, Misa and Masha approached.

Masha spoke with admiration.

"You're really incredible. We lost this battle."

At Misa's signal, one of the biker gang members pushed a bicycle toward them.

Misa said,

"As agreed, we'll lend you a bicycle. But to make sure it's returned safely—and not damaged—we'll be accompanying you the whole way."

"No problem."

Returning Gardevoir to her Poké Ball, Ethan mounted the bicycle and headed toward the bridge entrance.

About ten meters behind him, the biker gang followed.

After greeting the security guard, Ethan finally entered the unfinished super bridge, earning a look of admiration from the man.

What Ethan didn't know was that, atop a distant building, a man was watching him through binoculars.

After confirming Ethan's identity, the man took out his phone and made a call.

"This is Observer. Target Number Seven has been spotted outside Fuchsia City. He is currently crossing the sea bridge toward Sun Town."

A hoarse voice answered from the other end.

"Understood. Continue surveillance. I'll relay the information to the Enforcers immediately."

The bridge surface was smooth and clean.

After riding for more than half an hour, Ethan arrived at a rest area.

Just as he was about to stop and take a break, an invisible force suddenly grabbed him and yanked him off the bicycle.

He flew more than ten meters through the air.

At the exact moment he left the bike, a powerful attack struck it.

BOOOOM!!!

The explosion completely obliterated the bicycle.

Even the bridge itself was blown open, leaving behind a massive crater.

The sudden disaster left not only Ethan stunned, but also the biker gang members behind him.

They slammed on their brakes in panic.

"What happened!?"

"How did the bicycle explode!?"

Everyone stared at one another, unable to comprehend what had happened.

Only Ethan looked forward.

Standing side by side atop one of the bridge's support beams were four figures.

They stared down at him coldly.

They were none other than the four Rocket Executives Ethan had crossed paths with many times before.

( End of Chapter )
