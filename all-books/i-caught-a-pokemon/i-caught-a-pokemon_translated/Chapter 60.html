Hearing that there was a one million reward, both Ash and Brock were immediately tempted. However, before either of them could say anything, Misty loudly refused.

"Don't be ridiculous! Who would agree to something like that!?"

After saying that, she grabbed Ethan Vale by the arm and dragged him out of the office.

Back in the city, the group walked toward the shoreline. Even now, Ash and Brock still had no idea why Misty was so angry.

"Wait a second, Misty! Why don't you want to help her?" Ash asked in confusion. If they really earned that one million reward, he could finally pay Misty back for her bicycle.

"That Madam Gold is absolutely infuriating!" Misty complained. "Tentacool are so adorable, so why would anyone want to hurt them? I'm sure there's a reason behind why they're attacking humans!"

"Tentacool are adorable!?" Ethan, Ash, and Brock all stared at her with identical looks of confusion.

"Of course!" Misty said dreamily. "Tentacool feel soft and squishy when you touch them, and the red jewel on their heads is even called the 'Ruby of the Sea'!"

"Ruby?" Ash said speechlessly. "Whenever Water-type Pokémon are involved, your whole personality changes!"

At that moment, the citywide broadcast system suddenly activated, and Madam Gold's voice echoed through the streets.

"To drive away our enemies, the Tentacool, we are now recruiting capable warriors! Anyone who can drive away those hateful Tentacool will receive a reward of one million! Trainers who want this money—no, trainers who want to make a name for themselves here—gather at the harbor immediately, granny!"

The announcement repeated three times. Trainers all across the city immediately rushed toward the harbor. Some genuinely wanted to help, some were blinded by greed, and others simply came to watch the excitement unfold.

"How can she do this!?" Misty frowned and hurried toward the harbor. Seeing this, the others quickly followed after her.

By the time they arrived, the harbor was already packed with people. After squeezing through the crowd with difficulty, Ethan and the others saw Madam Gold sitting inside a tank while Team Rocket's trio appeared to have already accepted the task and were rapidly speeding away in a boat!

"Team Rocket!?" Misty instantly had a bad feeling. "Whenever those guys get involved, things always become even worse!"

Sure enough, after Team Rocket's boat reached a nearby coral reef, it was suddenly attacked by Tentacool and exploded.

Only then did the crowd realize that thousands upon thousands of Tentacool had somehow gathered there beneath the water. Their bodies overlapped densely under the sea while the red gems on their foreheads glowed ominously, forming a terrifying crimson ocean.

The crowd gradually fell silent. At last, they realized that humans might not be the only side trying to eliminate the other.

Then something even stranger happened.

For reasons nobody understood, after being provoked by Team Rocket, one Tentacool was suddenly drenched in a barrel of unknown liquid. Its body rapidly mutated, and it evolved into a gigantic Tentacruel dozens of meters tall!!

The moment the massive Tentacruel appeared, panic instantly spread across the harbor. Some people, sensing disaster, had already begun quietly fleeing the area.

"How could it possibly become that huge!?" Misty gasped in disbelief.

Ethan guessed, "It's probably Dynamaxing. Maybe that barrel contained some kind of substance imported from the Galar region. Otherwise, something like this shouldn't be possible."

"Dynamaxing!?" Ash was hearing the term for the first time. Then he suddenly remembered something and exclaimed, "Ah! I remember now! Back at Bill's lighthouse, we saw a giant Pokémon in the fog too! Was that also Dynamaxing?"

"Who knows?" Ethan shook his head. "There are too many mysteries in this world that science can't explain. There are countless truths still waiting for us to uncover."

Misty covered her mouth and laughed softly.

"You sound just like some kind of scientific researcher."

"Huh? Really?" Ethan blinked in surprise. Had spending too much time around Professor Cerise subconsciously influenced him?

At that moment, the giant Tentacruel had already climbed onto the unfinished super hotel. Its enormous tentacles wrapped around the building again and again, crushing it like a giant monster invading Earth until the entire structure collapsed completely.

Watching her dream hotel get destroyed with her own eyes, Madam Gold nearly fainted from rage.

"How dare you destroy my hotel! I'll never forgive you! Fire! Fire!!"

The tank beneath her immediately opened fire, while the four handsome attendants somehow produced rocket launchers and continuously bombarded the giant Tentacruel.

Unfortunately, the shells simply bounced harmlessly off Tentacruel's soft body and could not even pierce its skin.

However, their attack only enraged it further.

With a furious roar, the Tentacool army began invading the city, while the giant Tentacruel itself used its enormous body to create a massive tidal wave that surged toward the shore.

"Stop standing around! We need to get out of here now!"

Seeing the gigantic wave rushing closer and closer, Ethan first snapped Ash and Brock out of their daze before grabbing Misty's hand and running.

Unfortunately, the tsunami advanced far too quickly. The entire city was already being flooded by seawater. Ethan and the others had barely run a few dozen meters before the wave caught up to them!

As for the people behind them, many had already been swept away by the floodwaters. Buildings collapsed under the tremendous impact, windows shattered, cars were submerged, and the entire city had become an ocean of destruction.

"Gyarados, come out!"

Ethan hurriedly released Gyarados. The group scrambled onto its back in panic, and under Gyarados's guidance, they narrowly escaped disaster.

By the time they finally retreated safely to a television tower on a nearby hill, the city below had already become a scene of devastation.

Meanwhile, the giant Tentacruel was clearly not satisfied. Leading its swarm into the city, it lashed its countless tentacles everywhere, wreaking destruction on a massive scale.

Brock sighed heavily as realization dawned on him.

"So that Horsea was trying to warn us about this all along."

"But why are they doing this?" Misty asked in confusion. "If they keep acting like this, people will only hate them even more!"

At that moment, Ethan knew he had to explain things clearly.

Pointing toward the ruined hotel in the sea, he said,

"Look carefully. That hotel was built directly on top of the coral reef, and that reef was the natural habitat of these Tentacool. During construction, the workers inevitably destroyed the coral reefs and invaded their territory. That's why the jellyfish attacked them in the first place—and why this retaliation happened in the end."

(End of Chapter)
