Blaine didn't seem bothered by Ethan's attitude. Instead, he stared at him and asked:

"About that wig riddle just now… did you notice something?"

Ethan smiled.

"I heard that the Cinnabar Gym Leader, Blaine, is a passionate old man who loves riddles… uh… I mean, an elderly gentleman. And because he started going bald years ago, he likes wearing wigs. Besides, Gyms are League property. Even though this place has become an inn, other than the Gym Leader himself, I can't think of anyone else who would have the authority to remodel it."

"How clever!" Since his identity had already been exposed, Blaine admitted it openly. "That's right, I am Blaine—the Gym Leader of Cinnabar Gym!"

Then he awkwardly changed the subject.

"As for the baldness you mentioned, that's purely a rumor. Don't go spreading nonsense around. It's only because Cinnabar Island is so hot that I shave my head bald."

Blaine's explanation sounded rather weak and forced. Not only Ethan, but even Misty and Brock could tell he was obviously trying to cover it up.

Apparently, only Ash actually believed him.

He stepped forward excitedly.

"So you're Blaine! Mr. Blaine, I'm Ash from Pallet Town, and I came specifically to challenge you!"

"Ahem, ahem. I advise you to give up." Blaine shook his head and pointed behind him. "The Gym has already become an inn. I'm not a Gym Leader anymore. If you want a Badge, go try another town."

"Huh!?" Ash looked completely stunned. "It really turned into an inn!? How could that happen!?"

Blaine crossed his arms helplessly.

"Blame those hot springs they dug up a few years ago. Ever since then, this entire town has become a tourist resort. As for those weak trainers who come here with sightseeing in mind and only challenge me as a side activity… I got tired of them. So I simply turned the Gym into an inn and refused them all."

Hearing the reason, Ash shouted:

"Don't compare us to those people! We've been training and traveling seriously with the Pokémon League as our goal! We're not half-hearted challengers!"

"That's right," Ethan added. "Mr. Blaine, there's no way you can send us away with just a few words. Until we earn the Volcano Badge, we absolutely won't give up."

After hearing the two of them speak, Blaine carefully examined Ethan and Ash. Though surprised, he eventually seemed to acknowledge them.

"Very well. Since you insist, I can agree to battle you. But before that, you'll need to find the Gym's true location yourselves."

"The Gym's true location?" Misty looked at the inn built from the old Gym near the volcano and asked in confusion, "Isn't the Gym here?"

"Of course it's inside the inn," Blaine laughed. "But finding it won't be easy. You'll have to solve the proper mechanisms yourselves and locate its exact position."

With that, Blaine led Ethan and the others into the inn.

Since it had originally been remodeled from a Gym, the inn occupied quite a large area. The front section contained guest rooms and dining areas, while the back section housed an open-air hot spring.

After entering the lobby, Blaine turned toward them.

"I provide free lodging, food, and even access to the hot springs. Feel free to use everything here. But first, listen carefully to my clue."

Everyone nodded together.

"Please tell us."

Blaine slowly said with his hands behind his back:

"Above, a great flood. Below, a great fire."

Ash blinked blankly.

"Huh? That's it?"

"Yup, that simple." Blaine laughed heartily. "Since you've all heard it clearly, please enjoy yourselves."

After saying that, Blaine ignored them completely and walked away.

Once he was far enough away, Brock frowned thoughtfully.

"Above, a great flood. Below, a great fire… What does that mean? Some kind of disaster movie?"

Misty glanced at Ethan and noticed he didn't seem interested in discussing it.

"Taken literally, it probably refers to something involving water and fire."

Then her tone suddenly shifted excitedly.

"But didn't Blaine say the food and hot springs here are free? Why don't we relax first? Once we've rested properly, we can look for the mechanism afterward."

"That actually sounds great!" Even though Brock knew Misty was clearly just being greedy for comfort, he still agreed enthusiastically. "After traveling for so long, soaking in a hot spring really would help relieve fatigue."

For once, everyone agreed instantly and headed straight toward the inn's dining hall.

Inside the traditional Japanese-style room, Ethan and the others sat kneeling on tatami mats. Before long, several elderly cooks brought them lunch.

Since the inn was near the sea, most of the dishes consisted of sashimi, sushi, and miso soup. The spread was incredibly rich, and after tasting each item one by one, Ethan had to admit everything was delicious.

And because Ethan was already an adult, one of the cooks even specially served him a bottle of sake!

Taking a small sip, the faint aroma of alcohol filled his mouth.

Compared to Ethan's relatively refined eating manners, Ash and Brock were eating like whirlwinds, stuffing food into their mouths at astonishing speed. Even Misty completely abandoned any sense of ladylike behavior at that moment.

After everyone finished eating and drinking, Misty patted her slightly swollen stomach with complete satisfaction.

"Compared to our usual camping life, this place is basically heaven!"

After the meal, they toured the inn together, searching for any suspicious locations.

Only then did they realize that unlike the other packed inns throughout Cinnabar Town, Blaine's inn had absolutely no other guests besides themselves!

Apparently, because Blaine disliked ordinary tourists, he never fully opened the place to the public. Only people he personally acknowledged were invited inside.

Ethan laughed.

"Good thing he's a Gym Leader. Otherwise, with the way he runs this place, the inn would go bankrupt within days."

After resting and digesting a little, they changed into yukata robes and headed to the outdoor hot springs behind the inn.

Although it was an open-air bath, it wasn't mixed-gender. A long partition fence and curtain divided the hot spring into separate sections.

The spring water was warm and soothing. The moment Ethan entered the pool, not only did most of his fatigue vanish, but it also felt as though every pore in his body had suddenly opened.

At that moment, the phone beside him vibrated with a new message notification.

Picking it up, Ethan saw another message from the girl.

And from her wording, she completely avoided mentioning the past, instead simply sharing ordinary little events from her daily life.

She's really trying hard…

His heart softened, and Ethan casually replied.

Nearby, Brock noticed Ethan constantly typing and kindly reminded him:

"You've been holding your phone ever since last night. But staring at the screen for too long can be bad for your eyes, you know!"

( End of Chapter )
