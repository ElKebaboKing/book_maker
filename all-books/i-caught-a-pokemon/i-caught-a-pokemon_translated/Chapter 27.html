After paying, Ethan stored the Magikarp in a Poké Ball, officially obtaining his seventh Pokémon.

As he walked through the corridors, the number of passengers gradually decreased. By the time he entered the ship's guest room section, there were barely even any crew members around.

It seemed most people were still gathered in the main hall and on the deck. After all, they had only recently boarded the ship, so very few people would already be thinking about resting or wandering into the deeper interior of the cruise liner.

After circling around for a while, Ethan still found no trace of Team Rocket. Since there were surveillance cameras everywhere, he also did not dare to wander too openly. Pretending to stop by the restroom, he was just about to return to the main hall when he suddenly noticed a suspicious figure flash past the corner of the corridor ahead.

Team Rocket?

Glancing at Kirlia, Ethan silently chose to follow.

When he reached the corner and cautiously peeked around it, he happened to see the figure crouching outside a guest room door, using a lockpick to try opening it.

The man was middle-aged and wore a brown trench coat over a suit and tie. He had an average build, neither fat nor thin. Although his expression looked serious, the exhaustion on his face was obvious at a glance, clearly the result of frequent sleepless nights.

Looking up, Ethan noticed that the surveillance camera above had actually been temporarily disabled by some kind of signal jammer. No wonder this man dared to act so openly.

The man was completely focused. The lockpick in his hand rotated skillfully, and with a soft click, the lock popped open.

However, before he could celebrate internally, he suddenly noticed a shadow slowly appear across the door in front of him under the corridor lights.

Cold sweat instantly broke out across his body.

His hand moved toward his waist as he hurriedly turned around, only to discover that an extremely handsome young man around twenty years old had somehow appeared silently behind him and was now watching him with great interest.

"Y-You… who are you?"

Due to his extreme nervousness, his accent even shifted slightly, revealing traces of a foreign tone.

"Sir," Ethan asked casually, "you're an International Police officer, aren't you?"

"Huh!?" The man's expression changed drastically, and his heart nearly leapt into his throat. He immediately denied it.

"W-What nonsense are you talking about? I'm definitely not an International Police officer."

"Oh? Really?" Ethan said as he pulled a wallet out from behind his back and waved it in front of him. "But I happened to pick up your wallet just now, and your ID photo is clearly inside."

The middle-aged man hurriedly reached toward the back pocket of his pants and realized his wallet was indeed missing.

"Hahaha… so that's what happened…"

He quickly snatched the wallet back and forced out an awkward yet polite smile.

"To tell you the truth, I really am an International Police officer. I'm currently investigating a massive criminal organization, so please keep this confidential!"

"A criminal organization?" Ethan asked. "You mean Team Rocket?"

This time, the man completely lost his composure. He grabbed Ethan by the arm and quickly pulled him into a nearby stairwell.

"How do you know about Team Rocket!?" he whispered urgently.

"That's not exactly surprising, is it?" Ethan replied matter-of-factly. "They're infamous around here and have appeared on wanted notices more than once. Honestly, it would be stranger to find a trainer who doesn't know about them."

"That's true enough." The man laughed awkwardly before glancing at Kirlia. "You're a Pokémon trainer?"

Ethan took out his Pokédex and showed him his identification.

"Not just a trainer. I'm also a special research investigator for a Pokémon laboratory."

"I see." The man adjusted his collar and spoke seriously. "Then let me formally introduce myself. My codename is Handsome Guy. I'm currently investigating Team Rocket in the Kanto region in order to stop their various criminal activities."

"Your codename is Handsome Guy!?" This time it was Ethan's turn to stare in disbelief. He looked the man up and down suspiciously. "Isn't that a little narcissistic? No offense, but you really don't look anything like a 'handsome guy.'"

Apparently choking on his own saliva, Handsome Guy's face reddened. After coughing twice, he quickly explained,

"How could we possibly use our real names!? 'Handsome Guy' is just my codename! A codename!"

"Even so…" Ethan continued staring at him. "Choosing 'Handsome Guy' as your codename still sounds incredibly narcissistic."

Handsome Guy suddenly looked exhausted. He stopped trying to explain and simply waved his hand helplessly.

"Forget it. I still have work to do, so let's part ways here."

"Work?" Ethan asked. "You mean entering that room from earlier?"

Originally, Handsome Guy had not wanted to involve civilians, but remembering that Ethan had already witnessed everything, he hesitated before speaking.

"That's right. I was tracking a high-ranking Team Rocket executive and discovered he boarded the S.S. Anne. I suspected they might have some kind of plan involving this ship, so I followed him here and intended to search his room while he was away for clues."

"What a coincidence," Ethan said, deciding to reveal a small hint without being too direct. "Before boarding, I noticed a large group of suspiciously disguised people sneaking onto the ship. If Team Rocket really is here, it's probably them."

"What!? A large group!?"

Seeing Ethan nod, Handsome Guy frowned deeply.

"This is bad. Something major must be about to happen. Otherwise, there's no way they'd mobilize so many people at once. It looks like I need to contact headquarters for backup immediately."

However, just as he finished speaking, a loud horn suddenly echoed throughout the ship. The entire vessel trembled violently before swaying briefly.

Handsome Guy rushed to the nearby window and looked outside, only to discover that the ship had already left the harbor and begun sailing away from the port.

"This is terrible! If I don't contact headquarters now, it'll be too late!"

As he spoke, he pulled out his phone and attempted to make a call, only to discover that the signal had been completely jammed.

"Damn it!"

Still unwilling to give up, he moved around searching for reception, but there was still no signal whatsoever.

Ethan also checked his own phone and confirmed that all communication signals had indeed been fully blocked.

Sighing heavily, Handsome Guy looked toward Ethan with a complicated expression.

"You should leave this place as soon as possible. I'm heading to the captain's room next. What happens from here on has nothing to do with ordinary civilians like you."

…

When Ethan returned to the main hall, he found Misty and Brock sitting at a dining table, devouring food ravenously. Meanwhile, Ash sat nearby acting completely unlike himself—quiet as a shy girl, his face full of gloom.

"What's wrong, Ash?" Ethan asked. "You look miserable. Did you lose the battle earlier?"

Ash looked up at Ethan and replied dejectedly,

"No. I actually won."

"Oh?" Ethan said curiously. "Then why do you still look so depressed? Did something happen?"
