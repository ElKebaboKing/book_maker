Seeing the battlefield descend into complete chaos, Ethan quickly issued new commands and reorganized the battle formation.

"Kirlia, Charmeleon! The two of you take on Murkrow!"

"Jolteon, Wartortle—Arbok is yours!"

"Ivysaur, go help Exeggutor and Gloom surround Tyranitar!"

Hearing his commands, the Pokémon immediately repositioned themselves. At the same time, Ethan glanced at Haunter and ordered:

"Haunter, take them down one by one!"

"Understood!"

Haunter vanished instantly, and when it reappeared, it was already beside Arbok.

At that moment, Arbok twisted its body to dodge a Flamethrower attack before immediately counterattacking, spewing a massive amount of acid toward the two Pokémon in front of it.

Charmeleon had nowhere to evade when Kirlia suddenly grabbed it and used Teleport to move both of them to safety.

Seeing Haunter silently appear without Arbok noticing, Athena shouted:

"Arbok! Watch out behind you!"

The moment Arbok turned its head, a Shadow Ball was already flying toward it, slamming directly into its body!

It cried out in pain, but Haunter's assault wasn't over yet. Stretching out its saliva-covered tongue, it licked Arbok across the face.

The attack not only dealt damage but also triggered paralysis!

"Perfect chance!" Ethan shouted. "Kirlia, use Magical Leaf! Charmeleon, use Fire Fang!"

The two Pokémon attacked simultaneously, both strikes landing cleanly on Arbok. Combined with the burn effect triggered by the Fire-type attack, Arbok collapsed from exhaustion before it could even scream.

"Nicely done!"

Just as Ethan planned to repeat the strategy and shift focus toward Murkrow, Apollo suddenly issued an order from afar:

"Tyranitar, use Dark Pulse!"

Tyranitar opened its massive jaws, unleashing waves of dark energy along with over a dozen dark projectiles!

But its target was not any of the Pokémon present—

It was Ethan and Erika themselves!

Although Exeggutor hurriedly used Psychic to intercept the attack, the gap in power between the Pokémon was simply too large. While part of the attack was neutralized, the remaining shockwaves still hurtled toward Erika!

"Look out!"

Ethan shouted as he threw himself toward Erika, tackling her to the ground. In that split second, the impact struck. Though they shared the damage, the immense force still sent the two of them tumbling several meters away!

By the time they finally stopped rolling, Ethan felt burning pain across his arms, thighs, and back. Bruises had likely formed all over his body, and even his mask had flown off to the side.

As for Erika in his arms, she fortunately wasn't seriously hurt, though her pale face showed she had been badly frightened.

Once she realized what had happened and looked at Ethan's face, her large eyes filled with gratitude.

"Thank you."

Her soft body still rested against him. Despite the situation, Ethan quickly said:

"Now's not the time for that. We can't let it keep attacking us!"

Erika nodded and hurriedly climbed off him, immediately commanding her Pokémon to counterattack.

By the time Ethan stood up and put his mask back on, Haunter had already defeated Athena's Murkrow.

However, Apollo's Tyranitar was unbelievably powerful. Even after enduring repeated attacks from the Pokémon, it still stood there like an immovable mountain!

Even more troublesome, after Athena was defeated, Apollo calmly pulled out another Poké Ball and released a second Pokémon.

It was a Pokémon completely engulfed in flames, including its tail and forehead. Its appearance resembled an ugly duck, its entire body burning like a giant fireball!

"It's Magmar!"

Ethan quietly warned.

Beside him, Erika nodded. She recognized the Pokémon as well, and the situation was even worse because Magmar's Fire typing gave it a complete type advantage over her Grass-type Pokémon!

However, Erika noticed Ethan's hand instinctively move toward an Ultra Ball at his waist. Her eyes narrowed slightly as she whispered:

"At this point, stop holding back. If you still have a trump card, now's the time to use it."

Ethan hesitated briefly and was just about to release Zapdos—

When suddenly, a nearby window shattered, and a man jumped inside from outside!

Flaming red hair. Black cape. Everything about him was instantly recognizable.

It was Lance!

After scanning the room, Lance smiled.

"Looks like I arrived at the perfect time!"

"Lance…" Apollo narrowed his eyes and stared coldly at him. "I didn't expect even you to show up."

Lance waved casually, completely unfazed.

"Hey now, Apollo. This isn't the first time we've fought each other. No need to look so surprised."

As he spoke, a massive Dragonite flew through the broken window and slammed violently into Tyranitar, smashing it straight into the opposite wall!

Seeing Lance join the battle, Ethan lowered his hand and asked:

"Mr. Lance, how's the situation on your side?"

Lance gave Ethan an "OK" gesture.

"Don't worry. The special forces have already broken in. This entire floor will soon be under full control. These guys won't stay arrogant much longer!"

Hearing that, Apollo and Athena exchanged glances, both sensing the situation had turned disastrous.

If only a few intruders had entered the base, they could simply eliminate them.

But if the main forces had already breached the facility, then continuing to waste time here was meaningless.

With that realization, the two wasted no time. Recalling their Pokémon, they immediately fled without looking back!

"They ran?"

Ethan finally let out a sigh of relief.

Now that he had calmed down, he could clearly hear fierce fighting and chaotic footsteps echoing throughout the hallways outside.

Recalling most of his Pokémon and leaving only Kirlia and Haunter beside him, Ethan stepped out of the cafeteria.

Outside, International Police special forces filled the corridors everywhere. Large numbers of Rocket Team members were either fleeing or being arrested. Their forces had completely collapsed.

Thinking about Sabrina and Handsome Guy, Ethan turned to search for them, only to see the two approaching together with Agatha and Nanu.

Meeting Sabrina's gaze, Ethan asked:

"What happened to the Matori Matrix?"

Before Sabrina could answer, Handsome Guy spoke first.

"We drove them back. Or rather, once they saw the special forces entering the base, they retreated on their own."

"So…" Ethan muttered thoughtfully, "we still failed to capture them this time?"

"Yeah…" Handsome Guy sighed helplessly. "After all, this is their headquarters. It's filled with traps and secret passages. Capturing them really isn't easy."

After fully securing Basement Level 1, they continued advancing, taking over Levels 2 and 3 before finally reaching Basement Level 4.

Just as everyone believed the operation was about to end perfectly, Handsome Guy pushed open the stairwell door—

Only to see a middle-aged man in a formal suit calmly seated on a luxurious sofa in the center of the vast underground hall, quietly watching them.

(End of Chapter)
