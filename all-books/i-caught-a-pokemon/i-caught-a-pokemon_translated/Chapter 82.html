Articuno's appearance was quite different from Zapdos's.

Unlike Zapdos's sharp, dry-looking feathers, Articuno's translucent blue plumage appeared beautiful and fluffy. Its eyes were crimson red, and its short beak sat beneath a crest formed by three dark-blue diamond-shaped feathers. Most eye-catching of all was its long ribbon-like tail!

Its tail, nearly as long as its body itself, flowed gracefully through the air as Articuno flew and circled overhead, making its every movement appear elegant and majestic.

The moment Ethan saw it, he made up his mind.

No matter what, he would capture it this time!

Pokédex: "Beep. Articuno, the Freeze Pokémon. Ice and Flying type. One of the legendary bird Pokémon. Its beautiful, transparent-looking feathers are said to be made of ice. It can easily freeze moisture in the air to create blizzards. Legend says that people lost in snowy mountains, moments before freezing to death, will sometimes see Articuno appear before them."

Without hesitation, Ethan took an Ultra Ball from his belt and released the Pokémon inside.

As blue light flashed and a sharp cry rang out, Zapdos appeared in midair!

Facing Articuno, the fellow legendary Pokémon met its gaze directly. No words were needed—their fighting spirits were already blazing.

This was a clash of ice and lightning, as well as a battle over territory and freedom itself!

"Articuno! You already know why we're here. So what do you say? Will you battle us!?"

This was Ethan's attempt to provoke it. And since they were already inside Articuno's territory, he had no fear of it fleeing or refusing battle.

Compared to when he had first faced Zapdos, Ethan now had far more confidence and composure thanks to his stronger lineup. Even so, for this capture battle, he still wanted Zapdos to test Articuno's strength first.

In response to Ethan's challenge, Articuno flapped its wings, sending a freezing gust directly toward him!

Seeing this, Kirlia immediately stretched out its hands and used Psychic power to neutralize the icy wind.

"So that's your answer." Ethan nodded. "Then we won't hold back either. Go, Zapdos!"

The moment his words fell, Zapdos eagerly charged forward. The two Pokémon instantly clashed in the air, and in the blink of an eye, lightning and frost engulfed the entire island!

In terms of typing, Zapdos was Electric and Flying, while Articuno was Ice and Flying. Both Electric- and Ice-type attacks could deal massive damage to the other.

"Zapdos, use Discharge!"

As Zapdos spread its wings, a thick bolt of electricity erupted from its body and blasted toward Articuno!

Not willing to lose out, Articuno flapped its own wings, forming countless icicles around itself before launching them toward Zapdos!

Explosions crackled throughout the air as shattered ice rained down onto Ethan below.

Kirlia waved its arms to block everything, while Haunter floated beside Ethan and said,

"It's way too dangerous here! We should move farther away before we get caught in the crossfire!"

Ethan nodded and retreated behind a distant ice pillar.

Beneath the rocky dome overhead, the battle continued relentlessly. The two Pokémon chased one another through the sky until, after several exchanges, Articuno suddenly seized an opportunity. Twisting its body midair, it kicked Zapdos fiercely with its claws!

Zapdos cried out in pain and crashed to the ground with a heavy boom. Before it could recover, Articuno followed up immediately—second strike, third strike—landing consecutive kicks on Zapdos!

"That's Triple Axel! Zapdos, fight back quickly!"

Blinding electricity suddenly erupted outward as violent currents surged wildly in every direction, attacking everything nearby!

At the critical moment, Zapdos decisively unleashed Thunderbolt to protect itself!

The electric current spread across the ice surface, instantly melting and shattering the frozen ground into dozens of fractured chunks. Hit by such intense electricity, Articuno let out a pained cry and crashed onto the ground, clearly suffering tremendous damage!

"Good, Zapdos! Keep attacking!"

Taking advantage of the opportunity, the prideful Zapdos soared into the air again, spinning its body as its beak glowed brightly before driving downward toward Articuno!

BANG!

The attack struck directly, sending Articuno flying backward!

But as expected of a legendary Pokémon, even after taking two direct hits, it still had not fallen. Though its body was covered in injuries, its spirit only seemed to grow fiercer with battle!

After regaining the air once more, Articuno rapidly flapped its wings. In the next second, freezing winds mixed with snow began sweeping through the cavern. As the winds intensified, a raging blizzard slowly formed around Articuno itself!

"So it's using Blizzard… Looks like we've completely enraged it now." Ethan laughed bitterly before shouting to Zapdos, "In that case, we absolutely can't lose either! Zapdos, let's use our ultimate move too!"

Hearing the command, Zapdos shot upward into the sky. With a sharp cry, dark storm clouds suddenly gathered beneath the cavern ceiling. In just seconds, the clouds thickened visibly, blocking out the sunlight reflected into the chamber and plunging the entire Ice Temple into darkness!

Within the clouds, flashes of lightning constantly flickered. A terrifying thunderstorm was brewing!

"And now…" Ethan looked from side to side. "Let me see which is stronger—Articuno's Blizzard or Zapdos's Thunder!"

Once both attacks reached their peak, the ultimate moves erupted almost simultaneously. Amid a dazzling bolt of lightning, roaring winds and swirling snow completely engulfed the two legendary Pokémon!

CRACKKKK!!!

The entire cavern trembled violently, as though the mountain itself had shaken!

The thunder that followed was deafening within the enclosed space. Ethan had barely managed to cover his ears before the raging snowstorm swept over him, blinding his vision.

Ice pillars and icicles shattered one after another, while broken shards of ice scattered across the entire island!

When everything finally settled, Ethan rubbed his eyes and looked back toward the center of the battlefield.

The storm and lightning had both disappeared. After fully enduring each other's attacks, both Pokémon had collapsed.

But upon closer inspection, their conditions were entirely different.

Zapdos lay flat on its back with its eyes tightly shut, clearly unconscious.

Articuno, meanwhile, panted heavily as it struggled back onto its feet before spreading its wings and taking to the sky once more!

A sharp cry rang out from its beak, as though proclaiming its victory.

Zapdos had lost!!

Ethan shook his head. However, he had already prepared himself mentally beforehand, so the result did not truly surprise him.

After all, there was still a noticeable difference in strength between the two legendary Pokémon.

Recalling Zapdos into its Poké Ball, Ethan released Jolteon instead before turning to the four Pokémon beside him.

"Alright. Even though Zapdos was defeated, our objective hasn't changed. So we're changing strategies now."

He grinned slightly.

"From here on out… everyone attacks together!"

(End of Chapter)
