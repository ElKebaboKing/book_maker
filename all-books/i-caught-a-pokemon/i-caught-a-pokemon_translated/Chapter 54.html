Facing the aggressive Team Rocket trio, Ethan casually waved his hand.

The next second, all of his Pokémon attacked with full force at once, sending a chaotic barrage of moves flying toward the Meowth robot.

"Huh!?"

The cockpit warning alarms instantly maxed out under the relentless assault. The steel shell could no longer withstand the damage, and after a violent explosion, Jessie, James, and Meowth were blown out before they even had time to escape, collapsing into the wreckage.

Blinking in stunned disbelief, the trio looked around at the many unfriendly eyes staring at them and laughed awkwardly, completely losing the arrogance they had displayed moments earlier.

Their dreams had been so beautiful… so why was reality this cruel?

They had only just made bold declarations—how had the situation reversed so completely so quickly?

Before they could say anything else, a strange gust of wind suddenly swept them into the sky, carrying them farther and farther away until they disappeared among the clouds.

At last… it's over.

Ethan let out a long breath and looked around.

The entire area was in ruins. Destroyed cargo containers and damaged buildings littered the scene, making it look as though a hurricane had swept through.

His Pokémon had all suffered varying degrees of injury as well.

Ethan first recalled Zapdos, and just as he was about to recall the others, Squirtle's body suddenly began glowing with white light.

It had accumulated enough experience during the battle and was finally evolving!

A few breaths later, Squirtle reappeared before Ethan in a brand-new form.

Compared to before, its appearance had not changed drastically, but it now had sharp fangs, fluffy ear-like fins resembling tiny wings, and a tail shaped like an ocean wave.

Ethan took out his Pokédex.

Pokédex: "Wartortle, the Turtle Pokémon. A Water-type Pokémon and the evolved form of Squirtle. Much stronger than Squirtle, Wartortle is extremely loyal. It is said to live for ten thousand years, and its thick, fluffy white tail is considered a symbol of longevity."

Ten thousand years?

Good grief. If he raised one of these, wouldn't it be able to attend the funerals of his descendants generation after generation?

At that moment, security guards could already be seen rushing over from the distance.

Not wanting to linger any longer, Ethan confirmed that his mask was still intact, recalled all of his Pokémon, and left without looking back.

That evening, Ethan arrived at the Pokémon Center and handed his Pokémon over to Nurse Joy for treatment before sitting quietly in a corner and taking out his phone.

There was a message from Looker sent half an hour earlier.

Looker: I've arrived safely in Saffron City. How are things on your side?

Ethan: I also made it back safely to the Pokémon Center. The Team Rocket members were driven back for now, though I don't know if they'll come after me again later.

Looker: Incredible! Fighting so many enemies by yourself… truly worthy of being the man I chose. So, are you absolutely sure you won't consider joining the International Police?

Ethan: Forget it. I don't want to go through things like this too many times. How's your condition?

Looker: I'm lying in a hospital ward right now. After the doctors treated me, my wounds have already been stitched up. I'm basically fine now—just need some rest. Seriously, thank you so much this time. The doctors just said that if you hadn't treated me in time, I probably wouldn't have survived!

Ethan's fingers paused over the phone.

After thinking for a moment, he sent Looker another message, revealing that Team Rocket's secret base was hidden beneath the Game Corner.

Since Team Rocket had already targeted him, exposing one of their bases could be considered a fitting return gift.

Those executives probably would never dream that the secret base they had operated for so many years would be exposed in such an unexpected way.

The moment the message was sent, Looker immediately called him.

As soon as Ethan answered, Looker's thunderous voice exploded through the phone.

"You're sure Team Rocket's base is really hidden there!? Is the intelligence reliable!?"

Even without seeing Looker's expression, Ethan could imagine just how shocked he must be from the tone alone.

Besides Looker's voice, Ethan could also hear a nurse beside him desperately trying to calm him down.

"Sir, please don't get so agitated, or your wounds might reopen…"

Once Looker had somewhat calmed down, Ethan continued,

"I'm not completely certain, but I'd say there's an eighty or ninety percent chance. As for how to enter the underground base, your people will have to investigate that yourselves."

After spending so much time together, Looker trusted that Ethan would never joke about something this important.

He slowly regained his composure and sighed emotionally.

"We've spent an unbelievable amount of effort and resources investigating this matter, yet you managed to discover it so easily in only half a day… Alright, I'm reporting this to headquarters immediately!"

Ethan certainly did not dare take credit for it.

After all, this was merely the advantage of prior knowledge from the games.

"Wait, don't rush yet." Ethan stopped him. "There's actually something even more important I haven't told you. But you need to promise not to be too shocked after hearing it."

"Relax." Looker laughed confidently. "At this point, nothing could possibly shock me more than discovering Team Rocket's base."

Hearing Looker sound so calm, Ethan told him that Giovanni was actually the leader of Team Rocket.

"WHAAAT!?!?"

Looker's nearly voice-cracking scream echoed throughout the entire Pokémon Center through the phone speaker. Even Nurse Joy in the distance turned around curiously.

For a brief moment, Ethan thought he had gone deaf from the volume.

After several seconds of silence, the nurse's horrified voice suddenly rang out again.

"Oh no, sir! Your stitches have reopened completely! Please stay still—I'll get the doctor immediately!!"

This was followed by frantic chaos and the hurried sound of footsteps rushing away.

After calming down slightly, Looker gasped heavily and asked in disbelief,

"Giovanni is Team Rocket's leader!? You mean the Viridian Gym Leader? The man known as Giovanni of the Earth!?"

Ethan quietly replied with a simple "Mm."

Looker clicked his tongue repeatedly.

After confirming the information with Ethan several times, his thoughts became a tangled mess.

The revelation sounded outrageous… yet strangely reasonable at the same time.

With that man's personality and methods, even if he truly were Team Rocket's boss, would it really be that surprising?

"This matter is far too serious! I need to contact my superiors immediately—no, forget that, I'm contacting headquarters directly right now!"

After hanging up, Ethan spent the night at the Pokémon Center.

Originally, he had expected Team Rocket to continue sending people after him, but the entire night passed without the slightest disturbance.

Then, early the next morning, after finishing breakfast and preparing to challenge the Celadon Gym, he received another call from Looker.

This time, however, Looker's tone sounded far more formal and serious than the night before.

"After discussions at headquarters last night, we've decided to investigate and launch a raid on Team Rocket's base. As for Giovanni's identity, the matter is far too sensitive. We'll need further verification before taking action. However, all of this requires extensive preparation. To avoid alerting the enemy, you should leave the city as soon as possible."

(End of Chapter)
