Yuu Town Harbor, located between Vermilion City and Maiden's Peak, had been nothing more than a simple harbor town until a few months ago.

However, because it was also situated by the sea, a wealthy investor had set his sights on the area and planned to build a super luxurious seaside hotel offshore, intending to imitate Blue Lagoon and transform this small town into another famous vacation destination in the Kanto region!

After leaving Vermilion City, Ethan Vale passed through the town and was about to head toward Maiden's Peak when he unexpectedly ran into Ash, Misty, and Brock once again at the harbor.

The three of them were lazily sitting by the shore, staring blankly at the endless sea as though lost in thought.

"What are you three doing here?"

Ethan suddenly spoke from behind them, startling all of them at once.

"Huh? Ethan!" Misty immediately stood up. Her earlier sluggishness vanished instantly, and she once again radiated youthful energy and vitality.

Brock greeted Ethan as well before saying helplessly, "We're waiting for the ferry. We were planning to take a ship to Fuchsia City on the other side of the sea, but we just missed it. If we want to catch the next one, we'll have to wait at least another three hours."

After chatting for a while, Ethan finally understood why they were still lingering here. After challenging the Celadon Gym, they had not only spent a day resting in Blue Lagoon, but had also caught a Primeape in the wild, solved a missing children case, and even defeated Team Rocket on Beauty Street, helping Brock obtain a Vulpix!

As far as travel experiences went, their journey had certainly been colorful and eventful.

Compared to everything else, however, Ash cared more about Gym Badges. After learning that Ethan had also obtained the Rainbow Badge, he invited him eagerly.

"Do you want to come with us and challenge the Fuchsia Gym?"

"Not yet." Ethan shook his head and politely declined. "I still have some things I need to take care of, so you guys should go ahead first."

At that moment, Misty suddenly noticed a Pokémon surfacing in the distant sea.

"Huh? Look over there! It's a Horsea! It's so cute!"

Everyone turned toward the ocean and saw a seahorse-shaped Pokémon poking half its head above the water while watching them.

However, Brock, who was good at noticing details, immediately spotted something unusual.

"That's strange. It looks injured."

Because the distance was so great, Ethan narrowed his eyes and looked carefully after Brock pointed it out. Only then did he notice a large bruise on Horsea's face.

What incredible eyesight!

Ethan could not help glancing at Brock. Was it because he kept his eyes squinted all the time, allowing him to focus better than normal people?

After noticing their attention, Horsea did not leave. Instead, it cried out several times before spraying a large amount of black ink onto the surface of the sea, seemingly trying to convey some kind of message.

As it continued spraying ink, the black stain gradually formed the images of two jellyfish on the ocean surface!

Seeing this familiar scene, Ethan immediately recalled the related anime plot. Looking into the distance, he could indeed vaguely see a luxurious hotel still under construction on the sea.

"It's drawing?" Ash stared at the ink in confusion. "But what does drawing those two jellyfish even mean?"

Perhaps because of her love for Water-type Pokémon, Misty took out a Poké Ball and aimed it at Horsea.

"It's injured, so no matter what, treatment comes first! Horsea! Get into the Poké Ball!"

Just as she was about to capture Horsea, a loud explosion suddenly sounded from the distance, drawing everyone's attention once again.

A small cargo ship that had just passed near the shallow-sea hotel seemed to have been attacked by something. An explosion erupted from the vessel, splitting the ship cleanly into two as thick smoke billowed into the air and the wreckage began sinking into the sea!!

The people onboard were caught completely off guard and all tumbled into the water.

"What happened!?" Ash and Brock both stood up in shock.

"Now's not the time to ask that!" Misty cried anxiously. "People fell into the water! We need to rescue them first!"

Whenever it involved anything related to water, Misty always became especially energetic and decisive. She quickly put away her empty Poké Ball and immediately released her three Pokémon.

"Goldeen, Staryu, Starmie! I'm counting on you to save those people!"

Ethan also released his Wartortle to help with the rescue, while Ash and Brock split up to search for assistance. Before long, they managed to borrow a speedboat and rushed toward the stranded sailors.

The speedboat only had limited seating, and there were not too many sailors in the water. With Misty and the others already helping, Ethan decided not to interfere unnecessarily. Instead, he stood in place and looked toward the bottom of the damaged ship.

Sure enough, he vaguely spotted a group of creatures glowing red beneath the water rapidly swimming away.

Just like in the anime, it really was a group of Tentacool taking revenge against humanity!

After a chaotic rescue operation, the sailors were finally brought back to the harbor. However, their condition was terrible. Their bodies appeared paralyzed, and none of them could move at all.

"W-We were attacked by Pokémon…" one sailor grabbed Brock's arm and said weakly. "Q-Quick… notify the president…"

Inside the office of a company at Yuu Town Harbor, Ethan and the others finally met the "president" mentioned by the sailor.

She was a short, ugly old woman with red hair styled into braided bangs.

Beside her stood four handsome young men wearing nothing but briefs, obediently attending to her every need.

"I heard you saved my company's sailors? Thank you very much, granny!"

After introductions, everyone learned that her name was Madam Gold. However, she had an eccentric personality, was extremely long-winded, and liked to end every sentence with the word "granny" as a verbal tic.

"That's strange…" Ash stared at her face. "Didn't we meet you before in Blue Lagoon?"

Unexpectedly, Madam Gold's expression changed dramatically the moment she heard that.

"You idiot! Even if I'm that woman's cousin, don't lump me together with her! Granny!"

"Cousin!?"

Recalling the Blue Lagoon storyline, Ethan could only shake his head helplessly.

He truly could not understand this world sometimes. There was a Madam Gold in Yuu Town Harbor and another Madam Gold in Blue Lagoon. It was hard to believe that two people who looked so alike were merely cousins.

Even Professor Oak had a cousin in the faraway Alola region who looked almost exactly like him.

And then there were the dozens upon dozens of Officer Jennys and Nurse Joys. Not only did they all look nearly identical, but they also worked in the same professions. Sometimes Ethan could not help wondering if they were the products of some kind of mass cloning technology!

The more he thought about it, the more terrifying it became.

At that moment, Madam Gold walked over to a model display of Yuu Town Harbor and explained proudly,

"I'm going to build a tourist resort here that surpasses Blue Lagoon, and construct a super luxurious paradise hotel on the sea! Granny!"

"But those Tentacool that came out of nowhere have been attacking my workers nonstop lately, disrupting construction and causing us enormous trouble! Granny!"

"I see you all used Pokémon to save people, so why don't you help me drive those jellyfish away? If you can solve this problem successfully, I'll reward you with one million! Granny!"

(End of Chapter)
