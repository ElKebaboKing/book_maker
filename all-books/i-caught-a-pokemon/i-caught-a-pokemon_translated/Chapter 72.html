Actually, being brought here by Dragonite was far from a rare occurrence. Whether it was Ash and Goh during their travels or ordinary shipwrecked sailors, many people had once been rescued by these Dragonite.

Hearing Ethan's thanks, the Dragonite all narrowed their eyes happily and let out cheerful cries.

Dragonite were naturally kindhearted Pokémon. Whenever they discovered ships in distress at sea, they would guide them safely back to shore. Their bodies were incredibly strong as well, and according to legend, they could circle the entire globe in only sixteen hours, making them one of the fastest flying Pokémon in existence!

Seeing that Ethan was safe, the Dragonite gradually dispersed, simply leaving him there without further concern. They neither monitored him nor paid close attention to his actions, seeming completely unconcerned that he might cause trouble on the island.

Seeing this, Ethan shook his head helplessly.

With personalities like these, it was no wonder their habitats had grown fewer and fewer, and even the Dragonite species itself had become increasingly rare. By now, encountering a wild Dragonite on any continent was nearly impossible!

Looking deeper into the island, Ethan could clearly see that besides these Dragonite, the forest was also filled with many of their unevolved forms—Dragonair and Dratini.

The Pokémon wandered about freely, searching for berries and living carefree lives. Even more surprisingly, none of the Dragonite showed any hostility or resistance toward humans.

Taking out his phone, Ethan confirmed exactly what he had expected—there wasn't even the slightest signal.

After casually taking a few pictures as souvenirs, Ethan shook the raindrops from his clothes and headed deeper into the island.

At that moment, Haunter emerged from his shadow.

Looking around at the Dratini and Dragonair, it clicked its tongue in amazement.

"You know, both Dratini and Dragonair have such slender, elegant bodies. So why do they become so chubby and change color after evolving into Dragonite?"

"There's nothing strange about that," Ethan replied with a shrug. "You can't explain this world using common sense anyway. Honestly, if you ask me, Milotic actually looks more like the evolved form of Dratini and Dragonair!"

"Oh?" Haunter asked curiously. "Why do you say that?"

"I'm not just talking about their appearance and body shape." Ethan paused before asking, "Do you know the Marvel Scale Ability?"

Haunter shook its head.

"Never heard of it. What's that?"

Ethan smiled.

"Pokémon with that Ability increase their Defense when affected by status conditions. It's an extremely rare Ability. And if you look at all Pokémon species, only Milotic, Dragonair, and Dratini can possess Marvel Scale. So you understand the connection now, right?"

Haunter revealed a meaningful grin.

"Ohoho! I get it now!"

Just like that, they continued wandering around leisurely, enjoying the island like tourists, until they eventually arrived beneath a waterfall.

The rushing water poured down into a crystal-clear lake. On the smooth rocks nearby, many Dratini had gathered together, playing, resting, and chasing one another. Clearly, this was their nesting area.

Upon seeing Ethan and Haunter, the Dratini surprisingly showed no fear at all and curiously approached them one after another.

Although Dratini were naturally shy Pokémon, with so many gathered together in a familiar home environment, they had become much bolder.

"Everyone, come out and play with them too!"

With a wave of his Poké Balls, Ethan released all of his Pokémon—including Zapdos.

The Dratini became extremely excited upon seeing so many unfamiliar Pokémon species they had never encountered before. They circled around them nonstop, while Ethan's Pokémon also seemed delighted, happily playing together with the Dratini.

At that moment, Ethan suddenly noticed a lone Dratini curled up quietly in a corner, staring over with a mixture of envy and fear.

Following his gaze, Haunter also spotted it and asked in confusion,

"Why is it staying there alone? Did the others isolate it?"

"It doesn't really look isolated," Ethan said thoughtfully. "It feels more like it's afraid of me."

As he spoke, Ethan carefully approached the Dratini.

Sure enough, the moment the Dratini saw him getting closer, it nervously retreated several steps.

Seeing this, Ethan could only give up and return to where he had been standing before.

Soon, footsteps approached as several Dragonite walked over from the distance. Seeing so many Pokémon accompanying the Dratini, they all nodded happily, and their gazes toward Ethan became even friendlier.

Once they arrived nearby, Ethan turned to Haunter.

"Help me ask them what happened to that Dratini."

Haunter turned around and communicated with the Dragonite. After a short while, it relayed the story to Ethan.

"To be honest, it's actually a pretty sad story," Haunter sighed. "That Dratini's mother left this island and never came back. Ever since then, it's become like this. It refuses to interact with others, refuses to leave its nest, and its personality has become more and more withdrawn."

What Ethan hated most were emotional stories involving separation and loss. Looking at the Dratini, his eyes filled with sympathy.

"Did its mother encounter some kind of accident?"

"Not at all," Haunter replied with a shrug. "Apparently she ran away with a Trainer's Dragonite. Some companions saw her a few months ago, and they said she seemed to be living very happily."

"…What?"

Ethan stared in complete shock.

How did a tragedy suddenly turn into a soap opera!?

"She abandoned her child and ran away with someone else!? Alright then, forget I asked anything."

Even though the mother was still alive, Ethan somehow felt even worse for the Dratini after hearing the full story.

Abandoned at such a young age and turned into an orphan—well, maybe not an orphan… more like an orphaned dragon?

No matter how he looked at it, the little Dratini truly deserved sympathy.

Thinking this, Ethan turned around, rummaged through his backpack, and pulled out a can of Pokémon food before approaching the Dratini again and opening the lid.

Instantly, the delicious aroma drifted toward it.

"There's no need to be scared. This stuff tastes really good. Come give it a try."

Nearby, Ivysaur tilted its head slightly, vaguely feeling that this scene looked strangely familiar.

The Dratini sniffed deeply several times, and its stomach soon growled loudly. After enduring the temptation for quite a while, it finally crawled out from its hiding spot, unable to resist the smell any longer.

Slowly and cautiously, it approached Ethan little by little. Once it confirmed Ethan meant no harm, it quickly snatched the can and hurried back into its nest, devouring the food eagerly.

Taking advantage of the moment, Ethan stepped a little closer and said softly,

"I honestly don't know how to comfort you… but life is unpredictable. Try to look on the bright side. At the very least, your mother seems to be living happily now, right?"

(End of Chapter)
