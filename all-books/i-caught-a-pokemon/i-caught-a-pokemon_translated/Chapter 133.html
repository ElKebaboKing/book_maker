After the opening ceremony concluded, it was time for the tournament draw.

Ethan Vale and Ash left the stadium and met up with Chloe Cerise, Misty, Brock, and the others before heading to the nearby draw hall.

Inside, five registration counters had been set up, where several cheerful, attractive staff members welcomed the Trainers as they entered.

This was an extremely important step.

For the weaker competitors, a lucky draw could mean facing easier opponents throughout the early rounds, allowing them to advance with far less difficulty.

Seeing Ethan's group approach, one of the staff members smiled.

"Good morning. May I please see your Trainer registration cards?"

Before Ethan could respond, a figure suddenly darted past him.

Brock rushed to the counter in a single leap.

"Ah, beautiful miss! Your lovely smile has already been registered in my heart! When do you get off work? Would you like to have lunch with me?"

"Here we go again..."

Misty covered her forehead helplessly and dragged Brock away from the counter.

Watching the scene, Chloe couldn't help but laugh.

It was the first time she had seen someone who usually acted so mature suddenly become so flirtatious.

Thinking about Brock's former identity as a Gym Leader, the contrast left her completely astonished.

Surely he didn't flirt with every female Trainer who came to challenge his Gym... did he?

Ethan handed his Pokédex to the staff member.

After entering a few commands into the computer, she quickly located both Ethan's and Ash's registration records.

Ash eagerly asked,

"So... who am I battling in the first round?"

"Don't rush, Ash," Brock said after freeing himself from Misty's grip and casually leaning against the counter again.

"Before your opponent is decided, the battlefield has to be selected first."

Then he flashed the staff member what he believed was a charming smile.

"Am I right?"

"Y-Yes..."

Clearly overwhelmed by Brock's enthusiasm, the young woman instinctively stepped back.

"For the preliminary rounds, there are four battlefields: Rock, Grass, Water, and Ice."

"After winning battles on those four fields, competitors who advance to the fifth round will move to the main stadium."

Ethan took out the tournament handbook that had been given to every contestant upon arriving at the Athletes' Village.

After flipping through a few pages, he looked at Ash.

"Everything she just said is already explained in this handbook."

"If you had spent a few minutes reading it, you'd already know all of this."

Ash scratched the back of his head awkwardly.

"Well... I've never actually opened it."

"I honestly don't even know where I threw it."

The staff member pointed toward a large button on the desk.

"Now, please press this button."

"Wherever the roulette stops will determine the battlefield for your first-round match."

Ash, being impatient as always, pressed it first.

The electronic display above them lit up with images of the four battlefields.

After spinning several times, the indicator finally stopped...

Water Battlefield.

The staff member entered the result into the computer.

The screen immediately changed, revealing Ash's opponent.

It was a man with long dark-green hair, dressed in flashy clothing and wearing an arrogant expression.

"So..."

Ash stared at the screen.

"That's my first opponent?"

The staff member smiled.

"Congratulations."

"You've drawn the Water Battlefield."

"You've also been scheduled for the third match, beginning at 2:00 p.m."

"Please don't be late."

Next came Ethan's turn.

Taking a slow breath, he pressed the button.

Beep... Beep...

The roulette spun repeatedly before finally stopping on a field covered in lush green grass.

At the same time, another Trainer's image appeared on the screen.

He was a blue-haired man wearing sunglasses.

Ethan didn't recognize him.

However, Ash, Misty, and Brock all exclaimed in surprise.

Ethan looked back.

"You know him?"

Misty nodded.

"His name is Damian."

"He's a really unpleasant guy."

"We met him shortly after we began our journey."

"And... Ash's Charizard used to belong to him."

So it's him.

Ethan immediately remembered.

He recalled the incident, but he had completely forgotten what Damian looked like.

"Does he have any particularly strong Pokémon?"

"Well..."

Misty looked toward Ash and Brock.

Seeing both of them shake their heads, she turned back apologetically.

"We didn't spend much time with him."

"So we don't really know what Pokémon he has now, or how strong he's become."

"That's alright."

Ethan smiled casually.

"Having information is always helpful."

"But going into an unknown battle has its own excitement."

After recording the results, the staff member looked back at Ethan.

"Congratulations."

"You've drawn the Grass Battlefield."

"Let's see..."

"Oh—you've been scheduled for the second match, which begins at 11:00 a.m."

"Please be sure not to arrive late."

"Got it."

"Thank you."

After the draw concluded, contestants had two hours to prepare before the first matches began simultaneously across all four battlefields.

Losers would be eliminated immediately.

Winners would return the day after tomorrow for the second round.

Fortunately, Ethan's and Ash's matches didn't overlap.

Otherwise, Misty, Brock, and Chloe would have faced the difficult decision of choosing which friend to cheer for.

Leaving the draw hall, they stepped onto a bustling shopping street.

Both sides of the road were lined with souvenir shops, luxury boutiques, dessert cafés, and specialty stores.

The two girls were dazzled by everything they saw.

Every store looked tempting enough to spend hours exploring.

The streets were also filled with reporters interviewing contestants.

Besides television crews from the Kanto region, Ethan even noticed several foreign journalists covering the event.

Scanning the crowd, he unexpectedly spotted Jessie, James, and Meowth.

Disguised as reporters, the three members of Team Rocket were secretly searching for Trainers carrying rare Pokémon.

They noticed Ethan as well.

However, after making eye contact, they quickly looked away.

None of them dared approach him or cause trouble.

Good.

Neither side wanted any unnecessary conflict.

Ethan ignored them and continued walking.

After only a few steps, however, a group suddenly blocked his path.

"Boss Ethan!!"

Looking closer, Ethan immediately recognized them.

They were the biker gang members he had met on the great bridge near Fuchsia City.

"Huh?"

"What are you all doing here?"

Masha and Misa hurried over with broad smiles.

"We came all the way here specifically to cheer for you, Boss!"

As they spoke, they proudly showed him the huge collection of cheering supplies they had prepared—flags, banners, loudspeakers, and plenty of other support gear.

"..."

Ethan was genuinely touched.

Although he had never intended to become too involved with them, they had traveled all this way just to support him on such an important day.

That kind of loyalty wasn't something just anyone could show.

He nodded sincerely.

"Thank you all so much."

"After my match is over, lunch is on me."

Misa laughed.

"Come on, Boss."

"We're your crew."

"Cheering for you is simply what we're supposed to do."

( End of Chapter )
