It didn't take Ethan long to find a staircase leading to the lower level.

Following it down, he passed through a security gate and entered a laboratory filled with pipes, machinery, and scientific equipment.

To his surprise, the three members of Team Rocket—who had disappeared during the storm—were sneaking around inside, fiddling with the machines.

"So you're alright," Ethan said casually. "I thought you'd sunk to the bottom of the ocean."

Hearing his voice, the trio spun around in alarm.

"Y-You!? What are you doing here!?"

"The same thing as you," Ethan replied with a shrug. "Just looking around."

Unlike Team Rocket, who looked as though they were facing a powerful enemy, Ethan casually walked past them without the slightest concern that they might attack. Instead, he curiously examined the equipment, touching one machine after another as if he had wandered into a museum.

Seeing that Ethan had no intention of causing trouble for them, the trio secretly breathed a sigh of relief.

Although they had crossed paths with him only a few times, countless stories about him had already spread throughout Team Rocket.

From defeating the Matori Matrix, to helping the International Police destroy Team Rocket's secret base, to surviving the combined assault of four Team Rocket Executives—those feats had made it clear that, unlike Ash and the other kids, Ethan was someone they definitely did not want to provoke.

Knowing they were no match for him, James and the others had no desire to interact with him any more than necessary.

When Haunter peeked out from Ethan's shadow and made a goofy face at them, while Gardevoir stared at them with obvious hostility, Meowth wisely—and rather cowardly—hid behind James and Jessie.

On a nearby monitor, a video played on an endless loop.

Jessie had accidentally activated it while tampering with the controls.

Ethan stopped to watch for a moment.

Only then did he realize that this castle had originally been the laboratory where Mewtwo had been created.

After destroying the facility, Mewtwo had eventually returned to the island and rebuilt the castle upon the ruins of the old laboratory, turning it into its own base.

Despite everything it said, this place was still the closest thing Mewtwo had to a home.

Just then, violent tremors shook the building from above.

Ash and the others had apparently already begun battling Mewtwo.

"Haunter, Gardevoir... destroy everything in here."

Not only was Haunter surprised by the order—even Team Rocket, who had been quietly preparing to leave, stopped in their tracks and pricked up their ears.

"Huh?" Haunter looked around. "You want to destroy the place?"

"Aren't we here to challenge the world's strongest Trainer? Sure, Mewtwo has gone a little overboard, but this is still someone else's home. Is it really okay to wreck it?"

While randomly operating one of the control panels, Ethan explained,

"This is called preparing for the worst."

"If we don't destroy all this now, we'll be the ones paying the price later."

"Look around at all these tanks and incubation chambers."

"If we let this continue, who knows how many more innocent clones will be created."

Hearing that, James suddenly spoke up.

"The Mewtwo you mentioned... that's the powerful Pokémon created here, isn't it?"

Ethan looked at him in surprise.

"You know about it?"

"Hey!" Jessie whispered urgently, covering her mouth with one hand. "James, why are you chatting with him? Let's just get out of here!"

But James ignored her completely and continued speaking to Ethan.

"I heard rumors about Mewtwo back at the headquarters."

"I know a little about what happened."

"A Pokémon created for someone else's ambitions... that's really pitiful."

"I never imagined this place was the original laboratory."

"If you destroy it, then you'll actually be doing something good."

James' words were unexpected, yet somehow completely fitting.

Just like their famous motto, the Team Rocket trio might call themselves villains, but they often ended up doing genuinely good deeds.

After saying that, James turned to leave.

Just before walking out of the room, however, he suddenly remembered something.

"Oh, right."

"When those three cloned Pokémon rode the elevator upstairs..."

"I think I saw a tiny Pokémon following them."

"It looked like a little pink cat."

"I think it was the legendary..."

"Mew?" Ethan asked.

James blinked before nodding.

"Yeah. I think it really was Mew."

So Mew had come after all.

It had probably sensed another being with a similar origin and came to see Mewtwo for itself.

"Thanks."

Ethan waved at them.

"I appreciate the warning."

"But you should leave this island as soon as possible."

"Honestly, this is the last place you should be."

The tremors overhead grew even stronger, shaking the entire facility.

When Ethan lowered his gaze again, James and the others had already disappeared without a trace.

"Alright."

"Now that everyone's gone, let's get to work."

Haunter and Gardevoir nodded simultaneously before launching into a full-scale assault.

Shadow Balls and psychic power rained down upon the laboratory until every single machine had been reduced to scrap.

Boom! Boom! Boom!

Explosions echoed throughout the room as thick smoke filled the laboratory.

Within minutes, the entire facility had become a field of twisted wreckage.

Then, without warning, hundreds of strange black Poké Balls floated out from a connecting passage and flew toward the upper level.

Aside from their black color, their most striking feature was the single red eye embedded on each one.

"Mewtwo's making its move already!?"

A bad feeling immediately rose in Ethan's heart.

As expected, only a few seconds later, the black Poké Balls came flying back down the passage.

Judging from the glowing red eyes, they had already stolen a large number of Pokémon from Ash and the others.

However, with the laboratory completely destroyed, the Poké Balls seemed confused.

Hovering helplessly in midair, they appeared unable to proceed with whatever came next.

"Quick! Destroy them and free the Pokémon inside!"

At Ethan's shout, Haunter and Gardevoir immediately targeted the floating Poké Balls.

One after another, the strange Poké Balls exploded apart, releasing the Pokémon trapped inside.

Venusaur.

Gyarados.

Rhyhorn.

Many belonged to Hayden, Claire, and Logan.

Even Ash's Charizard and Squirtle, Brock's Ninetales, and Misty's Golduck had been stolen by these bizarre Poké Balls.

What an incredible kind of Poké Ball...

Ethan barely had time to marvel before a boy's shout echoed through the connecting passage.

The next instant, Ash burst through the corridor, chasing after one last black Poké Ball.
