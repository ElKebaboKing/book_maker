At first glance, Vaporeon seemed to have completely merged with the water in the pond.

There was no way to determine its location with the naked eye. Even as it moved beneath the surface, it created neither ripples nor splashes.

What an impressive natural ability.

"Jolteon, stay alert!"

Jolteon stared intently at the surface of the pond.

Facing such an unfamiliar situation for the first time made it increasingly restless. It instinctively paced along the water's edge, unwilling to jump into the pond while its opponent remained hidden beneath the surface.

"Vaporeon, use Water Pulse!"

At Mikey's command, a sphere of water suddenly burst out of the pond and struck Jolteon in an instant.

Although Jolteon had been prepared, Water Pulse traveled far too quickly to dodge.

Splash!

Fortunately, Water Pulse's power wasn't overwhelming. Since Water wasn't super effective against Electric, the attack didn't deal excessive damage, nor did it inflict the unfortunate confusion effect.

Jolteon staggered back a few steps.

When it tried to counterattack, the briefly visible Vaporeon had already disappeared beneath the water once again.

Is it planning to stay hidden the entire battle?

Seeing this, Ethan made his decision without hesitation.

"Jolteon, target the entire pond! Use Discharge!"

Electricity crackled between Jolteon's stiff fur.

The next moment, a powerful electrical current surged into the water.

Crackle...!

The pond churned violently, resembling a pot of boiling oil.

Vaporeon attempted to leap out of the water to avoid the attack.

Even though its swimming speed had increased underwater, it was still too late.

Using the water as a conductor, the electricity instantly enveloped Vaporeon's entire body.

Brilliant arcs of electricity exploded across its body, nearly turning the entire pond golden.

Vaporeon cried out in pain.

When the electrical assault finally ended, it struggled onto the shore.

Its body was now covered in injuries.

"Don't give it a chance to recover," Ethan commanded. "Finish it, Jolteon! Use Thunder Fang!"

Jolteon opened its jaws as electricity danced across its fangs.

Pouncing forward, it bit down hard on Vaporeon.

After suffering multiple super-effective attacks in succession, Vaporeon could no longer endure.

It collapsed onto the ground.

Rainer raised his right hand and announced,

"Vaporeon is unable to battle! The winner is Jolteon!"

Outside the battlefield, scattered applause and murmurs rose from the spectators.

"Huh? It actually defeated Mikey's Vaporeon? This guy's pretty impressive!"

"Seriously. He doesn't look familiar, though. Is he from around here?"

Mikey gently picked up his unconscious Vaporeon and sighed.

"My Vaporeon lost. Compared to both mine and my brother's, your Jolteon really is superior."

"..."

Hearing that, Rainer shot his younger brother a helpless look.

You were the one who lost. Why drag me into this?

Still, he had to admit that, despite both being Jolteon, Ethan's clearly outperformed his own.

Ethan smiled politely.

"Your Vaporeon is excellent too. This battle gave my Jolteon valuable experience."

After all, Jolteon had enjoyed a type advantage, and its overall strength was noticeably greater than Vaporeon's.

Winning wasn't something worth boasting about.

"Then it's my turn!"

Pyro eagerly rushed into the Trainer's box, squeezing Mikey aside with his hip.

"My Flareon isn't at a type disadvantage against your Jolteon like Mikey's Vaporeon was. That's exactly why we'll be able to have a fair battle and finally determine whose Eeveelution is truly the strongest!"

Pyro's fiery personality matched his Pokémon perfectly.

Without even asking whether Ethan was ready, he had already sent out Flareon.

Among Eevee's many evolved forms, Flareon retained the greatest resemblance to Eevee itself.

Its fur was a brilliant fiery red, while fluffy cream-colored fur decorated its neck and forehead. Its large, bushy tail swayed proudly behind it.

Pokédex:

"Flareon, the Flame Pokémon. A Fire-type and one of Eevee's evolved forms after exposure to a Fire Stone. It stores inhaled air inside its flame sac, allowing it to produce scorching flames reaching temperatures of up to 3,100°F (1,700°C)."

Looking at the determined Flareon, Ethan lowered his head and quietly asked,

"Jolteon, can you keep fighting?"

Jolteon turned around and confidently nodded.

Although it had taken a Water Gun and a Water Pulse during the previous battle, its stamina remained strong.

There was no problem continuing.

"Good. Then I'll be counting on you once again."

At the same time, Pyro shouted,

"Flareon, use Fire Spin!"

Flareon unleashed a swirling vortex of flames, attempting to trap Jolteon.

However, thanks to its incredible agility, Jolteon effortlessly escaped the attack zone.

"What incredible speed!" Pyro praised. "But let's see how many times you can keep dodging! Flareon, keep attacking!"

"Jolteon, use Quick Attack!"

Jolteon's speed exploded as it dodged another Fire Spin before charging straight into Flareon.

The impact knocked Flareon backward.

Even so, it quickly regained its footing.

"Darn it!" Pyro frowned.

"Flareon, use Fire Fang!"

Flareon lunged forward, its jaws engulfed in flames, and clamped down on Jolteon.

The attack came from such close range that Jolteon had no time to evade.

Jolteon twisted desperately, trying to throw Flareon off.

But Flareon refused to let go.

No matter how hard Jolteon struggled, it couldn't shake its opponent loose.

Seeing Jolteon grimace in pain, Ethan shouted,

"Use Discharge!"

Brilliant electricity erupted from Jolteon's body and surged into Flareon.

The electric shock forced Flareon to release its bite and retreat.

After the brief exchange, both Pokémon glared at each other while breathing heavily.

However, having already fought one battle beforehand, Jolteon was clearly at the greater disadvantage.

"Flareon, use Flamethrower!"

Flames gathered inside Flareon's mouth before blasting forward.

Jolteon once again relied on its superior speed to dodge the attack.

Although Flamethrower missed Jolteon, it struck the nearby grass instead.

In an instant, the flames spread like wildfire, setting the entire grassy field ablaze.

Now, with the burning grass on one side and the pond on the other, the battlefield had been split into two completely separate sections.
