Knowing that rewriting the script would take quite some time, Ethan released all of his Pokémon except for his two "goofy birds," allowing them to play in the Gym's large swimming pool.

When Daisy, Violet, and Lily saw his Dratini, they immediately gathered around it in amazement.

"Is that a Dratini!?" Violet exclaimed as she gently stroked its head. "It's even rarer than its final evolution, Dragonite! Where in the world did you catch it?"

Brock nodded.

"After traveling all this time, the only Dratini we've ever seen was a mother-and-child pair—a Dragonair and a Dratini—in the Safari Zone near Fuchsia City. They're incredibly rare. I can't believe you actually have one. You're really lucky."

Although Ethan's Dratini had become much friendlier after spending time with Charmeleon, it was still shy around strangers. Seeing so many people surrounding it, it quickly hid behind Charmeleon.

Ignoring the chatter around him, Ethan simply reminded Gardevoir to keep an eye on the more mischievous Pokémon and make sure they didn't cause any trouble.

He then returned his attention to the script lying on the table and began reading it carefully.

Since it was only a short play, the script consisted of just over a dozen pages. Besides the basic plot and character introductions, the rest contained scene directions, dialogue, monologues, and narration.

Lily asked,

"Violet wrote this script. What do you think? Does it have any problems?"

Ethan nodded.

"From a storytelling perspective, it's a little too simple. It lacks twists, suspense, and dramatic highs and lows, so it isn't particularly engaging. That said, I understand why—it'll only be performed by a handful of actors, and even with the Pokémon involved, you're still short on cast members."

Watching him analyze the script so seriously, Misty rested her chin on her hands and asked curiously,

"Ethan, what kind of job did you have before becoming a Pokémon Trainer?"

Ethan shrugged.

"I was originally in college, but because of circumstances beyond my control, I had to drop out. After that, I ended up in the Kalos region and found a job there. I lived like that for a while until I realized I couldn't spend my entire life drifting aimlessly. That's when I decided to become a Pokémon Trainer and came here."

Circumstances beyond his control?

Everyone exchanged glances. Although Ethan didn't explain further, they all assumed something unfortunate must have happened to his family.

Ash asked,

"So what kind of job was it? What made you think it was so boring?"

"It actually wasn't boring at all," Ethan replied with a smile. "I worked at a film studio, coordinating shooting locations for different productions and helping various crews with whatever they needed. Oh, and speaking of that... a famous movie star actually wanted me to enter show business and become an actor."

Misty's eyes lit up.

"A famous movie star? Who?"

Ethan smiled.

"Have you heard of Diantha?"

"Diantha!?"

Daisy, Violet, Lily, Misty, and Brock all jumped to their feet in shock.

Only Ash looked completely confused.

"Who's Diantha?"

After calming herself, Daisy explained,

"Diantha isn't just a famous actress—she's also the Champion of the Kalos region! Many of her films are considered timeless classics."

"Exactly!" Violet said excitedly.

"She's my idol! I've watched Lumiose Holiday, The Elegant Lady, and The Princess dozens of times!"

Lily still looked as though she couldn't believe what she'd heard.

"Are you really talking about that Diantha?"

"Of course."

Ethan nodded.

"We met by chance, and she's always been very kind to me. My Gardevoir, back when she was still a Ralts, was actually a gift from her. She's also the one who encouraged me to become a Pokémon Trainer."

"Wow!"

The three sisters were thrilled.

"If you ever get the chance, please get her autograph for us!"

"No problem," Ethan laughed. "If you ever visit Kalos, I'll even introduce you to her. She may seem difficult to approach at first, but she's actually very friendly."

The conversation revolved around Diantha for quite some time before everyone finally returned to discussing the script.

Ethan tapped the pages thoughtfully.

"Actually, I think the story itself is aimed at a very young audience. We could add a few new elements to make it much more entertaining."

Daisy hesitated.

"But... like you said earlier, we don't have enough actors."

"So what?" Violet pointed at Ethan, Ash, and Brock.

"Why don't we have them help us?"

"Huh? Us?"

Lily covered her mouth with a smile.

"Come on, you've already helped this much. You might as well help all the way."

Ethan hesitated, but Ash and Brock looked eager.

"No problem! Leave it to us!"

Since they had already agreed, Ethan had no reason to refuse.

Daisy smiled at him.

"You've got the perfect look to play the prince. By the way, what kind of new ideas were you thinking of adding?"

Ethan looked around at everyone.

"Well, let's all brainstorm together. The more ideas we have, the better."

His suggestion immediately got everyone talking.

Ash eagerly said,

"I think we should add more Pokémon battles! That would make it way more exciting!"

Ash's idea clearly represented what most kids his age wanted to see.

Ethan nodded and quickly wrote several notes, deciding to include more Pokémon battles.

Brock narrowed his eyes and said as casually as possible,

"I think the ballet performances should stay too. In fact, Daisy and her sisters should get even more stage time."

"Hmm..."

Ethan nodded again.

"Brock's opinion represents the tastes of a very large audience... especially adult men. We definitely have to respect that."

He continued writing.

"I also think the dialogue should be more humorous, and even the Pokémon should have funnier expressions and movements."

Meanwhile, when Misty heard Ethan would be playing the prince, her heart began pounding.

She shyly whispered,

"Then... maybe we could add some romance too? Like... hugging... maybe even... kissing..."

Violet instantly saw through her intentions and grinned mischievously.

"Of course! Since our little Misty asked for it, how could we possibly refuse?"

Misty's face instantly turned bright red.

She secretly glanced at Ethan, only to find him still concentrating on writing, seemingly unaware of her hidden intentions.

Quietly, she let out a sigh of relief.

After everyone's discussion, the final casting was decided.

Ethan would play the male lead—the prince.

Misty would play the female lead—the Mermaid Princess.

Brock would play a loyal sailor serving the prince.

Ash, dressed as a girl, would play the princess's maid.

The villains would be played by Lily as a fierce female pirate and Violet as an evil witch.

As for Daisy, she would play a merchant. Since her role had the fewest scenes, she would also serve as the narrator and overall stage coordinator.

With that, the script was finally complete and ready for rehearsals.

Ethan even gave it a brand-new title:

"Cerulean Gym's Annual Underwater Soap Opera Extravaganza!"

( End of Chapter )
