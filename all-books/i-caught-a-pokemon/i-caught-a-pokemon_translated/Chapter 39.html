At that moment, footsteps echoed through the hall as a middle-aged man approached.

He wore a baseball cap, had a thick beard, and was dressed casually.

"What can I do for you?" he asked.

"Hello." Ethan greeted him politely. "I'm here to challenge Gym Leader Sabrina. Is she here?"

The man nodded, his gaze shifting toward Kirlia.

"So you also like Psychic-type Pokémon? But judging from you personally, you don't seem to have any talent for awakening psychic powers."

"Huh?" Ethan looked puzzled. "I don't need to perform magic tricks. What would I need psychic powers for?"

"What did you say!? Magic tricks!?" The man looked as though he nearly choked on his own breath.

After coughing twice, he turned and walked deeper into the Gym.

"Follow me then. But don't blame me for not warning you—without psychic powers, it's impossible to bring out the full strength of Psychic-type Pokémon!"

Unable to bring out their full strength?

Ethan gently rubbed Kirlia's head but did not argue immediately.

The man was basically trying to say that without psychic abilities, a trainer could not truly connect mentally with Psychic-type Pokémon.

That might indeed affect most trainers and their Psychic Pokémon.

But for Ethan and Kirlia, it was not an issue at all.

After passing through a long corridor, Ethan arrived at the battlefield.

The floor was paved with marble tiles. Rows of stone pillars stretched all the way to the ceiling, and burning torches lined the arena.

Rather than a Gym, the place felt more like an ancient temple.

On the opposite side of the battlefield sat a beautiful woman with long black hair, pale skin, and a tall figure. She wore a tight red leather dress along with black stockings and quietly observed him from her chair.

This was the Saffron Gym Leader—Sabrina.

"Sabrina, another challenger has come for you," the middle-aged man announced before walking to the center of the battlefield to serve as referee.

Sabrina's body slowly floated upward before drifting gently toward the trainer's platform.

Simply relying on her own psychic powers to accomplish such a thing made Ethan both envious and amazed.

"I already foresaw your arrival," Sabrina said in a calm and emotionless voice, as though very little in the world could stir her feelings. "And besides psychic energy, your presence also carries the irritating scent of mischief."

As expected of a woman with powerful psychic abilities—she could even sense Gastly hiding within his shadow!

Because her story had left such a deep impression on him, Ethan still remembered it clearly even now.

When Sabrina was little, her immense psychic abilities had already awakened naturally. Unfortunately, her obsession with training those powers gradually caused her to shut herself away emotionally.

She made no friends, destroyed her own home, drove away her father with psychic powers when he tried to stop her, and even transformed her mother into a doll.

From that moment onward, Sabrina's personality split into two sides.

One was the overwhelmingly powerful Sabrina who refused to lose to anyone.

The other was a lonely Sabrina who loved pranks, longed for companionship, and desperately wished for friends.

Fortunately, Ethan had arrived at the perfect time.

Just a few days earlier, Ash had brought a Haunter from Lavender Town's Pokémon Tower, whose antics successfully restored Sabrina to normal.

At this moment, although she still appeared cold and emotionally distant, she was no longer completely closed off from the world. She had reconciled with her family, and occasionally, smiles even appeared on her face now.

Thinking of this, Ethan glanced briefly at the middle-aged man.

That should be Sabrina's father, right?

"May I ask…" Ethan refocused his thoughts. "What are the battle rules?"

Sabrina replied,

"A one-on-one battle. If you defeat my Pokémon, your challenge will be considered successful."

As she spoke, Sabrina slowly took out a Poké Ball.

A flash of red light appeared, and a Pokémon resembling a golden fox with a long mustache materialized on the battlefield.

Just as expected—it was the Kadabra that accompanied Sabrina in the anime!

The Pokédex spoke:

"Kadabra, the Psi Pokémon. A Psychic-type and the evolved form of Abra. Strange phenomena such as clocks running backward occur near Kadabra. It emits special alpha waves from its body that can cause headaches and discomfort to nearby enemies. Kadabra also has extremely high standards for trainers and will not acknowledge trainers lacking strong mental power. Additionally, it is said that without its spoon, Kadabra can only use half of its usual psychic abilities."

Ethan closed the Pokédex and pointed forward.

Kirlia slowly walked onto the battlefield.

"You're not using your Ghost-type Pokémon?" Sabrina asked.

Ethan shook his head without explaining further.

Gastly was simply too strong, and Ghost-types also held a type advantage. Using it against Kadabra would neither provide proper training for his Pokémon nor leave Sabrina with any chance to fight back.

At that moment, a Haunter suddenly floated out from the side.

It circled around Sabrina briefly before seemingly noticing Gastly hidden within Ethan's shadow. It immediately floated over excitedly.

Seeing it, Gastly also emerged from Ethan's shadow. The two Pokémon floated together and began making funny faces at each other cheerfully.

Haunter was Gastly's evolved form, and despite meeting for the first time, the two immediately got along perfectly.

It seemed this evolutionary line truly did love playful mischief.

But…

This should be the Haunter Ash brought from Pokémon Tower, right?

So it never returned to Lavender Town and instead chose to stay by Sabrina's side.

Once both Pokémon on the battlefield were ready, and seeing that Sabrina had no intention of attacking first, Ethan made the opening move.

"Kirlia, use Psychic!"

Kirlia raised both hands as Psychic activated instantly.

An invisible force surged toward Kadabra.

Sabrina never spoke a single word, yet Kadabra immediately lifted the arm holding its spoon and unleashed its own Psychic attack.

The two psychic forces collided in midair, erupting in brilliant light.

However, as both sides continued pouring strength into their attacks, Kirlia's expression gradually became strained.

Even though both Pokémon had evolved once, Kirlia's psychic power was still inevitably being suppressed by Kadabra!

In the end, Kadabra's Psychic struck Kirlia directly, forcing it to stagger backward several steps.

Fortunately, since both Pokémon shared the Psychic typing, their attacks did not deal especially significant damage to one another.

"Kirlia, use Magical Leaf!"

At Ethan's command, green light gathered in Kirlia's hands.

Immediately afterward, countless glowing Magical Leaves shot rapidly toward Kadabra.

Sabrina's expression remained completely unchanged.

Yet at the critical moment, Kadabra suddenly used Teleport and vanished, avoiding the attack entirely.

Without speaking aloud, Sabrina could communicate telepathically with Kadabra and issue commands directly through their mental connection.

And that…

Was Sabrina's true strength.

(End of Chapter)
