Ethan took out a Poké Ball and slowly held it out toward Gastly.

Gastly did not hesitate. Its body touched the button on the Poké Ball, and with a flash of red light, it entered the ball.

The Poké Ball shook slightly, and with a soft click, it finally became still.

He had really captured it successfully!?

But before Ethan could celebrate, the Poké Ball that had just completed the capture suddenly opened again, and Gastly floated back out on its own.

"I seriously can't stand the environment in there. It's dark and cramped," Gastly complained while swaying its body. "I'm used to living freely. I'd rather stay outside without restraints."

"Then don't stay inside the Poké Ball," Ethan said casually. "Honestly, I don't really like traveling alone either. Having Pokémon by my side is the best."

Gastly nodded and floated back toward Maiden's Peak.

A ghostly image of a woman slowly drifted out from the rock.

It was the real spirit of the woman!

The spirit spoke softly,

"Thank you, Gastly."

"No need to thank me." Gastly smiled. "I only wanted to let this legend, which people were gradually forgetting, return once more to their memories."

The spirit clasped her hands together.

"For all this time, I have remained here, waiting for the day I can reunite with him. No matter how long it takes, I will continue waiting."

Gastly moved aside slightly so the spirit could see Ethan.

"You probably heard everything from earlier. From now on, I'll be leaving this place to travel. Perhaps somewhere out there, I'll encounter your lover. When that day comes, I'll definitely deliver your feelings to him!"

"Thank you so much!"

The woman's voice carried a trace of joy as she slowly drifted backward and disappeared into Maiden's Peak.

…

By the time Ethan returned to the Pokémon Center, the sky was already beginning to brighten.

The moment he stepped into the warm room, the exhaustion he had been forcing himself to suppress instantly overwhelmed him, and his eyelids became unbearably heavy.

Ash was sleeping soundly, while Brock, who had already slept too much during the day, opened his eyes when he heard the noise.

After sufficient rest, Brock's complexion looked far better than before.

"Why did you come back so late?"

Yawning, Ethan replied,

"I went to settle the matter with the ghost while it was still nighttime."

"Oh, right." Ethan suddenly remembered something and added, "Gastly feels pretty apologetic about what happened earlier, so I figured it should apologize to you personally."

Brock stared in shock.

"You captured it!?"

Ethan did not answer directly.

Instead, from within his shadow, a black sphere wrapped in purple mist suddenly floated out.

"Waaah!"

Brock jumped in fright and cried out loudly.

Hearing the sound, Pikachu rubbed its eyes and poked its head up curiously to look over, while Ash merely turned over and continued snoring peacefully.

"You small-eyed guy," Gastly cackled mischievously, "besides being a pervert, you're not very brave either. What happened earlier was just a prank, so don't take it personally."

Brock had always been easygoing by nature. He had not truly minded the incident to begin with, and after hearing Gastly apologize, any remaining discomfort completely disappeared.

Ethan took out his phone and checked the time.

"I have no idea how long I'll sleep once I lie down. When Ash wakes up later, you guys should go ahead first. I'm planning to stay here for another day or two. If we get the chance in the future, we'll meet again."

"Aren't things already settled?" Brock looked confused. "Why stay longer?"

Although Ethan did not explain much, Brock still nodded.

"Alright then. We'll head out first."

After quickly washing up, Ethan collapsed into bed and immediately fell asleep.

When he opened his eyes again, it was already afternoon.

The past few days of excessive exhaustion and reversed day-night routines had completely disrupted his body clock. Even now, he still did not feel fully refreshed.

Ash and the others had already left long ago.

Originally, after hearing Ethan planned to remain here for a few more days, Misty had wanted to stay with him as well. But since Ethan never invited her directly, she ultimately gave up and continued traveling with Ash and Brock, though she kept looking back repeatedly before leaving.

As for why Ethan wanted to remain here, it was naturally because he hoped this beautiful legend would continue to be passed down properly.

Personally experiencing these events had made him truly believe that there really was such a thing as everlasting love in this world.

However, Gastly's previous methods were not ideal. Not only were they inefficient, but they also failed to truly spread the legend effectively.

Now that Gastly had become his Pokémon, Ethan believed he should do whatever he could to help preserve the story.

He wrote down everything that had happened since arriving at Maiden's Peak. He also visited the Maiden Shrine and learned more from the old priest about the love and sorrow tied to the place over the past two thousand years.

Combining all of it together, revising and polishing the details carefully, he eventually created a tragic yet heartwarming story over six thousand words long.

He recalled that during previous conversations with Chloe, she had once mentioned that her mother, Yoshino, had taught herself drawing and dreamed of becoming an illustrator.

So Ethan sent the story to Chloe by email and asked her to pass along a request, hoping Yoshino could create illustrations for it.

Chloe quickly replied with an "OK" emoji.

Not long afterward, an unfamiliar number called him.

When Ethan answered, a cheerful woman's voice came from the other side.

"Hello, this is Yoshino Cerise. Am I speaking to Mr. Ethan?"

Knowing this was Chloe's mother, Ethan quickly responded politely,

"Hello, ma'am. Yes, this is Ethan."

Yoshino Cerise said,

"Chloe just told me about everything. Your story was written beautifully. I had heard about the legend of Maiden's Peak before, but I never imagined it was actually real!"

Ethan smiled.

"It does sound unbelievable, but I really did see the woman's spirit with my own eyes. However, I hope you can keep this secret, ma'am. Otherwise, Maiden's Peak could attract people with bad intentions."

Yoshino replied,

"I understand. It's just like some of my husband's research projects—they also need confidentiality. From what Chloe told me, you'd like me to help create some illustrations?"

"Yes." Ethan hesitated briefly. "I'm really troubling you with this. But after learning the truth behind everything, I honestly feel this legend deserves to continue being passed down."

Yoshino laughed softly.

"It's no trouble at all. You don't need to be so polite—you're also part of the Cerise Laboratory family, after all. Though personally, I think instead of simple illustrations, turning it into a manga would work even better."
