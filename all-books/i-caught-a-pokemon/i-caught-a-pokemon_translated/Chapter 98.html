Ethan Vale looked down from the bridge.

A speedboat was racing toward them at incredible speed, and aboard it were none other than the five members of the Matori Matrix!

"Tch. Persistent bastards. They practically brought everyone."

When the boat reached the bridge, Kane took the lead. Using grappling hooks, the Matori Matrix members easily climbed up onto the bridge.

As they landed on the roadway, Apollo looked at Ethan and said,

"Originally, they were just backup forces. We planned to have them intercept you if you tried to escape. But you didn't run—you actually fought back. Seems we prepared more than necessary."

As the Matori Matrix released their Pokémon, the situation became extremely unfavorable for Ethan.

Not only was he outnumbered, but they also had powerful Pokémon such as Tyranitar and Aggron.

More importantly, the other four Matori Matrix members had clearly prepared specifically to counter Ethan's Zapdos.

The Pokémon they released were all Flapple.

These small Pokémon stood only about thirty centimeters tall. Their yellow-green bodies were wrapped in apple-like shells. As Grass- and Dragon-type Pokémon native to the Galar region, it was hard to imagine how much effort Team Rocket had spent obtaining them.

Clearly, they had made extensive preparations to neutralize Zapdos.

"Flapple, use Dragon Breath!"

High above, the four Flapple surrounded Zapdos, continuously firing streams of dragon energy.

After taking several hits, Zapdos became furious and instinctively unleashed Thunderbolt.

Unfortunately, because of the type disadvantage, the attack dealt only minimal damage.

"Hahaha!"

Lance burst into laughter.

"Your Zapdos is completely useless now, isn't it?"

Meanwhile, Aggron's massive body advanced toward Ethan like a steel tank.

Left with no choice, Haunter had to leave Ethan's side and engage it.

"So you've really got me cornered today, huh?" Ethan said calmly.

"To tell you the truth," Lance replied smugly, "we've already stationed people at both ends of the bridge. Whether it's police officers or passing Trainers, nobody is coming to save you."

"I see."

Surprisingly, Ethan seemed even calmer after hearing that.

"That actually makes me feel a lot better."

As he spoke, he pulled a second Ultra Ball from his belt.

The moment Apollo saw it, a sense of unease crept into his heart.

Another Pokémon?

He didn't know what trump card Ethan still possessed, but he knew one thing:

Whatever Ethan intended to do, he couldn't be allowed to succeed.

"Tyranitar! Hyper Beam!"

Tyranitar opened its enormous jaws and fired a terrifying beam of destructive energy.

But Gardevoir suddenly teleported in front of Ethan and created a psychic barrier, shielding him from the attack.

At the same time, Ethan threw the Ultra Ball into the air.

"Come out, Articuno!!"

A flash of blue light erupted.

A magnificent bird Pokémon with beautiful crystalline feathers appeared in the sky.

The moment it arrived, the surrounding temperature dropped by more than ten degrees.

"Articuno!?"

Athena let out an incredulous scream.

"That's impossible! How does he have Articuno too!?"

"Our intelligence never mentioned this," Apollo narrowed his eyes. "Did he catch it recently?"

Ethan pointed toward the Flapple.

"Articuno, take care of those Flapple first! Use Blizzard!"

Articuno spread its wings.

A freezing wind filled with snowflakes suddenly swept across the battlefield.

The gale intensified rapidly, forming a massive snowstorm around Articuno.

Within moments, all four Flapple were trapped inside.

Countless snowflakes struck them relentlessly with overwhelming force.

When the Blizzard finally subsided, the four Flapple fell straight toward the bridge.

Blizzard, an Ice-type move, was four times effective against Grass- and Dragon-type Flapple.

Combined with Blizzard's immense power, the attack instantly knocked them all out.

Freed from their harassment, Zapdos soared alongside Articuno.

With the two Legendary Birds fighting together, the battle's momentum shifted completely.

"Zapdos! Articuno!"

"Show them your true power!"

"Use Blizzard!"

"Use Thunder!!"

The two Legendary Pokémon climbed even higher into the sky.

With two resonant cries, massive storm clouds rapidly gathered overhead.

The clouds grew thicker and darker by the second, streaked with flashes of lightning.

At the same time, powerful winds carried countless sparkling snowflakes down from above.

The sky darkened like the bottom of a black cauldron.

Lambda subconsciously caught a snowflake.

It felt icy cold—and strangely tingled in his hand.

"We can't let them finish!"

"Attack those two Pokémon!"

At Apollo's command, Team Rocket's Pokémon unleashed a barrage of attacks toward the sky.

Unfortunately, Zapdos and Articuno were flying far too high.

Every attack fell short and dissipated harmlessly.

Taking advantage of the opportunity, Ethan recalled all of his Pokémon.

The snowfall grew heavier, coating the bridge in a thin layer of snow.

Then the storm reached its peak.

A dazzling bolt of lightning crashed down.

At the same time, a hurricane filled with razor-sharp snow swept across the bridge.

CRACK!!!

The lightning struck with such speed that nobody could react.

The electricity instantly melted the snow covering the bridge, turning it into vapor.

The immense electrical energy spread through the moisture, engulfing every Team Rocket Pokémon at once.

Bzzzzzt—!

Brilliant electricity exploded across the entire bridge.

Even from a considerable distance away, Ethan had to shield his eyes.

The lightning continued raging for several moments.

Finally, the electricity and snowstorm faded.

Silence returned.

Every Team Rocket Pokémon lay unconscious on the ground.

Only Tyranitar remained standing.

Its body was covered in black scorch marks, smoke rising from its mouth as it struggled back to its feet.

"How is this possible...?"

Athena stared in disbelief.

"Damn it!"

Lance cursed.

"Our entire team got wiped out!?"

Seeing the situation clearly, Apollo made an immediate decision.

"This operation has failed."

"Everyone, recall your Pokémon and retreat immediately!"

"Retreat?"

Athena clenched her fists.

"We invested so many resources and brought so many people! Are we really going to leave with our tails between our legs?"

"There's no other choice."

Apollo fixed his gaze on Ethan.

"Our intelligence was seriously inadequate. We completely underestimated his strength."

He paused before continuing.

"I never imagined that in just a few days, he would somehow acquire an Articuno as well."

( End of Chapter )
