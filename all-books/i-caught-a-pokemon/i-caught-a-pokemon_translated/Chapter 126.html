As they leisurely enjoyed the scenery along the road, Ethan explained the various Pokémon they encountered to Chloe, sharing facts and interesting details about each one.

Although Chloe had very little experience with Pokémon before, she was exceptionally intelligent. Most of the time, Ethan only needed to mention something once, and she would remember it perfectly.

Little by little, she went from knowing almost nothing about Pokémon to having a solid basic understanding.

Thus, the two of them leisurely made their way to the outskirts of Celadon City. They had originally planned to bypass the city and continue westward, but when they reached the open fields outside the city, they unexpectedly ran into someone familiar.

It was a beautiful young woman around eighteen or nineteen years old with black bob-cut hair, dressed in an elegant furisode kimono.

Just from her back, her attire, and her graceful demeanor, Ethan recognized her immediately.

It was Erika, the Gym Leader of the Celadon Gym.

At the moment, she was accompanied by an Eevee. A basket rested beside her as she crouched near a patch of flowers, carefully trimming and digging with a pair of gardening scissors and a small trowel.

As Ethan and Chloe approached, the Eevee noticed them first and let out a few cautious cries.

Hearing the sound, Erika turned around. The moment she saw Ethan, surprise appeared on her face.

Before she could speak, Ethan smiled in astonishment.

"Erika? What a coincidence! What are you doing out here?"

Standing up gracefully, Erika looked at him with her bright eyes, her voice as gentle as ever.

"I'm collecting some flowers to take back to the Gym for cultivation. What about you? Where are you headed?"

Collecting flowers?

Only then did Ethan remember that besides being the Celadon Gym Leader and the owner of a perfume shop, Erika was also an accomplished flower arranger.

"At this time of year, of course I'm heading to the Indigo Plateau to participate in the Pokémon League Conference."

Looking at the vibrant flowerbed before her, Ethan smiled.

"I always assumed the flowers you used during your classes were grown at the Gym. I didn't expect you to come into the wild to gather fresh flowers like this."

"The Indigo Conference? Has it already come around again?" Erika lowered her head to arrange a bouquet before smiling softly.

"I thought you would simply sail from Vermilion City."

Speaking of flowers, she gently stroked one of the blossoms.

"Although flowers grown in greenhouses are often more colorful and visually beautiful, I still prefer wildflowers that grow naturally. Having survived nature's own selection, they possess a unique fragrance that cultivated flowers simply can't replicate."

After saying that, Erika turned her attention toward Chloe.

"And this lovely young lady is...?"

"Let me introduce you."

Ethan stepped aside and gestured for Chloe to come forward.

"This is Chloe Cerise, the daughter of the renowned Professor Cerise of the Kanto region. She's still a student. Chloe, this is Erika. She may be young, but she's the Gym Leader of Celadon City."

"It's a pleasure to meet you," Erika said with a graceful bow.

Startled, Chloe quickly imitated her and bowed politely as well.

"It's nice to meet you too, Erika. My name is Chloe."

Watching the two girls exchange such formal greetings, Ethan couldn't help rubbing the back of his head.

In this day and age, seeing such traditional etiquette felt rather unusual.

Looking at them carefully, he noticed they shared remarkably similar temperaments.

Neither possessed the kind of breathtaking beauty that stunned people at first glance, yet both had delicate features and the sort of timeless elegance that became more attractive the longer one looked.

While the two girls chatted, Chloe's Yamper enthusiastically ran over to Erika's Eevee.

Fortunately, Eevee wasn't shy. Seeing that its trainer clearly knew these two humans, it quickly relaxed and happily began playing with Yamper.

Glancing at Eevee, Ethan asked,

"I don't remember seeing this Eevee before. Is it a new partner?"

Erika nodded.

"After meeting your Eevee, I completely fell in love with the species. I searched for a very long time before finally finding this one."

She then glanced up at the sky.

"Oh, it's already noon. Would the two of you like to try my homemade lunch?"

"A lunchbox? You made it yourself?"

Hearing those words, Ethan's eyes immediately lit up.

"An opportunity this rare? Of course I'd love to try it!"

Ever since arriving in this world, besides catching Pokémon and battling, Ethan's greatest hobby had become food.

Unfortunately, his cooking skills weren't particularly impressive. Even after traveling for so long, the meals he prepared could only be described as decent.

Erika spread a picnic cloth over the grass before taking neatly packed lunch boxes and delicate sweets out of her basket, arranging them one by one.

There were many different varieties of food, though only one or two pieces of each.

Every item was exquisitely crafted.

Even the way Erika arranged the dishes was gentle, graceful, and meticulously organized, clearly reflecting excellent upbringing and refinement.

Unable to help herself, Chloe remarked,

"Erika... you really seem like a noble young lady."

Smiling warmly at her, Erika replied,

"You don't have to call me 'Miss.' Since you're Ethan's companion, you can simply call me Erika—or if you'd like, you can call me 'Big Sister Erika.'"

As she spoke, Erika took out a thermos and looked at Ethan.

"Would you like some tea?"

When Ethan nodded, she unscrewed the lid and was about to pour some before giving an embarrassed smile.

"Ah, I'm sorry. I wasn't expecting to meet the two of you today, so I didn't bring any extra cups."

"No problem."

Ethan smiled as he and Chloe each took out their own travel cups.

"Just pour us a little."

Using the thermos lid as a cup, Erika poured tea for them.

The three of them sat together on different corners of the picnic cloth, sipping tea while enjoying the lunch Erika had prepared.

Ethan took the first sip.

The tea was pleasantly warm.

It tasted wonderful going down, and even after swallowing it, a lingering fragrance remained in his mouth.

Unfortunately, he didn't know much about tea.

He could only tell it was some variety of green tea, but beyond that, he couldn't say much.

Setting down his cup, Ethan picked up one of the tea cakes.

The moment it entered his mouth, its soft, delicate texture instantly won him over.

"This is amazing!"

He swallowed it almost immediately and spoke with obvious excitement.

"Where did you buy these? Next time I pass through Celadon City, I'm definitely buying more!"

Hearing Ethan's praise, Chloe smiled shyly before picking up one herself.

"Wow... this is absolutely delicious."

Erika covered her mouth with a gentle laugh, her eyes curving into crescent moons.

"I'm afraid you won't be able to buy them anywhere."

"They're all homemade."

"You made these yourself?"

Chloe stared at the desserts in amazement.

"They're so beautifully made—I honestly thought they came from a famous shop. Erika... you're incredible."

After praising her for a while, Chloe couldn't help sighing in admiration.

"Your cooking is amazing, you're so beautiful, and you're so graceful and caring. Whoever marries you in the future... I think he'll be an incredibly lucky man."

( End of Chapter )
