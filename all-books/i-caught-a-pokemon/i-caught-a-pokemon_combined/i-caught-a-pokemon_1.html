Mt. Ember stood in the north of One Island, the highest peak in the Sevii Islands.

Several centuries ago it had been an active volcano. At some point it had quieted, until people came to regard it as extinct. Even after so many years of wind and rain, its shape remained remarkably intact.

After another night's rest at Lorelei's house, Ethan and Sabrina returned to One Island with her as their guide.

According to stories passed down by the island's elders, Moltres lived somewhere on the mountain. Its fiery glow shone like a bright lamp, visible even at night. That was how Mt. Ember had earned its name.

They walked for a long time before reaching the mountain's foot. Ethan looked up. Shrubs covered its lower slopes, but the vegetation thinned as it rose, giving way to bare rock above the midpoint.

Smoke occasionally curled from cracks in the stone. Ethan had his doubts. Nothing about this looked particularly extinct.

They followed a path called Kindle Road higher up the mountain. The scent of the forest faded, replaced by a faint smell of sulfur. Even through his shoes and socks, Ethan could feel heat rising from the rock underfoot.

He wiped the sweat from his forehead and turned to Lorelei. "Are you sure this volcano is extinct?"

"Well..." Lorelei looked around, beginning to doubt it herself. "I haven't climbed it in a long time. I didn't expect it to change this much. Compared with my last visit, it does seem... perhaps... a little warmer."

Ethan knew the lines between active, dormant, and extinct volcanoes were not fixed. An active one could fall quiet, a dormant one could erupt, and even a supposedly extinct one could awaken. Why else would a heat-loving Moltres make its home here?

He looked back and saw Sabrina more than ten meters behind. She was breathing hard, her clothes soaked through with sweat. The climb had left her hot and exhausted.

She wasn't used to this kind of exercise. Even so, she said nothing and did not use her psychic powers to help herself along. She kept trying to match Ethan's pace.

Smiling, Ethan started to go back for her. Then his gaze fell on her. Sweat had made her clothes cling to her figure, outlining it with startling clarity.

He stared a little too openly. Sabrina seemed to sense it and looked up. Ethan reacted with all the speed of an Indigo League champion, pulled his gaze away, and greeted her with a broad smile.

"Tired? Do you want to rest for a while?"

Sabrina had apparently noticed nothing. She shook her head. "No. Let's keep going."

"We should take a break. You don't usually exercise this hard. If you push yourself on a climb like this, your legs will be miserable tomorrow."

She seemed to consider it. Lorelei added, "There's a place called Ember Spa not far ahead. The hot springs are good for tired muscles. People from the island go there to relax, especially the older residents."

After a moment's thought, Sabrina agreed.

The broad road had ended. Only a winding mountain path remained. They followed it to Ember Spaand fell silent at the sight of the spring.

Steam billowed from water that churned like a pot at a rolling boil. The three stood side by side, staring, until Ethan finally turned to Lorelei.

"That must be close to a hundred degrees. Are you sure the islanders come here to bathe, rather than make soup?"

Lorelei shot him a look, but had no answer. "It shouldn't be like this. How did it suddenly get so hot?"

"Could the volcano be about to erupt?" Sabrina asked.

"It won't erupt," Lorelei said firmly. "Maybe the earthquake out at sea a while ago changed something here."

The heat around the spring was worse than anywhere else. They could not rest there, so they continued deeper into Mt. Ember.

After a while, even the narrow path disappeared. Lorelei led them back and forth among broken rocks until they reached a cave almost blocked by a boulder.

She looked pleased with herself. "People climb this mountain every year, but I'm the only one who knows this place. If Moltres really is here, I believe it's on the peak beyond the cave."

The boulder covered nearly the whole entrance. Only a thin gap remained, barely wide enough for a small child to slip through sideways.

Ethan was about to ask Gardevoir for help, but Sabrina stepped forward first. She raised both hands, released her psychic power, and slowly pushed the rock aside.

It was almost three meters across and weighed several tons. Even she found the task difficult. Fortunately, she only needed to open a passage wide enough for them to enter.

With a rumble, the gap widened. Cool mountain air flowed out before they had even stepped inside. After the heat outdoors, it felt like standing under an air conditioner.

They entered one after another. Ethan looked around and saw no sign of tools on the walls. The cave appeared to have formed naturally.

It was cool and damp. The floor was slick in places, and green moss grew along both sides. They climbed another few dozen meters through the cave before reaching the far opening.

A strong sea breeze lifted their hair. They stood above a cliff, high enough to look over all of One Island. In the distance, they could even make out parts of Two Island and Three Island.

Chapter 178: Three Birdbrains Meet

Beside the cliff, a steep natural path rose between two walls of rock. They followed it into a hidden valley. At the bend, Ethan leaned out for one look, then quickly drew back.

"Well?" Lorelei asked.

Ethan let out a long breath and nodded. "You were right. It's there."

Lorelei and Sabrina peered into the valley. A large bird Pokemon rested on a flat rock, its eyes closed as if it were dozing.

Sabrina, the subject of their exchange, seemed to be the only one who did not understand. She had no idea that the scenery they were discussing was different from the scenery she had seen.

"Of course. I'll take you down."

Zapdos and Articuno were no less proud. Without waiting for Ethan's orders, they beat their wings, rose into the air, and flew into the valley.

Moltres cried out and rushed to meet the intruders. It had not expected them to be the other two legendary birds. Nor could it make sense of why Articuno and Zapdos had come together.

The three hovered in a tense triangle over the valley. Though outnumbered, Moltres showed no sign of retreating.

Ethan sprang out from behind the rocks. "Heh, heh, heh!" He imitated Haunter's wicked laugh, fully aware of how melodramatic he sounded. "Moltres, your time has come! Surrender!"

Then he gave his Pokemon their orders. "Zapdos, you're our main attacker. Articuno, you've got a bad type matchup, so focus on keeping it occupied."

To be safe, he released his other Pokemon as well. He kept them on watch, ready to stop an interruptionor prevent Moltres from escaping if the fight turned against it.

The three birds held their positions. Battle seemed only a heartbeat away.

Chapter 179: Carried Away on the Wind

In the valley, the battle to catch Moltres had begun. Lorelei and Sabrina, meanwhile, had reached the middle of Mt. Ember.

They knew the route from their climb, and going downhill was much faster. Yet the mood between them was nothing like it had been on the way up. They walked side by side in heavy silence.

Boom!

She straightened and studied the clouds, her expression grave. That was an enormous storm, and a Thunder attack of astonishing power. What kind of Pokemon could cause such a change in the sky?

Was that Blizzard? The clouds had formed from both the lightning and the snowstorm. No wonder they held such power.

As an Ice-type specialist, Lorelei knew an ordinary Pokemon could not send a Blizzard this far through such hot air. Did Ethan own a legendary Pokemon?

Several possibilities crossed her mind, but none explained everything she was seeing.

Sabrina watched the storm calmly. There was no surprise in her eyes, only open admiration.

Fire flared within the clouds as well. It twisted and darted through them, but could not escape. Several different bird calls reached them faintly from above.

"That fight must be fierce," Lorelei said. "Don't you want to see it?"

She hoped to stir Sabrina's curiosity. Sabrina gave her a cool glance. "Do you want to go back?"

Of course she did. The answer nearly escaped Lorelei before she swallowed it.

She was one of the Elite Four and had earned her place through years of battling. Naturally, she wanted to know how Ethan intended to catch Moltres. She could not understand Sabrina's lack of interest.

"You hardly ever get to see something like this. Aren't you curious how he'll catch it?"

"No. However unusual Moltres is, it's still a Pokemon. I don't need to watch."

Lorelei almost wanted to ask the League to be more careful when appointing Gym Leaders. Could they at least choose people who cared about Pokemon battles?

Another tremor interrupted her thoughts. This one was far stronger. Before she could see what had happened, an earthshaking blast sounded and the whole mountain began to sway.

The crater, quiet for centuries, erupted. A towering column of molten rock shot into the sky.

The battle had set off the volcano?

The eruption tore through the clouds. Lava fell back to the mountainside and began flowing downhill like a slow red wave.

"Ethan's in danger!" Lorelei said. "The valley is close to the crater. That eruption could have caught him!"

Sabrina showed no sign of panic. Lorelei stared at her. "Why are you still standing here? Don't you care what happens to him?"

"He's always been lucky. He won't die that easily." Sabrina looked toward the summit. "See for yourself."

Lorelei followed her gaze. A dark speck was moving through the sky toward them. As it drew close, she saw Ethan, unharmed, riding Charizard.

"Miss Lorelei," Sabrina said.

Her suddenly formal tone caught Lorelei off guard. "What is it?"

"You should hurry down. If you wait, you might not make it in time."

Why was she telling only Lorelei to go? "What about you? Are you waiting here for Ethan?"

Sabrina merely looked at her. Before Lorelei could ask again, a shadow swept past. Ethan caught Sabrina in both arms and lifted her away. The gust of Charizard's passing tossed Lorelei's hair and blew the glasses from her face.

She picked them up, put them back on, and looked around. Sabrina was gone. High above, Charizard was already racing down the mountain.

Ethan held Sabrina across his arms on its back. From below, they looked like a victorious knight carrying off a princess.

As they were about to disappear from view, Ethan looked back and shouted, "Miss Lorelei! Hurry! If you stay there, it'll be too late!"

Lorelei could have cried. He had left her behind? Had it never occurred to him to take her along?

Chapter 180: What They Gained and Where They Went

At One Island's harbor, Ethan and Sabrina had returned to the little tavern.

The Orange Islands lay farther south than the Sevii Islands. No one could say how many years ago the archipelago had taken its name from the sweet, juicy citrus fruit grown there.
