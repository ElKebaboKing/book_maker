As Sabrina came over to Ethan's side, Clair took the Blackthorn Gym Badge from inside her clothes.

"Please accept this. It's the Rising Badge, proof that you defeated me."

Ethan thanked her and took the still-warm badge. He turned it over a few times in his hand.

The Rising Badge was shaped like a blue dragon's head. Its smooth, mirrorlike surface had no facial features or other clear details, though, so he could not tell which Dragon-type Pokémon it was meant to resemble. At first glance, it looked more like a dark steel mask.

After he placed it in his badge case, Lance asked casually, "Where are you going next? Another Gym?"

Ethan weighed his answer. Lance had fought Team Rocket alongside him before, so Ethan told him about his plan to go to Mahogany Town and take on the organization. As an Elite Four member and a Pokémon investigator, Lance was hardly someone from whom he needed to keep it secret.

"What? There's a Team Rocket base there?"

Lance, Clair, and Kabu were all stunned. Mahogany Town was close to Blackthorn City and its Gym. Team Rocket had been hiding almost under their noses, and none of them had known.

"That's frightening," Lance said with a sigh, rubbing his chin. "Team Rocket is so close, and we haven't noticed a thing. Have they infiltrated the region that thoroughly?"

Clair suddenly thought of something and frowned. "If there really is a base in Mahogany Town, Vicious's hideout could be there. Now that he's escaped, there's a good chance he'll return to it."

"You're right." Lance considered this for a moment, then looked seriously at Ethan. "Are you certain the information about the base is reliable?"

"It should be. Looker told me, and I expect the International Police have already verified it." Ethan nodded. "He asked me to help deal with Team Rocket's executives. Do you want to come? I'm sure Looker would welcome you."

Lance thought it over. At first he seemed ready to agree, but in the end he shook his head.

"I'm afraid I can't this time. I'd like to arrest those people and repay them for the attack on the Holy Land, but there's something more important waiting for me in Kanto. I've already spent too long here, and I can't put it off any longer. Besides..."

He drew out the last word and smiled. "Besides, with you there, those Rockets won't stand a chance. You won't need me!"

"..." Ethan gave a wry smile and shook his head. "I'm just an ordinary person. You think too highly of me."

"Ordinary?" Lance laughed. "An ordinary person wouldn't beat Clair so thoroughly that she couldn't fight back!"

Clair rolled her eyes at being made the butt of his joke, but she could not argue.

Ethan knew Lance had a point. Looker and the others must have planned the operation carefully. Lance's presence would help, but the raid could go ahead without him. Ethan had invited him on a whim, so he did not press the matter.

"Well, I've accomplished what I came for. I should be going."

"Take care!"

Lance and the others escorted Ethan to the entrance before waving him goodbye.

...

Ethan and Sabrina left Blackthorn City and headed northwest. Their destination was Mahogany Town, a little over half a day's journey away.

This time Ethan did not retrace their route through the Ice Path. Instead, they crossed the mountains by the road on the surface, which was not particularly rough.

Several days remained before the ten-day deadline Looker had given him. They had more than enough time, but Ethan no longer felt like sightseeing anywhere else. With a major battle approaching, he had grown more settled and focused.

They walked in the sunshine while Charizard flew overhead with their luggage. Ethan and Sabrina traveled light, carrying only a small backpack each.

As they left the mountains surrounding Blackthorn, the forests on either side of the road grew thinner. They walked shoulder to shoulder, talking and laughing all the way. Anyone who saw them might have thought they were a couple in love out for a trip together.

Going downhill was easier than climbing, but the forward pull of their weight and the need to watch every step still made it tiring. After a little more than an hour, Sabrina's clothes were soaked with sweat.

Fortunately, the mountain road was not steep. They stopped now and then and treated the journey as a chance to enjoy the scenery.

Just as they were coming out of the mountains, Ethan caught a glimpse of a Pokémon darting among the loose stones beneath a cliff.

"Huh? Was that...?"

He turned to look. A small gray-green Pokémon was rummaging through the rocks, turning them over as though searching for mushrooms or something else to eat.

"It's a Larvitar!" Ethan recognized it at once.

He took his Pokédex from his waist pouch. Sabrina noticed and looked at him in surprise.

"You want to catch it?"

"Of course!" Ethan nodded. "Like Dragonite, its final evolution, Tyranitar, is incredibly powerful. It's a pseudo-legendary!"

Pseudo-legendary?

Sabrina had never played the Pokémon games, so she had no way of knowing what he meant. But it was rare for Ethan to show interest in catching a Pokémon, and that made her examine this one more closely. She had never seen a Larvitar before.

A long, blunt horn rose from the top of its head. Its eyes were red, its arms short, and each of its feet had a single toe. A diamond-shaped red plate covered its belly. The center of its tail tapered to a point, surrounded by a ring of flat plates.

It was small, but it had a cool, defiant look. Its eyes in particular made it seem perpetually unwilling to back down.

Unfortunately, Pokémon other than Psychic-types did not interest Sabrina.

Meanwhile, Ethan crept toward Larvitar. Before it noticed him, he pointed his Pokédex at it.

The Pokédex chimed. "Larvitar, the Rock Skin Pokémon. Rock and Ground type. It is said to be born deep underground and to grow by absorbing nutrients from the soil. Eating earth as it goes, it makes its way toward the surface where its parent awaits. After consuming roughly a mountain's worth of earth, it becomes a pupa aboveground and sleeps while it grows."

To be honest, Ethan had never heard such strange information from his Pokédex before. Surely the part about eating dirt was a joke?
