After saying goodbye to Morty, Ethan and Erika wasted no more time and hurried out through the Gym's doors.

They had barely reached the stone steps when Ethan raised a Poké Ball and summoned Charizard. It carried both of them directly toward the tea party.

Morty stood watching their departing figures for a long time. Only after Charizard finally disappeared into the distance did he sigh helplessly.

Sigh...

Today's battle had affected him profoundly!

Crystal Steelix, a Gengar that could speak...

Either one had broadened his horizons. Most of all, the Pokémon in which he took such pride had been defeated with astonishing ease!

That man was about his own age, yet he was incredibly powerful!

The wider world truly contained people beyond one's imagination. Morty had been a Gym Leader for a long time, and it had been years since he last felt so powerless...

...

Charizard streaked across the sky above Ecruteak City, casting a small shadow on the ground below.

When pedestrians noticed the shadow, they looked up and saw an orange Pokémon flash overhead.

They shook their heads. Trainers in such a blazing hurry were rarely seen in this slow-paced ancient city.

What could possibly be so urgent?

The tea party Naoko had mentioned was being held in a courtyard on the western side of Ecruteak City.

At 10:17 in the morning, Charizard slowly descended before the courtyard gate.

Although they were more than ten minutes late, the open gate revealed many people wandering leisurely around the courtyard, with no activity currently underway.

It appeared the tea party had not formally begun.

"Wonderful. We made it."

Erika climbed down from Charizard and patted her chest in relief.

Neither Ethan nor Erika liked arriving late. Seeing that the tea party had yet to start, they both relaxed.

They exchanged smiles and were about to enter when a woman in a kimono stepped out from behind the gate to welcome them.

She bowed slightly. "Welcome to our humble home."

The woman was several years older than Ethan and very beautiful. She wore a pale blue kimono, and her hair had been pinned up with great care, giving her an elegant and composed appearance.

Ethan immediately recognized her as one of the five sisters. Apart from Naoko, whom he had met yesterday, he did not yet know any of their names or where this woman fell among the siblings.

He took out the invitation and was about to hand it to her when a clear, delighted voice suddenly sounded from behind the woman.

"Mr. Ethan! Miss Erika! Wonderful, you're finally here!"

They looked up and saw Naoko, also dressed in a kimono, hurrying toward them from behind the woman.

"Miss Naoko." Erika bowed slightly in greeting. "I'm sorry. Something delayed us, so we arrived a little late."

Naoko quickly waved her hands. "It's fine. The tea party hasn't officially started yet. What matters is that you came!"

The woman beside her understood their relationship from the brief exchange and smiled softly. "I see. You're Naoko's friends. Please forgive my rudeness. I'm Kuni, her second-oldest sister."

"It's a pleasure to meet you, Miss Kuni." Ethan and Erika introduced themselves as well.

Before anyone could say more, Naoko took Erika's hand and led her into the courtyard.

"All right, let's not stand at the gate. Come inside, both of you!"

Following behind Naoko and Erika, Ethan entered the courtyard and discovered just how expansive it truly was.

It contained not only a pond, but also small bridges, pavilions, and traditional buildings. Cherry trees had even been planted all around it!

Judging by the scenery alone, the courtyard's layout possessed considerable charm and had clearly been arranged with great care.

As Naoko led them toward the main house, she explained the courtyard's history.

"This property belongs to our family. We hold a tea party like this about once a week, and every time we do, many people come to attend. It gets very lively!"

As they walked, Ethan looked around. Just as Naoko said, many men and women strolled through the courtyard together, smiles covering every face.

Seeing them made Ethan suddenly realize something.

He first looked at their clothing, then lowered his eyes to his own.

Everyone else wore a kimono. Even Erika was dressed in one as usual. Ethan alone wore casual clothes, making him seem like the only person in the entire courtyard who did not belong.

Whenever they passed other guests, men and women alike frowned in surprise at his clothes.

Clearly, given the fame of the Eevee Sisters, these people considered an invitation to the tea party a great honor and had naturally dressed for the occasion. How could anyone arrive in ordinary clothes as Ethan had?

Ethan had not deliberately tried to attract attention.

For one thing, he did not own a kimono and possessed only ordinary clothing. For another, he had never attended an event like this before and had genuinely overlooked the matter.

Naoko appeared to understand his thoughts and covered her mouth with a soft laugh.

"It's fine. You don't have to wear one. It's only a formality, and we aren't that particular about it."

Although this was only their second meeting, Naoko treated them with surprising warmth!

Ethan guessed that their encouragement at the Dance Theater yesterday had played a major part, unconsciously making Naoko feel closer to them.

Upon reaching the main house, Naoko first brewed tea for both guests.

As she placed the cups before them, she said, "The tea party will begin soon. Before then, my sister Miki is holding an ikebana class. Would either of you be interested?"

"Ikebana?"

Erika's eyes lit up when she heard there would be a flower-arranging class. She was an expert in that field!

Without hesitation, Erika replied, "What a coincidence. I'm also an ikebana practitioner in Kanto. If possible, I would love to exchange ideas with your sister!"

"Oh? You're an ikebana practitioner too, Miss Erika?" Naoko's interest immediately grew. "That's wonderful. I'm sure Miki will be delighted when she hears!"

Compared with Erika, Ethan showed little interest.

Seeing his expression, Naoko smiled. "Besides ikebana, Pokémon battles are being held on the other side of the courtyard. If you're interested, Mr. Ethan, you can go test your skills against the other Trainers!"
