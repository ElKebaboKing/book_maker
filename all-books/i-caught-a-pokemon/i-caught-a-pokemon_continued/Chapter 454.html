Ethan hesitated. "I can't understand what Psyduck is thinking..."

Before he could finish, Ash suddenly burst into laughter.

He glanced at Misty and mocked her without mercy. "Exactly! How could a rock possess such incredible power? Besides, your Psyduck is really special, Misty. Even another Pokémon probably couldn't understand anything it says!"

Misty was about to retort when she heard Ash ridicule her Pokémon. But after opening her mouth, she realized he was right. Her Psyduck truly was abnormal, almost as though something were missing inside its head.

How else could anyone explain a Water-type Pokémon that feared water and had nearly drowned itself on several occasions?

It also constantly emerged from its Poké Ball without permission, ignored commands, and stood around blankly during battle without displaying anything resembling the proper state of mind!

"I... I..."

There were far too many strange things about Psyduck. No matter what Misty wanted to say, all she could produce in the end was a heavy sigh.

Sigh. Forget it. The fact that my Psyduck survived in good health long enough for me to catch it was already an incredible achievement...

At the side, Ethan cleared his throat when he saw the two begin arguing again.

"All right, both of you, stop fighting and let me finish."

His expression clearly suggested that there was more to his answer, and everyone turned toward him.

"However..." Ethan emphasized the word before continuing, "although I haven't connected my heart with Psyduck and can't understand its thoughts, I really can sense its emotions faintly."

"Sense its emotions?" Everyone froze.

"Huh? Seriously?" Ash's eyes widened. "That's incredible!"

"It's true. How should I explain it? The feeling is very subtle." Ethan thought briefly, then continued. "You all know that the eyes are windows into the heart, and a person's expression reflects their emotions. By watching those two things, you can often judge someone's mood without asking. But when I stared at Psyduck just now, I could faintly sense that it felt relaxed without relying on either clue."

Ash and Misty looked at one another. The explanation had already passed beyond their understanding. In truth, they were still young, and much of what adults considered common sense remained unfamiliar to them.

But Erika and Brock both looked thoughtful, so Ash and Misty could only nod as though they understood.

"Ah... ah... So that's how it is..."

"In that case..." Erika mused, "the legend may have been exaggerated, and people today may regard the selection of a Sea Hero as nothing more than a ceremony, but the blessing truly does possess such wondrous power!"

"It seems so. The fact that the ceremony survived until today shows that it wasn't merely a performance." Ethan smiled and shook his head. "But this is obviously far from the legendary ability to connect our hearts."

"It's already extremely impressive," Brock added. "If you can perceive a Pokémon's emotions more clearly and accurately, you'll be better able to care for it. That would be valuable for any Trainer, but especially for Pokémon Breeders and doctors. Isn't this precisely the ability they dream of possessing?"

Only then did Ash finally understand. Eagerness filled his face as he asked excitedly, "Ethan, can you sense the emotions of Pokémon that aren't Water type?"

The emotions of other Pokémon?

The question stirred Ethan's curiosity, and he turned toward Pikachu, who stood beside Ash.

When Ethan looked at it, Pikachu tilted its head in confusion.

"Pika-pika?"

Ethan stared for a long while, but eventually shook his head.

"No. I can't sense anything at all. It appears this much of the legend is true. The ability granted to me by the Sea Spirit can probably sense only the emotions of Water-type Pokémon."

"No matter what, that's still amazing!" Misty wore such a broad smile that her eyes nearly curved into crescent moons. "Does that mean you'll be able to move freely underwater too?"

Her face shone with anticipation. Only moments earlier, a heartwarming scene had appeared in her mind: she and Ethan playing underwater with every kind of Water-type Pokémon, weaving between corals and jellyfish like a pair of happy aquatic spirits!

Wait. If Ethan really has that ability, doesn't that mean he'll start working toward becoming a Water Pokémon Master too?

Then if I want to become a Water Pokémon Master as well, we'll share the same goal and become a match made in heaven!

Hehe. Even the God of the Sea is helping me!

Ethan naturally had no idea what Misty was imagining. If he had known, he probably would have rapped her sharply on the head!

What kind of nonsense is running through that little head?

Water Pokémon Master? Ethan's goal had never been to master a single type!

He shrugged. "Who knows? That stone didn't alter my body. If I want to confirm it, I'll have to find an opportunity to go underwater sometime. But judging by this supposed connection of hearts, I doubt the other ability is as miraculous as Maya described. The legend must be greatly exaggerated!"

...

After the Whirl Cup ended, Ash, Misty, and Brock originally intended to bid Ethan farewell and resume their journey. The moment they heard that Sea Priestess Maya would hold a banquet that evening for Ethan, the Sea Hero, they immediately decided to remain and enjoy a feast. They would leave only after eating their fill and resting for the night.

No one could blame them for being gluttonous. All three were still growing. Brock was an excellent cook, but their constant travel and nights in the wilderness rarely gave them the chance to enjoy a proper banquet. With such a rare opportunity before them, they naturally had to reward themselves!

While they waited for evening to arrive, the group left the arena and headed toward the Pokémon Center.

Along the way, Ash and Misty enthusiastically discussed everything they had seen and experienced during the tournament. Every major competition helped them grow, and many moments were worth remembering.

Before returning to the Pokémon Center, Ethan found a delivery company and mailed his new trophy and medal back to the Cerise Laboratory.

Once he reached the Pokémon Center, he also transferred Blastoise and the red Gyarados back to the laboratory.

The tournament had given both Pokémon an opportunity to stretch their muscles. Ethan would have no need to call upon them again for some time.
