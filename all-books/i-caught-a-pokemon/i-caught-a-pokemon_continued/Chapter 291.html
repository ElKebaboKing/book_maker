"Here we are. This is Violet Gym."

Ethan and Sabrina went inside, but when he told a staff member why they had come, he learned that Falkner was not there.

"I'm sorry," said a young man in a white martial arts uniform. "Gym Leader Falkner went out this morning. I don't know when he'll be back. If you'd like to challenge him today, please wait in the seating area."

Unfortunate timing. Still, there was nothing for Ethan and Sabrina to do but sit and wait.

Ethan settled into a soft sofa and looked around to pass the time. He saw several other young men dressed like the one who had greeted them.

Are they all apprentices here? It seemed strange to him. This was a Gym specializing in bird Pokémon. Did every one of these young men aspire to be a master of Flying types?

They could learn from Ash or Lance instead. Everyone knew those two were the true masters of Flying-type Pokémon, birds included!

He glanced at Sabrina and asked quietly, "Do you know Falkner?"

She shook her head. "Kanto and Johto may be governed by the same Pokémon League, but their Gym Leaders belong to different regions. I've never met him, and we've never had any contact."

"I see."

Ethan thought back over what he knew. Understanding an opponent was half the battle, but he knew little about Falkner. He had heard that Falkner was famous for looking cool and handsome, though his father Walker outshone him.

Walker was traveling at present, which was why he had handed the Gym Leader's position to his son. Many of the Pokémon Falkner used had come from his father as well. It was a straightforward case of a son taking over the family profession.

Then again, that was hardly unusual. In Kanto alone, Brock had followed his father, Sabrina had followed hers, and Misty's family had passed down their Gym too.

They waited a while longer. As the minutes slipped by, Ethan grew restless.

"We can't sit here all day. Should we find a place to stay, or go take a look at Sprout Tower?"

Sabrina gave a soft sound of agreement. They had reached the Gym entrance on their way out when they met a young man coming in.

He looked about Ethan's age, though a head shorter. His martial arts uniform resembled the staff members' but was more elaborate. Thick, medium-length hair fell over part of his face, yet his fine features were still visible.

That must be Falkner.

He did look cool, with a composed air about him. Handsome, though? Ethan gave a private scoff. No one had ever dared call himself handsome in front of Ethan Vale.

Before Ethan could speak, the young man said, "I'm Falkner. Are you here to challenge the Gym?"

So it was him.

"Yes," Ethan said. "I'm the challenger. The woman with me is here to accompany me."

Falkner looked Ethan over once more and nodded. "I understand. Follow me."

He walked past them toward the main hall. Ethan noticed a Hoothoot perched on one foot on Falkner's shoulder.

Hoothoot resembled an owl, only rounder, with tiny wings and a small beak. It was a Normal- and Flying-type Pokémon. Its red eyes could look downright frightening at night.

Ethan remembered that an organ inside Hoothoot could sense the Earth's rotation. It called at the same time every day, and its internal clock kept remarkably accurate time. People in the past had valued it as a divine messenger that announced the hours. In some countries, it was even regarded as a god of wisdom that understood everything in the world.

Judging by how comfortable Falkner and Hoothoot were together, Falkner liked to keep a Pokémon outside its Poké Ball, just as Ash did with Pikachu.

Hoothoot tilted its head when it noticed Ethan looking at it. It hooted, fluttered its wings, and greeted him with surprising awareness. Ethan smiled back.

Falkner led them to the elevator in the center of the hall. The three rode it to the top floor.

That was where the Gym's battlefield stood. Ethan discovered that it had no roof. It lay open to the sun, enclosed by stepped spectator seating on every side. In the middle was an ordinary regulation battle court.

Ethan did not like battling on rooftops, ship decks, or raised platforms. They always felt too thin beneath him. He would hesitate to use a terrain-breaking move like Dig for fear of damaging the structure. He preferred solid ground, where he could battle freely without that worry.

Falkner walked to one Trainer's position and spoke formally. "Let me introduce myself properly. I'm Falkner, the leader of Violet Gym, which specializes in Flying-type Pokémon. I welcome your challenge."

"I'm Ethan Vale."

Falkner showed no surprise. "I know. You're the Indigo League champion. I thought you'd come to challenge the Silver League as soon as that tournament ended. I didn't expect you to take this long."

He recognized me already. Ethan said, "I went to the Orange Islands along the way and stayed there for a while."

They did not know each other well, so Falkner asked no more. Once Ethan had taken the opposite Trainer's position, he explained the rules.

"Each of us may use three Pokémon. The first Trainer whose entire team is unable to battle loses."

It was the usual sort of Gym match: two-on-two or three-on-three, with little difference in the basic rules.

Just as Ethan got ready, the elevator opened again. More than a dozen young men stepped out. They stood silently behind Falkner to watch, while one of the older students took the referee's position at the center of the field.
