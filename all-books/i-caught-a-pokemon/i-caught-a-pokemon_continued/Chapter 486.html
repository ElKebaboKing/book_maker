The Cianwood Gym was the city's official and only Gym. It specialized in Fighting-type Pokémon, and a challenger who defeated Chuck would receive the Storm Badge.

By the time Ethan arrived, Chuck had changed clothes and was waiting outside its doors.

Chuck watched him jog up at an easy pace rather than exhaust himself trying to keep up, as other challengers often did. He nodded approvingly.

"Good. You're not like those fools who never think. You saved your strength by running at your own pace, but you didn't cheat or shirk the work. A clever Trainer who isn't lazy—well done!"

Despite the praise, Ethan could not bring himself to feel pleased.

He bent slightly to catch his breath, wiped the sweat from his forehead, and looked up at Chuck.

Chuck was muscular, but he also had a rather large belly. Ethan had assumed that even if this judo master excelled at fighting, running might not suit him. Evidently his stamina was still far better than Ethan's.

Sigh. If I hadn't worn myself out last night and wasted time because I didn't know the way to the Gym, I wouldn't have taken this long...

"Mr. Chuck, can we begin the Gym battle now?"

Chuck nodded and led him inside.

What Ethan did not know was that Chuck had only just arrived himself. For all his talk and his approval of Ethan's effort, he had beaten Ethan here by no more than a few minutes.

Lately, Chuck had developed a taste for sweets, and his body fat had been climbing steadily. The run from the beach had left him drenched in sweat, his calves trembling.

He had gritted his teeth all the way back, then hurried to change into clean clothes before Ethan arrived so he could preserve his image!

He could only pull this off because Ethan did not know the route. Otherwise, Chuck might even have arrived after him!

For all his judgment, Chuck had hardly done much better.

Inside the Gym, a group of children rushed to meet Chuck and bowed together.

"Welcome back, Master!"

The youngest were only seven or eight, while the oldest were fifteen or sixteen. They all wore the same clothes and called Chuck their teacher. They were plainly his students.

Several Mankey, Machop, and Hitmontop stood nearby, surely the students' Pokémon.

Chuck strode past the group with his head held high.

"Did you all practice properly while I was away?"

The students bowed again and answered loudly, "Of course!"

"We didn't slack off!"

Their faces showed genuine trust and respect for Chuck. He managed his students well, and the Gym's atmosphere was clearly a good one.

Only after greeting him did the students notice Ethan behind Chuck. They studied the visitor curiously and, without anyone needing to say it, understood that a Gym battle was coming. Their excitement grew.

Compared with dull daily drills, a lively match was a welcome change!

At Chuck's signal, the students cleared a battlefield for the two Trainers. Then they sat on either side and watched intently.

Ethan had faced enough expectant audiences that their stares did not trouble him.

He and Chuck took their places at opposite ends of the field. The oldest student stepped into the middle to referee the match.

"Each Trainer may use three Pokémon. The Gym Leader sends out his Pokémon first. The challenger wins by defeating all of the Gym Leader's Pokémon!"

Before the match could begin, someone appeared in the doorway. Erika walked in.

She nodded to Ethan, then silently took a seat behind him.

At the sight of the beautiful older woman, several of Chuck's less worldly young students became awkward. They looked away shyly, unable to bring themselves to meet her face.

Erika was not only beautiful; she had the gentle air of an older sister. The younger boys were thoroughly flustered!

Across the field, Chuck regarded Ethan with solemn pride. "Allow me to introduce myself again. I am Chuck, king of Fighting-type Pokémon and Leader of the Cianwood Gym!"

With that, he sent out his first Pokémon.

A flash of blue light faded, and a Primeape stood before Ethan.

Primeape...

Primeape was a common Fighting-type Pokémon in both Kanto and Johto, popular with Trainers. Compared with its unevolved form, Mankey, the bulging cross-shaped vein on its forehead and its frightening glare made it appear even angrier and more intimidating.

After a moment's thought, Ethan took out a Poké Ball and released his own Pokémon.

When the light faded, Dragonair appeared across from Primeape.

"A Dragonair!!"

"Wow! I've never seen one before!"

Several students recognized the uncommon Pokémon and cried out in surprise.

Chuck, unlike his delighted students, frowned. "Young man, you know this is a Fighting-type Gym. Why didn't you send out a Fighting-type Pokémon to battle me?"

To him, matching Fighting-types against each other was the proper way to challenge his Gym. That was a man's idea of romance!

"Sorry..." Ethan shook his head. "I don't have any Fighting-type Pokémon at the moment, so I'll have to battle with another type."

"None?" Chuck's gaze sharpened. "Don't you like Fighting-type Pokémon?"

"It's not that. I just haven't met one I wanted to catch yet."

"What nonsense!" Chuck roared with laughter. "Look at Machamp, Machoke, Machop, Primeape, and Hitmontop. They're all adorable! Go catch yourself a few!"

Ethan smiled wryly.

He had not said there was anything wrong with them. His time and attention were limited, however, and he could not care for an unlimited number of Pokémon. Even if he encountered a wild Fighting-type, he would only catch one he truly liked.

Fortunately, Chuck knew he could not force the matter and let the subject drop.

Both Pokémon were ready. Chuck wasted no more time and ordered Primeape to charge straight at Dragonair!
