At two in the afternoon, Ethan and Erika crossed the grassland and arrived at a Pokémon Center built in the wilderness.

They had walked for hours without stopping since leaving Ecruteak City. Their calves ached, and both body and mind were exhausted. Now that they had reached this place, they finally had a chance to rest and recover their strength.

While crossing the grassland, they had encountered a group of Sunkern. Erika acted decisively and successfully caught one, making it the second Grass-type Pokémon she had captured since coming to Johto.

Seeing herself draw closer to her goal put her in an especially cheerful mood.

Geographically, the Pokémon Center stood where forest met open country, beside a three-way junction. Perhaps because so many Trainers passed through and rested here each day, the Pokémon League attached particular importance to the location and had built the Center on an impressive scale.

The automatic doors slid apart. When Ethan and Erika entered the three-story building, they found at least several dozen Trainers gathered in the lobby alone.

As soon as the soundproof barrier of the doors disappeared behind them, the voices of people and the cries of Pokémon rushed over them.

"Wow, it's so lively!"

Erika could not help exclaiming at the sight of so many people.

The Center had extremely comprehensive facilities. Televisions, satellite phones, and desktop computers were all freely available to Trainers. Outside, it even had a swimming pool where seven or eight Water-type Pokémon were playing together.

"It certainly is. The facilities almost rival those at the Indigo League. All right, that's enough looking around for now. I'm starving, so let's hurry and get something to eat."

At Ethan's urging, the two went straight to the dining room for a late lunch.

As they had agreed, they would spend the night here.

There were still many hours before nightfall, and after eating and resting, they could easily have continued onward. The map, however, showed that this was the last place offering accommodations before Olivine City. If they did not want to camp in the wilderness that night, they had to rest here and set out again the next morning.

Neither of them was in a hurry, and both preferred sleeping in soft beds, so they did not care much about losing time.

The Pokémon Center's food was as delicious as ever. Erika ate elegantly, taking small bites at an unhurried pace. Ethan observed far fewer rules, and his table manners were decidedly casual.

After lunch, the two discussed taking a look around the Center. Before they could rise, however, a teenage boy and girl suddenly approached their table.

"Excuse us. Are you Ethan Vale?"

At the girl's clear voice, Ethan turned. She was fairly pretty, with long, pale blue hair tied into two ponytails that defied common sense by curving upward. Dressed in a tight top and fitted shorts, she looked enthusiastic, uninhibited, and full of energy.

A Misdreavus floated beside her, just above her head.

The boy beside her wore a baseball cap backward, along with a red sweatshirt and yellow shorts. A lock of hair emerged through the opening in his cap and jutted rebelliously into the air.

Judging by their hairstyles alone, they clearly shared the same taste.

"That's me. What can I do for you?"

"It really is you!"

When Ethan confirmed his identity, the two exchanged a glance, excitement plain in their eyes.

The girl was so delighted that she even jumped into the air!

Noticing Ethan's and Erika's surprised looks and perhaps realizing that they had lost their composure, both teenagers hurriedly cleared their throats and calmed down again.

"I forgot to introduce myself. I'm Marina." The girl pointed to the boy beside her. "His name is Jimmy. We're both Trainers from New Bark Town!"

Marina? Jimmy?

Ethan frowned slightly. He had some impression of the pair. They seemed to have appeared in the anime several times, but no matter how hard he thought, he could not remember the relevant stories.

"What did you want with me?"

"Well, I watched your matches at the Indigo League, and I know you won the championship! Would you please battle me!?"

"Me too! Me too!" Jimmy refused to be left behind. "I want to battle you too!"

"You two are being far too loud..." Ethan shook his head helplessly, but did not refuse. "All right. I have no reason to turn down a challenge. We can treat it as a little exercise after lunch!"

With the pair interrupting them, Ethan and Erika naturally had to abandon their plan to explore the Center.

They went outside to the back courtyard. Like other Pokémon Centers, this one had a simple battlefield behind the building as part of its standard League facilities.

Before Ethan even reached the field, Jimmy and Marina began arguing over who would battle him first.

They quarreled for a while, each determined to be the first to face Ethan. Yet as the argument continued, Jimmy suddenly backed down for some unknown reason and surrendered the first match to Marina.

Marina eagerly took her place in the Trainer box on the opposite side and smiled at Ethan.

"Today really is my lucky day. I never imagined I'd get to battle a champion!"

Ethan shook his head, his gaze moving repeatedly between them. "I can tell that you're very close. Are you traveling together?"

Both of them blushed at once.

Marina waved her hands. "No. Jimmy and I only happened to meet here."

Jimmy added, "She's right. We grew up together, but ever since we left New Bark Town, we've traveled separately. We only ran into each other here by chance!"

"That's quite a coincidence."

Ethan chuckled. Their embarrassment made the situation obvious.

They must have feelings for one another, but their shyness had kept them from taking the next step in their relationship.

Their love might still be uncertain and unspoken, but compared with Ash's single-minded obliviousness, Jimmy was already far ahead!

"What rules would you like to use?"

Jimmy and Marina had planned for each of them to battle Ethan once, using two Pokémon apiece.

After hearing their proposal, however, Ethan rejected it. He told each of them to use only one Pokémon, with a single round deciding the winner.

The two were somewhat disappointed. They understood that battling an opponent like Ethan would bring them valuable experience and insight regardless of whether they won or lost, so they naturally hoped to face him for as long as possible.
