"It's like this," Luana said, looking a little embarrassed. "My son Travis set out with a Pikachu last year. He's been traveling for nearly a year, but I haven't heard a thing from him. Not one message. I can't help worrying."

Ethan raised an eyebrow. "You want me to find your son?"

"Yes." Luana gave a rueful smile. "I have to run both the Gym and the hotel. I can't spare the time to go looking. If you happen to meet him on your travels, could you please tell him to come home soon?"

No word at all? Had Travis run away, or simply set off on a journey? Ethan thought it over. Whatever the reason, he ought to have sent at least a message or made a call. Something about the family's relationship might have gone wrong, or perhaps Travis had quarreled with them before he left.

"His name is Travis, right? If I meet him, I'll encourage him to come back."

"Thank you. He's stubborn and likes to make up his own mind. If he won't come home, please at least ask him to call me."

"I will, but don't count on me finding him. It's a big world."

Luana did not seem too worried about that. "You're both going to Johto to challenge the Silver League. When the Silver Conference begins, there's a good chance you'll meet."

"He's challenging the Silver League too?" Ethan was surprised. "That is a coincidence. All right, I'll keep an eye out."

He did not promise more. Travis might never enter the Conference, or something might delay him along the way. Ethan did not want to give Luana false hope.

Delighted that he had agreed, she bowed slightly. "I'm counting on you. Thank you!"

Ethan and Sabrina left Kumquat Gym, climbed onto Charizard, and headed for their final destination: Pummelo Island.

It was the Orange League's headquarters. Drake, the League's strongest Trainer and the pride of the islands, waited at Pummelo Stadium for challengers.

Charizard carried them through the wind. High above the sea, watching clouds drift past, Ethan said, "Time goes quickly. Before I knew it, our journey through the Orange Islands was almost over."

One arm rested around Sabrina. After sitting so long, he grew bored, and his hand began to wander. Sabrina gave it a light pat. Ethan grinned and behaved himself.

"You sound as though you'll miss it here," she said dryly.

"Of course I will. The weather's warm, the scenery's beautiful, and it's a wonderful place to travel. Most of all, I'd like more time with you."

His sudden, rather corny declaration brought a small smile to Sabrina's face. She said nothing, only leaned back against him.

The Orange Islands held happy memories for her too. Here, she and Ethan had become lovers. They enjoyed the quiet closeness for several minutes before she suddenly looked down at a spot on the sea.

"I think that's Ash down there."

"Ash?" Ethan looked and saw a small dark shape moving over the water. He guided Charizard lower. It was a Lapras carrying Ash and his friends in the direction Ethan and Sabrina had just left.

"Hey, Ash! Misty!" Ethan called.

They stood and looked up at his voice. "Ethan!" Misty waved excitedly.

Once Charizard was level with Lapras, Ethan leaped onto its back. Lapras dipped a little beneath the weight of four passengers.

"Where are you headed?" Ethan asked.

"To Kumquat Island, of course! We're going to challenge Kumquat Gym," Ash said, rubbing his nose.

"So you already have three badges? You've made good time."

Ash could hardly wait to ask, "What about you, Ethan? Have you got your fourth?"

"Of course he has, you idiot," Misty said before Ethan could answer. She rolled her eyes at Ash, her voice full of admiration. "Ethan's much stronger than you. And didn't you see that he came from the direction of Kumquat Island?"

Ash scratched his head and laughed awkwardly. Misty had pointed out the obvious.

"She's right," Ethan said with a wave of his hand. "I've reached the end of the course. Next comes the final boss."

"Aw." Ash sighed. "I hurried as fast as I could, and I'm still a step behind you."

"Don't worry about it. Whether you get there a little earlier or later makes no real difference. It's only a few days."

As they spoke, they noticed Tracey leaning over the side with a glass bottle. He lowered its opening into the sea and filled it.

"What are you doing?" Misty asked.

"Collecting a sample," Tracey said as he screwed on the cap. "I'm a Pokémon Watcher, remember? Comparing seawater and studying the habitat here is part of my work."

Then Ash's Pikachu seemed to spot something. It cried out toward the water beneath them.

They looked down. A huge dark shape was approaching quickly below the surface. Its outline resembled a whale or some other large sea creature. Before anyone could react, it passed beneath Lapras and surfaced ahead of them in a burst of spray.

It was a roughly built submarine.
