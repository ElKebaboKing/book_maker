That evening, Ethan brought Erika, Ash, Misty, and Brock to the Temple of the Sea for the banquet.

Stars filled the sky, and the cool sea breeze swept across the group, carrying away the last warmth left after sunset.

The Temple of the Sea stood near the arena, west of Scarlet City on Red Rock Isle. The enormous structure had been built atop a sheer cliff. Looking down from there offered a complete view of the city and, on the other side of the cliff, a vast and violent whirlpool churning across the ocean!

The whirlpool looked extremely dangerous. No one knew when it had formed or how long it had existed. Like a toilet that never stopped flushing, it endlessly drew seawater toward some unknown destination.

The exposed reefs around it had been polished perfectly smooth by the current. The constant rush of water sounded rather harsh, though after listening for long enough, everyone gradually grew accustomed to it.

Despite the banquet's grandeur, none of the Whirl Islands' politicians or merchants had been invited. The guest list included only Ethan, the most important Sea Hero; Maya, the Sea Priestess hosting the banquet; and whichever friends and family Ethan wished to bring.

Seen that way, the banquet appeared remarkably pure. It carried no hidden political or commercial purpose and seemed to exist solely to celebrate Ethan's becoming a Sea Hero.

The group passed several security guards dressed like ancient soldiers and entered the temple together.

Even before drawing close, they noticed that the temple shared the arena's ancient Greek style. The moment they stepped through its doors, they saw a statue of the God of the Sea!

Just as Ethan had expected, the God of the Sea worshiped here truly was Lugia!

The statue reproduced Lugia at full scale. Apart from its color, the sculpture looked astonishingly lifelike, almost indistinguishable from the real Pokémon!

The group stopped and gathered around it for a careful examination.

They had seen the Legendary Pokémon in the Orange Islands, but only from a great distance. They had never been able to observe it as closely as they could now.

"What a beautiful Pokémon... But from this close, Lugia really is enormous!"

Misty looked up and ran a hand across one of Lugia's wings, unable to suppress her wonder.

As a Water Pokémon Trainer, her greatest dream was to catch either a Lugia or a Kyogre one day. With this statue standing before her, for one brief moment, even that dream no longer felt impossibly distant!

"It is huge." Ethan nodded. He was already quite tall for his age, but before this enormous birdlike Pokémon, he still felt exceptionally small. "According to the records, an adult Lugia can reach 5.2 meters in height and weigh 216 kilograms. Its wingspan can exceed ten meters. It's unquestionably a very large avian Pokémon and truly magnificent. Compared with it, my three sacred birds count as only medium-sized. They can't compare at all."

The group continued marveling at Lugia's statue. At that moment, footsteps sounded from the other side. They turned and saw Sea Priestess Maya approaching with graceful steps.

Smiling, Maya joined them and likewise looked toward the statue.

"As you can see, this is the God of the Sea, the being we of the Whirl Islands have always worshiped. Since ancient times, it has protected the islands from outside threats. It is unquestionably our God of the Sea!"

After briefly marveling at Maya's beauty, Misty asked out of her longing for Lugia, "Um... Were the whirlpools outside the islands also created by the God of the Sea?"

Maya gently shook her head. "Well... so much time has passed that no one can verify the truth. You may believe they were, or you may believe they weren't. The generally accepted scientific explanation is that those whirlpools formed because of the ocean trenches and currents near the islands."

Ethan raised his eyebrows. Her answer made him view the beautiful priestess with new respect.

Ordinary religious figures often tried to promote their faith by attributing every unusual event to the deity they worshiped. A priestess like Maya, willing to discuss the facts, was exceedingly rare!

Before Ethan could respond, Maya turned toward his companions.

She naturally knew something about Ash and Misty because both had competed in the Whirl Cup. Erika and Brock had not entered, however, so this was her first time meeting them.

Ethan cleared his throat and introduced both sides.

When Maya learned that Erika and Brock were Gym Leaders from Kanto, surprise filled her face. She smiled. "As expected of a champion—no, I should say, as expected of our Sea Hero! Your own strength is extraordinary, and even among your traveling companions are powerful Trainers like these Gym Leaders!"

"You've misunderstood." Ethan gently shook his head. "These two friends simply cannot remain at their Gyms for the moment because of certain circumstances, so they decided to travel for a time. They didn't abandon their work there specifically to accompany me."

Maya laughed softly. "Even so, doesn't receiving the recognition of Gym Leaders also prove how exceptional you are?"

...

At eight that evening, inside the Temple of the Sea's banquet hall, a long table overflowed with fruit, pastries, and delicacies. Several attendants played musical instruments nearby. Everything around him reminded Ethan of that night on Shamouti Island.

The scenes were so similar. Even the Sea Priestess seemed to correspond to the festival maiden from that occasion.

Light, beautiful music echoed around the hall.

The banquet had officially begun. Ethan and the others released their smaller Pokémon, and the entire hall instantly became chaotic yet exceptionally lively.

At the long table, Ash, Misty, and Brock devoured the food with abandon. Even Pikachu constantly tossed treats into its mouth. Their ravenous eating looked so comical that the attendants nearby covered their mouths and giggled.

Compared with them, Ethan and Erika ate far more elegantly.

Ethan was willing to spend money and had never deprived himself or his Pokémon when it came to food. Naturally, they did not behave like Ash and the others, who looked as though they had not eaten anything good in years.

Throughout the meal, Ethan and Maya spoke from time to time, creating a relaxed and pleasant atmosphere. Yet he faintly sensed that the banquet's purpose might not be quite so simple after all.

Although Maya wore a smile, some troubling matter seemed to weigh upon her brow.
