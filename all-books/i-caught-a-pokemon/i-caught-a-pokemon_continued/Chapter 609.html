They could leave the cave through the opening overhead and find their bearings on the surface at once. But that would defeat the purpose of taking this shortcut through the mountains.

Misty had thought of that too and was about to discuss it with Ethan. Before she could speak, he came closer and put a finger to his lips.

She blinked in confusion. Ethan pulled her behind a boulder and pointed into the distance.

On the far side of the waterfall, more than a hundred meters away, a group in matching blue-and-white uniforms was pushing and dragging a bespectacled man toward a cave entrance.

Misty's eyes widened. Someone was being kidnapped!

She turned to ask Ethan what they should do, but the frown on his face suggested he recognized the group. “Ethan, do you know those people?”

He nodded. “They're Team Aqua.”

“Team Aqua?”

“They're an organization in this region, something like Team Rocket. But they aren't evil, and they don't go around stealing other people's Pokémon.”

“Huh?” Misty raised an eyebrow. “That doesn't sound so bad.”

Ethan shook his head. “Their ultimate goal is to expand the ocean. They say they want to restore habitats Pokémon have lost to human greed. They believe the sea is the source of all life, and they want to reclaim it and create an ideal home for Pokémon.”

“Oh.” Misty's mouth fell open. “So that means...”

“Exactly. They want to control Kyogre, one of the ancient Pokémon and ruler of the sea, flood the land, and increase the ocean's size!”

Misty understood now. Team Rocket wanted to capture the strongest Pokémon and rule the world. Team Aqua had a narrower aim, but it intended to upset the existing ecological balance and remake the world.

Why did every organization like this cause so much trouble?

As she was thinking that, another group arrived quietly. They wore cloaks of the same color as one another and slipped into the cave after Team Aqua.

“Who are they?”

“Team Magma.” Ethan told her about his earlier encounter with them in Rustboro City. “They're another organization here. Like Team Aqua, they want to control an ancient Pokémon. Their target isn't Kyogre, though. It's Groudon, the ruler of the land.”

“Another ancient Pokémon,” Misty said flatly. “Don't tell me they have the same sort of plan?”

“You've got it.” Ethan gave her an approving thumbs-up. “Team Magma wants to expand the land and give humans more room to live. They believe human progress is closely tied to the world's happiness, and that coexistence with Pokémon holds that progress back. With their opposing beliefs, the two groups are always at odds in Hoenn. They fight often.”

Misty rubbed her temples.

It was all too complicated. Given a choice, she would rather face Team Rocket than an argument about how the world should sustain life.

“Do you know the man they took?”

Ethan shook his head. He had not been in Hoenn long and knew few people here. He had also forgotten some of what happened in the anime and games; even characters who had appeared there were hard to recall clearly.

Whatever the details, Team Aqua and Team Magma had both come to this remote part of the cave. Something important was clearly happening.

For the world's ecological safety, and for his own, Ethan could not ignore it.

They waited a little longer. When no one else appeared, Misty called out Starmie. With its help, they slipped into the pool and crossed quickly and silently to the other side of the falls.

The thunder of falling water covered what little noise they made.

At the entrance the two groups had used, Ethan and Misty peered inside. The others were already out of sight.

Team Aqua apparently had no idea anyone was following them. Team Magma thought it had stalked its prey unseen, but it did not know someone was following it in turn.

Ethan recalled the Pokémon beside him, then sent out Gardevoir and called Gengar forward.

They entered cautiously, leaving their flashlights off. Gengar scouted ahead. After a while, they spotted Team Magma hiding behind a cluster of rocks farther down the passage.

Team Aqua stood beyond them.

Its members were saying something to the man they had kidnapped, but they were too far away for Ethan to hear.

He signaled to Gengar, which understood at once, sank into the ground, and headed toward them from below.

Before it reached them, the situation changed.

Perhaps Team Magma had grown impatient or decided the moment was right. Its members burst from cover and attacked Team Aqua.

The surprise worked. Team Aqua had not expected an assault. Several members fell before they could organize a proper defense.

While everyone was distracted, Ethan and Misty crept to the rocks Team Magma had just left. From there, they could hear the voices and the sounds of fighting clearly.

They could see the members of both teams properly now, too.

Each side had about a dozen people in matching uniforms. Only the two women leading them stood out from their teams.

Team Aqua's leader was a tall woman of about twenty-six or twenty-seven. She wore sunglasses on her head instead of a bandanna and no standard uniform. A top left her midriff bare, and her trousers exposed the outsides of her thighs. Slightly wavy black hair fell down her back. Her skin was a little dark, and although Ethan did not consider her particularly beautiful, she looked sharp and capable.

Team Magma's leader was also a woman, but much younger, perhaps eighteen or nineteen. She wore a red hood like her teammates. Pale purple hair and a delicate face showed beneath it. Her red short skirt and high boots gave her a look both demure and cute.

The two leaders clearly knew each other. While their teams fought, they stood in place and stared coldly across the field.

The hooded young woman called to the taller one. “You weren't expecting us, were you? Team Magma has an intelligence network too. Don't think you're the only ones who found this lead!”

The tall woman was unmoved. “You caught us off guard. Do you really think that's enough? How naive. We surveyed this place a week ago. We know where every passage leads. If we decide to leave, none of you can stop us.”

“Leave? It won't be that easy,” the young woman said. “Enough talk. Hand over that man!”

She meant the bespectacled man Team Aqua had kidnapped. Ethan looked at him more carefully. He appeared to be around forty, with small round glasses, untidy hair, and a vest. He had the look of a scholar.

Rope bound him, and he stood behind the tall woman with a panicked expression, as though he had no idea what was happening.

The tall woman gave her younger rival a sidelong glance. “Dream on. We went to a great deal of trouble to bring Professor Cozmo here. Why would we give him to you? Courtney, I know what Team Magma wants. You plan to find the meteorite, stir up the volcano here, and make it erupt, all for that ridiculous dream of yours!”

“Our dream is ridiculous?” Courtney frowned. “Shelly, yours is just as absurd! Why are you after the meteorite? For your own laughable dream, of course!”

“What did you say?”

“You heard me!”

The two traded barbs with satisfaction, but their argument also told Ethan and Misty what was going on.

There must be a meteorite with unusual power somewhere here, and the kidnapped professor was an expert on it. Both teams wanted his help finding it for their hidden plans.

The fighting inside the cave continued fiercely. Pokémon fell on both sides. Team Magma had the advantage, but Team Aqua was recovering from its initial confusion and retreating toward a side passage as it fought.

Ethan and Misty exchanged a look.

“What do you want to do?” Misty whispered.

“Isn't it obvious?” Ethan grinned and took a Great Ball from his waist pouch. “Neither side is here for anything good. We'll use the confusion to give them both a big surprise.”

Misty looked at the ball. “You're bringing out a Legendary Pokémon already?”

She knew Ethan well enough to recognize that he kept his Legendary Pokémon in several Great Balls, apart from his other powerful partners. The ball in his hand told her what he intended.

“We've crossed these people before,” Ethan said. “There's no reason to worry about offending them again. Let's make this count.”

He quietly opened the Great Ball. Blue light flashed, and Raikou appeared beside him.

Raikou shook its head, seemingly unaccustomed to the darkness.

Ethan stroked its head and whispered instructions into its ear. Then he pointed ahead. Raikou sprang away like a streak of lightning, racing toward Team Magma and Team Aqua.

The golden electricity drew Courtney's and Shelly's attention almost at once.

“Who's there?” they shouted together.

They looked up and saw Raikou racing toward them amid crackling lightning. It was almost upon them.

“Is that... a Pokémon?”

Both women frowned. Neither recognized it immediately.

That was understandable. Raikou was a Legendary Pokémon of Johto, rarely seen even by people who lived there. These two were from Hoenn. Even if they had heard of Raikou, they would not necessarily connect the name to the Pokémon before them at a glance.

While they hesitated, Raikou reached the teams.

A dazzling burst of electricity erupted from its body and spread in every direction. The members of both teams and their Pokémon were caught unprepared and struck by the current.

No one had expected this turn.

When the teams first saw the extraordinary stranger, its grace and mystery had drawn their attention. They thought they had found a rare Pokémon. None expected it to attack without warning.

“Damn it! Are we under attack?”

Courtney and Shelly both looked toward the direction Raikou had come from, but they saw only darkness.

No one?

Was this a wild Pokémon attacking them because they had entered its territory? They had never heard of a Pokémon like this living near Meteor Falls.

Raikou's arrival changed the balance of the fight at once.

Team Magma was closest to the first burst and suffered the heaviest losses. But Raikou's attacks struck indiscriminately. A second wave of electricity brought down many Team Aqua members and Pokémon too.

Realizing how powerful the unknown Pokémon was, Courtney and Shelly gave the same order almost together. “Stop staring! Catch it!”

The teams broke off their fight and turned their Pokémon against Raikou.

Raikou was agile, though, and its electric attacks were fierce. Their ordinary Pokémon were no match for it. As soon as they engaged, more of them fell.

Meanwhile, Ethan and Misty slipped toward the battlefield.

In the confusion, no one noticed two dark figures drawing closer to Professor Cozmo.
