The blazing sun baked the ground.

For some reason, the weather had grown much hotter since the Indigo League ended. Cicadas hidden out of sight called without pause, their noise enough to wear on anyone's nerves.

Through that sweltering heat, a man and a woman arrived at one of Kanto's best-known holiday destinations: Blue Lagoon.

The resort was as busy as ever. People shopped, surfed, swam, and played beach volleyball. Even the summer heat could not keep them from enjoying themselves.

The streets were crowded, but passersby still turned to look at the pair. Both appeared to be around twenty. The woman was beautiful, her long black hair lifting in the occasional sea breeze. Her refined, cool expression drew more than a few admiring glances.

Her companion was tall and slender. A cap and large sunglasses hid most of his face.

They were, of course, Ethan and Sabrina, traveling together after leaving the Indigo Plateau. Walking side by side, they looked like a well-matched couple.

Sabrina noticed the stares and frowned. "Why is everyone looking at us?"

Ethan smiled. "You really don't get out much, do you? If I passed someone as beautiful as you on the street, I'd probably stare too."

Now that was how to answer a question. He had given her an explanation and slipped in a compliment at the same time.

Sabrina's mouth curved upward. She clearly appreciated it. For a moment, even the sun overhead seemed less oppressive.

They passed several tall buildings, and the view opened onto a long stretch of coast and fine white sand.

Ethan watched the people playing on the beach. "If we weren't trying to reach the Orange Islands soon, I'd love to spend a few carefree days here."

In a good mood, Sabrina tucked a strand of hair behind her ear.

"I've heard the Orange Islands are even prettier and better suited to a holiday. If you want to rest, there's no need to rush. Once you've finished what you want to do there, we can find somewhere quiet and slow down for a while."

At once, Ethan imagined the two of them alone on a tropical island, Sabrina sunbathing on the beach in a swimsuit.

She would look incredible. Would she ask him to help put on sunscreen?

Heat rushed to his face. He rubbed his suddenly itchy nose and forced himself to stop imagining it. The day was hot enough already. If he kept thinking that way, he might actually get a nosebleed—and that would be humiliating.

He turned his mind to their route instead.

The easiest way to reach the Orange Islands was to board a designated ferry in Vermilion City. But that would mean more time traveling overland first, and it would take some of the adventure out of the trip.

After discussing it, they had chosen the shorter route through Blue Lagoon. From here they planned to cross the sea on Pokémon.

Before leaving the coast, however, they went into an outdoor equipment store. Sabrina had packed lightly, and a longer journey would inevitably involve meals and nights away from comfortable rooms. She needed some practical supplies.

"Welcome!" a clerk called as they entered.

Cool air washed over Ethan and brought him instantly back to life. Summer really did make him appreciate air conditioning.

Looking at the shelves, he swept out an arm grandly.

"Everything's on me today. Miss Sabrina, shop to your heart's content!"

Show-off. Sabrina rolled her eyes, though she let him have his moment.

Ethan knew her family had money. Still, he had just won a substantial prize, and she had set aside a comfortable life to travel with him. He was not going to make her pay for the equipment they needed.

Faced with the rows of goods, Sabrina looked uncertain.

"I've never traveled like this before. I'm not sure what I should buy. What do you suggest?"

Ethan looked around. Clothes came first. She would be walking a long way, and her usual outfit was hardly suited to it. Something more athletic would be cooler and more comfortable.

He helped her choose a few things. When Sabrina emerged after changing, his eyes lit up.

Instead of her usual black stockings, she wore a light top and shorts. She looked much more at ease in the heat, though the outfit drew his attention to her long, pale legs.

Sabrina, wearing clothes like these for the first time, seemed a little shy.

"Well? Does it look all right?"

Ethan gave her a thumbs-up. "Of course. Trust my eye. You're beautiful and you've got a great figure—you make anything look good."

She was half amused, half exasperated. Had he coated his tongue in honey? She had never realized he could be so smooth.

Once they had chosen clothes, they picked out water bottles and a few other supplies. But when they came to the sleeping bags, Sabrina shook her head.

"I don't like sleeping out in the open. It doesn't feel safe." She looked toward the tents. "Let's get one of those instead. Even a simple tent would keep out wind and rain. It would feel a little more like a temporary home."

A tent?

Ethan followed her gaze. The smallest tents on display slept two people.

Surely she wasn't suggesting they share one. She just didn't want to sleep outside.

He nodded. "All right. Pick whichever you like."

After looking them over, Sabrina chose a large four-person tent.

Did she dislike cramped spaces? Ethan did not understand her choice, but since she offered no explanation, he did not ask.

They added canned food and several more items. By the time Ethan paid and they left the store, each of them carried an enormous backpack.

With Ethan's earlier luggage as well, it was too much to carry in this heat. They would be sweating after only a few hundred meters, and the load would slow them down.

Ethan opened a Poké Ball and released Charizard.

Charizard was strong and could use the wind to help it fly. This much weight would hardly trouble it. They might as well let it stay out and help with the journey.
