Thud!

Gyarados's enormous body crashed to the ground, and its weight made the earth tremble under everyone's feet.

After all that effort, and an entire Team Rocket research building collapsing, we've finally stopped it...

Still, so many Pokémon had surrounded one frenzied Gyarados, only for several of them to be knocked out. It would be embarrassing if word spread. These were not wild Pokémon; they were the trained mainstays of their Trainers' teams.

They were fortunate that Will and Karen, future Elite Four members, had joined the fight. If everyone had been an International Police officer like Looker, dozens or even a hundred Pokémon might have fallen, and there might have been other casualties too.

Yet judging from Will's and Karen's Pokémon, they still had some distance to go before reaching Elite Four strength. They needed more time to train their teams.

Ethan walked to Gyarados's head. After exposure to the wave, it had grown so much that its enormous head was nearly as tall as he was.

Its body was charred, but it had not fainted. It lay helplessly on the ground, weakly holding its eyes half open. Thankfully, the madness had gone from its gaze. Ethan saw its old gentleness there and finally let out a breath.

"Calm again, are you? You caused everyone a lot of trouble, even if you didn't mean to..."

The battle reminded Ethan of when Gyarados had first evolved from Magikarp. It had gone mad then too, and only settled down after taking a beating much like this one. They had somehow gone through the same thing again.

Even so, something good had come of the disaster. The wave had driven Gyarados out of its mind and caused all that destruction, but it had also changed its size and color and increased its strength.

Every aspect of Gyarados now seemed to have reached its limit. Ethan felt certain there was no stronger Gyarados anywhere in the world. If he used its Mega Stone to evolve it into Mega Gyarados someday, it would become a nightmare even more opponents would never forget.

He returned Gyarados to its Poké Ball and thanked Will, Karen, and Looker.

"Thank you, all of you. I'm glad there was no damage we can't put right. Otherwise, I'd feel terrible."

If they had not been there, Ethan would have released the three Legendary birds, and handling Gyarados would probably have been easier. Still, they had helped him without hesitation, and he owed them his thanks.

"Don't call me the next time something like this happens," Karen said with a snort, then teased him. "Honestly, your Gyarados was harder to deal with than Team Rocket!"

Ethan laughed and offered a few more words to placate them.

Officers who had been pursuing the Rockets returned one after another. They had searched all around but found no trace of Sebastian or Tyson.

Looker shook his head in disappointment. The two men in charge of the research facility had escaped, casting a shadow over the operation.

He turned to Karen, bringing the conversation back to their mission and giving Ethan a way out of the teasing. "How did things go on your side?"

Karen shrugged. "We cleared the base completely. But that executive called Lance got away."

"He escaped too?"

Looker was disappointed again. Still, they had destroyed a base and a research facility, stopped Project R, and arrested a great many Rocket members. The operation had weakened Team Rocket and met its main objectives. Nothing in the world could go perfectly every time.

Looker thanked the others again. He offered to take them back to camp for a celebration, but Ethan, Will, and Karen declined. Looker left some officers to secure the site and see if anything useful could be recovered. Then he led the prisoners away.

After he was gone, Ethan exchanged contact information with Will and Karen and said goodbye to them. The Team Rocket matter was settled for now. He could return to challenging Gyms in peace.

Ethan and Sabrina left the shore of the Lake of Rage and headed through the thick forest toward Mahogany Town.

The sun was already sinking, dimming the woods. If they did not want to sleep outdoors, they needed to reach town before dark. Fortunately, there was still time before sunset, so they did not have to hurry.

As they neared the far edge of the forest, a Pokémon resting on a branch caught Sabrina's attention. She stopped and looked up at it with open curiosity.

"Hm? What is it?" Ethan asked. When she did not answer, he followed her gaze.

It was a Natu.

Most of Natu's body was a round green head. Its crest, tail, and feet were red. Its bright wings had black stripes. It looked a little vacant, but its almost human eyes gave it an uncanny air.

Ethan recalled that the designers had drawn on the bird worship of Indigenous peoples of the Americas when creating it. More specifically, he thought they had taken inspiration from an attendant crow in Mayan mythology. Its colors had come from other birds regarded as spiritual.

"Interested in it?"

Sabrina nodded lightly, and Ethan could not help smiling. In all their time traveling together, the only other Pokémon she had shown an interest in catching was the talking Slowking from the Orange Islands. This was only the second. How rare.

"Natu is a Psychic-type Pokémon, isn't it?"

As he spoke, Ethan took out his Pokédex and pointed it at Natu.

The device chimed. "Natu, the Tiny Bird Pokémon. Psychic and Flying type. At this stage, its wings are not fully developed, so it cannot fly and moves by hopping. It usually searches for food on the ground, but on rare occasions it hops onto branches to peck at fresh buds. It is said to be forever watching something. If you meet its eyes, it will stare back, but the moment you make the slightest movement, it will hop away."
