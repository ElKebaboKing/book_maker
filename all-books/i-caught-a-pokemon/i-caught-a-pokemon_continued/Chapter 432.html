After the opening ceremony ended, the competitors were given a brief rest to make their final preparations. With the spectators waiting eagerly, the tournament soon began in earnest.

Today's matches formed an elimination tournament, but they would not immediately determine the champion. The sixty-four competitors would first pass through one round after another until only four remained. Tomorrow morning, those four would be reduced to two finalists, who would then battle for the Whirl Cup championship.

Erika and Brock once again sat in the stands, unable to cheer for the others from nearby. Since they had bought their tickets so late, they were forced to sit in the farthest section.

As in the preliminaries, each Trainer could use only one Pokémon during an elimination match. This forced the competitors to bring out their strongest Pokémon in peak condition. Although the format could not reveal the full extent of a Trainer's abilities, it greatly accelerated the tournament. A winner could be crowned in only two days.

That might be unfavorable for local tourism, but it suited Ethan and his friends perfectly. The tournament would not delay their journey.

Of the three, Ash was the first to battle. His opponent was a boy even younger than him who carried a fishing rod. For his opening match, Ash sent out Totodile. The other boy flicked his fishing rod, casting a Poké Ball over the water. When it opened, a Kingdra appeared above the surface.

Since their own matches had not yet begun, Ethan and Misty watched from the competitors' waiting area and focused their attention on Ash's battle.

In terms of typing, Totodile was purely Water type, while Kingdra was both Water and Dragon type. Kingdra had a fourfold resistance to Water, rendering Totodile's Water-type moves all but useless. Kingdra's Dragon-type attacks, by contrast, remained completely effective.

The matchup appeared extremely unfavorable for Ash, but as a master of battling at a type disadvantage, he had long since grown accustomed to such situations and paid it no mind.

As soon as the match began, Totodile dove into the water. Kingdra immediately used Twister beneath the surface. As its body spun faster and faster, it created an enormous vortex reaching between sea and sky, sweeping Totodile inside!

Totodile's body whirled around in midair with the current, unable to break free. Fortunately, the younger boy eventually ordered Kingdra to stop Twister, finally allowing Totodile to escape.

Kingdra's assault was not over. As Totodile fell, Kingdra fired a Water Gun directly at it.

Totodile twisted its body in midair and narrowly avoided the attack.

Totodile had been suppressed from their very first exchange. The sight poured cold water over Ash's confidence and made him abandon any thought of underestimating his opponent!

He had expected to sweep through the tournament just as he had in the preliminaries and charge all the way to the final. He had never imagined his first opponent would already be this strong!

The battle continued. The two Pokémon fought from the stone pillars into the water, where Kingdra expelled a thick cloud of ink. The ink polluted the seawater and blocked Totodile's vision. Unable to tell what Kingdra was planning, Totodile could only climb back onto a pillar and wait for it to reappear.

The black ink spread through the water, staining a vast area and making it impossible to see below the surface. Hidden from view, Kingdra waited like a hunter for Totodile to expose an opening.

Misty immediately frowned at the situation.

"Are all the opponents in the elimination rounds this powerful? They're on a completely different level from the preliminary competitors!"

Ethan shook his head noncommittally. "I wouldn't put it that way. Ash was simply unlucky to meet an opponent like this in his first match. Look—Kingdra is in its final evolutionary stage, so its physical abilities already surpass Totodile's by a considerable margin. It's also been raised quite well, and its tactics are sound. There's a reason Ash is struggling."

Misty fell quiet as she looked at the boy several years younger than Ash.

Seeing Ash unexpectedly drawn into a difficult battle gave her a sense of danger as well. She no longer dared to be careless in the slightest.

The battle had now reached a stalemate. The boy seemed intent on wearing down Ash's patience and waiting for him to expose an opening. Fortunately, Ash's Totodile was too carefree to worry about any of that. With its simple, happy nature, it began dancing for its own amusement!

Its mischievous, adorable behavior swept away the tense atmosphere and sent the audience into peals of laughter!

The other boy was still young, after all. Now he was the one who lost patience. Feeling that Totodile was looking down on him, he hurriedly ordered Kingdra to surface and attack with Hydro Pump.

Kingdra's belly swelled larger and larger. Totodile realized what was happening, leaped toward it, and created an instantly iconic scene!

Totodile opened its enormous mouth and bit down on Kingdra's long snout. In an instant, the Hydro Pump building inside Kingdra was trapped in its mouth!

The water Kingdra was about to unleash continued to swell its belly, inflating it like a water balloon!

The sudden turn of events left the boy frozen in place. With so little experience, how could he ever have encountered an opponent who refused to follow common sense?

He was not alone. Even the audience stared in astonishment at such a bizarre maneuver!

Kingdra continued to swell until Ash finally ordered Totodile to release its mouth. The water inside Kingdra found an outlet and erupted in a high-pressure jet. Like a punctured water balloon, Kingdra spun through the air and shot high above the battlefield!

Pfft, pfft, pfft...

Perhaps the water pressure was too powerful, or perhaps there was simply too much water. Kingdra left a blue afterimage in the sky, moving too quickly for the human eye to follow!

It spun more than a dozen times high overhead. Only after expelling the last mouthful of water did it plummet headfirst into the pool!

Splash!

Kingdra struck the water and threw up an enormous spray. Several seconds later, it floated back to the surface, unconscious and with its eyes rolled back!

It had clearly fainted completely!

The referee needed only one glance before announcing the result.

To be honest, Ethan had expected Ash to win before the match began, but he had never imagined victory would come this way.

Ethan knew perfectly well that Ash was the sort of person who won through the unexpected, yet he still managed to find fresh ways to surprise him!

The entertaining spectacle sent the audience into roaring laughter and completely reshaped their understanding of what could happen in a battle.

Of course, one person's joy was not another's. The younger boy watched himself lose in such a ridiculous fashion and felt as though his entire understanding of the world had collapsed!

In the waiting area, Ethan and Misty applauded along with the crowd.

Looking back over the match, Totodile had not actually inflicted any damage on Kingdra aside from biting its snout. Ultimately, Kingdra had been defeated by its own Hydro Pump!
