"Charizard, our turn! Use Dragon Rage!"

Ethan had spent most of this battle reacting to Drake's attacks. With the two Pokémon separated, a brief opening appeared, and he seized it.

Charizard opened its mouth and fired a blue-green beam toward Dragonite. Hitting a moving target in the air was difficult, though. Dragonite beat its wings lightly and slipped aside before the attack reached it. The energy skimmed past and shot into the sky, piercing a low cloud before breaking into particles and fading away.

Drake saw Charizard's attack end. "Dragonite, use Skull Bash!"

Dragonite gathered speed and dived like a meteor, carrying all its weight toward Charizard. Charizard had landed only moments ago. It could not take off quickly enough to evade the charge.

"Don't dodge," Ethan called. "Take it!"

Dragonite came down with a rush of wind. At the instant of impact, Charizard turned sideways and caught one of its arms in both claws. Dragonite's momentum continued to drive them downward. Charizard's muscles strained as it swung Dragonite around and around like a windmill. After more than a dozen turns, Charizard staggered, but it had bled off the force of the dive. It let go and flung Dragonite across the ground.

Dragonite rolled through the grass and scrambled back to its feet. The thick growth had cushioned the landing, leaving it unhurt apart from a little dizziness and fresh grass juice smeared across its body.

Now! Ethan saw it struggling to clear its head. "Use Dragon Claw!"

Charizard rushed in, blue light gathering around both claws as it raised them.

"Dragonite, dodge!" Drake warned. Dragonite shook its head, but the moment of dizziness had cost it too much. By the time it looked up, Charizard's claws were already before its face. They struck its belly several times. Dragonite grunted and stumbled back.

Ethan knew better than to let it recover. "Use Air Slash!"

Dragonite spread its wings to take off. Charizard cut the air with its claws before it could rise. A crescent-shaped wave struck Dragonite in the abdomen.

Bang! Dragonite flew backward. The two successive attacks had drained a great deal of its strength.

Charizard breathed out, relieved to have finally gained an edge. It relaxed for a heartbeat. Dragonite rose from where it had fallen and fired Water Gun without warning. The stream struck Charizard squarely and drove it back a few steps.

Even after being knocked down, Dragonite's strength and quick reactions let it answer immediately. The balance shifted a little toward Drake again.

"Evenly matched!" the announcer said. "Both Pokémon are landing attacks. I thought Charizard had taken control and might end the battle, but it looks as though the fight will continue!"

Privately, he was disheartened. Even if Dragonite beat Charizard, how would it face Ethan's other five Pokémon after exhausting itself here?

On the field, Charizard and Dragonite steadied themselves and paused on opposite sides. Both were breathing harder now, but neither took its eyes off the other.

Drake looked pale after seeing Dragonite thrown to the ground. His hand trembled inside his sleeve. Am I going to lose? How long had it been since he last felt this helpless before a possible defeat? A year? Two? Even before becoming Supreme Gym Leader, he had rarely been pushed this far.

He looked toward Ethan. The Indigo League champion's expression remained calm. Drake could only acknowledge his strength.

A breeze moved across the field and stirred their clothes. It seemed to signal a new exchange. The Pokémon moved before either Trainer spoke.

But Drake's approach had changed. Dragonite's recent setback seemed to have shaken his confidence. He was no longer pressing in with relentless attacks. Instead, he kept Dragonite at a distance and tried to wear Charizard down with ranged moves.

"Dragonite, use Water Gun!"

Charizard dodged the stream. When it tried to close in, Dragonite retreated on Drake's command, always keeping a safe gap.

A change of tactics? A battle of attrition? Ethan raised an eyebrow. Drake was fighting more cautiously with every exchange.

Ethan directed several attempts to get close. Each time, Drake attacked while withdrawing and denied Charizard an opening. Drake might be content to fight that way; Ethan had no wish to prolong the match.

He scanned the grassy field and found an idea. If you want to hide from me, I'll leave you nowhere to hide.

"Charizard, use Flamethrower!"

"Fire?" the announcer wondered aloud when he saw Charizard preparing the attack. "Ethan knows Dragonite resists Fire-type moves. Why waste an opening on that?"

Ethan gave no answer. Charizard sent a stream of flame across the field. Dragonite did not even spend energy countering it; a beat of its wings carried it safely aside.

Whoosh!

Dragonite had escaped the direct attack, but stray sparks landed in the tall grass nearby. Flames spread, climbing higher by the second. The wind fed them, and the thick growth carried them onward. With a rising crackle, patches of fire joined until half the grass field was burning.
