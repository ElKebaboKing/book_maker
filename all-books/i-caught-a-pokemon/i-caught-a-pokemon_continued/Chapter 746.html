Ethan asked Kennedy to mail the Meteorite Key Stone directly to Ever Grande City. He and Misty rested another night in Lilycove City before setting out for Fortree City.

At the Pokémon Center, Ethan took the chance to change his lineup. He transferred Articuno, Zapdos, Moltres, Raikou, and Suicune to Sabrina. Deoxys and the black Rayquaza were now the only Legendary Pokémon he kept with him. He sent his red Gyarados and Crystal Steelix to Cerise Park and brought back the team he had been training.

With Scizor, Eevee, Milotic, and Metang in hand again, Ethan finally felt like an ordinary Trainer. No sudden criminal organization to confront, no Hoenn legends to worry about. He could challenge a Gym at his own pace and focus on training his Pokémon. Wasn't that what a Trainer's life was supposed to be?

Before leaving Lilycove, he called Phoebe and arranged to meet her in Fortree. He still had the Blue Orb and Red Orb. It was time to return them.

Why not take them to Mt. Pyre himself? It was out of his way. Besides, after all the help he had given Phoebe, asking her to come to him was hardly unreasonable.

Ethan and Misty headed west from Lilycove. The day was mild, with a light breeze. They had no need to hurry. Relaxed for the first time in a while, they could enjoy the scenery and notice things they had passed over before. It was a rare pleasure after weeks of tense travel.

After more than half a day, they passed a Safari Zone, a protected habitat where wild Pokémon lived and bred. The law and the Pokémon League protected it, but visitors who paid the fee could use the provided Safari Balls to catch Pokémon without battling.

For anyone who disliked fighting, it could be an ideal place to catch a partner—if luck was on their side. Safari Balls did not have a high success rate, and a bad day could make it dismal. That was why the supposedly easier chance did not draw every Trainer.

Ethan had no Pokémon he wanted to catch there. He and Misty went straight past its entrance.

As they left the reserve behind, Misty could not resist teasing Ash. “You'd pass it by because you know there's nothing there you want. If Ash came this way, he'd definitely go in for a look!”

After traveling with Ash through two regions, she knew him well. He could never pass up a place like this.

“You're right,” Ethan said with a smile. “He loves finding something to get excited about. Besides, this is on the way from Verdanturf Town to Lilycove City. He must have passed it after challenging the Verdanturf Gym. I'd bet he already went inside.”

They followed the road north. After walking another few hours and stopping for lunch, they came past a high mountain range. Ethan glanced across the nearly empty road and spotted someone he had met before.

“Is that...?”

Misty followed his surprised gaze. A man of about twenty-four or twenty-five, with light blue hair and a well-cut black suit, walked ahead of them. He looked every inch a refined young gentleman.

“Do you know him?” she asked.

“You don't recognize him? Oh, you haven't met.” Ethan explained, “That's Steven, Mr. Stone's son. His father runs Devon Corporation.”

“So that's Steven.” Misty had heard the name from Ethan and Mr. Stone, even if she had never seen the man. “What's he doing here?”

Ethan wondered too, until he looked at the surrounding mountains. What did Steven enjoy most? Searching for rare stones. It was a running joke about him, but also true. Something unusual in this area must have attracted him.

Steven was about to enter a cave when he turned, as though he had sensed their gaze. He spotted Ethan and Misty, paused in surprise, then changed direction and walked over. His eyes briefly rested on Misty before he greeted Ethan with a smile.

“I didn't expect to run into you here.”

“A coincidence,” Ethan said. “If you're here, does that mean there's a special stone nearby?”

Steven shared his father's well-known interest in stones, so the guess did not trouble him. “That's right.” He moved aside to show the cave entrance. “I heard a Trainer exploring there discovered a hidden passage into its deeper levels. I thought there might be rarer stones inside.”

That sounded like Steven. A wealthy young man could afford to travel wherever his hobby led him.

A secret cave in these mountains, though... Ethan touched his chin. “Do you mean the Scorched Slab?”

Steven looked at him in surprise. “You know about it? You're not even from Hoenn, yet you know a place that obscure.”

Ethan had not foreseen anything. He remembered the cave from a game he had played. The details were hazy, but he recalled a rare Heatran somewhere in its depths.

Heatran was a Legendary Pokémon, and some considered it powerful. Ethan found it ugly. It held no appeal for him, even if one truly lived there.

“I heard the name by chance,” he said, waving the subject away. “I travel a lot and hear things. Something reminds me of one of them now and then. I don't actually know more than the name.”

Steven accepted that. Then he seemed to remember something else. “Kennedy told me you helped my father again recently.”

It took Ethan a moment to realize he meant the meteorite. “I wouldn't call it a favor. We helped each other. Without Mr. Stone's information, I couldn't have caught a powerful Pokémon I wanted.”

“Deoxys?” Steven smiled. “A pity I wasn't there. If I'd known, perhaps I'd have caught it myself.”

Ethan had doubts. Steven specialized in Steel-type Pokémon; would he even have tried to catch Deoxys? And if he had gone, he would also have faced Courtney's Team Magma members, Shelly's Team Aqua members, and Rayquaza's arrival. Could he have overcome all of them and caught Deoxys, as Ethan had? Even a former Champion might not have managed it so easily.

They had met by chance and both had places to go. After a short conversation, they said goodbye. Misty watched Steven disappear into the cave and offered her impression.

“He seems genuinely pleasant,” she said after a moment. “Polite, easy to talk to, without any of the arrogance you might expect from a wealthy heir. He's confident in his strength too, and I get the feeling he hates losing.”

“That's a good reading of him,” Ethan said. “Steven really is impressive. There's a reason he's so popular in Hoenn.”

The encounter soon slipped from their minds. They left the mountains and traveled for another day. By the following evening, they could just make out the lights of Fortree City ahead, though they still had some distance to go.

Fortree was known as a city in the trees, at play with nature. It lay in northern Hoenn at the foot of a long mountain range. The city was not large, but dense forest wrapped around it. Trees hid even its taller buildings. From a distance, it was hard to tell where the forest ended and the city began.

Tree houses stood throughout Fortree, built among tall, straight trunks and linked by hanging bridges. It lacked the bustle of a metropolis, but the green canopy and birdsong gave it a charm of its own. Most residents walked. Cars were rare, and roads rarer still.

Frequent heavy rain made the trees grow taller here than elsewhere. People said the residents were unusually healthy and long-lived. Wild Bug-type Pokémon often flew past the tree houses.

It was late when Ethan and Misty arrived, so they went straight to the Pokémon Center. At dinner, Misty heard that Fortree's annual Feather Carnival had taken place only a few days earlier. The sky-themed celebration drew the whole city together. She sighed at what they had missed.

“I wish we could have gone.”

She was a lively ten-year-old girl. Of course a festival would appeal to her.

“We couldn't help being delayed,” Ethan said. “You'll have time to enjoy yourself at the Ever Grande Conference. That will be more exciting than any carnival.”

...

The Fortree Gym stood on the outskirts and was the only building in the city that was not built in the trees. It resembled a four-sided step pyramid, like those of the Maya, rising hundreds of meters. Without a Flying-type Pokémon to carry a challenger, reaching the battlefield at the top meant climbing a long, exhausting staircase.

Two stone Pokémon statues flanked its entrance. Each stood more than ten meters tall, wings spread and head raised as if calling to the sky. Looking up at them from below was imposing.

After a night's rest, Ethan felt full of energy. The next morning, he officially came to challenge the Fortree Gym.

He and Misty walked down a long passage toward the entrance. A man who was clearly a staff member came out to meet them, smiling.

“Here to challenge the Gym?”

“We are,” Misty said before Ethan could answer. “But you came out as if you were expecting us. How did you know we were here?”

He gestured proudly at the building behind him. “This is the tallest structure in Fortree. From inside, we've got eyes as sharp as a hawk's. We notice anyone who walks up this passage. Come in—Winona, our Gym Leader, has been waiting for you!”
