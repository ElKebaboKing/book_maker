After circling the city, Ross finally brought the gondola alongside a canal bank.

He had shown Ethan and the others around for quite a while. They had passed nearly every attraction, and at least from the waterways, there was little left to see.

As they climbed ashore, Ross smiled and pointed to a nearby restaurant. "They make the best pancakes in Altomare. Give them a try!"

"Thank you for taking the time to show us the city," Misty said on behalf of the group. "We really appreciate it." Ross had originally invited her aboard, so it made sense for her to thank him.

"Think nothing of it." Ross waved a hand. "We Altomare locals are a friendly bunch. Besides, it was an honor to have the race champion on my boat. Enjoy the rest of your trip!"

They waved goodbye. Watching Ross walk away, Ethan reflected that although the man seemed a little frivolous, he was actually decent company.

What Ethan did not know was that Ross had first invited Misty aboard because she had caught his eye and he wanted a chance to get to know her. When he saw Erika, he had been even more taken with her.

The moment when Ethan had noticed Ross doing his best to be charming and funny was precisely when Ross had been trying to impress them.

Once they were aboard, however, Ross realized that both Misty and Erika had their hearts set on Ethan. He gave up on the idea entirely.

At least he had not kept pestering either of them after that. In that respect, Ethan's impression of him was fair enough.

After Ross left, the group visited the restaurant he had recommended and ordered pancakes in several flavors.

"I want chocolate!"

"Banana for me!"

"I'll have cream and jam!"

They each took a big bite. Ross had been right: the pancakes were delicious.

Just then, Ash glanced around and realized Pikachu had disappeared.

"Huh? Where's Pikachu?"

Everyone helped look. At last they spotted Pikachu around a corner, standing by a fountain in a square, facing a teenage girl.

"Pikachu!" Ash hurried over with a smile. "What, have you made a new friend already?"

From a distance, Ethan caught sight of the girl's face, and his eyes sharpened.

He remembered Altomare well from the movie. Seeing Ash on the cruise ship had made him suspect the movie's events were about to unfold.

Now he recognized the girl at once. She was Latias in disguise. Other details he had forgotten slowly came back to him.

Up close, Ethan studied her appearance. She had short red hair with two odd little pigtails and a pretty face. Her green T-shirt, white shorts, pink boots, and black knee-high socks made her look like any ordinary girl.

The girl turned on the fountain's tap. Clear water spilled out, and Pikachu opened its mouth to drink. Only then did Ash realize it had wandered off because it was thirsty.

"Thank you," he told her.

She said nothing. Instead, she circled Ash several times, leaned in to study his face for a while, then ran toward the canal, leaving him thoroughly puzzled.

Misty came up beside Ash and watched the girl go. She looked just as confused. "Why didn't she say anything?"

At that moment, a speedboat appeared on the canal. Two fashionably dressed women rode in it. They were too far away for Ethan or the others to hear what they said to Latias. The girl suddenly fled in a panic, and the women threw Poké Balls, releasing their Pokémon to pursue her.

Latias darted into an alley. The two Pokémon followed without hesitation.

Before anyone else could react, Pikachu raced after them. Ethan and the others exchanged a glance, then hurried in the direction Latias had gone.

The women's Pokémon were an Espeon and an Ariados. They caught up with Latias near a small bridge. Espeon enveloped her in psychic power and lifted her into the air. Ariados then spat out a mass of silk, binding her hands and feet together.

Ethan and the others arrived in time to see it happen. Beneath the bridge, the two women pulled up in their speedboat.

"What are you doing?" Ash demanded.

Perhaps it was because the girl had helped Pikachu at the fountain, or perhaps he simply could not bear to see someone in trouble. Ash grabbed at the silk and tried to pull it off her.

The two women watched him without any hurry. One smiled. "We're just dressing the girl up."

"Dressing her up?" Ash shouted. "You call this dressing her up? Can't you see she doesn't want it?"

While Ash argued with them, Ethan took a closer look.

Both women appeared to be in their mid-twenties. One had long, wavy blonde hair and wore a pink cropped tube top. The other had short silver hair and a red cropped outfit. Their makeup was immaculate, and both wore bracelets and necklaces. They had a fashionable look with a tough, delinquent edge.

Ethan could not remember their names, but he knew they were notorious thieves.

The silver-haired woman leaned back. "Really? Well, we're enjoying it."

"That's right," the blonde added. "I'm enjoying it too."

Their flippant tone only made Ash angrier. He tore the silk from Latias and shouted at them, "What are you trying to do? Why would you treat a girl like this?"

"Honestly. Is that any way to address a lady?" The silver-haired woman gave a disdainful snort. "Ariados, teach this rude, meddlesome brat a lesson!"
