Somewhere on the seafloor beyond the Whirl Islands, a secret Team Rocket base lay hidden.

Built from steel atop a broad shelf of level rock, the base was an enormous arched structure more than thirty meters tall. Even in the pitch-black depths, the lights shining from within illuminated a vast area around it.

Countless Water Pokémon swam far beyond the base's perimeter, as though none dared approach.

Through its glass windows, people could occasionally be seen moving inside. A considerable number of Team Rocket members were clearly stationed here.

This was Team Rocket's most secretive research base in the Johto region. Its director was Dr. Namba, a vigorous elderly man already over sixty years old.

The old man's fiery red hair and beard reflected his explosive temper. Baldness had unfortunately left him with hair only around the sides of his head. Despite his remarkable academic achievements, no matter how often he corrected people, everyone somehow instinctively remembered his name incorrectly.

Dr. Namba now sat in the control room with several black-clad Team Rocket members standing on either side. The large screen before him displayed live footage transmitted by the search team.

Although the team continued requesting further instructions, Dr. Namba had no attention to spare for them. Instead, his gaze rested on the people surrounding Lugia.

More precisely, he stared fixedly at Ethan!

He studied Ethan until his brow furrowed deeply, then muttered, "Honestly, isn't that Ethan Vale, the man they call Team Rocket's greatest enemy? Why is he here? This has become troublesome..."

After thinking for a while, he waved over one of the Team Rocket members.

The man approached him and asked respectfully, "Dr. Sanba, what are your orders?"

The instructions Dr. Namba had been about to give lodged directly in his throat. He could only glare fiercely at the man. "It's not Sanba, it's Namba!"

"I'm sorry, Doctor..."

Dr. Namba sighed helplessly. "Have the nearby operations teams converge on that location. Begin with Plan A and attempt to capture Lugia."

The Team Rocket member also glanced at Ethan on the screen, then nodded solemnly. "Yes, understood!"

...

After helping Brock clean up the bowls and utensils, Ethan patted his full stomach and stretched comfortably.

With his hunger satisfied and the warm sun shining down, drowsiness gradually crept over him.

Misty yawned as well, then tilted her head toward Ethan and asked, "Ethan, what should we do now? We can't just sit here doing nothing and wait for Team Rocket forever, can we?"

The moment she finished, Ash sprang upright from the ground with a kip-up. He turned toward Richie, his eyes blazing. "Since we still have time, Richie, how about a battle?"

Looking at his former opponent, Richie shook his head. "Sorry, I'm not in the mood. Now that I've seen Lugia, all I can think about is how to protect it. I really can't summon any enthusiasm for a battle."

"I see..." Ash scratched his head awkwardly and sat down again. "That's fine. We can have a proper match at the Silver Conference instead!"

"I'm sorry again," Richie said apologetically. "I don't plan to enter the Silver Conference..."

"What!?"

Ash was not the only one shocked. Ethan, Misty, and Brock all turned to stare at Richie.

Misty asked in confusion, "Why wouldn't you enter? Everything was going fine."

Brock also tried to persuade him. "Exactly. After everything you learned at the Indigo League, you're sure to earn an even better result at the Silver Conference!"

Faced with their confusion, Richie shrugged. "My loss at the Indigo League helped me fully understand my weaknesses. That's why I'm traveling from place to place now, catching powerful Pokémon and constantly training. Maybe I'll consider entering another League Conference after my strength has improved dramatically."

"How could..." Ash stared blankly at Richie and sighed in disappointment.

He regretted losing the chance to battle Richie and also felt disappointed that he would have one fewer evenly matched rival in the future.

After a long silence, Ethan noticed the gloomy atmosphere and clapped his hands to draw everyone's attention.

"All right, cheer up. It's time to discuss our real problem."

Once everyone looked toward him, Ethan continued, "If we want to protect Silver and foil Team Rocket's plan, we need to hit them hard first. We're exposed while they're hidden, and we have no idea when they'll act. That leaves us extremely passive and puts us at a major disadvantage. I intend to strike first and seize the initiative ourselves!"

Misty was always Ethan's most loyal supporter. She had no objection whatsoever to his suggestion. All she needed to do was support him without a second thought.

"Exactly! Ethan is right!"

"But..." Richie frowned and offered a different opinion. "If we already know that bad people have targeted Silver, shouldn't we keep it as far from danger as possible? Wouldn't taking it away immediately be better? Calling the police might also be a good choice."

Before Ethan could respond, Misty had already retorted, "You're too naive! What good would calling the police do? Wouldn't that be the same as announcing Silver's existence to the entire world!? And even if we flee, where could we possibly go? Knowing Team Rocket, they'll never give up until they capture Silver!"

She turned back toward Ethan with anticipation. "Go on, Ethan. What should we do?"

Ethan glanced at the embarrassed Richie and nodded. "Misty is right. We have to handle this ourselves. My idea is to lure the snake from its hole. We'll draw Team Rocket to a battlefield that favors us, drag them into the open, defeat them, and send them to prison. Then they'll never dare target Silver again!"

"I agree," Erika said first. "Taking the initiative is better than defending passively. I support Ethan's plan."

Brock rubbed his chin. "Ethan's approach really could work. Like Misty said, I don't think calling the police is a good choice either."

Seeing that everyone appeared to approve of Ethan's idea, Richie finally looked toward Oliver and discovered that even he was nodding along.

Apparently, the boy had no objection whatsoever to anything the Sea Hero proposed.

Richie sighed helplessly. As the newcomer to the group, he wanted to ask only one thing: just what kind of magic did the title of Sea Hero possess...?
