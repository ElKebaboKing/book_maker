Faced with Aya's accusation, Ethan could have invented any number of excuses to deceive her and make her feel better. But after a brief hesitation, he ultimately chose to tell the truth.

"Sorry. I left in such a hurry that I forgot to wake you..."

"You... forgot!? Damn you!"

Aya's blood boiled at his answer. Ethan had apologized, but his calm expression showed no trace of sincerity. Her fury surged until her eyebrows nearly stood on end.

He had actually forgotten her! How infuriating!

Did he have any idea how she had felt when she woke the next morning, discovered that she had been abandoned on the grassland, and found her face covered in scribbles?

Aya took a deep breath and forced herself to calm down. Then she crossed her arms and gave a cold snort.

"No matter what you say, abandoning a helpless woman there was your fault! I swore that very day that I would avenge myself. I never expected you to walk straight into my hands today!"

Ethan shook his head helplessly.

A helpless woman?

Who is she talking about?

You're more dangerous than almost anyone...

Although Aya spoke fiercely, the shuriken buried in the ground before his feet made her intentions clear. The sharp weapons appeared dangerous, but she had never intended to injure him.

Just like last time, she merely wanted to establish her dominance and frighten him.

If Ethan had sensed genuine killing intent, Gengar, hidden within his shadow, would certainly have intercepted the shuriken.

They appeared to have landed dangerously close, but Aya had controlled them perfectly. Her precise command of strength and technique fully demonstrated her extraordinary skill as a female ninja.

After listening to their exchange, the headmaster looked from the furious Aya to the calm Ethan and asked in surprise, "What is this? Do the two of you know each other?"

Aya turned toward the headmaster and spoke solemnly. "Teacher, I have a score to settle with this man. Please clear a battlefield for us!"

The headmaster froze. Although he did not know what grudge or misunderstanding existed between them, he shook his head.

This was a school, hardly an appropriate place for them to settle a private dispute. If word spread, people unaware of the truth might believe that the academy was poorly managed and encouraged its students to pursue personal revenge.

Before the headmaster could voice his objection, Aya seemed to recognize his concern and added, "You may not know this, but this man is the Champion of the latest Indigo League Conference. This is a rare opportunity. I believe watching a battle against an opponent like him would be extremely valuable for the students!"

"Oh? The Indigo League Conference Champion!?"

The headmaster stared at Ethan in surprise and carefully studied the young man before him. He had never imagined that the distinguished Champion of the Indigo League Conference would visit their ninjutsu academy.

He's truly young...

To win a regional championship at such an age, his future prospects were boundless.

And if the students could observe a Trainer like him battling from close range, they would undoubtedly learn a great deal.

Seeing hesitation appear on the headmaster's face, Ethan knew that the man was tempted and could not help sighing.

He understood that today's battle had become unavoidable. The matter also needed to be resolved, or Aya would inevitably continue pestering him afterward.

With that in mind, he nodded to the headmaster as well.

"Please don't worry, Headmaster. This isn't some bitter feud. It's merely an ordinary battle."

Since Ethan himself said so, the headmaster's concern eased. He glanced into the distance and saw the students watching curiously. After thinking for a moment, he finally agreed.

"Very well. Follow me."

Once Ethan accepted the battle, a smile finally appeared on Aya's face.

Only then did she have the presence of mind to look beside him. The instant her gaze fell on Erika, she exclaimed, "Huh!? Miss Erika!? What are you doing here!?"

As one of Kanto's Gym Leaders, Erika was naturally known to many people. Aya was Koga's younger sister and knew the other Gym Leaders especially well, so she recognized Erika at a glance.

"Hello, Miss Aya. We meet again." Erika placed both hands before her abdomen and gave a graceful bow.

Seeing this, Aya hurriedly returned the greeting with equal formality.

When she straightened, Erika smiled and explained, "Mr. Ethan and I are currently traveling together through Johto so I can capture Grass-type Pokémon."

"Traveling with him?"

Aya was puzzled. She could not understand how the two of them had ended up together, but before she could ask anything more, the group had already reached a battlefield.

When the students training there saw the headmaster approach, they tactfully moved aside. They did not go far, however, and instead studied Ethan with great interest.

They had not heard the earlier conversation, but the tense atmosphere between Aya and this man made it obvious that something was wrong.

Aya was the academy's finest student. Unlike the ordinary pupils, she was a favored disciple whom the headmaster trained personally.

She was also an accomplished ninja in her own right, placing her on a completely different level from students who had taken up the art later in life.

With her alluring beauty and formidable strength, Aya had always been the academy's star pupil, and her every action drew considerable attention.

The headmaster waved a hand, ordering all the students to stop training and gather around.

He cleared his throat and introduced Ethan to everyone.

"Everyone, take a break. Allow me to introduce the gentleman standing beside me. This is Mr. Ethan, Champion of the latest Indigo League Conference. He and Aya will now hold a Pokémon battle here!"

The students were astonished to learn the man's identity. When they heard that he would battle Aya, they began whispering and discussing the news among themselves.

Who could have imagined that the Champion would come here?

Judging from what they had just witnessed, Ethan definitely knew Aya, and something unpleasant had happened between them.

Some especially imaginative students looked from Erika beside Ethan to Aya and constructed an entire tale of seduction and abandonment in their minds.

Had this Champion been caught dating two women at once?

Ethan and Aya walked onto the battlefield and took their positions on opposite sides.

Once word spread, even more students poured out of the classrooms and dormitories. They surrounded the battlefield so completely that not a gap remained. At a glance, the crowd unquestionably exceeded two hundred people.

For students accustomed to peaceful, monotonous study, this much-anticipated battle brought an unexpected thrill.

Ethan looked around at the enormous crowd. He understood that nearly every student in the entire academy had probably gathered here.
