"Hui's Ursaring is down! Incredible! Ethan's Tyranitar has shown its tremendous strength, easily overpowering its opponent in a contest of raw power!"

The announcer's ringing voice carried over Arena A, but it could not drown out the crowd's thunderous cheers.

As the referee raised his flag, the announcer continued, "Another clean three-to-zero! Ethan has dominated once again and secured his place among the final sixteen!"

On the field, Ethan calmly took out a Poké Ball and recalled Tyranitar. He glanced at the dejected young woman opposite him, waved to the spectators, and turned to leave.

He had given Hui no room to breathe, defeating her with a powerful lineup of Blastoise, Dragonite, and Tyranitar.

Hui was about as strong as Kimura had been. Neither had caused Ethan much trouble.

Ethan and Erika returned to the ground-floor lobby. They had barely sat down when their friends began to arrive, bringing news of the day's preliminary results.

Jimmy and Marina came first. The childhood friends from New Bark Town had each won their second match and advanced together to the next round.

Gary returned next. As cool as ever, he wore a faint, enigmatic smile. It looked as though he had won easily again.

Then came Travis and Casey. Both had beaten their opponents today. Their matches had not gone quite as smoothly, but the results were good.

Ash was the last to return. He had fought brilliantly against Vincent. After losing two rounds, he sent out Bulbasaur, which defeated two of Vincent's Pokémon in succession and brought the match to a draw.

Ash had more points overall, so the draw was enough to put him among the final sixteen.

Vincent was the only one of the three childhood friends—Jimmy, Marina, and himself—to lose out in the preliminaries.

It was a severe blow to his confidence. After saying goodbye to Marina, he felt too ashamed to stay and hurried away from Silver Town.

Life rarely went exactly as one hoped. Those who had been eliminated would have to try harder next year.

With so many competitors gone, the lobby suddenly seemed empty. But the remaining Trainers had little time to reflect. The large screen flickered, displaying the next round's matchups.

From the final sixteen onward, the competition would be a knockout tournament. The bracket was split into upper and lower halves. Competitors in different halves would not meet until the final; each half would produce one finalist.

The format had advantages and disadvantages. Ethan could not say whether it was good or bad.

When everyone gathered around to read the bracket, though, the pairings stunned them.

First was the seemingly fated clash between Ash and Gary. Friends and rivals who had set out from Pallet Town together, they would finally face each other here.

For both of them, it had come much sooner than expected. Neither had been mentally prepared.

As if sensing the other's reaction, they looked at each other. Their eyes held a tangle of emotions.

That was not all. Whether or not the Pokémon League had arranged it deliberately, Jimmy and Marina, the childhood friends from New Bark Town, had also been paired against each other!

Their expressions were even more revealing than Ash's and Gary's.

They liked each other, yet now they had to fight for the one place in the next round.

Ethan looked away from those two pairings and searched for his own name.

When he found it, he could only sigh. It felt as though the League had it in for him too.

His opponent was Casey, a good friend to both him and Ash.

He had barely finished laughing at Ash's and Jimmy's misfortune before the same thing happened to him.

Worse, he had landed in the same half of the bracket as Ash, Gary, Jimmy, and Marina. Whoever won those matches would be on course to face him in the rounds ahead.

Ethan shook his head with a rueful smile and searched for the other familiar names.

Travis, whom they had only recently met, was the one person to escape this knot of friends.

He had been placed in the other half of the bracket. For him, though, it hardly mattered. He knew Ethan and the others, but they were still acquaintances rather than close friends.

Everyone reacted differently to the draw. For most of them, these were the matchups they had least wanted.

The matches were tomorrow, and no one felt like chatting now. After brief goodbyes, they returned to their rooms.

"Let's go too," Ethan said.

As the others dispersed, he and Erika went back to their room.

Once inside, Ethan walked straight to the sofa and sat down in silence. Erika came up behind him and gently pressed her hands to his shoulders.

"What's wrong? Something on your mind?"

Ethan nodded and sighed. "Did you see their faces? Everyone was happy to reach the final sixteen. Then they saw the bracket and didn't know what to say. None of them expected this draw."

"It couldn't really be helped." Erika sat beside him and settled against his chest. "Seven of the sixteen competitors are from your group of friends. Some of you were bound to be paired together."

Ethan knew that. He would give his all against whoever stood across from him. For now, he simply felt bad for his friends.

"Enough thinking about it. Let's watch the recordings and see how strong Casey has become."

They rose and headed toward the computer. Walking behind Erika, Ethan admired her figure and suddenly wrapped his arms around her from behind.

"What are you doing?" Erika blushed at once. They had slept together many times now and shared a room for several days, but every intimate advance still made her shy. "It's the middle of the day!"

"So what if it is?" Ethan slipped a hand beneath her clothes and squeezed her as he kissed her. "I think daytime makes it even more exciting."
