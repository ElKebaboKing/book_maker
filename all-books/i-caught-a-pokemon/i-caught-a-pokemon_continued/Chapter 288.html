"Mahogany Town in ten days..." Ethan made an okay sign. "You can count on us!"

"Then I'll see you there."

Looker waved goodbye and walked away, disappearing alone around the street corner. Once again, only Ethan and Sabrina remained.

Ethan stretched and said lazily, "Well, that's settled for now. I can finally start challenging Johto's Gyms!"

"Which one will you challenge first?" Sabrina asked.

Ethan took out his phone and opened the map. "As the crow flies, the closest Gyms are Violet Gym in Violet City and Blackthorn Gym in Blackthorn City. The road to Blackthorn is harder to travel, but it's close to Mahogany Town. If we go there, we won't have to double back much to meet Looker."

Sabrina nodded. "So you're going to Blackthorn City?"

"No. I think I'll go to Violet City and challenge Violet Gym first."

Sabrina stared at him. What was wrong with this man's head? Why explain the convenient route to Mahogany if he intended to go the other way?

Amused by her expression, Ethan brushed her cheek and laughed. "That's exactly why I don't want to go there first. It's too close to Mahogany. Once I finish the Gym battle, we'd have nothing else to do. Violet City is in central Johto, with roads leading in every direction. It makes a convenient starting point. We can go to Blackthorn later."

His leap in reasoning had almost thrown Sabrina off balance, but she had no objection to the plan. In truth, even if Ethan decided to settle down here for the rest of his life, she would stay beside him.

It was nearly noon. After eating lunch nearby, they left New Bark Town and headed west, planning to pass through Cherrygrove City again on their way to Violet City.

They were in no hurry. They walked at an easy pace, talking and enjoying the scenery.

As they passed a forest divided by a river, Ethan read an online description of the area. Thousands of Heracross and Pinsir lived there. It was an excellent place to catch Bug-type Pokémon. Ethan had little interest in either species, though, and already had Scyther. He did not go into the forest.

At its edge, they spotted the girl they had met the day before.

She was trying to catch a Pidgey. Strangely, as she battled, she delivered her own play-by-play commentary and cheered herself on.

"Chikorita, this is the deciding inning! We absolutely can't lose! Go, go, go!"

Urged on by her cheers, Chikorita swayed the leaf on its head and fixed Pidgey with a serious stare. More than a dozen leaves, sharp as blades, gathered above it and shot toward the bird.

Whoosh! Whoosh!

The glowing green Razor Leaves struck Pidgey again and again. It had started dodging, but most of the leaves still hit. Unable to bear the pain, Pidgey wobbled, lost its balance, and fell onto the grass.

"Nice work!" With victory in sight, the girl held her baseball bat to her mouth like a microphone. "Can our starting pitcher Chikorita make the wild Pidgey swing and miss for a strikeout? Let's follow up and settle it with a fastball! Chikorita, use Tackle!"

Ethan stopped to watch. He knew nothing about baseball, its terms, or what a strikeout meant. But he knew she was a fan, and she had turned the Pokémon battle into a baseball game in her mind.

At her command, Chikorita charged. Just as it reached Pidgey, the bird recovered, flapped its wings, and lifted lightly out of the way of the Tackle.

"Aw!" The girl sighed, then rallied at once. "Too bad, but that's all right! The first pitch was a ball, and we've still got a second one. Chikorita, Razor Leaf again!"

Chikorita sent another volley of leaves into Pidgey. This time the bird ran out of strength. It dropped from the air and lay unconscious.

"We did it!"

The girl cheered, pulled out a Poké Ball, and threw it at Pidgey with a proper baseball pitch.

Tap! Red light flashed as Pidgey went into the ball. It shook only a few times before the capture was complete.

"Yes! I caught Pidgey!"

She bounced in place, full of energy. Perhaps she was too happy to contain herself. She began swinging her arms in rhythm and burst into song.

"Let's sing a victory song to celebrate! Here we go: Riding the winds of Mt. Mortar, here it comes with Quick Attack! Light Screen, Thunder Punch! Leer, leer, Electabuzz! Go, go, Electabuzz!"

Ethan had no idea what to make of it. He knew Mt. Mortar was one of Johto's mountains, but he did not think it was known for Electric-type Pokémon.

Like Trainer, like Pokémon. Chikorita swayed beside her in time with the song, keeping the beat. Did they really have to break into an awkward song just because they were happy?

Ethan encouraged his own Pokémon, but he could never throw himself into it quite like this girl. He certainly could not talk to himself and work up such excitement all on his own.

Still, her behavior told him plenty about her personality. She was warm, lively, and brimming with energy. And after he listened a little longer, he had to admit the song was unexpectedly catchy.

When she finished, the girl turned around. Only then did she notice Ethan.

"Oh! It's you!"

Her eyes brightened, and she hurried toward him.

"What a coincidence! We met again!" Slightly out of breath, she tipped her face up and gave Ethan a brilliant smile. "I'm sorry about what happened last time. I forgot to introduce myself. I'm Casey, and I just set out from New Bark Town as a new Trainer!"
