Ethan jumped down from Charizard's back and crossed to the Ultra Ball. He picked it up from a puddle.

Who would have guessed that this one ball now held a Pokémon powerful even among Legendary Pokémon—one that had fought Rayquaza to a standstill for a time?

He heard splashing footsteps and turned. Misty was running toward him, out of breath.

“Ethan, you're amazing! You really caught Deoxys!”

In her excitement, she threw her arms around his neck and clung to him. Ethan could feel how genuinely happy she was for him. That was the gift of traveling with someone who truly cared. Many people would have envied him; Misty celebrated his capture as though it were her own success.

“All right, come down,” he said, setting her gently on her feet. Her sudden embrace had left him flustered. “I'm not even making that much of a fuss. How did you get so excited?”

Misty smiled broadly. She had noticed his awkwardness and blushed, but she was pleased that he no longer seemed to see her as only a child.

“Of course I'm excited. I helped too, you know!”

“You did.” Ethan patted her head. “You helped me a great deal, and your Pokémon fought well. This couldn't have gone so smoothly without you. Psyduck aside, of course.”

At the mention of Psyduck, Misty laughed. “That's not fair. If Psyduck hadn't held Team Magma up, would Zinnia have arrived in time?”

She had a point. Psyduck had accomplished little deliberately, but it had taken several attacks and bought them time until Zinnia stepped in. Without that delay, Ethan might have had to Mega Evolve his red Gyarados before Rayquaza appeared, revealing it early. Zinnia might never have come out of hiding, and Gyarados would have lost the element of surprise. Who could say whether the ending would have been the same?

Ethan laughed, in an even better mood. “That's why I told Mr. Stone not to underestimate you before we left. I was right. For helping so much today, I'll buy you a gift when we get back to town.”

“A gift?” Misty's eyes lit up. “What is it?”

“Whatever you want!” Ethan swept a hand out grandly. “If it's sold in a store, you can have it. I'm paying today.”

“I have to pick it myself? I thought you'd already chosen something for me. Oh well—a gift is still a gift!”

They chatted and laughed until their excitement gradually settled.

Their Pokémon were all around them, enjoying the aftermath of victory. The lively ones played together, while the quieter ones found places to rest alone.

Mega Gyarados changed again. A faint light flashed over it, and its body returned to its ordinary form.

“It's a shame Mega Evolution can't last forever,” Misty said. “Imagine always having a Pokémon that strong.”

“What we have is already good enough,” Ethan said. “If it lasted forever, Kalos would be full of Mega Evolved Pokémon. If everyone had the same advantage, how different would anything be?”

He had a point. If everyone started from the same place again, Mega Evolution would no longer set anyone apart.

Ethan recalled his Pokémon one by one, leaving only Gengar and Charizard with him. He took out the special communicator Kennedy had provided and contacted Devon's people. His part of the work was finished. The remaining recovery was best left to professionals.

The landscape had been devastated and would take a long time to recover. But Deoxys was captured. Devon could retrieve the meteorite safely now.

Ethan and Misty sat side by side on a nearby rock, looking out across the valley and enjoying a brief spell of quiet.

More than twenty minutes later, several Devon helicopters appeared overhead. The security guards from the observation post arrived around the same time.

Standing in the middle of the ruined battlefield, the team leader looked around in amazement. “Mr. Vale, you're incredible. You even managed to capture a Pokémon that powerful!”

Ethan smiled at him. “You saw it?”

“We did!” another guard said before his leader could answer. “We couldn't see much at first, but we dared to move closer later. We saw everything! You had all those Legendary Pokémon, drove off Team Magma and Team Aqua, chased Rayquaza away with that powerful Gyarados, and caught Deoxys!”

The guard was still breathless with excitement. The battle had plainly shaken him. He had never imagined one person could possess so many Legendary Pokémon.

The helicopters drew nearer, rotors thundering and throwing strong gusts across the valley. Ripples spread through the puddles around their feet.

Ethan glanced up at the descending aircraft, then back at the guards. “Those Pokémon are something I've kept private. I don't want word of this to spread. Would you keep it to yourselves?”

The guards looked briefly surprised. Then they nodded firmly.

“Of course, Mr. Vale. You can count on us. We won't tell anyone.”
