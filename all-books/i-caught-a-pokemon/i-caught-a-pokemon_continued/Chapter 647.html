May and the others hurried through the city, arriving breathless at the Pokémon Contest venue. From a distance, they saw Ethan leaning against the wall by the entrance. He had a cold drink in hand and was sipping it at his leisure.

“Huh?” May stopped short. “Ethan? Weren't you behind us? How did you get here first?”

Just then Dragonite strolled out from the other side of the wall, happily licking an enormous ice cream cone.

Ash jumped. “You flew here on Dragonite? That's cheating!”

“How is it cheating?” Ethan lifted his chin. “It's making good use of a Pokémon's abilities. You could have thought of it yourselves. Anyway, the deadline's almost here. May, you'd better get inside.”

She knew he was right. Without stopping to wipe the sweat from her forehead, she nodded and went straight into the hall.

Ash and the others filed in after her. Before they reached the doorway, Ethan handed each of them a cold drink. “I bought these for you. Catch your breath before you drink, or the cold will be hard on your lungs.”

Slateport's Contest venue stood on a square in the city center. Its hexagonal dome made it resemble an old circus tent, but it was a solid five-story building.

A crowd of spectators had already gathered outside. The show wouldn't start until one in the afternoon, but they had come early to enjoy the atmosphere.

Ethan was the last of the group to enter. From the doorway, he saw May at the counter, completing her check-in with a staff member.

A clock in the hall read 9:57. She had arrived only three minutes before the deadline. May really was lucky. This was the same girl who had encountered a Duskull in a forest in broad daylight!

May rejoined them and let out a long breath. Only then did she notice her stomach rumbling and remember how disheveled she must look. She gave them an embarrassed smile.

“Sorry for all the trouble...”

“It's fine.” Misty waved it off, then cast a pointed glance at Ash. “We've dealt with this sort of thing plenty of times before. We're used to it!”

Her meaning was obvious to everyone but Ash. He was too busy gulping down his drink to notice.

“Where do we go now?” he asked.

“Isn't it obvious?” Misty patted her empty stomach. “We get something to eat!”

They left the hall and walked, talking and laughing, to a nearby restaurant.

It had opened only recently, and there was just one other customer, a woman sitting in a corner. Yet the moment May saw her, she stopped.

“Oh?” May looked again to be sure, then hurried over. “Excuse me... are you Dr. Abby?”

The woman had long green hair arranged in twin ponytails over her shoulders. She looked about Ethan's age and was quite beautiful. In a white T-shirt and jeans, she seemed bright and a little playful.

She looked at May curiously and nodded. “I am. And you are...?”

“It really is you!” May exclaimed. “I'm a fan! Oh, sorry, I forgot to introduce myself. My name's May, and I'm a Pokémon Coordinator too!”

Before Abby could reply, Brock flashed past Ethan. In an instant, he was kneeling on one knee before her, offering a rose he'd somehow found.

“Beautiful lady! Do you believe in love at first sight? The moment I saw you, Cupid's arrow struck my heart. I'm sure you felt it too!”

“Huh?” Abby stared at the squint-eyed man. Before she could make sense of him, Misty and Max stepped up, each seized one of his ears, and dragged him away.

May breathed easier once he was out of the way. “I'm sorry, Dr. Abby. My friend bothered you. Please don't mind him.”

“It's all right.” Abby smiled gently. “Are you here to compete or to watch?”

“To compete,” May admitted with a shy smile. “This is my first Pokémon Contest, though. To tell the truth, I'm nervous.”

Misty, Ash, and the others looked puzzled, so May introduced them. “This is Dr. Abby, the legendary Pokémon Coordinator! She won a Grand Festival, and Coordinator Monthly ran a feature on her. She's my idol and one of Hoenn's most popular Coordinators!”

She turned to Abby. “These are my friends. They've come to support me.”

“Hello, Dr. Abby. I'm Misty.”

“I'm Ash, and this is my partner Pikachu.”

“Pika!”

“I'm Max, May's little brother!”

“Greetings! I'm Brock, and I'm currently single!”

They crowded around Abby to say hello. Only Ethan stayed quiet, casting May a curious look.

A few days ago, she had known almost nothing about Contests. Now she had an idol? Either she had been studying furiously, or Mr. Big had told her about Abby the previous evening. May was clearly eager to put everything she'd just learned to use.

“It's nice to meet you all,” Abby said warmly. She seemed as kind as she looked. “Those achievements are in the past. I'm no longer a Coordinator, only an ordinary Pokémon doctor.”

“A Pokémon doctor?” Misty asked. “Do you work here?”

“No. My clinic is in Foothill Town, not far from Verdanturf Town. I only came to Slateport to watch the Contest.”

“I see.”

A doctor... Misty suddenly remembered something. She turned to Ethan. “Ethan! Abby is a Pokémon doctor. Bring out Jigglypuff. Maybe she can get that Everstone out!”

Ethan realized she was right. He hurriedly took out Jigglypuff's Poké Ball and released it.

Jigglypuff looked much as it normally did, though a little less energetic.

“Dr. Abby, could you take a look? Jigglypuff swallowed a stone by accident, and we haven't been able to get it out.”

Abby immediately focused on the sick Pokémon. She asked for the details, examined Jigglypuff, and reached the same conclusion as Nurse Joy.

“There really are only those two ways to remove the stone. I'm sorry, but my clinic and its equipment aren't here, so I can't operate now. If you like, you can come with me to Foothill Town after the Contest.”

Ethan considered it. Going with Abby would mean retracing their steps, away from the route he had planned.

“Let's wait for now. Jigglypuff isn't in immediate danger, and I still need to challenge the Dewford Gym. After that, I'll visit you in Foothill Town.”

“Challenge a Gym?” Abby blinked. “You're a Pokémon Trainer?”

“That's right.”

She looked Ethan over. The more she studied him, the more familiar he seemed. Then she remembered Misty calling him by name, and her face lit up.

“Oh! You're the Champion of both Kanto and Johto!”

Her sudden cry startled the group. She had been so gentle since they met that nobody expected her to burst out like that, as excited as May had been a moment earlier.

“Yes, that's me...” Ethan was taken aback. “You know who I am?”

“Of course!” Abby said eagerly. “I watched your matches on television not long ago. You were incredible! Champion of two regions! I'm a fan. Could I have your autograph?”

Misty and the others exchanged glances. May looked as though her world had been turned upside down. She had just told her friends how accomplished Abby was, and now her idol was a fan of Ethan?

Then what did that make May? A fan of a fan?

Even Ethan hadn't expected it. They had won championships in entirely different kinds of competition, yet Abby admired him.

He might need her help later, so he agreed at once. “Of course. Where would you like me to sign?”

Abby opened her wallet, took out a photograph of herself, and offered it with both hands. “Here, please!”

Ethan studied the picture. It seemed to have been taken at an amusement park, with a tall Ferris wheel behind her. Abby smiled brightly at the camera.

He had a pen in his backpack. On the back of the photograph, he wrote: May your healing hands bring health and your kind heart bring comfort. —Ethan Vale.

It was a simple blessing inspired by the congratulatory banners he'd often seen in hospitals back on Earth. The words were a little conventional, but they suited a Pokémon doctor.

Abby read it, beamed, and carefully returned the photograph to her wallet. “Thank you for your kind words. I'll remember them and try to live up to them!”

She had even become more respectful in the way she addressed him. Conventional or not, the message had pleased her and brought them closer.

At Abby's invitation, they joined her at her table.

This was a rare opportunity. At Misty's encouraging glance, May forgot about breakfast and asked the renowned former Coordinator several questions.

Like Mr. Big the day before, Abby held nothing back. She shared her experience and insights with May.

The conversation went on until noon. With the Contest drawing nearer, they finally had to stop.

“Thank you so much!” May stood and bowed deeply. “I'm sorry I took up so much of your time.”

“Not at all.” Abby smiled. “It's a pleasure to see a young Coordinator working so hard. Now concentrate on the Contest ahead. I'll be cheering for you from the audience!”

After saying goodbye to Abby, May realized how hungry she was. She ate a few hurried bites at the restaurant, then rushed back toward the venue.

She had spent the entire morning hurrying from one thing to another. In a way, that helped: she had barely had time to feel nervous or imagine everything that could go wrong.

That calm didn't last long. Once she was ready and seated in the contestants' waiting room, her heart began to pound.

The rules allowed her friends to accompany her, so Ethan and the others could enter the waiting area and watch the Contest from there.

On their way through the corridor, Ethan saw far more Coordinators than he had seen at the Pokémon Center the day before. Each was preparing in a separate room, and tension spread through the whole area.

May wasn't the only anxious one. Many newcomers, and even some who had competed once or twice before, looked unsettled.

Ethan was much more relaxed. It wasn't his competition. Although he sat in the same waiting area, he was only there to watch. For him, the whole experience was new and interesting.

At 12:53, even the corridor fell quiet. As the start drew close, every contestant seemed to feel the pressure. The noisy waiting area went still.

Then music sounded, followed by a woman's voice echoing throughout the venue.

The Slateport Pokémon Contest had begun!
