The God of Ice was losing its fight outside. Melody bit her lip. "We can't waste any more time! We have to free the God of Fire and the God of Thunder before the collector catches the last one!"

The others knew she was right. They left the window and hurried back to the cages.

Misty and Tracey sent out their Pokémon to help Pikachu attack the bars around Moltres. Even Jessie and James understood what was at stake for once. Rather than interfere, they sent out Weezing and Arbok to help free the two legendary birds.

Bang! Bang! Boom!

Move after move struck the cage, raising thick smoke. When it cleared, the bars were untouched. A transparent shield protected them from every attack.

"What is that thing made of?" Tracey demanded. "How can it be so strong?"

"Maybe that's why the collector felt safe leaving us here," Ethan said. "He must trust his defenses completely."

Ash refused to give up. "I don't care what it's made of. If we keep attacking, even a shield this tough has to break eventually!"

He released Charizard, Squirtle, and Bulbasaur to add their attacks to the barrage.

That was Ash's way: meet a difficult problem head-on. Ethan remembered a member of Hoenn's Elite Four once describing Ash's tactics as full of force but short on calculation. Constant attacks might work, but they would take time and exhaust their Pokémon.

There had to be another way. Ethan studied the cage and the space around it. His gaze settled on the point where it met the floor.

"What are you thinking?" Misty asked.

"I don't know how the cage works," Ethan said, directing Haunter toward the floor, "but it needs power to hold a Pokémon inside and repel attacks from outside. Something must be feeding energy into it."

Haunter fired a Shadow Ball. It blew a large hole through the airship's floor. Even the hard alloy plating could not withstand the hit.

Fragments scattered. When the dust settled, Ethan peered into the opening. Wires of many colors lay exposed. He crouched and followed them toward the cage until he spotted a thick black conduit running beneath the floor and up to its base. A hum came from inside it, as though energy were rushing through.

"There it is! Haunter, break that conduit!"

"Got it!"

Haunter launched another Shadow Ball. The moment it struck the conduit and the energy inside, a violent explosion went off.

Bang!

Electricity crackled and flames burst upward. A wave of heat drove Ethan several steps back. He barely noticed; his eyes were on Moltres. Its cage flickered, lost power, and fell apart onto the floor.

"You did it, Ethan!" Misty cried.

Freed, Moltres spread its wings and gave a piercing call. It fired a huge burst of flame at the cage holding Zapdos. When the fire cleared, the cage that had resisted all their Pokémon's attacks was broken.

The group stared. Moltres's strength had done with one attack what their combined efforts could not.

They thought it had released Zapdos to help another captive. Instead, the moment both birds were free, they attacked each other. Fire and lightning met beneath the ceiling. Everyone ducked to keep out of the blast.

"They heard what the collector said," Melody cried. "Why are they fighting each other now, of all times?"

Ethan gave a rueful smile. "You call them gods, but they're Pokémon. They don't necessarily think about consequences far in the future. To them, a rivalry centuries old matters more than changes to the environment."

"But their islands could be destroyed too!" Melody said, dismayed.

Before Ethan could answer, Tracey spoke as a Pokémon Watcher. "People depend on their homes. Bird Pokémon like Moltres can move elsewhere if their nests are destroyed. And, as Zapdos showed us, they may even be able to change the conditions of a new habitat."

"Then... can we no longer stop them?" The battle was growing fiercer. Melody wondered whether she had made the wrong choice.

A stray attack smashed through the airship's wall. Moltres and Zapdos spread their wings and flew out through the opening.

The group went back to the window. Outside, the birds chased and attacked each other. They had not forgotten what the airship had done to them, though. They wheeled around it, striking it again and again.

Explosions shook the vessel. Fire and smoke spread through its interior. After a dozen seconds, something critical seemed to take a hit. The airship tilted sharply, and warning lights flashed throughout the hold.

"It's going to crash!" Misty shouted.

Before anyone could get away, the floor slid beneath their feet and they fell backward. The whole airship was descending at an angle toward Lightning Island below.

"Sabrina!" Ethan called.

She understood at once. Her psychic power surrounded everyone, shielding them as the airship fell.
