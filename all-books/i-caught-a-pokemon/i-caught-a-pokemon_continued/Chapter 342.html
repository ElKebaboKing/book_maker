They had not gone far when Ethan noticed several photographs displayed prominently on a wall.

He glanced over and discovered pictures of Dorian with Misty's three older sisters: Daisy, Violet, and Lily.

In one photograph, Dorian wore his synchronized-swimming costume beside the three sisters, who were dressed similarly. All four stood dripping wet beside a pool, clearly having just climbed out after a performance.

Perhaps they had enjoyed one another's company. A faint smile rested on every face.

Judging by their ages in the picture, they looked almost exactly as they did now. The photograph must have been taken recently.

"You know Daisy and the others too?"

"Of course." Dorian turned toward the photograph with a nostalgic expression. "I deeply admire Miss Daisy, Miss Violet, and Miss Lily. In fact, they inspired me to become a synchronized swimmer."

"After I began performing, I made a special trip to Cerulean City to practice with them. We took this picture together to remember it."

Dorian suddenly noticed Ethan's wording. "Wait. You said 'too.' Do you know the three sisters of Cerulean Gym?"

Ethan glanced at Sabrina and answered vaguely. "You could say that. When I first began traveling as a Trainer, Cerulean Gym was the first Gym I challenged."

The fact that both sides knew the sisters was a remarkable coincidence.

In truth, Ethan's relationship with Daisy and the others went far beyond a passing acquaintance.

Of course, he did not dare tell Dorian that he had once performed in Cerulean Gym's water ballet, helped the sisters complete their show, and kissed Violet at the finale. That might upset Sabrina.

He also had several photographs with the sisters on his phone. They had volunteered to pose with him in thanks for his help. Compared with Dorian's pictures, Ethan's were unquestionably more provocative and... enviable.

"Cerulean Gym was your first challenge? Then you're from Kanto?"

"No." Ethan shook his head. "Kanto simply happened to be the first region where I traveled."

"I see. That explains why you don't have a Kanto accent."

They continued forward as they spoke, crossing a long corridor and hall before reaching a wall made from enormous panes of glass.

The transparent glass gave them a clear view of the seafloor.

Sunlight filtered through the water and cast a faint glow below. Many wild Water-type Pokémon moved freely among the coral and sea grass.

No matter how many times Ethan saw it, the underwater world remained mysterious and quiet, endlessly fascinating.

Dorian approached the glass and pointed toward the seafloor.

"All right, we've arrived. This is the Coastline Gym's battlefield!"

This is the battlefield?

Ethan followed his finger. A section of the seabed outside the Gym had been cleared completely, and white lines marked out a rectangular arena.

He asked in surprise, "So the Coastline Gym lives up to its name? Its battles take place underwater?"

"That's right!" Dorian smiled, plainly satisfied by Ethan's reaction. Then he explained, "And it isn't only the Pokémon. The Trainers enter the sea during the battle too!"

An underwater battle? That's certainly rare!

This was the first underwater battlefield Ethan had ever seen. Naturally, Pokémon that could neither breathe nor move freely beneath the surface would be unable to participate, regardless of their type.

Dorian retrieved two underwater breathing devices and handed one to Ethan.

"Put this small respirator in your mouth, and you can breathe underwater for more than twenty minutes. That's plenty of time for one battle. By the way, can you dive?"

"I've been through a shipwreck, so I have some experience underwater. I'm no expert, but at least I won't panic in the sea."

Ethan accepted the respirator and asked, "What are the rules?"

"They're simple. A standard three-on-three battle. Defeat all three opposing Pokémon to win!"

Ethan considered the rules. They posed no problem, but choosing his Pokémon was more difficult.

Blastoise was far away in Cerise Park, and there was no transfer machine here to bring it over. Gyarados was the only Water-type Pokémon currently with him.

But using Gyarados would make the difference in strength absurd. Ethan also feared it might demolish the Gym along the way.

Fortunately, he had another Pokémon capable of battling underwater.

Ethan had not brought swim trunks, so Dorian provided a new pair free of charge. After changing, Ethan and Dorian climbed a staircase, placed the respirators in their mouths, and jumped into the sea through an access point.

Splash!

The instant Ethan entered the water, the sudden contact raised goosebumps across his skin. The midday sun had kept the sea from becoming too cold. After ten or twenty seconds, he gradually adjusted to the temperature and pressure.

He moved his arms and legs experimentally, then swam to one of the Trainer command areas. When he looked back, Dorian was already prepared. Qwilfish, Mantine, and Lanturn, the three Pokémon from the show, had gathered beside him without Ethan noticing their arrival.

It seemed the three were both Dorian's performance partners and his battle team.

Seeing that Ethan was ready, Dorian waved one hand. Qwilfish swam onto the battlefield first.

Qwilfish was a Water- and Poison-type Pokémon. Its back and belly had sharply different colors, and its round body resembled a perpetually inflated pufferfish covered with more than a dozen spikes.

Its spherical shape supposedly made it a poor swimmer. To fire the poisonous spines covering its body, it swallowed water and inflated itself. The larger its opponent, the more water it drank and the larger its body became.

Ethan took out a Poké Ball. Blue light flashed, and Dragonair appeared before Qwilfish.

Dragonair's Dragon type held no super effective advantage over a Water-type opponent, but Water-type moves dealt only half damage against a Dragon-type Pokémon.

That resistance immediately deprived Qwilfish of much of its offensive power.
