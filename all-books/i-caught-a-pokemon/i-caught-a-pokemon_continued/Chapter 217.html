Lugia rose from the sea. Its body bore serious electrical burns, but it still seemed strong enough to fight.

It shook water from its wings, opened its mouth, and fired a hot orange beam straight into the airship wreckage at Ice Island's crater.

Boom!

The wreckage exploded and burst into flames. Lugia had not forgotten the rings that had trapped it. This was its answer to the collector.

Ethan nodded. With the collector dealt with and the God of Thunder unable to fight, the situation was turning in their favor again.

Thrum, thrum, thrum...

Just as Ethan prepared to press the advantage, he heard a helicopter's rotors. He looked up. A helicopter wobbled toward Shamouti Island, its flight plainly unstable. While the group watched, it dropped onto a hill near the altar.

Bang!

It crashed close enough for Ethan to feel the impact. Smoke rose from the hillside.

"What's going on?" Misty asked. "Who would fly a helicopter here in weather like this?"

"Maybe it's something urgent," Ethan said. He could not leave the battle now. He turned to Sabrina. "Someone may be hurt. Would you check on them?"

"I will." She set off. Misty and Tracey knew they could do little from the altar, so they followed her.

Only Melody remained beside Ethan. The unexpected helicopter made him worry that events might change in a way he had not foreseen. He hurried his attack.

Moltres, finish the God of Ice. Zapdos and Articuno, help Lugia against the God of Fire!

It was four against two now. Ethan had a clear advantage, though defeating the remaining birds would still take work.

Time passed as the battle continued. Then Ash called from the ice. Charizard had brought him back from Ice Island in a little over ten minutes. Without the three gods blocking his way, he seemed to have collected the treasure smoothly.

Ash jumped from Charizard before it landed and came down beside Ethan.

"I'm back! I got the last treasure." He looked around. "Where are Misty and the others?"

"A helicopter crashed nearby. They went to help the people aboard. You—"

Ethan broke off. Sabrina's psychic power had touched his mind with a warning. He sent a command through Gardevoir and called his three birds back to the altar.

Ash watched them arrive with mixed feelings and a hint of envy. "Ethan, when did you catch them?"

"I'll tell you another time." Ethan put a finger to his lips, then turned to Melody. "Both of you, please keep this to yourselves."

He had been willing to show his birds to Ash, Misty, and their companions. He did not want to reveal them to strangers without reason. More people would learn eventually, and the news might bring him trouble rather than disaster, but caution could keep criminals from seeking him out.

Ash and Melody understood that he trusted them with something private. They nodded without hesitation. "We won't tell anyone."

Ethan returned Moltres, Zapdos, and Articuno to their Great Balls. Victory had been close, and he regretted losing the chance to defeat the other two birds himself. But Ash had returned with the treasure. He could take over from here.

Lugia was still holding its own against the two island birds. Taken together, the three gods no longer seemed likely to overwhelm them.

Footsteps sounded behind Ethan. Sabrina, Misty, and Tracey were returning with several people. Among them were Ash's mother, Delia, Professor Oak, and a woman in a white lab coat. Two pilots and several television crew members carrying cameras came along as well.

Ash stared. "Mom? Professor Oak? Professor Ivy? What are you doing here?"

Professor Oak shook his head. "Kanto has been experiencing extreme weather. We investigated and traced it to this area, so I asked Professor Ivy to come with me and look into it."

Ethan understood why the professors had come. He was less sure why Ash's mother had joined them. Ash seemed to be wondering the same thing.

"Mom, why did you come? I heard the helicopter crashed. Are you hurt?"

"I'm all right." Delia looked at him with concern. "Professor Oak said you might be nearby. I wanted to check on you."

"It's strange," Professor Oak said. "The flight was normal until we reached Shamouti Island. Then we were suddenly caught in a fierce blizzard. That's why the helicopter failed."

Misty and the others looked toward Ethan.

He gave nothing away and redirected their attention. "It was probably caused by the three legendary birds and Lugia."

"The legendary birds? Lugia?"

Professor Oak and the others looked into the distance and saw the legendary Pokémon. The television crew immediately raised their cameras and began filming.

"Remarkable," Oak said. "So they're behind the weather disturbances."

Ash was about to speak, but Ethan stopped him. "We can catch up later. Put the treasure on the altar. Stopping the birds is what matters now."

"Oh! Right." Ash slapped his forehead. "I nearly forgot the most important thing!"
