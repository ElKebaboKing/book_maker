Before Ash could decide what to do, Crystal Steelix burst out of the ground beneath Snorlax and drove it into the air with its head.

Broken stone and hard-packed earth scattered. Snorlax's huge body tipped backward, and its usual vacant expression gave way to unmistakable alarm.

"Crystal Steelix, use Iron Tail!"

At Ethan's command, Crystal Steelix swung its pointed tail with tremendous force. Snorlax could not dodge while airborne. It could only watch the glittering tail sweep toward its belly.

"Quick, use Protect!" Ash shouted just in time.

Snorlax hurriedly raised both hands before its chest. A faint glow enveloped it an instant before Steelix's tail struck.

Crack!

Protect spared Snorlax any damage, but Steelix's strength still sent it flying to the center of the field. It smashed through a boulder before coming to a stop.

After all the rounds the two Trainers had fought, the rock field was a wreck. Almost no boulder remained whole. It was turning into a field of rubble.

Ash knew he could not keep fighting this way. As soon as Protect faded, he called, "Don't let it go underground again! Use Headbutt!"

Snorlax broke into a run toward Crystal Steelix. Its heavy steps shook the loose stones around it. Nearing Steelix, it accelerated again, lowered its head, and struck hard.

This time, Snorlax's strength prevailed.

Crystal Steelix swayed under the impact. But before Ash could celebrate, its tail coiled around Snorlax and held it fast.

"Break free, quick!"

Snorlax strained with all its strength against Steelix's Bind. Both were powerful Pokémon, but Steelix was several times its size. It had no intention of letting go.

Snorlax struggled for a long time without escaping. The more it fought, the tighter Steelix's coils became. Its face reddened, and it looked close to its limit. Ash took out its Poké Ball to recall it, but Steelix's body blocked the red beam. The light could not reach Snorlax.

"How can that be?"

Ash tried aiming from different angles, growing more anxious each time. He still could not recall it.

Trapped in the coils, Snorlax found it harder and harder to breathe. Its awareness faded. Only when it had lost consciousness did Crystal Steelix fling it off the field.

"Ethan wins this contest of strength!" the announcer declared. "With Snorlax's defeat, Ash has now lost three Pokémon. We'll take a five-minute break before the second half of the match!"

"Well done."

Ethan praised Crystal Steelix, recalled it, and returned to his rest area.

He sat down, drank some water, then leaned back with his eyes closed and waited for the break to pass.

Across the field, Ash returned to his own rest area looking dejected. Brock handed him water as soon as he sat down.

"Don't be discouraged. We prepared for this before the match, remember? We knew there was a good chance he'd beat you. If you show everything you can do, you'll already have done well!"

Even Misty could not bear to see Ash so downcast. "Haven't you always wanted to beat Ethan? Your Charizard took down his Dragonite. Come on—this is the closest you've come to that goal since you started traveling. Don't waste the chance!"

Their encouragement eased Ash's mood. He calmed his impatience, and when the five minutes were over, he walked back to the field with resolve in his eyes.

"After that short break, our Trainers are ready to continue!" the announcer said. "What kind of exciting battle will they show us next? Let's find out!"

Ethan and Ash took their places in the Trainer areas on opposite sides of the field.

Ash opened the second half by sending out his Shiny Noctowl again.

By holding back a new Pokémon, he seemed to want to keep one last surprise in reserve.

After a long rest, Noctowl had recovered enough to move despite its earlier paralysis. This time Ethan did not send Jolteon against it. He took out his fifth Poké Ball instead.

"Go, Blastoise!"

Ash was startled to hear Blastoise's name. He knew Ethan had not been keeping it with him and had never expected him to bring it to this match.

Ethan smiled at Ash's surprise.

Just as Ash had called on Squirtle, Ethan had brought Blastoise for the tournament as part of his team rotation. He had not chosen it to counter Ash. This simply happened to be its turn to fight.

In the friends and family section, Brock was troubled. He and Ash had spent all of yesterday studying the Pokémon Ethan currently had with him. Neither had imagined that Ethan would bring in Blastoise instead.

Ash might have been surprised, but he was not afraid of facing a different opponent.

When the battle began, Noctowl flew straight into the air. To Ash's astonishment, Ethan adopted the same tactic Gary had used: Blastoise fired a steady barrage of Water Guns from the cannons on its back, filling the air above the rock field with streams of water.

It was the same field, and once again Blastoise faced a Flying-type Pokémon. The difference was that Noctowl had no way to break through the barrage as Charizard had.

Noctowl was smaller and harder to hit than Charizard. Water-type attacks were not especially effective against it, either. Even so, its position was far from good.

Whoosh! Whoosh, whoosh!

Blue streams of water streaked through the air, skimming past Noctowl.

It dodged left and right as best it could. But it had already fought once and spent some of its strength, and Jolteon's attack had paralyzed it. The rest had helped, yet the aftereffects lingered. After only a few evasive moves, a stream struck it head-on and knocked it from the air.

"Noctowl!"

Ash cried out anxiously. To his relief, the one hit had not badly hurt it. But it was soaked through. It tried several times to take off and could not.

A bird that could not fly was in a sorry position on the ground.

Blastoise closed in, struck with Tackle, then followed with Bite and knocked Noctowl down.

"Noctowl is unable to battle! Blastoise wins!"

Ash's expression did not change when the referee gave the result. He quickly recalled Noctowl and immediately took out his fifth Poké Ball.

This time he sent out Bayleef. Unlike most gentle Bayleef, Ash's was known for refusing to give up. It would keep fighting even against a strong opponent, no matter how many wounds it suffered. It had a fierce spirit of its own.

It was a good choice.

Whether Ethan used Blastoise, Crystal Steelix, or Jolteon, each would be at some degree of type disadvantage against Bayleef.

But those were not Ethan's only Pokémon. Without hesitation, he settled on the last member of his team for this match.

He threw his final Poké Ball onto the field. Blue light flashed, and his Scizor appeared before Bayleef.
