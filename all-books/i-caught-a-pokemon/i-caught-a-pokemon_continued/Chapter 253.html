Ethan exchanged glances with Ash and the others. They had no better plan, so they would follow Quackenpoker's advice and strike the mechanical Gyarados in the belly. They nodded to one another and gave their commands.

"Charizard, use Flamethrower!"

"Pikachu, Thunder Shock!"

"Starmie, use Swift!"

"Marill, Bubble Beam!"

Four different attacks converged on a single point. With a deafening boom, they smashed through the mechanical Gyarados's armor. The combined strike seemed to damage its power system as well. A moment later, the machine that had been menacing them exploded.

Boom!

Jessie, James, and Meowth had no time to react. The blast hurled them out of the cockpit and into the sky. They dwindled to three bright specks before disappearing in the distance.

"We're blasting off again!"

"How did it end like this again?"

Their voices faded away, and Ash and the others breathed a little easier. Before they could celebrate, though, another series of explosions erupted from the container holding the captured Magikarp.

Bang! Bang!

Magikarp flew into the air. As they fell, it looked as though the sky had opened and begun raining fish. More than a hundred of them wriggled on the way down, then splashed into the lake one after another.

"Oh no!" Quackenpoker's face changed. He had not expected their attack to endanger the Magikarp. If any were badly hurt and missed their annual group evolution, he would never forgive himself.

Fortunately, the blasts had only caught the Magikarp at their edges. They had suffered no more than minor injuries.

Once the last of them landed in the lake, a brilliant pillar of light rose from the water. A second followed, then a third, then more than a dozen. The beams reached straight into the clouds, cutting through the night. They lit the lake, the campsite, the forest, and everyone's faces. The whole of Rind Island shone beneath them.

"Yes! The Magikarp are finally evolving together!" Quackenpoker cried. Eyes wide with delight, he hurried back to camp and began working his equipment, determined to record it all.

Ethan and the others needed no explanation. A Magikarp was evolving beneath each beam. Tracey felt the energy rising from the lake. "No experience, no Trainer's help... Is this the power of the Lake of Birth and Evolution? It's incredible."

Charizard and the other Pokémon returned to their Trainers. Together they watched the rare spectacle, but after a while Ethan felt something was wrong.

There were finally enough Magikarp for the group evolution they had hoped to see. Yet the way it had come about was nothing like they had imagined.

When the pillars faded, a group of fierce Gyarados appeared before them. Every one of them looked furious. Anger blazed in their eyes.

Quackenpoker, normally so observant, was too absorbed in the evolution to notice. The Trainers sensed the change in the air immediately.

"Ethan..." Misty, the Water-type Trainer among them, was especially sensitive to Gyarados. She swallowed as she looked at him. "Why do they look so frightening?"

Ethan gave a wry smile. "They just climbed those waterfalls. Before they could catch their breath, Team Rocket caught them. Then our battle tossed them through the air. If I were one of them, I'd be even angrier."

As if to confirm his words, the Gyarados turned toward them. Their rage was almost palpable.

"This is bad!" Tracey cried. "They're looking right at us!"

"Do they think we were the ones who caught them?" Ash asked in alarm.

There was no time to work it out. The Gyarados closed in, more than a dozen huge mouths opening together. Terrible energy gathered inside them.

Ethan recognized the attack. "Hyper Beam! There are too many of them. Everyone, move!"

He seized Sabrina's hand with one hand and Misty's with the other, then ran. Behind him, Ash fled with Pikachu. Tracey was about to follow when he saw Quackenpoker still adjusting his equipment. He rushed over and grabbed the old man's wrist.

"Mr. Quackenpoker! We have to get out of here!"

Hearing the panic in Tracey's voice, Quackenpoker looked up. Only then did he see the Gyarados's expressions. "What? Why are they acting like that?"

He tried to gather his equipment, but there was no time. Tracey pulled on his arm. "Wait! My equipment!"

To Quackenpoker, the results of his research were more precious than his life. Somehow he tore free of Tracey's grip and spread his arms over the machines, trying to shield them with his frail body.

Ethan clicked his tongue. The old scholar's devotion was admirable, but his disregard for his own safety was maddening. Ethan could not leave him there.

"Ash!" he shouted to the boy running ahead. "Stop! Protect Quackenpoker!"

Ash turned, saw what was happening, and stopped at once. He, Misty, and Tracey threw their remaining Poké Balls, releasing more Pokémon. Working together, the Pokémon launched their attacks toward the incoming Hyper Beams.

More than a dozen beams were already bearing down on the campsite. Their Pokémon's attacks swept forward like a rainbow and met them just in time.

Bang!

So much energy colliding at once produced a tremendous sonic boom. At the center of the impact, a light as dazzling as the sun burst into view.
