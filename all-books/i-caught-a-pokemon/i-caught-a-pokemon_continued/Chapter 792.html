They discussed a few more details. By dusk, Ethan finally left with Misty, May, and Chloe.

Shelly and Courtney still had people to choose for their team and preparations to make before setting out. That would take time, but Ethan was not leaving for Sinnoh immediately. They had room to get everything in order.

Outside the warehouses, Ethan turned to those who had come to see him off and nodded. “Until next time, then.”

“Boss…” Misa and Masha looked at him with tears in their eyes. They had only spent a few hours together, and neither wanted him to go. They knew he had important things to do and could not stay with them forever. They could only say goodbye.

Shelly and Courtney took the parting more calmly. They expected to meet him in Sinnoh soon, and now they had work to occupy them.

Shelly waved lazily. “See you in Sinnoh.”

Courtney nodded with solemn purpose. “I'll be waiting for you there.”

Ethan smiled at them once more and walked away with the three girls. Shelly and Courtney watched until he disappeared. Then they glanced at each other and went back into their separate warehouses, leaving only Misa and Masha in the open yard.

Masha looked toward the road Ethan had taken, then toward the warehouses. “Sis, do you think Big Sister Shelly and Big Sister Courtney both like the Boss?”

“Isn't it obvious?” Misa snorted. “Neither of them is an ordinary woman. Only someone like the Boss could attract them. Just watch. When they reach Sinnoh, all they'll need is the right opportunity, and they'll offer themselves to him!”

Masha scratched his head and turned to his sister. “What about you?”

“Me?” Misa gazed after Ethan, stars in her eyes. “Isn't that obvious too? Of course I feel the same!”

…

After leaving the outskirts, Ethan did not return to Cerise Laboratory. He headed for Chloe's house. He knew the way and walked ahead of the others.

May, a few steps behind, leaned toward Misty and asked quietly, “Who were all those people?”

Chloe pricked up her ears too. She knew the bikers, but she had no idea who lived in the warehouses or where they had come from.

Seeing Chloe's curiosity, Misty straightened a little and cleared her throat. “You know Brother Ethan doesn't just challenge Gyms when he travels. He helps the International Police with their cases too. Those two women used to be officers in criminal organizations. After he defeated them, they agreed to work for him.”

May had spent her journey with Ash. Most of what she knew about Ethan's travels had come from Misty's stories. She had no idea that his adventures had been even more dangerous than Ash's. The International Police, criminal organizations—it all sounded thrilling. Ethan seemed more impressive than ever.

Out of the corner of her eye, Misty saw that Chloe wanted to hear more as well. Delighted, she chose some of Ethan's most exciting encounters with Shelly and Courtney and told both girls about them.

Ethan listened to the chatter behind him, his own exploits growing more glorious with each retelling. He could not help feeling a little pleased. At that moment, nothing was more satisfying than having three beautiful girls praise him.

They were still talking when they reached Chloe's house. The conversation had eased the tension between Misty and Chloe. Misty was not truly petty; despite her feelings where Brother Ethan was concerned, she liked Chloe as a person.

Ethan climbed the steps and rang the bell. The door opened almost at once. Chloe's younger brother, the cool-looking Souta, stood on the other side.

“You really came!” he cried. Delight and excitement shone in his eyes.

Souta had not changed much. He was still open and enthusiastic, and handsome too. Ethan could easily imagine him being popular with his classmates. He remembered that Souta had called himself a fan at their first meeting and had asked for an autograph. His enthusiasm plainly had not faded.

Professor Cerise and his wife stood behind Souta, both smiling. The Professor had told Yoshino that Ethan was coming and had left the laboratory early. In truth, he had reached home only a few minutes ahead of his guest.

“Aunt Yoshino, Souta. It's good to see you again.” Ethan handed them the gifts he had prepared. Misty and May stepped forward to greet them too.

“It's nice to meet you. I'm Misty.”

“And I'm May.”

The girls were on their best behavior. Yoshino took to them immediately and welcomed everyone in with a warm smile.

They had arrived early, and Yoshino still had much to prepare. She brought them into the living room, made tea, and asked her husband and children to entertain the guests before hurrying to the kitchen.

Souta was so excited that he seemed to have an endless supply of questions. He kept Ethan talking about his travels, his championship victory, and everything in between.

Ethan answered each question patiently at first. Souta's enthusiasm eventually became more than he could keep up with. Fortunately, he had someone to help. He glanced at Misty, and she understood at once.

Misty happily took over. She knew how to praise Ethan without revealing anything private about him. Before the whole room, she picked out some of their more exciting Hoenn adventures and brought them to life.

Everyone listened closely. Souta was especially appreciative; each delighted “That's amazing!” gave Misty a fresh glow of satisfaction. Praising Ethan made her happy too—perhaps even happier than praising herself.

After they had finished a round of tea, the doorbell rang again. Souta went to answer it and returned with Ren and Parker. Now everyone had arrived. When Yoshino finished cooking, Ren helped her set the table, and the warm family dinner began.

…

After spending the night at Cerise Laboratory, Ethan, Misty, and May set out early the next morning. They left Vermilion City and headed north toward the nearest Battle Frontier facility.

It was called the Battle Arena. Ethan knew little about it. He had only caught a few glimpses of it in the anime and had never looked into it closely. That did not matter much. He only needed to defeat its Frontier Brain and earn proof of victory.

The Battle Arena stood near a village on the plains outside Saffron City. Following the location on Professor Cerise's map, the three walked for much of the day before reaching the area.

Before entering the village, Ethan stopped and gazed toward Saffron City for a long while. Sabrina was there, so close that another stretch of road might bring him into her arms. His longing almost overwhelmed him. He wanted to go to her at once.

But reason held him back. This was not the time to rush. At the very least, he would finish his challenge at the Battle Arena first. Ethan pushed his yearning aside, let out a long breath, and looked over the village.

Though it lay in the countryside, farmhouses stood close together amid lovely scenery. The air was fresh, and the place felt restful. The area was outside the Pokémon League's jurisdiction, yet the League seemed to have built a Pokémon Center here for Trainers visiting the nearby Battle Arena. It had landscaped the surrounding grounds as well.

Ethan went straight to the Center with Misty and May. Inside, they found a large, lavishly furnished space. A few other Trainers were eating dinner there. As always, Nurse Joy ran the front desk.

They checked in and were about to head for the dining room when a slightly stout man in sunglasses and a blue casual shirt approached them with a smile.

“Excuse me. Are all three of you Trainers?”

Ethan turned and gave him a puzzled look. “And you are…?”

“Allow me to introduce myself. My name is Scott. You certainly look like Trainers, so may I ask what brings you here?”

Scott. If Ethan remembered correctly, this was the owner of Kanto's Battle Frontier, the man who financed all its Frontier Brains. What a coincidence to meet him here.

Before Ethan could reply, Scott took off his sunglasses and studied Ethan's face. “Wait… Have we met somewhere? You look familiar.”

Ethan had never met him. There was only one reason Scott might recognize him. A moment later, Scott gasped.

“Ah! I've got it! You're that cham—”

Ethan interrupted him with a small cough. There were not many Trainers in the Center, but he still did not want to draw attention.

“I am,” he said quietly. “Please keep your voice down. I don't want to disturb anyone.”

Scott realized his surprise had gotten the better of him and laughed. “Sorry! I simply never expected to see you here. You caught me off guard.”

“I'm surprised too.” Ethan smiled and revealed that he knew who Scott was. “I didn't expect to find the owner of the Battle Frontier in a place like this.”

Scott looked even more astonished. “You know me?”

Ethan would hardly tell him about watching the anime. He gave Scott a mysterious smile. “Is that so strange? A wealthy man who owns the Battle Frontier is bound to attract attention.”

He was making it up. Scott kept a low profile and rarely appeared in public. If he were truly that famous, far more people would recognize him. Scott seemed to understand that Ethan was merely being polite and smiled.

“So you've come to challenge the Battle Frontier, Mr. Ethan?”

“Of course. We arrived a little later than I hoped, so it looks like I'll have to challenge it tomorrow.”

“That's wonderful!” Scott's eyes brightened. “The Frontier Brain at this Battle Arena is Miss Greta, and she's very strong. Watching a champion like you battle her should be quite a spectacle. I wouldn't miss it for anything!”

Greta. Her name brought a fleeting, rather silly thought to Ethan's mind. He tried to recall what he knew of this Frontier Brain. She had appeared so little in the anime that he could remember neither her appearance nor her Pokémon.

It was no great concern. Whatever partners she used, he was confident he could win if he stayed alert.

After chatting with Scott a while longer, Ethan agreed to go to the Battle Arena with him the next day. He watched Scott leave, then took Misty and May into the dining room.

Once Scott was out of earshot, Misty let out a breath and laughed. “When he first walked up to us, I thought he was a talent scout. I never would have guessed he was the man behind the Battle Frontier. He doesn't look the part at all!”

…

The night passed quietly. After breakfast the next morning, the three spent a little more time at the Pokémon Center. When it was time to leave, they went outside.

A red sports car came roaring up and stopped at the entrance. Scott was at the wheel.

“Hop in! It's not far, but let me drive you to the Battle Arena.”

Ethan had not expected Scott's promise to go with them to mean this. Then again, perhaps there was a reason Scott was so stout.

They climbed in. After a little over five minutes along a winding country road, the car entered a small forest. A Japanese-style building appeared among the trees ahead.

So that was the Battle Arena? Ethan had imagined something like a Roman arena. This looked more like a temple. As they drew closer, he heard many voices calling out in the rhythm of training.

Scott smiled as he drove. “Miss Greta isn't only the Frontier Brain of the Battle Arena. She's an excellent karate master, and she's full of energy. Once you meet her, I'm sure you'll be friends in no time!”
