"Well..." Quackenpoker hesitated. He was desperate to save the captured Magikarp, but he was an ordinary Pokémon Watcher, not a skilled battler.

When he had caught Pokémon poachers before, shouting at them had usually been enough to make them flee in guilty haste. He had never faced an organization brazen enough to stay and fight like Team Rocket.

While he stood there uncertain, the trio kept working. A fishing net shot from the top of their Magikarp submarine. It swung across the nearby water, caught several more Magikarp, and hauled them into the submarine's mouth.

Quackenpoker could not stand by any longer. He looked to Ethan and the others. "Please. Can you get those Magikarp back?"

"Of course!" Ash spun his cap around and pointed at the submarine. "We're not letting them get away with this. Pikachu, let's beat them!"

"We'll help too!"

Misty sent out Starmie, and Tracey released Marill. With Pikachu, they rushed toward the submarine.

Pikachu leaped onto Ash's shoulder, sprang from his arm onto Starmie, and rode it across the water.

Marill looked small, but it was capable too. The round Water- and Fairy-type Pokémon was sometimes called a water mouse. Its fur resisted getting soaked, and the tip of its tail was said to hold an oil lighter than water. Even a strong river current would have trouble pulling it under.

Ethan took out a Poké Ball. "Charizard, you go too!"

Blue light flashed. Charizard spread its wings and breathed fire as it attacked Team Rocket from above.

Quackenpoker watched anxiously. Charizard looked formidable, but the others seemed so small. "Are those Pokémon strong enough? These criminals don't look easy to beat. Shouldn't you send out a few more?"

"Don't worry," Tracey said confidently. "Pikachu and Marill are strong. They can handle this."

He was not boasting or dismissing the danger. From their previous encounters with Team Rocket's trio, four Pokémon ought to be more than enough. Quackenpoker was not entirely convinced, but for now he had to trust them.

The Pokémon reached the submarine. Pikachu fired Thunder Shock, Starmie spun into it with Rapid Spin, and Marill struck with Tackle. All three attacks hit at once.

Bang! Bang-bang! Bang-bang-bang!

The metal plating held, but the repeated blows made an alarming pounding sound inside.

Team Rocket fought back. Two mechanical arms extended from the submarine's roof and reached for Pikachu and the others. However fast the arms swung, the Pokémon dodged them.

"Darn it!"

Warning lights flashed in the cockpit. James and the others gripped their controls as the vessel shook.

They had spent a great deal of their hard-earned savings upgrading what had once been a simple submarine. They had expected that preparation to make their plan easier. Instead, Ethan's group was putting the machine in danger already.

"This isn't working," Meowth said. "Time for our secret weapon!"

He slammed a paw onto a red button. Gears clacked. To the astonishment of everyone on shore, the submarine began to change shape. Its Magikarp form unfolded into a Gyarados-shaped battle machine.

"What?!"

Ash and Tracey stared. They had never expected a submarine to transform.

"Ha! We came prepared!" Jessie called smugly. "You're not the only ones who can evolve. Now face the power of our mechanical Gyarados!"

The machine looked fierce. It could fire electricity from its mouth, and its two mechanical arms, odd as they looked on a Gyarados, made it more dangerous. The arms nearly caught Marill several times. Each time, Pikachu reacted quickly enough to save it.

What a nuisance. Ethan shook his head. Team Rocket's technology was impressive. Electricity streamed across the lake from the machine's mouth, leaving Starmie and Marill almost nowhere to dodge.

For all their limited funds, the trio had built a remarkable machine. They seemed capable of nearly anything except defeating Ash.

Crackle!

Another electrical discharge spread over the surface. Marill and Pikachu moved aside, but Starmie was caught and shuddered in pain.

"This won't do!" Misty cried. "They're at too much of a disadvantage in the water. We have to draw that mechanical Gyarados onto shore!"

"But how?" Ash scratched his cheek. "It isn't moving."

He was right. Team Rocket knew the advantage the lake gave them. They stayed in the water and kept firing electricity, showing no intention of coming ashore.

"Charizard, use Flamethrower!"

Ethan tried attacking from the air. Fire washed over the mechanical Gyarados, but it dived under the water and surfaced again, the flames extinguished.

Team Rocket really had prepared for this. Even Pikachu's Thunder Shock seemed to do little to the machine. Its shell would not be broken quickly.

Tracey sighed. "It's stronger than I expected. We'll have to find a weak spot."

"Look there!" Quackenpoker pointed at the machine's underside.

Ethan and the others followed his finger but saw nothing unusual.

"That part is bulging," Quackenpoker explained. "The captured Magikarp must be stored there. They needed space for them, so the armor around that compartment should be thinnest. Break it open, and we can free the Magikarp!"

The others nodded. Quackenpoker had spotted the machine's likely weak point in moments. For all his uncertainty about battle, his trained eye as a Pokémon Watcher was impressive.
