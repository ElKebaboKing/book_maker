Crackle! Crackle!

Electric light flashed continuously across the Pokémon Ninjutsu Academy's battlefield.

The glare was so intense that the surrounding students had to turn their heads to keep the brilliant light from hurting their eyes.

More than ten seconds passed before the sound of electricity gradually weakened and the light faded with it.

When the battlefield finally grew calm, the students hurriedly looked back. Ariados lay unconscious on the ground with black smoke rising from its body, while Jolteon stood beside it and panted softly.

The students had not seen exactly how Jolteon defeated Ariados, but its fallen opponent made the outcome undeniable.

They whispered among themselves, astonished by Ethan's strength and worried about the situation Aya now faced.

She was the strongest ninja in the academy apart from the headmaster. Yet after two battles, this man had left her completely unable to fight back.

After glancing at its opponent, Jolteon walked to Ethan and rubbed its head against his trouser leg.

Ethan bent down and stroked its head as a reward.

"You did well."

Jolteon had completely overwhelmed Ariados through sheer strength in the previous battle.

The combination of the electrical net and Thunder Fang had inflicted pain while also paralyzing Ariados, temporarily preventing it from moving. Jolteon seized that instant to use Discharge at close range and finish it completely.

It had nearly electrocuted Ariados until it was charred.

Ethan could even smell roasted insect in the air.

Across the field, Aya took out a Poké Ball and silently recalled Ariados.

Her face showed no emotion. The defeat of two consecutive Pokémon had made her realize something and clearly feel Ethan's strength.

While she had been training, this man had grown even more.

The gap between them felt wider than it had during their previous meeting.

Taking a deep breath, Aya drew her final Poké Ball. Whether she could reverse the situation depended entirely on the next battle.

She knew that victory would give her a chance to make a comeback and fight on. But defeat would mean that she was truly less skilled than Ethan, and her confident declaration of revenge had been nothing but empty boasting.

"Go, Venomoth!"

The instant Aya's third Pokémon appeared, Ethan recognized it as the same Venomoth she had used during their encounter on his journey to the Indigo League Conference.

This Pokémon was probably her ace.

However, Ethan clearly remembered that Aya's longest-standing companion should have been a Venonat. She had used that Venonat against him when he challenged Fuchsia Gym.

And yet...

He looked around but saw no sign of Venonat.

It had clearly been placed inside its Poké Ball because its strength was insufficient.

Returning his attention to the battlefield, Ethan recalled Jolteon. After a brief moment of thought, he likewise sent out his third Pokémon.

Blue light flashed, revealing a serpentine Pokémon before Venomoth. The watching students exclaimed in amazement at the sight.

"It's Dragonair!"

"It's so beautiful!"

"Isn't it adorable!?"

For ordinary Trainers, Dragonair was an exceptionally rare sight, even more difficult to encounter than its final evolution, Dragonite. Its lovely appearance instantly captured the female students' attention.

Dragonair circled through the air several times before settling in front of Venomoth and watching its opponent cautiously.

Unlike the solemn Aya, Ethan now had a different consideration in mind.

In terms of strength, Dragonair was not part of his main team. It was not even as strong as second-string Pokémon such as Venusaur and Blastoise. At most, it belonged to his third string alongside Scyther and Pupitar.

Ethan naturally had his reasons for sending it out now.

The battle had reached its third round, and all three of his Pokémon were still available. Victory was already close at hand. If he maintained the momentum of the first two rounds, he could unquestionably win the match in one final push.

But was a swift, flawless victory truly that important?

After all, the real world was not governed solely by fighting and killing. Social considerations mattered too.

This was not a brutal elimination tournament, so there was no need to crush Aya mercilessly. She was the academy's star student and Koga's younger sister. Ethan would inevitably deal with her again in the future.

If he defeated her with overwhelming force, it would damage her reputation and leave her humiliated.

Given Aya's personality, Ethan even suspected that if he did so, she would continue challenging him until she finally won.

To prevent that situation, he needed to strike the right balance in this battle.

He had to avoid making Aya's defeat too humiliating while still doing his best to win.

Deliberately throwing a round was obviously impossible as well.

After careful consideration, Ethan therefore chose Dragonair as Venomoth's opponent.

Dragonair was a pure Dragon type, while Venomoth was Poison and Bug. Neither held a type advantage over the other, making the battle fairer. Dragonair had also not yet evolved into Dragonite and remained far from its peak strength, so it was a suitable match for Venomoth.

As long as Dragonair fought with everything it had, Ethan would not mind even if it lost. The battle could simply serve as valuable experience.

Once both sides were ready, the headmaster again signaled the start of the battle.

Perhaps because she had been driven to the edge and had no room left to retreat, Aya adopted a much more aggressive strategy than in the previous two rounds.

The battle became intense the instant it began.

Psybeam, Sleep Powder, Poison Fang, Gust...

Although Venomoth was a Poison and Bug type, it could also use Grass, Flying, and Psychic-type moves.

Its wings also allowed it to fly, making Venomoth considerably faster than Dragonair. This created a great deal of trouble for Ethan's Pokémon.

As her opponent, Ethan could clearly feel the intensity of Aya's desire to attack. With no better option, he ordered Dragonair to dodge as much as possible while searching for Venomoth's weaknesses and waiting for a chance to counterattack.

Fortunately, Dragonair was also versatile and could use moves from several other types.

It used Agility to evade Psybeam, Twister to blow away Sleep Powder, Thunder Wave in an attempt to paralyze its opponent, and Dragon Rage to strike back.

For a time, the two Pokémon exchanged attacks on nearly equal terms.

Compared with the first two rounds, this evenly matched battle was unquestionably more exciting. The watching students cheered enthusiastically, thoroughly enjoying the spectacle.

This was the kind of battle they had wanted and expected to see!
