At One Island's harbor, Ethan and Sabrina had returned to the little tavern.

They sat at the same bar as before, eating the local specialty and talking quietly. Then the door flew open with a bang.

Lorelei strode in, furious. Under the bewildered proprietor's gaze, she marched up to Ethan, slapped a hand on the counter, and sat down heavily beside him.

It reminded Ethan of their first meeting, though all three felt rather differently now.

"Miss Lorelei, what took you so long?" He smiled and offered her a half-finished glass of iced water. "You must be roasting after that walk. Have some water and cool down."

Lorelei snatched it and took a gulp. Then she tasted a trace of grease from the food. It was Ethan's used glass. She spat the water out.

"Are you messing with me?" She glared as if she wanted to tear him in two.

"Sorry, wrong glass." He hurriedly picked up a fresh one and handed it over. "Don't be so angry. How is Mt. Ember now?"

The mountain was far from town, with a river between them. Its eruption had caused little panic among the islanders. A thin fall of ash had merely left the town looking dim.

"I spoke to the police," Lorelei said. "The lava stopped when it reached the foot of the mountain. It destroyed the forest there, but went no farther. We were lucky."

Ethan breathed out. "I'm glad no one was hurt."

"Enough about that." Lorelei leaned forward. "Did you catch Moltres?"

"Shh. Keep your voice down." Ethan held up a finger. When no one turned to look, he took a Great Ball from his belt and showed it to her.

"It was dangerous, but I managed to take my chance at the last moment."

Lorelei stared at the ball. Moltres was really inside? He had actually caught it. That was impressive.

Then she noticed a rough firebird symbol scratched near the ball's button. "Did you carve that yourself? It's ugly."

"It doesn't need to look good," Ethan said. "It just has to keep me from reaching for the wrong ball when it matters."

Catching Moltres had completed his set of three legendary birds. To mark the achievement and tell their Great Balls apart, he had used a utility knife to carve a simple mark on each of them.

"I see." Light flashed across Lorelei's glasses as she looked up. "Now that you've caught Moltres and got what you came for, let's settle our accounts."

"Accounts? What accounts?"

She counted on her fingers. "The Sevii Islands have lost Moltres and their legend. You set off Mt. Ember, destroyed a forest, and covered the town in ash. You abandoned a poor, defenseless girl on the mountain. And then you tricked me with that glass of water."

Ethan stared at her. "You led me to Moltres. If we're assigning blame, that makes you an accomplice. Besides, the volcano showed signs of waking before we went up. I may have triggered it, but I didn't cause all that pressure to build."

"And who was the poor, defenseless girl? You're one of the Elite Four for Kanto and Johto. A little lava wasn't going to swallow you."

"So that makes it all right to leave me halfway up the mountain?"

"Don't be so petty. Charizard couldn't carry three of us," Ethan said with a smile. "You got back safely, didn't you?"

Unable to outargue him, Lorelei let the joke go. Her expression grew serious.

"Winning the Indigo League proves you're a strong trainer. After these last few days, I suppose I've come to respect you too. How about challenging me now?"

Privately, she resolved that if he accepted, she would battle with everything she had and pay him back twice over for all the trouble he had given her.

To her disappointment, Ethan shook his head. "Not yet. I'll challenge you and the others after the Silver Conference, once I've built up more experience."

Lorelei concealed her frustration and nodded. "All right. What will you do next?"

"There's nothing else keeping us here. Sabrina and I talked it over. After lunch, we're leaving for the Orange Islands."

"What a coincidence. I might go there myself in a few days. Though at this rate, I doubt we'll cross paths."

She stood and offered them each a hand. "Safe travels. You're welcome back here whenever you like."

Ethan and Sabrina rose and shook her hand in turn. "If we get the chance, we will. We like life on these little islands. Maybe when we're tired of traveling, we'll build a house somewhere quiet and come there to rest."

They left the Sevii Islands on Charizard and continued south.

As the open sea stretched beneath them, Ethan finally had time to think over the trip. He had met Lorelei, captured a Team Rocket executive, caught Moltres, and found both Never-Melt Ice and Onix metal. It had been a rewarding visit.

That made him look forward to the Orange Islands all the more. The Orange League was smaller and less prestigious than the Indigo League, with different rules. But it would still be a valuable journey for a trainer.

And there were Pokémon there he wanted to catch. He would not miss the chance.
