Maxie was pleased that Ethan would trade an orb for Courtney, though he kept his face still.

“A traitor can't remain in Team Magma,” he said. “She's useless to me now. If her life buys the orb, I'll at least get something out of her.”

He had unwittingly helped Ethan. Once Maxie had decided Courtney was a traitor, he could scarcely hide his disgust with her or say anything kind, even for the sake of bargaining.

Ethan turned to Courtney. “You heard him. Your boss has said you're no longer in Team Magma. When I get you back, body and soul, you'll belong to me instead.”

She raised her head and looked at him with complicated feelings. She had never expected him to trade an orb for her. Her life seemed insignificant beside all of Hoenn. She had held no hope. Now, after a swirl of thoughts, one simple feeling remained. She gave him a small smile and nodded, her eyes bright.

“I understand.”

Ethan faced Maxie again. “I'll make the trade. How do I know you won't go back on your word once you have the orb?”

Maxie snorted. “You'll have to trust me.”

“No. Your word isn't enough. I need to feel safe making this exchange.”

“What do you propose?”

“A compromise. Have the heavy one bring Courtney to the middle and let her go. As she walks to me, I'll throw the orb to you.”

Maxie considered it. The proposal was risky, but acceptable. Tabitha would stay close enough to seize Courtney again if anything went wrong. Maxie had no better option. Ethan was not simple to manipulate; if the terms favored Team Magma too obviously, he might refuse. Groudon and Kyogre were fighting, and the orb was within reach. Maxie could not let the chance pass.

“Agreed. But don't try anything.”

“You'll get an orb.”

Maxie gave Tabitha a signal. Tabitha led Courtney toward the center. At the same time, Ethan took the pouch holding an orb into view. Maxie watched every move. It looked like the pouch he had carried from the submarine, and the round shape inside was unmistakable. His tension eased. Perhaps Ethan meant to keep his word.

At the center, Tabitha released Courtney. She began walking toward Ethan. Ethan tossed the pouch to a spot a little way from Tabitha, who hurried over to collect it. Courtney reached Ethan while Tabitha was occupied.

“I...” she began.

Ethan held up a hand. “This isn't finished. We can talk when we're away from here.”

Tabitha did not open the pouch. He might have feared an ambush, or he might have been wary of Ethan's Pokémon. He felt the orb through the fabric, then ran back to Maxie.

Maxie took the pouch and smiled. The fool really gave up an orb for a woman. Ethan had turned a commander and laid an ambush, yet the orb had returned to Maxie's hands. As long as he kept it, he could find another chance to control Groudon and realize his dream. He shot Ethan a triumphant look and turned away.

“Oh,” Ethan said, feigning surprise. “I think I mixed them up.”

“What?” Maxie spun around.

Ethan held up a second pouch, smiled apologetically, and drew out a glowing red orb.

The Red Orb was still with Ethan. Maxie stared. Then what had Tabitha brought him?

He opened the pouch. Blue light shone across his face.

The Blue Orb? How did I get this? No—how did Ethan get it?

Maxie's information was incomplete. He knew Team Aqua had stolen the Blue Orb, but not that Shelly now held it. He had missed the moment she handed a pouch to Ethan. He never imagined Ethan had both orbs, much less that he would trade away the blue one.

“You tricked me!” Maxie shouted.

“Tricked you? I said I'd trade an orb. I never said which one.”

Maxie opened his mouth, then stopped. Ethan had never named the Red Orb. Maxie had assumed it, because he did not know another orb was available.

What am I supposed to do with the Blue Orb? Capture Kyogre? I came for Groudon!

Ethan was not finished. He looked toward Archie, who was busy directing Team Aqua's battle and had missed the exchange.

“Archie! Take a look at what Maxie has!” Ethan called.

Archie glanced up and saw the Blue Orb in his rival's hand. “Maxie! Why are you holding the Blue Orb?”

Maxie had no useful answer. He could hardly confess that Ethan had played him. Besides, why should he explain himself to his sworn enemy? If nothing else, holding the Blue Orb meant Team Aqua could not control Kyogre. The thought made him feel a little better.

Ethan added fuel to the dispute. “Careful, Archie. I don't have it anymore. Maxie might control Kyogre first and keep you from getting it at all.”

The two organizations already hated each other. Archie could not simply ignore that warning. He understood Ethan well enough to believe that Ethan would hide the orb rather than use it to control Kyogre. Maxie was another matter. Archie could imagine him seizing Kyogre solely to thwart Team Aqua, then turning it against them.

He would not allow it. At a wave of his hand, Team Aqua changed targets and charged Maxie.

Maxie wanted to curse Ethan, but there was no time. He had to reach his submarine. With a full force, he would not have feared a fight against Team Aqua. Now he had only Tabitha and a few members operating the submarine. He and Tabitha fled toward it.

Shelly stared at Ethan. The change had happened so quickly that she had only just followed it. “You really are devious. You handed Maxie that dangerous orb and made him Archie's problem. When did you switch them? I didn't even notice.”

Ethan looked pleased with himself. “When you gave me the Blue Orb. I expected another twist today, so I quietly swapped which pouch held which orb. But we can't leave the Blue Orb with Maxie. We still need to get it back.”

He turned to Courtney. “Can you fight?”

“Yes.” She nodded.

Since Ethan had traded an orb for her, something in Courtney had shifted. She could face Team Aqua, and even Maxie, without the same turmoil.

Ethan looked at the two women beside him, one in blue and one in red. Once they had stood on opposite sides of a bitter rivalry. Now both would fight with him.

“Good. Let's drive them all into the sea!”

His Pokémon struck Team Aqua's flank. This time Ethan's group was attacking rather than defending. Team Aqua had to fight Maxie's group in front and Ethan's from behind. Its formation began to break.

Without Archie or Matt to lead them, the members left to guard the rear could not hold Ethan back. They gave ground. At the other end, Maxie fought while retreating toward his submarine, Archie close behind him.

Team Aqua's line was caught between two opponents, but Archie no longer cared about Ethan. He wanted the Blue Orb back.

Matt battled Tabitha. Both favored direct action, and they were closely matched. Archie took on Maxie.

Maxie could direct an organization brilliantly, but he could not overpower the larger Archie in a direct fight. As the struggle intensified, his prospects darkened. At this rate, Archie would follow him into the submarine even if he reached it. He could not lose his last means of escape.

Maxie changed course. Instead of retreating to the submarine, he led the pursuing Team Aqua members toward the center of the island, where Groudon and Kyogre were fighting. If you want the orb, let's see whether you dare follow. We may all die together.

Archie and Ethan noticed the turn. Neither stopped pursuing.

Archie knew his old rival. Maxie valued his own life. If real danger caught them, Archie doubted Maxie would keep running toward the two Legendary Pokémon. This was another calculated maneuver to make the pursuit hesitate. Archie would not fall for it.

At the island's center, Groudon and Kyogre were fighting with single-minded fury. They paid no attention to the human conflict. Each had been disturbed from sleep, and neither would stop until it brought the other down.

Maxie, Tabitha, and their pursuers drew closer to that battle.
