That evening, Ethan and Misty found themselves between towns with nowhere to stay, so they camped beside a small lake nearby.

Ethan pitched his tent and invited Misty inside. Unlike the nights he had shared a bed with Sabrina or Erika, there was a lamp between them, and they each slept on one side with plenty of space. The tent merely kept out the wind and rain.

Even so, Misty's cheeks burned, and she could not help imagining where the night might lead.

With Erika gone, Ethan naturally took charge of dinner. His cooking was only average, but after spending so much time with Erika, he had learned a thing or two. He made a hearty seasonal soup for the main course, and it turned out surprisingly delicious.

He was pleased with it, at least, and Misty praised it enthusiastically.

They and their Pokémon all ate their fill. After the sun had disappeared, darkness settled around the lake and the moon climbed high. They cleared away the dishes, then sat in front of the tent, chatting as they watched the still water.

The campfire crackled. Starlight lay across the lake, its reflection stretching like a river of stars.

Nearby, Eevee and Torchic played with several of Misty's Pokémon. Gengar sat to one side like a meditating monk, admiring the beauty of the night sky.

Ethan stirred the lively fire with a stick and turned to Misty. “I'm surprised your sisters agreed to let you leave again so easily.”

“Why wouldn't they?” Misty smiled, though it looked a little strained. “They support my travels. Besides, even if they didn't, it wouldn't matter. I wouldn't listen to them!”

In fact, Misty had rested at home for a few days after returning to the Cerulean Gym. When she announced that she intended to set out for Hoenn, Violet and Lily had strongly opposed it.

The three Cerulean sisters were now wholly focused on their underwater ballet. They had long wanted to hand the Gym's work over to Misty as soon as they could.

Fortunately, Misty had spoken to their eldest sister, Daisy, in private. After Misty appealed to both her feelings and her reason, Daisy persuaded Violet and Lily to allow her a little more time to travel.

When that time ran out, Misty would have to return and take over the Gym.

She kept all this from Ethan. Even though the agreement came with a condition, she had long known that none of her sisters wanted to be Gym Leader. Cerulean Gym belonged to their family and could not simply be abandoned. Sooner or later, the position would fall to her.

For now, she was glad to have won herself more time.

Ethan took her answer at face value. Yet he had seen the anime, and even without Misty telling him, he understood that she might soon return to Cerulean Gym, take over her sisters' duties, and truly become its Gym Leader.

Thinking about Cerulean Gym reminded him that Misty was a Trainer who had grown up around Water-type Pokémon. He took out a Poké Ball and released the Feebas he had recently caught on the beach outside Petalburg City.

Even Misty, a Water-type specialist, frowned at the ugly, yellow-brown fish Pokémon.

“Ethan, is this one of your new Pokémon? Why does it look so... so...”

Knowing what she was trying to say, Ethan laughed. He brought up a picture of Milotic on his Pokédex and showed it to her.

“It may be ugly now, but look how beautiful it is after it evolves.”

His explanation made Misty realize she had judged Feebas by its appearance. Embarrassed, she stuck out her tongue.

Ethan then asked her for advice on caring for a fish Pokémon like this one.

He had raised Magikarp before, but holding Magikarp aloft and swinging it into other Pokémon was hardly a suitable way to handle Feebas.

Misty was delighted and flattered that he had asked. She contained her excitement and shared everything she knew. Having grown up in a Gym, she understood little details Ethan had never noticed. He learned a great deal from their conversation.

With Misty's help, he also tried several more specialized Water-type training methods in the lake, with good results.

By the end of the evening, he felt more confident about raising Feebas.

...

Early the next morning, they packed their belongings, buried the campfire ashes, and set out again.

After walking for a little over an hour, they left the plain behind, and Ethan arrived in Rustboro City for the second time.

With Misty beside him, he went straight to Rustboro Gym.

The official Pokémon League Gym stood in an open part of the city. Yellow earth and rocks surrounded it, clearly placed there to distinguish it from the busy streets nearby and tell challengers at a glance what type of Pokémon they would face.

The building itself looked like an immense imitation mountain, with the Gym carved into its base.

Its Leader, Roxanne, specialized in Rock-type Pokémon and was known as a talented student devoted to rocks. She also taught at a Pokémon Academy. Both jobs commanded respect and a measure of social standing, and she had a considerable reputation in Rustboro City.

Ethan composed himself, took a deep breath, and entered the Gym with Misty.

After greeting the receptionist, they were led directly to the battlefield.

Before they reached it, Ethan spotted a girl on the rock-strewn field. She wore a blue-gray skirt, red stockings, a red bow tie, and a matching ribbon in her hair. She was directing several students in matching uniforms as they cleaned up the field.

To be honest, it was hard to judge Roxanne's age.

Her clothes suggested a girl of seventeen or eighteen, but her face made her look about Ethan's age.

If he had to put it into words, Ethan thought she looked as though she were trying to dress younger.

He did not mean that as a criticism of her taste. Perhaps she simply had a youthful outlook and an image of herself she liked to hold on to.

Roxanne heard their footsteps and noticed them. She came forward a few steps, stopped before Ethan, and gave a small bow.

“Hello. I'm Roxanne, the Gym Leader here. Welcome to Rustboro Gym!”
