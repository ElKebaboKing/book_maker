Ethan saw what Wattson had in mind and indulged him. He kept Combusken on the field instead of switching Pokémon. Wattson was an old man, after all; a little consideration seemed only polite. More importantly, Combusken had taken little damage in the first round. It had spent some energy but could still fight.

Seeing that Ethan had not switched, Wattson smiled in a way he hadn't before. Ethan noticed but gave it little thought. He sent Combusken to its starting position for the second round.

Both sides were ready. Watt waved his flag, and the second round of the Mauville Gym battle began.

“Let's show them our new move!” Wattson called with a laugh. “Magneton, use Zap Cannon!”

Zap Cannon?

Ethan started. This wasn't a move just any Pokémon could use. Among Electric-type attacks, its power stood below only Bolt Strike and Electro Shot, matched Volt Tackle and Double Shock, and even exceeded Thunder. Wattson's Magneton had learned something that formidable?

It must be a very strong Pokémon. Now Ethan understood that odd smile. Wattson had sprung a powerful partner on him without warning. The man really was crafty beneath his cheerful manner.

Ethan shook his head ruefully. He had kept Combusken in partly to spare Wattson's feelings, only to be met with this at the start of the round. That hearty laugh had fooled him.

Still, a move as strong as Zap Cannon took time to launch, giving Combusken a chance to escape. They had begun on opposite halves of the field, far apart. Combusken wasn't immobilized or trapped, had plenty of stamina, and had flat ground beneath its feet. Hitting it from that distance would be difficult. Ethan realized Wattson also wanted to make an impression and frighten him a little. He relaxed slightly, though Magneton was plainly no easy opponent.

All those thoughts passed through his mind in an instant. At the same time he shouted, “Dodge, quickly!”

Magneton charged for a moment. All six of its U-shaped magnets exerted force together, forming a thick beam before it. The Zap Cannon fired toward Combusken like a rectangular shell of electrical energy, trailing lightning as it flashed across the field.

Combusken had already begun to run. As the attack neared, it used Quick Attack and accelerated just enough to slip past its edge. The hot glow of the current faded in the air, having singed only a few feathers.

Combusken kept the momentum from Quick Attack and closed the distance. At Ethan's command, it spat Embers toward Magneton, as it had against Voltorb in the previous round. Wattson had more options this time.

“Thunder Shock!”

Magneton stayed where it hovered, choosing to trade blows. Lightning and fire crossed in midair. Neither canceled the other; both passed through and struck their targets. Combusken and Magneton cried out almost together. Fortunately for each, neither attack caused an additional status condition.

Magneton was stronger overall than Voltorb. It could contend with Combusken from long, medium, or short range and lacked many of Voltorb's limitations. Winning would take a hard fight.

“Double Kick!”

Combusken rushed Magneton and launched a fierce, sustained assault. A long battle favored the troublesome Magneton, so Ethan wanted Combusken to give everything it had now. Wattson noticed the change of pace and raised his brows, directing Magneton to answer each attack.

Embers, electrical discharges, and Combusken's heavy breathing echoed around the battlefield. Combusken pressed close, alternating its clawed feet and using Fighting- and Normal-type moves. Magneton struggled against the pressure.

With no arms, it could not catch or block Combusken's Fighting moves. It had to keep sliding aside. Movement interrupted some of its attacks, forcing it to defend and trapping it in a worsening cycle.

Scratch, Double Kick, then Peck—within seconds Combusken landed several hits.

Magneton was strong enough to retaliate even in that position. A Thunder Shock struck Combusken.

Crackle!

Electricity flashed over Combusken, making all its feathers stand on end. In the end, Magneton, already on the verge of falling, managed to bring Combusken down with it. Both collapsed.

Ethan could accept a draw. Wattson found it much harder to take. He had caught the young Trainer off guard and still lost the round. A defeat was a defeat, though. At least Magneton had taken down one of Ethan's Pokémon with it.

There was something worth noting about Wattson's first two Pokémon. In the anime, both Voltorb and Magneton had seemed capable of strong attacks but unable to endure much damage. When Ash challenged this Gym, his Pikachu absorbed electricity from the Raikou robot, then defeated each of Wattson's Pokémon in a single hit. Three easy wins earned him the badge.

It was probably Ash's easiest Gym battle from the start of his journey all the way to his eventual championship. Even more remarkable, Pikachu had done it against fellow Electric types, which resisted its attacks. That showed how little punishment those Pokémon could take.
