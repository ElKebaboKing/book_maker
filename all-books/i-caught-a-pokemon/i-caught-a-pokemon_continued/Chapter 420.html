The chilly air swept over them and pierced straight through their clothes, bringing a biting cold.

High in the sky, Ethan and Erika rode Charizard rapidly toward the Pokémon Center. Charizard flew so quickly that the forest below streaked past in an instant, making it impossible to see any of the scenery clearly.

Of course, after seeing so much during their travels, neither was currently in the mood to admire the view. They did not particularly mind.

Still...

The speed was certainly impressive, but it also meant that Erika had to hold tightly around Ethan's waist to keep the powerful wind from sweeping her away.

For Ethan, naturally, this was entirely a benefit.

As expected of a young lady from a distinguished family. She really is remarkably soft and curvaceous!

Ethan cleared his throat. Though his thoughts were beginning to wander, he remained enough of a gentleman to pat Charizard and have it slow down. Erika could then sit upright, sparing her further embarrassment.

Since he was facing away from her, Ethan could not see the faint blush coloring Erika's beautiful face.

The cold wind was partly responsible. The other reason was not that she realized Ethan had taken advantage of the situation. Rather, she could feel the man's warmth, and while wrapping her arms around his waist, she had accidentally touched his abdominal muscles!

How could such intimate contact possibly leave her unmoved?

Fortunately, they shared an unspoken understanding and both pretended that nothing had happened.

...

As they approached the nearby forest, Ethan spotted a vast bank of dark clouds gathering high above before they had even drawn close.

The clouds had appeared as abruptly as the day before. The crystal had clearly created them again!

Amid the lightning that periodically flashed through the clouds, the two also heard the sounds of a fierce battle rising from the ground.

Huh? Have they already started fighting?

Ethan hesitated no longer and quickly told Charizard to descend. As they neared the battlefield, he immediately spotted the mechanical shark rampaging across it. More than a dozen Team Rocket members stood nearby, commanding their Pokémon in attacks against Jimmy and the others.

On the opposite side, Jimmy, Marina, Eusine, Kudo, and a slightly chubby boy were directing their own Pokémon against Team Rocket. Outnumbered as they were, the situation did not look good.

Then Ethan looked toward Raikou...

Huh? How did this guy get captured again!?

Just like yesterday, Raikou had been trapped inside the barrier once more.

It really doesn't learn from its mistakes...

Before Ethan could make any more remarks, Charizard folded its wings and slowly landed behind Jimmy.

Ethan's arrival instantly drew everyone's attention.

Jimmy, Marina, and the others lit up with joy. The Team Rocket members, by contrast, visibly wavered. Even Attila and Hun's expressions became far uglier.

That brought to mind the men's current state of mind.

From the beginning, they had prepared specifically for Ethan and regarded him as their strongest opponent. With all the personnel they had brought this time, they believed they had more than enough strength to deal with him. Under the crystal's control, even Raikou should have fallen easily into their hands.

Yet the battle had gone awry from the outset.

Ethan's initial absence had surprised them somewhat, but they had never imagined the two brats would have other people helping them!

Once the fighting began, they discovered that not only were the boy and girl extremely troublesome, but their three companions also possessed considerable strength!

They had planned to finish the battle quickly, only for the struggle to become increasingly deadlocked and draw them deeper in. Now Ethan had appeared at this critical moment...

This was the worst possible situation...

Ethan naturally knew nothing of their thoughts. The moment he jumped down from Charizard, he launched a verbal attack.

"Stop struggling. I doubt you've heard yet, but we've already taken your base!"

"What?" Attila and Hun's expressions changed instantly, while obvious unrest spread through the other Team Rocket members.

This was what it meant to strike at the foundation!

"Don't let him mislead you!" Hun frowned and shouted, "There's no way he could find our base. This is only a trick to disrupt us!"

Ethan chuckled. "You don't believe me? Then I'll tell you the truth: Professor Sebastian has already been arrested by the International Police!"

Hun cursed inwardly.

Ethan even knew the name of the man in charge. It did not seem like a bluff. Had the base truly been captured?

But the facility had been hidden so well. How had he found it? And where had the International Police come from!?

Ethan had no interest in wasting more words. He threw several Poké Balls in quick succession, sending his Pokémon into the battle.

Blue light flashed. Gardevoir, Jolteon, Scyther, Dragonair, Pupitar, and his newly acquired Eevee appeared together. With Charizard already beside him, seven Pokémon had instantly reversed the balance of the fight.

In sheer numbers, Ethan's side now matched Team Rocket, to say nothing of the difference in strength.

However, Ethan's immediate objective was not to defeat these people. After spotting the right opportunity, he quietly issued an order to Gengar.

Gengar silently appeared beside the barrier and gathered an enormous Shadow Ball above its right hand.

To ensure the barrier broke in one strike, Gengar had poured its full strength into the attack!

"Break!"

At Gengar's roar, the enormous Shadow Ball hurtled toward the barrier at tremendous speed.

The barrier that had completely suppressed Electric-type Pokémon shattered into countless fragments the instant it met the Shadow Ball. Without losing momentum, the attack flew straight toward the crystal at the barrier's center and struck it directly!

"No!"

Attila turned at the sound and saw what had happened. He roared and tried to rush back immediately, but before his mechanical shark had taken two steps, Ethan's Pokémon blocked its path!

A deafening explosion erupted inside the barrier after the Shadow Ball struck the crystal.

Thick smoke swept across nearly half the battlefield. In the next instant, a yellow figure leaped from the swirling cloud and landed atop a nearby hill.

It was Raikou, now free from the barrier.

Standing on the high ground, Raikou looked coldly at the humans who had fought over it. Without the slightest reluctance, it turned and left.

At the same time, Ethan gave Gengar a meaningful look. Gengar nodded in understanding, then vanished from sight.

Ethan was not finished. He turned toward Scyther and said quietly, "You go too."
