Slaking was one of Norman's signature Pokémon. Ethan knew it would be difficult to handle even without Norman's warning.

Fighting such a powerful opponent at close range seemed unwise. Of the three Pokémon Ethan was considering, he ruled out Tyranitar first.

Tyranitar's Rock typing gave it some protection against Slaking's Normal-type attacks. Both Pokémon relied on strength, so they might have been able to clash head-on. But Tyranitar was simply too slow.

Slaking would keep its distance and wear Tyranitar down. Rock Tomb might slow Slaking's movements, but an ape-like Pokémon would have no trouble climbing over the obstacles. The move would not achieve what Ethan needed.

He hesitated between Dragonite and Scizor, then chose Scizor.

“The next battle is yours. Go, Scizor!”

Scizor's Steel typing would blunt Slaking's Normal-type moves. Slaking also knew many Fighting-type moves, but Scizor's Bug typing would offset its Steel typing's weakness to them. The matchup gave Scizor an advantage from the start.

More important, Scizor had the body to withstand Slaking's attacks. Its strength, speed, and stamina might fall short of Slaking's, but its steel-hard frame could take a beating, and it hit hard itself. Its small wings gave it a little more room to maneuver. Slaking might still keep it at a distance, yet Scizor would have ways to respond.

Blue light flashed as Scizor appeared and moved into position. It tried to study its opponent. Slaking remained sprawled on the ground, barely moving. It only lifted its eyes for a brief glance, as if Scizor were not worth its attention.

That might have angered Combusken. Scizor merely took its stance and remained calm. It did not seem to care that Slaking was ignoring it.

Its composure was remarkable. Scizor was not easily provoked, a trait Ethan associated with many Bug-type Pokémon: cool and unsentimental.

“The third round of the Petalburg Gym battle begins now!” the referee announced.

“Scizor, X-Scissor!”

Ethan began with a probing attack from some distance rather than rushing in. Scizor crossed its blade-like arms and swept them forward. An X-shaped cutting wave shot out toward Slaking.

“Block it,” Norman said quietly.

Slaking lazily extended both hands and caught the attack barehanded. Bang! Bang! The cutting waves broke against its heavy palms.

They still left two swollen red marks. The hands Norman had trained to be hard as steel felt the sting. Slaking could no longer dismiss the Pokémon facing it. It rose from the ground, half-opened its eyes, and settled into a fighting stance.

Norman had learned something too. This Scizor was in a different league from the Metang and Combusken Ethan had used before. It had the strength to compete in a League tournament and contend for the championship.

He stopped watching and gave his own attack order. “Slaking, Hammer Arm!”

Slaking's right fist shone brightly. It surged across the field at startling speed, lifted that fist high, and brought it down on Scizor.

Scizor showed no fear. It raised both bladed arms to meet the blow.

Clang!

Slaking's massive fist struck the two arms with a ringing sound like metal on metal. Scizor retreated two steps, its arms numb.

Slaking's strength was terrifying. One blow had nearly overwhelmed Scizor's guard. But that was all. Scizor's defenses and type advantage kept the damage modest; it had only to catch its breath.

Slaking began thumping its chest. The gesture signaled that it was taking the fight seriously.

“Slaking, Earthquake!”

Another move outside Slaking's Normal type. Ethan was used to that by now. After Norman's earlier surprises, he might have been more startled if Slaking had used only ordinary attacks.

Slaking leaped high, landed heavily, and stamped the ground over and over. The force shook the whole battlefield. Scizor, standing directly before it, felt the tremors first. Ethan did not panic.

“Scizor, jump and use Slash!”

The small wings on Scizor's back came into play. Its weight prevented it from truly flying, but the wings could help it jump farther and glide briefly. They could even soften a fall from above.

Scizor sprang high above the violently shaking ground. While suspended in the air, it raised its right arm and slashed down at Slaking.

Slaking had felt the force of Scizor's earlier attack and did not take this one lightly. Its Earthquake had failed to catch Scizor, so it stopped stamping and lifted its broad hands to block the descending strike.

As Slaking swung an arm to counter, several copies of Scizor suddenly appeared. For a moment it could not tell which was real. Norman could offer no help from his position; Slaking had to trust its instincts.

It punched the nearest image. Its fist passed straight through.

A blade cut through the air behind it, headed for its back. Without turning, Slaking reached one hand behind itself and caught Scizor's surprise attack cleanly.

Ethan could only admire Slaking's ability. It was strong indeed. Despite their long exchange, neither Pokémon had gained a clear advantage.

Compared with an ordinary Slaking, Norman's was remarkably energetic. It had been lazy only for the brief time before the battle began. Ethan wondered how Norman had trained it.

To win, Scizor would have to bring greater force to bear. Ethan called it back to his half of the field and ordered Swords Dance.

As Scizor began building up its strength, Norman smiled. “Don't give it the chance. Slaking, Hyper Beam!”

Slaking opened its mouth. Before Ethan could react, a column of devastating energy erupted from it and shot straight at Scizor.

Ethan had not expected Slaking to have another attack of this kind in reserve. Scizor was halfway through Swords Dance, caught at the worst possible moment. It had no time to dodge before Hyper Beam struck.

The enormous surge of energy swallowed Scizor. Ethan frowned, unable to do anything about it now.

At least Scizor had taken little damage in the fight so far. Barring another surprise, it should be able to withstand this attack.
