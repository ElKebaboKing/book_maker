The speedboat raced across the sea, leaving two long trails in its wake.

Just as Oliver had claimed, his piloting skills truly were excellent. The boat traveled smoothly and rarely pitched violently, making it clear he was an experienced speedboat operator.

Of course, they also had the favorable weather to thank.

Unlike the conditions during their earlier journey to the island, both the sky and the sea were now calm. The weather was perfect for travel and spared them considerable discomfort.

The sea breeze swept over them, and wild Pokémon occasionally leaped from the water. Everyone's thoughts remained fixed upon the upcoming operation, however, leaving them with no interest in conversation, much less the passing scenery.

Vroom, vroom, vroom...

The speedboat's engine droned without pause.

Before long, the boat stopped at the harbor of Ogi Isle's only town. With Oliver leading the way, Ethan's group disembarked and headed directly into a diving supply shop.

The town might have been remote, but as a coastal settlement, it carried everything one could need.

Faced with the dazzling array of equipment, Ethan immediately paid for a diving mask and compact oxygen tank for each person.

Everyone in the group knew how to swim. Ash and Misty in particular were excellent swimmers and could hold their breath underwater for a long time. With their Pokémon assisting them, they needed little other equipment so long as they could breathe.

To make communication easier, Ethan also purchased several writing slates designed for underwater use and distributed them among the group.

When it was time to pay, the shopkeeper recognized Oliver and offered them a discount without compromising the equipment's quality. Ethan's present finances made the savings largely irrelevant, but the arrangement still spared him a considerable amount of money.

After saying goodbye to the shopkeeper, the group left the store. While they still had a signal, Ethan called Anabel and sent her their coordinates.

The International Police would not be participating in the operation, but maintaining communication remained essential. At the very least, it provided them with a safeguard in case something went wrong!

Once every preparation was complete, they boarded the boat again. Guided by Poliwhirl, they eventually reached another stretch of open sea.

Nothing about the area appeared unusual. Several small reefs rose from the water nearby and could serve as landmarks, but they were thoroughly unremarkable. It was impressive that Poliwhirl had remembered the location so precisely.

At the surface, Poliwhirl repeatedly pointed downward. This time, no translation from Gengar was necessary. Everyone understood that Team Rocket's base had to be somewhere below them.

The group began putting on their equipment. Once Ethan finished adjusting his gear, he turned to Oliver and instructed him, "You shouldn't come down with us. Stay here with Lanturn and support us from the surface!"

Oliver knew he could not offer much help below and sensibly agreed. He also understood that someone needed to watch the boat.

"All right. Please be careful!"

They left their luggage aboard, secured their gear, and reviewed their underwater hand signals several times. Then Ethan, Erika, Ash, Misty, Richie, and Brock sat along the side of the boat and fell backward together.

Splash... Splash...

Several heavy impacts sounded in quick succession. The next instant, Ethan felt the seawater engulf his entire body.

The cold water stung his skin, but after swimming for a moment, he quickly adjusted to the temperature.

Several Water-type Pokémon had already been waiting for them. Under their protection, the group sank beneath the surface and swam toward the seabed.

Visibility remained good when they first entered the water, allowing them to see far into the distance. As they continued descending, however, the light slowly vanished and their visible range shrank. Ethan could also clearly feel the water exerting greater pressure against his body!

The area was not truly deep ocean, so the six people and their Pokémon soon reached the bottom.

Through his diving mask, Ethan surveyed the darkness around them. He had no fear of the deep sea, but the environment still felt oppressive. Fortunately, the companions beside him and his own Dragonair provided considerable reassurance.

Following Poliwhirl, they passed through a coral reef and soon saw a brilliantly illuminated underwater base in the distance.

The base stood on a level stretch of seabed. Although the surrounding water was almost completely dark, the bright lights inside made the facility exceptionally conspicuous beneath the sea!

Everyone beside Ethan was astonished by its enormous scale. It was difficult to imagine that Team Rocket had built such a secret base here!

They remained concealed among the coral and observed the facility for quite some time. Apart from several security cameras, they saw no guards on patrol.

That was excellent news for Ethan's group. If they avoided the cameras, they should be able to enter the base quietly without anyone noticing.

However...

The cameras covered every angle around the facility. Avoiding them appeared almost impossible!

How are we supposed to cross?

Once they recognized the problem, everyone turned toward Ethan and waited for him to devise a solution.

During their long time together, Ash, Misty, Brock, and Erika had all gradually come to regard him as the group's central figure!

Even Richie, who had spent relatively little time with them, would not act recklessly in a situation like this. Whether because he respected Ethan's strength or simply believed he should listen to an adult, his first instinct was also to seek Ethan's advice.

Feeling everyone's eyes upon him, Ethan suddenly sensed an enormous amount of pressure.

He watched the cameras and unconsciously rubbed his chin as he considered their options.

After a moment's thought, an idea truly did come to him!

He took out his writing slate and quickly wrote several words, then gestured toward Misty as he conveyed his instructions.

After reading the slate, Misty nodded and directed her Corsola to swim straight toward the Team Rocket base.

While everyone else watched in confusion, Corsola boldly swam in front of one of the cameras like a curious wild Pokémon. As though fascinated by its own reflection in the lens, it blocked the camera with its entire body and stared intently into it!

Such incidents were common beneath the sea. Wild Pokémon were often attracted by human objects, so the behavior would not alarm anyone monitoring the feed.

At that instant, Ethan hurriedly waved for the others to follow. He led the way through the camera's field of view and rapidly approached the Team Rocket base!

Once everyone had safely reached the wall of the facility, Corsola calmly swam away as though it had lost interest in the camera. Sure enough, the maneuver aroused no suspicion!
