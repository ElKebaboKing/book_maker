"Mr. Ethan, I watched several of your matches during the Indigo League Conference. Your Pokémon were all incredibly powerful. Even after so much time, their performances remain vivid in my memory. If the opportunity arises, I would very much like to face you during this Whirl Cup!"

Trinity could speak and laugh freely with Misty while remaining calm and composed. That was because she believed she could defeat Misty, allowing her expression and tone to stay so relaxed.

When facing Ethan, however, she showed ample deference. It represented both respect and the anticipation and unease that came from confronting a powerful competitor!

"I'd like to battle you too, but she's your opponent right now." Ethan smiled softly and pointed at Misty beside him. "If you want to face me, you'll have to defeat this young lady first! A word of advice: don't underestimate her. She's very strong!"

This was Ethan's response, but it also showed that he was standing behind Misty and warning Trinity not to overlook her.

Receiving Ethan's acknowledgment made Misty's face instantly light up!

Nothing could make her happier than praise from the person she liked!

As an adult, Trinity naturally understood Ethan's meaning. She looked at Misty again and nodded solemnly.

"I won't. And if I'm unfortunate enough to lose when the time comes, I hope you'll still agree to a private battle with me, Mr. Ethan!"

The woman clearly did want to face him. She must have possessed considerable confidence in her own strength.

Of course, Ethan had absolute confidence in his Pokémon. Faced with a challenge, he agreed without the slightest hesitation.

"Of course. If you want to challenge me, I'll be ready anytime!"

After receiving his answer, Trinity shook hands with the group and departed.

Watching her leave, Misty felt her fighting spirit surge. She had resolved to give everything she had in the afternoon match and never disappoint Ethan!

More than anything, however, his trust and support left a small spark of happiness in her heart.

Still...

Being called a young lady was rather frustrating.

When will Ethan finally see me as a woman?

If she had her way, she wished he would address her differently. Perhaps as "darling"... or "baby"...

At the thought, her face flushed. She turned toward Ethan and found him looking at her too.

Her blush deepened. Believing he had discovered her secret thoughts, she was about to say something heartfelt when Ethan spoke seriously. "Be careful. That woman is extremely confident, so she must truly be strong. Otherwise, she wouldn't have sought us out herself!"

"Huh?" Misty froze, then nodded as even her ears turned redder. "Oh. Don't worry. I'll definitely take this seriously!"

After Trinity left, the group had no reason to remain in the restaurant, so they went to the waiting room to examine the pairings.

Almost no one remained there. Ethan and the others stopped before the bracket and found that Trinity had spoken the truth: she had been paired against Misty. Their match was second that afternoon. Ethan would battle immediately afterward against a mature-looking, middle-aged man who appeared successful in his career.

Perhaps Trinity's visit had placed her under genuine pressure, or perhaps the match simply felt too close. Misty took Ash and Brock to a nearby practice room to adjust her condition.

Once the three left, only Ethan and Erika remained. The entire waiting room fell silent.

Erika glanced around. Although the room was spacious, aside from several large screens, it contained only hundreds of chairs and rows of sofas. There was not even a single green plant. Disappointed, she looked away.

It was entirely different from what she had imagined.

She had expected every competitors' lounge to be quiet, comfortable, and fully equipped like the one at the Indigo League Conference!

When she turned around, she found Ethan reclining on a sofa and could not help laughing softly. "What is this? You have a match this afternoon too. Don't you need to make some adjustments?"

"Adjustments?" Ethan stretched widely. After a satisfying meal, drowsiness slowly crept over him and made his eyelids increasingly heavy. "How am I supposed to adjust? Blastoise is the only Pokémon I can lead with right now. Its strength is extremely consistent, it's in excellent condition, and its fighting spirit is high. I honestly don't know what there is to change."

Erika nodded. He had a point. With Blastoise maintaining such excellent form, there truly was no need for adjustments. As long as it displayed its usual strength, an upset would be very unlikely.

"Even so, if your opponents see you battle with only one Pokémon, they'll easily identify Blastoise's characteristics and devise tactics specifically against it."

"That would be perfect." Ethan smiled. "If things reach the point where Blastoise loses and Gyarados has to enter, I simply won't interfere. I'll let it fight however it wants."

This was not arrogance on Ethan's part. His enormous red Gyarados was simply so powerful that making it lose would be difficult.

Erika looked surprised. "I've heard you mention it many times. Is your Gyarados truly that strong?"

Ethan thought carefully and realized Erika had indeed never seen Gyarados after its evolution, so she had no understanding of its current strength.

"Do you remember that Team Rocket scientist we caught, Professor Sebastian?" Ethan explained the story in detail. "Back when I was traveling with Sab—uh... while I was traveling, I passed the Lake of Rage and met him for the first time. He struck my Gyarados with the waves from something called Project R, causing it to mutate. Now Gyarados isn't only exceptionally enormous; its color has turned red and its temperament has become even more brutal. Of course, its strength increased further in exchange."

"I see."

"To be honest..." Ethan suddenly sighed. "If it weren't my own Pokémon, even I might feel a chill down my back whenever I met those enormous red eyes. And its fighting style is so savage that it can hardly be called a battle anymore..."

This surprised Erika. For someone with Ethan's personality to describe his own Pokémon this way meant the Gyarados truly was dangerous.

That only made her want to see it for herself and learn what kind of power the red Gyarados possessed.

Erika suddenly remembered something and pointed toward the bracket. "By the way, you should pay some attention to this person."

Following her finger, Ethan saw the name "Zhe."

"What about him?"

"He's very strong too. You could call him the second favorite in this year's Whirl Cup, behind you." Erika considered for a moment. "I paid attention to his previous match. The strength of his Pokémon can't be underestimated. Speaking of rankings, Miss Trinity should probably be considered the third favorite. If we set Misty aside, the rest of the field is merely decent. No unexpected dark horse has distinguished themselves."

Erika's judgment was very accurate. If she considered the others merely decent, then a clear gap truly separated those Trainers from Trinity and Zhe.

Ethan nodded and examined the bracket once more. Zhe was in the same half, meaning he should become Ethan's next opponent if both successfully advanced.

The second favorite... He should be able to take a beating, shouldn't he?

Ethan was genuinely looking forward to it.

They waited in the lounge for a long time, but Misty and the others still did not return. Bored, Ethan released several of his smaller Pokémon and let them play nearby.

Listening to their cheerful cries and running footsteps, his eyelids grew heavier. Before he knew it, he had fallen asleep.

Some unknown amount of time later, he awoke and found his own jacket draped over him. A faint floral fragrance lingered on it, clearly left behind by Erika.

He slowly sat up and looked toward the other side of the waiting room. Erika had released her Pokémon as well and was using a brush to groom both hers and Ethan's.

From the side, her expression appeared focused and serious. Her sleeves were rolled up, revealing two smooth, pale arms.

Sensing that Ethan had awakened, Erika turned toward him.

"Awake now?"

Rubbing his eyes, Ethan sat upright. "How long did I sleep?"

He did not need to sniff carefully to notice the faint scent of Erika's perfume on his jacket.

Besides being a Gym Leader, Erika owned a perfume shop and had refined taste in fragrances. He could even detect a different scent on her each day. The one left on his jacket now carried the fragrance of daisies, delicate rather than overpowering, yet able to linger for a long time.

"Not long. Only a little over ten minutes. There's still time before the match. Wouldn't you like to sleep a little more?"

Ethan patted his cheeks to wake himself. By the time he lowered his hands, the last trace of drowsiness had disappeared.

"Misty and the others still aren't back?"

"They probably went directly to the battlefield." Erika shook her head. "Miss Trinity clearly placed her under considerable pressure. And your expectations have made her more determined than ever!"

They chatted for a while. When the rest period ended and the match gradually approached, the other competitors began returning to the waiting room. Ethan and Erika recalled their respective Pokémon, and Erika left alone to return to the stands.

Even then, Misty had not returned. Ethan had no idea how well her preparations had gone.

With Ash and Brock accompanying her, however, there should not be any problems. Ethan shifted into a more comfortable position on the sofa and turned his attention to the large screen once again.

After the announcer warmed up the crowd, the quarterfinals officially began on-screen!
