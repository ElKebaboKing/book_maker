"Don't worry." Nurse Joy smiled. "After emergency treatment, Raikou is no longer in danger. That said, Electric-type Pokémon possess a certain degree of resistance to electricity. This is the first time I've ever seen one suffer burns from such a powerful electrical attack!"

Although Nurse Joy appeared calm, she was brimming with excitement inside.

She had treated many rare Pokémon since taking charge of this Pokémon Center, but she had never seen a Legendary Pokémon before. This time, she had finally fulfilled that dream. Among all the Nurse Joys, she was undoubtedly the luckiest!

This is Raikou, a Legendary Pokémon! A Pokémon that everyone in Johto dreams of seeing, yet most people may never encounter even once in their entire lives! I can't believe I got to see it and even operate on it myself! Hehe, the next time all the Nurse Joys get together, I'll finally have something to boast about!

Jimmy naturally had no idea what she was thinking, but her words finally allowed him to breathe a long sigh of relief.

"That's great! So Raikou is all right now?"

"I wouldn't go that far." Coming back to herself, Nurse Joy gently shook her head. "Raikou suffered severe injuries, so its body is still very weak. It will need time and care to recover fully. I estimate that it won't wake until tomorrow morning. For now, let it rest here overnight."

Jimmy thanked her. Once Nurse Joy had left, Ethan and the others gathered in front of the window set into the wall. Through the enormous pane of glass, they could see that Raikou had been moved onto one of the Pokémon beds inside.

Raikou lay there with its eyes closed, breathing evenly. Wires and soft tubes had been attached all over its body. The machines beside it emitted steady beeps. Though Raikou still appeared unconscious, all its visible injuries had healed, and it was clearly out of danger.

After confirming that it was safe, Ethan patted Jimmy on the shoulder.

"All right, that's enough watching. We should get some rest too. Nurse Joy just said it won't wake until tomorrow morning, so there's no point wasting our time here."

This time, Jimmy did not insist on staying. Before leaving, however, he continued to stare at Raikou for a long while. Only then did he silently follow Ethan and the two girls out of the corridor.

Though they had prepared themselves for what lay beyond the corridor door, the sight still startled them.

Dozens of people had gathered outside. It looked as though every Trainer in the Pokémon Center had come. With all their Pokémon beside them, they had packed the entrance so tightly that there was hardly room to move! Fortunately, Chansey was standing guard, and no one dared to force their way through.

After all, a Pokémon Center's Chansey did more than simply assist Nurse Joy. It represented the entire Pokémon League!

These Trainers might scale mountains and dive into deep pools in the wild, but despite their apparent daring, they were still law-abiding citizens. None of them would break into a Pokémon Center, challenge the authority of the law, or risk a direct confrontation with the Pokémon League.

Unless an evil organization like Team Rocket or someone intent on committing a crime targeted this place, it was extremely safe. Anyone who forced their way inside would quickly find themselves wanted by Officer Jenny and the police.

Otherwise, so many Trainers would never entrust their Pokémon to Nurse Joy, much less sleep so soundly here.

Therefore, even though everyone knew Raikou was inside, no one dared make a reckless move.

Team Rocket had only just been defeated. Their mechanical shark needed repairs, and if they wanted to face Ethan again, they would need much stronger Pokémon. They were unlikely to return and invite another beating anytime soon.

The moment the crowd saw Ethan and the others emerge, their eyes lit up, and they surged forward together.

"Hey! Did you really bring back a Raikou!?"

"Did one of you capture it!?"

"I'll trade you every Pokémon I own for that Raikou!"

Chaotic voices filled the air. Both Jimmy and Marina were startled by such "enthusiasm." They froze, unsure how to respond. Fortunately, Ethan grabbed one of them with each hand and dragged them out of the crowd. Only after they entered the dormitory wing did they finally lose their pursuers.

"That was terrifying..." Leaning against the wall, Marina looked back to make sure no one had followed them, then wiped the sweat from her forehead with lingering fear. "I don't understand. Why are those people so obsessed?"

"What's so strange about it?" Ethan cast her a sidelong glance. "Think about how distracted Jimmy was just now. It isn't hard to guess how those people feel about Raikou!"

"Distracted?" Jimmy scratched his head in confusion, completely oblivious to his own behavior. "Was I? I was acting perfectly normal."

Marina merely rolled her eyes at him.

"You call that normal? Your eyes were practically glued to Raikou!"

After exchanging goodnights with Erika, Ethan returned to his room. He took a shower to wash away the day's exhaustion, then settled comfortably into bed.

Night had already fallen outside the window.

He wondered how Gengar was doing.

The thought had barely crossed his mind when an oval shadow passed straight through the wall.

It was his Gengar!

"I'm back!"

"How did it go? They didn't discover you, did they?"

"Those two? There's no chance they could ever detect me!" Gengar boasted before breaking into a wide grin. "I followed them all the way to a secret base. It's southeast of the Pokémon Center and isn't very far away. Hehe, I even slipped inside and looked around. It seems to be a Team Rocket research center, and I found someone we know there!"

Ethan raised an eyebrow. "Someone we know? Who?"

"That Team Rocket scientist who created the red Gyarados!"

"So it's him." Ethan nodded. The image of a researcher with square glasses and a goatee came to mind, along with Project R. "Professor Sebastian, if I remember correctly. I never expected him to leave the secret base at the Lake of Rage and come here! Is he carrying out some new research?"

"I don't know." Gengar shook its head. "I was afraid they'd discover me, so I didn't get too close."

"You made the right choice." Ethan patted Gengar on the shoulder, then took out his phone and sent the information it had gathered to Looker. "We'll leave the rest to the International Police. When it comes to dealing with Team Rocket, they're the real professionals!"
