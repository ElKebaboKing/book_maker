After leaving the ceremonial platform, Ethan returned to the competitors' waiting room.

Erika, Ash, and the others had been waiting there for some time. His arrival naturally brought another round of congratulations. Even Trinity, whom he had only just defeated, had not left and appeared there with everyone else!

Pop! Pop!

Brock and Ash held party poppers and covered Ethan with streamers and glittering confetti. Misty placed a bright garland around his neck. Everyone smiled, clearly delighted that Ethan had won the championship.

Erika stood nearby and applauded softly. Beside her, Trinity approached Ethan with a complicated expression and said sincerely, "Congratulations. Your Pokémon are far too powerful. I accept my defeat completely."

Trinity had suffered such a brutal loss, yet instead of leaving immediately, she had come to congratulate him. The sight left Ethan feeling extremely guilty.

Sigh. If I'd known, I would have switched Gyarados out halfway through. At least then she wouldn't have lost so miserably...

The good news was that, just as Ethan had hoped before the match, Trinity's expression showed none of Aya's former desire to take revenge upon him.

The battle had clearly beaten both the resistance and the courage out of her!

"I'm sorry for hurting your Pokémon so badly. But please don't hold it against me. That's simply how my Gyarados fights. Even if it tries to restrain itself, it really can't..."

Ethan's tone was completely sincere, and the final trace of unwillingness in Trinity's heart vanished.

She smiled. "You can't take all the blame. A Pokémon battle isn't a game played by children. It's dangerous and cruel by nature, so injuries are unavoidable. If you'd held back out of sympathy, I would have felt even more humiliated. Ultimately, I lost because my Pokémon still haven't trained enough. I was also overestimating myself when I imagined I could defeat such a powerful opponent."

Her words left Ethan even more embarrassed.

"Uh... I didn't expect you to possess such perspective. That's admirable. Still, you should go to the Pokémon Center as soon as possible and ask Nurse Joy to treat your Pokémon's injuries. You don't want them suffering any lasting complications."

Trinity naturally understood how urgent the situation was. She should have taken her Pokémon to the Pokémon Center immediately after the match. Even so, she had come here first because she wanted to congratulate Ethan at the earliest opportunity. Otherwise, people might think she was a sore loser who had slipped away without even saying goodbye!

Even in defeat, she had her dignity!

Now that she had offered her congratulations, however, it was truly time to take her Pokémon to Nurse Joy.

"In that case, I'll leave first. I hope the rest of your journey goes smoothly and that you continue catching powerful Pokémon! Oh, right—your next goal must be the Silver Conference, correct? That's the highest honor in our Johto region! I hope you defeat every formidable opponent and become champion in one decisive run, just as you did today!"

"Thank you for the good wishes. Take care of yourself too, and I hope we meet again someday!"

The two solemnly shook hands.

To Ethan, Trinity was a strong and respectable opponent.

To Trinity, Ethan would unquestionably leave a lasting impression. His red Gyarados in particular would probably remain an inescapable shadow in her mind for the rest of her life.

After watching Trinity depart, Ethan, Erika, and the others felt the atmosphere inside the waiting room become noticeably more relaxed. They talked for a while longer before the conversation turned toward the strange event that had occurred when Ethan received the blessing on the ceremonial platform.

They had all seen the change from the stands and found it exceptionally surprising.

Erika was the first to ask with concern, "What happened just now? Something like that isn't supposed to be part of the normal ceremony, is it?"

"Exactly!" Misty hurriedly added. "I saw that light wrap around you, and then you just went whoosh and flew into the sky! What happened?"

"It wasn't nearly that dramatic!" Ethan grinned and lightly tapped her head. "Whoosh? I'm not some kind of rocket! I only rose about a dozen centimeters above the platform. I was nowhere near floating high in the air!"

After the blessing, Ethan's conversation with Sea Priestess Maya had not been carried through the speakers. Erika and the others had seen the unusual event, but they had no idea what had happened.

Seeing everyone watching him curiously, Ethan shook his head and recounted both his experience and Maya's interpretation.

After listening, Brock rubbed his chin and analyzed the situation. "So unlike the previous winners, who held only an empty title, you actually received the Sea Spirit's blessing?"

Ethan hesitated. "I'm not entirely sure, but according to Maya, the gemstone definitely reacted to me somehow. She went to consult their records and see whether anything similar has happened during the ceremony's long history."

"Do you feel different anywhere?" Misty's eyes gleamed. "You're a Sea Hero! Traveling freely beneath the sea and connecting your heart with Water-type Pokémon are exactly what we Water Pokémon Trainers dream of! Why don't you test it right now? See whether you can truly understand a Water-type Pokémon's thoughts. Then everything will be clear!"

Erika nodded in agreement. "Misty is right. There's no harm in trying."

The instant Erika supported her idea, Misty pulled a Poké Ball from the pouch at her waist without waiting for Ethan's agreement. But just as she prepared to release Poliwhirl, blue light flashed from the pouch, and Psyduck ran out on its own again!

"You little pest!" Misty flew into a rage at the sight. She immediately seized Psyduck by the neck with both hands and shook it furiously. "Why do you always come out without permission? Tell me! Hurry up and tell me!"

Psyduck's face turned bright red, and it looked exceptionally uncomfortable beneath her grip.

Seeing this, Ethan smiled and waved his hand, stopping the rampaging Misty.

"Forget it. Psyduck will do. No matter what else we say, it's still a Water-type Pokémon."

Only then did Misty release it with obvious reluctance. Psyduck, nearly shaken unconscious, collapsed limply onto the floor.

Ethan cleared his throat, approached Psyduck, and crouched to meet its vacant stare.

Psyduck tilted its head and looked at him in confusion, having no idea why this human was staring at it. Then it glanced toward its Trainer, ignored Ethan entirely, and waddled around the waiting room on its yellow legs.

Ethan raised his eyebrows. Astonishment crossed his face. After remaining frozen for a moment, he stood with an equally puzzled expression.

The instant Erika and the others saw his reaction, they realized something must have happened!

Misty asked impatiently, "Well!? Well!? Ethan, could you understand what it was thinking just now!?"
