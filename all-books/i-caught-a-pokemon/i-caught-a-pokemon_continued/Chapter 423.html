As Raikou stared in confusion, water suddenly exploded behind it. Before it could react, a tremendous force slammed directly into it!

The impact sent Raikou flying until it finally struck a rock.

Bang!

A circular web of cracks spread across the stone. As Raikou slid to the ground, it looked toward the lake in alarm and saw an enormous head emerging from the water.

Water streamed from that massive head. Through the flow, a pair of fierce red eyes glared fixedly at Raikou, presenting a savage and intensely oppressive sight!

It was that Steelix!

Raikou could not understand why Steelix did not fear the water!

And with its immense weight and the mud covering the lakebed, how had it climbed out!?

Raikou tried to struggle to its feet, but a sudden wave of dizziness swept over it.

It had already suffered severe injuries when Team Rocket attacked the previous afternoon. A night of treatment had healed the visible wounds, but Raikou had yet to recover fully in either mind or body.

More crucially, it had eaten nothing and taken only a few drinks of water. Its reserves had been dangerously low all along!

On top of that, it had been trapped in the barrier for a second time moments ago. The repeated electric shocks had added many fresh injuries. Enduring this long had already demanded tremendous effort. Now, after taking a direct blow from Crystal Steelix, Raikou's head spun and darkness clouded its vision. It stood weakly in place.

Crystal Steelix naturally gave Raikou no time to recover. Its enormous body climbed from the water and charged straight toward it.

"It's over," Ethan said softly.

Just as he declared, Crystal Steelix raised its massive tail and used Iron Tail again, lashing it viciously into Raikou.

Smack!

Raikou was in no condition to evade. The instant Iron Tail struck, the blow knocked it unconscious!

Seeing Raikou collapse, Ethan took out the Ultra Ball he had prepared and hurled it with all his strength.

The Ultra Ball traced a perfect arc through the air. As it reached Raikou, red light burst from within and enveloped the Legendary Pokémon.

Like an enormous net, the light drew Raikou into the Ultra Ball. The ball shook violently several times, but each movement grew smaller. Finally, with a soft click, it fell completely still!

"Yes!"

Erika was delighted for Ethan from the bottom of her heart when she saw that he had captured Raikou.

Ethan could not suppress his own smile.

After Raikou slipped from his grasp at Greenfield, he had abandoned all hope of capturing it. He never imagined they would meet again!

He felt extraordinarily lucky, but he also had to acknowledge that this capture marked enormous progress for him.

At the abandoned power plant, he had taken advantage of the terrain and surrounded Zapdos with his entire team. Later, he captured Articuno using only a handful of Pokémon. After that, he battled Moltres with the two Legendary Pokémon Zapdos and Articuno. At almost every step along the way, he had relied on superior numbers.

Now he had finally captured a Legendary Pokémon in a one-on-one battle. How could that not count as growth?

Of course, Raikou had entered the battle in poor condition, and Ethan had exploited a favorable type matchup. Even so, he believed that as his Pokémon continued to grow stronger, the day would eventually come when he could capture such Pokémon with far greater ease!

Despite everything, the result still felt rather dreamlike.

Only a few days had passed since he sent away the three Legendary birds and freed several places on his belt. Yet he had already captured Raikou.

Capturing Raikou was wonderful news, but his belt had only one open place left. Space was tight again...

Olivine City.

A port city filled with the scent of the sea breeze.

Located in west-central Johto near Ecruteak City and Cianwood City, Olivine faced the ocean and was one of the region's most important tourist destinations.

A cruise ship called the S.S. Aqua docked at its port. Once a month, the ship made a round trip to Vermilion City in Kanto. Compared with other routes, it offered passengers a leisurely vacation at sea. Besides airplanes and the magnetic levitation train, the S.S. Aqua was therefore a popular third choice for travelers heading to Kanto.

For some reason, whenever Olivine City came to mind, Ethan always thought of Farfetch'd. The two had nothing in common except that their Chinese names shared the character for scallion, yet his thoughts invariably linked them.

At that moment, Ethan and Erika were walking along Olivine City's coastal boulevard toward the Olivine Gym.

They had arrived the previous evening. After spending the night at the Pokémon Center and eating breakfast, they had skipped any sightseeing and headed directly to their main objective.

The Olivine Gym was the city's official Gym. It stood in the northwest of the city and was led by Jasmine, a specialist in Steel-type Pokémon. Defeating her would earn the challenger a Mineral Badge.

Olivine's docks lay beside the road. Even with tall buildings blocking parts of the view, the pair still caught periodic glimpses of the city's landmark: the Glitter Lighthouse.

According to local stories, an Ampharos lived at the top of the lighthouse. Every night, its electric light guided passing ships. The Ampharos had become part of the city itself and was protected by Olivine's residents.

Ethan and Erika stopped beside the railing to admire the lighthouse from a distance, then continued toward the Gym.

After crossing nearly half the city, they finally reached their destination.

From the outside, the Olivine Gym stood four stories tall. It had a flat-roofed trapezoidal structure, rather like a pyramid with its peak removed. Its walls consisted of enormous unbroken panes of glass, giving the building a distinctly modern appearance.

Inside, Ethan explained his purpose to a staff member and was led to the battlefield.

Compared with the exterior, the battlefield was far more austere. It resembled an enormous warehouse, with no mechanisms or special features. Even the ground was an ordinary field of packed earth.

Ethan and Erika waited in the empty arena for several minutes before Jasmine finally appeared.

Jasmine was quite young, only seventeen or eighteen. She was petite and fairly pretty, with long flaxen hair falling behind her. A bun had been tied on either side of her head, making them resemble a pair of little wings.

She wore a pale blue dress with a huge bow at her chest, giving her an almost childlike appearance.

It was difficult to imagine that such a pure-looking young woman favored Steel-type Pokémon!

What does she see in them? Is it because she likes pole dancing?

Jasmine smiled when she saw them. "Welcome to the Olivine Gym."

Ethan stepped forward. "Hello, Miss Jasmine. I've come to challenge the Olivine Gym."

"I know who you are." Jasmine approached him and extended a friendly hand. "You're the Indigo League Champion. I watched your matches!"
