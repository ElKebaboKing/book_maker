Ethan followed Maya out of the banquet hall and deeper into the Temple of the Sea.

Night had fallen during the feast, and the candles in the corridors had been lit again.

Their warm, dim light fell across the stone beneath their feet. Ethan could once more hear the familiar sound of water swirling through the temple.

On the way, he tried to ask a few questions about the succession rite. Maya seemed to have reservations, however, and revealed nothing, making the whole matter feel mysterious.

Oh well...

If she did not want to explain, Ethan saw little point in asking again.

Whatever the rite involved, he trusted Maya not to harm him.

In private, another question kept returning to him. Why had the Sea Spirit's blessing affected him differently from everyone else?

Neither he nor Maya understood the unusual changes.

Sometimes he wondered whether they might have something to do with the fact that he had come to this world from another one. He could think of no other difference between himself and the people who had received the blessing before him.

And so they walked in silence, each occupied with their own thoughts.

At last they reached a room. Ethan glanced at its door and was surprised to recognize Maya's bedroom!

Huh!?

Hadn't she said they were performing a succession rite? Why had she brought him here? Would they hold it in her room, or did she need to collect something first?

Still wondering, he followed her inside.

On his previous visit, the room had been in ruins. Now it had been fully repaired, without a trace of the damage.

Two attendants were present. As Ethan entered, they came forward and removed his coat before he could refuse.

He could no longer contain his curiosity. "Miss Maya, what exactly is this succession rite?"

Maya walked to the balcony and looked at the moon. Then she turned to face him and, apparently ready to stop concealing the truth, began speaking slowly. "Since the Temple of the Sea was founded, the Sea Priestess has always led it. That position has always been held by a woman. It is both our tradition and an unchangeable rule."

Ethan nodded. Oliver had mentioned this while they were aboard the speedboat. As far as he knew, a woman had indeed always served as the temple's leader.

But why bring it up now?

Maya continued, "Compared with men, women are less likely to be swayed by wealth, status, beauty, or other temptations. We can remain more neutral, without a pronounced political allegiance. That has helped the temple avoid losing its way and maintain its special standing in the Whirl Islands. Yet one problem has troubled us throughout our history."

Ethan chuckled. "With all your wealth and influence, you still have problems?"

Maya gave him a long look. "Succession."

Succession...

Ethan stopped smiling and waited patiently for her to continue.

"I'm ashamed to say it," Maya said with a sigh, "but during the temple's first century or two, the position passed to whoever was most capable. A Sea Priestess would hand it over when a suitable successor appeared. Several centuries ago, however, an ancestor of mine wanted to keep that power. She changed the tradition and passed the office to her own descendants. It has remained in our family ever since."

Ethan recalled that Maya had told him she inherited the position from her mother.

Just as Maya said, her family had kept a firm hold on the office for generations.

Moonlight fell over her, lending her a sacred air, as though she wore a veil of silver.

"As a member of that family," she said firmly, "I must someday bear a child and pass on both our bloodline and the office of Sea Priestess."

Her meaning was plain enough that Ethan began to suspect where this was going.

"So..."

Maya took several steps toward him. "So it has long been common for the Sea Hero and the Sea Priestess to join together. Many of my ancestors were Sea Heroes themselves, and the children born of such a union inherited an even more perfect bloodline!"

So that's what she meant by the succession rite...

To be honest, Ethan had never imagined it would mean this!

Then again, perhaps generations of improved genes explained both Maya's beauty and her noble bearing!

"Since you already agreed to the succession rite," Maya began, "we could..."

"I'm sorry..." Ethan interrupted with a rueful smile and shook his head. "I didn't know what the rite meant when I agreed. And I have a girlfriend, so this... I'm afraid I have to refuse."

Maya did not seem surprised by his answer.

"Your girlfriend would be Miss Erika, wouldn't she?"

Ethan opened his mouth to say that Erika was not his girlfriend. Yet their relationship was no longer that of ordinary friends, and to an outsider they seemed inseparable.

But if he called Erika his girlfriend, what did that make Sabrina?

He and Sabrina had never stated their relationship outright, but in both their actions and their feelings, she was the one who should be considered his girlfriend.

After hesitating for a long while, he chose not to explain. "Even if I didn't have a girlfriend, I'm not ready to think about having children at my age."

Maya smiled. "Surely you don't think conceiving a child is so simple? Besides, I won't tell Miss Erika. I know your aim is to challenge the various League tournaments, not to remain here. If a child is born, I'll raise her myself. I won't bring you any trouble!"

"No..." Ethan sighed. "You've misunderstood. This isn't about whether she'd find out..."

Seeing his reluctance, Maya assumed he and Erika were deeply attached and that he did not want to betray her.

"You needn't make this more complicated than it is," Maya said gently. "You wouldn't lose anything. If we have a child, the Temple of the Sea will always stand behind you. I won't cling to you. I only want your help in continuing my bloodline."

Her insistence baffled him. "Why me?"

Maya shook her head. "A new Sea Hero is chosen every three years, so yes, I have other candidates. But you're different. Winning the Indigo League proved your strength. Saving the Gods of the Sea without trying to catch them proved you aren't driven solely by gain. And you saved me, which moved me deeply. Most of all, I like you. I don't want to let my chance with you pass me by in this lifetime!"

Her heartfelt confession moved Ethan.

He could not say what he felt, but reason told him he had to refuse. He could not do this to Sabrina.

Then Erika came to mind.

Nothing had happened between them during their travels together. Even so, both of them knew their relationship had begun to cross the boundary of friendship...

They worked together effortlessly, understood each other's views, and could talk about anything. They had become companions in spirit!

When he fell silent, Maya boldly stepped closer and took his hand.

"My Hero, the man I love, this is my final request. Please don't turn me down!"
