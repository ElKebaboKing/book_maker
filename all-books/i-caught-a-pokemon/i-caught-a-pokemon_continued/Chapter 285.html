"Just as I thought." Looker raised a hand as Silver refused to surrender. He released Growlithe and Croagunk. "I knew you wouldn't come quietly. Don't blame us when we use force to arrest you!"

Ethan was only helping Looker, not leading the arrest. As the battle began, he sent out Gardevoir to join Gengar, which was already beside him. The two guarded his side in case Silver tried to escape past him.

On the opposite side, Sabrina released Alakazam and the Slowking she had caught in the Orange Islands.

"Growlithe, Flamethrower! Croagunk, Sludge Bomb!"

Silver shouted his own orders. "Sneasel, Icy Wind! Zubat, Air Cutter! Gastly, Shadow Ball!"

Five Pokémon clashed. Attacks flew through the air, and cries of anger and pain echoed over the forest. Wild Pokémon that had been peering out from cover scattered in fright.

Silver had three Pokémon to Looker's two, giving him a slight numerical advantage. But with Ethan's and Sabrina's Pokémon guarding the sides, Looker did not have to hold back. He pressed Silver from the start.

Boom! Boom!

Explosions followed one another. Thick trees toppled under the continuing attacks.

Ethan watched for a while. When Zubat had just finished an attack, he told Gardevoir to use Confusion. Caught off guard, Zubat lost control of its flight and slammed into a rock.

With one opponent disrupted, Looker had less to contend with. His Pokémon drove Silver's back step by step.

Tch. Silver grew increasingly irritated. The International Police officer was difficult enough on his own. The other two were watching every escape route.

He could not win by staying in this fight. It was time to get away.

Gritting his teeth, Silver ordered all three Pokémon to attack together. They forced Looker back for a moment, then turned and charged toward a gap beside Ethan.

Ethan saw what he intended. He waved a hand. Gengar and Gardevoir struck together, and the difference in power sent Silver's Pokémon back.

"Damn it!"

Silver changed direction and tried Sabrina's side. Team Rocket's stories said she was formidable, but to him she was still a woman. Even with psychic abilities, surely she would be easier to overcome than a Psychic-type Pokémon.

He had chosen the most dangerous route of all.

Sabrina did not need either of her Pokémon to stop him. Her hair lifted as she released her own psychic power. An overwhelming force swept into Silver and his Pokémon and knocked them all to the ground.

Bang!

Silver's head swam. He was hurt and pale, but what shook him most was the power he had just felt. How could this woman be so strong? It seemed beyond anything a human should be able to do. Escape now looked impossible.

"Stop struggling," Looker said. He brought Growlithe and Croagunk closer. "Those two are far beyond what you can handle. Give up and come quietly."

A cornered beast might still fight, but Silver had not yet grown into the dangerous Team Rocket figure Looker had imagined. He could not break through the cage they had made around him. After the short, intense battle, Sneasel, Zubat, and Gastly were covered in injuries and had little strength left.

Looker took out handcuffs and stepped forward to arrest him.

At that moment, a brilliant light flared above the lake beside them. Everyone turned.

As the glare eased, Ethan narrowed his eyes. A Pokémon stood at its center, on a rock rising from the water. The remaining light hid its features, but he could make out a four-legged shape, flowing hair, and two long ribbon-like tails. It looked graceful even in silhouette.

"What Pokémon is that?" Looker called, shielding his eyes.

The light faded. Its shape became clear, and the sight startled them even more. There was no mistaking it.

"Suicune!" Looker cried.

The Pokémon's body was blue-green with white markings. A crest like a blue jewel adorned its head, and two pale streamers trailed behind it.

According to legend, Suicune had once been one of three unknown Pokémon that died in the fire at the Burned Tower. Ho-Oh restored them to life, and they became Suicune, Entei, and Raikou. Because of Ho-Oh, they were also known together as its guardians.

Looker still seemed unable to believe what he was seeing. "Why would the legendary Suicune appear here?"

No one answered. Ethan had seen and even caught several Legendary Pokémon, yet the sight of Suicune still thrilled him. He quickly took out his Pokédex and pointed it at the Pokémon.

"Beep. Suicune, the Aurora Pokémon. A Water type and one of the Legendary Pokémon. Gentle as a spring, it seeks places where clear water flows. It races over the land with gliding movements and can instantly purify dirty water. The north wind is said to rise when Suicune appears, and it runs away with that wind. It is called the embodiment of the north wind."

Suicune regarded them with calm eyes. When their gazes met, they understood why it had come. This powerful Pokémon valued peace. It had heard the fight and believed Ethan's group was disturbing and damaging the forest, so it had appeared to stop them.
