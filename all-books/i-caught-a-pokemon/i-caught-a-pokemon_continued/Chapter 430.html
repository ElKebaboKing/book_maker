After emerging from the competitors' passage, Ethan headed directly to Field F.

The preliminary battlefields had been set up across a pool inside the Whirl Stadium. The organizers had divided the water into sixteen small squares, where the competitors would battle for the sixty-four places in the main tournament.

Since all sixteen fields shared the same pool, however, the water beneath them was connected. If one match became too intense, the resulting waves could easily affect the competitors nearby.

Fortunately, Ethan and the others had explored the venue yesterday. They understood the conditions on the water and were mentally prepared.

Ethan entered the waiting area and quietly waited for his turn. Two matches were still ahead of his on Field F, so he used the time to glance toward Ash and Misty.

Ash had been assigned to Field A, while Misty was at Field B. Through the layers of competitors, Ethan could just make out Ash waiting for his turn as well. Misty had already begun her match.

The angle prevented him from seeing Misty's opponent, but with her strength, she would certainly be fine as long as she did not encounter anyone too troublesome.

Turning back, Ethan focused on his own battlefield and searched for potential opponents.

The first two matches on Field F ended quickly. After watching them, Ethan could only describe the battles as weaklings flailing at one another.

From his perspective, these people were simply too weak. It was not that none of the competitors showed promise, but the great majority performed below the usual standard.

It appeared this was the greatest drawback of removing the Badge requirement!

A little over ten minutes later, the first two matches were over. At the referee's signal, it was finally Ethan's turn to compete.

He stepped out of the crowd and onto the battlefield. For a moment, every competitor around Field F focused on him.

It was not because there was anything unusual about him. Almost every entrant received the same attention.

After all the experience he had accumulated, Ethan remained perfectly calm beneath so many gazes. He did not grow nervous and uneasy like the first-time competitors beside him.

For Trainers with social anxiety or who simply struggled to interact with others, dozens of eyes staring at them made their hands and feet turn cold and sent nervous sweat trickling down their backs.

The preliminary round used one-on-one rules. Each competitor sent out a single Pokémon, and one match decided the winner. There was no room whatsoever for error.

Ethan's opponent in this match was a boy only seven or eight years old. The child still looked very young, yet showed no stage fright at all. Eagerness filled his face, and he was clearly looking forward to the battle!

They took their positions on opposite sides and faced each other across the water.

Studying his opponent, Ethan stroked his chin.

Which Pokémon should he send out?

At present, he had only three Water-type Pokémon: Blastoise, the enormous red Gyarados, and Suicune.

Hmm...

He considered his options for a moment.

Suicune certainly could not be revealed on an occasion like this. It was not that Ethan refused to use Legendary Pokémon in battle. He simply had not encountered the right opportunity, and bringing Suicune out now would be absurdly excessive. If he ever faced an opponent like that Trainer who used Legendary Pokémon, he could answer in kind, but it would clearly be a long time before he met such a person.

As for his enormous red Gyarados...

Forget it. For one thing, it was far away at Professor Cerise's laboratory, and it was too late to change his team now. For another, Gyarados was simply too ferocious. Whenever possible, Ethan preferred to keep it out of battle. He had no desire to leave the other competitors psychologically scarred.

Having reached his decision, Ethan closed his hand around Blastoise's Poké Ball.

There was no other choice. He would have to rely on Blastoise!

Ethan threw the Poké Ball forward. At the same time, the young boy sent out his own Pokémon.

Blue light flashed, and a Poliwag appeared before Ethan.

Blastoise towered over Poliwag. With one Water-type Pokémon enormous and the other tiny, Blastoise looked down at its opponent and found it difficult to muster any interest, even though Ethan had warned it before the match not to grow careless.

Ethan would have liked to use Dragonair, but the organizers imposed a strict requirement on every Pokémon entering the tournament: each one had to possess the Water type. Dragonair might be capable of living in water, but it did not qualify under the rules.

As soon as the match began, both Trainers ordered their Pokémon to attack.

Poliwag used Water Gun. Blastoise simply endured the minor damage, charged straight up to Poliwag, and finished it with an ordinary Tackle followed by Bite.

The victory was swift and decisive, without the slightest complication.

Blastoise had once helped Ethan win the Indigo League Conference. Bringing a Pokémon like this to a small tournament amounted to a complete massacre.

"How could this happen?"

The young boy watched his Pokémon's eyes turn into spirals as it sank beneath the water. He collapsed onto the ground, unable to believe what he was seeing!

Seeing Poliwag defeated so easily shattered his confidence. All his enthusiasm from before the match vanished, and the loss dealt him an especially heavy blow!

After eliminating his first opponent, Ethan won his second and third matches just as easily.

Because every participating Pokémon shared the Water type, the Water-type moves they ordinarily relied upon were greatly weakened. They had to depend on attacks of other types instead, giving a tremendous advantage to tough Pokémon with thick hides and defensive options.

When all three matches ended, Ethan had secured his place in the main tournament. Upon returning to the waiting room, he found Ash and Misty arriving together.

They looked relaxed and chatted cheerfully along the way, clearly having advanced as well.

"How did it go? Everything went smoothly?" Ethan asked.

Ash flashed him an okay sign. "It was way too easy. There wasn't even a decent opponent!"

Misty nodded in complete agreement. "It really was easy. It wasn't what I imagined at all. I thought we'd meet stronger competitors!"

All three had performed normally, and none had suffered an unexpected upset. They had taken a successful first step, leaving everyone delighted. Now all they had to do was wait for tomorrow's main tournament.

After leaving the stadium, they met up with Erika and Brock. The two had watched every one of their matches from the stands and naturally knew the results perfectly well. When the competitors returned, another round of congratulations inevitably followed.

To reward everyone for their excellent performances, Ethan took them to another restaurant and treated them to a feast of grilled meat!
