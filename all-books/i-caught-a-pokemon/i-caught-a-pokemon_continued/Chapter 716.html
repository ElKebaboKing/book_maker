“What did you say?”

Ash's gratitude vanished in an instant. He was furious. The boy who had rescued him merely stood with his hands in his pockets and looked back calmly.

A slightly heavyset boy beside him smiled at Ash. “It's your first time here, isn't it? I heard you say you wanted to visit the Battle Tower. It isn't that way. Come with us.”

He stepped onto a moving walkway going in the other direction.

Ethan took a closer look at the four newcomers.

The boy who had rescued Ash seemed fourteen or fifteen. He wore a gray shirt and trousers that looked rather old-fashioned, and his hair reached past his ears. With his fair skin, fine features, and composed expression, he looked like someone who fought with his head. In that respect, he was Ash's opposite.

The heavyset boy who had spoken was about the same age. He wore a red headband, a black shirt, and shorts, giving him a faintly hip-hop style.

The other two were younger girls dressed alike, and their faces looked nearly identical. Twins? Ethan had not met twins since arriving in this world.

They seemed about Max's age. Both wore tall hats and short skirts, with only the colors of their outfits to distinguish them. Ethan looked again and noticed their bright, innocent smiles.

Finally, he glanced at the first boy's Blaziken. Its coat and physique showed that it had been raised well, and its gaze was sharp. He wondered which was stronger, this one or his own Blaziken.

The four were clearly traveling together, much like Ethan's group.

Once Ash heard that they knew the way to the Battle Tower, he forgot the insult. He followed the twins onto the walkway. Ethan and the others exchanged looks and went after him.

The Battle Tower held little interest for Ethan. It offered battles, and he had more pressing business: finding the Deoxys hidden somewhere in LaRousse. But he had only just arrived and did not know where to look, so for now he followed the group.

Then the Ultra Ball at his waist stirred. His Deoxys had sensed another of its kind nearby. The city's technology filled its streets with electromagnetic fields, however, interfering with Deoxys's senses. It could feel the other's presence only faintly and could not tell which direction it came from.

“Easy,” Ethan murmured. “This isn't the time to let you out. Wait a little longer, and you'll meet them soon.”

Deoxys settled down inside its ball.

Ethan stepped onto the walkway at the back of the group. Ahead of him, Ash was already chatting with the boy who had insulted him as if nothing had happened.

That was one of Ash's admirable traits. A small unpleasant exchange passed, and he let it go. Ethan would not have been so forgiving. If someone treated him that way, he would write them off.

Ash looked at Blaziken, then addressed its Trainer. “I'm Ash from Pallet Town. Are you going to the Battle Tower too? How about a battle with me?”

The twins turned around. They spoke one after the other.

“Our brother Rafe is really strong!”

“And so is his Blaziken!”

Rafe turned to Ash and nodded. “Ash, was it? Anyone can enter at the Battle Tower. If you challenge me there, I'll take you on.”

The heavyset boy spoke up. “You can't enter on your own. The Battle Tower holds Double Battles, so you'll need a teammate. Rafe's partner is me. Oh, I forgot to introduce myself. I'm Sid, and my partner Pokémon is Blastoise. It's strong too!”

“Double Battles?” The format caught Ash off guard, but he did not look worried. “I only need a teammate? I've got one. He's the best Trainer in the world!”

“The best Trainer?” Sid asked, puzzled. “Who?”

Ash grinned at the two boys and pushed through the people on the walkway to reach Ethan.

“Ethan!” His eyes shone as he spoke quietly. “Team up with me, okay? If we fight together, nobody will beat us. We could keep winning all day!”

Ethan met Rafe's and Sid's gazes and shook his head at once. “Ask someone else. I have something to take care of.”

“What?” Ash had never expected him to refuse. They were Trainers at a Battle Tower. Wasn't battling the obvious thing to do? Disappointed but undeterred, he turned to Misty.

“Hey, Misty. It's been ages since we battled side by side. Want to team up?”

She refused too. “I'm busy! May and I already decided to go shopping. You can't come somewhere like this and not look around.”

“Come on...” After two refusals, Ash's face fell. He put his last hope in Brock. “Brock, please. Team up with me.”

Brock smiled at his pleading look. “Have you forgotten? I'm not a Trainer anymore. Besides, there are plenty of beautiful older girls in the stands. How could I pass up the chance to sit with them?”

Ash stared. He had asked all three of them, and none would join him. One was a Champion who had won in two regions, another a former Gym Leader, and the third had grown up in a Gym family. How could none of them be interested in a battle?

“You guys!” he complained. He glanced back at Rafe and Sid in embarrassment. They only smiled and did not seem to mind. Ash turned to his friends again. “Just watch. I'll find a teammate without you!”

May and Max were there too, but May was a Coordinator, and as for Max... Ash did not bother asking.

Just then the twins called out, “We're here!”

At the end of the walkway stood a tall, cone-shaped building on a small hill. Its glass exterior glittered in the sunlight and looked as modern as the rest of the city.

The walkway ended at the foot of the hill. They would have to walk the rest of the way.

It was a convenient way to travel, though Ethan did not care for it. He preferred the feel of solid ground under his feet. A short ride was fine; after a long one, his legs felt strange when he stepped off. Misty and May, on the other hand, seemed to like it.

Keep being this lazy, and you'll both turn into little piglets, he thought.

They climbed the winding stairs and entered the Battle Tower.

A spacious lobby greeted them. Elevators stood around its edges, and visitors filled the room. People were everywhere, and not all had come to watch battles.

Despite its name, the Battle Tower was almost a multipurpose center. It had shops, restaurants, a cinema, amusement facilities, and a library. Those were additions to its main attraction: a battle arena large enough for several thousand spectators.

Sid had explained the rules to Ash on the way. The moment they reached the lobby, Ash announced, “I'm going to register!” and vanished into the crowd.

Rafe and his companions went another way. They had booked their place in advance and could head straight for the arena. That left Ethan's group in the lobby.

Misty and May whispered together, then Misty smiled at the others. “There's a while before the matches start. May and I are going to look around and come back for the battles.”

“I want to see the technology museum,” Max said. “I wonder what else they have here.”

Brock scanned the lobby. Seeing no beautiful women, he shook his head. “I'll go to the stands and save us some good seats.”

All four looked at Ethan. He waved them on. “Go ahead. I'll take a look around too.”

“Then we'll meet in the stands,” Brock said.

They separated. Ethan left the lobby and took an elevator to the highest floor. From there he hoped to survey LaRousse and find where Deoxys was hiding.

His memory of the movie was hazy. He recalled a scientist on an Antarctic expedition finding a fallen meteorite and two Deoxys within it. One had a complete body; only the other's core remained. Rayquaza had descended, fought the complete Deoxys, and defeated it.

The scientist had taken the remaining core to LaRousse and spent years trying to revive it. Eventually the Deoxys Rayquaza had defeated came to the city in search of its companion. Its arrival caused an uproar and led to another battle with Rayquaza.

In a way, that would be a chance to catch Rayquaza without visiting Sky Pillar. But Ethan did not want an ordinary Rayquaza. He clearly remembered a Shiny black one at Sky Pillar.

He could not be sure he would meet it. Still, it was a chance to catch a Shiny Pokémon, something he had long dreamed of. Even if the odds were tiny, he had to try.

The elevator reached the top floor. Ethan stepped out and immediately spotted someone both familiar and unfamiliar.

He knew the boy from his appearances in the anime. In this world, however, they had met only once, and that meeting had not gone well.

Green hair, hands in his pockets, that same cool, cocky air. Ethan glanced at him, looked away, and started to walk past.

He had gone only a few steps when the boy turned and called after him. “What are you doing here?”

Ethan stopped and looked back, surprised that the boy had spoken to him at all.

It was Drew, the gifted Coordinator he had met once in Slateport City.

Instead of answering, Ethan asked, “I could ask you the same thing. Why are you here?”

“What's so strange about that?” Drew smiled faintly. “I'm from this city. LaRousse is my home.”

Ethan had not known that Drew came from LaRousse. Drew glanced to either side of him, found no one else there, and hesitated before asking, “Where's the girl who's always with you?”

May came to Ethan's mind. In the anime, she and Drew had begun as rivals. As they met more often, their relationship had softened. He had started paying attention to her, encouraging and helping her, even if he still pointed out her faults in his usual cold tone. Some viewers had thought they made a good pair.

Ethan gave a private, humorless laugh. Had Drew already fallen for her? Was he asking Ethan where May was?

He was about to tease the aloof boy when something felt wrong.

Wait. At the Pokémon Center yesterday, Ash and the others had said May had met Drew several times at Contests since Ethan last saw him. Drew must know May did not travel with Ethan.

So why ask him where she was?

Unless...

Ethan suddenly understood. Drew was not asking about May. He meant Misty!

But why? Had one slap from her during their last meeting left him unable to forget her?
