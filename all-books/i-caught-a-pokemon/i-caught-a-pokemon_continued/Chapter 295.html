Magical Leaf was a Grass-type move, but many Psychic- and Fairy-type Pokémon could learn it. Gardevoir, Cleffa, Flabébé, and Falkner's Togetic were among them.

Grass was strong against both Ground and Rock. If Magical Leaf hit Crystal Onix, it could do four times the usual damage. It was one of Togetic's most straightforward ways to hurt Ethan's Pokémon badly.

Leaves gathered beside Togetic and shot toward Crystal Onix.

Falkner had not counted on how quickly Onix could dig. It had only just landed, but before the leaves crossed half the distance, it bored another hole and vanished underground.

Thud! Thud! Thud!

The leaves skimmed past its tail and lodged in the court. Crystal Onix was nowhere to be seen.

"What?" Falkner's expression changed. "How can it dig that fast?"

Ethan smiled at his surprise. The difficulty Drake's Onix had once caused him was now someone else's problem. Falkner was its first victim. Ethan had put what he learned into practice.

Drake had forced Ethan to use water to flush his Onix out of its tunnel. There was no water source here. Ethan was curious to see how Falkner would handle the same predicament.

Togetic circled the court, looking for a chance to attack. Below it were only three holes and some freshly dug earth. Crystal Onix showed no sign of emerging.

Falkner frowned as he considered his options. Onix's digging speed was far beyond anything he had expected. He could not strike an opponent he could not see, and the feeling of having power he could not use was maddening.

Should he attack again? The result would probably be the same. He would have to lower Togetic farther.

He knew that doing so might leave it no time to avoid an attack from below. But if he wanted to draw Crystal Onix out, he had little choice.

"Togetic, descend a little more! Watch the ground beneath you every moment!"

Falkner had realized that whenever Togetic tried Yawn or Magical Leaf, moves that could disable or seriously injure Onix, it simply stayed underground. From there, Togetic could do nothing to it.

Ethan was the challenger and ought to be seeking a quick win. But with Falkner already down two rounds, he was the one feeling the greater pressure.

Togetic floated lower, a little over two meters above the court. The next instant, Crystal Onix burst from the ground, its gleaming head driving straight toward it.

Falkner's eyelid twitched. "Dodge, then counterattack!"

To a Pokémon Crystal Onix's size, two meters was hardly any height at all. Its head was upon Togetic almost immediately.

Togetic tried to slip aside as it had before, but flying lower had cut its reaction time. Onix struck before it could finish moving.

Bam!

Togetic stood less than a meter tall, smaller than Crystal Onix's head. The blow hit with the force of a truck and sent it crashing to the court like a deflated ball.

It rolled several times, picking up dirt all over its white body.

Ethan seized the opening. "Rock Tomb!"

Crystal Onix raised part of its body and opened its mouth. More than a dozen huge rocks formed in the air and dropped toward Togetic.

Boom! Boom! Boom!

Togetic scrambled up and dodged the first few falling stones by the narrowest margin. It was not especially quick, though. The others struck it, and more rocks came down until its body was buried. The barrage changed the shape of the battlefield.

When Rock Tomb ended, the court fell quiet. Falkner's apprentices leaned forward anxiously, craning their necks toward the pile of stones. They had never imagined seeing their teacher's Pokémon battered like this.

Crystal Onix towered over the court. The heap of boulders before it made its advantage plain.

Ethan knew one hit would not finish Togetic so easily. If it could, it would never have become one of Falkner's strongest partners.

The rocks began to shake. With a crash, a mysterious force sent them flying apart. Togetic reappeared amid the rubble, covered in injuries.

Falkner relaxed a little at the sight of it. "Quick, use Magical Leaf!"

Still trying to attack? Ethan would not give Togetic the chance. "Stone Edge!"

Sharp stones erupted from the ground toward Togetic. Magical Leaves formed overhead at the same time. The attacks collided and canceled each other, but the force of the explosion shattered the rocks into a rain of fragments.

In the confusion, Crystal Onix rushed in before Togetic could take off. Its tail coiled around the smaller Pokémon and held it tight.

Onix squeezed. A chilling creak rose from Togetic's body. It felt as though every bone might break.

"No!" Falkner's face changed. "Use Sweet Kiss!"

Togetic could barely breathe. Gathering its last strength, it opened its mouth to send out a Fairy Wind. Before it could release the move, its world tipped sideways. Ethan had seen what it was trying to do and signaled Crystal Onix to fling it away.

Togetic rolled across the court again, the move trapped in its throat.

"Finish it!" Ethan said. "Iron Tail!"

Before Togetic's despairing eyes, Crystal Onix's huge tail shone brightly. It rose high enough to block the sun, casting a broad shadow over Togetic's face, then came crashing down.
