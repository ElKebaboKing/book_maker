As Ethan watched the enormous tsunami rise like a wall, he remembered how the movie ended. It, too, had brought a wave against Altomare. To save the city and its people, Latios had spent all his strength and given his life to stop it. He had become a new Soul Dew, replacing the corrupted jewel and continuing to protect Altomare.

Now Ethan was here. Surely there was something he could do.

He had to prevent that tragedy from happening again.

Thinking it through, Latios was a Legendary Pokémon. The reason stopping the tsunami had cost him his life must have been the injuries he had suffered in captivity and the power the machine had drained from him.

Even with Latias helping, the two of them had only so much strength left.

What if another Pokémon with extraordinary power over water joined them? Could that change the outcome?

Lugia, the God of the Sea, would have been ideal. Ethan had dealt with several Lugia, and he knew they would gladly help in a crisis like this. Unfortunately, every one of them was too far away.

He did have another Legendary Pokémon skilled with water.

Ethan took a Great Ball from his bag and weighed it lightly in his hand. "It's our turn to save this city," he said to the Pokémon inside.

Not far away, Latias and Latios had noticed the tsunami too.

They exchanged a look, then flew up and circled above the Tower of the Sun. From the tower, they had a clearer view of the wave's size and destructive force.

Latios floated there, breathing hard. He and Latias could have flown away and escaped it. But they had lived in Altomare so long, and perhaps it was simply their nature: Latios would not watch the wave destroy the city.

He seemed to reach a decision and was about to fly toward it when a man's voice came from behind him.

"Latios, wait! I have an idea we can try!"

Latios turned quickly. Somehow a man had appeared at the top of the Tower of the Sun.

He recognized the young man Latias had brought into the Secret Garden earlier, though he did not know his name. Dragonite hovered beside him; it must have carried him up there.

Latios had sensed something unusual about Ethan when they first met that day. It was not his manner. He had felt two powerful presences at the man's waist.

They were not others of his own kind, but he believed they belonged to other Legendary Pokémon.

Latios called weakly to Ethan. This time, Ethan understood the gist without Gengar translating.

He opened the Great Ball. In a flash of blue light, Suicune appeared beside him.

Resting a hand on Suicune's long crest, Ethan turned to Latios. "This wave threatens all of us. You don't have to face it alone. My Pokémon will help you and Latias."

Latios and Latias regarded Suicune. It was noble-looking and calm, and both could feel the abundant power within it.

Ethan pointed toward the sea. "The wave is coming mainly from one direction. Were you planning to fly above it and break its structure, so the section headed straight for the city would collapse?"

Latios gave a small nod. It was the only solution he could think of.

"It can work," Ethan said. "I had the same idea. But in your condition, it may cost you dearly. Let Suicune help."

He waved Dragonite over and climbed back onto its back.

"We can settle the details on the way. There's no time to spare. Let's get ahead of the tsunami while it's still far from the city."

Latios and Latias followed Dragonite without objection. Suicune leaped down from the tower, touched lightly against one rooftop after another, and bounded toward the coast.

At the seafront railing by the square, Ash and the others looked up as Dragonite carried Ethan overhead with Latias and Latios. They placed their hopes in him.

Lorenzo and Bianca saw Suicune leap past them and only then realized that Ethan had caught a Pokémon so powerful.

Erika alone watched him with worry. The danger ahead was immense. She did not know how he meant to help the city survive it.

The night sea wind was cold, especially with Ethan still soaked from the earlier rain and seawater.

The exposed seabed flashed by below. After only a dozen or so seconds, they were already far from Altomare.

Ahead of them, the towering wave advanced.

From this close, it was terrifying. Ethan looked up. The wall of water rose above him until it seemed to fill the sky.

"Here!" he shouted to Latios and Latias. "Use all your strength. Suicune will work with you!"

The roar of the wave forced him to yell for them to hear.

Suicune had reached the ground below them. Latias and Latios glanced down at it, nodded, and drew on all the power they had left.

Brilliant white light burst from their bodies. Together they shone like a small sun as they flew straight at the enormous wave.

Suicune acted at the same moment.

Its command of water was unparalleled. That was the reason Ethan had trusted it with this task.

After gathering its strength, Suicune opened its mouth and sent a thick beam of energy toward the point Latias and Latios were approaching.

One force came from the air, the other from the ground. They struck the same spot on the wave almost simultaneously.

A thunderous impact followed. The water erupted as though a bomb had gone off, tearing a huge gap in the wave.
