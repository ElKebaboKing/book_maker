Near the reefs along the shore, Oliver and Lugia happily played in the sea. The boy and Pokémon splashed water at each other, looking carefree and delighted.

Ethan and Ash sat on a large rock beside them, leisurely watching the pair.

Everything appeared perfectly normal and peaceful, like a simple diversion after lunch.

Strangely, however, no one else was in sight!

Erika, Misty, Brock, and Richie had all seemingly vanished without a trace!

At the same time, a massive submarine lurked silently beneath the water ahead.

Inside its command room, the captain watched the large monitor displaying images transmitted by the submarine's cameras. His brow instinctively furrowed.

One Team Rocket member asked impatiently, "Captain, we've already waited more than ten minutes. Shouldn't we act?"

The captain considered the question but did not issue an order. Instead, he hesitated. "According to our intelligence, this group has seven people. Why are only three left now? Where are the others?"

"We haven't found any trace of anyone else nearby." The Team Rocket member shook his head and ventured, "Could they have already left? This is only a deserted island with nowhere else to go. I think the others probably returned to the city."

They left...?

The captain rubbed his chin, apparently accepting the suggestion. He immediately relaxed somewhat. "If the group truly split up, their strength has undoubtedly been greatly reduced. That man Ethan Vale will still be difficult to handle, but this is certainly a rare opportunity to capture Lugia!"

He glanced at the man and gave his order. "There's no time to waste. Surface immediately and deploy the operations team!"

...

On the rock, Ash looked over the calm surroundings. With no sign that Team Rocket intended to attack, he turned toward Ethan.

"Ethan, will Team Rocket really come?"

"Be patient." Ethan leaned back and lay directly upon the rock. Watching the white clouds overhead, he lazily continued, "This is just like fishing. I'm sure they're watching us from somewhere right now. We've placed the delicious bait directly in front of them, so there's no need to worry. They'll bite."

As though to prove Ethan's words, before Ash could respond, the sea in front of them suddenly churned. A black submarine broke through the surface and rose from the water!

Its arrival was so abrupt that the enormous vessel appeared directly before Oliver and Lugia. The violently surging water instantly left the boy and Pokémon tossing helplessly in the waves!

Ash stood and followed the strategy they had established earlier, shouting at the submarine, "Who's there!?"

The moment he finished, a man's and a woman's voices came from inside.

"Since you asked us so sincerely!"

"Normally, we wouldn't answer!"

"But we'll make a special exception and tell you!"

"To prevent the destruction of the Earth!"

"To protect the peace of the Earth!"

"To carry out the evils of love and honesty!"

"Playful and mischievous villains!"

"Cassidy!"

"Butch!"

"We are Team Rocket, traveling through the universe!"

"A shocking pink, rosy tomorrow awaits us!"

When Ethan first heard the voices, he thought Jessie and James had arrived. But both the lines and the voices themselves were clearly wrong.

Sure enough, part of the submarine opened and an elevator slowly rose, revealing a man and woman who seemed both familiar and unfamiliar to Ethan.

They were familiar because Ethan had watched the animated series and recognized them at once.

They were unfamiliar because this was his first encounter with either of them since arriving in this world!

Both wore black Team Rocket uniforms. The woman had golden hair arranged in two exaggerated pigtails. Although she was fairly pretty, her shrewd expression suggested someone highly skilled at scheming.

The man had short, pale green hair parted down the middle. He looked both handsome and alert.

Cassidy and Butch!

The Team Rocket trio's greatest rivals within the organization had finally appeared!

These two looked somewhat younger than Jessie and James, and their performance within Team Rocket was stronger. The greatest difference was that Cassidy and Butch possessed excellent equipment and enormous operational budgets. They never needed to worry about money!

Of course, that did not necessarily mean Jessie and James were inferior. They had simply pursued Ash for far too long. Whenever they came close to succeeding, Ash ruined their plans, dramatically lowering their success rate, destroying their prestige, and steadily reducing their status within Team Rocket.

When Ash recognized the pair, he apparently worried that Ethan might not know who they were. "Ethan, those two are also from Team Rocket. I encountered them once before. They're just as persistent as Jessie and James!"

"Understood." Ethan nodded. "Stay alert and make sure you protect Silver!"

"Don't compare us with those two useless fools!" Cassidy looked down at them from above and pointed toward Silver with complete confidence. "That Lugia will belong to us today! Leave now if you know what's good for you, or don't blame us for showing no mercy!"

Butch also turned his gaze toward Ethan and snorted coldly. "I hear you've caused Team Rocket a great deal of trouble. Well, your luck runs out today!"

The pair were clearly brimming with confidence. For one thing, they had never dealt with Ethan and knew nothing of his true abilities. Even though they had heard his name, they assumed the stories were exaggerated.

Without witnessing it themselves, how could they believe that a single person had repeatedly foiled Team Rocket's operations?

Moreover, as one of Team Rocket's finest pairs, they had considerable faith in their own strength. Most of all, they had come fully prepared!

The next moment, more than a dozen Team Rocket members poured out behind them in a dark mass. They also glared down at Ethan with intimidating expressions. At the same time, engines roared on both sides of the submarine as more than a dozen speedboats approached from each direction, carrying dozens more Team Rocket members!

In an instant, Team Rocket's force had grown to seventy or eighty people!

They formed a crescent around the entire beach, leaving Ethan and the others no possible route of escape!
