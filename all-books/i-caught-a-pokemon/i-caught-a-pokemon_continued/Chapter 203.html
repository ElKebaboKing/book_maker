Maren blinked. "You're...?"

The woman removed her bird mask. Ethan could see now that she had short hair and was about Maren's age, perhaps twenty-four or twenty-five.

"It's me, Maren! It's been so long!"

"Oh! Carol!" Maren's eyes lit up. "We haven't seen each other since we graduated from the Mandolin branch school. It must be five years now!"

"It has," Carol said with a smile. "I never expected we'd meet again like this."

Happy as she was to see her friend, Maren looked over Carol's odd costume. "What's with the outfit?"

"The festival! Our annual festival starts today. It's a big occasion for everyone on Shamouti Island."

"Oh, is it that time already?" Maren made the motion of holding a flute to her lips. "Are you dressing up as the festival maiden and playing the flute again, like you used to?"

The ease of their conversation showed that the two women knew each other well. Ethan, meanwhile, had never heard of a Mandolin branch school. Perhaps it was a college of some sort, unrelated to Pokémon.

"I retired from that ages ago," Carol said. "I'm not a child anymore. My younger sister has taken over as the festival maiden."

She looked around, ready to beckon her sister over, but could not see her anywhere. "Huh? Where has she gone?"

At that moment, a girl appeared on top of the seawall.

"I don't want to be any festival maiden!" she called down, hands on her hips. "You push everything onto me. Being your little sister is such a pain!"

She looked a year or two older than Ash and Misty. A beret sat at an angle on her head, sunglasses covered her eyes, and she wore jeans, a pink top, and an orange shoulder bag. Her clothes were decidedly fashionable.

Carol was used to her sister's tone. She did not look offended in the least.

"Oh, there you are. Why are you dressed like that? Where's your festival costume?"

The girl waved the question away. "I am not putting on those tacky clothes yet. The festival doesn't start until evening. What's the rush?"

Carol sighed, then turned to the visitors. "As you can see, this is my younger sister, Melody. She's a bit rebellious. Sorry about that."

"We all go through that age," someone replied.

Carol turned back to Maren. "And who are your friends?"

"Oh, I forgot to introduce everyone. I'm on my way to see my parents on Hamlin Island, and I'm giving these passengers a ride along the way." Maren gestured toward Ethan and Ash. "These two are Trainers. The other three are their traveling companions."

"Pokémon Trainers?!"

The costumed islanders broke into cheers at once. Even Melody turned to look from the seawall. Ash and the others were baffled, and Maren seemed just as confused.

A short elderly man stepped out of the crowd and removed his mask. He spoke in a deep voice.

"Legend says that when the world faces destruction, the God of the Sea will appear. Together with an exceptional commander, it will calm the gods' wrath. That commander is a Pokémon Trainer."

He came closer and regarded them gravely. "And you are the Trainers chosen by the prophecy!"

Perhaps he noticed how tense Ash looked, or realized how solemn he sounded. His manner changed, and he smiled. "Relax. We say this every year. It's an island tradition!"

Ash breathed a sigh of relief. For a moment, he had thought someone was declaring him a chosen savior.

Melody had come down from the seawall. She stepped through the crowd and studied Ethan and Ash from head to toe.

"You're both Trainers?" She put a finger near her lips. "The handsome one certainly looks the part, but this little kid... Oh, whatever. It's only a tradition. Come on, a kiss for each of you!"

Melody took off her sunglasses. Before anyone quite understood what she meant, she stood on tiptoe and kissed Ethan on the cheek. Then she turned and kissed Ash as well.

Sabrina frowned. She had heard that it was customary, though, and did nothing. Misty reacted like an angry cat. She stepped in front of Ethan and demanded, "What was that for?!"

"Oh, why such a big reaction?" Melody gave her a teasing look. "Are you his girlfriend?"

"G-girlfriend?!" Misty went bright red and could not find an answer. Melody laughed at her expression.

"No, wait. That pretty older woman behind you must be his girlfriend. You must be his little sister."

Misty did not like that either, but she could only purse her lips, unable to think of a reply.

"Oh? Not his sister?" Melody found Misty increasingly fun to tease. She widened her eyes in mock surprise. "Then do you have a secret crush on him?"

Misty's face reddened further, as though Melody had exposed exactly what she was thinking. Embarrassed before the whole crowd, she opened her mouth but said nothing. She had to admit defeat and take a step back.

The storm clouds were breaking apart. The sun's last light fell through the gaps onto the island.

The elderly man called, "Let the festival begin!" The islanders beat hand drums, shook bells, and played unfamiliar instruments as they welcomed Ethan and the others into the town beyond the seawall.

Shamouti Island was one of the oldest islands in the Orange Islands and lay near the archipelago's geographical center. Its town was larger and more populous than any Ethan had seen on the other islands.

Modern buildings stood alongside traditional structures and totem poles. Men and women in customary dress filled the streets. Just as Carol had said, decorations and lights were everywhere. Shamouti Town was alive with celebration.
