Professor Oak's answer contained several obvious contradictions and was not particularly convincing.

Anyone who heard it would wonder why the Unown responsible for the incident would create Entei, then create the other two Legendary beasts as well.

Were Ash and his friends so powerful that the Unown needed to create two more beasts to oppose them?

But that made no sense either!

If the Unown could not defeat Ash's group, why would they then create the three Legendary birds to help Ash fight Molly?

It was impossible to understand.

Without knowing what was happening on the battlefield, they could only conclude that all six Legendary Pokémon had been created by the Unown and had begun fighting among themselves for some unknown reason.

After all, Entei provided a clear precedent. Under normal circumstances, Kanto's three Legendary birds would never appear in Johto without cause, much less suddenly battle the three Legendary beasts.

That had to be the answer.

Something they did not know about must have occurred.

Rumble!

Thunder continued to sound, and the distant battle showed no sign of ending. The dark clouds spread steadily until they began to cover the entire Pokémon Center.

If the battle continued much longer, even the people gathered there might be caught in its effects.

It was now around six in the evening. Worried, Erika took out her phone and tried to contact Ethan. Perhaps the weather temporarily disrupted the signal, because several attempts failed to reach him.

What was happening over there?

Erika frowned, deeply concerned about Ethan and the others.

Both the three Legendary beasts and the three Legendary birds had appeared. Who could remain unharmed amid the effects of a battle between Pokémon like those?

After a moment of hesitation, Erika realized that waiting here would accomplish nothing. While everyone's attention remained fixed on the battle, she quietly slipped away into the night.

There was no longer any need for her to distract Professor Oak, so she had no reason to remain. Rather than wait helplessly, she would go directly to the crystal tower and join Ethan.

After Erika left, the long-awaited rescue team finally arrived at Greenfield's Pokémon Center.

The team consisted of a helicopter along with several excavators, bulldozers, and trucks. After a brief period of preparation, they advanced toward the crystal tower from the air and across the ground.

With engines roaring, the excavators reached the outer edge of the crystal growth and began digging forward.

Progress was painfully slow. The crystal covered an enormous area, and even at full power the machines could advance only one meter at a time. At this rate, they might not reach the base of the tower before the following afternoon.

The helicopter was much faster by comparison. But with Pokémon battling in the sky amid such extreme weather, the pilot's repeated attempts to approach all failed. Each time the helicopter neared the edge of the clouds, violent snow, ice, and lightning forced it back to the Pokémon Center.

It appeared the helicopter could not begin its rescue until the battle ended.

The rescue effort had reached an impasse. Professor Oak happened to glance around and noticed that someone was missing.

"Huh? Where is Erika?"

Lisa stood nearby with her arms folded, leaning against a pillar. She answered calmly, "She slipped away a moment ago. She probably went to the crystal tower to help Ash and the others."

"What!? She went to the crystal tower!?"

Officer Jenny's eyes widened. In her agitation, her voice rose several notes.

"If you saw her leave, why didn't you stop her!? It will be completely dark soon, and the weather is incredibly dangerous. Lightning could strike at any moment. Going there now is far too reckless! What if something happens to her!?"

She craned her neck and searched the surroundings, hoping to call Erika back. But the night was already so dark that there was no sign of her.

Lisa merely shrugged, entirely unconcerned.

"So what if it's dangerous? We're Trainers. How could a little difficulty like this stop us? Besides, isn't she that man's companion? At a time like this, shouldn't she be at his side?"

...

Outside the crystal tower, Ethan had been watching the battlefield above with intense concentration when the entire structure suddenly shook.

Before he could react, the top of the crystal tower exploded. Huge sections of the crystal walls broke away and exposed the space within.

It was Molly's bedroom.

Illuminated by the room's lights, Entei could be seen battling a Charizard.

One glance was enough for Ethan to recognize Ash's Charizard.

Only then did he remember that near the end of the movie, Ash's Charizard had seen its Trainer in danger on television at Charicific Valley. It had hurried to the rescue and fought the final battle against Entei.

Ethan had been so focused on Zapdos's battle that he had failed to notice when Charizard arrived or where it had entered the crystal tower.

The battle inside was nearing its end, and the two sides appeared evenly matched. But Molly's bedroom was undergoing a sudden change. Crystal spikes erupted continuously, destroying everything around them.

Those spikes had even caused the collapse that exposed the room.

As parts of the crystal tower fell away, Ash and the others could finally breathe fresh air and see what was happening outside.

Three Legendary birds danced through the snow-filled sky.

Upon the jagged crystal below, three Legendary beasts roared at the heavens.

Illuminated by lightning, the six Legendary Pokémon appeared extraordinarily fierce.

Even as Ash and the others dodged the spikes, they stared in stunned disbelief at the battle below.

They had wondered what kind of Pokémon Ethan might be fighting, but none had imagined that six Legendary Pokémon were outside.

The sight was magnificent.

Brock's narrow eyes flew wide open as he stared in disbelief.

"Zapdos? Articuno? Moltres? Entei? Suicune? Raikou? What's going on!? Why are there so many Legendary Pokémon out there!?"

Unlike Brock, who knew nothing about Ethan's secret, Misty quickly recovered and searched the ground below. Sure enough, she spotted Ethan nearby.

There was no need to wonder. Those had to be Ethan's three Legendary birds. Only he could capture so many Legendary Pokémon!

But where had the three Legendary beasts come from?

Why were they battling Ethan?
