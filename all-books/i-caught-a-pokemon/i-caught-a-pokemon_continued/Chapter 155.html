Damian glanced around at the other competitors before turning back to Ethan.

"Now that we've both made it this far, I actually hope I don't have to face you too soon. You beat me once already, after all."

"Oh? Why is that?" Ethan asked, raising an eyebrow.

Damian laughed. "I made a point of watching your matches. You're even stronger than when we first met, and it looks like you're still holding something back. I kept asking myself whether I could beat an opponent like you. But that just makes me want the match even more. I want to face you in the final!"

"I see." Ethan nodded. "Then we'd both better keep winning. It would be a shame if one of us got knocked out before we made it there."

Around them, competitors began taking fishing rods and choosing places beside the pool. One after another, they cast their hooks into the water.

Ethan took the rod Chloe handed him and imitated the others as best he could.

The Magikarp wandered aimlessly through the pool, looking as vacant and sluggish as ever. According to the staff, the rods supplied by the League carried special bait that would attract them quickly.

Ethan, however, had never fished before. Even with such simple equipment, he found himself fumbling.

Fortunately, a Water-type expert happened to be free. With Misty's guidance, he soon got the hang of it and learned what to watch for.

To everyone's surprise, several Magikarp swam right past his hook without showing the slightest interest in the bait. He waited, but none of them bit.

Other trainers were having no such trouble. They caught Magikarp one after another, and their places in the draw were soon settled.

Ash was especially lucky. The moment he cast his line, a Magikarp took the hook.

He pulled it up and hugged it, laughing in delight. But when the staff member announced its number, his smile froze.

Pointing toward the big screen, she said gently, "Ash, you've drawn A3. Your opponent is the other A3 entrant—Richie."

"What!?"

Ash and Richie stared at each other in shock. They had only just become friends. Now one of them would have to knock the other out of the tournament.

Neither knew what to say. The mood around them turned heavy.

Off to one side, Ethan shook his head. Fate had a cruel sense of timing.

Still, he had his own problem. More and more Magikarp were being caught. Almost everyone else had an opponent, yet his hook remained empty.

"This is strange." Chloe crouched beside him with her cheeks resting in her hands and looked into the pool. "The bait is fine, you're holding the rod steady, and the line isn't moving much. Why won't any of them bite?"

"Who knows?" Ethan pursed his lips. "Everyone else has a matchup already. I'm about ready to jump in and grab a Magikarp with my bare hands."

Misty laughed, then bent down for a closer look. After a moment, she seemed to notice something.

"I bet you've got Gyarados's scent on you, Ethan. Magikarp may look clueless, and Gyarados may be their evolved form, but they're still terrified of it!"

"Really?" Her explanation sounded convincing enough to make Ethan raise his eyebrows. Then he scratched his head. "What am I supposed to do? Wait until everyone else has finished and take the last one?"

At that, Haunter poked half its head out of Ethan's shadow and rolled its eyes. It raised a finger and gave it a little wag.

An invisible force reached into the pool. A Magikarp was dragged toward the hook, its mouth pried open, and the hook forced into place. Haunter slipped back into the shadow without anyone noticing.

"Oh! I finally got a bite!"

Even Ethan had missed what happened at his feet. He quickly pulled on the line and hauled the Magikarp from the water.

The staff member checked its marking and entered the number into the computer.

"Ethan, your number is A4. Your opponent will be the other A4 entrant, Jimmy."

Jimmy?

Ethan turned toward the screen. It showed a young man with curly green hair. Ethan recognized neither his face nor his name. Still, anyone who had reached the Top 16 had to be capable.

With the draw complete, Ethan returned the fishing rod and looked over the bracket again.

Damian and Logan were both in Group B. He would not meet either of them before the final.

The harder part was that he and Ash were both in Group A. Even if Ash beat Richie, the bracket would put him against Ethan next. Having to battle one friend after another would be hard on Ash.

After the draw, Ash left alone without even saying goodbye to Richie. He looked lost in thought.

Worried about their traveling companion, Misty and Brock said goodbye to Ethan and hurried after him.

Ethan shook his head, then looked at Chloe.

"The draw's done. Let's go."

As the third and fourth matches in Group A, Ash's and Ethan's battles were scheduled for the following afternoon. Ethan had plenty of time to look into Jimmy's past matches and see whether he could find a weakness.

After lunch, he and Chloe returned to their lodging. Dratini was playing with Jolteon and Chloe's Yamper in the room.

When Ethan came in, Dratini hurried over with a cheerful cry.

After a long period of patient reassurance, most of Dratini's behavior problems had been resolved. It was still shy around strangers, but once the Indigo League was over, Ethan could take it traveling and battling without much concern.

Before he could even get a drink of water, a message arrived from a girl far away in Kalos.

From their conversation, Ethan learned that Alexa's interview had finally been published there the day before.

Kalos Trainer Crosses the Ocean to Challenge the Indigo League!

The story caused a small burst of discussion. People in Kalos did not follow the Indigo League closely enough for it to become major news, but it spread for a while through the circles that cared about the event.

For one girl who had been following Ethan all along, the report and its clear, handsome photograph were more than enough to make her leap with excitement.
