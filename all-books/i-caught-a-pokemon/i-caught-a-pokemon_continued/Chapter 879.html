“Oh, you're awake.” Gengar greeted Ethan with a grin, then glanced down. “You know, that isn't very decent. I'm your Pokémon, so you don't have to worry too much about appearances around me, but couldn't you put on some pants? Even a cloth would do.”

The reminder brought Ethan fully back to himself. His clothes lay some distance away, untouched by the mess around the bedding. Relieved, he hurried to dress, putting aside the fact that his skin still felt dirty.

The clothes were clean enough, though they had picked up a peculiar smell. The floor was worse. It stuck to his feet with every step, and each movement left his body aching. Then his stomach growled.

His surroundings and his condition were both miserable. The rich dinner he had eaten must have been used up during the night. No wonder he was starving.

Before washing and finding food, though, he needed to understand what had happened. Even if he had behaved recklessly after drinking, how had it gone on so long? And why had Miki, Kuni, and Zuki all come to him? Something was wrong.

Gengar moved closer, looking him up and down with a series of amused clicks of its tongue.

“Humans really can't handle drugs well. Such a small amount, and you were all overwhelmed.”

“Drugs? What drugs?” Ethan stared at it. Gengar clearly knew something. “And where were you last night? Why are you only back now?”

“Wandering around here,” Gengar said with a shrug. “Lucky for you I was. Otherwise, you might really have suffered.”

It told him what it had seen: Zuki putting an aphrodisiac in his sake, and Gengar adding the rest to her sisters' bottles.

Ethan stood speechless. Zuki had tried to drug him, and Gengar had responded by drugging the others too? What sort of turn had events taken?

It took him a while to absorb it. So that was why last night had happened. The person who had made it worse was his own Pokémon.

He rubbed his aching head. “You found out and didn't warn me or stop her. Instead, you helped her along?”

“Of course.” Gengar grinned. “She was determined to have you. I couldn't bear to spoil her plans. Besides, I didn't see how it could hurt you. I wanted to see what would happen. I didn't expect the eldest sister to get involved too, but still—three of them. Not bad!”

Not bad? My back feels as if it's breaking.

Ethan sighed. “Did you think my life wasn't complicated enough?”

Gengar looked genuinely puzzled. “Why is this a complication? You already have plenty of women. You seem to like being close to them. I thought you'd be happy with more.”

That left Ethan speechless. Was that truly how Gengar saw him?

Once he understood the cause, guilt began to settle over him. He felt he had taken advantage of all three sisters, however little control any of them had had. His gaze dropped to three faint spots of blood on the floor.

What a mess.

But why had Zuki tried to drug him in the first place? Had she meant to use the incident against him?

He questioned Gengar again. It recounted the conversation it had overheard between Zuki and Kuni. Only then did Ethan learn that Zuki liked him.

She likes me?

He thought back over her behavior. How was he supposed to have guessed? Who tried to attract someone by constantly picking fights with him?

Then he remembered Zuki's proud, guarded temperament. In that light, he could understand it. Understanding did not tell him what to do now.

Thinking only made him hungrier. His skin still felt sticky. Since he had no solution, he left the room, showered, and changed into clean clothes. Then he headed for the dining room.

Whatever awaited him there, he needed to eat first.

Halfway along the covered walkway, he saw May practicing with Naoko and Sayo in the distance. May was too focused to notice him. The younger sisters did, and their faces instantly turned red. They turned their backs without even greeting him.

Ethan stared. Why were they embarrassed? He had done nothing with them.

Unless they know what happened last night?

He was right, though he did not know it. He shook his head and kept going.

After a few steps, he met Kuni coming the other way. Both stopped. The awkwardness between them was unmistakable.

Kuni looked more unsettled than Ethan. Facing him again made it difficult for her to stay calm, and her legs felt weak. She drew a breath and forced herself to speak.

“You're…awake?” She tried to steady her voice, but it trembled.

Ethan scratched his head, unsure what to say. He could hardly ask how her body felt.

At least she had not met him with anger or accusations. Perhaps there was still a way to talk this through.

Before he could respond, Kuni said, “I was going to wake you. We've prepared lunch in the dining room. Please come with me.”

She turned and led the way. Ethan did not yet know what the sisters intended, but he had to face them honestly.

A dozen steps brought them to the dining room. Miki and Zuki were already inside. Both looked up when Ethan appeared, then lowered their eyes shyly.

The table held a generous lunch, every bit as fine as the previous night's dinner. Kuni joined her sisters on one side. The atmosphere was heavy. Ethan sat opposite them without hesitation.

Perhaps it was his imagination, but even after a night of airing and a thorough cleaning, he thought he could still catch a faint trace of yesterday's scent.

“Mr. Vale…” Miki spoke first. “Do you remember what happened last night?”

Ethan nodded.

Miki and Kuni exchanged rueful looks. Zuki, by contrast, seemed to grow calmer.

Miki took a breath. “You must have questions. Let me explain.”

She recounted everything she knew. From the sisters' point of view, there was no Gengar and no secret addition to their bottles. They believed the fragrance from Ethan's sake had affected them and led to what followed.

Ethan could hear their distress. He felt sorry for them. After a moment's hesitation, though, he decided not to reveal what Gengar had done. He thought it better that they not know. He acted as though this was the first time he had heard the story.

“I see. No wonder last night…”

Color rose in Miki's and Kuni's faces.

“Let's treat last night as if it never happened,” Miki interrupted, plainly unwilling to discuss its details. Then she met his eyes. “But I have one request concerning Zuki.”

Here it comes.

Ethan had known their calm invitation and elaborate meal were leading somewhere. Now they had reached the point.

“You understand why Zuki did this,” Miki said. “She came up with an extreme plan because she wanted you to accept her.”

Ethan looked at Zuki. She met his gaze steadily.

“I'm not worth doing all this for,” he said. “You must know I already have someone.”

“Erika,” Zuki said, with a look that suggested she had expected as much.

“Well…” Ethan scratched his head. “Not only Erika. There are others.”

“What?” All three sisters stared at him.

“You have other women besides Erika?” Kuni exclaimed, her voice suddenly much louder.

“You don't need to shout,” Ethan said with an embarrassed smile. “Relationships…sometimes they take unexpected turns.”

Zuki glared. “Does Erika know?”

He nodded. “She came into my life after someone else.”

The three women were astonished. They had met Erika and knew her as a traditional, reserved woman. Had she become involved with Ethan while knowing he was already with someone? Who was this other woman?

“You're quite the flirt,” Zuki said coldly.

Ethan gave a bitter smile. “That's why I said I'm not worth what you did.”

“What's the use of saying that now?” She glared again. “It's too late. Or do you plan to have your fun and walk away?”

Her words stung. He had enjoyed parts of the night, but it still felt unreal, clouded by the drug. A reckless thought crossed his mind, and he immediately kept it to himself.

Miki and Kuni exchanged uneasy glances. The news of Ethan's other relationships had upset what they had expected from this conversation.

Zuki was angry, but her decision did not waver. “You clearly aren't going to leave them. Fine. I still want to be with you.”

“Ahem!” Ethan coughed, startled by her directness. She knew what she wanted, said it, and acted on it. That was Zuki. She had hardly given him room to refuse.

He tried anyway. “This could affect your reputation and your future happiness. Shouldn't you think it over more care—”

Zuki narrowed her eyes, cutting him off. “Do you object?”

Her force of will was unmistakable. Ethan yielded. “…All right. I don't.”

Zuki nodded in satisfaction and smiled. “Then congratulations. You have another woman in your life, and a home in Johto too.”

Miki and Kuni had not expected her to embrace a relationship with Ethan despite knowing he had other women. Yet that was Zuki's nature: once she chose a course, she threw herself into it.

If they were in her position, perhaps they would have accepted it too. What else could they do? Pretend the night had never happened?

Zuki looked radiant, and Ethan wore a helpless smile. Watching them, Miki and Kuni felt a pang of sadness. Zuki had gained what she wanted. What had the two of them gained? They had lost something precious.

Even so, they loved their sister. If Zuki could be happy, that was a comfort.

Miki took several bottles of sake from the side and set them before the group.

“This is happy news, and it settles something we've all worried about. We should celebrate with a few drinks.” She saw Ethan eyeing the bottles nervously and laughed. “Don't worry. No one has put anything in the sake this time.”
