Following the map, Ethan and Sabrina finally crossed the vast, dense forest after two days of travel.

They had camped outdoors, made their way through difficult terrain, and defeated plenty of wild Pokémon that had harassed them along the way. The journey had left them tired in body and mind. At least their destination was close now. Once they crossed a few more mountains, they would reach Blackthorn City.

Ethan could have ridden Charizard over the forest with ease, but he had chosen to walk.

To him, the pleasure of traveling lay in overcoming difficulties alongside his Pokémon, taking in the scenery, and strengthening their bond along the way.

If all he wanted was speed and convenience, how would his journey be any different from Gary's hurried travels?

After leaving the forest, the two continued east. They had not gone far when Ethan stopped again.

"What's wrong?" Sabrina asked softly.

Ethan tipped his chin forward. Sabrina followed his gaze and saw that the road split across the open ground ahead. One branch went around the mountains; the other led into them.

Ethan studied the map for a while. "Both roads take us to the same place, but they're very different. The first goes around the mountains. It's longer, but easier to walk. The second passes through a mountain cave called the Ice Path. It's covered in ice and snow and will be bitterly cold, but it'll save us a lot of time and take us straight to Blackthorn City."

Sabrina considered it, then pointed to the road on the right. "Let's take the shortcut. The sooner we reach Blackthorn City, the sooner I can have a hot shower."

Two days without a bath had been hard on her.

"We might not have to wait until Blackthorn for that." Ethan pointed to the Ice Path on the map. "It looks like there's a Pokémon Center inside. We could stay there tonight!"

Sabrina blinked. "There's a Pokémon Center inside a cave?"

"That's right. Apparently, enough people use the cave to reach Blackthorn that the League built a Center there for their safety, so travelers can stop and rest along the way."

With their route decided, they headed into the mountains.

At first, the slopes were still lush and green. As they drew closer to the Ice Path, though, the temperature dropped noticeably, and the trees and other plants gradually withered.

By the time Ethan stood at the cave entrance, a gust of cold air from within made him shiver. Unlike the surrounding mountainside, the entrance was already coated in solid ice.

"It's freezing. We'd better change before we catch a cold."

Sabrina agreed. They took their thick clothes out of their packs. By now, they were so comfortable with each other that neither thought much of changing in front of the other. They simply had their Pokémon stand guard nearby while they put on their winter clothes.

The extra layers warmed them at once. Inside the cave, however, the wind still cut through them. Ethan had to call out Charizard, whose ever-burning tail finally kept the cold at bay.

Although the cave's official name was the Ice Path, local people preferred to call it the Ice Cave.

Like the other frozen caverns Ethan had visited, it had smooth, glittering walls of ice all around. Ice-type Pokémon wandered there too, but at the sight of intruders they fled deeper into the cave.

Creak...

The ice beneath their feet made soft sounds as they stepped on it. Farther in, the ice grew thicker and thicker.

Seeing how carefully Sabrina was walking, Ethan hurried to take her hand. "Watch out. It's slippery here. Don't fall."

He also called out Gengar to help if anything went wrong. By flashlight and firelight, they pressed farther into the cave.

There was only one main passage. Small openings branched off to either side now and then, but they led into tunnels barely half a person's height, too small for a human to enter. Even on his first visit, Ethan had no reason to worry about getting lost. He could also see footprints left by earlier travelers.

Following those prints, they walked for more than two hours before they came beneath a vast dome of ice.

Sunlight filtered down through the dome overhead and reflected softly through the cavern, lighting the space. On the ice ahead stood a three-story building.

Even seeing it with her own eyes, Sabrina was astonished. "I can't believe there's really a Pokémon Center in a place like this!"

What surprised her most was the light shining from its windows. The Center was clearly still open.

"Come on. We'll stay here tonight."

Ethan recalled Charizard, took Sabrina's hand, and walked into the Pokémon Center. The automatic doors slid apart, and warm air washed over them. Ethan felt as though the blood that had frozen in his veins had finally begun to flow again.

"Phew! Hello!"

At the sound of their arrival, a Jynx wearing a nurse's uniform came through a side door.

Huh? They have Jynx helping here instead of Chansey? As far as Ethan knew, the Pokémon assisting at Johto's Pokémon Centers had always been Chansey. He had not expected to find a Jynx here. And he had to admit, the sight of one in a nurse's uniform was rather hard on the eyes.

"Lutu-dulu!"

Jynx said something to them. Unable to understand Pokémon speech, Ethan turned to his Gengar.

"What did it say?"

Gengar, serving as translator, shrugged. "Nothing much. It's just saying hello."

"I see..." Ethan looked around but saw no sign of Nurse Joy. "Hello, Jynx. Is Nurse Joy here?"

No sooner had he asked than Nurse Joy herself came out from behind the door.

"Good afternoon, you two."

Ethan nodded to her. "Hello, Nurse Joy. We'd like to stay the night."

"Of course. We have plenty of rooms." Joy smiled and gestured them inside. "You must have had a tiring journey. Come have something to drink and warm yourselves up."

They followed Nurse Joy into the dining room and found that they were not the only guests. A man and a woman were sitting at a table eating dinner. Hearing Ethan and Sabrina's footsteps, both turned their heads.
