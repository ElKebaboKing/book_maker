Ethan's serious tone startled Miki, Kuni, and Zuki. They looked at the three Great Balls on the table.

“Why have you brought out Poké Balls all of a sudden?” Zuki asked.

Ethan glanced at Naoko and Sayo and hesitated.

The five were sisters who lived together. Now that he had defined his relationship with the three older women, the younger two would learn about it sooner or later. Some things could not be hidden in this house. He decided it was better to speak openly from the beginning.

He looked back at Miki, Kuni, and Zuki.

“Since we're together now, there are things I should arrange. I want to give you some peace of mind, so you won't be left worrying when I travel again.”

The three older sisters barely reacted. Naoko and Sayo, however, felt a jolt. They had suspected the truth, but hearing it from Ethan still startled them. All three of their sisters were with him.

One man with three partners was not a relationship the law would recognize. Was this really going to work?

Neither younger sister objected. They kept silent, marveling that Ethan had won over three women who had always held others to such high standards. Their feelings of envy were harder to explain.

Ethan caught their complicated looks. They know. How much had they seen? Surely they had not witnessed everything.

He glanced at May. She sat staring down at the table, pretending the subject had nothing to do with her. After Sabrina, Erika, Maya, and now the three sisters, perhaps she had grown used to his tangled relationships.

Miki, Kuni, and Zuki were still studying the Great Balls. One of them laughed lightly. “What are these? Don't tell me you've brought us Pokémon as gifts.”

May thought of something, particularly when she saw the ball marked with a flame. “Ethan, are you going to…?”

The older sisters caught her alarm. Perhaps the balls held something more significant than they had guessed.

Ethan smiled. “It's a remarkable coincidence. There are three of you and three Pokémon. This city is where their legend began. The Burned Tower, the Bell Tower—it almost feels as if our meeting was meant to happen.”

Three Pokémon, an old legend, and Ecruteak's towers. The sisters could think of only one possibility.

Zuki looked at the balls. “Are those…?”

“Yes,” Ethan said. “They hold Entei, Raikou, and Suicune—the three legendary beasts.”

Silence fell. Every face showed disbelief. No one knew what to say. Even May had not known Ethan had Raikou and Suicune.

He has those two as well as Entei? And the three legendary birds? There were Deoxys and Celebi too. How many Pokémon don't I know about?

Ethan looked around the table. “You don't believe me?”

“It's not that,” Miki said with a strained smile. “It's simply astonishing.”

He spoke with complete seriousness, yet the claim was hard to grasp. For most people, legendary Pokémon remained distant figures in stories. Not everyone encountered them as often as Ethan or Ash did.

Rather than explain further, Ethan picked up the Great Ball marked with a water drop. He aimed it toward the open courtyard and pressed its button.

Blue light shot through the dining room and out into the yard. As it faded, Suicune appeared.

The Pokémon's long, elegant form seemed part leopard, part deer, part mythical beast. It carried a noble air, as if a veil of moisture surrounded it.

Ethan knew Suicune well, and May was surprised only so much. The five sisters could no longer stay composed. Miki, Kuni, and Zuki held their surprise in their expressions, but Naoko and Sayo sprang to their feet.

They had grown up in Johto hearing stories of the three legendary beasts. Their own performances retold those legends. The beasts meant more to them than they did to most people.

“It really is Suicune,” Miki murmured. Minutes earlier she could not have imagined meeting it this way.

“I can hardly believe my eyes,” Kuni said.

Suicune looked around, found a comfortable place in the courtyard, and lay down. Its calm behavior finally helped the sisters accept that Ethan truly had it.

They turned to the two remaining Great Balls. If this one held Suicune, the others must hold Raikou and Entei.

Miki shook her head and gave Ethan a searching look. “You keep finding ways to surprise us. How did you come to have all three?”

“It seems we've chosen an extraordinary man,” Kuni said with a smile.

Three regional League titles and all three legendary beasts were feats most people could not hope to achieve. Zuki felt more certain than ever of her choice. He was every bit the man she had seen in him.

Ethan pushed the Great Ball marked with a flame toward Miki.

She looked up at him.

“Miki, you have a Flareon. You must be fond of Fire-type Pokémon. Entei represents fire, so I thought it would suit you.”

“No, no.” She hurriedly shook her head. “I couldn't possibly accept it.”

Before they knew what the balls held, the sisters could joke about Pokémon gifts. Now they knew they held legendary Pokémon. They could hardly treat them as presents.

Ethan picked up Entei's ball and placed it firmly in her hand.

“Don't worry. We're family now. There's no need to keep score over what's yours and what's mine. I'm not giving Entei away. I'd like its mistress to look after it and let it keep you company while I'm gone. If I need it, I'll call it back.”

The sisters understood. So long as they remained together, they could share in caring for his Pokémon. If that changed someday, the beasts would return to him.

“I understand.” Miki closed her fingers around the ball. “I'll take good care of it.”

“Be careful,” Ethan added. “I only caught Entei recently. We haven't had time to build a deep bond, and it might not always listen.”

“Leave it to me,” she said with a smile.

Ethan picked up Suicune's now-empty Great Ball and handed it to Kuni.

“You have a Vaporeon, so Suicune suits you. Will you look after it for me? It's gentle, and it's been with me the longest. I think the two of you will get along.”

“I…all right.”

Miki's acceptance made it easier for Kuni. She gave Ethan a warm smile, took the ball, and looked toward Suicune in the courtyard with delight.

Finally, Ethan offered Raikou's Great Ball to Zuki. She took it without ceremony or even a thank-you, though the smile at the corners of her eyes gave her away.

Ethan knew her temperament and was not offended. “It's a pity your Eevee evolved into Umbreon, so their types don't match. But you and Raikou both have fiery tempers. I think you'll get along.”

“Hmph. You're the one with a fiery temper.”

The three balls were now in the sisters' hands. For the time being, Entei, Suicune, and Raikou would remain in Ecruteak. Ethan would call them back whenever he needed them.

Miki, Kuni, and Zuki looked at the Great Balls they held. The responsibility felt heavy, and the moment almost unreal.

Naoko and Sayo watched Ethan hopefully, as if waiting for something from him too. But he had no other Pokémon to place in their care.

“As for you two,” he said apologetically, “we'll have to see about that another time.”

Their disappointment was plain. They understood why he had entrusted valuable Pokémon to the sisters who were now his partners, yet they could not help feeling envious. Neither was quite sure what she envied most.

Ethan cleared his throat and brought everyone's attention back to him.

“These are the three legendary beasts,” he told Miki, Kuni, and Zuki. “I know what they mean to people in Johto. Please keep them out of public view. And don't neglect their training while they're here. I'd like them to learn to work together, perhaps even develop a coordinated technique.”

After catching Entei, Ethan had considered using the three beasts against Brandon's three Regis. But Entei was new to him. It would take time for the three to work as a team; for now, a lack of coordination might become a weakness. His three legendary birds were a more reliable choice for the coming battle.

Leaving the beasts here offered them a chance to learn to cooperate.

The three sisters nodded. A Trainer at Ethan's level faced challenges ordinary Trainers did not. Pokémon as powerful as the legendary beasts might be needed, and they could not afford to let the trio grow idle.

“I'll buy a Poké Ball transfer machine in the next few days,” Miki said. “Whenever you need them, we'll send the three beasts to you at once.”

Then she frowned, as if remembering something. Before Ethan could ask, she laughed.

“With all the excitement, I nearly forgot. Zuki, don't you have the Clear Bell our family has passed down for generations?”

The Clear Bell?

Ethan suddenly realized there was something he had overlooked.

Zuki smiled too. “It's been sitting in storage so long it must be covered in dust. I might have forgotten it myself.” She turned to Ethan. “You don't know our family's history yet, do you? Perhaps there really was fate in our meeting. You became our partner and entrusted the three beasts to us. As it happens, we have something of our own to offer you in return.”
