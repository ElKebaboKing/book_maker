The Charicific Valley lay between Violet City and Azalea Town. Ethan had been there before, so he knew the way.

This time, though, they knew the date of the Grand Festival, and the journey was long. Ethan still had other business in Johto, and May needed enough time to train. They could not spend too many days on the road. After discussing it, they decided to ride Dragonite and reach the valley as quickly as possible.

Travel felt different the moment they climbed onto Dragonite. It spread its wings and carried them off the ground.

White clouds and blue sky stretched above them; mountains and forests passed below. The air was cold enough that May, sitting behind Ethan, instinctively leaned closer to him.

Compared with their usual journeys on foot, this was simply a way to cover distance. They could not take in nature at close range or meet new people and encounter unexpected things. Speed was its only practical advantage.

Still, soaring above the land gave them another kind of pleasure. From up here, they could take in sky and earth at a glance. Mountain ranges that seemed impossible to cross on foot slipped swiftly beneath them.

Hours in the air grew monotonous, and the cold wind made conversation difficult. Fortunately, after flying for most of the day, they reached the Charicific Valley before sunset.

Beyond several mountains, Ethan recognized the Charizard-shaped peak and the long river lined with Charizard statues.

Without needing a direction, Dragonite descended and followed the river upstream.

Ethan could not quite name how he felt about returning. But knowing his Charizard was close filled him with anticipation.

Behind him, Dragonite had slowed and the wind was no longer so fierce. May finally had a chance to speak. She looked around in surprise.

“So this is the Charicific Valley? It's magnificent. Ethan, who put all those Charizard statues in the river? And that mountain ahead—was it carved into the shape of a Charizard, or did it form naturally?”

“You'll have to ask the woman who lives here. I think she'd be happy to answer.”

Dragonite carried them to the great doorway in the Charizard-shaped mountain. It did not land there. Instead, it flew straight over the entrance and came down in the valley beyond.

The doorway was for first-time visitors and Trainers who had wandered here by accident. Ethan was an old acquaintance; he could simply fly over the mountain.

Beyond it lay the Charicific Valley itself. May finally saw what the place was really like.

Nearly a hundred Charizard were scattered among the rocky slopes. Some ate Berries, others rested, and many more gathered in pairs to play and spar. Orange figures stood out everywhere she looked.

“There are so many Charizard…”

Dragonite's arrival caught their attention. In an instant, every Charizard turned toward the newcomers. Liza, who managed the valley from a nearby cottage, noticed them too.

She came out with her female Charizard, Charla, and waved to Ethan. “You finally made it!”

Ethan was about to answer when an orange blur crossed his vision. A moment later, a tall, powerful Charizard stood beside him.

Time had passed. Its body had changed, and so had something in the way it carried itself. Ethan recognized his Charizard immediately.

“Charizard!”

He threw his arms around it, and Charizard hugged him so hard it lifted him off the ground. Both of them were overjoyed to be together again.

When Charizard set him down, Ethan stepped back and looked it over from head to toe. “You've grown stronger. Your eyes are sharper too. You've really come a long way!”

Charizard bared its teeth in a grin.

Liza had reached them by then. “You're a few days later than I expected, but you aren't too late.” She looked at May. “And who is this?”

May smiled openly. “I'm May. I'm traveling with Ethan now.”

Liza did not ask where Erika, who had accompanied Ethan on his last visit, had gone. She merely gave him a knowing look. Evidently she had taken May for his new girlfriend. He's quite the flirt, isn't he?

After they exchanged greetings, Liza invited them into her cottage.

The wooden floor creaked beneath their feet. Water was heating over the fireplace. Once Ethan and May had settled at a small table, Liza brought them a pot of steaming tea.

Ethan's Charizard and Charla came inside too. They were too large to sit at the table and settled on the open floor before the fireplace.

Ethan happened to glance their way. Charla kept casting flirtatious looks at his Charizard and teasing it with her tail. His Charizard sat aloof, barely acknowledging her.

Well, now. His Charizard had not only grown stronger here. It had won a female Charizard's heart. He wondered whether Liza had noticed.

Liza set several plates of snacks in front of them. “You made it today, but it's getting late. Why not stay the night? Tomorrow morning your Charizard can challenge the strongest one in the valley.”

Ethan looked at his Charizard. It was already watching him. He turned back to Liza and nodded. “We'd be glad to stay.”

“Of course!” Liza waved away his formality. “I bought plenty of ingredients today. I'll cook you something so good you'll jump out of your seats! It's rare that I get visitors, and I want to hear all about your adventures outside the valley.”

They drank tea, ate snacks, and talked beside the crackling fire. Ethan and May shared amusing stories from their travels, while Liza told them what Ethan's Charizard had been doing in the valley.

Liza was lively, cheerful, and easy to talk to. Their conversation went on until the windows had gone black with night. Only then did they realize how late it was.

“Oh! Is it that late? Give me a moment and I'll make dinner.”

She declined their offers to help, tied on an apron, and went into the kitchen. Before long, a tempting smell drifted out, along with the sounds of something simmering.

Dinner would take a while. Ethan finally had time to speak with his Charizard. Words alone could not tell him how much it had grown, though. After a few moments, he asked May to stay inside and went out into the darkness with Charizard.

May did not ask what they were going to do. She had a good idea. Ethan would have Charizard demonstrate its skills so he could judge its progress himself.

She began talking to Charla, only to watch the female Charizard follow them outside too.

So much for company. May spent a while alone in the cottage, then let out her Combusken and Eevee to keep her entertained.

That was the scene Liza found when she carried a pot of soup out of the kitchen. She set it on the table. “Hm? Where are Ethan and the Charizard?”

“They went out for something. They'll be back soon.”

“Out?” As a Trainer herself, Liza could guess why Ethan had taken his Charizard outside. She said no more, though she wondered why Charla had followed them.

At that moment the door opened. Ethan came back in with both Charizard.

“Oh, am I the last one?” He laughed when he saw May helping Liza set the table. “Looks like I got back at just the right time.”

May and Liza could see the pleased smile on his face. He was clearly satisfied with Charizard's growth.

After washing his hands, Ethan let his other Pokémon out, and everyone ate dinner together.

Liza had not exaggerated her skill as a cook. The meal was simple, with a pot of hearty seasonal soup as its main dish, but making such an ordinary dish taste this good showed real talent. Ethan and May praised it freely. They did not quite jump out of their seats, but May went back for enough extra helpings that her stomach rounded a little.

…

They spent the night at Liza's cottage. After washing up and eating breakfast the next morning, they headed toward the deepest part of the valley.

Many wild Charizard lived here, but they had their own territories. The broad clearing beyond the entrance belonged to them all and served as their main gathering place. Other parts of the valley were restricted, and not every Charizard could venture into them.

The strongest Charizard lived in one of those places. It had claimed the valley's highest and most comfortable spot. Today, Ethan's Charizard meant to take both its position and its title.

Perhaps Ethan and May's arrival had told the others what was coming. Perhaps the Charizard had expected this challenge for some time. As the group and its two Charizard walked deeper into the valley, Charizard streamed out from all sides and followed.

Everyone seemed to know that something important was about to happen. Tension filled the air. May had no part in the battle and would only watch, yet the mood made her nervous too.

They crossed the wide valley, climbed a small hill, and went up a long flight of steps to its highest point.

There, an enormous, exceptionally powerful Charizard reclined with its eyes almost closed, as though half asleep. Its bearing alone marked it as the valley's strongest.

“Is that the one?” May whispered, as if afraid to wake it. “It doesn't look easy to beat.”

“Did I ever say my Charizard would be easy to beat?” Ethan smiled. “We won't know which one is stronger until they fight.”

The wild Charizard were already forming a ring around them. Low growls rose from the crowd. They were excited, but restrained; none stepped forward. They all understood the importance of this challenge.

One Charizard among them caught Ethan's eye. It was no larger or smaller than the others, but while they were eager, it stayed half hidden in the crowd. It watched Ethan's Charizard with envy and frustration.

Ethan turned to Liza. “That's Ash's Charizard, isn't it?” He sounded certain despite phrasing it as a question.

Liza followed his gaze. “Yes, that's Ash's. It's fiercely competitive. Seeing yours challenge for the top first must be hard for it.”

Before Ethan could reply, she added, “One more thing. This is a matter of Charizard honor. No one else may interfere in the battle—not even a Trainer.”

Her meaning was clear. The two Pokémon would decide the outcome by their own strength and skill.

Ethan had not intended to interfere anyway. He had come to witness this moment with his own eyes.
