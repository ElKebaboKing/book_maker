The deeper they traveled into the crystal forest, the harder the terrain became. Crystal growths crowded closer together. Trees of every bizarre shape had crystallized with their branches and leaves intact, becoming so rigid that they could neither bend nor break. At times, the group had to climb to considerable heights merely to continue forward.

They did not dare fly openly overhead on their Pokémon. Doing so might draw Entei's attention and expose their position.

Fortunately, they soon discovered a small river that still flowed through the forest. Perhaps because the water remained in constant motion, it had not crystallized.

This was excellent news.

The river offered a clear path. Even when crystalline trees crowded its banks, they could not extend into the channel. If the group followed the water upstream, they could travel unobstructed all the way to the manor.

With that thought, Ethan stepped into the river and tested it.

The water was neither deep nor swift. Most of it barely covered his shoes, and even the deepest places reached only his calves. Most valuable of all, the river provided a direct route, saving them a great deal of time and energy.

Their shoes and trousers would inevitably become soaked, but they were Trainers accustomed to traveling through every kind of weather. No one cared about such a minor inconvenience.

The water was cold enough to make their feet sting after a while, but walking there was still far easier than forcing a route along the bank.

When Ethan waved, the others entered the river and began walking upstream.

Several minutes passed without danger, allowing them to relax slightly. Ethan still reminded everyone to remain alert and keep their voices low so Entei would not discover them.

The instant he finished speaking, a streak of flame suddenly crossed the sky above them.

They looked up and saw Entei standing upon a towering rock in the distance. It had launched the attack, but its target was not the group. The flames were aimed at a balloon airship high overhead.

The attack swept across the sky and struck the airship with perfect accuracy.

A violent explosion destroyed the balloon. Amid countless fragments of the shattered craft, several screams rang out. Three figures of different sizes plunged like meteors toward the crystal flower.

There was no need to guess. Only the unlucky Team Rocket trio would have followed them here at a time like this.

The trio's airship had been far too conspicuous and attracted Entei's attention, provoking its attack.

The three were unfortunate, but in another sense, Team Rocket had drawn attention away from Ethan's group.

When Entei turned its head, Ethan's thoughts raced. He quickly signaled for everyone to leave the water and hide.

They had barely concealed themselves when Entei surveyed the surroundings with a searching gaze.

Finding nothing unusual, it returned to the flower-shaped tower.

It's certainly cautious...

Ethan marveled inwardly, then looked toward the place where Team Rocket had fallen.

He laughed quietly. "We really owe Team Rocket our thanks this time. They drew the attack away from us. I hope they land safely. Honestly, I admit I've treated them too harshly before. The next time we meet, I'll definitely show them some mercy!"

Misty laughed at his joke. "I agree! I wonder whether they'll be happy when they hear the news!"

After the interruption, the group returned to the river. Several hundred meters farther upstream, they finally reached the base of the manor.

Crystal now surrounded the entire estate. From outside, not a single trace of the original building remained. It truly looked like a flower made entirely from crystal.

Fortunately, one drainage outlet remained open, providing a way into the flower-shaped tower.

The outlet stood more than ten meters above the ground, however, and the wall beneath it was perfectly smooth. They could not climb there by themselves.

A continuous stream of water poured from above, joined the river, and threw spray in every direction.

Ethan and Ash exchanged a glance. They would need their Pokémon's help.

Both took out Poké Balls and summoned their Flying-type Pokémon.

Ethan released Charizard, while Ash chose his Shiny Noctowl.

Blue light flashed, and Charizard moved to Ethan's side without needing instructions.

Ethan held out a hand to Misty. When she approached, he swept her into his arms and mounted Charizard. It carried them to the drainage outlet above.

Ash's Noctowl was far too small to lift both him and Brock to the opening.

His pride prevented him from asking Ethan for help, so Ash resorted to a more complicated method.

He took out two more Poké Balls and released Bulbasaur and Chikorita. Noctowl carried the two Pokémon to the outlet first. Then they used Vine Whip from above to secure Ash and Brock, allowing the two boys to climb slowly up the wall.

The method took longer, but unlike Misty, neither of them was a girl. They ought to rely on themselves.

Once Ash reached the opening, Ethan asked, "Where's your Charizard? Why didn't you simply send it out?"

Ash shrugged. "I left it at Charicific Valley to train!"

Charicific Valley? So Ash has already visited it.

Given the opportunity, Ethan wanted to see the place and let his own Charizard train there for a while.

Of course, unlike Ash, he would not leave Charizard there indefinitely.

After a short rest, the group entered the flower-shaped tower through the drainage outlet and found themselves in a greenhouse.

The roar of the water concealed their footsteps and conversation. Even so, they moved carefully and watched their surroundings.

Ethan looked around. Crystal had ravaged the greenhouse beyond recognition, transforming its once beautiful plants into rigid formations. Only a small conservatory upon a patch of land surrounded by pools remained untouched and retained its original appearance.

Still...

Professor Hale truly possessed an enormous estate. This greenhouse alone was nearly as large as a Pokémon Center. He must have been incredibly wealthy.

As the group waded toward the conservatory, Ethan suddenly received a call from Erika.

He answered, and before he could speak, Erika's helpless voice came from the other end.

"I have some unfortunate news. Professor Oak discovered your operation!"

"Huh? How did he find out so quickly!?"

Ethan had expected their departure to be noticed eventually, but he never imagined Professor Oak would discover it as soon as they reached the manor.

After Erika explained, Ethan learned that the television reporter had happened to spot them crossing the river and recorded their entire journey.

Professor Oak and the others had seen the live broadcast and learned that the group had already slipped away.
