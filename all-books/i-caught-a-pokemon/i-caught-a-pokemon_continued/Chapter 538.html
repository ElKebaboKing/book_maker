The shock wave from the impact erased most of the wall of water headed toward Altomare. The rest of the tsunami continued toward the city.

Suicune's body shone brightly. It opened its mouth again, resonating with the seawater. More than a dozen waterspouts rose from the remaining wave and twisted toward the sky. They were much smaller and weaker than the one the Soul Dew had made, but there were many of them. Water flowed upward along their spirals, breaking up the wave's movement from within.

The three Pokémon could not make the tsunami vanish altogether. They had, however, weakened the part bearing down on Altomare enough that the city could endure it.

Ethan did not hesitate. Just before the water reached Suicune, he returned it to its Great Ball. Dragonite carried him above the wave, where he searched for Latias and Latios.

Perhaps they had spent all their strength in the collision. Neither Legendary Pokémon had flown back up. They might be floating on the surface or sinking below it.

Finding them in such a huge, dark sea was no easy task.

Ethan searched without success for some time. At last Dragonite spotted them: Latias and Latios, pressed close together on the surface, drifting with the current.

At Ethan's signal, Dragonite descended. Only when they drew near could Ethan see that both Pokémon were exhausted and unable to move.

Dragonite suddenly cried out. Following its gaze, Ethan saw that the wave was about to strike the ruined castle on the exposed seabed. If he did not get Latias and Latios out of the water, the current would smash them against the stone.

"Dragonite, lift them!"

Dragonite flew above the pair and reached down with its short arms to take hold of Latios. But Latios was a large Pokémon, two meters long. After several attempts, Dragonite still could not get him into the air.

It tried Latias instead. She was smaller, yet carrying her was still a struggle.

And even if Dragonite managed to take Latias away, Latios would be left behind.

Ethan saw Dragonite's distress and felt his own frustration rising.

If Charizard were here, the two flying Pokémon could carry both Latias and Latios. But Ethan had left Charizard at Charicific Valley.

He had left Gengar on shore too, to protect Erika and the others. Gengar's powers might have lifted the pair clear of the sea if he were here.

Now Dragonite was Ethan's only flying Pokémon.

This was a problem.

The wave drew closer to the ruins. Knowing he could not wait, Ethan gritted his teeth and took two Poké Balls from his coat.

"Latios, Latias, please stay in these Poké Balls until I can get you ashore. I promise I'm not trying to catch you while you can't resist. I'll let you out as soon as we reach land!"

Latios barely had the strength to raise his head. He looked at Ethan and gave a faint nod. He seemed to understand the danger and that there was no other choice.

Beside him, Latias merely watched the Poké Balls in Ethan's hands with innocent curiosity, blinking at them.

Ethan aimed the two balls at them. Red light enveloped Latios and Latias and drew them inside.

The balls rocked only slightly in his hands. Neither Pokémon struggled. Two soft clicks sounded, and the balls fell still.

With both safe, Ethan nudged Dragonite's belly with his legs. Dragonite climbed and flew back toward Altomare.

As they left, the remainder of the tsunami struck the castle ruins. Under that immense force, the crumbling structure collapsed instantly, without raising so much as a visible splash.

The wave continued toward the city.

Dragonite flew fast. It soon landed in the square, and Ethan immediately took out the Poké Balls and released Latias and Latios.

He had meant what he said. Taking them into the balls had been a temporary measure to save them, not an attempt to take advantage of their helplessness. He let them out the moment he reached shore.

When the blue light faded, everyone crowded around the two very weak Pokémon on the ground.

"Latios! Latias! Are you all right?"

The others were a little surprised to see them emerge from Ethan's Poké Balls, but no one said anything. Even Lorenzo only sighed, his expression complicated.

Everyone understood that the rescue must have been dangerous. Ethan would not have done it unless he had no other way to save them.

Erika and Brock knelt to examine them. Finding no visible wounds, they nodded to the others.

"They've just exhausted their strength."

Before Ash could say anything, a deep rumble sounded behind them. They turned. The wave was nearly at the city. It still looked fierce, but after being weakened, it was nowhere near as terrifying as before.

"This square is low-lying," Lorenzo said. "We should get to higher ground."

They realized he was right and hurried up into the city. With everyone there to help, Ethan did not need to put Latias and Latios back into the Poké Balls.

The wave arrived quickly. It crashed against the city's seawall and poured into the canals, raising the water level visibly.

Wooden boats that had rested on the exposed canal beds were swept deeper into the city.

But the wave truly had lost much of its force. It flooded some low areas without causing further destruction.

Ethan and the others climbed a dozen or so steps. At that height, the seawater could no longer reach them.

About half an hour later, the water began to recede. The overwhelming danger seemed, at last, to have passed.
