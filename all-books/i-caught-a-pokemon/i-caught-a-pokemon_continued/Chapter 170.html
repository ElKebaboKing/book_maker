As a courtesy, Ethan took off his cap and sunglasses and offered Lorelei his hand.

"That's right, I'm Ethan. It's a pleasure to meet you, Miss Lorelei."

Lorelei glanced between him and Sabrina. "I never imagined two people with such different personalities would end up together. You've surprised me, but now that I see you side by side, you do make quite a pair."

She shook Ethan's hand, then asked, "So what brings the two of you all the way to this remote island?"

As the Saffron Gym Leader, Sabrina had met Lorelei several times. But Sabrina had never been one for conversation, and she had little interest in talking to people she barely knew. They had remained no more than acquaintances who exchanged greetings.

After hearing Lorelei's remark, though, Sabrina suddenly found her much more likable.

"We didn't come here on purpose," Ethan said with a smile. "We're headed for the Orange Islands. We were tired when we passed over this place, so we landed for a rest."

"I see." Lorelei gave him a knowing look. Once the proprietor had gone into the kitchen, she lowered her voice. "And here I thought you'd come looking for the Moltres on the Sevii Islands."

There was a Moltres here?

Sabrina turned to Ethan in astonishment. His expression had hardly changed. Lorelei must be telling the truth, and Ethan must have known about it already.

He had kept that quiet. The thing he'd said he wanted to see earlier could only have been Moltres.

With Lorelei having brought it up, Ethan saw no reason to conceal it. "I had considered looking for it. You know quite a bit yourself, Miss Lorelei. I didn't expect you to know there was a Moltres here."

"Oh, please." Lorelei rolled her eyes. "I was born and raised on these islands. I've heard stories about Moltres since I was little—enough to wear out my ears."

"You grew up here? That's the first I've heard of it. But do you think Moltres is only a legend?"

"Of course not," Lorelei said seriously. "Quite a few older islanders have seen what looked like signs of its activity. But it comes and goes so unpredictably that trainers who travel here to find it usually leave empty-handed. Over time, people started treating it as a story the locals made up."

The proprietor returned with two heaping plates of seafood fried rice. Ethan fell silent, leaving their discussion for later.

The aroma reached him as soon as the food was set down. Squid, crab, and prawns surrounded plump grains of rice soaked in sauce and glistening red. One look was enough to make him hungry.

It looked delicious. He eagerly scooped up a spoonful and put it in his mouth.

A moment later, the force of the chili made him cough and cough again. His face turned bright red. He grabbed his iced water and drank, finally easing the burn a little.

"Why is it so spicy?"

Lorelei burst out laughing. Sabrina tried a small bite herself and frowned.

It really was too hot for her.

Seeing that the two travelers knew Lorelei, the proprietor offered an explanation. "This is a fishing island. Most people here spend their days at sea, exposed to the wind and damp. That's why we like food with enough heat to warm us through."

"Exactly," Lorelei said. "It's a local specialty. It takes a few tries to get used to the spice, but then you can really appreciate how good it is."

Ethan took a few more bites. Once his tongue adjusted to the heat, he had to admit she was right. The rice was excellent.

Sabrina, however, refused to take another bite. Ethan asked the proprietor to make her something else.

When the man went back into the kitchen, Lorelei turned to Ethan. "I know you helped the International Police raid Team Rocket's base last time. As it happens, I have a difficult matter I'd like your help with."

She meant the secret base beneath the Celadon Game Corner. During Ethan and the others' raid, Lorelei and Bruno, another member of the Elite Four, had gone to Viridian City to arrest Giovanni. Unfortunately, he had been at the base, so they had found no one to arrest.

"Is it about Team Rocket?" Ethan asked.

Lorelei nodded. "The International Police have been hunting them all over mainland Kanto. It may look as though Team Rocket has been wiped out, but their reach is greater than we think. Lately, I've heard reports of strangers in matching uniforms moving around the Sevii Islands. I'm sure Team Rocket is planning something here."

"Then why not report it to the International Police?"

"I'm one of the Elite Four. Keeping this region safe is part of my job. I can't go asking those people to handle it for me." She waved a hand. "You two are different. Sabrina is a Pokémon League Gym Leader, and you're the newly crowned League champion. We're all with the League, so there's no need to stand on ceremony."

All with the League? Ethan gave a helpless smile. Since when had he become a League employee?

Still, Team Rocket posed a danger to him too. He had every reason to want them gone.

"All right. But I can only stay a few days. If this takes longer, you'll need to ask someone else for help."

"Don't worry." Lorelei patted her chest confidently. "I've nearly found their trail. Give me a few days and I'll know exactly where they are. Oh, and if we catch them, I'll take you to look for Moltres as a thank-you."

"Really?" Ethan blinked. "You know where it is?"

"Where else? Mt. Ember, of course. Finding its exact spot will take some luck, though."

Mt. Ember. Ethan thought it over. Back on Earth, he'd learned from the games that there was a Moltres on the Sevii Islands, but he couldn't clearly remember where to find it.

With a local like Lorelei to guide them, their chances would be much better.
