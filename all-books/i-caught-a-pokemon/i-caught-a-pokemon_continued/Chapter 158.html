Jimmy recalled the unconscious Persian and sent out Dodrio, a three-headed bird. He hoped its Flying typing would give it an advantage over Venusaur.

Ethan knew type matchups too well to allow that. He recalled Venusaur and sent Jolteon out against it.

Jimmy was about as strong as Gary, perhaps even a little stronger. Now he understood exactly how powerless Gary must have felt when Ethan kept turning every matchup against him.

Once this man won the first bout, he committed completely to keeping the type advantage. He would not give an opponent even the smallest opening in that respect.

Jimmy had no easy answer.

The second bout began just as he had feared. Dodrio gradually fell behind.

As a bird Pokémon that had spent its life running on the ground, Dodrio's wings had dwindled. It was a Flying-type and could use Flying-type moves, but it could not actually fly.

Its legs were powerful, however. Jolteon was a little faster, but Dodrio had remarkable stamina. It could keep up through their pursuit across the field.

That alone was not enough. As the fight intensified and Dodrio took one Electric-type attack after another, its strength began to fail. Even its steps grew unsteady.

Despite his advantage, Ethan remained careful. He did not let Jolteon rush in too close. Dodrio's three heads could attack independently, making it dangerous at close range.

"Jolteon, use Thunderbolt!"

After several more exchanges, Dodrio stood covered in wounds and barely able to continue. Jolteon's Thunderbolt finally brought it down.

Outside the field, spectators waved their cheering sticks and shouted.

Ethan's first two bouts had been less spectacular than his previous match, but they were still exciting to watch—especially for fans delighted to see him winning so confidently.

"Ladies and gentlemen!" Even the commentator sounded as if he were rooting for Ethan now. "Ethan Vale has taken a two-to-zero lead and reached match point! Will we see another clean sweep? Let's find out!"

Jimmy had fought hard to reach the Top 16. He was not willing to be eliminated so easily. He recalled Dodrio and threw his last Poké Ball.

"Go, Golem!"

Blue light flashed, and Golem's heavy body landed with a dull thud. Ethan immediately recalled Jolteon and sent out Blastoise.

Golem had an even worse matchup than Dodrio. As a Rock- and Ground-type, it was four times as vulnerable to Water-type attacks. Its chance of turning the match around was slim.

Even so, Golem refused to give up. It endured Blastoise's Water Gun and Hydro Pump through sheer toughness, curled itself into a boulder, and rolled close enough to make one last attempt.

Its arms closed around Blastoise. Golem leaped high, then turned its body over and drove them both toward the ground.

"There it is!" the commentator shouted. "Against an opponent with the type advantage, Golem has used Seismic Toss! That powerful move helped carry Jimmy all the way to the Top 16. Can it save him against Ethan Vale's Blastoise?"

The two Pokémon plummeted together. Blastoise struggled on the way down, but Golem's grip was too strong to break.

Ethan did not look alarmed. Another Pokémon might have been badly hurt, but Blastoise had a defense of its own.

"Blastoise, use Withdraw!"

Blastoise hurriedly pulled its body into its thick shell.

Boom!

The shell slammed into the ground, leaving a deep crater in the once-level field. Dust surged outward and hid a broad section of the arena.

Golem stood outside the cloud, panting. It and Jimmy stared hopefully into the crater.

When the dust cleared, the shell shifted. Blastoise stretched out its limbs and raised its head. It shook itself. The landing had jolted it, but it appeared otherwise unharmed.

"What!?"

Jimmy had not expected that. He gritted his teeth as the feeling of helplessness returned. That had been his last card, and Ethan had answered it so easily.

"Oh, dear!" The commentator sounded almost amused. "Withdraw protected Blastoise from the worst of Golem's Seismic Toss! How is Jimmy going to break through now?"

What Jimmy did next surprised everyone.

He spread his hands. "I surrender."

"What?" The referee looked at him, unsure he had heard correctly.

"I said I surrender." Jimmy raised his right hand and sighed. "There's no need to keep going. I lost this match."

He could see the situation clearly. Even if Golem somehow defeated Blastoise, Ethan still had two other Pokémon ready. Golem could not beat them both.

Why put his partner through more pain when the outcome was certain?

"Jimmy has surrendered!" the commentator exclaimed. "It may go against the spirit of never giving up, but it's a sensible decision. Congratulations to Ethan Vale on advancing to the next round!"

The crowd erupted. Cheering sticks clapped together throughout the stands.

Ash watched Ethan closely, his fists tightening. They were both in their first League tournament, yet their performances could hardly have been more different.

Ethan was already far ahead in strength. Ash had struggled through every stage only to finish in the Top 16.

He watched Ethan leave to the cheers of the whole stadium. Even Misty and Chloe, sitting nearby, were celebrating for him. Ash said nothing, but he envied it.

Defeating one strong opponent after another alongside your Pokémon—surely that had to be one of the happiest feelings in the world.
