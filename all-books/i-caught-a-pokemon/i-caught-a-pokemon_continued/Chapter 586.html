Celebi slipped unconscious again. Its body was shriveled and gray, like a plant withering before their eyes. It seemed close to death.

Everyone gathered around it. Ash and Sammy were frantic. Ash’s first thought was to get water, but when he turned toward the lake, he found it had changed.

The battle and explosion had destroyed much of the forest along its shore. Worse, leaking dark energy had spread through the water. The entire lake looked as though ink had been poured into it. Once clear, it was now murky and black.

“This...”

Ash gritted his teeth. His usually slow mind seemed to find an answer at once, and he turned to Ethan.

“Ethan, I heard Suicune can purify water. Ask it to do something!”

He did not appeal to Suicune directly. Ethan’s Pokémon were devoted to him. Gardevoir, Gengar, and Jolteon might listen to Erika, but his red Gyarados would obey no one else. Ash did not know what Suicune was like, so he asked Ethan.

Erika and the others simply looked at Ethan with hope. Sammy, Towa, Diana, and White, however, stared at him and then at the Pokémon beside him.

Could this Suicune actually belong to Ethan?

How was that possible? To people in Johto, Suicune and the other two Legendary beasts were noble beings beyond reach. None of them had imagined a Trainer could catch one like an ordinary Pokémon.

They did not want to believe it, but what happened next left no doubt.

Ethan turned to Suicune and nodded. “Go on. We’re counting on you.”

He had sent Suicune out partly because he knew this would happen. Even if Ash had said nothing, Ethan would have asked it to purify the lake.

Suicune raised its head and called toward the sky, its voice somewhere between a deer’s and a leopard’s. Blue-green light wrapped around its body, warm and peaceful.

It sprang toward the lake. To everyone’s amazement, it did not sink. It stood on the water as though it weighed nothing.

With several more bounds, Suicune moved toward the center. Each time a hoof touched the black water, blue-green light bloomed outward. The darkness receded, and the water became clear again.

Everyone on shore watched silently. Suicune circled the lake like an elegant dancer. By the time it had completed its circuit, the dark energy was gone and the water was clean.

Suicune returned to Ethan’s side. Towa, Diana, and White looked at him again, their estimation of him rising. They wanted to speak but could not find the words.

Ash recovered first. He lowered Celebi into the water, hoping it would restore moisture to its withered body.

His hope was in vain. Celebi did not respond. It looked dead.

“Celebi...”

No matter how Ash called, there was no movement. Its chest no longer rose or fell.

Everyone understood. Erika sighed. Misty turned away to wipe her eyes. Towa, Diana, and White bowed their heads.

Ash and Sammy blamed themselves.

“If only... if only I’d rescued you sooner...”

“No, it’s my fault. If I’d protected you better, that man couldn’t have caught you...”

The wild Pokémon seemed to sense what had happened. They emerged from the forest and called mournfully to Celebi.

It was as though the whole forest grieved for its guardian.

Only Ethan looked up at the sky, waiting for something.

He alone knew that the story should not end here.

A brilliant light flashed overhead. Blue-green radiance spread across the forest, just as it had when they first entered the Mysterious Forest. The voice of the forest that Towa remembered so well rang out again.

“Is that... the sound of time travel?”

They looked up, bewildered. What was happening? Could there be another Pokémon besides Celebi with the power to cross time?

The light brightened until they could barely keep their eyes open.

Once they managed to look again, dozens of Pokémon flew down from the sky.

Every one of them was a Celebi.

Before anyone could recover from the shock, several flew to the Celebi beside them. With psychic power, they lifted its lifeless body into the air. The others circled it as though performing a mysterious ritual.

Blue-green light blazed even more brightly.

Ethan and the others forced themselves to watch through the sting in their eyes. The fallen Celebi began to recover as though life were flowing back into it. Its body filled out, color returned, and at last its eyes opened.

In only a short time, it was lively again.

“Incredible,” Erika whispered. “They brought a dead Pokémon back to life...”

“And I never knew Celebi had companions,” Sammy said.

Ethan watched the sky, but he was not sure Erika’s explanation was right.

True resurrection would involve powers beyond anything he understood. Perhaps this was a reversal of time. If Celebi could travel through time, maybe the others had turned this Celebi’s personal time back to a moment before it died.

After restoring it, the visiting Celebi exchanged nods with their companion and flew back into the sky. Just as they had arrived, they returned to their own times.

Another possibility struck Ethan: perhaps they were not separate companions at all, but the same Celebi from different moments in its life.

That was only a guess. Who could truly know? Scientists spent their lives pursuing mysteries like this.

Click.

Ethan took out his phone and photographed the scene. The light was so strong that the picture came out badly overexposed. He could make out only a few dozen dark specks. Without looking closely, no one would know they were Celebi.

It was enough for him. When he was old, the photograph might help him remember this extraordinary day and tell his children about it.

Celebi seemed delighted to be alive. It flew circles overhead, calling happily as it swooped back and forth.

Then, as it passed over the wreckage on its way toward Ethan and the others, a pair of powerful hands shot out and seized it.

“What?”

Celebi cried out. Ash and the others stared in shock. The hands belonged to Vicious. He had survived the collapse and crawled from the debris.

They had assumed he was buried beneath the wooden creature. He was alive after all.

Vicious bared his teeth in a savage grin. Without a word, he activated the jetpack on his back and flew away with Celebi in his grasp.

“No!”

Ash took off after him, but two running legs were no match for a jetpack.

As Vicious pulled farther away, Ethan flashed past on Dragonite and gave chase.

“Leave the rest to me! Wait here until I get back!”

He urged Dragonite forward. Trainer and Pokémon rose over the forest and rapidly closed the distance.

Everyone else had relaxed when Celebi recovered. Ethan had only grown more alert. They had not truly reached the end, and danger could still come at any moment.

Vicious had seized precisely such a moment.

As soon as he saw it happen, Ethan recalled Suicune into its Great Ball. He sent out Dragonite, mounted, and flew after the hunter in one swift sequence. He had moved before he could even say a word to Erika.

To anyone watching, his reflexes might have seemed extraordinary. Ethan knew he had been waiting for that chance for a long time.

Ash ran a few more steps and lost sight of both Vicious and Ethan.

He had heard Ethan say he would handle it. Ash’s nature would not let him stand by.

Still running, he called back, “Ethan and I will take care of the rest! Wait for us here!”

“Wait!” Sammy sprinted after him. “I’m coming too!”

Ash nodded. Their personalities were different, but they were close in age and shared a love of Pokémon. He already thought of Sammy as a friend and would not turn him away.

Ash reached for Charizard’s Poké Ball, thinking he and Sammy could ride it after Dragonite. His fingers touched his belt before he remembered: after the Silver Conference, he had transferred Charizard back to the Charicific Valley. It was not with him.

On foot, they could not hope to catch the two fliers.

But Ash was too determined to give up. He drew Noctowl’s Poké Ball instead and threw it high.

“Noctowl, follow them!”

Noctowl glanced toward the distant sky and understood. It nodded, spread its wings, and flew after Dragonite. Ash watched its course, then ran into the forest with Sammy. Soon they too were out of sight.

At the lake, Erika, Misty, Brock, Towa, Diana, and White watched them go.

Erika shook her head. Everything had happened so fast that Ethan was gone before she could react.

She was not worried for him. With so many powerful Pokémon at his side, it was his enemies who ought to fear for their safety.

“They’ve gone after Vicious. We can trust them. Let’s set up camp here while we wait.”

Diana nodded. “Everyone must be tired after all this. I’ll make lunch.”

Misty and the others agreed. There was nothing more they could do now but wait for Ethan and Ash to return.

White looked once at the wreck of his airship in the lake and sighed. It had been in perfect condition when they set out. What a loss to suffer through no fault of his own.

...

Above the forest, Vicious flew toward its edge with his jetpack.

He held Celebi tightly. It struggled, but his gloves were specially made. They gave off a faint dark energy that kept it from escaping or attacking him.

He had not flown far when he heard something behind him. Ethan was pursuing him on Dragonite.

That man was impossible to shake.

Vicious gritted his teeth and pushed the jetpack to full power. Flames roared from it, and his speed surged.

He was foolish to think that would leave Dragonite behind.

Dragonite might look slow on land, but it was extraordinarily fast in the air. Even Vicious’s sudden burst of speed could not open a gap.

More importantly, a jetpack had limited fuel. Before long it ran dry, forcing Vicious to descend.

As he landed, an orange shape flashed before him. Dragonite came down opposite him.

Ethan dismounted and smiled. “You’re out of options. Stop struggling and let Celebi go.”
