At the far end of the long river stood a majestic mountain. A long flight of stone steps had been built at its foot, leading up to an enormous gate.

The gate stood four or five meters high. Its stone had a faint blue-green sheen, and it looked heavy enough to weigh thousands of pounds. Two huge Charizard statues stood on either side. Facing each other with their claws clasped, they looked as though they were testing each other's strength, or perhaps symbolizing friendship.

The two flying Charizard landed steadily in front of it.

Liza dismounted Charla, walked to the gate, and smiled back at them.

"Welcome to Charicific Valley."

She pressed a button on a remote control, and the massive stone doors slowly slid apart.

Well, would you look at that. They're electric!

Beyond the gate lay a vast green basin. Dozens of Charizard rested, soared, battled, or ate Berries throughout it.

Ethan helped Erika down from his Charizard. At the sight, she could not help saying, "There are... so many Charizard..."

Ethan nodded. It was an impressive sight.

He had seen wild Charizard before, but never so many together. Orange bodies filled the valley in every direction, enough to make his eyes swim.

At the sound of the gate opening, all the nearby Charizard turned to look at them.

These Charizard were large and powerfully built. Some bore permanent scars that made them look fierce. Their cold stares would put pressure on anyone, and even Ethan felt his brow twitch.

They were trying to intimidate him as soon as he arrived!

Liza led them into the valley.

"The Charizard here grow stronger without relying on humans," she explained as they walked. "They compete with one another, spar with their companions, and train on their own. Each wants to become the strongest Charizard in the valley!"

She glanced at Ethan's Charizard. "If you came to train yours, let it battle them. But I should warn you: an ordinary Charizard can't beat the ones here!"

Ethan nodded. Having seen the animated series, he knew these Charizard were far from ordinary.

In game terms, though they were wild, their levels would be high. If a newly evolved Charizard was around level thirty-six, these might be around level seventy.

That was an overwhelming difference.

They owed their strength partly to daily battles and training, just as Liza said. Time was another advantage.

A Trainer's Pokémon might evolve after gaining enough strength or through the influence of an Evolution stone. The Charizard here had spent years building their strength little by little before evolving. They had a far more solid foundation than ordinary members of the species.

Most were in their prime, not newly evolved youngsters. Dealing with the young Pokémon that accompanied many Trainers would hardly trouble them.

Ethan saw his Charizard eager for a battle and was about to choose an opponent when a relatively thin Charizard caught his eye.

It was noticeably smaller than the others. Alone in a corner, it held a large stone and repeatedly lowered itself into squats.

No other Charizard stood within dozens of meters of it. It was clearly isolated.

Ethan had a thought. "That's Ash's Charizard, isn't it?"

Liza needed only one glance to confirm it. "Yes, that's Ash's. That one works so hard. It's been here more than twenty days, and every day it challenges the other Charizard and trains with everything it has!"

Ethan sighed inwardly.

Ash's early ace was now the weakest Charizard here. That was a startling reversal.

How would Ethan's own Charizard compare with the wild ones?

Ash's Charizard noticed Ethan's. Its eyes flickered, but it did nothing more and lowered its head to continue training.

Just as Ash viewed Ethan as his greatest rival, his Charizard naturally regarded Ethan's Charizard as its own rival.

Seeing that rival arrive here only sharpened its sense of urgency.

By then, the group had walked into the heart of the valley.

The wild Charizard nearby had heard enough of Ethan's conversation with Liza to understand why he had come. One after another, they puffed out their chests and eyed his Charizard sidelong.

They had taught one overconfident newcomer a lesson not long ago. Now another had turned up!

Ethan was quietly relieved that he had raised his Charizard well. It was not quite as imposing as these sturdy wild Charizard, but the gap was nowhere near as great as it had been for Ash's.

As for which one to challenge...

He looked left and right. None of them appeared easy to beat.

Unable to decide, he left the choice to his Pokémon.

"Charizard, pick an opponent for yourself."

Charizard nodded and walked toward the most heavily built Charizard in the valley.

Ethan coughed and hurried in front of it.

"You..." he said with an exasperated laugh. "I told you to choose, but you need to know your limits! Why pick the strongest one for your first match?"

Charizard tilted its head, apparently confused. Ethan knew how careless it could be and sighed.

"Even if you're confident, start with a safer match. Choose a relatively weaker opponent. That'll let you get used to how they fight and give us an idea of how strong they are."

Charizard seemed to understand. Ethan added, "And I won't interfere in any of your battles here. You'll have to handle everything yourself, so don't let your guard down!"
