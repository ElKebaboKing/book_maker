“We should get away from here,” Ethan told Misty.

He signaled the red Gyarados to dive beneath the lake and shelter from the wide-ranging attack that was coming. Then he called Gardevoir to him through telepathy. She used Teleport to take Ethan and Misty to a distant peak.

Lightning, frost, and fire blazed in three colors against the dark clouds. A vortex of wind had formed and was drawing the clouds inward.

At Devon's observation post, several guards stared at the sky. They were too far away to see how Ethan was fighting Deoxys, or even which Pokémon he had sent out. But they had no doubt that the vast bank of black clouds was his doing.

“He's incredible...” a young guard said. “Those clouds appeared out of nowhere. Is that what a tournament champion can do?”

“Don't be ridiculous,” the team leader said, giving him a sideways look. “A Pokémon League tournament winner is stronger than an average competitor, and luckier too, but could one normally make all this? A small patch of cloud, perhaps. This...”

He looked up again. “A change in the weather on this scale means those aren't ordinary Pokémon. It has to be them. Only they could do this.”

The young guard swallowed. “You mean Legendary Pokémon? Can someone really find and catch them, then bring them into a battle?”

“Who knows? People aren't all alike. It's a big world. Things beyond anything we can imagine must happen every day.”

“I still can't believe someone could encounter a Legendary Pokémon so easily.”

“You can't?” The leader shook his head. “What about Deoxys? Most people have never seen one. We've been fighting it for the past two days. Isn't that proof enough?”

“I suppose it is...”

Above the clouds, Moltres, Zapdos, and Articuno flew with the rolling vapor as though performing a strange dance. With each beat of their wings, energy flowed from their bodies into the cloud mass.

Below them, Deoxys sensed the danger. Perhaps it could see in the clouds the powerful magnetic fields that now threatened it. It brought all four arms together and fired an energy ball larger and stronger than the first one into the sky.

The ball raced toward the Legendary birds. But they were far enough away to see it coming, and dodged with ease. It exploded in the clouds, scattering a broad swath of them. More clouds rushed in to fill the gap. The attack had achieved nothing.

Then the birds' dance stopped. Tri-Bird Assault was ready. This would be only the second time they had shown it before other people.

Water, lightning, and fire particles broke apart, reformed, and joined in the clouds. A brilliant column of energy, more than ten meters across and wider still as it poured downward, fell like a waterfall from the sky. Its radiance lit the dark day like a sun beneath the black clouds.

Deoxys hovered at the center of the attack's path. The glare forced it to shield its eyes with an arm. Most Pokémon would have fled from a strike they could not withstand. Deoxys did not move. It meant to take the attack with its own strength.

It was stubborn, at least.

Deoxys changed from Attack Forme to Defense Forme. Its body grew heavier, as though sheathed in thick armor. Then it raised its arms before its chest and formed another spherical barrier.

The barrier had barely appeared when Tri-Bird Assault reached it. Energy crashed down like a wave, engulfing Deoxys completely. The next instant, an explosion sent a terrible mushroom cloud rising from the ground.

The earth shook. A shock wave swept all the way to Ethan and Misty. On the peak, Misty ducked behind Ethan. Gardevoir and Gengar protected them both.

Once the blast passed, Ethan peered out at the devastation. He had seen this attack once before, yet its destructive power still astonished him.

“Deoxys couldn't have died in that, could it?” Misty asked.

“Died? No. It's not that fragile. I'm wondering whether it was injured at all.”

“Not injured?” Misty stared at him. “How could anything withstand an attack like that?”

Back at the observation post, Devon's guards still gaped at the distant blast. A violent gust blew dust into their open mouths. Before they could spit it out, the ground trembled beneath them and they nearly lost their footing. When the shock finally subsided, they cleared their mouths and hurried to look toward the battlefield again.

“C-c-captain!”

“Quiet! I see it!”

Before, they had only suspected Ethan had a Legendary Pokémon. Now they were certain. No ordinary Pokémon could cause an explosion like that. Only human technology—or a Legendary Pokémon—could do it.

Even that immense explosion finally faded. A great crater had opened beside the lake, and water poured into it. The last smoke drifted away from its center. Before they could make out what lay behind it, a pair of cold eyes opened.

Misty looked hard. There was Deoxys, covered in wounds, with only half its body remaining.

“It...” She stopped, horrified by its condition. Then the missing parts began to grow back before her eyes.

Rapid regeneration! In only a few breaths, Deoxys had restored itself. It looked just as it had before the attack.

“That's unbelievable,” Misty said. “It really survived that hit.”

Ethan nodded, his face impassive. This was what made Deoxys so formidable.

Was Tri-Bird Assault strong? Unquestionably. Three Legendary Pokémon combined their power in a technique far greater than the sum of three separate attacks. Even other Legendary Pokémon would have reason to avoid it.

He believed Mewtwo, Ho-Oh, Groudon, and Kyogre could survive a direct hit, though each would be hurt to a different degree. Perhaps none but Arceus—not even Eternamax Eternatus or the ice pillar Regice—would come through it as well as Deoxys had.

Defense Forme, the energy barrier, and changes within its DNA and cells had all reduced the force that reached its body. But those defenses were secondary. Deoxys's real advantage was its regeneration.

As long as its core remained intact, it could eventually recover from almost any injury, even the destruction of its head, limbs, or body. Sometimes that took seconds; at other times it might take three or four years. It would return whole, without a scar on the surface, though the process cost it a great deal of mental energy.

Every Pokémon had its distinguishing traits. Deoxys, born from a mutated virus from space, had a capacity to heal that seemed to defy reason.

That did not mean it could not be defeated or killed. If it suffered enough attacks in a short time, its mental energy would run out. That was the possibility Ethan was counting on. He could keep attacking until Deoxys could no longer endure it.

He looked up at the three Legendary birds. Tri-Bird Assault had drained most of their energy. They could still fight, but they could no longer carry the battle. Ethan had never intended to depend on them alone.

He reached into his pouch and took out two more Great Balls. Misty knew what they held: Suicune and Raikou. She marveled at how many powerful Pokémon Ethan could call upon. His second wave of attacks was about to begin.

But before he could release them, something unexpected happened.

Thirty or forty people emerged along a ridge to the left of the battlefield. Their matching orange-red uniforms marked them as Team Magma. Ethan recognized the hooded young woman leading them: Courtney.

That was not all. A second group appeared on a ridge to his right, more than fifty people dressed in blue and white stripes. Team Aqua had arrived too. At their head stood Shelly, whom Ethan also knew.

Wonderful. Everyone he had hoped to avoid had shown up. Not long ago, he had been the one disrupting their operations. Now they had come to make trouble for him.

Ethan lowered the two Great Balls and watched coldly.

Shelly surveyed the battlefield with a hand on her hip and a smug smile. “Well, well. What have we found? A Pokémon we've never seen before, three Legendary birds, and one irritating rat. What an unexpected treat.”

Courtney said nothing. She turned and fixed Ethan with a cold stare.

After he had disrupted their plans repeatedly, both teams had investigated him. They knew he was a two-time tournament champion from Kanto and Johto, and they had learned just how powerful he was. Courtney intended to settle their scores today.

Ethan felt their undisguised hostility and smiled. “I'm rather busy. Could you leave?”

Shelly regarded him with an amused half-smile. “What do you think?”

“Dream on,” Courtney said flatly.

“So there's no room to negotiate?” Ethan spread his hands.

“Of course there is.” Shelly licked her lips, danger flashing in her eyes. “Once I've caught you, we can negotiate exactly how I should punish you.”

Before Ethan could answer, Courtney said, “That won't happen. He's mine.”

“Yours?” Shelly turned toward her with a laugh. “You'd better leave while you can. Both the man and that Pokémon are already Team Aqua's.”

She had reason for her confidence. Team Aqua had brought more people and Pokémon than Team Magma. She had also seen Ethan and Deoxys fighting. They had been at it for some time, and both sides appeared depleted. To Shelly, the advantage was hers.

Three sides now faced one another, each determined to defeat the others and claim whatever could be won here. Yet both teams had underestimated something.

Surrounded by them all, Deoxys had had enough.

Blue light shot from its core into the sky. A vast blue aurora appeared from nowhere and spread over most of the battlefield. Strange, winding curtains of light hung down. It was beautiful.

Everyone looked up instinctively. At the same moment, Deoxys changed from Defense Forme back to Attack Forme and began to attack without distinguishing friend from foe.

An energy ball formed at the end of each of its four arms. With a slight motion, Deoxys launched them toward Ethan, the three Legendary birds, Team Magma, and Team Aqua.

It would defeat them all.
