Beneath the moonlight, Charizard glided in from the distance and finally hovered above the Temple of the Sea.

Holding Maya in his arms, Ethan leaped from Charizard's back and landed steadily on the balcony.

The curtains billowed in the wind. Shards from the broken window still lay scattered across the floor, reflecting faintly in the moonlight.

The tireless rush of the whirlpool continued nearby, but otherwise the surroundings were utterly silent. If not for the wreckage left by the battle, who could have imagined that so much had happened here in such a short time?

To keep Maya's soft, bare feet from being cut by the broken glass, Ethan had no choice but to carry her into the room.

The shards cracked beneath his shoes and splintered into even smaller pieces.

After gently placing Maya on the bed, Ethan finally breathed a sigh of relief.

He had not actually done much beyond riding Charizard back and forth, but anyone forced to remain awake this late and travel so far would inevitably feel exhausted in both body and mind.

Fortunately, the incident had finally come to an end.

"All right, you're safe here now." He smiled. "Team Rocket has been driven off, so they probably won't come back to trouble you again anytime soon."

"So those people were Team Rocket..." Maya nodded dejectedly. "I've heard their name many times, but today was my first encounter with them. I never expected that they would target not only the God of the Sea, but also the scepter in my hands."

She looked at Ethan with a solemn expression. "I know I've already said it, but I must thank you again. If you hadn't been there, the consequences might have been unthinkable..."

"You truly don't need to be so polite." Ethan shook his head. "My Gengar happened to notice them while it was wandering around. Otherwise, I never would've known they'd infiltrated the temple. Once I saw them, of course I couldn't simply stand aside."

He spoke with complete confidence, apparently forgetting that he had hesitated for quite a while before leaving his room.

From another perspective, if Ethan had never interfered, Team Rocket would have simply stolen the scepter and left. They would never have abducted Maya. She could then have awakened the others, and with Ash and his friends' ability to spring into action, recovering the scepter would have been easy. Maya would never have been placed in danger.

If events had unfolded that way, matters might actually have been much simpler.

Regardless, everything had already happened and reached a satisfactory conclusion. There was no need to dwell on possibilities that had never come to pass.

Both of them fell silent, and the atmosphere in the room suddenly felt somewhat strange.

After everything that had just happened, Ethan noticed that the way Maya looked at him had changed.

It was difficult to describe. Her gaze contained something that reminded him both of Sabrina in the past and Erika now.

He did not dare remain for long. After carrying Maya for so long and feeling her soft body against his own, he needed to step outside and calm himself down.

"It's already very late. You should have someone clean this up and then get some proper rest."

After hurriedly leaving those words behind, Ethan turned and walked away, presenting Maya with a dashing view of his back.

Behind him, Maya remained silent. She merely watched him with an unusual look in her eyes until he disappeared beyond the door.

...

Early the next morning, while everyone ate breakfast in the great hall, Maya briefly told the oblivious Ash and the others how Team Rocket had attacked her the previous night.

She glossed over the specific details and said little about how she had been rescued.

Only then did Ash and his friends learn what had happened during the night!

"Those Team Rocket creeps are horrible! How dare they attack Miss Maya at night!" Brock angrily slapped the table, then immediately changed his tone and asked with concern, "Miss Maya, were you hurt?"

"I'm fine." Maya shook her head and glanced at Ethan without drawing attention to the gesture. "Mr. Ethan Vale rescued me in time, so the incident never became any more serious."

Hearing Maya mention him, Ethan lifted another forkful of food to his mouth and stared straight ahead as though she were speaking about someone else entirely.

Despite the enormous commotion the previous night, Ash and the others had not noticed a thing. It could only be said that the Temple of the Sea was truly immense. The roar of the whirlpool had also prevented the sounds of Ethan's battle with Team Rocket from reaching their rooms.

Erika's gaze moved repeatedly between Ethan and Maya. With a woman's intuition, she sensed that the events of the previous night were probably not quite as simple as Maya had described.

Yet both of them behaved perfectly normally, revealing nothing at all.

Once the group finished breakfast, Maya personally escorted them outside the temple's main entrance.

Looking at the group as they prepared to depart, Maya spoke gravely. "Last night's events made me realize that the threat from Team Rocket has become urgent. Stealing my scepter may have been only one part of their plan. We must resolve this as soon as possible. We cannot allow them to capture the God of the Sea!"

"Leave it to us." This time, not only Brock but Ash and Misty also made the same promise. "Don't worry, Miss Maya. We absolutely won't let them succeed!"

Seeing their confidence, Maya finally smiled.

"I believe in you. I'll wait here for your good news!"

After leaving the Temple of the Sea, the group went to the harbor and found a small boat bound for Silver Rock Isle.

According to the clues Maya had provided, the God of the Sea had last appeared in the waters near Silver Rock Isle.

Silver Rock Isle was one of the Whirl Islands' four large islands. Located in the northeast, it was relatively small, nowhere near the size of Red Rock Isle, which Ethan and the others had just left.

Kasado City was the only city on Silver Rock Isle. The island had received its name from the silver rock found there in abundance. It was said that emblems made from silver rock offered some protection and brought their owners good fortune. Unfortunately, their fame also meant that many people in the city fashioned counterfeit silver rock into products for sale.

The group encountered trouble almost as soon as they departed the harbor.

The sea was far from calm that day, and the sky was rather overcast. Strong winds and waves made the small boat pitch constantly.

Apart from Ash and Misty, Ethan, Erika, and Brock all grew more or less dizzy from the motion. Ethan eventually could not bear it any longer. He simply released Charizard and had it carry Erika and him through the sky, which finally eased their nausea.

Fortunately, the two islands were close together. After traveling east for only two or three hours, the boat brought them to the waters near Silver Rock Isle.

As soon as they arrived, the weather abruptly changed. The clouds vanished, and the day became calm and sunny once more.
