Hooooonk!

With a blast of its horn, a small passenger ship slowly pulled into the harbor at Cherrygrove City in the Johto region.

Cherrygrove was known as the city where the scent of flowers drifted through the air. Despite its favorable seaside location, it was small and its economy had never developed much. It had only a modest dock, with just a few regular boats coming and going each day.

Fortunately, a direct route connected it to the Orange Islands' big city. Ethan had been able to make the journey without changing ships.

He and Sabrina disembarked. The moment his feet touched land, Ethan stretched his whole body.

"Ahh! More than thirty hours on a boat. I'm exhausted!"

Sabrina shot him a look. "Exhausted? Other than a few battles with the Trainers aboard, didn't you spend most of the trip lying in bed? How can you be tired?"

Ethan worked the soreness out of his back and grinned. "That's why I'm tired. A little ship like that rocks with every wave. You can't get a decent night's sleep. It only leaves you feeling worse."

Gengar poked its head out of Ethan's shadow. It looked around and sniffed the air. "So this is Johto? It's my first time here, but the air and the buildings don't seem any different from Kanto's!"

"They don't look very different," Ethan agreed. "The two regions are on the same landmass. The Indigo Plateau sits between them, so they're treated as separate regions. Even the Johto League is based on the Indigo Plateau, just like Kanto's."

Gengar shook its head. "Then why not put them together? The Indigo League, the Silver League... all these names are such a bother."

Ethan patted its head, amused. "Come on. They're still two regions. Pay attention and you'll notice plenty of differences. Johto's air is clearly damper than Kanto's, and it has a more rural feel. Buddhism and Shinto are also very prominent here. The local customs aren't the same at all."

"Huh?" Gengar gave him a strange look. "How do you know all that?"

Ethan spread his hands. "I wasn't only sleeping on the boat. I leafed through a travel guide when I got bored. That's where I learned it."

There was more he had not said. He vaguely remembered that when the Pokémon games' maps were designed, Kanto was modeled on Japan's real Kanto region, while Johto drew on Kansai and part of Shikoku. Their roots really did lie close together.

But that knowledge touched on his secret of coming from another world. He could hardly reveal it casually. Sabrina was his girlfriend, and Gengar was his Pokémon; both were utterly loyal and probably would not mind knowing. Still, the fewer people who knew, the better.

As they talked, they walked into Cherrygrove itself.

It was an ordinary city in southern Johto with no Gym of its own. True to its name, its greatest charms were the scent of flowers and the sea breeze. Flower beds stood throughout the city, filled with lovely blooms. Most were shades of red or pink. Ethan knew too little about plants to name many of them.

Erika would recognize them all if she were here, wouldn't she?

He and Sabrina followed the streets past a few quiet high-rise buildings toward the Pokémon Center. The residents seemed to take life slowly, and hardly any vehicles passed them.

Now that Ethan had reached Johto, his first order of business was to register for its League challenge. Until his information was entered, he could not challenge the Gyms here or compete in the Silver Conference.

They crossed at a zebra crossing and entered the Pokémon Center. At the sound of the door, Nurse Joy looked up from the papers she was sorting at the front desk. She stood with a smile.

"Welcome! How may I help you?"

Ethan had long since given up wondering why the Joys and Officer Jennys could be found everywhere. Wherever he went, there they were.

"Hello, Nurse Joy. I'd like to register for the Silver League challenge."

"Certainly. May I have your Pokédex?" She held out her hand.

Ethan gave it to her and watched her connect it to the computer. While he waited, he looked around the Pokémon Center.

Compared with those in Kanto, it had nothing unusual about it. The decor and facilities were much the same, and even Nurse Joy's uniform looked identical. Apart from Ethan and Sabrina, only one other Trainer was there, eating at a table. This quiet little city really was out of the way.

At the computer, Nurse Joy saw his records and covered her mouth in surprise. "Oh! You're the Indigo League champion? I didn't expect you to come challenge our Silver League! Let me see... You just won the Orange League trophy too? Two trophies in such a short time! That's amazing!"

"You're too kind. My partners did the hard work," Ethan said modestly. "It wasn't really down to me."

"Now you're being too modest." Nurse Joy laughed behind her hand. "How could a Pokémon's growth have nothing to do with its Trainer?"

When the registration was complete, she returned his Pokédex and gave him a new case for collecting badges.

"Welcome to the Silver League! Do your best. I'm rooting for you, and I hope you win the Silver Conference too!"

Even Nurse Joy thought so highly of him. Ethan smiled and shook his head as he left the Center.

His second order of business was to contact Looker. He remembered Looker's earlier call, asking him to get in touch as soon as he reached Johto so he could help deal with Team Rocket there. So much time had passed that Ethan hoped he had not delayed anything important.

He and Sabrina found a restaurant. Over lunch, Ethan called Looker. The phone rang for a long time before the call was answered.

Before Ethan could speak, Looker asked, "Hello? Have you reached Johto?"

"Just arrived. I called you as soon as I could."

"Excellent!" Looker sounded delighted. "We've just finished clearing out Team Rocket in Kanto. We're moving our people into Johto now and should have everyone in place within a day or two. I was about to ask for your help!"

Ethan's eyebrow twitched. "What exactly do you need me to do?"

"Take down the Team Rocket bases with the heaviest defenses, of course." Hearing Ethan's tone, Looker laughed. Then he lowered his voice conspiratorially. "I'm in New Bark Town on a special assignment. If you have time, come meet me here first."

"New Bark Town?"

Ethan froze for a moment. Wasn't that right near Cherrygrove City? What a coincidence!

"All right. Wait for me there. I should be able to reach you by tonight!"
