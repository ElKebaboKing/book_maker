Ethan naturally saw the electricity surrounding Chinchou, but he remained unmoved. He merely watched calmly as Blastoise slammed toward it!

Compared with Chinchou's small body, Blastoise was like a heavy truck charging forward with unstoppable force. Chinchou could never have dodged in time, and besides, it had no intention of trying.

The instant Blastoise struck, Chinchou's entire body flew seven or eight meters backward. It had no room to struggle before crashing into the water and throwing up layer after layer of spray.

The attack worked exactly as expected, but Blastoise suffered as well. Electricity from Chinchou coursed into it, leaving Blastoise standing in place with pain across its face. It had clearly taken far more damage than Chinchou.

Worse still, the electricity had apparently paralyzed Blastoise, greatly reducing its speed!

Under the present circumstances, that only made a bad situation worse.

Several seconds later, Chinchou's head emerged halfway from the water. It shook its body violently to clear the dizziness from the collision, then looked toward Blastoise as its two bulb-like antennae began rapidly gathering electricity!

Ethan shook his head. Before Chinchou could form the electrical attack, he took out a Poké Ball and recalled Blastoise.

"Oh? Faced with this unfavorable situation, Ethan has chosen not to persist and has voluntarily withdrawn his Pokémon!" The announcer's voice resounded across the battlefield, his excitement unmistakable. "Is it coming!? Is it finally coming? Has someone finally forced Ethan to reveal his second Pokémon!?"

No further encouragement was necessary. Trinity, the spectators, the announcer, the Sea Priestess, Erika, Misty, Ash, Brock, and everyone else focused their attention on Ethan!

At that moment, every eye in the colosseum was upon him!

In the Trainer's command area, Ethan lowered his head and silently returned Blastoise's Poké Ball to the pouch at his waist. Then he drew a deep breath and closed his hand around another Poké Ball that he had prepared long ago.

Since so many people want to see you, come on. Show the world your most ferocious side!

He hurled the Poké Ball with all his strength. Blue light flashed, and beneath the gaze of the entire audience, an enormous red Gyarados appeared, floating upon the water!

This was the first time it had entered the public eye since evolving into a red Gyarados!

Before anyone could even react to its immense size, a deafening roar erupted across the arena!

"ROAR!"

The sound overflowed with dominance, exploding beside everyone's ears as though it could shake heaven and earth. Most of the spectators instinctively covered their ears!

What astonishing presence!

Compared with an ordinary Gyarados, Ethan's Pokémon was unquestionably far more intimidating!

The cameramen closest to the battlefield felt it most keenly. The sudden roar startled them so badly that their bodies jerked, and several nearly dropped the cameras in their hands!

The announcer remained stunned for a long moment before finally returning to his senses and hurriedly beginning his introduction.

"Here it is! As expected, Ethan has sent out his Gyarados! This Pokémon performed brilliantly during both the Indigo League Conference and the Orange League. What kind of performance will it show us today...? Huh? Wait... Why is it red!?"

Only halfway through his introduction did the announcer notice that something was wrong. He frantically flipped through the information in his hands. Ethan had used Gyarados during both the Indigo League and the Orange Islands' Winner's Cup, but the records stated clearly that it had been an ordinary blue Gyarados. Yet this one...

"How strange... As far as I know, Ethan's Gyarados wasn't Shiny. Could this be one he caught recently? B-but... Other than their color, aren't Shiny Pokémon identical to ordinary members of their species? Why is this Gyarados so enormous!?"

The announcer was full of questions, but clearly no one could answer them. In fact, every spectator who knew something about Ethan was wondering the same thing.

Countless wondrous things happened in this world every day, but no one had ever heard of an ordinary Pokémon suddenly becoming Shiny!

Even if its color somehow changed, that would hardly make its body grow to such an outrageous size!

Moments ago, everyone had been filled with anticipation. Now their mouths hung open in astonishment, as though that alone could express the depth of their shock.

After releasing the red Gyarados, Ethan lowered his hand and stood calmly in place.

The spectators could not understand, but he knew that his red Gyarados was entirely different from a Shiny Gyarados. Its crimson scales represented even more ferocious power—and the finest product of Team Rocket's biotechnology!

Even from a distance, the red Gyarados inspired awe in everyone who saw it. But the person under the greatest pressure was undoubtedly Ethan's opponent, Trinity!

Standing in the command area on the opposite side, she felt her entire body turn cold beneath Gyarados's enormous red gaze. Every hair on her body stood on end!

What was this feeling?

Trinity owned a Gyarados herself and believed she knew the species well. Yet staring at the creature before her, she could only wonder what sort of monster it was.

Was this truly an ordinary Shiny Gyarados?

It was two or three times larger than her own Gyarados. Its voice and oppressive presence were both suffocating. Even every breath it took felt like the Grim Reaper gently dragging a scythe across her neck...

Goose bumps covered Trinity's body, and she shuddered. At that moment, an unexpected movement startled her back to awareness.

She looked down and discovered that Chinchou was trembling!

What? What's happening?

Is it frightened too?

As the red Gyarados's first target, Chinchou was shaking even more violently than its Trainer!

It had to tilt its head back merely to see its opponent's enormous face. Chinchou's entire body was not even as large as one of Gyarados's eyes...

"Chinchou, pull yourself together!"

Trinity called out several times. She was certain Chinchou had heard her, but its fear did not ease in the slightest. Her Pokémon even appeared ready to flee!

How can this be happening!?

Trinity understood that such a reaction meant Gyarados must possess the Intimidate Ability. Beyond that, the undisguised killing intent pouring from its body seemed almost tangible and was probably an even greater reason!

My goodness...

How could anyone possibly raise a Pokémon like this?
