The venue for the Whirl Cup's main tournament was different from the preliminary grounds. It was a vast, circular aquatic colosseum built in a distinctly ancient Greek style. No one knew how many years its every brick and stone had endured, giving the entire structure a profound sense of history.

The colosseum could hold twenty thousand spectators. Flowing seawater filled its center, and more than a dozen stone pillars of varying heights and sizes rose above the surface, providing places for both Trainers and Pokémon to stand.

Compared with the perfunctory preliminaries, the opening ceremony for the main tournament was undoubtedly grander. Fireworks and ceremonial cannons sounded in unison, while an elegant and beautiful priestess holding a scepter presided over the event!

By nine in the morning, not a single seat remained empty. Countless spectators had arrived early and were waiting in eager anticipation.

Ethan, Ash, Misty, and the other sixty-one competitors entered the colosseum across the sea aboard more than a dozen long, narrow wooden boats. As the surrounding crowd cheered and encouraged them, the competitors stood straight and did their best to present themselves with energy and confidence.

The audience greeted them with thunderous applause and cheers.

Amid that atmosphere, the male announcer's deep, clear voice began echoing over the colosseum, signaling the official beginning of the opening ceremony!

"Ladies and gentlemen, look! These are the competitors who survived the preliminary round of this triennial tournament and now strive to become a Sea Hero! Since ancient times, the Whirl Islands have possessed their own distinct culture and civilization. We revere Water-type Pokémon and have lived alongside them to this very day! That culture has never disappeared, and Maya, the Sea Priestess who has protected these traditions since antiquity, continues to pass it on!"

Ethan had only learned after becoming an official competitor last night that the Whirl Cup was more than a Pokémon tournament. In keeping with an ancient tradition, it would also choose a Sea Hero to participate in this ceremony!

As he listened to the introduction, Ethan turned once more toward the ceremonial platform in the distance.

It was not very far away, so he could see the priestess clearly. She wore a long blue dress and carried a golden scepter. Her long purple hair drifted gently in the wind. Even without considering her features, her bearing alone was already noble and elegant.

Ethan had already experienced a ceremony like this once on Shamouti Island in the Orange Islands, where he had also met the local festival maiden. This spectacle therefore did not feel particularly unfamiliar.

It's only a formality, he thought.

When Ash and Misty beside him noticed Ethan turn his head, they followed his gaze and also saw the Sea Priestess.

Misty pressed both hands to her cheeks, filled with envy. "She's so beautiful!"

Ethan could not tell whether she envied the priestess's elegance, the attention of the enormous crowd, or something else entirely.

The Sea Priestess truly was beautiful, but Misty had always considered herself the world's most beautiful girl. She was certainly no ugly duckling. She was simply still young and had not yet fully grown into her looks. When she reached the priestess's age, Ethan believed she would be every bit as striking.

At that moment, the announcer spoke again. "And now, please welcome Sea Priestess Maya for the tournament declaration!"

As his voice faded, Maya stepped forward. She raised her scepter and declared loudly, "Everyone, the triennial celebration of Water-type Pokémon has begun. On behalf of the Whirl Islands, I welcome you all! Here, we live in harmony with Water-type Pokémon and understand their hearts. Since ancient times, those who mastered the sea alongside their Pokémon have been celebrated as Sea Heroes, and every Sea Hero receives the blessing of the Sea Spirit, granting them the ability to understand the hearts of all Water-type Pokémon!"

The Sea Spirit? Understanding their hearts?

Ethan's gaze shifted to the scepter in Maya's hand.

The scepter was made of pure gold and gleamed beneath the sunlight. At its tip sat a round blue gemstone the size of a fist. The gem radiated blue light and was clearly no ordinary object.

"And so, I wish every competitor success and the chance to receive the blessing of the Sea Spirit!"

As Maya raised the scepter high, the Sea Spirit erupted with dazzling light. The glow was gentle rather than blinding, and it enveloped the entire colosseum!

Ethan and all the others seeing such a phenomenon for the first time stared in surprise.

So this was the Sea Spirit...

The light fell across Ethan and painted everyone pale blue. It produced no other visible effect, but even Ethan could not tell whether it was merely stagecraft or the blue gemstone truly possessed some wondrous power!

This world was far from ordinary, after all. It would hardly be strange if blessings like this genuinely existed.

At the very least, judging from the audience's increasingly excited and fervent reaction, they seemed to believe the competitors in the colosseum had already become somehow extraordinary!

Behind Ethan, Ash raised both hands and stared at the blue light upon them. "Did we really receive a blessing?" he murmured.

"Are you an idiot?" Misty rolled her eyes at him. "Didn't the priestess just say that only the final Sea Hero receives the blessing? This light is only a demonstration of that gemstone's power!"

"Huh? Is that how it works?" Only then did Ash fully understand the rules. He sighed in disappointment. "Aw, man. I like the title of Sea Hero, but it's only an empty title. If you win the tournament, don't you get anything else?"

Misty sighed helplessly. "Come on! Did you even read the tournament schedule beforehand? After the final, the winner receives a generous cash prize along with the title—and even a Mystic Water pendant! That's the real reward. But... hmph! None of it has anything to do with you. Ethan is definitely going to win!"

Misty was Ethan's most devoted supporter. Whenever he entered a tournament or battle, she backed him blindly and without hesitation!

Faced with Misty's extravagant praise and her relentless temptation of fate on his behalf, Ethan could only smile and shake his head.

He did not stop her, however. Winning was his goal as well. Now that he had entered, he intended to become the champion. What kind of Trainer competed without wanting to win?

Ash also knew how powerful Ethan was, but he naturally refused to accept Misty belittling him. Fuming, he began arguing with her.

As Ethan listened to the two bickering behind him, his thoughts returned to the Mystic Water.

Just as Misty had said, the winner would receive a Mystic Water in addition to the prize money. It was a wondrous stone that strengthened a Pokémon's Water-type moves when worn, making it a genuinely valuable reward!
