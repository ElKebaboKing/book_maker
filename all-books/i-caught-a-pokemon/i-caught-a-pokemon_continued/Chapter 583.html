After fleeing the battle, Ethan and the others lost the path back to Arborville and ran deeper into the forest. Only when they reached a lake did they realize they no longer knew which way to go.

Forest stretched in every direction. The trees were so thick with branches and leaves that every view looked the same.

“Are we lost?” Ash scratched his head.

“That’s all right,” Brock said. “We ran without any clear destination. Even that Pokémon hunter will have a hard time tracking us.”

The sun was sinking. Ethan looked at his exhausted companions and decided against pressing on. “We’ve come a long way. Vicious shouldn’t catch up with us for now. Let’s camp here tonight.”

No one objected. At the news that they could rest, Misty dropped onto a nearby rock and gasped for breath.

She was drenched in sweat, her clothes soaked through. Even if she wanted to keep running, her legs would not carry her.

They had been on the move since morning, going back and forth between the village and forest and then fighting a hard battle. Everyone was hungry.

At Brock’s direction, their Pokémon searched for branches and berries while he began preparing dinner.

Ethan sent out Jolteon and Eevee and stationed them at hidden points on opposite sides of the camp. They would warn everyone if Vicious appeared. Then he returned to Erika.

With each person tending to a task, the camp took shape quickly. They built a fire and set out their tents and sleeping bags.

Outside the tent she shared with Ethan, Erika had given Celebi emergency treatment. It had rested for some time, and its breathing was steady. It no longer seemed to be in danger.

“How is it?” Ethan asked as he sat beside her.

“It’s all right. I think it’ll wake soon.” Erika looked at him and lowered her voice. “You want to catch it, don’t you?”

“Huh?” Ethan raised his eyebrows. “You figured that out?”

Erika smiled. “You’ve made it obvious. You’ve been sneaking looks at Celebi all day. I can tell what you’re thinking.”

Ethan stared for a moment, then laughed.

After so many nights together, they knew each other intimately. Just as he had grown familiar with every inch of her, she had learned to read his small gestures and hidden wishes.

“What about you?” he asked. “Celebi is a Grass- and Psychic-type Mythical Pokémon. The Grass typing suits you perfectly.”

“Me?” Erika stroked Celebi’s large head. “Of course I like it. But liking a Pokémon doesn’t mean I have to catch it. And I know you want it more than I do. Why would I try to take that chance from you?”

Her unexpected kindness touched him. If the others had not been nearby, he would have kissed her deeply.

He slipped an arm around her. “Whether you catch it or I do hardly matters. What’s yours is mine, and what’s mine is yours.”

They did not know that Misty, sitting close enough to hear, was shaking her head at their sugary exchange.

She was not jealous of their relationship. She understood how they felt about each other and had long since accepted it. She just found their whispered conversation a little funny.

She knew she was still a minor and could not compete with Erika for Ethan’s attention on equal terms. She glanced down at her undeveloped figure and sighed.

If she wanted to win Ethan over, she would have to wait until she was grown up.

The previous night, she had not understood why Ethan had suddenly taken out the GS Ball. The moment she saw Celebi today, it all made sense. Ethan was going to try to catch it.

Misty did not know how he had anticipated finding Celebi here, but like Erika, she did not mind his wish to catch it. She would support the person she cared about.

And if Celebi were going to be caught, she could think of no one more worthy of such a rare Mythical Pokémon than Ethan.

The smell of dinner drifted across the camp. When Brock called them over, nearly everyone appeared at once.

Wearing an apron, Brock served each person like a devoted parent. He was about to put the pot back over the fire when he noticed Celebi hovering beside the table.

“Celebi? You’re awake?”

Everyone turned. Celebi, who should still have been asleep, was in the air beside the food, sniffing eagerly at the plates with its tiny nose.

It moved with such ease that it seemed fully recovered.

“It must be hungry,” Misty said. “Brock’s cooking smelled so good it woke up.”

Ash laughed. “You’re a little glutton, aren’t you?”

It made sense. Celebi had lived in this forest eating berries every day. However sweet they were, it had probably grown tired of them. Now the scent of cooked food was irresistible.

Brock filled a plate for Celebi. The instant he set it down, it dove in and began eating with great enthusiasm.

Seeing Celebi enjoy it made the others hungrier. They turned to their own plates.

For a while, no one spoke. They were too busy filling their stomachs. The only sounds were utensils tapping against plates.

When Celebi finished, it licked its lips and fixed its eyes on Brock.

Brock shook his head helplessly and gave it another full plate.

After the meal, Celebi seemed to have forgotten its fear of them entirely. With its stomach round from food, it flew curiously from person to person. It showed none of its earlier caution and made no attempt to leave.

Its nature seemed innocent and curious about humans. It was nothing like the fierce Legendary or Mythical Pokémon Ethan had met, or those that only hid from people.

Despite their sleepiness, Ash and the others played with Celebi for a while. Eventually they could stay awake no longer. Yawning, they crawled into their sleeping bags.

Ethan and Erika went into their tent and soon fell asleep. Celebi, however, remained outside. The lively camp had gone quiet so quickly that it felt lonely.

The moon hung high over the still lake. Celebi looked up at its light and thought about how much these humans had changed its life in such a short time.

It had never experienced a battle like that, or tasted food so delicious.

Light footsteps sounded nearby. Jolteon emerged from the trees.

Without looking at Celebi, it held its head high and scanned the camp. Once it was satisfied there was no danger, it turned and set off in the other direction.

Celebi fluttered its delicate wings and followed.

It had never met Jolteon, but its scent told Celebi that it belonged with the humans. Pokémon raised by humans were unfamiliar to Celebi. It wondered how their lives differed from its own.

Jolteon threaded through the trees. It glanced once at Celebi behind it, then paid the visitor no further attention.

With a few leaps, it crossed the bushes and the stream that fed the lake, reaching the other side of camp.

This was Eevee’s assigned watch area. Jolteon searched for a long while before finding it asleep in a thicket.

Jolteon placed a forepaw on Eevee. To its surprise, Eevee did not wake. It grumbled, rolled over, and went right on sleeping.

Jolteon did not hesitate again. A quick electric shock served as a wake-up call.

Crackle!

Eevee jerked awake and leaped up in pain. Thinking it had been attacked, it prepared to fight back. Then it saw Jolteon and wilted like a plant after a frost.

Celebi hovered overhead, holding its belly as it laughed. It did not know what Eevee had done, but it was clearly in trouble.

Eevee noticed Celebi above it. Unlike the aloof Jolteon, it had an exuberant nature. It called softly to the newcomer, and they soon began playing together.

Jolteon shook its head at the sight. Evidently guarding the camp was a job it would have to do alone.

Leaving them to play, Jolteon resumed its patrol.

From Eevee, Celebi learned roughly what separated wild Pokémon from Pokémon who lived with humans.

Most obviously, the latter had a more comfortable life. They did not have to worry about finding food every day.

With wide, innocent eyes, Eevee explained that living with humans meant three meals a day. Someone would bathe it when it was dirty. It had a soft bed when it was tired, help with training and growing stronger, and freedom to do as it pleased.

Celebi remembered the food it had just eaten. It was so envious that its mouth watered.

Compared with the berries it had always known, human food was wonderful.

They all slept soundly through the night.

Morning light spread across the camp and drove away the darkness. A new day had begun.

When Ethan came out of his tent, he immediately saw Eevee and Celebi chasing each other through the camp.

He rubbed his eyes, unable to believe it. When had those two become friends?

He stepped back into the tent, lifted the flap, and looked out again. They were still playing. He had not imagined it.

“What is it?” Erika asked from behind him when he lingered at the entrance.

“Nothing. I’m just surprised Eevee didn’t keep watch properly. It neglected its post to play with Celebi.”

Fortunately, Ethan trusted Jolteon to have taken up the burden of patrolling through the night. Otherwise, a surprise attack from Vicious could have caught them helpless.

Eevee still needed more discipline.

Ethan had no idea what Eevee had told Celebi overnight. He called Eevee over and gave it a firm warning. Meanwhile, Brock rose, washed, and began making breakfast. Celebi soon appeared beside him, watching with eager anticipation.

“Hungry?” Brock asked gently. “Just wait a little. It’ll be ready soon.”

When the generous breakfast was served, Celebi gobbled it down. The others could only laugh.

Even Misty pressed a hand to her forehead. “It looks so cute. I never thought it would be such a glutton.”

After breakfast, they packed up and set off again.

To guard against another attack by Vicious, they decided to return to Arborville first. He and Team Rocket might hesitate to strike in a village full of people. The villagers could also help, leaving Ethan’s group less exposed than they had been yesterday.

Ash sent out Noctowl to fly above the canopy and find their direction. Following its guidance, they headed for the village.

The walk was tense. No one knew where Vicious was or when he might attack. The uncertainty kept them ready for a fight at any moment.

Celebi fluttered over their heads. They chatted now and then. The mood was hardly cheerful, but at least the journey was not silent.

As they drew closer to Arborville, the danger they had feared finally arrived.

Air whistled past the trees. Two long mechanical arms shot from behind them and snatched at Celebi in the air.

Everyone was ready this time. The Pokémon walking guard reacted as soon as the claws appeared.

Pikachu sprang high and struck one arm aside with a powerful Iron Tail.
