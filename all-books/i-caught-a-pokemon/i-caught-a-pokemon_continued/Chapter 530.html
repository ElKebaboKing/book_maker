"Why is Latios here?" Misty exclaimed.

Brock's eyes widened too, though even at their widest they still looked rather narrow. He nodded uncertainly. "It really is unbelievable. And... it looks angry!"

"You people!"

As they stared at Latios, an indignant girl's voice rang out behind them. They turned to see someone hurrying toward them who looked exactly like the girl Latias had become.

She carried a drawing board and wore a white hat. This was the human girl.

"Huh?" Ash looked from the red-haired girl standing in front of them to the one in the white hat behind them. "There are two of you? You look exactly alike!"

The girl in the hat glared at him. "How did you get in here? Are you thieves?"

"Of course not!" Ash shook his head quickly. "We're Pokémon Trainers from Pallet Town and other places. We followed this girl inside!"

Her gaze swept across Ethan and the others, then settled on Ash. Perhaps remembering his strange attempt to talk to her earlier, she said coldly, "You seem very suspicious. Latios, drive them out!"

Latios rose at her command, ready to attack. Then an elderly voice called from nearby.

"Stop!"

They turned again. Lorenzo, the guide they had met at the cathedral, was walking toward them.

The round, grandfatherly man approached the girl and Latios. "Bianca, Latios, the boy is telling the truth."

"But Grandpa..." Bianca began.

Lorenzo raised a hand to calm her, then looked at the other girl. "Latias, did you want to play with them?"

"Latias?"

Ash, Misty, and Brock followed his gaze to the girl. Why had the old guide called her Latias?

Could she really be Latias in disguise?

With everyone watching, Latias nodded. Then she took Ash by the hand and led him to the swing. They climbed onto it together.

She pressed Ash into the seat and stood behind him. With a gentle push, she sent the swing moving back and forth with both of them on it.

Then her appearance changed. The girl vanished, replaced by a Pokémon several sizes smaller than Latios, with a red back half.

"Whoa!"

Startled by her transformation, Ash jumped off the swing.

Misty and Brock stared, stunned. At last they understood why the girl had said nothing along the way. It was not that she had refused to speak. As a Pokémon, she could not speak human language.

Even Bianca murmured, watching Latias play with Ash, "She seems to like that boy."

Lorenzo nodded. "She's so happy. She really must have led them here herself."

With the misunderstanding cleared up, Latios went over to Pikachu and licked its wound. It seemed to be offering an affectionate apology for hurting it earlier.

Ash and Pikachu soon grew friendly with both Legendary Pokémon. Latias and Latios carried Ash through the air as they flew around the garden. Togepi, watching from Misty's arms, squirmed free and jumped onto Latios's back as well.

Ethan took the opportunity to tell Lorenzo and Bianca how they had rescued Latias from the two thieves.

"I see." Lorenzo was grateful. "Thank you for helping her."

Bianca looked apologetic too. "I'm sorry. My name is Bianca. I was too quick to judge you."

"It's all right," Ethan said, shaking his head. "If strangers appeared here, I'd try to drive them off to protect the Pokémon too."

At Lorenzo and Bianca's invitation, they went to sit nearby. On the way, Lorenzo explained, "For generations, our family has protected this Secret Garden for the Latios and Latias who visit the island. Their power created it, and it is their home while they're here."

"A secret garden for Legendary Pokémon," Brock said with a knowing nod. No wonder its entrance had been protected by a barrier.

At a table, Bianca made tea for everyone. "This Latios and Latias are brother and sister. Latios is the older brother, and Latias is the younger sister. She's much livelier than he is. She often takes on my appearance and goes out to explore the city."

Just then, both Pokémon's eyes glowed white. Latios dove into the water, and Latias came over to the group. As the light flickered in her eyes, the scenery around Ethan and the others changed.

They seemed to be underwater, surrounded by clear water. Everything looked so real that they felt as though they were actually there.

Seeing Ash's astonished face, Bianca smiled. "This is one of their powers. They can link their minds and let us experience what Latios is seeing. We call it dream projection."

"Dream projection?" Brock murmured. "So everything we're seeing now is really happening around Latios?"

"That's right," Lorenzo said. "I think they're doing this to welcome you."

Ethan stayed seated and took in the scene. He could feel solid ground under his feet and hear birds in the garden beside him, yet the view before his eyes was like a three-dimensional movie. It almost convinced him he was underwater.

After they had enjoyed it for a while, Latios returned to them, and the projection ended.

Perhaps no one had ever been invited inside by Latias before, or perhaps Bianca felt she owed them patience after her mistake. Either way, she led them over to some stone tablets and continued explaining.

"Long ago, Latias and Latios brought a power that could drive away darkness: a jewel called the Soul Dew. Legend says one was left on this island after they saved it. The two thieves you told us about may have tried to capture Latias because they're after that Soul Dew!"
