Ethan arrived with time to spare before the Contest. He could ask May and the others how things were going.

“How are your preparations?”

He looked at May. She showed little sign of nerves. Her travels through Hoenn had helped her grow. She was nothing like the girl who had entered her first Slateport Contest. Travel really did teach people a great deal.

“Everything's ready,” May said with an easy smile. “I still feel I could have done more, but I'll show everyone the best I can do.”

“Confidence is a good thing. Give it your best.”

Ash suddenly spoke up. “Ethan, guess who we saw yesterday.”

It was surely someone Ethan knew, but he had no clue who. He saw no point in wasting effort guessing. “Who?”

Ash was poor at keeping a secret anyway. “Harley!”

“He's come to Kanto too?” Ethan was not especially surprised. As he recalled from the anime, Harley had appeared in Kanto. “First Drew yesterday, and now Harley. What's drawing all these people from Hoenn here? Did he say anything?”

“Not much,” Ash said. “But he's helping out at the Pokémon Center.”

“Helping?” Ethan seemed indifferent, though he asked, “Does he work here?”

Max answered. “He said Nurse Joy helped him before, and he's ashamed of his past. That's why he's helping here. He said he wants to take a new step forward.”

“He's talking nonsense,” Ethan said with a snort. “He hasn't changed. Don't believe him, at least not yet.”

“Really?” Ash hesitated. “Yesterday he told us the Kanto Contest rules are different from Hoenn's, and Nurse Joy confirmed it. She said he's been working hard here, too.”

“So? He told you the truth about that. It doesn't mean he's kindhearted or has turned over a new leaf. He can give you a little piece of common knowledge to earn your trust, then use it for whatever he's planning.”

Ash trusted Ethan far more than he trusted Harley. He knew Ethan would not lie to him and accepted his judgment without reservation. “So we need to watch out for Harley?”

“Don't worry. He's on my blacklist. I'll stay with May, and whatever scheme he tries, it won't work.”

May, Ash, and the others relaxed once Ethan promised to step in. May was particularly touched that he would protect her. To them, Ethan was the strongest person they knew. Harley would get nowhere against him, even if he brought several accomplices. Nor would his tricks fool Ethan.

“By the way,” Ethan asked May, “have you registered yet?”

“I was just about to go over and do it.”

“Just about to?” Ethan raised an eyebrow and glanced at the clock. “It's after eight already. Doesn't registration close soon?”

“No, it closes at eleven. We have hours left.”

“Eleven?” Ethan frowned. “Isn't that when the Contest starts? Why would registration close at the exact moment it begins? Where did you hear that?”

“Harley told us,” Ash said. Then realization struck. “Wait. You mean he lied to us?”

“Honestly…” Ethan sighed at them. “How many times will you let him fool you? There's a whole group of you, and no one thought to check? Why would you take his word for something like that? You're even more gullible than Team Rocket.”

His words were harsh, and they flushed with embarrassment. Brock felt it especially keenly. He was the oldest and usually the steadiest, yet he had been there for all of it and still let Harley deceive them again.

Seeing their uneasy faces, Ethan shook his head. “I'm not trying to blame you. Just be more careful with someone like Harley. We can talk about it later. Let's go register.”

May and the others realized that was urgent. They hurried out of the Pokémon Center toward the Saffron Contest registration desk. A little more than ten minutes later, they arrived breathless.

The registration hall was empty except for one staff member behind the counter. May's heart sank. She stepped forward quickly, handed over her Contest pass, and asked about the deadline.

“Registration closes at ten. You still have more than an hour,” the staff member said with a smile. Then she returned May's pass apologetically. “But this pass is for Hoenn. It can't be used here. Please exchange it as soon as you can.”

“What?”

May had feared a problem with the deadline, but she had never imagined her pass would be invalid too. She was at a loss. Ethan spoke patiently with the staff member and learned that a Kanto pass could be issued at the Pokémon Center.

It was a simple fix, but it meant another round trip. They hurried back to the Center, exchanged May's pass, and returned. This time her registration went through.

When they finished, it was already nine fifteen. There was still some time, though far less than they had thought.

If Ethan had not urged them to come early, they would have trusted Harley's eleven o'clock deadline and missed the Contest entirely. Harley had not changed at all. His diligent work for Nurse Joy had only been a way to fool May again.

“Harley's awful!” Ash punched the air. “He lied to us! I won't let him off next time I see him.”

As though summoned by the words, Harley walked through the registration hall doors, humming a tune. He had registered already and had no reason to return. But after misleading May yesterday, he wanted to see her face when she failed to enter. He had timed his visit for that.

Instead, he found May standing by the counter. His expression changed at once. Hadn't he told her registration closed at eleven? Why was she here so early?

His eyes fell on the man beside her. Realizing Ethan might be responsible, Harley glared with deep resentment. The last person he wanted to meet had appeared after all. Ever since losing their previous match, he had tried to avoid Ethan. Now they were face to face again, and Ethan looked more confident than ever. Harley's envy only deepened.

Then Ethan spoke, nearly making him choke with anger.

“Well, if it isn't the man I beat. Come to finish the battle we left unfinished last time?” Ethan asked with a smile.

Harley's face darkened. He was about to answer with something sharp when May, Ash, and Max marched up to confront him.

“You actually dared show your face here? You liar!”

“Wait…” Though they had caught him, Harley spread his hands and feigned confusion. “What are you talking about? Why would I lie to you?”

“You're still denying it?” Max demanded. “You deliberately told us the wrong registration deadline! And you said May's pass would be fine!”

Harley's mind raced. “Did I? Then I must have remembered wrong. Please believe me. This wasn't my fault…”

He kept explaining, but May and the others no longer believed him. Still, as long as he insisted it was an honest mistake, Ash and the rest had no way to prove otherwise. They glared at him and left the registration hall.

Once they were gone, Harley's face darkened again. He had not expected May to escape his trap. His plan was ruined, and the same trick would be harder to use in the future.

Damn it. It was all that man's fault. Harley cursed Ethan under his breath.

There was no point in staying. He headed for the doors, still lost in thought, and collided with another group entering the hall.

The impact caught him unprepared. He fell hard on his backside, and his distinctive green leaf-shaped hat tumbled to the floor.

“You—” He opened his mouth to shout, but a woman on the other side beat him to it, louder and ruder than he had intended.

“What's wrong with you? Watch where you're going!”

Harley looked up. The speaker was a purple-haired woman in a long red dress and oversized butterfly glasses. Beside her stood a man dressed like an assistant and a Meowth in a suit and bow tie. What an odd group, dressed so extravagantly.

He studied them as he got to his feet. “Are you here for the Contest too?”

“Of course!” The woman lifted her chin and looked down her nose at him. “Don't you know who I am? The great and mysterious messenger of love and beauty, the fairy known as Jessibella!”

A fairy? Jessibella? Harley had only just come from Hoenn and knew none of Kanto's Coordinators. But she said the name with such confidence that he assumed she must be formidable, perhaps even famous.

His manner changed instantly. “So it's you! I've heard so much about you. Even all the way over in Hoenn, your name reached me.”

“Huh?” His answer bewildered Jessibella.

She pulled her two companions aside and whispered where Harley could not hear. “What's with him? He's heard of me?”

They were, of course, Jessie, James, and Meowth in disguise. They had come so Jessie could indulge her dream of being a Pokémon Coordinator.

“I don't know,” James said with a frown. “Maybe he really thinks you're famous?”

“How could anyone be that stupid?” Meowth said in disbelief. “Maybe he's mistaken you for someone else.”

“Even better!” Jessie feared little. Her eyes shone with excitement. “He looks like a Coordinator. We can use him.”

“Are you sure?” James hesitated. “Won't he figure it out? And doesn't he look familiar? Haven't we met him somewhere?”

Jessie was no longer listening. She left James and Meowth and faced Harley with a radiant smile.

“How lovely that you know my name. You must be an accomplished Coordinator yourself.”

Harley eyed the trio. One moment they had been whispering together; the next they were beaming at him. Something felt off. He dismissed the feeling, having no idea how unreliable the woman before him truly was.

Besides, an ugly plan had already formed in his mind: he could use her to strike at May.
