With his keen powers of observation, Brock immediately caught the gap in Ethan's words.

"You haven't captured Erika's heart? But you didn't deny having a relationship with Miss Sabrina. So the two of you really are lovers!?"

This guy...

Ethan raised an eyebrow.

Whenever Brock saw a beautiful woman, he usually became a lovestruck fool, transformed into an eager sycophant, and could barely walk. But with no women present, his intelligence had instantly reclaimed the high ground. He was practically turning into the great detective Brock Holmes.

When Ethan remained silent, Brock pleaded urgently, "Ethan! Please teach me how to do it! I want to be popular with women like you!"

Ethan glanced toward the restroom. Seeing that Erika and the others had not returned, he sighed in relief. If Erika heard this conversation, it would undoubtedly create a terrible misunderstanding.

She might truly believe Ethan had invited her to travel with him because he harbored improper intentions.

He needed to get rid of Brock quickly.

Looking at Brock's prematurely aged face, Ethan coughed and cleared his throat.

"Actually, winning a woman's affection only requires two words!"

Brock's eyes flew open. "Two words?"

"That's right." Ethan nodded, confirming it again. "Only two words!"

Brock instantly perked up, his eyes blazing. "Which two? Be gentle? Never surrender? Show manners? Be funny?"

Ethan shook his head, seeming to hesitate over whether he should reveal the answer.

Seeing his reluctance, Brock pressed him with complete sincerity. "Please, Ethan! Tell me. What are the two words!?"

Ethan studied him with a meaningful gaze for a long moment, then spoke slowly.

"All right. Since you want to know so badly, I'll tell you. Listen carefully. If you want women to like you, then in addition to everything you just mentioned, remember the two most important words. They are—"

Ethan stretched out the pause as Brock's eyes grew brighter and brighter.

"They are?"

Ethan chuckled and delivered his answer.

"Be handsome!"

"I..."

Brock collapsed to his knees, feeling as though he might cough up blood.

He had expected some secret technique, only for the answer to be this.

If Brock possessed Ethan's looks, why would he ever worry about finding a girlfriend? His children might already be old enough to run errands!

Sigh... I really wasted my breath...

I'm such an idiot. I actually expected something useful from him...

Nearby, Pikachu clutched its stomach and rolled across the tabletop with laughter at Brock's petrified expression.

Ash scratched his head. He did not understand the subject very well, but Ethan and Brock's exchange was genuinely entertaining.

Nothing made him happier than having everyone together.

A few minutes later, Erika and the others returned from the restroom and found Brock crouched in a corner, repeatedly drawing circles with one finger. He looked utterly devastated.

"That's strange." Misty stared at him in confusion. "What happened to him?"

"Ignore him." Ethan waved a hand. "His inner world just suffered a crushing blow. He won't recover for a while."

The women remained puzzled but did not ask further.

At that moment, Nurse Joy and Officer Jenny entered with a man of about twenty-seven or twenty-eight.

After the introductions, Ethan and the others learned that the man's name was Schuyler. He was the missing Professor Hale's assistant.

Ethan studied him for a moment. Schuyler wore a loose coat, and his hair was rather disheveled. He certainly looked like a researcher too busy with his work to care much about his appearance.

After everyone exchanged greetings, Erika spoke first and voiced the question they all shared.

"Mr. Schuyler, could you please tell us what happened?"

Schuyler glanced aside. Only after Officer Jenny nodded did he answer.

"It was the Unown!"

"The Unown!?"

Apart from Ethan, Officer Jenny, and Nurse Joy, who had already heard the cause from Schuyler, everyone present was clearly encountering the name for the first time.

Ash asked curiously, "What are they? Are they Pokémon?"

"They are Pokémon, but exceptionally rare ones found only within various ancient ruins." Schuyler sighed and began explaining the incident.

"Perhaps you've heard that Professor Hale is the leading authority on Unown. He is also one of the few scholars capable of interpreting Unown script. For some time, the professor and I have searched ancient ruins in many places for traces of these mysterious Pokémon. A few days ago, we discovered a clue at a ruin in the desert. But while we investigated its hidden chamber, an accident occurred."

Schuyler paused, apparently still struggling to believe what had happened.

Once he regained his composure, he continued.

"We were each studying Unown script on one of the walls. While I copied the writing, I suddenly heard a strange sound behind me. When I turned around, the professor, who had been standing right there... had vanished without a trace!"

"Vanished?"

Schuyler nodded. "Only a box filled with Unown symbols remained where the professor had been."

Ethan rubbed his chin.

The mention of Unown inevitably reminded him of Foster, whom he met at the Ruins of Alph. Like Professor Hale, Foster could interpret Unown script and conducted research in the field.

They belonged to the same academic circle. The two men probably knew each other.

"What happened next?"

Schuyler smiled bitterly. "Last night, I brought the box back to Professor Hale's manor as a keepsake and gave it to his daughter, Molly. That same night, the manor began to crystallize. At one point, I entered Molly's room and saw a large group of Unown surrounding her. After that, everything happened exactly as you have seen..."

"I understand." Erika summarized what they had learned. "Then the Unown caused all of this."

"That's right." Schuyler nodded. "To stop the phenomenon from spreading, we must restrain the Unown and rescue Molly!"

Officer Jenny joined the conversation. "Experts will arrive shortly to investigate. But to prevent the situation from spreading farther, could I ask all of you to remain here for now? This incident is beyond what the police can handle. As Trainers, your help would be invaluable!"

Before Ethan could answer, the passionate and kindhearted Ash sprang to his feet and struck his chest.

"Don't worry, Officer Jenny! No matter what happens, we'll help!"
