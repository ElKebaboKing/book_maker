When Ethan returned to the main house and found Erika in the flower-arranging room, the class had just ended. A group of women passed him while conversing in low voices.

The narrow corridor could not accommodate so many people at once, so Ethan stepped aside to let them through.

Their white socks creaked against the wooden floor, and several different perfumes drifted into Ethan's nose.

How could he describe it? The fragrances certainly smelled pleasant, but they were slightly overpowering.

As the women passed, each cast a curious glance at Ethan.

Some were relatively reserved, but most looked at him openly and boldly, silently marveling at the handsome man.

Hmm... His clothes did seem entirely out of place here. Rather than a guest attending the tea party, he looked more like a tourist!

Once they disappeared at the end of the corridor, Ethan looked into the flower-arranging room. Only Erika, Naoko, and another woman in a kimono remained.

The woman's features resembled Naoko's and Kuni's. She had to be the sister Naoko had called Miki.

Seeing Ethan enter, Naoko quickly rose and made the introductions.

"Mr. Ethan, this is Miki, the eldest of us five sisters. Miki, this is the Ethan I mentioned earlier. He's traveling together with Miss Erika."

So she really was the eldest of the Eevee Sisters!

Ethan and Miki greeted each other with such formality that they looked almost like two people meeting for the first time on an arranged date.

"It's a pleasure to meet you, Mr. Ethan."

"It's a pleasure to meet you, Miss Miki."

Looking more closely, Ethan saw that Miki appeared two years older than Kuni, whom he had met at the entrance. She was also beautiful and possessed an exceptionally gentle bearing.

Ethan also noticed that Naoko, Kuni, and Miki all liked to wear a Poké Ball in their hair as an ornament.

Strange. Is this a particular taste shared by their family?

An elegant vase stood before Miki, holding several bunches of vivid, delicate flowers.

Ethan knew nothing about the art of flower arrangement, but he could still recognize beauty. He could tell that these flowers looked distinctly different from an ordinary bouquet, appearing even brighter and more beautiful.

Looking at Ethan, Erika asked softly, "What were you doing just now?"

Ethan sat down beside her and casually pointed his thumb behind him. "I fought a few battles in the back courtyard to pass the time."

"Did you win?"

"Do you even need to ask?" Ethan chuckled. "I fought five or six matches and won every one of them easily."

Hearing this, Naoko asked curiously, "Oh? Mr. Ethan, are you very skilled at battling?"

Before Ethan could answer, Miki gave her younger sister an amused look. "Naoko, don't you know who Ethan is?"

"Who he is?"

Naoko froze, then shook her head in confusion. She had only met Ethan yesterday and had not spoken with him at length. How could she know anything about his identity?

Isn't he simply an ordinary audience member?

Seeing her confusion, Miki realized that she was still completely unaware and explained, "Mr. Ethan is the Indigo League Champion."

"The Indigo League Champion!?"

Naoko cried out and stared at Ethan in disbelief. Realizing that she had lost her composure, she covered her mouth in slight embarrassment.

"So that's why your name sounded so familiar when I heard it yesterday!"

She had not watched Ethan's matches, but for a period of time, she had repeatedly heard his name in news reports and on television.

Ethan could only smile and nod at her astonishment.

He was not like those vain people who announced their achievements to everyone they met, desperate for the whole world to know they were champions.

Besides, winning a championship was not truly such an extraordinary accomplishment. A new winner appeared every year, and with all the regions across the world combined, who knew how many champions were crowned annually?

His goal was a Grand Slam. How could he grow complacent over the championship of a single region?

He cleared his throat and changed the subject with complete ease. "What are you doing?"

Miki smiled gently. "Miss Erika and I were exchanging our experiences with ikebana. Although we come from different regions, I never expected our methods and techniques to be exactly the same!"

Erika nodded as well. "Miss Miki is an excellent ikebana practitioner. Her technique just now was extremely skillful, and I learned a great deal from watching her!"

"Not at all." Miki quickly waved her hands. "I also learned a great deal from speaking with Miss Erika. And I feel that Miss Erika is so gentle and gracious. Her bearing is very similar to mine!"

Ethan was not the only one who could no longer endure this exchange of commercial flattery. Even Naoko complained, "All right, I know you're both excellent. Could the two of you please stop praising each other?"

Everyone laughed, and the atmosphere in the room became relaxed and harmonious.

After chatting for a while, Ethan and Erika said goodbye to Miki. Naoko then led them onward to visit another part of the property.

They left the main house and reached the pond, where the graceful sound of a stringed instrument drifted from somewhere nearby.

Following the music, they found a girl in a pink kimono seated on a red carpet, a Poké Ball decorating her hair as she played!

The girl was somewhat younger than Naoko. Entirely absorbed in her performance, she did not notice their arrival.

Apparently afraid to disturb the musician, Naoko said softly, "This is Sayo, my younger sister and the youngest of the five of us."

Sayo?

The name made Ethan think of Misty's eldest sister, Daisy. In the original version he remembered, both women had been named Sakura.

They stopped and listened for a while. Ethan understood little about this kind of classical music, but Erika nodded approvingly from time to time. When she noticed Ethan looking at her, she smiled.

"This is Twilight by the Fields, a classic traditional Yamato piece. It's also very popular in Kanto."

She turned toward Naoko. "You five sisters truly are multi-talented. Performing, flower arranging, playing music—you excel at everything!"

Naoko smiled shyly at Erika's praise.

"These are how we make our living, after all. We spend a great deal of time practicing every day."

The piece had not yet ended, but the group continued toward the other side of the courtyard.

After circling the pond, they reached an open area surrounded by folding screens. Naoko pointed ahead and introduced it to them.

"Here we are. This is where the tea party will be held. Since no one else has arrived yet, let's hurry inside!"
