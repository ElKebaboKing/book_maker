A few officers stayed behind to evacuate the hostages. Ethan, Erika, and Looker led the rest toward the top of the tower.

After losing the station's main building, the Team Rocket members had all retreated there. They were still fighting, like beasts driven into a corner.

Ethan could not understand what Team Rocket hoped to gain from this operation.

Such a public attack was bound to draw trouble. It exposed them to everyone and would surely provoke fear and opposition. For Team Rocket, the harm would far outweigh any benefit.

Was it all just for that announcement? And what came after it?

Ethan could make no sense of it. He could only guess that something had gone wrong within Team Rocket, leaving the executives temporarily unable to contact Giovanni. Or perhaps Giovanni himself had abandoned these people to serve some larger purpose they knew nothing about.

Lost and uncertain, the remaining members might have decided to band together and use the broadcast to find Giovanni.

Still turning the possibilities over in his mind, Ethan retraced his earlier route with the others and reached the passage connecting the main building to the tower.

A spiral staircase rose through it. Two elevators had once run straight to the top, but Team Rocket had locked them down. The long staircase was now their only way up.

Team Rocket's resistance there was fierce. Attacks poured down the narrow stairwell in such numbers that the officers could not advance another step.

The relentless barrage even made the tower tremble. Bits of the walls began to break away.

Ethan sent out his Pokémon too, but no one could withstand an assault like that head-on.

He had no choice but to use the same trick again. Gengar passed through the wall, circled behind the enemy without a sound, and struck. As the Team Rocket members fell into confusion, the officers' Pokémon surged forward. Covering one another in turns, they finally took the staircase after a hard fight.

Team Rocket had no defensible position left. Its members retreated again and again until they reached the very top of the TV tower.

Ethan followed the officers onto the observation deck. At once, he spotted three executives among the crowd—Apollo, Athena, and Rocket executive Lance!

He rarely came across them at all, yet here were three together. Their faces were grim. Their predicament had stripped away their usual composure.

Some dozen meters separated the two groups. The Team Rocket members glared at them, tense and ready. The final battle could begin at any moment.

Looker pushed through the officers and faced the three executives. "Team Rocket! What was the purpose of this attack? And where is Giovanni now?"

"We'd like to know where he is too," Apollo said with a sneer. "As for our purpose, wasn't our message clear? We wanted the world to see Team Rocket and hear of our great deeds! We wanted you to know we haven't been driven into a corner!"

The destruction of their bases across Kanto and Johto, and the steady arrest of their members, had plainly put Team Rocket under enormous pressure.

"You've lost your minds." Looker shook his head, then fixed them with a stern look. "You're surrounded. Put down your weapons and surrender while you still can!"

Apollo ignored him and turned toward Ethan in the crowd.

"I didn't expect to find you here too. You just keep turning up! If it weren't for you, Lambda wouldn't have been captured. We wouldn't have suffered one loss after another, and Team Rocket's great undertaking wouldn't have met with so many setbacks!"

Ethan's face remained expressionless. He had no intention of answering. This time he had decided to be nothing more than someone who got the job done. Their beliefs were too far apart for an argument to serve any purpose.

Looker saw that Ethan did not want to draw attention to himself. He said no more and gave the order for a full attack.

The two sides' Pokémon collided, and the battle turned fierce at once.

Nearly all the officers' Pokémon were Arcanine. Team Rocket had a much wider variety: Golbat, Weezing, Arbok, and Salandit. They filled the air and the ground, making an imposing sight.

Dozens of Pokémon attacked in the cramped space. Team Rocket had three executives, but Ethan and Erika fought alongside the officers. For the moment, their strongest fighters seemed evenly matched.

Team Rocket's ordinary members and their Pokémon, however, could not match the officers and theirs.

As more Team Rocket Pokémon fell, Apollo glanced around and saw that the battle was turning against them.

He exchanged a look with Athena and Lance. Their purpose had been served. It was time to withdraw.

In fact, Ethan's guess had been right: Team Rocket had undergone a major upheaval.

More than half a month earlier, Giovanni had taken the organization's elite members and vanished completely. No one left behind knew where he had gone.

Those he had abandoned were left to fend for themselves, like bait displayed in plain sight for the International Police and Ethan to pick off one group at a time.

And the tactic had worked to a degree. Over the past few months, almost all their bases and facilities had been cleared out. That success had made the International Police lower their guard. Believing Team Rocket was no longer much of a threat, they had recently stopped actively searching for its remaining members, apart from continuing the hunt for Giovanni.

Team Rocket had moved from operating behind the scenes into hiding deep in the shadows.

Had these members not attacked the TV tower today, Looker might already have left for another region to deal with a different organization.

But the people Giovanni had left behind would not simply surrender. Today's attack on the station was meant to tell their scattered comrades that Team Rocket still had members here. They hoped everyone would reunite, stand together, and fight their way out of their predicament.

Back on the observation deck, Apollo and the other two executives tried to retreat. Ethan had no intention of letting them.

His eyes had been fixed on the three of them since the battle began. The moment he noticed their intention, he directed his Pokémon to engage them and keep them there.

If they escaped today, he might never find them again in a world this vast. Whatever it took, he would use this chance to arrest all three executives at once!
