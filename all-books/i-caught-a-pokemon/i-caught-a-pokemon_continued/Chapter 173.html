The three of them passed through a long, narrow tunnel and came to a frozen waterfall. At last Ethan understood why Lorelei had called the place Icefall Cave.

A dozen or so streams, each less than a foot wide, still ran down its face. The rest of the huge waterfall had frozen solid into a thick wall of ice.

Before they could take in the sight, they heard faint voices from farther inside. They exchanged a glance and crept toward them.

Beyond a massive pillar of ice lay a lake scattered with floating ice. Several Team Rocket members crouched at its edge, busy with something. Beside them was a huge metal net holding dozens of unconscious Pokémon.

Team Rocket really was here. The grunts were taking out Poké Balls and using them to capture the helpless Pokémon one by one.

Lorelei was furious. No wonder they had seen so few Pokémon on the way down. These people had been poaching them.

There was an unwritten rule among trainers: catch Pokémon only in numbers that would leave their habitats and populations intact. Team Rocket was taking every Pokémon it could find. And they were doing it in Lorelei's homeland, practically under her nose.

Ethan checked the area. Seeing only a handful of grunts, he quickly conferred with Lorelei and Sabrina. He and Lorelei sent out their Pokémon, and together they charged.

Their footsteps alerted the grunts, who looked up in alarm at the three strangers bearing down on them.

"Oh no! It's that damn woman, Lorelei!"

That damn woman? Lorelei's brow twitched. Her anger flared. She sprinted ahead of the Pokémon, leaped, and drove her elbow into the speaker's face.

There was a heavy thud and a spray of blood. The grunt crumpled to the ground, several teeth scattering across the ice.

Ethan winced. Lorelei usually seemed so poised and easygoing. He hadn't expected this side of her. Best not to get on her bad side.

Only when their companion fell did the other grunts recover enough to send out their Pokémon. Both sides joined battle.

Team Rocket had the familiar Golbat and Weezing. They were no match for Ethan and the others. Within a few exchanges, all the grunts' Pokémon were down.

The grunts tried to escape in the confusion. Sabrina raised a hand and held them in place with her psychic power.

With the fight over, Ethan knelt to untie the metal net. Lorelei walked up to one of the grunts and planted a foot on him.

"Tell me why you're on the Sevii Islands. Where's your secret base?"

The grunt sneered at her. "Dream on, you damn woman. We're Team Rocket! You won't get a word about the organization out of me."

Lorelei kicked him several more times, but he gritted his teeth and said nothing.

Ethan freed the Pokémon in the net, roused them, and released those already caught in the grunts' Poké Balls. When the Pokémon opened their eyes and saw what had happened, they fled like startled rabbits. Within moments, they were all gone.

Ethan stood and found Lorelei close to losing her temper, with no answer from the grunt. He walked over.

"That isn't going to work, Miss Lorelei. They're loyal, and they know you won't do anything worse to them. That's why he's so confident."

"What would you do?" she asked.

Ethan rubbed his chin and smiled. "I suppose I could strip them and leave them to fend for themselves here. Or hold them in the water until the cold crippled them for life. But I'm a kind person. How could I do anything like that?"

Lorelei stared at him. So did the grunts.

Ignoring the looks they gave him, Ethan turned to Sabrina. "How about that trick you once used on Ash?"

Sabrina nodded and lifted a hand. In an instant, every grunt except the one Lorelei was questioning shrank into a doll.

"Th-that's psychic power!" The remaining grunt stared at her in terror. "Are you the Psychic-type Gym Leader from Saffron City?"

"So you've heard of Sabrina." Ethan beamed. "That makes this easier. If you don't want to spend the rest of your life as a doll like your friends, tell us everything we want to know."

The grunt swallowed. Fear showed on his face, but his loyalty to Team Rocket held.

"In your dreams!"

"Then we have no choice." Ethan spread his hands with a regretful look. "Sabrina, turn his friends back. Make him the doll instead, and leave him here forever."

He crouched in front of the grunt and gave him a thumbs-up. "I admire your courage. I'm sure one of your friends will be more willing to talk, though. You'll have to stay here, buried among the ice and snow, until someone finds you."

Sweat ran down the grunt's face. He could vouch for his own loyalty, but not for his companions. Any one of them might betray Team Rocket rather than be turned into a doll.

Was he going to be the sacrifice that bought them their freedom?

Sabrina started to lift her hand. The grunt blurted out, "Wait! I'll talk!"

"A wise choice." Ethan patted his shoulder, stood, and shrugged at the dumbfounded Lorelei. "What's that look for? I'm helping you question him. You could at least be grateful."
