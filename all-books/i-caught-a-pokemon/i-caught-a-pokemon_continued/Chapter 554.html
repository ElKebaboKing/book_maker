Ash's entrance had eclipsed every other competitor. All eyes in the stadium were on him.

There was only one way to describe it: he looked incredibly cool.

Ethan and the others, who had entered in the ordinary procession, could not compete for attention.

The announcer received an update and explained, "We've just learned that the original torchbearer injured his ankle and couldn't complete the relay. The boy you're watching now is Ash, who passed the screening trials and has also reached the preliminaries!"

After a pause, the announcer continued, "The flame in his hands is the undying sacred fire of the legendary Pokémon Ho-Oh, which ended a war and brought life back to the land and forests. And now Ash has carried it to the cauldron..."

Inside the stadium, Ash held the torch high and ran across most of the field. He climbed the steps to the great cauldron. When he touched it with the torch, a roar sounded and the whole cauldron burst into flame. The Silver Conference had truly begun.

The crowd broke into its loudest cheers yet. Even Ethan and the other competitors applauded as they looked up at the blazing flame.

Every Trainer felt a surge of excitement. Ethan was not alone in silently hoping for a perfect result at this Silver Conference.

Amid that excitement, Pokémon League president Charles Goodshow approached the podium.

He was a short man, but under the lights and in the charged atmosphere, he looked imposing.

Standing before the microphone, he cleared his throat and smiled. "To everyone who has advanced from the screening trials: Ho-Oh's sacred flame is here to watch over you. Give all you have, battle fairly, and leave no regrets!"

Goodshow never gave long speeches. A few simple words, straight to the point, were enough to stir the crowd.

...

By the time they left the stadium, the moon was high and stars dotted the sky.

Outside, Ethan met up with Ash, Erika, and the others. Misa, Masha, and the rest of the biker gang came hurrying toward him with brilliant smiles.

"Boss!"

They called out from far away, drawing looks from the people nearby. Some fans had recognized Ethan and wanted an autograph or a photo with him. When they saw the fierce-looking bikers converging on him, none dared approach.

In that respect, Misa and Masha were helpful.

"You lot..." Ethan shook his head with a wry smile. "You came all this way without telling me. If I hadn't spotted you in the stands, I'd still have no idea you were here!"

"Heh." Misa smiled sheepishly. "It's such an important tournament. Of course we had to come and cheer for you in person!"

"Exactly!" Masha said. "We wanted to surprise you. And we knew you had the screening trials yesterday, so we didn't dare interrupt!"

Misty, Ash, and Brock had met the bikers at the Indigo League, so they were not surprised. Erika alone looked from Ethan to the rough-looking group and back, unable to see how they fit together.

And why were they calling him Boss?

"How did you get here?" Ethan asked.

"We rode," Masha said. "All the way from Vermilion City!"

"Thanks to the money you invested," Misa added with a smile, "our club is making a profit. We're not the same as we were before. We even traded in all our bicycles for motorcycles!"

Ethan nodded. Going from bicycles to motorcycles was certainly progress.

Motorcycles suited a biker gang better too. What sort of gang rode bicycles every day? Their peers would laugh at them for years if word got around.

Still, riding all the way from Vermilion City to Silver Town could not have been easy. They had traveled a great distance to support him, and Ethan was touched.

"Come on. Let's all have a good dinner."

Ethan led the group to a nearby restaurant. The owner looked alarmed when so many bikers came through the door at once, fearing they were there to wreck the place. Their conversation soon put him at ease.

With Ash's group included, nearly thirty people squeezed around two large tables. The tables filled with delicious food. Most of the party were adults, so they ordered plenty of alcohol. Even Brock and Ash drank quite a bit in the excitement.

Over dinner, Ash told them how he had become the replacement torchbearer. As Ethan had guessed, Team Rocket's trio was responsible.

They had lured Ash away, stolen Pikachu, and locked him in a room. Luckily, Gary had arrived in time to free him.

Then, as Team Rocket fled, they collided with the original torchbearer. After rescuing Pikachu, Ash had to take the man's place.

"And that's how you ended up stealing the show," Misty said with a smile.

"Heh." Ash smiled bashfully. Even at his age, he knew that carrying the torch was a great honor.

He had carried one at the Indigo League too, though, so this time he was not quite as overwhelmed.

Everyone ate, drank, and talked merrily. From time to time, Misa and Masha glanced at Erika.

During the Indigo League, their boss had been with Sabrina, the beautiful psychic girl. They knew that. Now, after so little time, he was with another beautiful woman.

When they learned Erika was the Celadon Gym Leader, they gave Ethan a private thumbs-up.

As expected of their boss. He was a League champion, and the women in his life were Gym Leaders too. How could they not admire him?

Dinner lasted until ten at night. Then everyone said goodbye and returned to their rooms.

With matches beginning tomorrow, Ethan and Erika bathed quickly when they reached their suite and went to bed.

But Ethan was too excited to sleep. He lay awake for a long time. Ash, Gary, and most of the other competitors were much the same that night, tossing and turning in their beds.

Unable to fall asleep, Ethan spent a long while making love to Erika. Only after he had worn himself out did he drift off.
