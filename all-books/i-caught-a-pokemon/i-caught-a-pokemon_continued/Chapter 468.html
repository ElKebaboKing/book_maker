A dark mass of people stood before them. Despite being outnumbered more than ten to one, Ash showed no fear and gave no thought to retreat.

Ethan shook his head and addressed the pair atop the submarine in a tone of exaggerated disappointment. "Using superior numbers to bully a smaller group again? You people truly have no imagination. Can't you send a few elite fighters instead?"

He spoke with complete confidence, seemingly forgetting that during his first several encounters with Legendary Pokémon, he had also secured victory through strength in numbers.

Cassidy and Butch sneered.

He truly doesn't know when he's doomed!

Even now, instead of abandoning Lugia and fleeing, he intends to resist?

So what?

No matter how powerful Ethan's Pokémon were, Team Rocket possessed an overwhelming numerical advantage. Even if every member released only one Pokémon, a saturation attack would be enough to drown any opponent!

They had no intention of observing rules or honor. While their Pokémon attacked, the humans would target the Trainers directly!

Even if Ethan's and Ash's Pokémon could not be defeated quickly, Team Rocket needed only to capture the two Trainers. Their Pokémon would then have no choice but to surrender and place themselves at the enemy's mercy!

"I admire your courage," Butch mocked, "but some things can't be accomplished through bravado alone!"

As he spoke, the Team Rocket members took out their Poké Balls. The instant they released their Pokémon, dozens of Zubat and dozens of Weezing appeared in the air!

The winged Pokémon blocked most of the sky. From Ethan's and Ash's perspective, the dense swarm was truly spectacular!

Ash had seen situations like this many times and naturally refused to back down. He took out all his Poké Balls and hurled every one of them at once.

Bang! Bang! Bang! Bang!

The Poké Balls opened one after another. Blue light flashed, and Heracross, Bayleef, Cyndaquil, Totodile, and Noctowl appeared alongside Pikachu, who had remained at Ash's side. All six Pokémon were now assembled!

At the same time, Ethan sent out his own Pokémon.

Gardevoir, Charizard, Jolteon, Dragonair, Scyther, and the Eevee he had received from the Kimono Sisters also appeared before him.

In only moments, more than a dozen Pokémon had formed ranks and prepared for battle!

Tension filled the air. Although the two Trainers' Pokémon were considerably stronger than their opponents, they remained at an obvious numerical disadvantage. Both sides possessed strengths and weaknesses, making the eventual victor impossible to predict.

This balance existed partly because Ethan had not used his full strength. Pupitar and Crystal Steelix still remained in his bag, but neither Pokémon was well suited to fighting along the shore, so he had not released them.

Beyond those two, he also held two trump cards—Suicune and Raikou.

After spending so much time with Ethan, Suicune could now follow his commands. Raikou remained resistant, however, and needed more time before it would be completely subdued. Unless absolutely necessary, Ethan did not dare release Raikou so casually.

Besides, Ethan was certain that Team Rocket also had something in reserve!

Now they would see which side faltered first and exhausted its hidden methods!

At that moment, Gengar also emerged from Ethan's shadow and joined Gardevoir in guarding him from either side.

No matter what happened across the battlefield, these two Pokémon always thought first of protecting their Trainer.

You want to hurt my Trainer?

Then step over my body first!

Along the shore, the atmosphere froze. A great battle could erupt at any instant.

When Oliver saw so many villains and Pokémon trying to steal Silver, fear filled his heart and his legs trembled slightly. Even so, he called from behind them, "Mr. Hero, I'll help too!"

He prepared to have Lanturn swim forward and join Ethan's and Ash's Pokémon.

Ethan glanced at him and shook his head. "Keep Lanturn where it is. Your highest priority is protecting yourself and Silver!"

Oliver opened his mouth. "But..."

"No buts!" Ethan replied in a tone that allowed no argument. "Silver is your friend, isn't it? You're the final line of defense beside it. You have to keep it safe!"

Hearing the Sea Hero's words, Oliver understood the weight of his responsibility and nodded forcefully.

"Yes! I understand. Leave it to me!"

With events having reached this point, there was nothing more to say. In the confusion, no one knew which side moved first. The Pokémon on both sides charged simultaneously!

When the opposing forces collided, the battlefield quickly split into more than a dozen smaller clashes and descended into chaos.

It was not that Ethan's and Ash's Pokémon did not want to cooperate. There were simply too many enemies. They had to guard against hostile moves while taking care not to obstruct their allies' view, forcing each Pokémon to fight alone as the battles gradually spread in every direction.

Dozens of Zubat beat their wings and released piercing shrieks. Their agile bodies darted in every direction with dazzling speed. Each struck from ambush and immediately fled, giving their opponents no chance to respond.

The Weezing, by contrast, floated sluggishly through the air while spewing dense smoke from their mouths. Fearing the poison, the opposing Pokémon did not dare approach and could only attack from a distance.

Cyndaquil breathed fire across the ground. Pikachu fought ten opponents at once, its electricity leaving several Zubat that attempted a sneak attack twitching in agony. Noctowl beat its wings and scattered the thick black smoke...

At first, Ash could still issue a few commands. As the battlefield became more chaotic and the noise grew deafening, however, he could no longer provide much help.

Ethan remained effective. He placed one hand on Gardevoir's shoulder and used its telepathy to transmit his orders clearly and precisely into the minds of his Pokémon, allowing him to direct them from afar at critical moments.

Charizard, use Dragon Rage!

Scyther, hurry and help Eevee! The Zubat have it surrounded!

Jolteon, move farther away before releasing your electricity! Whatever you do, don't hit our allies!

Through the efforts of their dozen Pokémon, Ethan's side suffered occasional wounds, but far more opponents were defeated and fell from the sky.

Unfortunately, too many enemies remained. As Team Rocket released still more Pokémon, Ethan's and Ash's battle line began to falter. Several Zubat even slipped overhead while no one was watching and attacked Silver directly...
