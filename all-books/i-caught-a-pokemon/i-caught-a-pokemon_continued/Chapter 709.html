Shell craters of every size scarred the island. The taller piles of volcanic rock had been blasted apart. The bare little place looked more desolate than ever.

Among the flattened ruins, Ethan finally spotted Courtney. She was crouched in a crater. Her Camerupt stood over her, shielding her with its body. Scorched marks still smoked on its back. It had protected its Trainer through the barrage.

Courtney's orange-red uniform stood out against the dark stone. She looked shaken and disheveled, but unhurt. Ethan felt some relief. “Courtney, are you all right?”

She stood and shook her head. Ethan turned back to the fight. There was no time to look after her further. Archie and his members had reached him and Shelly.

Old enemies needed no more threats. The battle began at once. Moves crossed the air, and Pokémon collided.

Ethan held nothing back. Shelly still had complicated feelings about being called a Team Aqua traitor, but she attacked her former comrades without hesitation. She knew this was no time for mercy. Team Aqua showed her none in return.

Archie came straight for Ethan. Matt, the other commander, met Shelly. The two people who had once served together now fought a battle that could cost them their lives.

Then the sea erupted. A huge shape rose amid flying spray.

Groudon turned as if it had sensed the newcomer. A massive blue Pokémon, shaped like a winged whale, leaped from the water, rolled in the air, and crashed back down. Waves towered around it. Everyone looked toward the sound.

Its identity was unmistakable: Kyogre.

The two ancient Legendary Pokémon had met here. Ethan, Shelly, and Maxie were astonished. Team Aqua's members looked less surprised.

Archie had not initially followed Shelly. He had been tracking Kyogre's wake through the sea. Near these islands, he lost it and instead detected Shelly's concealed submarine. Then he saw the battle ashore. He chose his new target immediately. Now Kyogre had appeared of its own accord.

Its arrival was no accident. Kyogre and Groudon had fought since ancient times. Apart from sleeping and feeding, they seemed to spend much of their existence struggling against one another. Since its earlier awakening, Kyogre had been searching for Groudon. It could sense its rival somewhere in this stretch of sea but had not found the exact place. The recent fighting revealed the island and drew it here.

Kyogre's appearance transformed the battle. Groudon abandoned Team Magma and strode toward its old enemy. Kyogre rose from the sea and floated toward Groudon. They had eyes only for each other.

Ethan, Team Aqua, and Team Magma suddenly mattered very little. All of them had to clear the battlefield for the two giants.

Groudon roared. Kyogre answered. They collided, shaking the island. The sky changed color. Thick clouds gathered over the water, the light dimmed, wind swept across the rocks, lightning flashed, and the sea churned.

The worsening conditions made the human battle harder. Everyone had to fight while avoiding the two ancient Pokémon. Neither Groudon nor Kyogre had undergone Primal Reversion, but their attacks still struck everything nearby with enormous force.

Both Archie and Maxie saw an opportunity. Ethan held the orbs. Defeat him, and they could take what they wanted. Groudon and Kyogre were occupied with each other and might wear themselves down. Their ultimate dreams seemed suddenly close.

Archie drove his members into a fiercer attack on Ethan.

Maxie had fewer options. He had brought a small force and lost many of them to Groudon's beam. He could not make a frontal assault. He would have to outthink Ethan instead, a method that suited him well. Through the submarine's large monitor, he spotted Courtney on the island. An idea came to him.

...

“Tyranitar, Scizor, Dragonite—hold the line. Don't let them push you back! Metang, Milotic, Eevee, Blaziken, use your strongest attacks. No holding back!”

“Gardevoir, join them at the front. If you all hold, nobody can reach me. Gengar, stop taking it easy. There are too many of them for you to hide your strength now.”

“Raikou, Suicune, cover our flanks. Don't let any Pokémon slip around us!”

Under Ethan's direction, his Pokémon and Shelly's worked together. Their defense held Team Aqua in front of them. Ethan had committed every Pokémon with him except Deoxys, which he kept back for an emergency.

He had expected a simple pursuit. He would have brought a stronger lineup if he had known the chase would become this chaotic. He had to manage with the team he had. Deoxys remained a powerful reserve. It might not win a duel against Groudon or Kyogre, but it gave Ethan room to act.

Shelly moved closer after her Pokémon repelled one of Matt's attacks. “Archie's pushing harder. Can we really hold them?”

She had rarely fought while outnumbered like this. Team Aqua and Team Magma usually brought the larger force. Even knowing Ethan's strength, she felt uneasy. With almost anyone else beside her, she would already have fled.

“We have to,” Ethan said. “We could leave safely right now. But if we do, Groudon and Kyogre will exhaust each other, and the Teams will take them. We can't withdraw.”

“I know, but...” She glanced toward Team Magma's submarine. Maxie had not acted in some time. The less he showed, the more dangerous whatever he was planning seemed.

Ethan understood. “We can't spare anyone for him yet. Deal with Team Aqua first.”

He also wondered why Courtney had not gone back to her own side. During a brief opening between commands, he looked around. Maxie and Tabitha were on the island again, escorting Courtney toward him at a slow pace.

How did they catch her? And when did Maxie leave the submarine?

They stopped some distance away. Maxie called, “If you want her to live, hand over the Red Orb!”

Ethan looked at Courtney before answering. She stood with her eyes lowered, expression empty. She showed no fear and no expectation that Ethan would trade the orb for her. She seemed to have given her fate to someone else.

She likely had not resisted when Maxie seized her. She felt guilty for leading Ethan here and revealing their destination. Even after risking her life to delay him, she did not want to be called a traitor.

“Why do you think I'd give you the orb for her?” Ethan asked, his expression unchanged.

Courtney lowered her head further. Shelly glanced at Ethan but said nothing.

Maxie studied him. “I didn't expect that answer. I thought you were full of righteous principles.”

Ethan shrugged. He was bargaining and would not reveal how much he wanted to save Courtney. “One person's life matters, but so do many others. If keeping Groudon from undergoing Primal Reversion and falling into your hands requires a sacrifice, so be it.”

Maxie had not expected him to say that. Perhaps Ethan was bluffing. “You think I'll believe you? You were in contact with her long before this, weren't you? Your escape from the base was arranged. Otherwise, why would she defy my order and bring you here?”

Maxie did not know Courtney had pressed the detonator and tried to die with Ethan. Nor did he know Ethan had pulled her from the collapse. He thought she had simply ignored her orders and defected.

Ethan did not correct him. Maxie might even be fishing for information, trying to find a weakness in Ethan's account. Both understood that the other was difficult to manipulate.

Explosions sounded behind Ethan. His Pokémon were still battling Team Aqua, while Groudon and Kyogre shook the island so violently that it seemed close to breaking apart. He had little time left to bargain.

But he would not simply hand over the orb. He held up its pouch and eyed Maxie. “I might consider a trade. First tell me this: after it, would Courtney still be a member of Team Magma?”
