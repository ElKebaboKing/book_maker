"Miltank, use Rollout!"

At Whitney's command, Miltank broke into a run. It gathered speed, leaped high, and curled into a spinning wheel. When it landed, it rolled straight toward Tyranitar like a living cannonball.

It moved so fast that friction sent sand spraying from the hard-packed ground.

"Tyranitar, use Stone Edge!"

Ethan had seen Rollout before. Rather than meet it head-on, he chose a ranged attack to test how tough Miltank was.

Sharp stones rose from the ground and thrust toward it. Miltank did not even try to dodge. It plowed into the first stone at full speed!

Bang! Bang! Bang!

The force of the impact shattered the stone. Miltank smashed through the others in quick succession without losing momentum and kept rolling toward Tyranitar.

It was called a sturdy, powerful Pokémon for good reason. That body was formidable!

Thud!

Miltank finally struck Tyranitar with a tremendous crash. Tyranitar appeared to have taken the full force of the blow, but its armor was so thick that the attack did little damage.

Whitney was not concerned.

"Miltank, one more time!"

If one hit was not enough, she would keep at it. Eventually Tyranitar would reach its limit.

When Ethan saw Miltank repeat the move, he changed tactics.

"Catch it!"

Tyranitar spread its arms and waited for the right moment. As Miltank closed in, it wrapped both arms tightly around its spinning body.

Scrrrape!

Miltank's body ground against the armor on Tyranitar's arms. Its momentum forced Tyranitar several steps backward, but the hold slowed Miltank's spin until it stopped in Tyranitar's grasp.

"Now's our chance!" Ethan's eyes lit up. "Use Payback!"

Payback was a physical Dark-type move that built power before striking. At this range, there was no way Miltank could evade it.

Tyranitar raised its right hand, gathered energy there, and brought it down hard on Miltank. Miltank shot back toward Whitney's half of the field like a volleyball struck with all one's strength.

Rumble, rumble...

It rolled to a stop in front of Whitney. Its thick hide had spared it serious harm; aside from a few scrapes, it was still in good shape.

"Impressive! You actually stopped that move!" Whitney looked at Tyranitar with approval. Miltank's Rollout had given countless challengers trouble, and many had lost to it outright. She had not expected Tyranitar to break through it so easily.

Ethan smiled. Against overwhelming strength, a clever technique could only do so much.

Once Miltank stood again, Whitney changed her approach.

"Use Zen Headbutt!"

Miltank charged across the field. This time it did not roll. After passing the midpoint, it launched itself forward like a projectile, aiming its large head at Tyranitar.

Charging straight in again?

Ethan shook his head. Did she think the strength of this miniature Godzilla was just for show?

If a Tauros had used that move, its horns might have made the charge intimidating. This was a young cow with no horns at all.

"Use Dark Pulse!"

Tyranitar opened its mouth and fired a wave of Dark-type energy at Miltank.

Miltank could not dodge in midcharge. It plunged into the Dark Pulse, but its sturdy body carried it through the attack and into Tyranitar's lower belly.

Whitney did not stop there. "Jump and use Body Slam!"

Miltank regained its balance, sprang high, and spread its limbs as it came crashing down toward Tyranitar.

Body Slam was a physical Normal-type move whose force depended in part on the user's size and strength. Given Miltank's weight, even Tyranitar would have trouble taking a direct hit.

"Tyranitar, use Iron Defense!"

A blue-green glow covered Tyranitar, greatly strengthening its defenses.

It had barely finished using Iron Defense when Miltank landed on it with crushing force.

Ethan had never been landed on by a cow and did not intend to find out how it felt. Tyranitar's pained expression told him enough. Thanks to Iron Defense, though, it gritted its teeth and held on.

Miltank had now used its three main attacks. It was Ethan's turn to strike back.

As a Normal-type Pokémon that relied chiefly on physical attacks, Miltank did most of its fighting up close. Having failed to defeat Tyranitar early, it had few ways left to overcome it. The battle was turning against it.

"Use Hyper Beam!"

The two Pokémon were almost touching. Ethan had no fear that Miltank would dodge.

Tyranitar opened its jaws and released a powerful blast of energy.

Hyper Beam struck Miltank square in the chest and drove it out of the battlefield into the wall beneath the spectator seats.

When the beam faded, Miltank slid down the wall and collapsed.

The referee raised her colored flag. "Miltank is unable to battle! Tyranitar wins the first round!"

Whitney sighed and recalled Miltank, though she did not look especially downcast. "Everyone was right about you. You really are strong!"

"Your Miltank is strong too," Ethan said courteously. "If I'd used a different Pokémon, those attacks might have beaten it already."

He meant it. Most Pokémon would have struggled to withstand Rollout, Zen Headbutt, and Body Slam in succession unless they could evade Miltank or had exceptional defenses.

Whitney clearly appreciated the reassurance from someone stronger than herself.

"Miltank really is the partner I trust most." Smiling, she took another Poké Ball from her pocket. "Now for my second Pokémon. Come out, Ambipom!"
