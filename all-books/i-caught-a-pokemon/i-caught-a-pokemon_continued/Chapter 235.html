As Ethan came back to her, Sabrina glanced at the Scyther still standing where he had left them.

"Are we just going to leave? They've lost their leader. What will they do now?"

Ethan looked at her in surprise. That did not sound like the sort of question she would once have asked. It sounded more like something he might say. Then again, they had spent so much time together and been through so much. As they grew closer, her outlook had gradually changed. They were finding more in common, and perhaps, with time, they would come to see even more things alike.

"Don't worry about them. They won't die out," Ethan said with a shrug. "The world can be harsh and unpredictable. They might fight for a new leader tonight. Once that defeated Scyther recovers and hears what happened, it might even sneak back to compete for the position again."

Sabrina nodded. She had asked out of curiosity, not sympathy for the Scyther. Ethan's explanation answered her question, and her interest faded.

They pushed through the branches and left the forest.

Ethan took a Poké Ball from his belt. Without any further action from him, it vanished in an instant.

Capturing Scyther had given him a thirteenth Pokémon. Under the Pokémon League's rules and the settings of his belt, a ball beyond his carrying limit had to be transferred back to Cerise Laboratory. After some thought, he had decided to send Blastoise.

He already had Gyarados among his Water types, so their roles overlapped somewhat. Weighing his options, he chose to leave Blastoise in the laboratory's care for now.

His phone regained its signal as they emerged. It vibrated, and he saw a message from Professor Ivy. She had also tried calling half an hour earlier, while he was in the forest. What did she want?

Ethan did not call her back immediately. First he messaged Chloe to tell her about Blastoise and ask her to look after his Pokémon.

Her answer came almost at once: "Don't worry, I'll take good care of it. I'll go straight to Cerise Park after school~ :)"

Her quick reply warmed him and made him smile. Wasn't Chloe supposed to be a good student? She had answered him during class.

He shook his head. When he looked up, Sabrina was watching him with a meaningful expression. Ethan's smile disappeared. He bent over his phone with an air of seriousness and opened Ivy's message.

Since their relationship had deepened, Sabrina had become especially attentive to him. She would not welcome any hint that he was flirting with someone else. Ethan was pleased by her concern. The more she cared, the more important he must be to her.

When she saw nothing further to object to, Sabrina looked away. Ethan let out a quiet breath and turned his attention to the message.

It was fairly long. He skimmed it and understood that Ivy had written after her call failed. The news was simple: she and Professor Oak had finished their research on Shamouti Island and published the final paper. She had wanted him to know.

At the bottom, she had included a link. Ethan opened it and landed on a respected scientific website, directly on the paper Oak and Ivy had published.

Reading more carefully, he found much new material compared with the draft they had shown him. His butterfly effect explanation remained, as did his name among the authors.

He was excited, certainly, but felt little beyond that. It gave him a title as a research contributor, perhaps, without changing his everyday life.

Still, he decided to call both professors.

Professor Oak exchanged a few polite words with him. They did not know each other well, so there was little else to discuss. Even in their brief conversation, though, Ethan could hear that Oak wanted to encourage his future work.

Oak likely knew that the new Indigo League Champion had a promising future if he continued to grow. At Oak's standing, he had no need to flatter Ethan, but being on friendly terms with him could do no harm.

Professor Ivy was much warmer. She spoke with an easy attentiveness that put Ethan at ease and even made a few jokes. Before hanging up, she invited him to visit her laboratory whenever he had the time.

Sabrina was standing nearby. Ethan did not dare say much in response, and offered a few noncommittal words.

After the call, he saw that Sabrina was growing impatient. He put an arm around her shoulders and smiled. "Sorry to keep you waiting. We should get moving."

He was about to summon Charizard when she stopped him. "Wait. Let's use something else this time. I'm tired of seeing everything from the air."

Ethan considered it, then pointed toward town. "I saw a passenger ship at the harbor earlier. Let's find out whether it has a route we can use."

Sabrina agreed, and they retraced their steps to the town on Murcott Island. They crossed it and reached a small harbor.

Even before they arrived, they could see a medium-sized passenger ship, three or four stories high, moored in the bay. They asked about its route. Its next stop was Hamlin Island, separated by only a stretch of sea from their destination, Kumquat Island.
