As soon as the battle began, May eagerly gave Beautifly its first command.

“Beautifly, use Tackle!”

Beautifly rode the wind, gliding toward Taillow before charging at it. Glittering scales shed from its beating wings caught the sunlight and traced a long, shimmering path behind it.

It was a distinctive trait of butterfly Pokémon. Nearly all of them left some of that sparkling powder in their wake when they flew hard, and it was lovely to see.

“Taillow, dodge!”

A simple charge was never going to be easy to land on another Pokémon in flight. At Ash's command, Taillow twisted nimbly away from the Tackle.

Then Ash ordered a Tackle of his own, countering with the same move. But just as Taillow had dodged, Beautifly could avoid a straightforward charge too. It tilted its body at the last moment, narrowly slipping past Taillow. May breathed a long sigh of relief.

She had only recently begun traveling with Pokémon. When one of her partners was attacked, she couldn't remain as calm as Ethan, or even Ash. Her emotions rose and fell with Beautifly's every move.

Yet under that pressure, May showed the instincts of a Gym Leader's daughter. “Beautifly, use String Shot!” she called at just the right moment.

Beautifly's long, needlelike mouth expelled a strand of silk about as thick as a little finger. The thread seemed to move of its own accord; in an instant, it had wrapped around Taillow's wings and bound them together.

The spectators brightened. Max blurted out, “That was great timing, May!”

Unable to spread its wings, Taillow couldn't fly. It was too small to tear the silk apart by sheer strength, but the thread holding it up kept it from falling to the ground.

That surprise would have troubled an ordinary Trainer. Unfortunately for May, her opponent was Ash, who had a knack for inventing a way out.

“Taillow, find the gaps in the silk and break free!” he shouted.

Taillow darted this way and that within the threads, never letting them tighten completely. Beautifly couldn't quite hold it fast. The struggle reached a stalemate, and May, still a novice, began to panic.

She wanted to change the situation, but no matter how urgently she thought, she lacked the experience to see what to do. Then she remembered some of the battles she had watched at her father's Gym. An idea struck.

“Use Gust!”

Beautifly beat its wings. A powerful gust laden with countless glittering scales swept toward Taillow. It snapped the silk connecting the two Pokémon and drove Taillow into the ground with a hard thud.

“Well done!” Ethan and Misty cheered together. From their excitement, it was obvious whose side they were on.

Ash had no attention to spare for their favoritism. His eyes were fixed on Taillow lying motionless. “Taillow, get up!”

Perhaps Ash's voice reached it, or perhaps the silk around it had cushioned the fall. Either way, Taillow opened its eyes.

“That's it!” Ash grinned. “Break those threads!”

May had been delighted, thinking the fall would finish Taillow. Seeing it struggle to its feet sent her into a panic again.

“Use Silver Wind!”

Ethan raised an eyebrow. That was a poor choice. Misty had mentioned when they found May that Beautifly had tried Silver Wind many times already, failing every time. Betting the battle on a move it had yet to perform successfully would give Ash an opening.

Just as Ethan expected, Silver Wind failed again. Beautifly beat its wings, but the wind it gathered quickly dispersed in midair.

Taillow seized the chance to tear free of the silk. It flew back into the sky and struck Beautifly with Wing Attack, knocking it down.

Beautifly hit the ground and fell completely unconscious. Ethan felt a pang of regret. May had been so close to earning that remarkably common achievement: beating Ash as a novice Trainer!

Ethan and Misty rose and went over to the others. They were about to speak when a voice sounded behind them.

“That was a fine performance. With that kind of skill, you should have no trouble entering a Pokémon Contest!”

It was a man's voice, and it came from nearby. They turned to see a man around thirty, wearing a white headband and traditional clothes, walking toward them while applauding.

“Oh, no...” May smiled bashfully at the praise. “I still have a long way to go. You're too ki—”

Before she could finish, the man strode straight past her and stopped in front of Ash.

May stared.

The man patted Ash's shoulder and smiled. “Your Taillow did wonderfully. You're entering this Pokémon Contest too, aren't you?”

What? May was dumbfounded. He had been praising Ash, not her!

Ash looked surprised as well. He scratched his head awkwardly. “Um... you've got the wrong person. I'm not entering. The girl standing in front of me is.”

The man finally realized his mistake and turned to May.

She nodded uncomfortably. “Y-yes. I'm the one entering.”

“I see! Sorry, I got that wrong.” He laughed without looking embarrassed. “Your Beautifly performed well too. But I can tell that neither of you has entered a competition like this before, have you? You both seem very nervous!”
