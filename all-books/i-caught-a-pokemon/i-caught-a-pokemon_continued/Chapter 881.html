That evening, more than two hours after they had parted, Ethan sat across from Miki, Kuni, and Zuki in his guest room. The three sisters had bathed again.

This time, even Miki had nothing to say. What could she say? The boundary she had tried to draw earlier had fallen apart in the face of what they had done.

Ethan looked at the three women. Zuki met his eyes without flinching. Miki and Kuni looked down, shy and embarrassed. All three still wore a blush from the afternoon and seemed much more subdued than usual.

Ethan sighed to himself. He had made another mistake, but he would take responsibility for it.

“I think we need to talk about what we are to each other now.”

Miki and Kuni grew even more self-conscious. Though both were older than he was, neither spoke. They waited for him to continue, with a hope they were reluctant to admit.

Ethan paused. “None of us intended for things to happen this way. But I won't run from what happened. You may think I'm greedy for even saying this. Still, if you want me to—and unless your hearts belong to someone else—I will take responsibility for both of you.”

He had not said it outright, but they understood. He was offering to be with all three sisters.

It sounded like something from an anime or novel. If another man had said it to Miki, she might have slapped him. Now the circumstances were different.

She and Kuni remembered their own actions and Ethan's during the night and the afternoon. Those hours would remain with them for the rest of their lives. They could not simply pretend nothing had happened, leave him with Zuki, and step aside.

To their minds, his willingness to accept them both was the best outcome left.

Still, Miki and Kuni did not answer immediately. They turned to Zuki. Whatever they wanted, she should have a say. Only a short time had passed, but Ethan had already acknowledged Zuki as his partner.

Zuki understood her sisters' looks. She loved them, and her own actions had started all this. That was why she had sat quietly through the conversation. Ordinarily, she might never have tolerated sharing his attention.

She smiled at them. “Wouldn't this be good? We sisters can stay together, without worrying that we'll have to part someday.”

Her words made Miki and Kuni think of how close the three sisters themselves had been during those two encounters. The memories embarrassed them, though the drug had been responsible for much of it.

Zuki had given her answer. Miki and Kuni exchanged a look and saw that they agreed.

Miki smiled ruefully. “We've already been with you. I don't think either of us can treat that lightly. If you'll have us, we'd like to stay at your side. We won't ask you for any particular status.”

It was an old-fashioned way to see themselves. Ethan found it both moving and sad. Plenty of people would regard a sexual encounter as no reason to commit to a life together. These women spoke of lifelong loyalty. Their conservatism could be stubborn, but it meant they did not make promises casually.

By a bewildering turn of events, all three beautiful sisters had chosen him. He knew he would owe them more than words in return.

He had come to Ecruteak to discuss Eevee's evolution. They had not settled that question, yet he had gained three partners instead. That was a weighty obligation, one he might spend a lifetime honoring.

It also gave him a place in Johto where he could feel at home. The Temple of the Sea was a place he could visit, but it did not give him the same sense of belonging.

Once they had defined the relationship, Miki and Kuni relaxed a little. Beneath their lingering embarrassment, both felt glad. Whatever else had happened, they believed they had followed their hearts.

Then Kuni remembered something and cried out. “Oh! Weren't we supposed to perform tonight?”

“Oh no!” Miki and Zuki looked at the clock.

The five sisters were busy performers, taking the stage nearly every day or every other day. The upheaval in their household had made them forget tonight's scheduled show entirely.

“What do we do?” Kuni grimaced. “There's no time to do our makeup, change into costumes, and reach the Ecruteak Dance Theater. And…”

She glanced at the marks visible on Miki's and Zuki's necks. Even with makeup, they could hardly appear before an audience looking like this.

Miki looked at Ethan and gave a helpless smile. “We'll have to call our manager and cancel tonight's performance.”

“I suppose so,” Kuni said with a sigh. “We've never canceled a show since our debut. That streak ends today, and the audience already inside will have to leave.”

“It can't be helped,” Miki said. “We have something important to settle here. We could announce that we're ill and take a few days off. Once things are calmer—and Ethan has gone back to traveling—we can perform again.”

The way she said Ethan's name sounded natural and intimate. Kuni and Zuki considered the proposal, then nodded. These past two days had changed the course of their lives. They could hardly return to the stage before finding their footing.

“That's best,” Kuni agreed. Then she frowned. “But if we forgot a performance, surely Naoko and Sayo didn't. Why haven't they reminded us?”

Her sisters' expressions changed. Where were the younger two?

A possibility occurred to all three at once. Perhaps Naoko and Sayo had seen what happened in the dining room that afternoon. It had been broad daylight, after all. If so, of course they had stayed away.

The thought made the older sisters burn with embarrassment. If the younger girls had witnessed them, they hardly knew how to face them.

…

After the afternoon's exertion, Ethan washed and went to bed early. This time he slept alone in an actual guest room.

He and Miki, Kuni, and Zuki had agreed on their relationship, but it would take time to grow comfortable living as a family. More immediately, he was exhausted. His body needed rest.

He slept soundly until someone opened his door the following morning. Slowly, he opened his eyes.

Zuki stood there, neatly dressed, come to wake him. She wore light makeup and a subtle fragrance.

“You look beautiful today,” Ethan said before he could stop himself.

He was not given to sweet talk with other women. Zuki was different now.

Her lips curved at praise from the man she liked, though she still gave him a pointed look. She could change for someone she cared about without ceasing to be herself. That proud spirit was part of what made her appealing.

“Get up. Everyone's waiting for you at breakfast.”

Ethan dressed and washed while she waited. Then they went side by side to the dining room.

There he saw May, Naoko, and Sayo at close range for the first time in what felt like ages. So much had happened in two days. He had glimpsed them from across the courtyard before lunch yesterday, but sharing a room again felt different.

The others reacted to his arrival in their own ways.

Miki greeted him with a nod, a shy smile, and a faint blush. She seemed to have accepted what had happened. Kuni looked much the same, though she was more embarrassed and quickly turned her eyes away.

May, Naoko, and Sayo tried to act as though they knew nothing. Their darting eyes and tense expressions betrayed them.

Ethan cleared his throat and sat beside Miki, in the place a man heading the household might take.

The meaning was plain to Naoko and Sayo. They looked at Miki. She made no objection; she had even set out Ethan's bowl and chopsticks in advance. Their older sisters' relationship with him had changed profoundly.

Naoko and Sayo exchanged a glance. Would they have to call him their brother-in-law now? Their eldest sister's? Their second sister's? Their third sister's? It was hard to untangle.

“Everyone's here,” Miki said softly. “Let's eat.”

“Thank you for the meal!”

Breakfast was unusually quiet. They ate slowly, with only the sounds of chopsticks touching plates and people chewing. After the last two days, the stillness was welcome. They needed time to adjust to one another.

For a moment, it felt like sitting with a real family. Some awkwardness remained, but Ethan thought that would ease with time.

May liked the atmosphere too. She was used to following Ethan from place to place, and Miki, Kuni, and Zuki all treated her warmly.

After breakfast, Miki and Kuni cleared the dishes. They returned, and everyone sat together around the table to talk.

Miki asked the question on their minds. “Ethan, how long do you plan to stay?”

Using only his given name still felt a little awkward to her. She was older than he was, they were not married, and she did not want to speak to him too intimately in front of Naoko and Sayo. She settled on Ethan. He would not have minded any name spoken with affection.

He thought for a moment as the five sisters watched him.

“You know I have two things ahead of me: my battle with Brandon and May's Grand Festival. Brandon hasn't contacted me yet, so there's no need to rush to him. The Grand Festival is still some time away, and May can practice well here. I'd like to stay a few more days.”

Miki and the others breathed easier. None wanted him to leave so soon. Now that their relationships had changed, the older sisters would have liked him to remain for good, though they knew that was impossible.

May smiled as well. Naoko and Sayo had helped her improve quickly, and she could feel the difference. She also loved the food here.

Ethan looked around the table. Seeing Miki, Kuni, and Zuki so pleased that he would stay a little longer moved him. Their feelings for him were real.

He took three Great Balls from his belt pouch. One was marked with a flame, one with a water drop, and one with a lightning bolt. He set them on the table and looked at the three older sisters.

“There's something I need to tell you too.”
