New Bark Town: the town where the winds of a new beginning blow.

If Kanto's starting town, Pallet Town, was a cozy little place, New Bark Town was practically a small city by comparison. In the games it had been a remote town with few residents. Now that Ethan had set foot here, he found it far larger and more prosperous than Pallet Town.

It had tall buildings as well as shopping centers, schools, a hospital, and even an amusement park. The Johto Pokémon League had built a Pokémon Center here especially for new Trainers. Yet, like Cherrygrove City, New Bark Town was quiet. Few people were out on the streets, and those who were moved at an unhurried pace.

As Ethan and Sabrina walked toward the town center, it was clear that she was still thinking about the girl they had just met.

She glanced at him. "I've noticed you're always especially friendly to cute girls. Misty, Chloe at Cerise Laboratory, Melody in the Orange Islands, and now that girl. You don't have some peculiar preference, do you?"

The hairs on Ethan's arms stood up.

Danger. Danger. Danger!

"Hey, don't say things like that!" His heart hammered as he hurried to deny it. "They're my friends, or like little sisters to me. I've never had any improper thoughts about them. And didn't you see how well I treated Ash? I like helping younger Trainers. How does that give me a peculiar preference?"

Sabrina studied him for a long moment. "Really? I was only making an observation. Why do you look so nervous?"

"I'm not nervous!" He wiped the cold sweat from his forehead, trying to look unconcerned. "It's just a dangerous question. If someone overheard you, they might think I was a bad person!"

Fortunately, Sabrina did not press him. Ethan breathed a little easier.

Hadn't she asked something similar before? Had his behavior lately been too brazen? Had Sabrina noticed something again? A psychic girl's sixth sense was a frightening thing.

To keep her from asking more, Ethan quickly took out his phone and contacted Looker again. They agreed to meet at a coffee shop, which Ethan found easily with the map on his phone.

It was a small shop on a street corner. Evening was approaching, but it was not yet dinnertime, and hardly anyone was inside. As soon as Ethan entered, he spotted Looker skulking in a corner.

Looker wore his usual trench coat, but he had added sunglasses and a wide-brimmed hat. If he had not been the only customer, and if his build had not been so familiar, Ethan might not have recognized him.

Ethan dropped into the seat opposite him. "Long time no see. What is this? Is someone following you?"

"Keep your voice down!" Looker removed his glasses, revealing a face so haggard that he looked as though he'd been overindulging for weeks. He glanced toward the server and put a finger to his lips. "No one's following me. I'm following someone else."

"Following someone?" Ethan looked him up and down. "Come on. Dressed like that, you'd stand out in any crowd. They'll notice you at once!"

"I don't have much choice." Looker spread his hands. "Team Rocket has circulated my picture everywhere. Practically all of them know my face. Without a disguise, they'd recognize me even faster."

Looker noticed Sabrina and nodded in greeting. Before Ethan could say anything else, he raised his eyebrows at him with a leering expression.

Well done, kid. You really did win Sabrina over!

Ethan had no trouble reading him. He glanced at Sabrina, then answered with a look and a lift of his own eyebrows.

What can I say? I'm irresistible.

Oh, please. When did you get so shameless?

What would you know, you lonely old bachelor?

It was the kind of wordless understanding men shared. Sabrina could not possibly follow their exchange. She stared at them, puzzled by all the eyebrow waggling. Both looked rather indecent, and she was sure they were discussing something offensive.

Her expression grew more suspicious, and her gaze more dangerous. Ethan hastily cleared his throat and turned to Looker with a serious face.

"Back to business. Who exactly are you following here?"

Looker pointed through the window. "Look over there."

Across the street, inside a restaurant, a man was eating dinner alone with his back to the coffee shop. He wore a tight black top and dark jeans. Ethan could not identify him from behind, but one thing stood out: his medium-length red hair.

"Who is he?"

"Giovanni's son," Looker said quietly.

"Giovanni's son?"

Sabrina froze. She and Giovanni had been Gym Leaders for years, yet this was the first she had heard that he had a son. Ethan frowned too. Did Looker mean Silver?

So that was why Looker had been so secretive, and why he had traveled all the way to New Bark Town: he was tailing Silver.

"Surprised?" Looker asked when he saw their faces. "We only found out recently that Giovanni has a son. The sly old man hid him well."

Ethan tried a question. "So you're following him in the hope that he'll lead you to Team Rocket?"

Looker nodded. "His name is Silver. I intend to use him to find Giovanni's hiding place. And I suspect he has a scheme of his own for coming here."

Ethan sighed softly. Looker was bound to be disappointed. Silver did not get along well with Giovanni. Though he was Giovanni's son, he disapproved of his father's withdrawal and hated what Team Rocket did. He wanted to make his own way, had almost nothing to do with the organization, and generally acted alone.

Ethan could hardly tell Looker any of that. Besides, he wanted to see what Silver was doing in New Bark Town himself.
