Since it was still early, the pair did not rest at the hotel. Instead, they decided to visit the venue, familiarize themselves with the surroundings, and learn more about the tournament in advance.

The Whirl Cup was divided into preliminary and main rounds. The entrants would first compete in three preliminary matches tomorrow, narrowing the field to sixty-four Trainers. Those sixty-four would then proceed through several elimination rounds until a final winner emerged.

Hmm. The format did not sound any different from the Indigo League Conference.

However, because no Badges were required to enter, the number of participants was surprisingly large despite this being a local tournament held solely by the Whirl Islands!

When Ethan had lined up to register, fifty or sixty Trainers had already been waiting ahead of him. Not only had local Trainers from the Whirl Islands entered enthusiastically, but people from farther away had also come after hearing the news. It was easy to see how popular the event was.

But because anyone could register without needing Badges, the competitors' skill levels varied wildly. They included everyone from men in their sixties and seventies to children only seven or eight years old!

For these people, how well they performed was beside the point. Participation was what mattered!

And what if they somehow won?

Wouldn't they become famous overnight!?

The pair followed the street toward the tournament grounds.

The preliminary round would take place simultaneously across sixteen battlefields. After registering, Ethan had been informed that he was assigned to Field F.

He had also received a map to keep Trainers from getting lost. It clearly marked the location of every battlefield, a very thoughtful arrangement.

They had barely reached the area containing the venues, before Ethan could even find his own battlefield, when someone suddenly called their names.

"Ethan! Miss Erika!"

Turning around, they found Ash and his friends standing at the entrance to one of the battlefields!

Ash, Misty, and Brock were eating popsicles as they waved to Ethan and Erika.

Those three certainly had sharp eyes. Almost every time their groups met, they were the ones who spotted Ethan first!

Walking over, Ethan laughed. "I knew you wouldn't miss a tournament like this! Of course you'd enter!"

Even so, he felt somewhat emotional.

When the group had parted last time, they had all sensed that they would meet again before long. Ethan simply had not expected it to happen this quickly!

Ash truly was like the chosen child of this world. Perhaps it was ordained by fate: wherever something exciting happened, Ash was sure to appear.

After everyone exchanged greetings, Ethan turned toward Misty.

"Did you enter too?"

"Of course!" Misty puffed out her modest chest. "I'm a Water-type Pokémon Trainer! How could I miss something like this?"

Misty suddenly remembered something and hurriedly asked, "Oh, right, Ethan! Which battlefield were you assigned to?"

"Field F."

"Great! I avoided you!" Misty patted her chest, looking delighted.

Although she was a Gym Leader and aspired to become an outstanding Water-type Pokémon Trainer, she knew her own limits. She understood that she definitely could not defeat Ethan.

Before seeing him, Misty had even wanted to compete with Ash and find out which of them could win the championship. Now that Ethan had appeared, she changed her goal almost immediately.

Hmm... Second place would be pretty good too!

Seeing how relieved she looked, Ethan chuckled. "Don't worry. So many people entered the Whirl Cup that the chances of meeting during the preliminary round are very low."

As Ethan had said, if each entrant needed to win three matches before only sixty-four remained, then more than five hundred people had clearly registered!

That was an enormous number, almost equal to the scale of the Indigo League Conference!

With so many competitors, the chances of encountering one another in the preliminary round were extremely small.

Ash had a different opinion. He gave Misty a disdainful glance. "Misty, don't you have any ambition? How can you admit defeat so easily?"

Then he turned toward Ethan and clenched his fist, his fighting spirit seeming to blaze around him.

"Ethan! Whether we meet in the preliminaries or the main tournament, my goal is to defeat you and become the champion!!"

"Oh? That's the spirit!" Ethan laughed at Ash's display. "Then I'll look forward to it!"

...

That afternoon, Ethan treated everyone to a delicious seafood feast at a nearby restaurant.

He had to admit that Ash and Misty had been quite lucky to secure beds at the Pokémon Center. The place might be crowded and chaotic, but they could gather all kinds of information there, learn the latest developments immediately, and adjust their teams as circumstances changed.

Compared with them, Ethan's accommodations were more comfortable, yet they left him feeling less involved in the tournament.

After they had eaten and drunk their fill, Ethan and Erika said goodbye to Ash's group. When they returned to the hotel that evening, the atmosphere between them once again grew strange.

The suite had two bedrooms and a living room, all luxuriously decorated, so privacy was not a concern. But there was only one bathroom, which meant they had to bathe one after the other. That indistinctly intimate feeling was almost like living with someone of the opposite sex!

Ever the gentleman, Ethan gave Erika the main bedroom. After trying several times and failing to change his mind, she could only accept it.

Perhaps realizing that the atmosphere had become too intimate, Erika's face flushed faintly. She said nothing for the rest of the evening, took the first bath, and retired to her room early.

The bathroom was still filled with steam when Ethan entered, and he almost seemed able to sense Erika's lingering presence.

He, too, felt that something in their relationship had changed, but now was not the time to dwell on it.

After bathing, he lay on his bed with his thoughts entirely occupied by tomorrow's tournament. Before he knew it, he had fallen asleep.

The night passed quietly, without any midnight visit.

At eight the next morning, after breakfast, Ethan and Erika arrived at the preliminary venue early and met up with Ash and the others.

The spectators had already taken their seats, filling the stands on all sides. Although this was only the preliminary round, the tournament took place just once every three years, so the scene remained extraordinarily lively.

Erika and Brock sat together in the stands. One specialized in Grass types and the other in Rock and Ground types, leaving neither with any connection to this tournament. All they could do was serve as the cheering section.

Ceremonial cannons burst repeatedly in the sky, creating an atmosphere that was both festive and chaotic.

Ethan, Ash, and Misty entered the waiting room through the competitors' passage. On the television screens inside, they could watch the Whirl Cup's opening ceremony.

Time passed little by little. As nine o'clock approached, so did the official start of the preliminary round.

When the announcement summoning the competitors sounded through the waiting room, Ethan, Ash, and Misty stacked their hands together, then raised them high.

"Let's do this!"

They lowered their arms and shared a laugh before heading toward their respective battlefields.
