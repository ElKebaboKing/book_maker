“Hello, Roxanne. I'm Ethan, and I've come to challenge Rustboro Gym.”

“And I'm Misty,” she added. “I'm traveling with Ethan.”

When Ethan introduced himself, Roxanne looked closely at his face. Surprise crossed her expression.

“It's you!” she said, her voice brightening. “Only a few days ago, my students and I watched recordings of your Silver Conference battles and analyzed them together. I never expected to meet you in person so soon!”

The students around her hurried over as though a celebrity had appeared. Excitedly, they asked Ethan for photos, and one of them even produced an autograph pen as if by magic.

Their enthusiasm was overwhelming, but understandable. These students had not yet left school. They rarely had a chance to meet a Trainer who had already achieved so much in Pokémon tournaments.

Beside Ethan, Misty proudly puffed out her still-small chest.

Seeing the person she liked held in such high regard made her happy too.

Ethan could only spare a little time to satisfy the students' requests. When their excitement finally eased, he turned his attention back to Roxanne.

She smiled. “So you've come to Hoenn. Are you planning to enter the Ever Grande Conference? I can't wait to see how you perform there. Since it's you, would you like to battle my strongest Pokémon? To be honest, after watching your matches, I've wanted to face you myself.”

“Sorry,” Ethan said, shaking his head. “I've left my main team in someone else's care. This journey is mostly about training my new Pokémon. Please don't make any special arrangements for me. Treat me like a Trainer who's only just started out.”

“A beginner with two conference trophies? That's quite a remarkable ‘beginner.’” Roxanne covered her mouth as she laughed, then nodded. “All right. I'll treat you like an ordinary challenger and use my usual Gym team.”

The students perked up at the prospect of a Gym battle. They moved to either side of the field and began setting up recording equipment.

Roxanne gave Ethan an apologetic smile. “Would you mind if we record the match? As you can see, I teach at a Pokémon Academy as well as lead this Gym. These are my students and assistants. We usually record our battles with challengers, then discuss and analyze them afterward so the students can gain experience. I'd like to keep a recording of this one too.”

“Of course,” Ethan said with a nod.

His tactics and other secrets were not at stake, and the Pokémon he would use were still new. If the students wanted to watch, he saw no reason to refuse. He could consider it a contribution to the Academy.

After a little more conversation, he and Roxanne took their places in the Trainer's areas on opposite sides of the field.

One of the older students served as referee and announced the rules.

“The Rustboro Gym battle is about to begin! Each side may use two Pokémon. This is an elimination match, and only the challenger may switch Pokémon during the battle!”

They were ordinary Gym rules. Most Gyms used much the same format.

With the rules settled, Ethan looked at Roxanne again.

At first glance, her clothes suited a quiet, dignified young woman. Once she stood on the battlefield, though, she showed another side: confident, with a different look on her face.

As Gym Leader, Roxanne sent out her Pokémon first. She threw a Poké Ball, and Geodude appeared on the field.

Geodude was a very common Rock-type Pokémon. For Trainers who specialized in Rock and Ground types, like Brock and Roxanne, having one on the team was hardly unusual.

Even Trainers outside those specialties often caught a Geodude if they favored a tougher battling style.

But being Gym Leaders did not mean Brock and Roxanne had Geodude of equal strength. Ethan would have to battle Roxanne's to find out which was stronger.

As for what to use against it...

After a moment's thought, Ethan sent out Eevee.

Of the Pokémon he had caught recently, Eevee was the one most ready for a Gym match. It had not evolved yet, but it had already built up plenty of experience and strength. All it lacked was the right item to trigger its evolution.

Once he found one and Eevee evolved, Ethan was sure it could rise to the level of Dragonite, Scizor, and Tyranitar on his current team.

Eevee appeared, eager to fight, and studied its opponent intently.

Roxanne smiled again. “Unlike you, your Eevee doesn't seem very composed. It looks impatient.”

“That's true. Eevee's lively,” Ethan said, looking at it fondly. “But that's no bad thing. Pokémon with different personalities can bring out different tactics.”

The Pokémon on the field were tense and ready to fight, but their Trainers were relaxed enough to joke with each other.

The one person who was not enjoying herself was Misty, seated in the stands.

Perhaps she was imagining things, or perhaps she had grown too quick to worry. Roxanne was so friendly toward Ethan that Misty feared the Gym Leader might develop designs on him if they spent more time together.

After all, Ethan's charm had already won over Sabrina and Erika. Misty herself had fallen for him too.

What reassured her a little was that although Roxanne had the same standing as Sabrina and Erika, she was nowhere near as beautiful.

Ethan would never fall for her!

Misty felt rather pleased with that thought. With Sabrina and Erika setting the standard, and the world's most beautiful girl sitting right here beside him, why would Ethan be interested in someone so ordinary-looking?

Ethan was focused on the battle and had no idea what Misty was thinking.

If he had known, he would surely have given her a sharp knock on the head!

Still, she was right about one thing. After Sabrina and Erika, and even Maya, Ethan's standards had indeed risen. Girls who were not especially striking no longer caught his eye.

Roxanne looked fairly ordinary at first glance and pretty enough on a closer look, but she did not make his heart stir at all.
