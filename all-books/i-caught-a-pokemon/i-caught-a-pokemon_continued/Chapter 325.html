Boom!

Gyarados smashed through the research building's doors. The force of the impact sent the Rocket members and their Pokémon behind them sprawling. The last defense outside the building had fallen.

International Police officers poured in and arrested the Rockets who had not escaped. Looker left some officers to handle them, then pressed deeper into the building with Ethan and the rest.

Perhaps the Rockets had lost their will to fight. The group passed through a lobby, corridors, a lounge, and a dining area without meeting any serious resistance. With Gyarados clearing the way, they reached the building's central laboratory in only a few minutes.

The room was filled with electronic equipment. Ethan immediately spotted a man standing at a raised control station, looking down at them. He was so composed that he seemed to have been waiting for their arrival.

Apart from him, the laboratory was empty. No researchers remained, and even Tyson was nowhere to be seen.

Computers had been smashed all around the room. Papers lay on the desks, left behind in the rush, and chairs had been knocked over. The others had fled in a hurry.

Ethan also noticed a small device resembling a signal transmitter beside the man. Electricity danced across it with a dangerous crackle.

Before Ethan could look more closely, the man gave an elegant bow and introduced himself without waiting for Looker to speak.

"Hello. I'm Professor Sebastian. Welcome to my research facility. I've been expecting you for quite some time."

Sebastian was one of Team Rocket's important scientists. Unlike the hot-tempered Dr. Namba or the stern Dr. Zager, he spoke calmly and had a quick mind. He rarely acted on his own, preferring to leave tasks to his subordinates.

"So you're the one in charge?" Looker studied him. "What exactly are you doing here? What is Project R? Tell us the truth!"

Ever since he had heard the project's name, the question had gnawed at Looker. He needed to know the answer.

Sebastian chuckled, showing no desire to conceal it. "The complicated explanation would be wasted on you. In simple terms, Project R stimulates the DNA of Pokémon at their initial stage, forcing them to evolve and making them stronger and more destructive."

"I thought so!" Looker said. "Then you're responsible for the recent Gyarados attacks at the Lake of Rage? And for the reports of a red Gyarados there?"

Apart from the story Ethan remembered, he had heard Blackthorn residents discussing that red Gyarados when he passed through the city. Someone had discovered it recently, and word had spread quickly. According to the witnesses, it was stronger than an ordinary Gyarados, and its red scales made it easy to spot in the lake.

Ethan remembered, too, that Lance would eventually catch that Gyarados and make it an important member of his team.

"That's true," Sebastian admitted, then shook his head. "But it wasn't what we intended. That Gyarados evolved suddenly when we exposed it to the waves as a Magikarp. A great deal of Magikarp DNA remained in its body, turning its scales red. At best, it's a failed specimen. We meant to dispose of it, but it broke out of its enclosure and escaped. That's why you found your way here."

Because Sebastian was speaking so freely, Looker began to think he might be ready to surrender. He let his guard down a little.

"Are you telling us all this because you plan to give up and confess, hoping for a lighter sentence?"

"Give up? No, no, no..." Sebastian smiled softly. "We built this facility here after a thorough search because we saw potential in Magikarp. We thought Project R could make them evolve into even stronger Gyarados for Team Rocket to use. But after so many experiments, the results never met my expectations. I was preparing to change to another Pokémon altogether."

His voice rose with excitement. "Then I saw that Gyarados of yours. I finally understood that my research had been aimed in the wrong direction. If I experiment on a Gyarados that's already powerful, it will become stronger still!"

He fixed a burning gaze on Ethan's Gyarados.

Looker did not understand every detail, but he knew he had to stop whatever Sebastian intended. He pulled a pair of handcuffs from his belt and hurried toward him.

Sebastian gave him a sidelong glance, snorted, and worked the controls in front of him.

The small transmitter beside him swung around. It pointed at Ethan—or, more precisely, at the Gyarados behind him.

"What are you doing?" Looker shouted. He rushed forward, but after only a few steps, he saw Sebastian press a red button.

A visible wave burst from the transmitter and struck Ethan's Gyarados directly. It came too fast for Gyarados to dodge, and neither Ethan nor Sabrina could react in time.

Gyarados howled in pain. It twisted violently, its face contorted. Ethan frowned and started toward it, but then he saw the old, scarred scales along its body fall away and new ones take their place. Its whole body swelled visibly, growing far larger, and turned crimson red.

"Ha ha ha! It worked! It worked!" Sebastian burst into laughter before anyone could recover from the shock. "I should have realized it sooner. Experimenting on those dull-witted Magikarp was useless! Only an already powerful Gyarados can become the perfect ultimate weapon. Now you'll see the true power of Project R!"

"You madman!" Looker cried. "What have you done?"

Before anyone could collect themselves, a deafening roar filled the room.

"Raaah!"

Gyarados reared up, its eyes bloodred, and roared toward the ceiling. Its voice was more frightening than ever, and its enlarged body made it an overwhelming sight.

At last, it lowered its head. Everyone looked into its eyes and saw that every trace of reason had vanished. Only limitless anger and a savage urge to attack remained.

Gyarados had lost control completely.
