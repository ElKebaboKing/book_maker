Now it was time to look at Ethan's Pokémon. Since he had known what type his opponent would use, he made no changes in response to Metagross. Instead, he followed the strategy he had prepared the previous night and sent out Blastoise!

That's right. Blastoise!

Ethan had contacted Chloe from the Pokémon Center the previous night and asked her to transfer Blastoise to him. He wanted it for this Gym battle and to keep a Pokémon that had gone so long without fighting from growing rusty!

The Pokémon that had rendered distinguished service during the Indigo League would fight for Ethan once more today, pouring its heart into battle for its Trainer!

The two Pokémon stared each other down, fierce fighting spirit blazing in their eyes.

Within Ethan's roster, Blastoise ranked above average in strength. It could not compare with first-team Pokémon such as Gyarados and Gardevoir, so it was difficult to say which side would win against this Metagross.

Even so, the outcome was hardly certain.

Water-type moves held no advantage against Steel or Psychic types, but Steel-type attacks inflicted only half damage against a Water-type Pokémon.

That undoubtedly relieved much of the pressure on Blastoise.

Of course, that did not mean Blastoise was guaranteed to win. The result would depend on how the two Pokémon performed in the arena.

The referee waved his flags, signaling the start of the match. Metagross immediately charged toward Blastoise.

"Blastoise, use Hydro Pump!" Ethan commanded calmly in the face of the onrushing Metagross.

At his instruction, Blastoise lowered its body as the two cannons on its back began gathering energy. The next instant, once the energy reached its peak, both cannons fired at once!

Fwoosh! Fwoosh!

Two thick streams of water erupted from the cannons and shot directly toward Metagross!

A powerful Water-type move like Hydro Pump could inflict tremendous damage. Even without a type advantage, Jasmine did not dare let Metagross take the attack lightly.

She shouted, "Use Agility!"

Just before Hydro Pump struck, Metagross's speed rose sharply. It accelerated to one side and evaded the attack.

Hydro Pump missed, but Ethan was unconcerned.

Unlike an ordinary cannon shell, Hydro Pump did not become useless after a single shot. The move consisted of a continuous stream of water. As long as the water kept flowing, the attack would not end!

If the opponent evaded, Blastoise merely needed to aim again!

Blastoise rotated its body, and the two cannons on its back turned with it.

The thick streams drew an arc through the air and closed on Metagross once more. This time, Metagross had moved too close to the edge of the battlefield to evade comfortably. Hydro Pump struck it directly.

The rushing water pinned Metagross in place, leaving it unable to move.

However, the attack had already expended much of its energy. Hydro Pump stopped after only a few seconds and did not inflict significant damage.

Ethan was about to order Blastoise to repeat the attack when Metagross's eyes suddenly began to glow. A burst of Confusion followed and struck Blastoise.

Blastoise flew high into the air before crashing heavily back down.

Thud!

Its thick body struck the ground with a muffled impact, and the entire field seemed to tremble.

Metagross had already rushed in front of it by the time Blastoise climbed back to its feet.

Metagross raised its two forelimbs. Its steel-hard claws swung at Blastoise like a stream of comets, one strike following another without pause!

It was the Steel-type move Meteor Mash!

Ethan recognized the move but remained completely unmoved.

"Use Iron Defense!"

Blastoise possessed a thick hide and excellent defenses on both the physical and special sides. Steel-type moves also dealt only half damage. Ethan had no fear of the attack. Once Iron Defense took effect, Blastoise neither dodged nor retreated, withstanding the entire barrage of Meteor Mash head-on!

As Meteor Mash began to weaken, Ethan keenly spotted an opening and immediately launched a counterattack.

"Use Aqua Tail!"

Blastoise turned its body and lashed Metagross with a tail wrapped in flowing water.

Smack!

Metagross had yet to lower its forelimbs. The hit caught it off balance and forced it several steps backward.

It quickly recovered and steadied itself.

Although Metagross had taken damage, Jasmine did not relent from her command area. She shouted, "Use Psychic!"

Ethan issued an order of his own.

"Use Protect!"

A powerful, invisible shock wave formed from psychic energy and arrived in an instant. As it struck Blastoise, a faint light appeared before the Pokémon and blocked the Psychic attack!

In the spectator stands, Erika watched the battle with complete concentration while marveling at its intensity.

She had never expected Jasmine's Metagross to be this strong. It was fighting evenly with Ethan's Blastoise!

Fortunately, Blastoise was formidable as well. It deserved its place on the team that had helped Ethan win the championship. Even after going so long without battling other Pokémon, it had maintained an impressive standard.

And as for Ethan...

Her gaze drifted involuntarily toward the man.

When he commanded a Pokémon in battle, a light seemed to shine in his eyes. His entire presence took on a different brilliance, making him increasingly handsome and deeply compelling.

By the time Erika came to herself, she realized that she had been staring at Ethan for quite a while!

She was startled by the change in herself. Remembering what Jasmine had said before the battle, she could not help sighing.

Pressing a hand against her pounding heart, she marveled at her own transformation.

This is bad...

Could everyone really have been right?

In appearance, Ethan was undeniably outstanding. Spending time with him had also shown her his wit and humor. If she were choosing a boyfriend, he truly suited both her tastes and her temperament.

But...

She thought of Sabrina, and her heart suddenly tightened.

What a shame... If only I'd met him sooner...

Erika shook her head, dismissed the distracting thoughts, and focused on the battlefield again.

The two Pokémon were still fighting. Several injuries of varying severity now marked their bodies. Evidently, while Erika's attention had wandered, the Pokémon had exchanged another fierce series of attacks under their Trainers' commands.

The deadlock soon broke.

Metagross closed in again and used Zen Headbutt. This time, Blastoise immediately used Withdraw. After avoiding the damage, it flowed directly into Rapid Spin and launched a counterattack.

The spinning impact left Metagross frozen in place. Blastoise retained its momentum and struck the Pokémon's hard body, bouncing high into the air. It continued accelerating its rotation overhead, then came crashing straight down toward Metagross.

Boom!

The sound of a heavy object striking the ground echoed throughout the battlefield...
