“Ethan...” May tried the less formal name quietly. There was nothing unusual about it, yet she felt a little shy. Perhaps it sounded too intimate. Still, the change made them feel a step closer.

She remembered the sweet, genuine smile Misty wore whenever she addressed him that way. An odd feeling came over her.

Ethan noticed her embarrassment and smiled. “All right. How would you like to train?”

May quickly focused. “The other day, you beat Harley. Could you tell me how you did it?”

Ethan thought back over the match. There was no useful insight to offer. His Pokémon had simply overwhelmed Harley's.

“Well... Harley wasn't much of a challenge for me,” he said after a moment. “I didn't find anything about him particularly difficult.”

May looked disappointed. People truly were different. To her, Harley was a troublesome opponent. To Ethan, he had been no obstacle at all.

Wait—she was already calling him Ethan in her thoughts? She had only just started. It must be because she had heard Misty and Ash say his name so often.

Ethan saw her expression. “I may not have advice tailored to beating Harley,” he said, “but I can help you get stronger.”

“Huh? What do you mean?”

He took a Poké Ball from his pouch and released Gardevoir, then called Gengar out of his shadow.

“We still have tonight. I can help train your Pokémon so they're stronger for tomorrow's match.”

“R-really?” May's excitement was unmistakable. Gardevoir and Gengar were among Ethan's strongest Pokémon. Training against them would surely benefit her partners.

“Of course. This is a rare opportunity. I don't offer personal training very often.”

He was not boasting. His choice showed how much he cared about May's progress. Eevee and Milotic alone had defeated Harley, yet he had brought out Gardevoir and Gengar to teach her. Harley's pride would have suffered if he knew.

“Thank you so much!” May bowed deeply. “I'll work hard!”

“That's the spirit.” Ethan nodded approvingly. “We don't have much time, so let's begin.”

“Yes!”

May released Beautifly and Bulbasaur. Ethan looked at them. “These are the two you plan to use against Harley tomorrow?”

“I think so.” Her confidence faltered. “Is there a problem with that?”

“None. Bulbasaur can attack from the ground while Beautifly attacks from the air. Together, they can pressure an opponent from two directions. It's a good combination.”

May let out a relieved breath. “I'm glad. I was worried you wouldn't like my choice.”

“Choose the team you want, May. Don't let anyone else decide for you, including me.” Ethan shook his head. “You made the top 32. You're already a Coordinator people know about, and you need to trust your own judgment. In a Contest battle, what matters is showing off your Pokémon's strengths. Any partners you can do that with are valid choices.”

“I... I understand.”

The four Pokémon were ready. Ethan motioned for May to attack whenever she wished.

She prepared to give the command, but one more look at Gardevoir and Gengar made her swallow. Gengar grinned, and Gardevoir wore a gentle smile. Both seemed friendly. Yet May could feel an enormous pressure from them. She knew how powerful they were. The feeling was no trick of her nerves.

“Relax,” Ethan said. “The battle hasn't started, and you're already wavering. Look at your Pokémon. They're eager to begin.”

“Huh?”

May looked at Beautifly and Bulbasaur. They could sense the power facing them too, but neither shrank back. Their eyes burned with fighting spirit.

“Beautifly, Bulbasaur...” Their determination steadied her own.

“Good. Remember that feeling,” Ethan said. “There's nothing to fear. Go all out with the partners you trust most. Even if you lose, you won't regret giving everything you had.”

May took a deep breath and nodded. “Ethan, here I come!”

...

Bang! Bang!

Beautifly and Bulbasaur flew backward and collided. The moment they landed, they struggled to their feet again.

After more than an hour of training, both were battered and breathing hard. Their stamina and resolve were near their limits. Gardevoir and Gengar, on the other hand, seemed as fresh as when they had started.

They're so strong, May thought for what felt like the hundredth time. Watching them battle had never shown her just how formidable they were. Only facing them herself made it clear.

Beautifly and Bulbasaur had attacked without pause. They had not injured their opponents or even tired them out. Gardevoir and Gengar had held back, defending most of the time and avoiding vulnerable spots when they counterattacked, yet May's team was still in disarray. Only their precise control had allowed the training to last this long.

“Beautifly, Bulbasaur, how are you? Can you keep going?” May watched anxiously. Both Pokémon nodded, and she drew breath to order another attack.

Someone spoke before she could.

“So this is where you two disappeared to! Sneaking off for a date?”

May turned. Misty, Ash, Brock, and Max had arrived. Of course the voice belonged to Misty.

“A d-date? Ethan and I only came here to train!”

Ethan? All four caught the change in how May addressed him and glanced at one another. Misty had meant it as a joke, but her teasing had revealed something interesting. For May's sake, none of them said so aloud.

Ethan worried Misty might embarrass her further and changed the subject. “What brought you all out here?”

“We came to see what you were doing,” Misty said, spreading her hands. “May was gone from our room for ages. We worried something had happened to her—or that Harley had attacked her before the match. Then we knocked on your door and found you missing too. We came looking and caught you two secretly battling.”

Secretly? May's ears reddened at the suggestive word.

Ash, oblivious to the mood, saw her battered Pokémon facing Ethan's. “How did the training go?”

Misty, Brock, and Max turned to Ethan. So did May. She could not judge her own progress. She had attacked throughout the session and failed throughout it. Was that good or bad?

“Not bad,” Ethan said. “To learn how to hit, first you have to learn how to take a hit. They stayed on their feet this long, so their endurance has improved. Their attacks are more coordinated too. I think they'll do fine tomorrow.”

May brightened at his approval. The others were pleased for her as well.

“That's great!” Ash said. “If Ethan says they'll be fine, they will!”

Ethan's confidence also came from his prior-life knowledge. In the anime, May had beaten Harley. With this extra training, her chances should be better still.

He added a warning. “That's assuming a normal match. Don't underestimate him. Against someone who relies on tricks like Harley, carelessness is your greatest enemy.”

“I won't let my guard down.” May nodded firmly. Then, still eager, she said, “I feel like I need more work, though. Ethan, can we keep going?”

He shook his head. “That's enough for tonight. It's past ten. Rest is part of training. Take Beautifly and Bulbasaur to Nurse Joy so they can recover. If you spend all their strength tonight and they're exhausted tomorrow, it will all have been for nothing.”

Only then did May realize how late it was. She had barely noticed the time pass. She wanted to continue, but trusted Ethan's judgment and agreed at once.

“Thank you for everything tonight, Ethan. I promise I'll win tomorrow. I won't let you down!”

...

The Grand Festival arena was packed again. The crowd was even more excited than the day before. Two fierce matches had already warmed them up, and they were eager for the next.

May and Harley entered to a roar of cheers. Given their earlier friction and Harley's clash with Ethan, there was no friendliness left between them. This match would settle matters on the stage.

As in an ordinary Contest, the battle had a five-minute limit. Within that time, each Coordinator had to combine the beauty of their own presentation with their Pokémon's moves and win more points.

Without pleasantries or even a word to each other, May and Harley released their teams. May chose the partners she had settled on the night before: Bulbasaur and Beautifly. Harley sent out Cacturne and Banette.

One was Grass- and Dark-type, the other Ghost-type. The pair suited Harley's sinister, unpleasant manner. On type matchups alone, May held an advantage against Cacturne with both of her Pokémon. But knocking an opponent out was not the sole way to win. The visual appeal of their moves counted heavily too.

That complicated things. After Ethan's training, Beautifly and Bulbasaur might have won an ordinary battle with ease. This, however, was the Grand Festival.

Vivian saw that both sides were ready and raised her microphone. “The third Contest battle: May versus Harley! Begin!”
