Seeing their quarry escape, Team Rocket truly began to panic!

They had already lost their hidden base. If Raikou escaped as well, the entire operation would end with nothing to show for it, and they would have sacrificed their own headquarters in the process!

They wanted to give chase, but Jimmy and the others would never allow it!

Jimmy's group also wanted to follow Raikou after seeing it flee, but their goal was entirely different from Team Rocket's. They were motivated by goodwill, seeking only to protect and observe it. They also understood that Raikou could not be allowed to suffer any longer inside the barrier.

Now that Ethan had broken the barrier and allowed Raikou to escape, they felt some regret, but even greater relief.

One side tried to break through while the other defended with everything it had. Both sides unleashed their full strength, driving their Pokémon to their limits and forcing the battle into another deadlock!

Team Rocket, however, was already a spent force.

They could not sustain the fight. Ethan's Pokémon seized an opening and battered them mercilessly, soon overwhelming them completely.

Charizard was especially devastating. Using both fangs and claws, it tore the mechanical shark apart and reduced it to a field of scattered components!

Attila and Hun saw that the situation was hopeless and tried to escape. But as Team Rocket's Pokémon continued to fall, the men lost any chance of withdrawing safely. They were soon surrounded, reduced to cornered beasts making one final struggle.

"Damn it!"

Attila looked around and knew the battle was lost.

Finally, Gardevoir defeated Team Rocket's last Pokémon with Psychic. The villains had no means of resisting any longer.

"It's over!"

Eusine and Kudo wiped the sweat from their foreheads. Neither fought often, so the battle had demanded their complete concentration simply to hold off Team Rocket without being defeated and becoming a weak point. Now that every enemy Pokémon had fallen, they could finally let go of their anxiety.

The battle had been extremely difficult for them at first. Once Ethan joined, however, the pressure dropped sharply, and in the end his side swept straight through the enemy.

It was like breaking through a thin membrane. Once that first barrier gave way, everything afterward proceeded with incredible ease.

They could not help wondering how Ethan's Pokémon had become so powerful!

Jimmy took a climbing rope from his pack. With Eusine and Kudo's help, he tied the dozen or so Team Rocket members together.

Trusting in his muscular build, Attila tried to resist with brute force. Then Charizard lowered its head directly in front of him and stared eye to eye. His momentum vanished at once. Knowing he could never overpower a Pokémon, he had no choice but to submit.

Hun, who had remained calm throughout, merely stared coldly at Ethan.

"We underestimated all of you this time!" Hun's voice was icy. "I admit that, like the other Executives, I made the mistake of looking down on you. You defeated us. But don't think that capturing us and discovering one base will be enough to destroy Team Rocket!"

"Of course I know Team Rocket has vast resources." Ethan was unmoved by Hun's taunt. "I also know that losing a few people means practically nothing to you. But don't worry. Others will be joining you soon enough."

Understanding the implication, Hun gave a cold snort and turned his head away.

He refused to waste more breath arguing with Ethan. It was the last shred of stubbornness and dignity he had left!

Jimmy came over to Ethan.

"Raikou..."

Ethan glanced at him and smiled. "I've already sent my Pokémon to follow it. Do you want to come?"

To Ethan's surprise, Jimmy hesitated and ultimately shook his head!

"No. This is already enough."

Ethan was not the only one caught off guard. Even Marina was shocked.

"Jimmy, you..."

Jimmy smiled easily. Despite his still-youthful face, his expression showed unexpected maturity. "I may like Raikou a great deal, but I won't capture it. I could see in its eyes that it strongly rejects humans. Returning to the wild is the best home for it!"

Ethan admired his choice, then turned toward Eusine and Kudo.

"What about you two?"

Both men waved off the offer.

"There's no need for me to go," Eusine answered first. "I've only ever had one objective: Suicune! Seeing Raikou this time was already an unexpected reward. I have no designs on it."

Kudo smiled as well. "The same goes for me. Besides, I've collected some excellent data. I can already report back to Professor Elm!"

Ethan had never expected them to show such restraint!

But after failing to capture Raikou last time, Ethan himself had resolved that he would not miss this opportunity under any circumstances!

Raikou would be his!

The unfamiliar chubby boy, whom Ethan had met for the first time today, walked over with a cheerful smile and said he wanted to join the search for Raikou.

Ethan had only one question for him: Who are you? You haven't appeared even once before now!

Fortunately, Marina hurried to introduce him. Smiling, she said, "This is Vincent. Like Jimmy and me, he's a traveling companion who began his journey in New Bark Town!"

Vincent?

Ethan carefully studied the boy.

Vincent was not especially fat. Compared with others his age, he merely had a slightly broader build. His short gray hair was styled into something resembling a rooster's comb. He was fair-skinned and ordinary-looking, but the bright, sunny smile on his face made him seem very easy to get along with.

Since he was Jimmy and Marina's companion, Vincent had probably appeared in the anime as well, but Ethan had no recollection of him.

It did not matter. Even if Ethan remembered him, he still would not bring this boy along to find Raikou.

"In that case, I'll leave these Team Rocket members to you. I won't return to the Pokémon Center, so we'll say goodbye here."

Ethan waved to them and turned to leave. Seeing him go, Jimmy hurriedly called after him.

"Mr. Ethan!"

"What is it?" Ethan turned back toward him.

"You're entering the Silver Conference too, aren't you?"

"Of course."

"That's great!" Jimmy said excitedly. "Our battle yesterday was interrupted, so we never determined a winner. But that's fine. I like saving delicious food for last, and battles are the same! I'll officially challenge you at the Silver Conference. When the time comes, please battle me again!"

"Of course." Ethan nodded with a smile. "I'll be waiting for you there!"

He waved goodbye once more. Ethan and Erika watched the group leave, then lingered no longer. They climbed onto Charizard and pursued Raikou in the direction it had gone.
