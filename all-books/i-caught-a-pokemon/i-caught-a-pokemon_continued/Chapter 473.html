Any wild Pokémon mother would naturally be highly wary of humans who appeared near her child, especially an entire group of strangers.

As Ethan watched the God of the Sea, a thought suddenly occurred to him. He remembered the blessing he had received upon becoming the Sea Hero and hurriedly focused his mind, attempting to use his new ability to probe Lugia's heart.

Amazingly, although Lugia was not a Water-type Pokémon, perhaps its long life in the ocean allowed Ethan to faintly sense its current emotions!

He felt intense vigilance mixed with hostility.

By comparison, Silver's current emotions were light and joyful, making them much easier for Ethan to perceive.

Beyond that, however, he discovered nothing else!

In truth, emotions like these could easily be identified from the target's expression. There was no need to peer into its heart at all.

Furthermore, this was not a passive skill that allowed him to sense a Pokémon's emotions simply by standing nearby. Ethan had to focus his attention and actively reach out to perceive them!

Sigh. No matter how I look at it, this ability is still far too limited...

Before Ethan could look away, Lugia seemed to sense something and raised its head toward him.

Their eyes met. Ethan coughed lightly and instinctively turned his gaze aside.

Being caught secretly peering into someone else's heart always created a strange sense of embarrassment.

It seemed he should avoid using this ability on Pokémon of Lugia's level in the future. An ordinary Pokémon might never notice, but one as powerful as Lugia would probably detect the intrusion. If that caused a misunderstanding, the consequences would far outweigh any benefit!

Fortunately, Silver reached its mother's side at that moment. The reunion drew Lugia's attention away, and it stopped staring at Ethan.

The two Lugia nestled together in a heartwarming scene—although it would have been even better without the adult Lugia's intense hostility.

Guided by its mother, Silver leaped into the water. It appeared the two were preparing to leave together.

Before swimming away, however, Silver looked back and waved a wing toward Oliver and Ethan's group in farewell.

"Silver..."

Ash, Misty, and Richie involuntarily stepped forward, their expressions filled with reluctance to part.

Although they had spent little time together, Ash and the others had clearly grown very fond of Silver and did not want to say goodbye so soon.

Unfortunately, Silver's departure was inevitable. Their wishes could not change it.

Silver might often return to play with Oliver in the future, but they would have left this place long before then. Once they parted today, they might never meet again.

The group stood by the sea and watched the two Lugia, one large and one small, swim into deeper water until they disappeared completely.

Seeing Ash and Misty continue staring blankly at the ripples, Ethan tapped each of them on the head.

"All right, stop looking. Silver has already left. No matter how long you watch, it isn't coming back right now."

Misty rubbed her tearful eyes and murmured, "Didn't we come here to protect them? Is it really okay to let Silver leave like this?"

Ethan sighed. "Returning a child to its mother is the greatest protection we can offer. As long as they remain hidden in the ocean, Team Rocket won't be able to find them even if they try."

Seeing that Misty remained dejected, he comforted her. "Don't dwell on it. Both you and Silver still have long journeys ahead. If fate allows it, I'm sure you'll have another chance to meet someday!"

"Sigh... I hope so..."

Without Silver, the earlier cheerfulness did not return, and no one felt particularly inclined to talk. Fortunately, before long, Misty's Poliwhirl suddenly surfaced from the sea.

Ash immediately asked, "Well? Did you find Team Rocket's base?"

Poliwhirl nodded and began croaking at length. Since no one could understand it, they had to ask Gengar to translate.

After listening for quite some time, Gengar frowned at Misty. "Why is your Poliwhirl so noisy and long-winded? It keeps mixing in all these 'croak, croak' sounds and still hasn't reached the point."

Misty's expression darkened. Only then did Gengar provide the translation. "It did find Team Rocket's base. It's beneath the sea, not far from here."

"Beneath the sea!?"

Ash and the others exchanged looks. They had assumed the base would be built upon one of the islands. None of them had expected it to be hidden underwater!

The underwater location also left Ethan feeling that the situation would be rather difficult.

Conditions beneath the sea were extremely complicated. Ferocious Pokémon could lurk in the darkness, while currents and seaweed presented further dangers. Anyone hoping to remain underwater for an extended period would also need proper diving equipment.

Seeing everyone fall silent, Ethan cleared his throat. "Cheer up. Finding the base is good news. Now that we've confirmed it's underwater, we shouldn't waste any more time. Let's set out!"

Richie hesitated. "How are we going to get there?"

"First, we'll find a boat. Traveling by boat will save us a great deal of energy."

At the mention of a boat, Oliver hurriedly said, "I have one! It's tied beneath the trees behind us!"

With Oliver leading the way, they walked to the beach on the other side and entered a small grove. Sure enough, they soon saw a boat tied by rope beneath a large tree.

Only then did Ethan's group learn that whenever Oliver came to this island to play, he sailed here alone through the wind and waves!

They had assumed the boat would be a small wooden rowboat. When they approached, they discovered it was actually a speedboat!

Misty immediately looked at Oliver with newfound respect. "Oh? You can pilot a speedboat!?"

"Heh..." Oliver rubbed his nose and replied shyly, "It isn't that difficult. I've lived beside the sea since I was little, so handling a boat like this is easy for me!"

By now, everyone had rested for quite some time. Their Pokémon's injuries had been treated, and the Trainers had recovered most of their strength. They delayed no longer and boarded the boat.

As Oliver untied the rope, he asked, "Where are we going now? Straight to Team Rocket's base?"

Ethan shook his head. "We'll go to the town on Ogi Isle first. Since the base is underwater, we need to make preparations."

Diving equipment was the highest priority. They could not afford to be careless about it.

Ash, Misty, and Brock all nodded in agreement.

They could not help remembering their experience aboard the S.S. Anne in Kanto, when the ship sank beneath the sea. Their present situation was similar, and they could become trapped underwater at any moment. None of them wanted to experience the same ordeal a second time!
