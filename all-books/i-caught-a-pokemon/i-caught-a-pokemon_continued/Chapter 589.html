A long ship's horn echoed across Littleroot Town's harbor. When the liner that had carried them across the sea docked, Ethan and Erika followed the other passengers down the gangway and stepped onto a land neither had visited before.

Hoenn, here we come!

Littleroot Town was the starting point for Hoenn's new Trainers. A few days earlier, after parting from Ash and the others in Johto, Ethan and Erika had gone to the nearest port and boarded the ship that brought them here.

True to its name, Littleroot was a country town surrounded by fresh, unspoiled nature, as though it had yet to be colored by anything else.

It was still a town with a small harbor, though, and there were even a few tall buildings near the waterfront. The countryside and the city met here.

At the other end of town stood Professor Birch's home and laboratory. Like Professor Oak in Kanto and Professor Elm in Johto, Birch was Hoenn's leading professor and the one entrusted with giving starter Pokémon to new Trainers.

As someone entirely new to Hoenn, Ethan meant to begin his next journey here too.

He and Erika did not drop in on Professor Birch, however. They had never met him, had no particular reason to visit, and had no one to introduce them. They could get acquainted another time.

Ethan's first task in Hoenn was to register for the Hoenn League. Only then could he challenge the region's Gyms. When they asked people nearby, however, they learned that Littleroot had no Pokémon Center. He would have to register in neighboring Oldale Town.

“What a bother.” Ethan shook his head. “This is where so many new Trainers set out, but the League hasn't put a Pokémon Center here. You'd think they could make an exception.”

Erika shook her head. “They must have their reasons. The Pokémon League may be the world's largest organization, but its funding is limited. It can't provide everything everywhere.”

After several days aboard ship, both were full of energy. They did not linger in Littleroot, but headed north for Oldale Town.

Hoenn lay southwest of Johto, with an ocean between them. Although the regions occupied different landmasses, their customs and general feel were much alike.

Hoenn had all sorts of natural environments, from tropical rainforests to deserts, and islands of every size surrounded the mainland. Overall, its climate was warmer and its vegetation more varied than in Johto or Kanto.

Ethan remembered that Hoenn was supposedly based on Japan's Kyushu island, turned in a different direction. He also recalled that its name came from the characters for “abundant bonds,” a place rich in connections. Whether that was true, he could not say.

They took their time along the country road out of Littleroot, enjoying the scenery and watching the Pokémon they encountered. Most were familiar from Kanto and Johto, but this was their first time seeing species such as Poochyena and Zigzagoon.

Of course, Ethan had known those Pokémon from games and anime for years. Seeing living creatures with his own eyes instead of cold electronic data was another matter. In that sense, this really was his first encounter with them.

As they drew near Oldale, they spotted ancient ruins in the distance.

More than a dozen huge oval stones stood on a stretch of barren ground. Half buried, they surrounded the remains of several buildings, almost as if guarding them.

Curious, Ethan and Erika went over for a closer look.

Nothing fenced the ruins off; anyone seemed free to visit. Behind the standing stones were the remains of low houses built from rock. No one could tell how long they had stood there. Parts had weathered away, leaving the marks of age everywhere.

Several people dressed like scholars were busy among the ruins. One looked up, noticed the visitors, and came to meet them.

“Hello.” He was a man of about twenty-seven or twenty-eight, with tanned skin, work clothes, and a short ponytail. To Ethan, he looked a little like Brock with his eyes open. The man glanced between them and smiled. “Have you come to see the Oldale Ruins?”

“Oldale Ruins?”

“That's right. These are the Oldale Ruins. The town nearby was even named after them.” He caught himself. “I forgot to introduce myself. I'm Alden, a researcher studying the ruins here.”

“Nice to meet you. I'm Ethan, a Pokémon Trainer.”

“And I'm Erika.”

“Would you like to look around?” Alden asked. Perhaps afraid they had the wrong idea about his archaeological work, he added, “People know very little about this place, and it hasn't received much attention. I'm trying to help more people learn about the Oldale Ruins.”

“I see.”

Now Ethan understood why Alden had approached them. He exchanged a glance with Erika, and they followed the researcher into the ruins.

Alden's explanation gave them a general picture of the site. Ethan noticed, though, that the researchers were using instruments to probe what looked like an ordinary ruin.

“Mr. Alden,” he asked, “is there something here that hasn't been discovered yet?”

Alden nodded. “These ruins conceal a doorway linking the ancient Pokémon world and the modern one.”

“A doorway?”

“An actual door. It stands outside a stone chamber and has a peculiar mechanism we haven't been able to open. If we force our way in, the mechanism will trigger and destroy the whole structure. So we still know nothing about what's behind it.”

Ethan nearly spoke, then held his tongue.

As far as he could see, they did not need to break the door to get into the chamber. A Ghost-type Pokémon could pass through the wall.

A person could not follow, of course, but the Pokémon could at least see what was inside.

Then again, if the doorway held some strange power and transported whoever passed through it elsewhere, that would be a different matter.

Erika asked, “What did you mean by a link to the ancient world?”

“Ancient Pokémon,” Alden said with feeling. “I suspect there may be extinct Pokémon behind the chamber door, like Aerodactyl or Kabutops. There might even be species we haven't discovered yet.”

He looked at Ethan and smiled. “I guessed you were Trainers as soon as I saw you. I've invited several others into the chamber before, hoping their Pokémon could help me solve the door's mystery without damaging it.”

He did not have to finish. Clearly, none of those Trainers had succeeded.

By then they had reached the chamber entrance.

No one knew what kind of stone had been used to build it, but unlike the rest of the ruins, the chamber remained remarkably intact and solid. It had stood for thousands of years and looked capable of standing for thousands more.

Ancient builders' ingenuity was nothing to dismiss.

Ethan and Erika stepped closer. The door was a single heavy stone slab with no handle or keyhole, only several grooves. Something apparently needed to be fitted into them to open it.

Ethan pushed against the cold stone. It felt immensely heavy and did not move an inch.

Alden was right: they would need to find the proper way to open the chamber.

Looking at the door, Ethan suddenly remembered something. Wasn't this the first mystery Ash encountered after arriving in Hoenn and meeting May?

Thinking it over, he vaguely recalled that the chamber led underground. There was a subterranean lake inhabited by an ancient fish Pokémon, along with a few murals worth studying. Beyond that, there was nothing else.

What was the fish Pokémon called again?

Right—Relicanth. It was an ugly-looking Pokémon about which people knew little. They had not even found an evolved form for it.

More importantly, it was not unique to these ruins. Relicanth could also be found in deep undersea caves.

Once he remembered that, Ethan lost all interest in opening the chamber.

He would leave it to Ash. Ash would be here in a few days, and Ethan had no need to wear himself out prying open this stone door.

He and Erika made a show of examining it. After a while, they both shook their heads at Alden. The researcher was used to disappointment and did not seem too disheartened.

They spent a little longer exploring the site. As the day grew late, they said goodbye to Alden and continued toward Oldale Town.

Oldale was a place where little things gathered into something greater, a lovely town of colorful flowers and green trees.

It lay in southern Hoenn. Petalburg City was to the west, while the main road north led toward Mauville City and Slateport City. Many routes passed through Oldale.

New Trainers leaving Littleroot would part ways here, setting out in different directions to challenge Hoenn's Gyms and begin their adventures.

By the time Ethan and Erika arrived, dusk had fallen.

They went straight to the Pokémon Center. As in Kanto and Johto, Nurse Joy worked there, caring for Pokémon and the Trainers who stayed overnight.

“Welcome,” she said, greeting them with a sweet smile.

“Hello, Nurse Joy. I'm here to register for the Hoenn League.” Ethan introduced himself and handed her his Pokédex.

“One moment, please.”

Nurse Joy took the Pokédex and entered a few things into her computer. Then she saw Ethan's record: champion of both the Indigo and Silver Conferences, plus victories in the smaller Orange Islands and Whirl Islands tournaments.

What an incredible record!

She gave Ethan a surprised glance, but kept her composure. After completing his registration, she returned the Pokédex.

“You're registered, Mr. Ethan. Good luck with your Hoenn challenge!”

Ethan accepted it, but did not leave yet. He went to a transfer machine nearby and contacted Chloe at the distant Cerise Laboratory.

Now that he had reached a new region, it was time to adjust his team.

He and Chloe exchanged a few pleasantries. Then he transferred Blastoise, Venusaur, Jolteon, and Crystal Steelix back to her. All four had distinguished themselves at the Silver Conference and no longer needed more training on the road.

They were already strong enough to compete in a major tournament. He only had to make sure they did not lose their edge.

That left nine Pokémon with him: Gardevoir, Gengar, Dragonite, Scizor, Tyranitar, Eevee, Raikou, Suicune, and Celebi.

Gardevoir and Gengar were permanent members of his team. He would never swap them out; wherever he traveled, they would remain by his side.

Celebi was a new companion. He needed to strengthen their bond and keep his promise to let it taste more delicious food.

He had told Erika aboard the ship that he had caught Celebi. She had not seemed surprised. Instead, she had been sincerely happy that the man she loved had managed to catch it.

After his three legendary birds, Suicune and Raikou were his other trump cards for the journey. Naturally, he wanted to keep them close.

That left Dragonite, Scizor, Tyranitar, and Eevee as the four regular Pokémon he planned to train most in Hoenn. He wanted to bring them up to the level of Jolteon and Crystal Steelix.

Of course, four regular Pokémon would not be enough for the Ever Grande Conference. He would need to catch several more.

Night had fallen by the time they left the Pokémon Center, so they found a hotel in Oldale for the evening.

After dinner, they walked arm in arm through the quiet town, taking their time to feel what made this new region different from Kanto and Johto.

But the wind felt the same, and the forests were just as thick.

If the town's name had not told Ethan he was in Hoenn, he might never have known the difference.
