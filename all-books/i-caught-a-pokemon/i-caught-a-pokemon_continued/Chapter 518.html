Lopunny really did resemble a rabbit from Earth. It had pink eyes, a small nose, and two long eyebrows. Most of its fur was brown, while its feet and fluffy tufts were cream-colored.

But Ethan would not underestimate it just because it looked like a rabbit.

People said even a cornered rabbit would bite. In this world of Pokémon, that was all the more reason to be careful.

Whitney had chosen Lopunny for this final round. She must trust its strength.

Tyranitar and Scizor had each fought a round already, so Ethan sent out Dragonite.

All three were still growing. None could yet carry a battle alone the way Gardevoir or Charizard could, so giving each of them a turn to train was important.

The two sides took their positions, ready to begin.

As soon as the referee lowered her flag, Lopunny showed what made it different.

It charged at Dragonite. Its powerful legs carried it across the field in a series of quick bounds, closing the distance with startling speed.

Before it reached Dragonite, it leaped again. In midair it brought both feet together and aimed a flying kick at its opponent.

Ethan could have ordered Dragonite to fly out of reach. Instead, he decided to meet Lopunny head-on.

If it liked attacking from the front so much, he would give it a fight.

"Dragonite, use Dragon Rage!"

Facing Lopunny's airborne kick, Dragonite opened its mouth and released a blue-green shock wave.

Just before Dragon Rage could hit, Lopunny twisted nimbly and spun several times in the air. It somehow held itself aloft long enough for the wave to pass beneath it, then resumed its kick and struck Dragonite in the stomach.

Oh? Ethan's eyes lit up.

That was impressive footwork and an impressive kick!

But this was hardly the time to admire his opponent. Ethan recovered and gave another order. "Dragonite, use Thunder Wave!"

Against an opponent this agile, electricity was the best way to restrict its movement.

Sparks flashed from the two antennae on Dragonite's head and raced toward Lopunny.

At such close range, another Pokémon might have been struck and paralyzed. Lopunny used a sudden burst of strength to spring backward, easily avoiding the current.

Even from that brief exchange, Ethan could tell Lopunny would be harder to handle than Whitney's first two Pokémon.

"Lopunny, use Quick Attack!"

As it came at Dragonite again, Ethan called, "Dragonite, fly higher!"

With Lopunny proving so troublesome, he would have Dragonite use its advantage and attack from above.

Whitney watched Dragonite rise and thought for a moment. This was exactly what she had worried about when Ethan sent it out.

On the ground, Lopunny's jumping ability could let it dance around an opponent. Once Dragonite was in the air, things became difficult. Lopunny could leap up after it, but no jump could give it the same freedom of movement as wings.

"Use Wing Attack!"

Dragonite circled to build speed, spread its small wings, and dove toward Lopunny.

Lopunny was not going to wait for the hit. It ran across the field at speed, trying to shake Dragonite off.

There was no cover here. However it moved, it could not simply outrun an attack from above.

Dragonite closed the short distance in an instant. Just as it seemed about to collide with Lopunny, Lopunny leaped high into the air again.

The same trick would not work on Ethan twice.

As Lopunny dodged, Dragonite climbed sharply alongside it. Electricity crackled around both its fists.

Thunder Punch!

An electrified fist drove toward Lopunny's cheek. Everything happened too quickly for Lopunny to twist away in midair this time.

Bang!

Thunder Punch struck its face and knocked it down to the ground.

Thump...

Lopunny rolled several times. Its thick tufts of fur cushioned the fall, and it had not landed too hard. But as it tried to rise, paralysis seized its body and almost stopped it from moving.

Thunder Punch had triggered paralysis!

"Lopunny, hang on!" Whitney cried anxiously.

Lopunny tried to recover, but determination alone could not shake the effect.

Seeing Dragonite fly toward it again, Lopunny turned and ran, hoping to delay until the paralysis passed. Slowed as it was, it could not escape Dragonite's flying speed. Dragonite closed from behind like a hawk tracking its prey.

"Use Dragon Rush!"

At Ethan's command, Dragonite tucked in its wings and hurtled toward Lopunny like an arrow loosed from a bow.

"Lopunny, dodge!"

Hearing the rush of air behind it, Lopunny looked back. Dragonite was drawing closer, its large yellow head and little horn filling more and more of Lopunny's view.

Bang!

The collision lifted Lopunny off the ground. By the time it landed, it had flown out of the field. Its eyes rolled back, and it lay unconscious.

The referee took one look and raised her colored flag. "Lopunny is unable to battle! Challenger Ethan wins the Gym match!"

Another loss...

Whitney sighed and recalled Lopunny. Disappointment showed plainly on her pretty face.

Those had been her three strongest Pokémon, yet she had not won a single round. She felt frustrated, but there was nothing she could do about it. If she could not win, she could not win.

Though her spirits were low, she quickly gathered herself, smiled, and walked over to Ethan.

Erika reached his side at the same moment. Whitney looked at the handsome man before her and spoke sincerely. "You really are amazing. I couldn't beat you. Here, this is my Plain Badge. Please take it."
