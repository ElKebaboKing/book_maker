Ho-Oh flew away as suddenly as it had appeared. No one tried to follow. Gardevoir and the sisters crowded around Ethan, their faces anxious. Deoxys and the three birds returned to his side as soon as Ho-Oh was gone.

“Are you all right?”

“What happened?”

Everyone asked at once. Impatient Zuki had already reached out to feel his face. Ethan waved to show them he was all right, and she helped him stand steady.

His whole body felt unbearably hot, but he noticed no other immediate discomfort. The power that had entered him with Ho-Oh's feather seemed to be filling him with vigorous life. The weariness and strain of the past two days were already fading.

“What did Ho-Oh do to you?” Miki asked.

Ethan told them about his conversation with Ho-Oh and its supposed blessing. The sisters exchanged puzzled looks.

“It sounds like a good thing, doesn't it?” Kuni frowned. “Then why did you nearly fall? Is there a side effect?”

“It feels as though a fire's burning inside me,” Ethan said with a rueful smile. “And I'm desperately thirsty.”

Before they could question him further, he held up a hand. “We shouldn't discuss it here. Let's go before a crowd traps us at the tower.”

They had made enough noise and light to draw half of Ecruteak's attention. People would be arriving soon. The sisters put their masks back on, and Ethan recalled Deoxys and the three birds. Gardevoir used Teleport to bring them back to the spot where they had first entered the compound.

Monks had gathered beneath Bell Tower. They had not gone inside. Instead, they bowed repeatedly toward the tower and murmured prayers.

Ethan and the others shared a smile. The monks had come close enough to see what was happening, yet had not interrupted. They seemed to have taken the battle for a miracle of Ho-Oh.

Gardevoir was about to Teleport again when several figures and their Pokémon leaped over a wall on the other side of the compound.

The first Trainers to investigate had arrived. A moment later and they might have caught Ethan's group standing there.

The Trainers ran straight for Bell Tower, only to be stopped by the monks at its entrance. An argument quickly broke out. Ethan was too far away to hear the words, but the Trainers gestured urgently and several tried to push past. The monks stood firm and refused to let them through.

After a while, a few impatient Trainers mounted Flying-type Pokémon and rose along the outside of the tower toward its roof. Among them Ethan spotted a familiar face.

Miki followed his gaze. “That person looks familiar, but it's too dark to see clearly.”

“Morty, the Ecruteak Gym Leader,” Ethan said. “I didn't expect him to get here so quickly.”

“He's a Gym Leader,” Zuki said. “Looking after the city is part of his job.” She gave the group a quick glance, then urged, “Come on. Let's leave before they notice us.”

By the time they returned to the sisters' home, it was around one in the morning.

Everyone was hungry after the long night. Kuni and the others went to change, while Miki prepared a late meal. In the dining room, Ethan poured himself a glass of cool tea. He drank another, and then another. Nothing relieved his thirst. His skin still felt hot, and his cheeks had grown red.

So this was Ho-Oh's power. Like the phoenix he knew from stories, it seemed to carry an undying, blazing fire.

When Kuni and Zuki returned, his flushed face startled them. Ethan insisted several times that he felt no other ill effect, which eased their worry a little.

He ate a few hurried mouthfuls when the food arrived, then went to take a cold shower. The icy water cooled his skin. Before he had even dried himself, the heat returned. A restless fire remained inside him, accompanied by an unusually strong desire.

He went straight to Miki's room.

Miki was removing her makeup. As the eldest sister, she had seen to everyone else and cleaned the dining room before finally retiring. She had just placed her ornaments in their box when she heard the door open behind her.

She turned and saw Ethan, his eyes faintly red. “Your eyes...”

Before she could finish, he crossed the room, lifted her in his arms, and carried her toward the bed.

Miki was startled, but she felt his urgency. Blushing, she tucked her head against his chest and put her arms around his neck, listening to his heavy breathing and heartbeat. He laid her down and leaned over her.

...

Morning came. In the Japanese-style sitting room, the television carried a special report on Ho-Oh's battle with the three birds.

The footage did not clearly show any of the Pokémon. It showed only the fiery glow that had filled the sky when Ho-Oh collided with Tri-Bird Assault. A man and woman in the studio discussed it and offered their guesses.

Kuni, seated before the television, found their speculation amusing. “They're not entirely clueless. At least they know Ho-Oh was involved.”

“People did see Ho-Oh,” Miki said with a smile. She had been watching May, Naoko, and Sayo practice in the open space outside the room. “There's no hiding that part. But they'll never guess why it was there, or why it fought the three birds.”

“Let them wonder.” Kuni shook her head. “It's been so long since Ho-Oh appeared before people. Perhaps this will remind them of its legend.”

“It will also bring countless Trainers to Ecruteak looking for traces of it.”

“That sounds good for our shows.” Kuni stretched. “We could fill the theater every day.”

Miki glanced at the clock again. Kuni noticed. “How long has Zuki been in there?”

“Nearly two hours, since breakfast.”

“That long?” Kuni rose. “She'll wear herself out. I'll take her place. You've had a rest, haven't you? You can relieve me later.”

She left the sitting room and headed for Miki's bedroom. Soon Zuki came in, clothes and hair disheveled, face flushed and steps unsteady.

She sank down beside Miki and drank several gulps of water. Her throat was hoarse from calling out, and the water helped a little.

“Honestly, he's like a bull,” she complained. “He never gets tired. If this goes on, he's going to get me pregnant.”

Miki frowned. “Weren't there condoms in the room?”

Zuki set an empty box on the table and sighed. “We used them all.”

“Then just now you...” Miki gave a helpless smile. “And Kuni has gone in there without any left.”

“I can't help that. She'll have to look after herself.” Zuki turned to her sister. “We've been together so often these past few days. You took your pill, didn't you?”

Miki looked away.

“You didn't?” Zuki lowered her voice. “What if you get pregnant? These aren't even your safe days.”

Miki touched her abdomen. “It's too late to take it now. And at my age, I do want a child.”

“You're the one who told us to be careful,” Zuki said, rolling her eyes. “And you had this in mind all along. But if you do get pregnant, your life will change for good.”

“I know.” Miki smiled gently. “I've chosen Ethan. Whatever happens, that won't change.”

She added, “The rest of you must be careful, though. Our theater is doing so well. If all three of us become pregnant together, what will we do?”

“It's not entirely in our hands,” Zuki said. “If it happens, Naoko and Sayo will have to keep the shows going.”

“They're not even adults yet!” Miki laughed despite herself, then changed the subject. “How is Ethan?”

“Didn't I just tell you?” Zuki opened her collar to show the marks on her skin. “Like a bull. He was awake all night and has kept all of us busy again and again. He's still full of energy. Ho-Oh's blessing is something else.”

“That's not what I meant. I meant how does he look?”

“Oh.” Zuki understood. “Much better. His skin isn't hot anymore, and his face isn't so red. I think he'll be back to normal when Kuni's done.”

...

Ethan had been right. Rather than endure the heat alone, he had found relief in intimacy with the sisters.

From a little after one in the morning until well past eleven, they had taken turns keeping him company. The fire inside him was gone and his body felt normal. Even after a sleepless night, his mind remained clear and his muscles felt fresh. He thought he could keep going for hours.

Ho-Oh really had given him quite a blessing. Whatever else it might do, it had left him with a remarkable reserve of energy.

Kuni lay against his chest, eyes closed, too languid to move. Ethan stroked her back and was about to settle into the quiet warmth of the moment when Miki entered with a bowl of soup.

Seeing them lying together, she blushed faintly. They had lived together for only a few days, and she was still getting used to this. She knelt beside him and offered the bowl.

“You look like yourself again. Drink this first.”

The aroma made Ethan's stomach growl. He had eaten breakfast, yet now he was ravenous. Endless energy had to come from somewhere. Perhaps Ho-Oh's gift made him use up food, or the energy stored in his body, far more quickly. That might explain how he had stayed active all night without feeling tired.

At last he thought he understood what Ho-Oh had changed. What a gift for a man.

He really ought to thank Ho-Oh. This was the sort of ability many men would dream of. Ethan made up his mind on the spot: from now on, Ho-Oh was his best friend.

No one could tell him otherwise.
