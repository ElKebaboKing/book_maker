Looker knew that all their hopes now rested on Sabrina.

"Ms. Sabrina, we're counting on you," he said earnestly.

She kept her eyes ahead and did not answer him, but she stepped forward.

For the International Police alone, or even simply to destroy Team Rocket, Sabrina would not willingly put herself at the center of the fight. But Ethan wanted this, and that gave her a reason to act. What mattered to him mattered to her.

Seeing that she would help, Looker ordered the officers to provide cover. They threw smoke bombs in every direction and tried to draw Team Rocket's Pokémon away from her.

Bang, bang, bang...

Dozens of smoke bombs landed in the clearing. Thick white smoke poured out and swallowed the battlefield, finally giving the International Police a moment to breathe. The officers hurriedly recalled their Pokémon, sparing them both the Rockets' attacks and the radio waves.

The remaining officers fired their guns to draw Team Rocket's attention. Their weapons were far weaker than Pokémon attacks. Often they had barely fired a few shots before Golbat and Weezing struck back, pinning them down. Still, the gunfire distracted the Rockets, which was enough to help.

Watching it, Ethan once again marveled at how unevenly military technology had developed in this world. Perhaps people had put their efforts in the wrong direction or relied too heavily on Pokémon. Firearms had advanced slowly; there were few kinds, and their power was limited.

If the operation had helicopter support and plenty of advanced weapons, they might not have been trapped like this.

Then again, Looker and the others were police officers with Pokémon of their own. Who could have expected the battle to turn out this way?

Smoke drifted across the clearing. The poor visibility slowed Team Rocket's advance. Tyson ordered his Golbat to beat it away with their wings, but the delay had already given Sabrina precious time to approach the tower.

A few Pokémon tried to block her path. With a wave of her hand, she knocked each of them aside with psychic power.

Tyson noticed. He frowned at her and ordered the Rocket members beside him, "Don't just stand there! Stop that woman!"

Several of them leaped in front of Sabrina. Before they could do anything, a psychic force hurled them away.

"Move."

Sabrina's hair rose without a breeze. She floated a dozen centimeters above the ground, psychic power wrapped around her in a protective field, and her eyes glowed red. Neither people nor Pokémon could stand against her.

"Tch!" Tyson cursed as his people fell. He had to enter the fight himself.

He released his Pokémon and rushed toward Sabrina, but he was too late. She had reached the tower. Raising both hands, she lifted a Team Rocket truck high into the air with her power.

She glanced at the unfortunate driver inside. His face had gone pale with panic, but she sent the truck hurtling into the signal tower all the same.

"No!"

Tyson arrived in time only to watch it strike. The tower tilted under the impact, and its transmitter exploded.

Boom!

The result was immediate. Without the radio waves, the International Police officers' Pokémon regained their strength. Tyson stood frozen, staring wide-eyed at Sabrina. He could not understand how these people had accomplished so much after losing their Pokémon. That woman in particular was absurdly strong. Could a human truly do this?

With the waves gone, it was finally the International Police's turn to fight back. They released their Arcanine again and launched an all-out attack on Team Rocket.

Ethan considered the field and sent out several of his Pokémon as well. The officers' Pokémon had suffered injuries, and their numbers no longer gave them the advantage they had at the beginning. Without something to change the balance, reaching the research building might still be difficult.

Blue light flashed, and four Pokémon appeared: Charizard, Gyarados, Jolteon, and Scyther. Ethan left the others in their Poké Balls so they could rest and remain ready if needed.

Flamethrower. Discharge. X-Scissor.

His Pokémon often needed only two or three moves to bring down one of Team Rocket's.

Gyarados was especially destructive. Like a tiger among sheep, it charged across the field with its jaws wide, impossible to overlook. Even the Arcanine steered clear, afraid their own ally might hit them by mistake.

With Ethan's Pokémon helping, Team Rocket's defeat came faster and more decisively.

"Damn it!" Tyson gritted his teeth and backed away.

The Rockets gave ground before the assault. As more of their Pokémon fell and more members were arrested, Tyson had no choice but to order everyone into the research building.

The police soon captured every facility outside it. Only at the research building's entrance did a few Rocket members still resist, trying to hold their last line of defense. It was close to breaking.

Inside the building, alarms blared. Researchers and ordinary Rocket members ran in every direction.

One man remained calm. Dressed in a Team Rocket research uniform, he stood before the monitors in a laboratory and watched the screens. He wore small glasses, had bowl-cut hair and a goatee, and had a delicate-looking face. The panic around him seemed to have nothing to do with him.

Tyson and several Rocket members stumbled through a heavy security door and hurried to his side.

"Professor Sebastian! They've taken everything outside the base, and the Mahogany Town base has fallen too. We need to leave through the secret passage!"

Sebastian glanced at him and snorted. "Useless. You couldn't even hold one base, and I gave you Project R to help!"

Tyson flushed but could not argue.

"Have you destroyed all the data?"

Sweat broke out on Tyson's face. "It happened so suddenly that we haven't managed to destroy all of it..."

"Then destroy the rest physically," Sebastian said coldly. "Whatever you do, leave none of the data for the International Police."

He turned away from Tyson and looked with interest at a monitor. On it, a Gyarados had just defeated the last Golbat outside the building and was roaring at the sky.

Light flashed across Sebastian's glasses. He spoke almost to himself, excitement in his voice. "What a magnificent body. What perfect power! Compared with the flawed specimens in the Lake of Rage, this is the Gyarados I truly want!"
