Archie looked away from Shelly and turned to Maxie. His position had only grown worse, yet the helpless anger he had felt a moment ago had given way to a certain spiteful pleasure.

A nearby explosion had injured him badly enough that he spat blood. Still, he looked up at Maxie with a mocking smile and called, “Well, Maxie? How do you feel now? What are you going to do?”

Maxie hated the smug look on Archie's face. He also saw Ethan nearby, ready to act. Archie did not need to spell out his meaning. With Primal Groudon out of control, Archie no longer had much to lose. The pressure was now on Maxie.

Why was Archie so pleased? Was he dazed from his injuries? Ethan was their common enemy. Did Archie truly hate Maxie so much more?

Maxie had no time to puzzle over it. Primal Kyogre should be stronger than Deoxys alone. But Primal Groudon was already fighting it, and Ethan owned other Legendary Pokémon besides Deoxys. That combination was dangerous.

Before Ethan appeared, Maxie had spoken boldly, seemingly without fear. With Ethan actually in front of him, he felt enormous pressure despite controlling Primal Kyogre. At least he was not alone.

On Maxie's order, Team Magma grunts poured from the ship. Shouting wildly, they leapt onto the island and rushed inland. They had reason to fight hard. Team Magma was stronger than it had ever been. It was missing one commander, but gaining an ancient Pokémon more than made up for that loss.

They had had chances to capture Groudon before. Every grunt knew this was the closest they had come to realizing their goal. The one flaw was that they controlled Kyogre rather than Groudon. If those positions were reversed, they would not need to be here at all.

Still, if they seized Groudon now, Maxie and Team Magma could make their dream real.

Ethan, Shelly, and Misty released more Pokémon as the grunts charged. Shelly and Misty brought out their Water-type teams. Ethan had deliberately changed his lineup for this operation.

Deoxys was already in the fight. The black Rayquaza remained in its Ultra Ball, not yet ready to appear. Ethan released five other Legendary Pokémon: Zapdos, Articuno, Moltres, Suicune, and Raikou.

Spread apart, the five could form a net around every Pokémon on the field except Primal Groudon and Primal Kyogre. Brought together, they could strike like a fist powerful enough to trouble even those two ancient Pokémon.

Gardevoir and Dragonite also emerged to protect the three Trainers alongside Gengar. Ethan kept his other regular Pokémon in reserve. They were strong enough to matter, but he wanted an answer to any unexpected turn. Long experience had taught him to keep something back, even near the end.

The arrival of so many Pokémon changed the battle immediately. Primal Groudon and Primal Kyogre continued to treat each other as their greatest threat. Under Ethan's telepathic direction, Deoxys ignored Groudon and attacked Kyogre alone.

As in their previous battle, concentrating on one ancient Pokémon rather than dividing their strength was the key. Kyogre was Ethan's target again. This time, though, it had undergone Primal Reversion. Its abilities had grown considerably, and Deoxys's attacks were less effective.

Deoxys had been able to contend with Kyogre and Groudon before. Their primal forms were on another level. Maxie felt a little relief. As he had thought, Deoxys could not defeat a primal ancient Pokémon by itself. Perhaps only the Mega Rayquaza of legend could threaten them now.

Then he looked at the rest of the battlefield, and his relief vanished.

Team Magma and Ethan's forces had engaged, but so had the remnants of Team Aqua. Some of Archie's grunts had slipped away when the situation turned grim. Many more, angry at Team Magma for ruining their chance to secure Groudon, attacked Maxie's people. They hated Team Magma more than they hated Ethan.

Ethan had no such divided feelings. Both leaders were his enemies. He did not intend to spare either of them.

Deoxys, change to Attack Forme!

He sent the order in his mind, then gave his five Legendary Pokémon their assignments through Gardevoir's telepathy. Zapdos, Articuno, and Moltres swept across the sky toward Maxie's ship. On the ground, Raikou and Suicune broke through the grunts toward Archie.

Capture the leaders first. More importantly, take the orbs from their hands.

Maxie saw what Ethan intended. The three Legendary birds crossed the battlefield at speed.

“Stop them!” he shouted.

Tabitha and the elite grunts guarding him released their Pokémon. None dared hold back. Their defense lasted a single exchange. The birds scattered their Pokémon and left many unconscious.

Cold sweat ran down Maxie's face as Zapdos, Articuno, and Moltres closed in. He had made a mistake. He had counted on Primal Kyogre's might without recognizing that Ethan did not need to defeat it first. If Ethan captured him, the fight would be over.

Kyogre was strong enough to face the birds, but it could not protect Maxie from three directions at once, especially while it fought Groudon and Deoxys on the island. Even now, Maxie refused to give up.

He gripped the Blue Orb and urged Kyogre to return. With his other hand, he took several Poké Balls from his pocket and threw them. Blue flashes revealed his own Pokémon between him and the birds. Maxie was not Team Magma's strongest battler, but he was more capable than the elite grunts. Surely his team could buy him a little time.

He had underestimated the birds together. A commander or leader might have had some chance against one of them by sending an entire team. All three acting in concert were another matter.

They had fought under Ethan for a long time. Their individual power had grown, and so had their coordination. Ice, lightning, and fire poured forward, their attacks overlapping. Maxie's defenders fell almost at once.

The birds surrounded him in a triangle, leaving no way out. As he looked desperately from side to side, Zapdos darted its head forward. Its long, pointed beak caught the Blue Orb.

Maxie instinctively pulled the orb toward his chest. A human could not win a contest of strength with Zapdos. One sharp tug, and the orb was gone.

“No!”

For the first time in a long while, Maxie lost his composure completely. Primal Kyogre did not obey him personally. It obeyed the orb. Without it, he could no longer command Kyogre.

He reached for the stolen orb. Before he could touch it, enormous talons seized him and lifted him into the air. Moltres held him like prey, pinning his arms so he could not move. He could only cry out.

His thoughts went blank. He was the leader of Team Magma, a force that had shaken Hoenn. How had he been reduced to this?

On the island, Archie had no chance to laugh at his rival. Suicune and Raikou were coming for him. On the ground, grunts from both teams blocked their path, so they could not move as quickly as the three birds. But nothing in that crowd could hold them back for long.

“Damn it... Is this the end?” Archie pressed a hand to his aching chest and coughed more blood. He did not run. He leaned against a rock, too weak to move.

He was a strong man, but Groudon's shock wave had caught him at close range. His injuries were hidden beneath his clothes; inside, he was bleeding in several places. Even a small movement hurt badly.

Suicune and Raikou approached from either side. Then a roar sounded nearby. A huge, stumbling man rushed in front of Archie.

“M-Matt?” Archie stared at his back.

Team Aqua's commander was broader and more imposing than Archie himself, his bare torso usually making him look like a tower of dark iron. Now he was battered and covered in wounds. Even so, he stood between Archie and the two Legendary Pokémon.

“Boss, I'll hold them off! Get out of here!”

For all his faults, Archie inspired more devotion in his people than the cold Maxie did. Even now, someone was willing to risk himself for him.

“It's no use.” Archie shook his head. “I can't walk. You should leave. Team Aqua can't lose everyone here.”

“Are you joking?” Matt roared at Suicune and Raikou without turning around. “How could I abandon you?”

He charged at them. In the next instant, a heavy jet of water struck him and hurled him back. He landed beside Archie and lay still, unconscious from the impact.

Archie looked down at his loyal commander. Before he could say a word, two shadows fell over him. Suicune and Raikou stood close enough that he could feel the moisture around one and smell the sharp electric tang around the other.

Clack.

The Red Orb slipped from his fingers and rolled to Suicune's feet.

“Heh... ha...” Archie stopped looking at the Pokémon and raised his eyes to the clouded sky. “You really don't hold back...”
