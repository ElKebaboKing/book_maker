He welcomes us here?

Does that mean anyone can tour this ninjutsu school?

In Ethan's impression, ninjas had always been associated with secrecy. Ninja villages in particular would never open themselves to outsiders.

On second thought, however, it made sense. The ninja profession was gradually fading from history, and stronger, more capable Pokémon had replaced most of its functions. If ninjas did not find a way to change, they would truly disappear altogether. Since this was a school founded to innovate, he could not judge it by old assumptions.

With that thought, Ethan studied the elderly ninja before him once more.

Compared with an ordinary man his age, the headmaster was full of energy. His eyes shone brightly, and his body was robust, showing none of the weariness expected of someone in his sixties or seventies.

Ethan also remembered how the man had appeared behind him without a sound. He was unquestionably a powerful ninja with exceptional skill and vast experience.

"May we look around inside?"

"Of course. Please follow me."

With his hands clasped behind his back, the headmaster led the way and introduced the Pokémon Ninjutsu Academy as they walked.

"My academy has many students who study Pokémon ninjutsu day and night. Ariados's String Shot, Venonat's Stun Spore, Scyther's Double Team, and Pineco's Spikes can all be considered forms of Pokémon ninjutsu. The students train here to make themselves and their Pokémon stronger!"

"I see."

Ethan followed the headmaster's gaze. Many people in red ninja outfits were directing Pokémon in battles on a training field. Another group in yellow-brown ninja outfits sat on the ground and practiced some form of training. Farther away, students in green ninja outfits were training their Water-type Pokémon in a small pond.

Even without the headmaster's explanation, Ethan had noticed while watching the anime and playing the games that these moves strongly resembled the ninjutsu he remembered.

He simply had not expected ninjas in this world to classify them as ninjutsu, in addition to the Bug, Grass, Normal, Ground, and other types assigned by Pokémon scholars.

When they reached the center of the training grounds, the headmaster continued, "I believe you've already noticed that students wearing different clothes pursue different fields of study. Those in red specialize in Pokémon battling. Students in yellow and green study ways to increase the power of Pokémon moves. Inside the teaching building, some students in blue ninja outfits study beauty treatments and weight loss!"

"Beauty treatments and weight loss!?"

Ethan stared at him in surprise. What did those have to do with ninjutsu?

Would studying them allow someone to disguise and transform themselves?

Beside him, however, Erika's eyes lit up when she heard the words beauty treatments and weight loss.

She asked, "How exactly do the beauty treatments work?"

Erika was clearly very interested in this particular field.

That was understandable. No matter how beautiful a woman might be, it was difficult to remain unmoved by those two phrases. Even a famous actress earning over a hundred million every year would constantly watch whether her figure had changed and whether her face still retained its radiance.

"Through Pokémon, of course." The headmaster smiled mysteriously. "Take beauty care, for example. We discovered that the silk produced by Spinarak has excellent cosmetic effects. Using it as a facial mask makes the skin smoother and more delicate!"

Spider silk can improve your skin?

Erika was surprised. She had never heard such a theory before and could not help hesitating.

She could accept applying an ordinary herbal essence mask to her face, but silk produced by a Spinarak?

Sorry, but she simply could not imagine what cosmetic effect that could possibly have.

Was there any scientific basis for it?

Ethan, by contrast, did not find the idea especially surprising.

Back on Earth, many strange substances were used as medicine. Bovine bezoar, for example, sounded like a traditional remedy but was actually a dried gallstone taken from cattle.

There were many similar examples. If animal products could serve such purposes, Pokémon products naturally could as well. It was hardly astonishing.

Seeing Erika's doubt, the headmaster smiled. "Don't underestimate that silk. Its cosmetic effects are outstanding! Beautiful people become even more beautiful after using it, and even those who are less attractive will see an improvement!"

Erika was visibly tempted.

When the headmaster saw her reaction, a brilliant smile suddenly spread across his face. His kindly air vanished, replaced by the expression of a shrewd merchant.

"Well? Are either of you interested? We provide three nutritionally balanced meals a day, hot water around the clock, and free Wi-Fi. We're offering a special promotion right now: only 21,580 for fourteen days of study! Would you like to experience it?"

Ethan laughed inwardly.

So the man had been recruiting them after all. No wonder he had welcomed them inside and spent so much time explaining everything.

But Ethan had no intention of making his Pokémon study ninjutsu, and beauty treatments held no interest for him.

Just as he was about to decline, a sharp sound cut through the air nearby. Several silver flashes crossed his vision. With a series of metallic clinks, several shuriken buried themselves in the ground.

Hm!?

The shuriken had landed almost against the tips of his shoes. A few centimeters farther and they would have pierced them.

At the same time, a woman in a red ninja outfit leaped from a tall building nearby and landed in front of Ethan.

He looked carefully. The woman had long, dark green hair and striking features. Most distinctive of all was her alluring presence.

Wait, that face...

Ethan froze for a moment before recognizing her. She was Aya, the female ninja and younger sister of Koga, the Fuchsia Gym Leader!

What was she doing here?

Before Ethan could speak, Aya glared at him angrily through clenched teeth.

"I never expected to run into you here!"

Her attitude left Ethan baffled. Then he suddenly remembered that he had offended her after defeating her at Fuchsia Gym. Later, while traveling to the Indigo League Conference, he had encountered her by chance and defeated her a second time.

Has this woman really held a grudge all this time?

Ethan sighed inwardly, but maintained a gentle smile and tried to look as friendly as possible.

"Miss Aya, we meet again."

"Hmph." Aya crossed her arms and studied Ethan coldly. "The last time we parted, that Jigglypuff appeared out of nowhere and put all of us to sleep! And you! You simply abandoned me on the grassland and left me in the cold wind all night! Now that I've finally found you, it's time to settle both the old score and the new one!"
