He had won!

The stadium thundered with cheers. Ethan had never found this Indigo League particularly difficult, but when Gardevoir returned from the field, he could not contain his joy. He slipped his left arm around her shoulders, raised his right fist high, and shouted in triumph.

Countless cameras carried that moment around the world. Caught up in his excitement, Gardevoir smiled too.

Across the field, Logan recalled his unconscious Hitmonlee and came over, his expression complicated.

Ethan jumped down from the trainer's platform to meet him.

"Congratulations," Logan said.

Ethan wanted to offer him some encouragement. But Mewtwo had erased Logan's memories of New Island. As far as Logan knew, Ethan was simply a stranger who had beaten him.

"Thanks. Don't be too discouraged. You fought well. If you hadn't run into me, you would have had a real chance at the title."

He held out his hand. "I hope you'll come back stronger and challenge me at the next Silver Conference."

"Please don't." Logan shook his head with a rueful smile. "I'm not entering the Silver Conference. I don't want to meet you again. That match was almost enough to scar me for life. Honestly, I'd be happier if we never saw each other again!"

Ethan fell silent. That was more discouraging than he'd expected.

Seeing his speechless face, Logan laughed. The joke took some of the bitterness out of his loss. He gripped Ethan's hand firmly, then turned and walked away.

Ethan waved to the crowd once more and was about to leave when the referee came over.

"Please don't go too far, Ethan. Besides the awards ceremony, there's a closing ceremony later. As champion, you need to arrive early."

"Understood. Thanks for the reminder."

Ethan went through the competitors' tunnel to the lounge. He had only just opened the door when something popped. White, foamy champagne sprayed all over him.

Soaked from head to foot, he had no time to work out what was happening before people moved toward him and cheers filled the room.

He wiped his face. Professor Cerise stood at the front holding a champagne bottle. Chloe, Misty, Ash, and the others filled the lounge beside him, all wearing delighted smiles.

They were genuinely happy that Ethan had won.

"Make way! The champion's here!"

Parker's laughter and the others' applause welcomed him inside. They had come here after his victory to surprise him. The champagne had been bought before the match, ready for exactly this moment.

Nearby, Ash and Souta bounced around like excited monkeys. Brock clapped Ethan on the shoulder and joked that he hoped some of the good luck would rub off on him.

Sabrina brought over a towel and handed it to Ethan with a small smile.

"Congratulations, Champion."

Ethan grinned. The title still sounded unfamiliar, but from now on it would be part of his name.

He took the towel and dried himself. Looking at everyone around him, he felt unexpectedly moved. Without realizing it, he had made so many friends who cared about him.

Misa and Masha squeezed through the group to offer congratulations on behalf of all the bikers.

"Congratulations, Boss!" Masha rubbed his nose and smiled. "Having you as champion is great news. We're proud of you! Nobody will look down on us when we're out riding now."

"Listen," Ethan said, "don't go using my name to do anything bad."

"Of course not! We have a lot of energy, but we're not bad people." Misa gave him an emphatic pat on her chest. "Actually, Boss, we've been here a long time. It's probably time for us to say goodbye."

"You're leaving? Today?" Ethan asked in surprise. When she nodded, he said, "Stay one more night. I won the championship—we should all have a proper meal together. It won't be much of a celebration without you. Surely one night won't set you back."

Misa and Masha exchanged a look and agreed.

Professor Cerise brought out glasses, opened another bottle of champagne, and filled them. Then he saw Chloe and the other younger members of the group waiting expectantly and shook his head.

"Juice for you. You're still too young for champagne."

"What? Why?" Chloe protested. "It's a special occasion. We want to taste it too!"

Misty, Ash, and the others backed her up. Professor Cerise was not a rigid man, and Yoshino joined in their pleas. At last he relented and poured a little into a few more glasses.

Everyone raised a glass. As the eldest among them, Professor Cerise proposed the toast.

"To Ethan and his championship!"

"Cheers!"

They drank. Sabrina apparently had little tolerance for alcohol; after just one glass, her cheeks had gone pink.

Ash stuck out his tongue after his sip.

"So that's alcohol? It tastes awful! Soda's way better. It tastes like... like Tauros pee!"

"I think it's all right." Misty licked her lips and shrugged at him. "That's why I keep saying you're such a child. You have no taste at all."

"What did you say? You're about the same age as me! Stop pretending you're an adult!"

Everyone laughed at their bickering. The lounge filled with the easy warmth of a celebration among friends.

Once things quieted down, Ash looked at Ethan.

"So what do we do next? Is it time to go home?"

"There's one thing left before that," Brock said. "You were just told to attend the closing ceremony, remember?"

"Brock's right," Ethan said. "The Indigo League isn't really over until the closing ceremony is finished."
