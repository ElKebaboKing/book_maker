Arthur's expression was as dark as water. Only moments earlier, he had made a bold declaration to Ethan. Then, in the blink of an eye, his own Pokémon was once again completely countered by type.

The humiliation had arrived far too quickly!

Worse still, Electabuzz was in an even more unfavorable position than Slugma had been. As an Electric type facing the Ground-type Larvitar, its Electric-type moves were completely ineffective.

Damn it. This is even worse than the last battle!

Arthur sighed. The situation had already reached this point, and no amount of frustration would change it.

He removed his glasses and gently polished them with a cloth, using the motion to calm himself.

Forget it...

Panicking now would accomplish nothing. He needed to think of a strategy that could win this battle.

But the more he thought about it, the angrier he became!

Arthur clenched his back teeth. They had agreed to send out their Pokémon simultaneously, so how could he possibly have predicted that Ethan would disregard all expectations and continue using Larvitar?

Why didn't he switch Pokémon!?

Wasn't he afraid I might use a Grass or Water-type Pokémon specifically to counter him!?

Across from Arthur, Ethan was in a completely different mood. He felt very relaxed.

In truth, he had not given much thought to sending Larvitar out again.

Larvitar had performed without any problems in the previous battle. Thanks to its type advantage, the few attacks it had taken had hardly reduced its stamina. More importantly, victory had left Larvitar in high spirits and fully capable of fighting again. Why would Ethan need to switch to another Pokémon?

As matters stood, he once again held a complete type advantage in the second battle. Ethan would not expose an opening that allowed his opponent to reverse the situation. Unless Arthur were suddenly replaced by Ash, Ethan truly could not imagine how he was supposed to lose.

Yes, he was just that confident!

When the battle began, Electabuzz fell straight into a disadvantage, exactly as expected.

With Electric-type moves rendered useless, more than half of Electabuzz's offensive options disappeared. Under Larvitar's fierce assault, it had no effective way to fight back. Normal-type moves alone could never defeat Larvitar when it was free to attack without restraint.

Larvitar opened with Rock Slide. The move inflicted damage while forcing Electabuzz closer. Larvitar then charged forward, used Bite, and sank its teeth into Electabuzz's arm.

Electabuzz groaned in pain. Acting on instinct, it used Discharge in an attempt to force Larvitar to let go. But when the electricity struck Larvitar's rocklike skin, it produced no reaction at all.

Fortunately, Arthur hurriedly ordered Electabuzz to roll across the ground twice, finally throwing Larvitar off and onto the ground.

Outside the field, many of the spectators shook their heads in disappointment at the sight of the battle.

The match had not yet ended, but they could already foresee its result.

This was no different from the previous battle!

Although the favorable type matchup certainly helped, the difference in skill between the two sides was also obvious. The spectators could not understand why the Week Siblings had chosen to pester this man.

Still, the Champion of the Indigo League Conference lived up to his reputation. Ethan truly was strong.

The battle continued. Electabuzz managed to damage Larvitar with Quick Attack and the Fighting-type move Low Kick, but Larvitar ultimately finished it off with a Rock Throw.

Electabuzz collapsed weakly onto the ground. Frieda glanced at her brother and was about to raise her arm to announce the result when a dazzling white light suddenly erupted around Larvitar.

Anyone with even basic knowledge knew that this was the sign of a Pokémon evolving.

Ethan raised an eyebrow.

Larvitar had experienced many battles, large and small, since he captured it. Thinking back over that time, its evolution was indeed due.

The process did not take long. When the white light vanished, Larvitar's figure had disappeared, and a Pokémon with an entirely new form stood before him.

It was Pupitar!

Pupitar's form somewhat resembled the cocoons produced when Caterpie or Weedle evolved, but it was no insect. It was a genuine Rock and Ground-type Pokémon.

A hard, bluish-gray shell covered its entire body. Four sharp spikes protruded along its sides, with three smaller spikes on its forehead. A pair of blood-red eyes observed everything before it through two openings in the shell.

Delighted, Ethan took out his Pokédex and pointed it toward Pupitar.

The Pokédex beeped. "Pupitar, the Hard Shell Pokémon. A Rock and Ground type. Pupitar is a dangerous Pokémon known for rampaging wherever it goes. Its new body is gradually taking shape beneath its hard shell, which remains unharmed even when it strikes steel. Although Pupitar appears unable to move, it releases compressed gas to fly wildly through the air and relieve stress. Its tremendous power can bring down mountains when it goes berserk. Combining hardness with speed gives Pupitar extraordinary destructive force, making it an extremely troublesome Pokémon."

The Pokédex's description made it clear that although Pupitar had not yet evolved into Tyranitar, it was already beginning to reveal its fearsome and unruly nature.

Ethan took out a Poké Ball and recalled Pupitar. At that moment, Arthur and Frieda approached together from the opposite side.

"We lost," Frieda said.

She sighed. Despite admitting defeat, both she and Arthur were deeply unwilling to accept it. Their losses had simply felt too frustrating.

Even so, a loss was a loss. Neither was shameless enough to demand a rematch.

They reached inside their clothes. When they withdrew their hands, each palm held a glittering badge.

Frieda said, "This is my Exhilaration Badge. Please accept it."

Arthur added, "And this is my Carelessness Badge. Keep it safe."

Ethan smiled and accepted the badges from them.

The two badges looked no different from the Heartbeat Badge and Disappointment Badge he had previously received from Tuscany and Wesley. Only their colors varied slightly.

They had clearly been purchased in bulk.

Ethan put the badges away, nodded his thanks to the siblings, then turned and left with Erika without lingering.

Watching his retreating back, Arthur shouted, "Don't celebrate too soon! There are still three more Week Siblings. They'll avenge us for sure!"

Was I celebrating?

Ethan did not break stride. He merely touched his face with a puzzled expression.

Come on, he had not shown any expression at all!

This man called Arthur certainly loved inventing drama for himself.

Ethan could only hope that the remaining Week Siblings would not be as strange as him.
