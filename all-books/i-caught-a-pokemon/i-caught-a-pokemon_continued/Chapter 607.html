Nosepass fell.

Just as Roxanne had expected before the battle, it could not beat Tyranitar. Ethan had also used Eevee's round to get a general sense of Nosepass's abilities, so the outcome was hardly surprising.

Tyranitar had not won effortlessly, though. Nosepass had given it some trouble, and the fight had been far from meaningless.

Applause rose from outside the field. Roxanne's students clapped for Ethan's victory.

They were cheering against their own teacher, but they could hardly help themselves. Today's challenger was far too popular.

Roxanne glanced at the students and smiled helplessly. She recalled Nosepass and walked over to Ethan.

She took out an earthy yellow Badge and held it out to him.

“Congratulations on winning. This is the Stone Badge, proof that you defeated me. Please accept it.”

Ethan thanked her and took the Badge. Curious as ever, Misty hurried over to examine this Hoenn Gym Badge with him.

It looked like two triangular pieces of ore set slightly out of alignment. The design was distinctive, and, like most Hoenn Badges, it used a gradient of color. Ethan thought Hoenn's Badges were prettier and more finely made than those of Johto and Kanto.

He placed the Stone Badge in his Badge case and put the case back in his bag.

Nurse Joy had given him the plain case at the Pokémon Center in Oldale Town after he registered with the Pokémon League. With his first Badge inside it, Ethan had taken a firm first step toward the Ever Grande Conference.

A journey of a thousand miles began with a single step. Catching the first Pokémon and earning the first Badge were good beginnings.

With the challenge finished, Ethan said it was time to go.

Roxanne and her students warmly invited him and Misty to stay for lunch and talk more about battling, but Ethan politely declined.

Erika had only just left, and he was not in the mood. His backtracking over the past several days had cost him time, too. Ash would arrive in Hoenn soon, and the thought made Ethan feel some urgency.

He could not help it. Ash improved so quickly that Ethan had to keep pushing himself if he wanted to stay ahead.

Ethan and Misty left Rustboro Gym and headed out of the city.

Their destination was Lavaridge Town, surrounded by mountains to the northeast. It had Lavaridge Gym, the nearest Gym town apart from Petalburg, and its famous hot springs could ease sore muscles before the next stretch of their journey.

They traveled north.

As they went, the broad road narrowed into a path and then a winding track of loose stone. By noon, when they were ready for a break, they had reached the foot of an unbroken mountain range.

They looked out at one towering rocky mountain after another, with no end in sight.

“Oh no. Looks like we have another climb ahead of us...”

They exchanged weary looks, then sat on rocks beneath a large tree to rest.

After drinking some water and eating their packed lunches, they stared at the seemingly endless mountains and lamented the time and effort it would take to cross them. Then an old man in a straw hat, carrying a bamboo basket on his back, strolled out from another direction.

Misty spotted him. An idea came to her, and she approached with a bright smile.

“Excuse me, sir,” she asked sweetly. “Do you know a shortcut through these mountains?”

She clearly did not relish another mountain trek. Despite her habit of calling herself the world's most beautiful girl, she was a delicate young woman with soft arms and legs. Given a choice, why would she want to scramble along a mountain trail?

The man looked to be over seventy. He lowered his gaze to Misty, and her smile won him over. Without hesitation, he nodded and pointed to a concealed cave not far away.

“Of course. See that cave entrance? It's a shortcut.” He looked at them kindly and added, “You two are Trainers, aren't you? Be careful inside. Don't get separated, and don't go chasing after any Pokémon. There are passages leading every which way, and it's easy to get lost. Once you do, finding your way out is hard. Stay on the main path and you'll get through safely.”

“Stay on the main path?” Misty repeated, then nodded. “Don't worry. We'll be careful!”

They thanked the old man. Misty returned to Ethan with a delighted expression and raised her eyebrows at him triumphantly.

He knew how pleased she was to avoid the climb and gave a helpless smile.

He was glad of the shortcut too. Walking through a cave would be more comfortable than struggling over high mountains under the sun and wind.

Once they had rested enough, they set out again.

From a distance, the opening had looked small. Up close, they found the entrance was five or six meters high. The level gravel beneath their feet looked maintained, suggesting people used this route often.

That reassured them further. The cave really did seem to be a shortcut.

The temperature dropped sharply when they entered, but they had put on thicker clothes beforehand and were comfortable enough.

The old man had said the main path would take them through safely. Even so, Ethan released Eevee and Torchic as a precaution.

The farther they went, the darker it became. They turned their flashlights up to their brightest setting so they could see the way ahead.

Fortunately, the cave was neither a limestone cavern nor an icy tunnel like others Ethan had traveled. The path was easy enough to walk. The only discomfort was a damp draft that blew from somewhere deeper inside and slipped through their collars and sleeves.

Ethan pulled his clothes tighter and swept his flashlight from side to side.

The old man had been right. Beyond the main trail, countless passages branched off in both directions. Some entrances were large, but most were small enough only for little Pokémon to pass through.

How had the first person to discover this shortcut ever found their way out?

That thought made Ethan turn to Misty. “Did the old man tell you what this place is called?”

Misty thought for a moment, then nodded. “I think he called it... Meteor Falls.”
