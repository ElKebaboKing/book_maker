Even after all its travels and training, Tyranitar could not defeat Groudon one-on-one. It was outmatched in size, defense, speed, and power.

In the games, their numbers might not look far apart. This was a living world, not a table of statistics. Without Mega Evolution, Tyranitar could not easily cross the gulf between them. Nor was Ethan directing it. Two Tyranitar relying on brute-force collisions might still have failed.

That was the terrible strength of an ancient Legendary Pokémon. Its presence alone could make most Pokémon hesitate to approach.

Rocks rattled as Tyranitar pushed itself up from the pile. The short exchange had already left wounds across its body. Its eyes held no fear. It roared and charged Groudon again. Whether it could win or not, it had the courage to fight.

Ethan glanced over. He had not watched the exchange closely, but the result was no surprise. Tyranitar's collision had delayed Gengar's pursuit, giving Maxie and Tabitha a little more distance. That was inconvenient, not decisive.

Maxie had been Ethan's true objective all along. He was dangerous because of his ideas and the large organization he led, not because his own battling strength frightened Ethan. Capture him, and Team Magma would lose its leader. The organization might fall apart soon afterward, sparing Hoenn much harm and Ethan a persistent enemy. He did not want to miss this rare opportunity.

Ethan had no plan to catch Groudon or settle a contest of strength with it. Keeping it occupied long enough to secure Maxie would do.

He told Gengar to continue the chase. Tyranitar alone could not hold Groudon for long, so Ethan reached for more Poké Balls. Shelly's urgent voice sounded in his earpiece.

“This is bad. Team Aqua! They've found me!”

Team Aqua? For a moment Ethan thought he had misheard, or that Shelly had said the wrong name in her alarm. Why would Team Aqua be here? Then he realized she meant exactly what she had said.

Her submarine surfaced in the distance. The hatch flew open, and she jumped down and hurried toward him. She had brought the Blue Orb with her.

Since obtaining it, Shelly had guarded the orb carefully. She usually kept it close to prevent Team Aqua from stealing it again. It was awkward to carry outside, so she often left it aboard the submarine. Bringing it onto the island now showed how urgent the situation had become.

As she reached shore, another vessel broke the surface behind her: a huge blue submarine shaped like a shark. It was as imposing as Team Magma's orange craft. Its color and markings left no doubt who owned it.

Shelly ran up, breathless, and pressed the pouch holding the Blue Orb into Ethan's hands. “I was careless. Archie matched his signal and frequency to mine. My radar never showed him closing in. He followed me here without my noticing!”

Both submarines had come from Team Aqua. Archie had found a way to hide his approach from Shelly's instruments. He had caught them off guard while they were focused on Maxie.

How had Team Aqua found Shelly so quickly? She had kept hidden and done nothing conspicuous. Ethan could not answer the question now. He had both orbs in his hands.

That was dangerous. If someone took them from him, both would be lost at once. Few could steal anything from Ethan, but he could not ignore the possibility. He tied both pouches securely behind him.

Then he sent out Raikou and Suicune to help Tyranitar occupy Groudon. He turned back to the blue submarine. Team Aqua was here; he would have to see what they intended.

Gengar had nearly reached Maxie and Tabitha. They sent out Pokémon to fight back. Gengar was much stronger than most of its kind, but could not capture the two immediately. Their struggle stalled.

Across the island, Raikou and Suicune pressed Groudon from opposite sides while Tyranitar attacked with them. The three of them working against it brought to mind three heroes facing a single mighty warrior. Groudon sensed that the two Legendary Pokémon were exceptional. It did not consider them its equals, but it gave them its full attention.

The first part of Shelly's plan had worked. Waking Groudon had drawn Maxie to the island and set up Ethan's ambush. Team Aqua's unexpected arrival now trapped Ethan in the struggle he had hoped to control. Groudon was tying up his own forces.

Two broad-shouldered men climbed onto the top of the blue submarine: Archie and Matt.

Archie looked toward Ethan and Shelly with mocking eyes. “Didn't expect to see us again, did you?” His amplified voice carried clearly across the water.

“Archie!” Shelly called to her former leader, conflicted. “Why are you here?”

He bared his teeth in a grin. “To pay you back for last time, of course. And to take back what belongs to me.”

He nodded to Matt. Tubes emerged from the submarine's hull. At Matt's order, dozens of guns of different calibers opened fire, blanketing the island.

Boom! Boom! Boom!

Shells struck rock and burst all around them. Archie's idea was clear. If his Pokémon could not beat Ethan, he would try human weaponry.

Ethan could not simply stand through an attack covering the whole island. He sent out Gardevoir and had it shield him and Shelly with psychic power, then recalled Raikou, Suicune, and Tyranitar from their fight with Groudon. He still ordered Gengar to pursue Maxie despite the shelling.

Smoke and dust swallowed the island. The bombardment had begun so suddenly that Ethan had lost track of Courtney. He hoped she had found cover. Soon he could no longer see Groudon or Gengar either. All he could do was keep Shelly safe behind his Pokémon's defense and wait out the barrage.

Groudon would not suffer the attack quietly. After several shells struck it, it roared and fired Hyper Beam through the barrage. The thick energy column crossed the island and the sea in an instant and hit the blue submarine's port side.

The submarine lurched. Its guns went silent.

Archie dropped to one knee on the roof. Once the vessel stopped shaking, he stood and glared at Groudon. “Every one of you gets in my way! Fine. I'll destroy you first, then deal with that man and the traitor!”

He shouted down through the hatch, “Everyone out! Finish them all!”

A lower hatch opened. More than a dozen speedboats full of Team Aqua members roared toward the island. Their sailor scarves, fierce eyes, and wild shouting made them resemble pirates. They rode motorboats rather than wooden ships, and their weapons were Poké Balls rather than muskets and cutlasses.

Archie and Matt jumped into a speedboat and followed. They had come prepared, with both people and equipment.

As the smoke thinned, Ethan and Shelly saw the boats racing in. Ethan looked first toward Maxie's escape route. Archie had arrived with a formidable force; beating him would take a hard fight. Maxie was the easier capture if Ethan could still catch him.

But the shelling had changed that, too. Gengar lay overturned after taking a hit. Maxie and Tabitha had reached their submarine.

They did not depart. Maxie saw an opportunity in the confusion and ordered the members still aboard to attack. The enormous drill at the submarine's bow began spinning. It slammed against the island, tearing into the volcanic rock.

Maxie knew his position was poor. Ethan and Team Aqua both threatened him, and he could not let either side take Groudon. Better to disrupt everything than wait for defeat. If he could not have Groudon, neither would they—especially Team Aqua.

Ethan sighed. A few minutes had turned an easy operation into a battle on two fronts. The one advantage was that Groudon had stopped targeting him. Its attention had shifted to Team Magma's submarine, and particularly to the drill chewing through the island.

Groudon had been woken in anger. Now these humans were making an even greater racket. It walked toward the orange submarine, intent on destroying the noisy machine, whether or not anyone held the Red Orb.

Team Aqua's boats reached the shore. Members leaped out, shouting, and sent their Pokémon at Ethan. He and Shelly exchanged a glance and called out their own.

Neither Team had any intention of stopping short. The whole operation was out of Ethan's and Shelly's control. With both orbs in his possession, Ethan could still leave the island. But Courtney was missing. He had finally shaken her faith in Team Magma; abandoning her here would undo everything.
