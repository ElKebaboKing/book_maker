With Naoko accompanying her, Erika went to another room for the ikebana class. Ethan parted ways with them, crossed the wooden corridor, passed behind the main house, and entered the rear courtyard alone.

Although the scenery here was much the same as in the front courtyard, the atmosphere was clearly different.

If the front courtyard was a place where men and women admired flowers and water while conversing quietly, the rear courtyard was far livelier!

Just as Naoko had said, a sizable crowd had gathered around a simple battlefield to watch two Trainers compete.

The Trainers were an ordinary-looking young man and woman. Both wore kimono and appeared unremarkable.

The male Trainer had sent out a Sentret, while the female Trainer used a Teddiursa.

Under their Trainers' continuous commands, the two Pokémon were currently locked in battle.

However...

Scratch, Leer, Lick, Growl, Defense Curl, Tackle...

Whether it was Sentret or Teddiursa, Ethan saw them repeat the same basic moves over and over.

In his judgment, both the Trainers and their Pokémon were complete beginners. The battle could hardly be called exciting. They simply traded attacks, yet the surrounding spectators watched with great interest.

No high-level moves appeared, but people still loved watching a direct, physical brawl where every blow landed!

The match continued for quite some time. In the end, the female Trainer's Teddiursa proved slightly stronger and defeated Sentret.

The spectators applauded, offering their loudest cheers to the winner.

When Teddiursa returned to her side, the female Trainer stroked its head. Then she straightened, planted one hand on her hip, and looked spiritedly around the crowd.

"Who else wants to challenge me!?"

Her recent victory had clearly filled her with confidence.

Before anyone else could act, Ethan squeezed through the crowd and approached her.

"I will!"

The female Trainer's eyes brightened at Ethan's handsome face. She smiled. "Oh, a very handsome challenger. But I won't go easy on you!"

"That's excellent!" Ethan chuckled and answered honestly. "I hope you'll give it everything you have and put me under some pressure. But if you're only as strong as you were in that last battle, you won't be able to defeat me."

"Hmph. That's some boast!"

Apparently feeling that the man had looked down on her, the female Trainer thrust a hand forward and shouted, "Teddiursa, go! Teach that guy a lesson!"

Teddiursa ran onto the battlefield and let out several childish roars at Ethan.

"Good. You've got plenty of spirit."

Ethan smiled kindly at Teddiursa, then reached toward his waist pouch. His fingers passed over several Poké Balls before selecting one and throwing it onto the field.

"Come out, Scyther!"

Blue light flashed, and Scyther, which had not fought in quite some time, appeared before Teddiursa.

The moment it entered the field, Scyther struck an aggressive pose, ready for a fierce battle. But when it saw that its opponent was only a Teddiursa, it turned back toward its Trainer in confusion.

Hmm? What's going on?

Why is my opponent such a weak Pokémon!?

I haven't battled in so long, and this is what I face the moment I return?

Seeing its reaction, Ethan shook his head gently. "Stop staring. No matter who your opponent is, never underestimate them. Give it your full strength!"

At his words, Scyther turned and fixed a serious gaze on its opponent.

It had no choice. Since its Trainer had said so, it could only fight at full strength!

Locked beneath Scyther's gaze, Teddiursa felt like prey before a predator and instinctively shuddered.

Failing to notice her Pokémon's unease, the female Trainer issued an attack command.

"Teddiursa, use Fury Swipes!"

Trembling, Teddiursa ran toward Scyther. It raised its two small claws high as they gleamed with a sharp light.

Just as Teddiursa drew near, Scyther suddenly leaped forward. It raised an arm and crossed paths with Teddiursa in an instant. Everyone present heard a slicing sound as cold light flashed, and Teddiursa froze in place as though bound by a spell!

"Teddiursa! Are you all right!?" The female Trainer stared blankly, completely unaware of what had happened.

On the field, Scyther slowly lowered its arm like a swordsman returning a blade to its sheath.

The moment its arm came fully down, Teddiursa's body reacted as though a delayed mechanism had been triggered. It toppled heavily onto the battlefield, sending dust billowing into the air!

Scyther glanced back at its opponent and shook its head with an extremely human expression.

That's it?

I barely used any strength. How did you fall so easily!?

Weak. Far too weak!

"Teddiursa!"

The female Trainer cried out and rushed to her Pokémon, gathering it into her arms.

She could not believe her Pokémon had been defeated so simply in a single blow!

Is this man really that strong?

Even so, Teddiursa shouldn't have been knocked out in one hit!

After thinking for a long time, she could only blame the defeat on Teddiursa's lengthy battle with Sentret, which had consumed too much stamina.

Ethan sighed helplessly. How could he put it? The match had presented no difficulty whatsoever. The gap in strength between the two Pokémon was simply too great.

The spectators' reactions made that perfectly clear.

If the match had been evenly fought, victory would naturally have earned applause and cheers like those the female Trainer received. Scyther's win had been so effortless, however, that the spectators merely stared at one another, unsure of what had happened!

When the area suddenly fell silent, Ethan cleared his throat and looked around at the crowd. "Does anyone else want to battle me?"

With so many people present, there were naturally other Trainers eager to fight.

They had not yet realized just how powerful Ethan was. Like the female Trainer, they attributed Scyther's victory to Teddiursa's depleted stamina.

The crowd grew animated again. A man stepped onto the field and was cleanly defeated by Ethan, after which another female Trainer immediately came forward.

Ethan crushed his opponents in both matches. After winning five consecutive battles, he decided that this level of competition offered no useful training. He shook his head, recalled Scyther, and left without enthusiasm beneath the crowd's astonished stares.
