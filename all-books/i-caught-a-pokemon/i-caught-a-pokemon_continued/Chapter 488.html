Compared with the joy Ethan and Dragonite felt, Chuck's expression grew heavier after his initial surprise.

He had raised the difficulty and sent out Machoke, yet he had still lost? This young man was no ordinary opponent...

Chuck had to revise his estimate of Ethan's strength upward again.

It had been a long time since he had lost two rounds in a row. To salvage some dignity before his students and win back a round, it was time to show what he could really do!

Under everyone's puzzled gaze, he quietly left the room. When he returned, he was holding a Great Ball.

He took his place in the Trainer's area again and shook his head with a rueful smile.

"I thought I wouldn't need it after becoming a Gym Leader. I never expected I'd fight beside it again."

Ethan's eyes settled on the Great Ball. Chuck had not said so outright, but everyone understood that it must hold his strongest Pokémon!

"Master..."

Several of the older students recognized the ball and crowded around, excited and nervous.

"Is that... your ace Pokémon in there?"

"Master! I've never seen you command it before. You're finally going to let it battle!?"

"I've only heard how strong it is. I can't believe we finally get to see it!!"

Listening to their overlapping voices, Chuck sighed and stroked the Great Ball, as though remembering the past. After a long moment, he came back to himself and waved the students away.

"All right. Save your questions for later. We shouldn't keep our challenger waiting!"

Once the field was clear, Chuck threw the Great Ball with all his strength.

Bang! A blue flash revealed an unusual Pokémon before Ethan. It looked like an old man and held two concrete pillars.

What's that...?

Unfamiliar with it, Ethan took out his Pokédex and pointed it at the Pokémon.

"Beep. Conkeldurr, the Muscular Pokémon. Fighting type. Conkeldurr can swing the two concrete pillars it uses as canes by sheer strength, wielding them deftly in all kinds of moves. When serious, it throws the pillars aside and charges an opponent with its bare fists. It is said that humans learned to make concrete from Conkeldurr two thousand years ago."

Conkeldurr...

It was not a Pokémon native to Kanto, Johto, or Hoenn. You would normally have to go to Unova or another region to see one.

Ethan knew little about the species. He only remembered its evolutionary line: Timburr evolved into Gurdurr, which in turn evolved into Conkeldurr.

This had to be the ace Pokémon that had traveled the world with Chuck in his younger days! Bringing out the ace he had kept in reserve showed that Chuck was giving this battle everything he had.

Ethan glanced at Dragonite. It was eager to fight and still in good condition, so he kept it in the match.

"Dragonite, you can do it."

At Ethan's encouragement, Dragonite turned and nodded firmly to him.

The two Pokémon took their places opposite each other.

Conkeldurr looked every bit the old man it resembled, standing as steady as a mountain. Dragonite, by contrast, was like an excited youngster just starting out. It hovered in the air, beating its little wings, almost bursting with enthusiasm!

At the referee's signal, the battle began.

Now that it had arms and legs, Dragonite took the initiative in close combat. The moment the referee lowered his flag, it rushed at Conkeldurr like a gust of wind.

It was Extreme Speed, a physical Normal-type move that struck before an opponent could react.

Conkeldurr was nowhere near fast enough to dodge and took the hit squarely. But before it could be knocked off the ground, it gripped one of its concrete pillars with both hands. Using the pillar's weight, it managed to steady itself!

And it was not finished. With tremendous force, Conkeldurr swung its other pillar. It traced a full circle through the air with a rush of wind and slammed into Dragonite.

Bang! Dragonite flew backward. It beat its wings frantically and righted itself in midair just in time to avoid being knocked out of bounds.

Both Pokémon had taken damage in their very first exchange, but Conkeldurr seemed to have gained a slight edge!

Chuck's ace lived up to its reputation. A newly evolved Dragonite was no match for its experience!

"Dragonite, stop acting on your own!" Ethan called when Dragonite tried to charge in again. "Listen to me. Use Thunder Wave!"

Hearing its Trainer, Dragonite gradually calmed down.

As it approached Conkeldurr again, its two threadlike feelers released streams of electricity that wound toward the opponent along wavering paths.

Conkeldurr remained calm. It planted one of its concrete pillars in front of itself, and the electricity vanished the instant it struck the pillar.

The concrete conducted the current just enough to draw it down into the ground and disperse it. Conkeldurr clearly had a wealth of experience...

When that attack failed, Ethan promptly changed tactics.

At his command, Dragonite beat its wings rapidly. Energy gathered, and a visible whirlwind began to form between them.

Before the move could fully take shape, something whistled through the air. A concrete pillar flew across the field and struck Dragonite with perfect aim!

Smack! Caught off guard, Dragonite took the pillar squarely in its belly. Fortunately, the thick padding there cushioned the blow. It fell to the floor and suffered some damage, but nothing serious.

The whirlwind it had been building scattered in an instant!

Ethan frowned.

Dragonite was gradually losing ground. With its two concrete pillars, Conkeldurr could both attack and defend, making it a difficult opponent.

This can't go on...

As he thought, Ethan spotted the pillar that had rolled aside after striking Dragonite. An idea came to him.

If the pillars were Conkeldurr's greatest advantage, he only had to take that advantage away!

"Quick, throw that pillar away!" he shouted to Dragonite.
