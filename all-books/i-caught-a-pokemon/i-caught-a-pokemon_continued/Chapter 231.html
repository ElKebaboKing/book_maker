Ethan and Sabrina left Trovita Island and continued north. Their destination was Kumquat Gym, the last of the Orange League's four Gyms.

The Gym stood on Kumquat Island, a long way from Trovita Island. One lay in the south of the archipelago, the other in the north. Even on Charizard, the trip would take at least a day and a night, and their route would carry them past Shamouti Island again.

The sea breeze brushed their faces as Charizard bore them onward. They flew for hours. When the sky began to darken and Ethan's legs had grown numb from sitting, he decided not to push on. He patted Charizard's neck and guided it toward a nearby island.

There was still a long distance to cover, but nothing urgent awaited them. They could take their time.

As they descended, they saw thick trees covering most of the small island. Beyond a broad forest, a busy town finally came into view by the sea. Ethan felt relieved. At least it was not a deserted island where they would have to camp for the night.

"Shall we stop here?" Sabrina asked softly.

"Mm-hmm. Sleeping in a tent has its charms, but honestly, a soft bed and hot water whenever you want it are much better after a day of travel."

Sabrina agreed. If a bed was available, who would choose to sleep in a tent every night?

They noticed something unusual as soon as they entered the town. Almost every Trainer they passed had a Bug-type Pokémon at their side. Even wild Bug types poked their heads out around town, apparently unafraid of the people living there.

After walking a few hundred meters, Ethan had seen Caterpie, Weedle, Pinsir, Venonat, Paras, Butterfree, Beedrill, and Scyther. Bug Pokémon seemed to be everywhere. The island felt like a haven for them, and the residents took their presence for granted.

"There are so many Bug-type Pokémon," Sabrina said, frowning. She had never liked insects.

"There certainly are." Ethan heard the distaste in her voice and rubbed his chin. "It might have something to do with that forest. It looked dense from the air. It's smaller than Viridian Forest, but still large enough to be a good place for Bug types to live and breed."

They followed the street to a hotel and went to the front desk. The young receptionist rose when she saw them and gave a slight bow.

"Welcome."

In the conversation that followed, Ethan learned that they had landed on Murcott Island. As he had guessed, it was famous for its many Bug-type Pokémon. According to the receptionist, at least ten thousand lived in the forest outside town.

She looked from Ethan to Sabrina and smiled. "How many rooms would you like?"

"Two," Sabrina answered simply, as she usually did.

"Of course." The receptionist was about to register them when Ethan stopped her with a smile.

"Wait. Make it one room."

Sabrina looked at him curiously. He shrugged. "Two rooms would be a waste. We can spend the money we save on a more comfortable one."

She rolled her eyes at the excuse. Ethan was pressing his luck again, and he was hardly concealing what he wanted. She knew his reason, yet by now she found she could not refuse him.

Seeing that Sabrina did not object, the receptionist booked a single room. Ethan paid and took the key card. In a fine mood, he walked ahead, humming an unfamiliar tune. Sabrina followed with her arms folded, shaking her head helplessly, and entered the room after him.

Moonlight shone again that night. Ethan got what he had hoped for, and the two enjoyed each other's company.

...

Ethan slept until late the following morning. The other side of the bed was empty. He found Sabrina already washed and dressed, sitting with perfect poise at the table and eating breakfast. Her phone screen was still lit beside her, as though she had just ended a call.

When he opened his eyes, she spoke gently. "Did I wake you?"

He shook his head, got dressed, and sat beside her. "What's wrong? You look serious. Did someone call you?"

Sabrina tipped her chin toward the phone. "It was President Goodshow of the Pokémon League. He wants me to return to Saffron City and take charge of Saffron Gym again."

Ethan frowned. "Didn't you say your father was looking after the Gym for you? Why do they need you back?"

She sighed. "It seems they only feel comfortable when I'm there myself."

"Your father was the previous Gym Leader, wasn't he? Isn't that enough? Do those League people doubt his ability?"

"I'm the current Leader, and I defeated him. They clearly value me more than they do my father." Sabrina gave a cold snort. She disliked the League's attempt to dictate where she should be. But Ethan's concern warmed her, and her voice softened. "Don't worry about them. I didn't agree. Even if I don't go back, they won't dare do anything to me."

Despite her assurance, the news troubled Ethan. They had grown so close that he wanted to be with her every night. He did not want her to leave.

Yet being a Gym Leader was her job. If she wanted to keep it, sooner or later they would have to part for a while. What a headache. Perhaps there was no use worrying now. They would deal with it when the time came.

After Ethan ate breakfast, they left the hotel. Instead of departing Murcott Island, they headed for the forest beside the town. Since they had come this far, they might as well explore and see whether any Pokémon there were worth catching.
