After they finished laughing, Professor Elm said, "I came here at someone's invitation to help study a certain Poké Ball."

He noticed Ethan's travel-worn appearance and smiled. "Let me guess. You're here to challenge the Azalea Gym, aren't you?"

"That's right." Ethan nodded. "I came to challenge Bugsy, the Azalea Gym Leader!"

Professor Elm adjusted his glasses, tracing Ethan's route through Johto in his mind.

"Given where Azalea Town is, you must have made quite a detour to reach it only now. By the way, how many badges have you collected?"

"I have gone a long way around, but I've gained a lot along the way. As for badges, I've just earned my sixth."

"Six..." Professor Elm said thoughtfully. "That's a good pace. As far as I know, many Trainers who set out from New Bark Town have only four or five by now."

Was it really a good pace?

Ethan smiled and shook his head, but did not explain.

If Team Rocket and other matters had not interrupted him, and he had focused only on challenging Gyms, he would have collected all eight badges long ago.

But he would not have trained his Pokémon as well, or enjoyed the journey nearly as much.

Professor Elm noticed Erika beside him. "And who is this young lady?"

"Let me introduce you." Ethan stepped back to give them room. "This is Erika, the Celadon Gym Leader from Kanto. She's traveling with me now. Erika, this is Professor Elm, one of Johto's leading Pokémon researchers and a former student of Professor Oak!"

Erika greeted him with a graceful bow. Her bearing spoke for itself.

"It's a pleasure to meet you, Professor."

"And you, Miss Erika."

After they had exchanged greetings, Ethan asked, "Professor, that Poké Ball you said you were studying...?"

"It's a remarkable Poké Ball. A Trainer brought it all the way here from Kanto. No one has found a way to open it, so I was invited to study it with them. Since you're here, why don't you come and see it? As a champion, you might be able to help us!"

At Professor Elm's description, Ethan already had a guess.

A mysterious Poké Ball carried from Kanto all the way to Azalea Town could only be the GS Ball! Ash had brought it back from the Orange Islands to Professor Oak's laboratory, and it had then been sent here.

After all this time, they still had not figured it out!

Hmm...

They had nothing pressing to do, and the Gym challenge could wait. Ethan and Erika exchanged a glance, agreed to go with Professor Elm, and followed him toward the western side of town.

Azalea Town was neither large nor bustling. Its streets were nearly empty, with only the occasional passerby. Life seemed to move slowly here.

In truth, most small cities and towns in Kanto and Johto were like this, outside the busy major cities.

As they walked, they saw Slowpoke everywhere. The Pokémon lazed in the sun under trees, on walls and rooftops, and in the streets. Signs reading "Watch Out for Slowpoke" stood all around town!

The townspeople were entirely accustomed to them and lived alongside them in harmony. Even when Slowpoke openly ate fruit from a vendor's stall, the owner paid them no mind.

"Amazing, isn't it?" Professor Elm said. "People here hold Slowpoke in special reverence. Some even regard them as gods!"

"Because of the rain four hundred years ago?" Erika asked.

"Exactly. That rain saved the land and many lives. Slowpoke have been cherished here ever since. Harming one is forbidden, and anyone who does may even be taken to the police station!"

Professor Elm nodded, then gave them an approving look. "You even know that story? You must have done your homework before coming here!"

"Of course. You have to be prepared!" Ethan said. A moment later, he wiped sweat from his forehead and glanced up at the sun. "Speaking of which, it really is hot here."

"It is," Professor Elm agreed with a sigh. "The sun is fierce, and mountains surround the town on three sides. The cool, damp sea breeze can't reach it, which makes the heat worse."

"I see." Ethan understood. "No wonder there was such a terrible drought four hundred years ago."

They passed a Slowpoke lying across the middle of the road. Erika glanced at Ethan and asked quietly, "With so many Slowpoke here, you could catch one easily. Don't you want to?"

Ethan shook his head.

"No. I'm not interested in this species. Besides, if I caught one here, the townspeople would chase me through the streets. It wouldn't be worth the trouble."

The mention of Slowpoke made him think of the Slowking he had caught in the Orange Islands, and then of Sabrina.

What was Sabrina doing at the Saffron Gym right now?

As they chatted, the three crossed most of Azalea Town and reached its northwest corner.

Professor Elm stopped in front of a large, old-fashioned house. "Here we are. This is Kurt's home."

He knocked. Before long, a little girl opened the gate and peered out.

She was only four or five, with her hair tied into two curled pigtails. She looked like someone who took herself very seriously for her age.

"Who are you looking for?" she asked in a clear voice.

"Hello." Professor Elm stepped forward with a kindly smile. "I'm Professor Elm. Mr. Kurt invited me."

"Oh! I know you!" Recognition lit up the little girl's face. "Grandpa told me that if a professor with glasses who looks like a monkey came by, I should let him straight in!"

Professor Elm fell silent.

Ethan and Erika struggled not to laugh. Out of consideration for the professor, neither made a sound.

She called him a monkey...

In fact, when Ethan had first met Professor Elm, his thin frame and the peculiar shape of his hair had given him much the same impression.

"So you're Mr. Kurt's granddaughter..." Professor Elm managed an awkward smile. "How... lively and adorable you are."
