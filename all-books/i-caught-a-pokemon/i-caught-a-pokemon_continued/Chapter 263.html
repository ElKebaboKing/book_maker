Ethan and Sabrina left the beach and went straight back to their guesthouse. All the way there, Sabrina kept wondering why he had made a bet with the fruit vendor.

"You won the Indigo League. You're a champion. Why wager with an ordinary street vendor? If people hear about it, won't they think less of you?"

She looked genuinely concerned. Ethan smiled and shook his head. "It was a whim. I wanted to surprise him. When the match starts tomorrow and he sees that the man he bet against is the challenger, his expression will be priceless."

That was such a childish reason that Sabrina could only give him a sidelong look. "You're bored, aren't you?"

Ethan put an arm around her. "He decided Drake would win without knowing a thing about his opponent. I was teasing him a little. It's harmless. Don't let it bother you."

They crossed the street into the guesthouse courtyard and returned to their room. Ethan took off his shoes and lay on the bed, staring at the ceiling as he considered tomorrow's match. Sabrina sat on the sofa and switched on the television.

Above them, an old ceiling fan turned sluggishly. It squeaked in a way that made it sound as though it might fall at any moment.

From the recordings he had watched on the beach and the vendor's comments, Ethan had a reasonable idea of Drake's team. Ditto, Onix, Venusaur, Gengar, Electabuzz, and his ace Dragonite: those six Pokémon appeared in nearly every match. Unless something changed, Ethan expected to face them tomorrow.

Knowing the opponent's team did not make his own choices easy. Dratini was not ready to battle, and Blastoise had already been sent back. That left him only eight Pokémon to choose from. He would have to put together the best six he could.

Then he heard the television mention tomorrow's match. He sat up. A man and a woman were hosting a special segment from a studio, building excitement for the Winner's Cup.

The man wore a casual suit and spoke in a smooth broadcast voice. "As we've reported, Pummelo Stadium will host a Winner's Cup match tomorrow at ten in the morning, the first in quite some time. Come watch and cheer on both Trainers!"

His cohost, a playful-looking woman with short hair, nodded. "It's been so long since the last one. I can't wait!"

"And I've heard that this challenger is unlike the others," the man said, feigning mystery. "He has quite a reputation."

"Oh? Who is he?" the woman asked, playing along.

The man faced the camera, his words practiced and confident. "Since beginning his journey, this Trainer has competed in only one major tournament. Yet he fought his way through it with extraordinary strength, never losing a match."

"Really? Now you have to tell us who he is!"

"Tomorrow's challenger..." The man paused for effect. "...is Ethan Vale, the newly crowned Indigo League champion!"

"Ethan Vale?" The woman covered her mouth in surprise. "I never expected him to come to our Orange League! I've watched his matches. He's handsome, and his Pokémon left a strong impression on me."

"Exactly. After conquering the Indigo League, he's set his sights on ours. It seems the strength of our region has caught his attention. But when an undefeated champion meets an undefeated Supreme Gym Leader, who will come out on top? Tomorrow's match should be something special."

"I wish tomorrow would hurry up!" the woman said, clasping her hands. Someone off camera apparently signaled to her. She turned back to the lens with a sweet smile. "That's all for today's special broadcast. Remember: the Winner's Cup is tomorrow at ten, exclusively on Orange Islands TV. See you then!"

The hosts waved goodbye, and the screen switched to an advertisement.

Sabrina leaned back into the sofa and looked over at Ethan. "They're giving you quite a publicity push."

"I didn't expect this either." Ethan shook his head. "I asked them to treat me like any other challenger. Now they've made sure everyone will be watching."

He could already imagine the discussion once the match aired. Viewers from the Orange Islands would tune in, and many in Kanto would probably follow the live broadcast too. The attention put pressure on him.

He looked over the Poké Balls holding Gardevoir, Gyarados, Charizard, Jolteon, Venusaur, Crystal Onix, and Scyther. Then he glanced at the shadow at his feet, where Haunter rested. How should he choose?

He called out all his Pokémon for a brief meeting and went over the points he wanted them to remember.

That night, the match weighed on his mind enough to keep him awake, a rare thing for Ethan. To stop dwelling on it, he turned to Sabrina. They were intimate several times before exhaustion finally overtook him, and he fell asleep against her stomach.

He had prepared as much as he could. The rest would depend on how he performed tomorrow.
