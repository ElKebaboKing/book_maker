Ethan nodded, then glanced at the single flying-disc launcher in the corner. "Who goes first?"

"Isn't it obvious?" Cissy folded her arms. "As the Gym Leader, I'll show you how it's done."

She signaled Senta. He went to the launcher, made an adjustment, and pressed its switch. Three discs shot out together.

Seadra tracked them with its eyes. When they reached the right height, its long snout swelled and it fired three quick, separate jets of water. Every disc shattered.

Fragments rained onto the ground.

"Yes!" Senta clapped as if they had already won. Cissy gave Ethan a proud look. He might be an expert battler, but this was her specialty. She would not lose here.

Seadra withdrew, and Blastoise took its place.

Seeing it ready, Senta quietly nudged the launcher's speed setting up one notch. He wanted payback for what Ethan and Sabrina had done to him.

It was only a single setting, but the discs would fly much faster. The change could decide the contest.

Ethan was focused on Blastoise and did not notice. Haunter, however, watched from his shadow. A practiced prankster, it was quick to spot another trick—and the traps that went with it.

Haunter floated up and whispered in Ethan's ear. Ethan nodded slightly, then looked across the room at Senta without changing his expression.

The boy gave him a smug smile, almost daring him to object.

Ethan sighed. Their earlier encounter had taught Senta nothing. Blastoise already lacked Seadra's precision, and the faster discs would make the contest harder still.

After a moment's thought, he beckoned Blastoise over and spoke quietly to it.

"Blas?" Blastoise looked surprised. Ethan was serious, so it nodded. "Blastoise!"

Cissy assumed Ethan was giving his Pokémon a strategy. She was too far away to hear him. "Ready?" she asked.

"Go ahead."

To Cissy's surprise, Blastoise began building pressure in both of its back cannons before the launcher had fired. Starting so early would make Water Gun stronger, but also harder to control. Surely the Indigo League champion knew that. What was he planning?

Senta gave it no thought. Ethan had not challenged the setting, so the boy pressed the switch, already picturing Blastoise missing every disc.

The instant the launcher fired, two heavy streams of water burst from Blastoise's cannons. They were aimed at the machine in front of Senta, not at the top of the discs' flight.

The three discs had barely left the launcher and had no time to spread apart. The streams converged and destroyed them all at once.

Spray splashed across Senta's face and soaked the clothes he had only just changed into. Pieces of the discs struck him with the water.

"Hey!" He scrambled aside, too late to stay dry.

Cissy understood what Ethan had done. A disc was hardest to hit just after launch, while it was moving fastest. The usual time to shoot was near the top of its arc, when it slowed or began to fall. Ethan had instead caught all three before they separated.

Senta wiped water from his face. "That doesn't count! Sis, he aimed at me on purpose!"

"You're the one who did something on purpose," Ethan said.

At his signal, Haunter darted over, pulled the launcher free of its floor mount with both claws, and carried it to Cissy.

"Take a good look, Miss Cissy," Ethan said. "I hadn't realized this was another Mikan Gym specialty."

Cissy looked at the controls. Her expression changed at once. "Senta!"

Her own brother had tampered with a challenge in front of her and the Indigo League champion. He might have thought of it as a prank, but if trainers or islanders heard that Mikan Gym cheated, it could damage the reputation of the entire Orange League.

Anger overcame her. She strode to Senta and rapped him hard on the head with her knuckles.

"Ow! Sis—"

"Out!"

"But, Sis—"

"I said out!"

Her voice rang through the Gym. Senta had never seen her this angry. Shaking, he went through a side door without another word.

Once he was gone, Cissy drew a long breath and gave Ethan an awkward smile. "I'm sorry. I've let him get away with too much, and he's become a little troublemaker. I never intended for anything like this to happen. Please don't spread word of it."

Ethan did not press the matter further, but his voice was firm. "I told you he needed discipline. Doing this during an official challenge goes beyond a prank. It was dishonest and badly behaved."

Cissy's face flushed. She nodded. "You're right. I promise it won't happen again."
