The tunnel beyond the entrance was deep, narrow, and almost completely dark. Even with the submarine's powerful searchlight, Shelly could barely see the way ahead.

She drove as fast as she dared, but they did not catch Archie. After several hundred meters, light appeared ahead and the tunnel curved upward. The passage was nearing its end.

The submarine broke through the surface inside an underground cavern. Before Ethan could take in the surroundings, he saw another submarine of exactly the same design moored nearby. It had to be Archie's.

No one remained aboard. A single Team Aqua member stood guard on the shore.

Shelly opened the hatch and climbed out first. The guard stared, then saluted. “Boss, what are you doing here?”

His eyes moved to Ethan and Misty as they emerged behind her. They looked unfamiliar, and he wondered who they were.

Shelly calmly stepped into his line of sight. “Where's Archie?”

He pointed deeper into the cavern. “He left a moment ago. He can't be far.”

Without another word, Shelly led Ethan and Misty after him into the darkness. They walked faster and faster, soon nearly running as they clambered over protruding rocks and crossed channels of water.

Even their flashlights did little to improve the visibility. A careless step could send someone sprawling. Shelly lost her footing while climbing a rock and nearly fell; Ethan caught her in time.

She did not thank him. She simply hurried on. Ethan found it faintly amusing. Days ago they had been fighting fiercely over the same Pokémon. Now, for the moment, they were on the same side. Fate could change things quickly.

After several minutes, dim light appeared ahead. Ethan heard water rushing, with voices beneath the sound. They quickened their pace. Four figures came into view near the light, and a man's loud, rough voice rang through the cavern.

“Ha ha ha! Kyogre, the Legendary Pokémon of ancient times! After all these years, I've finally found you!”

Even before he saw the speaker, Ethan knew it must be Archie.

Closer up, Archie proved strongly built, though not as enormous as Matt. He wore a blue bandanna over his head and had a full beard. His blue, sharkskin-like diving suit and the anchor-shaped chain on his chest made him resemble a pirate. He looked tough and formidable.

Three senior Team Aqua members—two men and a woman—stood beside him.

Before them lay a brightly lit pool whose water gave off a strange blue glow. In its center floated an enormous blue Pokémon that seemed part whale, part bird, part fish.

It was Kyogre, the embodiment of the sea. Myth credited it with command over water and with expanding the ocean by drowning land beneath torrential rain and mighty waves. Pokémon as rare as Lugia were beyond most people's hopes of ever seeing; Kyogre was rarer still.

Archie stood before it, exultant. “Listen, Kyogre! I'm Archie, leader of Team Aqua! I've waited for this day for years. The world has gone wrong. Everyone cares only about their own interests, and nature and Pokémon suffer for it. Together, we'll put the world back the way it should be!”

He took out a crystal-bright orb about the size of his fist and held it toward Kyogre.

Shelly could contain herself no longer. She ran toward him, shouting, “Archie! Wait!”

“Hm?” His hand stopped in midair. He turned and frowned. “Shelly? Why are you here? No matter—come and look! Everything we've pursued is right in front of us. Our dream is about to become real!”

“Listen to me first!” she pleaded. “The world you imagine and the world Kyogre would create are completely different!”

Archie's face darkened. “What nonsense are you talking?”

His sudden harshness made Shelly flinch. She forced herself to continue, her voice shaking. “I studied it at the Weather Institute. I simulated what would happen when Primal Kyogre woke—whether your ideal world could exist, how far its power would reach. Every result led to the same thing: a disaster we couldn't escape. All living things would sink beneath the sea it brings!”

The three senior members looked stunned. Archie merely snorted.

“Simulations? Research? Cold figures and your guesses, nothing more!” He raised the Blue Orb. “With this in my hand, Kyogre will serve me!”

This was Ethan's first meeting with Archie. The man showed him only immovable stubbornness and a near-hysterical certainty. He would hear no one else's warning. He was like a gambler who had placed everything on one final card and believed he alone would win.

Ethan sighed and stepped up beside Shelly. “It looks as though talking to him failed. We should try my way now.”

Only then did Archie notice the man in a Team Aqua uniform beside her. He knew most of the members who worked closely with his commanders, but he had never seen this one.

“Who are you?”

“Who am I?” Ethan took off his bandanna and smiled. “I've crossed paths with your people plenty of times lately. I thought your leader would recognize me on sight. Even the ordinary members have managed that. Have you been so absorbed in finding Kyogre that you don't know who Team Aqua's greatest enemy is?”

“What?” Archie stared, confused. Two seconds passed before recognition struck. “You! You're Ethan Vale?”

Before Ethan could answer, Archie turned his fury on Shelly. “Traitor! You brought him here?”

“I...” Shelly tried to explain. Archie waved an arm before she could. The three members beside him released their Pokémon.

Several Mightyena, Golbat, and Crawdaunt surrounded Ethan, Misty, and Shelly.

Shelly had known this might happen. Facing it for real still left her heartsick. Ethan and Misty had come prepared. They threw their Poké Balls, and their own Pokémon appeared.

Ethan sent out Gardevoir, Dragonite, Scizor, Eevee, and Metang. Misty released Starmie, Staryu, Politoed, Corsola, and Psyduck.

Wait. Psyduck? Misty startled and immediately recalled it.

The two sides faced each other. Battle was moments away, but Shelly tried one last time. “Wait! Don't attack! Archie, I haven't betrayed Team Aqua. Ethan came here to—”

Archie had already decided she was a traitor. At his order, the senior members hesitated only briefly before attacking. Until seconds ago, Shelly had been their commander, the organization's strategist and second only to Archie. Their loyalty to him overrode it.

Powerful jets of water swept toward the three of them. Shelly stood frozen. Ethan pulled her out of their path, while Gardevoir teleported in front of her and stopped the Water Guns with Confusion.

Seeing Ethan protect Shelly only confirmed Archie's belief that she had betrayed him. He had trusted her enough to make her a commander, and now, he thought, she had turned against him.

The sight of Kyogre and the supposed betrayal drove him into a shark-like frenzy. Shelly's warning had planted a trace of doubt before; now he dismissed it as a lie invented to steal his orb.

Archie turned back toward Kyogre to use the Blue Orb. Ethan urged his Pokémon to attack faster. These senior members were much tougher than ordinary grunts, however, and their coordinated defense held.

The narrow cavern lay deep underground. Ethan could not risk using his Legendary Pokémon or attacks strong enough to bring the roof down and bury them all. His strongest regular partners were elsewhere. Given enough time, he might break through; right now he did not have it.

He called out Gengar. “Go through the ground and take that orb!”

“Got it!” Gengar understood the urgency. It sank into the cave floor without another joke and slipped toward Archie.

Team Aqua's senior members, though weaker than Ethan, continued to work together and refused to yield an inch.

“Everything you've done is for nothing!” Archie laughed wildly and hurled the Blue Orb toward Kyogre.

“No!” Shelly cried.

Time seemed to slow. The orb spun through the air toward the motionless Pokémon. Ethan followed it with his eyes, his heart in his throat. Gengar was still underground, too far from Archie to stop the throw.

Will Kyogre really undergo Primal Reversion?

Then the orb stopped in midair, as though an invisible hand had caught it. Everyone froze. Ethan recovered first and turned to Gardevoir. It had one hand outstretched, holding the orb in place with psychic power.

“Beautifully done! Bring it here!”

Archie's face changed. The orb flew over his head toward Ethan. He grabbed at it but could not reach its height.

Desperate, he pointed toward the cavern roof. “You three—attack that!”

The members followed his gesture. A huge stalactite hung above the center of the battlefield. They directed their Pokémon's attacks at it just as the orb passed underneath.

Several moves struck the stone. An explosion tore it free. The stalactite crashed down, burying the Blue Orb beneath it as smoke and dust blotted out the view.

That's bad!

Ethan and Archie both lunged toward the fallen stone. Pokémon on each side tried to stop the opposing Trainer, only to be intercepted by their counterparts. The cavern dissolved into chaos.

Both men reached the shattered stalactite almost together. But the Blue Orb lay somewhere among the rubble, and neither could spot it.
