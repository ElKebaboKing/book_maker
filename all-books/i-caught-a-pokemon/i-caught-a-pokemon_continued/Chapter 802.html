The black Rayquaza crashed into the lake. Mewtwo followed it under the surface. They fought through the water, churning it into chaos, then rose back into the sky. Water poured through breaks torn along the shore and flooded nearly half the surrounding forest.

They had been fighting for only seven or eight minutes, but Ethan could already judge the balance between them. Unfortunately, Rayquaza did not seem able to beat Mewtwo.

Its huge body brought no advantage. Against Pokémon of similar size, Rayquaza could use its bulk without giving ground. Against a human-shaped opponent like Mewtwo, that length became a burden. It could not guard its head and tail at once. Mewtwo was faster already, and Rayquaza's size offered even more openings to exploit.

More importantly, Mewtwo had overwhelming power and a formidable mind. It could move things at a distance, control the weather, shape objects with thought, turn back time, alter memories, and influence minds. It was not confined to trading one move for another. It found creative, unexpected ways to hurt Rayquaza.

Both Pokémon belonged among the world's strongest, yet Mewtwo looked more comfortable and held the advantage. Being called the strongest Pokémon created by human hands was no empty boast.

Ethan sighed. Psychic power was as outrageous as ever. Its uses went far beyond the cold figures in a Pokédex. In the real world, it often seemed stronger than anything else.

When Ethan caught the black Rayquaza, and later when Deoxys fought another Rayquaza in LaRousse City, Deoxys had at times suppressed its opponent despite having a weaker body. That alone showed what psychic power could accomplish. Mewtwo had a body to rival Rayquaza's as well.

After learning Rayquaza's style, Mewtwo drew fully on its own strength. It pressed its advantage with increasing ease.

That did not mean Rayquaza would fall quickly. For now Mewtwo was only ahead. To defeat Rayquaza, it would have to show still more.

Ethan, however, had not come here for a one-on-one battle. Having seen that Rayquaza alone could not presently beat Mewtwo, he threw his second Ultra Ball.

How had he built his reputation in the first place? He had always been good at overwhelming an opponent with numbers.

Blue light flashed, and Deoxys appeared beside him. Mewtwo noticed it immediately. It said nothing about facing two opponents at once. It had sensed both powerful Pokémon when Ethan entered the cave and had watched him bring out two Ultra Balls. It could hardly have believed he intended to use only one.

And Mewtwo's confidence came from its own strength. Let them both come.

Deoxys flew straight toward it. From inside its Ultra Ball, it had already used its abilities to watch the battle. It was eager to join in, and Ethan had now given it permission.

Its arrival changed the match at once. Deoxys was a Psychic-type Pokémon too, difficult for Mewtwo to handle even without Rayquaza waiting beside them. Under pressure from both, Mewtwo made more mistakes and took more hits. It had to fight seriously, and within moments it was on the defensive.

Yet Mewtwo did not retreat. It stood against two Legendary Pokémon at once, despite the turn in their favor.

All three clashed in the air. The battle grew fiercer, the weather more violent. Thick black clouds spread rapidly outward.

Kyogre and Groudon were not the only Pokémon whose battles could change the world around them. If Mewtwo, Rayquaza, and Deoxys fought without restraint, they too could cause enormous damage to the surrounding land. That was an important reason Ethan had chosen this remote place.

Fortunately, they all understood this was not a fight to the death. Each held back to some degree. Even so, they could not fully contain the energy spilling from their attacks.

Gengar rose from Ethan's shadow and hovered beside him. Watching the distant battle, it hesitated. “With this much going on, won't people notice?”

Gengar's fortunes were bound to Ethan's, and it thought about the problem from its Trainer's point of view. People discovering Deoxys and Rayquaza would matter little; Ethan had already caught them. If outsiders found Mewtwo, though, it would face endless pursuit and harassment. That would make Ethan's hopes of someday catching it much less certain.

Ethan shared the concern. “Don't worry. The battle will be over soon.”

“You're not planning to catch Mewtwo right now, are you?”

He shook his head. “Not yet. There still has to be an opening. I think it will come soon.”

Gengar wanted to know what he meant, but Ethan offered nothing more, so it kept the question to itself.

Overhead, the three Pokémon drifted closer to Ethan again. This time he did not retreat. He called to Deoxys from afar, “Let's raise the difficulty! Use Attack Forme!”

A human voice was small amid a clash like this, but Deoxys heard him. Its body shifted into Attack Forme.

At first Mewtwo did not seem concerned. Then they exchanged blows again, and Deoxys caught it unprepared. The alien-looking Pokémon had become a greater threat. Mewtwo's position worsened.

It gathered powerful spheres of energy in both hands. It drove Rayquaza away, only to be struck by Deoxys. Against either Pokémon alone, Mewtwo had no fear and believed it could win. Against the two together, even it struggled.

That was an achievement in itself. How many Pokémon could have held out against opponents of this caliber?

Ethan checked the time. Then, without warning, he recalled both of his Pokémon into their Ultra Balls.

Mewtwo was caught off guard. One moment it was deep in battle; the next, its opponents were gone. It hovered motionless.

“Why?”

It looked toward Ethan. If he had continued, he might truly have captured Mewtwo this time. Why stop now? What was he thinking?

Someone from Ethan's old world might have recognized the tactic. Ethan was drawing Mewtwo in by stepping back just when it expected him to press forward.

Of course he wanted to catch it. Mewtwo had been one of his favorite Pokémon even before he came to this world. But he did not merely want to possess it. He wanted Mewtwo to accept him willingly as its Trainer.

A Pokémon with Mewtwo's intelligence would not truly be bound by a Poké Ball alone. Unless it recognized Ethan of its own accord, he could never work with it as naturally as he did with his other partners. That was why he kept making these elaborate visits and withdrawing before a final capture.

Mewtwo did not know any of that. From its perspective, Ethan was growing stronger and more mysterious each time they met. It was also beginning to regard him more and more as an equal.

“No special reason,” Ethan said with a smile. “I told you I came to spar. We've done that, so it's time to stop.”

An interesting man. Mewtwo's eyes changed slightly. Its opinion of him rose again.

“Actually,” Ethan added, “I came to ask for your help with something.”

Perhaps Ethan's choice to stop had altered Mewtwo's view of him. It did not think to refuse at once. Instead it asked, curious, “You want my help? With those two Pokémon, what could you possibly be unable to do?”

It was a fair question. In this world, powerful Pokémon made a powerful Trainer. Someone of Ethan's standing could receive the finest welcome from organizations and officials alike. Money, influence, and admiration were well within his reach.

Mewtwo was clever enough to guess a moment later. “You want me to help you face some troublesome Pokémon.”

“Yes and no.” Ethan smiled mysteriously. “Don't worry. It won't be difficult for you.”

“Then couldn't those Pokémon of yours handle it easily?”

“That involves something private. Those two are strong, but they're also my hidden trump cards. There are situations where I can't let them appear. That's when I'd need your help.”

He did not wait for Mewtwo to agree. He patted Dragonite's shoulder, and it carried him away, leaving Mewtwo with his departing silhouette.

His voice reached it one last time. “Wait for word from me. That day will come soon!”

…

At dusk, while Misty waited anxiously, Dragonite brought Ethan back to the Pokémon Center where they were staying. She let out a long breath when she saw him.

She had not feared for his safety. Few Pokémon in the world could hurt him now. She had worried that something might delay him until he missed tomorrow's match.

“Where did you go, Brother Ethan?”

“Nowhere special. I looked around nearby.” Ethan did not tell her about Mewtwo. Instead he turned to May. “How is your practice going?”

“We've settled on the basic structure of my performance,” May said with a smile. “It still needs a few details. And now that I'm in Kanto, I'd like to catch some Pokémon from here.”

Kanto Pokémon. Ethan remembered the ones May had obtained here in the anime. If he wanted her to have the chance to follow that course, they would need to visit Professor Oak's laboratory first. Fortunately, it lay along their route and would not disrupt the plan.

“Brother Ethan,” Misty asked, “did you hear what happened near Cerulean City while you were out?”

“Cerulean City? What happened?”

“The news said there was extreme weather nearby. They think some Pokémon were fighting there!”

So that was all. It was the battle he had just watched. He had not expected it to make the news. Moving the fight away from Cerulean Cave had been wise. If it had happened near the city, crowds of Trainers might have rushed in. At this distance, no one had disturbed them.

“Who knows? Maybe a Legendary Pokémon appeared.”

Misty's memories of Mewtwo had been erased. Until she truly met it again, Ethan saw no reason to explain. His suggestion turned the girls' thoughts toward Legendary Pokémon. They began wondering aloud what could appear in Kanto besides the three Legendary birds.

They had not talked long before Misty remembered something else. “Oh, Brother Ethan! You haven't heard, have you? Tomorrow's battle between you and Tucker is all over Kanto!”
