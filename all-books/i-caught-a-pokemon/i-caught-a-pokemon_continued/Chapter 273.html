The match had reached its fifth round. Drake stood on the brink of defeat and sent out Electabuzz. Ethan answered by releasing his Crystal Onix.

Sunlight struck Onix's strange body, and it gleamed like cut crystal. Drake, the announcer, League staff, and spectators in the stadium and at home were all astonished by its beauty. Cameras turned toward it at once. Flashes snapped as photographers tried to capture every angle.

"Is that an Onix? What a beautiful color!"

"Could it be a Shiny Pokémon?"

"A Shiny Onix doesn't look like that!"

People all around Ash exclaimed over the sight. He, Misty, and Tracey had glimpsed the Crystal Onix during the battle with the Gyarados at Rind Island's Lake of Birth and Evolution. They had been too busy then to study it. Now, seeing it clearly, they understood why everyone was staring.

"Incredible..." The announcer swallowed. "Shiny Pokémon are rare, but this isn't one. Its body must have changed in a unique way. What a marvelous sight! Where did Ethan Vale catch it?"

He hesitated, remembering something. "I've heard a story from Sunburst Island. People there searched for years for a legendary Onix with a body that shone like crystal. Could this be the very Crystal Onix they were looking for?"

The name gave the audience an answer. Among those watching the broadcast, the glassworkers of Sunburst Island were particularly excited. They had dreamed of finding that Pokémon. They had thought it might only be a story, and now they had seen it with their own eyes. Ethan had found what they had sought for so long.

As the first surprise passed and the audience focused on the match again, they saw another difficult round ahead for Drake. Crystal Onix was a Ground- and Rock-type. Its Ground attacks would deal double damage to Electabuzz, and Electric-type moves would not affect it at all.

Many spectators already feared a fifth defeat. Their feelings had swung from absolute confidence in Drake to doubt, and now to expecting the worst.

Once the battle began, Electabuzz struggled exactly as they feared. Without its Electric-type moves, it relied on Normal-type attacks, which barely troubled Onix's sturdy defense. Electabuzz had to stay alert and dodge each of Onix's blows.

It did have Low Kick, a Fighting-type move that could hurt Crystal Onix and keep it from being helpless. But relying on one useful attack became harder once Ethan recognized it and guarded against it.

In the end, Crystal Onix used Rock Tomb to drive Electabuzz into a corner. With nowhere left to dodge, it took a Rock Throw and fell. The result had seemed inevitable, but the fifth win still brought a fresh wave of disbelief.

Five battles, five defeats for Drake. Would Ethan repeat his undefeated run at the Indigo League without losing even one Pokémon here?

Having seen his earlier championship, many Orange Islanders no longer felt that Drake's loss would disgrace them. Ethan had done this to strong opponents before. If he won, it would show how powerful he was, not prove the Orange League weak.

With Electabuzz down, everyone knew Drake had only one Pokémon left: his ace, Dragonite.

Drake recalled Electabuzz and looked across the field at Ethan. "You're remarkably strong. Of all the challengers I've faced—and even among the Trainers I know—you're the strongest."

Ethan rubbed his nose and shook his head. "I wouldn't call myself the strongest. I know plenty of people more capable than me. My Pokémon are the ones who carried me through these rounds."

After several exchanges with Drake, Ethan had a rough sense of his level. He judged the Orange League's strongest Trainer capable of reaching at least the final four at the Indigo League. Drake was no lightweight.

Drake took no comfort from the compliment. He reached to the Poké Ball at his chest and detached it from its chain.

"Dragonite, it's your turn."

The ball stirred twice in his hand, as though answering him. The bond between them was plain.

"Go, Dragonite!"

Drake hurled the ball skyward. Blue light flashed, and Dragonite appeared above the field.

"At last," the announcer said, looking up. There was little joy in his voice now. "It's been a very long time since I've seen Dragonite battle. Is it as strong as ever? Let's see whether it can bring Drake a chance to win."

Ethan took his eyes off Dragonite and recalled Crystal Onix. He drew the last Poké Ball he planned to use from his belt. He had sent out five different Pokémon in five rounds. None was badly tired, but he did not intend to use any of them again.

Drake had chosen a winged dragon. Ethan would answer with one of his own.

He enlarged the ball in his hand, weighed it briefly, and pressed its button. Fixing his gaze on Drake, he called, "This battle is yours. Go, Charizard!"
