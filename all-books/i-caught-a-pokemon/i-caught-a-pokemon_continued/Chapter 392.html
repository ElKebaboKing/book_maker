After leaving the forest, the two soon reached another fork in the road.

Two paths now lay before Ethan.

If they kept heading west along their current route for several kilometers, they would reach a protected area called National Park.

The park was a paradise for Bug-type Pokémon in the Johto region and home to many different species. It was said that the officials held a Bug-Catching Contest there every year around this time.

Contestants used Park Balls supplied by the park to catch Bug-type Pokémon. After battles and judging, the winner would not only get to keep the Pokémon they had caught, but also receive a fine prize from the park.

The generous rewards and the chance to obtain a Pokémon for free drew large crowds every year. Vendors also recognized the opportunity and came to set up stalls outside the park, selling small gifts and all kinds of food for a tidy profit.

Right now, the park was at its liveliest.

Unfortunately, Ethan had no Bug-type Pokémon he wanted to catch at the moment, so he had no intention of going there to look around. As a Grass-type Gym Leader, Erika preferred to keep a respectful distance from Bug-type Pokémon as well. After a brief discussion, the two turned north and followed the other road toward Ecruteak City.

...

Ecruteak City—a city steeped in history.

One of the oldest cities in northern Johto, Ecruteak stood within the forest between Mt. Mortar and the broad plains near Johto's western coast.

Known as a city of history and culture, it was not filled with skyscrapers and modern facilities like other cities. Traditional Japanese architecture defined its character instead.

The sun was setting as Ethan and Erika walked along a quiet street.

From the moment they entered the city, every street and building they saw possessed an old-fashioned elegance and a profound sense of history.

Rows of wooden houses with tiled roofs, soaring towers, stone bridges, and paved stone roads formed the city's prevailing scenery.

Although there was no festival or holiday, plenty of people still wandered leisurely through the streets in kimono and wooden sandals.

Seeing them, Ethan could not help glancing at Erika.

Beside them, clear water flowed quietly through a canal with a gentle murmur.

Erika was still dressed in the close-fitting kimono she wore for traveling. She blended in perfectly with the people around them, as though she were a local herself.

She doesn't look out of place at all...

Sensing his gaze, Erika turned toward him.

"What is it?"

"Nothing. I just feel that this city suits you very well."

Erika smiled softly but said nothing.

She liked it here too. The unhurried pace made the city feel exceptionally peaceful.

As they made their way deeper into the city center, Ethan and Erika took out their travel guide and read while they walked.

The guide explained everything clearly. The city's most famous landmarks were the Bell Tower and the Brass Tower, which respectively represented the places where Pokémon awakened and slept. They were also the tallest structures in Ecruteak. One stood in the east and the other in the west, facing each other across the city and forming part of its solemn character.

According to legend, both towers had been built seven hundred years ago to celebrate the friendship between humans and Pokémon. From their summits, visitors could enjoy a magnificent view of the entire city.

Johto folklore also claimed that Ho-Oh and Lugia had once lived atop the two towers!

Unfortunately, lightning struck the Brass Tower one hundred and fifty years ago, starting a fire that burned for three days and three nights. Although a downpour finally extinguished it, most of the tower had been destroyed, leaving only broken ruins at ground level.

The story that followed was even more widely known.

Three unidentified Pokémon perished in the blaze, only to be revived by Ho-Oh when it descended from the sky. This was the origin legend of Entei, Suicune, and Raikou!

Afterward, Ho-Oh and Lugia left Ecruteak City. For reasons unknown, the people never rebuilt the Brass Tower. Its ruins were preserved and gradually came to be known as the Burned Tower.

Of course, legends often contained exaggeration and artistic embellishment. They were not necessarily entirely true.

At the very least, Ethan remembered that more than one Suicune seemed to have appeared in this world.

Still, the mere mention of Ho-Oh made his temper flare.

That fellow Ho-Oh had stopped him from catching the three Legendary beasts not long ago!

Hmph. Ho-Oh, you'd better not let me run into you here!

We still have a score to settle!

Seeing Ethan lost in thought, Erika asked, "Where are we going now? Are you planning to challenge the Gym right away?"

Like the other major cities of the Pokémon world, Ecruteak City had an official Gym. Its Leader, Morty, specialized in Ghost-type Pokémon, and Trainers who defeated him received the Fog Badge.

But at this hour...

Ethan glanced at the setting sun and shook his head.

"It wouldn't be appropriate to visit the Gym now, and I'm getting hungry. Let's find somewhere to stay first."

Erika nodded. At the end of the road, they happened to find an inn.

As they checked in, the innkeeper heard their accents and smiled broadly. "You two arrived at exactly the right time!"

"Oh?" Erika looked at him. "Why do you say that?"

The innkeeper lowered his voice mysteriously. "The Kimono Sisters are performing onstage at the Ecruteak Dance Theater tonight! If you don't have other plans, you absolutely mustn't miss it!"

The Kimono Sisters?

Ethan rubbed his chin.

Having watched the anime, he naturally knew who the five girls were. If he remembered correctly, they were also called the Eevee Sisters.

They were five girls dressed in kimono who danced onstage with their Eevee beside them.

Erika, who had never heard of them, naturally looked puzzled.

She asked, "A stage performance? Are they famous?"

"What? You haven't heard of them?" Surprised by her question, the innkeeper scratched his cheek. "Ah, I forgot that you aren't from around here. How should I put it? The Kimono Sisters aren't only famous here. They're well known throughout Johto and are the stars of Ecruteak City!"

As he spoke, the innkeeper adopted a lovestruck expression and continued dreamily, "Five lovely, beautiful girls in their finest clothes, dancing beneath the stage lights... What a delightful sight! See them once, and I promise you won't be disappointed!"
