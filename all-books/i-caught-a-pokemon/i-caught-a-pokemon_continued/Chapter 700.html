“Ethan Vale?”

Maxie stared for a moment before he realized whom Courtney meant. That infuriating man who keeps ruining our operations?

Unlike Archie, Maxie usually worked behind the scenes. He had never met Ethan, but he knew the name well. It had appeared again and again in Team Magma's recent reports. Ethan had disrupted so many plans that the organization regarded him as its greatest threat.

Maxie knew how dangerous he was. He had simply never imagined he would appear here. Why is he everywhere?

“What's going on?” he demanded of Courtney. “Why is he here? You told me no one had come up the mountain to help her!”

“I don't know.” For once, the usually composed Courtney sounded shaken. “The patrols and lookouts didn't see him. I really don't know how he...” Her voice failed.

No one in Team Magma had dealt with Ethan more often than she had. She knew what his arrival meant. He had so many Legendary Pokémon, and after the battle involving Deoxys there were even rumors that he had caught it. Their operation might fail. They might struggle merely to get away unharmed.

Meanwhile, Ethan drove his Pokémon into Team Magma's line. The surprise attack had already spread confusion, and they pressed their advantage.

With Phoebe bearing the brunt of the enemy's attention, Ethan had not called on his Legendary Pokémon. He sent out only his usual team. Even so, they struck together like a clenched fist, too powerful for Team Magma's Pokémon to stop.

Misty was his one worry. She had sprung her own ambush from the other side, but her Pokémon were not as strong as his. The first rush had gone well. If the fight dragged on and Team Magma regrouped, she could become their easiest target. Joining her mattered more to Ethan than reinforcing Phoebe.

Misty's Water-type Pokémon had a favorable matchup, and the attacks from three directions kept Team Magma too confused to notice that her side was the weakest. She could not rely on that forever. Ethan ordered his Pokémon to push toward her.

As it turned out, he had worried too much. Team Magma's members might have faced Phoebe or an unfamiliar reinforcement with determination, but the man attacking them was Ethan Vale. Many had fought in the disastrous clash over Deoxys. The defeat was fresh, and the mere sight of him shook their courage. Some lost the will to battle at once.

Maxie saw his line falter and shouted, furious. “Don't be afraid! Don't retreat! Our ultimate dream is right in front of us. Once we have the Red Orb, Groudon will be ours! We cannot turn back now!”

His authority and conviction had their effect. The wavering members steadied. They were one step from realizing Team Magma's dream. They could not give up here.

Under Maxie's urging, they recovered their formation and held their ground. These elite members were strong, roughly the equal of ordinary Trainers. Working together, they put Phoebe under heavy pressure.

Maxie and Courtney sent out their Pokémon and joined the battle as well. Phoebe saw that Ethan's team was strong enough to ease some of the burden on hers. In fact, the moment he had appeared, much of Team Magma's attention had shifted from her to him.

She noticed the change immediately. I'm the Elite Four member. Why are they ignoring me to attack him?

Misty had said they had clashed with Team Magma many times. Perhaps their history ran deeper than Phoebe knew, and the members were eager to settle it.

Maxie seldom joined operations himself, but a leader's Pokémon were hardly weak. He had brought out five Houndoom. Perhaps he wanted to exploit their advantage against Phoebe's Ghost types; perhaps he simply meant to bring her down and seize the orb at once. Unlike Courtney and most of the members, Maxie aimed his team at Phoebe.

The black, hellhound-like Pokémon worked together and forced her backward. She was one of the Elite Four, but five of them, with other Team Magma Pokémon nearby, were difficult to handle.

The shrine stood behind her. Her grandparents had protected its treasure, and she understood what losing it would mean. She clenched her teeth and refused to give ground. Her title had been earned young because of her exceptional strength and talent, not by chance. Under her commands, her Ghost-type Pokémon withstood the unfavorable matchup and kept the shrine secure.

Then Misty arrived to support her.

Of the three defenders, Misty was the least powerful, but she had met the lightest resistance. Most of Team Magma was focused on Ethan and Phoebe. Apart from a few members directly in her path, almost no one paid attention to her, and she made use of the opening.

In a straightforward battle, she would have struggled against even one or two of these elite members, let alone a crowd. She had come from a Gym and might someday lead the Cerulean Gym, but that future was still ahead of her. In the confusion of this battle, she could choose opponents one at a time.

Traveling with Ethan had made her stronger. She had also witnessed enough large battles to keep her head now, answering each threat without panic. That composure helped her reach Phoebe so quickly.

The fighting had torn apart the beautiful scenery around the shrine. Maxie watched Phoebe hold out long enough for Misty to join her, and his earlier excitement cooled. He studied the field again. The sides were still locked together, but the longer the battle continued, the worse his position would become.

Ethan had not used any of the Legendary Pokémon mentioned so often in Team Magma's reports. He was plainly holding back, while Maxie's force was already fighting at full strength. A direct assault would not take the Red Orb now.

It was time to see whether the plan he had arranged before the battle would work. He was glad he had acted on his misgivings.

Maxie ordered a fighting withdrawal. It was no panicked rout. His people gave ground slowly, holding their formation as they moved toward the summit entrance. The battle would continue, drawing Phoebe's and Ethan's Pokémon farther from the stone shrine. That would give Tabitha his opportunity.

The maneuver went smoothly. The fight shifted toward the entrance. Phoebe and Misty noticed the change, but assumed Team Magma was finally being forced to retreat.

Ethan was less certain. Maxie's withdrawal was too orderly. Ethan could see the fight turning against Team Magma. If Maxie saw it too, why did he neither make one last desperate attack nor break away? He seemed content to prolong a fight he could not win.

Ethan was quick to notice small changes on a battlefield, and the movement felt wrong. He did not know the plan, but he would not follow Maxie's lead. As Misty and Phoebe drifted farther from the shrine, he shouted, “Don't chase them! It could be a trap!”

Misty and Phoebe heard only a warning. Maxie felt a jolt of fear. Has he seen through it?

He had heard stories about Ethan, but meeting him made the threat real. Ethan had not even revealed the Legendary Pokémon from the reports, yet his alertness alone marked him as a dangerous opponent. No wonder Courtney and Team Aqua had failed against him.

To Maxie's relief, the orange-haired girl stopped advancing, but Phoebe pressed on. His plan could still work. It needed only one more push.

He nodded to several nearby members. Their Pokémon immediately attacked Phoebe's grandparents' cabin. Flames caught the garden fence and began to spread toward the house.

“You monsters!” Phoebe cried.

She had grown up there. It was her grandparents' home. The fire burning through their garden kindled her own anger. She would not let the people who had set it escape. She drove her Pokémon forward after them.

Ethan wanted to stop her, but they were separated by the battle. By the time he saw the flames, Phoebe was almost out of sight.

Why does the League appoint children to the Elite Four? he thought with a sigh. She may be strong, but she needs more experience. Couldn't they have waited until she was fifteen or sixteen? Phoebe had charged straight into an obvious attempt to lure her away from the shrine.

He could not abandon her now. He had accepted Anabel's request, and if Phoebe came to harm, he would have failed her. But leaving the shrine unguarded was no better.

Ethan called Gengar over. “Stay here and guard it.” Then he shouted to Misty, “You stay too. Put out the fire first, then protect the orb. I'm going after Phoebe!”

“I understand. Leave this to me,” Misty said. “Be careful!”

It was not a perfect defense, but it kept the orb protected. Ordinary Pokémon would have trouble getting past Gengar, and Misty would be there as well.

Unlike Phoebe, Ethan had no objection to moving the orb if the situation demanded it. Should anyone break through, Gengar could simply take it underground. Team Magma could dig up the whole mountain and still fail to find it.

He would leave them no opening at all.
