"I see," Bugsy said. "You used Pupitar's unusual way of moving to keep up with Spinarak's silk swings. Clever!"

In midair, Pupitar slammed into Spinarak and knocked it out of its swing and onto the ground!

Bang! Spinarak landed hard. Its six legs helped it scramble up at once and flee beyond Pupitar's reach before its opponent could follow up.

Thump...

Pupitar landed in front of it. The change in Pokémon had raised the intensity of the battle, and the situation had reversed in an instant!

That did not mean Eevee was weak. Spinarak's way of moving among the branches was almost unfair, and Eevee had too few ways to attack it there.

In a straightforward fight on the ground, Eevee might well have won.

With Pupitar on the field, however, things were different.

Bugsy saw the shift and stopped trying to look unfazed. "Spinarak, restrict its movement!" he called.

Spinarak puffed up its mouth. Ethan knew how sticky its silk was. If Pupitar were bound in it, breaking free would be difficult. He would not let Bugsy have his way.

"Use Sandstorm!" Ethan ordered.

As Spinarak spat out silk, yellow sand swept across the entire field. At first, Pupitar was still faintly visible through the dust. The storm thickened, and in the blink of an eye it vanished from sight!

Spinarak was caught in the storm too.

It fired a few strands of silk toward the place where Pupitar had last been. As the sand grew denser, it lost all sense of direction and could no longer find its target!

Countless tiny stones mixed with the sand. Each sting was mild, but the constant impacts hurt Spinarak and made movement difficult.

Spiders generally had no eyelids to close. Tears kept their eyes moist, but what good were tears in a sandstorm?

Dust coated Spinarak's eyes at once. It rubbed them with its front legs, trying to clear away the sand that kept blowing in.

Each time it succeeded, fresh dust stuck to them less than a second later. The cycle left it unable to do anything else.

Bugsy could not see inside the storm either, but he knew Spinarak was at a severe disadvantage. "Spinarak! Watch out for Pupitar's surprise attacks!" he shouted anxiously.

The wind and sand broke up his words before they reached it.

Spinarak lowered its head and shielded its eyes with its front legs, easing the worst of the dust. It turned slowly, sensing for any sign of Pupitar.

And it found something!

A silver shape was creeping toward it through the yellow haze.

Spinarak had no time to think. It opened its mouth and fired a barrage of Pin Missile at the shape!

In the confusion, it could not tell whether it had hit. Then another silver shape approached from a different direction.

This time, Spinarak fired a Poison Sting. There was a faint metallic clink, and the needle spun away.

Did it miss?

Or did something deflect it?

The Sandstorm began to weaken, and the air slowly cleared. When dust stopped blowing into its eyes, Spinarak looked up. The object it had struck with Poison Sting was a huge rock!

Where did that come from?

Before it could think further, wind rushed behind it. Spinarak turned just in time to see something silver, like an iron mask, flying closer and filling its vision...

It was Pupitar!

Unable to dodge, Spinarak took the impact and was sent flying!

...

In the Trainer's area, Bugsy frowned uneasily. He stared into the Sandstorm and, as the wind finally began to die down, searched the middle of the battlefield.

Before he found his Pokémon, Spinarak flew out of the storm, landed beyond the field, and fainted!

What?

What happened!? How did Spinarak lose during one Sandstorm?

Several large rocks now lay on the field. Bugsy could guess that Pupitar had used a Rock-type move in the storm, but he had no idea what had happened in detail.

He glanced at Ethan across the field. The challenger looked calm, showing little excitement at Pupitar's victory.

Bugsy did not know how the battle had ended, but Spinarak had lost. He took out its Poké Ball and recalled it.

"Unlike your Eevee, this Pupitar is strong. But that's all right. Meet my second Pokémon!"

He threw another Poké Ball.

A green Metapod appeared before Ethan.

Metapod!?

What surprised Ethan was the pairing. Pupitar was in a cocoon-like stage, and Metapod had not completed its evolution either. Neither had hands or feet. Facing each other across the field, they made a comical sight.

Did Bugsy send it out just to make fun of my Pupitar?

As though sensing Ethan's doubt, Bugsy smiled confidently. "Don't underestimate my Metapod. It's a proud warrior among Bug-type Pokémon!"

First he had called Spinarak a silent warrior, and now Metapod was a proud one.

What ordinary person gave their Pokémon titles like that?

Bugsy might have become a Gym Leader at a young age, but he could still sound like a boy caught up in his own grand fantasies!

Ethan looked at the green cocoon and kept Pupitar in the battle.

Metapod, Caterpie's evolved form, was known for the hard shell that protected its soft body while it changed inside.

With two Pokémon in similar stages facing each other, Ethan wanted to see which shell was tougher.

Bugsy's Metapod, or his Pupitar!
