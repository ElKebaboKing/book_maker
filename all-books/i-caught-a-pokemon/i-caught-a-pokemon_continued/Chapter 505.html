Strengthened by Swords Dance, the two Scyther clashed again, fighting harder than before.

They shared the same type and used much the same moves. For a while, neither could gain a clear advantage.

They battled from the ground into the air and back again. Both used Fury Cutter so often that Ethan heard little but the ring of crossing blades, and his eyes could hardly keep up with their movements.

Perhaps worried that the fight was dragging on, Bugsy grew impatient. He repeatedly ordered Quick Attack followed by X-Scissor, looking for an opening through speed.

Ethan would not give him one. Double Team paired with Agility often let Scar evade and counterattack.

Little by little, Bugsy's Scyther fell behind.

Scar finally caught a gap in its opponent's offense and landed Night Slash, winning the battle.

Thud...

Bugsy's Scyther fell straight to the ground and fainted. Ethan's Scyther was still standing, but all its rapid movement and intense fighting had drained its stamina. Sweat covered its body.

The referee raised the colored flag. "Scyther is unable to battle! The challenger wins the Gym match!"

Bugsy recalled his Scyther and walked over to Ethan with a thoughtful expression.

"I lost. Your Scyther really is remarkable. I never expected to lose to another Bug-type Pokémon when I'm a Bug-type Trainer. I still have a lot to learn."

Scar returned to Ethan, who smiled and stroked its scarred forehead. "Before I caught this Scyther, it led its group. It was strong even among Scyther. And honestly, yours is strong too. If I'd slipped up, I could have been the one to lose."

"So that's how it is..."

Bugsy understood, but his curiosity about the island of Bug-type Pokémon Ethan had mentioned only grew.

I'll have to visit it on my next training trip!

Next month... no, next week!

After a little more conversation, Bugsy took a badge from his pocket and placed it in Ethan's hand.

"This is the Hive Badge, proof that you've defeated me. Take good care of it."

Ethan thanked him. Erika came to his side, and they examined the red-and-black badge in his palm. It looked like a ladybug, much like the ones Ethan had seen on Earth.

With this badge, Ethan needed only one more to enter the Silver Conference!

They chatted a little longer, then said goodbye and left.

After leaving the Azalea Gym, they did not depart town immediately. They found an inn with a hot spring and spent time soaking, easing their fatigue and restoring their strength.

They had walked so many mountain paths on the round trip to Charicific Valley that going on without a rest might have caused trouble.

After a night's recovery, both woke refreshed. The next morning, they set out for the city of Ethan's final Gym challenge!

...

Goldenrod City was a large, prosperous metropolis.

It stood on the coast in west-central Johto. As the region's center of finance, technology, and politics, it was the only international city on the shared Johto and Kanto landmass that could rival Saffron City.

Even the names Goldenrod and Saffron brought wealth to mind. People saw money and opportunity everywhere in both cities, a measure of how prosperous they were.

After a day and a half of travel from Azalea Town, Ethan and Erika finally arrived.

Their destination was the Goldenrod Gym.

Goldenrod City's official Gym was led by Whitney, who specialized in Normal-type Pokémon. Defeating her would earn Ethan the Plain Badge.

They walked through streets lined with tall buildings and busy traffic. Even the people dressed more fashionably here.

For a moment, Ethan could almost forget he was in the Pokémon world. It felt like being back in a great city on Earth.

In any world, people were busy trying to make a living...

Erika noticed him looking around and laughed softly. "Now that you've seen both Saffron and Goldenrod, which do you think is more prosperous?"

Trying to test me, are you?

"They're about the same," Ethan said after thinking it over. "Once cities reach this size, their main differences are the landmarks. One is on the coast and the other isn't."

Erika nodded. Once cities reached a certain scale and level of technology, they did begin to look much alike.

They followed the streets to the Goldenrod Gym.

It stood in the city center and covered an enormous area. From outside, it looked like a large sports arena.

Without the Pokémon League symbol above the entrance, Ethan might have mistaken the imposing building for a football stadium!

But at the entrance, a staff member told them Whitney had just gone out and was not at the Gym.

Ethan was disappointed.

He had had poor luck with Gym Leaders lately. Bugsy's absence had delayed him for three days, and now Whitney was away too.

Had his journey been going so smoothly that fate had decided to make things harder?

Fortunately, the staff member added that Whitney was only wandering around the city. Even if she did not return that day, she would certainly be at the Gym tomorrow. Ethan felt a little better.

Outside, Ethan glanced at the unfamiliar streets and turned to Erika. "It's only noon. Let's find somewhere to stay, then come back later."

"All right." Erika smiled. "Since we have free time and don't often come to Goldenrod, will you go shopping with me?"

Ethan looked at her and felt a pang of guilt.

She had traveled from place to place with him all this time without a complaint. How many people would do that for someone? He ought to make time for her.

"Of course. Where would you like to go?"

Erika thought for a moment. "I've heard the department store here is famous. Let's start there!"
