Blastoise had once again defeated two opponents by itself, and its outstanding performance successfully carried Ethan into the final.

The loser left the field in dejection, while the victor received a thunderous ovation from the spectators. Misty's voice rose above all the others, loud enough for Ethan to hear clearly even from some distance away.

When he looked toward her, he saw her fair, slender arm waving excitedly above the crowd as she shouted herself nearly hoarse!

But his attention was soon drawn to Erika beside her, gazing at him with eyes as gentle as water.

Their eyes met, and for some reason, both looked away at the same time.

They could each clearly sense that the longer they traveled together and the more time they spent at each other's side, the more a different sort of emotion wound itself between them...

Ethan gently shook his head, gathered his thoughts, and recalled Blastoise into its Poké Ball.

He already had one foot on the trophy. All he needed to do now was defeat the winner between Trinity and the female Trainer facing her, and the Whirl Cup championship would be his.

Still, despite winning, Blastoise did not look particularly well.

The long series of battles since the preliminary round had left it covered in wounds.

That could not be helped. The injuries had accumulated across many matches, especially during Feraligatr's frenzied assault just now. Blastoise had blocked most of those attacks, but they had still left far too many marks across its body.

The final would take place that afternoon. Judging by Blastoise's condition, even emergency treatment at the Pokémon Center might not restore it to normal in time.

Will I really have to use Gyarados in the final?

After leaving the arena, Ethan met Erika and the others outside, and the group immediately hurried to the Pokémon Center.

When he mentioned his concerns about the final along the way, Erika, Ash, and Brock all looked eager instead.

If Blastoise could not compete, so much the better!

They truly wanted to see Ethan's red Gyarados battle. All three hoped to witness its strength for themselves and discover how far it had developed!

Their reaction left Ethan able to do nothing but smile helplessly.

These people certainly enjoyed the excitement when they were not the ones in trouble!

Even so, Ethan knew perfectly well that if Blastoise continued fighting, it probably could not endure two complete battles the way it had before.

Sigh. It looks like I have no choice but to prepare to send out the red Gyarados...

Once they reached the Pokémon Center, Ethan approached the front desk and handed Blastoise's Poké Ball to Nurse Joy.

He watched her enter the treatment room. While they waited, the group sat in the lobby and watched Trinity's match on a television mounted on the wall.

Just as in her battle with Misty, Trinity used two different Pokémon in the two rounds. She won cleanly by the same two-to-zero score.

The match made it clear that a real gap in strength separated Trinity from her opponent.

Ethan smiled. "It looks like I'll be facing Trinity next. When she first approached us, I could tell she was strong. I never expected her to actually fight her way all the way into the final!"

Erika nodded and analyzed the matchup. "Judging by their strength and their performances in the tournament, there isn't much difference between Trinity and Zhe. But her Chinchou has a major advantage on this kind of Water-type battlefield, especially against other Water-type Pokémon. You need to be very careful!"

"I understand. I'll watch out for it."

At that moment, Misty suddenly leaned closer and whispered sweetly into Ethan's ear, "Ethan, Miss Trinity was the one who eliminated me. You have to avenge me this time, okay?"

Before Ethan could answer, Ash curled his lip from the side.

"Come on, Misty. You lost to Miss Trinity, so you should be the one to settle the score. Why ask someone else to avenge you?"

Slow-witted Ash had clearly failed to understand. Misty might have said that she wanted Ethan to avenge her, but her expression and movements showed that she was really acting spoiled with him.

Ash's interruption shattered the mood and immediately angered Misty. She turned and glared at him, all traces of the gentle, affectionate girl she had been beside Ethan disappearing!

"None of your business! Hmph! Don't forget that you're the loser I defeated in the round of sixteen. If you're unhappy about it, remember to come get revenge on me next time!"

"Darn it, Misty!"

Her words struck a sore spot and infuriated Ash, but his defeat was a fact, leaving him without the confidence to argue.

"Just you wait! The next time there's a tournament like this, I'll definitely beat you!"

Misty raised her chin and dismissed the threat with disdain. "Got it. I'll be looking forward to it!"

With Ash and Misty present, the atmosphere was destined never to become dull.

While the two continued bickering, everyone ate lunch in the Pokémon Center's restaurant. They had barely set down their bowls and utensils when Nurse Joy's voice sounded over the announcement system.

"Mr. Ethan Vale, please come to the front desk. Your Pokémon has finished recovering."

The announcement played several times. By the third repetition, Ethan and the others had already reached the front desk.

When she saw him, Nurse Joy presented a tray and handed Blastoise's Poké Ball back to him.

"Mr. Ethan Vale, your Blastoise's treatment is complete. However, I could only heal its wounds. Battling for several days in a row has left it mentally exhausted, so I recommend giving it a longer rest."

"I understand. Thank you for the warning."

Ethan accepted the Poké Ball and sighed softly to himself.

It appeared that sending Gyarados into battle truly was unavoidable. He could only hope the next match would not leave Trinity with any lasting psychological trauma...

After what had happened with Aya, Ethan was reluctant to offend another female Trainer in the same fashion.

...

At half past one that afternoon, Ethan parted from Erika and the others and entered the competitors' waiting room alone.

He spotted Trinity the moment he entered.

She was sitting on a sofa with her eyes closed. At the sound of approaching footsteps, she slowly opened them.

"You're here?"

Meeting his opponent before the match felt rather awkward. They were hardly familiar with each other, and in a short while, Ethan would probably have to defeat her in a fairly brutal manner.

All he could do was nod politely. Inwardly, he complained about how unreliable the organizers were. If this had been the Silver Conference or another major tournament, the competitors would certainly have been placed in separate waiting rooms. They would never have been allowed to meet beforehand like this!

Fortunately, Trinity paid no attention to his reserved attitude. She rose, walked over to him, and offered a friendly smile.

"According to our earlier agreement, I've now defeated Misty and successfully reached the final. It seems we're destined to decide the winner on the battlefield!"

Ethan had gone from being the challenger to the person others sought to challenge. That proved both the growth of his strength and the results of his journey!

There was nothing wrong with becoming someone else's goal. The more people challenged him, the stronger he would become!

But...

Ethan smiled bitterly to himself.

The friendlier Trinity acted now, the guiltier he would feel once the battle was over.

Sigh. You have no idea what's about to happen. I really hope you can remain this calm after the match...
