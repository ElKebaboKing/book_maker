Quackenpoker finished checking his equipment and joined Ethan and the others. He frowned at the battle, worry plain on his face.

"How are your instruments?" Ethan asked. "Did anything break?"

"They're all right. A few scratches, nothing serious." Quackenpoker adjusted his glasses. "The Gyarados aren't angry by choice. Something outside their control provoked them. We have to stop this rampage without hurting them."

"We can't settle this peacefully now." Ethan looked at him. For all his years, Quackenpoker could be astonishingly naïve. "The only way to calm them is to defeat them. We can't hesitate and hope for some other solution."

Quackenpoker's face flushed. As a Pokémon Watcher, he naturally wanted to protect the Pokémon. Being contradicted to his face by a younger man stung, too. He had known even as he spoke that his wish was unrealistic, but he had wanted to try.

"Even so—"

"There is no 'even so,'" Ethan cut in, his voice hard. "If we hesitate, they'll do more damage. We have to stop them now. Mr. Quackenpoker, battling isn't your field. Please stay out of the way."

The rebuke was harsh. Quackenpoker opened his mouth, then sighed and quietly stepped aside.

The fight remained deadlocked. Ethan had expected the trouble to end once Team Rocket was defeated and the Magikarp were freed, just as Ash and the others had. None of them had anticipated this.

He needed a way to break the stalemate. Ethan reached into the pouch at his right hip. Besides three Great Balls, it held two ordinary Poké Balls. One contained Dratini. With so little battle experience, Dratini did not belong in a fight this chaotic.

As for the other...

He drew out the ball, weighed it in his hand, then threw it forward. "Go, Gyarados!"

A flash of blue light filled the crater, now flooded with lake water, and his Gyarados appeared there.

Ethan had kept it back for two reasons. With so many wild Gyarados in the battle, he feared Ash's and the others' Pokémon might mistake his for an enemy. He also worried that the sight of so many of its own kind might send it into a rage and make it attack indiscriminately. Now he could no longer afford to hold it back.

"Gyarados, snap them out of it!"

"Rrrroar!"

Gyarados lifted its head and roared. The sheer force of its voice eclipsed the cries of the wild Gyarados and seemed to roll across all of Rind Island. The battlefield fell still. Ash's Pokémon and the enraged Gyarados alike felt the pressure of it. Timid ones, including Tracey's Marill and Misty's Goldeen, startled and turned to look.

The darkest part of the night had passed. In the faint light of approaching dawn, Ethan's Gyarados rose from the water amid drifting mist, an imposing figure.

It looked much like the wild Gyarados, but appearances were misleading. It had fought countless battles, including intense matches in the Indigo League. Its strength and presence were on another level from these newly evolved Pokémon.

Once it took in the scene, it showed no hesitation over fighting its own kind. It opened its huge jaws and bit into one wild Gyarados's long neck. Sharp teeth pierced the skin. The other Gyarados cried out in pain and turned instinctively to bite back.

Ethan's Gyarados still had its wits. It released its grip and leaned away, leaving the bite to close on empty air. Then it twisted and slammed its great tail across its opponent's face.

Bang!

The blow rang out across the lake. The wild Gyarados spun away. Ethan's partner was already swimming toward a second one. It struck with the force of a charging beast and knocked that Gyarados down too.

Even without losing its temper, Ethan's Gyarados was fiercer than the wild ones. It tore through them like a wolf among sheep. None could match it. A second fell, then a third.

As the wild Gyarados dwindled, the other Pokémon sensed that the battle had changed. Almost at once, they stopped attacking and backed away, leaving the field to Ethan's Gyarados and its own kind.

Ash and the others could see it was using no elaborate tactics. It was fighting on instinct and unleashing its strength.

"Ethan's Gyarados is incredible," Ash murmured. To him, it looked less like a battle than a merciless struggle.

"Of course it is!" Misty was startled by its ferocity too, but her eyes shone with admiration. "It helped Ethan win the Indigo League championship!"

Quackenpoker could hold back no longer. Forgetting the earlier rebuke, he came up to Ethan. "Mr. Vale, your Gyarados is being too cruel. Please recall it!"

Even Ethan had not expected this display. His Gyarados's growth had been astonishing. The signs had been there during the Indigo League, but seeing what it could do now still took him by surprise.

He considered Quackenpoker's anxious plea for a moment, then left Gyarados in the water. At this point, victory was only a matter of time. He felt sorry for the wild Gyarados, but this was also a chance to see just how far his own had grown.
