After passing the billboard, Ethan and Sabrina reached the stadium entrance.

Calling it a stadium was generous. It was a square grandstand built over the sea, large enough for four or five hundred spectators, with a very deep pool at its center.

Apart from stone seats and several large spotlights, the stands were empty. Judging by the stadium's height and the location of its entrance, however, the building likely extended underwater.

A boy of seventeen or eighteen sat idly behind a table. He wore a red-and-white T-shirt and a straw hat.

When Ethan and Sabrina approached, he hurriedly shed his sleepy expression and smiled at them.

"From your clothes, I'd guess you're Trainers. Did you come to watch the synchronized swimming show, or are you here to challenge the Gym?"

Ethan raised an eyebrow and carefully studied both the boy and the supposed Gym behind him.

"We're only passing through. We saw a building here and came over out of curiosity. But... this is a Pokémon Gym?"

"Of course!" The boy nodded. "It's both a performance stage and a battle Gym. I'm Dorian, Leader of the Coastline Gym!"

Dorian? The Coastline Gym?

Ethan and Sabrina exchanged a look. The same confusion appeared in both their eyes. Neither had ever heard of such a Gym in Johto.

Dorian guessed what they were thinking and quickly explained, "It's normal that you haven't heard of us. This is only my private Gym!"

Ethan gave a quiet sound of understanding.

"Private Gym" was little more than a flattering name. Like the Gym built by Damian, the Beast Tamer Ethan had met in Kanto, this one was neither known nor recognized by the Pokémon League.

Before Ethan could say anything else, Dorian stood and gestured for them to enter.

"You've arrived at just the right time. The show will begin soon. Please take your seats!"

Without waiting for Ethan's answer, he hurried into the building and left Ethan and Sabrina standing there in bewilderment.

He had not charged them admission, so the show appeared to be free.

Still...

Dorian had rushed away without giving them any chance to refuse, almost as though afraid Ethan might turn around and leave.

Could this really be some kind of tourist trap?

Ethan scratched his head.

Perhaps once the show ended, the older boy would suddenly change his expression while seven or eight burly men jumped out and demanded tens of thousands in ticket fees.

But never mind.

They had nothing else to do, so they could treat it as entertainment after lunch. With their strength, neither of them feared danger, fraud, or an attempt to extort money.

If anyone tried, Ethan's Pokémon would show them just how beautiful the world could be.

Ethan and Sabrina entered the stadium hand in hand and sat on one of the stone tiers.

The breeze stirred Sabrina's dark hair. Several strands brushed Ethan's face and tickled his skin.

Before long, energetic music sounded above the stadium. The calm pool began to churn. A moment later, Qwilfish, Mantine, and Lanturn leaped from the water, followed by a pair of rather thick legs rising above the surface.

It was a conventional opening pose.

Compared with the slender, pale legs of female dancers, however, this muscular pair looked distinctly out of place.

Sure enough, after completing several leg movements, the performer turned underwater. A head wearing diving goggles rose to the surface.

It was Dorian.

Ethan stared in silence.

So this is a man's synchronized swimming show...

Ethan had limited experience and had not watched many aquatic ballets. He knew men took part in the profession, but he still preferred watching women perform. Beautiful faces, graceful figures, and elegant movements all made for a far more pleasing sight.

With a boy performing instead, the whole thing felt strange to him.

The show continued. Dorian moved gracefully between the surface and the depths, interacting constantly with his three Pokémon.

He gave a vivid and committed performance. His coordination with the Pokémon was excellent, clearly the result of careful rehearsal and tremendous effort.

Dorian and his Pokémon were all working hard, and that dedication deserved recognition.

Even so, Ethan still preferred the Cerulean sisters' water ballet.

If he ever encountered another show with a male performer, he would stay well away and never watch it.

It was not that he disrespected male synchronized swimmers. He simply could not appreciate watching a man twist and turn through the water.

More than ten minutes later, the performance ended. Dorian surfaced with his three Pokémon and bowed to Ethan and Sabrina. Ethan responded with gentle applause.

Dorian and the Pokémon submerged again. A short while later, he appeared in the stands and approached Ethan while drying his wet hair with a towel.

He asked expectantly, "What did you think of the show?"

"It was excellent. I could tell you put a great deal of effort into it."

Sincere happiness appeared on Dorian's face at the praise from his audience. His reaction made it clear that he truly loved synchronized swimming.

Ethan looked around in surprise. "Do you run this entire stadium by yourself? Doesn't anyone else help you manage things?"

"I don't need anyone else." Dorian laughed. "My Pokémon and I are enough!"

"You could easily move your performances to a city. You'd have a much larger audience and would definitely become popular."

"I don't plan to do that yet." Dorian shook his head. "I want to build more stage experience here before performing in a big city. By the way, now that the show is over, would you like to challenge my Gym?"

Ethan had no reason to refuse. "Sure. Since this is a Gym, I couldn't simply pass it by."

Following Dorian, Ethan and Sabrina went through the stadium entrance, descended a staircase, and reached the underwater section.

Compared with the simple stands above, the floor here was covered in soft red carpet. Bright lights shone from the ceiling, green plants decorated the corners, and the luxurious interior gleamed. Huge glass windows revealed the underwater world outside, making the place resemble an aquarium.

The sight made one thing clear: Dorian's family had to be wealthy. A facility like this, offering performances without even charging admission, was far beyond what one young man could establish alone.
