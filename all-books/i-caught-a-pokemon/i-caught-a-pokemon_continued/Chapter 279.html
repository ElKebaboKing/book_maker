After leaving Pummelo Stadium and walking down the hill, Ethan took Ash and the others to the guesthouse where he was staying.

It was already past four in the afternoon. The spectators were gradually dispersing, but plenty still lingered in the streets, reliving and discussing the match. To avoid being recognized, Ethan pulled on a hat and glasses and covered himself up again.

That was the price of attracting so much attention. If he wanted to be left alone, he had little choice.

Once the group entered the guesthouse courtyard, Ethan spent generously on a table full of food and drinks from the landlady.

The most important thing after winning a championship was to celebrate, and what better reward could there be than a lavish meal?

By evening, it was dark outside. Ethan and the others let the larger Pokémon out into the courtyard and prepared food for them separately. The smaller Pokémon joined their Trainers around the low table on the tatami mats.

Under the bright lights, the room was packed and wonderfully lively.

The landlady had evidently seen Ethan win on television. Delighted to have such a famous guest staying with her, she brought out some complimentary sushi and a few bottles of sake.

Dish after dish covered the table, each giving off a tempting aroma. With everything ready, it was time to eat and drink to their hearts' content.

"Cheers, everyone!"

They clinked their cups and drank. Apart from Ethan and Sabrina, everyone stuck out their tongues afterward. Ash and Misty looked especially uncomfortable, and even Tracey's brow creased. None of them cared for the taste of alcohol.

"Cough... It's so strong!" Ash clutched his stomach, looking miserable. It felt as if every blood vessel in his body were on fire.

"Honestly, you three..." Ethan laughed at their faces. "This sake is already pretty weak. If you aren't used to it, why not just drink juice? You insisted on trying it, and now none of you can handle it!"

"What's wrong with that?" Misty said, ignoring his teasing tone. "We wanted to try it because we're not used to it. Besides, today is worth a drink!"

Everyone knew one another well, so the celebration was in full swing as soon as dinner began.

Ash ate as he always did, wolfing food down as if he had not had a meal in years and giving no thought to appearances. Beside him, Sabrina took small bites and chewed slowly, every bit the lady.

Gengar pulled faces and put on a show to entertain them. Tracey told funny little stories in a low voice from the other side of the table, while the Pokémon laughed and played. The sake even emboldened Ethan to tease Misty a few times, making her blush. For a while, laughter filled every corner of the room.

Halfway through dinner, with food in their stomachs, everyone finally slowed down.

"Oh, Ash," Ethan said, "if you want to challenge for the Winner's Cup too, don't forget to register tomorrow. The rules here say you can't challenge Drake without arranging it in advance."

Ash gnawed on a chicken leg. "I know," he said through a mouthful. "I'll go tomorrow!"

A mischievous look came into Ethan's eyes. "I've already spoken to the Supreme Gym Leader about you. I made sure he knows who you are, so he'll give you a proper welcome. All you have to do is enjoy the battle!"

"Oh, really?" Ash had no idea Ethan had set another trap for the sake of his growth. He thanked him with an innocent smile. "Thanks, Ethan! I'll work twice as hard!"

From across the table, Misty asked, "Ethan, what are you going to do next?"

"Head for the next region, of course," Ethan answered without hesitation.

"The next region?" Ash's eyes lit up. "Where are you going?"

"Probably Johto, to compete in the Silver Conference. It's the nearest one."

"Huh? You're not staying to watch my match?"

"I don't think so." Ethan shook his head. "I believe you can win. Besides, you're catching up to me too quickly. You're improving so fast that I need to leave ahead of you if I want to keep my lead!"

The unexpected vote of confidence lit a fire in Ash's heart. He was deeply touched.

After dinner, Ethan borrowed a karaoke machine from the landlady. Sabrina went off to enjoy the hot spring on her own, while the others sang enthusiastically until late.

They kept singing past eleven. Even after Ethan washed up, he was still too excited to settle down. He spent the rest of the night with Sabrina, keeping them both awake until the early hours.

The next morning, Ash had to leave early to register for his challenge.

Before going, he said his final goodbye to Ethan. "After I finish the Winner's Cup, I'm going back to Pallet Town to give the GS Ball to Professor Oak. Once that's done, I'll come find you in Johto!"

"Then good luck with your challenge. See you next time." Ethan rubbed his eyes, still half asleep. "And say hello to your mom and Professor Oak for me!"

Ash and Tracey left. Misty, as usual, kept turning back, reluctant to go.

Ethan watched until they disappeared, then returned to his room for another nap. By the time the sun was high, he and Sabrina had packed, checked out, and left the guesthouse.

Before setting out for the next region, however, Ethan had one last errand: he needed to visit the fruit stall where he had made that bet.

They followed the streets to the beach they had visited before. When they reached the stall, they found that the vendor had already packed up all his oranges.

The man looked distinctly awkward at the sight of Ethan. After all, he had said that Drake could never be beaten. Now the young man standing in front of him had eliminated Drake six to zero. How could he not feel uneasy?

Ethan broke the silence first. "Hello there! Remember me? I've come for my oranges, just as we agreed!"

"Of course, of course. I have them ready."

The vendor gestured to one side, where more than a dozen boxes of oranges were packed and waiting.

"Ha! I'm only joking. How could I possibly eat all that?" Ethan waved his hands. "Three boxes will be enough. Keep the rest. Besides, I don't want a headline saying, 'Shocking! Indigo League Champion Hides His Identity to Swindle an Old Man Out of His Oranges!'"

"Well..."

The vendor knew Ethan was joking, but he hesitated. He had lost their wager fair and square and had prepared to pay up. He had not expected Ethan to change his mind.

Seeing this, Ethan smiled. "It's fine. Any more than that would just go to waste. Let's do it my way."

"All right." The vendor nodded, then ventured another request. "Um... could I ask for your autograph? My grandson is a big fan of yours!"

"Of course."

Ethan took the pen and quickly signed the autograph board. Then he asked, "What's your grandson's name?"

"Achi."

Without lifting his pen, Ethan added: To Achi: May you become an outstanding Trainer!

The vendor accepted the board with both hands and put it away carefully. Then he lifted three boxes of oranges and offered them to Ethan.

Ethan had no need to carry them himself. A red Pokémon descended from the sky, and Charizard easily gathered all three boxes into its arms.

After saying goodbye to the vendor, Ethan soon found a shipping company. He handed the oranges over and paid to send one box to Cerise Laboratory for everyone to share. He sent his Winner's Cup trophy and the photograph of himself with his Pokémon along with it.

Since he had no permanent home, his room at Cerise Laboratory had become the place where he kept his trophies. Some of his personal belongings and his Indigo League trophy were piled up there too. With Chloe looking after them, he had no fear of anything going missing.

Ethan sent a second box of oranges to Erika. In their earlier chats, she had mentioned how much she loved plants. These oranges tasted so good that he wondered whether she might be able to grow them in her greenhouse.

Once that was done, Ethan and Sabrina climbed onto Charizard and headed for the Orange Islands' big city. From there, they could take a cruise ship directly to Johto and avoid a long detour.

With that, Ethan's adventures through the Sevii Islands and the Orange Islands officially came to an end. The journey had given him wonderful memories and a wealth of rewards.
