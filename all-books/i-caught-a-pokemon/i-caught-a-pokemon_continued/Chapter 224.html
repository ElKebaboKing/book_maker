Trovita Island lay in the southern Orange Islands, one of the few major islands in the region.

From the island's earliest days, powerful undercurrents had swept through the waters to its north, making the sea treacherous. Over thousands of years, the currents had claimed countless lives and torn countless families apart.

More than a century ago, hoping to prevent so many ships from sinking, the islanders had driven sixty or seventy enormous stone pillars deep into the seabed. The pillars created dozens of whirlpools of different sizes. They changed the course of the water and slowed the undercurrents, while becoming Trovita Island's most distinctive sight.

Ethan and Sabrina had set out at noon, so it was already dusk when they reached the island. Sunset spread a layer of gold across it.

They landed outside the town. Ethan followed his navigation straight to Trovita Gym without delay.

The Gym stood well away from town in a four-story building. From the outside, it looked more like an ordinary Pokémon Center than a Gym. As at the previous two Gyms, no one waited by its entrance. It seemed few Trainers came to challenge it.

Ethan climbed the high steps and went inside. He asked a staff member to announce their arrival. Before long, a red-haired man in a black shirt and white vest approached with a group of identically dressed attendants.

His bearing and confident expression suggested that he was the Gym Leader. His clothes, though, made Ethan think of a Latin dancer.

Ethan stepped forward to explain why he had come. The man glanced at him, walked straight past, and stopped before Sabrina.

"Beautiful lady, might I have the pleasure of knowing your name?"

He gave a showy little bow, as though inviting a dance partner onto the floor.

Ethan was at a loss for words. Danny had behaved like this too. Why were the male Orange League Gym Leaders so frivolous? Could none of them take their eyes off a pretty woman?

Sabrina's brows drew together. Ethan could see her annoyance, and before she could use her powers on the man, he moved between them.

"Judging by your outfit, you must be a waiter," Ethan said with a smile. "I'm here to challenge the Gym. Is the Leader available? And please stop bothering my companion. If you upset her, things could get very unpleasant."

"A waiter?" The man heard the mockery in Ethan's voice and took a step back. He studied Ethan as though noticing him for the first time. "Ah, forgive me. Seeing such a beautiful lady made me forget myself. But you've got the wrong idea. I'm no waiter. I forgot to introduce myself: I'm Rudy, the Gym Leader here, and the Orange Crew's Star of the South!"

He finished with a few dance steps. He was plainly proud of his title.

Ethan merely waved a hand. "Sorry. I'm from Kanto, so I'm not familiar with the titles you use in a little place like this. Can we get started?"

A little place?

Rudy felt his blood pressure soar. Ethan's indifferent tone seemed to shatter all his pride. In the Orange League, the Gym Leaders were the center of attention. The public followed their every move. When had anyone ever looked down on them like this?

And seeing a beautiful woman at Ethan's side made him even harder to bear. Rudy drew a deep breath and calmed himself.

He often considered himself handsome. Now he had to admit that the man before him was perhaps just a tiny bit better looking. What an irritating fellow.

Rudy pursed his lips and folded his arms. "It doesn't matter if you've never heard of us. My time is valuable, and I won't battle just anyone who wanders in. If you want to challenge me, you'll have to prove you're worthy of being my opponent."

"Prove it?" Ethan thought for a moment, then took his badge case from his bag and opened it. Inside were two Orange League badges.

"I've already beaten two Gyms before yours. Will these do?"

Rudy gave the badges a brief glance and snorted. "Even Gym Leaders vary in skill. Those two don't understand battling at all. Don't compare me to them."

"Really?" Ethan feigned surprise. "That's wonderful. To be honest, I was worried your match would be as childish as the challenges at those other Gyms. What a relief. It's been so long since I've had a proper Gym battle that I'm starting to forget what one feels like."

Beside them, Sabrina could feel the sparks flying as the two men traded barbs.

When someone approached her like this in the past, she had always dealt with him herself, using her psychic powers to "remind" him to keep his distance. She had not expected Ethan to stand up for her and quarrel with another man out of jealousy.

After last night, she could sense that Ethan had begun to think of her as someone no outsider was allowed to touch, almost as his private possession. She had never felt anything like it before. She ought to have disliked being thought of that way, yet a strange warmth filled her instead.

Rudy led them behind the Gym to a river. Steep cliffs lined both banks, their sides thick with shrubs. A small dock stood on the shore, with a motorboat moored beside it.

Rudy pointed to the boat. "Proving yourself is simple. Ride this motorboat down the river and have your Pokémon use its moves to knock down every target along the way. Do that, and you'll have earned the right to battle me."

His smile turned thin. "Oh, and it's getting dark. Normally I wouldn't run the test at this hour, but since you're in such a hurry, I'm sure you won't mind if it goes on into the night?"

"Of course not." Ethan knew Rudy was making the test harder, but his expression did not change. "Day or night, it's all the same to me."

"Confident, aren't you?" Rudy chuckled. "Then I look forward to seeing how you do."
