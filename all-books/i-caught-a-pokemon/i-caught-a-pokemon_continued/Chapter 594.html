Ethan had not expected to run into Team Magma here.

Like Team Rocket, whose roots ran deep in Kanto and Johto, Team Magma was powerful in Hoenn. It was locked in fierce rivalry with another organization, Team Aqua, and neither side could overpower the other. Only a still larger organization, the International Police, could take them on.

Team Magma and Team Aqua might not be as strong as Team Rocket, but in Hoenn they dominated.

Seeing the first Houndoom caught, the other two Team Magma members frowned and released their own Pokémon.

Like their matching uniforms, their Pokémon matched too: two more Houndoom.

These were different from ordinary Houndoom. Armor covered their bodies and heads, making them look fiercer and offering some protection.

The two charged Ethan from left and right.

Only Gengar and Ethan's newly caught Torchic stood beside him. Torchic was far too inexperienced to beat Houndoom like these.

Even so, it bristled and did its best, breathing Ember toward the attackers.

They easily dodged the little flames and kept coming.

But a small chick was not Ethan's only defender. Gengar looked left and right at the two black dogs trying to split its attention. A Dark Pulse surged from its body like a black wave and swept over both.

The Houndoom already in Gengar's claws was struck too. All three flew backward and fell at their Trainers' feet.

Gengar had defeated the other two with one attack. A Gengar a thousand years old really was formidable.

“That's... incredible!”

The Devon employee stared in amazement. The Team Magma members went pale.

What kind of power was that? Even Team Magma's three executives might not be able to knock out a Houndoom in a single strike.

Was this man as strong as their leader?

They reached for a second set of Poké Balls, but Gengar did not give them a chance. At Ethan's command, it used Hypnosis and put all three to sleep.

Only then did the Devon employee approach cautiously.

“Thank you so much,” he said to Ethan.

“It was nothing.” Ethan smiled and shook his head. “Check whether anything is missing.”

At the reminder, the man hurried to retrieve his briefcase and examine its contents.

While he did, Ethan turned away. He did not ask what in the case had been worth stealing in broad daylight.

Unless it held a Master Ball, neither valuable documents nor secret research—or anything else he could imagine—interested him much.

After searching the case, the man let out a long breath.

The robbers had only just taken it. They had not had time to remove or tamper with anything. Apart from some damage to the outside, nothing had been lost.

“Everything is still here. I can't thank you enough,” he said. “Oh, I forgot to introduce myself. My name is Kennedy. I'm secretary to Mr. Stone, the president of Devon Corporation.”

A secretary? Ethan had taken him for an ordinary employee.

Devon was no small company. Only a few years ago it had been a modest business mining iron ore and refining steel. Now it had grown into a giant that could rival Silph Co.

Most of its products were made for Pokémon Trainers. It had developed new kinds of Poké Balls and, Ethan had heard, was making remarkable progress on a project to revive Pokémon from fossils.

More recently, a machine that generated energy using magma as fuel had brought the company's reputation to new heights.

Ethan waved off Kennedy's thanks. “Anyone who could help would have stepped in.”

He found some rope, tied up the three Team Magma members, and called the police.

Before long, Officer Jenny arrived with several police cars. The officers woke the sleeping men and took them away.

As far as Ethan was concerned, the matter was over. But after Kennedy had given his statement, he sought Ethan out again.

He bowed. “I've told our president what happened. He asked me to invite you to the company as his guests so he can thank you.”

Ethan did not want to intrude, but Kennedy insisted and had been sent by Mr. Stone himself. After discussing it with Erika, Ethan agreed to visit Devon.

The Gym would still be there. Challenging it a little sooner or later made no real difference.

Kennedy told them more about the company as they entered Rustboro City and made their way toward its headquarters.

“So Devon's head office is in Rustboro?” Erika asked.

Devon products were common even in Celadon City, far away in Kanto. She had heard of the company but had not known its headquarters was here.

They reached the street outside the Devon Corporation building.

Ethan saw an imposing tower long before they reached it. It had more than sixty floors and looked at least two hundred and sixty meters tall. Sunlight shone on the gleaming glass exterior. A huge fountain sprayed water in front of the entrance.

There was no question that Devon's tower was the tallest building in Rustboro.

Its wealth astonished Ethan. How had a small company grown to this scale in only a few years?

Inside, a lavish lobby had a thoroughly modern feel. Kennedy led them into an elevator and up to the top-floor office of Devon Corporation's president, Mr. Stone.

“President” meant he was, in effect, the company's chief executive.

His full name was Joseph Stone. Despite his silver hair, he was only in his fifties. Perhaps he took good care of himself: his eyes were bright and he looked healthy.

He was sitting in a spacious, well-lit office. When Kennedy brought Ethan and Erika in, he promptly rose to welcome them.

“Let me introduce myself. I'm Stone, president of Devon Corporation. Welcome, both of you. Thank you for helping Kennedy recover those documents. On behalf of Devon, you have my sincere gratitude.”

Before Ethan could answer, Kennedy complained, “This only happened because you insist on wandering off into town to play with those children. I had to carry the documents out to find you!”

He sounded almost as if he were scolding his boss. Surprisingly, Mr. Stone merely laughed, entirely unbothered.

Perhaps he was used to it.

A company president would normally expect a secretary—especially a male secretary—to follow his instructions without argument. Seeing Kennedy talk to Mr. Stone this way opened Ethan's eyes.

“Oh, don't make such a fuss.” Mr. Stone waved a hand at Kennedy. “We have the documents back, and no one was hurt. That's what matters.”

Kennedy could only sigh. He seemed to have no way of curbing his president's whims.

They had only just met, but Ethan could already see that Mr. Stone put on no airs.

Remarkable. Perhaps that helped explain Devon's rapid rise.

When Mr. Stone turned to him, Ethan nodded. “Hello, Mr. Stone. I'm Ethan, a Pokémon Trainer.”

Erika greeted him gracefully. “And I'm Erika.”

At Ethan's name, both Mr. Stone and Kennedy paused.

“Ethan?” Mr. Stone asked. “Are you the young man who helped Silph Co. take back its building from Team Rocket in Kanto and won two League conferences?”

People in Mr. Stone's position might not watch Pokémon tournaments, but they knew Ethan's name. Reports of important events around the world reached them quickly.

“Yes, that's me.”

“What a coincidence.” Mr. Stone marveled at the meeting. “No wonder you had the strength to recover our documents from Team Magma so easily. You've accomplished a great deal for someone so young.”

Kennedy, too, had not realized that the stranger who saved him was a conference Champion.

They grew still warmer toward Ethan. Mr. Stone showed Ethan and Erika around Devon, including its work on a technology that could change the age: reviving fossil Pokémon.

In a vast laboratory stood a precision machine, Devon's pride. The Fossil Reviver could bring Pokémon back from fossils.

It was an extraordinary achievement. People might once again see ancient Pokémon that had gone extinct. For Trainers and for society as a whole, the possibilities were immense.

The technology was not yet fully mature. Revivals were costly, and Devon could not perform them on a large scale. Successful experiments had proved it was possible, though.

Ethan was still admiring the company's work when an alarm rang out. Before anyone understood what was happening, several people in blue-and-white striped clothes burst from the laboratory next door and ran toward a floor-to-ceiling window.

“What?”

Mr. Stone and Kennedy stared, as confused as the researchers around them.

Ethan was already running after the intruders.

His encounter with Team Magma had put him on his guard. One glance at these people told him they belonged to its notorious rival, Team Aqua.

Devon had dreadful luck today. Team Magma had robbed an employee outside, while Team Aqua had managed to infiltrate the company itself.

The alarm was sounding, but the Team Aqua members appeared to have succeeded already.

If he had not known the two teams would sooner destroy each other than cooperate, he might have thought they had planned this together.

The Team Aqua members carried devices like jetpacks on their backs. They had plainly planned to escape through the window.

They might have got away if Ethan had not been there. He had no intention of letting them leave.

As he ran, he took a Poké Ball from his pouch and released Gardevoir.

Through their telepathic bond, she understood without a spoken command. She used Confusion to raise a barrier in front of Team Aqua.

Caught off guard, the intruders slammed into the invisible wall and fell in a daze. By the time they shook off the dizziness and got to their feet, footsteps sounded behind them. They turned to find a handsome young man and a Gardevoir waiting there.

“Damn it!”

They assessed their position and felt the barrier behind them. Devon's security team would soon reach this floor and surround them, with more people waiting outside. If they delayed, escape would be far harder.

But Ethan blocked their path now. They would have to defeat his Gardevoir to get out.

Ethan noticed a bulging pouch at each intruder's waist. Several vials of amber liquid stuck out of them. They had clearly taken something important from the laboratory.

Kennedy ran up behind Ethan and spotted the vials. His face changed. “Mr. Ethan, that's our culture medium! It's essential for reviving fossil Pokémon, and extremely valuable. Please don't let them take it. Help us get it back!”

For the second time today, Kennedy had asked Ethan to stop criminals for him.

Without Ethan here, Devon Corporation might have suffered a disastrous loss.

But with him here...
