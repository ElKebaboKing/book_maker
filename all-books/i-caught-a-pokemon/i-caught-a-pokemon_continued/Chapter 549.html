Ethan's third battle was Group A's tenth match. It would not begin until around two in the afternoon, so he, Erika, and Misty left the Group A field to visit Ash in Group C.

The timing was fortunate. When they arrived, Ash was in his second match, pitting Phanpy against a woman's Machoke.

They did not call out and distract him. Instead, they found Brock watching nearby.

"You're here?" Brock asked when he saw them. "How did Ethan's battles go?"

"How do you think?" Misty said with a smile before Ethan could answer. "Ethan won two in a row without much trouble! What about Ash? Don't tell me he's already lost a match."

Brock shook his head. "No, but..."

He told them about Ash's first battle. Ash had won, but his opponent's Furret had used clever burrowing tactics, attacking again and again from underground. It had cornered Pikachu, and Ash had nearly lost. Then inspiration struck. He had Pikachu grab hold of Furret and go underground with it. Pikachu used Thunderbolt beneath the surface, stopping Furret's tactic and defeating it.

It had been a dangerously close match.

At least Ash had pulled through.

No opponent could be taken lightly. Among the entrants, there might be someone capable of a real surprise.

Ethan shook his head, worried on Ash's behalf.

Sending Pikachu underground too was the sort of idea only Ash would have.

Back on the field, Ash seemed to have taken the lesson to heart. He fought with unusual focus, and Phanpy performed well. Working together, they finished the match when Phanpy struck Machoke in the head with Tackle and knocked it unconscious.

"I won!"

Ash cheered before his opponent had even returned her Pokémon. His narrow escape in the first match had clearly shaken him, and he had stayed anxious throughout the second. Now that he was one win away from advancing, he could not contain his excitement.

When Ash came off the field and saw Ethan and the others, he hurried over with a broad smile.

"Congratulations." Ethan led the applause. "You did well."

"Heh, that one was easier than the first," Ash said, grinning even wider. "What about you, Ethan? You won too, right?"

"Yes. I haven't faced anyone too troublesome. Come on, let's have lunch."

They went to a nearby restaurant. While Ethan ordered, Misty told Ash about meeting Travis. Once the food arrived and they had eaten heartily, Ethan and Ash talked about their matches and traded a few observations.

There was not much advice to exchange, really. Mostly they encouraged one another.

The break was short. Soon after one o'clock, before they had even digested lunch, an announcement called Ash back to his field. Ethan did not go to watch this time. He returned to Group A with Erika and Misty.

They watched another match from the seats, and then Ethan's turn came.

His opponent was a recent graduate of a Pokémon school. Rather than collect badges, the student had qualified for the Conference by passing an exam at school.

Every region had a route like that.

Pokémon school graduates generally knew more theory than the average entrant. Overall, though, they tended to have less hands-on experience and were not always good at improvising.

They would never learn Ash's rough-and-ready style of battling in a classroom.

Ethan sent out Scizor against the student's Cyndaquil. Despite fighting at a type disadvantage, Scizor won. It was a vivid lesson for a young Trainer just setting out into the world.

Even against an opponent with a fourfold type advantage, a wide enough gap in strength could make victory easy.

The student returned Cyndaquil and left the field with a quiet sigh. His record in the trials was one win and two losses, a poor showing. Losing to Scizor despite having the favorable type matchup counted against him too. He would probably miss the preliminaries.

Ethan, by contrast, felt pleased.

He left the field, gave Misty a light high-five as she jumped up, and headed for the League hall.

Before parting earlier, he and Ash had agreed to meet there after their last battles. They were both likely to advance, and there was no need to gather at the fields first.

By the time Ethan reached the hall, Ash had already won his final match and was waiting for him.

They met each other's eyes. Neither needed to say anything. Both looked relieved; their last battles had evidently caused little trouble.

They waited as the other Trainers returned from their matches. Compared with the more than two hundred who had registered, far fewer people were left in the hall.

Photos of those who had advanced to the preliminaries appeared on a large screen. Ethan and Ash found their own pictures there, along with Gary's, Jimmy's, Marina's, and many other Trainers they had met while traveling.

Ethan searched more closely and spotted Travis's photo too.

Travis had reached the preliminaries despite losing his first match to Ethan. He really was strong.

Once everyone had gathered and seen the results, they began looking at the other Trainers around them.

With the field reduced, people who knew one another naturally began to notice familiar faces.

Gary spotted Ethan and Ash first. He did not come over. He glanced at them, confirmed that they had advanced, then leaned alone against a pillar and closed his eyes. He carried himself as if he stood apart from the crowd.

Ethan had just looked away when footsteps sounded nearby. Jimmy and Marina, whom he had met during the Raikou incident, approached with a chubby boy.

Jimmy's hearty voice reached them before he did. "Mr. Ethan! So you're entering the Silver Conference too, and you've made the preliminaries!"
