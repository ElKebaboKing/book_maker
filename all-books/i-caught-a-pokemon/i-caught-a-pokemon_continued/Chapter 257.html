Before Ethan could answer Misty's question, Quackenpoker spoke up. "What else? They need treatment, immediately!"

He went back through the ruined camp and pulled medical supplies from an overturned crate. Then he hurried toward the Gyarados.

"They're all badly hurt, especially Ethan's. That one could die if we don't treat it now. Hey, you! Young Pokémon Watcher! Don't just stand there. Come and help me!"

Tracey blinked before realizing Quackenpoker meant him. "Oh! Right!" He ran over.

None of the others had expected Quackenpoker to know how to treat such serious wounds. Then again, he had traveled for years, watching and caring for Pokémon. He was bound to have learned something about first aid.

Ethan looked at his Gyarados. It lowered its head to meet his eyes.

"You worked hard," he told it.

Gyarados swam slowly toward him. Its injuries were severe; in places, the wounds were deep enough to expose flesh and bone. Even after healing, it would likely carry scars that never faded. Such scars could also stand as proof of what this fearsome Pokémon had endured.

With Tracey's help, Quackenpoker cleaned Gyarados's wounds, applied medicine, and carefully bandaged them. He handled every step like a professional.

While they worked, Ethan, Ash, Misty, and their Pokémon searched for the luggage the shock wave had scattered. It had blown their things across a wide area, making the search slow and tiring. After going over the surroundings carefully, they found that nothing valuable was missing, though everything was in disarray.

The hours passed as each group worked. The sun climbed higher. When Ethan checked the time, he was surprised to see that it was already ten in the morning.

Stomachs began to growl. They had slept badly and were now hungry, tired, and sleepy. Quackenpoker, who had been awake all night, was only keeping himself going through stubbornness.

Once he had finished treating the last of the wild Gyarados with lighter injuries, Tracey was finally free to make breakfast.

Ash rubbed his empty stomach. "I'm starving. I think I could eat a whole cow."

"Then go help Tracey," Misty said, giving him a look. "Get the campfire going." She was famished herself. She had managed to ignore it until Ash complained, but now her stomach seemed to agree with him.

Ash rolled up his sleeves and went to help. With everyone pitching in, the smell of food soon drifted through the camp.

Quackenpoker finished tending the other Pokémon injured in the fight and washed the blood from his hands just as Tracey brought out breakfast. He had made a generous meal to help everyone recover from their exhausting morning.

Quackenpoker sat in a folding chair and ate a few mouthfuls. He felt dizzy with fatigue and sighed. "One night without sleep and I'm already at my limit. Age catches up with you, whether you admit it or not."

He looked around at the young people and thought about everything that had happened since they met. "Thank you. Without you, I don't know how I would have brought this crisis to an end."

Ethan smiled but said nothing. If Quackenpoker had never met Ash, Team Rocket would not have followed them here. Then none of this would have happened. In a way, they were the ones who had dragged Quackenpoker and the Gyarados into trouble. At least it was over now.

"You're too kind," Tracey said, waving away the thanks. "Of course we had to help." Whatever the others thought of Quackenpoker, Tracey still respected him deeply as a Pokémon Watcher.

The wild Gyarados had all awakened after their initial treatment. No longer angry, they slipped beneath the lake's surface. Together with the Magikarp that had not evolved, they swam downriver, left Rind Island, and headed for the open sea.

The Magikarp's annual evolution gathering had finally come to an end.

After breakfast, no one needed to say that it was time to part. Quackenpoker would stay to analyze his observations and catch up on sleep. Ethan and the others were tired too, but they had rested for a few hours during the night and did not want to linger.

Quackenpoker set up his tent again and said goodbye. He was about to climb inside when he remembered something.

"Ethan..." He approached with a complicated look. "There's something I'd like to give you."

Ethan was puzzled. Why would Quackenpoker suddenly offer him a gift?

The old man searched his pack and drew out a round, translucent stone. He held it out. Ethan's eyebrows rose in surprise.

"I found this while traveling in another region," Quackenpoker explained. "It has a remarkable power. In battle, it can make Gyarados evolve into a more advanced form."

"I know," Ethan said. "It's a Gyaradosite, a Mega Stone for Gyarados."

"Mega Stone?" Ash and the others had never heard the term. They crowded around to examine the stone, which seemed to glow with energy.

"Ethan!" Ash asked excitedly. "Does this mean a Pokémon can evolve even further?"

"Yes, but only temporarily. Once the energy runs out during the battle, it returns to its previous form."

Now it was Quackenpoker's turn to be surprised. "You already know about it? That saves me explaining. I'd like you to have it."

Ethan did not reach for the stone. "Why give it to me?"

Quackenpoker held his gaze for a long moment. "You're a Pokémon Trainer, so you can use it. And your Gyarados is special. Very special. I believe this stone will do the most good in the hands of you and your Gyarados."
