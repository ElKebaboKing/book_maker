Following May's pointing finger, Ethan and the others turned and saw a familiar figure. It really was Drew.

“It is him!”

Ash, Max, and Brock had mixed feelings about seeing him. Drew was not exactly a friend, but he was no enemy either. He was a rival with whom they had a peculiar relationship. Apart from their surprise that he had come to compete in Kanto too, they had little to say.

For May, though, the presence of a strong opponent who had beaten her before added a great deal of pressure.

Perhaps Drew heard her voice, or perhaps he simply sensed someone watching. He turned halfway and spotted the group. He made no move to greet them. After searching their faces and failing to find the person he wanted, he frowned and walked away without a word.

May claimed she did not mind, but his arrival weighed on her all the same.

They entered the park and headed for the Contest Hall. Before they reached it, they saw a crowd going inside one after another.

“That's strange,” May said. “The Contest isn't until tomorrow. Why is everyone going in today?”

“Probably a rehearsal.” Ethan gave a lazy yawn. “Events this big usually have one. You ought to go take a look.”

May thought he was right. She hurried ahead and joined the crowd, while Ethan and the others followed at an easier pace.

“Ethan,” Ash asked curiously, “with a chance like this, don't you want to enter a Contest yourself?”

He sounded rather excited. Ethan glanced at him and saw the interest in his eyes. He remembered that Ash really would enter a Contest someday and smiled.

“Sounds more like you want to enter. Contests are a whole new stage for me. Maybe I'll try one another day, but I'll pass for now.” Ethan patted Ash's shoulder. “You, though? I think you'd be a hit. Dress up the way you did before, give Pikachu a nice makeover, and you'd be famous in no time. Right, Ashley?”

Even Ash understood that Ethan was teasing him.

“Oh, come on!” He stood there, punching the air like a Primeape while Ethan walked away laughing. “I'm never dressing up as a girl again!”

Ash had never wanted to remember how Jessie and James tricked him into dressing as a girl so he could challenge Erika at the Celadon Gym. Ethan's reminder left him mortified.

Max did not know the story, but he recognized an embarrassing secret when he heard one. He also remembered Ash dressing as a woman for the grand ball at Faraway Lake during their Hoenn travels. He pressed Brock for an explanation, then laughed without sparing Ash's feelings.

“Ashley! Ashley!”

“Stop calling me that!”

Still bickering, they entered the Contest Hall.

Inside, workers were putting the finishing touches on the venue. Some worked on the stage design, others on the lighting. The Coordinators who had come in were studying the stage amid the bustle, no doubt imagining how they would perform on it tomorrow.

It resembled a vast theater. The facilities were not especially new, but the stage was enormous, with plenty of room for a performance.

They found May in the crowd. She was examining the venue too, perhaps deciding which Pokémon would suit it best. They did not disturb her. Ethan and the others turned toward the stage instead.

A fashionable woman stood at its center, drawing their attention. She wore a sunhat, a blue midriff-baring top, a short skirt, and white stockings. Her dark blond hair fell in broad waves. She was quite pretty, and the beauty mark beneath one eye looked particularly familiar.

“Huh?” Max said. “Doesn't she look like the woman who hosted the Hoenn Contests? What's she doing here?”

Ash scratched his cheek. “I think her name was… was…”

“Vivian!” Brock supplied at once, then frowned. “But something's different. She's every bit as beautiful as Vivian, and they look almost identical. Vivian's beauty mark is under her left eye, though, while this woman's is under her right. Their clothes and the way they carry themselves are different too. Could they be twins?”

Ethan drew a sharp breath. Brock's eye for women was frightening. One glance, and he had guessed the truth.

The woman was not Vivian, who was still in Hoenn. She was Vivian's younger twin sister, Lilian. Ethan knew that because he had watched the anime. Brock had worked it out by observation alone. Then again, he was the man who could tell one Nurse Joy or Officer Jenny from another. Perhaps he never forgot a pretty woman he had met.

The stage lights converged on Lilian. At the director's signal, she took out a Poké Ball and released an Espeon to begin the rehearsal.

Espeon performed move after move. Its graceful motions and dazzling techniques captured everyone's attention. Its fine bearing and flawless execution showed how carefully it had been trained. A woman trusted to judge performances clearly knew what she was doing.

Then something unexpected happened.

May must have lost track of her Pokémon, or perhaps it had been drawn in by the show. Her Squirtle slipped away, ran straight onto the stage, and sent bubbles floating everywhere.

May rushed after it in alarm. Ethan and the others hurried over too, afraid something might go wrong. Squirtle had already interrupted the performance. Fortunately this was only a rehearsal; in the actual Contest, the consequences could have been much worse.

May knew it. She jumped onto the stage, anxious to retrieve Squirtle. Before she could reach it, Lilian picked it up.

“You can't do that, Squirtle.” Lilian smiled gently at it, then handed it to May. “You're welcome to look around, but you can't let your Pokémon run onto the stage.”

Up close, her voice sounded quite different from Vivian's. Lilian spoke more steadily, as suited her manner, while her older sister was much more lively onstage.

“I'm so sorry,” May said. “This is its first trip away from home. I should have kept a closer eye on it.”

Squirtle was unhappy in May's arms. It squirmed and struggled to get free. Lilian noticed, crouched down, and smiled at it.

“Your Squirtle seems full of enthusiasm, and it clearly likes the stage. Why not let it give a proper performance here tomorrow?”

Lilian was easy to approach. Rather than getting angry over the interruption, she patiently encouraged Squirtle. That generosity put her ahead of many others in her profession.

“It's a little too soon for that.” May smiled awkwardly. “I've only had Squirtle for a few days. It needs to practice before it goes onstage. I'm sorry we interrupted your rehearsal, Miss Vivian. We'll get out of your way.”

“Vivian?” Lilian stopped short.

May grew uncertain at her reaction. “You are Miss Vivian, aren't you? You hosted the Contests in Hoenn. We met several times there! Don't you remember me? I'm May, from Petalburg City.”

“Oh, I see!” Lilian smiled in understanding. “The Vivian you mean is my older sister. I'm her younger sister.”

May had been apart from Ethan and the others when they noticed the resemblance. Only now did she learn that the woman who looked so much like Vivian was her twin.

Seeing May's astonishment, Lilian sighed. “I suppose someone from Hoenn would confuse us. She's my sister, but really, dressing in those frilly, puffy dresses at her age? She's trying far too hard to look young. I have much better taste in clothes and style.” Her tone brightened. “I haven't introduced myself! I'm Lilian, the host of the Kanto Pokémon Contests. It's a pleasure to meet you!”

It sounded as though Lilian and Vivian did not get along especially well. That was their business. Lilian still had work to do, so after greeting May, she returned to the rehearsal.

May inspected the stage and learned the rules and precautions from the staff. Then she and the others left the hall for the Pokémon Center beside it in the park.

May, Ash, Max, and Brock would stay there tonight and wait for tomorrow's Contest. Ethan, however, had a better place in mind.

After seeing them into the Pokémon Center lobby, he waved. “I'll be going. I'll see you before the Contest starts tomorrow. May, I'm looking forward to your performance.”

“I know,” May said. “I'll do my best!”

Ethan walked away without looking back. Ash scratched his head and turned to May. “Where's Ethan going? Aren't you traveling together? Isn't he going to help you practice?”

“Probably to see Sabrina,” May said. She was not concerned that Ethan would miss her training. Letting her watch his battles up close had already taught her a great deal. Choosing her Pokémon, planning her style, and deciding how to perform were things she needed to do for herself. Ethan could not make those choices for her. Max, Ash, and Brock were plenty of company.

Besides, May understood the nature of Ethan and Sabrina's relationship. She had no intention of getting in the way of their reunion.

Ash was briefly surprised, then nodded. He had witnessed Ethan's complicated relationships with Sabrina and Erika. He understood little about romance, but he knew both women were Ethan's partners. Of course Ethan could go to see either of them.

Brock alone watched Ethan disappear and shed tears of envy. Ethan's luck in love was something he could never hope to match.

May had guessed correctly. Ethan was going to meet Sabrina. This was his last stop in Saffron City before leaving Kanto again. He had only one more chance to spend time with her; who knew how long it would be before he returned? Even if tonight left him tired, it would be worth it.

He did not boldly stay at the Saffron Gym this time. It was too conspicuous. Coming and going there so often would draw attention, to say nothing of Sabrina's father being there to interrupt them.

Ethan called Sabrina and invited her out. They browsed a nearby mall, watched a movie, and finished with a romantic candlelit dinner. Then they went to a hotel for couples and spent a wonderful night together.

There was no need to recount all the intimacy and exertion that followed. When Ethan rejoined May and the others at the contestants' dormitory the next morning, everyone could see both happiness and fatigue on his face.
