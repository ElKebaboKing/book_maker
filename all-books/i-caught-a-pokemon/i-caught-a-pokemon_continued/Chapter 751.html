Ethan was certain Lisia had not been out for long.

Yet she had announced that she was tired and wanted to return to the dormitories with him. Her reason was obvious.

But...

He glanced at the hands clasped firmly around his arm. If he went back like this and Misty saw them, the scene would explode.

On the other hand, if he did not go back, was he supposed to keep walking around with her?

In truth, Ethan thought highly of Lisia. She was cheerful, lively, pretty, and an idol. She had a great deal going for her. He was hardly the sort of person who could not appreciate his good fortune.

If he had been alone, he would gladly have let a girl like her accompany him. The trouble was that there was already someone at his side. If the two of them met now, it would be like Mars crashing into Earth.

And he could not bring himself to turn Lisia down.

What a headache...

After turning it over in his mind, he started slowly toward the dormitories with her. He would think of a way out as they walked.

Lisia had no idea Ethan regarded her as a complication. She had slipped out to relax, and unexpectedly running into him had been a wonderful surprise.

He had left a deep impression on her. He was handsome, his Pokémon were powerful, he was famous, and beneath his composure he had a sense of humor. He matched everything she had imagined in an ideal partner.

If only that girl called Misty were not always with him.

Still, Misty was not here now. With no one to get in the way, Lisia could finally spend some proper time with Ethan!

Her good mood made her talkative. Watching the steady stream of visitors, she sighed. “That's the inconvenient thing about being an idol. Wherever I go, I cause a commotion. I can't even enjoy a quiet walk. But you haven't disguised yourself at all, Ethan, and nobody recognizes you on the street. I guess people in Hoenn don't know you yet. I'm sure you'll be the most popular person here after the Ever Grande Conference!”

She was right that fame made ordinary outings difficult. If Ethan had his way, people would still fail to recognize him after the Conference. That was unlikely, but he would be leaving Hoenn around then anyway, so it would not affect him much.

Something in Lisia's remark gave him an idea.

He glanced at Gardevoir. Understanding immediately, it used telepathy to speak to Gengar in his shadow.

A moment later, Gengar slipped out unseen and rose into the air a short distance away. It puffed out its cheeks and blew. A powerful gust swept over Ethan and Lisia.

The sudden wind snatched the cap from Lisia's head. She had already taken off her sunglasses and mask to talk to Ethan, leaving her unmistakable face in full view.

A chubby little boy happened to pass them. The flying cap caught his eye, and his gaze fell on Lisia's face. After half a second of stunned silence, delight and disbelief lit up his expression. He pointed at her and shouted, “Ah! It's Lisia!”

His cry set off an uproar. Lisia jumped, and everyone nearby turned to look.

She was one of the most popular Contest idols around. Anyone who followed Pokémon Contests knew her name. At first the passersby seemed doubtful, but once they saw that it really was her, excitement swept through the crowd.

“Lisia!”

“Wow, she's so cute!”

“My whole family are fans! Could you give me an autograph?”

Lisia stood frozen, still wondering where the wind had come from and how it had blown off her cap. Only when people surged toward her did she come to her senses. She released Ethan's arm, put on a stiff smile, and waved.

“H-hello, everyone...”

Within moments, fans had surrounded her. When she looked for Ethan, he had disappeared into the crowd.

By the time Ethan squeezed free, he was a good distance away. He glanced back. More people were hurrying toward the commotion, many without even knowing what had happened.

Gengar floated beside him, doubled over laughing. “You really are wicked! What a rotten idea!”

“I had to get away somehow.” Ethan shot it a look. “Enough laughing. Go back and protect Lisia. The staff responsible for keeping order will probably arrive soon, but make absolutely sure she's safe until they do.”

“Don't worry!” Gengar thumped its chest. “Leave it to me!”

Ethan watched it go. Lisia would not escape the crowd quickly, so he decided to find Misty first. Once Lisia was free, she was bound to come looking for him at the competitors' village. He could warn Misty in advance and perhaps keep her from getting too angry when Lisia appeared.

He left the snack street and followed the direction Misty and the others had taken. After checking two outdoor training fields, he finally found them at the third.

They had company. One of the two newcomers he knew, and the other he did not.

Drew was the first to catch his eye. Hands in his pockets, he stood in front of Misty with a deliberately cool pose. Misty did not so much as glance at him.

On May's other side stood a rather peculiar man with purple hair, a pointed green hat, and green clothes.

Ethan could not tell what they had been discussing. But as he approached, both men looked at him and then left at the same time, heading in different directions. That puzzled him. Drew's departure made sense, but what about the purple-haired man?

Misty waved Ethan over. He joined her and watched the stranger walk away. “Who was that?”

“His name's Harley,” Max answered. “He's odd. He often refers to himself the way a woman would, and he's always tricking people with underhanded methods!”

So that was Harley. Ethan remembered him vaguely from the anime, though he had never paid him much attention. Max's tone made it clear he did not like the man.

“What did he and Drew want?” Ethan asked.

Max shook his head. “Drew came to make his usual big talk. Harley had a disagreement with my sister before, and he came to apologize. But I don't think it's that simple. Who knows what he's up to?”

“Whatever his plans are, just be careful around him.”

Ethan did not dwell on it. He was about to ask May how practice was going when a girl's voice rang out behind him.

“Ethan! I knew you'd be here!”

His heart sank. He turned, and there was Lisia, hurrying toward him. Gengar followed beside her with a mischievous grin.

“Lisia!?” Misty's eyes widened at the sight of her. “What are you doing here?”

“Oh?” Lisia feigned surprise. “You didn't know? Ethan and I were walking around together for quite a while. If we hadn't been interrupted, we'd still be together now!”

Misty's anger flared instantly. Lisia rushed up and wrapped herself around Ethan's arm again. Gritting her teeth, Misty seized his other arm and tried to pull him away.

Ash, May, Max, and Brock exchanged bewildered looks. They had never seen Misty so angry.

“Who is she?” Ash asked. “Why is she fighting Misty over Ethan?”

“I don't know.” May shook her head. “Misty's never mentioned her to me.”

“Lisia... Lisia...” Brock rubbed his chin. Then recognition dawned. “Oh! It's her!”

The other three turned to him. “You've heard of her?”

Brock took the Grand Festival booklet from his pocket, flipped through a dozen or so pages, and read aloud: “...During the Festival, we've also invited Contest idol Lisia to perform...”

“A Contest idol?” The others looked even more surprised.

Brock closed the booklet. “I looked her up earlier. She's a Coordinator herself, and I think her fans call her Lissi. She's very popular in Hoenn and other regions too.”

“Now that you mention it, I think I've heard her name on television,” May said. “And since becoming a Coordinator, I've learned how hard it is. For her to become a Contest idol at this age, she must be amazing.”

Ash frowned. “Then how does Misty know her? And why do they seem so...?”

He stopped short of saying they seemed to hate each other. Lisia was close enough to hear.

Meanwhile, Ethan was almost dizzy from being shaken back and forth. Where had these two girls found so much strength? It felt as though they might tear him in half. When neither showed any sign of stopping, he pulled them toward each other and wrenched his arms free.

“Stop pulling! You're going to tear my arms off. If you've got something to say, settle it with words!”

Misty and Lisia let him go and turned to face each other instead. Ethan rubbed his arms, stepped back, and finally breathed out.

Gengar drifted over. Ethan frowned and whispered, “How did she find us?”

“I brought her, of course.”

“What!?”

Gengar grinned. “You told me to protect her. You didn't say what to do afterward! Once she realized I was your Pokémon, she kept pestering me to find you. I couldn't get away, so I brought her here. Besides, I wanted to see what would happen when those two girls got together again. It's every bit as good as I hoped!”

“You...”

Ethan shook his head. He had accounted for everything except Gengar's love of a spectacle.

They could not stay at the training field. Misty and Lisia showed no sign of stopping, and Ethan did not want to draw an audience. He took them back toward the dormitories.

May was too worried about Misty to continue practicing. She followed with Ash, Max, and Brock.

They had barely reached the dormitory entrance when Lisia's manager, having discovered her absence, came out looking for her.

“Lisia!” The manager hurried up, visibly anxious, and held out a phone. “Where have you been? Do you know someone photographed you arm in arm with a man? It's already online! People are saying your relationship has been exposed!”

Ethan and the others leaned in. The photograph showed him with Lisia on the snack street, at the moment she had been recognized, her hands still around his arm.

Someone had taken the picture and posted it in no time at all. They had been quick to spot what would make news.

Lisia only smiled, apparently unconcerned. Misty, by contrast, swayed and nearly fell. She had left Ethan alone only long enough to help May practice, and all this had happened?

Someone had stolen her man!
