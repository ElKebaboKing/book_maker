As the battle began, Ethan and Zhe issued their commands at the same time.

Unlike Zhe, who wanted to engage at close range, Ethan immediately ordered Blastoise to attack from a distance.

Quagsire was too slow to dodge in time, and Zhe was unwilling to let it take a beating without fighting back. He had no choice but to counterattack.

Two streams of water shot from the Pokémon's mouths in quick succession.

Water sprayed everywhere as the thick and thin streams met in midair. The thicker one was naturally Blastoise's Hydro Pump, while the thinner one was Quagsire's Water Gun.

Splash!

As the attacks collided, Hydro Pump crushed Water Gun and scattered it into countless droplets. Its remaining force continued forward and struck Quagsire.

Quagsire swayed slightly under the impact.

Although its Ground typing was weak to Water, Quagsire was also a Water type, sparing it from taking additional damage.

Quagsire had not chosen Water Gun because it was deliberately holding back its more powerful Water-type moves. As a dual-type Pokémon, it also knew a number of Ground-type moves, leaving it with a very limited selection of Water-type attacks!

Seeing Quagsire lose out at long range only strengthened Zhe's determination to bring it closer to Blastoise.

At his command, Quagsire dove into the water and swam toward Blastoise from below, preventing another attack from the surface.

A blue shape streaked through the water.

Several seconds later, Quagsire leaped from the water directly in front of Blastoise. Before it even landed on the stone pillar, it raised both claws and reached for its opponent!

The opening movement made its intention obvious. It was using Grab Throw, a Normal-type move employed by many Pokémon!

Blastoise had no intention of waiting helplessly for the fierce attack. It advanced instead of retreating, turned sideways, and aimed the hardest edge of its shell at Quagsire's abdomen with Tackle!

Bang!

The two Pokémon rushed at each other and immediately became locked in a grapple. But Blastoise's smooth shell offered Quagsire nothing to hold. Its Grab Throw failed, while the heavy impact forced it to stumble a large step backward!

Both of Blastoise's attacks had worked, seeming to set the tone for the battle.

Quagsire might not appear exceptional in any area, but aside from its extremely poor speed, its abilities were evenly balanced and had no obvious weakness.

That versatility made it difficult for certain opponents to target. Unfortunately, this time it faced a stronger Blastoise and an Ethan who had prepared thoroughly for the battle!

Other than stamina, Blastoise surpassed Quagsire by a wide margin in every area. That advantage made it almost impossible for Quagsire to gain the upper hand in a direct exchange!

The two Pokémon remained on the stone pillar and traded attack after attack. Within only a few breaths, they had already clashed several times!

As the number and frequency of their attacks increased, Quagsire suffered repeated damage, but Blastoise also took several solid hits.

This proved that Quagsire was genuinely strong enough to trouble Blastoise. Zhe clearly had not been idle, either. He had studied Blastoise's fighting style and characteristics as well.

Unfortunately, a gap still separated the two Pokémon, and no tactic could make up for it!

Finally, Blastoise and Quagsire both used Aqua Tail and swung their tails heavily into each other. With a tremendous crack, the water surrounding their tails exploded into spray and rained down through the air. The clash appeared evenly matched, but Blastoise was clearly superior. While Quagsire struggled to regain its footing, Blastoise closed in again and defeated it in one decisive rush!

Splash...

Quagsire toppled backward into the water.

After one look at the unconscious Quagsire, the referee promptly raised his flag and announced the result.

Despite a few twists along the way, Ethan had won the first battle with relative ease.

The audience found the outcome both unexpected and perfectly reasonable.

After all, Ethan was the Indigo League champion. It was only natural for him to win. They simply had not expected Zhe, the tournament's second favorite, to cause him so little trouble.

Was the difference in strength really that great?

In the Trainer's command area, Ethan quietly breathed a sigh of relief as Zhe recalled Quagsire with a helpless expression.

The victory might have looked easy, but the battle had put him under considerable pressure. After its early setbacks, Quagsire had erupted with tremendous fighting spirit in the middle and late stages, even recovering from its disadvantage for a time!

Fortunately, Ethan had prevailed in the end without encountering any serious danger.

While waiting for Zhe to send out his second Pokémon, his ears suddenly caught several familiar voices from the nearby stands.

Turning around, he saw Erika, Misty, Ash, and Brock sitting in the second row and waving enthusiastically at him!

Compared with his earlier matches, they had been fortunate enough to secure excellent seats this time, allowing Ethan to spot them at a glance.

Even more amusingly, Ash's Pikachu and Misty's Togepi each held a little fan in one hand. They bounced up and down as they cheered him on!

Ethan answered them with a broad smile, then turned back toward his opponent.

Zhe had already taken out his second Poké Ball. He drew a deep breath, lifted the ball to his lips, and murmured something to it as though performing a prayer before battle.

Is he preparing to risk everything?

Ethan crossed his arms over his chest without lowering his guard in the slightest.

The more critical the moment, the more sharply he could focus. Even if his opponent displayed a hundred and twenty percent of his strength, Ethan would give him no opportunity!

Come on. Let me see the last of your power!

Only after a long while did Zhe throw the Poké Ball forward. A flash of light revealed a Feraligatr standing upon the stone pillar.

As the final evolution of Totodile, one of Johto's three starter Pokémon, Feraligatr was extremely popular with the region's Trainers. Part of that popularity came from its appearance, which managed to look cool and goofy at once. The other reason, naturally, was its strength!

Feraligatr resembled a crocodile standing on two legs. Blue scales covered its body, together with rectangular plates of armor that strengthened its defense. Compared with its unevolved forms, its forelimbs had grown far more powerful, giving it a much more imposing attack.

Although this pure Water-type Pokémon usually moved at a slow, leisurely pace, it became too fast for the eye to follow when lunging at prey!

Its huge body was difficult to support on land, but by crouching low and driving itself forward with its powerful hind legs, it could move across the ground at great speed. In battle, it opened its jaws wide to intimidate an opponent, kicked off with its strong hind legs, and charged with astonishing speed. It could also catch prey in its enormous, powerful jaws, then whip its neck around and tear the victim apart.

Compared with Ethan's Blastoise, Feraligatr's overall capabilities were no worse; the two Pokémon merely emphasized different strengths. Since both were also pure Water types, their physical attributes alone made the outcome extremely difficult to predict.

Of course, a Pokémon battle depended on more than physical ability. Experience, momentum, and execution all mattered, as did whether the Trainers commanded properly. Even the battlefield and the spectators could greatly affect a match.

That was why many Pokémon performed exceptionally well under ordinary circumstances, only to collapse at a major tournament. Their state of mind affected how well they could display their strength.

But Blastoise had traveled with Ethan for a long time, excelled at major competitions, and spent plenty of time maturing at Cerise Park. Ethan was certain it could defeat this Feraligatr!

At the referee's signal, the second battle officially began.

Before Ethan could give an order, Feraligatr launched an all-out assault.

With his back against the wall, Zhe showed an enormous desire to attack. Under his command, Feraligatr erupted with even more astonishing power!

It flattened itself against the surface and drove all four limbs through the water like a true crocodile. In the blink of an eye, it closed the distance to Blastoise, then attacked ferociously again without even pausing for breath.

At first, Blastoise tried to defend and counterattack. Feraligatr, however, was willing to trade injury for injury and completely ignored whatever damage it might suffer!

The tactic worked. Blastoise was forced entirely onto the defensive. Fortunately, defense was already one of its specialties. Iron Defense, Protect, and the exceptional Withdraw sealed off Feraligatr's attacks without leaving a gap.

As time passed, however, Blastoise inevitably began to struggle under the pressure.

Like its Trainer, Feraligatr seemed to have staked everything on this battle. It ignored its rapidly dwindling stamina, a fire burning in its eyes as it attacked, attacked, and attacked again!

The assault came so quickly that the instant Blastoise blocked one move, another followed. It had no energy left to counterattack!

Even when it used Rapid Spin, Feraligatr merely dove underwater to avoid the move, causing Blastoise to waste its own strength instead.

The sight immediately stirred the spectators into a commotion.

Since Ethan entered the tournament, his Pokémon had always appeared overwhelmingly strong and defeated every opponent by an enormous margin. Now that the semifinals had arrived, someone had finally managed to suppress his momentum!

Perhaps suppress was not quite accurate, but Feraligatr was undeniably attacking without pause while Blastoise scrambled desperately to defend itself!

The audience brightened and instinctively sat straighter, eager to see whether Feraligatr could defeat Blastoise. Even Ash, Misty, and their companions watched wide-eyed, too tense to breathe!

On the field, Ethan narrowed his eyes.

Feraligatr's performance had caught both him and Erika's group by surprise. It had never fought like this in its previous matches. Its style now was completely different.

Zhe had clearly been driven into a corner and changed his approach, casting aside every reservation to risk everything on one desperate assault!

Ethan carefully searched for a weakness in Feraligatr's offense.

Passively taking a beating did not suit him. If the battle continued like this, Feraligatr would probably exhaust itself and stop attacking first. But Ethan could not call it a proper victory if he merely waited for his opponent to collapse from fatigue.

This was the moment to test his ability as a Trainer!

After observing for a while longer, he finally found an opportunity.

The two Pokémon's fierce battle and flying attacks had caused an enormous commotion. Neither side had overcome the other, but the stone pillar beneath them could not withstand such abuse. Without anyone noticing, it had slowly begun to crack!

The fracture was subtle, but it presented an undeniable danger. Better still, it faced Ethan directly, while Zhe could not see it at all.

An idea flashed through Ethan's mind. "Blastoise, stomp your foot, now!"

Blastoise froze for an instant, unable to understand the command. Its absolute trust in its Trainer allowed no hesitation, however. It raised its right foot and brought it down with all its might.

Crack!

Blastoise's body was already extremely heavy. Added to the force of its stomp, that weight shattered the pillar's once-flat surface and opened a large gap!

Both Pokémon instantly lost their balance and began to fall toward the water.

Blastoise had been mentally prepared. It merely stumbled, then used its weight to stand firm upon the pillar. Feraligatr, by comparison, was caught completely by surprise and fell straight into the water!

The sudden difference in elevation gave Blastoise a chance to breathe and sounded the beginning of Ethan's counterattack.

Following the principle of striking an enemy while it was down, Blastoise attacked Feraligatr from above. Water blasted repeatedly from the cannons on its back, striking Feraligatr several times. It struggled in the water but could not climb out, and finally had no choice but to dive below the surface.

Hiding underwater forever was impossible. After Feraligatr remained submerged for a while, Blastoise still revealed no weakness and made no attempt to pursue it. Left with no alternative, Zhe ordered Feraligatr to surface and try to climb back onto the pillar.

Unfortunately, it could no longer approach Blastoise.

The consequences of Feraligatr's earlier frenzy had also begun to show. Its stamina was nearly exhausted, and it had barely climbed onto the pillar before it began panting heavily.

Zhe's expression changed as he realized what was happening.

Ethan had no intention of letting such an opportunity escape!

At his command, Blastoise charged forward and slammed brutally into Feraligatr!

The impact sent Feraligatr tumbling backward. With a loud splash, it fell unconscious into the water just like its partner Quagsire...
