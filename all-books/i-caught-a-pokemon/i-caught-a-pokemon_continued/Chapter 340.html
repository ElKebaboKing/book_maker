Sabrina remained silent for a while, clearly weighing her choice. In the end, she accepted Ethan's advice and nodded.

"All right. I'll return to Saffron Gym for now."

She was reluctant, but she understood that there was no alternative. They were adults. They could not travel without a care like children; everyone had responsibilities that tied them to ordinary life.

Seeing that she was still upset, Ethan did not want Sabrina to think he was urging her to leave.

"It will only cost us a few days. I'll take you all the way back to Saffron City."

"Hm?" Sabrina looked at him in surprise. "What about your journey? It's all right. I can return by myself."

Ethan kissed her forehead without concern. "Compared with you, what do a few lost days matter? Besides, I brought you out of Kanto. Naturally, I should take you home myself."

Touched, Sabrina leaned gently against his chest.

Her forehead rested just beneath his chin. Ethan did not even need to lower his head to catch her fragrance.

They held each other for a while. When they noticed how late it had become, they mounted Charizard and set out for Violet City.

Their ultimate destination had changed, but there was no need to hurry back to Kanto during the night. They would rest in Violet City, recover their strength, and depart the following morning after a proper meal.

Charizard beat its wings and carried them high into the air.

What neither of them knew was that the Pokémon League usually supported Gym Leaders who traveled to train. It had never imposed excessive restrictions on such journeys.

If a Gym Leader's absence left no one to manage a Gym, the League would even assign someone to oversee it temporarily.

Only by continuing to improve and keeping their battle styles current could Gym Leaders provide challengers with professional guidance instead of falling behind the times.

The order demanding Sabrina's return was actually an open scheme by Team Rocket.

Ethan and Sabrina had caused the organization tremendous trouble. Team Rocket lacked the strength to eliminate them, so it called upon hidden connections within the Pokémon League to remove Sabrina from Ethan's side and weaken him.

Without Sabrina beside him, Ethan would at least become easier to deal with.

Darkness fell, and stars filled the sky.

By night, Ethan and Sabrina finally reached Violet City. Rather than entering the city center, they found an ordinary inn in the outskirts and stayed there.

After dinner and a shower, the two lay down together.

Perhaps because their separation was approaching, Sabrina was exceptionally tender that night, as well as unusually passionate and assertive. With her permission, Ethan even tried several new positions...

When exhaustion finally drew them to sleep, they remained wrapped tightly in each other's arms.

...

After resting through the night, they left Violet City again the following morning.

They mounted Charizard and flew east. High above the ground, there was little danger of anyone discovering them, so Ethan released all three Legendary birds as well.

The birds had spent a long time inside their Poké Balls. This time, Ethan decided to let them fly to their hearts' content.

Several Pokémon soared through the sky. Charizard flew steadily while carrying Ethan and Sabrina, but the three Legendary birds seemed to possess endless energy after so long without fresh air. They plunged into the clouds, then suddenly swept beneath Charizard, crying brightly as they enjoyed their rare freedom.

After a while, their restless natures began to show. Perhaps they still disliked one another. As though playing, they began attacking each other.

Lightning, ice, and flame filled the sky, leaving the once-perfect clouds in chaos.

They really can't behave...

Ethan shook his head and ignored them.

They could run wild for now. Once they exhausted themselves, they would finally settle down.

White clouds rushed past, close enough to touch with an outstretched hand.

Ethan had no idea how far they had traveled. At noon, when Charizard began to show signs of fatigue, he patted its neck and directed it toward a beach.

A cool sea breeze softened the sun's heat. The blue ocean met the sky at the horizon, leaving the view open and refreshing.

"This is a pleasant spot, and no one will disturb us. Let's have lunch here."

Sabrina naturally agreed. Knowing their separation was near, she looked at Ethan with constant affection.

Ethan spread out a picnic cloth and took out the boxed lunches they had bought earlier. Then he released all his Pokémon so they could enjoy the air.

Gyarados and Crystal Steelix lay nearby, lazily basking in the sun. Gardevoir sat gracefully beside Ethan, while the rest of the Pokémon ran to the water and played together.

The three Legendary birds circled several times before landing around Ethan.

Perhaps tired from their games, they preened their feathers and enjoyed the peaceful moment.

Lunch consisted of sandwiches and rice balls from a convenience store. They tasted good, but compared with a hot meal, they left Ethan feeling as though he could never quite eat his fill.

He had taken only a few bites when Jolteon seemed to discover something. It suddenly ran back and pointed one paw toward a distant stretch of beach.

Ethan followed its gesture. Far out over the water stood a building that closely resembled an aquatic sports stadium.

"Why would there be a stadium in such a remote place?"

Sabrina was curious too. She looked at Ethan. "Should we go and see?"

"Sure. We can treat it as exercise after lunch."

They finished eating, cleaned everything up, and recalled the Pokémon. Then Ethan and Sabrina walked hand in hand toward the building.

It looked close but required a surprisingly long walk.

Their footprints marked the sand, only for the waves to wash them away moments later.

Before they reached the stadium itself, they came upon a billboard.

It displayed a poster for a synchronized water ballet. A Qwilfish leaped high through the air, surrounded by six pairs of beautiful legs rising from the sea.

Huge letters ran across the bottom:

Pokémon Synchronized Water Ballet!

Water ballet?

The phrase immediately made Ethan think of the three Cerulean sisters, Misty's older siblings. He had not expected to find another water ballet performance in such an isolated place.

Still...

Ethan looked around. Apart from the two of them, there was not another person in sight.

Would anyone truly come to watch a ballet stage built on such a remote beach?

This isn't some kind of tourist trap, is it?
