Ethan stepped closer to the stone shrine. A red orb sat in the bronze bird's beak, casting a warm glow into the darkness.

"That must be the treasure Melody told us about. Ash, take it out."

"Huh?" Ash looked at him in surprise. "Aren't you going to do it?"

Ethan shook his head. "I have no interest in being the commander. You got here first, anyway. This part is yours."

Ash hesitated. He admired Ethan's strength and character so much that he had already decided that, if either of them was the commander in the legend, it had to be Ethan. But Ethan made no move toward the shrine.

Ash reached into the bird's beak. The orb was wedged in tightly, and he had to pull hard before it came free.

Only then did Ethan lean in for a better look. The orb was not red after all. It resembled a glass ball about the size of a fist. Something like a flame burned inside it, bathing the glass in red light.

Aside from the storm, the trip had been as easy as Melody had promised. The climb to the shrine had felt almost like sightseeing.

"Put the orb away," Ethan said. "It's time to head back."

They turned toward the stone staircase and found several figures standing on it. Team Rocket's trio had followed them and watched from hiding. Now that Ethan and Ash were leaving, they had finally shown themselves.

It was too dark for Ash to make out their faces at first. "Who are you?"

Jessie gave a cold smile. "Prepare for trouble!"

James folded his arms. "And make it double!"

"To protect the world from—"

"All right, enough," Ethan cut in. "I've heard your motto too many times. We all know each other by now, so skip the introduction. Tell us what you want."

"Exactly!" Ash said with a frown. "The weather's awful, and we're busy. Don't waste our time."

Jessie, James, and Meowth fell silent. Having their routine interrupted left them feeling as if the words were stuck in their throats.

Jessie coughed to hide her embarrassment and pretended she did not care. "Fine. Before the weather gets any worse, hand over Pikachu and that thing you just picked up!"

Team Rocket's determination to catch Pikachu ran through the entire animated series. Ethan could almost admire their persistence, however selfish their motives. They had failed countless times and only grown more stubborn. Their great ambition was still bound to fail.

"Keep dreaming," Ash said. "I'm not giving you Pikachu or the treasure!"

"Oh?" Jessie grinned at the slip. "So that thing really is a treasure. Then don't blame us for taking it by force!"

She and James reached for their Poké Balls. Before they could send out their Pokémon, a deep scraping sound came from below, as though something heavy were dragging across the stone steps.

They looked back. A huge dark shape came rushing up the staircase.

"What is that?!"

Team Rocket barely had time to cry out. The shape leaped high and bore down on them. They threw themselves aside to avoid being crushed.

Bang!

It landed and slid toward Ethan and Ash. Now they could see it was a small sailboat.

A boat? How had a boat reached the top of the island?

It stopped in front of Ethan. Before anyone could work out what had happened, several heads emerged from its cabin.

"Ethan! Ash! We've come to get you!"

That was Misty's voice. In the beam of Ethan's flashlight, he saw Melody, Tracey, and Sabrina with her.

"What are you doing here?"

"We were worried about you," Misty said with a smile. "So we all came."

"Honestly!" Melody said. "In weather like this, you should have gone straight back to Shamouti Island. Why would you sail out in such heavy rain?"

Ash scratched his head and gave her an apologetic grin. His impulsive decision had worried them.

"We're here now, so there's no point arguing about it," Ethan said. He looked over the sailboat. "How did you get it up here, though?"

They were at the highest part of Fire Island, at least several hundred meters from the shore in a straight line. Sailing a boat all the way up the stone steps without water seemed impossible.

"That was Melody," Misty said admiringly. "She's amazing with a boat. She caught the strong wind in the sails and steered us right up the staircase!"

Melody lifted her chin at the praise. "For someone who grew up on Shamouti Island, a boat is practically a cradle. It wasn't difficult."

The tension between Misty and Melody had faded. Ethan wondered what had happened after he left the banquet hall, or on their way here. Melody had also changed into an islander's outfit, which gave her a wilder look.

Team Rocket noticed the growing crowd and prepared to slip away. Before they could move, a brilliantly glowing Pokémon swept across the black sky beside the island.

Everyone recognized it at once.

"Z-Zapdos?!"

"The God of Thunder!" Melody exclaimed. Her surprise gave way to a frown. This was Fire Island, the God of Fire's territory. Why would the God of Thunder come here?

At the same time, the three Great Balls at Ethan's left hip began to tremble. Their occupants seemed to sense another of their kind and could barely wait to come out and fight.
