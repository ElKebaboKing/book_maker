Octillery was Remoraid's evolved form, but almost its entire body was red. People said it resembled the red octopuses of the eastern Pacific or the giant Pacific octopus. Others thought its shape and coloring might have been inspired by an octopus-shaped sausage.

Along with its soft body, it had a pair of rather vacant-looking eyes and a long, protruding mouth. An octopus also needed tentacles. Most of Octillery's extended behind it, while two longer ones reached forward. Each bore two round yellow suckers.

Ethan had spent time studying the Pokédex and had battled Octillery before. He knew they usually sheltered behind rocks or in caves on the seabed. They liked to squeeze into holes, dens, and even jars. They could spit ink to attack, steal other Pokémon's nests, and use their tough heads as weapons. Once their suckered tentacles caught an opponent, they could hold it fast, batter it with headbutts, and finish it when it could no longer move.

Their most famous weapon was the black ink they fired from their mouths to blind an opponent. Some people even used that ink in cooking. Ethan had no idea how it tasted; few people ate it.

Octillery could shoot water from its mouth just as Remoraid could. Even so, the two looked so unlike each other that people had long doubted Octillery evolved from Remoraid, preferring to believe it had always looked this way.

May had probably never battled one, and she knew little about it. That did not stop her from opening with a fierce attack.

Munchlax gathered its energy into its right hand for Focus Punch. Its fist shone like a lamp, and its round body charged toward Octillery with heavy strides.

Harley smiled faintly, unruffled, and gave his order. Octillery sprang high, landed squarely on Munchlax, and wrapped several tentacles around its head, locking it in a hold.

Munchlax tried to shake it loose but could not. With its sight blocked, it staggered like a drunk. It tried to swing its glowing fist upward and knock Octillery off, but Octillery was too slippery to hit. The display showing Munchlax's Contest points lost a small segment.

Octillery was not finished. Still clinging to Munchlax, it puffed up its long mouth and fired Octazooka straight at its head from point-blank range.

Boom!

The direct blow struck a sensitive spot and sent Munchlax falling backward. Even the audience heard the explosion. When it hit the floor, its Contest points dropped again.

The match had barely begun, yet Munchlax was already at a disadvantage May had never imagined. Ethan watched from the stands without changing expression. Harley was no easy opponent, and May had never faced a Pokémon quite like this one. Ethan had known the battle would be hard.

Harley had clearly trained Octillery well. Its performance suggested he had kept it as a surprise weapon.

May seemed to understand the danger. When Munchlax got up, she did not send it close again. She ordered Solar Beam, hoping to strike from a distance.

Harley gave another small smile. At his command, Octillery spat several Sludge Bombs. They hit Munchlax before it could fire, splattering over its mouth. Solar Beam was forced back and burst against Munchlax itself.

Harley did not miss the chance to mock May. From his seat, Ethan could not hear the words, but they were unlikely to be kind.

Ethan looked at May again. Octillery had shown how powerful and difficult it was, and Harley was trying to rattle her as well. What would she do now?

He had helped Munchlax train yesterday, but strength could not be built in a single afternoon. They had practiced a response to Rest. May still had to find a chance to use it—and survive until then.

After the two setbacks, May was shaken. She steadied herself quickly and told Munchlax to use Metronome.

It was a gamble on an unknown move, relying entirely on luck. Yet it might be her only way out of this position.

Harley's arrogance surfaced again. He stood with a hand on his hip and watched coldly. To him, Metronome was a desperate, dangerous choice that might hurt its user as easily as help. May must have run out of ideas.

Munchlax finished the move while everyone watched. A soft white light faded. Apart from Munchlax's rather stylish motions, nothing happened.

Confused murmurs spread through the stands. What had Metronome selected? Why was there no visible effect? Harley burst out laughing.

While he was still mocking May, a thick beam of energy suddenly dropped from above. Electricity crackled loudly as it engulfed Octillery, leaving it scorched in an instant.

Now the crowd understood. Metronome had worked, calling down a powerful Electric-type attack. Against Octillery, the hit was devastating. It wiped away the advantage Harley had built, putting the Pokémon back on nearly equal footing. May's gamble had paid off.

Harley ordered Octillery to use Rest.

Rest was a Psychic-type move. Used on oneself, it could restore all lost energy; used against a target, it could put that Pokémon to sleep. As a means of recovery, it was formidable. Provided its user was not knocked out while the move took effect, its energy could return in an instant and its other ailments could disappear as well.

Ethan smiled in the stands. Just as in the anime, Octillery had turned to Rest. Powerful as the move looked, it had a weakness. Ethan had prepared Munchlax to exploit it. Now he would see what it had learned.

May did not know what Ethan had practiced. During yesterday's training, most of her attention had been on Squirtle's appeal. She had not seen him concentrate on countering Rest. When Octillery went to sleep, she responded in her own way and ordered Munchlax to attack.

Munchlax hurried forward on its short legs and used Tackle against the apparently motionless Octillery.

Thump!

It put its full weight into the collision. But Octillery had anchored several tentacles firmly to the floor. For all the force of the hit, it did not budge.

May was surprised but did not give up. She ordered Focus Punch. Munchlax's white-glowing fist drove upward into Octillery's chin. Again, its grip on the floor kept the blow from having the effect May wanted.

Rest was nearly complete. Two attacks had failed, and May grew anxious. If Octillery woke with its strength fully restored, Munchlax, already worn down, would almost certainly lose.

What could she do? Ideas flashed through her mind and were discarded just as quickly.

Then something changed in the arena. With no new command from May, Munchlax looked at the sleeping Octillery and remembered its practice with Ethan the previous day.

Almost on instinct, it bent down and pried Octillery's tentacles off the floor, one by one. It gathered Octillery in its arms and carried it toward the edge of the stage.

“Oh?” Lilian exclaimed. “Munchlax has picked up Octillery! What is it trying to do?”

May and Harley were just as puzzled. Then Munchlax reached the boundary, lifted Octillery high, and threw it out of the arena.

Harley's face changed. May's lit up. The audience gasped together, then broke into laughter.

There was no need to fight through all the energy Rest might restore. There was no complicated counter to work out. Once Octillery landed out of bounds, it would be disqualified, however healthy it became. That was Ethan's simple and effective answer.

“Octillery, wake up!” Harley shouted in panic.

But Rest needed a little longer to finish. Until it did, Harley could not rouse Octillery, and Octillery could not simply choose to abandon the move halfway through without a special method.

It fell lower and lower. Harley watched helplessly as it landed beneath the stage with a thud.

A white flash crossed Octillery's body, and it opened its eyes. Rest had succeeded. Its energy and condition were as good as when the battle began. Yet it was staring up at Munchlax from an unfamiliar place.

How did I get here? Wasn't I on the stage? How did one nap put me down below it?

The audience laughed again at its bewildered expression. No one had expected a Contest final to end like this.

May was the most delighted of all. When she had run out of ideas, Munchlax had found an answer on its own. She had never imagined it would do that.

At the judges' table, the three judges exchanged looks, checked the remaining time and Contest points, and shook their heads with wry smiles. Neither the clock nor either Pokémon's points had run out. But the rule was clear: leaving the arena decided the match.

“Octillery is out of bounds! Munchlax wins!” Lilian announced brightly. “Congratulations to May, the winner of the Wisteria Town Contest!”

Harley's expression could not have been darker. He had expected Octillery to finish recovering in another moment and then win. Instead, Munchlax had simply thrown it off the stage.

It was nothing like May's usual battle style. He remembered the brief surprise on her face when Munchlax picked Octillery up. An idea occurred to him, and he turned toward the audience.

There were so many people in the stands, yet his eyes found one handsome young man almost at once. Ethan had his head tipped slightly back and was looking at Harley with the faintest mocking glint in his eyes.
