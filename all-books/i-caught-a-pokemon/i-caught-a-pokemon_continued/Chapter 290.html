"Chikorita! Are you all right?"

Casey's face changed when she saw how hard Chikorita had been hit. She felt a pang of worry. They had traveled together for only two or three days, but they had already formed a close bond. They had battled wild Pokémon all along the way and won each time without much trouble. Chikorita had never taken a blow like this.

"Hang in there! Use Growl!"

Chikorita shook off its dizziness and let out a cute cry. Growl was a basic move many Pokémon could use. It did no damage, but it weakened the opponent's attacks, which could be useful in its own way.

The sound reached Dratini's ears. There was no way for it to shut out the cry completely. Dratini looked unchanged, but Ethan knew its Attack had fallen.

His expression did not shift. From what he had seen, Chikorita was still inexperienced and knew few moves. It kept returning to Tackle and Razor Leaf. Dratini was stronger and had a wider range of attacks.

"Lower Attack or not, we can keep going. Dratini, Dragon Rage!"

Energy gathered inside Dratini. A moment later, a shock wave burst from its mouth and struck Chikorita before it had a chance to dodge. The little Pokémon was thrown to the ground.

After taking two powerful hits in succession, Chikorita had no strength left to struggle. It was unable to battle.

It was Ethan's first battle against another Trainer with Dratini, but it had spent plenty of time at his side and had trained well. Both Pokémon were still at an early stage. As Ethan had expected, Dratini's superior strength had been enough to defeat Casey's Chikorita easily.

Sabrina glanced at the fallen Pokémon and announced, "Chikorita is unable to battle. Dratini wins."

"Chikorita!"

Casey ran forward and scooped it into her arms. Thankfully, it had only fainted and was not badly hurt. She let out a relieved breath, then looked up at Ethan.

"I lost..." Her voice was full of disappointment.

After watching Ethan compete in the Indigo League, she knew he had many formidable Pokémon. Sending out Dratini had already been a special consideration for her. She still could not win. The gap between them was enormous.

"You did well," Ethan told her. "You lost this time, but Chikorita has a lot of room to grow. Before long, I'm sure it can become a strong Pokémon that holds its own in battle."

Casey was naturally cheerful. His encouragement helped her recover quickly.

"You're right! Chikorita will get stronger!" She recalled it to its Poké Ball and looked enviously at Dratini. "Your Dratini is amazing, though. My Chikorita couldn't keep up at all!"

Ethan stepped over and stroked Dratini's forehead in reward. "Don't rush. Your journey has only just begun. I'm sure you'll catch plenty of rare partners yourself."

Casey hesitated. "Maybe. But I really like Pokémon with yellow markings, like Pikachu, Beedrill, and Electabuzz. I've always dreamed of making a whole team of them!"

Sabrina finally asked, "Why are you so set on Pokémon with yellow markings?"

Casey rubbed her nose and grinned proudly. "Because my whole family supports the Electabuzz baseball team! Electabuzz is yellow, so I want lots of Pokémon with yellow markings!"

That was her reason? Ethan looked at the yellow stripes covering Casey's clothes and shook his head. She was a truly devoted fan.

"Oh, right," Casey said, looking up at him again. "You came to Johto to challenge the Silver League, didn't you?"

"That's right. I need to collect eight badges before I can enter."

Her eyes brightened and she clenched her fist. "Then I'm going to enter the Silver Conference too!"

"You too? That's great." Ethan nodded. "You said you watched my matches, didn't you? Going from spectator to competitor is a fine change."

"Heh." Her eyes seemed to shine. "I'll start challenging Gyms, one at a time. By the time we meet in the tournament, I'll be much stronger than I am today. I'll surprise you!"

Violet City was a place with the charm of an older age.

Built on a hill at a bend in the river, it was a sizable city, yet forests covered much of it. Greenery was everywhere, giving the whole place a close connection to nature.

After parting from Casey, Ethan and Sabrina passed through the Mysterious Forest and Donphan Valley. They finally reached Violet City at noon the next day.

In the city, they occasionally glimpsed wild Pokémon among the dense trees. It was plainly another place where people and Pokémon lived together in harmony.

Unfamiliar with the streets, Ethan opened the map on his phone again. Its description told him that Violet City had a Trainer School, where children learned about Pokémon in hopes of becoming excellent Trainers.

At the northern end of the city stood a great swaying tower, with many monks practicing inside. Sprout Tower was one of Violet City's best-known sights, and visitors invariably went to take a look.

The city's most famous destination, though, was Violet Gym. It was an official Pokémon League Gym specializing in Flying types. Its leader was Falkner, and Trainers who defeated him earned the Zephyr Badge.

Following the directions, Ethan and Sabrina crossed the city to the Gym. When Ethan reached the end of the street, a strange building came into view.

Violet Gym rose as a ten-story, pale purple tower. Windows covered its walls, which spiraled upward. Fitting for a Flying-type Gym, the battlefield appeared to be on the roof.
