As the conversation went on, both men's eyes came to rest on Ethan. He raised his brows.

Kurt considered for a moment, then nodded to Professor Elm. "You're right. Keeping the GS Ball here may not be wise."

By now, Ethan could hardly fail to understand what they meant.

They're going to give it to me?

Before he could speak, Kurt continued, "Now that we know what the GS Ball was made for, there's no real need to study it further. It does no good sitting with me."

He turned to Ethan and said solemnly, "So I'm giving this Poké Ball to you."

"I'm not sure I should accept..." Ethan slowly shook his head. "I can't take something so valuable."

Kurt smiled, picked up the GS Ball, and placed it directly in Ethan's hand.

"I'm only a craftsman. I can't protect this Poké Ball. The fact that you came here with Elm tells me he trusts your character. And winning the Indigo League proves what you're capable of. I can feel at ease leaving it with you."

"But..."

Ethan certainly wanted the GS Ball. Still, he had only just met Kurt. They were neither friends nor family; how could he accept such a gift without feeling awkward?

He looked to Professor Elm.

Elm met his gaze with a smile and nodded.

"Mr. Kurt is right. Take it. Neither of us is a Trainer or a collector. The GS Ball has no use sitting with us. It belongs in the hands of a Trainer like you, where it can serve its purpose!"

And what was a Poké Ball's purpose?

To catch Pokémon, of course!

Erika, too, watched Ethan encouragingly.

In the past, she would not have wanted a Poké Ball capable of catching a Legendary Pokémon to fall into a Trainer's hands. Now, though...

"Since Mr. Kurt and Professor Elm both agree, you should take it," she said softly.

Ethan glanced at her. He stopped protesting and accepted the GS Ball.

"Thank you, Mr. Kurt."

Professor Ivy had first found the ball in the Orange Islands. Ash had carried it to Professor Oak, and from Oak it had gone to Professor Elm. Ethan had never expected it to end up in his own hands.

After all those twists and turns, he was the one who benefited!

With a worry lifted from his shoulders, Kurt smiled in relief.

He arranged dinner and treated Professor Elm, Ethan, and Erika as honored guests.

Ethan had hoped to challenge the Azalea Gym while there was still daylight. But Kurt's hospitality was hard to refuse, and after receiving such a valuable Poké Ball, he wanted to thank the man properly.

That evening, drinks passed around the dinner table. With frank, adorable little Maizie there too, the mood was cheerful.

Kurt drank plenty. He even coaxed Professor Elm, who could not hold his liquor, into drinking enough to get thoroughly drunk.

After dinner, Kurt would not let his guests leave. He arranged for them to spend the night in his house.

Perhaps mistaking Ethan and Erika for a couple, Kurt's daughter-in-law did not even ask. She assigned them the same room!

When they came out of the bath and saw two futons laid close together on the tatami, they froze and looked at each other.

Outside, the moon hung high. After the noise of dinner, the house was quiet.

"This..." Ethan, dressed in a bathrobe, scratched his head. "Should we ask them for another room? Or I could sleep in Professor Elm's room..."

Erika's cheeks were faintly red. Perhaps it was the few drinks she had had with dinner, or perhaps something else.

She drew a breath. "Let's not. It's late, and I don't want to trouble them. When you're traveling, you can't insist on everything being just so."

Traveling? We're guests in someone's house!

But Ethan thought she had a point. Kurt and his family were already asleep, and it would be rude to disturb them so late.

Besides, he and Erika had faced situations like this more than once or twice. There was no reason to be embarrassed. During the Whirl Cup, they had even stayed in the same suite.

That suite had had two separate rooms, though. Now they were sharing one!

If they slept here like this, wouldn't everyone else take it as proof that they were a couple?

But the two of them...

While his thoughts circled, Erika went inside and began arranging her belongings. Then she moved their futons a little farther apart.

Well... not much farther. They went from nearly touching to about half a meter apart.

What difference would that make?

Perhaps embarrassed, Erika said as soon as she had finished, "It's late, and we have the Gym challenge tomorrow. We should get some sleep."

Without looking at Ethan, she slipped under her covers. All he could see was the faint outline of her graceful figure beneath the bedding.

Ethan sighed at the sight.

Erika was not a casual woman. She was a young lady from a good family, traditional in her habits and values.

She might be lively and playful in her youth, but at heart she was the very image of a refined, traditional woman.

And now she was willing to...

Ethan shook his head and pushed the thought away. He went into the room.

If Erika said she did not mind, what was he hesitating over?

He turned off the light and slid into his own futon.

Moonlight shone through the glass onto Erika, keeping the room from going completely dark.

Despite the drinks, Ethan tossed and turned. With Erika so close, he could not sleep.

He turned toward her. From where he lay, he could see only her hair and the little patch of pale neck showing above her covers.

Was she asleep?

She lay still and seemed to breathe evenly, but he suspected she was just as awake as he was.

The room was quiet except for their breathing.

Ethan found himself remembering all the time he had spent with Erika. Anyone could see that she had feelings for him beyond friendship. He had felt it himself.

That was no baseless confidence. Their experiences together had made it clear!

In their everyday glances and gestures, they were hardly acting like two people with nothing between them.

He was certain Erika understood how he felt, too.

For now, they were both holding those feelings back.

For an ordinary man and woman who had been through so much together, growing closer day by day, sharing a room like this would have been the natural next step.

Tonight might even have been the perfect chance to define their relationship!

But...

Sabrina came to mind, and Ethan sighed.

He had a girlfriend. How could he betray Sabrina's feelings?

Then he thought of the night he had spent with Maya and sighed again.

Ah. I'd almost forgotten. I already betrayed her once...
