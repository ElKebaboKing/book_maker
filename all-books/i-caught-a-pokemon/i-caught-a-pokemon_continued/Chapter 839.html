At the edge of the forest, Solana and Lunick saw the green light sweep across the trees as soon as Ethan's Celebi released it. They had been walking for only seven or eight minutes and had not gone very far.

“It's Celebi! He really found it!” Solana recognized the Mythical Pokémon's power. She turned back at once, but Lunick caught her arm.

“Wait. Ethan told us to stay away until he called.”

“What are we waiting for?” she asked. “If he's found Celebi, he's done what he promised. Every minute we delay, it might fly away—or it might be weak and waiting for our help.”

Lunick hesitated. She had a point. Solana was not trying to break her word lightly. Their priorities differed from Ethan's. They had been sent by Ranger headquarters to find Celebi and restore the forest. The vines and thorns had vanished, and Celebi had appeared. Why wait?

“But we did promise him…” Lunick said.

“Maybe he forgot to contact us. Besides, this is Celebi. Aren't you worried that he sent us away so he could catch it for himself?”

The secrecy of Ethan's request invited that suspicion. Lunick did not think Ethan would exploit an injured Pokémon, but he finally gave in.

“All right. We'll go back quietly. But we observe from a distance first. We can't charge up to him, and we absolutely mustn't offend him.”

“I know,” Solana said, rolling her eyes. “I'm not reckless. I only want a quick look.”

They hurried back along the trail.

…

After several bowls of noodles, the wild Celebi was plainly feeling better. It had grown fond of the man who had fed it and became bolder, even alighting on Ethan's palm.

“It looks much better. Do you think it's out of danger?” May smiled and reached over to stroke it. Its body felt odd: soft, yet textured almost like grass.

“Your treatment helped a great deal,” Ethan said. “All that traveling with Ash and the others wasn't wasted. You're good at tending Pokémon now.”

May smiled shyly. “I learned most of it by watching Brock.”

Gengar suddenly poked its head out of Ethan's shadow. The wild Celebi startled and flew a little higher. Gengar ignored its reaction and frowned at Ethan.

“I sense someone coming. I think those two are back.”

Ethan showed no surprise. Solana and Lunick did not know him well enough to trust him fully, and he did not know them well enough to trust them either. That was why he had asked May to stand watch. He had not expected them to return quite so soon, though. They must have turned back the moment they saw Celebi's light.

It no longer mattered. The treatment was finished.

Ethan recalled his Celebi into the GS Ball, and Gengar slipped back into his shadow. Barely ten seconds later, he heard faint footsteps among the nearby trees.

“Ethan…” May sounded worried. She did not know how they should handle this.

“Stay where you are,” he said, giving her a reassuring look. “Act normally. And use that Styler to signal them.”

May took Lunick's Capture Styler and used the method he had shown them to contact him. A receiving beep sounded close by. It was loud enough for Ethan and May to hear, and it left the Rangers hiding there thoroughly embarrassed.

There was no point concealing themselves now. Solana and Lunick came out of the trees.

“Um…” Neither could find a good explanation.

Ethan did not bother to wait for one. He opened his hands. Startled again by their sudden arrival, the wild Celebi rose into the sky and flew away.

“Celebi!” Solana called after it, but a few beats of its wings carried it out of sight.

Ethan had deliberately allowed it to leave. That was not how he described it.

“If only you'd arrived a little later,” he said with a rueful shake of his head. “We've treated its injuries, but we hadn't built enough trust yet. Now it's flown away.”

May hid a smile. Ethan himself had told her to contact them; now he was complaining they had come too early. It was an excuse meant to tease them.

Solana and Lunick could tell as well. That made the encounter more awkward.

“I'm sorry,” Lunick said at last. “When we saw the green light, we knew you'd found Celebi. We worried something might happen and came back on our own. Please don't hold it against us.”

“Hold it against you?” Ethan's tone gave little away. “I would have stepped in to help Celebi whether you were here or not. As for what you did…” He sighed. “Never mind. I'm used to it.”

He said no more. He gathered the litter from the ground, shouldered his bag, and walked away.

May returned the Styler to Lunick, nodded to both Rangers, then hurried after Ethan. Solana and Lunick watched them disappear into the forest. For a while neither spoke.

Lunick finally patted Solana's shoulder with a wry smile. “We've left him with a poor impression of us. Don't dwell on it. The matter here is settled, and we still have a long report to write.”

…

Meeting Solana, Lunick, and the wild Celebi had been a brief interruption to the journey. Ethan and May put it behind them as they left the forest. Only May kept wondering where the Celebi would go next.

They reached Fuchsia City without further trouble. Night was approaching, so they did not go straight to the Battle Pike outside town. They stayed at the city's Pokémon Center first.

After dinner, May asked Nurse Joy to examine her Egg. Several days had passed since the family at the breeding farm had given it to her. They had said it would hatch soon, and May wanted to make sure her travels had not harmed it.

Nurse Joy checked it carefully. “Everything looks fine,” she said with a smile. “You've cared for it well. The shell is clean and smooth, with no cracks, and everything inside appears normal. It should hatch into a healthy Pokémon.”

May let out a breath. “That's a relief. I've been worried that all this traveling might be bad for it.”

“Keep a close watch on it anyway,” Nurse Joy said. “I expect it to hatch within the next day or two.”

“That soon?” May's eyes brightened. “I understand. I'll take good care of it!”

…

After a night's rest, they set out for the Battle Pike the next morning. It stood on a hill not far outside Fuchsia City, with the sea visible from its grounds.

The facility's shape was striking. A gigantic Seviper seemed to be sprawled across the building. From a distance it looked almost alive, and a little unnerving. Its unusual design spoke to the Frontier Brain's particular taste.

Ethan and May followed a long stone path to the structure and knocked on its great doors. Before long, three young women in their twenties came out. Their brightly dyed hair and clothes gave them the look of a rough street gang.

“Seriously? A challenger this early? Can't we get any sleep?” one complained, rubbing her head.

“That's your hangover, not the challenger's fault,” another said.

“Only because you kept pouring me drinks!”

The woman in front had blond hair, a long white coat over a red crop top, and an exposed midriff. Her voice was husky, perhaps from smoking, and she had a peculiar rolling way of speaking. The other two wore different outfits but had much the same style and manner. Faint smells of tobacco and alcohol drifted out behind them.

May, unused to women like these and put off by their argument, took a step back.

Before Ethan could speak, all three noticed his face and brightened.

“Well, where did this handsome one come from?”

“We've never had a challenger this good-looking!”

They began appraising and teasing him right in front of him. Ethan disliked dealing with people like this. If he answered, they might take the invitation to crowd him. If he ignored them, they might only try harder.

A steadier woman's voice came from inside and rescued him. “You're making too much noise. What's going on?”

A tall woman with long black hair stepped through the doorway. She seemed about twenty-seven or twenty-eight and stood a head above the other three. Her narrow eyes and single eyelids gave her a sharp, cool look. Ethan recognized the Frontier Brain of this facility: Pike Queen Lucy.

They had never met in person, but he knew a good deal about her from the anime. What he remembered best was her taste in Pokémon—and in people.

Lucy favored serpentine Pokémon. She also liked narrow-eyed faces. In the anime, Brock's declaration of love had genuinely moved her. At home, she kept Abra, Snorlax, Cyndaquil, Swinub, Makuhita, and Skitty, all with the same narrow-eyed look. Brock happened to share it. Few mature women in the anime had returned his affection, which made Lucy especially memorable to Ethan.

He wondered whether she had met Brock yet.

Once Lucy appeared, the three women who had teased Ethan quieted down. Ethan stepped up and explained why he had come.

His introduction was unnecessary. Lucy recognized him and knew from the other Frontier Brains that he was challenging their facilities. She folded her arms and smiled.

“Greta told me about you. I thought it'd take you a few more days to get here, but you're already at my door. The other Frontier Brains didn't keep you busy for long, did they?”
