After they swam several dozen meters farther upward, the water around them grew steadily brighter. It was as if two powerful beams had pierced the sea and illuminated everything below.

With visibility improving, Ethan finally felt a tightness in his chest. He asked Erika for the respirator and took several deep breaths.

He had not kept track of the time, so he could not say precisely how long he had gone without it. Still, there was no question that he could hold his breath much longer than before!

By his rough estimate, the time had doubled. It was not an enormous change, but it was enough to let him swim underwater for considerably longer.

They continued upward. At last, they broke through the rolling surface and breathed open air again.

Only then did Ethan realize that the moon was high overhead. Night had fallen completely!

The two bright beams they had seen underwater came from the searchlights of two police patrol boats!

When the officers saw them surface, someone lowered a rope ladder from the nearer boat. Ethan helped Erika onto it first, then climbed after her. Once he left the sea, however, the air felt bitterly cold. His clothes were soaked, and even the light breeze made him shiver.

He endured the chill and climbed toward the top. Several pairs of hands reached down and pulled them aboard.

In the poor light, Ethan could make out only figures gathered around him. Before he could see their faces, someone draped a thick towel over his shoulders.

Its warmth chased away the cold, making him feel almost as though he had been given a new life.

As his eyes adjusted to the darkness, he recognized Ash, Misty, Brock, Richie, and Oliver. Even Anabel was there!

Noticing his look, Anabel gave a soft laugh. "It seems you ran into trouble down there. I could feel the tremors from the seabed even while I was aboard the boat."

"We did have some trouble." Ethan shook his head with a rueful smile. "Those mad Team Rocket scientists set off explosions in the base. Erika and I nearly died in there. Worse, we couldn't catch them. They escaped."

He told the group what had happened below, including the research into the virus.

Ash and the others exchanged astonished looks. None of them had expected Team Rocket to use a diversion to conceal its real purpose!

Misty's eyes shone with admiration. "It's a good thing you insisted on staying, Ethan. Otherwise, we'd never have learned what Team Rocket was hiding!"

Richie scratched his cheek, looking a little embarrassed.

Before the base sank, he too had assumed there was nothing more to the situation. Now he saw how far he still lagged behind this Champion in both strength and experience.

"I only stayed to test a hunch," Ethan said with a sigh. "I happened to be right this time. By the way, how did you all end up together?"

"We were worried about you, of course, so I arranged two boats to support you as quickly as I could," Anabel said with another smile. "As for me, after we landed and handed over the Team Rocket members we'd arrested, I immediately boarded another helicopter. I only arrived here a little while ago."

At the mention of Team Rocket, Ethan hurriedly looked around. "What happened to the members who escaped the base?"

Anabel stepped aside, revealing officers hard at work across the deck.

In one corner, dozens of soaked Team Rocket members crouched in handcuffs. The International Police officers searched and registered them one by one, then escorted the dejected prisoners into the cabin.

Everything proceeded in an orderly manner, another sign of Anabel's excellent command.

"When we arrived, they were just escaping from the base," she explained. "They were all floating on the surface, looking miserable, with no strength left to fight. We pulled them aboard and arrested them without any trouble!"

Ethan was relieved they had not escaped. Then another thought occurred to him. "Did you catch Cassidy and Butch?"

"No." Anabel shook her head regretfully. "We were about to arrest them when a submarine shaped like a Magikarp came out of nowhere and carried them away."

A Magikarp submarine?

Ethan immediately thought of the Team Rocket trio.

They were the only people he knew who traveled around in that homemade Magikarp submarine, causing trouble wherever they went.

But Jessie, James, and Meowth were bitter rivals of Cassidy and Butch. Why would they rescue them?

I can't make sense of that...

"There's nothing we can do now," Ethan said with a sigh. "Those two are persistent, and they're important members of Team Rocket. It would have been good to capture them too."

Anabel was more satisfied with the outcome. "Even so, this operation was a great success. We've dealt Team Rocket another severe blow and weakened them considerably. A few more operations like this, and we may be able to clear their forces out of Johto entirely!"

She studied Ethan appreciatively and added with a half-joking smile, "To tell the truth, I'm growing more impressed with you. Looker was right. You're practically our lucky charm!"

...

At daybreak, after spending the night resting aboard the police patrol boat, Ethan and his companions said goodbye to Anabel. They boarded Oliver's speedboat and set out for the Temple of the Sea on Red Rock Isle.

The matter was settled for now. It was time to return to Red Rock Isle and report to Maya.

In just two days, they had wrecked Team Rocket's base, helped capture a great many of its members, and thwarted its attempt to seize Lugia. Silver and its mother could finally live here without fearing that Team Rocket would disturb them.

They had originally intended to part ways with Oliver. The boy insisted on visiting the Temple of the Sea, however, because he wanted to see the beautiful Sea Priestess Maya for himself. He called his mother and received her permission, so Ethan's group brought him along to Red Rock Isle.

Richie naturally wanted to come and have a look too.

He had come here to train, and the Temple of the Sea was the most famous place in the Whirl Islands. He regretted missing the tournament a few days earlier, but seeing the temple and its surroundings would still be a worthwhile experience.
