At 7:26 that evening, Ethan and Erika strolled among the crowds on Ecruteak City's bustling pedestrian street.

A hazy half-moon hung high in the sky, hidden behind dark clouds and unable to illuminate the city.

Fortunately, colorful lights and glowing signs of every size shone above the stalls, driving away the darkness and bathing the visitors in dazzling light.

People streamed back and forth along the pedestrian street. From time to time, some stopped with their Pokémon at one stall or another.

Conversation, laughter, and the cries of stall owners rose and fell all around them. Yet the street did not feel noisy. Instead, the sounds created a wonderfully lively atmosphere.

Ecruteak truly deserved its place among Johto's greatest cities. Even on an ordinary night, it drew such a large crowd. Street performances featuring people working with Pokémon could be seen nearby as well, making the area exceptionally lively.

Ethan walked slowly with the crowd, his head lowered.

He held a stick of cotton candy in one hand and occasionally took a bite, while operating his phone with the other.

Beside him, Erika also carried a variety of snacks bought from the stalls. After sampling each one, her brows relaxed slightly.

Perhaps to follow the local customs, she had changed into a splendid kimono. Her exquisite features and outstanding bearing made passersby turn their heads one after another.

However...

Compared with her own cooking, these foods could hardly be called especially delicious. In an atmosphere like this, though, enjoying them once in a while was still quite pleasant.

A burst of playful laughter reached her ears.

She stopped and turned her head. A group of children wearing masks and carrying lollipops ran past her, laughing and playing.

Realizing they had nearly collided with the beautiful young woman, the children stopped and hurriedly apologized.

"Don't run around in the street. Be careful, all right?"

Erika gently reassured them, completely unbothered. When she saw that Ethan had already walked some distance ahead, she waved goodbye to the children, quickened her pace, and caught up with him.

Ethan had not noticed the small incident. His finger was tapping rapidly across his phone. A moment later, he gave it a triumphant flourish and held the screen up toward Erika.

"I never expected tickets for the Ecruteak Dance Theater to be this hard to get. I spent more than half an hour trying online, and my finger nearly went numb. I finally got lucky when someone canceled their tickets!"

Erika tucked her hair behind her ear and smiled.

"It can't be helped. We arrived late. If we'd made reservations a few days ago, we certainly could have gotten good seats. Look at it another way, though. It shows just how popular the Kimono Sisters really are, doesn't it?"

"That's true." Ethan nodded. "Thinking about it that way, getting tickets at all before the show starts makes us very lucky!"

As they spoke, the two reached the end of the road and stopped before a magnificent, finely constructed wooden building.

The three-story Japanese-style building had eight colorful flags planted out front, fluttering softly in the breeze. More than a dozen lanterns hung beneath its eaves. Lit by candles and the glow from within, the entire building looked resplendent!

A signboard also hung directly above the entrance.

Ethan looked up and saw four large characters on it: Ecruteak Dance Theater.

"We're here. This is the place."

Before entering, Ethan glanced at the noticeboard outside.

It displayed the program for tonight's performance and also served as its poster.

Looking more closely, he saw that the two Legendary Pokémon Lugia and Ho-Oh had been painted on it. In the composition, they seemed to chase each other while remaining in balance, forming a circle together.

One was a Water-type and the other a Fire-type. Their opposing elements appeared to symbolize life's endless renewal and continuing cycle.

In one corner, a title had been written in calligraphy: Stage Play—The Legend of the Burned Tower.

So that's what they're performing...

Ethan felt slightly disappointed.

They had already learned the general outline of the local legend from their travel guide, so the story naturally held little novelty for either of them. His interest immediately faded.

It was partly his own fault. He had been in such a rush while trying to secure the tickets that he had not checked tonight's program.

But they had already come all this way and bought the tickets. They could hardly turn around and leave now, could they?

Ethan brought up the electronic tickets for a staff member to inspect, then the two entered the Ecruteak Dance Theater.

They crossed the lobby, removed their shoes, and followed the other audience members down a long wooden corridor before finally reaching the performance hall.

Tonight's performance was completely sold out.

In addition to the main seating area before the stage, two levels of side seating rose on either side. Altogether, the hall contained at least seven or eight hundred places.

With the performance about to begin, every one of them was occupied, forming a dense sea of people.

Following the numbers on their electronic tickets, Ethan and Erika climbed to the second-floor side gallery and found their places.

Although they were called seats, each place consisted of nothing more than a cushion.

Following everyone else's example, Ethan knelt on his cushion beside Erika and waited quietly.

Their places on the second-floor side gallery were a little off-center and did not offer the best possible angle, but the view was excellent. The entire stage lay unobstructed before him.

Considering that he had obtained the tickets just before the performance, he was already quite satisfied with their location.

Cool air from the air conditioner flowed over his head. Although the venue was packed with people, it did not feel stuffy or hot.

Looking down at the main seating area below, Ethan saw that most of the audience consisted of young people and children. Many had brought their Pokémon, while elderly spectators were rare.

The older residents here have probably heard this story so many times that they're tired of it...

After glancing around casually, Ethan suddenly let out a soft sound of surprise.

Erika looked at him.

"What is it?"

Ethan said nothing. Erika followed his gaze and saw a man dressed as a monk kneeling among the audience.

A Bellsprout sat beside him, apparently his Pokémon.

Is he a monk visiting from Violet City?

I never expected a monk to come here for a stage performance. That's certainly unusual.

Much like monks in modern Japan, the monks in this world could eat meat, marry and have children, and even own businesses.

Therefore, while Erika found his presence surprising, she did not consider it particularly strange.

The performance time drew closer and closer.

The curtain had yet to rise, but Ethan's sharp eyes caught sight of a side door opening a narrow crack. A girl was peering out through it, apparently sneaking a look at the audience's reaction.

Is she a member of the staff? Or one of the performers?

Ethan studied her curiously. Perhaps sensing his gaze, the girl suddenly looked in his direction.

Their eyes met. Like a startled fawn, she immediately ducked back out of sight.
