The attacker was, of course, Vicious.

Both mechanical claws had been knocked aside. Vicious guided his enormous spider-shaped robot out from behind the trees, with Jessie, James, and Meowth still close beside him.

Yesterday’s failure had not made him give up on the trio. He had kept them with him.

“Did you think you could escape?” Vicious snarled. “After a night’s rest, it’s time for round two!”

“Hand over Celebi!” Jessie shouted.

“And Pikachu!” Meowth added.

The trio sounded fierce, but their bravado was thin. Yesterday they had attacked with confidence and lost anyway. Even with a senior executive on their side, they now knew better than to assume victory.

They had stayed because they were unwilling to let the chance go and wanted one more try.

If it ended as yesterday’s attack had, they would abandon Vicious without a second thought and run.

“You really won’t give up!” Sammy frowned. He released Charmeleon again, and Ash and the others hurried to bring out their Pokémon to guard the group.

“I advise you to stop this pointless resistance.” Vicious showed no fear. He released Dark Tyranitar once more.

“Roar!”

As soon as it appeared, Dark Tyranitar fired another Hyper Beam. No one could stand against that force. Ethan and the others scattered to dodge.

The beam broke their formation.

Jessie and James seized the opening, sending their Pokémon to attack. In an instant, the forest was thrown into chaos again.

But Vicious’s plan was different today. He did not waste time battling the people in his way. He took out a Dark Ball and threw it directly at Celebi.

“Look out!”

Ash had been watching him. The moment the ball left Vicious’s hand, Ash flung his backpack into the air. It struck the Dark Ball and knocked it aside.

He could protect Celebi once, but not twice.

Vicious calmly drew another Dark Ball and threw it. This time Ash could not interfere. Black light burst from the ball and wrapped around Celebi as it flew above them.

Whatever power the Dark Ball held, Celebi could not break free. The light swallowed it, and an instant later it was trapped inside.

“Celebi!”

Sammy stared in horror. It had happened too fast. Charmeleon had just dodged Dark Tyranitar’s attack and could not help him stop it.

“Hahaha!” Vicious laughed, barely able to contain his joy. “Prey never escapes a hunter. Celebi is mine!”

“Stop!”

Ash charged recklessly at the robot. Somehow, with the agility of a Mankey, he scrambled up one of its legs and lunged for Vicious.

Vicious was still absorbed in his triumph. Ash managed to reach him, and they grappled in the cockpit.

Vicious was an adult and kicked Ash away. But when he looked at his hand, it was empty. The boy had snatched the Dark Ball.

“Damn brat!”

He jumped down from the cockpit. Before Ash could get up, Vicious took the ball back and kicked him aside.

“You pests are a headache. If you care so much about Celebi, I’ll use it against you!”

He threw the Dark Ball. “Come out, Dark Celebi! Show them your true power!”

Black light flashed, and Celebi appeared before them again.

Its eyes were black now, and a frightening dark aura surrounded its body.

“Celebi, drive them away!”

At Vicious’s command, Celebi swept an arm through the air. A powerful blast of wind erupted around it, hurling Ash upward and washing over Ethan and the others so fiercely they could barely open their eyes.

Ethan crossed his arms to shield his face. Seeing Ash flying high above them, he called, “Gengar! Catch Ash!”

Gengar shot from Ethan’s shadow, caught Ash, and carried him safely to the ground.

“Celebi! Wake up!” Sammy shouted. “Don’t let that power control you!”

His words had no effect. Celebi looked at them with cold, distant eyes.

“It’s useless,” Vicious said with a triumphant laugh. “The Celebi you knew is gone. This is its new form. Now, Celebi, show me more of your power!”

A black barrier formed around Celebi. Then a violent whirlwind spun up with the Pokémon at its center. Branches, soil, rocks, and flowers were pulled inward. More and more debris gathered until a gigantic sphere enclosed Celebi.

The wind dragged at Ethan’s group too. They clung to nearby trees to keep from being swept away. Their heavier Pokémon could hold their ground, but smaller ones such as Pikachu would have been carried into the storm if they lost their footing.

Stopping Celebi was impossible. It took all their strength just to avoid being hurt.

When the wind weakened, the forest around them lay in ruins. Fallen trees were everywhere, as though a real hurricane had torn through.

The sphere at its center had grown to more than a hundred meters across.

Lifted by Celebi’s power, Vicious rose into the air above it.

Perhaps he had grown bored of playing with them. The sphere carried him toward the far end of the forest. Jessie, James, and Meowth dared not stay behind. They grabbed vines hanging from it and climbed aboard.

It moved north, crushing every obstacle in its way and leaving a long, ugly scar through the trees.

“What do we do now?” Misty asked, watching Vicious disappear. Half an hour earlier, everything had seemed normal. The change had come so quickly.

“What else? We go after him.” Ethan stood and helped Erika to her feet. “Vicious took Celebi. We’ll bring it back.”

“Right!” Ash nodded hard. “We can’t leave Celebi with someone like him!”

Agreed, they hurried after the sphere. They had not gone far when voices called down from above.

“Mr. Ethan! Ash!”

They looked up in surprise. A small airship was flying toward them. Three people sat aboard. Two were Towa and Diana from Arborville.

What were they doing here?

The craft came down before them, and Diana asked at once, “We saw that tornado from the village. What happened?”

“It wasn’t a tornado,” Sammy said quickly. “Celebi caused it. A Pokémon hunter captured Celebi, and we need to catch up with him!”

Everyone climbed aboard. As they traveled, Sammy explained what had happened yesterday and today.

“Those rotten Pokémon hunters!” The third person, a man steering the airship, struck its side with his fist before Towa or Diana could respond. “How could they do that to Celebi? I won’t forgive them!”

Noticing Ethan and the others looking at him, Diana made an introduction. “This is White. He lives in our village. The airship is his. When we saw the tornado, we asked him to bring us here.”

White was a man of about twenty-seven or twenty-eight, with slightly dark skin and loose, casual clothes. Ethan immediately noticed the necklace of three gold coins around his neck.

It seemed he held a position in Arborville similar to Diana’s.

“Hello.” White nodded warmly. “I handle supplies for the village. Diana and I grew up together, too.”

“Grew... grew up together?”

A cry of heartbreak came from behind Ethan. No one needed to turn to know Brock had made it.

Brock fell for one woman after another, and his fragile heart had broken yet again.

White flew after Vicious. With the airship, they no longer had to worry about falling behind. Within minutes, they were above the enormous sphere.

It had changed shape while traveling.

Almost as if it had evolved, the mass had sprouted arms, legs, a head, and wings. Made of branches and weeds, it was neither quite human nor quite Pokémon. It looked terrifying.

“Celebi!” Sammy leaned out and shouted down. “We’ve come to save you!”

Ethan thought that was a poor idea. Yelling would do nothing for Celebi and would alert Vicious to their presence.

Sure enough, Vicious scowled when he saw them pursuing him like flies he could not shake. “Celebi, bring them down!”

The plant creature raised its head, opened its huge mouth, and fired a fearsome wave of energy at the airship.

“Oh, no!”

White turned the controls hard and managed to angle the airship aside just before the blast reached them.

It still smashed a ballast compartment beneath the craft. The airship tilted and began falling toward a lake below.

“Ah!” Misty screamed. The others scrambled for something to hold. White gritted his teeth and wrestled with the controls, bringing the listing craft down onto the lake.

Only then did Ethan realize White’s airship could travel on water as well as through the air. It floated safely on the surface.

“We made it,” White said, wiping sweat from his forehead. He could not resist a little pride. “Not a bad ship, is it?”

“Stop showing off.” Diana patted his shoulder and turned to the others. “Is everyone all right?”

Before anyone could answer, the creature on the shore opened its mouth again. Another blast of energy shot toward them.

For all its frightening size, the wooden monster had no power of its own. Celebi inside it was the one attacking.

The blast arrived almost at once.

BOOM!

White’s beloved craft took the hit and exploded. The force threw Ethan and the others into the water.

Splash!

Cold lake water closed over Ethan’s head, and he sank beneath the surface.

But the man blessed by the God of the Sea could still move freely in water. After a moment’s alarm, he opened his eyes and searched around him.

He spotted Erika closest to him and swam straight to her. He caught her and brought her toward the surface.

They broke through the water together.

The airship was in pieces and burning. Nearby, Ash, Misty, and the others emerged one by one.

They swam to shore. Once there, they counted heads without bothering about their soaked clothes. Towa, Diana, and White were missing.

They were about to dive back in when the water splashed. White and Diana appeared, helping Towa between them.

White sank onto a rock and looked back at the ruined craft with a rueful shake of his head. A moment ago he had been bragging about it. Now it was gone.

Ethan looked around the lake. It seemed more and more familiar. Wasn’t this where they had camped last night?

He could even see the traces of their campsite not far away.

After all their fighting and fleeing, they had somehow ended up right back here.
