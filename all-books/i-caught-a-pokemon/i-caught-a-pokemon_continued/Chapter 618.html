Jigglypuff?

Misty stared. Had the voice really been more than her imagination? She came fully awake, hurried out of the water, and searched for the pink demon she remembered.

Listening carefully, she realized the song wasn't coming from nearby or even from inside the guesthouse. Jigglypuff seemed to be some distance away. She relaxed a little. That explained why they hadn't fallen asleep as soon as they heard it. If Jigglypuff had been any closer, they would already be out cold, just as before.

She turned to Ethan. “What should we do?”

“Go and take a look, of course.” He gritted his teeth. “Who knows how many people will suffer if we leave it alone? And it drew all over our faces last time. I haven't forgotten that!”

He sounded as though he had quite a grudge against Jigglypuff. Was he planning to catch it?

They left the spring, hurried to the changing rooms, put on their everyday clothes, and met again in the guesthouse courtyard. They also put on headphones to protect themselves from the song. The headphones couldn't block it completely, but they might at least weaken its effect.

Following the sound, they went out onto the hot spring street. It was about ten at night. The street should still have been lively, but people lay sprawled everywhere, fast asleep. They wore blissful smiles; one sleeper even had a bubble at his nose.

The singing continued and grew louder as Ethan and Misty approached the end of the street. Beneath a huge rock, they spotted a round, pink Pokémon. It had set down its microphone, taken the cap off a black marker, and was drawing on the sleepers' faces.

Jigglypuff, all right—and unmistakably the same one from the anime, the one that had followed Ash from Kanto through Johto and on to Hoenn.

If it was here, Ash must have arrived in Hoenn too. Ethan didn't know where he was yet, but he had surely started traveling the region.

To be safe, Ethan released both Gengar and Gardevoir and sent them around to flank Jigglypuff. He wasn't letting it get away this time. Then he took out Combusken's Poké Ball.

Misty had guessed correctly: he meant to catch Jigglypuff. Unlike his previous captures, he wasn't doing it to strengthen his team. He simply wanted this particular Pokémon. In all his travels, only this Jigglypuff and the Celebi in the mysterious forest had inspired that feeling.

Combusken's appearance caught Jigglypuff's attention. It had relied on its sleep-inducing song for so long that other Pokémon usually fell before they could threaten it. It gave Combusken no thought until it came close, then puffed out its cheeks and glared up at it with wide eyes.

Before Jigglypuff understood what Combusken intended, Combusken raised a clawed foot and kicked it. Jigglypuff rolled backward more than a dozen times and struck a rock.

Combusken looked at Ethan in puzzlement. Was that all? Its expression suggested it had expected a stronger opponent.

Jigglypuff had appeared many times, but it wasn't especially powerful. It knew few moves and was more of a mascot than a formidable fighter. Ethan remained cautious anyway, signaling Gengar and Gardevoir to close in from either side.

Jigglypuff swayed where it stood until its head cleared. Then it glared at the Pokémon that had attacked it for no apparent reason and ran angrily toward Combusken. It thrust out its little pink hands, aiming a Double Slap at Combusken's face.

Jigglypuff stood only fifty centimeters tall, while a Combusken was normally around ninety. It had to stretch up even to reach. After announcing its attack by charging straight in from a distance, it stood little chance of landing a hit. Combusken didn't need an order; it simply stepped back.

Jigglypuff refused to give up. It ran forward again and charged headfirst.

“Use Detect!”

A light flashed in Combusken's eyes. Jigglypuff's motion seemed to slow, every opening suddenly plain. As it came close, Combusken put a hand on its round head and guided its momentum toward a nearby rock.

Thud! Jigglypuff hit the stone and reeled. When it recovered enough to rub its aching head, it finally understood that this opponent would be hard to beat.

Without hesitation, it reached behind itself and pulled its microphone from wherever it kept it.

That was the moment Ethan had been waiting for. He caught Gardevoir's eye. Without a spoken command, she used her psychic power to snatch the microphone away.

Jigglypuff felt it leave its hand as though an invisible hand had seized it. The microphone floated toward the human man. Startled, Jigglypuff chased it, only to find Combusken blocking the way again.

“Great!” Misty breathed easier at the sight of the stolen microphone. Without it, the pink demon couldn't use that overwhelming song to put them to sleep.

“Jiggly!”

Jigglypuff puffed up with anger until it looked bigger, but anger couldn't change its predicament. Combusken's belly swelled, and it spat a burst of fire. Faced with the scorching flames and knowing it couldn't win, Jigglypuff turned to flee.

Ethan had no intention of allowing that. Gengar and Gardevoir came in from both sides and drove Jigglypuff back to where it had started.

“Combusken, Double Kick!”

Combusken sprinted up and launched two flying kicks. Both struck Jigglypuff, sending its round body through the air like a kite with its string cut. It tumbled onto the ground.

Ethan promptly threw a Poké Ball. Red light enveloped Jigglypuff and drew it in. Under Ethan and Misty's gaze, the ball rocked several times. Then another flash of red light burst out, and Jigglypuff escaped.

“Oh? It still has strength left?” Ethan was a little surprised, but it changed nothing.

He was about to tell Combusken to finish the battle when an idea occurred to him. Instead of throwing another ball, he took out Beldum's.

He released Beldum beside him. Misty had wondered why he'd stopped, but the moment she saw Beldum, she understood and couldn't help laughing.

Ethan picked Beldum up and walked toward Jigglypuff with a pleasant smile. This was a rare opportunity. Beldum knew only Tackle and normally couldn't defeat anyone, but Jigglypuff could barely fight back now. He wasn't about to waste the chance to give Beldum a win.

Besides, he'd done this sort of thing once before. And Jigglypuff had drawn all over his face; now he finally had a chance to take revenge with his own hands—or close enough.

Beldum deserved its name. Though less than fifty centimeters tall, it weighed a staggering ninety-five kilograms. Fortunately it could float; otherwise Ethan could never have lifted it alone.

He raised Beldum high over Jigglypuff's round body and brought it down hard. Combusken's attacks had left Jigglypuff strong enough to break out of a Poké Ball once, but no longer able to resist. Lying there, it could only watch the man loom above it and drop a dark shape toward it.

The rock-hard Beldum struck the soft pink Jigglypuff with a loud smack. Under that weight, Jigglypuff's eyes rolled back and it fainted.

“Done!”

Ethan dusted off his hands, picked up the Poké Ball, and pressed it against Jigglypuff. This time it had no strength left to struggle. The ball gave one slight wobble, then its button locked.

Excellent. He had taken his revenge and caught the pink demon—a double victory. Better still, it wouldn't be free to harass them again.

He turned to Misty, who was smiling back at him. Only then did he notice that in her hurry to get dressed, she had fastened her buttons crookedly. He had rushed too, afraid Jigglypuff would escape.

Around them, its victims lay asleep in every imaginable position. Jigglypuff had already covered many of their faces with strange drawings. They would probably wake soon, so Ethan and Misty left them undisturbed and returned to their guesthouse.

Neither felt like going back into the hot spring after all that. Misty looked a little disappointed as they said goodnight and went to their separate rooms.

...

The next morning, after a generous Japanese-style breakfast, they went to the Pokémon Center to collect the partners they had left there for treatment.

They left Lavaridge Town and entered Jagged Pass at its northeastern edge. One branch of the mountain route led toward Mt. Chimney's summit; the other passed through Lavaridge and out of the mountains. As its name suggested, Jagged Pass was full of steps and uneven stones. It was hard going, but for Trainers used to long, strenuous walks, it was mostly a matter of getting a little more tired.

Still, as they reached the middle of the pass, Ethan had the persistent feeling that unfriendly eyes were watching them. He thought back and vaguely remembered that Team Magma had a base somewhere around here.

He was in no mood to seek trouble with them and hoped they wouldn't come looking for it either. He remained tense and watchful all the way through. Fortunately, nothing happened. After half a day of walking, they reached the plains safely.

Their next destination lay to the southeast: Mauville City. It was a central Hoenn hub, one of the region's largest cities, and home to the Mauville Gym. They still had more than a day's walk ahead.

On the plains, they met far more Trainers. In a single day they encountered at least a dozen people traveling with Pokémon. Many were eager to battle. When they recognized Ethan and Misty as fellow Trainers by their clothes and gear, they came over to issue challenges. None of them had identified Ethan himself; they simply wanted a fight.

Ethan was in a good mood and accepted them all. Thanks to those matches, Combusken and Eevee steadily grew stronger. Feebas even had one chance to battle, though it lost badly, defeated almost as soon as the match began.

Ethan and Misty teamed up for several Double Battles as well. Their coordination improved with practice, and they beat everyone who faced them. At Ethan's insistence, Misty kept Psyduck firmly inside its Poké Ball.

All those battles made their progress unusually slow. By nightfall they had covered barely a quarter of the route. At that pace, Mauville was still another day or two away.
