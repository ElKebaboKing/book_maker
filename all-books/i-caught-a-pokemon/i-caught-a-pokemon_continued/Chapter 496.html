Early the next morning, after a simple breakfast, Ethan and Erika entered the unfamiliar mountains with Gardevoir, Scyther, Eevee, and Erika's Leafeon beside them.

Although they had reached the mountains, Charicific Valley lay deep within the range. They still had a long way to walk.

At first, they followed a broad mountain path. After circling the first two peaks, however, it grew narrow and rough. The trail climbed steeply, with sheer cliffs below. One careless step could send them slipping over the edge.

Bare boulders lay everywhere they looked, with hardly any plants among them. Even the view alone made the terrain look dangerous.

That was why they had brought their Pokémon out. If anything went wrong, the Pokémon could keep them safe.

Why not bring out Charizard or Dragonite to fly them straight to the top? As Ethan had said before, climbing a mountain like this was training for him and Erika as well as for their Pokémon.

Ethan crossed a high natural stone bridge first. He looked back, saw Erika picking her way along, and reached out a hand.

"Be careful. The stones can come loose here, so watch your step."

Erika smiled. Without hesitation, she took his hand, and he gently helped her across.

Once she was standing safely, they let go of each other by unspoken agreement, as if nothing had happened.

Their feelings remained unspoken, though both understood them. Deep down, each wanted to be closer to the other.

Holding hands was only a small moment along the way. They continued walking, and as they went deeper into the mountains, Ethan checked the map on his phone against their surroundings to make sure they did not lose their way.

They crossed another low peak and were about to stop for a rest when a woman's voice sounded above them.

"You two down there! You've got Pokémon with you, so I take it you're not ordinary hikers. Are you Trainers?"

They looked up. A woman stood on a boulder overhead, studying them from above!

She looked about twenty-three or twenty-four, perhaps younger. Her thick green hair was pulled into a messy high ponytail. She wore tall red boots, a very short skirt, and a top that left her midriff bare. In one hand she held an odd staff shaped a little like a sword. She had a distinctly rebellious look.

She stood so high that, from Ethan's angle, he could see under her skirt...

He swore he had not meant to look. It was pure chance!

Clearing his throat, Ethan hurriedly looked away. "Uh... who are you?"

The woman laughed and jumped down from the height!

She spun in the air, landed on a lower rock, then sprang to another. After several nimble leaps to break her fall, she landed safely in front of Ethan.

Her quick, fluid movements were nearly a match for Aya the ninja.

Before Ethan could say anything else, she leaned close and sniffed him vigorously.

Erika frowned at once.

"Hee-hee..." The woman straightened and smiled at Ethan. "I can smell something familiar on you. You have a Charizard too, don't you?"

She can tell by smell?

Does she have a dog's nose?

Ethan nodded. "Yes, I do have a Charizard."

Up close, she was quite pretty. Two bright gold earrings caught the light. Her muscles were firm, perhaps from years of training, and together with her hairstyle they gave her a wild air.

At his answer, she looked pleased with herself. "Hah! My nose can always tell when someone has a Charizard. I haven't introduced myself yet. I'm Liza, a Charizard Trainer!"

Liza?

So it was her...

Ethan remembered that a woman Trainer lived in Charicific Valley. Her formidable Charizard had once given Ash a great deal of trouble.

He had wondered before entering the mountains whether he might meet her. Now he had!

He nodded in greeting. "I'm Ethan, and this is Erika."

"So you're Ethan!" Liza exclaimed at his name. "Clair told me about you. She said you have a very strong Charizard. I've wanted to meet you for ages, and here you are!"

"Oh?" Ethan raised his brows. "You know Clair?"

"Of course. We're very close friends. I visit her whenever I can, and she often comes to see me."

As they talked, Erika cast Ethan a discreet glance.

She wanted to ask who Clair was.

And Liza had wanted to meet him for ages?

Erika also noticed that Liza had not called herself a Fire-type Trainer. She had said only that she was a Charizard Trainer.

Did she have nothing but Charizard?

Remembering the valley's name, Erika began to understand.

Before Ethan could ask another question, Liza said excitedly, "If you don't mind, would you like to come with me to Charicific Valley?"

Ethan and Erika exchanged a look. They had only just met her, but both could tell that Liza made friends easily.

"Of course." Ethan turned back to her and nodded. "Charicific Valley is why we came here."

"It is?" Liza asked in surprise. "I thought you were just passing through. Is there something you need there?"

"I've heard that the Charizard in the valley are all very strong. I came to train mine and see how it measures up."

"I see!" Liza laughed. "Then you've come to the right place. The valley's Charizard may be wild, but every one of them is strong! And did you know that another Trainer left his Charizard here not long ago?"

She must mean Ash...

His Charizard had no longer been with him the last time Ethan saw him. Given the timing, it made sense that it was here now.
