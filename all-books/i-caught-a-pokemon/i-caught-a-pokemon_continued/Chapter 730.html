Misty knew little about the two Pokémon, so she took out her Pokédex in the stands and aimed it at Solrock first.

It looked like an orange meteorite, a round, uneven body with eight yellow stone spikes radiating from its sides and an eerie black pattern around its eyes. Its design might have drawn on the sun imagery of Renaissance art and mysticism, such as the Sun card in the tarot's Major Arcana.

The Pokédex responded at once. “Solrock, the Meteorite Pokémon. Rock and Psychic type. Solrock is said to be a newly discovered Pokémon that fell from space, an embodiment of the sun that draws its power from sunlight. On clear days, Solrock gather in rows facing the sun to store solar energy, making them especially strong by day. They drift silently through the air, their expressions never changing, and can read an opponent's thoughts. By spinning, Solrock emit sunlight-like rays that temporarily blind their enemies.”

Misty turned the Pokédex toward Lunatone. Its shape differed, resembling a brownish-yellow crescent moon, but it shared Solrock's strange meteorite-like appearance.

“Lunatone, the Meteorite Pokémon. Rock and Psychic type. Lunatone were first found forty years ago at the site of a meteorite impact, leading some to believe they came from space, though that remains unproven. Research suggests the phases of the moon affect their condition and power. They become particularly active when bathed in a full moon's light. Their piercing red eyes inspire fear, and a stare can put an opponent to sleep.”

Side by side, the two truly did look like the sun and moon. With the hanging planets above them, the battlefield had become a little solar system.

Ethan reached slowly for his Poké Balls. This would not be an easy fight. Tate and Liza had battled together for years and understood each other perfectly. He needed to choose his Pokémon with care.

After a moment's thought, he threw two balls. “Go, Metang! Milotic!”

Blue light flashed, and his Pokémon appeared opposite Solrock and Lunatone. On paper, both had useful type matchups against Rock- and Psychic-type opponents. Milotic's Water type and Metang's Steel and Psychic types appeared to give Ethan a considerable edge.

But battles were not decided on paper. The twins' Gym Pokémon would be strong and well practiced as a team, especially in this arena. Ethan's two Pokémon had evolved only recently and were still growing. They had never fought together in a Double Battle. Their lack of experience could easily outweigh their type advantage. Psychic-type Pokémon were troublesome in real combat, too.

He gave himself roughly even odds. That was only a prediction. Once the battle began, their performances would decide who held the advantage and who won.

Rachel saw that both sides were ready and signaled the start. Tate attacked first.

“Solrock, Tackle!”

Solrock crossed half the arena in a blink and bore down on Milotic. Ethan recognized the test. Tate had chosen a simple physical attack rather than a Psychic move, trying to gauge his opponent's strength. It might surprise a novice, but it was too direct to catch Ethan.

“Milotic, Water Gun!”

A long jet of water struck Solrock head-on, hurting it and stopping its charge. At nearly the same time, Lunatone came at Metang from another direction with Tackle. Ethan ordered Metang to meet it with Take Down rather than dodge.

Bang!

Take Down carried a measure of recoil for its user, but in this collision Metang was plainly the winner.

Ethan had won both exchanges of the opening round. Tate and Liza glanced at one another, caution showing in their eyes. This challenger was not ordinary.

They often opened Gym matches with these sudden charges. Their Pokémon had trained to deliver them with great speed and force, and few challengers could dodge or withstand them. Fewer still turned the exchange against them. The twins realized Ethan would be difficult and stopped holding back.

“Lunatone, Ice Beam!”

“Solrock, Solar Beam!”

Both Pokémon gathered power. Then two differently colored beams shot toward their targets. Lunatone's cold light and Solrock's brilliant orange-gold beam suited the moon-and-sun theme of the field perfectly.

Ethan gave two quick orders. “Metang, Psychic! Milotic, Hydro Pump!”

Four attacks met in the middle of the arena. The collision erupted with a roar. Heat from the explosion turned the water from Hydro Pump into steam, which spread across more than half the battlefield.

Neither side could see through it. Ethan kept his Pokémon back rather than send them blindly into the cloud. But when the steam thinned, Solrock and Lunatone were gone from the ground.

Where had they gone? Ethan was surprised only for an instant. He looked up and spotted them hiding behind one of the large planet models beneath the ceiling.

Of course. They had used the steam to take cover and prepare a surprise attack. He had found them in time.

Across the field, the twins were startled that he had discovered them so quickly. This was a favorite tactic of theirs. Challengers heard about the hanging planets before a match, but many still forgot to watch overhead once the fighting began. By the time they noticed the hidden Pokémon, it was too late. Ethan had looked up immediately. Had he been guarding against it all along?

With their position exposed, the ambush had lost its value. It became a straightforward exchange again. Their familiar opening tactics had all failed within the first moments of the match. Now Ethan could counterattack.

“Metang, Flash Cannon!”

The Steel-type ranged attack was powerful and especially effective against Rock-type Pokémon. Energy gathered in front of Metang's chest and burst toward the pair.

Solrock and Lunatone knew the arena so well that they slipped behind a planet without waiting for an order. They avoided the attack, but the Flash Cannon tore through their hiding place. It melted a great hole in the model, severing several support cables. The planet, seven or eight meters across, crashed onto the battlefield below.

The twins winced as though the blow had landed on them. Their Pokémon were exposed again. They looked at the model broken into pieces on the floor. Had Ethan hit it by accident, or had he deliberately taken away cover to limit where they could hide?

It had to be an accident... probably. They exchanged a glance, then focused on the battle.

“Psychic!” they called together.

At last, both of their Pokémon used their strongest Psychic-type attack. Ethan remained calm.

“Metang, use Psychic to form a barrier in front of you!”

Metang could not hold off both attacks for long by itself. That was not the whole plan.

“Milotic, get behind Metang! Then use Surf!”

Despite its name, Surf did not require a sea or a Pokémon riding across water. Even on dry ground, the move could summon a great wave that rolled toward the opponent.

The two invisible Psychic attacks struck Metang's barrier with a crackling hiss. Their force pushed it slowly backward. Then water spread rapidly outward from Milotic and changed the balance of the fight.

A wave crashed across the uneven battlefield. It swept up the fallen planet model and carried it toward Solrock and Lunatone. Tate and Liza could not ignore it. Surf was powerful, and Water-type attacks struck their Rock-type Pokémon with twice the usual effectiveness.

They abandoned the attack on Metang and ordered both Pokémon to rise into the air. That was exactly what Ethan had been waiting for.

A Twister sprang up from the water before the wave had subsided. Solrock and Lunatone failed to get clear and were caught inside. They were small and moved by levitating rather than with strong wings; they could not simply fly out of the vortex.

Metang began gathering energy and aimed into the heart of the Twister. It waited for the wind to stop and expose the trapped pair. Tate and Liza felt almost as though their own hearts were spinning with their Pokémon. Seeing Metang ready to fire made them more anxious still.

Fortunately, Solrock and Lunatone's meteorite-like bodies were tough. They endured the full force of the Twister. As its energy ran out and the wind weakened, they escaped, apparently without serious injury.

The twins had no time to relax. Metang had already locked onto them.

“Dodge!” they shouted together.

Metang fired as they spoke. Flash Cannon blazed across the field like a huge, glowing shell. Before the two Pokémon could get clear, it exploded between them.

Boom!

For an instant, the entire space-themed arena was bright as day.
