It was only eleven in the morning. With plenty of time left in the day, Ethan and Sabrina went straight to Blackthorn Gym.

Perhaps because the same clan had governed it for generations, the Gym had an imposing, dignified air. Unlike other Gyms with unusual designs, it resembled a manor. Everywhere Ethan looked, he could sense the weight of its long history.

The courtyard held many statues of Dragon-type Pokémon and Trainers. Ethan did not know who the people were or what deeds had earned them a place there. He guessed they were past Gym Leaders from the Dragon Tamer clan, or powerful Trainers who had begun their journeys here. After their deaths or retirements, people had erected these statues so they could continue to stand watch over the Gym.

He and Sabrina crossed the courtyard and entered the building.

The broad, bright hall was silent. Fine murals and decorations lined it, and an expensive-looking carpet lay underfoot. Yet even after they had stood in the entrance hall for some time, no one came to greet them.

"Is anyone here?" Ethan called softly.

He waited, but still no one appeared. He was about to go farther inside to look for a staff member when a familiar man came down the marble staircase.

The man's spiky red hair looked like flame. He wore dark blue clothes beneath a black cape. Those distinctive features made him impossible to mistake: Lance, one of the Elite Four.

"Oh, Mr. Lance?"

"Ethan!" Lance looked surprised too. "What brings you here? Ah, what am I saying? There's only one reason you'd come. You must be here to challenge Blackthorn Gym, right?"

Ethan smiled and nodded. "That's right. I've come for the Rising Badge. Is the Gym Leader here?"

Lance was about to answer when a woman hurried down the stairs behind him.

She looked about twenty-seven or twenty-eight, with long, pale blue hair tied in a ponytail. Her unusual bangs struck Ethan as a little strange. His first impression was of a Gyarados in human form.

She wore a fitted blue bodysuit and tall boots and was fastening a cape behind her as she came down. Seeing Lance still at the foot of the stairs, she urged him on. "Lance, we don't have time to waste. We need to go!"

Only after passing him did she notice the unfamiliar man and woman talking with him in the entrance hall. She studied them in surprise.

"Who are these two? Friends of yours?"

"These two..." Lance stepped aside with a courtly gesture. "Let me introduce everyone. This handsome young man is Ethan Vale, the Trainer I told you about. Don't let his age fool you; he's very strong. The lady beside him is Sabrina, Gym Leader of Saffron Gym in Kanto. And beside me is Clair, the Leader of this Gym."

Sabrina gave a slight nod in greeting.

Ethan offered his hand. "Hello, Clair. I'm Ethan. I came to challenge Blackthorn Gym."

Clair looked him over carefully, then shook his hand. "So you're the one who won the Indigo League tournament. It's a pleasure to meet you. I'm sorry, though. A Gym battle isn't possible right now. Lance and I have a very difficult problem to handle, and we have to leave immediately."

"That's all right." Ethan waved it off. "I can wait here. Do you know when you'll be back?"

Clair was about to reply, but Lance spoke first, smiling. "Hard to say. Maybe a day or two. Maybe four or five."

"That long?" Ethan stared at him. "Then my Gym battle...?"

"It'll have to wait, of course." Lance paused, then added with a smile, "What we're dealing with is troublesome, and we can't just walk away from it. But if a powerful Indigo League champion helped us, we might get back much sooner."

Oh, so that's what you were leading up to!

The hint was obvious. Before Ethan could respond, Clair frowned at Lance.

"No. The Dragon Holy Land is forbidden ground for our Dragon Tamers. Outsiders aren't allowed in, and you know the rules!"

"It'll be fine." Lance gestured for her to calm down and explained patiently. "Ethan and I fought side by side against Team Rocket in Kanto. I trust both his ability and his character. Most of all, he has a Dragon-type Pokémon himself, so you could even call him a Dragon-type Trainer!"

It was the first time Clair had heard Lance praise someone so highly. She gave Ethan another, surprised look, thought for a moment, and said no more.

Ethan, however, had no wish to get involved in the Dragon Tamers' affairs. Family secrets might be at stake. If he accidentally heard or saw something he should not, he could invite unnecessary trouble.

Still, he said I'm a Dragon-type Trainer? Lance must have meant his Gyarados.

"If it's forbidden ground, I'd better stay out of it."

Before Ethan had finished speaking, Lance's strong hand landed on his shoulder.

"Come on, don't refuse." A smile appeared on Lance's normally composed face. "I've said all those good things about you, and now you're backing out? Besides, don't you want to see the Dragon Holy Land? All right, I'll tell you the truth. We're going there to stop someone. He has a kind of Dark Ball that can steal other people's Pokémon. He's very difficult to deal with, and that's why we need your help."

"A Dark Ball? It can steal someone else's Pokémon?"

For some reason, Ethan suddenly thought of Mewtwo.

"That's right. He's already stolen Pokémon from many innocent Trainers with those Dark Balls. He's a ruthless Pokémon hunter!"

"Aren't there any other Dragon Tamers who could help?"

Lance spread his hands helplessly. "Our clan believes in training a small number of exceptional people. Apart from us, the others are away on their journeys. Only the elderly and the children are here. We can't send them out to fight, can we? We'd be laughed at if word got around!"

"All right..." Ethan sighed and nodded. "Since you've put it that way, I'll help. So who is this person? What kind of opponent needs an Elite Four member and a Gym Leader to face him together?"

"That's the man I knew I could count on!" Lance clapped Ethan's shoulder again and broke into a grin. "We're short on time, though. I'll fill you in on the way!"
