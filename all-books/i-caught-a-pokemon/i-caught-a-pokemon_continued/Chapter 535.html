Gardevoir and Jolteon passed Annie on either side. Neither paid any attention to the thief now that she had no Pokémon left to fight with. They headed straight for the machine.

As the partners of a legitimate Trainer, they would not attack a human without an explicit order.

The machine appeared unguarded. But just as the two Pokémon drew close, the great waterspout swept in front of them.

It had already done irreparable damage inside the cathedral. It had cracked countless floor slabs and battered the walls, pillars, and everything else nearby.

That the building had not collapsed yet was a testament to its strength. In an island city like Altomare, the builders had probably taken wind and earthquakes into account.

"Go around it!" Ethan called.

Gardevoir used Teleport to cross the barrier of water. Jolteon raced around its side.

In the next instant, two streams split from the waterspout and blocked them again, threatening to close around them.

The Pokémon attacked the streams, but the result was the same as before. More seawater flowed in to replace everything they broke apart.

They had no choice but to retreat.

Ethan glanced toward Ash. He had not managed to free Latios either. Thinking quickly, Ethan called, "Let's get out of here. If this keeps up, the cathedral will collapse!"

Ash hated to leave, but he knew he could not free Latios alone. He nodded and followed Ethan out.

The fighting outside was nearly over. With help from Ethan's three Pokémon, Erika had taken some time but had defeated the revived fossils without anyone getting hurt.

Lorenzo and Bianca had taken shelter in a corner of the square. When they saw Ethan and Ash emerge, they hurried over.

"Did you save Latios?"

Before Ethan could answer, the cathedral's huge wooden doors exploded outward. The waterspout smashed through them, tore away half a wall, and surged into the square.

They needed no answer now.

"Don't stand there. Get clear!"

Ethan called Lorenzo and Bianca out of their shock and led them to the middle of the square, where they joined Erika.

Only now could he see countless thin streams rising from canals in every direction and converging on the cathedral. They were feeding the waterspout and making it grow.

"Ethan!"

Misty's voice came from behind them. They turned to see her Pokémon pulling a wooden boat toward the bank. Misty and Brock stood aboard.

The two jumped ashore and ran over. Seeing Lorenzo and Bianca with the group, they let out relieved breaths.

"Are you all right?"

After splitting from Ethan, they had found a gondola and had Misty's Corsola pull it along the waterways. They had needed to break through iron hooks blocking their route and fight off several fossil Pokémon. Those delays were why they had only just arrived.

"We're fine, but..." Bianca looked toward the cathedral with worry. "Latios and the Soul Dew are still inside."

In the dark, Misty and Brock had not noticed the waterspout looming over the square. Now they stared at it. "Why is there a waterspout on land? And how did it get so big?"

"Those two women made it using Latios and the Soul Dew," Ethan explained grimly. "It keeps drawing in seawater. We haven't been able to stop it."

Erika frowned as she understood the problem. "Then we have to break the link between them."

"But..." Ash looked downcast. "I already tried. I couldn't break the iron rings holding Latios."

Misty realized how serious the situation was. "What do we do?"

Ethan looked around and saw Annie coming out of the cathedral. His eyes brightened.

"This might be our chance," he said quickly. "The waterspout and the blonde thief are both out of that room. With fewer guards inside, someone could sneak up to the machine and pull the silver-haired woman out of it."

He turned to Misty and Brock. "You're the only two she hasn't seen here yet. You're best placed to do it."

"Understood!"

They nodded and hurried to one side of the square, approaching the cathedral out of sight.

Latias followed them. She seemed just as eager to free Latios.

In the middle of the square, the waterspout kept swelling. Under the same mysterious power, clouds began to cover the sky. Lightning flashed, and raindrops struck the group.

Rain fed the water; wind drove it wilder.

The waterspout stirred the surrounding air into something like a small hurricane. Anyone who knew nothing of the Soul Dew might have mistaken it for extreme weather. Only Ethan and the others understood the danger they faced.

They had to raise their voices to be heard over the wind and rain.

Ethan ignored his soaked clothes and shouted to Erika and Ash, "We have to break up that waterspout. We can't let it get any bigger!"

Both nodded. At their orders, their Pokémon concentrated their attacks on the spinning water.

They could not disperse it completely, but repeated hits tore holes through it. Water sprayed everywhere, and the vortex steadily shrank.

Fresh seawater still flowed in, yet they were holding its growth back.

"It's working!" Ash cried.

Ethan nodded. He knew this could not last, but it might buy Misty and Brock the time they needed.

"Keep going!"

While the Pokémon dodged the waterspout and chipped away at it, Misty and Brock reached a side window of the cathedral.

No one had spotted them on the way. The room inside seemed quiet. They exchanged a look, then climbed through the window with Latias.
