After walking for more than an hour, Ethan and Erika finally reached Greenfield's border. They left behind the travel destination they had visited only briefly but would remember for a long time.

They continued west beneath the mild sunlight. After crossing a broad river and climbing over a small hill, they entered another lush grassland before noon.

A clear lake lay at the center of the plain. Thousands of wild Pokémon of many different species lived around it in peaceful comfort.

Ethan and Erika stopped on a small rise and watched for a while.

Groups of Ledyba fluttered above the flowers. On the ground, more than a hundred Mareep gathered in small clusters and grazed leisurely. Even the lake contained dozens of Quagsire poking their heads above the water.

The Pokémon lived together harmoniously. No territorial disputes disturbed the scene. Instead, every species treated this place as a shared home.

It was an exceptionally rare sight.

Ethan took out his phone. With the click of the camera, he preserved the peaceful scene.

After closing the camera, he checked the map on his phone.

At their current pace, following the mapped route for another day would bring them to Violet City.

They had already challenged Violet Gym, so once they reached the city, they needed only a brief period of rest before continuing elsewhere.

At last, they had returned to their previous progress.

At that moment, Erika seemed to notice something and softly exclaimed.

Ethan followed her gaze. On the far side of the lake, nearly a hundred Hoppip formed a large colony.

Remembering why Erika had come to Johto, Ethan smiled before she could speak. "What do you think? Want to capture one?"

He had not forgotten that Erika traveled here to capture Johto's Grass-type Pokémon. Hoppip were common in this region and not rare at all, but no wild populations lived in Kanto.

At Ethan's question, Erika nodded.

"Since we've encountered them here, I certainly can't let the opportunity pass!"

She set off toward the Hoppip first.

Ethan followed her onto the grassland. Nearby wild Pokémon watched the two humans with curious eyes, but did nothing more. None panicked or fled.

Although this was a gathering place for wild Pokémon, they had clearly grown accustomed to the occasional presence of Trainers.

Hoppip had small, round, pink bodies with two long green leaves growing from their heads like wings. Their appearance was extremely cute.

They lived upon the ground but also drifted slowly through the air. From a distance, the colony resembled nearly a hundred red balloons.

Once they reached a suitable distance, Ethan took out his Pokédex and pointed it toward one of them.

The Pokédex beeped. "Hoppip, the Cottonweed Pokémon. A Grass and Flying type. Hoppip's body is extremely light, allowing it to float gently upon the wind. Because it rides air currents from place to place, it can be found almost everywhere except cold regions. It is said that when groups of Hoppip appear across mountains and fields, spring will soon arrive!"

Ethan put away the Pokédex and looked toward Erika. She did not immediately attempt to capture a Hoppip, but stood quietly and observed the colony.

He did not disturb her. Instead, he stepped back and left the stage to Erika.

This was her capture battle. He had no reason to interfere.

Unlike an ordinary Trainer, Erika did not choose the Hoppip that appeared strongest. She observed for a long time before focusing on one with an especially graceful form.

Only after that Hoppip drifted away from the others did she walk quickly toward it.

Ethan watched from nearby.

Erika sent out Leafeon, battled the Hoppip, defeated it, and successfully captured it.

The entire process lasted less than three minutes. Both Pokémon shared a type, but a Gym Leader's partner naturally had little trouble against an ordinary wild Pokémon. A failed capture would have been far more surprising.

When Erika returned, Ethan applauded lightly.

"Congratulations. As expected of Erika, you made that look effortless."

Capturing a Pokémon had clearly improved Erika's mood. She first rolled her eyes at Ethan's teasing, then could not help laughing.

After checking the time, she said, "All right. It's already noon. Let's have lunch here."

Ethan had no objection. The two left the center of the grassland, found a quiet place where the Pokémon would not disturb them, and set down their luggage.

Ethan released many of his Pokémon. They had camped together countless times and knew exactly what came next. Without needing instructions, each took up a task. Some fetched water while others gathered firewood, and everyone busied themselves with enthusiasm.

Erika naturally became the head cook and began preparing the ingredients.

Ethan assisted her. They worked together with practiced ease, and after little more than ten minutes, a meal that looked simple yet abundant covered most of their picnic cloth.

Ethan examined it. The food had excellent color, aroma, and flavor, while even the presentation was refined.

Erika truly embodied the grace of a traditionally raised young lady.

Her cooking was excellent.

The food looked appetizing enough on its own, and its aroma made it even more irresistible.

While cooking, Erika had also prepared food for the Pokémon. Ethan, Erika, and more than a dozen Pokémon now gathered to enjoy the meal together.

Erika's cooking rivaled even Brock's, and the Pokémon were thoroughly satisfied.

Traveling with Erika was different from traveling with Sabrina. At least Ethan did not have to worry about his meals.

To strengthen their bond, Erika also released the Hoppip she had just captured. Since no one else was nearby, Ethan decided to release Suicune as well.

But to prevent Suicune from escaping, he also released the three Legendary birds.

Erika had brought only two Pokémon on this journey. With Hoppip included, her team now barely reached three.

Perhaps because of Erika's natural affinity, Hoppip already viewed her favorably. After tasting the food she had prepared, it quickly accepted its new Trainer and became friendly with Leafeon and Tangela.

Suicune, by contrast, retained the pride of a Legendary beast. Although it had been captured, it still resisted Ethan.

It wanted to flee, but the three Legendary birds stood around it. Even while preening their feathers, they fixed Suicune with looks that clearly warned, You'd better behave, rookie.

Remembering the terror of Tri-Bird Assault, Suicune could only settle down helplessly and allow itself to be drawn into the large family.
