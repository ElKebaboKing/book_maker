Nurse Joy led Ethan and Sabrina to an empty table, but before leaving, she asked, "Are either of you feeling unwell?"

Ethan took his eyes off the other two guests and gave her a puzzled look. "Why would you ask?"

"The temperature changes so sharply between the cave and the outside that people who enter the Ice Path without preparing can easily catch a cold or come down with a fever," Joy explained with a smile. "That's why I ask. If either of you has caught a chill, I have a special ginger soup for you."

Ethan and Sabrina exchanged a glance. They had prepared before entering the cave and neither felt anything wrong.

"We're fine. Two coffees will do, with extra sugar, please."

"Of course. Just a moment!"

As Joy walked away, Ethan's gaze lingered briefly on her short skirt. Then he shook his head.

That's impressive...

Even in a place this cold, Nurse Joy wore the same standard uniform as always, with her arms and legs bare. Women really did seem remarkably resistant to the cold.

And the Pokémon Center was deep inside an icy cavern, where she rarely saw sunlight and had few people to talk to. Living here year after year would take more endurance than most people had.

Before long, Jynx brought their coffees. Ethan thanked it and took a sip while his drink was still hot. The warmth drove the last of the chill from his body.

Just then, the man and woman at the distant table came over.

Both looked about Ethan's age. The woman had long, curly brown hair, while the man's hair was parted to one side. Otherwise, they were so ordinary-looking that neither would have stood out in a crowd. Looking closer, Ethan noticed a resemblance around their eyes and guessed they were brother and sister.

"Hello," the woman said with a smile as she approached Ethan. "You look like Trainers too. Let me introduce us first. We're the Week Siblings. I'm Tuscany, and this is my younger brother, Wesley."

Tuscany? Wesley?

Were their names really that casual? Why not simply call them Tuesday and Wednesday and be done with the day-of-the-week joke?

But wait. The Week Siblings?

Now that he thought about it, the name sounded familiar. He seemed to remember that there were seven of them, each with a name tied to a day of the week.

Ethan gave them a friendly nod. "Is there something you wanted?"

Tuscany raised an eyebrow and came straight to the point. "How about a battle? We're traveling around and challenging the Trainers we meet. If you beat us, we'll give you a badge!"

"A badge?"

Seeing how little interest Ethan showed, Wesley spoke up. "You must not be from around here if you've never heard of us! I don't mean to brag, but the Week Siblings are fairly well known in Johto. We made these badges ourselves. They won't help you challenge the Silver League, but winning one from us proves your strength!"

"I see." Ethan nodded again. "I don't mind battling, but I haven't had dinner yet. It'll have to wait a little."

"That's fine." Tuscany waved a hand. "We have all evening. We can wait here."

Truthfully, Ethan would have liked a hot shower and a good night's sleep. He was tired. But someone had challenged him, and as a Pokémon Trainer, he was not going to back down.

"All right."

With his agreement, Tuscany and Wesley returned to their table.

After they finished their coffee, Nurse Joy assigned Ethan and Sabrina rooms. With so few guests and plenty of space, each of them effectively had a room to themselves. They left their luggage there, then went back to the dining room for dinner.

But as they ate, Ethan kept feeling two pairs of eyes glance his way. Knowing the siblings were waiting on him spoiled his appetite. After only a few hurried bites, he gave Sabrina a rueful smile to show he was done.

Sabrina ate at her usual unhurried pace. When she finished, Ethan saw her to the Pokémon Center's bathing area. Then he found Tuscany and followed the siblings outside to the battlefield behind the building.

Since the Center stood within the Ice Path, the battlefield had been made from the ice already there.

Night had fallen, and the sun no longer lit the dome overhead. With nothing else to do, Nurse Joy and Jynx switched on the floodlights to illuminate the field. They waved from a second-floor window.

Ethan faced Tuscany and Wesley from the opposite side of the field.

"The rules are simple," Tuscany said. "Ordinary one-on-one battles. Each of us sends out one Pokémon, and whoever defeats the opponent wins. If you beat both of us in a row, you'll receive two badges."

She took a Poké Ball from her waist pouch and waited for Ethan.

Unlike a Gym match, a casual battle like this called for both sides to send out their Pokémon at the same time. Neither Trainer would get to choose a counter after seeing the other's Pokémon, which made the contest fairer.

Ethan did not reach for a Poké Ball yet. "A friendly warning: send out your strongest Pokémon. I'd rather this not be too easy. I'm pretty strong, you know!"

Wesley snorted at his audacity. "Nobody has ever spoken to the Week Siblings like that! If you're asking for trouble, I won't hold back. Tuscany, let me go first. I'll teach this arrogant guy a lesson!"

He stepped past his older sister, reached deep into his waist pouch for a Poké Ball, and fixed Ethan with a hostile stare.

Ethan had angered them, but his confidence had not come from an inflated ego. Simple battles were no longer enough for him. He needed demanding opponents to improve, and he wanted as many of his battles as possible to be worth fighting. Asking for their strongest Pokémon made sense to him.

After a moment's thought, he sent out Venusaur.

A faint sneer appeared on Wesley's face when he saw it. Blue light flashed, and his own Pokémon appeared on the field. Ethan looked over: it was a Forretress.

Oh?

Bug was strong against Grass. This time, it seemed, Ethan was the one at a type disadvantage.
