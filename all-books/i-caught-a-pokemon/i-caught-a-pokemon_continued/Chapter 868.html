Charizard and Entei were locked in battle.

A few dozen seconds later, Ethan arrived with May, who was breathing hard from the run. Seeing that Charizard had caught Entei, Ethan wasted no time. He drew several Poké Balls and released more of his partners.

Gardevoir, Dragonite, Tyranitar, and Scizor appeared in flashes of blue light. At his direction, they spread out to surround Entei from several sides and block its escape.

Ethan did not intend for them to join the fight. Their job was to stand guard.

He turned his attention back to Charizard and Entei.

May caught her breath. “Ethan, are you trying to catch this Entei?”

“Yes. I met it while traveling through Johto before, but it got away. This time, I'm catching it no matter what.”

“With Charizard?”

“Of course.” Ethan smiled. “Charizard has just finished training at the valley. Its first match back should be against an evenly matched opponent, and Entei fits perfectly.”

“But…” May hesitated. “It just fought the strongest Charizard in the valley. I treated its wounds, but it hasn't recovered its energy. Then it carried us for hours, while Entei was resting here. Can it really win like this?”

“Don't worry.” Ethan took out the Charizardite X Liza had given him and a Mega Ring set with his Meteorite Key Stone. He held them up and smiled at her. “If Charizard gets stronger, its fatigue won't stop it.”

May remembered Liza saying the strange stone could make Charizard evolve into a more powerful form. So that was Ethan's plan. She did not know how he would bring about such a change, but he looked certain of himself.

She said nothing more. Instead, she stepped aside and scanned Entei with her Pokédex. Its entry taught her a little about the Legendary Pokémon, but she was even more eager to see the new form Ethan and Liza had mentioned.

In the valley, Charizard and Entei continued to fight. Within minutes, they had torn up the ground around them.

Entei remembered how dangerous Ethan was. Trapped and fighting for a way out, it held nothing back. As it drew upon its power, Ethan and May felt the temperature rise steadily. The air itself seemed to smell of flame.

The sky darkened. Thick clouds gathered over the valley in moments. Legendary Pokémon seemed to bring changes to the heavens whenever Ethan fought them.

Under Entei's full assault, Charizard began to give ground.

Its strength had grown by leaps and bounds in the Charicific Valley. It could face Entei alone. But May was right: after its battle with the valley's strongest Charizard and the long flight here, it was exhausted. It did not have the energy to win a prolonged fight as it was.

Ethan whistled for it to return.

Charizard beat its wings, broke away from Entei, and leaped backward. It floated a few meters before landing firmly beside Ethan.

Even after their short exchange, sweat showed on its body. Entei, by contrast, still looked full of energy.

Neither Pokémon had taken an obvious wound. Their power was close enough that the mismatch was one of stamina, at least for now.

Ethan went to Charizard and tied Charizardite X around its neck. Then he fastened the Mega Ring around his own wrist.

He took his time, treating each step with care. Entei watched from a distance. Instinct told it this was bad news, even if it had no idea what the human was doing.

Entei wanted to run. The Pokémon stationed on every side made it hesitate. They had not fought it yet, but each one felt dangerous.

Once the stone and ring were in place, Ethan and Charizard looked toward Entei together.

“Go on! Show it what you can do. Let it feel your new power!” Ethan shouted the last words.

He tapped the Key Stone on his Mega Ring twice. The Key Stone and Charizard's Mega Stone lit up together, scattering rainbow-colored light.

May and Entei stared as the flame at the tip of Charizard's tail changed from orange to blue.

A wave of air burst from Charizard as its body transformed. Its orange skin turned black and its wings dark blue. The underside from its jaw to the tip of its tail became sky blue. Blue clawlike points grew from its shoulders, two finlike spikes appeared behind its neck, and the tips of its horns sharpened and turned blue.

Its shape had not changed beyond recognition, but the colors, fierce eyes, and two streams of blue flame spilling from its mouth made it look magnificent and intimidating.

“ROAR!”

In its new form, Charizard bellowed at Entei. The sound rang through the whole valley, and even the clouds overhead seemed to part before it.

Mega Charizard had arrived.

Even Gengar came out to stare. May's eyes widened at the fierce, striking form. “This…this is what Charizard becomes?”

“Yes.” Ethan smiled. “Mega Charizard X.”

Charizard took a moment to grow accustomed to its transformed body. Then it spread its wings and charged before Entei could collect itself.

Entei did not want to fight such a formidable-looking opponent, but it had no way out. It braced itself.

The gap between them vanished. Entei had been on guard, yet the newly empowered Charizard still found an opening.

Other Trainers' Mega Charizard were powerful enough. Ethan's Charizard had already undergone an enormous improvement before Mega Evolving. In this form, its strength was frightening.

Entei had expected a change, but it had underestimated how much stronger Charizard would become. From their first exchange it felt pressure on every front. Mega Evolution had narrowed, perhaps even erased, the advantage of a Legendary Pokémon.

Charizard's type had changed from Fire and Flying to Fire and Dragon as well, making its close-range attacks still more threatening.

The two grappled again. From above, Charizard repeatedly struck with Dragon Claw, Dragon Tail, and Dragon Breath, pressing its advantage.

Entei was solely Fire-type. Its flames did reduced damage to Charizard, limiting its options. It still had some general-purpose Normal-type and Dark-type moves, so it was not helpless.

Charizard swept a claw wreathed in draconic flame toward it. Entei ducked beneath the strike and sprang up to bite Charizard's belly. Charizard twisted aside, dodging in turn, and the fight continued.

When Entei tried to evade again, Charizard's tail struck its body and sent it stumbling back several steps.

Entei steadied its breathing and studied its opponent warily. It was one of Ho-Oh's guardians, a Legendary Pokémon. When had a battle last forced it into such an undignified position?

And it was facing an ordinary Pokémon, not another Legendary one. Entei had never expected to be pushed so hard.

But it would not simply surrender. With a roar, it summoned every reserve of strength and lunged again. It fought as if drawing on one hundred and twenty percent of its power.

At the height of that surge, Entei unleashed its strongest counterattack. A thick, dazzling column of energy shot from its mouth toward Charizard flying low overhead.

This did not look like any conventional named move. It was neither an ordinary Normal-type nor Fire-type attack. It resembled the raw instinctive power that Rayquaza or Deoxys might use. Entei had truly been driven into a corner.

Charizard refused to give way. It raised both claws. Blue flames flared from its mouth and tail, then ignited around its hands. Instead of retreating, it charged straight at the beam.

At the point of impact, Charizard swung its claws. It stopped the column of energy head-on. With another slash, it shattered the attack.

Ethan watched without changing expression. His strategy had been simple: give Charizard enough strength to overcome the disadvantage of its fatigue.

It was working. Charizard had Entei on the defensive.

That did not make Entei weak. Its strength as a Legendary Pokémon was plain to see. Mega Evolution had simply made Ethan's Charizard stronger still.

Ethan thought back to the start of his journey. He had grown into a Trainer who could face a Legendary Pokémon with a partner that was not one itself. Time had passed quickly.

On the field, Charizard beat its wings and surged forward through the fading remnants of the beam.

Entei was still releasing energy. By the time it saw Charizard coming, it could not dodge. Two slashes knocked it to the ground. Before it could rise, a scorching Dragon Breath struck it.

“Now's your chance!” May cried.

Ethan knew it too. The battle had reached its turning point. He pulled a Great Ball from his pouch and threw it toward Entei.

The ball arced through the air and opened before it reached the fallen Pokémon. Red light streamed out and enveloped Entei.

Entei did not want to be captured, but Dragon Breath still blocked its vision and wracked its body. It failed to see the ball until the attack cleared and the red light had already closed around it.

It was too late to resist. Entei vanished into the Great Ball before it could even roar.

Click. The ball landed on the ground and began to shake violently.

Ethan, May, and all of his Pokémon watched it. Charizard watched too, but quietly gathered its strength. If Entei escaped, it would strike again before the beast could react.

The Great Ball gave it no chance.

Human technology could be unreliable at times, but sometimes it managed to do its job. With a soft click, the ball's light went dark and its shaking stopped.

After Raikou and Suicune, Ethan had now caught Entei. All three of Johto's Legendary beasts were finally his.
