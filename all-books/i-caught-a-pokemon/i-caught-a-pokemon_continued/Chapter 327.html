Gyarados had always been one of Ethan's most dependable partners. In battles large and small, when he left a fight to it, it never disappointed him.

True to its name, its ferocity left a lasting impression on every opponent. Now that it had become their enemy, Ethan and the others understood just how frightening it could be.

"Hey!" Will called to the people still staring. "Stop standing there. Attack together and calm it down!"

He recalled the unconscious Exeggutor and released Jynx and Slowbro. Both were among his main Pokémon. He was taking the fight seriously, determined to recover some pride.

"What a nuisance," Karen muttered. "Can't we just wait until it's finished making a mess and calms down on its own?"

Even as she complained, she released Houndoom and Murkrow.

Looker was Ethan's friend and the officer in charge of the operation. He could hardly stand by. He sent out Arcanine and Croagunk to help.

Seeing the three of them release Pokémon, Sabrina turned to Ethan. "Do you need me to act?"

"Not yet..." Ethan sighed. "Stay back and watch the field. Keep an eye out in case the Rockets return to cause trouble."

He released Gardevoir, Jolteon, and Dragonair. Nine Pokémon spread out and surrounded Gyarados.

It stood amid the ruins, still destroying everything within reach. Nothing around it remained intact after its repeated charges.

Will saw that everyone was ready. "Jynx, Slowbro, use Psychic!"

Two psychic attacks shot toward Gyarados. Ethan knew they needed to calm it as quickly as possible, so he too ordered his Pokémon to attack, though Gyarados was his own partner. Karen and Looker followed. Powerful moves flew from every side and struck Gyarados.

"Raaah!"

After taking so many hits, Gyarados threw back its head and roared. It turned toward its attackers with still more fury in its eyes and gathered water for a counterattack.

"Careful! Its moves are incredibly strong. If one hits you, it might—"

Will tried to warn them, remembering what had happened to Exeggutor. Before he could finish, Looker's Arcanine failed to dodge the Hydro Pump and went down in one hit.

Will fell silent.

Looker stared at his fallen Arcanine, scratched his head, and opened his mouth without finding anything to say. He quietly recalled it.

Will shook his head, drew a breath, and forced himself to continue. "...It might knock you out at once. Don't let your guard—"

Gyarados charged. Croagunk had leaped forward to fight it at close range, and Gyarados slammed into it before Will could finish. The impact sent Croagunk flying more than a dozen meters, as if a speeding tank had struck it. It crashed into a broken wall and slid down unconscious.

Will fell silent again.

Looker felt Will's speechless gaze and turned away in embarrassment. Both of his Pokémon had been defeated before the man could finish a sentence. It was hard not to feel he had let everyone down.

His Arcanine and Croagunk were experienced battlers, but even with Looker serving as an International Police officer, they were far below Gyarados. It was the Pokémon of a champion, and Project R had made it stronger still.

"Sorry. Arcanine and Croagunk are the only Pokémon I have. I can't help with the rest of this fight."

"Never mind," Will said with a wave. "Leave it to us."

The remaining Pokémon kept fighting. It was less a direct battle than a series of attempts to keep Gyarados busy. They dodged its attacks, worked together, and struck whenever an opening appeared. A single hit could end the fight for any one of them.

The struggle went on. Gardevoir's Psychic landed. Jolteon's Discharge hit too. Yet Gyarados continued to stand after taking attack after attack.

Karen stared in disbelief. "Is that really a Pokémon? It's taken more than a dozen hits and it's still fighting like this. What kind of monster did you raise?"

Ethan rubbed his nose and did not answer. He felt embarrassed for causing everyone so much trouble.

When the fight still showed no sign of ending, he said quietly to Gengar, "You go too."

Gengar nodded gravely. "I understand."

It vanished from Ethan's sight and appeared behind Gyarados an instant later. Gengar formed a ball of dark energy and struck while Gyarados was unaware. As Gyarados turned to bite, Gengar disappeared again and escaped.

With its target gone, Gyarados vented its anger on the others. Jynx was preparing another attack when Gyarados rushed in and swept it away with its tail. Murkrow tried to attack from above, but a Hyper Beam struck it and sent it spiraling to the ground.

Both were unable to battle. In only a few minutes, the group had lost four Pokémon. Will looked miserable. He had a sinking feeling that they might not be able to stop Gyarados.

Karen alone showed a flash of admiration. As she had said when she introduced herself, a powerful, wicked-looking Pokémon held a special appeal for her.

Fortunately, Gengar reappeared and traded attacks with Gyarados, drawing its attention. On Ethan's order, Jolteon used Thunder.

Dark clouds gathered overhead. More than ten seconds passed. Then, with a tremendous crash, a huge bolt of lightning struck.

Perhaps its rage had blinded it to danger. Gyarados made no attempt to dodge and took the full force of the long-charged attack. At last, the rampage ended.
