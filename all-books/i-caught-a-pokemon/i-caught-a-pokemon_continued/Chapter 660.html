With everyone watching, Machamp spread all four arms and charged at Tyranitar again. This time, they could see exactly how it had fallen behind.

Machamp broke through a rock blocking its path with a light shove of its shoulder. A few strides brought it in front of Tyranitar. It reached out as if to grab Tyranitar and use Seismic Toss. If it succeeded, the move would do serious damage.

Just before Machamp could touch it, Tyranitar turned. Its tail was not especially long, but it was immensely thick. It swept the tail straight into Machamp's legs.

Pain shot through Machamp's lower body. Caught off guard, it lost its balance and fell backward.

Oh no! Brawly's expression changed. He knew what was coming.

Machamp hit the floor with a heavy thud.

“Now's our chance! Hyper Beam!” Ethan called.

Everyone understood what he intended. Some of the Gym girls turned away, unable to bear watching. Others covered their faces, and a few even shed quiet tears.

Tyranitar lowered its head slightly. Energy gathered in its mouth and burst forth at point-blank range, striking Machamp squarely in the chest. The blazing white beam poured into it with a fierce, crackling hiss.

Under the tremendous impact, Machamp's body convulsed. Its eyes rolled upward, and it soon lost consciousness.

The referee raised her flag almost immediately. “Machamp is unable to battle! Tyranitar wins! The challenger wins the Gym match!”

As soon as she finished, Brawly shouted at Tyranitar. “Stop!”

But Tyranitar was not going to obey him. It continued pouring out the remaining energy of its Hyper Beam. Alarmed, Brawly kept looking toward Ethan.

When Ethan saw that the match was over, he waved a hand. Tyranitar closed its mouth at once, and the beam cut off.

Brawly hurried over to examine Machamp. Satisfied that it had suffered no serious injury, he recalled it.

“I lost...”

He was frustrated, but he arranged his face into an unconcerned expression. “Your Tyranitar is strong. That attack on Machamp's legs was especially clever. I can tell you must have trained specifically for this challenge. You came well prepared.”

Trained specifically? Ethan wondered. For what? Attacking legs?

He shook his head helplessly. Brawly really couldn't stand to lose without finding an excuse. Ethan could understand why. Two challengers had arrived today, and Brawly had sent out four of his best Pokémon across their matches. He had lost every round. In front of the Gym girls, his pride had taken a beating. He needed some way to save face.

Ethan had no desire to argue. He recalled Tyranitar.

Brawly took Ethan's silence as agreement. So the man really had researched his team before coming here. The thought made Brawly feel a little better.

He approached Ethan and offered him the same badge Bea had refused earlier. “This is my Knuckle Badge. Please take it.”

He watched Ethan closely. What if this man, like the woman before him, had come only to train and refused the badge too? To Brawly's relief, Ethan accepted it without hesitation.

Ethan looked down at the Knuckle Badge in his palm. True to its name, it resembled a boxing glove. Most of it was blue, forming the body of the glove, while an orange circle at the bottom looked like a protruding thumb. There was nothing more elaborate about it.

He put the badge into his badge case, gave Brawly a nod, and headed for the exit. Misty and Bea rose from the stands to join him, and the three left the Gym together.

Brawly let out a long breath as he watched them go. At last it was over. Those matches had been exhausting. It was only morning, yet he felt drained and thoroughly gloomy.

First he would get his Pokémon treated. Then he could go surfing and relax. His only regret was that he had never learned Bea's name or got her contact information.

What a shame. Still, Brawly's disappointment faded almost as quickly as it had come. Life went on, and there was no sense giving up a good day at the beach.

“Girls, the weather's great! Let's go surfing!”

He turned to invite them, only to find them huddled around one girl's phone, chattering as they stared at its screen. Brawly raised an eyebrow, put on a bright smile, and walked over.

“What are you looking at?”

“Brawly, come here!” the girl holding the phone said excitedly. “We found out who that man was!”

“What?” Brawly stopped. Hadn't Ethan been an ordinary challenger? Curious, he hurried over.

“I thought he looked familiar,” the girl continued. “I searched for him just now, and sure enough, he's no ordinary Trainer!”

On the phone, a video showed an awards ceremony. The man standing on the podium was the same one Brawly had just battled. Its headline read: Congratulations to Ethan Vale on Winning His Second Pokémon League Tournament!

“What? A tournament champion? His second?”

Brawly rubbed his eyes and looked again. There was no mistake. The man smiling on the podium was Ethan. He had won tournaments in two regions. No wonder his Tyranitar had been so strong.

Then Brawly remembered his claim that Ethan had studied his team in advance, and his face grew hot. A Trainer with two tournament titles had no need to research him specially just to challenge one Gym.

Fortunately, the girls were too busy praising Ethan to notice how flimsy Brawly's excuse had been. But hearing all that admiration made him jealous. Until a short while ago, those admiring looks had belonged to him.

...

After leaving Dewford Gym, Ethan, Misty, and Bea followed the familiar path out of Dewford Town. With their Gym challenges behind them, it was time to part ways.

Ethan turned to Bea. “Where will you go next?”

“There are other well-known Fighting-type Trainers in Hoenn,” she replied without hesitation. “I'll visit and challenge them all. Then I'll travel to another region.”

“I see. I hope our paths cross again.”

They had only just met, so after exchanging contact information, they said a simple goodbye and watched Bea leave.

While they exchanged numbers, Misty quietly watched their faces. Neither Ethan nor Bea showed anything unusual. She let out a relieved breath.

Once Bea had gone, Ethan and Misty returned to Dewford Town. At the Pokémon Center, Nurse Joy treated Combusken and Tyranitar. They had lunch there as well before heading to the harbor.

Ethan now held four badges: the Stone Badge from Rustboro Gym, the Heat Badge from Lavaridge Gym, the Dynamo Badge from Mauville Gym, and the Knuckle Badge from Dewford Gym. He was halfway to the eight he needed for the Ever Grande Conference.

They boarded a passenger ship bound for the mainland. Standing on deck in the sea breeze, with the blue water stretching around them, Misty asked, “Where are we going next?”

Ethan took out his map and studied it. “Petalburg Gym. I went there before, but Norman was away on business, so I missed my chance to challenge him. Petalburg isn't far from Dewford either. It makes sense to go now.”

“Norman is May's father, right?” Misty nodded. “I wonder what he's like.”

Ethan looked toward the horizon. “A man who lives for Pokémon. Serious, meticulous, and well liked. Most of all, he's strong.”

...

The ship sailed for much of the day. By evening, Ethan and Misty stepped onto Hoenn's mainland again. Though the island was part of Hoenn too, solid ground here felt different. It was reassuring to be back.

The voyage had been dull, and they did not push on after disembarking in the port city. They took a room at a hotel for the night.

They had arrived late, and the hotel had only one room left. Ethan and Misty had to share it. At check-in, Ethan spent a long time explaining himself under the shocked gaze of the receptionist. Only after the lobby manager examined the situation did they finally allow him and Misty to take the room.

That was one of the difficulties of traveling with a minor. Given a choice, Ethan would rather have stayed at a Pokémon Center or a small inn.

As the elevator doors closed behind them, he caught one last glimpse of the receptionist and manager. They were still staring at him as though he were a pervert.

Oh well. I'll get used to it eventually...

Nothing happened that night. Early the next morning, Ethan barely touched breakfast before hurrying Misty out of the hotel and heading straight for Petalburg City.

Misty had no idea why he was acting that way. Mature as she was compared with others her age, there were still things she did not quite understand.

The road toward Petalburg City was busy with Trainers who had only recently set out from New Bark Town. Ethan accepted every challenge offered along the way to give his Pokémon practice. With Eevee, Combusken, and Metang, he swept through the beginners. By the time they reached the outskirts of Petalburg City, he had won seventeen battles without a loss.

Misty found the whole thing hilarious. “Ethan, why are you picking fights with beginners? Did you see their faces after you beat them? Some of them looked so crushed they might take ages to recover.”

“What do you know?” Ethan shrugged and gave her a look. “I've been traveling for a long time, but those three Pokémon are still fairly new to battling. If they're beginners too, what's wrong with sparring against them to sharpen my team's skills?”

“Of course, of course. You're right.” Misty hid a laugh behind her hand. “But aren't you worried someone will find out? Lucky for you they didn't know your name. Otherwise we'd see headlines like, Shocking! Tournament Champion Crushes Beginners Near Their Starting Town!”

Ethan gave her a light knock on the head. Laughing, he entered Petalburg City in high spirits.

Just then his phone rang. The caller was Kennedy, the Devon Corporation secretary he had once rescued.

Ethan paused. Why would Kennedy call him out of the blue? They were unlikely to have much to do with each other again. Something must have happened.

He answered. “Hello?”

“Mr. Vale? Are you still in Hoenn?”

“I am. I'm near Petalburg City. What's going on?”

Kennedy sounded relieved to hear he was near Devon's headquarters. He came straight to the point. “That's wonderful. If it's convenient, could you come to Rustboro City? Mr. Stone has something very important to ask of you!”
