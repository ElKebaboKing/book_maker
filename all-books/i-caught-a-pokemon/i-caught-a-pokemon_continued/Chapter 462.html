After reaching these waters, the group did not continue to Silver Rock Isle. Following their map, they instead traveled to another nearby island—Ogi Isle.

Ogi Isle was at least two-thirds smaller than Silver Rock Isle. Far removed from the noise of modern society, it retained its original natural environment. There were no human towns anywhere on the island, only a handful of scattered homes.

Several even smaller islands also surrounded Ogi Isle. Large and small, they clustered together to form a relatively modest archipelago.

Once everyone had gone ashore, Ethan paid the fare and watched the boat depart.

From this point onward, they could rely only on themselves.

They had barely stepped onto the island, before their legs could even readjust to the solid ground, when Ash impatiently asked, "What should we do next?"

Ethan considered the question for a moment before sharing his thoughts. "Our immediate priority should still be finding Lugia. We need to protect it before Team Rocket captures it, or convince it to move somewhere else."

"But..." Ash looked around. The same dense forest stretched in every direction, without any visible differences. He scratched his cheek. "Where do we even start looking? It couldn't be hiding on this island, could it?"

At Ash's question, Misty rubbed her chin and offered her own analysis. "Judging from what we saw in the Orange Islands... Wouldn't a Legendary Pokémon like Lugia live in the deepest part of the ocean to avoid being discovered by humans?"

"That's difficult to say." Ethan shook his head. "Many of the Whirl Islands have waterways beneath them. Lakes and underground rivers on the islands can lead directly to the sea, so Lugia could still be hiding somewhere inland. Otherwise, why have so few people discovered any trace of it? Besides, didn't Maya tell us that her clue came from someone who spotted what might have been Lugia on one of these islands?"

Erika agreed. "Ethan is right. Searching for Lugia in the ocean would be like looking for a needle in a haystack. Our only option is to focus on these nearby islands and try our luck. Perhaps we can first find the person who provided the clue and ask them for more details."

Seeing the two of them echo each other with exactly the same opinion, Misty narrowed her eyes.

She might still be young, but she was a girl, after all. Her sensitivity toward romance placed her miles ahead of Ash, and even Brock, who fell in love with every woman he met, could not compare.

Misty had noticed that ever since Erika began traveling with Ethan, their time together had led her down the same path as Sabrina. Erika was beginning to fall for him!

The change was plain for anyone to see!

Sigh... Now I have another rival...

She sighed inwardly, but the small trace of unhappiness vanished as soon as she remembered that Ethan had invited her to travel with him in the next region.

Hehe. There's plenty of time, after all.

I'm still just a little girl. By the time I grow into a beautiful young woman, Erika and Sabrina will both be old ladies in their thirties. How could they possibly be more attractive than me?

I'm the world's most beautiful girl!

Ethan will definitely be mine in the end!!

The group rested beside the sea for a while. Once Brock was no longer dizzy, they chose a direction at random and began exploring the island's interior.

They crossed the beach, continued over a stretch of grassland, and entered a dense forest. Before they had traveled very far, a patch of bushes beside them suddenly rustled.

In the wild, movement like that usually meant a small Pokémon was hiding in the undergrowth. Ash immediately became interested. He cautiously approached the bushes and was about to part the thick grass when a Pokémon emerged on its own. It had yellow fur, long ears, a lightning-bolt-shaped tail, and the appearance of a mouse standing upright!

"Huh? Pikachu?"

Ash exclaimed when he saw the species he knew so well. He had never expected Pikachu to live on such a remote island!

After studying it more carefully, however, he suddenly found this Pikachu familiar. The distinctive tuft of hair on its forehead reminded him of someone in particular.

"You're Sparky, aren't you?" Ash crouched and looked at the Pikachu. Although the words formed a question, his voice brimmed with confidence.

Sparky?

Looking at the Pikachu, Ethan searched his memory and immediately recalled the boy named Richie from the Indigo League.

Sparky also seemed to recognize Ash. It called out to him, then began playing happily with Ash's Pikachu.

Once its identity was confirmed, Brock immediately looked around and murmured, "If Sparky is here, that means..."

Before he could finish, a boy dressed very similarly to Ash stepped out from behind a large tree.

"Sparky, don't wander off..."

Halfway through his sentence, the boy noticed Ethan's group. His words abruptly stopped, and surprise appeared on his face!

"Ash! What are you doing here?"

"Richie! I knew it was you!"

The two shouted almost simultaneously.

Just as they had expected, the newcomer was Richie!

Once everyone came face-to-face, however, the atmosphere inevitably became somewhat awkward.

During the Indigo League, Richie had eliminated Ash and advanced to the quarterfinals. Ethan had then eliminated Richie and moved on to the semifinals.

The three formed something like a food chain. Ethan in particular had defeated Richie decisively, without showing the slightest mercy.

After their battles, Ash and Richie had each shaken hands with their opponents and acted as though neither result bothered them. Yet a thorn still remained in both their hearts. Meeting the opponent who had defeated them again naturally caused some discomfort, even if no one mentioned those matches.

Fortunately, Ash was carefree, and although Richie was more thoughtful, both were passionate young Trainers. After a brief silence, the atmosphere quickly became lively again.

They found a flat rock and sat down, then inevitably spent some time catching up.

Looking at Ash and Ethan, Richie sighed with emotion. "We haven't seen each other since the Indigo League ended. Neither of you has changed at all!"

"You haven't changed either, Richie!" Ash scratched his head. "I really never expected us to meet again somewhere like this. I guess we must be connected by fate! By the way, Richie, what are you doing here?"

Richie answered without hesitation. "I came here to search for a mysterious Pokémon!"

A mysterious Pokémon!?

At his answer, Ethan and the others instinctively looked at one another.

Did Richie come here to search for Lugia too?

But how did he know Lugia was here!?
