“That's right. As you can see, we're twins.”

The boy who had appeared second stepped beside Jiro and nodded to Ethan and Misty. “Hello. I'm Ichiro Sawama, his older brother.”

Ethan and Misty looked from one identical face to the other. They could hardly tell the twins apart. Fortunately, the boys wore T-shirts of different colors beneath their outer clothes and had different Pokémon beside them, giving the travelers a way to match names to faces.

When Ichiro heard that Jiro had challenged them, he grew interested too.

“Let me join in! Like my brother, I haven't been traveling long. Since there are two of you and two of us, let's have a Double Battle!”

Ethan had been in Hoenn only a few days, but he had already noticed that Double Battles were more popular here than in Kanto and Johto. In Rustboro City, he had glimpsed several pairs of Trainers battling together on outdoor fields.

He and Misty exchanged a look. She smiled and discreetly covered her mouth.

One of them was a conference champion, the other almost a Gym Leader. The twins had chosen formidable opponents.

In a playful mood, Ethan and Misty kept their identities to themselves and agreed to the match.

They found a relatively level clearing in the forest and stood on opposite sides.

“We're visiting from other regions,” Ethan said. “We don't know much about Double Battles here. Are there any special rules?”

Ichiro had only recently begun his travels, but he knew the rules. He shook his head. “Nothing special. It's the same as anywhere else: knock out all the Pokémon on the other side to win.”

Ethan nodded and studied the brothers again. Since they were both beginners, he had no need to show his true strength. He could simply enjoy the match with them.

Misty seemed to have the same idea. One look was enough for them to understand each other.

Ethan and Misty each took a Poké Ball from a waist pouch. The twins needed none: they sent the Pokémon already at their sides onto the field.

Jiro's choice was the large Heracross beside him. Ichiro's partner was an Ariados.

Blue light flashed as Ethan and Misty released Torchic and Psyduck.

Ethan had chosen Torchic at least partly to train it. Misty's choice of Psyduck showed that she was treating the Double Battle as a bit of fun.

All four Pokémon looked eager, even Psyduck, whose eyes held an unusual excitement.

That was understandable. Misty rarely chose to send it into battle deliberately. After so long with her, Psyduck was thrilled to have the chance and resolved to do its best for her.

Ichiro picked up a small stone and tossed it high. The instant it struck the ground, the battle began.

Torchic darted forward first. It chirped fiercely and headed straight for the largest opponent, Heracross.

Its Fire-type attacks were strong against both Bug types, Heracross and Ariados, so Torchic had a good chance in this matchup.

Besides, it was a young chicken. Bugs hardly frightened it, however large they were.

As it drew close, Torchic opened its beak and used Ember.

Heracross did not dare meet the flames. It beat its thin, cicada-like wings and glided away.

Torchic chased after it, looking determined not to stop until it managed to burn Heracross.

Psyduck, meanwhile, lived up to Misty's expectations in quite a different way. Its performance was as consistent as ever.

It wanted to impress her, but as it waddled toward Ariados, the spider shot silk around its legs. Psyduck tripped and fell with ease.

Misty pressed a hand to her forehead and sighed. Trusting Psyduck had been foolish.

Still, she could not leave it there. “Psyduck, use Water Gun!”

Psyduck fired a hurried stream of water. Ariados dodged nimbly, closed the distance, and lowered two fangs glinting with poison toward Psyduck's neck.

A shadow flashed past. Ariados looked up just as Heracross slammed into it.

Torchic had chased Heracross across the clearing. It had ended up here by accident and crashed into its teammate, saving Psyduck in the process.

“Thanks,” Misty said to Ethan with a grin. She quickly had Psyduck strip the silk from its legs so it could move again.

The battle continued.

The forest clearing was small for four Pokémon, limiting how freely they could move.

Burning patches left on the ground by Torchic's flames narrowed Heracross's and Ariados's options still further.

Before Ethan could enjoy that advantage, Psyduck started spraying Water Gun at random and put the hot embers out.

Ethan stared at it in silence.

Was Psyduck secretly on the other team? Why was it making things harder for them?

He gave Misty a speechless look. She stuck out her tongue in embarrassment and hurried to order Psyduck to stop firing Water Gun so freely.

The battle went on.

Unlike Ethan and Misty, who had shown almost no teamwork, the twins knew how to cooperate.

Since Heracross struggled against Torchic, both brothers turned their attention to Psyduck.

They had seen that it was not very bright and kept getting in its partner's way. If they knocked it out first, they could deal with Torchic together afterward.

The combined attack put Psyduck in immediate danger.

Heracross charged into Psyduck and knocked it down. Before it could rise, Ariados came in and bit it on the rear.

Psyduck howled, sprang into the air, and ran wildly around the field, clutching its backside.

Any thought of impressing Misty had vanished from its mind.

Misty had hoped the attacks might give Psyduck a headache and unleash its powerful psychic abilities. But neither Heracross nor Ariados had struck its head. Psyduck was hurting, yet the source of its psychic power remained untouched.

Did it have to offer them its head to hit?

Its panicked running disrupted Torchic too.

When Heracross and Ariados came close together, Torchic saw a chance to catch them both with Ember. It had barely opened its beak when Psyduck charged blindly toward it, still clutching its backside.

Torchic chirped and jumped aside to avoid being hit by its own teammate. It lost the best moment to attack the two Bug types.

“How did it come to this...?”

Misty's face fell. Ethan could only shake his head. Psyduck's lack of control had made the battle much harder.

Thanks to Psyduck, Ethan realized this match was proving more difficult than yesterday's fight against Team Magma and Team Aqua.

Fortunately, the twins did not know Psyduck always behaved this way. They remained intent on knocking it out before confronting Torchic together.

That gave Torchic a little breathing room and kept it from facing both opponents at once.

Ethan knew he did not have much time. He needed to bring down one of them soon.

He focused on the field, searching for a way to win.

After countless battles over a long journey, he had learned to read what was happening quickly. This time, too, he spotted an opening.

Heracross and Ariados kept pursuing Psyduck. Its own panic made its movements so unpredictable that even the twins could not anticipate where it would run next. Otherwise, they would already have beaten it.

When Psyduck ran toward Torchic again, Ethan did not order Torchic to dodge. He told it to use Ember.

Misty was astonished. The twins stared too.

They soon saw what happened. At the last instant, Psyduck changed direction. Torchic's Ember struck the place it had been heading toward and engulfed Heracross, which had been close behind it.

A rare chance! Ethan had Torchic close in and knock the struggling Heracross to the ground with a headlong charge.

“Yes!”

Misty raised a hand and cheered. With one opponent down, she could finally relax a little. She would have felt terrible if Psyduck's antics had cost them the match.

She turned to Ethan admiringly. “That was amazing! How did you know Psyduck would change direction there?”

Ethan laughed. “Do you really think I can predict everything? That's Psyduck. I can't understand what it's thinking on a normal day, let alone when it's running around like that.”

“Huh? Then the timing...”

“What timing?” He waved the question away. “I was treating Psyduck as a target too.”

“What?” Misty's eyes widened.

Ethan shrugged. “It would have been fine even if Ember hit it. Psyduck's a Water type; a little fire isn't much of a threat. It might even have calmed down.”

That made a kind of sense, and perhaps he had had no other option, but Misty could only smile ruefully. She had thought Ethan had found exactly the right moment.

Whatever his intention, the result was good. Heracross was down.

Ichiro and Jiro had only Ariados left. With its poor type matchup against Torchic, the battle had turned completely around.

Psyduck was no help, but under Ethan's commands, Torchic soon defeated Ariados with another burst of fire.

Ethan wiped cold sweat from his forehead. That Double Battle had taken more effort than he expected.

It had been hard on Torchic too. It had battled two Pokémon while its own teammate caused trouble, and it was exhausted.

If there was a next time, Ethan would never team up with Psyduck again. It was far too much trouble!

Ichiro and Jiro recalled their Pokémon. Ethan was about to go over and speak with them when white light suddenly surrounded Torchic.

He knew that light at once. Torchic was evolving! The unexpected delight brought a smile to his face.

The glow did not last long. When it faded, a Pokémon much larger than Torchic stood before them.

Torchic had evolved into Combusken.

Its main colors were much the same, but its claws were sharper and its body stronger. It looked rather like a fighting rooster from Earth.

Misty had never seen a Combusken, so she aimed her Pokédex at it.

“Combusken, the Young Fowl Pokémon. A Fire- and Fighting-type Pokémon. It runs through hills and fields to strengthen its legs and hips. In battle, it uses scorching flames from its beak and powerful kicks. The fire inside it burns fiercely as it fights. Its legs combine speed and strength, allowing it to deliver ten kicks in one second. Its sharp cry focuses its mind and intimidates opponents. With a strong fighting instinct, it keeps battling until its opponent gives up.”
