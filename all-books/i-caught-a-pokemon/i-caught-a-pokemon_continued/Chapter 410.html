Once Ethan and Marina were ready, they threw their Poké Balls at the same time.

Two flashes of blue light revealed Ethan's Scyther and Marina's Croconaw.

As Totodile's evolved form, Croconaw looked fierce. Most of its body was blue, with a cream pattern across its belly. Its mouth was filled with teeth, and red bony plates resembling a rooster's comb rose from its head.

Ethan took out his Pokédex and pointed it at Croconaw.

The Pokédex announced, "Beep! Croconaw, the Big Jaw Pokémon. A Water type and the evolved form of Totodile. The tips of Croconaw's fangs curve backward like fishhooks. When it attacks, it opens its jaws wide, and once it bites down, it will never release its grip unless its teeth fall out."

In terms of typing, the Water-type Croconaw held no advantage over the Bug/Flying-type Scyther. Marina secretly breathed a sigh of relief.

She was already under tremendous pressure facing the Indigo League champion. If her type matchup had also been unfavorable, she might as well have surrendered immediately.

The two Pokémon met one another's sharp gazes, neither willing to yield.

When the battle began, Marina could no longer contain the pressure and attacked first.

Under her command, Croconaw went all out from the very beginning!

Marina evidently understood that to defeat a powerful opponent, she had to display overwhelming momentum from the start and try to bring it down in one continuous assault. She could not allow the battle to drag on!

Water Guns flew through the air, spraying water in every direction. Before the streams had even fallen to the ground, Croconaw had already rushed in front of Scyther.

Relying on its fearless momentum, Croconaw actually traded blows with Scyther without giving ground!

This showed that Croconaw had been raised very well. In the end, however, Scyther was clearly the more skilled of the two. As Croconaw continued attacking, its stamina rapidly dwindled. Scyther found its opportunity and struck with X-Scissor, defeating Croconaw in a single decisive blow!

"I lost!"

When Croconaw fell, Marina promptly conceded.

There was no sadness on her face. She had evidently never expected to defeat a champion. In fact, lasting so long against Scyther was already enough to make her happy.

Ethan glanced at his slightly winded Scyther and gave Marina an approving smile.

"You did extremely well. Remember, I've been traveling far longer than you have, so naturally I've also experienced much more training. I believe that given some more time, you'll become an outstanding Trainer!"

Marina's face instantly lit up with incomparable joy, as though his words had given her tremendous encouragement.

"Hehe." She rubbed her nose. "Becoming a Trainer isn't really my goal. My dream is to become an outstanding Pokémon Coordinator!"

A Pokémon Coordinator...

For some reason, Ethan suddenly thought of May and Dawn. Both of them also dreamed of becoming outstanding Pokémon Coordinators.

Wait!

The mention of Pokémon Coordinators finally reminded Ethan that Marina really did become one of Johto's finest Coordinators in the future!

No wonder she had looked somewhat familiar!

"I see. That's an excellent goal. Do your best. It's an equally difficult path!"

Before Marina could say anything more, Jimmy, who had been waiting impatiently beside the field, entered the Trainer box and crowded her out.

"It's my turn! My turn!"

Jimmy's first impression on Ethan resembled Ash in several ways. This kind of opponent was sure to be much more difficult than Marina, so Ethan did not dare grow careless. He recalled Scyther.

The two sent out their Pokémon together.

This time Ethan chose Dragonair, while Jimmy sent out a Typhlosion.

Marina's eyes immediately shone when she saw Dragonair.

"Wow, it's a Dragonair! I've never seen one before. It's so beautiful!"

Girls had little resistance to adorable Pokémon, especially one as rare as Dragonair. If a battle had not been underway, judging by Marina's expression, she might have rushed over and hugged it on the spot.

Ignoring Marina as she bounced excitedly beside the field, Ethan carefully studied the Typhlosion before him.

Like Marina's Croconaw, Typhlosion was Jimmy's first Pokémon and his most trusted partner.

Typhlosion resembled an Earth weasel, but it was much larger and more ferocious.

Ethan took out his Pokédex again and aimed it at Typhlosion.

The Pokédex announced, "Beep! Typhlosion, the Volcano Pokémon. A Fire type and the final evolved form of Cyndaquil. When the air around Typhlosion begins to shimmer with heat, it has finished preparing for battle. It creates waves of scorching air with its flames to conceal itself. By rubbing its blazing fur together, it attacks with explosive blasts of fire capable of incinerating anything. When Typhlosion reaches the height of its anger, it becomes hot enough to ignite anything it touches instantly!"

Judging from evolution alone, both teenagers had left New Bark Town with Johto starter Pokémon. Marina's Totodile had only evolved into Croconaw, while Jimmy's Cyndaquil had already evolved all the way into Typhlosion.

That did not prove everything, but it showed at the very least that Typhlosion was considerably stronger than Croconaw.

As it stared at Dragonair, flames erupted around Typhlosion's neck. This was its battle stance.

Jimmy waved a hand, adopting the same strategy Marina had used.

"Typhlosion, go! Show them what we can do!"

As soon as the battle began, the two Pokémon became locked in a fierce struggle, neither able to break away from the other.

They had only been fighting for a short while when an enormous mass of thick storm clouds suddenly drifted across the distant sky.

The clouds appeared so abruptly, bringing a cool wind and blotting out the sunlight overhead, that Ethan and the others noticed them almost immediately.

The clouds were as black as the bottom of a scorched pot, with lightning flashing through them from time to time. A torrential downpour was clearly inevitable.

"It's going to rain."

Ethan was surprised. The sky had been clear only moments ago. Why had storm clouds appeared without warning?

Whatever the reason, he had to finish the battle quickly. He had no desire to be drenched to the skin.

Jimmy was even more anxious than Ethan. Fire-type Pokémon were at a severe disadvantage in the rain. Typhlosion might not fear water the way Charmander did, but the weather would still seriously impair its ability to battle.
