"Let me introduce everyone..."

Ash and his friends had not met Jimmy's group, so Ethan made the introductions.

"These are Ash, Misty, and Brock. They've traveled together since Kanto and came to Johto for the Silver Conference, just like I did. These two are Jimmy and Marina, Trainers who set out from Professor Elm's lab in New Bark Town. We met during an incident and became good friends. And this is..."

Ethan looked at the chubby boy and furrowed his brow.

Marina had introduced him before, but they had known each other for such a short time, and it had been so long, that Ethan had forgotten his name.

The boy did not mind. Seeing Ethan at a loss, he smiled and quickly said, "Hello, I'm Vincent. Jimmy, Marina, and I have been friends since childhood."

"Childhood friends!" Misty's eyes lit up. "That sounds wonderful."

Ethan smiled at the three of them. "You all made the preliminaries. Professor Elm will be pleased when he hears. I stayed at his house the last time I passed through New Bark Town, and he mentioned you. He said you were mischievous as children and often came to play at his laboratory."

"Those were good days," Vincent said wistfully. "We didn't have a care in the world. It's a shame we've grown up and left."

As he spoke, Vincent stole a glance at Marina, apparently hoping she would share the memory. She did not look at him. Her eyes had turned to Jimmy.

Ash and the others might not have thought anything of it, but Ethan and Erika had enough experience to read what those looks meant.

Ah, the misery of a love triangle...

Everyone was friendly and close in age. After just a few words, the two groups were chatting easily.

While they were talking, someone else came over.

"Ethan! Ash! We meet again!"

It was a young girl's voice. Ethan and Ash turned and immediately recognized the white baseball cap and distinctive yellow jacket.

"Casey? You made the preliminaries too?"

Casey rubbed her nose and puffed out her chest proudly. "Of course! Nothing is too hard for a fan of the Electabuzz baseball team!"

After all this time, Casey had not grown much taller, and her devotion to the team had not changed at all. She truly was a passionate fan.

She stole a glance at Ethan, and her heart fluttered.

Casey had actually seen Ash more often than Ethan, and she and Ash had become friends. Once, when they met again at the Bug-Catching Contest in National Park, Ash had even given her a Beedrill he had caught.

Casey's battling ability was only fair, though. She had needed a good deal of luck to reach the preliminaries.

She was about to say something else when applause suddenly broke out in the hall. Everyone turned. The short Pokémon League president, Charles Goodshow, walked onto a raised platform under the competitors' gaze, his hands behind his back.

Goodshow cleared his throat. "Congratulations on passing the screening trials and reaching the preliminaries. I'll explain the preliminary rules now."

The hall fell silent as everyone waited for him to continue.

"The forty-eight Trainers will be divided into sixteen groups of three," Goodshow said. "In each group, the Trainer who earns the most points from victories will advance to the final tournament."

A wave of discussion broke out as soon as he finished.

Sixteen groups with three Trainers each meant only one place from each group was available. The elimination was harsh.

Then again, compared with previous years or tournaments in other regions, it was only a different format. Anyone aiming for a strong result would have to defeat their opponents sooner or later.

When the noise faded and the hall quieted again, Goodshow said, "Those are the rules. I hope you perform to your true ability, make this year's wait worthwhile, and give the spectators your very best!"

With that, he walked off, hands still clasped behind his back, as though he had come simply to take a look at the competitors.

After he left, a staff member explained the schedule for the next two days.

The day's battles were finished. The Silver Conference would officially begin at noon tomorrow. Before the matches, as at other sporting events, there would be a torch relay carrying the sacred flame, followed by Goodshow's opening address.

From then on, there would be no more closed trials like today's. The competitors would battle in public, before spectators and cameras.

Meanwhile, advertisements and promotions would spread throughout Johto, filling the streets and appearing on televisions and people's phones, so everyone would know the event was beginning.

The chatter in the hall went on for some time before the Trainers gradually left.

With so many friends gathered, Ethan made good on his promise from that morning. He led them to a nearby restaurant, ordered plenty of food, and treated everyone to a lively meal.

Some of them had barely known one another before. By the end, they were much more at ease together.

During dinner, Casey kept looking for a chance to speak to Ethan. Misty, already watching her closely, saw every attempt coming and headed it off. Casey never found an opening.

While they ate, photos of the forty-eight Trainers who had reached the preliminaries were posted online. Interest in the tournament surged.

People began guessing who would win this year's championship.

Support for many local Johto entrants climbed, but Ethan's rose even faster, putting him in first place.

Many people had not known who he was. Once other fans explained that he was the previous Indigo League champion, they took notice.

His good looks and strength won over a wave of new admirers. Their high hopes pushed his popularity still higher, well ahead of the pack.

Of course, no one would believe the Pokémon League had not helped promote him behind the scenes.
