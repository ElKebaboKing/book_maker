"Ladies and gentlemen, after more than two weeks, the Indigo League is finally drawing to a close. Please give all our trainers and Pokémon your warmest applause as they return to the field!"

The main torch burned brightly. It was six in the evening, and the sinking sun had left the sky growing dark.

At the host's call, more than two hundred trainers emerged in orderly lines from the tunnels on both sides of the stadium. Each walked with a Pokémon at their side and waved to the stands. Spectators rose, applauded, and called out the names of their favorites.

Although many of these trainers had been eliminated early, the League had invited them back to the Indigo Plateau for the closing ceremony.

Not everyone had accepted. Someone as proud as Gary, for instance, could hardly bear to return to the place where he had lost. But a few absences did nothing to dampen the crowd's final outpouring of excitement.

Misty and Brock applauded along with the others as they searched the procession for Ash. They soon found him walking beside Richie.

With a Pikachu beside each of them, they almost looked like twins. Ash was so nervous that he walked stiffly, like a puppet on strings. Misty laughed until she could barely stand straight.

After circling the field for more than ten minutes, the trainers lined up in front of the awards platform and listened to President Goodshow's closing speech. As at the opening ceremony, he kept it short and funny, to everyone's approval.

Then the competitors came forward one by one to receive commemorative gifts from him.

Each gift resembled a pair of folded wings. Opening it revealed a finely made keepsake and a golden Poké Ball badge bearing the Pokémon League emblem. It marked their participation and honored their efforts in the tournament.

When every other trainer had received a gift, it was time for the ceremony's central event: the awards.

Ethan led Logan and Lily onto the platform. As champion, runner-up, and third-place finisher, they were presented before the crowd.

Goodshow gave Logan a silver medal and Lily a bronze one. Then he turned to Ethan.

"Congratulations, young man." His smile was kindly. "You've been outstanding. I expect great things from you, and I hope you'll keep working toward even greater achievements."

Ethan looked at the white-haired man, still so full of energy, and nodded solemnly. "Thank you, Mr. President. I will."

Goodshow took the gold medal from a staff member and placed it around Ethan's neck. Then he handed him a glittering golden trophy shaped like a Poké Ball.

A photographer stepped in front of the platform and captured the moment.

As the flash faded, the other trainers watched Ethan with a mixture of envy and sadness.

Night had fallen. Ethan was still waving from the platform when a switch clicked and every stadium light went out.

The arena fell into darkness. Before the startled crowd could react, thousands of fireworks rose into the sky, opening in brilliant patterns and lighting the night.

...

After the ceremony, Ethan and the others found a restaurant. Several dozen of them celebrated together over a lively meal.

People kept offering Ethan drinks. By the time he returned to his room that evening, he was unsteady on his feet, smelled of alcohol, and felt dizzy. A cold shower finally cleared his head.

"Whew..."

He let out a long breath and fell backward onto the soft bed, arms and legs spread wide. At last the world was quiet.

Besides the trophy and gold medal, he had won a substantial cash prize. Even after heavy taxes, he would receive more than three million. Almost overnight he had become both a champion and a millionaire, gaining fame and money together.

Yet lying there and thinking back over it all, he still felt as if he were dreaming.

He picked up his phone. Messages of congratulations had arrived from friends ever since the morning's match. Only now did he finally have time to answer them one by one.

...

After breakfast the next morning, everyone packed and prepared to leave.

Chloe went with Professor Cerise. Their short journey together had changed her in many ways, and Ethan could see she was reluctant to say goodbye. But she had school waiting in Vermilion City and had to return to her ordinary life.

Ash, Misty, and Brock were heading to Pallet Town. Delia and Professor Oak had planned a celebration there for Ash. He had only reached the Top 16, but that was an impressive achievement for a child entering his first League tournament.

After their farewells, only Ethan, Sabrina, and the bikers remained at the lodge.

When Misa and Masha approached, Ethan thought for a moment and asked Misa to speak with him alone.

"What are your plans when you get back?"

"Plans?" She looked confused. "Nothing special. We'll probably carry on as before."

"I have an idea. I'd like to put up some money so your group can have a place of its own. Somewhere to meet and spend time."

"A place of our own? What kind?" Misa's eyes brightened.

"Maybe a bar, a pool hall, or something else that could bring in money to keep it running. Then, when you come to cheer for me at another tournament, you can afford proper accommodation instead of sleeping outside."

"That's our Boss! You've thought of everything!"

Since Misa was interested, Ethan took out his phone and opened the payment screen.

"I'll leave the arrangements to you."

"Me?" She stared at him. "Why don't you handle it yourself? Aren't you afraid we'll run off with your money?"

Ethan shrugged. "If you do, then I misjudged you. But I trust my judgment, and I trust you."

Misa was touched. She thumped her chest hard in promise.

"Then leave it to me! I'll make sure your place is run properly, Boss!"

Ethan transferred two million to her. It was not an enormous sum, but they lived in a small city. If Misa budgeted carefully, it would be enough to establish something worthwhile.

He deposited the remaining million and more in the bank. He had few material wants, and that money would keep him comfortable for a long time.

Once Misa and the bikers had waved goodbye, Ethan turned to Sabrina.

"That leaves just us. Where are you going? Back to Saffron City?"

She shook her head and smiled.

"No. I've decided to set my Gym Leader duties aside for a while and travel with you."

Now it was Ethan's turn to stare.

"What? Travel with me?"

His reaction made Sabrina's face tighten. "What, am I not welcome?"

"Of course you are!" Ethan grinned and offered a rare bit of flirtation. "With someone as beautiful as you beside me, even the hardest journey would be a pleasure."

Sabrina smiled again.

"In that case, Champion, where are we going next?"

Ethan pointed into the distance and laughed.

"I've already decided. We'll keep heading south, straight for the Orange Islands!"
