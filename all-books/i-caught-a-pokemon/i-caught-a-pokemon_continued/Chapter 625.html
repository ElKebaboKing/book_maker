Wattson had spent many years as a Gym Leader and had a lifetime of experience to draw on. After a moment's thought, he found a way out of the stalemate. Defense did not suit a man who laughed as freely as he did. To beat Combusken, he would attack.

“Voltorb, use Spark!”

If Rollout alone wasn't working, he could add electricity to the charge. With Spark, electric current would cover Voltorb's body as it struck its opponent. Combined with the way Voltorb moved, it would be a rolling ball of lightning.

Combusken couldn't simply kick it away again. Touching it would mean taking an electric shock, perhaps even being paralyzed.

At Wattson's command, visible currents began to dance over Voltorb's body and leap into the surrounding air. It looked dangerous even before it moved. When it rolled toward Combusken again, its speed and force were plainly different.

Ethan became more cautious. He would not have Combusken kick it casually this time, but meeting it head-on wasn't wise either.

“Combusken, use Ember!”

Combusken spat fire. Now that Voltorb was rolling at full speed, though, it slipped out of the attack's range before the flames hit the ground.

Ethan's gaze sharpened. He could no longer intercept it midway. First Combusken needed to avoid Spark. He changed plans at once. “Dodge it!”

Combusken obeyed without hesitation, turning and running toward the far end of the field. For a while the distance between the two Pokémon remained steady.

The average Combusken might not be exceptionally fast, but an individual could have a particular talent. Ethan's Combusken was a gifted runner. Its powerful legs gave it a burst of speed that it couldn't necessarily maintain forever, but for now that burst was enough.

Combusken ran. Voltorb pursued.

Wattson had not expected it. He was an Electric-type specialist, but years of facing challengers had taught him the capabilities of many other Pokémon, Combusken included. He had been sure Spark combined with Rollout would connect. This one was faster than he had imagined.

As Voltorb used up its energy, the electricity around it weakened and finally disappeared. It was slowly gaining on Combusken, but without Spark it was merely rolling again.

Wattson's heart sank. Why had the electricity faded at that very moment? His reactions were a little slower with age. Before he could give another command, Ethan called from the opposite side of the field.

“Now! Quick Attack!”

Combusken braked, spun around, and raced straight back at Voltorb. It moved even faster than before. In a flash, it was upon its opponent.

Oh no! Wattson opened his mouth, but Combusken had already collided with Voltorb. The impact sent the round Pokémon rolling far across the field before it finally came to a stop.

Wattson watched it wobble. Then Voltorb exploded violently.

Boom!

Combusken's collision had pushed Voltorb past the point at which it would explode. The worst part for Wattson was the distance: Voltorb was much too far away for the blast to catch Combusken.

What a shame. If only it had been a little closer. Wattson sighed and shook his head.

Watt raised his colored flag. “Voltorb is unable to battle! Combusken wins!”

“Well done!” Ethan called. Combusken answered with a cry.

Wattson was disappointed to lose the first round, but he composed himself. He recalled the unconscious Voltorb and took out another Poké Ball.

“You're good. I never expected your Combusken to run like that. But the next battle won't come down to speed!”

He threw the ball. In a flash of blue light, a Magneton appeared before Ethan.

Ethan had faced its unevolved form, Magnemite, many times in the wild and in matches. This was his first encounter with a Magneton, though he knew what it was.

It looked much like three Magnemite joined together. According to what people said, strong magnetic forces bound them so tightly that they could no longer separate, giving rise to a new Pokémon.

The combination changed the placement of its magnets. Two lower magnets projected forward, while each Magnemite at the sides bore only two screws. It had three heads instead of one, but it was not three times as intelligent. Its electrical power, on the other hand, had increased considerably.

Magneton could produce powerful magnetic fields and high voltages, strong enough to evaporate moisture nearby. Getting close to one could cause severe ringing in the ears. It also emitted mysterious electromagnetic waves that disrupted communications. Precision machines sometimes failed wherever Magneton lived.

Because of that, some large cities even had rules requiring Trainers to keep Magneton in its Poké Ball. Those who let one roam freely could receive a warning from the police.

Ethan understood why Wattson had sent it out. After the trouble Voltorb had had closing the distance, the Gym Leader had chosen a Pokémon that excelled at fighting from medium to long range against Combusken.
