Crystal Steelix measured more than nine meters long, with an enormous head to match. Banette, meanwhile, stood only a little over one meter tall. One Pokémon was gigantic and the other extremely small, creating a striking visual contrast as they faced each other on the same field.

But Ethan would not underestimate the Ghost-type Pokémon because of that.

A small body often contained tremendous power!

In terms of type, Crystal Steelix was a Ground- and Rock-type, which had no type advantage over the Ghost-type Banette. That was good news for Morty.

At least he did not need to worry about a type disadvantage preventing Banette from displaying its true strength.

When the battle began, both Pokémon moved at once.

"Banette, use Shadow Ball!"

"Crystal Steelix, close in!"

Under their Trainers' commands, Banette formed a Shadow Ball and hurled it toward Crystal Steelix. Crystal Steelix charged straight at Banette with unstoppable force!

The dark sphere drew closer, but Crystal Steelix neither dodged nor evaded. It simply continued charging directly ahead!

In truth, its enormous body made attacks extremely difficult to avoid on such a small field unless it used Dig to burrow underground.

But they were indoors, and the floor outside the battlefield was covered with wooden boards. If it used Dig and accidentally broke through the foundation, it could damage the building's structure.

There was no reason to demolish someone else's Gym over a single battle.

Smack!

As expected, Shadow Ball struck Crystal Steelix. Although the attack inflicted damage, Crystal Steelix relied on its rugged body and appeared completely unconcerned. Without slowing, it rushed directly in front of Banette.

The next instant, it opened its massive jaws and brought its huge head down toward Banette in a vicious bite!

Its open mouth alone was two meters tall. Although it did not contain rows of sharp teeth like Gyarados's mouth, one bite could still swallow Banette whole!

Faced with those enormous jaws at point-blank range, Banette vanished into thin air. When it reappeared, it was already behind Crystal Steelix!

Seeing Crystal Steelix's defenseless back before it, Banette prepared to use Shadow Ball again. Before it could act, however, the sound of rushing wind filled its ears and a lightning-fast shadow flashed across its vision!

Before Banette could react, tremendous force struck it. It felt as though something had whipped across its body, sending it crashing helplessly to the ground!

Boom!

Banette slammed into the battlefield, its head spinning.

When it recovered and looked up, Crystal Steelix was slowly turning its head to stare at it with blood-red eyes. Its tail was also drawing back into place!

Unlike the bewildered Banette, Morty had witnessed exactly what happened.

After Banette dodged, Crystal Steelix appeared to have predicted where it would emerge. Without hesitation, it struck Banette with Iron Tail.

The attack had been so fast that Morty had no time to warn his Pokémon!

It was as though Crystal Steelix had anticipated Banette's movement in advance!

"Not bad..." Morty looked toward Ethan. "Whether it was Gastly before or Banette now, you always seem able to predict their movements. You must be very familiar with the way Ghost-type Pokémon attack."

Both sides appeared to have suffered damage in the opening exchange, but Morty understood that Crystal Steelix clearly held the advantage!

Ethan shrugged without explaining.

Ghost-type Pokémon had only so many ways of attacking. His Gengar had vanished and reappeared more times than Ethan could count, so he knew that routine all too well!

Once Banette pulled itself together, Morty issued another command.

"Use Night Shade!"

Terrifying black light burst from Banette's body and transformed into a phantom that swept rapidly toward Crystal Steelix!

Ethan's eyes narrowed slightly, but he still had Crystal Steelix endure the attack head-on.

The phantom arrived in an instant and struck Crystal Steelix. It grunted and could not help taking a step backward.

Night Shade's damage depended on the strength of its user. Banette was undeniably powerful, so it naturally inflicted considerable damage on Crystal Steelix.

Shaking its head, Crystal Steelix fixed Banette with its blood-red gaze.

Unexpectedly, Morty did not follow up after the successful attack. Instead, he studied Ethan for a long moment and asked, "Why didn't you have your Pokémon burrow underground to avoid Night Shade? Isn't that one of the specialties of many Ground-type Pokémon?"

He had evidently realized that Ethan was capable of dodging but had deliberately done nothing, allowing Crystal Steelix to take the attack.

It made no sense!

Ethan initially froze at Morty's question, then laughed.

As expected of a Gym Leader, he had noticed that something was wrong so quickly...

He really was perceptive.

Ethan glanced beneath Crystal Steelix and replied helplessly, "Sorry. It isn't that I don't want to use Dig. With Crystal Steelix's size, I'm worried it might destroy the Gym."

"So that's why you've been holding back?" Understanding dawned on Morty, and he smiled. "It's fine. You must have noticed before entering that the Gym was built atop a high stone platform. Situations like this are exactly why. The space beneath the stone platform is completely filled with earth, so you can fight without restraint. Even if the Gym collapses, the Pokémon League pays for its maintenance!"

"I see." After hearing Morty's explanation, Ethan was finally reassured.

The battle resumed.

This time, when Banette repeated the same tactic and used Night Shade again, Ethan swept his hand forward. Crystal Steelix lowered its head and began drilling into the ground. In only an instant, its enormous body completely disappeared beneath the battlefield!

The phantom swept over the mouth of the tunnel without touching even Crystal Steelix's shadow.

"What incredible digging speed!"

Morty froze. He had never expected Crystal Steelix to burrow so quickly. It defied all common sense!

He had battled Ground-type Pokémon before, but he had never seen one enter the earth with such speed!

At that moment, tremors suddenly rose from underground. The soil split open, and Crystal Steelix burst out directly beneath Banette, launching it high into the air!

"This is bad! Banette, dodge quickly!"

Banette tried to vanish in midair, but before it could use the technique, Crystal Steelix opened its enormous mouth and swallowed it whole!

"Banette!"

Morty's eyes widened. Banette continued struggling desperately after being swallowed, but Crystal Steelix clamped its jaws shut and compressed the space inside its mouth. The sounds of resistance gradually weakened until, at last, they stopped completely.
