The base was still rising. Ethan had no idea what Team Rocket was plotting, but there was no time to lose. He knew he could not let them succeed, so he immediately ordered Gengar and Gardevoir to attack.

Ash and the others joined in, issuing attack commands to their own Pokémon.

Ash's Pikachu and Heracross, Richie's Pikachu Sparky and Charmander, Misty's Poliwhirl, Erika's Leafeon, and the Forretress Brock had caught in Johto all charged together. A barrage of different moves flew straight toward Team Rocket.

Cassidy and Butch could not withstand the assault. They had no choice but to fall back step by step.

One Pokémon after another was struck down, yet the pair did not panic. Their task was simply to buy time. If they held out until the research data and personnel had all been moved, they would have won.

The base continued its rapid ascent. In just a minute or two, the water outside grew much brighter. Through the windows, they could even make out the faint light of the sun penetrating the surface. At this rate, the base would soon emerge from the sea!

Meanwhile, Oliver waited anxiously in the speedboat above.

He leaned over the side and kept his eyes fixed on the water. He knew he could not see anything below, but watching was the only way he could ease his worry.

Suddenly the water began to churn as though something enormous were about to burst through it!

Startled, Oliver quickly steered the speedboat away, narrowly avoiding the currents that threatened to overturn it.

Splash!!

Seawater erupted high into the air. Beneath the spray, a base resembling a steel fortress rose from the depths!!

The surging water battered Oliver's speedboat.

He immediately recognized the structure as Team Rocket's secret base, but he could not imagine how they had built it.

Inside, Ethan's group fought their way forward another dozen meters. Suddenly, the last rays of the setting sun shone across their faces. They looked up in surprise and realized that the base had reached the surface!

The orange-red sunset was so beautiful that they briefly stopped in their tracks.

Before they could recover, an even sharper alarm sounded!

Unlike the ordinary alarm they had heard earlier, this one blared urgently. A cold female voice repeated the same message over and over: "Warning! The base's automatic self-destruct sequence has been activated. Detonation in five minutes. All personnel must evacuate immediately! Warning! The base's automatic self-destruct sequence..."

Self-destruct!?

Ethan and the others frowned. Cassidy and Butch appeared just as shocked by the announcement!!

Cassidy stood stunned for a moment before bursting out angrily. "Damn it! That old man tricked us! He's going to destroy the base!"

"That can't be right," Butch said in disbelief. "Didn't he tell us to buy time? Why would he destroy it?"

"Idiot!" Cassidy gritted her teeth. "How can you still not understand? The old man never told us the base would be destroyed! He sent us to hold off the intruders so we'd be blown up along with them!!"

"What!?" Butch finally grasped the danger. "Then what do we do now?"

"Do you really have to ask!?" Cassidy seized his arm. "We run!!"

After their hurried exchange, the two abandoned Ethan's group, turned, and fled in a panic.

Seeing them go, the Team Rocket members, whose morale had already collapsed, raced after them. In an instant, the corridor was empty!

Richie watched them disappear and listened to the urgent, cold alarm. Coming back to his senses, he said quickly, "You heard that, right? This place is going to explode. We need to get out too!"

Ethan frowned.

He had not expected Team Rocket to be ruthless enough to sacrifice its own base!

On reflection, it made sense. If the base was about to fall, Team Rocket would rather destroy it than leave it for someone else to claim.

That's extreme...

The group wasted no more time and ran in the direction Cassidy and Butch had taken.

Team Rocket members were fleeing everywhere they looked. The entire base had descended into chaos, and no one had any interest in stopping them.

Still...

As they ran, Ethan could not help glancing out a window.

The more he thought about it, the stranger Team Rocket's actions seemed.

The sea was right outside. Ethan's group could leave through an exit or even break a hole in the wall and escape. Why had Team Rocket deliberately raised the base to the surface, giving them more room to get away?

If the plan was to destroy the base with them inside, Team Rocket could have done it underwater. The surrounding sea would have made escape far harder. So why bring the base up?

Was it merely to let Team Rocket's own people leave safely?

However he examined it, something felt wrong. Yet every part of the plan also seemed reasonable, leaving him unable to find an answer.

They passed through several rooms in succession. Documents lay scattered everywhere, showing how hastily Team Rocket had fled.

Strangely, although this was a research base, Ethan saw only combat personnel. There was not a single researcher anywhere along their escape route!

That deepened his suspicions.

By the time they reached the exit, it was already open. A crowd of Team Rocket members fought to get through it. Cassidy and Butch were nowhere in sight.

When it came to running away, those two were truly in a class of their own!

The countdown continued. Soon the grunts crowded around the exit had all jumped into the sea and were swimming away with all their strength. Ash and the others were about to follow when Ethan suddenly stopped.

Misty, just behind him, asked in confusion, "Ethan, why did you stop? It's about to explode. Hurry!"

Ethan thought for a moment, looked at the others, and shook his head.

"You go ahead. I can't shake the feeling that this isn't so simple. I want to stay and see what's happening before I decide what to do!"
