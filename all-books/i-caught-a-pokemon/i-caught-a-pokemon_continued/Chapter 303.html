Lance rode ahead on his Gyarados, leading the way again. Ethan and Sabrina shared a ride as before, while Kabu joined Clair on hers.

The three Gyarados crossed the lake, steered between sharp rocks beneath its surface, and pushed through swirling currents before entering the mouth of the Dragon's Den.

To Ethan, the dark water cave looked less like a dragon's mouth than a snake's.

Kabu was quick to read his expression. Holding tightly to his Gyarados's scales, he offered an explanation. "The Dragon's Den is an intricate cave system formed long ago when a Dragon-type Pokémon ran wild. Among our clan, its formal name is the Dragon Cave. That's a mouthful, though, so over time everyone shortened it to Dragon's Den."

A Dragon-type Pokémon? Ethan could hardly imagine one reshaping the land and opening such a huge water cave unless it was a Legendary or Mythical Pokémon.

"What Pokémon was it? Latias or Latios? Or another Dragon type?"

"I don't know. It happened hundreds of years ago," Kabu said. "But according to legend, when the first head of our clan went to stop it, he obtained a Dragon Fang from the Pokémon. Since then, every three years we have washed the Dragon Fang in the lake's water and prayed for the health and prosperity of every Dragon-type Pokémon in Johto."

Kabu turned to Clair. "That reminds me, miss. Cleansing Day is a little over a month away. Now that you've inherited the Gym, you'll need to conduct the cleansing ceremony yourself."

Clair gave a quiet acknowledgment. "I know."

Kabu seemed fond of conversation, and Ethan was curious. They spoke for a while longer. Perhaps Kabu assumed Ethan was a friend of Lance and Clair, or perhaps he trusted him because he was a champion. Whatever the reason, he answered almost everything Ethan asked.

They talked at length about Blackthorn Gym. Only then did Ethan learn how vast these mountains were. Even the Ice Path they had crossed belonged to the same range as the Dragon's Den.

Water rushed and splashed against the Gyarados without troubling them. While they talked, the group passed through the long entrance and reached the Den's interior.

The farther they went into the water cave, the darker it became. Before long, they could barely see ahead and had to switch on their flashlights to find their way.

A draft swept through the tunnels. Ethan could not shake the feeling that something eerie was watching him from the dark, like a hostile spirit. More likely, wild Pokémon disturbed by the visitors had their eyes fixed on the group.

Side passages of every size branched away through the water cave, leading to unknown places. Kabu said that even after hundreds of years, the Dragon Tamer clan had not explored them all.

Several hundred meters farther on, the water grew colder and faster. At a fork, Lance patted his Gyarados on the neck and brought it to a stop.

There was no sign of which way Vicious had gone.

Lance looked back at Clair and Ethan, frowning. "This is the main fork. The two channels go to different places, and the waterways beyond are complicated. One wrong turn and we'll lose our way. We haven't seen any trace of Vicious, so I can't tell which route he took."

Clair studied the passages. "What do you want to do? Split up and search?"

Lance examined them for a moment, then turned to Ethan. "What do you think, Champion?"

Ethan rubbed his chin. "If Vicious is after the Dragon Holy Land, we should plan for the worst. We can't count on the maze to stop a notorious Pokémon hunter. He must have some way of finding the right route. I say we go straight to the Holy Land."

Clair nodded in agreement. "That's what I think too."

With everyone in agreement, Lance led them onward.

The three Gyarados wound left and right through the water cave for another half hour. At last they plunged over a vast underground waterfall into a cold pool and reached the far side of the cavern.

Ethan knew that if he had been traveling alone, he would never have dared take such a drop. The waterfall was surely the Dragon Holy Land's final barrier.

They left the water cave, and the view suddenly opened into light.

A huge valley spread before Ethan. Sunlight fell on green grass, flowering plants, and trees as far as he could see. In a prominent patch of flowers, two or three Dragonite lay basking in the sun. Several Dragonair and Dratini stayed close beside them.

"We're here." Lance looked at Ethan with a touch of pride. "This is a paradise for Dragon-type Pokémon and the secret sanctuary of our clan: the Dragon Holy Land!"

Ethan gazed around in surprise. "I thought the Holy Land was inside the cave. It's a valley? Why didn't we just fly here?"

"The route we took is the only way in," Clair explained. "A mysterious power protects this place. From above, you would see nothing but bare mountain peaks. You couldn't find the valley at all."

"I see."

Even so, Ethan looked around again. Groups of Butterfree fluttered overhead. A few Rattata chased one another across the grass, while Bellossom and Hoppip played farther away.

"There are plenty of Pokémon besides Dragon types here!"

"Of course," Lance said with a smile. "The mysterious power of the Dragon-type Pokémon keeps the flowers blooming through every season, and that draws many other Pokémon here. A healthy, complete ecosystem needs a variety of species."

Ethan nodded.

As for the Pokémon he thought of as dragons, he could see only Dragonite, Kingdra, Gyarados, and their earlier forms. There did not seem to be any especially rare Dragon-type Pokémon.

For a place with a name as grand as the Dragon Holy Land, it still has the limits of its region...
