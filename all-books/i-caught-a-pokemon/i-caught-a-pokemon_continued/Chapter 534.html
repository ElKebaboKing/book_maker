Annie was focused on the fight in front of her and did not notice that the hostages had been freed. Inside the machine, Oakley was busy operating something and paid Ethan's group no attention either.

Gardevoir took Lorenzo by one hand and Bianca by the other, then used Teleport to carry them behind the battle.

Before she had even found her footing, Bianca said urgently, "Ethan, Ash, please save Latios!"

Lorenzo was more concerned about the Soul Dew. "Be careful," he warned Ethan. "They've figured out how to use the jewel and the machine. The entire city of Altomare is under their control now!"

Ethan nodded. They had already seen what the Soul Dew could do: raise barriers throughout the city and revive fossil Pokémon. Both were powerful abilities, and he had no idea what else the women might unleash.

From across the room, Annie heard Bianca and Lorenzo and finally realized they had escaped. She did not care. She and Oakley already had Latios and the Soul Dew. The hostages had served their purpose.

Jolteon used its speed to keep the fossil Pokémon from getting close, attacking with a variety of Electric-type moves from a safe distance. Ash's Totodile, Bayleef, Cyndaquil, and Noctowl were fighting well too.

Pikachu stood out most of all.

Its small body let it dart among the larger Pokémon. It sprang onto a Kabutops's head, leaped to an Aerodactyl's back, and unleashed Thunderbolt, knocking out several nearby fossil Pokémon at once.

Working together, they brought down one attacker after another. The revived Pokémon had numbers on their side, but little else. The battle seemed to be turning in Ethan's favor.

When another Aerodactyl fell, Annie looked toward Oakley inside the machine. "If you don't do something soon, we're going to be in trouble!"

"I know!" Oakley's voice came from the sphere. She sounded entirely calm. "Don't worry. They're about to see the Soul Dew's true power!"

At first, Ethan and the others noticed nothing. They kept fighting the fossil Pokémon. Then they realized something was wrong.

Seawater was flowing in from every direction, through the doors and windows in ways water should not move. In moments, it gathered into a small waterspout that surged toward them.

"Noctowl, blow it apart!"

Ash recognized the danger before Ethan could act. He ordered his Shiny Noctowl to disperse the spinning water.

Noctowl beat its wings and sent a fierce gust across the room. The waterspout twisted under the force. Spray scattered everywhere and fell across the floor.

But as soon as the wind stopped, the water gathered again. With more seawater still pouring in, the vortex grew larger.

"You two, get outside!"

Ethan turned to Lorenzo and Bianca. They understood how dangerous the fight had become and knew they could do little by staying. After one anxious look at Latios, they nodded and ran out of the cathedral.

The waterspout pulled at everything around it. As it moved, it tore up the floor and sent pieces flying, drawing nearby objects into its center.

Even the mindless fossil Pokémon, intent only on attacking, were smashed by their own side's waterspout. Many of their fossil bones were sucked into it.

Ethan turned to Ash. "We can't let it grow any bigger. Let's break it apart together!"

"Got it!"

They called their Pokémon to them and ordered a combined attack. Multicolored bursts of power flew toward the waterspout at once.

Ethan had Gengar add his strength, hoping to destroy it completely.

Seven or eight attacks struck almost simultaneously. The waterspout burst into a shower of water that rained onto the floor. A moment later, the seawater flowed back together and formed the vortex again.

"It's useless!" Oakley poked her head halfway out of the sphere and laughed at them. "It's made of seawater, and the sea has more water than you could ever use up. Break it apart once, and it'll simply come back!"

Ash lowered his voice. "Ethan, what do we do?"

"If attacking the waterspout won't work, we'll stop them at the source." Ethan pointed at the rings holding Latios. "You free Latios. I'll smash the machine."

"Okay!"

They split up and ran around the massive vortex toward their separate targets.

At Ash's command, his Pokémon attacked the iron rings restraining Latios. The rings were made of something extraordinarily tough, and they seemed to absorb the power of every move that struck them. Electric attacks, flames, and water all disappeared into the metal.

Ash gritted his teeth. "Use Tackle!"

Noctowl dived from above and struck the rings. But they could discharge electricity too. The moment Noctowl hit them, the energy they had absorbed erupted as an electric shock.

Crackling sparks filled the air.

Light blazed around Noctowl. It was thrown backward and knocked out by that single shock, its feathers scorched.

"Noctowl!"

Ash rushed over and lifted Noctowl into his arms. He looked back at the machine, helpless and frustrated.

Meanwhile, Ethan was closing in on the other side.

To keep anyone from disturbing her younger sister, Annie sent Corsola and Ariados to block him.

Those two Pokémon were no match for Ethan's Gardevoir and Jolteon, especially with Gengar nearby, ready to join in.

After only a few exchanges, both defenders went down. Only Annie remained between Ethan and the machine.
