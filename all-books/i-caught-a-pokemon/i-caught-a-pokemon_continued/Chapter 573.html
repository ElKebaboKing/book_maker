Misty stared at Ethan's rest area. "Wait—is his Gengar going to battle?"

"It can't be..." Ash's mouth fell open. "How is Harrison supposed to fight if Gengar joins in?"

As Ash had said before, knowing Ethan's team made him fear Gengar more than any of the others.

Brock rubbed his chin and sighed. "It looks like Gengar will fight. What worries me is that Harrison barely knows Ethan, much less how frightening that Gengar is. How will he face it?"

The three fell silent and looked toward Harrison with helpless sympathy.

Harrison knew nothing of their concern. When the break ended, he slapped his cheeks to steady himself and walked resolutely back to the field.

Whatever happened next, he had to win!

Then he saw Gengar beside Ethan and stopped short.

What was this Gengar doing here? Harrison had watched every one of Ethan's tournament matches and had never seen it battle.

Was it another hidden ace? And why did the look it gave him seem almost malicious?

More urgently, Harrison had planned to send out Hypno in the fourth round. With a Gengar there to counter his Psychic-type Pokémon, he abandoned the idea.

The referee called for his choice before he could work out another strategy. Harrison decided quickly and took out his fourth Poké Ball.

"Go, Swellow!"

He tossed the ball high. Blue light flashed, and a large bird Pokémon soared into the sky.

The announcer took a moment to identify it. "Oh! Another Pokémon from Hoenn! Harrison has brought us another surprise!"

Given a choice, Harrison would have preferred not to rely on Pokémon caught in Hoenn.

He had come to Johto to catch new Pokémon and challenge the Silver Conference. What was the point of doing that if he fought only with his old Hoenn team?

But Ethan was too strong. Harrison had to use everything he had if he wanted a chance to beat him.

Ethan looked up at the bird.

Swellow, the evolved form of Taillow, resembled a swallow. Most of its plumage was dark blue, with red markings on its forehead, neck, and the tips of its tail feathers. It was said to roam in search of warm lands, wheeling gracefully high above the ground. When it spotted prey, it dived and caught it in sharp claws.

As with Kecleon, Ethan knew this species well.

He had played many modified versions of the games. Apart from Kanto's Pokémon, those of Hoenn were the generation he knew best. Ash would also catch a Swellow when he traveled through Hoenn in the animated series Ethan remembered. Many viewers would recognize it.

It was a shame Ethan had no bird Pokémon. He would have liked to stage a battle between two of them.

His other Pokémon had all fought recently and needed no special training.

There was nothing for it. Gengar could take this round.

Ethan turned to it and tipped his chin toward the field. "This one's yours."

"Heh, heh, heh..." Gengar grinned and floated forward. "Leave it to me. I finally get to have some fun!"

Once it reached the center, Gengar did not speak again in front of the crowd. That would have caused an uproar. It simply crooked a finger at Swellow, wearing a mocking half smile. The challenge was plain enough to make Swellow furious.

Its proud nature had been there since it was a Taillow. After evolving, Swellow was even less willing to let another Pokémon challenge it.

Harrison chose an aggressive opening.

At his command, Swellow used Focus Energy, sharpening its concentration so its next attacks would be more likely to strike a vital point. Then it used Aerial Ace, streaking over Gengar's head before turning sharply and diving at it.

Ethan stood in his Trainer's area with his hands in his pockets. He said nothing.

He had left the entire round to Gengar.

Just as Swellow reached it, Gengar changed shape. Its body became a bird-catching net, and Swellow flew straight into it.

"What?!"

Harrison's face changed. The announcer and spectators were just as astonished.

"Incredible!" the announcer shouted. "How did it do that? It changed the shape of its body! Did it use Hypnosis on all of us?"

Ash and his friends knew better. They had seen how powerful this Gengar was.

Back at Maiden's Peak, when it was still a Gastly, it had already shown remarkable transformations. It could become people, Pokémon, or even inanimate objects. It could speak human language too, unlike any other Gastly they had met.

Harrison recovered from his first shock. "Swellow, get out of there!"

Swellow struggled inside the net, trying to tear it open. But the net was Gengar's own body. It had no intention of letting Swellow break through.

Gengar was only teasing it. After letting it struggle for a while, Gengar laughed, returned to its usual shape, and released it.

Swellow hurried into the sky and stared down at Gengar, still shaken.

Its instincts told it Gengar was immensely strong—and had a nasty streak.

Harrison rubbed his temples. He wanted to tell himself he had imagined what had happened. He could not.

He forced himself to settle down and called up to Swellow. "Don't be afraid. Attack from a distance this time. Use Air Slash!"

Swellow beat its wings and sent two crescent blades of compressed air toward Gengar.

Gengar grinned and sank into its own shadow. The blades cut through the place where it had stood and struck nothing.

"It... disappeared!"

The announcer searched the field with wide eyes. Unlike Harrison's Kecleon, Gengar truly seemed to have vanished. Could it turn invisible too?

"Where did it go?" Harrison asked. The spectators might be excited by the spectacle, but he was frightened.

He had faced Pokémon stronger than his own before without feeling this way. Gengar's unknown abilities left him with no confidence at all.

Fear came from what he did not understand.

Swellow searched from above with its sharp eyes. It could not find Gengar either.

Then it heard a sound behind it. Before it could turn, Harrison shouted, "Behind you!"

Swellow twisted around. A giant flyswatter came down on it. The heavy blow darkened its vision and sent it plunging into the ice.

It shook its head. The dizziness had barely begun to pass when it looked up and saw Gengar in its normal form floating overhead.

Now Swellow understood. That wretched Gengar was not trying to fight properly. It was playing with it.

Swellow screeched and took off. Its pride fed its anger until it could no longer think clearly.

Blue flames seemed to flare around it. Its power rose, its wings folded, and it hurled itself at Gengar.

"Brave Bird!" the announcer cried. "After Gengar's tricks, Swellow has lost patience. It wants to settle this head-on!"

Swellow was one of the Pokémon Harrison had saved for the second half. It was strong enough to know an advanced move like Brave Bird.

Even so, it would not be easy to hurt Gengar.

Gengar faced the charge without concern. Rings of light appeared in its eyes and traveled toward Swellow. Too angry even to consider dodging, Swellow flew straight through them.

"Swellow..."

Harrison recognized the rings. Gengar was using Hypnosis, a move many Pokémon knew.

He watched anxiously. He wanted to recall his enraged Swellow but hesitated.

Swellow had used Brave Bird without his command. It was not quite as powerful as the similarly named Sky Attack, but it was still formidable. If it hit Gengar, it might knock it out at once.

Hypnosis did not always land. At Swellow's speed, and in its anger, perhaps it could push through the attack.

But Brave Bird would also hurt Swellow through recoil. Harrison hated the thought of that, and if Gengar dodged, the round was almost certainly lost.

While he wavered, Gengar watched Swellow close in and gave a cold smile.

Did it think it could break through his Hypnosis like this?

It was underestimating him.

Gengar opened its eyes wide. The rings that had been flowing slowly from them suddenly came faster, and their color deepened to purple-black.

Swellow's gaze wavered for an instant.

That instant was enough for Hypnosis to take hold. Its thoughts seemed to freeze. Sleepiness dragged at it, and its target grew hazy.

At its tremendous speed, the remaining distance disappeared in a heartbeat. Swellow dipped downward as it lost focus. It fought to open its eyes, only to find it was no longer heading toward Gengar. It was diving at the ground.

It was too late to correct course. Hypnosis took its consciousness, and a hundredth of a second later Swellow slammed into the ice.

Boom!

The impact shattered the ice and exposed the frozen earth below. Apart from a spray of snowflakes that landed before Gengar, the crash did not touch it.

"Swellow!" Harrison cried. Everything had happened too quickly for him to react. He should have recalled it when he had the chance.

Regret would not help now. Even if he had withdrawn Swellow, he did not know which of his Pokémon could face this strange Gengar.

"That was an... unusual battle," the announcer said, stumbling over the word. "Ethan's Gengar has defeated Swellow in a most surprising way. As with his Crystal Steelix, Ethan has shown us something none of us expected!"

Gengar floated back to Ethan wearing an enormous grin.

"Not bad," it said before Ethan could speak. "It wasn't enough to stretch my muscles, but I had fun."

Ethan shook his head helplessly. It was one thing to say that privately. If Harrison heard it, he would be furious.

His strong Pokémon had been hurt, mocked, and driven to a desperate last attack, and Gengar called it a game. Anyone might lose their temper at that. A hotheaded Trainer might even try to fight Gengar himself.

Ethan found the remark provoking too, but Gengar was his own Pokémon. He let it pass.

Fortunately, after Swellow's defeat, Harrison saw Gengar staying beside Ethan. It did not seem ready for another round, and he quietly breathed a sigh of relief.

At least he would not have to face it again.

His position was worse than ever, though. Four of his Pokémon were down. Elimination was drawing close.
