After entering Violet City, Ethan and Erika went to the Pokémon Center for a short rest. Before sunset, Ethan took Erika to the northern side of the city. They crossed a lake and once again arrived at Sprout Tower.

This was Ethan's second visit. Ordinarily, he would have had no reason to tour the place again. But Erika was visiting Johto for the first time and had never seen Sprout Tower, so naturally he had to let her experience its scenery.

Standing at the temple entrance and looking at the familiar tower, Ethan could not help feeling emotional.

Only a few days had passed, yet everything around him seemed the same while the person beside him had changed. Sabrina was no longer accompanying him; another woman had taken her place.

Beneath the setting sun, golden light washed over Sprout Tower and gave it an entirely different appearance.

The temple bell rang beside them. Its sound drifted far upon the wind, reaching all the way into central Violet City.

The two entered the temple and climbed to the top of the tower. At first, Ethan intended to explain the history of Sprout Tower to Erika. But halfway through, he discovered that she knew even more about it than he did.

Seeing his astonishment, Erika covered her mouth and laughed softly before taking out her travel guide once more.

"Just like Greenfield, the guide describes this place in great detail. I simply didn't expect the scenery to be even more beautiful in person than the book suggests!"

Is this place really that impressive?

Ethan raised an eyebrow in surprise.

Neither Sprout Tower nor its temple held much interest for him, yet Erika explored them with great enthusiasm.

On second thought, perhaps this had something to do with her upbringing. As the young lady of a distinguished family, she had grown up surrounded by classical and traditional things. She also loved nature, so this place suited her tastes perfectly.

They toured the grounds for a while. When night arrived, they ate a free vegetarian meal at the temple, then returned to the Pokémon Center to rest.

Before going to sleep, Ethan lay on his bed and browsed the internet on his phone. By chance, he noticed a news article.

Its headline read, Indigo League Champion Sets His Sights on the Silver Conference!

Intrigued, Ethan opened the article. The first thing he saw was a photograph from his battle against the Week Siblings outside Violet City earlier that day.

The photograph was clear and had been taken from close range, probably by one of the spectators.

It captured the exact moment his Larvitar defeated Slugma.

The article's author clearly had high expectations for Ethan. The entire piece praised his exceptional strength and claimed that if he entered the Silver Conference, he would reach at least the semifinals and achieve an excellent result.

An excellent result...

Ethan set his phone aside and stared at the ceiling of his room.

Moonlight shone through the window and fell across his face, illuminating his bright eyes.

Ever since winning the Indigo League Conference, his ambitions had grown along with his confidence.

For the Silver Conference, he had only one goal, just as the article suggested. That goal was the championship!

...

After breakfast, Ethan and Erika left Violet City. They walked west for less than half an hour, gradually leaving the city behind until they reached the edge of a forest.

A fork in the road lay before them, presenting Ethan with two different routes.

The first followed the main road south toward Azalea Town, where he could challenge Azalea Gym.

The other passed through the forest and continued west. Once they reached the area near the National Park, they could turn north toward Ecruteak City and challenge Ecruteak Gym.

Both routes would eventually lead to Cianwood City, forming a circular journey around the region. In truth, their choice made little difference.

Only the order of their destinations and the scenery they experienced along the way would change.

But since they were traveling together, Ethan could hardly make the decision alone. He asked Erika for her opinion.

Erika thought for a moment. "If either route works, why don't we visit Ecruteak City first?"

When Ethan looked toward her, she explained with a smile, "Ecruteak City is very famous. I've wanted to see it for a long time!"

"Ecruteak City... All right, then we'll challenge Ecruteak Gym first!"

Since Erika had chosen their direction, Ethan nodded and entered the forest with her.

Their footsteps sounded along the path.

They walked side by side along a narrow dirt road. From time to time, wild Pokémon poked their heads out from behind the trees and studied the two humans with curiosity. Once noticed, however, the timid Pokémon immediately jerked their heads back out of sight.

The forest was not especially dense, but it covered an enormous area. Even after walking for a long time, they had yet to emerge from it.

The sun climbed steadily higher. Fortunately, the shade of the trees protected them, and the journey did not feel hot.

At noon, they stopped for water. Ethan leaned against the trunk of a thick tree and opened the map.

His finger moved across the phone's screen. A moment later, he gave a soft sound of surprise.

Noticing the change in his expression, Erika looked at him curiously. "What is it?"

"There seems to be a school up ahead."

"A school? Here?" Erika's eyes widened. When Ethan nodded, she asked with curiosity, "Why would anyone build a school in such a sparsely populated forest?"

"Let me see... It's called the Pokémon Ninjutsu Academy!"

"Would you like to visit?"

"Sure." Ethan glanced down at the map again. "It's directly along our route anyway, so we might as well take a look."

After resting a little longer, the two resumed their journey. Ten or twenty minutes later, they rounded a massive boulder and immediately saw several tall buildings in an ancient style rising behind a wall made of wood and stone.

They followed the road to the academy's entrance. Finding the gate open, they walked straight inside.

An open training ground lay beyond the entrance. Dozens of men and women dressed in ninja outfits of various colors were practicing something with a group of Pokémon.

As Ethan and Erika surveyed the scene, an elderly yet vigorous voice spoke behind them.

"May I ask what brings the two of you here?"

Startled, Ethan hurriedly turned toward the voice. An elderly man stood watching them quietly. He wore a dark blue ninja outfit, appeared to be in his sixties or seventies, and had a full head of silver hair.

When did he arrive!?

He had approached without a sound. Ethan had heard absolutely nothing!

"I'm sorry. We're travelers passing through the area. We noticed a school here and wanted to take a look."

"I see." The old man nodded with a cheerful smile. "Allow me to introduce myself. I am a ninja and the headmaster of this academy. Welcome to our school!"
