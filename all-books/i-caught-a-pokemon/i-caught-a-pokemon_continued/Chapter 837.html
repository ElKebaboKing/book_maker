Ethan had not expected to meet a Pokémon Ranger here. Travel was tiring, but it let him visit new places, meet new people, and encounter something unexpected each day. That freshness was the reason he loved it.

Solana led Ethan and May along the forest trail for some time. They pushed through dense brush into the forest's depths, where the way ahead was completely blocked.

Huge thorny vines twisted across the path. Some were as thick as pipes, and each seemed hundreds of meters long. They had swallowed a broad stretch of the forest.

“There are so many thorns,” May said. “They look out of place, too. Ms. Solana, what happened here?”

Solana hesitated. Then she remembered Ethan's International Police credential and decided to tell them. “Celebi is behind this.”

“Celebi?” May stared at her, then immediately looked at Ethan. He calmly looked back as if to ask why she was watching him.

May knew there was more than one Celebi in the world, so meeting one anywhere was possible. She also knew Ethan's Celebi was with Erika and could not be here. Her glance had been instinctive. She stuck out her tongue at him. Solana missed the silent exchange.

At the wall of thorns, she took out a red and black handheld device that looked rather like a folding phone. She aimed its camera at a few wild Pokémon peeking out nearby. Information appeared on the screen almost immediately.

“What's that?” May stepped closer to look.

“Our Capture Styler,” Solana said as she operated it. “It launches and controls a Capture Disc. That lets us connect with wild Pokémon and become temporary partners. It also has phone and transmission functions, and a built-in Pokémon database. Professor Hastings invented it. It's indispensable to a Ranger.”

A Capture Styler? A Capture Disc? Professor Hastings? The unfamiliar names opened up a whole field May had never seen or heard about.

“What are you doing with it now?” she asked.

“Finding a Pokémon that can handle these vines.”

Solana scanned several Caterpie, Venonat, and Metapod. She studied their information, shook her head, and turned the device toward a group of Linoone. Their details filled the screen.

Ethan took a look. Unlike a standard Pokédex, the Styler did not read its results aloud. Its data seemed fuller and more specialized, though. It showed each Pokémon's height and condition, along with its moves and Ability. If every Pokédex could do that, Trainers would be able to choose Pokémon to catch far more deliberately. What remarkable technology.

“They know Slash?” Solana's eyes brightened. “You'll do!”

The Styler changed shape in her hand, becoming something like a pistol. She aimed at the Linoone and fired. No bullet came out; instead, a small object like a spinning top shot toward them.

As it spun, an antenna extended from the Styler. Solana waved the device, guiding the Capture Disc in loops around the Pokémon. Trails of light followed it and enclosed the Linoone.

“Is that the Capture Disc?” May asked, as curious as ever.

“It is. The Styler guides the disc around a wild Pokémon. The light trail carries a Ranger's feelings to it. When our hearts connect, the capture is complete, and the Pokémon is willing to help us as a temporary partner.”

Solana made it sound simple, but Ethan knew there was more to the device. Its light line could be broken by a Pokémon's attack or another blow. The Styler would take damage and lose energy. If its energy fell to zero during a capture, the disc could even explode.

Those problems were uncommon. The part that truly troubled him was that a Capture Styler could also capture someone else's Pokémon. Ranger technology could override the hold of a Poké Ball and take its occupant. If that capability became widely known and spread beyond the Rangers, it could threaten the balance of the world.

No wonder the Rangers kept it secret. For now, at least, the organization was using its tools to protect Pokémon and nature. Ethan did not know what the future might bring.

Solana had no idea he knew their deepest secret. She concentrated on the Linoone, moving her hand faster and faster. The circling light quickened and finally entered the Pokémon.

Their expressions changed at once. Their wary, curious looks became clearer and more open, as though they understood something new. Whatever the bond truly was, the difference in them was plain.

“Capture complete!” Solana smiled. She had done this many times before. “Plusle, use Helping Hand!”

Helping Hand strengthened the moves of the Pokémon it aided. Plusle hopped in front of the Linoone, chattered to them, and made a few encouraging gestures.

“Now, use Slash!” Solana called.

The Linoone obeyed as readily as a Trainer's own Pokémon. They charged the thorns together and slashed open a path through the wall.

May's eyes shone. She went to Ethan and whispered, “Look! The Styler really is amazing. She didn't have to catch those Pokémon in Poké Balls to get their help.”

“It is. Do you want one?” Ethan teased. “You could become a Ranger. Then wild Pokémon could help you too.”

“Don't be silly! My dream is to become a Top Coordinator. I'm not changing it.” May waved the idea away, then frowned. “But why doesn't Ms. Solana use her own Pokémon? With that Styler, she can get help so easily. Does she only have Plusle?”

“Who knows?” Ethan shrugged. “Maybe there's a reason we don't know, or some unusual Ranger rule.”

While they spoke, the Linoone's claws widened the gap until it reached farther into the forest. Solana beckoned Ethan and May after her and entered the dark passage first.

As they walked, she explained where the thorns had come from.

“They appeared suddenly last night. They blocked the paths, upset the local ecosystem, and frightened many wild Pokémon away. We came as soon as we received the report. We've confirmed the cause: Celebi.”

Ethan remembered the first time he had met Celebi in the Mysterious Forest. If a Celebi had made these thorns, it had probably done so instinctively after something hurt it. Whatever had happened last night might have been much like that earlier incident.

“So that's why you've closed the forest,” he said. “A Mythical Pokémon is involved.”

Solana had been watching them as they walked. Neither had shown much surprise at Celebi's name, and that impressed her. Perhaps they had dealt with Mythical or Legendary Pokémon before.

“Yes. We've received several earlier reports of thorns growing abnormally wherever Celebi appeared. We're still investigating why it happens, but something must have happened to Celebi in each case.”

“Then the immediate priority is finding this Celebi,” Ethan said.

“Exactly.” Solana sighed. “That hasn't been easy. We searched carefully after arriving but found no trace of it. We're beginning to wonder if it has already left the forest.”

“We?” May asked. “I thought you were alone.”

A figure leaped down in front of them as soon as she said it.

“Who said she was alone? I'm right here!”

The newcomer landed steadily. He was a black-haired young man about Solana's age, dressed in a similar Ranger uniform with a red headband. A Minun stood on his shoulder where Solana carried Plusle.

“Honestly, jumping down from that height again…” Solana rubbed her forehead. “This is Lunick, my partner on this assignment.”

Plusle and Minun made the pair look like more than ordinary teammates to Ethan.

“Hello,” Lunick said to the two travelers. “If Solana brought you this far, I assume you have permission from headquarters. I won't question it. Let's work together.”

Like Solana, he made a good first impression. Seeing two Rangers here, Ethan understood that the Celebi incident must be difficult even for them.

Rangers might sound numerous because their work spanned regions and included protecting both nature and Pokémon. In fact, their ranks were small and selective. Away from headquarters, a region might have only a handful. Sending two to this one forest showed how seriously they took a Mythical Pokémon.

Ethan and May introduced themselves. Unlike Solana, Lunick recognized Ethan's name immediately.

“Wait! You're Ethan Vale, the one who won League Conferences in three regions?”

Lunick followed events beyond Ranger work more closely than Solana did. Anyone traveling through Kanto, Johto, or Hoenn who kept up with news was likely to have heard of Ethan.

Ethan smiled. “Unless there's another person with my name, yes.”

Lunick's eyes lit up. “I haven't watched your matches, but I've heard about you. They say you're an incredible Trainer.”

“Hold on.” Solana was thoroughly confused now. “Aren't you a Special Investigator of the International Police? When did you become a League champion too?”

“I told you I travel as a Trainer.” Ethan laughed. “The International Police role is a side job. Being a Pokémon Trainer is my real one!”
