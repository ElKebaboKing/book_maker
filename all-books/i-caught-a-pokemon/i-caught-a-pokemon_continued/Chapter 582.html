The mood had reached a breaking point. Everyone knew a battle was unavoidable.

They reached for their Poké Balls almost together. More than a dozen flashes of blue light filled the clearing as Pokémon appeared beside their Trainers.

Jessie and James sent out almost every Pokémon they had. Their team was still small; even counting Meowth and Wobbuffet, they had only six.

Erika, Misty, and Brock were not traveling to train their teams, so they carried relatively few Pokémon too. Erika had caught some new Grass-type partners recently, but none were ready to fight yet.

Besides, she was holding the unconscious Celebi. She had no hands free for a battle.

That left Misty and Brock to face the trio.

Even so, the two former Gym Leaders had the advantage in both numbers and strength. Barring any surprises, they should have no trouble dealing with Jessie, James, and Meowth.

But the trio had a chance. They were capable fighters in their own way, and as the attackers, they had no reason to hold back. There was also no telling what strange invention they might bring out. Misty and Brock had the better odds, but they still had to be careful.

The real problem was Vicious.

It was not that his Pokémon were especially powerful on their own. He had Dark Balls that could forcibly capture another person’s Pokémon and make them stronger and more vicious.

Back in the Dragon Holy Land, Vicious had used one to control Lance’s Dragonite, causing them no end of trouble.

“Be careful,” Ethan warned Ash. “He has Dark Balls that can steal other people’s Pokémon. Don’t let him catch any of yours.”

“That’s easy to say...” Ash suddenly felt overwhelmed. How was he supposed to battle while worrying that his opponent might steal his Pokémon?

Vicious gave them a cold smile.

Since his previous defeat, the Dragon Tamers had been hunting him. He now kept plenty of Dark Balls close at hand. They were the main reason he had escaped capture again and again and was still living well.

“Let me show you my latest addition!” He pulled a Dark Ball from his vest and hurled it. “Go, my Dark Tyranitar!”

Black light flashed. A Tyranitar that looked deeply unnatural appeared before them.

Darkness shone in its eyes and seeped from its body. Under the Dark Ball’s control, it seemed to have lost all reason. It radiated menace and destructive power.

It threw back its head and roared. Before the sound had faded over the forest, energy gathered in its mouth. An instant later, an immense Hyper Beam shot toward Ethan’s group.

“Watch out!”

The beam was visibly thick and powerful. Everyone scattered. It tore through the space between them and struck a giant tree in the distance.

That was close. Ethan had felt the heat as it passed.

The ancient tree was more than ten meters across, yet the beam punched straight through and split it in two. It went on to strike a rocky hill, tearing away a great section before it finally faded.

“How can it be... so powerful?” Ash and the others stared, finally understanding how frightening a Dark Ball could be.

The attack startled the Team Rocket trio too. They were nearest its path, and the shock wave nearly knocked them off their feet. Once they recovered, however, they were delighted by Dark Tyranitar’s strength.

With a senior executive and a Pokémon like that on their side, revenge was within reach. They could teach Ethan a lesson and steal Pikachu while they were at it!

Elated, they ordered their Pokémon to attack. Misty and Brock had to meet them in haste.

Once fighting began, everyone was drawn into it. Ash gritted his teeth and sent out Muk and Bayleef. Sammy took an old-fashioned Poké Ball shaped like a flask from his coat, twisted off its cap, and released his Charmeleon.

Young Samuel Oak was neither a Pokémon researcher nor much of a Trainer yet. His Charmeleon was not especially robust, but it had spirit. Far from cowering before Dark Tyranitar, it opened its jaws and gave the larger Pokémon a threatening cry.

It was surprising to see the venerable, kindly Professor Oak’s first Pokémon as a youth: a fierce, aggressive Charmeleon.

With Pikachu among them, their Pokémon began to close around Dark Tyranitar from both sides. Vicious, however, had no intention of facing them with only one Pokémon.

He threw two more Dark Balls. A Sneasel and a Scizor appeared beside Tyranitar, the same black light in their eyes.

The Pokémon collided at once. Ash’s partners had grown strong during his travels, but the enhanced Dark Pokémon still had a slight edge.

Dark Tyranitar in particular seemed to have strength without limit. It fired Hyper Beam again and again, apparently unaffected by the move’s usual exhaustion.

Ash and his friends had to fight their immediate opponents while watching for its attacks. They could not let their attention wander for an instant.

Ethan studied the battle for a long moment, then cautiously took a Poké Ball from his belt.

The two sides looked evenly matched. Dark Tyranitar was the deciding factor.

If they could defeat it, the battle would swing their way.

But Ethan could not send all his Pokémon against it. He needed one powerful partner fighting alone, someone he could recall at once if Vicious threw a Dark Ball.

That made the choice difficult.

At last he turned to the partner he trusted most: Gardevoir.

Her Teleport could carry her to safety if Vicious tried to capture her, greatly reducing the danger.

“Gardevoir, find a way to draw its attention!”

Gardevoir moved toward Dark Tyranitar and attacked with Confusion. The move had worked countless times before, but Tyranitar’s strength was too great. She could not lift it.

Surprised, Ethan immediately ordered the stronger Psychic. This time Gardevoir drove Dark Tyranitar back several steps.

That got its attention. It turned on her and fired another Hyper Beam.

One hit from that enormous column of energy could leave her gravely hurt, if it did not kill her outright. Other Pokémon might have struggled to dodge it. Gardevoir vanished with Teleport and reappeared beyond its path.

She escaped. The forest behind her did not.

Trees and rocks shattered in an instant. Splinters and fragments covered the ground, and the beam gouged a deep furrow through the earth.

Dark Tyranitar was terrifyingly strong. The Dark Ball’s power had turned its Hyper Beam into destruction on a scale Ethan had seldom seen.

If it kept firing, the ruined trees would be the least of their concerns. A forest fire would be far worse.

“Gengar!” Ethan called down to his shadow. “Help her. Use Hypnosis!”

Gengar emerged without a word. It too seemed to understand the danger. Rings of hypnotic light spread from its wide eyes toward Dark Tyranitar.

The Hypnosis hit.

But the Dark Ball’s control was stronger. Dark energy filled Tyranitar’s body and stimulated its mind. The move had no effect.

Ethan clicked his tongue. What a nuisance.

From the cockpit of his spider-shaped machine, Vicious watched with a smug smile.

Dark Balls increased the strength of the Pokémon they controlled, but the degree of that increase depended on the Pokémon. In the Dragon Holy Land, he had controlled Lance’s Dragonite. To be honest, its boost had disappointed him.

This Tyranitar was different. Perhaps it was powerful to begin with, or perhaps it was especially suited to the Dark Ball. Together, they produced far more than either could alone.

If he controlled several Tyranitar like this, would anyone be able to defeat him?

Before he could enjoy the thought, the fight changed elsewhere.

Jessie, James, and Meowth were simply no match for Misty and Brock. Even with more Pokémon on their side, they soon began to lose.

They were cunning enough to recognize it. Before they could be sent flying, the trio slipped away and escaped.

Their departure put Vicious under immediate pressure.

Misty and Brock joined the other battle, attacking from the sides. They chose their targets well. Instead of challenging Tyranitar first, they helped Ash and Sammy defeat Dark Sneasel and Dark Scizor.

With those two out of the way, everyone turned on Dark Tyranitar together.

For all its strength, it could not answer so many attacks at once. It soon had no chance to destroy anything else and was forced to defend itself.

Vicious silently cursed the useless trio. His hand crept toward the remaining Dark Balls on the front of his vest.

Sammy had been watching him carefully ever since Ethan warned them that he could steal Pokémon.

He spotted the movement and cried out, “He’s going to use his Dark Balls! Be careful!”

Everyone looked at Vicious and saw what he intended. Their assault faltered.

Ethan assessed the field. “We can’t keep fighting like this. Celebi needs treatment. Let’s get out of here.”

Ash and the others regained their heads. They nodded, realizing that prolonging the battle would do Celebi no good.

They exchanged a look and ran together toward an opening in the trees.

The trio was no longer blocking their path, and Dark Tyranitar could not catch up from behind. Leaving proved surprisingly easy.

They plunged into the forest and ran a long way. When they finally looked back, Vicious was nowhere in sight.

He was not pursuing them. For the moment, they could breathe again.

Back at the battleground, three heads rose from the bushes as soon as Ethan’s group had gone.

Jessie, James, and Meowth saw Vicious still sitting in his mechanical spider, with Dark Tyranitar unharmed beside him. Smiling ingratiatingly, they approached.

“Lord Vicious.” James rubbed his hands together. “You’re amazing! You actually drove Ethan and those brats away.”

Vicious glanced at the cowards and gave a contemptuous snort.

They belonged to Team Rocket, but he had heard their names and all the tales of their disastrous exploits. He had assumed those stories were exaggerated jokes. Now he saw that they really were hopeless.

Fortunately, he had never counted on them for much.

When Vicious did not answer, James glanced in the direction Ethan had fled. “Um... Aren’t you going after them? If we wait, they’ll get too far away.”

“What would you know?” Vicious smiled coldly. “Catching prey, especially something as rare as Celebi, is more interesting when there’s a challenge. A hunter should enjoy the pursuit.”
