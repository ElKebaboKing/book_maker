Bang...

The airship struck Lightning Island and slid dozens of meters farther before coming to a halt near the volcano's crater.

Even with Sabrina's psychic power shielding them, the violent impact left everyone dizzy. Ethan helped her up, rubbed his temples, and looked around.

"Is everyone all right?"

"I'm fine!" Misty answered first.

"Me too!" Melody said.

Ash, Tracey, and Team Rocket appeared unharmed as well. Ethan pointed toward the hole the birds had made in the hull.

"After a fall like that, I don't know if this thing might explode. Let's get out of here."

Team Rocket needed no second warning. Jessie, James, and Meowth bolted for the opening before anyone else moved. Jessie and James even glanced back to pull faces at the others as they ran. Their Pokémon might be weaker, but when it came to fleeing, they considered themselves experts.

Ethan found their antics ridiculous. He said nothing and followed with Ash and the others.

Only after escaping through the torn hull did they grasp the airship's size. Its body, studded with propellers and wind-turbine blades, was more than five hundred meters across. Beside it, the people looked like ants. An explosion from something so vast could destroy them all.

They ran along the length of the hull for several minutes before they finally reached solid ground. Everyone was breathless except Sabrina, who had flown with her psychic power and looked as composed as ever.

A cold wind carrying snowflakes swept over them. They shivered. Melody looked around in disbelief.

Lightning Island had been green. Now it was a frozen world. Hard ice covered the ground, the trees, and the rocks. Fire Island and Ice Island, the surrounding sea, and even distant Shamouti Island appeared sealed in it. White stretched as far as they could see, like a warning that the end of the world had begun.

"How did it become like this?" Melody murmured.

"The God of Ice must have noticed the other two were gone," Ethan said. "Just as Zapdos tried to claim Fire Island, Articuno seems to be using its power to turn all four islands, Shamouti included, into its territory."

His breath fogged in the cold. The others were still staring, so he urged them on. "Don't stop. It isn't safe here. Keep moving."

As soon as he said it, the airship began sliding again. Equipment broke loose from its upper surface and fell around them.

Boom!

Debris smashed into the ice, spraying shards into the air. They covered their heads and ran. A huge blade fell onto a nearby shrine and brought the stone structure down with a crash. A glowing yellow orb bounced from the ruins and rolled to Ash's feet.

"The treasure from Lightning Island!" Melody recognized it at once.

It resembled the orb from Fire Island, but golden light flowed inside it in place of flame.

Ash reached down to pick it up. Lightning flashed past him, shattered the ice at his feet, and knocked the orb away.

After wrecking the airship, Moltres and Zapdos had gone back to fighting each other. Articuno had appeared from somewhere and joined them. The three legendary birds attacked without yielding, their moves tearing across the island.

The orb rolled farther away. Ash dived after it and caught it in both hands. They now had the second treasure for the festival.

"Look over there!" Misty pointed into the distance.

The sailboat they had arrived in had fallen from the airship into a lake on the island. After everything it had endured, it still looked intact.

"Thank goodness!"

Team Rocket scrambled into its cabin as if they had found a lifeline. Ethan and the others saw that it was their best chance of leaving Lightning Island and climbed aboard too.

A huge ball of fire struck the lake, splashing water over them. Misty wiped her face and called before she had even settled into a seat. "Melody, get us moving!"

"I know!" Melody snapped. Her expression was serious, and her movements were swift and practiced. The engine started, and she turned the sailboat toward the sea.

At the lake's outlet, Articuno swept past and sealed their path with Ice Beam.

"We're going to hit it!" Melody cried.

Ethan was about to tell Haunter to break the ice when Zapdos flew overhead. Brilliant lightning burst from its body, smashing the barrier and tearing away much of the outlet itself.

The lake poured toward the sea like water through a broken dam, carrying chunks of ice with it. The current seized the sailboat, hurled it out of Lightning Island, and flung it into the air.

"Aaah!"

Misty and Melody screamed. Ethan's heart leaped into his throat as the boat began to fall. Then a thick waterspout broke through the ice on the sea below, rose dozens of meters, and caught the boat squarely.

Everyone stared. Why had a waterspout appeared at that moment, and why had it saved them?

It carried the sailboat toward Shamouti Island. Ethan was the first to react. He hurried to the rail and searched the water below, but he could see nothing beneath the thick ice.

Then they heard a low, ethereal call.

Wooo...

It sounded a little like a whale, only longer and more haunting. The loneliness in the sound made Melody's eyes widen.

"That voice... Has Lugia appeared?"
