A few days earlier, as soon as Scott had told Ethan where the Battle Pyramid was, he had set out for it himself.

Scott came to watch even when an ordinary challenger reached the final facility. This time, a man with League titles from three regions was about to battle the strongest of the Frontier Brains. The match promised to be spectacular. How could he miss it?

He had been a long way off when he sent the location. To make certain he arrived in time, he had hurried all the way here—only to wait several days for Ethan.

If he'd known, he would not have rushed so much. At least Ethan had finally arrived.

But something was odd. Scott looked from Brandon to Ethan and back again. “How did you two end up together? Did you already know each other?”

“No. We happened to meet by chance.” Ethan shook his head and told Scott what had just happened in the underground ruins. Scott stood stunned for a while after hearing the story.

“An ambitious King of Pokélantis? What a shame I wasn't there. I'd have liked to see what a human soul actually looks like.”

May covered her mouth with a laugh. “Mr. Scott, you're lucky you weren't there. If you had been in that room, he might have possessed you!”

“True!” Scott laughed. “There aren't many men as handsome as me. I'm sure the King of Pokélantis would have wanted my body!”

After the brief joke, Brandon and Scott led Ethan and May into the Battle Pyramid. They passed through a doorway built in the same style as the pyramid and went straight to the battle hall on the first floor.

The hall was much plainer than the exterior. It contained a standard battlefield and nothing else, not even a table or chair. What it did have was space: even when the pyramid was airborne, there was plenty of room to battle.

A young man came to greet them. He looked about Ethan's age, though his face was somewhat boyish. He wore a headband and expedition clothes, and had an open, cheerful manner.

“Master, why are you back so early today?” Then he noticed Ethan and May behind Brandon. “Have challengers arrived?”

Scott introduced him before Brandon could answer. “Mr. Vale, this is Samuel, Brandon's apprentice and assistant. He referees matches here at the Battle Pyramid. He's a friendly fellow; I'm sure you'll get along. Samuel, this is Ethan Vale, the man I've been telling you about these past few days.”

“This is him?” Samuel's eyes lit up. He hurried over and looked Ethan up and down, curious about a man who seemed to be his own age.

“You're Ethan Vale? The one who won League tournaments in three regions? Wonderful! Ever since Mr. Scott told me about you, I've been looking forward to your arrival. After all these days, you're finally here!”

Brandon's expression cleared as he listened. No wonder Ethan's name had sounded familiar. He was the man Scott and Samuel had been discussing all week.

Three regional League championships? This would be no ordinary challenger. Perhaps he would even make Brandon fight seriously for the first time in a while.

Ethan did not pretend to be modest. He chatted with Samuel for a moment, gracious and at ease. There was no point in playing down his achievements when everyone already knew them. A Champion should carry himself with some confidence.

Brandon had already gone to the Trainer's position on one side of the field. Ethan gave the others a small wave and walked to his own position. Samuel hurried to the referee's area.

He was about to explain the usual rules when Brandon raised a hand to stop him. Samuel looked at him, puzzled. Brandon kept his eyes on Ethan.

“A Champion has come to challenge me. We needn't use the ordinary rules. Ethan, how would you like to battle?”

He's letting me choose the rules? That suited Ethan perfectly.

He smiled. “I've heard you have three rather unusual Pokémon, Mr. Brandon. I came to see them for myself.”

Brandon raised an eyebrow. He knew precisely which three Ethan meant. But he had never shown them to outsiders. How did Ethan know?

“You know which Pokémon I have?”

“Not all of them,” Ethan replied. “I don't know the rest of your team. But I have heard a little about those three.”

Scott was lost. “What three Pokémon? What are you two talking about? The more I listen, the less I understand.” Did Brandon have a secret even Scott knew nothing about?

May, by contrast, stayed calm. Before coming here Ethan had told her a little about Brandon, including some of the Pokémon he might use. She even knew which of Ethan's Pokémon he intended to send against them.

Seeing that his master did not mean to keep the matter hidden, Samuel turned to Scott. “Mr. Scott, how much do you know about the legendary giants?”

“The legendary giants?” Scott frowned. “The eternal Pokémon from the old stories? I think they're a group of Legendary Pokémon from Hoenn: Regirock, Regice, and Registeel. I've heard they came from rock, icebergs, and magma, though I don't know if that's true.”

“There are more than those three,” Ethan said. “Far away in Galar are Regidrago and Regieleki. They're legendary giants too. Most people around here don't know about them, so they group Regirock, Regice, and Registeel together as the three Regis.”

“I see. I certainly haven't heard of the other two.” Scott nodded, then realization struck him. He stared at Brandon. “Wait. Don't tell me you have those three Pokémon?”

Samuel nodded, then quickly shook his head. “Master has caught members of the three Regis, but not all three. He has two of them.”

“Two?” Ethan frowned. What did that mean? Brandon had only two? Had Ethan come too early?

“That's right. Master has caught Regirock and Registeel. We haven't caught Regice yet, but we have some leads. I believe we'll complete the trio before long!”

Ethan sighed inwardly. He really had arrived too soon.

Brandon, who had been quiet for a while, asked, “How did you know I was collecting the three Regis?”

Ethan invented a reason on the spot. “I happened to hear someone mention a powerful explorer traveling around in a mobile pyramid, looking for the three Regis. I thought it might be you.”

Whether Brandon believed him was Brandon's business.

Still, this was a disappointment. “I thought you'd have all three by now,” Ethan said, shaking his head. “I wanted to test myself against them. It seems I can't have the battle I came for yet.”

Brandon, Scott, and Samuel understood the implication. Ethan knew Legendary Pokémon were involved, yet spoke with such confidence. Either he had complete faith in the strength of his own Pokémon, or he had caught Legendary Pokémon of a comparable caliber.

After a moment's thought, Ethan looked at Brandon. “You have a lead on Regice, so perhaps it won't take long. I'm willing to wait.”

“What?” Scott and Samuel stared at him. Was he saying there would be no match today?

Then Brandon laughed. Interest lit his eyes, and his reserved manner fell away. “You've given me an intriguing challenge. I accept it. If you want to face all three Regis, I'll grant your wish. I'll contact you as soon as I've caught the last one!”

Scott and Samuel had waited days for this match and never imagined it would end like this. Had they waited for nothing? Yet the thought of seeing all three Regis battle Ethan soon made the extra delay seem worthwhile.

For Ethan, the journey had not been wasted. He had issued his challenge. However strong Brandon's other Pokémon were, they could not be as rare as the three Regis. Defeating all three together would mean much more to him. He was willing to wait for that chance.

They exchanged contact details. Ethan said nothing more and did not linger. He and May left the Battle Pyramid.

Scott and Samuel watched until the pair disappeared from sight.

“He really just walked away…” one of them said.

“And he wants to fight all three Regis. He's certainly confident,” said the other.

Even Brandon folded his arms and nodded. “He is an interesting man.”

…

From the Battle Pyramid, Ethan and May retraced their route toward Pewter City. After making the long trip here, neither had expected to leave without a battle. Still, waiting would make for a more exciting match.

After all, Ash had challenged Brandon three times in the animated story before earning his last Frontier Symbol. A little complication in Ethan's own journey was nothing to worry about.

“Where do we go next?” May asked.

The Grand Festival and the Battle Pyramid would both have to wait. Suddenly, they had no immediate destination.

“Since we can't battle Brandon soon, let's change plans. We'll go to Indigo Plateau first and decide what to do from there. Johto isn't far away, so before the Grand Festival begins or Brandon catches Regice, I can take care of a few things there.”

“Johto…” May nodded. She had no objection. As long as she had enough time to practice before the Grand Festival, it mattered little where she spent the intervening days.

Indigo Plateau was not far from Pewter City. Beyond the mountains northwest of town lay its broad expanse.

They returned to the city, ate a filling meal, replenished their travel supplies, and headed west into the rising and falling mountains.

They did not hurry. After several days of travel, they reached Indigo Plateau. They spent more than another day crossing it before finally arriving at the city where the Grand Festival would be held.

From high ground, Ethan looked down at the familiar city and felt a surge of memories. This was where he had won his first regional League title. After so much time, he had come back again.

This time, though, he was not here to claim another trophy. He had come as May's companion for the Grand Festival.

The Grand Festival and the Pokémon League Conference were held in the same city. They even shared many facilities and buildings. Only their main venues and dates differed.

“It's so big and beautiful!” May said, her excitement growing as she thought of the competition ahead. “Ethan, is this where you won the Indigo League?”

“It is. This was the first step toward my dream.” Ethan was moved too, though he found himself recalling all the little things that had happened during his earlier visit.

They stood there for a while, then gathered themselves and descended into the city.

The Grand Festival was still more than ten days away. There was no special atmosphere in the streets yet. Apart from the residents, Ethan and May did not see a single Coordinator.

But they had not come to stay. Ethan accompanied May to the registration office so she could sign up early. Then they left the city and continued west.

Johto lay ahead. Ethan had at least four things he needed to do there. Most important of all was a trip to the Charicific Valley, where he would watch his Charizard challenge for the summit.
