At the referee's reminder, Harrison recalled Swellow and took out his fifth Poké Ball.

But the Pokémon that appeared was still not his ace, Blaziken. It was another from Hoenn: Linoone.

It bore little resemblance to a bear. Barely half a meter tall and weighing a little over thirty kilograms, it looked much like a badger from Earth.

Linoone's streamlined body reduced drag as it ran. Most of its fur was light brown, marked by darker stripes, and its head tapered like an arrow. Its coat was resilient and soft. It was said that people in cold places liked to wear coats made from its fur.

True to its name, Linoone was fast enough to reach a hundred kilometers per hour. It was poor at turning, though. To avoid an obstacle, it had to make a sharp right-angle turn; without slowing abruptly, it could hardly turn at all.

Ethan studied the Pokémon and searched through the Poké Balls on his belt. He could not decide whom to send out.

His Pokémon had all taken several turns in recent battles. Every one of them was in good condition and comfortable in the arena. None particularly needed another round.

Who should he choose? Perhaps he still had too few Pokémon available.

This was where the privileges of a star competitor showed. Harrison was a semifinalist too. The referee had not spoken while he chose his Pokémon, but he had kept his eyes on Harrison as if urging him to hurry.

When Ethan stood thinking for much longer, the referee showed no impatience at all. Ethan's popularity, strength, and the League's favor brought him that small advantage.

He did not delay too long. After a little thought, he selected Crystal Steelix's Poké Ball.

"This round is yours. Go, Crystal Steelix!"

Rather than trying to match Linoone's speed with another fast Pokémon, Ethan chose one with extraordinary defense.

Thud.

Crystal Steelix's massive body shook the field as it landed.

Although this was the Ice Field, the long match had already melted much of its surface. Large stretches of frozen earth lay exposed.

"There it is again!" the announcer exclaimed. "No matter how often I see Ethan's Crystal Steelix, I can't help admiring its beauty and strength. Sunlight and the remaining ice make that clear body shine. What a sight!"

This was Crystal Steelix's third appearance since the tournament began. Each time it entered the field, spectators cheered.

Who would not love a Pokémon like this?

At the start of the round, Linoone rushed into motion. As Ethan expected, it ran straight and fast but struggled with turns.

Harrison was badly behind and could not afford to wait. He ordered an all-out attack.

Linoone mainly fought at close range. Against a giant with Crystal Steelix's defenses, that gave it no advantage.

There was little suspense. Linoone had traveled with Harrison for years after he caught it in Hoenn, yet it too fell to Crystal Steelix.

Given the difference in their size, the result was hardly surprising.

"Linoone charged! And Linoone is down! It barely did any damage!" the announcer cried. "That's Harrison's fifth Pokémon defeated. Has Ethan recovered from his last match with Ash? Will he start another unbeatable run?"

As the announcer went on, Harrison looked at his fallen Pokémon and shook his head helplessly.

He had done everything he could.

After Linoone's defeat, he no longer believed he would advance. Still, he wanted to defeat at least one of Ethan's Pokémon.

He had crossed the sea to get here. How could he return to Hoenn after losing six to zero? He had to win one round.

Resolve returned to his eyes. He took out his final Poké Ball. Everyone knew which Pokémon it held: his ace, Blaziken.

Harrison threw it. Blue light flashed, and the imposing Blaziken appeared on the field.

Blaziken was the striking final evolution of Hoenn's Fire-type starter. It was powerful and popular; even those who knew nothing about it could be won over by its appearance.

Ethan did not hesitate this time. He had already chosen an opponent for Blaziken.

He recalled Crystal Steelix and sent out his own ace.

"Go, Gardevoir!"

Gardevoir lifted its head proudly as it appeared. The two Pokémon faced each other across the battered remains of the Ice Field.

Harrison looked at Gardevoir and shook his head. "A familiar Pokémon. Two Pokémon from outside Johto, meeting and battling here—it almost feels like I'm back in Hoenn. This is my last chance, so I'm going to give it everything I have!"

"Come at me," Ethan replied with a smile. "I received this Gardevoir in Kalos. It was my first Pokémon, and it's very strong. I won't hold back either!"

They fell silent and focused on the battle.

If Gardevoir defeated Blaziken, Harrison would be eliminated and Ethan would advance to the final. If Blaziken won, Harrison would still have five more of Ethan's Pokémon to overcome. That was nearly impossible, and Ethan had no intention of giving him the chance.

Once both Trainers and Pokémon were ready, the referee waved his flag. The final round of their semifinal began.

Harrison went on the offensive again. The instant the flag dropped, Blaziken ran at Gardevoir.

Gardevoir sensed Ethan's instructions in its mind and prepared to defend, then counterattack.

"Blaziken, use Flamethrower!"

Before Blaziken reached the middle of the field, it opened its mouth and fired a stream of searing flames from a distance.

The attack came plainly from the front. Gardevoir raised a hand, forming an invisible psychic barrier before it. The flames struck the wall and spread around its sides.

Harrison had meant the flames as a distraction. While Gardevoir blocked them, Blaziken leaped out through the fire at one side and drove a raised knee toward it.

As a Fire- and Fighting-type Pokémon, Blaziken excelled at close combat. Against a Psychic-type like Gardevoir, closing the distance could deny it room to use its powers.

Ethan made a decision without speaking. Gardevoir immediately raised its other hand. A stronger force burst out, and Psychic knocked Blaziken back into the flames.

That was the benefit of their mental connection. Ethan could command Gardevoir with a thought, saving the time it would take to speak.

Blaziken stumbled into the fire but charged back out seconds later, running at Gardevoir with great speed.

Gardevoir tried Psychic again. Blaziken leaped high from a standstill and cleared the attack.

"Good opening! Use Fire Punch!" Harrison shouted.

Flames engulfed both of Blaziken's fists. It dropped from above and swung at Gardevoir with all its strength.

Ethan showed no alarm. One hand remained in his pocket; he merely narrowed his eyes. Gardevoir used Teleport and vanished from before Blaziken.

Bang!

Fire Punch struck the frozen earth, drying a patch of ground in an instant.

Blaziken straightened and turned. Gardevoir had appeared on Ethan's half of the field, putting a wide gap between them again.

Harrison frowned. Blaziken had spent so much energy only to return to the starting point. They had merely swapped sides.

He could not let that continue.

He knew Teleport was a common tactic for some Psychic-type Pokémon. If Gardevoir kept using it, Blaziken would tire first. Harrison had to limit its movement.

"Blaziken, once more! Use Flamethrower!"

The same attack had failed before. Ethan knew Harrison was not foolish enough to repeat it without a plan.

Blaziken was dangerous both at range and up close. If an attack landed, Gardevoir would take serious damage.

Ethan stayed alert. This time, rather than block the incoming flames, he had Gardevoir Teleport farther away.

That was the opening Harrison had wanted.

The moment Gardevoir vanished, he shouted, "Watch all around you! Be ready to use Quick Attack!"

So that was his plan. Ethan shook his head with an amused smile. Did Harrison think this was enough to defeat Gardevoir?

Gardevoir appeared nearby. Blaziken, already prepared, rushed at it. Quick Attack drove it forward even faster.

Harrison knew Gardevoir could not Teleport a second time immediately. But that brief gap would not be enough. Gardevoir was Ethan's ace because of its strength, not merely because it was his first Pokémon or knew Teleport.

As Blaziken closed in, Gardevoir raised both hands. Its expression was as calm as Ethan's. A strange pink-purple beam shot from its palms straight toward Blaziken.

Psybeam!

At such close range, Blaziken could not dodge. The attack struck it at once.

Its body jerked and its charge slowed. It did not become confused, but the interruption gave Gardevoir another opening.

Gardevoir brought its hands together and raised them. An invisible force lifted Blaziken helplessly into the air.

Oh no! Harrison saw that Gardevoir had caught it with Confusion. "Break free!"

Blaziken struggled, but Confusion worked differently here than in the games. Its ability to hold and move a target was formidable. Without a special way to escape, a small or medium-sized Pokémon could not break free by force alone.

Blaziken rose higher. Unlike the way he had won a round against Ash, Ethan did not plan to throw it out of bounds. He would defeat Harrison in a direct fight.

Gardevoir thrust its arms down. Blaziken plunged into the ground.

Bang!

Ice chips and dust scattered. When the cloud cleared, Blaziken slowly stood.

It had already taken some minor damage, but that one impact was not enough to finish it.

As its strength fell, its fighting spirit seemed to rise. Its feathers stood on end, and fire appeared to burn in its eyes. Blaze, its Ability, had activated, making its attacks much stronger.

Gardevoir met its stare calmly, showing no reaction to the change.

Harrison took a deep breath. This was the decisive moment. If Blaziken was going to beat Gardevoir, it had to do so now.

"Give it everything you've got, Blaziken! Use Flare Blitz!"
