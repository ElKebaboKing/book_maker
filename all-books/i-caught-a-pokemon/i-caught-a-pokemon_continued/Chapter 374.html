Although the dance of the birds had seemed to last a long time, only a few minutes had actually passed.

Once sufficient power accumulated in the clouds, the sky finally began to change.

What kind of sight appeared?

Countless particles of water, lightning, and fire broke apart and recombined, gathering into a single column of light that began pouring rapidly downward.

The column measured at least several dozen meters across. It shone so brilliantly that it appeared as bright as the sun against the black night sky.

Outside Greenfield's Pokémon Center, Professor Oak and the others stared at the distant scene in shock.

The female reporter rubbed her eyes and murmured, "What is that!? A waterfall flowing from the clouds!?"

"That isn't a waterfall!" Professor Oak provided a professional explanation. "Energy in the form of lightning, fire, and frost must have been broken apart, mixed together, and transformed into this visible phenomenon through the refraction of light. Gravity is drawing that energy down from high altitude, so it appears to flow like a waterfall!"

He truly deserved his reputation as the world's most famous Pokémon Professor. After watching for only a moment, he had already analyzed the composition and principle behind Tri-Bird Assault.

"Broken apart? Mixed together?" The reporter knew little about battling and hurriedly asked, "Is this a method of attack? I've never heard of anything like it. It must be extremely difficult, right?"

Professor Oak nodded.

"Naturally. This is far more than several moves working together. It is a qualitative transformation of energy itself! The fusion process is exceptionally dangerous. The slightest mistake could cause an explosion, so the users must control their strength with great care. It therefore places extraordinary demands upon their abilities and even involves a certain degree of science. I doubt any beings other than Legendary Pokémon could accomplish it!"

After hearing his explanation, the reporter clicked her tongue in amazement. "I never imagined the three Legendary birds would understand a method of attack like this!"

Professor Oak stroked his chin and thought for a moment.

"Ordinary wild Pokémon certainly wouldn't. But perhaps these three birds are unusual because the Unown created them."

After saying it, however, he began to doubt his own theory.

Was everything truly as he claimed?

If the three Legendary birds did not understand the principle, why would the Unown understand it?

No matter how he considered it, the scene before him was too strange. The three birds cooperated with such skill and precision that they seemed to have practiced together many times.

It almost felt as though a Trainer were directing them.

The thought startled him.

He chuckled to himself. Aside from an illusion created by the Unown, who could possibly possess all three Legendary birds at once? His imagination was running wild.

The Unown had to be responsible. Yes, that was the only answer.

The reporter took a deep breath to calm herself, then asked, "If an attack this powerful strikes the crystal tower, it will surely flatten the entire structure. Even the three Legendary beasts may not survive it, right?"

Professor Oak hesitated.

He had never seen this technique before and could not casually judge its destructive power. But one thing was certain: a combined attack prepared for so long by the three Legendary birds could not possibly be ordinary.

He looked toward the crystal tower with worry.

I hope Ash, Delia, and the others remain safe beneath that much energy...

...

The column of light poured down faster and faster. As it descended, every cloud, snowflake, and raindrop it touched evaporated completely.

The three Legendary beasts abandoned their futile resistance. They sprang away in different directions and tried to flee.

But it was already too late.

After only a few breaths, the column struck the place where the Legendary beasts had been fighting.

Hissssss!

When the light touched the crystallized ground, the violent explosion everyone expected did not occur. Instead, every hard crystal in its path disintegrated as though dissolved by powerful acid. The fragments became powder, then gas, and vanished without a trace.

It was not over. Before Professor Oak and the others, the column destroyed every crystal and finally reached the earth beneath it.

Only then did the explosion arrive.

Boom!

A mushroom cloud formed abruptly and expanded rapidly outward from the center of the battlefield.

In an instant, it engulfed the three Legendary beasts, which had managed to flee only a little over a hundred meters.

The aftershock continued onward. Powerful energy swept across the crystal tower next.

From so far away, Professor Oak and the others could not tell whether the three beasts were safe. But they clearly saw the crystal tower steadily disappear beneath the blast. Several breaths later, the column faded, leaving behind only a short section of ruined wall.

At the same time, Charizard, which had already flown far from the battlefield, suddenly encountered a tremendous shock wave. The force struck it like a physical blow. Unable to maintain its balance, Charizard crashed to the ground.

The impact threw Ethan and Erika from its back.

Gengar was also buffeted by the shock wave in midair. After stabilizing itself, it saw Ethan and Erika hurtling toward a mass of crystal rock and hurriedly used Ominous Wind to support them.

Even with the cushioning wind, the following shock wave swept them onward and sent them rolling across the ground.

Ethan's sturdy body appeared unharmed. He had protected Erika within his arms, so the fall had not hurt her badly either.

When Ethan pushed himself up, he discovered that he had pinned Erika beneath him. He hurriedly moved away and helped her to her feet.

"Sorry. I landed on you."

Fortunately, Erika was exceptionally composed. She knew the situation had been unavoidable and showed no displeasure. She merely shook her head to indicate that Ethan should not worry, then looked behind them.

The sky had become clear, like a mirror washed clean. Not a cloud remained for thousands of miles, and stars were already visible overhead.

Beneath that starry sky, the shock wave had devastated the crystal forest behind them. Every formation had shattered into countless tiny fragments that covered the ground.

Only the base of the once-towering crystal tower remained. Every petal-shaped structure above it had disappeared.

The tower had not even been the center of the attack, yet it had suffered such devastating damage and nearly vanished completely.

Was this the power of the three Legendary birds?

Rubbing her aching arm, Erika could not suppress her amazement.

It was terrifying...
