When Ethan emerged from the competitors' passage, enormous applause and cheers welcomed him. The sound was far more enthusiastic than during Misty and Trinity's battle, demonstrating that Ethan was unquestionably the most popular competitor in this year's Whirl Cup!

He had been delayed in the passage for a moment, so his opponent was already standing calmly in the command area at the far end, waiting for him.

Ethan walked unhurriedly to his position and studied the other man.

The middle-aged man wore a tasteful casual suit. Judging from its material and cut, it had probably been custom tailored and was clearly expensive. His hair was combed straight back with wax, while a neatly trimmed beard extending to his chin gave him a distinctly masculine appearance.

His style would not attract spectators who cared only about youthful good looks, but it still held considerable appeal for those with more particular tastes.

In Ethan's opinion, a man like this belonged in an office, watching the sunset while holding a glass of red wine and smoking a cigar, rather than standing here.

Pokémon battles truly had become something everyone loved in this world. People of every identity and profession wanted to take part.

Viewed another way, someone like him probably would never have entered if the Whirl Cup had imposed any entry requirements.

Separated by the broad pool, the two did not exchange greetings. At the referee's signal, they immediately released their Pokémon.

The middle-aged man's first Pokémon was a Clawitzer. Its species was common in the Kalos region, but differences in climate and waterways meant it had no natural habitat in Johto or Kanto.

Ethan had already watched recordings of the man's previous battles and knew something about his Pokémon, so the choice did not surprise him.

Clawitzer also supported the impression that this man was successful in business and traveled around the world frequently. Otherwise, he would hardly possess a Pokémon so closely associated with Kalos!

Most local Trainers had never seen Clawitzer and knew nothing about its traits or abilities. That ignorance had helped the middle-aged man win match after match and advance this far.

Clawitzer's own strength certainly contributed, but many of those Trainers had also paid the price for their lack of knowledge.

As always, Ethan sent out Blastoise.

The spectators could not help feeling disappointed when they saw it.

They supported Ethan, certainly, but he had used this one Pokémon from beginning to end, leaving them tired of the same sight. Blastoise might be extremely powerful and stronger than most Pokémon in the tournament, but they wanted to see a fresh face!

They truly could not understand why Ethan insisted on using only Blastoise when he clearly possessed other Pokémon!

When the middle-aged man saw Blastoise, the corner of his mouth rose slightly.

In his view, using only one Pokémon displayed Ethan's confidence, but it was also a way of flaunting his strength and presenting himself as exceptional!

As a successful businessman, the man ought to possess a broad perspective and avoid envying others. Clearly, he was not that sort of person.

He might appear suave and upright, but he was actually extraordinarily petty!

This fellow is nothing more than a brat who got lucky and won a championship. What's so impressive about him?

He even made me wait more than ten seconds for his grand entrance. Doesn't he just want to experience having every eye on him?

Judging Ethan by his own motives made the man dislike him more with every glance. Ethan's young, handsome face gave him an especially sharp sense of defeat.

Listening to the audience cheer for Ethan, the middle-aged man imagined the expression Ethan would wear once he defeated him.

Would the boy regret his arrogance?

Despite criticizing Ethan inwardly, he revealed none of it. In front of the cameras, he maintained a smile he considered irresistibly charming.

At another signal from the referee, the match officially began.

The people here might be unfamiliar with this Pokémon, but Ethan knew Clawitzer reasonably well through games, the animated series, and all the knowledge he had crammed since arriving in this world.

Clawitzer was a pure Water-type Pokémon whose shape resembled a crayfish from Earth. Most of its curled body was blue, while its right arm formed an enormous claw three or four times larger than the rest of it.

That claw was Clawitzer's primary weapon. Its feelers could sense prey, while the claw itself functioned like a cannon, firing many Water-type, pulse, and aura moves. A tube at the rear could even discharge water like a thruster, allowing Clawitzer to move rapidly underwater.

Its Ability, Mega Launcher, was also formidable. Apart from Heal Pulse, Mega Launcher greatly increased the power of pulse and aura attacks.

Even more interestingly, outside Clawitzer and its unevolved form, Clauncher, the only other Pokémon capable of possessing this Ability was Mega Blastoise!

That shared trait could have been one of the match's highlights.

Unfortunately, Clawitzer was not native to this region, and the local spectators knew very little about it. Among everyone present, only Ethan recognized this connection. Even the match commentator was unaware of it.

As the battle began, Clawitzer immediately took the initiative with Dragon Pulse.

The two Pokémon fought fiercely. Clawitzer attacked like a spear, launching assault after assault at Blastoise, while Blastoise stood like a heavy shield and blocked every strike!

Multicolored moves erupted from Clawitzer's enormous claw and streaked toward Blastoise. Blastoise answered with moves of equal power and did not yield in the slightest.

The dazzling exchange captivated the spectators. Huge explosions, torrents of water, and grand spectacles had always been what they loved most!

After a long struggle, the battle appeared deadlocked.

One side specialized in attacking and the other in defending. Now the only question was whether the spear would pierce the shield first or the shield would snap the spear.

As time passed, however, the more knowledgeable spectators recognized the truth. Clawitzer's assault might look astonishing, but it was all thunder and no rain, achieving almost nothing!

For Ethan, battling Clawitzer presented little difficulty as long as Blastoise remained wary of its enormous claw.

Just as Erika had said in the waiting room, apart from Ash, Misty, Trinity, and the still-unseen Zhe, the other competitors in this Whirl Cup were thoroughly ordinary.

While defending, Blastoise continually found openings and counterattacked successfully. Clawitzer, by contrast, never broke through its defenses!

Seeing Clawitzer gradually fall behind made the middle-aged man anxious.

Finally, he staked everything on one move. He ordered Clawitzer to approach Blastoise and use Crabhammer, hoping to defeat it at close range.

This was Clawitzer's strongest attack. Amplified by its enormous claw, the middle-aged man believed that Blastoise could never withstand a direct hit!

Yet Blastoise blocked the Crabhammer in which he placed such confidence with a single Withdraw!

The enormous claw shone with dangerous light and hammered heavily against the shell, only managing to send it spinning!

As soon as Crabhammer ended, the shell suddenly bounced toward Clawitzer. Blastoise's large head shot out, and a Skull Bash defeated its opponent instead!

The referee immediately announced the result.

Amid the spectators' cheers, the middle-aged man's expression involuntarily darkened.

He had spent considerable time studying Ethan's Blastoise before the match. Only now did he discover that its abilities were exceptionally balanced and that it possessed almost no weaknesses.

Like many other competitors, he preferred to send out his ace in the opening battle. A victory would allow him to press the advantage and defeat his opponent in one continuous push.

Now that he had lost, however, the situation immediately became troublesome.

Blastoise had defended for a long time, but remained in excellent condition and had suffered almost no damage. Judging by the strength it displayed, defeating it with his second Pokémon would be extremely difficult.

He was already considering retreat.

If even his second Pokémon failed to defeat this Blastoise, the humiliation would be tremendous and would undoubtedly damage the flawless image he always presented!

But with matters already at this point, he could not flee before the battle ended. Urged on by the referee, he gritted his teeth and drew his second Poké Ball!

Blue light flashed, and a Binacle appeared atop a stone pillar.

Seeing it made Ethan raise an eyebrow!

Another Pokémon common to the Kalos region!?

Did this man own businesses in Kalos or something?

As a Water/Rock-type Pokémon, Binacle looked like two claws protruding from a stone, with a face on each claw. It appeared both ugly and fantastical!

Those two claws were actually the Pokémon themselves. The reason they could be called "they" was that, like Dugtrio, they existed in a cooperative group!

To be honest, Ethan knew little about this Pokémon, primarily because it was far too ugly and did not suit his tastes at all!

Even after it evolved into Barbaracle, Ethan always politely declined any thought of approaching one because of its appearance!

Now that he faced one and knew so little about it, he had no choice but to take out his Pokédex and point it toward the Pokémon.

The Pokédex chimed. "Binacle, the Two-Handed Pokémon. A Rock and Water type. Two Binacle find a rock of suitable size along the coast and live together. They stretch their bodies and use the resulting force to carry the rock beneath them as they move. When waves wash over the shore, they rush forward to eat seaweed. If the two quarrel, one will move to another rock."

Ethan put away the Pokédex and pursed his lips.

Why did that description make them sound strangely adorable?

The realization suddenly horrified him. He hurriedly shook his head and flung the unbelievable thought away!

I refuse to let my tastes become strange!

Across the water, the middle-aged man finally breathed an inward sigh of relief when he saw Ethan use his Pokédex.

Judging from the first battle, Ethan was clearly very familiar with Clawitzer. He had continually avoided its attacks, which was why he won so easily.

The man had begun to believe Ethan possessed extraordinary breadth of knowledge. But now, at last, here was a Pokémon he did not understand!

Perfect. People always feared and hesitated before the unknown! As long as doubt entered Ethan's heart, the middle-aged man would have an excellent chance of winning!

His earlier dejection vanished, and some confidence returned!

Yet the moment the battle began, he realized things did not seem to be unfolding that way.

Why had this man changed strategies and begun attacking immediately?

Shouldn't he proceed cautiously against a Pokémon he had never seen before?!

On the battlefield, Blastoise abandoned the defensive strategy of the previous match and actively launched an offensive.

Ethan might not know much about this species, but Binacle was unevolved, its overall abilities lagged far behind Blastoise's, and it possessed the Rock type. What was there to fear?

If Blastoise had displayed elite defense in the previous battle, it now demonstrated fearless offense!

It first exchanged several Water-type moves with Binacle from a distance, then closed the gap and used close-range attacks to finish the Pokémon swiftly and cleanly.

Seeing his Pokémon defeated so easily made the middle-aged man's face alternate between pale and red!

He had believed this would be his chance to reverse the match and at least take one round. Instead, he lost even faster than before!

Binacle's own lack of strength was partly responsible, but ultimately, Blastoise's uninterrupted combination of attacks had brought it down.

The man stared deeply at Ethan.

They had not exchanged a single word from beginning to end, yet he had long since regarded Ethan as his greatest rival. Now that both of his Pokémon had failed to defeat Blastoise, he felt humiliated and viciously insulted!

Ethan's calm expression throughout the entire match irritated him even more!

A loss was still a loss, and there was nothing he could do. He was about to say something magnanimous for appearances when Ethan recalled Blastoise, turned his back, and simply walked away!

He! Just! Left! Like! That!

The middle-aged man's chest heaved violently. But Ethan was gone and his Pokémon had lost. What could he possibly do now?

As the victor, Ethan accepted the audience's cheers. As the loser, the middle-aged man could only depart in gloom, accompanied by the regrets of a small number of sophisticated women attracted to his bearing.

Under ordinary circumstances, a mature man like him would attract plenty of younger women. Here, however, strength determined everything!

Once defeated, elimination was inevitable.

As far as Ethan was concerned, the middle-aged man's strength had been decent, but nothing more.

They were strangers meeting for the first time. Ethan had no reason to offer him any words of consolation.

Apart from his memorable clothing and the fact that both of his Pokémon came from Kalos, nothing else about him left a deep impression.

Hmm... After today, Ethan would probably cast him from his mind and forget him entirely...
