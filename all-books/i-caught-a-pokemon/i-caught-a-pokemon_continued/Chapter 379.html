After dinner, Erika, Ash, Misty, and the others wished Ethan good night. Yawning, they returned to their rooms to rest.

Ethan left the dining room with Gengar and walked toward the Pokémon Center's front desk.

Nurse Joy had just announced over the intercom that after more than two hours of treatment, the Pokémon he had entrusted to her had mostly recovered.

It was already past one in the morning. The Pokémon Center was silent. Other than Ethan's footsteps, not a single sound could be heard.

By this hour, even the Trainers staying overnight had fallen asleep to restore their strength for the next day's journey. Few people remained active.

Ethan passed through a brightly lit corridor and reached the counter. Nurse Joy had been waiting for him. She picked up a small tray and held it out.

"Mr. Ethan, here are your Pokémon. They've recovered."

Four Great Balls and one ordinary Poké Ball rested quietly on the tray.

The four Great Balls naturally contained the three Legendary birds and Suicune, while Charizard occupied the regular Poké Ball.

Aside from Charizard, all four Legendary Pokémon had been in terrible condition. Ethan had handed them to Nurse Joy immediately after returning to the Pokémon Center.

"I'm sorry to trouble you so late, Nurse Joy."

"It's no trouble." Nurse Joy shook her head despite the dark circles beneath her eyes. "Plenty of Trainers check into the Pokémon Center at this hour. I'm used to it, and taking care of Pokémon is my responsibility!"

Nurse Joy might have been speaking politely, but Ethan still admired her dedication.

He picked up the Poké Balls and placed them in the bag at his waist.

With the newly captured Suicune included, Ethan once again carried twelve Pokémon, reaching his full team capacity.

Legendary Pokémon alone occupied four positions. Even after having his carrying limit adjusted, it still did not feel sufficient.

He would reorganize the team when he next had an opportunity to return to Cerise Park.

After thanking Nurse Joy again, Ethan turned and left.

Behind the counter, Nurse Joy watched him go with a face full of curiosity.

Several hours earlier, she had thought nothing of accepting the Poké Balls from Ethan. But when she entered the treatment room and discovered four Legendary Pokémon inside them, she had nearly jumped in shock.

These were Legendary Pokémon!

Greenfield was a popular travel destination visited by many Trainers every day. Even so, in all her time there, Nurse Joy had never met anyone who possessed even one Legendary Pokémon.

Much less so many at once!

Forget Johto. She doubted that any Trainer in any region could produce so many Legendary Pokémon at one time.

How had he accomplished it?

It was incredible!

Then Nurse Joy realized that the battle at the crystal tower could not have been what Professor Oak had suggested, with illusions created by the Unown fighting among themselves. The young Trainer before her must truly have used the three Legendary birds to battle the three Legendary beasts.

Did that mean Professor Oak's theory had been entirely wrong?

Despite her astonishment, professional ethics prevented Nurse Joy from revealing a Trainer's secrets.

After the news aired, countless people might travel great distances to search Greenfield for traces of the Unown and Ho-Oh. They would never imagine that the true central figure in the incident had been this man.

Nurse Joy smiled to herself. Perhaps that was for the best.

The event could remain a legend of Greenfield forever.

...

After waking the next morning, Ethan washed and dressed. When he left his room, he discovered that Ash and the others had already been waiting in the dining room for quite some time.

Empty plates were scattered across the table before them, showing that they had finished breakfast long ago. Their luggage lay piled on the floor nearby, and they appeared ready to leave at any moment.

"Morning." Ethan greeted them.

Ash looked up at the clock. "Morning? It's almost nine! You're way too slow, Ethan. You're the only one we're waiting for!"

"Sorry..." Ethan rubbed his eyes and leisurely sat at the table. "I went to sleep late last night, so I slept a little longer this morning."

Then he looked around and asked, "Huh? Where are Delia and Professor Oak?"

Ash shrugged. "Professor Oak left for the manor early this morning with Mr. Schuyler. He said they wanted to search for secrets the Unown might have left behind. As for my mom, she already left to return to Pallet Town!"

"Oh, right." Mentioning Delia reminded Ash of something. "Before she left, Mom told me to learn more from you!"

What could he possibly learn from me?

Ethan laughed at himself.

Delia had merely seen him with so many Legendary Pokémon and concluded that he was no ordinary person. She probably hoped some of his luck would rub off on Ash.

As a mother, she had put a great deal of thought into her son's future. Yet she had no idea just how fortunate her child already was when it came to encountering Legendary Pokémon.

Erika pushed a breakfast toward Ethan: milk and a sandwich. It had cooled somewhat, but she had clearly saved it for him.

After thanking her, Ethan finished the meal in only a few bites.

Once he was done, Ash and the others stopped waiting and rose to collect their luggage.

Everyone left the Pokémon Center together, knowing that they had to part again and continue their respective journeys.

Strangely, Misty showed little reluctance to separate this time.

Her feelings for Ethan had not weakened. Their routes through Johto would bring Ethan and Ash together again in a little over ten days. Misty would see Ethan again then.

With another meeting to anticipate, this farewell did not trouble her as much.

"Take care! See you next time!"

"Goodbye!!"

Outside the Pokémon Center, Ethan and Erika said farewell to Ash's group, then the two parties set out along different roads.

One group traveled west, the other north.

Now that they were already in Johto, Ethan and Erika no longer needed Charizard to carry them there. They chose to travel on foot toward the next city with a Gym.

After walking away from the Pokémon Center for several minutes, Ethan looked back and could no longer see Ash and the others.

Sunlight poured over the landscape. With the crystal gone, Ethan and Erika finally discovered that golden sunflowers covered every hill and field around them. The shining view was extraordinarily beautiful.

No wonder Greenfield had been chosen as the place most popular with women. It truly was lovely.
