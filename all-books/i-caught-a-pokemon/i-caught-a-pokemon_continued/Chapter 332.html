Pryce sent out his second Pokémon.

Blue light flashed. Ethan looked ahead and saw a Lapras.

Lapras were said to be highly intelligent Pokémon. Their gentle nature and dislike of battle, however, made them easy prey for humans. They had been captured in such great numbers that they had become increasingly rare. Now they were found only occasionally, along the shallows of remote islands.

Ethan studied it for a moment and rubbed his chin.

He had little experience with Lapras. His only real encounter with one had been in the Orange Islands, where he had seen Ash use it for transportation.

All he knew was that Lapras, like Dewgong, was a Water- and Ice-type Pokémon. Jolteon still held the type advantage.

Not bad. At least we can keep our advantage.

Once both sides were ready, Pryce raised one hand in his role as referee.

The battle began. This time, before Jolteon could act, Lapras attacked first.

It opened its mouth and immediately fired Ice Beam. The move was powerful, and Ethan had no intention of wasting Jolteon's strength by meeting it head-on. He ordered Jolteon to evade.

Jolteon leaped aside and avoided the energy-filled beam.

It had barely landed when a Confuse Ray flew toward it.

Jolteon ducked, and the strange light passed so close that it almost brushed its tail. When Jolteon raised its head again, Lapras had already gathered a Water Gun.

The stream shot from Lapras's mouth, crossed half the field, and arrived in an instant.

Jolteon had not even caught its breath. With no time to dodge, it took the attack directly.

Well, well!

Lapras had executed that little combination with impressive skill.

Those three opening moves told Ethan everything he needed to know.

Dewgong had defended while waiting for chances to counterattack. Lapras had abandoned defense entirely, seized the initiative, and chosen to trade attacks directly with Jolteon.

Pryce had used Pokémon with the same types in both rounds, but their fighting styles could hardly have been more different. After losing the first round, he had clearly changed his strategy.

But that was as far as Lapras would get.

"Jolteon, it's our turn to fight back!"

Jolteon shook the water from its fur and fixed Lapras with a sharp stare before charging.

Faced with the onrushing Jolteon, Lapras tried to repeat its earlier combination. This time Jolteon was prepared. It used Agility, evading the attacks while closing the distance.

"Now! Show them what we can do! Quick Attack!"

Jolteon accelerated again and slammed headfirst into Lapras.

The hit landed squarely against Lapras's head and almost knocked it over.

With Jolteon already in close, Pryce knew a thunderous Electric-type attack would come next.

"Use Sheer Cold!"

Ethan frowned.

Trying for a one-hit knockout?

Not a chance!

"Jolteon! Use Thunder Wave!"

Lapras drew upon the energy within its body. It was preparing Sheer Cold, a devastating Ice-type move capable of knocking out its target in a single hit.

Its body glowed as visible energy gathered rapidly inside its mouth. Sheer Cold was moments from completion when Jolteon released a faint electrical current and struck Lapras.

Thunder Wave!

"No!"

At the sight of that faint and seemingly harmless current, Pryce knew something had gone wrong.

Thunder Wave caused no damage, but it could paralyze its target and interrupt Sheer Cold before Lapras released it.

Lapras's body jerked. A tingling numbness spread through it, and the nearly completed Sheer Cold dissipated.

The attempt had failed at the final moment.

As soon as Pryce saw that, he knew the situation could no longer be salvaged.

Just as he expected, Jolteon's assault did not end there. It used the same combination that had defeated Dewgong: Thunder Fang, then Double Kick, and finally Discharge. Lapras fell as well.

Pryce sighed softly.

The young man truly was formidable. No wonder he had won the Indigo League Conference and earned Blaine's praise.

"I've lost the second round too."

After Lapras went down, Ethan secretly breathed a sigh of relief.

That was close!

If Lapras had completed Sheer Cold, it would have turned the entire round around.

Fortunately, Ethan had survived the danger and secured the win.

Pryce recalled Lapras and reached for his third Pokémon. Ethan called Jolteon back to his side as well.

Jolteon had not suffered any serious injuries in the two rounds, but it had used a great deal of stamina. If it continued, it might well be defeated. It was better to let it rest nearby.

Besides, Pokémon battles were contests of strength, but social graces mattered too.

Pryce looked calm, but he was already an old man. If Ethan swept all three rounds with a single Pokémon, Pryce would surely lose face. What if he took the humiliation too hard, flew into a rage, and made himself ill? Ethan would bear a great deal of responsibility.

And Pryce was an acquaintance of Blaine's. Ethan had to show him some consideration, no matter what.

Across the field, Pryce found Ethan's foolish grin utterly baffling.

It was fortunate that he could not hear Ethan's thoughts. Otherwise, Ethan truly might have made him ill from anger.

Pryce was old, but he had experienced more than most people could imagine. This was only a Gym battle. How could such a trivial matter disturb him?

Fortunately, he did not dwell on the strange look. Instead, he released his final Pokémon.

"Come out, Abomasnow!"

Blue light flashed. With a heavy thud, an enormous Pokémon appeared on the field.

Abomasnow?

The Pokémon stood on two legs. White fur covered its body, while its arms and feet were green, making it resemble an enormous snow monster. Ethan could not help studying it more closely.

This was the first time he had seen one in person, and the novelty fascinated him. Ignoring Pryce's gaze, he took out his Pokédex and pointed it at Abomasnow.

The Pokédex chimed. "Abomasnow, the Frost Tree Pokémon. A Grass- and Ice-type. Abomasnow is also known as the ice monster. It dislikes contact with outsiders and lives quietly in mountains covered by eternal snow, playing only with the drifts. Its great strength allows it to split even boulders with ease. When it shakes its massive body, it creates a blizzard that blankets its surroundings in white. It is said to appear from somewhere when the snow flowers bloom, then hide itself away again when they fade."
