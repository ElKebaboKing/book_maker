"Togetic is unable to battle! Crystal Onix wins!"

Even after the referee announced the result, Falkner's apprentices could not quite believe what they had seen. They stared at the unconscious Togetic, struggling to accept its defeat.

This was no ordinary Violet Gym defender like Hoothoot or Pidgeotto. Togetic was one of Falkner's best Pokémon, a symbol of his strength and pride as a Trainer and Gym Leader. Yet it had fallen all the same.

Falkner recalled Togetic with a sigh and slowly approached Ethan. He looked up at Crystal Onix towering over him, then shook his head.

"There's nothing more to say. My two strongest Pokémon could hardly fight back against yours. You've earned your champion's title, and I accept this loss." He took a badge from his clothes and offered it to Ethan. "The Zephyr Badge is yours."

"Thank you."

Ethan examined the badge. Its simple shape resembled a pair of slightly distorted wings. The material looked and felt like metal, gleaming brightly in the sun. It was heavy in his hand, perhaps the heaviest badge he had collected so far.

He put it in the new badge case Nurse Joy had given him when he registered for the Silver League, then nodded to Falkner.

"I'm sorry your Pokémon were hurt. Before I go, may I ask you something?"

"Of course. There's no need to be so formal."

"Why do you want to become a master of bird Pokémon?"

Falkner considered the question instead of answering at once. He pointed toward the blue sky. Ethan looked up. A few white clouds moved slowly overhead, but he saw nothing else.

Falkner smiled. "Ever since I was young, I've longed for the open sky. Over time, I came to love the bird Pokémon that fly through it."

"I see."

Ethan said it aloud, though he had doubts. Had Falkner really chosen this path without his father's influence? Even his name seemed to speak of Walker's hopes for a Flying-type Trainer. Ethan kept that thought to himself.

"Now that I've accomplished what I came for, we'd better be going," he said.

"Take care." Falkner shook his hand. "I wish you more success in the Silver League."

Ethan and Sabrina left Violet Gym as evening approached. They went to the city's Pokémon Center rather than looking for a hotel.

After the night they had spent apart, Ethan wanted badly to fall asleep holding Sabrina again. But first he had something more important to do, and he needed a place that was spacious yet reasonably private. The Pokémon Center seemed a good choice.

They ate dinner there, then went to the courtyard behind the building. Crystal Onix had fought well in its recent battles, but Ethan felt it still lacked strength. It had traveled with him for a long time. Perhaps the moment had come for it to evolve.

Orange light from the setting sun covered Ethan and Sabrina. The other Trainers staying at the Center had returned to their rooms. With no one in sight, Ethan released Crystal Onix.

Rumble...

Its arrival made the ground tremble a little. Walls enclosed the courtyard on three sides, and thick trees helped screen it from view, so no one else seemed to notice.

"You did well today," Ethan told Onix. "But tougher battles are waiting for us. I think it's time for you to evolve."

Crystal Onix lowered its head and looked at him. It said nothing, but Ethan understood its gaze.

"You want to get stronger too?"

Once he saw it agreed, Ethan took the piece of Onix metal he had obtained in the Sevii Islands from his pack. He had carried it for a long time. There had been many opportunities to use it earlier, but other considerations had made him wait.

Now he and Crystal Onix had built trust and affection, and Onix had grown stronger. It was time to decide. Holding the metal in his hand, though, Ethan realized he did not know how to use it.

Crystal Onix solved the problem itself. It leaned forward and touched the metal lightly with the horn on its forehead.

At once, the Onix metal seemed to come alive. It spread swiftly over Crystal Onix's body, and brilliant white light wrapped around the huge Pokémon. Ethan's eyes stung, and he stepped back instinctively.

He had seen this light often enough to know what it meant. Crystal Onix was evolving.

When the glow faded, a fiercer Pokémon stood before him, larger, longer, and stronger than Onix had been.

Crystal Onix had finally become Crystal Steelix!

Like before, its body was made of crystal. Its head had grown much larger. The gentle, almost foolish look it once had was gone, replaced by a fierce one. Red eyes made it seem dangerous. Its horn had disappeared, but its tail had become sharply pointed, making it look deadlier still.

Delighted, Ethan walked around it several times. Then he pointed his Pokédex at the evolved Pokémon.

"Beep! Unknown Pokémon. The Trainer has registered a similar Pokémon. Beginning automatic identification... Identifying..."

After a pause, the Pokédex continued. "Identification complete. Beep! Crystal Steelix, the Iron Snake Pokémon. A Steel and Ground type. Steelix lives even deeper underground than Onix. Pressure and heat beneath the earth have tempered its body until it is harder than any metal. Steel particles coat it and reflect a glittering light. It sometimes digs toward the center of the planet. Its eyes can see even in the darkness deep underground."

The longer Ethan looked at its fearsome new shape, the more he liked it. He was about to put away the Pokédex when it spoke again.

"Beep! No corresponding entry in the database. Upload data?"

That would share Crystal Steelix's information on the network. Just as he had done when he first caught Crystal Onix, Ethan selected No without hesitation.

"Beep! Upload canceled."

Crystal Steelix gently twisted its body, getting used to its new form. Gengar emerged from Ethan's shadow and looked it over from head to tail.

"Hey, you look good! You've got a lot more presence than before."

Pleased by the praise, Steelix narrowed its eyes happily. It lowered its enormous head to Ethan's hand and rubbed against him affectionately.

Only up close did Ethan realize just how large its head had become. It was taller than he was, at least two meters across.

Its evolution had changed its Ground and Rock typing to Ground and Steel. Ethan knew fights against Fighting- and Fire-type Pokémon would now be harder for it. Even so, its increased strength had made his team far more powerful.
