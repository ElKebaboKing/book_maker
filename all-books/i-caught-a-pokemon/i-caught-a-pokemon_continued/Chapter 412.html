Raikou roared, leaped down from the cliff, and landed in the clearing.

It turned toward the fallen Electric-type Pokémon and roared again. The deafening cry pierced straight to the heart and instantly startled every Pokémon awake. Once they recovered, they struggled hurriedly to their feet and fled the area.

It was obvious that Raikou had appeared specifically to rescue them!

What it did not know was that the Electric-type Pokémon had merely been bait used to draw it out.

Once the other Pokémon had escaped, Raikou charged toward Team Rocket. It bit through one of the shark machine's clawed arms and freed the captured Mareep.

Neither the Electric-type Pokémon's escape nor Raikou's hostile glare caused the two Team Rocket officers any alarm. Instead, they began analyzing Raikou's strength.

"Attila, how much power did Raikou release just now?"

Attila, the blond man, glanced at the machine's readings and answered calmly, "Its Electric-type energy is exceptionally powerful, and its bite force is high. But don't worry. It's strong, but its power remains entirely within the limits we can control!"

"I see. We can investigate the detailed data after we capture it."

Ethan and the others were close enough to hear every word of their conversation.

In fact, the men made no effort to be discreet. They spoke as though Raikou did not concern them at all, as if it were already theirs!

This naturally infuriated Raikou. When Attila extended all six mechanical claws from the shark machine and attacked, Raikou leaped high and used Thunder from midair!

Crack!

Raikou was already tremendously powerful, and the overcast weather strengthened Thunder even further.

A thick bolt of lightning raced toward the shark machine at incredible speed.

But just as with Raichu's attack, the crystal changed the lightning's direction halfway through its flight and forcibly drew it in!

"Don't waste your strength." The silver-haired man watched Raikou and spoke in a soft, cold voice. "This Crystal System can absorb every kind of Electric-type energy. No electrical attack can escape its grasp!"

Raikou would not readily believe his claims. When the crystal absorbed its attack, it simply changed targets and focused directly upon the crystal.

Crackle!

Another dazzling bolt of electricity shot out.

As expected, the crystal absorbed this attack as well. Perhaps it had stored too much energy, because violet light suddenly flashed across its surface and formed a spherical barrier around Raikou.

Attila laughed. "Good! It fell into the trap!"

The barrier was pale violet, and currents of electricity surged across it from time to time, making it look extremely dangerous.

Raikou paced inside the barrier, then fired electricity toward the crystal. When the bolt struck the barrier, it rebounded with even greater force and slammed back into Raikou.

Electricity exploded and flashed across Raikou's body. Electric-type attacks inflicted only half damage against another Electric type, but this attack was so powerful that pain still showed on Raikou's face.

Refusing to give up, Raikou attacked the crystal and tried to break the barrier from within. The reflected electricity injured it again.

Like the crystal itself, this barrier had clearly been designed specifically to counter Electric-type Pokémon!

But Raikou was one of Johto's three Legendary beasts. Even in pain of both body and mind, it endured in silence without letting out a single cry.

"How utterly foolish." Watching Raikou struggle in vain, the silver-haired man shook his head. "The more fiercely you resist, the more severe your injuries become. Can't you understand something so simple? Oh well, I can't blame you. I overestimated you. You may be called one of the three Legendary beasts, but in the end, you're still only a Pokémon."

Raikou gave the men a weak growl. Its voice was not loud, but its refusal to surrender was unmistakable.

You can imprison me, but you will never make me submit!

Seeing Raikou continue its futile struggle, both Team Rocket officers sneered.

Raikou could never escape the barrier on its own. Unless something unexpected happened, it would exhaust itself and be captured with ease. All the two men needed to do was wait.

Attila was even thinking that if they captured Raikou, their positions within Team Rocket would surpass those of the four Executives in a single leap!

But the unexpected always happened.

"Stop!"

Watching from the forest, Jimmy could no longer remain idle. He burst from hiding and ran straight toward Team Rocket.

"Release Raikou right now!"

The shout startled the two men. They had not expected anyone else in such a remote place. But when they turned and saw only a teenage boy, they relaxed again.

"Where did this brat come from?"

The moment he finished speaking, three more people emerged from the trees.

They were naturally Ethan, Erika, and Marina.

Now that Jimmy had rushed out, there was no point in remaining hidden, so they revealed themselves together.

The silver-haired man gave a cold snort. "So a few sneaking rats were hiding nearby!"

Ignoring his mockery, Jimmy repeated, "Hey! Hurry up and release Raikou!"

"Don't be naïve." As though he had heard an absurd joke, Attila laughed at him. "What nonsense are you spouting? We invested tremendous effort to lure out this treasure. How could we possibly release it?"

Jimmy understood that a few words would never make them give up Raikou. Steeling himself, he said in a low voice, "Then don't blame me for using my Pokémon to stop you! Go, Typhlosion!"

Typhlosion stepped forward and gave Team Rocket a threatening roar.

"What a nuisance." The two men were naturally not frightened by a single Pokémon. "If you want to play so badly, we'll entertain you for a little while!"

They took out Poké Balls together. Two flashes of blue light revealed a Steelix and a Skarmory before Ethan and the others.

Ethan raised an eyebrow at the two Pokémon.

He had expected Team Rocket to send out Pokémon in response to Jimmy's intervention, but he had never imagined they would choose these two.

Both of them were weak to Fire!

Steelix and Skarmory were both Steel types, and most Steel-type Pokémon were vulnerable to Fire-type attacks!

Jimmy had sent out Typhlosion first, openly revealing his choice. Ethan could hardly believe that the two men had still responded with a pair of Pokémon at a type disadvantage!
