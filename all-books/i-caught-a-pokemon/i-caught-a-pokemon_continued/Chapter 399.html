In terms of type, both Gengar and Dusknoir were Ghost-type Pokémon, allowing each of them to inflict heavy damage on the other.

Some people might find that surprising and assume battles between Pokémon of the same type should always result in reduced damage.

In reality, battles between Ghost types were special. Like Dragon, Ghost was one of only two types whose attacks were super effective against Pokémon of the same type!

On the surface, then, this appeared likely to be a fair but exceptionally fierce battle.

Unlike the pure Ghost-type Dusknoir, however, Gengar possessed Poison typing as well.

Poison-type moves inflicted only half damage against Ghost-type Pokémon and would serve little purpose. Gengar did not actually know any Poison-type moves anyway, so this detail had almost no effect on the battle.

As a Ghost-type Gym Leader, Morty naturally understood all of this perfectly, and he believed the man before him did too.

Come on. These are both our third Pokémon. Let's see which of them is stronger!

On the field, Dusknoir and Gengar stared at each other amid the battlefield left somewhat disordered by the previous two rounds.

Dusknoir's single red eye flickered with an eerie light, and an invisible presence locked onto Gengar.

Its opponent, meanwhile, merely stuck a finger into one ear as though it did not care about the battle at all.

Seeing this, Morty looked first at Gengar and then at Ethan behind it. His brow furrowed before relaxing again, and the corner of his mouth rose slightly.

Not only is the Pokémon being careless, but its Trainer isn't even correcting it...

Did winning two rounds make him underestimate me? Does he think I'm some minor opponent he can defeat with ease?

He shouldn't let his guard down like this...

If he dares look down on me, I'll make him pay for his arrogance!

When the referee announced the start of the match, Dusknoir attacked first at Morty's command!

As the Pokémon saved for last, Dusknoir was naturally Morty's most trusted partner. Ever since he began traveling, it had helped him overcome countless opponents on his journey to becoming a Gym Leader.

Morty believed that even against one of the Elite Four, Dusknoir would not be inferior!

Hmph. Surely this man can't be stronger than the Elite Four!?

The previous two matches were only appetizers. Losing them meant nothing. Come on! I'm serious now. It's time to show you my true strength!

Morty was brimming with confidence. Once the fighting began, however, events unfolded rather differently from what he expected.

Whenever Dusknoir attacked, Gengar used highly unconventional methods to dodge and counterattack at the same time.

For example, Dusknoir now used Shadow Sneak, stretching its shadow across the ground and traveling within it so it could quickly appear behind Gengar and launch a surprise attack.

But why had Gengar suddenly transformed into a light bulb!?

The bulb shone with extraordinary brightness, flooding the area with light. It not only dispelled the shadow, but also illuminated Dusknoir and forced it into view!

That was not all. When Dusknoir used Curse and attempted to attack Gengar, Morty watched in disbelief as Gengar produced a pair of headphones from nowhere, placed them over its head, and completely blocked out the curse's voice!

Aaah! What kind of fighting style is this!?

Most absurd of all, when Dusknoir used Will-O-Wisp to burn Gengar, Gengar actually transformed into a fire extinguisher!

The extinguisher sprayed a great mass of white foam, putting out the ghostly flames at once!

Is that supposed to be a Pokémon move!?

You've got to be kidding me!

The succession of bizarre events left Morty completely bewildered. He instinctively rubbed his eyes, unable to believe what he was seeing!

Is this really a Pokémon battle?

Isn't it some kind of magic show!?

While Morty remained stunned, Gengar decided that Dusknoir seemed to have exhausted all its tricks. Losing interest, it cackled and finally attacked. In only a few moves, it defeated Dusknoir with ease.

Seeing Dusknoir collapse on the field, Morty could not accept that his ace Pokémon had lost in such a manner!

"Th-this..."

He opened his mouth, wanting to say something, but his mind was blank and his voice seemed lodged in his throat. He could not produce even a single syllable!

Outside the field, the referee saw Dusknoir lying motionless. After glancing at Morty, he raised his right hand and announced the result.

"Dusknoir is unable to battle! Gengar is the winner! The challenger has won this Gym battle!"

Ignoring its fallen opponent, Gengar held its head high like a triumphant general returning from victory and floated back to Ethan.

When it drew near, Ethan spread his hands in exasperation.

"Hey, you said you wanted to fight and experience a Gym battle again after so long. I thought you meant you would trade moves with Dusknoir in a satisfying battle. I never expected you to fight like that..."

The match reminded Ethan of his first meeting with Gengar at Maiden's Peak. Back then, it had used the same sort of methods to defeat Ash's Pokémon.

"Surprised?" Gengar laughed. "There are many ways to defeat an opponent. Don't limit yourself to one approach. Just answer one question: did I win or not?"

Ethan fell silent.

Is whether you won really the issue here?

Can't you see you've made Morty question everything he knows about life!?

Ethan sighed helplessly and was about to say something when Morty approached with a complicated expression.

Ethan had seen that expression on far too many faces.

They all belonged to people who had lost to him and were filled with unwillingness, helplessness, and regret.

"I lost, and I lost completely. As for this match... Hmm... how should I put it? It truly opened my eyes and taught me a great deal."

Ethan had no idea how to comfort him. He could only laugh awkwardly. "Honestly, I didn't expect it to end this way either."

Morty took a gleaming badge from inside his clothes and offered it to Ethan.

"This is the Fog Badge. Please accept it."

Ethan took it and looked down. The badge was indigo and shaped like a ghost.

After thanking Morty, he placed it in his badge case.

He had been in Johto for quite some time, and this brought his total to four badges. His journey was now halfway complete. Once he obtained four more, he would be able to challenge the Silver Conference.

Ethan was about to say a few words of thanks to Morty when Erika hurried over from the side.

She first nodded politely to Morty, then pointed at the time on her watch and said to Ethan, "It's almost ten. We should go."

Ethan looked and saw that the time had somehow reached 9:57!

He had not expected the battle to take so long. It was now impossible for them to arrive at Naoko's tea party on time...
