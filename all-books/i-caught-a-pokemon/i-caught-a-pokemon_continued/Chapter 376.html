Beneath the night sky, Ho-Oh hovered in midair and studied Ethan for a long time, its gaze filled with scrutiny.

Sensing its hostility and remembering how it had destroyed his Great Ball, Ethan immediately became wary.

He had already realized why Ho-Oh had come.

It was here to save the three Legendary beasts!

Ethan understood that Ho-Oh had chosen the perfect moment to prevent him from capturing them. It must have been watching from behind the scenes for quite some time.

Like the three Legendary beasts, Ho-Oh had probably arrived early to investigate after sensing the abnormal events in Greenfield. But it was clearly far better at concealing itself. After the beasts intervened, Ho-Oh remained hidden among the clouds and watched events unfold.

Only when Ethan defeated the three beasts and attempted to capture them had Ho-Oh been forced to reveal itself.

The mantis stalked the cicada, unaware of the bird waiting behind it.

Hmph.

Ethan took a deep breath and returned Ho-Oh's stare without yielding.

What a joke. He had gone to enormous effort and used his greatest trump card to defeat these three Legendary Pokémon. How could he allow Ho-Oh to rescue them so easily?

If Ho-Oh wanted to play the waiting predator, it would not find the task simple.

Before meeting Ho-Oh, Ethan had held a very favorable opinion of it. It was jokingly called Ash's personal cameraman, and it was unquestionably more powerful than most Legendary Pokémon. Its phoenix-inspired design also made it far more impressive, while its rare appearances added an air of mystery.

But by daring to interfere now, Ho-Oh had placed itself directly against him.

There was no room for negotiation. Neither could retreat, so a confrontation was inevitable.

Even so, Ethan found the situation difficult.

If his team had been at full strength, he would not have feared Ho-Oh. He could simply have ordered the three Legendary birds to surround it.

If that happened and Ho-Oh failed to fight with everything it possessed, even a moment of carelessness might bring it down from its lofty position and turn it into Ethan's Pokémon.

But Ethan had just used Tri-Bird Assault, and the three Legendary birds could no longer fight. Among his remaining Pokémon, only Gengar and Charizard could confront Ho-Oh in the air.

Those two were powerful members of his regular team, but compared with Legendary Pokémon, they still fell short.

The situation was truly unfavorable.

Faced with a Pokémon this powerful, Ethan once again felt that the overall strength of his team remained insufficient.

Then he remembered Dragonair. It was not yet as strong as Gengar or Charizard and could not approach Ho-Oh's level, but it could at least fly for a while and briefly restrain the opponent.

On paper, his available lineup lacked strength. But with his other Pokémon supporting from the ground, they still had a chance to fight.

Whatever happened, Ethan had to try. Even if he could not defeat Ho-Oh, he would not stand by and watch it rescue the three Legendary beasts.

Defeating Ho-Oh was not the true objective. He merely needed to hold its attention while capturing the beasts as quickly as possible.

With that thought, Ethan stopped hesitating. He quickly took five Poké Balls from his bag and threw them all at once.

Blue light flashed repeatedly as Gardevoir, Jolteon, Crystal Steelix, Scyther, and Dragonair appeared on the battlefield.

Sensing the hostile atmosphere, Erika also realized what Ho-Oh intended.

She looked at Ethan and saw that he had no intention of abandoning the three beasts. Erika took out two Poké Balls and released Leafeon and Tangela to support him.

Including Gengar and Charizard, Ethan's side now fielded nine Pokémon.

Ho-Oh surveyed the many pairs of eyes watching it and showed no fear whatsoever.

It released a soft cry and beat its wings. Golden light suddenly fell upon the nearest Entei. In the next instant, the dying Pokémon seemed reborn from fire. Energy returned to its body, and it stood once more.

Damn it!

Ethan cursed inwardly.

No wonder Ho-Oh had remained so calm. This had been its plan.

Like Tri-Bird Assault, its power of revival lay completely beyond the abilities of ordinary Pokémon. It was Ho-Oh's innate power and nearly impossible to guard against.

The Johto legend in which Ho-Oh revived three dead Pokémon and transformed them into the three Legendary beasts had clearly not arisen without reason.

At the very least, this proved that Ho-Oh possessed the ability to revive humans or Pokémon.

And it now appeared that beyond restoring life, Ho-Oh could also restore a Pokémon's condition.

The ability was nearly invincible.

As expected, one could never relax when facing Pokémon even rarer, more precious, and more powerful than the three island birds or the three Legendary beasts.

With Entei restored, the balance of power shifted and began tilting toward Ho-Oh's side.

But after its earlier defeat, Entei knew that the human before it was dangerous. It did not intend to fight again and instead searched for an opportunity to escape.

Recognizing its intention, Ethan immediately issued an order.

"Stop them!!"

Of the nine Pokémon belonging to Ethan and Erika, Gengar, Charizard, and Dragonair flew into the air to entangle Ho-Oh and prevent it from using its restorative power again. The remaining Pokémon spread out across the ground and blocked Entei's escape routes.

At the same time, Ethan took out two more Great Balls and threw them toward Raikou and Suicune.

Entei had fully recovered, making its capture impossible for now. Ethan could only focus on the other two Legendary beasts.

The Great Balls traced arcs through the air and struck Raikou and Suicune.

Unfortunately, red light flashed, and less than a second after entering the balls, both beasts burst free.

The captures had failed.

Ethan silently lamented the lost opportunity.

At that moment, Ho-Oh used flames carrying an intense heat wave to force Gengar and Dragonair back. It found an opening, nimbly slipped around Charizard, and flew above Raikou.

Ho-Oh beat its wings. Golden light poured down once again, and as it enveloped the Raikou lying upon the ground, the Legendary beast miraculously recovered and stood.

Alarmed, Ethan pulled out another Great Ball. Before Ho-Oh could reach the final fallen beast, he threw it toward Suicune.

Bang!

Red light flashed.

This time, after the Great Ball drew Suicune inside, it shook violently several times. The movement gradually weakened, making it clear that the Pokémon within had lost the strength to struggle.

Finally, the Great Ball became completely still with a soft click.

He had it!

Relief flooded Ethan alongside his delight.

He had failed to capture Entei and Raikou, but at the final moment, he had successfully captured Suicune.
