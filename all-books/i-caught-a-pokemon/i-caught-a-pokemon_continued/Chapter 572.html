"Tyranitar, see that red pattern? Aim there and use Dark Pulse!"

At Ethan's command, Tyranitar opened its mouth. A wave of black energy formed there and shot toward the place where Kecleon had vanished.

The attack startled Harrison all over again.

Now Ethan even knew the flaw in Kecleon's camouflage. Harrison felt certain Ethan must have been to Hoenn. How else could he understand Kecleon so well?

"Kecleon, move!"

Harrison had barely spoken when the invisible Kecleon darted aside. His training deserved credit. Kecleon was not the slowest of Pokémon, but it was hardly known for speed.

Harrison's Kecleon, however, moved with astonishing quickness. In a flash, it escaped the path of Tyranitar's attack.

Boom!

Dark Pulse struck the thick ice and blasted out a deep crater. Shattered pieces sprayed in every direction.

Kecleon reappeared on the other side, unharmed.

He could fight this!

Harrison let out a long breath, and some of his tension eased.

Ethan had frightened him into a moment of panic. Thinking clearly now, Harrison realized that seeing through Kecleon's camouflage did not decide the battle. Kecleon could still use its speed to keep Tyranitar at a distance.

"Kecleon, look for an opening!"

Kecleon began circling Tyranitar. It moved so fast, and its body blended in so well, that it was almost impossible to follow with the naked eye.

Even its visible belly pattern became a blur at that speed, making up for the weakness in its camouflage.

It ran around Tyranitar more than a dozen times. Tyranitar turned to follow, but it could not keep up. By the time it spotted Kecleon, the smaller Pokémon was gone again.

Without a target, Tyranitar could not attack.

Kecleon suddenly stopped and lashed out with its long tongue. Harrison was certain Tyranitar had not noticed in time.

The supple tongue stretched several meters. Its tip struck like a fist, carrying surprising force, and hit Tyranitar in an instant.

Harrison frowned. With a dull smack, the tongue bounced off Tyranitar's armored body.

Was its armor that hard?

At least Kecleon slipped away again before Tyranitar could retaliate, giving its opponent no opening.

Speed was testing itself against defense.

In his Trainer's area, Ethan considered how to defeat such an opponent. He glanced across the smooth ice and suddenly had an idea.

"Tyranitar, use Earthquake!"

Tyranitar raised its right foot and stamped down. Cracks raced outward from beneath it in every direction.

The sharp, alarming sound of breaking ice filled the stadium. Deep fissures opened across the once-solid field, leaving the surface uneven. One move had permanently changed the terrain.

Kecleon lost its footing. Its small body rocked with the tremor. As it recovered, another Dark Pulse came straight toward it.

"Dodge!"

Kecleon jumped aside and narrowly escaped.

It tried to run again, but the broken surface slowed it badly. Cracks and raised slabs of ice stopped it from moving as freely as before.

This was bad.

Harrison saw the danger too. "Use Psybeam!"

He seemed to want Kecleon to attack from a distance and perhaps confuse Tyranitar. But preparing Psybeam forced Kecleon to stop, giving Tyranitar an opening.

Just as Kecleon fired, several pointed rocks broke through the ice and thrust toward it.

The moves met in the middle of the field. Psybeam struck the stones and never reached Tyranitar. Kecleon, meanwhile, was hit and launched high into the air.

The blow broke its camouflage. As Kecleon fell, a huge shadow covered it.

It looked up to find Tyranitar towering over it with a cold stare.

"Got you." Ethan smiled. "Finish it with Hyper Beam!"

Tyranitar opened its mouth. A fierce column of energy formed inside.

"Get out of there!" Harrison shouted.

Kecleon struggled to its feet and tried to run. Hyper Beam erupted before it could take a step, engulfing it completely.

Boom!

The glare faded. At the center of the field, Hyper Beam had melted a great hole in the ice. Kecleon lay motionless beside it.

The referee raised his flag. "Kecleon is unable to battle! Tyranitar wins the first round!"

"Yes!" Misty waved a flag and cheered from the stands. She was much more openly enthusiastic than she had been during Ethan's match against Ash.

Both competitors had been her friends then, and cheering for one would have felt unfair to the other. Harrison was another matter. She hardly knew him.

Harrison recalled Kecleon and took out his second Poké Ball with a grave expression.

He threw it, and the crowd saw a black Pokémon resembling a lean hound. Two large curved horns rose from its head, and its long tail had a devilish shape. It was Houndoom, a Dark- and Fire-type Pokémon familiar to Johto audiences.

They were disappointed and relieved at the same time. Houndoom brought less novelty, but seeing too many Pokémon from elsewhere made this feel less like the Silver Conference.

Ethan had faced Houndoom many times before. He knew the species well.

He recalled Tyranitar and sent Jolteon back out.

If Harrison chose a canine Pokémon, Ethan would answer with one of his own.

Jolteon did not look like an ordinary dog, but its appearance called to mind a sheepdog, a fennec fox, or a coyote. It belonged among the canines in Ethan's eyes.

As the round began, both Pokémon raced across the field.

Houndoom was slower than Jolteon, but it was still quick compared with most Pokémon. The shattered ice made the terrain difficult, yet neither agile fighter was troubled by it.

They skipped over protruding chunks and deep fissures without slowing.

For some reason, Jolteon fought on the move. It avoided meeting Houndoom head-on, attacking as it retreated and darting back and forth across the field.

Harrison did not understand the tactic, but he ordered Houndoom to chase it.

Flames swept through the arena as they fought. Heat and energy raised the temperature of the Ice Field. Some broken pieces began to melt.

Harrison noticed nothing at first. Then Houndoom stepped through a puddle, and the splash made him realize why Ethan had chosen Jolteon.

Oh no!

Before he could give another order, Ethan shouted, "Now! Use Discharge!"

Jolteon's stiff fur stood out. Brilliant electricity burst from its body and traveled across the wet ice toward Houndoom.

Ordinarily, Houndoom could have dodged an attack like that. Water now carried the current along a faster, less predictable path, leaving it no chance to avoid the shock.

Crackle!

Electricity raced through Houndoom. It endured until the shock subsided, then coughed out a puff of black smoke. Before it could recover, Jolteon was already in front of it, raising both forelegs.

Double Kick!

Thump! Thump!

Both kicks struck Houndoom's head. It fell and did not rise again.

Another victory for Ethan.

In the stands, Misty turned to Brock, puzzled. "Why does Ethan's match against Harrison seem easier than his match against Ash? He's making it look effortless..."

"I wouldn't say that," Brock replied. "Harrison's real ace, Blaziken, hasn't appeared yet. Maybe we haven't seen everything he can do."

"Hey, you two!" Ash lifted his head proudly between them. "Don't you know how strong Ethan is? Just think about the Indigo League. Not even the finalists managed to knock out one of his Pokémon! This is normal for him. Heh, only my Charizard and Pikachu really gave him trouble!"

"You..." Misty rolled her eyes, amused. "Are you getting a big head just because you beat two of Ethan's Pokémon? You're easy to satisfy."

The match had reached the third round of the first half. Harrison still did not send out Blaziken. Instead, he chose Miltank.

Many people wondered why he would hold back his ace while falling behind. Ethan understood as soon as he saw the choice: Harrison was saving his gamble for the second half. He wanted to face Ethan with everything he had after the break.

Knowing Harrison's plan did not make Ethan dismiss Miltank. He gave it proper respect and sent Crystal Steelix onto the field.

Crystal Steelix's appearance drew a surge of cheers. Beautiful and imposing, it had won over many spectators just as Ash's Charizard had—perhaps even more.

Harrison's Miltank was a nimble, sturdy close-range fighter. It battled differently from Whitney's Miltank at the Goldenrod Gym.

Both knew Rollout, but Harrison's did not rely on it. It closed with Crystal Steelix again and again, meeting the giant Pokémon head-on with Tackle, Stomp, Zen Headbutt, and Body Slam.

Miltank attacked fiercely, but it never broke through Crystal Steelix's defenses. Steelix caught it in a Bind, bit it, and finally drove it into a large block of ice with Double-Edge. Miltank lost consciousness.

Ethan had won all three rounds of the first half with three different Pokémon.

At halftime, Harrison sat in his chair looking grave. Nervous sweat ran down his forehead.

He had reached the semifinals. One more win would take him to the final. No one wanted to be eliminated now.

But...

He looked across at Ethan. Ethan sat with his eyes closed, resting calmly. Harrison found that composure almost as daunting as Ethan's team.

His opponent was too strong. It felt as though they were not even on the same level.

How had Ash managed to knock out two of Ethan's Pokémon in the previous match?

In the other rest area, Ethan waited for halftime to end.

The first half had not been difficult for him. Of Harrison's Pokémon, the one he truly needed to watch was Blaziken.

Then the shadow at his feet trembled. Gengar floated out of it and came to his side.

"Let me fight in the next half too!"

Ethan opened his eyes in surprise. "You?"

"Yes!" Gengar nodded. "I've watched the others battle for days. To be honest, I can't sit still anymore!"

Ethan shook his head with a wry smile. "I could let you, but wouldn't that be a little cruel to Harrison?"

Gengar laughed and thumped its chest. "Just pick the weakest one for me. I only want to stretch my muscles. Don't worry—I'll go easy on it!"

Ash and the others had been watching Ethan from the stands.

His halftime rest had seemed perfectly ordinary—until Gengar rose from his shadow and began whispering beside him. Then they sensed something was amiss.
