"Gyarados! The rest are yours!" Ethan called.

"No!"

Quackenpoker's face changed. Ethan had ignored his plea and ordered Gyarados to keep fighting. The old man reached out and took a few steps toward him, intending to stop him by force. Then two icy stares seemed to strike him at once.

Danger! A chill ran through him. He stopped and glanced at Ethan's side. The silent, long-haired woman stood there watching him coldly. Sabrina looked slender and had barely said a word all night, yet something about her presence was deeply threatening.

Haunter floated above Ethan's head, staring at Quackenpoker with a mocking grin. Its expression suggested it would attack the instant he took another step.

Under their combined gaze, Quackenpoker broke into a cold sweat. He dared neither move nor speak. He was no coward, but the warning in their eyes was unmistakable.

On the battlefield, Ethan's Gyarados heard his command and fought without restraint. The brief, fierce clash had already left several wild Gyarados defeated by the other Pokémon. Only ten remained in the lake.

With the others' Pokémon backed away from the fight, the wild Gyarados lost their other targets. They surrounded Ethan's partner, fixing their red eyes on it.

"Rrrroar!"

The sight of one of their own attacking them only fueled their fury. They roared as if they meant to tear this ally of the humans apart. Ethan's Gyarados showed no fear. It answered with a louder, more commanding roar.

One Gyarados outshouting a whole group of them was a sight Ash and the others would once have found impossible to imagine. Now they could only stare. The wild Gyarados paused at its challenge, then grew angrier and surged forward together.

Eleven Gyarados thrashed and bit at one another. The lake churned wildly. Waves rose more than ten meters, slammed into the shore, and scattered into spray.

There were many wild Gyarados, but their huge bodies got in one another's way. Only two or three could reach Ethan's partner at a time. That gave it room to maneuver and breathe, keeping the group from overwhelming it at once.

Teeth, headlong collisions, sweeping tails: the huge Pokémon fought with raw strength and instinct. The brutality of it made everyone watching uneasy. Even the ground beneath their feet seemed to tremble.

Humans and Pokémon alike watched in silence. Only the roars and cries from the lake broke the stillness along the shore.

"Ethan," Misty asked softly, "can Gyarados really take on so many by itself? Shouldn't you give it some commands?"

Ethan shook his head. He was uncertain too. "Honestly, with the same types on both sides and that many against one, I don't know what instructions would help. I'll leave this to Gyarados. If it loses, we can step in and finish the fight."

As he spoke, his Gyarados knocked down another of the wild ones. But the victory came at a cost. Bites had opened wounds across its body, and a patch of scales had been torn away. Blood ran into the water beneath it.

It seemed not to feel the pain. It opened its jaws and went after another opponent.

Such intense fighting drained its strength quickly. Ethan frowned as he watched, worry tightening in his chest. He had only meant for Gyarados to break the stalemate. He had not expected the battle to turn into a struggle among its own kind.

After letting it fight this far, recalling it now would leave the confrontation unresolved and might shake its spirit. He decided to let it fight with everything it had.

One wild Gyarados fell, then another. Ethan's partner fought on, bloodied but relentless, like a warrior refusing to yield.

At last, the final wild Gyarados collapsed before it. Everyone stared in disbelief.

"It... it won?"

"That's incredible!"

Blood covered Ethan's Gyarados, and many of its scales were damaged. It looked close to collapse itself, held upright only by sheer determination. Yet it rose among its unconscious counterparts, lifted its great head, and let out a long, ringing cry.

"Rrrroar!"

It sounded as though it were announcing to the world that it was the strongest Gyarados. At that moment the sun began to rise over Rind Island. Its first light fell on Gyarados, coating it in a pale, silvery sheen.

Someone swallowed. It was Quackenpoker. He had seen countless Gyarados, but the sight before him left him stunned.

"I've seen so many Gyarados, but never one so..." He nearly said brutal, then reconsidered with Ethan standing beside him. "So formidable. It's the first I've seen with such... such fierce strength."

Ethan's feelings were mixed. In his mind, Gyarados was still the Magikarp he had first seen in a tank aboard the S.S. Anne, or the fierce battler that had distinguished itself during the Indigo League. In so little time, it had grown beyond even those memories.

This fight had changed it further. Its confidence and strength had risen sharply.

When the lake finally fell quiet, Misty looked around and asked carefully, "Ethan... what do we do now?"
