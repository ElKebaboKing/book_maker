Ethan and the boy took their places on opposite sides of the battlefield. Misty and the middle-aged man stood behind their respective Trainers to watch.

Mauville Park was busy, but most visitors were skateboarding, walking dogs, or jogging. No one else was using the battlefield. As soon as people noticed a battle was about to begin, those with time to spare gathered around. More and more came until over twenty spectators stood outside the field: men and women, young and old, all curious about the two Trainers and which Pokémon they would choose.

Ethan had been watched often enough to ignore a crowd. The boy across from him plainly hadn't. He looked around nervously, growing more uncomfortable as the audience swelled.

Only when he saw Ethan calmly take a Poké Ball from his waist pouch did the boy pull himself together. He took a deep breath and focused on the match.

Then he bowed to Ethan again. “My name is Wally. It's a pleasure to meet you!”

Since his opponent had introduced himself, Ethan replied in kind. He didn't need to bow to a child, so he nodded and smiled. “I'm Ethan, a Trainer traveling around the world.”

Wally took out his Poké Ball. This was an informal battle in a public park. Apart from sending out their Pokémon at the same time, they had no elaborate rules and no need for a referee.

They threw their Poké Balls almost together. Two flashes of light revealed their choices. Ethan had selected Combusken, which had only recently evolved. He wanted it to gain experience in battle.

Wally's choice surprised him: a Ralts.

Only then did Ethan vaguely recall that a boy named Wally had appeared in the games. What a coincidence to meet him here.

Ethan turned his attention back to Ralts. Having raised one himself, he could tell at a glance that Wally had cared for it well. It looked healthy and spirited. The boy was a capable Trainer.

He also remembered that Wally's Ralts was male and would eventually become a Gallade, showing remarkable strength later in the games.

Ethan studied the matchup. Ralts was still in its first evolutionary stage and couldn't match a second-stage Combusken physically. But Ralts's Psychic typing gave it a super-effective advantage over Combusken's Fighting type. The battle would not be as easy as it looked.

Several spectators noticed the type matchup and murmured to their companions. They didn't seem optimistic about Combusken. Wally relaxed a little too. He had issued the challenge himself, and the result mattered to his hope of challenging the Gym. Seeing the type advantage naturally pleased him.

When both Pokémon were ready, Ethan and Wally exchanged a glance and called their commands in unison.

“Ralts, use Confusion!”

“Combusken, Sand Attack!”

One Trainer had chosen to attack, the other to hinder his opponent. As Ralts used Confusion, Combusken kicked a spray of sand toward it.

Both moves landed. Sand filled Ralts's eyes, while the psychic force knocked Combusken backward.

At first glance, Ralts seemed to have won the exchange. Combusken had only recently evolved, though, and Ethan had not had time to train it in all the moves it could learn. Confusion was an invisible attack with decent reach, difficult to evade on foot. Knowing that, Ethan had chosen Sand Attack to limit what Ralts could do next.

It worked. Combusken had been hit, but Ralts could not follow up while its eyes were full of sand. It rubbed at them, trying to clear its vision.

“Get close, Combusken!” Ethan called. Combusken ran into Ralts's half of the field. By the time it arrived, Ralts had rubbed the sand away. It looked up, and their eyes met.

“Ralts, Confusion, quickly!”

“Quick Attack!”

Wally tried the same move again. This time, with the distance greatly reduced, Ethan had more options. Combusken burst into a sudden sprint, zigzagging toward Ralts. Confusion missed.

Wally's heart jumped. “Quick, use Double Team!”

His haste was understandable. If a Fighting-type Pokémon reached Ralts, it could be dangerous. Ralts resisted Fighting-type moves, but it would be hard to break away once Combusken was close.

Ralts split into four identical figures, each moving in exactly the same way. Looking at them alone, it was impossible to tell which was real.

Ethan had anticipated it. As Ralts used Double Team, Combusken opened its mouth and sprayed Embers over all four figures at close range. The fire passed harmlessly through three copies and caught the real Ralts.

Ralts cried out in pain. Combusken was already upon it. A Double Kick struck home, followed by a slash from its claw that knocked Ralts to the ground.

The battle had not lasted long. Most of the spectators were surprised that Combusken had won despite its type disadvantage.

Wally's face fell at the sight of his defeated partner. Ethan smiled faintly. He knew what Wally had hoped to prove, but he had never intended to let the boy win.

Wally had only recently become a Trainer, yet he had raised Ralts well. Against another beginner, he might truly have won. Unfortunately, he had picked the wrong opponent.

Ethan, rather mischievously, had said nothing about the gulf between their abilities. He had simply treated the match as practice for Combusken.
