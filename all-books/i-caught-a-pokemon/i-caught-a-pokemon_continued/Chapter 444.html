Looking at the anticipation on Trinity's face, Ethan could hardly bear what was coming. He carefully chose his words and tried to sound as polite as possible. Perhaps then, after Gyarados entered the field, she would not resent him quite so much.

"Miss Trinity, defeating Misty and reaching the final proves that you're an exceptionally capable Trainer. I'm looking forward to our match and hope we can both display our true abilities and give it everything we have. That said, if some accident occurs during the battle or something gets a little out of control, I hope you'll be understanding."

Ethan had phrased the warning as tactfully as possible, but Trinity clearly failed to grasp its true meaning.

"Naturally." She smiled faintly, then grew serious. "Since you've put it that way, Mr. Ethan Vale, I'll be honest too. I don't like admitting it, but you're the strongest Trainer I've ever met! I don't believe I can defeat you, but you won't win easily either!"

Seeing her fighting spirit burn so brightly, Ethan hesitated, then decided to be more direct.

"Um... Can you promise me one thing?"

Trinity froze at his suddenly solemn manner.

"Hmm? What is it?"

Ethan drew a deep breath and said with complete seriousness, "Please promise me that no matter what happens during this battle, you won't hold me responsible afterward..."

Trinity: "?"

...

The final was scheduled for two in the afternoon.

Bang! Bang! Bang...

As the appointed time approached, fireworks once again erupted above the arena. In the daylight, no one could clearly see their flames or patterns, leaving only the sound of explosions rolling across the sky. Even so, the display brought the atmosphere inside the colosseum to its peak.

Everyone waited eagerly for the final to begin.

On the ceremonial platform, the beautiful Sea Priestess Maya stepped forward in a flowing white gauze dress. She offered her blessing to the two final competitors of the tournament.

"After several days of competition, the triennial Whirl Cup has finally reached its championship match, and this battle will determine our new Sea Hero! Both Trainers have already left a deep impression on us with their brilliant performances. Now, let the sea grant them its blessing! May they show us an even more magnificent battle!"

Her voice traveled clearly through the speakers to every listener. As her final words faded, she raised her scepter high. The Sea Spirit once again released a dazzling light that quickly enveloped the entire colosseum, further igniting the crowd's enthusiasm!

On the battlefield, Ethan and Trinity stood in the command areas on opposite sides.

Unlike the semifinal and every preceding round, the final allowed each Trainer to use three Pokémon. That would make the match even more intense, but for Ethan, it was hardly good news.

Apart from Blastoise and his red Gyarados, he would have to include Suicune simply to field three Pokémon. The moment Suicune appeared, it would cause an unprecedented sensation. Ethan doubted it would take until the following day for his name to spread across half of Johto through the internet. Half an hour would probably be enough!

I have no desire to become famous that way. I'd better finish this as early as possible...

At the referee's signal, both Trainers released their Pokémon simultaneously.

Blue light flashed. Ethan naturally sent out Blastoise, while Chinchou appeared on the stone pillar in front of Trinity!

Chinchou had defeated Misty and many other opponents in the earlier rounds. It was no exaggeration to say that Trinity could never have reached the final without its contributions!

But...

Ethan raised an eyebrow.

She's using it right from the start?

Chinchou was the Pokémon with the greatest advantage on this battlefield, yet Trinity had chosen to lead with it. Without knowing what Ethan's final two Pokémon would be, she had adopted an extremely risky strategy in the hope of defeating as many of his team as possible.

While Ethan admired her courage and determination, he could not help worrying a little for Blastoise.

He had no certainty of victory against an Electric-type Pokémon, especially with Blastoise in poor condition. The situation could hardly have been worse.

No matter how many disadvantages he faced, however, he would never lose courage before the battle even began!

Once both sides were ready, the referee swept down his flag, and the championship match officially began!

From the opening exchange, Ethan tried to keep Blastoise out of the water and prevent its body from becoming wet. It remained on a stone pillar, turned the cannons on its back, and repeatedly launched ranged attacks at Chinchou.

Chinchou spat out a stream of water in return, but when the two attacks collided in midair, its stream was scattered with ease!

If they competed using only Water-type moves, Chinchou was clearly no match for Blastoise, the so-called mobile water cannon!

Trinity naturally understood Ethan's plan. She smiled lightly, stopped wasting time in a Water-type exchange, and directly ordered Chinchou to gather electricity!

If it could not compete in overall strength, it would use its type advantage and defeat Blastoise in one decisive assault!

Watching the mass of electricity grow larger in the sky, Ethan's eyelid twitched.

The situation truly looked grim...

On this aquatic battlefield, Blastoise had nowhere to evade or draw out the exchange. It could dive into the water, but an Electric-type move of that magnitude would still inflict tremendous damage.

Waiting helplessly for the attack was clearly impossible. After a moment's thought, Ethan ordered Blastoise to dive into the pool.

Compared with taking the lightning directly, the water would conduct and disperse its current, preventing the entire attack from concentrating on Blastoise.

Blastoise leaped from the stone pillar and struck the water with a tremendous splash. The instant it disappeared beneath the surface, a thick bolt of lightning crashed down!

Crack!

Thunder roared as the lightning shattered the stone pillar into pieces. Fortunately, it only grazed Blastoise and did not strike it directly.

The sight made Misty's heart clench. She had lost to the same kind of attack and feared that Ethan might follow in her footsteps.

Ethan, you have to win...

Trinity was unsurprised that the first attack had missed. She ordered Chinchou to gather electricity again. This time, its power grew even greater, and the attack aimed directly at the water!

I have to stop it immediately!

Ethan's gaze sharpened. At his command, Blastoise sped through the water toward Chinchou's stone pillar. It burst from the pool and slammed toward Chinchou!

Trinity had clearly anticipated such an attack.

Without the slightest panic, she issued her command, and electricity suddenly erupted across Chinchou's body!

The current flashed continuously around it, making the small Pokémon look extraordinarily dangerous!

In the stands, Ash and the others held their breath.

They understood the situation perfectly. If Blastoise continued its charge, it would inevitably touch the electricity surrounding Chinchou. Even if its attack succeeded, Blastoise itself would unquestionably suffer far more severe electrical damage!
