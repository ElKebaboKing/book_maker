Noctowl and Jolteon were still battling on the field.

Jolteon ran behind a huge boulder, stopped sharply, and turned to face Noctowl. A dazzling Electroweb burst from its body and spread toward the bird.

But Ash's Noctowl was clever. It had read Jolteon's intention before Jolteon even turned. With a slight twist and a beat of its wings, it flew clear of the web.

The Electroweb brushed past Noctowl and dissolved in the air.

From his place in the Trainer's area, Ethan watched Noctowl's response and nodded in approval.

Ash's Shiny Noctowl was a little smaller than others of its kind, but it was remarkably intelligent. It could think for itself and even direct other Pokémon in battle.

Ash had worked hard to catch it. Unlike most Pokémon, Noctowl made its own judgments on the battlefield, assessed the situation, and seized openings that lasted only an instant.

Now, as soon as it escaped Electroweb, Noctowl spotted Jolteon's brief pause. Without waiting for Ash's command, it beat its wings and counterattacked with two Air Slashes.

"Jolteon, dodge!"

Jolteon sprang to one side. It avoided one blade of air, but the other struck it.

Ethan raised an eyebrow. He had expected Noctowl to be smart, but seeing it in action was still impressive.

Another Pokémon might lose this opening in the time it took to hear its Trainer's command and act on it. Noctowl could recognize the chance and move on its own.

It could fight, it could lead other Pokémon, and, above all, it was Shiny. Ethan could hardly contain his envy.

He pushed the thought aside and called out, "Jolteon, let's get serious too! Use Agility!"

A faint glow surrounded Jolteon. Its movements seemed lighter, and its speed surged. It raced up another rock and leaped toward Noctowl from the higher ground.

"Thunder Fang!"

At Ethan's command, Jolteon opened its jaws, baring fangs that crackled with electricity, and lunged for Noctowl.

Agility made it terrifyingly fast. Noctowl began to dodge the moment Jolteon jumped, but Jolteon still caught its left wing in its teeth.

Crackle!

Electricity flashed. Noctowl cried out in pain and, enduring the shock, shook Jolteon loose.

The bite had also paralyzed it. Its wing would barely move, and its body wobbled in the air. It could scarcely keep flying.

"Darn it..."

Ash gritted his teeth, took out his Poké Ball, and recalled Noctowl himself.

"Rest in there for a while. I'll send you out again once the paralysis wears off," he murmured to the ball. Then he took his third Poké Ball from his belt and threw it onto the field. "It's your turn now! Go, Charizard!"

Blue light flashed, and Charizard appeared in the air. The crowd had barely made out its shape when it let out a fierce roar.

Charizard had not always acted this way. After spending so long among the wild Charizard of Charicific Valley, it now showed a wilder side when facing a major battle.

"Here it is! At last!" the announcer cried. "Ash's Charizard showed astonishing strength in the round of sixteen, helping him defeat Gary and winning over a great many fans. Facing tournament favorite Ethan today, can it put on another incredible performance?"

Charizard...

Ash was bringing it out against him already?

Ethan had thought Ash would save Charizard as his final trump card. Instead, Ash had lost patience and sent it out in the third round.

Still, Ethan had seen Charizard fight the day before yesterday. When Ash had not sent it back to Charicific Valley afterward, Ethan knew it would appear in today's match. That much had gone exactly as he expected.

He recalled Jolteon and took out his own third Poké Ball.

"Come out, Dragonite!"

Charizard was not a Dragon type, but it looked just as people imagined a dragon should. Once Ethan knew Ash would bring it, he had decided to arrange a clash between two dragonlike Pokémon.

It would draw plenty of attention, and the Pokémon League would surely welcome the spectacle.

Sure enough, the sight of the two dragonlike Pokémon flying above the field brought the audience to its feet. Cheers rolled through the stadium.

High above the rocks, Charizard and Dragonite locked eyes. Both seemed to burn with the desire to fight. The referee waved his flag, and before either Trainer gave an order, they charged at each other.

Charizard had struggled against the Charizard in Charicific Valley, but that was only by their standards. On a tournament field it was exceptionally strong, and Ethan had to be wary of its Seismic Toss in particular.

The two Pokémon tangled in the air. Charizard struck first with Dragon Tail, whipping its tail toward Dragonite. Dragonite answered with a Dragon Tail of its own.

Crack!

The tails collided with the sound of a snapping whip. The air itself seemed to shudder from the impact. Neither attack got through. They separated, spread their wings, and slammed into each other again.

"The same move, the same power!" the announcer shouted. "Incredible! It's as though these two can read each other's minds. They've opened with the very same tactics. Is it coincidence, or do they understand each other?"

That exchange lasted only a moment. Once their Trainers intervened, the two Pokémon began to show their different styles.

"Charizard, use Flamethrower!"

"Dragonite, Dragon Rage!"

Red and blue energy burst from their mouths and met in midair. A violent explosion hurled both Pokémon back toward their respective sides of the field, where they faced each other in a brief standoff.

This was easily the strongest opponent Ethan had faced since entering the Silver Conference.

Charizard's strength and experience were both first rate. With a Trainer as unpredictable as Ash directing it, even Ethan found the battle difficult.

Under their Trainers' commands, the fight swept from sky to ground, smashing so many boulders that the rock field nearly became a plain. Then the two rose into the air and grappled again.

The audience watched, stunned and delighted. At a glance, the battle looked evenly matched. Anyone who knew what to look for, though, could see a difference in the Pokémon's condition.

Charizard was covered in wounds, yet it remained spirited, growing fiercer as the fight went on. Dragonite was just as battered, but it panted heavily. Its strength was running out.

With Ash, Brock, Delia, Professor Oak, and everyone else who cared for Ash watching, Charizard came through in the end. One final Flamethrower knocked Dragonite out!

"It won!"

The announcer's roar set off the loudest cheers yet. From the sound of the crowd, anyone might have thought Ash had won the final and lifted the trophy.

"Unbelievable!" the announcer went on breathlessly, without waiting for the cheers to die down. "After that grueling battle, Charizard has won! And that means the legend of Ethan's unbeaten run, stretching from the Indigo League to this Silver Conference, has finally come to an end!"
