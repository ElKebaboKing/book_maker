"The field's on fire?" The announcer still thought the blaze was an accident. He had not understood Ethan's purpose.

Ethan pointed toward the center. "Charizard, get onto that pile of rocks!"

Charizard beat its wings and landed atop the little hill made from a dozen or so boulders. It was the highest point on the field and the only place not covered in grass.

Crackle, crackle!

Fire spread quickly. Soon it threatened the entire field. Spectators in the nearest seats leaned away from the heat.

"It's so hot my face feels roasted!"

"The air's wavering. I can hardly see the battle!"

People fanned themselves, trying to push away the hot air.

Dragonite suffered too. It tried to climb into the sky, but smoke obscured its way. The air above the field was hotter still, stinging its eyes and making it choke. It had to descend again.

Drake frowned. The fire was too large for Dragonite's Water Gun to put out now. He had first assumed it was accidental. Ethan's composure told him otherwise.

So that's it. He's set the field ablaze to force a final confrontation. Dragonite might resist Fire-type attacks, but it could not remain in a spreading blaze indefinitely.

"Rrrroar!" Charizard called down from the rocky height. Dragonite had no attention to spare for it. Uneasy, it edged toward the few patches not yet burning.

The two Pokémon were accustomed to different environments. Charizard, like others of its line, was at home with volcanic heat and would feel this fire less. Dragonite lived around lakes and islands, its skin accustomed to damp sea breezes. The heat was harder for it to bear.

"Dragonite has nowhere safe to stay!" the announcer said, surveying the field. "Even if Charizard doesn't attack, it could soon be burned by the fire and heat!"

Drake knew it too. He had to finish the match before Dragonite was trapped. There was no point drawing it out any longer.

"Dragonite, stop running! While you still have room, settle this with one attack! Use Hyper Beam!"

Dragonite opened its mouth and gathered energy. Ethan saw that his plan had worked.

"Charizard, use Inferno!"

The fire cast both Pokémon in red light. Even their eyes seemed to hold flames. A few seconds later, they fired together. One beam was red, the other blue, and both surged across the field.

The announcer sprang to his feet. "This is it! Dragonite has used the immensely powerful Normal-type move Hyper Beam, and Charizard is answering with the advanced Fire-type move Inferno!"

He was so excited that he sprayed the microphone as he spoke and knocked over his water cup with an elbow without noticing.

The attacks raced past the midpoint. They did not collide. Instead, they passed side by side and struck the opposing Pokémon almost simultaneously.

Boom! Boom!

Dragonite fell backward through firelight and smoke. Charizard toppled from the rocks. Both hit the ground and lay still.

"Both attacks connected!" the announcer cried, his voice cracking. "With power like that, who can possibly stand up and claim victory?"

Several seconds passed without movement. Keeping clear of the flames, the referee stepped forward, ready to call a draw. Then Charizard stirred. It struggled, trembling, to its feet.

"Charizard!" the announcer shouted. "Its fighting spirit burns as fiercely as its fire. It's the first to rise!"

Ethan let out a quiet breath. Hyper Beam was devastating, but Charizard had entered the exchange with enough stamina. It had survived with a little strength left, just as he had hoped.

The crowd turned to Dragonite. On the giant screen, they could see that its eyes were open. It had not fainted, but the aftereffects of Hyper Beam held it on the ground, unable to move.

Drake's face changed. He shouted encouragement, urging Dragonite to get up, but nothing worked. He could only watch Charizard stagger toward it.

Charizard stopped over the Pokémon it had battled so fiercely. A glimmer of respect entered its eyes.

"It's time," Ethan called. "Charizard, finish it with Dragon Claw!"

Charizard raised both claws and struck the defenseless Dragonite without hesitation. The super-effective blow knocked it unconscious.

"Dragonite is down!" the announcer's voice carried a sob across Pummelo Stadium. He had feared this result, but seeing Drake lose made it no easier to accept. Orange League supporters around him felt the same.

Their champion had lost to the Indigo League champion. To them, it felt as though Ethan had swept aside the pride of the Orange Islands.

In Kanto, the response was the opposite. Cheers erupted at Cerise Laboratory and at the biker gang's clubhouse.

"He won!"
