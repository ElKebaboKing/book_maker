Thud. Thud. Thud.

Moltres, Zapdos, and Raikou dropped Maxie, Archie, and Matt beside Ethan. The two seized orbs rolled to his feet. He picked them up before looking down at the captives.

Matt aside, the two leaders were in a sorry state. Archie had passed out from his injuries. Maxie was still conscious, but Moltres's rough flight had left him staring blankly, as though he could scarcely understand what had happened. His usually neat swept-back hair was a mess, his uniform torn, and his glasses gone.

“You...” Ethan began.

Gengar appeared with two long vines it had found somewhere and, in a blink, tied all three men securely. It certainly acted fast. Ethan appreciated that Gengar could see what needed doing without an order, though it had cut off whatever he might have said.

Never mind. The leaders had lost, and now they were captured. There was little to discuss with Maxie.

Ethan looked toward the battlefield. Team Aqua and Team Magma members saw their leaders taken prisoner and panicked. Their panic became a desperate resolve. Both sides stopped attacking each other and surged toward Ethan, determined to rescue their leaders.

“Hold them back!”

There was no time to relay individual orders by telepathy. At Ethan's gesture, his five Legendary Pokémon moved in front of him. Shelly and Misty directed their Water-type Pokémon to the flanks so no one could circle around.

Pokémon came in masses across the ground and through the air, a dark wave that looked frightening from a distance. Ordinary grunts and elite members alike might take some effort to defeat, but they could no longer threaten Ethan. His strongest lineup was with him now.

Zapdos and Raikou, the fastest of the five, struck first.

“Zapdos, Raikou—Thunderbolt!”

One attacked from the sky, the other from the ground. Electricity burst outward in all directions, flashing across the field. Most of Team Aqua's Water-type Pokémon fell almost instantly. Even from far away, the crackling current conveyed the power behind those attacks. Zapdos and Raikou might not have bodies as massive as Groudon's or Kyogre's, but their moves were formidable.

Suicune, along with Shelly's and Misty's Water-types, had to move around the edge of the electrical assault to avoid being caught in it. A few enemy Pokémon came through elsewhere, but their numbers were too small to change the fight.

Once the current subsided, the other Legendary Pokémon reached the front and engaged Team Magma's forces. Suicune cut into their Fire-type formation like a blade. Powerful Water-type attacks poured from its mouth, easily dropping any Fire-type that tried to block it. Mightyena and Crobat escaped that first charge, only to face Articuno and Moltres.

Seeing both enemy formations break, Shelly and Misty sent their own Pokémon forward. For practical purposes, Team Aqua and Team Magma had already lost. No ordinary force could withstand five Legendary Pokémon at once.

Then Ethan spotted a conspicuous figure slipping out of the chaos toward the shore. The broad body and bright Team Magma uniform gave him away: Tabitha.

He was running? With Maxie captured, Tabitha had decided not to be taken alongside him. He moved with surprising agility for his size, scrambling back onto Team Magma's ship. Several escape boats hung along its side. He headed straight for them.

Ethan had not spent all this time preparing only to let a commander escape. He could not catch every grunt, and he did not intend to try. The two leaders and their commanders were responsible for this. He would not let one slip away before his eyes.

He was about to send Dragonite after Tabitha when a tarpaulin on one of the boats flipped aside. A small, shapely woman in a hooded Team Magma uniform stood up. Her face was too far away to see clearly, but Ethan knew who it was: Courtney, who had remained out of sight until now.

Why was she hiding there?

Before he could think further, she kicked Tabitha in the stomach and sent him sprawling. She released a Pokémon and had him restrained before he could rise. The movements were smooth, fast, and so unexpected that Tabitha barely reacted.

“She does have some sense,” Shelly said. She sounded grudgingly impressed, then gave a dismissive snort. “An ambush at Team Magma's escape route. Clever, perhaps, but it's only a small trick.”

Even though Courtney was on their side now, Shelly could not quite bring herself to praise her old rival. Water and fire did not readily mix.

With the human fight settled, Ethan turned his attention back to the lake. That was where the true danger lay. Magma had nearly filled its once-clear water. The ground around the ancient Pokémon had broken apart. And this was still only the beginning of their battle.

As Primal Groudon and Primal Kyogre fought, the upheaval spread. Storm clouds stretched for hundreds of miles and kept advancing. Lightning lit the sky and thunder shook it. Dozens of waterspouts rose from the sea into the clouds, with great whirlpools churning beneath them. Fiery meteors punched holes through the cloud cover and crashed into the island and ocean, throwing up earth and spray.

It looked like the end of the world.

Within half a minute, another violent shock split the large island into many pieces. Wind howled. Waves more than ten meters high swallowed whatever they could pull from the shore. No one could keep fighting in such conditions except the Pokémon that had caused them.

Ethan's group and the Pokémon of both teams stopped. The remaining grunts cared only about escaping. If they stayed, they would die here with their leaders.

Ethan, Shelly, and Misty had to carry Maxie, Archie, and Matt away from the shattered island. They returned to the smaller one where Ethan had met Shelly. Rain lashed their faces.

The water revived both leaders. They saw what their Pokémon had done—and then saw grunts struggling in the sea. Maxie showed little reaction. Archie turned pale.

“Save them!” he pleaded.

In this sea, even strong swimmers would likely die once they fell overboard. Ethan could not stand by and watch. He frowned. He might not be able to save many himself, but he had to try.

Then he spotted Courtney on Team Magma's ship. She had gathered several people and was already helping those in the water. That was a great relief. The ship and its crew could handle the immediate rescues, leaving Ethan free to focus on the two ancient Pokémon.

He held up the two orbs, both still glowing faintly, and turned to Shelly. “How do I make these stop them?”

“Why ask me? I have no idea.” She spread her hands. “Try thinking at them?”

Ethan actually tried. The Red Orb had long since lost any hold over Primal Groudon and did nothing. He worked at the Blue Orb for a while too. Perhaps Primal Kyogre was too far away, or perhaps the orb had stopped working, but it gave no response. He plainly did not know how to use it.

He could hardly turn around and ask Maxie. If the orbs would not solve this, he would have to use force, just as he had planned.

First he needed to contain the weather. If it kept spreading, the nearby islands and eventually the Hoenn mainland would suffer.

Ethan whistled. Zapdos, Articuno, and Moltres rose into the sky. Through Gardevoir's telepathy, he could still direct them from far below. He sent them to three points around the battlefield, forming a triangle that enclosed its center.

He intended to use something like a technique for fighting a wildfire. When flames spread outward, a controlled fire can burn toward them and create a barrier. He would have the birds' power drive against the expanding storm, form an opposing flow, and stop it from reaching farther.

Ordinary Pokémon could not do it. These three were not ordinary.

At Ethan's command, they beat their wings and released a torrent of energy. Fire. Ice. Lightning. Their powers surged from three directions into the vast weather front. The wind, clouds, waves, and falling fire seemed to strike an invisible wall. They could advance no farther.

The storm was a byproduct of the two ancient Pokémon's battle. Holding it back was within the birds' strength, so long as they kept up the effort.

Containment was not a lasting answer. The battle continued to produce more violent weather inside the triangle. If enough accumulated, its eventual release could be devastating. Ethan needed to stop the source quickly.

He drew a breath and looked around at the people with him. At last, the moment had come.

Watch closely. Time to show off.

Under the eyes of Shelly, Maxie, Archie, and Matt, Ethan put the orbs away and took out another Ultra Ball he had prepared.

Every gaze fixed on it. As far as they knew, Deoxys was the strongest Pokémon he owned, and it was already fighting the two primal Pokémon.

What could be inside this ball that he would bring out now? Did he have another Pokémon strong enough?
