Beautifly met Roselia's Magical Leaf with Gust. It beat its wings, and a powerful current of air scattered the oncoming leaves.

But Drew had clearly trained Roselia for just such a response. Rather than panic at the strength of the Gust, he gave a faint, confident smile.

Roselia's eyes snapped open. The Magical Leaves still flying toward Beautifly split into two streams, curved around the wind head-on, and attacked from either side!

May stared in shock. The spectators' eyes widened too. Even Ash, Brock, and Misty could scarcely believe it.

That wasn't something Roselia could learn simply by repeating the move over and over. How had it done it?

Ethan touched his chin. He was the only one among them who understood.

Magical Leaf dealt Grass-type damage, but it didn't behave like an ordinary Grass move. Its enchanted leaves could home in on a target instead of flying straight ahead. When they encountered an attack like a strong wind, they could drift with the air and then use that tracking ability to reach the opponent from another direction.

May, unfortunately, was still a beginner. She couldn't even recognize every move yet, let alone understand the principle behind this one. Its sudden change of course left her at a loss for a command.

“Dodge it!”

Watching May stand frozen on the battlefield, Ash and the others clenched their fists in the waiting room. They were so anxious they wanted to take over the battle themselves. While May hesitated, the leaves struck Beautifly.

Fortunately, Magical Leaf did little physical damage to Beautifly. It wasn't a disaster in an ordinary battle.

But a Contest battle wasn't only about knocking out the opponent.

Ethan looked at the big screen. The red bar showing May's points had fallen by about a fifth. Drew had taken the early lead.

He checked the clock too. A minute and a half had passed, leaving May only three and a half minutes. That was no help to her.

Drew's smile widened. He kept pressing the attack, ordering Roselia to use Stun Spore.

The powder spread across the battlefield and closed in on Beautifly, limiting where it could move.

May could have ordered another Gust to blow it away. In her panic, though, she chose a different response.

Beautifly fired String Shot to restrain Roselia. Roselia used Magical Leaf again, slicing through the silk. The leaves kept going and struck Beautifly.

May had now been caught by the same move twice. Her point bar dropped further, almost to half its original length.

Misty and the others felt their hearts sink. At least May hadn't given up. She ordered Beautifly to use its strongest move, Silver Wind.

But Drew's Roselia was plainly the stronger Pokémon. Its Petal Dance canceled the rush of Silver Wind, then sent petals sweeping straight toward Beautifly.

This time May reacted in time. Beautifly barely managed to avoid the attack.

Before she could breathe easier, Roselia took the opening stance for Solar Beam. May's face changed. She knew Beautifly couldn't withstand that attack in its present condition, so she hurriedly ordered String Shot to stop it.

She was too late.

The silk had only just left Beautifly's long mouth when Roselia unleashed Solar Beam. A dazzling pillar of light surged across the field and swallowed Beautifly. For an instant, it lit the entire arena.

Beautifly had no chance to react. Its body vanished completely inside the beam.

“She's lost...” Brock shook his head and sank onto the sofa in the waiting room.

He wasn't alone. Anyone watching could see that Beautifly's battle was over.

Sure enough, when the beam faded, Beautifly fell headfirst to the floor, battered and unable to rise.

“Beautifly is unable to battle,” Vivian announced regretfully. “This match is over! Congratulations to Drew, who advances to the next round!”

The arena erupted in applause for the winner.

Drew glanced at May. This time he said nothing. He and Roselia turned and walked away, leaving the crowd with a pair of cool, retreating silhouettes.

May recalled Beautifly, her expression troubled. She gave the battlefield one lingering look, then returned to the waiting room by the opposite entrance.

The mood there was subdued. When she came in, Misty and the others wanted to comfort her, but none of them knew quite what to say.

“May...”

“Sis...”

Seeing their worried faces, May forced a small smile. “It's okay. You don't need to comfort me. I'm fine... Really, I'm all right...”

They could all see how disappointed she was.

From the sofa, Ethan watched them stumble over their words and spoke coldly. “She lost once. Why are you all moping around as though it's the end of the world?”

What?

Ash and the others stared at him. Shouldn't he be trying to comfort May gently?

Ethan ignored their looks and turned to her. His voice was stern. “Pull yourself together! Drew beat you decisively. His appeal and his battle were both better than yours. Everyone saw it.”

May's eyes reddened, but Ethan continued without softening his words.

“There's no shame in that. He started earlier than you, and his Pokémon is stronger. Those are facts, which means you have to work harder. You're upset because he mocked you before the Contest and then beat you in the battle, aren't you? This was your first Contest, and you already did well. If you can't bear a setback this small, you might as well give up becoming a Coordinator now!”

It was a merciless speech, unlike the Ethan they were used to. Yet it had an immediate effect.

May's disappointment gave way to the words echoing through her head. She knew he was speaking for her sake, so she wasn't angry.

She wanted to tell him that she understood all of that. She wasn't as fragile as he thought; she just felt bad for the moment and would soon recover. But when she opened her mouth, all she managed was, “I understand. The next time we meet, I'll do better than I did today!”

Max stared at his sister, shocked that she had accepted the scolding so readily. Then he looked at Ethan.

His idol could actually keep May in line! When had that happened? It was incredible.

Vivian's startled cry came from the television, drawing their attention back to the Contest.

On the screen, another second-round battle was underway: Drew, who had just eliminated May, was facing Robert.

What surprised Ash and the others was Roselia's condition. It had seemed so powerful against May, but now Robert's Milotic had left it covered in injuries.

Three minutes had passed. Milotic had taken only one hit, losing about a fifth of its point bar. Drew had only a fifth of his own bar left.

Robert pressed his advantage, ordering Milotic to use Iron Tail. The blow sent Roselia flying again.

Milotic didn't have a favorable type matchup against Roselia. To dominate it this thoroughly, it had to be strong enough to overcome that disadvantage.

It was much like Drew's battle with May: the Coordinator with the type advantage was losing. Only now Drew was the one forced to feel May's frustration.

Like May, he kept searching for a way to turn the battle around. With the end of the match approaching, he ordered another Magical Leaf.

This time it brought him no luck. Milotic answered with Twister, sweeping every leaf aside at once.

May immediately understood the difference between herself and Robert. Magical Leaf could be stopped. Beautifly's Gust simply hadn't been powerful enough to do it.

Ring, ring, ring!

Before Drew could try anything else, the bell sounded to end the match. His face changed as Vivian announced, “Time is up! Robert and his Milotic advance to the final!”

“Drew lost?” Ash and the others exchanged incredulous looks.

They had expected either Drew or Robert to win the Contest, but they hadn't imagined such a one-sided battle. There was no time even to enjoy Drew's defeat.

Ash watched Robert on the screen. “He's that strong?”

Ethan nodded. Even outside a Pokémon Contest, Milotic's battle strength could carry Robert far in an ordinary competition.

On the field, Drew stood motionless, as though he still couldn't believe the result. Only minutes ago, he had gone to May's waiting room to mock her. Now he had been eliminated himself. The reversal was so swift it must have stung.

Ethan glanced at May and shrugged. “See? There's always someone stronger. You thought Drew was your biggest opponent, but he couldn't beat Robert. Look at him—he's taking it even harder than you did. Maybe he'll lie in bed tonight and secretly cry into his pillow.”

The others all pictured Drew doing just that. They shuddered. It was an image they would rather not see.

After Drew's defeat, the last match in the other half of the bracket soon produced its winner. Robert met a female Coordinator in the final and won again with overwhelming strength, taking the Contest title as expected.

Ethan and the others stayed in the waiting room to watch Robert receive his prize. Only when the entire Contest had ended did they leave the venue.

It was dusk outside. Spectators poured out through every exit. Ethan's group came out by the contestants' passage, and soon spotted Dr. Abby waiting in the distance.

She wore a sunhat and large sunglasses, so she had no need to worry about being recognized. When she saw May, she came straight over.

“That was a wonderful performance,” she told May gently. “For a beginner to score 26.7 in her first Contest and reach the second round is an excellent result!”

“Thank you, Dr. Abby.” May had had time to recover and could now face her loss calmly. “Your advice helped me so much. I would have done much worse without it.”

They talked for a little while. Then, as the light faded, Abby waved goodbye. She had come to Slateport only to watch the Contest. Now it was over, she needed to return to Foothill Town. She had waited outside solely to say farewell to them.

“If you ever come to Foothill Town, you must visit me,” she said.

Ash nodded vigorously. “We will!”

“Until we meet again, then!”

“Have a safe trip, Dr. Abby!”

They watched her leave, then Misty and the others headed back toward the Pokémon Center.

Ethan turned to follow. Out of the corner of his eye, he noticed the Team Rocket trio crouched before an elderly man beside one of the venue's walls. What were they doing?

He watched for a moment. Jessie and Meowth were crouching there half asleep. Only James was writing furiously in a notebook, recording everything the old man said as he talked without pause.

Well, that was rare. Team Rocket had followed them here but hadn't caused any trouble. The sun must have risen in the west. Something had kept the trio occupied.

Ethan shook his head and looked away. He wasn't going to interfere.

The Contest was over. Tomorrow morning, he would set out again and continue his own adventure.
