From the spectator balcony, Lance applauded softly when the first round ended. He turned to his uncle.

"See? I told you he was strong. Clair couldn't do a thing against him!"

"He does have some skill."

The middle-aged man gave Ethan another look. After that restrained praise, he turned and left.

As chief of the Dragon Tamer clan, he had little desire to watch a member he valued fight a battle that seemed destined to end in defeat. Better to spare himself the frustration and leave.

On the field, Clair took out her second Poké Ball. Ethan called Gardevoir back to his side, but did not return it to its ball. It would watch the next round from nearby.

They released their Pokémon at the same time again. Clair chose Altaria, and Ethan chose Crystal Steelix.

As the blue light faded, each studied the other's Pokémon with interest.

Clair admired Crystal Steelix. No matter how many times she saw its clear, crystalline body, she found it beautiful. If only there were a Dragon-type Pokémon that looked like that...

Ethan was seeing a real Altaria for the first time. He gave it a longer look too. Its sky-blue, birdlike body was almost hidden by soft wings resembling cotton clouds. It looked charming and light.

For a Dragon type, it looked far more like a bird. Almost by instinct, Ethan raised his Pokédex toward it.

"Altaria, the Humming Pokémon. Dragon and Flying type. On clear days, Altaria flies freely among the clouds. Its cottony wings carry it high on rising air currents, and it sings in a beautiful high voice. Listeners can become so enchanted by its melody that they feel as if they are dreaming."

Despite Altaria's Dragon type, its birdlike appearance had once earned it a joking place beside Druddigon and Flygon among Pokémon called embarrassments to dragons. Once it Mega Evolved into Mega Altaria, its type changed to Dragon/Fairy. It could then turn its Fairy power against other Dragon types, earning an even grander joke title: traitor to dragons.

Ethan had not expected Clair, a Dragon Tamer, to make such a famously unusual Pokémon one of her best partners.

The matchup was awkward for him. Crystal Steelix's Ground-type moves would not affect a Flying-type Altaria, while Flying-type moves could still hit Steelix normally. That took away a large part of his offense. Fortunately, Steelix still knew Rock-type moves after evolving, and its Steel type gave it another way to fight back.

Clair saw the matchup too and smiled. "Looks like I have the type advantage this time."

Ethan nodded. He could have chosen Charizard or Gyarados and tried to overpower her more easily. Instead, he wanted to use the match to train his other Pokémon, especially in even or unfavorable battles.

As soon as the round began, Clair attacked with the same boldness she had shown before. Altaria pressed Crystal Steelix hard.

Ethan did not have Steelix burrow rapidly in and out of the ground as he had done against Falkner at Violet Gym. He kept it on the surface, counterattacking and watching for a gap in Altaria's movements.

But Rock Tomb failed to catch Altaria, and it easily dodged Stone Edge. Every Rock-type attack missed.

Altaria kept striking. Crystal Steelix's enormous body made it seem clumsy by comparison, leaving it largely on the defensive.

Bang! Bang! Bang!

Attacks landed across Steelix's body. Its crystalline surface was exceptionally hard, though, and Altaria had no move that could exploit a weakness. Even when its strikes connected, they dealt little damage.

The standoff lasted some time. When Altaria came in once more with Wing Attack, Ethan narrowed his eyes.

"There's our chance! Dragon Breath, now!"

Crystal Steelix raised its head and breathed a blue-green flame that engulfed Altaria.

"What?!" Clair was startled. Then she shook her head at herself. "My mistake. I so rarely battle a Steelix that I forgot it can use Dragon Breath."

"I can't blame you," Ethan said. "There are so many kinds of Pokémon. Who remembers every move outside the ones they see often? Even a Pokémon professor must forget some."

Ethan himself did not know why an Onix that evolved into Steelix could learn Dragon Breath. Perhaps the old stories of snakes becoming dragons were not mere tales. A Pokémon that lived deep underground might have picked up a trace of dragon power.

He turned his attention back to the field. The surprise Dragon Breath had caught Altaria unprepared and dealt double damage, finally making up some of Steelix's disadvantage.

Altaria emerged with burns over much of its body. It flew back into the air and regarded Crystal Steelix warily.

"Don't be afraid!" Clair shouted. "Just be careful. Come in again with Wing Attack!"

Altaria dived once more, but its newfound caution dulled the fearless force of its earlier attacks.

"Crystal Steelix, Gyro Ball!"

Steelix's great body began to spin faster and faster. The force of its rotation made the whole Gym tremble.

Altaria could not pull out of its dive in time. It struck the spinning Steelix and was thrown back onto the ground.

Ethan seized the opening. "Iron Tail!"

Crystal Steelix swung its heavy tail down at Altaria before it could rise.

Bang!

The blow drew a pained cry from the Pokémon whose voice was usually so beautiful.
