"Congratulations! You've captured another Legendary Pokémon!"

Erika recalled her two Pokémon and walked over to Ethan, looking with some envy at the Great Ball containing Suicune in his hand.

She was a Grass-type Trainer who captured only Grass-type Pokémon. But Suicune was still a Legendary Pokémon, rarely seen under ordinary circumstances, much less captured. Knowing it now rested quietly inside that Great Ball made it difficult to remain calm.

"Still, that was close. Ho-Oh nearly rescued Suicune too!"

"It really was..." Ethan sighed, showing little joy. "That old sneak—uh, Ho-Oh hid itself far too well. Thanks to your help, it didn't completely succeed. I really appreciate it!"

"I barely helped at all." Erika shook her head gently. "Leafeon and Tangela only joined the commotion and helped make your side look more intimidating."

Despite her words, a faint smile appeared on Erika's face.

She understood that her two Grass-type Pokémon might not have endured even one attack from Ho-Oh, given their type disadvantage and Ho-Oh's overwhelming power.

Even so, she had refused to stand aside and had sent them out to help Ethan.

Knowing they were outmatched was one thing. Choosing to act was another. Companions traveling together were supposed to help one another in difficult moments.

After thanking her, Ethan looked away from Erika and returned his attention to the Great Ball.

If he could have chosen, he would have preferred the Shiny Suicune from the movie.

Compared with an ordinary Suicune, that Shiny Suicune possessed more vivid colors and looked unquestionably more impressive.

Unfortunately, encountering a Shiny Suicune was a matter of pure luck.

Forget it...

Ethan was not trying to collect all three Legendary beasts. Capturing this Suicune already represented an enormous reward.

Before arriving in Greenfield, he and Erika had planned only to rest there for one night and continue their journey at dawn. Who could have imagined that he would capture a Legendary Pokémon here?

It was already an unexpected windfall.

Rapid footsteps approached from the distance. Ethan turned and saw Ash, Misty, Brock, and Delia running toward them.

"Ethan! Erika!!"

When Ethan looked their way, Misty waved to them.

Before he had even caught his breath after reaching them, Ash asked excitedly with shining eyes, "Did you defeat the three Legendary beasts? Wait, forget that for now! Ethan, we thought we saw Ho-Oh just now! Did you see it too!?"

The name Ho-Oh immediately rekindled Ethan's anger. He rolled his eyes and refused to answer.

Ash and the others found his reaction extremely strange and could not understand it.

They had been occupied with Molly and the Unown and had not witnessed Ethan's confrontation with Ho-Oh.

Erika covered her mouth and laughed softly, then told them what had happened. Only then did they learn that Ethan had clashed with Ho-Oh.

"What!? Ethan captured Suicune and fought Ho-Oh!?"

Ash, Misty, and Brock all focused on the hand holding the Great Ball. Their expressions matched the one Erika had worn earlier.

Internally, Ash was stunned.

Ethan had captured yet another Legendary Pokémon in such a short time?

Did that not mean the distance between their strength had grown even wider?

"It's only Suicune. It's nothing worth making a fuss about." Ethan waved dismissively with extraordinary false modesty.

In a sense, he was not wrong. Goh had also captured a Suicune in the anime. Of the three Legendary beasts, Suicune had received the least dignified treatment.

When Ash tried to say more, Ethan interrupted him.

"What happened on your side?"

With the conversation returning to serious matters, Misty eagerly recounted everything. Ethan finally learned that after the crystal tower was destroyed, Ash had used his gift for persuasion to convince Molly to release her obsession. Once she let go, the false Entei had no reason to continue existing and faded into the air.

"What about Molly and the Unown?"

"Molly stayed at the manor." Misty shook her head. "As for the Unown... I don't know where they went. By the time we finished dealing with Entei and looked around, every one of them had disappeared!"

They had vanished into thin air?

Ethan rubbed his chin.

They had probably returned to wherever they originally existed. In that case, the matter had finally been resolved and brought to an end.

The group chatted a while longer before Ethan checked the time on his phone. Without anyone noticing, it had become nearly ten at night.

After fighting for so long, everyone was exhausted and sleepy. Several Pokémon had depleted their strength and urgently needed to recover, while Delia needed to be escorted somewhere safe. After a brief discussion, the group decided to return to the Pokémon Center and rest.

They had walked only a short distance toward the Pokémon Center when the roar of helicopter rotors sounded overhead.

The noise grew rapidly closer.

There was no need to guess. With the thunderstorm gone and no Pokémon blocking the way, the rescue helicopter that had been circling outside the danger zone could finally approach safely.

But...

The group exchanged glances and smiled knowingly.

Whether it was the police or the rescue team, they had arrived far too late. They always appeared only after everything was over.

...

Inside the Pokémon Center's dining room, everyone gathered around a long table and eagerly ate the lavish dinner Nurse Joy had specially prepared as a reward.

A window stood beside their table. Through it, they could see Professor Oak being interviewed by the female reporter and her camera operator.

The wall prevented them from hearing the reporter's questions. But the group had agreed upon their story before returning, so even Professor Oak did not know what had truly happened at the crystal tower.

They told Professor Oak only that the Unown had caused everything and that Ho-Oh had ultimately appeared to prevent the situation from spreading any further.

Professor Oak accepted the explanation. Like the reporter, he had witnessed Ho-Oh's appearance at the end.

It was a well-intentioned lie.

It protected Ethan's privacy while preventing the battle between Legendary Pokémon from causing panic among the people of Johto. The story accomplished both goals at once.

Whether the audience believed it would depend entirely upon each viewer.
