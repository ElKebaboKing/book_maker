Olivine City and Cianwood City were both coastal cities, and they were not particularly far apart, so many ships traveled between them every day.

At the docks, Ethan and Erika easily found a medium-sized passenger ferry bound for Cianwood City.

The ferry did not travel there directly, however. It would first pass through the Whirl Islands and make a brief stop before continuing toward Cianwood City.

The route would undoubtedly take much longer, but it was still their best option at present.

They bought their tickets, boarded the ferry, and found seats on the deck.

The damp, salty sea breeze swept over them. Before long, a tremor ran through the hull, and the ferry slowly pulled out of the harbor.

Ethan took out a Poké Ball and released the Eevee that Naoko had given him in Ecruteak City.

It was clearly Eevee's first time aboard a ship. Bursting with excitement, it found everything novel. One moment it ran to the railing to gaze across the sea; the next, it scampered all over the deck, brimming with energy. Perhaps the motion made it seasick, however, because it had not been running for long before it curled up at Ethan's feet and refused to move again.

The little fellow was obviously seasick!

Ethan shook his head in amusement. Leaving Eevee alone, he turned his gaze toward the sea.

Traveling across the ocean truly was pleasant. After growing accustomed to rushing from place to place, relaxing once in a while felt wonderful. It refreshed the mind and even seemed to slow the passage of time.

He ordered two cocktails from the bar on deck, then drank and chatted with Erika as they enjoyed this rare leisurely journey.

The ferry cut through the sea, leaving two even wakes behind it. But after seeing the same scenery for so long, their interest soon faded.

After more than half an hour at sea, Ethan lounged comfortably in a deck chair and was nearly dozing off from boredom when an elderly man suddenly sat beside them.

At first, the man merely sat there in silence. As the voyage grew dull, however, he appeared to feel lonely and struck up a conversation with them.

The old man spoke with uncommon insight and had his own perspective on many subjects. As a native of the Whirl Islands, he also knew at least a little about every place and event in the area.

For example, he told them about a flock of enormous Pidgey living on the islands. More than three times the size of ordinary Pidgey, they were giants among their kind. Yet precisely because they had grown so large, they had lost the ability to fly.

Pidgey that could not fly?

Had their ability deteriorated?

The strange story surprised them both. Their interest piqued, they began asking the old man more questions.

Through his explanation, Ethan learned that the Whirl Islands did not refer to a single island, but to an entire archipelago.

It consisted of four large islands—Blue Point Isle, Red Rock Isle, Yellow Rock Isle, and Silver Rock Isle—along with numerous smaller islands. Together, they formed a spiral. Powerful whirlpools surrounded the archipelago, and the dangerous waters had once made it extremely difficult to approach. Only with the advent of modern ships had people escaped the currents' constraints and allowed the Whirl Islands to flourish.

According to legend, the Whirl Islands had been one enormous island in ancient times. Changes in the earth's crust had split it into the four main islands.

Beneath the surface, a series of dark, maze-like caverns therefore connected the islands. Exploring downward from any island was exceptionally difficult, and rumors even claimed that Lugia could be encountered in the deepest reaches of those tangled caves!

Hearing the old man mention Lugia reminded Ethan of the one known as the God of the Sea that he had encountered in the Orange Islands.

The Lugia here in Johto were clearly different from that one. If Ethan remembered correctly, these were a mother and her child—two Lugia in total.

Glancing at the Eevee by Ethan's feet, the old man smiled. "Are you two also going to enter the Water-type Pokémon tournament?"

"A Water-type Pokémon tournament?"

Ethan and Erika looked at each other, then shook their heads in confusion. They had never planned to stop at the Whirl Islands, so they had done no research on the area.

"This is the first I've heard of it. What kind of tournament is it?"

"Then you've arrived at exactly the right time!" the old man said eagerly. "It's held in Inland City on Blue Point Isle once every three years, specifically for Trainers who use Water-type Pokémon. The winner receives a generous cash prize and becomes famous overnight! As Trainers, you simply can't miss an event like this!"

Ethan did not care about becoming famous, but that generous cash prize the man mentioned...

His funds still felt far too meager. If there was money to be made, this tournament suddenly became much more appealing!

At the thought, he glanced at Erika.

She saw the eagerness in his eyes.

"Then you should enter." She smiled softly. "A tournament like this is certain to draw many skilled competitors. It will be excellent training for your Pokémon too!"

Ethan nodded and asked, "Will you enter?"

"I'm a Grass-type Trainer!" Erika shook her head gently. "I don't have any Water-type Pokémon with me for a tournament like this!"

At three that afternoon, the ferry arrived at the port of Inland City on Blue Point Isle. It was the most prosperous city in the Whirl Islands and the archipelago's capital.

Under their original plan, the pair would have remained aboard and continued to Cianwood City. Now that Ethan had made the Water-type tournament his new objective, however, they disembarked here instead.

Only after leaving the docks and entering the city did they realize just how lively it was!

The tournament had drawn vast numbers of Trainers and spectators from far away. All that activity brought them considerable trouble as well.

Ethan first went to the local Pokémon Center and registered for the preliminary round. Just as he was about to arrange a room there, Nurse Joy informed him that every room was already occupied by Trainers.

With no other choice, they left the Pokémon Center and tried several nearby hotels and inns, only to find every one completely full.

With so many visitors pouring in, the entire city was probably the same.

In the end, Ethan simply booked an expensive, upscale suite at the hotel closest to the tournament venue.

Perhaps because the rate was so high, one such room was still available. But that meant the unmarried man and woman would inevitably be spending the night alone together!

A peculiar atmosphere settled between them.

Since beginning their journey, the two had never even shared a tent in the wilderness. If they had, this might have felt less awkward. Instead, they had skipped straight to this step, and it felt uncomfortably like a couple checking into a hotel together!
