A huge crystal tail swept toward them with tremendous force.

Blastoise reached out to shield Ethan from the Crystal Onix's Iron Tail. The blow struck its body and drove it several steps backward. It lost its footing and fell onto its back.

Ethan's eyes brightened. The Onix had knocked over a sturdy Blastoise head-on. Its crystal body was not its only unusual strength.

"Your turn, Venusaur. Use Vine Whip!"

Two vines shot from the flower on Venusaur's back and lashed the Crystal Onix. This time it reacted, drawing back in pain.

"You were right," Sabrina said. "Grass-type moves hurt it badly. That supports your guess that it's still Rock and Ground type."

Ethan nodded. Then he remembered his Pokédex, took it out, and pointed it at the Onix.

"Beep. Identifying Pokémon... Identification complete. Pokémon resembles Onix. Details unknown. No matching record in the database. Upload this Pokémon's information?"

Even the Pokédex had no information on the Crystal Onix. Ethan shook his head and selected No.

"Beep. Upload canceled."

He put the Pokédex away. He had seen enough. It was time to end the battle and catch the Onix.

"Venusaur, use Razor Leaf! Everyone else, guard the cave entrance. Don't let it escape. Blastoise, get into the lake and block the route underwater!"

His Pokémon moved into position. Venusaur's Razor Leaf struck the Crystal Onix head-on. Its great size made it an easy target.

Hundreds of sharp green leaves swept over it. The Onix roared in pain and tried to retreat into the lake. Blastoise was waiting there. It drove the Onix back with Skull Bash, throwing water high into the air. Spray fell across the shore.

"Too late to run," Ethan said. "Venusaur, Razor Leaf again!"

The leaves struck a second time. Enduring the pain, the Crystal Onix countered with Stone Edge. As Venusaur dodged, the Onix lunged at the cave wall, trying to break through the rock and escape.

Gardevoir stopped it with Psychic before it could bring the wall down.

After so many attacks, the Crystal Onix swayed. It looked ready to collapse. Ethan promptly threw a Poké Ball.

Red light drew the Onix inside. The ball shook hard for several moments. Ethan watched until, with a final click, it fell still.

Sabrina applauded softly. "Well done. Congratulations on catching such a rare Pokémon."

Ethan raised his eyebrows at her, unable to hide his pride. He picked up the ball and placed it in the pouch at his waist.

With the Crystal Onix, he now owned twelve Pokémon. Both pouches at his hips were full. If he caught a thirteenth, its Poké Ball would be sent back to a laboratory.

Diantha had obtained his Pokédex from Professor Sycamore in Kalos and given it to him. Ordinarily, an extra Pokémon would be transferred to Sycamore. Ethan had never met the professor and did not want to ask a stranger to care for his partners, so he had changed the destination to Cerise Laboratory when he was there.

He had grown close to Professor Cerise and his family, as well as Parker and Ren. When he asked if they would look after any Pokémon sent back, all of them readily agreed.

Chloe had promised especially earnestly that she would care for them herself, without making anyone else do it. Earlier in their acquaintance, she would never have said that. She had not once been so eager to look after Pokémon. But Ethan had changed Chloe in his own way, just as he had changed Sabrina, and these would be his Pokémon she was caring for.

Ash had Professor Oak to help him. With Chloe looking after his partners, Ethan could travel and catch the Pokémon he liked without worrying about those he sent home.

His thoughts returned to the cave. They had found what they came for and could leave. First, Ethan took out his phone and photographed the crystal chamber for a keepsake. Then, taking a chance, he leaned close to Sabrina and took a selfie with her.

Click.

The crystals reflected light around them. In the photograph, Ethan and Sabrina stood shoulder to shoulder. He grinned broadly, showing his teeth. Beneath her smooth black hair, which fell past her shoulders, Sabrina wore a faint smile.

Ethan liked the picture. But he knew Sabrina might have her own opinion about how she looked in it. Before saving it, he showed her the screen.

"What do you think? Is it all right?"

Sabrina glanced at it without changing her expression. The setting was beautiful, and they looked well matched. Their heads even inclined slightly toward each other. She had never been in love, but she could see what that small movement suggested.

"It's fine. It would make a good phone wallpaper. Send me a copy later."

She tried to sound casual. She did not seem to notice how bright her eyes had become, or how light her voice sounded.
