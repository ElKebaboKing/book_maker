Like Ethan, Sabrina generally caught only Pokémon she liked. For some reason, the unusual-looking Natu had struck her as adorable, and she wanted it for herself.

This time she did not fight it personally. She sent out Kadabra and chose to have a Pokémon battle.

At Sabrina's signal, Kadabra attacked as soon as it appeared. Natu tried to hop down and flee, but it could not fly. Kadabra caught up with Teleport, and Natu had no choice but to fight back in haste. A wild Pokémon that had not yet evolved was no match for one of a Gym Leader's main partners. Kadabra knocked it down after only a few exchanges.

Sabrina threw a Poké Ball and caught Natu easily.

Ethan had watched the whole thing. He applauded softly and smiled. "That wasn't easy to come by. It's only the second Pokémon you've caught on this journey! And if you hadn't sent out Kadabra, I would have thought you were going to handle the battle yourself again."

Sabrina gave him a small smile. Catching a new Pokémon had put her in a good mood.

"Don't you think it's cute?"

"Cute?"

Ethan raised his brows and tried to picture Natu again. He could not see what about it was cute. But Sabrina's tastes had never been easy to predict. As long as she was happy, it did not matter.

For its part, Natu probably could not understand how a moment's rest on a branch had ended with its capture. It had only looked her way once.

After that brief interruption, the two continued through the forest and reached Mahogany Town before dark.

Near the entrance, Ethan noticed a badly damaged wooden sign lying among some rocks. He brushed away the dirt. It read: Welcome to Mahogany Town, home of the ninjas.

He could not tell how long the sign had lain there. It had fallen apart through years of neglect, cast aside by time like the ninjas who had once lived in the town. Ethan set it aside and shook his head. Mahogany was the home of the ninjas in name only now.

Low wooden houses in a Japanese style lined the streets. Elderly residents talked outside their doors, and young children ran about freely. Life moved slowly here. Few vehicles passed, and Ethan saw surprisingly few Pokémon.

Lights glowed inside the homes, and the smell of cooking drifted into the street. Ethan's stomach growled. He was tired after the long day. Since meeting Looker and raiding the Rocket research facility, neither he nor Sabrina had eaten a proper meal.

First they needed somewhere to stay and something for dinner. The Gym challenge could wait until tomorrow; it was already late. With that settled, they headed toward the center of town.

They passed an ordinary-looking store with a police cordon outside. International Police officers were still coming and going. This had to be the entrance to Team Rocket's Mahogany base.

As they had done with the Celadon Game Corner in Kanto, Team Rocket had hidden the entrance inside a business. Frequent visitors would draw less suspicion there. For all their precautions, the base had still fallen.

Ethan and Sabrina paused to look, then moved on.

Mahogany Town was small and had no Pokémon Center. Following the map, Ethan found a guesthouse near the Gym. He liked the relaxed atmosphere of such places better than that of hotels, and chose them whenever he could.

They ate a filling dinner and bathed together. Planning to visit the Gym early, they went to bed soon afterward. Ethan was not ready to sleep, though. He made love to Sabrina until midnight before finally drifting off.

...

Mahogany Gym was the town's official Gym. Its Leader, Pryce, was an elderly man who specialized in Ice-type Pokémon. Defeating him earned a challenger the Glacier Badge.

Pryce was formidably strong. He had been a competitive Trainer in his youth, and age had only made his vast experience harder to overcome. He trained his Pokémon strictly, kept his distance from them, and avoided forming bonds of trust. That had earned him the name "Pryce of Winter." In some ways, he was the strongest of Johto's Gym Leaders.

Early the next morning, Ethan arrived outside Mahogany Gym with Sabrina, yawning and looking sleepy.

The Gym stood inside a courtyard. From the street, it looked much like an ordinary indoor swimming pool and had no distinguishing features. Given Mahogany's location and small population, there was little reason to establish a Gym here. Rumor said the Pokémon League had created it to persuade Pryce to stay and provide him a place to spend his old age.

The courtyard gate was open, so Ethan went in without ringing the bell. The instant he crossed the threshold, a wave of cold air struck him. Goose bumps rose on his skin, and every trace of sleepiness vanished.

He looked toward one corner of the courtyard. Snow and ice covered the area beside a pool, making it seem like a tiny winter world cut off from everything around it. Dozens of Ice-type Pokémon of various species played there happily.

Outside the wall, the street had been pleasantly warm. Within it, the air felt like winter. A single wall separated two seasons.

As Ethan watched with interest, footsteps sounded behind him. He turned. A stern-faced old man was walking slowly out of the Gym.

The man frowned at the sight of him in the courtyard. "Who are you?"

That must be Pryce.

Ethan gave a small nod and smiled, asking a question whose answer he already knew. "Hello. I've come to challenge Mahogany Gym. Are you its Leader?"

His warm smile stood in sharp contrast to Pryce's cold manner.
