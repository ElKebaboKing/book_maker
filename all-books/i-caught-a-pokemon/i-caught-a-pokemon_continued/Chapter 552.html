When Ethan reached the café beside the competitors' village, Alexa and two colleagues wearing Lumiose Press vests were already waiting outside.

She had crossed the sea alone with two Pokémon to report on the Indigo League. This time, she had brought a professional team and was clearly well prepared.

"Mr. Ethan, it's been so long!" Alexa smiled and offered her hand.

"Hello, everyone."

Ethan shook hands with all three. Then he smiled at Alexa. "Sent to Johto this time? It seems no League tournament can do without you. You must be busy!"

"I can't help it. It's part of the job."

After a brief exchange, Alexa and her colleagues welcomed Ethan into the café.

To give the interview a relaxed, elegant setting and keep strangers from interrupting, Alexa had rented the whole place. Apart from the four of them, only one employee remained behind the counter.

Ethan sat on a sofa. Alexa set a steaming cup of coffee before him while her colleagues prepared the equipment.

They aimed the camera at Ethan and arranged the microphone, lights, and reflector. Then they gave Alexa an okay sign.

Alexa nodded, sat opposite Ethan, and crossed her legs at an angle.

His eyes were drawn to them. Alexa was beautiful, and she had a lovely figure too.

Her close-fitting pants showed off the shape of her legs in particular.

After only a glance, Ethan looked away without giving himself away.

"Mr. Ethan, may we begin?" Alexa asked, unaware of his glance. "If you're nervous, don't worry. This is recorded, not live. We can make small edits afterward if anything doesn't turn out as you'd like."

"No problem. I'm ready."

Seeing how calm he was, Alexa smiled. "Then let's begin."

The camera started rolling. Alexa faced the lens. "Welcome, viewers, to this year's Silver Conference. I'm special correspondent Alexa. Today we're honored to have a remarkable competitor with us: the previous Indigo League champion and an old friend of ours, Mr. Ethan Vale!"

The camera turned to Ethan, who smiled and waved.

"Hello to everyone watching in Kalos. I'm Ethan, and it's good to see you again."

After his greeting, Alexa asked her first question. "What are you hoping to achieve at the Silver Conference?"

"My goal?" Ethan answered without hesitation. "To go as far as I can. I think I should at least try to match my result at the Indigo League."

Alexa could not help smiling.

Match his Indigo League result? Ethan had won that tournament. Was this his way of saying he intended to win the Silver Conference too?

He was not being modest at all.

With the reporters outside, Ethan had been cautious and formal. He did not know them and worried they might misrepresent his answers. Lumiose Press was no gossip tabloid, and Alexa had earned his trust, so he felt no need to hide behind rehearsed phrases.

That was why his manner with her was so different.

Alexa did not know exactly what he was thinking, but she liked the answer.

Viewers enjoyed an interview with some humor. Nobody wanted a string of stiff questions and answers that sounded prepared in advance.

Ethan was handsome, but he was also relaxed and lively.

What audience would not like a competitor like that?

Alexa asked many more questions, and Ethan answered each in the same easy tone.

They talked comfortably. Nearly twenty minutes later, the interview ended, and Alexa was delighted with how it had gone.

At her request, Ethan then brought out Dragonite, Scizor, and Tyranitar, the three Pokémon he had used in the screening trials. They posed for several photographs to use in the interview video and news cover.

It was much like their first interview.

Now, though, they knew each other better, and Ethan was more at ease with the process.

...

Once everything was finished, Ethan said goodbye to Alexa and headed for Silver Stadium to meet Erika and the others.

He felt drained. The shoot and interview had exhausted him more, in body and mind, than a whole day of directing his Pokémon in battle.

Given the choice, he would rather deal with Ash's Pokémon every day than sit through another shoot and interview.

Silver Town was even busier than it had been yesterday. The opening ceremony was about to begin, and a crowd packed the area in front of Silver Stadium.

Ethan went through the competitors' entrance to the stadium's waiting room. Erika and the others had been looking out for him for some time.

"You're finally back!" Misty said, relieved. "What took so long? The opening ceremony is about to start. I thought you might miss it!"

"Don't worry. I was watching the time."

As one of the Trainers who had reached the preliminaries, Ethan had to take part in the ceremony. He and the other forty-seven would enter the stadium through the competitors' passage, face the crowd, welcome the arrival of the sacred flame, and listen to Goodshow's speech.

Those steps were both mandatory and an honor. Even if he found them tedious, he would have to be patient.

But...

Ethan glanced around. "Where's Ash?"

"I don't know." Misty shook her head. "I saw him leave with two staff members. Maybe he's taking part in some activity."

Ethan was about to say more when other staff entered the waiting room and began lining up the competitors for the procession. He stopped asking questions, said goodbye to Erika and the others, and joined the crowd.

"We'll see you later!" Misty waved. "We'll be in the stands, enjoying the show!"
