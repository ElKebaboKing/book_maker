As the Pokédex had said, Scizor's steel body might be too heavy for flight. The wings on its back could no longer support its weight. At most, they might slow a fall by letting it glide briefly.

In exchange, its new Steel typing had greatly improved its ability to take a hit. It was like a humanoid suit of armor.

Ethan patted Scizor's chest. It rang with a hollow clang that stung his palm, as if he had struck real steel.

"Not bad!"

Smiling, he walked around Scizor several times and admired it. Scizor seemed pleased with its new body too. It raised its large pincers for a closer look. The only thing it found strange was that its blade-like arms had become pincers.

Well, at least it can grip things now...

Ethan released a few of his smaller Pokémon so they could get used to their "new" companion. Then he waved goodnight to Erika and returned to his room, yawning.

All he wanted was sleep. Spending an entire afternoon shopping with a girl was tiring work.

After breakfast the next morning, the two left the guesthouse and headed straight for the Goldenrod Gym.

They had walked the route the day before, but in daylight it had a different kind of beauty.

They followed a broad street beside the river, with tall buildings on either side. A few entrances led underground to Goldenrod's shopping district, where visitors could find beauty services, snacks, and fashionable goods.

Erika looked interested. Before she could ask, Ethan offered, "If there's time after the Gym challenge, let's come back and look around!"

His thoughtfulness delighted her. Perhaps she had used the new moisturizer she bought yesterday; her face looked especially soft and fresh, adding to her charm.

They crossed a bridge to the other side of the city. A magnetic levitation train station immediately came into view. Completed only a few years ago, it was a major feat of engineering. Its trains could exceed 550 kilometers per hour and, according to what Ethan had heard, carry passengers across Johto and Kanto to Saffron City in just a few hours.

The station looked futuristic. Elevated tracks ran out of the city like a long zipper, vanishing beyond the horizon.

Farther on stood Goldenrod TV Station, a six-story tower. It was a large broadcaster whose signal covered all of Johto. Nearly every town and village could receive its programs, and its ratings had long held first place.

The tower had originally been an enormous old wooden structure. When age and decay destroyed it, the television station was built in its place.

Ethan and Erika stopped to admire the tower and took a photo together. As they were about to leave, they noticed something wrong.

The street below the station was being cordoned off. More than a dozen police cars stood outside, and officers had put up barriers around the entire building.

Among them, Ethan spotted a familiar figure he had not seen in a long time: Looker!

Looker and several officers stood by the hood of a police car, studying a plan. They spoke with furrowed brows, apparently discussing something serious.

"Something must have happened," Erika said quietly.

Ethan nodded. "When Looker turns up, an evil organization is usually involved. Here in Johto, that probably means Team Rocket."

"Should we go see?"

Ethan hesitated, then shook his head.

"The police are already here. We shouldn't interfere. If we get too involved in matters like this, people may think we're overstepping and trying to take charge of everything."

Two more police cars raced past them and stopped beneath the station.

Ethan and Erika were about to leave when Looker turned around. As if sensing Ethan's presence, he spotted him at once.

They were more than a hundred meters apart. Looker had eyes like radar!

His face lit up. He left the other officers and hurried toward Ethan.

Ethan could no longer simply walk away. He gave Erika a wry smile and went to meet him.

Looker called out before he was close. "Ethan, my friend! What a coincidence! I never expected to see you here!!"

My friend?

When had Looker started addressing him like that?

"I didn't expect to see you either. What's going on?" Ethan asked.

Looker sighed. "Team Rocket broke into the television station this morning. They've taken over the entire building!"

They broke into the station?

Ethan realized this seemed to match a plot from the games.

Looker looked him over and glanced discreetly at Erika. Then he smiled. "You're looking well, Ethan. Anabel told me about your raid on that Team Rocket base not long ago. Thank you—you helped us enormously again. How is your badge journey going?"

"Well enough. I have seven badges, and I was on my way to challenge the Goldenrod Gym. Do you need any help with Team Rocket?"

He offered mainly out of courtesy. The police had surrounded the station. As far as he could see, Team Rocket had no escape; clearing the building was only a matter of time.

To his surprise, Looker laughed. "You'd help? Wonderful! To be honest, I was just worrying about what to do. Running into you now is perfect!"

Ethan fell silent.

Looker did not hesitate to accept at all...

With a sigh, Ethan asked, "Have you run into a problem?"

Looker did not answer immediately. Instead, he led Ethan and Erika over to the police car.

The officers watched curiously. Their commander had rushed away without a word and returned with a young man and woman they had never seen.
