In the president's office on the top floor of Devon Corporation, Steven sat in the main guest chair. Ethan and Erika sat together on one side. Erika listened as Ethan and Steven talked at an unhurried pace, adding a few words now and then. The atmosphere was easy and pleasant.

Steven was unfailingly polite. He was not naturally a talkative man, but in company he showed the social grace of a well-raised heir. He made sure everyone felt included. The way he spoke was impressive, and his effortless elegance never felt forced.

Apart from the three of them, only Mr. Stone was in the office.

Kennedy had work to do and the Team Aqua incident to handle. Mr. Stone, by contrast, sat at his desk watching the young people chat with a smile, as though he hoped someone as accomplished as Ethan would become his son's friend.

Steven pushed cups of tea toward his guests. “I've spent this past while traveling and collecting all sorts of stones. I only happened to return so I could send the ones I found to Devon for study. I'd barely come through the door when I heard what had happened.”

“What kind of stones?” Erika asked.

“Remarkable stones with different powers,” Steven said with a smile. “They're hidden in mountains, caves, and under the sea. Some have names already. Others don't, and we still have no idea what they can do. You've heard of Mega Stones, haven't you? Before anyone discovered their power, no one knew their true value. The same was once true of Evolution stones. I want to find out what potential other stones might hold.”

It was an unusual hobby and meaningful research. If Steven discovered something important, it might change the world for people and Pokémon alike.

Speaking of stones...

Ethan had seen plenty on his travels. Most were common, well-understood things like Fire Stones, Leaf Stones, and Everstones. The only truly precious one he had encountered was the Soul Dew formed from Latios and Latias in Altomare.

When he told Steven about it, Steven was astonished.

“What? You've seen the Soul Dew?”

“Yes. We saw it in the Water Capital. It held incredible power: it could revive fossils and even cause a tremendous tsunami. It was beautiful to look at, too.”

“I've heard of it,” Steven said eagerly. “They say the Legendary Pokémon Latias and Latios gave their lives and all their power to create it. I thought it was only a legend. To think it truly existed! I should go there and see it myself.”

For someone who loved collecting stones, a marvelous crystal like the Soul Dew held immense appeal, even if it had not formed naturally.

Now that he knew one had really existed in the Water Capital near Johto, how could he sit still?

“You'll be disappointed,” Ethan said. “The Soul Dew was polluted and destroyed. It's gone. You won't see it if you go.”

He told Steven the broad outline of what had happened in Altomare, leaving out the fact that he owned Suicune and had caught Latias and Latios in Poké Balls.

“What an enviable experience,” Steven said. Then he looked at Ethan. “You came to Hoenn for the Ever Grande Conference, I assume?”

“Of course.” Ethan smiled. “I want to catch Hoenn Pokémon, challenge its Gyms, and enter the Ever Grande Conference.”

“Then be careful.” Steven's eyes brightened. “Hoenn Trainers are strong. The Ever Grande Conference is even more competitive than the Indigo and Silver Conferences. Winning here won't be easy. As it happens, I won the last Ever Grande Conference. If you need advice, you can ask me anytime.”

That man...

Ethan nearly laughed. All the talk of caution sounded fine, but Steven really wanted him to know he was the previous Champion.

...

After exchanging contact information with Steven, Ethan and Erika waved goodbye as he saw them out of Devon Corporation.

Steven had wanted a friendly battle to see which of them was stronger. Ethan had politely declined.

Most of his main Pokémon were elsewhere. With a team built around Dragonite and Tyranitar, he could not be sure how the match would go.

Winning the Silver Conference had also given him a chance to challenge the Elite Four, but he had turned that down too.

It was not a lack of confidence. He wanted to save such challenges for last. Once he had competed in every conference, he would visit the Elite Four one after another and test himself against them.

They left the street where Devon stood and were about to head for Rustboro Gym when Erika received a video call from another region.

The caller worked at the Celadon Gym. Ethan had seen her at Erika's perfume shop before and knew she and Erika were close friends.

“Erika! The Gym repairs are finished. Someone from the Pokémon League came to ask where you were. When we told them you're traveling in Hoenn, they said to contact you right away. They want you back to run the Gym!”

Ethan and Erika both frowned.

When Erika had left Celadon, she had explained that a fire had damaged the Gym badly enough to close it while the Pokémon League rebuilt it.

That closure had allowed her to travel with Ethan.

So much time had passed that they had both known this news would come. Hearing it now still unsettled them.

What they had been expecting had finally arrived.
