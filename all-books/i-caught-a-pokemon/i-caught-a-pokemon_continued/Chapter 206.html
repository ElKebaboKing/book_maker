The thing you worried about most always seemed to happen.

If something could go wrong, it would, however small the chance. Engineer Edward Murphy had put forward that rule in 1949. Ethan did not know whether it applied in the Pokémon world, or whether anyone here had ever expressed a similar idea. But after setting out, they had been grateful to face only heavy rain. Now the sea had turned fierce again.

Waves nearly two meters high struck the speedboat one after another, making it impossible to stand steadily. Thunder cracked overhead as lightning tore across the night sky.

Crack! Boom!

Maren had grown up by the sea, but even Ethan could tell they had sailed into a terrible storm.

"What rotten luck. I hope lightning doesn't hit the boat." Maren looked up with a rueful smile. "We went through a storm before reaching Shamouti Island. I thought this trip might be calm, but..."

She sighed to herself. They should have stayed on Shamouti and waited until morning. If she had not offered to take Ash, perhaps they would have avoided this storm altogether.

Back on Shamouti Island, Melody watched the rain through a window of the banquet hall, her brow furrowed. Wind stirred the torches, making shadows writhe around the room as uneasily as her thoughts. Rain blew onto her bangs and white veil, but she paid it no attention. She kept looking in the direction Ethan and the others had gone.

They were bound to be in danger at sea in weather like this. Melody made up her mind.

"Carol, lend me a boat!"

"What?" Carol stared at her. "What are you planning to do at this hour?"

"If I hadn't said those things, they wouldn't have gone out in the rain. I put them in danger over a silly tradition. It's my responsibility!"

Sabrina and Misty looked at Melody with new respect. Their brief acquaintance had made her seem willful, but she knew when something mattered.

As Melody prepared to go into the storm, Sabrina stood. "You aren't to blame. None of us expected the weather to change like this."

Coming from Ethan's traveling companion, the reassurance helped Melody feel a little better. Sabrina had not tried to stop her, either. From the way she rose and spoke, Melody could tell she intended to come along.

"Me too!" Misty said. "I'm worried about them. If you're going to look for them, take me with you."

Tracey looked from one to the other, a chicken leg in one hand and roasted meat in the other. He silently got to his feet to go with Misty.

"You all..." Melody hesitated. She knew she should not ask more people to risk going to sea, but they were determined. "All right. If anything happens, though, don't say I didn't warn you!"

...

Bang!

A huge wave lifted Maren's speedboat and smashed it against a submerged reef off Fire Island. The impact flung it upward before it crashed onto the shore.

Maren and Ash were left dazed by the landing. Only Ethan reached the ground safely, protected by Haunter.

They were on Fire Island, their destination, but the darkness kept them from seeing much of it.

"Ow, my back. I think it nearly broke..." Maren groaned. Despite the pain, she examined her boat. "Some parts of the hull are damaged, but at least we made it ashore in one piece."

Pikachu suddenly cried out, jumped from the boat, and ran into the darkness.

"Pikachu! Where are you going?" Ash shouted. Pikachu did not look back. Something on the island seemed to be drawing it away.

"Pikachu!" Ash had no choice but to run after it.

"Come back!" Maren called as he left the shore. "In this weather, you should stay here!"

Ash had already vanished. Her voice echoed across the pebble-strewn beach.

"Never mind," Ethan said with a smile. "Thank you for bringing us here, Miss Maren. We can handle what comes next. Stay and see what the boat needs; we'll be counting on it to get back."

"All right." Maren shook her head. "You're the Trainers. It's your call. Good luck out there."

Ethan switched on a flashlight and left the shore.

Fire Island, as he had expected, had formed from a volcanic eruption. It was home to the Orange Islands' God of Fire. Almost nothing grew on it; bare rock stretched all around him.

Just as Melody had promised, a man-made stone staircase led deep into the volcano. Ethan sent out Gardevoir and Jolteon to walk beside him as a precaution.

Haunter floated out of his shadow. "Be careful," it said close to his ear. "I feel something strange, just like that Pikachu did. It really does seem as though something is about to happen."

"I understand. Keep watch, all of you."

Ethan began climbing the staircase, whose end he could not see. The closer he came to the volcano, the quieter it grew. Apart from his and his Pokémon's footsteps and the occasional gust of wind, he heard nothing. At least the rain had finally stopped.

He climbed for a long time without incident, but saw no sign of Ash or Pikachu. Only when he reached the crater did he find them on a platform.

"Ash, you got up here fast. Did you find anything?"

Ash turned at the sound of his voice and stepped aside. "Ethan, look at this."

By flashlight, Ethan saw a shrine built of massive stones. Set within it was an oddly shaped bronze statue of a bird.
