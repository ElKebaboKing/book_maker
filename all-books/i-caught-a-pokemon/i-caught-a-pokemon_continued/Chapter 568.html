Compared with Charizard, Dragonite had less battle experience and had evolved more recently. Overall, it was a little weaker. Losing the round was a shame, but Ethan could accept it.

He did not care much about his winning streak, either. Everyone lost sometime. His earlier opponents simply had not been strong enough to trouble him, allowing the streak to grow. Ash breaking it now did not mean much.

Ethan recalled the unconscious Dragonite and took Jolteon's Poké Ball from his belt.

The score stood at one to one. Although they were tied in knockouts, Ethan still held an advantage in the condition of their Pokémon.

Most of all, Charizard had spent so much strength defeating Dragonite that it could hardly manage another intense fight.

"Let's see which Pokémon Ethan will choose next... Oh! It's Jolteon again!" the announcer said. "That makes sense. It took little damage against Noctowl, and after that warm-up, it's in excellent shape!"

Seeing Jolteon, Ash wanted to recall Charizard and send out someone else. Charizard's stubborn spirit had been stirred, though, and it shook its head firmly at him. Ash could only let it stay in the battle.

Jolteon was plainly stronger than Dragonite, and Charizard was already badly worn down. They had barely begun to fight when Jolteon's Electroweb shocked Charizard unconscious before it could mount a counterattack.

Charizard had not shown the same heroic strength it had against Gary. Its run ended here, without defeating another of Ethan's Pokémon.

"What a shame..." The announcer shook his head. "Charizard did bring down Dragonite, but it used up so much energy doing it that Jolteon defeated it before it could show us anything more. I'm sure the audience would have loved to see more of its power in this arena. I certainly would!"

The announcer was right. Charizard's performance two days earlier had won it many fans. They had expected another display of strength today, perhaps one that would put Ash on the path to victory. Instead, it had struggled to beat one Pokémon and then fallen easily to Ethan's next choice.

If the crowd wanted to see Charizard fight again, they would have to hope Ash advanced to the next round.

After a moment's thought, Ash took out his third Poké Ball.

He threw it forward, and a round, sleepy-looking blue Pokémon appeared on the field, as large and soft-looking as a giant stuffed toy.

It was Ash's Snorlax.

Ethan narrowed his eyes.

Of the dozen or so Pokémon Ash could call on, Ethan considered three especially dangerous: Pikachu, Charizard, and this Snorlax.

Pikachu and Charizard were both famously strong, capable of astonishing upsets. Any opponent had to treat them with care. Snorlax looked dull and slow, but its strength was as enormous as its appetite.

Even among Snorlax, this one was unusual.

For one thing, it could swim—and it used the butterfly stroke. It was also faster than most of its species.

A Pokémon with that kind of physical power called for another powerhouse.

Ethan recalled Jolteon. After considering his options, he took out his fourth Poké Ball.

"Go, Crystal Steelix!"

He had considered both Crystal Steelix and Tyranitar. But Tyranitar had not yet reached its full strength, and Ethan did not want another result like Dragonite's. Crystal Steelix was the steadier choice.

"Rooar!"

Crystal Steelix roared at Snorlax as soon as it appeared. Spectators seeing its huge, translucent body for the first time gasped in wonder.

"There it is! Ethan's Crystal Steelix!" the announcer exclaimed. "Ladies and gentlemen, we heard before the match that Ethan had an extraordinary Steelix—one with a body as beautiful as crystal and tremendous destructive strength. It's quite unlike an ordinary Shiny Steelix. Seeing it in person is breathtaking!"

Some people had seen this remarkable Pokémon before, but only a few. Now it was on display before the whole city at a major tournament, causing a sensation.

It was far more striking than a typical Shiny Pokémon. Countless spectators learned that a Pokémon could have an extraordinary body like this as well as a different color.

The crowd's reaction did nothing to slow the battle.

As the referee waved his flag, Crystal Steelix slithered forward and charged Snorlax with the force of a heavy truck.

Snorlax still wore its placid, vacant expression. At the instant Steelix was about to hit, it raised both arms and braced them against the giant head.

Steelix's massive body already had momentum behind it. It kept driving forward, pushing Snorlax through several boulders and toward the boundary line.

"Snorlax, don't let it beat you!" Ash shouted.

The muscles in Snorlax's arms bulged visibly. It clenched its teeth and dug its feet in, straining until at last it stopped just short of the line. It had escaped being forced out of bounds.

Before Ash could relax, Crystal Steelix opened its jaws and snapped toward Snorlax.

Snorlax was broad and more than two meters tall, yet Steelix's open mouth seemed large enough to swallow nearly its whole body.

Now Snorlax showed its surprising agility. Before Steelix had fully opened its jaws, Snorlax rolled neatly to the side and escaped them.

That was close...

In the section reserved for friends and family, Brock wiped cold sweat from his brow. As a Trainer who specialized in Rock- and Ground-type Pokémon, he knew how formidable Ethan's Crystal Steelix was.

Had Snorlax reacted a moment later, it would have ended up entirely inside Steelix's mouth.

Snorlax moved alongside Steelix, gathered power in its right fist, and swung a mighty Mega Punch at it.

"Crystal Steelix, burrow underground!"

At Ethan's command, Steelix plunged headfirst into the earth. It avoided the punch and, with astonishing speed, bored a hole so deep its bottom could not be seen.

"What...?" Ash's eyes widened.

He had challenged the Orange League too, and he had fought its Supreme Gym Leader, Drake. He could tell at once that Ethan's Crystal Steelix could dig as fast as Drake's Onix. He remembered all the trouble that Onix's digging had caused him.

So Ethan had taught his Pokémon the same technique?

Ash had beaten Drake's Onix in the end and had some experience dealing with Dig. But Squirtle's Water-type attacks had won that fight. Snorlax had no way to force Crystal Steelix out of the ground, much less deal it any effective damage down there.

What should he do now?

Cold sweat gathered on Ash's forehead.
