Ethan and Erika followed Anabel out of the Pokémon Center.

Now that Anabel had brought her people here, Ethan naturally intended to join them in dealing with Team Rocket.

Jimmy, Marina, Eusine, and Kudo remained behind to watch over Raikou and monitor its condition.

Before leaving, Ethan exchanged phone numbers with Jimmy. That way, the two could contact each other immediately if anything unexpected happened.

The moment Ethan stepped out of the Pokémon Center, he saw seven or eight armored police vehicles parked neatly along the roadside.

In front of them, more than forty International Police officers stood ready. They wore tactical uniforms and helmets and carried batons and riot shields.

The officers' partners—Growlithe and Machamp—stood solemnly beside them.

Much as Weezing, Ekans, and Golbat had become symbols of Team Rocket, Growlithe and Machamp had practically become the signature Pokémon of the police.

One look at the officers' stern faces and piercing gazes made it obvious that this was a highly trained elite unit!

Ethan and Erika were not the only ones watching them. Passing Pokémon Trainers also stared in amazement at the force.

Anabel surveyed the scene. Satisfied that the International Police were presenting an imposing image, she nodded.

"Get in!"

At her command, the officers and their Pokémon boarded the vehicles. Ethan and Erika joined Anabel in the first police SUV.

With Gengar guiding them, the convoy set out toward Team Rocket's base.

Along the way, Anabel explained how they had managed to arrive fully equipped so early in the morning. They had been carrying out a special mission nearby. As soon as Looker requested assistance, they had turned around and come straight here.

Ethan chuckled inwardly.

They had abandoned an ongoing special mission to deal with Team Rocket instead. Apparently, Team Rocket held the highest priority!

It made sense. A vast organization operating across multiple regions was exactly the sort of target that warranted intervention!

After traveling for some time, the convoy left the smooth main road and entered the forest.

The difficult terrain slowed the vehicles and made the ride extremely bumpy.

Seated in the front passenger seat, Anabel held the overhead grab handle with one hand and turned back toward Ethan. "Thanks to your discovery, another Team Rocket research facility has been exposed. Once we clear out this place, more than half of Team Rocket's power in Johto will have been eliminated. If we maintain this momentum, we'll eventually uproot them completely!"

Ethan remained skeptical of Anabel's confidence.

That might sound reasonable, but Ethan knew that no matter how many low-ranking members were arrested, Team Rocket would eventually return as long as its mid- and high-ranking officers remained free—especially while Giovanni avoided capture.

The series of blows would certainly have a major impact on Team Rocket. However, as long as its core members remained safe, the organization could withstand even tremendous losses.

Of course, the International Police currently had the wind at their backs. Ethan was not about to voice his concerns and dampen their morale.

The seven or eight police vehicles continued deeper into the forest until they reached the outskirts of Team Rocket's base and finally stopped.

Team Rocket had surveillance devices farther ahead, and patrols also watched the area. From this point onward, they would have to proceed cautiously.

To capture the entire group in one operation, Anabel split off two more teams and sent them around the left and right sides of the base, forming a pincer.

Anabel had great confidence in the operation.

The enemy was exposed while her own force remained hidden. With the right tactics, they could catch Team Rocket completely off guard!

The group took cover behind a boulder and looked ahead.

Team Rocket's secret base had been built inside a bare, low hill in the forest.

Every tree around the hill had been cut down. With no cover at all, approaching without being seen was impossible. The moment anyone stepped into the open, Team Rocket would certainly spot them.

Anabel was unconcerned. In fact, she had already prepared for a direct assault. Her force had reached the perimeter undetected, so even if Team Rocket noticed them now, the enemy would have precious little time to react.

Anabel turned to Gengar.

"How many people are inside the base? What's the layout?"

Although Looker had relayed the information the previous night, caution demanded that she confirm it once more before the operation.

Gengar grinned. "The inside of this hill has been almost completely hollowed out, and the base is hidden inside it. As for the number of people, there are more than a hundred. But don't worry. Most of them are researchers with practically no combat ability."

More than a hundred?

Anabel calculated rapidly. She had still brought too few people. Even if half of Team Rocket's personnel were researchers, securing everyone would remain difficult.

"What about the entrances and exits?"

"I've already scouted all of them. There are three in total." Gengar suddenly remembered something and added, "But there's also a helipad inside the base, with a helicopter parked on it."

Anabel considered this for a moment, then looked up at Ethan. "Mr. Ethan, we're a little short on anti-air capability. Could I ask you to handle that helicopter?"

"Leave it to me." Ethan took out a Poké Ball and released Charizard. "If any aircraft gets into the sky, I'll make sure it comes straight back down!"

Then Ethan pulled a map from inside his jacket and handed it to Anabel.

"I drew this map last night based on Gengar's description. It's somewhat crude, but I hope it helps."

Anabel thanked him, accepted the map, and studied it carefully. After a long while, she nodded.

"This is extremely useful. It will save us from taking several wrong turns. Still, I never imagined the base would be this large. There's no telling how long they've been operating here!"

More than ten minutes later, the teams assigned to surround the base reached their designated positions. Anabel decisively gave the order to attack.

"Move out!"

The officers charged forward. They had barely run a few steps when a Team Rocket patrol spotted them and triggered the alarm.

As the siren blared, Team Rocket's base instantly descended into chaos.

Professor Sebastian was conducting research in a laboratory. He looked up from his instruments and frowned at the commotion around him.

"What's going on?"

A researcher ran over. "Professor! We're under attack!"

"Under attack!? By whom!?"

"The International Police!"

"The International Police!? Damn it!" Professor Sebastian's expression changed. "How did they discover this place? And at such a critical moment!"

His heart sank. There was no time to worry about anything else. He hurriedly ordered, "Move all the research data you can. Destroy everything we can't take with us!"
