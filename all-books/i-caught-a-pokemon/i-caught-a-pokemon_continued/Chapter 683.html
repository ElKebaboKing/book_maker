Anabel stood on the deck of the maritime police ship, scanning the blue sea through binoculars. Before long, a shark-shaped submarine surfaced and sped toward her.

She lowered the binoculars and called to the crew behind her. “They're coming. Ready the crane!”

It was the submarine carrying Ethan, Misty, and Shelly. Ethan had persuaded Shelly to change course and meet Anabel by using a firmer approach, reminding her who held the advantage.

The submarine slowed and stopped alongside the police ship. The trained crew quickly secured it and began lifting it from the water.

As it rose, Ethan looked out of the window. Far away, Archie and Matt's two submarines had stopped, unwilling to approach.

Archie might have lost his temper, but he had no appetite for a direct fight with the maritime police. Compared with Team Rocket, Team Aqua still lacked nerve.

Escaping Archie did not end the matter. Ethan turned his gaze elsewhere. A larger danger still demanded attention.

He had prevented Archie from capturing Kyogre or causing its Primal Reversion. But the battle had angered and woken it. After leaving its cave, Kyogre had created a terrifying whirlpool in the surrounding sea.

The ocean was indifferent, and so was its embodiment. Perhaps Kyogre had not meant to target the intruders at all. Perhaps the disturbance was an accidental consequence of its passing, or perhaps it meant to teach the humans and their followers a lesson. Whatever the reason, the whirlpool had formed.

It was enormous. It encompassed the entire maze of islands and the waters around them. Sea rushed toward its center and dragged at everything nearby. The low parts of some islands were submerged, leaving weaker Water-type Pokémon helpless as the landscape changed around them.

Fortunately, Kyogre had not undergone Primal Reversion. Sea level had not risen violently as Shelly's simulations predicted. The consequences could have been far worse.

Clang. In little more than ten seconds, the submarine had been hoisted level with the deck. Ethan opened the hatch, climbed out first, and jumped onto the police ship.

Anabel was waiting for him. She froze at the sight of his Team Aqua uniform. “Why are you dressed like that?”

“It made the operation easier,” Ethan said. He turned and helped Misty onto the deck.

Shelly emerged last, moving slowly. Anabel recognized Team Aqua's second-in-command and immediately reached toward her belt.

“Easy.” Ethan raised a hand. “She's working with me now. She isn't an enemy.”

“Working with you?” Anabel looked as if he were joking. Shelly was one of Team Aqua's chief strategists, second only to Archie. How could Ethan have won her over?

Yet Shelly stood there beside him. Anabel could hardly deny what she saw. Had Ethan somehow started dismantling Team Aqua from within?

She was about to ask her officers to take Shelly aside when Ethan stepped in front of her. He had read her intention.

“Sorry. I still need her. I can't hand her over yet.”

He did not say it outright, but Anabel understood: he meant to protect Shelly.

His sudden firmness took her aback. She would have warned most people against speaking that way to the International Police. With Ethan, she had no wish to cause a rift. His strength mattered, but so did the friendly cooperation that had begun with Looker and continued over time.

“You've misunderstood,” she said with a light laugh. “We only need to ask a few questions. We should register her change of status too. Otherwise it may complicate matters for her when everything is reviewed later.”

Ethan nodded. Anabel had decided how to handle it, and he was glad she understood. “Thank you. But as I said, I can't hand her over until this is fully settled.”

“All right.” Anabel was not troubled. If Shelly really was cooperating, a short delay would change little.

Throughout their conversation, Shelly listened silently as though they were discussing someone else. Anyone from Team Aqua who knew her would have been astonished to see her so subdued.

She looked out at the raging whirlpool. Kyogre's power was astounding. It also brought back the memory of what had happened in the submarine minutes earlier.

After she had refused to change course or accept police protection, Ethan had shown her a side of himself she had not expected. He had asserted his will in a way she could not resist.

It was different from Archie's rough temper, which always seemed a step from violence. Ethan's manner remained outwardly courteous, much like her first impression of him. Yet his anger could be frightening too. In that moment she had understood that, in one respect, he could be more intimidating than Archie.

She was accustomed to power struggles within Team Aqua and respected strength. Her thoughts had gone blank, and she had followed Ethan's instruction. Thinking back, it felt as though he had taken control of her.

The police ship set off after Archie and Matt. Both submarines dived and soon vanished, leaving Archie at large once again.

Anabel returned with nothing to show for the chase. The whirlpool still churned ahead. Kyogre had gone, but its power continued to disturb the sea without any sign of stopping.

At least Ethan's earlier message had given Anabel time to warn other vessels in the area. Any losses should be limited.

While Anabel and her crew worked, Ethan, Misty, and Shelly stood at the rail, watching the whirlpool. After a long silence, Shelly glanced at Ethan.

“Aren't you going to do something? You have a Suicune. Surely it could calm a whirlpool like that.”

“Why do you think I should?” Ethan asked.

She looked at him in surprise. “Isn't it obvious? We—I've looked into your history. You've worked with these people since Kanto, then Johto and Hoenn. You've stopped one crisis after another. Isn't this what you do?”

“You've got me wrong.” Ethan smiled faintly. “Most of the time, I acted because someone asked me to. On the few other occasions, including this one, leaving things alone would have put all Hoenn at risk. Primal Kyogre would have been a catastrophe for its people and Pokémon, and I couldn't have escaped it either. Look at the situation now.”

Shelly turned back to the sea. The whirlpool was frightening, but unlike what she had modeled at the Weather Institute, its effects were limited to this stretch of water. At worst it might affect Sootopolis City to some extent. Human society had the means to handle that danger.

“I see. You aren't quite as overflowing with sympathy as I thought.”

“People who can't restrain their sympathy end up playing savior,” Ethan said, narrowing his eyes. “I've always considered that an insult. I might have a little sympathy, but that's all.”

...

After more than two hours, the whirlpool gradually subsided. Anabel's ship had patrolled its perimeter throughout, keeping watch and searching for distress calls. Fortunately, everyone remained safe.

Ethan said goodbye to Anabel. He, Misty, and Shelly boarded the submarine again.

He had gained no prize from the expedition, but stopping Archie's plan was success enough. He had also turned an enemy into someone who might stand with him.

Shelly had nowhere obvious to go after leaving Team Aqua. She had not declared a new allegiance, yet Ethan expected that she would seek him out eventually. She was used to a dangerous, hidden life and unlikely to settle easily into ordinary society. Unless she joined another organization, he might be her only remaining refuge.

Could she disappear under a new name and live quietly? That would require a complete change of character.

The submarine traveled unseen through deep water and reached Sootopolis City safely. Archie did not appear again. Ethan found that odd at first, then understood. Unable to recover the Blue Orb for now, Archie would be following Kyogre before it vanished from sight. Otherwise he might spend another decade finding it again.

It was a frustrating but sensible choice for him.

Back in Sootopolis, the submarine surfaced inside Team Aqua's secret warehouse. Ethan stepped onto solid ground and voiced his concern.

“Aren't you worried Archie could have people waiting here for us?”

“No. I built and maintain this warehouse myself. No one else knows about it, not even the other commanders. Archie doesn't know how many members Team Aqua has; he certainly doesn't know about this place.”

Ethan understood. It had been Shelly's private base all along. While she belonged to Team Aqua, it served Team Aqua. Now that she had left, only she knew where it was.

Shelly took out the Blue Orb and offered it to Ethan. It still shone brightly, its flat, diamond-like faces casting colored light around the warehouse.

Ethan glanced at it but did not reach for it. “Keep it.”

“What?” Shelly stared. “You're giving it to me? Aren't you taking it back to Mt. Pyre?”

“Archie stole it from Mt. Pyre once. He could do it again. Returning it there wouldn't solve anything.”

“Then why leave it with me? Aren't you afraid I'll disappear with it?”

“Where would you take it? Back to Archie?” Ethan gave a short laugh. “Besides, it's like a lamp with endless power that you can't switch off. If I carried it while traveling, I'd draw attention everywhere.”

“So you want me to keep it?”

“Yes.” He met her eyes. “You've shown me I can trust you with something important. Whatever else we disagree on, you can tell right from wrong when it matters. You led me to Archie. I think you're the right person to hold the orb.”

After a long silence, Shelly put it away, almost as if she were following an order. “What should I do with it?”

“Keep it safe for now.” Ethan sighed. “This isn't over. There's also the Red Orb, which can awaken another ancient Pokémon. If all else fails one day, we may need the Blue Orb to hold that Pokémon in check.”
