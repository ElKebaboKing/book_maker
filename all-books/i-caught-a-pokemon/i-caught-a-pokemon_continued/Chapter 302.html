A chilly mountain wind whistled past.

North of Blackthorn City, three Charizard flew in a single-file line, speeding over the mountains. When the clouds above them parted, sunlight fell across the faces of their riders.

It was Ethan's party, on its way from Blackthorn Gym. Lance led on his Charizard, Clair followed him, and Ethan and Sabrina rode together on Ethan's at the rear.

As Pokémon raised by Dragon Tamers, Lance's and Clair's Charizard were exceptionally strong. All three looked fierce as they flew together, an imposing sight against the sky.

Their destination was the Dragon's Den in these mountains. Beyond the Den lay the Dragon Tamers' sacred ground, the Dragon Holy Land.

While they flew, Lance explained what had happened. The Pokémon hunter wielding the Dark Balls was Vicious, the Iron Masked Marauder, one of Team Rocket's highest-ranking executives.

No one knew why this notorious hunter had set his sights on the Pokémon of the Dragon Holy Land. As soon as Lance received word, he had abandoned his other work and rushed back to Blackthorn to face the dangerous intruder with Clair.

A top executive. Vicious. A Pokémon hunter...

Ethan searched his memory. He remembered the man from the fourth Pokémon movie. Vicious had been its main villain and had dragged Ash into an incident spanning forty years.

As Ethan recalled, Vicious held a high position in Team Rocket, but his ambitions were too great for him to remain under Giovanni. Their ideals differed, and Vicious had long wanted to replace him, take command of Team Rocket, and conquer the world.

He had nearly every trait one could expect in a villain: cruelty, deceit, and total self-interest. He hurt people and Pokémon alike and seized anything he wanted. There was nothing redeeming about him.

But Ethan remembered that Vicious was supposed to be hunting Celebi right now. Why had he come here instead?

Clair glanced back at Ethan. Seeing that his Charizard had kept pace all this time, she gave him an approving look. "Not bad. You can keep up with us. You've raised your Charizard well!"

"Training is only part of it," Ethan replied without a trace of modesty. "My Pokémon has plenty of natural talent too!"

From their brief acquaintance, Ethan had found Clair outwardly tough but softer underneath. She acted decisively and spoke with confidence. Despite her commanding manner, she was easy enough to get along with.

After another ten minutes or so of flight, they landed beside a lake.

The lake was not large, but its current was swift. Ethan traced the water toward its source and found a dark opening in the mountainside beside it. River water poured steadily out of the cave.

From a distance, the rock around the entrance looked like a gaping mouth. Two fang-shaped stones hung above it, with two more rising below. Whether nature or human hands had shaped them, they gave the water cave an eerie appearance.

Charizard folded its wings, and Ethan jumped down. Almost as soon as he landed, a short, silver-haired old man stepped out from behind a rock and hurried over.

"Miss Clair! Master Lance! You're finally here!"

Lance went straight to the point. "Kabu, what's the situation? Where is Vicious?"

The old man had clearly been waiting for some time. He wiped sweat from his forehead. "I've been following him. I saw him enter the Dragon's Den with my own eyes!"

"He's already inside?"

"Damn it!" Clair turned toward the water cave and punched the air. "How did he get here so quickly? And how did he know this was the entrance?"

She noticed Kabu studying Ethan and Sabrina and briefly introduced them.

"This is Kabu. He's served our Dragon Tamer clan as a butler for decades, and he referees battles at Blackthorn Gym. Kabu, these two came to help us. Ethan is the Indigo League tournament champion, and Sabrina is the Leader of Saffron Gym."

Kabu greeted them at once. To Ethan, he looked at least seventy. He wore a well-fitted suit, and though his hair was silver, his face had few wrinkles. He stood straight and alert, with bright eyes.

"So you're Ethan Vale!" Kabu said, delighted. "I've heard about you and what you've done. With your help, we'll have a much better chance of catching that Pokémon hunter!"

By then Lance had recalled his Charizard. He took out another Poké Ball, cutting the conversation short.

"There's no time to waste. Let's enter the Dragon's Den. We have to stop Vicious before he reaches the Holy Land and enslaves the Pokémon there!"

He turned to Ethan. "The next stretch is by water. The currents inside are complicated, so be careful."

"I will."

Lance and Clair released Gyarados at the same time and leaped onto their backs.

They certainly lived up to their reputations as Dragon Tamers. First both had ridden Charizard; now both had brought Gyarados. Their Pokémon choices overlapped a great deal.

Then again, if it was a matter of having the same Pokémon...

Under their gaze, Ethan opened a Poké Ball and released his own Gyarados.

Splash!

Its huge body plunged into the lake, throwing up a spray of water. Gyarados roared toward the sky, then fixed its fierce eyes on the other two. Even against members of its own species, it radiated a more threatening presence than either of theirs. Both backed away a little under its domineering stare.

The difference was plain to see.

Sensing something unusual about this Gyarados, Clair looked at Ethan with surprise. "When Lance said you had a Dragon-type Pokémon, I didn't think much of it. I never expected you to have both a Charizard and a Gyarados. Anyone who saw us together without knowing you would think you came from the Dragon Tamer clan too!"

"What can I say?" Ethan spread his hands and smiled. "Charizard and Gyarados are two of my favorite Pokémon. How could I pass up either of them?"

The answer clearly pleased Clair. She nodded, then noticed the scars covering Gyarados's body. Large and small, they ran across almost every part of it.

People said scars were marks of honor. Clair needed no explanation to see that this Gyarados had survived extraordinary battles. That was how it had become so fearsome.
