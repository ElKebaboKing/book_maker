The seawater slowly receded.

Although it did not seem to have caused a disaster, it had still left its mark.

The ground floors of some buildings had flooded, soaking furniture and appliances in seawater. Many small boats had vanished too. No one knew whether they had drifted into the city or been carried out to sea as the water withdrew.

For the people who owned them, the losses were considerable. Fortunately, no one had been hurt in the tsunami. That was a stroke of luck amid the misfortune.

The commotion woke the city's residents. They switched on their lights and leaned out from their balconies to look around. Seawater had risen above the canals and spread half a meter deep into the city. They were baffled, but none of them knew what had happened.

Perhaps when day broke, they would hear on the news that a great tsunami had struck in the night, only to be stopped by something and dissipate.

On a rise above the city, Misty watched everything settle back into calm and let out a sigh. "It's finally over..."

"Wait!" Brock suddenly remembered something. "What happened to those two thieves?"

The sky was already beginning to lighten. While there were still few people about, Ethan had Lorenzo and Bianca take Latias and Latios, who were too weak to change their appearances, back to the Secret Garden. He and the others hurried straight to the cathedral.

But Annie and Oakley were long gone. All that remained were fossils scattered across the floor and the damaged machine.

As for the corrupted Soul Dew, it had shattered into fragments strewn everywhere.

"Damn it!" Ash said angrily at the sight. "They caused all of this, and in the end they just got away!"

"It is a shame," Ethan agreed. "But we couldn't help it. All our attention was on the tsunami. One mistake could have been disastrous. How could we have spared anyone to guard them? I believe we'll meet them again someday, though. When we do, we'll bring them to justice."

Brock hesitated. "Earlier... I thought the woman called Oakley had suffered a backlash from the Soul Dew too. She looked awful. It seemed like she was seriously hurt."

"Hmph." Misty raised her head with a snort. "They brought it on themselves. Serves them right!"

"All right." Seeing that they were about to say more, Ethan cut them off. "We can't stay here. Let's go before someone sees all this and decides we're the ones responsible. We'd never be able to explain ourselves."

They left the cathedral and returned to the Secret Garden.

When Bianca saw them come back, she prepared hot tea and brought over towels and some pastries.

"Come and dry yourselves off. After everything you've been through, you must be hungry and tired."

She was right. Latias had woken them in the middle of the night, and they had not had so much as a sip of water since. If they had not eaten a large meal before bed, they would have been ravenous by now.

With the tension finally gone, everyone's eyelids grew heavy and their energy drained away.

After a little care, Latias and Latios had recovered some strength. They were no longer in danger. The two Pokémon lay close together, dozing as they drifted off to sleep.

The sight filled Ethan with relief.

Compared with the ending of the movie, he had saved Latios's life and spared Latias the pain of losing her family.

After eating a little, the group stayed in the garden rather than returning to the Pokémon Center. They lay down fully dressed in one of its wooden cottages. Bianca found several blankets for them, warm and snug, and Ethan lost consciousness almost the moment he lay down.

He slept soundly.

When Ethan woke, everyone else was already up and out of the room. He rubbed his groggy head and stepped outside. Ash and Pikachu were playing with Latias and Latios.

"You're awake?"

Erika, some distance away, was the first to notice him.

She was brushing his Jolteon's fur. His Gengar had shamelessly attached itself to her as well, pestering her to scratch it.

It looked as though his Pokémon had already taken Erika for the lady of the house. They showed no reserve around her at all.

Looking around, he saw Misty and Ash helping Bianca prepare food. The mood was as relaxed and cheerful as ever, just as it had been when they first came to the Secret Garden.

Ethan nodded to Erika, then walked to the pool where the Soul Dew had once rested. He turned to Lorenzo. "What will you do now that the Soul Dew is gone?"

Lorenzo shook his head. "Even without the Soul Dew, having Latias and Latios here is the best outcome we could have hoped for. I'm sure they'll continue to protect this place, and I'll make sure the legend of the Soul Dew lives on!"

Speaking of Latias and Latios...

Ethan took out their Poké Balls and held them out to Lorenzo.

"These are their Poké Balls. I'd like you to keep them here."

"Oh?" Lorenzo did not take them at once. He looked at Ethan curiously. "You aren't taking them with you?"

"Taking them?" Ethan shook his head. "They belong here, and they like it here. Where would I take them? Besides, I already have plenty of Legendary Pokémon at my side. Let them go on living here without a care."

Lorenzo remembered Suicune leaping past them earlier. He knew Ethan was not boasting. With a Legendary Pokémon like that beside him, Ethan had less reason than most people to covet others.

But another question still troubled him. "Why give me their Poké Balls instead of releasing them completely?"

"It's a precaution," Ethan said with a small smile. "I expect more people like those two thieves will set their sights on them someday. Even ordinary people who happen upon them might try to catch them. Since they're already registered to these Poké Balls, at least no one with bad intentions can capture them in another."

"I see!" Lorenzo said as understanding dawned. "Because of how Poké Balls work, no one else can catch them with one now. And even if someone gets hold of them, we can call them back into these balls!"

Ethan smiled and nodded. That was exactly what he meant.

Latias and Latios seemed to sense something. They left Ash and Pikachu, flew over, and saw the two Poké Balls.

Under their gaze, Lorenzo accepted the balls. With solemn care, he placed them where the Soul Dew had once been.

"Then this is where they'll stay..." He looked at the two Poké Balls side by side and spoke from the heart. "From now on, they'll take the Soul Dew's place in guarding this Secret Garden!"
