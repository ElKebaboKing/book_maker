After a night's rest, the third morning of the tournament arrived in the blink of an eye.

Today's waiting room was no longer crowded like it had been during the previous two days. With most of the competitors eliminated, the entire room felt empty. Aside from Ethan's group, everyone who remained seemed preoccupied. Even their voices had unconsciously grown much quieter.

Advancing this far brought both pressure and anticipation for the future.

They had already reached the final sixteen. With just a little more effort, any one of them might become the Sea Hero and obtain both fame and fortune. How could they avoid feeling excited and anxious?

All the remaining competitors had one thing in common: ever since learning that Ethan was the Indigo League Conference champion, they kept their distance. No one attempted to speak with him. Instead, they merely sent cold looks his way from time to time!

Their hostility was almost visible. They had clearly identified Ethan as their greatest rival!

Ethan did not care in the slightest and continued laughing and chatting with Ash and Misty.

Ash and Misty's good mood disappeared the instant the pairings were announced.

When the Whirl Cup staff posted the bracket on the wall, the three went to look and discovered that Ash and Misty would face each other in the very next match!

Their expressions immediately froze!

What terrible luck.

If given a choice, both would have preferred to battle their friend after achieving a better result, rather than eliminating one another as early as the round of sixteen.

It could only be called a trick of fate, one that had played an enormous joke on them.

Their feelings were complicated. This meant one of them was certain to be eliminated.

Statistically, however, there was no avoiding it. Even if Ash and Misty had not been paired together, either one still had a chance of facing Ethan. A meeting between them was almost inevitable.

Ethan stepped in to smooth things over.

Fortunately, after a brief moment of shock, the two quickly adjusted and became filled with fighting spirit.

Once they returned to normal, Ethan turned his attention to his own opponent. During the break, he looked up recordings of the Trainer's previous two matches online and gained a general understanding of what to expect.

His opponent had used two different Pokémon in those battles, but neither was particularly strong. This was the sort of competitor who had advanced only with great difficulty.

Even so, Ethan's personality would never allow him to grow careless.

Ash and Misty's battle was the second match of the morning. Once the two Trainers in the opening match determined a winner, their turn soon arrived.

After wishing them both luck, Ethan sat on a sofa in the waiting room and watched the live broadcast on the large screen.

The rules changed for the round of sixteen. Each Trainer could now use two Pokémon. This not only provided room to recover from a mistake, but also allowed the Trainers to display their abilities more fully.

Amid the crowd's cheers, the match officially began.

The atmosphere inside the colosseum made it obvious that the spectators were even more enthusiastic today.

As the field continued to shrink, the remaining competitors naturally became more skilled, making the battles increasingly exciting and professional. That was why the audience was so supportive.

Drawing back from his thoughts, Ethan focused on the battlefield again.

Although neither wanted to face a friend, Ash and Misty became completely serious once the match began. Neither showed the slightest intention of holding back.

When the time came to strike, they would show no mercy!

For the first battle, Ash sent out Totodile, while Misty chose her Poliwhirl.

Both Pokémon had defeated their opponents through unexpected accidents in yesterday's opening matches. Their condition had improved greatly in their second matches, where they eliminated their opponents through genuine strength.

Sending them out again today showed that both Trainers had considerable confidence in them!

The first battle lived up to their expectations. The two Pokémon fought fiercely and injured each other as the struggle continued. After a long exchange, Poliwhirl finally dove underwater, approached Totodile, and used Double Slap to knock it unconscious, putting Misty at match point.

It was not that Totodile had performed poorly. Poliwhirl had simply fought even better!

Now behind in the match, Ash confidently sent out Kingler.

Ethan remembered this Pokémon vividly. It had performed brilliantly during the Indigo League Conference, single-handedly sweeping three opponents and helping Ash win several matches in a row!

Misty also knew Kingler could not be underestimated, but she did not change Pokémon. Instead, she kept Poliwhirl in to face it.

When the battle began, Kingler immediately struck the surface with Crabhammer. Incredibly, the attack churned the water across the entire battlefield and created four enormous whirlpools!

The four vortices violently stirred the surface. Caught by the current, Poliwhirl could only drift helplessly across the water at high speed!

Seeing that single attack, Ethan had only one thought: what ridiculous strength!

Creating whirlpools that powerful with one blow defied all reason!

Kingler rarely traveled with Ash. He remembered it only when entering tournaments, while it normally remained with Professor Oak. For it to possess such strength despite that arrangement meant it had grown exceptionally well!

Ethan remembered that back on Earth, faced with all the unreasonable things Kingler could do, many viewers had jokingly credited Professor Oak. They claimed the professor had secretly given Ash's Pokémon private training and raised Kingler and the others to such an extraordinary level.

Of course, anyone who thought about it knew that was only a joke.

As a highly respected Pokémon Professor, Professor Oak had countless matters to handle every day. How could he possibly have time to train Pokémon?

On the battlefield, Kingler easily defeated Poliwhirl with that technique, tying the score at one to one.

For the third battle, Misty took out a Poké Ball and intended to use Corsola. Somehow, Psyduck once again—and again, and yet again—came out on its own instead!

No one knew how many times it had ignored Misty's instructions and decided to battle without permission!

The funniest part was that the moment Psyduck appeared, it immediately performed a special trick for everyone!

After falling into the water, it flailed as though it were drowning and even swallowed several mouthfuls of seawater!

The sight delighted the audience. Who could have imagined that a Water-type Pokémon would not know how to swim?!

Fortunately, after thrashing around for quite some time, Psyduck finally managed to climb onto a stone pillar. At least it had avoided drowning itself.

Most crucially, the broadcast director chose that exact moment to switch the camera to Misty.

Every spectator received a clear view of her face, flushed bright red with embarrassment because of Psyduck's behavior!
