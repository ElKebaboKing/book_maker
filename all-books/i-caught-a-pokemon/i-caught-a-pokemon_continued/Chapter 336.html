Foster's suggestion matched what Ethan already had in mind, so he nodded. Foster led him to a storeroom, where Ethan collected a shovel, brushes, and a complete set of excavation tools before leaving the research center again.

This was the benefit of knowing someone. The Ruins of Alph park offered visitors a chance to experience excavation for themselves. Although the equipment looked crude, borrowing it normally required a deposit. With a wave of Foster's hand, however, every fee was waived.

The deposit would have been a trivial amount of money, hardly worth mentioning, but special treatment still felt pleasant.

Foster had other matters to handle. Once they stepped outside, he excused himself and left on his own.

Ethan and Sabrina watched him go, then returned to the valley. Looking more carefully this time, they saw mounds of freshly excavated earth and broken stone waiting beside the dirt road, along with rows of temporary scaffolding.

A large group of archaeologists worked among them, completely absorbed in their digging.

Ethan glanced over and saw a complete Aerodactyl fossil embedded in the rock. Workers were slowly cleaning it with brushes. The lines of its skeleton were clear and intact, showing how perfectly it had been preserved.

The sight filled Ethan with anticipation for his own excavation.

Maybe I'll dig up something valuable too!

They passed the workers and found a quiet corner with no one nearby.

"This spot will do."

As Ethan prepared to swing the shovel, Sabrina stared at him in surprise. "You're really going to excavate?"

"Of course!" Ethan laughed. "Travel isn't only about battling. We need to relax now and then too. I'll treat it as a new experience. What about you? Want to join me?"

Sabrina had no interest in such work and no desire to cover herself in dirt. She sat on a nearby rock, crossed her legs, propped up her chin, and quietly watched him.

Ethan selected what he considered the perfect location, gripped the shovel in both hands, and drove it into the ground.

Heave-ho! Heave-ho!

Dust filled the air, and freshly dug soil piled higher beside him.

At first, he worked enthusiastically. After hacking at the ground for several minutes, however, his patience began to fade.

He looked at the modest hole he had dug and wiped the sweat from his forehead.

His idea had sounded wonderful. Reality was far crueler.

Archaeology clearly demanded physical strength, patience, persistence, and a calm temperament. Ethan had begun on a whim and was plainly incapable of repeating the same tedious motion one shovel after another.

Worse, he had found nothing at all. His interest drained away by the moment.

His eyes shifted. He cleared his throat and called out Gengar.

"What do you want?" Gengar rose from his shadow and gave him a sidelong look.

Ethan gestured toward the ground with his chin. "Sink underground and see whether there are any fossils nearby. That way I won't waste my strength like I just did."

"Why me?" Gengar yawned, thoroughly unwilling. "Why don't you ask Crystal Steelix to do something this troublesome?"

Ethan shook his head. "It would attract too much attention. Besides, its body is enormous. If it burrowed around, it could easily damage the geological layers. You're the best choice. Enough complaining. Get down there."

Left with no alternative, Gengar sank into the earth. Seven or eight minutes passed before it emerged again.

"Too bad. You picked a terrible spot. There aren't any fossils below us at all." Gengar's voice carried a trace of delight at Ethan's misfortune. "Even the nearest half-decent stone is more than ten meters down."

Another ten meters?

Ethan raised an eyebrow. Whatever patience remained vanished completely, and he tossed the shovel aside.

"Forget it. Let's go look at the ruins instead. Archaeology really isn't for me!"

Amusement showed at the corners of Sabrina's eyes, but she said nothing. She stood and accompanied Ethan toward the ruins on the other side of the valley.

They were called ruins, but in practice they consisted of more than a dozen stone chambers clustered together like houses.

Each chamber had been built from heavy stone slabs and looked extremely primitive. Wind and rain had worn them badly, making it impossible to tell how long they had stood there.

They truly bear the marks of time...

Because the chambers were protected historical artifacts, visitors were not allowed inside. Ethan stopped at the barrier and watched for a while. He was about to leave when he spotted Foster again. After Ethan greeted him, Foster arranged for the two of them to enter.

Foster smiled. "How was the excavation? Did you find anything?"

"No. Nothing but dirt and ordinary rocks. I didn't even see the shadow of a fossil." Ethan sighed in disappointment and returned the tools. Then he looked up and studied the structures again. "Are these the Ruins of Alph?"

"That's right." Foster nodded. "These are the true Ruins of Alph, the only perfectly preserved complex of ancient buildings in Johto!"

As Foster spoke, Ethan looked around. Many researchers besides Foster were searching through the area as though seeking something. Yet the stone chambers had neither doors nor windows, and their interiors were empty. Nothing seemed especially noteworthy.

"What are you looking for?"

"We don't actually know. We simply feel that these ruins aren't as simple as they appear, so we decided to search them thoroughly again."

A thought suddenly occurred to Foster. "Mr. Vale, could I ask you for a favor?"

"A favor?" Ethan raised an eyebrow. "Have you run into a difficult problem?"

"Nothing like that." Foster gave a helpless smile. "Recently, we've occasionally seen mysterious Pokémon moving through the ruins. But whenever we try to find them, we can't locate a single trace."

"I thought that perhaps we researchers simply don't understand Pokémon behavior well enough to discover them. Since you're a Trainer, I'd like you to see whether you can uncover any clues."

Mysterious Pokémon?

From Foster's description, Ethan suspected he meant the Unown hidden within the ruins.

"Of course I'll help." Ethan agreed without hesitation. "But I'm only an ordinary person. Please don't expect too much from me..."
