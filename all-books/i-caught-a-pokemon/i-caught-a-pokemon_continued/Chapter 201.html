After leaving Navel Island, Ethan and Sabrina continued toward the next island on Charizard's back.

Sabrina sat behind Ethan with her hands around his waist, chatting with him about this and that. All at once, she sensed something below.

"Look down there."

Ethan glanced toward the sea. An ordinary speedboat was heading west.

"What about that boat?"

"I can sense two familiar presences. I think there are people we've met aboard it."

"Really?" Ethan raised an eyebrow and patted Charizard's neck. "Take us down for a look!"

As Charizard descended, Ethan made out several figures moving about on the boat. A closer look revealed Ash and Misty.

So they had reached the Orange Islands too. Ethan had spent a long time on Sunburst Island. Even if Ash had relaxed in Pallet Town for a few days, he would have had time to start challenging the Orange League by now.

Ash, Misty, and Pikachu were enjoying the view from the stern. A woman in orange clothes and a hat steered the boat, while a man in a green T-shirt leaned near the bow, drawing in a white sketchbook.

Charizard's shadow swept over them. They looked up and spotted Ethan.

"Ethan!"

Misty, who had been looking listless, sprang to life. She hurried to the railing and waved both arms at him.

Ethan smiled and had Charizard fly alongside the boat. When they drew level with the bow, he swung himself off Charizard's back and jumped onto the deck.

"Misty, Ash! You've made it to the Orange Islands too. I can't believe we ran into each other out on the sea, with so many islands around us."

"It must be fate!" Misty said. Holding Togepi, she beamed at Ethan and looked him over with bright eyes.

They had kept in touch through a messaging app since parting at the Indigo League, but she had not seen him in person for a long time. He was as handsome as ever, and she had missed him.

Sabrina used her psychic power to float down to the speedboat. Ash stared when he saw her.

"Miss Sabrina? What are you doing here?"

She did not seem inclined to answer, so Ethan spoke for her. "She's taken a break from her Gym duties. We're traveling together now."

Ash and Misty exchanged a look. They had never expected someone as hard to approach as Sabrina to travel with Ethan. Then again, she had gone out of her way to watch his matches at Indigo Stadium. The two were clearly close.

Misty felt a pang of envy. She wanted to travel at Ethan's side too, but she and Brock were Ash's companions. Until they officially parted ways, she could not simply abandon that idiot.

The man in green came over from the bow to greet the newcomers. He was about Ethan's age, with hair parted down the middle and a pale orange headband across his forehead. He looked gentle and easygoing.

Ethan recognized him at once from memories of the anime, though they had never met. So it is him.

Seeing how familiar the others were with Ethan, the man looked between him and Sabrina. "Who are these two?" he asked Misty.

"I'll introduce you!" Misty said eagerly. "This is Ethan, one of our friends and Ash's biggest rival. And this is Miss Sabrina, the Saffron Gym Leader from Kanto. She specializes in Psychic-type Pokémon!"

"Nice to meet you." The young man politely held out his hand. "I'm Tracey Sketchit, a Pokémon Watcher. I met Ash and Misty recently, and I'm traveling with them now."

Ethan took the introduction in stride, but Sabrina gave Tracey a second look. A Pokémon Watcher? That was an unusual occupation.

"I'm Ethan Vale," Ethan said. "I'm a Trainer, like Ash."

Tracey paused at the name and frowned. "Ethan Vale... Why does that sound so familiar?"

Misty straightened proudly. "Of course you've heard of him! Ethan won this year's Indigo League Conference. Everyone in Kanto knows his name!"

"Oh! Now I remember!" Tracey exclaimed. "I read about you. You're the Champion who set a record by winning every match!"

"That's me." Ethan waved off the praise with a smile. "Misty's making too much of it. The tournament gave me a moment in the spotlight, that's all."

Tracey liked him immediately. Ethan had achieved so much, yet spoke without a trace of arrogance. That impressed him.

Ethan turned his attention to the woman at the controls. Misty said, "That's Miss Maren. We met her on the last island. Since we're headed in the same direction, she kindly offered us a ride."

Maren could not leave the helm. She gave Ethan and Sabrina a polite nod. "Hi, you two."

"Hello," Ethan replied. He looked around, then asked a question whose answer he already knew. "Where's Brock? Isn't he traveling with you?"

"Brock..." Misty sighed. "He stayed at Professor Ivy's laboratory when we reached Valencia Island."

"For Brock to choose to stay behind, Professor Ivy must be beautiful," Ethan joked with a laugh. "Where are you heading now?"

Before Misty could answer, Ash jumped in excitedly. "We're going to the next island to challenge the second Gym! Ethan, how many badges have you got?"

"Only two so far. I'm on my way to Trovita Island to challenge the third Gym."

"Trovita Island?" Maren thought for a moment. "That's more or less the same direction we're going. If you're Ash's friends, why don't you come along? I can take you part of the way."

"That would be great." Ethan sat down without hesitation. "We're tired of flying anyway. Now we can relax, feel the sea breeze, and travel by boat for a while."
