The Goldenrod Department Store stood in the southern part of the city. Its plain name might have suggested an old building selling secondhand goods and trinkets, but it was actually Goldenrod's largest shopping center for Pokémon products—and the largest shopping center of any kind in the city!

It had six floors above ground and two below, eight in all. The spacious, bright interior was lavishly decorated, and its merchandise appealed to more than just people shopping for Pokémon supplies.

Ethan and Erika entered and found the place crowded. Trainers were everywhere, but so were fashionably dressed men and women.

Clearly, countless people came to see it every day, just as they had!

The first floor sold cosmetics and jewelry, useful to Trainers whose travels exposed them to wind and sun. Ethan went around it with Erika while she bought a bottle of moisturizing lotion and several face masks.

After so many long days on the road, setting out at dawn and returning after dark, Erika had had little time to care for her skin. Her face felt dry, so the products would come in handy.

The second floor's theme was "Travel Companions." It carried all sorts of things Trainers needed on the road. Ethan restocked supplies there and bought more Poké Balls and medicine for his Pokémon.

The third floor was devoted to "Everything for Victory," selling items for Pokémon battles. Ethan had never been very interested in such things, so he and Erika went straight up to the fourth floor.

The fourth and fifth floors each had their own Pokémon-related themes. Ethan did not spend much time browsing, but he did buy a Metal Coat.

He knew the item well. A Metal Coat had helped his Crystal Onix evolve into Crystal Steelix. Now he intended to use one to evolve Scyther.

By coincidence, the Metal Coat was known to help only Onix and Scyther evolve, and he happened to own both species!

It might as well have been made for him.

After more than an hour of shopping, they reached the sixth floor. Its theme was "When You're Tired of Shopping"—a place to rest. It was the busiest, liveliest floor of all. Following an online recommendation, Ethan and Erika ate dinner at a popular restaurant there.

Soft, lovely music filled the restaurant. The setting and mood were just right. They sat facing each other, relaxed, looking very much like a young couple on a date!

Anyone watching their gestures, expressions, and the way they looked at each other would have taken them for a real couple.

They laughed and talked through dinner.

There was still time afterward, so they watched a recently popular Pokémon movie. By the time it ended, night had fallen. They walked shoulder to shoulder back to the guesthouse they had booked earlier.

After checking in at the front desk, Ethan walked Erika to her room. As he was about to turn away, she looked back at him with bright, smiling eyes.

"Thank you for coming with me. I had a wonderful time today!"

Meeting her gaze, Ethan answered before he could think better of it. "If you like it, I'll keep going shopping with you whenever you want!"

As soon as he said it, he felt something was off. Why did that sound so much like a confession?

Erika seemed to think the same thing. She stared at him for a moment, then gave him a radiant smile. Without a word, she went into her room.

That smile made Ethan's heart race.

He drew a deep breath, tried to steady himself, and returned to his own room.

He fell onto the bed and stared at the ceiling for a long time, but his restless feelings would not settle.

Sigh. So much for a purely platonic friendship between a man and a woman...

The longer he traveled with Erika, the deeper their bond grew. It was hard not to want more. Ever since what had happened with Maya, Ethan felt as though an inner restraint had fallen away, and he wanted Erika more than ever.

Thinking back, perhaps agreeing to travel with her had trapped him in a situation of his own making.

The harder he tried to calm down, the more his thoughts wandered. At last he took a cold shower. Only then did he regain his composure.

To keep from dwelling on it, he took a Poké Ball into the courtyard, released his Scyther, and brought out the Metal Coat he had just bought.

He looked his Pokémon over and smiled. "Scyther, let's evolve!"

Scyther looked at the item in his hand and seemed to understand. It nodded firmly.

Once the leader of its group, it was a proud fighter. It had no objection to evolving.

Gengar rose out of Ethan's shadow to watch, interested in the spectacle.

Ethan stepped forward and pressed the Metal Coat against Scyther's forehead. The instant it touched, brilliant white light burst from its body!

Erika noticed the light in the courtyard and opened her window. Fresh from her bath and dressed in a robe, she smiled at Ethan, then turned her attention to Scyther.

She had seen him buy the Metal Coat at the department store and knew what he planned. Its evolution was no surprise to her.

Ethan looked away from Erika and back to his Pokémon.

He had seen the light of evolution many times. He took several steps back, and just as he stopped, the glow vanished.

A cool-looking Pokémon covered in red stood before him. Scyther had evolved into Scizor!

Scyther had two possible evolutionary paths. Using a Metal Coat was one common route.

Scizor was a humanoid Pokémon clad in crimson armor. Two pairs of translucent wings rose from its back, though they were mostly decorative.

The longer Ethan looked at his newly evolved Scizor, the more he liked it. At last he took out his Pokédex and pointed it at the Pokémon.

"Beep. Scizor, the Pincer Pokémon. Bug and Steel type. Scizor's body is as hard as steel, reportedly stronger than even the finest tempered metal. Ordinary attacks cannot harm it. To keep its metal body from overheating and melting during battle, it beats its wings rapidly to regulate its temperature. Despite its slender build, its attacks are astonishingly powerful. Its steel-bearing pincers can crush even the hardest things they catch!"
