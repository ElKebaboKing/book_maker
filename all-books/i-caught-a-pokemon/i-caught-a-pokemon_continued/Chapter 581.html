They hardly needed to ask what the wild Pokémon had gathered around.

Celebi might be hiding inside that hollow!

Ethan and the others exchanged glances, then hurried toward the two fallen trunks. The wild Pokémon did not try to stop them. They simply turned and watched with curious eyes.

At the base of the lower trunk, the path ended. Fortunately, a vine hung down from above. Ash jumped up, caught it, and began climbing without a second thought.

There was only one vine, and it swung under his weight. Everyone else had to wait for him to reach the top before using it to climb onto the trunk.

Watching him scramble halfway up in no time, Misty laughed. “He’s fast. Even a Mankey would have to admit defeat.”

The others laughed too. With climbing skills that nimble, Ash could put even a primate Pokémon raised in the forest to shame.

“Of course it would,” Ethan teased. “You think a human-shaped Pokémon is a joke? Put Ash on a battlefield, and I bet an ordinary Pokémon couldn’t beat him!”

Once Ash reached the top, Sammy, Misty, and Brock climbed after him.

Ethan chose an easier way. He brought out Dragonite, who carried him and Erika up to the trunk.

When Ethan jumped down from Dragonite’s back, the surface felt slick beneath his feet.

The trunk was broad, but moss covered it. They had to watch every step.

The Pokémon outside the hollow moved aside as they approached. Ethan looked in and saw a small, light green Pokémon lying weakly inside. It had a round head.

“That’s Celebi?” Ash and Misty exclaimed together. Realizing how loud they were, they quickly lowered their voices.

Sammy nodded. He knelt on the trunk and held out his hand. “Come out...”

But he had underestimated how wary Celebi was. It had brought him through time in a moment of danger, probably without meaning to. The two did not actually know each other.

Despite Sammy’s warm smile and gentle voice, Celebi shrank back from his approaching hand. A violent shock wave burst from its body and swept outward.

Everyone nearby was thrown away. Ash and Sammy, who were closest, took the full force and flew several meters.

The others were hit less severely. Erika and Brock were standing to one side and felt the least of it.

Misty was light enough to be lifted off her feet. Fortunately, Ethan stood behind her. She fell into his arms, and they staggered back together until Dragonite’s soft belly stopped them.

Dragonite tilted its head, bent down, and helped its Trainer and Misty stand straight.

Ash landed at the edge of the trunk and almost fell. He grabbed the hanging vine just in time.

He clambered back up, using both hands and feet, and returned to the hollow. “That was dangerous! We’re not bad people!”

Perhaps his tone sounded threatening. Celebi used its power again. Branches and leaves beside the hollow grew wildly and lashed at Ash, whipping him away from the opening.

“Both of you, calm down.” Ethan sighed at their recklessness. “Celebi is weak, helpless, and frightened of humans right now. Rushing at it won’t work.”

“Oh. I see...”

Ash realized he had been too hasty. Smiling now, he approached Celebi again.

“Sorry I scared you. We’re good people. We came to help!”

No matter how nicely he spoke, the branches kept swatting at his face.

“Let me try,” Erika said.

She came to the hollow. Perhaps it was the natural air about her, or perhaps her beautiful face, but Celebi seemed to relax a little.

The branches slowed. Erika reached in carefully and lifted Celebi into her hands. They stopped moving altogether.

Ethan could only stare.

If he had not known Celebi had no gender, he might have thought the little creature was attracted to pretty women. Why else had it relaxed the moment Erika took Ash’s place?

Was that simply her advantage?

As Erika brought Celebi out, they finally saw it clearly.

Its body was green, pale above and grass green below. Its large, rounded head gave it the look of a doll. A pair of wings rose from its back, and green antennae tipped in blue grew from its forehead. It was adorable.

The others immediately crowded around, taken with it at once. Celebi blinked its big eyes and looked from one person to another.

But it was injured. Once it had seen that these humans meant it no harm, it could hold out no longer. Its eyes closed, and it fell into a deep sleep.

In truth, this was a rare chance to catch it.

The rarer a Pokémon was, the harder it was to find. A Mythical Pokémon like Celebi was rarer still. In its present condition, a single thrown Poké Ball might be enough.

But Ethan could not do that.

Sammy would surely stop him, and Ash and Brock would probably object as well. More than that, Ethan himself did not want to take advantage of an injured Pokémon.

He would wait until this affair was over and look for a chance to catch Celebi on his own.

For now, they needed to treat its injuries. They had medicine with them, but it would only be enough for first aid. After discussing it with everyone, Sammy decided they should take Celebi back to Arborville for proper care.

Ethan thought of flying Erika and Celebi there on Dragonite. Sammy pointed out that, if someone was hunting Celebi, the sky might be no safer. It would be slower to travel together, but they would be stronger as a group. A Pokémon hunter might think twice before attacking them all.

Ethan was about to promise that he could drive off any hunter who appeared.

Then another thought stopped him. If he separated from Sammy and the others, the next part of the movie’s story might not happen as it should. Until the events played out and the danger was resolved, Sammy could not return to his own time. And Ethan would have no chance to catch Celebi.

He let the matter drop. Together with Ash and the others, he climbed down from the trunk and started back toward Arborville.

Everyone except Ethan was anxious. They hoped the return journey would pass without trouble.

But events had a way of going wrong at the very moment one most feared them.

Halfway back, as they reached a stream, several smoke bombs burst at their feet.

Bang! Bang!

Thick smoke billowed up, swallowing the whole group in an instant.

“Cough, cough...”

Ash stumbled out of the cloud with tears in his eyes. “Who’s there?” he shouted.

As if his question were a cue, two familiar voices answered from ahead.

“Since you asked us so sincerely!”

“We’ll tell you out of the kindness of our hearts!”

“To protect the world from devastation!”

“To unite all peoples within our nation!”

“To denounce the evils of truth and love!”

“To extend our reach to the stars above!”

“The cute and charming villains!”

“I’m Jessie!”

“And I’m James!”

“We are Team Rocket, blasting through the galaxy!”

“A white hole! A white future is waiting for us!”

“That’s right, meow!”

Only the endlessly persistent Team Rocket trio could deliver those lines with such ease and confidence.

Jessie, James, Meowth, and Wobbuffet stood on a tree not far ahead, looking down at them.

It had been a long time since Ethan last heard their entrance speech. More precisely, it had been a long time since they dared show themselves in front of him. On previous occasions, they usually hid until he had gone before emerging again. They certainly had not tried to trouble him.

They still followed Ash almost everywhere. But for them to appear so openly before Ethan today meant something was wrong.

“Team Rocket!” Misty called, frowning. “Leave us alone. We’re busy!”

“Ahahaha!” Jessie covered her mouth with one finger and laughed theatrically. “Enough talk. Hand over the Celebi you’re carrying!”

So Celebi was their target. The incident had only just happened. They could not have learned of it this quickly unless someone were directing them.

The smoke had almost cleared. While everyone watched the trio, two long mechanical claws shot out with a whir and lunged at Erika.

More precisely, they reached for the Celebi in her arms.

No one else had time to react. Ethan alone had remained on guard. The instant he saw the claws, he pulled Erika into his arms.

Ash and the others heard the rush of air behind them and spun around in shock.

“What?”

The claws missed. Their owner finally showed himself.

A huge black robot, shaped like a pillar, stepped slowly out from behind a giant tree. In its cockpit sat a man in a yellow hood and a red vest.

Ethan recognized the outfit immediately. It was Vicious, the Iron Masked Marauder, a Pokémon hunter and high-ranking Team Rocket executive.

What rotten luck. Ethan had known Vicious was central to Celebi’s story, but after their clash in Blackthorn City’s Dragon Holy Land, he had not expected the man to evade Clair’s pursuit and turn up here.

“You dodged that too?” Vicious said. “Impressive.”

His gaze remained fixed on Ethan. He had been here for some time. Spotting Ethan from a distance, he had recognized a formidable opponent and held back instead of revealing himself.

Ordinarily, he would have slipped away unnoticed. But Celebi was the Pokémon he had long dreamed of capturing. If he let this chance pass, who knew when he would encounter it again? He could not bring himself to leave.

While he searched for a way to break the deadlock, he happened upon the trio lurking nearby. After a short exchange, he learned they were Team Rocket members too, and devised a simple plan.

It was clear now that their diversion had failed.

“You’re here for Celebi, aren’t you, Vicious?” Ethan asked, though he knew the answer. “You got lucky and escaped last time. I didn’t expect you to come to me yourself. You won’t be so fortunate today.”

Ash frowned at the sharp exchange. “Ethan, you know him?”

Ethan nodded. “He’s a senior Team Rocket executive and a Pokémon hunter. He broke into the Dragon Holy Land near Blackthorn City to capture the Dragon-type Pokémon there. Lance, Clair, and I stopped him.”

His outfit alone had made his Team Rocket allegiance obvious. After hearing Ethan’s account, Ash and the others grew even more wary.

Brock turned to Sammy. “Was he the hunter you met before?”

Sammy shook his head. “No. That was another hunter.”

The hunters were different, but they both wanted Celebi. Ash and his friends would not let this one have it.

Jessie, James, and Meowth jumped down from the tree. With Vicious on the opposite side, Ethan’s group was caught between them.

The trio had badly misread the situation. They thought that joining forces with a highly regarded Team Rocket executive would be enough to beat Ethan and his friends.

Still, it looked like a chance to turn their fortunes around. If they won, they could settle old scores and seize the Mythical Pokémon Celebi. Their standing in Team Rocket would change beyond recognition.

And if they lost?

They would be sent flying, just as they had hundreds of times before. They were used to that.

They were still afraid of Ethan, but taking this gamble might bring a tremendous reward, and failure would cost them little more than usual. Why not try?
