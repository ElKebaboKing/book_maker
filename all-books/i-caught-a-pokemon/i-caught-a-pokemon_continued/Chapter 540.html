After a generous lunch prepared by Brock and Bianca and another night's rest, they had fully recovered from the previous day's exhaustion.

What a remarkable visit to Altomare it had been. They had entered a water race and won the championship, toured the beautiful city, and met Latias, Latios, Lorenzo, and Bianca. They had thwarted the schemes of two thieves said to be the best in the world and saved the city from a massive tsunami.

Any one of those events would have kept them busy under ordinary circumstances. Yet all of them had happened within twenty-four hours!

Now that everything had settled, it was time to leave the city and continue their journey.

They told Lorenzo and Bianca they were going. Lorenzo took the news calmly, but Bianca's face showed how reluctant she was to see them leave.

They had known one another only a short time, but everything they had been through had forged a strong friendship. Of course she wished they could stay longer.

She also knew they were Trainers with places to go. They could not remain in one city for long.

"I have something for you."

With that, Bianca dashed away. A dozen or so seconds later, she returned carrying a sheet of drawing paper.

"I drew this while you were sleeping. Thank you for saving my grandfather and me, and for saving Latias and Latios. Thank you for saving the city too. I don't have anything valuable enough to show how much that means to me, so I made you this picture."

"A picture?"

Ash was about to reach for it, but Ethan took it first.

The others crowded around to look. It was a crayon drawing of all seven of them, standing side by side with the water city of Altomare behind them. Latias and Latios flew above their heads, while Pikachu perched on Ash's shoulder. Bright colors filled the page, and everyone wore a broad smile. The picture was full of life. Bianca was clearly a skilled artist, and she had put her heart into it.

"It's beautiful!" Misty said. "Thank you, Bianca."

"Oh, it's nothing..." Bianca blushed. "Compared with what you've done, this hardly counts. I just hope you like it."

"Don't say that," Ethan said with a smile. "What could matter more in a gift than the thought behind it? We'll treasure your picture."

He had said we, but he ignored the look on Ash's face that said he wanted it too. Ethan tucked the drawing inside his coat.

It was a precious memory, and there was only one copy. How could he possibly hand it over to that kid? When he had the chance, he would get it framed and hang it in his room.

Hearing that Ethan and the others were leaving, Latias and Latios came over as well. Latias in particular hated saying goodbye. She rubbed her cheek affectionately against Ash and Pikachu, then circled the rest of the group a few times.

Latios was steadier than his sister. He simply watched them with gratitude before turning his gaze to Ethan.

He was not oblivious to what had happened. Last night—no, in the early hours of this morning—Ethan had taken him and his sister into Poké Balls. From that moment, they had ceased to be wild Pokémon. Their lives might look much the same as before, but they were bound to those balls, and Ethan could return them to them whenever he wished.

Did that mean he and his sister were now Ethan's Pokémon? Still, Latios knew it had been the only way to save them, and the man seemed decent. He did not particularly mind.

Leaving the Secret Garden, the group went back to the Pokémon Center for their luggage and then headed to the harbor.

They would take a ship back to the Johto mainland and resume their travels.

Lorenzo and Bianca saw them all the way onto the ship. When the passenger ferry pulled away, the two waved goodbye. As it sailed farther and farther out, their figures finally disappeared from view.

Goodbye, Altomare. Goodbye, Bianca.

A sea breeze brushed across the group.

Standing on deck, they watched the water city shrink in the distance. They could not help feeling sad to leave. Who would not want a little more time with friends, if they had the chance?

But that was life. After saying goodbye to yesterday, they still had an unknown tomorrow ahead of them.

"Look!"

Misty suddenly pointed toward the sky. The others looked up and caught glimpses of two shapes among the clouds, one blue and one red. They immediately realized that Latios and Latias had come to see them off.

"Goodbye!"

Ash waved at the clouds and shouted. The other passengers looked skyward at his strange behavior. A few gasped as something seemed to flash past.

"Huh?" A man in a flowered shirt, with a gold chain around his neck, rubbed his eyes. "I think I just saw Latias and Latios!"

"What are you talking about?" His girlfriend rolled her eyes. "Latias and Latios are only legends. This is a busy city. Why would they be here?"

"I did see them! I'm telling you!" The man pursed his lips in annoyance and pointed upward. "Look for yourself... Huh? Where did they go?"

The woman looked up. The sky was just the sky; there was no Pokémon in sight. She shook her head helplessly. "You had too many cocktails. You must have imagined it. Why would there be Legendary Pokémon here?"

Ethan and the others exchanged knowing smiles as they listened.

Like the statues on Altomare's shore, the legend of Latias, Latios, and the Soul Dew would live on in the city, giving its people something to talk about for years to come...

...

The ferry did not retrace the route they had taken to Altomare. It sailed straight to a small harbor outside Cherrygrove City.

It was a slow ship. After a night at sea, Ethan and the others finally set foot on the Johto mainland the next morning.

They parted ways at the harbor. Ash, Misty, and Brock were heading off to earn Ash's final badge, while Ethan and Erika would make for Silver Town.

Knowing they would meet again soon, they kept their goodbyes casual and simply waved.

"Then I'll see you at the Silver Conference!" Ethan said with a smile.

"That's only a few days away." Ash rubbed his nose, unconcerned. "As soon as I've challenged Blackthorn Gym, I'll come find you!"

"Clair won't be easy to beat. Don't get so confident that you slip up," Ethan said with a chuckle. "It's settled, then. I'll be waiting for you in Silver Town!"
