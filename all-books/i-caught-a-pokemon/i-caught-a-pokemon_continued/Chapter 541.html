Cherrygrove City was a small seaside town on the southern coast of Johto. It had no Gym, and it was not even as well known as Azalea Town.

The scent of flowers and the sea breeze were its defining features. Ethan had passed through on his way from New Bark Town before, but he had not spent much time looking around, and the place had left little impression on him. Erika, on the other hand, seemed perfectly suited to it.

She liked the city as soon as she set foot in it.

From the harbor, Ash and his friends headed north. Ethan and Erika needed to go east, then turn north at New Bark Town toward Mt. Silver and Silver Town.

After parting from the others, they did not linger in the little city. They headed straight out.

Erika had some luck. Shortly after leaving Cherrygrove, she came across another Grass-type Pokémon on a plain and caught it.

Her trip through Johto had been rewarding. She had set out hoping to catch Pokémon, and by now she had caught five or six, more or less meeting her original goal.

There was still some time before the Silver Conference, so they were in no hurry. When they reached New Bark Town that evening, they visited Professor Elm. At his warm invitation, they stayed the night at his home.

There Ethan met the professor's wife and son. The boy looked so much like his father that Ethan would have recognized the relationship without an introduction.

Ethan and Erika gave the family the gifts they had brought for the visit, earning an even warmer welcome.

At dinner, Ethan and Professor Elm shared a few drinks. Perhaps Kurt was not there to keep filling his glass, or perhaps his wife was watching him. Either way, the professor did not get thoroughly drunk again as he had at Kurt's house.

At bedtime, however, Ethan and Erika found themselves in an awkward situation they had faced more than once before.

Maybe Professor Elm's wife had noticed how they looked at each other and how close they stood. Or perhaps Elm had seen them stay under the same roof at Kurt's house and told her about it. Whatever the reason, she had put Ethan and Erika in the same room.

Once inside, they exchanged wry smiles. They had lost count of how often this had happened.

They were used to it by now and made no attempt to explain themselves to Professor Elm. This time, though, their hearts beat a little faster. The room held only one double bed, and there was only one blanket.

"Well..." Ethan looked at Erika. "Should I sleep on the floor tonight?"

Erika's ears reddened slightly. "It's all right. We can share the bed. We don't have to stand on ceremony when we're traveling."

At that, Ethan did not insist. He had only offered as a way of edging toward what he wanted anyway. With a soft bed right there, why would he sleep on the hard floor?

Erika bathed first. When Ethan had finished washing too, she was already lying in bed.

The next morning, Ethan came to the dining room yawning. The moment he sat down at the table, he found Professor Elm smiling at him.

With his wife cooking in the kitchen, Elm cleared his throat and spoke quietly. "You're young and full of energy, but you should pace yourself. Some things shouldn't keep you up so late!"

Erika had just entered the dining room and heard him. She stopped in her tracks, her face turning scarlet. Ethan gave an embarrassed smile. He knew exactly what Elm meant.

Spurred on by the drinks last night, he had finally made love to Erika. She was a little fuller figured than Sabrina and Maya, and he had been so taken with her that they had gone at it several times. Before he knew it, they had stayed up well past midnight.

They must have disturbed Professor Elm's sleep.

Erika sat beside Ethan, keeping her head down. She had just stripped the disheveled, bloodstained sheet from the bed and tucked it into her bag. If she left it here for the professor and his wife to find, she would never be able to face them again.

Fortunately, Elm's wife brought in the food, giving Erika a reprieve from her embarrassment.

After breakfast, they formally took their leave of the couple.

"I hope you win the championship!" Professor Elm said with a smile. "I'm sure you can do it. If I can find the time, I'll come and watch your matches in person!"

"Thank you for your good wishes," Ethan said. He and Erika stood outside and bowed slightly to the couple seeing them off. "And thank you for having us. We'll be on our way now!"

"Have a safe trip!"

They waved goodbye, then headed north from Professor Elm's house and left New Bark Town.

After that night, both their feelings and their relationship had changed completely.
