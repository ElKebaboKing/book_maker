Zuki and Kuni returned to the dining room, each lost in her own troubled thoughts.

Though they had argued in Zuki's room, they had not been away long. No one at the table suspected anything. The others assumed the sisters had met outside and come back together.

Zuki placed the bottle with the marked cork in front of Ethan. She set the other bottles before Miki, Kuni, and herself.

Her expression did not change, and her hands never faltered. She did it all as naturally as she might have poured tea. She had clearly made up her mind.

Kuni watched in alarm, even more nervous than Zuki seemed to be.

It was too late for regrets. All she could do was hope that Zuki got what she wanted. If she did end up with Ethan, it might benefit their whole family. His looks and strength aside, his achievements would raise the sisters' standing.

Yet Kuni knew what Zuki was doing was wrong. It was unfair to Ethan and to Erika. Still, she had been unable to stop her sister. For now, she could only watch and hope nothing went awry.

She also braced herself to clean up any mess. Whatever happened, Zuki was her sister. Even if Miki and Ethan condemned them afterward, Kuni would stand beside her.

No one could see her thoughts.

Ethan thanked Zuki as he accepted the sake. If she was making an effort to reconcile, he ought to respond kindly.

But she went back to her seat without a word or a change of expression, leaving him puzzled. She really did seem unlike herself today. Then again, she was often distant. He let it go and uncorked the bottle.

At once, the mild fragrance of sake mingled with a strange, pleasant scent that drifted through the dining room.

“That smells wonderful,” Ethan said. For some reason, his heart began to race as soon as he breathed it in.

Everyone else smelled it too. “It does,” Miki agreed, then glanced at Zuki in confusion. Do we have sake this fragrant in the house?

The bottle looked ordinary, yet its scent was remarkable. Kuni glanced at Zuki as well.

Did she add too much of that liquid? Will someone notice?

Zuki did not meet her eyes. It was her first time adding it to a drink, and she had no way to judge the dose. She had used her best guess and added a little extra to make sure it worked. Now she too worried Ethan might grow suspicious.

Fortunately, no one thought the sake had been tampered with. Its fragrance had caught everyone's attention. Even Naoko and Sayo, who never drank, sniffed the air and looked over curiously.

“It smells lovely,” Sayo said, her eyes bright. “Miki, could we try a little?”

“No!” Kuni answered before Miki could. “You're not old enough to drink.”

Her sharp tone startled the two younger sisters. Kuni realized she had overreacted. Ordinarily, a little sake might have been all right. Tonight, the bottle in front of Ethan made it dangerous. If anyone mixed up the bottles or drank from his by mistake, there would be trouble.

“Why not?” Miki said. “We're celebrating our guests' visit. The girls are growing up, and sooner or later they'll attend gatherings where people drink. A small taste won't hurt.”

Kuni reconsidered. If she kept objecting, she might draw attention to herself.

“All right. One cup each, no more. I'll pour it.”

She took the bottle before her, removed its cork, and poured a cup for Naoko and one for Sayo.

For a moment she thought the fragrance had grown stronger when she opened it, as though it hovered right before her face. She dismissed the feeling. The dosed bottle was still in front of Ethan. Surely the smell had simply spread through the room.

By now she could no longer tell where the scent came from. It seemed to fill the very air.

Is it really that potent?

Even without drinking from the marked bottle, Kuni's mouth felt dry and her heart beat faster. She glanced around. Her sisters all seemed at least a little flushed.

We can't stay in a room full of this smell. Dinner needs to end soon.

Naoko and Sayo knew nothing of her worries. Delighted, they accepted their cups and set them carefully in front of themselves.

They had secretly tasted alcohol a few times before, but even the mildest drinks had seemed sharp. This sake smelled far more inviting.

May saw them receive a cup and looked hopefully at Ethan. She did not ask, but her meaning was clear.

Ethan shook his head. “You're the youngest here. Stick with juice. You can try sake in a few years.”

May knew he meant well, and she was too young. She was not greatly disappointed, though the scent did make her curious.

Miki and Zuki uncorked their own bottles. The mixture of sake and the sweet scent grew stronger still. No one could have said which bottle it came from.

Faces warmed across the room. Hearts quickened. Even their thoughts seemed less firmly under their control.

That dulled attention, combined with the sake Miki, Kuni, and Zuki had already drunk, kept them from noticing what they normally might have. Their preoccupations did not help.

Zuki watched; Kuni held her breath. The group raised their cups together. Naoko and Sayo each took a cautious sip. Ethan, Miki, Kuni, and Zuki drained theirs.

The cups were small, holding only a large mouthful. Ethan could ordinarily manage a dozen or so without difficulty. Yet after this one, the sake tasted strange.

It was less like ordinary sake than a sweet flower wine steeped with rose petals. It was pleasant enough, even delicious.

The others did not notice as much—at least not yet.

In her room, Zuki had shaken the marked bottle after adding the aphrodisiac, mixing it thoroughly through the sake and releasing its fragrance. Gengar had skipped that step with the other bottles. Much of their added liquid had settled near the bottom.

For now, the sake Miki and the others poured contained little of it. The effect would grow stronger as they emptied their bottles.

They drank a few more cups. Ethan was visibly losing his composure. His gaze had grown unfocused, his cheeks were red, and he breathed heavily. He looked drunk.

Miki had two rosy patches on her cheeks as well, though no one noticed. She laughed at him. “Are you already tipsy, Mr. Vale? Your tolerance isn't very good. We haven't had many cups at all.”

She poured herself another drink and swallowed it. Her condition was becoming more like Ethan's. The same was true of Kuni and Zuki.

All three sisters were accustomed to entertaining guests and could usually drink far more than Ethan. But the lower they got in their bottles, the more their thoughts slowed. The sake tasted odd, yet they could no longer examine why.

Zuki and Kuni had kept their eyes on Ethan. Both saw that something was wrong with him, and both knew he was not merely drunk. The aphrodisiac must be working.

“I'll take him to his room,” Zuki said.

She poured the last of her bottle into her cup. Perhaps to steady her nerves and keep herself from backing out, she swallowed all of it, then rose to help Ethan.

Everyone looked at her. Kuni was the most anxious of them all. The crucial moment had arrived, and she watched Zuki without blinking, afraid something would go wrong.

Miki was the eldest and normally the most attentive. She ought to have realized how strange it was for Zuki to volunteer to escort Ethan. But the drug was beginning to cloud her mind too. Though less affected than Ethan, she felt too hazy to think it through.

Naoko and Sayo exchanged a look. Things were unfolding in a way neither had expected. Still, their older sisters said nothing, so they stayed quiet.

May did not know Zuki well enough to find the offer odd. She was amused instead by Ethan's apparently poor tolerance. He could not even keep up with Miki and her sisters. He might as well have drunk juice with May.

Under their eyes, Zuki reached Ethan and helped him rise from the tatami.

It was the first time the two had been so close. The aphrodisiac had left him confused and aroused, but it had not taken away his ability to move. As soon as she supported him, he stood.

Zuki looked at his face and quickly turned her eyes away. Even without the drug, her own heart was pounding. She had made her decision, but of course she was nervous. She had never been this close to a man.

She felt his rapid heartbeat and his breathing. She saw the growing heat and fascination in his gaze. It embarrassed her.

Men had looked at her that way during performances before, and those looks had disgusted her. From Ethan, it felt different. That was the chief reason she had gone to such lengths, even chosen such a scheme, to claim him.

Some things have to be won for yourself.

Supporting Ethan, Zuki led him out of the dining room. They disappeared into the night.

With two people gone, the table fell quieter. Dinner was drawing to a close.

Kuni drank a few more cups with Miki. Several minutes later, May had finished eating, and Naoko and Sayo had emptied their cups. At last the meal ended.

Kuni's face was red too. Suppressing the restless feeling in her chest, she turned to the younger sisters. “You two should share a room with Miss May tonight.”

All three girls were pleased. They had become good friends through their afternoon's practice and had plenty more to talk about. A shared room would give them time together.

“We'll go ahead, then,” Sayo said.

Naoko and Sayo, both flushed, left with May.

Once the three were gone, Kuni hesitated. She could not stop worrying about Zuki and Ethan. “Miki, I'll leave the rest to you. I'm going to see how Zuki is doing.”

Miki swallowed the last of her sake and waved her on.

Kuni rose, but heat rushed into her face. Her heart was thudding, and she could not keep her balance.

Strange. Am I drunk too?

She did not dwell on it. She steadied herself against the doorframe, then stumbled out along the covered walkway toward Zuki's room.
