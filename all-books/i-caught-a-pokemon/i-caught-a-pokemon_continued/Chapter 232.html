A herd of Pinsir was fighting a group of Heracross for territory. Hard shells slammed together, pincers met horns, and more than a hundred Pokémon were caught in the fierce struggle.

Above a lake, a large, menacing swarm of Beedrill swept past. Ethan could only wonder whom they were going after.

The deeper he and Sabrina walked into the forest, the more obvious its dangers became. Even the air smelled different, thick with rotting wood and a mixture of fungi. They kept quiet and stepped lightly, doing their best not to attract the attention of the fiercely territorial Bug Pokémon.

They followed their instincts along winding paths, moving steadily farther in. After half a day, though, their hopes of finding a rare species had come to nothing. They had seen only common Pokémon, none that Ethan wanted to catch.

"Let's go," he said with a sigh. It felt as though they had wasted their time. He was about to call Charizard when Sabrina suddenly looked deeper into the trees.

"What is it?"

Despite the layers of forest between them and whatever lay ahead, Sabrina had sensed something. "There's a battle going on. Both Pokémon are stronger than the ones we've seen so far. One of them might be what you're looking for."

She expected Ethan to head off at once. When he did not move, she turned and found him staring intently at her neck. She touched it, but felt nothing unusual.

"What are you looking at?"

"Ahem. Nothing." Ethan guiltily looked away.

Only now had he noticed the marks he had left on the pale side of her neck. Several reddish bruises were visible there, enough to suggest what they had been doing the night before.

He had missed them because Sabrina usually wore her hair loose. It covered the marks until she moved. No wonder people in town had given them knowing looks and meaningful smiles earlier. Sabrina had been puzzled then, but Ethan understood now.

He absolutely could not let her find out. If he did, she might keep him out of her bed for days.

Rather than explain why he had been staring, he hurried in the direction Sabrina had indicated. She frowned at his strange behavior but followed him.

They went around several thick trees and walked another few dozen meters. The forest opened into a large clearing. Dozens of Scyther stood around a small pond, watching something in silence.

Ethan and Sabrina exchanged a glance and moved closer. Through the gaps in the crowd, they saw two Scyther fighting a duel. Wings beating, blade-like forearms clashing, they attacked with no sign of holding back. This was no ordinary game. Both looked intent on winning at any cost.

The other Scyther watched with solemn expressions, as though observing a ritual.

The scene reminded Ethan of nature documentaries from his old world, with their harsh contests in which the strongest survived. A winner would lead the group onward through the forest. A loser might have to drag its wounded body away alone and wait for death. Perhaps these fierce Scyther lived by the same rule here.

"Were those the two strong Pokémon you sensed?"

"Yes," Sabrina said softly. "They're both strong, but the one with the scar on its forehead is clearly stronger."

Ethan looked again. One of the Scyther was more muscular, with an X-shaped scar across its forehead. Apart from that, the two looked much alike. But the scarred one held a decisive advantage, pressing its opponent back with a relentless series of attacks.

"It is strong," Ethan agreed. "Unless something changes, the other one is going to lose. Its reactions, speed, and strength are all far behind."

Sure enough, the other Scyther soon fell. Wounds covered its body, and it could no longer move.

The scarred Scyther looked down at its rival with a proud glint in its eyes. It raised its scythe-like forearms in triumph. The others burst into celebration. They showed no sympathy for the loser, only delight in their new leader.

Survival of the fittest, Ethan thought.

After a while, the fallen Scyther regained a little strength. It struggled upright and gave the victor and its companions one lingering, resentful look. Then it staggered away without turning back.

As the two Scyther drew farther apart, Sabrina asked, "Which one do you want? The injured one can hardly resist now. You could probably catch it with a single Poké Ball. The winner still looks full of energy. Catching it would take more effort."

"If I'm catching one, it has to be the stronger one. But..." Ethan hesitated.

His collection of Pokémon kept growing, and he was already struggling to give them all enough attention. After considering it, he made up his mind. Endless second-guessing was not like him.

Besides, Scyther was a Bug- and Flying-type Pokémon, a combination he did not yet have. Catching it would give his team more options. Its evolved form, Scizor, looked impressive too, and it could later Mega Evolve into an even stronger Pokémon.

His hesitation gone, Ethan strode toward the Scyther with the scar on its forehead.
