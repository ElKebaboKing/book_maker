Centered on the manor, the blue-violet wave instantly crystallized everything within three hundred meters. The newly formed crystal tower continued changing until it gradually assumed the strange shape of an enormous flower.

Two pointed projections resembling stamens curved toward the sky, giving the structure an eerie appearance.

Faced with such a mysterious and unknown event, the town police sent officers to investigate while setting up a perimeter to keep the residents outside.

Unfortunately, visibility was poor at night. The officers lacked specialized equipment and did not dare venture too far inside. Even after studying the area for a long time, they learned nothing.

The wave continued spreading and gradually crystallized the police tape as well.

After several attempts to establish new perimeters failed, the officers had no choice but to use loudspeakers and repeatedly urge the watching townspeople farther back, keeping them away from the strange energy.

Fortunately, perhaps because its power was weakening, the wave advanced more and more slowly. More than ten minutes had passed since Ethan and Erika approached, yet it had spread less than a meter.

Moreover...

Ethan turned and looked at the town behind him.

The manor stood far away. If the wave continued at this speed, it would not reach the edge of town even by tomorrow.

This was undoubtedly good news for the residents. They relaxed somewhat, knowing they had plenty of time to move their possessions before the phenomenon threatened their homes.

Ethan and Erika retreated with the crowd. With police officers handling the situation, there was no need for them to intervene.

Ethan checked the time again and said, "It's too dark, and there's nothing we can do to help. We should go to the Pokémon Center on the other side of town, rest, and find something to eat."

Erika nodded.

After witnessing something like this, they could not pretend nothing had happened and quietly leave. Erika was a Gym Leader. Even outside Kanto, helping preserve public safety remained part of her responsibility. She had to learn the cause of the incident.

She also understood that the situation might settle by morning. But if it grew worse, her current exhaustion would leave her unable to resolve anything.

The immediate priority was to rest properly and recover as much strength as possible so they would be ready for whatever happened next.

"I understand. Let's go."

They turned and crossed to the other side of town. Outside the Pokémon Center, they found Nurse Joy staring toward the manor as well.

After they explained why they had come, Nurse Joy led them inside, assigned them two dorm rooms, and served a delicious dinner.

Ethan wanted to ask Nurse Joy what had happened, but she shook her head. She had no idea what had caused the events outside.

It seemed they could only wait until morning.

After dinner, Ethan and Erika began yawning one after another.

It was already past nine. The day's exhaustion left them with no energy for conversation. After exchanging good nights, they went directly to their separate rooms.

Ethan removed his clothes, washed away the day's fatigue, and lay on the bed without wanting to move another muscle.

His eyelids grew heavier. In his dazed state, he did not know when sleep finally took him. When he awoke the next morning and stretched, he still felt somewhat drowsy, but his physical exhaustion had completely disappeared.

Excellent. Back to full strength!

After getting up and washing, Ethan remembered the previous night's events and went outside for a look.

The crystal tower had not disappeared. The wave continued to spread, advancing more than two hundred meters during the night without any sign of stopping.

Fortunately, it remained some distance from the town, leaving the residents safe for now.

In the clear daylight, Ethan could see the phenomenon much better.

Even from a distance, he could faintly make out plants and stones sealed within the crystal like insects trapped in amber.

No trace of the manor remained at the center. In its place stood something resembling an enormous alien forest, radiating strange light and filled with bizarre shapes. Against the beautiful, harmonious grassland around it, the difference was jarring.

It looked like an ecosystem from another planet.

After watching for a while, Ethan turned and studied the Pokémon Center behind him.

They had arrived in darkness and had not seen its appearance clearly. Now he noticed that unlike Pokémon Centers in ordinary cities, this one resembled a holiday resort built in a European country-house style.

He returned inside and met Erika in the dining room.

Although it was breakfast time, no other Trainers were present.

When they asked Nurse Joy about it, she explained that despite Greenfield's reputation as a tourist destination and its ranking as the place girls most wanted to visit, most travelers who came here were ordinary people. Those who stayed overnight generally chose the town's hotels instead.

At their prompting, Nurse Joy also shared some information about the manor.

"This town is called Greenfield," she said. "That manor is Professor Spencer Hale's home. I heard he recently traveled elsewhere for research and suffered some kind of accident. Only his assistant returned!"

"Professor Spencer Hale?"

Neither Ethan nor Erika seemed to recognize the name, so Nurse Joy explained, "Professor Hale is highly respected in the research community for his intelligence and kindness. But... because his work keeps him so busy, he often leaves Molly, his daughter, home alone."

As a woman, Erika immediately seized on the key detail.

"Alone? What about her mother?"

Nurse Joy shook her head regretfully. "Her mother was a researcher too, but she disappeared a long time ago. Now only the butler remains at the manor to care for Molly each day."

What an unfortunate family...

Ethan sighed.

He had encountered far too many families like this in news reports, television shows, and films. Every one of them was filled with misfortune.

A sudden commotion rose outside. The jumble of voices sounded as though many people had gathered beyond the doors.

Curious, Ethan, Erika, and Nurse Joy went out to look. Several police cars stood outside the Pokémon Center, where Officer Jenny spoke with the town police and several unfamiliar men.

Even Officer Jenny had come.

Whatever happened the previous night was clearly severe. Ordinary officers could no longer handle the situation, or Officer Jenny would not have made a special trip here.
