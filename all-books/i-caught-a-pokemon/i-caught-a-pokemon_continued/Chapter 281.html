According to the map, New Bark Town lay east of Cherrygrove City. It was not very far in a straight line, but a large forest stood between them. The dense growth and the many wild Pokémon living there made the road difficult to travel.

For new Trainers, though, the forest was a paradise. After receiving their first Pokémon, they could test their skills by catching weaker wild Pokémon there and building up their teams.

Ethan and Sabrina left Cherrygrove and followed a country path eastward.

What kind of assignment had brought Looker all the way to New Bark Town? It was only a little town on the outskirts. Apart from Professor Elm's laboratory, there was nothing there that Team Rocket would want to target.

Had someone raided Professor Elm's lab?

As Ethan wondered about that, he and Sabrina enjoyed the scenery and spotted many wild Pokémon along the way. Johto was so close to Kanto that Pokémon moved freely between the two regions. Pidgey and Raticate still poked their heads out of the woods here. There were also plenty of Johto Pokémon: Sentret, Hoothoot, Ledyba, and Spinarak.

After they had walked for some time and entered the area around New Bark Town, Ethan noticed a girl at the edge of the forest. She was trying to catch a Sentret.

As they approached, he saw that she was pretty. She wore a yellow-and-black striped jacket, a white baseball cap, and shorts that reached high on her thighs, leaving her pale legs bare. Her hair was tied into two long, low ponytails. She held a baseball bat and was shouting with all her might. His first impression was that she must be an ardent fan of some baseball team.

A Chikorita stood in front of her, pawing at the ground with its forefeet. It seemed ready to charge and deliver a final blow to the Sentret.

Chikorita's body was pale green, with a dark green leaf on its head and a ring of buds around its neck. Its head was large in proportion to the rest of its body, and it had red eyes, four short legs, a single nailed toe on each foot, and a small tail.

It was the first Chikorita Ethan had seen in person. Curious, he took out his Pokédex and pointed it toward the Pokémon.

"Beep! Chikorita, the Leaf Pokémon. A Grass type. Gentle by nature, Chikorita loves basking in sunlight. It uses the leaf on its head to sense the temperature and humidity around it and find a warm place. Though it may wave its leaf to threaten an opponent, the sweet scent it releases calms them both down."

As one of Johto's three starter Pokémon, Chikorita was certainly popular. Ethan, however, had little interest in raising one.

The Sentret had already taken several hits and was running low on strength. It was searching for a way to escape when it caught sight of Ethan and Sabrina. Its eyes brightened, and it ran straight toward them without hesitation.

"Chikorita, don't let it get away! Use Tackle!" the girl shouted.

Chikorita prepared to charge, but Sentret had already darted behind Ethan and Sabrina. The little Pokémon hesitated and stopped.

That moment was enough. Using the two travelers for cover, Sentret plunged into the forest beside them and vanished.

"Ah! My second Pokémon!" The girl dropped to the ground like a deflated balloon, watching her nearly captured Sentret get away.

Ethan glanced back toward the place it had disappeared. What a clever Sentret. It had even known how to use them as a shield.

He shook his head and started to leave. He had taken only a few steps when the girl shouted behind him, "Hey! You two, don't go!"

She had scrambled back to her feet and came trotting over. Ethan turned to face her.

"What is it?"

"You have the nerve to ask?" she said angrily. "If you hadn't suddenly shown up, that Sentret never would have escaped!"

Her voice was clear, with a strong Kansai accent.

"That's my fault?" Ethan shrugged. "You're the one who couldn't catch it. If you'd knocked it out sooner, it wouldn't have had a chance to run away."

"I don't care! You got in my way!" She waved her baseball bat around, fierce in a way that was more cute than threatening.

Sabrina frowned at the girl's unreasonable behavior and was about to teach her a lesson. Ethan hurriedly caught her hand before the young Trainer could get hurt.

After a second look, he had recognized the girl from her clothes. She had appeared several times in the animated series, and he remembered her fairly well. That was one reason he was not angry. He was a grown man; there was no point in quarreling with a little girl.

He considered her for a moment, then smiled. "What would you like us to do about it?"

His smile made the girl's cheeks turn red instead.

Wow, he's handsome. And he looks even better when he smiles!

She had not meant any harm. She was frustrated that a Pokémon she had almost caught had escaped, and seeing Ethan simply walk away had annoyed her. She had rushed over without thinking. Now that he was speaking to her so politely, she felt rather embarrassed.

Seeing her stumble over her words, Ethan said, "You look like a new Trainer. Maybe that Sentret just wasn't meant to be yours. If you still want it, you could try your luck in the forest and see whether you find it again. Or you could look for a different Pokémon to catch."

"I... I'm sorry. I got carried away."

She lowered her head and fiddled with her baseball bat as though she intended to examine every inch of it.

"It's all right." Ethan waved it off. When she remained silent, he took that as the end of it. "Good luck with your catching. See you!"

He and Sabrina turned and walked away without looking back.

"Oh? G-goodbye..."

The girl answered automatically. She watched Ethan go until he disappeared. Only then did it occur to her what she had missed, and she smacked herself on the head.

"You big idiot!"

At her feet, Chikorita looked up in curiosity, its little head full of confusion. Before it could work out what its Trainer meant, the girl sighed again.

"I didn't even ask his name. Oh well. We'll probably meet again. I'll ask him next time!"
