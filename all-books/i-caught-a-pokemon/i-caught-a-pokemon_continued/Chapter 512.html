No one could say who attacked first. In a single instant, Ethan's Pokémon and those of Apollo and Athena moved together, charging at one another.

During the standoff, they had already picked out their opponents.

Gengar took on both Hypno and Wobbuffet, the two Psychic-type Pokémon, by itself.

Crystal Steelix, a Ground- and Steel-type, used its enormous body to stop Muk and Arbok.

Ethan's Tyranitar sought out Apollo's Tyranitar for a one-on-one fight. Jolteon set its sights on the fleet-footed Persian.

Scizor rushed at Lickitung and Vileplume. Dragonite faced the two Dark-type Pokémon, Houndoom and Sneasel, alone, while Gardevoir held back Golbat and Kabutops with her psychic power.

Ethan had not specifically arranged these matchups. In their daily training, he had taught his Pokémon to exploit type advantages and create favorable openings in a chaotic battle. When this situation arose, they naturally made those choices themselves.

Of course, these were only the matchups at the start. Once the two sides clashed and the field grew confused, Pokémon would lose track of their opponents. When that happened, Ethan's team would need to work together until the battle steadied enough for each to find its target again.

They had practiced this often. Though outnumbered, they fought in an orderly way.

Apollo and Athena could see that Ethan's Pokémon were not only individually powerful but also well trained. That made them difficult to deal with.

No wonder he was their organization's foremost enemy!

Fortunately for them, the first change in the battle came from the Tyranitar.

Ethan's Tyranitar had evolved far more recently than Apollo's and had much less experience. Their clash shook the ground, keeping every other Pokémon well clear. Before long, Ethan's began to lose and was defeated.

It fell backward and struggled to rise, but it had exhausted its strength and could not stand.

That one defeat tipped what had been a stalemate toward the two Team Rocket executives.

With no opponent to hold it back, Apollo's Tyranitar entered the other fights and quickly disrupted Ethan's formation.

Ethan's remaining Pokémon still worked to defeat their opponents, but Team Rocket's Pokémon were strong in their own right. They would not fall easily. Even when outmatched, they kept moving rather than meeting Ethan's Pokémon head-on. Seeing their comrade win gave them a further burst of confidence.

The two executives could not hide their delight.

"It looks like we'll be the ones winning today," Athena said with a sneer.

"I wouldn't be so sure." Ethan shook his head. "And I'd advise you not to sound so certain. It makes the reversal that much more embarrassing."

He recalled his defeated Tyranitar, then took the two Great Balls from his belt pouch.

Apollo had been watching his every move. Ethan showed no sign of worry despite his disadvantage. Seeing him calmly bring out two more Great Balls made Apollo's eyes narrow.

"Didn't you say you left the three Legendary birds somewhere else?"

"I did." Ethan tossed the two balls lightly in his hands and smiled. "I wasn't lying. I left them elsewhere. But that doesn't mean I have no other trump cards!"

He hurled both Great Balls. Blue light flashed, and two four-legged Pokémon appeared before Apollo and Athena.

The executives recognized their familiar forms and the powerful presence surrounding them at once. Their faces darkened.

Suicune and Raikou!

Damn it. How did he have these two Legendary beasts?

Both executives' expressions changed sharply. Neither had expected this. The speed at which Ethan was growing stronger was astonishing.

Raikou alone might have been understandable. Professor Sebastian's report on the earlier incident had told them that Ethan helped Raikou fight Team Rocket. But when had he captured it? The report had clearly said Raikou escaped!

And where had Suicune come from? Why had they heard nothing about it beforehand?

After the first shock, Apollo studied Ethan, then slowly breathed out.

So that was why he had dared to pursue them alone. He had the strength to back it up.

One detail offered Apollo a little comfort: the two Legendary beasts did not seem to be in the same state of mind.

Suicune simply stood there, watching him calmly. Raikou was much more restless.

Perhaps because Ethan had captured it only recently, Raikou's attention was not on them. It glanced around, as though studying the place for a way out.

Unfortunately for Apollo, it did not flee. After a few quiet words from Ethan, Raikou gradually settled and turned its attention back to him.

When those cold, tiger-like eyes fixed on Apollo, a chill ran down his spine.

Suicune and Raikou were still only standing there, almost as though they had no intention of working. Ethan laughed and scolded them. "You two can stop posing now. Hurry and deal with them while no one else is around! And if you do well this time, I'll let you out for some fresh air more often!"

At his urging, they finally moved together. Suicune was graceful as a doe, mist gathering around it. Raikou sprang like a hunting cat, electricity flickering across its body as it charged straight into the enemy's ranks.

Calling it a tiger among sheep would hardly have been an exaggeration.

The battle shifted almost at once.

With the two Legendary beasts easing the pressure, Crystal Steelix abandoned its opponents and turned on Apollo's Tyranitar. It had been eager for that fight for some time.

It had disliked the look of Tyranitar from the start. Now it would show that Pokémon which of them was truly the fiercer one!

Gengar, on the other hand, grew even less inclined to fight. It floated back to Ethan's side without making any move to attack.

Even during the earlier clash, Gengar had never taken the battle very seriously. It was sure Ethan would win in the end and had done little more than keep its own opponents occupied.

Now that two such powerful Pokémon had joined the fight, Gengar relaxed completely and watched with the air of a spectator enjoying a show.
