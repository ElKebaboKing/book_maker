Of all Ethan's Pokémon, which was the crowd's favorite? Not elegant Gardevoir, nor mischievous Haunter, which had beaten two opponents in a row during his semifinal against Lily.

It was Gyarados.

Its imposing form, the spectacles it created, and the strength it had shown in battle had all left a deep impression. In the League's official "My Favorite Pokémon" poll, Gyarados led second-place Gardevoir by more than a hundred thousand votes.

Rhyhorn, the unevolved form of Rhydon, was not particularly bright, but its strength was immense. Its bones were said to be a thousand times harder than a human's. A simple charge could smash apart a tall building.

Its short, thick legs made turning while running difficult. Once it started forward, it tended to keep charging. With so little room in its head for more than one thought, it could continue running until its strength ran out.

Gyarados held a clear type advantage over Rhyhorn. It was also strong enough that a contest of raw power would not necessarily go against it. Rhyhorn, as a Rock- and Ground-type, was four times as vulnerable to Water-type attacks.

Logan knew all that. As the bout began, he decided to strike first.

"Rhyhorn, use Take Down!"

Rhyhorn drove forward like a tank, its heavy body shaking the field with every step.

Thud! Thud! Thud!

Gyarados was slow on land. Ethan decided not to waste its strength on a futile attempt to dodge. They would meet the charge head-on.

"Gyarados, stop it!"

Gyarados opened its mouth and gathered energy. A powerful Hydro Pump blasted straight at Rhyhorn.

Bang!

The water struck Rhyhorn in the face. To Ethan's surprise, Rhyhorn kept coming. It pushed through the thick jet until it reached Gyarados, then drove its sharp horn into the larger Pokémon with all its momentum.

Gyarados howled in pain and tipped backward. At the last moment, it braced its tail against the ground and steadied itself.

Ethan frowned. Take Down caused recoil to the user, yet the damage to Gyarados was greater than he had expected.

After a moment's thought, he understood.

"I see. Your Rhyhorn has the Reckless Ability!"

Logan was alarmed that Ethan had worked it out from a single collision, but he admitted it readily.

"Impressive. You saw right through it. Yes, its Ability is Reckless."

The spectators could not hear their exchange. Up in the commentary booth, however, Lance had reached the same conclusion.

Seeing the host's confusion, he explained over the microphone.

"Reckless increases the power of moves that hurt their user through recoil, such as Take Down, Flare Blitz, and Brave Bird. They hit harder as a result."

"So that's why Gyarados nearly went down?" the host asked. "Could Rhyhorn turn the match around with it?"

Lance shook his head.

"I doubt it. The type disadvantage is too severe. Now that Ethan knows about the Ability, I don't think he'll let Rhyhorn build up speed for another attack like that."

The host nodded. The next exchange proved Lance right. Ethan shut down Rhyhorn's movement and gave it no chance to charge.

At his direction, Gyarados stayed close and wound its body around Rhyhorn whenever it tried to move away. Without room to run, Rhyhorn could not repeat Take Down.

Logan tried one command after another, but Rhyhorn could not shake Gyarados.

"Rhyhorn's Stone Edge lands a solid hit!" the host called rapidly. "But Gyarados answers with Aqua Tail—oh, my! With that fourfold type advantage, the blow nearly knocks Rhyhorn senseless!"

Rhyhorn's condition worsened fast. After several Water-type hits, it was barely hanging on.

Logan raised a Poké Ball, hoping to recall it and save it for later. Ethan saw what he intended.

Trying to escape? It would not be that easy.

"Gyarados, wrap around it!"

Gyarados coiled its long body around Rhyhorn. The red beam from Logan's Poké Ball struck Gyarados instead and failed to recall its target.

"Time to finish this!" Ethan shouted. "Use Crunch!"

Gyarados opened its jaws and bit into Rhyhorn's belly—the softest, most vulnerable place beneath all that armor.

Rhyhorn struggled, but as Gyarados's teeth sank in, its strength drained away. At last it slumped to the ground, unable to battle.

"Two to zero!" As the referee raised the red flag, the host's voice rang through the stadium. "It's that familiar, relentless pace! Ethan Vale has beaten two Pokémon in a row. Is he going to win the final as cleanly as all his earlier matches?"

With so many fans behind him, Ethan was met by a tremendous roar of support. Cheers rolled through the stadium like a wave. He knew they were cheering for him, yet standing on the field, he felt almost overwhelmed by the noise.

His heart pounded with excitement, and a shiver ran through him. Goose bumps rose all over his skin.

Logan had it worse. He looked around, his face paling.

Wasn't a League final supposed to pit two evenly matched trainers against each other in a thrilling battle? He had fought his way through one obstacle after another to reach this point. Why had he ended up facing someone this frightening?
