The banquet was only halfway over, yet dozens of white plates covered in scraps were already piled high before Ash, Misty, and Brock. The three released enormous burps and lounged lazily against their wooden chairs, patting their swollen stomachs. They clearly could not eat another bite.

Whenever an opportunity like this arose, they showed their bodies no mercy. Unless they ate so much that they needed to support themselves against a wall on the way out, they would never be satisfied!

Now that they had eaten and drunk their fill, the group finally had enough leisure to chat with Sea Priestess Maya.

Brock was especially enthusiastic. In fact, the first time he saw this beautiful priestess, he had stood petrified for quite a while.

Now he held nothing back as he recounted everything they had seen and experienced during their journey. He poured out every amusing and interesting story he could remember, doing everything possible to win the beautiful priestess's favor.

Maya had encountered far too many enthusiastic men who admired her appearance. She possessed her own methods for dealing with them and always maintained a polite but faint distance.

Perhaps because she had been bound to this place since childhood, however, Maya genuinely seemed interested in their travels.

She listened eagerly to their strange and thrilling stories, and an unguarded trace of longing occasionally appeared on her face.

Most of all, Ash described their battle against the three sacred birds on Shamouti Island, their attempt to stop the collector, and how their efforts alongside Lugia, the God of the Sea, finally ended the disaster. The story shocked not only Maya but also the nearby attendants, who stared at the group in astonishment.

"You've seen the God of the Sea with your own eyes!?"

"Of course!" Ash answered honestly without the slightest exaggeration. "We didn't just see it—we even fought beside it! Counting Ethan's Pokémon, there were seven Legendary..."

Before he could finish, Misty suddenly stomped hard on his foot. The movement was so forceful that even Maya glanced toward them.

"Ow!" Ash screamed in pain, then glared angrily at Misty. "Misty! Why did you step on me!?"

This idiot...

Misty rolled her eyes at him in complete exasperation.

He's unbelievably stupid. He almost revealed Ethan's secret!

Those Pokémon are his trump cards. How could Ash expose them to an outsider so casually?

But with Maya present, Misty could hardly explain. Instead, she picked up a large chicken leg and stuffed it directly into Ash's mouth!

"You obviously haven't eaten enough, right? Then have another chicken leg!"

Perhaps Misty used too much strength, because the chicken leg seemed to jab directly into Ash's throat. He made a series of muffled noises and clutched his mouth in pain.

Ignoring the fallen Ash, Misty sat straight and calmly wiped the grease from her hands with a napkin.

Ethan was thoroughly accustomed to their quarrels, but Maya had never witnessed anything like it. She worried that the two might truly become upset. Only when she saw everyone else treating the exchange as completely ordinary did she realize how close Ash and Misty were. Their actions were merely the rough play of companions who trusted each other completely!

Companions... What a wonderful word...

In any case, the interruption allowed Ash's unfinished revelation to pass unnoticed.

Everyone chatted for a while longer. But as the banquet approached its end, Maya's presence suddenly changed, and she became formal.

Ethan understood that serious business was usually discussed at times like this, and he immediately focused.

At the long table, Maya cleared her throat and formally invited Ethan to remain at the Temple of the Sea.

Only then did Ethan learn that when a local Trainer from the Whirl Islands won the Cup, the temple's enormous influence generally persuaded them to stay and assist the Sea Priestess with its work. The arrangement brought fame and benefit to both the winner and the temple, creating an ideal outcome for everyone.

Competitors from outside the islands, however, were not subject to the temple's influence, and very few ever chose to stay.

Ethan refused almost immediately.

Maya regretted his answer but was not surprised.

Even before offering the invitation, she had known that someone like Ethan would almost certainly never remain here. Nevertheless, tradition required her to ask.

Maya sighed, composed herself, and continued. "After returning here from the arena, I consulted a great many records. Although nothing like this has happened during the last two thousand years, the very first Sea Hero experienced exactly the same phenomenon when the Temple of the Sea was founded."

The first Sea Hero?

That's incredibly ancient.

Ethan asked curiously, "Apart from that person, has no one else experienced anything similar throughout all that time?"

"No." Maya shook her head. "A Sea Hero is chosen every three years. Aside from a period several centuries ago when unrest on the islands forced the tournament to stop, nearly six hundred Heroes have been selected in total. The details of every blessing they received were clearly recorded in scrolls. We examined them one by one and found nothing unusual."

At this point, Maya stared intently at Ethan. "After the blessing ceremony, did you notice anything different about yourself?"

Before Ethan could answer, Misty exclaimed excitedly, "We already tested it! Ethan can't truly connect his heart with Water-type Pokémon, but he can sense what they're feeling!"

"I see." Maya remained stunned for a long while. Then she studied Ethan deeply and nodded. "It appears that you are the Sea Hero who truly received the God of the Sea's blessing!"

Ethan could not resist asking, "Miss Maya, what exactly is the Sea Spirit? Why does it possess this kind of power?"

Maya thought for a moment before answering slowly. "According to the records, the Sea Spirit came from the ocean's deepest reaches. It is the brightest and purest existence beneath the sea, formed from the will of the ocean itself. We do not know precisely what it is. Some also claim that it may be a crystal formed after the God of the Sea—Lugia—died for some extraordinary reason!"

The will of the ocean?

A crystal left by Lugia?

Both possibilities sounded profoundly mysterious.

For a time, everyone fell silent.

They all wondered why Ethan had received the God of the Sea's blessing for the first time in thousands of years.

What separated this man from the hundreds of Sea Heroes who had come before him?
