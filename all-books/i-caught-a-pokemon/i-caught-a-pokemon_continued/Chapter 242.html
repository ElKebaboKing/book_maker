As soon as the match began, Luana's smile disappeared. Her whole bearing changed.

"Alakazam, Marowak! Show them what we can do!" she called sharply.

Alakazam and Marowak rushed toward Charizard and Scyther. One carried spoons and the other a bone club. Both came armed, as though each had an extra weapon from the moment it entered the field.

Ethan would not give ground. He waved his hand, sending Charizard and Scyther forward together.

As the teams closed in, Alakazam stopped first. It raised a spoon toward Charizard and used Confusion. Ethan had watched the opponents closely from the start and saw the movement.

"Charizard, watch Alakazam! Dodge!"

Charizard beat its wings and rose into the air, weaving from side to side to avoid Alakazam's aim.

Meanwhile, Scyther and Marowak collided. Marowak swung its club in a Bone Rush, and Scyther met it with both scythe-like forearms.

Clang! Clang!

Marowak's bone was remarkably hard. Sparks flew where it struck Scyther's blades, and each clash rang like metal on metal.

Alakazam, having lost Charizard as a target, turned on Scyther. A Psychic attack knocked it to the ground. Charizard took advantage of the opening to breathe fire down from above and burn Alakazam.

Both sides had taken damage in their first exchange. Ethan, however, was not pleased. Their Double Battle was turning into an uncontrolled melee. He began adjusting his plan.

"Switch opponents! Scyther, take Alakazam. Charizard, hold off Marowak!"

To his surprise, Scyther ignored him. As soon as it got up, it charged Marowak again.

Ethan sighed. Scyther had once led its group and had a proud streak. He had caught it only recently, and it was not yet used to taking his orders or working with his other Pokémon.

He had spent the past few days talking to it, feeding it, and caring for it. It had begun to follow his instructions, and he had thought they were making progress. This battle showed that the problem remained. Scyther had let its temper take over.

He would have to shift his priority from Alakazam to Marowak. "Scyther, keep fighting Marowak. Charizard, leave Alakazam and help it!"

Luana heard him. She watched Scyther for a moment, then smiled at Ethan. "What, you can't command your own Pokémon?"

"I only caught it recently," Ethan said with a rueful smile. "It was a leader before that. It isn't used to taking orders from someone else. Getting it to listen every time may take a while."

"Oh? You brought a newly caught Pokémon to challenge my Gym?" Luana sounded surprised. "Did you have no one else available, or are you that confident in its strength?"

Ethan shook his head and did not explain. Losing a Gym match would not be the end of anything. The Gym was not going anywhere; a challenger could rest, prepare, and try again. Ash, throughout his journey, had needed a second attempt at many Gyms. Ethan could afford the same if necessary.

While they spoke, all four Pokémon clashed again. For now, Ethan could not rely on Scyther to cooperate. The best he could hope for was that it would beat Marowak by itself. He still had no intention of abandoning it.

"Charizard, Smokescreen!"

Charizard breathed a thick cloud of black smoke toward the ground. It spread across nearly half the field. Marowak and Scyther had been fighting fiercely when the cloud reached them. They looked around for danger, then turned back and found that neither could see the other, even though they stood only a short distance apart.

Outside the smoke, Alakazam held back. It could not attack the cloud at random without risking a hit on Marowak, and it would not enter blindly. It waited for the smoke to clear.

Ethan did not wait. "Charizard, Dragon Rage into the smoke!"

Charizard paused in midair and fired a wave of energy from its mouth. It blew the smoke apart and caught both Marowak and Scyther.

Bang! Bang!

Both Pokémon rocked backward from the hit.

"What...?" Luana stared. Ethan had attacked his own Pokémon along with hers.

He ignored her surprise and spoke coldly to Scyther. "Have you calmed down now?"

Scyther had disobeyed him at a critical moment. He needed to bring it back to its senses. The unexpected hit from behind had stunned it. It turned toward Ethan, the frenzy fading from its eyes, and finally nodded.

"Good." Ethan let out a quiet breath. "Then stop standing there. Switch targets and go after Alakazam!"

Scyther rushed toward Alakazam. Marowak tried to follow and keep its opponent engaged, but Charizard dived between them and blocked its path.

Alakazam used Confusion again, trying to stop Scyther from a distance. At the crucial moment, Scyther burst forward with Quick Attack. It avoided the psychic force and closed in at once.

Both blades gleamed. Scyther raised them high, crossed them, and brought them down hard on Alakazam.

X-Scissor!
