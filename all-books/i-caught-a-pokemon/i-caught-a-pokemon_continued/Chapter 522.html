Flygon rolled several times across the ground and out of bounds, coming to rest on its back at Monica's feet.

Dragonite stood on the other side of the field, breathing a little hard. After the muddy rain, the two Pokémon had fought another short but fierce exchange. Dragonite had proved the stronger and ended the battle with Aqua Tail.

The match had gone fairly smoothly for Ethan. He had not faced any serious danger.

Monica recalled Flygon with a rueful smile. She and her brothers picked their way around the mud and came over to Ethan.

"You're truly strong. We were no match for you," she said sincerely. Santos and Sunny were plainly less ready than their sister to accept their losses.

Ethan smiled at the boys' childish stubbornness and said to Monica, "Your Flygon is strong too. If I hadn't broken up its Sandstorm, Dragonite might have been the one to fall."

Monica did not know whether he was simply being polite, but the words eased her disappointment.

She took a badge from her coat and offered it to him. "Here. This is my Invigoration Badge, proof that you beat me."

Santos and Sunny each brought out a badge too. Though reluctant, they held them out to Ethan.

"This is my Sound Sleep Badge."

"And this is my Laughter Badge."

Like the badges held by the other four Week Siblings, these three were nearly identical apart from their colors. Ethan accepted them without ceremony.

By defeating these last three siblings, he had collected every one of their badges.

"Thanks."

Monica did not want to linger after losing. "Well, perhaps we'll meet again someday. We should go wash up now."

Ethan had hardly noticed the mud until she mentioned it. He looked them over: all three were filthy, a sharp contrast to his clean clothes.

He watched them leave, and Erika came to his side.

"The Week Siblings are quite well known in Johto. How does it feel to have beaten all of them?"

"How does it feel?" Ethan laughed and shook his head. "I don't feel much, but at least I won't have to worry about them tracking me down again."

They left the battlefield and went straight to Goldenrod City's port.

There they bought tickets to Altomare, the City of Water. A city hailed as the most beautiful in the world drew ships from Goldenrod and many other places. More than a dozen vessels sailed between Altomare and a metropolis like Goldenrod every day.

...

"Long, long ago, an elderly couple lived on the island of Altomare. One day, they found an injured little brother and sister by the sea. They cared for the children, who gradually recovered."

"Then one day, an evil monster attacked the island. Just as it seemed Altomare would be swallowed up, the brother and sister changed before the old couple's eyes."

"They were the Eon Pokémon, Latios and Latias."

"The two Pokémon flew into the sky and called their companions. Together they brought a power that drove away the darkness: a jewel called the Soul Dew. Peace returned to the island. From then on, Latios and Latias often stayed there, and the evil Pokémon never attacked Altomare again."

On the ship's deck, Erika finished telling Ethan the legend and closed her travel guide.

Ethan reclined comfortably in a deck chair, wearing large sunglasses and holding a cocktail. He popped the cherry from his drink into his mouth, but behind the sunglasses, his eyes stayed on Erika.

She was a gifted storyteller. Her voice was gentle, and her tone rose and fell with the tale.

He could not help imagining that if they had children one day, Erika would tell them bedtime stories just like this.

She would make a wonderful wife and mother.

Before Ethan could respond to the legend, a girl nearby laughed. "Miss Erika tells that story beautifully, but it's only a fairy tale, isn't it?"

Her voice sounded familiar. Ethan and Erika turned to find Ash, Misty, and Brock, whom they had parted from not long ago.

"How do we keep running into you three?" Ethan pushed his sunglasses up and smiled at Misty. "The Soul Dew, Latios, and Latias are all real. They're still in that city today. Perhaps we'll even see them this time!"

Then he asked, "Are you going to Altomare too?"

"Of course!" Ash pulled a poster from his coat and showed it to Ethan. "We're entering the annual Pokémon water race. Are you two competing?"

"No. Erika and I are going on holiday." Ethan shook his head. "A Pokémon water race does sound interesting, though."

He took the poster and read it. A race would be held in Altomare soon. Competitors would stand in small boats and be towed by Water-type Pokémon through the city's winding waterways. The first to reach the finish would win a generous prize.

"Since you're here too, come with us!" Ash said eagerly. "Misty and I are both entering. Heh, I can't beat you in a Pokémon battle, but I'll definitely take first place in a race like this!"

"Honestly, where does that confidence come from?" Misty rolled her eyes at Ash, then said proudly, "I'm a Water-type Trainer. A race like this should suit me best. But as for first place... if Ethan decides to enter, he'll definitely win!"

Brock glanced at Misty and sighed helplessly.

He could see that she was Ethan's most devoted fan, beyond any hope of changing her mind. If anyone criticized or underestimated Ethan while she was around, she would mock them without mercy.

How had Ethan managed to win her over so completely?
