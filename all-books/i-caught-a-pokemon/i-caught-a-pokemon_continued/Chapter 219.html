Professor Ivy's main field of study was the way Pokémon's forms varied by region. She looked about twenty-six or twenty-seven, perhaps older. She took such good care of herself that Ethan could not judge her age from her appearance.

Her loose white lab coat did little to hide her striking figure. She often looked sleepy and moved at an unhurried pace, yet she was beautiful and carried herself with an easy, mature charm.

Brock had been so taken with her when they met that he had left Ash's group of his own accord. He was still at her laboratory, cheerfully doing whatever odd jobs needed doing.

Ethan gave the professor a glance, then offered his hand. "So you're Professor Ivy. It's a pleasure to meet you. Misty has told me about you, but I didn't expect we'd meet here."

He treated researchers with considerable respect, whether it was Professor Oak, Professor Cerise, or Ivy. In truth, he had recognized her the moment he saw her. His memories of the anime had left a strong impression of her. Apart from the Crystal Onix, she was among the things he remembered most clearly from the Orange Islands story.

Ivy smiled and shook his hand. "No need to be so formal, Mr. Vale. I've heard plenty about you too. I watched several of your Indigo League matches on television. The way you handled your opponents so decisively was impressive."

"You're too kind. My opponents helped make me look good."

After a little more polite conversation, Melody warmly invited everyone back to Shamouti Town. The streets held far fewer visitors now. Ethan could not tell whether the battle had driven them off or the festival had simply run its course.

Although the crisis with Lugia was over, Professors Oak and Ivy and Delia chose to stay for the time being. The two professors had been struck by what they had seen. They planned to write a paper together on how Pokémon battles affected the surrounding environment.

The journalists who had arrived with them would have to wait for a ship calling at Shamouti Island that evening before returning to mainland Kanto. The helicopter's two pilots would remain until their company sent people to investigate the crash and complete the necessary questioning.

It was nearly dusk. Melody led the group to the banquet hall they had visited before. She had food brought in, then found Carol and the elderly man. The three spoke quietly, glancing toward Ethan and Ash as they talked.

Melody was plainly telling them what had happened. She kept her promise to Ethan, though, and did not reveal his three legendary birds.

Grrr...

Ethan's stomach brought his attention back to the table. Platters of fragrant food covered it. More than ten hours had passed since they left the hall the previous night. They had neither eaten nor rested properly, and all of them were ravenous.

They exchanged smiles and started eating without worrying about table manners. Only Sabrina remained elegant, taking small bites and chewing slowly.

Oak, Ivy, and Delia ate at a more ordinary pace. They had left Kanto around midday, only a few hours earlier, so it was simply dinner time for them.

For a while the banquet hall was quiet except for chewing and swallowing. Conversation resumed only after Ethan and the others had satisfied their hunger.

With Ash's stomach finally full, Delia had a chance to talk to her son. She watched him fondly as he told her all about his recent travels, adding a question or comment now and then.

Nearby, Oak and Ivy were discussing their proposed paper with enthusiasm. Misty listened from beside them until she could not resist mentioning Ethan's explanation of the butterfly effect.

"The butterfly effect?" Ivy looked toward him with interest. "I haven't heard of that theory. It seems you have an unusual way of looking at science as well as talent for battling, Mr. Vale."

Oak smiled at Ivy. "I almost forgot to mention it. Ethan isn't only a Trainer. He's also an assistant to Professor Cerise."

"Cerise? The one in Vermilion City?" Ivy looked surprised. "So you work with him. Does that make you something of a scholar too?"

Ethan shook his head quickly. "I don't have the education or experience to call myself that. I just run errands for Professor Cerise. I'd be embarrassed to claim to be a scholar in front of you two."

Ivy took that for modesty and plainly did not believe him.

The elderly man approached the table with Carol and Melody. He thanked Ethan and the others for helping Shamouti Island through its crisis, then welcomed the two professors.

"Commanders, thank you! Without you, the world might have been destroyed!"

Ethan knew the entire world would have survived even if the island had exploded or frozen over. The belief was deeply rooted among the islanders, though, and he had no desire to argue. He accepted the man's thanks.

Only now did Ethan learn that the elder who had played up the prophecy earlier was Shamouti Town's mayor.

Yet looking at Carol, he felt he had forgotten something. She turned to him.

"Mr. Vale, didn't Maren come back with you?"

"Oh no." Ethan gave a rueful smile. "We left her on Fire Island. I'm sorry. She should still be there; her boat ran aground, so she can't get back alone."

"I see." Carol smiled helplessly. "It isn't your fault. Don't worry. I'll go and bring her back myself in a little while."
