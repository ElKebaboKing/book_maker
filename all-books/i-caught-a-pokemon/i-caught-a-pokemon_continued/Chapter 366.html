Since the Unown were not with Molly at the top of the crystal tower, Ethan could only search elsewhere for their trail.

He summoned Gengar and had it break through the crystals with Shadow Ball, opening a path back to the entrance through which they had entered Molly's bedroom.

This time, Ethan decided to conserve his strength. He climbed directly onto Gengar's back and let it carry him swiftly downward.

They had flown through the crystal network for only a short while when they encountered Brock and Misty along the way.

Both were panting heavily as they struggled up the staircase. When they saw Ethan flying down from above, they stared in surprise and hurriedly waved to him.

"Ethan! Over here!"

Ethan patted Gengar's shoulder. Understanding his intent, Gengar carried him over to the two of them.

"How did you end up together?"

"Don't even ask." Misty sighed. "The Pokémon Molly created were unbelievably strong! Every one of them was powerful. It wasn't as if we couldn't fight back, but both battles were extremely difficult. Then she suddenly disappeared for some reason. We don't know where she went, so we took the opportunity to come up here and see what was happening!"

Seeing Ethan alone, Misty grew curious and asked, "Where are you going, Ethan? And where's Ash?"

"I split up with him so I could search elsewhere for the Unown. Ash is fighting Entei alone above us right now. You should hurry up there and help him!"

Misty's eyes lit up. "Should I come with you to find the Unown? Brock can go support Ash by himself!"

"That won't be necessary." Ethan smiled and stroked her hair. "Don't underestimate my strength. Besides, Ash needs help more than I do! Now hurry up there, both of you!"

"All right..."

Misty lowered her head in disappointment. Although Ethan's affectionate gesture made her happy, joining him on an adventure, even for a short time, had always been her greatest wish...

After parting from Misty and Brock, Ethan continued downward on Gengar.

Perhaps Molly was now concentrating entirely on her battle with Ash and had neglected to maintain the imaginary spaces. As Ethan retraced their path, he discovered that both illusory levels they had passed through had disappeared.

This was good news. At least it saved him a great deal of time.

After flying down a little farther, Ethan still had not found the Unown. Instead, he encountered the Team Rocket trio once again!

The two humans and one Pokémon were drenched in sweat, using both hands and feet to struggle toward the top of the tower. Ethan had no idea what they intended to do there.

When they saw him, all three shuddered, then huddled together and trembled. They looked small, helpless, and afraid.

"Oh no! It's Ethan!!"

"We're doomed! Why is he here too!?"

Perhaps Ethan's reputation within Team Rocket had grown too fearsome, or perhaps their past encounters had left too deep an impression on the trio. At some point, Jessie, James, and Meowth had lost all thought of resisting him. Whenever they saw Ethan, they appeared terrified.

"Hey!" Ethan first had Gengar stop in midair, then called out to them. "James, Jessie, have you seen any Unown?"

When Ethan spoke gently instead of attacking at once, the trio visibly relaxed.

But...

Unown?

Jessie and James exchanged confused looks. This was clearly the first time either had heard the name.

Seeing their bewilderment, Ethan patiently described the Unown. "They're mysterious-looking Pokémon shaped like letters."

At his explanation, the two finally understood.

James pointed downward. "After we crashed... No, after we arrived here, we saw lots of Pokémon like that in one of the rooms. They kept gathering together and flying in circles while giving off a black light. We don't know what they were doing, but they looked frightening!"

"Thanks."

Ethan waved to them and was about to leave when he remembered the moment Entei had shot down their airship. He could not help smiling.

"You distracted Entei for us earlier. It wasn't intentional, but you still did a good job! Keep it up in the future!"

Before the trio could react, Gengar carried Ethan into the darkness and vanished.

After he left, the Team Rocket trio remained utterly baffled. They had no idea what he had meant.

James looked at Jessie in confusion. "Distracted it? Did we do something earlier?"

"I don't know." Jessie shrugged, clearly unconcerned. "Don't worry about it. That man is far too dangerous. As long as he doesn't cause trouble for us, we should be grateful!"

James hesitated.

What she said was true. Still, if Ethan had treated them like Ash and shown open hostility immediately, James would not have thought much of it. This warm, friendly attitude was far more chilling. He could not tell what Ethan was planning.

But Jessie was right about one thing. Ethan was far too dangerous. They should stay as far away from him as possible.

Seeing the two continue chatting at their leisure, Meowth kicked them and urged, "That's enough, you two! Whether it's Ethan or those Unown, this whole place gives me the creeps. Let's grab the treasure and get out of here!"

"Right, right! Meowth is right!" At the mention of treasure, Jessie's eyes gleamed and she forgot everything else. "Since Ethan flew down from above, the treasure must be hidden at the top of the tower! Once we get it, we'll leave this horrible place immediately!"

The Team Rocket trio resumed climbing. After they had completely disappeared from view, Ethan and Gengar, who had supposedly left, suddenly flew out from behind a nearby corner.

Watching the direction in which they had gone, Gengar asked in surprise, "Those people came here looking for treasure? But there isn't any treasure here!"

"Who knows?"

Ethan shook his head helplessly.

Those three greedy fools were imagining far too much. This place looked dangerous at a glance. How could there possibly be treasure here? Their efforts would be completely wasted.

Perhaps their fates had simply become intertwined. The trio clung to Ash like a tail. No matter where he went, they followed close behind and could never be shaken off.

"But if they're heading up there too, won't they cause trouble for Ash and the others? Should we go help?"

"Forget it. Finding the Unown is more important." Ethan seemed entirely unconcerned about the Team Rocket trio. "We'll leave them to Ash. Besides, having those three involved may not be a bad thing. They might even help in some unexpected way!"
