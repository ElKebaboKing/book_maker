Ethan rode Dragonite back with Celebi, searching for Sammy and Vicious.

The forest below looked the same in every direction. Fortunately, Dragonite remembered their approximate route. Before long, they heard Sammy calling and found him.

But when Dragonite landed, Ethan saw only Sammy and Charmeleon. Vicious was gone.

“Where’s Vicious?”

“I’m sorry.” Sammy looked ashamed. “His eyes seemed badly hurt, so I let my guard down. He jumped into the river while I wasn’t watching. I wanted to follow him, but I was afraid you wouldn’t find me when you came back, so I stayed here.”

Vicious had escaped again?

It was regrettable, but not Sammy’s fault. Ethan did not reproach him. “Was he pretending to be blind?”

Sammy shook his head. “No. I saw blood coming from his eyes. He was really hurt. He found the river by listening for it, then stumbled in.”

Ethan had nothing to say to that. Vicious had nerve.

If Ethan were blind, he would never dare jump into a river without knowing where he could get out. It was almost a death sentence. Vicious would rather gamble on the current than be captured. He must have feared losing his freedom more than dying.

But even if he reached the shore, how would a blind man without Poké Balls or Pokémon find his way out of this forest?

Then Ethan remembered something else. Vicious had not seen the blue-green light and heard the voice of Celebi’s time travel before today. No one had explained it to him. He probably did not even know he had landed forty years in the past.

Perhaps he hoped to find the spider-shaped robot he had left behind and escape in it. He could search this forest forever and never find a machine that belonged to another era.

For all he had done, it was a miserable end.

Sammy noticed Celebi beside Ethan and brightened. Ethan told him they had returned to his original time.

“So I’m back forty years ago? Back where I belong?”

“That’s right. You—”

An engine roared nearby. A motorcycle burst from behind the trees.

Its rider was a thin-faced man in his thirties or forties. He wore an old-fashioned jacket and a deerstalker hat that had long since gone out of style in Ethan’s day. A black patch covered his injured right eye. He did not look friendly.

A Scyther and a Houndoom ran beside the motorcycle. The man and his Pokémon stopped in front of Ethan and Sammy.

His gaze passed over Celebi and settled on Sammy. “I thought you’d disappeared. So this is where you went. You made me search everywhere. What’s wrong? Too frightened to run anymore?”

Sammy took an instinctive step back, and Celebi moved away too.

Bewildered, Ethan looked at Sammy. “Who is he? Why does he talk as though he owns the place?”

“He’s the Pokémon hunter who was chasing Celebi in this time!” Sammy started to pull Ethan away, then remembered the strength of Ethan’s Pokémon and Suicune in particular. He steadied himself. “He cornered us. That’s why Celebi had to take us to your time.”

“I see.” Ethan faced the hunter with a smile. “Picking on a child isn’t impressive. Would you like to battle me?”

“Who are you?” The man studied him, hearing the confidence in his voice. “Another meddler?”

Then he spotted Dragonite and laughed. “This is my lucky day. I’ll catch Celebi and a rare Dragonite. That one will fetch a fine price.”

Ethan had heard enough. He whistled.

Dragonite, already angry, flew at the hunter. The man opened his mouth to threaten the reckless young Trainer, but his Scyther and Houndoom were knocked down in a few swift blows.

“What?”

Dragonite rammed him off his feet and planted a foot on him before he could rise.

“That’s it?” Ethan shrugged. “I thought you’d be more of a challenge.”

Sammy and Celebi breathed easier at the sight of the hunter’s effortless defeat.

“Who are you?” The man stared up at Ethan with his one visible eye.

“That doesn’t matter. What matters is that your luck just ran out.” Ethan turned to Dragonite. “Break up his motorcycle, destroy his Poké Balls, and throw him in the river. He can keep Vicious company. They may even get along.”

Dragonite nodded and obeyed. It kicked the hunter into the river, then tore his motorcycle apart. Finally it found the Poké Balls on the bike, released every Pokémon inside, and crushed the empty balls underfoot.

It dusted off its hands and returned to Ethan with a proud look.

With the danger handled, it was time to part from Sammy.

Ethan offered him a hand. “Celebi will take me back to my time now. You should continue your sketching journey.”

Sammy shook his hand. He had a thousand things he wanted to say, but none would come out.

He had met Celebi, traveled forty years into the future, seen the girl who had once given him bread as an old woman, and helped rescue Celebi from a hunter. He had only just made friends with Ash and could not even say goodbye to him. It all felt like a dream: precious, and hard to wake from.

At last he bowed deeply. “Thank you so much. Please say goodbye to Ash and Towa for me. Ask them to forgive me for not being able to do it myself.”

“I will.”

Ethan patted his shoulder and smiled knowingly at the young Professor Oak. The future authority of the Pokémon research world had just bowed to him. He could not help feeling a little pleased.

After their farewell, Sammy stepped back beyond the reach of Celebi’s power.

Blue-green light shone, and the ethereal voice sounded once more. Ethan and Celebi vanished before Sammy’s eyes.

Sammy stood looking at the empty ground for a long time. When he had calmed himself, he turned with determination and resumed his travels.

...

When the light faded, Ethan was back where he had left his own time.

Traces of their battle remained, but Ash was gone. He had probably returned to the lake to meet Misty and the others after seeing them vanish.

Ethan did not hurry to find Erika. With no one around, he put his plan to catch Celebi into motion.

First he brought out Eevee to play with it. He had seen how well the two got along. Eevee’s company would help Celebi relax.

Then he set down his bag, filled a kettle from the river, and heated the water. With Celebi watching, he opened a cup of noodles and poured the hot water in.

Yes, he intended to use food again to tempt Celebi into coming with him.

The tactic rarely failed with a glutton. A Pokémon that had lived in the wild for years could find human food especially hard to resist.

He and Celebi were on friendly terms now, and it had no reason to fear him. They were alone in a peaceful forest, with time and opportunity on his side.

If he still failed to catch it, he might as well bang his head against a block of tofu.

When the noodles were ready, he peeled back the lid. A savory smell rose into the air. Celebi, already eager, was nearly drooling.

“Want some?” Ethan asked.

Celebi nodded repeatedly.

Ethan held the cup toward it. As Celebi leaned in, he drew it back and sighed as though disappointed.

Celebi looked up, not understanding.

“Such a shame,” Ethan said.

He left the thought unfinished. Celebi’s stomach growled at the smell, and it chirped to ask what he meant.

“I’m about to leave this forest. We won’t be able to stay friends.”

Before Celebi reacted, Dragonite turned its head away. It had heard this sort of speech before and could guess what would come next.

Celebi looked downcast. It had known Ethan’s group for only two days, but those days had held more excitement than its quiet life ever had. Even with so little experience of the world, it understood that people had to part.

“If only you could travel with us,” Ethan went on. “You could leave this forest and see the wide ocean, bustling cities, and all kinds of wonderful food...”

At the mention of food, Celebi swallowed and stared at the noodles. Until recently, it had eaten only wild fruit. It wanted to devour the fragrant meal in one bite.

Ethan handed it the cup. Celebi forgot all the dignity expected of a Mythical Pokémon and began gobbling the noodles.

Cup noodles smelled and tasted good, even if most people tired of them after eating too many. To Celebi, tasting them for the first time, even the cup itself looked tempting.

It finished the noodles, patted its round belly, and still seemed to want more.

Ethan judged the moment right and spoke in an enticing voice. “It’s a pity you can only stay in this forest. If you came with us, I’d treat you to even better food. You’ve never had strawberry cake, have you? Bright red strawberries on top, smooth cream underneath... And there’s something called ice cream...”

As Ethan talked, Celebi drooled. Even Dragonite smacked its lips at the description.

Eevee called to Celebi too, backing up what its Trainer said. Before joining Ethan, Eevee had lived with the five Kimono Sisters in Ecruteak City and tried plenty of delicious food. It spoke from experience.

Trainer and Pokémon continued painting a picture of life beyond the forest. When Ethan saw interest brightening Celebi’s eyes, he finally asked, “Would you like to travel with us?”

Celebi hesitated. The outside world sounded wonderful, but it still wanted to remain in its forest.

Eevee redoubled its efforts. Its calls reminded Celebi of the fun they had shared yesterday and of all the advantages Eevee had described in living with humans.

Dragonite added a few words of encouragement.

Seeing Celebi waver, Ethan took out the GS Ball and held it before the Pokémon.

“What do you say? Come with me and see the world outside?”

Everything went quiet. Ethan had finally shown what he wanted. Dragonite and Eevee watched Celebi, waiting for its choice.

Neither Pokémon was foolish. They had understood from the beginning that their Trainer wanted to catch Celebi and had been helping him persuade it.

After all three had made their case, Celebi gave Ethan an innocent smile and nodded.

Good. They had talked it into it.

Ethan touched the GS Ball to Celebi’s forehead. Blue-green light sprang from the ball and enveloped it.

Celebi entered. The ball rocked a few times, then fell still.

At last Ethan let out a breath. He had caught Celebi.

He barely had time to celebrate before footsteps and voices sounded nearby.

As he stood, Erika and the others emerged from the trees, looking worried.

“Huh?” Misty froze at the sight of him and turned to Ash. “You said Ethan disappeared! He’s standing right here!”

Ash had panicked when Ethan and Sammy vanished. After searching the area without finding them, he had run back to the lake and told the others. Erika and the group had hurried here.

Ash looked around. “Ethan, where did you go? And where are Sammy and Celebi?”

“You might not believe this, but I went forty years into the past. I helped Sammy defeat the hunter who had been pursuing him and Celebi.”

“I see.” Ash scratched his head. “So where are they? Didn’t they come back with you?”

“Sammy stayed. That’s where he belongs.” Ethan smiled. “He asked me to say goodbye to you and Towa. He hopes you won’t mind that he couldn’t do it in person.”

“Sammy...” Ash’s face fell. They had only just become friends, and already they had parted.

“Don’t be sad, Ash,” Towa said. “Sammy comes from another time. Too much contact between you might change the present. We know he got home safely. We should be happy for him.”

“You’re right.” Ash nodded, then looked back at Ethan. “What about Celebi?”

“Celebi...” Ethan smiled faintly. “It went where it belongs.”

That was a riddle. Ethan was not unwilling to tell Ash, but Towa, Diana, and White were there. They lived beside the forest. He did not know how they would feel about him catching its guardian. He could explain to Ash another time.

With that, the matter was finally settled.

Ethan and Ash’s group had journeys to continue.

They waved goodbye to Towa, Diana, and White. The three villagers turned back toward Arborville while the travelers headed for the other side of the forest.

Perhaps they would never meet again. None of them would forget what had happened here.

They walked on without much conversation. Beyond the forest, they reached a town below the Indigo Plateau. It was time for Ethan and Ash to part too.

Standing opposite Ash, Misty, and Brock, Ethan smiled. “I’m leaving for Hoenn. Once you’ve been home and rested a few days, come as soon as you can. Otherwise I’ll get far ahead of you.”

“Don’t worry.” Ash thumped his chest. “I’ll go back to Pallet Town, talk with Mom, and head straight to Hoenn!”

They smiled at each other. Ethan said goodbye to Misty and Brock as well.

Brock took the parting easily enough. Misty smiled at Ethan. “You won’t be waiting long for me. I’ll return to the Cerulean Gym, let my sisters know I’m safe, and come find you right away.”

“I’ll wait for you.” Ethan and Erika waved once more. “See you in Hoenn!”

(End of the Johto Arc)
