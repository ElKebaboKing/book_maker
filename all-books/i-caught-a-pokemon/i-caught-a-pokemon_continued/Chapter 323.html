Looker was not frightened by the man's threats. He studied him, trying to place him among the Team Rocket members in the International Police files. No matter how quickly he searched his memory, he could find no match. The man was clearly no low-ranking officer, though.

"Who are you? Are you in charge here?"

The man folded his thick arms across his chest and stared coldly at Looker. "I'm Tyson, captain of Project R's operations unit. I'm also the man who's about to kill you."

"Project R?" Looker frowned. The unfamiliar name unsettled him. "What is that? Another of your evil schemes?"

"Pathetic," Tyson mocked. "You came charging in without even knowing what Project R is. I suppose you've had quite a stroke of 'luck.'"

"Tell me. What is Project R?"

"Heh. The dead don't need to know so much."

Tyson had no interest in talking further. He swept out a hand, and the Rocket members around him released their Pokémon. More than a hundred Golbat and Weezing filled the sky and the ground. Opposite them stood the International Police officers' Arcanine: a dark wave facing a golden one.

A battle was about to break out. Looker took a deep breath. The Rockets were stubbornly resisting, just as he had expected. He called to them one last time.

"Team Rocket! Give up! You're surrounded, and you cannot escape! Surrender now and spare yourselves needless suffering!"

The only answer was an attack from their Pokémon.

"Arrest them!"

Looker gave the order for a full assault. More than a hundred Arcanine surged toward Team Rocket's Pokémon.

Ethan and Sabrina stayed beside Looker instead of joining the charge. The officers could handle this kind of melee. They needed to save their own Pokémon for the more difficult opponents ahead.

But just as the police began to move, something changed. The running Arcanine looked as though they had been struck by an unseen blow. Every one of them stopped with pain on its face and curled up on the ground, trembling violently.

"What?"

Looker was caught completely off guard. He looked around, unable to see what had happened. No matter how their Trainers urged them on, the Arcanine could not stand. They were in agony and had lost their strength. Team Rocket's Pokémon, meanwhile, were unharmed and kept charging.

"What's going on? Were they attacked?"

Looker's carefully prepared plan had encountered a disastrous complication. He was no longer as composed as before. If the operation failed and the International Police suffered heavy losses, he would bear much of the responsibility.

"Ha ha ha!" Tyson laughed at the disorder among the police. "That's one part of Project R! Weren't you certain you had us beaten a moment ago? Let's see what you can do now!"

Ethan felt the shadow beneath him shifting. He looked down. Even Gengar had emerged from it, clutching its head with both hands, its face contorted in pain.

"My head feels awful!" Gengar's voice shook. "All my strength is draining away, and it feels like something is stirring inside my skull!"

Ethan frowned. Gengar was so powerful that another Pokémon could hardly hurt it without its noticing. Unless...

"Is it some kind of supernatural force?"

"No..." Gengar shook its head with difficulty. "It feels like a radio wave is affecting me. It appeared all at once, and I can't resist it. Wait... I can't take any more. I have to hide somewhere!"

Gengar opened the Poké Ball on Ethan's belt itself and slipped inside. It was the first time since Ethan had caught Gengar that it had stayed in its Poké Ball. The wave had to be hurting the Pokémon badly.

But a radio wave? Why couldn't Ethan feel anything? Was it aimed only at Pokémon? If so, even releasing the three Legendary birds might not help.

"Damn it!" Looker heard Gengar's explanation and understood. "Most of their Pokémon are Golbat and Weezing. If this is a radio-wave attack, they must have adjusted the frequency so their own Pokémon aren't affected!"

While they spoke, Team Rocket's Pokémon reached the officers and began attacking the helpless Arcanine. Arcanine fell one after another.

One officer called urgently to Looker. "Should we withdraw?"

Looking at the field, Looker did not know what to do. Reason told him they had to retreat, but he could not bear to abandon the operation. He turned to Ethan, hoping the friend he trusted might see a way out.

Ethan scanned the base and finally looked up at the signal tower beside the research building.

"A small device couldn't affect this many Pokémon at once. Look there. I think that tower is broadcasting the wave. If we destroy it, the Pokémon should recover."

The signal tower. Looker followed Ethan's gaze, then looked back at the Rockets.

"But how do we destroy it now?"

The signal covered the whole base. Rockets stood between them and the tower, and without their Pokémon, the police could not get near it.

Ethan sighed softly and looked at Sabrina. If the wave did not affect humans, he would need her help.

He did not want the woman he loved to take such a risk in the middle of a chaotic battle. Even Sabrina could not withstand attacks from so many Pokémon without cover.

Sabrina met his gaze, gave no sign of fear, and nodded lightly. After so long together, they understood each other without needing to speak. She knew that with everyone else at a loss, she was the one who would have to act.
