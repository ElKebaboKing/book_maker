"Let's set that aside for now." Maya looked toward Ethan and parted her red lips. "Actually, besides celebrating your becoming a Sea Hero, I arranged this banquet because I have an extremely urgent matter for which I'd like to request your help."

Here it comes!

Ethan silently thought, I knew it. He was about to hear what Maya wanted, but before he could speak, Brock suddenly shot to his feet and thumped his chest loudly.

"Miss Maya! Whatever it is, please tell us! There's no need to be polite!" Brock's eyes had completely transformed into hearts the moment she mentioned needing help. "Whatever your request, we'll brave fire and water and do everything in our power to help! So... is this urgent matter a sweet romance with you?"

Ethan and the others stared at him speechlessly. Misty in particular felt goose bumps rise across her body.

This guy Brock...

He sounded perfectly serious at first. How did he become a completely different person by the end?

Even Misty could never bring herself to say something so embarrassing to Ethan. Yet Brock had shamelessly declared it before so many people!

His skin had to be the thickest in the world. It was as though his entire personality had changed and he had suddenly become a lecher!

Fortunately, Misty knew exactly what he was like. She shoved Brock aside, looked apologetically at Maya, and said, "Sorry. Please ignore him. There's something wrong with his head. What exactly do you need?"

Maya did not answer. Instead, she looked toward Ethan again.

Compared with the others, the Sea Hero's opinion mattered far more to her. In Maya's view, only Ethan, who had received the sea's blessing, was qualified to accept this task and see it through.

Ethan did not agree immediately. He asked first, "Please tell me what you need. Has something happened?"

Only after he spoke did Maya explain. "It concerns the God of the Sea..."

"Lugia?"

"That's right." Maya nodded. "There are signs that an evil organization has targeted the God of the Sea in the Whirl Islands. They're attempting to capture it..."

"Huh? Wait!" Ash and the others froze, then exclaimed in shock, "You mean there's a God of the Sea here too!?"

"Of course." Maya glanced at him. Since Ash was Ethan's companion, she no longer concealed the truth. "Gods of the Sea do live beneath the waters of the Whirl Islands. And there is more than one..."

Misty and Ash looked at each other. The news was simply too astonishing. A Legendary Pokémon coveted by countless people was hiding beneath the nearby ocean!

Ash clicked his tongue. "I thought there was only one Lugia in the entire world..."

"Of course not," Ethan explained. "The same was true of the one we encountered in the Orange Islands. Lugia may be extremely rare in this world, but it isn't unique. That isn't limited to Lugia, either. The same applies to Moltres and the other sacred birds. In fact, another form of the three birds even exists in the Galar region."

"He's correct," Maya agreed. "This is the Temple of the Sea, so naturally the deity we worship is the local God of the Sea from the Whirl Islands. But there is certainly more than one God of the Sea in the world. They reproduce just like other Pokémon."

"I see." Ash scratched his head. "Ethan, you really know a lot."

Ethan merely smiled. It was less that he knew a great deal than that he had played and watched a great deal!

Maya's explanation also caused certain memories to surface in his mind.

According to the animated series, a mother and child pair of Lugia did indeed live in Johto. Judging by their location, they seemed to inhabit the Whirl Islands. Ethan also clearly remembered that the young Lugia was named Silver!

Of course, this Silver had absolutely no connection to Giovanni's son of the same name.

But speaking of Giovanni...

Ethan looked at Maya. "This evil organization you mentioned wouldn't happen to be..."

Maya completed his thought. "Team Rocket, an evil organization causing trouble throughout Johto!"

So it was them after all.

Johto and Kanto were Team Rocket's principal territories. Besides them, Ethan could not imagine another evil organization daring to hunt Pokémon so brazenly in this region!

"Don't worry." After thinking briefly, Ethan accepted the request. "Leave this matter to us. We've dealt with those people many times. Since we've encountered this situation, we would never allow them to succeed even if you hadn't asked for our help!"

...

At nine that evening, after washing up, Ethan lay in a guest room inside the Temple of the Sea.

When the banquet ended, Maya had discussed additional details with the group. By the time they finished, the night had grown late, so she invited everyone to remain at the temple.

The Temple of the Sea was not merely a place of worship. It was Maya's home as well. The office of Sea Priestess passed down through a single family line, and Maya's ancestors had lived here for generations.

In the future, if she married and had children, one of them would inherit the priesthood in turn.

That's quite the secure lifelong position...

Looking through the window at the star-filled sky, Ethan smiled softly.

Becoming Sea Priestess guaranteed food and shelter, earned tremendous respect, offered wealth and privilege, and brought an exceptionally high social position. It was practically a guaranteed route to success in life.

As his thoughts wandered, sleepiness gradually overcame him. Just as he was about to drift off, a dark shadow suddenly emerged from the wall.

Ethan lifted his eyelids and saw that it was his Gengar.

The fellow had wandered all over the temple since the banquet began, grandly calling its excursion an adventure. After so long, it had finally remembered to return.

Ethan yawned and prepared to turn over, only for Gengar to approach his bedside.

"Hey, get up! I just saw Team Rocket!"

Team Rocket!?

The three words instantly banished Ethan's sleepiness and brought him fully awake!

He sat up and asked in surprise, "How did Team Rocket get here? I didn't expect them to reach the temple already. Are they planning to attack Maya, or did they come searching for clues about Lugia?"

"I don't know." Gengar shook its head. "Anyway, those three sneaked inside looking suspicious and are wandering around the place."

"Those... three?" Ethan froze before realizing whom Gengar meant. He curled his lip in disappointment. "Come on... When you said Team Rocket, you didn't mean the executives. It's only James and the other two..."

"What's wrong with them?" Gengar looked at him in surprise. "Why does it sound like you look down on them? Aren't they members of Team Rocket?"

Ethan laughed outright. "How should I put this? Those three may belong to Team Rocket, but they never accomplish anything important. There's no need to worry. Ash and the others are more than enough to deal with them."

He began to lie down again, but after hesitating for a moment, he still draped his jacket over his shoulders.

"Forget it. I'm awake now anyway. I might as well go see what those three came here to do."
