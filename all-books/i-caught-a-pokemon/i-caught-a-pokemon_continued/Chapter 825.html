Inside the farmhouse, Ethan sat with May in a warm living room. He raised his cup of tea and took a sip. May nibbled a cookie from the table.

Across from them sat the family who owned the farm. The freckled girl with pigtails they had met outside was Nicolette. The middle-aged couple beside her were her father, Christopher, and her mother, Jeannie. They looked like a close, happy family.

Despite their age and the work of managing a large farm, Christopher and Jeannie did not look weathered by years outdoors. They seemed younger than they were. Their home was warm, tidy, and well kept. The family appeared to be doing well.

“We haven't introduced ourselves,” Ethan said, setting down his tea. “I'm Ethan Vale, a Trainer traveling through Kanto. Sorry to turn up uninvited.”

“And I'm May,” May said quickly. “I'm a Pokémon Coordinator and Ethan's traveling companion. I want to become a Top Coordinator!”

“A Top Coordinator!” Christopher and Jeannie exchanged a look, then smiled at their daughter. “Nicolette wants to be one too, but…”

Ethan and May followed their gaze.

“But what?” May asked. “Anyone can become a Coordinator, can't they? Do you disapprove?”

“Of course not,” Christopher said. “We've often encouraged her to travel as a Coordinator and gather experience. It's just…”

Nicolette lowered her head, looking embarrassed and ashamed.

Ethan knew how this sort of encounter would unfold if Ash were here. The family would reveal its trouble, and Ash would step in to solve it. May had once traveled with Ash; almost instinctively, she asked, “Just what? Has something happened?”

“Nicolette's situation is complicated,” Jeannie said with a sigh. “We couldn't explain it in a few words.”

She seemed reluctant to say more, so May changed the subject. “What kind of farm is this?”

“You may have seen part of it on your way in,” Christopher said. “People entrust us with their Pokémon Eggs, and we care for them until they hatch.”

“Pokémon Eggs?” May knew Pokémon hatched from Eggs, but she had never seen one herself.

“That's right,” Jeannie said with a smile. “Would you like a tour?”

May looked at Ethan. When he nodded, she smiled eagerly at their hosts. “Yes, please! I've never seen a Pokémon Egg. I'd love to look.”

Christopher and Jeannie had farm work to do, so they asked Nicolette to show the visitors around. Nicolette was happy to have a legitimate excuse to escape her chores for a while.

She led Ethan and May out of the farmhouse. May fell a few steps behind and gave Ethan a questioning look. She had not traveled with him for long, but she knew he rarely spent time on such small detours. Why had he agreed to visit this farm?

She had a vague feeling that there was more to it, much like when they had visited Professor Oak's Laboratory. She had no evidence, yet it felt almost as though Ethan knew something in advance. On that earlier visit, she had received Squirtle. Could something similar happen here? Her heart beat a little faster.

The farm had an unusual layout. Four large storage buildings stood around a central tower, arranged like the blades of a windmill. The farmhouse was at their base. Perhaps the plan made it easier to oversee everything.

Nicolette led them into one of the storage buildings. Two long rows of shelves were filled with Pokémon Eggs.

The Eggs varied in size, color, and pattern. There were so many designs that May could not begin to tell what species they held. The sight was astonishing.

“Wow! So many Eggs!”

It was Ethan's first time seeing so many together too. He was impressed.

Nicolette rubbed her nose and smiled proudly. “Pretty amazing, isn't it? People have entrusted all of these to us. We take very good care of every Egg.”

Ethan approached a pink Egg with a pale ring around it. The room was warm, evidently for the Eggs' development. Each one had a numbered label for tracking and rested on a soft pad so it would not roll. Their shells were clean and smooth, as though wiped regularly. The family did seem attentive.

“What kind of Pokémon will hatch from them?” he asked.

Nicolette thought for a moment. “Pichu, Vulpix, Growlithe, Machop, Ponyta, Tauros… all sorts!”

Most appeared to be Eggs of common Kanto Pokémon. Ethan supposed that made sense. Owners of rare Eggs would usually raise them themselves unless they lacked the time or ability.

May stopped at another shelf. It also held Eggs, though fewer than the others. “Why aren't there as many over here?”

“Those belong to us,” Nicolette said with a shrug. “For one reason or another, some people abandoned the Eggs they left here, so we're raising them. Actually, you've come at a good time. Have you ever seen a Pokémon hatch? One of these Eggs should hatch soon. Would you like to stay and watch?”

Before May could answer, Ethan nodded. “If you don't mind, we'd love to.”

That settled May's suspicion. Ethan had some reason for bringing her here.

She was right. He had not stopped at the farm on a whim. In the anime story, May would receive her second Pokémon since coming to Kanto here. Ethan wanted to make sure she had that chance, which was why he was willing to spend time at the farm.

Even if May had guessed that he had a purpose, he could never tell her what he knew. He would keep it to himself.

Outside the storage building, Nicolette turned toward May with a determined look. “May, let's have a battle!”

“Huh?” May stared at the girl, who was about her own age. “Why all of a sudden?”

“Because of what my parents said inside. I want to prove I don't have to leave home to train. I can stay here and still become a great Coordinator!”

“I suppose you could train here,” May said, scratching her cheek. “Why not tell your parents how you feel?”

“I want to.” Nicolette hesitated and blushed. “But they won't accept it. They think I have to travel to learn about the world. If I battle you, I can show them what I can do.”

“Show your strength?” May nodded. “So you want one battle?”

“Yes!” Nicolette nodded hard. “You just won the Saffron Contest. If I can even tie with you, my parents will have to acknowledge my ability.”

May saw her confidence but was uncertain. She returned to Ethan and asked quietly, “What should I do?”

The family had offered them tea and cookies and given them a tour. It felt awkward to battle their daughter as guests. Winning might seem rude; losing might be just as unhelpful.

“Go ahead and battle seriously,” Ethan said. “She's overconfident. If you don't beat her, she may think winning a Contest is easy. I don't agree that she can learn everything by staying home. Helping her see that would do her parents a favor.”

“I understand.”

Ethan's advice decided the matter for May. She stepped back toward Nicolette and reached for a Poké Ball, then hesitated. Ethan wanted her to win, but how hard should she fight? Should she use Combusken at full strength, or give Nicolette more time against Squirtle? She decided to let Nicolette choose.

May took out three Poké Balls and released all three of her Pokémon. Combusken, Squirtle, and Munchlax appeared before Nicolette.

“What's this?” Nicolette asked.

“These are all the Pokémon I have with me,” May said, spreading her hands. “You want to battle, and I'm not sure which to use. You can pick my opponent for me.”

Another Coordinator might have taken that as an insult. Nicolette had never even left home to compete. She did not think of it that way and instead studied the three Pokémon carefully.

She had seen Combusken and Squirtle perform at the Saffron Contest. Battling either seemed risky. That left Munchlax. It sat drowsily, looking as though it had barely woken up. Nicolette pointed.

“I choose Munchlax!”

May had not expected that. Nicolette claimed she wanted to prove her strength, yet she had selected the least battle-tested of May's Pokémon. Perhaps she did not know Munchlax had never fought. But her careful choice and expression suggested she had picked it precisely because it looked weak.

“My Munchlax…” May began.

Nicolette was already releasing her own Pokémon. A Vileplume appeared before Munchlax. Perhaps afraid May would change her mind, Nicolette ordered an attack before Combusken and Squirtle had even moved away.

“Vileplume, show them a beautiful Petal Dance!”

Vileplume lowered itself, aimed the flower on its head, and sent out a cloud of pink petals. They struck Munchlax instantly. Munchlax had not even tried to dodge.

Vileplume was fully evolved and used the move with accuracy. Whatever Nicolette lacked in travel experience, her Pokémon had some strength.

The attack left Munchlax dazed. Worse, it became confused and began hopping about wildly. Perhaps because of that confusion, it raised both arms and used Metronome without being told.

Metronome could call up almost any known move at random. Even the Pokémon using it could not know what would happen. With luck, it might unleash a powerful attack. With bad luck, it might hurt itself. It was unpredictable and risky, especially when Munchlax was already confused.

Nicolette swallowed and grew cautious despite her early success. Come on, she thought. Show me the strength of the Saffron Contest winner.
