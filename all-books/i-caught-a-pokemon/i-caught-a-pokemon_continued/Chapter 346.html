Ethan slept straight through until after four in the afternoon.

When he woke, Chloe did not tell him what had happened to Goh. She was kindhearted and never made jokes at another person's expense. Besides, the incident had been nothing more than a minor episode to everyone else, but an undeniable disaster for Goh. There was no reason to build her own amusement on his suffering.

More than that, Goh was her childhood friend. She did not want his relationship with Ethan to become too strained.

In the end, however, Ethan still heard the whole story from Gengar.

After recounting what had happened, Gengar chuckled. "I wanted to teach that boastful kid a lesson too, but Gyarados beat me to it!"

Ethan did not think much of it.

So Goh had gotten a fright. What of it?

This was Goh, after all. No matter what he said or what even more outrageous thing he did, no one would be surprised. Nor did anyone expect much better from his behavior.

He could consider it a lesson for running his mouth.

Ethan passed the afternoon at a leisurely pace. After dinner, Chloe brought snacks to his dorm room again.

The two of them drank tea, ate, and chatted. Ethan picked out several interesting stories from his recent travels and showed her some of the photographs he had taken along the way. She did not reluctantly leave until eight that evening, after her mother called for the third time to urge her home.

Early the next morning, before Ethan had even eaten breakfast, Chloe appeared at the Cerise Laboratory once more.

Professor Cerise could only smile wryly.

This girl! She normally doesn't visit the laboratory even once a week, but she's been coming here awfully often these past two days!

After a night's rest, Ethan was back in top condition. He also knew he had already delayed too long. It was time to leave.

He said goodbye to Professor Cerise and the others, as well as a reluctant Chloe, then set out on his journey once more.

Before departing, however, he left Gyarados behind.

As he had said before, Gyarados had become so powerful that ordinary training could no longer improve it. It might as well remain at Cerise Park and give Ethan's other Pokémon more opportunities to grow.

...

North of Cerulean City, beyond the city limits, stood Nugget Bridge.

A famous pedestrian bridge, Nugget Bridge was known far and wide for its unusual construction, spherical decorations, sweeping view, and the golden light that gleamed from its surface beneath the sun.

After leaving Vermilion City, Ethan had come straight here.

After careful thought, he had decided that before leaving Kanto, he ought to find Mewtwo and strengthen their bond.

He could not remember exactly where Mewtwo was hiding, however. All he could do was search the surrounding area from memory and see whether luck would favor him.

As Ethan stepped onto the bridge and looked into the distance, the river flowed quietly beneath his feet, its surface sparkling with gentle ripples. Together with the lush green forest all around it, the water formed a beautiful scene.

It was wonderfully refreshing.

In high spirits, he casually took several photographs. He had only reached the middle of the bridge when several people suddenly ran over from the side and blocked his path.

A closer look revealed five young boys and girls.

The oldest among them was only fifteen or sixteen, while the youngest looked about seven or eight. Judging from their clothes, the group consisted of schoolgirls, a boy holding a bug net, and young boys wearing caps. Each had a Pokémon beside them, and all five smiled at Ethan with knowing expressions.

Ethan stopped and studied their faces one by one.

"What do you want?"

Their leader was a boy dressed in green from his cap to his trousers, with a red scarf tied around his neck. He looked Ethan over, then stepped forward.

"I'm Ethan. These are Ali, Timmy, Reli, and Cale. You look like a Trainer too, so battle us!"

His tone left no room for refusal. From the practiced way the group blocked the road and the ease with which he delivered his challenge, they had clearly stopped many people like this before.

Ethan Vale sighed and said calmly, "If I tell you I'm not in the mood for a battle, will you let me cross?"

"No way!" shouted Cale, the youngest of them, who carried the bug net. "If you're a Trainer, how can you back down from a battle? If you won't accept our challenge, don't even think about crossing this bridge!"

Ethan shook his head helplessly.

Although he had never met them before, encountering these five on this particular bridge stirred his old memories. He still had some impression of them.

In the games, these five had been typical sources of easy experience. He never imagined he would encounter them in the real world.

The thought flashed through his mind as he examined their Pokémon.

Sandslash, Persian, Arcanine, Golduck, and Venomoth. Every one of them was the final form of its evolutionary line.

It seemed there was no avoiding a battle.

At that moment, one of the schoolgirls seemed to notice something. She pointed at Ethan's face and cried, "Wait! I recognize him! Isn't he the Indigo League Champion?"

Only after hearing her did the others realize why Ethan looked so familiar.

Ethan's fame in Kanto had already risen to extraordinary heights, nearly matching that of the Elite Four. Even if people did not recognize him at first glance, a longer look was usually enough to remind them that he was the Indigo League Champion.

Advertisements and promotions featuring Ethan had been especially overwhelming during the finals, spreading across nearly all of Kanto.

"It really is him!"

The five exclaimed repeatedly and were about to say more, but Ethan had no desire to waste time talking. He immediately released Dragonair, Larvitar, and Scyther.

"If we're going to battle, all five of you can attack together. I'm in a hurry!"

With a wave of his hand, his three Pokémon charged.

Seeing Ethan launch his attack, the five Trainers hurriedly ordered their own Pokémon to fight back.

Bang! Bang! Bang!

Eight Pokémon clashed across the bridge. Fortunately, it had been built exceptionally well. Even after several minutes of battle, it had not suffered the slightest damage.

When the thick smoke from their moves slowly dispersed, all five opposing Pokémon lay on the ground. Of Ethan's three partners, only Larvitar had sustained a few minor injuries. Dragonair and Scyther were merely breathing a little harder.

Ethan gave a whistle and walked past the group without looking aside. His Pokémon quickly followed at his heels.

Staring after him, then at the five unconscious Pokémon, the green-capped boy said in disbelief, "S-so strong! Is this what a Champion can do? He defeated every one of us with only three Pokémon!"

The schoolgirl murmured, "I can't believe we got to battle a Champion! We were completely crushed, but no matter what, we can still say we fought him. People will be so impressed when they hear about this!"
