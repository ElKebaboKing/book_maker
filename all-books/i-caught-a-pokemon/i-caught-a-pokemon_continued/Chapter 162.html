The crowd looked toward the field. Logan had sent out Pidgeot, while Ethan had chosen Charizard.

One was a Normal- and Flying-type. The other was a Fire- and Flying-type. Neither held an obvious type advantage, and Logan felt a little relieved.

He was determined to win the Indigo League, but he knew Ethan would be hard to face. Starting their first blind matchup at a type disadvantage would make that goal even more difficult.

Ethan studied Pidgeot. Compared with Pidgeotto, its evolved form was much larger, with broader wings and a more striking crest.

It had powerful chest muscles. Spreading its wings could be enough to intimidate an enemy, and even a gentle flap stirred up a great wind. At full strength, its wingbeats could produce gusts strong enough to bend large trees.

Just as both sides were ready, something amusing happened.

The battle had not yet started, but Charizard snorted hot air through its nostrils and fixed Pidgeot with a fierce, wide-eyed glare. Its face twisted into a terrifying scowl.

After spending several days staring down Ash's Charizard, it had taught itself an enhanced version of Leer. This was no ordinary glare. Its expression was frightening, and the anger in its eyes made it look ready to swallow its opponent whole.

A camera caught the moment and put Charizard's face on the stadium's giant screens. The spectators burst out laughing.

"Well!" the commentator said. "Ethan's Charizard certainly looks fired up. It seems eager to get this battle started!"

The humans could laugh. Pidgeot was the one being stared at. For a moment, it almost broke into a cold sweat.

Was this Charizard out of its mind? Did it want to roast Pidgeot alive?

Uneasy, Pidgeot looked away.

At exactly ten o'clock, the final began.

Right from the start, Charizard attacked aggressively. Without even waiting for an order from Ethan, it spat flames and rushed toward Pidgeot.

Logan quickly ordered Pidgeot to evade and counterattack.

"Pidgeot, use Quick Attack!"

Pidgeot beat its wings and accelerated, dodging the flames before circling to Charizard's side.

But just as it closed in, Charizard turned its head and gave it a dreadful smile.

"Charizard, use Slash!"

At Ethan's command, Charizard raised a powerful claw and brought it down on Pidgeot. Pidgeot's Quick Attack still struck Charizard, but it could not dodge Slash. Both took damage and separated again almost at once.

The commentator turned to his guest. "Lance, which of these trainers do you think will win the first bout?"

Lance smiled. "That's difficult to say. Both Pokémon are excellent. It may come down to which of them is in better condition today."

How slippery, the host thought.

He had hoped to draw out a biased or controversial prediction and give people something to talk about. Lance had answered without leaving him a single opening. With nothing more to press, the host turned his attention back to the field.

After that first exchange, the two Pokémon fought as they flew around the arena. They dove, climbed, rolled, and wheeled through the air in close pursuit. They moved so quickly that spectators had to keep their eyes on the screens to follow every turn.

Pidgeot was Logan's opening choice for good reason. It would not be beaten easily. Wing Attack, Feather Dance, and Hurricane gave Charizard plenty of trouble.

Charizard answered with Flamethrower, Dragon Rage, and Air Slash. For a while, neither side could take control.

Then, as they crossed paths again, Ethan spotted his chance.

"Charizard, now! Use Fire Fang!"

Pidgeot had no time to react. Charizard's flaming teeth closed around it. Pidgeot cried out in pain, fell from the sky, and lost the ability to battle.

The crowd cheered.

Logan had expected Ethan to be difficult. Though he had lost the first bout, he had prepared himself for a setback. He recalled Pidgeot just before it struck the ground and took out his second Poké Ball.

"Go, Rhyhorn!"

A heavily armored Pokémon appeared, its shape somewhere between a rhinoceros and a horned dinosaur.

Taking the battle to the ground, then?

Ethan nodded to himself. Rhyhorn was a Ground- and Rock-type, so Charizard's Fire-type attacks would deal only half their usual damage. He recalled Charizard without hesitation.

After considering his choices, Ethan selected the Pokémon best suited to this matchup.

"Good work, Charizard. Gyarados, you're up!"

Gyarados emerged with a mighty roar and fixed its blood-red eyes on Rhyhorn. Sensing the threat, Rhyhorn scraped at the ground with its forefeet and grew restless.

Fans of Gyarados cheered even louder. Logan, meanwhile, inwardly cursed. He had to speak gently to Rhyhorn and calm it before fear threw it completely off balance.

"Both of Ethan's Pokémon have made quite an entrance with those sharp looks," the commentator joked. "I think he may have invented a whole new school of battling!"

Lance glanced at him and played along. "Oh? What school would that be?"

The commentator laughed. "The Menacing Stare School, of course!"
