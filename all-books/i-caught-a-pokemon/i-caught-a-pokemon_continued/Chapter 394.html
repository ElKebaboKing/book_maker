The wait passed in the blink of an eye. At exactly eight o'clock, the ceiling lights went out together, plunging the entire performance hall into darkness.

No one panicked, because everyone understood that the performance had officially begun!

With a soft click, a projector switched on and cast images of Lugia and Ho-Oh onto the curtain.

Under the audience's watchful gaze, the curtain slowly rose. As Lugia and Ho-Oh vanished, they revealed a brilliantly lit and lavish stage behind them. The next moment, grand and stirring music thundered through the hall!

Beneath the spotlight, golden light fell like rain. A young woman in an exquisite black kimono stood at the center of the stage in full costume and slowly began to dance.

Ethan rubbed his arms.

The instant the music began, goosebumps had risen across his skin.

The opening created an incredible atmosphere, and the music was beautiful. It had clearly been designed with great care!

He turned toward Erika and saw that she too had straightened in her seat. The opening appeared to have captured her interest as well.

Noticing his gaze, she nodded. "This performance really does seem worth looking forward to!"

"It certainly does."

Within that atmosphere, the woman raised a sleeve to cover the corner of her mouth and slowly began to speak.

"Long, long ago, the people of Ecruteak built two tall towers in this city. These two towers were known as—"

"The Bell Tower!"

As she spoke its name, the pillar to her left suddenly lit up. The image of a magnificent nine-story tower appeared upon it.

"And the Brass Tower!"

The moment her words fell, the pillar to her right lit up as well, revealing another soaring tower.

"One day, two Legendary Pokémon arrived here from a distant land: Ho-Oh, the rainbow-colored deity, and Lugia, the god of the sea..."

As the woman narrated, lights and projections formed one scene after another, gradually unfolding the legend before the audience.

"Ho-Oh descended upon the Bell Tower, while Lugia descended upon the Brass Tower. The people spoke with these two Pokémon, and a deep friendship formed between them. That friendship endured until one hundred and fifty years ago, on the fateful night when moonlight shone upon the towers."

As she turned, a Poké Ball somehow appeared in her hand. It opened, and an Umbreon emerged onto the stage.

The ring on Umbreon's forehead flashed. In an instant, a moon seemed to appear beneath the ceiling.

The lights dimmed and moonlight poured down. The atmosphere and music shifted with it. Everyone in the audience knew what was about to happen, and their hearts tightened in anticipation.

All eyes, including Ethan's, were drawn into the story. As it continued, the woman in black faded from view and another woman in a yellow kimono stepped out from the side.

Taking over the narration, the woman in yellow declared, "One hundred and fifty years ago, Lugia's peaceful existence was cruelly taken away!"

As she spoke, she also produced a Poké Ball. Light flashed, and a Jolteon appeared on the stage.

In time with the woman's words, a dazzling bolt of electricity burst from its body and illuminated the stage, just like the terrifying lightning from a century and a half ago!

"Thunder crashed and fierce winds howled as a violent storm engulfed all of Ecruteak City. Then, most terrible of all, a vicious bolt of lightning struck the Brass Tower..."

In the projected scene, the lightning set the Brass Tower ablaze in an instant. As the fire spread, more than half of Ecruteak City was swallowed by a sea of flames.

Next, a third woman in a red kimono rose onto the stage on a lift, accompanied by a Flareon.

She called Flareon forth and had it ignite flames across the stage, recreating the raging inferno shown in the projection.

The excellent voice acting and visual effects made the audience feel as though they were there, witnessing the disaster with their own eyes.

As the performance continued, all three women danced together. Every motion of their hands and feet was perfectly synchronized, a beautiful sight that revealed how many times they must have rehearsed.

From the side gallery, Ethan now understood that the colors of their outfits corresponded to the types of their respective Pokémon.

The woman in black represented Umbreon, the woman in yellow represented Jolteon, and the woman in red naturally represented Flareon.

The costumes had clearly been designed specifically to match their Pokémon.

At that moment, a fourth woman in a blue kimono entered from the other side of the stage while continuing the narration.

Her Vaporeon released a torrent of water like a downpour and extinguished the flames.

Finally, a girl in a pink kimono stepped out, and all five of the Eevee Sisters had appeared. They raised their fans and formed a single line. With delicate movements of their fans and light, measured steps, they carried the stage play to its climax!

When the girl in pink released Espeon, the story reached its conclusion.

Espeon used its power. The storm cleared on both the stage and in the projected story, and sunlight shone over the fire-ravaged city. Everything seemed to return to the way it had once been.

Everything except the destroyed Brass Tower.

"...And so, to ensure that the people would never forget what had been lost, and to prevent them from repeating the same mistakes, the ruins of the Brass Tower were preserved."

The five dancers raised small bells and shook them gently. When the ringing stopped, all five bent forward in a bow.

With that, the performance came to an end.

Thunderous applause erupted below the stage and continued without pause.

Ethan and Erika rose to applaud as well.

Although they had already learned the story, seeing it presented in this form was undeniably impressive. No wonder it had drawn even a monk to the audience.

As the curtain slowly descended, Ethan checked the time and discovered that an entire hour had passed without him noticing!

He had thought it lasted only twenty or thirty minutes.

The performance had truly been worth the visit.

After the show, Ethan and Erika followed the crowd out of the performance hall. In the corridor, they found another poster for the stage play.

This poster was different from the one outside. It showed five women in their finest costumes—the Eevee Sisters themselves!

They had been too far from the stage to see the performers' faces clearly. Looking at the poster now, Ethan saw that all five sisters were beautiful. Although each differed in age by a year or two, their features still bore a family resemblance.

The two also stopped to avoid the congested crowd. They waited for quite a while until the departing audience had thinned before preparing to leave.

But just as they started walking, an Eevee suddenly ran up from behind them.

At the same time, a girl in a pink kimono came hurrying after it.

She called out loudly, "Eevee, don't run off! Wait for me!"
