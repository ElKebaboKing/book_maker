Team Magma began climbing the mountain.

They had brought more than thirty people for the operation. That was scarcely more than the force they had once sent against Ethan, but this time every member was among Team Magma's best. They were far stronger than ordinary grunts.

One Elite Four member could hardly fight them all. With Maxie and two commanders present as well, Maxie had every reason to believe the operation would succeed.

They shared a destination, but split into three groups to keep Phoebe from escaping with the orb. Maxie, Courtney, and Tabitha each led one group toward the summit from a different direction. Special communicators kept all three leaders in contact. If one group encountered trouble, the other two could hear about it at once and come to its aid. They had prepared for every eventuality—or so they thought.

Groups this large could not climb as quietly as one or two people. Footsteps and voices carried through the mountain air. Maxie did not mind. He had never intended to hide such a force. Let Phoebe hear them and understand the consequences of opposing Team Magma. If she realized resistance was pointless, she might even hand over the orb and spare herself a beating, along with everyone else's time.

But she seemed unlikely to do that. Maxie was disappointed. Why can't people understand our great dream? He gave a cold smile and turned his attention to leading his group uphill.

At first, the climb went smoothly. Mt. Pyre made him uncomfortable, as if countless eyes were watching him. He had heard about the many Ghost-type Pokémon that harassed visitors here. A few delays would be acceptable if he obtained the Red Orb in the end.

Then the fog changed. It had once covered only the summit, but now it spread downhill. His group had barely reached the halfway point when thick mist swallowed them.

Maxie initially dismissed it as a shift in the weather. A succession of mishaps soon convinced him something in the fog was attacking them.

A huge rock rolled down from above and knocked one member's Pokémon off the mountainside. Before they recovered, a tree fell across their path, nearly crushing two Pokémon clearing the way.

And his group was not alone. Through the communicator, Maxie heard the other two groups reporting their own bizarre incidents. Alarmed shouts and confusion filled his earpiece.

Wild Ghost-type Pokémon had to be behind it. Nobody saw an attacker, and few other Pokémon could play tricks without leaving a trace. Yet Ghost types usually bothered people traveling alone. Why would they suddenly target three armed groups?

It felt as though someone had singled them out.

Uneasy, Maxie ordered his people to clear the fog around them. They blew it aside, only for more mist to flow immediately into the opening.

He frowned. The climb had become a real problem.

...

On the summit, Ethan lay in the soft grass, resting in the warm sun before the coming battle. Misty had settled beside him and was watching the clouds. Phoebe had disappeared after they agreed on their tasks; neither of them knew where she had hidden.

More than half an hour passed before Gengar returned.

The moment it appeared, Phoebe sprang from a distant tree. She swung and leaped between branches with the agility of a jungle explorer, then landed beside it.

“Well? Did you find Team Magma?” she demanded before Gengar could speak.

Gengar edged away from her eager face and smiled at Ethan, who had sat up. “I did. They're on their way.”

“How long until they get here?” Ethan asked.

“Hard to say.” Gengar laughed. “I asked some new friends to make a little trouble for them. They should be busy trying to deal with the pranks.”

“New friends? Who else is here?” Misty asked. Then she understood. “Oh, the wild Ghost-type Pokémon!”

“Of course. There are plenty of Duskull, Shuppet, Sableye, and Misdreavus on this mountain. They're a fun bunch. I told them what was happening, and they were delighted to help.”

Ethan shook his head with an amused smile. “Sending you was the right choice.” He looked at Misty and Phoebe. “The wild Pokémon won't stop Team Magma for long. Let's get ready to fight.”

Maxie, Courtney, and Tabitha eventually reunited near the summit. Each looked over the others' disheveled troops. The pranks had left the members miserable, though at least few had been lost along the way.

We should never have split up, Maxie thought. We didn't catch Phoebe trying to escape, and all we gained was this trouble.

The commanders seemed about to speak, but Maxie's face stopped them. He surveyed the force and snorted. “Don't stand around. Keep moving.”

The thirty-odd elite members advanced again under the protection of their Pokémon, filing through the narrow entrance to the summit. The climb had been troublesome, but they had made it. Defeat Phoebe, and the orb would be theirs.

Still, the accidents had cast a shadow over Maxie's confidence. Perhaps the rest of the operation would not be as simple as he had imagined. He narrowed his eyes and beckoned Tabitha over.

Tabitha looked puzzled. Maxie whispered a few instructions in his ear. The commander was visibly surprised, but nodded and left with several trusted members.

Courtney watched them go without comment. Maxie was the sharpest mind in Team Magma. He must have had a reason.

The others soon reached the fog-free part of the summit and saw Phoebe's grandparents' cabin in the distance. They searched it, found no orb, and continued to the stone shrine.

There, Maxie saw Phoebe waiting calmly in front of the shrine. His gaze moved past her to the Red Orb, shining softly inside.

It really is here! Delighted, he started forward. Phoebe stepped into his path.

“Team Magma, you don't belong here. Leave now, or don't blame me for what happens next!”

Maxie checked his surroundings. Phoebe appeared to be alone. He smiled instead of attacking. “Young lady, don't you understand? The world needs people like me to make it better. The oceans cover an enormous area while humanity crowds onto the land. If we awaken Groudon and bring about its Primal Reversion, the seas will dry and the land will expand. We'll have more room to live and can build an ideal world for humankind.”

Phoebe was young, but his obsessive vision did not impress her. “What nonsense. I may favor Ghost-type Pokémon, but even I know the ocean is the cradle of humanity. Our climate and ecosystems depend on it. If you did what you're proposing, we'd be the first to suffer.”

“So I can't persuade you.” Maxie shook his head in feigned regret. “I was going to offer you a chance to join Team Magma. But someone so shortsighted could never understand our great dream. Now give us the orb behind you.”

Phoebe answered by throwing Poké Balls. Six Ghost-type Pokémon appeared before Team Magma: Mismagius, Banette, Drifblim, Chandelure, Dusknoir, and Sableye.

Maxie looked them over. He had never dealt with Phoebe before, but he knew she was a Ghost-type specialist. Her team confirmed it.

“Do you really think those six can stop us just because you're one of the Elite Four?” he asked with a contemptuous snort.

The elite members behind him grinned and sent out their Pokémon. Blue flashes filled the clearing. In moments, sixty or seventy Pokémon surrounded Phoebe and her team.

Most Trainers would have panicked at the sight of so many hostile Pokémon ready to pounce. Phoebe did not hesitate. She attacked first.

Even Maxie had not expected that. Foolish girl. His members gave their own commands without waiting for him.

He did not expect to need Courtney's help, much less to fight himself. Surely their elite members could overwhelm her. At the start, Phoebe's stronger Pokémon knocked several opponents down. But the crowd closed around them, and they began to struggle.

There were simply too many foes. Team Magma could not easily withstand their attacks, but Phoebe's Pokémon could not defend against strikes coming from behind and both sides. The elite members' partners were strong, too. They were nothing like the wild Pokémon Phoebe might casually drive away. At this rate, her defeat seemed only a matter of time.

Yet the more the battle went Maxie's way, the more uneasy he felt. He studied the clearing and glanced at Courtney, who was also watching without taking part. He could see nothing wrong.

Perhaps I'm imagining it.

Two explosions sounded at once, one to either side. Several Team Magma Pokémon flew into the air with the blasts.

Maxie whipped his head around. More than a dozen Pokémon burst from the forest on both flanks and joined the battle.

An ambush. His heart jolted, then he felt relief. This was what had troubled him. They had caught him off guard, but now their hidden force had revealed itself. There was nothing else to fear.

Then he noticed Courtney's expression.

“What is it?”

She was staring past the newly arrived Pokémon at a familiar man. Her voice sank. “Now we're in trouble. That's Ethan Vale.”
