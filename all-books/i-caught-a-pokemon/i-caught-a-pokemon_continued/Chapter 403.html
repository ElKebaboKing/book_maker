Behind the folding screens lay a square tatami area ten meters across. The group removed their shoes and stepped onto the mats through the entrance. The first person they saw was a coolly beautiful woman in a black kimono, kneeling before them.

The woman appeared about the same age as Ethan and also wore a Poké Ball in her hair. Even without an introduction, that unusual ornament made it clear that she had to be one of the Eevee Sisters!

With this meeting, Ethan had now encountered all five sisters.

When she saw Ethan and the others enter, the woman performed a deep bow according to formal etiquette.

During an earlier conversation with Erika, Ethan had learned that this was a shin bow, the most formal bow used between host and guest. It required bending to ninety degrees and was generally reserved for tea ceremonies and other important occasions.

"Welcome to my tea room."

Erika returned the same formal bow. Ethan was uncomfortable with such etiquette and merely gave Zuki a solemn nod.

Naoko introduced her. "This is Zuki, my third-oldest sister."

She then told Zuki who Ethan and Erika were.

"Zuki, this is Mr. Ethan, and this is Miss Erika. One is the Indigo League Champion, and the other is a Gym Leader from Kanto!"

After learning that Ethan was a Champion, Naoko had also discovered from him that Erika was a Gym Leader!

The news delighted her. Who could have imagined that the two audience members she met at the Dance Theater were far from ordinary and would even become her friends?

Pokémon had become part of every aspect of life in this world, while Pokémon battling was its most popular form of competition. Gym Leaders and Champions associated with it naturally possessed considerable status. Becoming friends with people like them could hardly be a bad thing!

Of course, this did not mean that Naoko was snobbish or driven by vanity.

Given the five sisters' standing in Ecruteak City and the popularity of their performances, they had met many famous people since their debut. Even Morty, the Ecruteak Gym Leader, frequently came to watch their shows.

But those connections belonged to her older sisters, or else involved people drawn by the reputation of Ecruteak's Kimono Girls.

As the fourth of five sisters, Naoko was still young—only seventeen—and had not truly entered society. She usually did little beyond rehearsing and performing with her sisters. Ethan and Erika could therefore be considered the first friends she had genuinely made on her own!

"Miss Zuki, thank you for having us."

Ethan and Erika greeted her.

Zuki's gaze rested on Erika for a moment before finally moving to Ethan.

Like Naoko, she was clearly surprised by their identities.

"What brings the two of you to Johto?"

"We're traveling here and preparing to challenge the Silver Conference."

"I see."

After exchanging a few pleasantries, Zuki turned to the matter at hand.

"I will now prepare tea for you both."

Many tea utensils had been arranged beside her, along with a water jar kept warm over a low wood fire. She picked up a tea cloth and began wiping the tea caddy, scoop, and other utensils.

Seeing Ethan's curious expression, Erika realized that this was his first tea ceremony and explained quietly, "This is the usucha, or thin-tea, method. The ceremony begins with the formalities and purification of the utensils. Next, she will use a ladle to pour hot water into the tea bowls for the cleansing procedure. Only after that will she sift and add the tea, whisk it, serve it, and allow us to drink. At the end, we'll admire the bowls, and she will put away and clean the utensils before the ceremony closes with our thanks."

"That's rather complicated..."

Back on Earth, many particular people followed elaborate procedures when brewing tea. For Ethan, however, making tea simply meant adding leaves, pouring hot water into a cup, waiting for it to cool, and drinking it in large gulps.

As long as it quenched his thirst, it was good tea!

But a tea ceremony was clearly different from ordinary tea drinking. The tradition had been passed down for hundreds or even thousands of years, so a degree of complexity was only natural.

Seeing how familiar Erika was with every step, Ethan could not help asking, "Can you prepare tea like this too?"

"Of course." Erika nodded. "I occasionally hold tea ceremonies at my Gym. If you're interested, I can prepare a pot especially for you after we return to Kanto."

She truly came from a distinguished family and seemed accomplished at everything!

After marveling inwardly, Ethan said nothing more and quietly watched Zuki's every movement.

Guests were supposed to remain silent while observing the preparation of tea.

Although Ethan and Erika had spoken softly, the room was small enough that Zuki inevitably heard them. She said nothing and simply continued her work. Her movements were smooth and practiced, making it obvious that she had performed them countless times.

Ethan watched her carefully wipe the tea bowls, then use a ladle to pour hot water into them. Holding a bowl close with both hands, she performed the motions for cleansing the whisk before pouring the water into a waste-water vessel.

She then picked up the tea-powder caddy and used the scoop to measure the powder three times. After emptying it into the bowl, she leveled the powder with the scoop and tapped it once against the rim, producing a crisp sound.

The entire process was soothing to watch. Tea prepared by a beautiful woman was an especially pleasing sight.

Finally, Zuki whisked the tea in each bowl with steady force for a long time. She then presented the two bowls with both hands, placing the green tea before her guests.

"Thank you for waiting. You may now drink the tea."

Ethan looked down.

He had expected to drink from a teacup, but they were using bowls! Nor had Zuki used tea leaves, but a kind of powdered tea.

Inside the gray ceramic bowl, the green liquid was still slowly turning beneath a dense layer of tiny bubbles.

How could he describe it? The bowl did not resemble tea so much as water left after rinsing a brush. It was neither clear nor thick, though it did carry the distinct fragrance of tea leaves.

Rather than immediately lifting the bowl, Ethan looked toward Erika so he could first learn the proper way to drink.

Erika gave Zuki a small nod of thanks, then raised the tea bowl. She rotated it once before taking a gentle sip.

"Mmm..." Erika savored it for a moment, then smiled. "Both the technique and flavor are superb, and the water temperature is perfect. I've learned a great deal."

Following her example, Ethan lifted his bowl, rotated it once in his hands, and took a small sip.

The tea tasted sweet, leaving a lingering fragrance in his mouth. Unfortunately, he knew little about tea and could offer no real insight. He could only nod with false composure and pretend to be calm.

"Mmm... It truly is excellent tea."
