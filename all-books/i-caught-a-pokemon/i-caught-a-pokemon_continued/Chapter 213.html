"Lugia?!"

Ash and the others hurried to the side of the sailboat and searched the sea. They could see nothing.

Ash looked back at Melody. "Are you sure it was Lugia? Could you have heard something else?"

"It has to be," Melody said. "Lugia is the God of the Sea. The prophecy says it will appear when the world faces destruction and stop the fighting. Besides, what else in these waters could wield that kind of power?"

"I suppose so..."

The waterspout carried the sailboat several kilometers at remarkable speed, toward the coast on Shamouti Island's southern side. The God of Ice had turned the area into a white, frozen landscape. A journey that should have taken more than ten minutes took only three.

At the shore, the waterspout swung the boat up to an altar on the island's cliff.

Bang!

Snow flew as the sailboat came down near the cliff's edge, pitching everyone off their feet once more. Ethan rubbed his aching back. He had lost count of how often he had fallen today; after so much battering, even his bones felt sore.

They had barely begun to feel safe when the sailboat tipped backward. Everyone scrambled up and jumped clear just before it fell over the cliff.

Boom!

The boat that had carried them through so much finally broke apart below.

"That was close!" Ash lay on the snow and let out a long breath. When he stood and turned toward the altar, he found himself face to face with a pink Pokémon wearing a shell on its head.

"Huh? What's that?"

Ash took out his Pokédex and scanned the Pokémon he did not recognize.

"Slowking, the Royal Pokémon. Water and Psychic type. It evolves from a Slowpoke holding a King's Rock. Slowking is exceptionally wise and perceptive, and stays calm no matter what happens. It spends each day puzzling over the mysteries of the world, but if the Shellder on its head is removed, it forgets everything it has learned."

"Slowking?"

It was the first one Ash and his friends had seen. Slowpoke and Slowbro could be found around many bays, but Slowking were rare, whether in the wild or in a Trainer's care.

The Pokémon stood with its hands behind its back, like a kindly grandfather, and regarded them. No one, Ethan included, had noticed it arrive. It was as though it had been standing at the center of the altar all along, silently watching. Wild Pokémon usually kept their distance from people to avoid being caught; this one seemed entirely at ease.

Before anyone spoke, Slowking raised a hand and pointed toward a stone tablet in the altar.

"Place the treasures there."

"It talked!" Tracey pointed at it. "That Slowking can speak human language?"

They had encountered talking Pokémon before—Team Rocket's Meowth and Ethan's Haunter, for example. They were still rare enough that no one had expected to find another one here.

Melody thought for a moment. "Carol once told me there was an unusual Slowking on Shamouti Island, but I'd never seen it. So this is where it's been living."

Ash was still staring. Slowking repeated, "Place the treasures there."

"Oh. Right." Ash went to the tablet and set the orbs from Fire Island and Lightning Island into a recess.

Slowking stepped closer. Seeing only two, it shook its head. "Two? That won't do. The ceremony cannot be completed without all three treasures."

While they spoke, Ethan studied the area. The altar stood on the opposite side of Shamouti Island from the town, facing the three smaller islands. Built entirely of rock, with hibiscus growing nearby, it looked ancient.

He turned to say something to Sabrina and found her watching Slowking with interest.

"Thinking of catching it?" he asked.

She nodded. "It's a rare Psychic-type Pokémon."

Ethan understood why she wanted it. As a Gym Leader traveling for so long, she could use another Pokémon on her team. But he could not remember exactly what part this Slowking played in the story, so he leaned close and whispered, "The timing's wrong. Let's leave it alone for now. When this is over, you can try to catch it."

Slowking turned toward them as soon as he finished speaking. It looked into their eyes and felt a sudden, inexplicable unease about the pair.

Ethan cleared his throat. To keep Slowking from growing suspicious and fleeing, he led Sabrina a little farther away.

Only then did he notice how quiet it was. He looked around. Team Rocket had vanished. They must have used the confusion after landing to slip away.

Those three never miss a chance.

Over Lightning Island, the God of Thunder, the God of Fire, and the God of Ice continued to fight. Somehow their battle had drifted closer to Shamouti Island.

Everyone frowned. If the birds came any nearer, their attacks would reach the town. The people living there would be in danger, and those at the altar would be caught in the fighting first.

Just then, the waterspout appeared again. It burst through the ice and rose in the birds' path, forcing all three back.

The waterspout broke apart into a spray of droplets. High above them, a strange white Pokémon emerged.
