“Ladies and gentlemen, the Saffron Pokémon Contest has reached its final battle! After round upon round of competition, two Coordinators will now face each other for the Saffron Ribbon. Please welcome May and the fairy Jessibella!”

At Lilian's introduction, May and Jessie returned to the stage amid the crowd's cheers. Both had performed better with each successive match. Their exciting battles had won over the audience, and the hall rang with applause.

For Jessie especially, this was a rare moment of public admiration. She held her head high, smiling and waving to the spectators. She could hardly have looked more pleased with herself.

Then she looked across at her opponent and jumped. It was the girl who had traveled with the twerp in Hoenn!

Wait. How had that girl ended up here?

Careless as ever, Jessie had not paid attention to the identities of the other entrants, even as they took the same stage. She only recognized May when they came face to face in the final.

In the stands, James and Meowth sighed at her reaction.

“Jessie finally noticed who she's up against, meow.”

“Yeah,” James said, spreading his hands. “That girl's no pushover. Jessie won't beat her easily.”

“It's not the girl I'm worried about.” Meowth searched the audience. “You saw her traveling with that man last time. If she's here, then…”

He did not finish, but sweat broke out on James's forehead. “You mean that man is nearby?”

“He has to be, meow. Even the Boss finds him troublesome. I don't want him noticing us.”

“What do we do?” James asked.

Meowth thought for a moment, then snapped his claws. “Plan B, meow!”

“Plan B? Since when do we have a Plan B?”

“Since now, meow!”

“That's a little sudden. What is Plan B?”

Rather than answer, Meowth asked, “Have you forgotten what we're here for?”

James blinked innocently. “To help Jessie enter the Contest?”

“Idiot!” Meowth scratched James across the face in frustration. “We're here to capture Pokémon for the Boss, meow!”

James fell with a cry, then scrambled up again. Holding his face, he said, “Oh, that. Of course I knew.”

The final battle had begun. May had chosen Squirtle again. Jessie had sent out Wobbuffet, the trio's unofficial fourth member.

In the second round, Jessie had won her battles by using Wobbuffet's Counter and Mirror Coat to send her opponents' attacks back at them. The method was simple, but it had defeated two competitors. Both had helped her by stubbornly attacking head-on.

May had apparently studied those matches. She did not rush into an attack. Instead, Squirtle kept moving, searching for an opening in Wobbuffet's defenses.

Meowth watched the field intently. “Jessie will probably lose if this keeps up. We should get ready now, meow.”

“Ready for what?” James asked.

“See that man?” Meowth pointed with one claw.

James looked toward a shadowed part of the hall. Harley, whom they had met earlier, leaned against a pillar and watched the battle.

“Him? What about him?”

Meowth grinned. “He wants Jessie to get revenge for him. We ought to collect a fee, meow.”

James's eyes lit up. “You mean…?”

“Exactly. He placed second in the first round, so his Pokémon must be strong. We can take them to the Boss.”

In the corner, Harley stood with his arms crossed, glaring at May. He had lost through carelessness. If he had finished Squirtle promptly, the battle might have gone differently. He regretted his mistake, but the result was settled.

Jessibella was his last hope of seeing May humbled. Come on, then, he thought. Show me what you can do. Don't disappoint me.

A sly smile crossed his face. Then a man and a Pokémon appeared before him.

He focused and recognized Jessibella's assistant and companion. “You two? What do you want?”

James rubbed his hands together and smiled awkwardly. “Well, you've seen how troublesome that girl is. The great Jessibella might need some of your strength to beat her.”

Harley frowned more deeply. “My strength? What do you mean?”

“Your Pokémon, of course!” Meowth said.

“But the battle has started. She can't change Pokémon now. And I've been eliminated. How could I lend her mine?”

James gave him a brilliant smile. “We have a special technique for summoning Pokémon to help during a battle. It's Jessibella's signature move. It can turn a match into two against one—or even more!”

“Oh?” Harley's eyes brightened.

He had never dealt with Team Rocket before. He did not know these two were Pokémon thieves and still believed Jessibella was a famous Coordinator. He had no reason to suspect them. Besides, Harley often used tricks himself, so the proposed technique did not trouble him.

Still, he asked cautiously, “Why do you need my Pokémon?”

“We wouldn't bother you if we had a choice,” James said. “Jessibella didn't bring many Pokémon on this trip.”

Harley hesitated. Jessibella seemed famous, had two assistants, and had topped the first-round rankings. Surely someone like that would not deceive him.

“All right. Which Pokémon do you need?”

James and Meowth could barely contain their delight, but they kept smiling calmly. “If possible, could we borrow Cacturne, which you used in the appeal round, and Ariados, which you used for battle?”

Without further argument, Harley took out two Poké Balls and handed them over. James and Meowth exchanged gleeful looks, then carefully composed themselves.

“With your Pokémon, Jessibella is certain to win,” James said gravely. “Thank you. We'll leave you to enjoy the match. Look forward to the good news!”

Before Harley could answer, they hurried away. He assumed the Contest's tight schedule required them to move fast and turned back toward the stage.

The battle was going badly for Jessibella. Squirtle was avoiding a direct attack on Wobbuffet and wearing down its score by other means. No wonder her assistants had come to him.

Harley narrowed his eyes at May. With my help, he thought, you'll be humiliated.

After deceiving May so many times, Harley had been deceived by Team Rocket. They had no intention of helping Jessie defeat May. They simply wanted his Pokémon, while he foolishly believed his own scheme had succeeded. Even a clever trickster could fall for someone else's trick.

As Harley waited, the final battle entered its last minute. May continued directing Squirtle around Wobbuffet, trying different ways to attack.

She still had not recognized Jessie, but watching Wobbuffet's two earlier battles had taught her how it fought. It could not launch an attack of its own. That was typical of Wobbuffet. The purple-haired woman's familiar voice and manner nagged at May, but she had not connected the clues.

Squirtle could not easily defeat Wobbuffet outright. Its steady pressure had, however, reduced Wobbuffet's Contest score to a dangerous level. Squirtle's score had barely fallen. If the clock ran out, May would win even without knocking Wobbuffet out.

Jessie was growing frantic. No matter what she ordered, Wobbuffet stood there looking blank and harmless. It had no way to take points from Squirtle without first receiving an attack.

Harley was getting anxious too. He kept waiting for Jessibella to use the technique her assistants had described. She made no such move. What was she doing? There was almost no time left.

At that moment, every light in the Saffron Contest Hall went out. Darkness swallowed the room. The battle stopped, and spectators looked around in confusion, talking among themselves.

“Huh?” Lilian's voice echoed through her microphone. “What's going on?”

A spotlight suddenly came on, illuminating Jessie in the middle of the stage.

Jessie looked around, just as puzzled as everyone else. Two figures leaped to her side. Music started up from somewhere.

Under the light, the crowd saw a man in a white uniform and a Meowth standing upright beside Jessibella. James and Meowth had shed their disguises.

The audience had no idea what this interruption meant. Neither did Jessie. She stared at her partners. “What are you doing here?”

Meowth ignored her and laughed toward the stands. “I heard someone ask, ‘What's going on?’”

James leaned back against him and raised a fist. “From beyond the far horizon!”

Almost by reflex, Jessie tore off her disguise, jumped beside James, and leaned against him. “From the other side of the great thunderbolt!”

“A voice is calling us!”

“Thanks for waiting, meow!”

“The brave, blooming flowers of evil!”

“The lovely, tough villains!”

“Jessie!”

“James!”

“And Meowth, that's me! Meow!”

“Wherever Team Rocket goes—”

“The world!”

“The universe!”

“Waits for us!”

“Wobbuffet!”

Only after finishing the entire routine did Jessie come to her senses. She turned on her partners. “I'm asking you! What are you two doing up here?”
