Violet City was a densely populated metropolis. At this hour, many people were entering and leaving the city, and when passersby noticed a Pokémon battle taking place, they stopped to watch with curiosity.

It was like seeing street basketball, dancing, or singing back on Earth. Whether people understood it or enjoyed it, they would still glance over, even while hurrying past. Almost no one could walk by without looking.

Pokémon battles were also among the most popular sports in this world, making it even harder for people to resist their curiosity.

Within moments, the crowd grew to several dozen people. Someone soon recognized the Week Siblings. The seven brothers and sisters had traveled through Johto for years. Their skill varied, but they had defeated many Trainers, while countless others had taken badges from them. They were quite famous.

The spectators discussed them animatedly and studied the opponent standing across from the Week Siblings, wondering who might take another badge from the family today.

That scrutiny quickly led someone who had watched the Indigo League Conference to recognize Ethan.

The discovery first prompted a cry of surprise. When nearby spectators asked what had happened, the news spread from one person to another, then from two to ten. Exclamations of "The Champion!" and "That's him!" rippled through the crowd.

Hearing the commotion, Erika turned and saw people stirring excitedly. Some had even taken out their phones and begun recording.

She shook her head and returned her attention to the battle.

Both Ethan and Frieda had received this kind of attention more than once. Neither reacted to the spectators. They focused on the battle and ignored everything happening beyond the field.

Frieda knew that her type matchup was unfavorable, so she planned to seize the initiative and turn the situation around.

As soon as Arthur signaled the start of the battle, she issued her first command without hesitation.

"Slugma, use Ember!"

Slugma's throat moved. In the next instant, several blazing flames erupted from its mouth.

Larvitar watched the approaching Ember and braced itself, waiting for Ethan's command. When no instruction came even after a moment, it looked back in confusion.

That glance revealed Ethan staring blankly ahead, entirely unmoved by the approaching flames. His mind was clearly elsewhere.

Larvitar's cool little face immediately fell.

Huh? What's going on!?

The flames grew larger in Ethan's vision as they closed in on Larvitar.

Looking at that small burst of fire, Ethan had momentarily fallen into a rare daze.

It was difficult to blame him for losing focus during the battle. Only a few days earlier, he had witnessed a battle between the three Legendary beasts and the three Legendary birds. After growing accustomed to a clash that seemed to tear heaven and earth apart, the contrast made it difficult to adjust to such a minor skirmish.

When had he last seen Ember used?

It felt so long ago that he could no longer remember clearly.

His distraction lasted only an instant. Fortunately, he recovered in time. Before the flames reached Larvitar, he finally gave a command.

"Larvitar, dodge it, then use Rock Throw!"

Larvitar had already prepared to withstand the attack. Hearing Ethan's order, it hurriedly leaped to the side. The dodge came a little too late, however, and the flames still swept over most of its body.

Fortunately, Larvitar resisted Fire-type moves. Although the flames appeared to engulf it, the attack inflicted little actual damage.

After this small mishap, Ethan sharpened his focus. Under his direction, Larvitar soon engaged Slugma in earnest.

As the two Pokémon exchanged attacks, it became clear that Slugma possessed some strength. Its speed was simply far too low, however, and its type disadvantage left it unable to accomplish very much.

Under Larvitar's constant attacks, Slugma's stamina soon neared its limit. Ethan decided to press the advantage and finish it in one push.

"Larvitar, use Bite!"

Larvitar opened its large mouth and bit into Slugma, only to release it abruptly in the next instant.

Ethan looked carefully and saw thin wisps of white smoke rising from Larvitar's mouth. It had clearly been burned.

Oh?

He immediately realized that this Slugma possessed the Flame Body Ability.

When a Pokémon with Flame Body was struck by a move that made physical contact, there was a chance that its attacker would be burned.

But what a coincidence!

Ethan could not help laughing.

Larvitar's Ability was Guts. When a Pokémon with Guts suffered from a status condition other than fainting, the power of its attacks increased, while the harmful effects of a burn were greatly reduced.

Frieda truly was both miserable and unlucky.

From the moment the Pokémon were sent out, Ethan had countered her in both type and Ability.

It was an extraordinary coincidence. The very outcome she had feared most was exactly what she received.

Under such conditions, the result went without saying. Larvitar defeated Slugma with ease.

When Slugma fell, Frieda recalled it into its Poké Ball with an unwilling expression and returned to Arthur's side.

She had been suppressed from beginning to end. It felt as though she had been defeated effortlessly before displaying even half her strength. How could she possibly accept that?

Not only had she failed to avenge her older brother and sister, but she had also lost herself. A fire burned inside her with nowhere to go, leaving the frustration trapped in her chest.

Arthur had no time to comfort his younger sister. He immediately raised his right hand.

"Larvitar wins this battle!"

At the result, quiet discussion spread through the surrounding crowd.

This was different from what they had imagined. Both competitors were famous, so the spectators had expected an intense, closely contested battle rather than such a one-sided affair.

Arthur patted Frieda on the shoulder, then stepped forward to stand opposite Ethan. Frieda took his place as the temporary referee.

Pushing up his glasses, Arthur said solemnly, "My sister was unlucky in that last battle. Your type completely countered hers. But you won't be so fortunate in the next one!"

Ethan shrugged without answering. He treated Arthur's declaration as nothing more than a customary show of confidence.

He first praised Larvitar, then recalled it. He watched Arthur quietly and waited for him to produce a Poké Ball.

Arthur did not keep him waiting for long. After a brief moment of thought, a confident smile appeared at the corner of his mouth, and he took a Poké Ball from inside his clothes.

Once both Trainers were ready, Frieda gave the signal and the second battle began.

But as Arthur threw his Poké Ball, he was astonished to discover that Ethan had not switched Pokémon at all. Ethan sent out Larvitar again!

Arthur's eyes widened in disbelief, and his expression immediately darkened.

Ethan watched Arthur's face pass through two or three expressions in an instant and had no idea what was wrong. Then two streaks of blue light flashed. Once he saw Arthur's Pokémon, Ethan could not help laughing again.

Arthur had sent out an Electabuzz.

By sheer coincidence, the Electric-type Electabuzz was just like Slugma: Larvitar's Rock and Ground typing countered it once again!

Arthur must have believed himself clever. Knowing that Ethan excelled at exploiting type matchups, he had assumed Ethan would never use Larvitar again after its previous battle. Instead, Arthur's cleverness had worked against him. Ethan's simple decision to stick with the same Pokémon had defeated his entire plan!
