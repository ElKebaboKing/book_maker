Ethan's arrival caught the Scyther group's attention. Led by the one with the scar, they turned hostile eyes on him. As he looked around, they seemed ready to rush forward and tear him apart.

"Easy, easy." He waved a hand to calm them, then fixed his gaze on the scarred Scyther. "Let me introduce myself. I'm Ethan, a Trainer who travels around. I've come with bad intentions—uh, I mean, good intentions. First, congratulations on becoming the leader. There's also something important I'd like to discuss with you."

At another time, entering Scyther territory so boldly would have brought the whole group down on him. But their new leader had just won its duel. It was riding a wave of confidence, and a challenge now would give it a chance to establish its authority. Ethan had realized that, which was why he dared approach.

Sure enough, the scarred Scyther barked at its companions to stand back. It stepped forward and stared hard at Ethan.

"Scyther, Scyther!"

Ethan raised an eyebrow. He could not understand Pokémon speech, but fortunately he had an interpreter close by.

"Hey, Haunter. Stop sleeping and come do some work. What did it say?"

Haunter drifted lazily out of Ethan's shadow. "You only think of me when you need something." It rolled its eyes, but Ethan urged it on. "Fine. It said, 'You sneaky human. Get off my turf before I lose my temper, or one word from me and my boys will cut you in half!'"

Ethan hesitated and looked at Haunter. "Are you sure those were its exact words? It only made a few sounds. And even if Scyther are fierce, I doubt one talks like a street thug."

"What's so hard to understand?" Haunter spread its hands. "What did you expect from a Scyther? All muscle, no brains. I polished it up and filled in the context. The wording might be rough, but the meaning's about right. Don't worry about such a small detail."

Ethan stared at it. That explained the swagger. He had wondered why a Scyther living far from humans would call the others its "boys." Haunter had added its own flourishes.

After a long pause, Ethan sighed. "I see. Thank you. How thoughtful of you."

"You think so?" Haunter looked pleased. "I think so too!"

The Scyther in front of them was growing impatient. It rubbed its sharp forearms together, making a clack like blades striking. Ethan shook his head, ignored Haunter, and turned back to it.

"What I wanted to discuss is a partnership between strong allies. We could take on the Pokémon League together. Interested in defeating powerful opponents and making it to the very top?"

Scyther tilted its head. With its limited experience, it knew nothing of the Pokémon League and had no idea why Ethan suddenly wanted to climb a mountain.

Ethan reconsidered. "Let me put it another way. I challenge you to a battle. If I lose, I'll give you human food. If you lose, you'll become my partner and travel with me. How about it? Do you accept?"

That, Scyther understood. It looked down on Ethan. This human thought he could challenge it? How foolish.

It gave a loud cry. The other Scyther immediately moved aside, clearing a wide space. Ethan needed no translation this time. Just as he had expected, it had accepted.

He was about to take out a Poké Ball when something came to mind. He glanced toward the forest's edge. The Scyther defeated in the earlier duel had staggered almost to the trees.

As Ethan watched it go, he wondered whether this wounded Scyther might be the one Ash and his friends would encounter on their journey, the one Tracey would eventually catch. The more he thought about it, the likelier it seemed. There could hardly be so many coincidences.

But it had nothing to do with him, and he had no time to pursue it. He returned his attention to the scarred leader, took out the Poké Ball he had already chosen, and threw it into the clearing.

"I choose you! Go, Crystal Onix!"

Blue light flashed, and Crystal Onix appeared. It was even larger than Gyarados, though its entrance was calm rather than imposing.

This was its first formal battle since Ethan had caught it. In the meantime, it had trained and fought in private for a long while, so he was confident in its strength.

Most of all, he had the type advantage. Ground-type moves could not hurt Scyther, but Rock-type attacks would deal four times the usual damage. That put Crystal Onix in a strong position.

The sight of such a huge opponent did not make Scyther retreat. It roared and charged first. Even against a powerful foe, it was eager to fight.

Scyther moved fast. It closed the distance in a single rush, raised its gleaming scythes, and swung hard at Crystal Onix. Ethan recognized the Normal-type move Slash.

"Crystal Onix, don't let it intimidate you. Use Harden!"

A sheen passed over Crystal Onix's body. Every part of its surface grew tougher.

Clang!

Scyther's blade struck with a metallic ring. Crystal Onix did not even have a scratch. Its stone-like body truly was hard. Onix were naturally well defended, and Harden only made that advantage greater. The impact left both of Scyther's forearms numb.
