When Ethan first faced a Legendary Pokémon, he had needed to rely on superior numbers and fight with everything he possessed for even a chance at defeating one. The situation was entirely different now. He had the confidence to confront them head-on.

Why had he spent his entire journey constantly strengthening his Pokémon and expanding his team?

He had done it to remain undefeated in competition and leave no regrets in his career. He had also done it so that when a situation like this arose, he would have the power to respond.

Now, he faced powerful opponents with no one else nearby. There was no reason to hold back. He could send out his strongest cards.

Without hesitation, Ethan reached into the bag at his waist and took out three Great Balls.

It was time for them to take the field.

Come on. Let Johto's Legendary beasts witness the power of Kanto's three Legendary birds!

The three Great Balls trembled gently in his palm. The Pokémon inside seemed to be telling their Trainer that they had waited long enough and could barely restrain themselves any longer.

Ethan threw the Great Balls into the sky and shouted, "Come out, Zapdos, Articuno, Moltres!!!"

Blue light flashed, and three enormous avian Pokémon appeared in midair. They spread their wings, climbed higher, and soared beneath the open sky.

The three Legendary birds released piercing cries as they circled overhead. Their arrival seemed to transform the previously clear sky.

A wind had risen, and it carried danger...

Entei and Suicune looked up at the three Legendary birds with solemn expressions.

They sensed an aura and majesty similar to their own from the three Pokémon above.

Neither wanted to battle such dangerous opponents. They had only come to investigate the disturbance and had no desire to become entangled in a meaningless fight.

But it was already too late for them to leave.

Ethan would never have sent out the three Legendary birds if he intended to let them escape easily.

"Go! Defeat them!"

At Ethan's command, the three Legendary birds charged directly toward Entei and Suicune.

Faced with three fierce birds rushing toward them, Entei and Suicune had no reason to stand and fight.

The two Legendary beasts fled in opposite directions, trying to leave. But no matter how fast creatures on the ground could move, how could they escape hunters in the air?

Entei and Suicune were soon pinned down and forced to fight.

By then, Gengar, Gardevoir, and Charizard had returned to Ethan's side, leaving the battlefield to the Legendary Pokémon.

Ethan first recalled Charizard so it could recover inside its Poké Ball. Then, protected by Gardevoir and Gengar, he retreated more than ten meters and established a safe distance.

He had no intention of involving his ordinary Pokémon in this battle. In fact, he had already decided that if Entei and Suicune escaped despite being surrounded by the three Legendary birds, it would prove that his own skill was insufficient and the two Pokémon were not destined to join him. He would not send out the rest of his team to continue chasing them.

He might lose a rare opportunity, but fighting evenly matched opponents would still provide excellent training for the three Legendary birds. The encounter would not be entirely wasted.

Of course, if nothing unexpected occurred, Ethan was highly confident that he could defeat Entei and Suicune.

Turning his attention back to the battle, he could see that just as Entei and Suicune had sensed the birds' strength, the three Legendary birds recognized the power of the two beasts. Entei and Suicune also understood that the battle was unavoidable. From the moment they clashed, neither side held anything back.

A continuous series of explosions thundered across the sky.

The five Legendary Pokémon fought in a tangled melee. Their attacks possessed such power and range that the entire crystal tower and the surrounding crystal forest became their battlefield.

For the moment, Ethan did not intend to direct the battle. He allowed his Pokémon to act freely, making this a true contest of raw strength.

Lightning and fire intertwined. Water spread across the ground, while snowflakes drifted through the air only to melt in the waves of heat before they could land.

Compared with battles between ordinary Pokémon, a clash involving the Legendary birds and Legendary beasts was far more dangerous and uncertain.

The danger came from more than their overwhelming individual strength. Every one of these Legendary Pokémon possessed unusual natural traits.

Moltres and Entei, for example, dried the surrounding air whenever they beat their wings or drew breath, making fires far more likely to start.

Articuno chilled the area around it and could easily produce a blizzard. Wherever Suicune passed, the ground became damp and muddy as moisture filled the air.

Even Zapdos caused nearby living creatures to build up static electricity, just as its feathers did.

Boom! Boom! Boom-boom-boom!

The battle and explosions outside soon caught the attention of Ash and the others inside the crystal tower.

They were still struggling against the false Entei when the disturbance beyond the walls made them stop in surprise.

"What's happening!? Where are those explosions coming from!?"

Brock looked up at the ceiling in astonishment, but the crystal tower was completely sealed and revealed nothing.

Of the three, Misty understood Ethan best. Seeing the confusion on Ash's and Brock's faces, she said with certainty, "Isn't it obvious? Ethan must be battling some Pokémon out there!"

"Ethan?" Ash murmured. "What kind of Pokémon could he possibly be fighting...?"

Two battles now raged simultaneously. Ethan noticed nothing unusual outside, but everyone within the tower, including the false Entei and Molly, became distracted to some degree.

Outside, the battle continued to intensify.

The entire crystal tower shook under the relentless attacks. The sky soon changed color as dark clouds piled on top of one another. The shifting air currents and moisture from the battle appeared to be creating a terrifying thunderstorm overhead.

At last, a deafening crack split the air. A thick bolt of lightning struck one of the crystal tower's spires and shattered it.

Countless fragments of crystal rained from above, finally blasting Raikou out of hiding.

To avoid the falling debris, Raikou had no choice but to leap from its concealed position and onto the battlefield.

Looking at the Pokémon that resembled both a leopard and a tiger, Ethan wore an unsurprised expression.

You couldn't stay hidden forever!

With Raikou revealing itself, all three Legendary beasts had truly gathered at last.
