In the valley, the battle to catch Moltres had begun. Lorelei and Sabrina, meanwhile, had reached the middle of Mt. Ember.

They knew the route from their climb, and going downhill was much faster. Yet the mood between them was nothing like it had been on the way up. They walked side by side in heavy silence.

Sabrina had little desire to talk. Lorelei was still annoyed at being sent away. They had no obvious subject in common, and neither had spoken since leaving the valley.

Sabrina did not mind. With her quiet nature, she found the unhurried walk pleasant rather than awkward.

Boom!

Lightning cracked behind them. Lorelei turned. Thick storm clouds had gathered over the summit, and bolts flashed within them.

Another bolt struck the peak. The force shook the mountain. Lorelei lost her footing and nearly fell, catching herself on a rock before Sabrina could see her stumble.

She straightened and studied the clouds, her expression grave. That was an enormous storm, and a Thunder attack of astonishing power. What kind of Pokémon could cause such a change in the sky?

Then snowflakes drifted out of the clouds and fell around them. Lorelei caught one. Even in this heat, it felt cold in her hand.

Was that Blizzard? The clouds had formed from both the lightning and the snowstorm. No wonder they held such power.

As an Ice-type specialist, Lorelei knew an ordinary Pokémon could not send a Blizzard this far through such hot air. Did Ethan own a legendary Pokémon?

Several possibilities crossed her mind, but none explained everything she was seeing.

Sabrina watched the storm calmly. There was no surprise in her eyes, only open admiration.

Fire flared within the clouds as well. It twisted and darted through them, but could not escape. Several different bird calls reached them faintly from above.

"That fight must be fierce," Lorelei said. "Don't you want to see it?"

She hoped to stir Sabrina's curiosity. Sabrina gave her a cool glance. "Do you want to go back?"

Of course she did. The answer nearly escaped Lorelei before she swallowed it.

She was one of the Elite Four and had earned her place through years of battling. Naturally, she wanted to know how Ethan intended to catch Moltres. She could not understand Sabrina's lack of interest.

"You hardly ever get to see something like this. Aren't you curious how he'll catch it?"

"No. However unusual Moltres is, it's still a Pokémon. I don't need to watch."

Lorelei almost wanted to ask the League to be more careful when appointing Gym Leaders. Could they at least choose people who cared about Pokémon battles?

Another tremor interrupted her thoughts. This one was far stronger. Before she could see what had happened, an earthshaking blast sounded and the whole mountain began to sway.

The crater, quiet for centuries, erupted. A towering column of molten rock shot into the sky.

The battle had set off the volcano?

The eruption tore through the clouds. Lava fell back to the mountainside and began flowing downhill like a slow red wave.

"Ethan's in danger!" Lorelei said. "The valley is close to the crater. That eruption could have caught him!"

Sabrina showed no sign of panic. Lorelei stared at her. "Why are you still standing here? Don't you care what happens to him?"

"He's always been lucky. He won't die that easily." Sabrina looked toward the summit. "See for yourself."

Lorelei followed her gaze. A dark speck was moving through the sky toward them. As it drew close, she saw Ethan, unharmed, riding Charizard.

"Miss Lorelei," Sabrina said.

Her suddenly formal tone caught Lorelei off guard. "What is it?"

"You should hurry down. If you wait, you might not make it in time."

Why was she telling only Lorelei to go? "What about you? Are you waiting here for Ethan?"

Sabrina merely looked at her. Before Lorelei could ask again, a shadow swept past. Ethan caught Sabrina in both arms and lifted her away. The gust of Charizard's passing tossed Lorelei's hair and blew the glasses from her face.

She picked them up, put them back on, and looked around. Sabrina was gone. High above, Charizard was already racing down the mountain.

Ethan held Sabrina across his arms on its back. From below, they looked like a victorious knight carrying off a princess.

As they were about to disappear from view, Ethan looked back and shouted, "Miss Lorelei! Hurry! If you stay there, it'll be too late!"

Lorelei could have cried. He had left her behind? Had it never occurred to him to take her along?
