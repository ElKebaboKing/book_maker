Misty lifted her chin at Ash's question. “Is there a problem? I can go wherever I want. Do I have to report to you first?”

She had been Ash's first traveling companion, and they had spent a long time together. With him, her sharper side came out in full force. Ash was never quick with words. Faced with that reply, he stammered and could find nothing to say.

Brock, May, and Max caught up a few steps behind him. Brock's reunion with Misty was every bit as dramatic. After the Silver Conference, the three of them had returned to Kanto and firmly parted ways. Now they had somehow run into one another again in Hoenn.

“Brock! Didn't you say you were going home to look after your brothers and sisters?”

“Weren't you going back to help your sisters at the Gym?”

They pointed at each other, shocked and incredulous. Neither had expected to see a recently departed companion here. May and Max looked from one to the other, unable to follow the argument.

“So this is how you take care of your siblings?” Misty asked, giving Brock a sideways look.

“And I don't see your sisters anywhere,” Brock said with a smile.

Caught between them, Ash wiped sweat from his forehead. “If this was going to happen, we might as well have kept traveling together.”

“Hmph. Who wants to travel with you two?” Misty lifted her head proudly. “I'm traveling with Ethan now. Just the two of us!”

May and Max didn't know Misty, but they could tell that beneath the bickering, the three old companions cared deeply about one another. Unable to join in, the siblings went over to Ethan.

“Good to see you again, Mr. Ethan.” May looked up at him with a wide smile. “I didn't expect to meet you here. Where's Miss Erika?”

“She went back to Kanto.” Ethan shook his head, then asked a question he already knew the answer to. “How did you end up traveling with Ash and Brock?”

“It's a long story.” May sighed. “Not long after we last met, I went to Professor Birch's lab to get my first Pokémon. That's where I met Ash. Pikachu was sick at the time and accidentally wrecked my bicycle. I ended up traveling with them. Max insisted on coming along after we reached the Petalburg Gym, and we met Brock out in the wild.”

At the word “bicycle,” Misty immediately turned toward her. “Wait. Ash wrecked your bike too?”

“Too?” May studied this pretty but rather fierce girl. “What do you mean, too?”

Misty shot Ash a look. “Exactly what it sounds like. When I first met him, he took my bicycle to save Pikachu. It was destroyed, and he still hasn't paid me back. I only traveled with him afterward for Pikachu's sake.”

“What?” May stared. “He's done this before?”

If Misty had traveled with Ash for so long and still never received a replacement, what hope was there for May's bicycle? Ash could only smile awkwardly at the direction the conversation had taken. Even Pikachu looked uncomfortable.

Misty had long since stopped caring about the bike. She held out her hand to May. “Hi. I'm Misty.”

May paused, then quickly shook it. “Hi. I'm May.”

So the two girls who accompanied Ash through different regions in the anime finally met. It was as though one generation of traveling companions had passed the torch to the next, while each continued on her own path.

Ash had endured enough embarrassment. Afraid the others would find another story to tell at his expense, he turned to Ethan. “How many badges have you got now?”

Ethan saw what Ash was doing and was glad to help. “I only just finished the Mauville Gym. I have one more than you.”

“Just one more?” Ash's spirits rose. Ethan had set off much earlier, yet he was only a badge ahead. Maybe Ash could catch up soon. He would have to work even harder.

“That's perfect timing,” Ash said with a grin. “I've just finished the Dewford Gym, and I was on my way to challenge Mauville!”

“It is good timing,” Ethan agreed. “We're going in opposite directions. You're heading for Mauville City, and I'm going to Dewford Town. Meeting here wasn't easy.”

“Oh, come on,” Misty said with a wave. “Once or twice might be a coincidence. How many times have you two run into each other by now? I've stopped being surprised. We shouldn't stand in the street talking. Let's find somewhere to sit.”

She had a point. Their chance meetings had grown familiar even to Ethan and Ash, and the group was blocking foot traffic. They chose a nearby café and went inside.

Over coffee and desserts in a corner booth, they caught one another up on their travels. May and Misty grew more comfortable together as they talked.

“You're in for a rough time,” Misty told May, laughing behind her hand with the authority of experience. “You'll find out how painful it can be to travel with Ash. Worst of all, Team Rocket's trio shows up everywhere!”

Brock nodded emphatically.

May thought back over everything that had happened since meeting Ash. “So being attacked by strange people, getting lost, and going hungry weren't unusual? That happened all the time before?”
