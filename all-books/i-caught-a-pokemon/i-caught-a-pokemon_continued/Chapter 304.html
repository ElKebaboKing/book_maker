Standing in the Dragon Holy Land, Clair scanned the valley carefully. Everything looked as it should, and Vicious was nowhere in sight. She let out a breath of relief.

"Good. We made the right choice. That Pokémon hunter hasn't reached this place yet!"

Lance nodded. "We still can't let our guard down. First, let's go to the shrine and tell the Elder what's happened."

The Elder? Who's that?

Curious, Ethan followed Lance and Clair toward the center of the valley.

Pokémon watched them as they walked. Perhaps they were used to the Dragon Tamers, because unlike many wild Pokémon, they showed no suspicion or hostility toward humans. A few bold ones even greeted the group.

Deep in the valley, beside a pond and beneath a few solitary trees, Ethan saw a shrine.

Lance pointed. "Here we are. This is the Dragon Shrine."

It looked like a small temple covered in vines and flowers. On either side stood a stone pillar carved with inscriptions. Both pillars, like the shrine itself, bore the marks of years of wind and rain. Ethan could not guess how long they had stood there.

What caught his attention most was the flame burning fiercely in a stone basin inside. It had no visible fuel, yet it continued to burn without fading.

Noticing Ethan's gaze, Lance smiled. "After our clan fought that Dragon-type Pokémon, we built this shrine to pray for peace between people and Pokémon. We placed a sacred object here called the Flame of Prayer. Incredibly, it has burned for five hundred years and is still burning today."

A flame that's burned for five hundred years? That was extraordinary power. Ethan could imagine how formidable the Dragon-type Pokémon from the legend must have been.

Inside the shrine, an old man in armor and a helmet sat cross-legged on a cushion, dozing with a dragon-headed staff in his arms.

He was short, scarcely a meter and twenty centimeters tall by Ethan's estimate. Thick, long white eyebrows hid his eyes, and a white beard like a goat's covered his mouth. On his aged face, only his nose and deep wrinkles remained visible. He snored steadily and did not notice the group approaching.

He looked even older than Kabu, perhaps well into his nineties. Ethan took care not to startle him.

The others exchanged glances. Finally, Lance cleared his throat twice, stepped forward cautiously, and spoke in a low voice.

"Elder... Elder..."

He waited, but the old man did not respond. Lance sighed, unwilling to raise his voice. It was hard to imagine an Elite Four member looking so helpless, like a child caught doing something wrong.

Fortunately, footsteps sounded behind the shrine. A Dragonite came around the building.

At a glance, Ethan could tell it was stronger and sturdier than the other Dragonite he had seen.

Clair spoke softly. "It belonged to the Gym Leader from two generations ago. After its Trainer died, Dragonite carried on his wishes and stayed to protect this land. The Pokémon here love and respect it."

Dragonite clearly knew Lance, Clair, and Kabu well. It waved a greeting, then saw that they needed to speak with the Elder and gently shook him awake.

Yawning, the Elder slowly opened his eyes and finally noticed the people beside him.

"Ah, it's you..."

At the sound of his voice, Lance, Clair, and Kabu hurried to bow. Their respect for the Dragon Elder was unmistakable. Ethan followed their example. When he raised his head, he noticed an ornament on the Elder's helmet shaped like Rayquaza.

The likeness was remarkably lifelike. As far as Ethan knew, Rayquaza was a Legendary Pokémon that lived in the ozone layer and had never descended to the ground. Few ordinary people even knew it existed. Yet the Elder knew its appearance well enough to wear it as a totem. Ethan wondered whether the Dragon Tamer clan had once encountered Rayquaza.

Could Rayquaza have been the Pokémon that fought the clan's founder?

Lance respectfully explained the situation.

"A Pokémon hunter?" Despite his age, the Elder's thoughts were clear. He struck the ground firmly with his staff and spoke in a grave, weathered voice. "We cannot let him disturb the peace here. He must be stopped!"

Then he turned to Ethan. His eyes were bright and wise, and his gaze seemed to look straight through Ethan to his soul.

"Young man, your destiny is intertwined with Dragon-type Pokémon. Now and in the future, you have a strong connection to the path of dragons. Would you like to join our Dragon Tamer clan and become a great master of Dragon-type Pokémon?"

Ethan was not especially moved, but the offer stunned Lance and the others. As far back as any of them remembered, Dragon Tamers had always been raised within the clan. They had never heard of someone being invited to join it. Ethan's acceptance would set a precedent.

"Me?" Ethan blinked, then shook his head. "I'm sorry. Becoming a Dragon Tamer sounds wonderful, but I love other Pokémon too, not only Dragon types."

The Elder did not seem surprised by his refusal. He sighed with a hint of regret and turned his attention to Sabrina.

"As for you, young lady, your destiny cannot be predicted. Psychic power is the source of your strength. It protects you, but it also binds you. It seems the path of dragons is not yours in this life."

The fact that he could discern Sabrina's psychic abilities told Ethan the Elder was no ordinary man. Then again, this world held more than extraordinary Pokémon. Plenty of its people were far from normal too!

Sabrina had no interest in becoming a Dragon Tamer or a master of Dragon-type Pokémon. She was about to say so when a tremendous blast sounded from a distant mountainside.

Everyone turned. Choking smoke billowed from the mountain where an explosion had torn through the rock. As the stone broke apart, a dark opening appeared.
