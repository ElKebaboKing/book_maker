At seven that evening, Ethan and Zuki brought their respective Eevee evolutions to the battlefield behind the main house, accompanied by Erika, Miki, and the others.

The sky had not yet grown completely dark. In the last of the evening light, the two stood facing one another in the Trainer boxes.

Many Trainers had battled here during the day. Those fairly intense matches had left the field uneven and pitted, and because the tea party had ended only recently, the courtyard servants had not yet had time to repair and level it.

Neither Ethan nor Zuki seemed concerned by such details.

After hesitating, Naoko approached Ethan and quietly warned him, "Mr. Ethan, although we spend so much time performing onstage, we're also skilled at battling. Zuki may be slightly weaker than Kuni, but she's still very strong. Please be careful!"

Naoko's actions clearly favored an outsider over her own sister, but the other three sisters said nothing. Ethan was their guest, and Zuki had forced him into a battle, which was hardly proper hospitality. That was precisely why they did not take Zuki's side and instead remained neutral.

"Thank you for the warning."

Ethan nodded, grateful for Naoko's kindness. This beautiful young woman clearly had more sense!

He distinctly remembered that in the games, the player's character also battled the five sisters upon reaching Ecruteak City and received a bell afterward. He had no doubts about their strength.

Of course, they might not be extraordinarily powerful, but they were certainly stronger than the average Trainer.

Ignoring her younger sister, Zuki planted her hands on her hips, stared intently at Ethan, and sneered. "I'll show you that battling is another one of our talents! This time, I'll make sure you see our strength! We aren't weaker than anyone!"

As she finished, she cast Erika an almost imperceptible glance.

Only then did Ethan understand why Zuki was being so particular about this!

He remembered saying that the sisters belonged onstage rather than on a battlefield, which was why Erika shone more brightly in his eyes. That remark had probably infuriated her!

Ah, the frightening competitive streak between women.

What a nuisance... This is such a nuisance...

Miki and the others merely looked bewildered when they heard Zuki's words. Still, they could guess that something unknown to them must have happened between her and Ethan.

But... what could it have been?

Now that the battle was unavoidable, Ethan could only focus. His gaze moved from Zuki to Umbreon.

Umbreon had black fur, red eyes, and brilliant golden rings across its body. It resembled a combination of a fox and a black cat.

Every Eevee evolution had its own distinctive features, but judging solely by appearance, Ethan thought Umbreon possessed the coolest presence of them all.

He had always liked this evolution. Only because he needed to deal with Zapdos had he reluctantly evolved his Eevee into Jolteon instead.

Without the incident involving Zapdos, he most likely would have chosen Umbreon.

That was no longer possible. He would have to catch another Eevee someday if he wanted to fulfill that wish.

Shaking his head to clear away those distracting thoughts, Ethan took out his Pokédex and pointed it at the Umbreon before him.

This was not a game but a living reality. The information he had once known was clearly insufficient, so he needed a more complete understanding of his opponent.

The Pokédex announced, "Beep! Umbreon, the Moonlight Pokémon. A Dark type. Umbreon is nocturnal and becomes active at midnight, when moonlight covers the land. It evolves under the influence of the moon's waves. Moonlight altered its genetic structure. Its black fur blends into the darkness, allowing it to lurk unseen as it waits for prey. The ring-shaped markings on its body naturally glow with an eerie light as the sky grows dark."

From the Pokédex's description, Umbreon's power reached its peak at night.

Once he had learned this, Ethan calmly put away his Pokédex and looked up at the sky.

The sky was steadily darkening, and the moon already hung low above them. Complete darkness had not yet fallen, but the conditions that strengthened Umbreon would soon be met!

Ethan was not afraid, but he knew that he should finish the battle as soon as possible.

Meanwhile, neither Zuki nor her sisters had expected Ethan to examine Umbreon with his Pokédex at a time like this. Surprise appeared on all their faces.

They had assumed that a major tournament champion like Ethan would already know every Pokémon well!

Jolteon and Umbreon slowly entered the field. Neither held a type advantage over the other, which was good news for both Trainers.

Since this was only a single battle, there was no need for a referee. As soon as both Pokémon were ready, the match began.

At Zuki's command, Umbreon charged straight toward Jolteon.

Despite its small, agile appearance, Umbreon's Speed and offensive strength were fairly ordinary. In exchange, both its physical and special defenses were exceptional, and it possessed considerable stamina.

That meant it had no fear of fighting Jolteon at close range. Among smaller Pokémon, it was one of the few capable of withstanding a relentless barrage!

Before the watching crowd, Umbreon rushed up to Jolteon, opened its jaws, and bit down viciously.

Jolteon remained perfectly calm in the face of the attack.

It had fought alongside Ethan across countless battlefields and helped him conquer both the Indigo League and the Orange League. Its strength had been thoroughly tempered, and it naturally possessed a wealth of experience.

Jolteon evaded the bite with incredible speed, raised its forelegs, and kicked Umbreon hard.

Double Kick!

As a Fighting-type move, it was the only move Jolteon knew that could inflict super-effective damage on a Dark-type Pokémon!

Thud! Thud!

After the two light impacts, Umbreon recoiled in pain and was driven two steps backward by the force.

Everyone watching could clearly see that Jolteon had gained the advantage in their first exchange!

That exchange essentially set the tone for the entire battle!
