Chapter 414: Rescuing Raikou

After watching Gengar leave, Ethan took out his phone and sent Looker a message, briefly explaining what had happened here.

When he turned his attention back to the others, he found Jimmy and Marina staring blankly in the direction Gengar had gone.

"What's wrong?"

"Y-your Gengar can talk!?"

Ethan had seen that reaction far too many times to find it strange anymore, so he merely offered a casual explanation. After Jimmy and Marina marveled at it for a while, they suddenly remembered something and hurried toward Raikou.

Raikou lay weakly on the ground. The earth beneath it had been charred black.

The damage had all been caused by its own electricity rebounding against it. It was easy to imagine how long it had struggled and how much it had suffered while Ethan and the others were fighting. The sight reminded Ethan of the collector at Shamouti Island, who had likewise used technology to toy with the three Legendary birds.

Against the most advanced human technology, even Legendary Pokemon could be rendered helpless.

Just as Ethan was about to approach, his phone chimed.

Ding-dong.

Looker had replied. He was currently occupied with a mission and was too far away to reach them anytime soon. However, he had contacted another International Police officer, who would meet Ethan and help handle the situation.

Another International Police officer? Who could it be?

Truthfully, apart from Looker, Ethan had little desire to deal with too many members of the International Police. Whether they were pursuing an evil organization like Team Rocket or a Trainer like him, those people were never easy to deal with.

At that moment, a weak roar came from up ahead. Ethan looked up and saw that Jimmy had barely taken a few steps before Raikou growled him to a halt.

Raikou clearly held no fondness for humans. It struggled to its feet, cast Jimmy a sidelong glance, then turned away and staggered toward the forest.

Jimmy stood rooted to the spot, unsure what to do.

Judging solely by his behavior now, he did not intend to capture Raikou. He merely watched it with a complicated expression.

As a native of Johto, Jimmy had grown up hearing stories about Ho-Oh, Lugia, and the three Legendary beasts. More than a desire to capture them, he felt a deep reverence and admiration for those Pokemon.

Ethan, on the other hand, very much wanted to capture this Raikou. But if he tried to take advantage of its weakened state and force the issue, it would certainly resist him. Besides, Jimmy and Marina were right there, and they had all discovered Raikou together. He could not simply disregard their feelings.

The best solution was to wait for Raikou to leave, then find it alone later and attempt to capture it.

As Raikou moved farther away, Jimmy finally could not help calling after it. "Raikou! You're badly injured! Where do you think you're going!?"

He took another few steps, seemingly intending to catch up, only for Raikou to attack without warning.

Bzzzt!

Raikou whipped around, and a bolt of electricity shot from its forehead straight at Jimmy!

The attack struck him head-on. Jimmy groaned in pain amid the dazzling arcs of electricity.

"Jimmy!"

Marina cried out and tried to rush forward, but Erika grabbed her and held her back.

"Don't go over there! It's too dangerous!"

Fortunately, Raikou's strength had already been greatly diminished, so the shock lasted only a few seconds. When the electricity faded, Jimmy toppled backward and crashed to the ground.

Once the danger had passed, Erika released Marina and allowed her to run to Jimmy's side. It was plain to see how close those two truly were.

Before Marina could reach him, however, Jimmy sprang back to his feet and charged toward Raikou again.

His astonishing vigor left Ethan staring in disbelief. He had taken a direct electric attack and seemed completely unaffected. His constitution was every bit as ridiculous as Ash's! Well, that must have been a trait unique to hot-blooded young heroes...

After all, electricity could never kill a fool...

Once his amazement subsided, Ethan could only feel envious.

He was not envious of their single-minded foolishness, of course. He simply envied their physical resilience.

"Raikou!" Perhaps being attacked for no reason had angered Jimmy, or perhaps he was embarrassed after making a fool of himself in front of the girl he liked. He clenched a fist and shouted at Raikou's back, "I'm only trying to help!"

Raikou clearly had no need for his help.

Another bolt flashed. This time it merely blasted a scorched mark into the ground in front of Jimmy. He froze in place as cold sweat ran down his temples.

Even someone as slow as Jimmy could see the coldness in Raikou's eyes!

At that moment, Raikou also noticed Ethan behind him. As man and Pokemon met each other's gaze, Raikou's eyes suddenly contracted in recognition. Ethan realized from its reaction that this was the very same Raikou he had fought and defeated at Greenfield!

The discovery sent a jolt of alarm through Raikou. Knowing it could not defeat the three Legendary birds, it turned and bolted without the slightest hesitation.

But the sudden movement aggravated its injuries. Its consciousness blurred in an instant, and its body toppled uncontrollably to one side. As it lost consciousness, Raikou laboriously turned its head. Through its closing eyes, it saw that man walking slowly toward it.

...

Pokemon Center. Outside the emergency treatment room.

Ethan sat on a corridor bench and devoured a hamburger in only a few bites.

It was an extra meal. After all that exertion, he had already burned through everything he had eaten earlier.

Beside him, Erika and Marina were speaking quietly. Ethan could not hear what they were saying, but Marina's shy glances toward Jimmy offered a clue.

While the three of them remained relaxed, Jimmy alone paced endlessly along the corridor, like an anxious father waiting outside a delivery room.

They were here because they had placed the unconscious Raikou inside a Poke Ball, brought it to the Pokemon Center, and entrusted it to Nurse Joy for emergency treatment.

The red light above the treatment room had been glowing for a long time.

"Let us in too! We want to see Raikou!"

A commotion came from beyond the corridor door. Since Raikou had been placed inside a Poke Ball, bringing it into the Pokemon Center had inevitably caused a sensation.

Trainers driven by all kinds of motives had gathered for a glimpse of Raikou. If not for the closed door and the Chansey guarding it from the other side, they would undoubtedly have forced their way in.

Even so, more and more Trainers continued to gather outside, and no one showed any intention of leaving.

Tap, tap.

Footsteps sounded from inside the treatment room. The door opened, and Nurse Joy stepped out.

Jimmy's eyes lit up at the sight of her, and he was the first to hurry forward.

"Nurse Joy... How is Raikou!?"
