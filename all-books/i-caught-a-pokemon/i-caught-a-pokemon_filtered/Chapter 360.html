Chapter 360: Entei Abducts Delia

Ash had never seen Entei before. Faced with such an unusual Pokemon and overwhelmed by its majestic presence, he could not contain his astonishment. He took out his Pokedex and aimed it at Entei.

The Pokedex announced, "Entei, the Volcano Pokemon. A Fire-type and one of the Legendary Pokemon. Entei has an enormous body and harbors a passion like molten magma. Some originally believed it was born from an erupting volcano and ran ceaselessly across the land because it could not fully contain the power swelling within it. According to another folk legend, it is a unique Pokemon resurrected by Ho-Oh. Entei is said to breathe flames hotter than volcanic magma, incinerating everything in their path."

"Entei!?"

When they heard that the Pokemon before them was the Legendary Entei of Johto, Ash, Misty, Brock, and Lisa all sprang to their feet so violently that they did not even notice their chairs toppling behind them.

Apart from Ethan, no one present had imagined they would encounter Entei here.

Why has Entei come here?

The same question filled every mind amid their shock.

Facing such a Legendary Pokemon, none of Ash's group dared act rashly. They feared that even the slightest movement might provoke it.

Ethan remained far more composed. After all, he had already captured the three Legendary birds.

He even studied Entei with interest and quickly noticed that every place its paws crushed beneath them began to crystallize.

That proved this was the same as in the movie. The Pokemon before them was not the real Entei. It was connected to the manor's crystal and had been manifested by the Unown.

As the two sides faced one another, perhaps fate itself intervened. Delia either suffered an inexplicable lapse in judgment or decided that, as the oldest adult present, she ought to confront the situation. She stepped forward and demanded, "Who are you? Why have you come here?"

Her sudden impulse resembled volunteering herself to the enemy. No one had expected it, and there was no time to stop her.

Entei had come specifically to find her, yet Delia had presented herself willingly.

Sure enough, Entei studied her and spoke in a human voice.

"Mama... You are Mama..."

Before anyone could understand its meaning, a strange light flashed in Entei's eyes. The glow struck Delia. As though hypnotized, her mind became dazed, and she stood perfectly still.

The next instant, Entei bounded in front of her, caught her body across its back, and carried her away.

"Mom!"

Ash cried out and rushed after them. But with only a few bounds, Entei opened a huge distance. Even with everyone running at full speed, they could only watch it pull farther away and race toward the enormous flower-shaped crystal.

It soon vanished into the bizarre crystalline forest.

Ash reached the edge of the crystal first and tried to charge inside, but Brock seized him from behind.

"Brock! Let go of me!"

Ash struggled desperately, but Brock clamped both hands around his arm and prevented him from advancing a single step.

In this contest of strength, the super rookie from Pallet Town, said to possess the strength of a thousand, lost to the man of Pewter City, whose resolve was as solid as stone.

"Wait, Ash!" Brock gritted his teeth as he held him back. "We don't understand the situation. Don't do anything reckless! We don't even know whether these crystals can harm humans. If we get crystallized too, we'll be in real trouble!"

"But Mom..."

Ethan knew the general direction of the story and understood that Delia would not be harmed. He still did not reveal everything, offering reassurance instead.

"Don't worry. The three Legendary beasts of Johto are relatively benevolent, unlike Kanto's irritable Legendary birds. Entei must have taken your mother for some special purpose, but at least her life shouldn't be in danger."

The moment he finished, several Great Balls at his waist began shaking violently. His words had clearly offended their occupants, who were protesting furiously.

"Is that really true...?" Ash still looked uncertain.

In reality, Ethan's confidence came entirely from his advance knowledge of the story. If one of his own relatives had been carried away, he could never have remained so calm.

Even so, he understood that the situation needed to be resolved quickly. He and Erika had stayed precisely to help the people here deal with the incident.

"Ethan is right." Erika reached them, breathing hard, her chest rising and falling slightly. She also tried to persuade Ash. "We can't act impulsively now. Our opponent is the Legendary Pokemon Entei, and its strength is immense. We also don't know what lies beyond the crystal. We mustn't rush. The most important thing is to meet with Professor Oak and see whether his investigation uncovered anything."

This was the benefit of traveling with Erika.

If Sabrina still stood beside Ethan, she would never offer suggestions or try to persuade anyone. She would merely watch with cold detachment and silently follow Ethan's lead.

Erika had far more initiative and thought carefully for herself. Most valuable of all, her presence could offer Ethan new approaches whenever they encountered a problem.

Ash naturally wanted to rescue his mother immediately. But he was still a child and lacked maturity. With everyone around him giving the same advice, he had no choice but to suppress his panic.

He kept looking back every few steps, his face filled with unwillingness, as he returned to the Pokemon Center with Ethan and the others.

Ethan called Professor Oak along the way and explained what had happened. When they reached the Pokemon Center, Professor Oak had just returned by car.

They met in a lounge. Professor Oak's expression was grave after learning of Delia's abduction, but his view matched Ethan's.

"I never expected Entei to appear here..." He sighed, then looked at Ash reassuringly. "Don't worry. Pokemon rarely abduct humans without reason, especially Legendary Pokemon like Entei. I'm sure your mother will be safe."

Professor Oak's reassurance eased Ash's mind considerably, though he immediately asked, "Professor, what did your investigation find?"

Professor Oak took out a computer and tapped several commands, displaying the investigation results for everyone.

"According to my findings, the crystallization itself does not harm humans or Pokemon. The slowing rate of expansion suggests its energy may be weakening. Even so, traveling directly across it would be dangerous. Officer Jenny has called for bulldozers. By this evening, they should clear a route to the manor. Once that is done, we can go there and learn what is truly happening!"
