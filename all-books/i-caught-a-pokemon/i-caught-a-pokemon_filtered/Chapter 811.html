Chapter 811: The Battle Factory

After leaving the forest, the group stopped for a late breakfast at a Pokemon Center near its edge. Nurse Joy gave them the Battle Factory's exact location, and they set out again.

Beyond a short canyon, an enormous steel building rose before them. It looked every bit like a factory.

So that's why they call it the Battle Factory, Max said, gazing up at it. I thought it was just a name. It really looks like one! What do they make here? Surely they didn't build a factory just to match the name.

Who knows? Ethan said. We'll find out inside.

They were about to enter when a man's voice called from overhead. Mr. Vale! Ash! I've been waiting for you!

They looked up. Scott, in a blue shirt, was smiling and waving from an outdoor walkway on the second floor.

Mr. Scott! Ash waved back. I didn't expect to see you here!

May looked puzzled. You know him too?

Of course. On my way home from the ship, I passed through Viridian Gym. I met Mr. Scott there and battled Agatha from the Elite Four! That's when he explained the Battle Frontier to me again.

You battled an Elite Four member? May, Max, and Brock had not heard about that part of Ash's trip. They crowded around him with questions.

How strong was Agatha?

Did you beat her?

Ash scratched his cheek and gave an embarrassed laugh. Well... she beat me completely. Her Pokemon were incredibly strong.

While they spoke, Scott hurried down the stairs and reached them a little out of breath. He greeted Ash, then turned apologetically to Ethan.

Mr. Vale, I'm sorry about what happened at the Battle Dome.

Scott owned the Battle Frontier, so he knew exactly what had happened. The match had attracted so much attention that the internet was still arguing about it. People mocked Tucker for promoting himself as Ethan's equal when he had proved so weak. He had become a public joke.

Scott had little power to prevent it. He had recruited the Frontier Brains because of their abilities, and each had a different temperament. He relied on them to build the Frontier's reputation and could hardly afford to alienate them. Even if he had wanted to stop Tucker, he might not have been able to.

Ethan understood. He had no intention of blaming Scott. The matter was over, and until Scott brought it up, he had nearly put Tucker out of his mind.

Don't worry, he said. That was Tucker's decision, not yours or the other Frontier Brains'. I don't hold it against you. It's behind us, isn't it?

Yes, yes. It's behind us, Scott said quickly.

The exchange made little sense to Ash and Max. They quietly asked Brock, but he knew only that Ethan's match with Tucker had caused a stir across Kanto. He had not been there and knew nothing of what lay behind it.

After a few more words, Scott led them toward the factory entrance. Before they reached it, an engine roared on the other side of the doors. The doors burst open, and a strange red, six-wheeled armored vehicle shot out.

The machine was angular and rugged, with rows of huge exhaust pipes pouring black smoke from its rear. It circled wildly across the open space. Its brakes seemed to have failed. A man's alarmed voice could be heard from inside.

Not again, Scott sighed. It's out of control. He turned to the group. Could you help stop it?

Leave it to us!

Ash, May, and Brock eagerly released their Pokemon. Ethan stayed back, certain they could handle it.

Three flashes of blue light revealed Ash's Grovyle, May's Combusken, and Brock's Mudkip. The three Hoenn starters, one of each type, had come together here in an unexpected way. With Pikachu's help, they stopped the runaway machine.

Perhaps damaged it until it stopped would have been more accurate. Even a tough armored vehicle could not withstand several Pokemon attacking at once. Fortunately, only one or two wheels and some related parts were broken, and stopping it prevented worse destruction.

The vehicle's hatch opened. A man in a red cap, black sleeveless shirt, dark trousers, and a long vest climbed down from the roof.

Thanks to you, I made it out! He wiped sweat from his forehead. This wild horse won't listen to its owner. Really, thank you!

Then he saw the damaged tires and wheel fittings, and his face fell. Oh, that's bad. It took me so much work to build this masterpiece. Looks like another major repair.

It's only a car, Scott said with a smile, beckoning him over. Let me introduce

Only a car? the man burst out. He pointed proudly at the machine. This is the Noland J-9, a supermachine I built myself! It runs on steam. Isn't that stylish?

Ash and the others exchanged doubtful glances. Neither its shape nor its colors looked particularly stylish to them.

Max muttered, So it's a steam car. Haven't those been obsolete for ages? Clean energy and controlled nuclear power are where the future is going.

The man heard him. What do you know? he cried, throwing back his head. Steam is a man's dream! A rugged supermachine like this is what a real man should drive!

The discussion was running away from them. Scott cut in. Enough about the car. Remember the Trainer I told you about yesterday? He's here.

The man grew serious at once. He stepped closer and studied the visitors, finally fixing his gaze on Ethan.

You're the champion?

I am.

At this distance, Ethan could see him clearly. He had brown hair, sparse facial hair, and slight lines around his mouth. He looked at least thirty-five. His muscular arms suited someone who worked on machines, while his clothes made him seem younger than he probably was.

The man laughed. It really is you! I've waited a long time for this. Oh, I haven't introduced myself. I'm Noland, the Frontier Brain in charge of the Battle Factory.

Ethan had guessed. So had May, Max, and Brock. A man driving out of the Factory was likely connected to it. Only Ash seemed to realize who he was when Noland said it aloud.

Ash and the others introduced themselves. Noland looked at Ethan with bright anticipation.

Ever since I heard you were coming, I've been waiting to battle you!

Then we'll have our match, Ethan said, meeting his gaze. That's why I'm here.

Noland was not merely being polite. He genuinely wanted to test himself against Ethan.

Scott grinned. If you want to face this champion, you'd better give it everything you've got. He beat Tucker in only two attacks.

I'm nothing like that performer, Noland said confidently. My Pokemon are strong!

Me too! Ash called. I came to challenge the Battle Factory. You can't battle only Brother Ethan!

I haven't forgotten you. Noland turned to Ash with a hearty smile. Scott told me about the spirit you showed against Agatha at Viridian Gym. I'll give you my best too.

Noland took them inside the Battle Factory, but instead of leading them straight to the battlefield, he brought them to a grassy area. A young man was feeding a large number of Pokemon there. He looked up at their approach.

Master, who are they?

Noland introduced them. This is Sergio, my first apprentice. He helps me around the Factory. Sergio, these Trainers have come to challenge me. The one in the middle is Ethan Vale, champion in three regions.

You're Mr. Vale? Sergio hurried over, excited. Ever since Scott told us you might come, Master has been waiting for this day. We're glad you're finally here!

His words made Noland's anticipation clear. Ash felt a small pang of envy. He had come to challenge Noland too, yet Ethan received all the attention. Then he remembered Ethan's strength and achievements. Of course Noland would be eager to face him. Ash respected Ethan as much as anyone.

After Sergio had greeted them, Noland gestured toward the Pokemon. These are my partners. I usually let challengers choose freely which one they'd like to face. But for you two, I've prepared a special Pokemon.

Which one? Ash asked eagerly.

I'll keep that a secret until the battle. Noland smiled.

Ethan had already guessed and could not resist spoiling the surprise. Articuno, isn't it?

What? Noland and Sergio stared at him. Articuno was the Factory's greatest secret. How could Ethan know?

Noland glanced instinctively at Scott, then remembered that even Scott did not know. He could not have told Ethan.

Ash and the others were stunned too. Brother Ethan, Ash asked, are you saying the Articuno we saw earlier belongs to Mr. Noland?
