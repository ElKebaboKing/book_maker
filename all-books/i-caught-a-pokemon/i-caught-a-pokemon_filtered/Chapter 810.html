Chapter 810: Cleffa and Articuno

That afternoon, Ethan and the others stood outside Professor Oak's laboratory to say goodbye to Oak, Professor Birch, Delia, and Tracey.

Ash had hurried home to pack after lunch and had just returned to join them.

Take care, Oak said first. I wish you every success with your challenges. Birch and Tracey offered encouragement too, while Delia quietly gave Ash one reminder after another.

Okay, Mom, I know. I'll take care of myself. Don't worry. Ash seemed embarrassed to be the only one treated like a child in front of everyone. After assuring her again, he hurried over to Ethan and the others.

Then we're off, Ethan said to Oak. May, Ash, Max, and Brock waved goodbye as well. They left the laboratory together and walked out of Pallet Town, disappearing from their friends' view.

...

They were all heading for the Battle Factory near Mt. Moon, so Ethan and Ash's groups traveled together for now. The conversation and laughter made the road livelier.

Even so, they kept a clear division between their two parties. Ethan and Ash walked side by side at the front, each leading his own group. May and Max followed behind them. The siblings talked quietly, but May stayed closer to Ethan while Max stayed nearer to Ash. May had already settled into her new role as Ethan's traveling partner.

Brock brought up the rear behind Max, carrying his pack, enjoying the scenery, and keeping an eye on everyone.

With so much to discuss, time passed quickly. By the time they felt tired and thirsty, Mt. Moon was not far away.

Ethan looked back and saw sweat on Max's forehead. The boy was struggling to keep up, so Ethan called a halt.

Let's rest here and have something to eat. If we start again soon, we should reach Mt. Moon before dark and camp there.

As the oldest among them, Ethan naturally took charge. Neither Ash nor Brock objected. Following his lead seemed entirely normal to them. He had earned their trust through his strength and reliability.

Now Brock could show his skills. While the others gathered firewood and water, set up a table, and laid out the utensils, he tied on an apron and set to work. With the Pokemon helping, he soon served everyone a meal.

Let's eat.

Ethan tasted the rich vegetable soup and gave Brock a thumbs-up. This is excellent! Just as good as I remember.

Brock was a remarkable cook. Of the people Ethan knew, perhaps only Erika could match him. Brock smiled bashfully at the praise. Ash and the others had eaten his food throughout their travels, so they simply enjoyed it as they always did. They had grown accustomed to eating well.

Ethan remembered something and turned to May. I should warn you: I can only make simple meals. If you travel with me, you'll have to put up with rougher food on the road.

May's face fell. Along with shopping and dressing up, she loved good food. She hated the thought of giving that up during a journey. But she had made her choice.

It's fine, she said with a rueful smile. I'll eat whatever we can make on the road. But when we reach a city, I'm having a feast!

Everyone laughed. May's love of food was no secret.

As they ate, the grass nearby rustled. A wild pink Pokemon with a star-shaped outline emerged.

Huh? Max stared. Why is there a Cleffa here?

It was indeed a Cleffa, the youngest stage of the Clefairy and Clefable line. Its pink body, tiny black eyes, brown pointed ears, small mouth, and curl of hair on its forehead made it rather cute, though not everyone would agree.

Max had reason to wonder. Cleffa usually appeared at night. They rarely strayed far from places where meteors had fallen and generally lived in mountains. This was flat land, and evening had only just begun.

Then again, Mt. Moon was close. Cleffa, Clefairy, and Clefable lived there. Perhaps this one had wandered away while playing and followed the smell of dinner.

For a rare wild Pokemon, that was risky. Another group of Trainers might have reached for their Poke Balls at once. Fortunately, Ethan and Ash's friends had no interest in catching it.

Sensing no hostility, Cleffa hopped closer. It inspected one Pokemon, then another, pestering Ash's and May's partners with its curiosity until they seemed both amused and at a loss.

It's friendly enough, Max observed. It doesn't seem lost.

Maybe it's hungry?

May rose and approached it, hoping for a closer look. Cleffa was comfortable with the Pokemon but wary of humans. It darted behind May's Munchlax, and she stopped where she was.

The other Pokemon greeted it warmly. Cleffa soon relaxed and began playing with them.

For Ethan and the others, the encounter was a small interruption. In the original anime story, Team Rocket's trio had attacked Ash's group after Cleffa appeared. With Ethan here, the three did not dare show themselves. Without their interference, nothing more came of it.

They prepared some food for Cleffa too. After dinner, the group set off again. In barely half an hour, the little Pokemon seemed to know them all.

The sun sank, and darkness began to spread. They had traveled only half a day from Pallet Town and had just eaten, so everyone still had energy. Rather than camp there, they kept walking toward Mt. Moon.

At the mountain's foot, before they entered the rocky slopes, a Clefairy and a Clefable came looking for Cleffa. Their anxious expressions made it clear they were its family.

The group returned Cleffa to them and started to leave, but Cleffa stopped them. At first they could not understand what it wanted. Then they realized it was inviting them into Mt. Moon.

They had not expected that. After a brief discussion, they agreed that spending the night inside the mountain might be better than camping outdoors.

Following Cleffa along a winding path, they climbed halfway up and entered a cave. Deep inside lay an enormous round meteorite beneath a shaft of moonlight. Groups of Clefairy and Clefable danced around it, their celebration both joyful and mysterious.

For Ash and Brock, this was a return visit. They had come here near the start of their first journey. Everything seemed to have changed, and yet nothing had. They were no longer inexperienced beginners. They had grown through success and failure. Still, they were traveling and pursuing the same dreams.

May and Max had never seen the place and looked around in wonder. Ethan was glad finally to see it with his own eyes as well.

...

They rested in the cave that night and set out again early the next morning. Beyond Mt. Moon, the Battle Factory was closer, though they first had to cross a vast forest.

Without Ethan, Ash's group would almost certainly have got lost. With him, there was little danger of it. He read the map accurately and, when a path divided, sent Dragonite above the trees to check their direction. They kept to the right route.

It made the journey less unpredictable. It was also a major reason Ethan could travel so much faster than Ash.

They were nearly out of the forest when something cold touched Max's face. He looked up and saw a large blue bird with long, beautiful tail feathers gliding high above them. Snowflakes drifted down from it.

Look! There's a beautiful Pokemon in the sky!

The others followed his pointing finger. Ash and Brock stared at the bird, then turned almost at once toward Ethan.

Why are you looking at me? Ethan asked, puzzled.

Brother Ethan... Ash hesitated. Is that...?

Yes, Ethan said. It's Articuno.

Articuno? May and Max exclaimed. Delighted, they pulled out their Pokedexes and aimed them at the sky.

While the siblings watched the bird, Ash and Brock kept their eyes on Ethan. He understood the question in their expressions and shook his head.

Don't jump to conclusions. This has nothing to do with me. There isn't just one Articuno in the world. There are even Articuno with a different form in Galar. Meeting one on a journey isn't so strange.

Ash and Brock had seen Ethan's Articuno in unforgettable battles: the clash of the Legendary birds at Shamouti Island in the Orange Islands and the battle between the Legendary birds and beasts at Johto's crystal tower. No wonder they thought of his Pokemon first. But this Articuno clearly did not belong to him.

It was gone in little more than ten seconds. May and Max had been so absorbed in watching it that they missed the conversation. They kept gazing after it, remembering that brief, beautiful sight.

Too bad it flew away, Max said. Still, seeing a Legendary Pokemon this beautiful right after arriving in Kanto makes the trip worthwhile!

That was hardly the whole story. The man standing beside Max had an Articuno of his own, along with the other two Legendary birds. Ash wanted to tell him, but Ethan only smiled. Ash held his tongue. Ethan could reveal it himself when he wished.

It really was beautiful, May agreed. Then she frowned. But why would Articuno be here? There aren't any snowy mountains or ice fields nearby.
