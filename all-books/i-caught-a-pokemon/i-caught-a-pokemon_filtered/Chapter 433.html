Chapter 433: An Overwhelming Victory

After Ash's match ended, it was Ethan's turn to take the field.

His expression remained unchanged. Having already competed in two major tournaments, he could face a scene like this without the slightest disturbance. He emerged from the competitors' passage at a calm pace. At the opposite end of the field, his opponent was clearly attending such an event for the first time and looked far more constrained. The Trainer could only stare at the path ahead, too nervous even to raise his head and look around.

As usual, the announcer gave a brief introduction when the two competitors entered, allowing the spectators to learn a little about them.

The introduction of Ethan's opponent proceeded normally, but when the announcer turned to Ethan, there was a noticeable pause.

Two seconds later, the announcer continued in a slightly startled voice. "Oh? I've just received word that our competitor Ethan is no ordinary Trainer. He's the champion of this year's Indigo League Conference and an Honorable Trainer of the Orange League! Who would have thought our Whirl Cup could attract a competitor of such distinction?"

The Indigo League Conference champion?

An Honorable Trainer of the Orange League?

The spectators immediately became restless.

The Orange League title was one thing. Although the Orange Islands surpassed the Whirl Islands in their number of islands, economy, and population, their competition still belonged to a minor League. Its title did not carry enough weight, and its international standing was only slightly above that of a local tournament like the Whirl Cup.

But the Indigo League Conference champion was entirely different!

That was a true League Conference, almost equal to the Silver Conference in both scale and level of competition. It demanded their attention!

Every spectator craned their neck to look at Ethan. Even Maya, the Sea Priestess seated in the ceremonial section, turned toward him in surprise.

They could hardly believe that a League Conference champion had come to compete in a local cup tournament!

With a smile on his face, Ethan waved to the spectators and walked unhurriedly into the Trainer's command area. His opponent had already been timid, and when he heard that he would be battling a champion, his eyelids began twitching involuntarily!

He swallowed and forced himself to look up at Ethan. The other Trainer was not only handsome, but calm and assured in every gesture. What little confidence the boy possessed immediately evaporated.

How am I supposed to fight someone like this...?

At the same time, Ethan studied his opponent.

The boy was plump and appeared to be fifteen or sixteen. Tension and awkwardness were plainly visible on his face. Judging from his condition, he was even less composed than the six- or seven-year-old boy who had battled Ash!

Ethan did not underestimate him because of that. With a few lucky exceptions, anyone who had emerged from the preliminary round had to possess at least some ability.

Once both Trainers were ready, the referee gave the signal, and they sent out their Pokemon simultaneously.

With no other choice available, Ethan used Blastoise. The other boy sent out a Corsola.

Corsola was primarily pink, with four branching horns growing from its back. It was a common Pokemon along seashores.

The two Pokemon stood atop their respective stone pillars and stared at one another.

The announcer was clearly paying close attention to Ethan. When Blastoise appeared, the rapid rustling of papers could even be heard over the loudspeakers.

After several seconds, the announcer explained, "According to our records, Ethan's Blastoise was among the Pokemon he caught early in his journey. It accompanied him all the way to the Indigo League Conference final and is one of his ace Pokemon! I never expected him to send it out already. Is he determined to eliminate every possible risk and ensure his advancement?"

Although records about Ethan were available, the announcer clearly did not know him well enough. Blastoise currently ranked only among Ethan's second-string Pokemon, yet the man had mistaken it for one of his aces.

By ordinary reasoning, however, the assumption was understandable. The earlier a Pokemon joined a Trainer, the longer they spent together and the more battles it experienced. Such Pokemon often grew quickly, earned the Trainer's trust, and were consequently trained as aces.

The battlefield used for this tournament was spacious: a perfectly circular pool four times the size of an ordinary field. High walls surrounded it, removing any concern about leaving the boundaries and giving Water-type Pokemon ample room to display their abilities.

At that moment, the referee gave the command, and the match officially began.

Every spectator focused on the battlefield. They all wanted to see what this champion was capable of.

Unfortunately, although Corsola appeared far more steadfast and reliable than its Trainer, it was clearly no match for Blastoise and could not force it to use its full strength.

The battle was completely one-sided and presented Ethan with no difficulty whatsoever.

Corsola barely endured several exchanges before Blastoise hoisted it high into the air and slammed it heavily onto a stone pillar, knocking it completely unconscious.

The spectators were somewhat disappointed by the result, but it only made them anticipate Ethan's later matches even more.

Amid their cheers, Ethan calmly left the battlefield and headed toward the competitors' passage. His expression was as relaxed as it had been when he entered.

After successfully advancing to the round of thirty-two, he would battle again at three that afternoon. Another victory would place him in the round of sixteen and allow him to continue his championship run tomorrow. Until then, he could rest in the waiting room and await the result of Misty's match.

Misty was scheduled immediately after him in the ninth match of the morning. The timetable was so tight that Ethan had not even reached the waiting room to wish her luck before she entered the battlefield.

Inside, Ethan joined Ash, and the two began watching Misty's match together.

Her battle had already begun.

Her opponent was a middle-aged man in a straw hat named Harrison.

Harrison's clothing made him look rather like a pirate. As for their Pokemon, Harrison sent out a Qwilfish, while Misty, for reasons of her own, surprisingly chose a Corsola as well!

However...

The spectators did not think much of Corsola.

One of the species had just been defeated with ease by Ethan. Although this was a different Corsola, the audience still had a poor impression of it.

Fortunately, compared with the somewhat unkempt middle-aged Harrison, Misty's image as a beautiful young girl still inspired many spectators to cheer for her.

After watching Ash and Ethan's matches, Misty's fighting spirit was burning brightly, so she launched the first attack.

Perhaps because his opponent was a cute young girl, Harrison wore a dismissive expression. He clearly did not take Misty seriously and believed he would advance with ease!
