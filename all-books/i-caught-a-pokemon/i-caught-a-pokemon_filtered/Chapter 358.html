Chapter 358: No Fair Play

As soon as both sides were ready, the free-for-all began.

Unexpectedly, Ash and Brock immediately focused their opening attacks on Ethan's Pokemon.

"Pikachu, use Thunderbolt! Noctowl, use Air Slash!"

"Onix, use Tackle! Golbat, use Supersonic!"

Although this was a six-on-six battle, all four Pokemon completely ignored Erika and Misty's partners under Ash and Brock's commands. As though they had planned it beforehand, they worked in perfect harmony and attacked Dragonair and Larvitar together.

The four aggressive Pokemon surrounded Ethan's partners. In an instant, their moves erupted at once and covered Dragonair and Larvitar from every direction.

There was nowhere to evade. Dragonair and Larvitar used attacks of their own to resist, but they were badly outnumbered. They canceled some of the incoming moves, yet two others continued toward them with much of their power intact.

With no alternative, they endured the concentrated barrage head-on.

Boom!

When the dust from the explosion dispersed, both Pokemon had suffered injuries of varying severity.

Ash and Brock clearly understood Ethan's strength. They knew he was unquestionably the most powerful person present. If they wanted to win this chaotic battle, eliminating him first was their highest priority.

Seeing them target him so blatantly, Ethan shook his head in amusement.

These two...

What did I ever do to them?

They have no sense of fair play at all. Why are they both targeting me?

Still, it did not matter. This was only an exhibition match, so victory and defeat were unimportant. Even with Ash and Brock giving him such special attention, Ethan had no intention of issuing commands. He let his Pokemon fight freely.

Although Dragonair and Larvitar faced four opponents, their momentum did not falter.

They counterattacked and struggled fiercely, but Ash and Brock's Pokemon were no minor opponents who could be handled with ease.

Pikachu was especially troublesome. Its small body was extremely agile, darting back and forth through the battlefield and proving very difficult to hit. Its moves carried formidable power as well, making it impossible to ignore.

Most of all, the standard battlefield was simply too small for so many Pokemon to fight at once. Dragonair and Larvitar had almost no room to maneuver.

After only a few exchanges, the still inexperienced Larvitar was knocked to the ground. Dragonair continued holding on, but it looked precarious and clearly could not last much longer.

Just as Ash and Brock were about to defeat both of Ethan's Pokemon, bad news came from the other side of the field.

A series of explosions rang out.

They turned and stared in surprise. At some point, both of Lisa's Pokemon had already been defeated.

While Ash and Brock concentrated on Ethan, Dragonair and Larvitar occupied most of their attention. Without any defensive support from their teammates, Erika and Misty had been free to attack Lisa without restraint.

Surrounded by two Gym Leaders, Lisa's Pokemon fell even faster than Ethan's.

Meeting their astonished gazes, Lisa shrugged helplessly.

"There was nothing I could do. They're both really strong. I couldn't last until you two finished off that man."

Her words also carried a complaint about their all-or-nothing strategy. Ash and Brock had ignored everything behind them and failed to relieve any of the pressure on Lisa, so she had lost almost immediately without any real chance to enjoy the battle.

Unfortunately, neither of the two oblivious young men caught her hidden meaning.

With Lisa's Pokemon defeated, the situation on the field completely reversed.

Brock surveyed the battle and inwardly cried out in alarm.

Now free to act, Erika and Misty directed their Pokemon toward Ash and Brock. Together with Dragonair in front, they caught the opposing team in a pincer attack.

Still, matters were not quite as bad as they appeared.

The numbers stood at five against four, and the type matchups were unfavorable. But Misty's Psyduck seemed lost in its own world. It merely stood there with a vacant expression, completely unaware of the battle and unable to help.

Excluding Psyduck made it four against four. Victory remained possible.

A sharp light entered Brock's eyes.

"Ash, let's use everything we've got!"

Ash recognized the danger too. Under their shouted commands, their Pokemon unleashed one hundred and twenty percent of their power, pushing their abilities almost to the limit.

The two sides fired move after move at one another. As more and more energy gathered, it ultimately produced a tremendous explosion in the center of the field.

A small mushroom cloud rose, and red firelight illuminated the nearby grass.

The blast covered an enormous area and carried terrifying power. Noctowl and Golbat were blown clear by the shock wave, but every Pokemon on the ground, including Dragonair, Onix, and Pikachu, lost the ability to battle.

Noctowl and Golbat remained conscious, though both were covered in injuries and looked to be in poor condition.

Seeing this, Ash jumped with delight.

"Yes! We're going to win!"

"Calm down." Brock remained composed. "Once we defeat Psyduck, then we'll have won."

Only then did Ash notice that although the explosion had defeated most of the Pokemon, Psyduck had remained at the edge of the battlefield and escaped completely unharmed.

He did not take it seriously in the slightest.

"Don't worry. Misty's Psyduck is a clueless idiot. It'll be easy to defeat!"

Under their Trainers' commands, Noctowl and Golbat attacked Psyduck. Sure enough, Psyduck merely clutched its head and fled back and forth beneath their assault without any idea how to counterattack.

Misty watched anxiously and shouted, "Psyduck, fight back!"

Unfortunately, Psyduck could not understand its Trainer's order and continued running in panic with its head in its hands.

Ash finally relaxed, already seeing victory before him.

At that moment, perhaps after enduring too many attacks, Psyduck could no longer bear the pain. Its eyes suddenly glowed blue. With a burst of frantic quacking, it froze both Flying-type Pokemon in midair.

"Oh no! Psyduck used Confusion!"

Brock's expression changed drastically. Misty reacted in the opposite way, beaming and laughing without the slightest concern for appearances.

"Great job, Psyduck! Take them down!"

Psyduck extended its two short palms. When it swept them downward, Noctowl and Golbat slammed heavily into the ground.

Bang!

The situation had reversed completely. Both Pokemon fainted on the spot and lost the ability to battle.

No one had expected Psyduck to overturn the entire match at the very end.

Then again, although Psyduck made the decisive contribution, Ethan's team might have won much earlier if it had not forced its way onto the field and Misty had used a different Pokemon instead.

Regardless, the result was satisfactory.

"We lost..."

Ash felt slightly discouraged. Even so, he and Brock remained delighted. After all, they had defeated Ethan's Pokemon.

Those were powerful opponents they could never overcome in an official match. Yet in this casual exhibition, they had finally accomplished it.
