Chapter 152: The Trap Hidden in the Ice Field

As the battle resumed, Ethan and Gary gave their commands at the same time.

"Gyarados, use Aqua Tail!"

"Nidoking, Double Kick!"

Both Pokemon rushed toward each other. Gyarados slithered forward like a serpent, while Nidoking struggled to find its footing on the smooth ice.

Once they were close enough, water began to spiral around Gyarados's enormous tail. Nidoking leaped and launched its Double Kick.

The two collided.

Nidoking's first foot struck Gyarados. Before it could follow through with the second, it heard a rush of wind beside its ear. Turning its head, it saw a thick tail sweeping straight toward its face!

Smack!

The blow caught Nidoking squarely and sent it crashing to the side. Before it could get up, Gyarados had already slithered over. Its jaws opened wide as it lunged to bite.

"Nidoking! Get up, now!"

At Gary's shout, Nidoking rose from the ice and drove the horn on its forehead toward Gyarados's open mouth. The horn struck inside its mouth.

Gyarados roared in pain. Its fury surged, and in an instant it lost control. With no thought for its next move, it repeatedly slammed its massive body and head down at Nidoking beneath it.

Bang! Bang! Bang!

Ice flew everywhere, and cracks spread across the field. After taking several blows, Nidoking's head spun. It tried to struggle upright, only to be smashed back into the ice.

"This is bad!" Swept up in Gyarados's ferocity, the commentator found himself watching from Gary's point of view. "Gyarados has gone berserk and is using Thrash! Can Nidoking withstand that kind of savage force?"

Gary clenched his fists and stared at his Pokemon.

Fortunately for him, Nidoking was not Gyarados's only target now that it had lost its senses. To those blood-red eyes, everything nearby looked like something to destroy. After striking Nidoking a few more times, Gyarados turned its fury on the ice blocks beside it.

The huge blocks shattered under its blows. Fine ice mist hung in the air, making Gyarados's form appear and disappear through the haze.

In the stands, spectators held their breath. For the first time, many of them seemed to understand just how violent and fearsome a Gyarados could be.

Only then did Nidoking get a moment to breathe. Covered in wounds, it dragged itself out of a hollow in the ice and escaped Gyarados's reach.

Gary let out a small breath of relief. He had no time to check Nidoking's injuries. Gyarados was still rampaging, and he did not want to waste the chance.

"Nidoking, use Poison Jab!"

Purple, poisonous light flared around Nidoking's horn. Despite its injuries, it charged and drove the horn hard into Gyarados's chest.

Gyarados roared again. It had made no attempt to dodge while caught in its rage, but the blow drew its attention back to Nidoking. To keep the same thing from happening twice, Gary ordered Nidoking to retreat to a safe distance.

More disappointing still, Poison Jab had failed to poison Gyarados.

Tch. Was even Lady Luck against him today?

Across the field, Ethan had watched calmly. He had no intention of recalling Gyarados to help it shake off its confusion. He waited until it regained its senses before speaking again.

"Gyarados, feeling clearheaded? Then use Aqua Tail again!"

Water wrapped around Gyarados's tail once more.

As it surged forward, Nidoking tried to dodge. Its foot slipped, and it fell awkwardly onto the ice. The sudden mishap caught Gary completely off guard.

Yet even with Nidoking helpless in front of it, Gyarados could not land Aqua Tail.

Just as it reached Nidoking, before it could strike, a loud crack sounded beneath them. The ice gave way!

Deep, cold pool water lay below. Neither Pokemon had time to react before both plunged into it.

Splash!

Gary's expression changed instantly.

"No!"

He was hardly alone. The commentator, the spectators, and everyone else watching the match had been caught by surprise.

Everyone except Ethan. He seemed to have expected it.

In fact, he had deliberately kept the fighting in that part of the field. Jolteon's earlier Thunderbolt and Gyarados's Thrash had left the ice there badly weakened. When the two heavy Pokemon came together on top of it, their weight finally broke through.

Gyarados was a Water-type. Once it reached the pool, it was back in its element. Nidoking, on the other hand, could not swim. It flailed a few times, then sank beneath the surface trailing bubbles.

Glug, glug, glug...

"Nidoking!"

Gary shouted toward the pool and raised his Poke Ball to recall it, but he could not see Nidoking anywhere through the clear water.

Damn it!

"Well..."

Faced with an empty battlefield, even the experienced commentator seemed at a loss for what to say.

The spectators stared at the water. They could not see what was happening below, but violent ripples spread across the surface. Gyarados was clearly still attacking Nidoking underwater.

Curiosity held the crowd in place. Another dozen seconds passed before the water gradually grew calm.

After a few more breaths, Gyarados's imposing body burst from the pool. It roared at the spectators around the field, loud enough to shake the stands.

Now everyone understood: the match had been decided beneath the water.

Nidoking still had not surfaced. Gary felt his heart sink.

Ethan knew Nidoking was too heavy, and too poor a swimmer, to make it ashore on its own. He signaled to Gyarados. It nodded and dove back into the pool.

A few seconds later, the water stirred again. Gyarados surfaced with the unconscious Nidoking draped over its tail and brought it safely to the edge.

The crowd broke into cheers.

Before the referee could even raise a flag, the commentator called out the result.

"It's over! Incredible! A heavyweight clash between Nidoking and Gyarados on the Ice Field ended underwater after the ice collapsed!"
