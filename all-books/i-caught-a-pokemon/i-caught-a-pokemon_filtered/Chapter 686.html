Chapter 686: The Spirited Idol

This time Ethan and Misty waited even longer than before.

How much longer? Long enough for Ethan to spot another little boy who had just entered the park, challenge him, and defeat him in short order. The boy left looking miserable.

Milotic gained more experience, while the boy acquired a defeat he would have trouble forgetting. Misty's teasing had come true: Ethan really was sweeping through the local children.

I, Ethan, am incredibly strong!

It might not have been the fairest way to find opponents, but the battles were useful. Milotic had grown completely comfortable with its evolved body and was beginning to move with ease. If it kept improving at this rate, it would soon catch up to Eevee and Combusken. That would give Ethan another powerful choice for his team.

At noon, Ethan and Misty bought hot dogs and coffee in the park and ate lunch there. Only afterward did Kinta finally return.

The two younger boys were with him, along with a girl Ethan had never met. Even from a distance, she seemed to radiate youthful energy.

She looked about Misty's age, pretty and a little playful. Long locks framed either side of her face, and a ponytail rose from her blue-green hair like a bolt of lightning.

Her dark blue miniskirt was dotted with tiny stars. She wore a matching top that left her midriff bare, a white stocking on one leg, blue boots, cloudlike decorations at her wrists and ankles, and large earrings. The other leg was bare. She looked like someone from an idol group.

Kinta, so aloof earlier, now led the way with a broad smile. He kept turning to speak to her, with none of his former swagger left. The two younger boys trailed behind, blushing and staring at the ground. Neither dared say a word.

The girl herself showed no sign of arrogance or spoiled behavior. She had simply drawn all three boys' admiration.

Yet something about her bothered Ethan. Why did she look familiar?

As he tried to place her, Misty stepped up beside him without a word. Her face was blank, but Ethan could feel her hostility toward the approaching girl.

Kinta stopped in front of him and puffed out his chest. This is the help I went to find! If you can beat her, I'll admit you're strong.

Ethan nodded. He did not particularly care who his next opponent was. He beckoned Milotic toward the starting position, but before he could give it a command, Kinta frowned.

Wait. Don't tell me you don't know who's standing in front of you?

Ethan looked at the idol-like girl again. Their eyes met. He still could not identify her, though she seemed to be studying his face with the same uncertainty.

Kinta could bear his ignorance no longer. You really don't know her? She's the most popular and famous Pokemon Contest idol in all HoennLissi Lisia!

Lisia? An idol? The name meant nothing to Ethan. He was sure he had seen her in the anime back on Earth, but he could not remember which series she had appeared in or what had happened around her.

Misty, however, reacted to the nickname. I've heard of you! Aren't you the beautiful Coordinator who sings CharmLove?

Since becoming friends with May, Misty had learned a great deal about Pokemon Contests and their best-known performers. She had seen Lisia in reports and on the news more than once. Only a few nights ago she had listened to CharmLove before bed and thought it was a good song. Finding its singer here in the park was astonishing.

Lisia gave them a bright smile. Hello! I'm Lisia, and yes, I'm a Contest idol. Lissi is just what my fans like to call me. And that song is my current theme song.

Then she looked Ethan over again. Um...have we met somewhere before? I feel as though I've seen you.

Misty's heart gave a jolt. She studied their expressions, searching for some sign that they knew each other. She could do little about the older women in Ethan's life, but she did not want another pretty girl her own age getting close to him. I was here first!

Ethan was certain he had never met Lisia. Today was the first time he had even heard her name. She must have seen footage of him competing in a League tournament. As an idol, she would probably encounter news and popular topics more often than most people. One of his battles might have left an impression.

He had no wish to draw attention to his identity. You must be mistaken. This is the first time we've met.

I suppose so. Lisia accepted that and turned to the reason she had come. You're the one who beat Kinta?

That's right. So you're my next opponent?

Of course! she said. Kinta and I have been friends since we were little. He came to ask me for help, so I can't let him down.

Facing a Coordinator would be a new experience for Ethan. He had never imagined himself battling one. Let's see what difference there is between your battles and ours.

He was about to send Milotic into place when something on Lisia's hair ornament caught his eye: a Key Stone.

He had not expected to see one in Hoenn. A Key Stone was crucial to a Pokemon's Mega Evolution, and hardly anyone would wear one merely for decoration. Lisia, then, could Mega Evolve a Pokemon too.

That made her more interesting. Neither of them was likely to use such power in this match, but it suggested she was far more capable than Kinta or his two younger friends.

Lisia noticed where he was looking. Does he recognize it? she wondered. This power comes from another region...

Before she could think further, Kinta folded his arms and addressed Ethan. Don't say I didn't warn you. Lisia doesn't battle very often, but she's strong! She's Mr. Wallace's niece, too. If you want to surrender, now's your chance.

Wallace's niece? Ethan was surprised again. A casual chain of park challenges had brought him from two little boys to an idol related to a Gym Leader and Champion. The world sometimes felt enormous and sometimes remarkably small.

But did Kinta really think a famous relative's name would frighten him off? That was childish. He would not be afraid to face Lisia, Wallace himself, or anyone else in Hoenn.

Before Ethan could reply, the two younger boys chimed in. That's right! Lisia's really busy. She was filming when we found her. She only slipped away because Kinta begged her over and over. If you're scared to battle, just surrender!

They meant to impress Ethan with how much trouble she had taken to come. To Kinta, their words sounded awful.

You two, shut up! he snapped, face reddening as he glared at them. Had he really looked that desperate when he asked Lisia? Why did they have to tell Ethan he had begged?

A quick glance showed that Ethan seemed not to care, and Kinta relaxed. Then he caught Misty looking at him with an amused expression. His spirits sank again. So much for saving face.

Ethan took his place in the Trainer's area and directed Milotic to the starting position. Across the pool, he and Lisia faced one another while they waited for her Pokemon.

Even her voice reminded Ethan of Casey in Johto. Both girls seemed full of energy. Perhaps their Pokemon were the same.

Lisia did not take out a Poke Ball. Instead, she snapped her fingers toward the sky.

A moment later, a Pokemon swooped down and landed beside her. Lisia patted the Altaria's shoulder and smiled. Here's my partner for the battle!

Ethan saw nothing unusual in the choice, but Kinta looked alarmed. Lisia, you're using Ali? Are you sure that's a good idea?

Why wouldn't it be? she asked.

Kinta glanced at Ethan, then leaned close to her ear. You have a festival performance in a few days and Contests in other cities after that. He's no easy opponent. What if Ali gets hurt?

He was genuinely worried for her. Lisia shook her head with a smile. You can't be afraid of an injury every time you battle. Besides, Ali is strong. We won't lose easily. And even if Ali does get hurt, Nurse Joy can heal Ali in no time.

Kinta was about to argue, but Lisia had already turned to Ethan. All right! Let's have a sparkling, gorgeous battle!

Ethan raised an eyebrow. Where had that sudden burst of excitement come from? Then he remembered Casey. Perhaps this was simply what an energetic girl was like when a battle began.

This time Kinta took the role of referee. Once both sides were ready, he cast one more worried look at Ali and brought his arm down.

It was a battle between a Pokemon in the water and one in the air. Milotic was a Water type; Altaria was Dragon and Flying. At first glance, Milotic seemed to be at a disadvantage. Altaria's Dragon typing resisted Water attacks, and it could strike downward from above while Milotic fought upward from the pool.

But the pool also posed a danger to Altaria. Apart from the two circular platforms, the field was almost entirely water. If Altaria's fluffy feathers became soaked, it would lose its aerial advantage and might be unable to take off again for a while. Stranded on a platform, it would be at Milotic's mercy.

Milotic, meanwhile, could dive beneath the surface whenever it needed to evade an attack. Both Pokemon had ways to use the field. The question was which Trainer would seize the initiative.
