Chapter 439: She Crawled Into a Gyarados's Mouth as a Child!

As far as Ethan was concerned, the first match offered nothing worth watching. He would have difficulty learning anything from it. Both competitors performed reasonably well, but that was all. Nothing especially exciting happened.

The spectators clearly felt the same way, judging by their lackluster cheers.

Once the first match ended and Misty and Trinity entered the battlefield, Ethan finally perked up and sat straight.

The real show was finally beginning!

On the screen, Misty's expression was solemn, while Trinity looked equally serious. Both clearly placed exceptional importance on this match. Victory would carry one of them into the semifinals, but more than that, each had a reason she could not afford to lose!

Once they were ready, the referee gave the signal, and both Trainers released their Pokemon simultaneously.

Two flashes of blue light appeared. Misty sent out Poliwhirl, while Trinity chose a Gyarados!

The instant she saw the ferocious Gyarados, Misty's expression changed. In her panic, she unconsciously stepped backward!

Her heart pounded wildly. Merely meeting Gyarados's eyes nearly robbed her of all courage.

Seeing this, Ethan shook his head helplessly. Even after so long, Misty had not overcome her fear of facing Gyarados.

The source of that fear could be traced back to her childhood. Once, while playing outside, she encountered a Gyarados living near the Gym. It was sleeping with its enormous mouth open. Misty was so young that she could barely walk, and her curiosity drove her to crawl directly into its mouth!

Sensing something unusual, Gyarados instinctively closed its jaws. At that moment, Misty thought she was about to be eaten. Fortunately, Gyarados noticed the problem and immediately spat her back out. Even so, the experience frightened Misty into wailing. From that day onward, Gyarados left an indelible shadow in her heart!

The incident had been terrifying and dealt her an enormous psychological blow, one that still haunted her now. But she could not continue this way...

How could the future Gym Leader of a Water-type Gym fear a Water-type Pokemon?

Only after overcoming this obstacle and learning to command Gyarados would she truly become a qualified Water-type Pokemon Trainer. For now, it appeared she still needed much more time to temper herself...

As both her friend and the object of her affection, Ethan sincerely wanted to help. After thinking for a moment, he concluded that the fastest way to solve the problem was to fight poison with poison!

When an opportunity arose, he might as well let Misty spend more time with his enormous red Gyarados.

Despite its terrifying appearance, the red Gyarados was extremely gentle toward people it trusted and would never harm Misty. Hmm... He could only wonder whether that would erase the shadow in her heart.

On the battlefield, Misty understood that she could not retreat. She forced down her fear and adjusted her state of mind. The moment the referee announced the start of the match, she took the initiative and attacked Gyarados.

At her command, Poliwhirl used Bubble Beam. Dozens of bubbles large and small burst from the spiral on its belly and flew toward Gyarados.

Trinity answered the move directly with Hyper Beam!

Gyarados opened its enormous mouth, and a powerful beam of light gathered inside before shooting straight toward Poliwhirl!

Hyper Beam's tremendous power became evident the moment it met Bubble Beam. The searing heat instantly dissolved every bubble, and the beam continued forward until it struck Poliwhirl!

Boom!

The attack sent Poliwhirl flying across the battlefield and crashing into another stone pillar. By the time it slid to the ground, it had already fallen unconscious!

It had been knocked out in one hit!

The referee needed only one glance before decisively announcing the result.

The battle had barely begun. Gyarados defeated Poliwhirl in their first exchange, leaving the spectators in heated discussion as cries of disbelief sounded everywhere.

This was not their first time seeing a one-hit knockout. In several of Ethan's previous matches, Blastoise had defeated its opponent with a single attack.

But this was a quarterfinal. Every weak competitor had already been eliminated. Seeing a Pokemon lose in one blow at this stage was extraordinarily rare!

The difference in strength between these two Pokemon could only be described as enormous!

Misty's expression darkened when Poliwhirl fell. Her match had begun in the worst possible way. She had prepared extensively beforehand, but even after accounting for every possibility, she had never imagined Poliwhirl would fail to withstand a single move!

It also demonstrated how well Trinity's Pokemon had been trained. Otherwise, she would never have approached the group during lunch and spoken with such confidence!

Misty glanced at the camera aimed toward her. She knew Ethan was watching through the screen at that very moment, and the thought gave her tremendous courage.

Ethan is watching me right now. How can I disappoint him?

Only one Pokemon has fallen. The match isn't over, and I haven't lost yet!

Taking a deep breath, she solemnly drew her second Poke Ball.

Blue light flashed as Misty sent out Corsola once again. Gyarados had enjoyed a brief chance to recover, and the aftereffects of using Hyper Beam had faded. It could move normally again.

The two Pokemon now seemed to be starting on equal footing.

This time, Trinity launched the first attack. At her command, Gyarados used Hydro Pump, sending a thick stream of water toward Corsola. Misty answered with Mirror Coat once again!

Strange light flashed over Corsola's body. Hydro Pump struck it, then rebounded with even greater force!

Facing its own attack, Trinity ordered Gyarados to use Hyper Beam again. The powerful blast instantly scattered the returning water and continued toward Corsola.

Fortunately, Corsola jumped into the pool in time and avoided the Hyper Beam.

The attack shattered the stone pillar where Corsola had stood, scattering fragments in every direction without touching the Pokemon itself.

Watching from the waiting room, Ethan had learned enough from Gyarados's attacks to recognize its tremendous power. That last strike had not only destroyed a stone pillar, but vaporized a large volume of seawater in an instant!

Yet its failure to defeat Corsola immediately changed the situation.

Just as Ethan anticipated, Gyarados could not move temporarily after using Hyper Beam, giving Corsola an opening.

The water churned, and countless spikes of energy suddenly erupted from beneath the surface. They broke through the water and shot toward Gyarados at astonishing speed.

Anyone with a discerning eye could identify the Normal-type move: Spike Cannon!

Unable to evade, Gyarados was struck repeatedly by Spike Cannon. The pain sent its body toppling uncontrollably into another pillar. The impact was so powerful that the pillar itself tilted to one side!

Trinity's heart tightened, but Gyarados had not yet regained the ability to move. No matter how anxious she became, she could do nothing.

A moment later, Corsola suddenly leaped from the water and rammed all four horns on its back viciously into Gyarados!

Bang!

Corsola's body might have been small, but it contained tremendous strength.

The impact made Gyarados cry out before collapsing outright!

Ethan shook his head at the sight.

It was genuinely baffling. Just as Gyarados had knocked out Poliwhirl in one blow, Ethan would never have believed a Gyarados could fall after only two or three attacks if he had not witnessed it himself!

Had Corsola struck a vital point?

Trinity's Gyarados clearly emphasized offense at the expense of defense. Something had undoubtedly gone wrong with the direction of its training.

Such an approach worked perfectly while maintaining the initiative. Its tremendous offensive power could defeat opponents easily. But once the opponent counterattacked, Gyarados could withstand very few hits.

After defeating Gyarados, Misty danced with joy. Even through the screen, Ethan could feel her delight.

Trinity, by contrast, did not appear particularly disappointed. She calmly recalled Gyarados, then smiled as she threw her second Poke Ball!

Blue light flashed, and a Chinchou floated on the water.

Compared with the enormous Gyarados, the small Chinchou created a striking contrast. The spectators had never expected Trinity's final Pokemon to be so diminutive.

Yet no one dared underestimate it. Chinchou had fought brilliantly several times during Trinity's earlier matches and had already left a deep impression on the crowd!

Chinchou was both Water and Electric type. Its Electric-type moves were undoubtedly devastating on a battlefield like this. Any opponent daring to enter the water would be shocked mercilessly!

Misty swallowed. She also knew the real challenge had arrived!

Sure enough, when the third battle began, Chinchou immediately fired electricity into the sky. Dark clouds gathered overhead under the current's influence, and only seconds later, a thick bolt of lightning descended!

Chinchou had opened with Thunder!

Even among Electric-type moves, Thunder possessed exceptional power.

Faced with enough force to knock out Corsola in one blow, Misty's expression changed dramatically. She hurriedly ordered Corsola to protect itself with Mirror Coat!

In a flash, the lightning struck Corsola. Fortunately, it reacted in time and successfully used Mirror Coat at the final possible instant. Once reflected, the bolt shot toward Chinchou with even greater power!

Crackle!

Chinchou did not dodge and took the lightning directly. Yet when the smoke and electricity disappeared, it remained floating there entirely unharmed!

Ethan shook his head helplessly.

Clearly, this Chinchou possessed the Volt Absorb Ability. Electric attacks caused it no harm and restored its stamina instead!

Now the situation was truly dire.

On this battlefield, Chinchou's Electric-type moves dealt super-effective damage to Corsola, while the electricity reflected by Corsola could not harm Chinchou at all. How was Misty supposed to fight?

Misty also realized how unfavorable the situation had become. When she saw Corsola's current condition, worry filled her face.

Mirror Coat could reflect an opponent's move with greater force, but its user still suffered some damage. Corsola had used it several times within a short period, placing an enormous burden on its body!

Before Misty could devise a solution, Chinchou attacked again.

This time, it used Confuse Ray. With no other choice, Misty ordered Corsola into the water to evade.

But that accomplished nothing. Chinchou immediately extended its two long, lantern-like antennae into the pool and indiscriminately used Confuse Ray beneath the surface!

Intense pink light suddenly spread through the water. Refracted and reflected by the sea, it made the entire battlefield look extraordinarily eerie!

In the end, Confuse Ray completely enveloped Corsola underwater. With nowhere left to flee, Corsola could only climb back onto a stone pillar. Its eyes had turned entirely pink under the move's influence, and its body swayed unsteadily. Its awareness had clearly fallen into complete confusion!

No matter how Misty called out and encouraged it, Corsola could not regain clarity. Even her command to use Recover could no longer reach its ears.

Trinity gave Corsola no opportunity to recover. At her command, Chinchou used Thunder Shock and defeated Corsola completely!

As soon as Corsola fainted, the referee announced the result. Misty had lost to Trinity and was eliminated from the tournament in the quarterfinals!

In the waiting room, Ethan sighed and rose to leave.

He did not watch Misty's helpless expression on the screen or wait for her return so he could console her. His own match was about to begin.

He still met Misty walking the opposite way inside the competitors' passage. When she saw him, her body trembled. She lowered her head, unable to meet his eyes.

After losing, the person she felt she had disappointed most was Ethan. He had supported her so strongly, yet she had failed to win and betrayed his trust!

Ethan must be disappointed in me now, right?

Ethan approached Misty and smiled gently at her guilty expression.

"What's wrong? Are you upset because you lost?"

"Of course not..." Misty shook her head, but her voice grew quieter. "Miss Trinity's Pokemon really were powerful. I couldn't defeat her this time because I wasn't strong enough. It's just... It's just..."

She repeated herself twice, but no matter how she tried, she could not say what came next.

Ethan extended a finger and gently lifted her chin. His brilliant smile instantly filled her vision.

"It's true that you aren't strong enough yet, but you don't need to lose heart, and you certainly don't need to feel that you've disappointed anyone. When you fall, you should get back up in the same place. Your Pokemon really do need to improve. After the Silver Conference ends, why don't you travel with me through the next region?"

Misty's body jolted. She could hardly believe her ears!

Ethan had actually invited her to travel with him?

She had not misheard him, had she?!

Once she confirmed that Ethan was not joking, she wiped the tears from the corners of her eyes and nodded rapidly. At that moment, she felt as though brilliant, warm sunlight had surrounded her heart!
