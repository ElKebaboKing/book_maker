Chapter 230: The Spike Shell Badge

When Ethan accepted the third round, Rudy drew a deep breath and sent out his Pokemon first. Blue light flashed, and a Starmie appeared on the battlefield.

So eager to win back a round, Rudy had abandoned even his own rule requiring the challenger to choose first.

Starmie was a Water- and Psychic-type Pokemon. Ethan could have matched its Water type with Blastoise, but after a moment's thought, he chose Gardevoir.

Rudy wanted to win, but Ethan had no intention of losing. He would give him no such opening. And if Starmie really was Rudy's strongest Pokemon, he had every reason to take it seriously.

Rudy frowned at Gardevoir. He had clearly never seen one, and the unknown made him uneasy.

"What Pokemon is that?"

"Gardevoir," Ethan said. "Don't worry. I'm still following the rules. They do share a type."

That told Rudy the graceful, humanlike Pokemon had at least a Psychic typing. He ignored Ethan's jab at his own breach of the rules. Winning this round mattered more than anything now. If he wanted to save face, he had to give it everything he had.

Both Pokemon were ready. Rudy decided to strike first.

"Starmie, Water Gun!"

A jet of water shot from one of Starmie's hard points. Ethan did not move or speak, but Gardevoir understood his command through their mental link. A barrier formed from psychic power appeared before it.

Splish, splash!

The water struck the invisible barrier, broke into spray, and ran across the ground.

A Psychic type after all. Rudy clicked his tongue. What a nuisance.

Seeing that he could not break through Gardevoir's defense, he tossed his head and snapped his fingers.

"Then I won't hold anything back! Come on, let's dance to the music!"

Dance? Ethan stared. One of Rudy's attendants in the hot-air balloon switched on a speaker. Fast, rhythmic music poured out, filling the battlefield.

Mahri swayed to the beat. "It's my brother's music! Great, he's finally using that move!"

On the field, Rudy lost himself in the rhythm and performed a short modern dance. Ethan watched in embarrassment and instinctively stepped away. A man who could dance like that without a trace of self-consciousness was frightening. Better to keep his distance.

Worse, Starmie began spinning to the music too.

After his dance, Rudy raised his voice. "This is our dance of passion! Starmie, give them a Thunderbolt full of passion!"

Ethan blinked. He had not expected Starmie to know an Electric-type move. Then his eyes fell on the puddle Water Gun had left in front of Gardevoir. He understood Rudy's plan and hurriedly told Gardevoir to move.

Electricity burst from the spinning Starmie in every direction. Gardevoir used Teleport to appear elsewhere. An instant after it vanished, a current struck the puddle. It crackled sharply, and white vapor billowed upward.

That was close. Rudy did have a tactic after all.

Water Gun could soak an opponent, setting it up for an Electric-type attack. Without advance warning, who would expect a Water-type Starmie to use Thunderbolt? A Trainer caught off guard could suffer badly.

But surprise was what made the combination work. Once it had failed, it had lost its advantage. Starmie had no other unexpected trick to reveal.

Ethan could also see what Rudy's dance training had achieved. It made his Pokemon more agile, but had given them no extra power.

The result was soon clear. The music seemed to make Starmie faster still, but Gardevoir found its opening. Magical Leaf staggered Starmie; Disarming Voice followed, and Dazzling Gleam finally knocked it flat.

Thud!

Starmie toppled over and lay motionless. Rudy felt his hands and feet go cold. He had lost completely.

Three rounds, three defeats. However much he hated admitting it, he now understood the gulf between himself and Ethan.

With a helpless sigh, Rudy recalled the unconscious Starmie. He took a badge from his clothes, walked over, and tossed it to Ethan.

"You won. By the rules, this badge is yours!"

Ethan caught it easily. He had been about to say something polite, but Rudy turned and left without looking back. He seemed unwilling to remain there another second. He had lost face, and there was already bad blood between them. Perhaps he had nothing to say. At least Ethan was spared the trouble of trying to console him.

He shook his head and studied the badge. The Trovita Gym's Spike Shell Badge was flat, shaped like a spiny shell with more than a dozen points. An unidentified pink stone was set in the center of its spiral. Ethan did not find it as attractive as his first two Orange League badges.

He had just placed it in his badge case when startled cries came from above. With the match over, Sabrina had climbed over the balloon's railing and jumped before it could descend.

"What?"

Mahri and Rudy's attendants stared in shock. Even Rudy looked up at the sound and widened his eyes. Was she trying to get herself killed?

Ethan whistled sharply. Gardevoir teleported to his side, and he quickly returned it to its Poke Ball. He leaped onto Charizard and flew toward Sabrina.

Sabrina had intended to use her psychic powers to land safely. But when she saw Ethan coming for her on Charizard, she smiled faintly, let her powers rest, and watched him with a gentle gaze.

Charizard swept beneath her like the wind. Ethan caught her securely in his arms.

Cradled like a princess, Sabrina looked up at him and smiled. "Now that we have the badge, shall we leave?"

"Of course. There's nothing here to keep us." Ethan looked at her face and could not resist kissing her. Then he lightly nudged Charizard's side with one foot. "Charizard, don't land. Let's head straight for the next Gym!"

Charizard roared in answer and beat its wings powerfully. With Rudy watching in mixed feelings and Mahri looking on in admiration, it flew into the distance and disappeared.
