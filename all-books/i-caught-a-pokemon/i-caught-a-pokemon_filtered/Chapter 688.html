Chapter 688: So That's What You're Like, Juan!

The girl who had called out was Lisia, the idol Ethan had battled only yesterday. He had not expected to find her here too.

Kinta had said she was Wallace's niece. Standing side by side, their matching blue-green hair and similar features made the family resemblance plain. They must both have taken after Lisia's mother's side of the family.

Ethan smiled and waved to her. Misty pursed her lips and looked away. Why does this girl keep turning up?

Wallace looked from his niece to Ethan in surprise. You know each other?

Of course! Lisia told him all about the previous day's battle, complete with gestures and dramatic detail.

When she finished, Wallace laughed. You say the match ended in a draw?

It did! Lisia nodded. I lost, but he said himself that we could call it a draw.

Then you did very well. Not many people can hold a man who's won the championships of two regions to a draw!

What? Lisia stared at her uncle. What do you mean?

You still don't see it? With a flourish, Wallace pointed at Ethan. This is the man everyone's been talking aboutthe winner of the Indigo League who went on to win the Silver Conference!

What? Lisia turned to Ethan in shock. She had battled the Champion of two regional tournaments without knowing it.

Now she remembered why he had looked so familiar. She had seen videos of his battles and posters promoting him.

You really kept that from me!

Kept what from you? Ethan smiled. I told you the truth. Yesterday was our first meeting. You recognized me from somewhere else, and you never asked my name. How could I have hidden it?

Lisia was ready with a retort, but a moment's thought stopped her. Neither she nor Kinta had asked his name, even after the battle. Strictly speaking, Ethan was right. She still felt deceived, and her cheeks puffed out in protest.

Ethan found the expression amusing. He shook his head and spoke more formally. Then let me introduce myself now. I'm Ethan Vale, a Trainer. I came today to challenge Mr. Juan, the Leader of the Sootopolis Gym.

That's better. Lisia's irritation vanished as quickly as it had come. But my teacher isn't here. He's been so busy lately that he hasn't attended to the Gym for days.

Her teacher? Ethan had known Wallace was Juan's student. So Lisia had studied under him too. Uncle and niece had learned from the same master, an unusual arrangement. Both had become outstanding in their own fields. Their talent mattered, but Juan must have been an excellent teacher as well.

Still, Juan really was absent. Had Ethan come for nothing? He exchanged a disappointed look with Misty.

Wallace and Lisia came down the red-carpeted marble stairs to meet them. Unlike yesterday, Lisia circled Ethan with unabashed curiosity. When she saw his troubled expression, she smiled.

You don't have to worry. You're lucky you ran into me today! One call from me, and my teacher will come straight back, wherever he is.

Would that be all right? Ethan asked. You said he's busy. The festival's large enough that the whole city has spent days preparing. Everyone I saw outside seemed to take it seriously. I wouldn't want him to change his plans just for me.

Don't worry. Lisia waved away his concern. They hold this festival every year. My teacher knows it inside out. He doesn't need endless rehearsals. Besides...

Her smile turned mischievous. He only looks busy. Really, he's hiding from those women who are a little too enthusiastic about him. That's why he hasn't dared come back to the Gym!

So that explained the middle-aged women waiting outside. Ethan had learned a little piece of local gossip. Being popular could be troublesome too.

...

Lisia called Juan. While they waited, she and Wallace showed Ethan and Misty around the Gym. Few visitors would receive a personal tour from them, but they treated Ethan as an equal.

As they talked, Ethan learned more about both of them. Wallace, for one, was extremely busy. He offered few details, yet Ethan sensed that he was investigating something important. His return to Sootopolis was only a short pause to rest and consult some information. He might leave again that afternoon or the next day. Meeting him now had been fortunate.

Lisia's schedule was little easier. She was touring and performing around the world, spending most of her time outside Hoenn. The coming festival had brought her home: as an idol from Sootopolis, she had been invited to film a short promotional piece. It also gave her a brief chance to rest with her familyand had led to yesterday's encounter in the park.

Fame kept both of them busy. The admiration they enjoyed came with demands on their time.

Ethan wondered what he would do when his own travels ended. Would he settle somewhere quiet and enjoy an early retirement, or keep himself occupied as they did? He put the thought aside. That decision lay far in the future. For now, he had enough to do.

After the tour, Wallace had other matters to handle and left the Gym. He had wanted to watch the man who had won two regional tournaments battle his teacher, but his schedule would not allow it.

Without her uncle there, Lisia grew even more inquisitive. She asked Ethan how he had begun as a Trainer, which rare Pokemon he had met, and what adventures he had experienced. He could hardly keep up with her questions.

Perhaps a life planned around performances made travel sound especially appealing. With each answer, her eyes seemed to shine brighter.

Misty grew more annoyed by the minute. She had already been wary of a pretty girl her own age. Now Lisia's interest in Ethan seemed suspicious to her. Eventually she had had enough, idol or not, and began making pointed remarks.

Only then did Lisia give the equally pretty girl at Ethan's side her full attention. From the first moment, Lisia had liked Misty no more than Misty liked her. Perhaps two equally outgoing girls, one sharper and the other more playful, were bound to rub each other the wrong way.

How strange, Misty said coolly. You're a Coordinator, yet you're so interested in a Trainer's travels. Aren't you crossing into someone else's territory?

What she meant by territory was clear enough to her, if no one else.

Coordinators and Trainers both travel with Pokemon, Lisia replied, giving her a sidelong look. How is that crossing a line? Speaking of lines, I think distance is far more frustrating. You can see someone but never reach them. However hard you try, you're still standing in the same place.

They were young, but neither missed the meaning behind the other's words. Ethan listened to them trade barbs and felt a headache coming on. How had the conversation turned into this?

Rapid footsteps from the hall interrupted them. Before Ethan saw who was coming, a strong, slightly resonant voice rang through the Gym.

Little Lisia, your teacher is here! Why did you need me back so urgently?

Ethan turned. A man around forty was hurrying toward them in an ornate blue suit with a white bow tie. He had a neat little mustache and a white feather ornament on his head.

This was Juan: the current Sootopolis Gym Leader, teacher to Wallace and Lisia, a Trainer and Coordinator, and the man the local ladies called their ravishing Gym Leader.

Ethan could see why Juan had so many admirers. Even to another man, his charm was obvious. It was more than his handsome face; it came naturally from the way he carried himself. Like Wallace, Juan had gathered a remarkable collection of titles and admirers. Ethan could imagine Lisia doing the same when she was older.

But why was Juan speaking to her with such extravagant affection?

Juan reached Lisia, dropped to one knee like an actor on a stage, and lifted his voice. The moment you called, I set everything aside! Little Lisia, it's been so long. You grow more beautiful and adorable every time I see you.

Ethan understood. Juan was hopelessly fond of young girlsor, more accurately, doted on Lisia like a daughter. He wondered what the women outside would think if they saw this display.

Lisia seemed used to it. She smiled sweetly. I didn't call you here for myself. He did. She indicated Ethan. He's here to challenge the Gym. You were away, so I took the liberty of contacting you.

Juan seemed to notice the other visitors for the first time. His eyes settled on Ethan, a handsome man standing close to Lisia, and his expression changed at once.

Who are you? How do you know Lisia?

He studied Ethan with the alarm of a father who had just seen a prized daughter approached by a flashy young delinquent. Ethan felt the hostility and could not resist teasing him.

Sorry to trouble you, he said pleasantly. I am here to challenge the Gym. Lisia saw you were out and kindly called you for me, just as she said. As for how we met, perhaps you should ask her.

You've got some nerve! Juan thought. His first suspicion had only been a wild guess. Now he was convinced this young man was trying to charm his precious student.

I see. Juan spoke through clenched teeth. A Gym challenge, is it? Good. I haven't had a proper chance to stretch in quite some time. Since you've come to me, let me see what you can do!

He snorted, turned, and strode deeper into the Gym without giving Ethan a chance to answer. One teasing reply had been enough to make him angry. At this rate, Ethan would not need to ask Juan to use his strongest Pokemon. Juan would choose them himself.
