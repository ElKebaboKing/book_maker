Chapter 707: An Easy Operation

Team Magma's enormous submarine rose slowly and broke the surface in less than half a minute. The hatch opened. Maxie came out at once with Tabitha and the other members.

They had not even stepped off the submarine when they saw a huge red figure roaring amid flowing magma in the center of the island. They knew Groudon well enough to recognize it immediately.

What? Tabitha cried. Groudon? Wasn't it supposed to be underground? How did it get up here?

Maxie's face darkened. Groudon should have been asleep. Their instruments had tracked its stable energy for some time. Without an outside disturbance, it might have slept for months or even years. Why had it awakened now, at the worst possible moment?

Controlling a dormant Groudon and controlling an awake one were very different undertakings. He scanned the islands, but saw no person or Pokemon besides Groudon.

Boss, what do we do? Tabitha asked. He knew this change threatened the entire plan.

Maxie had to choose: take his force in and attempt to capture Groudon despite the danger, or withdraw and try again after regrouping. He made his decision in less than two seconds.

We can't wait. We have to control Groudon before that man catches up.

Tabitha stared at him. You think Courtney can't stop him?

Of course she can't. We've sent many people against him before, and they failed. She can't do it alone.

But you gave her the controller to destroy the base. You know her. She'll use it. How could he survive an explosion that wipes out the entire place?

Don't underestimate him. I doubt an explosion would kill him easily. If he were that simple to deal with, he wouldn't be a threat to both us and Team Aqua.

Then why leave Courtney there and sacrifice the base?

Maxie gave him a cold look. Once an enemy discovers a secret base, it has no value. Better to blow it up ourselves than let them dismantle it. At least the destruction might delay him and keep him from following us.

Tabitha nodded. For all his broad build and easy smile, he could be cruel. Neither he nor Maxie mentioned the members who might still be inside the base, as though their lives barely mattered.

They moved cautiously onto the island. Every eye settled on the ancient Legendary Pokemon. Even members who knew little about Groudon were awed by the sight.

It stood more than three meters tall. Its head looked helmeted, while overlapping red plates armored its back and tail. Its gray underside appeared softer. Spikes covered its powerful body. Cold eyes and sharp teeth made it formidable, and its heavy, spiked tail looked strong enough to dig through the earth.

Some said Groudon's design recalled Behemoth from the Hebrew Bible, an unconquerable primordial lord of the land. In Hoenn's legends, Groudon embodied the earth and made new land. It could clear rain clouds, and its harsh light and heat evaporated water to extend the continents. People suffering from floods had once hailed it as a savior.

Other stories said it battled Kyogre repeatedly in pursuit of nature's energy until Rayquaza stopped their struggle. Afterward, it slept in magma beneath the earth, gathering strength. Its waking could cause volcanoes to erupt. However often someone saw it, its presence remained overwhelming.

Now Groudon was visibly furious at being awakened. Magma welled out of the hole it had torn in the ground and spread across the island, making it look more imposing still.

Hidden behind volcanic rocks at a distance, Courtney watched with wide eyes and held her breath. Her heart raced. She had seen this scene in her mind more than once. Soon she would learn whether those images had been hallucinations or true glimpses of the future.

Maxie and his people followed the same path toward Groudon that she remembered. Their movements matched her vision exactly. She was almost certain now: she had seen what was about to happen.

Boss... Her fists tightened.

Groudon noticed the intruders. Energy gathered in its mouth. It fired Hyper Beam, carving a terrible path across the island and the sea. Then it turned toward the approaching group. The beam swept toward them.

Danger! Courtney could not stand and watch Maxie and the others die as she had foreseen. She rose without hesitation. Boss, look out!

Maxie and Tabitha thought for an instant that they had imagined her voice. Courtney? She was supposed to be at the base holding Ethan back. How could she be on this island?

Her warning gave them just enough time. They threw themselves clear as the beam reached them. The members behind them did not. The explosion hurled Maxie and Tabitha a considerable distance, but their quick escape kept them from serious injury.

When they recovered and looked back, a crater marked the place where their members had stood. None had survived. Groudon's power was terrifying. Maxie's face changed color. An instant later and he would have died with them.

Before he could demand an explanation from Courtney, he sensed something beside him. He turned. Gengar floated at his shoulder, grinning.

The shocks had slowed Maxie's reaction. Gengar snatched the pouch from his belt before he moved.

The orb! The pouch looked ordinary, but it held the Red Orb. Maxie had thought it safest on his person. He reached for it, too late. Gengar rose with a cackling laugh and flew away.

A man stepped out from behind volcanic rocks and caught the pouch. Ethan Vale.

Maxie understood at once. He looked between Ethan and Courtney, fury contorting his face. Courtney! You betrayed me! You betrayed all of Team Magma!

Courtney stood rigid, unable to answer. Betrayal had not been what she wanted. Circumstances had brought her here. But who would believe that?

Ethan took the Red Orb from the pouch, checked it, then put it back and secured the pouch behind him. Your commander is my prisoner, Maxie. I had her guide me here. You didn't expect me to escape your base, reach the island first, and take back the orb, did you? Your plan is over.

That made Maxie angrier. Prisoner? Courtney was walking freely, with no one forcing her. Ethan's explanation only deepened his suspicions. Everything made sense now. Courtney had told Ethan where they were going. Ethan had awakened Groudon. She was a traitor.

He opened his mouth to accuse her again. Groudon did not give him the chance.

It attacked whatever moved before it. Ethan had revealed himself and become another target. Groudon fired Hyper Beam toward him, Courtney, and Maxie.

Halfway there, a second Hyper Beam shot in from another direction. The beams met in a violent explosion. Groudon turned toward the attacker.

Tyranitar came out from behind the volcanic rocks and stared it down. Groudon was larger and far more imposing, but Tyranitar did not flinch. The two Pokemon stepped toward each other. A fierce battle was about to begin.

Tabitha limped to Maxie's side. A stone fragment from the explosion had cut his knee. The wound was not grave, but it made walking difficult.

Boss, what now?

Maxie had no ready answer. His members were dead, Courtney had apparently betrayed him, the orb was gone, and Groudon was raging beyond their control. He had arrived confident and lost everything before the operation could truly begin.

He took a breath and forced himself to think. We withdraw for now.

Withdraw? Tabitha glanced at the fallen members. Leaving like this felt unbearable.

Maxie looked toward Ethan. He wins this time. It isn't over. We need to rebuild our strength, then find a chance to take the orb back.

Ethan could not hear their words, but he read their intent. He had no intention of letting them escape. So far the operation had been easier than any he could remember. Shelly's plan had barely begun, and he already had the orb.

He was about to send Gengar after Maxie and Tabitha when something flashed across his vision. The next instant, his Tyranitar slammed into a volcanic boulder.
