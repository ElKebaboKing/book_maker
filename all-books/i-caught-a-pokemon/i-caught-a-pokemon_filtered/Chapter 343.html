Chapter 343: Dragonair Sweeps the Match

Inside the Gym, Sabrina had entered the spectator area and chosen a seat at random.

From her position, the view was wide and clear, almost like watching an underwater show. She could see the entire battlefield.

She sat with her legs crossed, looking completely relaxed. Deep down, she clearly believed Ethan would win this match with ease.

This was only a private Gym. Its strength could not compare with an official one. Unless Ethan became careless and suffered an embarrassing upset, he could not possibly lose.

Meanwhile, on the seabed, Dorian stood in his Trainer command area. When Ethan looked over, Dorian pointed to an indicator light beside them.

They could not speak underwater and had to communicate through simple hand signals and the lights.

Through his diving goggles, Ethan watched the indicator change from red to green. The match had officially begun.

He waved to Dragonair. Understanding immediately, Dragonair charged toward Qwilfish.

Dragonair was not a Water-type Pokemon, but it could still move through the sea with the agility of a sea snake. The inability to issue verbal commands, however, posed a serious test of the understanding between Ethan and Dragonair.

Dorian faced no such difficulty. He regularly rehearsed underwater dances with his Pokemon and was an experienced swimmer. He and his partners seemed entirely comfortable.

Dorian merely cast one glance toward Qwilfish, and it attacked with perfect understanding.

It swallowed seawater and inflated. Once its body reached a certain size, it exerted its strength and fired more than a dozen poisonous spines at Dragonair at once, covering a broad area.

Dragonair immediately answered the sharp projectiles with Dragon Rage. The stronger move knocked every spine aside, then continued forward with power to spare and struck Qwilfish.

The scattered spines slowly disappeared into the current.

After landing one hit, Ethan did not stop attacking.

He made a sweeping motion with his right hand. Before Qwilfish could regain its balance, Dragonair slammed into it and sent it tumbling backward through the water more than a dozen times.

Two consecutive hits caught Dorian by surprise and tightened his nerves.

Water-type Pokemon held an advantage beneath the sea, yet Dragonair seemed equally at home here.

The battle had begun under an ominous sign for Dorian.

He shook his head and drove away the unlucky thought. Once Qwilfish regained its balance, he signaled for it to counterattack.

The spines across Qwilfish's body began glowing a sinister green. It charged at Dragonair in return.

Poison Jab!

If it landed, the Poison-type move would deal considerable damage and might poison Dragonair as well.

At the critical moment, Dragonair glanced back. Ethan's right hand was drawing circles through the water. Understanding his signal, Dragonair opened its mouth and used Twister.

What would Twister do underwater?

On land, it naturally formed a powerful tornado capable of sweeping everything away. At the surface, it could become a waterspout and pull seawater upward. Underwater, as it did now, Twister created a vortex that continually swallowed the surrounding sea.

The vortex expanded rapidly. Its pull grew stronger and stronger until it occupied half the battlefield. Unable to evade in time, Qwilfish was sucked inside.

Qwilfish's balloon-like body made it a poor swimmer. Ethan was exploiting its lack of speed, knowing it could not escape the vortex's reach.

The tremendous suction dragged Qwilfish into the current. It struggled desperately, but no matter how it twisted its body, it could not leave the area.

Gurgle...

Countless bubbles rose as the vortex churned the water.

Qwilfish struggled for a long time. At last its strength failed, and the vortex pulled it into the center, spinning it wildly beyond any control.

When the vortex finally dispersed, Qwilfish's eyes had rolled back. It was completely unconscious from the violent rotation.

Dragonair did not even need a finishing blow. Qwilfish had already lost the ability to battle.

Its body floated helplessly toward the surface. Seeing that it was unconscious, Dorian hurriedly took out a Poke Ball and silently recalled it.

He stole another glance at the perfectly composed Ethan and could not help marveling at the strength of this man's Pokemon.

Unfortunately, Dorian had neither watched Ethan's matches nor followed the related news. He had no idea that the man before him was the current Indigo League Champion and an Honorary Trainer of the Orange League.

Even without knowing Ethan's identity, Dorian could clearly feel the enormous difference between their strength.

He shook his head, steadied himself, and sent out his second Pokemon. This time, Mantine swam onto the field.

Ethan had rarely encountered the species and studied it for a moment.

Mantine was a Water- and Flying-type Pokemon. It resembled a manta ray from Earth, one of the flat fish sometimes known as devil rays. It was said to be remarkably gentle, usually swimming leisurely through the sea, though it occasionally leaped from the water and flew above the surface.

Ethan remembered that Mantine had excellent special defense. He would need to rely primarily on physical attacks.

He checked Dragonair's condition.

It had taken no hits and spent little stamina in the first round. Although Dorian had changed Pokemon, Dragonair remained in excellent shape. Ethan kept it on the field.

The indicator changed from red to green again.

As the battle began, Dragonair immediately used Dragon Rush. It closed the distance and slammed into Mantine. While evading the counterattack, Dragonair seized an opening and used Wrap, coiling its tail tightly around Mantine's body.

Mantine soon used its size to break free, but Dragonair's smooth combination had already landed and cost it a considerable amount of stamina.

The momentum shifted the next instant. At Dorian's urging, Mantine went on the offensive.

The two Pokemon chased each other through the sea. Moves streaked back and forth beneath the water, leaving rings of ripples in their wake.

This round was clearly more difficult than the first. Mantine swam far faster than Qwilfish, making it much easier for it to evade attacks.

Even so, it was still no match for Dragonair.

Eventually, Dragonair found an opening and knocked it unconscious against the seabed with a single Dragon Tail.

After defeating two opponents, Dragonair returned to Ethan and happily rubbed its forehead against his arm.

The two underwater rounds had strengthened the understanding between them even further.

By this point, Ethan had formed an assessment of Dorian's ability.

Dorian's first two Pokemon were somewhat stronger than those of an average Trainer. He certainly possessed some skill, enough to explain why he dared establish a Gym here.

But Dorian's battle style was too conventional, and his commands showed nothing particularly impressive. That prevented his Pokemon from displaying their full strength.

After losing both rounds, Dorian sent out his final Pokemon: Lanturn.

Lanturn was a Water- and Electric-type Pokemon based on a two-horned anglerfish, the deep-sea predator that hunted with glowing antennae. Of course, Lanturn looked far cuter than a real anglerfish.

This Pokemon was Dorian's final hope. Whether he could recover even one round depended entirely on Lanturn.

Unfortunately for him, the first two victories had filled Dragonair with momentum and confidence.

In the third round, Lanturn's Electric-type moves caused Dragonair considerable trouble. Even after suffering injuries, however, Dragonair continued fighting with all its strength.

In the end, Dragonair completed a three-Pokemon sweep, defeating every member of Dorian's team by itself.

Even after Lanturn lost consciousness, Dorian could hardly believe his eyes.

I was defeated by a single Pokemon...?

Being swept three to zero was deeply humiliating. It meant the difference between the two Trainers was simply too great.

For Dorian's dream of leading a Gym, the loss felt like a bucket of cold water poured over him.

Am I truly not strong enough to become a Gym Leader?
