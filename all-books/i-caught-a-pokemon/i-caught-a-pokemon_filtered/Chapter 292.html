Chapter 292: Raising the Difficulty

"Both sides, send out your Pokemon!" the referee called.

As Gym Leader, Falkner went first. His opening choice was the Hoothoot that had been standing on one foot on his shoulder.

"Hoothoot, I'll leave the first match to you!"

"Hoothoot!"

It flapped to the center of the field. Falkner's apprentices took seats in the stands and fixed their eyes on the court. Their serious expressions made the atmosphere tense.

Sabrina gave them a glance and walked alone to the seats on the other side. She sat down gracefully, entirely at ease.

Ethan studied Hoothoot and considered his own team. His fingers passed over the Poke Balls on his belt and stopped at Gardevoir's.

"Come out, Gardevoir!"

He threw the ball. Blue light flashed, and Gardevoir appeared before Hoothoot.

"Gardevoir," Falkner said, folding his arms as he looked it over. "It's from another region. We don't often get to see one here."

"That's how regional differences work," Ethan said with a shrug. "Kanto and Johto Pokemon are uncommon in other regions too."

Neither Pokemon had a clear type advantage over the other. Hoothoot was Normal- and Flying-type, but it could use several Psychic-type moves. Ethan had chosen Gardevoir with that in mind.

"Begin!"

The two Pokemon started battling. After only a few exchanges, Ethan's brow slowly furrowed.

Gardevoir was not doing badly. Quite the opposite: it was doing too well. It controlled the match from the start and gave Hoothoot no chance to fight back. Whether Hoothoot tried the Flying-type Peck or the Psychic-type Confusion, Hypnosis, or Reflect, Gardevoir calmly dealt with it and counterattacked.

Falkner had raised Hoothoot well, but it was still an unevolved Pokemon. It could not match the fully evolved Gardevoir.

That made Ethan realize the problem. Winning was far too easy.

After the Indigo League tournament and the Orange League, bringing his championship team against the ordinary Pokemon used in Gym challenges was almost unfair. Gym Leaders owned strong partners, but they usually sent out less powerful ones against new Trainers. Otherwise, few beginners would ever earn a badge.

Those matches might still challenge other Trainers. For Ethan, they offered little resistance. A one-sided victory taught him almost nothing and did not help his Pokemon grow.

He had two choices. He could challenge Gyms with his newer Pokemon, or find some way to make the battles harder.

Hoothoot finally fell. It had barely inflicted any real damage on Gardevoir. Falkner's apprentices stirred in their seats, murmuring to one another. They had seen Ethan compete before, but only now, watching their teacher face him, did they realize they had underestimated the champion.

Falkner shook his head. "Your Gardevoir is strong. The difference between them was plain. Hoothoot had little chance."

Ethan showed no pleasure in the win. He sighed. "That was my mistake. I wasted a chance to get stronger through a proper battle."

Falkner raised an eyebrow. "What do you mean?"

Ethan met his gaze. "I'd like a more demanding match. Falkner, would you use your strongest Pokemon?"

"So you think little of the team I chose?" Falkner gave a rueful laugh. "Are you sure? That goes against the League's purpose in setting Gym challenges."

Ethan did not change his mind. Falkner considered him and nodded.

"Fair enough. This is too easy for you. If you're asking for my best, then I'll show you what I can really do."

He recalled Hoothoot and put the three Poke Balls he had chosen back into his pouch. Then he took out two different balls. They looked ordinary, but Falkner's expression suggested they were special to him.

"To be honest, I want a real contest with you too," Falkner said. "I want to see what an Indigo League champion can do. Now I'll show you how powerful a bird Pokemon can be when it dances through the sky!"

He threw the next Poke Ball upward.

"Come out, Staraptor!"

In a bright flash of blue, a fierce-looking bird Pokemon appeared overhead.

Staraptor. Ethan looked up. It was a large, powerful bird, the final evolved form of Starly and Staravia. Its sharp eyes, claws, and beak gave it a formidable air. Its tail feathers spread like a fan, and the dramatic crest on its head made it look especially striking.

This was Ethan's first time seeing one in person. He raised his Pokedex.

"Beep. Staraptor, the Predator Pokemon. Normal- and Flying-type. It leaves its flock to live on its own and takes great care over the shape of its crest. Its strong muscles and wings let it fly while carrying small Pokemon. Fierce by nature, it will challenge opponents larger than itself and keep attacking even when badly hurt."

Ethan felt a very different presence from Staraptor than he had from Hoothoot. This might truly be one of Falkner's strongest partners.

He called Gardevoir back to rest and threw his second Poke Ball.

"Your turn, Jolteon!"

To Ethan, Falkner's switch felt like skipping the first playthrough and going straight into a harder second one. He had asked for the greater challenge, so he would not underestimate it. He needed to seize the initiative from the start.
