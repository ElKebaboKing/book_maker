Chapter 465: Silver

Oliver nodded and dove beneath the water. A short while later, he surfaced again beside a white Pokemon with wings shaped like arms and a blue tail fin.

With a single glance, Ethan and the others immediately recognized the Pokemon as the legendary God of the SeaLugia!

Compared with the Lugia they had encountered in the Orange Islands, however, this one was far smaller. It stood only about as tall as a human child. Even among its own species, it was clearly still very young.

Oliver introduced the two sides. "This is my friend Silver. Silver, this is the Sea Hero and his traveling companions. Go ahead and say hello!"

Lugia's eyes curved into happy crescents as it called cheerfully to them. It did not seem shy around strangers or particularly frightened of humans.

Faced with such an enthusiastic God of the Sea, Ash, Misty, and the others quickly recovered from their initial bewilderment. They hurriedly bowed and nodded in greeting.

"S-Silver... H-Hello..."

"I'm Ash."

"And I'm Misty!"

This was the first time any of them had observed a Lugia from so close. Even Ash and Misty had only seen one from a distance in the Orange Islands. They had never been able to study one carefully at such close range.

For Misty in particular, Lugia and Kyogre were among the Pokemon that every Water Pokemon Trainer dreamed of and ultimately hoped to catch. Now that one had appeared directly before her, how could she possibly remain calm?

Likewise, this was the first time Richie and Brock had ever seen a Lugia with their own eyes. Even though this one was still only a juvenile, the sight brought them tremendous shock and wonder. They could hardly contain the surging excitement in their hearts!

Erika was the exception. Perhaps seeing so many Legendary Pokemon around Ethan had already made such encounters feel ordinary. Although this was also her first time seeing Lugia in person, she was surprised to discover that she felt only a little curiosity toward the God of the Sea. Beyond that, she had no other thoughts, almost as though she were looking at an ordinary Pokemon.

Perhaps this is what it means to have broadened my horizons...

With that thought, she turned toward Ethan. Sure enough, he looked calm and composed. Lugia's appearance had caused almost no visible change in him.

After everyone exchanged greetings, Oliver explained that he and Silver shared a secret base. Whenever other humans appeared, Lugia usually hid there.

When Ethan spotted Silver's shadow moments ago, it had been quietly leaving for that secret base!

Misty studied Silver for quite some time before finally asking with curiosity, "Oliver, how did the two of you meet?"

For some reason, Oliver blushed in front of the bright and beautiful girl. He lowered his head, unable to look Misty in the eyes. "I-It was a little while ago... I was playing beside the sea when I happened to see it... A-And then we became good friends."

Before he even finished speaking, his ears had turned bright red!

Unlike dense-headed Ash, Oliver had clearly awakened to romance much earlier despite being only seven or eight years old. Or perhaps, having always lived among these islands, this was simply his first encounter with such a pretty girl, and the sight had stirred his young heart!

This also proved that although Misty often called herself the world's most beautiful girl with some exaggeration, she truly was very pretty. Combined with her spirited, cheerful personality, she undoubtedly held tremendous charm for innocent young boys!

"I see. You really are lucky." Misty nodded in understanding. "Then you're the one who gave it the name Silver too, right?"

"Th-That's right..." Oliver scratched his head in embarrassment. "Most of its body is silvery white, so that's why I chose the name."

"That makes sense. It looks like Silver really likes the name!"

At Misty's praise, Oliver felt his heart pound so violently that it seemed ready to leap from his chest!

Nearby, Ash tilted his head and asked Oliver in confusion, "If you and Lugia are such good friends, why didn't you tell us it was here?"

The reason was so simple that, among everyone present, perhaps only the innocent and naive Ash still failed to understand it.

Before Oliver could answer, Richie explained for him. "Oliver wanted to protect Silver, so of course he wouldn't reveal anything about it. He didn't know us at all before now. If we'd been bad people with malicious intentions, we would've tried everything possible to capture Lugia the moment we learned it was here!"

"That's right." Oliver nodded, then secretly glanced at Misty again.

Once Oliver and Lugia came ashore, everyone sat together nearby. It was already noon, so Brock naturally took out his cooking equipment and began preparing lunch.

Erika hurried over to help. With two skilled cooks working together, a delicious aroma soon drifted through the air.

Both Oliver and Silver were drawn by the scent and repeatedly swallowed their saliva.

Oliver ate home-cooked meals every day, so the unfamiliar aroma had merely awakened his appetite. Silver, however, had clearly never seen fragrant human food before. It practically drooled with hunger!

Its behavior amused everyone and eased much of their tension around the Legendary Pokemon. Viewed without its divine aura, Lugia was simply another kind of Pokemon!

Before long, lunch was finally ready, much to Silver's eager delight. The instant Brock served it a heaping spoonful of food, it impatiently began devouring the meal.

Everyone exchanged looks and stopped watching it. They sat on the ground, picked up their bowls and utensils, and focused on finishing the food before them.

While they ate lunch, two men in black diving suits hid suspiciously behind a cluster of reefs in the distance. A large red R was printed across each man's chest, and they observed the group through a spy camera.

One of them took out a communication device and whispered, "This is Search Team C. Reporting to Dr. Danbawe've located the target!"

After a burst of electronic static, an elderly but powerful voice sounded through his earpiece.

"It's not Danba, it's Dr. Namba! Honestly, how many times do I have to tell you before you'll remember?"

"Ah... My apologies." The Team Rocket member wiped cold sweat from his forehead and apologized, then reported seriously, "Dr. Namba, we've discovered Lugia on a small island off Ogi Isle. Requesting further instructions!"
