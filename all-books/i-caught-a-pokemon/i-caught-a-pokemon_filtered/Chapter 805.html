Chapter 805: May Joins the Team

Ethan, Misty, and May waited in the lounge for more than an hour. Neither Tucker nor any of his staff appeared. They might as well have been forgotten.

Misty was growing impatient when the door finally opened. The staff member who had registered Ethan's challenge entered with an apologetic expression and a gleaming gold Symbol in his hand.

I'm sorry to keep you waiting, Mr. Vale. This is the Battle Dome's Tactics Symbol. It's yours now.

Ethan was not surprised that a staff member had brought the award instead of Tucker. If he had lost so badly, he would hardly want to face his opponent either. Tucker also had a serious public relations crisis to manage.

Ethan accepted the Symbol without comment, but Misty was not ready to let Tucker off. We've been traveling a long time, and this is the first time a Gym Leader hasn't handed over the prize himself. Mr. Tucker must be very busy right now.

Her sarcasm rubbed salt in Tucker's wounds. Fortunately for him, he was not there to hear it. The staff member looked uncomfortable, but he would not be passing it on.

You're joking, miss. His smile was stiff. We're not a Pokemon Gym, so our customs are a little different. It's getting late, and I won't disturb you any longer. Excuse me.

He shut the door without waiting for Misty to answer and hurried away. The moment it closed, Misty burst out laughing.

Serves him right! He tried to use Brother Ethan, and this is what he gets. I'd like to see how he keeps calling himself a Frontier Brain after that!

May scratched her cheek. She thought Tucker had behaved badly too, but she was less fiery than Misty. To her, the matter seemed settled. There was no need to keep after him.

Ethan examined the Tactics Symbol. He had worked so hard to earn it, after all, and it was proof that he had beaten Tucker.

Apart from a shape resembling the letter H on its face, it looked much like any other badge. It seemed to be made of solid gold, though. The Battle Dome and its image-conscious Frontier Brain certainly had money to spare.

With their purpose accomplished, the three collected their belongings and left. The Battle Dome would be little more than a minor episode in their journey, hardly worth dwelling on.

They headed west along the main road and reached Cerulean City that evening. Misty had not wanted to face her return home, but once they entered the city, she said little about it.

There was no reason to stay at a Pokemon Center when her home was here. She led Ethan and May straight to the Cerulean Gym.

The Gym looked much as Ethan remembered. Even the enormous picture of a Seel was still there. But parts of the outer wall and roof had begun to deteriorate, giving the building a neglected air.

After seeing Gyms across several regions, Ethan had to admit that Cerulean's was small and outdated by comparison. Once Misty took charge, she might need to renovate the entire place.

May saw it differently. She looked around with excitement. So this is your home? Wow, what a beautiful Gym! It looks like an aquarium. Do they put on shows inside?

Misty fell silent.

She did not blame May for the question. She blamed her three sisters for turning a perfectly good Gym into a place that visitors mistook for an amusement attraction.

What upset Misty most was Cerulean Gym's reputation throughout Kanto as the weakest official Gym. She often wanted to argue against it, but remembering her sisters' battling ability left her with little to say.

Just come inside, she said with a sigh.

Ethan and May followed her in. Perhaps because evening was approaching, there were no challengers or spectators for the water ballet. The great interior felt empty and cool.

Daisy! I'm home! Misty called.

No one answered. She led them deeper into the Gym.

For Ethan, returning here brought back his first Gym challenge and the later visit when he had helped with a performance. So much had happened since then, yet both visits felt like they had taken place only days ago.

Thinking of the performance also brought back the kiss he had shared with Misty's second sister, Violet.

They kept walking until voices reached them near the pool. Daisy, Violet, and Lily were practicing there, surrounded by Pokemon.

Whatever their skill as Gym battlers, the three had earned their reputation as the Cerulean sisters. They were beautiful and graceful, and their long, strong legs caught the eye. Many spectators came to their shows as much to see them as to watch the water ballet.

They gave the Gym's battles less attention, but no one could doubt their passion for the performances. Even after the day's shows ended, they often practiced late into the evening.

Nothing's changed, Misty said, spreading her hands with a helpless sigh. Then she cleared her throat.

Daisy, Violet, and Lily turned together. Their faces lit up when they saw her.

Misty!

Mist!

You little brat! You finally decided to come home!

They grumbled, but they were sisters who had been apart for a long time. Of course they had missed her. Their delight lasted less than a minute before Violet and Lily started scolding her.

You stayed away so long this time! We've had to run the Gym and keep up our performances. Do you know how exhausting that's been?

Exactly! You're not leaving again, whatever you say!

Misty's smile froze. She had not expected her second and third sisters to change their tune so quickly.

Daisy, the eldest, noticed their visitors. She smiled at Ethan. Mr. Vale, thank you for taking care of her.

Violet and Lily seemed to notice Ethan only then. Like two mermaids, they sprang from the water, climbed onto the poolside, and crowded around him.

Lily's eyes shone with curiosity. Nothing happened between you two on the road, did it?

Violet nudged Ethan with her elbow and grinned. She didn't try anything improper with you?

That's enough! Misty cried, embarrassed and flustered. What are you talking about? I'd never do that!

May had heard about Misty's sisters before, but meeting them showed her just how bold and lively they were. So these were Misty's sisters. Compared with them, May was far too gentle with her own brother, Max.

Perhaps to stop them teasing her in front of Ethan and May, Misty hurriedly pulled May over to introduce everyone.

This is May, my friend from Hoenn. May, these are my sisters. First, this one with the long blond hair who looks so gentle is Daisy, my eldest sister.

Daisy gave May a warm smile. Welcome to Kanto. Thank you for keeping Misty company on the journey.

Oh, you don't need to thank me, May replied quickly. Compared with the other two, Daisy seemed poised and caring, just as May would expect of an older sister.

Misty pointed to Violet. This is my second sister. She may be pretty, but she bullies me all the time, and she can be rather willful. Be careful around her.

Honestly! Violet put her hands on her hips. Is that any way to describe your sister? Then she turned to May with a fresh smile. Welcome to Cerulean Gym!

Ah... thank you. May hardly knew what to say.

Finally, Misty indicated Lily. And this is my third sister. She looks sweet, like a princess, but she loves taking my things!

Don't listen to her, Lily said cheerfully. She never looks after them, so I put them away for her.

Once they had all exchanged greetings, Misty began declaring how hungry she was. Daisy had no choice but to leave Violet and Lily with their guests while she made dinner.

Perhaps being home had let Misty relax completely. Ethan could even hear a hint of childish pleading in her voice. For all her complaints, she loved her family.

Dinner did not take long. As they ate together, Misty excitedly recounted the things she had seen and done on the journey. She could hardly get the words out fast enough. Ethan could hear how much she wanted to share her happiness with her sisters.

Daisy listened to her adventures, then turned to Ethan with a serious expression. You really have gone to a great deal of trouble for her. Thank you.

It's the other way around, Ethan said, waving off the thanks. Misty looked after me throughout our time in Hoenn. My journey wouldn't have gone so smoothly without her.

Misty knew he was being polite, but his words still moved her. Her eyes grew damp.

Brother Ethan...

...

That night, after a comfortable hot bath, Ethan was getting ready for bed when someone knocked on his door. At this hour, he thought he knew who it must be. He closed his robe, rose, and opened it.

Misty stood outside, just as he expected. To his surprise, May stood beside her.

Why had they come together? Misty visiting alone would have made sense. She was home now, and they would part the next day. She might want a private conversation before then. But why was May here? Had they met in the hallway, or planned this together?

While he wondered, Misty led May inside without ceremony. Unlike Misty, May was blushing.

What brings you both here? Ethan asked.

Misty sat on the edge of his bed. Before answering, she looked at May. May blushed more deeply. She opened her mouth twice but could not get the words out.

Misty sighed and turned to Ethan. All right, I'll say it. Brother Ethan, May wants to travel with you in the next region!

What? Ethan raised his eyebrows.

Misty and May had kept their discussions quiet. Ethan had assumed May had come to Kanto only to look around with Misty. Where had this idea of traveling with him come from?

He looked at May, but she lowered her head rather than meet his eyes. She gave a silent nod.

Well... Ethan hesitated. Why bring this up so suddenly?

As he remembered it, May had gone to Johto alone after the Ever Grande Conference to enter its Pokemon Contests. Instead she had come to Kanto with Misty, and now she wanted to travel with him. He could hardly help being surprised.

What's strange about it? Misty said with a smile. May's a Coordinator. Of course she needs to travel, grow stronger, and enter Contests in different places. And what better way to learn than traveling beside a champion?

There was sense in that. Still, they had given him no hint beforehand. They had simply come to his room and asked. He had had no time to prepare for it.

But he had no reason to refuse. Once Misty left the group, Ethan would be traveling through the next region alone. May's company would spare him that loneliness.

He looked at May again. This time she met his gaze, and he saw the determination in her eyes.

All right. Ethan gave her a gentle smile and nodded. Welcome to the team, May. Officially.
