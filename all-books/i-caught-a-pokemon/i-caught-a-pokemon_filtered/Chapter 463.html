Chapter 463: Richie Joins the Group

The two Pikachu chased each other nearby, having the time of their lives.

Richie did not notice the strange looks exchanged by the group and continued enthusiastically, "Before coming here, I researched that mysterious Pokemon. I still don't know exactly what it is, but I used the reports and rumors online to work out where it might appear and where it might be headed. Ogi Isle was the most suspicious location, so I came here immediately!"

Richie truly was impressive. Ethan and the others had reached this place using information from Maya, while Richie had uncovered everything entirely through his own investigation!

Since information from two different sources ultimately pointed to the same location, the chances that Lugia was somewhere near Ogi Isle were clearly greatest. It also proved that Ethan and the others had come to the right place.

Only then did Richie belatedly ask, "By the way, why did all of you come here?"

Ash opened his mouth to answer, but suddenly hesitated. He glanced at Ethan and ultimately swallowed his words.

That alone showed how much Ash had grown. He had actually learned to think before he spoke!

In the past, he probably would have answered without the slightest consideration!

Fortunately, there was nothing they needed to conceal. When Ash looked toward him, Ethan chose to tell the truth. "We're actually here for much the same reason as you."

Richie looked at them in mild surprise. "You mean you're also searching for that mysterious Pokemon? I thought I was the only person looking for it."

After hearing the news, Richie's heart instinctively tightened. If both groups shared the same goal, did that mean one side was destined to leave disappointed?

There was only one mysterious Pokemon. No matter who eventually caught it, the result would affect their relationship.

Ethan was one thing. To Richie, he was merely a powerful opponent who had defeated him, and the two had no real friendship to speak of. But Richie and Ash had similar personalities and shared a sense of mutual appreciation. He did not want a dispute over who caught the Pokemon to damage that precious bond.

Ethan saw his concern immediately and explained with a smile, "Although that mysterious Pokemon is our goal too, we're here for a different reason. To be precise, we came to protect it."

"Protect it?" Richie froze, then seemed to realize something and urgently asked, "Does that mean you know what the mysterious Pokemon really is?"

Ethan smiled calmly. "It's Lugia, the God of the Sea."

"Lugia!?" Richie's eyes widened at the name, and he cried out in shock. "The mysterious Pokemon is Lugia!? Is that true!?"

When Ethan nodded, Richie remained stunned for quite some time before finally accepting the truth.

He had imagined all kinds of possibilities for the mysterious Pokemon's identity, but the rarest possibility he had considered was a Shiny Pokemon. Who could have expected it to be the God of the Sea?

Judging from Richie's lingering astonishment, he was still processing the revelation.

Ethan continued, "According to the information we received, Team Rocket has already targeted it. Someone asked us to come here and protect this Lugia."

"I understand." Richie released a long breath, then spoke solemnly. "Please let me join you. I'll help too!"

"What? You don't plan to catch it anymore?"

Richie shook his head with a complicated expression and gave a wry smile. "It's the God of the Sea. A human shouldn't capture it."

Ethan looked at him in surprise.

Possessing that kind of restraint was genuinely admirable.

The last person Ethan remembered meeting with the same quality was Jimmy. When faced with Raikou, Jimmy had remained equally clearheaded. He also believed that Legendary Pokemon should not be captured by humans and ought to live freely in nature.

Unfortunately, Ethan completely lacked that kind of restraint. As a result, that very Raikou was now resting quietly inside one of his Great Balls!

With their goals aligned, everyone ended their rest and prepared to begin searching for Lugia immediately. Before they set out, however, Richie shared one piece of information.

"Before you arrived, I already explored all of Ogi Isle, but I didn't discover any sign of the mysterious Pokemon... of Lugia."

Ethan nodded. "In that case, let's focus directly on the smaller islands nearby."

Following his gaze, the others looked toward the island closest to Ogi Isle. It was a small and uninhabited island, less than half Ogi Isle's size. Two towering mountains stood at its center, surrounded by a dense ring of forest.

Encircled by the sea and even farther removed from humanity, an isolated island like this offered an excellent habitat for Lugia or any other Pokemon.

The group returned to the shore and looked across the broad expanse of water. With their boat gone, they released Pokemon of their own to carry them across the sea.

Ethan climbed onto Charizard and pulled Erika up behind him. Misty took Brock with her, while Ash brought Richie along.

The distance was only a little over two kilometers. It did not take long for the three Pokemon to reach the other island one after another.

Ethan was the first to set foot on its shore. He took out his map, only to discover that the island was not labeled with a name.

Another nameless island...

Once the others came ashore, the group headed into the island's interior together.

They had not gone far when they spotted a young boy floating in a small pool alongside a Lanturn. Both watched the newcomers nervously.

The boy looked about six or seven years old. He wore only a pair of swimming trunks, and his hair had the round style common among young boys. Lanturn, the evolved form of Chinchou, had a distinctly fishlike body and yellow markings across its face that resembled a mask.

Unlike Chinchou, Lanturn had only one antenna on its head, but the stalk branched into two yellow, spherical lights. The larger one hung in front of its head, while the smaller one extended from the branch.

Before Ethan could take out his Pokedex, Ash had already pointed his own at Lanturn.

The Pokedex announced, "Beep. Lanturn, the Light Pokemon. A Water and Electric type. Lanturn inhabits the deep sea and is known as the star of the deep. It releases a dazzling light to blind its prey, then strikes with electricity while the target is unable to move before swallowing it whole. Because prey is scarce on the deep seafloor, it is said to compete fiercely with Lumineon."
