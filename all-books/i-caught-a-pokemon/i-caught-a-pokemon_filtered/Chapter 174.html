Chapter 174: Never-Melt Ice

After Ethan's threat, the Team Rocket grunt seemed to give up resisting. He answered almost every question Lorelei asked, and she learned a great deal.

After their failures on mainland Kanto, Team Rocket had shifted its attention to remote places where it could rebuild its strength. Catching Pokemon quickly and increasing its fighting power had become a priority.

To Lorelei's surprise, they had built no secret base on the Sevii Islands. They had only put up a concealed warehouse in the forest on Five Island. To avoid drawing the locals' attention, they had sent just over a dozen people.

Lorelei felt some relief. Team Rocket had not been here long. She had feared it might already have worked its way into businesses and institutions across the islands. Instead, they could stop it before it took root.

"Who's in charge here?" she asked.

The grunt hesitated. "Lord Lambda. He's one of the four executives."

Lambda again? Ethan knew him well by now, and Lambda knew both him and his Pokemon. They had crossed paths many times. But Ethan would not underestimate him just because he had beaten him before. He never took an opponent lightly.

With no more questions to ask, Sabrina restored the other grunts to their normal size. Ethan used Team Rocket's own rope to bind their hands, tied them together, and prepared to take them back to the surface.

As they were about to leave, he spotted something glinting beneath an ice pillar. It looked unlike the ice around it.

What was that? Ethan went over and picked it up.

The object was cold and clear, shaped much like an ordinary chunk of ice. But it felt as hard as stone. Stranger still, it showed no sign of melting in his warm hand.

"What is this?"

Lorelei came closer. Her eyes lit up as soon as she saw it. "Oh! That's Never-Melt Ice."

"Never-Melt Ice?"

"It's a piece of ice changed by a strange power," she explained. "They say no amount of heat can melt it. Give it to a Pokemon to hold, and its Ice-type moves grow stronger."

So that was what he'd found. Ethan had played several Pokemon games, but he had mostly been interested in getting through the story. He had barely used even his healing items. Any useful equipment he found usually ended up forgotten in storage.

Lorelei looked as though she wanted the ice. Ethan promptly put it in his backpack.

She stared at him, then seemed to think better of speaking.

Ethan kept his face impassive. Had she thought he would give it to her? She might be an Ice-type master, but he wasn't about to hand another woman a gift like that in front of Sabrina. Besides, he had an Ice-type Pokemon himself. The ice would suit Articuno perfectly.

Unlike in the games, though, a chunk this large could not simply be handed to a Pokemon to carry. He would have to work out how to use it later.

They retraced their path through Icefall Cave, escorting the grunts outside.

Now that they had a firm lead, there was no need to search Six Island or Seven Island. Lorelei called a local police officer she knew. She asked him to take custody of the grunts and have officers ready near Five Island to support them when needed. They would keep their distance for now so Team Rocket would not be alerted.

After handing over the prisoners, the three of them ate a quick meal and took the speedboat straight to Five Island.

Its formal name was Time Island, though even Lorelei could not see what the place had to do with time. It was one of the most beautiful of the Sevii Islands, and tourism thrived there. Visitors arrived in great numbers every year. A wealthy local had even built an upscale resort in the north to accommodate them.

As they docked, a ship named the Sea Rapidash arrived with another load of tourists.

They passed through the crowds without stopping. Lorelei led them along familiar paths into the forest at the island's center.

Branches crowded overhead, and some of the towering trees were so broad that two people could barely reach around a trunk together. Deeper in the woods lay Team Rocket's warehouse.

Ethan crouched behind a tree and peered through thick bushes. A building almost blended into the surrounding forest. At its entrance, grunts carried crates in and out.

"Clever little rats," Lorelei muttered.

Team Rocket had disguised the building well. Without the prisoner's directions, someone searching at randomor flying quickly overheadcould have missed it entirely.

After watching a while, Ethan turned to her. "What's your plan?"

Lorelei studied the warehouse instead of rushing in. "What do you suggest?"

Sabrina said nothing, as if the matter did not concern her. Knowing he would get no proposal from her, Ethan thought for a moment.

"There aren't many of them, but we shouldn't let anyone escape. Let's start outside and take them down one group at a time."

Lorelei nodded. She was confident they could capture everyone, but called the officer and asked him to bring his people closer as a precaution.

Before she could move, Ethan took a strange mask from his backpack and put it on.

"What are you doing?" she asked.

"Practicing a good habit." His voice sounded quite different behind the Gengar mask. "I do good deeds without looking for credit. How could I let these people see my face?"
