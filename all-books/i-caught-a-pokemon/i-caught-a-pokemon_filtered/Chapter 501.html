Chapter 501: Admitting the Gap

Against Bugsy's first Pokemon, Ethan did not hesitate. He sent out Eevee.

He had chosen Eevee for this battle three days earlier, when he first came to the Azalea Gym. He wanted the match to help it train and grow quickly.

Since leaving Ecruteak City with Ethan, Eevee had seen a great deal. It had fought only a few one-on-one battles, but had joined quite a number of skirmishes large and small. It had gained plenty of experience.

It was not exceptionally strong, but it could put up a fight.

Blue light flashed, and Eevee appeared before Spinarak. It glanced this way and that, brimming with excitement.

Bugsy was not especially surprised to see an eager Eevee.

In his time as a Gym Leader, he had seen countless Eevee, as well as Flareon, Vaporeon, Jolteon, and other evolved forms. He had encountered plenty of much stranger, rarer Pokemon too.

An unevolved Eevee was so ordinary that Bugsy began to think the challenger must be ordinary as well.

Why else would a Trainer who had traveled so long still have an Eevee in its first form?

Given enough time together, Eevee could evolve into Espeon or Umbreon even without an Evolution stone. The fact that this one had not evolved suggested to Bugsy that, barring the unlikely possibility that Eevee did not want to, it and its Trainer had not formed a close bond yet.

Bugsy had a good eye. Eevee and Ethan were not close yet. What he did not know was that Ethan had only recently received it, so they had had little time to build trust.

Both Pokemon were ready. The referee swept his colored flag down, and the battle began.

Eevee showed its restless nature at once. Before Ethan could give an order, it rushed eagerly at Spinarak.

Ethan sighed under his breath.

They had not been together long enough. Eevee had also grown used to a relaxed, free life with the Kimono Sisters, and even outside battle it did not listen to Ethan very well.

This can't continue...

He would have to find time to train it properly.

Spinarak braced for Eevee's charge. The white pupils in its black eyes tracked every movement. As Eevee drew close and a hind foot touched the ground, Spinarak suddenly fired a strand of white silk at it.

The silk streaked through the air and reached Eevee almost instantly.

"Dodge it!" Ethan shouted.

Eevee had already thought to evade. It rolled lightly to the side, and the silk skimmed its fur before shooting past!

It had escaped by the narrowest margin. The close call startled Eevee into taking its opponent seriously.

Ethan called again at the right moment. "Eevee! Stop acting on your own and listen to me!"

They had at least spent some time together, and the danger had caught Eevee's attention. It obeyed without hesitation.

"Use Sand Attack!"

Eevee turned and scraped the ground hard with its hind legs. Wave after wave of dust mixed with sand and small stones flew toward Spinarak.

Across the field, Bugsy responded calmly. "Spinarak, use Agility!"

At his command, the normally slow Spinarak became a blur and escaped the Sand Attack in a flash.

Dust and grit covered the spot where it had stood, but did not touch even its shadow.

In their first exchange, Eevee had gained nothing and had nearly been caught by silk. It appeared to be at a slight disadvantage. Bugsy's Spinarak was clearly no weakling.

In most physical qualities, from speed to stamina, Eevee was better than an ordinary Spinarak. If their training were equal, it should have had a good chance of winning. That was why Ethan had dared send it out.

But this Spinarak had been thoroughly trained through its many Gym battles. It could not be judged by the standards of an ordinary member of its species.

The two Pokemon clashed again.

Eevee tried Tackle, hoping to use its greater speed to land a hit.

Spinarak used String Shot to anchor silk to a tree. It swung aside from the attack like a pendulum. When Eevee charged again, Spinarak swung swiftly from tree to tree and reached the far side of the battlefield with ease.

Once more, distance opened between them.

Even Erika, watching from the bench, sensed that something was wrong.

Spinarak seemed stronger, and the trees gave it a terrain advantage. With no favorable type matchup to help Eevee, winning this battle would be difficult.

How will you handle this?

Erika watched Ethan with growing concern.

He considered the situation for a moment and decided not to be stubborn. He took out Eevee's Poke Ball and recalled it.

"Oh? Giving up already?"

Bugsy folded his arms. He looked calm, apparently unconcerned by the switch.

Ethan felt no shame in admitting it. "My Eevee isn't a match for your Spinarak yet. It needs more training. So I'll use a somewhat stronger Pokemon against you."

He threw another Poke Ball. In a flash of blue light, Pupitar appeared before Bugsy.

"Pupitar?"

Bugsy grew a little more serious. Pupitar was rarer than Eevee, and its physical strength was greater. He could not afford to dismiss it.

When the battle resumed, Spinarak had a much harder time.

As Pupitar charged, Spinarak tried the same trick, using its silk to swing across the field and create distance. But Pupitar expelled compressed air from its body and reached Spinarak again almost at once!

Once that escape route failed, Spinarak lost its greatest advantage.
