Chapter 191: Mateo and Marissa

When they left the crystal cave, Sabrina expected Ethan to head for their next destination. Instead, he turned Charizard back toward Sunburst Town.

"Didn't you catch the Crystal Onix?" she asked. "Why are we going back?"

"Remember the little girl who threw those bottles into the sea? I want to find her and let her see the Crystal Onix."

Sabrina looked at him in surprise. "When did you start going out of your way to help strangers?" Before he could answer, she added, "Knowing you, that can't be the whole reason."

"You're right." Ethan laughed. "Her message led me towell, reminded me about the Crystal Onix. She helped me indirectly, so fulfilling her wish is the least I can do. But there's something else I want to ask them to help me with."

"Them?" Sabrina repeated.

Back in town, Ethan searched the busy streets. Most of Sunburst's glass shops were clustered there, but after checking more than a dozen, he had not spotted the girl.

He decided that looking at random was getting him nowhere. He entered a glassware shop, where the owner greeted them with a salesman's smile.

"What can I find for you? We have everythingcups, vases, and glass ornaments of all shapes!"

"Hello. Do you know a girl of about five or six named Marissa? Her older brother is a glassmaker."

"Marissa and her brother? You must mean Mateo. Are you planning to buy something from his shop?"

Before Ethan could respond, the man shook his head. "I don't like speaking ill of him, but his work isn't very good, and he hardly has anything to sell. I have a much better selection here. In fact, there's a sale todaybuy enough and I'll take fifteen percent off."

He was determined to make a sale. Ethan had not come to shop. "Sorry. I just want to visit them."

The owner's face fell. He pointed listlessly down the street. "That one over there. Mateo's shop."

Ethan thanked him. He and Sabrina crossed to the storefront the man had indicated.

Its sign was old, faded, and damaged in places. The shop appeared to have been there for many years. Yet at the busiest time of day, no customers stood outside it. Only a few ordinary pieces sat in the display window.

Could a shop like this do what Ethan needed? Since they had come all this way, he went inside.

The little girl they had seen on the beach was working at the counter. She stood on tiptoe, trying to wipe a table taller than she was. She wobbled as though she might fall.

At their footsteps she looked up and smiled. "Good afternoon! Welcome!"

Then she recognized them. "It's... you!"

The sight of Ethan and Sabrina brought back their encounter the night before. Marissa peered past them, apparently checking whether the frightening Charizard had followed them into the shop.

Ethan glanced around. Unlike the worn storefront, the interior was spotless. Someone clearly cleaned it every day. Through the door to the workroom, he could see a young man in an apron working glass near a furnace. Ethan could feel the heat from where he stood.

"Your name is Marissa, isn't it?"

She stepped back, wary. "How do you know?"

"Don't be afraid. We found one of your messages in a bottle. That's why we came."

"Really?" Her face lit up. "Do you know where the Crystal Onix is?"

Instead of answering, Ethan looked toward the workroom. "Could you ask your brother to join us? We can talk together."

Mateo had heard voices in the shop but assumed they belonged to customers. At the mention of the Crystal Onix, he set down the unfinished piece in his hands and hurried out.

"I'm Mateo, her brother. What do you need?"

Ethan took a sealed bag wrapped in layers of cotton cloth from his backpack. He opened it and showed Mateo the Never-Melt Ice he had found on the Sevii Islands.

"Master Mateo, this is a rare piece of Never-Melt Ice. Could you make it into an ornament that a Pokemon can wear?"

Mateo examined it and shook his head. "I'm sorry. I work with glass. I don't make things like that."

"That's a shame," Ethan said with an exaggerated sigh. "I was going to let you see the Crystal Onix after you finished, as payment for the work. If you can't do it, never mind."

"Wait. What did you say?" Mateo's eyes widened. "You can show me the Crystal Onix?"

Ethan took a Poke Ball from his waist and turned it between his fingers. "Yes. I didn't come all this way to make a joke at your expense."

Mateo stared at the ball for several seconds, then gave a disbelieving smile and shook his head.

"I'm sorry, but I can't believe you've caught it. I've searched for years, and so have other glassmakers on this island. I can't imagine an outsider finding and catching the Crystal Onix before any of us."
