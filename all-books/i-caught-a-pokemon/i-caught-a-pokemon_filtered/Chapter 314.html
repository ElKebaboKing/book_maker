Chapter 314: As If She Knew the Future

Once the two Trainers had settled the rules, Kabu raised his red and green flags level with both hands.

"Both sides, send out your Pokemon together!"

Clair seemed to have chosen already. She took out her first Poke Ball without hesitation and stood waiting, anticipation bright in her eyes.

Ethan smiled. She looked almost impatient to begin. For all her confidence, he did not think her Pokemon would be hard to predict.

This might be a blind selection, but Clair was a Dragon Tamer and a Dragon-type specialist, battling at Blackthorn Gym. She was unlikely to use anything outside the Pokemon she favored for that role. Many Dragon-type Pokemon also had the Flying type, and the field had a pool at its center. A Water/Dragon Pokemon was likely to be somewhere on her team as well.

Those clues narrowed the possibilities and let Ethan rule out several of his own Pokemon with poor matchups. But whom should he choose for the first round?

After thinking it over, he drew the first Poke Ball from the pouch at his right hip.

"I choose you!"

They threw their balls at the same moment. Blue light flashed, and two Pokemon appeared on opposite sides of the pool.

Clair had chosen the Charizard that fought so well against Vicious in the Dragon Holy Land. Ethan had sent out his first partner, Gardevoir.

Like their Trainers, the two Pokemon watched each other across the water, eagerness for battle growing in their eyes.

In terms of typing, Gardevoir's Fairy-type moves would deal only half damage to a Fire-type Charizard. Apart from that, neither had a clear type advantage over the other. Ethan seldom relied on Fairy-type moves anyway, so the resistance would have little effect on his plan. Their raw ability would matter more.

Kabu looked from one side to the other. Both were ready. He swept his flags down.

"The match begins!"

The two Trainers called orders almost at once.

"Gardevoir, Confusion!"

"Charizard, take to the air!"

Gardevoir struck from a distance with Confusion. Charizard had just lifted off and twisted aside, narrowly avoiding it. As it circled overhead, it found its chance and opened its mouth to use Flamethrower.

Fire poured toward Gardevoir. Facing the rolling heat, it used Teleport and reappeared safely out of the flames.

The first exchange had been a test. Neither Pokemon had landed a hit.

Clair frowned and ordered Charizard to keep attacking, hoping to force Gardevoir to spend all its time fleeing and set the pace herself. But Gardevoir escaped every attack with Teleport.

"Tch. Psychic-type Pokemon are such a nuisance," Clair muttered. She was hardly the first Trainer to feel that way.

Outside a game, psychic powers could be formidable. Confusion and Teleport caused an opponent no end of trouble. Worse, Clair could not tell where Gardevoir would reappear, so she could not prepare her next command. She could neither strike it nor pin it down.

Gritting her teeth, she called, "Charizard, cover a wider area! Don't leave it anywhere to escape!"

The battle went on. Ethan, unlike Clair, looked calm.

He knew Charizard too well to panic. He had one of his own and spent every day with it. Though Clair's Charizard differed in temperament and build, its moves and habits were much the same.

Ethan imagined himself in Clair's place, thought through how he would attack Gardevoir with a Charizard, and let Gardevoir read those thoughts through their telepathic connection. That gave it warning of what Clair's Charizard might do next.

Gardevoir defended, then counterattacked. Charizard began to take damage. To Clair, it felt as though Gardevoir could foresee its every move. The longer they fought, the worse the battle felt.

This is trouble... Only by facing Ethan herself did Clair fully appreciate how powerful his Pokemon were. The undefeated Indigo League tournament champion was a difficult opponent indeed.

More unsettling still, she had never felt this helpless even when battling Lance. Could Ethan be stronger than him?

Charizard's stamina was running low, and its breathing had grown ragged. When it exposed an opening, Gardevoir fired Psybeam, leaving it briefly confused. Then Psychic locked its body in place. Gardevoir followed with Hypnosis, sending it falling from the sky.

Charizard's eyelids grew heavy. Its awareness faded, and its strength seemed to leave it all at once. Headfirst, it dropped toward the pool in the center of the field.

Whoosh!

Tens of meters remained. Then only a dozen. Then a few.

"Charizard!"

It showed no sign of waking. Clair's expression changed. Another Pokemon might wake from the shock of hitting the water, but Charizard was vulnerable to it. Above all, the flame on its tail could not be allowed to go out.

She snatched out its Poke Ball and recalled it just before it struck the pool. In doing so, she conceded the round.

Kabu raised the green flag, his expression complicated. "Charizard is unable to battle. Gardevoir wins the first round!"

Looking down at the Poke Ball in her hand, Clair sighed. She could not entirely hide her disappointment. "I lost."

Ethan smiled and shook his head without speaking. After Hypnosis, he had intended to use Dream Eater to finish Charizard. Clair's recall had given him the victory before he needed it.
