Chapter 209: The Collector

The captives hung inside a sturdy iron cage in the airship's hold. They were packed together so tightly that none of them had room to move.

Ash rubbed his forehead where he had struck it and winced. "Ow... Is everyone all right?"

"I'm fine!" Tracey answered first. His face was pressed against the bars, badly squashed by the crowd. He was plainly trying to sound braver than he felt.

"Ethan?" Ash called.

"I'm fine too," Ethan said evenly. He was less battered than Tracey, but his position brought its own difficulty. Sabrina was crowded against him in front, Misty and Melody on either side, and Jessie behind him. He had ended up there by accident when the cage lurched. Whatever it looked like, he had not arranged it.

Sabrina shifted and glanced back. "What have you got in your pocket? Something's pressing against me."

"Wait, Sabrina. Please don't move for a moment," Ethan said, embarrassed. "There's barely room to breathe in here."

She gave him an exasperated look. "I still don't understand why you stopped me earlier. If you'd let me act, we wouldn't be in this cage."

"Don't worry." Ethan tried to ease his cramped posture. "They caught us for a reason. Whether they mean to let us go or keep us, we'll find out what they want. This gives us a chance to see what the people aboard this airship are planning."

Misty and Melody shifted too, leaving Ethan even less room. He cleared his throat and searched the hold for something else to focus on.

His eyes found the God of Thunder, bound nearby. Not far from it, Moltres was imprisoned in another cage.

"The God of Fire! It's here too!" Melody exclaimed. She leaned across Ethan for a closer look, making the cramped space more awkward still.

While everyone watched Moltres, Ethan noticed a low display case on the floor. Beneath its glass cover lay an ancient stone tablet.

"What's that?"

He nudged Melody with his elbow to draw her attention to it. In the crowded cage, the gesture came out awkwardly, but she was too intent on the tablet to notice.

"...The God of Fire, the God of Thunder, and the God of Ice must not be touched..." Melody read. "Those are the words of the prophecy! What are they doing here?"

"That isn't hard to explain," Ethan said, forcing his attention onto the tablet. "It's because of those words that the God of Fire and the God of Thunder were captured."

Melody understood at once, then turned to him in disbelief. "Fire, Thunder, Ice... Are the people on this airship after the God of the SeaLugia?"

Only then did she realize how closely she was leaning against Ethan. She gave a small cry, pulled away, and blushed.

Misty watched with a teasing smile. She looped her arm through Ethan's, lifted her chin at Melody, and stayed exactly where she was. Ethan pretended not to notice either girl's reaction.

"I suppose we'll have to hear their plans from them," he said.

As if on cue, the hold shuddered. A lift descended from the ceiling. Beneath a roof painted with angels, a man with a faintly sinister air reclined on a sofa, examining the prisoners as he came down.

"You're the first people to come aboard without an invitation." When the lift reached the floor, he stood and let his cold gaze pass over them. "What do you think? The God of Fire, Moltres, and the God of Thunder, Zapdos. My newest acquisitions."

Misty and the others were stunned.

"Hold on, mister!" Misty shouted. "If you want to catch Pokemon, why not battle them fairly? Isn't this an insult to them?"

"I'm no Pokemon Trainer," the man replied in a low voice. "I'm a collector. Capturing rare Pokemon and displaying them for my enjoymentthat is collecting. Please feel free to admire my collection. As a courtesy, don't touch anything."

He snapped his fingers. Gears turned, and the iron cage holding the group split in two. Everyone lost their footing and tumbled from the air.

Ethan grabbed Sabrina before they fell. He turned and held her above him, partly to protect her and partly so she would not have to reveal her psychic power. A barely perceptible breeze caught them, slowing their landing.

The collector gave them a glance and noticed nothing unusual.

Ethan had considered overpowering him immediately. But if he did, he might never see Lugia appear. He chose to wait and see how events unfolded.

A synthetic female voice sounded beside the collector.

"Articuno detected. Tracking... Beep. Target acquired."

"Excellent." The man smiled and returned to his sofa as the lift carried him toward the upper control room. "The God of Ice, the last of the legendary birds, has shown itself. My plan will soon be complete."
