Chapter 809: A Reunion, a New Partner, and a Departure

Pallet Town. Professor Oak's laboratory.

Ethan and May arrived the following morning, dusty from the road. After Max's call, they had traveled as quickly as they could, but they still had to camp outside for a night. Even then, Ethan had not flown the rest of the way on Dragonite. Unless there was no other choice, he seemed determined to travel under his own power.

May was used to sleeping outdoors after her journey with Ash. She did not find it hard. She even thought this way of traveling was good for her.

Only one thing made her shy: instead of sleeping separately in sleeping bags as she had with Ash and their friends, she and Ethan had shared a tent. Being alone in one tent with him felt awkward. Still, it was warmer than sleeping in the open, and they did not have to worry about sudden wind or rain. She had been too nervous to sleep at first, but once she drifted off later in the night, she rested almost as well as she would have at an inn.

They climbed the slope and steps to the laboratory. Ethan knocked on the door. Hurried footsteps sounded inside, and Max opened it with a broad grin.

May! Mr. Vale! You're finally here!

May put her hands on her hips. What's going on? Why are you here?

Max had not explained much on the phone the previous day. Now she meant to get an answer.

Heh. Max rubbed his nose, looking pleased with himself. If you can come to Kanto, why can't I? You didn't bring me, so I came with Professor Birch!

Someone else appeared from another room: Tracey Sketchit, whom Ethan had not seen in a long time. After traveling through the Orange Islands with Ash, Tracey had stayed at Professor Oak's laboratory and become one of his trusted assistants.

Tracey and Max looked unexpectedly well matched standing together. Tracey was a Pokemon Watcher, and Max might be suited to following the same path.

It's good to see you again, Mr. Vale, Tracey said with a smile. Congratulations on winning in Hoenn. I watched your matches on television. They were exciting!

Ethan chatted with him and introduced May. Tracey then explained how Max had come to be there.

Once Max returned home, he had felt increasingly left out. Ash and Brock had gone back to Kanto, and even May had followed Misty there. The group of four had scattered, leaving him alone. He talked at length with Norman and Caroline, but they would not let a young boy cross the sea to Kanto by himself.

Then Professor Birch happened to visit. When Max learned that Birch was going to Professor Oak's laboratory, he seized the chance to accompany him.

So that's what happened. May shook her head. Honestly, I can't get away from you anywhere. If I'd known you were going to insist on coming, I might as well have brought you myself.

I wouldn't have gone with you, Max retorted, turning his head. Not with that scary girl around. Where is she, anyway?

He meant Misty. Oddly enough, Max was unafraid of his own older sister, but Misty inspired both respect and fear in him.

May covered her mouth to hide a laugh. She's gone home to take over her Gym. It's just Brother Ethan and me traveling together now.

Max brightened and seemed about to say something, but May added, Of course, we'll video call her almost every day. You can see her whenever you like!

His face fell. Any thought of joining them vanished.

I don't want to see her.

In the anime, Max had got along reasonably well with Misty. They had met only a few times there. On this journey, he had seen far more of her headstrong side, including the time she slapped Drew. She had left an unforgettable impression on him and shown him just how frightening an older girl could be.

Ash and Professor Oak joined them from another part of the laboratory. After the greetings, Ash grinned at Ethan.

You took your time getting here, Brother Ethan. I would've set out yesterday if I hadn't known you were coming! Have you started the Battle Frontier challenge?

Started? Ethan drew two gleaming Symbols from his pocket and held them up. While you were resting at home, I earned two of them.

What? Ash stared. Already?

He had only been home for two days since returning from Vermilion City. How had Ethan moved so quickly?

Ethan shook his head. Don't worry. I rushed because I need to go to Johto for a few days. You might have all your Symbols by the time I get back.

Ash relaxed. He was not really trying to beat Ethan for speed; he just did not want to fall too far behind. Ethan was the Trainer he hoped to catch up with.

After they talked a while longer, Ethan turned to Professor Oak. He had not forgotten why he had come.

Professor, could you give May one of the Kanto starter Pokemon?

Everyone but Oak looked surprised. Even May had not expected Ethan to ask for a Pokemon on her behalf.

Brother Ethan, I...

He gave her a reassuring look, and she swallowed the rest of her words.

A Kanto starter? Oak nodded readily. Of course. Giving Pokemon to new Trainers is part of my job. May has traveled already, but she has only just come to Kanto. On new ground, she's a newcomer in a way.

Max considered that, then smiled. Professor, does that make Mr. Vale a newcomer whenever he reaches a new region? A beginner who's won three regional championships sounds terrifying!

Everyone laughed. Ash joined in at first, but his smile gradually faded. When would he reach Ethan's level?

Oak led them to the research room where the starter Poke Balls were kept. May could choose whichever she liked.

They had barely entered when a small blue, turtle-like Pokemon rushed out of the room and collided with May's leg. Professor Birch came hurrying after it, out of breath.

It was Squirtle, one of Kanto's three starters. It had been running too fast to see where it was going. The collision knocked it onto its back, and its rounded shell made it hard to right itself. It flailed in alarm.

May bent down and picked it up. Then she looked at Birch. Professor Birch, what happened?

May? When did you arrive? He caught his breath. I was observing the three starters, and this Squirtle suddenly ran off.

Again? Tracey sighed. How many times has it done that this month? Something must have frightened it. It's timid and bolts at the slightest disturbance. We've been trying to help it overcome that habit, but so far nothing's worked.

May looked down. Squirtle was pressed against her chest, with two large tears at the corners of its eyes. It looked pitiful. She kept holding it as they followed the others inside.

Three Poke Balls sat on a device in the center of the research room. Two were closed; the third was open. Squirtle had evidently come from that one.

There's also a Charmander and a Bulbasaur, Oak told May. Counting the Squirtle in your arms, which would you like?

Well... May tried to set Squirtle down so she could consider the choice. It would not let go. When Max offered to help, Squirtle pulled its head into its shell.

It seems to like May, Ash observed.

Everyone could see that. At the very least, Squirtle felt safe in her arms.

What should I do? May asked helplessly.

Why not choose Squirtle? Ethan said. You need to choose one, and it doesn't want to leave you. Take it with you. Personally, I think it's a good choice.

May considered his words. She had only just met Squirtle, so she could not yet know whether it was the right partner for her. But Ethan had brought her here and asked for a starter. She did not believe he had done either thing without a reason. Perhaps he knew something she did not.

His encouraging look settled it. She nodded. All right. I'll choose Squirtle!

Ethan felt a small sense of satisfaction. With his help, May had met the Squirtle she had obtained in the anime. He could have taken her to catch a stronger Pokemon or one better suited to her, but he did not want his presence to keep these two from finding each other.

Now that she had chosen it, that concern was gone. He had achieved what he came for. Still, they had only just arrived, so he could hardly leave before sharing a meal.

Their visit made the laboratory livelier. At noon, Ash's mother Delia prepared a generous lunch for everyone. Over the meal, Ethan mentioned that he intended to set off afterward.

Ash responded immediately. Right! It's time to get moving. Pikachu and I are ready for our next adventure! Seeing Ethan's two Frontier Symbols had left him eager to begin his own challenge.

I'm going too! Max stood up.

To everyone's surprise, he chose to travel with Ash rather than with his sister. Ash was taken aback for a moment, then nodded happily. Max had been part of his Hoenn group, and it would be good to have him along again.

Delia looked from one boy to the other with concern. Neither of you is very good at looking after yourselves. Will you really manage on your own?

A voice came from outside the room. Then count me in!

Ash turned. Brock was coming through the door with his pack on his back.

Brock! Ash leaped up and hurried to meet him. What are you doing here?

Coming along on your next adventure, of course. Brock wore his familiar expression, but the smile was clear in his voice. Looks like I arrived at just the right time.
