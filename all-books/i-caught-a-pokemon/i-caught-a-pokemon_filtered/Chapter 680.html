Chapter 680: You Should Choose a Better Path

Wearing Team Aqua uniforms, Ethan and Misty followed Shelly into the small submarine.

It was smaller than a minibus and shaped rather like a shark. Unlike Team Rocket's crudely built Magikarp submarine, though, it carried sophisticated equipment. A huge round window at the front gave a clear view ahead.

There was room for six people, including the pilot. Neither Ethan nor Misty knew how to handle a submarine, so they sat behind Shelly and watched her work the controls.

A short while later, the craft trembled gently as its engines started. Through the window, Ethan watched it sink until water covered the glass. Once they were fully submerged, the cabin felt strangely oppressive. Perhaps it was only his imagination.

Shelly steered through a channel excavated beneath the warehouse and out to sea. She found her bearing without consulting a map and guided them past the shallows into deeper water.

The submarine was small but fast. Coral and schools of fish Pokemon slipped behind them almost at once. After confirming their course by satellite navigation and nautical chart, Shelly engaged the autopilot.

She leaned back, wanting a moment to rest and consider what lay ahead. Remembering the two enemies seated behind her kept her tense.

Ethan noticed. He still needed her help, so he chose a casual question to ease the atmosphere. How long have you been with Team Aqua?

Shelly recognized that he was making conversation rather than interrogating her. Many years. I was among the first to follow Archie.

Then you must be one of the people he trusts most.

After a pause, she said, No. He has never truly trusted me or the other commanders. The only person Archie trusts completely is himself.

Ethan nodded. That did not surprise him. A leader might value his followers without trusting them in everything; he still needed authority and a clear head.

Can you tell me about the other commanders?

Shelly hesitated. Ethan smiled. I'm not asking because I plan to target them. We may have to deal with them soon. It would help to know who they are.

She fell silent again. Leading an enemy toward Archie was already a betrayal. Giving away information about her comrades would make her feel still more like a traitor.

What difference would it make? she said at last. Your Pokemon are stronger than ours. None of us could beat you.

At least tell me their names. We're supposed to be sneaking in. If I don't know who anyone is, I'll give myself away.

She could not argue with that. Fine. Apart from me, Team Aqua has two other commanders and some senior members. You don't need to know the senior members. Remember the commanders.

Their names are Matt and Amber. You met Amber at Devon Corporation. Thanks to you, he's still in prison, so you won't have to worry about seeing him.

Matt is a big, strong man. He isn't much of a thinker, but he acts decisively. Archie helped him years ago, and Matt has made Archie's dream his own. He would give his life for it. Apart from Archie, he'll be the hardest person for us to handle.

I see. Ethan nodded. So there's no talking either of them around. If we clash, I'll have to stop them by force.

It was a reasonable conclusion, but Shelly did not like hearing it.

The silence grew heavy. Ethan tried another question. Now that you know Team Aqua's goal was wrong, do you still intend to stay?

What do you mean? Shelly asked. Are you trying to persuade me to leave? Our methods were wrong, but our ideals weren't. What's wrong with striving to make an ideal home for Pokemon? Even without Kyogre, we'll keep working toward it.

Will you? Ethan shrugged. The world seems pretty wonderful already. We could take better care of it and stop damaging it. There's no need to remake it completely. If you want change, why use a Pokemon's power? Work on the economy, environmental protection, construction, or how people treat one another.

In the round window's reflection, he saw Shelly give a faint, cold smile. You have your reasoning, and we have our beliefs. I can't make you agree with me, but you can't change my mind either.

Stubborn woman. Ethan shook his head. I think it's a waste. You're intelligent and capable. You shouldn't follow one path all the way into darkness.

What? Are you trying to recruit me?

Recruit you? Ethan laughed. I don't run an organization or have some grand plan to carry out. What would I recruit you for? We're talking, that's all. Do you intend to spend your whole life in an outlaw group, hiding from the world? You should think about what kind of life you want later.

Shelly said nothing for a long time. Ethan could not tell whether his words had reached her.

At last she murmured, That's quite a speech from someone your age. She was plainly older than him and did not enjoy being lectured.

Ethan heard the barb. He shifted in his seat, put his feet on an empty chair, rested his hands behind his neck, and looked out the window.

I don't have another motive. If you don't want to hear it, forget it. I just don't want to watch you sink further into this until you end up like Amber, alone in prison for the rest of your life.

Shelly had no answer.

Misty sat quietly, doing her best not to draw attention. This exchange felt like something between adults, far beyond what she knew how to join. She turned back to the view outside.

The submarine remained quiet for a long while. Ethan thought the conversation was over when Shelly asked softly, Do I...really still have a chance to leave?

Of course. He looked toward her, but from behind he could see only her long hair. You recognized the danger and set out to stop Archie. Most people wouldn't even admit they were wrong. That ought to count in your favor. If it comes to an accounting later, I'll speak for you.

What would I do afterward?

Whatever you want, as long as you obey the law. Surely you've had some ambition of your own. If you can't settle into an ordinary life, I could suggest somewhere else.

Where?

I know a group of people in Kanto. Ethan considered his words. They're friends, not followers of some cause. They're good people. If you need a place to belong, you could meet them. But that only makes sense if you genuinely want to leave Team Aqua. Decide that first, and I'll tell you where they are.

Shelly seemed about to reply, but Ethan ended the subject. We can talk about the future later. Look at the chartthe submarine's slowing down. Are we close?

She checked and found they had reached the coordinates she had entered. Taking a breath, she set aside her thoughts, turned off the autopilot, and took the controls herself.

The submarine rose until sunlight poured through the window again. Ahead lay a maze of hundreds, perhaps thousands, of tiny islands and coral reefs. None was very high; even the tallest rose only a few meters above the sea.

With no visible markers, Shelly turned left and right from memory, threading the craft through the complicated channels toward the islands' center.

Stay alert, she said. We'll see the others soon. If someone recognizes you, don't say I didn't warn you.

Don't worry, Ethan said lightly. I can always fight my way through.

Shelly fell silent. He had left her speechless more than once, but she knew he was capable of doing exactly that. She concentrated on steering.

After several minutes, Team Aqua members came into view, guarding a larger islet. Shelly stopped the submarine at the water's edge and opened the hatch.

Before she climbed out, Ethan warned her, We're in this together now. No tricks.

I'm more worried than you are, she replied, rolling her eyes.

She climbed onto the submarine and greeted the guards. Matt, where's Archie?

The hatch remained open, so Ethan could hear them. He studied the man at the front of the group. Matt was tall and heavily muscled, built like a boxer. His hair was cropped short and his chest bare. Thick chains hung at his neck and waist, and a large Team Aqua emblem marked his chest.

Matt frowned at Shelly. Before answering, he peered into the submarine. He could make out only two figures in Team Aqua uniforms and lost interest.

Why are you back? he asked. Weren't you leading a group near Mt. Chimney?

That can wait. Where's Archie? I need to speak to him urgently.

He took a team to the entrance of Kyogre's cave. He should be there by now.

Shelly's face changed. She jumped back into the submarine, sealed the hatch, and started it toward the deeper part of the island maze.

Once Matt was out of sight, she turned to Ethan. We're too late. Archie has already gone to Kyogre.

I heard. Ethan sighed and checked his equipment. We'd better prepare for the worst.

On the other side of the islands, open water appeared again. Ethan glanced at a larger island nearby and noticed a lighthouse.

Where exactly is Kyogre?

Under this island. Hold on. I'm going at full speed.

Shelly shoved the control lever forward. Ethan felt a jolt as the submarine plunged rapidly into the deep water beneath the island.

After more than a minute of descent, a well-hidden cave entrance emerged from the dark seafloor.

Ethan had to admire Team Aqua's persistence. How had they found a place like this in the vast ocean? It must have taken years of work by hundreds, perhaps thousands, of people.
