Chapter 226: Another Move

After leaving the Gym, Ethan and Sabrina returned to Trovita Island's town. They chose an upscale restaurant for a generous dinner, then looked for somewhere to spend the night.

They passed the Pokemon Center, but Ethan suggested they would sleep more comfortably elsewhere. He took Sabrina to a nearby hotel and booked two rooms with large beds.

The Pokemon Center offered free food and lodging, but its men's and women's dormitories were plain and offered little privacy. They were convenient when a Trainer needed a bed at a moment's notice, yet they did not suit him tonight.

In a larger group, he would have been happy to stay there. Sharing stories and travel experiences before bed could deepen friendships and broaden everyone's horizons. With only the two of them, though, it would feel too quiet.

More to the point, after getting closer to Sabrina the night before, Ethan hoped to go a little further. The Pokemon Center's separate dormitories would be an obstacle.

The bustle of the day faded, and moonlight covered the island. Late at night, the streetlamps glowed dimly. Life moved slowly here. Shops had closed early, there was little entertainment, and hardly anyone walked the streets. The whole world seemed to have fallen silent.

In her room, Sabrina washed up and changed into her pajamas. Just as she was about to get into bed, someone knocked.

Knock, knock, knock.

She did not need her psychic powers to know who would dare come to her door at this hour. When she opened it, Ethan stood outside, smiling broadly.

She gave him a sidelong look. "What is it?"

His smile grew brighter. "Nothing. I couldn't sleep, so I thought I'd come by and see you."

Come by? Sabrina almost laughed. Whatever he called it, she knew perfectly well what he wanted.

She stayed in the doorway, deliberately giving him no room to enter. Would he take the hint and leave? Instead, Ethan brazenly squeezed past her and sat down on the sofa. She watched him with an amused half smile.

There was no stopping him. Shaking her head, she closed the door and left him alone.

Neither spoke. The silence grew peculiar. Sabrina climbed into bed while Ethan sat on the sofa, watching her every movement with bright eyes. When she actually closed her eyes, he endured the wait for a long while, then rose and walked to the bedside.

"So... who do you think will win tomorrow? Me or Rudy?"

It was an absurdly obvious question. Ethan only wanted to break the silence and get her talking. Several seconds passed. Sabrina kept her eyes shut and breathed evenly, as though asleep.

No answer? Ethan raised an eyebrow. She could not really be asleep. Even an ordinary person would hardly lower their guard with someone else still in the room, much less a psychic like Sabrina.

Since she would not acknowledge him, Ethan took off his shoes and lay down beside her. The mattress creaked and dipped slightly beneath their combined weight.

Quiet returned. He could hear their hearts beating hard.

He waited a little longer. Sabrina still did not move. Ethan gave a small cough and slowly reached toward her. His hand edged beneath the warm covers, and the moment his fingers touched her pajamas, she opened her eyes.

She turned to look at him. Ethan thought she would stop him. Instead, she said softly, "Turn off the light..."

...

At noon the next day, Rudy sat inside Trovita Gym with one leg crossed over the other, tapping his fingers impatiently on a table.

He had expected Ethan to take his challenge seriously and arrive early in the morning. Rudy had waited three or four hours and drunk several cups of coffee, yet Ethan was nowhere to be seen.

Rudy was not especially invested in the battle or in Ethan himself. He wanted to show Sabrina what a gentleman he could be and make an impression on her. He had risen early, put on his favorite clothes, styled his hair, and used hair gel, all so she would be dazzled when they met.

He checked the clock. It would be twelve in a few minutes, and Ethan still had not appeared. Had he decided he could not win and slipped away rather than face Rudy?

As Rudy brooded, a little girl of five or six came over. "Big brother, is the person you're waiting for still not here? Mahri's hungry. Let's go have lunch."

"All right." Rudy doted on his younger sister and would never let her go hungry. "Let's go to the dining room. Today's main dish is your favorite."

He patted her head fondly and stood to leave with her. Then the Gym doors opened. Ethan came in slowly, yawning, with Sabrina beside him.

Rudy frowned. Of all times to arrive, they had chosen this one.

But when he looked at Sabrina, he paused. Her complexion was rosy, and she somehow seemed even more beautiful than the day before. Her usually cool bearing now held a hint of allure.

"What?" Ethan chuckled at Rudy's stunned expression. "Did you know I was coming? Is that why you're here to welcome me?"

Rudy nearly exploded with anger. As if he would stand there waiting to welcome Ethan!

"Hmph. You finally showed up? I thought you'd run away before the match."

"You were waiting for me all this time?" Ethan looked surprised, then spread his hands. "Sorry. I went to sleep late last night, so I couldn't get here earlier."

"Wow!" Mahri stared at Sabrina. "You're so pretty!"

Most people would have been pleased to hear a compliment from such a young child, however little they cared about praise. Sabrina's expression did not change at all.

Ethan knew that was simply her nature. He bent down to the girl and answered for her. "You're very cute yourself. Do you live here at the Gym?"

"Mm-hmm!" The girl nodded vigorously and lifted her chin. "I'm Mahri. He's my big brother, and I live here!"
