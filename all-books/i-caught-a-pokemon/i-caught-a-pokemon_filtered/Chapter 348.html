Chapter 348: Testing Mewtwo in Battle!

Gardevoir stared intently at Mewtwo, its entire body tense. Its fighting spirit instantly surged to its peak.

Its psychic power was invisible, but the pebbles slowly rising around it made the immense energy surrounding Gardevoir unmistakable.

That power continued to gather, ready to erupt at any moment.

Seeing Gardevoir react as though it faced a mortal enemy, Mewtwo gave a cold snort and turned toward Ethan.

"So this is what you truly intend? Your actions seem very different from your words. I thought that because neither of us belonged to this world, our last parting might have left us with some mutual understanding. Yet when we meet again, you challenge me!"

Ethan shook his head.

During their last private conversation, he had confessed to Mewtwo that he had come from another world. Since then, Mewtwo had regarded him as a different kind of existence, believing that Ethan, like itself, would reject this world.

But Ethan and Mewtwo were fundamentally different.

He looked at the powerful silver-purple Pokemon before him, its appearance somewhere between a cat and an alien, and spoke solemnly.

"You're the only one who feels no sense of belonging. After traveling for so long, I've completely become part of this world. My friends are here. My partners are here. The woman I love is here. But all you do is hide away and observe everything with cold detachment. If you continue like this, no matter how much time passes, you'll still be alone."

Mewtwo's brow furrowed slightly.

It disdained speaking with humans and had never regarded any living creature as its equal. No one had ever said such a thing to it before. Ethan's words naturally stirred something within it.

But only slightly.

When Mewtwo remained silent, Ethan continued, "Besides, you've misunderstood. If I truly intended to begin a capture battle, I wouldn't have sent out only Gardevoir. I came to find you because Gardevoir is a Psychic-type Pokemon like you, and it has always regarded you as its greatest opponent. Since I was returning to Kanto, I brought it here to spar with you!"

Spar?

Mewtwo returned its attention to him.

How pleasant he makes it sound. In the end, he still wants to fight!

After all those words, he merely wants to test my strength again.

Mewtwo understood that if it refused, or if it grew careless during the coming battle and exposed an opening, this man could turn the supposed spar into a capture battle at any moment.

With that thought, Mewtwo rose, stretched, and descended to the ground. It folded its arms across its chest. The sheer pressure radiating from it felt almost tangible, stirring Ethan's bangs.

Compared with Gardevoir, Mewtwo was visibly more powerful.

Their two energies met in the air, grinding and colliding with sharp crackles of electricity.

"Even when the challenger is weak, I do not mind accepting. If that is what you want, I will see this battle through to the end!"

Seeing Mewtwo agree, Ethan nodded with open appreciation.

Now that's confidence! This is exactly how the strongest man-made Pokemon should speak. Only an opponent like this is worth all the effort it will take to win over!

Just as Ethan had said, his present strength gave him a good chance of capturing Mewtwo without using a Master Ball. If he sent out every Pokemon he had at once, including the three Legendary birds, Gyarados, and Gengar, then as long as Mewtwo did not try to flee, he would most likely succeed.

But Ethan had chosen not to do that.

There was plenty of time. Slowly building a relationship like this was also a good approach. Mewtwo was unique, and if possible, Ethan wanted to capture it through more peaceful means.

Most of all, Mewtwo remained intensely hostile toward humans. Forcing it into a Poke Ball could very well provoke it into rebelling.

As for this battle...

Ethan already knew that if he sent out Gardevoir alone, it was certain to lose.

Even so, the battle needed to happen. Its purpose was to put pressure on Mewtwo while also showing Ethan how wide the gap between Gardevoir and Mewtwo remained.

Go, Gardevoir! Use Psychic!

Ethan issued the command through their mental link, and Gardevoir immediately attacked.

It used Teleport to appear behind Mewtwo, then unleashed the psychic power it had been gathering as a surging Psychic attack.

The immense wave of visible energy arrived in an instant. Mewtwo merely turned and raised its right arm. A barrier formed before it.

Psychic struck the barrier but failed to break through.

The difference between them was immediately clear.

Despite the obvious gap in power, Gardevoir was not discouraged. Its desire to fight burned high, and it knew Ethan had given it permission to use everything it had. When its first attack failed, it immediately launched another.

Magical Leaf, Psychic, Psybeam, and Moonblast followed one after another!

At first, Mewtwo handled the uninterrupted barrage with ease. But once its barrier shattered, even it had to show some of its true ability and fight back.

Bang! Bang! Bang!

Psychic attacks screamed through the air, colliding and exploding.

The two Pokemon exchanged more than a dozen rounds in the blink of an eye.

They poured energy at one another, but even after so long, Mewtwo remained uninjured. The surrounding rocks had not been so fortunate. Shock waves from the battle shattered stone, blasted open the lake's surface, and left deep craters across the ground.

The once beautiful scenery had been ruined beyond recognition.

To avoid being caught in the battle, Ethan retreated several steps. Gengar also intervened discreetly, deflecting every chip of stone that flew toward him.

"Be careful. Those two are making far too much of a commotion. If you get dragged into it, we'll have gained nothing from this!"

Ethan nodded and looked back toward the battlefield. The fight continued. Mewtwo had flown into the air, and Gardevoir wrapped itself in psychic power and pursued it.

They darted constantly between the hanging stalactites. As the battle intensified, their movements soon became too fast for Ethan's eyes to follow. He could only judge their approximate positions and the course of the battle from the explosions and the sensations Gardevoir sent through their mental link.

Gengar watched the battlefield closely, leaned toward Ethan's ear, and whispered, "This is a perfect chance to capture it. Want me to help?"

Ethan refused without hesitation.

"No. Leave it to Gardevoir. This battle is its trial. Besides, as I said before, we didn't come to capture Mewtwo today!"

In the brief moment his attention wandered, Ethan looked up again and saw a white figure hurtling toward him.

It was Gardevoir. Mewtwo had caught it with an attack, sending it tumbling to the ground directly in front of Ethan.

Gardevoir rolled several times before stopping at his feet. Ethan hurriedly crouched and helped it upright.

Only at close range did he see how poor its condition was. Wounds covered its body, it looked thoroughly battered, and even its consciousness had grown hazy.

"You worked hard."

Ethan sighed softly. Just as he had expected, Gardevoir had suffered a crushing defeat.

Ever since he obtained Gardevoir, it had handled its battles with ease. This was the first time it had ever been left in such a miserable state.

Whoosh!

Mewtwo used Teleport to appear before Ethan as well. It stared down at him, blue light glowing in its eyes as power continued to gather, ready to launch its next attack at any moment.

Its body remained spotless. The battle with Gardevoir had inflicted almost no damage at all.

Still, Mewtwo's chest rose and fell slightly. Defeating Gardevoir had required some effort, which at least made it seem less invincible.

As expected of Mewtwo. The gap between them remained obvious. Gardevoir was not yet its match and would need to become stronger.

Ethan took out its Poke Ball and recalled it.

"All right. We'll stop here this time. Gardevoir isn't your match, so there's no reason to continue."

Mewtwo tilted its head in confusion when Ethan truly ended the battle.

Although it had defeated Gardevoir, it had not relaxed its guard.

Ever since Ethan entered the cave, Mewtwo had sensed the immense strength of the Gengar beside him. It could also feel three fierce presences within the Poke Balls at Ethan's waist, all locked firmly onto it.

Those presences were eager to fight. They seemed to be waiting only for one command from their Trainer before bursting forth to challenge Mewtwo themselves.
