Chapter 649: Which Pokemon Is Most Beautiful?

On the television, Drew's Roselia had begun its performance.

It leaped into the air and used Petal Dance, sending petals swirling across the stage. As the petals floated overhead, Roselia spun and sprayed Stun Spore toward the ceiling, then followed it with Magical Leaf. The moves blended into one another. Powder and glowing leaves intertwined beneath the spotlights in a dazzling display.

The sequence was as beautiful as it was intricate. Vivian praised it, and the audience burst into applause. Drew had brought the arena to life.

Roselia wasn't finished. It used Petal Dance again, vanishing from sight amid the cloud of petals. Before the spectators could wonder where it had gone, Roselia reappeared and launched another Magical Leaf. The sharp-edged leaves shredded the airborne petals into countless tiny pieces. They spun down slowly like pink snow.

Amid that snowfall, Roselia and Drew bowed to the audience in perfect unison and left the stage.

Clap, clap, clap!

Thunderous applause filled the arena. Screens before the three judges displayed their scores: Mr. Contesta gave Drew 9.8, Mr. Sukizo gave him 9.9, and Nurse Joy gave him 9.7. Together, they made an impressive 29.4 points for the round. Drew had taken the lead over every contestant before him.

Vivian was surprised by the score, but she didn't call the next contestant yet. Instead, she went to the judging table to interview all three judges.

You each gave him a very high score. Could you tell us why?

It was an important part of the show. For viewers watching their first Pokemon Contest, the judges' explanations would make Drew's score easier to understand.

Nurse Joy looked at her screen and smiled. The Coordinator and his Pokemon worked together beautifully. Of all the performances so far, theirs was the most pleasing to watch. That's why I gave them 9.7.

When Vivian offered the microphone to Mr. Sukizo, the short Pokemon Fan Club president simply smiled. No special reason. I liked it.

Mr. Contesta, the director of the Pokemon Contest Committee, offered a more technical explanation. It was an excellent stage performance. Roselia is clearly well trained, and that kind of skill takes a great deal of practice. Combining some of those moves requires precise timing. A mistake could easily have spoiled the whole effect.

In the waiting room, Misty frowned after hearing the judges' comments.

He really does have some talent. No wonder he's so confident.

May said nothing. Drew's performance had put a great deal of pressure on her. Could she really stand up to him after a showing like that?

The Contest continued. Not long after Drew left the stage, the door to May's waiting room opened. Drew walked in with his hands in his pockets.

Oh, you made it after all. He looked over the group, then fixed his eyes on May with a sneer. You saw my performance, didn't you? That's real beauty. A beginner like you couldn't hope to manage it.

At his mention of beauty, Misty's temper flared. Did you come here to pick a fight? Do you want me to hit you again?

Drew remembered her punch all too well. He stayed by the doorway rather than coming any closer, though his tone remained defiant.

Pick a fight? I have better things to do. As a more experienced Coordinator, I only came to wish you luck. I hope you at least make it through the first round. It would be a shame if you were eliminated right away!

Everyone could tell he had come to boast and taunt her.

May privately admired his performance, but she wasn't going to admit that to his face. She stood up and answered firmly, I will make it through! Just watch me!

At that moment, a tremendous cheer rose from the television. They all turned toward the screen.

A man with long blond hair and a purple suit was performing with his Pokemon. He carried himself with the same sort of elegance as his partner: a Milotic.

Under the spotlights, Milotic seemed wrapped in a mysterious radiance. Its whole body shimmered.

Milotic doesn't even need to perform! Vivian cried excitedly. Its beauty alone has set the whole arena alight!

Milotic?

Ash stared openmouthed at the colorful Pokemon on the screen. Its long body reminded him a little of a Dragonair. It's so beautiful. What kind of Pokemon is that?

You don't know? Misty gave Drew a dismissive glance, unwilling to waste another word on him, then turned to Ash. Didn't you battle Ethan's Feebas before? Milotic is what Feebas evolves into!

What?!

Ash was stunned. That ugly, weak Feebas evolved into something this beautiful? Milotic wasn't humanoid, but even Ash could appreciate its looks.

Ethan was the only person in the room who felt little excitement at the sight of it. To him, Milotic was elegant, certainly, but he wasn't convinced it was exceptionally beautiful.

When he had caught Feebas, he had mainly wanted to make up for the shortage of Water-type Pokemon on his team.

If he had to name Pokemon he genuinely found beautiful, he would choose Gardevoir, Pheromosa, and Meloetta.

Even when it came to elegance, he thought Latias surpassed Milotic. She was more beautiful too.

That reminded him of Latias. After all, she had once been inside one of his Poke Balls. What were she and her brother Latios doing now? Had any humans disturbed them?

Then he thought of Bianca and her grandfather looking after them, and the barrier protecting their home. They were probably still living as carefree as ever in Altomare, the City of Water.

While Ethan's mind wandered, the performance on the screen came to an end. The audience cheered just as loudly as it had for Drew. Even the score was the same.

Only then did Ethan hear Vivian announce the man's name: Robert.
