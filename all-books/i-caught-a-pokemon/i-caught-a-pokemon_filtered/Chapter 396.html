Chapter 396: Raising the Difficulty

Following the Gym rules, Morty sent out his Pokemon first.

"Go, Gastly!"

He threw a Poke Ball. Blue light flashed, and a Gastly appeared before Ethan.

Gastly was Gengar's first evolutionary stage, and Ethan knew it extremely well after having owned one himself. He understood not only its moves, but also the way it attacked.

He rubbed his chin.

Hmm...

This looked like it would be an easy battle.

Since his main objective was training and his opponent was only in its first evolutionary stage, Ethan considered his options before sending out his own Pokemon.

Blue light flashed, and a Pokemon resembling a cocoon appeared on the field.

"Oh? A Pupitar..."

Morty rested one hand on his hip and studied Pupitar with a slightly grave expression.

He rarely followed news from other regions, so he naturally did not recognize Ethan or know how strong the man before him really was.

Still, anyone who possessed a Pokemon as rare in Johto as Pupitar had to possess at least some abilityor perhaps merely excellent luck?

Whatever the case, Gastly was undeniably at a disadvantage.

In terms of type, Pupitar's Ground typing gave it an advantage against Gastly's Poison typing. This placed Pupitar ahead from the very beginning.

Sure enough, once the match began, it quickly swung in Pupitar's favor.

Pupitar performed extremely well. Under Ethan's commands, it always prepared for Gastly's attacks in advance. After the two Pokemon traded attacks and wore each other down, Pupitar finished the match with Crunch, defeating Gastly cleanly and decisively.

Morty shook his head and helplessly recalled Gastly.

How could he put it? Gastly had lost far too quickly and easily...

For Ethan, the battle had been effortless for two reasons. First, Gastly was only in its initial evolutionary stage and was not especially powerful, while Pupitar possessed higher overall capabilities. Second, Ethan knew Gastly exceptionally well and could prepare for its various moves in advance.

The battle also showed that Morty was simply following the usual process and had treated Ethan as an ordinary challenger.

In the spectator area, Erika applauded softly.

Just as she had said before, Ethan's victory came as no surprise to her.

Back on the field, losing so easily made Morty take the match somewhat more seriously.

He was a Gym Leader, after all. He had his pride!

After a moment's hesitation, he prepared to send out his second Pokemon according to the usual progression he followed with other challengers. But when he saw Ethan's calm expression, with no apparent excitement over the victory, an idea suddenly occurred to him. He reached into a different pocket and took out another Poke Ball.

"Go! This will be my second Pokemon!"

Blue light flashed, and a Pokemon resembling a gray doll with a zipper across its mouth appeared before Ethan.

Oh?

Banette?

Ethan recognized the Pokemon immediately, but...

That isn't a Pokemon native to Johto, is it?

And from its presence alone, I can tell that it's definitely one of Morty's strongest Pokemon!

He shouldn't send it into an ordinary Gym battle like this. Did he recognize me and decide to fight at full strength?

With that thought, Ethan gave Morty a surprised look, only to find the man smiling back at him.

From that smile, Ethan instantly understood what he meant.

I see. He thought my last victory was too easy, so he's raising the difficulty...

"What's wrong?" Morty asked with a smile. "Confused? If this Pokemon seems too troublesome or difficult for you to handle, I can replace it with another one!"

Ethan smiled as well and shook his head. "There's no need. I believe I can handle it. Besides, didn't the referee say before the match that the Gym Leader can't switch Pokemon? How could I ask you to make an exception? More importantly, the battle hasn't even begun. Perhaps I won't be the one who finds it troublesome. Perhaps you will!"

Morty raised his eyebrows in surprise.

What an interesting man!

Anyone bold enough to make such a claim must have some real strength. That only proved Morty's last-minute change in strategy had been correct!

Come on, then. Show me what you can do!

By then, Ethan had already waved Pupitar back to his side.

Since his opponent had changed strategies, he could not continue using Pupitar in the battle.

Unfortunately, it seemed his training plan would have to end here.

When Pupitar returned, Ethan crouched and stroked its head in praise before recalling it into its Poke Ball.

Afterward, he stood, took out his Pokedex as usual, and aimed it at Banette.

Knowing both oneself and one's enemy was the surest path to victory. Before battling it, he should at least learn a little more about it.

The Pokedex announced, "Beep. Banette, the Marionette Pokemon. A Ghost type. Banette was originally a pitiful discarded doll. Cursed energy seeped into its stuffing, and its powerful resentment transformed the doll into a Pokemon. It makes its home in garbage dumps and can be found in dark alleys. It is said to search for the child who abandoned it, wandering in pursuit of revenge. If its need to be cherished is fulfilled, it will return to its original form as a doll."

What an interesting concept...

But the part about a doll turning into a Pokemon is probably only a myth, right?

Ethan put away his Pokedex and considered which Pokemon he should use against it.

His third-string Pokemon certainly would not be enough. It appeared he would have to call upon one of his main fighters.

Having made his decision, he searched the pouch on his right hip and threw a Poke Ball.

"Go, Crystal Steelix!"

Blue light flashed. A Pokemon of tremendous weight and presence, its body gleaming with crystalline light, crashed heavily onto the battlefield.

Boom!

The thunderous impact made Morty's brow twitch. When he saw the Pokemon clearly, he too was captivated by its beauty and imposing form!

He rubbed his eyes and stared at the Pokemon in disbelief.

"Is that a Shiny Steelix? No... that can't be right. A Shiny Steelix isn't this color. Is it some kind of mutation?"

Ethan nodded. "You're right. It is a mutation. I happened to catch it through a fortunate coincidence. I suspect it's the only one of its kind in the world."

"I see..." Morty exclaimed. "So this is your true strength!? I can tell from its presence alone that it's completely different from the Pupitar you used before. Now I'm looking forward to this battle even more!"

Presence?

Ethan's lips curved upward at Morty's words.

If you want to talk about presence, you haven't seen my enormous red Gyarados yet. Once you do, you'll understand what true pressure feels like!
