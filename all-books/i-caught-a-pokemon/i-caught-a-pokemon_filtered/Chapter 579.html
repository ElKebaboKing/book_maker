Chapter 579: Another Familiar Movie Plot

The winner of this years Silver Conference is Ethan Vale of the Kalos region! This is his second championship trophy since the Indigo League! Lets congratulate him!

The announcers ringing voice echoed through the stadium. The result was settled, and the tournament had truly reached its end.

Of course, Ethan still had to take a commemorative photograph with the Pokemon who had won him the title.

He brought out Gardevoir, Dragonite, Scizor, Tyranitar, Crystal Steelix, Jolteon, Blastoise, and Venusaur, all of whom had battled in this tournament. Gengar could not contain itself either. It hurried to Ethans side with a huge grin.

The Pokemon gathered around him. With the enormous trophy in his arms, Ethan heard the click of the camera, and the moment was preserved forever.

After leaving the field, Ethan returned to the waiting room.

He had braced himself before opening the door. It did him no good. Streamers burst over him the instant he stepped inside.

Congratulations, Ethan!

You won the championship!

Congratulations!

Voices came from every direction. Ethan pulled a streamer from his hair and saw familiar face after familiar face, all smiling their heartfelt congratulations.

Thanks, everyone.

They crowded around him as he came in. Until then, the victory had not quite felt real. With everyone talking at once, he felt as though he were floating on a cloud, his limbs light and weak.

Then came the part everyone enjoyed: a celebratory feast.

Winning the Silver Conference also brought Ethan another cash prize. It was about the same amount he had received for winning the Indigo League, and it swelled his savings considerably.

With the money he had now, he could stop being a Trainer, settle somewhere quiet, and live comfortably for the rest of his life.

That was not a path he was considering yet. If the day came when he was tired of traveling and wanted to settle down, the option would be there.

The whole group headed to a restaurant, where Misa, Masha, and their friends were already waiting. They had heard the news and arrived ahead of them.

The meal lasted until late at night. The worst of it was that Misa, Masha, and the other bikers kept filling Ethans glass. He was in such a good mood that he drank more than he should have.

By the end of the night, he had no idea how he got home.

When he woke again, it was eleven the next morning. His head throbbed from the hangover, and he reeked of alcohol. Only after a cold shower did he feel a little better.

Here.

Erika handed him a glass of warm water. As he drank it slowly, she smiled and said, Professor Elms family, Miki, and the others have already gone home. You were still asleep, so they didnt come in to say goodbye.

Ethan set the glass aside and tried to empty his mind. Thinking made it feel as though something were stirring around inside his skull.

What about Misa and Masha?

They just left too. I asked them to take your medal and trophy to Cerise Laboratory. Ive packed our luggage as well. Only Ash and the others are still waiting for you.

Ethan looked around. The room was spotless, and their things were ready to carry out. Having a thoughtful woman beside him certainly made life easier. Erika had taken care of everything without him having to worry about a thing.

He dressed, picked up his luggage, and went downstairs with her to the competitors village lobby.

The lodging was deserted. Apart from the staff, Ethans group seemed to be the only people left. The Trainers who had lost had long since gone, taking their disappointment with them.

Ethan, youre awake!

Misty was the first to turn at the sound of footsteps. One look at his weary face told her how he felt.

You slept forever, Ash grumbled. Weve already eaten lunch!

Can you blame me? Ethan said. Those people kept pouring me drinks last night. My head still feels like its going to split open.

Once they were together, they wasted no more time at the village and set off.

At yesterdays celebration, they had agreed to leave Silver Town as a group. They would travel to a town not far away, then part ways there.

Silver Town sat high on the Indigo Plateau, and the terrain was difficult. Ash, Misty, and Brock needed to get down to lower ground before they could make the return trip to Kanto comfortably.

Ethan and Erika would go with them that far, then head for a nearby port and take a ship to Hoenn.

Once again, Ethan would begin exploring a new region ahead of Ash. Ash was eager to follow, but first he had to return to Pallet Town and spend a few days with his mother.

They talked as they walked. After more than an hour, they had descended from the plateau into a thick forest.

The path narrowed. With Erika walking ahead, Misty came up beside Ethan and whispered, Ethan, dont forget. You promised wed travel together.

He glanced at her teasingly. Whats this? You dont want to travel with Ash anymore?

Who wants to travel with a guy like him? Misty shook her head. When Ethan still did not mention his promise, she grew anxious. You said you would! You cant take it back.

Dont worry. Ethan laughed and patted her head. Go home first and let your sisters know youre safe. After that, you can come find me in Hoenn.

Few people seemed to visit the forest below the plateau. It had a wild, ancient feel, with tall trees so dense that their branches nearly hid the sky.

They passed a magnificent waterfall and followed the mountain path for a long while. At last, shortly before sunset, they reached a village standing among the trees.

It was a small and unusual place. Most of its buildings had been built in the branches of giant trees. Everywhere they looked, there were treehouses that seemed part of the forest itself.

Lets stay here for the night, Ethan said.

But the village was remote. They wandered through it without seeing many people, much less a restaurant or an inn.

After they had made two rounds, a woman hanging laundry outside one of the treehouses spotted them from behind its railing and waved.

Over here! Can I help you?

Ethan looked up. Were passing through and hoped to find somewhere to eat and stay. Is there an inn here?

Oh, its you! Now that she could see his face, the woman looked astonished. I watched your match yesterday. I never expected you to come to our village. We dont have anywhere for travelers to stay, but if you dont mind, youre welcome at my home.

Her home?

Ethan exchanged glances with Erika, Ash, and the others. They all nodded, so they went to the base of the tree and climbed the stairs.

Several wooden staircases joined the treehouse to the ground. The climb was no harder than going upstairs in an ordinary building. When they reached the outer deck, they could see their host clearly.

She looked about twenty-six or twenty-seven and was very pretty. Her long, pale green hair was tied in twin tails. She wore a pink tank top and jeans, giving her a bright, youthful look that seemed a little out of place in this rustic village. Most striking of all was the necklace around her neck, strung with three gleaming gold coins.

Welcome to Arborville. Im Diana.

Hello. Im Ethan.

Im Ash!

And Im Misty.

Erika was about to introduce herself when someone flashed past her. Brock had somehow dropped to one knee before Diana. Gazing up at her with tender eyes, he said in a deep voice, Im Brock. Who would have thought I could meet a beautiful lady like you in a village like this?

Erika had no words.

Fortunately, Misty gave Brock a sharp knock on the head before he could embarrass everyone further.

After the introductions, Diana invited them inside. The treehouse looked unusual from the outside, but its interior was much like any other wooden home.

An elderly woman was there too. She looked surprised to see Diana bringing in a group of strangers.

Diana, who are they?

Grandmother. Diana stepped aside. Theyre travelers passing through. This is Ethan, who won the Silver Conference yesterday. They needed somewhere to stay, so I invited them in.

Then she turned to the visitors. This is my grandmother, Towa. She watches over the entrance to the forest.

The entrance? Ethan and the others had never heard of such a role.

Towa studied the group. There was a man and woman at its center, two children, and a darker-skinned companion following along. They hardly looked like troublemakers.

Youre welcome here, she said. But I must warn you: if you go into the forest tomorrow and hear the voice of the forest, you must not move.

Unlike Diana, Towa wore traditional clothes and a headscarf. She carried a long wooden staff and looked rather like a tribal shaman. She too had a necklace of gold coins, though hers held five instead of three. Perhaps the difference reflected their positions in the village.

Um... Erika said. Which forest do you mean? We didnt hear anything on our way here.

I dont mean the ordinary forest you came through. I mean the Mysterious Forest behind us.

Towa looked out the window. Following her gaze, they saw a larger, denser stretch of ancient forest beyond the other side of the village.

The Mysterious Forest?

Is there something unusual about it? Erika asked cautiously.

Its an old village tradition, Diana said with a wave of her hand, answering for her grandmother. Then her expression grew serious. But if you dont want to get lost, you really should listen to her.

Ethan and the others looked toward the forest again. Unless they took a long detour, crossing it would be the shortest way to the next town. Both local women had given them a clear warning. Was there really something in those trees, some inexplicable power?

That makes me even more excited, Ash whispered. I want to go in right now! Do you think there are any amazing Pokemon in there?

Towa fell silent.

So did Diana.

They stared at the suddenly excited boy. They had spelled out the danger as plainly as they could. Why did that only make him more eager?

Did he not know how to be afraid?

That evening, Towa and Diana prepared a generous spread of local dishes for their guests. The main course was a thick vegetable and mushroom soup. It tasted wonderfully fresh, like something no big-city restaurant could ever reproduce.

I should take some of these seeds home, Erika whispered to Ethan after dinner.

He agreed wholeheartedly.

Still, he felt bad about eating and sleeping there without paying. Towa and Diana refused to accept a cent.

Please dont worry about it, Diana said, seeming to read his concern. Guests from afar have always been welcome here. Make yourselves at home.

Late that night, Diana settled them all in an empty treehouse. Ethan lay on a futon on the floor. Erika slept soundly to his left, beside the wall. Misty lay to his right, turning over again and again, perhaps too excited to sleep so close to him.

On the other side, Ash and Brock were fast asleep on their own bedding without a care in the world. Brock was already snoring.

Moonlight came through the window and fell across Ethans face.

Over dinner, he had finally remembered where they were. His memories of the movie were a little hazy, but this was unmistakably the village where Ash had met Celebi and Professor Oak as a boy.

Did that mean Ethan might get to see Celebi too?

He pulled his bag closer, searched through it, and took out the GS Ball.

At the sound, Misty turned toward him. She was startled when she saw the ball in his hand.

Huh? Ethan, isnt that the GS Ball? How did you get it?

Kurt gave it to me, Ethan said softly. Funny, isnt it? After all that, the ball ended up in my hands. And I have a feeling Ill have a chance to use it very soon.
