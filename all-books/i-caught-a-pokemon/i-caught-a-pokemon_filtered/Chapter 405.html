Chapter 405: Zuki's Challenge

During dinner, Erika chatted quietly with the five sisters, and the atmosphere was relaxed and cheerful.

Ethan paid no attention to what they were discussing. He focused entirely on the food before him, neither looking up nor joining their conversation, all to avoid Zuki's gaze.

The others did not know what had happened. Seeing him behave this way, they noticed nothing unusual and merely assumed he was naturally unsociable and disliked occasions like this.

Then Sayo, the youngest sister seated nearby, suddenly remembered something and asked, "By the way, Mr. Ethan, Miss Erika, since you're both from Kanto, do you know Ash and Misty?"

"Of course I do." Hearing those familiar names, Ethan could no longer pretend to be indifferent. He smiled and said, "They're like a younger brother and sister to me. Do you know them too?"

Sayo nodded. "We met them at a pastry shop. They came to one of our tea parties with Brock, and later we defeated Team Rocket together. All three of them were easy to get along with, and we became friends. Oh, I also remember Misty saying that one of her older sisters was called Sakura tooher name and mine are the same in Japanese!"

Ethan was not surprised that Ash and his friends had befriended the Kimono Sisters as well.

Given their personalities and their uncanny knack for drawing people in, not to mention Team Rocket's help, they could become friends with practically anyone.

"That's right," Ethan said with a smile. "The Daisy I know isn't only a Gym Leader. She's also an accomplished water ballet performer. Their shows are extremely popular in Cerulean City. If I had to make a comparison, I'd say the local people love them just as much as the people here love you!"

Miki and the others exchanged surprised looks.

"A Gym Leader who performs water ballet? That must be wonderful! If we ever have the chance, we'll definitely go and see it!"

At that moment, an Eevee ran in from the entryway. Its little legs carried it unsteadily toward them, and it nearly knocked over Ethan's low table.

The bowls and chopsticks rattled, and sake splashed from its cup.

Ethan reacted quickly and caught the table before it toppled. On closer inspection, wasn't this the same Eevee he had encountered at the Ecruteak Dance Theater the previous night?

"Eevee! How did you get out?"

Naoko rose and scooped it into her arms. A little embarrassed, she said, "Please don't mind it. This Eevee is actually very lively, but it startles easily, so it can seem a little clumsy."

Remembering how it had darted around in a panic the night before, Ethan understood. Before he could respond, however, five more Eevee evolutions of different types came running in through the doorway behind it.

Flareon, Vaporeon, Jolteon, Espeon, and Umbreon were the very partners the five sisters had performed with the previous night.

In an instant, Eevee and its evolutions were racing all over the room.

"It's so... lively..."

"Please excuse them." Miki sighed helplessly. "These little ones have endless energy. With them around, it's difficult to enjoy a quiet day."

Erika shook her head. "There's no need to apologize. We have Eevee evolutions too, so we understand exactly how you feel."

She was right. Whatever their type, the Eevee evolutions all seemed restless, and their boundless energy often exhausted their Trainers. Dozens of Grass-type Pokemon also lived at Erika's Gym. Most Grass types were relatively quiet, but once they started playing, the noise could become just as maddening.

"Oh?" Kuni's eyes lit up. "You have Eevee evolutions too? What types are they?"

Ethan and Erika took out Poke Balls and released their Pokemon. In two flashes of blue light, one yellow and one green Eevee evolution appeared before the five sisters.

The yellow one was naturally Ethan's Jolteon, while the leaf-green one was Erika's Leafeon!

The other Pokemon immediately crowded around the newcomers.

They sniffed one another and exchanged cheerful cries, as though holding a conversation of their own.

Miki already had a Jolteon, and Jolteon was a common enough evolution, so Ethan's did not attract much attention. Erika's Leafeon, on the other hand, immediately captured the girls' interest.

"It's a Leafeon!"

"It's so cute!"

"It's all yellow and green. I can almost smell fresh grass!"

While the other sisters focused on Leafeon, Miki, a fellow Jolteon Trainer, carefully studied Ethan's Pokemon instead.

"You've raised this Jolteon very well!" Looking it up and down, she praised, "Its fur is stiff, its body is long and well proportioned, and its limbs are powerful. I can tell it must be very strong!"

Before Ethan could answer, Zuki gave a soft laugh.

"Of course, Miki. A champion raised it. How could it possibly be some weak little Pokemon?"

She placed deliberate emphasis on the word champion. Sensing the hostility in her voice, Ethan frowned, unable to understand exactly what she meant.

When their eyes met, Zuki said coolly, "Champion, battle me."

"What?" Ethan stared at her.

Zuki repeated herself. "I said, battle me. We'll use our Eevee evolutions!"

"Well..."

"You wouldn't refuse, would you?"

Ethan knew Zuki was doing this deliberately. She wanted revenge for what he had said earlier!

So she really had taken those words to heart!

How petty!

Hearing that the two were about to battle, Miki, Kuni, and the others exchanged bewildered looks. They could not understand why Zuki had suddenly challenged Ethan.

Their suspicious gazes moved between the pair as they wondered whether some conflict had occurred without their knowledge.

They wanted to intervene, but Zuki was the most stubborn of the sisters and always did as she pleased. Once she made up her mind, trying to persuade her was bound to be futile.

Even Erika gave Ethan a helpless look.

"All right. I understand."

Ethan let out a long sigh, but there was no way to explain the situation.

Knowing the matter had to be settled, he had no choice but to rise. At his soft whistle, Jolteon immediately leaped back to his side.

Seeing that Ethan had not backed down, Zuki chuckled. An unreadable smile appeared on her face, making her seem colder and more aloof than ever.

At her gesture, Umbreon also returned to her side.

As if sensing their Trainers' fighting spirit, Jolteon and Umbreon confronted one another in turn. Sparks seemed to fly as their gazes met!
