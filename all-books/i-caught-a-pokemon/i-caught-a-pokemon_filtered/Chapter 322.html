Chapter 322: Raid on Team Rocket's Research Facility

The Lake of Rage was Johto's largest lake. According to local accounts, it had once been a volcanic crater. When the ground collapsed, rainwater filled the hollow and formed the lake they knew today.

To the west lay a vast maze of trees. Whenever it rained, the lake rose and water spread through the forest until the whole place became a marsh. Amphibious Pokemon emerged in numbers, and the woods filled with life. When the rain stopped, the water slowly receded and the trees returned to their usual quiet.

But at some point, the lake's Gyarados had grown unnaturally violent. They began wrecking boats and attacking swimmers, and nearby residents no longer dared approach the water.

Half a month ago, the local police had sent several officers to find out why the Gyarados were behaving this way. They found no answer, but they did stumble upon Team Rocket. Wisely, they made no move against the group. They reported the discovery to the International Police, setting this operation in motion.

Looker led Ethan, Sabrina, and a team of officers to a hillside near Team Rocket's base.

They hid behind the rise and looked toward the lake. A broad clearing had been cut into the once-dense forest, and Team Rocket's secret base stood within it. Even from this distance, the complex looked substantial.

At its center was a modern three-story research building. A signal tower, warehouses, and a power station stood nearby. Team Rocket had even built a small dock for easy access to the Lake of Rage. They had prepared thoroughly; clearly, they had meant to stay for a long time.

Too bad for them. Today, it all ends.

When Ethan's group arrived, many Rocket members were carrying crates out of the warehouses and securing them on vehicles, apparently preparing to leave. Yet they worked without urgency.

"That's a fortunate sign," Looker said with relief. "It's been more than a day since you and Lance appeared in Blackthorn, and nothing has happened to them in the meantime. Maybe they've decided it was only a coincidence. They may be packing in case the worst happens while they wait to see how things develop."

Ethan nodded. Looker's reasoning was clear and made sense.

Sabrina tugged at his sleeve. Following her gaze, Ethan spotted Vicious at once.

He really is here!

Vicious was still riding his mechanical spider. He seemed to be on his way out of the base. Was he going to hunt more Pokemon?

"It's Vicious," Ethan said quietly. "He's leaving. Should we catch him now?"

Looker studied Vicious and thought for a moment. "Leave him for now. We can send someone to follow him. Our people haven't finished surrounding the base. If we reveal ourselves now, the others could escape."

Besides the team with Looker, two more International Police teams were circling in from other directions to seal off the research facility. Their routes were long and would take time. Until they were in place, no one could risk alerting the Rockets.

Ethan had no choice but to watch Vicious go. He could not stop him, but he could send Clair a message at once.

Lance had already set off for Kanto after Ethan left Blackthorn City. Clair was the only one he could tell where Vicious had gone. With luck, she could join forces with other Dragon Tamers and catch him.

She replied quickly with only a short Got it. Even so, Ethan could feel her determination.

The sun sank toward the west. The group waited quietly for more than half an hour before Looker received a signal that the other two teams were in position.

He did not give the order to move yet. Instead, he checked his phone again. Soon Karen called, and because Ethan stood beside him, he could hear her report clearly.

In Mahogany Town, Karen and Will had already led the International Police into Team Rocket's underground base. Their raid was going well. At first they had met almost no resistance and had taken more than half the base. The Rockets had regrouped and counterattacked, but still could not stop their advance.

They had also found the Rocket executive Lance there and fought him. Karen assured Looker that even an executive was no match for her and Will. She expected to finish clearing the base soon and then come to reinforce the team at the lake.

Ethan nodded. If they captured that Lance, he would be the second Rocket executive sent to prison, after Lambda, whom Ethan had caught earlier.

Suddenly the research facility became restless. Its people had probably learned that the Mahogany base was under attack. Some of them left to reinforce the town, while the others began moving their supplies faster.

Now!

Looker's eyes sharpened. "Begin the operation!"

At his command, International Police officers burst from the forest on three sides and ran toward the research building. Speedboats raced across the lake toward the dock as well. The net was closing.

As the officers approached, Team Rocket's detection equipment found them. Alarms rang through the base, and the members who saw the advancing police fell into obvious panic.

"Damn it! The International Police are here too!"

"We're surrounded!"

While they milled about, a tall, rough-looking man in a vest and black gloves came out of the research building with a group of Rocket members.

"What are you panicking for?" he snapped at those trying to flee. Then he looked at Looker and scoffed. "They're only International Police. What is there to fear? Just wipe them all out!"

Either the man had complete faith in his own strength, or he had another advantage in reserve. Ethan knew it had to be the latter. Apart from Team Rocket's familiar trio, its members were rarely foolish enough to rush into a fight they could not win.
