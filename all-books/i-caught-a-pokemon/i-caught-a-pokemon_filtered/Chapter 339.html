Chapter 339: The Mural of Arceus

The chamber had been concealed extraordinarily well and required mechanisms to reveal it. It had to be a very special place.

Foster restrained his excitement and followed Ethan down a flight of stone steps with several other researchers. The empty room disappointed them slightly, but after the earlier chambers, they did not dwell on it. They went straight to the innermost wall with practiced ease.

After finding Ho-Oh's picture, they desperately wanted to know which Pokemon would appear next.

They hurried forward and, as expected, found another stone slab. This time, however, there was no puzzle. A simple mural stood before them.

They had prepared themselves for something extraordinary, but the Pokemon in the picture still astonished them.

It had a white body and a strange form resembling a horse, qilin, or centaur. Its four pointed hooves were gold. A mane rose high from its head, while its face was gray with green eyes and red pupils. A golden pattern marked its forehead, and its ears pointed upward. Around its torso rested a cross-shaped wheel set with four jewels.

The Pokemon resembled no creature from Earth. It seemed dangerous and alien, yet radiated a mysterious brilliance.

"It's Arceus!"

Foster recognized it at once and spoke in a low voice. He tried to calm himself, but the excitement trembling through his words completely betrayed his shock.

The other researchers cried out as well.

"My goodness! It's really Arceus!?"

"Why would it appear here? Did the people of that era already know of it and worship it?"

No one had ever truly seen Arceus, but references to it appeared frequently throughout ancient records, providing evidence that it really existed.

Seeing his companions lose their composure, Foster recovered and called to them, "Calm down! This is only a mural. It doesn't prove anything by itself!"

His words finally quieted the group.

Ethan approached the mural and ran a hand across it.

"Doesn't this look like a large door to you?"

At his suggestion, Foster and the others examined it more carefully. Only then did they notice that the mural had been set into the wall and truly resembled a stone door.

"Where does it lead?"

"Could it lead to the realm of the gods?"

While the researchers exclaimed around him, Foster used all his strength to push and pull. The stone door did not move at all. He searched beside it but found no switch.

Ethan also asked Gengar to pass through and inspect the other side. It found nothing but rock and soil.

This was probably the gate to the Sinjoh Ruins. Unfortunately, they had no way to open it.

Still, Ethan had a feeling he would return here someday. Clearly, that day was not today.

Despite finding no way through, Foster and the others remained thrilled. They eagerly took out notebooks and cameras and began documenting and studying the mural.

For Ethan, the matter had reached its end.

Since he had confirmed that the door could not be opened for now, he decided to leave. Perhaps someday he would encounter a way to unlock it.

Looking back over the past few hours, this unplanned visit to the Ruins of Alph park might have seemed like a waste of time that left him empty-handed. Yet it had still brought rewards. Ethan had made a new friend among the researchers and laid the groundwork for something in the future.

For now, however, he had no desire to stay any longer.

"Mr. Foster, the rest is up to you. I've already stayed too long, and it's time for me to leave."

"What? You're leaving already?"

Foster looked regretful, but he quickly collected himself, approached Ethan, and offered his hand.

"Mr. Vale, I sincerely thank you for helping us find this place. Without you, we might never have discovered its secret in our entire lives. The vanished transmission, the mysterious Unown script, Ho-Oh, Arceus... There are so many mysteries still waiting for me to solve. It's a shame you can't stay and continue the research with us!"

Ethan clasped his hand firmly.

"There's no need to thank me. It was only a small favor. Besides, I enjoy solving puzzles, and this was genuinely fascinating. I'd like to uncover all these secrets too, but I'm a Trainer. Challenging the League is what I should be doing."

"All right. Then I wish you success at the Silver Conference!" Foster nodded gratefully and smiled. "I'll remain here and continue the research. If I discover anything, you'll be the first person I contact!"

"I hope everything goes well."

"Take care!"

After leaving the Ruins of Alph park, Ethan and Sabrina saw the sun beginning to set. They were preparing to mount Charizard and fly to Violet City for the night when Sabrina suddenly received a call.

It came from the Kanto Pokemon League. Ethan did not deliberately listen to the conversation, but fragments of speech let him catch several key points.

The League had noticed that Sabrina had been away too long. It ordered her to return to Saffron Gym as soon as possible and resume her duties as its Gym Leader.

Even though Sabrina's father had been acting in her place, the officials still believed the "Psychic Beauty" was better suited to lead Saffron Gym.

Sabrina wanted to ignore them, abandon the position, and continue traveling with Ethan. Fortunately, Ethan stopped her in time.

Being a Pokemon League Gym Leader brought social status and public respect, but it was also a job.

Ethan could not let Sabrina risk losing her Gym Leader qualifications merely to accompany him on his travels.

He spoke gently. "Sabrina, it's all right. Go back."

She opened her mouth, but Ethan continued reassuring her.

"This is important work. We can't be too willful about it. And a short separation doesn't mean anything. It only gives us something better to look forward to when we reunite. This is part of your life, and I won't selfishly keep you beside me."

Sabrina fell silent. Her expression darkened, making it clear that she did not wish to leave him either.

"It's settled, then." Ethan stroked her dark hair. "Don't take it too hard. You've been away for quite a while. You can return home and rest for a time. Once this matter is over, you can travel with me again."
