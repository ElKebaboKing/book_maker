Chapter 172: Icefall Cave

Once the possibility occurred to Lorelei, she wanted to search the cave at once. But the stars were already out. She restrained herself and decided they would go in after daybreak.

Day and night made little difference underground, but they were tired and needed sleep.

Late that night, Ethan lay awake in the guest room. Moonlight shone through the window.

By its light, he took two Great Balls from his belt and held them up. Zapdos and Articuno were inside.

"You two haven't had much fresh air since the Indigo League started," he murmured. "Don't worry. Once I find Moltres, I'll let you spread your wings and fight as much as you like."

The Great Balls rocked at the same time. Then both sprang open, releasing Zapdos and Articuno into the room.

The temperature plunged. Snowflakes drifted from their feathers, and sparks flashed around the furniture.

They opened their beaks to cry out.

Alarmed, Ethan caught a beak in each hand before either bird could make a sound. "Hey! Why did you both come out? This is someone else's house. Don't wreck anything!"

He could guess why they had appeared. They had been cooped up for too long, and the prospect of battling Moltres had made them restless.

Since they were out already, he let them move around the room instead of recalling them immediately.

That was a mistake. Both Pokemon were curious about everything. Zapdos was especially excitable; one sweep of its wings knocked the contents of a bookshelf onto the floor.

Fortunately, the carpet muffled the fall. Even so, someone had heard. Footsteps approached along the hall.

Ethan snatched up the Great Balls and recalled both birds in a lightning-fast blur.

A moment later, Lorelei spoke from the other side of the door. "Is everything all right in there? I thought I heard something."

"It's nothing." Ethan hurried to put the fallen things back. "I accidentally kicked the foot of the bed."

"I see. I brought you some milk. May I come in?"

With everything back in place, Ethan opened the door.

Lorelei was wearing pajamas and looked as if she had just finished washing up. She held a steaming cup of milk. Her eyes moved quickly around the room, but she saw nothing out of place and handed him the cup.

"Drink it while it's hot. We're close to the lake and the cave, right where the cold air comes out. Nights get chilly here. Something warm should help you fall asleep."

Ethan accepted it with a smile. "I've been having trouble sleeping. Your timing is perfect."

When Lorelei had gone, he closed the door and drank the milk. Then he let out a soft sigh.

Quite a few people already knew he owned Zapdos and Articuno. Even so, he took care to protect that secret when he could. The fewer people who knew, the safer he would be.

The next morning, the three of them ate breakfast, put on heavy clothes, gathered lights, and entered the cave.

Lorelei had been the first to discover it and had given it a fitting name: Icefall Cave.

Its entrance was narrow, but the passage opened into a much larger space. The path sloped steadily downward. Between the slippery ice underfoot and the sharp icicles overhead, every step demanded care. Their flashlights cast a blue-green glow across the frozen surfaces.

Lorelei led the way. "Watch where you step. The ice is thin in places and can give way beneath you."

"We will," Ethan said.

After his time in the Seafoam Islands, he knew how to move through a place like this. He followed Lorelei and, whenever they reached a dangerous stretch, turned to offer Sabrina his hand.

Sabrina could have used her psychic powers to cross the broken ice. For some reason, she chose to take Ethan's hand and walk over carefully instead.

Lorelei happened to look back and caught them at it. Just as she thought.

A man and a woman traveling alone together? Knowing Sabrina, Lorelei could hardly believe the girl felt nothing for Ethan. It was harder to picture the cool, distant Psychic-type Gym Leader in love. Would she show a softer side when they were alone?

The ice creaked beneath their feet. Lorelei pulled her coat tighter as Ethan and Sabrina looked around.

"The cave goes down several hundred meters," she told them. "There are several levels. At the bottom, an underground current connects it to the sea."

Ethan frowned. "How did you discover an underground current? Don't tell me you swam through it yourself."

Lorelei laughed. "Why not? It was good training."

Ethan gave her a thumbs-up. Who else would jump into water that cold just to learn where it went? She must have had an extraordinary amount of time on her hands.

They continued downward. Only after walking a long way did they spot a few wild Dewgong and Cloyster. Ethan and Sabrina let them go, but Lorelei frowned. They still had no sign of Team Rocket, and those were the first Pokemon they had seen.

Even on his first visit, Ethan could tell something was wrong. "Is it usually this quiet?"

Lorelei hesitated, then shook her head. "No. There used to be plenty of Pokemon living here. Now... You're right. Something must have happened farther down to drive so many away."

They quickened their pace. The deeper they went, the colder it became, and the passageways branched in every direction like a natural maze.

The cave's remoteness was a mercy. Anyone who wandered in without a guide and took a wrong turn could easily be trapped there.
