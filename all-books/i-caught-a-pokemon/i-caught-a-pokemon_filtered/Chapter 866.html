Chapter 866: Who Will Be King of the Charizard?

The strongest Charizard in the Charicific Valley slowly opened its eyes.

It rose to its full, imposing height. Only then did Ethan see several large scars across its chest. They were marks of past battles, proof of how many formidable opponents it had faced and defeated.

When its gaze settled on Ethan's Charizard, a fierce pressure seemed to burst from it. No one became the valley's strongest without earning the title.

Ethan's Charizard did not retreat under that threatening stare. It stepped forward and returned the look just as fiercely. Its own presence grew steadily stronger until it matched the wild Charizard's.

They stood face-to-face, neither intimidated, neither willing to yield. A tense, combative hush fell over the platform.

It's been a long time since I felt an atmosphere like this, Liza said with a smile. They look evenly matched.

Ethan looked at his Charizard instead of answering her. Go on. I'll be here to see you win!

Charizard nodded and strode toward the valley's champion.

The two stopped opposite each other, ready to fight. Ethan, Liza, and May stepped back to give them room. Even Gengar floated out of Ethan's shadow, folded its arms, and settled in to watch.

All around them, the watching Charizard fell silent.

The two opponents said something to each other. Gengar translated for the people beside it.

Just insults. You're no match for me, I'll beat you, that sort of thing. Your Charizard isn't very clever, if you ask me. I'd swear at the other one until it got angry enough to lose its head. Then winning would be easy!

Ethan gave Gengar a sidelong look. Just watch quietly.

A light breeze lifted a leaf from the ground. At that instant, both Charizard moved.

The valley's champion spread its wings and shot into the sky. It ruled this place, and it preferred to fight from above. Like a lord looking down on a challenger, it could use its aerial skill to defeat opponents.

Challengers usually had no choice but to follow. That gave the champion room to exploit its greater speed, pull ahead, and seize the initiative.

This challenger, however, was different. A Pokemon trained under a human had learned a more systematic way to fight.

Instead of rising after its opponent and falling into its rhythm, Ethan's Charizard breathed fire. A huge fireball roared toward the path the wild Charizard was about to take.

Fire was not especially effective against another Fire-type Pokemon. Everyone knew that. But a direct hit was not the point.

Climbing with its head raised, the wild Charizard did not notice the fireball at first. Its keen senses and agility saved it at the last moment: it tilted aside and easily avoided the attack. At their level, a long-range strike was unlikely to land cleanly.

Boom.

The fireball exploded beside it. The blast missed its body, but thick smoke spread out and swallowed it, hiding its view.

When the wild Charizard burst through the smoke into the open air, Ethan's Charizard had vanished. It looked around in surprise.

Ethan's Charizard shot out of the haze and slammed headfirst into the wild one's lower belly before it could react.

Yes! Gengar shouted.

Beautiful! May cried at the same moment.

From below, they had seen the whole move. Ethan's Charizard had followed its fireball upward, using the burst of flame and smoke to mask its approach.

No Trainer was giving it commands, but tactics learned through training had become part of the way it fought. That was the difference between it and a wild Pokemon. It had gained the advantage in their first exchange.

The unexpected hit told the valley's champion that this challenger was dangerous. Its usual way of defeating opponents had failed.

With a roar, it righted itself in the air and charged at Ethan's Charizard.

The champion was fast. A few beats of its wings brought it close enough to grapple in midair, and its powerful body gave it the advantage in a contest of strength. Its flames were surely formidable too, though it had yet to show them.

In raw speed, power, and physique, the larger wild Charizard surpassed Ethan's. But those advantages only went so far. It lacked the same refinement of technique.

Ethan's Charizard met it with the structured methods and varied experience of a trained battler.

They fought in the air, beating at each other with wings and claws. Dragon-type energy and flames flashed between them.

Then Ethan's Charizard caught an opening. It seized the wild Charizard from behind, locked its arms around it, and drove it toward the ground with Seismic Toss.

The wild Charizard struggled, but its strength did not exceed Ethan's by enough to break free in time.

Before the watching crowd, it struck the earth with a thunderous crash. Most of its body plunged into the soil, leaving a great crater in the platform.

Ethan's Charizard flapped away and landed nearby. It had hurt its opponent twice, but the fight had not been as easy as it looked. Making those openings and keeping up the intense aerial struggle had cost it energy. Its chest rose and fell with heavy breaths.

Dust from the impact hid the wild Charizard from view.

May watched the cloud, but nothing emerged. That looked painful. Could the throw have beaten it?

Not so easily, Ethan said. If that were enough, it wouldn't be the strongest Charizard here.

As if to prove him right, a large shape appeared within the dust. Its eyes glowed red as it walked steadily toward them.

When it stepped clear, May gasped. What? It doesn't even have a scratch?

It's smart. Ethan narrowed his eyes. Just before it hit, it used Dragon Breath against the ground. It broke up the hard surface and softened the impact, even though it couldn't escape the hold.

So all that effort was for nothing? May asked.

Not quite, Liza said. It reduced the damage; it didn't avoid it. It fell from a great height. Look closely beneath its old scars. Do you see the new scrapes?

May studied the Charizard again. Faint abrasions did mark its body. She had missed them among the older scars. Ethan's Charizard had achieved something after all.

The wild Charizard roared and sprang forward, flying low over the ground as it drove straight at its challenger.

Ethan had seen enough by now to understand how it fought. It relied on its formidable body and the most direct kind of attack. That could overwhelm an ordinary Trainer's Pokemon. Against his Charizard, it was less certain.

His Charizard exhaled black smoke. A thick Smokescreen covered it just as the wild one rushed inside.

Both disappeared from sight. Flashes of flame pierced the smoke, and Charizard cries rose from withinsome furious, some pained. Their voices were too similar for anyone outside to tell which one had the upper hand.

As the smoke thinned, the two Charizard came back into view. Both were breathing hard, standing about three meters apart and watching each other.

Another draw. By every means available, Ethan's Charizard had checked the superior close-range fighter and kept the contest even.

Liza watched with open admiration. Your Charizard is remarkable. I haven't seen one push our strongest this hard in a long time.

She was right about the valley's champion. Outside this place, it would be far stronger than even Lance's Charizard. To Ethan, its sheer physical strength nearly rivaled the Charizard of Leon, Galar's strongest Champion.

Now Ethan understood why Ash's Charizard had remained here so long after Ash declared it would become the strongest. With an opponent like this guarding the top, it was no wonder Ash's Charizard had yet to claim that place.

Today, though, Ethan believed the title would change hands.

Go on, Charizard. Show it what you can do!

Ethan did not speak aloud, but his Charizard seemed to feel his fighting spirit. An even fiercer presence burst from it.

Strangely, Ethan felt something in return. He seemed to sense Charizard's blood, its lungs, the fire in its eyes, every breath it took.

And with that came its fierce desire to defeat the opponent before it. The bond between them felt closer than ever.

Before Ethan could think further, his Charizard abandoned the tactics and careful technique it had used until now. It flew straight toward the wild Charizard and initiated a close-range fight.

The champion did not know why its challenger had grown still more determined. But a direct clash suited it perfectly, and it flew to meet the attack.

Now the struggle for the valley's honor truly began.

Claws struck flesh. Fists hit bodies. The two traded savage blows without holding back. Neither was trying to take the other's life, but every strike showed the ruthless force of a primal fight.

May had never seen Pokemon battle this way. She had imagined battles as an exchange of named moves and counters. This looked brutal.

The sight shook her ten-year-old understanding of the world. Pokemon did not always live peacefully with one another. This world, too, had its struggles between the strong and the weak.

Even so, she frowned and kept watching closely. This was the most important battle of Ethan's Charizard's life so far. She also knew she could learn something from the two Pokemon before her.
