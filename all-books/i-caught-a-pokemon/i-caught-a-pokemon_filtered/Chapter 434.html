Chapter 434: A Battle Full of Surprises

Harrison would naturally pay a heavy price for looking down on Misty.

Under Harrison's command, Qwilfish first dodged Corsola's attack, then countered with Water Gun. Misty immediately used Mirror Coat, reflecting the Water Gun straight back!

The attack retraced its path and struck Qwilfish, causing damage to the Pokemon that had fired it.

Misty's timing with Mirror Coat could only be called perfect. She used it at the very instant the attack landed, leaving Harrison no time to react.

The reversal took Harrison completely by surprise, and he hurriedly checked his Pokemon's condition. Fortunately, because Water Gun shared Qwilfish's type, it had not inflicted any serious injuries.

Although Harrison was somewhat startled, he still did not take Misty seriously. He assumed she had merely succeeded through luck and promptly launched a second attack.

Qwilfish opened its mouth and began frantically drawing in seawater. Its body continuously expanded until it resembled a spiny ball.

Ethan recognized the technique at a glance. This was a method of strengthening Pin Missile, a charged attack.

Using the water inside its body to increase the pressure would launch the needles faster and with greater force, preventing the opponent from defending effectively.

This was Ethan's first time seeing such a technique. It broadened his horizons and allowed him to learn something from Harrison.

He had to admit that middle age brought experience. Someone like Harrison might not be especially powerful, but after so many years as a Trainer, he had certainly not wasted his time. People like him generally possessed one or two special techniques.

Facing such an attack, Misty rapidly considered her options.

Mirror Coat could not reflect Pin Missile, so she neither repeated the same tactic nor sent Corsola into the water to evade it. Instead, she chose to meet it head-on with Spike Cannon.

The choice was extremely appropriate. Spike Cannon attacked in much the same fashion as Pin Missile, and its unbroken stream of projectiles could offset Pin Missile's advantage in speed.

White light shone from both Pokemon simultaneously. A moment later, the two similar moves launched at nearly the same time.

Whoosh, whoosh, whoosh, whoosh!

Countless needles and spikes of energy streaked toward one another. The strengthened Pin Missile was clearly faster, crossing the air in an instant. It finally collided with Spike Cannon above a point closer to Misty, producing a chain of explosions!

The resulting shock wave knocked Corsola over. Harrison had anticipated this outcome and ordered Qwilfish to dive beneath the water beforehand, allowing it to avoid the blast.

The lingering force and smoke slowly dissipated.

Before Corsola could rise, Harrison decisively ordered Qwilfish to use Tackle, intending to press his advantage and finish Corsola in one stroke.

Even through the screen, Ethan could see a trace of a triumphant smile on Harrison's face!

Then, to everyone's astonishment, Corsola happened to rise just as Qwilfish leaped in front of it, turning its body toward the oncoming Pokemon. In the next instant, Qwilfish became wedged between the four horns on Corsola's back!

The force of the impact lodged it firmly in place, leaving it completely unable to move!

The sudden turn of events brought stunned expressions to every spectator's face and froze Harrison's smile where it was!

On the battlefield, Corsola was naturally uncomfortable with another Pokemon embedded in its back. Top-heavy, it paced awkwardly in place and tried to shake Qwilfish loose, but Qwilfish's spines were tangled between its horns and would not come free.

Qwilfish struggled desperately as well, but it could not exert any strength!

The two Pokemon remained locked in place!

Everyone stared at the scene in amazement!

After Totodile had bitten down on Kingdra's snout only a short while ago, they never imagined they would witness another moment this dramatic!

Misty remained dazed for a moment before realizing this was an opportunity that might never come again. She ordered Corsola to use Spike Cannon once more.

White light flared around Corsola. Unable to counterattack or evade, Qwilfish took the attack at full force!

The impact of Spike Cannon finally tore Qwilfish free and launched it into the air. After enduring the attack directly, it flew for several seconds before slamming heavily onto a stone pillar and falling unconscious!

The moment Qwilfish lost the ability to battle, the referee announced the result.

Misty jumped for joy, while Harrison's face fell. He had never imagined he would lose the match in such a fashion!

The spectators applauded, and Misty waved to them with a radiant smile.

In the waiting room, seeing Misty win this way left Ethan lost in thought.

Just like Ash's match, he had never expected both of their battles to end with such dramatic accidents!

It felt as though neither had displayed much of their true strength. Both had won through sheer happenstance.

As the foremost Trainer of unpredictability, Ash maintaining this style was nothing unusual. But now even Misty was fighting this way. She seemed to be growing more and more like Ash.

Regardless of the circumstances, all three had advanced to the round of thirty-two, which was certainly cause for celebration. As for the process... Well... It did not really matter.

After each of them completed one match, none had any more battles that morning. Once Misty returned, the three left the waiting room and headed into the concourse to meet Erika and Brock.

Erika and Brock naturally showered them with praise. Ash and Misty's matches had been especially entertaining, so the pair discussed them with great enthusiasm. Their vivid descriptions of the crowd's excited reactions left both Trainers rather embarrassed.

The group ate lunch at a restaurant inside the colosseum, then rested for a while. Once every morning match had ended, the field of thirty-two was complete. Following an announcement over the loudspeakers, Ethan, Ash, and Misty hurried back to the battlefield for the round of thirty-two.

Once again, luck kept the three from being paired against one another. This time, Ash and Misty displayed the strength expected of them, defeating their opponents through ability and successfully advancing.

The two were delighted, but the spectators were somewhat disappointed.

Compared with ordinary battles, the audience actually preferred seeing something more "exciting"!

As for Ethan, there were no surprises.

He continued using Blastoise, which performed as steadily as ever. With a substantial advantage, it defeated a female Trainer and carried Ethan into the round of sixteen.

Thus, Ethan, Ash, and Misty all successfully reached the final sixteen. From this point onward, with the weaker competitors eliminated, none of their remaining opponents would be easy. The difficulty would undoubtedly rise sharply!
