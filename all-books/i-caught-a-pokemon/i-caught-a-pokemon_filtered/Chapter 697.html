Chapter 697: Tomboy Phoebe

Neither of them knew when Team Magma would arrive. To be safe, Ethan sent out Dragonite as soon as they left Lilycove City, and they flew toward Mt. Pyre.

White clouds seemed close enough to touch. The ground swept past beneath them.

The mountain looked nearby on a map, but reaching it on foot would take most of a day. They would have to cross a stretch of water, then make their way through rugged hills. Even the paths vanished as the terrain grew steeper.

With Dragonite carrying them, they avoided all of that and made much better time.

After several hours in the air, Dragonite reached a peninsula. Ethan could see all of Mt. Pyre below.

It was nothing like the bare, lifeless mountain people might imagine. A high, green peak rose above dense forest, with rivers and the sea in the distance. Fog veiled its summit year-round, hiding every trail. The scenery was beautiful, yet the mountain felt strangely gloomy and dangerous. Perhaps that was only because so many Pokemon had been buried there.

People even said terrible things appeared on Mt. Pyre after dark. Some villagers at its foot had made a rule never to climb it once night fell.

Ethan suspected Ghost-type Pokemon were responsible for most of the stories, perhaps with the occasional rare Pokemon mixed in. There was little to fear once one understood what was happening. People who did not had plenty of room for their imaginations to run wild.

Following the coordinates Anabel had sent, Ethan directed Dragonite to land halfway up the mountain. The summit was still some distance away, but flying into the thick fog risked a collision with the rocks. Walking would be safer.

He also wanted to use the weak signal they still had to update Anabel on their progress.

His relationship with the International Police had never been better. They worked together readily, each helping the other. Ethan had repeatedly involved himself in their investigations and helped bring down criminal organizations. Anabel and Looker, for their part, had been just as willing to offer him their goodwill and cooperation.

They kept Dragonite beside them as a guard and began climbing.

Forest covered much of Mt. Pyre's surface, while its interior was riddled with connected caves. Together they formed a vast maze in three dimensions. The mountain was a natural Pokemon graveyard, and its caves also sheltered the Ghost-type Pokemon that appeared and vanished so mysteriously.

They had walked only a few hundred meters above the landing site when they had already passed several cave mouths. Some were just large enough for a person to squeeze through. Others were so small that only a Pokemon like Rattata could enter freely.

A chill seemed to rise from nowhere. Misty shivered. If we weren't here for this, I don't think I'd ever set foot in a place like this.

Ethan climbed onto a rock, then turned and helped her up. It used to have a different name. People called it Mt. Fire, and it wasn't a burial place for Pokemon. At some point that custom began, and over time people started calling it Mt. Pyre instead.

As they climbed, tendrils of fog appeared around them. Misty gripped Ethan's arm and looked from side to side, afraid something frightening might emerge without warning.

Using a few obvious landmarks, they found a narrow, secluded trail. Phoebe's grandparents had probably worn it into the mountainside over decades of walking. They followed the rough path, crossed several caves, and finally reached the summit.

For some reason, the fog that had blanketed the mountain below did not reach this area. Sunlight poured down on an open space that seemed cut off from the rest of the mountain. The eerie feeling was gone as well. The air was fresh, the sun warm, and the view lovely. Ethan could understand why Phoebe's grandparents had wanted to stay.

Better still, they had seen no sign of Team Magma on the way up and no trace of a battle. The group apparently had not reached the summit yet.

But where was Phoebe?

Ethan was about to look for her grandparents' home when something whistled through the air beside him. He turned and saw a small stone flying toward his head.

He did not need to dodge. Dragonite swept a wing through the air and knocked the pebble away.

Only then did Ethan and Misty see who had thrown it. A girl about Misty's age stood on the branch of a large tree, watching them with dark eyes.

Her clothes were striking. Two large red flowers adorned her head. A blue bandeau covered her chest, leaving her collarbones, arms, and waist bare. A blue skirt with a high opening shifted in the breeze, revealing a glimpse of her leg. Such an outfit would hardly draw notice by the sea, but it seemed daring on this mountain.

Ethan recognized her almost immediately: Phoebe, one of Hoenn's Elite Four, and the person they had come to meet.

You're Phoebe, aren't you? he called up. Anabel asked us to help you deal with Team Magma.

Of course I know you're here to help. Otherwise, I wouldn't have greeted you with only a pebble.

Phoebe came down from the branch with the agility of a monkey and landed on the ground. Ethan glanced at her feet. The thick grass helped muffle her steps, but the straw sandals she wore made them quieter still.

Misty stared. This girl was one of the Elite Four? She looked about Misty's own age!

Back in Sootopolis City, Misty had resolved not to let any more girls get close to Ethan. Looking at Phoebe, though, she felt no jealousy at all. It had nothing to do with Phoebe's rank. In Misty's private and admittedly unkind opinion, Phoebe was ordinary-lookingmore like a tomboy than anything else. Even the two red flowers could not soften the impression made by her short, boyish hair.

Her outfit showed off her legs and waist, but her skin was slightly dark and she had little in the way of curves. Misty could not imagine Ethan being attracted to her. He cared about looks, after all, and the girls around him were beautiful. With Mistythe world's prettiest girl, by her own reckoningalready at his side, why would he look at Phoebe?

Feeling completely at ease, Misty gave Phoebe a warm look.

Phoebe knew nothing of the appraisal Misty had just made. She came up to them, studied the two of them with curiosity, then shook her head.

I told Anabel how serious this is. Why did she send only you two? Where's Drake? Didn't she say he was nearby?

Clearly, Phoebe did not know Ethan or what he could do. She had expected more than a young man and a pretty girl. That was understandable. Not everyone followed events in other regions as closely as Drake and Wallace did. Even an Elite Four member had her own concerns, and Phoebe was young.

Ethan knew little about Phoebe in return. She had a remarkably low profile. Like Sidney and Glacia, she had not even appeared in the main anime. She seemed to receive little attention from its creators, either.

Since she plainly doubted them, Ethan said, There are only two of us, but we're both strong.

Exactly! Misty added. We've dealt with Team Magma plenty of times. We know them well. We'll be fine.

Phoebe watched them for a moment, then nodded. I suppose you're right. Anabel wouldn't have sent you here if she didn't trust you. If she believes you can handle this, I have no reason to doubt you. We shouldn't talk out here, though. Come with me.

She led them to a wooden cabin. It was small, with a vegetable garden outside where ripe produce hung among the plants. A drying rack and farming tools made the place feel lived in. It was plainly her grandparents' home.

The cabin was silent. Through an open window, Misty could see that nobody was inside.

Where are your grandparents? she asked. Aren't they supposed to guard this place?

Guard it? They're just an ordinary old couple who live here, Phoebe said. They don't have the strength to protect anything. I've already driven off several small Team Magma attacks. There could be another fight at any time, so I sent them somewhere safe at the foot of the mountain.

She did not enter the garden. Instead, she guided Ethan and Misty past it and deeper into the summit.

Before long, they reached a stone shrine. It resembled the one Ethan had seen on Shamouti Island: a small tower of weathered stones stacked together. A slab inside seemed intended to hold offerings. Two hollows had been carved into its center. One was empty; the other held a stone shaped like the Blue Orb, but glowing red.

That's the Red Orb, isn't it? Ethan asked.

Yes. Phoebe nodded. The Blue Orb was in the other hollow. Team Aqua stole it, and we still don't know where it is. One is gone already. I absolutely won't let anyone take this one too.

Ethan hesitated. He did not tell her that Shelly had the Blue Orb.

When Anabel's police vessel had rescued them from the submarine at sea, Ethan had deliberately asked Shelly to hide the orb before she left the submarine. Apart from the few of them and the members of Team Aqua involved, no one knew it was in her possession.

Now, with Phoebe talking about it, Ethan chose to keep quiet. Team Aqua and Team Magma were both after the orbs, and leaving them here had plainly failed to protect them. With two Legendary Pokemon involved, it would be better to return the Blue Orb once everything had settled.

Misty noticed that Ethan did not want to reveal its whereabouts. She changed the subject, feigning ignorance. If the orb is so important, why don't you take it away or hide it somewhere else?

She had a point. Hiding it would seem safer than standing guard over it.

But Phoebe answered without hesitation. Hiding won't solve this. When something like this happens, you need to deal with it instead of running away. Defeat the people coming after the orb, and the matter will truly be over.

For all her youth, Phoebe was ready to take responsibility. Ethan and Misty were reminded that she was no ordinary girl. She was one of the Elite Four who represented Hoenn.
