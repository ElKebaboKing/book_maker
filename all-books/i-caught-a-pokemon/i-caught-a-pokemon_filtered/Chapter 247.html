Chapter 247: The Lake of Birth and Evolution

Quackenpoker did not explain further. He checked the time, frowned at how much had passed, and said, "We can't waste any more time here. You'll understand when we arrive. Come on, hurry."

He turned and quickened his pace toward the summit.

After spending a little time with him, Ethan and the others knew he meant no harm. He was simply an eccentric researcher who paid little attention to ordinary manners. They still had plenty of questions, but with Quackenpoker walking away, they followed.

Beside them, the Magikarp continued swimming tirelessly against the current. Soon the school reached a cluster of three small waterfalls. They tried to climb, but the water rushed straight down. Even at full effort, many Magikarp were swept off partway up. Only a few thrashed hard enough to overcome the force and reach the water above.

Ethan stopped again. For the Magikarp, this was an enormous obstacle. He could hardly believe he was seeing such a sight. They were working too hard for this to look like a simple spawning migration. It reminded him of the old tale of carp leaping the Dragon Gate.

In this world, though, a Magikarp that made it over the falls would not become a dragon soaring through the clouds. It would become a fierce and terrifying Gyarados.

He took out his phone to film. In the short time he watched, hundreds of Magikarp failed, exhausted, and were washed downstream. The others kept coming, one wave after another, throwing themselves at the falls.

Tracey turned to watch too. "Mr. Quackenpoker, what's at the top that makes them work so hard?"

"Only an ordinary lake," Quackenpoker answered without stopping. "It doesn't look like much. I call it the Lake of Birth and Evolution."

"The Lake of Birth and Evolution?" the others repeated.

"That's where these Magikarp were born. Every year at this time, they come back to complete their evolution."

Evolution? Ethan stared. An ordinary Magikarp did not evolve easily. It often required a Trainer's careful raising and training. Was Quackenpoker saying that a Magikarp only had to return to this lake to evolve?

If that was true, the place would seem magical to anyone raising Magikarp. Ethan thought like a Trainer, though, and his interests were not quite the same as everyone else's. Tracey, as a Pokemon Watcher, saw a different question.

"Mr. Quackenpoker, how many Magikarp make it up each year?" he asked eagerly.

Quackenpoker gave him a thoughtful look, glanced back at the falls, and shook his head. "It varies. From what I've seen in past years, at most one in ten gets over the waterfalls and reaches the lake."

Only one in ten? The number surprised them, though the force of the water made it understandable. Given the size of the school, that could still mean nearly a thousand Magikarp reached the summit.

"Do all the ones that reach the lake evolve?" Ash asked, astonished.

"Don't be ridiculous." Quackenpoker rolled his eyes, amused and annoyed at once. "Getting there doesn't guarantee anything. Only one in ten of those Magikarp evolves into Gyarados."

One tenth of one tenth. That would still produce around a hundred new Gyarados, hardly a small number.

Ash scratched his head, about to ask more. Quackenpoker waved him off. "Enough questions. If we don't move, we'll miss their mass evolution!"

Ethan and the others fell silent. Now that they had come this far, they wanted to see how the Magikarp evolved together. They hurried on.

None of them noticed a clump of bushes moving shortly after they left.

"Hey, did you hear all that?" a voice asked from inside. It sounded almost human, though a little raspy. Team Rocket's trio poked their heads out.

"Of course I did!" James's eyes widened with excitement. "A lake of evolution! What an amazing discovery!"

"Idiot," Jessie said, folding her arms and giving him a sidelong look. "It's an opportunity."

"Oh? How?"

Jessie lifted her chin. "If it's a lake of evolution, we catch enough Magikarp and throw them in. Then we'll have lots of powerful Gyarados! With those, we can build a magnificent, earthshaking, unbeatable, greatest-ever Gyarados army!"

"A Gyarados army?" James pictured himself commanding a whole force of them. "The Boss would finally take notice of us! We could even hold our heads high in front of that woman with the watermelon-rind glasses!"

"Exactly!" Jessie clenched her fists, eyes blazing. "With a Gyarados army, Team Rocket will be unstoppable. No one will stand a chance against us!"

"But..." James hesitated. "Will it really be that easy? Gyarados and we have never gotten along."

"Don't worry. I've thought of that," Jessie said with a confident laugh. "We catch them while they're still Magikarp. Then they can't disobey us after they evolve!"
