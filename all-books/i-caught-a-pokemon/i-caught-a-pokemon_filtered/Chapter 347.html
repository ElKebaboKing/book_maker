Chapter 347: Cerulean Cave

After leaving Nugget Bridge, Ethan continued west along the river. With his careful search and the combined efforts of several Pokemon, he soon discovered a hidden, unmarked cave at a bend in the river.

Without hesitation, he led his three Pokemon inside.

At first, the cave descended along a dark passage. Ethan walked several dozen meters before a faint light appeared ahead. Once his eyes adjusted to the darkness, he saw that the glow came from hundreds upon hundreds of crystals scattered throughout the cavern.

Even without a flashlight, their ghostly blue-green light revealed the scene before him.

It seemed he had indeed come to the right place. The only question was whether Mewtwo was here now.

Drip... Drip...

Water fell from overhead at a steady rhythm, leaving the ground extremely slippery. Unlike the ice caves Ethan had visited before, this place was noticeably warmer and more humid.

According to rumor, the cave originally had no name. The people who happened upon it had called it Cerulean Cave because of its proximity to Cerulean City. It was also known as the Unknown Dungeon.

Judging by its interior, most of Cerulean Cave consisted of soluble rock and limestone. Dissolution had formed stalactites and stalagmites, making it a typical solution cave, while large deposits of quartz lay throughout it.

Ethan walked deeper for some time. Perhaps sensing an intruder, a large flock of Golbat burst from the darkness and swept rapidly over his head.

Screeech!

The Golbat's piercing cries and violently beating wings filled the cavern with noise, putting Ethan's three Pokemon on edge.

Scyther and Dragonair hurried to shield Ethan behind them, but fortunately, the Golbat did not attack the unexpected visitors.

Once the flock had flown away, Ethan continued deeper into the cave.

After advancing several hundred meters, he reached an underground lake. Its surface stretched far across the cavern, and a swift current divided the cave in two, completely blocking his path.

As Ethan considered how to cross, an enormous Graveler suddenly leaped out of the darkness.

Bang!

The Graveler, its entire body seemingly wrapped in stone, landed heavily. Its immense size alone gave it an intimidating presence.

Clearly displeased by the intrusion into its territory, it opened its mouth and roared at Ethan.

The sound echoed through the cave, making his ears ache.

Ethan studied it carefully. The Graveler looked more than two meters tall. With a body that large, it unquestionably qualified as a dominant Pokemon.

From the limited information he had found online, Ethan knew that Cerulean Cave's damp, dark environment supported a wide variety of Pokemon. To survive in such an inhospitable place, they had developed abilities far above the average for their species.

This Graveler was clearly one of the strongest among them.

Still, it had approached with hostile intent and could not be reasoned with. Ethan had no reason to hold back. He immediately issued an order to the three Pokemon beside him.

"Finish this quickly. Take it down!"

Dragonair, Larvitar, and Scyther charged and engaged Graveler. Its dense body and excellent defenses allowed it to flaunt its strength for a while, but it was far too slow. Looking clumsy beneath the three Pokemon's combined assault, it soon fell at a disadvantage.

Neither side restrained its attacks. Powerful moves struck again and again, making the entire cavern tremble.

Small stones and grains of sand continually fell from overhead. Fortunately, the cave's structure was exceptionally sturdy, and there was little danger of a collapse.

While dodging the debris, Ethan observed the battle. By wild Pokemon standards, this Graveler truly was strong, much like Scyther had once been. But it was still a wild Pokemon. With only a few brief commands from Ethan, his three partners soon overwhelmed and defeated it.

Crash!

Graveler slammed heavily into the ground and lost consciousness.

Looking at its motionless body, Ethan had no desire to catch it. He simply called to his Pokemon and hurried past.

"Let's go."

Perhaps because they had defeated Graveler, the rest of the journey was undisturbed. Many pairs of eyes still watched from the darkness, but no other wild Pokemon dared to trouble them.

After walking for several more minutes, Ethan finally reached a cliff. He peered down, but the darkness below was so deep that he could not tell where it ended.

He picked up a stone and tossed it over the edge. A long while passed before he heard a faint splash.

There seemed to be another underground lake below.

Gengar emerged from his shadow and glanced down the cliff. "Are we really going farther? Getting down will be easy, but climbing back up won't. Returning afterward could be a real problem!"

Ethan smiled. "That's what you're here for. We won't need to climb at all. You can just fly me back up."

"Thanks for thinking so highly of me." Gengar rolled its eyes, unwilling to argue with him. "More to the point, are you sure Mewtwo really lives here?"

Ethan shook his head. "I'm not certain. But once I reach the deepest part of the cave, I think everything will become clear."

He recalled Dragonair, Scyther, and Larvitar, then climbed onto Gengar's back. Carrying him, Gengar flew down the cliff.

They descended for two or three minutes before the darkness finally opened into a vast space.

Sure enough, an even larger underground lake lay below them, with a small island at its center.

As soon as they landed on the island, Ethan saw Mewtwo.

Mewtwo sat cross-legged with its eyes closed, suspended in midair within a protective sphere of light.

Although Ethan had been prepared, he had not expected to find Mewtwo so easily.

At this point in time, it really was here.

That was the advantage of possessing his memories. Another person might encounter Mewtwo by chance and later wish to find it, but they would have no idea where it had gone into hiding.

Sensing someone approach, Mewtwo slowly opened its eyes. The sight of Ethan clearly surprised it.

"You... You're the human from that time?"

"We meet again." Ethan waved. "Why are you hiding here? This cave is so deep. Finding you wasn't easy!"

"I came here to distance myself from you humans." Mewtwo stared at Ethan and spoke in a deep voice. "Did you enter this cave specifically to capture me?"

Ethan shook his head. "No, no. I only came to say hello. I really was just stopping by to see you."

His words sounded innocent, but his hands had never stopped moving. He had already taken a Poke Ball from his waist and released one of his Pokemon.

Blue light flashed, and Gardevoir appeared before Mewtwo!
