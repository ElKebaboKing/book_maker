Chapter 633: Ash: Is That It?

There was no mistaking Ethan's Water-type Pokemon. It was the Feebas he had caught not long ago.

Ash had only recently arrived in Hoenn, and most of the region's Pokemon were still unfamiliar to him. What was this one?

Seeing the astonishingly ugly yellow-brown fish with its thick lips for the first time, he took out his Pokedex and scanned it. Once he had heard the description, he had a few basic facts about Feebas.

Feebas? Ash put his Pokedex away and nodded. It doesn't look very strong. I'm surprised you'd catch a Pokemon like that, Ethan. In that case, I'll use this one!

He pulled out a Poke Ball and threw it forward. A flash of blue light appeared, and a Corphish landed in the pool.

Ethan had never battled a Corphish, but he knew its evolved form, Crawdaunt, very well. He had lost count of how many Crawdaunt belonging to Team Aqua he had defeated.

As Crawdaunt's unevolved form, Corphish looked even more like a small crayfish. It had an orange shell, a white belly, three pointed projections on its head, and two large claws. Its little beadlike eyes gave it a rather vacant expression.

Corphish was said to be full of energy wherever it went. It could adapt to almost any environment, ate whatever it found, and was hardy enough to multiply even in filthy rivers. Like Feebas, it could live in a muddy, foul-smelling ditch. It seized prey with its sharp claws, and once those great pincers closed, it would never let go, no matter what happened.

The two Water-type Pokemon swam a few laps around the pool.

After warming up, Ethan's Feebas returned to its own half and prepared to battle. Ash's Corphish, however, swam right up to Ethan and sank to the bottom of the pool!

Hey! Corphish, what are you doing?

Embarrassed, Ash hurriedly called it back to his side. He scratched his cheek and forced a smile. Sorry. I only caught it recently, and this is its first battle like this. It doesn't understand all the rules yet.

It's doing fine. Ethan waved away the apology. You should have seen Feebas when I first let it out after catching it. It didn't even understand what I said. It just kept swimming wherever it pleased, as clueless as a Magikarp. It took a lot of work to train it to behave like this.

Sure enough, Ash looked much less embarrassed. He breathed out and asked, Ethan, how many Pokemon have you caught since coming to Hoenn?

Combusken, Feebas, and a Beldum. Three Hoenn Pokemon in all.

Only three?

Ash counted up his own team. Besides Pikachu, he had caught Taillow, Treecko, and Corphish since arriving in Hoenn. He had only earned two badges so far, but his three captures matched Ethan's.

Ethan had started several days earlier, yet the gap between them was nowhere near as large as Ash had imagined. Even in badges, he was only one behind Ethan!

Just then, Ethan drew out his words with a note of relief. Though besides those three, I did catch someone else. A memorable little troublemaker, and an unexpected bonus.

Memorable and troublesome? Ash stared at him. Did you catch another Legendary Pokemon?

Of course Ash envied Ethan for having so many Legendary Pokemon. He wasn't jealous, but when Ethan mentioned a memorable troublemaker, Ash immediately assumed another Legendary had joined his team.

Of course not. Ethan shook his head. Come on, Legendary Pokemon aren't that easy to find. You can't just catch one whenever you feel like it. All right, I know I've already caught several, but I haven't even encountered one since coming to Hoenn. Have another guess. You've run into this Pokemon many times on your travels. You'd definitely remember it!

He turned to Brock with a grin. You know it too. Why don't you guess?

Brock's eyes widened a little. Huh? I know it?

What could it be?

Ash and Brock frowned and thought hard. They had traveled through two regions and seen so much along the way that neither could bring to mind a Pokemon that fit Ethan's description.

At last Ash gave up. What is it? Come on, Ethan, tell us! Stop keeping us in suspense!

You still can't guess? Ethan laughed helplessly. I'll tell you after the battle. Let the mystery last a little longer.

Ash was itching to know, but Ethan clearly wasn't going to say more. He held back his curiosity and turned his attention to the pool.

Both Pokemon were ready. At Brock's signal as referee, the battle began!

Ash took even this informal match seriously. He brought the same concentration he showed at a League tournament and was fully engaged from the first exchange.

But as they traded attacks, something seemed wrong.

It wasn't that Feebas was too strong. Quite the opposite: after several rounds, Ash realized with surprise that it kept using only simple moves like Splash and Tackle. Compared with his Corphish, Feebas had almost no attacking options at all!

What? Ash deflated like a punctured balloon, the light vanishing from his eyes. I was really looking forward to this battle. Is that it?
