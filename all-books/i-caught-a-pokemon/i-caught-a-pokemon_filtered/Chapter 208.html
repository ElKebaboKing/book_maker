Chapter 208: The Airship

Lightning fell in every direction each time the God of Thunder beat its wings.

Ash and Team Rocket instinctively ducked beneath the sailboat. Ethan stood where he was. As a bolt flashed toward him, Gardevoir raised an arm and caught it on a psychic barrier. The lightning struck with a bang, scattering sparks.

The Great Balls at Ethan's waist shook harder. His three legendary birds seemed ready to burst out at any moment.

"Easy," he murmured, touching the balls. "It isn't trying to attack me. Besides, the main actor in this drama hasn't appeared yet. It's not your turn. Be patient a little longer."

The Great Balls let his birds sense the God of Thunder without letting it sense them. That spared Ethan an unexpected complication.

Still, this Zapdos was larger than his own, though it looked otherwise much the same. It was probably stronger too.

The God of Thunder circled above the group a few times, as if searching for something. Then it folded its wings and landed atop Fire Island's shrine. Electricity burst from its body and spread outward. A strange blue mist began to fill the crater.

Footsteps approached. Sabrina, Misty, Melody, and Tracey came to Ethan's side.

Sabrina watched the bird. "What is it doing?"

Ethan did not answer at once. He touched his hair. Like everyone else's, it had gone dry and was lifting of its own accord. Even the fine hairs on his skin stood up.

Static electricity?

"It might be changing the magnetic field here with its own power," Ethan guessed. "I think it wants to turn this island into its territory too."

Melody looked around in alarm. "But this is Fire Island. If the God of Thunder came here so openly, the God of Fire should have driven it off by now!"

"Something must have happened," Ethan said. He was already certain the collector had captured Moltres. Otherwise Zapdos would not have invaded its territory.

Pikachu, which had been behaving strangely since they reached the island, sent a thin thread of electricity toward the God of Thunder.

Jessie huddled nearby, trembling. "What's it doing? An attack that weak could never beat Zapdos!"

"Idiot," Meowth said. "It's talking, not attacking. Pikachu wants to know why Zapdos came to Moltres's island."

"You can talk with electric shocks? That's amazing!"

Ethan glanced at Meowth. "He's right. Electric-type Pokemon can communicate through electrical waves, much as Psychic-type Pokemon use telepathy."

"So that's how it works! What a handy ability!"

Pikachu's signal drew no response at first. Zapdos did not even look its way. Perhaps the questions finally irritated it: the bird beat its wings and sent a current to meet Pikachu's.

The others looked lost, so Meowth translated. "Zapdos says the owner of this island has disappeared. Since it's gone, Zapdos will rule here. It says the island belongs to it now!"

"What? The God of Fire is missing? How?" Melody cried.

Her shock was understandable. In the hundreds of years since the legends of the three sacred birds began, nothing like this had happened.

Then another disturbance caught everyone's attention. The currents from Zapdos and Pikachu suddenly flew upward, beyond either Pokemon's control.

The thick clouds parted. A vast airship was approaching Fire Island, its long, lightning rod-like spikes drawing in the electricity.

Whether it sensed an enemy or resented the affront to its power, Zapdos unleashed a much stronger attack at the vessel. Every bolt vanished into the spikes, no matter how powerful it was. As the airship closed in, it began actively pulling electricity from Zapdos.

In mere seconds, the bird lost an enormous amount of power. It screamed in anger and stopped attacking. Now it was panting, as though the strength had been drained from its body.

Instinct told it to flee. Zapdos launched itself into the air, but a hatch opened in the airship's underside. Several square metal rings flew out and encircled it.

"What... what is that thing?" Melody stared.

Only Ethan knew the answer.

Zapdos struggled, forcing the rings back for a moment with an electric shock. It swept over the group's heads. Some rings continued after it; others stopped around Ethan and his companions.

Sabrina's hair lifted without wind as psychic power stirred within her. She was ready to strike, but first she leaned toward Ethan. "Do we fight?"

"No." Ethan tried to piece together his scattered memories of the story. "Let's watch for now. If I rememif I'm right, we'll meet the person behind this very soon."

He recalled Gardevoir and Jolteon into their Poke Balls.

The rings around them released strange currents. In a moment, the whole group and the sailboat were trapped inside.

After their first stunned pause, Ash and the others struck at the rings, only to be shocked and left covered in burns and scrapes. Whatever this technology was, even Pikachu's attacks could not damage it.

Magnetism lifted the rings, carrying the captives and the sailboat slowly toward the airship.

"Aaah!" Misty and Melody cried out in fright.

As they entered the vessel, Ethan looked back at the God of Thunder. It had managed to fly only a short distance before the pursuing rings caught and bound it too. They were bringing Zapdos aboard.
