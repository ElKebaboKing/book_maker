Chapter 523: Maybe I Can Beat Ethan!

Altomare stood alone amid a vast expanse of sea. Apart from one tiny, deserted island, there was no other land within a hundred and fifty nautical miles.

Canals crisscrossed the city, and most people traveled by small boat. In that respect, it resembled Venice on Earth.

Or rather, Venice was clearly the model from which this city had been made.

Ethan, Erika, Ash, Misty, and Brock left the dock and strolled into the great city.

Before leaving the waterfront, they saw a museum and two stone pillars commemorating Latias and Latios. Perhaps, as Ethan had said, the legendary Pokemon really were close by.

Ash was impatient to enter the race. As soon as they disembarked, he led the others toward the registration desk. On the way they passed one clear, narrow canal after another. Fast boats sped along the water, and brightly colored, old-fashioned European houses with carved decorations lined the streets.

"It really is beautiful here," Misty said, looking from side to side. "No wonder they call it the most beautiful city in the world!"

As a Water-type Trainer, she obviously loved the place.

Ethan saw it differently. Beauty was a matter of taste. Altomare had a character all its own, but plenty of cities in this world impressed him just as much.

Nimbasa City in Unova, Lumiose City in Kalos, and Wyndon in Galar all came to mind.

The race's registration desk was inside the city's arena. After asking for directions, they found it easily.

There, a staff member explained that the water race was a customary part of Altomare's summer festival. Every year it drew crowds, brought the city substantial income, and awarded its winner an Altomare glass winner's medal.

At the mention of a medal, Ash's eyes lit up.

After Ethan, Ash, and Misty registered, Ash grinned at the other two. "Just watch. That medal is mine!"

Misty scoffed. "Don't brag. Ethan will win, and I'll come second. As for you, finishing anywhere but last would be an amazing result!"

"Oh, come on!" Ash groaned. "You never hold back, do you?"

The race was scheduled for nine the next morning. Altomare had been preparing for it for some time; almost every resident and visitor knew a major competition was about to take place.

The group checked into the Pokemon Center. Later they watched a splendid parade of flower-decked boats along the canal. That afternoon Ethan treated everyone to a lavish meal at a local restaurant.

It had become a habit. Almost every time he met Ash and his friends, Ethan bought them a feast.

Over dinner, he learned that Ash had earned seven badges. Once he won the Rising Badge at Blackthorn Gym, he too could register for the Silver Conference.

"Good luck, kid," Ethan said with a chuckle. "The Gym Leader there is Clair. She won't be easy to beat."

They returned to the Pokemon Center as the sun was setting.

Perhaps because he had eaten too much and wanted to work it off, Ash eagerly challenged Ethan to a battle. Ethan agreed.

They went to the battlefield behind the Center. Ethan sent out Eevee again, while Ash chose Pikachu.

The two Pokemon fought for a long time. At last, a panting Pikachu defeated Eevee with Iron Tail.

Ash was delighted. He knew Ethan had not used one of his strongest Pokemon, but beating any of Ethan's team filled him with hope.

Maybejust maybehe could defeat Ethan at this year's Silver Conference?

The thought set his heart pounding. He could hardly calm down.

He had never given up on the idea of beating Ethan!

...

After a night's rest and breakfast the following morning, Ethan and the others put on light shirts and shorts and arrived at the racecourse before the start.

The race would take place on the water. Light clothes would make it easier to move and less troublesome if they got wet.

The organizers provided each competitor with a board and a set of ropes. The racers would stand on the boards, secure themselves, and connect their boards to their Pokemon so they would not be thrown off.

Ethan had temporarily transferred Blastoise over the day before specifically for this race.

With a Pokemon that excelled in both endurance and explosive speed, he was confident he could win.

Erika and Brock were not competing. After cheering the others on, they stood on a nearby bridge, where a large screen showed the race live.

Ethan fitted the ropes around Blastoise and stepped onto his board.

He was in the city's widest canal. About thirty competitors had entered. Like runners at the start of a marathon, they lined up in several rows and waited for the signal.

The course twisted through Altomare's complicated waterways. The start was also the finish: they would loop around the city and return to this spot.

Before the race, Ethan had heard that the favorite was Ross, last year's winner.

Ross's Wailmer was famous for its speed. The previous year, it had led from the front and won by an overwhelming margin.

Ethan happened to stand in the middle of the group. Ross was diagonally ahead of him, while Ash and Misty stood nearby.

Both looked poised and confident, as though the favorite did not concern them at all.

The crowd grew more excited as the start drew close.

Three Natu and a Xatu perched together in a stack like a signal light. They spread their wings one after another. When the Xatu at the bottom gave its cry, the race began!
