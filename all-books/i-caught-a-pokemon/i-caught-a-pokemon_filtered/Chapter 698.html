Chapter 698: Team Magma, Move Out!

The Red Orb gave off a gentle, warm light, tinting the shrine around it a faint red.

Ethan looked around. No wonder Team Aqua had stolen the Blue Orb so easily. There was no security here at all. Nobody had even tried to conceal the light of the orb that remained.

Fortunately, few people even in Hoenn knew the two orbs existed, much less came looking for them. Phoebe's grandparents lived here mainly to stop mischievous wild Pokemon, or the occasional lost visitor who stumbled upon the shrine, from carrying an orb away without understanding it. Their role was as much to keep the tradition and watch over the place as to guard it against thieves.

Ethan drew his gaze away from the Red Orb. Do you have any news about Team Magma?

Phoebe sat on a broken stone railing, swinging her legs. When I drove those people off earlier, I heard them mention Maxie. It sounds as though their leader is coming in person.

Maxie... Ethan stroked his chin.

Unlike Archie, Maxie did not strike him as a particularly charismatic leader, despite commanding a large organization. He could be neurotic and fixate on tiny details. He even had a habit of using numbers in place of ordinary descriptions. Most of the time he appeared cool and composed, but when he pursued a goal he could be merciless. If necessary, he would sacrifice anyone or anything but himself.

Ethan had always found the names of the two leaders amusing. In the language of his previous life, Maxie's name paired a red flame with a pine tree, while Archie's paired water with a tung tree. Fire and water suited bitter enemies. Yet both names ended in trees. Their methods and ideals differed, but perhaps their aims were more alike than either would admit.

For someone whose name evoked fire, Maxie rarely entered a battle himself. He usually sent his subordinates instead. If he had come for the Red Orb personally, word that Team Aqua had taken the Blue Orb must have reached him and put him under enormous pressure.

Phoebe mistook Ethan's silence for fear. Don't worry. I'll handle the commanders. You two just keep the ordinary members off me.

That's quite a promise, Ethan thought. Misty met his eyes, and he could tell she had heard the same confidence in Phoebe's voice. They had come to help. If Phoebe wanted the harder opponents, neither of them had reason to argue.

Maybe this will be easy. Ethan smiled at Misty, then said, All right. Leave the grunts to us.

Phoebe seemed pleased. A moment later, she remembered something. I don't even know your names.

I'm Ethan.

And I'm Misty.

Just as they had expected, Phoebe showed no reaction to Ethan's name. She really had never heard of him. That suited him. Everywhere else he went, people recognized him and treated him differently. Here, for once, Phoebe was the one looking out for him.

After their introductions, Phoebe took charge of assigning tasks. Her plan was simple: they would hold the only entrance and prevent Team Magma from entering the summit.

Ethan considered it, then objected. We'd have the advantage of waiting for them, but we'd also be completely passive. They know where we are, and we don't know when they'll attack. If we stand guard for half a day, we'll be worn out before they even appear.

Even without knowing his strength, Phoebe looked at him with new respect. You're thinking further ahead than I did. What do you suggest?

Ethan surveyed the summit and the three of them. First, send a Pokemon down to scout. We should at least learn what they're doing. Team Magma doesn't know Misty and I are here, which gives us an advantage. We can hide and strike when they show themselves.

Phoebe thought it through. It was a better plan than hers. She had powerful Pokemon and the rank of an Elite Four member, but she was still young; strength in battle did not automatically give her the experience to consider every part of a defense.

Let's do that, she said. But you should know the mountain is complicated, and there are Ghost-type Pokemon everywhere. Anyone who didn't grow up here could get lost or have trouble shaking them off. My Pokemon need to stay ready for Team Magma, so I can't send them all the way down to scout.

Ethan understood. Phoebe could not let her Pokemon stray far from the orb. She needed one of theirs for the reconnaissance, though she plainly doubted how well it would manage.

Don't worry. I have exactly the Pokemon for this. The wild Ghost types won't bother it, and it can scout without anyone noticing.

He clapped his hands. Gengar slowly rose from his shadow.

Mt. Pyre would feel like home to Gengar. Ethan was less concerned that the wild Ghost-type Pokemon would harass it than that it might decide to harass them.

Oh! You have a Ghost-type Pokemon too?

As Hoenn's Ghost-type Elite Four member, Phoebe was naturally fond of them. Some even said she could communicate with Ghost-type Pokemon. Her eyes lit up. She circled Gengar, smiling. You've raised it well. Look how round it is! You must feed it wonderfully.

She reached out to pet it. Gengar slapped her hand away before she could touch it.

Little girl, you can compliment me without calling me round. What's round about me? I'm perfectly slim!

What? It can talk? Phoebe pointed at Gengar, her eyes wide.

You're shouting, kid. Gengar stuck a finger in its ear, then turned to Ethan. You just want me to watch those people, right?

Gengar spent almost all its time in Ethan's shadow, except when Ethan made it leave for certain private moments. It heard the conversations around him and needed no further explanation.

Ethan smiled. As long as they don't discover you, feel free to play a few pranks. Give the coming battle an appetizer.

Got it! Then I'll have some fun!

With a cackling laugh, Gengar sank into the mountainside and disappeared.

When Ethan turned back, Phoebe was still staring in the direction Gengar had gone, her eyes shining. She wiped a trace of drool from her mouth as soon as she noticed him watching.

It's so special! I love it. Would you trade Pokemon with me? I have lots of strong Ghost types. You can choose any of them!

Trade my Gengar? In your dreams.

Absolutely not. Ethan shook his head, his voice cool and firm. Gengar is my partner. I would never trade it. Forget about it.

...

A large number of Team Magma members had gathered in a thick forest at the foot of Mt. Pyre. Their orange-red uniforms stood out sharply against the green trees and undergrowth.

The loose formation suddenly stirred. Courtney, at the rear, rose to see what had happened. Maxie was approaching the front of the group with another commander, Tabitha. Courtney hurried to meet them.

Maxie looked about Archie's age, but had none of Archie's fierce bearing. He wore black half-rim glasses and a neat Team Magma uniform. His long red hair was combed back, and his slim figure gave him a scholarly air. Behind the lenses, though, his eyes were cold. His pale face showed no expression. He looked like a snake waiting to strike, and nobody underestimated him.

Beside him walked Tabitha, a tall, heavy man in a loose Team Magma jacket. His eyes remained narrowed, and a sly smile seemed fixed at the corners of his mouth.

With both of them here, their objective could only be the Red Orb. Ethan had guessed correctly: a spy had told Maxie that Team Aqua had stolen the Blue Orb, and the news had made him anxious. He would not allow Team Aqua to awaken Kyogre first.

It puzzled him that so much time had passed without news of Team Aqua's next move. Still, their theft had revealed where the orbs were kept.

Team Magma had searched for so long, never suspecting that the Red Orb lay on a mountain where people buried their Pokemon. The discovery was surprising, but they were not too late. If they seized it, they could find and awaken Groudon before Team Aqua managed to awaken Kyogre.

Maxie would not permit this operation to fail. He stopped before Courtney. What's the situation?

We've searched the whole mountain below the summit, including the caves, Courtney said with a slight smile. The orb isn't anywhere there. It must be at the top.

Good. Maxie nodded. And the woman?

He meant Phoebe.

As I reported, the Elite Four member drove off several of our probes. She's still on the summit.

Then she must be the guardian. Is she alone? Has anyone else approached the mountain?

She's alone. I placed hidden lookouts all around Mt. Pyre. None of them has seen anyone go up.

That was a lucky accident for Ethan. He had not known Team Magma had surrounded the mountain. By descending from the air straight onto the mountainside, he and Misty had avoided every lookout.

Well done. Maxie surveyed the gathered members, then raised his voice. You know our objective is on this mountain. Once we have it, we'll be one step from realizing our great dream! A child whom people call one of the Elite Four thinks she can stand in our way. Show them that even the Elite Four cannot stop us. Team Magma, move out!
