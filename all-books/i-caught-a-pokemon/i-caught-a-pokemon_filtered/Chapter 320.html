Chapter 320: Chain Reaction

Ethan picked up Larvitar's Poke Ball from the ground.

At first, he had planned to send Gyarados back to Cerise Laboratory so it could rest at Cerise Park.

With Blastoise already there, sending Gyarados too would leave him without a Water-type Pokemon and make his team less complete. But Gyarados was overwhelmingly strong. Even at a League tournament, it would be an MVP contender. Bringing it to a Gym battle or a casual fight was practically unfair.

Rather than let it occupy a place on his team, he could send it back and focus on training his other Pokemon, giving them more chances to battle.

But then he reconsidered. They were about to fight Team Rocket. He could not show the three Legendary birds, and Mahogany Town stood beside a lake. This was precisely the sort of place where he might need Gyarados.

I'll send it back after we clear out the Team Rocket base.

Instead, Ethan transferred Venusaur to Cerise Laboratory. It had not battled much lately and was among the second tier of his Pokemon in strength.

Including Blastoise, two of his Kanto starters were now at the laboratory. Both were mild-tempered, so they could keep each other company and should get along well in Cerise Park.

Six Pokemon still isn't enough to carry...

He shook his head, took out his phone, and messaged Chloe.

Ethan: I sent Venusaur back. Could you look after it for me?

She replied almost instantly.

Chloe: Leave it to me! I'll go to the lab when school lets out at noon and take it into the park!

She ended the message with three image stickers: Mission accepted! Salute! Cute!

That girl...

Her reply had come so quickly that Ethan suspected she had set a special alert for his messages. Otherwise, given her personality, she would hardly have been watching her phone constantly during school.

...

Mahogany Town had an atmosphere of suspicion and fear. It was known as a hiding place for ninjas. In ancient times, the town was said to have been their home. As the ages changed, ninjas had gradually lost their place and vanished into history. Apart from people like Koga, who still studied the ninja arts, they were rarely seen.

Once Ethan and Sabrina left the Blackthorn area, the road grew level and the mountains receded far behind them.

A day or two remained before their agreed meeting time. Before entering Mahogany Town, Ethan called Looker to say that they had arrived early.

When Looker heard that Ethan intended to challenge the Gym first, he hurried to stop him.

"Wait a little," Looker said hesitantly over the phone. "Team Rocket has been established here for who knows how many years. They have eyes everywhere. You're a sensitive target and already one of their greatest enemies. If you show yourself in Mahogany Town now, you'll put them on alert."

One of their greatest enemies? Already?

"The International Police and the Elite Four are ahead of me," Ethan said, surprised. "If Team Rocket wants to go after someone, why would they start with me?"

"It's easy to understand." Looker laughed as he explained, sounding almost pleased at Ethan's misfortune. "If I were Team Rocket, I'd go after you first too. The International Police are an official force, and Elite Four members move around so much that they're difficult to find. You won a championship, but you're still a newcomer without a firm base. Isn't it best to stop you before you grow any stronger?"

That made sense. Still, Team Rocket must have thought highly of him to make him a top enemy.

"All right. I'll stay outside town for now. But..."

Ethan told Looker about fighting Vicious alongside Lance in the Dragon Holy Land.

"What? Lance is in Blackthorn City? And the two of you defeated one of Team Rocket's highest executives?"

Ethan confirmed it. Looker's tone changed at once.

"That complicates things... You and Lance appeared in Blackthorn at the same time. Team Rocket won't believe that's a coincidence. They'll think you're coming for them! Damn it. They've been clearing out the base these last two days. Now I see why: they're preparing to evacuate!"

So it set off a chain reaction...

Before Ethan could speak, Looker continued. "We'll have to change our plan. I'll send you my coordinates. Come meet me immediately!"

Looker hung up and quickly sent his location. Ethan and Sabrina exchanged a look and smiled helplessly. They had no choice but to change their destination.

Following the map, they went around Mahogany Town and reached an unnamed forest outside it. From a rise overlooking the trees, they could see the town on the left and Johto's famous Lake of Rage on the right.

The Lake of Rage was also called Gyarados Lake. So many Gyarados lived there that people dared not approach it lightly; that was how it had earned its name.

As they entered the ordinary-looking forest, Ethan could sense many people watching from concealment. He did not even need Sabrina to warn him. They had to be International Police officers guarding the perimeter.

The two fell silent. After several more minutes among the trees, they reached the coordinates and found an International Police camp.

As before, the officers had set up a temporary command post, with patrols moving through the camp. Perhaps they had already been told to expect Ethan and Sabrina. No one stopped them after checking their identities, and they were allowed straight inside.

Ethan put on his Gengar mask before entering the camp.

Heh. At last, that moment has come again!

The instant he donned it, he felt like a theatrical young hero, his whole bearing changed. The mask no longer hid his identity from Team Rocket; everyone there knew who he was. But he was not about to skip the ritual.

An officer guided them into the largest tent. Inside, Ethan found Looker and the bald commander whose codename was Zero. Seeing Zero reminded Ethan of Danmo, but he looked around and did not see Danmo anywhere.

Zero came over first and offered them both a hand.

"We meet again. First, welcome to the operation. On behalf of the International Police, I sincerely thank you both for all the help you've given us."

"You're too kind." Ethan smiled and shook his hand. The mask muffled his voice until he sounded like another person. "Team Rocket has committed countless crimes. The whole world knows it's an evil organization, and it often comes after me as well. Looker is a good friend of mine, too. For public and personal reasons alike, I couldn't stand aside from an operation like this."
