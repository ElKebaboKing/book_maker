Chapter 223: Melody's Feelings

When Ethan and Sabrina returned to Shamouti Town, Professors Oak and Ivy were arriving at the banquet hall too. They exchanged friendly nods.

The festival was over, and the islanders had returned to their ordinary lives. The crowds had scattered. With Ash and his friends gone as well, the town felt quiet.

Inside the hall, Ivy took a laptop from her bag, opened a document, and showed it to Ethan.

"Have a look. This is what we've gathered from yesterday's and today's investigation. It's only a first draft, but the overall direction is settled. We still need to revise some details."

"Me?" Ethan raised his eyebrows. "It's your research. I'm not sure someone outside the project should be reading it."

"It's fine," Oak said. "We included your thoughts on the butterfly effect without asking first. Strictly speaking, you've contributed to the paper."

Ethan felt embarrassed. The butterfly effect was not his idea. It had been easy enough to mention it in conversation. Seeing it in a research paper made him feel as though he had taken credit for someone else's work.

He could hardly explain the truth without talking about the world he had come from and its history. So he moved closer to the screen.

With both professors watching, he pressed the down arrow and began to read. Within a few lines, he found formulas and technical terms he did not understand. Long blocks of academic prose made his eyes swim.

Oak and Ivy were respected Pokemon researchers. Ethan knew better than to invent an opinion about work he could not follow. Fortunately, they seemed to want to include him, not test him.

He scrolled down, trying to look thoughtful. He found the passage based on his butterfly effect explanation. At the end, after the two professors' names, he saw his own.

"You've put me down as an author? Is that really appropriate?"

"You were directly involved in what happened," Ivy said. "Your account matters. Professor Oak agreed, so don't worry."

Oak nodded. "We only did the thinking afterward. Without you and Ash taking action, we wouldn't be here investigating at all. And you're a field investigator for Professor Cerise's laboratory. Including your name makes sense."

"All right." Ethan sighed softly. Once the paper was published, other researchers would know his name. He did not intend to become a Pokemon scholar, so he expected little practical benefit beyond more attention. But the professors meant well. Arguing further would only dismiss their kindness.

...

After so many delays, it was finally time to leave Shamouti Island. Ethan collected his luggage and went outside with Sabrina. He called Charizard, ready to fly to the island of the next Gym.

The professors still had research to do, so they stayed. Delia joined them to see the travelers off. Melody and Carol came too when they heard Ethan was leaving.

On the steps, Melody watched him with mixed feelings. Their time together had been brief, but she liked him. The moments aboard the collector's airship had stayed with her. He was handsome, and he had three legendary birds besides. Yet once he flew away, she might never see him again.

Her feelings had barely begun to grow, and already she had to let them go. She wished he could stay.

Melody lifted her flute and played a gentle, lonely tune. It drifted over the town. However much she wished otherwise, she could not change his plans.

Ethan did not know what she was thinking. He only noticed that the girl who had been so cheerful earlier seemed subdued at their parting.

He waved farewell and helped Sabrina onto Charizard's back. As he prepared to climb up after her, Melody called out.

"Ethan, wait!"

It was the first time she had used his name without an honorific. He turned. She hurried over and gave him a great hug, surprising the others. Then she stood on tiptoe and kissed his cheek.

Tears shone in her eyes. "Don't read anything into it. It's the customary farewell kiss."

"Melody..." Carol watched her sister from a distance. She had once been the festival maiden herself. She knew perfectly well that there was no such custom.

Melody had made it up. Carol understood her sister's feelings, and her heart sank. After Ethan and Sabrina checked out, Carol had cleaned his room and found used tissues in the wastebasket. She knew the two travelers were close. Melody was younger than Ethan, and he already had someone beside him. Her sister was hoping for something that could not happen.

Ethan, remembering the greeting Melody had given him when they arrived, took her explanation at face value. He returned the hug briefly.

"Take care. Maybe we'll meet again someday."

He said a proper goodbye, let go, and climbed onto Charizard. A light nudge from his feet sent it beating its wings and rising into the sky.

The wind from its wings made the people below shield their eyes and scattered Melody's hair. Only Carol saw the tears running down her sister's cheeks.

For Ethan, Shamouti Island had been one episode in a longer journey. His ambitions lay across a much wider world, where countless adventures still waited.
