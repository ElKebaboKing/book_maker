Chapter 874: Zuki

While Ethan and May talked in the sitting room, the five sisters gathered in their dressing room. They chatted as they removed their makeup and changed clothes.

After showing the guests in, Miki, the eldest, had gone to give instructions in the kitchen. She returned to find the youngest, Sayo, struggling with her costume. Miki smiled, sat behind her, and helped her out of it before attending to her own clothes.

Farther into the room, beyond Naoko, sat Kuni and Zuki. Those two were closest in age, with many shared interests, and were especially close to each other.

Kuni wiped off her lipstick and glanced at Zuki, who was removing her makeup. She seemed to want to say something, but held back.

Zuki noticed. What is it? Why do you keep staring at me?

I should be asking you that, Kuni said quietly. Don't think I missed the look on your face earlier. Is it because of Ethan?

Zuki's eyes shifted. Don't make things up. You know me. I'm always like this.

Oh, come on. Kuni gave a little laugh. I know what you're usually like. When have you ever paid a man any attention? Most of them leave you completely unmoved. Yet whenever you see Ethan, you seem to go out of your way to challenge him.

Zuki stared at her sister. Was that really true?

Thinking back, she had behaved the same way when she first met Ethan. Even now, before anything had happened between them, she felt something she could not put into words.

I have nothing against him. I just

Kuni shook her head, cutting her off. I know what sort of person you are. You're proud, and you're the best Pokemon battler among us. You think women shouldn't lose to men. You dreamed of becoming a Trainer when you were little, but in the end you had to choose the stage. There are some things we can't change, Zuki. We can only accept them.

Zuki fell silent. She had never thought seriously about some of those things before. Hearing Kuni say them aloud struck a nerve.

By then Miki had finished helping Sayo. She had not heard the conversation. Sayo, Naoko, would you two go and keep our guests company?

The two youngest sisters left together. As Miki began removing her own makeup, she looked toward Kuni and Zuki. Don't take too long. We shouldn't keep them waiting.

Kuni and Zuki focused on getting ready. Once Kuni had finished, she went to join Miki. As she passed Zuki, she leaned close to her ear.

Don't overthink it. For now, at least try to be kinder to Ethan.

She patted Zuki's shoulder and left with their eldest sister.

Alone in the dressing room, Zuki stopped what she was doing and looked at herself in the mirror.

People generally agreed that she and Kuni were the most beautiful of the five sisters. Close in age and somewhat alike in appearance, they were both reserved, but their beauty differed. Kuni had a thoughtful, cultured air; Zuki was cooler and more striking. In truth, none of the five lacked beauty.

Zuki studied her reflection and sighed. Kuni was right: she was too proud. For years, no man had managed to catch her interest.

As far as she was concerned, the men she met were nothing special. Yet one after another would come back from some short journey with a few Pokemon and parade them in front of her. It was infuriating. If she had been free to travel and become a Trainer, she was certain they would be no match for her.

But, as Kuni had said, she had chosen to be a performer.

Her gaze traveled from her face down over her figure, and she sighed again. She was no longer particularly young. It was not simply vanity that had kept her alone: of all the men she had met, she had found none worthy of her.

She had met rising politicians, business leaders, renowned authorities on Pokemon, and young actorsplenty of men considered successful.

Only...

For an instant, the mirror seemed to show Ethan in place of her reflection.

She had to admit that his appearance suited her tastes. He was still a man, of course, but he did not repel her. Quite the opposite: she liked him.

Unlike the other Trainers she had met, Ethan had achievements she could respect. He had won the League tournaments in Kanto, Johto, and Hoenn. Few could do that. In fact, no one had managed it since the Leagues began. And as he continued traveling, he would surely earn still more honors.

He shone so brightly that she felt both jealousy and envy.

She envied his accomplishments, the freedom he had as a man, and the strength of his Pokemon. She even envied the woman named Erika who had traveled beside him.

Why couldn't she be the one at Ethan's side?

That was exactly what Zuki thought. She was convinced that only someone as exceptional as Ethan was worthy of her.

She did not believe herself less beautiful than Erika. She was taller, and her bearing was every bit as fine. Was the only difference that Erika had met him first?

Deep down, Zuki knew why she kept picking fights with Ethan. She was too proud and stubborn to admit it, even though there were better ways: she wanted him to notice her.

So far, it did not seem to have worked.

After staring into the mirror for a long while, Zuki shook her head helplessly. Her eyes dropped to the drawer in her dressing table. She frowned and pulled it open.

Among her usual perfumes was a small pink bottle. She moved the others aside and took it out.

The bottle held something unusual. Years ago, when she and Kuni had first begun performing, she had acquired it to make herself more alluring. It was not perfume, but a liquid containing an aphrodisiac. Mixed in a small amount with perfume, it was supposed to make men's hearts race and draw their attention to her.

She had been short of confidence before her debut, and Kuni had suggested the trick. Zuki had used it only once. It worked as promised, but the men's leering looks had disgusted her so much that she hid it away and never touched it again.

Holding the bottle, her eyes flickered.

Perhaps now...

...

In the sitting room, Ethan and May watched the two Eevee chase each other. They had barely taken a couple of sips of tea when Gengar emerged from Ethan's shadow.

It yawned, glanced around, and looked surprised. This place seems familiar. Haven't we been here before?

Of course, Ethan said with a smile. It's the Kimono Sisters' home. We were guests here once. Don't you remember?

Oh, this place! Gengar nodded. Since it had been here before, it saw no reason to stay put. It told Ethan it was going to explore the old house.

Be careful, Ethan called after it. Don't cause trouble!

I know, I know. Stop nagging. Gengar rolled its eyes.

It turned and almost collided with Naoko and Sayo, who were entering with plates of tea cakes. Letting out a wicked cackle, it pulled a frightening face at them. Both girls recoiled in alarm, and Gengar went off laughing.

That Gengar... Ethan shook his head. There's no stopping it.

Naoko and Sayo set down the plates and sat opposite Ethan and May. They had changed into everyday clothes. Ethan smiled and nodded to them.

Only a little over a year separated the two younger sisters. Both were in their teens and slightly older than May. Sayo, though the youngest, had a composed manner and eyes much like Miki's. Ethan thought their personalities might be alike too.

Naoko's features were a little more mature, but a trace of sadness lingered in her expression. Her timid, darting gaze made her seem especially vulnerable. Both were already lovely, and Ethan imagined they would grow into beauties like their older sisters.

Naoko bowed lightly. Mr. Vale, Miss May, please wait a little longer. Our sisters will be out soon.

You're both so pretty, May said without thinking.

Close up, she found them even more striking without their stage makeup than they had been in costume.

Naoko and Sayo smiled. You're the pretty one, Miss May.

Ethan watched the three exchange courtesies and shook his head. Come on, you're friends and close in age. You don't need to be so formal with one another.

The three looked at each other and laughed. The mood grew easier, and they began chatting.

May told them stories from her travels and explained that she was a Pokemon Coordinator working toward the Grand Festival. Naoko and Sayo listened with envy. They offered to give her advice on her stage performances.

May was thrilled. They were not much older than she was, but they were already professional performers with a popular show. Their advice might give her new ideas and a fresh style.

I'd love that. Thank you!

She took them by the hands and led them out to the lawn. Eager to begin, she released her other Pokemon and practiced under the sisters' eyes. Naoko and Sayo lived up to their offer and gave her several pointers.

Ethan leaned against the low table with his cheek in his hand. He was happy for May. Here, time itself seemed to slow.

How peaceful.

When he retired, he would like to find a place like this, drink tea, admire flowers, and watch the clouds drift by.

Footsteps approached. Miki, Kuni, and Zuki appeared along the covered walkway. They stopped outside the sitting room to watch May practice. Only after some time did they come in and kneel on the tatami across the low table from Ethan.

If I'm not mistaken, Miki said, Miss May is a Coordinator?

A good eye, Ethan replied. She is. She's traveling with me so she can enter Pokemon Contests in different places.

I thought so. She has a very steady stage presence. I'm sure she'll do well.

Kuni refilled Ethan's tea. I imagine this visit has something to do with Eevee?

Miki and Kuni were observant. They had even guessed his reason for coming. Then again, it showed how well they knew his Eevee.

That's right. Eevee reached the point where it could evolve a while ago. It shows signs of changing at any time, but since we haven't decided what it should become, it's been holding back.

I thought as much. Miki and Kuni looked toward Eevee, then turned back to him. What do you think would be best for its evolution, Mr. Vale? We said before that Eevee is your Pokemon now. Doesn't that mean your opinion should carry the most weight?
