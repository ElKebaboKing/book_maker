Chapter 563: Oak's Pokemon Are Impressive

The second round between Ash and Gary began.

Heracross attacked first. It landed several moves on Magmar in succession, then used its great strength to knock Magmar into the air, taking an early advantage.

Magmar answered with a startling maneuver. While airborne, it used Fire Blast, then plunged into the flames of its own attack. Like a blazing fireball, it hurtled down at Heracross.

The impact drove Heracross backward until it struck a huge boulder. Gary gave another command, and from point-blank range Magmar hit Heracross with Flamethrower.

Flames swallowed Heracross. Its silhouette was barely visible inside them.

Just when everyone thought it had lost, Heracross beat its wings and raised a powerful gust. The wind blew out the flames around it in an instant.

That gave Ethan an idea. Watching other Trainers battle could always teach him something new.

With the fire extinguished, Heracross launched its counterattack. It charged through Magmar's Flamethrower and struck with Megahorn, one of the strongest Bug-type moves, knocking Magmar out.

"Ash really is a master of fighting at a type disadvantage," Ethan said, watching the wounded, panting Heracross on the screen. "It hung on through sheer determination, ignored the matchup, and turned the battle around. I have to admire that."

Hearing the envy in his voice, Erika smiled. "Some things can't be learned. That's his talent."

With the score tied at one apiece, Ash and Gary moved into the third round.

Perhaps hoping to take control of the match, Gary sent out Blastoise. It was his first Pokemon and his strongest. Bringing it out now was surely meant to establish a lead and widen it.

Ash kept Heracross on the field against Blastoise. But when Blastoise fired Hydro Pump, determination alone could not save the exhausted Heracross. It went down easily.

Two to one. Blastoise had put Gary ahead as soon as it entered.

Ash's third choice was Muk.

The two Pokemon traded attacks. Muk even found an opening and enveloped Blastoise completely in its soft, mudlike body. But Blastoise kept firing Hydro Pump from within. As water built up inside Muk, it could no longer hold on and was forced to release its opponent.

Ash made a quick decision. Rather than let Muk continue fighting, he recalled it.

"What a match!" the announcer cried. "Both Trainers are showing everything they've got. Gary's Blastoise has been trained evenly across the board, with no obvious weakness. What Pokemon will Ash choose to face it next?"

Ash considered for a moment and sent out Bayleef.

Ethan was surprised. He had expected Ash to bring out Pikachu for a battle between their two aces, but Ash clearly had another plan.

Then again, if Ash thought the way Ethan did, he would not so often fight at a type disadvantage and create so many memorable moments.

Pikachu was not even beside Ash. It sat with Misty, Brock, Togepi, and the others, cheering him on. It seemed Ash had no intention of using it in this match.

Grass-type Bayleef had a clear advantage over Water-type Blastoise. But that was the sort of advantage other Trainers could rely on. When Ash used the favorable type, he seemed to lose nine times out of ten.

He really was that unpredictable.

At first, Bayleef and Blastoise looked evenly matched. Then Blastoise found an opening. It bit down on Bayleef, swung it around, and hurled it out of the field. Bayleef struck the wall beneath the stands and fell unconscious.

"Incredible!" the announcer shouted. "Gary's Blastoise has taken down three of Ash's Pokemon in a row! Who can stop its winning streak?"

Gary still had five Pokemon available. Ash had only three left.

Back in the lobby, Erika turned to Ethan. "Gary's Blastoise really is strong. I battled it before, and I still remember it. Ash is in trouble now. What if Gary wins?"

"Have faith in him." Ethan was not especially worried. The battle appeared to be turning Gary's way, but Ethan held to his earlier judgment. "It isn't over. Either one could still win, and I believe Ash has the strength and luck to come back."

After a five-minute break, the match resumed.

Ash opened the second half with Snorlax. Gary gave Blastoise a rest and sent out Arcanine.

Arcanine was another of Gary's signature Pokemon. He had trained it exceptionally well, and it was not to be underestimated.

But Ash's Snorlax was powerful too. Arcanine trapped it in Fire Spin at the start of their clash. Snorlax answered with a Hyper Beam that knocked Arcanine out in one blow.

Gary, the announcer, and the spectators were all stunned.

The crowd erupted. After losing three Pokemon, Ash had begun the second half by taking a round back with Snorlax's overwhelming strength.

Gary's expression did not change. He recalled Arcanine and, instead of returning Blastoise to the field, sent out Nidoqueen, which had performed so well against Tauros in the opening round.

Ethan understood the choice. Gary was giving Blastoise time to recover.

Gary knew Ash would not be easy to beat. He intended to save Blastoise for their final confrontation.

But he had underestimated Snorlax. Nidoqueen fought well under Gary's direction, yet before long Snorlax found its chance. It dodged Nidoqueen's Hyper Beam with unexpected agility and knocked it out with a single Ice Punch.

"S-so strong!"

The announcer could scarcely believe that the heavy, placid-looking Snorlax had such ability.

Applause burst from the stands. Even Erika praised it. "Ash has raised that Snorlax remarkably well. It has strength and agility. I wonder how he trained it."

Ethan laughed. "Rather than Ash, maybe you should credit Professor Oak for raising it so well!"

"What?" Erika blinked.

"Didn't you know?" Ethan smiled. "Ash leaves the Pokemon he's caught at Professor Oak's Laboratory. Oak looks after them and helps train them. Compared with other Trainers who leave Pokemon there, he clearly pays Ash's partners more attention. Even his own grandson Gary doesn't get that treatment!"
