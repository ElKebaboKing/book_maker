Chapter 653: Before the Typhoon

Ethan had no idea what Shauna was thinking. If he had known, he would probably have shaken his head at how little of the world she had seen.

That was one benefit of traveling: it kept a person's view from being limited to a single place.

To the people of Dewford Town, Shauna certainly had something to boast about. But however famous Brawly was on the island, his reputation barely reached beyond it. Across Hoenn, he was one Gym Leader among many. He couldn't compare with an Elite Four member or a Champion, and other Gym Leaders were just as admired in their own hometowns.

Ethan had won the Leagues of two regions, while Bea was a Gym Leader herself. Neither was lesser than Brawly. Why would his local fame impress them? Shauna was only claiming to be his student and girlfriend.

Ethan stepped to the place where Bea had stood, facing Shauna across the field.

After its battle with Pangoro, Shauna's Meditite clearly couldn't fight again. Perhaps she underestimated Ethan, or perhaps she had no stronger Pokemon available. Either way, she sent out a Machop.

It wasn't that Ethan looked down on Machop. Since his time in Kanto, he had fought so many Machop and their evolved forms that he knew their moves and abilities well. They no longer felt particularly difficult to face.

Shauna knew none of that. My rules are simple. Beat me in a one-on-one battle, and you'll be allowed to challenge Brawly in an official Gym match tomorrow.

She stressed Brawly's name, hoping Ethan would realize how hard it was to earn an audience with the Gym Leader. He remained impassive, apparently missing the point altogether.

What a blockhead.

Understood. Ethan took out a Poke Ball. Since he had come to a Fighting-type Gym, he felt like following its custom. As it happened, he had a Fighting-type Pokemon of his own.

Come out, Combusken!

A blue flash released Combusken opposite Machop. Neither Pokemon stood a meter tall, and they stared at one another as though sparks were passing between them.

Oh, Combusken? Shauna put her hands on her hips. What an ordinary Pokemon.

Ethan glanced at her Machop. She was calling his Pokemon ordinary? Combusken lived mainly in Hoenn, with only a few elsewhere. Machop could be found in practically every region.

He couldn't be bothered to argue, so he ignored the remark. Shauna's smile froze awkwardly. She let it pass and focused on the field.

As acting Gym Leader, she would referee her own battle as well as compete. Once Combusken was ready, she collected herself and called, Begin!

At once she sent Machop charging straight toward Combusken.

Ethan didn't use a Fire-type move to wear it down from a distance. Combusken, Bulk Up!

Bulk Up was a Fighting-type move that tightened the user's muscles, raising its Attack and Defense. Ethan's choice made his intentions clear: like Bea, he wanted to settle this at close range with Fighting-type moves.

Combusken raised its arms and struck a bodybuilder's pose. Its muscles seemed to tense all at once.

Shauna understood what he was doing and laughed. Come on, then! That's just what I want. Let's really go at it!

Really go at it? She probably meant nothing suggestive, but was that how she wanted to phrase it?

Even from their brief meeting, Ethan could tell Shauna was rather like Ash: open and impulsive, with just a little cunning. Every feeling showed on her face. Fighting someone like that was both exhausting and easy.

While Combusken used Bulk Up, Machop crossed half the field. It leaped and kicked toward Combusken's head.

Ethan had watched it closely. As Machop raised its leg, he read the attack and ordered Combusken to meet it with Double Kick.

Bang!

Their feet collided in midair, apparently evenly matched. Before Machop could fall, Combusken spun and raised its other leg, driving the second kick into Machop's lower stomach.

Machop had stopped the first kick, but the second caught it badly. Strengthened by Bulk Up, the blow sent it stumbling several steps back before it could regain its balance.

Be careful! Shauna called. Don't trade blows with it yet. Find a way to get close!

Machop charged again. Having learned from the first exchange, it kept its movements compact until it reached Combusken, then picked its moment to strike.

It feinted, lifted its right hand high, and brought it down sharply. Karate Chop looked like a simple strike, but it carried considerable force.

To Shauna's surprise, Ethan gave his command almost the instant Machop moved, as though he had known what was coming. Combusken raised both arms, blocked the chop, and kicked Machop in the lower stomach again.

What?!

Hit in the same place twice, Machop could no longer hide the pain. It clutched its stomach and staggered back several more steps.

Shauna stared at Ethan. How did you know what move I was going to use?

What's so strange about it? he asked. If you'd battled as many of the same Pokemon as I have, you'd have a fair idea of what they can do and how they fight. Your Machop isn't especially strong, and it hasn't shown any unusual moves. It's fairly easy to predict.

He was trying to explain that experience against Machop had helped him anticipate its attacks. Shauna, simple-minded in much the same way as Ash, missed the point. All she heard was that he'd called her Pokemon weak.

She would teach him a lesson! Shauna ordered Machop to attack without holding anything back.

She did, after all, have the nerve to stand in for a Gym Leader. When she pushed Machop harder, Combusken found it difficult to defend. The two Pokemon exchanged more than a dozen blows. Machop took hits, but it gritted its teeth and refused to retreat. Neglecting its own defense, it landed several punches and kicks on Combusken too.

A wild barrage could trouble even an experienced fighter. For the moment, Ethan couldn't shake Machop off and had to keep Combusken trading blows with it.

But Shauna's approach carried a cost. Combusken's Bulk Up had made it tougher and increased the force of its attacks. Meeting it head-on while ignoring defense was a poor choice, however fierce Machop looked.

At first Ethan wondered if Shauna had a plan or a last-minute move that would turn the match around. As the fight went on, he realized he had given her too much credit. Like Ash when Ethan had first met him, her strategy was simply to attack, attack, and keep attacking.

That style had its uses, but this was not the time for it.

Combusken used Double Kick again, catching Machop in the chest. Already battered, Machop could take no more. It toppled backward and hit the floor unconscious.

Yes! Misty sprang up and applauded before Shauna could announce the result. She made no effort to hide her delight.

She had been irritated with Shauna for a while. The woman's opinions of herself and Brawly were so self-important that Misty had wanted to argue with her. She had held back only because Ethan was in the middle of a battle. Now that he had won, she saw no point in a debate and merely raised her eyebrows at Shauna.

You win, Shauna said dejectedly, ignoring Misty's triumphant look. Come back tomorrow morning and you can challenge Brawly.

Before and after the battle, she seemed like two different people. Combusken's last kick had knocked down her pride along with Machop.

Pokemon battles came down to strength. A Trainer whose Pokemon was stronger had reason to feel confident. Shauna had now suffered two defeats in a row, decisively losing to Bea and Ethan. It had plainly affected her.

Ethan didn't know her well, and he had achieved what he came for. He and Misty exchanged a glance, said goodbye, and left the Gym.

They hadn't met Brawly, but Ethan had at least earned the right to challenge him. He wouldn't need another qualifying battle tomorrow.

The sky was growing darker. Outside the Gym grounds, once she was sure Shauna couldn't hear them, Misty complained, I've never seen a Gym where you have to qualify just to challenge the Leader! What a big deal they make of him. You'd think we were here to face an Elite Four member!

Ethan smiled, then shook his head. Who knows? It must be a local custom. Brawly's a surfer as well as a Gym Leader, and he probably can't spend all his time here. Maybe the preliminary battle is how they screen challengers.

They passed back through the distinctive gate and started down the gentle path from the cliff. Before they reached town, they saw a bank of heavy clouds stretching along the distant horizon and drifting toward the island.

Looks like a downpour is coming. Let's hurry to the Pokemon Center.

Unlike its counterpart in the games, Dewford Town in this world was a busy and important Hoenn town. It had no forest of skyscrapers, but many tall buildings rose over lively streets.

A sudden gust swept through. The endless bank of clouds seemed closer already.

They quickened their pace to avoid the rain and reached the Pokemon Center before nightfall. After checking in, they sat in the dining area to wait for dinner. Around them, other Trainers were beginning to talk about a typhoon and heavy rain.

Outside the window, rows of palm trees lining Dewford's streets swayed. Their broad leaves shook in the wind, silently bearing the force of the approaching weather.

Misty listened for a while, then lowered her voice. From what they're saying, that cloud bank we saw is part of a typhoon.

Ethan had heard them too. He nodded. That's not unusual for an island town. The weather can turn extreme without much warning.

Will it affect tomorrow's Gym battle?

He looked outside again. The Pokemon Center stood high enough for him to see flashes of lightning within the clouds. At their present distance and speed, the typhoon might not make landfall until well after midnight.

He sighed. I hope not, but I can't say. If it does, there's nothing we can do. Even after the Gym battle, we may have to wait for the storm to pass before leaving. No boat can sail in a typhoon and its heavy rain.

So we might be stuck here. Misty stuck out her tongue, but she didn't seem worried.

A seaside Pokemon Center would surely have been built to withstand storms. At worst, they would stay a few extra days. What was so bad about that?
