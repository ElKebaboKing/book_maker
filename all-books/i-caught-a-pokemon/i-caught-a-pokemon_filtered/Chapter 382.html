Chapter 382: You Two Look Like a Couple

The four of them moved to a gravelly patch beside the road, took positions on opposite sides, and prepared to battle.

Before they began, however, Ethan could not suppress his curiosity. "How did you recognize me just now? Were you waiting here specifically for me, or did you happen to see me and recognize me immediately?"

The corner of Frieda's mouth lifted. "After you defeated our older brother and sister, they sent us your photograph. We also looked up your information while trying to find you, which was how we learned that you're the latest Indigo League Conference Champion! Our being here is only a coincidence, but we watched several of your matches, so we recognized you the moment you appeared!"

So that was how it happened.

Ethan nodded. Since they had deliberately researched him, these two probably knew a fair amount about him.

Arthur pushed up his glasses again and calmly added, "Besides that, our sister told us about you. She said you were exceptionally strong and warned us never to underestimate you. She also mentioned that you traveled with a cool-looking woman who had long, straight black hair, which made you very easy to recognize..."

Only after saying this did he seem to notice something. His body suddenly went rigid.

He looked at Erika, studied her shoulder-length hair, and then looked at the Hoppip following beside her. He rubbed his eyes in disbelief and checked two or three more times. Once certain that he had not made a mistake, he blurted out, "Huh? You have a different woman now?"

Dark lines seemed to cover Ethan's face, and even Erika could not avoid feeling awkward.

This was already the second time someone had misunderstood her relationship with Ethan. She could not help touching her face. Do we really look that much like a married couple?

"Ahem." Ethan coughed and introduced them. "This is Erika, my traveling companion. She isn't 'my woman.'"

Arthur pushed up his glasses yet again. His gaze moved constantly between Ethan and Erika, clearly trying to analyze every possible meaning of the word companion.

Hm? They're definitely not partners? Merely companions?

His suspicion was understandable. He had just watched Ethan and Erika approach from the distance, talking and laughing with radiant smiles on their faces. Anyone who saw such a scene would probably assume that their relationship was far from ordinary.

Fortunately, Frieda was clearly just as unable to tolerate her brother's tactless personality. She also had no interest in other people's love lives, so she gave a light cough and drew the conversation back on track.

"All right, let's return to the matter at hand and decide the rules of this battle!"

Ethan breathed a quiet sigh of relief when Frieda changed the subject.

He was also afraid that Arthur might continue spouting nonsense and upset Erika.

Before answering, he first looked around at their surroundings.

They were outside the city on its outskirts, where the infrastructure was limited, so there was naturally no dedicated Pokemon battlefield. Beneath their feet lay ordinary ground that was open but scattered with stones and weeds.

They could call it a gravel field for the time being.

But having a proper battlefield made no real difference. This was not an official competition, so there was no need to care about such details.

After a moment's thought, Ethan said, "Since there are two of you, I'll have one battle against each of you. Each battle will be decided in a single round. How does that sound?"

"No problem." Frieda continued, "What about the rules for sending out our Pokemon?"

Without hesitation, Ethan said, "We'll send them out simultaneously, of course."

Frieda nodded. In a battle in the wild, this was the most common arrangement. Since neither side moved first or second, no one could deliberately choose a favorable type matchup, making the battle as fair as possible.

After all, they had watched Ethan's matches and knew that the man before them liked to exploit type advantages to secure victory.

Erika stepped back several paces to clear the field for Ethan and raised a small fist toward him.

"Good luck!"

Ethan returned her smile, then turned toward Arthur and Frieda. The brother and sister exchanged a glance before Frieda stepped forward first.

Looking at her, Ethan recalled the Pokemon Tuscany and Wesley had used at Ice Path, both of which had left a deep impression on him.

Tuscany had sent out a Lotad, while Wesley had used a Forretress. Ethan wondered what Pokemon the fourth and fifth Week Siblings would send out against him this time.

Serving as the temporary referee, Arthur raised his right hand high.

"Both sides, please send out your Pokemon at the same time!"

Ethan and Frieda each took out a Poke Ball and released the Pokemon inside.

Two streaks of blue light flashed. Larvitar appeared before Ethan, while across from it emerged a completely red Pokemon that resembled a lava snail.

Oh? A Slugma...

Ethan recognized it at a glance. It was the Fire-type Pokemon Slugma.

Slugma's entire body was red and composed of magma. It had a pair of yellow eyes and two flame-shaped feelers atop its head, while two teardrop-shaped structures hung beside its mouth.

Since this was the first time Ethan had seen a Slugma in person, he took out his Pokedex and pointed it toward the Pokemon.

The Pokedex beeped. "Slugma, the Lava Pokemon. A Fire type. Areas where Slugma gather generally have higher ground temperatures, and large numbers of them can be found in volcanic regions. They crawl slowly from place to place in search of warmth. It is said that scorching magma circulates through their bodies in place of blood, carrying the nutrients and oxygen they need. Slugma remain in constant motion without sleeping or resting, because if their bodies cool, their skin hardens and leaves them unable to move. For that reason, they sleep near magma."

Its body can cool?

Ethan put away the Pokedex and could not help marveling at what an unusual Pokemon it was.

In terms of type, however, the Rock and Ground-type Larvitar held an overwhelming advantage against the Fire-type Slugma. Both Ground and Rock-type moves would deal twice the damage to Slugma, while Fire-type moves would deal only half damage to Larvitar.

Faced with this matchup, Frieda's expression turned bitter. They had sent out their Pokemon simultaneously, yet the result had still turned out like this!

Seeing her expression, Ethan smiled lightly and said without concern, "Would you like me to switch to another Pokemon? I don't mind at all."

"No!" Feeling that Ethan had looked down on her, Frieda firmly shook her head. "Even with a type disadvantage, I'll still defeat you!"
