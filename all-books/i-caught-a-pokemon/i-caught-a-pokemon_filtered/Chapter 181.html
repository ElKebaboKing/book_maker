Chapter 181: Arriving in the Orange Islands

The Orange Islands lay farther south than the Sevii Islands. No one could say how many years ago the archipelago had taken its name from the sweet, juicy citrus fruit grown there.

Its tropical climate and beautiful scenery made it ideal for surfing, snorkeling, and other water sports. Travelers came from far away to holiday there every day.

The distance from other regions had also given its Pokemon a distinctive southern character. Even the local Officer Jennys and Nurse Joys looked more tanned and fit than their sisters elsewhere, thanks to the abundant sunshine.

Charizard folded its wings and landed on one of the archipelago's busy islands. Ethan jumped down onto the beach, then turned and helped Sabrina off its back.

They had flown for so long in one position that their bodies were stiff. At last they could stretch their legs.

Sabrina looked around. Shallow, clear water lay before them and a thick grove of coconut palms behind. The place was lovely, but there was no one in sight.

"Where are we?"

"This should be Mikan Island," Ethan said, patting Charizard's head. "Charizard followed the navigation on my phone. If it's right, there's a lively town beyond those palms, and an official Orange League Gym on the other side of the island."

Sabrina knew little about the Orange Leagueor most leagues outside the Indigo League. "I didn't expect a handful of small islands to have a Pokemon League of their own."

"It works differently from the leagues in Kanto, Johto, Hoenn, and the other regions," Ethan said. "It isn't an international conference run by the Pokemon League. As I understand it, a group called the Orange Crew organizes it. The islands don't have a Champion in the usual sense. Earn four Badges and defeat their Supreme Gym Leader, and you become an Honorable Trainer."

"Orange Crew? Supreme Gym Leader? Those are grand titles. Are they strong?"

Ethan shrugged. "The average local trainer may be a little weaker than one from Kanto, but the Gym Leaders and their Supreme Gym Leader are good. If they entered the Indigo League, I'd expect them to reach at least the top thirty-two."

Top thirty-two was enough to impress him? Sabrina gave him a puzzled look. His tone was perfectly serious, so she could not tell whether he was praising the Indigo League or slighting the Orange League.

At least she now had a rough idea of how it worked. "You know quite a lot about it."

Ethan laughed and waved his phone. "Thanks. I looked most of that up just now. It pays to know what you're walking into."

Show-off.

With no one nearby, they decided to rest before going into town. Sabrina took out their equipment and, with some effort, heated a kettle of water. Ethan opened his Poke Balls and let out all his Pokemon, including the three legendary birds.

The quiet beach came alive. Moltres and Articuno spread their wings, and Sabrina's eyes went straight to them.

"Those flames are beautiful. And those wings!"

She had seen Ethan's Zapdos and knew he had just caught Moltres. The surprise was Articuno. When had he caught that one?

"I can't believe you found all three."

Ethan looked at the birds with satisfaction. Their different colors and powers made them a rare sight together.

"None of them is invincible on its own. Finding them was harder than catching them. I was lucky enough to know where they lived."

He paused, feeling he had made them sound too ordinary. "Don't underestimate them, though. Moltres may look tame now, but Zapdos and Articuno had to fight together to wear it down."

Sabrina followed his lead. "What happened up there?"

Ethan launched into the story with such enthusiasm that a little spit landed on her face. "Not long after we started, Moltres breathed fire until the whole valley was burning. The heat was terrifying. I had Zapdos use Thunder and Articuno use Blizzard. The rain and snow put out the flames and soaked Moltres. Without that, we couldn't have kept it under control."

His description gave Sabrina a clearer picture of the storm she and Lorelei had seen from the mountainside.

"And then you made the volcano erupt?"

Ethan had no answer. Sabrina smiled at the rare sight of him speechless and poured him a cup of hot water.

"Don't look so glum. Whatever happened during the battle, you caught Moltres in the end."

Ethan accepted the cup with both hands and smiled again. "True. As far as I know, apart from someone who briefly had all three, no one has managed to collect them like this."

The birds moved apart, each settling in its own corner of the beach. They shared a trainer and showed no real hostility, but their pride kept them from resting together.

Ethan scratched his head. Moltres and Articuno were roughly equal in strength, though Articuno's type put it at a disadvantage against Moltres. Zapdos was the youngest and the weakest of the three. But it hated losing. Since its earlier battle with Articuno, it had worked hard and grown much stronger. It had been crucial in the fight against Moltres.

Now Ethan had to find a way for the three to live together peacefullyand, someday, to fight as a team whose combined strength exceeded what any of them could manage alone.
