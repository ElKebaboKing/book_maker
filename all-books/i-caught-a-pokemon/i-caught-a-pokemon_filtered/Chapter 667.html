Chapter 667: Mega Red Gyarados!

Team Aqua had been close to collapse. Seeing Rayquaza and Deoxys tire, Shelly gritted her teeth and ordered an all-out attack.

She could no longer afford caution. She could retreat in disgrace, as Team Aqua had before, and accept another defeat. Or she could stake everything on one last attempt. Withdrawing now would waste all they had done, leave them with heavy losses, and crush their morale. Continuing might fail toobut after a few tense seconds, Shelly made her choice.

Gather everyone we have left! Ignore Rayquaza. Target that powerful Pokemonattack!

At her urging, the remaining Team Aqua members regrouped and launched a final assault on Deoxys.

The shift caught the attention of Ethan and Team Magma. Ethan had the best view from his peak, and little on the battlefield escaped him. Shelly's choice did not surprise him. He would have been reluctant to walk away empty-handed too.

What would Team Magma do?

Though they were far apart, Ethan could almost see Courtney's hesitation. In the middle of Team Magma's gathered force, the elite grunts turned to her.

What do we do now, Commander Courtney? one asked.

Courtney considered her options. With all Ethan's Pokemon back at his side and Team Aqua no longer drawing his attention, his position was stronger than ever. Raikou, Suicune, Moltres, Zapdos, Articunoeach was difficult enough to face, even without the rest of his team. Together they were beyond what Team Magma's remaining force could defeat. Capturing Ethan was no longer possible.

She had to change her plan. Her eyes moved to Deoxys. Shelly's decision made sense. In a situation they could no longer control, they should seize whatever prize they still could.

Change targets. Forget the man. We're going after Deoxys too!

Are we competing with Team Aqua for it? an elite grunt asked.

Yes. We can't stand by and let them have it. Even if we can't catch Deoxys ourselves, we won't let Team Aqua succeed!

Understood!

Courtney gathered her people and led them toward Deoxys and Team Aqua. The attack on her flank infuriated Shelly.

That wretched woman! Shelly had thought Courtney had finally shown some sense by going after the most dangerous human opponent. Now she was coming after Team Aqua again. Shelly should have known better than to trust anyone from Team Magma.

Her force wavered, but the move was not wholly unexpected. The organizations were sworn enemies. A shared opponent could never make either one stop watching the other. Shelly split off some members to hold back Team Magma, then urged the rest to attack Deoxys and Rayquaza harder.

The melee reached its peak. The earlier battle lines had vanished. Deoxys, Rayquaza, Team Aqua, and Team Magma were all fighting in the same space. Zinnia, near its center, found enemies on every side and was soon in serious danger.

For the moment, Ethan's position was untouched.

Ethan... Misty was growing anxious. They're all going after Deoxys. Could one of them catch it?

Her fear was reasonable. Team Aqua alone had kept Deoxys occupied, and now it faced Team Magma and Rayquaza too.

Ethan rubbed his chin. If I stood aside, one of them would certainly get it. But why would I stand aside?

He looked to either side. Gengar and Gardevoir would stay with him as guards. He waved the rest of his Pokemon forward into the battle.

Keep fighting. The more confused it gets, the easier it will be for me to take what I want.

Ethan's Pokemon plunged into the melee like tigers among sheep. The fight had entered its final stage. Apart from Ethan and Misty, everyone was caught up in it now.

After so much fighting, nearly every Pokemon on the field was wounded and exhausted. A fresh, powerful Pokemon appearing at this moment could change the balance decisively. Team Magma, Team Aqua, and Zinnia seemed to have no such reserve.

Ethan walked a few steps to the edge of the peak. A light breeze stirred his bangs. From here he could see the whole battlefield.

High above, Rayquaza and Deoxys were the two most conspicuous fighters. Yet each had to face the other while watching for attacks from below. Their power drew much of the other Pokemon's fire.

On the ground, Team Magma and Team Aqua fought across the valley, red uniforms clashing with blue. At this distance Ethan could not make out individuals, but the colors marked each side clearly. Nowhere in the valley was peaceful.

What a sight, he murmured. Misty could not tell whether he sounded impressed or regretful.

She sensed there was more behind his words and came to stand beside him. Do you still have a way to turn this around?

I don't know whether it'll settle everything, Ethan said. But I can promise it will surprise the people and Pokemon down there.

A surprise? Misty frowned. She had stood beside him when he exchanged his Pokemon before departure. Every one of them was already on the field. What else could he have?

Before she could ask, Ethan looked down and exposed his left wrist. A band was fastened there. Misty immediately noticed the bright, rounded stone set into it.

Isn't that the Key Stone we found at Meteor Falls?

She was about to ask when he had made the wristband and why he wore the stone there. Ethan smiled.

It's time...

What? His voice had dropped almost to a whisper, and she had not caught it.

Ethan glanced at her and laughed. It's time they saw just how savage things can get!

He thrust his arm into the air. The Key Stone on his wrist began to shine.

Before Misty understood what he was doing, a brilliant light flashed beneath the lake at the edge of the battlefield. It shone through the water for long enough to draw everyone's attention. Rayquaza, Deoxys, Zinnia, Team Magma, and Team Aqua were all startled.

The glow suddenly vanished. The lake exploded upward, and a gigantic red shape broke the surface.

Only then did Misty remember Ethan's red Gyarados. After it had driven Deoxys out of the lake, it had disappeared below the water. The battles that followed had completely pushed it from her mind.

Ethan had kept a formidable Pokemon hidden all this time. But as the water streamed off it, Misty saw that its shape had changed.

It was no longer than before, yet it looked far heavier and much thicker. Its skin was darker and more solid, as though covered by armor. The horn on its forehead was larger and harder, and its two whiskers had grown very long. The gray structures along its back had become one enormous dorsal fin. It looked fiercer than ever.

What happened to it? Misty's eyes widened. She looked from the changed Gyarados to the stone on Ethan's wrist. Wait... Could it be...?

That's right. Ethan smiled. Mega Evolution. Gyarados just became Mega Gyarados.

Mega red Gyarados! Misty's heart raced. Ethan had told her of this temporary evolution, discovered and widely used in Kalos. It could greatly increase a Pokemon's strength in battle. But how had he managed to use a power from another region?

Ethan read the question on her face. Mega Evolution takes two things. The Trainer needs a Key Stone that resonates with the matching Mega Stone held by the Pokemon. And the Trainer and Pokemon need a deep bond.

Misty understood. Ethan had caught Gyarados back in Kanto when it was a Magikarp. Since its evolution, they had fought countless battles together. Their bond was beyond question.

They had found the Key Stone at Meteor Falls only recently. Long before that, in the Orange Islands, the Pokemon Watcher Quincy T. Quackenpoker had given Ethan a Gyaradosite beside the Lake of Birth and Evolution. He had both stones.

But how did you learn to use them? she asked.

That wasn't hard. I looked through Kalos Trainer forums. Some generous Trainers had explained the method in their posts.

Misty remembered seeing him on his phone often during their travels. She had thought he was chatting with someone, perhaps a girl in Kalos whom he treated like a younger sister. It seemed she had helped him look for information too. So that was what he had been doing.

On the battlefield, Mega Gyarados crashed back into the lake. The splash from its enormous body swept nearby Pokemon aside.

No one else had expected it. The teams had arrived too late to see Ethan use Gyarados to drive Deoxys from the water. They did not even know a Pokemon was hiding below. At the sight of the red giant, they assumed the melee had enraged some terrifying creature that lived in the lake.

Let's begin. Through Gardevoir's telepathy, Ethan sent his command across the battlefield to Mega Gyarados. Use all your strength. Tear this place apart!

Raaah!

Water exploded outward again. Mega Gyarados sprang from the lake with its huge mouth open and lunged at Rayquaza and Deoxys in the air. It rose dozens of meters, showing the tremendous power in the muscles of its lower body.

Neither airborne Pokemon dared dismiss this newcomer. Both sensed its strength and extreme ferocity. Rayquaza dodged one way and Deoxys the other. Mega Gyarados missed them and fell back into the lake.

The people and Pokemon nearby were less fortunate. Huge waves swept through them. Even Team Aqua's Water-types lost control in the surge and were dragged under; the Fire-types fared worse.

As in Rayquaza and Deoxys's battle, the small fry were not Mega Gyarados's targets. They suffered from the force of its attack all the same.
