Chapter 469: A Three-Sided Ambush and a Hidden Plan

When the Zubat suddenly attacked, Lanturn, which had remained at the very rear, instantly puffed out its cheeks and released a net of electricity that blocked their path.

Unable to evade in time, the Zubat flew straight into the net. They shrieked in pain, then abandoned Lugia and Oliver to attack Lanturn instead.

Lanturn did not dodge or retreat. It immediately battled them head-on.

Although Oliver's Lanturn was not especially skilled at fighting, it was clearly doing everything possible to protect Oliver and Silver!

Ash nervously watched the battle. When he saw the two sides fighting back and forth, he quietly asked Ethan, "Is it time for Misty and the others to move?"

Ethan glanced at Gengar.

"How long has Brock been gone?"

Gengar answered without hesitation, "Almost an hour!"

An hour...

Ethan calculated silently. An hour was not enough for Brock to return yet, but if they delayed a while longer, he should still make it in time!

With that thought, he told Gengar, "Send the signal to Misty and the others."

At his instruction, Gengar immediately formed a Shadow Ball and hurled it into the sky.

The Shadow Ball soared straight upward. At a certain height, it suddenly exploded, unleashing a mass of dark energy across the sky. Against the blue daylight and white clouds, the burst was exceptionally conspicuous. Its black fire could be seen from several miles away!

Cassidy and Butch naturally noticed the flarelike explosion from the submarine.

They looked up at the lingering black flames and sneered.

Calling for help?

This island was extremely remote, and hardly anyone lived even on nearby Ogi Isle. No matter how loudly they called for help, who could possibly rescue them?

Did they expect the police on Silver Rock Isle?

Silver Rock Isle was still a long distance away. Even if the police rushed here, Team Rocket would have captured Lugia and left long before they arrived!

Still, Cassidy and Butch understood that the longer the battle continued, the greater the chance of unexpected complications.

They needed to finish this quickly!

Without further hesitation, they ordered their subordinates to intensify the attack. Then both took out Poke Balls and leaped down to join the battle!

Butch sent out Primeape and Hitmontop, while Cassidy released Houndour and Sableye!

Primeape and Houndour were familiar enough. Ethan had frequently encountered both species during his travels. Hitmontop and Sableye, however, were Pokemon he had never seen alive before.

Hitmontop was a Fighting-type Pokemon with a brown body. Its limbs and tail had bandage-like markings and blue protective structures, while a single spike rose from the top of its head. It fought upside down with both its hands and feet, often performing flips. When it stood upon its head, it resembled a spinning top!

Fighting-type Trainers greatly favored the species. As far as Ethan knew, however, every Hitmontop was male, with no corresponding female species. He had no idea how they reproduced.

Sableye, meanwhile, was a Ghost- and Dark-type Pokemon. Its small body was deep purple, and its sapphire-like eyes gave it a sinister, almost alien appearance. Such a Pokemon certainly suited a villain.

Cassidy's and Butch's four Pokemon were naturally stronger than those of ordinary grunts. They still could not defeat Ethan and Ash in individual battles, but their entry into the fight created considerable trouble.

More significant was the fact that, as soon as the pair joined the battle, the Team Rocket members released dozens more Pokemon, making their assault even fiercer!

The battlefield became overwhelmingly chaotic. Two fists could not defend against four hands, and Ethan's and Ash's Pokemon gradually began struggling to hold their ground!

At that moment, several explosions rang out. Cassidy and Butch turned and saw more than a dozen Pokemon suddenly rush from the forest to the north, northeast, and northwest!

Without saying a word, the newcomers immediately joined the battle and attacked Team Rocket's Pokemon!

Behind them, Erika, Misty, and Richie also emerged from the forest!

At the critical moment, the three had arrived to reinforce the battle!

Cassidy and Butch exchanged looks.

So these are Ethan's reinforcements?

I see. Their companions never left. They were hiding here all along...

Had they anticipated Team Rocket's arrival and prepared an ambush, or did they have some other objective?

Fortunately, the three Trainers' appearance only introduced another variable to the battlefield. Their arrival briefly threw the grunts into confusion, but nothing more.

Even with a dozen additional Pokemon, Ethan's group remained at an overwhelming numerical disadvantage!

Do they truly believe this will be enough to defeat us?

Hmph. How naive!

In contrast, Ethan stood silently upon the large rock and watched the battlefield.

In front of him, a Weezing that attempted to attack was immediately knocked down by Gardevoir's Psychic.

Before the battle began, Erika, Misty, and Richie had remained hidden in the distant forest. Their purpose was to charge out at the signal, surround Team Rocket from three directions, and coordinate with Ethan from within the encirclement to catch the enemy by surprise!

To prevent Team Rocket from detecting them, Ethan had deliberately sent the three far away and instructed them to hide deep within the forest. They were to return only after seeing the signal.

Judging from the result, that part of the plan had clearly succeeded!

Of course, the ambush served more than one purpose.

Ethan's primary objective had been to appear weak enough that Team Rocket would dare act. He fully understood that this small ambush alone could never eliminate the entire force.

Its real purpose was to entangle Team Rocket and prevent them from escaping!

Ethan's true reliance was Brock, whom he had sent to find reinforcements an hour earlier!

What should someone do after encountering criminals?

Call the police, of course!

Even if Ethan defeated all these Team Rocket members, capturing and restraining them afterward would still present a serious problem!

Unfortunately, there was no signal on the island, so Brock had to travel to a nearby city and notify the police. The round trip would take considerable time, and Ethan's group needed to keep Team Rocket occupied until he returned.

Concerned that Officer Jenny might not have enough strength to handle such a large Team Rocket force, Ethan had also given Brock the phone numbers of the International Police officers Anabel and Looker. Brock could request their help directly!

Team Rocket could never have imagined that Maya had warned Ethan about their plan to target Lugia in advance. They believed themselves the hunters without realizing that Ethan had long since turned them into the prey!

With Team Rocket unaware of the trap and the timing working against them, being surrounded by the police had become their inevitable fate!
