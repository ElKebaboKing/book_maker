Chapter 880: A Second Time

Miki's teasing caught Ethan out, and he felt embarrassed. To change the subject, he asked, Where are Naoko and the others? Shouldn't we invite them to eat with us?

Miki saw through the question, but answered without pointing it out. They're helping May practice. They'll eat on their own later.

In fact, she had deliberately sent Naoko, Sayo, and May away before lunch. She did not want the younger girls to hear the awkward conversation ahead or be caught in an argument if it went badly. In that respect, she had taken care to protect them.

What Miki could not know was that her two youngest sisters had already seen what happened between Ethan and the older three the night before.

Ethan nodded and pulled the cork from his sake bottle.

After last night, he had no desire to drink again soon. An identical bottle was enough to make him uneasy. But if a toast would ease the atmosphere and help them move past what had happened, he could manage one.

He poured himself a full cup. Thankfully, the strange fragrance was gone. He began to relax.

Zuki rose with her cup, came around the table, and sat beside him. A faint blush colored her face, and her eyes held a softness he had never seen from her before.

She poured her own cup from Ethan's bottle and raised it toward him.

Please drink with me, she said. I hope you'll look after me for the rest of our lives.

Zuki and her sisters had grown up surrounded by old customs. Like Erika, each retained something of a traditional, devoted woman's manner. Once she chose someone, Zuki could be gentle and attentive without giving up her own character. She had been raised to regard that sort of loyalty as natural.

Ethan was still adjusting to her change in manner and to the idea of Zuki as his partner. But he had given his answer. He would not take it back.

Her words touched him most of all. She was entrusting her life to him.

He lifted his cup, touched it to hers, and drank. Zuki emptied her cup too. Her face remained cool and beautiful, but her gaze grew softer as she refilled Ethan's cup.

She looked like a considerate wife.

Ethan raised his drink again. Across the table, Miki and Kuni wore complicated expressions. With a quiet sigh, he offered them a toast as well. Both drank with him, though the gesture only sharpened their sadness. They were sisters, yet the night had left each of them with a very different future.

A few cups whetted Ethan's appetite. He stopped standing on ceremony and began eating in earnest.

Lunch was much quieter than dinner had been. Everyone had private thoughts, and the meal passed in a subdued calm.

By the time Ethan had eaten his fill, he had finished another bottle of sake. Then his heart began to race again. His thoughts turned hazy, just as they had the previous night.

He stiffened. What? They said there was nothing in this sake!

He looked up. Miki, Kuni, and Zuki were flushed and their eyes had begun to lose focus too.

They felt it as well. Miki struggled to keep herself upright and her thoughts clear. What's...happening? There was no scent this time. Why...

She turned toward Zuki, plainly suspecting her sister had dosed the sake again. But Zuki was as bewildered as the rest of them. She had done nothing. There was no reason to do anything now. How could this be happening?

Kuni could no longer speak. She sat breathing heavily, her face growing redder as her gaze settled on Ethan.

The mood from the previous night filled the room again. What little reason they had left warned them that matters were about to go badly. Would it happen all over again?

None of them knew the answer lay in the bottles from last night.

While Zuki and Kuni argued in Zuki's room, Gengar had poured most of the remaining aphrodisiac into all the other sake bottles. Zuki had brought the bottles to dinner, but Ethan was affected and left with her before every bottle had been opened or emptied. Miki and Kuni had finished only the bottles in front of them. Several dosed bottles remained.

Because no one suspected those bottles, they had been set out for lunch and served again.

Why was there no scent now? Overnight, the fragrance had dissipated in the sake, leaving the drug's effect. The mixture had become harder to detect, and all four had drunk from it.

Unfortunately, none of them knew that. Within moments, the effect had fully taken hold.

Miki forced herself to look at the others. Zuki had slumped against Ethan. Certain of her feelings this time, she held his waist without reserve and kissed him.

Kuni was moving toward him too.

Miki's thoughts blurred further. The memory of the previous night broke what remained of her resistance. She knew Ethan was with Zuki now, but her own feelings drew her toward him alongside Kuni.

What followed echoed the night before, though now all four were together from the beginning, and the room was different.

...

Outside in the courtyard, Naoko and Sayo continued helping May with her performance practice.

May valued the rare opportunity and worked hard. Under their guidance, her steps and stage presence took on a more practiced form. She was no longer moving like someone who had learned everything on the road. The sisters' advice was an important step in her development as a Coordinator.

Even determined practice had its limits. May was soon sweating and exhausted. She sat down on the wooden veranda to catch her breath. Her Pokemon flopped down nearby, equally unwilling to move.

Naoko and Sayo exchanged pleased smiles. They were happy to see her progress.

It's getting late, one of them said. Let's take a break and have lunch.

At the mention of food, May's stomach growled. She had spent the whole morning dancing and jumping through routines. She had burned a great deal of energy, and the food here was especially good.

They tidied up and headed toward the dining room.

On the way, Naoko remembered that Miki had specifically asked them to come late so the older sisters could speak privately with Ethan. Surely their discussion was over by now. What had they needed to talk about so secretly?

Only a few dozen steps later, they reached the corridor outside the dining room. Naoko heard something and stopped.

Wait. Why does that sound familiar?

The voices rose and fell without being muffled. She could hear them even from here. She recognized the sounds she and Sayo had heard outside Zuki's room the night before.

Naoko's face flamed. Had this been what Miki and the others meant to discuss with Ethan? In broad daylight?

Sayo and May heard it too. Both stopped, blushing.

Sayo had seen enough the previous night to know. The memory had distracted her even during May's practice, so she recognized the sounds at once.

May understood them as well. Not long ago, at the Temple of the Sea, she had caught a glimpse of Ethan and Maya through a door. She knew what the voices meant.

They sounded like Miki, Kuni, and Zuki. Ethan was the only man in the house. May could draw her own conclusion, but it made no sense to her. When had Ethan become involved with all three women? Last night they had seemed like polite acquaintances.

Had their relationship changed that quickly? Or had there been something between them all along that she had failed to notice?

The three young women stood with different thoughts, none daring to take another step.

Um... Sayo's heart raced. I'm...not that hungry after all. Maybe we should eat later.

Yes, May said with an awkward smile. We could practice some more.

Naoko had already turned back. Sayo and May followed her without another word.

The journey back was nothing like their cheerful walk to lunch. They all knew what they had heard, but no one mentioned it.

They sat along the veranda wall and stared into the courtyard. Practice was out of the question. None felt like it, and May was still hungry.

They stayed there for a long while as the sun shifted overhead. May's stomach growled again, breaking the silence. She stuck out her tongue in embarrassment when the sisters looked at her.

Only then did they notice that all three were sitting in exactly the same posture. They exchanged a smile, and the tension eased a little.

Why don't we find something in the kitchen? Sayo suggested.

Naoko and May agreed. They found enough food there to fill their stomachs, but afterward they were unsure what to do.

Perhaps they imagined it, or perhaps the people inside had forgotten themselves completely, but they thought they could still hear the dining room from the kitchen. When would it end?

Once May had recovered her energy, she returned to practice, giving them something else to focus on. Yet all three were distracted. May kept making mistakes, and Naoko and Sayo could not give the work their full attention. Their progress slowed.

...

Ethan wished he could have a cigarette. There was none nearby, and he did not smoke anyway.

Once the drug's effect had passed, he sat up and checked the time. It was after seven. The light outside was fading, and night would soon fall.

Compared with the previous night, he thought he had fared better. This time it had lasted only three or four hours. He had not fallen asleep afterward, nor had he lost himself quite so completely. Perhaps his body had built a little resistance. His memories were clearer too.

He was still exhausted, especially after the night before. At least he had eaten a great deal at lunch and could keep going.

Beside him, Miki, Kuni, and Zuki dressed in silence, their faces flushed. Their hair was loose and disordered. None seemed able to find anything to say.

Miki and Kuni looked as though they wanted to speak. In the end, they said nothing. They bowed to Ethan and left the room on unsteady feet.
