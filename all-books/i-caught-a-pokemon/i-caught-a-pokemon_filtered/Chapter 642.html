Chapter 642: Cacnea and Seviper

Captain Stern still had work to do, so Ethan and the others left his office rather than keep bothering him. They wandered through the museum on their own.

Despite the museum being closed, Stern seemed to be the only person on the premises. There were no other staff members in sight, not even a security guard making rounds. Yet he trusted them to explore without worrying that anything would go missing. He was a remarkably openhearted man.

Without a guide beside them, they could relax. They went wherever they liked and talked freely about the exhibits.

Beyond the displays they had already seen, the museum covered seawater quality, samples of sand and sediment from different depths, and electronic audio presentations about the ocean.

Max learned a great deal during their visit, and even Ethan and the others felt more knowledgeable by the time they finished exploring.

Before they realized it, dusk had settled outside the windows. How quickly the hours had passed.

Then a soft click sounded nearby. Everything went dark. The lights across the museum had gone out at once.

Huh? Ash looked around in confusion. Did the power go out?

Probably, Misty replied casually. She wasn't worried.

None of them panicked. Outages were unusual, but everyone had experienced one. Ash had run into several during his travels and thought little of it.

Ethan, however, suspected that someone had broken into the museum. The question was whether it was Team Magma or Team Rocket. Either was possible.

The last daylight coming through the windows kept the room from growing completely dark. Ethan beckoned to the others. Let's go back to Captain Stern's office. I think something's happened.

Something's happened? Misty asked. What makes you think that?

This is a museum in a thriving coastal city. Its power shouldn't fail easily, Ethan said. And even if it did, there should be a backup generator. The lights still haven't come on. If the generator isn't working either, something's wrong.

The others realized he was right. They hurried back along their route to Stern's office.

At the doorway, they found the room in disarray. Someone had clearly searched it roughly. Stern and the case were both gone, and a large hole had been smashed through the window. Sea wind blew in, scattering papers from the desk across the floor.

What happened here? Ash said.

Ethan exchanged a glance with the others. Without hesitating, they jumped out through the broken window one after another.

Outside the museum, they spotted two groups facing off a short distance away.

One was the familiar Team Rocket trio. The other wore matching red uniforms: Team Magma.

What were both groups doing here, and how had they ended up confronting each other?

Before Ethan could get closer, he heard Jessie shout, You lot! Hand that treasure over to us!

That single line told Ethan what had happened. Team Rocket must have seen Team Magma enter the museum and leave with the case. Mistaking its contents for treasure, they had decided to rob the robbers.

Fortunately, their interference had stopped Team Magma from getting far.

But where was Stern? Ethan looked around and couldn't see him anywhere.

Apparently, Team Rocket had already delivered its introduction to the Magma members before Ethan arrived. That was a relief. He had heard their motto so often that his ears had grown tired of it.

As Ash started forward, Ethan caught his arm. Wait, he said quietly. If we rush them, they'll run. Let Team Rocket keep them occupied. You two circle around and block their escape.

At his direction, Ash and Misty slipped off in opposite directions, each taking a different path around the two groups. Max stayed behind Ethan as they approached slowly from the front.

The dusk gave them cover. Neither Team Rocket nor Team Magma noticed they were being surrounded; both groups were focused entirely on each other.

This was Ethan's first encounter with the trio since they had followed Ash into Hoenn. Their Pokemon lineup had changed in the new region. Besides Meowth and Wobbuffet, Jessie and James had two unfamiliar Pokemon standing guard before them: a Cacnea and a Seviper.

Cacnea, a Grass-type Pokemon, looked just as its name suggested: a walking ball cactus.

It had a round body, sturdy arms, three dark green diamond-shaped patterns on its chest, a yellow flower atop its head, and sharp spines all over. Its light green color gave it a healthy look.

Cacnea usually lived in dry places with little rain and favored harsh environments such as deserts. It stored water inside its body and could survive thirty days without a drink.

The yellow flower on its head bloomed only once a year. The drier and harsher its surroundings, the more beautiful and fragrant the bloom became.

Its spines were extremely sharp and could snag all sorts of things by accident. It lured prey with the flower's strong scent, then fired its spines to bring them down. It could also fight by swinging its thick, needle-covered arms.

Seviper was another matter. It resembled a black snake, with a golden lower jaw, gold armor on its head and back, six small gold markings on its belly, sharp red fangs, and a bladelike tail.

Seviper and Zangoose were said to be eternal enemies, their rivalry continuing through generations. The scars on Seviper's body marked their fierce battles. It was relentless and dangerous: poison seeped from the sharp blade of its tail. Lurking in grass, it could bite prey with its venomous fangs, then slash an opponent with its tail and spread more poison into the wound.

To be honest, Ethan thought it ugly. It didn't suit his tastes at all. Only someone like Jessie would take a liking to that sort of snake Pokemon.
