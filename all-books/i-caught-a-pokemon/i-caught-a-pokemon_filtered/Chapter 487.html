Chapter 487: Dragonair Evolves

Thud...

After tracing an arc through the air, Primeape crashed to the ground and rolled backward several times. Its limbs went limp. It had fainted and could no longer battle.

Only a few minutes had passed since the match began, yet Dragonair had won the first round with ease.

Chuck's students stared in disbelief. This was nothing like what they had expected! Their master was a Fighting-type expert. How could one of his Pokemon be defeated so easily? The two Pokemon did not even have a type advantage over each other...

Some rubbed their eyes, as though checking whether the Primeape on the floor really was the fierce Pokemon that usually battered other challengers.

Primeape was certainly strong, but among Chuck's Pokemon it was only a reasonably capable member of his team. He had not recognized Ethan and had treated him as an ordinary challenger.

In the end, he had underestimated his opponent.

"To beat my Primeape that easily, you've got real skill. Looks like I'll have to show you a little of what I can do!"

Chuck gave Ethan a surprised look. He took out the Poke Ball he had only recently put away and recalled the unconscious Primeape.

He admitted to himself that he had underestimated this opponent. He had expected an ordinary challenger, never someone so strong. Primeape was often the first Pokemon he sent into battle, and for all the times it had opened a match, it had rarely lost so quickly.

It was time to raise the difficulty.

Chuck waved to the Machoke beside him, the one Ethan had seen at the beach. Machoke leaped straight into the battlefield.

Bang! It landed steadily, and the floor gave a tremendous thump under its weight.

"Young man," Chuck said, "I can see you're no ordinary Trainer. From now on, I won't treat you like one of those green kids who only recently started training Pokemon!"

Ethan nodded and smiled. "Mr. Chuck, you'd better be careful too. My Dragonair is quite strong."

After losing the first round, Chuck's choice to send out Machoke showed just how much confidence he had in it.

Machoke...

Ethan had battled its unevolved form, Machop, many times, but this was his first time facing a Machoke.

Machoke was a pure Fighting-type Pokemon with gray skin and powerful muscles all over its body. It wore a gold belt that restrained its strength. A black marking around its waist resembled briefs, and it had five digits on each hand and foot. Six red marks lined each arm. It looked exceptionally strong.

Ethan did not switch Pokemon for Chuck's second round. Dragonair had barely been hurt in the first, so he left it on the field.

When the battle resumed, Chuck was plainly more serious than before.

If Primeape was a hotheaded amateur that followed its instincts and vented its anger on its opponent, Machoke was a fighting expert with both strength and technique.

As soon as they exchanged blows, Ethan felt how much harder this round would be.

Machoke attacked methodically, often flowing straight from one move into the next. Dragonair struggled to keep up.

Its lack of limbs was part of the problem. Against Machoke's close-range attacks, Dragonair could not block or parry with its body. It had to open up some distance before it could strike back.

"Machoke, use Seismic Toss!"

After its repeated attacks failed to finish the fight, Machoke leaped in front of Dragonair at Chuck's command. It reached out to seize Dragonair in its arms, but Dragonair twisted its body and slipped away before Machoke could grab it.

Dragonair seized the opening and immediately whipped an Aqua Tail at Machoke.

They were too close for Machoke to dodge. It could only raise both hands to shield its head and take the blow.

Smack! Aqua Tail drove Machoke back several steps, but it shifted its weight in time and stopped its retreat.

What a difficult opponent...

Ethan and Chuck had the same thought at the same moment.

The two Pokemon clashed again. Machoke pressed the attack while Dragonair used its long, supple body to keep moving. Each pushed and pulled against the other, trading blows without either gaining a decisive advantage.

Chuck's students watched without blinking. At their level, this was a thrilling battle!

To their disappointment, Machoke landed several solid hits, but Dragonair refused to go down. Instead, Dragonair paralyzed it with Thunder Wave and then defeated it with Hyper Beam!

"He lost again..."

The students shook their heads as Machoke fell. The first round was one thing, but Machoke had had a real chance to win this one. Dragonair had simply proved stronger.

To Ethan, there was nothing strange about the result. Machoke was clearly still not Chuck's strongest Pokemon, and holding its own against Dragonair for so long was already impressive.

Chuck applauded. He was just about to praise Dragonair's performance when a white light suddenly burst from its body!

Chuck knew that light well. It was the glow of a Pokemon evolving!

"Hm? It's evolving!?"

Ethan was delighted by Dragonair's evolution too.

By rights, Dragonair should have evolved long ago. Today, it had finally found the opportunity!

When the light faded, a new Pokemon stood before them.

Its body was orange, its belly a soft cream color. Two small wings grew from its back, and two feelers rose from its head. There was no mistaking the big, plump Dragonite!

Dragonite twisted this way and that for a while, apparently unaccustomed to its new body.

Ethan could not help laughing at the sight. "Congratulations, Dragonite! You might not be as cute as you were before, but you're stronger now!"

Dragonite turned and called back to him, then looked at its two claws. Now that it had hands and feet, it would need quite some time to get used to them.
