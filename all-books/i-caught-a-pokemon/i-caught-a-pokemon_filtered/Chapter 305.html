Chapter 305: Splitting Up

To everyone's astonishment, a huge black mechanical spider crawled out of the new opening in the mountain. Four steel clawed arms extended from its body.

The group was too far away to make out the face of the person at its controls, but Ethan had little doubt who it was: Vicious.

So the danger they had come to stop had arrived after all. Unable to find the proper exit, Vicious had simply made a new one. Team Rocket's top executive certainly had his own way of doing things.

Worse, more than a dozen Team Rocket members in black uniforms jumped out of the hole behind the spider. Each carried a specialized gun.

The Elder frowned. "Go. You must protect the peace of the Dragon Holy Land! I'm too old to fight as I once did. I'm counting on you now."

"Don't worry. Leave this to us."

Lance gave the Dragon Elder a solemn nod, then turned to Ethan and Clair. As the oldest, most experienced, and most composed Trainer among them, he naturally took charge. He made his decision without hesitation.

"I didn't expect so many of them. We'll split up. I'll deal with Vicious. You two take care of the Team Rocket members first. We cannot let them reach the heart of the Holy Land."

Clair bristled at being assigned the grunts. "No. I want to fight that Pokemon hunter too!"

"Don't rush in." Lance held up a hand to calm her. "Vicious has Dark Balls that can steal other people's Pokemon. We can't all charge at him without a plan. I'll keep him occupied. Once you've cleared out the Team Rocket members, come join me."

He looked to Kabu. "Stay here with Dragonite and protect the Elder. If any of them slip through, don't let them get past you."

Lance's orders were clear and sensible, so no one objected further.

With no time to lose, Lance summoned his Dragonite, climbed onto its back, and flew straight toward the black spider. Clair released her Charizard and rushed toward the Team Rocket members.

They vanished into the distance in moments. Before following, Ethan turned to Sabrina.

"We should split up too."

"Oh?" She tilted her head. "You don't need my help?"

"They're ordinary grunts. Surely we don't need the Leader of Saffron Gym to deal with them!" Ethan joked. Then he looked in the direction Lance had gone, concern showing on his face. "Vicious is the real danger. If his Poke Balls can take someone else's Pokemon outright, even Lance may have trouble with him. I'd feel better if you went to support him."

"I understand."

Sabrina nodded. Psychic power flowed from her body, and without any visible movement, her feet lifted off the ground.

As she was about to leave, Ethan added, "Be careful. It would be best if you didn't use your Pokemon either. Rely on your psychic powers."

"Don't worry." She gave him a gentle smile, warmth in her eyes. "I know what to do."

Sabrina flew toward the mechanical spider. Ethan mounted his Charizard and headed for the Team Rocket members.

The valley was vast, but a flying Pokemon could cross it in minutes.

When Ethan arrived, Lance had already engaged the spider. Dragonite was attacking its legs, apparently trying to stop it from moving. Wary of Vicious's Dark Balls, Lance had not sent out any other Pokemon, and Dragonite kept a safe distance even while attacking from afar.

Clair, meanwhile, was battling the Team Rocket members. Alongside Charizard, she had released Dragonite, Salamence, and Altaria. The four Pokemon flew overhead, diving now and then to strike at the grunts.

Team Rocket had come prepared. Its members sent out great numbers of Golbat and Weezing, two Pokemon so common among them that they might as well have been their symbols.

In moments, dozens of Pokemon were chasing one another through the air. Moves flashed in every direction, so many that it was hard to follow them all. The wild Pokemon nearby had long since scattered, frightened by the explosion and the battle.

Clair was powerful, but there were too many opponents. Her Pokemon had to evade attacks from the Golbat and Weezing while the grunts' guns repeatedly launched capture nets at them. Dodging took so much effort that they could barely mount an effective offensive.

Ethan narrowed his eyes and jumped down from Charizard. With both hands, he released every Pokemon he had with him except the three Legendary birds, Venusaur, and Scyther.

"Go! Don't hold back. Take them down!"

Blue light flashed. Gyarados, Crystal Steelix, Jolteon, Dratini, and Gardevoir appeared one after another. With Charizard already out, Ethan had six Pokemon in the fight.

Gyarados roared toward the sky. Its deafening voice seemed to still the entire valley. Intimidated, every Pokemon paused for an instant. Before Gyarados could move, however, the impatient Jolteon had already charged into the battle.

Electric light burst again and again. Each hit left several Golbat tingling and numb, sending them tumbling helplessly toward the ground.

Charizard and Crystal Steelix joined the fray behind Jolteon. Even Dratini, relying on its agility, worked around the edge of the fight, finding isolated opponents and attacking them.

Gyarados came last, hauling its massive body over the ground despite how awkwardly it moved on land.

Ethan's fierce Pokemon tore into the enemy ranks. The balance shifted at once. With the pressure on Clair's team suddenly lifted, her Pokemon could finally show their strength.

Gardevoir, though, took a step back. It knew Gengar was protecting Ethan and that he was unlikely to come to harm. Still, as loyal as ever, it remained close beside him, guarding both Ethan and Clair.
