Chapter 637: We're Friends

When Misty had first met Ash, she'd been fierce enough to slap him just for seeing Pikachu hurt. After Drew's insults, there was no chance she would let him off lightly.

Drew was stunned. He stepped back and stared at her, eyes wide. Only when his cheek began to swell and burn did he seem to realize he'd actually been hit.

You... you dared to hit me?

He held his face in disbelief. Perhaps his upbringing stopped him, or perhaps Misty's forceful manner intimidated him, but he didn't strike back. He only glared at her furiously.

Consider that a lesson! Misty lifted her chin and put a hand on her hip. Make fun of my friend again, and I'll give the other side of your face the same treatment!

She had held back. If she'd aimed for Drew's eye, she could easily have ruined the fairly handsome boy's looks. The punch had been so sudden that Ash, May, Brock, and Max were as astonished as Drew. None of them had expected Misty to throw a punch so quickly.

Only Ethan gave a helpless smile. He hurried forward and put himself between Misty and Drew.

He hadn't recognized the boy by appearance alone, but as soon as Drew introduced himself, Ethan remembered who he was and what lay ahead for him.

Drew would become May's greatest rival in Pokemon Contests. After this meeting, the two would cross paths many more times; eventually, Drew would even begin watching May's progress quietly from afar.

For all his scorn, Drew was rather like Gary had been early on: sharp-tongued, but decent beneath his cool exterior. Ethan had remained calm during the provocation because he knew what Drew was really like. Misty's punch, however, had probably turned the boy against them for good.

That wouldn't matter much to Ethan and his friends. They were traveling Trainers and would leave Hoenn after the Ever Grande Conference. For May, who lived here, making an unnecessary enemy of a wealthy and capable Coordinator was another matter.

But what was done was done. Ethan wouldn't waste time hesitating over it. He stepped up and took responsibility. That's what happens when you insult people. You need to learn some basic respect.

You! Drew's face went red. No one had ever spoken to him that way, let alone dared to do it. He glared at the group. Get out of here! You're not welcome!

Misty rolled her eyes. Why should we leave just because you say so? We were here first!

Drew pointed behind him. This is the private beach belonging to my family's resort hotel. It's not a place for you to do whatever you please!

They followed his finger and saw a four-story hotel nearby. The stretch of sand lay within its grounds. So that was why there were no other visitors: it was a private beach. They had wandered onto the property, which also explained why Drew had come over.

Ethan and the others exchanged glances. Misty turned to leave first, and the rest followed.

Only after they had gone a long way and returned to the crowded public beach, with Drew nowhere in sight, did May quicken her steps to catch up with Misty.

Misty, thank you for what you did back there!

It's nothing. Misty waved it off. We're friends. How could I stand there while he talked to you like that? I couldn't stand his arrogance either, as if everyone else were beneath him. And he had the nerve to say we weren't beautiful! Does he have any idea how insulting that is to a girl?

Misty... May was deeply moved. Even Ash and Max thought Misty looked incredibly cool just then.

They left the beach and found another secluded spot to help May practice again. With Ethan and the others joining in, they could try more kinds of training. Ash even volunteered to face May in a mock Contest battle.

That suited Ethan and Misty, who had never seen a Pokemon Contest in person. They could learn more about its rules before the real event.

They chose an open space outside the city. Ethan and Misty sat on a large rock and put a kettle of tea on to boil nearby. May and Ash took positions on opposite sides of the field, as they would for an ordinary Pokemon battle.

Brock stepped into the middle to referee. After looking from one Trainer to the other and seeing that both were ready, he called, The mock Pokemon Contest battle begins now!

Having run a Gym himself, Brock made a convincing referee. He looked every bit as capable as a professional.

As soon as Brock finished speaking, Ash threw a Poke Ball with an excited grin. Taillow, I choose you!

Ash's energy and enthusiasm were hard to match. He had already battled Ethan twice, yet he was still eager to face May. With that much love for Pokemon battles, he truly did seem like this world's destined protagonist.

May sent out the Beautifly she had been practicing with.

As they watched, Ethan explained to Misty that a Pokemon Contest had two stages: an appeal round and Contest battles.

In the appeal round, Coordinators displayed their Pokemon's moves beautifully to impress the judges and audience. Those with high scores advanced.

Compared with other regions, the Grand Festivals in Kanto and Hoenn had two appeal rounds. In the first, Coordinators showed their Pokemon using moves; in the second, they could use moves freely.

During the battle stage, Coordinators had to bring out the beauty of both themselves and their Pokemon in combat. The battles followed double battle rules, and a computer selected each Coordinator's opponent at random. A loser was eliminated; whoever won the final match took the championship.

The rules sounded complicated to Misty. What a lot to keep track of... She shook her head with a wry smile. Our Pokemon battles are simpler. Beat your opponent and you advance. There aren't so many extra rules.
