Chapter 857: Solana's Gesture of Goodwill

Solana led them along a forest path to a Pokemon Center among the trees. It was unlike the Centers in busy towns, looking more like a research facility. That made sense. A Center in a remote forest would do more research than tend to the Pokemon of passing Trainers. Otherwise, why build it somewhere so quiet?

One thing had not changed: the person in charge was a familiar-looking Nurse Joy. Seeing her face made the place feel welcoming.

Solana asked as soon as they met, Nurse Joy, is the system working any better?

She must have spoken to this Joy before, whether in person or by phone. Otherwise she would not have known so much about the Center's trouble.

No. We still can't use it. Joy sighed. None of the equipment will turn on, and the power has stopped. We've switched to backup power, but that won't last much longer if this continues.

Whether or not Deoxys had caused it, the damage from the geomagnetic anomaly was plain to see. Solana told Joy about the Deoxys they had just seen.

Oh! Joy looked startled, then thoughtful. So the anomaly that began last week was caused by it?

That seems likely. Solana nodded and stepped aside to introduce Ethan and May. But even if Deoxys is involved, Mr. Vale is here. I'm sure we'll be able to resolve this.

Joy looked at Ethan in surprise. Ethan gave Solana an equally surprised glance. Was he imagining it? They had met only recently, yet she seemed far more admiring of him now than she had then. It felt almost unreal. Still, it was a welcome change.

I'll help as much as I can, he said. He turned to Joy. Can you tell us exactly what happened?

Joy thought back. It started about a week ago. The magnetic field suddenly became abnormal, our equipment began failing, and we couldn't carry out our work. We contacted Ranger headquarters, and Solana came afterward.

Solana nodded. Headquarters assigned me to investigate the cause, but I hadn't found any clues. We only learned Deoxys was involved a moment ago.

Ethan understood. The two were saying much the same thing. Neither Joy nor Solana knew more than the outline, and perhaps not much more than he did.

Before reaching the Center, he had assumed Deoxys had arrived by chance and produced the aurora. The week-long disturbance suggested there might be more to it. He rubbed his chin and considered the possibilities.

Nurse Joy, has a meteorite ever fallen near here?

A meteorite? Joy blinked. You think that's connected to the magnetic anomaly? There was one nearby, but that was ten years ago!

Just as I thought. Ethan owned a Deoxys and knew a great deal about the species. The mention of an old meteorite gave him a likely explanation.

He signaled to Solana and May. They understood, smiled politely at Joy, and followed him into the adjoining dining room.

Mr. Vale, do you already have an idea? Solana asked as soon as they sat down.

Yes. It has to do with that meteorite. The room was empty apart from the three of them, so Ethan spoke openly.

But Nurse Joy said it fell ten years ago, Solana said. Why would it disrupt the magnetic field only now?

The meteorite arrived ten years ago. Something inside it may have awakened only recently.

Solana was quick to follow his reasoning. Are you saying a Deoxys was inside?

Rather than answer directly, Ethan asked, How much do you know about Deoxys?

She thought for a moment. I know one appeared in LaRousse City. According to a report from Professor Lund that Ranger headquarters obtained, Deoxys traveled across space with a meteorite before reaching Earth. It seems to be a Pokemon associated with meteorites.

That's close enough, though calling it a Pokemon born alongside meteorites isn't quite right. Deoxys does travel through space with them. That's why we saw one here.

His certainty convinced Solana that he was probably right. Even so, one question troubled her.

Mr. Vale, how do you know all this? Even a League Champion or an International Police officer shouldn't understand Deoxys this well.

She was not trying to pry. It simply seemed unbelievable. The Rangers had worked hard to obtain even one of Professor Lund's reports. It was kept at headquarters, where only senior Rangers had access to its contents. Yet Ethan apparently knew more than they didperhaps even more than Lund, who had studied Deoxys for years.

How could that be?

Ethan weighed how to answer. Should he conceal the truth or reveal something about himself? Then he thought it over. His enemies already knew about many of his Pokemon. Telling an organization like the Rangers would do little harm and might help them understand what he could contribute.

He took an Ultra Ball from his waist pouch and set it quietly in front of Solana.

She blinked at it. What about this Ultra Ball?

Ethan smiled, speaking as if it were nothing remarkable. There's a Deoxys inside.

For a second Solana was still. Then she realized what he had said.

What!? She shot to her feet and stared at the ball. Her cry was so loud that Joy looked over from the other side of the Center.

You're too loud... Ethan had expected surprise, but even he was startled by her reaction.

Solana sat down quickly. Then she leaned toward the Ultra Ball with the wide-eyed curiosity of a child.

There's really a Deoxys in there? She tried to lower her voice, though the astonishment still came through. How did you do it? I mean, how did you meet it and catch it? That's incredible. It's such a rare Mythical Pokemon. I'd never even seen one in person before today!

Ethan put the Ultra Ball away. How I caught it is personal, so I'd rather not say. He shrugged. I told you for one reason: Professor Lund has studied Deoxys for years, but a Trainer who lives with one can understand it in ways no one else can.

If Misty had been here, she might have applauded his display of confidence with both hands. May, however, looked at him with much the same quiet admiration as Solana.

No matter how many times she heard it, May still found it astonishing that the man beside her owned so many Legendary and Mythical Pokemon.

Solana steadied herself, but her estimation of Ethan rose higher still. She now understood how he had found Celebi so easily before. Perhaps that kind of thing was easy for someone like him. If someone told her he had a Celebi of his own, she would hardly be surprised.

I'll find out where that meteorite fell, she said, rising from the table.

While Solana investigated, Ethan and May ordered food and filled their stomachs. She soon returned with a document in her hand and several additional pieces of equipment.

To end the disturbance and its effect on local equipment as soon as possible, they wasted no more time. They left the Pokemon Center and headed deeper into the mountains.

Ethan was reminded of Hoenn. He had once accepted a request from Devon Corporation and gone to deal with a meteorite and a Deoxys there. After all this time, he seemed to be facing a similar incident again.

The three followed Solana into the higher hills. Dense forest gave way to rocky ground, and the path climbed steadily. Soon they reached the foot of the mountains.

Solana did not lead them up the slope. She stopped before a cave beneath a cliff. Its opening was more than twice a person's height. Ethan could not tell whether it had formed naturally or been dug out. Cold air streamed from it, and the interior vanished into darkness.

It's in there. Solana checked her document against her Capture Styler before turning to the others. If the records are right, the meteorite is somewhere inside the cave.

Inside? May asked. Didn't it fall from the sky? How did it end up in a cave?

It did fall from the sky. It struck the ground above this cave, broke through the roof, and landed inside.

That's quite a coincidence.

While they spoke, Ethan studied the Capture Styler in Solana's hand. Isn't that affected by the magnetic disturbance?

Every electronic device they had tried on the way here, from phones and Pokedexes to Poke Balls, had failed. Even Solana's Plusle seemed drained, lying listlessly on her shoulder. Yet the Capture Styler still worked. They were closer to the meteorite now, where the disturbance ought to be stronger.

Solana looked a little proud. Rangers work in extreme places and weather all the time. The Capture Styler was designed with conditions like these in mind. Magnetic disturbances were one of the problems its designers tested for. After a lot of redesigning and trials, they made a device that could keep operating here.

She raised another device for them to see. And this one detects geomagnetic activity. It still works too.

Ethan nodded. Whatever the Ranger Union's size, he had to admire its technology.

I didn't realize your equipment was so useful. If ordinary Trainers had one, we wouldn't have to worry about getting lost anywhere.

He meant it as an idle observation. Solana heard it differently and made a mental note, though she said nothing.

They followed her into the cave. Rangers really were confident in their skills. They traveled the world with a Capture Styler, often without Poke Balls or powerful Pokemon of their own. Wild Pokemon were not necessarily a danger to them; almost any one they met could become an ally.

Their flashlight beams reached far down the dark passage, giving them a narrow path of light. May stayed close behind Ethan, carefully watching the ground and the black spaces around her.

She was afraid of the dark, as many people were. More than the darkness itself, she feared something dangerous might leap out and attack.

Fortunately, perhaps because of the geomagnetic anomaly or the power of Deoxys, they walked for a long time without anything happening.
