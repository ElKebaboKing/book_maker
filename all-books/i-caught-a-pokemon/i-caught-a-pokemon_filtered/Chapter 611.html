Chapter 611: The Tables Turn

As Zinnia studied Ethan, he happened to turn toward her. Their eyes met.

She did not seem embarrassed to be caught looking. Instead, she smiled slightly and introduced herself. Hello. I'm Zinnia, a descendant of the Draconid people.

The Draconid people?

Misty had only recently arrived in Hoenn and had never heard the name. Most people in the region probably had not heard of them either.

Zinnia turned to her and waved the question away. We're just people whose families have lived near Meteor Falls for generations. We have a different name, that's all. It's nothing special.

It was hardly that simple, Ethan thought.

Despite Zinnia's casual explanation, he knew the Draconids were more than ordinary people living apart from the world. They were Trainers who specialized in Dragon-type Pokemon.

They lived in the hidden Draconid Village beneath Meteor Falls, reached by an underground waterway.

The reason they had remained here went back two thousand years, to a battle between Primal Groudon and Primal Kyogre.

According to the records, the two ancient Pokemon had fought fiercely, causing devastation as meteors streaked across the night sky. Their battle ended only when a third ancient Pokemon, Rayquaza, appeared.

A thousand years later, a meteor fell on what was now Sootopolis City and awakened Groudon and Kyogre. The Draconids' Lorekeeper of that time stood upon a rainbow stone and prayed for Rayquaza's return. Her wish brought about Rayquaza's Mega Evolution. After the ensuing battle, Groudon and Kyogre returned to the depths, and Rayquaza went back to the sky.

Afterward, the Draconids built Sky Pillar to honor their god and painted murals on its walls. They foretold that another meteor would strike the world after the next thousand years. When it came, helpless people would have to call upon Rayquaza again to protect the planet.

They sounded like guardians of the world and devoted followers of Rayquaza.

Professor Cozmo heard Zinnia's modest description and, worried that Ethan and Misty might not know the truth, explained the Draconids himself. His account was much the same as what Ethan already knew.

For Ethan, it was a familiar story. Misty finally understood what sort of people Zinnia came from.

Groudon... Kyogre... and Rayquaza... Misty murmured, longing in her eyes. Hoenn has so many incredible Pokemon.

Ethan smiled. It's no stranger than Ho-Oh and Lugia in Kanto and Johto. Pokemon like them possess immense power, remain hidden in the world, and are often revered as gods. Raikou is a Legendary Pokemon too, but those three are on another level.

Thinking of Ho-Oh and Lugia helped Misty grasp what Groudon and the others might be like. Ho-Oh could bring the dead back to life, and Lugia was called the god of the sea. These Hoenn Pokemon must have their own extraordinary powers.

They talked for a while longer. Ethan and Misty learned that Cozmo and Zinnia had met only recently. Cozmo had come alone to investigate meteorites, lost his way, and happened to be rescued by Zinnia. Team Aqua had taken an interest in him around that time, leading to his abduction.

By the way, Misty asked belatedly, Professor Cozmo, what field do you study?

Me? He shrugged. Meteorites. All kinds of meteorites. Did you know that wormholes can even send a meteorite to another universe?

Perhaps he rarely had anyone to discuss his work with. Once he began, he talked without stopping. Zinnia finally coughed a few times, and Cozmo realized he had been going on too long. He fell silent in embarrassment.

They stayed in the chamber for a long while without Team Magma or Team Aqua finding them, and gradually relaxed their guard.

By the time it was around three or four in the afternoon, they left the hiding place by another passage and headed for the surface.

With Zinnia guiding them through the caves she had known since childhood, they no longer had to worry about getting lost. She soon led them toward the nearest exit.

The journey had been peaceful. As they drew closer to daylight, their steps grew lighter.

Then, near the exit, Team Magma ambushed them.

After losing their trail in the maze, Courtney had stopped to think rather than search blindly. The tunnels were complicated, but only a few openings led outside. If her team waited long enough at one of them, they might catch their quarry.

Now they had.

Dozens of Houndoom lunged at Ethan's group from every direction. Misty and Cozmo were startled. Ethan had expected Team Magma to keep pursuing them and had stayed alert, so their appearance did not surprise him much.

What did catch his attention was Zinnia. She remained steady in the face of the ambush, showing no sign of panic.

She had nerves of steel.

Courtney stepped out from among her Team Magma members and watched the surrounded group coldly.

While setting the ambush, she had called for reinforcements. Members stationed nearest Meteor Falls were on their way. The group had a powerful Pokemon, but once those reinforcements arrived, she was confident she could keep them all here.

Her one concern was Shelly and Team Aqua. They would surely come after Team Magma in retaliation, just as Courtney had followed them earlier.

Inside the ring of Houndoom, Ethan and the others sent out their Pokemon.

Misty chose Starmie and Politoed.

Politoed had been with her since it was a Poliwag. It had evolved into Poliwhirl, battled Ash in the Whirl Cup, and later evolved again into Politoed. It was a strong partner.

Professor Cozmo was not helpless in a fight either. He threw a Poke Ball, releasing a strange Pokemon that floated in the air. Its orange body resembled a meteorite, with eight pointed rocks projecting from it.

It was Solrock, a Rock- and Psychic-type Pokemon. Ethan had never seen one in person before, but this was no time to study it.

Almost as soon as Cozmo sent out Solrock, Zinnia released her own Pokemon.

It was a fierce-looking Salamence. The Dragon- and Flying-type Pokemon planted all four feet on the ground, spread its red wings, and growled at the Houndoom. Its open mouth showed rows of sharp teeth.

Finally, Ethan assessed the field and threw two Poke Balls in succession, bringing out Dragonite and Tyranitar.

Courtney sneered when she saw those were the only two he had sent out. Where's your electric Pokemon? Why aren't you using it? Afraid?

Ethan smiled. His answer shook the confidence of her team.

Of course not. I'm keeping it ready for Team Aqua. As you know, Electric-type moves have the advantage against Water-type Pokemon.

Courtney's face darkened. She glanced to one side.

He was right to worry about Team Aqua. She had been worried about them too. She had already assigned a squad to guard their rear in case Team Aqua appeared and disrupted their formation.

For now, however, capturing Cozmo was the urgent task.

At her command, twenty or thirty Houndoom closed in from several directions. In an instant, the battle began.

Courtney soon realized it would not be as simple as she had imagined.

Apart from Cozmo's Solrock, which was only moderately strong, the other three Trainers' Pokemon were unexpectedly difficult to handle.

Dragonite, Tyranitar, and Salamence were especially formidable. The Houndoom fell after only a few exchanges. The opposing Pokemon seemed to belong to an entirely different class.

Alarmed, Courtney wondered who these people were and where they had come from.

She waved Camerupt into the fight and called out, Who are you?

Ethan was busy directing his two Pokemon and had no interest in answering. Zinnia looked at Courtney, who seemed about her age, and spoke instead. I'm a friend of Professor Cozmo. And this is Draconid territory. How could I let you do whatever you please here?

Draconids?

Understanding flashed in Courtney's eyes. She had thought those people had disappeared long ago. So they still existed.

That single answer told her she would have to defeat the group before she could take Cozmo.

She said nothing more and signaled for everyone to attack at once.

She had clearly taken Ethan and Misty for Zinnia's companions, and thus for Draconids as well.

There was no more to discuss. Ethan's group had to break through the encirclement and get Cozmo away. Team Magma was determined to capture the professor.

The Pokemon clashed in a chaotic melee.

One Houndoom bared its fangs and leaped at Dragonite. Dragonite sent it flying with a sweep of its tail.

Several others spat flames at Dragonite, but Politoed and Starmie fired Water Guns together and drove the fire back.

Zinnia's Salamence stood out as well. It held off four or five Houndoom at once and still managed to defeat them one by one.

Yet Tyranitar was the most imposing Pokemon on the field.

It resisted Fire-type attacks, and its thick armor gave it excellent protection. It charged through the Houndoom like a moving tank, scarcely acknowledging their presence.

Team Magma's Pokemon were no match even for many ordinary Trainers' partners, let alone this group.

They did not collapse the instant fighting began, but they could not stop Ethan and the others for long. Their sheer numbers forced Dragonite's group to dodge the many attacks coming their way, and that was almost the only reason Team Magma was still holding out.

Courtney's Camerupt entered the fight and recovered some ground for her side.

That reprieve would be brief. Once Misty's two Water types were free, they could turn their attacks on the slow-moving giant.

Courtney frowned as she surveyed the field. This was not how she had expected the battle to go.

During the earlier surprise attack, everything had happened too quickly for her to judge Ethan's strength. She had noticed only the power of his Electric-type Pokemon. Now, fighting him directly, she understood how dangerous the group was.

Hurried footsteps sounded behind her.

Courtney brightened. Her reinforcements must have arrived. She turned to order them into battle and froze.

They were not Team Magma. It was Shelly, leading Team Aqua toward them.

How had they got through? Courtney had stationed a squad behind her to watch for exactly this. Her heart sank.

Shelly took in the battle and burst out laughing.

Courtney, the tables really have turned! Now we're the ones who can profit from the fight. Did you imagine this would happen when you ambushed us earlier?

The happier Shelly looked, the angrier Courtney became.

With enemies on both sides, Courtney knew Team Magma could not win today. She cast a hard look at Ethan.

That man had attacked them from behind. Without him, victory today would have belonged to Team Magma!
