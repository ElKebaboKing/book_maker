Chapter 553: The Silver Conference Opens

Ceremonial cannons sounded, and fireworks burst high overhead.

At three in the afternoon, Silver Stadium was packed with spectators from across Johto. Television stations switched to live coverage of the annual Pokemon League tournament.

Friends and family of the competitors and ordinary viewers alike turned their attention to the event. Huge screens in shopping centers and along the streets showed the broadcast without pause.

Office workers sneaked a look while on the job, and students secretly took out their phones. Some bosses and teachers let it pass, because they were just as interested in the Conference.

With so many people watching, the announcer's voice rang through the stadium, clear to everyone there.

In front of a television, Sabrina sat comfortably on a sofa, one leg crossed over the other. She had just finished a Pokemon battle and was watching the broadcast at her leisure.

"...The opening ceremony and the competitors' procession will begin in this very stadium shortly. The audience here can hardly wait. This year's Silver Conference has brought together powerful Trainers. Their contests of strength and skill begin tomorrow. Who will take the championship? Stay with us and find out!"

Sabrina watched the screen and remembered the first part of her journey through Johto with Ethan. If the Pokemon League had not pressed her to return so urgently, she would surely still be at his side.

The thought soured her mood.

If Ethan had not urged her to stay, and her father had not pleaded with her, she would have resigned as Gym Leader and continued traveling the world with him.

Just then, Sabrina's father entered the room. "Sabrina, another challenger is here. Could you..."

He stopped when his daughter rose, her face like ice. She had been fine a moment ago. Who had upset her now?

As she walked coldly toward the battlefield, he could only feel sorry for the challenger.

He hoped Sabrina would not go too far and would remember to hold back.

Others were watching too: the Cerise family at the Cerise Laboratory in Vermilion City, including assistants Ren and Parker; Professor Ivy in the Orange Islands; Melody on Shamouti Island; Maya at the Whirl Islands' Temple of the Sea; the Kimono Sisters in Ecruteak City; and even people as far away as Kalos.

Some were only Ethan's friends. Others had a much closer bond with him.

As they watched, the broadcast cut to a torchbearer raising the sacred flame high. He had left Ho-Oh Temple and was running toward Silver Town between rows of spectators.

In Silver Stadium's waiting area, Ethan stood in line and followed the staff's directions toward the field.

The moment the competitors emerged, every spotlight swung toward them. Cheers and shouts erupted from the stands.

Like the others, Ethan smiled and raised his right hand to acknowledge the crowd.

A camera caught his smile and put it on the giant screen, drawing even louder cheers.

Thanks to the Pokemon League's promotion, he was the most popular competitor here. Nearly every commentator and news outlet listed him among the favorites to win.

Ethan saw himself on the screen. He had nerves of steel and showed no stage fright; if anything, his smile grew wider.

He searched the stands for Erika, Misty, and the others. But Silver Stadium held eighty thousand people. Finding them in that crowd was all but impossible.

His search led him to something else instead.

In a small section near the front of the stands, more than twenty people waved banners and flags. They wore matching outfits, some with hair dyed bright colors, and cheered at the top of their lungs.

Ethan's sharp eyes picked out the words on a banner: Boss Ethan Is Number One!

Huh?

He looked again. The flag bearers wore black leather jackets and had unusual hairstyles. They looked like a biker gang.

Those people at the front seemed especially familiar...

Wait. They were...

Misa, Masha, and the rest of the biker gang! They had come all the way to Johto to watch the opening ceremony?

No one had told Ethan they were coming. They had certainly surprised him.

The camera found them too. The broadcast director gave them a close-up, putting their banner and flags in front of viewers across Johto.

Soft laughter rose from the stands. No one was mocking them; their bright, enthusiastic display was simply too funny and out of place.

When Misa, Masha, and the others saw themselves on the giant screen, they waved their flags even more vigorously.

Those guys...

Ethan smiled wryly to himself.

That banner had made him a target. Even competitors who had paid little attention to him before would take notice now.

It might make things troublesome, but these lovable fools had come only to support him. There was little he could do to repay them.

All he could do was win every match and give them performances they could be proud of.

The competitors' procession lasted more than ten minutes. Once everyone was in position, the stadium gradually quieted.

Now they awaited the sacred flame. It would enter the stadium and light the great cauldron.

As time passed, the sky slowly darkened.

Tens of thousands waited expectantly. At last an athlete carrying the torch came running out of a stadium passage and onto the field.

But when he emerged, the spectators were startled. This was not the young man who had set out from Ho-Oh Temple. The torchbearer was a small boy in casual clothes and a baseball cap, holding the sacred flame with a determined face. A Pikachu ran beside him.

"Ash?!"

Everyone who knew him stared in surprise. No one had expected Ash to become the torchbearer.

Only Ethan shook his head with a rueful smile.

There was only one reason Ash would have gone missing and then appeared with the torch: Team Rocket's trio must have caused trouble somewhere out of sight.
