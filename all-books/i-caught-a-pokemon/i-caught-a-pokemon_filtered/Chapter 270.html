Chapter 270: A Creative Opponent

Venusaur stood alert. The ground beneath it began to tremble, at first faintly, then hard enough to shake the entire field.

Bang!

Earth and broken stone burst upward as Onix erupted from below and struck Venusaur in the belly. The blow hurled it five or six meters into the air. Venusaur's size and weight slowed its ascent after another meter or two, but then it began to fall.

"Venusaur, steady yourself!" Ethan shouted.

Two vines shot from Venusaur's flower and braced against the ground as it came down. It landed on its feet rather than crashing onto its side.

Onix was still aboveground. "Now! Use Razor Leaf!"

Venusaur began to attack, but Onix slipped straight back into the hole it had made and vanished before the leaves could fly. Ethan could only marvel at its speed underground. Drake's Onix had left him almost no opening.

"Well done!" Drake smiled. "Keep it up. Let's do that again!"

The announcer sounded delighted by Venusaur's apparent trouble. "What a predicament! Ethan chose Venusaur for its type advantage, but what good is that if it can't hit Onix? A few more attacks like this and Venusaur will be worn down!"

The crowd cheered. Ethan shook his head with a rueful smile. The announcer did not have to make his preference quite so obvious.

Venusaur might seem unable to reach Onix, but Ethan had a plan. Why else had he sent it toward the pool? He glanced at the water behind his Pokemon and called, "Venusaur, channel the pool water into the hole Onix made!"

"What?" Drake stared. His gaze moved to the pool, and his face changed. He had battled with Onix in Pummelo Stadium countless times. Other opponents had always tried to fight it conventionally. Ethan was the first to use the battlefield's water against its Dig tactic.

Venusaur swung its vines like heavy hammers, striking the ground again and again. Soon it had carved a shallow channel between the pool and Onix's tunnel. Water ran through the channel and poured underground.

Gurgle, gurgle.

The pool looked small from above, but it was deep enough for Water-type Pokemon to battle in. Its volume was substantial, and Onix's tunnel was not long. The water quickly filled it, bubbling as it went.

Underground, Onix recoiled instinctively from the water. It dug deeper, but the water followed. With nowhere left to retreat, it had to surface.

Ethan had been waiting for that. "Venusaur, finish it now!"

The vines lashed out again. Onix had only just emerged and could not dive back underground. Venusaur caught it and wrapped the vines tightly around its body.

Smack! Creak!

The vines struck and tightened. Vine Whip was a basic Grass-type move, but against Rock- and Ground-type Onix, it still dealt heavy damage. Onix strained to tear the vines apart. Venusaur was strong too, and it dug its four feet into the soil like posts.

For a moment, neither Pokemon could pull the other loose. Ethan used the opening. "Venusaur, use Razor Leaf!"

More than a dozen sharp, curved leaves flew from Venusaur's flower. Onix could not move. Every leaf struck home. Grass was strong against both of its types, and the fourfold-effective attack knocked it to the ground.

The fall of Onix's huge body left the announcer gaping. The reversal had come almost too quickly to follow. Venusaur had appeared helpless; a moment later, it had beaten Onix.

"Onix is down!" he cried, dismayed. "Ethan Vale used the pool to force it out! What quick thinking and adaptability!"

The stands erupted. The audience had expected Onix's tough body to endure far more than two hits, even after seeing Ethan choose Venusaur. Now Drake, their undefeated Supreme Gym Leader, was unmistakably on the back foot. Ethan had won both rounds.

Many Orange Islanders began to worry about where the match was heading. Drake's face had turned grave as well. Another loss. He recalled the unconscious Onix and looked at Ethan with mixed feelings.

He had expected an exhilarating Winner's Cup battle, not to fall behind so decisively. Was this the strength of an Indigo League champion? Still, the score was only two to zero. He had four Pokemon left. The real battle was just beginning.
