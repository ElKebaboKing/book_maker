Chapter 441: Jessie's Beautiful Ambition

Ethan left the battlefield and met Erika and the others outside the colosseum.

Although he had successfully advanced to the semifinals, Ash and Misty had both suffered elimination and could no longer compete.

With Ethan the sole survivor, the group's hopes were now concentrated entirely on him.

To be honest, neither Ash nor Misty had expected this before the tournament, when both had been overflowing with confidence. They had believed a local competition like this would let them display their abilities and achieve even better results!

Now it appeared they simply were not strong enough to reach their intended goals.

The result inevitably left both somewhat discouraged, and it struck Ash especially hard.

He had always regarded Ethan as the person he admired most and his greatest rival!

During their travels through the Orange Islands, both Ash and Ethan had defeated Drake and become Honorable Trainers of the Orange League. For a time, that convinced Ash he had once again reached the same starting line and could compete with Ethan!

After reaching Johto, he had caught many Pokemon during his journey, won the Grass Pokemon Tournament, and earned victories in a series of smaller competitions. All of it greatly increased his confidence!

I've become stronger than before! The gap between Ethan and me must be shrinking!

Ash had firmly believed this for quite some time. It was also what gave him the courage to challenge Ethan during the Whirl Cup!

Yet after only a few matches, cruel reality had dragged him back down!

Once again, just as at the Indigo League Conference, his run had ended in the round of sixteen!

His opponent had been Misty, who specialized in Water-type Pokemon, and her Psyduck had defeated him through an unexpected reversal. Even so, a loss was a loss. His own inadequacy was the only honest explanation, and there was no need to make excuses.

Fortunately, Ash had always been broad-minded and had already recovered. What he could not understand was why Misty looked so happy after losing her own match. She practically glowed!

No matter how much he questioned her, Misty merely gave him a smug smile and showed no desire to explain. Only when she looked toward Ethan did some inexplicable emotion seem to pass through her eyes!

The change was so obvious that even Ash could sense it. Unfortunately, in his innocence, he had no idea what the emotion meant.

Unable to understand, he could only suppress the question.

Although Ash and Misty had been eliminated, the three friends had no intention of leaving immediately. They planned to watch Ethan's matches tomorrow before resuming their journey.

As friends, companions, younger siblings, and rivals, they naturally cared greatly about Ethan's progress. With him still competing, how could they abandon him here?

Scratching his head, Ash turned toward Brock. "All of Ethan's matches are finished for today, right? What's tomorrow's schedule?"

Brock took out the guidebook issued by the tournament and flipped through several pages. "Let's see... According to the schedule, the semifinals are tomorrow morning and will determine the last two competitors. The final begins at one in the afternoon and decides the championthe Sea Hero. After that comes the blessing ceremony for the Sea Hero. If everything goes smoothly, we can leave the morning after tomorrow with the trophy and honor."

Like Misty, Brock knew Ethan well enough to believe without doubt that he would become the final champion.

Brock understood perfectly that Ethan was entirely different from Ash and Misty. He already possessed exceptional strength and never gave his opponents the slightest opportunity!

Most of all, they knew about Ethan's red Gyarados. With it available, how could he lose?

Listening to the cheerful conversation around him, Ethan felt exceptionally relaxed and content.

For a time after arriving in this world, he had drifted like a rootless plant, with neither attachments nor support. Now he had friends, companions, and a lover. Their trust and her love made him cherish this world more with each passing day, and they would undoubtedly make him stronger!

White clouds drifted slowly beneath the blue sky, perfectly reflecting his mood.

Yet for some reason, Ethan felt as though he had overlooked something.

Thinking carefully, the tournament had indeed proceeded too smoothly and peacefully. It was like enjoying a delicious feast only to realize it lacked an appetizer, leaving the experience just short of perfect!

Why did he feel that way?

Ethan remained puzzled until he looked at Ash twice and suddenly realized what was missing!

Of course. The Team Rocket trio that's always on Ash's heels hasn't appeared...

Without their interference, this Whirl Cup had undoubtedly been less lively.

But those three could never resist excitement. With such a major event taking place, things should not have remained this quiet.

It was easy to guess that they were hiding somewhere nearby and preparing some grand operation!

...

In fact, Ethan was correct.

Team Rocket had followed Ash and the others to the Whirl Islands long ago. The trio had even disguised themselves as vendors and infiltrated the colosseum. Over the past few days, selling popcorn and cold drinks had already earned them a tidy profit.

Earning money was not their true objective, of course. They had set their sights on the scepter held by Sea Priestess Maya!

The reason they had endured until now without causing trouble was that one of their earlier covert attempts had multiplied the number of guards around the ceremonial platform. Even now, they had not found the right opportunity!

But as the tournament approached its conclusion, they had grown increasingly anxious!

The day's competition was not yet over. Two Trainers were fiercely battling for the last place in the semifinals.

In an unnoticed corner of the stands, the Team Rocket trio crouched in convenience-store uniforms with their heads close together.

"Did you see that just now?" Meowth whispered.

"Of course," James said with a sigh. "Ethan seems even stronger than the last time we saw him. He defeated his opponent so easily again, so the championship is practically his! Ugh. With him here, stealing that scepter will be much harder!"

Jessie glared fiercely at him. "What? Do you two want to give up!?"

Startled by her intensity, James hurriedly ducked his head.

"O-of course not... I only think we should be more careful with our plan..."

"Neither of you is planning to run away at the last moment, are you!?" Jessie narrowed her eyes, a cold gleam within them making the warning unmistakable. "So what if Ethan is here? He's busy with the tournament. How would he have time to meddle in our affairs? And stop being so afraid of him! We're Team Rocket. How can we retreat every time we see him?"

Despite her words, James was deeply unwilling to make an enemy of Ethan.

Under Jessie's oppressive glare, however, he and Meowth hurriedly shook their heads and denied everything. "Of course we aren't afraid of him. We were only thinking... Are we stealing the scepter to sell it for operating funds? We've already made a lot of money over the last few days. Do we really need to take such a risk...?"

Meowth nodded vigorously. "Dat's right... Ethan's bad news. We should stay as far away from him as possible..."

"He's already frightened you out of your wits, hasn't he?" Jessie raised her chin and glanced down at James and Meowth. "I've made up my mind. We are taking that scepter! Look at its golden shaft, and especially that Sea Spirit. How beautiful! I am determined to succeed, and neither of you is allowed to ruin this operation! Ahh, only I am worthy of such a gorgeous gemstone! Hmph, hmph. It exists to make me even more beautiful! Ahahahaha!"

Jessie laughed wildly without the slightest concern for her image, already lost in her fantasy as though the Sea Spirit had fallen into her hands!

James and Meowth were thoroughly accustomed to this behavior. Yet despite Jessie's determination to obtain the Sea Spirit, they could not avoid worrying.

The man and Pokemon exchanged a glance and saw deep concern in each other's eyes.

James cautiously asked, "But... the calling card we left announcing the theft didn't have the intended effect. It only made them increase security..."

"That's exactly the effect I wanted!" Jessie's eyes flashed with confidence. "This is where my brilliance comes in! The stronger their defenses and the more security personnel they have, the safer they'll feel. That means they're even more likely to lower their guard at the crucial moment. And that will be our opportunity to strike!"

Only after hearing her explanation did James and Meowth understand.

"I see! You're a genius! So all we have to do is wait?"

...

Once all four semifinalists had been decided, the next pairings were released immediately.

In the upper half, Trinity would face a female Trainer. In the lower half, Ethan would battle Zhe, the tournament's second favorite!

The winners of those two matches would meet in tomorrow afternoon's final and determine the final Sea Hero.

After eating dinner with Ash and the others, the group remained at their restaurant table to study Ethan's next opponent.

With so many Trainers eliminated, many competitors had chosen to leave rather than remain to watch. Numerous rooms had suddenly opened at the Pokemon Center. Seeing this, Ethan checked out of the hotel and moved there with Erika.

The sleeping arrangements were considerably less comfortable, but the new setting helped him focus more completely on his opponent.

They watched recordings of Zhe's matches several times. Based on his Pokemon's performances, everyone contributed observations and soon discovered a great deal.

Erika's knowledge, Brock's analysis, Misty's eye for detail, and Ash's flashes of inspiration combined so effectively that Ethan barely needed to contribute. They quickly discussed nearly every aspect of Zhe and exposed him down to his underwear.

Ethan could not help observing a moment of silence for the man.

In terms of strength, Zhe was roughly equal to Trinity and perhaps slightly stronger, but he was certainly no match for Ethan. After Ash and the others analyzed him, nearly every strength and weakness of his Pokemon had been identified!

With their help, Zhe's odds of victory plummeted further. Unless Ethan's Pokemon performed abnormally poorly, defeating him would be nearly impossible!

Yes, he was that confident!

That night, both Trinity and Zhe tossed and turned, sleeping very little. Only Ethan slept soundly.

Consequently, when he arrived at the colosseum the next morning and saw his opponent, he noticed the exhaustion on Zhe's face at a glance!

Their semifinal was scheduled first. Ethan examined the man whom the spectators considered his greatest rival in the Whirl Cup.

Zhe was around Ethan's age, roughly twenty. He had short black hair, lightly bronzed skin, a green short-sleeved shirt, and beige shorts. He also appeared calm and steady by nature.

Hmm. Even their styles were somewhat similar.

From the information they had gathered, Zhe was a native of the Whirl Islands and had already earned some local fame before entering. He had spent recent months traveling and, like Ethan's group, was collecting Badges in preparation for the Silver Conference.

Before the spectators learned that Ethan had entered, they considered Zhe the leading candidate for the championship. That belief ended abruptly once Ethan joined the field.

The semifinal rules remained unchanged: each Trainer could still use only two Pokemon.

Against Ethan's Blastoise, Zhe sent out a Quagsire.

From recordings of Zhe's earlier matches, Ethan knew this Quagsire had appeared several times and possessed considerable strength. It was almost equal to Zhe's other Pokemon, and together they had carried him successfully into the semifinals.

Quagsire was a Water/Ground-type Pokemon with a slightly plump, light-blue body that was always slick and slippery. Its two eyes resembled black dots and gave it a vacant expression somewhat similar to Ditto.

Quagsire were ordinarily relaxed and easygoing. They opened their mouths underwater and quietly waited for food to wander inside. Since they scarcely moved, they did not become hungry very often.

Their senses were somewhat dull, and they sometimes showed no concern even after bumping into something.

But no one should underestimate them because of that!

Quagsire were naturally powerful Pokemon. In a Trainer's hands, they could display even greater strength!
