Chapter 636: Misty Loses Her Temper

How's the practice going? Ethan asked them directly.

Misty and May exchanged a glance. For an instant, they both looked utterly dispirited. Neither said a word, but Ethan could feel the sudden awkwardness in the air.

Max had no such reservations. He spread his hands and shook his head as he criticized his sister. Terribly. Her poses are a mess, she and Beautifly can't work together, and Beautifly's moves are a mess too!

Huh? Ash and Brock stared at May. If things were really as bad as Max said, how could she enter the Contest?

May blushed under their gaze. She wanted to defend herself, but ever since they had arrived at the beach, her practice really had been awful. Misty and Max weren't the only ones who thought so. Even May could barely stand to watch herself.

Beautifly hadn't fully mastered its moves yet. Earlier, just after they reached the beach, it had used Gust and sent a whole group of innocent visitors running. That was one reason they had retreated here.

Ash and Brock could tell from May's expression that Max hadn't been exaggerating. Neither wanted to say anything that might discourage her further. Ash clenched a fist and said, Don't give up! You've just got to keep practicing and do your best when the Contest starts!

I know that! May shot him a look. Her gloom vanished, replaced by determination. Do you think a little setback is enough to stop me? I was practicing the whole time before you arrived. I haven't been slacking off!

She picked up three flying discs and threw them into the air at once. Beautifly, use Gust!

The spinning discs soared toward Beautifly. It beat its broad wings, raising a powerful gust almost at once. The wind caught the discs, lifted them, and sent them back toward May along a new course.

May leaped lightly. Her arms swept through graceful arcs, and she caught the first two discs with ease. Her pose was lovely, and a calm, confident smile rested on her face.

Oh? Ethan and the others brightened. Their coordination looked far better than Max had claimed.

The thought had barely formed when the third disc whistled over May's head. She was still in the air with a disc in each hand and had no way to catch it.

Everyone turned to follow its path. A rise of rocky ground stood behind the beach, with a staircase linking it to the sand. On those steps was a boy about Ash's age, with slightly wavy green hair. He had one hand on the railing and was watching them.

No one had noticed him arrive. They had no idea how long he had been standing there, and May's missed disc was flying straight at him!

Look out! May cried, her face changing. She had thrown it. If it struck someone, she would feel awful.

Ethan raised a hand, ready to have Gengar intervene at any moment. Not everyone was as tough as Ash. A disc spinning that fast could leave a nasty bruise even if it missed anything vital.

The next instant, Ethan lowered his hand. Without so much as lifting his eyes, the green-haired boy reached out and caught the disc smoothly. The effortless catch astonished Ash and the others.

Relieved that he wasn't hurt, May ran a few steps toward him. I'm so sorry! Are you all right?

The boy straightened without looking at her. He flicked the disc and let it spin on his finger, then tossed it down at May's feet.

You don't plan to enter a Pokemon Contest with a simple trick like that, do you?

May looked down at the disc, then up at him. Her apologetic expression froze, and her brows drew together.

With his hands in his pockets, the boy came slowly down the steps and stopped in front of her. They stood face-to-face. His next words cut straight through her composure.

There's nothing beautiful about you.

May flared with anger. Before she could reply, he raised a hand to stop her and continued in a smug, superior tone. My name is Drew. I'm a Pokemon Coordinator too, but don't compare yourself to me. Neither you nor your Pokemon is the least bit beautiful. Give up that ridiculous dream of entering the Contest.

What did you say? May was furious now. Anyone could see that a boy who kept throwing the word beautiful at her had come looking for a fight.

Ethan was the oldest there. Seeing May bullied, he couldn't just stand back and watch. He started forward to support her, but Misty darted past him first.

Misty strode up to Drew. She was far less inclined than May to let an insult pass. So you're here to pick a fight? Fine, let's battle. We'll see whether your Pokemon is as impressive as your mouth!

She and May had known each other only a short time, but they were already close friends, almost like sisters. Misty wasn't about to let some obnoxious kid treat May this way.

Battle? Drew looked Misty up and down and shook his head with disdain. I see. You're one of those savage Pokemon battle Trainers. Always wanting to fight. How crude. And just like her, there's nothing beautiful about y

A dark blur flashed before his eyes. Drew blinked, then opened them wide as he realized it was Misty's fist!

He forgot all about acting cool and hurried to dodge. He had barely turned his head when pain struck his cheek. She had punched him.
