Chapter 238: Poliwrath Presses On

"Begin!"

A sailor acting as referee swept down his arm. Before either Trainer spoke, Jolteon and Poliwrath both ran forward to meet each other.

The battle had barely started, but Ethan was already forming a plan. Poliwrath was both Water and Fighting type. Electric attacks would be twice as effective against it, yet its victory in a fighting tournament proved it was no ordinary opponent. Better to avoid close combat.

"Jolteon, don't get any closer! Keep your distance and use Thunder Shock!"

Jolteon braked sharply. It circled Poliwrath, adjusting its position to stay a safe distance away. Electricity crackled through its stiff fur and shot toward its opponent.

Poliwrath was not fast enough to dodge the bolt. To avoid taking a direct hit, it stopped and countered with a move of its own. An icy beam erupted from its mouth and drove through the middle of Jolteon's attack.

Ice Beam. It was a common Ice-type move among Water Pokemon, and Ethan recognized it immediately.

The attacks were unevenly matched. Ice Beam shattered Thunder Shock into fading sparks and kept going straight toward Jolteon. Jolteon darted aside just in time, letting the beam pass close by its body.

With the exchange turned around, Jolteon shook its fur and fired another Thunder Shock.

Poliwrath's breathing had grown slightly ragged. A powerful move took more energy and more time to recover from. It could not fire Ice Beam over and over without pause. This time it was still gathering itself and had no beam ready to block the attack.

The electricity came too quickly for Poliwrath to dodge. Thunder Shock struck it squarely, crackling across its body in golden arcs. Pain showed on its face.

Still, Poliwrath gritted its teeth and pressed toward Jolteon. It clearly wanted to close the gap. Jolteon was too agile, always moving away before Poliwrath could reach it.

Mugsy's brow gradually furrowed. The type disadvantage itself did not frighten him. What worried him was Ethan's refusal to let Poliwrath fight up close.

Ethan had guessed right. Poliwrath's fighting ability was exceptional, even stronger than one might expect from its Water typing. It had used that strength more than once to turn around matches against opponents who assumed they had already won. Now Ethan seemed to have seen through Mugsy's best chance.

He had to find a way to bring Poliwrath closer.

"Poliwrath! Aim at the ground beneath Jolteon. Ice Beam again!"

Poliwrath had recovered enough to use the move. Its new beam crossed the path of the first and spread ice across half the field. The deck beneath Jolteon's feet became slippery. To avoid falling, it had to slow down.

"Now! Body Slam!" Mugsy called.

Poliwrath closed in, leaped high, and spread its arms and legs as it dropped toward Jolteon with all its weight.

Ethan had understood Mugsy's intention the moment Poliwrath aimed at the deck. If Mugsy was determined to get close, Ethan would meet him head-on and see which Pokemon could take the harder hit.

Jolteon started to move away. "Don't dodge! Use Discharge right there!"

Jolteon obeyed without hesitation. It stood its ground and gathered power. A stronger burst of electricity erupted from its body just as Poliwrath dropped to within a few inches. Neither Pokemon could avoid the other now.

Crackle!

Bang!

Blinding gold light filled the deck. The spectators raised their arms over their eyes. They could not see the impact, but the sounds suggested that Body Slam had hit Jolteon and Discharge had struck Poliwrath.

Had they knocked each other out?

As the light faded, Jolteon shook its head, dazed by the blow. After a moment it recovered enough to climb back to its feet.

Poliwrath lay on the deck, smoke rising from its body. It looked scorched inside and out. Just as everyone thought the match was over, it coughed out a puff of black smoke and unsteadily stood again.

What determination! Ethan and the spectators were stunned. Poliwrath was badly hurt, yet it was still trying to fight for the win.

Was Mugsy not going to concede? Ethan waited, then looked toward him. Mugsy was frowning, but he made no move to stop the match.

Ethan could not understand it. In a fight for one's life, or a contest that mattered deeply, a Trainer might have reason to keep going even at terrible risk. This was only an informal match between travelers. Pushing a Pokemon so hard here was wrong. Mugsy was gambling with its life.

Ethan did not try to lecture him. Mugsy would have to live with his own decisions. All Ethan could do was end Poliwrath's suffering as quickly as possible.

"Jolteon, finish it. Thunder Fang!"

Jolteon nodded gravely. It too was impressed by Poliwrath's resolve. But as its opponent, the greatest respect it could offer was to defeat it without holding back.
