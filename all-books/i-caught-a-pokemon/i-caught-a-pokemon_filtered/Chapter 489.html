Chapter 489: The Storm Badge

Dragonite hurried forward. Its speed let it reach the concrete pillar before Conkeldurr could retrieve it, and it kicked the pillar out of bounds.

Rumble...

The pillar rolled right off the battlefield. Under the rules, an object that left the field could not be brought back into play.

It was a somewhat unsporting move. Everyone knew that a Marowak's bone club was its weapon. Throwing that club out of bounds might be perfectly legal, but it would still leave a faint impression of an unfair victory.

On the other hand, Conkeldurr had brought its concrete pillars as weapons. Dragonite was fighting barehanded and at a disadvantage. Taking away a weapon first was reasonable enough.

Conkeldurr grew wary. Afraid that Dragonite would throw its remaining pillar out of bounds too, it no longer dared to toss it around as freely as before.

Without an effective long-range attack, interrupting Dragonite's moves would be much harder.

Sure enough, when Dragonite gathered power for Dragon Rage, Conkeldurr hesitated over the pillar in its hand. Rather than throw it, Conkeldurr charged forward.

Dragon Rage formed quickly. A shock wave born of rage burst from between Dragonite's hands, streaked through the air, and engulfed Conkeldurr in an instant!

Boom!

Blue-green light swallowed Conkeldurr.

Conkeldurr held its last concrete pillar across its chest, trying to shield itself from the attack.

But concrete could not withstand a Pokemon's move. After only a few seconds of sustained Dragon Rage, the pillar crumbled into fragments on the ground.

Fortunately for Conkeldurr, the pillar had blocked most of the damage.

When Dragon Rage faded, Conkeldurr, its body scorched by the energy, had already closed in on Dragonite again!

"Conkeldurr, use Dynamic Punch!" Chuck roared.

Conkeldurr poured all its strength into its right fist and swung hard at Dragonite's face!

The two Pokemon were almost touching. With Conkeldurr's pillars gone, Dragonite had no reason to fear a close exchange. Electricity flashed around its hands, and it met the blow with Thunder Punch.

As the Pokemon crossed paths, both fists struck their targets.

Bang! Bang!

Dynamic Punch was a powerful Fighting-type move that always confused an opponent it hit.

Dragonite rocked backward from the blow. It caught its balance, but its head swam and its body swayed where it stood. It was clearly confused!

Yet Conkeldurr could not take advantage of the opening and land a finishing blow.

Dragonite's Thunder Punch had unluckily paralyzed it! Its movements slowed, and when it tried another move, electricity coursed through its body and stopped it in its tracks.

Neither Pokemon could move. They stood like a pair of statues guarding a doorway!

The field fell quiet. Now everything depended on which Pokemon recovered first and finished off the other.

"Conkeldurr! You can do it!!"

"Snap out of it, Conkeldurr!!"

Chuck's students instinctively cheered it on. Of the three rounds, this was its best chance to win. They could not bear to see their master's ace fall to the challenger as well!

If Conkeldurr lost, Dragonite would have fought all three of Chuck's Pokemon on its own. That would be a blow to the Gym's morale and reputation.

After more than ten seconds, the two Pokemon recovered at the very same moment.

They moved together.

Conkeldurr used the Fighting-type move Hammer Arm!

Dragonite used Aqua Tail!

In the blink of an eye, both moves struck home.

Smack! Bang!

Before anyone could make out what had happened, Dragonite and Conkeldurr flew backward and collapsed on the ground.

What!?

The students sprang to their feet and stared at the field. A long moment passed, and neither Pokemon got up. The student acting as referee looked from one to the other, then decisively raised his flag.

"Conkeldurr and Dragonite are both unable to battle! The challenger wins this Gym match!"

They had taken each other down! Ethan, Chuck, and the students all felt a pang of regret.

Chuck's three Pokemon had all been defeated. Even if the final round was a draw, it could not change his loss.

He looked at the two unconscious Pokemon with mixed feelings.

So Dragonite really did take on all three...

At least Dragonite had fallen too. That salvaged a little dignity.

Ethan walked over to Dragonite and gathered it into his arms.

At the sound of his soft voice, Dragonite slowly opened its eyes.

"You did well," Ethan told it. "That Conkeldurr was a tough opponent. You were amazing to beat it!"

Just as he said, Dragonite had defeated two opponents by itself. Then, with a body it had only just gained through evolution, it had fought Chuck's ace to a mutual knockout. That was an outstanding performance!

Footsteps sounded nearby. Ethan looked up to see that Chuck had recalled his Pokemon and was approaching, applauding.

"That was an excellent battle. Congratulations, you've won!"

As he spoke, Chuck tossed a gleaming badge toward Ethan.

Ethan caught it neatly and opened his hand to examine it.

It was the Storm Badge from the Cianwood Gym.

Shaped like a clenched fist, it symbolized strength and fighting. Beyond that, there was nothing unusual about it.

Chuck smiled. "To be honest, I haven't enjoyed a battle this much in a long time. It's been ages since Conkeldurr last fought, too. This was a rare experience. Next time, I hope you'll bring a Fighting-type Pokemon of your own to face me!"

"I will!"

Erika came to Ethan's side. She took the badge, turned it over in her hands for a while, and then placed it in his badge case for him.

The two were about to say goodbye and leave when Chuck suddenly held out a hand to stop them.

Seeing Ethan's puzzled look, Chuck laughed heartily. "It's already lunchtime. Why don't you eat with us before you go?"
