Chapter 182: Mikan Gym

After a night at the Pokemon Center in Mikan Town, Ethan and Sabrina set out the next morning for Mikan Gym on the other side of the island.

They followed a winding path beneath the palms. Looking up at trees twenty or thirty meters tall, Ethan warned Sabrina, "Watch out for falling coconuts. They're ripe this time of year, and a storm can knock them loose. Hundreds of unlucky people get killed by them every year."

He meant only to caution her. As soon as he finished speaking, a coconut fell from the tree directly above him.

Ethan heard it and looked up. It rushed toward his head, then stopped in midair barely a hand's breadth away. Sabrina had not even turned her eyes. She flicked a hand, sending the coconut aside to land heavily at his feet.

"You're welcome," she said lightly, a small smile on her face.

Ethan was struck by how much more playful she had become. He scratched his head. He had warned her, and he was the one who had nearly been hit.

Not that he'd asked for help. Gardevoir and Haunter were there to protect him.

He nudged the coconut out of the way with his foot, and they continued. Near the edge of the grove, they spotted a warehouse-like building by the sea.

"Is that Mikan Gym?" Sabrina asked.

"I think so."

Ethan started toward it. His shadow rippled, and Haunter rose out of it and floated to the base of a palm.

Haunter bent over a coconut half buried in the ground. "Did you find something?" Ethan asked.

"Heh, heh, heh. Come and look!"

Ethan and Sabrina exchanged a glance and went over. A fishing line had been tied to the coconut. Its other end ran up into the palm fronds.

A trap? Was it for hunting, catching Pokemon, or snaring people?

They followed the line with their eyes. Hidden high among the leaves was a boy of seven or eight. For a moment, all six eyes met and no one moved.

The boy had meant to wait until the visitors triggered his trap, then laugh at them. Once he saw that they had found it, he grinned and tipped over a bucket beside him.

A full bucket of cold water spilled from the tree. Ethan frowned. Gardevoir's psychic power caught the falling water and sent it straight back onto the boy.

"Wah!" His grin vanished. Soaked and startled, he lost his footing and fell headfirst from the palm.

From that height, a fall to the ground could have killed him. Gardevoir caught him in midair and held him there.

"Who are you?" Ethan asked, narrowing his eyes.

The boy ignored the question. He thrashed and yelled, "Put me down! Now!"

Ethan patted Gardevoir's shoulder. Understanding, it lowered an arm and let its psychic hold go. The dripping boy landed on his backside. He was close enough to the ground to avoid injury, but came up covered in dirt and grass.

"Now tell me who you are."

He said nothing and tried to run. As soon as he turned, a huge tongue swept across his face.

"Ugh!" An icy shiver ran through him, and he shook from the cold.

Haunter grinned. "Think you can just leave?"

Now Ethan could get a good look at the boy. Between his clothes, his spiky hair, and his bronze skin, he looked almost like a younger Brockexcept for his larger eyes.

"I'll ask again. Who are you? Was this trap your idea of a joke?"

Unable to escape, the boy shouted, "It wasn't a joke! I made that trap for challengers like you who think too highly of themselves!"

"So you're from Mikan Gym?"

"That's right! Let me go, or my sister will make you sorry!"

His sister? Ethan frowned. The boy was arrogant, and the trap suggested he had played this trick on other visitors. What a troublesome brat.

Taking Ethan's silence for fear, the boy sneered. "I've seen plenty like you. You all come here sure you can beat my sister, then leave with your tails between your legs. I can tell how weak you are. You'd better turn around before you embarrass yourselves."

Before Ethan could answer, Sabrina's expression turned cold.

"What an obnoxious child. Since your sister hasn't taught you manners, perhaps I will."

She swept an arm through the air. The boy rose high above them and began tracing wide circles, speeding up and slowing down as he went.

"Aaaah!" He screamed. Even when he begged, Sabrina showed no sign of stopping.

"Sis! Help me!"

After several circles, his cries were close to tears. A woman came hurrying toward them from the Gym.
