Chapter 645: What Have You Swallowed Now?

At ten that evening, Ethan, Ash, Brock, and Max sat on the lower bunks in the boys' dormitory on the Pokemon Center's third floor. Misty had sneaked in as well. Snacks lay in a heap before them, and they ate while they talked.

Max glanced at the wall clock. It's so late. Why hasn't May come back yet?

Should we go look for her? Ash suggested. The light in his eyes showed that sneaking out at night appealed to him.

I don't think we need to. Ethan glanced at the darkness beyond the window. May knows her own mind. If she thinks she's practiced enough, she'll come back on her own.

The others had traveled with May long enough to know he was right. She wasn't someone they needed to worry about constantly.

Then Ash remembered something. Oh, Ethan! What was that other Pokemon you said you caught? Come on, stop keeping us in suspense!

Brock remembered too and looked at Ethan curiously.

Ethan smiled. He had no reason to hide it any longer. He took out Jigglypuff's Poke Ball.

You can see for yourselves.

With Ash, Brock, Max, and Pikachu watching, Ethan opened the ball. Blue light faded, and Jigglypuff's round figure appeared before them.

J-Jigglypuff!?

Ash recognized it at a glance. This was the Jigglypuff they had crossed paths with so many times before! He sprang up, backed away several steps, and pointed at it, too frightened to get a word out.

Brock and Pikachu were hardly calmer. Both retreated, remembering the terror of Jigglypuff's song and its marker.

Only Max looked from one face to another in confusion. Why were they so afraid? Jigglypuff looked adorable.

Misty covered her mouth to hide a laugh. She remembered how she had reacted when she'd first seen it again; she hadn't done much better.

Calm down. Ethan pressed his hands downward. Look. It isn't holding its microphone.

Ash and Brock looked at Jigglypuff's hands and saw that the microphone was indeed gone. They let out a breath of relief. Without that essential prop, Jigglypuff couldn't deliver its irresistible lullaby!

Finally at ease, they sat down again and examined it with curiosity.

That's amazing, Ash murmured. I never thought you'd manage to catch it, Ethan.

Ethan nodded. I've rid the world of a menace. No one has to worry about suddenly being put to sleep by a song coming from nowhere anymore!

The group laughed and talked about Jigglypuff, recalling all the trouble it had caused them. Looking back, those painful memories were funny too.

Max pieced together the story from their conversation. He had never imagined this cute Jigglypuff was nicknamed the pink demon and had caused so much astonishment.

He kept watching it with interest. Almost as soon as it appeared, Jigglypuff went straight to a corner, opened Ethan's backpack, and began rummaging inside. It moved with such practiced ease that it clearly had done this before.

What is it looking for? Max asked.

Its microphone, of course, Ethan answered without turning around. Don't worry. I've hidden it. It won't find it.

Ash and Brock relaxed even more. They stopped watching Jigglypuff and went back to talking with Ethan and Misty.

Jigglypuff alone refused to give up. It turned the backpack over, determined to search every last corner. One by one, it tossed out the contents: medicine, unused Poke Balls, and spare clothes.

A pair of Ethan's boxer shorts landed beside Misty. She glanced at them and blushed. Not wanting Ash and Brock to see and embarrass Ethan, she quietly hid them behind her back.

When she was sure no one had noticed, she let out a breath. She considered stopping Jigglypuff from emptying the bag, but Ethan didn't seem concerned, so she said nothing.

Jigglypuff searched more than half the backpack without finding its microphone. In a sulk, it dropped down beside the bag.

Then it noticed a bare stone at the bottom of the pack. Was its microphone hidden underneath?

Hopeful, Jigglypuff picked up the stone. There was nothing below it. That made it even angrier. It hurled the stone hard at the dormitory wall.

Bad luck for Jigglypuff. The stone struck the wall with a bang, bounced back, and flew straight at its face.

It was too busy sulking to notice. Something flashed across its vision, and the hard, smooth stone shot directly into its mouth.

Gulp!

Before Jigglypuff could react, it swallowed the stone.

It gagged and coughed, clutching its throat as it rolled across the floor. The fright was bad enough, but the stone was so large that it seemed lodged in its throat. Jigglypuff couldn't breathe.

The commotion caught the others' attention. They had heard the stone strike the wall, and when they turned, they saw something rebound into Jigglypuff's mouth.

Hey, hey! Ethan hurried over, exasperated. What have you swallowed now?

He checked his backpack. Everything else was there. Only the Everstone he had picked up during his earlier travels was missing.

This was bad.

Ethan knelt beside Jigglypuff, patted its back, then held it upside down and shook it. No matter what he tried, the Everstone wouldn't come out!
