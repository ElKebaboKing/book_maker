Chapter 365: No More Words Needed

After parting from Misty, Ethan and Ash climbed the staircase and continued running toward the top of the flower-shaped tower.

By now, Ethan had recognized the pattern. Molly challenged them on every level, forcing one person to remain behind each time. She did it for no reason beyond her own enjoyment.

Another way to put it was that a lonely little girl wanted someone to stay and play with her, and her chosen form of play was a Pokemon battle.

It also showed that under the Unown's influence and Entei's encouragement, Molly had become both willful and reckless.

Tap, tap... tap, tap...

The sound of footsteps and heavy breathing echoed constantly along the stairs.

Below them, Misty and Molly had already begun battling, and explosions rang out without pause. Misty's Starmie was locked in combat with a Pokemon Molly had conjured. As Ethan and Ash passed out of that space, the ocean and sky quickly disappeared, and the crystal network once again filled their vision.

They ran upward through the crystalline passages for several more minutes. By the time they finally reached the summit of the flower-shaped tower, they were panting and their legs felt weak.

Both were exhausted and drenched in sweat. Their stamina was not lacking; the tower was simply far too tall.

Perhaps because space had been distorted, the distance they had climbed vastly exceeded the crystal tower's actual height.

By a rough estimate, they had spent more than ten minutes running up stairs from the lowest level. Yet from outside, the tower had appeared less than a hundred meters tall.

Whether it was the crystal, the manor, Entei, or Molly herself, everything here defied reality and could no longer be judged by common sense.

The two emerged through a petal-shaped opening and entered a spacious room.

This was clearly Molly's bedroom.

Ethan looked around. Although the room's style remained strange, it felt considerably warmer and more welcoming than the levels below. A large, soft bed stood in the middle of the bedroom.

Ash immediately spotted Delia sitting on the bed.

"Mom!!"

He shouted and hurried toward her.

This was a reunion between mother and son, so Ethan did not approach them. After catching his breath, he examined Delia's condition from a distance.

Just as they had suspected, Delia had not been injured or restrained, and she had regained her normal state of mind. She must have broken free of Entei's control after arriving here.

Ash had taken only a few steps toward her when he noticed a little girl lying across Delia's lap and stopped abruptly.

The girl's face closely resembled the photograph, so she was clearly Molly. Perhaps her consciousness was still battling Misty on the level below, because she slept without noticing their arrival.

"Mom... are you all right?"

"I'm fine." Delia kept her voice low, seemingly afraid of waking Molly. "Why did you come here?"

"To rescue you, of course!"

"Honestly, you... You're still as reckless as ever! You should have left something like this to the police!"

Delia clearly did not know how many perilous adventures Ash had experienced since beginning his journey. In her heart, he remained her little boy, and she did not want him risking his life to save her.

What she did not realize was that both Ethan and Ash had experienced journeys far beyond those of ordinary Trainers. They had survived dangers of every kind. Why would they be afraid of Entei and the Unown?

"I'm your son! How could I just sit back and do nothing?" Ash reached Delia, then looked at the little girl in her arms and lowered his voice. "Is that Molly? Where are the Unown? Aren't they here?"

Delia gently pointed toward the level below. "If you're looking for the Pokemon that brought me here, it left just a moment ago."

"That's called Entei. But it isn't a real Entei. It's something the Unown created!"

Realizing that Delia knew nothing about what had happened afterward, Ash told her everything they had learned about the Unown and Entei.

Seeing the two continue chatting at an unhurried pace, Ethan stepped forward. "All right, this isn't the time to relax. We should leave as quickly as possible."

Entering had been easy, but leaving would not be so simple.

Delia had barely begun to rise when Molly suddenly opened her eyes.

"None of you can take my mama away!" Her young voice was filled with fury as she looked at Ethan and Ash. Then she turned toward Delia. "Mama, don't leave me!"

"Well..." Delia looked troubled. "Molly, I'm not your mother. I'm Ash's mother! Don't you remember? You visited Pallet Town with your papa and mama several years ago..."

"No... I don't want to listen! You are my mama!!!"

Delia's words had clearly struck a nerve. Molly became wildly agitated, as though she refused to accept reality.

Under her furious gaze, countless sharp crystals suddenly burst from beneath their feet and spread rapidly outward with Molly at their center.

"Watch out!"

Ethan dodged frantically from side to side, narrowly avoiding being skewered by the crystal spikes.

More than ten seconds passed before the eruption subsided and the crystals stopped growing. By then, sharp spikes occupied almost the entire room.

Less than a meter of open space remained around Ethan. When he looked toward Ash, he discovered that the crystal formations had separated them into two different areas.

A shadow flickered, and Entei appeared beside Molly, staring coldly at Ash.

"Papa! They're trying to take Mama away!"

Ash was stunned. The papa Molly spoke of was Entei!

Entei radiated authority, looking ready to attack at the slightest disagreement.

"Leave that woman here and get out at once!"

Its tone was hostile and it looked ready to pounce, but Ash had come too far to retreat now.

He pulled Delia behind him protectively and shouted, "Not a chance!"

At this point, there was nothing more to discuss. They could begin fighting immediately.

Molly and Entei wanted to drive Ash away and keep Delia as her mother. Ash had to rescue his mother, find the Unown, and put an end to the chaos.

Whoever possessed the greater strength would have the final say.

Knowing they could delay no longer, Ethan abandoned any thought of crossing the crystals to join Ash. Instead, he shouted across the barrier, "Ash, let's split up! I'll leave this place to you. Take Delia back while I find the Unown!"

"All right! I understand!"
