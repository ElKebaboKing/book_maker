Chapter 272: Victory in Sight

"I'm your trump card?" Gengar eyed Ethan suspiciously. "I thought you always said those three foolish birds were your trump cards. Since when was it me?"

Gengar had just won him a match, and Ethan was eager to praise it. He pulled the newly evolved Pokemon close and smiled. "Nonsense. They're only the obvious choice. None of them are as thoughtful or dependable as you. You're my real trump card. No one else comes close."

Gengar had not yet learned how easily its Trainer could flatter someone. It grinned with pleasure. Behind its back, the three Great Balls on Ethan's belt shook furiously in protest. Ethan quietly laid a hand over them, trying to soothe the birds without Gengar noticing.

On the field, the referee crossed the red and green flags before his chest. "We will now change the battlefield!"

Under Orange League rules, the field changed after one side lost three Pokemon. The new terrain added variety for the audience and gave the trailing Trainer a chance to alter the course of the match. Both Trainers could take a brief rest while it was prepared.

With a lighter heart, Ethan went to a chair beneath a sunshade. He was closer to the stands now. The nearby spectators watched him with a mixture of hostility, curiosity, and awe. He could hear some of their unguarded comments, but paid them little attention. He drank water, leaned back, and closed his eyes to rest while he considered the battles still ahead.

Three more wins would earn him the Winner's Cup, the second trophy of his career.

The announcer continued filling the break. "It has been so long since we've seen the field change that I can't remember the last time. Ethan Vale has pushed Drake to this point. The Indigo League champion truly is a top-class Trainer!"

After Drake's three losses, the announcer was beginning to fear how the match might end. Praising Ethan now might prepare the crowd for the possibility of their champion's defeat.

Clack, clack...

Gears and machinery broke Ethan's train of thought. He opened his eyes. A lift lowered the rocky field, panels shifted, and a new grass field rose into place at Pummelo Stadium.

A small hill of piled rocks stood at its center. The rest was mostly level, but thick grass grew more than a meter high across it. The field looked as though it had not been used in some time.

"A grass field!" the announcer said. "It hasn't been tended lately, and look how tall the grass has grown. Usually this field is straightforward, but now smaller Pokemon can hide in the growth and strike from cover." He saw the referee signal. "The change is complete! Both Trainers have rested and had time to plan. Let's turn our attention back to the match!"

The audience looked away from the new field. Ethan and Drake were rising from their seats and returning to their command areas.

...

Elsewhere in Pummelo Town, three figures ran through streets still crowded with spectators, heading for the stadium.

"Misty! Tracey! Hurry up! I can hear the cheering!"

It was Ash, with Misty and Tracey struggling to keep up. Misty watched the boy in the cap ahead of her while she ran, breathing hard.

"Ash, wait for us! You're going too fast!"

"Misty's right!" Tracey called from behind. "And listenthe match isn't over. We can still make it!"

Ash looked at the stadium, now close by. He was desperate to get inside, but he slowed down a little.

Since parting from Ethan, the three had hurried to the Kumquat Gym. Ash had won there and earned his fourth badge. Then they had traveled day and night to Pummelo Island. Ash wanted to catch up with Ethan's progress, but more than that, he wanted to watch his match in person.

They entered the stadium and pushed into stands so full that spectators occupied the aisles. Just as they reached a place where they could see, Ethan's Scyther crossed paths with Drake's Venusaur and brought it down with a clean X-Scissor.

"Venusaur is down! It's four to zero!" the announcer cried. "Drake chose Venusaur for this grassy field, but even so, it couldn't withstand Ethan's Bug-type Scyther!"

Four to zero? Ash looked at the scoreboard. Four icons on Drake's red side were dark. Not one of Ethan's Pokemon had been defeated. Ethan was as strong as ever.

"That's wonderful! Ethan's going to win!" Misty's eyes lit up. She fixed them on Ethan, waved both hands, and called out her encouragement.

Her voice could not carry far through the noisy stadium, but some Orange Islanders nearby turned to look. They were surprised to see a girl in her early teens cheering so hard for the visiting champion.

Misty did not care. Even if Ethan could not hear her, she wanted to support him here, far from home, and cheer for the person she liked most.
