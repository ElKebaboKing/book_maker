Chapter 614: A Chance Meeting with Bea

Torchic's evolution into Combusken filled a gap in Ethan's team: he now had a Fighting-type Pokemon.

If he came across another Fighting tournament, he could enter instead of watching the excitement from the stands.

Of course, Combusken was not a pure Fighting type. In a tournament restricted by type, that might limit what it could do compared with a Pokemon that had only Fighting typing.

Never mind. He had a Fighting-type partner now!

Ichiro and Jiro approached Ethan. Both looked downcast, clearly dissatisfied with losing the Double Battle.

It was understandable. Psyduck had been such a hindrance that they felt one more push would have knocked it out. Ethan's victory seemed to owe everything to Torchic's type advantage. With a different Pokemon, the twins believed they could have won.

What the inexperienced brothers had not yet realized was that battles offered no such convenient ifs. Had Misty chosen someone other than Psyduck, they might have lost much sooner.

Ichiro sighed. We lost. Your Torchic was strong, and congratulations on its evolution into Combusken.

Jiro was less willing to let it go. I'm not satisfied with how that ended. Will you battle us again?

Ethan checked the time and shook his head.

Sorry. We've been delayed too long already. He spoke before Jiro could argue. You're Trainers too, so I imagine you're aiming for the Ever Grande Conference. If you want a rematch, we can have a proper one there.

The Ever Grande Conference?

The brothers looked at each other. Jiro was disappointed, but he could hardly insist. He watched as Ethan and Misty recalled their Pokemon.

The match is over, so we'd better be going, Ethan said.

They said goodbye and headed farther into the forest.

This time they met no more novice Trainers or wild Pokemon. They crossed without trouble and reached another town by dusk.

The map named it Verdanturf Town. It sat at the foot of the mountains. Not far to the north stood Mt. Chimney, and at that mountain's base was Lavaridge Town and its Gym.

After so much travel, Ethan and Misty were glad to spend a night resting in a town.

Nature seemed to surround Verdanturf. It lay in a valley with lovely scenery and wonderfully fresh air.

Misty breathed in deeply. It even smells faintly sweet. Isn't this close to the volcano? Shouldn't there be ash everywhere? The air's so clean, and I can't see any dust at all.

Ethan looked toward the faint outline of Mt. Chimney. Misty was right that nothing here felt unpleasant. Yet the mountain was a dormant volcano, and despite its long quiet spell, volcanic ash still drifted down from it. Verdanturf was close enough that it ought to feel the effects.

He held out a hand and paid attention to the air. Soon he understood.

It's the wind, he said with a smile. There's a warm, gentle breeze here most of the time. It keeps the ash from blowing into town.

Misty felt for it too. A light wind passed over her hand, just as he had said. It was not strong, but it carried the ash and other particles away from Verdanturf.

As they walked through town, they noticed its unusually unhurried pace. People sat together drinking tea and talking. Most were lively older residents; few young people were in sight.

They bought food and other necessities at a shop, then headed for the Pokemon Center to stay the night.

Verdanturf was small, with few buildings. From the town center, they could already see the Pokemon Center ahead.

They had barely come through its automatic doors when they met a girl on her way out.

She looked seventeen or eighteen, with fine features, short silver-gray hair cut to her ears, and a broad band over her head. She wore close-fitting black-and-white athletic clothes. Her tan skin and firm muscles gave her an air of health and strength.

A panel of black mesh covered her midriff. It was thin enough that Ethan could faintly see her navel.

He and Misty stood in the doorway, blocking her path. They stepped aside to let her pass.

The girl paused, nodded politely, and went straight out of the Pokemon Center.

Misty had never seen a woman quite like her. She turned to watch until the girl disappeared.

She looked so cool! Misty told Ethan, her voice excited.

He nodded. The girl had a cool, capable air about her.

Although he had seen her only briefly, she had made enough of an impression for him to recognize her. It was Bea, the Fighting-type specialist and Gym Leader of Stow-on-Side in Galar.

She was no ordinary Trainer. People called her a prodigy of Galar's century-old karate tradition.

But what was she doing here?

After a moment's thought, Ethan had an idea. In the anime, Bea spent her free time traveling the world to sharpen her skills and those of her Pokemon. She challenged famous Fighting-type Trainers wherever she went, in Kanto and beyond. Hoenn was no stranger a destination.

Misty noticed that he was still looking toward the door. Jealousy rose at once.

She's gone. What are you still looking at?

Ethan heard the edge in her voice. He turned and lightly rapped her on the head with his knuckles. What are you thinking? She was carrying a backpack, so she's clearly setting out somewhere. I'm just wondering where she'd go this late, when traveling is so inconvenient.

It was only a passing encounter. They checked in with Nurse Joy and soon put it out of mind.

Nurse Joy also told them Verdanturf would hold a Pokemon Contest in a little over ten days. That gave them something else to talk about.

A Pokemon Contest? Misty asked, puzzled. What kind of competition is that?

Nurse Joy smiled at the visitor from another region. Unlike a regular battle tournament, it's a competition that showcases a Pokemon's appeal. Trainers and Pokemon perform, judges give them scores, and those scores decide the winner.

That sounds interesting. Misty's eyes lit up, then she sighed. But it's not for another ten days or more? That's too bad. We're leaving tomorrow.

Don't be so disappointed, Ethan said. If we have time, we can come back when it starts. And our journey has only just begun. Even if we miss this one, we'll have other chances to watch a Contest.

That made sense to Misty. Her mood lifted, and Ethan's words, our journey has only just begun, made her secretly happier still.

She and Ethan had only recently started traveling together. There was no need to rush anything.

...

After a night at the Pokemon Center and breakfast the next morning, they left Verdanturf and headed toward Mt. Chimney.

The rest had cleared away their fatigue. Both looked lively again.

Mt. Chimney stood in north-central Hoenn and rose more than fifteen hundred meters. Even before they began climbing, the air seemed hotter.

Sweat soon soaked their clothes and dripped to the ground.

Travel near the mountain was more tiring than on the other roads. This time, however, they were not headed up the volcano. After more than half a day's walk, they reached Lavaridge Town at its foot.

Though Verdanturf was not far away, the two towns felt as if they belonged to different climates: one cool and refreshing, the other hot.

Verdanturf was a pleasant place for older people to spend their later years at leisure. Lavaridge was a hot-spring destination. Geothermal heat fed hundreds of springs, large and small, making it attractive to older residents too.

Misty had read in a travel guide that the springs had separate baths for men and women, as well as mixed baths. Her heart beat faster. She remembered the time she and Ethan had spent at a hot spring on Cinnabar Island in Kanto.

Would she have another chance to bathe with him here?

The thought made her face grow hotter. Fortunately, the weather already had both of them flushed, so Ethan was unlikely to notice.

They entered town and headed straight for Lavaridge Gym.

Despite its name, Lavaridge Town looked more like a small city than Verdanturf did.

Tall buildings stood beside older ones. The two styles blended naturally, climbing and dipping with the ground at the foot of the mountain. Hot-spring water flowed beneath bridges, sending up fine wisps of steam and giving the streets a layered look.

Everywhere they turned were men and women in yukata, laughing as they admired the scenery or stopped at stalls to bargain with vendors.

Some people lay buried in warm river sand, enjoying its fine grains and heat. A few dozen minutes there could wash away the fatigue in their bodies.

There was something related to the hot springs along every street. Misty looked around eagerly and bought a hot-spring egg for a snack.

After making their way through town, they reached Lavaridge Gym and were startled by its size.

The Gym stood beneath Mt. Chimney. Behind its high courtyard walls rose a four- or five-story building with white walls and red roof tiles. From outside, it looked less like a Gym than a hot-spring resort.

They had barely entered the courtyard when they spotted a beautiful red-haired woman training with two Slugma.

The Slugma were breathing fire at her. She was using herself as the target, repeatedly dodging their attacks.

Ethan had often seen Trainers practice alongside their Pokemon, but never one who had the Pokemon attack her directly.

The woman heard their footsteps and turned. At that moment, a burst of fire struck her and set her clothes alight.

She yelped and ran about in alarm.

Misty stared. She had assumed the woman was skilled enough to make herself a target. Apparently she was as vulnerable as anyone else.

Recovering, Misty quickly sent out Starmie.

Starmie, use Water Gun!

Water poured over the red-haired woman and put out the flames.

Ah... I'm saved. She wiped sweat from her forehead. Without even checking for injuries, she hurried toward them. Thank you so much! You helped just in time. Who are you? What brings you here?

Her questions came so rapidly that Ethan seemed to feel her heat even from more than a meter away.

He took a step back from her enthusiasm. Hello. I've come to challenge Lavaridge Gym.

The woman jumped in excitement.

Wonderful! I've waited so long for someone to challenge the Gym! she said. Oh, I forgot to introduce myself. As you can see, I'm the Gym Leader here: Flannery!
