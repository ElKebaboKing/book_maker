Chapter 344: Sabrina's Departure

Ethan recalled Dragonair and climbed a ladder from the seabed back into the Gym.

In the changing room, he removed his swim trunks and set aside the diving mask and underwater respirator. After rinsing off, drying himself, and changing into his regular clothes, he returned to the hall to meet Sabrina. Dorian arrived from the other side at almost the same moment.

When he saw Ethan, Dorian spread his hands with a bitter smile.

"Your Dragonair is incredibly strong. My Pokemon weren't a match for it at all! Is it your ace? How did you train it to become so powerful?"

My ace?

Ethan considered the question.

How should he answer? If he told Dorian that Dragonair was only part of his third line of Pokemon and nowhere near the level of his true aces, would that crush him too badly?

After choosing his words, Ethan smiled.

"Dragonair hasn't reached its final evolution yet. It's still growing. But once it evolves, I believe it will become one of my aces. As for training, I think that if you spent a little less time practicing synchronized swimming and placed more emphasis on battle training, your Pokemon could eventually reach this level too."

Expressing the same idea tactfullythis was the art of language.

Dorian understood perfectly. The powerful opponent that had defeated him so easily was merely one of Ethan's ordinary Pokemon.

The difference between them was painfully obvious. It was difficult not to feel discouraged.

Had Dorian truly chosen the wrong path by trying to become both a Gym Leader and an excellent synchronized swimmer?

They spoke briefly for a while longer. A great deal of time had passed, so Ethan prepared to leave.

The Coastline Gym had no badge or similar prize to prove that he had defeated Dorian. That removed some of the satisfaction of collecting a reward, but the visit had still been worthwhile. Dragonair had gained valuable experience and moved another step closer to evolving.

Ethan waved goodbye. With Dorian seeing them off, he and Sabrina left the Coastline Gym and called out Charizard again.

This time they would not stop along the way. Their destination was Saffron City, home of Saffron Gym.

...

Saffron City, Kanto.

By the time Ethan and Sabrina arrived, it was already the morning of the third day.

Charizard had alternated between flying and resting. The journey from central-eastern Johto to central Kanto covered more than two thousand kilometers. Excluding breaks along the way and their nightly rest, Charizard had spent nearly a day and a half in the air.

The long journey had left it thoroughly exhausted.

Ethan could have switched to Zapdos or another flying Pokemon along the way and allowed Charizard to rest. Instead, he used the opportunity to train Charizard's endurance and test its limits.

Now he had his answer. Charizard possessed tremendous stamina. Even after carrying Ethan and Sabrina so far, it looked only somewhat listless.

In the open space before Saffron Gym, Charizard beat its wings, descended, and landed.

The breeze from its wings stirred weeds in the flowerbeds and lifted the skirts of several women, drawing glances from pedestrians. Fortunately, people were accustomed to seeing Pokemon in the city, so the arrival attracted little attention.

Ethan looked at Saffron Gym.

It remained exactly as he remembered. Despite all the time that had passed, the building had not changed at all.

The people, however, were no longer the same in their hearts.

With Ethan's help, Sabrina jumped down from Charizard. She stared at the place where she had grown up and, for the first time, felt resistance toward returning.

She did not want to go inside.

Unfortunately, now that they had arrived, she knew they had to part.

She turned and embraced Ethan of her own accord for the first time.

Ethan breathed in the scent of her hair and gently patted her shoulder.

"All right. Once you go through those doors, you'll be the cold and aloof Gym Leader again. Don't let the psychic students training inside, or the challengers who fear you, see you acting like such an affectionate young woman. It might damage your authority!"

Sabrina did not care about such things, but she obediently released him.

She kissed Ethan on the cheek and softly said goodbye, then walked toward the Gym entrance. Just before stepping inside, she turned for one final look.

"My heart will always belong to you."

Her voice was gentle but solemn. Ethan froze.

Was that a confession?

He tried to answer, but Sabrina had already passed through the doorway and disappeared.

Ethan remained where he stood and waved at the empty air. He watched the entrance for a long time. Only after several minutes did he sigh and climb back onto Charizard.

Memories of every moment with Sabrina filled his mind. Without her graceful figure beside him, his heart felt empty.

Fortunately, they had not broken up. This was only a temporary separation. They could speak by phone, and Ethan could return to visit her in the future.

Sigh...

He shook his head and hid his sadness.

Where should he go next?

Should he return directly to Johto, or remain in Kanto for a while?

After a moment's thought, Ethan decided to visit Cerise Laboratory first.

He had gone to the trouble of returning to Kanto and did not wish to leave immediately. At the very least, he should greet the people who had always looked after him.

Charizard carried Ethan into the air and flew south.

After more than another hour, he reached Vermilion City. Instead of immediately disturbing Professor Cerise, he stopped at a gift shop to buy presents and fruit. Only then did he continue to Cerise Laboratory.

The laboratory had not changed either.

Ding-dong!

Ethan rang the bell. When Chloe opened the laboratory door and saw him standing before her, her eyes widened. She froze for a long moment.

Professor Cerise and the others were equally astonished by Ethan's sudden visit.

"Ethan? Why are you back!?"

Ethan smiled without explaining the exact reason. He simply handed each person a gift.

"I returned to Kanto to deal with something. I'll be leaving tomorrow, but I still had some time and wanted to visit all of you."

Then he turned to Chloe. "Why are you here? Don't you have school today?"

"Ethan, have you completely lost track of time? It's Saturday. School's closed!"

Chloe had recovered from her surprise. Seeing Ethan made her extraordinarily happy. Her face remained composed, but her racing heartbeat betrayed her.

Her answer made Ethan realize his mistake.

He spent every day wandering through the wilderness, battling or traveling. Ordinary life had long since left him behind. How could he possibly remember the exact day of the week?
