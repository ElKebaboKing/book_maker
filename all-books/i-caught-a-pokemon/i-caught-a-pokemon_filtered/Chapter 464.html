Chapter 464: The Boy Named Oliver

When the group reached the edge of the pool, Richie crouched down and asked the young boy, "Hello. Do you live on this island?"

As they studied the boy, he secretly studied them in return. Although his gaze remained full of caution, he slowly nodded.

"More or less. I live on Ogi Isle, but I often come here to play. Why? Is something wrong?"

While he spoke, Ethan's sharp eyes noticed what appeared to be a dark shadow quietly retreating through the water behind the boy.

The shadow vanished beneath the surface in the blink of an eye, but Ethan still caught sight of it.

The boy also seemed to be deliberately hiding it. He positioned his body directly in their line of sight, clearly unwilling to let them discover the shadow!

Unfortunately, it was too indistinct, and the reflection on the water further obscured Ethan's view. He could not tell exactly what it was.

Whatever the case, it had certainly been a Pokemon.

As he considered this, he felt Erika's shoulder gently bump his own.

When he turned toward her, Erika blinked meaningfully at him. She said nothing, but she had obviously seen the shadow too.

Neither of them gave any sign that they had noticed it.

At that moment, Ash heard the boy's answer and eagerly asked, "If you're from around here, have you seen any mysterious Pokemon?"

The question was so blunt that everyone except Ash clearly saw panic flash across the boy's face!

"There's nothing like that around here!" the boy answered stiffly. His wandering gaze, however, made it obvious that he was lying.

Now Misty, Brock, and Richie also realized that something was wrong, not only Ethan and Erika.

This boy definitely knows something!

Careless Ash noticed nothing unusual. Still unwilling to give up, he continued, "Then have you heard any rumors?"

Under Ash's direct questioning, the boy found it increasingly difficult to hold his ground.

"A-Absolutely none!"

The next moment, he seemed to realize that he should not keep retreating. Perhaps afraid that Ethan and the others would doubt him, he forced a smile and added, "Think about it. If there really were some mysterious Pokemon here, it would've caused a huge commotion and been caught a long time ago, right? So I think you shouldn't believe such ridiculous rumors."

"I see..."

Ash appeared completely convinced, without the slightest suspicion.

To Ethan, however, the boy's explanation only made his attempt to hide the truth more obvious.

Of course, Ash's reaction did not mean he was stupid. He was simply too young and immature to read people's expressions, and he lacked experience dealing with others.

As Ethan watched the boy, he tried to recall the plot involving Lugia. Unfortunately, those memories had grown too vague and distant. No matter how hard he thought, he could not remember any more details.

One thing was certain: this boy had to be a key figure. Ethan could even make the bold guess that the shadow that had just departed might have been Lugia!

With that thought, he stepped forward and smiled at the boy. "We've talked for all this time, but we still don't know your name."

Looking at the handsome, cheerful young man before him, the boy believed that his deception had worked and secretly breathed a sigh of relief.

"My name is Oliver, and this Lanturn beside me is my Pokemon."

Ethan smiled pleasantly. "Then, Oliver, what was the Pokemon that just left your side?"

At Ethan's question, Oliver's expression changed dramatically. Although he was immersed in the cold pool, sweat still ran down his forehead!

"I-I don't know what you're talking about..." he stammered. "You must have seen something else. It's only ever been Lanturn and me here!"

After hearing Ethan's words and seeing Oliver's reaction, the others finally realized that another Pokemon had quietly left while none of them were paying attention!

"Really?" Ethan's smile did not fade. "But I saw it very clearly. A Pokemon swam away from directly behind you."

"There wasn't any Pokemon! Your eyes were playing tricks on you!" Oliver no longer wanted to speak with them. After hurriedly answering, he began swimming away. Even as he swam, he looked back and called, "Oh! I suddenly remembered something I need to do. Goodbye!"

He actually ran away...

Watching Oliver swim farther away, Ethan shouted after him, "Even if you don't tell me, I know the Pokemon that just left was probably Lugia, wasn't it?"

This time, Oliver could no longer pretend to remain calm.

He stopped in the water and looked back at Ethan. He opened his mouth to ask something, only to swallow the words before they emerged.

"Don't worry," Ethan said gently. "We came here to protect it from being captured by bad people. We don't mean it any harm!"

Oliver was only seven or eight years old. Hearing the sincerity in Ethan's voice, he could not resist asking, "Really?"

"I wouldn't lie to you." Ethan suddenly remembered something else. "Oh, right. I forgot to introduce myself. My name is Ethan Vale, and I'm this year's Sea Hero!"

"The Sea Hero!?"

At that title, Oliver's eyes widened with delight!

To children from the Whirl Islands, the Sea Hero represented strength and courage. Sea Heroes were greatly respected and welcomed by the local people, and their status in the children's hearts was every bit as great as that of a League Conference champion!

This did not mean the people of the Whirl Islands were ignorant enough to believe a Sea Hero was equal to a League Conference champion. The Pokemon League simply possessed far less influence here than the Temple of the Sea. Moreover, no Trainer from the Whirl Islands had won a major tournament in a very long time, leaving the local people with little sense of connection to the Pokemon League.

Once Oliver learned that Ethan was the Sea Hero, his attitude reversed completely. Not even the slightest doubt remained.

He swam back to shore and suddenly found the man before him far more agreeable. All his resistance disappeared. He circled Ethan, examining him from every direction as though observing some rare creature.

Ethan waited until Oliver finally calmed down a little, then said, "All right. Can we have an honest conversation now? And could you let us meet Lugia too?"
