Chapter 806: Seeing Erika Again

After spending the night at Cerulean Gym, Ethan and May said goodbye to Daisy, Misty, and the others early the next morning.

Misty and May faced each other, reluctant to part. Both had a great deal on their minds.

Misty... May began, then fell silent.

Keep your chin up. Misty seemed to have come to terms with their parting. Though she hated to see May go, she smiled. Next time we meet, you'd better be a great Coordinator. I'll watch all your Contests on television, so don't go losing to everyone else!

May could be sentimental, but she was optimistic at heart. Encouraged by Misty, she nodded firmly.

Don't worry. I'll become a top Coordinator!

Take care.

They clasped hands tightly. They did not need to say more.

Then Misty turned to Ethan. One look at the face she loved and the tenderness in his eyes nearly brought her to tears. She took a deep breath and steadied herself. Her eyes were still red as she took something from her belt pouch and held it out.

Brother Ethan... Her voice trembled. I can't keep traveling with you. I prepared this earlier, and I want you to have it. When you see it, I hope you'll think of me.

Ethan looked down. It was a fishing lure shaped like Misty.

When had she made it? He had not noticed a thing. In the anime, Misty had given Ash a lure like this. Now she was giving one to Ethan. It was cute, though he wondered whether it could actually attract Water-type Pokemon. For that matter, he seldom found time to fish.

Whatever its practical value, it was a gift from Misty. He accepted it with care.

Thank you.

Ethan nodded to the three Cerulean sisters, then waved to Misty one last time and set off with May.

Misty watched them until they disappeared into the distance. Even then, she did not look away.

Daisy, Violet, and Lily exchanged glances. Violet finally teased her. You can stop looking. They've gone too far to come back just because you stare after them.

She expected Misty to argue as usual. Instead, Misty wiped the corner of her eye and turned with a bright smile.

What are you talking about? I was thinking about how to run the Gym. I've decided to make Cerulean Gym the strongest in Kanto!

Her sisters stared for a moment, then shook their heads and laughed.

Honestly, Misty... Still, this is good. With you here, we can concentrate on our water ballet!

Yes! Finally, someone to share the work!

...

Not long after they left the Gym, May hesitantly asked, Brother Ethan, where are we going next?

She had felt comfortable when Misty was with them. Now that it was just her and Ethan, her heart beat faster. She was nervous and unsure how to act around him.

Fortunately, Ethan was as gentle with her as ever. I was going to visit Professor Oak's laboratory, but my plans have changed. I need to go to Celadon City first.

May knew who lived there. She had met Erika near the beginning of the Hoenn journey and liked her very much, so she had no objection.

Ethan had planned to challenge the Battle Dome, take Misty home to Cerulean, visit Professor Oak, and only then go to Celadon. Tucker's campaign had told everyone about the challenge, however, forcing Ethan to see Erika sooner. He suspected the visit would demand plenty of his strength and energy.

To reach Celadon from Cerulean, they had to pass through Saffron City. Together with the roads they had already traveled, the route formed a rough rectangle. It involved some backtracking, but they were moving quickly and had lost little time. When Ethan called Ash the previous night, Ash had only just returned to Pallet Town.

Ethan already had two Frontier Symbols and was moving faster than Ash. Pallet Town was simply farther from the place where their ship had docked.

They passed through Saffron City without lingering. Ethan met Sabrina, and the three had lunch together before he and May hurried on. The Saffron Contest was drawing closer. If May did not want to miss it, they needed to keep moving.

At first, traveling alone together felt rather quiet. May was friendly, cheerful, and sweet, but she was not especially talkative. Neither was Ethan. Their first stretch on the road was subdued. Once they knew each other better, he was sure they would find plenty to discuss.

They continued west. Kanto's large cities were mostly clustered in its center. The land here was relatively flat, with dense forests but few mountains. When they tired, they rested for a while, then set out again.

May got to see a side of Kanto she had not known. Overall, though, Kanto, Johto, and Hoenn were not so different in appearance.

Will Sinnoh be like this too, Brother Ethan? she asked.

Big cities look much the same wherever you go, Ethan said, glancing up at the sky. Tall buildings, busy people. You notice the differences between regions in remote mountains, by lakes, and in places people visit on holiday.

May looked disappointed, so he quickly added, Sinnoh is a large island in its own right. It has plenty of distinctive scenery worth seeing. Mt. Coronet and its many lakes are beautiful. And the snow-covered north makes an impression you won't forget.

May pictured the places he described. That sounds wonderful. You hardly ever see snow in Hoenn. She smiled. You know so much about Sinnoh, Brother Ethan.

Ethan waved the praise away. Only from what I've read. I haven't seen it for myself. I'm looking forward to setting foot in Sinnoh just as much as you are.

The conversation eased the awkwardness between them. May began asking him questions from time to time, and he answered each one. Ethan knew a great deal, and even without battling, May felt her understanding of the world growing. Traveling with him felt quite different from traveling with Ash.

...

The road from Saffron to Celadon was shorter than the one from Cerulean to Saffron. They could see the city's outline before dark.

They did encounter a few Trainers along the way. None recognized Ethan. They simply met his eyes on the roadside and instinctively wanted a battle, as Trainers often did. Ordinarily he might have accepted, but he was in a hurry and declined each challenge.

Once inside Celadon City, he and May headed directly to the Gym.

This visit would be different from the brief meeting with Sabrina. Erika had already heard that Ethan was coming, so he could no longer surprise her. She had contacted him several times on the road to ask where he was. Now she was waiting at the Gym with a generous dinner prepared.

Like a wife waiting for her husband to come home, Ethan thought.

Celadon Gym had changed beyond recognition. A fire had destroyed most of it before Erika first joined Ethan on his travels. After a long reconstruction, it was finally complete.

If the address had changed, he might not have recognized it at all.

The old Gym had resembled a greenhouse shaped like a Gloom. The new one was a vast, modern facility. Its scale and equipment had been transformed, though it retained the Vileplume-like steel solar panels on its roof. Rising in the center of the city, it looked like an enormous conservatory.

Even from the entrance, the building was imposing. It drew the eye away from the tall commercial towers around it.

Ethan stood before the doors for a while. Then, with an uneasy heart, he entered.

The building had changed, but the staff had not. The same lively young women worked there. They recognized him immediately and stayed at a distance, whispering excitedly among themselves.

They knew how Erika had been when she returned from her travels. None believed she had come back without falling for someone. Erika had tried to conceal it at first, but how could she? Ethan's Pokemon had appeared at the Gym. Erika watched his matches and spoke to him on the phone. The staff had seen it all.

And now, knowing he would arrive today, Erika had been restless from morning onward. What more proof did they need?

Still, none of them would say it aloud. Erika treated them well, but she was their Gym Leader and owned a perfume shop. They could keep their deductions to themselves. Besides, Ethan was a champion in three regions. Any rumor about him could become enormous news. They would gossip only in private.

Someone had to receive the visitors, however. One of the older staff members stepped forward with a smile.

May I help you?

She knew perfectly well why he was there. Ethan saw the barely hidden amusement on their faces and shook his head.

I'm here to see Erika. Is she in?

Of course. Please follow me.

She led Ethan and May through the Gym's great greenhouse to a Japanese-style room. She asked them to wait outside while she went in to announce their arrival.

May tilted her head as she watched the woman go. She knew about Ethan and Erika, but she did not understand why they needed such formalities.

Ethan noticed her confusion. Celadon is different from other places. You've seen how grand the Gym is and how many people come and go. Here, Erika is a Gym Leader first and our friend second. Until we've made our relationship public, we have to follow the Gym's usual procedures.

May nodded, though she only partly understood. She had not seen Sabrina leap into Ethan's arms outside the Saffron Gym and took his explanation at face value. Adults sometimes had obligations that children like her and Ash did not have to worry about.

At that moment, the room's door opened. Erika emerged beside the staff member, dressed beautifully for the occasion. She smiled at Ethan and gave a small bow.

As Ethan had said, they had to behave normally before others while their relationship remained private, no matter how intimate they were when alone. That also suited Erika's character. Even if she and Ethan married one day, she would remain poised and reserved in public. She would never disregard onlookers as readily as Sabrina did.

You're here, Erika said.

Ethan met her tender gaze and nodded. Yes. I came to see you.

They needed no embrace or kiss. A glance and a few simple words were enough to carry all the love between them.
