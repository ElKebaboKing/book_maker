Chapter 706: You Made a Move on Her Too?

Dragonite carried them more than ten kilometers from shore and out over the open sea. Flying let them cross straight over the islands below, and at Dragonite's speed they caught up with Maxie in little more than half an hour.

The enormous orange submarine, shaped like a Crawdaunt, was traveling underwater. It was not very deep. From the sky, Ethan could see its outline and the broad shadow cast by its hull. It would have been hard to notice from the side, but from above it stood out clearly.

He breathed easier. He had known they were following the same route, yet he could not relax until he found Maxie. Now there was still a chance to prevent Team Magma from awakening and controlling Groudon. Even if he could not recover the orb, he could at least ruin this operation.

Courtney's feelings were far less simple. She had led an enemy straight to her boss. She could not bear to imagine his face when he saw her with Ethan. Would he be angry? Shocked? Disappointed?

Ethan sat in front and could not see her expression. Even if he had known her thoughts, he would only have smiled to himself. It was too late to think of returning to Team Magma. Maxie would see them together and conclude, as Archie had with Shelly, that his commander had betrayed him.

Courtney could offer a hundred explanations and still fail to remove the suspicion. Even if Maxie eventually chose not to punish her, could he trust her as he once had? Would he give her important assignments? If he distanced her or stripped her of her rank, she would feel the change and begin to question her place in Team Magma herself.

Ethan was doing this openly. He wanted to force a break between Courtney and Team Magma and weaken the organization. Only complete trust from Maxie could prevent it, and Maxie was hardly that generous.

Smiling, Ethan let Dragonite overtake the submarine and fly on toward its destination. They knew the route now. There was no need to keep following from behind. Maxie would be surprised to find Ethan there first, with Courtney beside him. That, too, would make her supposed betrayal look certain.

Neither spoke as they flew, each occupied by different thoughts.

Another half hour passed. At about four in the afternoon, they reached a remote cluster of eight or nine volcanic islands. Even the largest was only two or three soccer fields across. There was almost no vegetation and no visible crater. The land rose no more than five or six meters above the sea. At a glance, there was nothing remarkable about the place.

If Courtney had not confirmed the coordinates, Ethan would never have guessed that Groudon lay hidden here.

A message arrived from Shelly. Sootopolis City was farther away than the Hoenn mainland, but not by muchonly a dozen or so minutes more by their routes. After driving her submarine hard, she had entered the island group.

They agreed on where to meet. Ethan guided Dragonite down to the largest island.

He dismounted and surveyed the place. Apart from the wind and waves, he heard nothing. He could not see a single Pokemon. The island felt dead.

Are you certain Groudon is here? he asked Courtney as she climbed down.

She nodded. We don't know its exact position. But our instruments have confirmed it repeatedly. Groudon is deep below us somewhere.

Maxie has a lot of patience. If you've found it, why haven't you tried catching it? You have enough members. Surely you could do it without a Master Ball.

Courtney was quiet for a moment. We only located it a few days ago. We also lost some Pokemon in our last battle with you, and they haven't all recovered. We couldn't be completely sure of capturing Groudon before it escaped, so we didn't rush in.

So I helped delay you? Ethan laughed. That was lucky.

The ground seemed warm beneath his feet. Perhaps Groudon's presence explained why nothing grew here.

A shark-shaped submarine broke the surface nearby. Its hatch opened as it approached the island. Shelly leaped out and crossed the rocks in a few nimble bounds.

Her gaze passed over Ethan and settled on Courtney. Courtney stiffened.

Well, well. Shelly's voice was teasing. I never expected us to meet like this. She turned to Ethan. Was having me around not enough? You've made a move on her too?

Courtney stared at Ethan. Was that the sort of relationship he had with Shelly? Had he saved Courtney for reasons beyond stopping Maxie?

She's joking. Don't take her seriously, Ethan said quickly. He frowned at Shelly. Stop saying things that sound like something else. She'll get the wrong idea.

Did I say anything untrue? Shelly snorted. You brought both of us to this point. You can't expect me to believe your motives were entirely pure.

Her attitude toward Ethan still puzzled him. She came whenever he called and did not try to flee when she was away from him, yet she continued to needle him. Perhaps the teasing was her last form of resistance. If it stopped, she might have no resistance left at all.

If anything, you should thank me for helping you avoid a miserable end. Ethan shot her a look. Before she could unsettle Courtney further, he changed the subject. Enough. We need to decide how to keep Maxie from controlling Groudon.

Shelly quieted at his look, but could not resist one more remark. What's to decide? This is what you're best at. Send out your Pokemon. What could stop you?

Ethan rubbed his chin. That won't work this time. Right now, we know they're coming and they don't know we're here. If we reveal ourselves too early, Maxie could leave with the orb. We can't guard these islands forever. Sooner or later we'd miss him.

Courtney looked as though she wanted to speak, then held back. Until recently, Team Magma's fortunes had been hers. Now two people were discussing how to defeat her boss and her comrades in front of her. It was uncomfortable to hear.

Ethan and Shelly were too absorbed in their discussion to notice.

What do you want to do, then? Shelly asked.

That's what I'm asking you. You're supposed to be the cleverest person in Team Aqua. This is your chance to offer a plan.

Now he remembers that? Shelly grumbled inwardly. Still, she considered how to stop Maxie and recover the orb. An idea came to her quickly.

She leaned close and laid it out in a low voice. Ethan frowned. He weighed it for a while before asking, Isn't that too risky? If it goes wrong, we could make things worse.

Then don't use it, Shelly said. It's your choice.

Ethan thought again and decided to trust her. The worst likely outcome was that Maxie escaped with the orb. As long as he did not control Groudon, Ethan would have accomplished the essential part of the mission.

...

Shelly left Ethan an earpiece for communication and returned to her submarine. It sank out of sight. She would remain hidden as their last safeguard. If Maxie escaped, she would track him until Ethan could act again.

Courtney went to the far side of the island and concealed herself behind a pile of stones. Ethan was not counting on her help. Watching without interfering would be help enough. He wanted to see what she would choose: rush to Maxie's aid despite everything, or heed both her vision and Shelly's warning and side with the truth.

He watched her go. I hope she makes the right choice.

Gengar rose from his shadow. It had seen and heard everything. Are you sure it's wise to trust those two women?

Who knows? Ethan shrugged. I can't read their minds. But I do know that neither of them can go back to the way things were, whether they want to or not.

With both women in position, Ethan had to act. Maxie would arrive soon. He took a Poke Ball from his belt.

Tyranitar appeared on the island with a roar.

Ethan pointed to the highest pile of volcanic rock. Let's force Groudon out. Tyranitar, make some noise!

Tyranitar roared again and gathered energy in its mouth. It fired Hyper Beam straight into the rocks, blasting the pile apart. Turning its head, it swept the beam across the island. Explosions tore through the stone. Fragments rained down.

When the attack ended, the ground under Ethan remained still. That didn't wake Groudon?

Once Tyranitar had recovered from the recoil, Ethan gave another command. This time it used Earthquake. A shock wave spread from its feet. Volcanic rocks toppled, the sea around the island churned, and the entire island trembled.

Ethan nearly lost his footing. Gengar lifted him into the air.

As he rose, a rock formation in the distance burst apart under an immense force. A huge red figure surged up from beneath it.

Shelly's voice came through the earpiece at the same moment. Radar just picked up a suspicious contact. Be careful. They're here!
