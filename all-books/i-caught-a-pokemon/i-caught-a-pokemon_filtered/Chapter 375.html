Chapter 375: Ho-Oh

After raging for only a minute or two, the column of light exhausted its energy. It became a cloud of multicolored sparks and disappeared completely from the world.

At the center of the battlefield, inside the reception hall at the base of the crystal tower...

The explosion had finally subsided, and the earth had stopped shaking. Ash and the others, who had thrown themselves to the floor, lowered the arms protecting their heads and cautiously glanced at the ceiling above.

Only moments ago, a towering structure and thick crystal walls had stood there. Now the column of light had erased everything without leaving even the smallest fragment behind.

Who could have imagined the terrifying experience they had just survived?

The column of light had chased them all the way down the tower. Racing against time, they had used both hands and feet in their desperate descent and had nearly thrown themselves straight down the stairwell. They had escaped that irresistible power by the narrowest possible margin.

Looking back, it had been unbelievably dangerous.

Only now did they understand why Ethan had told them to hurry when they parted.

He had wanted more than for them to deal with the Unown immediately. He had been trying to keep them from being consumed by his attack.

Cool moonlight fell across them and helped calm their thoughts.

They looked at one another. Fortunately, no one had been injured.

When they looked behind themselves and saw dozens of Unown still circling there, they felt another wave of relief.

At the most critical moment, they had finally reached the reception hall. Faced with the same danger, the Unown had released their power to block the column of light and protect everyone.

They had not acted out of kindness. The Unown had also sensed the threat from such overwhelming destructive force and merely protected themselves.

The mass of energy at the center of the Unown, which Ethan had described, was completely exhausted from resisting the attack.

This was excellent news. In a roundabout way, Ethan had solved the problem of the Unown's swelling energy.

Ash stood and brushed the dust from his clothes before turning toward Molly.

The Unown had become weak, and their power sustained the false Entei. As a result, Entei had lost much of its strength and now appeared extremely feeble.

This was a rare opportunity. It was time to bring the incident to a complete end.

Ash exchanged a glance with his mother, Delia. Then mother and son walked toward Molly together to settle the matter at last.

...

Beneath the starry sky, Ethan heard the beating of wings and looked up. Moltres, Articuno, and Zapdos descended from high above and landed beside him.

Creating such an attack had exhausted all their strength. Their weakness was visible, as though every drop of energy had been drained from their bodies. They could no longer battle.

Tri-Bird Assault was more than an ultimate attack against their enemies. It was the birds' final resort.

Once they used it, failure to win would prove that they were simply outmatched and further resistance was pointless.

The technique either reversed the entire battle and defeated their opponents or left the birds powerless, unable to resist whatever followed.

Fortunately, it appeared that the three Legendary beasts could not withstand Tri-Bird Assault.

Ethan had won the final victory.

Beside him, Erika looked at the three enormous bird Pokemon and marveled at their beauty and elegance.

Previously, she had seen the three Legendary birds only in videos and photographs online or on television. She had never imagined that she would one day observe them in person from so close.

"They're so beautiful..."

Her words immediately raised the birds' opinion of her. All three turned toward Erika and decided that this woman was far more pleasant than the silent Psychic girl they had met before.

"They really are beautiful, and powerful too." Ethan smiled and nodded. "Capturing each of them took a great deal of effort."

He walked up to the three Legendary birds and stroked their feathers.

"You worked hard in this battle. Return to your Poke Balls and get some rest."

Ethan recalled all three with their Great Balls. Once he finished, he mounted Charizard again and extended a hand toward Erika.

"All right. Let's see what our attack accomplished!"

After pulling Erika onto Charizard's back, Ethan had Charizard turn around and fly toward the battlefield.

The ground below was devastated. Powdered crystal created by the blast wave covered everything. At the center of the battlefield, the explosion had opened an enormous crater.

The three Legendary beasts lay near the crater, each panting heavily and on the verge of collapse.

After landing, Ethan took three unused Great Balls from his bag and threw one toward the nearest Suicune.

Watching the Great Ball fly closer filled him with satisfaction.

The three Legendary beasts had become too weak to resist. Like lambs awaiting slaughter, they seemed certain to be captured.

At that moment, however, a streak of fire suddenly cut across the sky. It flashed past Ethan and shattered the Great Ball.

The ball burned to ash within the flames.

"What!?"

Ethan froze, then turned toward the sky. Beneath the black, star-filled night, a large bird Pokemon beat its wings and looked down at him from above.

Its unique appearance allowed Ethan to recognize it immediately.

Ho... Ho-Oh!?

Ethan rubbed his eyes and confirmed that the Pokemon before him was not a Fearow, but the genuine Ho-Oh.

Beside him, Erika covered her mouth in surprise and stared at the Legendary Pokemon, which was even rarer than the three Legendary beasts.

Most of Ho-Oh's feathers were gold and red, while its tail feathers were yellow and the feathers along the underside of its body were white. Green feathers tipped its wings, green stripes encircled its neck, and its beak was yellow. Black markings surrounded its red eyes, and a yellow crest rose from its head. It glittered with golden light, magnificent and noble.

Once he confirmed Ho-Oh's identity, Ethan hurriedly took out his Pokedex and pointed it toward the Pokemon.

The Pokedex beeped. "Ho-Oh, the Rainbow Pokemon. A Fire and Flying type and one of the large Legendary bird Pokemon. Ho-Oh is a Pokemon recorded in ancient myths. It is said to live at the foot of a rainbow and to fly endlessly through the sky on brilliant, rainbow-colored wings, forming rainbows wherever it passes. According to legend, anyone who sees Ho-Oh will be granted eternal happiness!"

Ethan had never expected all three Legendary beasts to appear during this incident. That alone had been extraordinary. Yet in the end, even Ho-Oh had arrived.

But why was it here? And why had it chosen this moment to reveal itself?
