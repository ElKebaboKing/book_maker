Chapter 495: A Detour to Charicific Valley

Lost in his thoughts, Ethan drifted off without knowing when he had fallen asleep.

When he woke, daylight filled the room. Erika's futon had been folded neatly beside his, but she was gone.

He rubbed his eyes. He had just finished dressing when Erika opened the door and came in.

Her white socks made soft tapping sounds on the tatami. She looked at him and said quietly, "You're awake? Mr. Kurt has breakfast ready. He asked me to come get you."

Erika looked as bright and beautiful as ever.

"Did you sleep well last night?" Ethan asked.

Her cheeks reddened slightly. Like Ethan, she had tossed and turned for a long time, torn between shyness and uncertainty. Only after hearing his breathing settle into sleep had she relaxed enough to drift off herself.

"Quite well. It was more restful than sleeping outdoors."

That much was true. A Pokemon Center, guesthouse, hotel, or campsite could not match the warmth of a home. Even as guests staying under someone else's roof, they had woken with much of their fatigue gone.

After Ethan washed up, they went to the dining room for breakfast. Professor Elm, whether because he had drunk too much or for some other reason, was still asleep with a hangover.

Ethan and Kurt ate and talked, chatting about all sorts of things. They drank morning tea too. The sun climbed higher, yet Professor Elm still showed no sign of waking. Ethan asked Kurt to say goodbye to the professor for him, then left the house with Erika while Kurt and Maizie saw them off.

Once they had turned out of the street and Kurt's home was out of sight, Ethan shook his head, lost in thought.

Erika noticed his expression. "What's wrong?"

Ethan sighed. "Meeting Professor Elm in Azalea Town was a pleasant surprise. I never expected to receive a Poke Ball this valuable too. I'm happy, but I also feel as though I owe someone a huge favor."

"Don't worry about it so much." Erika smiled. "As Mr. Kurt said, that Poke Ball was like a ticking time bomb in his house. He never knew when someone might come looking for it. Giving it to you removed the danger. If anything, he should thank you!"

Her words lightened Ethan's mood.

"You always know how to make someone feel better..."

"Not really." Erika blushed faintly. "I'm only telling the truth."

As they spoke, they reached the Azalea Gym. It stood on open land outside town, surrounded by green grass. The sprawling arched building looked less like a Gym than a botanical garden.

Bugsy, the Azalea Gym Leader, specialized in Bug-type Pokemon. Defeating him would earn Ethan the Hive Badge.

Ethan had settled on a strategy during the walk and even chosen the Pokemon he would send out. But as he approached the entrance with eager anticipation, he found the doors shut and a notice posted on them.

Hm?

He read it quickly, surprised by what it said.

Bugsy was not at the Gym. Early that morning, he had gone to a more distant forest to catch Pokemon. He had left a message asking challengers to wait a few days, as he would be gone at least that long.

"Well..." Erika frowned at the notice. "I didn't expect that."

She turned to Ethan. "What should we do? It looks like Bugsy won't be back for days."

Ethan frowned too. Their matching expressions made them look rather like a married couple.

"Sigh. If only we'd challenged him yesterday."

Erika hesitated. "Should we go looking for him?"

Ethan shook his head. "We don't know where he went. With so many forests nearby, finding him would be like looking for a needle in a haystack."

He took out his phone, opened the map, and studied it.

Erika leaned in. They both stared at the map in silence.

Bugsy's absence had caught Ethan off guard, but it was understandable. Gym Leaders had to train and catch new Pokemon from time to time if they wanted to keep improving and stay current.

Still, it meant a delay for Ethan.

His gaze moved over the map. Azalea Town was not far from Goldenrod City, but a round trip there would take more than three days. They might as well stay in the area.

But if they did not go to Goldenrod, where could they go?

His eyes passed over one spot, and an idea slowly brightened his face.

"Let's go here!"

Erika followed his finger. He was pointing to a valley among the mountains, between Azalea Town and Violet City. She read its name.

"Charicific Valley?"

Ethan nodded and smiled. "I've heard the Charizard there are very strong. I wonder how they compare with mine. This is a good chance to find out."

Erika had no objection. In truth, she would gladly go wherever Ethan went. The distance and difficulty of the journey made little difference to her.

With a new destination in mind, they gathered their spirits, changed direction, and set out for Charicific Valley.

The valley was a natural park among towering mountains. Because its terrain was treacherous and many fierce Charizard lived there, ordinary visitors were usually barred for their own safety.

Of course, Trainers had a way of reaching places others could not.

They left Azalea Town and headed north. After half a day's walking, the land gradually rose, the trees and other plants thinned out, and a long mountain range appeared on the horizon.

The mountains looked close, but distances were deceiving. Reaching them on foot would take much longer than it seemed.

Fortunately, the two had time to spare. They walked at an easy pace, stopping now and then to admire the scenery and enjoy the change of mood.

They finally reached the foot of the mountains after dark. Entering the mountains at night would add unnecessary danger.

After discussing it, they decided to pitch their tent at the foot of the mountains and set out again in the morning.
