Chapter 596: Steven Stone

After Ethan brought down the Team Aqua members, Mr. Stone and Kennedy came over and stood behind him.

Those people... they must be from Team Aqua, Mr. Stone said. I can't believe they slipped all the way in here. Mr. Ethan, thank you. Without you, we might have suffered a terrible loss today.

In a short time, Ethan had helped Devon Corporation through two crises, one involving Team Magma and the other Team Aqua. Mr. Stone could hardly help being moved.

Ethan had done more than defeat a few criminals. By opposing both dangerous organizations, he had drawn their attention and might have brought considerable trouble upon himself.

There's no need to thank me, Ethan said. I couldn't stand by after seeing it happen.

He sighed to himself all the same.

He did not fear Team Magma or Team Aqua. The International Police stood behind him, and he had many powerful Pokemon. Even if both groups came after him, what could they really do?

What bothered him was the security at companies like this one.

Silph Co. in Kanto and Devon here in Hoenn were multinational giants with armed guards. Perhaps because they had never faced such attacks before, they guarded their entrances and neglected the inside of their buildings.

At that moment, a light came on above the elevator at the end of the corridor. The doors opened, and more than a dozen security officers hurried out toward Kennedy.

You lot! Kennedy scolded. You're too late! If Mr. Ethan hadn't been here, they would have escaped!

The guards looked miserable, but the delay was hardly their fault. They had responded as soon as the alarm sounded. Devon Tower was enormous; gathering, setting out, and riding the elevator up to this floor all took time.

Only four or five minutes had passed. Arriving already showed that they were well trained.

Ethan spoke up for them. Mr. Kennedy, please don't be angry. No one expected Team Aqua to appear inside the tower. I happened to be right here. If I'd been elsewhere, I couldn't have caught them so quickly either.

The guards gave him grateful looks. They did not know who he was, but they could see that he had caught the intruders. Instead of taking all the credit, he was defending them.

Kennedy calmed a little, though he still glared at the security team. Even so, letting Team Aqua slip into this building unnoticed was a serious failure. I want a report from every one of you after this.

The reports were a nuisance, but better than losing their jobs. Relieved, the guards nodded and tied up the Team Aqua members. Kennedy recovered the stolen culture medium from their bags.

Then the elevator opened again. A young man strode quickly down the corridor.

Father! Mr. Kennedy! What happened?

Ethan turned. The newcomer was about his own age, dressed in a well-fitted suit. He had medium-length blue-gray hair, pale skin, and refined features, like a young aristocrat.

Kennedy stared for a moment, then greeted him respectfully. Young Master Steven, what brings you here?

Steven?

Ethan knew Steven from the anime and games. Now that he had met this immensely popular character in person, he studied him with surprise.

He also remembered that Steven was Mr. Stone's son.

Sure enough, Mr. Stone smiled. Mr. Ethan, let me introduce my son, Steven Stone. He's a Trainer too. You're close in age; I'm sure you'll have plenty to talk about.

He called Steven over and told him, This is Ethan, Champion of the conferences in Kanto and Johto.

Ethan? Steven paused, then approached with a warm smile and offered his hand. I live in Hoenn, but I've heard your name. I know you're a remarkable Trainer, and I've wanted to meet you.

Steven was the son of an extraordinarily wealthy man. Yet he was so well mannered that Ethan saw none of the arrogance people often expected from someone born into money.

On meeting him, Ethan's first impression was simply of a courteous young man. No wonder Steven was so popular in the anime and games.

Ethan shook his hand. Steven was a little shorter than Ethan, who stood over 180 centimeters tall, and looked lean and well proportioned.

You're very kind, Mr. Steven. The truth is, I've heard far more about you.

It was no empty courtesy. Steven's name was famous in the anime and games, and he played an important part in both. Few people familiar with them would fail to recognize him.

Steven did not hear Ethan's deeper meaning. He took it as a polite compliment.

After their greetings, Mr. Stone smiled. Today was my first meeting with Ethan too, yet he's already helped us twice.

Twice? Steven had only just arrived. What do you mean?

Kennedy told him about Team Magma stealing his briefcase and Team Aqua taking the culture medium.

Steven's gratitude grew as he listened, and he looked at Ethan with new warmth.

I had no idea, he said sincerely. You helped my father and Mr. Kennedy. That makes you my friend. If you ever run into trouble, please come to me.
