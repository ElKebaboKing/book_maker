Chapter 720: Single-Handedly

They're Deoxys. Misty could no longer keep the information to herself. They're Pokemon from outer space. They're incredibly strongevery bit as powerful as Rayquaza. They're rare Psychic-type Mythical Pokemon.

She did not say that Ethan owned one. She could not tell whether either of the Deoxys above the forest was his.

Deoxys? Mythical Pokemon? Ash repeated the name. He did not stop to wonder how Misty knew so much about them. Even he understood how rare they were. After seeing Ethan with so many Legendary Pokemon, Ash had envied him. If he could catch one now, perhaps he could close the gap between them.

Footsteps sounded behind him. He turned to see Rafe, Sid, and the twins hurrying toward them.

You? Ash said.

Rafe looked equally surprised. Are you going to that forest too?

What?

Don't pretend you don't know, Sid said with a grin. One look at that shape tells you the green one is Rayquaza, a Legendary Pokemon of Hoenn. And those two are fighting it and gaining the upper hand. They're powerful too. Who wouldn't want to catch Pokemon like that?

So we had the same idea! Ash remembered his defeat at the Battle Tower, and his competitive spirit flared. I'm the one who's going to catch them. I won't lose to you!

Then you'd better hurry, Rafe said. He glanced behind him. We're not the only ones after them. Some of those Trainers are well known at the Battle Tower. Keep standing here, and someone else will take your chance.

Ash and Sid noticed the others at last. More than twenty Trainers were rushing toward the river, ignoring the evacuation order. The two boys stopped talking and ran for the forest.

Brock, Max, Audrey, and Kathryn went after them. In a moment, only Misty, May, and Tory remained.

The glowing figure that had stayed beside Tory in the park had not followed him outside. It seemed unable to travel far from that place.

Tory had meant to lead everyone to the shelter at his father's laboratory. Instead, Ash had run toward the most dangerous place he could see. Tory hesitated. His fear of Pokemon urged him to stay back, but Ash had become his friend. He gritted his teeth and followed.

May looked at Misty. What's really happening?

She had stayed behind because Misty seemed to be keeping something to herself, and Ethan was missing. May could not shake the feeling that the two things were connected.

I'm not sure of everything, Misty said, watching the fight above the forest. But one of those Deoxys is definitely Ethan's. I don't know where the other came from or why they're fighting Rayquaza.

He didn't tell you?

You know what he's like. He's always involved in something big, and he keeps a lot of his plans to himself. There were other people around when he called, too. Anyway, let's go see what's happening.

...

Above the forest, Rayquaza's battle with the two Deoxys grew fiercer. Little by little, Rayquaza drew the fight toward LaRousse.

Ethan frowned. He had chosen the forest specifically to keep the city out of danger, yet the fight was moving toward it.

Through Gardevoir's telepathy, he asked his Deoxys to stop Rayquaza. It was a predator at the top of the food chain, though, and not so easily held back. The two Deoxys could defeat it together, but if Rayquaza wanted to move, they could not simply pin it in place.

Ethan saw what it was doing. The open sky above the forest favored the Deoxys. Rayquaza seemed to be heading for the city's crowded buildings, where it could maneuver among them and perhaps find a way to win.

It was clever from Rayquaza's perspective. For the people below, it would be a disaster.

Ethan stood on a tree branch with a view toward LaRousse. Something else surprised him: a crowd was coming this way from the city. Ash and his friends were among them, along with many other Trainers.

What were they doing here?

Pokemon began carrying the newcomers across the broad river between the city and forest. The excitement on their faces gave Ethan his answer. They were here to catch Rayquaza and the Deoxys, not to stop the battle.

Trust Trainers to react that way. Legendary Pokemon were fighting right outside a city. Anyone with a dream of catching one would want to try their luck, however impossible it seemed for an ordinary person.

For Ethan, they were a problem. With two Deoxys against Rayquaza, the outcome had looked nearly certain. Add a crowd of Trainers, and anything might happen.

He thought for a moment, watching Rayquaza edge toward the city, and had an idea. If the Trainers were so eager to meet a Legendary Pokemon, he would let them meet Rayquaza up close.

He gave his Deoxys an order through Gardevoir.

Steer the fight into those Trainers. Wherever the crowd is thickest, send Rayquaza there.

His Deoxys stopped attacking and concentrated on blocking Rayquaza's route. After several attempts, it turned the Pokemon away from the city and toward the Trainers crossing the river.

They had not expected that. Before setting out, they had watched the three Pokemon's course. Though the fight had been drifting cityward, its direction looked predictable. They had deliberately taken a wide route to avoid being caught beneath it, hoping to intercept the Pokemon from the far bank.

Why had the battle suddenly changed direction and accelerated toward them? A few quick-thinking Trainers sensed trouble and turned away. Most reacted too late. By the time they understood, Rayquaza was almost upon them.

Commanding a Pokemon from safety was one thing. Fighting with your own body inside the range of an opponent's attack was another. It tested both a Trainer's nerve and their survival.

Plenty of people could direct a battle calmly from behind the lines. Far fewer could keep their composure when an attack might kill them.

Boom!

Rayquaza fired another blast. It missed Deoxys and exploded against the river below. The shock wave and towering spray caught several Trainers riding Water-type Pokemon. People and Pokemon alike were thrown into the river before they could react.

Ash, Rafe, and Sid had been near the front and escaped that strike. They looked back at the others in the water, stunned.

What power! Sid sat astride his Blastoise and wiped river water from his face. That's what a Hoenn Legendary Pokemon can do? One missed attack threw up that much water and knocked so many people in. Are we really going to try to catch a monster like that?

Of course, Rafe said, his expression steady. It just removed most of our competition. We'll have more time to catch Rayquaza before they make it back to shore.

Even so, the middle of a wide river was a poor place to fight Rayquaza and two Deoxys.

The fallen Trainers had barely surfaced and had not yet climbed back onto their Pokemon when a second attack came. This time it came from one of the strange Pokemon fighting Rayquaza.

One Deoxys continued to engage Rayquaza. The other produced hundreds of copies of itself. They were not insubstantial illusions like Double Team. They seemed solid, and they attacked Pokemon and Trainers in the water and air without distinguishing between them.

That was the scene Misty and May found when they reached the riverbank. The copies were darker than the original Deoxys but could fly as it did. They used no named moves, striking at close range with their tentacle-like arms instead.

The attacks looked crude and full of openings, but they carried tremendous force. Smaller Pokemon could not match them.

May understood what she was seeing. Is Ethan trying to stop those people from catching Rayquaza?

Of course. Misty nodded. They've been told to evacuate, and they came here to interfere anyway. He has to stop them.

But some of them could get hurt. And what if they find out it was his Pokemon?

Oh, relax. Misty waved a hand. If you go looking for a Pokemon like Rayquaza, you should expect a few injuries. And how would they know? As long as Ethan stays out of sight, nobody will connect Deoxys to him.

May hesitated. Something about that did not sit quite right with her, but she accepted Misty's point for the moment.

Then her face changed. Waitwhere's Max?

They argued all the time, but he was her little brother. She scanned the bank in alarm and soon spotted him nearby with Brock, Tory, Audrey, and Kathryn.

Misty and May hurried over. The others had followed Ash to the river but had no Pokemon to carry them across, so they had stayed on the bank. That had spared them the attacks.

Max watched hundreds of copies fighting the Trainers. What an incredible Pokemon. It can make all those copies and hold off so many people by itself. I think it's the strongest Pokemon I've ever seen. Can a human really catch something like that?

And he thought this was impressive? Misty smiled to herself. If Max had seen Deoxys in that valley, holding out against Team Magma, Team Aqua, Rayquaza, and Ethan's strongest Pokemon all at once, he would know how powerful it truly was.
