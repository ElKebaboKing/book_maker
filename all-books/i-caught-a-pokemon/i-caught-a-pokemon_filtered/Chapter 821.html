Chapter 821: The Saffron Contest

Harley's unexpectedly strong performance put some pressure on May. Pressure could also be a source of motivation. Seeing a rival do so well might draw out her own potential.

Harley had given the Saffron Contest an impressive opening. The other competitors performed in turn, but perhaps his act had shaken their confidence. Most earned relatively low scores.

Ash and the others had never imagined Harley would lead the field. It was only the first round, yet his score made clear that he had come prepared.

Competitors took the stage, performed, and left. As time passed, the atmosphere in the hall grew more excited. Most of the first round was over, and fewer and fewer people remained to perform.

May had drawn the final slot, for better or worse. It gave her more time to prepare, but watching so many other acts could increase her nerves. Fortunately, she had steadied herself, and Ethan was beside her. She felt a little nervous, as anyone would, but it would not affect her performance.

When another competitor left the stage, a purple-haired woman in a red dress and butterfly glasses walked out.

Lilian introduced her as the fairy Jessibella. Ethan recognized her at once: Jessie of Team Rocket. He was briefly surprised that she had entered, then remembered Jessie's nature and found it entirely plausible.

He had no intention of stopping her. He liked Team Rocket's trio well enough. He could enjoy the spectacle and see what Jessie had planned.

Jessie released a Pokemon to begin her act. It was not one of her own, but James's Cacnea.

James had caught Cacnea in a forest near Rustboro City in Hoenn. It had traveled with the trio for a long time. Less than half a meter tall, it resembled a round cactus or a green jack-o'-lantern. A golden flower like a crown sat on its head. It had thick arms and a body covered in spines. Its silly appearance did not suggest great power, but it evolved into Cacturne, Harley's signature Pokemon.

Ethan did not know the species especially well. Still, one look at Cacnea's shape gave him an idea of its abilities.

Jessie and Cacnea faced one another. The audience waited to see what dazzling move they would perform. Then the two ran together and embraced.

The crowd stared. That was it? It was such a simple action. And Cacnea's spines looked sharp, yet Jessie had hugged it without hesitation, taking every prick. It was an unprecedented sort of performance. People winced in sympathy and wondered what she meant by it.

Lilian suddenly cried out, tears streaming down her face. How moving! This is love! Love and peace! Their bittersweet embrace has touched our hearts!

With that explanation, the spectators realized the hug might carry a depth of feeling they had missed. They supplied their own interpretations, and soon many were in tears like Lilian. A spotlight fell on Jessie and Cacnea, adding to the mood.

Even May, Ash, and the others seemed to feel the bond between person and Pokemon. They clenched their fists, moved by the scene.

Ethan might have been the only person in the hall who remained expressionless. Wasn't Jessie simply stuck on Cacnea's spines? How had everyone imagined so much more? Even the three judges were smiling and applauding.

He could not understand it. Lilian, Contesta, Sukizo, and Nurse Joy were all professionals. Surely they could see the predicament Jessie was in. Why were they responding this way?

Then Ethan saw what he had been missing, and why he had resisted the idea of entering a Contest himself. He approached things rationally. That served him well in Pokemon battles: he exposed few weaknesses, spotted his opponent's quickly, and used them to win.

A Contest demanded something more emotional. A Coordinator and Pokemon could create beauty together, an elusive feeling visible onstage but hard to put into words. That was what Ethan lacked. Perhaps the judges, Lilian, the crowd, and even May and Ash had genuinely felt something in Jessie's awkward, motionless act.

Jessie's accidental performance had succeeded beyond anyone's expectations. Was she a rare natural talent after all?

Her act was not finished. Whether she had grown used to the spines or was simply enduring them, she moved to the next part.

Still embracing Jessie, Cacnea used Pin Missile. It guided the sharp needles into the air, where they collided and burst. Hot, rainbow-colored light showered down like stars, surrounding Jessie and Cacnea. For a moment, Jessie really did look like the fairy whose name she had taken.

Gasps rose from the stands.

Amazing... May murmured beside Ethan. She studied the screen. Looks like Harley isn't the only difficult opponent. This woman is one too.

Ethan said nothing. May had not recognized Jessibella at all. She genuinely believed Jessie was a formidable Coordinator. Ethan hesitated, then decided not to tell her the truth. Given Team Rocket's usual standards, they would eventually reveal themselves without his help. For now, the extra pressure might bring out more of May's potential.

Jessie earned an extremely high score for her first-round performance. It was even higher than Harley's, putting her in first place.

Ethan looked up at the ceiling. The result surprised him, yet somehow it made sense. When Jessie took something seriously, she really could be impressive.

There was one small incident as she left. After being pierced by Cacnea's spines twice at close range, Jessie could not move at all. Lilian called for the medical staff, who carried her off on a stretcher.

That did nothing to change her score. If anything, the spectators saw it as further proof of her dedication, and their applause grew louder.

After Jessie, it was finally time for the last competitor in the first round: May.

Good luck, Ethan told her in the waiting room. I know you can do it. Just show them what you've practiced.

Don't worry. I'll do my best!

May gave him a sweet smile, rose, put aside her doubts, and walked steadily down the competitors' passage. Soon she appeared onstage.

Seeing her relaxed, confident face on the screen, Ethan focused on the performance ahead. In truth, he knew surprisingly little about his own traveling companion's plans. He did not even know which Pokemon she would use or how she had arranged her act. Leaving her to go on a date the night before the Contest had hardly made him a reliable companion.

But he and Sabrina rarely had time together. When she was so close, how could he avoid seeing her? He would have to make up for neglecting May during their later travels.

May had not complained in the slightest. That only made Ethan feel more guilty. It was why he had been so attentive to every detail earlier.

Lilian briefly introduced May and her Contest record. Then May's performance began.

Squirtle had come onstage with her, but May did not risk an untested act in the first round. She threw a Poke Ball and sent out Combusken.

She plainly wanted Squirtle to grow comfortable with Contest performances, even though it would not perform today. Ethan was struck again by how much May had grown.

Combusken spun and landed steadily at center stage. May pulled off her bandanna and tossed it into the air. Combusken used Fire Spin, followed by Sky Uppercut. The rising heat carried the bandanna high. Combusken leaped into the flames, sprang upward, and struck the spread-out cloth with another Sky Uppercut, sending it neatly back onto May's head.

May and Combusken jumped together to the center of the stage and finished with a graceful bow. The act was over.

Ethan rubbed his chin. It had shown Combusken's speed, skill, and strength, but it was more exciting than beautiful. The overall effect felt a little plain.

Combusken was a Fire and Fighting Pokemon. May had chosen a cool, energetic style rather than a delicate one. Still, Ethan could sense Ash's influence in her act. There was nothing inherently wrong with it, but a performance built only on impressive moves could struggle to earn top marks. It might also limit May's growth as a Coordinator.

Even so, the audience enjoyed it, and the judges gave her a respectable score. Ethan estimated that she was above most of the field. She should make the next round.

Moments after her performance, the screen announced the eight competitors who had advanced. May had made it in seventh place, safely above the cutoff. Jessie and Harley had taken the top two spots and advanced as well.

For May, seventh was progress. At least she had no longer qualified in last place; she had climbed a rung. The eliminated competitors had to accept their disappointment. Those who advanced had little time to celebrate before their next matches.

As in Hoenn, the second round was a series of Contest Battles. But Kanto had one important rule difference: competitors could use different Pokemon for the performance and battle rounds. One Pokemon could specialize in stage appeals while another focused on combat, improving both displays and making the battles more intense. The audience welcomed that prospect.

Shall we keep going? Lilian returned to the center of the stage with a bright smile and pointed toward the screen. First, let's see the opening matchups for the second round!
