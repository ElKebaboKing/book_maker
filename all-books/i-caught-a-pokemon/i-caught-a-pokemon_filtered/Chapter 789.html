Chapter 789: Back to Cerise Park

When the group disembarked in Vermilion City, it was time to part. For some of them the separation would last only a few days. Brock and Misty, though, had finished their journey and might stay home much longer.

Ash and Brock left first. They waved goodbye to Ethan and followed the road northwest. Each needed to see family. Ash had his mother to visit; Brock had his own household to return to. Even traveling together at the start, they would part along the way. It made the farewell a little sad.

Ethan wanted to see Sabrina and Erika at once, but he had arrived in Vermilion City. How could he leave without visiting Professor Cerise? His Pokemon were staying there too. He had to see his partners.

Misty and May came with him. Misty was back in Kanto, yet she had no intention of immediately returning to the Cerulean Gym. She said she wanted to visit Ethan's Pokemon and brought May along toward Cerise Laboratory.

Seeing the two girls whispering together behind him, Ethan suspected they had another reason. He did not think much of it. They would be parting soon, he assumed, and perhaps Misty simply wanted more time with him.

Misty had something else in mind. Out of Ethan's hearing, she leaned toward May. We'll meet Chloe Cerise soon. Be careful. She looks like a good girl, but she isn't simple. She's someone you'll have to watch out for later.

Someone to watch out for? Isn't that a little... May stopped herself. Misty seemed to see a rival behind every tree.

May knew Ethan was handsome, kind, attentive to her feelings, strong in battle, and much like the Trainer she aspired to become. He was exactly her type. But did Misty really think so many girls would fall for him? Wasn't she overestimating his appeal?

Well, May had to admit that he had begun to attract her too. She kept the thought to herself. She knew how much Ethan mattered to Misty, and Misty was becoming prickly with their own separation approaching. There was no reason to provoke her.

Ethan had not told Professor Cerise or Chloe he was coming. He wanted to surprise them. He took out a few souvenirs he had brought from Hoenn, bought more at a nearby gift shop, divided everything into parcels, and carried them into the laboratory grounds.

He had lived there for some time, and he still had a room of his own. Entering the English-style courtyard, he looked around and felt how familiar it all was.

Misty had visited before, but she had spent her time at the battle field behind the building. This was her first visit through the main entrance. She and May stopped whispering and looked around with curiosity. The enormous domed park behind the laboratory caught their attention even more than the building itself.

Brother Ethan, May asked, is this where your Pokemon stay?

Yes. Ethan nodded. This is where I received my Pokedex and officially began as a Trainer. My whole journey started here.

The beginning of Ethan's journey. May looked toward Cerise Park again. After hearing his words, the laboratory seemed almost mysterious, as if it had acquired a different light.

The three followed the stone path to the laboratory's front door. Ethan had just raised a hand to knock when light footsteps approached inside. The door opened.

A girl with long reddish-purple hair braided down her back stood there, carrying a bamboo basket full of Berries. She had been about to go out. Looking up, she found a boy and two girls on her doorstep.

It was Chloe. Ethan opened his mouth to greet her, but she blinked and shut the door again.

He stared. What was that about?

Before he could knock, the door opened once more. Chloe looked at him, blinked again, and closed it a second time. Ethan understood: she thought she was seeing things and was checking to make sure he was real.

Did she think she was still dreaming? It was not unreasonable. He had not warned her he was back in Kanto, and now he was standing outside her home.

He was about to call her name when she seemed to accept what she had seen. With a delighted cry, she flung the door open for a third time.

Brother Ethan! You're back?

One sentence and her delighted expression put Misty on alert. Chloe was not like Lisia, though. Misty might count her as a rival, but she could still talk to her normally. They would not clash as openly as Misty and Lisia did.

I'm back. Ethan smiled at Chloe. What were you doing just now? Didn't you trust your own eyes?

Chloe blushed and smiled sweetly. Of course I didn't. You came back without saying a word. Who would have expected you?

I wanted to surprise you. Ethan introduced the others. You've met Misty. This is May, a friend we met while traveling in Hoenn. She came to see Kanto with Misty. May, this is Chloe Cerise, Professor Cerise's daughter.

Hello.

Hello.

It's been a while, Chloe, Misty added.

May and Chloe shook hands. Despite Misty's warning, May liked her at once. Chloe looked as gentle as she sounded. It was hard to see her as an enemy. Chloe seemed to take to May just as readily.

Perhaps it was a matter of temperament. Misty and Lisia had disliked each other at first sight. May and Chloe felt none of that.

Ethan glanced at Chloe's basket. Were you going somewhere?

I was. Seeing him had put a bounce in her voice. It's Saturday and I have the day off, so I was going to give your Pokemon some extra Berries in Cerise Park.

The answer moved Ethan. While he had traveled, Chloe had been quietly looking after his Pokemon. A simple thank-you could not repay that care.

You've worked hard for them, he said. Unsure how else to express it, he handed her a gift. I picked up some souvenirs while I was in Hoenn. I hope you like them.

To Ethan it was a small gesture. To Chloe, the thought alone brought warmth. Thank you. She accepted the parcel and smiled at him again.

Ethan looked past her. Are the Professor, Ren, and Parker here?

Chloe nodded. They are. Would you like me to tell them you've come?

No, thanks. Ethan gestured toward Misty and May. Neither of them has seen Cerise Park properly. Why don't you show them around? I'll go in and see the Professor.

He nodded to the two girls and went into the laboratory with his remaining gifts. Chloe turned to them with a smile. Come with me.

Basket in hand, she led the way. Misty and May exchanged a glance and followed.

The park gate was only a minute's walk away. Through the enormous glass, May saw no Pokemon at all. Why is it so empty? I thought Trainers kept their Pokemon here.

Chloe used her access card to open the gate. The park hasn't officially opened to everyone yet, she explained. Only Ethan's Pokemon live here for now, and there weren't that many to begin with. He took several to the Ever Grande Conference, so only a few are left.

Inside, a long gravel path stretched before them. Small lawns lay on either side, with low hills and dense forest beyond.

After a few steps, Chloe left the path and led them through a little stand of trees to a thick patch of flowers. She pushed the branches aside. There was Ethan's Venusaur, dozing among the blooms.

It felt strange. Only days earlier they had watched Venusaur fight at the Conference. Ethan had then sent it and several other old partners back here. Seeing it in the flowers now was almost like watching it cross space in an instant.

Chloe set down her basket, took several green Berries from its full supply, and placed them before Venusaur. Wepear Berries are its favorite, she told the girls. They're sweet and crisp. Would you like one?

Misty and May shook their heads together. At Chloe's voice, Venusaur opened its eyes, looked at her, and nodded with a smile. It opened its mouth and swallowed the Berries.

Chloe brushed soil from herself, picked up the basket, and guided them farther into the park. Only then did Misty and May begin to spot the Pokemon tucked away around them.

Scizor was practicing with its blade-like arms against a tree trunk. Tyranitar rested in a pile of rocks. Jolteon chased butterflies through the forest. Blastoise soaked in the water. Crystal Steelix poked its head from a cave. Chloe seemed to know where each would be, and they came to her willingly when she approached.

The sight left Misty with an uneasy, complicated feeling. While she had been away, another girl had cared this well for Ethan's Pokemon.

They finally reached the park's largest lake. Misty's pace slowed as she recognized where they were. What is it? May asked.

Before Misty could answer, the lake exploded upward. A huge red Pokemon rose from the water.

May understood at once why Misty had stopped. She too went rigid. Its fierce appearance and enormous cold eyes made her breath catch and her heart race. The sudden arrival was frightening.

It was Ethan's gigantic red Gyarados, the unquestioned ruler of the park. It had the largest lake to itself. Even Ethan's other Pokemon, Venusaur and Blastoise included, preferred to keep their distance from this place.

Neither Misty nor May dared move closer. This was May's first time seeing it. She had heard scattered descriptions from Misty and Ash, but nothing had prepared her for its size. She had never imagined a Gyarados could be so enormous or intimidating. And could Gyarados really be red?

Chloe walked to the lakeshore without concern and threw all the remaining Berries from the basket toward Gyarados. It swallowed them, turned, and sank back beneath the water.

Chloe gathered up the empty basket. Seeing the other girls' faces, she smiled. It looks frightening, but it's obedient. As long as nobody attacks it first, it would never start a fight.

May hesitated. Aren't you afraid of it?

Afraid? Chloe shook her head. They're Ethan's Pokemon. They would never hurt me. What is there to fear?
