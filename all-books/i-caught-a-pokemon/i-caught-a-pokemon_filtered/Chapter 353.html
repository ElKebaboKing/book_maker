Chapter 353: Greenfield

Hearing that they could finally rest, Erika inwardly sighed with relief.

Despite her relaxed expression, she had been forcing herself onward. Erika was a powerful Trainer with a strong will, but her body was still that of an ordinary human woman. The long journey had left her physically and mentally exhausted.

Once Charizard landed, Ethan jumped down first and helped Erika dismount.

Their hands touched briefly. Ethan felt the delicacy of her fingers and the smooth softness of her pale skin. His mind went blank for a moment, and he instinctively gave her hand a small squeeze.

Erika did not seem to notice anything unusual. Her expression remained composed as she thanked him graciously.

A light breeze brushed her cheek. Perhaps it was only an illusion caused by her exhaustion, but even the air here seemed to carry the fragrance of fresh grass.

After sitting on Charizard for so long, her legs felt weak when she returned to the ground. She sat on a rock and gently pounded them several times. Only once the fatigue eased did she begin looking around.

In the final light before sunset, Erika saw an enormous forest behind them. Ahead stood several connected hills of modest height, all covered in thick green grass.

So it had not been an illusion. The scent of grass was real.

The sight lifted the gloom the journey had left in her heart.

Human development around Celadon City had gone too far. Skyscrapers stood everywhere, while wastewater and exhaust poured into the environment. Dense grasslands like these had disappeared. For Erika, who loved plants, the scene was wonderfully refreshing.

Nearby, Ethan took a drink of water, then opened the map on his phone and checked their location.

"Let's see... Hmm... Not bad. According to the map, if we cross those hills ahead, we'll reach a place called Greenfield. There's a small town there with a Pokemon Center where we can stay. We really are lucky. At least we won't have to camp outdoors tonight!"

"Greenfield?"

Erika frowned slightly. The name sounded familiar. She hurriedly took a travel guide from her backpack.

After turning several pages, she pointed to a photograph with a hint of delight.

"So this is where we are! It says Greenfield is a peaceful place filled with greenery. Even the wind passing through it can soothe the hearts of people and Pokemon. Many Grass-type Pokemon live there, and it ranks first among the 'Places Girls Most Want to Visit' in Johto! I can't believe my very first stop in the region brought me straight here!"

The place girls most want to visit? First place?

This was the first Ethan had heard of such a ranking.

If girls wanted to visit it so badly, the scenery must be exceptionally beautiful, or perhaps the area had some romantic legend.

And the wind could soothe the heart? Did some mysterious power exist there?

Still, Ethan glanced at the guide in Erika's hands. She had even prepared something like this. Clearly, she had done plenty of research for the trip.

After resting for a while, the two set out again.

Ethan knew Erika had spent more than enough time on Charizard, and their destination was not far away. He suggested they walk the remaining distance.

Erika did not object. She needed to stretch her legs and gladly accepted the proposal.

They crossed one small hill after another at a brisk pace. Time passed, and the sky gradually darkened. When they finally looked up again, countless stars filled the heavens.

It's already this late...

The stars looked truly beautiful so far from the city.

At last, they reached the crest of the final hill. They expected their first glimpse of Greenfield to reveal some magnificent landscape, but instead they witnessed a shocking scene.

A peaceful plain stretched below, with a quiet town at its center. Yet from a manor near the town, a brilliant blue-violet pillar of light suddenly erupted.

It appeared without warning. In an instant, it pierced the clouds and climbed into the heavens, blazing against the night sky.

Blue-violet light engulfed the entire manor. The radiance gradually consumed it until the whole estate crystallized, resembling an enormous tower.

Before Ethan and Erika could understand what had happened, a blue-violet wave spilled from the manor. It slowly spread through the surroundings and covered the entire hill.

Every building, plant, and stone touched by the wave instantly transformed into crystal, forming a bizarre crystalline labyrinth.

"What..."

Ethan and Erika exchanged a glance, each seeing the other's surprise.

"What's happening?"

"I don't know. Should we go take a look?"

They hurried toward the town and reached its outskirts a little more than ten minutes later.

Many residents had already noticed the strange light. They poured from their homes and gathered outside the manor, staring at the wave as it continued expanding and talking anxiously among themselves.

The phenomenon was so unnatural that no one dared approach. Helpless, they stood at its edge and gradually retreated as the light advanced.

Ethan and Erika joined the crowd.

The residents' astonished conversations made it clear that they were just as shocked and confused by the sudden pillar of light and spreading crystal. No one knew what had happened.

Ethan and Erika moved through the crowd to the very front.

The wave continued expanding at their feet, although it had slowed to a crawl.

After studying it for some time, Erika asked in amazement, "What in the world is this?"

"It's difficult to say, but it must be some kind of energy." Ethan looked toward the manor as he spoke. "We don't need to enter to know that an immense power is erupting inside, trying to transform the surrounding environment."

He picked up a branch and slowly extended it into the wave. The instant they touched, the branch's tip crystallized, and the transformation raced toward Ethan's hand.

He did not dare wait. He hurriedly threw the branch aside. By the time it struck the ground, it had turned entirely to crystal.

What a strange power...

As Ethan marveled, police sirens suddenly blared behind him.

He turned and saw that even the town's police had mobilized. Several patrol cars sped toward the crowd, braked sharply, and stopped beyond it.

The situation had now become a major incident.
