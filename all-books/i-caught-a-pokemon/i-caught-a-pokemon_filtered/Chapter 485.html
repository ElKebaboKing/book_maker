Chapter 485: Chuck, the Human Pokemon

After sailing west, Ethan and Erika reached the docks of Cianwood City around noon.

The city stood on the western edge of Johto, not far from the Whirl Islands. Separated from Johto's mainland, it enjoyed beautiful scenery and a pleasant climate. The city was not especially large, but its streets were clean. It looked like an excellent place for a holiday.

They did not linger and headed straight for the Cianwood Gym.

They had barely left the harbor when shouts of "Hey! Hah! Hey! Hah!" reached them from the beach.

The noise was impossible to ignore.

They turned and saw a shirtless man sparring with a Machoke!

The man had an unusual hairstyle and a thick mustache. Loose white judo trousers covered his legs, while his bronzed skin gleamed in the sun. He looked exceptionally strong.

He was human, yet he traded blows with Machoke without giving an inch. He had to be a formidable fighter!

Erika gave him a glance, lost interest, and started onward. After a few steps, she noticed Ethan was still standing in place and looked back at him curiously.

"What is it?"

"I don't think we need to go to the Cianwood Gym to find its Leader." Ethan tipped his chin toward the man. "That's probably him."

At Ethan's words, Erika studied the man again in surprise.

"Oh? That's him?"

Ethan nodded.

The Cianwood Gym Leader was Chuck, a judo master and a Trainer specializing in Fighting-type Pokemon.

Ethan did not actually remember Chuck's face. The man had so few appearances that he barely stood out even among the Gym Leaders. Still, in a city this small, someone who fit Chuck's description so closely was very likely to be him.

After all, a man who could fight a Pokemon with his bare hands was hardly ordinary. He was practically a human Pokemon himself.

They let Chuck finish his training and watched from a distance. Only when he and Machoke paused to rest did they approach.

Chuck had noticed them some time ago. As they drew near, he wiped his sweat with a towel and turned toward them.

"Excuse me. Are you Mr. Chuck?"

Chuck nodded at the young man's polite question.

"Who are you?"

Ethan got straight to the point. "I'm Ethan Vale, and this is my traveling companion. I've come to challenge the Cianwood Gym."

"Ethan Vale?" Chuck frowned in puzzlement. "I feel like I've heard that name somewhere..."

Unable to recall where, he gave up and looked Ethan up and down.

"If you want to challenge the Cianwood Gym, you must be a Trainer. It's a shame. You're young and should be bursting with energy, but you look exhausted. You haven't got a bit of spirit!"

"Spirit?" Ethan blinked.

He could not deny that he looked exhausted.

After spending the night in Maya's bed, he had barely slept. Then the long, rocking boat trip had done nothing to help. Of course he did not look energetic.

But what did his spirit have to do with challenging the Cianwood Gym?

Seeing Ethan's confusion, Chuck crossed his arms over his chest and declared with booming conviction, "Spirit means vigor and drive! As a Trainer and a man, you ought to train alongside your Pokemon and make yourself just as strong. That's what it takes to be a truly qualified Trainer!"

Ethan had no answer.

He chose to remain silent rather than challenge Chuck's view.

Everyone had their own way of training. Why should a Trainer have to become as strong as a Pokemon?

Such an approach might suit someone like Chuck, who specialized in Fighting-type Pokemon. But were Trainers of other types supposed to match their partners' strength too?

As an ordinary human, Ethan could hardly imagine ever becoming as powerful as Gardevoir or Gengar.

Chuck mistook his silence for agreement. He clapped Ethan on the shoulder and laughed. "Now that you understand, let's start by running to the Cianwood Gym!"

"What?"

"Get moving!" Chuck laughed again. "You can't challenge my Gym without showing some determination!!"

Without waiting to hear whether Ethan agreed, Chuck and Machoke took off toward the city!

Ethan watched them go, speechless.

Erika came to his side with a light smile. "Are you going to run after him?"

Ethan sighed, took off his backpack, and handed it to her.

"Every Gym has its own rules. If I want to challenge Cianwood, I'll have to follow Chuck's. I'll catch up with him. You can take your time getting there."

Chuck was already far ahead. Ethan had little choice but to set off after him at a brisk pace.

He left the beach and entered the city's streets.

Chuck and Machoke raced along like two walking mountains of muscle. The people in the street seemed used to the sight. They greeted Chuck with smiles, and he nodded back as he ran.

At least he was clearly well liked in the city.

But Ethan had been too tired the night before. After running for several minutes, he found that he could not catch Chuck. In fact, Chuck had already vanished from sight!

Gengar drifted out of Ethan's shadow and circled him with a few amused clicks of its tongue.

"That's a primitive way to travel. Want me to carry you? Chuck's so far ahead that no one would notice."

"No thanks." Ethan panted several times and shook his head. "I haven't run in ages. I'll treat it as exercise!"

Normally, his stamina would have served him better. Last night had taken too much out of him, however, and he had not rested properly. Today he was in poor condition.

Fortunately, he adjusted his breathing and changed his pace. Instead of trying to catch Chuck, he slowed to a jog.

Chuck had only said Ethan had to run to the Gym, not race him there. Arriving a little later would make no difference.

The Gym was a little over twenty minutes from the beach. By the time Ethan reached its doors, he was sweating heavily.

Still, although his body was a little tired, the run had shaken off the morning's lethargy. His thoughts were clear again, and he felt much more alert.
