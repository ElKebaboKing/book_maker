Chapter 309: A Decisive Strike

Crash. Crack...

One tree after another fell around Clair. Dragonite's relentless attacks were draining her strength, and once it destroyed the trees she had been using for cover, she could no longer evade it so easily. Several times, she escaped only by a narrow margin.

Fortunately, her Charizard noticed her danger and returned in time. It struck the controlled Dragonite head-on and sent it flying, giving Clair room to breathe.

On the mechanical spider, Vicious took another empty Dark Ball from his waist pouch. He watched the battlefield closely, ready to capture a new Pokemon.

The scene was too chaotic for a careless throw. Pokemon from both sides darted around and attacked without any predictable pattern. Unless he found the right opening, his target would dodge and he would waste one of his precious Dark Balls.

Ethan saw all of this. It was time for him to act.

The Dark Balls were the one thing making Vicious so dangerous. While he held one ready, everyone had to be wary. Take those balls away, though, and Vicious would be a tiger with neither teeth nor claws. The mechanical spider beneath him would mean little against so many Pokemon. Once Dragonite was free, it could tear the machine apart by itself.

Without drawing attention to himself, Ethan spoke softly. "Gengar, it's your turn."

Gengar lifted only half its head from Ethan's shadow. Apart from Sabrina, no one nearby noticed.

"Hey, I've been waiting forever. What do you need me to do?"

It sounded eager. Ethan narrowed his eyes at Vicious.

"The Dark Balls decide this fight. Any Pokemon that approaches him openly gets stopped by his controlled Pokemon or caught by a Dark Ball. You have to get close without a sound and strike from the shadows. Break every Dark Ball before he can react."

"Heh heh heh!" Gengar gave a wicked grin. "That's what I do best. Leave it to me!"

As it prepared to go, Ethan added a warning. "Be careful. Don't let a Dark Ball catch you. If it does, none of us will be able to beat you. And remember, smash the balls so the Pokemon under their control are freed."

"Don't worry." Gengar thumped its chest. "No one can catch me unless I let them!"

It vanished into the shadow and sped beneath the ground toward the spider.

On the open battlefield, Lance was still trying to break through Vicious's defenses. He pointed to a gap.

"Aerodactyl, go through there!"

Aerodactyl was a fierce Pokemon from ancient times. For years, people had accepted the theory that a great meteor impact had driven its kind to extinction. More recently, evidence had suggested some survived. They were occasionally found deep underground or in remote canyons.

Its gray body looked like a pterosaur from a science-fiction film, and it had a savage temperament. Ethan thought its Mega-Evolved form looked even more striking. He had never managed to encounter one himself, and he had no idea where Lance had caught his.

At Lance's command, Aerodactyl dived past Beedrill and Ursaring and came at Vicious from the left.

Vicious showed no fear as it closed in. This was exactly the chance he had been waiting for.

You think you found an opening? I left it there for you. Come closer, Aerodactyl. You're about to be mine.

Aerodactyl reached him in moments. Before it could attack, Vicious raised his arm to throw the Dark Ball.

But his plan did not survive contact with the shadow that flashed before his eyes. It came so close and so silently that the shock nearly stopped his heart.

Whoosh!

A breeze brushed past him. Before he understood what had happened, his hand was empty. He looked down. The Dark Ball had vanished.

"What?!"

He looked up and saw Gengar floating above him with a grin. Several Dark Balls were clutched in its hands.

"My Dark Balls!"

Vicious was horrified. Whose Pokemon is that? When did it appear? How did it get so close?

He had no time to think. He shouted at his controlled Pokemon, "Quick! Get those Dark Balls back!"

They never had a chance to move. Gengar laughed in Vicious's face, revealing four gleaming white fangs. Black light gathered in its left hand and formed a Shadow Ball. It slammed the attack into the Dark Balls held in its right.

Bang!

The Shadow Ball's energy shattered them at once.

"No!"

Vicious stared in fury. Ever since Mewtwo had awakened and destroyed the laboratory where it was made, these had been among Team Rocket's last Dark Balls. They were the basis of his power.

He could recruit more grunts if his followers died. He could capture more Pokemon if they escaped. Broken Dark Balls, however, could not be replaced.

With the balls destroyed, the black light vanished from the eyes of the controlled Pokemon, Dragonite among them. They paused, then regained their own minds.

Only now did Lance and Clair understand what had happened. They looked from Gengar in the air to Ethan, standing calmly below, and both breathed a sigh of relief.

"Beautifully done!"

Vicious saw that his Pokemon had come to their senses. Knowing he was in trouble, he struck a button on the spider's control panel. Thick smoke poured from the machine and swallowed everything around it.

Two thrusters extended from underneath. Fire burst from them, and the mechanical spider carried Vicious up into the sky.

"He's escaping!" Lance frowned. "Stay here. I'll go after him. We can't let him leave!"

He called Dragonite over and was about to climb onto its back when Ethan stopped him.

"Don't chase him. There's a reason people say not to pursue a desperate enemy. I suspect he still has a Dark Ball. If you go after him alone, you'll walk straight into a trap."
