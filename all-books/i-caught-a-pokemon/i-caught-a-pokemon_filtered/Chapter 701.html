Chapter 701: Fire Behind the Lines

Phoebe was already far ahead. Ethan gave Misty and Gengar brief instructions at the shrine, then followed with his Pokemon.

He crossed a dozen shallow craters left by the battle and soon reached the narrow approach to the summit. Everyone coming or going on foot had to pass through it.

Phoebe had said before the fight that the altitude and fog made the rest of Mt. Pyre's upper slopes dangerous. This was the only entrance she knew. Her original plan had been to block it and keep Team Magma below. Ethan's proposal had changed that. Their ambush had driven Team Magma back faster and hurt them more, but her home had caught fire. Phoebe could hardly call the outcome a success. Without that loss, she might have thought the plan perfect.

The narrow passage had slowed Team Magma's many Pokemon. Phoebe had caught up with them and was attacking fiercely. They fought back, filling the air with moves and explosions. Their advantage was in numbers; hers was in the strength of her Pokemon. Both sides took losses, though Team Magma continued to retreat, inching downhill. Before long, they would reach the halfway point and find escape easier.

Ethan and his Pokemon joined Phoebe. Stop chasing them. Our job is to protect the orb. Even if we defeat all of Team Magma, we don't have enough people to hold them prisoner.

Phoebe did not turn. I won't let them get away with burning my grandparents' house! At the very least, I'm capturing Maxie!

She was furious and meant to pursue him. Her family's home mattered more to her than anything. Still, she heard Ethan's concern and answered with confidence. Relax. This is the only way onto the summit. We've driven them out of it, so they can't reach the orb. And you left Misty guarding it, didn't you?

So she had noticed that Misty stayed behind. Her anger had not made her forget the larger situation entirely. But Ethan did not share her certainty about the single entrance.

Fog made it risky for a flying Pokemon to approach the summit from the open sky. Without a clear view, it could fly straight into rock. Yet there was another way. A Pokemon could stay close to the mountainside and climb along it. It would not need to navigate the fog at all.

Team Magma favored Fire types, but it also had flying Pokemon such as Koffing and Zubat. They might be too small to ride, yet some could carry a person by the claws or on a harness. A strike from behind was possible.

Ethan glanced at Phoebe and shook his head. He would follow her lead for now and hope his suspicion came to nothing.

He could have eliminated the risk. A few Legendary Pokemon guarding the shrine could withstand even Team Magma's full force. But he wanted to see what Maxie had planned. He also suspected that merely holding the orb was not his best opportunity. This might be a chance to destroy Team Magma's force in one blow. Better to turn the tables than wait for them to keep coming after him.

He let the battle continue and watched for what came next.

...

At the rear of Team Magma's line, safely away from the fighting, Maxie watched Phoebe and Ethan follow his retreat. He sneered. Strong as they were, they had taken his bait.

Even Ethan, whom the reports portrayed as such a menace, had come after them. Maxie had expected more thought from him. What could they accomplish by chasing Team Magma? They could not capture everyone. In the end, they would have to turn back empty-handed.

Team Magma's earlier failures must have owed something to Ethan's strength, but perhaps more to the simpleminded subordinates who had faced him. Maxie could not beat him head-on; he admitted that much. Ethan had nevertheless failed to notice the maneuver behind him. Strategically, Maxie considered it a victory.

The orange-haired girl still guarded the orb, but he had seen her struggle against his elite members. She had managed to fight a pair at a time largely because her Water-type Pokemon had the advantage. A commander would be more than she could handle.

Maxie checked the time. The move he had arranged should be under way.

An explosion shook the summit behind them. Phoebe's face changed.

No! She turned. Smoke rose in a thick column from the direction of the shrine. Only then did she realize that someone had attacked it. But Team Magma's people were right in front of her. Where had the attackers come from?

She hurried back with her Pokemon. Maxie ordered his members to counterattack. They pressed forward without regard for losses, entangling her team and preventing her from taking a step toward the summit. A moment ago, she had refused to let them leave. Now they would not let her go.

Maxie watched Ethan as well. Will you finally call out those Legendary Pokemon?

To his surprise, Ethan's expression did not change. Even with the shrine under attack and Team Magma holding them here, he showed no urgency and summoned none of his Legendary Pokemon.

Doesn't he care that the orb is being stolen?

Maxie did not know what Ethan was thinking. All he needed was to delay them long enough for Tabitha to seize the orb. Before entering the summit, he had told Tabitha to watch the battle from the flank and wait for an opening if they reached a stalemate. The order had now paid off.

Team Magma could delay two powerful Trainers, but not indefinitely. Even using his regular team, Ethan gradually fought a path back to the summit with Phoebe. Maxie's members did not pursue. They withdrew instead.

From a distance, Ethan saw flames engulfing Phoebe's grandparents' cabin. Misty was nowhere nearby. Only her Starmie remained, spraying water at the blaze with little effect.

Phoebe's heart sank at the sight. She had no time to fight the fire. She ran past the house toward the stone shrine.

The trees and bushes around it were burning too. Heat rolled outward, fierce enough to feel before they reached the clearing. Smoke billowed as branches crackled in the flames.

Ethan searched frantically for Misty. After scanning the area several times, he spotted her beyond the ring of fire, shielded by Gengar and several Water-type Pokemon.

She was sitting on the ground, smeared with mud. Her knees were bruised. Tears filled her eyes when she saw Ethan and Phoebe.

Ethan, someone stole the orb! she cried, pointing toward the shrine.

They looked up. Above the shrine, a heavyset man in a Team Magma uniform was rising into the air, carried by two Weezing. One arm clung to a Pokemon; the other cradled the Red Orb.

The orb! Phoebe shouted. She tried to send her Pokemon after him, but the flames leaped higher and cut them off.

The man smiled down at them, apparently untroubled by the heat, as the Weezing carried him farther away. Team Magma's orange uniforms were more than decoration. Their members worked constantly around Fire-type Pokemon, flames, and lava. The fabric resisted high temperatures, allowing them to operate close to a blaze.

In moments, Tabitha and the Weezing vanished into the fog.

Phoebe turned to Misty. What happened?

With Ethan's help, Misty got to her feet. I was putting out the fire when that man suddenly reached the shrine and took the orb. I tried to stop him, but several others attacked me from the side and set fires everywhere. It took everything I had to get out. By the time I looked up, he'd already flown away. Her voice was full of self-reproach.

Neither Ethan nor Phoebe blamed her. She had been alone, unable to guard two places at once. She had not expected to be ambushed, especially when their side had been the one to spring the first ambush.

Ethan looked at Gengar. It shook its head helplessly. It could not be in two places at once either. The attackers had appeared without warning, and Tabitha had set a fire the moment he arrived, preventing Gengar from reaching the shrine immediately.

Gengar might still have found a way to take the orb back. But when it saw Misty attacked and surrounded by flames, it made its choice: save her first. Her life mattered, and Gengar knew what she meant to Ethan. Even with the orb gone, it would make the same choice again.

This is my fault. Phoebe lowered her head. If I hadn't insisted on chasing them, we could have put out the fire sooner and kept the orb safe. I was too impulsive.

Her decision had been a mistake, but she was still young and had already done a great deal. Ethan was less despairing than either of them. In fact, he felt a little disappointed in Maxie.

That was it? He had expected something more clever than a simple lure. If Gengar had stood a little closer to the orb, Team Magma might have failed. One extra instruction from Ethan could have changed the outcome. He and the others had let their guard slip.

Then again, a simple plan could be effective. He assessed the fires and spoke quickly. This isn't the time for blame. A house can be rebuilt, and we can take the orb back. You two stay and put out these fires before they spread. I'll pursue Team Magma. Tabitha vanished into the fog, so I can't track him directly. Maxie can't have gone far. If I follow him, I'll meet Tabitha again sooner or later.
