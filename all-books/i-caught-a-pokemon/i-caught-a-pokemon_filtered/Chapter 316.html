Chapter 316: Why Isn't It Afraid of Water?

"Altaria!" Clair cried.

Before Altaria could rise from the ground, Ethan gave Crystal Steelix another command. "Crunch!"

Steelix opened its enormous jaws and bit down. Its mouth was so large that it caught most of Altaria's body.

Creak...

Everyone could hear the pressure of its teeth against Altaria's skin. Altaria screamed again, the sound making Clair's heart clench.

Fortunately, Steelix's teeth were not especially sharp, and Altaria's thick, cloudlike feathers cushioned it. The bite did not break the skin or damage its bones or organs. It still hurt terribly. Caught in those jaws, Altaria might as well have been trapped in an enormous vise.

"Altaria, break free!"

Clair's voice seemed to rouse it. Altaria gathered its strength despite the pain and attacked from inside Steelix's mouth. A violent explosion forced the jaws apart, and it escaped.

It was battered and barely able to stay upright, with little of the grace it had shown when Ethan first saw it.

Crystal Steelix stood tall, head raised. Black smoke poured from its mouth. Altaria's attack had done considerable damage; blood flowed, and Steelix was dazed for a moment. Its brain had not been injured, though. As soon as it recovered, it turned its huge head and glared at Altaria.

Ethan sighed softly. Before evolving, his Crystal Onix had been a calm Pokemon, the sort to lie quietly in a corner without making a fuss. After evolution, both its shape and its temperament had changed. It had grown more savage. Its fierce appearance now rivaled Gyarados's. Anyone faced with that giant head and its blood-red eyes would feel a chill.

Its hard, rocklike body felt cold too, without a trace of warmth. It almost seemed cold-blooded. Magikarp had changed just as dramatically when it evolved. Perhaps the more harmless a Pokemon appeared at first, the fiercer it became later.

Ethan had only ten Pokemon in his regular team, and two had already drifted toward that savage temperament. He was not sure whether that was good or bad.

Across the field, Clair clenched her jaw. Altaria looked too exhausted to go on. One more attack from Crystal Steelix might finish it.

I can't let this continue. If she lost, Ethan would lead two rounds to none. Winning three straight with her final Pokemon would be nearly impossible. She had to turn the situation around. Even if Altaria fell, it needed to take one of Ethan's Pokemon down with it, leaving her some chance in the last round.

Her gaze fell on the pool, and she had an idea.

"Altaria, Take Down! Knock it into the water!"

Ethan nodded to himself. Given the situation, it was a good choice. Altaria would be hurt too, and wet feathers would keep it from flying again. But if it pushed such a heavy Steelix into the deep pool, surely Steelix could not climb back out. Clair hoped for a mutual knockout.

Against an ordinary Steelix, the plan might have worked. Against Crystal Steelix, it would not.

Altaria caught its breath, spent the last of its strength, and charged as though it had no intention of returning.

Bang!

Crystal Steelix did not dodge. Its solid body tilted backward, and both Pokemon fell into the pool behind it.

Yes! It worked! Clair's eyes lit up. In her excitement, she did not wonder why Ethan, who clearly understood her plan, had never told Steelix to evade.

Water surged over the edge of the pool and spread across the field until it soaked the tops of Clair's shoes. She ignored it and stared into the water.

The pool churned. Then a huge, translucent crystal head rose from beneath the surface. Clair could scarcely believe what she was seeing.

"Crystal Steelix? How is that possible? Why is it all right in there?"

She had imagined two outcomes: both Pokemon falling and the round ending in a draw, or Steelix remaining trapped underwater while Altaria somehow won. Steelix emerging unharmed had never occurred to her.

Then a thought struck her. "When your Steelix changed form, did its typing change too?"

"No," Ethan said, shaking his head. "It just gained a resistance to water. When I first found it, it was already living underwater."

Clair stood stunned for a moment, then sighed. "I see. Life really is remarkable..."

Altaria had still not surfaced. Worry creased Clair's brow.

Seeing her concern, Ethan snapped his fingers.

Snap!

Crystal Steelix raised its tail from the pool. Altaria was wrapped tightly around it, unconscious.

Altaria...

Ethan gave a small wave. Steelix flicked its tail and tossed Altaria down in front of Clair. It rolled a few times, then lay limp and motionless. Water spilled from its mouth. It was completely out cold.

At the edge of the field, Kabu spared his young mistress a few moments of sympathy before raising the green flag again.

"Altaria is unable to battle. Crystal Steelix wins the second round!"

Another loss... Clair recalled Altaria. She looked at Ethan with a complicated expression, thought for a moment, and made up her mind.

"Ethan, before we start the third round, I have a request."

"A request?"

Clair nodded. "Will you send out your Gyarados for the final battle?"
