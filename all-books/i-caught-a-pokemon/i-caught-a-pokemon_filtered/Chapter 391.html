Chapter 391: Will This Never End?

Whoosh!

Two figures streaked rapidly through the air.

With a heavy impact, Venomoth and Dragonair collided in midair. A moment later, their bodies crossed and separated again.

They had fought for a long time. The battle's intensity had left both Pokemon injured to different degrees, and each was now in a precarious state.

In the Trainer areas on either side of the field, Ethan and Aya felt their hearts rise into their throats.

Victory or defeat could now be decided by a single thought. Either side still had a chance to win. Everything depended on who could seize an opening and defeat the opponent in one decisive strike.

Perhaps hoping to recover some stamina and give herself greater room for error, Aya issued another command while Venomoth turned in the air.

"Venomoth, use Leech Life!"

At the command, Ethan's eyes sharpened. He realized that the moment to decide the match had arrived.

Leech Life inflicted considerable damage and restored its user's stamina. If an attack like that connected, Dragonair's current condition meant it would almost certainly be knocked out immediately.

His thoughts raced, and he decisively gave his final command.

"Dragonair, use Aqua Tail!"

Venomoth flew toward Dragonair at tremendous speed. At the same time, it opened its mouth and revealed sharp fangs aimed directly at Dragonair's neck.

Dragonair, meanwhile, fixed its eyes on its opponent and gathered energy into its tail, fully prepared for the clash.

The students held their breath. As Trainers themselves, they understood that this was the critical moment that would decide the outcome. None dared make a sound, afraid that even the slightest noise might affect the battle.

But who would win?

Would Ethan extend his victories from the first two rounds and defeat Aya in one sweep?

Or would Aya win and force the match into another round?

The suspense was almost unbearable!

Several seconds later, beneath the gaze of hundreds of eyes, the two Pokemon finally collided again.

In that instant, Dragonair twisted its body like a rope and avoided Venomoth's gaping jaws. Its tail, wrapped in rushing water, simultaneously whipped into Venomoth with tremendous force.

Smack!

Venomoth flew backward even faster than it had approached and crashed to the ground.

Everyone looked closer and saw spirals in its eyes. It had clearly lost consciousness.

"Venomoth!"

Aya froze, then hurried forward and gathered Venomoth into her arms.

She looked up at Ethan and opened her mouth. In the end, she could only sigh helplessly.

"I lost..."

She had found it impossible to accept losing both previous rounds without a single victory. In this final battle, however, Venomoth had possessed a real chance to win and had allowed her to glimpse hope.

Although it had unfortunately lost in the end, the performance made her feel somewhat better.

At least she had not been crushed one-sidedly. Her efforts had not been wasted.

Her inability to defeat Ethan did not mean she was too weak. He was simply more skilled.

And she also understood perfectly well that even victory in this round would have changed little.

Given Venomoth's condition, it could never have continued against Ethan's Charizard and Jolteon.

Perhaps this was already the best possible result.

The headmaster raised his right hand high.

"Ethan wins this match!"

The watching students applauded and cheered. Their response showed respect and admiration for the victor, as well as recognition and encouragement for the defeated Trainer.

Amid the cheers, Ethan walked onto the battlefield. He first recalled Dragonair into its Poke Ball, then looked toward Aya.

"Your Venomoth was trained extremely well, and that battle was genuinely exciting. If Dragonair hadn't twisted its body at the last instant and prevented Leech Life from connecting, you would certainly have won that round!"

What did true modesty look like?

This was it!

Anyone who had not witnessed the earlier rounds might hear Ethan's words and assume this had been the decisive battle. Everyone present, however, knew that he still had two Pokemon in nearly perfect condition. Losing this round would hardly have mattered.

"I don't need you to comfort me!" Aya rose and met Ethan's eyes. "You were stronger. I accept my defeat in this battle completely."

Perhaps even she failed to notice that her tone had unconsciously softened.

Recognizing the change, Ethan smiled silently.

He had not actually praised her merely to flatter or comfort her.

Aya had truly performed well, and Venomoth had fought brilliantly. Fortunately, Dragonair had endured the pressure at the end and secured a narrow victory without mishap.

If Muk and Ariados had been a little stronger, they really might have caused Ethan as much trouble as Venomoth.

Of course, even then, Aya still could not have defeated him!

Whatever the case, the matter was finally over. At last, this woman would stop pestering him.

But just as the thought crossed his mind, Aya seemed to see straight through it and suddenly smiled.

"Don't get too pleased with yourself. Once I finish my studies here, I'll travel from place to place for further training. The next time we meet, I will defeat you!"

What!?

Ethan stared at her in speechless shock.

Will this never end?

How is there still going to be a next time!?

Please, spare me...

...

That afternoon, Ethan and Erika ate lunch at the academy before leaving.

Many people, including the headmaster and Aya, came to see them off.

At the academy gate, Ethan and Erika waved farewell. Then they turned and continued west without looking back.

They walked side by side along a forest path beneath the sparse trees.

After the battle, Erika had received a free trial beauty treatment. The spider silk treatment had left her face exceptionally smooth, adding even more radiance to her beauty.

Ethan, by contrast, remained silent and appeared weighed down by worry.

Erika was clearly in an excellent mood. Seeing his expression, she could not help finding it amusing.

"What's wrong? Why do you look so troubled?"

"Sigh..." Ethan released a long breath. "I thought the matter would end here. I never imagined that woman would keep challenging me in the future. She's truly difficult to handle."

At his words, Erika covered her mouth and laughed softly. "A beautiful woman keeping you in her thoughtsisn't that supposed to be a good thing for men?"

"No, thank you." Ethan hurriedly shook his head. "After defeating her over and over, I'm afraid that the next time we meet, her shuriken will fly directly at my head. Honestly, I'd rather we had never visited this place. If I could choose again, I'd take a wide detour around it..."
