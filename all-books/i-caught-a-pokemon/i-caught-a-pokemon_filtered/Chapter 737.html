Chapter 737: The Great Battle

The answer came quickly.

Ethan tossed the Ultra Ball high. Blue light flashed, and a long, dragonlike Pokemon appeared in the sky, its body gleaming black as ink.

Shelly, Maxie, Archie, Matt, and Courtney aboard the ship all recognized it at a glance. So did the Team Magma and Team Aqua members who had fallen into the water. Their mouths hung open and their eyes widened in disbelief.

R-Rayquaza!?

It was impossible. Insane. How could this man have a Rayquazaand a black Shiny Rayquaza at that?

Everyone was stunned, but Maxie and Archie in particular felt as though everything they knew had been overturned. Ethan had only recently acquired Deoxys. How much time had passed since then? And now he had caught Rayquaza too? Were Legendary Pokemon just lying around for him to find?

Shelly was surprised as well, though she recovered sooner. When they had last parted, Ethan had said he would try his luck at Sky Pillar. She simply had not expected him to find the place and return with a black Rayquaza.

Rayquaza circled overhead and roared. The cry rang across the battlefield, drawing the attention of Primal Groudon and Primal Kyogre at once.

It was not the Rayquaza they had encountered before, but they knew the species well enough to recognize that hateful presence. After a glance, however, both turned away. Like Deoxys, a Rayquaza that had not Mega Evolved was no equal to them in their current forms.

They had overlooked something. Deoxys and the black Rayquaza were on the same side. They shared an objective and had no reason to fight each other. More than that, they had a Trainer.

Having a Trainer meant more than ceasing to be wild. Under a Trainer's direction, Pokemon could act with far better judgment and make much more of their strength.

Until now, Ethan had been occupied with Team Aqua and Team Magma and had let Deoxys attack as it pleased. With both organizations routed, he could give the ancient Pokemon his full attention.

He recalled Raikou and Suicune, then signaled Shelly and Misty to call back their Pokemon too. Only when that was done did he look toward the distant battlefield.

The real fight was just beginning.

Deoxys, Rayquaza, attack Primal Kyogre together!

That's it. Deoxys, keep striking its belly. It's vulnerable there!

Rayquaza, leave Primal Groudon alone. Let's take down one opponent first!

Rayquaza, come back! I said to leave Groudon and go after Kyogre!

All four Pokemon clashed. Outwardly, Ethan looked composed, as though he had the whole battle under control. Apart from Gardevoir, no one knew how exhausting it was to direct themmostly because of Rayquaza.

He had caught it too recently. They had no dependable bond yet and had barely communicated. Rayquaza was fighting the two primal Pokemon because it sensed their power and, by nature, wanted to challenge them. It was hardly obeying Ethan.

Still, Rayquaza had entered a Poke Ball. Whether it had been hours or days, it was no longer a wild Pokemon. They would need time to get used to each other, but it had made no attempt to flee. It even seemed to understand that Deoxys was now its ally, rather than seeking revenge for its earlier defeat.

For now, Ethan was satisfied. He had never expected to direct Rayquaza as easily as his other Pokemon. He only needed it to unleash its strength.

Primal Reversion had greatly increased Kyogre's and Groudon's abilities, but neither could endure unlimited punishment. Rayquaza and Deoxys in Attack Forme were both exceptionally powerful attackers. If Rayquaza drew their attention and gave Deoxys room to land its blows, it would have done everything Ethan needed.

That was exactly what happened. Groudon and Kyogre might have dismissed Rayquaza at first, but its relentless attacks were impossible to ignore. After another fierce hit, Primal Kyogre turned to face it.

That was Ethan's opening. With Kyogre's back exposed, he ordered Deoxys to gather power for a heavy strike.

Primal Groudon went after Deoxys at that very moment. Deoxys had to dodge, losing its chance to attack.

He could not relax for an instant.

Unlike Kyogre, Groudon had been hastily controlled with an orb by Archie. The attempt had failed and driven it into a frenzy. It had lost all restraint. In its confused mind, whoever stood before it was a target.

When Kyogre went after Rayquaza, Deoxys naturally became Groudon's first choice.

So it would not be simple. Ethan could accept the missed opportunity. A fight of this intensity required patience. If he waited without rushing, another opening would come.

For now, he had Deoxys Teleport to Kyogre's far side, putting Kyogre between it and Groudon. Groudon promptly switched targets and attacked Kyogre again.

That was what a Trainer could do. Ethan's wider view let him choose a position that protected Deoxys and turned Groudon against its old rival. A wild Pokemon might have dodged Groudon's charge, but it would not necessarily have placed Kyogre in its path. Now Kyogre faced enemies on three sides.

Kyo!

Groudon's blow struck, and Kyogre cried out in pain. The scene stirred a bitter memory. Just days ago, it had faced the same three-sided assault and eventually fled in defeat. Was it happening again already?

Agitated, Kyogre drew on a tremendous reserve of power. Energy began to gather in its mouth. At almost the same moment, as though sensing its intent, Groudon started building power too.

Oh no! Shelly cried from afar. Origin Pulse and Precipice Blades!

She had seen Kyogre and Groudon use their signature moves against each other before. The resulting explosion had left her deeply wary of both attacks. And they had been in their ordinary forms then. Now both had undergone Primal Reversion. The impact would be far worse. Worse still, the moves were unmistakable; there was no mistaking what was coming.

Archie and Maxie had witnessed that previous collision too. Their faces changed as they realized they were once again within range. Ordering such a move against someone else was one thing. Being helpless in its path was quite another. Neither wanted to be obliterated by it.

Ethan understood the danger as well. Even if Deoxys and Rayquaza stopped one of them, the other would still release its attack. They would have to get clear.

He ordered Deoxys and Rayquaza away through Gardevoir's telepathy, then said to Shelly and Misty, I don't think we're safe here. Let's move back.

Neither objected. Taking the three prisoners with them, they hurried toward the far side of the small island. Ethan also warned the three Legendary birds in the sky to brace themselves.

The birds were outside the attacks' direct range. Their task was to stop the blast wave afterward, keeping it from devastating the surrounding sea and land. It might not put them in danger, but it would hardly be easy.

After running a few steps, Ethan glanced back. Deoxys had Teleported beyond the attacks' range. The black Rayquaza, however, was still weaving around Groudon and Kyogre, attacking them.

It really did not know when to stop. Perhaps it had no idea what was coming. Perhaps it did, but believed it could withstand the blow. Either way, this lesson might make it more willing to listen.

Only seconds later, Groudon and Kyogre were ready. They unleashed their moves almost together. Two torrents of energy, far larger and brighter than those of their last encounter, surged from opposite sides and collided at the center.

BOOM!

Red and blue pillars shot into the sky. The explosion was so loud it hurt their ears. Before they could make out the scene, a powerful shock wave reached them and knocked Maxie and the other prisoners sprawling.

Gardevoir raised a psychic barrier around Ethan, Shelly, and Misty. They were nearly a kilometer from the blast, yet the barrier lasted only seven or eight seconds before the shock wave shattered it. The wind knocked all three to the ground. Fortunately, the barrier had absorbed most of the force, and they escaped with nothing worse than a fall.

They rose and looked toward the battlefield. The energy of Precipice Blades and Origin Pulse had not fully faded. It swelled outward like a mushroom cloud.

Ethan searched for his Pokemon. Deoxys had escaped the blast with Teleport, and even the shock wave could not break through its defenses. It appeared unharmed.

The black Rayquaza was nowhere in sight. Ethan scanned the area again and again, but could not tell where the blast had thrown it. He trusted a Legendary Pokemon at the top of the food chain to survive. It might be badly shaken and injured, but he did not believe its life was in danger.

Unable to find it for the moment, he turned his eyes skyward. The shock wave had reached the three Legendary birds, which strained with all their strength to hold it back. Inside their triangle, enormous waves churned. Beyond it, the sea remained comparatively calm.

But they could not hold on much longer. Their defenses might give way at any moment.
