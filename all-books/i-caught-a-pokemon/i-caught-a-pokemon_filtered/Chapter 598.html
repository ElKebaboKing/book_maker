Chapter 598: Erika Leaves, Misty Joins

The sudden video call changed Ethan and Erika's lighthearted mood at once. They looked at each other, weighed down by the news.

They could do nothing about it. Unless Erika resigned as Gym Leader, she had to follow the Pokemon League's instructions. Giving up her work to travel with Ethan indefinitely was not realistic.

He sighed.

Ethan had been through a separation like this when he parted from Sabrina. It had not made him used to it. If anything, this hurt more.

Erika looked so sad that she could not speak. Ethan took a deep breath, gathered himself, drew her into his arms, and comforted her softly.

Then you should go home. You've been away a long time. Your perfume shop and your Gym both need you. There are more people who depend on you than just me.

Erika buried her face against his chest and held his waist tightly. She said nothing.

It was the first time Ethan had seen this vulnerable side of her so clearly. Usually she was gentle and thoughtful, with the composure of a traditional Japanese lady. She seldom let her feelings show this freely.

It made plain how unwilling she was to leave him.

Ethan held back his own pain and kept speaking gently until she calmed a little.

The Rustboro Gym battle was out of the question now. Ethan had no heart for it. Rustboro had no international airport, so Erika could not fly straight to Kanto. They would have to leave the city, pass through Petalburg, return to Littleroot Town, and buy her a ticket aboard a passenger ship.

They made the journey mostly in silence.

Ethan could not go back with her; he had his own travels to continue. At least he could see her safely to the harbor.

They crossed Petalburg Woods, passed through Petalburg City, and reached Littleroot Town the following evening.

The starting town looked much as it had when they first arrived. But the confidence and anticipation with which they had begun their Hoenn journey had gone. They felt entirely different now.

They had been so eager to set out and so sorrowful to return.

They had missed the day's sailing. They checked into a hotel, rested overnight, and planned to go to the docks the next morning.

That night Ethan saw how fierce Erika's passion could be.

Perhaps she poured her reluctance to part into their lovemaking. She kept reaching for him until he had filled her. Only then did she lie in his arms with faint tear tracks on her cheeks and seem to fall asleep.

But Ethan could not sleep.

The strength he had shown during the day fell away. Erika did not want to leave him; he wanted her to stay just as badly.

He had tried to be steady throughout their return, giving her warmth and someone to lean on when she needed it without adding to her pain.

Now that she lay beside him, he finally let himself feel what he had hidden.

His eyes grew wet as he stared without blinking at the moonlight beyond the window. Then a soft hand brushed the moisture from the corner of his eye.

He looked down. Erika was watching him with tender eyes.

She had been awake all along. She had only pretended to sleep so he would not feel worse.

Their eyes met. They smiled and kissed again.

...

After breakfast the next morning, Ethan took Erika to the harbor.

They embraced one last time. He whispered in her ear, Wait for me in Celadon. I'll contact you every day. And don't worrymy travels won't last forever. When I take a break, I'll come see you.

I know. I'll be waiting. Erika kissed his cheek, stepped back, and bowed deeply with her hands before her chest. I wish you victory in every battle. May all your hard work lead you to win every conference you enter.

Erika boarded the ship. She looked back again and again, but the liner departed on schedule and carried her out of sight.

Ethan stood on the dock for a long time. It felt as though she had taken half his soul with her. Memories of their time together kept rising in his mind, surrounding his heart with warmth.

When he had steadied himself, he sighed and turned to leave. Then someone called to him from a distance.

The voice was so familiar that he knew who it belonged to before he looked around.

Ethan! A pretty, energetic girl with a side ponytail came running up, a small backpack on her shoulders. She was out of breath but still spoke brightly. What are you doing here? Did you come to meet me?

Of course it was Misty.

Unlike before, she was alone. Ash and Brock, usually at her side, were nowhere to be seen.

As they had agreed, she had spent a little time at the Cerulean Gym after returning home, then crossed the sea to join Ethan in Hoenn.

Before Ethan could answer, Misty frowned and looked around. Huh? Where's Erika?

Her smile eased much of Ethan's sorrow. He shook his head and told her about the call that had forced Erika to return to Kanto.

What? Misty's eyes widened. Just now? Then I missed my chance to say goodbye!

Misty had come to Hoenn by ship too. By chance, hers docked just after Erika's had sailed.

For a moment the two ships had passed each other, as though their passengers' paths were changing places.

I didn't expect to find you here either. When I heard your voice, I thought I was imagining it. Ethan reached out and ruffled her hair. Why didn't you tell me you were coming?

Hee hee. Misty looked up at the face she liked so much, her heart racing. I wanted to surprise you. I was going to call once I reached Hoenn, but I found you right away!

Then she realized what Erika's departure meant. From now on, she and Ethan would travel together alone, with no one else around.

No Erika, no Ash, no Brock. She really would have Ethan to herself.

Was she dreaming?

The surprise left her dazed. Everything felt almost too wonderful to be real.

Yes!

Her delight showed on her face. She almost shouted it aloud.

This was perfect. Even fate seemed to be helping her.
