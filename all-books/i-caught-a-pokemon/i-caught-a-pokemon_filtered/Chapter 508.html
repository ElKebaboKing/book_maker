Chapter 508: An Unexpected Incident

Several floor plans of Goldenrod TV Station lay across the hood of the police car. They clearly showed the layout of every level.

A video device sat nearby. Looker tapped a few controls and played a recording.

It was security footage from that morning. Employees had just begun arriving, and the lobby was full of people hurrying to work. Everything looked normaluntil the automatic doors opened and dozens of Team Rocket members in black uniforms rushed in. With their Pokemon's help, they quickly seized the lobby.

Terrified workers fled the station. The Team Rocket members occupied it level by level until they had complete control. Then the footage cut off.

When Ethan had seen it all, Looker said grimly, "This is an outrageous incident. I can't imagine what Team Rocket is trying to do. Acting this openlyare they declaring war on the whole world?"

"Can you still see what's happening inside?" Ethan asked.

Looker shook his head. "They cut every security camera feed. We've lost contact with the building. Worst of all, many people didn't get out in time. They're being held hostage..."

A crackle interrupted him. The device's screen flickered, and a new video broke in without warning.

It appeared not only on that device but on electronic billboards outside nearby buildings, on televisions and computers in people's homes, and even on their phones.

A pink-haired woman in a Team Rocket uniform faced the camera. "Attention, everyone in Johto. We are Team Rocket, and today we announce our arrival! All members who hear this, gather here immediately! Boss Giovanni, if you see this message, please join us too!!"

The message ended, and the screens returned to normal. If not for the deep furrows in Looker's brow, the interruption might have felt like an illusion.

"What are they trying to accomplish?" he muttered. "There's no escape from that building. And why ask Giovanni to join them? Was this operation not his plan?"

Ethan thought for a moment. "From that message, they've clearly lost contact with Giovanni. Their bases across Johto have been cleared out one after another. Their local forces must be close to collapse, scattered and desperate enough to try this."

Looker nodded, agreeing with the assessment.

"In any case, after a spectacle this big, Giovanni could appear here at any moment. It might be a once-in-a-lifetime chance to capture all of them together!"

Ethan shook his head slightly, but said nothing.

Giovanni probably would not appear. Since his identity as a Gym Leader had been exposed, he had been keeping a low profile and quietly rebuilding his strength. This public operation did not fit his current approach.

Besides, Giovanni had never been a man who sought attention.

Ethan looked at Looker. "What's your plan?"

Looker hesitated. "I assembled a special police unit to storm the station. I was going to ask you to slip inside first. But after that broadcast..."

He did not finish, but Ethan understood.

Looker hoped to wait for Giovanni to appear, then arrest him along with the rest.

It was an appealing idea. Ethan doubted events would oblige him.

Looker respected Ethan enough to ask his opinion even after forming his own. "What do you think we should do?"

Ethan told him. Looker fell into thought.

At last, he sighed deeply. "You're right. I overlooked Giovanni's character and made this too simple. He would never put himself in danger for an operation this reckless."

"Even if we don't catch Giovanni, it's all right," Ethan said. "These are probably Team Rocket's last substantial forces in Johto. Arrest them, and Giovanni will have almost no one left to carry out his plans here."

The reassurance helped Looker settle on a decision.

"Exactly. We need to arrest this group before they can do any more harm!"

He put his original plan into motion. At his orders, ranks of officers gathered quickly. They wore helmets and carried riot shields, ready for an assault.

Looker returned to Ethan.

"Ethan, I'll need your help. Before my officers enter through the front, I want you to slip in elsewhere. Use the opening to rescue the hostages and arrest whoever is leading this operation."

"No problem. I'm used to this kind of work."

Ethan agreed with a smile. He had done enough of it by now.

He turned to Erika. He knew she would come with him even if he did not ask.

Sure enough, determination shone in her eyes.

They smiled at each other and quietly discussed where they could enter the station.

Team Rocket would be guarding the entrances closely, particularly the back door and roof. They would leave the police few obvious openings.

Ethan studied the plans for a long time, searching for a hidden route. The drains and ventilation ducts were too small for a person. In the end, Erika suggested coming from above and breaking through a window.

Team Rocket had too few members to watch every window on every floor. It could catch them by surprise.

The area around the station was rather open, though. They would need cover to approach unseen.

When they explained the plan, Looker smiled. "That's no problem. I'll launch a feint to draw their attention and use smoke grenades nearby to hide your approach!"
