Chapter 587: Across Time

Vicious stared at Ethan with hatred.

He held Celebi in one hand and felt around his vest for a Dark Ball with the other. His fingers found nothing.

Only then did he remember that he had not recalled Dark Tyranitar, Dark Sneasel, or Dark Scizor during the battle. Their balls had been lost as well.

Cursing under his breath, he searched the other side of his vest and pulled two Dark Balls from the bottom.

They held ordinary Pokemon he had caught almost casually. Even enhanced by dark energy, they could not compare with his Sneasel or Scizor, much less Tyranitar.

But they were all he had left to fight with. Perhaps they could buy him a little time.

He threw both balls. Dark Poliwhirl and Dark Graveler appeared before Ethan, each wrapped in black light.

Ethan did not send Dragonite into the battle. He took two Poke Balls from his belt instead.

Gardevoir, Jolteon, go!

Blue light flashed, and they faced Poliwhirl and Graveler. Ethan had chosen two of his best partners. He was taking Vicious seriously and holding nothing back.

Once the fight began, however, Vicious surprised him.

As all four Pokemon moved, Vicious turned and ran. He vanished behind a tree. Gardevoir and Jolteon knocked the Dark Pokemon down almost as soon as they had engaged them.

Ethan understood. Vicious had reached the end of his resources.

He hurried after him with his Pokemon. Passing the two fallen opponents, he rounded the tree and found a Dark Ball flying straight at them.

Vicious knew he could not outrun Ethan. He had hidden to ambush him. If he could steal even one of Ethans Pokemon, the Trainer would have a serious problem.

But Ethan expected a desperate trick. As the ball came toward him, a stone rose from the ground beside him and struck it.

Smack!

The Dark Ball did not break, but the blow knocked it off course. It fell to the ground without opening or releasing its black light.

Ethan had learned the trick from Ash. He could not fight the Dark Balls light once it opened, but a well-aimed object could deflect it while it was still flying.

Only Gardevoir, linked closely enough to understand his thought without an order, could have moved the stone in time.

His ambush failed. Ethans Pokemon surrounded Vicious, who now had no other way to resist.

Footsteps sounded behind them. Ash and Sammy arrived, breathless from running.

They had made remarkable time, though Ethan and Vicious had not flown far. Ethan had already spotted Ashs Shiny Noctowl following them from above, so their appearance did not surprise him.

With the boys there too, Vicious had no hope of escaping.

Y-you! Ash bent forward, struggling for breath. He looked from Vicious to the Celebi in his hand. Let Celebi go!

You pests... Vicious shouted bitterly. I had Celebi! I was so close to leaving Team Rocket and becoming the ruler of this world! This is all your fault!

Leaving Team Rocket? Ethan raised his eyebrows.

He had not known Vicious harbored that ambition, though it made sense. Team Rockets executives were hardly selfless people. A Pokemon hunter with power enough to change the world would not be content to serve under someone else.

But Vicious would never achieve that dream.

Its over, Ethan said. Hand Celebi to us. I promise youll reach the police unharmed.

Vicious laughed. When he stopped, he fixed them with a savage look. You really think youve won?

He looked almost deranged. Ethan wondered what else he could possibly have planned. Unwilling to let him create another surprise, he ordered his Pokemon forward and stepped closer himself.

Vicious slowly drew his last Dark Ball.

Ethan, Ash, and Sammy all watched the ball. Was he about to release some powerful Dark Pokemon, or try to catch one of theirs?

He did neither. He pressed the Dark Ball toward Celebis head.

Oh, no.

Ethan grasped his plan in an instant. Vicious meant to control Celebi again, then force it to carry him into another time.

If he succeeded, everything they had done would be wasted. Once he left their time, they might never encounter him again.

They had to stop him. Ethan shouted an order to his Pokemon and ran toward Vicious.

Sammy moved too.

Ash had not yet understood what Vicious intended, but Sammy had. The moment the Dark Ball approached Celebi, he guessed the danger and sprinted forward.

Hearts pounded. For a moment, time itself seemed to slow.

Vicious raised his arm. His finger moved toward the balls switch. Ethan and Sammy rushed closer, urgency plain on Sammys face, but the distance looked too great. They would be too late.

Everyone had overlooked the most important one there.

Celebi.

Hunter and rescuers alike had focused on it. Celebi saw the Dark Ball nearing its head and remembered what it had just suffered under its control.

It would not endure that pain again.

I wont let it control me!

Celebi gave a piercing cry. Power erupted from its body. The light and sound of time travel spread out from it, enveloping both Celebi and Vicious, then expanding farther.

Vicious took the first impact. Even through his mask, the flash blinded him. He turned his head away, felt a powerful force strike him, and lost his grip. Celebi broke free.

Ethan and Sammy had no time to understand what was happening before the blue-green light swallowed them too.

Farther away, Ash shielded his eyes and turned from the glare. When it faded enough to look again, Ethan and the others had vanished.

The forest fell silent. If Viciouss two fallen Dark Pokemon had not still been lying behind him, Ash might have thought he had imagined it all.

Ethan... Sammy... Celebi... Where had they gone?

...

Ethan felt weightless and unsteady, as though gravity had disappeared.

Before he could understand it, he heard a man scream. It was not Celebi. The voice was painfully familiar: Vicious.

What had happened to him?

When the light around Ethan vanished, he looked ahead. Vicious lay on the ground, rolling and clutching his eyes.

My eyes! My eyes!

His cries echoed through the trees and startled several bird Pokemon into flight.

Ethan looked around. His Pokemon were there, and Sammy stood nearby with the same bewildered expression. Ash was nowhere to be seen.

Had they traveled through time?

He turned toward Celebi. It was already flying away.

Celebi, come back!

Sammy called too, but Celebi kept flying as though it had not heard either of them.

It must be frightened, Ethan said with a frown. Stay here and watch him. Ill bring Celebi back!

Without waiting for Sammys answer, he recalled Gardevoir and Jolteon, mounted Dragonite, and flew after Celebi.

Wait... Sammy took two hurried steps, but Ethan was already high above him. He sighed.

He did not mind keeping an eye on Vicious. He simply did not know whether his Charmeleon could handle such a dangerous hunter alone.

For now, Vicious seemed hurt. He kept rolling on the ground, apparently no longer a threat.

Sammy sent out Charmeleon. Fighting down his fear, he approached and kicked the last Dark Ball away from Vicious. Only after it rolled well out of reach did he feel a little safer.

...

High above the trees, Ethan and Dragonite followed Celebi.

Frightened, it fled without choosing a direction. Celebi flew fast enough that Dragonite had to give everything it had to keep up.

Ethan did not know which time they had reached. He did know that Celebi was his only way home. He could not lose sight of it.

He had wanted to catch Celebi before. Now he had another reason he could not let it escape.

At last Celebi seemed to tire, or perhaps it calmed down. It descended into the forest.

Ethan let out a long breath and directed Dragonite after it.

Celebi landed in a fruit tree, picked several berries, and ate. At the sound of wings behind it, it turned quickly. Seeing Ethan on Dragonite, it relaxed.

From their time together yesterday, it knew he was not a bad person. It did not know that, although his methods differed from Viciouss, Ethan also hoped to catch it.

Out of the wolfs den and into the tigers jaws, one might say.

But Ethan had no evil use in mind. He liked Celebi and did not want to spend the rest of his life regretting a missed chance to catch this Mythical Pokemon.

He smiled, trying to appear as friendly as possible.

Celebi, where are we? Can you take me back to the time we came from?

Bi!

Celebi tilted its head. Its eyes shone as it answered, but Ethan could not understand a word.

Fortunately, he had a translator for moments like this.

Gengar, come on out. Time for work!

Gengar emerged from his shadow, stretched widely, then floated around Celebi with interest.

It says this is the time it originally lived in. It left here a few days ago.

Its original time? Ethan understood at once. This must be the world Sammy had come from. Had they traveled forty years into the past?

Can it take me back?

Celebi nodded gently. Bi, bi-bi.

Gengar translated. It can. But it wants to know why you dont want to stay here.

Because my friends and the woman I love are in that time, Ethan said, relieved that he could return. If I stay, I may never see them again. Theyll be worried too.

Celebi nodded as though it partly understood. To it, any time and any forest seemed much the same. Time had changed little about its home.

It raised its hands to use its power. Ethan stopped it.

Wait. Sammys back there waiting for me. I cant just leave him. At the very least, I need to go back and speak to him first.
