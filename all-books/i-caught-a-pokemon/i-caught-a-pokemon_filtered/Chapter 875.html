Chapter 875: The Aphrodisiac

I've thought about it, of course, Ethan said with a light laugh. But I believe we should see this through together. Eevee came to me from you. In the end, what it evolves into ought to depend on what you and Eevee think.

Miki and Kuni had not expected that answer.

They knew Ethan pursued the highest honors a Trainer could earn. Eevee was his Pokemon now, and its evolution and the abilities it gained would matter greatly to him. Yet he had brought it back to consult them.

It surprised and touched them. He was showing the sisters, and Eevee itself, the utmost respect.

If that's how you feel, Miki said, then we really should discuss it carefully.

Kuni nodded. She was about to ask Zuki, the sister with the most experience in battle, for her opinion. It might also ease the tension between Zuki and Ethan. But Zuki was gazing out the door, lost in thought. She did not seem to be following the conversation at all.

Honestly. I told her to act normally. What's she doing now?

With Ethan sitting in front of them, Kuni could say nothing. She looked away in resignation.

At that moment, a maid announced that dinner was ready. They stood, called to May and the younger sisters outside, and went together to the dining room. Their footsteps creaked along the wooden floor of the covered walkway.

Ethan washed his hands outside the room, then followed the others into another space furnished in a traditional Japanese style.

A long, low table stood on the tatami, laden with colorful dishes. The sight alone made him hungry. He recognized some of the food; other dishes were entirely new to him. Every one looked and smelled delicious.

Please sit down, Mr. Vale.

As the eldest sister and head of the household, Miki took the host's place. Ethan sat to her left, Kuni opposite him, and the others settled around the table.

To his surprise, May paid little attention to the feast. She was still bent toward Naoko and Sayo, talking with them about their earlier practice.

It was remarkable how quickly they had grown close. Ethan listened for a moment and smiled. May had clearly gained a great deal from the sisters' advice.

Once everyone was seated, the meal began. Guided by Miki and Kuni, and enlivened by May and Sayo, the conversation was warm and cheerful. They talked about the sisters' performances, then about Ethan and May's travels.

Adventures like theirs were exciting however many times the sisters heard about them. They also fed the five women's longing to experience a journey for themselves.

During dinner, Miki told the others that Eevee was ready to choose an evolution. In practice, it was mainly news to the two youngest sisters. Everyone joined the discussion eagerly.

Jolteon! Sayo suggested first. Electric attacks are so strong!

Miki sighed. Sayo, have you forgotten? Mr. Vale already has a Jolteon. We even met it before.

Sayo stuck out her tongue. Oh! I forgot.

Naoko laughed. Just because you have a Jolteon, Sayo, doesn't mean someone else's Eevee has to become one too.

I know!

They considered other possibilities. Espeon, Vaporeon, and Flareon each had supporters, but none satisfied everyone. The more they talked, the less certain they became.

Time passed as they ate and discussed Eevee. Several bottles of sake had been set out. Miki and Kuni kept pouring for Ethan, and he returned their toasts. Before long, the bottle in front of him was empty.

Ethan had only an average tolerance for alcohol. The sake was mild and the bottle small, but he was beginning to feel a little lightheaded. He was still all right.

Miki moved to fetch another bottle. Zuki rose before she could.

I remember some good sake in the cellar. I'll bring it up.

Without looking at anyone, she walked straight out.

Everyone except May, who did not know Zuki well, was surprised. Even without knowing the history between her and Ethan, a person could tell they did not get along. Why would Zuki volunteer to bring him more sake?

Was she trying to make peace? The others assumed she had found a way to do it. Kuni even thought their conversation in the dressing room might have helped.

Then she felt a prickle of unease. She knew her sister too well. If Zuki wanted to improve matters, she would not have looked so distant in the sitting room or at dinner. And she had left with a grave expression.

Excuse me, Kuni said quietly. I need to use the restroom.

She left the dining room and hurried toward the kitchen and wine cellar. Zuki was nowhere to be found, even when Kuni went all the way inside.

That made her more certain her sister had something in mind. Worried Zuki would worsen her relationship with Ethan, Kuni went looking for her.

She found her at her bedroom.

The light was on and the door half open. Kuni approached and saw Zuki standing at a table. An uncorked bottle of sake stood before her. In her hand was a small pink bottle.

It had been years, but Kuni recognized it at once. It was the aphrodisiac they had bought together when Zuki was younger.

What is she doing?

Alarmed, Kuni pushed through the door. Zuki!

Zuki turned, startled by her voice. She did not stop what she was doing. She poured the liquid from the pink bottle into the sake.

Why are you here? she asked.

Her face was expressionless. She put the pink bottle aside, corked the sake, and gave it a gentle shake to mix the liquids. Her tone and movements were as casual as if she were doing nothing of consequence.

Kuni knew better. She hurried into the room. What are you doing?

Nothing. Just adding a little fragrance to the sake.

Don't lie to me. I know what that is. Kuni stepped close and lowered her voice. Who are you planning to give that bottle to?

She knew the answer. Zuki would never harm her family. That left only Ethan.

That's none of your concern.

Zuki did not look at her. She scratched a small mark on the sake bottle's cork, then took several identical bottles from a cupboard and set them together.

Kuni grabbed her sister and drew her aside. What are you trying to do? Have you lost your mind?

Backed against the wall, Zuki had to face her. I have a plan. Stay out of it.

Her stubborn, serious expression only troubled Kuni more. Do you even understand what that stuff is?

Of course. Zuki smiled faintly. Once Ethan drinks it, he'll be at my mercy.

You fool! Kuni scolded her. It isn't that simple. It's an aphrodisiac. How can you give it to someone else, especially a man? You could end up hurt yourself. And once you do this, our relationship with Ethan could be ruined for good.

You're making it too complicated. Zuki gave a derisive smile. I know what I'm doing. If he gets to have me, why would he turn against us?

Kuni staggered back, as if she no longer knew her sister.

If he gets to have her? Was that really what Zuki intended?

You...you... She could scarcely speak.

She had thought Zuki meant to play a cruel prank: make Ethan lose his composure in front of her and embarrass him. She had never imagined her sister intended to involve herself physically.

Only now did Kuni understand that she had misread everything. Zuki had not challenged Ethan because she disliked him. She liked himenough to try to obtain him this way.

How could that be? Was this truly the proud sister Kuni knew?

Yet Kuni had to admit that an exceptional man could draw women's attention just as a beautiful woman could draw men's. Someone like Ethan might be one of a kind. Still, surely no man was worth going this far for.

You really are mad, she murmured. What if one of us drinks that sake by mistake?

Don't worry. I marked the bottle. There won't be a mistake.

Kuni could not persuade her. What if this gets out of hand? What if you lose control of it?

Lose control? Zuki snorted. Once a man has had a taste, why would he give it up? After the first time, there'll be a second without any prompting. And if I have something I can use against him, he won't be able to get away without the story becoming public.

There was no reasoning with her. Kuni heard resolve in every word. Zuki had thought this through; it was no passing whim. That made it even more frightening.

Kuni felt she had to stop her. But with Zuki's temperament, how could she change her mind? She had a strong feeling things would not unfold as Zuki expected, and the thought filled her with dread.

Kuni was right. Events were already moving away from Zuki's plan.

While the sisters argued, half of Gengar's head rose slowly from the floor. The table hid it from them. Neither noticed their uninvited observer.

Gengar had seen Zuki pour the liquid into the sake and heard every word they had said. It had spotted her leaving dinner while it was wandering the house in search of interesting things. Curious, it had followed her here.

Now, listening to them argue about drugging Ethan, Gengar grinned silently. So this human woman had a scheme of her own.

It did not think Ethan stood to lose much. Even without the aphrodisiac, its Trainer was forever getting close to women. If it had been another kind of trouble, Gengar might have stepped in. But this?

Heh. What's one more? Or perhaps two...

An idea struck it. Gengar snatched the pink bottle from the table. With a tug of psychic power, it loosened the corks of the other sake bottles.

Eager to see what chaos would follow, it poured the remaining liquid from the pink bottle evenly among them. It closed each bottle again and tucked the now-empty pink one away.

The sisters had noticed nothing. Gengar's grin widened.

The show's about to start. You think you'll have the last laugh? Let's see how this really turns out.

Thoroughly pleased with its prank, Gengar sank back through the floor and vanished as quietly as it had come. Neither Kuni nor Zuki knew it had been there.

The two were still arguing over the marked bottle. Kuni could not talk her sister out of the reckless plan.

At last she tried another approach. Don't forget Erika. Anyone can see there's something between her and Ethan. She may even be his girlfriend now.

When the sisters had first met Erika, she and Ethan had not yet become a couple. Even then, a woman choosing to travel everywhere with a man suggested something. After all this time, Kuni was sure their relationship had grown closer.

So what? Zuki did not seem concerned. She only got there before me. I want him, and I intend to have him.

Before Kuni could say more, Zuki moved past her and gathered up all the sake bottles from the table.

She turned back, determination in her eyes. My mind is made up. Stop me if you want. But we've been away too long. We need to return before they start wondering where we are.
