Chapter 648: The Pokemon Contest Begins

The television in the contestants' waiting room was showing the Contest live.

Ethan and the others sat on the sofa. On the screen, a beautiful woman in an elegant red gown stood at the center of the arena with a microphone in hand. She had curly blonde hair and a beauty mark beneath one eye.

Ladies and gentlemen, thank you for waiting! Her sweet voice carried clearly through the room. The Slateport City Pokemon Contest is about to begin! First, please welcome today's three judges!

The camera turned toward the judging table. Two men and a woman sat there. Ethan recognized the woman immediately from her familiar face and uniform: Nurse Joy.

Her presence at a Contest didn't surprise him. After a glance, he turned his attention to the men beside her.

The one nearest Nurse Joy was remarkably short. Even seated, he didn't reach her shoulder. He looked at least fifty years old and kept his eyes narrowed. Despite his black suit, he had a quiet, unassuming air and hardly stood out at all.

The other man was about the same age, perhaps a little younger. He wore a burgundy suit and a bow tie. His hair was swept back, with a section in the middle dyed silver-gray. He looked quite fashionable.

As you can see, the host continued, our judges today are Mr. Sukizo, Mr. Contesta, and Nurse Joy! Let's give them a warm welcome!

Applause rolled through the arena.

Ethan looked over all three judges. Even without the introductions, he knew these were important people.

Leaving Nurse Joy aside, the short man, Mr. Sukizo, was the president of the Pokemon Fan Club. He regularly judged Pokemon Contests and appeared at venues all over the circuit.

Mr. Contesta was the director of the Pokemon Contest Committee, the person in charge of the entire competition.

Compared with them, Nurse Joy was the one people saw most often, and probably the one they found easiest to approach.

Once the introductions were over, the host explained the prize. The winning Pokemon and Coordinator will receive the Slateport Ribbon! Coordinators who earn five Ribbons from Contests in different locations qualify for the Grand Festival!

A picture of the Slateport Ribbon appeared on the big screen. It was a gleaming gold circle like a medal, with a green ribbon tied into a bow around it. The design seemed simple at first, yet it had a distinctive charm.

And now, the competition is about to begin! I'm your host, Vivian. Please welcome our first contestant!

With that, the Slateport Contest was officially underway. Under countless watching eyes, contestant number one made a dazzling entrance with their Pokemon.

Gulp!

In the waiting room, May swallowed nervously as the Contest began. Ethan and the others heard her and turned. She was trembling.

May, what's wrong? Misty asked with concern. Are you nervous?

May nodded. I don't know what's happening. I thought I'd prepared myself, but now that it's really starting, I can't make my legs stop shaking...

With her turn approaching, Misty and the others weren't sure how to encourage her. They could only keep cheering her on.

Fortunately, Ethan had enough experience with competitions to know how to steady her. Take your time. Breathe slowly. Didn't Mr. Big tell you last night? If a Coordinator gets too nervous, their Pokemon can pick up on it.

May drew a deep breath and let it out slowly. She repeated it several times, and her racing heart began to settle.

Thank you, everyone...

Contestants were already taking the stage on the television. A male Coordinator and his Wingull were showing off the beauty of their moves beneath the spotlights.

When their performance ended, the three judges gave them a score of 27.6.

It wasn't a poor score, but it wasn't especially high either. The performance had made no real mistakes, yet nothing had stood out. Unless the pair did something much more impressive in the second round, winning would be difficult.

Several more contestants performed after him. Their scores stayed between 25 and 28.

That didn't mean their performances were bad. Each Pokemon appeared to have received careful training. None had quite managed to make a lasting impression.

Then contestant number twenty-four was called, and that changed.

Next up, contestant number twenty-four!

As Vivian introduced him, a green-haired boy walked onto the stage. His face and manner were unmistakable: it was Drew, whom they had met the day before.

So he really did enter!

May's gaze sharpened. Misty even gritted her teeth at the sight of him.

His good looks and confident manner might have won over plenty of girls in the audience, but May and Misty had already put him firmly on their list of people they disliked.

Once Vivian had finished introducing him, Drew made a dramatic entrance beneath the spotlight, complete with a pose so exaggerated it looked like something out of a fantasy show.

Watching him put on airs, Ethan couldn't help thinking that if Drew ever became a battle Trainer and visited Alola, he would perform a Z-Move pose without the slightest embarrassment.

It was a natural gift!

After striking what he clearly thought was a dazzling pose, Drew began his performance.

His partner on stage was a Roselia, a small, humanoid Grass-type Pokemon with arms and legs. Three spikes rose from its head. Pale green skin and leafy growths draped its body like a dress, with a touch of yellow among the leaves on its chest. Its eyes were usually closed beneath long lashes. A blue rose adorned its left hand, and a red one its right. It looked like a little spirit of the forest.

Whatever offense Drew's sharp tongue had caused, Ethan had to admit the boy had chosen his partner well.

Compared with the Pokemon that had performed before it, Roselia's humanlike form could make its dancing much more graceful and pleasing to watch.
