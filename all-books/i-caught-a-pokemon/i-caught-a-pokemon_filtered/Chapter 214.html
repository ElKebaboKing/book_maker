Chapter 214: Lugia

"It's Lugia!"

Melody called out its name, caught between relief and dread. Lugia had finally appeared in their hour of need and saved them. But the battle had already gone so far. Would events unfold as the prophecy warned?

Ash stared at the Pokemon he had never seen before. "So that's Lugia... the God of the Sea?"

Lugia was one of the rarest legendary Pokemon in the world. It normally slept at the bottom of deep ocean trenches. People said a single beat of its wings could raise a storm that lasted forty days. Its power was so great that it could also calm a raging sea. Whenever a violent storm arose, people thought of Lugia.

Whatever happened next, the group had seen the legendary Pokemon with their own eyes. That alone made the journey unforgettable.

Above them, the Gods of Ice, Fire, and Thunder stopped fighting each other for the moment. They fixed their attention on the newcomer.

Lugia gave another high, haunting call. It seemed to be trying to end the battle, but the three birds neither answered nor showed any sign of stopping.

For a moment, all four Pokemon were still. Their power seemed to clash in the air before any of them moved. It crackled like an invisible struggle.

Tracey swallowed as he watched from the altar.

After two or three tense seconds, the impatient God of Thunder attacked first. Zapdos beat its wings and sent lightning at Lugia. The God of Ice and the God of Fire followed at once, striking together.

Lugia twisted and climbed to avoid the lightning, fire, and ice. The powerful moves passed close by, but it managed to answer with attacks of its own.

Alone against three opponents, it was still at a severe disadvantage. Lugia was stronger than any one of the birds, yet together they pressed it hard.

"This is troubling," Slowking said after watching for a while. "The God of the Sea appears only when the world faces destruction. But it seems unable to stop that destruction."

Ethan narrowed his eyes at the battle and thought quickly. At last he made a decision.

"We can't wait any longer. Ash, let's divide the work. Go to Ice Island and get the last treasure as fast as you can. If the legend is true and Melody's flute can calm them, we'll need it to stop this."

"I understand." Ash nodded, then noticed Ethan was not moving. "What about you? Aren't you coming?"

"No." Ethan took three Great Balls from his waist. "I'll stay and hold them off. That will take some pressure off Lugia and give you time."

"Hold them off?" Melody shook her head. "They're too powerful. Ordinary Pokemon can't join a battle like this!"

"Ordinary Pokemon..." Ethan gave a quiet laugh but did not argue. He looked straight at Ash. "Go. Your job matters. Bring that treasure back safely."

Ash understood how serious this was. "Leave it to me." He turned his cap backward and hurried away with Pikachu.

In Ethan's hand, the three Great Balls stirred. His Pokemon had been eager to emerge for some time. There were no outsiders here he needed to hide them from, and he saw no reason to make them wait longer.

Now's my chance to show offno, to show what I can do.

He threw the Great Balls high. "Come out, all of you!"

Three flashes of blue light appeared in the sky. To everyone's astonishment except Sabrina's, three more legendary birds emerged above them.

"Moltres? Zapdos? Articuno?" Misty cried.

"I can't believe it! Another set of the three legendary birds?" Tracey stared upward.

The others could hardly believe what they were seeing. During the giant Tentacruel incident, Misty had suspected that the Zapdos which appeared might have something to do with Ethan. She had never imagined he had caught Moltres and Articuno as well.

Ash had not gone far. Hearing the commotion, he looked back and stopped in his tracks. Ethan had captured all three legendary birds? When had that happened? The distance between them had grown greater without Ash even realizing it.

Melody was more stunned than anyone. To the people of Shamouti Island, the Gods of Thunder, Fire, and Ice were sacred and untouchable. Ethan owned others of their kind. She stared at him, unable to understand.

Who is he?

Ethan knew nothing of their thoughts. Lugia was taking hit after hit. He swept an arm toward the battle.

"Moltres, Articuno, Zapdosgo! Let's shake things up!"

The three birds cried out, circled once, and flew straight toward their counterparts.

Melody finally recovered enough to grab Ethan's sleeve. "Wait! If the fighting gets worse, won't it do even more damage to the environment in other regions? That's what you said!"

"I know," Ethan said with a rueful smile. "But should we stand by because we're afraid of damaging it? I'd rather end this quickly than let it drag on. Nature can recover in remarkable ways. We have to respect its power, and trust its ability to heal."
