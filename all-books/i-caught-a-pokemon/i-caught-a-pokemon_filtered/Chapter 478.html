Chapter 478: Taking the Opposite Course

"What!?"

Everyone stared at Ethan in disbelief when they heard he intended to stay!

"Ethan, what are you talking about!?"

Misty even reached out to feel his forehead. Finding no sign of a fever, she asked in astonishment, "There are only a few minutes left on the countdown. We need to leave!"

She took his arm and tried to pull him through the exit.

Ethan shook his head, gently removed her hand, and gave a wry smile. "I'm not joking. Something about this really does feel wrong. Think about it. We haven't done much yet, and we haven't even seen the person in charge of the base. They only resisted for a little while before deciding to blow the whole place up. Doesn't that seem strange? Isn't it an overreaction?"

Erika considered this calmly. "Perhaps... they realized they couldn't hold us off. Or there might be important data in the base that they can't allow anyone to obtain. That would explain such an extreme response."

"Exactly!" Misty hurriedly agreed. "You heard the alarm too. They wouldn't lie about an explosion. And lookTeam Rocket is rushing to escape!"

"Even so..." Ethan shook his head, holding to his judgment. "I still feel that something is unusual. I'll stay and see what happens. Don't worry. I'm not an idiot. If an explosion really does occur, Gardevoir can Teleport me outside at that very moment!"

Despite his explanation, the others could hardly allow him to take such a risk. They all tried to dissuade him.

"That's too dangerous! You're gambling with your life!"

"They're right. Let's hurry and get out of here!"

Their objections surrounded him, but Ethan chose to trust his instincts.

"I've made up my mind. There's no need to keep arguing. Besides, if the base does explode, my Pokemon will protect me!"

Seeing how determined he was, everyone turned to Erika, hoping she could persuade him.

Erika thought for a moment as they watched her. Then, to their surprise, she said, "In that case, I'll stay too."

Her decision was just as firm as Ethan's. She seemed ready to follow him wherever he went.

"Huh?"

"Miss Erika, have you lost your senses too!?"

Everyone was stunned. They had counted on the mature, levelheaded Erika to talk Ethan out of his plan, not to stay and join him!

Erika sighed helplessly at their expressions. "There's nothing else I can do. He's already made up his mind. I can't leave him here alone. Someone has to keep an eye on him."

She turned her gaze toward Ethan.

He met her eyes. Although her decision surprised him, Gardevoir could Teleport them both out together. After a moment, he nodded.

Seeing that Erika was staying, Misty said without hesitation, "Then I'll"

Ethan raised a hand to stop her before she could finish.

"You should all leave. The two of us are enough. Gardevoir can't take more people than that at once."

"But..."

Misty still wanted to argue, but at Ethan's signal, Ash and Brock half pulled, half pushed her out of the base.

Before leaving, she looked back. "I'll wait for you outside. Please be careful!"

Once the others had gone, no one remained nearby except Ethan and Erika. The alarm still echoed through the halls, but otherwise the entire base had fallen silent, as though it had been abandoned.

"Warning. One minute remains until the base is destroyed. All personnel must evacuate immediately. Warning. One minute remains..."

With so little time left, Ethan did not delay. He turned and walked briskly deeper into the base.

Erika had recalled Leafeon as a precaution. Following Ethan, she could not help asking, "How certain are you about this?"

Ethan shrugged. "How could I be certain? It's only a guess. We have to stay ready for the worst."

Erika sighed. "If the whole base explodes at once, we'll never escape. We really could die here together."

Ethan gave a small laugh. "Then why did you dare come with me?"

At his teasing tone, Erika suddenly fell silent.

To be honest, she did not know what she had been thinking when she chose to accompany him into such danger.

Even knowing they might die at any moment, she still felt no regret.

As the silence grew heavy, Ethan realized his remark might have been poorly chosen. He cleared his throat and changed the subject. "Don't worry. This base is mainly a research facility. For safety, the laboratories should be kept away from explosives. If the base really is destroyed, it will probably happen in stages, not all at once. That should give us at least a few seconds to react."

Time passed quickly as they continued forward.

"Warning. Ten seconds remain until the base is destroyed... Ten... nine..."

As the moment approached, both Ethan and Erika grew tense.

For all his confident words, Ethan was far from certain.

Gardevoir took one of their hands in each of its own. At the slightest sign of danger, it would use Teleport.

"Five... four... three... two... one..."

When the countdown ended, Ethan felt a faint tremor beneath his feet. Several explosions sounded in the distance.

Gardevoir was about to Teleport, but Ethan stopped it. He listened closely.

The blasts seemed to be some distance from their position. There had been several in succession, but none felt especially powerful.

"What are you waiting for!?" Startled by the explosions, Erika put a hand to her chest. "The base really is exploding. Your guess was wrong. Come on, we need to leave!"

"Wait a moment." Ethan held a finger to his lips. "Don't go yet. Listen..."

Erika paid close attention. To her surprise, after those first few blasts, the explosions stopped completely!

The alarm had gone silent too!

Huh?

What's going on?

Wasn't the base supposed to be destroyed!?
