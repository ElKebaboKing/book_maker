Chapter 253: A School of Gyarados

Ethan exchanged a look with Ash and the others. They had no better plan for now. Quackenpoker's suggestionto strike the mechanical Gyarados in its bellygave them their best chance.

They nodded and called out together.

"Charizard, use Flamethrower!"

"Pikachu, Thunder Shock!"

"Starmie, use Swift!"

"Marill, Bubble Beam!"

The four attacks converged on one spot. Fire, electricity, stars, and bubbles struck the mechanical Gyarados in a tremendous blast.

Boom!

Its shell broke open. The combined attack punched through the armor and seemed to damage the machine's power system too. A moment later, the Gyarados that had been so threatening exploded.

Boom!

Jessie, James, and Meowth had no time to react in the cockpit. The blast hurled them into the sky. They dwindled into three bright points and disappeared into the distance.

"What a horrible feeling!"

"How did it turn out like this again?"

Their cries faded away. Ash and the others breathed a little easier, but they had no time to celebrate. A chain of explosions ran through the container holding the captured Magikarp.

Bang! Bang!

The Magikarp flew into the air. As they fell, they seemed to fill the sky like a sudden shower of red flowers. More than a hundred wriggling fish splashed back into the lake.

"Oh, no!"

Quackenpoker's expression changed. He had not expected their attack to put the Magikarp in danger. If the Pokemon were hurt badly enough to miss their annual mass evolution, he would never forgive himself.

Fortunately, the blasts had not engulfed them. The Magikarp had been caught at the edge of the explosions and suffered only minor injuries.

As the last one landed, a brilliant pillar of light erupted from the lake. Then a second appeared, a third, and more than a dozen others. The beams shot straight through the night toward the clouds. They lit the lake, the campsite, the forest, everyone's faces, and even the rest of Rind Island.

"Yes! The Magikarp are finally evolving together!" Quackenpoker stared wide-eyed, delight breaking through his worry. He hurried back to his equipment to record it all.

Ethan and the others knew what each pillar meant. A Magikarp was evolving beneath every one. Tracey watched the surging light in awe.

"No battle experience, no Trainer helping them. Is this the power of the Lake of Birth and Evolution? It's incredible."

Charizard and the other Pokemon returned to their Trainers. Together they watched the rare sight.

After a while, Ethan sensed something was wrong. Enough Magikarp had gathered to begin the mass evolution, but what had happened before it was nothing like what any of them had hoped for.

When the light vanished, a group of fierce Gyarados filled the lake. Every one of them looked angry. Their eyes burned with rage.

Quackenpoker was so absorbed in the successful evolution that he missed the change in their behavior. The Trainers noticed at once.

"Ethan..." Misty swallowed. As a Water-type Trainer, she was especially sensitive to Gyarados. "Why do they look so frightening?"

Ethan gave a helpless smile. "They struggled up those waterfalls, barely caught their breath, and Team Rocket captured them. Then our fight caught them in an explosion and sent them falling through the air. I'd be angrier than they are."

As if to prove him right, the Gyarados turned toward the group on shore. Their hostility was almost palpable.

"This is bad!" Tracey cried. "They're looking right at us!"

"They don't think we were the ones who caught them, do they?" Ash asked, alarmed.

There was no time to find out. The Gyarados closed in. More than a dozen enormous jaws opened, gathering destructive energy inside.

Ethan recognized the move. "Hyper Beam! There are too many of them. Everybody, get out of the way!"

He grabbed Sabrina with one hand and Misty with the other and ran. Behind him, Ash fled with Pikachu. Tracey started to follow, then saw Quackenpoker still working at his equipment. He hurried back and caught the old man by the wrist.

"Mr. Quackenpoker! We have to leave now!"

The alarm in Tracey's voice made Quackenpoker look up. Only then did he notice the Gyarados.

"What? How did this happen?"

He tried to pack his instruments, but there was no time. Tracey pulled him away.

"Wait! My equipment!"

To Quackenpoker, three years of research were more precious than his own life. He found the strength to break free, spread his arms, and tried to shield the instruments with his frail body.

Ethan clicked his tongue. The old man's dedication was admirable, but his disregard for his own safety was maddening. Ethan could not abandon him.

"Ash! Stop running! Help protect Quackenpoker!"

Ash turned, saw what was happening, and stopped. Misty and Tracey joined them. They threw more Poke Balls, releasing their remaining Pokemon. Working together, the Pokemon launched attacks toward the incoming Hyper Beams.

The beams were almost upon the camp. Their counterattack swept forward in a band of colors and met the Gyarados' attacks just in time.

Bang!

So much energy colliding produced a deafening shock wave. At its center, a light as brilliant as the sun flared across the lake.
