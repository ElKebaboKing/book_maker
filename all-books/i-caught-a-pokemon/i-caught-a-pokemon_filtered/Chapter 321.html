Chapter 321: Will and Karen

Ethan greeted Looker. While they were talking, the tent flap lifted and a man and a woman came inside.

Ethan turned toward the sound. He had never met either of them, yet he recognized them at once.

The man had shoulder-length purple hair and wore an elaborate red-and-black outfit. A half-mask concealed his eyes, making his age hard to judge and lending him an air of mystery.

The woman had long, wavy, pale-blue hair. Her yellow strapless top left her waist bare. She was beautiful and smiling, but there was a shadow about her brows that made her look calculating.

"You're here!" Looker stepped forward to introduce them. "These are two more people we've invited to help. On the left is Mr. Will, an expert in Psychic-type Pokemon. On the right is Ms. Karen, who specializes in Dark-types."

It really is Will and Karen. Ethan had not expected them to come too. As in the previous operation, the International Police were clearly taking this one seriously.

Will and Karen were no ordinary Trainers. Both were exceptionally strong. A few years from now, they would join Koga and Bruno as the new Johto Elite Four. It was said that no one had ever seen Will's true face beneath his mask.

Perhaps thinking that Ethan and Sabrina would not know the newcomers, Looker added, "Will and Karen are highly respected here in Johto. We went through the Pokemon League and made quite an effort to contact them. We specifically asked them to join us against Team Rocket."

After introducing Will and Karen, Looker pointed toward Ethan and Sabrina. He began with Sabrina.

"This is Ms. Sabrina, the Gym Leader of Saffron City in Kanto. Like Mr. Will, she's an expert with Psychic-type Pokemon. She's also a master of psychic powers herself."

Will brightened at the name of the beautiful, long-haired woman standing before him.

"So you're Ms. Sabrina!" He bowed like a courteous gentleman. "Meeting you is even better than hearing about you. You're as lovely as your name. Even though you're in Kanto, you're famous among us psychics in Johto. I'm Will. It's a pleasure to meet you!"

Smiling, he waited for a reply. Sabrina merely frowned a little and said nothing.

What? What's going on?

Is she really that rude? He had been polite and praised both her beauty and her skill. Surely she could at least greet him.

After two awkward seconds, he suddenly remembered that Sabrina was known not only for her powerful psychic abilities but also for her icy, unapproachable manner.

He stiffened. With the others watching in barely concealed amusement, he straightened up in embarrassment.

Looker cleared his throat, pretended not to notice, and pointed to Ethan instead.

"And beside Ms. Sabrina is a good friend of mine. His name is Eth"

Ethan raised a hand and cut him off. He adjusted his mask, then spoke in an exaggeratedly dramatic voice. "Hello. My codename is Player. It's a pleasure to meet you both!"

Not this again!

Looker slapped a hand to his forehead. Even Sabrina turned her eyes away uneasily. Why did Ethan keep doing this? As soon as he put on that mask, he seemed to become someone else.

"Player?" Will and Karen both paused at the codename.

Will looked at the man whose face was covered even more thoroughly than his own and was astonished by his choice of name.

Karen studied Ethan from head to toe with interest. "Player, as in the person playing a game?"

"Exactly!"

"Why choose a name like that?"

Ethan grinned. "Part the clouds and look at reality. This world is one game that never ends, and we're nothing more than data inside it!"

"An interesting way of putting it!" For some reason, Karen nodded solemnly at his plainly absurd joke. "To me, the world really is like a playground. And honestly, compared with Mr. Looker's codename, yours sounds much more normal. Let me introduce myself again. I'm Karen, and I love Dark-type Pokemon. They're strong and wild. I especially love their wicked looks and their powerful dark energy!"

Oh, no. There's something wrong with the way this woman thinks! Ethan was only pretending to be dramatic. Karen, it seemed, was entirely serious.

Will struck a flamboyant pose. "Let me introduce myself again too! My name is Will. I'm a magician who travels the world, training with Psychic-type Pokemon day and night!"

He flicked his hand and produced a bright rose from nowhere, then held its stem between his teeth. To his dismay, the tent fell silent again.

Once the introductions were over, Zero cleared his throat and drew everyone's attention.

"Back to the matter at hand. We've called you together because there's an urgent development."

The mood in the tent turned serious. Everyone stopped joking.

Karen looked at Zero. "Why have we assembled early? Has something changed?"

Zero glanced at Ethan without showing what he thought. For several reasons, he chose not to reveal the cause.

"The situation has changed. Team Rocket has begun evacuating, so we must act immediately."

Looker went to the tactical board and called the others over to assign their roles.

"We'll split up again. Mr. Will and Ms. Karen will lead a team of International Police officers in a raid on a store in Mahogany Town. It may look unremarkable, but it's an important entrance to Team Rocket's underground base."

"And Eth" Looker caught himself. "Player and Ms. Sabrina will come with me and lead the other officers in a raid on a research facility on the shore of the Lake of Rage. That's our most important target. Remember, Team Rocket may put up fierce resistance. We may even encounter their executives. Stay alert and be careful at all times!"
