Chapter 826: Back on the Expected Path

Under Nicolette's wary gaze, Munchlax's Metronome produced Splash. Its round body bounced up and down on the grass like a Magikarp stranded on a riverbank.

So the famous moment had happened after all. May looked embarrassed. Nicolette's guarded expression froze. After all that caution, Munchlax had used a move that did nothing. She felt foolish for treating it as a dangerous opponent.

Then she recognized her chance. Vileplume, use Stun Spore now!

Orange powder poured from Vileplume's flower and settled over Munchlax. Still bouncing helplessly, it could not dodge. Now it was confused and paralyzed, trembling where it stood. May watched anxiously. She wanted to help it pull itself together, but she had no idea how.

Nicolette pressed the advantage. Vileplume, Bullet Seed!

It was a sound tactic against most Pokemon. Munchlax, unfortunately for Nicolette, did not behave like most Pokemon.

Vileplume fired a rapid stream of seeds. They struck Munchlax in a steady barrage, bursting in small explosions and raising a cloud of smoke.

Nicolette grinned. Ha! So much for the Saffron Contest winner. You're nothing special!

That angered May. Until now, she had treated the battle as a friendly test. Nicolette's taunt made her take it seriously.

What did you say? May glared at her, then called into the smoke. Munchlax, get up! The real battle is just starting. Show her what you've got!

Perhaps Munchlax sensed her anger. Two sharp, shining eyes appeared in the smoke. Munchlax burst out with a glowing fist, accelerated across the dozen or so meters between them, and slammed into Vileplume.

The blow was immense. Vileplume cried out and flew backward, landing hard some distance away.

What? Nicolette turned to check on it. Even May was astonished by the move Munchlax had used.

Was that...?

Focus Punch, Ethan said with a smile. It looks as though your Munchlax learned it without your knowing.

The surprise was welcome. That punch had brought Munchlax into the fight and answered Nicolette's insult.

Great job! May gave Vileplume no time to recover. Finish it with Solar Beam!

Ethan shook his head. A Grass-type move against a Grass and Poison Pokemon: May really had picked up some of Ash's habits.

Munchlax obeyed without question. It gathered energy and fired a long beam from its mouth toward Vileplume. The Pokemon had only just risen. It ducked instinctively, and the beam passed over its head, brushing the flower's petals before striking a small hill in the distance.

Only a little of the attack had touched Vileplume, but that was enough. Its eyes rolled back and it collapsed.

No way! Nicolette cried. It barely grazed Vileplume, and it still went down? A Contest winner really is different!

Her attitude had changed completely. She had the cause wrong, though. She thought Solar Beam had decided the match. The decisive blow had been Munchlax's earlier, accelerating Focus Punch.

May had won without the battle slipping away from her. She and Nicolette recalled their Pokemon. When they turned toward Ethan, they saw Christopher and Jeannie watching from nearby. They had witnessed the battle.

Nicolette had hoped to prove herself to her parents, only to lose so plainly. She lowered her head in shame. Beside Ethan, May received his praise and smiled brightly, making the contrast harder for Nicolette to bear.

Then Ethan spoke to her. I think traveling and seeing more of the world will teach you more than training at home. May is only a year or two older than you, but her experience has made her stronger.

Nicolette looked up. Though she argued against leaving, she was not as opposed to travel as she claimed. Part of her even wanted to go. She stayed because she loved her home and her parents. Her room, the shelves full of Eggs, the lovely farm, and all the familiar things around her gave her reasons not to leave.

She admired May's life as a Coordinator and wanted to prove she could match her. Losing had shown her the gap between them. Ethan's words made the thought of travel more appealing, though she would not admit it aloud.

Are you a Coordinator too? she asked him. She wanted to know who was advising her before deciding what to say back.

No, Ethan said, making no effort to boast. I'm just a Trainer.

May did not like the way Nicolette had asked. Ethan isn't an ordinary Trainer. He won the last Indigo League Conference!

What? Nicolette knew what a championship meant, even though she had never watched a League battle. She stared at Ethan. You? Is that true? You're not making it up?

Ethan had not expected May to bring up his old victory. He understood that she did not want Nicolette to look down on him, so he nodded.

May's telling the truth. But we aren't listing our accomplishments to impress you. We want you to know that going out into the world lets you see more of it and keep improving.

Ethan would not ordinarily have said so much to a girl he had just met. He had a reason: he wanted May to receive the Egg he knew she could gain here. If they helped Nicolette's parents persuade their daughter to travel, the family might thank them with a gift.

He could have tried to buy the Egg instead. That would have been a mere transaction. May would have had no part in earning it and less reason to treasure it. Better to let her help someone and receive it in return.

A Contest winner's example might not have been enough to sway Nicolette. Hearing from a League champion as well shook her resolve. Ethan then let Gardevoir give a small demonstration, restoring the patch of hillside that Munchlax's Solar Beam had damaged. Nicolette was stunned.

Could traveling really make someone that capable?

That afternoon, after the farm work was finished, Nicolette and her parents sat down to tea with Ethan and May again. May told Nicolette stories of the places she had seen and the things she had experienced since becoming a Coordinator. At last, Nicolette made up her mind.

Thank you, she said, looking solemnly at Ethan and May. I've decided to be brave and travel.

Christopher and Jeannie looked at each other in surprise and relief. Their daughter had finally overcome the fear that had held her back. They were about to thank the visitors when a red warning light came on.

It's time! Nicolette rose first. Remember when I told you one of the Pokemon Eggs was about to hatch? It's happening now!

The family hurried outside. Ethan and May, curious to see a hatching, followed them to the tower surrounded by the storage buildings.

An orange-red Egg marked with swirling patterns rocked gently on a strange machine. Several beams of white light shone on it. From its color and markings, Ethan guessed it held a Vulpix. The Egg began to flash. It would hatch any moment.

We arrived just in time, May said. She stepped closer to study it.

Ethan had seen Pokemon hatch often in the anime, but never in person. He found the sight fascinating too.

Before long, the Egg changed. A bright light flashed, and its entire shape transformed into a Vulpix. May had expected the Pokemon to break through a shell. To her surprise, no shell remained at all.

Nicolette set to work caring for the newborn. She dried it with a towel, examined it, and fed it milk, moving through each task with ease. Young as she was, she had plenty of practice looking after newly hatched Pokemon.

More than half an hour passed. Ethan and May had been guests for a long time and ought to be going.

Christopher and Jeannie had still made no offer of a gift. Without Team Rocket causing trouble here, there had been no dramatic incident to make them especially grateful. Ethan hesitated, wondering whether to raise the matter himself. Then Nicolette approached May with an Egg under a transparent cover.

May, please accept this Pokemon Egg.

Huh? May glanced back at Ethan. Can I really take it?

Of course. Nicolette smiled. You and Ethan helped me decide to leave home and become a real Coordinator. You liked the Eggs so much that I wanted to thank you with one.

Ethan studied its color and pattern. It was an Eevee Egg. He nodded to himself. Their visit had been worthwhile.

In the anime, May had received Eevee, her second new Pokemon in Kanto, from this farm. Even without Ash here, Ethan had helped bring events back to their expected course.
