Chapter 854: The Show Begins

Jessie and Cacturne began their appeal. Cacturne opened with Needle Arm and Cut, using precise strokes to carve a long white radish into the shape of a tower.

In a way, it resembled May's disc-catching routine with Combusken. Both displays showed what their Pokemon could do with skill and control. Cacturne earned a round of applause.

With her opening safely behind her, Jessie moved to the next part. Cacturne used Cotton Spore to send countless spores into the air. They floated like dandelion seeds until it swept across them with Bullet Seed, bursting them into still finer tufts. The soft white flecks drifted down like snow, turning the stage into a dreamlike scene.

Harley's Cacturne was certainly strong, with ability worthy of a top Grand Festival entrant. Jessie's ideas and direction were excellent too. The two worked remarkably well together. Cacturne showed no discomfort at performing for a different Trainer.

Jessie really did have a gift for this. Her appeal earned the second-highest score so far, just behind May's.

The first round was not even half over, but May and Jessie had outperformed the rest of the field so thoroughly that it was hard to imagine later entrants scoring above them. Both had effectively secured places in the next round already.

That was good news for them and added even more pressure for everyone else. More than a hundred Coordinators now had only six remaining places to compete for.

May no longer had to worry about that. When even the crowd favorite Jessibella failed to surpass her score, she let out a relieved breath. All she had wanted was to get safely through the appeal round.

Traveling with Ethan had taught her something of his approach. Her style had begun to resemble his, and her Pokemon were steadily growing stronger. Once she reached the battle stage, her experience and her Pokemon's strength made her much harder to eliminate.

The Contest continued at a lively pace. With so many entrants, the appeal round took an unusually long time, stretching into the afternoon. Eventually everyone had performed, and eight Coordinators advanced.

When the big screen displayed the results, Ethan saw May at the top, just as expected. Jessie had fallen to fourth place but had qualified too.

Jessie's score had been high when she finished. Other Coordinators had given strong appeals afterward and, despite the pressure, pushed her down the rankings. Her position hardly mattered. Reaching the battle stage was what counted.

There was little time left, so the second round began after only a short break. May and Jessie landed on opposite sides of the bracket. If both kept winning, they would not meet until the final.

From what Ethan had seen, a final between them seemed likely.

He slipped away to use the restroom. By the time he returned, the first battle had started. Neither May nor Jessie was in it; the opening match was between two other Coordinators. That was why he had felt free to leave. May took the stage only after their match ended.

Her opponent was a handsome young man with a mature air. He sent out Skarmory. May chose Squirtle.

Squirtle was still in its first evolutionary stage. Professor Oak had given it to May after she came to Kanto, and it remained a little timid. Yet it had grown remarkably fast. Against a formidable opponent with a stronger body, it did not retreat. At May's command, it withdrew into its shell to avoid Skarmory's attack, then countered with Rapid Spin and gained the advantage.

If Brock had been watching, he would have recognized how much May's battle style had come to resemble Ethan's: steady and unhurried. It had once shared something with Ash's style, but that likeness had all but disappeared.

No style was inherently better; the question was which Trainer it suited. For May, the change had helped considerably. She no longer fought with Ash's impulsiveness.

Of course, anyone who had reached the second round from a field of more than a hundred was no weakling. The young man had underestimated Squirtle and paid for it, but once he adjusted, he gradually steadied the battle.

May had changed too. Squirtle looked harmless, yet it was much stronger than an ordinary Pokemon of its age. Alongside Bubble and Water Gun, it could use Ice Beam. As Skarmory swept past, Squirtle froze one of its wings. Unable to keep its balance, Skarmory crashed onto the stage.

Squirtle took advantage of the opening. Smaller and more agile, it moved around its opponent and brought Skarmory down for good.

Ethan applauded with the rest of the audience. As a Trainer who spent every day with May, he could see her progress clearly. He knew she was taking cues from him and beginning to follow a similar path.

Still, her growth was nothing like it had been in the animated story he remembered. He could not say what kind of Trainer she would eventually become. A female version of himself, perhaps?

One thing was certain: having May travel with him and watching her change had taught Ethan how satisfying it could be to guide someone else. It was a different feeling from raising Pokemon.

He suddenly understood why Yuma, the former top Coordinator who now worked as a Pokemon Breeder, had offered to coach Brock and Mollie at the last Contest. Seeing May grow stronger little by little filled him with a satisfaction he could hardly match.

Jessie took the stage after May. For the battle round, she again used one of Harley's Pokemon rather than her own: Banette. The two groups had clearly made an arrangement.

Ethan did not interfere. He preferred to watch what happened. The Team Rocket trio was too afraid of him to try anything against him directly. Whatever Harley had talked them into, Ethan expected Harley to be the one who suffered for it.

That was not blind arrogance. Ethan knew his own strength, and he knew the trio.

Onstage, Jessie and Banette were working together as smoothly as Jessie and Cacturne had. Banette obeyed her without resistance. Harley had probably spoken to his Pokemon beforehand, but Jessie, like James, also had a natural ease with Pokemon.

Banette was strong enough that Jessie barely had to direct it. Truthfully, detailed battle commands were not her strength. Relying on its own understanding of the fight, Banette defeated its opponent with ease.

Like May, Jessie followed that victory with more wins. Both reached the final, where they would at last face one another.

By then the sky had darkened. The spectators were tired, but they roused themselves and waited eagerly for the last match of the Mulberry Contest. After this, anyone wanting to see another Contest Battle would have to wait for the Grand Festival. They did not want to miss the final.

Now, let's welcome our two finalists to the stage!

Lilian sounded as lively as ever, despite having hosted the Contest all day. A spotlight fell on one entrance. May walked out waving to the cheering audience.

First is May, who has traveled all the way from Hoenn! She has four Ribbons so far. Will she collect her fifth today and secure a place in the Grand Festival? Let's watch and find out!

The spotlight swung to the other entrance, and Jessie emerged at an unhurried pace, smiling.

And now, with her magnificent costume, the dazzling Jessibella! Will she earn her first Ribbon in today's Contest? Let's look forward to her performance!

Only then did Ethan realize Jessie still had not won a single Ribbon. After all this time, that was almost hard to believe.

If Jessie stopped hounding Ash and seriously entered Contests in one place, she might have collected five Ribbons by now. On the other hand, would she still be Jessie if she gave up chasing Ash and Pikachu? She had chosen her own path and had to live with what it cost her dream.

May and Jessie took their positions and threw their Poke Balls. Squirtle and Banette appeared onstage once more. Both had fought several matches already, but Pokeblocks and brief rests had helped them recover. Neither had taken much damage, and both looked ready.

Lilian saw that they were prepared and gave the signal. The final battle of Kanto's last Contest before the Grand Festival began.

Harley sat up straight in the stands. At last! Excitement flashed in his eyes. He had waited so long for this moment.

Why else had he entrusted Cacturne and Banette, his two strongest Pokemon, to Jessie? If she used them to defeat May here, May would be eliminated. Harley would get his revenge.

He clenched his fists, imagining it already. In his mind, his Pokemon were stronger than May's. His earlier loss had been nothing but bad luck, and he was certain they would win if given another chance.

One thing still troubled him. James had yet to do anything to hinder May. Their plan was that, once Jessie had Harley's Pokemon, James would disrupt May's performance as well. Why had there been no sign of him?

Harley felt uneasy. What if James had gone to rescue Meowth? But he could not leave now, at the most important moment of the Contest.

Then he spotted James peering out from the entrance to the contestants' passage. Harley relaxed. As long as James was here, he had not gone to free Meowth, and their cooperation could continue. May had merely faced fewer obstacles than Harley had planned.

Onstage, Banette moved the instant Lilian announced the start, without waiting for Jessie to command it. Electricity formed in its hand and struck Squirtle before May could react.

The timing was perfect. Banette had quietly gathered energy before the battle began. As soon as Lilian finished speaking, it released the attack and caught May and Squirtle off guard.

Harley could not be accused of breaking the rules. Banette had used the move only after Lilian's signal. Before then it had neither moved nor visibly changed. It had found a loophole, nothing more.

The Electric-type attack hit the Water-type Squirtle hard. May's point bar on the screen shrank a little. Squirtle was not so easily beaten, though. It rose almost at once, determination burning in its eyes, and fired an Ice Beam from its mouth, catching Banette by surprise in return.
