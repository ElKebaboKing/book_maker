Chapter 471: The First Phase Ends

Rumble, rumble, rumble...

The thunderous roar of helicopter rotors drew closer from the distance. It was clearly audible even amid the chaos of the battlefield.

Cassidy and Butch looked toward the horizon. Four helicopters had appeared in the distance and were rapidly approaching the island.

The roar grew louder and louder until it hurt their ears. By the time the helicopters reached the beach, both could clearly see the enormous International Police insignia emblazoned upon their fuselages!

"The International Police!?"

The pair exchanged looks, shocked to discover who had arrived.

Although they had known helicopters were coming as reinforcements, they had assumed they carried ordinary police officers. Neither had imagined the International Police would appear!

These officers were far more difficult to handle than ordinary police. Their members and Pokemon were all well equipped and highly trained, and their ranks even included experts capable of matching Team Rocket's four executives. They also recruited outside assistance for their operations. Unless they were pursuing major criminals who crossed national and regional borders or organizations like Team Rocket, they rarely took action!

Their presence here could mean only one thing: they intended to wipe out the entire Team Rocket force!

If they had any choice, Cassidy and Butch would have preferred never to confront these people!

The arrival of the International Police shattered their last hope and became the final blow to Team Rocket's morale.

The grunts were visibly thrown into confusion. Those trapped on the front line had no choice but to continue fighting Ethan's group's Pokemon, while the members farther back lost all desire to battle and stared fearfully at the helicopters overhead!

Once their initial shock passed, Cassidy and Butch also began to panic.

"Damn it! Weren't they supposed to be several minutes away!?"

The International Police had clearly arrived faster than they expected.

The timing left them caught between two impossible choices. They could neither seize Lugia from the people before them nor retreat safely in time!

Seeing the situation, Cassidy had no choice but to abandon the mission. She shouted to everyone around her, "Stop fighting! Retreat immediately!!"

With that, she and Butch recalled their Pokemon and led the withdrawal toward the submarine.

Before the pair could reach it, however, the submarine had already begun to descend!

Its crew could hardly be blamed for abandoning their companions. Compared with the ordinary members, the submarine was an extremely valuable Team Rocket asset and could not be allowed to fall into enemy hands.

When the report of the approaching helicopters had first arrived, everyone could have withdrawn immediately and escaped at their leisure. They had delayed for too long, however, and now it was already too late to flee!

The helicopters circled overhead as dozens of International Police officers rappelled to the ground. Their entry into the battle instantly balanced the two sides' numbers, sending the Team Rocket members fleeing in every direction!

They offered almost no resistance. Every Team Rocket member had only one thought: as many of them as possible had to escape, and they had to run as far away as they could!

But this was an isolated island. Other than Cassidy, Butch, and a small number of members who managed to enter the submarine, where could the rest possibly go?

On the other side of the battlefield, Ethan stood atop the large rock and watched the submarine nearly disappear beneath the water. He hurriedly waved to Misty.

"Misty, have one of your Pokemon follow them quietly and find out where their secret base is!"

"Got it!"

Misty nodded, then turned and issued instructions to one of her Pokemon. The next instant, Poliwhirl leaped into the sea and rapidly swam after the submarine!

Poliwhirl drove itself forward, leaving a long wake across the surface.

Ethan watched it catch hold of the submarine's edge and descend into the ocean with it. Only after both vanished from sight did he slowly look away.

If his Blastoise or Gyarados were here, he would not have needed to trouble Misty. For now, he could only hope that Poliwhirl would locate Team Rocket's secret base and return safely with the information.

Across the beach, the arrests continued.

Although the Team Rocket members had scattered in every direction, the helicopters made it impossible for them to hide. As time passed, more and more were captured and placed in handcuffs.

There was no longer any need for Ethan's group to intervene. When Erika and the others approached, everyone reunited and gathered their Pokemon together.

To avoid causing unnecessary commotion, Ethan had signaled Oliver to hide Silver the moment the helicopters appeared.

Silver's existence could not truly be concealed from the officers. A little questioning would be enough to learn about it from Team Rocket. Even so, keeping Silver out of sight for now would still save them considerable trouble.

At that moment, footsteps sounded nearby, and a woman in a black suit approached the group.

"Mr. Ethan, I didn't expect us to meet again so soon!"

Hearing the familiar voice, Ethan turned and saw Anabel, from whom they had parted only recently!

He nodded to her. "Miss Anabel, thank you for all your hard work!"

"There's no need to say that!" Anabel waved a hand. "Arresting Team Rocket is our duty. I wish we had more opportunities like this, so you truly don't need to be polite with us! Still, I should thank you again. You drew out so many Team Rocket members. You're practically our lucky star!"

Lucky star?

I don't want to be anyone's lucky star.

Besides, Team Rocket wasn't even targeting me this time...

Ethan wanted to explain, but the matter involved Silver, so he reconsidered and remained silent.

After they exchanged a few more pleasantries, Brock suddenly came running from the distance, panting heavily.

Although Brock had not participated in the battle, he had also worked extremely hard. Traveling back and forth to report the crime had been an indispensable contribution!

They chatted a little longer before Anabel left to handle the Team Rocket arrests, leaving Ethan and his companions together once again.

Ash wiped the sweat from his forehead and released a long breath. "Whew, it's finally over!"

"You're relaxing too soon." Ethan shook his head. "Team Rocket must have a secret base somewhere nearby. If everything goes well, we may soon be the ones launching an attack and clearing it out. For now, everyone should rest and treat your Pokemon's injuries!"

At his words, the others recognized that he was right and went to care for their Pokemon.

Only Richie remained where he stood. With a complicated expression, he looked at Ethan and said, "That was truly dangerous. I want to knowif the plan had failed, or if the International Police hadn't arrived in time, what would we have done?"

"Failed?" Ethan glanced at Richie as his right hand unconsciously brushed the two Great Balls in the pouch at his waist. "All we could do was make sure this operation had the greatest possible chance of success. But if it truly reached that point, then of course we would protect Silver with everything we had!"
