Chapter 460: A Dragon-Riding Rescue

Charizard beat its wings and streaked through the sky. The distance between it and the hot-air balloon visibly shrank with every moment.

Inside the balloon, Meowth sensed something behind them. It looked back, and cold sweat instantly covered its body.

"It's Ethan! He's chasing us on Charizard!"

Jessie spun around at Meowth's cry. When she saw Charizard already drawing close, the smile on her face froze!

"What's happening? Didn't we just shake him off?" Her voice was urgent, with none of the triumph she had shown during their escape.

"Idiot! You should've known that was impossible..." James sighed. His tone was gloomy, but his expression clearly said, I expected this all along. "He's Team Rocket's greatest enemy! He isn't as easy to deal with as the twerps. Even the Boss can't do anything about him. How could a couple of smoke bombs possibly hold him back?"

Jessie immediately panicked. "What do we do!? Our balloon can never outrun him!"

"What else can we do? We have to think of something!"

James recovered his composure and pulled a rope with determination. He tried to increase the heat and lift the balloon to an altitude Charizard could never reach, allowing them to escape Ethan's pursuit.

But how could a rising hot-air balloon possibly match Charizard's speed?

An orange blur flashed before the trio's eyes as Charizard swept past them!

The two sides were so close that, under the moonlight, Team Rocket could clearly see Ethan's calm face!

"This is bad! He caught us!" Meowth shouted in complete panic.

James silently groaned. At that moment, he truly wanted to ask who the villains were supposed to be!

How had the glorious Team Rocket been reduced to such a miserable state? They belonged to an evil organization feared wherever its name was heard, yet one man was chasing all three of them away!

Complaints aside, James understood that they had to resolve the situation immediately.

He made a quick decision. "We have to reduce our weight somehow! Otherwise, we'll never escape!"

The instant he spoke, Jessie and Meowth both turned toward Maya in the corner.

Rope bound Maya's hands and feet, while tape covered her mouth. She could neither move nor make a sound.

When she saw the trio looking at her and understood what they intended, Maya's furious eyes became piercing!

Jessie answered without hesitation. "This woman is the heaviest and the most useless thing here. Throw her out first!"

"That doesn't seem right..." A drop of cold sweat rolled down James's face. "She's a living person, and a woman at that. We can't just throw her away. We should get rid of the ballast instead..."

"Are you an idiot? Who has time to worry about that now? Of course we're throwing all of it out!" Meowth scolded James, then turned to Jessie in confusion. "But now that ya mention it... Why did you grab this woman in the first place? Weren't we only supposed to steal the scepter?"

"Well..." Jessie scratched her cheek awkwardly. "She held it too tightly, and I couldn't take the scepter from her, so I brought her along. Anyway, stop wasting time. We only grabbed her by accident, so hurry and throw her out!"

"But..."

James was deeply reluctant. He might be a villain, but no matter what happened, he could never bring himself to throw a woman from the balloon.

The delay gave Charizard enough time to circle the balloon. When it appeared before them again, it suddenly raised its head. A stream of searing fire erupted from its mouth and burned straight through the balloon overhead!

Once the balloon burst, the basket below lost its lift and naturally began plummeting toward the sea.

"Aah!"

The Team Rocket trio screamed miserably. They clung tightly to one another, but as the basket dropped, the violent motion flung all three into the open air!

At least they no longer had to argue about whether to throw Maya overboard. Everyone was airborne now, and no one needed the balloon!

Maya naturally fell with them.

Her hands and feet remained bound. Unable to struggle or call out, she could do nothing but watch the ocean rush closer!

Just as panic filled her heart, a figure flashed before her. Before she could react, she landed within a pair of strong arms!

When Maya recovered and looked up, she was delighted to find Ethan holding her securely!

At the same time, Gengar flew into the air and caught the golden scepter.

Both Maya and the scepter were now safe.

As for Team Rocket, the darkness prevented Ethan from seeing where they had fallen, and he had no time to worry about them.

Even if he had seen them, he probably would have remained unmoved.

Those people had fallen from the sky hundreds of times. Their lives were remarkably durable, so there was no need to worry about whether they survived!

You're on your own.

Ethan was about to speak to Maya when he suddenly noticed the soft, smooth sensation beneath his hands.

He looked down. Maya wore a white silk nightgown. The material was thin and half transparent, and under the moonlight, the contours beneath it were faintly visible.

One of Ethan's hands supported her bottom, while the other held her back!

Both his palms and fingers could clearly feel Maya's firm, warm body.

The vivid sensation struck directly at Ethan's nerves!

Maya noticed the change in his gaze and immediately understood her situation. There was nothing she could do, especially with the ropes preventing even the slightest movement. She lay in his arms like a lamb awaiting its fate!

When Team Rocket entered her room, Maya had been resting. She had possessed no time to change her clothes.

Ethan cleared his throat and looked away. He hurriedly placed Maya on Charizard's back, removed the tape from her mouth, and untied the ropes around her.

"You're safe now."

As he spoke, Ethan removed his jacket and draped it over Maya's shoulders.

"Thank you for saving me."

Maya's face reddened slightly, but she thanked him with natural composure.

She appeared quite calm on the surface, though she inevitably felt shy inside.

Maya might be a Sea Priestess who usually seemed far beyond anyone's reach, but she was still only a woman in her twenties. How could she remain completely unaffected after being held so closely by a man?

Within that atmosphere, she remembered the danger moments ago. Beneath the moonlight, she looked at the handsome face so near her own and suddenly felt her heart, silent for so many years, begin pounding wildly...
