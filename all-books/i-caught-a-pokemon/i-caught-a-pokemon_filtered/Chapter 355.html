Chapter 355: Brock's Desperate Question

Like Nurse Joy, Officer Jenny seemed to appear everywhere, maintaining order in cities across the world. Yet within the police force, the Jennys served both as its public face and as core personnel. If one of them had been dispatched, the incident had clearly caused a major disturbance.

While Nurse Joy went to greet Officer Jenny, a television news van and several engineering vehicles approached from the distance.

Dozens of people soon packed the space outside the Pokemon Center. They began setting up equipment and unloading tools, forcing Ethan and Erika to step aside and give them room.

At that moment, several more people came running from the far end of the road.

Ethan looked carefully. It was Ash and his friends!

Ash ran at the front, with Misty close behind. The person bringing up the rear was Brock, whom Ethan had not seen for a long time. He had replaced Tracey and resumed traveling with the group.

A woman Ethan had never met ran alongside them as well.

When Ash and the others reached the Pokemon Center, they suddenly noticed Ethan. Their eyes lit up, and they hurried toward him.

Before Ash could speak, Misty exclaimed happily, "Ethan! What a coincidence! I can't believe you're here too!"

The moment she finished, she noticed Erika and stared with wide eyes.

"Huh!? Miss Erika!? Why are you here too? And why are you with Ethan?"

"Hello." Erika smiled and nodded to them. "Ash, Misty, Brock, it's good to see you again. As you can see, I'm traveling with Ethan. I came to Johto to catch some new Pokemon."

"You're traveling with him!?"

All three cried out in surprise. Ash and Brock were astonished, but Misty looked as though she had suffered a devastating blow and instantly turned to stone.

For a moment, Ash almost imagined he could hear her heart turning to glass and shattering.

Ethan greeted them as well. "I never expected to meet you here either. What a coincidence. Brock, you're traveling with Ash again. Seeing the three of you together makes me feel nostalgic and happy."

Nostalgic?

Brock scratched his cheek in confusion, naturally unable to understand Ethan's meaning.

Ethan offered no explanation. Instead, he turned to Ash and asked about the most important matter of his journey.

"You've been in Johto for quite a while now. I assume you've already earned several badges?"

Ash grinned, threw open his jacket, and revealed two gleaming badges inside.

"That's right! I already have the Zephyr Badge and the Hive Badge. What about you, Ethan?"

"Two already? You're moving quickly. I'm not much farther ahead. I only earned my third badge a few days ago."

Ash quietly sighed in relief. As long as Ethan had not left him too far behind, he still had a chance to catch up.

Ethan rubbed Misty's hair and brought her back to her senses, then looked at the unfamiliar woman beside the group.

She appeared about Ethan's age. She had long hair and wore a short red top, a red kerchief, and a traveler's backpack. An Aipom sat on her shoulder, completing the appearance of an ordinary Trainer.

"And you are...?"

Before Ash or the others could introduce her, the woman spoke for herself.

"Hello. My name is Lisa. I met Ash and his friends in the forest. Since we were all heading here, we traveled together."

Lisa?

Ethan searched his memory carefully but found no information related to her.

Ash suddenly felt that someone was missing. He looked around and, not seeing Sabrina, asked in surprise, "Ethan, where is Miss Sabrina?"

Ethan sighed. "She returned to the Saffron Gym."

She went back to the Saffron Gym!?

Misty immediately brightened, but after glancing at Erika, she sighed again.

Sabrina leaves, and then Erika appears. Sigh... When will it finally be my turn?

Erika and Ethan appeared to be only ordinary friends for now. But relying on a woman's intuition, Misty felt it was only a matter of time before Erika fell into Ethan's hands.

Meanwhile, the moment Ethan mentioned Sabrina, he noticed something unusual in Brock's expression. Brock looked as though he wanted to say something but held himself back.

Before Ethan could think further, Lisa asked, "We saw a huge mass of crystal from the hilltop. Do you know what happened here?"

"I'm not entirely sure." Ethan shook his head and described what they had witnessed the previous night. He concluded, "Officer Jenny has arrived, though. I think we'll learn more soon."

As he said that, Ethan suddenly realized something.

At first, he had assumed they had merely stumbled across an unexpected incident. The instant he saw Ash, however, he recognized that this must be the plot of one of the movies.

No wonder it had felt familiar.

Still... what was that movie called?

The Emperor's Crystal? Or Entei and the Crystal Tower?

Ethan scratched his head. The memory was far too old, and neither the title nor the story remained clear.

Regardless, now that Ash was here, the truth should soon emerge and the incident would be resolved.

Nurse Joy and Officer Jenny were still speaking outside. Wisely choosing not to disturb them, the group returned to the Pokemon Center's dining room.

They rested there for a while and drank several cups of coffee.

When Erika, Misty, and Lisa went to the restroom together, a figure suddenly flashed before Ethan. Brock dropped to his knees, seized Ethan's hand in both of his, and gazed up at him.

Before Ethan could react, Brock asked with desperate anticipation, "Ethan... No, Brother Ethan! Please teach me how you did it!"

Ethan stared at him in surprise. "How I did what?"

Brock spoke rapidly. "Ash and Misty told me you traveled with Miss Sabrina for a long time, and now you've replaced her with Miss Erika! How did you capture the hearts of two beautiful women and convince them to travel with you? What's the secret!?"

Ethan could not help laughing.

No wonder Brock had looked so hesitant outside. This was what he wanted to ask.

Ethan pulled his hand free with some effort and shook his head.

"Stop talking nonsense. I haven't captured Erika's heart. We're honestly only friends!"
