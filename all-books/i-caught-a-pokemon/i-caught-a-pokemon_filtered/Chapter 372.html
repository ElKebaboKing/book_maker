Chapter 372: Tri-Bird Assault

Ethan released Charizard and had it carry him toward the top of the crystal tower.

Although Charizard had previously endured an immensely powerful attack from Suicune that struck for quadruple damage, a short rest had restored some of its stamina. At least for now, it appeared to have recovered from the worst of its injuries.

For Ethan, the three Legendary birds had once again been exposed in front of other people. But Ash and Misty were already extremely close to him, and both had seen his birds battle the three island birds at Shamouti Island. There was no need to hide the truth from them.

Brock was also a good friend. More significantly, his character made him worthy of trust, so Ethan did not need to conceal anything from him either.

As for Delia, she was an elder whom Ethan trusted to keep the matter private. She was unlikely to reveal his secret.

That left only Molly. But she was still an immature little girl. Even if she told someone that she had seen a Trainer who owned all three Legendary birds, who would believe her?

People would assume it was the fantasy of an innocent child or that Molly lacked the experience to distinguish the Legendary birds from other rare avian Pokemon.

Ethan even doubted that a girl of Molly's age and knowledge knew what the three Legendary birds were.

There was no reason to guard against her.

After reaching the top of the tower, Ethan did not enter. Instead, he had Charizard hover outside so he could look at the others through the collapsed wall.

"How are things in there? What just happened!?"

There was no longer any safe place to stand near the tower's edge. Crystal growths spread throughout the room as sharp spikes erupted one after another. Ash and the others had to order their Pokemon to shatter the endless crystal simply to avoid being skewered.

Crack!

Another thunderclap split the sky.

The sound reflected and traveled through countless crystal formations, startling everyone and seeming powerful enough to rupture their eardrums.

Between the thunder, wind, rain, and snow, Misty had to cup both hands around her mouth like a megaphone for her voice to reach Ethan.

"We don't know! Everything suddenly changed in an instant!!"

Misty sounded confused, but Ethan immediately understood what had happened.

Something had gone wrong with the Unown.

Because of the sudden crisis, the false Entei had stopped fighting Ash. It now protected Molly while avoiding the crystals alongside everyone else.

The Unown had clearly lost control of their power and were attacking indiscriminately.

Ethan pointed toward the bottom of the tower and shouted to Ash, "There's a mass of energy created by the Unown in a room on the lowest level! It must have reached its limit and started going out of control. Hurry down there and stop it!"

Then he repeated his warning.

"Move as fast as you can!!"

Seeing Ethan's grave expression, Ash understood the severity of the situation and nodded solemnly.

"Got it! Leave it to us!"

Ash, Misty, Brock, and Delia hurried toward the petal-shaped staircase. Even Molly and the false Entei understood that they could not remain there. They followed the others down the tower.

Before leaving, Ash took one last envious look at the three Legendary birds soaring through the sky.

Every time he saw those birds, he thought Ethan was incredible.

Ash had envied him many times before, but these were the Legendary birds. An ordinary person might live an entire lifetime without seeing even one, yet Ethan had collected all three.

It was impossible not to admire him!

Ethan knew nothing of Ash's thoughts. After separating from the group, he patted Charizard and had it descend to the ground.

He looked up at the battle. Although the three Legendary birds held a slight advantage, the three Legendary beasts were formidable and would not be defeated easily. Ethan could not tell how much longer the fight might continue.

Given enough time, the birds might find an opportunity to defeat the beasts. But time was running out, and the tower could explode at any moment. They needed to settle the battle quickly.

With that thought, Ethan beckoned Gardevoir over. With her help, he used telepathy to issue an order directly into the minds of the three Legendary birds.

"The situation has changed. We don't have time to waste. Use that move!"

He meant the combination technique they had used against the three island birds at Shamouti Island. That attack had severely wounded those birds and secured victory in the battle.

After further practice, the technique was now fully complete, and Ethan had formally named it Tri-Bird Assault.

Gardevoir's telepathy found its targets and carried Ethan's command into the minds of the three Legendary birds.

Moltres, Articuno, and Zapdos paused when they heard him. Then all three released high cries, abandoned their respective opponents, and flew together.

On the ground, the three Legendary beasts had been engrossed in battle. When their opponents suddenly disappeared, they looked toward the sky in unison.

High above them, three enormous birds of different colors formed a circle and began spiraling upward. Their power drew the clouds into the same motion, sending them whirling rapidly toward the upper sky.

The night grew darker.

Because of their different types, the three Legendary birds radiated red, blue, and yellow light. The colors shone brilliantly in the darkness and were clearly visible even to Professor Oak and the others at the distant Pokemon Center.

Although they stood far from the battlefield, everyone outside the Pokemon Center felt their hearts sink as the black clouds twisted upward like a tornado.

They could all sense that something momentous was about to occur.

"Professor..." The female reporter turned toward Professor Oak and asked cautiously, "What are the three Legendary birds trying to do?"

"I don't know." Professor Oak shook his head. He extended one hand and felt the powerful wind rushing between his fingers. "But whatever it is, what happens next may be terrifying..."

At the center of the battlefield, the fur of all three Legendary beasts stood on end. They were visibly restless.

They did not know what the three Legendary birds intended, but they felt profound fear.

The beasts wanted to flee, yet instinct screamed a frantic warning within them. If they took even a single step, they would face an irresistible judgment from the heavens.
