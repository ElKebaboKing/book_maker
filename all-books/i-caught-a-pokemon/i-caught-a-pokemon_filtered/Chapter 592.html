Chapter 592: Catching Feebas

The beach was broad and close to both the main road and Petalburg City. It was a natural place to raise Pokemon.

New Trainers were everywhere, turning over gravel and rocks in search of Pokemon hidden beneath them.

They were not demanding. A Krabby, Tentacool, Shellder, or almost any other Pokemon would make them happy for a while.

Some Trainers were even battling nearby, testing their skills against one another.

For beginners, this was a hunting ground and one of the easiest places to find wild Pokemon outside forests and grasslands.

Trainers were not the only visitors. Families of three and young couples wandered along the shore too.

Ethan and Erika looked like an ordinary couple. No one paid them much attention beyond a passing thought that they made a good-looking pair.

After enjoying the sea breeze and wading in the shallows, they watched Torchic pace at their side, dodging the waves as though terrified of getting its feathers wet. They both found it funny.

They stood there quietly for a while. Then Erika turned to Ethan. Since we're here, aren't you going to turn over a few rocks like the others? You might find a surprise.

What rare Pokemon would be in a place like this?

Ethan shook his head, unconvinced. But with nothing else to do, he left the sand and made his way among the rocks.

They had moved away from the crowd earlier to avoid being disturbed. No one else was near this secluded part of the shore. The tide was going out, exposing much of the rock that usually lay beneath the sea.

Stepping from one rock to the next, Ethan looked down at his feet.

Seawater remained in the hollows, forming little pools after the tide had retreated. Pokemon that had not escaped in time were stranded in them.

Even on a short walk, he saw quite a few.

A wild Horsea was hiding between two rocks. As Ethan leaped over it, the Pokemon unexpectedly squirted ink at him.

He barely dodged. Frowning, Ethan jumped down and paid it back with a kick that sent it toward the sea.

Horsea spun through the air a dozen times before landing in the water with a splash.

Hmph. Rough as that looked, he had saved its life.

A few steps later, two tentacles lunged at him. Ethan seized them and found a Tentacool trying to ambush him.

He frowned again, pulled the tentacles up, wrapped them around a small rock, and tied a knot.

That would teach it to attack him. Let it see what it could do without free tentacles. It could wait there for someone else to catch it.

After those two encounters, Ethan lost interest in searching. Seeing that he had strayed far from Erika, he found a relatively flat route back.

He had gone only a few steps when he heard a faint flapping sound nearby.

Something was stranded in the shallows, struggling to save itself. He could hardly ignore it. Ethan changed course and went around a huge rock.

On the other side, a fish Pokemon lay in a tiny, shallow pool. Most of its body was above the surface, and it beat its tail repeatedly, trying to work its way back to the sea.

So it had been stranded by the tide.

The sea was still some distance away, separated from the pool by a wide stretch of rock. The fish could barely move now. Even if it could, it would likely dry out before it reached the water.

Ethan looked closer. Its body was dull yellow-brown and uneven, like an ordinary fish made uglier. It had a vacant stare, thick lips, and fins that looked torn and ragged.

Erika came around the rock just then. She had seen Ethan start back toward her and suddenly veer away, so curiosity had brought her over.

She saw the Pokemon, whose appearance was far from her idea of beautiful.

What Pokemon is that? It looks a little... strange.

Erika's good manners let her put her surprise gently. Someone else might have simply called it ugly.

Ethan smiled. Have you heard of a remarkable fish that's terribly ugly before it evolves, but becomes a beautiful Pokemon with scales that shine in different colors under the sun?

There is a Pokemon like that? Erika looked down at the stranded fish. You mean this one?

As a Trainer, she knew plenty about Pokemon, but her expertise was mostly in the Grass types of Kanto and Johto. She had never met this species or even heard of it.

Ethan nodded. It's called Feebas. It can survive even in a filthy ditch. It looks dull, and it's easy to catch, but most Trainers want nothing to do with something so ugly and ragged. Few know how beautiful it becomes after evolving.

He took out his Pokedex and pointed it at Feebas.

Beep. Feebas, the Fish Pokemon. Water type. Feebas lives in many habitats and gathers in large numbers in rivers rich with aquatic plants or on the seabed. Although abundant, they prefer to live in groups. They have remarkable vitality and swim well. This hardy Pokemon will eat anything and does not mind polluted water, making it a subject of study for many researchers.

Ethan called up an image of Milotic and handed the Pokedex to Erika.

See? This is what it becomes. Beautiful, isn't it?

Erika looked at the lovely Pokemon on the screen, then at the Feebas in the pool. She could hardly believe they were connected.

Incredible, she said. There's such a difference before and after evolution. I can't see a trace of its old appearance.

Exactly. Ethan put the Pokedex away. Most people don't know Milotic evolves from Feebas. They treat it as an extremely rare Pokemon in its own right.

Erika glanced around. There was only one Feebas here. She understood.

The Pokedex said they live in groups. It must have been left behind accidentally when the tide went out.

That seems likely. Look at these rocks. They're low, but together they form a wall around the pool. Even though the water receded slowly, it trapped Feebas until the sea was gone and left it exposed to the air.

After all that explanation, Erika saw Ethan take out a Poke Ball. You're going to catch it?

He nodded. I need another Water-type Pokemon. Feebas is weak, and its looks put most Trainers off, but with enough care it will evolve into Milotic. Milotic is beautiful and quite strong.

Ethan threw the ball. Feebas looked so weak that he expected no resistance. But as soon as the red light drew it inside, the ball shook once and Feebas burst back out.

Unwilling to believe it, he picked up the ball and tried again. This time, too, Feebas escaped after barely two seconds.

What a stubborn will to live.

Ethan finally understood why scholars studied the species. Even stranded in a tiny pool, this one still had strength to struggle.

No wonder Feebas could survive in ditches and polluted water.

He waved Torchic over. This one's up to you. Knock it out.

Torchic tilted its head, then opened its beak and used Ember. The little flame struck Feebas without doing much harm and quickly went out in the pool.

Ethan sighed. Don't use Ember. Get closer and use Scratch.

Torchic approached and swiped with a claw. It took two scratches to knock Feebas unconscious.

Ethan threw his Poke Ball at the motionless fish. This time, the capture succeeded.

Good. He had caught his twentieth Pokemon!

Satisfied with his find, Ethan left the beach with Erika.

Before coming here, he had not expected to catch anything. Yet in only two days in Hoenn, he had already caught two Pokemon.

Hoenn really was a treasure trove. There were so many wild Pokemon here. Perhaps that was why its Gym Leaders and Trainers were a little stronger on average than those in Kanto and Johto.

Back on the main road, they headed north and soon reached the entrance to Petalburg Woods.

Night was approaching. They were far from the city, with no houses nearby, so it seemed they would have to camp outdoors.

Since that was settled, they entered the woods while there was still daylight.

If they had to sleep outside, what difference did it make whether they camped in grassland or forest?

Petalburg Woods reminded Ethan of Viridian Forest in Kanto. Dense branches shut out much of the light, leaving the interior dim and easy to get lost in.

Both forests held plenty of Bug-type Pokemon. Petalburg Woods had other species too, giving it a wider variety of life.

And almost as many Trainers came to catch Pokemon here as in Viridian Forest.

They walked for a while. As the sun sank, the whole forest darkened.

Ethan and Erika chose a level spot near water and stopped for the night.

They had barely pitched their tent when a huge Zigzagoon approached their camp in a zigzagging path.

Ordinary Zigzagoon were small, less than half a meter tall and weighing a little over thirty pounds. This one was nearly six feet tall, almost the size of a person.

Ethan wondered if they had met a dominant wild Pokemon and trespassed on its territory. He was about to warn his team to be ready when the Zigzagoon rose onto its hind legs and stood like a human.

Before he could recover from that surprise, it reached up with both hands and pulled off its own head.

In the fading light, Ethan stared. But as the figure stepped closer, he saw it was no Zigzagoon. It was a big boy wearing a Zigzagoon costume. The head he had removed was only a mask.

Hey, you two!

Before Ethan could speak, the boy called out, Don't camp here! You're ruining my plan!

His angry tone made Ethan narrow his eyes.

Why? Does this forest belong to you? he replied coldly. We'll camp where we like.

The boy ran up to him. I've been hiding here for half the day trying to get close to the Zigzagoon! Then you arrived and scared them all away!

Before setting up camp, Ethan had seen several Zigzagoon on the clearing. He had paid them little mind. He had simply sent Torchic charging forward, and they had fled into the trees. Looking back, he supposed this was their habitat.

Who would have imagined someone dressed like this was hiding nearby?

Up close, Ethan noticed that the boy looked remarkably like Ash. Apart from his slightly different hairstyle, he might have been a larger, equally impulsive version of him.

If Ethan had not known Delia had only one child, he might have mistaken this boy for Ash's long-lost older brother.

So what? Ethan glanced at him. If the boy had spoken politely, Ethan would have been friendly in return. With that attitude, he saw no reason to smile. If they ran off, go after them. Or can you only catch them by dressing up and sneaking close enough for an ambush?
