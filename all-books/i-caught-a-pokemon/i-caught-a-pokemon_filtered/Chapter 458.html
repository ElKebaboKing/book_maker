Chapter 458: An Eerie Wind Blows

Late at night, inside the Temple of the Sea.

The temple seemed to have fallen completely silent. Almost no sound could be heard except for the violent whirlpool below the distant cliff, which continued rushing tirelessly and filled the night with the endless roar of water.

Ethan quietly moved through the corridors. The surroundings were so still that only his own footsteps echoed again and again. Gengar accompanied him, but the Pokemon made no sound when it moved. Like a shadow, its presence felt almost no different from walking alone.

During the day, the temple had appeared solemn and mysterious. At night, its entire atmosphere transformed, becoming strangely eerie and frightening.

Ethan and Gengar had not traveled far before he noticed that, for some unknown reason, the corridor contained no electric lights or other modern illumination. Instead, an oil lamp hung every few meters. Their hazy flames lit the entire passage and brought a faint, dim glow to the night.

That was not difficult to understand. How could a temple display its mystery without deliberately creating a little atmosphere?

The arrangement also provided another benefit. The burning flames dispersed some moisture from the air and kept the Temple of the Sea's interior dry.

After walking another dozen meters, Ethan turned toward Gengar beside him.

"Where exactly did you see the Team Rocket trio?"

Gengar raised one short, thick arm and pointed ahead. "Not far in front. There's a shortcut nearby that can take us around them. Follow me!"

Under Gengar's guidance, Ethan reached a staircase around the next corner. Gengar manipulated something on a nearby lampstand, and a hidden door suddenly appeared in the wall!

"Go inside." Gengar cackled. "I discovered this secret passage while exploring earlier. It connects to most parts of the temple and is extremely well hidden."

It was hard to imagine that Gengar had uncovered even the temple's secret passages in only a few hours!

Wasn't that practically the same as exposing the other side's greatest secret?

Then again, Pokemon of Gengar's lineage generally enjoyed passing through walls and loved dark places. It was hardly surprising for one to discover such a secret.

Beyond the hidden door, the world grew even darker. A narrow, low passage stretched ahead, just wide enough for a single person.

Unlike the corridor outside, the secret tunnel was so dark that Ethan could not see his hand before his face. Gengar had long adapted to darkness and encountered no difficulty, but Ethan was human and possessed no such ability.

With no alternative, he released Jolteon and had it gather electricity to illuminate the path beneath his feet.

Ethan followed the passage through turn after turn and several intersections. Eventually, they passed through another hidden door and left the tunnel.

Before Ethan could determine where he was, footsteps suddenly sounded in the distance and drew closer little by little. With no time to think, he hurriedly recalled Jolteon and concealed himself in the darkness.

...

Deep within the Temple of the Sea, the Team Rocket trio crept quietly through the nearby passages.

It was far too late to use a flashlight. Without a map and unable to ask anyone for directions, Jessie and James had become completely lost after sneaking inside and had wandered back and forth for a long time.

Both now felt their lower legs aching and weak. They could not take another step.

Finding a stone stair, James simply gave up and sat down. He massaged his legs and frowned. "Hey, Jessie, didn't we already pass through this place? Why does it look so familiar?"

"Did we?" Jessie leaned against the stone pillar beside him and let her tongue hang out like a dog, apparently hoping it would ease her exhaustion. "Everything in here looks the same. You must be mistaken!"

Meowth's expression changed when he heard them. He hurriedly jumped over and made a shushing gesture. "Shh! Keep your voices down! Don't wake the guards!"

"What are you afraid of?" Jessie curled her lip carelessly. "The tournament is over, so Officer Jenny and the police have already left. Only a few women and those guards at the entrance remain. If they dare get in our way, I'll take care of them!"

James hesitated. "But I saw Ethan and the twerps move in here too. If we wake them, we'll be in real trouble!"

"So what if they're here?" Jessie forced out the brave words, though her expression clearly lacked confidence. "We'll leave the instant we get the scepter. It's pitch-black outside. Once we pass through the doors, we can hide behind any tree we like. They won't find us even if they try to chase us!"

Jessie's reasoning sounded convincing enough to James and Meowth, easing some of their concern.

What none of them knew was that Ethan was watching from the shadows beside them with narrowed eyes!

So these people... have set their sights on Maya's scepter?

After overhearing their entire conversation, Ethan finally understood their objective.

The scepter was made from solid gold, while the Sea Spirit mounted upon it was a priceless treasure with tremendous symbolic importance. If Team Rocket truly stole it, the loss would deal a devastating blow to Maya, the Temple of the Sea, and the entire Whirl Islands.

Ethan could only wonder whether targeting the scepter had been the trio's own idea or an order from Team Rocket's leadership.

The resting trio naturally had no idea that the person they feared most was hiding only a short distance away!

After they rested for another moment, a draft suddenly swept through the corridor, making all three shudder at once.

James pulled his clothes tighter and complained, "This miserable place looked perfectly fine during the day. Why does it feel so eerie and frightening at night!?"

"You're imagining things. It's all in your head." Jessie rolled her eyes. "Honestly, how can you call yourself a member of Team Rocket when you're this timid?"

James looked around. The longer he studied the place, the more it made his skin crawl. He urged, "All right, we've rested long enough. Let's hurry and keep moving!"

"Coward..."

Jessie was about to continue when an eerie laugh suddenly reached their ears. It sounded near and far at once, as though something were laughing directly beside them. Yet when they turned to look, they saw nothing!

Even so, the laughter continued to echo clearly in their ears!

Gulp!

All three swallowed unconsciously. After exchanging glances, they hurriedly scrambled to their feet.

Jessie looked at James and forced a dry smile. "Y-you have a point. We really shouldn't stay here. Let's hurry and steal that scepter!"

After what had happened, neither James nor Meowth would agree to walk in front. One grown man and one Pokemon pushed Jessie ahead of them instead.

Jessie wanted to curse the two cowards shoving her forward. She was naturally terrified as well, but since she had proposed the operation, she could only summon her courage and lead the way...
