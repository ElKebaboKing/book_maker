Chapter 738: The Curtain Falls

Ethan decided to let the birds stop resisting. They had held back the worst of the shock wave. What remained would lose strength crossing the wide sea before it reached the Hoenn mainland. At worst it might arrive as a severe storm. Cities and villages could still suffer damage, but human society could withstand it.

He had done enough. Now he needed to focus entirely on Primal Groudon and Primal Kyogre.

It was time for them to experience the three birds' combined strength.

The birds had used Tri-Bird Assault when Ethan caught Deoxys. Deoxys had blocked it with both a barrier and its Defense Forme, yet the attack had destroyed half its body. Only its rapid regeneration had made it appear unharmed afterward.

He wanted to see how Groudon and Kyogre would take it.

They were too far away for Gardevoir to pass on his orders directly. Ethan found another way: he sent the instructions to Deoxys by telepathy and had Deoxys relay them to the birds. Before long, contact was restored.

At his command, Articuno, Zapdos, and Moltres beat their wings and climbed above the clouds. They drew together and began gathering power.

The collision of Precipice Blades and Origin Pulse had finally subsided. Its shock waves were still spreading, but Groudon and Kyogre had resumed fighting at the center. Both bore fresh wounds. Their moves had canceled each other out, but the explosion had hurt them all the same.

As for Rayquaza, Ethan searched the battlefield again. The once-verdant large island had been all but wiped away. Broken branches and other debris floated on the water and steadily sank. Rayquaza was nowhere to be seen.

The blast must have driven it beneath the sea. Ethan doubted its life was in danger, but at such close range, the explosion might have knocked it unconscious. It could take some time to recover.

If only it had listened to him. He shook his head. There was no time to look for it now.

Ethan raised his eyes to the clouds above the battle. The explosion had scattered much of them along with everything below. Sunlight had broken through, and the extreme weather had vanished. That respite lasted only a moment.

Under the three birds' power, thick clouds formed again. Lightning flashed and thunder followed. The sky over the sea darkened once more.

Groudon and Kyogre noticed none of it. Intent on each other, they collided at close quarters and kept fighting, oblivious to what was building overhead.

Go on. Keep fighting. Now it's your turn to take an attack from me.

The birds' dance continued above the clouds. After a few minutes, they had gathered enough power. A tremendous peal of thunder sounded, and at their direction a column of energy poured from the sky like a waterfall toward Groudon and Kyogre.

Only then did the two primal Pokemon sense something wrong. They looked up as the torrent descended around them. It was too late to dodge; in their confusion, neither even seemed capable of reacting to the danger. They could only take the blow.

Within moments, the energy swallowed them both. Ethan and the others could no longer see either Pokemon. All that remained before them was the dazzling column and the harsh hum of its power as it poured down.

Maxie, Archie, and Matt had never seen Tri-Bird Assault before. Their mouths hung open. If they had not witnessed it themselves, they would never have believed Pokemon other than Primal Groudon and Primal Kyogre could produce an attack this strong.

That man truly was a monster.

The torrent stopped in less than half a minute. The birds had already spent part of their strength holding back the explosion, so they could not sustain this attack as long as they had before. It was enough.

As the light cleared, everyone looked toward the center of the battlefield. Both ancient Pokemon lay on the ruined island. After a long pause, Primal Kyogre stirred and slowly lifted itself from the ground. It was covered in wounds, but it could still move.

It had actually endured the attack. Its exceptional special defense and stamina had surely helped.

Primal Groudon did not rise. It remained where it had fallen. The glow along its body faded, and it reverted from its primal state to its ordinary form.

Groudon had given out first. Kyogre had been the one surrounded and attacked earlier, and both had suffered from the collision of their signature moves. Groudon had started with more strength, yet it was the one that fell. Its hard hide gave it formidable physical defense, but that could not protect it from this torrent of energy.

Ethan let out a breath. The birds could use Tri-Bird Assault only once in this fight. If it had accomplished little, he would have needed another plan. Instead, it had knocked Groudon out and left Kyogre badly injured. The situation had turned in their favor.

He called the weakened birds back and was about to send Deoxys in to finish the fight when the sea beside the battlefield burst open. His black Rayquaza surged out of the water.

It was covered in wounds, much like Primal Kyogre. The blast from Precipice Blades and Origin Pulse had injured it badly. Even so, Rayquaza charged at Kyogre without hesitation.

Ethan stopped Deoxys and let Rayquaza have its chance.

By now, the outcome was nearly settled. Ethan had prepared other options for this battle. He had even made an arrangement with the two Deoxys he had met in LaRousse: if his Deoxys produced an aurora, they would come to help. It appeared he would not need to call them.

Once Groudon and Kyogre failed to withstand Tri-Bird Assault, their defeat became all but certain. The one still standing might turn its attacks entirely on Ethan's Pokemon, but it no longer had the strength to change the battle.

Then there was the unconscious Groudon.

Ethan studied it for a long time, torn over what to do, before looking away. Was Groudon strong? Of course. It belonged among the most powerful Pokemon in the world, especially after Primal Reversion.

Did he want to catch it? The thought tempted him a little. Only a little.

There were Pokemon he liked far more. His feelings toward Groudon and Kyogre were lukewarm, and he did not need to catch every powerful Pokemon he encountered. Keeping one of these two with him might bring more trouble than it was worth. Better to let them sleep undisturbed in hidden places and allow their legends to live on.

Rayquaza and Primal Kyogre were fighting again. Though both were badly injured, the difference in strength soon put Rayquaza on the defensive.

Then the balance changed. As Kyogre's remaining strength fell, its Primal Reversion ended. Its body visibly shrank back to its usual size, and the -shaped markings vanished within seconds.

Look! Misty said, brightening. It doesn't have enough energy left to keep its primal form!

Ethan hurriedly took the two orbs from his bag. Their glow had returned to normal. The connection between them and the ancient Pokemon had plainly broken.

The orbs were only a trigger. They could induce Primal Reversion and make Groudon and Kyogre stronger, but they did not supply the energy to sustain it. That energy came from the Pokemon themselves, and the stronger form consumed it at a tremendous rate. Once their reserves fell below a certain point, they reverted.

With Kyogre back in its ordinary form, the fight turned again. It was no match for the black Rayquaza now. After being pushed back for so long, Rayquaza finally gained the upper hand and pressed its attack.

Kyogre seemed to lose much of its fighting spirit along with its primal form. Unable to withstand Rayquaza's assault, it turned and fled. This was the second time it had left a battle in defeat.

Rayquaza tried to pursue it. Ethan called it back through telepathy. Still unaccustomed to being a Trainer's Pokemon, Rayquaza did stop chasing Kyogre, but it did not return. It darted back and forth over the sea, as though proclaiming itself the battle's true victor.

Ethan had no patience for that behavior. He gave Gardevoir the Ultra Ball. She Teleported within range and recalled Rayquaza herself.

As Gardevoir returned, several dark shapes crossed the sky. Ethan looked up. The three Legendary birds folded their wings and landed beside him.

It's finally over, Shelly said, surveying the devastation. What do we do now?

Ethan looked across the battlefield. Kyogre had gone so far that it was barely a dark speck in the distance. Groudon lay where it had fallen, showing no sign of waking.

Farther out, Courtney's ship sat beyond the battle zone. She had rescued most of the teammates who had fallen into the water, but the ship had taken damage from being so close. It seemed to have lost power and remained motionless.

Ethan recalled the birds, glanced at Maxie and Archie, and answered Shelly. First we'll wake Groudon and get it away from here. I notified the International Police on our way here, so they should arrive soon. We'll hand these people over when they do, explain what happened with you and Courtney, and then I'll return the two orbs. That should settle everything.

Yes. It was over. Maxie and Archie were both in custody. Their commanders had either left their organizations or been captured. Even without a wider pursuit, Team Aqua and Team Magma would fall apart sooner or later.

After all these years, Shelly had never expected it to end this way. Yet now that the moment had come, she felt strangely uncertain.

What am I supposed to do next?
