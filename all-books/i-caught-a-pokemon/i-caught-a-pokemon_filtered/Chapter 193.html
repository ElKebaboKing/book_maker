Chapter 193: An Ornament of Never-Melt Ice

"Please take a look, Mr. Ethan," Mateo said. "It took a day longer than I expected, but I've made what you asked for."

Dark circles lay beneath his eyes, and he looked exhausted. Ethan felt a twinge of guilt and patted his shoulder. "You worked hard. Sit down and rest for a while."

Mateo handed him a box that gave off cold air. Ethan opened it and examined the ornament inside.

Somehow Mateo had shaped the Never-Melt Ice into something like a necklace. It covered part of the neck, almost like a short collar. The material remained bitterly cold to the touch. After only a few seconds, Ethan had to put it on the ground.

Sabrina came over to look and gently nudged it with the tip of her shoe. The ice was remarkably hard and showed no sign of melting despite being reshaped. Whether it could survive a hard Pokemon battle was another question.

Ethan glanced at Mateo and took the Great Ball marked with a snowflake from his pouch. Mateo already knew about Articuno, so Ethan released it without trying to conceal it.

Articuno appeared and stretched its wings lazily. Their stay on Sunburst Island had felt like a holiday, and Ethan had let it out for fresh air each day.

Before it could take in its surroundings, Mateo hurried toward it, staring with open fascination.

"So this is the legendary Articuno? I've only seen photographs. Look at its bearingand those feathers! It's incredible."

Mateo kept talking to himself and moved too close. Articuno bristled, about to attack, but Ethan stopped it in time.

"Easy," he told Articuno. Then he turned to Mateo. "Please give it some room. You might get hurt."

"Ah, I'm sorry." Mateo stepped back. "Seeing a legendary Pokemon in person got me carried away."

With Mateo's help, Ethan lifted the ornament and fitted it around Articuno's neck. Mateo had judged the size perfectly, and Ethan liked the way it looked. He still could not be sure whether wearing it would strengthen Articuno's Ice-type moves.

"Thank you," Ethan said. "This is better than I expected."

Mateo seemed to want to ask something. Ethan smiled, kept his promise, recalled Articuno, and brought out the Crystal Onix.

After his experience with Articuno, Mateo waited for Ethan's permission before approaching. He studied the Pokemon closely, walking around it again and again.

He had imagined that seeing the Onix he had dreamed about for so long would give him the same insight his grandfather had found. After dozens of circuits, nothing had come to him. He frowned.

"Is something wrong?" Ethan asked.

Mateo sighed. "It's beautiful, but I don't feel any sudden inspiration. Seeing Articuno just now affected me more."

Ethan thought back to the animated story. "Then why not battle it? You might see something in the way it moves and fights."

"A battle?" Mateo considered it, then nodded. "All right. It's worth trying."

Ethan and Sabrina backed away to make room in the guesthouse courtyard. There was no battlefield nearby, so this would have to do.

"I won't give the Onix commands," Ethan told Mateo. "Battle it however you like. But this is a guesthouse. Keep it under control and don't break anything."

"I understand."

Mateo stepped back and released his Pokemon. It was a Cloyster, a Water and Ice type.

Against an ordinary Onix, that would be a sensible choice. Mateo clearly did not know this Crystal Onix shrugged off Water-type attacks. Ethan said nothing and watched.

Seeing his expression, Mateo gave a rueful smile. "I caught Cloyster a long time ago because I hoped to use it against the Crystal Onix. That won't happen now, of course."

Ethan had caught the Onix first. Mateo had missed his chance to make it his own.

When the battle began, Mateo ordered Cloyster to attack. Its Water Gun struck the Crystal Onix, which showed no concern at all.

"What? Why didn't it work?"

Disbelieving, Mateo ordered several more Water Guns. The Onix answered with Iron Tail, sending Cloyster spinning away.

Mateo stared. "That's impossible. Why don't Water-type moves hurt it?"

"I forgot to mention it," Ethan said calmly. "It was living in a lake when I found it. Something about its body seems to protect it from water."

"Really?" Mateo's surprise only deepened his interest.

He recalled Cloyster, thought for a moment, and sent out Charmeleon.

"I'm not much of a trainer. These are the only two Pokemon I own. But don't underestimate Charmeleon because it hasn't fully evolved. It helps me make glass. It did a great deal of the work on Articuno's ornament, too."

Mateo faced the Crystal Onix. "If water won't work, let's see what my other partner can do."
