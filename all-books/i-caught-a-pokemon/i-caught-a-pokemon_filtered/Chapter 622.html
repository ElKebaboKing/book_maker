Chapter 622: Traps and Raikou at Mauville Gym

After strolling through the park and unwinding, Ethan and Misty checked into the local Pokemon Center. They didn't eat lunch there, though. They went to the nearby Mauville Food Court instead.

Despite its name, the food court was an indoor restaurant area on a basement level. Stall followed stall; there appeared to be a hundred of them, selling food from Hoenn and other regions alike. They arrived at lunchtime, when the place was packed. Almost every one of the hundreds of tables in the central hall was occupied.

With so many stalls cooking at once, their combined fumes made the air smell strange and a little sharp. Ethan and Misty worked through the dense crowd, looking over the stalls. After a long search, they chose a few meals with peculiar names.

Misty ordered a Village Sandwich. It contained sliced meat, bacon, ham, tuna, and egg, none of which explained the name. Had someone brought the recipe from a village?

Ethan chose Magnemite-yaki. Despite the name, it didn't contain a Magnemite. It was simply a selection of okonomiyaki, akashiyaki, imagawayaki, dorayaki, tonpeiyaki, and takoyaki.

Carrying their food, they searched again before finding half an empty table in a corner. At last they could eat. The food court's reputation was deserved: besides its enormous size, the food was delicious. They finished their lunches, still wanted more, and each ordered a bowl of miso ramen. Only then were they full.

To walk off the meal, they visited another of Mauville's famous attractions: the Mauville Game Corner. Like the Celadon Game Corner in Kanto, it was large and equipped with novel machines. They bought tokens and spent a happy while trying almost every kind of game. Before leaving, Ethan won a Starmie plush for Misty from a claw machine.

They returned to the Pokemon Center thoroughly satisfied. They hadn't challenged the Gym, but the afternoon had been full all the same. Misty especially felt as though she had been on a date with Ethan, and she could hardly have been happier.

...

After a night's rest, Ethan and Misty returned to Mauville Gym early the next morning. The notice had been removed from the door. The Gym was open again, and this time they could go inside.

The moment they entered, the building had a surprise for them. A long, enclosed corridor stretched beyond the entrance. As soon as they stepped into it, the floor trembled, then began to move like a horizontal elevator, carrying them along a barely visible track into the Gym.

Whoa! Misty staggered in surprise and grabbed Ethan's sleeve to keep from falling. She wasn't timid. Anyone would be startled by a moving floor with no warning.

The platform sped down the corridor and passed through an electric gate. Then the track sloped upward, and they began to climb.

What is this? Misty grumbled. Isn't this a Gym? Why does it feel like an amusement park?

It's an Electric-type Gym, Ethan said with a smile. Its Pokemon probably give it plenty of power. They might as well find a use for the electricity instead of wasting it.

Maybe, but... The platform kept rising, and Misty had a bad feeling. Don't tell me this is

It reached the top before she could finish. Then it lurched forward and hurtled downhill like a roller coaster.

Aaaah!

Misty screamed. A roller coaster in an amusement park wouldn't normally frighten her this much. Here they were simply standing on a moving platform, without seats or even safety belts.

Ethan caught a handrail with one hand and wrapped the other arm around Misty's waist. That kept them from being thrown off as the platform raced through the narrow passage.

A dozen or so seconds later, the space ahead opened into a large room. They thought they had reached the end of the ride, but the track stopped at the entrance. Beyond it lay a deep pool.

The platform shook as it left the rails. It skipped violently across the water like a flat stone, nearly bouncing them into the pool. Even Ethan lost his grip. Both of them flew into the airthen, as though the whole contraption had been carefully designed for this, landed safely back on the platform.

It crossed the room, caught another track at the far side, and carried them deeper into the Gym. They got up and rubbed their aching backsides.

Before Misty could complain, another electric gate opened at the end of the passage. Beyond it stood a magnificent golden Pokemon. Ethan recognized it immediately; he owned one himself.

Raikou? Misty cried. She had never imagined seeing a Legendary Pokemon from Johto inside a Hoenn Gym.

The moving platform began to slow, but its momentum still carried them right up to Raikou. It roared and opened its mouth. Electricity flickered insidea clear warning that an attack was coming.

They had nowhere to dodge while standing on the platform. Whatever this Gym was trying to do, Ethan guessed the encounter was some sort of test for challengers. He wasn't going to sit still for it.

Misty reached for a Poke Ball, but Ethan held out a hand to stop her and threw one of his own. Blue light flashed, and Tyranitar appeared before Raikou.

Tyranitar roared and charged without caring whether its opponent was a Legendary Pokemon. Ethan used the moment to study the Raikou more closely.

He knew how unlikely it was for one to appear in Hoenn. Even if Raikou existed as a small population rather than a single Pokemon, they were extraordinarily rare. Finding a second one in Johto would be difficult enough, let alone finding one captured by a Gym Leader here. Ethan already carried a Raikou in his own Poke Ball, making the coincidence even less plausible.

Now that he looked carefully, he spotted something wrong. The figure before him had Raikou's imposing appearance, but lacked its living presence. Its eyes were empty, like those of a cold machine.

A replica might fool someone who had never met Raikou. It could not fool Raikou's own Trainer.
