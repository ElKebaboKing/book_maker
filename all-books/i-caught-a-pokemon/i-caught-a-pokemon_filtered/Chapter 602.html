Chapter 602: It's a Matter of Character

After landing its first blow, Dragonite kept up its momentum and wheeled back toward Pelipper. This time Anthony did not dare meet it head-on. He attacked from a distance instead.

His retreat told Ethan how little confidence lay behind his bluster, and Dragonite pressed its advantage.

Pelipper parted its bill slightly, sending a bolt of electricity at Dragonite. With a small turn and its tremendous flying speed, Dragonite dodged easily. It closed the distance and drove its head into Pelipper's belly.

The impact dented Pelipper's stomach. Its bill flew open in spite of itself, nearly exposing its secret. It caught itself just in time, clamped its bill shut, and fled farther away.

Ethan traced a circle with his finger. Dragonite caught the gesture with a glance from above and immediately gave chase.

High over the battlefield, Pelipper was fleeing toward the other side when it looked back and saw Dragonite bearing down on it again. It opened its bill a fraction. A dazzling jet of fire burst out: Flamethrower!

Along with the Grass-type Vine Whip and Electric-type Thunder Shock it had used earlier, this Fire-type attack brought its displayed repertoire to five types when its own Water- and Flying-type moves were counted. It certainly seemed versatile.

Anyone who did not know its secret might have been fooled.

Dragonite met the flames calmly. It beat its wings and used Hurricane, blowing the Flamethrower straight back toward Pelipper.

Pelipper was startled. It had not produced those flames with its own power, and they could burn it just as easily as anything else.

Fortunately for Pelipper, the wind from Hurricane was fierce. Though it blew the fire toward Pelipper, the opposing gust also lifted the bird and carried it clear of the flames in an instant.

But Pelipper had barely steadied itself when Dragonite drew close without a sound. Water wrapped around Dragonite's long tail. It twisted and lashed out with Aqua Tail, striking Pelipper squarely across the face.

Smack!

The hit landed hard. This time Pelipper could not keep its bill closed. It opened wide, and three conspicuous Poke Balls flew out amid a spray of saliva.

Pelipper crashed to the ground. The three balls fell beside it and bounced several times.

One rolled all the way to Misty's feet. She stared at it for a moment before she understood what it was and what Ethan had meant earlier.

So you're a fraud! She glared at Anthony. I actually thought your Pelipper was something special. All you've been doing is fooling people with this trick! And you still dare call it the strongest Pokemon in the world? You have no shame!

Anthony's face went pale. With his lie exposed, he said nothing. He did not dare defend himself.

He took out a Poke Ball and recalled his unconscious Pelipper, then hurried onto the field to gather the three fallen balls. Without stopping, he slunk away from the battlefield.

He looked nothing like the arrogant man who had stood there a moment ago.

The secret behind Pelipper's Electric-, Grass-, and Fire-type attacks was simple: Anthony had hidden several Poke Balls inside its bill before the battle. Whenever it needed a particular attack, Pelipper moved its tongue to release the appropriate Pokemon.

Once that Pokemon had used its move, Pelipper moved its tongue again to recall it into its ball. An opponent watching from outside would be none the wiser.

The technique took skill. Anthony and Pelipper must have practiced it extensively to perform it so smoothly, without anyone noticing and without Pelipper being caught by the attacks inside its own bill.

If Anthony had devoted that time and effort to training instead of tricks, Pelipper could have grown much stronger. With that dedication alone, it might have been able to beat ordinary Trainers honestly.

In the end, Anthony's attempt to take a shortcut had only hurt him.

Misty was still talking indignantly at Anthony's retreating back. Ethan shook his head and patted her shoulder.

Come on. Let's go.

Huh? Misty looked at him in surprise. We're just leaving? He's deceived so many people. If we walk away now, he's getting off far too easily!

What else can we do? Ethan said with a shrug. Ultimately, this is a problem with his character. The people he beat haven't really suffered any loss. What would calling the police accomplish? And did you see his face when he left? He was embarrassed that he'd been exposed, but he wasn't ashamed. Once we're gone, he won't change his ways. He'll keep using the same trick to fool people.

Then what are we supposed to do? Misty grew angrier. Just stand by while he keeps beating Trainers who have no idea what's happening?

Misty. Ethan rubbed the top of her head. The world isn't made up of right and wrong alone. People have their dark sides too. We can't stand by when we face truly bad people, but with someone like him, there's little we can do. One day he'll meet someone he can't push around, suffer a serious loss, and get the lesson he deserves.

Then let's tell everyone what he's doing! Misty said, unwilling to let it go. Once they know how underhanded he is, no one else will fall for it!

Ethan shook his head. That would take us a great deal of time. Anthony could simply move somewhere else, and all our effort would be wasted. Come on. Don't let someone like him trouble you any longer.

Between reassurance and persuasion, Ethan finally led Misty away.

The encounter showed him how differently the women in his life would have reacted. If Erika had been beside him, she would have shaken her head, laughed, and put the matter behind her, just as he had.

Only Misty, so young, energetic, and full of a sense of justice, would keep arguing over it.

With Sabrina, it would have been simpler still. A wave of her little hand, and Anthony would have become a doll, left in a corner for a few years!

Fortunately, Misty's anger never lasted long. Once they were back on the road, she soon forgot Anthony altogether. Before long, she was her cheerful, lively self again.
