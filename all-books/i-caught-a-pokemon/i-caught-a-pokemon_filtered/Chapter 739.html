Chapter 739: It's All Over

Just as Ethan had predicted, several International Police patrol boats appeared on the horizon about half an hour later, speeding toward them.

After receiving Shelly's and Courtney's messages, he had contacted Anabel before coming here. Her patrol boat had spent a long time at sea, but it had finally arrived.

They never seemed to be there when he needed help most, yet the International Police never missed the aftermath. Late, but here at last. Fortunately, Anabel had remained offshore nearby. Had she gone ashore, she could not have reached them so quickly.

While they waited, Ethan had Misty's Water-type Pokemon spray Groudon with clean water to wake it. Groudon proved sensible enough. When it opened its eyes, its old rival was gone and several formidable opponents were watching it closely. Without hesitation, it dragged its wounded body toward the sea and kept walking. Before their eyes, the water swallowed it completely.

Too heavy to swim on the surface, it could only hold its breath and leave along the seabed. The sight hardly suited the dignity of an ancient Legendary Pokemon. But then, as Ethan had observed more than once, Groudon could not fly.

With both ancient Pokemon gone, the crisis was truly over. Now came the cleanup.

There was little left on the battlefield to clear. Ethan and the others recalled the Pokemon they no longer needed, keeping only Dragonite and Gengar out to guard the prisoners.

Courtney left Team Magma's ship in a speedboat, bringing the captive Tabitha with her. Most of the Team Magma and Team Aqua members she had pulled from the water took the remaining speedboats and lifeboats and fled when they saw the patrol boats. Only a few with serious injuries could not be moved.

After a battle like that, the escapees were too shaken to think of rescuing their leaders. Their leaders? Their own lives mattered more.

Ethan made no effort to stop them. They were minor members. With both leaders and their commanders captured, what could the rest accomplish? At worst they might cause local disturbances, and Officer Jennys across Hoenn could deal with that without calling in the International Police.

The engine grew louder. Courtney reached the little island, brought Tabitha ashore, and marched him toward them.

Well done. Ethan sent Gengar to meet her. Gengar tied Tabitha up beside Maxie and the others. Ethan then turned to Courtney. I wondered where you'd gone. Setting an ambush along the escape route caught him completely off guard. Thanks to you, we've got both leaders and all their commanders.

Shelly rolled her eyes and pursed her lips, looking almost jealous, but for once she did not argue with Courtney. She had to admit that without Courtney's kick, Tabitha might have escaped. That would have spoiled an otherwise complete victory.

She did not look at Courtney, but two men beside her stared at the former commander with open hatred: Maxie and Tabitha. Maxie had abandoned Courtney first, yet she had undeniably turned against Team Magma, joined Ethan, and helped bring them down. A traitor, as far as they were concerned. They wanted to tear her apart. Bound and defeated, they could do nothing but curse her silently.

Courtney paid them no attention. After everything that had happened, she had fallen for Ethan just as Shelly had. His praise brought a faint smile to her lips and a new brightness to her eyes.

That only made Shelly more jealous. Courtney had fallen for him quickly.

A rhythmic roar sounded overhead. A helicopter crossed the sea and descended onto the island. Its door opened, and Anabel jumped out.

Well, well. She looked Ethan over in amazement. You've made a spectacle of yourself again.

The birds had stopped most of the escaping energy, but some had become wind, cloud, and rain that spread across the surrounding sea and coastal areas. Hurricanes and enormous waves had struck the islands. The patrol boats approaching the battlefield had pitched violently in the storm.

I didn't cause this. Ethan nodded toward Maxie and the other prisoners and shrugged. They did. I was merely passing by, happened to recover the orbs, happened to catch them, and happened to resolve the whole thing.

That is quite a lot of things to happen by chance. Anabel smiled at his joke. Whatever you call it, thank you. You spared Hoenn's people a terrible crisis and brought the leaders of Team Magma and Team Aqua to justice. Everyone can see what you've done. We won't forget it.

It's nothing. I like testing myself in situations like this and growing stronger. Personally, I'd welcome more of them. He paused, then added with a half-smile, Though a more tangible reward would be nice.

He meant it as a small joke. To his surprise, Anabel nodded.

There is a reward.

Really? Ethan stared at her. There actually is?

Of course. The higher-ups have decided to give you the honorary title of Special Investigator of the International Police. You'll receive a cash award as well.

Special Investigator? And cash?

His eyes lit up. The title meant little to him. It was honorary; it would neither change his life nor give him license to do as he pleased. The money, thoughthat was welcome. He had no idea how much it would be, but he was pleased to get any at all.

Maxie's and Archie's faces darkened. They knew exactly why Ethan was receiving an award, and it had come at their expense. Their fury accomplished nothing.

The decision was made some time ago, Anabel continued. You know how slow approvals can be in a department like ours. It's nearly finished now, and you should receive everything soon.

How much money?

She kept that to herself. You'll find out when it arrives. I can tell you it won't be less than the prize money you won at a League Conference.

That was vague. The Indigo and Silver Conferences took place on the same landmass and split the field between them. Competition at each had been thinner, and their prizes smaller. He had heard the Ever Grande Conference paid generously, but which one did she mean?

Even at the lower end, it would be two or three million. A sum like that for nothing more than what he had already done was excellent news.

So I wasn't working for free after all, he said with a grin. But a title and a reward? It sounds as though you're trying to tie me to the International Police.

Is that strange? Anabel asked frankly. A Trainer as strong as you, with sound principles, is exactly the kind of partner we want. For the stability of every region, of course we'd make an effort to keep your cooperation.

Strong, yes. Ethan could agree with that. Sound principles? His outlook on life and his values were fine. His views on romance, though...

Ahem. Best not to dwell on that.

It was a funny coincidence. Professor Cerise had made him a special investigator for the laboratory, and now the International Police wanted to give him the same title. He really seemed destined for the name, though he had never set out to investigate anything. One event had simply led to another.

Those are the official thanks, Anabel said. For my personal thanks, how about I treat you to dinner once this is all settled?

Shelly, Courtney, and Misty all looked at her at once. Each gaze held at least a little hostility. Ethan felt the weight of their stares and hurriedly waved a hand.

I don't have much time right now. Some other day.

Anabel knew how busy he was, with Gyms to challenge and the Ever Grande Conference approaching. She did not press him. All right. Let me know when you can.

While they talked, a patrol boat came alongside the damaged Team Magma ship. Police officers boarded in a stream, secured the vessel, and moved the injured Team Aqua and Team Magma members onto their boat.

After their conversation, Anabel took Ethan and the others aboard a patrol boat, escorting Maxie and his fellow prisoners with them. There was a great deal of work ahead: questioning and detaining the members of both organizations, for a start.

At Ethan's request, Shelly and Courtney received special immunity from the International Police. They were classified as informants. Once they had given a full account of what had happened, they would face no further penalty. It was the best outcome they could have hoped for.

There was one condition. They had spent years in their organizations and taken part in their activities. To make certain they had truly left that life behind, and to ensure their treatment did not look entirely without consequence, they would remain under a guardian's supervision for one year.

The person assigned to supervise them was, naturally, Ethan.

It was mostly a formality. He would keep traveling, not watch them every hour. Anabel knew that perfectly well. She had used her authority to give them a workable arrangement, even if her superiors might object to it.

The International Police wanted to maintain their good relationship with Ethan. Provided a request did not go too far or violate a principle she had to uphold, Anabel would do what she could for him. This decision showed how much she valued him.

Everything proceeded in order. The whole affair seemed settled at last.

While Shelly and Courtney gave their statements in an interview room aboard the boat, Ethan and Misty leaned against the deck railing and looked into the distance. The dark clouds had cleared. Even the sea appeared calm again.

If several islands marked on the map had not been shattered and sunk beneath the water, a person might never have guessed that such a battle had taken place here.

It's finally over, Misty said, smiling as the sea breeze stirred her bangs. Now we can focus on traveling and getting ready for the Ever Grande Conference.

Yes. Team Aqua and Team Magma won't be springing up in our path anymore. Ethan sighed. One more Badge and I can enter the Conference. That went fast.

Misty's mood turned wistful. Their journey through Hoenn really was nearing its end. After the Ever Grande Conference, she would have to return to Cerulean City and take over the Gym.

But I haven't spent enough time with Ethan yet.

She hated the thought of leaving.

Ethan did not know what was on her mind. He said nothing more, and the two of them quietly watched the view.

Some time later, two sets of soft footsteps approached. Shelly and Courtney joined them at the railing.

Ethan smiled at their expressions. You both look all right. How did it go? Is everything settled?
