Chapter 310: Charizard's Mega Stone

Vicious had escaped swiftly and without hesitation. He had not even left them a parting threat, much less stopped for the Team Rocket members he had abandoned.

The battle had been short but fierce. Trees had fallen, the ground had collapsed, river water had been cut off, and bushes still burned. Vicious's controlled Pokemon had shown no restraint. Almost half the valley now lay scarred.

Clair looked at the destruction of a place she knew so well and let out a long sigh.

Lance patted her shoulder. "Don't dwell on it. Nature is powerful. It won't take long for this place to recover."

"Lance is right," Ethan said. "And with so many Grass-type Pokemon living here to care for it, I'm sure the Dragon Holy Land will be even more beautiful than before."

Hearing Ethan's formal way of addressing him, Lance grew serious. "After everything we've been through together, do you really need to keep calling me 'Mr. Lance'? Just call me Lance from now on."

Ethan had no reason to refuse the Elite Four member's friendly offer. Counting Lorelei, Lance was the second member of the Elite Four he had come to know well. Even the shrewd Agatha thought highly of him. Little by little, Ethan was building relationships that would be valuable in years to come.

They called their Pokemon back to their sides and looked toward the bound Team Rocket members nearby.

The grunts had begun to wake, but their hands and feet were tied, leaving them no way to escape. They had seen their top executive desert them, and their Pokemon had been defeated. With their resolve gone, they sat dejectedly and waited to learn what would happen to them.

"What do we do with them?" Ethan asked.

Lance glanced their way and snorted. "Take them out of here, then contact Officer Jenny and have them sent to prison. But first, we need to report to the Elder."

They left Pokemon to guard the prisoners and walked back toward the Dragon Shrine.

Along the way, Lance praised Gengar's timely intervention again and again. Ethan, meanwhile, could not contain his curiosity about Clair.

"How did you do that back there?"

"Do what?" Clair asked, unsure what he meant.

Ethan waved his hands and made a few swishing sounds. "Jumping from branch to branch like a ninja. How did you get so quick and light on your feet?"

"Oh, that." Clair smiled. "I've trained alongside Pokemon since I was little. Over time, my own body grew stronger too. And I know Dragonite's attack rhythm, so I could avoid it more easily."

"Is it really that simple?" Ethan was doubtful. "Those didn't look like movements an ordinary person could make."

Lance, walking ahead of them, laughed. "Clair really is an ordinary person. If you ever meet Bruno, you'll find out what a truly powerful human looks like. He can wrestle with Pokemon. His strength will astonish you!"

Bruno... right. Ethan rubbed his chin. As he remembered it, Bruno's strength was extraordinary. He had even beaten an Onix with his bare hands. The man was practically a human Pokemon. Ethan ought to meet him if he got the chance. Bruno was the only member of the Elite Four he had not dealt with yet.

A few minutes later, they reached the Dragon Shrine.

The Elder and Kabu had watched the battle from a distance. Seeing the group return unharmed, they came out to meet them. Lance explained what had happened. The Elder praised him and Clair for their efforts, then turned to Ethan.

"Young man, you've helped us greatly. Our Dragon Tamer clan always repays its debts. Given your bond with the path of dragons, I would like to give you one of these as your reward."

He drew two stones from his clothes.

One was yellow and the other blue. Both were round and translucent, polished until perfectly smooth. A pattern like a genetic lock lay inside each, and faint light glimmered in them under the sun.

Ethan recognized them at once. They were both Mega Stones for Charizard. The yellow Charizardite Y would let Charizard take its Y form, while the blue Charizardite X would produce its X form.

Seeing his astonishment, the Dragon Elder seemed to think he did not understand what they did. "A Dragon Tamer brought these remarkable stones back from a journey through Kalos. They hold a great power that lets Charizard evolve further for a short time. To use one, though, you'll have to travel to Kalos yourself and learn how. Their powers conflict, so the two cannot be used together. You may choose only one as your reward."

"This is... rather valuable, isn't it?"

"Don't hesitate. You've earned it."

Ethan still wavered. He looked up and saw Lance and Clair smiling encouragement at him. After a moment, he reached out, but his hand hovered between the stones.

One of two... It was a difficult choice.

Mega Charizard X and Mega Charizard Y each had their own strengths, and both looked magnificent. Ethan loved them both. But the Elder had offered him one choice, not two, no matter how much he wanted each of them.

He thought for a long time, then made up his mind. He gritted his teeth and took the yellow Mega Stone from the Elder's hand.

Mega Charizard Y. That's the one!

Mega Charizard X changed its body's color and its typing from Fire/Flying to Fire/Dragon. Mega Charizard Y kept its Fire/Flying typing, but greatly increased the power of its moves. That suited the balance of Ethan's team better. Neither form was simply stronger than the other; the decision came down to a Trainer's preference.

Once he had chosen, Ethan could finally stop agonizing over it. Together with the Gyaradosite he had obtained earlier, the Charizardite Y was his second Mega Stone. Even before beginning his journey through Kalos, he had gained another head start over other Trainers.
