Chapter 673: What Did You Teach It?

Slakoth and Metang are both unable to battle! The first round is a draw!

The referee held both flags out to his sides, confirming that Ethan and Norman's first battle had ended with both Pokemon knocked out.

There had been no avoiding it. After their head-on collision, the two Pokemon had fought on for some time. Their early testing of each other gave way to the simplest and most brutal stage of the match: close combat, face-to-face.

Norman had raised his Slakoth exceptionally well. In performance, strength, and experience, it had the edge over Metang.

But Metang relied on its excellent defenses and its resistance to Slakoth's Normal-type attacks. Trading blows with its Steel-type moves, it managed to take Slakoth down with it.

Ethan was pleased with the result. Norman could only smile ruefully. There was little he could have done. When two Pokemon were not far apart in strength, a type advantage could decide the outcome, just as it had here.

Well done, Norman told Ethan. My Slakoth may not have evolved, but it's strong. Plenty of challengers before you have underestimated it and paid the price.

Ethan knew Norman was neither boasting nor trying to save face. Slakoth had shown enough to give an inexperienced or overconfident Trainer a very memorable surprise.

Metang certainly benefited from its type advantage, Ethan said with a smile. I'm happy it managed a draw.

Norman nodded, pleased by the young man's modesty. Someone who had won two regional League tournaments in succession really was exceptional. At the very least, his attitude deserved praise. Ethan was worthy of the admiration Max had for him; he would make a good example for Norman's son.

Both Trainers recalled their Pokemon. Norman took out his second Poke Ball.

On to round two. I should warn you: this Pokemon is stronger than the Slakoth you just faced.

Understood. Let's see it, then. Now that the warm-up's over, I should get a little more serious myself!

Perhaps I spoke too soon about his modesty, Norman thought. He smiled and threw his Poke Ball.

A flash of blue light revealed a white Pokemon standing like an ape. It had a tuft of red hair on its head, sharp claws, and a tail.

From her seat beside Caroline, Misty raised her Pokedex again.

Beep. Vigoroth, the Wild Monkey Pokemon. Normal type. The evolved form of Slakoth. Unlike the lazy Slakoth, Vigoroth is always restless and eager to make a commotion. It cannot sit still for even a minute. Its heart beats ten times faster, and its blood races through its body. If it does not move, stress builds up and its health deteriorates.

Slakoth's evolved form? Misty lowered the Pokedex and studied the agitated Pokemon in front of her.

Judging by the description, Slakoth and Vigoroth were complete opposites. It was hard to believe they belonged to the same evolutionary line. Maybe it had so much energy because it had slept too much before evolving?

While Misty speculated, Ethan considered something else. At Dewford Gym, Brawly had sent out Makuhita and then its evolved form, Hariyama. Now Norman was using two stages of the same line too.

Will he send out Slaking after this one? Is this some kind of Hoenn Gym Leader tradition?

Even as he wondered, Ethan took out the Poke Ball he had prepared.

Go, Combusken!

He hurled the ball, and Combusken entered the field for the first time since the Dewford Gym battle. Ethan's frequent use of it showed how seriously he was training it.

Combusken took one look at its opponent and felt its fighting spirit flare. Vigoroth was not about to back down. It glanced sideways at Combusken, then wiggled its backside in an unmistakable taunt.

Combusken's inner fire burned hotter. The tension between them made the second round feel explosive before it had even begun.

The referee gave the signal. Both Pokemon rushed forward. As they met, Combusken kicked high and Vigoroth drove out a straight punch. Leg and fist collided.

Vigoroth was plainly stronger than Slakoth. But Ethan's Fire- and Fighting-type Combusken had a better matchup than Metang had had, and it was no newcomer to battle. After several hard fights, it had experience Metang lacked.

Vigoroth, Slash!

Combusken, Double Kick!

They clashed again without either gaining ground.

Then Combusken sprang back to create some distance and used Flamethrower, hoping to wear Vigoroth down from farther away. To Ethan's astonishment, Vigoroth used the very same move.

The two streams of fire met between them and swelled into a huge ball of flame. Ethan and Combusken stared at it in disbelief.

What? Vigoroth knows Flamethrower?

Ethan thought of Slakoth's Blizzard and fell speechless. What other strange moves had Norman taught his Pokemon?

Norman smiled at their expressions. He had seen that same shock on countless challengers' faces.

He taught his Pokemon moves outside their own types precisely to catch opponents off guard and give them an unexpected experience in battle. It was a small pleasure of his, and part of his style as a Trainer.

Vigoroth raised its brows at Combusken and muttered something taunting. It might as well have been saying, Didn't expect that, did you? I can use your move too. What are you going to do about it?

Furious, Combusken charged. Ethan did not stop it. A little anger could sharpen a Fire-type Pokemon's fighting spirit, though it was a double-edged sword; the Trainer had to know how to use it.

Sure enough, Combusken's attacks became faster and fiercer. Scratch, Peck, Double Kick, Quick Attackmove after move poured out in a relentless assault.

Even Vigoroth, with its agile limbs and boundless energy, struggled to keep up. It discovered that Combusken could match its speed, which came as a shock. Vigoroth had faced other Combusken in Gym battles, but none had been this fast.

Defending against it grew harder. Norman saw the problem too. His brow furrowed as he searched for a way out.

In its current state, Combusken was attacking as quickly and as hard as it could. With Vigoroth's strength and the moves it knew, Norman could see little chance to break through yet.

He would have to wait until Combusken tired. He could only hope Vigoroth held out that long.

Ethan did not know what Norman was thinking. While Norman looked for a way to defend, Ethan searched for Vigoroth's weakness. He knew his own Combusken better than anyone, and he knew it could not maintain this furious assault for long.

The question was whether Combusken would break through Vigoroth's defenses before its stamina faded, or Vigoroth would endure and turn the match around.

So where is your weakness?

Ethan frowned and watched Vigoroth's every move. And as he looked more closely, he really did notice something.
