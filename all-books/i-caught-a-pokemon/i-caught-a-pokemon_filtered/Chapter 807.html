Chapter 807: A Pokemon to Remember Their Love

The young woman who had shown them to Erika's room discreetly took her leave. Before going, she glanced at May, as though she hoped to take this cheerful third wheel along and leave Ethan and Erika alone.

May, however, was staring at the lavish dinner laid out in the room. The smell alone had her mouth watering. If Ethan announced that it was time to eat, she looked ready to dive in.

The staff member shook her head with an amused smile and left by herself.

Erika looked toward May, and Ethan explained, May is traveling with me from today onward. Once we've finished what we need to do in Kanto and Johto, we'll go to Sinnoh together.

Erika had already heard about the arrangement over the phone. Ethan said it again so they could acknowledge it openly.

Then I hope you'll look after him, Erika told May gently.

Oh, no... May waved her hands. You're too kind, Miss Erika. I should be thanking him. I'm the one learning from Brother Ethan!

We needn't keep thanking one another. Erika stepped aside and gestured toward the meal. We're all among friends. Let's eat before everything gets cold.

May had been waiting to hear that. She wanted to start immediately, but growing up in a Gym had taught her manners. She followed Ethan and Erika into the dining room and waited for them to take their seats.

She meant to sit beside Ethan, only to find Erika had already taken that place. After a moment's surprise, May sat opposite him. A seat was a seat. She picked up her chopsticks.

It would become the decision she regretted most that day.

The feast was delicious, but May also had to sit through an entire meal of open affection. Erika showed just how attentive she could be. She placed food on Ethan's plate, poured his wine, peeled shrimp for him, and even wiped his mouth with a napkin.

Suddenly May's dinner did not taste quite as good. She could remember her mother, Caroline, fussing over her that way only when May had been very young.

Erika and Sabrina were entirely different kinds of women.

After the meal, the staff member returned and escorted May toward a guest room. May looked back once. Ethan, a little tipsy, was walking in another direction with Erika supporting him.

Wasn't that Erika's room? Was Brother Ethan not staying in a guest room?

She caught herself. That was a naive thought. Misty had not needed to tell her that both Erika and Sabrina were romantically involved with Ethan. She could see it for herself. It was startling, and it did not seem entirely proper, yet with someone as remarkable as Ethan, she could understand how it had happened.

May had learned more than most girls her age from her family and her travels. She did not know exactly what Ethan and Erika would do, but she understood they would share a bed, as her parents did.

The thought made her heart race and her ears grow hot. She looked away, pushed it from her mind, and followed the staff member with her head lowered.

...

May could not sleep that night. She did not usually struggle in an unfamiliar bed, but she was in a strange place without anyone she knew nearby. She had eaten so much, and her mind kept wandering.

For the first part of the night she thought about the upcoming Saffron Contest. Around midnight her thoughts turned to the journey to Sinnoh. Later, she could think of little but the relationship among Ethan, Erika, and Sabrina.

Did Erika and Sabrina know about each other? If they did, had they both accepted the arrangement? If they did not, was Ethan deceiving them both?

Misty had asked her to watch out for Lisia and other girls. Surely Erika and Sabrina were not the girls she meant?

May worried over it all night. Just as she finally grew sleepy, rain began tapping against the window. The sound woke her fully again.

Ethan did not sleep much that night either. In his case, he scarcely had time. May's mind kept working; Ethan's body did. It was the ox that tired from plowing, while the field only grew more fertile.

Muffled breathing carried through the Gym's lodging area for much of the night. Fortunately, the young women who worked there had gone home after their shifts, so no one noticed. Even so, Erika kept her voice down as much as she could, perhaps afraid May might hear something unsuitable for her.

...

Ethan woke late the next morning. The clock showed that it was past ten, an unusually long sleep for him. When he tried to rise, his eyelids felt heavy and his body seemed drained.

Erika was already gone. Her bedding had been folded neatly. Ethan stared out the window at a flowerpot full of rainwater and sat there for a long while before his head began to clear.

He dressed. They had been occupied as soon as they entered the room the previous night, so only now did he look around properly. It was unmistakably Erika's bedroom: elegant Japanese furnishings, calligraphy and paintings, and carefully placed vases. It suited a refined young woman from a distinguished family.

After taking it in, he went out. He met May in the room where they had eaten dinner. Each noticed the other's dark circles and tired face.

Ethan knew why he looked that way. What had happened to May? Had she been awake all night too and only just got up?

Before he could ask, Erika entered carrying their breakfast. She looked radiant.

You're awake? She smiled warmly and set the food before them. You were both sleeping so soundly that I didn't want to disturb you. You must be hungry. Come and eat.

In one respect, Erika was formidable. They had been together until late, yet she looked refreshed and lovelier than ever, while Ethan felt as if she had drained the strength from his bones.

They had arrived only the previous evening. Ethan could hardly leave immediately after spending a single exhausting night here. He would stay another day, and perhaps recover some energy.

Provided he did not exhaust himself again. Somehow, that seemed unlikely.

After breakfast, Ethan let his Pokemon out in the Gym's greenhouse to roam. With nothing else to do, he found a reclining chair and settled in.

Erika still had the Gym and her perfume shop to run. One challenger came to battle her. Ethan watched briefly. Erika held back, and the challenger left happily with a badge. Ethan lost interest and returned to the greenhouse to enjoy his quiet day.

May had no such rest. Her thoughts from the night before had given her new ideas for her performance, and she was practicing under the eyes of several young women from the staff.

They were curious about Ethan but could hardly question him directly with Erika nearby. Instead they gathered around May, asking for stories and hoping to learn more through her.

They had chosen the wrong person. May had not shared Ethan's most extraordinary adventures. Apart from what she had seen at the Conferences, she knew most of his exploits only from Misty's accounts.

Nor did May enjoy boasting. She understood that some matters were private. She answered harmless questions briefly and pretended not to know when a question crossed that line.

Even so, her account of Ethan's Ever Grande Conference battles impressed the women.

He sounds amazing, said a green-haired girl. I should have watched with Erika when she was following his matches in secret.

There's still time, another said with a laugh. He's going to Sinnoh, isn't he? We can watch the Sinnoh Conference with Erika.

The women chattered and laughed. May wanted to ask for a little peace so she could practice, but she could not find an opening.

Then their questions shifted to her.

Wait, did you say you came eighth in the Hoenn Grand Festival?

I didn't place eighth, May said quietly. I made it to the top eight.

That's still wonderful. Few newcomers do so well in a major competition their first time.

What do you mean, few? Ethan entered a Conference for the first time and won the championship!

Erika certainly knows how to choose a man!

May sighed as the women returned to gossip. She finally understood the saying that three women could put on a play. Here, there were six or seven. Once they began talking, every subject ended in more teasing and laughter.

Something small and green flickered through the trees on the far side of the greenhouse. It caught May's eye.

What's that? she asked without thinking.

What?

The staff stopped talking and followed her gaze. They saw only plants, but they seemed to know what she meant.

Oh, that... The woman who had escorted Ethan and May the previous evening smiled. It's our little secret. Erika asked us not to tell outsiders, but you came with Ethan, so you're different. Hasn't he told you?

May was even more puzzled. Told me what?

The women laughed again. Only when May grew visibly frustrated did the woman lower her voice.

What you saw was Celebi, a Mythical Pokemon.

Celebi?

May had traveled across an entire region. She was no longer a beginner, and she had spent plenty of time with her Pokedex. She certainly knew of Mythical Pokemon. What she could not understand was why Celebi was here, or why the staff seemed so accustomed to seeing it.

You really don't know, one of the women said. Ethan caught that Celebi.

It's Brother Ethan's Pokemon?

May had only just begun traveling with him. She had not seen his battles with Team Magma and Team Aqua, and Misty had mentioned Deoxys only once when May first saw it. She had no idea he had other Mythical or Legendary Pokemon. This revelation stunned her.

There was more. Do you know why Celebi stays here? another woman asked. Before May could guess, she went on. Because Celebi is the Pokemon that marks the love between Ethan and Erika!

May had heard of a keepsake exchanged between lovers. She had never heard of a Pokemon serving that purpose, though the meaning was clear enough.

It was hard to believe. This was a Mythical Pokemon. Had Ethan really given one away just like that?
