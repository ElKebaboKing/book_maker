Chapter 545: Just Like a Married Couple

As they escorted Sneasel back, Erika leaned close and asked Ethan quietly, "This Sneasel seems special. Aren't you going to catch it?"

Ethan thought for a moment, then shook his head.

"No. It looks cool and it's strong, but I'm not especially interested in this kind of Pokemon. I'd rather wait until I meet one I really like."

Erika smiled. He had said more than once that too many Pokemon would stretch his attention thin. If he was going to catch one, he needed to truly want it.

Clearly, this Sneasel was not to his taste.

They were only halfway back when Dragonite gave a soft cry. It had spotted something.

Following its gaze, they looked up. Far away, a large yellow bird wreathed in flames flashed through the clouds.

Ethan had seen it often enough to recognize it almost immediately.

"Huh? Is that Moltres?"

Erika was surprised too. "There's a Moltres here?"

Ethan nodded. There were tales of Moltres being seen in the mountains around Mt. Silver. He had not expected them to encounter one themselves.

Moltres appeared for only a moment before vanishing from sight.

After that brief interruption, they continued down the mountain and soon reached Ho-Oh Temple again.

The attendant could not hide his delight when he saw that Ethan had brought Sneasel back.

"We'll leave Sneasel with you, then. We won't get in your way any longer."

At Ethan's signal, Scizor handed Sneasel over to the attendant. How they talked things through was up to them; Ethan did not intend to take part.

To thank them, the attendant invited Ethan and Erika to stay for a vegetarian meal while he spoke with Sneasel. After they had eaten, he returned with the news that Sneasel had agreed to their proposal. It would continue living near the shrine, try to coexist peacefully with people, and neither disturb visitors nor interfere with the sacred flame.

Ethan was glad to hear it. Many people devoted their lives to helping humans and Pokemon live together. Seeing the temple and Sneasel set aside their mistrust and share a place was the best outcome he could have hoped for.

He and Erika said goodbye and continued toward Silver Town.

The town was close to Ho-Oh Temple. After another half hour on the road, they arrived at the site of the Silver Conference.

The town truly had been built for the tournament. Advertisements, souvenirs, and decorations bearing the Silver Conference theme were everywhere.

They followed the broad main road around the stadium to the Pokemon Center behind it.

Ethan had already completed the final registration formalities, so there was little to do. He collected a guide to the Silver Conference, checked in, and went to the competitors' village.

Few entrants had arrived yet, although some Trainers who had collected all their badges in previous years were already there.

Even that small crowd made the atmosphere livelier.

The competitors' village was behind the Pokemon Center, down a short street lined with snack stalls and vendors. There were tempting things to eat and little amusements everywhere.

They had plenty of time to explore later. For now, they went straight to their lodgings.

At the Indigo League, the competitors had stayed in separate little houses. Here, everyone stayed in one large hotel, with each entrant given a spacious private suite. Every suite had a living room and bathroom, like a luxury apartment.

Ethan took out the keycard. When he and Erika saw the room they had been assigned, they exchanged a smile.

It looked comfortable, far better than they had expected. It felt like a home.

And speaking of home...

Ethan gazed at Erika. She would make a wonderful wife. A home and a wife: everything was here!

Erika felt his eyes on her and seemed to have the same thought. Deliberately avoiding his gaze, she set their bags aside and began putting away their clothes. Once she had arranged their things, it looked as though they lived there together.

"Ahem... Well." Ethan cleared his throat. "We've been walking a long time. We're both dirty. Why don't we take a bath first?"

Erika blushed. She knew exactly what he was thinking. "No... It's still daytime..."

Despite her words, she let herself be persuaded, and Ethan pulled her into the bathroom. How they bathed, and what else they did there, was nobody else's business.

...

Over the following days, Ethan and Erika lived much like a married couple. He trained his Pokemon, devised strategies, and worked on their weaknesses. Erika looked after him and prepared nutritious food for his Pokemon.

There was a dedicated restaurant at the competition grounds, but they never visited it. Erika prepared all three meals each day, and she clearly enjoyed doing so.

In their free time, they walked arm in arm through the area, buying small trinkets and gifts or stocking up at the supermarket. Each time, Ethan would buy several boxes of condoms under Erika's embarrassed gaze. They were going through them quickly, using two or three almost every day, sometimes more.

As they enjoyed their time together, the days passed and the start of the tournament drew closer.

Finally, on the day before it began, Ash and his two friends arrived at last.

Ethan had no idea what had delayed them. Blackthorn Gym and then Silver Town should have taken only a few days, yet somehow they had not arrived until now.

All three looked surprised when they saw Erika. Misty could not help saying, "That's strange, Erika. It's only been a few days, but you look so well. Your skin is glowing, and you seem positively radiant!"

Even though she was speaking to children, Erika could not help feeling shy.

She gave Ethan a sidelong look.

What else could it be? Ethan had been satisfying her every day.
