Chapter 791: Seeing Courtney and Shelly Again

The meeting room was reasonably tidy. Ethan took a seat and turned to Misa and Masha.

How have you been?

Very well, thanks to you. Misa smiled. The bar makes a profit every day, though the repair shop is still our main business and our biggest source of income. I think we'll earn back the initial investment soon.

Ethan understood that teaching people how to earn a living was better than simply giving them money. He had only meant to find something useful for a gang that spent its days drifting about. He had not expected the business to give them a new start.

They talked for a while, and he got a clearer picture of how everyone was doing. Then he changed the subject. By the way, where are Shelly and Courtney? I thought they'd come here too.

The two big sisters don't usually come to the bar, Masha said. They're in the warehouses not far behind us.

Big sisters? They had been here less than a month, yet Shelly and Courtney had already earned the bikers' respect. Then again, they had been senior officers in Hoenn's two largest organizations. Handling a bicycle gang would hardly be difficult for them.

In that case, take me over to see how they're doing.

Misa and Masha led everyone out the bar's back door, along an overgrown passage, and up to two warehouses. One was red and the other blue.

Shelly and Courtney had not come to Kanto alone. When they accepted Ethan's arrangement, each brought members of her organization who wished to follow her. They also took most of Team Aqua's and Team Magma's remaining supplies and equipment. With that much support, they had established themselves quickly.

Some bikers might have underestimated them for being women, or for being beautiful. But with their people and equipment behind them, the gang had learned to follow their directions. The newcomers had practically taken over the place.

That was what Ethan had wanted. Shelly and Courtney could keep the bikers in line and stop them from causing trouble.

The warehouses were far more heavily guarded than the bikers' base. The sentries noticed Ethan's group as soon as they approached. With a glance around, Ethan even picked out a lookout hidden farther back.

The precautions made sense, but these people had been pardoned. They had spent so long as villains that they could not shake their habits. Even if the guards were only keeping out thieves and curious strangers, the arrangements were remarkably thorough and professional.

Everyone from Team Aqua and Team Magma knew Ethan's face. Those who had come to Kanto also knew they owed their pardons from the International Police to him. No one stopped him. As at the bar, someone hurried off to notify Shelly and Courtney, while the others welcomed him inside with complicated expressions.

Few of them could look at Ethan without feeling something. He had single-handedly broken up their organizations and captured their leaders. He had also enabled the ordinary members to walk away free. To them he was both savior and destroyer, someone they respected and feared in equal measure.

Up close, Ethan saw that the warehouses resembled the secret base where Shelly had once hidden in Hoenn. Both looked ordinary outside, but their interiors had been converted into bases. The red warehouse naturally belonged to Courtney and the former Team Magma members. Shelly and her former Team Aqua members occupied the blue one.

The bases stood side by side but were clearly separate. They looked peaceful enough, yet the relationship between the two groups remained as uneasy as the relationship between their commanders. That they could live this close without a fight was already a testament to Shelly and Courtney's restraint.

Ethan was still deciding which warehouse to visit first when the two women came out with their people to meet him. Both smiled when they saw him. Then each glanced at the other, and both smiles disappeared. It would be a long time before they truly made peace.

Shelly spoke first. I didn't expect to see you here. I thought you'd head straight for another region after the Ever Grande Conference.

Ethan heard the light teasing in her voice and smiled. How could I? I asked you both to come here. Of course I had to check on you before I left.

Courtney said nothing, but the look she gave him made it clear she was happy he had come.

Choosing a warehouse was another matter. Going with Shelly would upset Courtney; choosing Courtney would upset Shelly. Ethan hesitated, then decided that if he chose neither, he could make no mistake. He stayed where he was and talked to them outside.

After only a minute or two, both women invited him in. In the end he chose Shelly's warehouse. That did not mean he valued her more. Shelly's temperament simply made her harder to placate. Courtney was less likely to take offense and easier to soothe.

Shelly shot Courtney a triumphant glance. Courtney was annoyed, but she and her people followed them in.

Inside, Ethan found much the same layout as the warehouse base he had seen in Hoenn, minus the submarine and with more rooms for the members. Not bad. He looked around and nodded. He had not expected them to establish a base so quickly. It was still rudimentary, but at least their lives here were taking shape.

Your friends helped a great deal. Shelly glanced at Misa and Masha. We couldn't have settled in so fast without them.

Oh, it was nothing, the siblings said, smiling ingratiatingly. We only ran errands and did some heavy lifting.

That was more or less true. The bikers knew the area, had numbers, and could supply muscle. Shelly still did not trust them with certain work. But they had helped, and she remembered even small favors.

Misty, May, and Chloe had fallen quiet as soon as they entered the bikers' bar. They had followed Ethan without speaking. Now they looked around the warehouse curiously, staying close behind him like three little attendants.

After a tour, Ethan sat on a sofa and asked Shelly and Courtney, What are your plans from here?

What plans could we have? Shelly sat opposite him and leaned back. We're settled, more or less. Once we've adjusted, we'll need to take on work to support ourselves.

Take on work? Ethan asked.

Of course. People like us aren't going to find ordinary jobs and sit quietly at a desk. In a few days, I'll start looking for contracts to catch Pokemon for other people. Legal business.

Catching Pokemon for clients? Ethan rubbed his chin and nodded. It's close to what you used to do. At least your skills and experience won't go to waste.

He turned to Courtney to hear her plan. Instead, she asked, Which region are you traveling to next?

I came back to challenge the Battle Frontier. After that, I'll travel through Sinnoh.

Sinnoh. Courtney nodded. Going by your past journeys, you'll probably come into conflict with another organization there. If that happens, let me come and help you.

Shelly stared at her in shock. So that was her move! Shelly had been thinking about how to put down roots here, while Courtney had already thought of following Ethan into another region. Why had that not occurred to her? She could not afford to underestimate this woman.

While Ethan considered the offer, Shelly hurried to add, I'm going too!

Honestly, Ethan had never thought that far ahead. He had been focused on settling the two women in Kanto, not on what might happen when he encountered other organizations elsewhere.

As his travels continued, further clashes were likely. His Pokemon made him confident he could face those groups, but sometimes he lacked enough people to get things done. Nor could he place his full trust in the International Police. Having capable people he knew well at his side would help.

Misa and Masha could hardly sit still as he weighed the idea. Boss! Count us in too!

You? Shelly shot the siblings a dismissive look before Ethan could answer. This isn't a street fight. You two are barely even delinquents. You're far too green. Unless you're prepared to risk your lives, you'll only get in the way, whether we're facing Legendary Pokemon or a regional organization.

Misa and Masha swallowed. Her grave tone frightened them, but neither backed down. Before they could reply, Ethan raised a hand.

Shelly's right. It's too dangerous for you at present. If the day comes when you can handle yourselves independently, I'll consider bringing you in.

Hearing it from Ethan disappointed them, but they nodded obediently. We understand, Boss. You can always count on us to support you. Even if we can't fight beside you, we'll keep this place running well!

That's more like it. Shelly nodded and acknowledged their strengths. Fighting isn't your strong suit, but you're good at repairs. Most of the people who came with us are field operatives. We're short of ground support, and you can fill that gap.

Being praised by Shelly filled the siblings with pride. They felt as if they had finally earned her approval. Both thumped their chests. Don't worry, Big Sister! We can only repair simple motorcycles and machinery for now, but we'll work twice as hard. We won't let you down!

Shelly had won them over with a few words. She had lifted their spirits, given them a useful role, and put their skills to work. She had been one of Team Aqua's leading minds for good reason.

Ethan had begun to form a plan. Since you both want to help, and I could use that help, why don't you lead a special operations team? Each of you can select a few people from those you brought here.

A special operations team? Shelly and Courtney nodded together. They exchanged a look, then turned away with blank faces.

Who's in command? Shelly asked.

Here they went again. Neither was willing to yield to the other, and Ethan could already feel a headache coming on. Still, he had to settle it. He sighed and told Shelly, You can command the team for now. But don't make major decisions alone. Discuss them with Courtney, and if you can't resolve a disagreement, contact me.

Shelly gave Courtney one more satisfied look before nodding to Ethan. Understood. I know when to put serious matters first. What about personnel and equipment? Any requirements?

Ethan thought for a moment. We don't need a large team. Keep it under ten people if you can. As for equipment, prioritize mobility. I want you able to reach wherever you're needed quickly.

Mobility... Shelly nodded. Then I'll have to return to Hoenn. Team Aqua has another secret base, and there's a submarine there that would suit us. The International Police never seized it. We can use it to get around.

So Shelly had kept something back after all. Ethan distinctly remembered her telling Anabel, aboard the marine police ship, that she had disclosed every Team Aqua secret. Apparently she still had a hidden asset.

Neither she nor Courtney was easy to deal with. Fortunately, they were on his side now. He no longer had to worry about either of them scheming against him.
