Chapter 656: Brawly Meets a Cold Reception

Ethan opened his eyes, groggy. He had fallen asleep without realizing it. Sitting in one position for so long had stiffened his shoulders. Misty was asleep too, holding one of his arms. On her other side, Bea's eyes were closed and her breathing even.

He checked the time. It was already three in the afternoon.

Outside the cave, the rain had stopped at some point. The wind still howled now and then, reminding him that the typhoon might not be over. But with the rain gone, they could finally leave.

He was about to wake the others when he eased his arm free. Misty felt him move and opened her eyes at once.

Huh? She rubbed them, bewildered. When did I fall asleep?

Her voice woke Bea as well. Bea blinked drowsily. Compared with her usual unreadable expression, she looked almost comically dazed.

The fire had gone out, but Dragonite's warmth had kept them comfortable. Listening to its strong heartbeat, all three had drifted off without noticing. Ethan turned to Dragonite. It smiled at him, apparently unbothered by having sat still for so long.

Thanks for putting up with us, he told it.

Misty got to her feet and stretched. When she saw that the rain had stopped, she exclaimed, Has the typhoon passed? I thought we'd spend the night in here!

Ethan glanced sideways at her. You sound a little disappointed.

I don't! Misty stuck out her tongue.

To be honest, she was a little sorry to leave. Being trapped had been boring, but it had also given the three of them an unusual memory. After hours in the cave, cut off from the outside world, she had grown used to it. Returning to ordinary life felt oddly sudden. Still, they couldn't stay there forever.

They gathered their rubbish, stamped out the last embers of the fire, and left the cave.

The wind continued to blow, though much less fiercely. In the distance, the seemingly endless dark clouds were moving away. This time the typhoon really did appear to have left Dewford Island behind.

Let's go back to town first, Ethan said.

Huh? Misty looked toward the Gym. Now that the storm's gone, shouldn't we check whether it's open?

We'll challenge it tomorrow. It's already late, and I doubt the staff will rush over to open up as soon as the typhoon passes. Besides, our clothes may be dry, but we still look a mess. Let's clean up at the Pokemon Center first.

Misty agreed. Rain had left an unpleasant smell on them even after they dried.

They went down the hill and reached Dewford Town after another ten or fifteen minutes. It looked as though someone had washed it clean.

As Ethan had predicted, the town's drainage system had worked well. Despite the long downpour, hardly any water remained in the streets. They were merely damp.

The town had not escaped entirely. Some signs and trees leaned at odd angles, and debris carried in from who knew where littered the roads. Residents had come out of their houses and were cleaning up together.

The Pokemon Center was crowded with dozens of Trainers. Ordinarily scattered across Dewford, they had all gathered here because of the storm. Ethan hadn't realized there were so many in town.

Nurse Joy looked relieved when she saw the three return. Where have you been? Your luggage was in your rooms, but none of you were here. I was terribly worried!

As the person responsible for the Center, she could hardly ignore three missing guests. If something had happened to them during their stay, it would have affected her too. She had been anxious since the head count.

Sorry we worried you, Misty said. We were stranded outside and couldn't come back until the storm passed.

I see. I'm glad you're all safe. Nurse Joy nodded. Go and rest. I've prepared ginger soup in the dining room to help everyone warm up.

Thank you.

They left the counter and went to their separate rooms. Ethan took a hot shower first, washing away the smell of rain. He put his dirty clothes in the washing machine, changed, and went to the dining room.

From a distance he saw Bea sitting alone in a corner. Misty hadn't arrived yet; she was apparently still getting ready. Ethan walked over and sat across from Bea.

She gave him a brief glance, then went back to minding her own business. After their time in the cave, she seemed to have quietly accepted Ethan and Misty as friends.

Neither of them had much to talk about. A Chansey helping in the dining room brought over ginger soup, and they sipped it in silence.

Several bolder Trainers had been considering approaching Bea while she sat alone. Seeing a man join her, they sighed in disappointment.

Misty arrived after they had finished their bowls. She too had just showered, and her hair was still damp. When she spotted Ethan sitting face to face with Bea, her gaze sharpened.

Why did this feel familiar? As though she were the extra person?

Then she reconsidered. Ethan was impressive, but not everyone would fall for him. Bea was so reserved that surely things wouldn't turn out as they had with Sabrina or Erika.

Surely not...

The next morning, after breakfast and a night's rest, Ethan and Misty set off for the Dewford Gym again. Bea went with them.

The town had recovered completely. There was scarcely a sign that the typhoon had passed through.

Outside Dewford, they followed the mountain path past the cave where they had sheltered. Misty looked at it fondly. Even Ethan and Bea glanced its way more than once.

They continued uphill to the Gym's unusual entrance sign. To their surprise, it had vanished. The wind must have blown it away.

They exchanged looks. Misty felt a flash of satisfaction. She wasn't being cruel, exactly. If the Gym had opened yesterday, they could have sheltered inside instead of being stranded in a cave. She felt little sympathy for the missing sign.

Bea looked disappointed. As a lover of Fighting-type Pokemon who owned Machop, Machoke, and Machamp, she had liked the sign's design. It suited her taste.

They passed the place where it had stood and reached the main doors. Unlike yesterday, the Gym was open. Someone was finally there to take challenges. At least the trip hadn't been wasted.

Inside, a dozen or so young women sat beside the battle court where Ethan had fought two days earlier. They wore swimsuits and had surfboards beside them. The youngest looked about eighteen or nineteen, the oldest in their early twenties. Their athletic figures and sun-browned skin suggested they spent a great deal of time surfing.

One man sat among them, surrounded on every side. He had spiky blue hair, sunglasses, a sports T-shirt, and shorts. He too was suntanned and fairly handsome.

Only one person could sit there so comfortably, receiving so much attention: Brawly, Leader of the Dewford Gym.

Ethan couldn't help feeling a little envious. Not all the girls were striking, but there were certainly a lot of them. Brock would probably have burst into tears at the sight.

NoAsh had already challenged this Gym. Brock had probably done exactly that.

Brawly noticed the three enter. Oh? Challengers this early? I thought today would be perfect for surfing, with the typhoon gone. Looks like I'll have to put that off for a bit.

Surfing again? Misty planted her hands on her hips. We won the qualifying matches two days ago! Weren't you supposed to accept our challenges yesterday? Why was the Gym locked? We came all this way, got drenched, and ended up trapped in a cave!

Brawly looked baffled. Qualifying matches? What qualifying matches?

Misty frowned. Don't pretend you don't know! We battled your acting Gym Leaderyour top student and girlfriendto earn the right to face you!

Brawly understood. He gave a rueful smile, turned toward the crowd, and called quietly, Shauna!

Shauna was among the women. She had already realized she was in trouble. Flushing with embarrassment, she pivoted and ran into another room as fast as she could.

Misty watched her go, completely confused.

Brawly sighed and turned back to the challengers. I'm sorry. We don't have any qualifying matches here.

What? Misty stared. But two days ago...

Shauna isn't my acting Gym Leader. The Dewford Gym doesn't have one. She took it upon herself to watch over the place while I was away.

Ethan could only marvel at Shauna's nerve. Without anyone's permission, she had fought them and made her invented rules sound official. He wasn't angry so much as aware that he had been fooled.

She had probably wanted to impress the man she liked. Her feelings for Brawly had driven her to quite a bit of trouble.

Brawly was embarrassed too. He said nothing more about surfing. Instead, he whistled for the women to clear the battle court, then asked the three visitors, Are all of you here to challenge me, or only one?

Ethan glanced at Bea just as she looked at him. He smiled and offered her the first match. Ladies first.

Bea nodded. Without a speech, she walked straight to the challenger's side of the court.

Hostile looks followed her. She was considerably prettier than most of the women around Brawly, and they felt the threat at once. What if Bea liked him, or he liked her? They wouldn't stand a chance.

On the other hand, the man who had come with her was handsome tooyounger and better-looking than Brawly. Together, they made an enviable pair. Surely someone with a companion like him wouldn't be interested in Brawly. The women felt both relieved and strangely disappointed.

Brawly faced Bea and studied her with open appreciation. Something about her felt familiar. Even before they battled, he could tell she was a Trainer devoted to Fighting-type Pokemon, just as he was.

The oldest of the women stepped to the edge of the court to referee.

The Dewford Gym battle is about to begin! Each side will use two Pokemon. The Gym Leader may not switch Pokemon freely during the match. When both Pokemon on either side are unable to battle, the match is over!

They were ordinary Gym rules, with nothing unusual about them.

Once the referee finished, Brawly flashed what he thought was a dashing smile at Bea. You already know who I am, but I'll introduce myself anyway. I'm Brawly, the Gym Leader here and an excellent surfer!

He gave her another brilliant smile. Bea's expression didn't change at all. Brawly's confidence hit a wall, and for a moment he stood awkwardly in place.

He recovered enough to ask, And what's your name?

Bea said nothing. He waited, but she still didn't answer. This time Brawly couldn't hide his embarrassment, especially with the other women watching him.

They had never seen him pay so much attention to someone. Their glances turned complicated.

Misty had to stifle a laugh. After a day with Bea, she knew her well enough. If Brawly thought this sort of flirtatious introduction would get Bea chatting, he was setting himself up for disappointment.
