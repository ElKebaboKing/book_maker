Chapter 734: Primal Reversion

Archie turned toward Maxie and fought to keep his anger from showing. Don't get too pleased with yourself! Have you forgotten? That man is nearby. Are you going to let him profit while we fight?

Ethan, hidden in the brush, felt a jolt. That man could only mean him. But how did Archie know he was nearby? Had their hiding place been discovered?

After a moment, he decided Archie was bluffing. He wanted to make Maxie hesitate and buy himself enough time to gain control of Groudon. Archie could act decisively, but subtle schemes were not his strength.

Maxie was not fooled. How surprising. Even a brute like you can think of a trick now and then. He looked down at Archie with contempt. Do you really expect that to frighten me? I already control Kyogre, and I can trigger its Primal Reversion whenever I wish. Once Groudon is mine too, what can he do with a single Deoxys?

He plainly expected Ethan to interfere eventually. In Maxie's mind, though, everything would be settled by the time Ethan arrived. With both ancient Pokemon under his command, who could oppose him? Even Rayquaza would have to retreat from the two of them attacking together.

Archie had not expected Maxie to dismiss the warning so completely. Kyogre was closing in, and Matt and the other Team Aqua members could not stop it. Archie's eyes hardened.

They think I'm helpless? If I can't have it, neither can anyone else!

He turned and used the Red Orb on Groudon before it had been sufficiently weakened.

You fool! Maxie shouted, his eyes widening.

Controlling Kyogre had taught him how dangerous it was to use an orb rashly on an ancient Pokemon still at full strength. Archie was willing to drag everyone down with him.

Kyogre, stop him! Maxie raised the Blue Orb, trying to send Kyogre forward. He was too late. Archie's Red Orb flashed, and a streak of red light struck Groudon in the center of the lake.

Wrapped in the light, Groudon roared at the sky. Its body began to change.

No! Maxie cried.

Archie laughed wildly. Groudon grew from just over three meters tall to more than five, a massive figure nearly two stories high. Orange-yellow, magma-like light spread across its skin as though it wore molten rock. Marks shaped like the Greek letter omega appeared on its arms and knees. Its presence pressed down on everyone nearby.

There was no mistaking it. Groudon had undergone Primal Reversion.

So that's... Primal Reversion? Misty stared from the brush. Is that what it looks like when it evolves?

It isn't evolution, Ethan said, frowning. Primal Reversion restores an ancient Pokemon's original form. This is what Groudon really looked like when the world first came into being, in that age of chaos before land and sky took shape.

Its original form...

Their fear had come true. Primal Groudon was out of control. The orb could not command it. Hot, chaotic fury consumed its thoughts until it wanted only to destroy everything around it.

The Team Aqua members surrounding it were the first to suffer. Its attacks had grown far more powerful. Gaining a Fire type made it more vulnerable to Water-type attacks, but it could now knock out entire groups of Pokemon before they could use those attacks.

It roared. The whole island shook violently, great cracks opened across the ground, and magma erupted in fountains more than ten meters high. The molten rock had a source. Groudon slept along volcanic networks, which was why its emergence from underground so often brought a flood of magma with it.

The eruptions added to the chaos, but Primal Groudon's overwhelming force caused most of Team Aqua's losses. A single Fissure took out a broad swath of their Pokemon. It did not continue hunting down every grunt, however. Its attention shifted to Kyogre.

They had been enemies for thousands of years. Groudon's fixation on Kyogre seemed etched into its very DNA. Even in its confused rage, Kyogre remained its chief target.

The violent shaking reached the three in the brush. Ethan lost his balance and fell onto Shelly, pinning her beneath him. The feel of her body startled him. Their position was awkwardly intimate, much like moments he had shared with Sabrina and Erika. He tried to get up at once.

He knew how sharp Shelly's tongue could be. Earlier she had caught him merely looking at her. What would she say about this?

In his haste, he fumbled and put a hand on her chest. Worse, out of habit, he gave an unconscious squeeze.

Oh no.

Realization hit a moment later. His face changed. To his surprise, Shelly said nothing at first. Only when his hand remained where it was did she ask in a voice of bland expectation, How much longer are you planning to hold on?

What? Oh. Sorry...

Ethan pulled away, stood, and faced the battlefield as though nothing had happened. Shelly got up and straightened her clothes.

Enjoy that?

Cough! Cough...

He should have known she would say something. Avoiding her amused face, he fixed his attention on the lake.

Primal Groudon and Kyogre had begun to fight. Kyogre held a type advantage, but Groudon's primal power seemed to make that advantage irrelevant. At their first exchange, its heavy punch knocked Kyogre down from the air.

Misty had fallen in a different direction. She had not seen the embarrassing encounter. She rubbed her dizzy head as she got up, looked toward the lake, and caught sight of the blow.

It knocked Kyogre down with one hit? she cried. Ethan, we have to move now!

Not yet. He reached for his Poke Balls but kept his voice patient. They've only just begun fighting. If we reveal ourselves now, Maxie may cut his losses and escape. Let him trigger Kyogre's Primal Reversion too. Once both are consumed by their fight, neither side will be able to leave easily. Then we go in.

They did not have to wait long. Maxie had little choice. His team could not withstand Primal Groudon, and he would not give up this opportunity. He raised the orb and called on Kyogre's primal power to oppose it.

Blue light blazed around Kyogre. Its body swelled to nearly twice its former size, almost ten meters long. It became partly translucent, with a faint yellow glow from within. Marks resembling the Greek letter alpha appeared on its forehead and wing-like fins.

Primal Kyogre had appeared on the island beside Primal Groudon. After countless years, the ancient rivals stood before the world again in their legendary forms.

Unlike Groudon, Kyogre still obeyed Maxie. It did not attack indiscriminately. Its focus remained on Primal Groudon alone.

Maxie stood at the bow of his ship with the Blue Orb in his hand and looked down at the two Pokemon. Pride and triumph swelled in him. Who else in the world commanded power like this? With Primal Kyogre, he had reached the very top. If he gained Primal Groudon as well, no opponent could stand against him. He could achieve his greatest ambitionand perhaps go further still.

Boom!

The two primal Pokemon collided. It was only a direct impact, yet the weather changed at once. Lightning flashed and thunder rolled through an already clouded sky. A visible shock wave rushed outward. The island looked ready to break apart, and the surrounding sea formed more than a dozen small whirlpools.

This was the terrifying strength of primal Pokemon. Power like this was intoxicating.

Do I really command it? Maxie thought.

He began to smile, then froze. A figure burst from an unremarkable thicket across the lake. It moved so fast that it reached Primal Kyogre before he understood what was happening and struck it with a powerful attack.

What?

The attack had caught him completely off guard. An instant later, he recognized the intruder.

Deoxys! he snarled.

He had expected Ethan to meddle eventually. He had not expected an attack at this precise moment from this hiding place. If Deoxys was here, Ethan had to be here too. Maxie looked toward the thicket and saw one man and two women emerge.

Ethan. Maxie's eyes blazed with anger. How did this man keep appearing at the most critical moment?

Archie was startled to see him too, then burst into laughter. He laughed until he was short of breath.

I see! You were hiding here all along. I thought we'd kept this operation secret, but you found out anyway. You even slipped onto the island without anyone noticing. What a failure on my part!

His gaze fell on Shelly beside Ethan. He did not need to ask. She had surely helped bring him here.

Shelly met Archie's complicated look without a flicker of discomfort. The past was behind her. She had a new life and someone new to rely on. At last, she had left the shadow of Team Aqua behind.
