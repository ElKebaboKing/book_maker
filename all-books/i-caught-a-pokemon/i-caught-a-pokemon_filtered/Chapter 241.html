Chapter 241: A Double Battle

The woman put a hand on her hip and looked at Ethan. "Hello again! Sorry, things were so hurried last night that I never introduced myself. I'm Luana. I own this hotel, and I'm also the Leader of Kumquat Gym!"

She owned the hotel? Ethan had assumed she merely worked with it. Was being a Gym Leader really so profitable? A building like this was enough to make him wonder whether he should open a Gym of his own after traveling the world and challenging its Leagues.

"Pardon me." She was watching him, so he put the thought aside and introduced himself. "I'm Ethan, a Trainer from another region. I came specifically to challenge you, Miss Luana."

Luana nodded. Most Trainers who came here wanted a Gym battle. That was no surprise. But her eyes shifted from Ethan to Sabrina. Even though she was a woman who had seen many beauties, Sabrina caught her attention. Her gaze, however, was cold and carried a faint pressure. She did not look easy to approach.

"Such sharp eyes. Are you here to challenge the Gym too, miss?"

Sabrina stood with her arms folded and said nothing. Ethan answered for her. "She's my companion. She's a Trainer as well, but she's only here with me, not to challenge you."

"I see." Luana turned back to him. "How many badges do you have? You haven't come to me for your first one, have you?"

Ethan took his badge case from his bag and opened it. Three badges of different shapes lay inside. "I've beaten the other three Gyms. Kumquat Gym is my final stop."

"Well, well. Three badges already!" Luana sounded impressed. "Beat me and you'll qualify for the Orange League Winner's Cup."

Sabrina considered the name. Ethan had explained the Winner's Cup to her before. Once a Trainer beat all four Gym Leaders, they could challenge the Orange League's strongest Trainer. Defeat that person as well, and they would claim the League's highest honor.

"That's right," Ethan said, thinking back over the past days. "I'm only one step away from the Winner's Cup."

Luana laughed heartily and explained her Gym's rules without further delay. "We each use two Pokemon at the same time. One battle decides it. As soon as either Pokemon on a side goes down, that side loses."

A Double Battle. Ethan had never encountered one in a Gym before. But unlike an ordinary Double Battle, this match would end as soon as a single Pokemon fell. If his two Pokemon focused their attacks on one of Luana's, could they win almost at once?

The rule seemed strange and rather rough to him. Still, it had been in place for some time. Luana must have her reasons, and there was no point debating it now. He would follow it.

"No problem. I'm ready when you are."

A referee came forward and stood between them. "The official badge match between Kumquat Gym Leader Luana and challenger Ethan is about to begin! Both sides, send out your Pokemon!"

This was the most formal Gym battle Ethan had seen since arriving in the Orange Islands. A professional referee presided over it, and both the size of the venue and the field itself were far beyond the previous three Gyms. Each detail must cost money. By comparison, those other facilities seemed decidedly rustic.

His impression of Luana and Kumquat Gym rose sharply.

Footsteps sounded around the hall. Ethan glanced toward the stairways and saw groups of people entering the spectator seats. They were Trainers staying at the hotel. The managers had told them a Gym match was about to start, and they had come to watch.

Because the hotel and Gym shared a building, its guests could observe challenges and learn from them. As far as Ethan knew, no other Pokemon League Gym let unrelated tourists watch a challenger's match in quite this way. It was one of the Kumquat Hotel's distinctive features.

The spectators were considerate. They found seats and watched quietly, making little noise.

Luana's voice drew Ethan's attention back to the field. "Have you decided which Pokemon to use?"

She took two Poke Balls from her pockets, enlarged them in her hands, and threw them onto the battlefield. "These are my partners!"

Two flashes of blue light revealed Alakazam and Marowak. Alakazam was a Psychic type; Marowak was a Ground type. Ethan saw no obvious synergy between them and wondered what Luana had in mind. He did not underestimate her.

He had considered his own pair as soon as he heard the rules. Seeing Luana's Pokemon did not change his plan. Two more flashes of blue light brought Charizard and the Scyther with the scar on its forehead before her.

Two Flying types? Luana narrowed her eyes but could not work out his strategy.

In truth, Ethan had no special tactic behind the choice. He wanted the experienced Charizard to help the newly caught Scyther through its first Gym battle. As it happened, Scyther's Bug typing also gave it an advantage against Alakazam.

With all four Pokemon ready, the referee stepped back and brought down his arm. "The Kumquat Gym challenge begins now!"
