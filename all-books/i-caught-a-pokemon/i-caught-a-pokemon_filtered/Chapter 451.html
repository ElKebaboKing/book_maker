Chapter 451: Awarding the Prizes

After the announcer finished speaking, the arena fell silent for a moment.

Two or three seconds later, the spectators suddenly awakened. They rose to their feet, applauded, and erupted into wave after wave of cheering!

They might have supported Trinity at times during the match, but once the battle ended and they escaped the red Gyarados's shadow, there was still only one competitor whom they recognized above all others and whom the crowd had most expected to win: Ethan.

Just as most people had confidently predicted before the tournament, Ethan had emerged victorious in the end.

Applause and cheers crashed over him like a tidal wave. Standing in the Trainer's command area with a smile on his face, Ethan waved to the surrounding spectators.

From his position, he could clearly see ceremonial fireworks constantly bursting overhead, the spectators applauding and celebrating in the stands, and countless colorful flags and streamers fluttering along the walls below!

A sight like this sent excitement surging through the heart. No matter how many times he experienced it, he would never grow tired of it...

The celebration continued for quite some time before the announcer's voice sounded again.

"Competitor Ethan Vale, please proceed to the awards platform!"

According to the tournament procedure, the awards ceremony naturally came next. An ordinary person would probably have returned through the competitors' passage, entered the corridor, and climbed the stairs to reach the ceremonial platform.

Ethan chose the opposite approach and decided to put on a show!

He beckoned to the red Gyarados, which swam back to him. As it lowered its enormous head, Ethan took several steps forward, left the command area, and stood directly upon its head!

Before anyone understood what he intended, the red Gyarados swam toward the water below the ceremonial platform. Its entire body slowly rose from the pool, climbing higher and higher. Like an elevator, its enormous head eventually rose above the platform itself!

Countless small streams cascaded between its scales like miniature waterfalls.

Sea Priestess Maya sat in the place of honor upon the ceremonial platform. Six other girls stood to either side, dressed as festival attendants in long white gauze dresses and carrying ceremonial fans. Officer Jenny and more than a dozen police officers formed a guard around them!

The red Gyarados still carried the menace of its recent battle. The instant it appeared, everyone nearby, including the officers, received an enormous fright!

When they met those terrifying blood-red eyes, they instinctively retreated several steps, panic written openly across their faces!

Only Maya and Officer Jenny remained where they were, apparently unafraid of the red Gyarados.

Officer Jenny's composure was hardly surprising. No matter how serious an incident became, members of her family were rarely seen panicking. Maya was clearly no ordinary person either. Although a trace of alarm appeared on her face, she remained seated. The Sea Priestess truly possessed considerable courage!

From other sides of the arena, the red Gyarados appeared to have draped most of its body across the wall, leaving its enormous head above it like a giant ready to break into the platform at any moment!

Ethan first swept his gaze around the platform, then jumped from the red Gyarados's head and landed steadily.

He took out its Poke Ball and recalled it. The moment the terrifying monster disappeared, everyone felt the pressure abruptly ease. The invisible mountain weighing on their hearts vanished, and even the air seemed fresher!

Maya rose and approached Ethan.

"Mr. Ethan Vale, congratulations on winning the Whirl Cup. On behalf of the people of the Whirl Islands, I offer my heartfelt joy at your victory and my thanks for the wonderful matches you have given us! Since you entered the tournament, we have watched you defeat every opponent in succession with tremendous strength. You truly deserve the title of champion!"

"You're too kind..." Ethan smiled. "I simply performed well. Many excellent Trainers also helped me grow a great deal."

Now that he saw her from nearby, Ethan discovered that Maya possessed not only a beautiful face but an excellent figure. Most striking of all was her remarkably pale skin. Combined with her long purple hair, Greek-style clothing, and the scepter in her hand, she strongly reminded him of Athena from Saint Seiya.

Maya offered several more words of congratulations while quietly studying Ethan in return.

This handsome Indigo League champion appeared no older than she was. Yet despite his youth, he had already accomplished so much. Including this victory, he had won three regional competitions of varying size. It was genuinely astonishing.

Most of all, his incomparably ferocious and imposing red Gyarados was absurdly powerful.

Drawing back from her thoughts, Maya noticed Ethan still looking at her. She smiled faintly and gestured to the side.

"I will now present your awards and bestow the blessing of the sea upon you. Please stand upon that platform first."

Ethan walked forward several steps and climbed onto a prepared dais. When he turned to look around from there, the entire arena spread out beneath his eyes!

The view itself had not changed, but the new angle made standing here feel completely different from standing in the Trainer's command area!

How could he describe it? The sight made his blood surge even more strongly and brought an entirely different realization. Only now did it truly strike him that he was the winner of this tournament!

Maya and four attendants approached him.

Ethan looked down and saw that each attendant carried a silver tray. Three different kinds of objects were arranged across the trays.

Maya introduced them. "These four rewards are your medal, trophy, prize money, and the winner's prize for this Cupthe Mystic Water. They all belong to you now."

As she spoke, the four attendants stepped forward one after another and presented the objects on their trays to Ethan.

Maya first lifted the medal and personally placed it around Ethan's neck. It gleamed like gold, though he could not tell whether it had truly been made from the precious metal. Due to its angle, he could see only the front, which bore an engraved whirlpool design.

Next, Maya handed him a heavy golden trophy and a check for one million.

After thanking her, Ethan naturally turned his gaze toward the final reward, the Mystic Water.

Compared with symbolic objects like a medal and trophy, a Mystic Water capable of increasing the power of a Pokemon's moves was unquestionably far more valuable!

In fact, Ethan had already decided who would receive the Mystic Water before he even won the championship.

He currently owned only three Water-type Pokemon. Blastoise had already moved into a more relaxed role, while the red Gyarados rarely battled. The Mystic Water would have a much greater effect in Suicune's possession.

If Ethan encountered another Legendary Pokemon later in his journey, a Suicune equipped with Mystic Water would naturally possess a greatly improved chance of victory.
