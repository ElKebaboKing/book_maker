Chapter 504: Scyther Versus Scyther

Seeing someone else's Tyranitar was very different from owning one himself.

Tyranitar was immensely powerful. Once it had grown to its full strength, Ethan would have another Pokemon that could stand alongside the three Legendary birds or the three Legendary beasts. It was a major addition to his team's depth!

From her seat, Erika looked at the imposing Pokemon and remembered the Tyranitar that had struck her. She was happy for Ethan, though she could almost feel that old pain in her back again.

Ethan admired Tyranitar for a moment. He touched its hard armor and praised it, then recalled it rather than having it continue the battle.

As at the Cianwood Gym, he had planned to use this challenge mainly to train his Pokemon. Fully evolved partners such as Dragonite and Tyranitar were not what he had in mind.

Once Ethan had finished, Bugsy took out his third Poke Ball.

"Impressive. It's been a long time since anyone forced me to use my third Pokemon. Come on out, my magnificent warriorScyther!"

He threw the ball hard. Blue light flashed, and a Scyther appeared on the battlefield!

Magnificent warrior?

Another overblown title. Yet sending it out last suggested that Scyther was Bugsy's ace.

Unlike Gym Leaders who had traveled to other regions, Bugsy used only Pokemon common to Kanto and Johto. They were not weak, but his youth meant he had not yet ventured far from home. Becoming a Gym Leader so early had tied him to this place.

Early fame was not always an advantage.

But if Bugsy was sending out a Scyther...

Ethan searched his waist pouch and drew out his own Scyther's Poke Ball.

When two Trainers had the same Pokemon, a direct contest was too tempting to pass up.

"It's your turn!"

Ethan threw his ball. A Scyther with a scar on its forehead appeared across from Bugsy's.

"Oh?" Bugsy's eyes lit up. He studied Ethan's Scyther, then looked at its scar. "Another Scyther? It looks well trained too. Good! Now we can see which one is stronger!"

Ethan smiled. "Mine may not have a grand title like yours, but it has quite a history. Before I caught it, it lived on an island full of Bug-type Pokemon. Don't let its ordinary looks fool you. They called it 'Scar.' With its two scythes, it cut its way from the south of the island to the north, then from east to west, for three days and three nights without blinking once!"

Huh?

Ethan's Scyther glanced back at him, baffled. When had any of that happened?

Was this "Scar" some other Scyther?

Bugsy failed to recognize the joke. Instead, the island of Bug-type Pokemon caught his interest.

"Where is that island?" he asked.

Ethan had hoped his tired joke would amuse this boastful kid. When his only listener did not play along, he sighed in disappointment.

Is this what a generation gap feels like?

"It's far away, in the Orange Islands," he said, his enthusiasm fading. "It's easy enough to find, but you'll need a boat to get there."

Bugsy nodded thoughtfully. As a Bug-type expert, he was plainly interested.

At the referee's signal, the two Pokemon moved to the center of the field.

They stared at each other, eager to fight!

The colored flag fell. Both Scyther charged at almost the same instant and raised their scythe-like arms together!

They met like master swordsmen. Their blades crossed and threw off bright sparks!

They swung again and again, dozens of strikes flashing by until their scythes were nearly a blur.

As the pace climbed, Bugsy's Scyther seemed to falter first. It jumped back, out of Scar's reach.

Bugsy was startled.

As Ethan and Erika had guessed, Scyther really was his ace. He had complete confidence in it.

Falling behind in the first exchange was the last thing he expected!

It also showed him that, unlike Ethan's unimpressive Eevee, both Pupitar and Scyther were above average.

Scar's eyes turned cold at its retreating opponent. It pressed forward again without giving Bugsy's Scyther a chance to rest.

Bugsy clenched his teeth. He would not meet it head-on. "Scyther, use Double Team!"

Three copies split from Bugsy's Scyther. The move confused Scar, which slashed at the nearest figure.

Its sharp scythe cut through nothing but an image.

A blade whistled behind it. The real Scyther had hidden to the far right and attacked while Scar was swinging.

Clang!

To Bugsy's Scyther's surprise, its ambush failed. Scar turned in time and blocked the blow with its own scythe!

"It's that strong?"

Bugsy frowned. He could hardly believe the sneak attack had failed under those conditions.

"Scyther, use Swords Dance!"

At his order, Scyther whirled and leaped, sweeping its scythes through a dance. Light covered its body, greatly increasing its attack power.

Bugsy must have hoped that extra power would break through Scar's defense.

Ethan smiled and answered in kind.

"Scyther, join it! Use Swords Dance!"

Scar performed the same battle dance, its own attacking strength rising sharply.

Ethan looked from the equally strengthened Pokemon to Bugsy.

There. Now we're both ready. Let's see whose Scyther is stronger!
