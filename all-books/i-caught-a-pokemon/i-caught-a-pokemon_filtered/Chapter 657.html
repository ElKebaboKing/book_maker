Chapter 657: A Contest of Strength

Brawly dressed fashionably, was fairly handsome and outgoing, and had no shortage of admirers. The girls who spent every day with him at the Gym were proof of that. If he had tried to talk to someone else, he might have succeeded. He had simply chosen the wrong person.

After an awkward silence, he rubbed his nose and took out a Poke Ball, pretending nothing had happened. Whether he felt embarrassed was anyone's guess but his own.

He threw the ball. A plump Pokemon resembling a sumo wrestler appeared on the field.

Misty had never seen one before. From the sidelines, she pointed her Pokedex at it.

Makuhita, the Guts Pokemon. Fighting type. Makuhita loves to train. It builds up energy by eating well, sleeping well, and exercising every day, growing stronger through repeated, rigorous practice. It enjoys strengthening its body more than anything. By charging into thick tree trunks, it toughens both its body and its determination. It practices slapping over and over. Its perseverance lets it withstand attacks and rise again, no matter how many times it is knocked down.

It sounded like a Pokemon born to fight. Being solely a Fighting type could leave it at a disadvantage against certain opponents, but it also let it specialize in fighting.

On the other side, Bea released the Pangoro that had battled Shauna two days earlier. It had performed well then, so Ethan wasn't surprised. He knew Pangoro wasn't even Bea's strongest Pokemon. Her real ace was a tremendously powerful Machamp capable of Dynamaxing.

Brawly seemed eager to show off his knowledge of a Pokemon uncommon in Hoenn. Stroking his chin, he said, A Pangoro! You rarely see one in this region. Here in Dewford Town, years may pass between visits from Trainers who have one. But I'm familiar with them. I encountered plenty while traveling in other regions, and I know how strong they are.

Someone else might have replied politely, perhaps downplaying Pangoro's strength, saying she had caught it by chance, or asking where Brawly had traveled. Bea remained as impassive as ever. She showed no interest in continuing the conversation.

In that respect, she reminded Ethan a little of Sabrina. Both kept strangers at a distance with a cool manner. But silence was not the same as rudeness. When Brawly sent out his Pokemon, Bea bowed with perfect courtesy.

He blinked and hurriedly bowed back. After several failed attempts to talk to her, he was beginning to understand that she simply didn't say much.

He also noticed that although she had come with Ethan, the two barely interacted. They didn't seem close, let alone a couple. With that settled in his mind, he calmed down a little.

He knew he had been too eager earlier and had upset the women around him. Easy now. No need to rush.

He could show Bea what he was capable of by defeating her Pangoro in battle instead.

With both Pokemon ready, the referee raised her flag. The match begins now!

As the flag dropped, both Trainers acted.

Feel the force of my wave! Brawly made an exaggerated surfing motion on dry land. At his shout, Makuhita charged straight at Pangoro.

Pangoro didn't even lower its head. Its eyes shifted down toward the approaching Pokemon while it slowly chewed the bamboo shoot in its mouth.

Bea took a karate stance in her command area. At nearly the same moment, Pangoro assumed the identical stance with its back still turned to her, as though it had sensed her movement.

Here it comes, Ethan thought, narrowing his eyes.

Of all the things he disliked in a Pokemon battle, the coordinated posing and dancing some Trainers did with their Pokemon ranked near the top. He would never do it himself.

Bea drove a fist forward. Pangoro moved in perfect unison, throwing its right fist into Makuhita. It was a style Bea hadn't shown against Shauna. Perhaps Shauna hadn't been strong enough to draw it out; only against Brawly was Bea revealing how seriously she could fight.

Ethan didn't know what the gestures accomplished. Maybe mimicking her Pokemon helped Bea feel the battle through its movements while she trained herself and gave commands.

The blow caught Brawly and Makuhita by surprise. Bea hadn't spoken a command, so neither had expected Pangoro to attack just then.

Makuhita was well trained, though. It quickly recovered, rubbed its aching chest, and lunged again. Both Pokemon favored direct physical attacks. There were no dazzling effects as they closed and traded blows.

As the exchange went on, Brawly sensed that something was wrong. Pangoro was much too strong.

Makuhita wasn't his best Pokemon, but it had won many Gym matches and helped him defeat more than a dozen challengers. He had always considered it capable.

Against Pangoro, it was outmatched. Its strength, technique, durability, and reactions all fell short. It even had less battle experience.

After only a dozen exchanges, Pangoro seized an opening. One Vital Throw slammed Makuhita onto the floor, and it could not get up.

The girls watching from the sidelines covered their mouths in shock. They were used to seeing Brawly as the stronger Trainer, whatever challenger stood opposite him. When had they last seen one of his Pokemon lose so decisively?

Brawly hadn't expected Makuhita to fall so fast either, but he didn't panic. He had traveled enough to know that the world held many powerful Trainers and Pokemon. Losing a round or two wasn't the end of anything.

Still, he had resolved to defeat Bea's Pokemon, and she had beaten his almost immediately.

The referee took a moment to recover her composure before raising her flag. Pangoro wins the first round!

Yes! Misty applauded from the spectator area. As Bea's friend, she was on her side. Brawly's unsubtle attempts to flirt with Bea had also irritated her. The angry looks from the women around him didn't bother her at all.

Your Pangoro really is strong, Brawly said. He had lost the round, but refused to sound defeated. It's been a long time since I've met an opponent like you. You've impressed me. The next round won't be so simple, though. I'll have to get a little more serious.

He recalled his unconscious Makuhita and took out a second Poke Ball.

Come out, Hariyama!

Blue light flashed. A Pokemon larger, stronger, and fiercer-looking than Makuhita appeared on the field. Misty scanned it with her Pokedex too.

Hariyama, the Arm Thrust Pokemon. Fighting type. The evolved form of Makuhita. Known for its immense strength, Hariyama loves testing itself against very large Pokemon. It can stop an oncoming train with one palm and send a ten-ton truck flying with a slap. Despite its love of contests of strength, it is kindhearted and courteous, and it honors its opponents.

Misty had guessed the relationship from their appearance, but still looked surprised. Hariyama evolves from Makuhita? If he had this stronger option, why didn't he send it out first?

Ethan hadn't expected Brawly to use Makuhita's evolution in the second round either. He had assumed the Gym Leader would choose a different species. Most Trainers didn't raise two Pokemon of the same kind unless they had a particular reason to. Evolution rarely changed a Pokemon's battle style as much as it changed its strength, so an opponent who had seen the first form could often anticipate the second.

Unless Brawly had great confidence in Hariyama, sending it out now seemed unwise.

The women at the Gym reacted quite differently. The moment they saw Hariyama, they stirred. The challengers might not know it, but this was Brawly's ace. He was bringing out his strongest Pokemon against Bea. Was she really that formidable?

The women spent more time surfing than studying battles. Still, after watching so many Gym matches, they had learned to recognize some things. Bea's Pangoro was unlike most of the challengers' Pokemon they had seen.

Ethan and Misty exchanged smiles. Brawly had claimed he was getting only a little more serious, yet he had already brought out his ace. He clearly didn't want to lose face in front of Bea.

Makuhita stood about a meter tall. Hariyama reached two meters and thirty centimeters, roughly twenty centimeters taller than Pangoro. It weighed nearly twice as much too: a genuine heavyweight.

Everyone looked to Bea. Would she switch Pokemon, or send Pangoro into another round?

She seemed oblivious to their attention, but her decision was clear. Pangoro returned to its starting position on her side of the court.

It wasn't surprising. Bea had won the first round and still held the initiative. Even if she lost this one, she would have another Pokemon for the deciding battle. Pangoro could still fight, so why withdraw it?

Brawly breathed a little easier when he saw she was keeping Pangoro in. He needed to regain some ground. If she switched now, beating two fresh Pokemon would take longer and make it harder to show her how strong he was.

Both Pokemon readied themselves. The referee waved her flag again, and the second round began.

Hariyama, use Arm Thrust!

Hariyama strode toward Pangoro, raising its enormous red palms as it ran.

Bea took her karate stance a second time. Pangoro matched her, and the two towering Pokemon collided.

Their large hands locked together. Both pushed with all their strength, but neither gained ground. Their muscles bulged, their bodies trembled, and strain showed at their temples. What looked like a simple test of strength had both Pokemon giving everything they had.

At first, Hariyama's greater size left Brawly feeling confident. Only a few seconds later, Pangoro roared and began forcing Hariyama backward.

In the contest of strength, Pangoro had come out ahead.
