Chapter 610: Zinnia

The broad cave, strewn with loose stones, suited Raikou well.

Team Magma and Team Aqua had plenty of Pokemon, but Raikou used the uneven ground to stay ahead of them. Fighting alone meant it did not have to avoid an ally's attacks, and that freedom became an advantage.

Courtney and Shelly frowned when all those Pokemon failed to bring it down.

They could see that their people were trying. The stranger was simply too strong. Even its ordinary electric shocks seemed far more destructive than those of most Electric-type Pokemon.

Courtney was the first to lose patience. She raised a hand and sent out her own Pokemon.

It was a large creature with long orange-red fur, a head rather like a donkey's, a camel-like body, and two rocky humps on its back.

Camerupt, a Fire- and Ground-type Pokemon that lived in tropical regions.

It stood almost two meters tall. The moment it appeared, its presence set it apart from the other Pokemon on the field.

At Courtney's command, Camerupt advanced slowly toward Raikou and opened its mouth to spray a stream of scorching flame.

Across the battlefield, Shelly was about to send out a Pokemon of her own. But something felt wrong. She stopped and scanned the cavern carefully instead.

Courtney had once been a scientist and was now a field commander. Shelly, meanwhile, was regarded as Team Aqua's strategist. Despite her daring, revealing clothes, she was observant and known for her sharp mind. Only the team's leader held more influence within Team Aqua.

That was why the sudden appearance of a Pokemon that hurled electricity had bothered her from the start. There had to be more to this than it seemed.

Her close watch rewarded her. At the edge of the battlefield, two figures, one taller and one shorter, were creeping up behind her. They were not Team Magma members. One was a man she had never seen before, the other a girl in her teens.

Who were they? Where had they come from?

Her eyes narrowed. Stop! Who are you?

The shout exposed Ethan and Misty and drew Courtney's attention to them as well.

Courtney could see they had sneaked in behind her team. They must have been followed. She glared at the pair with open hostility.

Ethan was not particularly surprised to be caught. Between the dark cave and the chaotic battle, he had managed to get closer than he expected.

With both women's eyes on him, he shouted, Now!

Before anyone understood what was happening, Gengar rose from the ground in the middle of the battlefield. It gave a chilling cackle, holding a sphere of dark energy in each hand, and hurled both into the crowd.

Bang! Bang!

Boom! Boom!

The spheres exploded among them. It happened so quickly that even Raikou's sudden arrival seemed tame by comparison. Team Magma and Team Aqua had no time to defend themselves; their members were blown off their feet.

At the same moment, Raikou redoubled its assault. It roared and gathered power in its mouth. A moment later, a brilliant electric blast like a cannon shot out, dividing the battlefield in two.

It was Zap Cannon, a powerful Electric-type move.

It might not be the single most damaging Electric attack, but it could still bring a battle to an end.

Working together from inside and outside the groups, Gengar and Raikou brought down most of the nearby people and Pokemon. Even Courtney and Shelly were caught in the shock wave and fell backward onto the ground.

Ethan and Misty used that moment to hurry to Professor Cozmo. They cut the ropes around him with a knife, took his hand, and led him back the way they had come.

Electricity and the exploding dark spheres broke apart many of the rocks. The whole cavern trembled faintly.

Dust filled the air. Shelly and Courtney covered their mouths and noses. They now knew for certain that the unfamiliar Pokemon had been sent by someone.

Its Trainer had to be one of the two people escaping with Professor Cozmo.

By the time the dust settled, the pair had vanished. So had Raikou and Gengar.

Damn it!

Courtney sprang up, ready to lead a pursuit, but Shelly suddenly burst into laughter beside her.

What are you laughing at? Courtney demanded coldly.

Shelly laughed so hard that tears gathered at the corners of her eyes.

At you, of course. You went to all the trouble of following us here, only to end up doing someone else's work for them!

Courtney glared. And you didn't? Your team put even more effort into this than ours!

I don't care. Shelly spread her hands. Our main objective has always been in the ocean. The meteorite would only have given us another way to achieve it. Losing it won't hurt us much. But seeing how badly this upsets you? Nothing could make me happier.

The rival organizations hated each other so much that one side's setback pleased the other more than its own success might have.

Courtney knew Shelly was provoking her, yet she could not keep her anger down.

Team Magma had spent a long time planning this operation, moving people into place and watching Team Aqua closely. Courtney had expected to deal its rival a serious blow. Instead, a stranger had taken the prize.

She had no time to argue. With a wave of her hand, she rallied her team and led them after Ethan.

Once they were gone, a Team Aqua member helped Shelly to her feet. Boss, what do we do?

Her smile vanished, and anger flashed in her eyes. We go after them too, of course!

For all her talk about not caring, she had only been trying to mislead Team Magma and infuriate Courtney. Team Aqua had spent weeks on this. Losing Professor Cozmo mattered very much.

Shelly intended to find out who was bold enough to cross Hoenn's two largest criminal organizations at the same time.

And this time, she would follow Team Magma's example and wait for a chance to benefit from someone else's fight.

She gathered her scattered members and led them after Ethan and Team Magma.

Elsewhere in the maze of caves, Ethan, Misty, and Professor Cozmo were running back toward Meteor Falls along the route they had taken.

When they reached the waterfall, Ethan started toward another passage. Professor Cozmo called him back.

Wait! This way!

The professor was beckoning from a different opening. Ethan and Misty followed him into a narrow cave where only one person could move sideways through at a time.

They had barely turned into it when Courtney and her team reached the waterfall behind them.

The delay had cost Team Magma sight of their quarry.

More than a hundred openings of different sizes surrounded the falls. Anyone could be hiding in any one of them. Most pursuers would have been at a loss, but Team Magma was accustomed to operating underground and had its own way of tracking people.

Its members released several Houndoom. The fierce, dog-like Pokemon, at home around caves and lava, sniffed the ground before racing toward the narrow passage Cozmo had chosen.

Excitement flashed in Courtney's eyes. There! After them!

Ethan heard the commotion and looked back. They had found his trail again. He shook his head helplessly.

They were persistent. No wonder Team Magma had managed to follow Team Aqua so long without being discovered. Those noses were useful.

One group fled, the other gave chase.

Professor Cozmo seemed to know the cave. He led Ethan and Misty through one turn after another, deeper inside.

It was not enough to shake Team Magma. The gap narrowed until Ethan could almost hear their labored breathing.

He was considering stopping to hold them off when he heard stones shifting overhead.

He looked up. A huge boulder fell from above and landed behind them, sealing the passage completely.

Before they could recover from the surprise, a girl's voice called from a ledge high above.

Come this way!

A vine dropped down in front of them.

It was too dark for Ethan to see the speaker's face, but her clear voice sounded young.

Professor Cozmo, who was leading, seized the vine and climbed without hesitation. Ethan recalled Raikou, then he and Misty climbed after the professor onto the ledge.

Only there did Ethan see the girl clearly.

She looked seventeen or eighteen and was strikingly beautiful. Short black hair fell below her jaw. She wore a cape over a black T-shirt, shorts that exposed the tops of her thighs, and a strange-looking band around one ankle. There was something mysterious and a little wild about her.

Most distinctive of all was the odd, scale-shaped collar visible at her neckline.

Her appearance was so recognizable that Ethan knew her almost at once. This was Zinnia, a character with an important role in the games.

At her feet stood a round pink Whismur with large ears. It had evidently pushed the boulder down to block the passage.

Before Ethan could say anything, Zinnia quickly drew up the vine. This isn't the place to talk. Follow me!

She turned and went into a deep tunnel behind her. At that moment, Team Magma smashed through the boulder below. Ethan and Misty wasted no time. They followed Professor Cozmo into the tunnel.

The passage was only a little taller than a person and stretched a long way into the darkness. Yet Zinnia moved quickly without a flashlight, as though she could see perfectly well.

After a long walk and a crossing of another underground river, she finally stopped.

Ethan looked around. They had reached a stone chamber furnished with a stone bed, stone cabinets, and other belongings. Someone appeared to live here regularly.

Zinnia lit an oil lamp on a stone table, illuminating the room.

She looked first at Professor Cozmo and shook her head. I didn't expect Team Aqua to catch you. That was careless.

Those words alone told Ethan that Zinnia and the professor knew each other.

Professor Cozmo sighed without answering her. He turned to Ethan and Misty instead.

Thank you for saving me. I haven't even asked your names.

I'm Ethan, and this is Misty. We're Trainers passing through. We saw Team Aqua abduct you at Meteor Falls and followed them. That's how we ended up there.

I see. Cozmo thanked them again. Without their help, Team Aqua or Team Magma would likely have held him captive and forced him to help them find the meteorite.

Then something seemed to occur to him. He pushed up his glasses and asked eagerly, Ethan, was that Raikou, the Legendary Pokemon from Johto?

As a Pokemon researcher, Cozmo had recognized more than the two commanders had. His words were a question, but he already sounded certain.

Zinnia looked at Ethan with curiosity when she heard the name too.

Yes. Ethan nodded. He had sent Raikou into the open and had no reason to hide it. That was Raikou. It's my Pokemon now.

Cozmo looked at him with newfound respect. The man standing before him had caught a Legendary Pokemon. Even Zinnia could not help studying Ethan with greater interest.
