Chapter 515: Whitney and Miltank

After the incident with Team Rocket, neither Ethan nor Erika felt much like challenging a Gym.

It was already noon, and Ethan's Pokemon had spent some of their strength in the fight. He decided to give them a day to rest and challenge Whitney the following morning.

For the rest of the day, he made good on his promise to Erika early. They explored Goldenrod City together, visiting nearly every major sight recommended in their travel guide.

They shopped, ate desserts, and enjoyed the afternoon.

That evening, they took rooms at a hotel near the Gym. After a night's rest and breakfast the next morning, they finally stepped through the doors of the Goldenrod Gym again.

Unlike on their first visit, Whitney was there.

A staff member led Ethan to the battlefield, where he met a cheerful-looking girl with red hair in twin tails.

Like the type Whitney specialized in, the arena looked thoroughly ordinary. Tiered spectator seats surrounded it. The packed earth underfoot was marked with white lines. From every angle, it seemed well suited to a Normal-type Gym.

"Welcome to the Goldenrod Gym. I'm Whitney, the Gym Leader," she said as they approached.

Ethan took a closer look at her. She seemed about sixteen or seventeen, pretty and full of youthful energy. She wore a white short-sleeved shirt, denim shorts, and green-and-black striped socks.

He went straight to the point. "Hello, Whitney. I'm here to challenge your Gym."

"I know who you are," Whitney said with a smile. "I watched your matches at the Indigo League and heard about you from other Gym Leaders. They all said you're an amazing Trainer. It's good to finally meet you!"

Then she added, "And thank you for defeating Team Rocket yesterday and rescuing all those hostages."

"You heard about what happened at the TV station?" Ethan asked in surprise.

"Of course!" Whitney smiled. "There isn't much that happens in a Gym city without its Leader finding out. I should have gone to help, but I happened to be out. By the time I heard, you'd already taken care of everything."

Unlike Chuck and Bugsy, Whitney already knew something about him. That meant he would have to use the second approach to his Gym challenge today.

"I was only nearby and happened to be able to help," Ethan said lightly. Then he explained what he wanted. "If you're willing, I'd like you to use your strongest Pokemon against me."

"Of course!" Whitney nodded. "I was thinking the same thing. I want a real battle with a Champion like you!"

She turned to Erika. "You must be Miss Erika. You're very pretty! I hear you're a Gym Leader too. Is that right?"

"You're too kind, Whitney. You're lovely yourself." Erika gave a small, courteous bow. Since Whitney knew Ethan, Erika was not surprised that she knew of her as well. "I am a Gym Leader. Right now, I'm traveling with Ethan."

It was their first meeting, and their compliments were polite enough. But Whitney noticed the gentle glance Erika cast toward Ethan when she spoke of traveling with him.

Whitney was no sheltered child. She could tell there was more between the two of them.

Were they a couple?

When the introductions were over, a fashionably dressed female staff member came to the middle of the field to referee.

"I'll explain the rules. Each side will use three Pokemon. The first Trainer to defeat all three of the other Trainer's Pokemon wins!"

Ethan nodded. Erika quietly wished him luck, then took a seat nearby to watch.

Once Ethan and Whitney stood in their respective Trainer areas, Whitney, as the Gym Leader, brought out her first Pokemon.

She threw a Poke Ball. Blue light flashed, and a pink, cow-like Pokemon appeared before Ethan.

"This is the partner I trust mostMiltank!"

Miltank looked much like a cow, though pinker and cuter. The species was entirely female; Tauros was its male counterpart.

A Miltank could produce twenty liters of nutritious milk a day. As on Earth, its milk had become part of countless households' daily lives, and demand for it was enormous.

Speaking of milk, Ethan discreetly glanced at Whitney's figure. Perhaps she had grown up drinking Miltank milk; she looked surprisingly mature for her age.

Miltank were common on farms, but Ethan had yet to meet a Trainer who used one in battle.

Not knowing much about the species, he took out his Pokedex and pointed it at Miltank.

"Beep. Miltank, the Milk Cow Pokemon. A Normal-type. Gentle by nature, it does not like fighting people. Children who drink its milk are said to grow into healthy, strong adults. Because Miltank is also sturdy and powerful, some Trainers use it in battle."

Even the Pokedex devoted most of its description to Miltank as a dairy Pokemon. Its battling ability received only a passing mention.

Ethan put the Pokedex away, disappointed, and considered what to send against it.

He still remembered Whitney's Miltank defeating all three of Ash's Pokemon in the animated series. That alone said plenty about its strength.

Yet Whitney had chosen her ace for the first round. Ethan had no idea what other Pokemon she might use afterward. He could not underestimate her.

It also showed that Whitney meant what she had said. She had brought her strongest team and intended to battle him at full strength.

After some thought, Ethan decided to send out Tyranitar.

Against a Pokemon that fought with strength, he would use one that could answer in kind.

Besides, his Tyranitar had lost to Apollo's the day before. It still had plenty of room to grow, and this was a good chance to sharpen its power.

The moment Tyranitar appeared, it showed its imposing nature. It threw back its head and roared toward the high ceiling.

"Tyranitar? That's quite a presence," Whitney said, instinctively covering her ears. She clearly respected a Pokemon this strong.

Seeing both Pokemon ready, the referee raised her flag.

"The Goldenrod Gym badge match between challenger Ethan and Gym Leader Whitney begins now!"
