Chapter 848: The Tomboy

Ethan's words were a little unfair to Brock, but Ash and Max did not argue. He was free to cheer for whomever he liked. Brock was a friend, yet Ethan had never traveled with him; May was his traveling companion. Different relationships brought different loyalties.

Ash and Max had traveled with both contestants, and Max was May's brother besides. They were the ones who found it hard to choose a side.

Onstage, the battle continued. After the last exchange, Brock finally managed to put some distance between Marshtomp and Munchlax. He tried Mud Shot again to hamper Munchlax's movement, but May was ready.

Munchlax, Solar Beam!

A surge of energy interrupted Mud Shot and struck Marshtomp hard. It had to scramble away.

Both finalists were plainly stronger than the earlier contestants. They traded attacks and counterattacks on nearly equal terms, thrilling the audience. The match felt less like a graceful Contest Battle and more like a straight fight between Trainers. No one minded. A good battle was a good battle.

Even the best battle had to end. May's Munchlax found an opening, closed in on Marshtomp, and defeated it with Focus Punch. The Gardenia Ribbon was hers.

Brock shook his head ruefully. He regretted not going farther, but May was his friend and former companion. He applauded and congratulated her sincerely. Traveling beside Ethan had clearly helped her grow.

May accepted her fourth Ribbon amid the crowd's cheers. One more would qualify her for the Grand Festival coming soon. She and Ethan were now at roughly the same stage in their respective journeys, which meant neither would have to hold the other up for long.

Brock did not seem discouraged by his defeat. Yuma had become his mentor, and the prospect of spending time with such a beautiful woman made him happy. During the interval, she had even come backstage and invited him to travel with her afterward. He was still thinking it over; leaving Ash and Max would be hard. But from his point of view, things were going well.

Then they left the Contest Hall. Outside, Brock saw Yuma standing with Mollie.

His heart dropped.

Yuma smiled faintly as he approached. Brock, after that match I can see you're already an excellent Coordinator. I have nothing more to teach you.

His hopes sank. Miss Yuma, what do you mean?

She looked toward Mollie. This was her first Contest too. I've decided to become her mentor.

What? Brock felt as if the sky had fallen. He made one last attempt. But I... I think I still

Yuma shook her head before he could finish. Keep working hard, Brock. I hope we meet again at a Grand Festival one day.

She left with Mollie without giving him another chance to speak. Brock took the second blow like a man made of glass. In the evening light, Ethan could almost imagine him shattering across the ground.

It was a little pitiful. Ethan shook his head. He could have seen this coming. Brock's endless, eager infatuation with women might have been more than Yuma wanted to handle. More than that, Brock's ability left her little pleasure in teaching him.

Why had she approached him? She wanted to show a beginning Coordinator how a Breeder's skills could help in a Contest. Brock had entered his first and placed second. He hardly resembled a beginner. What was there for her to teach?

Ash patted Brock's shoulder with a mischievous smile. Come on, don't lose heart. Better luck next time.

Next time? Brock's expression crumpled. He shouted at the sky, There won't be a next time! I'm never becoming a Coordinator again!

His Coordinator debut ended on a bitter memory. Still, Brock had been turned down by women many times before. He would survive this too.

...

After a night in Gardenia Town, the two groups said goodbye in the morning and set out for their separate destinations. Ash, Brock, and Max headed for Ash's next Frontier facility. Ethan and May continued toward the Battle Tower.

It was not far from Gardenia Town. Ethan remembered seeing a Battle Tower in LaRousse City, Hoennthe technological city where he had encountered wild Deoxys and Rayquaza and later battled its block-shaped robots. That tower belonged to Hoenn's Frontier facilities, however, and had nothing to do with Kanto. He had passed by without challenging it.

Kanto's Battle Tower stood at the far eastern edge of the mainland, alone in a broad forest, distant from towns and people. After more than a day's travel, Ethan and May reached the forest.

Its remoteness made the tower hard to find. There was no road or trail underfoot and no landmark to guide them. They soon lost their bearings among the trees.

Fortunately, Ethan was not Ash. He did not keep wandering at random. He found the forest's only lake and followed the water. That led them easily toward the tower. Long before they reached it, they could see a building more than twenty stories high through gaps in the foliage.

That's the Battle Tower? May asked, looking up.

It is. Ethan smiled. Surprised? Did you expect a tower shape?

A little. She stuck out her tongue. The Battle Factory, Battle Dome, and Battle Palace didn't look quite the way their names suggested either, so I was prepared. I just didn't expect a building this...

Luxurious?

Yes. She examined what looked more like an expensive office building. Why build it so grandly in an empty forest? It doesn't fit the surroundings at all.

As they drew nearer, they heard a voice ahead. They assumed it belonged to a wild Pokemon until they went around several large trees and found a short, purple-haired youth muttering beneath one of them.

The youth was only a little taller than May, with delicate features and an apparent age somewhere in the teens. Hearing their footsteps, the figure started and turned around with a small cry. The voice was androgynous. With those boyish clothes and build, most people would have taken the stranger for a boy.

Ethan knew better the moment he saw her. She was Anabel, the Frontier Brain of the Battle Towerthe Salon Maiden.

Her face, voice, and figure all made it easy to mistake her for a boy. Ash and his friends had done so in the anime, not realizing their error until someone corrected them. Even Ethan found it hard to imagine that this tomboy would one day become the more feminine Anabel he knew in the International Police. A resemblance around her eyes was almost all that linked them at first glance.

Sorry. Did we startle you? May asked. She knew none of that and had never met this Anabel.

It's all right. Anabel put a hand against her chest and studied them. What brings you here?

Remembering the International Police Anabel's request, Ethan showed no hint of recognition. He smiled and explained that he had come to challenge the Battle Tower.

I thought so. Anabel smiled back. People rarely come here for anything else, but I always ask. Oh, I forgot to introduce myself. I'm Anabel, the Frontier Brain of that tower.

You're the Frontier Brain? May was astonished. She had pictured an imposing adult when she heard about the Battle Tower's ruler, not this delicate-looking boy. Ethan had given her no warning to expect otherwise.

Anabel led them toward the tower. She explained why she had been in the forest: she was patrolling the surrounding land. She considered herself its guardian. When no challengers came, she spent much of her time checking the forest's condition and looking for wild Pokemon that might need help.

From directly below, the building seemed taller still. A green-haired man with the look of a ninja came out as they reached its doors.

Back early today, Anabel? he greeted her. Then he noticed Ethan and May. More challengers? It feels as though there've been more of them lately. That must keep you busy.

Anabel waved a hand. It's my job.

Their easy exchange sounded like ordinary workplace conversation. It made it even harder for May to connect the youth beside her with the grand title of Frontier Brain.

Inside, they entered a bright hall. This was the Battle Tower's arena. At first glance it looked like a normal indoor venue, almost a basketball court, complete with a wooden floor.

Ethan could not understand that choice. The flooring might survive a light match, but a single Pokemon using Dig could tear holes through it in minutes. Had the people who built these Gyms and Frontier facilities never considered that? Were they rich enough not to care, able to repair any damage, or secretly in business with a flooring supplier?

If he ever ran a Gym, he would never choose a battlefield floor like this.

May headed to the seats. Ethan and Anabel took the Trainer positions at opposite sides of the arena.

The Frontier battle between challenger Ethan Vale and Battle Tower Brain Anabel will now begin! Each side may use three Pokemon. Only the challenger may switch Pokemon during the battle!

The rules sounded much like those of an ordinary Gym match. In a sense, that was an awkward weakness of the Battle Frontier. Its facilities were less conveniently placed and less famous than official Gyms. Beyond the exceptional strength of some Frontier Brains, they offered little to distinguish themselves. No wonder Scott, who owned the whole enterprise, traveled around recruiting challengers and spreading the word.

Anabel had already taken out her Poke Ball. She threw it firmly, blue light flashed, and Alakazam appeared before Ethan.

Whatever the difference in appearance between this tomboy and the Anabel of the International Police, their choice of Pokemon, at least, was much the same.
