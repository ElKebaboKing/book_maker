Chapter 652: Meeting Bea Again

At eight the next morning, outside the Pokemon Center, Ethan and Misty stood opposite Ash and his friends. They had eaten breakfast and were saying goodbye.

Ethan, Ash, and Brock were used to parting ways. For Misty and May, this farewell was much harder. They held hands, embraced, and clung to each other as though they might never meet again.

At last Ash couldn't stand it. You'll see each other again before long. It might only be a few days. Do you have to make such a fuss?

What would you know? Misty wiped at the corner of one eye and glared at him. Someone as clueless about feelings as you could never understand a bond between women!

Women? Brock looked Misty and May up and down and shook his head. It's a little early for you two to call yourselves women. Try again in ten or fifteen years...

Misty threw her backpack at his face before he could finish. Brock fell over at once.

Max regarded him without sympathy, adjusted his glasses, and turned to May. Sis, if you two hate saying goodbye so much, why don't we all travel together?

Travel together? Misty and May both brightened for a moment. They looked at each other, then sighed in unison.

They would have loved to. But Ethan and Ash were friends and rivals. Meeting now and then was one thing; traveling as a single group was another. It simply wasn't going to happen.

And if one of the girls left her own group for the other? Misty couldn't leave Ethan, while May couldn't abandon her little brother. Each had her own reason to stay. Neither could switch traveling companions on a whim.

They would have to wait for another chance someday.

At least the discussion eased their sadness enough for them to talk normally. Ash breathed a sigh of relief and waved to Ethan. See you next time, Ethan!

Ethan nodded, smiling as he looked over Ash's group. See you next time.

They went their separate ways, one group heading west and the other east. Soon they were out of sight of one another.

As Ethan had planned, his next destination was Dewford Island, a small island off the Hoenn mainland.

It lay in Hoenn's southwest. A short mountain range ran through its center, with three or four peaks that drew stone collectors to climb there every day. The busy town of Dewford stood at the foot of the mountains.

At Slateport's harbor, Ethan and Misty soon found a small passenger boat bound for the island. They bought tickets, boarded, and spent an uneventful half day at sea. They reached Dewford Town at three in the afternoon.

There was still daylight, so Ethan checked his navigation device and headed straight for the Dewford Gym with Misty. She had no time to take in how the island differed from the mainland towns. Hurrying after him, she crossed the town center in short order.

The Gym stood on a seaside cliff outside town. They followed a path through palm trees and arrived at a striking gate.

A gold-painted sign gleamed above it. At the top was a relief of a Machamp and a Machoke running together. The two Pokemon were shoulder to shoulder, wearing broad smiles. To Ethan, they looked almost comically intimate.

Odd as the design was, anyone could tell at a glance that this was a Fighting-type Gym. That was what made the gate so distinctive. Its strangeness served a purpose.

The Dewford Gym lay beyond it: an ordinary square, two-story building apart from its roof. Three ridges rose along the top, like those on the heads of the Machop evolutionary line, making the whole roof resemble a giant Machamp's head placed over the building.

What an unusual sense of style... Misty stared up at it. The Gym Leader isn't another strange older man like Chuck in Johto, is he?

Ethan gave her a look. Careful, or Chuck will visit your dreams tonight and beat you up. Then he grinned. The Leader here is Brawly. He's a fashionable guy, a professional surfer as well as a Gym Leader. He favors direct physical attacks, like waves crashing in from the sea. That's why he's called a big wave in fighting.

A big wave in fighting? Misty blinked. That's a good nickname. I just can't picture someone like that with a team of heavily muscled Fighting-type Pokemon.

They reached the Gym doors as they talked. Ethan raised a hand to knock, then noticed the doors were slightly ajar. Through the gap came the sounds of a battle.

Another challenger today?

Curious, Ethan pushed the doors open without knocking and went in with Misty. The battle court lay directly inside. Two women were battling there.

On the left stood a woman of about twenty. She wore a red athletic swimsuit and had long, lake-blue hair tied in a large ponytail. She was attractive, with the firm, well-defined muscles of someone who exercised constantly.

Her position suggested that she belonged to the Gym, though Ethan couldn't tell how she was connected to Brawly. Perhaps she was acting as Leader while he was away.

Then Ethan looked at her opponent and stopped. It was Bea! He and Misty had met her briefly at the Pokemon Center in Verdanturf Town.

She still wore her racer-like training outfit and the black headband tied to resemble rabbit ears. Her face was as serious as ever, as though it had no other expression. What was she doing here?

Then again, Bea specialized in Fighting types and traveled around the world. Of course a Fighting-type Gym would draw her. Ethan simply hadn't expected to encounter her again so soon after Verdanturf.

The woman from the Gym was using a Meditite. It was a Fighting- and Psychic-type Pokemon with gray-blue skin, a head shaped as though wrapped in a large turban, creamy ring patterns on its arms, and large black-pupiled eyes. It looked a little like a monkey from India.

Meditite trained at yoga deep in the mountains and could float in the air. Daily yoga was essential to it. Meditation strengthened its mind, and it could spend hours at a time doing nothing else. It was said to eat only one Berry a day; enduring hunger was another way to discipline itself.

Bea's Pokemon was a Pangoro. Though it came from outside Hoenn, its appearance suited its name: it resembled a panda, only larger, fiercer, and stronger. A long bamboo shoot stuck out of its mouth like a cigarette. At first glance, it looked like a member of a street gang.

Pangoro could be hot-tempered and impulsive, but it was bold and deeply loyal to its companions. That drew many Trainers to it. Winning its respect, though, meant negotiating with fists, so few people managed to train one.

It was a wild Pokemon proud of its arm strength, the sort that preferred to solve problems with a punch. One blow could turn a large dump truck into scrap metal. Yet it would not tolerate anyone bullying the weak in front of it. Even when attacked, it charged forward without flinching, swinging arms strong enough to snap a utility pole. By watching the sway of the leaf in its mouth, it could also read an opponent's movements. It had a rough sense of justice as well as a fierce nature.

Those two Pokemon were the source of the sounds Ethan had heard outside. Neither Trainer noticed him and Misty enter. Both were completely absorbed in their battle.

On the court, Meditite and Pangoro fought at close range. They exchanged Fighting-type moves, advancing and countering without pause.

Their types didn't give either one a simple advantage. Meditite's Psychic-type attacks couldn't hurt the Dark-type Pangoro, but its Fighting-type moves were twice as effective. Pangoro's Dark-type attacks were strong against Meditite's Psychic type, while its Fighting-type moves would do only half damage.

Bea, however, made no attempt to win with Dark-type attacks. She kept Pangoro on Fighting moves alone. The woman facing her did the same with Meditite.

There was less dazzling move work than in a battle of ranged attacks, but every exchange had the force of fist meeting flesh. It was no less compelling to watch.

Thud! Thud! Thud-thud!

Each of Pangoro's punches seemed to push a wave of air ahead of it. The blows looked immensely heavy.

Meditite was smaller and lacked Pangoro's raw strength. It had to circle and maneuver to stay in the fight, and it began to lose ground in their direct exchanges.

Fists met fists, palms struck palms, elbows collided. Detect, Arm Thrust, Karate Chop, Circle Throw, Vital Throw, and High Jump Kick followed one another in rapid succession. In little more than ten seconds, the Pokemon traded over a dozen blows.

Pangoro was the stronger of the two. Meditite held its own early on, but as the battle continued, its strength failed. Pangoro knocked it to the floor.

The woman sighed at her Pokemon's defeat. All right, you win. You've earned the right to challenge Brawly. But as I said, he's out surfing. If you want to battle him, you'll have to come back tomorrow morning.

Bea frowned slightly but said nothing. She recalled Pangoro and headed for the door. Turning, she came face to face with Ethan and Misty.

Her steps paused. She clearly remembered seeing them somewhere, but after a polite nod, she walked out of the Dewford Gym without another word, leaving behind a strikingly cool silhouette.

She really was rather cool.

Before Ethan could look away, the woman from the Gym noticed him and Misty. Who are you? she asked in surprise.

Sorry, Ethan said with a smile. We came in without knocking. I'm Ethan Vale, a traveling Trainer. I came to

She waved a hand before he could finish. I know, I know. You're here to challenge the Gym, right?

Of course.

She shrugged. Then you probably heard what I told the last challenger. The Gym Leader is away right now. You'll have to wait until tomorrow. And before you can challenge him, you have to beat me.

That's fine, Ethan said. I haven't asked your name, though. You are...?

Oh, I forgot to introduce myself. The woman drew herself up proudly. I'm Shauna, Brawly's top student and his girlfriend. I also act as Gym Leader when he's away!

I see. My apologies. Can we start our match now?

He said the right words, but Shauna heard very little respect in his voice. How irritating! The woman with Pangoro had been the same.

Didn't these people know how famous Brawly was in Dewford? He was the most popular man on the island, a star surfer as well as a Gym Leader. Almost every girl in town wanted to be his girlfriend.

So why did challengers react with such indifference when she told them she was his top student and girlfriend?
