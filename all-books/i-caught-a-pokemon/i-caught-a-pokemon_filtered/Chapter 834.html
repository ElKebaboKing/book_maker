Chapter 834: It Depends on What Munchlax Learned

In the open yard behind the Potpourri Island Pokemon Center, their Pokemon helped carry over the crates of fine fruit they had won in the orienteering race. After a quick wash, Ethan, May, and all their Pokemon sat in a circle and began a feast.

Some fruit looked like pineapples, some like apples, melons, pears, or grapes. They resembled familiar varieties, but there were differences Ethan could not name.

He picked up a pear-shaped fruit and took a small bite. It was sweet and so juicy it nearly overflowed. He could not think of a grand description for it. It was simply delicious. No wonder the island reserved fruit this good for its prizes.

May held a different fruit in each hand, biting first one and then the other. Happiness was written all over her face. She really did love food. They had worked hard and broken a record to win this meal; perhaps that made it taste even better.

Ethan finished his fruit and reached for another. His hand found an empty crate. He turned and discovered May's Munchlax sitting beside him. Somehow, in the short time they had been eating, it had emptied two large boxes by itself.

The contrast with his Gardevoir was striking. She ate in small, graceful bites, while Munchlax had swept through the fruit like a storm. Between May and her Pokemon, Ethan was surrounded by eager eaters.

He did not mind. He had only wanted to taste the fruit. His companions and their Pokemon mattered far more than whether he got another piece. He leaned back and watched them enjoy themselves.

There was no Team Rocket, Team Magma, or Team Aqua to fight now, and no mighty Legendary Pokemon looming over them. Their journey held only the Battle Frontier and Contests. Compared with the upheavals they had lived through, it might seem quiet. Ethan did not find it dull. These gentler days gave his life variety and let him gather strength for battles yet to come.

No one could fight constantly, however tough they were. This was closer to the everyday life of most ordinary Trainers. In a way, Ethan had returned to how his journey began.

...

After leaving the Pokemon Center, Ethan and May remained on Potpourri Island and visited its town, Wisteria Town, again. The orienteering race was not the only event taking place here. The Wisteria Town Contest would be held the next day.

It was excellent news for May. Another win would bring her closer to the Kanto Grand Festival. But her good mood lasted only until they reached the Contest hall. There she spotted a rival she could not stand: Harley.

Like the Silver Town Contest venue, Wisteria's hall was no great arena. It was a three-story building with a rounded base set on a lawn. A street of food stalls stood outside. At its entrance, they saw Harley.

He wore his familiar outfit and his usual sly smile. Ethan could not tell whether Harley had recovered the Pokemon the Team Rocket trio had tricked away from him, but he looked well enough. Ethan guessed he had, or he would hardly be here.

Harley saw May and instinctively took a few steps toward her, ready to taunt her or try another trick. Then he noticed Ethan at her side and stopped.

He had almost forgotten that May was no longer traveling with those easily fooled children. She was with this infuriating man now. After being thwarted by Ethan several times, Harley did not dare approach so casually. He resented Ethan and feared him too.

Harley glared at them from a distance and turned away. Privately, he resolved to make May lose badly today.

He had gone, but the sight of him weighed on May. She disliked admitting it, yet Harley was a strong Coordinator. His schemes were hard to anticipate, and his stage performances were strange and changeable. Apart from Drew, he was the most dangerous rival she had met. She could not treat him lightly. This Contest would be harder than the one in Silver Town.

You'll have to be careful, Ethan said. With me here, Harley won't get away with any underhanded schemes. But he's still an excellent performer. If you battle him, keep your guard up.

I know.

The unexpected encounter had spoiled May's desire to stroll around. After looking over the arena inside the hall, she and Ethan went to a nearby open space to plan her performance.

Ethan sat on a bench holding May's Egg. Which Pokemon will you use this time?

May had left her other Pokemon in Hoenn. For now, she had only Combusken, Squirtle, and Munchlax, along with the Egg that had not yet hatched. Her choices were limited. Combusken and Squirtle had already taken part in Contests. Naturally, she looked toward Munchlax.

Munchlax? Ethan followed her gaze and nodded. Good idea. It's time Munchlax got some experience and shared the work. To be safe, let's practice the way we did last time. I'll lend you one of my Pokemon.

He took Eevee's Poke Ball from his pouch and released it. May had considered using Combusken and Squirtle to train with Munchlax, but having Ethan's Eevee as a practice partner was even better.

With little time to spare, May took the Pokemon to the open ground and began training.

Watching her, Ethan suddenly felt as though he were playing a game in which he had to raise and guide a character. That character was May. He wondered how far she could grow in the end.

...

The next day, the Wisteria Town Contest began. The host and the judges were the same familiar faces. They were professionals, but seeing them at every Contest could make a spectator wonder whether anyone else ever filled those roles.

Ethan took a seat in the audience and watched from start to finish. The first few Coordinators performed well enough to open the event on a good note. Then May's turn came.

She chose Squirtle, not Munchlax, for the first-round appeal. Squirtle's confidence and strength had grown through its recent training, and it was becoming more accomplished onstage.

Squirtle combined Rapid Spin and Bubble Beam to create a splendid spiral of bubbles. It finished with Ice Beam, shattering the bubbles into a great fountain of ice. The polished display earned May a high score.

Ethan was impressed. Squirtle did not make a single mistake. May's talent was becoming more obvious with every performance. Before longperhaps by the end of the Kanto Grand Festivalshe could truly be a first-rate Coordinator.

After May left the stage, the excitement she had created seemed to lift the next few entrants. They performed better than expected and won applause, though none reached her level. Then Harley came out, and the atmosphere changed.

He used Ariados again, one of his most frequent choices. So he had recovered his Pokemon after all. Whatever the Team Rocket trio had tried, they evidently had not come out ahead against Harley.

Ariados wove a web like the one it had used against May before. That did not mean Harley had run out of ideas. Ariados climbed to the top and plunged down like a bungee jumper. With Scary Face, it became a terrifying, bandaged-looking spider, only the two dark spots on its back showing like eyes staring ahead.

Harley's style was as eerie as ever. Its distinctiveness still won applause and appreciation from most of the audience.

Several more Coordinators performed after him. Perhaps his act had left the hall in too strange a mood: their performances faltered, and the crowd's response grew cool. Ethan shook his head. As in a Pokemon battle, an outstanding Contest performance could make ordinary entrants look weaker and affect the audience watching them.

When the three judges finished scoring, the screen displayed the eight Coordinators advancing to the second round. May's portrait was first. She had earned the highest score in the appeal round.

It was a delightful result. May had once barely advanced in last place; now she was the leading Coordinator in the first round. Ethan had not witnessed every step of her growth, but he had accompanied her through several important moments. Seeing this achievement moved him.

The eight qualifiers were paired for the battle round. Unlike at the Saffron Contest, May and Harley were placed in separate brackets. Each defeated their other opponents and reached the final.

Another meeting that felt destined. It was no great surprise, though. Both were now far stronger than most entrants, and neither had faced another rival who could stop them. The final would show which of them was better.

They sent out their Pokemon. As agreed the day before, May used Munchlax in the battle round. Harley chose Octillery.

Munchlax had already appeared in May's earlier matches that round. It had battled splendidly, defeating its opponents with little difficulty and carrying her to the final.

Ethan had not seen Harley's Octillery in any previous battle or performance. Harley had probably caught it recently. Even so, he had clearly devoted care to its training; on its first Contest stage, it had performed extremely well.

Two Pokemon making their Contest debuts had both reached the final. What would happen when they met? Lilian presented the matchup as a mystery for the audience. Ethan, however, remembered the anime and the outcome it had shown.

Ordinarily, May's Munchlax would lose to Harley's Octillery. This time Ethan had intervened. During their practice battle the day before, without calling May's attention to it, he had made a point of training Munchlax to interrupt Rest.

Rest was Octillery's specialty. In the anime, it had recovered a great deal of health with the move, turned the battle around at a crucial moment, and defeated Munchlax.

Ethan could not guarantee that Munchlax had learned enough from one session. At least it would be less likely to freeze in confusion when Octillery tried Rest. Beyond that, the outcome depended on Munchlax's own judgment and talent.

With Ethan watching expectantly, the decisive battle began.
