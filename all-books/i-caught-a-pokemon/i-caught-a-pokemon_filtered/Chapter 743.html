Chapter 743: Making a Rival for Herself

Seeing Anabel's astonishment, Shelly wanted to say that if even the International Police did not know what the creature was, the rest of them certainly could not.

One thing seemed clear: this unknown Pokemon was at least in Groudon and Kyogre's class, perhaps stronger. Its extraordinary shape and the pressure it gave off made that plain. A single glance was enough to tell anyone it was no ordinary Pokemon.

Anabel did not know what it wanted, but it had attacked a police boat. That made it an enemy. Worse, its intent felt unmistakably hostile. Recovering from her surprise, she organized the officers around her to fight back.

Poke Balls flashed across the deck. A large group of Arcanine appeared and, at their officers' orders, sent a mass of flames into the sky. The volley looked formidable. As the fire reached the creature, however, it passed straight through its body and sped on without hitting anything.

It was like punching a shadow. The attack met no resistance, not even a ripple.

How is that possible?

The officers stared in disbelief. None understood why they could not touch it.

Shelly was the first to grasp what they were seeing. It's a projection! Its real body isn't here. We can't hit it!

Anabel's brow tightened. A projection? What kind of Pokemon could damage a ship like this using only a projection? How terrifying would the real creature be? And what did it want?

No one could answer her. The Pokemon soon showed them its purpose.

Two shafts of black light shot from its hands into the ship. Moments later, they emerged carrying Archie and Maxie, wrapped in darkness, out of the cabin where the men had been held.

Anabel had not imagined the prisoners were its target. Their flailing and bewildered faces showed that Archie and Maxie knew no more than she did. Even the normally composed Maxie looked wildly around him.

Before the watching officers, the two men rose higher and higher. They reached the creature, and all three vanished into the black opening overhead.

The attack ended as quickly as it had begun. Anabel and her officers had had no means of stopping it. They could only watch the prisoners disappear. Even after the sky returned to normal, the officers stood on deck for some time before they could take in what had happened.

Two dangerous criminals already in custody had been rescued by an unknown Pokemon. They had thought the affair was over. Why had another problem appeared now?

Anabel sighed, her head already aching. Shelly looked at her with some sympathy. After Maxie and Archie were captured, Shelly's and Courtney's past offenses had been cleared. The case no longer involved them. Once they left Hoenn, they would not even need to fear retaliation from their old organizations. For Anabel, however, the trouble was beginning again.

We should contact Ethan, Shelly suggested after a moment. He needs to know. And he might be the only one who can tell us what that Pokemon was.

...

When Ethan received the news, he had just arrived with Bea and Misty at an inn in the small town. The town was too small to have a Pokemon Center, and most of its residents had evacuated and not yet returned. The three had searched for some time before finding this hot spring inn at the foot of a mountain.

That search made Ethan admire Bea all the more. Before they came, she had been eating and sleeping near the park in the forest, exposed to the weather with only a simple tent. She had stripped her life down to the bare necessities for the sake of training. No wonder her body was so strong.

The innkeeper was an elderly woman. She said she had lived through all manner of storms and saw no reason to evacuate. If she died, she would die with her house. She grumbled that young people could not bear a little bad weather and only knew how to build muscles that were no use to them.

Following her running commentary, Ethan and the others entered a guest room and sat down in its Japanese-style sitting area. The woman went to prepare dinner. With no outsider nearby, Ethan finally told Bea and Misty what he had learned.

They were attacked? Misty's eyes widened. I thought it was all over. Are there other Legendary Pokemon in Hoenn besides Rayquaza, Groudon, and Kyogre?

From Anabel's and Shelly's descriptions, Ethan already knew the attacker's identity. It isn't from Hoenn. In a sense, it isn't even a Pokemon of this world.

You know what it is?

Ethan nodded and sighed. It's connected to the creatures called Ultra Beastsbeings from another dimension. More precisely, it's one of the Legendary Cosmic Pokemon. They come from the same place as Ultra Beasts, but they're much stronger and aren't counted among ordinary Ultra Beasts anymore. Ultra Beasts don't naturally belong in this world. Most arrive through wormholes called Ultra Wormholes. They're dangerous, especially when someone puts their power to use. The damage can be catastrophic.

Ultra Beasts? Ultra Wormholes? Legendary Cosmic Pokemon? Misty had never heard any of these terms. Her understanding of the world had shifted yet again.

Bea was equally lost. She did not interrupt. Instead she quietly turned over the unfamiliar information, trying to guess what Ethan and Misty had experienced.

So one of those Legendary Cosmic Pokemon attacked Anabel? Misty asked.

Yes. The one known as the Plunderer of LightNecrozma.

The Plunderer of Light. Misty could not imagine how powerful a Pokemon must be to earn a name like that.

But I don't understand, she said. Why would it rescue Archie and Maxie?

To an outsider, the two men and Necrozma had no apparent connection. Why would such a powerful creature save humans who must seem insignificant to it?

Ethan saw one possible answer in its sudden appearance. Team Rainbow Rocket was already beginning to form.

Led by Giovanni, Team Rainbow Rocket would surpass Team Rocket, Team Magma, Team Aqua, and the other criminal organizations across the regions. It would gather their leaders under one banner. Their immense ambition and malice would resonate with the power spilling from Necrozmathe power to open Ultra Wormholes. Under Giovanni's influence, they would be summoned to Alola and become Team Rainbow Rocket.

Its goal was simple: to rule every world.

With the Aether Foundation's knowledge of other worlds, Team Rainbow Rocket would build a powerful army of Ultra Beasts. Its ordinary members would be extraordinarily strong, and each leader would gain at least one Legendary Pokemon. In wealth and fighting strength, the organization would stand at the world's summit. If no one stopped it, conquering the world might be only a matter of time.

Of course, the stronger the enemy, the greater the resistance it would face. By assembling leaders from every region, Giovanni would also draw opposition from them all.

But that was supposed to be a story for much later, in Alola. Why were there signs of it already? The timing was far too early.

Had Ethan's actions caused a butterfly effect? Team Rocket had suffered a heavy blow, and Team Magma and Team Aqua were on the verge of collapse. Had those changes altered the future?

That explanation did not quite fit. Even if Giovanni had conceived the idea, where would he get the power before reaching Alola? How would he contact Necrozma?

Another possibility struck Ethan: someone in the future was trying to change the present.

In a world of Pokemon, changing time was not impossible. Someone might have reached back through time and brought these events forward. Otherwise, why would Necrozma appear here and take Archie and Maxie specifically? Whoever was behind it must be gathering strength for something.

Whatever the answer, the thought set alarm bells ringing. His enemies were moving. The future looked more dangerous than before.

At least Ethan's own strength continued to grow. If this was connected to him, perhaps his future opponents feared him enough to act early. It was only a guess, but he wanted to believe it.

He shook his head and kept the speculation to himself. Sharing it with Bea and Misty would only trouble them; it would do nothing to help right now.

Ethan changed the subject. The heavy mood gradually lifted. The three were no longer strangers and had shared experiences to draw on, so their conversation wandered easily from one topic to another.

Misty picked out some of the most exciting things she and Ethan had done recently. Bea, never one for many words, gave them a brief account of her own travels. All three learned more about one another.

For the first time, Bea heard that Ethan had won two regional League Conferences, helped the International Police deal heavy blows to three criminal organizations, and even saved the world without anyone noticing.

Misty grew animated as she spoke and stood up at one point in her excitement. Bea did not think she was lying. She had spent enough time with them to have a sense of their characters. Even so, the accounts sounded almost impossible. Bea stared at Ethan, struggling to connect these feats with the handsome man sitting before her.

So that's who he is. He's remarkable.

She remembered offering to fight for him earlier that day. In hindsight, the offer was almost funny.

Misty looked down and caught Bea's expression, the brightness in her eyes. She abruptly regretted how much she had said and sat back on the tatami. She had seen that look too many times.

Everything she had told Bea was true. That was precisely the problem. Why had she gone on about Ethan's virtues to another woman?

What an idiot I am. I've found myself another rival.
