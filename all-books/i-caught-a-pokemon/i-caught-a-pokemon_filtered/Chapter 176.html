Chapter 176: An Onix's Grave

There was nowhere to hide in the office. Ethan looked around and soon noticed a filing cabinet against the wall. A fresh scrape marked the floor at its base.

He hurried over and shoved the cabinet aside. Sure enough, it concealed a dark doorway. The door stood slightly ajar, and through the gap he could make out stairs leading underground.

Air moved through the opening. Whatever lay below was connected to the outside. This had to be the warehouse's escape passage.

Lorelei came over and looked down the stairs. "The executive must have gone this way."

She started toward the doorway, but Ethan stopped her.

"What now?" she asked.

"I know Lambda. He's devious. We should be careful."

He sent Haunter ahead to check the passage. When Haunter returned a short while later and gave them an okay sign, Ethan followed Lorelei and Sabrina through the door.

Beyond it ran a long, straight tunnel, less than a meter wide. The uneven floor and square shovel marks along its walls suggested a hurried excavation.

They had taken only a few steps by the dim emergency lights when Haunter warned them. Ahead lay a sprung trapdoor. Beneath it, more than a dozen sharp spikes gleamed.

There really were traps. Lorelei felt a chill as she looked down. She was glad Ethan had sent Haunter first.

He was brave, observant, and decisive, with powerful Pokemonand handsome besides. No wonder Sabrina had taken an interest in him.

They went around the trap and hurried on. After another minute or so, the tunnel sloped upward. They emerged from a cave into dense forest, well beyond the warehouse.

"There he is!" Lorelei spotted a man running toward the edge of the woods. It had to be Lambda.

He heard her shout and looked back. When he saw Ethan and the others, his face changed. They had caught up already?

Distracted, he tripped over a fallen branch and sprawled face-first onto the ground. With his pursuers closing in, he scrambled up on hands and knees and ran on, dignity forgotten.

Ethan pointed ahead. Gardevoir used Teleport to appear directly in Lambda's path.

"Get out of my way!" Lambda threw out Poke Balls, hoping his Pokemon would hold Gardevoir back. Haunter and Dewgong joined the fight. Within seconds, Lambda's Pokemon were defeated.

He ran on alone for several dozen meters and reached the coast, panting. A rough little harbor stood in the bay, with cargo scattered around it. Team Rocket had evidently built it to bring in supplies.

A speedboat waited at the dock. It was his last chance to escape.

Lambda untied it and jumped aboard. Before he could start the engine, Sabrina arrived and pulled both man and boat up from the water with her psychic power.

"Aaaagh!" He screamed in helpless fury. Suspended in the air, he had nowhere left to run.

Sabrina dropped the boat heavily onto land. The impact threw Lambda down, and before he could rise, Ethan reached him and punched him in the temple. Lambda went limp.

Ethan looked at him uneasily. He had never hit anyone quite that hard. Had he killed him?

Lorelei checked, then shook her head with a smile. "He's fine. Just unconscious. He'll wake up after a while."

That settled the matter. Capturing a senior Team Rocket executive was cause to celebrate. Haunter lifted Lambda with Ominous Wind, and the three set out toward the warehouse.

In their haste to pursue him, though, they had lost track of the hidden tunnel's exit. Lorelei remembered where the warehouse stood, so they found another route through the trees toward the island's center.

Their operation had succeeded. Once they handed Lambda to the police, his detention and transfer would be out of their hands.

As they walked, they came upon a strange clearing. In its middle stood a stone pillar more than ten meters high. Its stacked rocks formed the shape of a giant Onix tail. A gravestone rested at its foot.

Curious, Sabrina went closer and read its inscription.

Here rests Tectonix, my treasured partner. May this memorial keep his memory alive.

"Tectonix was an Onix?" she asked.

"Yes," Lorelei said. "This place is called Memorial Pillar, or the Onix's Grave. They say an Onix is buried beneath it. It's been here for more than ten years, and everyone around here knows it."

Ethan gazed at the grave. When he had reached this place in the games, the Onix's memorial had already saddened him. Seeing it before him now, after forming so many bonds with his own Pokemon, affected him much more.

"Losing a Pokemon unexpectedly must be terrible for its trainer," he said.

Weeds grew thick around the monument. Moved by the neglected sight, Ethan bent down and began pulling them out one by one.

Lorelei and Sabrina watched quietly. They were trainers too, and understood why he wanted to do it.

He kept working until sweat ran down his cheeks. After clearing much of the ground, he moved to another patch of tall grass and parted it. Beneath was a small pile of stones. Words had been carved on the one at the top.

Thank you for your kindness, stranger. I buried something here in the hope that it will help you.

Something was underneath? Curious, Ethan moved the stones aside. A rock with a metallic sheen lay buried below them.

How long had that little marker been hidden in the grass? If he had not decided to clear the weeds, he would never have noticed it, much less the object beneath it. But what was it?

Sabrina and Lorelei came over. Sabrina studied the rock and shook her head. Lorelei, however, recognized it.

"I believe that's the legendary Onix metal."

"Onix metal? What's that?" Ethan asked.

"So there are things you don't know." Lorelei rolled her eyes, plainly pleased to have caught him out. She noticed Sabrina's expression and quickly checked herself.

"Ahem. Onix metal is an extremely rare mineral. Some people work it into a Metal Coat and put it on an Onix. It changes the Pokemon's body and lets it evolve into Steelix. For a trainer with an Onix, it's a real treasure."
