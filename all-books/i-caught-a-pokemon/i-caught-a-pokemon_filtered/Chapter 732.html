Chapter 732: As Long as I Stay Hidden

In an unnamed archipelago northeast of Mossdeep City, Ethan and Misty approached the edge of Hoenn. A dozen or so nautical miles farther out lay the open sea beyond the region.

Dragonite had carried them here in little more than an hour. Thick storm clouds still covered the sky. Ethan could not tell whether they were natural or caused by some strange power, but they made excellent cover. Beneath them, Dragonite was only a black speck. Even someone looking up would struggle to notice it.

Following the coordinates, Dragonite landed on one of the smaller islands. Ethan found Shelly again on a shallow stretch of shore. She was sitting atop the cabin of a submarine identical to the shark-shaped one that had sunk before. She leaned back with one leg crossed over the other, looking entirely at ease.

She acknowledged Ethan's arrival with a wave. He and Misty hopped down from Dragonite and went to her.

Ethan looked over the submarine. Wasn't your last one destroyed? Where did you get this?

I borrowed it from another Team Aqua base. Shelly stretched and climbed down. They keep these things in secret bases all over the place. Even they don't know how many they have. Find an unguarded base, and you can take one without anyone noticing. It's nothing remarkable.

She made it sound simple. Ethan knew it could not have been. Shelly had been Team Aqua's strategist and second-in-command. Even Archie or Matt would have had trouble locating an unguarded submarine so readily. Shelly knew the organization's logistics better than anyone. She was its brain, and she knew where everything was kept.

He did not need the details. Where's Archie?

Under their plan, Shelly had been tracking and watching him. Since she was here, Archie had to be nearby.

She studied Ethan before answering. You really did guess right. We planned for the possibility, but honestly, I never imagined Archie could have survived what happened to him.

Why not? Never underestimate anyone, especially someone that strong. Ethan glanced at her. If it had been me in his place, would you have assumed I was dead?

You're different. You're the most extraordinary person I've ever met. I know Archie. He isn't as fortunate as you.

Ethan understood her meaning: Archie did not command a collection of Legendary Pokemon that could save him. Pokemon power mattered, but survival also came down to the person. Archie was hardened and at home in the sea. Ethan had never believed a simple explosion or the water itself would kill him. His return had seemed almost inevitable.

There was no point arguing with Shelly. She already thought Ethan unique, and he was content to let her. It cost him nothing and added a little mystery to her view of him.

It doesn't matter now, he said. Courtney just contacted me. Maxie and Kyogre are coming this way too. They'll arrive before long. We need to get to work.

Maxie and Kyogre? Shelly frowned as she grasped the danger. How did they know Archie and Groudon were here? I thought we'd find Archie, deal with him, and then turn to Maxie. Now they're converging again. What timing.

Maybe the two Legendary Pokemon can sense each other. They've fought for so long that they probably know each other's presence. Kyogre found Groudon that way last time. Ethan patted the pouch at his waist. In any case, I'm better prepared now. I won't let them escape again, and we won't have to go looking for Maxie afterward.

I hope so. Shelly was less confident. Don't forget Maxie has the Blue Orb. I've heard Archie has obtained the Red Orb as well.

So Archie had gotten it after all. Neither leader had the orb Ethan himself wanted, but with those artifacts they might control the ancient Pokemon and trigger Primal Reversion. That made them dangerous.

All the more reason to hurry.

Shelly led Ethan and Misty across the shallows between islands to a larger one. It was more than twice the size of their landing spot, thick with forest and with a lake at its center. At the forest's edge, a Team Aqua grunt peered out at them. Shelly walked straight toward him.

The grunt relaxed visibly when he saw her. Then he noticed Ethan and grew tense again.

B-boss...

Shelly nodded. What's happening?

The leader's by the central lake. Groudon is there too.

Shelly and Courtney had each said they would recruit people inside their organizations to help. Shelly had clearly succeeded. No wonder she had been able to relax on the beach: she had already planted an informant near Archie.

The grunt guided them through the trees. At the lake, they hid behind several large trunks and looked across the water. Dozens of Team Aqua members were spread along the far shore. Beside them stood helicopters, speedboats, and a large landing craft. Archie and Matt were talking aboard the craft.

They brought plenty of equipment, Ethan said. Archie looks prepared. But where's Groudon?

The grunt checked Shelly's face. She nodded, and he answered. It's beneath the rock in the middle of the island. The leader has located it. They're about to begin.

They had arrived just in time. Shelly turned to Ethan and lowered her voice. What do you want to do?

Ethan rubbed his chin. You're the strategist. What would you suggest?

She first dismissed the grunt. That's all we need from you. Go back.

He understood they needed to talk privately. Of course. Please be careful from here on. He left without delay and ran back toward the shore.

Ethan watched him go. He seems to respect you more than Archie. How did you turn him?

After a pause, Shelly sighed. Only some Team Aqua members joined for that impossible ideal. Most just need a living.

A living? In a criminal organization? Her explanation sounded odd to Ethan, but he did not challenge it. He waited for her plan.

We have two options, Shelly said. First, we attack now, before Archie and his people know we're here. We could catch them completely off guard.

That sounds good, Misty said. They have no idea we're nearby. We'd get the most out of a surprise attack.

Ethan watched Team Aqua through the brush. It's the simplest and most effective answer. I have enough strength to hit them hard in a short time. But...

But it exposes us, Shelly finished. Team Magma will be on guard when it arrives. If Maxie doesn't want a direct fight, he may abandon the plan and leave with Kyogre.

Exactly. We could stop Archie before he controls Groudon, but if Maxie gets spooked and escapes, finding Team Magma and Kyogre again would take time. Other things might happen beyond our control. Courtney alone probably couldn't keep them here.

Misty understood at once. She had not thought that far ahead, but the risk was clear once they explained it.

Then...?

We watch and wait for Team Magma, Shelly said. Once the two sides clash, we move in and take advantage.

That works too, Misty said. They'd wear each other down and be too tangled together to escape. We could catch both sides at once.

Then she saw that Ethan and Shelly still looked troubled. What? Is there a problem with that plan too?

A serious one, Shelly said. By then both teams may have gained control of Kyogre and Groudon. If those Pokemon undergo Primal Reversion and fightand Ethan joins the battlethe scale of it could devastate these waters and even affect the mainland.

Misty's head ached. So the first plan is better after all?

Shelly looked to Ethan instead of answering. Misty followed her gaze. Whatever they decided, he would make the final call. They would carry out his choice even if the plan had flaws.

It may not be as complicated as we're making it, Ethan said. Think about their last attempt. Archie and Maxie failed because we interfered. They're trying again. Do you really think they haven't prepared for us?

You mean... Shelly seemed to see where he was going.

Just that. They may look determined to finish this, and each may have an ancient Pokemon. But they'll both be watching for me. Even if they fight each other, neither can commit completely while expecting me to appear.

Misty's eyes brightened. So they'll hold something back in case we show up. They might not even dare use Primal Reversion right away.

Exactly. The longer I stay out of sight, the less certain they'll feel.

Shelly saw the weakness in that. When do we enter, then? If they keep their strength in reserve, the moment we reveal ourselves they might join forces against us.

We'll see who has more patience. Ethan grinned. They can't remain at a stalemate forever. As long as we stay hidden, they'll keep fighting, even if they're only testing one another. Eventually one side will start losing and have to strike harder. That's when we move.
