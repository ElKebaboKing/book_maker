Chapter 239: Kumquat Island

With hundreds of people watching, Poliwrath seemed to find a sudden last reserve of strength. It attacked Jolteon of its own accord with Mega Punch, its fists blurring as it struck.

The attack looked fierce, but everyone could see it was a final struggle. Jolteon followed the punches and dodged them nimbly.

Discharge and Body Slam had broken the ice left beneath Jolteon's feet by Ice Beam. With no danger of slipping, it could run freely again. As its speed returned and Poliwrath's strength ebbed, not one of the punches connected.

After dodging more than a dozen, Jolteon saw an opening and opened its jaws. Lightning flashed between its closely set fangs as it bit down on Poliwrath.

Crackle!

Electricity was strong against Poliwrath's Water type. The super effective Thunder Fang sent another surge through its body. This time Poliwrath could not even cry out. It collapsed and could no longer fight.

The referee declared Ethan the winner.

Applause rose from the crowd. Many had expected Mugsy to win, or at least to fight an even match. Instead, the handsome newcomer had beaten the Trainer on a winning streak with surprising ease. More than a few women among the spectators looked at Ethan with fresh interest.

Once the result was certain, Mugsy recalled his badly hurt Poliwrath, his face dark. Without another word or a backward glance, he pushed into the crowd and disappeared.

Ethan guessed the defeat had embarrassed him too much to offer even a polite farewell. His manner was a world away from his confidence before the match. Yet Poliwrath needed urgent care, and tending to it ought to be Mugsy's first concern.

Poliwrath had been unlucky. It had formidable fighting skills, but Ethan's type advantage had worn it down before it could make full use of them. Pokemon battles could change in an instant. No Trainer knew what an opponent would send out next, and luck played its part in every victory or defeat. Mugsy had drawn a poor matchup. Ethan might have struggled just as much in his place.

Jolteon returned to Ethan's feet. The deck quieted. Spectators looked around and murmured to one another, but after a long wait no one came forward to challenge him.

"Never mind." Without another opponent, Ethan had no reason to keep battling. He recalled Jolteon and left with Sabrina as the crowd watched.

...

Hooooonk!

With a deafening blast of its horn, the passenger ship eased toward a harbor. After more than twenty hours at sea and stops at three islands, it reached Hamlin Island at dusk the following day.

Ethan had not battled again since his match with Mugsy. The other Trainers aboard were generally weaker. Although they organized several more matches, none interested him as Mugsy had. He was the Indigo League Champion now; he no longer needed to accept every casual challenge as he once might have.

He had also used the voyage to spend time with Sabrina. It felt like a small holiday for the two of them.

As it happened, Sabrina's period had begun. Ethan fetched warm water and helped keep her abdomen warm, attending to her with care. Perhaps no one had ever looked after her so tenderly. His efforts touched her deeply, and throughout the day she regarded him with unusual warmth.

Ethan did not know what she was thinking. To him, caring for the woman he loved when she felt unwell was simply the right thing to do.

One thing did puzzle him: Mugsy had vanished after their match. Ethan had expected him to recover his confidence and ask for a rematch, but it seemed he had left the ship at one of the stops.

Perhaps Poliwrath's injuries had forced him to seek treatment at a town's Pokemon Center. Or perhaps losing with his strongest Pokemon convinced him he could not win, and he would rather leave than face Ethan again as the loser. Whatever the reason, Ethan doubted they would meet again.

"Where to now?" Sabrina asked gently, interrupting his thoughts.

They stood at the harbor, with Hamlin Island's town ahead of them.

"Let's keep going," Ethan suggested, looking up at the sky. "Kumquat Island isn't far. It has the final Gym. If we leave before sunset, we can find somewhere to stay when we arrive."

Sabrina agreed. These days, when they had a decision to make, she generally went along with Ethan's plans even if she had ideas of her own.

Ethan called Charizard, and they climbed onto its back for the flight to Kumquat Island.

They soared above the sea. Perhaps because night was approaching, the wind felt colder than before. Sabrina knew she was still not feeling her best, so she wrapped them both in psychic power to shield them from the chill.

They flew for more than two hours. By the time Kumquat Island appeared beneath them, night had fallen. The rest of the island was too dark to see clearly, so Ethan directed Charizard toward the harbor.

Even at this hour, the docks were brightly lit. A large passenger liner from another region was moored in the bay, and crowds of tourists streamed off it. Their chatter filled the lighted waterfront. Kumquat Island seemed to be another popular destination for travelers.

Ethan patted Charizard's neck, signaling it to descend gently. Even with its speed reduced, the sudden arrival startled several people below. Ethan apologized, recalled Charizard, and joined the flow of people walking with Sabrina toward the island's town.
