Chapter 646: Poor Jigglypuff

Jigglypuff looked more uncomfortable by the second. We'd better take it downstairs to Nurse Joy, Brock said quickly.

Ethan nodded. This was beyond anything he could handle. He carried Jigglypuff down to the Pokemon Center's front desk, with the others hurrying beside him.

Misty rang the desk bell. A little later, Nurse Joy came out from the back, yawning.

Before Ethan could explain, she spotted Jigglypuff's condition. Her sleepiness vanished, and she rushed over. What happened to it?

Misty quickly told her the whole story. Joy nodded, called for an assistant Pokemon, and had Jigglypuff placed on a stretcher and taken into the treatment room.

The red light over its door came on. Only then did the group feel they could breathe. They sat on the benches nearby.

Ash looked at Ethan. Why did you have a stone in your backpack?

It wasn't an ordinary stone, Ethan said with a rueful smile. It was an Everstone.

An Everstone?

I know what that is! Max pushed up his glasses. Unlike Evolution stones, the books say a Pokemon holding an Everstone can't evolve. It stays in its present form!

Huh? Ash stared. So if Jigglypuff swallowed one, does that mean it can never evolve?

Would that be so terrible? Misty asked. Your Pikachu keeps refusing to evolve into Raichu. Besides, I think Jigglypuff is cuter than Wigglytuff.

While they waited for Jigglypuff to be treated, May returned.

She entered the Pokemon Center and saw them gathered around the benches by the desk. The mood seemed subdued. Puzzled, she came over. What are you all doing here so late? Haven't you gone to bed?

Only then did they notice her arrival. It was almost eleven at night.

It's a long story, Misty said, and gave her an outline of what had happened. Remembering the stone bouncing off the wall straight into Jigglypuff's mouth almost made her laugh.

What? May stared. Is Jigglypuff all right?

I think it will be, Ethan said. Then he asked, How did your practice go? You're back late.

May made an OK sign, brimming with confidence. No problem! Just watch me tomorrow!

The others exchanged glances. What had she practiced in those few hours to become so sure of herself?

They were about to ask when the red light went out. The treatment-room door opened, and Nurse Joy emerged. Everyone hurried to their feet.

Nurse Joy, how is Jigglypuff?

She looked apologetic. It isn't in immediate danger, but the Everstone is in its stomach. I've tried several ways to remove it without success.

They hadn't expected even Nurse Joy to run into a problem she couldn't solve. What can we do now?

There are two options. A specialist hospital could break up the stone inside its stomach and remove the pieces, but that requires surgery there. Or we can wait for Jigglypuff to bring it up or pass it naturally. With a stone that large, that could take a long time.

Everyone looked at Ethan, waiting to hear his choice. He didn't hesitate. Is there a hospital here that can do the procedure?

Joy shook her head. This city has hospitals for people. For that kind of Pokemon surgery, you'd need to go to a larger city.

Then we don't have much choice. Ethan sighed. As long as Jigglypuff isn't in immediate danger, we'll arrange treatment when we reach a larger city. Until then, we can only hope it gets the stone out on its own.

...

They rested at the Pokemon Center overnight. The next morning, Ethan, Ash, Brock, and Max finished breakfast in the dining room, but Misty and May still hadn't come downstairs.

Today was Contest day. Everyone knew girls sometimes took a while getting ready, whatever their age, but this was taking far too long.

Ethan called Misty. Only then did he learn that May had been unable to sleep for half the night. Misty had stayed up talking with her, and both had finally gone to bed late. They were still asleep. Without Ethan's call, they might have slept even longer.

He ended the call and checked the time: 9:07 a.m.

The Contest itself began at one in the afternoon, but entrants had to arrive at the Contest hall before ten to confirm their registration and draw a number. Anyone late would be barred from competing.

Ethan told the others. Their answer was a chorus of helpless sighs. After all May's hard work, missing the entry deadline would be unbearable.

Fortunately, only a few minutes passed before May and Misty came flying down the stairs.

Beside Ethan and the others, who were ready to go, the girls looked rushed. Their clothes were wrinkled from being put on in a hurry, and water still glistened on their faces from a quick wash. Even so, they were naturally beautiful.

We're going to be late! We're going to be late! May didn't spare the group a glance or stop for breakfast. She ran straight out of the Pokemon Center.

Misty hurried after her, and the sight made Ash and the others nervous enough to follow. In a moment Ethan was alone, watching their figures disappear. He shook his head.

He had already decided that if May came down too late, he would have Dragonite fly her ahead to the Contest hall so she could confirm her place. But they had rushed away before he could say a word.

Traveling with Ash had evidently rubbed off on the whole group. Now they all charged into things in the same frantic way...
