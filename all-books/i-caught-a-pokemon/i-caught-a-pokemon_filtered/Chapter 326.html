Chapter 326: Nothing but Fury

Gyarados swept its tail sideways, sending several International Police officers flying.

"Gyarados, wake up!" Ethan shouted. "Don't attack our own people!"

No matter how he called, Gyarados gave no sign of hearing him. Its huge tail swung again and nearly knocked Ethan over. Sabrina caught him with her psychic power and pulled him to her side before he could be hurt.

"It's no use..." Blue light flashed, and Gengar emerged from its Poke Ball. It had recovered from its earlier pain. "I saw what happened from inside the ball. That wave has taken control of Gyarados. It can't hear anything you say!"

Gengar was right. Ethan knew the wave was the cause. He turned to the transmitter and told Sabrina, "Destroy it!"

Without hesitation, she waved a hand. Her power swept forward, slicing the transmitter in two as though with an invisible blade. It was ruined.

Yet Gyarados remained in a frenzy. It showed no sign of regaining its senses.

"Ha ha ha!" Sebastian laughed. "Don't waste your strength. It's too late! Endless rage has filled your Gyarados's mind. It will destroy everything it sees. Tremble before its power!"

Ethan took out Gyarados's Poke Ball and tried to recall it. A red beam struck its body but had no effect.

What? It didn't work?

He looked down at the ball. It was still linked to Gyarados; their bond had not been severed. But the Pokemon had become so powerful that, if it resisted, the Poke Ball could not draw it back.

Even the Poke Ball can't stop it...

Gyarados opened its jaws. A great mass of energy gathered in its mouth. It looked ready to use Hyper Beam.

Oh, no! Ethan knew a Hyper Beam unleashed indoors could destroy the entire research building. Before he could act, the energy reached its peak.

Roar!

A thick beam burst from Gyarados's mouth and punched through the laboratory wall. It turned its head, sweeping the attack across the room. Tables, chairs, equipment, and masonry in its path melted under the heat. Windows shattered and walls collapsed.

Everyone fled in confusion to avoid the beam. When it ended, they looked up at the wreckage of the laboratory in horror.

That was far too powerful...

Looker stared. Even Ethan was shocked. This was still Hyper Beam, but its force had increased by more than a single level.

More astonishingly, Gyarados could still move freely after using it, ignoring the move's usual aftereffect. Was this the true power of Project R?

Much of the building had been destroyed. Its damaged structure shed broken pieces of wall from above.

"This place is going to collapse!" Looker shouted to everyone. "Get out, now!"

He himself ran forward, still hoping to arrest Sebastian. Before he could reach the stairs, a support beam fell across his path.

Sebastian gave Looker a meaningful smile. He glanced once more at Gyarados, nodded in satisfaction, opened a hidden door, and disappeared through it.

"Don't run!"

Looker tried to pursue him, but another beam fell and fire sprang up ahead. He had no choice but to turn back to Ethan.

Ethan looked at Gyarados with mixed feelings. It was still tearing the room apart. With a sigh, he ran out of the building alongside Sabrina and Looker.

The collapse had spread into the long corridor. They had covered barely a dozen meters before pieces of ceiling and wall began falling around them. Sabrina held the debris back with her psychic power so they could pass safely.

"Whew!"

They had only just emerged when a tremendous crash sounded behind them. The imposing research building had become a heap of broken walls.

Gyarados rose above the wreckage, still pouring out energy and venting its fury without tiring. To keep the officers out of its path, Looker ordered them to scatter.

"Spread out! Search for the Rockets who escaped. There must be a secret passage nearby. Block them before they get too far!"

Footsteps approached. Will and Karen had arrived with another team of International Police officers to reinforce them. Even from a distance, they had seen the extraordinary Gyarados laying waste to everything around it.

"Incredible..." Karen looked up at it in surprise. "That Gyarados is incredibly strong. Is it Team Rocket's final weapon?"

"No..." Looker glanced at Ethan and smiled bitterly. "It's actually EthMr. Player's Pokemon. Team Rocket took control of it, and now it's out of control."

"Your Gyarados?" Will looked at Ethan and shook his head with a show of composure. "Whatever the cause, we can't let it keep wrecking the place. We just need to stop it, right?"

He released his Exeggutor. "Sorry, but your Pokemon may get hurt!"

Will gave the order. "Exeggutor, use Confusion!"

He expected a simple fight. A frenzied Gyarados would attack in predictable ways and could no longer think clearly.

But Exeggutor's psychic force struck Gyarados and seemed to vanish into it. Gyarados showed no pain or reaction until it noticed the attacker. Then one long-range Hydro Pump knocked Exeggutor out immediately.

What?!

Will stared at his fallen Pokemon. "You're joking! Exeggutor is a Grass- and Psychic-type. It resists Water-type attacks. How could one hit take it down so easily?"

Looker gave him a rueful smile. "Now you understand why we're all standing here with no idea what to do..."
