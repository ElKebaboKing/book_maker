Chapter 450: Final Victory

This attack was clearly far more effective than the last.

When the electrical light vanished, wisps of blue smoke rose from the red Gyarados's body. Electricity was racing through it while the moisture outside its scales evaporated, combining to create the visible smoke.

Trinity brightened at the sight.

The evidence proved that Electric-type moves truly held a major advantage against the red Gyarados!

No matter how unusual it looked, the red Gyarados was still a Water- and Flying-type Pokemon!

Now that Trinity knew it could be countered, several more attacks like that one would give her a real chance to win!

But before she could celebrate for long, the red Gyarados threw back its head and released a roar that shook heaven and earth!

"ROAR!"

The sound was deafening, making everyone instinctively cover their ears.

The attack had clearly enraged the red Gyarados. Its mind was no longer calm, and it became incomparably violent. Its entire presence changed, finally displaying the madness associated with the name Gyarados.

It fixed its prey with an unwavering stare and surged forward. Its enormous body smashed straight through the rubble, and it opened its jaws as it swam ferociously toward Chinchou!

Chinchou nearly lost its soul from fright. It immediately turned and fled.

Seeing it hide among another pile of rocks and attempt the same trick again, Ethan had no intention of giving it another opportunity.

Nice idea. You got away with that move once, but you think I'll let you do it again?

"Gyarados, stop chasing it. Use Twister!"

At its Trainer's voice, the red Gyarados, which had only just entered a violent frenzy, stopped immediately. The sight left the audience dumbfounded!

Its obedience was absolute!

Forget this terrifying red Gyaradoseven among ordinary Gyarados owned by other Trainers, how many could stop instantly after falling into a rage?

The red Gyarados raised its enormous head. Its anger receded as reason once again took control of its mind. Following Ethan's command, it used Twister in the water.

A gale instantly swept across the pool and formed a huge tornado. Then the water below began spinning with it, faster and faster, violently shaking the entire surface!

An enormous whirlpool appeared within the arena. It covered the entire battlefield and began drawing in seawater with tremendous force.

Anyone could see that Ethan intended to recreate the technique Ash's Kingler had used in an earlier match!

Kingler had used that tactic to create four small whirlpools and defeat Misty's Poliwhirl. Ash had still been eliminated by Misty in the end, but the memorable scene had remained a subject of excited discussion for quite some time.

Compared with Kingler's four small whirlpools, however, the single whirlpool created by the red Gyarados was clearly much larger and far more dangerous!

Trinity naturally remembered the earlier battle well. The moment she saw Ethan use this tactic, her expression changed dramatically!

So the red Gyarados had not sacrificed its moves after all!

With no time to think, she hurriedly shouted, "Chinchou! Find somewhere to hide!"

Chinchou had naturally seen the whirlpool. Even without Trinity's command, it desperately swam in the opposite direction!

Unfortunately, the red Gyarados had already destroyed every pillar on that half of the battlefield. The remaining piles of rubble were submerged in the pool. Even hiding behind them offered Chinchou no guarantee of safety.

The whirlpool's enormous range and suction soon caught Chinchou.

The red Gyarados was also inside the current, its eyes fixed unblinkingly upon its prey. Relying on its immense body, it completely ignored the whirlpool's pull and swam directly across the current toward Chinchou.

As the red Gyarados drew closer, Chinchou thrashed even harder in an attempt to escape. But it was simply too weak to resist the whirlpool's suction. After struggling a dozen times, the rushing water dragged it in.

The whirlpool even affected the command areas on both sides, repeatedly spraying water across Trinity's face.

The distance between the two Pokemon continued shrinking. Faced with such danger, Trinity gritted her teeth and made one final decision.

"Chinchou, use Discharge!"

Within the racing current, Chinchou stopped struggling and allowed the water to carry it toward the whirlpool's center. The instant it passed beside the red Gyarados, every trace of electricity within its body erupted and spread violently in all directions!

Crackle! Crackle!

Light flashed without pause, accompanied by the constant roar of electricity!

The red Gyarados bellowed. It neither dodged nor retreated, but simply opened its jaws and bit toward Chinchou.

The instant electricity struck the red Gyarados, its blood-filled jaws swallowed Chinchou whole!

Trinity closed her eyes in despair.

She had no idea whether Chinchou's final Discharge could defeat the red Gyarados. But once her Pokemon entered those enormous jaws, its prospects were unquestionably grim!

Electric light exploded across the red Gyarados. Once Chinchou disappeared into its mouth, however, the light quickly vanished from view. Even so, the spectators could still see Chinchou struggling inside through the gaps between Gyarados's teeth!

After what had happened with Poliwrath, everyone knew that being swallowed might look terrifying, but Chinchou would not truly be eaten. The knowledge reassured them somewhat.

Sure enough, just as the audience expected, Chinchou's struggles gradually weakened. After only a few breaths, it became completely still.

I've lost...

Trinity collapsed onto the ground, unable even to cry.

She had imagined such a wonderful outcome, but in the end, she failed to defeat this terrifying monster.

It's far too strong...

Her mind returned to Ethan's words before the match. Only now did she finally understand what he had meant.

No wonder he asked me not to hold him responsible. He knew this would happen all along...

But even if Trinity wanted to blame him and seek revenge, how could she ever defeat such a monster, no matter how much she trained her Pokemon?

At Ethan's signal, the red Gyarados opened its mouth and spat the battered Chinchou into the water.

Before Ethan could say anything to Trinity, countless ceremonial cannons fired around the arena, followed by the announcer's ringing voice!

"We have a new champion! Just as we predicted before the tournament, Ethan has seized victory in this year's Whirl Cup with overwhelming strength! Now, let us give him our loudest cheers and applause!"
