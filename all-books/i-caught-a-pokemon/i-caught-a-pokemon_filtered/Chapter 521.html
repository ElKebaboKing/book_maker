Chapter 521: Sandstorm and Rain Dance

Flygon resembled a dragonfly, just as its name suggested. It was no insect, though, but a true Dragon-type Pokemon.

Most of its body was pale green. Two green antennae rose from its head, and two green wings with red edges spread from its back. Its long tail had eight sections, alternating between pale and darker green.

Flygon normally lived in hot, dry regions. Red, dome-shaped structures covered its eyes and protected them from sand.

It was not native to Kanto or Johto, and Ethan had seldom encountered one. He took out his Pokedex and pointed it at Flygon.

"Beep. Flygon, the Mystic Pokemon. A Ground- and Dragon-type. Flygon beats its powerful wings to stir up desert sand and conceal itself, so it is usually surrounded by a sandstorm in flight and rarely appears before people. It sometimes has Krookodile hide in the storm and shares the prey it catches with them. The sound of its wings in a sandstorm is said to resemble a woman's beautiful singing, earning it the name 'desert spirit.' Prey drawn in by the sound lose their senses, are caught in the storm, and become Flygon's food. People have also gone missing in the desert this way."

Ethan put the Pokedex away and rubbed his chin as he watched the great dragonfly-like Pokemon hovering above the ground.

That description made it sound like a remarkably sophisticated hunter. It worked with other Pokemon and even used sound to lure its prey into a trap!

Perhaps the resemblance to a dragonfly went beyond its appearance. Its habits had an insect's ruthless edge. Insects could be far crueler than humans or other animals.

There was no referee for this informal match. As soon as both Pokemon were ready, the battle began.

Flygon beat its thin wings rapidly and lifted its nearly two-meter body into the air. Dragonite refused to be outdone. It flapped its little wings and rose opposite Flygon.

Without waiting for their Trainers' commands, the two Pokemon met and grappled in the air.

The fights with Sunny and Santos had been little more than appetizers, chances to warm up. The battle with Monica was the main course. Both its intensity and the strength of the Pokemon were on another level.

"Flygon, use Supersonic!"

"Dragonite, use Safeguard!"

Supersonic sent out waves that could confuse its target. Ethan had Dragonite use Safeguard to protect itself from that sort of condition.

Visible waves of sound swept over Dragonite, but under Safeguard's protection it remained unaffected.

Seeing that Supersonic would not work, Monica gave another order. "Flygon, use Sandstorm!"

Yellow sand blew in from nowhere and covered the entire area behind the Pokemon Center. It obscured everyone's sight and swallowed both Pokemon.

Monica wanted a battlefield favorable to Flygon, where it could defeat Dragonite from within the sand.

Ethan was untroubled. "Dragonite, use Rain Dance."

Dragonite roared at the sky. Clouds suddenly covered what had been a clear day. There was no thunder or lightning; large drops of rain simply began to fall.

If Monica wanted to change the weather, he could answer in kind. Rain mixed with the sand and fell from the sky as mud.

Ethan had anticipated it and released Gardevoir. She used her psychic power to form an invisible barrier above Ethan and Erika, keeping every bit of mud off them.

The Week Siblings and the children watching were not so lucky.

Monica stayed where she stood to direct Flygon. Mud soon speckled her face and clothes. Her younger brothers were so concerned about Flygon that they kept their attention fixed on the fight.

The children ran in alarm to an awning nearby and watched from a distance, but they had moved too slowly to avoid the mud completely.

They had only seen battles this fierce on television. Now that one was happening before them, they did not want to miss a moment, however unpleasant the weather became.

The two Pokemon in the middle of the battlefield suffered most.

Mud surrounded them and badly obscured their vision. Even Flygon's specially protected eyes were uncomfortable when mud got into them.

Worse for Monica, Flygon's wings were large and thin. Once they were coated in mud, it could no longer stay aloft and had to land.

The packed earth below had softened under the muddy rain and become difficult to cross.

Fortunately, the downpour of mud lasted only a short while before it ended.

The sky cleared again. Apart from the mess left behind, there was little sign that the weather had changed at all.

Pokemon power had its limits. They could alter the weather for a time, but not change nature forever.

Dragonite was coated in mud too, and its flight was growing labored. Ethan called, "Use Dragon Rush!"

Dragonite climbed sharply, shedding some of the still-wet mud. After circling a few times, it dove at Flygon with tremendous speed.

"Flygon, get into the air!" Monica shouted.

A thick layer of mud weighed down Flygon's wings. It struggled to shake them clean, but as it finally tried to take flight, Dragonite struck it with all the force of its dive.

Bang!

Flygon was hurled a long way across the field. The impact also shook most of the mud from Dragonite's body.

With the attack successful, Ethan ordered Dragonite back into the air.

He had no wish to keep battling a Ground-type Pokemon this strong on ground that was difficult to move across.
