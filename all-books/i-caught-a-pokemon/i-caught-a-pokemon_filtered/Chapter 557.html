Chapter 557: How Their Friends Fared

When Ash and the others returned to the lobby, they were all smiling. To Ethan's surprise, Macey had come back with them.

Huh? What was going on?

Ethan quickly pulled Misty aside and asked in a low voice. Only then did he learn that Ash and Macey had met before entering the arena that morning, after the two groups had parted ways.

Macey had approached him to pick a fight. She wanted to find out whether Ash was worthy of being her opponent.

Just then, Team Rocket's trio had attacked. They happened to capture Ash's Pikachu and Macey's Vulpix, but Ash used his other Pokemon to defeat them and rescue both captives.

That had won Macey's heart on the spot.

Even after Ash beat her in their match, she felt no resentment. She admired him more than ever, which was why she now kept following him around.

"I see." Ethan could not help laughing. "So Ash is finally having some luck in love!"

When Ethan and Misty rejoined the others, he greeted Macey politely. Only then did she learn that Ash and Ethan were good friends.

With her standing right there, Ethan could hardly congratulate Ash. He made a few minutes of casual conversation instead.

Eventually Macey's attention became too much for them. Misty coaxed her away on the pretext of going to the restroom, giving Ethan and Ash a chance to breathe.

"She really is..." Ethan shook his head with a wry smile, leaving the thought unfinished. Then he teased Ash. "Only you could have girls falling for you wherever you go."

Ash rubbed his hair awkwardly, his face turning a little red.

He wanted to retort, Doesn't Misty act the same way around you? But with Ethan and Misty to answer to, and Erika standing nearby, he did not dare say it aloud.

Seeing him blush, Ethan pressed on. "Why not make it official and ask her to be your girlfriend?"

"What are you talking about...?" Ash's face grew redder. "I... I haven't thought about that..."

Ethan shook his head, half amused and half exasperated. "You're such a kid."

"That's enough." Erika saw how embarrassed Ash was and caught Ethan by the arm. "Stop teasing him. He is still a kid."

At Erika's intervention, Ethan dropped the subject and talked to Ash about the day's matches instead.

Both of them were finished competing for the day, and both had won their first match. It was a happy result for them, but every winner meant a loser. The defeated Trainers who returned to the lobby all looked weighed down by worry.

They were already on the brink of elimination. One more loss would end their Silver Conference run.

"Your match was close," Ethan told Ash. "You nearly lost to Macey. Good thing Squirtle held up under the pressure and beat Electabuzz despite the type disadvantage."

"It was tense," Brock agreed with a nod. "Watching from the stands, I was worried for Ash the whole time!"

Just then, other competitors Ethan knew began returning.

Their expressions revealed how their matches had gone. Gary was among the first through the door. As composed as ever, he wore a small backpack, spared no one a glance, and walked straight to the elevator with his chest out.

He had plainly won, and won easily.

Jimmy and Marina entered after him, talking and laughing together. Their matches had evidently gone well too.

Casey and Travis were scheduled to compete tomorrow. Neither had battled today, so their results would have to wait.

Watching their two group opponents battle today had given them a chance to prepare more thoroughly and tailor their teams for tomorrow.

That might not be entirely fair to the other competitors, but it was how the tournament schedule worked.

Highlights from the day's battles spread quickly online. Some media outlets even put together a top-five list.

Surprisingly, not one of Ethan's battles made the cut. His matches had been so one-sided that there had been little suspense to make them exciting.

Interest in the preliminaries remained high. Some spectators enjoyed the matches so much that they watched the replays again and again.

For now, none of that mattered much to Ethan and the other competitors.

After a night's rest, the second day of the preliminaries arrived. Ethan had no match scheduled.

From the stands, he watched Kimura battle the other Trainer in their group, a young woman named Hui. The contest was fierce, but at the end Kimura's Magmar lost to Hui's Feraligatr. With two defeats, Kimura was out of the Silver Conference. He would never get the chance to avenge his loss to Ethan.

Hui had watched the first day's matches and seen that Magmar was Kimura's strongest Pokemon. She had prepared a counter for it, and that preparation won her the decisive exchange.

With her win over Kimura, Hui had three points, the same as Ethan.

Tomorrow they would battle for the place in the last sixteen. The winner would advance; the loser would be eliminated.

After leaving the arena, Ethan met his friends again and heard how their matches had gone.

Vincent, who shared Ash's group, had made an entrance intended to look cool in his match with Macey, only to take a spectacular tumble. He then lost the battle too.

That left Ash and Macey on three points each. If Ash beat Vincent tomorrow, he would advance outright.

If he lost, though, he would have to face Macey again in a tiebreakera battle tangled up in their new affection and rivalry.

Travis had also lost his match today. He still had one more chance tomorrow, so advancing was not out of reach.

Casey had better luck. Her opponent today was not especially strong, and she won without much trouble.

With that, the second day of the preliminaries ended. Everyone awaited tomorrow's final matches.
