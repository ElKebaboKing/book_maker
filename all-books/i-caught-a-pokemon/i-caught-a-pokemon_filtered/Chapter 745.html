Chapter 745: Partings, an Investigation, and Expectations

The next morning, Misty pestered Ethan awake. When he opened his bleary eyes, she was already washed and dressed, kneeling beside him and tickling his cheek with a strand of her hair. It itched, though pleasantly.

Ethan, you really can't hold your liquor, she said, covering a laugh. You only had a few cups last night, and you slept this late!

You have no idea, Misty.

It was seven twenty. He had slept a little later than usual, but sake was hardly the reason. He had spent half the night turning over what had happened with Bea and had barely slept until dawn. He could not tell Misty that.

Bea's and Misty's bedding had already been folded and put away. Misty was here, but Bea was not. The rain had stopped sometime in the night. A faint smell of food drifted in from outside; breakfast must be ready.

Ethan washed and dressed, then followed Misty into the courtyard. A middle-aged man in work clothes was on the roof, repairing something under the elderly innkeeper's direction.

Bea was practicing martial arts in the courtyard. Each punch and kick seemed to cut audibly through the air. A sheen of sweat covered her skin. She must have been training for some time.

Such dedication. Ethan wished he had her persistence, but he was at least half lazy. Given the chance, he would rather spend a little longer in bed.

Misty was still looking up at the repairman. You don't know this yet, but it rained hard last night. The roof leaked over our room. Bea and I nearly got soaked!

Really? Ethan did his best to look unaware. I slept so deeply I didn't even hear the rain.

Hearing his voice, Bea glanced toward him. Her punch stopped. After two or three seconds, she resumed training as though nothing had happened.

The greeting Ethan had been about to give caught in his throat. Guilty as he felt, he noticed that pause at once. Had she realized what happened? Of course it was possible. Bea had not been drinking. How could she fail to notice him touching her in the night? But if she had noticed, why had she not stopped or scolded him? Perhaps he was imagining things. Then again, why had she paused just now?

Misty's gaze moved suspiciously between them. Something about the atmosphere felt wrong. Had anything happened while she slept? Surely not. She had been beside them and heard nothing, and she herself had woken Ethan that morning.

They were probably embarrassed, she decided. It was one thing to sleep near each other in the dark and another to open their eyes and see each other's faces.

...

After a cold shower, Bea came into the sitting room. Ethan and Misty were already waiting at the table. They began breakfast once she sat down.

As on the previous night, the food was delicious. The dishes looked simple, but simple ingredients tested a cook's skill all the more.

Neither Ethan nor Bea spoke. Misty finally broke the silence. Bea, where will you go next?

After a night's rest, it was time to travel again. Trainers rarely stayed in one place for long; another unknown adventure always waited. Ethan and Misty had one last Gym to challenge. Bea, now that she knew this island's exercises offered her little, would look elsewhere for training.

I think I'll leave Hoenn, Bea said without looking up. I've challenged all the well-known Fighting-type Trainers here. It's time to visit another region.

Ethan felt relief. He could no longer face her with the ease he had before. Some time apart might let the embarrassment fade. By their next meeting, perhaps they could both forget it.

So soon? Misty sounded disappointed. After the evening together, she felt closer to Bea. I thought you'd at least stay for the Ever Grande Conference. Now you won't get to watch Ethan win another title!

Hey, Ethan cut in. Don't talk as though I've already been named Champion. Hoenn isn't like Kanto or Johto. There are plenty of strong Trainers here. I can promise I'll do my best not to lose through carelessness, but winning won't be easy.

After breakfast, they packed and left the inn for the harbor. The evacuated residents had begun returning overnight. The town looked far livelier than it had when Ethan and Misty arrived; its streets were no longer empty.

They watched Bea board a ferry heading in another direction. Ethan and Misty then boarded their own ship. As the horn sounded and the ferry eased away from the pier, they stood by the rail, each lost in thought.

At that same moment, uninvited visitors reached the sea southeast of Mossdeep Island.

A helicopter bearing the TV Mauville logo flew toward the place where Primal Groudon and Primal Kyogre had fought. In the back sat the reporting duo Ethan had met at the park in Mauville City. They craned their necks to see the water below.

Ty, the cameraman, searched for a while before turning to Gabby. Are you sure this is the place? Weren't there several islands? I can only see a few shoals.

You idiot. Those shoals were the islands, Gabby said. Something flattened them.

The helicopter landed on a relatively even stretch of exposed ground, and they climbed out. Ty checked a satellite map but could not match the place around him to the islands marked there.

Remind me why we came all this way? he asked.

Were you listening at all? Gabby sighed. If we want to get back into the studio and move up at the station, we need a big story and the truth behind it. Come on, investigate. It took me a lot of work to get someone to fly us here. I don't intend to leave empty-handed.

As reporters, they had heard more than most people. The extreme weather that had affected much of Hoenn had spread from this area. They were certain the official explanationunusual air pressure and tectonic movementwas not the truth. Someone or some Pokemon had fought here and affected the entire region.

What Pokemon could wield enough power to do that? The obvious answers were Groudon and Kyogre.

Short on time, they began searching for evidence. They found fragments of a ship, uniforms lost by Team Aqua and Team Magma members, marks left by the battle, and even an International Police armband.

The clues pointed to a hidden fight involving both criminal organizations, with the International Police involved as well. Gabby grew excited. Publishing the truth would cause a sensation. If the police had concealed it, there must be an extraordinary story to uncover. The reporter who broke it would surely gain recognition at the station, a promotion, and better pay.

They photographed the scene, collected and secured the evidence, and searched on for something more decisive. What they had found showed that a battle had occurred and that Team Aqua and Team Magma were connected to it. It did not prove Groudon and Kyogre had been present or caused the weather.

They worked slowly through the site, overlooking no corner. For a long time they found nothing else. At the place where Ethan had stood, however, they noticed footprints and a patch of ground that remained unusually intact, as though some power had protected it.

After studying the traces, Gabby's eyes brightened. Someone was directing Pokemon against Team Aqua and Team Magma. Maybeand this is a guessthis person even fought Groudon and Kyogre!

That's hard to believe, Ty said. Who could possibly be strong enough to battle two Legendary Pokemon? One of the Elite Four?

The Elite Four... Gabby considered it. She knew little about battles, but she knew they were among Hoenn's strongest Trainers. Perhaps. Based on the route and what we've heard, Drake seems the likeliest to have come here.

Ty thought she might be right, yet something troubled him. He looked toward the remains of the island erased by Precipice Blades and Origin Pulse. Comparing them with the earlier satellite map, he could picture the large island that had stood there. Now it had vanished entirely.

What kind of power could wipe an island away? Could even one of the Elite Four stop a battle on that scale?

He said nothing. They bent to their search again, hoping for conclusive evidence, but found none. They had come too late. After more than a day of wind and rain, seawater had washed away most of the traces.

They had arrived full of expectation. Hours later, they left without the proof they needed.

...

After nearly a full day at sea, Ethan and Misty's ferry from Muscle Island docked at Lilycove City. It was much slower than flying, but comfortable and leisurely. They were in no hurry and had enjoyed the journey.

Ethan had barely disembarked when Kennedy called. The Meteorite Key Stone Ethan had found at Sky Pillar was ready.

Devon Corporation had worked quickly. Kennedy had gathered the necessary specialists before the stone even reached the company's headquarters. As soon as it arrived, they took it to the workshop and worked through the night. The job was finished in little more than a day.

Ethan looked forward to seeing it. If the stories were true and the Meteorite Key Stone held energy from space, might his Pokemon grow stronger through Mega Evolution than other Trainers' Pokemon did?

That was only a guess. He did not yet know whether the stone was truly special, whether it differed at all from an ordinary Key Stone, or whether it would even affect ordinary Pokemon. He would find out when he had it in his hands.
