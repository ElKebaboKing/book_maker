Chapter 359: Entei Appears!

After the match ended, everyone recalled their Pokemon and left the battlefield together.

On the way back to the Pokemon Center, they naturally praised one another's performances. Erika and Lisa, who had never seen Misty's Psyduck before, offered especially high praise.

Compared with an ordinary Psyduck, Misty's was unquestionably more unusual and memorable.

When Misty laughingly told them that her Psyduck was a Water-type Pokemon that could not swim at all, both women marveled even more.

"A Psyduck that can't swim!?" Lisa was astonished. "Is yours the only one, or can none of them swim? Wait... that can't be right. I've seen plenty of evolved Golduck, and they're all excellent swimmers!"

"Well..." Misty scratched her cheek awkwardly. "I think... my Psyduck is simply strange..."

As Ethan listened to their conversation, he reflected on the battle. Because he had not issued any commands, he had been free to observe the entire field and evaluate everyone's performance.

Ash and Ethan were not the only ones continually improving. After traveling for so long, Misty and Brock had grown tremendously as well.

Each of them had shed their early inexperience and was striding toward their dream.

It seems I need to work even harder. I can't relax for a moment, or before I realize it, this group will overtake me!

They returned to the Pokemon Center's dining room and ordered several drinks. Before their seats had even grown warm, the sound of vehicles approaching came from outside once more.

Ethan and the others went out with Officer Jenny, Nurse Joy, and Schuyler. A small bus approached from downhill under the escort of a police car.

When the bus stopped, an extremely familiar man stepped out first.

The familiar long white coat, familiar short silver-gray hair, and familiar face could only belong to Professor Oak.

"Professor Oak!?"

Ash and his friends cried out in surprise. They had never expected the expert to be Professor Oak.

They had assumed Professor Elm from New Bark Town or some other specialist would come to investigate. None of them imagined Professor Oak himself would arrive.

On further thought, however, the choice was hardly surprising. Few people in the entire world held greater authority, and Pallet Town was not terribly far from Johto. Inviting him made perfect sense.

Schuyler addressed this giant of the academic world with immense respect.

"Professor Oak, we've been awaiting your arrival!"

Professor Oak nodded to Schuyler, then turned toward Ethan and the others.

He was already surprised to find Ash in Greenfield by coincidence. The strange pairing of Ethan and Erika made him raise his eyebrows even farther.

"So all of you are here too. Good grief. If I'd known, we could have left the matter directly to you, and I wouldn't have needed to travel all this way!"

"Huh? Why would you leave it to us?"

Before Ash could understand Professor Oak's meaning, Pikachu seemed to notice something. It cried out and ran straight toward the bus.

"Pikachu, what is it?"

Before Ash could wonder any further, his mother, Delia, stepped off the bus behind Professor Oak.

"Huh? Mom? Why did you come here with Professor Oak?"

Ash continued making a fuss, but Ethan had long grown accustomed to seeing Professor Oak and Delia together. If the two ever appeared separately, that would truly surprise him.

Delia crouched and stroked Pikachu, then walked over to Ash. Mother and son exchanged warm expressions of concern, and a nearby reporter broadcast the touching scene through her camera.

After the brief greetings, Officer Jenny, eager to solve the case, drove Professor Oak directly to the crystal formation for a close investigation.

The others led Delia to a nearby sunshade. There, they learned why she had traveled to Greenfield with Professor Oak.

Delia took a sip of juice before explaining, "Professor Hale often visited Professor Oak to study in the past. That's when we met."

She took a photograph from her bag and placed it on the table.

Ethan leaned forward for a look. Delia and Ash stood to Professor Oak's right in the picture. Ash held up a victory sign and smiled brightly.

A family of three stood on Professor Oak's other side. The parents appeared middle-aged, while their daughter looked several years younger than Ash.

Delia pointed at the photograph. "We took this in Pallet Town several years ago with Professor Oak and Professor Hale's family. They're all wonderful people."

Despite her words, Ash could not remember them no matter how hard he tried. He had been too young at the time.

Delia continued, "I saw the report on the news last night. Once I learned that Professor Hale had disappeared too, I became terribly worried about his daughter. That's why I came here with Professor Oak to see what was happening."

"I see."

At that moment, Pikachu suddenly sensed something and stared into the distance with alarm. Even Gengar emerged from Ethan's shadow and moved protectively in front of him.

Their tense vigilance made it seem as though a powerful enemy was approaching.

"Huh? What's wrong with you two?"

Drawn by the Pokemon's behavior, everyone, including Ethan, hurriedly turned to look. A few breaths later, a figure suddenly descended from the sky. A great quadrupedal Pokemon appeared before them.

Ethan recognized it at a glance.

It was Entei!

Erika gasped beside him and covered her mouth in surprise. She had clearly recognized it as well.

Like the Suicune they had encountered before, Entei was one of Ho-Oh's guardians.

It stood before them in a majestic pose, radiating an imposing presence. Everyone felt immense pressure when its gaze fell upon them.

Entei had orange-red fur and powerful limbs encircled by iron bands. Red, six-pointed structures framed its cheeks, while a golden crest resembling a three-leaf clover sat upon its forehead.

Many people said it resembled a lion, but to Ethan, it looked more like an enormous Tibetan Mastiff.
