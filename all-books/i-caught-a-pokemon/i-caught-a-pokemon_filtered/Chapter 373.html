Chapter 373: Dance of the Birds

As the three Legendary birds continued circling higher, the clouds grew thicker. Lightning flickered within them from time to time, but unlike before, no bolts fell at random. The electricity remained contained inside the clouds. The three birds were clearly accumulating energy and preparing something terrifying.

Gengar turned toward Ethan and urged, "We should get out of here too! It'll be dangerous if we stay any longer!"

Gengar had witnessed the technique's creation and knew how terrifying the approaching power would be. It also understood that aside from a very small number of Pokemon, nothing could endure the coming torrent of energy.

Ethan nodded.

Tri-Bird Assault possessed tremendous power and an enormous range. Ethan was not a Pokemon. If he remained within the attack area, it might reduce him to ash without leaving even a trace. To avoid being caught in it, he needed to travel as far away as possible.

After recalling Gardevoir, Ethan mounted Charizard and flew into the distance with Gengar.

Before leaving, however, he glanced back at the crystal tower.

He hoped Ash and the others had already reached the lowest level. If they were still wasting time higher in the tower, they would certainly be caught in the attack...

Then Ethan reconsidered. Even if they were caught in it, they would probably be fine.

After all, the physical abilities of a superhuman from Pallet Town could not be judged by common sense. Ash could throw an entire tree, lift a Tyranitar, and push a Wailmer. He could endure electrocution and fire without lasting harm. No matter what happened to him, nothing short of the plot itself could kill him.

Even Misty and Brock appeared to have become extraordinary after traveling with Ash for so long.

There was no need to worry about them.

On the battlefield, the oppressive tension made the three Legendary beasts increasingly restless. Acting on instinct, all three launched moves toward the sky in a desperate attempt to stop the Legendary birds.

Fire, lightning, and water shot from the beasts and flew straight into the clouds.

But the powerful attacks vanished into the dark mass like stones dropped into the ocean. They penetrated only a few layers of cloud before disappearing completely, and the holes they created were quickly filled by the surrounding clouds.

The attacks had no visible effect and barely caused a ripple.

In terms of individual strength, the Legendary birds and Legendary beasts were essentially equals. The birds were not vastly stronger. If they could unleash power on this scale, then the beasts should have been capable of producing equal force to oppose them.

Unfortunately, the three Legendary beasts possessed no coordination and could not combine their strength into an attack this powerful. More significantly, the Legendary birds could fly into the clouds and draw upon the force of the weather itself, causing dramatic climatic changes that maximized their energy.

Rain borrowed strength from the wind, and the wind magnified the rain.

Pokemon confined to the ground found it far harder to accomplish the same feat. Without the ability to draw upon the power of the sky, their attacks naturally fell far short.

Charizard raced over the crystal forest, carrying Ethan toward the Pokemon Center.

High above him, beyond the clouds, Moltres, Zapdos, and Articuno were wrapped in streams of vapor as though performing a strange dance of the birds. With every beat of their wings, energy visibly drifted from their bodies and dispersed into the clouds.

The lightning grew smaller, but it gathered steadily toward the three Legendary birds.

The wind, rain, and snow produced by the earlier battle abruptly ceased, leaving a silence like the calm before a storm.

As Ethan flew farther from the center of the battlefield, he suddenly noticed a figure on the ground moving toward the crystal tower.

A closer look revealed Erika.

Huh? Why was she here?

Had she come looking for them?

Though puzzled, Ethan did not slow down. He patted Charizard's neck and had it descend rapidly toward Erika.

Elsewhere, Erika sensed something approaching and looked up. Ethan was charging toward her on Charizard.

She was about to call his name when Charizard swept past her.

"Get on!"

Before Erika could react, Ethan grabbed her hand. Her entire body rose from the ground and was pulled into the air.

"Ah!?"

Erika stared in shock. By the time she recovered, Ethan was holding her in his arms.

On Charizard's back, Ethan supported Erika with one arm behind her while his other hand happened to catch her beneath the hips.

With her soft body against him and that same softness beneath his hand, Ethan found his thoughts beginning to wander.

But he had not done it deliberately.

The situation had been too urgent and his approach too sudden. There had been no time to land and wait for Erika to climb safely onto Charizard, so he had ignored the usual boundaries between a man and woman and abruptly pulled her against his chest.

Being held this way left Erika deeply embarrassed.

But her temperament kept her from becoming angry over something like this. She knew Ethan must have a reason, so she suppressed her shyness, pretended to remain calm, and asked, "What's wrong? What happened?"

Ethan forced himself to concentrate. "Look behind us!"

Erika froze for a moment, then looked past his shoulder.

A mass of clouds in the black sky radiated a dangerous light. It was dazzlingly conspicuous against the night.

Even from this distance, Erika could sense the danger within it.

She had known that Ethan and the others were battling enemies at the crystal tower. But while traveling through the crystal forest, crystallized vines and rocks had blocked her view. Unlike Professor Oak and the others, she had been unable to see what was happening and knew nothing about the change in the sky.

Now that she saw it, she could not hide her shock.

"Wh-what is happening!?"

Ethan concealed nothing. He told her everything about the three Legendary birds and his plan to use Tri-Bird Assault.

"What!?"

Erika was stunned by his explanation.

Only now did she learn that all six Legendary Pokemon were real.

Ethan truly was battling Legendary Pokemon. Even more unbelievable, all three Legendary birds belonged to him.

When had he captured them?

The question reminded her of the time she, Ethan, and the International Police had raided Team Rocket's base. While facing the Team Rocket executive Apollo, Ethan had seemed ready to use some hidden trump card.

Had he already possessed the three Legendary birds back then?

Erika had believed she understood the man before her to some degree. Now she realized that he still carried far too many unrevealed mysteries.
