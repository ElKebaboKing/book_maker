Chapter 836: A Pokemon Ranger

Ethan would not have bothered to mock Harley. The contempt Harley thought he saw was all in his own mind. Ethan had not even been looking at him.

Ethan had scarcely been concerned by men like Maxie and Archie. Harley was not even an opponent to him. That judgment was dismissive, but Ethan considered it fair.

Harley might be a dangerous rival for May. Against Ethan, he was not as strong as the elite members of the criminal organizations Ethan had fought. On those sprawling battlefields, his Pokemon might have fallen to a single move from one of Ethan's main partners, just as an ordinary grunt's would. Harley's only extra weapon was a collection of underhanded schemes, and those were easy to see through.

Why would Ethan go out of his way to taunt someone like that? If Harley ever became a threat to him, perhaps Ethan would spare him another glance. It was an arrogant view, and Ethan did not pretend otherwise.

May's Wisteria Contest victory brought her another step closer to her goal. Beating Harley again had also strengthened her confidence, which would help her grow.

Ethan left the hall before her and waited by the rear entrance, leaning against a wall. He watched the spectators drift out through the front doors, still talking about the match.

May emerged holding her Ribbon. Her face lit up when she saw him. She had worked out that Munchlax's decision to throw Octillery out of bounds must have come from Ethan's training. Now she wanted to thank him.

You earned half of this Ribbon with me, she said. If you hadn't taught Munchlax that trick, I don't know what I would've done.

It was true. In the last moments of the battle, May's mind had gone blank. If Munchlax had not acted on its training, she would have lost.

That just shows you've still got room to improve, Ethan said. As for me, I only did what I should have. It wasn't a big deal. He tossed her backpack to her and smiled. We've won, and that's worth celebrating, but our journey isn't over. Time to get moving.

May answered with a broad smile. Right!

...

Both islands had small towns, but the quiet settlement on Metallica Island could not compare with Wisteria Town in size, population, or prosperity. At its dock, Ethan and May easily found a ship back to the Kanto mainland. They would not need another bumpy ride on Dragonite.

Their next destination was the peninsula where Fuchsia City stood. It was close enough that the crossing took only a little more than half a day. Ethan's next Frontier challenge, the Battle Pike, lay near Fuchsia.

They disembarked at a port town as evening approached. Rather than press on in the dark, they checked into its Pokemon Center.

While they were arranging their room, a familiar-sounding woman called from the dining area. You there, handsome. Want to join me?

Her voice had a mature warmth to it. Ethan was sure he had heard it before. He turned and found a red-haired woman with glasses and a ponytail sitting at a table, waving to him. He recognized her at once: Lorelei, one of Kanto's Elite Four.

He had not expected to meet her here. With a curious May beside him, Ethan crossed to her table and sat opposite her without waiting for an invitation.

What a coincidence, he said. I never thought I'd run into you here.

It is a coincidence. I didn't expect you back in Kanto, Lorelei said with a smile. What brings you here?

She studied him as they spoke. It had been a long time since they had met, and he seemed to carry himself differently now.

Ethan told her he had returned to challenge the Battle Frontier. Her surprise was plain.

You've already won League Conferences in three regions. I didn't think you'd come back to challenge a place like that.

What's wrong with it? Ethan heard the faint dismissal in her voice and laughed. Don't underestimate the Frontier Brains. Some are exceptionally capable, and their Pokemon can be dangerous. If you went in thinking otherwise, one of them might even beat you.

He noticed May looking at Lorelei with curiosity and introduced them.

This is May, my current traveling partner. She's a Coordinator. May, this is Lorelei, a member of the Kanto Elite Four.

The Elite Four? May stared. The elegant woman in front of her looked only in her twenties. But age was not the reason for May's surprise; Phoebe of Hoenn's Elite Four was younger still.

May was amazed to encounter someone of Lorelei's standing in an ordinary Pokemon Center, casually greeting Ethan like an old friend. Then she remembered that Ethan had won three regional League Conferences. It was hardly strange for him to know members of the Elite Four.

A Coordinator? Lorelei looked May over kindly. I was about your age when I first set out. Seeing you reminds me of myself then. Keep working at it. Your dream follows a different path from mine, but it isn't an easy one, especially for someone so young.

Women seemed to win people's affection more readily than men, especially women with Lorelei's presence and standing. A single encouraging remark from her won May over at once.

Then Lorelei remembered something and turned back to Ethan. You were the one who told a man named Drake to challenge me, weren't you?

Drake? For a moment Ethan could not place the name.

Don't play dumb, Lorelei said with a huff. The head Trainer of the Orange League.

Oh, him! Ethan finally remembered the man celebrated throughout the Orange Islands as their strongest Trainer. Had Ethan told him to challenge Lorelei? Perhaps he had.

Did he win? Ethan asked.

Of course not. Lorelei smiled. I gave him a thorough lesson. He lost badly.

She did not describe the match, but Ethan could imagine it. Sounds rather painful for him.

Neither of them considered Drake worth discussing for long, and the conversation moved on. Lorelei had stopped here only by chance. She was on her way to an event in Cerulean City and had decided to stay the night when it grew late.

It was a rare meeting. They were also closer in standing now and had more to talk about, so the conversation lasted until late. In the morning, they exchanged brief farewells and went their separate ways.

Fuchsia City stood on a peninsula, though the city itself was not on the coast. From the port town, Ethan and May headed northeast. At a forest, they left the main road and took what looked like a shortcut through the trees.

Before long, something felt wrong.

Ethan... May held her Eevee Egg as she walked beside him. Doesn't this forest seem a little...

Too quiet? Ethan scanned the trees and nodded. In a forest this large, we should hear something. Be careful. Something has happened here.

May grew alert and watched their surroundings as they went deeper. The trees thickened around them.

Suddenly a woman leaped out and blocked their path. A Plusle jumped down from her shoulder and copied her, stretching its little arms across the trail.

Please stop! the woman called. This area is closed. You two mustn't go any farther!

Ethan had almost thought they were being ambushed. So much for that excitement. But why would a wild forest be closed? He had heard nothing about it.

He looked the woman over. She appeared a year or two younger than him, petite and attractive, with red eyes and a teal ponytail that sprang upward. Her orange and white uniform covered a black one-piece suit. The combination made her look energetic and, Ethan thought, a little alluring.

She seemed familiar. Then he realized he had seen her in the anime, though never in person. He had a guess about who she was.

Closed? he asked. What do you mean?

It's a forest in the wild, May added. Why can't we go in?

The woman kept her voice calm. The situation inside is complicated. I can't explain it in a sentence or two. Something serious has happened. This area won't reopen to ordinary Trainers until we Pokemon Rangers have dealt with it.

Ethan spotted the insignia on her armband and nodded to himself. He had guessed right: she was a Pokemon Ranger.

Then perhaps we've come at the right time. Ethan opened his wallet and showed her the credential the International Police had given him recently. I'm not an ordinary Trainer. I'm a Special Investigator of the International Police. Maybe I can help.

A Special Investigator?

Even May glanced at the credential, wondering when Ethan had gained another title. The Ranger's eyes widened. She took the card, examined it carefully, and confirmed it was real.

Forgive me, she said, handing it back. I didn't expect to meet someone from the International Police here. In that case, please come with me.

Ethan had thought the credential was merely an honorary award he might never use. It had proved useful sooner than he expected. Association with the International Police could open doors.

He and May followed the woman into the forest. As they walked, she introduced herself.

I'm Solana. The Ranger Union sent me here to handle a special incident.

After so much travel, Ethan had finally met a member of the Pokemon Rangers. Their purpose and the Ranger Union's were easy enough to understand: they protected nature and Pokemon.

Rangers were elusive. An ordinary Trainer might rarely see one unless they ventured into an area where the environment had been seriously damaged. The organization worked across regions. Instead of relying on Poke Balls for their work, Rangers used Capture Stylers to connect with Pokemon, forming temporary partnerships and borrowing their strength to help people and protect the natural world.

They handled accidents and natural disasters, often without recognition. It was a distinctive and admirable profession, balanced between everyday duty and danger.

Ethan introduced himself and May. I'm Ethan Vale. Besides being a Special Investigator, I'm a traveling Trainer. This is my companion May.

Ethan? Solana frowned. She felt she had heard the name somewhere, but she paid little attention to news from the wider world and could not place it.

Where had she heard it?
