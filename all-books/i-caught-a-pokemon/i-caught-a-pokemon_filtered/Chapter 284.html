Chapter 284: The Arrest

Silver had clearly studied the grounds and Professor Elm's laboratory before coming here.

From a distance, Ethan and Looker watched him check both sides to make sure no one was around. He hurried to the building, forced open a window, and climbed inside. The whole maneuver was so smooth and efficient that Ethan could not help admiring it.

He exchanged a glance with Looker. They crept to the window and peered in, showing only the tops of their heads. Inside a research room full of delicate instruments, Silver took one of three Poke Balls lined up on a machine and slipped it into a pouch at his waist. Then he carefully returned the way he had come.

Ethan and Looker hurried back behind the tree before he emerged.

Silver did not bother hiding his tracks. He left the window open and walked straight out of Professor Elm's grounds without a pause.

Ethan turned to Looker and whispered, "What do you want to do? Should I stop him?"

Looker had frowned through the entire theft. Lost in thought, he did not answer for a long while.

Was that really it? How disappointing!

Given Team Rocket's usual methods, and the fact that Silver was Giovanni's son, Looker had assumed he must be the organization's second most important figure. He had imagined Silver arriving with Team Rocket members to carry out a spectacular raid. At the very least, he had expected them to strip the whole laboratory of Pokemon and research material.

Instead, after all the effort of following Silver here, he had witnessed a simple theft. Just one Poke Ball! When had Team Rocket become so unambitious?

You're the son of Giovanni, the Earth Gym Leader. Where are your standards? Your pride? How is this any different from the work of a petty thief?

"Let's wait," Looker said at last. He still could not let go of his theory. "I feel there's more to this. Follow him and see where he goes."

Ethan signaled for Gengar to keep tracking Silver. He and Looker went back over the wall and met Sabrina outside.

Silver walked directly out of New Bark Town toward Cherrygrove City. They followed for a long time. The moon climbed high, and stars filled the sky. When they were nearing the forest where Ethan had met the girl with the baseball bat, Looker finally ran out of patience.

"We can't let him keep going," he said with a sigh. "I read too much into it. He didn't come here to lead some major Team Rocket operation. He came to steal a Pokemon from Professor Elm, plain and simple."

Ethan looked at him. "So what now?"

Looker thought it over. "We have two choices. We can leave him alone and keep following him. Perhaps he'll eventually lead us to Team Rocket's hideout. But that could take a great deal of time and effort, and if he has other things to do, we'll be following him forever."

"The odds of that working are slim," Ethan agreed.

"Then we take the second option. Arrest him and question him. That should tell us everything we need to know."

Ethan weighed the choices and nodded. "All right. We have a Team Rocket member in front of us. No reason to let him walk away. Let's move."

They whispered over a plan. Ethan hurried ahead through the forest and caught up with Silver beside a lake, blocking his path.

"Who are you?" Silver demanded with a frown.

Before Ethan could answer, footsteps sounded behind him. Silver turned and saw a man and a woman approaching from either side. The three had him surrounded. They had plainly come for him, and their intentions were anything but friendly.

Looker held up his identification. "Silver, I'm with the International Police. You're under arrest. Come with us quietly. Don't resist and get yourself hurt."

"The International Police?" Silver did not look frightened. He sneered. "What crime have I committed that calls for you to arrest me?"

"If we're watching you, you can assume we know where you come from," Looker said. "You just left Professor Elm's laboratory. Must I tell you what you did inside?"

Damn it. When did they notice me?

They knew exactly what he had done at the laboratory. Silver realized they must have been following him for some time. If he wanted to get away, he would probably have to fight.

He took out the Poke Ball he had stolen and spun it around one finger.

"Fine. The Poke Ball is right here. If you want to take me in, let's see whether you can manage it!"

He had scarcely finished speaking when the Poke Ball lifted itself from his finger. It shot toward Sabrina, who caught it neatly in her hand.

"What?"

Silver stared at her in shock. Then recognition seemed to strike him. His pupils contracted as he looked her over.

"It's you! The woman with psychic powers!" His face paled, and he turned to Ethan. "Then you must be Ethan Vale. People in the organization keep talking about a man and a woman causing trouble for us everywhere. I never thought I'd run into you!"

A few ordinary International Police officers would not have worried him. This pair was different. They had made a fearsome reputation within Team Rocket. Elite units and even the four Executives had fallen to them. For ordinary members, merely hearing their names was enough to make their skin crawl.

What rotten luck. Why did I have to meet them?

Silver dropped any thought of underestimating his opponents. He took several Poke Balls from his belt. Three flashes of blue light followed, and three Pokemon appeared before them.

Ethan looked from one to the next: Sneasel, Zubat, and Gastly.

They were already good Pokemon. Why had Silver bothered stealing a starter from Professor Elm's laboratory?
