Chapter 520: Oh No, I'm the Villain

The first battle was fairly simple.

Sunny had been full of confidence beforehand, and his Heracross was strong compared with the Pokemon of other children his age. But under Ethan's direction, Eevee kept moving, using its agility to stay out of Heracross's reach. It never gave Heracross a chance to bring its strength to bear. After wearing it down, Eevee won easily with Swift.

Heracross itself had been capable. Sunny's commands, however, reflected his age and experience: attack, attack, and attack again! Ethan had seen the opening that created and used it.

The win had come easily. The difference was less in the Pokemon's strength than in tactics and judgment.

"What? A Week Sibling lost?"

The children watching began to whisper when Sunny's Heracross fell. Only then did they turn their attention to Ethan.

They had been rooting for Sunny and had seen how nimbly Eevee moved, but their confidence in the Week Siblings had made them believe he would somehow turn the fight around.

Instead, his disadvantage had simply grown until he lost. The children stared at Ethan, wondering who he was.

"You idiot! You didn't even touch that Eevee once. How embarrassing. Watch me!"

Santos pushed his younger brother aside. Though only ten, he had more experience and a little more maturity than Sunny, and he was sure he could win.

To him, Ethan's Eevee did not look difficult to handle. Sunny had only lost so badly because he had been foolish.

"Come out, my Pokemon!"

Santos threw a Poke Ball, and a Houndour appeared on the field.

Ethan had fought Team Rocket's Houndoom many times. Houndour was its unevolved form, weaker in every respect, and he was familiar with how to face it.

He did not switch Pokemon. Eevee was still in good condition, so it stayed in the battle.

Once they began, Ethan found that Santos's Houndour was indeed more troublesome than Heracross, despite not yet evolving.

Houndour and Eevee were similar in build and habits. By the sort of classification used on Earth, both were canine-like Pokemon. Ethan could not simply keep circling and evading as he had against Heracross.

This fight would come down to the Pokemon's abilities and their Trainers' commands.

As a battler, Ethan was far more skilled than Santos.

The two Pokemon traded move after move in a long, close fight. Both were covered in scrapes by the end. After one final clash, Houndour collapsed unconscious. Eevee swayed on its feet, but its stronger will kept it standing long enough to claim victory.

All told, the two battles had been fairly straightforward. Ethan showed little excitement over winning.

Santos walked off with the same disappointed look as his brother, and Monica stepped into the Trainer's position.

She had not expected either of the boys to win. Her older and stronger siblings had already lost to Ethan, so these two children had stood little chance.

She did not expect to beat him herself. Even so, a battle against a Trainer this strong would help both her and her Pokemon grow.

"It's my turn," Monica said with a smile. "If you beat me too, every one of the Week Siblings will have lost to you. Not many people can say they've done that."

Ethan did not underestimate a woman five or six years older than himself. As the eldest, Monica was likely to have more experience, a steadier outlook, and more ways to challenge him.

When her Pokemon appeared, Ethan sensed something unusual about it even before he took in its appearance.

It was a Shiny Flygon. Its coloring differed strikingly from that of an ordinary Flygon.

Flygon was not native to Johto or Kanto. Monica's partnership with one suggested that she had traveled to other regions. That was another reason not to take her lightly.

Still...

A Shiny Pokemon. Ethan practically drooled with envy.

When would he finally catch one of his own?

Bringing his attention back to the battle, he considered what to use against this Ground- and Dragon-type Pokemon.

After some thought, he chose Dragonite.

They were both Dragon-types. Neither needed to hold back. This would be a battle between dragons!

Monica was surprised to see him send out Dragonite.

Dragon-type attacks were effective against both of their Pokemon. Neither side had a safe type advantage, and one mistake could easily turn the battle. Was Ethan really that confident?

"Go on, Monica!" Sunny called from the side.

"Avenge usand all our older brothers and sisters!" Santos shouted.

Monica hardly reacted, but Ethan's eyebrow twitched.

Avenge them? Why did that make him sound like the villain?

He had defeated the siblings, yes, but only in ordinary Trainer battles, and he had accepted their challenges rather than seeking them out. How had the two boys decided he was the bad guy?

The children watching joined in after seeing the Week Siblings lose twice.

"You can do it, Monica!"

"The Week Siblings are the best!"

"Beat that man, Monica!"

They were Johto natives. Naturally, they favored the local siblings they had known about for years.

Monica quieted her brothers, then gave Ethan an apologetic look. She understood their wish for their sister to avenge them after a loss. But with the young spectators joining in, the cheering had begun to sound as though everyone had united against Ethan.

That was not what the Week Siblings stood for. Still, Monica did not know the children, and she could hardly lecture them.
