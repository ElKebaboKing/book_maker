Chapter 666: Rayquaza's Grand Entrance

Once Zinnia explained how the two teams had come here, Ethan breathed easier. Thank goodness. Team Aqua and Team Magma had stumbled on the situation by chance and tried to take advantage of it, much as Ethan had disrupted their plans before. That was far better news than a leak inside Devon.

No wonder Courtney had refused to answer his question. It was hardly an impressive plan to boast about.

The fighting below had settled into another stalemate. Team Magma could not break through, and Ethan's Pokemon could not yet push them back.

Close to Ethan, Zinnia released an Altaria to join Salamence. Zinnia might have been a little stronger than Courtney, though not so much stronger that she could overwhelm her. With Salamence and Altaria working together, she could hold Courtney and the elite Team Magma members to a draw despite their greater numbers.

On the other side of the valley, Team Aqua had made no real progress against Deoxys either. They had tried to account for its power and still underestimated it. With the three Legendary birds harassing Team Aqua, Deoxys was wreaking havoc. Even the elite members' Pokemon fell quickly before it. Only Shelly's Pokemon posed any meaningful threat.

Team Aqua did have numbers. Like ants wearing down a snake, they might eventually exhaust Deoxys's stamina and mental energy. They could defeat and capture it, though at a terrible costif the Legendary birds stopped interfering.

Ethan had not recalled the birds even when his own position was in danger. He clearly had no intention of giving Team Aqua that chance. As far as he was concerned, their dream of catching Deoxys could remain a dream.

Water continued spreading through the valley. In places, it was already more than half a meter deep. Battles raged everywhere. Fire and Water-type attacks reflected in countless pools, coloring one side of the valley fiery red and the other blue.

Then the sky changed again.

A great mass of dark clouds appeared overhead. Almost at the same moment, Deoxys, Raikou, Suicune, Moltres, Zapdos, and Articuno all raised their heads. The other Pokemon followed their gazes, and some stopped fighting altogether.

Ethan had nothing to do with this change in the weather. He sensed the disturbance and looked up with Misty. Courtney and Zinnia paused as well.

Before everyone's eyes, a Pokemon burst through the clouds and descended.

It was enormous and long-bodied. Green segments ran down its dragonlike form. Two long, flat, hornlike structures rose from its head, with two smaller ones below. Its two limbs ended in three-clawed hands. Red-edged, rudderlike fins projected from its shoulders and lower body, with a similar fin at the tip of its tail. Yellow rings patterned its length.

Most people present had never seen it before. Even so, one name came to mind for all of them: Rayquaza.

It's Rayquaza! Misty cried. It really came back!

Ethan glanced down beside him. Zinnia was trembling with excitement. She had believed Rayquaza might return, but even she had not expected it to appear so suddenly. This time, she vowed to herself, she would not miss her chance.

Team Aqua and Team Magma reacted very differently. At first, they had merely been searching the Mt. Chimney area for their own purposes. Even when the meteorite fell nearby, neither team had cared. Their goals were Groudon and Kyogre. What use was a mere space rock?

Only after hearing that a powerful Pokemon had appeared near the impact site did they gather their forces and come to capture it. Finding Ethan already fighting that Pokemon had delighted them. They might take the creature and eliminate a persistent enemy in one operation.

The rival team's arrival had been a surprise, but each group knew the other well enough to put it aside and focus on its own target. Despite some setbacks, the plan had seemed to be progressing.

Rayquaza's appearance changed everything. Capturing one Deoxys was already taking all their strength. How could they deal with another Pokemon of that caliber? Neither team had come to capture Rayquaza, but having it above them filled everyone with dread. Even the aftershocks of a fight involving it could kill them.

Rayquaza did not pause after breaking through the clouds. It flew straight at Deoxys. Shelly's expression changed. As Zinnia had said, Rayquaza was territorial and quick to seek revenge. Wounded in their earlier battle, it had now returned to settle the score.

Deoxys reacted at once. It abandoned the Team Aqua Pokemon surrounding it, rose into the sky, and met Rayquaza head-on.

Beside Ethan, Zinnia suddenly sprinted toward the ground beneath the two. Salamence and Altaria broke away from Team Magma and followed her.

Gardevoir! Ethan called.

Gardevoir, still fighting Team Magma farther away, used Teleport and appeared at his side. Ethan did not try to stop Zinnia. Instead he quickly told the three Legendary birds through telepathy to withdraw.

Deoxys and Rayquaza would fight until one fell or both were badly hurt. Ethan did not want his Pokemon caught between them. He could conserve their strength and watch for a chance to benefit from the outcome. With Zinnia gone, the birds could also return to protect him and Misty.

Above them, Deoxys and Rayquaza were already fighting. They seemed to see only each other, recognizing an opponent of equal standing. They chased, struck, and defended from sky to mountain, from mountain to ground, and back into the sky.

Their blows shook the earth. Mountainsides collapsed, trees were destroyed, craters opened, and even the shape of the land changed. Nothing nearby escaped the effects of their battle.

The three Legendary birds beat their wings and returned to Ethan. Courtney saw them arrive and immediately withdrew her people from his position.

She had struggled badly enough against one Raikou before. Facing three Pokemon of that same legendary rank with only her two grunts would be absurd. She did not leave the area, though. She drew the rest of Team Magma out of the fighting and gathered them together nearby.

Ethan glanced at her and looked away. Courtney apparently intended, as he did, to wait and make a move when the struggle overhead was over.

Team Aqua had it worse. Rayquaza and Deoxys were fighting right beside its people. After surrounding Deoxys earlier, they could not retreat even if Shelly ordered it.

Deoxys fired an energy ball that missed Rayquaza and blasted several Team Aqua Pokemon away instead. Rayquaza charged with its enormous body. Deoxys turned aside, leaving several Team Aqua members on a rock in its path. Rayquaza struck the rock and sent them flying into the air with it.

They were paying dearly for being so close.

With the birds guarding them and Ethan's other Pokemon withdrawing, Misty finally felt safe enough to take out her Pokedex. She aimed it at Rayquaza.

Rayquaza, the Sky High Pokemon. Dragon and Flying type. It is said to have lived in Earth's ozone layer for hundreds of millions of years. Flying at night, it resembles a shooting star. It lives so high above the clouds that it cannot be seen from the ground. It appears to feed on water and particles in the atmosphere, and it also eats meteorites.

Humans knew far more about Rayquaza than they did about Deoxys, partly because technology had improved. Satellites, spacecraft, and other objects sent skyward sometimes caught glimpses of it passing through the ozone layer. Repeated sightings and observation had given researchers a rough picture of its habits.

That was still only a rough picture. Its abilities, origins, birth, and reproduction remained mysteries.

Rayquaza... Misty lowered her Pokedex and watched it intently. She knew it belonged among Pokemon of Ho-Oh's stature and that the Draconid people had long revered and guarded it.

Thinking of the Draconids, she searched for Zinnia. The cloaked girl was running across broken rocks and flooded ground toward the other battlefield, forcing her way through every obstacle.

What is she trying to do? Misty asked.

Isn't it obvious? Ethan watched too. Whether they call it protection, inheritance, or something else, the Draconid people have always wanted one thing...

He did not finish, but Misty understood. Deoxys could be right in front of Zinnia, as could Kyogre or Groudon, and she would not care. She wanted to catch Rayquaza.

Zinnia had already reached the center of the battle. Explosions burst nearby, and other Pokemon blocked her path, but none stopped her from running toward Rayquaza. Her resolve carried her through.

Could she truly catch it on her own?

Above the valley, the fight had reached its fiercest point. Rayquaza's attacks had destroyed parts of Deoxys's body several times. Each time, rapid regeneration restored it. Rayquaza was suffering too. Deoxys's blows in Attack Forme were powerful enough to hurt even a Legendary Pokemon.

Still, Rayquaza appeared to hold a slight advantage.

Another collision shook the mountains. A small hill in the distance was flattened outright.

Zinnia tried calling to Rayquaza. She held up several bright, shining objects to catch its attention. Rayquaza ignored the tiny human beneath it, absorbed in the fight. Her voice vanished beneath the explosions.

Desperate, she ordered Salamence and Altaria to attack Rayquaza. They had barely entered the space where Rayquaza and Deoxys fought when the two Pokemon struck them down.

Zinnia had no way into the battle. Ethan shook his head. She had acted too hastily. How could she hope to catch Rayquaza amid this chaos without clearing away the people and Pokemon around it? Even speaking to it would require a better place and time.

He turned his attention back to the two combatants. Their furious exchange was draining them quickly. After only a few minutes, Deoxys had spent much of its stamina. Its movements and attacks visibly slowed. It was finally tiring. It had fought for a long time and regenerated more times than anyone could count.

Rayquaza was covered in wounds of different sizes and had begun to breathe heavily as well.

Almost everyone's eyes brightened. Both Pokemon were nearing their limits. If anyone meant to act, this was the moment.
