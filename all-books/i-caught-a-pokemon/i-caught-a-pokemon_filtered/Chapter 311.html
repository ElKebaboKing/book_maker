Chapter 311: A Clever Deception

By the time they left the Dragon Holy Land, dusk had fallen.

After the battle, Ethan felt relieved. As they came out of the mountains, the group looked up. Beneath orange-red clouds, a dozen or so Pidgey flew leisurely across the peaks in a V formation. Everything looked peaceful.

Blackthorn City lay not far away. Lights had come on in its buildings, cars, and streets, while neon signs added splashes of color. The city seemed newly alive against the silent forest around it. Ringed by mountains, it offered a striking view at this hour every day.

No one looking at that scene would have guessed that a fierce battle had just torn through a valley nearby.

They marched the Team Rocket prisoners onward for some time before meeting Officer Jenny and the police officers she had brought to receive them outside the city.

Jenny praised the group for capturing so many Rocket members at once. Like them, she regretted Vicious's escape.

"You nearly caught him. It's a shame Team Rocket's top executive got away. Still, arresting this many grunts deals the organization another serious blow. You've done us a great favor."

"Vicious won't remain free for long," Lance said gravely. "He carries those dangerous Poke Balls, and he attacked the Dragon Holy Land. The Elder has ordered the rest of our clan to pursue him as soon as they receive word. We won't stop until he's caught."

Lance spoke freely to Jenny about the Holy Land and the Elder. The Dragon Tamers and Blackthorn's police must have had a close relationship. After centuries of influence here, Ethan thought, perhaps the clan effectively controlled the whole city.

Lance and Jenny spoke a little longer. Then Lance left Kabu to complete the handover while he and Clair led Ethan and Sabrina toward Blackthorn.

Before leaving, Ethan looked back at the Rocket members. Jenny's officers were putting them in handcuffs. They would likely spend years, perhaps decades, in prison. Jenny was right: their arrests had weakened Team Rocket again.

Many of the grunts looked about Ethan's age. They had gone astray and sacrificed the best years of their lives for one man's absurd ambitions. It hardly seemed worth it. Ethan hoped they would come to understand that while they served their sentences.

They reached Blackthorn Gym after sunset. The day's battle and the journey back and forth had exhausted everyone, so Clair postponed Ethan's Gym match until the next day.

Lance and Clair warmly invited Ethan and Sabrina to stay. The two accepted rather than find other lodgings.

Though called a Gym, the place was really a large manor converted for that purpose. It had guest rooms, dining rooms, baths, and every other convenience, all luxuriously furnished. Inviting two outsiders who were not Dragon Tamers to stay there showed how thoroughly the clan had accepted them.

Hmm. If this were another game, my reputation with the Dragon Tamer clan would have reached Friendly by now, at least.

After a brief rest, Ethan and Sabrina were invited to dinner.

A five-meter wooden table stood in the dining room, set with tall glasses and spotless white plates. Ornate draperies, Pokemon ornaments, and a marble floor gleamed beneath the bright lights. Even their knives and forks were finely worked silver. Ethan took it all in, impressed by the clan's wealth.

At the other end of the table, Lance and Clair had changed out of their battle clothes into everyday wear.

Clair was quite attractive, Ethan thought, once he looked past her unusual hairstyle. Her firm muscles also gave her a less delicate appearance than many other female Trainers.

"A toast to our victory!"

"Cheers!"

Conversation flowed through dinner, though Lance and Ethan did most of the talking. Clair added a comment now and then. Sabrina said nothing, eating her meal with her usual elegance.

They drank expensive red wine. Ethan could not tell good wine from bad; it all tasted bitter and sharp to him. The fine food was delicious, but he could not say exactly what made it better than ordinary ingredients. Even so, he thoroughly enjoyed the meal.

Afterward, Clair arranged a separate room for each of her guests. Evidently, she had not realized Ethan and Sabrina were a couple. She thought they were simply traveling companions.

Ethan could hardly explain it to her. He gave Sabrina a rueful smile as she teased him with a look, then went helplessly into his own room.

Once inside, he put the awkwardness behind him and went straight to wash the dust from his body. Humming a song he had liked back on Earth, he soaked comfortably in a hot bath until the day's fatigue eased away.

He had only just lain down on the bed when Gengar sidled over with a leering grin.

"What's with you?" Ethan gave it a puzzled look. "Why are you smiling like that? Did you find some money?"

"What would I do with money? It's just paper." Gengar rolled its eyes, then chuckled. "I've got something good to show you!"

It reached into its body and pulled out a black Poke Ball from somewhere inside.

Huh? Ethan looked closer. It was one of Vicious's Dark Balls.

"A Dark Ball?!" His eyes widened. "How did you get that?"

"Heh heh." Gengar grinned, clearly proud of itself. "Everyone saw me destroy the Dark Balls Vicious had. What they didn't see was that I secretly kept the unused one."

"Well, look at you." Ethan shook his head. "That was quite a trick. You fooled me, Sabrina, Lance, and Clair. We really thought you'd smashed every ball you had in your hands."

He took the Dark Ball and examined it. Apart from its black shell and a dozen or so gray stripes, its shape was much like an ordinary Poke Ball.

He turned it over in his hands, but felt no excitement. With a Master Ball already in his possession, he had no desire to use this device. As an honorable Trainer, he would never steal someone else's Pokemon. Besides, a Pokemon captured by a Dark Ball became evil, its mind badly affected.

"Well, now that I have it, I'll keep it as a souvenir. A collector's piece." Ethan sighed. "I hope I never have to use it."
