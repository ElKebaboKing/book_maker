Chapter 479: The Pokemon Virus

It was as though the whole spectacle had been arranged just to drive everyone out of the base.

The explosions had stopped completely. Now the base seemed to have lost power and was rapidly sinking again.

Given its enormous size and weight, the seawater could not hold it up on its own.

Erika turned toward a window. Water soon covered it once more, the last light of the setting sun disappeared, and the familiar underwater scene came back into view.

She frowned. "It looks as though we're heading back to the seafloor."

Ethan nodded and looked deeper into the base. "Come on. Let's see what Team Rocket is really up to!"

The two advanced cautiously. After walking some distance, they found the facility deserted. The irritating alarm had stopped long ago, and now the only sounds were their footsteps and breathing.

Tap, tap, tap, tap...

Their hurried steps echoed through the empty base.

As they went deeper, they took a quick look inside every room they passed. Each was in the same state of disarray, but nothing else happened.

At the heart of the base, however, they found something different.

This was clearly the research area. It was thoroughly protected: thick bulletproof glass surrounded it, and its only entrance had a blast door.

Through the glass, they could see people moving inside!!

Ethan and Erika exchanged a glance. At Ethan's signal, Gardevoir used Psychic. With a tremendous bang, the force tore the entire door from its frame!

Inside, they first saw a short old man dressed in white. He stood with his back to them, working at a control panel.

Several others nearby wore protective suits. They stood frozen as Ethan and Erika entered, watching them with shock, unease, and panic in their eyes.

The old man turned and regarded Ethan without expression.

"You really are persistent. Even after all that, we still couldn't fool you."

Ethan glanced at the red hair ringing the man's bald crown. "Who are you?" he asked quietly.

The old man smiled faintly. "Allow me to introduce myself. I'm Dr. Namba, the director of this base."

Dr. Namba...

Ethan only vaguely remembered him. The scientist had appeared so briefly in the anime that the storyline had barely touched on him.

Ethan looked around. The laboratory was filled with precision equipment, some of it still operating. Rows of test tubes in different colors made the place look like a chemistry and biology lab.

"You went to such lengths to hide yourselvesexplosions, raising the base to the surface, even fooling your own people. What are you researching here?"

Dr. Namba studied him for a long moment.

"There's no harm in telling you. We're researching a Pokemon virus."

"A Pokemon virus!?"

Shocked, Ethan searched his memory. There had indeed been a story about such a virus in the anime, though he could no longer remember the details. He had a vague recollection that Team Rocket had even kidnapped Professor Oak over the virus research!

Dr. Namba saw Ethan frown and gave a cold laugh.

"Viruses, bacteria, vaccines... They're all essentially the same. People usually want nothing to do with a virus. I see it differently. A virus can change a Pokemon's genes and greatly enhance its abilities. There may be some harm, but on balance, the benefits far outweigh the costs!"

There he goes. Another stubborn old man who has taken an idea much too far...

"So the Pokemon locked up downstairs are your test subjects?"

"Naturally." Dr. Namba snorted, then spoke with regret. "It's a shame we didn't finish organizing and transferring the data. I thought we had plenty of time, but clearly we don't."

He waved to the people around him. The researchers looked as though they had been granted a reprieve and hurried into an elevator to one side.

The elevator descended toward an unknown destination.

Ethan made no move to pursue them.

They were ordinary research staff. The real source of the problem was Dr. Namba.

Even if Ethan arrested every one of those people, Dr. Namba could recruit more to work for him as long as he held to his ideas.

His target was clear: the old man standing before him!

Dr. Namba turned away and made a few more casual adjustments at the control panel.

His composure made Ethan wary of another trick. Ethan started toward him, speaking to keep the scientist's attention divided.

"Aren't you worried?"

"About what?" the old man replied without looking up.

"About being captured and handed over to the International Police."

Dr. Namba shook his head. "You still don't understand. When my research succeeds, the virus will produce Pokemon that are bigger and stronger than ever! What I'm doing will be a great achievement for humanity!! As a Pokemon Trainer, surely you want more powerful Pokemon of your own?"

Before Ethan could answer, Erika, who had remained silent until now, could no longer contain herself. "A great achievement!? You must be joking. You want to use a virus to change their genes. That's a violation of life itself!!"

Dr. Namba glanced at her and snorted. "Young lady, you have a great deal to learn. Humans and Pokemon alike evolved by adapting to their environments little by little. We've been changing our genes all along."

Perhaps realizing that neither of them shared his beliefs, Dr. Namba did not bother explaining further.

He gave Ethan one last look.

"Young man, I know who you are and what you've done. Frankly, you've achieved a remarkable amount at your age. You could go even further in the future. But for now... good luck."

Without hesitation, he pressed a red button on the control panel.

The next instant, the base shook violently and powerful explosions sounded on both sides!

Boom!!

The walls shattered, releasing thick smoke and flashes of fire!

Ethan immediately threw himself over Erika to shield her. Gardevoir raised a barrier around them both!

Smoke filled the room, and fragments of machinery scattered across the floor.

When Ethan raised his head and looked for Dr. Namba, the scientist was already inside the elevator the researchers had used. Its doors were slowly closing.

Before Ethan could give chase, seawater surged in from all sides. In an instant, it covered his feet!
