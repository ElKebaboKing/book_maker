Chapter 389: Heat Wave and Lightning

"Muk!"

Aya hurriedly looked toward Muk. The blades of air had cut two enormous gashes into its body. Sludge splattered everywhere, and so much of Muk's body seemed to have vanished that a huge portion was missing.

The attack had nearly split it into three pieces!

Fortunately, as a sludge Pokemon, Muk possessed some ability to heal itself. Although it had been cut open, the missing portions began churning and closing at a visibly rapid rate within moments.

Even so, Muk was in terrible pain. Its already ugly face twisted into an even more distorted expression.

Strike while the opponent was weak and finish it off!

Following that principle, Ethan swept out a hand and issued another command before Aya or Muk could react.

"Charizard, use Heat Wave!"

Charizard's chest expanded. It blew forcefully, releasing a scorching wave of faint orange-red air from its mouth. The heat rushed straight toward Muk and enveloped more than half the battlefield.

Heat Wave struck Muk in an instant and inflicted tremendous damage. Perhaps because the temperature was so high, Muk's body also became extremely dry. Large amounts of unidentified liquid flowed out of it, causing its body to shrink. Its movements slowed, and even the wounds that had been steadily healing seemed to stop closing.

"Muk, are you all right!?"

Seeing Muk suffer such severe damage, Aya shouted anxiously. Her earlier satisfaction had vanished entirely.

Only Muk's repeated cries of pain answered her.

"Graaah!"

Muk constantly writhed beneath the scorching air, but the evaporation of the liquid inside it had left its skin dry and badly cracked. It still tried to struggle, but Ethan swept out his hand again. Charizard dove close and struck with Slash, knocking Muk down directly.

Bang!

Muk's heavy body crashed to the ground. When it failed to rise, the headmaster announced the result.

"Charizard wins the first battle!"

And yet...

Although the headmaster had announced the outcome without any change in expression, he was inwardly astonished.

Aya was his finest student!

As the younger sister of Gym Leader Koga, she was naturally no weakling herself.

He had expected the two Trainers to exchange attacks for a long time before a winner emerged. He never imagined Charizard would be so powerful that it defeated Muk with such ease.

Is this the level of a Champion!?

Across the field, Aya rubbed her eyes, seemingly unable to believe what she had seen.

Only after several seconds did she finally accept the outcome. She sighed, took out a Poke Ball, and reluctantly recalled Muk.

The two sides had appeared evenly matched only moments ago. How had Muk been defeated in the blink of an eye?

"Damn it..." Aya looked up at Ethan resentfully. "I admit I was too careless just now, and that gave you an opening. But this time, I absolutely won't let my guard down!"

As she spoke, she took a second Poke Ball from inside her clothes and threw it onto the field.

Blue light flashed, revealing a spider-like Pokemon before Ethan.

He rubbed his chin.

Oh?

Ariados after Muk...

Ariados had six limbs and the typical structure of an insect. Unlike an ordinary spider, however, it also possessed a single horn on its forehead. Its body displayed yellow, black, red, and purple, making its poisonous nature apparent at a glance.

Since this was the first time Ethan had seen an Ariados in person, he took out his Pokedex and pointed it toward the Pokemon.

The Pokedex beeped. "Ariados, the Long Leg Pokemon. A Bug and Poison type. Research shows that Ariados never builds a nest in one fixed location. After darkness falls, it wanders in search of prey. Small hooked claws at the ends of its feet allow it to walk across ceilings and vertical walls. Upon finding prey, Ariados binds it tightly with thin, durable silk to prevent movement, then bites with its fangs and slowly drains its bodily fluids. Additional note: some regions use Ariados silk to weave cloth, which is widely valued for its durability."

Ethan put away the Pokedex and looked at Ariados again.

Like a hunter concealed within its web, Ariados watched Charizard coldly through purple eyes that gleamed with a chilling light.

Without much hesitation, Ethan recalled Charizard despite the fact that the previous battle had consumed little of its stamina. He likewise sent out his second Pokemon.

Bang!

When the light vanished, Aya focused on the new arrival and saw a Jolteon.

Ariados's Bug and Poison typing had no advantageous or disadvantageous matchup against the Electric-type Jolteon. Aya breathed a sigh of relief. Though she did not want to admit it, she knew that Ethan placed her under tremendous pressure.

The headmaster swept his hand down to signal the beginning of the match. Without waiting for their Trainers' instructions, the two Pokemon immediately began fighting.

Poison Sting, Leech Life, Bug Bite, Pin Missile...

At Aya's commands, Ariados used one move after another. But Jolteon was far too fast, while Ariados was far too slow. Almost every attack missed.

Ariados could neither hit nor catch its opponent. Jolteon, meanwhile, continued creating trouble for it without pause.

Damn it!

The constant harassment left Aya increasingly agitated.

With no other choice, she finally ordered Ariados to keep launching webs in an attempt to restrain Jolteon.

Pop! Pop! Pop!

Ariados repeatedly opened its mouth, firing clumps of white silk toward Jolteon one after another.

The tactic did have some effect. Although none of the webs struck Jolteon, they remained scattered across the battlefield. Their stickiness forced Jolteon to become cautious. To avoid having its legs trapped, it had to take exceptional care with every step, and its speed inevitably fell.

This gave Aya a chance to recover.

"Now's our chance! Ariados, get close to it!"

Ariados moved its legs and rapidly approached Jolteon.

At the sight, Ethan smiled silently.

Aya was clearly fully focused in this second battle. She had not relaxed for even a moment and was using all her strength in an attempt to defeat him.

But that attempt was destined to fail.

As Ariados drew closer, Ethan shouted, "Jolteon, finish the battle!"

At Ethan's command, Jolteon stopped holding back. Its body erupted with dazzling light, and countless thick currents of electricity shot rapidly toward Ariados.

The currents spread like a vast electrical net and enveloped Ariados in an instant.

At the same time, Jolteon sprang into the air and lunged toward Ariados.

Electricity crackled between its teeth. Jolteon opened its mouth and sank its fangs into Ariados in the next instant!
