Chapter 195: Danny, Star of the East

Before it grew completely dark, Ethan and Sabrina found the village's only inn and took two rooms.

On an island this sparsely populated, they had no reason to fear it would be full. As far as they could tell, they were the only visitors there.

The country inn was not particularly comfortable. When the evening breeze came through the open window, it carried the smell of Tauros dung.

The residents might no longer notice it. Ethan and Sabrina found the smell hard to bear, especially as it came and went with each gust of wind. At least the rooms were clean and tidy. They were still warmer and more comfortable than camping outdoors.

Ethan moved Sabrina to a room sheltered from the wind, then shut his own windows and door. It grew hot inside, but that kept most of the odor out.

By seven or eight that night, the village was silent. Outside his window was darkness, with scarcely a light in sight.

Used to the pace of city life, Ethan lay awake. He tossed and turned for over an hour before finally drifting off. The mattress was hard, and when he woke the next morning, his body ached. He felt more tired than before he slept.

The view was lovely, but he had no wish to stay here again.

After washing up, he and Sabrina ate a generous breakfast in the inn's main room. As they prepared to check out, the broad-shouldered innkeeper, a woman weathered by years of outdoor work, looked them over.

"Did you sleep well?" she asked with a smile. "You look like you've come to challenge Danny."

"Well enough, apart from your Tauros having an upset stomach last night," Ethan said with a rueful smile. "Is Danny the Navel Gym Leader?"

"That's right! He's the Star of the East in our Orange Crew. He's beaten many challengers. Don't underestimate him." Pride showed in her voice.

"Thanks for the warning." Ethan gathered their bowls and utensils together. "I'm a strong trainer too. Only a few days ago, I beat Cissy, the Star of the West."

The innkeeper raised her eyebrows. "You've beaten Cissy? Then you must have some skill."

Sabrina was more cautious. "How did you know we were here for the Gym?"

"Easy to guess. Navel Island is a farming island. Few people live here, and we don't have special goods to draw travelers. We hardly see outsiders in any season. New faces like yours must have come to challenge Danny."

"I see," Ethan said. "Where can we find his Gym?"

She pointed through the window toward the volcano. "At the summit. Danny doesn't spend much time there, though. Look by the shore or around the foot of the mountain before you go up. You might find him."

Ethan and Sabrina left the inn and followed a narrow path toward the volcano.

Halfway there, as they passed the shore, cheerful tropical music reached them from the sea. They turned to see a man sailing a small boat across the waves. An old-fashioned speaker at his feet was playing the music.

Sabrina gave him a glance and kept walking. Ethan stayed for a closer look.

The man appeared to be in his late twenties, with healthy bronze skin. He wore loose shorts and a red shirt patterned with Geodude. He did not look like someone who sat quietly indoors.

The innkeeper had said Danny liked outdoor sports and was good at surfing and climbing. Few young people remained on the island; most had gone to larger cities. Ethan guessed the man in the boat was Danny.

Sailing to music so early in the morning seemed an enviable way to spend a Gym Leader's time. When Ethan eventually stopped traveling, he hoped he could live as freely.

He did not interrupt the sailor. Instead, he caught up with Sabrina and continued toward the Gym.

From the water, Danny had noticed them heading for his mountain. He hauled on the sail and turned his boat toward shore.

"Hello! You two up aheadwait a moment!"

The boat ran onto the sand. Danny leaped down with an easy flourish and landed in front of them.

"Are you here to challenge Navel Gym?"

"Yes," Ethan said.

Danny smiled broadly. "Thought so. I'm Danny. Good to meet you!"

He even gave them an exaggerated salute. Ethan's guess was right. Danny was remarkably quick to strike up a conversation.

"I'm Ethan. Did you need something?"

"You looked like you're from off the island," Danny said. "You might not know the way to the Gym. I happen to be headed up the mountain myself, so I could show you."

He sounded helpful, but he had not admitted he was the Gym Leader. Ethan suspected there was a reason for that.

Danny turned to Sabrina. "And may I ask the name of this lovely lady?"

No answer came. Sabrina looked at him with an unreadable, level gaze, as if he were a stone or a tuft of grass beside the road and not worth her attention.
