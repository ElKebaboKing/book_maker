Chapter 682: Run, Keep Running!

Archie spotted the Blue Orb first among the broken stones. To his delight, it lay closer to him than to Ethan. He needed only a few steps to reach it.

He grinned fiercely, strode forward, and bent to pick it up. Just as his hand touched it, the orb lifted off the ground on its own.

What?

Archie looked up. Gengar had appeared above him, cradling the orb. Heh heh heh! This shiny stone is mine now! It flew back to Ethan.

Damn you! Archie roared. He threw a Poke Ball, releasing a Muk in front of Ethan.

Ethan smelled it before he could see it clearly. The stench made his eyes sting. After several coughs, he forced himself to look at Muk.

People sometimes claimed that the more a Muk reeked, the stronger it was. There was no scientific proof, but Archie's Muk was the foulest Ethan had ever encountered. Perhaps the rumor had some truth.

Muk, Sludge Bomb!

Muk reached into its own body and hurled a murky purple mass at Ethan. Gengar met it with Shadow Ball. The attacks exploded together, spraying foul droplets and scraps in every direction.

Ethan hurried back while Archie prepared to pursue him. Then a deep, whale-like call echoed through the cavern.

Kyogre had woken.

The noise of the battle had disturbed its sleep. It opened its eyes to find strangers invading its home. Whether from anger, territorial instinct, or simple irritation, it reacted furiously.

It did not even use an attack. Merely turning its body shook the whole cavern. Everyone realized they had provoked the ancient Legendary Pokemon, the embodiment of the sea.

Stalactites broke from the ceiling in such numbers that no one could avoid every falling stone. One enormous piece came straight toward Ethan. Gengar moved above him and caught it with its body.

Everyone else scrambled for safety. Misty and Shelly were in the path of the rockfall too, but Gardevoir was beside them and kept them safe. The other Pokemon defended themselves as best they could. Ethan spared Archie and his senior members no attention.

Another, larger rock fell. Gengar blocked it as well, but grunted under the impact. The orb slipped from its hands, struck the ground, and rolled away.

It came to rest near Shelly. She reacted quickly and picked it up.

The shaking stopped, and the stalactites ceased falling. Kyogre paid the intruders no further attention. It turned, dove into the pool, and vanished.

No! Archie cried in despair. Bruised from dodging the falling stone, his bandanna gone, he looked almost unrecognizable. He turned blazing eyes on Shelly. This is all your fault! Give me the orb!

Fortunately, everyone had the instincts of a Trainer and none had been seriously injured.

I'm sorry... Shelly stepped back. She looked pained but sounded firm. I can't stand by and let you drag Team Aqua and all Hoenn into ruin.

Traitor! Archie ordered his Pokemon to charge her without restraint.

Shelly still seemed ready to explain herself. Ethan called to Misty, grabbed Shelly's hand, and pulled her toward the way they had come. The three fled.

He could have tried to capture Archie here, but two risks stood in his way. Shelly had not turned on Team Aqua. Even after Archie called her a traitor and attacked her, she refused to strike back. Her loyalty was still with the organization. If Ethan tried to arrest Archie, Shelly might attack Ethan from behind.

And they were deep underground. If a desperate Archie brought down the cavern, no one might escape. The chance was small but too serious to ignore.

Kyogre was gone and the Blue Orb was in their hands. Leaving now and making a new plan was the sensible choice.

Being pulled away finally brought Shelly back to herself. The upheaval had shaken even her usual composure.

Covered by their Pokemon, the three ran back to the place where they had moored the submarine. The Team Aqua guard was waiting anxiously. His eyes lit up at the sight of Shelly, but before he could ask a question, the three recalled their Pokemon and climbed aboard without stopping.

He had no idea what had happened farther inside. The tremors only made him more curious. Had Archie fought Kyogre? Why had Shelly returned alone?

The submarine was already diving when Archie and the senior members finally arrived.

Where's Shelly? Archie demanded.

She left in her submarine.

What? Why didn't you stop her?

Why would I? The guard stared at him.

Archie ignored him and boarded his own submarine. Only after everyone was inside did one of the senior members tell the guard that Shelly had betrayed Team Aqua.

That can't be right, the guard murmured after a long silence. There must be some mistake.

His disbelief showed how highly Team Aqua regarded Shelly. He would sooner believe Archie and the others had misunderstood or were playing some kind of cruel joke.

Enough, Archie snapped. Pursue her! Contact Matt and tell him to stop that traitor!

The guard hurried to open a channel. Matt answered almost at once. Archie, have you brought Kyogre under control?

Archie exploded. Stop the traitor!

Traitor? What are you talking about?

Don't let Shelly escape! She betrayed me and all of Team Aqua! She brought that man Ethan Vale to Kyogre and stole the Blue Orb!

What? Matt was stunned. As a commander, he recovered faster than the guard. He doubted Shelly had truly betrayed them, but stopping her until they understood would do no harm. He waved his people into their submarine.

...

Shelly steered through the same long, narrow underwater passage. She had stopped Archie from using the orb, yet her thoughts were in turmoil. How had she become a traitor?

Ethan did not need to see her face to sense it. He glanced out the window. I know what you're thinking. But if you let it distract you from piloting, Archie will catch us before long.

She stiffened slightly. After several seconds, she gave a curt reply. I know.

The submarine surged forward, moving faster than before. The delay had already cost them, though. Archie's craft was closing in; its bright searchlight swept over their stern. Their communicator lit up.

Shelly hesitated, then accepted the call. Archie's furious voice filled the cabin. Shelly! You dared betray Team Aqua! I'll tear you apart!

I didn't. Please listen

He cut the connection before she could finish. Only the dead tone remained.

Ethan shook his head at her back. You can't reason with someone who's lost control. Besides, he has expelled you. You don't have to stay involved with Team Aqua now.

I'm not a traitor! This happened because of you!

Because of me? Ethan shot her an irritated look. Without me, Archie might already have caused Kyogre's Primal Reversion. Which would you choose: seas swallowing the world or being called a traitor?

Shelly had no answer. Angry, she ignored him and focused on piloting.

The tunnel brightened, and the submarine emerged into deep water. Almost immediately they saw another craft waiting outside the entrance.

Shelly reacted with a sharp turn, barely avoiding a collision. Matt, she said through her teeth.

The failed interception let them slip past, but Matt and Archie both chased after them. Neither submarine fell far behind.

They may follow us to the ends of the earth. Ethan glanced back. Does this thing have weapons?

A harpoon launcher and two torpedoes. They all fire forward.

That's unfortunate. Their submarines seem to be the same model. They can attack us from behind, and we can't fire back.

Relax, Shelly said without a trace of panic. I'm Team Aqua's best and fastest pilot. They won't catch me.

Ethan's phone rang. Anabel was calling. He answered immediately.

Thank goodness, I finally reached you, she said with relief. I'm aboard a maritime police vessel near the area. Where are you?

Her timing could not have been better. I'll send you my coordinates. Send me yours too. We stopped Archie, but he's chasing us now. We need support.

After the call, Ethan told Shelly about Anabel. To his surprise, she seemed unmoved.

You think I need help from the police? she scoffed. We're Team Aqua. We don't work with them, and we certainly don't ask them to protect us.

Are you still Team Aqua? Ethan gave her a knowing look. Archie threw you out in the cave. Right now you're on your own.

Again, Shelly had no answer. However unwilling she was to admit it, could she still call herself a member?

Even so, I don't need the police! she declared. Even if they catch me, even if they blow this submarine to pieces, I won't ask for help. I'll lose them myself!
