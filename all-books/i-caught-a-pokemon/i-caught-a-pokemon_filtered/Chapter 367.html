Chapter 367: The Second Entei

Ethan and Gengar continued flying downward. Before long, they reached the lowest level of the crystal tower and found the room James had mentioned.

Gengar landed, and Ethan jumped from its back. Looking toward the room, he saw that someone had smashed the door open by force. The Team Rocket trio had clearly done it when they fled earlier.

Before Ethan could approach, Gengar sensed something.

"Be careful!" it warned. "Something inside that room feels extremely strange! I can sense a tremendously dangerous energy surging in there!!"

Gengar's solemn expression immediately put Ethan on guard.

Even without Gengar's warning, however, Ethan knew this had to be the most dangerous place in the tower. It was the source of the energy behind the crystallization.

He nodded and quietly moved to the doorway, where he peered inside.

Judging by its layout, the room had originally been a spacious reception hall. Like every other part of the manor, however, crystal had consumed and encased the entire space. Everything gleamed as though they had entered a world made of crystal, and several purple crystal flowers had even formed in the corners.

Dozens of Unown gathered in midair, forming a circle and flying around it in a steady rotation as though conducting some mysterious ritual.

At the circle's center floated a huge mass of red energy. It crackled while continuously radiating an eerie light.

The energy was unstable. Its shape changed constantly, as though it might explode at any moment.

One glance told Ethan that Molly's recent emotional outburst had caused this. If they did not bring it under control, the energy would eventually reach a critical point. Any further disturbance might blow everything here to pieces.

This was bad...

As a precaution, Ethan released Gardevoir and Charizard. With those two and Gengar at his side, he felt slightly more secure. Only then did he lead the three Pokemon carefully into the reception hall.

The Unown showed no reaction to his intrusion and continued circling without pause.

Then again, with no faces beyond their single eyes, Ethan would have had no way to tell whether they were speaking, angry, or furious.

Standing in a relatively safe position, Ethan carefully studied the Unown.

Their forms resembled the twenty-six Latin letters from A to Z, with question mark and exclamation mark forms mixed among them. Each Unown possessed a single large eye.

"What do we do now?" Apparently afraid of disturbing them, Gengar whispered beside Ethan's ear. "How do you plan to stop them under these conditions?"

Ethan considered the question.

"The safest method would be to wait until Ash defeats Entei and Molly works through her feelings. Once she returns to normal, these Unown should disperse on their own. But I don't like placing my fate in someone else's hands. We don't know what's happening above us, and if anything goes wrong, it may be too late to fix it afterward!"

From his answer, Gengar knew that Ethan intended to resolve the matter himself. Given the current situation, it also understood how difficult that would be.

"This energy is far too dangerous. If we handle it badly, it will definitely explode! I suggest that we first stop the Unown from accumulating any more energy, then find a way to release what's already there!"

Ethan had no better idea. Gengar's suggestion seemed to be their only available solution.

"All right. First, we'll make those Unown stop!"

He turned toward Gardevoir. Understanding his intent, she nodded and immediately used Confusion on one of the Unown.

Gardevoir's psychic force pulled the Unown out of the circle and slowly drew it toward Ethan.

Ethan could see it struggling constantly, trying to break free with its own power. But Gardevoir had used all her strength to ensure that she succeeded. A single Unown could not resist her, and she soon extracted it from among its companions.

Before Ethan could celebrate, all the other Unown began trembling at once. His intervention had clearly disturbed them.

To avoid provoking them any further, Ethan had no choice but to make Gardevoir release the Unown and watch it return to the circle above.

That had failed...

Ethan sighed and turned toward Gengar. "All right, it's your turn. Try Hypnosis this time."

Gengar obeyed without hesitation and used Hypnosis.

Concentric ripples appeared in its eyes, but the waves of hypnotic light passed over the Unown without affecting them at all.

Perhaps the Unown never slept, or perhaps their current ritual made Hypnosis ineffective. Whatever the reason, the second attempt had also failed.

What could they try next?

Just as Ethan ran out of ideas, a figure flashed before him. Entei smashed through a wall and appeared on the other side of the room!

It studied Ethan and the Unown, obvious thought and confusion appearing on its face.

Huh?

Why had Entei come here too?

Ethan could not help feeling surprised.

Entei should have been battling Ash at the top of the tower right now.

Had Ash lost?

Or had the attack on the Unown caused them to create another Entei to stop him?

Whatever the answer, its appearance gave Ethan a new idea.

If Entei was an illusion created by the Unown, perhaps defeating it would indirectly weaken the mass of energy.

With that thought, he immediately commanded the Pokemon beside him.

"Attack that illusion!"

The moment he spoke, Charizard and Gardevoir moved around the Unown and attacked Entei directly.

Their attacks flew toward it from opposite sides.

Strangely, Entei did not fight the two approaching Pokemon. Instead, it turned and retreated through the hole it had smashed in the wall.

It ran away?

Ethan raised an eyebrow in surprise, then glanced at the mass of energy and understood. Perhaps Entei did not want to battle here because it feared causing an explosion.

After all, an explosion would engulf even the Unown that had created it.

That suited Ethan perfectly.

He was equally afraid that a battle might agitate the energy. Since Entei had left, Ethan led his three Pokemon through the hole and gave chase.
