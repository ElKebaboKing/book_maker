Chapter 788: Champion

In the rounds that followed, Ethan gave Milotic, Metagross, and Eevee a turn in the final.

Objectively, Hua was a capable Trainer. He might have been somewhat weaker than Tyson, but not by much. He could never have fought his way to the final otherwise.

On another day, without bringing in partners such as Jolteon and Tyranitar, Ethan would have needed more time to beat him with the Pokemon he had used here. But Hua had lost his nerve. His attacks weakened, his defense fell apart, and in that state he could not threaten Ethan.

It was a pity. Hua had reached the final without the confidence to match. He had let fear put him behind before the battle began, and the weight of the occasion had badly affected his performance.

Steven or Ash would not have been shaken this much in his place. Not everyone had their temperament, though. Hua was an ordinary Trainer in his first final. Drawing Ethan might have been the worst luck of his life.

Champion!

Champion!

Champion!

Gardevoir came out last as Ethan's safety net and defeated the final opponent with ease. The championship battle was over.

We have a new Ever Grande Conference champion! the announcer shouted. He had to strain his voice to rise above the stadium. Nearly hoarse, he cried, Ethan defeats Hua six to zero! Let's congratulate the champion who dominated his way here and set a record: Ethan!

The stands erupted again. The sound seemed to swallow everything else, until all Ethan could hear was the roar of the crowd.

This was what it meant to be the public's choice. Ethan had fought flawlessly, commanding his matches so completely that he had won over the audience as well as his opponents. Their cheers answered him.

Standing in his Trainer's area, Ethan felt his ears ring. He had won two Conferences before. Apart from Tyson, he had faced few strong opponents this time, and many battles had felt one-sided. Still, the sight before him stirred him deeply.

No wonder so many Trainers pursued a championship their whole lives. Being praised by thousands like this was intoxicating.

Hua came over with admiration on his face and held out a hand. Congratulations. You deserve this championship. I lost, and I have no complaints.

He meant it. Losing to someone else would have left him full of regret, wishing he had performed better. Against Ethan, the gap was so large that frustration gave way to reluctant respect.

Ethan shook his hand. You're strong too. You didn't win this time, but I believe you can reach your goal in the next Conference.

Hua had been unlucky and lucky at once. Meeting Ethan in the final had given him no real chance to compete and made him one of the most thoroughly beaten finalists. Yet without Ethan eliminating Tyson, Hua would not have reached the final at all. Ethan had opened the way for him and then closed it himself.

After a brief exchange, Hua stepped back and joined the applause. Ethan waved to the audience for more than a minute before they went to the podium together.

There Ethan met Pokemon League President Goodshow again. The old man's face was complicated. Congratulations on winning another Conference.

If anyone in the stadium had hoped Ethan would fail, it was Goodshow. Ethan heard the lack of warmth in his voice and smiled. Thank you. Don't worry. I'll keep working hard and try to win the next region's Conference too!

The words struck Goodshow like a knife. His smile froze. That was precisely what he least wanted to hear. Ethan planned to enter another region's tournament?

Goodshow forced an awkward smile. He would not reveal his feelings on the podium. He moved on to Hua and the third-place Trainer, whose placing had been decided the day before, shook their hands, and offered encouragement.

When Goodshow stepped aside, the three Trainers accepted trophies from the ceremony attendants in turn and waved to the audience before the ceremonial flame. Salutes boomed. Streamers and balloons filled the air. The whole stadium was wrapped in celebration.

At last Ethan posed for a photograph with his entire championship team. The Ever Grande Conference had officially ended.

It was finally over. From his first steps on Hoenn soil to this moment, this journey had reached its conclusion.

Ethan left through the competitors' tunnel and found reporters waiting backstage. They surrounded him in layers and gave him no chance to slip away. This time he could not avoid them. He answered several questions, and only when they were satisfied did he escape to the rest area.

Ash and the others greeted him with more congratulations.

Ethan, congratulations on your third Conference trophy!

Congratulations, Champion!

Misty returned his phone. It had hardly stopped ringing since his victory. In little more than ten minutes, messages had poured in from nearly everyone who could contact him. There were too many to answer now. He could thank them that evening.

For the moment, there was a familiar tradition to observe after a championship: a celebratory meal. Ash had been looking forward to it more than anyone. Ever since Ethan entered the rest area, he had been asking when they could go eat.

...

The Conference ended, but its excitement lingered. People kept discussing the matches that had stayed with them. Ethan and his friends had other plans.

Trainers did not stay still. After one night of celebration, Ethan and Ash gathered their things and prepared to set out again. Their destination was the Battle Frontier in Kanto.

The Battle Frontier consisted of seven private battle facilities outside the Pokemon League's control, gathered within one region. They were rather like privately run Gyms. Each facility had its own leader, a Frontier Brain. Defeating one earned a Frontier Symbol, and a challenger needed the first six Symbols before qualifying to face the seventh facility.

Private did not mean amateur. The facilities were well equipped, and every Frontier Brain was formidable. Some had exceptionally rare Pokemon and were stronger than most Gym Leaders, perhaps even comparable to the Elite Four. The Battle Frontier was less famous than the Pokemon League, but it was a powerful group.

Ash had learned about it from Ethan and was instantly interested. He had already planned to return to Kanto. Now he wanted to challenge the Frontier as soon as possible.

Ethan was in no hurry to enter another region after the Ever Grande Conference. The Frontier gave him a reason to revisit Kanto, and he could escort Misty home along the way.

Only Max needed to leave the group and return to his family's home in Petalburg City. He hated parting from Ash and Brock, but after traveling for so long, he knew he had to see his parents. When he turned to May, however, his sister avoided his eyes.

Um... May rubbed her cheek awkwardly. You'll have to go home without me. I need to visit Kanto too.

What? Max stared. You're going to Kanto?

May and Misty had kept their agreement quiet. The others were not particularly observant either. Apart from the two girls, nobody knew May had agreed to travel with Ethan next. Even her brother was learning only now that she would not go home with him.

Ethan, Ash, and Brock were surprised too, but none pressed her. They had seen May and Misty's friendship grow. If Misty was going home, it seemed natural that May might want to visit her there.

Max could not accept that his sister was leaving him to return alone. What do you need to do in Kanto?

May glanced guiltily at Misty and searched for an excuse. Misty pulled her behind her and lifted her chin at Max.

It's between girls. Why do you need every detail?

Max could challenge his sister, but he had no courage to argue with Misty. Still, he could hardly believe he would be going home by himself.

But Mom and Dadwill they even agree?

Misty cut him off with a wave. That's for us to handle. I'll explain it to your parents myself. Don't worry. I'll bring your sister back to you safe and sound.

Max looked at May one last time. She gave him an apologetic smile. He could only sigh.

Early the next morning, they went to the harbor at Ever Grande City. Ethan's group had originally planned to take Max home first and sail for Kanto from Petalburg. Morrison offered to handle that part instead.

You all go straight from here. I'll take Max home.

Are you sure? May asked, embarrassed.

Of course! Morrison laughed. I'm glad I met you all and glad we're friends. I can't go with you to Kanto, but I can do this one last thing for you.

Ash and the others were moved. Even Ethan thought Ash had made a truly good friend.

Max was less enthusiastic. He and Morrison did not have much to talk about, and he imagined a dull journey back to Petalburg. He wanted to refuse, but Morrison meant well and May seemed to have accepted. Max sighed again.

They watched Morrison and Max board their ship first. Max looked back with the expression of someone abandoned. Then Ethan, a smiling Misty, Ash, May, and Brock boarded an ocean liner bound for Kanto.

...

Hooooonk!

On a calm blue sea, a large liner that had crossed from Hoenn sounded its long horn and eased toward port.

It was Ethan's ship. After several days at sea, they had reached Kanto that morning. The harbor ahead belonged to a city Ethan knew well: Vermilion City.

When he had first arrived in Kanto from Kalos, Vermilion City had been his first stop. Now, after so much time, he had returned by sea again.

His thoughts were different. Back then, all he could think about was Pokemon. Now he wondered about Sabrina and Erika. They were both important women in his life. After so long apart, of course he missed them.

Yet the closer the ship came to shore, the more nervous he felt about being home again.

Ash and the others did not know what was on Ethan's mind. They stood beside him at the deck railing, watching the approaching harbor with complicated feelings of their own.

We're finally back, Ash said. Kanto was home. He returned after each regional journey to rest and begin again. He had reached only the final eight this time, but he had grown. That was something to be glad about.
