Chapter 407: The Second Eevee

Bang!

Umbreon tumbled through the air several times before crashing back to the ground and collapsing in the dirt.

On the battlefield, Jolteon stood panting as it watched its opponent closely.

After a brief but intense battle, both Pokemon had suffered injuries to varying degrees.

Umbreon had relied on its tough body, and as time passed, the moonlight shining down on it had granted it a little additional strength. Early in the battle, that power and its reckless momentum had allowed it to fight Jolteon without falling behind.

Even more troublesome was the poor visibility at night. Jolteon could not track an Umbreon that blended almost completely into the darkness. Umbreon suffered no such disadvantage and repeatedly attacked from the shadows, causing Jolteon a great deal of trouble.

This was enough to prove that Umbreon truly was strong. No wonder Zuki had possessed the confidence to challenge a champion.

Unfortunately for her, Jolteon eventually found an opportunity. It lit the battlefield with electricity, exposing Umbreon just as it prepared to launch another surprise attack. While Umbreon was caught off guard, Jolteon seized the moment to unleash a short combination and reverse the momentum.

Umbreon's body stirred as it tried to rise unsteadily from the ground.

Seeing it still struggle to its feet, Ethan gave a signal. Jolteon approached and finished it with a close-range Discharge.

The battle was over!

Zuki stared at her Pokemon for a long moment before finally recalling Umbreon.

Her brow remained furrowed, and she was clearly unwilling to accept the result.

She had meant to teach Ethan a lesson, only to discover that she could not defeat him at all!

Miki, Naoko, and the others watching nearby exchanged glances.

The battle had truly shown them the difference between the two sides.

The Pokemon had appeared evenly matched while they fought, and only after struggling for quite some time had Jolteon finally defeated Umbreon.

But Umbreon was Zuki's only Pokemon. It had remained at her side ever since she caught it and had battled countless opponents, so there was no question that its strength had been thoroughly honed. Ethan, meanwhile, was a champion. Jolteon could hardly be his only Pokemon!

Zuki had used the partner who accompanied her day and night against merely one member of Ethan's team. She had even held the advantage of time and setting, yet she had still lost. Ethan clearly deserved his reputation!

Perhaps Zuki felt humiliated, or perhaps she simply did not know how to face Ethan. Without making any harsh parting remarks, she turned and left.

Seeing this, Miki, the eldest of the five sisters, hurried forward to smooth things over.

"I'm truly sorry. Zuki has a proud, aloof personality, so she can be rather willful. She's like that with everyone. We've spoken to her in private countless times, but she always does as she pleases. She isn't a bad person at heart, so please don't take this to heart, Mr. Ethan!"

Ethan naturally had no intention of arguing with Zuki. Besides, he had offended her first, so in one sense, he had ultimately brought the battle on himself.

"It's all right." Ethan waved a hand. "Miss Zuki didn't really do anything wrong. I did say something that offended her earlier. If I have the opportunity, I'll apologize to her in person."

Ethan's humble response only made Miki and the others feel even more apologetic.

Zuki had not yet gone far and naturally heard what he said. Her steps paused briefly, but she did not look back. A few breaths later, she disappeared into the night.

Kuni spoke up. "It's already very dark. Let's hurry back inside."

Ethan checked the time. Just as Kuni had said, it was nearly eight o'clock before they realized it.

It was time to leave.

He and Erika intended to use the opportunity to say goodbye. They had already been guests for a long time and could not continue imposing on the sisters. During their earlier conversation, however, Naoko and the others had learned that they were only staying at a guesthouse. They repeatedly urged the pair to remain for the night and refused to take no for an answer.

Ethan and Erika understood that the sisters felt apologetic for Zuki's rudeness and hoped to mend relations this way. After a little hesitation, they accepted.

To Ethan, one place was as good as another. Besides, the other four sisters had clearly taken a liking to Erika. The moment they returned to the room, they pulled her into another lively conversation.

Dinner was reheated and served again.

Zuki had dragged Ethan into battle before he could finish eating, so he had yet to fill his stomach.

He took a few bites while the girls' laughter and cheerful voices filled his ears, as though the battle had never occurred. Only Zuki never returned.

"Huh? Miss Erika and Mr. Ethan aren't a couple?"

Naoko's surprised voice suddenly reached him. Ethan turned and saw Erika waving her hands as she explained.

"We're only traveling companions. We don't have the kind of relationship you're imagining."

"So that's how it is." Understanding dawned on Miki and the others. "You two look so well matched that we thought..."

Ethan had lost count of how many times people had mistaken them for a couple. He had long since stopped caring, and even Erika now looked somewhat helpless.

It was hardly surprising. When a man and woman traveled alone together, people naturally assumed there was something special between them.

After spending the night, the two did not rush to leave Ecruteak City the next day. Accompanied by Naoko, they visited Bell Tower and Burned Tower.

Setting aside its legend, Bell Tower was an ordinary tall tower. Ho-Oh was nowhere to be seen. Its only attraction was the excellent view from the top, where the entire city spread out below.

Burned Tower was even less impressive. It was merely a field of ruins with little worth seeing.

Ah, we came full of excitement and left disappointed.

Once they had toured both places, it was time for Ethan to leave the city. As they prepared to part, Naoko hesitated for a long while before looking toward him.

"Mr. Ethan, could I entrust this little one to you?"

Following her finger, Ethan realized that she meant the clumsy Eevee.

"Huh? Eevee? Why do you want to entrust it to me?"

Naoko sighed. "I'm a stage performer. Most of my time is spent rehearsing or performing, so I can't stay with it as much as it needs. If I leave it on its own, it easily gets lost. I already have an Espeon, and I truly don't have the energy to raise another Pokemon. Most of all, I want it to experience the world outside."

"Well..."

Ethan hesitated rather than accepting at once. "Miss Naoko, you should understand that I'm a Trainer. As I continue traveling and battling, I have to rotate my team from time to time. I can't promise to keep Eevee beside me every moment."

"I understand." Naoko smiled. "Whenever you change your team, you can bring Eevee back here for me to look after. This will always be its home. I simply want it to see more of the outside world."

Seeing the seriousness in her expression, Ethan crouched and looked at the Eevee.

"Would you like to come with me?"

Eevee turned to look at Naoko. Seeing the encouragement in her eyes, it faced Ethan again and cried out.

That presumably meant yes.

Ethan stroked its head and could not help smiling. He had never expected a visit to the Kimono Sisters' home to bring such an unexpected reward!

His sixteenth Pokemon was his!
