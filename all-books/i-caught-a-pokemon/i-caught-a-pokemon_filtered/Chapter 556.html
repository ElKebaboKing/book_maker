Chapter 556: Two Very Different Battles

Venusaur's appearance was no spur-of-the-moment decision.

That morning, Ethan had contacted Chloe and asked her to send Venusaur through the laboratory's transfer machine.

He was not going to leave a Pokemon he had caught behind and forget about it the way Ash sometimes did. Venusaur had gone a long time without battling, so Ethan could not be certain of its present strength. But the foundation it had built remained, and it should have no trouble in the preliminaries.

When Ethan's opponent saw Venusaur, his expression turned hopeful and conflicted at once.

From the Indigo League matches he had watched, Venusaur had seemed the most conventional of Ethan's Pokemon. It had no obvious weakness, but it had not done anything especially dazzling either.

Compared with facing Charizard, Gyarados, or Gardevoir, facing Venusaur ought to be easier.

But Venusaur was still powerful. This would be no easy battle.

When the Trainer failed to act, the referee glanced at him and urged, "Kimura, please send out your Pokemon."

Only then did Ethan learn his opponent's name.

It had been listed on the match schedule, but the man was not a notable Trainer or someone Ethan recognized from the animated series. Ethan had not bothered to remember it.

The referee's reminder brought Kimura back to himself. He quickly considered which Pokemon he should use against Venusaur.

After a few seconds, he took out a Poke Ball with a grave expression.

When he released his Pokemon, the spectators saw that Kimura had made careful use of his advantage in choosing second. He had sent out Ledian, a Flying-type Pokemon with a type advantage over Venusaur!

"A fine choice! Flying beats Grass, and it looks as though Ethan faces a tough challenge!" the announcer called, his voice ringing over Arena A. "Both sides are ready. Let's see who takes the first victory!"

...

Preliminary matches were three-on-three. In his first match of the preliminaries, Ethan fought carefully. Venusaur, Scizor, and Tyranitar defeated Kimura's Ledian, Magmar, and Jumpluff in turn, giving Ethan a clean three-to-zero win.

Kimura's Pokemon had fought hard in all three rounds, but none had caused Ethan much trouble.

Anyone could see the clear difference in strength between their Pokemon.

"Boss is as strong as ever!" Masha was excited, though the one-sided match had left him a little unsatisfied. "Even a type disadvantage barely slowed him down. No one's going to stop him from taking the title!"

"I wouldn't go that far," Misa said. She was more cautious than her younger brother. "These are only the preliminaries. The competitors' strength varies a lot. Once we reach the last eight, we'll see the truly dangerous ones."

Ethan shook hands with the dejected Kimura, nodded to the crowd, and left Arena A through the competitors' tunnel.

His first preliminary win had earned him three points. If nothing went wrong and he beat the other Trainer in his group, he would advance to the last sixteen.

For Kimura, advancing was now possible only in theory. Ethan would have to lose to the third Trainer, Kimura would have to beat that Trainer, and then Kimura would have to face Ethan again in a tiebreaker and win.

The chances were vanishingly small. Kimura could hardly imagine Ethan losing to the third Trainer, and he had no confidence that he could beat Ethan himself in a rematch.

The spectators cheered warmly for Ethan as he left. His victory was what most of them had hoped for and expected. If he ever lost a match, that would be the result they found unbelievable.

Ethan met Erika outside the arena. Side by side, they returned to the ground-floor lobby of the lodgings and watched Ash's match on the large screen.

It had reached its crucial stage.

Ash's opponent was a girl about his age named Macey. She wore her hair in two braids and had rosy cheeks. She was plain-looking, and at first glance she did not seem especially clever.

But the state of the battle surprised Ethan: Ash had been driven to the brink of defeat.

The score was one to two. Macey had lost her Magcargo, while Ash's Phanpy and Totodile had both been knocked out!

Erika stared at the screen in surprise. She had battled Ash herself and had known him long enough to understand his ability. She had never expected to see him in this position.

"This..." She searched for the right words. "I thought he might fall behind, but I didn't expect such a hard fight."

Ethan studied the battle and sighed. "Look at Macey's team. She's revealed all three Pokemon: Magcargo, Quilava, and Electabuzz. Ash chose Totodile and Phanpy in response. Totodile has the advantage against her two Fire-types, while Phanpy's Ground type gives him an answer to her counterattack."

"True." Erika nodded. "Thinking that far ahead doesn't sound like him."

Ethan shook his head. "Brock and Misty probably helped him plan it. But even with all that preparation, Macey isn't easy to beat. Now we'll see how Ash gets out of this."

As they watched, Ash sent out his last Pokemon.

It was not Pikachu, the partner he had trusted for so long. It was Squirtle, whom he had left with Officer Jenny to lead a firefighting squad!

That was a surprise.

Against Macey's Electabuzz, however, sending a Water-type as his final Pokemon hardly looked like a good choice.

Fortunately, Ash was at his best when fighting against a type disadvantage. He showed his tenacity once again.

Squirtle performed brilliantly, defeating Electabuzz despite the unfavorable matchup, then taking down Quilava with its type advantage. Ash came through the battle with a narrow victory!
