Chapter 767: An Unexpected Draw

Ethan did not want Gabby inside his room. But she had dressed for this visit and made up her mind. A few words at the door were not going to send her away.

They could hardly keep pushing back and forth in the hallway, either. Anyone who saw them might think he had called for some kind of private service. If a photograph got out, he would never be able to explain it.

Fortunately, Misty and May came down the hall at that moment, laughing together. They saw the two of them at the door.

Misty eyed the woman warily. Miss Gabby? What are you doing here?

Gabby knew Misty traveled with Ethan, but dismissed her as a girl too young to understand what was happening. She waved a hand and smiled. I just came to visit Mr. Ethan. Aren't you two asleep yet? Staying up late is a girl's worst enemy, you know.

She seemed to think that would send them away. Misty was not so easily fooled. As she drew closer, she caught Gabby's perfume and took in her clothes. Her intentions were plain.

Misty abandoned her plan to return to the girls' room. She took May by the hand and led her straight into Ethan's.

Gabby froze. Those two had gone inside? Then what was she supposed to do? Ethan had let them pass without a word, which made it even harder to believe.

She stared at him. Did he prefer little girls? Both of them at once? No wonder he had kept her out. Apparently a mature woman was not to his taste. What a strange appetite.

Inside, Misty released May's hand, went to the table, poured herself a glass of water, and drank it in one go. May sat stiffly on the bed, glancing around with curiosity and embarrassment.

She had never entered a man's room before. When she had come to Ethan for special training, she had stayed at the doorway. Now she was inside, sitting where he had clearly lain down, and her heart was beating faster.

So this was Ethan's room.

His clothes were neatly set out where he had taken them off. His belongings and a glass he had drunk from were close at hand. The room was clean and tidy, yet everything in it seemed to carry some trace of him.

Misty, by contrast, was as comfortable as if she were in her own room. She picked things up and used them without hesitation. May felt a little envious. It was not Misty's relationship with Ethan she envied; she simply wished she had an older brother she loved and could depend on. Instead, she had a younger brother who never listened to her.

Misty came over with a canned drink, handed it to May, and dropped onto the bed beside her.

You saw that, didn't you? she said with a snort. That reporter practically poured perfume over herself. She looked ready to throw herself at Ethan. There'll only be more people like her. We have to keep watch.

Even if you say that...

May forced a weak smile. If things were already like this, how was she supposed to prevent it in the future? Agreeing so impulsively that day might have been a mistake. It was too late to take it back now.

Bang.

The door shut heavily. Ethan joined them and shook his head. She's finally gone. Good thing you arrived when you did. I don't know how I would have got out of that otherwise.

Wouldn't you have liked it? Misty rolled her eyes. A beautiful woman comes to your door. Isn't that exactly what you want?

You little troublemaker! Ethan put an arm around her neck and rapped her on the head for teasing him.

May watched them tussle, a feeling she could not name rising in her chest. Would she and Ethan be like that one day?

...

On the third day of the preliminary tournament, Ethan faced his final qualifying opponent before noon. After two rounds, only sixty-four of the Ever Grande competitors remained. Empty beds filled the dormitories, and there were far fewer matches each day.

The weaker and less fortunate Trainers had been steadily eliminated. Those left behind were stronger. Ethan's opponent looked ordinary and was not someone he had encountered during his travels, but he was no weakling. Against someone else, he might well have advanced.

Unfortunately for him, he had drawn Ethan. He had drawn the strongest person in the field. Ethan had yet to make a dazzling show of himself, but at this point no other competitor was likely to get past him either.

Ash, Tyson, and Morrison all had matches at the same time, so Misty was once again the only one cheering Ethan on. Her voice alone could drown out the other supporters. She had no intention of losing on enthusiasm.

For this battle Ethan sent out Metagross and Eevee. Both had already competed, but they were among the less powerful members of his team and could use more practice. They had not fought together in his earlier matches, either. Ethan was curious to see how well this new pairing would work.

Their opponents were Sandslash and Farfetch'd. Sandslash's Ground type gave it an edge over the Steel-type Metagross, while Metagross resisted Farfetch'd's Flying-type moves. The overall matchup was difficult to call. Each pair's performance would decide it.

The opposing Trainer had clearly watched Ethan's previous two matches. Knowing Eevee and Metagross would be difficult to handle, he ordered an all-out attack from the start. That suited Ethan perfectly.

He had already helped Eevee and Metagross adjust to the stage, so he did not need to spend this match doing it again. He ordered them forward. All four Pokemon met in a head-on clash, and Ethan won.

It was a victory of raw strength. Their Trainers' orders mattered, but in close combat the Pokemon had to rely on their own reactions and condition. Eevee and Metagross, already settled into the tournament, surpassed Farfetch'd and Sandslash on both counts.

The crowd loved the change of pace. After Ethan's earlier matches, they had taken him for a Trainer who liked to draw battles out. Now they saw that he could attack fast and meet an opponent head-on.

His match ended quickly, while the others were still fighting. Ethan did not go to watch them. He took Misty straight to the main stadium. This had been the last preliminary round, and the Trainers who advanced would gather there for the next draw.

The winners arrived one after another. Ash, Tyson, and Morrison advanced as expected. By the time they reached the stadium, all thirty-two entrants in the final tournament were present.

Standing in the middle of the field, Ethan looked around. Six hundred Trainers had begun the Conference; now only thirty-two remained. The gathering space felt enormous. Many people he had met on his travels had left the stage disappointed.

Others had reason to celebrate. The remaining Trainers wore small smiles and waved to the spectators in the stands. Even the lowest finish among them would be a place in the top thirty-two. For most people, that was an honor they could accept with pride. Strength and luck had both been necessary to stand here out of six hundred entrants.

Now that the main tournament was about to begin, cameras filled the stadium. The sparse press presence from earlier rounds had given way to the full glare of the Ever Grande Conference. Flashbulbs went off so often that the draw looked like a press event. Even as the light stung their eyes, the competitors kept smiling and showed the cameras their best side.

Portraits began rolling across the big screen. Unlike the earlier rounds, the final tournament would use full six-on-six battles. Trainers would have room to use their complete teams rather than see their hopes ended by one bad exchange. They could fight freely and show the audience more of what they could do.

After a dozen seconds, the portraits stopped. Ash and Morrison quickly found their opponents. Then they saw the pairing for Ethan and Tyson and turned to them in surprise.

Ethan and Tyson would face each other in the round of thirty-two.

Ethan looked away from the screen, untroubled. Ash and the others knew he and Tyson had met only a few days ago, but even a passing acquaintance could make a hard-fought match feel uncomfortable. Both were strong, too. To Ash, Ethan was the best Trainer in the world; Tyson's performances had marked him as another contender for the championship. One of them would have to leave early.

That was a pity, but it was as far as the others' thoughts went.

Ethan knew more. In the anime's timeline, Tyson was the winner of this Ever Grande Conference. Ethan had not expected to meet him in the very first round of the final tournament. What an interesting draw.

He did not mind. An opponent was an opponent, whether they met now or later. The only thing he regretted was what the early pairing meant for Tyson. A Trainer who might otherwise have had the strength to reach the runner-up spot would now have his result fixed in the top thirty-two, missing a chance to make his name.

As for the possibility that Ethan might lose to him, the thought did not even occur to him.

If it happened, Ethan would hardly be the only one unable to accept it. The Legendary Pokemon he had beaten, and the opponents he had defeated in other regions, would be just as bewildered. By now, people who had lost to him wanted him to go further too. Losing to a champion seemed understandable and far less embarrassing.

Ethan glanced at Tyson among the competitors. Tyson looked back, his eyes bright with fighting spirit. Ethan answered with a small smile.

Come on, then. Show me what you can do.

...

After they left the stadium, Ash suggested celebrating their success, and everyone agreed. Ethan treated the group to a feast at a nearby restaurant.

Morrison heard there would be good food and shamelessly joined them. Ethan did not object. Morrison was Ash's friend, and that was reason enough to include him.

Tyson went to a restaurant on his own, perhaps wanting to keep some distance before tomorrow's match. Ethan did not press him. They hardly knew each other.

After the meal, Ethan finally agreed to speak with Alexa and Gabby at a nearby cafe. He had put the interviews off as long as he reasonably could. The world ran on relationships as well as Pokemon battles. Alexa had interviewed him twice already; he was not going to turn her down now.

As for Gabby, her attempt the night before had failed. Ethan worried she might resent him and write something spiteful, or try to charm her way in again, so he agreed to her interview too.

There was a further reason to choose her. Anyone willing to go so far for a promotion was fiercely ambitious. If Ethan could not make her a friend, he had no wish to make her an enemy. He was not afraid of trouble, but avoiding needless trouble was better.

More reporters would pursue him as he advanced. Since an interview was inevitable, he could let Gabby have the opportunity and make it known that she had his exclusive Hoenn interview. That would satisfy her and give him an answer for everyone else. One decision could solve both problems.
