Chapter 470: Advance or Retreat

The melee continued, but just as Cassidy and Butch had expected, the arrival of Misty's group changed little. Their Pokemon had caught Team Rocket off guard and caused some confusion at first, but that was the extent of their impact.

Each time they defeated one opponent, several more Pokemon surrounded them!

Before long, even the reinforcements were intercepted and drawn into battle by greater numbers of Team Rocket Pokemon, leaving them trapped within the encirclement as well.

With the overall situation seemingly decided, confident smiles returned to Cassidy's and Butch's faces.

Hmph. So what if they have reinforcements?

Against overwhelming numbers, their struggle is ultimately pointless!

While the pair remained at ease, however, the submarine's captain soon detected something unusual on the radar.

A Team Rocket member hurriedly turned around and reported loudly, "Several unidentified helicopters have been detected over the southern waters! They're rapidly approaching this location!"

Helicopters!?

The captain frowned once again and asked in surprise, "Why would helicopters appear in a place like this? Are you certain they're coming for us?"

The Team Rocket member nodded. "Judging by their flight path, they're heading directly toward us!"

"How long until they arrive?"

"Approximately ten minutes!"

"Ten minutes?" The captain sighed, took out his communication device, and contacted Cassidy and Butch outside the submarine. "The situation has changed. An outside force has suddenly intervened. You must end this quickly and resolve the battle within eight minutes!"

An outside force?

Cassidy and Butch exchanged looks, both surprised by the captain's words.

This was a deserted island in a remote area. No one would come here without a reason. From the situation, the approaching force had to be coming for Team Rocket!

But how had anyone else learned they were here?

At that thought, both turned toward Ethan in the distance.

Within Team Rocket, everyone from Giovanni himself to the most ordinary grunt regarded this man as extremely dangerous. No one wanted to deal with him.

They had even heard that the four executives had warned the rank and file that, whenever they encountered Ethan in the wild, they should flee immediately unless they were carrying out a critical mission!

If even the four executives judged him so highly, it showed just how fearsome Ethan's reputation had become within Team Rocket.

Could Ethan have arranged everything, from the earlier ambush to the approaching helicopters?

Otherwise, the timing could not possibly have been so convenient!

With that thought, the pair no longer dared act carelessly, and their wariness of Ethan deepened.

Whatever else happened, their most urgent task was to capture Lugia as quickly as possible and leave before the helicopters arrived!

Eight minutes was neither long nor short, but in a chaotic battle like this, they had no idea whether it would be enough to defeat the opponents before them.

Knowing that time was running out, Cassidy and Butch did not dare delay. At their urging, Team Rocket's Pokemon intensified their assault and launched desperate attacks without regard for their own safety!

Almost immediately, Ethan sensed the change in the atmosphere.

Team Rocket's Pokemon had thrown themselves into a frenzy, attacking without restraint. Something must have happened to provoke such a dramatic shift in their offensive!

There was no need to guess. Brock had to be approaching with the real reinforcements!

His confidence surging, Ethan told Ash, "The International Police are here. It's time for us to fight back!"

Ash's spirits rose at the news. He nodded, then directed the battle with even greater energy.

Both sides fought with renewed ferocity and abandoned all restraint. In only moments, the beach of sand and reef became covered in battle scars of every size, leaving the entire area completely transformed!

Countless rocks were pulverized by the barrage of moves, while powerful energy evaporated the nearby seawater!

Time passed little by little.

One side attacked while the other defended. Their struggle over Lugia became a fierce battle for territory. As Team Rocket attempted to break through to Silver, the ground around it changed hands several times in only a few minutes!

More than a dozen Zubat gathered into a living tornado and swept forward with piercing shrieks, seemingly hoping to reach Silver through sheer momentum. They had traveled only halfway when more than a dozen attacks scattered the formation in every direction.

"...Bzzzt... Cassidy! Haven't you captured Lugia yet!?"

The captain's impatient voice came through Cassidy's earpiece. She checked the time. Five or six minutes had already passed, yet they had neither approached Lugia nor defeated the Pokemon before them!

"Not yet! Please give us a little more time!"

After ending the call, she slammed her fists together and gritted her teeth. "Damn it! Why are these people so difficult to deal with!?"

Butch considered the situation, then asked tentatively, "Should we retreat now?"

"What? Retreat now!?" Cassidy stared at him with wide eyes. "Are you joking? We still haven't captured that Lugia! How can we retreat early?"

Butch sighed and gestured for Cassidy to look around. "Wake up and look at the battlefield. Forget eight minuteseven if we had another hour, there's no telling whether we could defeat these people. Instead of wasting more time here, we should retreat now and prepare for our next attack!"

At Butch's words, Cassidy slowly surveyed the entire battlefield.

Just as he had said, a great many of their members' Pokemon were already injured. Almost every second, another Pokemon fell from the sky, completely unable to battle.

The Pokemon belonging to those brats had also suffered injuries, and their Trainers had recalled some of them into their Poke Balls. Yet the strongest of Ethan's Pokemon, the most troublesome opponents of all, still rampaged across the battlefield like gods of war!

"This..."

Cassidy opened her mouth, but no words came. She knew the situation had turned against them, especially with the helicopters about to arrive. Once that happened, their position would collapse and become even worse!

She simply could not accept it. They had already fought for so long. If they gave up now, all their effort would have been wasted!

Should they continue attacking or retreat?

The two choices flashed repeatedly through her mind. In the end, Cassidy followed reason and chose to retreat.

Just as the pair prepared to give the order, however, they discovered that leaving would no longer be so easy.

After the chaos of the battle, the two sides had become completely intermingled. Misty's group had also formed an encirclement from the outside, trapping Team Rocket's Pokemon and members in a mire. Even if they wanted to flee, they could not safely withdraw at a moment's notice!

The realization instantly left both Cassidy and Butch drenched in cold sweat.

So that's it! Was this that man's true objective!?

He pinned us down from both inside and outside, all so his reinforcements could capture every one of us when they arrived!?

How vicious!
