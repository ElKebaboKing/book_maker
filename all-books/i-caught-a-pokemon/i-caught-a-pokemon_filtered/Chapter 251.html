Chapter 251: The Magikarp Submarine

Ethan and the others joined Quackenpoker at the lakeshore. They did not need binoculars to see what was happening. Under the clear moonlight, one patch of the Lake of Birth and Evolution looked as though it were boiling. Bubbles rose and burst across its surface.

The maddening mechanical hum came from beneath them.

Misty pulled her clothes tighter against the cold. "Mr. Quackenpoker, does this mean the Magikarp are starting to evolve?"

He hesitated. "They should begin evolving around this time, but that patch of water isn't behaving normally. It doesn't look like a sign of evolution."

His voice sounded a little hoarse. Ethan glanced over as the old man lowered his binoculars. Quackenpoker seemed alert despite staying up all night, but his eyes were bloodshot.

The rumbling grew louder, and the lake churned more violently. Before anyone could work out what it meant, a gigantic Magikarp burst through the surface, beams of light blazing from its eyes.

Splash!

Water streamed off its body and down its gaping mouth. For a moment, it looked monstrous. Then Ethan and the others noticed the seams in its metal plates, the screws set into its scales, and the searchlights mounted where its eyes should have been.

It was no giant Pokemon. It was a Magikarp-shaped submarine.

"A submarine?" Quackenpoker stared. "Why would one be here?"

Ethan's gaze moved to its front. Between the two searchlights was a red letter R. That settled it. He had seen the trio's familiar vehicle plenty of times. Where Ash went, Team Rocket never seemed far behind.

"Not them again," Ash said through clenched teeth.

"Team Rocket?" Quackenpoker had never heard the name. "They don't sound like good people. Who are they?"

"Don't ask!" Ethan called, but he was too late.

The submarine's lights flared. Voices boomed over a loudspeaker.

"Since you asked so sincerely, we'll gladly tell you!"

Ethan's heart sank. He was not afraid of what Team Rocket might do. The problem was that speaking to them seemed to trigger their favorite routine, and once it started, they would recite their motto at full volume without a trace of embarrassment.

Sure enough, they had begun.

"To protect the world from devastation!"

"To unite all peoples within our nation!"

"To denounce the evils of truth and love!"

"The cute and charming villains!"

"I'm Jessie!"

"And I'm James!"

"We are Team Rocket, blasting through the galaxy!"

"A white hole! A white future is waiting for us!"

"That's right, meow!"

Ethan could not see them inside the submarine, but their voices made it easy to imagine how smug they looked.

"We've heard that speech enough times!" Ash shouted. "Team Rocket, what are you doing here?"

"Building our Gyarados army, of course!" Jessie replied. "When it's ready, Team Rocket will rule the world. Everyone will tremble beneath us! Ahahaha!"

"A Gyarados army?" Tracey demanded. "So that's your plan. Are you the reason the Magikarp haven't evolved yet?"

"You make it sound so nasty," James said, pushing Jessie aside to take the microphone. "This isn't a protected area or anyone's private property. We got here first. We didn't steal a thing. We caught those Magikarp fair and square!"

More Magikarp were still swimming into the lake, but after hearing James, Ethan saw how sparse their numbers had become. There were fewer than he had seen earlier.

"You caught them?" Quackenpoker was furious. "So you are behind this! Release those Magikarp at once!"

"Mind your own business, old man," Meowth sneered. "Hand over your Pokemon and Pikachu while you're at it, or we'll make you sorry!"

Ethan could not understand how the trio could still challenge them so boldly after failing so many times. Each defeat seemed to make them more determined. Give them a short rest, and they would be back, full of energy. Whatever their aim, that refusal to give up was almost admirable.

Quackenpoker was not intimidated. "Not a chance! Release the Magikarp, or I'll call the police!"

"The police?" The trio broke into laughter. Jessie could barely catch her breath. "Old man, we're criminals! What good will calling the police do? This island is far from any town. We'll be gone long before anyone gets here."

Quackenpoker was a first-rate Pokemon Watcher, but outside his field he could be surprisingly naive. Out here, he thought a call to the police would solve an immediate threat. Even Ash knew better.

Ethan had understood the hard lesson on his first day in this world. When danger was in front of you, waiting for someone else was no use. You needed the strength, and the Pokemon, to deal with it yourself.

He patted Quackenpoker on the shoulder and shook his head. "There's no point arguing with them. Right now, we have to handle this ourselves."
