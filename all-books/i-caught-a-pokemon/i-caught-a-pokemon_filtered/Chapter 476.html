Chapter 476: The Base Is Flying!

On the screen, Ethan's Pokemon were charging deeper into the base, smashing through everything in their path. None of their opponents could withstand even a single exchange. Butch frowned. "Dr. Kimba, what should we"

"It's Dr. Namba!" Dr. Namba cut him off before he could finish. He glared at Butch and Cassidy. "You two are useless! Not only did you fail to capture Lugia, you led those people straight here!"

"We didn't lead them here..." Cassidy began to protest, but Butch hurriedly pulled her back.

He knew arguing would accomplish nothing now. With the enemy at their door, they could not afford to start fighting among themselves.

Butch kept signaling to her with his eyes. Cassidy swallowed her anger, sighed helplessly, and stopped arguing with the base's commander.

Once his partner had calmed down, Butch rubbed his hands together and gave Dr. Namba an ingratiating smile. "Yes, yes, you're absolutely right... So what do we do now? Should we retreat?"

"Retreat!?" Dr. Namba rejected the suggestion outright. "Not yet. There's a great deal of important research data and results here. We need time to move them. You two, take every member we have left and stop the intruders. Whatever happens, don't let them reach the core area!"

"Huh? Us?"

Cassidy and Butch froze. After their recent defeat, neither had much courage left to face Ethan's group again.

"Of course you. Who else, me?" Dr. Namba snorted. "Remember, hold them off as long as you can. The data needs enough time to be moved!"

Butch and Cassidy exchanged a look. They were deeply reluctant, but they understood how important the research was to Team Rocket. They could only steel themselves and obey.

As soon as they had left the command room, Dr. Namba turned to a nearby grunt. "Prepare the submarine. We have to evacuate immediately!"

"Huh?" The grunt stared at him. "Didn't you just order Cassidy and Butch to eliminate the intruders? We're leaving now? What about the data?"

"You're all idiots!" Dr. Namba cursed. Then he tapped his temple. "The data is all in my head. As long as I get away safely, you can consider it moved. Understand?"

"Then why did you send Cassidy and Butch out? Couldn't we just leave now?"

Dr. Namba looked back at the large screen, a cruel expression crossing his face. "Hmph. Giovanni and so many executives have failed to stop these people. How could those two possibly manage it? Sending them out will teach the failures a lesson. Besides, I've prepared a little gift for our visitors from afar. I'm certain they'll enjoy it!"

...

In one of the base's corridors, Ethan and his companions directed their Pokemon through a fierce battle with Team Rocket.

Since being discovered, they had advanced another dozen meters and climbed a staircase to the base's second floor.

Here the resistance was particularly strong. Every few steps, they had to defeat several more Team Rocket members.

Scorch marks, cracks from impacts, and traces of electrical attacks covered the walls. The floors and walls had been damaged, exposing cables in several places. Behind the group lay more than a dozen fallen grunts and dozens of defeated Pokemon.

The fighting had been intense!

Seeing Cassidy and Butch again made Ethan's group realize that the base had probably mobilized every available fighter to stop them.

The pair brought more than a dozen people and completely blocked the passage ahead. Ash and the others were not frightened in the least. They flexed their hands, eager for another fight. Team Rocket's members, on the other hand, feared that a fierce battle might rupture the base and flood it with seawater, so every one of them acted cautiously.

"Damn it! You followed us all the way here!" Butch glared at Ethan's group as though he wanted to tear them apart.

"What, are you surprised?" Ethan smiled and shook his head. "When you fled in such a hurry, you should have known we wouldn't let the matter end there!"

In truth, neither Cassidy nor Butch had expected to be followed. They had escaped in a rush aboard a submarine and had never imagined anyone could track them.

Still, their assignment was to delay Ethan's group for as long as possible. Since Ethan had not attacked immediately and seemed willing to talk, they played along.

Erika saw through their intentions at once.

She leaned toward Ethan and whispered, "They're stalling for time."

Ethan nodded slightly. He had noticed it too. He did not know what Team Rocket was planning, but he had no intention of letting them succeed.

Just as he was about to order an attack, the floor shifted beneath his feet. A violent tremor followed, as though some enormous machine had suddenly started and lifted them all!

"Ethan, look outside!!"

Misty cried out and pointed toward a window in the corridor. She looked as though she had seen something impossible!

Ethan hurried over and looked out at the seabed. Everything below them was falling away. Nothe entire base was rising rapidly toward the surface like a spacecraft!

Ash and the others joined him at the window and exclaimed, "What's going on!?"

At first, they suspected a Team Rocket trap or some secret weapon. But when they looked back, Cassidy and Butch appeared just as bewildered. Clearly, neither knew what was happening.

The two even lost their footing and nearly fell!

Noticing Ethan's group watching them, they quickly regained their balance and pretended nothing had happened.

In private, however, they exchanged an instinctive glance.

Cassidy whispered, "What's happening? Isn't this the base's secret escape system? Why has it been activated already!?"

"I don't know..." Butch shook his head. "Maybe Dr. Rumba has some other plan. Never mind that for now. This place isn't safe anymore. Let's finish our assignment and get out of here!"
