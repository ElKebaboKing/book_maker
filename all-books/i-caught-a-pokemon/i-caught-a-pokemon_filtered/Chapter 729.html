Chapter 729: Tate and Liza

Ethan and Misty left the island that held Sky Pillar on Dragonite. They flew through the night to a nearby island covered in green, pitched a tent, and rested there. The next morning, after recovering their strength and eating breakfast, they set off again.

Where next? After some thought, Ethan chose the Mossdeep Gym in Mossdeep City.

Neither Shelly nor Courtney had sent any news, so Archie and Maxie evidently had not begun to act. Ethan had prepared for them, but there was no reason to wait idly in the meantime. He could use the interval to challenge a Gym and win another Badge.

He had planned to go to Fortree City, but Mossdeep was closer to his current position at sea. Plans did not always survive a change in circumstances. It seemed he would be following Ash's route after all, although Ash had probably challenged Mossdeep Gym and left already.

Dragonite raced over the wide ocean. Flying close to the water put it in a playful mood. Without warning, it rolled sideways to show off, nearly throwing Ethan and Misty off its back.

You little... Ethan rapped it on the head. Dragonite barely felt the blow. It grinned, then settled down.

A school of Mantine leapt from the water and glided alongside them for a while before returning to the sea. The breeze on Ethan's face felt wonderful.

His mood was much lighter than it had been when he crossed these waters with Zinnia the day before. The sky seemed bluer, and even the sea looked clearer. Nothing around him had changed. Catching Rayquazaand a Shiny one at thathad changed the way he saw everything.

He felt kindly disposed toward everyone. If he saw a Team Rocket grunt fall over right now, he might even stop to help them up.

...

Mossdeep City lay on an island off the northeastern corner of Hoenn. Like Sootopolis City, it was separated from the mainland, but Mossdeep sat near the region's northeastern edge. It was less bustling than the larger cities. Its green hills, clear water, and coastal setting gave it a charm of its own.

Wailmer and Wailord lived nearby. People could stand on the shore and watch some of the largest Pokemon in the sea at close range.

The Mossdeep Space Center was another of the city's famous sights. Researchers there studied space and launched rockets, making it one of Hoenn's important scientific facilities. The Sunflora weather satellite and the crewed Ho-Oh III rocket had both launched from here. Above all, its researchers were interested in Pokemon hidden deep in space and still unknown to humanityPokemon like Ethan's Deoxys.

Its location was said to have been chosen for Mossdeep's mild weather. The surrounding sea was clear for much of the year. Strong winds, heavy rain, and other extreme conditions were rare, which made rocket launches easier.

None of that was Ethan's purpose in coming. He had one destination: Mossdeep Gym.

The city's official Gym was unusual. It had two Gym Leaders, a rarity even on a world scale: Liza and Tate, the Mystic Combination. They were twins, Liza the older sister and Tate the younger brother. Perhaps their youth and their exceptional ability had persuaded the League to let them run the Gym together.

Both specialized in Psychic-type Pokemon. They were even said to understand each other's thoughts without speaking and perhaps to read other people's minds. Psychic Gym Leaders seemed to have talents of their own. Sabrina did, and so did these twins. Even Phoebe, the Elite Four member Ethan had met recently, claimed she could converse with Ghost-type Pokemon.

Defeat the twins, and a challenger would earn the Mind Badge.

After landing on the island, Ethan did not go straight to the Gym. First he called Kennedy, the secretary at Devon Corporation. He wanted help cutting and polishing the Meteorite Key Stone he had found at Sky Pillar so that he could carry it like a normal Key Stone.

He had made up his mind the day he found it. Whether the rumored cosmic energy actually strengthened Mega Evolution or not, Meteorite Key Stone sounded far more impressive than an ordinary Key Stone. That alone was enough reason for him to make the switch.

Kennedy agreed readily, without even needing to ask Mr. Stone. Ethan had done Devon an enormous favor. This little job was nothing by comparison. A word to Kennedy's staff, and they would turn the stone into a beautifully finished piece.

Ethan thanked him and ended the call. He found a reputable courier, filled out the paperwork, and sent the stone to Kennedy. All he had to do now was wait. Within a few days, he expected to have a new Key Stone bracelet.

With that arranged, he and Misty finally headed for Mossdeep Gym. As they walked, they heard people all around them discussing the same recent event. Another rocket had launched successfully the previous morning. More than a day had passed, but the news remained a favorite topic at meals and in the streets.

That's a shame, Misty said, listening to the conversations. If we'd come a day earlier, we might have seen it launch. I've never watched a rocket take off in person. It must be spectacular.

Neither have I, in all twenty years of my life, Ethan said. It is a shame, but there'll be another chance. For me, what I gained yesterday would be worth missing rocket launches for the rest of my life.

Something occurred to him. We missed the launch, but Ash and the others probably didn't. I saw you talking to May earlier. Are they still here?

Misty shook her head. They beat the Gym yesterday and already left.

So we just missed each other.

Mossdeep Gym stood on the outskirts of the city. From outside, it looked more like a science museum than a Gym. Ethan was used to that. Gym Leaders' interests, other work, and ideas about design had given most Gyms strange appearances. He had seen much more unusual buildings than this one.

Inside, his impression held. The place felt like a large exhibition hall about rockets, space, and planets, filled with models and educational displays.

A woman working at the front desk stopped writing when they entered. Can I help you?

She looked about thirty-five, with thick, slightly wavy dark-blue hair and large round glasses. Her voice was gentle. She might have been an employee, but she also had the air of someone who belonged here.

Hello, Ethan said. I'd like to challenge the Gym. Are both Gym Leaders here?

Your timing is good. They just got back. Come, I'll take you to them.

She led Ethan and Misty deeper into the building and onto the battlefield. It was even more striking than the exhibits. The arena resembled the pitted surface of the moon. Darkness around it suggested the depths of space, while large planet models hung overhead and turned slowly, as if orbiting the field.

Ethan looked around with delight. No other Gym he had visited had felt quite so immersive, as though he had really stepped into outer space.

At one side of the arena, a boy and a girl in pale-blue martial arts outfits were practicing. They looked about ten years old, with similar fine features. Their hairstyles differed, and the girl had the slight beginnings of a figure, but otherwise they were difficult to tell apart.

These had to be Tate and Liza.

Liza, Tate, you have a challenger, the woman called.

They looked over together. Coming, Mom!

Mom? Ethan and Misty turned back to their guide. They had suspected she was more than an ordinary employee, but neither had expected her to be the Gym Leaders' mother. Looking closely, Ethan could see the family resemblance, especially in their dark-blue hair.

The girl came forward first and nodded to them. Hello. Welcome to Mossdeep Gym. I'm Liza, one of the Gym Leaders.

And I'm Tate, the other Gym Leader, the boy said.

I'm Ethan, your challenger.

I'm Misty. I'm traveling with him.

After those polite introductions, they moved straight to the match. Ethan and the twins took their places in the Trainer areas. Their mother went to the side of the field to referee.

My name is Rachel, she told Ethan. Before we begin, I need to explain this Gym's rules. First, the battlefield includes more than the ground in front of you. Those planets hanging from the ceiling are part of it too.

Ethan looked up at the models. Their size and position meant they could obstruct sight or block attacks. A Pokemon that could fly or jump high might even land on one and attack from above. The possibilities made this a truly three-dimensional battlefield. He would have to watch the air as carefully as the ground.

Rachel saw that he understood and continued. This is a Double Battle. Liza and Tate always use Solrock and Lunatone. You must send out two Pokemon at the same time. Neither side may switch Pokemon during the match. There is no time limit; the battle ends when one side's Pokemon can no longer fight.

With two Gym Leaders, Ethan had expected a Double Battle. He nodded to show he understood.

Then both sides, send out your Pokemon!

Liza and Tate released theirs almost simultaneously. As promised, they were Solrock and Lunatone, both Rock- and Psychic-types. Two flashes of blue light gave way to floating Pokemon shaped like the sun and moon.

Ethan studied them. He did not know either species particularly well. It wasn't that they were weak. They were simply obscure, had no evolutions, and looked unusual. Even when he played the games back on Earth, he caught them only to complete his Pokedex, never to use in battle.
