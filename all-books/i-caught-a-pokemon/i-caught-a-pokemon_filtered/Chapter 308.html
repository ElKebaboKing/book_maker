Chapter 308: All or Nothing

At Lance's proposal, the others exchanged glances and nodded. They had no better plan left. They would have to put their hopes in breaking the Dark Ball.

Clair attacked first. "Charizard, aim at the Poke Ball in his hand! Flamethrower!"

Charizard had retreated to the edge of the battlefield to avoid the black light. At Clair's command, it turned back, fire gathering in its mouth as it dived toward Vicious.

At the same time, Lance released an Aerodactyl and directed it to circle around from the side.

"You go too," Ethan told Gardevoir with a nod. A moment later, it used Teleport to appear behind the mechanical spider.

The three Pokemon slipped around the controlled Dragonite and converged on Vicious.

"You think numbers will win this? How naive!"

Without hurrying, Vicious took three more Dark Balls from his clothing. He opened them, and three Pokemon wreathed in black light appeared before Ethan and the others.

"My evil Pokemon, destroy the enemies in front of you!"

Ethan watched Vicious closely as he brought out the balls. From a distance, he had not seen how the man handled them. Now he realized that the Dark Balls containing Pokemon had been fixed to Vicious's red vest.

Before being enlarged, they hung across its front like buttons, almost as if they were decoration. Whenever Vicious needed one, he plucked it free and expanded it in his hand. He had carried at least six filled Dark Balls in plain sight. After this release, only two of those button-like balls remained.

But Ethan glanced at the pouch on Vicious's waist. A man like him surely carried spare, empty Dark Balls beyond those six.

By then the black light had faded. The new Pokemon were a Houndoom, an Ursaring, and a Beedrill.

Ethan had seen Beedrill plenty of times, so he paid closer attention to the other two.

Houndoom resembled a lean black dog with two curved horns. It had sharp claws and fangs, bony ridges along its body, and a thin, long tail like those depicted on little devils and succubi. Ethan had heard that its designers drew on hellhounds and the black dogs of European legend.

Ursaring looked much like a large brown bear. Its defining feature was a yellow ring on its belly. Tufts of longer fur at its shoulders resembled armor, and its tail was somewhat larger than an ordinary bear's. Otherwise, it was not so different from the brown bears Ethan had seen back on Earth.

All three were ordinary species that could often be found in Johto's wilds. Now, though, black light shone in their eyes. The Dark Balls had clearly seized their minds and greatly increased their strength. The moment they appeared, they blocked the attacking Pokemon.

Seven Pokemon clashed, moves exploding in every direction. For a time, neither side could get the upper hand. Enhanced by the Dark Balls, Vicious's Pokemon were much harder to fight than their species suggested. With Dragonite among them, his four held off Ethan's, Clair's, and Lance's three before any could reach him.

As the fight continued, Ethan noticed something else. Perhaps Vicious relied too heavily on the Dark Balls and let his controlled Pokemon fight on instinct. He did not seem skilled at directing them. He acted more like their owner than their Trainer.

He did not switch their opponents to take advantage of type matchups or arrange any tactics. He merely let them attack at will, showing off their destructive power.

With the battle deadlocked, Clair looked at Lance. "What now? Do we press harder?"

Lance sighed and considered it briefly. Then his expression hardened. "At this point, we have to. Look at his clothes. He has very few Dark Balls left. He can't have many more tricks to play."

He threw two more Poke Balls to the ground, his gaze fixed on Vicious.

I'll defeat him. I'll take Dragonite back, and I'll protect the Dragon Holy Land myself!

Clair saw her cousin's resolve. Protecting the Holy Land and helping Lance recover Dragonite mattered equally to her. Without hesitation, she took out two Poke Balls of her own.

Blue light flashed, and four more Pokemon joined the fight. The balance began to turn in their favor.

Vicious's smile faded. For once, he grew serious. Lance was right: he had few Dark Balls left. Along with the six filled ones on his vest, he had only two or three empty spares in his pouch.

But if he could turn a few more of his opponents' best Pokemon against them, victory would be easy. Their trained Pokemon would be much stronger prizes than any wild Pokemon he could catch and control.

Fools. I'll beat you, and your Pokemon will help me realize my dream!

Watching both sides commit everything, Ethan knew neither would back down now. Whatever had brought them to this point, they were all betting on the outcome of this fight.

He signaled Sabrina to wait, then took out two Poke Balls. "Venusaur, Scyther, go help them!"

Blue light flashed as the two Pokemon joined the battle. Ethan's group surged forward and surrounded Vicious's controlled Pokemon. No one held back. The fighting shook the entire valley.

Vicious removed the last two balls from his vest and released the rest of his Pokemon. Their arrival helped him recover some of the ground he had lost.

Then attacks collided in a violent explosion, wrapping Ethan and the others in thick smoke. Vicious's eyes sharpened. He pointed at Clair.

"Dragonite, attack that woman!"

The controlled Dragonite abandoned its opponent, beat its wings, and sped straight toward her.

"Look out!"

It all happened so quickly that Dragonite reached Clair in an instant. Ethan was about to send Gengar out of his shadow to protect her, but Clair kept her composure. She slipped nimbly past Dragonite's attack, leaped into a tree, and sprang between branches like a ninja, avoiding each strike.

Ethan stared in amazement. Apart from Sabrina, he had never seen an ordinary human evade a Pokemon's attacks so calmly.
