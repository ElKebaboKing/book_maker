Chapter 426: Battling Lucario

Jasmine showed no surprise when she saw Metagross collapse.

Winning against a Champion would have been ideal, but losing was hardly unacceptable.

Jasmine had watched Ethan's matches and understood just how frighteningly strong the man before her was. He had won the Indigo League without suffering a single defeat.

Besides, Metagross had not been helpless. It had driven Blastoise into desperate situations several times during the battle. Ethan had simply proven the more skillful Trainer, and her Metagross truly was not as strong as Blastoise.

What Jasmine did not know was that Ethan's Blastoise had gone a very long time without battling. If she had known, she probably could not have remained as calm as she was now.

For Ethan, the victory had not come easily either.

Jasmine's Metagross was genuinely powerful. The injuries covering Blastoise made that obvious. If Blastoise had not taken half damage from Steel-type moves, it might have been the one to fall.

Of course, Pokemon battles were like that. They were fair and unfair at the same time, and there was no room for so many hypotheticals.

Regardless of the outcome, both Trainers had acknowledged each other's strength.

Jasmine recalled Metagross and took out her second Poke Ball.

"This one is next!"

She threw the Poke Ball across the arena. Blue light flashed, and a slender, gray-blue Pokemon about 1.2 meters tall appeared on the field.

The Pokemon resembled a real-world dog, though it stood upright. A spike protruded from the back of each forepaw, with another on its chest. Its upper body was lean, giving it an imposing bearing.

Ethan recognized it immediately.

Lucario!

He had never expected Jasmine's second Pokemon to be Lucario!

This species was extraordinarily popular and widely loved. Given the opportunity, Ethan would have liked to capture one himself.

After all, who would not want to become a hero of Aura?

Ethan took out his Pokedex and aimed it at Lucario.

The Pokedex beeped. "Lucario, the Aura Pokemon. A Fighting and Steel type. Lucario possesses the ability to sense Aura. Through Aura, it can read thoughts and actions and understand human language. It can even sense the emotions of an opponent more than one kilometer away. By detecting Aura, Lucario can perceive opponents that would otherwise remain invisible. Because it understands human thoughts, it only becomes close to Trainers with righteous hearts."

Ethan recalled Blastoise while considering which Pokemon to send out next.

Lucario's combination of Fighting and Steel made it exceptionally powerful in close combat, forcing him to choose with care.

His fingers moved slowly across his Poke Balls before settling on Charizard's.

If Jasmine had saved Lucario until the final round, it had to be her ace. Ethan needed to answer with an appropriate level of strength.

Among his other Pokemon, apart from Gengar, only Charizard could face Lucario without suffering any disadvantage.

Blue light flashed, and Charizard appeared on the field. It beat its wings, rose into the air, and studied its opponent with a commanding gaze. Lucario refused to yield and glared straight back from the ground!

Once both Pokemon were ready, the referee waved his flags, and the battle began again.

The intensity reached its peak from the very start.

Ethan attacked first. At his command, Charizard used Flamethrower from the air, launching a ranged assault. Lucario responded without hesitation and met it with Aura Sphere.

A blue-green sphere of energy formed between Lucario's paws and flew skyward, colliding with the sea of flames.

The two attacks detonated in a violent explosion. The shock wave from Aura Sphere extinguished most of the fire.

With its first attack unsuccessful, Charizard searched for another opportunity.

This time, it changed tactics.

Charizard spread its wings and accelerated continuously through the air. Once it had built enough speed, it dived toward Lucario.

This was the Flying-type move Wing Attack. It closed on the target at tremendous speed and struck with devastating force. Because the attack descended from above, hitting a small or midsized Pokemon like Lucario would not only cause damage but also knock it to the ground and leave it exposed!

Jasmine hurriedly shouted, "Quick, use Detect!"

Light flashed in Lucario's eyes as it watched Charizard's every movement. In that instant, Charizard seemed to slow before its gaze like a film shown in slow motion, every detail becoming perfectly clear!

To everyone else, Charizard moved almost too quickly to see. In a single breath, it had already reached Lucario!

Lucario leaped aside and evaded the attack. As the two Pokemon passed one another, it seized Charizard's wing, put all its strength into both arms, and hurled Charizard away!

But Lucario had underestimated its opponent.

Charizard had barely been thrown any distance before it beat its wings violently and forced itself to change direction in midair. It closed on Lucario again!

At the same time, flames erupted across Charizard's entire body. It accelerated once more and slammed heavily into Lucario.

It was the powerful Fire-type move Flare Blitz. Although Charizard would also take some damage, a successful hit inflicted tremendous harm and carried a strong chance of burning the target!

Lucario clearly had not expected Charizard to counterattack after being thrown!

Unable to evade, it could only watch as Flare Blitz struck head-on. With a tremendous bang, Lucario flew backward like a kite with its string cut!

"Lucario!" Jasmine cried out anxiously.

She had every reason to worry. Lucario possessed outstanding offensive power, but its comparatively small frame was too fragile to withstand many attacks like that!

Thud!

Lucario crashed heavily to the ground and rolled several more times. As it struggled to rise, two crescent-shaped blades of air swept across half the battlefield straight toward it!

"Quick, dodge!"

At Jasmine's urgent cry, Lucario tried to leap aside. Before it could move, however, its body suddenly stiffened. Burning pain swept through it and left it unable to move!

Looking down, Lucario realized it had been burned!

Fwoosh! Fwoosh!

At that moment, the two Air Slash blades reached it. Twin impacts hurled Lucario backward once again!
