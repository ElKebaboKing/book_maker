Chapter 877: Sisters

Zuki supported Ethan as they left the dining room and headed for her bedroom.

He could still walk, but only with her help. Supporting a grown man was hard work. Fortunately, Zuki was tall enough to manage.

The effort made her blood pump faster. Whether from that or something else, she felt that something was wrong with her too.

When the cool night air touched her skin, she realized how hot she felt all over. It was almost as if the aphrodisiac had affected her as well.

Did I breathe too much of that scent?

It was the only explanation she could think of. She shook her head, trying to clear her mind, and guided Ethan along the covered walkway.

Inside her room, she settled him on a chair while she spread bedding on the tatami. Then she helped him onto the futon.

By now the drug had taken hold of Ethan. He reached for her and leaned in close. Zuki shivered at the unexpected touch. Her thoughts blurred, and she nearly lost control of herself.

What's happening?

She had thought she had only caught the scent, but she was clearly feeling its effects. In a way, it strengthened her resolve.

First, though, she had one important task.

Pulling herself away from Ethan, she went to a cabinet and took out a camera she had prepared in advance. She adjusted it, switched it on, and set it on a stand aimed at the bedding. Only then did she breathe out.

It was her last resort: evidence she could use against Ethan afterward. If she recorded him with her while he was under the drug's influence, she believed he would have no way to deny what happened. To keep it from becoming public, he would have to accept her.

She sensed movement behind her. When she turned, Ethan was already there, reaching to embrace her. She did not step away.

He pulled her close and kissed her insistently, tugging roughly at her clothes. The tension she had been holding in suddenly gave way. Desire rose through her until she could barely think.

She held him in return, answering his kisses. They fell onto the bedding together and rolled into each other's arms.

Her last clear memory was of everything around her seeming pink, and a fierce heat within her that demanded release.

...

Kuni had not gone far from the dining room before she stumbled and had to brace herself against a wall. She drew several ragged breaths before her racing heart calmed.

The relief did not last. Heat kept pressing against her reason. Her legs trembled, and Ethan's face appeared in her mind unbidden.

Part of it came from feelings she had not acknowledged. The drug stirred them further.

Startled by the image of him, Kuni broke into a cold sweat. It cleared her head a little.

Why am I thinking of Ethan?

She tried to push him out of her thoughts, but could not. The image remained, joined by a strong longing she did not want to name.

Her heart thudding, she gritted her teeth and moved slowly along the wall toward Zuki's room.

Like Zuki, Kuni assumed the fragrance in the dining room had caused her condition. She was worried about her sister, though, and forced herself onward despite how unsteady she felt.

As she reached the part of the house where Zuki's room lay, she heard strange sounds from a distance.

She had never heard them in person, but she understood what they were. They were unrestrained sounds of pleasure, and whoever made them seemed wholly caught up in it.

She really did it?

Zuki had been bold enough to carry out her plan. Yet could that voice truly be hers? The tone matched, but Kuni could scarcely imagine her cool, aloof sister sounding like that.

Almost without deciding to, Kuni went closer. At Zuki's door, she slid it open a crack. The light inside showed her everything.

She swallowed. The sight overwhelmed her, leaving her mind blank. At this distance, Zuki's voice reached her without anything to muffle it. What remained of Kuni's reason warned her to leave immediately.

When she came back to herself, she found she had already stepped into the room.

Wait. When did I come in?

Ethan and Zuki were too absorbed in each other to notice her. Kuni was relieved for a moment.

I have to leave.

But her legs carried her toward the bedding instead. They finally gave way, and she sank down beside the couple with a small sound.

As she pushed herself up and looked ahead, Ethan met her eyes. The heat in his gaze was enough to break what control she had left. Like Zuki before her, Kuni felt her thoughts disappear into the moment.

...

Miki finished clearing the table and stepped outside the dining room. The house was quiet. She assumed her sisters had all gone to bed.

She looked up. The moon was full, though drifting clouds covered most of it, leaving only a slender portion visible.

Like Kuni, Miki was near the limit of what she could bear. Her skin burned, her thoughts were muddled, and her limbs felt weak. The world seemed tinged pink.

There was no man beside her, no familiar scent to stir her further, so she could still hold on to some controlbut only just. She had even washed her face with cold water several times.

She knew nothing of the drug. She could not understand why she felt this way. Had her tolerance for sake really become so poor? She had never reacted like this before. Worse, she found herself thinking about a man.

Ethan.

What am I thinking? Miki laughed at herself. She was several years older than him. Many men preferred someone their own age or younger. Kuni and Zuki were closer to Ethan's age and might suit him better.

But he appeared to have a girlfriend already. If he had not, Miki might have tried to introduce him to one of her sisters. A connection like that would be good for their family.

She did not know how closely her thoughts resembled Kuni's.

Simply thinking about Ethan brought back the longing she had briefly suppressed, stronger than before. She shook her head, trying to drive him from her mind. It did no good.

She needed to get to bed. With a sigh, she leaned against the wall and made her way toward her room.

As she passed near Zuki's room, she heard unusual sounds. They drew her toward the door. By the time she fully realized where she was, she stood before it.

The door was open. Miki stared at the scene inside, understanding at last what she had heard.

Both Kuni and Zuki were there with Ethan. How had this happened? Was she dreaming?

She could not look away. Her mouth went dry, and something long held down seemed to surge up inside her, threatening to sweep away her thoughts.

Almost without willing it, she moved slowly toward the three of them. Her awareness blurred.

The full moon cast a silvery light over Ecruteak City.

In the sisters' guest room, Sayo rubbed her eyes and sat up. The clock on the wall showed that it was past two in the morning. She had tossed and turned for hours without falling asleep.

She usually slept well. What was wrong tonight?

She turned and saw Naoko lying awake, her eyes bright in the dim room.

You can't sleep either?

No, Naoko said softly. My heart keeps beating too fast.

Sayo felt the same. Across the room, May was sleeping soundly, though sprawled in a way that was hardly ladylike.

Could it be the sake? Sayo whispered.

Probably, Naoko said with a wry smile. We shouldn't have been so eager to taste it.

They had drunk the dosed sake too, but only one small cup each, poured from near the top of a bottle where Gengar's added liquid had not fully mixed. Of everyone who drank, they had received the least. It had only made their hearts race and kept them awake.

Who knew it would have such a strong aftereffect? Still, it had a faint rose scent, and it tasted good. Sayo wriggled under her blanket and spoke sweetly. Naoko...

Naoko sighed. You need to use the bathroom again, don't you?

Her younger sister had two small habits she had never grown out of. She disliked sleeping alone and, despite being a teenager, still shared a room with Naoko. She also woke at night to use the bathroom and was afraid of the dark, so she always asked Naoko to go with her.

Naoko could hardly refuse her sister.

They left the light off to avoid waking May. Each put on an outer layer, and they slipped out of the bedroom, one after the other.

For all its space and elegance, the large house had its inconveniences. The bathroom was a long walk down the corridor from their room.

Their footsteps echoed in the quiet passage. Tonight, however, they were not the only sounds.

Naoko stopped and listened. Sayo...do you hear something strange?

I do. Sayo grew nervous. What is that? Could it be a ghost?

It doesn't sound like one. I think it's coming from Zuki's room.

Zuki's room? But why would she still be awake?

Curious, the sisters exchanged a glance and turned toward Zuki's door.

As they drew closer, they made out several women's cries and moans. Neither had heard anything like it before, but both understood at once.

They stopped, blushing and at a loss for what to do.

Is that...Zuki? Sayo asked, her face burning.

Yes. Naoko faltered. But I think I hear Miki and Kuni too.

What are they doing?

The question escaped Sayo before she thought it through. There was only one man in the house tonight: Mr. Vale. But how could that be possible?

The sounds continued. The sisters stood listening for a while, embarrassed and uneasy. At last they edged toward the door. Since it was happening right beside them, they felt they had to see what was going on.
