Chapter 855: Harley: Not Again!

Ice Beam froze Banette inside a block of ice. It soon broke free from within and spat several clusters of blue flame toward Squirtle.

Will-O-Wisp could melt ice, and its ghostly appearance and name might make it seem like a Ghost-type move. It was Fire-type, though, and would do little to a Water-type Pokemon like Squirtle. Even so, May did not want to lose points. She had Squirtle use Withdraw, then Rapid Spin away from the flames.

Contest Battles were not decided simply by which Pokemon fell. Beautifully executed attacks that struck an opponent affected the score. Even a weak hit reduced a Coordinator's point bar. The losses might look small, but they could add up.

Jessie ordered Banette to use Thunder Shock again, hoping its type advantage would bring Squirtle down. Golden electricity formed around Banette and shot toward its opponent. Squirtle countered with Bubble. The bubbles poured from its mouth and collided with the current in midair.

To everyone's surprise, the Water-type attack overpowered the electricity. It drove the current back, then carried on to strike Banette.

What!?

Jessie stared from the stage. Harley's expression changed in the stands. Had May's Squirtle really become this strong? Electric-type moves had the advantage against Water types. How had Bubble pushed one back?

His confidence faltered.

Jessie, meanwhile, showed her own peculiar talent. After Banette was hit, she scolded and berated it. The abuse from its temporary Trainer made Banette furious. Jessie seized on that anger and ordered Frustration.

Banette rushed Squirtle and attacked in a frenzy of punches, kicks, and headbutts.

Frustration was a Normal-type move that almost any Pokemon could learn. It let a Pokemon pour its dissatisfaction into an all-out attack. With little thought for technique, it used whatever could strikehands, feet, knees, head, or tail. The weaker its bond with its Trainer, the stronger the move became.

Many Trainers and Pokemon would never use it. Jessie and Banette had no bond to speak of, and Banette was only on loan, so neither hesitated.

Squirtle was caught off guard and took several hits. The attack did damage, but its wild flailing had little of the beauty a Contest rewarded. May's point bar fell only a little.

After her first moment of alarm, May regained her composure. Squirtle withdrew into its shell, avoiding Banette's reckless blows. Then it used Rapid Spin to knock Banette away, breaking the attack and hurting it in return.

It was a sensible response, though a fairly standard one for a Pokemon with a shell.

At a distance again, Banette used Will-O-Wisp to force Squirtle out. The spinning shell was a difficult target. The ghostly flames swept past without touching it.

Jessie changed tactics and tried Thunder Shock. Squirtle's shell was ricocheting unpredictably across the stage, too fast for the electricity to catch.

At one moment the spinning shell flew above Banette. May saw her chance and gave a command. Powerful jets of water burst from the shell as it spun, and Squirtle crashed into Banette.

A Normal-type attack alone could not harm a Ghost-type Pokemon. The added water changed the spinning shell's impact, and Banette felt it. The move was far more spectacular than its own Frustration. Jessie's point bar dropped sharply.

Harley watched in growing distress. The scores on the screen plainly favored May, and the clock was running down.

Jessie had clever ideas, but to him her battle commands lacked any strategy. She seemed to know only how to keep attacking. Harley was certain he would never have handled Banette so poorly himself. Now he could only hope that Banette and its unreliable temporary Trainer somehow turned things around.

Come on. Try harder!

His hopes went unanswered. Since beginning to travel with Ethan, May had changed more than her style. Watching him battle had broadened the way she thought. She had not been tempered by many great battles, but she now fought more like an experienced Trainer, gave commands more easily, and recognized an opponent's weaknesses.

After several intense minutes, she spotted another opening. Squirtle used Bubble once more. Jessie ordered Banette to dodge the stream, unaware that the bubbles were bait.

While Banette concentrated on avoiding them, Squirtle fired an unexpected Ice Beam and froze it in a block of ice again. This time May did not let it escape. Squirtle followed immediately with Water Gun, striking the block and sending it off the stage.

As it happened, Banette broke free just as Water Gun landed. The stream hit Banette directly, carrying it and the shattered ice out of bounds and into Jessie.

Banette was not knocked unconscious, but it had left the stage, and Jessie's point bar was empty. The match had turned in an instant.

Jessie stamped her feet in fury. In the stands, Harley slammed a fist against his seat. What was wrong with her? He had lent her his Pokemon and given her the perfect chance, and she had still lost to May!

Before Lilian could announce the result, every light above the stage went out. Darkness filled the hall.

The audience was confused. Harley, though, had a dreadful sense that he had seen this before.

Two spotlights came on and converged on Jessie. Music sounded from somewhere, and James, now wearing his Team Rocket uniform, leaped onto the stage and stood back-to-back with her.

Jessie threw aside her costume pieces in one swift change and stood in her familiar Team Rocket uniform. They had even put roses between their teeth, striking extravagant poses.

I heard someone ask, What's going on?

The brave, blooming flowers of evil!

The lovely, tough villains!

Jessie!

The world!

The universe!

Waits for us!

Wobbuffet!

All three members of Team Rocket stood exposed before the entire Mulberry Contest Hall.

Unlike her appearance at the Saffron Contest, Jessie showed no shock or sadness over her defeat. She laughed loudly instead.

It's a little disappointing that I didn't win, but that's all right. The mission's accomplished. Sogoodbye!

She recalled Banette into its Poke Ball. James pressed a detonator, blasting a large hole in the roof. Bits of masonry fell, and the audience felt the cool night wind. The trio caught a rope, climbed up through the hole in moments, and disappeared.

Harley stared, open-mouthed. The scene and their exit were so familiar that it felt as though the Saffron Contest was happening again. The memory stung.

He could not understand how Meowth was here. Hadn't he locked Meowth in the warehouse? And how had these three made off with his Pokemon again?

A gust of night air made him shiver and jolted him back to himself.

Cacturne! Banette!

This betrayal hurt worse than the last one. Harley felt as if a knife had gone through his heart. He had trusted them, and they had fooled him all over again.

He did not even stop to glare at May. He sprinted for the hall doors and set off after Team Rocket once more.

After he left, the staff restored the lights. The spectators looked from the empty stage to the hole in the roof, unable to make sense of what had happened.

Lilian had witnessed the Saffron Contest incident, though, and quickly drew their attention back to the result.

It seems Jessie chose a rather unusual way to leave after her loss, she said lightly. Unexpected, perhaps, but certainly as flashy as her name! And now the result is clear. Let's congratulate May, the winner of this year's Mulberry Contest!

Cheers and applause filled the hall. May waved to the crowd, smiling brightly. The match had taken an unexpected turn, but she had won her fifth Ribbon. At last she had qualified for the approaching Grand Festival, just in time.

...

After the awards ceremony, May left through the contestants' passage and found Ethan waiting outside. She carried her new Ribbon and a booklet. Holding both up for him to see, she could hardly hide her excitement.

Congratulations, Ethan said. You keep getting better. If you perform like that at the Grand Festival, you might even win it.

I've learned a lot from traveling with you, May said with a smile. When I battled Jessie just now, I imagined I was you. I kept asking myself how you'd fight Banette. Before I knew it, I'd won!

Ethan felt both pleased and proud. May saw him as an example to follow; perhaps the path he had taken was a good one after all.

But something still feels strange, May added.

Strange? What do you mean?

Team Rocket, of course. When I traveled with Ash, those three were always causing trouble. This time they didn't interfere with us or wreck anything. Even after losing, they just slunk away. That's not like them!

It was new to May, but Ethan was used to it. And had the trio really done nothing? Harley was probably running himself ragged chasing them across town. He had schemed against May for so long, only to be played by Team Rocket twice.

Wherever the two sides ended up, Ethan was sure they would fight again. Villains had a way of making each other suffer.

He shook his head and looked at the booklet in May's hand. What's that?

A guide to the Grand Festival. Mr. Contesta gave it to me at the awards ceremony.

I see. Ethan nodded. With that in hand, you're officially a Grand Festival contender now.
