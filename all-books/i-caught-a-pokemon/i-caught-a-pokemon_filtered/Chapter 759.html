Chapter 759: May's Battle

As Vivian declared the match open, the timer at the edge of the field began to count down.

5:00... 4:59... 4:58...

Harley gave the first commands. Banette used Screech, followed by Cacturne's Bullet Seed. The attacks swept toward Bulbasaur and Beautifly before May could issue an order, forcing her to defend.

Last night's training paid off immediately. Her Pokemon had spent so long avoiding Gardevoir and Gengar that they calmly evaded both attacks. Harley's opening failed.

Bulbasaur struck back. Two vines shot from its bulb and caught Cacturne and Banette around the ankles. Harley told them to dodge, but the vines were too fast. Before they could break free, Beautifly sent a Silver Wind into them. The strong gust hit both for a solid blow.

May kept the combination going. Bulbasaur spun like a top, flinging the two bound Pokemon into the air. As they whirled, Beautifly struck them with a second Silver Wind. The wind accelerated their spin, and they crashed heavily to the ground.

The crowd cheered. Spectators admired the stylish combination, and the judges nodded approvingly. A large portion of Harley's yellow point bar vanished. May had scored first.

Yes! Misty cheered from the stands. Keep this up and she'll beat Harley for sure!

Caroline was pleased too, but puzzled. How did May improve so much in one night?

Max hurried to explain Ethan's training session. Caroline looked at Ethan with gratitude. Thank you for taking care of May.

Several people sat between them. Ethan leaned forward and waved the thanks away. May has talent, and she worked hard for this. I only helped a little.

As the adults spoke politely about May, Misty fell thoughtful. She seemed to be seriously considering something.

The battle continued. Less than four minutes remained. Cacturne and Banette staggered to their feet, still dizzy from their spin and unable to locate their opponents properly.

Ethan had taught May to press an advantage. As Harley shouted for his Pokemon to recover, a storm of petals swept around them. Petal Dance dealt damage, and another section of Harley's point bar disappeared.

The match was going entirely May's way. That was not necessarily because Beautifly and Bulbasaur were stronger than Cacturne and Banette. Her attacks flowed so smoothly from one to the next that Harley could find no opening. Interrupt her rhythm once, and he might stop the assault. But May had yet to make a single mistake, leaving him to watch his Pokemon take hit after hit.

He did try to counter. Once Cacturne endured the petals long enough, Harley ordered Pin Missile. Needles shot into the swirling flowers and shredded some into fragments that drifted down.

It bought only a moment. Even a cactus covered in spines could not shoot down every petal. While Cacturne was occupied, May launched her finishing combination.

Bulbasaur used Razor Leaf. Beautifly used Silver Wind. Wind lent speed to the leaves, and the leaves gave the wind force. The intertwined attacks struck Cacturne and Banette in an instant. Neither could withstand them. Both fell on their backs, unable to battle.

Harley's face flushed and paled by turns. He stood frozen, unable to accept the result. But Vivian's voice rang through the arena.

Harley's points have fallen to zero! May advances to the next round!

Applause rose from the stands. Ash, Misty, and their friends clapped and cheered. Seeing May defeat Harley fairly on the stage was satisfying after everything he had done.

Ethan glanced at the clock. It had stopped at 2:27. He could not remember how long May's match with Harley had taken in the anime, but surely it had not ended this quickly or easily. Their training had made a difference. He felt a small glow of pride. Perhaps, when he stopped traveling, he could make a living teaching new Trainers.

May celebrated with her two Pokemon. Harley recalled his own, plainly bitter. He glared at her once and left. Unlike his private battle with Ethan, this loss had happened before an entire arena. He could not pretend it had ended otherwise.

May soon led Beautifly and Bulbasaur backstage to rest. Victory was worth celebrating, but she had more matches today. After this round, the final sixteen would be paired again, then reduced to eight and four. The champion would be decided today as well. There was no time for her to rejoin her friends; she needed to recover and prepare.

Other pairs followed her onto the field, including Drew and Robert. Both performed steadily, beat their opponents by wide margins, and advanced.

Just over an hour later, May returned to face a plump Coordinator named Anthony. Ethan had never met him. Ash and his friends had helped Anthony recover Ribbons stolen by Team Rocket on the day of the preliminaries, and had become friendly with him. May had not helped with that incident and had only met him a few times. In any case, even friends had to compete seriously here.

Anthony was roughly as capable as Harley, with the strength to reach the top eight. Unfortunately for him, May had momentum. She maintained the form she had shown against Harley, using Combusken and Bulbasaur to defeat his Swalot and Pinsir.

Anthony gave her a difficult match, though. He hid Pinsir inside Swalot's mouth and used the two together in an unusual combination that troubled May for a time. She held firm, then seized the instant Pinsir emerged from Swalot's mouth to overcome them both.

The timer showed 0:37 remaining. It had been one of the closest matches so far. May's point bar had fallen by nearly half. But a win was a win, and she had reached the top eight.

For a newcomer, it was an excellent achievement. At a Festival of this scale, May had already surpassed Ash's result at his first Indigo League tournament. She deserved to celebrateand unlike Ash in that tournament, she still had a chance to push on toward the title.

Drew and Robert battled again soon after and advanced to the top eight as expected. The competition was now much fiercer. None of the eight remaining Coordinators was weak, and the next draw might decide who had a path to the final. A gentler matchup could carry someone forward; drawing Robert might end a run immediately.

Contestants and spectators alike held their breath as the portraits spun across the large screen. The four quarterfinal pairings appeared.

May's opponent was Drew.

Everyone with Ethan was startled. They knew the shrinking field made a meeting likely, but had not expected it already. May had just fought Harley, and now she had drawn the other person with whom she had a difficult history.

Ethan alone was unsurprised. The anime had paired them here too. It felt like a fated encounter. Just as Gary had been Ash's early rival in Kanto, Drew was May's. They seemed bound to meet.

May was backstage, so they could not see her reaction. It was easy to imagine her mixed feelings.

The quarterfinals began after a short break. At least May was not in the first match. She had a few more minutes for her Pokemon to recover and to plan against Drew. It was not much. Once the first pair finished, May took the stage.

Even on the big screen, her expression looked heavier than in any earlier match. The confidence she had carried against Harley and Anthony was gone. She pressed her lips together, grave and tense.

Max swallowed. May looks really nervous. I've never seen her like that.

Of course she is, Ethan said. She's facing Drew. He's consistently performed better than she has. At a moment like this, anyone would feel the pressure.

At Vivian's signal, both Coordinators released their teams. May chose Skitty and Combusken. Drew sent out Roselia and Flygon.

Ethan frowned. He did not know May's reasoning. Skitty and Combusken were not at a clear type disadvantage, but Bulbasaur and Beautifly had trained together the previous night. The coordination they had developed might have served her better.

Did she have another plan? Or were Bulbasaur and Beautifly too tired after their earlier matches, leaving her no choice?
