Chapter 600: The Amazing Pelipper

Misty and Anthony took their places on opposite sides of the field. Anthony, of course, would battle with Pelipper. After a moment's thought, Misty sent out Starmie.

Apart from Psyduck, which needed a headache to bring out its true power, and the Gyarados she had caught later, Starmie was perhaps her strongest Pokemon. It had accompanied her throughout her travels and was one of the partners she trusted most.

The two Pokemon stared at each other. Starmie was wary; Pelipper looked far more confident.

Misty studied Pelipper for a while, still skeptical. Even now she could sense nothing particularly powerful about it.

She glanced at Anthony and found him looking just as confident as his Pokemon. She decided to stop worrying about it and gave her first command.

Starmie, use Swift!

The large gem at Starmie's center flashed. A shower of glittering stars shot from one of its points toward Pelipper.

Both Pokemon were Water types, so Misty had chosen not to attack with Water-type moves. She intended to rely on Starmie's Normal- and Psychic-type moves to settle the battle.

Pelipper flapped its wings and rose into the air, easily evading Swift.

Misty was not surprised. She stayed alert for its counterattack.

What came when Pelipper flew above Starmie astonished her. It was no Flying-, Normal-, or Water-type move. It was an electric shock!

Golden light suddenly flared inside Pelipper's enormous bill. A bolt shot through the narrow opening between its jaws and struck toward Starmie at tremendous speed.

What!?

Misty stared in shock. As a Trainer who specialized in Water types, she had made a point of studying Hoenn's Water-type Pokemon in the Pokedex before coming here. Pelipper had left a strong impression on her.

But she clearly remembered that Pelipper was a Water- and Flying-type Pokemon. How could it suddenly use an Electric-type attack? It made no sense!

Starmie tried to dodge, but the current moved too fast. It struck in an instant.

Electric attacks were strong against Water types. Sparks crackled across Starmie's body, and though it had no visible face, Misty could still feel its pain.

Starmie!

Before she could do anything else, Pelipper opened its bill a fraction again. A second bolt of electricity shot out and knocked Starmie down.

What kind of Water-type Pelipper was this? It might as well have been an Electric-type Pokemon!

Misty hurried onto the field and gathered Starmie into her arms.

She looked back at Anthony, baffled. Why can your Pelipper use Electric-type moves?

Anthony lifted his chin proudly. What's so strange about that? I told you my Pelipper is the strongest Pokemon in the world! Well? Now you've seen what it can do!

Being beaten by an attack that seemed to defy common sense left Misty dissatisfied.

She recalled Starmie and took out another Poke Ball. Before she could send out her second Pokemon, however, a flash of blue light came from her waist. Psyduck had let itself out again!

Psyduck wandered around the field, looking this way and that. It seemed oblivious to the tense atmosphere, and even to the fire in its Trainer's eyes.

You little! Misty fumed. Why do you always come out and cause trouble at the worst possible moment?

Anthony shook his head and laughed. Seriously? You can't even keep your own Pokemon under control. Can you really call yourself a proper Trainer?

That made Misty even angrier. After what Anthony had said, she could hardly switch Pokemon now. She shouted, Fine! Since you're already out, you can battle! Go, Psyduck! Show him what you can do!

Psyduck tilted its head, looked at Misty, then turned to Pelipper. Only then did it belatedly realize that Misty was in a battle.

It showed no fear. Quacking excitedly, it charged at Pelipper.

That's it! Misty cried, thrilled to see Psyduck actually eager to fight for once. Show it your strength! Use Zen Headbutt!

Psyduck ran at Pelipper with long strides, wearing an expression that was somehow both dopey and fierce. It made strange noises all the way, looking surprisingly aggressive.

But it had only crossed half the distance when two vines suddenly emerged from Pelipper's bill. They reached Psyduck, bound it tightly, lifted it into the air, and slammed it hard against the ground.

Now Misty was truly stunned.

Vines could come out of Pelipper's mouth? Was it some new variety? Or were all Hoenn's Pokemon this outrageous?

While Misty stood at a loss, Ethan watched from behind her. After observing the battle, he had begun to understand what was happening.

There had to be an unseen reason for Pelipper's impossible attacks.

The secret lay inside the enormous bill that could swallow a small Pokemon whole.

Unlike an ordinary Pelipper, Anthony's kept its bill barely open during the battle. No matter what situation it faced or who its opponent was, it never opened wide. It seemed to be taking great care to avoid doing so.

There could hardly be anything but a secret inside it.

The sight also stirred Ethan's memory. Hadn't Ash encountered something like this in the anime? A man named Anthony had come up with the absurd idea of hiding Poke Balls containing Pokemon of different types inside Pelipper's bill. During a battle, he would command Pelipper to release whichever Pokemon had the type advantage, using those favorable matchups to win.

Anthony had certainly put his scheming mind to work to devise a trick like that.

A Trainer who did not know what was happening could easily be caught off guard. This was what gave Anthony the nerve to call his Pelipper the world's strongest Pokemon.

Psyduck went down before it even had a chance to unleash its powerful psychic abilities.

Misty was still unwilling to concede. As she reached for a third Poke Ball, Ethan stepped beside her and stopped her.

Forget it. You're no match for him. Let me take over from here.
