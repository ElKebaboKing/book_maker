Chapter 267: A Copy Is Still a Copy

The roar of Ethan's Gyarados stunned the Orange Islands spectators into silence. Many clapped their hands over their ears and stared at the fierce Pokemon.

What kind of Gyarados is that?

"Whoa!" Even the announcer was taken aback. His professional instincts returned quickly, and he leafed through his notes while he spoke. "According to our records, Ethan Vale's Gyarados showed astonishing strength at the Indigo League. It defeated many formidable opponents and left quite an impression. What will it show us today?"

In front of a television elsewhere, Masha, his older sister Misa, and the biker gang watched Gyarados's entrance with wide eyes.

"Oh, wow!" Masha shouted. "Sis, look! The boss's Gyarados is even fiercer than the last time we saw it!"

Misa studied the scars of different sizes across its body and nodded. "It must have been through a serious fight. It looks stronger. Is it just me, or has it grown larger too?"

In the stadium, even spectators safely beyond the battlefield felt a trace of pressure from Gyarados. Ditto, standing directly before it, felt far more. Under that cold, merciless stare, fear gripped it before the match had even begun.

Drake frowned. "Ditto, don't back down! Use Transform!"

Ditto nodded and forced itself to concentrate. Its soft body shifted and swelled, changing until it had Gyarados's form. The original's blood-red eyes still unnerved it, but at least it no longer stood frozen in fear.

The two Gyarados looked almost identical, down to their size and scars. Ditto had copied the outer form and abilities, but its eyes lacked the original's particular ferocity.

High on one side of the stadium, the scoreboard displayed the first Pokemon chosen by each Trainer and the result slots beneath their pictures. Seeing both sides ready, the referee stepped forward.

"Trainers, prepare yourselves!"

Ethan and Drake nodded. The referee brought down the flag, officially starting the battle. Neither Pokemon waited for a command. They charged toward the pool at the field's center, bent their bodies, and dove in.

Water-type Pokemon could make better use of their strength in water. Now that Ditto had become Gyarados, it understood that the pool gave its borrowed form an advantage too.

They surfaced almost together. Ethan struck first. "Gyarados, use Hydro Pump!"

He had skipped any cautious opening and chosen a powerful attack. Drake stayed calm. "Ditto, use the same move!"

Both Pokemon opened their mouths. Pressure built, then two huge jets of water shot toward each other. They collided in front of Ditto with a crash, burst apart, and rained down over the field.

Ethan had not expected that attack to decide anything. It was a distraction. Before the spray cleared, he called, "Gyarados, close in and use Aqua Tail!"

Gyarados sped across the pool. The first exchange seemed to have convinced Drake he could match Ethan move for move. He ordered Ditto to approach and use Aqua Tail as well.

Yet a question nagged at him. Ethan knew Ditto now shared Gyarados's Water type. Why keep choosing Water-type attacks? Even a clean hit would do reduced damage. An Indigo League champion should not make such a simple mistake.

He had no time to think it through. Both Pokemon twisted and swung their enormous, water-wrapped tails. The tails crossed in the air, forming a great X. Their impact boomed through the stadium. Water sprayed from them like a sudden downpour.

"What an attack!" the announcer cried. "When those tails struck, it looked as though rain fell over the field. Beautiful! So far, the two Pokemon appear evenly matched. Neither has gained an advantage!"

Privately, he wondered if Ethan's Gyarados was all show. It had looked fearsome, but Drake's Ditto was holding its own.

Ethan heard the commentary. Evenly matched? A slight smile touched his mouth. Ditto had stepped into his trap. There was no reason to hold back now.

"Gyarados, show them your strength! Use Thrash!"

Gyarados's eyes widened. It lifted its head and roared, letting fury drive out reason. Only one thought remained: tear apart the opponent before it.

Across the field, Drake's frown deepened. Thrash? Why choose a move that made Gyarados lose control? It might be powerful, but its wild attacks would be hard to aim and could leave openings. Worst of all, the move would confuse Gyarados afterward. If Ditto survived, Ethan would have handed Drake a helpless target.
