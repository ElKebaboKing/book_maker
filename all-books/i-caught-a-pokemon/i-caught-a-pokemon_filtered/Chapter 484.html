Chapter 484: Leaving the Island

Ethan had drunk quite a bit of fruit wine at the banquet, and now he could feel its effects rising.

Maya's expression was so earnest that he could not doubt her sincerity or her resolve. The warmth of her hand in his stirred another feeling that tugged at his thoughts.

Alcohol and impulse gradually wore at his judgment. Fortunately, what remained of his reason still held him back.

He could faintly hear music drifting from the distant banquet hall. Ash and the others were clearly still enjoying the feast.

No...

This is getting out of control. I can't betray Sabrina. I have to leave now...

Before he could say anything, Maya seemed to make up her mind. She tugged gently at the sash around her waist, and her clothes slipped from her body...

Ethan stared at her, eyes wide with disbelief. For a moment, even his thoughts seemed to stop.

The two attendants exchanged smiles, quietly left the room, and gently closed the door behind them...

...

Early the next morning, Ash and the others were eating breakfast in the banquet hall.

Their bags had already been packed and stacked neatly nearby.

From the Whirl Cup through the protection of Lugia, everything they had come to do was finally finished. They could resume their travels.

It had not been very long, yet looking back made the whole experience feel strangely distant.

They waited through breakfast, but Ethan never appeared.

Misty glanced toward the guest rooms and asked worriedly, "It's this late and he still hasn't come. Should we go look for him?"

Erika shook her head. "I don't think he came back to his room at all last night."

"You don't think something happened to him?"

As they spoke, footsteps sounded. They turned and saw Maya approaching with a smile, Ethan beside her and yawning.

Ash immediately asked, "Ethan, did the succession rite go well last night?"

Ethan's expression turned awkward at once.

Before he could answer, Maya said warmly, "It went very well, thanks to Ethan's help."

Misty noticed a subtle difference.

Maya seemed much more intimate with Ethan now.

At the very least, her way of addressing him suggested that something between them had changed.

Curious, Misty asked, "Miss Maya, what kind of rite was it?"

Maya shook her head. "It's an ancient rite. Only a small number of Sea Heroes, those whose courage and strength we recognize, might be chosen by a Sea Priestess to take part."

"Oh..." Ash looked wistful. "I was so busy eating last night. I wish I could have seen it."

"Cough! Cough! Cough!"

Ash's words startled Ethan into a fit of coughing.

That's really not something you should watch, kid...

Sigh. Impulse really can get the better of a person...

With the wine giving him courage, he had crossed that line after all...

Then again, Maya was beautiful, noble, and powerful. She had remained untouched until now, wanted a child with him, asked for nothing in return, and even offered to keep it secret. It was hard not to waver under those circumstances.

In the end, though, he had only himself to blame. His resolve had not been strong enough to resist her beauty...

Noticing that something was wrong with his expression, Erika came over. "You're only arriving now. Have you had breakfast?"

"Mm... I ate a little while ago..."

"You don't look well rested. Are you tired?"

Looking at Erika's lovely face, Ethan could not help coughing again.

"Yes... a little tired. But don't worry. I'll be fine after some rest..."

Sigh...

Erika plainly suspected nothing, and Ethan was filled with guilt and unease as he looked at her.

How was he supposed to face her from now on?

...

At the harbor on Red Rock Isle, Ethan, Erika, Ash, Misty, Brock, Oliver, and Richie prepared to say goodbye.

Their time together had been happy and brief. Parting was another matter.

According to their earlier plans, Ethan and Erika would travel to Cianwood City to challenge its Gym. Oliver would return to Ogi Isle, and Richie would continue his training journey. Ash, Misty, and Brock would set out for other places and the Gyms Ash had yet to challenge.

Maya came to see them off with two attendants. While the travelers exchanged their final farewells, she stood quietly to one side.

Now and then she looked toward Ethan, only to turn her gaze away before anyone noticed.

"Take care, everyone. I'll be going first!"

Richie waved. He was returning to Kanto from here, and because of his schedule, he would be the first to leave.

"You take care too. Maybe our paths will cross again!"

Ethan and the others waved as Richie boarded his boat and departed. Then Oliver untied his speedboat, and Ash, Misty, and Brock climbed aboard with him.

Since their routes all passed Ogi Isle, Oliver could carry the three of them for another leg of their journey.

"Ethan, we're heading out too!"

Ethan nodded to them. "Travel safely. We'll see each other next time."

Their farewell was casual. All of them knew their journeys often crossed, and they would surely meet again.

After the speedboat left the dock, only Ethan, Erika, Maya, and her attendants remained.

Maya approached Ethan and said softly, "As I told you before, this won't be easy. I'll likely need your help several more times."

Neither needed to say aloud what she meant.

Ethan's gaze passed over Maya's neck. Her collar concealed it, but he knew there was a faint red mark there, left by one of his kisses the night before.

He sighed and met her eyes. "I'll come back to see you often. If anything happens, let me know right away."

Whatever Maya had said about not asking him to take responsibility, after last night Ethan could hardly leave her entirely on her own!

Maya nodded with a smile. "I understand. It's getting late. You should be on your way."

Erika sensed something unusual in their exchange, but said nothing. Like Maya earlier, she stood a short distance away and quietly waited for them to finish.
