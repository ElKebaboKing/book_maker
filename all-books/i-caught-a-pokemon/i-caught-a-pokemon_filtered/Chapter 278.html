Chapter 278: Celebrations and Interviews

After Drake and Carl left, a staff member carrying a camera came over to Ethan.

"Mr. Vale, the League needs a photograph of you with the Pokemon you used today. Could you bring them all out?"

"Of course." Ethan thought over the six rounds. None of his Pokemon had been badly injured; they could all move freely and did not need urgent treatment. They had enough energy for a picture.

He released the other five. With Gengar already beside him, all six were present. At the photographer's direction, Gyarados and Crystal Onix took positions in the back. Their height gave the picture a commanding background. Charizard and Scyther stood to Ethan's left, Venusaur and Gengar to his right.

The Pokemon held their heads high around him. Ethan raised the Winner's Cup trophy in the middle, smiling as broadly as his partners.

Click!

The staff member took the photograph. "Once it's developed, we'll display one copy in the Palace of Victory and give another to you. One last thing: which of your Pokemon would you like us to make a statue of?"

Ethan looked around. All six had performed well, making the choice difficult. After thinking it over, he picked Charizard. Defeating Drake's strongest Pokemon meant something special, and their battle had been both dramatic and memorable. Charizard was the right choice for the statue.

They discussed the details. The staff member brought out a clay slab that had not yet dried and took impressions from Ethan and each of his Pokemon. Once that was finished, Ethan was free to leave the field. He went through the competitors' tunnel toward the lounge.

He sighed. The victories might have looked easy, but the match had worn him out. Now he could finally relax.

Pop! Pop! Pop!

As he opened the lounge door, three streamers shot out from behind it and draped over him.

"Ethan, congratulations!"

He looked up to find Ash, Misty, and Tracey laughing at him, spent party poppers in their hands.

"Ash? When did you three get here?"

Ash rubbed his nose. "During the fourth round. We wanted to arrive earlier, but we were too late."

Misty looked at Ethan warmly. "You beat the Supreme Gym Leader. You're amazing!"

In high spirits, Ethan indulged in a rare boast. "It was nothing. I could have faced them with my hands in my pockets and still won!"

Show-off, Sabrina thought, giving him an amused glance.

Ethan entered and handed the trophy to Sabrina. Looking down at the streamers all over his clothes, he felt a warmth in his chest. His friends had thought to prepare this little celebration for him.

Ash and the others gathered around Sabrina to inspect the cup. It stood about twenty centimeters high and was coated in gold paint. A golden Poke Ball with two small wings crowned it. The whole thing looked heavy and gleaming.

Ash stared at it, hands clenched with longing. He did not need to envy Ethan for long; his own chance to challenge for this trophy was next.

While the others talked excitedly, Ethan took out his phone. The screen was crowded with missed calls and congratulatory messages sent as soon as his friends had seen him win. Professor Cerise, Chloe, Ren, Parker, Misa, Masha, Melody, Carol, Lorelei, Professor Ivy, and many more had reached out.

He sat on the sofa and began answering them one by one. Then Carl returned and knocked on the door.

"Sorry to interrupt the celebration," he said when he entered. "Reporters are waiting outside. Would you be willing to speak with them?"

A post-match interview was customary, but Carl remembered the awkward scene at the entrance. This time he had decided to ask Ethan before arranging it.

Ethan could hardly refuse now. Having won and drawn so much attention, he ought to say a few words. He followed Carl to the interview area. Cameras turned toward him, and flashes began to pop again.

He greeted the reporters politely, composed rather than gloating. One asked at once, "Mr. Vale, how do you feel about winning the Winner's Cup without losing a single Pokemon?"

Ethan considered his words. "An undefeated result wasn't something I set out to achieve. I just played each round as it came, and this was the outcome."

It was an answer that gave the reporters little to pick apart. They asked many more questions, and he answered each carefully. Then one asked, "What do you think of Drake?"

Ethan knew a careless answer could anger Orange League fans. He thought for a moment. "Drake is very strong, one of the best opponents I've faced. If he competed in the Indigo League, I think he could at least finish as runner-up."

"If you rate him that highly, why only runner-up?"

Ethan meant to stay modest, but a smile slipped through. "Because however hard he fights, the champion would still be me."

The reporters stirred. That was a bold claim from someone who had seemed so restrained. Yet Ethan had won both the Indigo League and the Orange League's Winner's Cup, and he had just beaten Drake. They could hardly say he lacked the results to support his confidence.
