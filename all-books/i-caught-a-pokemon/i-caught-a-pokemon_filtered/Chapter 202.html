Chapter 202: Shamouti Island

It had been a long time since Misty and Ash had seen Ethan, and they seemed to have endless things to tell him. Ethan kept them laughing, and Tracey joined in now and then. Before long, the speedboat was full of cheerful conversation.

Elsewhere in the Orange Islands, as the boat headed toward its next stop, a strange fortress floating high in the sky arrived above a small group of islands.

In its control room, an oddly dressed man studied a map with a cold smile.

"Soon my wish will come true. The God of Fire, the God of Ice, the God of Thundereven the God of the Sea. All of them will be part of my collection!"

A radar display flashed, and a synthetic woman's voice filled the room.

"Life signs detected from three Legendary Pokemon. Begin attack?"

The man rose and walked to a floor-to-ceiling window. Through the glass he looked down at the three islands, a chill in his eyes, and pressed a switch.

The command sped through the fortress's circuitry. Machinery whirred. Several gun barrels extended from its hull and aimed at one of the islands.

Bang! Bang!

Shells struck the island in violent explosions, shattering rock and trees and changing the shape of the ground. After more than a dozen shots, a Moltres wreathed in flame burst from a cave. It cried out in fury and charged toward the fortress.

The guns swung after it and opened fire. Moltres took several hits, but reached the fortress and spat a torrent of flame. As they fought, even the sky seemed to change.

...

Blue sky and white clouds hung over the speedboat. For its passengers, the journey was peaceful.

After talking until their throats were dry, and with the excitement of reunion settling, everyone had found somewhere to rest. Ethan leaned against the railing, took a drink of water, and told Misty, who sat kneeling beside him, a few amusing stories from his time in the Orange Islands.

Across from them, Sabrina watched the sea while listening to accounts of events she had shared with him. Occasionally, she cast an assessing glance at Misty.

Misty was too young for Sabrina to consider her a romantic rival. Still, seeing her sit so close to Ethan made Sabrina uncomfortable. Her eyes flicked to Misty's slightly developed chest, and she shook her head with a quiet sigh.

Never mind. She's pretty, but she's still a child. There's no need to make anything of it.

The speedboat cut through the waves for some time. At last, they could see a city on the next island in the distance.

"We're nearly there," Maren called. "Make sure you have your bags!"

She had barely finished speaking when thick storm clouds rolled over the sky. In moments, it went as dark as the bottom of a black pot.

"Huh? Is the weather changing?" Misty asked.

Ethan studied the sky and shook his head. "A change this sudden must have been caused by a Pokemon."

"A Pokemon?!" Ash and Misty stared. What kind of Pokemon could alter the sea and sky on such a scale?

"He's right," Maren said. "The sea can be merciless and unpredictable, but its changes still follow patterns. It shouldn't go completely dark without any warning."

A huge wave struck the speedboat. Everyone lost their footing and tumbled sideways. Even Maren was caught off guard and fell from the helm.

The waves wrenched the wheel around. In an instant, the boat veered off course and began following a current away from their destination.

Maren struggled to her feet and grabbed the wheel, but could not turn it back no matter how hard she tried.

"It's no use! We've lost control! I can't move it!"

Ash tried to reach her, but another great wave slammed into the hull.

"Everyone, hold on! Don't fall into the sea!" Maren shouted.

The speedboat rocked wildly and drifted farther away with the current. Lightning flashed and thunder rolled through the clouds. Wind tore across the sky, and the sea heaved beneath them. Against forces like these, they felt how small people truly were.

The boat pitched and rolled for what felt like an age. At last the wind began to weaken, and the waves eased. Only then could they raise their heads and see where they had ended up.

Maren was dizzy. When she recovered enough to look around, she found that the storm had blown them toward a different island. She studied it for a moment.

"Shamouti Island? I can't believe we ended up here!"

Shamouti Island? Ethan froze. He knew this place. Perhaps it was fate: whenever he ran into Ash out in the wild, some new plot seemed to begin.

"Miss Maren, is Shamouti far from where we were going?" Misty asked.

"Yes. It's near the center of the Orange Islands. We've been blown a long way off course." Maren nodded. "But since we're here, we might as well take a break."

The speedboat drifted into Shamouti Island's inner bay, toward a beach beside the harbor, and washed onto the shore.

Ash was the first to jump onto the sand. He let out a long breath. "We're finally safe! It feels good to be on land. That ride nearly shook me to pieces!"

"I think..." Tracey said, his voice trembling, "we may have found ourselves in a different kind of trouble."

No one understood until they followed his gaze. A crowd of strange creatures had gathered on a nearby seawall. At first glance, they seemed to have human bodies, but their heads resembled birds', and colorful feathers covered them.

Without a word, they leaped from the seawall and surrounded the travelers, staring at them with eerie eyes. Everyone backed away in alarm. Misty hid behind Ethan.

They were about to send out their Pokemon to defend themselves when a woman's voice came from one of the creatures.

"Oh? Is that Maren?"

The travelers paused. Looking more closely, they realized the creatures were people in costumes.
