Chapter 184: Empty Cans and Flying Discs

Seadra was the evolved form of Horsea, stronger and covered in hard horns and venomous spikes. Its tail curled, and its snout had changed from a round tube to a three-sided shape.

Pokemon observers said that even a light touch against a Seadra's dorsal fin could cause paralysis. At night, it wrapped its tail tightly around coral so the current would not carry it away while it slept.

Its small body made Seadra agile in the water. Blastoise was much larger and looked clumsy by comparison. But it had an advantage of its own: with its mouth and the two cannons on its back, it could fire three streams of Water Gun at once. Even if it could not match Seadra's precision, it could make up some ground with the number of shots.

Cissy seemed keen to show the champion from Kanto what she and the Orange League could do. She called to Senta, who brought out a bubble machine.

"Our Gym combines Pokemon moves with sports," Cissy said. "Win more of the three contests than I do, and you'll pass the challenge. Let me show you what I mean."

Senta switched on the machine. Six or seven bubbles, each about the size of a table tennis ball, floated into the air.

Before Cissy gave an order, Seadra sent out narrow bursts of Water Gun, each stream no thicker than a chopstick. It popped the bubbles one after another.

Ethan clapped. The bursts were short, fast, and precise. Seadra had narrowed Water Gun to a fraction of its usual width, yet still landed every shot. Its control of the move was excellent.

Most trainers tried to make their Pokemon's attacks bigger, stronger, or longer lasting. Few trained them in the opposite direction, for such fine control.

"Impressive. My Blastoise can't use Water Gun as delicately as your Seadra. But I'm here now, and I won't give up without trying."

Cissy raised her eyebrows at his admission, then smiled with a hint of pride. "So even the Indigo League champion has things he isn't good at. Perhaps a Gym Leader from a little island has something to teach him."

His willingness to concede the point seemed to have reassured her. Apart from his title, perhaps he was no different from the other challengers.

The two Pokemon floated at opposite ends of the pool. Once both were ready, Cissy called, "Begin!"

A thin stream and a thick one shot toward the rows of cans. The cans rattled sharply as they were struck and spun into the air. Their numbers on both sides fell quickly.

Ethan watched calmly. However hard Blastoise tried, it could not compress and fire its water as finely and rapidly as Seadra. But the contest counted hits, not perfect aim. Blastoise's broad stream could knock a can away even when it only grazed the edge.

Seadra began to shoot faster, and Blastoise fell behind. Ethan called, "Blastoise, fire from all three at once!"

Water Gun burst from both of Blastoise's back cannons as well as its mouth, forming a triangle of streams aimed at the cans.

Not every stream found a target, but on each volley one or two additional cans went flying.

"Sis, that's cheating!" Senta cried. "It's firing three Water Guns!"

Cissy frowned but said nothing. The cannons were part of Blastoise's body; it was not using outside help. Besides, she had never set a limit on the number of streams.

With both cannons working, Blastoise caught up. Soon it and Seadra had hit the same number of cans. They knocked away their final targets at the same time.

Cissy sighed so quietly that no one else would have noticed, then smiled. "Your Blastoise did well. This round is a tie."

Knocking down cans was Seadra's specialty. Before the contest, Cissy would never have expected it to finish level with a challenger. Still, she was confident about what came next.

Ethan thought the tie was a good result. Blastoise had never been asked to control Water Gun so carefully. He had been ready for a loss, but its three streams had brought him an unexpected draw.

Cissy pressed the remote again. Gears turned, and one wall of the Gym slid open. Beyond it lay blue sea and sky, with palm leaves stirring in the breeze. Fresh air swept into the building.

"Next, we'll test moving targets. Seadra, show them."

A machine in the corner launched a red flying disc high into the air outside. Seadra watched it rise. At the top of its flight, it drew in its belly and fired a Water Gun that shattered the disc.

Pieces fell to the ground. Ethan applauded again. "Clean and accurate. You've trained Seadra well."

Cissy lifted her chin slightly. "A moving disc is harder to hit than a stationary can. Miss the right instant and it's gone. This is our second contest. Are you ready?"
