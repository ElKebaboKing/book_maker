Chapter 531: The Soul Dew and a Sudden Crisis

Ethan noticed something familiar carved into a nearby stone tablet. He pointed it out to Lorenzo. "Isn't that the device we saw in the cathedral?"

"It is," Lorenzo said with a nod. "My ancestors built it to draw on the Soul Dew's power and protect this island. The tablet shows how the Soul Dew is truly meant to be used."

He led them to a small, separate pool and asked them to look at its bottom.

In the clear water rested a round, transparent orb on a stand. Countless glimmers of light flowed through it like drops of water.

"Is that...?"

"The Soul Dew," Lorenzo said. "It is the treasure Altomare has protected for generations."

"That's the Soul Dew?"

Ash, Misty, and Brock had never expected the mysterious jewel from the legend to be sitting so openly in a pool, with almost no visible protection. Then again, the barrier outside hid the garden, and two Legendary Pokemon lived here. Where could it be safer?

Still, was this really the Soul Dew? It looked very different from the splendid gem they had imagined.

They leaned forward together for a closer look. A railing kept them back, however, so they could only make out its general shape.

Bianca smiled. "Latios trusts you, and meeting you here must be fate. But please promise me you won't tell anyone that the Soul Dew is here."

"Don't worry," Ethan said. He and the others nodded firmly. They understood how important the treasure was and had no intention of spreading word of it. "We won't tell a soul."

They played with Latias and Latios for a while longer. When evening came, they finally said it was time to leave.

Latias immediately looked disappointed and reluctant to let them go.

Even so, Ethan and the others waved goodbye to the two Legendary Pokemon and left the Secret Garden.

Lorenzo and Bianca guided them away. Instead of heading back through the city, they took them to the nearest canal bank and into a wooden workshop.

It was Lorenzo's workplace. Besides serving as a guide, he repaired gondolas and spent his spare time here working on them. In truth, gondola repair was his profession; guiding tourists and telling them about Altomare and its Legendary Pokemon was something he did out of interest.

Lorenzo pointed to a speedboat in the canal. "Bianca, take them back to the Pokemon Center."

Ethan and the others were about to decline. They felt bad asking Bianca to take them all the way back. But a look around showed them how far they had wandered from the city, so they accepted. On foot, they might not reach the Pokemon Center before dark.

Everyone boarded the speedboat. Bianca handled it expertly, steering them smoothly along the canal toward the city center.

They did not know that, soon after they left the Secret Garden, the thieves' spider-shaped scout had already slipped inside.

The motor droned steadily. Ethan and his friends were absorbed in the scenery along the banks, so the sound hardly bothered them.

The boat continued down the canal. A gentle breeze brushed their faces, making the ride wonderfully pleasant.

It would have been even better if they had not been so hungry.

They reached the Pokemon Center before nightfall. After arranging their rooms, they hurried to a nearby restaurant and ordered a table full of food.

At their warm invitation, Bianca stayed for dinner too.

When the dishes arrived, Ash's way of eating startled her at first. She had spent enough time with him to see that he was a straightforward boy who acted on whatever came to mind. So she ate at a more measured pace and watched him devour his meal as though she were watching an eating show.

After dinner, they chatted and exchanged contact details. By the time they left the restaurant, the moon was high. Altomare looked beautiful in a different way under its evening lights.

They waved Bianca goodbye and returned to the Pokemon Center.

From the morning's water race to dinner, it had been a long day. Tired out, they said good night and went to their rooms.

Ethan took a hot shower to wash away the day's fatigue. When he returned, Ash and Brock were already snoring. He shook his head, lay down, and picked up his phone.

He was still concerned about the two thieves. He could not remember the plot clearly, but he knew they would cause more trouble.

With nothing else to do, he searched online for information about them.

There was almost nothing on the internet about thieves of their caliber. Ethan finally sent Looker a message.

Only a few minutes later, Looker sent back two files. Ethan opened them and found photographs of the women at the top.

They were sisters. The blonde was named Annie, and the silver-haired woman was Oakley. They ranked as the world's foremost thieves. Both were agile, and neither had failed a job since beginning her career.

Below the photographs was a list of their crimes. Ethan skimmed it. Most of their targets had been valuable jewelry, paintings, calligraphy, and other works of art.

As he read, drowsiness overtook him. He could not remember when he fell asleep.

The stars glittered beneath a high moon.

Deep into the night, the whole city was quiet. At some point, a cool breeze brushed Ethan in his sleep and woke him. As soon as he opened his eyes, he saw Latias flying toward him in a rush. Her anxious expression and the scratches on her body told him something had happened.

Before he could turn on the light, Latias tumbled into the room and struck the floor with a loud crash. Ash and Brock woke at once.

Ethan switched on the room light. Ash and Brock stared at Latias in surprise.

"Latias, why are you here? What happened?"
