Chapter 517: Whitney's Mistake

Blue light flashed, and a purple, monkey-like Pokemon appeared before Ethan. It had a forked crest on its head and two long, unusual tails.

Ethan pointed his Pokedex at it.

"Beep. Ambipom, the Long Tail Pokemon. A Normal-type. Ambipom live in large groups high in enormous trees. Their tails can handle so many tasks with such dexterity that they barely use their hands anymore. They link tails to form a ring, sharing their feelings and strengthening their bonds. People are sometimes invited into the ring. If Ambipom wraps both tails around you, it truly feels close to you."

Ambipom could often be found in forests across Johto and other regions. They traveled in groups and attacked people or Pokemon that entered their territory.

Ambipom evolved from Aipom. Though it was seldom seen in the city, Ethan knew something about it.

He remembered Ash catching an Aipom later in his travels, then trading it to Dawn. It eventually evolved into Ambipom.

The species was fast and agile. It would not be easy to fight.

After considering his options, Ethan recalled Tyranitar and sent out Scizor.

If his opponent had strange tails, he would answer with a Pokemon whose pincers were just as distinctive.

Whitney studied Scizor as it appeared before Ambipom.

Scizor's Steel typing would halve the damage from Ambipom's Normal-type attacks. Ambipom also relied on its two tails for most of its offense, and Scizor's huge pincers might leave it little room to use them.

So that was why Ethan had chosen Scizor. It was a clever choice.

When the round began, both Pokemon charged.

Scizor swung in with Slash. Ambipom braced one tail against the ground and lifted itself out of reach, then snapped its other tail at Scizor's head. Scizor blocked with its right pincer.

Suddenly, Ambipom let out a piercing cry. Caught off guard, Scizor startled and froze for a moment. Ambipom seized the opening and struck Scizor over and over with its long tails.

Ethan saw the sequence clearly. Ambipom had used Astonish to create an opening, then followed with Fury Swipes.

The combination flowed so smoothly that it had plainly been practiced many times.

Another Pokemon might have taken serious damage from it. But Ambipom was striking Scizor, whose body was as hard as steel.

Its tails were strong, yet every blow against Scizor's red, armored chest made only a dull thud.

Fortunately, Astonish had not left Scizor flinching.

"Scizor, use Night Slash!"

Scizor swung both pincers at once. But just as it attacked, Ambipom wound a tail around each pincer and bound them together like handcuffs. Scizor could not complete the move.

Ethan had expected Ambipom's tails to be flexible. He had not imagined it could use them like that.

Whitney smiled. "With its hands tied, how will it fight now?"

Ethan remained calm. "Use Iron Head!"

"What!?" Whitney's smile vanished.

Scizor drew its head back, then yanked its pincers toward its body, pulling Ambipom close. Its hard red head slammed into Ambipom's face.

Bang!

Steel met bone. Ambipom nearly fainted from the impact.

It released its grip without thinking and dropped to the ground. Swaying as if drunk, it could barely stay on its feet.

"Now! Use X-Scissor!"

Scizor readied its attack. Seeing Ambipom still dazed, Whitney cried, "Ambipom, snap out of it!"

It heard her, but its head was spinning. It tried several times to stand steady and could not.

"It's over," Ethan said softly.

He was right. Scizor's X-Scissor struck Ambipom while it could hardly stand. Scizor followed immediately with another Slash and knocked it to the ground.

The referee raised her colored flag. "Ambipom is unable to battle! Scizor wins the second round!"

Whitney sighed with regret. Battles could turn like this. She had been doing well, but one mistaken decision had cost her the round.

Had her strategy really been wrong? She had won against many other Trainers by binding a Pokemon's arms. What she had misjudged was just how hard Scizor's head was.

Against another opponent, perhaps it would have been Ethan's Pokemon lying defeated.

Whitney pushed the thought aside, recalled Ambipom with a rueful smile, and took out her last Poke Ball.

"You're amazing. You've defeated two of my Pokemon so easily. No wonder you're a Champion! Now, my final Pokemoncome out, Lopunny!"

The ball opened, and a fluffy, athletic-looking brown rabbit appeared before Ethan.

Lopunny...

Ethan rubbed his chin. First a cow, then a monkey, and now a rabbit. Whitney's team was like a little zoo!

Lopunny were common in Sinnoh, Unova, Alola, and Galar, but wild ones had not been found in Johto.

As he had done with Ambipom, Ethan took out his Pokedex and aimed it at Lopunny.

"Beep. Lopunny, the Rabbit Pokemon. A Normal-type. It is highly alert and runs with light, bounding steps. If it senses danger, it wraps itself in the soft, fluffy fur at the tips of its ears and leaps nimbly away. Though it dislikes fighting, it can deliver tremendously powerful kicks when threatened."
