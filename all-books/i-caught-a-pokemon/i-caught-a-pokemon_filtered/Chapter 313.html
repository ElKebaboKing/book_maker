Chapter 313: Lance's Confidence in Ethan

The next morning, Ethan and Sabrina washed up and ate another generous breakfast. Then Clair led them down to the first floor of the Gym.

Sabrina noticed that Ethan still looked sleepy. She lowered her voice. "Why do you look so worn out? Didn't you sleep well?"

"I slept fine. Why?"

"You have dark circles under your eyes."

Dark circles? Ethan was silent for a long moment. He rubbed his eyes without thinking, then gave her a rueful smile and shook his head.

He had talked with Serena until after one in the morning before they finally said good night. Of course he looked tired after so little sleep. He could not tell Sabrina that, though.

They crossed a long corridor, descended marble steps, and entered a broad hall. This was Blackthorn Gym's battlefield.

It was a combined land-and-water field. The outer area was bare, ordinary ground. In the center stood a square pool of clear water, some four or five meters deep, enough for most Water-type Pokemon to battle in.

Many of Clair's Dragon-type Pokemon were also Water types and fought well in water. Ethan guessed that was why the field had been built this way.

He and Clair walked toward the opposing Trainer areas. On the second-floor spectator balcony, Lance appeared. Rather than sit down, he leaned casually against the railing. He seemed keen to watch their match.

Lance nodded when Ethan and Clair looked up at him. He had barely greeted them when he heard footsteps behind him.

A middle-aged man had climbed the stairs and was walking slowly toward him. He carried himself with a steady authority. His hair was the same color as Lance's, and there was a resemblance in their faces.

"Uncle?" Lance looked surprised. "Clair said you had gone to the Pokemon League. How are you back so soon?"

The man was Blackthorn Gym's former Leader, the current chief of the Dragon Tamer clan, and Lance's uncle.

"I finished my business there, so I came home. I had just walked in when I heard there was a Gym challenge. I came to have a look and found you here."

He stopped beside Lance and studied him. "It has been a long time since I saw you. You look just as you did before."

Lance laughed. "At my age, my face isn't going to change much except to grow older!"

After a few more pleasantries, his uncle changed the subject. "I heard someone broke into the Holy Land yesterday?"

"Team Rocket. I came back because of it," Lance said. "Their leader was a top executive named Vicious. We've dealt with the attack. He escaped, but we handed all the others over to the police. The Elder has ordered the Dragon Tamers still in Johto to hunt him down."

"Well done." The man praised him, then his expression hardened. "Team Rocket dared to enter the Dragon Holy Land. We'll make them answer for it."

He looked toward the field. "I'm surprised you're interested in a Gym battle, though. I didn't think your work as a Pokemon investigator left you time to watch matches."

"It usually doesn't," Lance said with a smile. "I ought to return to Kanto as soon as possible. But the challenger is a friend of mine, and we owe him a great deal for keeping the Holy Land safe yesterday. I wanted to see his match before I go."

"Oh? Your friend helped us that much? He must be strong, then." The man glanced at Ethan.

"He is." Lance's voice turned serious. "Clair won't be able to beat him. She's certain to lose this match."

Clair can't beat him? His uncle was astonished. He knew her strength better than anyone. He would never have stepped down and handed the Gym to her otherwise. How could someone so young be even stronger?

"I've never known you to praise a person this highly," he said. "It isn't like you."

"I can't help it." Lance shrugged. "He's strong, and the speed at which he improves is extraordinary. He impressed me the last time we met. Now I don't even know how far he's come."

The man studied Ethan. It was the first time he had seen that face in person, yet it looked familiar.

"You've said a great deal about him. Which of you is stronger?"

"We haven't battled, so I couldn't say." Lance looked toward Ethan on the field and spoke almost to himself. "But I think we'll find out soon after the Silver Conference."

Ethan had no idea they were discussing him. His attention was on Kabu, who was approaching the field with a small red flag and a green one in his hands.

Taking his place as referee, Kabu raised his voice. "The Blackthorn Gym challenge will now begin! On the green side, Ethan Vale, winner of the Indigo League tournament! On the red side, Clair, Leader of Blackthorn Gym! This will be a three-Pokemon elimination match. The battle ends when all three Pokemon on one side are unable to continue!"

He paused, then added, "Only the challenger may switch Pokemon during the match. Clair, please send out your first Pokemon!"

"Please wait," Ethan said.

Clair and Kabu both turned toward him.

Ethan smiled at Clair. "I'd like to change the rules a little, if you're willing."

"Oh? What would you change?"

"I'd rather not battle the Pokemon you ordinarily use to defend the Gym. Please use your best Pokemon. And let's both send out our first Pokemon at the same time. That seems fairer."

Clair thought back to the power Ethan's Pokemon had shown in the Dragon Holy Land. He had also won the Indigo League tournament. An ordinary Gym team would not be the right test for him.

"All right. I agree. I want to find out whose Pokemon are stronger too."

Up on the balcony, the former Gym Leader finally recognized Ethan when he heard Kabu announce his name.

Of course. That's why he looked familiar!

The young champion had drawn so much attention recently that his name appeared in the news again and again. Even after leaving the Gym and paying less attention to League competitions, the man had heard about Ethan often.
