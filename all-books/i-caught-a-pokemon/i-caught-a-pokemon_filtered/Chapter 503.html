Chapter 503: Evolution into Tyranitar

For Bugsy to send out Metapod after losing the first round, he had to be confident in its strength.

Ordinarily, a Trainer's second Pokemon would be stronger than the first or have an advantage against the opponent. Yet Ethan could not understand this choice. At the Metapod stage, its ways to attack were extremely limited. It basically had only Harden, and some Metapod could not even move.

Why send out a Pokemon with such a natural disadvantage?

Bugsy must have his reasons, Ethan decided. Perhaps he had given it some unusual training.

When the battle resumed, Pupitar attacked first.

It released compressed air from its body and shot toward Metapod like an arrow from a bow. In a blink, it reached its opponent, opened its jaws to reveal small pointed teeth, and bit down hard.

Ethan had not ordered it to do that. Pupitar wanted to test Metapod's shell with its own teeth.

A contest of toughness was more than talk!

"Metapod, use Harden!" Bugsy shouted as Pupitar rushed in.

Metapod remained in place, but light flashed over its body. Harden was the one Normal-type move a Metapod could use to make its shell tougher and protect its soft interior.

Pupitar's teeth closed around Metapod with all their strength.

Creeeak...

The scrape of teeth against its shell made a shrill sound like metal grinding on metal.

Protected by Harden, Metapod did not yield. Pupitar nearly broke its teeth. It tried biting several other spots, but could not get through the shell!

Metapod certainly lived up to its reputation for a hard casing!

Bugsy spotted an opening. "Metapod, jump!"

Metapod pushed off sharply with the tip of its body and sprang high into the air, breaking free of Pupitar.

It reached almost to the Gym's ceiling. Pupitar looked up, and the next moment Metapod dropped straight toward it like a cannonball!

Pupitar could not dodge. Metapod slammed into it with a tremendous bang, and the two Pokemon bounced apart.

Only Pupitar's own hard shell let it withstand the blow.

Bugsy saw Ethan's surprise and smiled. "Well? Didn't expect that, did you? My Metapod isn't like the ones you've met before!"

For all the pride in Bugsy's voice, Ethan really was impressed.

Most people's Metapod could hardly move inside their rigid shells. Somehow, Bugsy had trained his to leap and strike!

He deserved his reputation as a Bug-type expert.

Metapod tried another dive from above, but this time Pupitar was ready and dodged.

After those exchanges, Ethan understood its approach. Trusting its shell, Metapod hurled itself around like a solid cannonball!

It had no concern for the risk in either attack or defense.

How could he stop that smooth little sequence?

His gaze crossed Pupitar, and after a moment's thought he had an idea.

When Metapod jumped high again, ready to repeat its dive, Ethan suddenly called, "Pupitar, use Stone Edge!"

At the command, Pupitar formed a huge, solid rock in front of itself. Metapod was already dropping toward it!

"Oh no!"

Bugsy had expected Pupitar to fire sharp stones at the airborne Metapod. When he saw the single massive rock appear, his face changed. He understood what Ethan intended.

It was too late to make Metapod change course!

Before everyone's eyes, Metapod plunged headfirst into the rock.

Bang!

Whether by perfect force or chance, Metapod neither shattered the rock nor bounced off. It punched a small hole and lodged its entire body inside!

Metapod twisted frantically, caught off guard by the turn of events. However hard it struggled, it could not break free.

Got it.

Ethan smiled. "Finish it, Pupitar!"

With a burst of compressed air, Pupitar jumped in front of the rock and aimed at Metapod's exposed back. It unleashed Dark Pulse.

A terrifying wave of energy surged from Pupitar's body and struck Metapod almost instantly. Trapped in the rock, it could not evade. Dark Pulse swept over it, the rock broke apart, and Metapod flew away with the fragments.

Clatter...

Metapod rolled several times and came to rest outside the battlefield.

Its eyes were spinning. The referee raised a colored flag. "Metapod is unable to battle! Pupitar wins!"

Before Bugsy could speak, white light suddenly enveloped Pupitar.

Bugsy looked startled. Ethan raised his brows too.

Another evolution?

Dragonite had evolved during the Cianwood Gym challenge, and now Pupitar was doing the same. It had happened several times back in Kanto too, always just after one of Ethan's Pokemon defeated an opponent.

In game terms, Gym Leaders' Pokemon were stronger than those of ordinary Trainers. Defeating them meant gaining more "experience points"!

The light faded. A Pokemon more than two meters tall stood on the battlefield, its body protected by hard gray-green armor.

It was Pupitar's evolved formTyranitar!

Tyranitar threw back its head and roared. The deafening sound echoed through the Gym, sending distant Bug-type Pokemon fleeing into the shrubs!

Its entrance reminded Ethan of his Gyarados, far away at Cerise Park. Tyranitar was not as enormous, but it was every bit as fierce a species!

This was not Ethan's first time seeing one. When they had raided a Team Rocket base in Kanto, Apollo, one of Team Rocket's four executives, had commanded a Tyranitar. That Pokemon had struck Erika hard enough to draw blood. Ethan could still remember it clearly!
