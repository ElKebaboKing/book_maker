Chapter 490: A Chance Encounter in Azalea Town

Chuck's warm invitation was impossible to turn down, so Ethan and Erika stayed at the Gym for lunch.

Ethan had never expected this before the battle. He had come to the man's Gym, defeated him, and taken his badge. Now he was eating his food too. It felt a little shameless.

Then again, Chuck did not seem to mind. Why should Ethan worry so much about it?

Chuck lived at the Gym. Besides the battle hall, it had dormitories for his students. A few lived nearby in Cianwood City, but most ate and slept at the Gym.

Before lunch, Ethan and Erika met Chuck's wife.

She was a beautiful woman with long red hair and a gentle, capable air.

Frankly, given Chuck's rough appearance, he must have been extraordinarily lucky to marry someone like her!

Ethan and Erika sat down with the students. Chuck took the seat at the head of the table, glanced between his guests, and smiled. "Are you two a couple?"

The question silenced both of them.

Erika had lost count of how many times people had asked her that.

She was about to deny it when Ethan, instead of answering, asked, "Mr. Chuck, did you meet your wife while you were traveling too?"

Chuck nodded. He looked at his wife, sweet memories shining in his eyes. "That's right. Meeting her on my travels was why I wanted to settle down instead of wandering from place to place."

"Oh? I see. That's something to envy..."

Several students brought out plates of food and an enormous bucket of rice. Chuck's wife served everyone a bowl, but for Chuck she used an especially large one.

It was almost the size of a washbasin!

At Ethan and Erika's astonished looks, Chuck laughed. "Nothing strange about it. I get a lot of exercise!"

Once they began eating, Ethan discovered that Chuck was not the only one with a huge appetite. His students seemed to take after him, clutching their bowls and eating as heartily as he did!

It made sense. As Fighting-type Trainers, they trained with their Pokemon every day and burned a great deal of energy. They were growing children too, so large appetites were only natural.

The food vanished from the table in no time.

Over lunch, Chuck and his students grew increasingly curious about Ethan, and they admired him all the more.

After peppering him with questions, they finally learned that this handsome young man was the champion of the latest Indigo League!

"No wonder..." Chuck shook his head with a wry smile. "If I'd known you were the champion, I'd have brought out my strongest team from the start. Maybe I wouldn't have lost so badly."

After lunch, Ethan and Erika left the Cianwood Gym with everyone seeing them off.

As they neared the end of the street, Ethan looked back.

Erika gave him a questioning look. He sighed. "We only knew him for a few hours, but Chuck really is a warmhearted man..."

A few hours after their last visit, the two returned to the harbor.

This time, their destination was the Azalea Gym.

With the Storm Badge in hand, Ethan needed only two more badges before he could challenge the Silver Conference. Goldenrod City was closer to Cianwood, but they had missed the boat sailing there. They had to head for the more distant Azalea Town and challenge its Gym first.

They bought tickets and boarded a small passenger ship, which slowly carried them away from Cianwood City.

They sailed through the afternoon and night, sleeping aboard the ship before finally reaching the Johto mainland the following morning.

A night's rest had fully restored Ethan's energy and stamina. If he raced Chuck again now, he was sure he could keep up!

Unfortunately, there would be no rematch. He could only tuck that regret away in his memories.

Azalea Town was not a harbor town. The ship docked at a small city, and from there they still had a two-hour walk north to reach it.

They disembarked and left the city behind.

Just as they entered the wilds, Erika caught another Sunkern.

It was the second Sunkern she had caught since arriving in Johto.

Ethan could not understand why she wanted two of the same Pokemon. When he asked, Erika gave a little laugh.

"I'm not catching them for battles. They'll help keep the ecosystem in the Celadon Gym's greenhouse healthy and make its Pokemon population more varied!"

"I see..."

As they talked, they passed through the dense Ilex Forest.

It was a paradise for Bug-type Pokemon. The trees grew so thick that their branches blotted out the sky, leaving the forest as dark by day as it was at night.

Here, Ethan used Pupitar and the Eevee he had received from the sisters in Ecruteak City to defeat several overconfident Bug-type Pokemon that attacked them. Both of his Pokemon gained some battle experience.

They also caught a fleeting glimpse of a Farfetch'd. Ethan liked the species and wanted to catch it, but it disappeared deep into the forest before he could try. He could only smile, give up, and reflect that he and this Farfetch'd were not meant to meet.

At last they emerged from Ilex Forest, and the view opened up before them.

Their destination, Azalea Town, was close at hand.

Azalea was a town where people and Pokemon lived together in a simple, peaceful way.

It stood on a peninsula in southern Johto. Once it had been an unremarkable town, but a Trainer had founded the Azalea Gym there a few years earlier, and its name had gradually become better known.

The locals also told a less widely known legend.

Four hundred years ago, a Slowpoke had yawned and brought down rain, ending a long drought. Because of that, Slowpoke held a special place in the hearts of the townspeople.

When Ethan and Erika entered the small town, they spotted a familiar figure before they could even ask where the Gym was.

Ethan looked more closely. It was Professor Elm from New Bark Town!

Professor Elm had seen Ethan too. His eyes lit up, and he waved.

"I never expected to run into you here!"

"Professor, what brings you here?"

They spoke almost at the same time. Their voices overlapped, and after exchanging a glance, they both burst out laughing.
