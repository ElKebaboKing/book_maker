Chapter 377: Ho-Oh Retreats

Ho-Oh released an angry cry.

It was clearly furious that Suicune had been captured before its eyes.

Ho-Oh beat its wings and formed dozens of scorching fireballs, scattering them toward Ethan like an enormous net.

Before the flames traveled halfway, Gardevoir and Gengar hurried over from the side and stopped them together.

Bang! Bang! Bang!

Psychic force and Shadow Balls collided with the fireballs, canceling every attack in midair.

Smoke from the explosions enveloped Ethan. He walked several steps forward and emerged from the haze, then picked up the Great Ball containing Suicune. Straightening, he tossed the ball lightly in one hand and stared intensely at Ho-Oh.

The gesture was as provocative as it could possibly be.

With Raikou also restored to full strength, the two remaining Legendary beasts used their tremendous power to break free from the encirclement with ease.

They first nodded to Ho-Oh, then departed without pause. After only a few leaps, both vanished into the darkness and disappeared from sight.

Thanks to Ho-Oh's forceful intervention, the prize already within Ethan's grasp had escaped.

Just as he had said before, once Legendary Pokemon devoted themselves to fleeing, no one could stop them.

Ethan had been in a position to capture all three beasts safely, yet had obtained only Suicune. Naturally, he felt both angry and regretful.

Entei and Raikou had escaped and could no longer be captured. With no need to divide his attention between them, only Ho-Oh remained on the battlefield, allowing Ethan to focus entirely upon it.

"All right. Now it's just you and me. It's time to settle this debt!"

Sensing that another battle was about to begin, the Pokemon belonging to Ethan and Erika gathered around. From the air and ground, they surrounded Ho-Oh in a pincer formation.

Ho-Oh appeared unconcerned. It simply fixed Ethan with a sharp gaze.

Sparks seemed to clash between the eyes of human and Pokemon.

At that moment, an androgynous voice suddenly sounded within Ethan's mind.

"Human, release that Pokemon. Do not disrupt the balance of nature!"

Disrupt the balance?

Ethan smiled noncommittally. Those words sounded impressive.

But what balance did Ho-Oh mean?

Did it mean the Legendary beasts resolving territorial disputes between Pokemon? Or preventing conflicts and wars among humans?

In Ethan's view, humans and Pokemon were both creatures living within nature. There was no essential difference between them. As long as they did not deliberately destroy or pollute their environment, nature's own resilience and the passage of time provided the best balance.

Moreover, some Pokemon possessed far more energy than humans and stood above ordinary laws. Like the three island birds of the Orange Islands, their every movement could alter the weather. Many natural disasters were caused by Pokemon such as them.

Why did Ho-Oh not go balance those creatures?

Most significantly, how could Ethan spit out meat that had already entered his mouth?

He wasted no words arguing. Ethan swept one arm forward and ordered an attack.

At his signal, Gengar, Gardevoir, Charizard, Jolteon, Erika's Leafeon, and Tangela all attacked Ho-Oh at once.

Crystal Steelix and Scyther remained in front of Ethan, prepared to intercept any counterattack Ho-Oh might launch.

Thunder Shock, Psychic, Shadow Ball, Flamethrower, Razor Leaf, and other attacks of many colors flew toward Ho-Oh.

Ho-Oh remained perfectly calm. Rainbow light surrounded its body. With a powerful beat of its wings, it unleashed a burst of golden energy that canceled every incoming move.

The resulting explosion sent a powerful shock wave in every direction. Several Pokemon could not keep their eyes open as it swept past them and rushed toward Ethan.

Before the wind could reach him, Crystal Steelix blocked it entirely with its enormous, solid body.

The first exchange appeared evenly matched.

But Ho-Oh had produced that much force through defense alone, demonstrating its tremendous strength.

After the brief clash, Ethan judged Ho-Oh to be approximately as powerful as Mewtwo in its ordinary form.

The difference between their overall strength was not impossible to overcome, but for now, a considerable gulf still separated Ethan's Pokemon from Ho-Oh.

Still, Ethan did not plan to defeat Ho-Oh through force. His Pokemon only needed to occupy its attention. The true weapon he intended to use against it was another decisive trump card.

While his Pokemon held Ho-Oh in place, Ethan opened his bag and searched through it. From the very bottom, he took out his Master Ball.

Ever since Silph gave him the Master Ball, Ethan had kept it hidden deep within his bag and never found the right moment to use it.

Originally, Ethan had never intended to use the ball and wanted to preserve it indefinitely. But Ho-Oh had dared to ruin his plans. It deserved to be struck by a Master Ball.

Take responsibility for your actions!

Ethan raised his right hand and prepared to throw.

At that moment, the ground began shaking violently, as though the entire world were trembling.

Unable to keep his footing, Ethan fell against Crystal Steelix. After stabilizing himself, he looked around and saw the crystals beneath his feet retreating like shadows toward the ruins of the crystal tower at incredible speed.

Huh!?

Not only did the crystals covering the ground vanish, but every plant and rock they had consumed returned to its original state in an instant.

The crystal disappeared without a trace, as though it had never existed.

There was no need to guess. Ash and the others must have resolved the matter involving Molly and the Unown.

Amid the confusion, Ho-Oh beat its wings and climbed high into the sky.

When Ethan gave the order to attack, Ho-Oh had clearly realized that words would never persuade him to release Suicune.

Taking it back by force would be equally difficult with so many Pokemon watching Ho-Oh from every direction.

On the ground, Ethan looked up.

At this distance, even a Master Ball could not capture Ho-Oh unless it chose to descend.

"Ho-Oh, what's wrong? Are you running away?"

Ho-Oh gave Ethan one last, penetrating look. Ignoring his provocation, it decisively flew higher.

Ethan could only watch as Ho-Oh became a streak of golden light, shot straight into the heavens, and disappeared beyond the horizon.

"Damn it!"

Still clutching the unused Master Ball, he swung his hand through the air in frustration.

Ash had somehow resolved the crisis at exactly this moment. His timing had been truly "perfect."

At least Ethan had successfully captured Suicune. He had not left empty-handed.
