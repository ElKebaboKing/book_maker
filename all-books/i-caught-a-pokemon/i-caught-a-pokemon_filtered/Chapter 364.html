Chapter 364: Battle of Avatars

Molly was a very common name. Since coming to this world, Ethan had already met several people who shared it.

But when Ash heard her call Delia her mother, he immediately grew anxious.

"Give my mom back!!"

Molly had visited Pallet Town with her parents several years ago, and she and Ash had even appeared in a photograph together. Clearly, however, neither remembered the other. They had met only once and had both been very young. Now, they were no different from strangers.

Even after the woman admitted that she was Molly, Misty still found it impossible to believe. She moved closer to Ethan and whispered in confusion, "That's Molly? But didn't Delia say she was still a little girl?"

Ethan considered the question before answering slowly, "If this entire world is something she created from her imagination, then it isn't surprising that she can appear before us as an adult."

Meanwhile, Molly stared fixedly at Ash. Even when faced with Delia's actual son, she refused to yield and showed no sense that she was in the wrong.

"She's my mama now! If you want her back, you'll have to battle me!!"

Somehow, the situation had turned into a game of stealing someone's mother...

Although Molly had taken the form of an adult, her mind remained immature, and she still spoke like a child.

It made sense. Only an innocent little girl like her would behave in such a childish way.

Faced with such an absurd demand, Ash had no choice but to agree even though he had not wanted to battle. Just as he was about to step forward, however, Brock suddenly moved ahead of him.

"Ash, I'll hold her here. The rest of you go rescue Delia!"

"Huh?" Ash froze. "But..."

"No buts!" Brock declared firmly. "Delia must be somewhere above us, waiting for you to save her. You can't let yourself be delayed here!"

Then he smiled and added in a half-joking tone, "Besides, Molly may really be a little girl, but she has appeared before us as a beautiful older woman. That means I should be her opponent!"

Brock's actions undoubtedly moved Ash.

This was what made a reliable companion so valuable. At a crucial moment, he would always step forward and share his friends' burden!

Still, knowing Brock's personality, Ethan had reason to suspect that he had chosen to stay simply because he had been captivated by Molly's appearance.

Seeing Brock insist on battling her, Ash hesitated. When he could not decide, Ethan made the decision for him.

"All right, we'll leave her to you. Be careful, and don't let your guard down!" After warning Brock, Ethan turned toward Ash and Misty. "Come on. We don't have much time. Let's go!"

Once Ethan had decided, Ash's uncertainty vanished. He stopped hesitating and hurried after Ethan with Misty as they ran toward the staircase.

The three passed Molly and Entei without encountering the resistance they had expected. Molly looked straight ahead and made no attempt to stop them. She did not even spare them a glance, as though she had no fear that they might take Delia away.

After climbing the staircase and reaching a higher level, Ethan looked back one final time.

Brock and Molly had already taken their positions. Brock sent out Golbat, while Molly conjured a Poke Ball and released a Flaaffy to face it.

In the blink of an eye, the two Pokemon began battling!

Misty glanced back worriedly. "Will he be all right on his own?"

"Trust him!" Ethan replied without slowing. "He is an official Gym Leader, after all. He won't be defeated easily!"

As they climbed higher, the grassland beneath them soon disappeared. When they left that level, their surroundings once again became utterly silent, as though they had entered an empty void.

The environment grew increasingly bizarre. Crystal formations spread in every direction like tree branches or cell nuclei. It felt as if they were moving through the body of some alien creature.

They continued running upward for several minutes before light finally appeared ahead.

When they entered the next space, the scenery changed once more. This time, an endless ocean at sunset stretched before them!

The sunlight was gentle rather than glaring. Palm leaves swayed in the sea breeze. Their shoes left rows of footprints in the sand, only for the waves to wash every mark away as they rolled onto the beach.

Everything before them appeared completely real, but they knew it was another illusion created by the Unown, just like the grassland below.

When the sea breeze brushed Ethan's face, he could not smell the ocean at all.

These illusions had likely been shaped from Molly's memories. But Molly was still so young that she could not remember every detail, leaving the world she created imperfect.

As they searched for Delia, light suddenly flashed beside them. Molly appeared before them once again!

Misty's eyes widened in surprise.

"What's going on? How did she get here? Isn't she supposed to be battling Brock right now!?"

Ethan did not remember this part of the story, so he could not tell whether Molly had quickly defeated Brock and teleported here or whether this was a new avatar.

Either way, he did not forget to warn the two behind him. "This place is far too strange. Stay alert and wait to see what she does!"

Before they could question her, Molly once again folded her arms and smiled at Ash. "I told you, you can't take Mama back! Which one of you will battle me this time? I want to face someone stronger!"

Her words told Ethan that Brock must have lost the previous battle.

As Molly finished speaking, Entei appeared in front of her and regarded them with a calm gaze.

After seeing the ocean around them, Misty had already made her decision. She knew that after Brock, it was her turn to step forward.

She took a step forward and said solemnly, "I'll handle her this time! Ethan, Ash, keep going and rescue Delia!"

Ethan hesitated, then nodded.

"Be careful! It looks like she defeated Brock, so she won't be easy to handle. But her real body should be with Delia right now. Once we reach them, the illusion should disappear!"

"I understand!"

After what had happened with Brock, Ash raised no objections this time. He followed Ethan and ran toward another staircase nearby.

Once again, Molly made no attempt to stop them. She focused entirely on Misty. The two women stared at each other, their fighting spirit surging as they prepared for battle!
