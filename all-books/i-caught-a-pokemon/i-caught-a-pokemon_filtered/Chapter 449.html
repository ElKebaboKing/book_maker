Chapter 449: Surely He Doesn't Have a Suicune Too?

With Poliwrath defeated, Chinchou was the only Pokemon Trinity had left that could still battle.

Watching Gyarados and Poliwrath meet such brutal ends one after another sent her heart plunging to the bottom.

To be honest, after those two battles, Trinity no longer held any hope that she could defeat Ethan.

Even if she somehow defeated the red Gyarados, what would that accomplish?

Ethan still had an exceptionally powerful Blastoise waiting for her. And apart from those two Pokemon, who knew whether he had an even more outrageous monster?

A Pokemon as overwhelmingly powerful as the red Gyarados was not even Ethan's final trump card. Trinity genuinely could not imagine what formidable opponent might appear after it!

A weak Pokemon certainly would not catch this man's attention. So what could it be?

Surely he doesn't have a Suicune too?

The thought made her laugh.

What nonsense am I imagining?

How could anyone encounter and capture Suicune so easily? Besides, perhaps even Suicune could not defeat a Gyarados this powerful!

Trinity brought her attention back to the battlefield and looked toward the red Gyarados again.

She had lost all hope of victory, but that did not mean she would surrender without resistance. Fortunately, Poliwrath had delayed the battle long enough to give Chinchou plenty of time to recover.

Now she could only place every remaining hope upon Chinchou and pray it had regained its composure!

At the very least, I have to give it everything I have and defeat this red Gyarados if I can. I need to recover a little dignity!

Even if I lose, I refuse to be swept aside this easily!

Trinity took out her first Poke Ball and threw it with all her strength. Blue light flashed, and Chinchou appeared upon the water once more!

After calming down inside its Poke Ball, Chinchou appeared far more composed than the first time it faced the red Gyarados. Yet that calm was only superficial. The instant its eyes met the red Gyarados's gaze again, its entire body began trembling against its will!

"Chinchou!" Trinity cupped both hands around her mouth and hurriedly encouraged it. "Don't let its presence frighten you! And don't look into its eyes!"

Chinchou quickly looked away. Sure enough, once it stopped meeting those terrifying eyes, it felt blood flow through its body again. The numbness and pounding in its chest faded. It could still feel the red Gyarados's cold gaze, but at least it was no longer too frightened to move!

Seeing this, Trinity quietly sighed in relief.

Chinchou still could not move as freely as usual, but as long as it did not flee and could continue the battle, she could ask for nothing more!

The referee waved his flag, officially beginning the fourth round!

At Trinity's command, Chinchou floated across the surface and swiftly swam to the side.

The red Gyarados had destroyed every stone pillar on this half of the battlefield. That left medium and large Pokemon with nowhere to stand, but for a small Pokemon like Chinchou, the devastation created a major advantage.

The piles of shattered stone provided endless obstacles it could use to maneuver around the red Gyarados!

Chinchou thrashed its body desperately and reached a heap of debris before the red Gyarados could catch it. In a panic, it slipped through a passage formed by two fallen stones and successfully escaped to the other side.

The red Gyarados was too large to pass through. It could only go around the rubble or smash through it, preventing it from catching Chinchou immediately.

That brief delay gave Chinchou an opportunity.

Both antennae erupted with dazzling light. A second later, an electrical current left their tips and shot toward the red Gyarados!

Crackle!

The electricity struck with perfect accuracy. Perhaps Chinchou had fired too hastily, or perhaps the red Gyarados's body was simply too enormous. The current failed to cover even its entire body before running out of power and dissipating!

The instant it hit, Trinity stared at the red Gyarados without blinking.

She wanted to see whether this terrifying red Gyarados shared the same Water and Flying typing as an ordinary Gyarados and therefore feared Electric-type moves just as much!

Unfortunately, the current had vanished too quickly for her to see any sign of pain. Still, the red Gyarados had paused slightly when struck, so the Electric-type attack had clearly produced some reaction!

Hmm... It seems to have worked, but it also doesn't appear to have done very much...

Was the last attack simply too brief and too weak?

Trinity was about to order Chinchou to use a stronger move, but the red Gyarados had already smashed the pile of rubble aside with its enormous body and appeared before Chinchou again.

This time, Chinchou did not wait for her command. It fled in panic toward another heap of broken stone.

This running battle involved no fierce exchanges of moves, so it was hardly spectacular. Even so, the spectators watched with intense concentration, afraid to miss the slightest detail!

Perhaps out of sympathy for the weaker side, most of the crowd once again placed themselves in Trinity's position. Whenever Chinchou narrowly escaped danger, they found themselves holding their breath for it!

Gulp...

In the stands, Misty unconsciously swallowed.

She was already afraid of Gyarados, and this red Gyarados terrified her even more. As Ethan's companion, she wanted to cheer for him and wanted him to avenge her loss. Yet she could not stop herself from worrying about Chinchou!

When Misty noticed the change in her loyalties, she first froze, then hurriedly reassured herself.

This isn't betraying Ethan! After all... after all, Chinchou is a Water-type Pokemon too. I want to become a Water Pokemon Master, so worrying about it is perfectly natural!

On the field, the red Gyarados had caught up to another pile of stone.

Beside a broken pillar, Chinchou made one agile turn, slipped away from the red Gyarados, and reached another safe position.

It hurriedly turned around and gathered its electrical power with all its strength. When the energy finally reached its peak, an enormous sphere of electricity formed between its antennae.

The terrifying monster approached again. Chinchou did not even have time to aim before hurling the electrical sphere at the red Gyarados in a panic!

The sphere flew through the air.

Perhaps the red Gyarados was simply too enormous to miss. Despite Chinchou's lack of aim, the attack still struck its body.

Crackle!

In an instant, an extraordinarily brilliant burst of light exploded across the red Gyarados!
