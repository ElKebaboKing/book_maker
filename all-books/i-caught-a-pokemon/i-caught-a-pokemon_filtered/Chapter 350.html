Chapter 350: Meeting Erika Again

At four in the afternoon, Ethan returned to Celadon City. When he reached the Celadon Gym, he was surprised to find it under renovation.

Scaffolding completely surrounded the front half of the Gym. Many workers stood upon it with tools in hand, drilling and hammering in a ceaseless clatter. On the ground, more than a dozen Machamp continually carried construction materials back and forth.

Everything proceeded in an orderly fashion. Through the gaps in the scaffolding, Ethan could faintly see clear traces of fire damage.

Wind and rain had faded the marks considerably, but they still showed that the Gym had once suffered a major fire.

Huh?

What happened here?

While Ethan stared in surprise, a woman's voice suddenly called from nearby.

"Ethan, over here!"

He turned and saw Erika.

Despite how long it had been, Erika remained as beautiful as ever. Dressed in a kimono, she carried herself with dignity. Reserved, gentle, and quietly poised, she was the very picture of a graceful traditional lady.

Ethan approached and greeted her, then looked back at the Gym in confusion.

"Was there a major fire here? When did it happen? Why didn't you ever mention it?"

"It was quite a long time ago." Erika thought for a moment. "I believe it happened a few days after you challenged my Gym, while you were still in Kanto, when we worked with the International Police to raid Team Rocket's base."

Back then...

Prompted by her words, Ethan searched his memory and recalled that the anime had indeed featured such an incident. It truly had happened a long time ago.

Although he already knew the answer, he asked, "This didn't happen when Ash challenged your Gym, did it?"

"Oh? You know Ash too?" Erika nodded. "When Ash came to challenge the Gym, Team Rocket happened to attack it. They were a strange group made up of two humans and a talking Meowth. They started the fire and left the Gym in ruins. Fortunately, Ash and his friends were there. Their help prevented the flames from spreading any farther."

Is that really how it happened?

The corners of Ethan's mouth twitched with suppressed amusement.

Two humans and one Meowth could only be the Team Rocket trio.

Only they would pursue Ash so enthusiastically and tirelessly.

But looking at cause and effect, if Ash had not been there, the trio might never have followed him to the Celadon Gym. Without coming here, they would not have launched their attack, and the fire would never have started.

The Gym had been dragged into trouble entirely because of Ash.

Ethan wisely kept that thought to himself. Instead, he asked, "If the fire happened so long ago, why did repairs only begin now?"

"There was nothing I could do." Erika shook her head with a wry smile. "I reported the damage to the League the day after the fire, but its finances were strained at the time, and the request had to pass through layer after layer of approval. That's why it took so long. Fortunately, the money was finally released a few days ago. I began the work at once and decided to expand the Gym while we were at it!"

No wonder she has time to visit Johto and catch Pokemon. The Gym is temporarily closed...

Ethan suddenly remembered the greenhouse inside the Gym that had left such a deep impression on him.

"By the way, were the greenhouse and its Pokemon all right?"

Erika nodded. "Thanks to the Pokemon working together, the fire was extinguished in time. It never reached the greenhouse, and everything there escaped unharmed. That was the one piece of good fortune in the disaster."

At that point, Erika suddenly thanked him. "Speaking of which, I should thank you for the oranges you mailed us. They tasted wonderful and were quite different from Kanto's fruit. After we ate them, we planted the seeds in the flower beds. They've already grown into saplings!"

Those oranges had shown Erika just how greatly regions could differ and how every place possessed its own climate and customs. Without them, she might never have decided to use the Gym's renovation as an opportunity to visit Johto and catch Pokemon from the region.

Ethan had nearly forgotten about the fruit. Hearing her mention it, he relaxed.

"I'm glad they grew. I was worried the seeds might not sprout or adapt to the climate here!"

Erika suddenly looked behind him and searched the area.

"How strange. Where is Miss Sabrina? Weren't the two of you traveling together?"

"Don't ask..." Ethan waved a hand. "The League kept urging her to return and oversee her Gym. Sabrina couldn't keep refusing, so the reason I came back this time was to escort her to the Saffron Gym!"

"Oh? I see..."

Erika was surprised. She had never heard of the League interfering with a Gym Leader's movements.

The thought flashed through her mind. Then, seeing Ethan still standing outside, she suddenly came to her senses.

"Oh, I'm so sorry! I've kept you standing here while we talked and completely forgot to invite you inside. You've traveled for so long, so you must be tired. Please come in and have some hot tea!"

Ethan was indeed thirsty. He did not refuse and followed her into a large temporary wooden building behind her.

Several female staff members Ethan recognized from the Gym were inside. When they saw him, they covered their mouths and giggled, boldly studying him with heated, unconcealed gazes.

Ethan could hardly bear the attention. He hurriedly nodded a greeting and followed Erika into a small reception room at the back of the building.

Upon entering, he first noticed the faint scent of perfume blended with flowers. Although pleasant, it still made his nose itch slightly.

The room itself was elegant and refined despite belonging to a temporary building.

Most striking of all, perhaps because of Erika's personality or love of nature, green plants and carefully trimmed flowers stood everywhere. Surrounded by them, Ethan felt an immediate warmth.

Still...

The intensely feminine atmosphere made him feel somewhat out of place. For some reason, his mouth even became dry.

Erika did not notice anything unusual. She knelt on the tatami across from him and prepared a pot of hot tea for them.

"Please excuse the surroundings." She placed a cup before Ethan and smiled when she noticed him looking around. "This wooden building is the Gym's temporary facility. We use it to handle our daily work. Since we cannot hold battles here, it was built rather simply."

"This is already wonderful." Ethan took a sip of tea, composed himself, and offered a compliment. "For a wanderer like me, a house like this is the home I dream of having!"

At his praise, Erika covered her mouth and laughed softly, her mood brightening considerably.

She had battled Ethan and witnessed his strength. They had also fought Team Rocket side by side, and Ethan had protected her several times inside its base. Although Erika had never said so aloud, she had long regarded him as a friend.

Receiving praise from a friend was undoubtedly a joyful thing.

Erika's smile was infectious and made her even more charming. She radiated a gentle warmth completely different from Sabrina's.

A woman glowing like this was undeniably attractive.

After only two glances, Ethan did not dare look any longer and hurriedly turned away. He cleared his throat and guided the conversation directly toward its purpose before his thoughts could wander.

"You said you wanted to visit Johto and catch Grass-type Pokemon. Do you have a specific target in mind? If I know what you need, I can make the right recommendations."
