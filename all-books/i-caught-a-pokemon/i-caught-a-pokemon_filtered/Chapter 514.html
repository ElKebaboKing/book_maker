Chapter 514: The Executives Are Captured

Athena's idea was sound enough.

To surround the executives, Ethan's Pokemon had spread out. All of them were some distance away, too far to rescue him at once.

And Tyranitar's Hyper Beam was immensely powerful. If it struck Ethan, it would leave nothing of him behind.

What Athena did not know was why, with so many Pokemon helping to surround them, Ethan had kept Gengar at his side.

Hyper Beam shot toward Ethan, its tremendous energy seeming to warp the air around it.

Ethan did not move. He looked wholly unconcerned for his safety.

Seeing him neither dodge nor try to block, Athena's face twisted into a savage grin.

What a fool. He would regret his arrogance in a moment!

No. He probably would not even have time for regret.

The beam closed the distance in an instant. Ethan could almost feel its heat.

He would have liked to dodge it himself, if he could. But Hyper Beam was too fast and covered too wide an area. How could a human body get clear in time?

At the last possible moment, Gengar flashed in front of him. With a cackling laugh, it formed a Shadow Ball and hurled it straight into the Hyper Beam.

Boom!

The two attacks exploded the instant they met.

A tremendous burst of energy spread outward from the impact. Dazzling light swallowed the Pokemon nearby.

The shock wave whipped Athena's bangs across her face. She raised a hand against the blast, then hurriedly looked toward Ethan as the light faded. He was still standing there, completely unharmed!

Gengar had shielded him from every trace of the explosion's force.

Damn it!

Athena knew Ethan had a Gengar of unfathomable strength, but she had never imagined it could stop a Hyper Beam.

The two moves were not remotely equal in power. Hyper Beam could shatter rocks and melt trees and buildings. Yet that small Shadow Ball had canceled it out!

She could hardly believe her eyes. Gengar was absurdly strong!

The explosion also seemed to guide the police cars in the distance. Their sirens grew louder. Brakes squealed, and several cars entered the open ground and stopped at the edge of the battlefield.

Looker and Erika were first to step out. Before they could take in the scene, two flashes of red light showed that Ethan had recalled something into Poke Balls.

Looker gave it no thought. He hurried over to Ethan and let out a breath of relief when he saw him unharmed.

Then he looked across the battlefield. Ethan's Pokemon had Apollo and Athena surrounded. Looker's face brightened.

"You really are something. You caught them!"

Ethan shook his head. "It's a little early to say I've caught them. I have them trapped, though."

Erika came to Ethan's side as well. She paid no attention to Team Rocket, looking him over carefully instead.

"Are you all right?"

"What could have happened to me?" Ethan said with a smile. "Those two are the ones in trouble."

"Good. That explosion frightened me. I was worried about you."

The more time Ethan spent with Erika, the more certain he became that she would make a wonderful wife and mother one day.

By then, a large number of officers had surrounded the area. Ethan waved his Pokemon back, and the officers' Pokemon immediately took their places.

Looker and his officers could handle what remained. Ethan was no longer needed in the fight.

He stepped back with Erika and watched.

Looker faced Apollo and Athena. "Surrender now. You have nowhere left to run!"

Even trapped, the two executives would not give up without a fight. They ordered their Pokemon to attack again. But they were facing several times their number in trained officers, and even a Pokemon as strong as Tyranitar gradually lost ground.

Golbat and Kabutops fell. Lickitung and Vileplume ran out of strength too.

Finally, the officers' Pokemon subdued Sneasel and Persian. The two Team Rocket executives had no strength left with which to resist.

Officers rushed in and fastened handcuffs around their wrists.

With the matter settled, Ethan felt a great weight lift from his chest.

It was finally over...

Without their Pokemon, even executives were ordinary people.

He still did not know what had happened between Giovanni and the four executives. But with Apollo, Athena, and Lance captured, Team Rocket had undoubtedly lost much of its strength.

The days when Team Rocket could ambush him seemed far behind him. Giovanni, hidden away in the shadows, was unlikely to come looking for trouble either.

Apart from Giovanni remaining at large, everything seemed to be moving toward a happy conclusion.

Looker had the two executives placed in a police car and gave his officers a few more instructions. When he was done, he returned to Ethan.

"Ethan, my friend, I can't thank you enough for your help today!"

Ethan waved a hand lazily. They knew each other too well for formalities.

Looker glanced at Erika, cleared his throat, and put an arm around Ethan's shoulders to lead him aside.

"Ahem. Ethan, my friend, your Pokemon are so powerful that nothing seems beyond them. I admire that. But there's something I ought to tell you. Miss Erika is a fine woman, but Miss Sabrina is wonderful too. Don't try to keep a foot in both camps and end up losing them both."

Ethan was a friend Looker trusted and got along with, and he was especially popular with women. Looker did not want him to make a mess of his relationships.

Ethan felt a little embarrassed. He had not expected even Looker to worry about his love life. "Don't worry. I know what I'm doing."

Yet despite saying it, he still did not know what to do about his feelings for Erika.

He could never bring himself to give her up. Pretending he felt nothing for her would be lying to himself.

But if he followed his heart and accepted Erika's feelings, where would that leave Sabrina?

He sighed. What a headache.
