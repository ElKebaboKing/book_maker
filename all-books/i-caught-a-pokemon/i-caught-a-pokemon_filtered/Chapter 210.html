Chapter 210: Two Ways Forward

Ash and the others had seen the legendary Zapdos and Moltres, been hauled aboard an airship for no reason they understood, and met the man who claimed to own both birds. Too much had happened too quickly for them to think clearly.

Only after the collector vanished into the ceiling again did they begin to recover. He had barely acknowledged them. Perhaps he saw them as ordinary bystanders caught up in his plans, and that was why he had left them loose in the hold.

The room fell quiet. Ash looked around and scratched his head. "What do we do now? Should we free them?"

Melody was more concerned with the stone tablet. She stepped up to its case and studied it closely.

"It says exactly what our prophecy has said for generations. I don't know where he found it, but we have to stop him. If he breaks the balance between the three gods, the fury of the heavens and earth could destroy the world!"

"Destroy the world?" Misty frowned. She had heard the warning more than once now. "Aren't you overstating it? They're three legendary Pokemon. Even if someone captures them, how much could that affect the whole world?"

"Don't underestimate this!" Melody said. "The prophecy has been passed down on Shamouti Island for hundreds of years. If its conditions are met, disaster will follow!"

Misty shook her head, unconvinced. She trusted Ethan's judgment more than an old tale.

"What do you think, Ethan?" she asked.

Instead of answering, Ethan asked, "Have any of you heard of the butterfly effect?"

"The butterfly effect? What's that?"

Everyone turned toward him. Ethan gathered his thoughts.

"A small change in the starting conditions of a complex system can cause a much larger chain of effects over time. That's the butterfly effect."

Their blank faces told him he needed an example. "Imagine a Butterfree in Kanto flapping its wings a few times. Weeks later, those small changes might contribute to a huge storm in Johto."

"Really?" Ash's eyes widened. "A Butterfree could make a hurricane just by flapping its wings?"

"It sounds far-fetched, but the idea has a scientific basis." Ethan turned to Melody. "Shamouti Island lies near the center of the Orange Islands. Is there a major ocean current nearby?"

"Yes. A current runs through the deep water near Shamouti. It splits into three around the islands of the gods, then flows toward different seas."

"That's what I suspected," Ethan said. "The God of Thunder, the God of Fire, and the God of Ice could affect their surroundings the way that Butterfree affects the air. If they fought, storms and lightning from the battle could spread through the region and affect other places within hours or weeks. Shamouti would be hit first. If the fighting devastated the island people live on, wouldn't that feel almost like the end of the world to them?"

Shamouti Island was small. There was little room to escape a tsunami or another disaster. A single catastrophe could kill its inhabitants.

"I... I see," Ash said. He had not followed every part of the explanation, but he understood what mattered. He clenched a fist. "Then we need to free these two and let them return to their islands. That should keep them from fighting, right?"

"Exactly!" Melody said. "We have to restore things to how they were. That's how we'll avoid a disaster."

They had agreed, but Ethan rubbed his chin and said nothing at first. There was another way to look at it. If the collector captured all three birds, none would remain on its island to fight the others. In that sense, captivity could remove the immediate danger to people for good.

These were the legendary Pokemon of Shamouti and the wider Orange Islands, though. The choice belonged to the people who lived here. After a moment's hesitation, Ethan laid out both possibilities for Melody.

The others fell silent. They had not thought of his second approach, and all eyes turned to the festival maiden. Melody's brow tightened as she weighed the choice.

At last she set her jaw. "No. I can't stand by while the God of Thunder and the God of Fire fall into a villain's hands. The three gods have lived in peace for hundreds of years. I believe they can continue to do so!"

"Good choice," Ethan said. "I'd make the same one. Let's get these two out of their cages."

Ash was the first to rush toward Moltres. He grabbed the bars, intending to bend them apart by force, but a burst of energy threw him backward the instant he touched them.

"Idiot," Misty sighed, covering her face. "If those cages can hold legendary Pokemon, they must have powerful defenses. You weren't going to break one with your hands."

Ash blushed. "I knew that! I was testing how strong it was. Pikachu, use Thunderbolt!"

Pikachu fired a fierce electric attack into the cage. The glare faded. The bars were untouched.

"It's that strong?" Ash stared.

The airship shuddered. Alarmed, the group hurried to a window and looked out. After a short flight, the vessel had reached another island and was battling a large bird Pokemon. Shells shot from its cannons while the bird dodged and answered with blasts of ice.

Melody recognized it at once. "Oh no! The God of Ice has appeared too!"
