Chapter 228: Charizard vs. Ninetales

A flash of blue light brought Rudy's first Pokemon onto the battlefield. Ethan looked over and found himself facing a Ninetales.

It resembled a fox, with gleaming golden fur and nine long tails. Its eyes were a vivid red, but they made it look noble and elegant rather than frightening.

Ethan recognized it at once. Ninetales had been among the Pokemon dancing at the Gym. It looked no different from any other member of its species. He wondered whether Rudy's training method would produce any unusual results in battle. Rudy must have confidence in it, at least, to send it against Charizard.

Still, after all Rudy's talk, Ethan had half expected him to send out a Charizard of his own. Charizard was both Fire and Flying type, while Ninetales was only Fire type. Their types matched in one respect but not the other. Apparently Rudy did not have enough Pokemon to make every matchup identical.

It was hardly a serious problem. Battles between Pokemon with exactly the same types could show which was stronger and which Trainer commanded better, but they also lost some variety and suspense.

One of Rudy's attendants served as referee from the hot-air balloon. Sabrina and Mahri leaned forward to watch the battlefield below. From up there, they had a clear view of the entire field.

Sabrina had absolute faith in Ethan. Mahri adored her brother just as unreservedly. Neither believed the person she supported could lose.

When everyone was ready, the referee blew a whistle. Ethan gave the first command.

"Charizard, Wing Attack!"

Charizard beat its wings and climbed, circling to build speed.

Ethan had two reasons not to open with a Fire-type move. Ninetales would take only half the usual damage from fire, and he first needed to work out which Ability it had.

Ninetales generally had Flash Fire or Drought. Flash Fire was troublesome: a Fire-type attack would be absorbed and strengthen Ninetales's own Fire-type moves. Drought, on the other hand, would turn the weather harshly sunny when Ninetales entered the field.

A few clouds still drifted across the sky, briefly blocking the sun. If this Ninetales had Drought, Ethan expected them to clear and the sky around them to brighten. Nothing had changed, so its Ability had to be Flash Fire.

Across the field, Rudy's expression did not change when Ethan refrained from using fire. He had not expected it, yet it made sense. His Ninetales did indeed have Flash Fire, giving it an advantage against Fire-type opponents.

At least Ethan understood Abilities. He was not one of those beginners who ordered move after move without adapting their tactics to an opponent's strengths.

"Ninetales, Flamethrower!"

Rudy had fewer reservations. Whether Charizard had Blaze or Solar Power, he judged that the first offered Ethan no help here and the second was hampered by the conditions. Ninetales's flames would deal only half damage, but it could keep firing from a distance and wear Charizard down.

Charizard was already diving. As the flames rushed toward it, it turned slightly and slipped past them with ease. It swept toward Ninetales like the wind.

"Ninetales, dodge!" Rudy shouted.

Years of dance practice had at least made Ninetales nimble. It sprang to the side and avoided the attack. As the two Pokemon passed, Charizard's back was exposed for an instant. Rudy seized the opening.

"Now! Quick Attack!"

Ninetales launched itself like an arrow and slammed into Charizard. Charizard staggered back and swung a claw at it, but Ninetales nimbly evaded that too.

"Good! Use Confuse Ray!" Rudy called.

Confuse Ray was a Ghost-type move that could leave its target confused for a time. Like many Pokemon, Ninetales could use moves beyond its own type. Alongside common Normal-type moves, it had a few Ghost-type techniques.

Ethan knew that already. He also knew Ninetales could learn Psychic-type moves. The hit Charizard had taken did not shake him.

"Charizard, Dragon Rage!"

Before Confuse Ray reached it, Charizard fired a shock wave from its mouth.

Boom!

The two attacks met in a fierce explosion. Because it happened so close, smoke swallowed Charizard completely. Rudy strained to see whether the blast had caught it.

Then Charizard emerged from the smoke, and several crescent blades of air struck Ninetales hard. Air Slash!

Ninetales cried out in pain and fell. Rudy's expression changed.

"Ninetales! Get up!"

Charizard flew clear of the smoke and headed straight for Ninetales as it struggled to rise.

"Well done! Charizard, Seismic Toss!" Ethan shouted.

Charizard closed in and wrapped its thick forelimbs around Ninetales, pinning its joints so it could not move. Beating its wings, it carried Ninetales high into the air.

They soon rose level with the hot-air balloon. Mahri could clearly see the panic in Ninetales's eyes.

Charizard turned and plunged toward the ground. Ninetales seemed to realize what was coming and struggled desperately, but it could not break free.

"It's over," Sabrina said quietly. Mahri heard her and covered her eyes in fear.

Charizard spun faster as it descended. The hard stone rushed closer. Rudy could do nothing but watch as Ninetales was driven into the mountaintop.

Boom!

The ground shook. For a moment, the entire small mountain seemed to tremble.
