Chapter 370: Fire, Water, Thunder, and Ice

Raikou resembled the saber-toothed tigers of ancient Earth. It had golden fur, powerful limbs, sharp fangs, and a pale blue tail shaped like a bolt of lightning.

Beyond those features, its most memorable trait was the X-shaped whiskers beneath the black metal crest on its forehead.

Compared with the majestic Entei and composed Suicune, Raikou appeared far more ferocious.

Its gaze and sharp claws also made its volatile, dangerous temperament plain to see.

Watching secretly from the shadows, refusing to reveal itself carelessly, and waiting for an opening to deliver a lethal strike all suited the habits of feline predators. Raikou closely matched the creatures that had inspired its design.

Since this was Ethan's first time seeing one in person, he took out his Pokedex and pointed it at Raikou.

The Pokedex beeped. "Raikou, the Thunder Pokemon. An Electric type and one of Johto's Legendary Pokemon. A member of Ho-Oh's guardians. Raikou is said to have descended with lightning. This fierce Pokemon carries the speed of lightning within its body. Because it bears rain clouds upon its back, it can release lightning at any time. It roams the land while discharging its surging power in the form of thunderbolts. Its long cry resembles a lightning strike, shaking the air and making the earth tremble!"

Judging by the Pokedex's description, Raikou fully deserved its reputation as a Legendary beast.

Then again, hiding during a battle of this intensity had never been realistic. Raikou could not remain uninvolved. As the fighting became fiercer and spread across a wider area, it would inevitably have been forced to reveal itself sooner or later.

What surprised Ethan was that the moment Raikou appeared, it joined the battle without hesitation and engaged Zapdos directly.

Raikou had clearly spent a long time observing from hiding and realized that the three birds had come for all of them.

Whether driven by instinct or an awareness of the danger, it joined forces with Entei and Suicune against the three Legendary birds.

Ethan guessed that Raikou chose Zapdos because both were Electric types. After sensing the powerful current within its opponent, Raikou must have wanted to determine which of them was stronger.

Whatever the reason, Raikou's arrival instantly turned the battle into a three-on-three contest. No longer at a numerical disadvantage, the Legendary beasts finally restored the balance.

The battle continued.

Six Legendary Pokemon clashed across the battlefield, and the energy leaking from their attacks transformed the weather into something extraordinarily strange.

A fierce wind rose amid flashes of lightning and crashes of thunder. Then rain mixed with shards of ice suddenly poured from the sky.

The six Pokemon darted and leaped through the storm. Repeated explosions tore vast sheets of crystal from the tower, making it appear ready to collapse at any moment.

Night had now fallen completely. The rain and snow grew heavier as visibility worsened until they could barely see in front of themselves.

Gardevoir used psychic power to form a barrier above Ethan, keeping the rain from reaching her Trainer. Even so, the frigid air made him pull his clothes tighter around himself.

The darkness prevented Ethan from seeing the battlefield clearly. Even the six Pokemon could perceive little more than the vague outlines of their opponents.

This made the battle considerably more difficult.

At that moment, another deafening crack sounded. Lightning fell from the clouds and streaked across the sky, briefly illuminating the entire battlefield.

Using that flash, Ethan hurriedly surveyed the situation.

He had captured the three Legendary birds long ago. Their personalities kept them from accepting one another, but they shared the same Trainer and had spent considerable time adjusting to one another. In battle, they occasionally cooperated by instinct and produced several impressive combinations.

The three Legendary beasts, by contrast, fought together against the birds but still acted independently. They had no coordination and sometimes had to evade one another's attacks to avoid being caught in the crossfire.

Even so...

Although his side retained a slight advantage, Ethan remained extremely tense.

When the battle had been three against two, victory had seemed certain. Now, Ethan could no longer predict the final result.

Pokemon were intelligent creatures. If the three Legendary beasts realized the battle was turning against them and decided to flee, even the three Legendary birds could probably stop only one or two. Capturing all three would be impossible.

I hope all three are stubborn enough to fight instead of retreating!

After the lightning faded, the weather changed without warning. The rain stopped abruptly and gave way to a raging snowstorm, while flaming meteors fell from the sky.

Rumble!!

The battle could be heard from far away, all the way to the Pokemon Center.

Professor Oak, his assistant Schuyler, Nurse Joy, Officer Jenny, Lisa, and Erika stood on a nearby rise and looked toward the crystal tower.

From their position, they could vaguely see three bird Pokemon entering and emerging from the clouds, sending fire, lightning, and frost toward the ground. Three Pokemon upon the crystal tower retaliated against the sky.

The female television reporter watched the battlefield excitedly nearby while her camera operator attempted to record the battle.

Unfortunately, although they could faintly identify the Pokemon, the distance was too great. Even at maximum zoom, the camera captured only blurred silhouettes.

When the reporter realized she could see nothing clearly, an idea came to her. She hurried to Professor Oak and held out her microphone.

"Professor Oak, why are Kanto's three Legendary birds battling Johto's three Legendary beasts?"

The question troubled her deeply. Six Legendary Pokemon had never gathered for a battle like this before.

Once the news aired, it was guaranteed to send ratings soaring.

Regrettably, the video quality was too poor to show what was truly happening. Even if they broadcast it, viewers would see nothing but blurry footage.

If only they could move closer.

The reporter desperately wanted to do so. But after Ethan and the others had slipped into the crystal tower, Officer Jenny had strictly forbidden anyone else from approaching the crystal forest.

"Well..."

Faced with the reporter's question, Professor Oak considered the matter and offered the most plausible explanation he could think of.

"Since Molly created Entei from her imagination, I suspect the Unown can also read other people's memories and create additional Pokemon from nothing. The three Legendary birds were probably formed from Ash and his friends' memories so they could oppose Molly's three Legendary beasts..."
