Chapter 411: Raikou Reappears

They might have hoped to finish the battle quickly, but the storm clouds arrived far too fast. Before the two Pokemon could determine a winner, a thick bolt of lightning struck the mountain forest near the Pokemon Center.

Crack!

The sound startled Marina, making her scream instinctively.

"Ah!"

After the lightning struck, dense black smoke began rising continuously from the forest.

"What happened? Did it hit something?"

Recovering from their surprise, Jimmy and Marina stared in that direction, confusion on both their faces.

Ethan turned and exchanged a glance with Erika.

"Should we go take a look?" Erika asked softly.

"Yes. We'd better check," Ethan said with a nod. "It'll be serious if that starts a fire."

Their concern was justified. Rain might fall soon, but once a fire began, it would damage the local ecosystem even if it was quickly extinguished. The trees were densely packed, allowing flames to spread rapidly and easily reach places the rain could not penetrate.

The unexpected development drew everyone's attention, making it impossible to continue the match. Without worrying about the coming rain, the four Trainers and their Pokemon ran toward the smoke.

Lightning flashed across the sky from time to time, raising everyone's tension with it.

After entering the forest and forcing their way through layer after layer of trees, they finally reached a wooded ravine.

Before they drew close, the crackle of electricity reached them from within the ravine.

That was definitely not the sound of lightning!

They slowed their steps and approached quietly. Parting the branches of the bushes ahead, they saw a crystal-like object in a clearing, continuously releasing electric current.

Before they could determine what it was, they spotted two men in uniforms marked with the letter R manipulating something beside the crystal.

Behind the men stood an enormous machine resembling a shark on land!

"It's Team Rocket!" Erika frowned.

"Team Rocket?" Jimmy and Marina looked confused. "What's that?"

Erika briefly explained, "They're a wicked criminal organization. They steal powerful Pokemon for their own selfish purposes and intend to rule the world!"

While Erika introduced Team Rocket, Ethan wondered why the organization had appeared here.

One of the men was powerfully built, with bronze skin, short blond hair, and sunglasses. He looked like a man of action.

The other was tall and slender, with a pale face and medium-length silver-gray hair. He appeared calm and sinister.

With their matching appearances, they resembled a legendary Gold Horn and Silver Horn. Such distinctive men were clearly no ordinary grunts.

Yet Ethan had no impression of either of them.

Their faces were completely unfamiliar!

Still...

He looked around and confirmed that this was an ordinary clearing. According to the map, there were no ruins, cities, or unusual mineral deposits nearby. Almost no one normally visited the area.

If that was true, what was Team Rocket's purpose here?

While the group secretly observed, Pokemon began emerging from the forest one after another and entering the clearing.

They slowly approached the crystal-like object as though drawn toward it. Then they stood motionless, staring at the crystal with curiosity.

Ethan's gaze swept across the Pokemon, and he quickly noticed the pattern.

"Look! Every Pokemon coming here is an Electric type!"

At his words, Erika looked more carefully. He was right. The crystal had attracted Magnemite, Voltorb, Mareep, Pikachu, and their evolved forms!

"Could Team Rocket be using that crystal to lure and capture Electric-type Pokemon!?"

"Most likely. Even the lightning strike just now may have been caused by the crystal." Ethan considered it for a moment. "But catching Pokemon like this is normally a job for ordinary grunts. There would be no need to send executives or anyone of similar rank. Those two must be after something more than this!"

As soon as he finished speaking, the huge shark-shaped machine extended two clawed arms under the blond man's control and dragged several Mareep through a hatch.

Seeing its companions attacked, a Raichu fired an electric blast. Yet as the current traveled toward the shark machine, it abruptly changed direction in midair and was absorbed by the crystal!

That was not the end. After absorbing the electricity, the crystal reflected it back toward Raichu and the surrounding Pokemon with far greater power!

Struck by the blast, Raichu and the other Pokemon screamed in pain. They were knocked out instantly and collapsed to the ground.

What incredible power...

Ethan narrowed his eyes.

He had seen it clearly. The crystal did more than absorb electricity and reflect it. Some strange energy appeared to amplify the current tremendously!

"Hahahaha!" The blond man's wild laughter filled the clearing. "This is fantastic! These Pokemon can't put up any resistance at all! They're all lambs waiting for slaughter. There are more here than we could ever catch!"

As the mechanical arms continued moving, more and more Pokemon were dragged into the machine's compartment.

"Damn it! They're capturing Pokemon this way! I won't let them. I have to stop them!"

Jimmy was clearly a hot-blooded young man. The moment he saw what was happening, he prepared to rush out and stop the pair. Before he could move, however, another bolt of lightning struck nearby.

At the same time, the clouds overhead formed a swirling vortex.

Under everyone's watchful gaze, another enormous, dazzling bolt descended. Within the golden light, a Pokemon resembling both a leopard and a tiger appeared atop a high cliff, majestic and proud, its entire body radiating power.

"That is..."

Jimmy stared at the Pokemon for a long moment before abruptly realizing something. He hurriedly took out his Pokedex and pointed it toward the cliff.

"There's no need to identify it." Ethan drew a deep breath and said calmly, "That's Raikou."

Hearing that this was Raikou, one of Johto's three Legendary beasts, Jimmy and Marina became intensely excited.

"Raikou!?"

"That's Raikou!?"

Ethan watched the Pokemon as well.

Remembering the Raikou he had nearly captured in Greenfield, he could not be certain whether this was the same one.

But now that Raikou had appeared, one thing was clear: Team Rocket had come here for it!
