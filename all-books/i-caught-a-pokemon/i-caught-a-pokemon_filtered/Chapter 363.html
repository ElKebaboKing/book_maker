Chapter 363: Molly

If Professor Oak could see them on television, then unless Ethan was mistaken, Molly could also learn from the live news broadcast that they had already entered the flower-shaped tower!

Seriously...

After traveling so carefully for so long, they had been exposed by a television station at the very end. All their efforts had gone to waste!

At that moment, Professor Oak seemed to snatch the phone away on the other end. They heard him complain, "You slipped off there without saying a word! Why couldn't you wait until nightfall before acting!?"

Hearing the reproach in his voice, Ash looked embarrassed and hurriedly apologized. "I'm sorry, Professor. This was all my idea. Ethan and the others had nothing to do with it! They came to stop me, but I insisted on going, so they had no choice but to take the risk with me..."

Fortunately, Professor Oak did not truly hold the matter against him. In fact, even if Ash tore a hole in the sky, Professor Oak would never seriously punish him. He was merely angry that they had acted without notifying him first.

They had treated him like an outsider!

Besides, entrusting the matter to Ethan and the others had been his plan from the beginning. Why had they been so impatient?

But there was no point blaming Ash now. Professor Oak sighed and said helplessly, "All right. Since you've already reached the manor, I won't tell you to come back. We'll have to rely on you from here! You must rescue Delia!"

The moment he finished, Schuyler suddenly interrupted from nearby. "After you entered, did you find any Unown!?"

"Unown? No!"

"I see..." Professor Oak pondered their answer. "According to Professor Hale's papers and notes from before his disappearance, Unown appear able to sense the consciousness of other living beings. In other words, they can perceive a person's thoughts and give those thoughts physical form. We therefore believe all the crystallization in Greenfield was created after the Unown sensed Molly's thoughts!"

"Is there any way to stop it?"

"The only solution I can think of is to find those Unown and use opposing thoughts to reverse the crystallization!"

At this point, Misty could no longer resist interrupting. "Professor, do Unown really possess that much power?"

"A single Unown naturally couldn't accomplish this," Professor Oak explained. "That is why we believe there may be more than one inside! You must be extremely careful!"

Before ending the call, Professor Oak added, "Of course, if the situation becomes too difficult, protecting yourselves comes first. The rescue team will arrive tonight! Don't try to force your way through!"

After the call ended, Ash breathed a sigh of relief that Professor Oak had not criticized him. At the same time, he was a little unconvinced that the professor thought so little of their abilities.

"Honestly, Professor Oak! He's seriously underestimating us! We don't need any rescue team at all!"

Ethan shook his head and smiled. "He means well. He was invited here as the Pokemon Professor responsible for resolving this incident, so he has to consider every possibility. He can't simply ignore the danger we're facing."

With the call finished, the group continued onward.

They crossed the pools, entered the conservatory, and passed through a long connecting corridor before finally reaching the interior of the manor.

They had still encountered no danger, which allowed their constantly tense nerves to relax slightly.

The interior branched in every direction. After being consumed by crystal, it had become a labyrinth. Without hesitation, the group began climbing toward the top of the tower.

Even though they had never visited the manor before, they knew Molly and Delia had to be up there!

They made their way through interlocking crystal formations and reached an upper floor. Suddenly, the scenery before them changed. Every crystal vanished, replaced by a vast, lush grassland!

Blue sky and white clouds stretched above them. Everything appeared peaceful and serene.

"How is this possible!?"

Ash and the others stared in astonishment, scarcely able to believe their eyes.

They were inside the crystal tower. The entire structure was completely enclosed and sealed from the outside, so how could there possibly be bright weather and open grassland here?

It defied all common sense!

Ethan surveyed their surroundings. Seeing that Ash and the others were still stunned, he warned them, "Don't be deceived by what you see! Just as Professor Oak said, this should all be something Molly imagined. It's her inner world. If you don't believe me, pay close attention. You can't feel even the slightest breeze, can you? And there's no warmth from the sun."

At his words, the others concentrated on their senses and finally noticed what was wrong.

Just as Ethan had said, everything looked beautiful and pleasing, but it undeniably felt false.

It was like a painting.

Just then, Misty pointed toward the distant sky and cried, "Look! There's a staircase over there!"

"That must be the path to the next floor. Let's hurry."

The group ran toward the staircase. Before they reached the halfway point, however, Entei suddenly leaped down from above!

A woman in her twenties rode upon its back.

She was beautiful, with long, curling blonde hair. She wore a miniskirt, knee-high socks, and a pair of large earrings, giving her an extremely fashionable appearance.

Ethan and the others stopped at once.

At first glance, the woman seemed vaguely familiar. After looking more carefully, they realized she resembled the photograph of Molly that Delia had shown them!

It was incredibly strange.

According to Schuyler, no one except Molly remained inside the crystal tower. But Molly was still a young child and could not possibly be this old.

Who was she?

Before they could speak, the woman looked down at them from Entei's back. "Are you Pokemon Trainers?"

For some reason, Misty felt an instinctive hostility toward her. She shouted, "What if we are? Who are you?"

The woman ignored her completely. Instead, she looked directly at Ethan and Ash, folded her arms, and smiled. "You two should battle me!"

"Battle?" Ash shook his head and refused without hesitation. "I don't know who you are, but we don't have time to waste here!"

Then Ash turned his gaze toward Entei.

"Entei! Where did you take my mom!?"

Unexpectedly, his words seemed to strike a nerve in the woman. She shouted, "No! Your mom isn't here! Only my papa and mama are here!"

With those words, she revealed her identity.

She really was Molly!
