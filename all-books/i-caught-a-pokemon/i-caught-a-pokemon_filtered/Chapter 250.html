Chapter 250: A Late-Night Talk in the Tent

Yes!

Misty almost laughed aloud when Ethan agreed to let her sleep in the tent. She had no wish to lie next to Sabrina, so she sat down on Ethan's other side without worrying about how it might look. He was now between the two of them.

She and Ethan had hardly spent any time alone since parting at the Indigo League. Here was a rare chance to talk without Ash getting in the way. She had so much to say that she chattered like a happy bird, scarcely minding that Sabrina was there. Before long, the tent was filled with her voice and laughter.

Sabrina gave her a sidelong glance. She did not want to interfere with Ethan's friendships. Besides, Misty was still a young teenager and no threat to their relationship. Sabrina did not enjoy listening to the two of them laugh together, though. She took headphones from her bag, put them on, and lay down with her eyes closed.

Misty noticed. With Sabrina no longer listening, she became even more open about how much she had missed Ethan.

"Ethan, guess who I dreamed about last night?"

"Who?" he asked, though he could guess.

"You, of course!" Her cheeks turned pink, but she met his eyes. "I dreamed about when we went to the hot springs on Cinnabar Island. Isn't it funny? Only a few hours later, we met again. My dream came true!"

The hot springs on Cinnabar Island. Ethan remembered that visit, and his nose began to itch. She had brought it up on purpose.

If Misty were a few years older, her hints might have been harder for him to brush aside. As it was, she was much too young. He pretended not to understand, gave a few vague answers, and quickly changed the subject.

Misty did not seem to notice. Even if she had, simply talking with Ethan made her happy.

A light breeze stirred the tent and made its wind flap quiver. Sabrina fell asleep, so Ethan lowered his voice. He and Misty chatted about other things until after eleven, when Ash and Tracey began snoring outside. Then he urged her to sleep.

"You need your rest, Misty. You won't grow properly if you don't get enough sleep. And you don't want your skin to lose its glow, do you?"

"All right..."

Reluctantly, she lay down and grew quiet. Sleeping so close to Ethan felt like a dream. They were in the same small tent, breathing the same air. Was this what it meant to live under one roof?

Ethan switched off the portable lamp. The tent went dark. Misty listened to his steady breathing but kept tossing and turning. After a long while, she rolled over to look at his face.

There was too little light to see more than his outline through a gap in the tent. She smiled to herself anyway. She thought he looked handsome even in profile. If only she could see him every day.

Her thoughts wandered until sleep finally caught up with her. She drifted off with a contented smile.

Ethan let out a breath. Misty might be young, but she spoke her mind and was bold enough that she could be harder to handle than Sabrina. Now that she was asleep, he could rest too.

...

Late that night, the camp was still. The only sound came when Quackenpoker added wood to the fire and the logs crackled.

Knowing that his age made it harder to stay awake all night, he had brewed himself a pot of strong tea. Its bitter taste and the stimulating compounds in it helped him resist sleep. A hot mouthful also drove off the cold and warmed him through.

A noise from the lake caught his attention. Had the Magikarp begun to evolve together? He sprang up and looked toward the water.

...

In the tent, Ethan heard a low mechanical hum through his sleep. At first he ignored it, but it went on until he could no longer rest. He forced his eyes open, felt around for the portable lamp, and switched it on.

It was only three in the morning. He had slept for less than four hours.

He was about to get up and see what was happening when he realized something was wrong. Looking down, he found Misty had somehow slipped into his bedroll. He was holding her in his arms.

Ethan went rigid. He had grown used to sleeping beside Sabrina and must have mistaken Misty for her in his half-asleep state. At least he had not done anything worse, though Misty's pajamas had become a little disarranged.

Her brow tightened as if she might wake. Ethan quickly drew his hand out from beneath her and acted as though nothing had happened.

A few seconds later, Misty opened her eyes and saw Ethan getting dressed. She sat up, still sleepy.

"Ethan? What's wrong? Did something happen?"

She felt a draft at her collar and realized that one button of her pajamas had come undone. She covered herself and turned slightly aside. Nothing else seemed out of place.

How had she ended up in Ethan's bedroll?

Before she could think it through, he answered. "I'm not sure. There's a noise outside. Let's go have a look."

Sabrina woke too. All three got dressed and left the tent. The night air was cold enough to sting, but it drove the last of Ethan's sleepiness away.

Ash and Tracey were climbing out of their sleeping bags, yawning. They looked at one another. None of them knew what had happened.

The campfire still burned, though much smaller than before. Quackenpoker stood beside the lake with binoculars, watching the water.
