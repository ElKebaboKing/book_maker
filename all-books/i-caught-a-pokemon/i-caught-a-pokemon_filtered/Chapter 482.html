Chapter 482: The Succession Rite

Maya had already received the news by telephone.

When the speedboat reached the harbor on Red Rock Isle that afternoon, Ethan spotted her from afar. Dressed in a long white robe and holding her golden scepter, she waited at the dock with a group of attendants.

Even standing still, the Sea Priestess drew every eye. Her long purple hair moved in the wind, framing her beautiful face.

As soon as the boat docked, Ethan and the others stepped onto the pier. Maya greeted them with a warm smile that still held a hint of regal reserve.

"Welcome back, my Sea Hero. And welcome to you, the Hero's assistants!"

She offered Ethan the back of her smooth, pale hand.

My Sea Hero...

He found the form of address a little odd, but thought no more of it. Perhaps it was simply how the Sea Priestess addressed the Sea Hero.

Ash and the others noticed that being called the Hero's assistants placed them a step below Ethan, turning companions into followers. None of them minded much.

They had spent enough time beside him to grow used to standing in his shadow.

Ethan recognized the offered hand as a matter of etiquette. He bowed and lightly kissed it.

Behind him, Brock wanted to copy the gesture and steal a kiss for himself. By the time he bent forward, however, Maya had already withdrawn her hand!

He stood there heartbroken, like a statue drained of all color. His misery was so complete that those nearby could almost hear his fragile heart shatter.

Here we go again...

Erika and Ash knew Brock well and had seen this happen often enough during their travels to be entirely untroubled. Richie and Oliver, on the other hand, looked genuinely worried.

"Is... is he all right?"

"Don't worry about him." Misty waved a hand without so much as blinking. "This happens every so often. You'll get used to it."

Meanwhile, Maya looked at Ethan gently and said with a soft smile, "I've heard how you destroyed Team Rocket's base. Thank you for everything you did, and for protecting the God of the Sea. You must be exhausted after that journey."

Ethan and the others responded modestly.

"It was the least we could do."

After a brief exchange, Maya stepped aside. "I've had a lavish dinner prepared to welcome you back and celebrate your success. Before then, please come to the temple and rest."

Ash and Oliver were the most excited by the promise of a feast. Both still had the appetites of children, and no amount of good food ever seemed enough. A chance to eat their fill would be a fine reward for the effort of the past few days!

Escorted by the attendants, the group followed the stone path up the mountain.

Maya and Ethan led the way, with Erika, Ash, Richie, and Oliver following close behind. Only Misty paused beside Brock. With a weary sigh, she seized his ear and dragged him toward the Temple of the Sea while he remained frozen in despair.

Passersby glanced curiously at the procession. When they recognized Sea Priestess Maya and Ethan, the Sea Hero, they stared all the more.

What could have happened to bring the Sea Priestess and the Sea Hero out together?

People murmured among themselves. Fortunately, the Temple of the Sea commanded such respect here that, despite their curiosity, no one dared interrupt the group.

Following the path uphill, they soon reached the temple at the summit.

Like Ash on his first visit, Richie and Oliver immediately found their eyes drawn to the enormous statue of the God of the Sea.

To Oliver in particular, the Temple of the Sea was a sacred place. Simply standing there made him tremble with excitement!

If it could inspire that reaction in a child, it was easy to see what place the temple held in the hearts of the people of the Whirl Islands.

Maya arranged rooms where everyone could rest and recover. At around five in the afternoon, they were invited to the banquet hall.

Gentle music filled the room. A long table was laden with all kinds of food, while attendants played stringed instruments or stood nearby with pitchers of wine, ready to serve their honored guests.

Maya sat at the head of the table and raised a cup in their honor. As at the previous banquet, no other guests attended.

The group took seats along both sides of the table. With little ceremony, the feast began.

They all knew one another now, and even Maya no longer felt like a stranger. The celebration quickly grew lively!

The Whirl Islands produced a fruit wine made from a local grape. Lightly sweet and low in alcohol, it suited the occasion perfectly. Ethan and Erika drank some, and even Ash and the younger guests had quite a bit.

Cups passed around the table as the feast went on in a cheerful atmosphere.

Halfway through the meal, Maya suddenly left her seat and approached Ethan.

"My Hero, could I ask you for one more favor?"

Perhaps because she had drunk some of the fruit wine, a faint blush colored her cheeks. She looked less aloof than usual and a little more alluring.

Ethan set down his knife and fork and looked up at her. "Of course. What do you need?"

They had grown familiar during their time together. Besides, he was the temple's Sea Hero. Unless she asked something unreasonable, he saw no reason to refuse.

Perhaps his eyes deceived him, but Maya's cheeks seemed to redden even further at his answer!

Looking straight into his eyes, she said, "I'd like you to help me carry out the succession rite."

The succession rite?

Ethan glanced at the others and saw the same confusion on their faces.

Judging by its name, it sounded like an ancient ceremony of the Temple of the Sea. Since it was temple business, Ethan had no reason to turn her down.

"Of course. Do you mean now?"

Maya nodded and let out a long breath. She then addressed Erika and the others apologetically. "Please continue your meal. The succession rite will take a long time, so forgive us for leaving you without our company."
