Chapter 169: Meeting Lorelei

Beneath the white clouds, Charizard carried Ethan and Sabrina swiftly south across the sky.

The clouds shielded them from the sun, and the rushing wind drove away the summer heat. Even high above the sea, they were hardly warm. In fact, the cold air struck their faces hard enough that Sabrina pressed close against Ethan's back, trying to keep it off her bare skin.

Ethan felt her against him. His thoughts wandered for a moment, but he understood why she had moved closer. Without hesitation, he patted Charizard's neck and had it fly lower.

An endless blue sea stretched beneath them. They had been flying for a long time since leaving Blue Lagoon before a group of islands finally appeared in the distance.

From above, the islands seemed scattered at random. They varied in size, but none lay very far from the others. Green and isolated out at sea, they gave Ethan an impression of quiet, peaceful life far from the rest of the world.

"Sabrina, look! Islands up ahead!"

She leaned out to see them. Speaking softly into the headwind, she had to bring her mouth close to his ear for him to hear her.

"Are those the Orange Islands?"

Ethan took out his phone. After so long without reception, it had finally picked up a weak signal. People must live on at least some of the islands below. He checked their position, then shook his head.

"No. These are the Sevii Islands. We're still only halfway to the Orange Islands."

Sevii Islands? Sabrina had never heard the name. She had spent most of her life inland in Saffron City, rarely traveled far, and paid little attention to news about remote places.

Charizard turned toward the nearest island at Ethan's direction.

"Are we landing here?" she asked.

"Yes. The two of us and our luggage weigh around a hundred and thirty kilos. Charizard's been carrying us a long way. It needs to rest and get its strength back." Ethan looked toward the island. "Besides, there's something here I really want to see. Let's take a look while we're here."

Something he wanted to see? Sabrina raised an eyebrow. She had no idea what could interest him on an island that had never been part of their plans.

The Sevii Islands, also known as the Seven Islands, formed an archipelago south of mainland Kanto. As the name suggested, it consisted of seven large islands and a number of smaller ones nearby, near the far southern reaches of Kanto's seas. People lived on all seven main islands, each of which had a few small towns.

For convenience, the islands were numbered One Island, Two Island, and so on through Seven Island. But each also had a name of its own.

The island where Ethan and Sabrina were about to land was commonly called One Island. Its proper name was Knot Island, a name meant to express the welcome the Sevii Islands offered to travelers from afar.

Its town was small. Most residents made their living fishing, and there were no tall buildings. Apart from a well-equipped little dock, the most impressive building was the Pokemon Center in the middle of town.

Ethan guided Charizard down onto a beach outside the town.

Glittering sand spread as far as he could see, dotted with large pearls no one had picked up. In the sunlight, the whole beach seemed to sparkle. Ethan took a photo with his phone, then gathered a few smooth, round pearls and put them in his backpack as souvenirs.

He brushed the sand from his clothes, and the two of them walked into town toward the dock.

A small tavern stood nearby. Although the Pokemon Center offered free facilities, they stopped at the tavern close at hand instead. Ethan left Charizard outside with some food, then opened the door and went in with Sabrina.

The streets had been quiet, but there were plenty of people inside. Most looked like older fishermen, sitting in small groups in the corners and talking in low voices.

Every head turned when Ethan and Sabrina entered. The room fell silent. The fishermen watched them until they took seats at the bar, then turned back to their conversations.

"What can I get you?"

The proprietor was an older man too. He wiped an already spotless plate as he looked them up and down.

"Two glasses of iced water to start," Ethan said, unbothered by the scrutiny. "Do you have a local specialty? We'll have two servings of that as well."

"In that case, I can"

Before the proprietor could finish, a woman dropped onto the stool beside them.

"Give them two plates of our special Sevii seafood fried rice," she said. "Put it on my tab!"

Ethan and Sabrina both turned toward her.

Her long, bright red hair was tied in a ponytail. She wore a fitted skirt, black stockings, high heels, and glasses. She was attractive, with a thoughtful air about her, though Ethan's attention was drawn most of all to her ample figure.

That was impressive. He could not remember ever seeing anyone quite like her.

Like him and Sabrina, the woman looked out of place among the local fishermen. Yet neither the proprietor nor any of the patrons gave her a second glance.

Sabrina recognized her face and stared in surprise. "Miss Lorelei?"

"It's been a long time, Miss Sabrina." Lorelei smiled, then studied Ethan. "And if I'm not mistaken, you're the newly crowned winner of this year's Indigo League. Mr. Champion, is it?"

So it really was Lorelei! She was one of the Elite Four and a master of Ice-type Pokemon. Ethan had never expected to run into her here.
