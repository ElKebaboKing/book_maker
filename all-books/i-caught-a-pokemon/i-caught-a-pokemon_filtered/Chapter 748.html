Chapter 748: Making the Battlefield Favorable

Cotton Guard stopped Metang's Metal Claw. It also left Altaria tucked into its plumage, unable to move or counterattack.

Ethan took the opening. Metang used Psychic, dealing damage while an invisible force lifted Altaria high into the air.

Winona hesitated, then ordered Altaria to remain in its defensive posture. Most Trainers who lifted an opponent that high would slam it to the ground for a second hit. Cotton Guard could not protect Altaria from Psychic, but its cushioning would lessen the impact when it fell. Better to prepare now than react too late.

Her sensible prediction worked against her. Altaria hung in the air like a large, fluffy ball. Metang made no move to throw it down. Instead, it kept applying Psychic. After several seconds, Winona realized Ethan might never have intended to drop it at all.

Another seven or eight seconds passed with Altaria suspended in pain. She could not let it continue. Winona abandoned her plan and ordered Altaria to escape as it had before.

Ethan noticed the first movement. It was too late to start struggling now.

At the instant Altaria released Cotton Guard, he gave the command. Metang finally sent it plummeting toward the ground.

Winona could only watch. Altaria could not raise Cotton Guard again quickly enough, nor could it fire Dragon Pulse at the ground to push itself free as it had earlier. Ethan had waited for precisely this moment. She had to admit that his patience and timing were excellent. His perception alone set him apart from an ordinary Trainer.

Altaria struck the earth with a crash. Its plumage offered some protection, but the fall from that height, added to the damage it had already taken, knocked it unconscious.

The referee raised his flag. Altaria is unable to battle! Metang wins the first round!

As he spoke, white light enveloped Metang. Everyone at the arena recognized an evolution.

For Ethan, it was welcome news. Metang had traveled with him for some time and fought in many individual and group battles. Today, it finally evolved. Aside from Eevee, every Pokemon he currently owned had now reached its final form. His team had grown stronger again.

The glow faded, revealing a more imposing Pokemon: Metagross.

It looked almost as though two Metang had been joined. Its blue-green body resembled a spacecraft or lander, with four thick legs extending from its sides. It had red eyes and a striking silver X across its face.

Misty was delighted for Ethan, but she did not know much about Metagross. She pointed her Pokedex at it.

Beep. Metagross, the Iron Leg Pokemon. Steel and Psychic type. Research suggests its four brains connect into a complex neural network, making it more intelligent than a supercomputer. By processing information in parallel, the brains can complete calculations almost instantly. It analyzes opponents and defeats them with this computing power. Metagross can fold its four legs and float or fly. In addition to psychic power, it possesses tremendous physical strength. It can seize prey with all four legs and pin it with its claws. At temperatures below freezing, its magnetic force grows stronger, so Metagross living on snowy mountains are especially active.

Across the field, Winona could not share Ethan's pleasure. His Pokemon had evolved after beating hers. She shook her head, recalled Altaria, and took out her second Poke Ball.

A Pelipper appeared over the field. Ethan noticed it and returned his attention to the match. He kept Metagross out. It had taken little damage and could still fight. More importantly, it had only just evolved. This battle would help it learn the feel of its new body and abilities.

Winona was not surprised. Metagross had been strong even before evolving. It would be troublesome. At least the matchup had shifted in her favor: Pelipper was Water/Flying, so Metagross's Steel-type attacks would do only half their usual damage against it.

Both Pokemon moved into position. At the referee's signal, the second round began.

Pelipper was no weaker than Altaria. With less need to fear Steel-type moves, it concentrated on avoiding Metagross's psychic attacks. It flew in unpredictable directions, used Water Gun to keep its distance, and struck whenever Metagross left an opening.

Ethan offered almost no instructions. Having won the first round, he did not consider this one's outcome crucial. He wanted to see what Metagross could do without his direction and get a measure of its new strength.

The two Pokemon traded attacks for a while. Pelipper's best tactic seemed to be alternating long-range Hydro Pump with close-range Steel Wing. Moving between near and far attacks gave Metagross trouble.

But the Pokedex had been right about its intelligence. After taking only two hits, Metagross appeared to recognize the pattern. When Pelipper tried it again, Metagross seized the bird with several of its powerful claws.

Winona startled. Was she about to watch the previous round repeat itself? Pelipper, get free!

Pelipper could not break Metagross's grip. At first it could beat its wings and struggle. Metagross's claws did not budge. It even tried to catch Metagross in its huge beak, but Metagross was far too large to fit.

As Pelipper tired, the claws tightened. After a long struggle, its eyes rolled back and it fainted in Metagross's hold. Metagross's red eyes still gleamed. It did not let go until its opponent was completely unconscious.

The referee inspected Pelipper and raised his flag with a faint shake of his head. Pelipper is unable to battle! Metagross wins!

Metagross had won again without much difficulty, though it had taken some damage across the two rounds. It could probably fight once more, but a third round might leave it and its opponent both badly hurt. Ethan had seen what he wanted to see, so he recalled it.

Impressive, Winona said. Two losses in a row, both decisive, left her no choice but to acknowledge it. You've raised MetangMetagross, I should sayexceptionally well. My Pokemon barely had a chance to fight back.

Even an inexperienced spectator could have seen it. Winona knew Ethan was no ordinary challenger.

Your Altaria and Pelipper are strong too, Ethan replied. Against most challengers, they'd be difficult opponents.

Winona's expression eased. Then this is the last round. Let's see if I can win one back.

Her attitude had changed over the two battles. She now regarded Ethan with the respect due a strong opponent.

She threw her third Poke Ball. A Shiny Swellow appeared high in the air.

Wow! Misty cried from the stands. It's turquoise! I've never seen a Swellow that color. It's beautiful!

Compared with an ordinary dark-blue Swellow, its fresh coloring was certainly unusual. Whether it was prettier was a matter of taste. Its fierce gaze and sharp talons, however, were unmistakable.

After a moment's thought, Ethan chose Milotic.

He would have enjoyed meeting Winona in an aerial battle with Flying-type Pokemon, if he had another partner that needed training. Dragonite was the only Flying-type he carried, and it was too strong for this match to help it improve.

His regular team was still small. They had all grown into powerful partners, but gaps in type coverage sometimes limited his choices. His journey was not even halfway over, though. He would have more chances to expand it.

Milotic emerged onto the field. Pelipper's Water Guns and Hydro Pumps had already turned the earth muddy. That suited Milotic's scaled body, making it easier for the Pokemon to move. Neither Milotic nor Swellow had a clear type advantage, which made this round seem fairer than the last two.

Nature was another matter. A bird might seem to have an advantage over a snakelike creature in the food chain, but strength could overturn the roles of predator and prey. There was no simple rule for this match.

Swellow circled above while Milotic coiled on the ground. Both watched the other eagerly. The arrangement resembled the previous rounds, but these Pokemon would fight in their own ways.

The referee signaled the third round. At Ethan's order, Milotic attacked first. It sent a stream of water sweeping across the sky.

The blue arc traced a long line, but Swellow was too fast to hit so easily. It made a few circuits overhead, staying ahead of the water with room to spare.

Ethan had not expected a hit. The water fell across the arena, soaking the earth. Puddles began to form. Winona saw his intention at once and frowned.

He was reshaping the battlefield to favor Milotic.
