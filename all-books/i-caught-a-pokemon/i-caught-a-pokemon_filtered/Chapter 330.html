Chapter 330: Challenging Mahogany Gym

"That's right. I'm Pryce, the Leader of this Gym."

Age had turned his hair and eyebrows gray, but he was still sturdy. He did not walk with the unsteady steps of many older people, and his eyes remained sharp.

Strangely, in this chilly courtyard, he wore only a T-shirt, shorts, and open-toed sandals, as though he were on holiday on a tropical island. Ethan had to admire the old Ice-type Gym Leader's tolerance for cold.

Still, Pryce's voice had sounded rather stiff. Ethan looked back at the courtyard gate and apologized. "Sorry. I saw the gate was open and came in without announcing myself."

Pryce looked from one visitor to the other, then studied Ethan for a while. Fortunately, the uninvited entry did not seem to bother him much.

"Follow me."

He turned and headed back toward the Gym. Ethan and Sabrina exchanged a look, and Ethan shrugged.

Pryce did not seem fond of conversation and had an odd temper. But Ethan had come to challenge him. As long as Pryce would battle, his cold manner made no difference.

Ethan remembered why Pryce had supposedly become like this. When he was young, he had been burned in a battle and left bedridden. His Piloswine had gone away during that time and never returned. Pryce believed it had abandoned him. He lost trust in Pokemon and became strict and distant.

There was a misunderstanding behind the story, of course. Ethan had no wish to get involved. Ash could sort it out someday; he was always eager to help with such things.

Following Pryce inside, Ethan felt an even colder air than in the courtyard. It must be more than ten degrees below freezing in here.

He pulled his clothes tighter and looked around. An enormous ice battlefield occupied the middle of the Gym. Ice pillars of many sizes stood across it. The field radiated cold like a giant block of ice, making the whole room feel like a freezer.

There was a deep pool beneath the ice, but its surface had frozen solid. Unless someone deliberately broke it, even large Pokemon such as Gyarados or Steelix could battle on top without suddenly falling through.

Ethan's breath became a white cloud that quickly vanished. Beyond the field, the Gym was sparsely furnished. A few rows of seats stood in the spectator area, and the rest of the large room was empty.

At Pryce's direction, Ethan walked to the opposite Trainer's position.

"You've arrived early," Pryce said. "Sheila, our referee, can't get here this soon. I'll handle the judging myself. Any objection?"

"None."

To Ethan, a referee was not essential. He and Pryce could tell who had won. A referee mainly made the match feel more formal and impartial.

Pryce explained the rules as usual. "The Mahogany Gym challenge has three rounds. Defeat all three of my Pokemon and you succeed."

It sounded like any other Gym challenge, but Ethan paused to think. Many Ice-type Pokemon were also Water types. That combination could give several members of his team trouble: Scyther, Crystal Steelix, Dragonair, Charizard, and even his new Larvitar.

Gyarados would have no particular problem, but it was too strong. Ethan did not consider using it. The same went for Gengar, which he kept out of ordinary battles.

At another Gym he would have liked to train Dragonair and Larvitar. Here, Gardevoir and Jolteon looked like his only practical choices.

As Pryce reached for a Poke Ball, Ethan hurried to stop him. He explained that he wanted to face Pryce's best Pokemon.

"Young man, I judged you correctly," Pryce said. "You have confidence enough to ask for my strongest Pokemon outright."

Ethan blinked. Pryce clearly knew something about him. "You know me?"

Pryce nodded, then shook his head. "I've watched a few of your battles on television, nothing more. But Blaine and I have known each other for years. He's spoken of you. From the way he talked, I could tell he thought highly of you."

That explained why Pryce had studied him outside. Still, Ethan found their friendship surprising. Pryce was reserved and used Ice types, while Blaine was warm, excitable, fond of jokes, and favored Fire types. They could hardly have been more different.

Seeing Ethan's understanding, Pryce continued, "You're not like an ordinary challenger. Since you ask, I'll make an exception and battle you with my strongest team."

He put away the Poke Ball he had taken out and chose another from his pouch.

For fairness, Ethan had hoped they might send out their Pokemon at the same time, as he had done in his matches at Violet and Blackthorn Gyms. Before he could suggest it, Pryce threw his ball.

The old man had his pride. Letting a visitor challenge his strongest team already broke his routine; he was not going to change the rest of his rules as well. Ethan accepted it.

He looked at the field. Pryce had sent out a Dewgong.

Dewgong was a fairly common Pokemon, and Ethan had fought them many times. He knew the species well. Still, if Pryce had chosen this one for his strongest team, it was bound to be formidable.

Ethan thought for a moment, then threw his own Poke Ball. Pryce was taking the battle seriously, and Ethan needed to start with a win.

"Go, Jolteon! The first round is yours!"
