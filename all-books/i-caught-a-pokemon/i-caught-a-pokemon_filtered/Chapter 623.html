Chapter 623: Battling Wattson

Tyranitar lumbered toward the Raikou. The imitation's attack came first: a huge stream of electricity burst from its mouth and struck Tyranitar.

The blast looked powerful, but Tyranitar's typing did not leave it weak to Electric-type attacks. It hurt, yet Tyranitar kept advancing, step by heavy step.

Ethan could have ordered a ranged attack such as Hyper Beam. In the narrow corridor, the Raikou could hardly have dodged it; it would have needed a move of similar power to cancel the beam. But Ethan knew this was a Gym test, and he didn't want to damage the building. He chose to fight up close.

Once Tyranitar reached its target, it opened its jaws and used Bite. The false Raikou lacked a living Pokemon's judgment. It stood still and let Tyranitar sink its teeth into it.

Rrrip!

Tyranitar's teeth were small and not especially sharp, but they easily tore a piece from the Raikou's body. Misty saw wires inside and finally realized it was a fake. Perhaps the damaged circuitry failed, because its eyes went dark and it stopped moving.

That's it? Misty put a hand on her hip and shook her head. It made such a dramatic entrance, but it broke after one bite.

It was probably only a small test, Ethan said. If it were as strong as a real Raikou, most Trainers would never get through the door.

As he spoke, a hidden door opened in the wall. Two men came out.

The first was an elderly man in his seventies, short and rather round, with a prominent belly. He had styled what little white hair remained on his head, and a matching white mustache and beard covered much of his lower face. He wore a camouflage vest marked with a lightning-bolt symbol over work overalls. Despite his age, he seemed full of energy and came out laughing.

Behind him walked a younger man with round glasses, apparently his assistant.

The old man had evidently heard them from the other side of the door. He grinned at Ethan. You're right! It's only a little test. That's my Laughing Million-Volt No. 2, a welcome for challengersa little jolt to charge up your lives! Well? Did it scare you?

Hardly, Misty said with a dismissive look. She had been startled, but she wasn't going to admit it. Your imitation is nowhere near as strong as a real Raikou.

Oh? The old man raised his eyebrows. You know what it's called, so you must be from outside Hoenn. And from the sound of it, you've seen a real Raikou before?

Misty realized she had said too much. Her expression changed, and she glanced at Ethan.

He stepped forward with a smile. We have. I fought one while traveling in Johto.

That wasn't a lie, merely half the story. He had fought Raikouand then caught it.

The old man had no reason to doubt him. He stroked his beard. You've battled a Legendary Pokemon? Your travels must have been exciting!

Ethan recalled Tyranitar. To move away from the subject and get to the reason for their visit, he asked, Are you Mr. Wattson?

That's right. The man nodded. I'm Wattson, Leader of the Mauville Gym. This is Watt, my assistant.

Watt gave them a friendly nod. Hello. Welcome to Mauville Gym.

It's a pleasure to meet you. I'm Ethan, a traveling Trainer, and this is Misty, my traveling companion. I'm sure you know why we're here, Mr. Wattson. Would now be a good time for a challenge?

Of course! Follow me.

Wattson and Watt led them out of the corridor and into a spacious room. It was plainly the Gym's battlefield. Unlike a rocky field or a pool, the Electric-type Gym's arena was simply flat ground.

Misty chose a seat in the stands. When she used to travel with Ash, every Gym battle had made her worry. Watching Ethan was different. She rarely had to fear for the result; she could cheer a little and enjoy the match. It was one of the greatest differences between the two Trainers.

Ethan and Wattson took their places in the Trainer areas, and Watt stepped forward to referee.

The Mauville Gym battle is about to begin! he called. Each side may use three Pokemon. The Gym Leader may not switch Pokemon freely during the match. When all three Pokemon on either side are unable to battle, the match is over!

They were standard Gym rules, identical to the ones Roxanne had used at Rustboro. Once Watt had finished, Wattson threw his first Poke Ball.

A Voltorb appeared before Ethan.

This is my Voltorb, Wattson said cheerfully. A warning: it can fight for a long time. If you want to beat it, you'd best finish the battle quickly!

His warning showed Ethan how cheerful the old man was and how much confidence he had in his partner.

Thank you. I'll keep that in mind.

Ethan smiled politely and sent out his own Pokemon without much deliberation. Blue light flashed, revealing Combusken.

Wattson looked surprised. Why didn't you use that Tyranitar? It seemed very strong a moment ago. Oh, I seeyou must be saving it for last!
