Chapter 234: The Thirteenth Pokemon

"Crystal Onix, our turn to fight back! Use Rock Throw!"

Crystal Onix roared. A huge rock formed in midair and hurtled toward Scyther.

At the last moment, Scyther used its speed to dodge. Its blades flashed more sharply than before, and it struck Crystal Onix in the belly again. But just like the first attack, this one failed to break through Crystal Onix's defenses. It did only slight damage.

The watching Scyther stirred. Even the scarred leader had not expected its opponent to be this difficult. Their new leader appeared to be on the attack, but the others could see the problem: if its blades could not get through Crystal Onix's hide, attacking again and again would accomplish nothing.

Scyther had come close once more. Ethan seized the chance.

"Don't let it get away! Use Bind!"

Crystal Onix swept its thick tail toward Scyther, trying to coil around it and hold it by force.

"Scyther!"

Scyther answered at once with Double Team. Four images of it appeared before Crystal Onix. Its tail stopped in midair. It looked from one Scyther to another, unable to tell which was real.

Ethan could not pick out the real one either, but there was a simple way to deal with so few copies.

"Crystal Onix, don't let them confuse you. Hit them all with Rock Tomb!"

Crystal Onix roared again. More than a dozen huge rocks fell into the clearing, smashing the false Scyther and forming a rough ring of stone that blocked every route around it.

Bang!

Scyther had nowhere to dodge. Rock Tomb caught it too and knocked it to the ground. Rock was strong against both of Scyther's types, Bug and Flying. The attack dealt four times the usual damage.

Scyther had landed several hits while Crystal Onix remained in good condition. Crystal Onix had needed only one solid blow to badly hurt Scyther. The type advantage was decisive, and to Ethan it confirmed the value of the battle strategy he had chosen.

Scyther rolled several times, coming to a stop covered in dirt. It looked a mess.

Ethan held off on another attack. He wanted to see what Scyther could still do.

After a moment, it shook its head and struggled upright. Panting, it leaped onto a rock, fixed its eyes on Crystal Onix, and raised its arms in an unusual stance.

Swords Dance. Ethan recognized the move immediately. Scyther knew its attacks were not getting through and meant to raise their power.

Its limbs moved in a solemn dance. A faint golden light gathered around it. With fresh confidence, Scyther charged again.

The rocks scattered across the clearing slowed it down, though. It had to leap from one to another to approach Crystal Onix.

Ethan followed it with his eyes and gave his next command calmly. "Crystal Onix, use Stone Edge!"

Crystal Onix slammed its tail against the ground. After a slight tremor, a line of sharp stone spikes burst upward toward Scyther.

Bang! Bang! Bang!

The pointed stones tore through the earth. Just as they reached it, Scyther surged forward with Quick Attack. It avoided Stone Edge and arrived in front of Crystal Onix.

The two Pokemon crossed paths in an instant. Scyther's blades came down harder than before. Crystal Onix gave a muffled grunt, and its body rocked slightly.

Swords Dance had finally made Scyther's attack count. Even so, it had not done much damage.

Before Scyther could feel any relief, Ethan's steady voice reached it.

"Crystal Onix, bind it!"

Scyther wanted to dodge, but the rocks around it limited its movement. It was running out of strength, and the burst of speed from Quick Attack had left it unable to move as quickly as before. Crystal Onix's tail wound tightly around it.

"Nowhere to run," Ethan said. "Crystal Onix, finish it!"

Scyther struggled as the coils tightened. Crystal Onix was too strong to break free from. Its eyes rolled back, and it slipped into a daze. Crystal Onix lifted it high, then slammed it down against a rock.

Thud!

Scyther lay still for a long while. Ethan thought the battle was over and reached for a Poke Ball. To his surprise, Scyther rose with great difficulty.

He raised an eyebrow. It had lost consciousness, yet its stubborn fighting spirit kept its body facing Crystal Onix, blades held ready. What a remarkable warrior.

Ethan would not stop now. He threw the Poke Ball. Red light drew Scyther inside, and the ball shook violently. After rocking a few times, it released Scyther.

The capture had failed. Scyther stood in the same place, still holding its fighting stance.

"Crystal Onix, one last hit."

Crystal Onix swung its tail in an Iron Tail. The blow sent Scyther falling backward. This time, it did not rise.

Ethan retrieved the Poke Ball he had thrown. He studied the Scyther with the scar across its forehead for a long moment, then gently touched the ball to its head.

Brave warrior, become my partner.

Red light flashed again. This time the ball shook only once before the capture was complete.

The surrounding Scyther erupted in angry cries. Several looked ready to rush forward and rescue their leader.

Crystal Onix sensed their intent and moved in front of Ethan, throwing back its head with a roar. Beside it, Ethan narrowed his eyes and turned toward the group.

Faced with the two of them, the Scyther took a step back together. Shaken, none dared come any closer.

Ethan picked up the Poke Ball, shook his head, and walked back to Sabrina with Crystal Onix.

She was watching him with a gentle expression.

"Thanks to you, I caught a fine Pokemon," Ethan said with a smile. "I really appreciate it. We're done here now. It's time to leave."
