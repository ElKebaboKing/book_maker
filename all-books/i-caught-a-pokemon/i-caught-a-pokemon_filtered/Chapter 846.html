Chapter 846: Brock's Coordinator Debut

After so long apart, they had plenty to catch up on. As usual when they met again, Ethan treated everyone to a generous meal at a good restaurant. Brock's cooking was delicious, but gathering around a table to eat, drink, and talk had a different pleasure of its own.

Ash, Brock, and Max met May's new Eevee there. They played with it and congratulated her. Then they learned she intended to use the recently hatched Pokemon for her appeal in the first round of the Gardenia Contest. The three exchanged uneasy looks.

Isn't that risky? Brock asked after a pause. Eevee only hatched a few days ago. Would entering it in a Contest already be too much?

He had a point. May had only recently taken first place in an appeal round before advancing to the Contest Battle stage. Her run of wins looked promising, but she had achieved it without facing a formidable rival. Against someone like Drew, she would have a much harder fight.

To Ash and the others, this seemed the time for May to consolidate what she had learned rather than introduce an untested Pokemon. Eevee lacked experience and had never taken part in anything like a Contest. That would place even more pressure on May's own ability as a Coordinator.

May had thought about it. It'll be all right. I've shown Eevee lots of recordings during our breaks these past few days, and we've practiced a lot. We work well together. As long as we don't make a major mistake, I think we'll get through the appeal.

They still looked worried. Ethan spoke up. May has made her decision. Let's trust her. Besides, this is a small-town Contest. There won't be as many entrants, and probably no especially troublesome opponents. She has a good chance.

With Ethan supporting her, the other three let the matter rest.

After lunch, they went to inspect the venue. Like the Silver Town Contest that had brought them across the office worker, the Gardenia Contest was modest in scale. Its hall sat amid a stretch of lawn. From a distance it looked like half a Poke Ball set facedown on the grass, right down to the round shape and similar colors. There were not many contestants, and the stands held only a few thousand seats.

They helped May register, then came outside to the lawn. The Contest was tomorrow, so Coordinators were practicing all around them with their Pokemon: Bellsprout, Ninetales, Sentret, Koffing, and many others.

There was plenty of activity, but Ethan saw no familiar face among them. It appeared to be an ordinary small Contest without a particularly dangerous rival. May felt a little more confident.

She was about to practice with Eevee on the grass when she met a girl coming the other way with a Raticate.

The girl was about May's age and height. She wore a sleeveless green dress, and her sea-green hair was tied in a side ponytail. Pretty and rather sweet-looking, she seemed anxious now, glancing about as if she were lost.

Hello, she asked May timidly. Could you tell me where to register for the Contest?

May pointed to a small door beside the hall's main entrance. Over there.

Oh, thank you so much! The girl brightened. She was close in age to Luna, the wealthy child they had met the day before in Commerce City, but her manners could hardly have been more different.

As the girl started away, May called after her. Um...is this your first Pokemon Contest?

Yes! She nodded eagerly. The Gardenia Contest will be my very first!

A new Coordinator. Seeing her brought back May's own early days. Perhaps she had looked just as uncertain then.

I'm May. I'm entering too. If there's anything you don't understand, you can ask me.

That would be wonderful! I'm Mollie. It's nice to meet you.

After another grateful nod, Mollie went briskly to register. May knew time was short and took Eevee onto the grass to practice.

Ethan and the others left her to it and sat on a nearby bench to watch. Ash could not sit still for long. After only a few minutes he jumped up.

Ethan, just waiting here is boring. Since we have the chance, want to battle?

Ethan had nothing else to do and was about to agree when a woman approached them. She walked straight toward Brock, drawing every eye.

She seemed to be in her late twenties. Long, smooth hair fell past an orange T-shirt; she wore a fitted red skirt and black stockings. Her composed appearance concealed a lively warmth.

Oh my...how wonderful! So charming!

She stood before Brock, looking him over with obvious enthusiasm and murmuring praise. Even Brock grew shy under that attention. His face turned crimson, and he trembled with excitement.

Was this finally his chance? She was beautiful, and a little older than him, exactly the kind of woman who caught his eye. Hearts practically appeared in his eyes.

You're too kind! Compared with you, my charm is nothing!

The woman did not seem to hear him. She leaned closer and reached outto lift the Bonsly he had been holding.

This Bonsly is absolutely perfect!

Brock's smile froze. Only then did everyone realize she had been admiring his Pokemon, not him. It was an awkward misunderstanding. Ethan could almost hear Brock's heart breaking.

...

They later gathered beneath a sun umbrella: Ethan, May, Ash, Brock, Max, and the woman, each with a hot drink at the table. She still held Brock's Bonsly.

Ethan had only just met it, but Ash had mentioned it during lunch. Brock had caught Bonsly by chance a few days earlier while traveling past a ninja school. Though it had been with him only a short time, his careful attention had left it in excellent condition. This woman had noticed immediately.

I'm sorry about earlier, she said with a smile. My name is Yuma. I'm a Pokemon Breeder. It's a pleasure to meet you.

The others were surprised to meet another Breeder. Brock seized on the connection. Miss Yuma, I'm a Breeder too! What a coincidence!

Yuma smiled. I could tell as soon as I saw you. She looked down at Bonsly with admiration. Its sheen and its condition make it clear. You care for it differently from the Trainers and Coordinators here. That's something a Breeder can do.

You can tell all that just by looking? Ash asked.

Of course. Breeders concentrate on managing Pokemon's health. Trainers and Coordinators focus more on strengthening their bodies and moves. That affects their diets and even how their Pokeblocks are mixed. I can see the difference at a glance.

Her explanation showed her expertise. Ash, May, and Max were impressed. Ethan had to acknowledge it too. He had talent for battling, but May knew more about appeals, and Yuma clearly knew more about caring for Pokemon. Each field demanded its own skill.

Delighted to meet a fellow Breeder, and a beautiful one at that, Brock brought out his other Pokemon. Marshtomp and Forretress appeared in two flashes of blue light.

They're beautiful! Yuma examined them and praised them freely. Brock showed her the Pokemon food he had prepared as well. After tasting it, she was even more impressed.

Amazing. She looked at him with shining eyes. I didn't expect to meet such an accomplished Breeder here. Would you consider entering the Contest?

Brock? In a Pokemon Contest? The suggestion surprised them all. Why would a Breeder ask another Breeder to compete as a Coordinator?

Me? Brock asked.

Yuma smiled at their puzzled faces. You might not guess it now, but I used to be a Coordinator. I even competed in a Grand Festivaland won.

Winning a Grand Festival was the Coordinator's equivalent of a League championship. It was no small feat. Ash and the others exclaimed again. May was especially startled: the woman who had introduced herself as a Breeder had once been a top Coordinator.

Breeding and Coordinating do share a great deal, Brock said. But why do you want me in this Contest?

My Breeder's skills helped me win that Grand Festival, Yuma explained. I believe it's my job now to pass what I learned on to the next generation of newcomers.

They understood. Yuma had recognized Brock's skill from the way he cared for his Pokemon and wanted him to bring that skill into a Contest, just as she had.

Before Brock could answer, May stood. Miss Yuma, please teach me those techniques!

I'm sorry. Yuma shook her head. I want to train a complete beginner. You're already a capable Coordinator in your own right.

May was disappointed to be turned down, but praise from a Grand Festival winner still pleased her. Yuma looked back to Brock. He had made up his mind.

I understand. His face took on a determined expression. Then he clasped Yuma's hand with a broad smile. If that's what you wish, Miss Yuma, I'll do it!

Before she could add another word, he let go and dashed off toward registration. Ethan laughed to himself at Yuma's astonished face. She could hardly have expected to meet a Breeder with Brock's peculiar personality. Perhaps she was already wondering whether events had escaped her control.

Brock raced back almost immediately and proudly waved his Contest Pass in front of her. He had been fast enough to give her no chance to reconsider.

Ethan could no longer hold in his laughter. He turned to May. Looks like Brock is making his Coordinator debut here. You've gained a troublesome opponent.
