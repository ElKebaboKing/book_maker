Chapter 542: Ho-Oh Temple

After leaving New Bark Town, Ethan and Erika headed north. They traveled all day, leaving the plains behind and reaching the mountains around Mt. Silver by nightfall.

They passed a Pokemon conservation area along the way, but Ethan had no interest in going in. After another half hour of walking, they found a level spot beside a stream and pitched their tent just before dark.

After dinner, Erika grew restless under Ethan's increasingly heated gaze.

She knew what was about to happen.

Last night, he had made his move after drinking, and she had found it hard to refuse him. Tonight it was just the two of them. If she let it happen again, she would be accepting what he wanted.

But given how they felt about each other, she could not bring herself to turn him away.

Besides, she had already given herself to him the night before. There was little point in holding back now.

For Ethan, being alone meant he could finally relax and enjoy himself without worrying about anyone interrupting.

It had been Erika's first time last night, and they had been guests in someone else's home. He had not been able to do all he wanted. Now she was a little more accustomed to him, and no one was around to listen through the walls. He could give himself over to the night.

After they washed up by the stream, he grinned and led Erika into the tent early. As he lowered the flap, he glanced at Scizor and Jolteon, who were keeping watch outside, and motioned for them to move farther away and leave him alone.

The flap fell shut. By the light inside, Gengar, which had been sent out too, could see Ethan's shadow eagerly pulling Erika down. It turned to Scizor and Jolteon and spread its hands.

"See that?" Gengar grumbled. "Young people. It's barely dark and they're already in there having fun, leaving us out here in the cold!"

Gengar had lived for more than two thousand years. It had seen just about everything and was used to this sort of thing between humans.

It was only mating. What was there to be embarrassed about? Did they really have to make Gengar leave? Did they honestly think it wanted to watch?

Gengar poked the burning wood with a stick. Scizor and Jolteon merely yawned, found comfortable spots beside the campfire, and lay down. They did not seem to mind keeping watch at all. Gengar let out a long, helpless sigh.

You two obedient fools deserve to sit out here in the cold.

Ah...

It looked up at the sky and sighed to itself.

My companions aren't too bright. This team is hard to lead...

Inside the tent, Ethan laid out the bedding and gazed at Erika before slowly moving closer.

"Wait..." Erika's face turned bright red. "Turn off the light first..."

Ethan smiled. That was an easy request to grant. He reached over and switched off the camping lamp, plunging the tent into darkness.

Fortunately, moonlight filtered through the fabric, so it was not completely black inside.

He guided Erika down onto the bedding, then reached into his bag and took something out.

It was the pack of condoms he had left over from his time with Sabrina. She had departed so suddenly that they had not used them all. He had not expected to use them with Erika.

Well, at least they would not go to waste.

...

After a night's rest, the two packed up and set out for Silver Town.

Ethan had certainly enjoyed himself the night before.

He was tired, though.

Erika looked more radiant than ever. When she glanced at Ethan, her gaze was so gentle it seemed ready to melt.

Their relationship had advanced quickly. In both body and heart, she now felt she belonged to him, and everything she did showed a tender, caring side of her.

Even the way she spoke to Ethan had grown softer.

Mt. Silver stood in eastern Johto. Strictly speaking, it was part of the same mountain range as the Indigo Plateau, not far from the site of the Indigo League. It belonged to Johto by location, however, placing the two tournament sites in different regions.

Silver Town sat at the foot of Mt. Silver beside a lake. It had Johto's largest Pokemon Center, several competition venues, and a fully equipped village for the competitors. The whole town had been built around the Silver Conference.

Before entering Silver Town, however, Ethan and Erika passed Ho-Oh Temple.

It was said that the ceremonial flame for each year's Silver Conference came from this temple. Some Trainers considered it a sacred place and visited before the tournament to pray for a good result.

Ethan had not come to pray. He was simply curious and wanted to look around.

They walked through the open gate into the temple grounds.

As they had expected, a few other Trainers were there. There would probably be more as the tournament drew near.

On the day before the opening, crowds of Trainers would flock here to watch the flame carried to the stadium.

Erika opened her travel guide again and smiled. "There's a legend about this temple. Long ago, a terrible battle was fought here, and countless Pokemon were killed or wounded. The fighting grew worse until the land was barren. Ho-Oh, furious, burned the humans' weapons, then restored the lake and the earth. Seeing that, the people reflected on what they had done. They called the fire that remained the sacred flame and kept it in the temple, passing it down from generation to generation. Now it serves as the source of the Silver Conference flame. That's how the temple got its name!"

"Another legend about Ho-Oh... Why does it turn up everywhere?"

Ethan pursed his lips at the name. He still held quite a grudge against Ho-Oh. If it had not interfered, he would have caught all three Legendary beasts by now!

Erika chuckled at his expression. After only a few laughs, she saw the suggestive way he was watching her lips. Remembering the night before, she blushed and quickly stopped smiling.

They climbed a long flight of stone steps and finally reached a shrine building.

But just as they stepped onto the hilltop, before they could approach the shrine, two Pokemon suddenly sprang out and blocked Ethan's path.

On closer inspection, they were a Machoke and a Machop.

"Huh?" Ethan stopped and looked at them in puzzlement. "What's this about?"

Machoke and Machop made threatening gestures at him. Then a dark shape flashed before his eyes, and a catlike Pokemon leaped between them!
