Chapter 546: The Competitors Gather

"All right, that's enough talk for now," Ethan said with a smile. "You look worn out from the road. Go register before it closes, then get back to your room and wash up."

Ash remembered that this was the final day to register. He wasted no time and hurried into the Pokemon Center with Pikachu.

Ethan, Erika, Misty, and Brock stayed outside to catch up. They had barely begun talking when Ash came rushing back.

"All done!" He gave them a thumbs-up, grinning broadly.

"Let's go," Ethan said. "I'll show you to your room. Then, as usual, dinner's on me!"

"Yeah!" Ash and Misty cheered together.

They were about to leave when another familiar face came toward them.

"Ash! Ethan! I didn't expect to see you here. It's been a long time!"

They turned toward the voice. It was Gary.

Ethan remembered meeting him shortly after arriving in Johto. Gary had been passing through and had said he intended to enter the Silver Conference. Ethan had heard nothing of him since. Now here he was.

But something had changed.

Whatever sort of journey Gary had been on, he looked steadier than before. His sports car was gone, and so were the cheerleaders who had once followed him everywhere. He stood alone with a small bag on his back.

The sight of his fellow Pallet Town Trainer and rival filled Ash with energy.

"Gary!" Ash pointed at him in delight. "So you're entering the Silver Conference too!"

Gary sighed. "You really don't pay attention, do you? After the Indigo League, we battled at my grandfather's house. I told you that day I was going to enter the Silver Conference."

"Huh?" Ash scratched his cheek awkwardly. "Did you? Sorry. I forgot..."

"Honestly..."

Gary turned away from Ash and fixed his gaze on Ethan.

"This time, I'm going to avenge my last loss. I'll beat you!"

He was still bothered that Ethan had knocked him out of the Indigo League.

Ethan was not intimidated. He smiled lightly. "I'll take you on whenever you're ready. First you'll have to reach me. Don't let someone else eliminate you along the way!"

"Don't worry," Gary said, shaking his head. "I've staked everything on this tournament. I'm going to be the champion."

"No, I'm going to be champion!" Ash cut in, unwilling to back down. "I'll battle both of you and beat you both!"

"Is that so?" Gary smiled. "I could say exactly the same. I'll defeat the two of you and win it all."

The tournament had not even begun, yet Ash and Gary were already competing with each other. They each wanted to beat the other, but both clearly saw Ethan as their strongest opponent.

After their brief exchange, Gary left wearing a confident smile.

Ethan looked around. Silver Town was much busier than it had been when he and Erika arrived.

Trainers entering the tournament had gathered here, along with countless spectators who had come early to watch. Some places were already crowded enough to slow everyone down.

Ethan spotted a few faces he recognized, though he did not know the people. He had glimpsed some of these Trainers at different Pokemon Centers and had seen others compete in the Whirl Cup.

He even saw Jimmy and Marina. They were far away and had not noticed him, so he did not go out of his way to greet them.

The group left the Pokemon Center and headed down the shopping street toward the competitors' village.

The street was bustling too. There were far more vendors and goods for sale than before.

Ash and Misty looked from side to side. Snacks, Pokemon masks, pillows, and all the other little items caught their attention. Their youthful excitement was plain to see; they seemed to wish they had more eyes to take it all in.

Brock was more composed. He narrowed his eyes and looked only at the pretty older girls.

Halfway along the street, they saw a crowd of young children surrounding an elderly man, chattering as they asked him for something.

As they drew closer, they recognized him. It was Charles Goodshow, the Pokemon League president whom they had met before. He held a bunch of balloons and handed them out to the five- and six-year-olds around him. Being short himself, he almost disappeared among the children.

Once the children had gone, Ash approached him first.

"President Goodshow!"

Goodshow's eyes lit up at the sight of Ash and Ethan behind him. "Oh, it's you two!"

Now Ash was surprised. "You remember us?"

"Of course." Goodshow pointed first at Ash, then at Ethan. "I remember every competitor who leaves an impression. Ash, you reached the top sixteen at the Indigo League. You often won by surprising your opponents and battling against the type matchup. And you must be our Indigo League champion!"

Goodshow could not help laughing heartily as he finished.

No wonder he had risen to lead the Pokemon League. Ethan, as champion, was easy to remember. But Goodshow still recalled Ash so clearly after all this time. His memory was impressive.

"President, what are you doing out here?" Ethan asked.

Goodshow smiled. "As you can see, I'm in charge of the tournament, and I'm doing what I can to give our young supporters a little present."

"A present? You mean the balloons?"

"That's right." Goodshow nodded. "They may be inexpensive, but the thought matters. Didn't you see the smiles on all those children's faces when they received one?"

He was right. The children had been excited about the tournament already. Now they chased one another with their balloons, happier still.

"My, the last time I saw you was at the Indigo League." Goodshow stroked his beard. "And here you are entering the Silver Conference. I hope you perform brilliantly in every match ahead and give the crowd something to cheer about!"
