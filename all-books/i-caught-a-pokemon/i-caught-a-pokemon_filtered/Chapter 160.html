Chapter 160: Here to Cheer Him On

Once the two finalists were confirmed, the Pokemon League announced a three-day break. The final would begin at ten o'clock on Wednesday morning.

During the break, League staff took promotional portraits of Ethan and Logan. Posters for their match soon appeared on streets throughout the major cities.

Edited promotional videos ran constantly on television, online, and across the giant screens in shopping districts. The League wanted people all over Kanto to hear about the final as they went about their daily lives.

Excitement grew. Everywhere, people talked about the coming match.

Someone even started an online poll. More than six million people took part, and Ethan received ninety-three percent of the vote, overwhelming Logan.

On television programs, hosts, experts, and even tournament judges predicted an Ethan victory. They did not all expect another six-to-zero sweep, but most thought he had an excellent chance of lifting the Indigo League trophy.

The festive mood kept building. So did the expectations surrounding Ethan.

He only grew more cautious. He refused to relax just because the public had decided he would win.

To prepare, he rented an indoor battle arena in town and used every available moment for training. Misty, Brock, Ash, and their Pokemon all came to help.

Ethan's Pokemon were spread across the arena. Under Misty's guidance, Gyarados and Blastoise practiced against other Water-types.

Misty had grown up at a Water-type Gym, and it showed. She spotted details in their training that Ethan had never noticed and knew exactly how to work on them.

His Charizard and Ash's Charizard stood glaring at each other. Neither looked willing to back down, and both seemed ready to fight at any moment.

The rest of Ethan's Pokemon trained near Brock, each working on something different.

Ethan looked from one group to another. He was satisfied. They had done everything they could; now they only had to wait for the match.

Masha came in from outside.

"Boss, another crowd of reporters is here asking for an interview. What do you want me to do? Shall I chase them off like last time?"

Ethan was about to say yes. Then he reconsidered and shook his head.

"I'll talk to them this time. I've reached the final. If I keep refusing every interview, it'll look ungracious."

Masha went outside to make arrangements. At his order, the bikers cleared a space beside the arena and gathered the reporters together.

When Ethan came out, their eyes lit up. They rushed to surround him. If the bikers had not been standing nearby and watching closely, microphones might have been shoved into his face again.

"Ethan! What do you think of Logan as an opponent?"

"Are you confident you'll win?"

"Is it true you were involved with organized crime before becoming a trainer? Is that cool-looking girl in the biker gang your mistress?"

"Ethan, one more question"

The reporters asked about everything from Logan and Ethan's background to his ambitions, career, and rumored relationships. The questions sounded simple, but many were traps. A careless answer could be distorted and printed everywhere that same evening.

Ethan answered carefully. He did not need to sound brilliant; he only wanted to avoid giving anyone a quote they could use against him.

For the moment, the Indigo League had made him the most talked-about man in Kanto. By the end of the interview, handling the press felt more exhausting than a hard battle.

Time passed quickly.

On the last day before the final, several unexpected visitors arrived at the Indigo Plateau to cheer for him.

The warmest surprise was Professor Cerise and his wife Yoshino.

They had brought Chloe's younger brother, Souta, along with the laboratory assistants Ren and Parker. All five had traveled here together.

Ethan was surprised, and so was Chloe. None of them had breathed a word about the trip. When she opened the door and saw her family and the assistants standing there, she stared and rubbed her eyes as though she might be imagining them.

"Mom? Dad? What are you doing here?"

Professor Cerise glanced at Ethan and laughed.

"How could we miss a moment this important? As your 'family,' we had to make time to come and cheer for you, however busy we were!"

Warmth spread through Ethan's chest. For a moment, he could not think of anything to say in thanks.

Parker clapped him on the shoulder. "I've been following your matches in secret! I never expected you to be this strong. You made it all the way to the final!"

They had barely settled the visitors into the lodge, without even having time for tea, when the doorbell rang again.

Ethan opened the door. A beautiful young woman with long black hair stood outside, her expression as cool as ever.

"Sabrina? You came too?"

She had found the lodge on her own, without telling him she was coming. It was another surprise, and a welcome one.

He hurried to invite her in. "Why didn't you tell me? If I'd known, I would've asked you to come a few days earlier. You could have helped train my Pokemon!"

Sabrina rolled her eyes. "Do you think I have that much free time? People challenge the Saffron Gym every day. I can't just leave whenever I please."

Ethan raised an eyebrow. "Are you going to have to rush back, then?"

"No. My father is acting as Gym Leader for now and taking care of things. I can finally take a little break."

Ethan missed what she was hinting at. By then they had reached the living room, and Sabrina stopped short at the sight of so many people.

He quickly introduced everyone.

Ash, Misty, and Brock had met Sabrina before. They greeted her, but all three were surprised by how familiar she and Ethan seemed with each other. Ash had helped Sabrina come through her troubles, yet their own relationship with her had never been more than cordial.

With so many visitors, the lodge felt crowded. Fortunately, it had two floors and enough rooms.

Professor Cerise shared one bedroom with Souta; Yoshino and Chloe took another. Brock and Ash shared a third, while Ethan, Sabrina, and Misty each had a room of their own. That filled all six bedrooms.

That evening, Ethan asked Brock and Misty to buy fresh meat and vegetables. They held a barbecue outside the lodge. The lively company and Brock's cooking made for a wonderful meal.

Seeing Sabrina laughing and talking with Ethan, Brock could not help feeling envious. After dinner, he took Ethan aside and quietly asked him for advice on getting along with girls.

"What? You want me to teach you how to pick up women?"

"Shh! Keep your voice down!" Brock said, embarrassed. "You even managed to become friends with someone as icy as Sabrina. How did you do it? Teach me a few tricks."

"There aren't any tricks. If you want to be friends with a girl, you just have to be sincere." Ethan waved a hand. But when he saw Brock's disappointed face, he reconsidered. "All right. There is one simple thing. Two words, and you can be as popular with girls as I am."

Brock's eyes widened, shining with sudden hope.

"What two words?"

Ethan gave him a confident smile. "Good looks!"

"Urgh!"

Nearby, Ash had heard enough and pretended to retch.

Why were these people so obsessed with girls? Ash could never understand it. Compared with all that trouble, spending time with Pokemon was much better.
