Chapter 878: Afterward

Two heads appeared at the open doorway. Naoko and Sayo peered into the roomand saw a sight they would remember for the rest of their lives.

They could only stare in shock. They had never imagined this side of their three older sisters. All three of them, at the same time, with one man. Had they truly been so carried away that they had not even closed the door?

The younger sisters stood rooted to the spot, their faces growing redder. The scents from the room only made it harder to think.

Sayo heard heavy breathing beside her and turned. Naoko looked dazed and was beginning to lose awareness of her surroundings. Sayo realized they could not stay there.

She pulled the door shut, putting it between them and the room.

Bang!

The noise did not interrupt anyone inside, but it startled Naoko back to herself. She looked at her own hands and blushed deeply, mortified by how the drug and the scene had affected her.

I... I... She could not finish.

Sayo was in no mood to tease her. She took Naoko's hand and led her back toward their bedroom. The bathroom could wait.

Neither spoke as they hurried along the corridor. Only when they were back in familiar surroundings did they breathe freely again.

May was still fast asleep, unaware of anything happening in the house. She had merely shifted into another untidy sleeping position.

Naoko and Sayo slipped under their now-cool blankets and pulled them over their heads. An uneasy silence filled the room. Sleep was even further away than before.

They tossed and turned until almost dawn, when both finally dozed. What they had witnessed followed them into their dreams. They woke embarrassed and avoided each other's eyes, unaware that the other had slept just as poorly.

Between the three older sisters and these two, Ethan had kept all five Kimono Sisters from a restful night.

Only May slept well. When she woke, the weariness of their journey had vanished. She felt refreshed and full of energy.

Good morning! she said brightly.

She was already dressed and had folded her bedding neatly by the time Naoko and Sayo rose. For a moment, her cheerful manner reminded them of Ethan.

You're up early, Miss May, Naoko said, rubbing her eyes and forcing herself to sound alert.

Early? May glanced at the clock. It was past seven. Then she remembered that stage performers sometimes worked late into the night. Perhaps their schedules were irregular.

She looked more closely at the sisters. Both seemed exhausted, with dark circles beneath their eyes.

Did you sleep badly? I hope I didn't keep you awake.

Naoko and Sayo exchanged a look.

No, it wasn't you, Naoko said quickly. It was just... She struggled to find an explanation.

Sayo supplied one. We had some sake last night, and it made it hard to sleep.

Naoko flushed. That's right.

I see. May accepted it. Even Ethan had seemed drunk last night, so it was easy to believe the sisters might have had trouble sleeping.

That reminded her of him. He ought to be awake by now. Would he look as worn out as these two after his drinking?

When Naoko and Sayo had dressed, the three left the bedroom.

Their room lay deep within the house. Any route to the dining room, washstand, or bathroom took them past Zuki's room. The two sisters walked nervously, afraid of what they might see.

Everything was quiet as they approached. But the door they had closed during the night stood open again.

They looked inside. Ethan was asleep under a blanket on disordered bedding. No one else was there.

Naoko and Sayo were relieved. At least they had not found anyone else.

May, however, was puzzled. Is this the guest room where Ethan stayed? It looks more like a woman's room.

From its furnishings, it clearly was. There was a strange smell toonot exactly unpleasant, but hard to identify.

Naoko and Sayo were once again at a loss for an answer. Fortunately, May assumed the guest rooms might all be decorated this way.

Ethan was still asleep. Thinking he must be suffering from all the sake, she let him rest and went with the sisters to wash up.

Once they were ready, the three entered the dining room. Miki, Kuni, and Zuki were nowhere to be seen. Where had they gone?

...

A little earlier, around the time Naoko and Sayo woke, the three older sisters had been sitting together in Miki's room without a word.

Before them stood a camera. They had just finished watching its recording. Zuki had meant to capture something she could use against Ethan. Instead, it showed all three sisters going to him in turn and what followed.

It was painful to watch and harder still to remember. Silence weighed on the room.

They had changed into fresh clothes, but marks on their necks and their exhausted, strangely radiant faces revealed how they had spent the night.

At length, Miki sighed and looked at her sisters. That was quite a spectacle. Now tell me: what happened?

Her voice was hoarse, whether from the night itself or simple thirst.

None of them had lost their memory. The night returned in fragments, but they remembered being with Ethan until dawn. Only when the feverish feeling finally subsided had they collapsed from exhaustion. They woke almost immediately in shock, confusion giving way to alarm.

All three had fled the room, trembling, weak, and sore. Not even Zuki had dared stay.

Kuni glanced at Zuki. Miki's flat question left no room to avoid an answer.

Zuki told the whole story: how she had mixed the liquid into the sake, how Kuni had tried to stop her, and how she had given Ethan the marked bottle.

That's everything, she said with a sigh. I didn't judge the amount properly. That must be why the scent affected all of us.

Even now, she did not realize the sake the three sisters had drunk had been dosed too. She thought only the fragrance had affected them.

Miki said nothing for a long time. Her silence pained Zuki. This was her fault. She had dragged both older sisters into it.

I'm sorry I involved you.

Miki and Kuni looked at her. Zuki rarely apologized. They were surprised to hear her admit fault, even though what she had done plainly demanded an apology.

And it had done more than merely involve them. Miki bore marks left by both of her sisters as well as by Ethan, and she had left marks on them too.

What happened filled them with embarrassment, anger, regret, and helplessness. Yet neither Miki nor Kuni could bring herself to hate Ethan. Zuki had set it in motion. He, too, had been drugged.

They even found themselves thinking that, if it had to happen, at least it had been with Ethan. Both recognized his good qualities. They would have been distraught if it had been some other man.

Then again, Zuki would never have attempted anything like this with anyone else. Ethan was a victim of her scheme, but he was also the reason she had devised it.

What do we do now? Kuni asked. Her body trembled from sheer exhaustion after the long night. Miki and Zuki felt just as worn out.

Zuki fell silent. Her plan had gone completely out of control, and both her sisters were entangled in it. If only she had been involved, she might have known what to do. Now she had no idea.

The room fell quiet again as all three considered their options.

How much do you think he'll remember? Kuni asked hesitantly.

Probably as much as we do, Miki said after a moment. He drank directly from the marked bottle, but the drug doesn't erase memories. He'll remember.

That was a difficult prospect. They fell silent once more.

Finally Miki made her decision. We should tell him the truth. It's the best choice we have. If we're honest and ask his forgiveness, perhaps we can put this behind us as quickly as possible. That would be best for both sides.

She turned to Zuki. Don't worry. After all three of us have been with him, I hope he'll accept you.

Kuni glanced at Zuki. Seeing her nod, she sighed. I suppose that's all we can do.

...

No one disturbed Ethan, and he slept until late morning.

When he finally sat up, he stared around the unfamiliar room. His head was still foggy. His body ached and felt drained.

The bedding beneath him was damp. Sweat and other fluids marked the tatami nearby, along with a few spots of blood that had not been cleaned away. Torn scraps of clothing lay farther off. He had nothing on, and his skin felt sticky.

Where am I? How did I get here? What happened?

Then fragments of the previous night came back to him.

I...with all three sisters?

As he sat there in disbelief, Gengar passed through the wall and appeared before him.
