Chapter 842: It's Time for Eevee to Evolve

At Ethan's command, Eevee changed course. Instead of swimming for the bank, it headed for a floating log nearby.

Water churned behind it. Eevee did not need to look back to know Seviper was closing in; it could almost feel the venomous snake's cold breath.

Fortunately, the log was close enough. Eevee clambered onto it just before Seviper caught up. Without stopping to shake the water from its fur, it ran along the wood and leaped. Seviper's tail slashed at it as it sailed over the broad stretch of pool, but Eevee landed safely on the far bank.

Lucy silently cursed the missed opportunity. It had escaped after all.

She knew, though, that surviving two close calls was no accident. Eevee's ability to get through Seviper's assault unharmed was real. Its species was physically weaker than Seviper, yet it had managed to fight on even terms. That said plenty about its strength.

She had already been surprised by Ethan many times. He had done it again. If only he had narrow eyes too...

The battle drew her attention back. Now that Eevee had reached land, the situation had reversed. If Seviper came ashore where it was, Eevee would be waiting to harry it. But retreating across the pool or taking the long way around did not suit Lucy's style.

Fortunately, she had another tactic.

At her order, Seviper stayed afloat instead of coming ashore. It opened its mouth, and a torrent of fire roared across the water toward Eevee.

Ethan had not expected Lucy's Seviper to know Flamethrower. Or rather, he had seen it use the move in the anime and forgotten. It hardly mattered. On open ground, with that much distance between them, Eevee could easily dodge.

Just as he was about to give the order, a thought stopped him. Lucy was a Frontier Brain, the Pike Queen. She must know a straightforward Flamethrower could not hit Eevee so easily. Why use it?

He understood almost at once. The flames raced over the pool toward the bank. Lucy did not expect a direct hit, though she would welcome one. The fire was chiefly a screen. Seviper was already swimming toward shore behind it, intending to climb out while Eevee's view was blocked.

But the flames blocked Lucy's view too. She watched Seviper near the bank and raise its head above the edge. Just when she thought it would get ashore, Eevee appeared beside it.

What?

How had Eevee gotten there? Shouldn't Flamethrower have driven it away?

Before Lucy could think it through, Eevee used Take Down and knocked Seviper back into the pool.

He saw through it.

Lucy had treated Ethan as her most difficult opponent, yet she had still underestimated him. Flamethrower could have attacked Eevee and covered Seviper's landing at the same time. Once Ethan recognized the plan and moved Eevee to the bank, the screen worked against Lucy. Seviper had lost sight of Eevee behind its own flames and failed to notice where it had gone.

Splash!

Seviper fell back into the water. Before it could recover, Eevee struck it with Swift, adding another wound.

Lucy could no longer risk approaching Eevee head-on. Her first two attacks had achieved something and made it seem as though she controlled the fight. She had felt the slightest relaxationnot even enough to call carelessnessonly for Ethan to seize back the initiative. Until the battle was truly over, she could not let down her guard.

He had earned his championships. Even a Pokemon that was not one of his main battlers could hold its own against her Seviper. She could not underestimate either of them.

At Lucy's command, Seviper dived, surfaced farther away, and climbed safely onto the bank. Eevee used the pause to shake the remaining water out of its fur. Flamethrower had already evaporated most of it, so the dampness no longer hindered its movement.

They faced each other again. Neither attacked first.

The battle had moved beyond its opening and middle stages. Everyone sensed that the deciding exchange could come at any moment, perhaps with the next move, and watched even more closely.

To Lucy, May, and the women in the stands, either Pokemon still seemed capable of winning. Neither held a clear advantage. Ethan saw it differently. Seviper had shown nearly every tactic it possessed. Unless Lucy had some entirely new move in reserve, what he had already seen no longer posed much of a threat.

It was time to decide the matchon his terms.

At his command, Eevee began its real counterattack. Ethan focused completely. Eevee accelerated and darted in with Quick Attack again and again. Seviper repeated its earlier moves, but they had lost their effect. Ethan read each one, and Eevee's agile footwork carried it out of danger. It found opening after opening and kept landing blows.

The pressure on Lucy rose sharply. Even May and the women watching were startled.

What happened? one of Lucy's supporters asked. It's like we're watching a different person. The boss's Seviper can't fight back at all.

Weren't they trading blows just a moment ago?

May wondered the same thing. She had watched Ethan battle at the Ever Grande Conference and had seen him fight others during their travels. He usually gave her the impression that he won almost effortlessly as a match went on. She had never seen him take on this sharp, relentless style. It was an unfamiliar side of him.

Those were only impressions from the sidelines. Lucy and Seviper felt the change directly.

Lucy could not understand it. She had seemed to have a real chance a moment before. Now Seviper could barely defend itself. Its attacks missed; its movements could not carry it away from Eevee's strikes. Its strength drained rapidly, and it could not organize an effective counterattack. It could only endure.

What had happened? Had she somehow triggered a switch?

She tried several times to change the pattern, but nothing helped. Even when another Quick Attack knocked Seviper down for good, Lucy could scarcely believe it.

We've lost? Weren't we ahead earlier?

The referee announced the result. Only then did Lucy come back to herself and look at Ethan with mixed feelings. So this was what he could really do.

Ethan let out a long breath when he saw he had won. That had not been easy. How long had it been since he had needed to concentrate so hard?

Lucy's Seviper was formidable, but Eevee itself was another reason the match had been so demanding. As Ethan had already noticed, its body was beginning to struggle against opponents this strong. The problem was neither a lack of experience nor poor execution. Its small, adorable form imposed a limit.

Not every Pokemon was Ash's Pikachu, capable of battling Legendary Pokemon one day and losing to a beginner the next. For most Pokemon, their stage of evolution affected both the strength they could bring to a fight and the limit of their potential.

Eevee had done more than enough. But its evolution had become urgent.

Lucy recalled her unconscious Seviper and came over, her expression still complicated. Ethan had seen that look on defeated opponents more times than he could count.

You're incredibly strong, she said. I lost this battle fair and square.

He had heard those words countless times too. Still, praise from the Pike Queen pleased him.

You're giving me too much credit. Your Seviper fought well. It nearly brought down Eevee more than once.

Lucy did not take the polite remark at face value. Nearly winning was still only a hypothetical. Eevee had not fallen. And had she truly had the chance to bring it down?

May and the other women joined them. Lucy's supporters surrounded Ethan and studied him as though he were some remarkable creature. This time his clear victory over their boss had subdued them. They did not dare tease him so freely, though their looks were full of amazement and admiration.

Lucy was not talkative. She had lost, and there was no more to say. She took a medal from her coat and placed it in Ethan's hand.

Please accept my Luck Symbol. It's proof that you defeated me.

Ethan thanked her and carefully put the gleaming gold Symbol into its case.

He had now conquered the Battle Factory, Battle Arena, Battle Dome, Battle Palace, and Battle Pike. Five Frontier Symbols were his. If he defeated the Battle Tower, he would have all six he needed to challenge the last Frontier facility: the Battle Pyramid.

Time had passed quickly. His journey through Kanto's Battle Frontier was nearing its end.

Ethan and May said goodbye and left. By the time they came out of the Battle Pike, evening was close. They returned to Fuchsia City rather than traveling on. After a night at the Pokemon Center and time for their Pokemon to recover, they set off again.

Their next destination was the Battle Tower in southeastern Kanto. There Ethan would meet the Anabel who lived in this world.

Honestly, if the Anabel of the International Police had not warned him in advance, he would have had no idea how to act around the Battle Tower's Anabel.

He would have to pretend he did not know her. That much was certain: he had never met this Anabel. But he knew that face. Treating her like a stranger, keeping any familiarity out of his voice, meeting her eyes without betraying himselfhis acting would soon be put to the test.

...

The Battle Tower was a long way from Fuchsia City. Reaching it meant crossing half of Kanto, a journey that could not be finished in a day. They had three options.

The easiest and quickest was to fly, either on an airplane or on Dragonite. That was also the option Ethan least wanted. The other choices were a ship or the road.

He discussed it with May, and with his suggestion and her agreement, they chose to walk. The distance was considerable, but they would arrive eventually. More important, they would not miss the stories and scenery along the way.

They followed the main road out of Fuchsia City, left the peninsula behind, and walked for most of the day. After lunch, they finally reached the Kanto mainland.

Then the Egg in May's hands began to hatch.

Nurse Joy's estimate had been right. Ethan and May watched expectantly as the shell cracked open and a newborn Eevee came into the world.
