Chapter 294: Togetic

So close? Ethan shook his head at Falkner's remark and said nothing. There were no guarantees about what might have happened.

Staraptor's ability to fly had kept it beyond Jolteon's reach at times. Without that advantage, it might have lost even faster. Jolteon had plenty of Electric-type moves it had never used. Staraptor's Flying type had put it at a disadvantage, but flying had also spared it some of the punishment it might otherwise have taken.

Still, Staraptor had fought like a Gym Leader's strongest Pokemon. Ethan had to acknowledge its strength. Against one of Ethan's second group of partners, it could probably win four battles out of ten. Fortunately, Jolteon had come out ahead this time.

Falkner composed himself and picked up his last Poke Ball. Ethan watched it closely. What would come out? Unlike his easy match with Hoothoot, this demanding battle had made him eager for the next round.

The answer came quickly. Falkner threw the ball high. "Come out, Togetic!"

Blue light flashed, and a white Pokemon marked with red and blue triangles appeared above the battlefield.

Togetic? Misty had a Togepi, Ethan remembered, and Togetic was Togepi's evolved form. It had a longer neck and legs and a pair of wings on its back. Compared with Togepi, still wrapped in its eggshell, Togetic looked almost like a teenager beside a small child.

It was rare, and Togepi needed a close bond with its Trainer to evolve. Ethan had not expected Falkner to have one. Since he had never seen a Togetic in person, he took out his Pokedex and aimed it upward.

"Beep! Togetic, the Happiness Pokemon. A Fairy and Flying type. No wild Togetic has yet been recorded. It brings good luck. When it flies, it can float steadily through the air with scarcely a beat of its wings. It is said to travel the world searching for kindhearted people to share happiness with. When it finds a gentle person or Pokemon with a pure heart, it appears and scatters glittering feathers known as happiness powder, bringing them good fortune."

Once his surprise faded, Ethan thought about which Pokemon to use. Togetic had come out after Staraptor, so it was likely even stronger. This would not be easy.

Of his Pokemon, Charizard and Gengar seemed best placed to exploit its typing. Both were too powerful for the sort of contest he wanted, though. After considering it, Ethan chose Crystal Onix.

He had several reasons. Crystal Onix's Ground-type moves could not hit a Flying Pokemon, but its Rock-type moves would be twice as effective against Togetic, making up for that weakness. He also needed to train his less experienced partners. His strongest Pokemon were pulling farther ahead, and he did not want the gap between them and the rest of his team to grow too wide.

Jolteon had gained experience in its hard fight with Staraptor. Crystal Onix deserved the same chance.

Ethan recalled Jolteon and threw his third Poke Ball. "You're up, Crystal Onix!"

Blue light flashed. A huge Pokemon landed heavily on the court.

Rumble!

Its weight sent a faint tremor through the entire building. Fortunately, Violet Gym's sturdy, unusual design bore the impact easily.

"What kind of Onix is that?" one apprentice whispered.

"Is it a Shiny? But that's not the color of a Shiny Onix!"

The young men behind Falkner could no longer keep quiet. Even with all the Pokemon they had seen, Crystal Onix's beautiful form astonished them.

Falkner studied it for a long moment. "A remarkable Pokemon. It has power and beauty, like a finely carved work of art."

Ethan smiled. "It's a Crystal Onix. I came across it by chance. Without a habitat like the one where it grew up, I doubt there's another like it in the world."

"Crystal Onix. A fitting name."

After a brief moment of admiration, Falkner focused on the match again. The referee gave the signal. Having lost the first two rounds, Falkner attacked first once more.

"Togetic, use Yawn!"

Ethan narrowed his eyes. "Go underground!"

An odd light spread toward Crystal Onix. Just before it reached the great serpent, Onix bored a hole in the court and vanished beneath it with surprising speed.

In his battle against Drake, Ethan had been impressed by how quickly Drake's Onix could dig. He had studied its technique afterward, then spent time training his own Crystal Onix. It was not quite as fast as Drake's, but it had become much quicker than an ordinary Onix using Dig.

Ethan was still nervous about using the move here. They were battling on the top floor. If Crystal Onix dug too deep and broke through the floor, the damage would be disastrous. His Pokemon could fall out of bounds and be eliminated, and Ethan might have to pay for the repairs himself.

Falkner had prepared for battles on this roof, though. The floor was thick enough. Ethan's fear did not come true.

Yawn's light passed over the opening without affecting Crystal Onix. The sight reminded Falkner of a hawk hunting a snake. To catch this one, he would first have to lure it from its hole.

He had Togetic descend toward the ground. As he hoped, Crystal Onix sprang from its tunnel and drove the point of its head toward Togetic.

"Dodge!"

Togetic shifted to the side. The attack missed by a hair.

There! Falkner's eyes lit up. This was the opening he had waited for.

"Togetic, Magical Leaf!"
