Chapter 752: May's Road Through the Grand Festival

Misty was still fuming when she returned to the dorm room she shared with May.

She was not angry with Ethan. She was angry at herself for letting Lisia find an opening while she was away. Misty dropped onto her bed and brooded, leaving May unsure how to comfort her.

Um... May chose her words carefully. Today was an accident. None of us knew Lisia would be here, so you can't blame yourself for letting your guard down. Look on the bright side: she'll leave after the Grand Festival. Then you won't have to see her again.

Misty's feelings for Ethan were no secret. Everyone around them could see it, even bookish Max. But May's words only made her look more dejected.

There are things you don't know, Misty said helplessly. When my journey in Hoenn ends, I have to go back to the Cerulean Gym. Ethan will go on to Sinnoh, and Lisia has performances there too. I'm worried...

She did not finish. May understood: Misty feared Lisia would take Ethan from her. May wanted to reassure her, but what could she say? With Misty far away, neither of them could know what would happen. Comfort offered against the unknown would mean little.

Misty leaned against the wall, looked up at the ceiling, and sighed. If only I could keep traveling with him...

...

Ladies and gentlemen! Here in Slateport City, the event every Coordinator dreams of has finally arrived: the Pokemon Contest Grand Festival!

Vivian stood at the center of the open-air arena, microphone in hand, announcing the start of the annual Festival. She wore a long red gown. Her blond hair, blue eyes, sweet appearance, and stirring voice added their own brightness to the occasion. One of the most popular Contest hosts, she was a familiar presence at ordinary Contests and major celebrations alike.

The arena could hold tens of thousands of spectators. This was the first day of competition. As the booklet had explained, 247 Coordinators would compete in the preliminary round for 64 places in the next stage.

Ethan sat in a front row with Misty, Ash, and the others, holding popcorn and enjoying the show at close range. Because May's turn was later, she sat with them for the moment too.

Watching the entrants gave them a sense of the field's strength. Several faces were familiar. Ethan had seen only one previous Contest and knew few Coordinators, so the first one he recognized was Robert.

Robert had beaten Drew to win the Slateport Contest. He was the second entrant to take the stage today, and he was as elegant as ever. His Milotic gave a splendid appeal and earned 98 points, an exceptionally high score. The audience cheered. May looked uneasy.

He's just as impressive as before, Ethan said. Ninety-eight is almost a perfect score. Unless something unexpected happens, Robert will probably reach the final rounds. He may be your biggest obstacle to winning.

May nodded. Compared with him, she knew she was still inexperienced in every respect. Even so, she believed in herself. She had earned five Ribbons, proof that she could hold her own against anyone here.

Four more Coordinators followed Robert. All performed well. Each had won five Ribbons, after all; none could be dismissed. They might not match Robert, but their appeals were exciting in their own ways.

Then it was Drew's turn. His partner was Roselia again. It showered the stage with petals and shone in the spotlight, winning Drew 95 points. So far, only Robert and Drew had reached scores that high.

May swallowed. The pressure was building.

Don't be nervous, Ethan told her from the next seat. Forget their scores and show everything you can do. If you give it your all, this will help you grow, whatever the final result.

He knew from his prior-life knowledge that May would not win this Grand Festival. He wanted to soften the blow in case her hopes rose too high. His words helped her settle a little.

May looked at the man who had won tournaments in two regions. Were you nervous the first time you entered a Pokemon League tournament?

You've picked the wrong person to compare yourself with, Ash said, laughing before Ethan could answer. I've never seen Ethan look nervous!

Ethan rubbed his nose. Ash was right that May should not use him as her standard. He had felt nervous in tournaments, but never as visibly as she did. It was not the opponents or even reaching the final that unsettled him. It was the prospect of a championship within his graspand the joy of a victory that close.

The others chuckled. May realized she had asked the wrong person, smiled ruefully, and turned back to the stage.

More than a dozen entrants later, the peculiar man Ethan had met yesterday stepped up. Harley's Pokemon was Cacturne, a green, humanoid cactus with dark green protrusions that made it look somewhat like a scarecrow. Harley's own outfit suddenly made sense: he and his Pokemon had dressed to match.

Cacturne performed a swift appeal with Bullet Seed. Harley received 84 points. That fell short of Robert's and Drew's scores, but it was respectable and seemed enough to advance.

At that point the staff called May backstage to prepare. She took a deep breath and stood.

Go, sis! Max called.

Show them your very best, May!

Ethan and the others offered their encouragement. May gave them a small smile and headed down the competitors' passage.

Misty watched her go with a worried frown. Why do her steps look so heavy?

She'll be fine, Ash said. May's really good. Trust her.

Ash and his friends traveled with May and had seen far more of her performances than Ethan or Misty had. If they were confident, the others could afford to be too.

Several more entrants performed before May's turn finally came. She wore her usual outfit, but a faint smile rested on her face as she walked calmly to the center of the stage. She had come a long way since her first Contest.

That was why Ethan believed so strongly in traveling widely. Seeing more of the world and experiencing new things helped a person grow. Keeping someone confined to a comfortable, familiar place when they were old enough to travel would only hold them back.

Next, please enjoy an appeal by Coordinator May! Vivian announced.

May spun gracefully and released Beautifly. The Pokemon beat its wings, scattering powder across the stage. In the sunlight it glittered like golden rain. The entrance alone drew approving applause. It was not the loudest response of the day, but the audience appreciated what it saw.

In a competition this important, every point mattered. Coordinators trusted their strongest partners rather than risk an unfamiliar Pokemon and a mistake that could eliminate them. It was much like a League tournament in that respect: different in nature, but demanding the same care.

May and Beautifly continued. Beautifly danced through the air, casting wave after wave of powder until the stage seemed caught in an endless golden shower. Then it used Silver Wind, bringing the appeal to its peak. At the finish, it landed on May's arm, and the two struck a pose together.

Applause rose again. Ethan and his companions clapped while turning their eyes to the large screen for the score.

The judges at this Grand Festival were different from the usual panel of Nurse Joy, Mr. Sukizo, and Mr. Contesta. Two women and a man with greater expertise in this kind of assessment had taken their places. They held less prominent positions, but had sharper eyes and a more neutral perspective. The audience trusted their scoring.

After conferring, the three awarded May a total of 88 points.

Nearly ninety! The score showed the judges' approval and put May ahead of many competitors, including Harley. Based on the performances so far, a place in the next round seemed secure.

Ethan and the others relaxed. May's mother, Caroline, let out an especially long breath. She had been more nervous than anyone and wanted her daughter to shine on this stage.

As May left the arena, the group rose to meet her backstage. Each entrant had only a minute or two for an appeal, but with entrances and exits, all 247 performances would take close to nine hours. It was a test of endurance for the judges, and spectators came and went throughout the day. Most people chose to watch the entrants who interested them. With May finished, Ethan's group had no reason to stay until the end; they could check the list of qualifiers after the preliminaries concluded.

They met May coming toward them on a path outside the arena. Caroline, Misty, Ash, and the others praised her until she grew embarrassed.

Just then Harley appeared from nowhere and came up behind May, smiling broadly.

That was wonderful! he said with an exaggerated flourish. His tone was flattering, yet there was something hard to place beneath it. It looks like you'll have no trouble reaching the main competition!

May jumped. Since he had come to congratulate her, she answered politely. You did well too, Harley. Your score was high. I'm sure you'll make it through.

Oh, no. He waved both hands, still smiling. Your Silver Wind was the truly impressive part! I hope you'll keep using it in the matches ahead!

At the mention of Silver Wind, May thanked him earnestly. I really do owe you for suggesting it. I'll keep using it!

It's a promise, then. See you!

Harley hurried away again. May and the others were about to move on without giving the exchange another thought when Ethan spoke.

May, keep your distance from that man.

She blinked. Huh? Why?

He seems sly. That advice sounds helpful, but I suspect he's plotting something. He kept bringing up Silver Wind. There's more to it than he lets on.

Exactly! Ethan sees it too! Max nodded vigorously. I told my sister Harley was bad news and we shouldn't trust him. Now that Ethan's saying it, I know I'm right!

May might dismiss advice from Max, but she could not ignore Ethan's warning.

I understand. She nodded seriously. Don't worry. I'll be careful around him.
