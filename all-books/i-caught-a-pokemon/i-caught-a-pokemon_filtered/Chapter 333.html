Chapter 333: Battle in the White Mist

Abomasnow lived deep in snowy mountains. Although its habitats were spread across many places, the species was almost never seen in Johto and was extremely rare here. Pryce must have traveled through other regions to train when he was young.

"Raar!"

The moment Abomasnow landed on the field, it pounded its chest with both hands and roared at Ethan.

Its name and build alone made its ferocious nature easy to see. Its deep voice carried so powerfully that even from a distance, Ethan could almost feel the breath of a blizzard wash over him.

Oh?

You dare challenge me?

As expected of Pryce's final ace, its sheer presence set it far apart from Dewgong and Lapras.

But no matter how fierce Abomasnow was, could it possibly be more frightening than Ethan's Gyarados?

Ethan had spent so much time beside Gyarados, constantly exposed to its oppressive presence, that Abomasnow could not intimidate him. Even when it glared at him with reddened eyes, his expression did not change. If anything, he found it a little... simple and adorable.

He smiled and shook his head, then returned his attention to the battle.

Which Pokemon should he use against it?

After considering the members of his team, Ethan ultimately chose Gardevoir.

He could have sent out Charizard. Fire held a fourfold advantage, and Charizard could have roasted Abomasnow and won with ease. But as he had just decided, Ethan wanted to spare Pryce some dignity. Instead, he chose Gardevoir, whose type held no special advantage over Abomasnow.

Once both Pokemon were ready, the battle officially began.

Abomasnow opened with Powder Snow, blowing it toward Gardevoir. While Gardevoir evaded, Abomasnow began running after it. Once it was close enough, it leaped and landed directly in front of Gardevoir.

"Use Teleport!"

Ethan remained calm. Gardevoir vanished and reappeared elsewhere, but the moment Abomasnow saw its opponent in the distance, it roared and charged again.

Despite its huge and clumsy appearance, Abomasnow was remarkably agile. On the ice, it could even glide forward on its feet as though skating.

That gave it tremendous speed across the frozen field.

Gardevoir extended both hands and tried to stop it with Confusion. Before the move could activate, however, Abomasnow produced a snowball from somewhere and hurled it at her.

Whoosh!

Gardevoir tilted her head and avoided the enormous snowball, saving herself from a direct hit to the face. But the attack interrupted her move. When she looked toward Abomasnow again, it was already in front of her.

So fast! And what excellent reactions!

Ethan glanced at Pryce in the distance.

Abomasnow's ability to adapt showed how well Pryce had trained it.

But if Gardevoir could not shake it, she would have to meet it head-on.

Visible cold began rolling from Abomasnow's enormous fists. A moment later, it swung at Gardevoir. Every punch carried tremendous force and looked powerful enough to crush whatever it struck.

Ice Punch!

Gardevoir hurriedly leaned aside, stepped back, then twisted away again. Her movements looked graceful, but each punch missed her by the narrowest margin.

"Use Double Team!"

At Ethan's command, Gardevoir created four copies. Five Gardevoir now stood before Abomasnow, including the real one.

Abomasnow was not a particularly intelligent Pokemon. Faced with so many Gardevoir, it stopped in confusion and struggled to decide which one to attack.

Pryce folded his arms and suddenly called in a deep voice, "Attack the one on the far left!"

There was no substitute for experience. Through careful observation, Pryce had already distinguished the real Gardevoir from her copies.

With Pryce's guidance, Abomasnow no longer hesitated and lunged straight at her.

Her position exposed, Gardevoir used Teleport again and opened the distance. Fortunately, Ethan had only meant for Double Team to confuse Abomasnow and buy her a little time, so being discovered changed nothing.

"Use Psybeam!"

This time Gardevoir attacked before Abomasnow could close in. Psybeam struck it squarely.

The move disoriented Abomasnow. Its eyes reddened further as confusion took hold, and it flew into a frenzy, smashing everything around it as though rage had overwhelmed its mind.

"Good," Ethan said calmly. "Follow with Psychic!"

Gardevoir gathered her psychic power and struck Abomasnow. The tremendous force made its huge body sway before it crashed to the ground.

Despite the hit, Abomasnow's hide was thick and tough. It did not seem seriously hurt. Once its confusion cleared, it climbed back to its feet.

It can certainly take a beating!

Perhaps Pryce feared another Psybeam. "Use Mist!" he shouted.

Abomasnow roared, and a vast cloud of white mist formed in an instant. It spread rapidly across more than half the field.

Everything turned white, severely limiting Gardevoir's vision. Abomasnow, however, seemed unaffected and charged toward her.

Thud, thud, thud!

Its footsteps drew steadily closer.

Gardevoir looked from side to side. She knew Abomasnow was approaching but could not see its position, forcing her to judge its direction by sound.

Visibility was extremely poor, but Ethan remained outside the mist in the Trainer's area and could still serve as her eyes.

From beyond the battlefield, his wider view let him make out a huge white-and-green shadow rampaging through the mist. Through their telepathic connection, Gardevoir could sense where the danger was coming from even though her eyes could see nothing.

Thud, thud, thud!

Abomasnow reached Gardevoir and hurled its enormous body at her. To its surprise, although she should have been blind inside the mist, she vanished with Teleport once again.

Abomasnow sniffed the air, then whirled around. Gardevoir stood behind it with both hands extended, firing a blast of energy directly at it.

"Awooo!"

On the other side, Pryce could also see Abomasnow's vague silhouette. Unlike Ethan, however, he could not grasp the exact situation inside the white mist.

Explosions sounded through the fog, followed by one muffled cry after another. Almost every cry belonged to Abomasnow.

Pryce knew things were going badly.

He had intended for Abomasnow to use its natural familiarity with fog and snow to defeat Gardevoir. Now it seemed he had only hurt himself with his own strategy.

He urgently wanted to see what was happening and issue new commands, but the mist was at its thickest and would not disperse anytime soon. No matter how anxious he became, there was nothing he could do.
