Chapter 459: Maya Is Abducted

As the Team Rocket trio gradually disappeared into the distance, a wicked grin spread across Gengar's face in the shadows.

Both the eerie draft and the terrifying laughter had naturally come from Gengar. It had created them solely to frighten the trio.

Gengar simply had not expected them to be so cowardly. It had barely done anything, yet one small scare had already sent them fleeing in panic!

It spread its hands and shook its head at Ethan. "Come on! They're supposed to be members of Team Rocket. How can they be this timid? They can't withstand the slightest scare. What a disgrace to Team Rocket!"

"All right, enough playing around." Ethan rolled his eyes helplessly. "Let's hurry and drive them out before they cause unnecessary damage."

Leaving the shadows, Ethan and Gengar quickly pursued the trio.

Perhaps the three had been too badly frightened. After Gengar's scare, they fled blindly without caring where they went. By pure accident, they actually found their way to Maya's door!

The moment they saw the magnificent doors, delight filled their faces, and they rushed straight inside.

Just as Ethan arrived outside, a woman's scream came from within!

It's Maya!

Ethan did not hesitate. He kicked the door open and charged inside. By the bright moonlight, he saw Jessie and Maya struggling over the golden scepter!

Both women gripped the staff tightly and pulled in opposite directions, while James and Meowth stood nearby without appearing willing to intervene.

When Ethan entered, the two sides reacted very differently.

"This is bad! It's Ethan!"

James's expression changed dramatically. The tremor in his voice made his terror unmistakable!

He could never have imagined that Ethan would suddenly appear the moment they found their target, before they even managed to seize the scepter!

By contrast, delight flashed across Maya's face. She shouted, "Mr. Ethan Vale, these people are trying to steal the Sea Spirit! Hurry and stop them!"

Perhaps Ethan's sudden arrival gave Maya additional strength. She pulled the scepter closer to herself.

Jessie clearly panicked after seeing him. Combined with Maya's sudden effort, this left her unable to wrench the scepter from the priestess's grasp!

"Darn it!" Jessie cursed under her breath. "Where did this woman suddenly find so much strength!?"

Ethan approached one step at a time. Unable to seize the scepter quickly, Jessie shouted at James and Meowth, "Hey! Stop standing there like idiots! Hurry and stop him!"

"Huh?" James and Meowth stared at her in disbelief. "Us? Stop him? Are you joking? That's Ethan!"

"Enough nonsense! I am taking this scepter!" Veins bulged across Jessie's forehead. When they continued dawdling, she gritted her teeth and shouted, "Hurry! I can't hold on much longer!"

Their partner had given an absolute command. Despite trembling in fear at the thought of facing Ethan, James and Meowth finally placed themselves in front of Jessie.

James threw a Poke Ball and released Weezing. Meowth crossed both arms, and a dangerous cold light glittered along the tips of its claws.

Yet faced with the imposing Gengar, both Weezing and Meowth retreated a step despite themselves!

"Darn it!"

Realizing that its fear had shown, Meowth roared to build its courage, then leaped forward and pounced directly at Gengar.

Gengar merely tilted its body and avoided the attack with ease. Before Meowth could turn around, Gengar extended its enormous tongue and struck with Lick. A deep chill instantly spread across Meowth's entire body, leaving it paralyzed!

A short distance away, James felt a headache coming on. Gengar had merely extended its tongue, and Meowth had already lost the ability to battle.

Even so, he could only steel himself and command Weezing to attack.

"Weezing, use Sludge Bomb!"

Sludge Bomb was a Poison-type attack. Against the Poison- and Ghost-type Gengar, it could inflict almost no meaningful damage.

James had no better alternative. Weezing's typing placed it at an inherent disadvantage against both of Gengar's types!

It could still use certain Normal-type moves, but closing the distance would probably result in it being defeated in one strike just like Meowth.

As Sludge Bomb drew closer, Gengar cackled and gathered a Shadow Ball in its right hand. It threw the attack, which scattered Sludge Bomb instantly and continued forward. With a bang, Shadow Ball struck Weezing and sent it crashing into the wall!

Both Pokemon had been defeated in succession. James lost all will to resist and darted directly behind Jessie.

"You two idiots!"

Jessie was furious at their uselessness, but she managed only one curse before Gengar began approaching her!

She started in alarm. With no time to think and no attention left for stealing the scepter, she released it, reached toward her waist, and pulled out two round objects.

From a distance, Ethan immediately saw that they were not Poke Balls. They were smoke bombs!

Clatter...

The smoke bombs rolled across the floor. An enormous cloud erupted from them and filled the entire room within seconds.

Ethan covered his nose and retreated several steps.

At first, he assumed the trio intended to use the smoke to escape. Then he suddenly heard Maya cry out, followed by a window shattering and glass raining across the floor!

Everything happened in the blink of an eye.

When the smoke thinned enough for Ethan to make out the room, Team Rocket was nowhere to be seen!

Jessie, James, Meowth, and Weezing had all vanished. Even Maya and the golden scepter had disappeared with them!

Ethan hurried through the shattered floor-to-ceiling window and onto the balcony. A crude hot-air balloon was already rising slowly into the sky, and Jessie's triumphant laughter drifted faintly toward him!

Ethan smiled bitterly and shook his head, but he was not particularly angry at the outcome.

No matter what they did, the trio was destined to fail.

He simply had never expected Jessie to be so audacious. Stealing the scepter was apparently not enough. She had abducted Maya as well!

I truly have no idea what goes through that woman's mind...

Ethan sighed and took out a Poke Ball. He threw it into the air, and after blue light flashed, Charizard appeared on the balcony!

Ethan vaulted onto Charizard's back. The Pokemon spread its wings, and its orange body streaked through the sky at tremendous speed in pursuit of the balloon...
