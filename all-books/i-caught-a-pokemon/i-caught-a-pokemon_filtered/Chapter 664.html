Chapter 664: A Four-Sided Battle

Boom! Boom! Boom! Boom!

The four energy balls exploded one after another, and the battlefield fell into chaos.

The ball aimed at the three Legendary birds came from too far away to catch them. They dodged it easily. Gengar met the one aimed at Ethan and Misty with a Shadow Ball, canceling it out. Gardevoir raised a barrier with her psychic power to stop the remaining shock wave before it reached the two Trainers.

The other two explosions, aimed at Team Aqua and Team Magma, caused much more trouble. Their members stood close together, and none had expected the Pokemon they had surrounded to strike first. They tried to scatter when they saw the energy balls, but some were inevitably caught in the blasts.

That was the signal for the battle to begin in earnest.

Courtney and Shelly had both escaped the attacks. Each looked back to assess her team's losses. The damage was still manageable, but they could no longer afford to wait. The two commanders issued orders to attack almost at the same moment.

Remarkably, both teams set aside their hostility toward each other. Neither attacked its longtime rival. They focused instead on Ethan and Deoxys.

Ethan had foiled their plans repeatedly. As long as he remained free to interfere, neither organization could rest easy. Deoxys, meanwhile, was a Pokemon of extraordinary power. Capturing it would give either team a stronger position when the time came to face Groudon or Kyogre.

Water from the lake surged through the breach into the enormous crater left by Tri-Bird Assault. Some flowed through collapsed ground toward other parts of the battlefield. In a short time the lake level dropped noticeably, and much of the ground became muddy. Soon the waterlogged areas would join into one. No one paid attention; Courtney and Shelly had ordered all their people forward.

Hundreds of flashes of blue light appeared. Almost every member of both teams released two or three Pokemon at once. The ridges on either side filled with Water-type and Fire-type Pokemon. There was no reserve. The teams meant to overwhelm their opponents with numbers and Pokemon that were, individually, capable enough.

It was a simple, old-fashioned plan. Sometimes that was all it took to catch an enemy off guard.

Seeing roughly half the people on each ridge rush toward him, Ethan threw the two Great Balls in his hands. Raikou and Suicune appeared, one on either side.

Their types were exactly what he needed. At his signal, Raikou charged toward Team Aqua while Suicune moved to stop Team Magma. Electric and Water attacks gave them an advantage against the two organizations' Pokemon.

Even so, two Legendary Pokemon could not face such numbers alone. Concentrated attacks would eventually drive them back, type advantages or not. Fortunately, Ethan had brought more of his old mainstays.

He drew four Poke Balls from his pouch and threw them. Charizard, Venusaur, Blastoise, and Jolteon appeared before him. All four had traveled through Kanto with Ethan. Every one of them was strong.

Chloe had sent Blastoise, Venusaur, and Jolteon from Cerise Park along with the red Gyarados. Ethan had also contacted Liza, who sent his Charizard from the Charicific Valley. With so many longtime partners at his side, Ethan's team was as strong as it had ever been. Even one of the Elite Four would have to take it seriously.

Through Gardevoir's telepathy, Ethan told the three Legendary birds to draw back a little from Deoxys. They could keep it occupied, but he wanted them out of the two teams' attacks as much as possible and conserving their remaining strength.

He kept Gardevoir and Gengar close to protect himself and Misty. Then he sent out the four newly released Pokemon to support Raikou and Suicune. Charizard and Blastoise headed for Team Magma; Jolteon and Venusaur raced toward Team Aqua.

The four-sided battle was underway.

Most of Team Magma's Pokemon were Houndoom, Mightyena, and Golbat, with some Magcargo and Camerupt among them. Except for the flying Golbat, they moved slowly across the spreading lake water. They had to pick their way from one dry patch to another to keep their bodies out of it.

Team Aqua advanced faster. Its force consisted mostly of Crawdaunt and Walrein, with a smaller number of Pelipper. The flowing water helped them close the distance. They were the first to reach both Ethan's Pokemon and Deoxys, and the first to engage them.

More than a dozen Walrein fired Water Guns at Raikou together. Raikou leaped aside and gathered electricity, ready to send a shock back through the streams. Before it could do so, several Crawdaunt charged in with their huge claws raised.

Raikou frowned and sprang away again. The moment it landed, more Water Guns came at it. The attacks were relentless and irritating.

Then two Vine Whips lashed in from the side and broke the jets into sprays of droplets. Raikou glanced back to find Venusaur and Jolteon arriving. It nodded to them and turned its full attention to the fight.

With partners beside it, the pressure eased. Raikou no longer had to guard every direction at once. Powerful electricity flashed across the field each time it attacked. None of Team Aqua's Water-type Pokemon could take a direct shock from the Legendary Pokemon without being badly hurt.

Jolteon fought differently. Smaller and faster than Raikou, it darted back and forth through the crowd. No Water-type could keep up, and it struck Pokemon with electric attacks as it passed.

Venusaur lacked their speed and was too large to avoid becoming a target. It held its ground beside Raikou instead, each covering the other's back as they dealt with opponents one at a time.

In any small encounter, the three had the advantage. Team Aqua, however, had brought far too many Pokemon. Even with half its force attacking Deoxys and another portion occupied by Raikou's group, plenty of them used the spreading water to circle around toward Ethan and Misty.

When more than a dozen Team Aqua Pokemon bore down on them, Gengar and Gardevoir stepped in front of Ethan. They were his most reliable guards. Even a Legendary Pokemon would struggle to hurt their Trainer while they stood beside him.

Before they could act, Misty raised her hand and threw her own Poke Balls. Starmie, Politoed, Corsola, and Psyduck appeared.

Ahem... Misty promptly recalled Psyduck, which had let itself out again without permission. She turned to Ethan as if nothing had happened. Don't forget me. Let me help too!

Ethan smiled. How could I forget you? I was just about to ask.

At Misty's direction, her other three Pokemon joined Gengar and Gardevoir and met the approaching Team Aqua force.

Across the battlefield, Team Magma's Pokemon had engaged Suicune, Charizard, and Blastoise. Suicune was more measured than the fierce Raikou, but its attacks were no less powerful.

The first opponents they met were Golbat flying overhead. The nimble bats kept their distance like a swarm of flies, harassing Suicune with Air Slash and Supersonic. It worked. Suicune grew frustrated, while its Water Guns struggled to hit such agile targets.

Charizard soon dived through them like a dragon, scattering the Golbat. During its time away from Ethan at the Charicific Valley, it had grown considerably. Ethan did not yet know exactly how strong it had become, but it was visibly larger and more muscular.

Liza had told him that his Charizard and Ash's were now challenging the strongest Charizard in the valley. They had yet to succeed, but both were steadily improving.

Charizard breathed fire and drove the Golbat back. A few stubborn ones tried to attack it. Charizard wheeled in the air, caught one with a claw, and squeezed until the Golbat went limp. Then it tossed it to the ground.

Below, Blastoise stood beside Suicune and filled the defensive role Venusaur played on the other side of the battlefield. Its thick hide and shell let it take punishment. The two heavy cannons on its back poured out Water Guns at any of Team Magma's Fire-type Pokemon that dared come close.

With the Golbat no longer harassing it, Suicune could use its power freely. Where Blastoise fired from its cannons, Suicune needed only to guide its energy with an elegant motion to send great waves of water surging over Team Magma's Pokemon.

That side of the battle also seemed to be turning in Ethan's favor. Yet, as with Team Aqua, Team Magma had so many Pokemon that some could always slip around the defenders and attack Ethan. Each one added pressure to the last line protecting him and Misty.

For the moment, Ethan was safe. He looked past the fighting around him toward Deoxys.

It faced nearly half of Team Magma and Team Aqua on its own, while still having to guard against attacks from the three Legendary birds. Deoxys showed no fear. In Attack Forme, it was devastating. Whenever one of its strikes landed, an opposing Pokemon fell.

The sight worried Shelly and Courtney, but tempted them too. Every injured team member or defeated Pokemon was a loss to their organizations' fighting strength. Yet Deoxys's terrible power could help either woman fulfill her team's ambitions if she captured it.

They needed to work out how to catch Deoxys and defeat Ethan. Their greed was already costing them. Had either team committed most of its strength against one opponent and left only enough Pokemon to keep the other from escaping, it might have beaten them in turn. Instead both teams wanted Ethan and Deoxys at once. Neither dared leave an opening for its rival. Their forces were stretched thin, the battle was stalling, and they were slowly losing ground.

It was not the result either commander had expected. After a moment's hesitation, both women led their elite squads into the fight.

Shelly took Team Aqua's best members straight toward Deoxys. Courtney led Team Magma's best toward Ethan.

They noticed their different choices at the same time. Each glanced back across the field, surprised and baffled by the other's decision.

To Shelly, the obvious move was to catch Deoxys first, then deal with Ethan. He had five Legendary Pokemon in his possession. Despite her confident talk before the battle, she thought securing Deoxys was the safer choice. If Ethan escaped, Team Aqua would at least have gained something.

Courtney saw it differently. Deoxys was valuable, but it was not essential. Her main target was the infuriating man who had ruined their operations again and again. This was a rare chance to capture him. Then he would be at her mercy, and his Pokemon would belong to Team Magma. With so many Legendary Pokemon under their control, catching Deoxys afterward would be easy.

Neither plan was plainly right or wrong. But once each woman saw the other pursuing a different target, both were satisfied. They could concentrate on their own objectives without worrying quite as much about a knife in the back.

They led their squads forward. Each had six or seven elite members beside her, people better trained than the ordinary grunts and equipped with stronger Pokemon.

As soon as Courtney joined the assault, Ethan felt the pressure rise sharply. Why did she hate him so much? She was ignoring Team Aqua and Deoxys to come after him. Weren't the two organizations supposed to be bitter enemies?

On the Team Magma side, the new arrivals tipped the battle against him. Strong as his Pokemon were, Suicune, Charizard, and Blastoise could not face so many attackers at once.

Ethan frowned and told them through Gardevoir's telepathy to fight as they withdrew toward him. Then he surveyed the rest of the battlefield.

Team Magma had pulled some of its people away from Deoxys to reinforce Courtney. Only a smaller group remained there, mostly to watch Team Aqua press its attack. Courtney truly meant to defeat Ethan in one push.

Team Aqua, meanwhile, was drawing members away from its fight with Ethan to help Shelly surround Deoxys. The few who stayed behind fought cautiously to keep Ethan occupied. That shift had at least eased the pressure on his other flank.

Ethan used Gardevoir's telepathy to send new orders across the field. Raikou and Venusaur would stay and attack the Team Aqua Pokemon that remained. Jolteon would return and join the fight against Team Magma.

He also told the three Legendary birds to stop attacking Deoxys and target only the Team Aqua force surrounding it.

They really have underestimated me. Team Aqua wanted Deoxys? He was not going to let them take it.

Ethan turned back to his own position. Suicune, Charizard, and Blastoise were still fighting as they retreated. They were now only a few dozen meters away. At his signal, Gengar and Misty's Pokemon moved up to support them, joining the front line. Only Gardevoir remained beside Ethan and Misty for protection.

Misty hesitated. The fight no longer looked as easy as it had before; their side seemed to be at a disadvantage. She took out Psyduck's Poke Ball and released it again.

Oh, fine. You may be an idiot, but surely you can help protect Ethan and me...
