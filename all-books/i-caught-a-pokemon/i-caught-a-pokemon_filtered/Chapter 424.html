Chapter 424: A Shift in Their Relationship and Metagross

Ethan was not especially surprised that Jasmine recognized him.

Although he had competed in another region's tournament, Kanto and Johto occupied the same landmass and shared a border. Apart from Gym Leaders like Morty of Ecruteak City, who only followed competitions within his own region, most Gym Leaders kept a keen eye on major tournaments.

They often watched matches to broaden their own perspectives. They could not possibly view every battle, but they generally would not miss the most important contests, such as the semifinals and final.

Still...

No matter how Ethan looked at her, Jasmine seemed like a delicate young woman better suited to quiet pastimes. It was difficult to imagine her sitting in front of a television, emotions rising and falling with every turn in the match!

Then again, that was only to be expected from a woman who liked steel poles... No, Steel-type Pokemon. Beneath that fragile exterior, she clearly possessed a passionate heart!

Those thoughts flashed through Ethan's mind.

After their handshake ended, Jasmine turned toward Erika.

She studied Erika for a moment, looking thoughtful, before sudden recognition crossed her face. "You're Miss Erika of the Celadon Gym, aren't you?"

"Huh?" This time, it was Erika's turn to be surprised. "You know me?"

Jasmine nodded. "When I was little, my parents took me to Kanto, and we attended one of your family's tea gatherings. I spoke with you then. I still remember tripping and falling, and you personally tended my injury. Of course, it was a long time ago, so you may have forgotten."

Erika truly had no memory of it. Embarrassed, she said, "I'm sorry. I don't remember."

"It doesn't matter." Jasmine waved it off without concern. "We were both very young then, so forgetting is perfectly normal. Besides, haven't we just met again now?"

In his mind, Ethan gave Jasmine a thumbs-up.

What an artful response!

Meeting someone again after forgetting them could have been deeply awkward. Yet with only a few words, Jasmine had both spared Erika embarrassment and strengthened the connection between them. Impressive!

Jasmine's gaze moved between Ethan and Erika. Covering her mouth, she asked softly, "Why are the two of you traveling together? Could you be a couple?"

There it was. Another person had misunderstood their relationship.

Ethan had lost count of how many people had made the same mistake!

He sighed helplessly. This was an unavoidable problem whenever an unattached man and woman traveled together.

Erika, however, had far more to think about after hearing the question.

In the past, she had always responded calmly to such remarks. Perhaps too many people had said the same thing along the way, however, because a strange feeling had begun to stir inside her. The many small moments she had shared with Ethan were hardly dramatic, yet they had quietly and imperceptibly changed her state of mind.

Erika stole a glance at Ethan. For some reason, the tips of her ears turned faintly red, and a trace of panic fluttered in her heart.

Fortunately, she quickly regained her composure and gently shook her head at Jasmine. "You've misunderstood. We're merely traveling companions."

"I see."

Jasmine gave her a peculiar smile. Mature beyond her years, how could she fail to notice Erika's embarrassment? She was certain that even if the pair were not a couple, there was at least some ambiguity between them!

Erika could not tell from Jasmine's mysterious expression whether she believed the explanation. Fortunately, Jasmine did not pursue the subject, allowing Erika's tense heart to relax.

The three chatted a little longer. Once the time seemed right, they finally turned to the matter at hand.

Jasmine walked slowly to the command area on the opposite side of the field and faced Ethan from afar. Erika took a seat in the spectator stands.

The staff member who had escorted Ethan inside assumed the role of referee.

Once Ethan was ready, the referee announced the rules according to custom.

"The match between Jasmine on the red side and challenger Ethan on the green side is about to begin! This will be an elimination match in which both Trainers may use two Pokemon. The battle will end when either Trainer has no Pokemon able to continue!"

As the referee finished, Jasmine reached for a Poke Ball, but Ethan suddenly stopped her.

"Miss Jasmine, if possible, could you use your strongest Pokemon?"

Jasmine looked startled for only a second before understanding his intention. She smiled and nodded.

"I see. Weaker Pokemon can no longer offer you meaningful training, can they? Very well. I'll send out my most powerful Pokemon!"

She turned and approached a platform at the side of the arena. After placing the Poke Balls she had brought onto it, she selected two different ones.

By the time she returned to her command area, Ethan had also chosen his first Pokemon.

At the referee's signal, both Trainers threw a Poke Ball. Two flashes of blue light filled the arena, and a pair of Pokemon appeared on the field.

Jasmine's Pokemon drew attention first. Its body was blue-green, with four powerful limbs. A silver metallic cross formed an X across its face. Its entire body resembled an enormous iron disk and gleamed with a metallic luster!

Ethan recognized it instantly.

Metagross...

This Pokemon was powerfulextraordinarily so.

Under the games' classification, it belonged to the same pseudo-legendary tier as Dragonite from the first generation. It could not be underestimated.

Since this was Ethan's first time encountering one, he took out his Pokedex and aimed it at Metagross.

The Pokedex beeped. "Metagross, the Iron Leg Pokemon. A Steel and Psychic type. Metagross is formed from the combination of two Metang. It possesses tremendous psychic power and also prides itself on extraordinary physical strength. Its four brains are said to connect into a complex neural network, making it more intelligent than a supercomputer. It can perform calculations to analyze and defeat its opponent, calmly driving the target into a corner. Metagross can also retract its four legs to hover in the air and fly with its limbs folded."

Ethan put away his Pokedex and studied Metagross once more.

In terms of type matchups, most types could gain no advantage against Steel, apart from a select few.

Of the eighteen known types, Steel took only half damage from ten and was entirely immune to Poison-type moves. Among the seven remaining types, only Fighting-, Ground-, and Fire-type moves inflicted double damage.

That alone was terrifying!

No type other than Steel could claim anything comparable!
