Chapter 351: Traveling with Erika

"A target?" Now that Ethan had raised the subject, Erika answered seriously. "Not really. My decision to visit Johto was rather sudden, so I haven't formed any specific plans."

"I see..." Ethan rubbed his chin. "All right. I'll start by explaining where I encountered different Pokemon along my route. I haven't spent very long traveling through Johto either, and I certainly haven't explored the entire region, but I can at least give you a general reference."

He took out his Pokedex and opened the map on his phone. Using both devices together, he carefully explained the Grass-type Pokemon he had recorded and the places he had visited.

He spoke for five or six minutes. Only when his throat began to feel dry did he pause, raise his cup, and take a sip of tea.

During the break, Erika brought out a plate of confections and refilled his cup.

Erika came from a distinguished family and had been raised as a traditional lady. She was educated, courteous, deft with her hands, and skilled in cooking and flower arranging. She attended to every detail with remarkable care.

When someone spent time with her, they never needed to ask for anything or worry about what came next. Erika always anticipated every need.

She was beautiful and possessed considerable strength too. She was practically the perfect woman.

Ethan thanked her and ate one of the confections.

It had been shaped with exquisite care and was soft enough to melt in his mouth. It looked beautiful and tasted just as good.

The flavor reminded him of the time he and Chloe encountered Erika in the wilderness while traveling to the Indigo League.

Erika had brought tea confections she made herself on that occasion as well, leaving a deep impression on Ethan.

Tasting the same flavor again, he found it just as unforgettable.

After eating several pieces in a row, Ethan resumed his explanation. Erika listened closely, adding an occasional comment. With his guidance, she soon gained a general understanding of Johto.

At last, Ethan put away his Pokedex and phone. Erika sighed softly.

"From the sound of it, I'll have to visit quite a few places if I want to collect several Grass-type Pokemon."

"It isn't as complicated as you think." Ethan smiled. "Transportation is very convenient now. A flight will take you to Johto quickly, and the cities are close together. You usually won't need to camp in the wilderness. The trip can be as relaxed as a hiking excursion. By the way, when do you plan to leave?"

Erika considered the question. "Probably within the next couple of days. Before I depart, I still need to pack and arrange a few matters."

Ethan nodded in understanding.

Unlike him, she was not free from worldly obligations and able to embark on a journey whenever the impulse struck.

In addition to serving as a Gym Leader, Erika owned a perfume shop and worked as a flower arranger. She had a large household and extensive business interests, with many affairs requiring her personal attention each day.

"Your life is clearly filled with responsibilities. That means you have even more reason to rest and relax." Ethan offered his advice. "This is a rare opportunity, and the Gym is under construction anyway. You should stay away for a few extra days. While catching Pokemon, you can visit some of Johto's famous sights and treat the journey like a long vacation."

Then, merely out of politeness, he casually added, "I'm returning to Johto tomorrow. Be sure to contact me once you arrive. Or you could simply come with me tomorrow!"

"That..."

Erika hesitated.

Go to Johto with him?

Having a traveling companion would naturally be ideal, but...

She could not help feeling uncertain.

When Sabrina had been present, Erika would not have thought much of joining them. She and Sabrina were merely acquaintances rather than close friends, but at least both were women. Now that Sabrina had left, would a man and woman traveling alone together invite misunderstandings?

Erika looked at Ethan and saw only sincerity in his eyes. Remembering the many times he had helped her, her heart softened. She decided she should not judge him through such vulgar suspicions or compare him with ordinary men.

He already had a girlfriend. Besides, they would only be traveling companions. What did it matter? She must not overthink things.

With that, Erika made her decision.

Meanwhile, Ethan regretted the invitation almost as soon as he voiced it. At most, he and Erika were ordinary friends. Asking her to travel with him had been too forward and might easily make her think he had ulterior motives.

But the words had slipped out instinctively. It was already too late to take them back.

To prevent Erika from misunderstanding, he could only explain.

"Well... You're a woman traveling alone, so you might encounter problems that are inconvenient to handle. With two people, we'd be able to look after each other. Of course, if you don't want to, you can leave on your own. Just pretend I didn't say anything."

The explanation sounded feeble even to him. Yet just when Ethan expected Erika to refuse, she nodded and accepted.

"All right. I'll prepare tonight, and we can leave together tomorrow!"

Huh?

Now it was Ethan's turn to stare in astonishment.

She actually agreed?

He could swear to the heavens that the invitation had only slipped out. He had possessed no improper thoughts whatsoever.

Forcing himself to remain composed, he said, "Uh... Sure... Of course that's fine. We'll leave together tomorrow..."

Erika did not notice the strain beneath Ethan's calm. She rose and nodded to him.

"Good. I'll make the arrangements now. By the way, will you stay here tonight? We have several spare guest rooms. After breakfast tomorrow, we could depart directly."

"No, thank you." Ethan hurriedly shook his head. "I'll spend the night at the Pokemon Center. Let's meet outside the Gym tomorrow morning."

After leaving the Celadon Gym, Ethan went to the Celadon City Pokemon Center and checked in for the night.

Lying in bed that evening, he could not shake the feeling that something was wrong.

He tossed and turned, thinking he should warn Sabrina in advance to prevent any unnecessary misunderstanding. But after considering it from every angle, he still did not dare tell her.

Knowing Sabrina's personality, if the news displeased her, Ethan would be in serious trouble.

She might suddenly appear before him and turn him into a doll.

Keeping it secret for now seemed wiser.

Fortunately, Erika would only accompany him for part of the journey. Once she finished catching Pokemon, she would naturally return to Kanto. As long as Ethan never mentioned it himself, Sabrina would never know.
