Chapter 599: The World's Strongest Pokemon

Ethan and Misty left Littleroot Town and headed toward Petalburg City.

He had traveled this road back and forth several times in only a few days. By now he knew the area better than many Trainers native to Hoenn.

They crossed the open countryside. Like Ethan when he first arrived, Misty looked around at everything on this new continent with eager curiosity.

After a while, though, she realized that apart from a few different Pokemon, Hoenn looked much like Johto and Kanto.

The mountains were mountains, and the trees were trees.

As her initial excitement faded, she turned her attention to Ethan and talked with him while they walked.

When he asked what Ash was doing, Misty thought for a moment. I heard he was getting ready to leave too. He'll probably reach Hoenn tomorrow or the day after. But he left all his Pokemon at Professor Oak's lab. It sounds like he's traveling with only Pikachu.

That did not surprise Ethan. Ash would follow the same pattern on later journeys to Sinnoh, Kalos, and elsewhere, setting out with Pikachu and little else.

Ethan himself had given Celebi to Erika when she returned to Kanto, asking her to care for it at the Celadon Gym for now.

Like leaving his three legendary birds with Sabrina, it gave him and Erika a bond across the distance. It also gave Celebi a more comfortable place to stay.

Erika would care for it, feed it plenty of delicious things, and let it enjoy the familiar natural setting of the Gym's greenhouse. Celebi would be more relaxed and happier there.

If Ethan needed it, he could have Celebi transferred back at any time. Showing it more of the world would have to wait for another opportunity.

After they passed Oldale Town, Ethan guessed Norman might not yet have returned to the Petalburg Gym. This time he chose a different road toward Rustboro City.

It was longer and more remote than their earlier route, but it gave him new sights to enjoy instead of making him retrace every familiar step.

Beyond a small, unnamed village, they came to a Y-shaped crossroads with a huge signboard.

It stood conspicuously in the grass. Anyone passing by would be sure to notice it.

They stopped, and Ethan read the words aloud: The Gym with the world's strongest Pokemon lies ahead!

The world's strongest Pokemon? Misty's eyebrows rose in anger. Her family ran a Gym too, and she could not stand such a boast about another Gym and its Pokemon. That Gym Leader has some nerve. I want to see what Pokemon he dares call the strongest!

Ethan had won two conferences and had the three legendary birds and Suicune, yet he never called any of his Pokemon the strongest. What gave the owner of a little Gym near a remote village such confidence?

Ethan found her indignant look funny.

He was curious too. He wanted to see which Pokemon could supposedly live up to that claim.

With both in agreement, they left the main road and followed the side path toward the far end of the village.

They reached the wall of a large enclosure. Before they made it to the gate, a boy of about ten came out crying, clutching his Pokemon.

Ethan and Misty exchanged startled looks.

What had happened in there? Had some extraordinarily strong Pokemon beaten the boy so badly he cried?

They entered the yard. A modest building stood ahead, with an outdoor battlefield in front of it.

On one side of the field, a Pelipper and a man of about thirty-five or thirty-six were strolling back toward the building. The man had a crew cut and wore a gold belt.

He heard Ethan and Misty's footsteps and turned to look them over.

Oh? Two more challengers? he said casually. It's a busy day.

With her hands on her hips, Misty called, Is this the Gym that claims to have the world's strongest Pokemon?

That's right. The man nodded. I'm Anthony, the Leader here. And this Pelipper beside me is, without question, the strongest Pokemon in the world!

Ethan and Misty looked at Pelipper.

Ethan had battled Amber's Pelipper at Devon Corporation not long ago. He knew a little about the Water- and Flying-type species, but he could see nothing different about this one.

Misty eyed it skeptically too. She had seen many Legendary Pokemon, and she traveled with a two-time conference Champion. An ordinary-looking Pelipper hardly seemed worthy of the title strongest.

Besides, this place looked more like a private Gym than an official one.

She had plenty of questions, but she asked, Good. You're who we came to find. Will you accept a challenge?

With Misty at his side, Ethan's travels had taken on a different rhythm. She called herself the world's most beautiful girl and met every situation with energy and a stubborn streak. Apart from Bug-type Pokemon, almost nothing intimidated her.

Of course! Anthony laughed. We've just had a match, but Pelipper barely exerted itself. It has plenty of energy left. Which of you wants to go first?

He had just made a challenger cry and still sounded completely confident.

Misty exchanged a glance with Ethan and stepped forward.

Ethan doesn't need to handle this. I'll take you on first!

Seeing that his opponent was only a girl in her early teens, Anthony shrugged and led Pelipper back to the Trainer's position.

Your choice, he said with a smile. If Pelipper makes you cry, don't blame me.
