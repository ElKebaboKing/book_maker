Chapter 668: Capturing Deoxys!

Gyarados, use Twister!

Ethan sent Mega Gyarados its first direct command through Gardevoir's telepathy. Gyarados plunged into the lake and rolled its enormous body through the water. Its power drew the air into a huge tornado at the center of the battlefield.

Wind dragged the dark clouds along with it. Compared with a Twister made by another Pokemon, this one was broader, thicker, and far more destructivejust like Mega Gyarados itself.

The wind howled. The tornado swept unpredictably through the valley, tearing up trees and hurling broken rocks in every direction. Many Pokemon could not resist it. Some were lifted into the air; others disappeared beneath the flying debris.

Misty stared at the devastation. Only after a long moment did she think to aim her upgraded Pokedex at Mega Gyarados. She had updated it on arriving in Hoenn and had not expected it to contain information on this form.

Mega Gyarados, the Atrocious Pokemon. Water and Dark type. Mega Evolution places a heavy strain on its body, driving it to rampage even more violently. It sprays water from vents on its sides to skim across the surface at speeds faster than sound. Anything caught in its path, even a large ship, may be broken in two. Instinct drives it to destroy everything, but it will obey a Trainer it truly trusts.

The entry helped Misty understand what she was seeing. She also noticed a bright stone hanging beneath the red Gyarados's neck. That had to be its Gyaradosite. When had Ethan put it there?

Back on the battlefield, lake water rose into the Twister. It became a waterspout wandering erratically through the valley. No one could continue fighting under those conditions.

Team Aqua and Team Magma, with so many people gathered together, took the worst of it. They fled as sheets of water fell from above, soaking their clothes and blinding them. A misstep could send someone into the waterspout.

Their formations collapsed. With water and sky seemingly joined around them, they could hardly tell which way to run, let alone find their teammates or Pokemon. The two teams were in disarray.

The waterspout separated Rayquaza and Deoxys too. Both still wanted to fight until one of them won, but neither could get close. They were strong enough to force their way through the storm, yet neither wanted to do that.

Rayquaza tried to charge at Deoxys. Before it reached its opponent, a Mightyena came hurtling out of the waterspout with a terrified howl and struck Rayquaza's head.

Rayquaza shook the Pokemon off and pressed on. Half a tree trunk spun toward it next. It angrily knocked the wood aside with a claw, only to find the wandering waterspout directly in its path again.

The interruptions kept coming. At last Rayquaza lost patience. It circled the sky furiously, roared, and abandoned Deoxys. Then it flew upward and away.

No!

Behind a large rock, Zinnia was sheltering from the storm. She reached toward Rayquaza as it departed, hoping to call it back. The wind scattered her cry before it could reach anyone. She could barely hear herself.

On the distant peak, Ethan watched Rayquaza leave without a change in expression. Its arrival had been unexpected. He had never planned to capture two top-tier Legendary Pokemon at once, and he believed he would have another chance to encounter it. Letting it go did not trouble him.

His objective had always been Deoxys. In fact, driving Rayquaza away was part of the decision he had just made. He felt a little sorry for Zinnia, but he could not risk his chance to capture Deoxys safely for her sake. She had lacked the strength to hold Rayquaza's attention and had missed her brief opportunity.

The confusion had reached its height. Ethan judged the time right and sent another series of telepathic orders.

First, the three Legendary birds were to occupy Deoxys again so it could not escape as Rayquaza had. The rest of Ethan's Pokemon would strike Team Aqua and Team Magma while they were struggling and drive them off the battlefield.

The plan worked. Neither organization could withstand the renewed assault. They retreated step by step until Ethan's Pokemon forced them to the edge of the valley.

When Shelly and Courtney counted their people, each found only a dozen or so nearby, with just twenty or thirty Pokemon at hand. Some members had been scattered and could not be found. Others were trapped among the ruins and rock piles still covering the battlefield.

Even the people who remained with the commanders were wounded, and their Pokemon were in miserable condition.

How had this happened? Though they stood in different places, Shelly and Courtney looked up at the enormous Pokemon still rampaging through the valley almost at the same time.

Neither had seen its form before, but they could tell it had something to do with Gyarados. Both had investigated Ethan's team. With a moment to think, they realized this monster must be his. How it had changed hardly mattered now. Neither team had the strength to continue.

They had not lost every person or Pokemon, but they had come close. Their only thought was to leave as quickly as they could. The two forces departed as bedraggled as they had arrived confident. In only a few minutes, the battle had turned completely.

Ethan paid them little attention. With Team Magma and Team Aqua gone, the contest had returned to where it began: Ethan against Deoxys.

There was Zinnia too, though she no longer seemed part of the fight. Once Rayquaza left, she walked silently toward the edge of the valley without even saying goodbye. She was not blaming Mega Gyarados for driving it away. She felt lost and needed somewhere quiet to collect her thoughts.

With the other teams out of the way, Ethan knew the capture battle was truly nearing its end.

The earlier destruction had spread lake water over much of the valley. Mega Gyarados moved freely through the shallow flood, leaping out at intervals to attack Deoxys.

Stay here, Ethan told Misty.

He called Charizard through Gardevoir's telepathy. As it swept past, Ethan jumped onto its back. Before Misty could answer, Charizard carried him toward the center of the battlefield.

After raging for so long, the vast waterspout finally dissipated. Its pull had cleared the remaining dark clouds. The wind died, the rain stopped, and blue sky returned.

Ethan's other Pokemon gathered nearby. They did not attack. They stood guard and watched Mega Gyarados face Deoxys. Ethan circled high on Charizard, far enough away to avoid being caught between them. He would leave the rest of this fight to Gyarados.

He was determined to capture Deoxys. If he succeeded, it would become the strongest Pokemon on his team. Gengar, Raikou, Suicune, Zapdos, Moltres, Articunonone could beat it alone. Only Mega Gyarados could contend with it for a short time.

Mega Evolution had changed Gyarados into a Water and Dark-type. Deoxys's Psychic-type moves no longer threatened it. Deoxys could still use Normal, Dark, and Ghost-type moves, though those were less formidable than its Psychic attacks. It was not helpless.

Mega Gyarados burst from the water, its jaws open wide, and lunged at Deoxys. Deoxys tried to dodge, but all its earlier fighting had left it slow. For once, it did not move in time.

It barely managed to raise an energy barrier before Gyarados's huge jaws closed around it.

Splash! Both Pokemon crashed into the lake and vanished beneath the surface.

Ethan leaned forward, trying to see what was happening below. From his position, he could make out only Gyarados's massive dorsal fin slicing wildly across the water and great streams of bubbles rising around it. The unseen struggle had him as unsettled as the churning lake.

Then the surface exploded. Deoxys flew out, half an arm missing, and stopped in the air. It hovered there panting, watching the water beneath it.

It was in terrible shape. Its rapid regeneration, which had saved it again and again, did not restore the severed arm. No new tissue appeared at all. Deoxys had finally spent so much energy that it could not even heal itself at once.

The odds were turning Ethan's way, but he needed one more decisive blow.

Gyarados! Don't let it rest. Finish it with Hyper Beam!

Mega Gyarados gathered power. When it had drawn in enough, it opened its jaws and fired a thick, blazing column of energy straight at Deoxys.

Deoxys did not dodge. Everyone could see that it lacked the strength. Instead it gathered what power remained for one last clash. Its two arms came together before its chest, and it fired a blazing beam of its own.

Deoxys had used Hyper Beam too.

The two beams met in midair, one rising from the lake, the other descending from above. The collision exploded in a dazzling flash. Ethan's eyes were overwhelmed. Then a shock wave struck him and Charizard, sending them tumbling through several turns in the air.

Charizard beat its wings and caught the current before they could fall into the water. As soon as the blast passed, Ethan looked toward the center of the battlefield.

Both Pokemon had been thrown backward. Mega Gyarados fell into the lake and resurfaced. Deoxys tumbled away like a kite with its string cut, smashed through several rocks, and landed on its back in the valley.

Ethan's eyes lit up. The chance he had been waiting for had arrived.

He urged Charizard forward. It flew straight toward Deoxys. Ethan took out the Ultra Ball he had prepared and hurled it with all his strength.

The ball traced an arc and struck the fallen Deoxys. Red light enveloped it and drew it inside. The Ultra Ball shook violently under Ethan's anxious gaze. Its movements gradually weakened.

At last, with a soft click, it fell still.

Joy flooded Ethan. He had spent so long on this, fielded almost all his strongest Pokemon, driven off Team Magma and Team Aqua, forced Rayquaza away, and even used Mega Evolution.

At last, he had captured Deoxys!
