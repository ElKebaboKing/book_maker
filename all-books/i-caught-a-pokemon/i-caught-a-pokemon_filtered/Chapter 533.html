Chapter 533: Breaking into the Cathedral

Dragonite and Latias carried them through the night sky. At their speed, they crossed most of the city in a flash.

Within minutes they reached the cathedral and landed in the square before it. Ethan had barely begun to take in his surroundings when several Pokemon lunged out of the darkness.

Looking closer, he recognized the Aerodactyl and Kabutops they had seen revived in the dream projection. It was too dark to count them. Shadows darted all around the square, and pairs of pale eyes watched them coldly. The Soul Dew's power appeared to have revived every fossil.

Several Kabutops charged at them, brandishing their sharp scythes. Before they could strike, Gengar hurled one Shadow Ball after another and drove them back for the moment.

Ethan, Erika, and Ash seized the chance to jump down from Dragonite and Latias.

Erika surveyed the situation and volunteered to cover them. "Leave this to me. Hurry inside and save Lorenzo and Bianca!"

Ash hesitated. Erika was a Gym Leader, but there were so many hostile Pokemon that leaving her here alone still seemed dangerous.

Ethan trusted her. He did not try to talk her out of it. Instead, he took several Poke Balls from his bag and placed them in her hands.

"Let these guys help too."

However urgent the rescue was, he would not leave Erika to face that danger by herself.

She accepted the Poke Balls without ceremony, brought out her own, and released both their teams.

Blue light flashed. Erika's Pokemon were Leafeon, Vileplume, and Bellossom. She had caught several others since coming to Johto, but they had little training and experience. This was no time to send them into such a fight.

The Pokemon Ethan had lent her were Crystal Steelix, Tyranitar, and Scizor.

They were tough enough to hold the front line. Together with Erika's Grass-type Pokemon, they could handle the attackers here.

More importantly, Ethan's Pokemon knew there was something romantic between their Trainer and Erika. None of them objected to taking her orders.

Ethan and Dragonite were already running toward the cathedral doors alongside Ash and Latias.

An Aerodactyl swooped at them, but Dragonite slammed it aside before it could reach them.

There were too many revived Pokemon. No sooner had that one been repelled than two or three Kabutops leaped into their path.

Pikachu fired an Electric attack at once. Kabutops was part Water-type, so the attack should have been super effective. Under the Soul Dew's influence, however, it did far less than expected.

Ash stared. Pikachu, too, seemed stunned for a moment.

The three Kabutops showed brief signs of pain but did not stop. They came down with their scythes ready to strike. Something flashed across Ash's vision, and all three were knocked away by a tremendous force.

He turned and saw Ethan's Crystal Steelix drawing back its tail. It had saved him.

Even while directing the fight farther away, Erika had kept an eye on Ethan and Ash.

"Stay alert," Ethan called as he pressed toward the doors. "The jewel's power has changed them somehow. Bring out the rest of your Pokemon!"

Ash nodded and quickly released Totodile, Bayleef, Cyndaquil, and his Shiny Noctowl. Protected by their Pokemon, he and Ethan fought off more attacks and finally reached the cathedral entrance.

Sharp iron hooks guarded these doors too. At a signal from Ethan, Gengar formed a Shadow Ball and smashed through them by force.

Bang!

Iron fragments flew backward. The Shadow Ball kept going and destroyed the lock on the cathedral doors as well.

Ethan kicked the doors open. There was no time to worry about damage to the building. He charged inside first, heading straight for the room with the machine.

As he had seen in the projection, Lorenzo and Bianca were bound together in spider silk. Hope lit their eyes when Ethan's group burst in, but silk covered their mouths. They could only struggle, as though trying to tell the rescuers something.

Before the machine, Latios was trapped in a frame of more than a dozen iron rings. The device was steadily draining power from his body.

"You pests again!" Annie, the blonde thief, stepped into Ethan's path. "This time you can't stop us!"

At her words, more than a dozen revived fossil Pokemon rushed in. Ethan could only conclude that the two women had gathered up fossils from all over Altomare; otherwise there could not be so many attackers.

Those fossils were valuable relics, important for research and display. That made Ethan and the others reluctant to attack them without restraint.

But rescuing the captives and Latios mattered most.

Ethan took out two Poke Balls and released Gardevoir and Jolteon against the advancing fossil Pokemon.

His and Ash's Pokemon met them in battle. Latias used the opening to fly to Latios and repeatedly ram the device holding him.

"It's no use." Annie glanced at her. "You can't overcome the Soul Dew's power. You were lucky to escape us twice before. I never thought you'd come straight to us this time!"

At her gesture, Espeon and Ariados charged at Latias together. While everyone's attention turned to them, Ethan's Gardevoir used Teleport and appeared beside Lorenzo and Bianca.

Ethan had never forgotten that rescuing the hostages was his most important aim.
