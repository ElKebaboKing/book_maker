Chapter 856: The Aurora Incident

Only two things remained for Ethan and May to do in Kanto: Ethan's final Battle Frontier challenge and May's eagerly awaited Grand Festival.

Neither would be easy. Brandon, known as the Pyramid King, was regarded as the strongest Frontier Brain. His powerful Pokemon had defeated countless confident challengers. The Grand Festival would be full of strong Coordinators too. Every one of them had earned five Ribbons and would be a difficult opponent.

Ethan had so many powerful Pokemon that losing seemed impossible. May's challenge was less certain. If she could break through to even the semifinals, it would be a huge step in her growth.

It was late, so they spent the night in Mulberry City. The number of visiting Trainers meant they could not get a room at the Pokemon Center. With no other choice, Ethan endured the front desk worker's scrutinizing look and took May to a hotel next door, where they booked one room.

May was a little shy about it, but she made no objection. She slept soundly in the soft, comfortable bed and woke at dawn feeling as if all the fatigue of the past few days had vanished.

They set out from Mulberry and headed west again. Kanto's Grand Festival would be held on the Indigo Plateau, rather than in one of the region's large cities. That changed the order of their plans.

Ethan had intended to accompany May to the Grand Festival before challenging the Battle Pyramid. Once he learned where the Festival would take place, he decided to find the Pyramid first instead.

He opened the live map Scott had sent him. As expected, the Battle Pyramid had moved again. It had left Viridian City and was now near Pewter City.

Pewter is Brock's hometown, isn't it? May asked.

That's right. He used to lead the Gym there. His father is filling in as Gym Leader now.

Walking beside Ethan, May hopped over a small puddle without turning her head. Do you think Brock will ever go back to the Gym? He's a Pokemon Breeder now. That isn't really the same as being a Trainer, is it?

Ethan thought of the animated story's later Sun and Moon era, when Brock was still a Gym Leader and had become much stronger. He shook his head.

I'm sure he'll go back. Being a Breeder doesn't stop him from training Pokemon. A Gym Leader has status, a salary, and allowances, too. Why would he give that up easily?

They had walked well beyond Mulberry by then and reached a plain. After crossing it and rounding a low hill, a wide landscape opened before them. Snow-covered mountains stretched across the distance. Below them lay a vast forest and a blue lake reflecting the clear light. The view was beautiful enough to make them stop and breathe it in.

What an amazing view! May said.

They stayed to admire it for a long time, and both took out their phones to keep a picture of the scene. More than a dozen Altaria happened to fly overhead, adding to its beauty.

Then two bands of aurora suddenly appeared in the sky, one cyan and one red. They were lovely, but their arrival was jarring.

Huh? An aurora? May stared. Something about it was familiar. Why would there be an aurora here? And why did it appear so suddenly?

As soon as she spoke, she remembered where she had seen something like it. It was just like... She turned quickly to Ethan, who nodded.

Yes. It's an aurora from Deoxys.

Deoxys? There's one here too?

It isn't so strange. Including mine, I've met three Deoxys, and that was all in Hoenn. They don't stay within regional borders. They fly wherever they want, so finding one in Kanto isn't much of a surprise. Since we've seen it, let's go and find out what's happening.

May had no objection. Wherever Ethan decided to go, she was happy to follow. They set off down the slope toward the forested hills beneath the aurora.

The Altaria that had just flown overhead seemed to lose their bearings in its light. They had been moving steadily in one direction. Whether the aurora frightened them or a magnetic disturbance confused them, they began flying erratically and soon scattered.

There was no time to worry about them. Ethan and May made their way briskly down the slope and into the forest.

May took out a compass to check their direction. Its needle spun wildly. Alarmed, she looked at her phone and found that it had lost its signal as well.

The magnetic disturbance here is serious, Ethan said with a frown. He took a Poke Ball from the pouch at his waist, only to find that it would not open.

May tried her own Poke Balls. None of them would release the Pokemon inside. They won't open! Ethan, what do we do?

Without their Pokemon, most Trainers were little different from ordinary people when danger struck. They were not people with extraordinary abilities like Bruno or Bea, nor psychic users like Sabrina.

Someone else might have panicked. A Poke Ball that could not be opened from the outside, though, was a small problem for Ethan.

Gardevoir.

At his quiet call, the first Poke Ball in the pouch at his left hip opened at once. A flash of blue light appeared, and his Gardevoir emerged on its own.

That was one of the advantages of having a Psychic-type Pokemon. Gardevoir was not the only one that could do it, either. If Ethan wished, powerful partners such as Deoxys, Rayquaza, the three legendary birds, the two legendary beasts, and even his enormous red Gyarados could leave their Poke Balls by themselves.

Once Gardevoir appeared, Gengar rose from the shadow at Ethan's feet. With both Pokemon beside them, the two could handle most unexpected dangers.

May relaxed at the sight. Having Pokemon close gave them the confidence to face a wild Pokemon if one appeared, rather than having no option but to run.

The forest was large, and they had no clues to follow. They wandered in search of the aurora's source. At least a broad path ran between the trees, so they were unlikely to lose their way.

The deeper they went, the more something felt wrong. May looked around and lowered her voice. Ethan, isn't it a little too...quiet?

She was right. There was no sound from wild Pokemon. Even the wind seemed to have gone still under the strange magnetic field. It reminded Ethan of the forest where they had met Celebi.

Something's ahead, Gengar said suddenly.

Ethan glanced at Gengar and Gardevoir. Both were looking toward the trees ahead, but neither seemed especially wary. Whatever they had noticed was probably not dangerous.

He was about to send Gengar to investigate when a figure jumped down from a large tree and landed in front of them. It was Solana, the Pokemon Ranger they had met before.

What was she doing here? Ethan had only just been thinking of their last encounter with Celebi, and now she had appeared again. Then again, Rangers patrolled nearby. They reached a disturbance as quickly as firefighters reached a blaze.

You two? Solana looked surprised to see Ethan and May. Then she grew awkward, evidently remembering how badly their previous meeting had ended.

May did not dwell on that. Solana, why are you here?

To May, an aurora made by Deoxys hardly seemed like an incident. It had caused no obvious disaster; it was simply something a Pokemon could do. Why would the Rangers be involved?

Solana looked grateful for the friendly question. Whether May meant to or not, she had given Solana an easy way past the awkwardness.

There's a severe geomagnetic anomaly in this area. It's disrupting our equipment, so Ranger headquarters sent me to investigate.

A geomagnetic anomaly. Just as Ethan had thought. May looked behind Solana but saw no sign of Lunick. Where's your partner?

Lunick? He has other work to do, so he isn't with me. What brought you two here?

May glanced at Ethan. He still did not seem inclined to speak. We were passing through. We saw the aurora and came to have a look. That's how we happened to meet you.

Passing through? Solana was not quite convinced. They had been nearby when Celebi appeared, and now they were here for this aurora. She had not known Ethan long, but she doubted it was all coincidence. Was there something the Rangers had missed?

The Ranger Union had a strong intelligence network, but it knew little about Deoxys. Solana did not recognize the two-colored aurora as one of its distinctive signals. She was about to learn.

The sky twisted. A moment later, a Deoxys emerged from the distortion.

Deoxys!? Solana cried. She had not expected to encounter one here, but she recognized it immediately.

It looked toward Ethan from a distance. He could hardly make out its features, yet he had the distinct feeling it bore him no hostility. Then he understood: it must be one of the two Deoxys he had met in LaRousse City.

What a coincidence. The Deoxys seemed to have appeared merely to show itself. After that brief glimpse, it vanished again.

For Solana, even a moment was enough to change her understanding of the situation. I see. The geomagnetic anomaly must have something to do with Deoxys. It wouldn't have appeared here otherwise.

She turned to Ethan, her expression serious. Mr. Vale, this involves a Mythical Pokemon again. To be safe, would you please help me?

Worried he might recall their last disagreement, she added quickly, I promise nothing like last time will happen. If you give me an instruction, I'll carry it out.

Ethan considered her earnest request. He had nothing against Pokemon Rangers; in fact, he admired their work. Solana's conduct the last time had simply made him feel she had gone back on her word.

Now that she was willing to acknowledge it, he had no wish to keep their relationship strained. He would likely deal with Rangers again, and he could hardly stand aside when a Deoxys he knew was involved.

Don't count on me for too much, he said. But if you really need my help, I won't refuse to get involved.

He suspected there would be little danger or fighting here, as long as Rayquaza did not intrude.

Solana let out a quiet breath of relief. After the Celebi incident, she had looked into Ethan's backgroundnot out of suspicion, but curiosity. Strangely, there was no record of his earlier life, as though he had appeared from nowhere.

That mattered less to her than the almost legendary career he had built since becoming a Trainer. She was deeply curious about him. If she could, she wanted to know him better and repair the poor impression she had left with him before.
