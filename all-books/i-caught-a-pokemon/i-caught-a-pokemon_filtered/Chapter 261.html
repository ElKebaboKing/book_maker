Chapter 261: A Look Around the Orange League

"That isn't quite it," Carl said, shaking his head. "The Palace of Victory was built only a little more than ten years ago. It honors the recent winners, not everyone who won before then. And do you remember what I said earlier?"

"Earlier?" Ethan raised an eyebrow. Carl had talked throughout the tour. It was hard to guess which remark he meant, though it must have been something memorable.

After a moment's thought, Ethan asked, "Is it because of the Supreme Gym Leader?"

"Exactly!" Carl laughed. "Our current Supreme Gym Leader, Drake, is so strong that no one has defeated him since he took the position. That's the main reason we've added so few statues in recent years."

So his name was Drake. Ethan did not remember much about him, or how strong he was. He knew only that Ash would eventually beat him and win the Winner's Cup.

Ash was not a reliable measure of an opponent's strength, though. One day he could defeat a legendary Pokemon; another day he might lose to a novice Trainer. If Drake had held the Winner's Cup for years, Ethan could not afford to underestimate him.

They finished their circuit of the Palace of Victory, completing their tour of the Orange League facilities. Ethan had gained a rough understanding of its history and present condition.

It had once enjoyed a brief period of glory. But the Orange Islands' remoteness, small population, limited economy, and relatively few Trainers had held it back. The League remained popular locally without making much of a mark beyond the islands. Carl's voice carried regret when he spoke of its prospects. Still, none of that dimmed the local Trainers' affection for it.

They went back down the hill to headquarters. Just as Carl had predicted, no one had come to register a challenge in the hour or more they had been away.

At the entrance, Carl smiled. "Mr. Vale, please excuse me. That's the end of our tour. We have to publicize tomorrow's match, and time is short."

He pointed toward town. "Have you found somewhere to stay tonight? There's a Pokemon Center in that direction with free rooms and meals. Most challengers stay there. It's quite comfortable."

"Thank you, Mr. Carl, but we've found a guesthouse in the town center," Ethan said. "It has a hot spring, and the food is excellent."

Carl's gaze passed between Ethan and Sabrina. He could tell they were a couple. Perhaps, he thought, they had chosen a guesthouse because it offered them more privacy than a Pokemon Center. He said no more about it.

"Then I'll see you tomorrow. I'll be waiting at the entrance to Pummelo Stadium before the match."

"Thank you. We'll see you there." Ethan held out his hand.

Carl shook it. "I won't keep you. I hope you perform well tomorrow and win."

"Thanks. I hope all goes smoothly."

Ethan and Sabrina walked toward the center of town. Once headquarters was out of sight, Sabrina gave a sudden, dismissive snort. "That Carl is a hypocrite."

"Why do you say that?" Ethan asked.

"He wants Drake to win. Yet he wished you victory to your face."

Ethan took her hand and smiled. "That's courtesy, not hypocrisy. It's how people get along. Should he tell every challenger he hopes his Supreme Gym Leader stays undefeated? The Orange League would have an even harder time attracting anyone."

Sabrina glanced at him. "I wish people could communicate more simply. If we could share our thoughts directly, without hiding our wants and feelings, there would be fewer lies."

Ethan shook his head, amused. It was an idealistic thought. A world where everyone heard everyone else's unguarded thoughts would surely be a mess.

In the town center, the warm sunlight made Ethan reluctant to go straight back to the guesthouse. Hand in hand, they went to the beach. After looking out over the sea, they rented a large parasol and two loungers and settled down in the breeze.

Ethan let a few of his smaller Pokemon out to play on the sand. Then he took out his phone and searched for information about the Winner's Cup. He read about Drake's usual Pokemon and his reputation, then looked through records of recent Orange League matches.

One rule stood out. During a Winner's Cup battle, the Supreme Gym Leader could not switch Pokemon at will. He could send out his next Pokemon only after the one currently fighting had been defeated.

Ethan, as the challenger, could change his lineup to suit the battle and bring in a Pokemon with a favorable type. Drake would have to keep fighting with whichever Pokemon was already out.

Sabrina looked up from the phone, frowning. "That puts the Supreme Gym Leader at a disadvantage. If the League wants its own Trainer to keep winning, why would it make a rule like that?"
