Chapter 635: I'm Done Battling

Before long, the light surrounding Beldum faded completely. A Pokemon with a new form stood before Ethan, Ash, and Brock.

Its blue-green body resembled a flying saucer. It had two arms, a silver spike on its face and another on its back, and two pairs of silver blades extending diagonally backward from its sides. It looked a little like two Beldum joined together.

What Pokemon is that?

Ash had been upset about the battle and thought Ethan had fooled him, but the sight of Beldum evolving swept those feelings away. He picked up his Pokedex and aimed it at the new Pokemon. It responded almost at once.

Metang, the Iron Claw Pokemon. Steel and Psychic type. Metang has two brains connected by magnetic nerves, which produce powerful psychic energy, though its intelligence does not change. Its steel body can collide with a jet without injury. The claws on its hands can tear through thick iron plates as if they were silk. It floats using magnetism and can rotate its arms backward to move at high speed, flying through the air at one hundred kilometers per hour and passing swiftly over rugged mountains. When it spots prey, it grips it with sharp claws and uses powerful psychic energy to stop it moving, leaving no escape. Metang is especially fond of minerals that emit magnetic forces, and it will pursue a Nosepass at one hundred kilometers per hour.

The long description made Ash realize that Metang was nothing like the weak Beldum he had just faced.

He had challenged the Rustboro Gym himself and battled Roxanne's Nosepass, so he knew how formidable that Pokemon could be. Metang hunted even a troublesome Nosepass! It had to be strong.

His excitement returned. Ethan, how about another battle? Use this Pokemon!

But although Ash wanted to fight, Ethan had no intention of battling him again.

He had agreed to the earlier matches to help his two weak Pokemon, each with only a narrow range of attacks, evolve sooner. Now one of them had evolved. Why spend more time here?

Ignoring Ash's eagerness, Ethan laughed and turned him down. Let's leave it. It's getting late. We should go see how May and the others are doing with their practice.

He took out a Poke Ball and recalled Metang without hesitation. Ash's expression froze.

But...

Before Ash could say more, even Brock nodded enthusiastically.

I agree! Brock's eyes opened wide and shone with excitement. There are bound to be lots of beautiful women in swimsuits on the beach. Let's hurry over!

You two... Ash was about to ask if either of them was really a Trainer, but Ethan and Brock had already set off together, talking and laughing. Hey! Don't leave me here by myself!

He watched them walk away, sighed helplessly, and ran after them.

...

When Ethan and the others reached the shore, the long beach was crowded with visitors. Just as Brock had hoped, along with families and older vacationers, there were plenty of pretty young women in bikinis. Some lay sunbathing on the sand; others sat beneath parasols sipping cold drinks. It was quite a sight.

Brock clasped his hands, drooling as he gazed around in delight. Wow! No wonder this beach is such a famous summer destination. It really does have so many beautiful women!

Ethan and Ash ignored him and searched the beach. Even after looking for some time, they couldn't find May, Misty, or Max.

That's strange, Ash said. Where are they? Didn't they say they were coming to the beach to practice?

Ethan glanced at the crowd. There are too many people here. They probably went somewhere quieter. Let's check nearby.

Each taking one of Brock's arms, Ethan and Ash pulled him away from the beach and headed farther along the shore.

The visitors grew fewer and fewer. After five or six hundred meters, they reached a huge rock where hardly anyone else was around.

As they were about to search farther, they heard unusual sounds from the other side of the rock, as though someone was talking. They went around it and immediately spotted Misty and the others.

So this is where you were hiding! Ethan said. You were hard to find.

Misty smiled when she saw them. The main beach was too crowded. We had to come here to practice in private.

A dark gray butterfly Pokemon fluttered gently around May.

Ethan hadn't seen it in person before, but he knew it was May's Beautifly, the Pokemon she planned to enter in the Contest.

Beautifly had pale blue compound eyes and brilliantly patterned wings. It was about the same size as the Butterfree they had seen so often in Kanto and Johto, but Ethan thought it far more beautiful.

Beautifly gathered in flower fields to collect pollen and traveled on spring breezes in search of more. When flowers filled a garden, they flew from bloom to bloom. They loved sweet pollen so much that one would come to a flowering pot set by a window and use its long, needlelike mouth to draw pollen from it.

Despite its lovely appearance, Beautifly was aggressive and loved a fight. When angered, it attacked fiercely, piercing opponents with its slender proboscis and draining their bodily fluids. Greedy for nectar, it would even drive away Combee already feeding on flowers so it could have the sweet food to itself.

Ethan had learned while chatting at the cafe that May had caught this Beautifly only recently. In just a few days, it had evolved all the way from a Wurmple into its present form. Bug-type Pokemon really could grow at a remarkable speed.

But from the look of things, May and Beautifly still weren't working together very smoothly...
