Chapter 331: Jolteon vs. Dewgong

As the Poke Ball opened and blue light flashed, an eager Jolteon appeared before Dewgong.

Dewgong was a Water- and Ice-type Pokemon, which made Jolteon's Electric-type moves super effective against it. Before the battle had even begun, Ethan seemed to hold a considerable advantage.

Pryce knew that perfectly well, but his expression did not change.

He had been a Pokemon Trainer for more than fifty years and had fought countless battles. There was hardly a situation he had not seen.

Over those years, even before Ethan arrived, Dewgong had faced dozens of Jolteon. Pryce naturally had his own methods for handling an unfavorable type matchup.

Come, young Champion. Show me what makes you different from all the others!

As soon as the battle began, both Trainers gave their commands.

Expecting Ethan to open with an Electric-type move, Pryce's first order was for Dewgong to raise a wall of ice in front of itself.

Dewgong clapped its flippers together, and an ice wall suddenly rose from the ground. The next instant, Jolteon's Thunder Shock struck it with lightning speed.

Bang!

Perhaps Dewgong's power had made the wall exceptionally thick and solid, or perhaps electricity simply held no advantage over ice. Whatever the reason, the attack failed to shatter it and only sent layers of ice chips spraying away.

"Jolteon, go around it!"

At Ethan's command, Jolteon raced toward one side.

Unfortunately, although its claws were sharp, they were poorly suited to running across ice. Its speed dropped sharply, giving Dewgong more time to react.

Jolteon had only just reached Dewgong's other side and begun gathering electricity when Dewgong fired an Ice Shard at it.

Boom!

Ice and electricity collided in a violent explosion. The current shattered the Ice Shard into glittering dust that drifted down between the two Pokemon like a cloud of white mist.

Ethan raised an eyebrow.

Two failed attacks in a row made him realize that the old man before him would not be easy to defeat.

Dewgong was clearly powerful, and Pryce had a wealth of experience. Ethan had fought hot-blooded opponents, impulsive ones, calm ones, and careless ones. This time, he had met a far more calculating adversary.

Under Pryce's direction, this Dewgong was the strongest of its species Ethan had encountered since beginning his journey.

Pryce's unruffled expression made one thing obvious: he had even anticipated Jolteon's difficulty moving across the ice.

This battle is going to be harder than I expected...

Pryce was clearly a cut above Johto's other Gym Leaders, even the formidable Clair.

But that was fine.

Ethan glanced at Jolteon.

The stronger the opponent, the more worthwhile the challenge. If Pryce's defense left no opening, Ethan would simply smash through it with all their strength.

"Jolteon! Stop holding back and unleash everything you've got!"

At Ethan's command, Jolteon's fur stood on end with excitement. No longer caring whether it might slip, it burst forward and raced across the ice, launching a fiercer assault on Dewgong.

Tap... tap... tap-tap-tap...

Jolteon's claws touched lightly against the frozen surface as it ran faster and faster. It nearly fell several times, but it was an agile Pokemon. Within a few breaths, it had found its rhythm.

To keep its footing, it drove its claws hard into the ice with every step, anchoring itself before pushing forward again.

Pryce narrowed his eyes at the sight.

Impressive! It learned and adapted in the middle of battle!

Still running, Jolteon first moved into an open area and fired Pin Missile, forcing Dewgong to defend. While Dewgong's view was blocked, Jolteon bounded rapidly between the ice pillars and closed the distance. Electricity flashed between its jaws as it lunged to bite Dewgong.

By the time Dewgong caught sight of Jolteon again, the two Pokemon were already face-to-face.

Pryce frowned. He recognized Thunder Fang at a glance.

They were far too close now. Dewgong had no time to form another ice wall.

Not good!

"Dewgong! Quickly, use Icy Wind!"

Dewgong opened its mouth and blew a bitter wind filled with snow and ice toward Jolteon. To Pryce's surprise, Jolteon did not retreat. It forced its way through the damaging gale and sank its electrified fangs into Dewgong.

"Gong!"

The super effective hit made Dewgong cry out in pain.

That choice caught Pryce off guard as well.

Most Trainers disliked trading injury for injury. They preferred to protect their own Pokemon while gaining the greatest possible advantage at the smallest cost.

This was real life, not a game. Pokemon truly felt pain. Few could overcome their fear and charge through an opponent's attack to deliver their own.

What Pryce did not know was that Ethan had not deliberately trained Jolteon to fight this way.

Perhaps Gyarados's example had influenced the others. Ethan's Pokemon had all become bold and aggressive. Even when badly hurt, they would find a way to strike back.

They had gradually influenced one another until the entire team shared the same fighting style.

Jolteon kept its jaws locked on Dewgong and worried at it for a while longer.

At first, Dewgong struggled fiercely and tried to counter with Aqua Tail. Jolteon released its bite, struck Dewgong with Double Kick, and immediately followed with Discharge, knocking it down.

Crackle!

Dazzling electricity spread in every direction. Under its heat, the solid ice across the field began to crack and melt, and water seeped up through the fissures.

Dewgong's eyes rolled back, and its body slowly sagged to the ground.

Seeing it unconscious, Pryce shook his head lightly, took out its Poke Ball, and recalled it.

"You won the first round."

His voice remained calm, revealing neither pleasure nor anger.

A man of his age and self-control would not readily display his thoughts through emotion.

"Thank you for the match."

Ethan nodded to Pryce, then beckoned to Jolteon.

"You worked hard. Come back and rest."

He was about to recall Jolteon, but it remained where it stood and merely turned to call at him.

Hm? What's wrong?

One look at Jolteon's excitement gave Ethan his answer. The battle had brought it fully into form, and now it was enjoying itself too much to stop.

"You want to keep battling?"

Jolteon nodded emphatically. Ethan thought for a moment, then gave it a helpless look and laughed.

"All right. Since you're insisting, you can fight one more round!"

Across the field, Pryce watched in silence. The closeness between Ethan and Jolteon filled him with melancholy.

Had there not once been a time when he, too, had trusted his Pokemon and shared such an inseparable bond with them?

Unfortunately, after what had happened, he had come to understand that Pokemon were merely animals. They could never become partners whom humans could trust with their lives.
