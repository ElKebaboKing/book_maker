Chapter 352: Back to Johto

Early the next morning, Ethan ate breakfast at the Pokemon Center and returned to the Celadon Gym.

It was still early. The construction workers had not begun their shift, and the temporary wooden building remained locked. Ethan was in no hurry. He found a sunlit spot and waited quietly.

The morning breeze felt slightly chilly. Before long, Erika approached from the distance, accompanied by several pretty young women.

She still wore a kimono, but unlike her more elaborate everyday outfits, today's clothes were clearly better suited for travel. The garment fit more closely, with cords securing the sleeves and trouser legs.

Every time Ethan saw Erika, she wore a different style of kimono. He could not tell whether her family rules required it or she simply loved dressing this way.

When she reached him, Erika bowed slightly and apologized.

"I'm sorry I'm late. Have you been waiting long?"

"Not at all." Ethan smiled. "I only just arrived. So... are you ready? If everything's settled, we can leave now!"

Erika nodded, accepted her backpack from one of the women, and waved to them.

"You can go back now. This is far enough. I'll leave the Gym and the shop in your care!"

The women surrounded Erika with tears in their eyes, clearly reluctant to part with her.

"Miss Erika! Please take care of yourself!"

"Miss Erika, you have to come back soon!"

"Miss Erika..."

Their overlapping chatter filled Ethan's ears. Each voice would have sounded pleasant on its own, but together they created such a noisy clamor that his head began to ache.

"You girls..." Erika smiled and shook her head, then gently reassured them. "There's no need to be so sad. This isn't a final farewell. Don't worry, I'll be back soon."

The scene showed how well Erika got along with them despite technically being their employer. Ethan even remembered the relaxed, harmonious atmosphere when he first visited the perfume shop and Celadon Gym. It demonstrated just how approachable Erika was.

After saying goodbye to the reluctant women, Erika left the Gym grounds with Ethan. They continued along the street until the group disappeared from sight.

"They really hate to see you leave."

Erika's expression became nostalgic. "We've spent a long time together. I even met some of them when they were children. After all these years, we've become very close friends!"

Such a deep friendship. That's rather enviable...

They continued down the street. Upon reaching an intersection, Ethan stopped and turned toward Erika.

"Now we need to choose our route to Johto. Would you prefer to fly, take a ship, or travel to Saffron City first and board the magnetic levitation train to Goldenrod City?"

As Ethan had said the previous day, modern transportation was highly developed. In addition to walking or riding Pokemon, they could choose much faster human methods.

An airport stood on the outskirts of Celadon City. Although the city lay in central Kanto, the nearby inland sea also gave it a large port, with many ships traveling to other regions every day.

Flying was naturally the best option, requiring the least time. The magnetic levitation train came second, although they would need to backtrack to Saffron City. Sailing was the slowest route and would waste far more time, making it Ethan's least favored choice.

Left to Ethan, riding Charizard back to Johto would have been ideal. But he was traveling with Erika now. Out of respect, he could not decide alone and needed to ask her opinion first.

Erika thought for a moment. Instead of choosing, she asked, "How do you normally travel?"

Ethan answered without hesitation. "I walk for short distances and rely on Charizard for longer ones. Charizard isn't the fastest flier, but it's convenient. We can stop or depart whenever we want, so we have complete freedom!"

"Then let's ride Charizard together." Erika smiled. "You're still challenging Gyms, so you must need to train your Pokemon continually along the road. We can follow your usual method. There's no need to accommodate me!"

"But..."

"Don't worry." Erika made a small fist. "I'm a Trainer too. I'm not that delicate!"

She really is considerate...

Ethan still hesitated, but since Erika had insisted, he stopped trying to persuade her.

"All right. We'll ride Charizard back to Johto. A long flight can be tiring, though. If you feel uncomfortable, don't hesitate to tell me. We can always stop, rest for a while, and continue afterward!"

When Erika nodded, Ethan took out a Poke Ball and released Charizard.

He secured Erika's luggage along one side of Charizard, then helped her climb aboard like a gentleman. Only after she settled into place did Ethan mount as well.

When he traveled with Sabrina, she normally sat in front and leaned back against his chest. With Erika, propriety between a man and woman required more distance. She sat behind Ethan and kept a small gap between them so their bodies would not touch.

Once everything was ready, Ethan patted Charizard's neck and asked it to fly as steadily as possible. Then he tapped his heels against its sides. Charizard sprang into the air, spread its wings, and soared into the clouds, carrying them toward Johto.

...

Above an unidentified area of Johto.

After departing Celadon City and flying for an entire day, they finally reached Johto before nightfall.

They had landed only once along the way to eat lunch. Apart from that break, they had traveled without stopping.

Just as Erika had said, she truly was less delicate than her appearance suggested.

Before she was a perfume shop owner or flower arranger, she was a Gym Leader and a Trainer. Neither the biting air nor the monotonous journey had shaken her. She endured them without complaint.

At least, that was how she appeared.

Ethan checked the time. Seeing how late it had grown, he looked back at Erika.

"All right. Now that we've reached Johto, there's no need to hurry any farther. Let's stop here and rest for the night!"
