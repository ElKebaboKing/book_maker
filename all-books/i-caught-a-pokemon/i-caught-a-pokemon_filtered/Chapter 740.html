Chapter 740: Meeting Bea for the Third Time

It is, Shelly said with a long sigh. She leaned on the railing beside them and watched the white clouds on the horizon. Thanks to you, we're both in the clear. They no longer classify us as members of Team Aqua and Team Magma. We're officially your informants now. We've cut ties with those organizations completely.

Ethan looked to Courtney. She nodded; she had received the same treatment.

Did Anabel say what will happen to Maxie and Archie?

What else? They'll go back to the mainland and stand trial. I expect they'll spend the rest of their lives locked up with other serious offenders.

Ethan was not surprised. There was no death penalty in this world. A life sentence, leaving them to grow old alone in a small cell, was the harshest punishment they could receive.

Good. What will you two do next?

Shelly gave him a half-smile. What can we do? That woman just put us under your supervision. We're still under observation. Officially and privately, it seems we're tied to your side.

No one's restricting your freedom, Ethan said. The supervision is mostly a formality. I'm going to keep traveling; I can't have you with me all the time. Anabel knows that. It's probably why she made me your supervisor.

He thought a moment. Actually, do what I suggested before. Both of you should go to Kanto. I have an investment there, and a group of people who've had trouble fitting into ordinary society have gathered around it. You'd fit in. More than that, I'd like your help keeping an eye on them.

His expression and tone told Shelly and Courtney there was more to the request.

Are they causing trouble? Courtney asked quietly.

Not yet, Ethan said with a shrug. The investment started small, and I didn't pay it much attention. But the group keeps growing. I worry they'll use my name to make trouble. I'd feel better if the two of you watched them for me.

I see. Courtney agreed at once. If that's what you want, I'll go to Kanto.

Shelly rolled her eyes at being beaten to the answer. As you command, Mr. Supervisor.

Are you making fun of me? Ethan glared at her. This is a necessary step toward clearing your names.

Don't like the title? Shelly drew out the question and gave him a mischievous look. Then I suppose I'll call you Boss. Or would you rather we call you Master?

Ethan nearly choked. Shelly really was bold. No wonder she had risen to the second-highest position in an organization like Team Aqua. She knew how to say something outrageous without blinking.

But this was not a joke to make in front of Misty. Even Boss sounded wrong. Anyone who heard it might think he had set up another organization like Team Aqua or Team Magma.

Then he remembered that the bikers already called him that, and the name had spread. He sighed. They could call him whatever they liked.

...

The patrol boats were taking Archie and Maxie back to headquarters, away from Ethan's route. When they passed Muscle Island, Ethan and Misty disembarked. They would see whether they could transfer to a ship bound for the Hoenn mainland.

Shelly and Courtney would accompany Anabel to the International Police's Hoenn headquarters first. Once they completed the final paperwork, they could leave from there and fly to Kanto.

After saying goodbye, Ethan and Misty stood on Muscle Island's pier and waved to them. They watched the patrol boat until it vanished, then turned to look around. Leaving the boat here had been a last-minute decision. They knew nothing about the island.

It was small, but distinctive. A ring of forest surrounded a modest town. At the island's center, two connected rock formations looked like an arm flexing its muscles.

What surprised them was the port. No ships were docked there, and there was not a person in sight.

That's strange, Misty said, looking around. Where is everyone?

Ethan checked the time. It was only about three or four in the afternoon, too early for dinner. Even if people were eating, surely someone would still be out on the streets.

The empty streets were not really a problem for them. The empty harbor was. How would they get back to mainland Hoenn? Would he have to ride Dragonite again? It would be fast, but sitting in one position for hours while the sea wind battered him was uncomfortable. His body had had enough of it.

Let's find someone to ask. Given the island's size and location, ships may not come often. We might have to stay here tonight.

They left the harbor and walked through town. It was not entirely deserted. Through a general store's window, they saw an elderly woman struggling to arrange the goods. They had just started toward her when a chorus of shouts rose from a nearby forest park.

It sounded like a crowd exercising, voices ringing out in a vigorous rhythm. Curious, Ethan and Misty exchanged a look and headed for the park instead.

Past several thick trees, they came upon a clearing where thirty or forty men and women were training. The youngest were in their mid-teens, the oldest in their forties. All wore sports clothes, though some had taken off their shirts, and others wore hats or sunglasses. They did squats, threw punches, climbed, and lifted weights. Every one of them looked athletic.

That's... Misty stared. Quite a sight.

Seeing so many people exercise together was startling. The whole park seemed thick with sweat and raw enthusiasm, almost too much for either of them.

Then Ethan noticed someone familiar in a corner of the clearing: a gray-haired girl in a black-and-white athletic top and shorts, working through the exercises with the rest.

Wait. Isn't that Bea?

Misty followed his gaze. It is! What is she doing here, training with all these people?

Let's ask.

They circled the edge of the park and approached Bea. She was so focused on her workout that she did not notice them until Misty greeted her. Bea turned in surprise.

You two?

She plainly had not expected to meet Ethan and Misty here again.

Good to see you again, Bea, Misty said. What are you doing here?

Training my muscles with everyone so I can grow stronger.

Your muscles? Ethan glanced around, puzzled. I understand the idea, but will this actually make you stronger? Is it doing anything for you?

Bea was strong enough to contend physically with Pokemon. Her reputation as a once-in-a-century talent in Galar was well deserved. The others in the park, however, were doing fairly basic exercises. With Bea's conditioning, even twice this difficulty would probably bring little improvement.

She had not considered that. The man in charge told me that if I followed his method, I would definitely get stronger.

That was all it took to convince her? Ethan and Misty exchanged another look.

Is it possible, Misty asked carefully, that he misled you?

Bea looked at her in obvious confusion.

Does the man even know how strong you already are? Ethan asked.

Bea tilted her head. What do you mean?

For all her dedication to training and her position as a Fighting-type Gym Leader, she seemed innocent about other people and their intentions.

I mean he might not understand your abilities. He saw how young you are and assumed you're an ordinary beginner. This routine is probably just wasting your time.

Bea thought that through in earnest. I see. That explains why I haven't noticed any changes in my body.

Ethan and Misty stared at her. She was only realizing that now?

At that moment, a young man came over from the other side of the clearing, perhaps because their conversation had interrupted Bea's training. He looked about twenty-seven or twenty-eight, with tanned skin, a lean muscular build, a sleeveless athletic shirt, and short reddish-brown hair. He had an open, good-looking face.

He glanced at Bea, then at Ethan and Misty. Good afternoon. Is this your first time on the island?

He gave them a brilliant smile. His teeth were so white that they almost seemed to flash. This must be the man Bea had mentioned. At least he seemed friendly and straightforward.

It is, Ethan said. We hoped to take a ship back to mainland Hoenn, but there aren't any at the harbor. Why is town so empty? And where did all the boats go?

Oh, that. A warning went out that the waters around here would be dangerous for the next few days. Most people took the boats to larger cities this morning to wait it out. Didn't you receive the warning before you came?

Dangerous waters. Ethan understood: the warning must have been about Groudon and Kyogre. Even this town's emptiness was a consequence of their battle. The storm they had raised had finally blown back into his own plans.

If the town evacuated, Misty asked, why did all of you stay?

The man waved a hand. A few waves and a storm can't break our spirit. Trainers should toughen themselves in extreme weather!

They were a determined group. Many Trainers these days sheltered in a Pokemon Center at the first hint of rain, unwilling to endure any discomfort. These people still welcomed a challenge.

Unfortunately for them, the storm they had been waiting for would never come. Ethan had already ended it.

Even with the warning lifted, the town's boats would not return until its residents did. No ship was likely to call today. Ethan and Misty would have to spend the night.

The man looked them over. You're Trainers too, aren't you? Why not join us? The secret to winning Pokemon battles lies in a Trainer's muscles. Without a body honed through hard work, you can't win!

Only a Fighting-type Trainer would say that. The group's physiques and exercises made it clear that Fighting types were their specialty. No wonder Bea had joined them.

Ethan could not agree with the man's claim. Surely Pokemon battles depended first on the Pokemon. A Trainer mattered too, but building muscles while neglecting tactics would only get a person so far. He saw no point in arguing with this crowd.

Thanks, but we'll pass. He smiled. We're friends of Bea's. We saw her here and just came over to say hello.
