Chapter 446: A Bloody Scene Reappears

At the side of the field, the referee was the first person in the entire arena to recover. Seeing Trinity still frozen in shock, he hurriedly swept down his flag to signal the start of the battle.

Trinity hastily came to her senses. She forced herself to focus and issued a command, but no matter how loudly she called, Chinchou showed no response!

It's over!

The poor thing has been frightened out of its wits!

Trinity silently cried out in alarm. She immediately took out its Poke Ball and decisively recalled Chinchou.

I'd better let it rest inside for a while...

She sighed and raised her head. After studying the red Gyarados for a long moment with a complicated gaze, she appeared to reach a decision. A look of determination filled her face as she took out her second Poke Ball.

Blue light flashed, and a Gyarados appeared in the pool.

Trinity understood perfectly that an ordinary Pokemon would lose the contest of presence against Ethan's red Gyarados before the battle even began. It might be terrified into helplessness just like Chinchou. At this point, her only option was to confront the red Gyarados with her own!

Fortunately, her Gyarados also possessed the Intimidate Ability. It should be able to withstand the red Gyarados's pressure!

Unfortunately, that assumption was far too optimistic...

Like every other member of its species, Trinity's Gyarados roared when it appeared. Yet compared with the red Gyarados, its voice was unquestionably inferior. Then their gazes met, and Trinity clearly saw her Gyarados recoil despite itself!

What!?

Even Gyarados can't do it?

Trinity's expression changed. If even her Gyarados lost its courage before the battle, there would be no point continuing. She might as well surrender immediately!

Fortunately, it had not been completely frightened out of its wits. Although it recoiled at first, it soon straightened its body again. At the very least, it still appeared capable of resisting, which eased some of the tension in Trinity's heart.

Ethan naturally saw every detail of her Gyarados's reaction.

There were differences even between Gyarados!

No ordinary specimen could compare with his. This absolute monster had fought its way through brutal life-and-death struggles and then undergone further enhancement!

Once Trinity's Gyarados regained its fighting spirit, Ethan had no intention of giving her additional time to adjust.

"Go."

Ethan spoke softly. His low voice seemed unable to reach the red Gyarados across the distance and difference in elevation. Yet the Pokemon appeared connected to him in mind and spirit. Even from the slightest movement of his lips, it understood his intent.

The red Gyarados's enormous body plunged beneath the water, sending rings of waves across the surface.

Trinity's gaze sharpened. Rather than waiting helplessly, she ordered her own Gyarados underwater to search for the red Gyarados.

Currents surged below. On the enormous screen, the spectators could clearly see two shadows, one large and one small, collide beneath the surface!

An ordinary Gyarados was generally about six and a half meters long. That was already enormous compared with most other Pokemon. Yet judging by the two shadows now, the red Gyarados was fully three times its opponent's size. By that estimate, it had to be nearly twenty meters long!

What did that mean?

A six-and-a-half-meter Gyarados was comparable in length to a medium truck capable of carrying eight tons. The nearly twenty-meter red Gyarados was closer to one of the enormous, long and heavy trucks used for the largest loads.

Of course, the comparison was not entirely accurate. Their width hardly resembled that of a truck. More than anything, the description captured the overwhelming impression they created!

When the two Pokemon collided, the water's surface suddenly churned violently. Before the audience could see what had happened, spray exploded upward. The red Gyarados burst from the water with the other Gyarados pinned before it and continued driving it backward. They smashed through two stone pillars in succession before the red Gyarados finally crushed its opponent against the arena's thick outer wall!

Bang!

A web of cracks spread across the wall's stonework, demonstrating the red Gyarados's tremendous strength!

Both Pokemon were famous for their power, yet the red Gyarados had instantly overwhelmed one of its own species!

Before the fragments of the shattered pillars could even fall into the water, a wail of pain rang from Trinity's Gyarados!

While the audience still stared in shock at the red Gyarados's outrageous power, it opened its jaws and bit deeply into its opponent's neck!

Gyarados screamed again, even more miserably than before!

Trinity frowned. At first, she believed the red Gyarados was using Bite, but she immediately noticed something was wrong!

This was no move. It was simply tearing into its opponent with its teeth!

The announcer noticed the difference as well and cried out in disbelief, "What!? The red Gyarados is ripping the scales from Gyarados's body! What kind of move is this!?"

It tore away a large patch of scales. Bright red blood immediately sprayed from the wound and dyed the water beneath Gyarados crimson!

And the attack was not over. The red Gyarados opened its enormous mouth once more and bit into the other side of Gyarados's neck!

Cries of alarm erupted from the stands!

Even Sea Priestess Maya shot to her feet on the ceremonial platform!

No one present had ever witnessed such a bloody scene!

How could this be called a Pokemon battle? It was a primal struggle to the death!

Across the history of Pokemon competition, some ferocious Pokemon had certainly appeared. Yet even those creatures generally followed the rules of battle and used the familiar moves everyone recognized.

But now...

The red Gyarados's fighting style revealed nothing but raw instinct!

There was no technique whatsoever. It simply crushed everything with power!

After only one exchange, Gyarados, a species renowned for its ferocity, had completely lost the ability to fight. The sight demonstrated just how powerful the red Gyarados truly was!

In the command area, Ethan also knew it was time to end the battle. If he allowed this to continue, Trinity's Gyarados would probably die on the spot.

He lightly snapped his fingers. The red Gyarados immediately released its opponent at the far edge of the battlefield and ceased its attack. It dove beneath the water, and when it surfaced again, it had already returned to Ethan's side.

This astonished the spectators all over again!

In their experience, the more ferocious a Pokemon was, the less likely it would be to obey its Trainer. Once battle overwhelmed its reason, it would never stop until its stamina was exhausted!

So why did Ethan's red Gyarados obey him so perfectly?

It defied all common sense!

How had he possibly raised it?

Ethan did not know that Maya was watching from the ceremonial platform. Her thoughtful gaze moved repeatedly between him and the red Gyarados, as though she were deciding something...
