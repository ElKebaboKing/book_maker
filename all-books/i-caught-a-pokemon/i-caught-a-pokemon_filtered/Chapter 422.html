Chapter 422: The Battle to Capture Raikou

After parting with Jimmy and the others, Ethan and Erika flew east above the forest on Charizard. They had not gone far when a Pokemon approached them head-on.

It was Ethan's Scyther!

When Scyther saw him, it hurriedly waved its scythe-like arms to catch his attention.

Ethan had sent Scyther out so it could report back once Gengar pinpointed Raikou's location. Now that it had returned to meet him, they had clearly found Raikou's trail.

Ethan patted Charizard on the neck and had it move closer to Scyther.

Once the two Pokemon were flying side by side, Ethan immediately asked, "How did it go? Did you find Raikou?"

Scyther nodded and began saying something. Unfortunately, Ethan could understand simple gestures, but with Gengar elsewhere, anything more complicated was completely beyond him.

That did not matter. Scyther simply took the lead and soon guided Charizard to a small lake in the forest.

From her seat behind Ethan, Erika spotted a Pokemon in the distance.

"Look! It's Raikou!"

Ethan followed the direction she indicated. Sure enough, Raikou was lying on a boulder, repeatedly extending its tongue to lick the places where it had been burned by electricity.

Much like animals on Earth, wild Pokemon resembling animals had substances in their saliva that reduced inflammation. By licking their injuries, they treated their own wounds. Their immune systems were also many times stronger than a human's, so they did not need to worry about bacteria in the saliva causing an infection.

To avoid attracting Raikou's attention, Ethan had Charizard land some distance away. Then he and Erika advanced carefully and quietly.

The lake was not large, and a stream flowed through it. The rushing water conveniently masked their footsteps.

On the opposite side of the lake, Gengar peeked from behind a large tree. When it spotted Ethan in the distance, it gave him a thumbs-up.

Ethan had no idea what Gengar meant, so he ignored it and focused all his attention on Raikou.

The two continued drawing closer. Only when they reached the lakeshore and lost the cover of the trees did Raikou finally realize someone was approaching. It sprang to its feet in alarm.

But it was already too late!

Ethan and Erika stepped in front of it with Scyther. Their gazes met, and Ethan wasted no words before stating his purpose.

"Raikou, battle me!"

Raikou's eyes contracted when it saw that man!

It had already witnessed his strength and knew he possessed the three Legendary birds. Why would it possibly fight him?

From Raikou's perspective, he was an opponent against whom resistance was utterly hopeless!

That such a proud Legendary Pokemon would lose its nerve before the battle even began showed just how deeply Tri-Bird Assault had scarred it!

Without another thought, Raikou turned to flee. Before it could take more than a few steps, a figure dropped abruptly from the sky. Charizard landed in front of it and blocked the way!

Raikou halted and changed direction, only for Gengar to appear before it and close off that route as well!

Forced to turn back, Raikou gave Ethan a threatening roar and assumed a battle stance.

Ethan took a Poke Ball from his belt and released his Pokemon.

Light flashed, and Crystal Steelix's enormous body appeared before Raikou.

Ethan did not intend to overwhelm Raikou with superior numbers this time. He wanted to capture it in a one-on-one battle. Complete fairness, however, was impossible. Raikou's individual strength was simply too great. It might have suffered injuries recently, but Ethan could not underestimate it.

Crystal Steelix was immune to Electric-type moves, which greatly weakened Raikou. With its primary means of attack taken away, the two Pokemon's strength became much more evenly matched.

"Crystal Steelix, use Tackle!"

At Ethan's command, Crystal Steelix drove its enormous body toward Raikou.

Faced with the onrushing opponent and no path of retreat, Raikou had no choice but to fight.

As Crystal Steelix closed in, Raikou suddenly accelerated and slipped aside to evade it.

Raikou was already extremely fast, but Ethan saw clearly that it had used Quick Attack in that instant, leaving Crystal Steelix unable to touch it.

Raikou was not finished. As the two Pokemon crossed paths, it opened its jaws and used Crunch, biting down on Crystal Steelix's body!

Crystal Steelix cried out in pain and swung its massive tail. Raikou had already released its bite, however, and leaped safely aside.

"You're quite good!"

Ethan could not help praising Raikou's performance, and the more he saw, the more he liked it.

Every movement resembled that of a calm hunter in the jungle. Once it found the right opportunity, it could often deliver a fatal blow.

But Ethan also had great confidence in Crystal Steelix. He trusted that it would not disappoint him and believed in its abilities.

"Crystal Steelix, use Rock Tomb!"

After Raikou's attack, Crystal Steelix opened its enormous mouth and used Rock Tomb.

More than a dozen boulders abruptly formed in the sky and began crashing down around Raikou.

If the jagged stones struck, they would inflict considerable damage. They would also seal off Raikou's routes of movement and greatly reduce its speed.

Crash! Crash! Crash-crash!

The rocks plunged rapidly into the earth.

With its swift footwork and exceptional jumping ability, however, Raikou avoided every one.

Ethan was not surprised.

Against such an agile opponent, he had never expected Rock Tomb to land. The attack's main purpose was to restrict Raikou's movement so it could no longer use its speed to circle Crystal Steelix.

Sure enough, once rocks of varying sizes covered the lakeshore, Raikou could no longer attack Crystal Steelix as it had before. Every movement had to be made carefully to avoid colliding with them.

Crystal Steelix's movement was also affected, but it was already extremely slow, so the difference hardly mattered. Besides, with its enormous body, it could simply crush the rocks into gravel and continue onward without much difficulty.

Unable to use Electric-type moves and now deprived of its speed, Raikou found itself at an ever-greater disadvantage.

Crystal Steelix rampaged across the shore. Every roll of its massive body shook the earth, and it could snap thick trees in half without even trying!

Driven into a corner, Raikou revealed the cunning of a hunter once more.

It positioned itself with its back to the lake, baiting Crystal Steelix into an attack. As Crystal Steelix charged and drew near, Raikou leaped sideways. It avoided the impact, and with a tremendous splash, Crystal Steelix was unable to stop and plunged headfirst into the water!

Splash!

A huge wave rose and crashed against the lakeshore. When the surface finally settled, Crystal Steelix had vanished from sight!

Its plan successful, Raikou turned back toward Ethan.

Ground- and Rock-type Pokemon were weak to water. Trainers knew it, and Pokemon understood the principle as well.

With an opponent that heavy, falling into the lake should have made it virtually impossible to climb out again!

But...

Raikou had expected to see panic in the man's eyes. To its confusion, Ethan showed none at all. Instead, he watched it with an almost amused smile!
