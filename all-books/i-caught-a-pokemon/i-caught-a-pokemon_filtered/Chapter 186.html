Chapter 186: The Wave-Riding Race

With everything said, Ethan let Senta's behavior go. He had come to challenge the Gym, not to decide how Cissy should raise her brother or protect its reputation.

"So the second contest was a tie as well," he said. "What do you have planned for the last one?"

Cissy no longer underestimated him. "A wave-riding race. Win it and you'll earn Mikan Gym's Coral-Eye Badge."

She recalled Seadra and led Ethan and Sabrina outside to a beach beside the Gym.

Waves washed over their feet as Ethan and Sabrina walked side by side, leaving two lines of footprints, one deeper than the other. Ethan looked back at the building. From this angle, it resembled a red soda can lying on its side more than a warehouse. Even its windows were shaped like the can's opening.

Cissy pointed toward a buoy with a red flag far out at sea. "The first Pokemon to reach that flag, go around it, and return here wins."

Ethan could barely make out the flag bobbing among the waves. He estimated it was at least six or seven hundred meters from shore. The round trip would be more than a kilometer, demanding endurance as well as speed, especially from a small Pokemon like Seadra.

Cissy had prepared for that. She opened a Poke Ball and released a Blastoise of her own.

The two Blastoise stood together. They looked alike, but Ethan had no trouble telling his partner from hers.

His Blastoise had used some energy in the first two contests. Ethan recalled it and sent out a different Pokemon.

"This one's yours, Gyarados!"

The enormous Gyarados roared as it appeared. Its intimidating presence made Cissy's Blastoise retreat several steps.

She reassured her Pokemon, then studied Gyarados. "That's quite a presence. I've seen many Gyarados, but few as imposing as yours."

"Really? I hadn't noticed anything unusual," Ethan said with a shrug. "Most of the time I use it to travel across the sea."

"You use a Pokemon this strong as transport? I suppose the Indigo League champion can afford to waste talent."

"Oh, it's nothing. I have several Pokemon like it. Gyarados isn't even my strongest."

He waved away the praise modestly, though inside he was delighted. The hard battles of the Indigo League had helped Gyarados grow quickly, and it had become more frightening than ever.

Fortunately, the rough lesson Ethan had given it soon after its evolution still held. It remained obedient and had never refused one of his commands.

Cissy shook her head and let the matter drop. She urged Blastoise into the sea, then jumped onto its back.

Ethan was still standing on the beach when she slapped her forehead. "Oh, I forgot to tell you. The trainers ride along in this race."

He wondered why that was necessary, but he was not afraid of a little rough water. It was a Gym rule. Without arguing, he gripped one of Gyarados's scales, climbed smoothly onto its back, and patted its head. At his signal, it swam out beside Blastoise to the starting line.

Senta was gone, so Sabrina acted as the starter. She raised a starting pistol and fired a shot into the air.

Bang!

Both Pokemon surged forward. Even braced for the start, Ethan and Cissy were jolted by the acceleration.

They raced away like arrows, gathering speed. Blastoise could not quite match Gyarados for pace or stamina and fell behind early.

Wave after wave broke over them. Cissy drew on years of racing experience and tried to use the current to help Blastoise catch up. But Gyarados had carried Ethan over the sea often enough to know the water well.

From its back, Ethan watched the course ahead. "Gyarados, take the inside line!"

They had started side by side, but the shorter route around the flag would save both time and effort. Reaching that position first mattered.

Gyarados began to turn. Its body suddenly jolted. Ethan almost lost his footing, but caught its neck and kept himself out of the water.

He looked back. Cissy had tried to claim the inside line too, and her Blastoise's hard shell had struck Gyarados.

"Sorry!" Cissy called. "There's bound to be some contact in a Pokemon race. I hope you don't mind!"

Ethan shook his head and looked ahead again. Gyarados was much heavier than Blastoise. Now that he expected the contact, it did not worry him.

They brushed against each other a few more times, but Ethan stayed in front. Gyarados rounded the buoy first and turned for shore.

Cissy's Blastoise chased with all it had. The gap only grew: one body length, then two, then three.

Gyarados reached the finish well ahead. Cissy could only smile ruefully as Ethan won the race.
