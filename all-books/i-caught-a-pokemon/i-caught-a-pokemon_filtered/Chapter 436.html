Chapter 436: Ash Suffers an Upset

Ash's face lit up when Psyduck entered the battle. Delighted, and seemingly afraid that Misty might change her mind, he hurriedly ordered Kingler to attack.

Kingler moved sideways across the water and charged rapidly toward Psyduck.

Perhaps Psyduck had struggled in the water for too long, or perhaps Kingler's momentum was simply too frightening. Whatever the reason, Psyduck appeared to have lost its will to fight. It crouched trembling on the stone pillar with its head bowed, leaving its enormous head completely exposed to Kingler.

Ash naturally would not let such an opportunity pass.

When Kingler reached Psyduck, Ash did not hesitate. He ordered it to raise its oversized pincer, clamp down on Psyduck's vacant-looking head, and lift the duck's entire body high into the air!

As the pincer continued squeezing, Psyduck writhed in unbearable pain, its plump body twisting back and forth. The spectators felt sorry for it, yet could not help finding the sight a little amusing.

Everyone was thinking the same thing. Psyduck could not swim and showed no desire to battle. Had Misty truly sent it out for any reason other than comic relief?

The spectators knew nothing about Psyduck's circumstances. Only Ethan, along with Brock in the stands, shook his head helplessly at the sight.

Of all the places Kingler could have squeezed, why did it have to choose Psyduck's head...?

Ash truly had let his success go to his head...

Had he still not remembered that the worse Psyduck's headache became, the more overwhelming its power would grow?

Sure enough, once the pain became intolerable, faint blue light suddenly shone around Psyduck's body.

Only then did Ash belatedly realize what was happening. As unpleasant memories surfaced, his expression changed dramatically, and he hurriedly ordered Kingler to release its grip.

But it was already far too late to do anything.

In an instant, a shock wave of psychic force erupted with Psyduck at its center. Before Kingler could react, the blast engulfed it and sent it hurtling away!

Kingler continued flying backward until it crashed into Ash.

The impact sent Ash and Kingler tumbling together. When Ash finally climbed to his feet and looked down, he found spirals in Kingler's eyes. It had fainted outright!

The referee raised a colored flag and announced the result. The spectators stared in shock. They had never imagined that the Psyduck which had just embarrassed itself could possess such tremendous power!

Had it been concealing its strength all along?

In the waiting room, Ethan also looked slightly surprised to see that Misty had won.

To be honest, before the match began, he had believed Ash would ultimately emerge victorious, no matter how many twists and turns the battle took. Even after Psyduck entered the field, he still thought Ash would have the last laugh.

He truly had not expected Misty to win!

Psyduck's ability was simply too overpowered. By coming out without permission, it completely changed the course of the match and accidentally handed Misty the victory.

After losing to Misty, Ash looked utterly dejected and deeply frustrated. He formed a stark contrast with Misty, who clutched Psyduck and bounced around in excitement.

Ethan looked away from the screen and rose to his feet. Leaving the two of them behind, he prepared for his own match.

His battle would begin immediately after Ash and Misty's. Although he trusted that his Pokemon would never lose, he did not dare become careless.

Fortunately, because the permitted team size had changed, he had arranged last night for his enormous red Gyarados to be transferred from Cerise Park.

With that great terror in reserve, Ethan would dare battle even Lugia or Kyogre using Gyarados alone! In a local cup tournament like this, it could practically crush entire teams singlehandedly!

As he walked through the competitors' passage, Ethan recalled his video call with Chloe the previous evening, when he had asked her to return Gyarados.

When Chloe learned that he might use Gyarados in the tournament, her mouth dropped open in shock!

The enormous red Gyarados projected an overwhelming presence inside Cerise Park. Even when it lay motionless in the water, its intimidating aura kept Professor Cerise and the others from daring to take a single step closer!

Even the two assistants, Ren and Parker, could feel its predatory gaze when they watched from far away!

Of everyone at the laboratory, only Chloe firmly believed that none of Ethan's Pokemon would hurt her. That faith allowed her to approach Gyarados without fear.

Even without seeing it fight in person, the dominating presence Gyarados radiated was enough for Chloe to imagine how savage it would become in battle.

Thus, while shocked that Ethan was taking Gyarados back, she also observed a moment of silence for his opponents.

Of course, as far as Ethan was concerned, Blastoise alone was sufficient for ordinary circumstances. Gyarados probably would not need to appear anytime soon.

When Ethan entered the colosseum, the audience greeted him with enormous cheers. Even Maya in the ceremonial seating focused her gaze on him.

His swift, decisive victories over the previous two days had already won over a large group of spectators, so quite a few people now supported him.

Ethan could not tell whether it was his imagination, but there seemed to be more security around Maya today. Officer Jenny stood beside her, and more than a dozen guards surrounded the area.

Had something happened?

Before he could think further, Ethan reached the Trainer's command area and stood across the water from his opponent.

This time, his opponent was an elderly man with white hair. He leaned on a cane and could not even straighten his back.

But a sharp light gleamed in the old man's eyes. He was clearly no simple opponent.

Once both Trainers were ready, the referee gave the signal, and they released their Pokemon simultaneously. Ethan sent out the same Blastoise as before, while the old man chose a Tentacruel.

From the recordings he had reviewed, Ethan knew that this Tentacruel was probably the strongest Pokemon the old man had revealed.

Sending it out in the opening round suggested that he intended to seize the momentum and defeat Blastoise immediately.

The strategy was undoubtedly correct. The old man had clearly watched Ethan's previous matches and studied Blastoise's characteristics. Once the battle began, Tentacruel met Blastoise attack for attack, and for a time they fought on equal footing!

But the struggle was ultimately futile. Although Tentacruel caused Ethan some difficulty, Blastoise was still stronger. Under Ethan's direction, it found an opening and finished the opponent with a single decisive strike!
