Chapter 388: Battling Muk

A gentle breeze blew beneath the pleasant sunlight.

On one of the Pokemon Ninjutsu Academy's battlefields, Ethan and Aya stood on opposite sides and watched each other.

Seeing that he was ready, Aya asked evenly, "How do you want to battle? One round to decide the winner, or an all-out match until only one Pokemon remains?"

Although she had proposed this battle, she did not want anyone to believe she was forcing Ethan to accept her terms. In her view, allowing him to decide the rules would make the contest appear fairer.

Instead, Ethan tossed the question straight back to her.

"Anything is fine with me. You can decide the rules."

Tch!

He's really infuriating!

Does he think victory over me is guaranteed!?

Aya gave a cold snort at Ethan's relaxed demeanor. She had already resolved to defeat this man. Now, seeing how little concern he showed, she silently swore to fight with everything she had and make him witness the results of her intense training at the ninjutsu academy.

I won't let you defeat me so easily like you did the last two times! And this time, there won't be any bothersome Samurai or Jigglypuff to interfere with us!

With that thought, she took a deep breath and suppressed her impatience before speaking.

"Then we'll have a three-on-three battle. Both sides will send out their Pokemon simultaneously, and whoever has the last Pokemon standing wins!"

"Fine with me."

Ethan shrugged without any objection.

He was not actually looking down on Aya. To avoid defeat and finally free himself from her constant challenges, he had already decided to use his main team. With those Pokemon entering the battle, Ethan did not believe he could lose.

Because both participants held special status, the headmaster naturally served as referee.

Once both Trainers had taken out their first Poke Balls, he swept his arm downward.

"The first battlebegin!"

As the headmaster's final syllable sounded, Ethan and Aya threw their Poke Balls simultaneously. Two flashes of blue light revealed one large Pokemon and one of medium size on the field.

Aya had sent out a Muk. The instant it appeared, everyone around the battlefield smelled a sharp, revolting odor.

It reeked like an ancient sewer that had never been maintained, making people gag with nausea.

Muk's opponent hovered in midair on beating wings, a dragon-like Pokemon whose eyes blazed with fighting spirit.

It was, of course, Ethan's Charizard.

Muk was a pure Poison-type Pokemon, and Ethan was not surprised that Aya had chosen it.

As the younger sister of the Poison-type Gym Leader Koga, Aya was also an expert with Poison-type Pokemon. Kanto and Johto had only so many members of that type, so Ethan could roughly predict which ones she might send out.

Perhaps eager to defeat Ethan, Aya ordered an attack the moment both Pokemon appeared.

"Muk, use Sludge Bomb!"

Muk pulled a mass of unknown sludge from its body and hurled it at Charizard in the air. But how could such an attack hit a flying Pokemon?

Charizard merely climbed a little higher and easily avoided the sludge.

The projectile traced an arc through the air before exploding. Liquid splattered in every direction but failed to touch Charizard.

Aya did not seem concerned that the attack had missed.

Hmph. It was only a probing attack. I'd be truly surprised if Sludge Bomb could defeat Charizard!

Without waiting for Charizard to stabilize in the air, Ethan called, "Our turn to counterattack. Charizard, use Flamethrower!"

At Ethan's command, Charizard dove above Muk and opened its mouth. In the next instant, a blaze of searing fire erupted from it.

Flamethrower spread toward Muk like a vast net.

Aya's eyes sharpened. She knew that Muk's sluggish speed made dodging the flames impossible, so she chose another method without hesitation.

"Quick, use Acid Armor!"

Just as Flamethrower was about to engulf Muk, its body seemed to melt into a puddle of sludge and change shape. It abruptly sank lower and flowed rapidly to one side.

Charizard's Flamethrower had been aimed at Muk's body. Although the wide flames still burned part of it after the sudden transformation, the result fell far short of what Ethan had expected.

At least to the naked eye, Muk appeared unharmed once the flames vanished.

Acid Armor...

Ethan rubbed his chin.

To be honest, he rarely used Acid Armor and did not know much about it. He only knew that the move temporarily increased Defense and was generally used by soft-bodied Pokemon.

Now it seemed that Muk's already impressive defenses, strengthened further by Acid Armor, had allowed it to withstand Flamethrower without suffering any effective damage.

Aya had indeed improved greatly. No wonder she possessed such confidence.

However...

She was not the only one who had grown!

"Charizard, use Fire Spin!"

Charizard gathered energy once more. This time, because the move worked differently, Muk would be surrounded by continuous flames if it failed to dodge and tried to defend itself with Acid Armor again.

Aya naturally recognized Ethan's intention.

Muk was formidable in battles on the ground, but its lack of effective movement options also made it a living target.

Her only choice was to have Muk release Smog in an attempt to cancel out Fire Spin.

Muk opened its mouth. A stream of murky, foul-smelling gas surged toward the swirling flames.

When the two moves collided, some unknown substance within the gas caused Fire Spin's flames to ignite the entire cloud of Smog.

Like a dust explosion, more than half the battlefield erupted with a boom, only for the fire to vanish again almost immediately.

Seeing the result, Aya glanced at Ethan provocatively and laughed.

Well? I'm not the same person I was before!

Ethan looked at her without concern.

He had not expected the flames to ignite the gas and cancel Charizard's attack, but carelessness on the battlefield was unacceptable.

At that very moment, while Aya was still celebrating, Charizard had already prepared its next attack.

"Charizard, use Air Slash!"

Aya jolted in surprise at Ethan's command.

But in the time it took her to blink, two pale, moonlit blades of compressed air streaked across the field and slashed viciously into Muk!
