Chapter 497: Charla

Liza gave a loud whistle. As it echoed through the mountains, a Charizard flew toward them from afar.

It circled overhead several times, then dove and landed neatly beside her.

Liza patted its shoulder and smiled at her visitors. "This is my Charizard, Charla. Charla, say hello!"

Charla politely nodded to Ethan and Erika.

Ethan looked up at the Charizard. A pink bow sat on its head, and its name made it clear that it was female.

A small balloon and gondola were tied behind her by long ropes. The gondola was full of groceries, vegetables, and other everyday supplies. Liza must have just returned from shopping in town when she met them.

Still...

The sight reminded Ethan of a Charizard far across the sea in Alola that spent its days delivering milk from morning to night.

Different regions, but the same lot in life...

Liza climbed onto Charla's back and looked over her shoulder. "Come on, follow me! Would you rather ride in my gondola or on your own Charizard?"

Ethan glanced at the gondola.

Groceries already filled half of it. If both he and Erika climbed in, they would be cramped.

Besides, he had a Pokemon of his own. There was no reason to squeeze in among the supplies.

"Thanks, but I'll ride my Charizard."

Ethan recalled the Pokemon he had brought out for the hike, then took Charizard's Poke Ball from his waist pouch and released it.

Blue light flashed, and his Charizard appeared before Liza.

She examined it closely from head to tail, then nodded.

"You've raised this Charizard well. It's large too. It can hold its own beside an average wild Charizard from the valley!"

Comparing a Trainer's carefully raised, battle-tested Pokemon to a wild one might sound strange, but Ethan knew the Charizard of this valley were all exceptionally strong. They could not be judged by ordinary standards.

After Erika recalled her Leafeon, Ethan helped her onto Charizard's back. He then sprang up and took the seat in front of her.

Liza's eyes brightened at the sight.

A Charizard allowing its own Trainer to ride was nothing unusual. Allowing someone else to ride along was much rarer!

Given how proud Charizard tended to be, that was no small thing.

"Your Charizard trusts and obeys you completely," she said admiringly. "That's impressive!"

Something seemed to occur to her, and she chuckled. "Another Trainer came here before you. His Charizard wasn't nearly so obedient."

Ethan glanced at her. "You mean Ash, don't you?"

Liza was surprised that he knew the name. "Oh? You know him?"

"Of course. We're good friends, and we saw each other just a few days ago!" Ethan nudged Charizard gently with his feet. It took to the air at once.

The two Charizard climbed together. From high above, Ethan could finally see most of the broad mountain range spread out beneath them.

It really is vast.

Liza guided Charla alongside Ethan.

"Charicific Valley is up ahead, but we'll still have to fly for a while," she called. "How about a race?"

The wind forced her to raise her voice so Ethan could hear.

"A race?"

Liza nodded. "The terrain here is tricky. Let's see whether your Charizard can keep up with Charla!"

So that was it. Liza wanted to test him.

She was Clair's friend and had heard Clair praise him. Naturally, she wanted to see for herself how good his Charizard was.

"All right."

At his agreement, Liza's expression sharpened. "Then stay close!"

At her signal, Charla shot forward like an arrow from a bow.

She really is an impatient one...

Ethan's Charizard had heard their exchange and was eager to try. At his command, it hurried after Charla!

One behind the other, the two Pokemon dove between the mountains. The wind at that speed whipped Erika's hair about. She had to close her eyes and wrap both arms tightly around Ethan's waist to keep from being thrown backward.

Her body pressed against his back, and Ethan could feel her curves all too clearly!

She was holding him very tightly.

But this was no time to think about that!

Charla knew the terrain. She flew without hesitation, sometimes clearing a boulder and immediately making a sharp ninety-degree turn.

Each time, Ethan's Charizard had to slow down and steer carefully around the bend before catching up again.

They flew past stone bridges, skimmed over a river, and passed through a rocky tunnel. For all Charla's speed and effort, she could not shake her pursuer.

Liza looked back at Ethan, privately impressed.

He's hard to lose!

No wonder Clair, a Gym Leader from the dragon clan, had spoken so highly of him!

As the two Charizard cleared the mountains, the view suddenly opened. Charla began to slow.

In that instant, Ethan's Charizard accelerated and drew level with her again.

"Not bad!" Liza called. "Keeping pace with Charla proves your Charizard is well trained!"

She turned to face forward. "Here we are. This is Charicific Valley."

Ethan and Erika looked ahead. A broad, clear river flowed between the mountains below them. High cliffs rose on both sides, forming a vast valley.

Dozens of enormous Charizard statues carved from stone stood in the river!

They stared ahead with wings spread wide, solemn and imposing, as though judging the challengers bold enough to come here.
