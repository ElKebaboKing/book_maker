Chapter 447: Why Doesn't Hypnosis Work?

Trinity recalled Gyarados with a bloodless face. The Pokemon looked utterly miserable, most of its body stained crimson. It was clearly incapable of continuing the battle.

Besides, could that really be called a battle?

At least in Trinity's entire life, she had never encountered anything like it.

To the side, the referee raised his flag and announced that Gyarados was unable to battle.

This was different from Ethan voluntarily recalling Blastoise. Blastoise had not lost. It still possessed the strength to fight and could return to the field whenever Ethan chose. Gyarados, however, truly could no longer continue.

It had not yet fallen unconscious, but the missing scales exposed large patches of bloody flesh. If the battle continued, it might die in the arena. The referee could never stand by and allow that. Such an outcome would be a major competition accident, and besides the storm of public criticism, he would be held responsible as well.

In her command area, Trinity also knew how dire Gyarados's condition was. She raised no objection to the referee's decision.

She drew a deep breath and looked up at the red Gyarados once more, meeting its blood-red eyes again!

After witnessing the last battle, some of her fear had eased. She still could not face the monster with her usual composure, but she was no longer as terrified as before.

However...

This Gyarados was far too powerful. How could she possibly break through the present situation?

Trinity hesitated.

She had intended to send Chinchou back into the match. Its Electric-type moves would inflict quadruple damage upon a Water- and Flying-type Gyarados, so it should have a chance to defeat the red Gyarados!

But then she remembered that Chinchou had lost even the will to fight in its presence and had been unable to move at all. Helpless, she abandoned the idea.

Forget it. I'd better give Chinchou more time to recover. I can only keep it as my final trump card.

With complicated emotions written across her face, Trinity took out her third Poke Ball.

She threw it, and after blue light flashed, a Poliwrath appeared before the crowd.

The surrounding spectators looked at one another in confusion.

They could not understand Trinity's choice for her third Pokemon.

If even Gyarados was no match for Ethan's red Gyarados, what use could this Poliwrath possibly be?

What they did not know was that Trinity had no desire to make this choice either. She understood that Poliwrath could never defeat the red Gyarados, but it was her last remaining Water-type Pokemon. At this point, she had no choice but to risk everything and hope Poliwrath could delay the battle long enough for Chinchou to recover its courage!

On the opposite side, Ethan also chose not to change Pokemon.

With Chinchou no longer on the field, Blastoise could have returned. But now that the red Gyarados had already revealed itself, there was no need to hide it any longer.

Let's end the battle like this!

When the match resumed, Trinity adopted a different strategy.

She would maintain the greatest distance possible!

At her command, Poliwrath immediately climbed to the top of a towering stone pillar and attacked the red Gyarados from above.

A Water Gun burst from the spiral on its belly. The stream traced a slight curve through the air and struck the red Gyarados squarely on its enormous head.

But the attack seemed no more than a tickle. The red Gyarados showed no reaction whatsoever. It did not even narrow its eyes!

The red Gyarados swam toward Poliwrath's stone pillar, sending circles of ripples across the water.

The response appeared to reveal something to Trinity.

Hmm? It isn't counterattacking from a distance?

Her eyes brightened. She could not help wondering whether the red Gyarados had sacrificed its ranged moves in exchange for its extraordinary strength.

After all, Poliwrath was the evolved form of Poliwhirl, and every one of its abilities was considerably stronger. A Water Gun from Poliwrath should never have been so weak that another Pokemon could simply ignore it.

The red Gyarados's indifference was therefore highly suspicious!

Its performance against the other Gyarados also seemed to support the theory, giving Trinity a tremendous boost in confidence.

Within a few breaths, the red Gyarados arrived before the pillar.

It gave the structure a light bump, and the entire stone pillar shattered. The instant it began to collapse, Poliwrath took a running step, leaped high, and sprang toward the nearest pillar.

Poliwrath was modeled after the frogs of Earth, and its movement now demonstrated that resemblance perfectly.

The pillars stood five or six meters apart, but Poliwrath crossed the gap with ease. The instant it found its footing, it spun around and immediately fired Hypnosis at the red Gyarados!

The waves of light representing Hypnosis raced across the red Gyarados and struck it squarely on the head. Yet just as with Water Gun, the red Gyarados did not react at all!

What!?

This time, Trinity was not the only person stunned. Even the announcer sounded astonished!

"How is this possible!? Why doesn't Hypnosis affect the red Gyarados!? Is it holding some kind of item?"

The stands erupted into discussion. Ash was equally confused. He looked toward his companions and asked, "That's strange. Why isn't Ethan's Gyarados affected by Hypnosis?"

Brock rubbed his chin as possibility after possibility flashed through his mind. Finally, he offered his answer.

"Maybe it has something to do with the red Gyarados's emotions."

Ash and Misty immediately turned toward him.

"Its emotions?"

"That's right." Brock nodded and continued, "You both know that Hypnosis is a Psychic-type move. It affects the brain and forces whoever it hits into a sleeping state. But Pokemon like Gyarados are usually filled with rage, and that powerful emotion affects their minds. It can easily counteract the effect of Hypnosis!"

The explanation seemed quite reasonable. At least Ash and Misty both believed that had to be the cause.

But was simple anger truly the only reason?

Compared with an ordinary Gyarados, Ethan's red Gyarados obeyed his commands far more readily. It displayed no sign of having lost its reason at all!

At the side, Erika pondered for a moment before joining the conversation. "That isn't entirely accurate. Plenty of examples show that Hypnosis can still affect ordinary Gyarados and put them to sleep. If it failed now, the real reason is probably that this red Gyarados is so unusual!"

Because the red Gyarados is unusual?

Everyone focused on it again. Just as Erika had said, no matter how they looked at this Pokemon, it simply could not be judged by common sense...

No matter what happened around it, nothing seemed particularly strange anymore...
