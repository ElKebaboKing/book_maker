Chapter 867: Entei Again!

The two Charizard grappled on the ground, surged into the sky, and crashed back down. They smashed several Charizard statues and wrecked most of the rocks and trees on the platform. Even the watching Charizard retreated to avoid being caught in the fight.

It had become that fierce.

The opponents were tangled together and moving too quickly for anyone to tell which was which, let alone who had the advantage. However long the struggle continued, it would eventually have a winner.

One Charizard suddenly whipped its tail around. With a sharp crack, the other flew backward and struck a boulder.

Before the fallen Charizard could rise, its opponent swooped down and planted a foot hard on its stomach.

Raargh!

The Charizard on the ground cried out. It could endure no more. Its eyes rolled back, and it lost consciousness.

ROAR!

The standing Charizard threw back its head and bellowed toward the sky, announcing its victory to the whole valley. It looked every bit a conqueror.

Only now could the spectators see who had won. As Ethan had hoped, it was his Charizard.

Yes! It won! May cheered. Gengar let out a long whistle.

Both crowded around Ethan with smiling faces and congratulations. He nodded to them, then looked at Charizard.

That wasn't easy.

Fresh wounds stood out on its body. It had worked hard for this victory. Yet without a Trainer directing the fight, it had defeated the valley's strongest Charizard by its own strength. Ethan could see how far it had grown.

Well done. He met Charizard's eyes and smiled with pride.

At once the wild Charizard around them raised their heads and roared in chorus. Their cries seemed both a celebration and a proclamation that a new king had been born. The sound echoed throughout the Charicific Valley.

In the crowd, Ash's Charizard cast one envious look at Ethan's. It clenched its claws, turned with renewed determination, and walked away.

It was proud and ambitious too. How could it be content to watch another Charizard reach the top first? There was nothing it could do about today's result, but it could turn its envy and frustration into a reason to train harder.

After accepting the crowd's cheers, Ethan's Charizard walked over and stopped before its Trainer. May hurriedly took a healing spray from her bag and treated its wounds.

Neither Ethan nor Charizard cared much about ruling the valley. Charizard was not going to stay. But defeating the valley's strongest had been the proof it needed that it was ready to leave.

With its departure, Ethan would gain another exceptionally powerful partner alongside his red Gyarados.

Liza approached, applauding. Congratulations! You really did it!

As the valley's caretaker, she had watched Ethan's Charizard grow throughout its time here. She was delighted by the result.

I suppose you'll be off on new adventures together. Heretake this as a gift to celebrate the win. She drew a blue-green stone from her clothes and offered it to Ethan.

He had never seen this particular stone in person, but recognized it immediately. It was a Mega Stone: Charizardite X. He had not expected Liza to own one, much less give it to him.

When he did not reach for it, she smiled. Take it. I happened to find it while traveling in another region. I hear it lets a Charizard evolve further into a new form.

Ethan raised his eyebrows. It's so valuable. Why don't you keep it?

I'm not trying to become the strongest Trainer. And living out here, I have no use for it. It will do far more good in your hands than mine.

Then thank you. Ethan accepted the Charizardite X.

Earlier, in the Dragon Holy Land, he had received Charizardite Y from Clair's Dragon Tamers. Now, unexpectedly, he owned both of Charizard's Mega Stones.

He could choose between its X and Y forms whenever the time came. Whichever form he wanted, he would have the stone for it. That was a thrilling thought.

Thinking of transformation reminded Ethan of the strange feeling he had experienced during the battle.

Could it have been a sign of something like the Bond Phenomenon?

If so, perhaps his Charizard might gain a third form, one belonging to Ethan alonean Ethan Charizard alongside Mega Charizard X and Y. He found himself looking forward to the possibility.

He would have to study that feeling when he had time. If a bond transformation was possible, he wanted to discover how to bring it about.

Now it was time to say goodbye.

Despite its long stay, Charizard had made no friends in the valley. It had devoted itself entirely to training. Nothing held it here, and there was no one it needed to visit before leaving.

Only Charla watched its departure with obvious reluctance. Her pleading looks went unanswered by Ethan's Charizard.

It cared more about growing stronger than romance, much like its Trainer. It could not read Charla's feelings at all.

Well, a heart free of distractions made for a faster blade. On the other hand, if it had noticed her interest at any point during its long stay, there might have been a whole brood of little Charmander by now.

Ethan thanked Liza again for looking after Charizard. He and May waved goodbye, climbed onto Charizard's back, and flew away.

...

Charizard's trial was over, and it had returned to Ethan's team. The change made his roster much stronger.

He had little reason to send it into an ordinary Gym match or a tournament round against lesser opponents. In the wild, though, when he faced Legendary Pokemon or a dangerous organization, he now had another powerful card to play.

Ethan expected to save that card for some major battle in the future. Instead, he found a suitable opportunity soon after leaving the Charicific Valley.

Their next destination was Ecruteak City. Ethan wanted to take his Eevee back to visit the Kimono Sisters and talk with them about how it might evolve.

Before that, he also meant to visit the Whirl Islands. Sea Priestess Maya was there, and he had shared an intimate night with her. Now that he was back in Johto and not too far away, failing to see her would feel callous.

He could swear he was not going merely because he wanted her body. All right, that was part of itbut only a little. More than anything, he did not want to be a man who forgot someone after what they had shared. He owed her consideration.

Charizard carried them over the mountains and soon beyond the valley's borders. They flew west for most of the day.

As they neared Goldenrod City, Charizard sensed something unusual in a dry valley below. It slowed suddenly and dropped lower. Ethan looked around, puzzled, until Charizard shifted a wing to direct his attention to one spot.

There he saw a familiar shape.

It was a large, four-legged Pokemon more than two meters tall, with orange-red fur and a powerful body. Red, six-pointed markings spread over its cheeks, a golden three-lobed crest rose from its brow, and a pale trail of smoke drifted behind its back.

What's that Pokemon? May asked. It looks so cool!

Cool? Ethan gave a sharp snort. Of course it does. That's Entei, one of Johto's famous Legendary Pokemon.

There was no mistake. The figure Charizard had spotted was Entei.

It lay resting beside a hot spring in the valley, unaware that anyone had found it. Ethan could not let this chance pass.

He did not yet know whether this was the same Entei he had met before. But he had Raikou and Suicune, and missing the last of the three Legendary beasts had long bothered him. This time he would not let anythingor anyoneinterrupt his attempt.

Behind him, May stared in shock. That's Entei? She had never expected to come across a Legendary Pokemon here. She reached for her Pokedex to learn more.

Before she could use it, Charizard abruptly dived straight toward Entei.

Is Ethan going to catch it? May wondered. He seemed to have made up his mind as soon as he saw it. He had not even said a word, yet Charizard was already carrying them down.

Did they understand each other that well? Or had Ethan clashed with Entei before? The way he had recognized it immediatelyand the edge in his voicemade May suspect the latter.

Below them, Entei's eyes snapped open. It looked up and saw Charizard dropping from the sky. It stood at once, gathering itself to face the intruder.

Then its gaze shifted to the humans riding on Charizard's back. Recognition struck. Without another moment's hesitation, Entei turned and ran.

That told Ethan everything. It really is you!

Another Entei might have stood its ground when someone entered its valley. This one had fought Ethan before and lost. It knew what he could do, and its immediate flight had given it away.

It looked nothing like the majestic Entei of legend. Trainers who had grown up admiring tales of it would be stunned to see it fleeing like this.

But escape would not be so easy. Charizard landed, and Ethan slid from its back. He helped May down, then pointed after Entei. Charizard shot away like an arrow.

It had taken part in the last battle against this Entei. Loyal to Ethan, it understood what finding the beast again meant. That was why it had recognized the familiar presence during the flight and descended on its own.

Trainer and Pokemon wanted the same thing: having found Entei, they would not let it slip away.

Charizard accelerated and soon closed the gap. Entei weaved between great rocks and through the valley's uneven terrain, trying to shake its pursuer. Charizard had too much experience to fall for such simple evasions.

From above, it could see farther and fly a more direct course. No matter how sharply Entei turned, the distance between them continued to shrink.

Perhaps Entei realized it could not get away. As if preparing to leap forward again, it suddenly twisted back and lunged at Charizard with its jaws open.

Charizard had been ready for a counterattack. The moment Entei turned, it folded its wings and dropped lower, slipping easily beneath the bite.
