Chapter 499: The Strongest Charizard

After Ethan talked it out of challenging the strongest Charizard, his Charizard chose another opponent.

This one looked unremarkable. It was about the same size as Ethan's Charizard, did not stand out among the others, and was not as muscular as some. It seemed fairly young.

Ethan's Charizard crooked a finger at it. That small provocation was enough. The wild Charizard grew restless and strode toward its challenger.

Perhaps they had seen this many times, or perhaps it was simply the valley's custom. Either way, the other wild Charizard moved aside to leave an open space for the fight.

Ethan stepped back to stand with Erika and Liza.

As he had promised, he would not interfere. He intended to watch and leave the battle entirely to his Charizard.

The two Charizard faced one another, their eyes seeming to burn with fire.

A breeze happened to stir the leaves. The instant one green leaf fluttered to the ground, both Charizard lunged.

Orange bodies blurred together. They clasped each other's claws and pushed, the muscles in their arms swelling visibly.

With the same type and moves, sheer strength would decide which was better!

Both faces twisted with effort.

Yet their strength seemed evenly matched. They strained against each other for a long time without either gaining ground!

Refusing to give in, they changed tactics. Each grabbed the other's waist and tried to throw its opponent, but after another long struggle they were still locked in place.

They really are evenly matched...

While the Charizard tried every way they could to bring each other down, Ethan turned to Liza. "Where would this one rank among the Charizard here?"

Liza had said she managed the valley. She knew every Charizard that lived in it.

She thought for a moment. "There are about a hundred Charizard in Charicific Valley. This one ranks somewhere in the lower middle."

The lower middle...

Ethan looked back at the battle.

The two Charizard were rolling together now, bruised and filthy from the dust they had kicked up.

Neither had gained an advantage. Their equal showing gave Ethan a clearer idea of how strong the valley's wild Charizard were.

Becoming the strongest here would take a long time.

Tails lashed, teeth snapped, and claws pulled...

They fought for quite a while longer, but in the end they stopped at a draw.

Ethan's Charizard returned to him panting hard. Even as it caught its breath, it kept staring at its opponent. The two glared at each other, neither willing to admit defeat!

Liza seemed to notice Ethan's dissatisfaction and smiled. "That was a good result. When Ash's Charizard first arrived, it was beaten black and blue. It couldn't even beat my Charla."

Ethan's interest stirred. "And now?"

Liza shrugged. "It still can't beat the other Charizard, but at least it can hold its own against Charla now."

Ethan knew that Charla was different from the valley's wild Charizard. Liza had caught her before she evolved, and she was a Trainer's Pokemon. Here, she ranked near the bottom.

Ash's Charizard could only fight a female Charizard to a draw. No wonder it was training so fiercely by itself in the corner.

It was ashamed!

Nearby, Erika took a spray from the medical kit and quietly cleaned and disinfected the wounds on Ethan's Charizard.

Her thoughtfulness moved him.

No one had asked her to do it. Because she cared about him, she had thought of caring for his Pokemon too.

For the moment, the battle had taken too much out of Charizard. It would not have the strength to challenge another opponent until it recovered.

...

At noon, Liza invited Ethan and Erika into her home.

She had a wooden cabin in the valley. It looked plain from the outside, but inside it was warm and comfortable, with furniture and appliances. She had clearly made it her home.

Lunch was a thick seasonal soup made from seven or eight ingredients. Perhaps because it contained tomatoes, it was red and rather heavy-looking.

It might not have looked appealing, but it smelled wonderful and tasted unexpectedly good!

"Don't be shy. I made a whole pot, so eat as much as you like!"

The soup really was delicious. Ethan had several bowls.

Liza rarely had guests, and she was enthusiastic company. Over lunch she talked at length about life in the valley and listened with equal interest to Ethan's stories from his travels.

She paid close attention even to the smallest incident.

After the meal, while Erika and Liza cleared the dishes, Ethan hesitated, then said, "Miss Liza, there's something I'd like to ask you."

"I know." Liza turned toward him and smiled, as if she had already guessed. "You want to leave your Charizard here too, don't you?"

He had not expected her to know.

"Yes." Ethan nodded. "Now that I've seen this place, I realize there are far stronger Charizard out there and mine still has a lot to learn. I'd like to keep it with me, but I can't give its training all the time it needs. I thought it might grow stronger if it stayed here for a while."

Liza agreed without hesitation.

"Of course! This is a home for Charizard. I'm happy to welcome as many as want to stay."

...

Outside the cabin, Ethan called his Charizard aside and told it about the plan.

At the idea of leaving its Trainer, Charizard immediately shook its head and muttered at length. It clearly wanted to stay by Ethan's side.

"You fool!"

Ethan rapped it hard on the head and gave it a stern look. "I'm not leaving you here forever. Do you think we'll never see each other again? This is a rare opportunity. The Charizard here are strong. Work to beat them all as quickly as you can and become the strongest one in the valley!"

His words made Charizard think of the opponent it had just fought and the others that looked even stronger.

Its eyes grew resolute. It nodded firmly at Ethan, as if promising to meet his expectations!
