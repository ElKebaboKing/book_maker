Chapter 161: The Final

Bang! Bang!

On the day of the final, fireworks burst one after another above Indigo Stadium.

The smell of spent powder hung over the arena, adding to the intensity of the spectacle. The crowd cheered all the louder, swept up in the tournament's last battle.

Dozens of people in Pokemon mascot costumes emerged from either side, circled the field, and waved to the spectators. Sharp-eyed fans quickly recognized them: each mascot represented a Pokemon one of the two finalists had used in an earlier match.

Professor Cerise and Yoshino looked around from their seats. This was their first time experiencing the atmosphere of a League match in person.

"So this is what the Indigo League looks like from the stands," the Professor said. "It's lively!"

It was so noisy that Chloe had to cup her hands around her mouth, even though she was sitting right beside him.

"This is only the beginning! Wait until the battle starts. Then you'll see how excited this crowd can get!"

Thousands of balloons rose into the air, briefly blotting out the sky before disappearing into the distance. People exclaimed in delight.

Sabrina sat with her arms folded, as composed as a lady at a quiet gathering. She looked almost out of place. Someone who liked silence as much as she did was bound to find this frenzy uncomfortable.

Then again, Chloe might have felt the same way if the preliminary rounds had not given her time to grow used to the noise.

On the field, President Goodshow held a brief opening ceremony. Then the commentator's impassioned voice rang through the stadium.

"Ladies and gentlemen, the Indigo League final is about to begin! After round upon round of competition, only two outstanding trainers remain. Today we'll discover which of them is this year's very best!"

The crowd applauded. When the clapping died down, he continued.

"I'm not calling this match alone. We've invited a renowned trainer to share his expert insight with us. Please welcome our special guesta member of the Kanto and Johto Elite Four, Lance!"

For a moment the spectators were stunned. Then cheers erupted.

Lance rarely appeared in public, and nobody had expected him here. As a popular Champion and Elite Four member with a striking image and an upright, passionate nature, he had a great many admirers. His presence made the final feel even more exceptional.

"Hello, everyone. I'm Lance," he said. "It's an honor to be at Indigo Stadium to commentate on this match."

The host smiled. "With how busy you are, have you had a chance to follow this year's tournament?"

"Of course. I haven't seen every match, but I've followed several of the important ones. It's heartening, as a more experienced trainer, to see so many talented people competing here."

"Could you give us a quick assessment of the finalists?"

"Certainly." Lance thought for a moment. "Logan is a capable trainer. His Pokemon have performed well from the preliminaries onward. He's lost a few individual bouts, but there's no question that he belongs in this final."

The host nodded. "And what about Ethan Vale?"

Lance's answer surprised everyone.

"This isn't the first time I've met Ethan, so I know a little about him. He's a strong trainer, and his performance here hasn't surprised me at all. If I have the chance, I'd like to face him in a proper battle myself."

"Oh?"

The host looked startled, and murmurs spread through the stands.

Everyone knew that the League champion could formally challenge the Elite Four. Defeat all four members, and a trainer could enter the Hall of Fame, earn extraordinary honor, and have their name remembered for generations.

"I never expected such high praise from Lance," the host said.

Someone appeared in the tunnel on the right. At a cue from the director, the host changed subjects.

"And now, look to the right! Our finalists are entering the arena. First onto the field, representing the green cornerLogan!"

Logan walked out smiling and waved to the crowd. He received a generous round of applause.

Everything seemed ordinaryuntil Ethan emerged from the opposite tunnel.

"Please welcome the red corner's Ethan Vaaale!"

Applause and cheers far louder than before exploded through Indigo Stadium. Ethan barely had to appear for the atmosphere to reach its peak.

Logan saw it and sighed. They were both finalists. Why was the difference in popularity so enormous?

Ethan walked slowly to his trainer's platform and glanced at the commentary booth.

He had heard every word Lance said.

He had not expected Lance to come, much less to recognize him. When they had fought side by side before, Ethan had worn a mask. How did Lance know who he was?

Did being an investigator give Lance ways to discover the truth even when Ethan hid his identity? Or had Looker been careless enough to tell him?

The referee signaled, leaving Ethan no time to think it through. He focused on the field and raised a Poke Ball at the same time as Logan.

Ethan had seen Logan battle on New Island. Over the past few days, he had also studied recordings of his tournament matches. He had a fair idea of what Logan could do.

Blue light flashed. Two winged Pokemon appeared on the field.

"Oh!" the host said with a smile. "Both trainers have opened with Flying-type Pokemon. What a coincidence!"
