Chapter 306: Vicious

Flames burned and the ground shook. A fire dragon wheeled through the air while a water dragon roared below.

The battlefield was chaos. After so many hard-fought battles, though, Ethan had grown. He could divide his attention, spot openings amid the confusion, and give useful orders to several Pokemon in turn.

"Gyarados, bite Golbat's wing! Don't let it get away!"

"Good work, Jolteon! Keep using Discharge and scorch them all!"

"Crystal Steelix, yes! Charge straight through and crush that Weezing!"

Under Ethan's direction, his Pokemon steadily cleared out Team Rocket's forces.

While Ethan and Clair fought the grunts, Lance kept Vicious occupied and drew him farther from the main battle. The two fights drifted apart. Vicious must have understood the tactic and the disadvantage it left his people in, but he did not seem to care. That was his nature: he thought only of himself. To him, the Team Rocket members were expendable, there to be used up.

The battle around Ethan raged on. First a few of Team Rocket's Pokemon lost consciousness and fell from the sky. Then a dozen, and finally dozens more.

Ordinary grunts and their Pokemon were no match for Ethan and Clair. Within minutes, their forces were nearly spent.

Seeing that victory was close, Clair relaxed enough to look properly at Ethan's Pokemon. Before meeting him, she had never watched one of his battles. She knew only that he was the latest Indigo League tournament champion, and she had not had a clear sense of his strength.

Now she could see that his Pokemon were formidable. Judging by their performance, they were even stronger than hers. Lance had good reason to value this champion.

Her gaze rested on Crystal Steelix for a few seconds. She was about to remark on its beauty and imposing size when she noticed a small, snakelike Pokemon weaving nimbly along the edge of the battlefield.

"Oh? You have a Dratini too?"

As Dragonite's first form, Dratini was known to be even rarer than Dragonite itself. People generally encountered it only where Dragonite lived.

"Yes," Ethan said with a nod. "I caught it on Dragonite Island."

"Dragonite Island..."

Clair had heard of the island but had never had the chance to find it. She had not expected Ethan to have been there. She studied him for a long moment.

"You managed to find Dragonite Island. Perhaps the Elder was right. You really do have a strong connection to the path of dragons."

Ethan smiled but said nothing.

By now the grunts had lost all their Pokemon and stood defenseless. They still tried to resist with their capture nets, but humans alone could do little against Ethan's and Clair's Pokemon. They were subdued in short order.

Only when every Team Rocket member had been dealt with did Ethan finally let himself relax. During the fight, he had worried that ordinary grunts might also carry Dark Balls and steal his Pokemon. Evidently, they did not.

It made sense. A Dark Ball could capture a Pokemon and even take one from another Trainer. In that respect, it surpassed a Master Ball. Something so valuable had to be scarce, even for Vicious. Why would he hand them out to his subordinates?

Ethan picked up the capture nets scattered on the ground. He and Clair used them to bind the grunts together, then recalled their defeated Pokemon into Poke Balls.

Some distance away, Vicious heard his followers' cries. He shot Lance a sideways glance and sneered. "This is pointless. Don't think beating a few lowly grunts means you can beat me! Before my Dark Balls, every Pokemon will submit. All your efforts are wasted!"

The mechanical spider swung its huge claws and forced Dragonite back. Then Vicious pulled a black Poke Ball from inside his coat and aimed it at Lance's Pokemon.

Lance's eyes widened. "Dragonite, dodge!"

Black light burst from the Dark Ball. Dragonite was far away and tried to evade it, but the light still spread over its body.

"Dragonite!"

Lance snatched Dragonite's Poke Ball from his waist pouch and tried to recall it. The red recall beam struck the black light and was canceled out. Dragonite struggled with all its strength, but before Lance's horrified eyes, the black light drew it into the Dark Ball within seconds.

Holding the ball, Vicious laughed wildly.

"Ha ha ha! You knew I was coming, so you must know who I am. Then you should have heard about this Dark Ball. It makes any Pokemon it captures evil and draws out its power to the utmost! No Pokemon can break free of it. Now obey me. Come out, evil Dragonite!"

Black light flashed again. Dragonite appeared before Lance, its eyes glowing with darkness.

The instant it emerged, it opened its mouth and fired Hyper Beam directly at him. The attack came without warning. Even Lance had no time to prepare.

Hyper Beam crossed the distance in an instant. Lance could already feel its terrifying heat. Just as it was about to strike him, it stopped in midair as though it had hit an invisible wall.

Boom!

The energy exploded. Thick smoke rolled outward and flames spread in every direction, but Lance was unharmed. He lowered the arm he had raised to shield his face. Hearing movement behind him, he turned and saw that Sabrina had arrived just in time.

She descended beside him without even glancing his way. Her hair moved in the air, and she kept her cold gaze fixed on Vicious.

She could not travel as quickly as a Pokemon, so she had arrived later than the others. That delay had put her in position to save Lance at the crucial moment.

"Thank you..." Lance said. Then he looked back at Dragonite.

Only seconds had passed, yet the Pokemon felt utterly unfamiliar to him, as if its entire nature had changed.

"Dragonite! Come to your senses!"

At the sound of his voice, Dragonite shuddered. For a moment, a trace of resistance appeared in its eyes. Then the darkness swallowed it again.

"Oh?" Vicious had noticed even that fleeting response. He grinned cruelly. "You and Dragonite must have a deep bond. But it only listens to me now!"

Lance refused to give up. "Dragonite! Don't let that dark power consume you! Wake up!"

"Hmph. How stubborn. How many times do I have to tell you?"

Impatience flashed in Vicious's eyes. He waved a hand. "Dragonite, what are you waiting for? Finish them!"
