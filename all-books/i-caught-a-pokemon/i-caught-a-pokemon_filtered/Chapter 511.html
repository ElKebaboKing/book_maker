Chapter 511: I Didn't Lie to You

Apollo, Athena, and Lance backed toward the windows. It looked as though they meant to break the glass and escape through the air.

At Ethan's command, Dragonite, Jolteon, and Gardevoir closed on them at tremendous speed. Any Team Rocket Pokemon that tried to block their path was knocked down without mercy.

In mere moments, a number of Team Rocket's Pokemon had fallen before them.

Looker saw what Team Rocket was planning and shouted, "Don't think you'll get away!"

"Get away?" Athena snorted. "This is a tactical withdrawal. We've already done what we came here to do. There's no point staying any longer!"

As she spoke, she took out a controller and pressed it. With a tremendous bang, every pane of glass around the circular observation deck exploded at once!

Shards flew everywhere. The officers' Pokemon instinctively ducked their heads. By the time they looked up, the Team Rocket members had jumped from the tower, opened gliders on their backs, and were sailing away!

They had planned their escape route all along.

"Damn it! After them!" Looker's face changed. He had stationed officers around the TV station, but Team Rocket was clearly gliding farther away. Finding them again in a city this large would be like hunting for a needle in a haystack.

Ethan had kept his eyes on the three executives. As they prepared to leap from the tower, his gaze sharpened. Gardevoir, linked to him telepathically, caught his intention at once. She stretched out a hand and used Confusion, dragging Lance back just as he jumped through the broken window.

"What!?" Lance's face went pale. He tried to break free, but human strength was no match for psychic power. Within seconds, he was hauled back into the tower.

Several nearby officers sprang on him and pinned him down.

Before they had even finished, Ethan whistled and leaped through the shattered window. A yellow figure flashed beside him. Dragonite spread its wings and caught him on its broad back before he could begin to fall.

Gardevoir teleported onto Dragonite's back beside him. Ethan took out a Poke Ball and, while they were still close enough, turned to recall Jolteon.

The whole sequence flowed together and took only a few seconds.

Apollo and Athena had already glided some distance away. When they looked back and saw Lance captured, their expressions shifted.

Seeing Dragonite carry Ethan toward them made their mood heavier still.

The two exchanged a glance and nodded. They broke away from the main group and glided in another direction.

Team Rocket members dotted the sky like paper airplanes, but Ethan ignored the ordinary members. He kept Apollo and Athena in sight.

When they separated from the others, he patted Dragonite's neck, signaling it to change course.

The streets of Goldenrod City lay below. Pedestrians sensed something passing overhead and looked up. Faced with a sky full of gliders, they stared in surprise, thinking some extreme-sports club must be holding an event.

Apollo and Athena glided beyond the city and toward a freight train station in the suburbs. Losing altitude all the while, they finally landed there.

They removed their equipment and had barely found their footing when Dragonite descended in front of them. Ethan swung down and stood in their path.

Apollo narrowed his eyes at him. "You really don't know when to stop chasing us."

Athena folded her arms and snorted. "You came after us alone. Do you think you've already won?"

Ethan gave neither of them an answer. He took out his Poke Balls and released his Pokemon one after another.

Gardevoir, Dragonite, Jolteon, Tyranitar, Scizor, Gengar, and Crystal Steelix stood around him. All seven looked formidable.

Apollo and Athena surveyed them and seemed to abandon the thought of running. They took out their own Poke Balls and released their teams.

Ethan looked from one group to the other. Apollo had Houndoom, Golbat, Persian, Hypno, Tyranitar, and Wobbuffet.

Athena's team consisted of Sneasel, Muk, Arbok, Lickitung, Kabutops, and Vileplume.

In an instant, the Pokemon filled the open space. They watched their opponents closely. None made a sound, but every one of them was eager to fight.

Athena glanced over the teams and sneered. "It seems we have the advantage in numbers."

"Numbers don't decide everything," Ethan replied coolly. "Strength matters more. Besides, the International Police could arrive to reinforce me at any moment. Where are your reinforcements?"

Athena's smile froze. He was right: the International Police could come to his aid at any time. No one would be coming to help them.

"Don't be careless," Apollo warned her with a sidelong glance. "Remember, he still has the three Legendary birds."

"You don't need to worry about them." Ethan smiled and shook his head. "I left the three Legendary birds somewhere else."

What? That he would volunteer the fact that his strongest Pokemon were not with him astonished them both. Why would he leave such powerful Pokemon elsewhere?

Even so, they weighed his claim carefully. How could they know he was telling the truth?

If they believed him and lowered their guard, he might release all three birds at the height of the battle and catch them completely off guard.

Ethan saw their doubt but said nothing more.

Why was it so hard to get anyone to believe the truth these days?

Still...

His fingers brushed the two Great Balls in his belt pouch. The three Legendary birds were absent, but he had two Legendary beasts with him. They could serve as his trump cards just as well.

"Don't worry. I really didn't lie to you. The three Legendary birds aren't with me."

His earnest expression only left Apollo and Athena more baffled. They exchanged a glance. Time was short, so they decided to waste no more of it. They would defeat the man before them and leave.

And if they could finish him here, Team Rocket would be rid of one of its greatest threats at a stroke!
