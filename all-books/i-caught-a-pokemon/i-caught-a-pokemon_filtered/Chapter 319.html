Chapter 319: The Fourteenth Pokemon

Ethan stepped lightly toward Larvitar, closing the distance a little at a time. Careful as he was, once he came close enough, it noticed him.

At the sight of a human, Larvitar started and immediately ran.

Trying to escape? No chance!

Larvitar was one of the few Johto Pokemon Ethan especially liked. He had finally found one; he could hardly let it get away.

He snapped his fingers. After Larvitar had run a dozen meters or so, Charizard dropped from the sky and blocked its path.

Larvitar looked up at the much larger Pokemon and its unfriendly expression. Deciding Charizard was best avoided, it hurriedly changed direction and kept running.

It had gone only a few steps when Gengar rose from the shadow of a rock ahead, cackling.

Startled, Larvitar turned again, only to find Ethan in its way. It tried the last direction and came face to face with Sabrina. Surrounded on all four sides, it had nowhere left to run.

Ethan smiled. For some reason, the scene reminded him of a television program in which people cornered a wild boar in a watermelon field. Larvitar was the little boar this time. On a closer look, it even wore much the same expression.

Larvitar opened its mouth and made a threatening sound, apparently hoping to frighten them off. But at only sixty centimeters tall, it did not look threatening at all. Charizard, Gengar, and Sabrina paid it no mind.

"Calm down, Larvitar." Ethan crouched and spoke gently. "Don't be afraid. I'm no good person."

What?

Larvitar bared two rows of sharp teeth. Beside Ethan, Sabrina struggled not to laugh. That opening line sounded strangely familiar.

Ethan took out a Poke Ball and released his Dratini.

Now he truly sounded like a villain trying to tempt someone. "You have two choices. Battle my Pokemon. If you lose, you come with me from now on. If you win, I'll leave you some food to make up for bothering you, and I won't trouble you again. What do you say?"

Larvitar looked around. It could not escape. Then it looked at the rather cute Dratini in front of it and made a series of grunting sounds.

Ethan could not understand. He turned to Gengar. "Hey, Gengar, stop standing there and translate. What did it say?"

Gengar shrugged. "It said, 'In your dreams!'"

"Dreams?" Ethan rubbed his chin and gave Larvitar a sunny smile. "Sorry, that isn't one of the choices. You look rather lonely wandering around looking for food. Come travel with me and you'll have food and lodging covered. You can live in a comfortable Poke Ball, stay out of the wind and rain, and eat delicious meals until you're full every day. I guarantee your quality of life will improve!"

Larvitar was unmoved. If anything, it became still more wary.

Gengar clutched its belly and laughed at Ethan's failed pitch. Ethan rolled his eyes at it, cleared his throat, and stood up.

"All right, enough joking. If you won't choose, I'll take that as agreement to battle!"

He waved Dratini forward. "This capture battle is yours. Go on, Dratini. Show it what you can do!"

Dratini had been eager to begin and charged at once. Larvitar could hardly stand there and take the attack, so it fought back. The two Pokemon collided and were soon locked in a struggle.

Ethan watched Larvitar closely as they fought. He had no intention of giving Dratini further commands. Neither Pokemon had evolved yet, and the fight was unlikely to cause much damage; he could let them work it out for themselves.

After a while, he could see that Larvitar was still weak and knew no advanced moves. It used Bite and Tackle over and over, with an occasional Rock Throw. Even so, it was a little stronger than most wild Pokemon at its stage, or it would not have survived here alone.

Against Dratini, that strength was still no great threat. More importantly, Larvitar kept glancing at Charizard and Gengar while it fought, apparently afraid that they might abandon fair play and attack it from behind. With its attention divided, it could not fight at full strength and gradually fell behind.

After taking several of Dratini's attacks, Larvitar seemed ready to try breaking out again. It knocked Dratini back with a Tackle, then suddenly used Sandstorm.

Keeping that move hidden until now worked remarkably well. Yellow sand surged up without warning. Grit and small stones whirled around the people and Pokemon, briefly blinding them all.

Larvitar chose a direction and ran. But perhaps it made too much noise. Dratini quickly broke free of the sand, caught up, and used Wrap to pull Larvitar to the ground. Exhausted, Larvitar fainted.

"Well done!"

Ethan praised Dratini, then threw an empty Poke Ball at Larvitar. As red light enveloped the wild Pokemon, a white glow suddenly surrounded Dratini too.

Hm? That light... Is it evolving?

Dratini had been with him for a long time and fought many battles, large and small. It was certainly time.

The white light soon faded. A few seconds later, the Pokemon before Ethan was no longer Dratini, but Dragonair. Its body had grown longer and slimmer, covered in sapphire-blue scales. It had bright black eyes, a small white horn on its forehead, and two little winglike fins on either side of its head.

Delighted, Ethan took out his Pokedex again and pointed it at Dragonair.

"Beep! Dragonair, the Dragon Pokemon. A Dragon type. Like its unevolved form, Dratini, it lives in beautiful seas and lakes and is regarded as a sacred Pokemon. Though it has no wings, it is sometimes seen flying through the air. It can control the weather and ride the wind into the sky. A mysterious aura surrounds it, and it stores tremendous energy within its body. By concentrating that energy in the crystal-like orbs on its neck and tail, it can alter the weather."

Larvitar's Poke Ball flashed a few more times, then stopped with a click.

Good. He had caught it too. Today he had gained his fourteenth Pokemon, Larvitar, and Dratini had evolved into Dragonair. Two happy events at once, and another increase in his team's strength.

There was just one problem. The number of Pokemon he carried had reached the limit. Under the rules, he had to send one back immediately.

So which one should he transfer?
