Chapter 812: Ethan Has an Articuno Too?

There was no point keeping the secret now that Ethan had guessed it.

Yes, there's an Articuno here, Noland admitted. But it isn't exactly mine. We're good friends. He looked at the others. How did you know?

Ash quickly told him about the bird they had seen over the forest.

So that's what happened. Noland shook his head with a rueful smile. It sneaked out to play again. I wanted to surprise you when the battle began. Looks like that plan's gone.

Would they really have been that surprised? May glanced from side to side. Ash, Max, and Brock stood to her right; Ethan stood to her left. Every one of them had encountered Legendary Pokemon before.

Ethan had Celebi and Deoxys. Ash frequently met Legendary Pokemon too and sometimes befriended them. Compared with ordinary Trainers, their group had seen many rare Pokemon. Learning that an Articuno lived here might startle them briefly, but it was unlikely to leave them speechless.

Even so, having an Articuno willing to stay at the Factory was remarkable, whether Noland had caught it or not. Legendary Pokemon were rare even among Elite Four members, champions, Gym Leaders, and Frontier Brains.

Noland explained how the bird had come to be here. Not long ago, he had been flying one of his homemade aircraft at night when he encountered an injured Articuno. He helped it, and they became friends. Since then, Articuno had visited the Factory from time to time.

It sounded like a simple incident that might happen anywhere. Perhaps friendships built in such ordinary ways were the most genuine.

I see, Brock said, stroking his chin. Since stepping away from Gym work, he had begun thinking more and more like a Pokemon Breeder. He understood the bond Noland described. Then another thought occurred to him. If you haven't caught Articuno, does that mean it's still a wild Pokemon?

Sergio understood his concern. Don't worry. We've kept its presence strictly secret. We never show it to ordinary challengers. Today is the first time we've told visitors.

I can confirm that! Scott laughed. If you hadn't come, I might still have no idea it was here.

He did not sound offended. He was praising how well Noland had protected the secret.

We'll keep quiet too, Ethan said for the group. None of us will spread the news.

I trust you, Noland told Ethan with a broad smile. You're a champion, after all.

After a little more conversation, Noland led them to the Factory's battlefield. It occupied a vast, mostly empty workshop. Steel beams and pipes lined the space. The dim lights and metallic smell suited the name Battle Factory.

They had already settled on Articuno as Noland's Pokemon. He had no other partner that could match it, so each challenge would be a one-on-one battle. The first Pokemon to win would decide the match.

Beforehand, both Ethan and Ash used the Factory's transfer machine to adjust their teams.

This would be Ethan's first official battle against a Legendary Pokemon. Ash, too, looked unusually serious.

Ethan met Ash's eyes. This time he did not offer Ash the first match. He indicated that he would go ahead.

Ash did not mind. They were friends, and both would have a turn. He was curious which Pokemon Ethan would choose against Articuno.

Noland and Ethan took their places on opposite sides of the field. May, Ash, Max, Brock, and Scott went to the viewing area. Sergio stood beside the field as referee.

Noland gave a sharp whistle. A beautiful blue bird flew out of a side passage and circled overhead. Snowflakes drifted from its wings across the battlefield.

It was the Articuno they had seen above the forest. After several beats of its wings, it descended onto Noland's half of the field. May and Max finally had a close view of it.

It's beautiful, Max said, wide-eyed.

May's thoughts went to Pokemon Contests. Imagine using Articuno in a Contest. Everyone would be watching it...

She was thinking like a Coordinator even here. After admiring Articuno, the spectators all turned to Ethan. What would he send out against it?

Ethan did not release a Pokemon at once. He took three Ultra Balls from his pouch and held them in his palm.

Mr. Noland, he said with a smile, I'd like you to choose your opponent from these three.

Noland raised his brows. I've heard of a defender letting a challenger choose which Pokemon to face, but never the other way around. If you're offering me three choices, I assume all three are powerful?

You could say that. Ethan nodded lightly. They're not my strongest, but each is on the same level as your Articuno.

Noland's eyes narrowed. On the same level? Did Ethan mean strength, or something else? Were all three Ultra Balls holding Legendary Pokemon?

The possibility startled him. It sounded impossible. Then again, this was a man who had won championships in three regions. Who knew what partners he kept in reserve?

May, Max, and Scott had much the same thought. Only Ash and Brock exchanged a look of recognition.

So he's going to use one of those Pokemon against Articuno? Brock swallowed.

Looks that way. Ash frowned. I've never seen Brother Ethan use them in an official match.

What are you talking about? Max demanded. Does Mr. Vale have a Legendary Pokemon too?

Brock let out a breath and gave him a wry smile. Watch. You'll know soon enough.

Their evasive answers only made Max and Scott more impatient. May knew a little more about Ethan. Could he be planning to use Deoxys? But there were three Ultra Balls. What was in the other two?

Noland made his choice. He pointed to the ball at the far right of Ethan's hand. They all look alike. I'll trust my instincts and pick that one.

Ethan glanced down. A tiny snowflake was engraved on its surface. Of all three balls, Noland had chosen this one.

What a fateful choice.

Ethan put the other two away and tossed the selected Ultra Ball into the air. Blue light flashed. Another magnificent blue bird appeared above the field.

Everyone except Ash and Brock gasped. The feathers, the imposing body, the familiar shapeit was unmistakably an Articuno.

Ethan had one too?

Mr. Vale has an Articuno? Scott cried.

Snowflakes drifted down as Ethan's Articuno circled the workshop, then landed on his side. The two birds faced each other across the field.

The spectators looked back and forth, rubbed their eyes, and finally accepted what they were seeing. May, Max, and Scott were still astonished.

Max turned to Ash and Brock. You knew from the start that Mr. Vale had an Articuno?

That's not all, Brock said with a smile. You saw the three Ultra Balls. This one held Articuno. The other two...

The other two? Max's eyes widened.

Across the field, Noland asked the same question. I never expected you to have an Articuno of your own. What was in the other two balls?

Ethan smiled. I told you. They're on the same level as Articuno.

He had not named them, but in Kanto the answer was obvious.

So that's it, Noland said with a rueful nod. Incredible. You've brought all three together.

Max was impatient for confirmation. Do they mean...?

Yes, Brock said. The other two Legendary birds of Kanto: Moltres and Zapdos.

May, Max, and Scott looked at Ethan anew. They had a much clearer sense of the Pokemon he commanded and the strength behind his achievements.

On the field, the Articuno studied one another. Their fighting spirit rose. They were the same size and carried themselves with the same proud bearing. The only visible difference was the clear, glittering collar worn by one of them.

Sharing a species did not make them affectionate. Each recognized a formidable opponent in the other. Their strength, temperament, and Legendary pride all drove them to settle which was superior.

With both sides ready, Sergio raised his flags. The Battle Frontier match between Ethan Vale and Factory Head Noland begins now!

Gears turned overhead. The ceiling shuddered and slowly drew back, revealing the open sky.

As everyone looked up, Scott proudly explained, This is one of the Battle Factory's best features. For an aerial battle, the roof opens so both Pokemon have room to fly. For a water battle, the field can quickly fill and become a pool. Whatever Pokemon you bring, we can provide a suitable battlefield.

He had every reason to be proud. The Factory was his investment and part of his Battle Frontier. The challengers' amazement gave him immense satisfaction. It was one of the main reasons he had built the Frontier in the first place.
