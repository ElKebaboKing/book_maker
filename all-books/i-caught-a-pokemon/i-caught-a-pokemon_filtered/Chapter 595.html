Chapter 595: Team Magma, Then Team Aqua

The Team Aqua members wore bandanas and looked rather like pirates. Three were plainly ordinary grunts. From the way the fourth stood at the front and carried himself, Ethan guessed he was an officer.

He wore a bandana too, but it was clear that he was bald. His cheeks were gaunt, and his narrow eyes made him look thoroughly untrustworthy.

One of the grunts whispered to him, Sir Amber, what do we do?

Amber studied Ethan and gritted his teeth. Don't be afraid. There's only one of him. Beat him, and we can leave!

He reached for a Poke Ball. The others released their Pokemon too.

Several flashes of blue light filled the corridor. When they faded, lobster-like Pokemon stood before Ethan.

He recognized them at once: Crawdaunt, the Water- and Dark-type Rogue Pokemon. Just as Team Magma used Houndoom, Team Aqua issued Crawdaunt to its ordinary members.

Each stood more than a meter tall. Its shape suggested an American lobster from Earth. Most of its body was red, with two blue markings on its chest. Perhaps the similarity to Team Aqua's uniforms was why the organization had chosen it.

A yellow five-pointed star marked each Crawdaunt's forehead, and a huge pincer sat on either side of its body. They had fierce tempers, picked fights readily, and were notoriously difficult to raise.

They brandished their claws constantly, grabbing other Pokemon from their ponds and throwing them out. Any challenger approaching their territory was beaten without mercy. Few other creatures could live in a pond occupied by Crawdaunt.

Psychic attacks would not work on these Dark-type Pokemon. Seeing the aggressive Crawdaunt, Ethan took out another Poke Ball and released Scizor.

The Crawdaunt lifted their pincers. Across from them, Scizor raised its own. Both sides waited for their Trainers' commands.

They all had powerful claws, but even before experience and strength came into it, Scizor's Bug type gave it an advantage against Crawdaunt's Dark type.

The Crawdaunt had numbers on their side, at least. Amber, the officer, also sent out his own Pokemon.

It was a Pelipper.

Pelipper's beak was larger than the rest of its body. Like a pouch, it could hold things the Pokemon swallowed whole.

Pelipper built nests on seaside cliffs and protected their young inside their beaks. On calm days they floated on the water to rest. They flew low over the sea looking for food, dipping their great beaks into the water to scoop prey and seawater up together.

There was nothing wrong with Pelipper itself. But a flying Pokemon was a poor choice in the cramped space of an office building. It could barely spread its wings here, much less fly to a safe height.

Unless...

Just then, the Pokemon in the corridor moved together and charged.

Despite their advantage in numbers, the three Crawdaunt had no coordination. They crowded toward Scizor, blocking one another's paths and attacks.

Scizor moved like a silent warrior, using X-Scissor again and again. Its great red pincers traced crossing cuts through the air. By the time it passed the Crawdaunt, all three had fallen.

The Team Aqua grunts stared, stunned. Only now did they realize they had met a powerful opponent.

Only Amber's Pelipper remained between Scizor and them.

What are you waiting for? Amber snapped at the others. Use your other Pokemon!

Huh? Ohright!

The grunts fumbled for their second Poke Balls. While the next flashes of blue light appeared, Pelipper suddenly flew toward a window.

Amber sprinted after it, threw his arms around its body, and tried to crash through the glass with it.

So that had been his plan. The moment the battle went badly, he meant to escape amid the confusion.

He was abandoning his subordinates. The three grunts gaped as their officer fled.

Before they could react, Pelipper hit something invisible with a thud and came to a halt.

Beside Ethan, Gardevoir slowly lowered her arm.

She had used Confusion to block Pelipper's path. Ethan had guessed what Amber intended; he was not about to let him escape.

The failed attempt left Amber where he had begun. For Team Aqua's morale, though, it was a heavy blow.

The officer in charge had tried to leave them behind and save himself. How were they supposed to keep fighting?

One of the grunts stared at him in disbelief. Amber! Why would you do that?

You fools, Amber said with a weary sigh. Don't you recognize him? He just won the Silver Conference. How could we beat him here? If we all get captured, who will deliver the culture medium we stole to our leader?

Amber really had recognized Ethan. Faced with such a difficult opponent, he had weighed his options and chosen to flee.

His loyalty to Team Aqua was no less than anyone else's. Getting the stolen culture medium to its leader had been his first thought.

Still, however necessary he believed it was, leaving his own people behind was a betrayal that would chill them.

To Amber, the organization's great plan outweighed any one member. Given the choice again, he would do the same thing.

The grunts wanted to argue, but Ethan gave them no time.

At a wave of his hand, Scizor and Gardevoir advanced together. They knocked out Pelipper and brought the Team Aqua members under control.
