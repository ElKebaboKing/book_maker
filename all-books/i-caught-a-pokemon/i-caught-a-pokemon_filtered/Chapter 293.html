Chapter 293: Taking On the Ace

Jolteon's Electric type gave it an advantage against Staraptor's Flying type. It could deal extra damage with its Electric attacks, while taking only half damage from Flying-type moves. On offense and defense alike, the matchup favored Jolteon.

Falkner understood that perfectly well, but he did not let it trouble him. Since becoming Gym Leader, he had met plenty of challengers who studied his team beforehand. Most brought Pokemon strong against Flying types, expecting an easy Zephyr Badge. Many had miscalculated and received a harsh lesson from his well-trained partners.

What irritated him most was the claim that a Flying-type Pokemon was bound to lose to an Electric type. Nothing in the Pokemon world was that certain.

"Staraptor, Wing Attack!"

At Falkner's command, Staraptor soared upward and circled several times. Then it spread its wings and dived at Jolteon, moving fast enough to make the air whistle.

Ethan did not panic at an attack that brought Staraptor straight to him. "Jolteon, Discharge!"

Brilliant electricity burst from Jolteon's body in every direction. Staraptor could be as fast as it liked; to reach Jolteon, it would have to pass through the current.

A moment later, Staraptor did exactly that. Golden light crackled over its body as the electricity struck. It endured the pain, kept its speed, and swept a wing across Jolteon like a blade.

Thud! The two Pokemon passed each other. Both had taken damage, but Staraptor was plainly worse off.

Trading blows seemed unfavorable for Falkner, yet he had few alternatives. His confidence in Flying Pokemon could not change the fact that their attacks did half damage to an Electric type. He needed to close the distance and find another way to win.

At least the first exchange had brought Staraptor close enough. "Staraptor, Close Combat!"

It drove both wings at Jolteon like a flurry of fists. Ethan meant to order another Electric attack, but Staraptor was already upon them. Jolteon had no time to gather its power.

He changed tactics. "Don't let it beat you up close! Jolteon, Double Kick!"

Jolteon reared and struck with its front paws in quick succession, meeting Staraptor head-on.

Bam! Bam!

Wings and paws collided. Both Pokemon stumbled back a step, each hurt by the exchange. Staraptor, still suffering from the electric shock, had a little less strength left. It faltered before quickly regaining its balance.

That instant was enough for Ethan. "Jolteon, Thunder Fang!"

Electricity filled Jolteon's open jaws. It lunged and clamped its teeth onto one of Staraptor's wings. Staraptor shrieked in pain.

"Shake it off!" Falkner shouted.

Staraptor thrashed its wing, but Jolteon held on. Falkner frowned. "Damn it! Use Endeavor!"

Endeavor was a Normal-type physical move. Jolteon had no resistance to it, and Ethan would not simply watch it take the hit. He whistled. Before Staraptor could strike, Jolteon released the wing and sprang backward.

Staraptor's other wing swept through empty air. It had freed itself, but Jolteon had a mouthful of scorched feathers.

Staraptor panted. Its strength was running out. When it tried to lift the bitten wing, deep tooth marks showed where Jolteon had gripped it. The pain drew a miserable cry from the bird.

Falkner could see that Staraptor had been driven to the brink. He had to take a final risk.

"Staraptor, Brave Bird!"

Brave Bird? Ethan frowned. Among Flying-type moves, its power was second only to Sky Attack and matched Dragon Ascent. For many Flying Pokemon, it was a signature weapon. Falkner must be ready to stake everything on this attack.

Ethan suspected Staraptor had the Reckless Ability, which would increase Brave Bird's power further. But the move also hurt its user. In Staraptor's condition, the recoil might make it collapse even if it struck Jolteon. Falkner seemed willing to trade one Pokemon for the other to win this round.

Fighting through its pain, Staraptor beat its wings and climbed high above the court. A few loose feathers drifted down in the sunlight, giving it a tragic, resolute air.

After circling again, it folded its wings and plunged toward Jolteon with deadly precision.

"Jolteon, dodge!"

Jolteon sensed the danger and sprinted to escape before Staraptor reached the ground. Brave Bird was too fast. In the blink of an eye, Staraptor slammed into it.

Boom!

Jolteon flew seven or eight meters before hitting the ground. Its type resistance had halved the damage. Though it was breathing hard, it managed to stand.

Staraptor was less fortunate. The recoil from that collision had knocked it unconscious.

Seeing Staraptor motionless, the referee reluctantly raised a hand. "Staraptor is unable to battle! Jolteon wins the second round!"

Falkner's apprentices sighed softly, their shoulders slumping. Even his ace had fallen. Sabrina alone leaned back in her seat and gave a gentle round of applause.

Falkner recalled Staraptor, then looked at Ethan with a sigh. "Too bad. It was so close. If I'd worn Jolteon down a little more earlier, we might have knocked each other out."
