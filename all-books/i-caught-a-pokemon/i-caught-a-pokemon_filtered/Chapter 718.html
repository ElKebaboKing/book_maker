Chapter 718: Close Encounter of the Third Kind

Ethan and the others did not stay for the next match. They rose from their seats in the dark and left the arena through the passageway.

By the time they reached the competitors' side, they could see two people standing with Ash and Tory: a middle-aged man whose hair was the same color as Tory's, dressed in a neat, formal suit, and a pretty young woman.

As Ethan's group drew near, the man spoke to Tory with a note of excitement. Tory, you've finally decided to take part in a Pokemon battle?

Tory glanced at Ash and lowered his head. No... I only ended up there because of a misunderstanding!

He clenched his fists and ran away.

Ethan and the others reached Ash just as the man sighed and turned to leave. The young woman alone remained. She gave Ash an apologetic smile. I'm sorry you had to see that. Could you tell me what happened?

Ash looked back at Ethan's group, then nodded to her. Of course.

...

They gathered around a table at an outdoor cafe near the Battle Tower. The woman introduced herself first.

I'm Yuko, Professor Lund's assistant. He's the man you just saw, and Tory is his son.

Ethan had searched for the laboratory from the tower's top floor, only for its head researcher to appear right in front of him. Some people really were luckier than others.

He studied Yuko. She looked about twenty-seven or twenty-eight. Her makeup was neat, her hair was swept back from her forehead, and she wore a white top with black trousers. She had the capable air of a dependable assistant. A gentle smile and lively eyes softened that impression.

Brock went red at the sight of her. He pressed his hands to his cheeks and said dreamily, An assistant at your age? That's amazing! If you don't mind, Miss Yuko, I'd like to be your assistant too. You could teach me personally, hand in hand...

Max and Misty, seated on either side of him, each twisted one of his ears and stopped him before he could finish.

Stop right there! We don't need to hear about holding hands!

Many women would have been startled by Brock's shameless approach. Yuko simply kept smiling. She was too well-mannered to let her expression change or comment on it. Clearly, she had dealt with difficult situations before.

Her gaze did not linger on Brock. She sipped her coffee and turned to Ash. It seems Tory caused you trouble. I'm sorry.

Max asked the question that had been bothering him. What's wrong with him?

Yuko hesitated before answering. Tory doesn't dislike Pokemon. He's afraid to be near them. He went through something terrifying a few years ago. After that, his cheerful personality changed completely. Now even getting close to a Pokemon frightens him, let alone touching one. The Professor and I saw him in a battle on television and thought he might finally have recovered. But...

I see... Ash looked guilty. I didn't know. I pushed him into battling with me. I shouldn't have done that.

Misty turned to him. How did you meet? How did you end up on the same team?

Ash scratched his cheek awkwardly. Well, I only meant to ask him for directions. But when he saw Pikachu, he ran away without a word. I was curious, so I followed him. Somehow we ran right into the entrance for the arena, and the lift carried us onto the field.

That sounded like Ash. Most people would have asked somebody else after the first person fled. Only Ash would chase after him. It was a strange twist of fate that had brought Tory into his path.

Only you could do something like that, Max said. He was surprised, though perhaps he should not have been. But did they really let you onto the field so easily? Wasn't there any screening?

No. Ash shook his head. We went into the lift passage, and it took us straight up.

Yuko explained, That's because of the city passports you're carrying. Once you enter the competitors' passage, the system scans your information automatically. It's one of the Battle Tower's features. There's no need to go through a long registration process.

Oh, so that's why. Ash finally understood how simply following Tory had turned them into teammates.

He remembered how Tory had looked after the match. When will he get better?

Well... Yuko paused. We've tried counseling several times. The doctors say time alone won't heal this trauma. If he has the right experience, he may recover.

The right experience? Ash stood. Then I'll help him have one! If he and Pikachu become friends, maybe he'll stop being afraid.

He had already taken responsibility for helping Tory. He rubbed Pikachu's head, plainly confident that no one could resist his partner's charm for long. Without waiting for Yuko to agree, he ran off.

Oh! Yuko watched in surprise as the older boy vanished from sight. She had not expected such eagerness to help.

...

After they left the cafe, Max and Brock went looking for Ash and Tory, worried that Ash might get lost. May wanted to join them, but Misty drew her toward a nearby shopping center instead.

That left Ethan alone. Misty had invited him to shop with them, but he had declined.

You two enjoy yourselves. I'm going to walk around the park.

All right. See you later!

He waved to the girls, then watched Yuko disappear down another street. He called out Gengar.

I know, I know. Gengar shrugged before Ethan could speak. You want me to follow her, right? How many times is that now? Can't you give someone else the dirty work for once?

That's how important you are, Ethan said with a grin. None of my other Pokemon can fly through the sky and slip through the ground the way you can. Who else could do this job?

Then his tone grew serious. Be careful. I don't know what security the laboratory has. Stay hidden, and don't alert anyone.

Leave it to me. I'll handle it. Gengar patted its chest and silently followed Yuko.

Ethan waited until she was far enough away that she would not notice him, then set off in the same direction. He meant to try his luck at the laboratory now. If he could get inside, he might see the Deoxys core directly.

With Gengar ahead of him, there was no need to stay close behind Yuko. He walked at an easy pace, taking in LaRousse's sights and the particular feel of the city of technology.

He had not gone far when the Ultra Ball at his waist began shaking violently.

A moment later, a broad red aurora appeared in the sky. It came so suddenly and looked so out of place that within moments it seemed to cover the entire city.

Ethan looked up and understood at once. The other Deoxys had arrived.

His phone rang. As soon as he answered, Misty's voice came through. Ethan, is that...?

Yes, it's Deoxys, he said. Don't tell anyone yet. Stop shopping and find Ash and the others. Stay together. Something may happen soon.

What about you?

I'll act as things develop.

All right!

Ethan ended the call with a sigh. Deoxys had arrived before he could put most of his plans into motion. Still, he had come here hoping his Deoxys would meet the others. A little sooner or later would not change that.

People in the street were looking up. They were puzzled by an aurora appearing where it had no business being, but most treated it as strange weather and thought little more of it.

It vanished almost as quickly as it had come, lasting less than a dozen seconds. His Ultra Ball shook even harder once the light was gone.

Ethan found a quiet corner and released Deoxys and Gardevoir. Through Gardevoir's telepathy, he spoke to his Pokemon.

Can you sense where the other one is?

...Can't tell... the electromagnetic interference... too strong...

There was a quick way to contact the newcomer: have his own Deoxys send up an aurora. But that would be conspicuous and draw attention.

Professor Lund and Yuko, who had brought the Deoxys core to LaRousse, would probably realize as soon as they saw the red light that a Deoxys had come to the city.

Yet Ethan could not simply ignore it. In the movie, the searching Deoxys had attacked the entire city after failing to find its companion. He needed at least to calm it down.

His reasons for coming were simple: let his Pokemon meet the two Deoxys, stop the newcomer from attacking LaRousse, and prevent its later battle with Rayquaza from wrecking the city. After thinking it over, he decided to use the aurora to get its attention.

He would not send the signal from above the city. With Deoxys and Gardevoir's Teleport, he traveled to the broad forest outside LaRousse instead. Even if someone realized what was happening, they would not immediately find the person responsible.

In a flash, Ethan and his two Pokemon appeared among the trees. Once he was sure no one else was there, he nodded to Deoxys.

Go ahead.

Deoxys looked skyward. Blue light shot from its core, and an enormous blue aurora spread over the forest like a net.

Almost at the same moment, the red aurora reappeared above the city. The two fields of light lay side by side, answering each other, their colors crossing where they met.

As the red light flared, Ethan saw a figure flying toward them at tremendous speed. Gardevoir did not need to warn him. In less than half a minute, it landed opposite him.

A Deoxys identical to his own stood before him.

The two Pokemon gazed at each other. Together they rose into the air and slowly circled one another, like dancers tracing a great ring.
