Chapter 178: Three Birdbrains Meet

Beside the cliff, a steep natural path rose between two walls of rock. They followed it into a hidden valley. At the bend, Ethan leaned out for one look, then quickly drew back.

"Well?" Lorelei asked.

Ethan let out a long breath and nodded. "You were right. It's there."

Lorelei and Sabrina peered into the valley. A large bird Pokemon rested on a flat rock, its eyes closed as if it were dozing.

It had the shape of a heron and a commanding bearing. Yellow feathers covered its body, and its long beak was straight and sharp. Red flames burned along its crest and wings.

So that was the legendary Moltres.

Stories said that when it was injured, Moltres bathed in a volcano's molten lava to heal. When it spread its fiercely burning wings, it could light up the night sky. It was even said to bring an early spring to snowy lands it visited and to illuminate mountain paths for people in danger.

Lorelei and Sabrina withdrew quietly so they would not wake it.

"I suppose my work as a guide is done," Lorelei said, adjusting her glasses. "What will you do now? Do you need my help?"

"No. I can manage." Ethan took two Great Balls from the left side of his belt.

Lorelei glanced at them, wondering what they held. Pokemon from his championship team? Probably not. If they were ordinary Pokemon, why keep them in Great Balls? They must be something rare.

There were so many kinds of Pokemon in the world that she could not guess what else the new Indigo League champion had in reserve.

Then Lorelei smiled. "Aren't you curious why I made us climb instead of having you fly us up from the foot of the mountain?"

She plainly wanted to explain. Ethan played along. "Please tell me, Miss Lorelei. Why didn't we fly?"

"We didn't want to alert Moltres," she said, pleased to have his attention. "This place is well hidden and would be hard to spot from the air. Besides, walking made it more of an adventure. After such a long climb, we got to enjoy a rare view, didn't we?"

She waggled her eyebrows at Ethan and glanced toward Sabrina's chest. Now he understood what view she meant. His earlier look had not escaped her after all.

He agreed privately, but kept a perfectly straight face. "The view doesn't matter. I just enjoy climbing. To be honest, I thought you had us walk because you don't own a Flying-type Pokemon and were too embarrassed to ask for a ride."

Lorelei glared. He had to bring that up again? She should have taken the longest route and worn him out.

Sabrina, the subject of their exchange, seemed to be the only one who did not understand. She had no idea that the scenery they were discussing was different from the scenery she had seen.

A flutter of wings came from the valley. Moltres must have woken. Ethan knew the moment might pass if he delayed any longer.

He met Sabrina's eyes. She understood, nodded, and turned to Lorelei.

"Miss Lorelei, I'm not feeling well. Could you take me down the mountain?"

"What?" Lorelei nearly choked. After all that work finding Moltres, Sabrina wanted to leave? Of course. They were sending her away.

She had led them all the way here, and now they would not even let her watch? She forced a friendly smile, trying not to speak through clenched teeth.

"Of course. I'll take you down."

She kept looking back as they went. Her displeasure was obvious.

Only when both women were far enough away that Lorelei could not double back unnoticed did Ethan open the Great Balls and release Zapdos and Articuno.

He was not guarding his secrets out of pettiness. Word of his Pokemon would spread eventually. But their strength helped keep him safe, and the later other people learned about them, the better.

During the earlier attack at the bridge, for instance, he had already caught Articuno before Team Rocket came for him. Its sudden appearance had taken them by surprise and let him drive them off.

The disturbance caused by the two birds soon caught Moltres's attention. It gave an angry cry, as if demanding to know who had entered its territory.

Zapdos and Articuno were no less proud. Without waiting for Ethan's orders, they beat their wings, rose into the air, and flew into the valley.

Moltres cried out and rushed to meet the intruders. It had not expected them to be the other two legendary birds. Nor could it make sense of why Articuno and Zapdos had come together.

The three hovered in a tense triangle over the valley. Though outnumbered, Moltres showed no sign of retreating.

Ethan sprang out from behind the rocks. "Heh, heh, heh!" He imitated Haunter's wicked laugh, fully aware of how melodramatic he sounded. "Moltres, your time has come! Surrender!"

Then he gave his Pokemon their orders. "Zapdos, you're our main attacker. Articuno, you've got a bad type matchup, so focus on keeping it occupied."

To be safe, he released his other Pokemon as well. He kept them on watch, ready to stop an interruptionor prevent Moltres from escaping if the fight turned against it.

The three birds held their positions. Battle seemed only a heartbeat away.
