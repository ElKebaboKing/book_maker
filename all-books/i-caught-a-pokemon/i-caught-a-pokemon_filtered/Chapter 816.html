Chapter 816: A Bright Future Ahead

Dragon Breath struck Articuno, sending it falling from the sky. Ash quickly ordered Charizard to close in and grab it. Articuno recovered just as Charizard reached it, turning sharply away from both claws.

Ash had not expected that. Articuno had only just taken a direct hit, yet it had already regained control. Watching Ethan's match had not shown him how tough a Legendary Pokemon truly was. He had to battle one himself to feel its extraordinary endurance. There was a reason Ethan's Articuno had needed so long to defeat it.

While Ash was still surprised, Noland called a counterattack. Ice Beam struck Charizard. The Fire-type weathered it and flew at Articuno again.

This time Articuno dodged Charizard's Flamethrower, closed the distance, and hit it with Steel Wing. Charizard could not shrug that blow off as it had Ice Beam. It plunged from the sky and struck the ground hard.

Max frowned. Why does it suddenly look like Charizard can't beat Articuno?

It can, Ethan said, shaking his head. Noland has worked out Ash's pattern. He's relied on Flamethrower since the start. He also tried to set up Seismic Toss, but he couldn't land it. If Ash keeps counting on Charizard's type advantage without changing his approach, the match will get harder for him.

So he's in danger?

He is, Ethan said with a small smile. But he's Ash. Charizard has the favorable type and plenty of strength. This is a setback, and he'll find another way. Let's watch.

They trusted Ethan's judgment, but Ash would have to change the fight himself. Noland gave him little time.

Charizard had barely risen when Articuno flew overhead. At Noland's command, Water-type energy gathered in its beak. A large sphere of water shot toward Charizard.

Ethan recognized Water Pulse. Water and ice were closely related, but an Ice-type Pokemon without Water typing could not normally use Water attacks unless it had learned them. No one had expected Articuno to know this move, much less keep it hidden until now.

It caught Ash off guard. Charizard could not dodge. The Water Pulse struck it squarely, turning the type advantage against him and paying back the earlier Fire attack.

Ash's early confidence had vanished. He watched Charizard with a tight frown.

This is bad, Brock said. I didn't know Articuno had a Water move. Ash will have to be more careful about getting close.

Noland kept a good surprise in reserve, Ethan said. If he'd used Water Pulse against my Articuno, Ash would have known to expect it. He waited until Charizard was already struggling, then struck hard. For someone who acts so rugged, he's a careful tactician.

Max and the others began to see why Noland was a Frontier Brain. Against Ethan, he had appeared outmatched. That did not make him weak. Ash could lose if he gave Noland an opening.

On the field, Charizard was badly hurt. Its body showed the damage from Water Pulse, but it could still fight.

Noland pressed his advantage. Ash ordered Charizard back into the air to avoid the next attack. The roles had reversed. Ash often seemed to suffer most when he began with a favorable type matchup. He did sometimes win such battles, but rarely without putting everyone through a scare.

Charizard tried to keep its distance. One lapse let Articuno catch it in a combination of Ice Beam and Water Pulse. Shards of ice struck across its body. Charizard raised its wings in time to block the worst of them.

Ash... May, Max, and Brock watched anxiously.

Max was about to ask Ethan what Ash could do when the scene stirred a memory. Hadn't he watched Ash face a similar reversal at the Ever Grande Conference? Ash had escaped that trouble in the end. Max kept his question to himself and waited.

Charizard's wings had been frostbitten. Its movements slowed. Noland ordered Articuno to repeat the attack.

Ice Beam came first, then Water Pulse. The moves met in the air, creating a spray of hard ice that shot at Charizard.

Ash called for Flamethrower, but the fire could not melt the flying shards quickly enough. They passed through the flames and battered Charizard. It staggered in midair, nearly falling again.

Noland saw his chance to end the match. Articuno, let's finish this! Climb fast and get above Charizard!

Articuno accelerated upward. Noland thought victory was close. Ash, who had been waiting for an opening, moved at last.

Charizard, get above Articuno! Use Overheat!

Charizard's body blazed red. Heat surged through its frost-stiffened wings, restoring their movement. Like a great ball of fire, it climbed rapidly past Articuno and took the higher position.

It must have learned Overheat during its training in Charicific Valley. Ash had kept the move back for a reason. Overheat was enormously powerful, but the effort sharply reduced the user's special attacking strength afterward. Repeated use made each later attack weaker. Its timing mattered. It was best used to win in one stroke.

Ash had judged that the match had reached that point. Noland saw his intention. He smiled and met the challenge rather than retreating.

Charizard attacked from above with Overheat. Articuno rose to meet it with Water Pulse. A blazing column of fire and a dense water sphere collided. Light flashed, steam erupted, and an explosion shook the air.

Water Pulse could not match Overheat's raw power. But Water had the advantage against Fire, making the clash far less straightforward than their strength alone suggested.

Ash watched for the outcome and missed Noland's small smile. Articuno shot out of the steam, its wings gleaming with Steel-type energy. It slashed Charizard heavily as it passed.

Charizard was near its limit. Steel Wing had not knocked it out, but Articuno turned for another pass, both wings shining again.

Everyone could see Noland meant this to be the last blow. With so little strength left, Charizard would fall if it landed. Ash had spent his hidden move. He could only call, Block it!

He was putting his trust in Charizard. Hurt, exhausted, and forced onto the defensive, it seemed moments from defeat.

But Charizard had once surpassed even Pikachu as Ash's unquestioned ace. It had defeated one of Ethan's Pokemon at the Silver Conference. It had earned that reputation.

As Articuno came in, Charizard raised both claws and caught its wings by sheer force. It stopped the Steel Wing attack.

Noland had not expected that. Ash's eyes lit up.

Now! Seismic Toss!

Charizard held Articuno fast. Drawing on its last reserves, it carried the bird into a spiraling dive. They fell faster and faster, straight toward the ground.

Boom!

They struck in an instant. Dust burst across the field, and the impact shook the spectator area. Max and the others grabbed the railing, but none took their eyes from the settling cloud.

When the dust cleared, Charizard lay motionless. Articuno was struggling to its feet.

What? Max cried. Ash lost? But that move

The result seemed impossible. Then, before he could finish, Articuno collapsed. Charizard slowly pushed itself upright.

It... Max stared.

Charizard looked down at the unconscious Articuno and roared toward the sky, proclaiming its victory over a Legendary Pokemon.

We did it! Ash and Pikachu jumped with joy before Sergio had even called the result.

Ash had defeated Articuno. He had scarcely dared imagine accomplishing that before. This victory showed how far he had grown from the inexperienced boy he had once been.

Sergio announced the outcome and took Articuno for treatment. He and Noland exchanged a rueful look. Noland went to meet Ash as Ethan and the others came down from the stands.

My first day battling with Articuno, and I've lost twice, Noland said with a sigh. You're both stronger than I expected.

Ethan's Articuno had beaten his more comfortably, as one might expect of a three-region champion. Ash surprised him most. The boy had enjoyed a type advantage, but defeating a Legendary Pokemon one-on-one was still a remarkable feat.

Noland accepted both losses without complaint. These two Trainers had bright futures ahead of them.
