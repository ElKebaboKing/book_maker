Chapter 416: Eusine

After a night's rest, Ethan met up with Erika the next morning and went to the cafeteria. Jimmy and Marina were already waiting there. Trays sat in front of them, but all the food had been eaten, leaving only a few scraps behind.

The two were chatting idly and had apparently been there for quite some time.

Ethan glanced down at the time, then approached them in surprise. "It isn't even seven yet, and you've already finished breakfast? You got up far too early!"

Ethan was not someone who slept excessively. Trainers like them simply had no work or school to attend. Except when they needed an early start on the road, they generally had no reason to keep an early schedule. They slept and woke whenever they felt like it.

"It's all Jimmy's fault!" Marina greeted them with a huge yawn, then began complaining. "He was afraid Raikou might quietly leave, so he went to the treatment room before sunrise to see it. He made me get up with him, and now I'm exhausted."

"Huh?" Erika asked curiously. "Even so, shouldn't he have gone by himself? Why were you with him?"

Marina's face reddened slightly. "His footsteps were too loud. They woke me up."

Ethan and Erika exchanged a glance.

That excuse was rather flimsy...

The boys and girls slept in separate dormitories some distance apart, and the rooms were well insulated against sound. Unless Jimmy had deliberately gone to wake her, Marina could not possibly have heard his footstepsunless she had already been awake long before he left!

The girl was not telling the truth...

Then again, as childhood friends who had grown up together, Marina could probably guess Jimmy's thoughts with ease.

She must have known Jimmy would sneak off to visit Raikou, so she had dressed and washed up ahead of time, then calmly waited for him!

What did she mean, his footsteps woke her up? She had clearly wanted an opportunity for the two of them to be alone!

Although Ethan and Erika saw through her little scheme, neither of them exposed it.

Young love in its uncertain first bloom was a wonderful thing. They would let the two enjoy it for themselves!

Leaving them alone, Ethan and Erika went to choose some of their favorite foods.

Much like a hotel, the Pokemon Center served breakfast as a buffet. Guests could simply take whatever they wanted, which was extremely convenient.

There were not many people in the cafeteria. Compared with yesterday's commotion, the crowd had visibly thinned. There were two reasons for this: many people were not awake yet, while others had left the Pokemon Center the previous night.

Pokemon Trainers were busy people too. Since they could not see Raikou, most of them had given up on the idea rather than waste more time. They had continued on their way and resumed their adventures!

Once Ethan and Erika finished breakfast, they naturally wanted to check on Raikou. Though Jimmy had already visited once, he still followed behind them toward the treatment area.

Marina sighed helplessly when she saw his tireless enthusiasm.

Didn't you just see it? What else is there to look at!? Just how obsessed with Raikou are you!?

Marina grumbled inwardly about Jimmy's behavior, but there was nothing else she could do. She followed after them.

The four entered the corridor. Before they could reach Raikou's room, a man burst in behind them with tremendous urgency!

Hearing the rapid footsteps, Ethan and the others turned around. The man looked to be in his twenties. He wore a casual purple suit, white gloves, a white cape, and a red bow tie. His blond hair was swept back, apart from a single lock that rose into the air above his forehead.

What an unbelievably flamboyant outfit.

That was Ethan's judgment and his first impression of the man.

As the stranger hurried past, Ethan even caught the scent of men's cologne!

"Where's Raikou!? Where's Raikou!?"

Still shouting, the man strode to the treatment-room window, braced both hands against the glass, and peered inside.

When he saw Raikou sleeping there, he gasped in wonder and cried excitedly, "It really is Raikou! There's no mistake! No mistake at all! How magnificent! This is the Legendary Pokemon spoken of alongside Suicune! I never imagined I would have the honor of seeing you here!"

Perhaps because his voice was so loud, Raikou's tail twitched inside the room. Fortunately, it did not wake immediately.

Seeing the man behave this way, Jimmy could not help shouting, "Hey! Who are you!?"

Only then did the man look toward Ethan and the others. The moment Marina, who stood at the very back, saw his face clearly, she became completely infatuated.

"He's so handsome!"

With that exclamation, she darted in front of the man and stared at him without blinking, a blush even spreading across her face!

But...

She thinks this man is handsome?

Ethan gave Marina a puzzled glance, then studied the stranger more carefully.

As far as appearances went, the man was only slightly better looking than average. He was nowhere close to what Ethan would call handsome!

Seeing Marina behave this way and Ethan looking utterly baffled, Jimmy's expression darkened. He muttered, "Oh... Now I remember. Ever since she was little, Marina's never been able to resist handsome men wearing capes!"

She can't resist handsome men in capes?

Ethan frowned.

So that was it. He had never imagined that Marina would develop such an odd fixation at her age...

And it truly was exceptionally strange. Some people were drawn to faces, some to legs, and others to chests, but this was the first time Ethan had ever heard of someone attracted to men in capes!

He really could not understand the way Marina's mind worked!

Perhaps Marina's enthusiasm was too much for him, because the man cautiously took a step backward. As he did, he happened to notice Erika beside them and exclaimed in surprise, "Huh? You're Miss Erika, aren't you? What are you doing here?"

Ethan's gaze moved between the man and Erika.

"You two know each other?"

Erika examined the man's face carefully, but eventually shook her head.

"I'm sorry. Have we met before?"

The man smiled. "My hometown is Celadon City. I challenged the Celadon Gym several years ago, but you defeated me. It's only natural that you don't remember. Ah, I should introduce myself first. My name is Eusine, and I suppose you could call me a Pokemon researcher."

Eusine?

So this was him!

After hearing that the man was a researcher, Jimmy suppressed his irritation and asked, "Mr. Eusine, what do you study?"

Eusine grinned. "I'm an expert on Ho-Oh's guardians. I've spent years tracking Suicune!"

Tracking Suicune?

Ethan and Erika exchanged a glance, then instinctively looked toward the Ultra Ball at Ethan's waist.

If they told Eusine that Ethan had a Suicune inside that ball right now, his pride would surely take a heavy blow. After all, Eusine had searched for years, while Ethan had not only encountered Suicune but captured it with ease. No matter how they looked at it, the truth would be rather crushing.

It was only a passing thought. Eusine was obsessed with Suicune and was also arrogant and conceited. Ethan had no desire to deal with someone like that.
