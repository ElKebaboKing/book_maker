Chapter 283: Following Silver

Ethan doubted Silver would leave the restaurant immediately, so he waved over the server and ordered a quick meal for himself and Sabrina, with two coffees.

The server soon brought several sandwiches with different fillings, along with vegetable salad, fries, and chicken nuggets.

While they ate, Looker gave them a general account of the operation the International Police were about to carry out.

He spoke openly, apparently unworried that Ethan or Sabrina might leak his plans. The two had been a great help in Kanto, after all. The International Police could not clear out Team Rocket with their own forces alone. They needed the Pokemon League and Trainers like Ethan to help them.

It took four sandwiches to fill Ethan up. Just as he reached for a fifth, he saw Silver rise from his table across the street and leave the restaurant.

To Looker's amazement, Ethan demonstrated the rare skill of eating a sandwich in three bites. He stuffed the rest into his mouth, hurried to pay with his cheeks bulging, and left the coffee shop with Sabrina and Looker.

The evening air was cool at this time of year. A breeze passed over them, raising goose bumps on their skin. The streetlights were already on, but there were fewer and fewer pedestrians.

Silver walked northwest through town at an easy pace, seemingly unaware that anyone was following him.

As Looker made to slip after him, Ethan caught his arm.

"No. It's dark, but the streets are nearly empty. If we stay close enough to follow him, he'll spot us."

"I know," Looker said with a helpless shrug. "But if we wait, he'll be out of sight. I came in a hurry, and there are no other International Police officers nearby to work with me. I'll have to take the risk."

In Ethan's old world, they would probably use some piece of surveillance technology. Here, though...

"We have another option." Ethan smiled and looked down at his shadow. "This is exactly when you ask a Pokemon for help. Gengar, you heard us, right? I'm counting on you."

Gengar's head rose out of the shadow. It gave a wicked chuckle. "Of course. Shall I abduct him, or wait until no one's around and kill him?"

Ethan stared at it.

"Please..." He sighed, exhausted already. "Just follow him quietly and see where he's going and what he's doing. Do you have to make it sound so horrifying?"

Ethan could have sent Charizard to watch from above, or asked Sabrina to keep track of Silver with her psychic powers. After considering his options, he had decided Gengar was the best choice.

"Ha! I know," Gengar said with a laugh. "I was only joking to lighten the mood!"

"Who feels like joking now?" Ethan shook his head. "Leave markers along the way so we can find you. Above all, don't let him notice you, and don't play any pranks on your own initiative."

Gengar patted its chest. "Leave it to me!"

Before their eyes, its body slowly turned transparent, as though it were vanishing into thin air. Soon they could not see it at all. A light breeze brushed past them, and they knew Gengar had silently gone after Silver.

Looker watched the direction they had gone with envy. "You have so many powerful, dependable Pokemon. That's impressive."

"Why don't you catch a few more yourself?" Ethan asked. "I think you only have a Growlithe and a Croagunk. Isn't that dangerous for an International Police officer?"

"I can't manage it," Looker said with a smile. "My assignments keep me busy every day. I don't have time to catch more Pokemon, much less raise and care for them. I may have only two, but both are partners I can count on. They're enough for me."

The three waited until Silver was out of sight, then slowly followed the route he had taken. They saw neither Silver nor Gengar along the way, but every few dozen meters a clear arrow appeared in a conspicuous place, keeping them on course.

The sky darkened as they crossed most of the town. At last, they reached a large walled property.

Inside stood an imposing three-story building with a round tower. It looked like some kind of research facility.

Looker brightened, as though his suspicions had been confirmed. "Professor Elm's laboratory! I knew it. This is Team Rocket's target!"

He scanned the area, but no matter how carefully he looked, he could not find any other Team Rocket members.

Professor Elm... Ethan rubbed his chin, trying to recall what he knew. Elm was Johto's Pokemon professor and provided starter Pokemon to new Trainers. He also seemed to have studied under Professor Oak. Beyond that, Ethan remembered little.

Gengar's head appeared above the courtyard wall. It beckoned to Ethan and whispered, "Over here! That Silver fellow went into the grounds!"

With no one else nearby, Ethan asked Sabrina to stay outside and watch their backs. Then he and Looker climbed over the wall.

As soon as they landed, they saw Silver crouched among some distant bushes, staring fixedly at the three-story building. They hurried behind a large tree before he could spot them.

Silver clearly had no idea that someone was watching the watcher. He would never have guessed that his every move was under observation.

The grounds remained quiet. After watching for some time, Looker finally voiced his confusion. "How strange. Why is Silver alone? Where are the other Team Rocket members?"

Ethan was silent for a moment. "Perhaps there aren't any. I think he's acting on his own this time."

"On his own?"

Looker's face fell. If that was true, he had spent all this effort for nothing.
