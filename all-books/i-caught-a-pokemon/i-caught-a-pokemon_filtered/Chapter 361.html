Chapter 361: Toward the Crystal

At that moment, Professor Oak suddenly received an email.

It had no sender address and no signature, appearing on his computer almost from nowhere. This astonished him. As a Pokemon Professor, he protected his computer with powerful encryption. An ordinary person could never break into it, and even a hacker should not have been able to do so without leaving any trace.

After a brief hesitation, Professor Oak moved the cursor and opened the message. It contained a video.

When he played it, a girl of six or seven appeared on the screen.

Despite her childish face, the girl spoke into the camera with grave composure.

"Papa and Mama have both come back. Molly is very happy. Everything can stay exactly as it is now. Please don't let anyone come inside and disturb us!"

The video ended with her final words. It lasted barely more than ten seconds, yet its contents shocked everyone.

Misty asked in surprise, "When she said Mama, she must have meant Ash's mother, right? But why would she call Mrs. Ketchum that? And something else is strange. Molly said her father came back too, but didn't Professor Hale disappear?"

As she spoke, Misty gave Ash a suspicious look.

Could this girl Molly be the secret daughter of Delia and Professor Hale? Is that why she took Delia and called her Mama?

Misty's thoughts were not deliberately indecent. The word Mama simply invited far too much speculation.

At least the video indirectly confirmed that Delia had not come to any harm after being abducted.

Unfortunately, no one except Ethan could answer Misty's questions. Even Ash could only stare in shocked confusion.

...

After leaving the room, Ash looked deeply worried. He sat silently in a corner of the dining room, completely unlike his usual cheerful, optimistic self.

One glance told Ethan what he was thinking. Ash was undoubtedly trying to determine how he could leave immediately to rescue his mother without anyone stopping him.

He remained distracted until noon. After eating lunch with Ethan and the others, Ash waited until no one seemed to be watching, made his decision, and quietly slipped out of the Pokemon Center with Pikachu. He ran toward the enormous crystal flower.

Ash believed he had left without anyone noticing. Before he had traveled even a few hundred meters, however, Ethan's voice came from beneath a nearby tree.

"What is it? You don't want to wait until tonight, so you're going to rescue your mother now?"

Ash turned in surprise. Ethan sat quietly upon a rock, smiling at him. He appeared to have anticipated Ash's actions and waited there for quite some time.

Although astonished to see him, Ash nodded.

"Ethan? Why are you here? Did you come to tell me not to go?"

Ash respected Ethan deeply, regarding him as both his greatest rival and a role model. If Ethan had truly come to make him wait until evening, Ash would have returned with him obediently despite his reluctance.

"I understand how you feel." Ethan rose and walked over. "But you can't charge in there so recklessly!"

As expected, Ethan really did come to persuade me to go back...

Ash sighed and prepared to turn around, but Ethan suddenly changed direction.

"In fact, I didn't come to stop you. I intend to go with you!"

"What!?" Ash's eyes widened.

"You seem surprised." Ethan smiled. "I can't let you venture into that place alone. I'll have to accompany you!"

"Ethan is right!"

Misty's voice suddenly came from farther away.

They turned and saw Misty and Brock running toward them, both breathing hard.

When they arrived, Misty struggled for breath, her chest heaving. "I'm coming too!"

Brock refused to be left behind. "And me!"

Looking at them, Ethan smiled and shook his head. "I knew neither of you would miss something like this. Now that everyone's here, we can leave together!"

"Of course!" Misty smiled proudly. "How could we let Ash go alone?"

Ash was deeply moved. "Everyone..."

Misty looked around and asked in surprise, "Huh? Where is Miss Erika? Isn't she coming?"

Ethan shook his head. "She and Lisa stayed behind to deal with Professor Oak and Officer Jenny. If they learned Ash had slipped away, they'd certainly try to stop him. I asked Erika to remain there and keep their attention occupied!"

"As expected of you, Ethan!" Misty's eyes lit up. "You planned everything in advance. How thoughtful!"

"Enough flattery." Ethan lightly tapped her head. "There's no time to waste. Let's get moving!"

Once the four joined together, they continued toward the crystal forest.

Greenfield truly deserved its ranking as the place girls most wanted to visit. Golden sunflowers covered the hills along their route, creating a beautiful sight as far as the eye could see.

If the immense crystal forest had not clashed so completely with the landscape nearby, the view would have been even more refreshing.

They hurried across the fields. Once they reached the forest's edge, they did not pause. Professor Oak had confirmed that the crystallization could not harm them, so they stepped directly onto the crystal.

Inside the forest, complete silence surrounded them. It felt as though every form of life had vanished, leaving only their running footsteps and heavy breathing.

Hard crystal covered the ground. All around them, strange plants stood encased like objects trapped in amber.

Ethan stomped down hard but could not crack the crystal.

Brock kicked it too. He used so much force that its hardness nearly broke his toes.

"It's so hard! If the crystal surrounds us by accident, we'll be in real trouble!"

As they ran, Misty suddenly asked, "How are we going to deal with Entei? It's a Legendary Pokemon!"

Before Ethan could answer, Ash glanced at Pikachu with complete confidence.

"If we give it everything we have, we can definitely defeat it!"

"Ash is right!" Ethan agreed. "We're Trainers. It doesn't matter whether it's Entei. As long as it's a Pokemon, there must be a way to handle it!"
