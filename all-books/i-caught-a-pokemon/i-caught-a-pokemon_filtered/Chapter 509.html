Chapter 509: Surprise Attack

Bang! Bang!

Several smoke grenades burst near the TV station. Thick white smoke spread outward, blocking Team Rocket's view from inside and drawing their attention.

Knowing this usually meant the police were beginning their operation, the Team Rocket members did not dare let down their guard.

Sure enough, officers began trying to attack the front entrance. Team Rocket concentrated most of its members in the first-floor lobby, determined to hold them off.

With their Pokemon providing cover, the two sides fought fiercely. The officers were well trained, but their opponents fought with equal desperation. The assault continued for several minutes without breaking through the defense.

Amid the confusion, nobody noticed a Dragonite carrying a man and a woman. It made a wide detour, climbed high into the sky, then dove and smashed through a window at the top of the tower, bursting into the station!

Crash!

Glass shattered and scattered across the floor. Ethan jumped down from Dragonite's back. Without pausing to look around, he flicked his hand and released two Pokemon.

A flash of blue light, and Gardevoir and Jolteon stood on either side of him. Gengar appeared in front of him at the same time. After setting Erika down, Dragonite rose behind them. The four Pokemon scanned their surroundings, guarding Ethan and Erika from every direction.

Whoever the enemy might be, they would have to get past these Pokemon to harm their Trainers.

Everything around them was quiet. No enemy appeared. Only then did Ethan have a chance to take stock of the place.

They were in the tower above the TV station. The main building stood seven or eight stories below, joined to the tower by a narrower structure. The two sections were separate areas.

Judging by its layout, this was probably a circular observation deck. It was empty now, save for signs of a hurried departure. The station staff had likely been seized by Team Rocket and confined elsewhere.

That suited their purpose. At least they had made it inside without being spotted.

Looker's voice crackled through Ethan's earpiece. "...How are things going?"

"Everything went smoothly," Ethan said quietly. "Team Rocket hasn't noticed us yet."

"Excellent!" Looker let out a long breath. "I've cut the station's internal camera feeds, so they won't see you on the monitors. We'll keep up the attack on the front entrance for a few more minutes. Use that time to make your move!"

"Understood!"

After the call, Ethan sent Gengar to check the area. Once it confirmed there were no Team Rocket members nearby, he and Erika headed down.

Erika released her Leafeon and Bellossom as well. Together, their Pokemon were a force no one could afford to ignore.

That was why Looker had been so pleased to see Ethan. With a Champion involved, one or two people could do nearly the work of an entire elite unit.

They could move quietly, too.

Once they left the room, Gengar went ahead to scout. It could pass through walls without a sound, leaving Ethan little reason to worry that the two of them would be discovered.

They did encounter a couple of patrolling Team Rocket members along the way, but Gengar knocked them out before either could notice anything.

Ethan tore strips from their clothes to gag them, then used handcuffs he had obtained from Looker to secure them to a railing. Leaving them there, he continued downward.

Team Rocket was short on people, and most of its attention was on the front entrance. The infiltration went remarkably smoothly.

After passing through the structure connecting the tower to the main building, they soon found the captive staff in a conference room.

Although fighting was still going on in the lobby, more than a dozen Team Rocket members remained to guard the hostages. Rescuing everyone without raising an alarm would be nearly impossible.

But they had already made it this far. Being discovered now did not matter much.

After a brief discussion, Ethan and Erika split up and approached the conference room's front and rear doors. When Erika was in position, Ethan took out his Gengar mask and put it on for the first time in a long while.

At his command to move, their Pokemon broke through both doors at once. Before the Team Rocket members or their Pokemon could react, attacks flew across the room. Explosions and sparks erupted, sending the defending Pokemon tumbling backward.

It all happened in an instant. The hostages screamed in fright and instinctively crouched low to avoid the attacks.

When the flames and smoke from the explosions cleared, the Team Rocket members and their Pokemon lay scattered on the floor.

A few Team Rocket members had escaped unconsciousness. Shocked to see someone rescuing the hostages, they hurriedly pulled out their radios and alerted the others.

Only then did Team Rocket realize that someone had slipped into the station from another direction while they were fighting at the front.

Damn it! Were these police officers all this devious?

Meanwhile, outside the station, Looker saw a disturbance ripple through Team Rocket's defensive line. He understood at once what had happened. Without waiting to contact Ethan, he made the decision and ordered a full assault.

With the police throwing all their strength into the attack and an enemy striking from behind, Team Rocket came under enormous pressure. Its seemingly solid defense collapsed almost at once.

The Team Rocket members fought as they retreated deeper into the station. The officers pressed forward, quickly took the first floor, and began pushing toward the second.

Ethan could hear the fierce battle clearly, even from the conference room.

Having freed the hostages, he and Erika stayed there to protect them. They could not risk Team Rocket lashing out at the captives in desperation. Only when Looker arrived with his officers did they finally relax their guard.

"Thanks for all your work. Is everyone all right?" Looker asked.

"What could have happened?" Ethan smiled faintly. "They were only ordinary members. It wasn't difficult at all."

Looker nodded. "Rescuing the hostages has removed our biggest concern. But those Team Rocket members have fallen back to the top of the tower. Worse, we've spotted several other executives among them!"

Executives?

Ethan's interest sharpened immediately.

The last time he had encountered a Team Rocket executive was at the Rocket warehouse in the Sevii Islands. He, Sabrina, and Lorelei had faced Lambda, one of the four executives, and eventually captured him and sent him to prison.

So which executive would appear today?
