Chapter 175: Inside the Warehouse

At Ethan's direction, Haunter used Hypnosis on the Team Rocket lookouts and their Pokemon, putting them all to sleep.

The guards had been patrolling diligently. Against a Haunter that could appear behind them without warning, they never even had time to cry out.

Watching from a distance, Lorelei adjusted her glasses. Haunter made it look practiced. Clearly, this was not its first time doing such a thing.

"Your Haunter is remarkable. Its Hypnosis works so quickly and so well. It reminds me of Agatha's Gengar. No, it might be even stronger."

"Of course it is." Ethan held up two fingers. "If one of your Pokemon lived for two thousand years, it would be this strong too."

"Two thousand years? How is that possible? Has it really been alive that long?" Lorelei thought for a moment that he must be joking.

Haunter returned in time to hear her. "What's so surprising, little girl? The last time I went to sleep, you might still have been an embryo in your mother's belly."

Lorelei had no answer. Between Haunter's remark and Ethan pocketing the Never-Melt Ice, they had managed to get on her nerves. Like trainer, like Pokemon.

With the outer guards dealt with, the three approached the warehouse's rear entrance. They thought they might slip in unnoticed, but a concealed infrared sensor spotted them.

An alarm sounded inside. Heavy steel shutters dropped over the doors and windows, front and back.

"I knew it wouldn't be that easy," Lorelei sighed.

Stealth was no longer an option. She messaged the police officer and told him to bring his people in at once. Then she sent out a Dewgong to attack the rear door.

The door and its shutters were made of an exceptionally hard material. Dewgong's attacks left only shallow scratches. They were not going to break through quickly.

A man's slightly raspy voice came from a speaker beside the security camera.

"Lorelei? Damn it. We went to all that trouble to build this warehouse, and you've already found it. Save your effort. Without the code, you'll never get inside."

It was Lambda, one of Team Rocket's four executives. Lorelei turned and saw a small keypad mounted on the wall beside the door.

"What are you so pleased about?" She glared up at the camera, venting her frustration on him. "We can't get in, but you can't get out. You'll be trapped there eventually."

"We'll see about that," Lambda said with a nasty laugh. "Let's see whether you can catch me."

Ethan looked around. He saw no reinforcements or heavy weapons that might explain Lambda's confidence. Then a thought occurred to him.

"He sounds awfully calm. He must have another way out. Knowing Team Rocket, I'd guess there's an escape passage inside."

Lambda fell silent. The pause was almost an admission. Only then did he seem to notice Ethan.

The camera turned and focused on him.

"You again? Did you think I wouldn't recognize you behind a mask?" Lambda's voice was full of resentment. "You keep turning up to ruin Team Rocket's plans. One day you'll fall into our hands, and then you'll learn what happens to people who stand against us!"

"I'll look forward to it." Ethan was unmoved. "For now, you'd better worry about yourself. Once we find your escape passage, you won't have anywhere to run."

He gave Haunter a look. Grinning wickedly, Haunter passed straight through the thick wall.

Team Rocket had planned to shelter behind the warehouse's defenses until it could withdraw in good order. Haunter's sudden appearance inside threw that plan into chaos.

Cries and sounds of battle came through the walls. Ethan and the others could not see what was happening, but the noise soon died down. The keypad by the door spat a few sparks and went dark.

Haunter had damaged its wiring. Sabrina and Gardevoir used their psychic powers together to pull the heavy warehouse door open.

Lorelei had been wondering how they could possibly get in. Watching Ethan and Sabrina solve it so easily left her impressed.

"You two really are dependable. If I'd been alone, I might have had to stand here and watch them escape. Asking you to help was a wise decision."

"We all have our specialties," Ethan said with a smile. "Dealing with Team Rocket is ours."

Inside, several grunts and their Pokemon lay sprawled on the floor. Ethan looked around, but Lambda was nowhere in sight.

Had he escaped?

In one corner, hundreds of cages had been stacked together. Most were empty, but many still held Pokemon Team Rocket had poached.

Lorelei moved to release them. Ethan stopped her.

"There are too many. Freeing them all will take time. Leave that to the police. We need to catch Lambda before he gets away."

Lorelei realized he was right and rejoined him. "Where would he have gone?"

The warehouse had only one floor, but it was spacious. Beyond the main storage hall, several smaller rooms served as offices and living quarters. They searched them one by one without finding Lambda.

At last, only one office remained. Ethan kicked open its door. Lambda was not there either.

A dozen monitors on the wall showed live camera feeds, and a microphone sat on the desk. They had found the room where Lambda had spoken to them.
