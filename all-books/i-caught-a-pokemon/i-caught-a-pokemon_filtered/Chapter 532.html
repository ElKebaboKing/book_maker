Chapter 532: The Defense Mechanism

Latias spoke to them urgently, but Ethan and the others could not understand Pokemon language. Her expression alone told them something serious had happened.

Ethan already had a suspicion. The Secret Garden must have been attacked, and the attackers could only be the two thieves.

Ash and Brock looked at him. Ethan understood what they wanted and addressed the shadow at his feet. "Gengar, stop sleeping. Time to work."

They needed Gengar, who had so often served as their interpreter.

At Ethan's urging, Gengar slowly rose out of the shadow. Absurdly, he was yawning, wearing a nightcap, and hugging a large pillow.

"Hey," Gengar grumbled, glancing at Ethan. "Do you know what time it is? It's the middle of the night! I need my beauty sleep to take care of my skin."

"Enough," Ethan said with a helpless sigh. Then he turned serious. "Something's wrong. Translate for us. What is Latias saying?"

Latias realized this unusual Gengar could speak human language and repeated her account.

Gengar dropped the jokes after listening. He turned to the others. "There was trouble in the Secret Garden. The two thieves who attacked Latias during the day broke in. They captured her brother and stole the Soul Dew!"

Ash and Brock's expressions changed at once.

"How could that happen?" Ash asked. "Wasn't there a barrier protecting the garden? How did they find it?"

"They must have followed us without our noticing," Brock said, resting a hand under his chin. Then he sighed. "I still can't believe they succeeded with two Legendary Pokemon there."

"That isn't so strange," Ethan said, shaking his head. "Being Legendary doesn't guarantee a Pokemon is strong. Even two of the same species can differ enormously. Their condition, experience, and surroundings all affect how well they can fight."

None of them could go back to sleep after that. Erika and Misty were in a separate room, so Ethan called Erika to explain. Then he, Ash, and Brock hurried to get dressed.

By the time they reached the Pokemon Center lobby, Erika and Misty were rushing in too, their clothes hastily thrown on. People said women took longest to get ready, but in an emergency they had kept things simple.

Though Ethan had told them about Latias over the phone, seeing her there still surprised them. Fortunately, the lobby was empty at this hour, so she had no need to worry about being seen.

Even if someone else had been there, she could have taken Bianca's form again.

Erika thought a step further. "What about Lorenzo and Bianca? Are they all right?"

As guardians of the Secret Garden, neither would have stood by while it was attacked.

Latias shook her head. Then her eyes began to shine, just as they had in the garden. She was using dream projection.

In an instant, the scene around them changed. Through Latios's eyes, they saw Bianca and Lorenzo bound together in spider silk. Annie and Oakley stood nearby, murmuring to each other beside Latios. They seemed puzzled by the sudden change in his eyes.

"Why does this place look familiar?" Misty frowned at the view around them. "It looks like some kind of hall..."

"You don't need to guess." Ethan pointed to something nearby. "It's the cathedral. Look at that machine. Isn't it the one we saw today?"

The others looked around. He was right. They were seeing the cathedral they had toured that afternoon.

"Then let's get over there!"

They were about to leave to rescue everyone when the projection showed Oakley slipping deftly into the sphere at the center of the machine. Somehow, with Latios and the Soul Dew, she set it in motion.

As it turned, the fossils embedded in the floor stirred. Before their eyes, they rose as though alive.

The Soul Dew's power was astonishing.

"We have to hurry," Ethan said. "Whatever those two intend to do, they're almost ready."

The others nodded. As soon as Latias ended the projection, they ran for the Pokemon Center entrance.

Before they could reach it, iron hooks shot up from beneath the paving stones outside, surrounding the doorway.

The same sharp hooks blocked street entrances, bridges, and narrow alleys throughout the city. Fortunately, it was late enough that few people noticed the change.

Misty had been running so fast she nearly scraped herself against them. Ethan pulled her back just in time. Shaken, she stared at the sudden obstruction. "What are these?"

Latias called out several times. Gengar translated immediately. "She says it's an ancient defense mechanism, built when her ancestors saved the island!"

"A defense mechanism?" Ethan shook his head. "It looks like our enemies have turned it into a weapon against us."

The hooks were too sharp and densely packed to push through. Fortunately, they had Pokemon to help. One Shadow Ball from Gengar blasted a large hole through the seemingly sturdy barrier.

Outside the Pokemon Center, Ethan surveyed the silent street. He took out a Poke Ball and turned to the others. "Walking will take too long. Let's split up. Erika and I will fly ahead on Dragonite. You find a boat and take the canals."

Misty and Brock knew their own Pokemon could not carry them through the air as Ethan's could. They nodded and ran toward the canal.

As Ash was about to leave with them, Latias caught his clothes in her mouth and indicated that he should climb onto her back.

Ash hesitated only a moment. Knowing how urgent this was, he climbed aboard. Latias spread her wings and took to the air beside Ethan's Dragonite. Together they raced toward the cathedral.
