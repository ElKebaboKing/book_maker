Chapter 676: That Shouldn't Have Happened

After several seconds, Slaking's Hyper Beam faded.

Just as Ethan had hoped, Scizor was still standing. Its body was covered in marks, but its steel-hard shell and determination had carried it through the flood of energy. Ethan finally let out the breath he had been holding.

Good! Scizor, Quick Attack!

Scizor breathed out a puff of dark smoke, then accelerated sharply toward Slaking. Despite the hit it had taken, it had managed to complete Swords Dance.

With its strength raised, Scizor drove its gleaming bladed arms at Slaking from two different angles.

Norman would not let it land an easy blow. Block it!

Slaking swung its heavy hands into position. Scizor used Double Team again, splitting into several images and confusing its judgment.

Slaking looked left and right. Just as before, it could not tell which Scizor was real. With an angry roar, it clawed wildly at the images, trying to break them all. But Scizor was already too close for Slaking to deal with every copy in time.

Its claws scattered two images in front of it. As it turned toward the others, a sharp pain struck its lower back.

Slaking looked around. Scizor had appeared behind it again, in the same place and the same stance as last time. Slaking had guessed wrong once more.

This time, boosted by Swords Dance, Scizor's attack did real damage. It followed up with Fury Cutter, its bladed arms moving so fast they blurred as it struck Slaking again and again.

Slaking cried out in pain and hurriedly dodged away.

Hm? Its reaction surprised Norman. Slaking's defenses were formidable. Even with Swords Dance, Scizor's attacks should not have hurt quite this muchunless...

He understood and looked up at Ethan. Your Scizor has Swarm, doesn't it?

Ethan nodded. That's right.

From the name alone, Swarm sounded to some people like a heightened sense that helped a Pokemon spot and dodge attacks. Its real effect was different. When the Pokemon's stamina fell low, Swarm strengthened its Bug-type moves, much as Blaze, Torrent, and Overgrow strengthened moves of their respective types.

Slaking's Hyper Beam had worn Scizor down enough to bring that Ability into play.

The two Pokemon faced each other again. Both had taken damage in the exchange, and they seemed nearly even once more.

In the stands, Misty swallowed nervously. Even she could see that Petalburg Gym was the toughest Ethan had challenged so far. Metang still needed more training, but Norman was also an exceptionally strong Gym Leader. His experience and Pokemon surpassed those of most Leaders. Judging by Vigoroth and Slaking alone, he was far stronger than Brawly at Dewford Gym.

Ethan assessed the field. Neither Pokemon had much more stamina than the other. The next moment might decide the match.

Almost together, he and Norman called out their orders.

Scizor, X-Scissor!

Slaking, Hammer Arm!

Both Pokemon charged, arms raised. Every eye followed them. Misty and Caroline could no longer stay seated; they stood beside each other, each hoping the person she cared about would win, though neither could say so with the other right there.

Scizor sent another X-shaped cutting wave toward Slaking. This time Slaking knew better than to catch it with its bare palms. With Swords Dance behind the attack, its power had grown too great. Yet Scizor could not simply withstand Slaking's Hammer Arm either.

The moves met in an instant and canceled each other out. Scizor followed with both bladed arms, but Slaking caught them in its hands. Relying on its greater size and strength, it lifted Scizor and threw it away.

Scizor beat its wings in the air, landed steadily, and rushed back, slashing with both arms again.

Everything happened in seconds. The Pokemon traded several blows so quickly that Ethan and the others could barely follow them. The furious pace drained their strength.

At last both slowed, unable to keep up the same intensity. Ethan spotted an opening.

Double Team!

Scizor split into multiple images. Slaking recognized the move that had hurt it twice already. It was frightened of guessing wrong again, and furious enough to want to knock Scizor down before it could ever use the trick once more.

But the question remained: which one was real?

Slaking scanned the images. Having been fooled twice, it thought it knew the answer. It clenched a fist, spun sharply, and punched toward the spot behind it at an angle.

Didn't expect me to turn around, did you? I won't fall for the same trick a third time!

It could almost picture Scizor flying away when its punch landed. The heavy fist struck Scizor square in the faceand passed straight through its head.

Another image. Slaking had guessed wrong again.

How is that possible? It froze in disbelief. Scizor had attacked from that very spot the last two times. Why had it changed position now? It made no sense.

There was no time to think. Slaking heard a blade cut through the air behind its head, but its fist was still extended and it could not dodge. Pain exploded across its back. Its eyes rolled up, and it collapsed forward.

The referee took one look and raised his flag. Slaking is unable to battle! Scizor wins!

It's finally over... Ethan breathed a quiet sigh of relief. That had been hard.

If even he had struggled this much, how many challengers could win a badge here when Norman held nothing back? Then again, a region with Gym Leaders like this would produce stronger Trainers. At the Ever Grande Conference, Ethan might face fewer beginners than he had at the Indigo and Silver Conferences. Hoenn's tournament could be his most challenging yet.

Norman recalled Slaking. He, Caroline, and Misty all applauded.

That was a wonderful battle, Norman said. Scizor's last Double Team trap completely fooled Slaking. Did you train it to do that?

Yes. I developed that tactic earlier. I've trained my other Pokemon that know Double Team to use it the same way.

Mixing real and false attacksthat makes you genuinely hard to predict. You deserve your Champion titles.

After a few more words of praise, Norman took a badge from his jacket and held it out. Please accept the Balance Badge, proof that you've defeated Petalburg Gym.

Ethan thanked him and took it. The badge was an odd shape. Rather than a simple circle, it looked like a pair of scale pans joined togetheror perhaps a set of headphones. Strange taste.

He put the Balance Badge in his badge case, thanked Norman again, and headed for the Gym entrance with Misty.

Oh? Are you leaving already? Caroline sounded disappointed. I thought you might stay a little longer. At least have dinner with us.

Stay longer? Ethan shook his head. If they did, his ears would be ringing from another long conversation.

Thank you for offering, he said politely, but I still have other Gyms to challenge, and I'm short on time. Perhaps we can visit again another day. We could come back when May and Max return.

I understand. Caroline knew a Trainer could not remain in one place for long, and she did not press them. I should thank you for today. Norman hasn't enjoyed a battle this much in a long time. Thank you, truly.

So that's why it was such a difficult battle. Norman was giving it his all...

It made sense. If he fought every challenger with this much strength, hardly anyone would win his badge.

Norman laughed at his wife's disclosure and shook Ethan's hand. Keep going. I have high hopes for you, and I believe you'll win the Ever Grande Conference too. When the day comes, Caroline and I will be cheering you on from in front of the television.

Thank you...

Ethan and Misty waved to Norman and Caroline, who had accompanied them to the door. After adjusting their bags, they left the Gym.

With that, their journey through western Hoenn had come to an end. Ethan had won his fifth badge and taken another step toward the Ever Grande Conference.

It was not yet two in the afternoon. With plenty of daylight left, they walked out of Petalburg City without delay.

After they had gone some distance, Misty said, May's parents are such lovely people. Caroline cooks so well, they're funny, and they're both so stylish...

Ethan stayed quiet and walked beside her. From her tone, he sensed a little envy. It was not a subject he wanted to handle carelessly. As far as he knew, Misty had grown up with her three older sisters, and he had never seen their parents. One wrong comment could hurt her.

Fortunately, Misty had only meant to voice a passing thought. Her smile soon returned. Where are we going next?

Ethan took out his phone and opened the map. The nearest Gym from here is Fortree Gym in Fortree City. But getting there means crossing central Hoenn, and it's a long way on foot.

So we'll take a ship?

He nodded and pointed to another city on the map. Let's sail to Sootopolis City and challenge Sootopolis Gym first. From there, we can travel to the other two Gym cities. The distances and transport connections will be much easier to manage.

Misty had no objection. Ethan had made the decision, and she was happy to follow.

They left Petalburg City and reached the seaside town beside Petalburg Woods before sunset. There they bought tickets for a luxury cruise ship bound directly for Sootopolis City.

It was too late to board that evening. The ship would dock the next morning, take on supplies, and then depart again, so they spent the night near the port.

After dinner, they walked along the shore. Ethan gazed at the sea in the dark and said, Once we're aboard, it may take several days to reach Sootopolis. Could you help me train a Pokemon during the voyage?

Huh? Misty was surprised he had asked her, but she agreed readily. Which one? I can help, but you know I'm best at training Water-type Pokemon. I'm not very good with the others.

That's fine. I want to train Feebas, Ethan said with a smile. I don't have enough Pokemon ready for battle yet. I caught Feebas ages ago, but without a suitable place to train it, I've hardly had the chance. I want to see it evolve into Milotic soon. More than that, Milotic would give me another dependable member of the team. Then I wouldn't have to keep relying on Combusken and Metang in battle.
