Chapter 297: Sprout Tower and Gary Again

After spending the night at the Pokemon Center, Ethan and Sabrina did not leave Violet City right away. They crossed a lake and visited Sprout Tower in the north of the city.

The tower stood within a temple on the outskirts. Away from the noise of the city center, with hills and water nearby and forest all around, it was well placed for quiet religious practice.

Admission was free, and the tower was famous, so many tourists came every day. It was only nine in the morning, though, and the monks had just finished their morning observances. Few visitors had arrived yet.

Ethan and Sabrina walked through the temple gate. Standing among the solemn buildings, Ethan almost felt a sense of meditative calm settle around him. He glanced at Sabrina and laughed at himself.

What calm? Go away. He and Sabrina were inseparable these days, and several younger girls liked him too. With all those ties pulling at him, he was hardly destined for a life of Buddhist detachment.

They walked farther inside. The temple's layout was simpler than he expected. The monks lived in buildings around the edge, and a few were sweeping the courtyard. In the center, a three-story wooden tower rose from a stone platform.

That must be Sprout Tower.

Seeing it in person was a little disappointing. When Ethan had first heard the name, he had imagined six or seven stories. Instead, it looked much like an ordinary wooden pagoda, with wooden walls and blue-gray tiles. It was neither shaped like a Bellsprout nor especially tall.

Gengar popped out of his shadow and looked around. It seemed to share his question.

"Why call it Sprout Tower? It doesn't look like a Bellsprout at all!"

Ethan consulted the travel guide he had picked up at the Pokemon Center. "It says there's a huge pillar inside that sways constantly to keep the tower balanced. Its movement resembles a swaying Bellsprout, so that's where the name comes from."

"A swaying pillar?" Gengar asked. "Wouldn't that make the building unstable? Why design it like that?"

"I'm not entirely sure. The guide doesn't explain it. But I've heard flexible structures like that resist earthquakes well. People used them in ancient buildings, and some modern buildings still use the principle. It looks unsteady, but structurally it works."

As they talked, Ethan and Sabrina climbed the steps and entered through the tower's open door.

Creak.

The wooden floor sounded beneath their feet. The first thing they saw inside was an enormous timber moving back and forth like a pendulum. Nearly two meters wide, it hung down through the upper floor and extended below the ground, swaying in a steady rhythm.

"It's... enormous!"

Curious, Gengar passed through the walls to inspect the floor above, then sank underground. When it returned, it was still muttering in amazement.

"I checked. Nothing is pushing it. It keeps swaying entirely because of the clever way it's built. That's incredible!"

"It is," Ethan agreed.

With Gengar still talking, Ethan and Sabrina took the stairs to the second and third floors. Ethan was disappointed again. Apart from the great timber, Sprout Tower looked like any other wooden tower. There was little else to see.

They had arrived with high expectations and found almost nothing to reward them. Still, it was a famous landmark. Ethan took several photographs as souvenirs.

They lingered until about eleven, when they tried the free vegetarian meal provided by the temple. Simple as it was, the food tasted excellent. It might have been the best part of their visit.

While eating, Ethan noticed that seemingly every monk practicing there had a Bellsprout. Perhaps they had chosen the Pokemon to match the temple's name and accompany them in their training.

When they had eaten their fill, Ethan and Sabrina put their hands together to thank the monks, then left the temple and went straight out of Violet City.

They still had several days before their appointment with Looker. Their next plan was to travel northeast through the vast, dense forest to Blackthorn City and challenge its Gym. Afterward, they would turn west to Mahogany Town to meet him.

They had walked along the broad road for half an hour and were nearing the forest when a red sports car appeared ahead.

It sped past Ethan, raising a trail of dust. Before the dust settled, the car reversed and came back. The scene was so familiar that Ethan instinctively looked inside.

There, in the passenger seat, sat an old acquaintance about Ash's age.

"Gary?"

Of course it was Gary Oak. As before, the car was full of young women in revealing outfits. Gary looked just as surprised to see Ethan.

"Ethan? I didn't expect to run into you here!"

"I'm surprised too." Ethan waved, then glanced at the car. "It has been a while, but you haven't changed. Still traveling by sports car?"

"Naturally." Gary folded his arms and lifted his chin. "It's the fastest way to travel. You walkers can't compare!"

Seeing Gary reminded Ethan of Ash. "If you're here, have you seen Ash?"

Gary snorted. "We parted at the laboratory yesterday. He set off in this direction too, but at his pace he's probably still trudging across the Indigo Plateau."

Only as far as the Indigo Plateau? That really was slow.

Ethan turned his attention back to Gary. As he had when they first met, he took a Poke Ball from his pouch and tossed it lightly in his hand.

"Since it's been so long, how about a battle right now?"

Gary shook his head. "Now? No. Our next big match has to wait until the Silver Conference. I won't lose to you again. This time you'll find out what defeat feels like!"

He gave Ethan no chance to reply. At his signal, the young woman driving pressed the accelerator, and the sports car sped away.

Vroom! Dust rose again.

Ethan watched the car shrink into the distance and smiled. Strong rivals were appearing one after another. With them and the opponents he had yet to meet, the Silver Conference promised to be lively indeed.
