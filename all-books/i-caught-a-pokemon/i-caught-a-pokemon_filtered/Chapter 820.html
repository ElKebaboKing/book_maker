Chapter 820: The Fairy Jessibella

More than accomplished, Harley said with a smile. I placed very highly at the Hoenn Grand Festival!

That was only half true. He had entered the Grand Festival, but he had been eliminated in the top thirty-two. It was hardly a lofty finish. Lying came so naturally to him, however, that nothing in his voice gave him away. He sounded every bit as confident as Jessie, and she and her companions did not doubt him.

Oh? Then you're not bad, Jessie said, surprised that he might be so skilled. She laughed. Still, you've got a long way to go before you can compare with the fairy Jessibella!

Naturally, naturally. Harley rubbed his hands together, all flattery. I heard your name even in Hoenn! You're entering the Saffron Contest too, aren't you?

Of course. Jessie smiled smugly. A little Contest like this is nothing to me. If I enter, first place is as good as mine!

Few people could boast with Jessie's utter certainty. Because she seemed to believe every word, Harley believed her too. The apparent entourage of assistants only strengthened his impression that she was a formidable Coordinator.

How impressive! Let me congratulate you on your victory in advance.

After a dozen more compliments, Jessie was so pleased with herself that she had forgotten the limits of her actual ability. Harley made himself seem humble. Simpleminded Jessie took it as proof that he was captivated by her charm and had become a fan.

Once he had laid the groundwork, Harley raised his real purpose.

Well... He looked troubled. If you wouldn't mind, could you help me with a small problem?

A small problem? Jessie agreed without thinking. Of course! Do you want my autograph?

Harley froze. He nearly said no, but an autograph would make him look more like a genuine fan and help his plan. He forced himself to nod.

Yes, exactly. Though the autograph is only part of it. I really do need Miss Jessibella's help with something.

He searched his pockets for something she could sign. Finding nothing else, he reluctantly handed her his Kanto Contest pass.

Jessie produced a pen from somewhere and scrawled her name across it in large letters. Her handwriting twisted and lurched without the slightest grace. Could a person really write so badly? Was she actually a celebrity?

Harley's eyebrow twitched. He could not say that to her face. He pretended delight and carefully tucked the pass away.

Thank you so much. I'll treasure it!

At least Jessie remembered that he had asked for help. She tossed the pen to James, then turned to Harley with a broad smile. Now, what does the great Jessibella need to do for you?

Excitement glinted in Harley's eyes. He had been waiting for this.

It's simple, he said, trying not to laugh. A nasty little girl has been giving me trouble. She bullies me all the time. If you meet her in the Contest, could you teach her a lesson for me?

A girl in the Contest? Jessie had not expected that request, but she waved it off. Don't worry. That's just the sort of thing we're best at She coughed. I mean, leave it to me!

After leaving the registration hall, Ethan and the others went straight to the Saffron Contest Hall to wait with May.

The Contest had not begun, but spectators were already arriving. Within a few minutes, the seats were full. The competitors' waiting room was growing crowded too. It was becoming difficult to talk or do much else there, so Ash, Max, and Brock gave May their last good wishes and went to the stands. Ethan stayed with her.

He had promised to prevent Harley from interfering again. Yet Harley never came near May as the opening drew closer. Neither did Drew, whom they had seen yesterday.

Drew's absence meant little, but Harley's was unusual. Ethan did not believe for a moment that he had given up. Suspecting another scheme, he became more careful. He checked the schedule, May's clothes and props, and even her Poke Balls himself.

Such attentive care moved May. Her gratitude kept growing, though she said nothing and held the feeling quietly in her heart. Some people could change a person's feelings without being noticed. Ethan was more like sunlight: she could see and feel the warmth of everything he did for her.

She watched him go back and forth, checking each detail. Her heart settled, and her last trace of nervousness faded. When Ethan returned, she gave him a big smile.

He looked baffled. What is it? What's making you so happy?

Nothing. May shook her head lightly. I just think you'd be well suited to entering a Pokemon Contest.

Counting Ash, that made two people who had suggested it. Ethan wondered what it was about him that made them say so. Was it only because he had beautiful Pokemon like Articuno and Milotic?

Because I've learned the whole process? he asked with a smile. Trainers and Coordinators have a lot in common, but they're still different paths. I've been a Trainer a long time, and I've achieved a little success. Starting over as a novice Coordinator would feel strange.

May understood his meaning. As a champion, he was reluctant to enter a Contest as a beginner. Pokemon Contests were not decided solely by battling strength. Judges made subjective calls, which brought uncertainty. Winning would be wonderful, but losing might embarrass him.

May thought he was worrying too much. Performances and battles were different things. A loss in one did not diminish what he had achieved in the other. Yet she only smiled sweetly and did not press him. Ethan had his own reasons. If he did not want to enter, she would not bring it up again.

There was nothing wrong with concentrating on battles, either. He could give that path his full attention and climb still higher.

As they talked, the Saffron Contest began. Like the other competitors in the waiting room, Ethan and May turned to the large screen on the wall.

Brilliant lights swept the stage. Lilian, the host they had met yesterday, stepped into the spotlight and took command of the hall.

She waved to the audience and spoke over lively music. It's showtime! Pokemon and Coordinators are here to mix love and dreams into one spectacular performance! Let's bring the Saffron Contest to life! I'm your host, Lilian. Come along and enjoy it with me!

Under the spotlights, Lilian looked even more dazzling than she had the day before. Her youthful energy and lovely face caught everyone's attention.

A wave of cheers rose from the stands. The audience seemed eager for this rare Saffron Contest.

The camera operator thoughtfully swept the crowd, showing their reactions on the screen. By coincidence, Ethan spotted Brock the moment the camera passed him. He stood out like a firefly in the dark.

Brock was not making his usual extravagant gestures or staring with hearts in his eyes. Even so, his lovestruck, fidgeting manner set him apart from the people around him.

I feel like... May scratched her cheek awkwardly. Brock is being a little...

Embarrassing? Ethan laughed. I agree. Falling for someone so openly, no matter where he is, takes a confidence I could never match. Though I have to admit, his taste is good.

They were only joking, not trying to hurt Brock. They would have said the same thing to his face. That was the ease of traveling together.

Lilian introduced the Contest's prize: a gleaming Saffron Ribbon. Its elaborate shape resembled a shield topped with a crown. Every competitor looked at it longingly. The Ribbon was an honor and a ticket to a greater stage.

Once she had explained the rules, Lilian introduced the judges. Ethan might have expected a different panel now that the stage had moved from Hoenn to Kanto. Instead, he recognized every face.

There was Mr. Contesta, head of the Pokemon Contest Committee; Mr. Sukizo, president of the Pokemon Fan Club; and Nurse Joy.

This Nurse Joy might be from Saffron rather than Hoenn. Brock was probably the only one who could tell which Joy she was.

Seeing familiar judges eased May's mind. She knew Contesta's and Sukizo's preferences after so many Contests and could plan her performance accordingly. A new panel would have been harder to read.

There was a drawback too. Familiar judges made competitors less nervous, but those judges had seen their acts many times. Without something striking or original, it would be harder to earn a high score. Familiarity cut both ways.

After introducing all three, Lilian stirred the audience into another round of cheers. The first round of judging finally began.

To Ethan's surprise, Harley was the first performer.

So that's why he wasn't in the waiting room, Ethan said, shaking his head. He didn't have time to make trouble.

Harley's unusual outfit drew immediate attention and applause as he came onstage. The cheers filled the hall.

The Saffron audience seemed more enthusiastic than those at the Hoenn Contests. Perhaps they simply enjoyed performances more, or perhaps Lilian was particularly good at exciting a crowd. Either way, Harley received a warm welcome as the first act.

At center stage, he released his partner. Even in a new region, he had chosen the same Pokemon: Cacturne.

Harley's character left much to be desired, and he never missed a chance to target May. But he had skill and a clever mind. His stage performances rarely went wrong. Working in step with Cacturne, he gave Kanto's audience a fine show.

For the finale, Cacturne used Needle Arm to pierce the Cotton Spore drifting above the stage. It made a striking finish and earned Harley a very high score in the first round.
