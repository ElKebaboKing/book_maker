Chapter 703: Sacrifice and Premonition

What is that?

The Team Magma guards stared at the unfamiliar Pokemon. Could it be an extremely rare species? No human appeared behind it, so it might even be wild. They exchanged eager looks, each thinking of catching it first.

Their captain studied it. Confusion gave way to recognition, and his face changed. No! That's Deoxys! We're under attack!

He knew more than the ordinary guards. He had not joined the force sent against Ethan and Deoxys, but he had heard the survivors talk about that battle many times. He had watched the footage they brought back, too. Ethan's Pokemon had astonished him, and Deoxys had stayed in his memory. Some members later speculated that Ethan had caught it after the others withdrew.

The recognition had taken him a moment. Now all those reports rushed back at once.

What? The other guards did not know what Deoxys was, but they understood under attack. They reached for their Poke Balls.

Deoxys struck first. It did not even change form. Energy gathered before its chest, formed a sphere, and shot into the group. The blast took all of them out of the fight.

Wailing alarms sounded throughout the base.

Chaos broke out. The combat guards moved toward the entrance to stop the intruder. Noncombat staff began packing and preparing to flee. A secret base ceased to be secret once an enemy found it. Even if Team Magma repelled this attack, they could no longer use the place.

Outside, Ethan stepped from behind a tree with Gardevoir and Scizor protecting him. He walked toward the tunnel. Deoxys alone could break through every defense and force open the base.

Waves of guards rushed out, their Pokemon attacking as fiercely as they could. Deoxys overwhelmed them. It advanced at a measured pace, leaving unconscious people and Pokemon behind it, along with the marks of its energy blasts.

News of the breach soon reached Maxie.

They found us after all. He was surprised by the speed of the counterattack, but not shocked. His exhilaration over the Red Orb had subsided, and he could think clearly again. He made his decision at once: abandon the base.

He regretted losing it, but the orb mattered far more. Once Team Magma controlled Groudon, they could build as many new bases as they wanted. This one was a small loss beside Groudon's strategic value.

Prepare the submarine, he told a nearby member. Evacuate the noncombat staff. Move any files we can carry and destroy the rest.

Yes, sir!

...

Sector One has fallen!

They've breached Sector Two!

The intruder is in Sector Three and engaging the guards!

Ah!

He's too strong. We can't stop him!

Fall back! Fall back!

Sector Three is lost! He's heading for Sector Four!

Orders, screams, and explosions crowded Team Magma's communication channels until voices blurred together. One fact remained clear: the intruder was driving deep into the base, and no one could hold him.

Scizor struck a heavy blast door with X-Scissor. Its pincers cut the door into four sections. As the pieces fell, Ethan entered the inner part of the base.

He had added Blaziken, Metang, and Eevee to the Pokemon beside him. The fighting would give them experience, and they could protect his rear from surprise attacks. Deoxys led far ahead like a spearpoint, clearing everyone who resisted from the front. Ethan's other Pokemon dealt with stragglers and any counterattack. With that formation, he had crossed the first three sectors easily.

The layout grew complicated near the core. Ethan had never been here before and no longer knew which way to go. He wanted Maxie, but had seen neither him nor either commander. They were probably keeping out of his path on purpose.

Gengar emerged through a wall. It made an okay sign with one hand. All done. I slipped into their control room and cut the circuits. I don't think they've even realized there's a problem.

Good work. Ethan had one less threat to worry about. Do you know where Maxie is?

I heard a call while I was in there. He's gone to some underground harbor. Sounds like he's leaving on a submarine.

So there was a hidden harbor after all. Ethan had missed it from outside. If he had entered through it, he might have blocked their escape completely. But he knew about it now, and he could still catch them before they left or soon afterward.

Do you know the way?

Not exactly, but I know roughly which sector has the entrance.

Then lead me there.

Gengar guided Ethan in a new direction. He drove his Pokemon forward faster, aware that time was running out. As they approached the harbor route, the guards fought harder. That told him they had chosen the right way. Team Magma was making its last stand, but Deoxys broke through it.

...

An enormous orange submarine waited in Team Magma's underground harbor.

One might imagine that Team Magma, with its interest in land, volcanoes, and magma, would have no use for a submarine. But it was one of Hoenn's major criminal organizations. It needed ways to travel. It also owned helicopters, though their conspicuous size kept them grounded much of the time.

This submarine was more than a simple transport. Its orange hull and unusual shape resembled a Crawdaunt. A huge drill protruded from the bow, promising considerable destructive power.

Maxie entered the harbor through several heavy doors with Courtney, Tabitha, and dozens of elite members. A member waiting by the dock hurried to meet him.

Boss, the submarine is ready. We can depart whenever you say.

Maxie nodded to the people behind him. Board.

You heard him. Come on! Tabitha led the members up the gangway. Courtney was about to follow when Maxie stopped her.

Wait.

She turned. You stay here, he said.

Courtney stared, startled. Before she could speak, Maxie continued. That man will arrive soon. We can't let him pursue us. Hold him here and buy time for what comes next.

But I... I can't stop him.

Then delay him. Maxie tossed her a controller. You've dealt with him longer than anyone else. You can manage that much.

Courtney caught it and recognized the base's self-destruct control. Pressing it would set off explosions throughout the facility. No one inside would escape.

He wants me to die with Ethan? Has he cast me aside?

She looked up. Maxie's eyes were calm. There was no pity, regret, or other feeling in them. She understood his words as a judgment on her failures: the earlier operations, and now her lookouts missing Ethan on Mt. Pyre. Team Magma's predicament had followed from her mistakes.

After several silent seconds, she nodded. I understand. You have my word: I won't let him leave here alive.

Maxie passed her with his hands behind his back and climbed aboard. As he reached the hatch, Courtney called from the dock.

Boss! She looked up at him, her voice full of expectation rather than fear. I look forward to humanity's progress. Change the world!

Maxie stopped and looked down at her. Everything will unfold as I've planned. Until we meet again.

He entered the submarine. Its hatch closed slowly, separating them like the walls of two different worlds.

Boom!

An explosion sounded in the distance. The fighting was still going on, and by the sound of it Ethan was getting closer. Courtney stood watching the submarine start and sink until it vanished beneath the water. The ripples had barely settled when a series of blasts shook the harbor entrance.

Deoxys floated through rolling smoke. Ethan hurried in behind it, guarded by his other Pokemon.

He searched the harbor for Maxie. Only Courtney stood on the dock. The last ripples on the water were disappearing.

Too late? Ethan walked toward her. Where's Maxie?

Courtney did not seem to hear. She stared at the flashing alarm light on the wall and murmured, The red warning lights are beautiful. I can see the wonderful world after our dream comes true.

Ethan frowned. Was she ill? She was talking nonsense. Her eyes brightened, and a strange smile spread across her face. Beneath her usually calm manner lay something wild. Had that always been there?

Then her expression changed. Her eyes widened as though she had seen something terrifying.

Groudon, beyond anyone's control. Destruction. Fury. Even the boss dying at its hands...

What is this? Why would it turn out that way?

She came back to herself before she could understand the vision. Deoxys was right in front of her. Only then did she seem to grasp that Ethan had arrived.
