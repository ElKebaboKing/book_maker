Chapter 188: I'm Actually from Another World

Perhaps there were too many tourists on Sunburst Island. Every hotel was full that night. After a long search, Ethan and Sabrina found a somewhat shabby guesthouse at the edge of town.

Its proprietor was a kindly, short woman with silver hair who wore traditional clothes. Seeing the well-matched pair, she took them for a couple and prepared one room. Ethan explained, and she cheerfully found Sabrina a separate room.

"Oh, forgive me," the old woman said with a smile. "These eyes aren't what they used to be. But I've met a great many people, and when I was young, I brought quite a few couples together. The two of you really do suit each other."

Ethan expected Sabrina to be embarrassed. When he glanced over, she looked entirely at ease.

For Sabrina, that was the strange part. In the past, a remark she disliked could have earned its speaker a punishment from her psychic powers. Even after Ash and Haunter had softened her manner, she would not have tolerated someone talking freely about her like this.

Ethan was not oblivious. After so much time together, he could tell Sabrina felt something for him. He suspected their growing closeness had changed her.

But he had come from another world and could not know whether he might suddenly be sent back. He also could not be sure what Sabrina's feelings meant, or how deep they ran. He had never dared to say anything himself. If he had misunderstood, he might lose her friendship.

He believed that if they were meant to be together, a moment would come when it happened naturally.

They slept, enjoyed a good breakfast, and set out on Charizard the next morning.

Sabrina looked down from the sky. All she could see was thick forest and several uninhabited islets near Sunburst Island.

"How do you plan to find the Crystal Onix?"

Ethan patted Charizard's neck and directed it higher. "I don't have much of a plan. There's a place I'd like to check, and I hope we get lucky."

"Get lucky?" Sabrina shook her head.

She did not expect much from this search. Lorelei had helped them find Moltres in the Sevii Islands because she knew the area. Here they had almost no information and no guide. It was like looking for a needle in a haystack. Sabrina would not have known where to begin.

Ethan seemed confident, though, so she held her doubts. She wanted to see for herself whether an Onix made of crystal existed.

Charizard climbed until all of Sunburst Island lay beneath them. People in town looked as small as ants. Ethan asked it to fly steadily while he leaned over and searched the islands below.

He ruled out several possibilities. Then he spotted a small island that seemed almost joined to Sunburst. It showed no sign of people. Dense forest surrounded a bare hill, making it look much like the other empty islets. To Ethan, one detail set it apart.

That was the one. He told Charizard to fly toward it.

A narrow channel separated the two islands. Through the clear water, he could make out a sandbar formed by deposits beneath the surface. At high tide it was invisible, and few people on Sunburst Island knew it was there. Only when the water receded far enough did it briefly become a path between the islands.

Charizard circled the bare hill. Within moments, Ethan found a well-concealed cave entrance.

"I think that's where we need to go!"

Sabrina heard the excitement in his voice. She had been wondering something since Mt. Ember.

"How did you know there was a cave here? And how did you know about Moltres? Where are you getting all this information?"

Ethan chuckled. "I'll tell you the truth. I came here from another world. Before that happened, I learned about these places from games and an animated series. That's why I seem to know what's coming."

Sabrina gave his shoulder a light thump, half amused and half annoyed. "If you don't want to tell me, fine. You don't have to make up something so ridiculous."

Ethan shrugged and smiled to himself. Sometimes the truth sounded too extraordinary to believe, while an invented story was easier for people to accept.

Charizard folded its wings and landed at the cave mouth. Ethan jumped down first, then naturally offered Sabrina a hand.

Neither could say when such touches had become familiar. They still made their hearts quicken, but they no longer questioned them. When no one else was around, Sabrina seemed more willing to talk with Ethan. Sometimes they caught each other's eyes and quickly looked away. Neither spoke of the feeling growing between them.

Ethan released Gardevoir and called Haunter from his shadow. With them on guard, he switched on a flashlight and led the way into the cave.

Drip. Water fell from the roof at intervals, splashing against the rock below.

The entrance was narrow. They had gone only a dozen steps when the flashlight found crystals exposed in the walls. They threw back sparkling points of light.

"It's beautiful," Sabrina said.

Seeing the crystals, she began to think that if the Crystal Onix was real, this might well be its hiding place.
