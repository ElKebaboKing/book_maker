Chapter 357: A Three-on-Three Free-for-All

Before anyone realized it, the time had reached ten in the morning. Ethan and the others remained in the dining room, waiting for the experts Officer Jenny had mentioned.

Seated beside the window, Ethan looked through the glass and saw a female television reporter broadcasting live in front of the Pokemon Center.

"...We now return to our earlier report. We have arrived in the heart of Greenfield, a peaceful area long known for its abundant greenery, where an unprecedented phenomenon has occurred! The source of the incident has been confirmed as the home of Professor Spencer Hale, a renowned Johto researcher. Details remain unclear at present... We are awaiting the arrival of experts and hope they can..."

The news feed had only a few seconds of delay. Before the reporter even finished speaking outside, Ethan was already watching the same report on the dining room television.

By now, the entire Johto region probably knew about the incident. Any development here would be relayed by the reporters to countless viewers watching from home.

After another ten minutes of waiting and several cups of coffee, Lisa apparently grew bored and approached Ethan.

"Hey, that younger girl over there said all your Pokemon are strong. Since we're only waiting around, how about battling me?"

The mention of a battle instantly energized Ash. He left his seat and hurried over, staring at Ethan with shining eyes.

"Ethan! Honestly, I've wanted another battle with you for a long time. Heh, I've caught plenty of dependable partners since coming to Johto. This is the perfect chance to show you!"

Looking at their confident expressions, Ethan rose and stretched.

"Sure. Waiting like this is getting boring. Let's have a battle. But if both of you want to challenge me, I don't know whom to accept first. How about this? Since there are so many of us here today, let's hold a three-on-three team battle!"

"Three against three?"

Ethan nodded. "There are exactly six of us. We can split into two teams and have a fun free-for-all!"

"Yes!" Misty agreed first, nearly bouncing with excitement. "I want to be on Ethan's team!"

The idea interested Ash and the others as well. They had fought one-on-one and two-on-two battles before, but none of them had ever tried three against three.

Brock had somehow recovered his spirits. Apparently eager to repay Ethan for teasing him, he rushed over with an approving expression.

"In that case, I'll join Ash's team!"

Seeing everyone else's enthusiasm, Erika felt too embarrassed to refuse. After a brief hesitation, she joined Ethan's team.

That naturally left Lisa on Ash's side.

With the teams decided, they left the Pokemon Center and went to the outdoor battlefield behind it. Each side took its place in a Trainer command area.

Misty stood to Ethan's left and Erika to his right. Across from them, Ash occupied the middle, with Brock and Lisa on either side.

Apart from Ethan and Erika, everyone eagerly prepared for the battle ahead.

Erika was usually dignified and would never have joined this kind of match under ordinary circumstances. Yet surrounded by this group, even her mood had unknowingly become more lively.

Noticing the change in herself, Erika sighed softly.

Oh well... I'll consider it a chance to relax.

Ash clenched his fists eagerly. "Ethan, what are the rules?"

Ethan thought for a moment. "Each person sends out two Pokemon, giving each team six Pokemon on the field at once. The team with at least one Pokemon still standing at the end wins!"

"Got it!"

Misty leaned over and warned Ethan quietly, "Be careful. Lisa is really strong. When she battled Ash earlier, their match went all the way to the sixth Pokemon. Ash only managed to win by luck!"

Oh? She fought Ash to a draw until the final round?

Ethan nodded, then smiled at Misty.

"Don't worry. Our opponents may be strong, but we're no weaker!"

With no further questions, Ethan signaled the start of the match. Both teams released their Pokemon at once.

Ethan chose Larvitar and Dragonair. Erika sent out Leafeon and Tangela.

Misty had originally intended to use Starmie and Poliwag. But immediately after she released Starmie, before she could even reach for her second Poke Ball, blue light flashed. Psyduck disobeyed her and emerged on its own.

Looking at the vacant expression of a Psyduck that did not even understand the situation, Misty pressed a hand to her forehead.

"You idiot! Why did you sneak out again!?"

She tried to recall Psyduck, but by sheer coincidence it hid behind several of the other Pokemon. After two failed attempts to catch it in the return beam, Misty's temper flared. Giving up entirely, she simply let Psyduck join the battle.

Across the field, Brock sent out Onix and Golbat.

Lisa chose the Aipom at her side and a Quagsire.

Ash sent out Pikachu and an unusually colored Noctowl.

The Noctowl was clearly a Shiny Pokemon.

Ordinary Noctowl had brown plumage, while Ash's Shiny Noctowl was golden.

Sunlight shone across its feathers, making their golden luster exceptionally dazzling.

Ethan could not deny that he was envious.

Even Ash had caught a Shiny Pokemon. Ethan had traveled for so long, yet he had never encountered a single one.

Sigh. There's nothing to be done. Finding a Shiny Pokemon truly depends on chance.

Ash's Noctowl was somewhat smaller than an ordinary member of its species. But because Ethan had seen the anime, he knew it possessed exceptional intelligence to compensate.

At that moment, Erika pointed to her Leafeon and thanked Ethan.

"Look. Thanks to the Leaf Stone you gave me, my Eevee was able to evolve into Leafeon!"

The Leaf Stone Erika mentioned was the prize Ethan won from the three Eevee brothers after defeating them when he passed through Stone Town.

He had tossed it into the bottom of his backpack and later gave it to Erika when he encountered her in the wilderness. Ethan had forgotten the matter until she mentioned it again.

"You don't need to thank me." Ethan smiled. "It's better for an item to be put to good use. It wasn't helping anyone in my possession, so I'm glad it benefited you!"
