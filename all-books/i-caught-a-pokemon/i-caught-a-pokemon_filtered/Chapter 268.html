Chapter 268: The First Win

While Drake weighed the risks of Thrash, Ethan's Gyarados opened its massive jaws. Saliva still clung to its sharp teeth as it lunged for Ditto's neck.

Drake had no time to dwell on his doubts. "Ditto, dodge!"

Ditto was already trying to evade. At his shout, it twisted aside just in time. Gyarados's jaws snapped shut inches from its neck.

But missing the bite did not put any distance between them. They continued struggling in the pool, churning water against the surrounding rocks and throwing up spray.

Then Gyarados lunged again and drove its head into Ditto. This time Ditto could not get away. It rocked under the blow and let out a pained grunt.

Drake saw the problem. Their Aqua Tails had brought them too close together. Ditto's copied body was too large to slip quickly out of Gyarados's reach the way a small Pokemon might. If it could not escape, it would have to fight at close range. Ditto had become a Gyarados too; Drake believed it could match the original's ferocity.

"Ditto, use Bite! Hold it in place!"

Ditto opened its jaws and bit into Gyarados's neck. With its mind lost in Thrash, Gyarados made no attempt to dodge. It roared and writhed as the teeth sank in.

"Good!" Drake's tense expression eased. For an instant, he thought Ditto had secured the advantage.

He and Ditto had underestimated Ethan's Gyarados. Driven by pain, it did not retreat. It twisted sharply even with its neck caught in Ditto's mouth and bit back into Ditto's neck. The two huge Pokemon coiled around one another like pythons. Each clamped down harder; neither would release its grip.

Ditto had copied Gyarados's body, but it had spent its life fighting orderly Trainer battles. It had never endured this kind of raw struggle, with an opponent willing to take injury to deal it. After only a few seconds, the pain became too much. Ditto opened its mouth.

"No!" Drake saw his Pokemon falling behind after just a few exchanges. "Ditto, use Thrash too!"

If Gyarados became so dangerous when it fought without restraint, perhaps Ditto had to do the same to stand against it. Ditto obeyed. Its eyes reddened, and it abandoned reason, striking wildly.

To everyone's surprise, it still could not move Gyarados. Ethan's Pokemon pinned it beneath its body. Ditto's struggles achieved nothing and brought on still harsher bites. Sharp teeth tore through the scales of its transformed body, and blood poured into the pool, turning a wide patch of water red.

Isn't this too much? Many spectators stared, horrified.

"Why? Why? Why?" The announcer stood and braced his hands on the desk, his voice almost a shriek. "They both look like Gyarados and they're using the same moves! Why can't Drake's Ditto fight back against Ethan's?"

The crowd asked the same question. From appearances, this was a contest between identical Pokemon, yet Ditto was plainly losing the close fight. The answer, perhaps, was clear only to Ethan.

His Gyarados was not winning with clever tactics or elegant moves. It was displaying the fury inside it and the brutal power it had learned to use. Ditto could copy its form, but not that experience. Ethan's Gyarados had fought through blood and pain. It was nothing like an ordinary Gyarados.

Ditto finally lost consciousness under the assault. Its borrowed form gave way, leaving its small purple body in Gyarados's grasp. Even then, Gyarados kept attacking.

"Enough! Stop!" Drake shouted.

Ethan looked at him. "You know Gyarados has lost control. It won't listen to anyone right now, not even me."

Drake knew he was right. He raised Ditto's Poke Ball, waited for an opening, and recalled it from across the field.

With the outcome decided, the referee raised the green flag to award the first round to Ethan.

"I can hardly believe it," the announcer said. "Ditto has defended Drake's opening position against so many challengers, yet it has fallen in this first round! Ethan's Gyarados was unstoppable. Congratulations to Ethan Vale on taking the lead!" There was a trace of reluctance in his voice.

Sparse applause came from the stands. Most spectators were still shaken by what they had seen. The broadcast carried the same images to Kanto, where the reaction was quite different.

At their clubhouse, the biker gang erupted in cheers. "He won! The boss is amazing!" "That Gyarados is incredible!" "So much for the Orange League's Supreme Gym Leader!"

There was laughter and celebration at Cerise Laboratory too.

Ethan recalled his still-raging Gyarados, giving it time in its Poke Ball to rest and recover from the effects of Thrash.

Across the field, Drake regarded him seriously. "You deserve that Indigo League title. Your Gyarados is the most... formidable one I've ever seen."
