Chapter 559: The Final SixteenFacing Casey

Early the next morning, Ethan left the lodgings with Erika, refreshed and in excellent spirits. They stopped outside the competitors' entrance to Silver Stadium.

The final tournament would use full six-on-six battles. Each Trainer could field six Pokemon, leaving more room to recover from a mistake than in the previous days' matches and offering a better measure of the strength of an entire team.

For the spectators, more battles to watch were an even better reason to welcome the change.

Before Ethan went in, he happened to meet Casey at the entrance. Her expression grew complicated when she saw him, but she still walked up to him, fidgeting.

"Mr. Ethan..." She wished she could call him Ethan the way Misty did, with a more familiar form of address, but she had never found the right moment to make that change. They were not as close as Ethan and Misty. "I never thought we'd be opponents. I... I barely slept last night. I don't know how to face today's match. I really don't want to meet you like this..."

"That doesn't sound like you." Ethan saw the dark circles under her eyes and shook his head, interrupting her. "The Casey I know doesn't dwell on things like this. I'd rather see you full of energy, the way you usually are."

"Full of energy... me?" Casey's eyes widened. She had not expected him to say that, and she felt a rush of warmth.

He was right. She was so unlike her usual self that she could hardly recognize herself.

"That's right." Ethan smiled. "Don't put any pressure on yourself. Treat it like a practice match, or any ordinary battle. Whatever the result, give everything you've worked for a chance to show. Don't let your nerves leave you regretting today's match!"

He was her opponent, yet he was trying to encourage her. Moved, Casey pressed a hand to her chest and declared in her strong Kansai accent, "I understand! I'll give this battle my very best! And... and you do your best too!"

She bowed deeply, gave Ethan one last look, then ran off with a blush on her face.

Erika laughed softly as she watched her go.

She was not jealous of a child, nor upset that Ethan had encouraged a young girl in front of her.

She was amused because his words had restored Casey's confidence in an instant. The girl would probably fight all the harder now. Ethan had created trouble for himself that he need not have faced.

Then again, his willingness to do it showed how confident he was in his own strength.

Ethan and Erika entered through the competitors' tunnel and went to a private waiting room.

When Ethan's match began, Erika would not have to sit in the stands. She could stay here and watch.

Ethan had specially requested it from the League. In fact, every Trainer who reached the final sixteen received better accommodations. They had private rooms to prepare in, where friends and family could watch the matches and wait for them afterward.

The battles had also moved from the four smaller arenas into the main Silver Stadium. It had better equipment, held a larger crowd, and drew far more attention.

In the waiting room, the atmosphere grew heavier as the start of Ethan's match approached.

He stopped joking with Erika. Instead, he opened recordings of Casey's earlier matches on the computer and reviewed the Pokemon she had used.

For a Trainer who had only recently begun her journey, Casey had grown enormously.

Across her previous matches, she had used five Pokemon: Meganium, Pidgey, Rattata, Beedrill, and Elekid. She had suffered setbacks now and then, but her team was strong overall.

Reaching the final sixteen as a newcomer showed real talent. At the very least, she had matched Ash's result from the Indigo League.

After skimming several of Casey's matches, Ethan lay back on the sofa and turned to the waiting room's large screen.

The day's first match was underway.

One of the competitors was Travis, who had been placed in the other half of the bracket. His opponent was another Trainer from that half, Jon Dickson.

Their battle was nearing its end. Ethan had fought Travis before and knew his ability. Though Travis was about Ash's age, he was a little stronger than an average Trainer. Yet Jon was overwhelming him.

That surprised Ethan and made him wary of Jon Dickson.

He had not seen everyone else battle yet, but he had a feeling Jon might win that entire half of the bracket.

Erika had noticed his strength too. She frowned. "He's worth watching. I think he's a local Johto Trainer, and I've heard spectators say he's one of this tournament's favorites, apart from you."

Ethan nodded and committed Jon Dickson's name to memory.

On the screen, Travis's Pikachu was fighting Jon's Rapidash. Pikachu was extremely agile, but Rapidash was fast too, and its charges carried tremendous force. It soon drove Pikachu to the brink.

"He's going to lose..." Erika murmured, watching the battle turn against Travis.

She was right. Pikachu resisted fiercely, but in the end it fell beneath Rapidash's blazing hooves.

Travis had defeated only one of Jon's Pokemon in the entire match. The gap between them was unmistakable.

On the screen, Travis gathered Pikachu into his arms, looking desolate. He had traveled across the sea from the Orange Islands to Johto, but his Silver Conference journey had to end here.

He had not achieved the result he wanted. He would have to return to the Orange Islands disappointed and full of regret.

Ethan had no time to dwell on it. At the announcement over the speakers, he rose and headed for the battlefield.

His match with Casey was next. One of them would follow Travis out of Silver Stadium, carrying their own disappointment.
