Chapter 258: The Winner's Cup

"Thank you."

After hearing Quackenpoker's reasons, Ethan accepted the Gyaradosite and slipped it into his pocket. He did not yet know how to use its power, but he already had a Mega Stone. It was a promising start, putting him one step ahead.

"Well, I need some sleep," Quackenpoker said. "Perhaps our paths will cross again." He turned and lifted the flap of his tent.

Tracey took a step toward him. Pokemon Watchers were few and far between, and despite their brief acquaintance, he felt a natural kinship with Quackenpoker. The old man said they might meet again, but at his age, and with their travels taking them in different directions, that seemed unlikely.

"Mr. Quackenpoker," Tracey called earnestly, "please take care of yourself!"

Quackenpoker paused. He did not turn around, but waved over his shoulder before ducking inside. Once the tent flap fell, the camp grew quiet.

Ethan and the others picked up their luggage and followed the river back to the coast. The Gyarados and Magikarp were already out of sight. After everything they had been through together, it was time for their own paths to part.

Misty found this goodbye harder than the last few. She still had to travel with Ash, though. Their friendship meant she could not simply abandon him.

She looked at Ethan, lips parting as she tried to find the words. Ash suddenly barged between them.

"Ethan! I'm heading straight for the Kumquat Gym. Just wait! I'll catch up to you soon!"

Misty nearly stumbled. Gritting her teeth, she brought her fist down on Ash's head.

Thump!

"Ow! Misty, why'd you hit me?"

Ash looked genuinely bewildered. He had no idea what he had interrupted. Misty could only glare at him and mutter that he was hopelessly dense.

"Nothing. My hand was itching, and I felt like hitting you!"

"Misty! That's awful!" Ash protested.

Their familiar bickering brought a warmth to the farewell. Ethan smiled. "Ash, there's no need to rush. Traveling is how you learn. Take it one step at a time and give yourself room to grow."

"I know, but..." Ash clenched a fist. "You're the champion. Of course I want to catch up to you!"

"You?" Misty scoffed. She was Ethan's staunchest supporter. "You'll never catch up to Ethan. Give it up."

"Champion, huh..." Ethan rested a hand briefly on Misty's head, then looked out across the sea. "The Indigo League wasn't as demanding as it could have been. I was lucky enough not to face an opponent who truly pushed me, so I won. To be honest, I think I should be aiming for you instead, Ash."

"What?" Everyone turned to him in surprise.

Ash pointed to himself. "Me?"

"Yes." Ethan nodded solemnly. "I don't remember if I've told you this before. We'll both face plenty of strong opponents in the future, but I've always believed that if anyone can defeat me, it'll be you."

Ash stared. Sabrina, Misty, and Tracey were just as astonished. It was an extraordinary vote of confidence. Ash's best result so far was reaching the top sixteen at the Indigo League. Ethan had gone through the tournament undefeated and become its champion. Everyone who knew them could see the gap between their present abilities.

Ash trembled with excitement. "Ethan, I promise I'll work even harder! I won't let you down. One day I'll become the Trainer you believe I can be!"

"I believe you," Ethan said. "Just remember that reaching that goal means taking each step seriously."

He patted Ash on the shoulder and waved toward the sky. A few seconds later, Charizard landed beside him. Ethan and Sabrina climbed onto its back.

"It's time for us to go. Safe travels, all of you. See you next time!"

Before Ash or the others could reply, Ethan nudged Charizard gently with his heels. It spread its wings and shot away from Rind Island like an arrow.

Ash and Misty watched from the beach until Charizard shrank to a dark speck. Their feelings were different, but in each of them, a fierce resolve burned.

...

Pummelo Island lay in the northwestern Orange Islands. It was small, somewhat farther north than most of its neighbors, and not especially populous. Its town was no great metropolis.

Even so, islanders held it in high regard. The Orange League headquarters and the venue for the Winner's Cup were both here, keeping Pummelo Island at the center of their attention.

After several hours in the air, Charizard landed in a forest on the island. Ethan recalled it, and he and Sabrina walked into a town whose buildings and decorations had a sporting theme.

They did not go straight to Orange League headquarters. First they found a guesthouse with a hot spring, ate a full meal, soaked for several hours, and slept through the night. By morning they had washed away the dust and fatigue of their travels.

Rested and refreshed, Ethan went to headquarters to register his challenge.

Unlike many tournaments, the Orange League's championship match had no fixed date each year. A challenger who earned all four badges first had to apply here. Once the League confirmed that its Supreme Gym Leader was available, it prepared overnight and held the Winner's Cup match the following day.

Challengers arrived often enough that some years saw more than a dozen Winner's Cup matches held one after another.
