Chapter 408: Adjusting the Team

Ethan accepted Eevee's Poke Ball from Naoko.

The moment the ball entered his hand, Eevee officially became his Pokemon.

After saying goodbye to Naoko, Ethan and Erika did not immediately leave Ecruteak City with Eevee. They first planned to visit the local Pokemon Center.

Now that he had received Eevee, Ethan was carrying more than twelve Pokemon. He needed to adjust his team first to keep Eevee from being transferred away.

Watching their backs from a distance, Naoko waved and shouted, "Mr. Ethan, I hope you win another championship at the Silver Conference! Good luck!"

The two turned and waved back to her.

During the day and a half they had spent together, the women had formed a deep friendship, and Ethan had grown familiar with the sisters as well.

The previous night, Erika had promised them that she would return to visit after the Silver Conference.

Because they knew they would meet again, there was little sadness in this farewell.

Following a broad street, the two reached the Pokemon Center.

After greeting Nurse Joy, Ethan considered which Pokemon he should transfer away.

In the end, he called Sabrina.

After weighing his options, he decided to send her the three Legendary birds. He seldom released them under ordinary circumstances, and they occupied three places on his team. Suicune was more than capable of handling unexpected trouble, and if anything proved too much, he could simply transfer the birds back.

As for why he contacted Sabrina...

Sabrina was his woman, and leaving the three Legendary birds in her care made him far more comfortable than keeping them at Cerise Park. Chloe was nowhere near strong enough to control or supervise the birds, and the park offered little privacy, making it easy for someone to discover their existence.

Most of all, the three birds had already spent time with Sabrina. They knew about her relationship with Ethan and would not resist her too strongly.

Sabrina answered quickly. With Erika beside him, however, Ethan felt too embarrassed to chat for long. He went straight to the point and told Sabrina what he needed.

...

At Saffron Gym in Saffron City, Kanto, a battle was underway.

Sabrina, the Gym Leader, had sent out Kadabra against a female challenger's Blastoise.

The two Pokemon attacked one another across the field, moves flying everywhere in what appeared to be an intense battle.

At that moment, however, Sabrina was not paying attention to the match. Standing in the Trainer box on the left, she held her phone to her ear and nodded occasionally at whatever she heard.

Once the call ended, she turned without hesitation and walked straight toward the Gym's entrance.

Her departure left only the utterly bewildered challenger and Sabrina's father, who was serving as referee.

Seeing her simply walk away without another thought for the battle, her father hurriedly shouted, "Sabrina! Where are you going!?"

Without looking back, Sabrina answered, "The Pokemon Center."

Her flat tone made it sound as though the ongoing match did not matter at all.

"What are you going there for!?" Her father was astonished. "You're in the middle of a battle! What is supposed to happen if you leave!?"

Sabrina ignored him and continued out the door.

"Ah..."

Faced with her behavior, her father could only sigh helplessly.

This was profoundly irresponsible and a serious dereliction of her duties as Gym Leader. If someone reported it to the Pokemon League, it would undoubtedly cause a great deal of trouble.

Yet as her father, he had no power to restrain his daughter.

He still remembered her cold personality vividly. Although traveling had changed her considerably and made her somewhat more normal, anyone who angered her could immediately experience the terror of being dominated by psychic power!

Now a single phone call had drawn Sabrina away. As a man with experience in such matters, he naturally understood that something was wrong with his daughter's emotional state. Someone outside the Gym had to be pursuing her!

That had to be it!

Damn it! Who could it be!?

By then, Sabrina had already vanished through the entrance. It was too late to question her even if he wanted to.

With no other choice, he turned toward the equally bewildered challenger and gave her an awkward smile.

"Ahem. As you can see, Sabrina had urgent business and needed to leave immediately. I'll serve as your temporary opponent instead!"

...

After confirming over the phone that the three Great Balls had reached Sabrina, Ethan finally breathed a sigh of relief.

He had been afraid something might go wrong during the transfer. These were three Legendary Pokemon, after all. The consequences would be unthinkable if they were lost.

Now it seemed that his concern had simply clouded his judgment.

Modern technology was highly advanced. Unless Team Rocket interfered, the odds of encountering a machine failure were extremely low.

With the three Legendary birds gone, Ethan still had two open spaces even after counting Eevee's Poke Ball. That should be more than enough for the next stage of their journey.

His standards were high, and he did not catch Pokemon indiscriminately. Two open spaces were more than sufficient.

After saying goodbye to Nurse Joy, Ethan and Erika left the Pokemon Center, departed Ecruteak City, and continued southwest.

Their next destination was Olivine Gym in Olivine City.

The straight-line distance between Olivine and Ecruteak was not especially great. After leaving Ecruteak, however, they had to follow Route 38 to its end, turn south, and pass through Route 39 before reaching the city.

That detour naturally made the journey much longer.

The country path passed between vast grasslands. Green grass filled their view, while only at the far edge of the horizon could they faintly see the rise and fall of distant mountains.

The weather was clear. Although the sun shone, its light was not harsh, and the breeze against their bodies felt indescribably pleasant.

Eevee was extremely lively. It scampered in every direction around the two travelers, stopping now and then to smell a flower, sniff the grass, or turn over a stone. It could not remain still.

Faced with such boundless energy, Ethan had to call Gengar out of his shadow and tell it to watch Eevee so it would not get lost.

Gengar had lived for more than a thousand years and behaved like an old man. It possessed neither Eevee's energy nor its curiosity about everything around it. After following the youngster for only a short while, Gengar was exhausted in both body and mind, its face wearing an expression of utter despair.

Walking beside Ethan, Erika glanced at Eevee and asked with a soft laugh, "Have you thought about what you want this Eevee to evolve into?"

"I haven't made any plans yet." Ethan looked down at Eevee and shook his head. "Naoko gave it to me along with all her hopes for it, so... I think the choice should be Eevee's."
