Chapter 345: Goh's Second Scare

At noon, Ethan ate lunch with Professor Cerise, Chloe, and the professor's two assistants, Ren and Parker. Afterward, he and Chloe went to Cerise Park to visit Blastoise and Venusaur.

With no other Pokemon competing for space, Blastoise naturally occupied the park's largest pool. Venusaur claimed the thickest patch of shrubs near the water as its territory.

Both Pokemon lived comfortably here and looked full of energy. Chloe had clearly taken excellent care of them. When their Trainer arrived, they eagerly approached and rubbed their heads against Ethan's arms.

"Have you two settled in here? You haven't caused any trouble, have you?"

Both Pokemon shook their heads.

Ethan already knew their personalities. Blastoise and Venusaur were the clearest representatives of the peaceful side of his team. His question was merely an instinctive expression of concern.

After patting their heads, Ethan turned to Chloe and thanked her earnestly.

"My Pokemon must have caused you plenty of trouble. Thank you for all your hard work."

"You don't need to be so polite with me." Chloe lowered her gaze toward her toes, looking slightly shy. "Blastoise and Venusaur are both very gentle. I like them too."

Ethan shook his head. "No matter what, I should thank you. It's a little embarrassing, but I'll catch more Pokemon in the future. I'm afraid I'll cause you even more trouble then."

"It's fine." Chloe gave him a bashful smile. "As long as they're your Pokemon, it doesn't matter how many come here!"

What a thoughtful girl...

Despite her words, Ethan knew Chloe had always maintained a certain distance from Pokemon. The fact that she would go this far for his sake already showed how much he meant to her.

Only their close relationship made her willing to do it. If these Pokemon belonged to someone else, Chloe certainly would not have taken on the extra responsibility.

Moved by the thought, Ethan took out his Poke Balls and released every Pokemon except the three Legendary birds so they could play in the park.

He spoke with Chloe for a while, then gave a huge yawn and returned alone to the dorm room where he had once lived.

The room looked exactly as it had when he left. Someone had clearly cleaned it regularly. Everything was spotless, without a trace of dust.

His Indigo League and Orange League trophies stood on the bookshelf. A glass cabinet displayed the Gym badges he had collected from both regions. The polished mementos, each with a different shape, gleamed softly in the reflected sunlight.

Everything else was just as familiar. Lying on the bed left Ethan in a daze, almost as though he had returned to the days when he first arrived in Kanto.

It also showed that Professor Cerise truly regarded him as family. The professor had not reclaimed the room merely because Ethan had left.

Warmth filled Ethan's heart.

Sunlight fell across his face.

After days of travel, Ethan had barely rested properly. Now that he was lying in bed, exhaustion swept over him. Without noticing, he gradually fell asleep.

Meanwhile, in Cerise Park, Chloe brought out several plates of Berries for Ethan's Pokemon.

"Help yourselves. These all grew here in the park, and they're delicious."

She crouched on the ground with a faint smile, watching the Pokemon before her. Her affection for Ethan extended to his partners; no matter how she looked at them, they seemed increasingly lovable.

At that moment, a dark-skinned boy wandered in from outside with his hands behind his head, whistling.

Only one person would enter this unfinished park as though he belonged there: Chloe's childhood friend, Goh.

"Goh? Why are you here?"

Goh strolled over and muttered, "I went to your house and couldn't find you. Your mother told me you'd come here. Huh? Whose Pokemon are these?"

"Ethan's, of course!"

"Ethan?"

The image of a tall, handsome man appeared in Goh's mind.

"They're his?" Goh looked around. Seeing only a handful of Pokemon scattered through the park, he dismissed them. "The last time I came here, he had hardly any Pokemon. He's been traveling all this time and still only has this few? That's hardly anything!"

Chloe frowned immediately.

"Ethan catches only Pokemon he likes. Do you think everyone shares your ideas? Didn't you watch any of his battles? He became a Champion even with only a small number of Pokemon!"

"So what?" Goh lifted his chin. "I admit he's a great battler, but the number of Pokemon someone catches determines whether they're a qualified Trainer! If it were me, my Pokemon would already fill this entire park!"

What a ridiculous theory!

Chloe rolled her eyes. His words had ruined her good mood in an instant.

Goh did not care when she ignored him. He wandered leisurely toward the water. Just as he drew close, he suddenly felt something strange beneath his feet.

He looked down. The surface exploded, and an enormous body rose from the pool.

"Waaah!"

Goh jumped in fright. Once he saw what had appeared, he found a horrifying, gigantic head staring at him with blood-red eyes.

Gyarados!

Gyarados opened its jaws as though preparing to swallow him. A chill instantly swept across Goh's body. His legs gave way, and he fell into the pool.

Splash!

Hearing the noise behind her, Chloe turned and covered her mouth in surprise when she saw Goh in the water.

"Goh? Are you all right?"

Goh swallowed several mouthfuls of pond water. Fortunately, he knew how to swim. After flailing in panic for a moment, he scrambled back out.

Soaked through, he climbed onto the bank and prepared to speak. Then he turned and saw Gyarados's enormous head drawing closer. He could almost feel its breath.

Goh shut his mouth and hurried away.

Why is this Gyarados so terrifying?

It didn't make a sound. Was it trying to eat me?

Through his confusion, Goh vaguely remembered that during his last visit to Cerise Park, this same Gyarados had frightened him into the water.

After all this time, it had happened again.

How awful!

He wanted to curse the Pokemon for having no manners. But with Gyarados still watching him through red eyes, Goh wisely kept his mouth closed.

Still, why was this Gyarados so frightening?

Were they always this enormous?

After this encounter, Gyarados had clearly left an indelible shadow in Goh's heart.
