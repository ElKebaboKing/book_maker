Chapter 808: May Thought She Had It Figured Out

May practiced amid the chatter for more than an hour. When the lunch break finally came, she ate and returned to the greenhouse alone.

Since its reconstruction, Celadon Gym's greenhouse had grown to several thousand square meters. It was divided into sections of different sizes and filled with lush plants and Grass-type Pokemon.

Oddish, Gloom, Vileplume, Bellsprout, Weepinbell, Victreebel... Erika had also brought back Grass-type Pokemon from Johto and Hoenn. They lived together peacefully, sometimes peeking out of the vegetation as they went about their leisurely lives.

May was looking for someone else. She walked slowly through the greenhouse, searching the trees and shrubs for Celebi. The glimpse she had caught earlier had made her want to meet it.

It was a Mythical Pokemon, a rare creature most people would never see in their lives. Celebi was here in the Gym. This might be her only chance to look at it closely.

Besides, it was Brother Ethan's Pokemon. It would not flee in fright or attack her. She could approach without worrying about danger.

The dense greenery and winding paths made the search difficult. May walked for a long time without seeing Celebi again, but she would not give up. Determined to find it, she pushed into the deepest part of the greenhouse.

The going grew harder. Thick trees crowded around her, their foliage limiting her view to a meter or two. The air even seemed thin. After only a dozen or so meters, she felt as though she could barely breathe.

She stumbled and caught a trunk to keep from falling. A few steps later, she rounded a tree blocking her way and found a small clearing. The air felt a little easier to breathe.

May was about to sit and rest when she spotted a small green Pokemon flitting above a bed of flowers.

Celebi!

Her energy returned at once. She hurried closer and finally got a good look at the Mythical Pokemon. It resembled a little green onion with transparent wings on its back, large blue eyes, and two short antennae. It was adorable.

May approached carefully. Celebi noticed her. Perhaps it was used to spending time with Erika and the staff, or perhaps it had grown comfortable around people since being caught. It showed no fear. Instead it circled May with interest, studying the unfamiliar girl.

Hello, May said with a smile. I'm a friend of Brother Ethan's. It's nice to meet you.

Bi~

Celebi tilted its head, looking puzzled.

Before May could say more, Ethan spoke from behind her. You gave it the wrong name. He came to her side and looked at Celebi. I caught it, but we haven't built much of a bond yet. If you want to get close to it, you'd do better to mention Erika.

Huh? But isn't it your Pokemon?

It is. Ethan smiled. I left it with Erika soon after catching it. You know what Erika and I mean to each other. Celebi belongs to me, strictly speaking, but it's also a Pokemon we share.

Now May understood why the staff called Celebi a token of Ethan and Erika's love.

Are you going to leave it here permanently?

We'll see. The main reason I left it here was so it could keep Erika company while I'm away. Celebi is Grass-type as well as Psychic-type, so it suits her.

Celebi had not seen Ethan in a long time, but it recognized the Trainer who had caught it and spent some time with it. It flew around him. Up close, Ethan noticed it seemed a little rounder. Erika had clearly taken good care of it.

He smiled at a memory. It's a little glutton, just like you. I caught it with a cup of instant noodles.

Instant noodles? May gaped. A Mythical Pokemon that so many people dreamed of meeting, caught with a cup of noodles?

That's what comes of loving food, Ethan said lightly. Then he sighed. I promised to take it around the world and let it taste food from everywhere. It doesn't look as though I can do that anytime soon.

Why not? Why can't it travel with you? Don't you have that... that other Pokemon?

May did not finish, but Ethan knew she meant Deoxys. Misty had told him she had revealed that secret to May. Since May would be traveling with him now, she would learn about his hidden partners over time anyway. There was little reason to conceal everything from her.

Their circumstances are different, he explained. Deoxys was born to fight. Under my training, battle has become its main purpose. Celebi is capable in a fight too, but it's more peaceful and less driven to compete. I never know when I'll have to face a powerful enemy. With room for only a limited number of Pokemon on hand, I can't always bring Celebi, however strong it is.

May saw what he meant.

When my travels end, I'll keep my promise and take Celebi around the world, Ethan added. For now, it can stay with Erika a while longer.

After a quiet day at Celadon Gym, the three had dinner. Ethan and Erika went back to her room and spent another demanding night together. This time Ethan saved some strength, allowing him to rise early the next morning.

They had already stayed an extra day. Today they had to set out.

Erika understood. Though she hated to see him go, she prepared his things with loving care.

I packed lunches for you. There should be enough to last until you reach Pallet Town.

She handed him the parcel at the Gym entrance. This time she seemed less concerned about onlookers. She straightened his clothes and gave him a few gentle reminders.

Ethan was moved. He drew her into his arms, breathing in her perfume and the scent of her hair.

Go back inside, he murmured near her ear. You don't need to see me off any farther. I'll come back when my Sinnoh journey is over.

A day or two together could not ease the longing caused by a long separation. Yet Erika knew his ambitions. He wanted to challenge the world, and she supported him despite how much she missed him. She looked forward to their next reunion, and to a time when he would no longer need to travel.

I'll wait for you.

With the Gym staff watching, Ethan kissed Erika lightly. He waved goodbye, then left with May without looking back.

He could not settle into a comfortable life yet. Coming to this world had given him an extraordinary opportunity, and he meant to make something of it. Erika and Sabrina were both accomplished, beautiful Gym Leaders. He wanted to keep growing into someone worthy of them.

Champion of three regions? That was nowhere near enough. He intended to become the strongest in the world.

...

From Celadon Gym, Ethan and May headed west toward Pallet Town. Their destination was Professor Oak's laboratory.

May assumed they were simply going to meet Ash. Ethan had another purpose: he wanted her to receive the Squirtle that, in the anime, had belonged to her.

Now that May was traveling with him, he had a responsibility toward her. He knew little about being a Coordinator. Apart from battle training and sharing what he knew of the world, he could offer only so much guidance. At the very least, he would see that she did not miss out on the Pokemon she was meant to have.

The Battle Factory near Mt. Moon was also not far from Pallet Town, so the trip would serve his Frontier challenge as well.

With a destination in mind, the road felt easier. After half a day's travel, they reached the long bridge across the sea where Team Rocket had once ambushed Ethan. The sight brought back many memories. This time, to save time, he and May flew over it on Dragonite instead of crossing on foot.

May had never ridden with Ethan before. She sat behind him, watching the sea flash past below. The flight excited her, but their closeness made her heart beat a little faster. When Dragonite turned with the air currents, she found herself pressed against Ethan's broad back. Her cheeks warmed.

At her age, thoughts and feelings could rush in without warning. She was beginning to discover what it meant to like someone. She had spent most of her time with boys like Ash and Max. Ethan was the first mature man she had come to know this closely. Everything she had witnessed over the last few days had unsettled her, stirring unfamiliar emotions.

In some ways, she was going through what Misty once had. She might even be starting down the same path.

When Dragonite landed, Ethan helped May down and noticed her flushed face. He assumed the cold wind had caused it.

Pallet Town was closer now that they had crossed the bridge, though they would not reach it immediately.

Brother Ethan... May hesitated. Why don't we ride Dragonite all the way? Wouldn't that be faster?

Misty had asked him the same question once. Looking at May, Ethan seemed for a moment to see Misty standing there instead. Then the illusion faded, leaving the cheerful girl before him.

He pointed to the streams, trees, and stones beside the road. What do you feel?

Huh? May was confused for a moment, then thought she understood. I feel nature. Do you mean that riding Dragonite is only a way to travel, and we'd miss what we can learn from all this?

You're thinking too hard. Ethan laughed. I mean that when we walk, we can enjoy the scenery along the way. From the sky, we see clouds and the ground racing past.

May stared at him. She had considered her answer so carefully, only for the reason to be that simple. She pressed a hand to her forehead, but she had also discovered his playful side.

They walked on. Just as Ethan was about to suggest a break and something to eat, May's phone rang. It was Max.

Why is Max calling me? she wondered.

As soon as she answered, her brother's excited voice came through. Where are you, sis? Bet you don't know this! I'm visiting Professor Oak's lab with Professor Birch right now!
