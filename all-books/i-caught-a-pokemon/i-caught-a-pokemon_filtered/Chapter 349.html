Chapter 349: A Call from Erika

After saying that, Ethan ignored Mewtwo's reaction, turned around, and simply left.

With both hands in his pockets, he showed Mewtwo only his carefree back.

Mewtwo watched him walk farther and farther away, climb onto Gengar, and fly toward the upper levels of the cave until he vanished. Its brow furrowed deeply in genuine confusion.

Did that man truly come here only for one simple battle?

He should have known that Gardevoir was no match for me. Why go to such unnecessary trouble?

He even had other, stronger Pokemon he could have used. He could have surrounded me and attacked, so why did he give up?

Faced with so many questions, Mewtwo sank into thought.

It truly could not understand. The man had only become more difficult to read.

Hmph. Humans really are complicated creatures!

Meanwhile, Ethan retraced his route and returned to the surface.

Once warm sunlight shone on them again and Gengar was certain Mewtwo could no longer overhear their conversation, it could not contain its curiosity.

"Are we really leaving just like that?"

Clearly, it shared Mewtwo's doubts.

"What else would we do?" Ethan asked. "Send all of you out and fight it together?"

"Huh? Weren't we supposed to?"

Gengar scratched its head. It had lived for thousands of years, but it still lacked insight into the human heart and could not understand why Ethan had abandoned this opportunity.

"We had a great chance of winning! I admit it's very powerful, but if you'd ordered us to attack together, we would've had at least a fifty-percent chance of capturing it. Add the Master Ball you obtained earlier, and success would've been practically guaranteed!"

"You're oversimplifying it." Ethan patiently explained, "Capturing Mewtwo would be easy, but what happens afterward? It may be a Pokemon, but humans created it, and its mind works much like ours. Without a sound foundation between us, crudely using a Poke Ball to capture a 'human' would leave a serious problem. Sooner or later, something would go wrong."

"So what?" Gengar heard his explanation but remained unconvinced. "You could do what you did with the three Legendary birds. Capture it first, then build a relationship afterward!"

If only it were truly that simple...

Ethan chuckled and shook his head.

Humanoid Pokemon, especially those capable of human speech like Mewtwo and Gengar, were extremely troublesome. He certainly could not treat them like ordinary animal-shaped Pokemon and casually capture them whenever he pleased.

Why else did he indulge Gengar so much, let it remain beside him at all times, and never force it into its Poke Ball?

He was preventing Gengar from becoming dissatisfied.

Keeping Gengar by his side also let companionship draw it into his perspective, gradually making it identify with him more deeply.

Compared with human cunning, these Pokemon were still a little inexperienced. They could not see the roots of matters like this.

"All right." When Gengar looked ready to say more, Ethan cut it off. "Stop dwelling on this. Don't worry. I'm making a deeper impression. I believe that sooner or later, we'll be able to capture Mewtwo without even fighting it!"

...

Cerulean Cave was not far from the city, so Ethan did not ride Charizard. He walked to the Cerulean City Pokemon Center instead.

He handed Gardevoir's Poke Ball to Nurse Joy and asked her to treat its injuries, then went to the Pokemon Center's dining area and ordered lunch.

The entire room was empty. Ethan was the only Trainer there.

Looking at the vacant seat across from him, he sighed again.

Sigh...

My first day without Sabrina. I miss her!

The two of them had once been inseparable, but now he was alone. It felt deeply unfamiliar.

They had spoken on the phone and exchanged messages the previous night, but Sabrina was not someone who liked keeping her phone close. Whenever Ethan contacted her, a long time usually passed before she replied.

That was simply her personality. He could not force her to change.

Besides, Ethan had been the one who persuaded her to leave. No matter how lonely he became, he had to grit his teeth and endure it.

Sabrina presumably felt the same way.

With that thought, Ethan transformed his longing into appetite and attacked his meal one bite after another.

Before he had eaten more than a few mouthfuls, however, his phone suddenly rang.

At first, Ethan thought his thoughts had somehow reached Sabrina and prompted her to call at that exact moment. But when he checked the screen, Erika's name appeared instead.

Erika?

Why is she calling me?

Ethan raised his eyebrows in surprise. The two occasionally exchanged messages, but Erika had never called him before.

Did something happen?

He answered. Before he could speak, Erika's familiar voice came from the other end.

"Hello, Ethan. Have your travels been going well?"

Ethan was glad to hear from a friend at a time like this. In high spirits, he could not resist making a joke.

"Of course! I'm the Indigo League Champion, remember? Defeating those Gyms has been easy!"

Only after saying it did Ethan remember that Erika was a Gym Leader too. His joke had included her.

Fortunately, Erika did not take offense. They exchanged a few casual pleasantries before Ethan turned the conversation toward its purpose.

"I doubt you called me out of nowhere for no reason. Tell me, is something going on?"

"There is something." Erika smiled. "Are you still in Johto?"

Ethan's curiosity rose. "I returned to Kanto yesterday, but I'll be heading back soon. What is this about? Why did you need to call me?"

"Well..." Erika spoke softly. "I've been thinking of visiting Johto in the next few days to catch several Pokemon. You've already traveled there for quite some time, while I've never been to Johto. I wanted to ask you about the distribution of its Grass-type Pokemon."

"You want to catch some new Grass-type Pokemon?" Ethan thought for a moment. "I did learn a little during my travels, but it would be difficult to explain everything over the phone. How about this? I'm in Cerulean City now, and I'll pass through Celadon this afternoon. We can discuss it in person when I arrive."

"All right. I'll see you at the Celadon Gym later!"

After ending the call, Ethan did not give the matter much thought. He was returning to Johto anyway, and making a special trip through Celadon City would cost him little time.

Just as he finished lunch, Nurse Joy called him to the front desk over the intercom.

Ethan went to the counter, accepted the Poke Ball from her, offered his thanks, and released Gardevoir.

After Nurse Joy's treatment, not a single wound remained on Gardevoir's body.

"Are you feeling better?"

Gardevoir nodded. Its expression remained subdued, however, and Ethan knew it was still dejected by its defeat.

"It's all right." Seeing its mood, he smiled reassuringly. "Mewtwo's strength is unfathomable. Even an ordinary Mythical Pokemon might not be its match, so there's no reason to take this loss to heart. Besides, our journey has only just begun. We'll definitely grow stronger from here. When that time comes, you can challenge it again!"
