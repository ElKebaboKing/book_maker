Chapter 417: One Arrival After Another

Eusine looked at Erika in puzzlement and asked curiously, "Miss Erika, what brought you to Johto? Is everything all right at the Celadon Gym?"

"It couldn't be helped." Facing Eusine's confusion, Erika gently shook her head. "A fire broke out at the Celadon Gym some time ago. It's currently being repaired and rebuilt, so I'm using the opportunity to catch a few Grass-type Pokemon here."

"I see."

Eusine nodded in understanding, then turned his attention to Ethan.

However, he did not recognize Ethan and merely assumed he was an ordinary male companion traveling with Erika.

That was hardly surprising. Eusine was not a professional Pokemon Trainer and spent most of his time tracking Suicune. He rarely watched Pokemon tournaments. Although television news had occasionally broadcast one or two of Ethan's battles during the Indigo League and Eusine had heard his name, he could not connect that name with the man standing before him.

Moreover, Ethan was only a League Conference Champion. His fame was still nowhere near that of the Elite Four, so his face naturally was not familiar to everyone.

Only after Eusine revealed Erika's identity did Jimmy and Marina realize that Ethan was not the only remarkable person here. The beautiful woman traveling with him had also concealed her true standing: she was a Gym Leader! Their respect for her rose at once.

Compared with the two of them, who were novice Trainers only just beginning their journeys, all three people before them possessed a certain degree of social standing!

Erika then asked the question everyone most wanted answered. "Mr. Eusine, how did you learn that Raikou was here?"

"I happened to be nearby. Last night I met a Trainer who had just left this Pokemon Center, and he told me what had happened. Honestly, I was stunned when I learned Raikou was being kept here. A Legendary Pokemon of its stature is almost impossible to encounter. Oh, did you capture it?"

"Well..."

Marina was about to answer, but before she could speak, a door at the other end of the corridor suddenly opened. Officer Jenny emerged alongside a man with short blue hair.

The blue-haired man joined them and answered on their behalf. "You've misunderstood. They didn't capture Raikou. They rescued it from Team Rocket and brought it here for emergency treatment."

Ethan and the others looked at him in confusion. None of them knew who he was.

And why had he come out of the room with Officer Jenny?

Eusine, however, recognized him.

"Kudo!? I never expected to see you here!" Eusine sounded delighted, clearly surprised to encounter the man.

"It's been a long time, Eusine."

The man called Kudo extended his right hand. As the two shook hands, it was clear that they knew each other well.

Kudo?

The name meant nothing to Ethan. He studied the man carefully but found nothing remarkable about his perfectly ordinary appearance. However, Kudo wore the sort of vest used by archaeological fieldworkers, so he was probably a scholar as well.

The man's sudden emergence from the room left Jimmy and Marina thoroughly confused. They could not help asking, "Officer Jenny, who exactly is Mr. Kudo?"

Officer Jenny smiled. "This is Pokemon researcher Kudo. Professor Elm asked him to come here specifically to help Raikou recover and to collect some data on it while he's here!"

"Professor Elm asked him?" Ethan frowned. "How did he know we'd encountered Raikou?"

"I contacted the professor last night!" Marina raised her hand and stuck out her tongue. "Something this important obviously had to be reported to him! I just never expected him to send someone all the way here to deal with it!"

Only then did Ethan learn that Marina had contacted Professor Elm after they separated the previous night. On reflection, there was nothing unreasonable about it. She had no intention of capturing Raikou, and sharing discoveries from one's journey with a Pokemon Professor was common practice.

If Ash discovered something while traveling, he would certainly tell Professor Oak too.

Still...

So many people had been drawn here for no reason. Ethan worried that some unexpected complication might arise in the end!

After learning that Kudo had come to address the situation, Eusine nodded approvingly.

"It's best to be cautious. This is Raikou, one of Johto's Legendary Pokemon. We absolutely cannot let it fall into the hands of evildoers! And I doubt Team Rocket will give up on Raikou after a single defeat. They'll certainly come back!"

"You don't need to worry about that." Officer Jenny took over, then turned her gaze toward Ethan. "We just received word from the International Police. They're sending someone here to deal with those Team Rocket members."

Something stirred in Ethan's mind. "Officer Jenny, do you know whom the International Police are sending"

Before he could finish, the corridor door behind him opened. A woman in a black suit entered, her gray hair tied into a long ponytail.

"Miss Anabel!?"

The woman was Anabel, the International Police officer Ethan had worked with during an earlier operation!

In addition to her identity as an International Police officer, she was a powerful Trainer, the Salon Maiden of the Battle Tower, and one of the Frontier Brains of Hoenn's Battle Frontier. She also held a high position within the International Police. Not only was she Looker's superior, she was also the director of its UB Task Force headquarters!

With so many important titles to her name, Ethan never expected such a senior official to meet him here in person!

"It's been a long time, Mr. Ethan. You've done us another tremendous favor. Thank you for all the help you've given the International Police lately!"

Anabel greeted Ethan and Officer Jenny first, then turned her gaze toward Erika.

"Miss Erika? I never expected to see you here."

Their names sounded rather similar, and they had worked together in Kanto before, so the two women were no strangers to one another.

"Good morning, Miss Anabel." Erika placed a hand against her abdomen and bowed gently. "I didn't expect you to handle this matter either. You look very well!"

Truthfully, Ethan had never expected Looker to send Anabel. It was welcome news. Since they had worked together before, he had no reservations about her and was quite willing to cooperate.

After exchanging a few pleasantries, Ethan went straight to the point.

"How many people did you bring this time, Miss Anabel?"

Anabel smiled faintly. "Don't worry. I've come fully prepared. I'm confident we can take down that research facility in a single operation!"
