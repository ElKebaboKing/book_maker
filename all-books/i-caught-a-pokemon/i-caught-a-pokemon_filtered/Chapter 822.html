Chapter 822: How Far May Has Come

The matchups for the eight remaining competitors appeared on the screen. Ethan immediately found May's name.

The draw had put her in the first battle, and her opponent was Harley. What a twist of fate. Harley had placed second in the performance round, while May had placed next to last among the qualifiers. On paper, the pairing favored him.

But Ethan, Ash, and the others did not consider May the weaker of the two. They were confident she would win. Facing Harley early had its advantages: she could send him home and be rid of him. For Harley, it was a chance for revenge.

He hated both Ethan and May and wanted them out of his way. He would have liked to save his confrontation with May for later, but defeating her now would keep him happy for days. Both Coordinators were determined to teach the other a lesson.

Ethan paid little attention to the other matchups, Jessie's included. He saw no other competitor as dangerous as Harley. If May beat him, she would likely win the Saffron Contest. This match might decide everything.

After a short break, the second round began. Before the expectant crowd, May and Harley returned to the stage.

Lilian explained the rules. You have five minutes. Show off your Pokemon's moves beautifully while reducing your opponent's score. That's the key to victory! Both Coordinators are ready, so let's see what they'll do in our first battle!

The five-minute countdown started. May and Harley released their Pokemon at the same moment.

To Ethan's surprise, May chose Squirtle, which she had caught only recently. It was a risk. But unlike the appeal round, this battle did not require a precisely rehearsed routine with its Trainer. Squirtle needed to show what it could do. May had also kept it beside her during the earlier performance. Evidently she had considered using it here all along rather than choosing on impulse.

Harley had changed Pokemon too. Instead of Cacturne, he sent out an Ariados. Allowing different partners for the two rounds gave Kanto Coordinators more flexibility than they had in Hoenn and promised a wider range of performances.

Neither Pokemon had even settled into place before Harley ordered an attack. Ariados shot a long strand of silk at the ceiling and swung toward Squirtle. In an instant, it was right in front of Squirtle, using Scary Face.

Startled by Ariados's sudden appearance and its frightening expression, Squirtle rolled backward twice and came to a stop by May's feet.

It had been with May only a short time. Its courage was still developing, and such a reaction was understandable. May did not panic. She encouraged Squirtle back to its feet and ordered Ice Beam.

Ethan had not expected a newly caught Squirtle to know the move. May must have spent considerable effort training it when he was not around. Unfortunately, Squirtle could not yet control that much energy freely. The force of its own Ice Beam sent it flying backward. Off balance, it fired far past Ariados.

Harley laughed at the attempt, but he did not waste his opening. Ariados used Spider Web to bind Squirtle and haul it toward the ceiling.

Wrapped tight like prey, Squirtle could only squirm as Ariados drew it closer. The spider's threatening presence made it more frightened. No blow had landed, yet Squirtle's Contest score dropped on the screen. A Contest Battle measured more than physical damage.

Whether Harley wanted to humiliate May or show off, he delayed his next attack. He had Ariados spray silk all around the stage, building a huge web. Then Ariados tossed Squirtle onto it like a ball of yarn, pulled it up, and tossed it again. It repeated the game several times. Squirtle could not break free, and its score fell further.

Ethan understood what Harley was doing. He wanted May to look helpless and foolish. But his arrogance would cost him. May was no longer the inexperienced girl he had once been able to torment.

When Harley finally grew tired of playing and sent Ariados in to finish Squirtle, May gave her command. Squirtle fired another Ice Beam. This time the web held its body steady. The beam struck Ariados squarely. Its recoil also tore Squirtle loose, breaking through layer after layer of silk and sending it back to the stage.

Ariados froze solid where it stood. The cold raced along its silk and turned the entire web into ice. The battle had reversed in an instant.

Harley's face changed. He had been so pleased with himself that he had never considered this possibility. Ariados's score plunged. Ice Beam had hurt it, and the judges clearly appreciated the spectacular effect it had created.

May seized the opportunity. Before Ariados could break free, she ordered Bubble. Countless bubbles struck the spider, shattering the ice on its body and dealing more damage.

Ariados thawed. Harley was about to order a counterattack when he saw that the silk beneath its feet remained frozen solid. Ariados could not move properly.

Squirtle leaped onto the web, took a running start, and used Tackle. It knocked Ariados to the floor below. The impact and fall together left the spider unconscious.

What? Harley stared. His Ariados had gone from complete control of the match to defeat in moments. Worse, he had lost to May in his very first battle of the second round. The humiliation stung, but his anger could not change the result.

All three judges' screens showed a large red X. Lilian announced, Ariados is unable to battle! Squirtle turned a hopeless position into a victory. The winner is May!

The audience erupted. Ash and the others cheered until they were nearly hoarse, their delight a sharp contrast to Harley's dark expression. He had deceived them only yesterday. May's victory was a satisfying answer.

May jumped for joy and hugged Squirtle. Without so much as a glance at the furious Harley, she turned and left the stage. Her confident departure made his face grow darker still. He wanted to say something cutting, but she was already gone.

Ethan greeted her with applause when she returned to the competitors' lounge.

That was a great battle, he said. I didn't know Squirtle had learned Ice Beam. And you beat Harley! That's worth celebrating.

Hehe... May smiled. No one was happier about advancing past Harley than she was.

...

Elsewhere, Harley came down from the stage intending to leave the Saffron Contest Hall and its painful memories behind. Instead, he met Jessie, James, and Meowth on their way to the stage.

Then he remembered he still had a way to get back at May. The woman calling herself the fairy Jessibella might prove useful.

Miss Jessibella! he said, putting on an ingratiating smile. Did you see the awful girl I just battled? She's the one who's been tormenting me!

Jessie stared at him blankly. What? I was getting my injuries treated. I didn't see your match. Did someone beat you?

Cacnea's spines had injured her during the first round. The medical staff had treated her quickly and carefully after carrying her offstage, but she had only just returned. She had missed May and Harley's battle entirely.

Harley's smile stiffened. It doesn't matter that you missed it. I nearly won, but that nasty girl used a trick and got lucky. I've lost my chance now. You have to avenge me!

Remembering their friendly conversation, Jessie did not question him. She patted her chest and promised, Don't worry! No one can beat the fairy Jessibella. She's just some little girl. I'll get revenge for you!

Harley was delighted. He lavished more praise on Jessie until she was floating with pride, then took his leave.

Once he was gone, James and Meowth exchanged uneasy looks. Didn't he seem to be up to something?

Who cares? Jessie said. For once, her reasoning was sound. We want to win the Saffron Contest. We'll have to beat anyone in our way. Getting revenge for him is just a bonus.

James and Meowth began to imagine victory too. Jessie's first-place score in the appeal round had astonished them. If she kept this up, they might honestly win something for once.

Full of hope, they sent Jessie onto the stage.

With Harley eliminated, there were few competitors who could challenge either May or Jessie. Jessie combined real ability with a touch of luck and swept past two opponents in succession. She and May reached the final battle.

It was an unprecedented showing for Jessie. Ethan found himself reconsidering her talent for performance. If she gave up being a villain and truly pursued life as a Coordinator, perhaps she could reach heights most people never would.
