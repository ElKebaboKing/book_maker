Chapter 475: Let's Start Wrecking Things!

The plan proceeded smoothly. Ethan's group waited several more minutes and finally relaxed when the Team Rocket base showed no reaction. They had safely passed the first obstacle.

As Ethan touched the thick wall, however, the question of how to enter the base presented them with a second problem.

If they were willing to be ruthless, they could simply tear several holes through the facility. Seawater would surge inside without pause and flood the entire base in little more than ten minutes, leaving those within no chance to escape and allowing Ethan's group to eliminate everyone in one stroke.

That would be far too vicious. Their primary objective was to capture Team Rocket, not exterminate them.

If they did not damage the wall, however, they would need to locate an entrance to enter the base.

As an underwater structure, the entire facility had to be tightly sealed. Even if they found an access point, opening it would still be a problem.

Ethan explained their predicament to the others through his writing slate. After reading it, Ash and the others merely scratched their heads in confusion, unable to offer a useful idea.

Sigh...

I really can't rely on these people at all...

Ethan helplessly looked away and happened to notice Misty's Corsola once again.

Hmm?

An idea instantly occurred to him. It seemed they would have to ask Corsola for help again!

...

At that moment, two Team Rocket members sat in the underwater base's security room with their legs crossed. They leisurely drank tea and only occasionally glanced over the surveillance screens.

Years of uneventful isolation and tedious work had long since eroded their proper vigilance, leaving both guards thoroughly complacent.

Even so, they remained at their posts and maintained the bare minimum of security.

Then a Corsola appeared and quickly caught their attention.

One guard watched the screen and shook his head helplessly. "How annoying. That Corsola is back again!"

The other guard laughed. "Does it really think the camera is a mirror? Sigh. These wild Pokemon are so adorably stupid!"

On the screen, Corsola swam in front of the camera once again. It looked from side to side, then opened its mouth wide and bit down upon the lens. As it twisted its body and shook from side to side, it actually tore the camera loose!

"Damn it!" One guard immediately sat upright and cursed. He picked up his radio and called, "Maintenance team! A wild Pokemon damaged one of the exterior cameras. Get out there and inspect it!"

"Another one!?" an impatient voice answered through the radio. "Seriously, how many times has this happened this month? Do these wild Pokemon think this place is some kind of treasure trove?"

Despite all their complaints, the maintenance personnel had no choice but to head toward the exit, change into diving suits, and prepare to swim outside to repair the camera.

Meanwhile, Ethan and the others remained hidden against the base's outer wall, waiting quietly for an opportunity to appear.

Fortunately, they did not wait long. A circular passage opened, and a Team Rocket member carrying tools swam out. Without pausing, he headed straight toward the camera Corsola had damaged.

His movements were thoroughly practiced, as though he had experienced the same situation many times before.

The passage had not yet closed. Recognizing that the opportunity could not be missed and seeing that the maintenance worker had already moved away, Ethan's group swam forward with all their strength. They entered one after another and finally made it safely inside the base.

Splash...

Ethan and the others surfaced from the water.

Beyond the passage stood a pressure chamber resembling part of a ship. The group left the water, climbed a ladder, removed their diving masks and underwater respirators, and each took a deep breath.

The air was surprisingly fresh, without the stale quality found inside a submarine. The facility truly deserved to be called a secret base. Its air purification system had clearly been designed extremely well.

Misty asked quietly, "What do we do now?"

Ethan looked in both directions. "They haven't discovered us yet. Let's go farther inside and find out what kind of facility this really is!"

Everyone recalled their Water-type Pokemon and selected partners better suited to fighting within narrow spaces.

Ethan recalled Dragonair, allowed Gengar to emerge, and also released Gardevoir. With these two Pokemon beside him, no one could harm him unless they encountered a Legendary Pokemon!

The group passed through the chamber and silently entered a corridor. Perhaps the base had truly never experienced an intrusion, because they encountered no patrolling guards and saw no further surveillance equipment. A few researchers occasionally hurried past, but Gengar scouted ahead and detected them early, allowing Ethan's group to hide with ease.

They continued forward, passed through several bulkheads, and turned into a room resembling a warehouse. There, they immediately discovered dozens of Water-type Pokemon confined inside metal cages, lying listlessly on the floor.

Misty covered her mouth in shock. "Why are all these Pokemon locked up here?"

"They're probably being used for some kind of research." Seeing no one nearby, Ethan nodded to the group. "Hurry and release them all."

Erika and the others had already intended to do exactly that. The cages were extremely sturdy, however, and they had no keys, leaving them no choice but to have their Pokemon destroy the bars directly.

Naturally, that created unavoidable noise.

Once the wild Pokemon regained their freedom, they paid no attention to caution and fled in every direction. Keeping the operation quiet was no longer possible.

Only seconds later, someone heard the commotion and rushed into the room, colliding directly with the escaping Pokemon!

"Damn it! Intruders! They've released the Pokemon! Sound the alarm!!"

Sirens instantly blared throughout the facility. That no longer mattered. Ethan's group had come to destroy the base, and reaching this point undetected was already a considerable success.

Before the Team Rocket member could take any other action, one of the group's Pokemon knocked him unconscious.

Seeing this, Ethan no longer bothered concealing their presence. He told the others, "This works just as well. Let's wreck the place and defeat everyone we encounter!!"

That was their primary objective. Ash and the others nodded excitedly, ordered their Pokemon forward, and charged down the corridor. Every person who crossed their path was swiftly defeated.

The entire base instantly descended into chaos.

Team Rocket's members could not understand when or how the intruders had entered. They scrambled out of their rooms in confusion, only to run directly into the terrifying group waiting for them!

In front of a large screen, Dr. Namba, Cassidy, and Butch recognized the familiar faces in the footage and immediately realized that disaster had arrived!

Damn it! Why do these people keep haunting us? How did they follow us all the way here!?
