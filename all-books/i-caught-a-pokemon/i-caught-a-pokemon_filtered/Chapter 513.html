Chapter 513: The State of the Battle

At the edge of the battlefield, Jolteon chased Persian through the open ground, along pipes, and over shipping containers. The two Pokemon moved so quickly they were almost a pair of blurs.

Persian was agile, but it could not match Jolteon's speed.

Whether Persian sprang a surprise attack or wheeled around to strike from an unexpected angle, Jolteon could evade it with Agility. If Persian closed in and used a move like Fury Swipes, Jolteon answered with Thunder Fang or Double Kick.

Nor was Jolteon afraid of fighting up close. Simply letting electricity run across its body was enough to make Persian wary of touching it.

The two stayed at the outskirts of the battle. Neither had won yet, but at this rate, victory would eventually tip toward Jolteon.

The most fearsome clash in the center of the field was between Crystal Steelix and Tyranitar.

After defeating Ethan's Tyranitar, Apollo's had found itself caught up in a fight with Crystal Steelix.

Tyranitar stood two meters tall and looked like a figure in heavy armor. Crystal Steelix, however, was nearly ten meters long, with a huge head and a body as hard as steel. Tyranitar's presence was no less imposing, but it was plainly outmatched in size.

That size came with immense strength. Crystal Steelix needed only to shift its body for Tyranitar to have to use all its power to resist.

Both Pokemon excelled in strength, and their battle shook the ground. No other Pokemon dared come within a dozen meters for fear of being caught in it.

Still, Tyranitar was a pseudo-legendary Pokemon. Once it fought in earnest, it managed to hold its own against Crystal Steelix despite the size difference.

Elsewhere, Golbat wheeled through the air while Kabutops strode across the ground, swinging its scythe-like forearms. The ancient, shell-backed Pokemon and its flying partner faced Ethan's Gardevoir.

Her psychic power left both of them at a severe disadvantage.

Golbat could not fly under her restraint; it hung motionless in the air. Whenever Kabutops drew close, Gardevoir teleported to another position and struck it with Psychic, knocking it to the ground.

Again and again, she kept both opponents firmly under control.

If dividing her attention between them had not made the fight more difficult, it might already have been over.

In the southern part of the battlefield, Scizor fought Lickitung and Vileplume alone. Vileplume was at a clear type disadvantage. Its attacks did little to Scizor, while Scizor's Bug-type moves punished it so badly it could scarcely fight back.

Lickitung, a Normal-type Pokemon, fared little better. Scizor's Steel typing reduced the effect of its Normal-type attacks, leaving them far less useful than they might have been against another opponent.

Despite fighting two at once, Scizor seemed to grow stronger as the battle went on. When one of its great pincers caught Lickitung's long tongue, the grip left Lickitung in agony.

To the north, Dragonite faced Houndoom and Sneasel.

Both were Dark-type Pokemon. Houndoom was also a Fire-type, while Sneasel was an Ice-type.

Houndoom's Fire-type moves posed little danger to Dragonite. Sneasel's Ice-type attacks, on the other hand, were four times as effective against it.

Fortunately, Dragonite could use its little wings to maneuver nimbly through the air, and Sneasel could not land a hit. Dragonite also knew a varied range of moves and could often surprise its opponents.

That was the state of the battle before Suicune and Raikou joined it. With the two Legendary Pokemon now in the fight, everything changed.

Muk and Arbok were the first to feel it.

They had originally faced Crystal Steelix and had been unable to do much against it. Now Raikou replaced it as their opponent, and their position only worsened.

Raikou barely glanced at them. It leaped past and let loose a Discharge that scorched both Pokemon.

Before they could even fall, a thick Hydro Pump swept across the middle of the field. Houndoom failed to dodge and was knocked down. It was strong enough to survive the powerful hit, though it could not shrug it off.

Seeing three of their Pokemon struck down in an instant made Apollo and Athena's brows twitch.

They had held some hope of winning before Raikou and Suicune appeared. The sight of those two Legendary Pokemon had wiped it away.

They exchanged a glance and saw the same desire to retreat in each other's eyes.

There was no point fighting on. It was time to leave.

At their signal, their Pokemon drew back toward them and began edging out of the open space.

Ethan noticed their intention at once. He had committed his entire team to this fight; letting the executives escape now would make all that effort pointless.

"Surround them!" he called to his Pokemon.

They spread out and blocked Apollo and Athena's routes away. The two executives realized Ethan meant to keep them there and tried to move faster.

But the situation had reversed. Outmatched in both numbers and strength now, how could they simply leave?

Ethan's Pokemon closed around them like a net, leaving only Gengar at his side to guard him. As the circle tightened, the space in which Apollo and Athena could move shrank to a small patch of ground.

Faint police sirens sounded from the street outside. The executives knew they were nearly out of time.

Escape had become almost impossible.

Athena stared at Ethan. With nearly all his Pokemon away from his side, a ruthless idea seized her. "Tyranitar, use Hyper Beam!" she shouted.

Tyranitar was Apollo's Pokemon, but it obeyed her command. It opened its great jaws. Terrifying energy gathered in its mouth, then shot straight toward Ethan!

If they could take Ethan down, his Pokemon would surely lose their composure. Their own crisis would be over at once!
