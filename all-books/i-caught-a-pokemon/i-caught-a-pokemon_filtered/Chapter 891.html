Chapter 891: Umbreon

May was more modest now than she had been when she traveled with Ash. She had met stronger people and seen more of the world. When she had first arrived at this point in the anime, she had declared that she would win the Grand Festival and show everyone. After traveling with Ethan and improving so quickly, she sounded more measured instead. That was a good change.

The friends had plenty to talk about after so long apart. As the conversation went on, though, Ash noticed that Ethan seemed distracted.

Something wrong, Ethan? he asked. No appetite? Are you feeling sick?

Before Ethan could reply, May covered a laugh. I bet he's missing someone.

After spending day and night with Miki and the others, she assumed he was already saddened by leaving them.

Ethan shook his head. May, do you remember why we went to Ecruteak in the first place?

Of course. We went to decide what Eevee should evolve in She broke off. They had forgotten all about it.

We really did let that slip, she said with a rueful smile.

You and I both did. So did the sisters. Ethan sighed. He had let himself get too comfortable in their home, spending his days laughing with them and his nights even more pleasantly. Somehow the reason for the visit had fallen out of his mind.

Eevee had contributed to that. Back in the place it used to call home, it had roamed and played all day, often out of Ethan's sight. That made it easier to forget the unfinished decision. Ho-Oh's strange blessing had distracted everyone further.

The deeper problem was that no one had settled on an evolution. It seemed Ethan would have to make the final choiceor at least narrow it down for Eevee.

After dinner they returned to the Pokemon Center. Ethan released Eevee in their room. Ash and the others kept talking nearby, leaving him alone with his decision.

He considered every form Eevee could take. Jolteon was out first; he already had one. Vaporeon and Flareon were common, and he had no shortage of Water- or Fire-type Pokemon. That left Espeon, Umbreon, Leafeon, Glaceon, and Sylveon.

He ruled out Glaceon next. He had just caught the Ice-type Snowman Snorlax, and Ice types had their natural disadvantages. With Gardevoir, Metagross, and Deoxys on his team, he had little need for another Psychic type such as Espeon.

The choice was down to Sylveon, Umbreon, and Leafeon: Fairy, Dark, or Grass. Any of the three could strengthen his roster.

Sylveon was popular, and its airy, charming appearance made it a favorite with girls. But Eevee was male, and Ethan could not quite picture him battling in that soft pink form. Nor could he imagine giving commands to Sylveon in a tournament. It did not seem to fit the composed, exacting style he liked to bring to a fight.

So Umbreon or Leafeon. Erika had a Leafeon that had evolved with a stone Ethan gave her. Zuki had an Umbreon. Either choice would echo one of the women close to him.

No wonder they had talked it over so many times without finding an answer.

After thinking for a long while, Ethan still could not decide. He took Eevee outside for some air. Perhaps a walk would clear his head.

The moon hung high above the small town. Though it was eight or nine in the evening, the pedestrian street was still busy with tourists and Coordinators taking a break before the competition.

Ethan bought a snack for himself and one for Eevee, then left the crowd and walked along the lake in the competitors' village. A second moon shimmered on the water. They strolled side by side, trading bites of their snacks as they admired it.

Other Trainers and their Pokemon were walking by the lake too. To anyone watching, Ethan and Eevee looked like any ordinary, contented pair.

There Ethan put the remaining choice to his partner. Would you rather become Leafeon or Umbreon?

Two options were easier to face than eight. Eevee looked up at the moon and spoke softly. Ethan could not understand Pokemon speech, so he called on his usual interpreter.

Gengar.

Gengar rose from Ethan's shadow. As it reached his shoulder, it casually took the snack out of his hand and began eating.

Ethan glanced at his empty hand. You can eat in a moment. What did Eevee say?

Without turning around, Gengar answered, Ever since he was little, his favorite thing has been sitting on the roof to watch the moon. He thinks it's the most beautiful thing in the world.

Ethan looked up at the bright moon. Eevee's meaning was plain enough. He liked the moon and wanted to become Umbreon.

Are you sure? Ethan asked.

Eevee met his eyes and nodded earnestly.

Then Umbreon it is. Ethan would have respected either choice. Umbreon suited him too. Tyranitar, a Rock and Dark type, was his only Pokemon with the Dark type. A pure Dark-type Umbreon would fill a gap in the team.

With the choice made, Ethan began thinking about what they would need. If I remember right, Umbreon doesn't need an Evolution stone. It needs to be nighttime and for Eevee to have a strong bond with its Trainer. I wonder if you

He never finished. White light flared around Eevee and enveloped him.

Ethan understood. Eevee had been ready to evolve all along. He had held it back because he had not decided what he wanted to become.

You weren't any more decisive than I was, Ethan said, laughing and shaking his head. If I'd known you wanted Umbreon, we could have spared ourselves all that discussion.

Then again, without Eevee's patience, they might never have gone back to Ecruteak or experienced everything that followed. Ethan had reason to thank him.

The glow caught the attention of nearby Trainers. They could not see Ethan's face clearly in the dark, but a Pokemon evolving was always an exciting moment for its Trainer and an enviable one for onlookers.

When the light faded and revealed an Umbreon, the passersby looked away again. Umbreon was less common than Flareon, Vaporeon, Jolteon, or Espeon, but its evolution needed no stone. Trainers still encountered it often enough that it was no great surprise.

Ethan did not care what anyone else thought. His Pokemon had chosen a form he liked, and it suited the team. That was enough.

As Umbreon examined his new body, Ethan lifted him high with a delighted grin. This form really does suit you. Zuki will be happy when she hears you chose Umbreon too.

Back in their room, Ash, Brock, Max, and May noticed the change immediately.

Umbreon?

You chose Umbreon?

He evolved already?

They crowded around to look. They had all seen Umbreon before, but Ethan had gone out only to clear his head. Returning with an evolved Pokemon was unexpectedly quick.

Ash stared at Umbreon and thought of Gary, his first rival back in Pallet Town. Along with Blastoise, Umbreon was one of Gary's signature Pokemon. Seeing Ethan's brought Gary vividly to mind.

May was simply happy for Ethan. She knew how much discussion and trouble Eevee's evolution had prompted. Like Ethan, she also thought Zuki would be delighted by the choice.

After a few words with the others, Ethan moved to a corner with Umbreon and called Zuki on video. She answered almost at once.

To his surprise, she was dressed for the stage and sitting in a luxury car. The sisters had evidently finished a performance and were on their way home. Ethan could hear the others beside her, and Miki and Kuni leaned partly into view.

I thought you'd wait a few days before calling, Zuki said. You couldn't hold out even one night?

Ethan only smiled. Have you just finished a show?

Yes, and it was packed, Miki said. Your timing is good. A few minutes ago, we were wondering whether you and May had reached the venue.

May leaned into the camera to greet them.

Across the room, Brock heard several women's voices and turned at once. The glimpse of beautiful, brightly dressed sisters on Ethan's screen left him frozen in place.

With so many people listening, Ethan kept the conversation brief. Then he lifted Umbreon into view. Look who this is.

Surprised cries came from the car.

Eevee!

He chose Umbreon?

All five sisters were pleased that Eevee had finally made up his mind, and startled that Umbreon was his choice. Almost together, the other four turned toward Zuki.

The corner of her mouth lifted in a small, private smile. She had an Umbreon of her own. Now Ethan's Eevee had chosen the same form, and she could not help feeling as though her man had followed her lead.
