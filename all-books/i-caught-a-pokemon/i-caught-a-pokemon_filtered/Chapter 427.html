Chapter 427: The Mineral Badge

Lucario struggled to its feet. Burn marks covered its body, and the burn itself made it tremble faintly beyond its control.

Its condition was clearly dire. It was suffering tremendous pain and had already reached the brink.

After taking two heavy attacks in succession, Lucario's stamina was nearly exhausted. Fortunately, its will remained strong. Even now, it refused to abandon the battle.

"Lucario, quickly use Close Combat!"

Jasmine clenched a fist and gave another command. In truth, she was deeply worried about Lucario and had considered stopping the match several times. But if her own Pokemon refused to surrender, how could she give up?

Ethan knew this was Lucario's final stand, so he naturally did not dare lower his guard.

Close Combat was one of the most damaging Fighting-type moves, capable of unleashing tremendous force in an instant. Relaxing now would result in a severe loss!

"Charizard, dodge it!"

Charizard leaped backward and opened the distance, avoiding the punches Lucario threw toward it.

Seeing this, Lucario drew a deep breath. Red light suddenly erupted across its body, as though all the blood within it had begun to burn. With no thought for its own survival, it charged toward Charizard!

Final Gambit!

Ethan narrowed his eyes.

This move poured everything into an attack against the opponent. The user would be left on the verge of collapse afterward, but the target would suffer damage equal to the user's remaining strength.

The worse the user's condition, the more viciously it would fight!

It seemed Lucario intended to trade injury for injury, force Charizard into the same desperate condition, and take it down as well!

Fighting-type Pokemon were often like this. The more severely injured they became, the fiercer their attacks grew, and the more willing they were to risk everything in an exchange.

But now that Ethan understood Lucario's intention, he naturally would not allow it to succeed.

At his command, Charizard flew high into the air. Facing an opponent far above it, Lucario no longer had enough strength to attack with Aura.

Weakened by both exhaustion and its burn, Lucario could not even lift its legs. It could only raise its head slightly and stare up at Charizard with unwillingness written across its face!

High above, Charizard's abdomen expanded as flames gathered inside its mouth. It opened its jaws and unleashed a stream of searing fire that crossed the distance in an instant and struck Lucario directly.

This time, Lucario did not even have the strength to evade. The flames swallowed it whole.

Fire blazed across the field. Through the glow, Lucario's body could just be seen slowly collapsing...

Several seconds later, the flames quickly faded.

When the final spark died, Lucario lay unconscious on the ground, its body blackened and scorched.

The referee stepped forward. After confirming that Lucario had completely lost consciousness, he raised the green flag and announced the result.

"Lucario is unable to battle! The winner is the challenger, Ethan!"

Jasmine recalled Lucario to its Poke Ball and approached Ethan with her expression unchanged.

"What breathtaking pacing. I accept this loss wholeheartedly!"

Charizard landed beside Ethan. He stroked its neck, then turned toward Jasmine.

"Your Metagross and Lucario are both powerful. Of all the Gym opponents I've faced, they rank among the best."

Ethan was not merely consoling Jasmine. Her two Pokemon truly were formidable, and Metagross in particular had been trained exceptionally well!

Jasmine smiled faintly. Receiving praise from a Champion was undoubtedly something to celebrate.

She reached into her pocket, took out a badge gleaming with metallic light, and offered it to Ethan.

"This is my Mineral Badge. Please keep it safe."

Ethan thanked her and accepted the badge. Looking down, he found an octagonal badge with a smooth gray mirrored surface, as though it had been fashioned by polishing an ordinary piece of steel.

Compared with other Gym Badges, the Mineral Badge was exceptionally plain.

He had barely placed it inside his badge case when Erika approached from the spectator stands.

"That was an excellent match." She looked at Ethan and smiled gently. "Especially the first round. For a while, I thought your Blastoise might lose."

Knowing she was joking, Ethan played along and teased her in return. "Then I must have disappointed you. Clearly, my skill carried the day~"

His smug, self-satisfied expression made both women cover their mouths and laugh. The atmosphere was relaxed and friendly.

After chatting a little longer, Ethan announced that they should leave.

Jasmine wanted them to remain a while, but Ethan declined.

She clearly wished to spend more time with Erika. Yet when Ethan remained firm, she could only accept the decision reluctantly.

Jasmine turned to Erika and said sincerely, "Miss Erika, I hope you'll visit me again after your journey ends!"

"Don't worry." Erika nodded. "When I have time, I'll definitely come back to see you. And this time, I promise I won't forget you again!"

...

After leaving the Olivine Gym, Ethan first returned to the Pokemon Center and had both Pokemon that had fought treated. Once their treatment was complete, he did not transfer Blastoise back. He intended to keep it beside him for a while longer.

After eating lunch at the Pokemon Center, the pair lingered no longer. They prepared to continue toward the next city with a Gym.

Two possible routes once again lay before them.

They could travel by sea, pass through the Whirl Islands, reach Cianwood City, and challenge the Cianwood Gym.

Or they could head directly south, first reach Goldenrod City and challenge the Goldenrod Gym, then continue southeast to Azalea Town and challenge the Azalea Gym.

Another choice...

Only those three Gyms remained. Once Ethan earned three more badges, he could enter the Silver Conference. Regardless of which direction they chose, he would need to challenge all three. The only question was their order.

Rather than waste time thinking about it, Ethan handed the decision to Erika and let her choose their route.

Erika hesitated briefly before settling on Cianwood City.

"Let's go to Cianwood." She gazed toward the distant blue sea and sighed. "We've spent so many days flying through the sky or traveling overland. It would be nice to change our surroundings and relax aboard a ship for once."

"A ship..."

Ethan had no objection. He walked with her toward Olivine's docks. Yet he could not tell whether it was his imagination, because since leaving the Olivine Gym, something about Erika seemed to have changed.

The change was difficult to define.

The clearest sign was that whenever their eyes met, she lowered her head and looked away, unable to hold his gaze!
