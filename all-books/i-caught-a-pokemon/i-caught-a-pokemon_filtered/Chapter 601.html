Chapter 601: Then I'll Play Along

Misty recalled Psyduck and gave Ethan her place in the Trainer's area.

Across the field, Anthony took the change of opponent in stride. Whoever he faced, he was convinced his Pelipper could not lose.

That's quite an impressive way to battle, Ethan said with a smile, clapping his hands. But it's a cheap trick, and hardly sporting. Someone is bound to expose it sooner or later. When that happens, won't you be ashamed to face all the Trainers you've beaten?

His words struck Anthony like a spike. His expression changed, and his heart began to pound.

Impossible! Had this man discovered Pelipper's secret?

That could not be. Pelipper had never opened its huge bill all the way, or even wide enough to reveal anything inside. No one could have spotted it by watching the battle.

Yet what Ethan had just said made it sound as though he knew something.

What are you talking about? Anthony demanded. He forced himself to keep his voice steady. I'm not using any trick. Pelipper can do all this because it trained hard!

Is that so? Fine. I'm not going to argue with you. If that's what you say, so be it.

Ethan shook his head. Anthony's excuse did not concern him. In truth, once he realized that this supposedly strongest Pokemon was little more than a contrived combination, he had lost interest. Still, since he had come all this way, he might as well battle it and experience this combination's strength for himself. Then the time he had spent here would not be entirely wasted.

Besides, Anthony had beaten two of Misty's Pokemon in succession. Ethan had to help her settle the score.

Misty had heard the implication in Ethan's words. Ethan, are you saying there's something wrong with his Pelipper?

He pressed a hand down gently, signaling her to be patient. Rather than explain, he smiled. Watch and you'll see. I'll show you what he's really up to.

Seeing Ethan so sure of himself made Anthony uneasy. For a moment, he even thought of backing out.

Then he caught Ethan's knowing half smile, gritted his teeth, and held his ground.

So what if this man knew something? Anthony's scheme had helped him beat at least a hundred Trainers. Which of them had ever defeated him?

As long as no one could win, his Pelipper was still the strongest Pokemon in the world!

This match would be like all the others. Pelipper would use those same methods to defeat this man and teach him a harsh lesson.

Ethan had no idea Anthony was wrestling with his doubts and had resolved to make him pay. Choosing to be cautious, he passed over the newly caught Pokemon, including Torchic. Instead, he took out a Poke Ball and sent out Dragonite.

Anthony's brow furrowed as soon as Dragonite appeared.

After running this private Gym for so long and battling Trainers of every sort, he had begun to recognize patterns.

Young children with unseasoned faces and restless movements were usually beginners who had only just set out. Most of their Pokemon were weak and unevolved.

Anthony could beat those Trainers easily, often without resorting to his special methods at all.

At the other end were Trainers weathered by long days on the road. They were much harder to deal with.

They had more experience and a wider variety of Pokemon. Some were formidable enough that Anthony had to give everything he had and exploit type advantages just to scrape out a win.

But even those Trainers were not the most troublesome. The handful he truly dreaded looked very much like the man standing before him.

They met any situation with an air of calm indifference. Their Pokemon had been through countless battles and had grown into their final forms. With that much experience, even an unfavorable type matchup did not seem to hinder them as much.

Ethan and his Dragonite clearly belonged to that group.

And Ethan had all but named his secret. Anthony's eyelid twitched as a bad feeling rose in his chest.

There was no backing out now. If he made an excuse and refused to fight, word would spread. The reputation and business he had spent so long building would be ruined, and he would become a laughingstock.

But he had little confidence in winning, either.

All he could do was hope Pelipper performed as usual and beat this man.

While Anthony fretted, the two Pokemon took their positions.

There was no referee and there were no special rules, so no one needed to announce the start. As soon as Pelipper was ready, Ethan whistled. Dragonite skimmed low over the ground and charged straight at it.

Anthony snapped back to the battle and hurriedly focused on the field.

Pelipper, use Wing Attack!

He had never battled a Dragonite before, but he knew it was a Dragon- and Flying-type Pokemon. Pelipper's electric shocks, vines, and any Water- or Fire-type attacks it might use would not give him the type advantage against a Dragon type.

Fortunately, Pelipper was a Flying type too. Its Flying-type attacks would still work normally, so he was not without options.

Pelipper had barely raised a wing before Ethan understood Anthony's plan. He did not change his command. Dragonite continued closing in.

The two Pokemon crossed paths low in the air and separated again after only a brief clash.

That single exchange set off alarm bells in Anthony's mind.

Both Pokemon had used Wing Attack. At the moment they met, however, Dragonite had turned slightly and avoided Pelipper's strike. Pelipper was not so lucky. Dragonite's small wing hit it, sending it wobbling so badly that it nearly fell to the ground.

Anthony had seen enough battles to judge their strength from that first exchange. Dragonite was unquestionably stronger and more skilled than his Pelipper.
