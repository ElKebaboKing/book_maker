Chapter 624: Misty: Nice Shot!

Voltorb looked remarkably like a Poke Ball. Even now, some people claimed that humans had modeled the first modern Poke Balls on it. The story might be nothing more than a rumor or a joke, but plenty believed it. Voltorb certainly resembled a Poke Ball.

It had no arms or legs, only a round body with a red upper half, a white lower half, and a pair of eyes. Stranger still, people had discovered Voltorb around the same time Poke Balls first went on sale. The earliest sightings were said to be inside a company that made them. Voltorb also appeared in power plants. Why it looked so much like a Poke Ball remained a mystery, which only encouraged the belief that the two were connected.

One theory held that Voltorb had come into being when a Poke Ball was exposed to a powerful pulse. Its resemblance to the ball served as camouflage. People sometimes mistook it for one and touched it, only to receive a numbing shock. The slightest provocation could even make it explode. Experienced Trainers handled Voltorb carefully.

Despite the risk, it remained popular with Electric-type Trainers. Wattson's willingness to use one in the opening round showed how well he knew his own Voltorb. He wouldn't put himself in danger lightly.

At the edge of the field, Watt saw both Pokemon were ready and gave the signal. The Gym battle began.

Combusken immediately ran toward Voltorb on its two strong legs. Since catching it, Ethan had noticed its gift for speed. It enjoyed running and was good at it. If Charizard, his other Fire-type Pokemon, was strong and unwilling to yield, Combusken was quick and restless. Ethan already had a general idea of how he wanted to train it, including after it evolved into Blaziken.

Wattson watched it close in and gave his first command without concern. Voltorb, use Screech!

Screech, sometimes called a piercing noise, lowered the target's Defense. Starting with it suggested Wattson meant to weaken Combusken before finishing it with a powerful attack. Ethan had fought enough battles to see the plan from that one move.

Voltorb flashed blue. The air around it shuddered as a violent sound spread outward in every direction. Combusken could not dodge an invisible attack that covered the whole area. And whether it meant to use fire or a close-range Fighting move, it needed to get nearer to Voltorb.

Screech passed through Combusken just as it reached the middle of the field. At the same time, Combusken opened its mouth and sprayed Embers toward Voltorb.

The sound reverberated through Combusken's body. It grimaced and dropped to one knee. Across the field, Wattson took the oncoming flames seriously. Voltorb could be unstable; even a small injury might trigger an explosion, and fire was hardly a small threat.

Voltorb, use Rollout!

It was a smart command. Rolling away would evade the Embers and bring Voltorb closer to Combusken. Combusken could use Fighting-type moves up close, but Voltorb had Electric attacks such as Discharge and the threat of an explosion. It needn't fear close combat. At worst it could blow itself up and take its opponent down with it, a tactic Voltorb Trainers often used against difficult foes.

Voltorb rolled forward. Its smooth, round body picked up exceptional speed on the flat field. In a blink it passed beyond the flames and raced toward Combusken.

Ethan smiled faintly. He had already seen what Wattson intended and prepared an answer. As Voltorb drew close enough to collide, he called, Double Kick!

Combusken sprang up and kicked the oncoming Voltorb. It was like kicking a soccer ball: Voltorb shot back the way it had come. Before it could use Discharge, it was rolling toward its original position.

Nice shot! Misty blurted from the stands. Embarrassed, she quickly fell silent. This was a Pokemon battle, not a soccer match.

Voltorb finally stopped spinning and looked upjust as another wave of fire came toward it. This time it could not use Rollout to escape. The Embers hit directly, and Wattson's heart leaped.

Even after raising Voltorb for years, he could not know exactly how much damage would make it explode. It might endure several attacks without trouble, or a collision with a small stone while rolling might set it off. As he watched the flames cover it, he could only hope it would hold together a little longer.

Perhaps his silent plea worked. When the Embers faded, Voltorb had a few scorch marks but was otherwise unharmed.

Excellent! Wattson laughed again, his hearty voice filling the Gym. He hardly sounded like an old man. Now it's our turn to fight back!

That was easier said than done. Voltorb and Combusken stood at an awkward distance: close enough for Combusken's Embers to reach, but beyond the range of Voltorb's Discharge. If Voltorb tried Rollout again, Combusken would simply kick it back as before.

They tested that standoff several times. Wattson frowned. It clearly wasn't working, and for a moment he couldn't decide what to do next.
