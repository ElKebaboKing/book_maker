Chapter 448: It Was Only Bite

The battle continued on the field.

After discovering that Hypnosis had no effect, Trinity made an immediate decision and ordered Poliwrath to continue attacking with Water Gun.

The stream curved through the air and once again struck the red Gyarados with perfect accuracy.

But the red Gyarados completely ignored the damage from Water Gun. Even when the pillar collapsed and countless chunks of stone crashed against its body, it paid them no attention!

Trinity clicked her tongue inwardly.

This is completely absurd...

Precisely because she also owned a Gyarados, Trinity understood how terrifying this red Gyarados truly was.

Water Gun was one thing. Due to the type matchup, it could inflict only half its usual damage upon a Water-type Pokemon. But the falling rocks caused very real physical damage!

Gyarados did not possess outstanding physical defense. Being struck by so many rocks and continuing as though nothing had happened defied all common sense!

Even an armored vehicle struck from above by so many enormous stones could hardly escape without a mark!

Water Gun might not be causing any visible damage for now, but Trinity firmly believed that if Poliwrath used it often enough, the red Gyarados would eventually reach its limit!

Every little bit of stamina it consumed would help!

In the water, the red Gyarados turned and swam after its escaping prey. Its massive head remained half submerged, red eyes fixed upon its opponent as its enormous serpentine body approached Poliwrath little by little. The pressure was overwhelming!

Once it reached Poliwrath's pillar, the red Gyarados raised its head again and gave the structure a light bump!

Bang!

It did not appear to use much force, but before its immense body, the stone pillar collapsed almost instantly.

At the top, Poliwrath repeated its previous maneuver. The moment Gyarados struck, it took a running start, leaped again, and sprang toward another nearby pillar.

This pillar stood slightly farther away, nearly ten meters from the first. Fortunately, Poliwrath's powerful legs and running momentum allowed it to land on the very top by the narrowest of margins.

Ignoring the falling debris, the red Gyarados turned around and began closing in on Poliwrath once more.

It appeared neither anxious nor hurried, moving like a remarkably patient hunter.

By contrast, Poliwrath barely found its footing before frantically launching another Water Gun. When the red Gyarados drew near, it had no choice but to leap toward yet another pillar.

This was already the fourth pillar beneath its feet. With virtually no time to breathe, it moved, attacked, moved again, and attacked again without pause. Sweat poured from its body. Worse, the next pillar was even farther away than the previous ones, a full fourteen or fifteen meters!

That distance was too great to cross with a simple jump!

Sure enough, Poliwrath began to fall after covering barely more than half the gap!

Fortunately, just before hitting the water, it caught the base of the pillar. It hurriedly climbed the rest of the way with both arms and legs.

But the delay allowed the red Gyarados to close in. This time, Poliwrath did not dare attack. Before it could even catch its breath, it was forced to leap toward the next pillar.

It resembled a frog fleeing in utter panic, but no matter where it went, it could never escape the enormous shadow looming over it!

Stone pillars fell one after another under the red Gyarados's attacks. Soon, every pillar had been destroyed except for the last one beneath Poliwrath.

Poliwrath stood atop the remains of a collapsed pillar and panted heavily. It was completely isolated now, with nowhere left to escape!

The spectators placed themselves in Trinity's position and swallowed nervously, feeling her profound fear and helplessness!

Where could Poliwrath go now?

They looked across the battlefield. Poliwrath's half was filled with piles of broken stone submerged beneath the water. More than a dozen intact pillars still stood on Ethan's side, but reaching them required crossing most of the field. With its remaining stamina, could Poliwrath truly make it?

Even if it possessed enough strength to swim across, the red Gyarados would inevitably catch it!

Difficult. Far too difficult!

Everyone had believed that forcing Ethan to withdraw Blastoise would open a clear road for Trinity and reveal the first light of victory. Instead, she had encountered a boss monster!

Worse, this was the kind of ultimate boss that could not be defeated at all!

Trinity's face was creased with worry, but the battle did not bend to her wishes. Under her helpless gaze, the red Gyarados swam toward the final stone pillar beneath Poliwrath!

Poliwrath looked toward its Trainer and saw the deep concern in her eyes. Yet it understood that with so little stamina remaining, it no longer possessed the strength to keep fighting...

The red Gyarados reached the last pillar. But contrary to the audience's expectations, it did not destroy the structure.

Instead, most of its body rose from the water. Its enormous form climbed higher and higher above the surface until its head towered well past the top of the pillar. Only then did it stop!

At last, the audience gained a true sense of just how enormous the red Gyarados was!

Less than half of its body had emerged, yet it already stood a full head taller than the stone pillar rising from the water!

The red Gyarados looked down upon Poliwrath at the pillar's summit and suddenly opened its enormous jaws. A deafening roar erupted. Poliwrath felt as though a moisture-laden hurricane had struck it and was forced onto all fours simply to keep from being blown off the pillar!

To the spectators, this hardly resembled a Pokemon battle. It looked more like a tiny human attempting to fight some colossal beast!

In the command area, Trinity's heart rose into her throat.

Poliwrath...

By now, she could already imagine the fate waiting for Poliwrath. Even so, she did not choose to recall it.

Defeat was inevitable, and she was deeply worried for her Pokemon, but surrendering outright was impossible!

As a Pokemon Trainer, Trinity possessed her own dignity and limits. With so many spectators watching and the championship at stake, surrendering to an unbeatable opponent would turn her into a mere backdrop. No matter how highly Ethan was praised afterward, everyone who remembered this match would mock her!

Yielding without a fight has never been my style!

"Poliwrath, use Take Down!"

Trinity shook her head and loudly issued her final command.

At the sound of her voice, Poliwrath gathered the last of its strength and launched a charge at the red Gyarados with no thought for its own survival!

It ran forward in long strides, moving faster with every step. Then it leaped and slammed toward the red Gyarados's neck!

For most Pokemon, the neck was one of the body's weak points!

Faced with the attack, the red Gyarados merely opened its enormous mouth, revealed its sharp teeth, and lowered its head. In a single bite, it swallowed the charging Poliwrath whole!

"Poliwrath!"

In an instant, Trinity's scream was the only sound echoing over the entire arena...

"This is terrible!" the announcer's voice suddenly rose. "Poliwrath has been eaten by the red Gyarados!"

The audience clearly saw the red Gyarados close its mouth. A second later, its jaws made an unmistakable chewing motion!

The crowd erupted. People sprang to their feet, unable to believe their eyes!

Was this truly still a Pokemon battle?

Amid the chaos, Ethan alone remained standing calmly in place.

Of course he could see the audience's reaction, but they were imagining far too much. No matter how ferocious his red Gyarados was, Ethan would never train it to behave that way!

Unlike a wild Pokemon, it had long been caught and trained by a Trainer and had left its most primitive habits behind. How could it possibly do such a thing?

It had taken Poliwrath into its mouth only because the red Gyarados was so enormous. The Dark-type move Bite had simply become a much more savage-looking act of swallowing and chewing!

Sure enough, several seconds later, Gyarados abruptly opened its mouth. With a forceful spit, it sent Poliwrath flying out whole.

Like a deflated ball, Poliwrath tumbled onto the stone pillar with blood covering its body and fell completely unconscious.

Only after seeing the red Gyarados spit Poliwrath out did the audience release a collective breath and sit down again with lingering fear. Even Trinity could not resist wiping the cold sweat from her forehead.

They were not making a fuss over nothing. The red Gyarados was simply too frightening, causing everyone to instinctively imagine the worst!
