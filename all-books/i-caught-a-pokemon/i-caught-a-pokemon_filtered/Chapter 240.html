Chapter 240: The Red-Haired Woman and the Hotel

They had gone only a few steps when Ethan spotted several groups of hotel employees stationed around the harbor. Dressed in distinctive uniforms, waving flags, and calling through megaphones, they competed for the arriving passengers.

"Welcome! Come stay at the Green Tangerine Hotel!"

"Still need a room? Check in at the Seven Oranges Express Hotel!"

"The Little Kumquat Inn has plenty of vacancies! Clean rooms and low prices!"

The calls came from every direction. A large crowd was disembarking, and the hotel staff had come to the port instead of waiting for guests to find them. They pressed forward like sharks drawn by the scent of blood. Competition on Kumquat Island was clearly fierce.

Among them, one curvy red-haired woman stood out. It was less her looks than her fiery manner and powerful voice. She could drown out everyone around her without a megaphone.

"Travelers, over here! If you're choosing a hotel, choose the Kumquat Hotel! It's the best in the whole Orange Archipelago. Rooms and food alike, I guarantee you'll be satisfied!"

Ethan smiled at the bold claim. The best hotel in the entire archipelago? Probably the most expensive too.

He and Sabrina walked past her toward the other hotels. The woman noticed him and stepped into their path.

"Wait a moment, you two!" She smiled at Ethan's puzzled look. "You're Trainers, aren't you?"

When he nodded, she went on. "I saw your Charizard land. You've raised it well. Our hotel is perfect for traveling Trainers. We have all sorts of Pokemon training equipment and even dance fitness classes for them. You can stay and train in one place!"

Ethan blinked. "Dance fitness classes just for Pokemon?"

"Exactly!" She grinned. "The dancing gets their blood flowing, loosens their joints, and helps them regulate their breathing. It's a wonderful training method. I bet you haven't tried it. So what are you waiting for? Come stay with us!"

Without waiting for an answer, she waved over a server to look after them, then turned back to recruit more tourists.

Ethan raised an eyebrow. Pokemon dancing sounded remarkably like Rudy's approach at Trovita Gym. The woman had been so enthusiastic, though, that he had not found a moment to refuse. He looked at Sabrina. She gave a slight nod, so they followed the server out of the harbor.

He led them several hundred meters along the coastal road to a high-rise building. The sign identified it as the Kumquat Hotel. Lights shone against its facade in the dark, making it look grand. At more than twenty stories tall, it was substantial enough to support the woman's boast, at least from the outside.

"Please come in." The server bowed slightly and gestured toward the entrance.

Ethan and Sabrina entered. The lobby surprised them at once. More than twenty male attendants stood in two lines and bowed together.

"Welcome!"

While Ethan was still taking in the reception, a man and a woman approached with friendly smiles.

"Welcome. We're the managers here. Would you like to check in?"

Managers greeting guests for an ordinary room? Ethan could hardly believe it. Like the red-haired woman, everyone was almost excessively welcoming. Still, it made a good first impression. Whatever the rooms were like, treating ordinary travelers as honored guests was a pleasant way to start.

At the desk, Ethan booked one room for them both. Sabrina looked surprised. Since her period had begun and they would not be intimate tonight, she had expected him to sleep separately. Ethan simply shrugged at her expression. They cared about each other; sharing a room was hardly only about sex.

He had expected the rate to be high, but the price was unexpectedly reasonable. He had braced himself to be overcharged. Perhaps he had judged the place unfairly.

They took the elevator to the seventeenth floor, entered their room, put away their luggage, and looked through a small booklet the staff had given them. It was a visitor's guide to the island.

Kumquat Island was a well-known tourist destination. Its specialties, Ethan learned, were seafood and hot springs said to be good for the skin. Unfortunately, Sabrina could not use the hot springs while she was on her period, so she would miss one of the island's attractions. He wondered whether she could at least enjoy the seafood. If she had to avoid that too, the trip would have lost both of its local pleasures for her.

The night passed without incident.

The next day, they first visited the dance class. A woman in exercise clothes was teaching a group of Pokemon. The steps looked much like those at Trovita Gym. Ethan quickly lost interest.

After breakfast at the hotel, he asked a server how to reach the Gym. To his surprise, Kumquat Gym was inside the hotel itself.

He had never heard of a Gym built into a hotel. At the very least, the hotel and the Gym Leader must have some sort of partnership, if they were not owned by the same person.

The server led them through the lobby to an arena in a rear hall. So this was Kumquat Gym. It was lavishly equipped, though its lack of a separate entrance or exterior still felt strange to Ethan.

A woman stood on the far Trainer's side of the field. Ethan looked at her and recognized the red-haired woman from the harbor. She must be the Gym Leader.

He sighed to himself. He had forgotten much of the story of the Orange Islands. If he remembered it better, he would have recognized her at once. Then again, even if he had, he would still have had to pretend they were meeting for the first time.
