Chapter 570: Ash Is Eliminated

Bayleef was one of Johto's three starter Pokemon and a pure Grass type. Against Scizor's Bug and Steel types, it was at a severe disadvantage both attacking and defending.

Ash often fought well despite a type disadvantage, but against Ethan this time, he produced no miracle.

Razor Leaf and Magical Leaf barely troubled Scizor. Even when Bayleef landed Normal-type moves such as Tackle and Body Slam, they did only half their usual damage.

Scizor, meanwhile, pursued Bayleef without pause. Slightly faster and aided by its small wings, it swung its enormous pincers at Bayleef again and again.

Bayleef could neither overpower it nor shake it off.

With no other choice, Ash recalled Bayleef before Scizor could knock it out.

He had only one fresh Pokemon left. Ash glanced down at his feet, and his yellow Pikachu understood at once. It dropped to all fours and ran toward the center of the field.

"Oh?" The announcer sounded surprised. "At this crucial moment, Ash is sending in a Pikachu? Compared with Charizard and his other Pokemon, this little one looks perfectly ordinary. Why save it for last? Is there something special about it?"

That was only how outsiders saw Pikachu.

Anyone who knew Ash knew Pikachu was the partner he trusted mostand his strongest.

Its performances were wildly unpredictable, though. Pikachu could stand up to a Legendary Pokemon one day and lose to a rookie's starter the next. Not even Ash could be sure which Pikachu would show up today.

Pikachu reached the middle of the field and called brightly to Ethan. Confidence shone on its face.

It looked just like Ash when he was feeling sure of himself. Perhaps Pokemon really did take after the humans they spent so much time with.

Ethan stroked his chin as he watched it.

Knowing Pikachu as he did, he hesitated to send out a Pokemon with an obvious type advantage against it. That might be exactly what triggered one of Ash's improbable comebacks. The safer choice was to let Scizor continue and see how Pikachu fought.

"Scizor, I'm counting on you for this round. Be careful!"

Despite its Bug and Steel types, Scizor was not especially vulnerable to Electric attacks. Its Steel-type moves would be less effective against Pikachu, but otherwise the matchup posed little problem.

Scizor nodded to Ethan, walked up to Pikachu, and looked down at it.

Pikachu refused to be intimidated. It planted all four paws, raised its tail high, and readied itself for battle.

"Ash is on the brink now!" the announcer called. "If Pikachu falls, all he'll have left is an injured Bayleef. But he's chosen to put his faith in Pikachu, so let's cheer it on and see what it can do!"

At the start signal, both Pokemon rushed forward almost together.

Scizor reached out with one huge pincer. Pikachu nimbly jumped onto its arm, twisted, and swung its tail toward Scizor's face.

Most fighters avoided going for the face. Pikachu had tried it at the very first opportunity.

Scizor calmly brought up its other pincer to shield its head. Thunk! Pikachu's Iron Tail struck the steel claw with a dull metallic clang.

The move caught the announcer's attention. "Oh! This Pikachu is different from others of its kind. It's quick on its feet and surprisingly strong. There really is more to it than meets the eye!"

They separated after that first exchange.

"Pikachu, use Thunder Shock!"

"Scizor, use Double Team!" Ethan ordered.

As Pikachu began its attack, Scizor produced five copies of itself. Six identical Scizor now stood before Pikachu, leaving it no way to tell which one was real.

Pikachu looked from one to another, then chose the Scizor standing where the original had been before Double Team. Golden electricity shot from its body toward that figure. It had guessed wrong. The current passed through an illusion and struck a rock behind it.

At the same time, the other Scizor images rushed Pikachu. Every one raised its right pincer and swung down.

"Pikachu, dodge!"

Pikachu glanced frantically between them, trying to pick out the real Scizor. It found out only when a steel pincer struck it and sent it tumbling aside.

Pikachu fell toward the shattered rocks. The moment it landed, it sprang up, raised its tail, and formed a large ball of electricity at the tip. It hurled the Electro Ball at Scizor.

From the Trainer's area, Ethan could see that Pikachu's gaze was steady and its attention fixed. It was giving this battle everything it had.

The Electro Ball still missed. Scizor used Agility as Pikachu released it and escaped the attack's path. Pikachu's counterattack struck nothing.

That exchange set the pattern for the rest of the fight. Both Pokemon battled fiercely, trading blows until they were covered in wounds and gasping for breath.

Against a weak opponent, Pikachu seemed to grow weak too. Against a strong one, it rose to match that strength.

At the height of the struggle, both Pokemon used Quick Attack and charged straight at each other. They crossed in a blurand a moment later, both collapsed.

The referee stepped forward. After confirming that neither could battle, he crossed his flags before his chest.

"Scizor and Pikachu are both unable to battle! This round is a draw!"

The crowd erupted in cheers. Both Pokemon had fought brilliantly, giving the spectators everything they could have hoped to see.

The simultaneous knockout surprised them even more, leaving the match poised on a knife's edge.

The announcer praised Pikachu too. "Incredible! Ash's Pikachu was far more formidable than it looked. It brought down Ethan's Scizor! Even though the round ended in a draw, that performance deserves our admiration!"

And yet...

Many spectators felt a pang when they remembered that Ash had only one Pokemon left, and Bayleef was already hurt.

Ash's unpredictable, varied style had won him many fans during the tournament. They believed he had the strength to reach at least the semifinals. Now that elimination by Ethan seemed inevitable, they could not help feeling sorry for him.

Against someone else, perhaps he might have gone further.

Ash walked onto the field and picked up Pikachu, which could barely move.

"Pika..."

Pikachu managed to open its eyes and weakly call to him.

"I know." Ash smiled gently. "You brought down Ethan's Scizor. You did great. Rest now."

He meant it. Ash had ended Ethan's unbeaten run and knocked out two of his Pokemon, an achievement beyond most Trainers. Even if he was eliminated, he could be proud of that.

Ash carried Pikachu to the friends and family section and entrusted it to Misty. Then he returned to his position, turned his cap backward, and took out Bayleef's Poke Ball with a determined look.

He knew as well as anyone that his injured Bayleef could not realistically defeat Ethan's four remaining Pokemon.

But Ash was not the kind of Trainer to surrender. As long as one of his Pokemon could still fight, he would fight beside it to the end.

"Go, Bayleef!"

He threw the Poke Ball across the field. Blue light flashed, and Bayleef appeared again. Ethan gave Ash an approving smile, then slowly took out a Poke Ball of his own.

"Unless something unexpected happens, this should be our last round in this tournament," Ethan said. His voice was encouraging. "You've grown so much, and you've given me an exciting battle. To thank you, I'm going to fight you with everything I have."

Ethan threw the ball. When Ash saw what emerged, he could only shake his head and laugh.

Gardevoir. Of course Ethan had chosen Gardevoir.

Even now, he was bringing out a Pokemon like that. He was giving Ash no mercy at all!

...

"Congratulations to Ethan! He has defeated Ash and advanced to the semifinals!"

As the announcer called the result, Ethan recalled Gardevoir. He walked forward through the audience's cheers and shook Ash's hand.

"Ethan, you're amazing. I lost to you again."

Ash was not dispirited as some might have expected. He scratched his cheek and smiled sheepishly.

"You were amazing too," Ethan said with a smile. "You beat my Dragonite and Scizor, didn't you? Keep going. I'm sure you'll be even stronger by the time you reach the next region."

Ethan had mentioned in an earlier conversation that he planned to travel through Hoenn after the Silver Conference. Ash had been eager to go as well and had already made plans. He looked forward to meeting Ethan there.

"I will!" Ash clenched his fist. "I'll get stronger, and then I'll challenge you again!"

Ethan left through the competitors' tunnel and returned to the lounge, where Erika greeted him with a radiant smile.

"Congratulations! You've made the semifinals!"

Now that they were alone, Ethan no longer hid his happiness. He drew her into his arms and celebrated with a long, passionate kiss.

He felt a little bad for Ash, but he had won, and he was happy about it.

There were two matches today. Ethan's battle with Ash had been the first. Jimmy and Harrison would meet in the afternoon.

The winner would face Ethan the day after tomorrow.

They left the stadium for the Pokemon Center on the ground floor of the dormitory building. Ethan handed his injured Pokemon to Nurse Joy, and then he and Erika ate lunch at the restaurant.

Ash's group soon came into the lobby with Delia and Professor Oak.

With Ash eliminated, Delia had no reason to stay. She had wanted to take him back to Pallet Town, but Ash refused.

Having come all this way, Ash wanted to remain until the tournament ended. He especially wanted to see Ethan win the championship. Unable to persuade him, Delia packed her bags and returned to Kanto with Professor Oak.

After seeing them off, the others gathered together. By one in the afternoon, both Ethan's and Ash's Pokemon had been treated and regained their strength. Jimmy and Harrison's match began just then.

"Who do you think will win?" Misty asked, watching the screen.

Only then did Ethan learn that Ash, Misty, and Brock had visited Ho-Oh Temple before entering Silver Town. They had met Harrison there and had become friends of a sort.

Harrison was a Trainer from Hoenn. Like Ethan, he had come to Johto specifically to challenge the Silver Conference.

"Probably Harrison." Ethan leaned back on the soft sofa. He had never met Harrison, but he gave his judgment anyway. "Jimmy's still a little young. Harrison has a steadier style, and his Pokemon are stronger overall."

The others nodded. They could see Jimmy was still somewhat inexperienced and would likely have trouble beating Harrison.

They ate dessert while watching the match. Just as they had expected, Jimmy fell behind early.

Compared with his match against Marina, Jimmy began this one in top form.

Even so, while the exchanges looked close, his Pokemon could not match Harrison's across the board. When Jimmy finally sent out his ace, Typhlosion, it faced Harrison's Blaziken. Jimmy had never seen this Hoenn Pokemon before and was plainly at a loss.
