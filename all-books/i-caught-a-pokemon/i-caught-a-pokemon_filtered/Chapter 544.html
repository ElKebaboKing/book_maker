Chapter 544: In Pursuit

Sneasel stood less than a meter tall, but it was quick and agile. It dodged a Fire Punch from Dragonite, then darted in and raked its sharp claws across Dragonite's body, leaving long scratches.

Before it could press its advantage, a shadow flashed before it. That energetic Eevee had charged in again.

What a nuisance. Eevee was not especially strong, but Sneasel could not shake it. It seemed to enjoy chasing Sneasel, as though the pursuit itself were a game. Whenever Sneasel had Dragonite where it wanted it, Eevee jumped in and disrupted its rhythm.

Sneasel found a moment to look at its two followers. They were not doing well either.

To its surprise, Machop and Machoke had the type advantage, yet they still could not beat the human's Tyranitar and Scizor.

The gap in strength was too wide for type advantage to make up.

Seeing that, Sneasel pushed itself to its limit. It forced Dragonite and Eevee back for a moment, then leaped in front of its followers.

It bared its teeth and barked a few threats at Tyranitar and Scizor. Then, without looking back, it led Machop and Machoke into the mountain forest.

"It... it ran away?"

The temple attendant's face brightened. Had the Sneasel that had troubled them for so long really been driven off?

He had seen the fight clearly, though. Sneasel had barely been hurt. Why had it chosen to flee?

Still, its departure meant the shrine that held the sacred flame was back in the temple's hands.

Ethan glanced at the attendant and shook his head. "Don't celebrate yet. Sneasel has only gone away for now. If we don't settle this properly, it'll come back sooner or later."

The attendant's face fell. He looked hopefully at Ethan. "Could you help us drive them away from here?"

Compared with the Trainers he had asked before, this man's Pokemon seemed much stronger. For the first time, the attendant felt hopeful.

Ethan considered the request and did not answer at once.

Sneasel was a wild Pokemon. Perhaps it had come here because it had nowhere else to go and had made the shrine its territory.

It had never gone down the mountain to attack people. It only fought when someone approached. That suggested it was not malicious; it simply wanted others to keep away.

If they drove it off, where would it go? Would it have to wander in search of another home?

But leaving it in control of the temple was no solution either.

Ethan explained his thoughts to the attendant, who was silent for a while.

"I see. I hadn't thought it through," the attendant said with a sigh. Working at a temple, he knew he ought to show compassion. "I was only trying to make it leave. I never considered things from its point of view. Now that you've pointed it out, I realize Sneasel must have left its own group to make a home here."

Erika thought for a moment. "Then why not call it back and talk? If it truly likes this place and doesn't want to leave, it could stay and live alongside you, as long as it agrees not to interfere when you take the sacred flame to the tournament."

The attendant hesitated. "But you saw it. It won't talk to us."

"That's easy enough," Ethan said. "If it won't talk, we'll battle it until it will."

With that, he led his Pokemon into the forest. Erika went with him, following the direction in which Sneasel had fled.

Mt. Silver's forests were known for their thick growth and sturdy trees. Towering trunks stood everywhere, and anyone unfamiliar with the terrain could easily get lost by venturing too far in.

Fortunately, Sneasel, Machop, and Machoke had entered only moments earlier. Ethan could follow the fresh footprints they had left behind.

Most were Machoke's. Its feet were large, and its body was packed with muscle. Wherever it stepped on soft ground, it left a clear print.

Perhaps Sneasel had not expected anyone to follow.

After only a few hundred meters, Ethan and Erika found all three Pokemon resting beneath a tree.

They were startled to see the humans and scrambled into a fighting formation.

"Sneasel, wait. We aren't here to fight. We only want to talk about the temple!"

Ethan tried words before force. He held off on ordering an attack, hoping they could settle the matter by talking.

But he had underestimated how wild Sneasel was. He had barely finished speaking when it growled, and all three charged.

So much for that. Just as Ethan had said, they would have to beat Sneasel before it would listen.

"Dragonite, you and the others, go!"

Ethan waved his Pokemon forward again. This time he changed the pairings to give them different practice. Dragonite and Eevee faced Machoke and Machop, while Tyranitar and Scizor took on their leader, Sneasel.

The change, combined with the strength of Ethan's Pokemon, immediately made a difference.

Dragonite, Tyranitar, and Scizor were no longer facing opponents that countered their types. They could fight at full strength, and their opponents gave ground almost at once.

Sneasel, for example, had been comfortable against Dragonite. Now Tyranitar and Scizor confronted it together. It would have struggled with either one alone, let alone both of them.

Before long, it was panting and drenched in sweat from dodging their attacks.

When Sneasel found a chance to look at its followers, Dragonite and Eevee had already beaten them.

Panic threw it off. In a moment of inattention, Scizor caught it by the neck with one huge pincer and slammed it against a tree trunk.

With the fight decided, Ethan walked over and looked at Sneasel.

"Well? Ready to behave?"

Sneasel turned its head and snorted. It clearly was not happy about losing.

Ethan paid no mind to its attitude. He smiled and motioned to his Pokemon. With the others escorting them, Scizor carried Sneasel back toward Ho-Oh Temple in its pincer.

He left Machop and Machoke alone. He had their leader; there was no reason to concern himself with the two underlings.
