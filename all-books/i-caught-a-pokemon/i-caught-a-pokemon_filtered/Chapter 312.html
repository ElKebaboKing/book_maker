Chapter 312: The Girl in Kalos

After putting the Dark Ball safely away, Ethan leaned back against his soft pillow and looked out the window.

Stars filled the night sky. A clear moon faced the window, spilling a pale beam across his bed.

A faint snore came from the foot of the bed. Jolteon was curled up on the rug, fast asleep. Dratini lay coiled close against Jolteon's belly and slept just as soundly.

Perhaps their drowsiness was catching, or perhaps the warm, reassuring room was getting to Gengar too. It gave an enormous yawn, wished Ethan good night, and slipped into the shadows.

Everything fell quiet. For a moment, it felt as though he were the only person left in the world.

Ethan smiled faintly, turned off the light, and picked up his phone from the bedside table.

Several new messages waited on the screen. He opened the first one in his messaging app. It was from Misty.

She, Ash, and Brock had set out from Pallet Town, crossed the Indigo Plateau, and reached Johto. Their first stop was Professor Elm's lab in New Bark Town. They planned to give him the mysterious GS Ball, which had left both Professor Oak and Professor Ivy at a loss, so he could study it.

Misty had sent the message at noon. By now, Ethan supposed, Ash had probably reached New Bark Town and registered for the Silver League.

As for Brock being with them again, things appeared to have played out much as Ethan remembered from the animated series. Tracey had returned from the Orange Islands with Ash and Misty, then stayed at Professor Oak's lab after meeting the professor he admired. Brock had rejoined the journey, bringing the old trio back together.

Ethan sent Misty a short reply and scrolled through the older messages. He went back dozens of entries. Apart from greetings and good wishes from his friends, nearly all of them came from a girl far away in Kalos.

He sighed.

His feelings about her were complicated. After winning the Indigo League tournament, they had exchanged messages often for a while, and their relationship had begun to improve. Then he had become intimate with Sabrina. Spending every day with her, with his thoughts fixed on her, Ethan had gradually stopped writing to the girl in Kalos.

She still sent a message every few days, but he usually acted as though he had not seen it. He and Sabrina had been inseparable, and between traveling and battling, he had not had the energy to consider the girl's feelings.

Now that he was alone, his mind settled. For the first time in a while, he let himself think about what had happened between them. Reading line after line of her messages, he could almost see her standing before him, speaking sadly in a low voice.

He sighed again. After hesitating for a long time, he could not bring himself to keep ignoring her. His fingers moved across the screen.

All Is Bubbles: Are you asleep?

A few minutes passed without an answer. She must have gone to bed, Ethan thought.

He set the phone aside and stared at the ceiling, remembering the time they had spent together.

Her name was Serena. Yes, the beautiful girl who would one day travel with Ash and have feelings for him.

When Ethan first arrived in the Pokemon world, he had landed unconscious in a heap of grain on her family's farm. Serena found him there and pulled him away from a group of Rhyhorn that had gathered around him.

He stayed at her home for a time, and both Serena and her mother took good care of him. Over the following months, he and Serena spent nearly every day together. They grew steadily closer.

He played with her, helped her learn to ride Rhyhorn, went shopping and ate meals with her, and accompanied her to movies. He comforted her when she was unhappy and encouraged her when she lost heart. In schoolwork and daily life alike, he gave her attention and help until he had become part of nearly every corner of her world.

Eventually, Ethan earned some money from part-time work and saved enough to move out. He rented a place nearby. Moving away did not weaken their bond. With her mother no longer watching them so closely, they grew even closer.

On her way to school, Serena would let herself into his place with a key, wake him up, and bring breakfast her mother had prepared. After school, she would come back and stay until late at night. She spent much of each day following him around; the two were almost inseparable.

Even when Ethan went to work with a theater company, the others would see Serena trailing behind him like a little shadow. Crew members often teased them about it.

Whenever they did, Ethan would joke, "I'm raising my future wife from childhood!"

Serena never contradicted him. She would blush and keep following him. As time went on, she smiled and laughed more around him. She was so comfortable with him that when she was thirsty, she would drink straight from his cup without a second thought.

What Ethan had meant as a joke began to feel less like one. Perhaps his constant care for her had worked as quietly as spring rain. By the time he realized it, he had filled her heart without leaving room for anyone else.

Sometimes he would turn and find her watching him, head tilted, her clear eyes full of affection. That was when Ethan understood how serious her feelings had become.

Of course he liked her. Serena was beautiful; how could he feel nothing? But she was still underage. He could not act on those feelings.

He had cared for her partly out of gratitude for taking him in. She was also an important character from the animated series, and talking with her helped him understand and settle into this world. Her beauty had played a part in how close they became too. Yet he had always thought of her as a younger sister.

From then on, Ethan deliberately put distance between them.

Serena was perceptive. When she tried to visit him as usual, he repeatedly refused to see her. She soon sensed that something was wrong. Confused and frightened, she grew visibly worn over the next several days. At last, one night, she gathered her courage and confessed her feelings, hoping to bring him back.

Ethan still remembered the moonlight that night. It had been as clear as the moonlight falling into his room now.

Serena told him, "I like you." He answered coldly, "I only think of you as a little sister."

She broke down at once. Crying, she went home in a daze. For days she shut herself away, the light gone from her eyes.

Ethan had regretted what he said, but he could not undo it. After struggling with his guilt for a long time, he left Kalos. With the Ralts Diantha had given him, he traveled to Kanto, hoping time would soften what had happened.

As his memory reached that point, the phone beside him lit up. Its dim glow seemed startlingly bright in the dark room, like Serena's eyes used to be. He had not seen them in so long.

He picked up the phone. Serena had written, Pink Dream: I was taking a bath. You're still awake?

She had added a cute smiling face, as if she needed to assure him that she really had been bathing and had not deliberately delayed her reply.

Ethan sighed. How had the girl who used to be so happy become so anxious around him?

His finger hovered over the screen for a long time. At last he changed the subject.

All Is Bubbles: I've reached Johto. I've seen lots of Pokemon that are completely different from the ones in Kalos.

Pink Dream: Johto? I only learned about it in geography class, and I wasn't paying much attention, so I don't know a lot. Is there anything fun to see there? (laugh)

All Is Bubbles: It's not so different, really. Mountains, rivers, and lakes. In many ways it's like Kanto or Kalos.

After a moment's thought, Ethan opened his photos and sent her several pictures he had taken recently.

Pink Dream: Wow, it's beautiful! I'd love to see it. Oh, and congratulations on winning the Orange League! (playful)

Ethan blinked. He had not told Serena about that. How did she know? As though she had guessed what he was thinking, a second message arrived.

Pink Dream: I looked you up! That's how I found out you have a second trophy now. I watched every match. You won them all and became champion! That's amazing!

Ethan sighed again. He had lost count of how many times he had done that tonight.

After rejecting her confession, he had expected Serena to resent him and turn away. Yet her messages still showed how much she cared.

He had thought the passing of time would weaken her feelings, but looking back, he had been too cruel. Even if he had to refuse her, he should not have done it so harshly.

His travels and the time he had spent with Misty and Chloe had changed his perspective. Perhaps instead of avoiding Serena, he could face the problem, help her work through the hurt, and find another way for them to be in each other's lives.

Maybe, after the Silver Conference, I should go back to Kalos and see her.
