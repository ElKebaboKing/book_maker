Chapter 229: An Easy Victory

Ninetales lay limp on the ground, its eyes rolled back. It had lost consciousness.

Charizard stood nearby, breathing a little hard. Apart from a few superficial injuries, Ninetales had done it no real harm.

Rudy stared in disbelief. Had his Ninetales truly been defeated so easily? Even the referee had not expected the battle to end so quickly. As one of Rudy's attendants, he knew how troublesome Ninetales could be. It had helped Rudy beat countless challengers and was among the Gym's best Pokemon. Never before had it been beaten so cleanly.

The referee favored Rudy, but he still had to call the result fairly. "Ninetales is unable to battle! Ethan wins the first round!"

Fine. Rudy recalled Ninetales, admitting to himself that he had underestimated Ethan. He had thought yesterday's Jolteon was Ethan's trump card. Now it seemed Charizard was his real ace.

There were still two rounds. If Rudy won both, the match would be his. He let out a long breath and steadied himself.

"All right, let's get on with the second round." Then he seemed to remember something. He smiled slyly. "Oh, I forgot to tell you: our rules don't allow you to use the same Pokemon twice. Charizard can't battle again."

It sounded as though Rudy had made the rule to stop Charizard, but Ethan did not mind. All his Pokemon could fight. Whichever he chose would do.

He called Charizard back to his side and patted its shoulder. "Good work. You earned a rest."

Without returning it to its ball yet, Ethan took another Poke Ball from his belt and threw it onto the field.

"Now it's your turn. Go, Jolteon!"

Blue light flashed, and Jolteon appeared in the middle of the battlefield. Rudy looked smug. Just as he'd expected! Ever since Jolteon had passed the test yesterday, he'd been certain Ethan would use it in the match. Prepared for it, he confidently threw his own Poke Ball.

Up in the hot-air balloon, Mahri brightened at the sight of the familiar Pokemon. Her disappointment over Ninetales vanished.

"Great, it's Electabuzz! That's one of my brother's strongest Pokemon too! He won't lose this time!"

"Oh?" Sabrina glanced at her. "You trust your brother that much?"

"Of course!" Mahri nodded vigorously. "He's the strongest person in the world! Your friend can't possibly beat Electabuzz!"

The strongest in the world? Couldn't possibly? Sabrina frowned. The Trainers who came to challenge this remote Gym were probably weak on the whole. Rudy's frequent wins had given Mahri a false impression.

She ought not to get angry with such a young child. Misty or Chloe would likely have laughed off the innocent boast. But after growing so close to Ethan, Sabrina could hardly hear someone belittle him and say nothing.

This was Sabrina, after all: a psychic who had once used her formidable powers to toy with other people's bodies and minds. Sometimes even her parents, and even Ethan, could not guess what she was thinking. It was a mistake to expect her to react like anyone else.

"No. You're wrong," Sabrina said. Her expression did not change, but her words cut through Mahri's certainty with the same merciless directness she had once shown at Saffron Gym. "I don't know who the strongest person in the world is. It certainly isn't your brother. My companion is about to beat him."

For a moment, the beautiful woman seemed hateful to Mahri. She shook her head. "That's not true! I don't believe you! My brother won't lose!"

"You don't believe me?" Sabrina gave a cold snort and raised her chin. "Then watch the field."

Alarmed, Mahri grabbed the railing with both hands, rose onto her toes, and looked down.

While they had been talking, the two Pokemon had already clashed fiercely. Mahri caught the battle at the moment it turned. Under Jolteon's relentless attack, Electabuzz could not hold on. It rolled several times and fell to the ground. It tried to struggle back up, but its strength was spent, and it collapsed.

"What?" Mahri's eyes widened. "How could that happen? My brother lost again?"

To Mahri it was unbelievable. To Sabrina, the outcome had never been in doubt. Ethan was the newly crowned Indigo League Champion, his victory still fresh in everyone's minds. Rudy was the Gym Leader of a small league on a remote island. There was a considerable gap between their abilities.

Before Mahri could recover, the referee announced the result from the balloon. "Electabuzz is unable to battle! The challenger wins!"

On the field, Ethan saw how embarrassed Rudy was. He did not press the advantage. "Well? Are we continuing? If this is best of three, I believe I've already won."

Rudy's mouth moved, but no answer came at first. He could not bear to lose so easily. He wanted to take at least one round. It was not only the loss of face before Sabrina. More than anything, he did not want to retreat in disgrace before the little sister he adored.

Besides, he was convinced Ethan could have no more formidable Pokemon after Charizard and Jolteon.

"You won," Rudy said at last. He took a badge from his clothes and affected an easy manner. "Under the rules, you can take my badge now. But my strongest Pokemon hasn't appeared yet. It would be a shame to leave it at that. What do you say? Do you dare fight the last round?"

Afraid Ethan might simply take the badge and go, he hurried on. "Of course, if you lack confidence and want to skip it, I understand. It was my mistake not to lead with my strongest Pokemon. But the rules are the rules. Since you managed to win two rounds, I can't stop you leaving."

Ethan recognized the taunt and saw exactly what Rudy wanted. He had already lost, yet he still wanted to save face. He could not admit defeat without another word.

Still, Ethan rarely turned down a battle that would give his Pokemon experience.

"All right." He sighed as though reluctant, hiding a smile. "If you insist, I'll play along for one more round."
