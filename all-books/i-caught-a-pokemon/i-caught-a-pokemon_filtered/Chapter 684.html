Chapter 684: A Champion Battles the Kids

After exchanging contact numbers with Shelly, Ethan and Misty left the warehouse district and walked from the harbor into central Sootopolis City.

Sootopolis was an island city that drew both traveling Trainers and many tourists. It was far busier and more prosperous than remote Dewford Island.

Its buildings were distinctive too. Nearly all of them were white, and their style reminded Ethan of Santorini in Greece. The city had an unmistakably Mediterranean feel, unlike the rest of Hoenn. He could see why visitors came from far away.

Evening was approaching, so they went to the Pokemon Center instead of challenging Sootopolis Gym. After checking in and eating dinner, they turned to training.

Ethan had a feeling Feebas was close to evolving. It seemed to need only one last push. He had no scientific proof, but years as a Trainer had taught him to trust that feeling.

As it happened, Sootopolis was preparing for a major festival. Excitement filled the city. The most anticipated finale was said to be a performance by the Sootopolis Gym Leader.

That Leader was Juan, not Wallace, who would take the position later. Juan might have an ordinary-sounding name, but he was an accomplished man: a Gym Leader, a Pokemon Coordinator, and Wallace's teacher.

Wallace, known as the Prince of Water and the Artist of Water, carried himself with an air of elegance. He had won the Ever Grande Conference and become a leading Coordinator. Juan had trained a remarkable student.

Now in his forties, Juan was elegant in manner and dress, witty in conversation, and devoted to beautiful battles. He was especially popular with local women. With someone so admired as the festival's star performer, Sootopolis was clearly taking the celebration seriously.

None of that was Ethan's immediate concern. He wanted to keep training Feebas.

In the pool behind the Pokemon Center, Misty's Pokemon again served as training partners. Ethan and Misty worked with Feebas late into the night.

Their effort paid off. Ethan had just ordered Feebas to use Tackle against Misty's Starmie when it stopped in the water. Brilliant white light enveloped it.

Ethan felt a surge of emotion. At last. He had waited a long time for this.

Pokemon such as Beldum and Feebas, or Magikarp before them, had required much more attention and unusual methods to evolve than partners ready to battle from the start. He had spent enormous effort on each. Now that work had paid off.

The light faded. An elegant Milotic floated in the pool.

Misty had seen and scanned a Milotic before, so she was not quite as overwhelmed as on her first encounter. Its beauty still astonished her. It's gorgeous! I could look at one again and again.

It's all right, I suppose. Nothing that special, Ethan said with a laugh. His pleased voice gave away how proud he was.

Misty had an excited thought. If you ever need to leave Milotic somewhere, could you send it to me?

To you? At Cerulean Gym?

Of course! Her eyes curved with delight. I helped it evolve, didn't I? And I love Milotic. It would be happier with me than at Cerise Park!

You're planning awfully far ahead. Ethan gave her a look. It only just evolved. It needs a lot more growing and training before it's where I want it. Even after Hoenn, it'll probably travel with me through the next region, maybe longer. You're already claiming it?

I'm just happy for you! she said with a grin.

They spent a long while admiring Milotic. When it was nearly two in the morning, they finally recalled their Pokemon and went to their separate rooms.

Ethan was physically exhausted, but too delighted to fall asleep at once. Milotic had evolved and could now battle properly. It was time for the next stage of its training.

...

Ethan slept until nearly noon. His body had rested, but he still felt sluggish when he woke. One occasional late night was manageable at his age.

He washed, met Misty in the dining room, and ate lunch. Then, instead of going to Sootopolis Gym, he sought out battles.

Juan was often away from the Gym during festival preparations, so Ethan might have arrived to find it empty. Meanwhile, the Pokemon Center was full of Trainers. Newcomers and their Pokemon were out on the streets too. Their varying levels of experience offered exactly what Milotic needed.

The second stage of training would still mean repeated battles, but now Milotic would face other Pokemon in real matches instead of simple practice sessions.

Ethan gave Misty the afternoon to explore the city and enjoy the festival atmosphere. He went to a nearby park on his own.

Children chased each other through the grounds, as they did in any park. Ethan looked around and soon found what he wanted.

Sootopolis was a seaside city with pools and waterways throughout its streets, and this park had one too. In a simple aquatic battlefield at its edge, two boys of perhaps seven or eight were battling with Water-type Pokemon.

They looked young and innocent. Ethan approached them with a broad smile.

One wore a baseball cap; the other had rosy cheeks. Both had a little runny nose. Their Pokemon were Poliwag and Krabby. Neither was especially strong, but they were putting up an energetic fight.

The boys stopped to stare at the handsome stranger. Adults had never shown much interest in their games before.

Ethan turned Milotic's Poke Ball in his hand. You two look strong. Would either of you like a battle?

Recognition from an adult thrilled them. They nodded eagerly, though they had never met him. Sure!

The boy in the cap hurried to one side, and Ethan took his former place. I should warn you: I have only this one Pokemon, and it just evolved. It's not very strong yet, so don't expect too much. Which of you wants to go first?

Me! The rosy-cheeked boy shoved past his friend and sent Krabby toward the middle of the pool. Come on!

Krabby brandished its great pincers at Ethan, as spirited as its Trainer.

Ethan nodded approvingly and released Milotic. Blue light flashed, and it appeared in the water.

Both boys squealed with delight. Milotic!

Wow, it's so pretty!

They forgot about battling and crowded Ethan with questions. Where did you catch it? Can I touch it?

Their sudden shrieks made his ears ring. One thing at a time. Let's battle first.

Children could be distracted by anything new. Reminded why Ethan was there, they finally refocused.

All right! I'll beat it! the rosy-cheeked boy declared.

Once both Pokemon had taken their places, the park battle began.

Krabby, Bubble!

The boy ordered an attack immediately. At his age, he had little sense of strategy, type matchups, or how to adjust during a fight. Whoever the opponent was, his answer was to attack straight ahead.

Krabby scuttled across a shallow platform in the pool, spraying bubbles as it went. They expanded until some were as large as basketballs, then drifted toward Milotic.

Krabby and its young Trainer were not strong, but the move could still hurt. Most of Ethan's other Pokemon would have dodged or countered on their own.

Milotic, however, had evolved from a rather vacant Feebas only the night before. Its body was stronger, but it had even less battle experience than Krabby and still carried some of Feebas's habits. It might dodge by instinct; it could not yet judge the fight for itself.

That was no problem as long as it followed Ethan's instructions. Milotic, dive, then use Water Gun!

Both Pokemon were Water types, so their Water-type moves would do less damage to each other. Milotic nevertheless had plenty of ways to use the water around it.

Its smooth body slipped beneath the surface. Like a water snake, it swam past the bubbles and surfaced behind Krabby. A strong stream from its mouth struck Krabby's back.

Water Gun did little direct damage, but its force knocked Krabby into the pool.

Don't let it climb out. Use Wrap!

Crab-like Pokemon could not swim through the water as swiftly or nimbly as fish and many other Water types. Krabby's powerful claws could still keep an enemy awayif its Trainer knew how to use them.

The rosy-cheeked boy did not. Before he could react, Milotic coiled its long body around Krabby. As the hold tightened, Krabby waved its pincers helplessly, unable to reach Milotic at that angle.

The boy's expression changed. The more anxious he became, the harder it was to think of a useful command. Krabby, shake it off!

Krabby could not. The pressure mounted. It blew a stream of tiny bubbles and fainted in Milotic's coils.

The match had been so easy that Ethan had scarcely needed to think. A few simple commands had won it.

I...I lost, the boy said. His face reddened as he hurriedly recalled Krabby.

His friend pushed him aside and sent out Poliwag. You idiot. Watch me!

For all his confidence, the boy in the cap lost even faster. Milotic dodged Poliwag's Water Gun, used Disarming Voice to catch its attention, and then trapped it with Wrap. The same hold ended the second match.

The boys stared at Milotic in admiration. Ethan smiled calmly. Milotic had gained confidence in those two battles and grown comfortable in its new body.

A familiar voice sounded behind him. Honestly, Misty said, laughing. I wondered what you'd do after sending me off. I never expected to find you here beating up little kids!
