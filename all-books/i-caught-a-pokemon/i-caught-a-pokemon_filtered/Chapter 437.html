Chapter 437: An Opponent Comes Calling

After Tentacruel was defeated, the old man shook his head helplessly.

That had been his strongest Pokemon. He had never expected it to cause Ethan so little difficulty. The rest of the match would be troublesome...

Before the battle, he had deliberately researched Ethan's team and learned about his even stronger Gyarados. Yet if he could not defeat Blastoise, it appeared his journey through the Whirl Cup would end here.

He was deeply unwilling to accept that. He was already advanced in years and did not know whether he would still be able to compete in the next Whirl Cup. Reaching the final sixteen this time had been his best result across several attempts.

But the gap in strength truly left him in despair. That sense of powerlessness made him understand that Ethan genuinely was different from the other competitors. He was extraordinarily strong!

Really, why would a young man with this much strength enter a tournament like this?

The old man sighed softly. Urged on by the referee, he released his second Pokemon.

Blue light flashed, and a Pokemon resembling an octopus appeared before Ethan.

It was Octillery, a pure Water-type Pokemon. It looked much like an octopus from Earth, with an entirely red body and two suction cups on each tentacle that gave it a slick, slippery appearance.

Octillery generally lived among rocks and caves on the seabed. They had a habit of squeezing into holes and liked remaining in rocky crevices or jars. Sometimes they would suddenly spray ink to escape a powerful enemy or create an opportunity for an ambush.

Despite its soft, squirming appearance, Octillery possessed a hard head. It would bind prey with its tentacles to prevent escape, then ram the victim repeatedly and deliver a final blow once all resistance had ceased.

Facing the old man's second Pokemon, Ethan neither needed nor intended to switch Blastoise. He kept it in the battle.

Given Octillery's size, its tentacles could not wrap around Blastoise's enormous body, rendering that advantage useless.

Fortunately, Octillery knew several Ice-type moves, so it was not completely helpless against Blastoise.

As soon as the battle began, Octillery used Aurora Beam, attempting to exploit Ice-type power against Blastoise from a distance.

Blastoise easily evaded the attack by leaping from its stone pillar into the water. Octillery lacked the power to freeze the enormous battlefield solid, leaving it with no effective answer.

Aurora Beam froze only a small patch of the surface, turning the spray from Blastoise's dive into ice crystals without striking its target.

Once Blastoise approached through the water, Octillery could no longer even use its Ice-type moves.

With no better option, the old man ordered Octillery into the water as well. He hoped it could continually swim away, maintaining distance while searching for an opening in Blastoise's defenses.

Ethan answered the strategy by using overwhelming force to crush every trick. He ordered Blastoise to pursue Octillery relentlessly and gave it no opportunity to escape!

Underwater cameras relayed the action to the large screens, allowing the spectators to clearly watch the two Pokemonone large and one smallchasing each other below the surface.

The old man silently cursed Ethan for having no respect for his elders. How could he use such a shameless tactic against an old man?

Whether on land or in the water, Blastoise was considerably faster than Octillery. The two Pokemon swam in pursuit beneath the surface, but Octillery could never shake Blastoise.

Out of options, the old man ordered Octillery to release ink. Its long mouth sprayed continuously, instantly staining a vast section of the water black. Octillery intended to hide within the ink and gain a brief chance to catch its breath.

Ethan considered the darkened water for a moment, then decided to borrow from Ash's earlier battle.

At his command, Blastoise withdrew into its shell and began spinning at high speed. As it rotated through the water, it created an expanding vortex!

The force of the vortex pushed the ink farther away and successfully diluted it!

The polluted pool could not return to normal in a short time, but it became much clearer. Without the ink's protection, Octillery could no longer conceal itself!

Seeing this, the old man could only make one final attempt. He ordered Octillery to bind Blastoise tightly with its tentacles. But Blastoise possessed tremendous strength and relied on its heavy body. It broke free after only a few movements.

Ethan seized the opportunity while the two Pokemon were at close range and ordered Blastoise to unleash several moves in succession. It finally defeated Octillery in one stroke with Skull Bash.

The referee immediately announced the result.

When the spectators saw Ethan use Blastoise alone to defeat both of his opponent's Pokemon with ease, they erupted into enthusiastic cheers.

At this point in the tournament, his popularity, strength, and handsome appearance had made him the undisputed favorite in their eyes. More people focused on him each day and looked forward to seeing him win the championship!

Ethan waved to the audience and returned to the waiting room.

Ash and Misty both rose to congratulate him. Misty was sincerely delighted, while traces of dejection remained in Ash's expression.

His traveling companion and the rival he admired had both advanced, while he alone had been eliminated. How could he feel entirely at peace?

Fortunately, Ash had a carefree personality. After feeling down for a while, he quickly cast aside his troubles. In fact, he had already returned to normal before Ethan's match ended.

Ethan left with the two of them to meet Erika and the others.

Although he and Misty had successfully advanced to the quarterfinals, today's competition was not over. The remaining eight competitors would battle again that afternoon to determine the semifinalists.

The probability that Ethan and Misty would face each other had also increased considerably.

The tournament schedule truly was packed. But there was no other choice. The Whirl Cup was only a local competition. If it dragged on for too long and wasted too much time, it could easily miss the Trainers who traveled constantly while preparing for the Silver Conference.

Without those elite Trainers, only the island's less capable residents would remain. The number of entrants might stay the same, but the average strength of the competitors would fall considerably. Even if they forced themselves to choose the best of a weak field and named that person Sea Hero, the title would carry no authority. It might even damage the Whirl Cup's reputation further.

With only sixteen competitors remaining, the round took merely two or three hours to complete.

Ethan's group had not even finished lunch at the restaurant when the quarterfinal pairings were released.

Before Ethan and Misty could check who they would face, a beautiful young woman approached them.

She appeared to be twenty-four or twenty-five. Light makeup covered her face, and her long red hair was tied into a side ponytail. She wore two large bead-like earrings, a red T-shirt, and a long pink skirt.

Upon reaching the group, she first looked Ethan up and down with curiosity for quite some time. Only then did she turn toward Misty.

"You're Misty, right?"

Misty looked up at her in confusion. She clearly did not recognize the woman.

"That's right. Who are you?"

Misty had barely finished speaking when Brock leaped to his feet as though struck by one of Cupid's arrows and hurried in front of the woman before she could answer.

"I know! I know!" Brock's eyes seemed to transform into hearts. "You're the beautiful lady who won every one of her previous matches with ease!"

Brock could fall for every beautiful woman he met and remember each one's distinctive qualities perfectly. It had to be acknowledged as an impressive talent.

The woman looked at him in surprise. "Oh? You know me?"

"Of course!" Brock seized her hand and dropped to one knee like a knight proposing marriage. "Apart from my friends' matches, I've watched every one of yours! A beautiful lady like you shines brilliantly wherever she goes!"

The woman was clearly overwhelmed by Brock's sudden enthusiasm. She yanked her hand free, retreated a large step, and stared warily at the narrow-eyed man.

Seeing Brock behave this way, Misty raised a fist and struck him hard on the head. Without allowing any argument, she dragged him back.

"Sorry. Please ignore him. There's something very wrong with his head!"

Beside them, Ethan, Erika, and Ash exchanged glances and smiled wryly.

To themeven to Erika, who did not know Brock particularly wellthis was his normal behavior. Sometimes he even took notes! If a day ever came when he did not hound a beautiful woman like a lovesick fool, that would be the truly abnormal sight!

Ignoring Brock, the woman turned her attention back to Misty.

"Allow me to introduce myself. My name is Trinity, and I'm your opponent in the next match!"

Her opponent in the next match?

Misty froze. She had never expected her opponent to seek her out, and her expression immediately became serious.

Trinity clearly had not come with hostile intentions, however. She smiled at Misty. "I've watched your matches too. You and your Pokemon work together seamlessly, and your instincts in battle are excellent. I can see that you're a genuinely powerful Water-type Pokemon Trainer. Your Psyduck in particular was fascinating. It left a very deep impression on me!"

Hearing Trinity offer such an assessment immediately softened Misty's expression.

"Thank you for saying that!"

Trinity extended her hand and smiled as Misty shook it.

"I'm truly looking forward to our battle. Let's both give it our very best!"

Only after saying this did she turn her burning gaze toward Ethan once more.
