Chapter 694: So You're a Quick One!

When we parted, I thought it would be ages before you contacted me again. Yet here you are only a few days later, visiting me on a night like this.

Shelly leaned against the wall of her secret warehouse, arms folded, and teased Ethan. It was past seven in the evening. Darkness had fallen, and the cargo district outside was silent.

He ignored her tone. When I was here last time, I think I saw a place inside that would suit me. Do you still have it?

Shelly's expression changed. She covered her chest with one hand and watched him warily. What are you planning? I knew your reasons for helping me weren't entirely innocent. I even expected a day like this, but I didn't think you'd be in such a hurry!

Ethan stared at her. After two puzzled seconds, he realized what she thought he meant.

Don't flatter yourself. He rolled his eyes. I need somewhere private to handle something without being interrupted. I hoped I could use a room here.

Handle something? Shelly did not believe him. Something physical, you mean? I wondered why you came so late without that little girl who's always with you. Now I know.

Ethan sighed. He could see why she might suspect him. A man had appeared at her quiet warehouse after dark, faintly smelling of the glass of red wine he had drunk at dinner, and had pushed his way inside. It was an unusual visit however she looked at it. Explaining further would only make it sound worse.

He walked past her toward the indoor pool that still held her shark-shaped submarine. Beyond a partition, a hidden staircase led him to the warehouse's second floor.

The warehouse was roughly 160 meters long, 80 meters wide, and seven meters high. Among dozens of similar buildings near the docks, its exterior looked unremarkable. Shelly had transformed the inside into a fully equipped secret base.

She had made the second floor difficult to notice from outside by cleverly dividing the tall interior. It held several rooms for living: a bedroom, a bathroom, even a dining room with everything she needed. Another section remained open and spacious, perhaps reserved for a project she had not had time to finish after leaving Team Aqua.

Ethan opened the door to that empty area without asking and stepped inside. He released Gardevoir, Raikou, and Suicune from their Poke Balls, then called Gengar out of his shadow.

Shelly arrived a few steps behind him. The two Legendary Pokemon took positions in opposite corners. Gardevoir, Ethan's first partner, and the powerful Gengar that could speak human words stood to either side of him. All four looked ready for danger. Shelly understood at once that he truly had something important to do here.

All right, I take back what I said. You didn't leave the girl behind and come here to try anything with me.

Take it back? Ethan gave her a helpless look. She had sounded entirely serious a moment ago. Now she wanted to call it a joke. She was a convincing actress.

Good. Would you mind keeping watch outside for anything unexpected?

You want me out of the room. You can say it plainly. Shelly snorted. I know what you're going to do, even without watching. But this place is small and not especially sturdy. Don't put it through too much.

I know what I'm doing, Ethan said. He admired how quickly she had understood his concern. There won't be a problem.

Shelly gave him one last look and left. Ethan took out an Ultra Ball and aimed it at the center of the room.

All guard Pokemon are in position. The third communication with Deoxys begins now.

He said it steadily, almost like a researcher recording an experiment. Then he pressed the ball's release button. Blue light flashed, and Deoxys appeared.

Since defeating and catching it by the lake, Ethan had tried to speak with Deoxys twice in private. Communication with an intelligent Pokemon from outer space had been difficult. He could only approach it little by little through Gardevoir's telepathy, taking care not to provoke it. At least it did not resist him as its Trainer; he had beaten it openly in battle.

Deoxys emerged calmly. It looked over the Pokemon guarding the room before turning its gaze to Ethan. A small blue aurora shone from its chest and appeared before him.

That light was why he needed a hidden room. Outdoors, even somewhere remote, the aurora could attract notice from kilometers away. A busy Pokemon Center offered still less privacy. Here the light could not escape to draw anyone's attention.

Because of that need for seclusion, Ethan had not yet come to understand or work with Deoxys as naturally as he did with his other Legendary Pokemon.

He had tried asking Gengar to translate. Gengar could speak with many Pokemon, but Deoxys's alien language meant nothing to it. Gardevoir's telepathy was the only way Ethan could converse with Deoxys clearly.

Ethan nodded to Gardevoir. It placed a hand on him. An odd sensation reached his mind immediately. Having experienced the connection before, he knew Gardevoir had linked him to Deoxys.

...Friend...

Loneliness filled the faint thought that reached him.

Don't worry, Ethan answered silently. As I told you before, you weren't the only one to land on this planet. Our journey has barely begun. We'll find others like you someday. And remember, my Pokemon and I are your companions and friends now too.

From the films and anime Ethan remembered, Deoxys traveled vast stretches of space alone. Cold meteorites were the closest things it had to a home. No wonder it longed for friends, and perhaps even crossed the stars looking for its own kind.

...Promise...

Yes. That's our promise, Ethan said. In return, I need you to fight strong opponents alongside me.

At the thought of a strong opponent, Deoxys seemed to remember Rayquaza, with whom it had battled twice. Its emotions flared, and the feeling rushed through Gardevoir's link into Ethan.

Easy, he urged. I don't mean Rayquaza. I mean other opponents of comparable strength: Ho-Oh, Groudon, Kyogre, and others we haven't met yet. I may have to face them someday.

...I understand. I will help you...

Ethan smiled. Of their three attempts to communicate, this had been the easiest and most productive. He and Deoxys seemed, at last, to have reached an understanding.

...

When Ethan left the room and descended the stairs, he found Shelly leaning against a wall, bored. She looked up at his footsteps, noticed his lighter expression, glanced at the clock, and shrugged.

Finished already? That was quick.

Perhaps she meant something else. Perhaps Ethan was reading too much into it. Either way, he felt insulted.

Quick? he said. Impossible. I'm young, strong, full of energy, and never tire. My record speaks for itself. Anyone would tell you so. How could I be quick?

Shelly's lips curled. So you really are that kind of man. I knew it. You were upstairs less than seven minutes. Take out the time you spent getting ready and walking up and down, and you managed barely more than three. What would you call that?

Efficient! It proves I'm good at what I do.

Is that so? Shelly stretched, then walked past him toward the stairs. If I'd known, I wouldn't have bothered sending you upstairs. You could have finished right here even sooner.

Without turning around, she raised a hand to stop his reply. Don't come looking for me for this sort of thing again. It wouldn't be worth the trouble. I'm going to bed. Turn out the lights and lock the warehouse when you leave. Good night, quick man.

She was doing it on purpose. Ethan watched her go upstairs in an excellent mood and knew she was teasing him.

He had played a part in bringing her to this life. Without his intervention in the struggle over Kyogre, Hoenn might have suffered badly, but Shelly would probably still be with Team Aqua, the group she had regarded as home. Her jokes suggested her feelings toward him had changed. Perhaps she was also letting out some resentment over the pressure he had once put on her.

Fine. Things are improving, so I'll let the insult go this time. But if she called him a quick man again, he would have something to say about it.

...

When Ethan returned to the Pokemon Center, Misty was already asleep. He went to his room, washed, and soon fell asleep himself.

After the talk with Deoxys, he could imagine facing Pokemon at the very peak of the world without needing to overwhelm them with a group. He might still lose, but compared with before, it was a considerable step. As Deoxys grew stronger, only a few opponents would remain beyond his confidence.

The night passed quietly. After breakfast the next morning, Ethan and Misty went straight to the port and took a ship away from Sootopolis Island.

The festival was still several days off, and Ethan could not wait there indefinitely. Knowing Juan would perform the finale gave him even less reason to stay. Leaving early suited Misty perfectly.

They would pass several smaller islands before returning to the Hoenn mainland and heading for the next Gym city. The journey was short compared with their voyage from Petalburg; they expected to reach the mainland in about a day.

As Ethan's badges accumulated, the Ever Grande Conference drew nearer. He could almost imagine smelling the flame of the opening ceremony.
