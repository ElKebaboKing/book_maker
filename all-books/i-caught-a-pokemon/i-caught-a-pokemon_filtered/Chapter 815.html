Chapter 815: Just as I Expected!

Ash and the others came over to meet Ethan. Before anyone could congratulate him, Max sneezed.

Noland looked at the boy, then back at the frozen battlefield. He gave Ash an apologetic smile. Cleanup will take a while, and Articuno is hurt. I'm afraid it'll be afternoon before I can battle you.

That's fine! Ash said. I'd wait as long as it takes for a match with Articuno. After watching you and Brother Ethan fight, I'm more fired up than ever!

Nothing excited Ash more than Pokemon battles. Even food, sleep, and catching new Pokemon came second.

As they left the battlefield, the air grew noticeably warmer, though the continuing Blizzard kept it from feeling comfortable.

Noland had a solution. He called out his Magmar and had it use Sunny Day. The storm soon dispersed, clear weather returned, and warm sunlight fell across the Factory again.

While Ethan and the others rested, Noland and Sergio gathered a large group of Fire-type Pokemon to clear the field. The Pokemon melted the snow and ice quickly. Articuno's recovery would take longer.

The Factory had its own treatment equipment, so they did not need a Pokemon Center. But wounds and spent strength could not be healed instantly.

Ash could barely sit still. He had already decided which Pokemon would face Articuno, and waiting only made him more restless. The others watched him pace the lounge with amusement.

Seriously, Max said, spreading his hands. Aren't you tired? Sit down and rest. At this rate, you'll be worn out before the battle even starts.

Ash wanted to sit, but his excitement would not let him.

From the sofa, Ethan asked, though he thought he knew the answer, Who are you planning to use?

Everyone looked at Ash. He grinned and held up a Poke Ball.

This guy, of course. I had it sent over from Liza especially for this match!

May and Max did not know which Pokemon he meant. Brock nodded. I see. With its type and its strength, that's probably your best choice.

Max was puzzled, but he had a more pressing question. He edged toward Ethan.

Um, Mr. Vale... could I spend a little time with your Articuno?

So that was why Max had kept glancing at him. Seeing Ethan's Articuno had made the boy eager to meet it. Ethan laughed.

Of course. It can take you for a flight, if you like. Just keep yourself warm.

He took out the Ultra Ball marked with a snowflake and released Articuno. Blue light flashed, and the lounge grew colder at once. Goosebumps rose on everyone's exposed skin.

No one paid the chill much attention. They were too busy admiring the beautiful blue bird. At such close range, the lights of the lounge seemed to give its feathers a faint glow.

Even Ash, who had met and befriended many Legendary Pokemon, felt a thrill. Those had been wild Pokemon. Ethan had actually caught this one. Ash envied him, though he did not begrudge him the achievement.

Ethan gave Articuno a few instructions and helped Max onto its back. Pikachu leaped nimbly into Max's lap. At Ethan's word, Articuno flew out through an open window and climbed into the blue sky.

It was not an especially large Articuno, but a small boy and Pikachu were easy for it to carry.

May and Ash hurried outside. By the time they reached the grass, Articuno had vanished into the clouds.

That Pikachu, Ash said, shaking his head with affectionate exasperation. It went off to have fun and left me behind!

Brock joined them. They talked on the lawn for perhaps fifteen minutes before Max's jubilant shouts announced Articuno's return. It emerged from the distant clouds and landed gently before Ethan. Max and Pikachu hopped down, both grinning.

How was it? Ethan asked.

Amazing! Max said. Articuno flies so fast! It took us through the clouds and over a lake and a forest, but the ride was smooth the whole time. Right, Pikachu?

Pika!

The only thing... Max scratched his head. My backside got a little cold.

Everyone laughed.

By afternoon, with the Factory's Pokemon helping, the battlefield was fully restored. Noland's Articuno had healed and recovered its strength. When Sergio brought the news, Ash's eyes lit up.

My turn at last! I've been waiting forever!

Then make sure you win, Ethan said with a smile. We'll watch you from the stands.

I will! Ash thumped his chest. I'll beat it!

The field looked as though the morning's blizzard had never happened. The Fire-type Pokemon had even dried the water left by the melting ice. Their work was impressive.

Noland was waiting on his Trainer's platform. He folded his arms as Ash approached. Come on, Ash! Show me what you can do!

At Noland's call, Articuno appeared again and settled on the field. The roof remained open for its flight. Time had passed, and though it was still day, the sun was sinking westward.

Ash took out his Poke Ball. I'm counting on you. Go, Charizard!

Charizard emerged with a roar and fixed a fierce gaze on Articuno. Ash had brought it from Liza at Charicific Valley specifically for this battle.

Ethan and Brock studied it at once. Charizard looks well, Brock said. Stronger, too. It must have trained hard in the valley. I wonder how much it has improved.

Ethan agreed. He thought of his own Charizard, which was also training there. He had kept in touch with Liza by occasional messages every few weeks and knew a little of the valley's hierarchy.

A wild Charizard currently held the top position. Ethan's was preparing to challenge it. Ash's belonged to the tier below. Ethan's Charizard had arrived stronger and trained just as diligently. Seeing the power Ash's now carried made Ethan still more eager to visit Charicific Valley. How far had his own partner come? Surely it was stronger than Ash's by now.

While Ash and Noland spoke, Ethan and the others joined Scott in the stands. May and Max cupped their hands around their mouths and cheered for Ash.

Charizard and Articuno faced each other across the field.

Articuno had already lost once today. Its opponent then had been another Pokemon of its own standing, but a defeat was a defeat. Its Legendary pride would not accept a second loss.

Ash and Charizard had reasons they could not afford to lose either. Charizard had trained for a long time in the valley, and its Fire type gave it the advantage. Ethan had beaten Articuno already. Ash would not let himself fall here while chasing the Trainer he looked up to.

Sergio signaled the start. As in Ethan's match, both Pokemon took flight at once, rising out of the open-roofed arena.

Ash struck first. Charizard, Flamethrower!

Charizard turned in midair and sent a stream of blazing fire toward Articuno. Noland had expected a Fire attack and ordered Ice Beam in response. He had underestimated the strength of Charizard's flames.

The moment the moves met, the heat overwhelmed and melted the icy beam. The fire continued on and struck Articuno, driving it down into the ground amid a cloud of dust.

Even a Legendary Pokemon needed a moment to recover from a Fire-type attack.

Well done, Charizard! Ash's eyes shone. Just as I expected! You can stand up to Articuno!

The spectators wore different expressions, but they were thinking the same thing: Ash was getting ahead of himself again.

One successful attack did not settle a battle that had barely begun. Articuno rose and charged. Ash tried Flamethrower again, but Articuno used Agility. Its speed surged, and it dodged the flames easily.

Then it approached Charizard from another direction and used Mist. A dense cloud enveloped them both.

The spectators could not see inside. A heavy impact sounded, and Charizard shot out of the fog and crashed to the ground.

Charizard! Ash cried, clenching his fists.

It was the very tactic Ethan had used against Noland earlier. Ash had watched that match and ought to have been ready for it, yet Noland had caught him by surprise.

Fortunately, Charizard had been knocked down rather than badly hurt. It shook its head and got back to its feet.

Articuno dived again. Ash ordered Charizard to use Dragon Breath repeatedly to keep it back. Blue-green energy streamed from Charizard's mouth. With Agility's added speed, Articuno avoided the first attacks.

But Charizard had traveled and fought for years, then trained for a long time in Charicific Valley. It did not panic when Articuno evaded it. Several breaths forced Articuno toward one position. Charizard then fired a faster, stronger Dragon Breath straight into its belly.
