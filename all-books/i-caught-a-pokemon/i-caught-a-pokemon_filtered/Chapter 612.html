Chapter 612: Why Are We Back Here Again?

Team Magma retreated.

Courtney had come full of expectations and left in disgrace. None of her plans had succeeded. The operation was a complete failure.

Shelly did not know that Courtney had already been losing before Team Aqua arrived. She assumed Courtney had seen her and withdrawn rather than face her, and she felt pleased to have taken her revenge.

Well? Courtney had ambushed her earlier, and now it was Shelly's turn. At last Courtney knew what defeat felt like.

Shelly glanced at Professor Cozmo, then waved Team Aqua's members forward. They took the places Team Magma had just abandoned and continued the assault on Ethan's group.

Cozmo had escaped her once, but he would still end up in her hands.

As soon as the fighting resumed, Shelly realized something was wrong.

Misty, Zinnia, and Cozmo kept the same Pokemon on the field: Politoed, Starmie, Salamence, and Solrock.

Ethan alone made a change. He recalled Tyranitar, which faced an unfavorable type matchup, and sent out Scizor to fight alongside Dragonite.

Team Magma and Team Aqua were much alike in strength, from their commanders down to their ordinary members. Like Courtney, Shelly had badly misjudged Ethan's group.

She had felt relieved at first when the mysterious Pokemon that spread electricity everywhere did not appear. Assuming Team Magma had beaten it, she paid little attention to the others.

Her team outnumbered them by far. And after the battle they had just fought, how much strength could Ethan's group have left?

Then she watched Team Aqua's Pokemon fall one after another.

Shocked, she realized the people in front of her were nowhere near as weak as she had thought.

She took out her own Poke Ball without delay and threw it hard.

A blue Pokemon resembling a sea lion appeared on the battlefield. It had two long tusks.

It was Walrein, an Ice- and Water-type Pokemon. Its thick layer of fat protected it from bitter cold, leaving it comfortable even below freezing, and could cushion an opponent's blows. Its two powerful tusks grew throughout its life and could shatter an iceberg weighing ten tons in one strike. If a tusk broke, it would grow back.

Walrein was not exceptionally tall, but it stood out among Team Aqua's Crawdaunt.

A red Aurora Beam shot from its mouth toward Dragonite. Ethan had been watching Shelly, though, and was ready.

Scizor darted into the beam's path. It swept its huge pincers across its body and used X-Scissor. A cross-shaped wave of energy struck the Aurora Beam.

Boom!

The two moves collided in a violent explosion, canceled each other out, and faded in the air.

Seeing how difficult the group was to fight, Shelly asked the same question Courtney had.

Who are you people?

This time Zinnia stayed silent, and Ethan answered. You don't need to know. All you need to know is that while we're here, you won't take anyone away.

Shelly studied him for a long moment. Recognition dawned.

I remember you! You're the one who ruined our operation at Devon Corporation a few days ago. Now you've taken someone from us again. Are you determined to make an enemy of Team Aqua?

Ethan remembered that the police had taken away everyone who broke into Devon, including one of Team Aqua's officers. Even if Team Aqua knew the operation had failed, he had not expected its people to know his part in it. Yet Shelly had heard and recognized his face. Their intelligence network was impressive.

He had crossed groups like this often enough that being identified did not trouble him much.

Zinnia and Cozmo looked at him with surprise. They had not known he had dealt with Team Aqua before.

Old grievances and the new rescue made Shelly hate Ethan even more.

But hatred did her little good. Her members' Pokemon kept falling, and even Walrein was driven back again and again.

Then the field changed once more.

Ethan took out a Great Ball and released Raikou. The sight made Shelly's heart sink.

Now she understood why Courtney and Team Magma had withdrawn so decisively.

She gritted her teeth and gave the order to retreat too. She was clever enough to decide quickly when a fight could not be won.

Before leaving, she said coldly to Ethan, Don't celebrate yet. This isn't over. No one who angers Team Aqua gets away with it.

She waved an arm, and Team Aqua fell back. Within moments, every member was gone.

It's finally over...

Misty let out a long breath and dropped onto a nearby rock.

She had faced threats like that several times before. Like Ethan, she paid Shelly's warning no mind.

After all, even Team Rocket's enormous organization had never managed to stop Ethan. He was stronger now than he had been then.

Professor Cozmo alone looked troubled. I'm sorry I dragged you into danger, he said.

It's all right. Zinnia shook her head. No matter how powerful they are elsewhere, they won't be a match for us at Meteor Falls.

By us, she seemed to mean the Draconids. Ethan guessed that Zinnia was not the only one with considerable strength among her people. That would explain her confidence in facing Team Aqua.

It's fine, he told Cozmo. You heard Shelly. I'd already made an enemy of Team Aqua. They'd come after me whether or not this happened.

Cozmo gave a rueful smile. Even so, you saved me. I don't know how to thank you.

Once they were certain both teams had gone, Zinnia escorted them back to the cave's main passage. There she stopped.

Ethan, Misty, and Cozmo said goodbye to her and headed together for the exit.

Before entering the cave, Ethan had expected nothing more than a convenient crossing. Instead, he had caught a Beldum, found several Key Stones, and rescued Cozmo from two rival teams.

It had been quite a journey.

They walked for a long time. By the time they emerged under the open sky, stars filled the night. Ethan checked the time: it was already past nine o'clock.

He turned to Cozmo. What will you do now?

Go home, of course, the professor said at once.

Where is home?

Fallarbor Town. It's not far. Would you like to visit?

Fallarbor Town... Ethan thought for a moment, then shook his head.

I'd better not. There isn't a Gym in Fallarbor, and I'm short on time. I won't trouble you.

That's a pity. Cozmo sounded disappointed but did not press him. Good luck with your Gym challenges, then!

After a little more conversation, they parted. Cozmo had not gone far when Misty cupped her hands around her mouth and called after him, Professor! Be careful, and don't let anyone kidnap you again!

Cozmo turned and waved. He only smiled, then continued alone toward Fallarbor Town.

Ethan and Misty watched until he vanished along the mountain road. They exchanged a smile and set off too.

They feared Team Magma or Team Aqua might still be nearby and attack in the night, so they did not camp there. They continued downhill under the moonlight instead.

Fortunately, the moon was bright enough for them to make out the path without flashlights.

They walked until midnight. Only when they were sure they had left the cave far behind did they pitch the tent, eat a quick dinner, and crawl inside.

Between the long trek, two battles, and the late bedtime, they slept until the sun was high overhead. They emerged from the tent yawning.

Ethan rubbed his eyes, sat outside in a daze for a while, then heated water and began making breakfast.

It was dull work that demanded patience.

At times like this, he particularly missed Erika and Brock. They both cooked delicious food and took care of every part of the meal. Ethan only had to sit down and enjoy it.

Now he had to feed Misty as well as himself.

After they and their Pokemon ate their late breakfast, they prepared to leave. That was when Ethan noticed something wrong.

They had hurried through the darkness the night before without checking their overall direction. Somehow, they had circled back near Rustboro City.

They stared at the map in silence.

How? Misty rubbed her eyes in disbelief. I remember heading east yesterday. How did we end up back here?

However much they wanted to deny it, the map and their surroundings were clear.

It must be the terrain, Ethan said, rubbing his temples. Our general direction was right, but the path wound downhill. Last night we had nothing to navigate by but the moon, so we circled back.

Even with an explanation, it felt uncanny. How many times had he come to Rustboro now? Why did he keep ending up here?

If he had known this would happen, he might as well have gone with Cozmo to Fallarbor Town. Fallarbor had no Gym, but at least it would have carried them forward.

They had lost still more time.

There was nothing to do but check their bearings and set out again.

Fortunately, they were near Rustboro only in the broad sense. They were far from the city itself, at the edge of a forest. Crossing it would take them to the other side of the mountain.

That was some consolation. They put the mistake behind them and entered the trees.

Ethan did not know the forest's name, but it looked broad and dense on the map. They had not gone far when someone suddenly sprang from the bushes ahead.

After yesterday's fighting, Ethan first thought Team Magma or Team Aqua had found them. A closer look showed only a young boy.

He had a round face, seemed about Misty's age, and had innocent-looking eyes. A small pack rested on his back. He was probably another traveling Trainer.

A Heracross taller than the boy stood beside him. It was plainly his Pokemon.

The boy looked just as surprised to see Ethan and Misty and stopped where he was.

Oh. Misty patted her chest. I thought you were a bad guy or a wild Pokemon!

Even as she spoke, she took a few steps back and hid behind Ethan. Heracross looked sturdy and impressive, and it was not especially furry, but a Bug-type Pokemon was still a bug. She could not keep her skin from crawling.

The boy saw nothing amiss. He looked them over and asked, Are you Trainers too?

Of course.

Ethan nodded pleasantly and was about to walk past. The boy brightened. If we're all Trainers and we've met out here, how about a battle?

Ethan stopped and hesitated.

Normally he welcomed a challenge, but he had not recovered from yesterday and felt little enthusiasm for another match.

Still, making eye contact and then battling was a well-worn custom among Trainers. It was hard to refuse.

The boy looked eager, and the huge Heracross stood ready beside him. Ethan nodded at last.

All right. One on one.

The boy beamed. Let's introduce ourselves first. I'm Jiro Sawama. I only started traveling recently, and I'm training here in the forest!

Ethan could hear the boy's youthful energy. After journeys through two regions, he felt some of that freshness fading in himself.

He was about to give his own name when another person emerged behind Jiro, looking exactly like him.

Huh? Misty stared. You're twins?
