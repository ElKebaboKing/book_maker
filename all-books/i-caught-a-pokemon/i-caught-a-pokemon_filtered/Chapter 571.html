Chapter 571: A Pokemon from Hoenn

Jimmy was not the only one surprised by Blaziken. Ash, Misty, Brock, and Erika had never seen a Pokemon like it either.

"What kind of Pokemon is that?" Misty asked.

When Ethan had visited Ho-Oh Temple, he had dealt with the Sneasel causing trouble there. Ash and his friends had still met Harrison at the temple, but without that incident or an interruption by Team Rocket, there had been no reason for Harrison to show them his Blaziken.

He had kept it hidden through the preliminaries and the round of sixteen as well. His matches had gone smoothly enough that he had not needed it until now.

Its first appearance caused a stir among people who knew little about the Pokemon of other regions. They stared and exclaimed at the unfamiliar sight.

Ash took out his Pokedex and pointed it at the upright, striking Pokemon on the screen. It gave him only one answer: "Sorry. No data available."

"No data?"

Ethan sighed at their reactions. "You can stop scanning. That's Blaziken. Its main habitat is Hoenn, the region we're going to visit next. Like my Gardevoir, it isn't found on the Kanto or Johto mainland. It's from a later 'version,' you could say."

"Blaziken?" Erika ignored his remark about versions. She studied the vivid red on its body. "Then it's a Fire-type Pokemon?"

"Fire and Fighting. Like Charizard and Typhlosion, it's the fully evolved Fire-type starter of its regionHoenn's, in this case."

"A starter from Hoenn..." Ash murmured. His eyes grew brighter. He longed to set out at once and begin a new adventure there.

On the field, Jimmy had never seen Blaziken before, but its coloring told him it was probably a Fire type.

When two Fire-type Pokemon faced each other, their Fire attacks would do less damage. The contest would come down to their other strengths.

Jimmy had not realized Blaziken was also a Fighting type.

That mistake let Blaziken get close. It seized the chance, unleashed a series of Fighting-type attacks, and knocked out Typhlosion.

Typhlosion was Jimmy's ace. Losing it in its first exchange all but settled the match.

Harrison went on to defeat Jimmy's remaining Pokemon without much trouble. Only a strong showing by Jimmy's Beedrill saved him some pride. Without it, Harrison might have swept the match six to zero.

There was a gap between their abilities, but it should not have been this wide. The unfamiliar Pokemon and Typhlosion's defeat had shaken Jimmy's confidence so badly that he failed to bring out his team's true strength.

"What a shame," Ethan said after watching the whole match. He shook his head. "Against Marina and now against Harrison, Jimmy never got his mind settled. He worked too hard during his travels to let that hold him back."

Erika sighed too. "It's hard to avoid. Most people would be affected by something like that."

"True. Jimmy was unlucky to meet this opponent."

Ethan turned and stared directly at Ash. Ash looked back, baffled.

"Why are you looking at me, Ethan?" Ash glanced down at his clothes. They were not dirty.

"No reason." Ethan smiled. "I was just thinking that only someone as oblivious to feelings as you could keep his head clear no matter what a girlor anything elsedid."

The others understood his implication and laughed softly. Ash, looking perfectly serious, said, "Of course! Whoever my opponent is, I'll give the battle everything I've got!"

That made Misty and the others laugh even harder.

After the match that afternoon, Ethan's group found Jimmy in the ground-floor lobby. He looked dejected. Marina stood beside him, speaking quietly and trying to comfort him.

"Ethan..." Jimmy gave them an awkward smile. "Well, I lost."

Ethan did not know what to say, so he patted Jimmy's shoulder.

Jimmy knew Ethan would face Harrison next. "Please be careful," he said. "Harrison is strongespecially his Blaziken. Watch out for it!"

Only after the battle had Jimmy learned the Fire Pokemon's name. By then it was too late. What good could that knowledge do him now?

"Thank you for the warning. I will."

Ethan shook Jimmy's hand and watched him and Marina enter the elevator.

Like Vincent before them, the two childhood friends from New Bark Town would probably leave this place with regrets.

What a cruel tournament...

The night passed quietly.

The following day's two matches belonged to the other half of the bracket. By the end of them, Ethan had seen that Jon Dickson really was strong. He defeated his opponent by a wide margin.

The afternoon match, by contrast, was unremarkable.

Even the winner was not especially formidable. Their battle drew the lowest ratings of the quarterfinals.

If Ethan beat Harrison, it seemed his opponent from the other half would indeed be Jon Dickson.

The Pokemon League noticed the same possibility. Its promotions began playing up Ethan and Jon Dickson, building anticipation for a meeting between two strong competitors.

Interest in the Silver Conference rose again as the semifinals approached.

During the break, Ethan filmed a promotional video. Alexa also pulled him aside for another exclusive interview, leaving him busy from morning to night.

He was surprised she had stayed in Silver Town so long. Even now she was still working outside the stadium.

By the time everything was finished, Ethan returned to the dormitory somewhat tired. At last he had a chance to rest and prepare for tomorrow's match.

The building felt deserted compared with the first days of the tournament.

Apart from staff and the rush at mealtimes, he hardly saw any competitors. The Silver Conference was nearing its end.

...

Boom! Boom!

Fireworks burst one after another above Silver Town, stirring the crowd's excitement.

After breakfast, Ethan and Erika entered the waiting room at Silver Stadium. Even through the walls, he could faintly hear the announcer.

He leaned back in a chair and rested with his eyes closed. Before long, an announcement summoned him to the field. He opened his eyes and stood.

"Good luck," Erika said.

She came over and straightened his collar. Ethan drew her into his arms and kissed her lightly.

"Don't worry. Wait here for the news that I've won!"

They smiled at each other. Ethan said goodbye, left the waiting room, and walked through the competitors' tunnel. To the cheers of the crowd, he reached his position opposite Harrison.

It was the first time Ethan had studied Harrison properly. They had seen each other in the dormitory lobby before, but had never spoken.

Harrison looked three or four years older than Ethan. Perhaps from traveling so long, his skin was a little tanned. He was tall and broad, with short brown hair and an ordinary face made cheerful by a bright smile.

As strangers, they exchanged friendly nods but no words. Then both turned to the referee.

"We'll now draw the battlefield and decide who sends out a Pokemon first!" the referee announced.

The wheel on the big screen began to spin. Ethan and Harrison watched its pointer closely. When it finally slowed, it came to rest on the Ice Field.

The Ice Field?

It was a decent field. The thick ice would not trouble the Pokemon, but standing beside it for a whole match could give an underdressed Trainer a cold.

Next came the draw for who would send out first.

For once, Ethan escaped his run of bad luck. Harrison drew the first move.

Harrison shook his head in disappointment. He had studied Ethan for a long time and knew how strong he was. Facing him would be difficult enough; having to reveal his first Pokemon made it worse.

Those were the rules. Harrison gathered himself and took out his first Poke Ball.

The arena transformed. The open ground in front of them parted, and a field covered in thick ice rose into place.

Before Ethan could adjust to the expanse of white, cold air slipped inside his clothes. He shivered.

"Both competitors are ready, and the field is set!" the announcer cried. "The long-awaited Silver Conference semifinals are finally beginning! Let's cheer for our favorite Trainers!"

Harrison could not match Ethan's popularity, but Ethan's supporters applauded him too.

Harrison hurled his Poke Ball forward. "Go, Kecleon!"

Another Pokemon from Hoenn.

An ordinary Johto Trainer might have been as bewildered as Jimmy when faced with an unfamiliar species. Harrison's opponent was Ethan, though. He had never set foot in Hoenn in this world, but back on Earth he had cleared the Hoenn games and their Ever Grande Conference countless times. He knew its Pokemon well.

The Kecleon before him stood only about a meter tall. Its grass-green body might make it look like a Grass type, but it was actually a Normal type.

It resembled a chameleon from Earth. Kecleon could change its body's color to blend into its surroundings and approach prey unseen. It had one limitation: the pattern across its belly never vanished, so its camouflage was never complete.

Others might not know how to deal with it. Ethan needed only a moment to settle on a plan.

He took a Poke Ball from his belt and sent out his first Pokemon. Blue light flashed, and Tyranitar appeared on the ice.

"Rooar!"

Like his red Gyarados, Tyranitar liked to roar on entering a battle, spreading its intimidating presence. Its tall, armored body was enough to make Harrison's Kecleon take a frightened step back.

Harrison frowned. Starting a battle afraid was a bad sign.

Tyranitar looked down at its much smaller opponent. A cruel smile crossed its face, and contempt flashed in its dark eyes.

Seeing that it was taking Kecleon lightly, Ethan warned it. "Don't underestimate this one. It can turn invisible. Be carefuland watch its long tongue. That's its main way of attacking."

Tyranitar did not think much of the warning. Harrison, on the other side of the field, looked alarmed.

How did Ethan know Kecleon's traits? Had he been to Hoenn and seen one before?

That was bad news. Harrison had never used Kecleon in an earlier round, just as he had held Blaziken back. He had meant to surprise a strong opponent with it.

Instead, Ethan seemed to see through it the moment it appeared.

This would be troublesome. Harrison knew he had to give the battle everything he had.

As the match began, he gave the first command. "Kecleon, disappear!"

Before the crowd's eyes, Kecleon's body began to fade. Within a second or two, its color blended with the white ice beneath it, and it vanished from sight.

Spectators gasped and stared. Even the announcer cried out in astonishment. Most of them were used to Johto Pokemon; seeing such a strange ability for the first time showed them how varied Pokemon could be.

Ethan remained still. He narrowed his eyes and studied the place where Kecleon had disappeared. Sure enough, he could make out a red pattern shaped like a wave.

The mark was faint, but it stood out against the white ice. It was the one part of Kecleon that could not disappear.
