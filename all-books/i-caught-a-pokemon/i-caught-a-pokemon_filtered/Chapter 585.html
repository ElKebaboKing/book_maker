Chapter 585: Let the Story Play Out

Heavy footsteps shook the ground. Everyone turned. The enormous creature of branches and weeds was striding toward them.

Ash and the others had no time to rest. They sent out their Pokemon again, determined to free Celebi.

Pikachu, Charmeleon, and Mistys and Brocks Pokemon charged from either side. Vicious answered with Dark Tyranitar and several other Dark Pokemon.

Ashs group was trapped at the lakeshore. They had been thrown into the water, and Whites airship was destroyed. There was nowhere left to retreat.

They could only fight with everything they had. This time none of them held back.

Battles broke out all around. The fighting spread quickly beyond the edge of the lake.

Brock even sent out Onix. Using its massive body, it wrapped around Dark Tyranitar to create an opening for Ash and the others.

Ash and Sammy took that chance. They ran to the base of the plant creature and began climbing it with their bare hands.

They hoped to reach Celebi while Vicious was distracted. Halfway up, however, the Team Rocket trio blocked their way.

Jessie, James, and Meowth had been hiding on the creature since climbing aboard earlier. At last, they had a useful role to play.

Heh! Jessie grinned. If you brats want to interfere with Lord Vicious, youll have to get past us!

Team Rocket! Ash shouted, angry and frantic. Get out of the way!

Not a chance, James said with a smile. You should hand over Pikachu instead!

They had clashed too many times for either side to back down. A fight began at once.

Ash and Sammy had left their Pokemon below in their hurry to climb. The trio had Meowth beside them and the high ground on their side. Their position was much easier to defend than to attack.

Pikachu saw Ash in danger and leaped onto the creature to help. That finally eased the pressure on the two boys.

Moves flew in every direction. Nowhere on the field was safe.

Erika brought out Leafeon to stand guard before herself, Towa, Diana, and White. The three villagers had no Pokemon with them and had come only to help. She would not let them be harmed.

Only Ethan had yet to take part.

Since arriving in the village two nights earlier and realizing Celebi was involved, he had been waiting for an opportunity.

He wanted to end the crisis, send Sammy back to his own time, and find a way to catch Celebi himself.

His memories of the movie were too hazy to guide every move. He could recall something of its ending, but a battle changed from moment to moment. He had not found the right point to step in.

Was this it? Or should he wait a little longer?

If Ethan truly wanted to defeat Celebi, he had the strength and plenty of chances to do it. Viciouss Dark Balls would not change that.

But what then?

Celebi would take Sammy back to the past, and Ethan would come away empty-handed.

Still, he could not simply leave things as they were. First they had to get Celebi out of that monstrous shell.

Ethan glanced toward Towa, Diana, and White. Their attention was fixed on the battle. He slipped behind a large tree.

From a pouch at his right side, he took a Great Ball and released the Pokemon inside.

A graceful blue Pokemon appeared before him: Suicune, one of his Legendary partners.

Suicune, the embodiment of the north wind, had the power to purify water. In the movie Ethan remembered, its arrival had changed the battle. Ash and the others had rescued Celebi and ultimately cleansed the dark energy controlling it.

Whether another Suicune was nearby, Ethan did not know. His own might even be the one he remembered seeing in the film.

He could not gamble on a second Suicune appearing. He had to send his and let it play its part so events could move forward.

Pointing toward the plant creature, he said quietly, See that? Celebi is inside. Find a way to get it out and purify the dark energy around it. The man beside it has Dark Balls. Dont let him catch you.

Suicune nodded and raced toward the creature.

Not far away, Towa, Diana, and White watched the battle helplessly. They could see how it was going, but they had no way to assist.

Then an elegant blue shape flashed past them and bounded into the fighting.

When they recognized it, all three stared in shock. It was Suicune, the Legendary Pokemon!

Erika alone looked for Ethan as soon as Suicune appeared. Sure enough, he emerged at an unhurried pace from the direction it had come.

He gave her a nod. She understood that this was the Suicune he had caught.

So he had finally decided to intervene.

Suicune reached the battlefield as Dark Tyranitar attacked Onix. It sprang behind Tyranitar and kicked it down, then leaped onward toward Celebis wooden shell.

Vicious noticed it at once.

What? Suicune? Shock turned to delight. Excellent! Another rare Pokemon right here. You can join my collection too!

He drew a Dark Ball and threw it without hesitation. Suicune had been watching for exactly that. It dodged cleanly.

So alert? Vicious frowned. Suicunes caution surprised him, but he was too pleased at the chance to capture one of Johtos Legendary beasts to dwell on it.

His attention shifted from Celebi to Suicune. His hold over Celebi weakened.

Above them, Pikachu fought brilliantly. Within a few exchanges, it defeated Jessie, James, and Meowth.

Luck did not save them this time. One Iron Tail sent all three tumbling from the creature into the forest below, where they vanished among the trees.

With the way clear, Ash and Sammy climbed higher. They soon reached the creatures mouth.

Inside its wide-open jaws, they could see Celebi wrapped in a tangle of protective vines.

Celebi, stop! Ash called.

Please dont do this anymore! Sammy shouted.

Their voices alerted Celebi. It opened its eyes wide, and an invisible barrier spread outward from its body, throwing both boys away.

They nearly fell, but each had a firm grip. After sliding down a little, they climbed back toward the mouth.

Vicious was busy trying to catch Suicune. It darted left and right without a pattern, and several more Dark Balls missed.

As his supply dwindled, he grew hesitant.

Tyranitar! Forget those brats Pokemon and deal with Suicune!

Before Dark Tyranitar could obey, Vicious heard voices behind him. He turned and saw the two boys at the creatures mouth.

They had slipped past him. Damn them! Why were they so persistent?

He had never faced an opponent quite like Ash. An ordinary Trainer would not climb a giant creature like a Mankey, risking everything to rescue a Pokemon. Even the usually steadier Sammy had followed Ashs lead into danger.

Celebi, get rid of them! Vicious ordered.

He was too late. Ash and Sammy had already crawled into the creatures mouth and followed the vines to Celebi.

Facing Celebi in its shroud of dark energy, Ash cried, Dont listen to him! Remember who you really are!

Ash had brought out his strongest weapons: an appeal to memory and a plea to wake up. This time they worked.

Dark energy surged around Celebi. For a brief moment, clarity returned to its eyes. It was struggling against the power inside it.

Encouraged, Ash and Sammy reached for Celebi and tried to pull it free from the center of the vines.

Their touch shook it further. The entire wooden creature began to tremble out of control.

Vicious lost his balance outside. He had to crouch and grab the grass beneath him to keep from falling.

Everyone noticed the change.

Misty and Brock brightened. Ash and Sammy must have done something inside. With renewed hope, they pressed their attack on the Dark Pokemon.

Vicious had lost control of the fight. His Pokemon fought like mindless instruments of destruction, and with Suicunes help, the others knocked them down. The balance of the battle changed in moments.

You miserable pests!

When Vicious saw all his Dark Pokemon defeated, he ground his teeth in anger.

Then he noticed something worse. The Dark Ball on his vest that held Celebi under control began leaking black smoke. It shook violently, as though about to burst.

Th-this cant be happening!

He snatched it up. The ball shattered in his hand, sending out a billow of smoke. Celebi had broken free of his control.

No!

He barely had time to cry out before the wooden creature collapsed beneath him. Vicious and the tangled branches fell together.

Inside it, Celebis power lifted Ash and Sammy without a breath of wind. The two boys and Celebi floated safely out and descended to the ground.

Rumble!

The huge mass of wood fell apart. Misty and the nearby Pokemon hurried away from the falling debris.

When the dust settled, Vicious was nowhere to be seen. Ruin lay everywhere.

Was it over?

Everyone but Ethan could hardly believe the result. Vicious had held the advantage only moments ago. How had it reversed so suddenly?

They had little time to think. Celebi landed with Ash and Sammy. Misty and the others forgot about Vicious and crowded around it.

Towa, Diana, and White approached slowly, eyes wide. Arborville stood at the entrance to this forest, and they had kept its legend for years, but this was their first sight of its true guardian.

So this was Celebi. It was beautiful beyond belief.

While everyone surrounded Celebi, Ethan stayed where he was and searched the wreckage with his eyes.

He knew Vicious would not be defeated so easily. The battlefield looked quiet, but the hunters final desperate move was still to come.

Ethan did not warn the others. They were close to the end, and each step from here could affect his chance to catch Celebi.

Suicune walked back to him. Ethan would have preferred not to reveal that he owned a Legendary Pokemon, but after defeating Dark Tyranitar, Suicune had returned to its Trainer without a thought.

Both Suicune and Raikou had accepted their situation and acknowledged Ethan as their Trainer. Neither would run away even if given the chance.

Such gradual changes could be powerful. Once they accepted their new lives, they would go on fighting beside him like his three Legendary birds and could never quite return to what they had been before.

Before Vicious reappeared, Ash cried out in shock.

Ethan went over. Celebis body was visibly shriveling. Its spirits had collapsed, and its breathing was faint.

H-how could this happen? Ash stared at it in disbelief.

Ethan understood at a glance. It must have exhausted its strength breaking free of the Dark Ball. The dark energy is striking back at it now. If we dont find a way to treat Celebi, its life could be in danger!
