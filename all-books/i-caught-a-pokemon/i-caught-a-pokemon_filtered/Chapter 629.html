Chapter 629: Ash: Misty, What Are You Doing Here?

Ethan had two possible routes ahead of him. After passing through Rustboro City three times and losing so much time, the itinerary he had planned when he first arrived in Hoenn no longer made much sense.

He was now near the center of the region. Whichever direction he took to challenge another Gym, he would eventually have to double back toward the opposite side. That meant wasting time.

He thought it over and talked with Misty. In the end they decided to head south to Slateport City, then take a ship to the island in the southwest and challenge the Dewford Gym. After that they would face the Petalburg Gym and finish the western part of their Hoenn journey.

Misty had no objection. They left Mauville City and followed the main road south. The city's tall buildings gradually fell behind them, replaced by dense forest.

Slateport was a long way from Mauville, with a bridge across the sea along the route. Walking alone would take at least two days, more when meals, rest, and camping were counted. But they were traveling; there was no reason to complain about the distance. As long as they kept going, they would arrive.

They crossed the forest in half a day and reached the coast. Far across the water stretched a winding bridge whose end they could not see, as though it reached the horizon.

That's a long bridge... Misty stared into the distance. How far do we have to walk to reach the other side?

It was Ethan's first time there too, so he could not answer. Fortunately, a bicycle rental shop stood beneath the bridge. They could ride across much faster and with less effort.

There was no reason to pass up that convenience. They went in, paid a deposit, and rented two bicycles.

The owner had a clever business. Customers rented at one end of the bridge and returned the bicycles at the other, in either direction. He had almost cornered the trade. As long as the bridge stood, he could make a living from it.

Ethan mentioned that to Misty, who laughed softly. Are you jealous? When you get tired of traveling, you could open a shop like this. I'd definitely come work for you!

To take that as a simple joke would have missed her meaning. Beneath I'd come work for you, she was telling Ethan she wanted to stay with him whatever he did in the future.

Ethan seemed not to hear the hint. Spend my whole life beside one bridge? No thanks. I'd rather settle on a deserted island than waste my time here.

Once they rode onto the bridge, their pace improved. Damp, salty sea air brushed their faces. They cycled side by side and left many pedestrians behind.

Wide rest areas appeared every few kilometers, presumably for people crossing on foot. Because the bridge connected two cities, many Trainers used it each day. Some battled in those rest areas, making full use of the available space.

A few people tried to stop Ethan and Misty for a match. Ethan did not want to be stuck on the bridge after dark. He shook his head and rode past.

It proved the right choice. The sun was setting and the sky turning to dusk when they finally rolled off the bridge and onto land. Anyone who had lingered to battle behind them would have to hurry through the night against the hard sea wind or sleep on the cold bridge. The more fun the battles had been, the more miserable that journey would be.

...

Slateport City was a port where people, Pokemon, and nature met.

On Hoenn's southern coast, it had first grown around clean water, plentiful food, and a thriving market. It remained an excellent place to shop. Over time it had expanded into a major harbor, with ships coming and going every day.

After more than a day on the road, Ethan and Misty arrived on the morning of the third day. From a distance they could see an enormous lighthouse on the city's southern side. Not far away stood the famous Oceanic Museum, with a Pokemon Contest venue nearby.

They were about to head for the harbor when several familiar figures appeared on the opposite side of the street.

The boy in front wore a blue hoodie and a red cap. He had a cheerful, not especially thoughtful-looking face, and Pikachu sat on his shoulder. The clothes, backpack, stride, and Pikachu were all familiar. Who else could it be but Ash?

Three people walked with him. The tallest was Brock, the lecherous squint-eyed oddball Ethan knew so well. On the other side were a brother and sister: May and Max, whom Ethan had met at the Petalburg Gym.

They were here too? And, just as expected, the four had begun traveling together.

As Ethan and Misty recognized Ash, Ash spotted them. Ethan! Misty too?

His eyes lit up, and he ran over. Pikachu was just as delighted. As soon as Ash came close, it leaped from his shoulder into Misty's arms.

What a coincidence, Ethan said, looking Ash over. When did you get here?

A few days ago. I've already got two badges! Ash lifted his shirt at one side to show the Stone Badge and the Knuckle Badge. Then, before Ethan could reply, he turned to Misty with an indignant look.

Misty! You said you weren't going to keep traveling with me because you had to stay at the Cerulean Gym. So what are you doing here with Ethan?
