Chapter 395: An Invitation and the Ecruteak Gym

Something had frightened Eevee. It fled in a panic, charging blindly forward. By the time it saw someone blocking its path, it was already too late to slow down!

Bang!

The next instant, it crashed directly into Ethan's shin.

The impact was not very powerful, but Ethan still swayed and took a step backward. Fortunately, Erika reached out and steadied him.

Eevee, meanwhile, had been left dizzy by the rebound. Its unsteady legs gave way beneath it, and it toppled to the floor with stars spinning around its head.

"Um..." The girl hurried over from behind. Seeing the trouble Eevee had caused, she quickly bowed to Ethan and asked cautiously, "I'm so sorry. Are you hurt?"

Ethan looked at her.

Hmm?

He recognized her at once as one of the Eevee Sisters. She was also the final performer, the one who had appeared with Espeon for the grand finale!

Wait...

Her face looked familiar. Wasn't she the girl whose eyes had met his before the performance began?

When the girl saw Ethan's face, she clearly recognized him as well. Even more embarrassed now, she quickly crouched down and gathered Eevee into her arms.

Ethan shook his head. "I'm fine. What happened to this Eevee?"

Gently stroking Eevee's head, the girl replied helplessly, "A Fearow went wild backstage and frightened the poor thing. That's why it ran out in such a panic."

"I see."

It was only a minor incident and had caused him no harm, so Ethan naturally did not take it to heart.

At close range, he could see that the girl was around sixteen or seventeen years old. She was beautiful, stood about one meter sixty tall, and wore a pink kimono. Her hair had been pinned up in an elaborate style with floral ornaments, ribbons, and decorative hairpins. Ethan even spotted a Love Ball among the ornaments on her head!

She wears a Poke Ball in her hair?

What an unusual style...

Noticing Ethan studying her, the girl blushed slightly. "Ah... I forgot to introduce myself. My name is Naoko. I'm the fourth of the Eevee Sisters."

"I'm Ethan, a traveling Trainer. Miss Naoko, your performance just now was wonderful. It was truly captivating!"

"Really?" Naoko's eyes lit up with excitement. "Before the show, I was afraid I might ruin everything."

She had secretly watched the audience before the performance precisely because she feared making a mistake or failing to meet her own expectations. Receiving such praise now naturally made her extremely happy.

However...

Why does the name Ethan sound so familiar?

I feel as though I've heard it somewhere before...

Before she could think any further, Erika spoke from beside him.

"Miss Naoko, you should have more confidence in yourself. Ethan is right. Your performance was truly excellent. Even though we already knew the story, your enthralling acting and dancing still drew us in!"

Delighted by their approval, Naoko smiled brightly.

Suddenly remembering something, she took a card-like object from her pocket and offered it to Ethan.

"That's right. Please accept this as my apology and thanks!"

Ethan took the card and turned it over. It was an invitation.

"What's this?"

Naoko smiled. "It's for a tea party held by the five of us. We host one every so often. If you have time, please be sure to join us tomorrow!"

Ethan looked down at the invitation. The tea party would begin at ten tomorrow morning. If he rose early and went to the Gym first, he should be able to make it as long as the challenge went smoothly.

"Understood. Then we'll see you tomorrow."

After bidding farewell to Naoko, the two left the Ecruteak Dance Theater.

It was already midnight. The moon hung high above them, and almost no pedestrians remained on the streets.

Life moved slowly in this city and placed little pressure on its residents, so most people had already retired for the night.

Ethan yawned repeatedly from exhaustion. When they returned to the inn, he and Erika wished each other good night and went to their separate rooms.

The night passed without incident.

Early the next morning, after breakfast, Ethan planned to ask Erika to go ahead to the tea party and meet him after his Gym challenge. She refused.

"We're traveling together, so how could I let you go into a battle alone? Let's visit Naoko together after you've challenged the Gym."

Moved by her words, Ethan accepted her kindness.

They followed the street to the Ecruteak Gym.

The Gym stood in northern Ecruteak City, not far from their inn. Built atop a massive stone platform, it followed a traditional design of timber and blue roof tiles, resembling an ancient Japanese building.

Its appearance matched the style of Ecruteak City perfectly. After all, everything in the city preserved the customs and architecture of earlier times.

At the entrance, Ethan pushed open the door and found a spacious interior resembling a sports hall. A man sat kneeling on a cushion in the center with his eyes closed in meditation.

Hearing someone enter, the man opened his eyes.

"What brings you here?"

"Is Morty here? I've come to challenge the Ecruteak Gym."

The man studied Ethan for a moment, rose, and walked over to him.

"I am Morty, the Gym Leader. I welcome your challenge."

Only then did Ethan realize that Morty was genuinely tall, standing at almost the same height as Ethan himself. They were also close in age. Morty wore casual athletic clothing and a headband, while his medium-length orange hair gave him a somewhat frivolous air.

After Ethan introduced himself, Morty wasted no time on idle conversation and went straight to the matter at hand.

At his signal, Ethan and Morty took their places on opposite sides of the battlefield.

The Ecruteak Gym used an ordinary standard field. This suited Morty's style. Since he specialized in Ghost-type Pokemon, he had no particular requirements for the terrain.

A member of the Gym staff stepped to the side to serve as referee.

"Good luck."

Erika offered Ethan quiet encouragement, then walked to the spectator area and settled down to watch the match.

She had great faith in Ethan's strength and did not believe he would fall here.

When the referee saw that both sides were ready, he raised the two colored flags in his hands.

"The Gym battle will now begin! Each side may use three Pokemon. The match will end when all three Pokemon belonging to either the Gym Leader or the challenger are unable to battle! The challenger will attack first. Only the challenger may switch Pokemon during the match; the Gym Leader may not switch freely!"

These were common match rules.

Rules like these often meant that the Gym Leader was too powerful and needed such restrictions to bring the battle closer to the level of an ordinary Trainer.

Ethan could have asked Morty to send out his strongest Pokemon, as he had done at the Violet and Blackthorn Gyms. After considering it, however, he decided to focus on training instead and use this opportunity to exercise some of his other Pokemon.
