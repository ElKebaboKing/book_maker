Chapter 381: Meeting the Week Siblings Again

After lunch, the two rested where they were for a while. Once they had recovered some strength, they recalled their Pokemon and continued toward Violet City.

The sun slowly sank in the west. Sunlight fell across them, stretching their shadows longer and longer over the ground.

Violet City was still some distance away, so Ethan had expected them to camp outdoors that night. Fortunately, before darkness fully settled, they reached a small town at the foot of a mountain and found an inn.

At the sight of the kindly innkeeper, Ethan visibly relaxed, and even Erika breathed a sigh of relief.

This was one of the great blessings of travel. Given the choice between a soft, comfortable bed and a cold tent, who would choose the tent?

More importantly, they could take a hot bath before bed and wash away the day's fatigue.

Amusingly, a small misunderstanding arose while they were checking in.

The innkeeper had seen countless guests over the years. Faced with a handsome young man and a beautiful young woman traveling together, she instinctively assumed they were a couple deeply in love. Relying on experience, she did not even ask before taking it upon herself to give them one room.

In fact, she privately thought they made an excellent match.

Ethan and Erika did not notice the oversight at first. Only after the innkeeper led them to a large room and they saw two futons laid side by side on the tatami floor did they realize what she had misunderstood.

Erika gently explained with a faint blush. The innkeeper finally understood that her experience had led her astray, apologized with an embarrassed smile, and changed their reservation to two smaller rooms.

Neither of them took the matter to heart. After enjoying a lavish dinner, they put it completely out of mind.

By then, night had fully fallen.

The town happened to be holding a festival, so its streets were exceptionally lively after dark. Vendors stood in long rows, their calls rising and falling while an endless stream of visitors passed among them. With nothing else to do and plenty of time before bed, Ethan and Erika discussed it briefly, joined the crowd, and wandered around for a while.

They followed the packed flow of people, stopping here and there as they walked from one end of the festival to the other. Their spirits relaxed, and they bought several beautiful souvenirs along the way.

When they returned to the inn, each carrying several small bags, they looked at one another and smiled. Only then did they exchange good nights and retire to their separate rooms.

The night passed peacefully.

By the following morning, a full night's rest had restored them completely. Full of energy once more, they left the town and resumed their journey toward Violet City.

This time, Erika released her newly captured Hoppip to travel at her side and deepen the bond between them.

After a night of reassurance, Hoppip was clearly much closer to Erika. It had truly begun to regard her as its Trainer.

Ethan could not help feeling a little envious. Compared with Hoppip, his Suicune was clearly nowhere near accepting him.

And because of Suicune's special status, he could hardly let it accompany him openly as Erika did with Hoppip.

Of course, Erika's success also had much to do with who she was. Her beautiful appearance naturally put Pokemon at ease, and her gentle personality helped even more. Years of living among plants had given her an air of nature that made it easy for her to grow close to Grass-type Pokemon.

Still, Ethan believed that with enough time together, Suicune would eventually follow the three Legendary birds and sincerely accept him as its Trainer.

The two followed a country road westward. Along the way, they admired the scenery and chatted about everything under the sun.

Erika was knowledgeable, well-read, and an engaging conversationalist. Unlike the quiet Sabrina, she took the initiative to bring up subjects that interested Ethan.

Traveling was ordinarily tedious, but with Erika beside him, laughing and talking, Ethan instead felt that time passed quickly.

In fact, he could feel that Hoppip and Erika were not the only ones growing closer during the journey. The unfamiliarity between him and Erika had also faded, replaced by greater ease and intimacy.

By afternoon, they finally reached the outskirts of Violet City. Here, the natural landscape gradually disappeared, replaced by tall buildings and the bustle of human civilization.

Just as Ethan was about to enter the city, someone suddenly called to him from nearby.

"Trainer over there, please wait!"

Hearing the voice, Ethan turned. A young man and woman had been resting on a bench, and for some reason, their eyes lit up the moment they saw him.

The man appeared to be twenty-two or twenty-three and wore black-rimmed glasses. The woman was slightly younger and had fashionable, short, curled hair.

"What can I do for you?"

The man pushed up his glasses, looked at his phone, then looked at Ethan as though confirming something.

"It really is you. You're the Trainer called Ethan, aren't you?"

Hm? They know my name?

Ethan raised an eyebrow and studied them in surprise, but he had no impression of either one.

"Do you know me?"

Several possibilities crossed his mind. The most likely answer was that they had watched one of his matches.

The question lingered until the man introduced them.

"We do and we don't. Ah, I forgot to introduce us. We're members of the Week Siblings!"

The Week Siblings!?

Hearing that name, Ethan immediately understood.

He remembered defeating the second and third members of the Week Siblings at Ice Path. What had their names been again? Right, Tuscany and Wesley!

Were these two their siblings? Had they come for revenge?

The young woman spoke. "I'm Frieda, and this is my older brother, Arthur. You can tell from our names that we're the fourth and fifth members of the Week Siblings!"

Ethan's expression did not change. He merely smiled. "It's a pleasure to meet you. So, what do you want with me?"

The brother and sister exchanged a glance, then approached Ethan.

"I believe my sister once explained this to you," Arthur said. "Whenever someone defeats one of us, we tell the others so that the rest of the family can avenge the loss. Our sister already told us about you, so this time, we're here to challenge you!"

"I see." Ethan nodded. "Judging from your attitude, I suppose this battle is unavoidable?"

"That's right!" Frieda said. "But don't worry. In return, if you defeat us, we'll give you our badges!"

The mention of badges brought back another memory.

After defeating Tuscany and Wesley, he had received the Heartbeat Badge and the Disappointment Badge from them. The badges served no real purpose, but they were still sitting in his backpack even now.
