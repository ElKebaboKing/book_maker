Chapter 887: Battling Ho-Oh

The Pokemon overhead were in the thick of battle, but Ethan's command reached his three birds. They had been listening for it even as they fought.

Without hesitation, Articuno, Zapdos, and Moltres climbed higher together. They began to circle in a rising spiral, drawing the sky around them into motion with their power.

They had used Tri-Bird Assault during Ethan's previous confrontation with Ho-Oh, when he was trying to catch the three legendary beasts. But the attack had been aimed at the beasts. Ho-Oh had arrived only after the birds struck, so Ethan did not know whether it had seen their combined technique.

For now, he assumed it had. Ho-Oh had appeared almost as soon as he defeated the beasts. Perhaps it had been watching from somewhere nearby all along.

Ho-Oh watched energy pour from the three birds, a trace of gravity entering its gaze. It had indeed seen this attack. Although it had been far away and felt only the last of its power, the sight had left a deep impression.

Ordinarily, Ho-Oh would not stand still beneath such an obviously devastating move. But it had accepted Ethan's challenge. It would not evade so readily. This was a test of whether that human truly deserved to possess all three beasts, and a trial of whether Ho-Oh itself could withstand the birds' strength.

Heavy clouds blotted out the stars and swallowed the moon. In moments, a spiraling mass of dark cloud hung over Bell Tower. Lightning flickered and thunder rolled within it.

On the roof, Kuni hesitated. What are the three birds doing? she asked Ethan quietly, as if a louder voice might draw the lightning down.

Using a combined attack we developed together, he said. It's immensely powerful. It can seriously hurt even a Pokemon like Ho-Oh.

A combined attack? Kuni had never heard of such a thing. She was impressed, but she could not help asking, Doesn't that make it dangerous for us to stay here?

Miki, Zuki, Naoko, and Sayo all looked at Ethan. They were not merely close to the danger. They stood almost directly beneath the heart of that ominous cloud. Whatever came downlightning or something elsemight miss Ho-Oh, but Bell Tower would surely lie in its path. The surrounding compound and forest were at risk too.

Kuni wanted reassurance. Instead, she found Ethan wearing a knowing smile.

Hold on, Zuki said. You did remember that we're standing here when you gave that order, didn't you?

Of course I did. I'd never disregard your safety. Ethan spread his hands, perfectly confident. Then he gave a small snort. This is also a trap for Ho-Oh.

A trap? Zuki asked. What do you mean?

What is this tower to Ho-Oh? Ethan looked at them. It used to live here. If it doesn't want its former home to end up like the Burned Tower, it can't dodge Tri-Bird Assault. It has to stop it.

The sisters understood. It was another way for Ethan to repay Ho-Oh for the trouble it had caused him. If Ho-Oh took the full force of the attack, it would likely be hurt, and Ethan would have his satisfaction.

But did he truly intend to gamble with Bell Tower?

What if it doesn't stop the attack? Miki asked. It left this place more than a hundred years ago. It may have abandoned it long since. If that's the case...

She did not finish. Bell Tower was no empty patch of wilderness. It was a famous landmark, one of Ecruteak's symbols, and the only one of the two great towers still standing. If the sisters helped destroy it, the whole city would blame them.

Don't worry. Ethan shrugged. Then we use Plan B.

Plan B? The sisters exchanged looks. Since when did you have a Plan B? Why didn't you tell us?

Since just now. Plan B is this Pokemon right here. He patted Deoxys on the shoulder, looking every bit like a man with an ace in hand.

The sisters studied Deoxys's expressionless face again. Could this strange Pokemon really stop the attack? They had never even heard of its kind. They could not know that Ethan's Deoxys had once withstood Tri-Bird Assault itself.

Ethan had no time to explain. He looked up at Ho-Oh. First, I'll need to give it another push.

He called toward the sky. Ho-Oh! This is the three birds' strongest attack. If you can take it, I'll acknowledge you as the strongest bird Pokemon in the world. If you can't, you'll have to watch your old home be destroyed. Show me what you choose!

It was a ruthless thing to say. Ethan had deliberately laid the choice before Ho-Oh: face the attack and risk injury, or move aside and let Bell Tower share the fate of the tower that burned more than a century ago.

High above, Ho-Oh seemed not to hear him. In truth, it had already decided. Even without his provocation, it would not retreat.

When Ho-Oh remained in place, Ethan knew his gambit had worked. Still, he turned to the sisters. Ho-Oh, Deoxys, and Gardevoir give us three safeguards, but this is dangerous. You should leave before the attack reaches us.

Zuki shook her head at once. I'm staying. She narrowed her eyes at him. What kind of woman leaves her man here alone? If you won't go, neither will I.

That was Zuki all over. Miki spoke up too. She's right. If you're staying, so are we. I trust you to protect us.

You two... Their faith warmed Ethan even as it moved him. They were not being careless or failing to understand the danger. They were putting their trust in him. If they chose to remain, he would respect it.

He looked back up. Tri-Bird Assault had finished gathering power and was ready to fall. Over Ecruteak, the fireworks were slowing; the night market was almost over.

Then let this attack mark the night's end.

Ethan signaled to the birds. At the same moment, Gardevoir and Deoxys raised protective barriers around the top of Bell Tower.

Beneath the clouds, the three birds drove the last of their power into the attack. Looking somewhat drained, they flew aside and out of its path.

A luminous torrent poured from the cloud like a waterfall through the darkness, descending toward Ho-Oh. It shone so brightly that everyone still on Ecruteak's streets noticed. People turned toward Bell Tower, pointing and talking among themselves.

Those who had already ventured close to the tower were more than curious. They were horrified.

One of them was Ecruteak's foremost Trainer and Gym Leader, Morty.

A minute or two earlier, Morty had sensed the clash between Ho-Oh and the three birds almost as soon as it began. He had rushed toward the tower with several other Trainers.

Ethan had spotted them crossing the woods. Deoxys's copies intercepted them before they could reach the tower.

Morty was the strongest Trainer among them. Though the ambush had caught him off guard, his Pokemon dispersed one copy after another. His Ghost types handled the strange attackers especially well, and from their behavior he began to suspect they used Psychic power.

But the copies kept coming. No matter how many he defeated, he could not clear a path through the forest. Bell Tower was near, yet he had been unable to take another step toward it. He had never encountered anything quite like this.

Morty understood why they had been stopped. Someone did not want them interfering with the battle above Bell Tower.

From this close, he could identify the combatants. He had never seen them in person, but as a Gym Leader he knew them at a glance: Ho-Oh, Articuno, Moltres, and Zapdos.

Ho-Oh, above all, was unmistakable to anyone raised in Johto. The three birds' distinctive forms revealed them just as plainly.

Why were they fighting? Why here? Questions crowded Morty's mind. The mysterious copies raised another: who controlled the Pokemon that had sent them, and what connection did that person have to the legendary birds?

The copies were not individually overwhelming, nor did they show many different attacks, but their endless numbers made them maddening opponents. Morty considered several names. Could it be Lance, Bruno, Will, or Karen? Some hidden figure no one knew? Team Rocket? None of the possibilities satisfied him.

The other Trainers gradually gathered around him. They knew their local Gym Leader, and unlike him they were ordinary Trainers nearing the limits of what they could withstand.

Mr. Morty! a sweating man asked anxiously. What are these Pokemon? I've never seen one before. Why are they attacking us?

Morty had questions of his own. Does your Pokedex have no information on them?

Nothing, a woman answered. It just says Unknown.

That might mean their Pokedex data was incomplete, or that the Pokemon was not native to Johto. Either way, they knew almost nothing about it.

Morty was about to speak when he felt something above them. He raised his headand saw a sight he would remember for the rest of his life.
