Chapter 590: Catching Torchic

After a night's rest and breakfast the next morning, Ethan and Erika left Oldale Town.

Apart from the Oldale Ruins, there was little else to see. They had no reason to spend more time there.

They put their heads together over the route for a while. In the end, Ethan decided to visit Petalburg City first, then make a wide circuit through Rustboro City, Fallarbor Town, Fortree City, Lilycove City, Mossdeep City, Sootopolis City, and Pacifidlog Town. From there they would return to Hoenn's mainland and challenge the Gyms in Slateport City and Mauville City.

It was only a preliminary plan. If anything changed, they could alter the route whenever they needed to.

Before challenging a Gym, though, Ethan wanted to catch a few Pokemon and expand his team.

He could not keep using Pokemon that had already fought in a conference. They would win him badges easily, but the battles would do little to develop his team.

Where nearby might he find a Pokemon he liked?

His gaze settled on the mountains in the distance.

Trainers had already searched the roadside woods and grasslands thoroughly. There might still be rare Pokemon hidden deep in those mountains.

After packing supplies, Ethan and Erika left the main road and headed into the hills.

The farther they went from town, the rougher the way became. They often had to change direction through ankle-high grass, keeping watch as they waded across streams or pushed into dense forest.

Both were used to such journeys. They could still chat as they walked, hardly troubled by the obstacles.

The landscape around them grew greener and greener.

With no one else likely to see them this far from town, Ethan let Celebi and Eevee out together.

Eevee was one of the Pokemon he hoped to train most in Hoenn, and he expected a great deal of it. It had far too much energy to stay quiet, though. One moment it sniffed something to the east, the next it was nosing around to the west, and between discoveries it played with Celebi.

With those two beside them, Ethan and Erika never felt bored.

After a long walk they reached the foot of a mountain. Just as they were about to climb, something flashed through the grass at their side and ran off.

They looked up in time to see a wild Ralts disappear into a distant thicket.

Ralts...

The familiar sight stirred a rush of feeling in Ethan.

That was right: wild Ralts lived in Hoenn. They were still rare here, but a small number of Trainers had already caught them.

Ralts was Gardevoir's first form, and Gardevoir had been Ethan's very first Pokemon. She had stayed with him through the hardest part of his journey.

But both he and Gardevoir had grown so much since then. They could never go back to how they were.

He sighed.

It was a fond memory, and a bittersweet one.

When his thoughts settled, Ethan and Erika began climbing.

There were noticeably more wild Pokemon on the mountain. The lower slopes still held mostly common species, though. Even by the time they reached halfway up, they had passed dozens of different Pokemon without wanting to catch any.

That was hardly surprising. If rare Pokemon were so easy to find, they would be everywhere.

Ethan had caught some of his own Pokemon because he knew the anime's story and where to look. Searching a mountain like this was a matter of lucka blind chance of stumbling upon the right one.

Habitats and territory mattered too. Unless a Pokemon wandered or migrated, turning over every stone on a particular mountain or around a lake would reveal only the few species that lived there.

The path grew steeper. Ethan and Erika stopped talking and concentrated on the climb.

By the time they reached the summit, Ethan had broken a light sweat despite the stamina he had built over his long travels.

They had found no rare Pokemon, but the view was worth the effort. Oldale Town and the Oldale Ruins lay spread out below them.

After enjoying the mountain breeze, Ethan grew hungry. He and Erika set up a temporary camp.

They meant to eat lunch, rest a while, and then head back down. But just as the meal finished cooking, its aroma spreading through the air, a nearby patch of grass rustled.

Something was hiding there.

Celebi noticed it first and clutched its food protectively, as if afraid the newcomer would steal a bite.

Eevee ran up to the grass and barked at it.

The grass shook again. Startled by Eevee, a tiny Pokemon like a young chick leaped out in a panic.

Wait, wasn't that...?

Although he had never seen one in person, Ethan recognized it at once: Torchic, the first form of Blaziken.

It had a small chick-like body covered in fluffy orange feathers and two tiny wings. It clearly could not fly.

Ethan took out his Pokedex and pointed it at Torchic.

Beep. Torchic, the Chick Pokemon. Fire type. Torchic has a flame sac inside its body, and the fire burns for as long as it lives. Holding one is as warm as holding a hot-water bottle. When attacked, it spits fireballs formed in its belly. These scorching fireballs can reach one thousand degrees Celsius and burn an opponent. Torchic fears nights so dark it cannot see its own feet. It pecks at the ground to swallow small stones that help its digestion and raise the temperature of the flames it breathestwo benefits from one stone.

Back at Cerise Laboratory, Ethan had asked Professor Cerise to download information from regions around the world onto his Pokedex. He did not need an update in Hoenn; it worked here as usual.

He liked Torchic and both of its evolutions, Combusken and Blaziken. Even before arriving in Hoenn, he had put Blaziken on his list of Pokemon he absolutely wanted to catch.

And now he had found a Torchic here. It was as if a pillow had appeared the moment he felt sleepy. The hard trip into this remote place had paid off.

Still...

What was Torchic doing up here?

Most Fire-type Pokemon preferred drier places. Few would come alone to a mountaintop like this.

When Torchic started to flee, Ethan quickly called Eevee back. Did the smell draw you here? Would you like some?

Torchic had not yet reacted, but Celebi hurriedly shielded its plate. It seemed terrified that Ethan might give away its portion.

Torchic hesitated at Ethan's words. It knew nothing about these humans and did not really want to approach, but the food smelled too good. The aroma had drawn it here before it could stop itself.

Seeing its uncertainty, Ethan smiled. How about this? Have a battle with me.

He pointed to Eevee. If you beat Eevee, you can eat anything here. If you lose, you'll come with me and become my Pokemon. What do you say?

Torchic looked up at Ethan, then at the eager Eevee. Its little brow furrowed as it weighed the offer.

Ethan did not want to give it too long to think. What's there to decide? Don't tell me you're scared. Or do you doubt you can win?

At being called frightened by a human, Torchic flared up.

It was bold by nature. Otherwise, it would never have followed the smell and hidden so close to them in the first place. It would not let this human look down on it.

It cried out fiercely at Ethan. He did not need Gengar to translate: I'm not scared! Come at me!

Ethan and Erika exchanged a glance, and both smiled.

Ethan was pleased his provocation had worked. Erika shook her head in amused exasperation.

For a grown man to resort to such a childish trick! He could have let Eevee attack, defeated Torchic, and caught it outright.

A patch of open ground lay beside their camp. Torchic strode over with its head high, and Eevee bounded out to face it.

Ethan gave no commands for this capture battle. He sat comfortably to one side and watched.

He looked almost like a spectator.

That did not mean he was indifferent. Both Pokemon were still in their first forms, but Eevee had traveled with him for some time and fought in battles large and small. It had the experience to handle this without his help.

The two Pokemon faced one another with a dozen or so meters between them. Neither backed down. As though they had reached the same decision at once, they suddenly charged.

Ethan sat up a little straighter and watched Torchic's movements closely.

When it reached Eevee, Torchic lifted a claw and swiped at its face. Eevee was a little faster. It twisted aside and avoided the blow.

Then Eevee slammed into Torchic. The little Pokemon stumbled and nearly fell, but regained its balance after several steps to the side.

It did not retreat. Its belly swelled, and a burst of sparks flew from its open beak toward Eevee.

At such close range, the flames caught Eevee. It had learned how to deal with fire during its earlier travels, though. It rolled across the ground until the sandy soil smothered the flames.

The brief injury did not slow Eevee much. It got up and grappled with Torchic again.

After watching for a while, Ethan saw that Torchic kept returning to the same few attacks. It had no unusual moves, and at this level it could not defeat Eevee.

Sure enough, as the battle continued, Torchic visibly tired. At last it fell to Eevee's Swift.

Ethan took out the Poke Ball he had prepared and threw it at the fallen Pokemon.

Bang!

Red light wrapped around Torchic and drew it into the ball. The ball shook a few times, then clicked and fell still.

Good. He had caught Torchic!

Ethan picked up the Poke Ball and immediately let his new Pokemon out again.

Erika tended to both Pokemon's injuries and gave Torchic a taste of her cooking. Within minutes of meeting them, it had become completely at ease.

Ethan laughed softly as he watched Torchic play with Eevee and Celebi, with none of its earlier awkwardness left.

A Pokemon with a nature like that was easy to befriend. Treat it kindly, and it would return that kindness.

Thanks to Torchic's temperament, Ethan's long search had not been wasted. The trip had brought him an unexpected new Pokemon.

Counting Torchic, he had obtained nineteen Pokemon over the course of his travels. If he included Latias and Latios in the Water Capital, the total was twenty-one.

It was not an enormous number, but every one of those Pokemon was exceptional.

They rested at the summit until their strength returned. Then Ethan packed their things, and he and Erika started down.

They had come with hopes and were leaving satisfied.

He was so pleased that he hummed through most of the descent.

At the foot of the mountain, he recalled Celebi and Eevee to avoid attracting attention. He kept Torchic beside him so they could spend more time together and deepen their bond.

By the time they regained the main road and reached Petalburg City that evening, Torchic was entirely comfortable with Ethan. It looked like a Pokemon that had lived with him all along.

Petalburg stood in southwestern Hoenn. It was neither large nor populous, yet it was famous far beyond its borders.

That was because Norman, the Petalburg Gym Leader, was so strong that many novice Trainers hesitated to challenge him.

People said Norman's family had originally lived in Johto and had moved to Hoenn only so he could fulfill his duties as a Gym Leader.

He had pursued strength all his life. People often said he seemed born for Pokemon.

It was too late for Ethan to call at the Gym and issue a challenge now. He would have to wait until the next morning.
