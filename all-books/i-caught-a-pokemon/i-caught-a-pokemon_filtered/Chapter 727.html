Chapter 727: Battling the Black Rayquaza

They waited from noon into afternoon, from afternoon into evening, and from evening deep into the night. They ate twice, and Ethan even groomed his Pokemon. At last, the moment Zinnia had been waiting for arrived.

Ethan stretched, recalled all his Pokemon except Gardevoir and Dragonite, and went to stand beside her.

Stars filled the sky. By chance, a meteor shower crossed it now, countless bright streaks trailing long tails through the darkness. The sight was magnificent.

It's beautiful... Misty murmured. Even during her travels, she had rarely seen anything like it. She might only be accompanying Ethan, but the climb up such a tall tower had been worth it for this view.

The meteors were beautiful, but Ethan and Zinnia had their attention elsewhere.

It's time, Zinnia said.

She took a deep breath, stood in the center of the altar, and raised her right hand toward the deep night sky. At once, the Key Stones wound around her leg shone green. The light ran up her calf and gathered along her arm. Ethan could not tell how she did it, but the green flames in the stone basins flared to more than a meter high. Her cloak and hair stirred without wind. When she opened her hand, several round Key Stones lay in her palm.

Green light enveloped them. Zinnia closed both hands around the stones, held them over her head, and called to the sky, Rayquaza!

This was the summoning method she had devised by combining a sacrifice of Key Stones with her people's secret arts. The green light in her hands burned like a beacon.

Almost immediately, a thick pillar of light plunged from the sky toward the Dragonhark altar. Something long and twisting moved within the green beam as it descended.

Roar!

A deafening cry came from inside. They could not yet see its source, but Ethan knew that roar. It belonged to Hoenn's Legendary Pokemon, Rayquaza.

The green pillar vanished, revealing a Shiny Rayquaza with a black body before them.

It had worked. Zinnia had summoned Rayquaza! Its color was strikingly different from an ordinary Rayquaza's, which only made her joy stronger. Her method really worked.

Ethan was delighted too. All the thought and time he had invested had paid off: she had called the black Rayquaza he wanted.

The green light slowly faded, its last traces making the black Rayquaza look even more imposing. Zinnia quickly opened her hand and presented the Key Stones, still faintly glowing, to it.

Rayquaza, please hear my wish! she called, hope and fervor in her voice.

Summoning Rayquaza was only the first step. What mattered was whom it would choose.

As soon as she finished speaking, the green light around Rayquaza disappeared. The glow around her Key Stones went out with it. Even the flames in the basins shrank into tiny flickers.

Zinnia stared from Rayquaza to the dull stones in her hand.

It didn't choose me? Why? I did everything...

The light seemed to leave her eyes as well. Then she noticed another glow behind her. She turned slowly.

Ethan's Meteorite Key Stone shone brightly in his hand, as though calling out to something. Rayquaza's attention had shifted to it. The Pokemon roared at him.

It's reacting to the Meteorite Key Stone? Zinnia said.

Ethan looked down at the stone and then up at her. It seems Rayquaza has chosen me.

She stared at him, eyes wide, her thoughts impossible to read. Ethan did not try to provoke her. He walked past and stopped before Rayquaza, leaving Zinnia to watch his retreating back.

The black Rayquaza regarded him from above. He met its gaze and liked it more the longer he looked. Its strength was no different from that of a green Rayquaza, but its black coloring was undeniably more striking. One glance was enough to remember it.

He would not risk delaying. Ethan took the Ultra Ball he had prepared.

It's up to you now. Go, Deoxys!

He threw the ball. It opened, and his Deoxys appeared before Rayquaza.

The black Rayquaza clearly had never encountered a Deoxys. It sensed the power in this unfamiliar Pokemon, though, and fixed it with a cold stare. Deoxys had the advantage of experience: it had faced Rayquaza's kind several times and knew what such an opponent could do. Few Rayquaza ever had a chance to fight a Deoxys. Against an equally strong foe it did not understand, this one could easily suffer for that lack of knowledge.

Both Pokemon prepared themselves, the tension between them rising. Rayquaza looked ready to attack when Zinnia finally broke her silence.

I...

Whatever it is, let's talk after the battle, Ethan said, cutting her off with a wave without looking back.

He didn't know what she wanted to say. Right now it didn't matter. The black Rayquaza was in front of him, and he needed to focus on it.

Zinnia hesitated, sighed, and stepped aside without another word. She had agreed to a fair contest before summoning Rayquaza. Unhappy as she was with the outcome, she had to let Ethan take his chance.

Ethan faced Rayquaza again. Let's see what you can do.

Deoxys, this battle is yours!

Deoxys moved. It placed a hand near its core and released the energy it had been gathering, sending a blast straight at Rayquaza. Rayquaza had been guarding against an attack; at nearly the same instant, it fired a powerful beam from its mouth. The two attacks collided.

Boom!

The resulting burst of energy blazed against the night, dazzling and dangerous.

That was the end of their opening test. They rushed at each other, and the fight at close and middle range began in earnest. The two Legendary Pokemon grappled, twisted, and chased each other above Sky Pillar so quickly they were almost impossible to follow. Gardevoir's telepathy let Ethan guide Deoxys throughout.

Ethan had commanded fierce battles before, but these two were different. Beyond a limited amount of guidance, he could do little to direct them. Much of what they could do was not tied to a fixed set of moves.

For a Trainer, that was both a blessing and a frustration. Pokemon this powerful could use their abilities freely, without being constrained by familiar moves. They could surprise an opponent in ways it could hardly defend against. On the other hand, Ethan could give only a broad plan of attack rather than precise instructions for every exchange. It left a Trainer in an awkward position.

He did not mind. If giving Deoxys room to act helped it defeat and capture Rayquaza, what did it matter? Nor was he merely a spectator. He might not dictate each movement, but his experience let him read some of Rayquaza's intentions and warn Deoxys in time.

After Deoxys missed one attack, Rayquaza abruptly twisted and lunged with its jaws open. The counterattack came at exactly the right moment, during a gap in Deoxys's offense. Against an evenly matched opponent, a bite from behind at that instant would be hard to avoid.

But Ethan had watched every movement. He caught Rayquaza's intention as soon as it turned and sent the warning through Gardevoir.

As though it had eyes in the back of its head, Deoxys used Teleport without even looking around. Rayquaza's jaws closed on empty air. Deoxys reappeared behind it and struck the would-be attacker with a sphere of energy.

Rayquaza cried out. Below, Misty raised a fist in delight.

Yes! Keep it up!

She had no stake in catching the Pokemon herself, but she was firmly on Ethan's side. Every advantage he gained thrilled her.

Zinnia's feelings were far less simple. Rayquaza had not chosen her, and part of her did not want Ethan to catch it. If he failed, they might be on equal footing again and she could have another chance.

Yet this was an extraordinary opportunity. She had summoned Rayquaza herself and could not bear to see it simply fly away, even if she was not the one to catch it. No human had ever succeeded in capturing a Rayquaza. Ethan was not one of the Draconid people, and their relationship had been strained, but his strength was undeniable. If anyone was to accomplish such a feat, she would rather it be a Trainer of his caliber than some unknown person.

Above them, the black Rayquaza grew angrier after being struck from behind. Its attacks became fiercer, wilder, and harder to predict.

Ethan knew it was pressing its offensive advantage, but he was not afraid. Deoxys was its equal in strength and had a Trainer supporting it. Rather than retreat, he ordered Deoxys to change from Normal Forme to Attack Forme and meet Rayquaza head-on.

The fight intensified at once. They clashed in the air, then crashed down onto the Dragonhark altar, forcing Ethan and the others to dodge out of their path. They fought back into the sky, vanished into the clouds, and finally emerged above the tower again. Every place they reached became part of the battlefield.

Each collision of their attacks exploded with a thunderous sound that echoed across the island. Fortunately, they were far from any settlement. Ethan did not have to worry about drawing a crowd or damaging anyone's property. He could let Deoxys fight with everything it had.

Neither Pokemon wanted to yield. Deoxys had Ethan's command and was determined to defeat the opponent he wanted to catch. Rayquaza had a fierce temper of its own. Having been summoned here, it had no intention of slinking away.

For both, only one thought remained: defeat the other at any cost.
