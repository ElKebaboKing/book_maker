Chapter 775: Into the Top Sixteen

Meowth! The battle isn't over. Let's go! Tyson called.

Meowth answered at once and rushed toward Venusaur again. Venusaur had drawn back, and even that modest gap would not be easy to cross.

Two vines whipped toward Meowth before it had gone far. This time it did not try to dodge them outright. It used Double Team, sending images of itself at Venusaur from several angles.

Tyson watched anxiously. Ethan had seen through Sceptile's Double Team at the start of the match. Would he find the real Meowth too? After everything else Ethan had done, Tyson would hardly be surprised if he did.

The vines swept through several images without hitting the real one.

Yes! Tyson punched the air.

Ethan could not identify the real Pokemon every time. Distance, size, movement, and the surroundings all mattered. He had to study the differences between the images. Meowth was small, and the move had happened in a blink. One glance was not enough.

Tyson's relief did not last. Once Venusaur cleared the first images, its vines swept back across those remaining and struck the real Meowth from behind. A sharp crack rang out. Meowth was driven to the ground like a ball with the air knocked out of it.

Many spectators winced for Tyson. Those vines are too fast, the announcer said. Seed Bomb injured Meowth earlier and slowed it down. Before that, it might have escaped.

Meowth lay still for a moment, then forced itself up and advanced once more. The crowd cheered its persistence. Tyson saw the welt across its back and knew it was fighting through pain.

Come on, Meowth. You have to beat Venusaur.

Ethan watched in silence. After that blow, Meowth seemed to draw on some last reserve. Its speed returned to what it had been at the start, and its reactions sharpened. It blocked the vines and shredded Venusaur's Razor Leaf with its claws.

Ethan realized what its Ability must be: Technician. It strengthened lower-powered moves, making simple attacks more effective. The force in Meowth's claws as they stopped the vines and tore apart the leaves made the Ability clear. No wonder it had improved so much in the relatively short time since Tyson caught it. Technician suited it perfectly.

Even with that surge, Ethan was not afraid. At his command, Venusaur attacked in earnest. The two Pokemon traded moves in what looked like the deciding exchange of the round.

Meowth revealed another of its specialties: Iron Tail. Its tail struck with the hardness of steel, knocking Venusaur's vines aside and freeing its claws for other attacks.

Meowth was using everything it had. Venusaur still looked composed. When it began fighting at full strength, Meowth could no longer keep up.

Venusaur caught it with a sudden Take Down, upsetting its rhythm. It hid a powerful Power Whip among its other vine attacks, staggering Meowth again. Then, while Meowth was dazed and unable to defend itself, Venusaur finished charging Solar Beam. The concentrated blast struck and knocked Meowth to the ground.

S-so strong... Morrison stared. They were trading blows a moment ago. How did Meowth lose so suddenly?

Because Ethan started fighting seriously, Misty said with a snort. He gave Tyson's Meowth time to show what it could do, so everyone could see Tyson wasn't weak. If it had been a stranger, Ethan would have ended it sooner.

Living alongside Ethan had taught Misty how he thought. She was right.

Ethan did not know Tyson well, but they had spent several days together. Beating him at full force from the first instant would have been like slapping him in public. Battles were not the only thing that mattered; people had to live with one another afterward. Tyson was already going to be eliminated. Letting him display his strength for a while cost Ethan nothing.

The referee raised a flag. Meowth is unable to battle! Venusaur wins this round!

Tyson came out of his daze at the announcement. He looked at his unconscious partner for a long moment, then shook his head with a helpless smile. Even Meowth had not managed to defeat one of Ethan's Pokemon.

Ethan was astonishingly strong. Tyson had been beaten so thoroughly that he could not argue with the result. He had already felt the distance between them and prepared himself for the loss. Now that it had come, he could accept it. But whatever fighting spirit he had left seemed to go with Meowth.

For his final Pokemon, Tyson sent out Hariyama. Ethan considered the two remaining members of his selected team, Gengar and Gardevoir. Then he chose neither and brought Tyranitar back.

Hariyama's Fighting type had a fourfold advantage over Tyranitar's Rock and Dark types. Anyone watching could see that Ethan was giving Tyson an opening. He had made the choice before the crowd and the cameras without concern.

Tyson no longer had the heart to take advantage. Hariyama fell before Tyranitar in a brief, one-sided finish. Ethan had won all six rounds without losing a Pokemon.

Congratulations to Ethan for reaching the top sixteen! the announcer called. And let's applaud Tyson as he leaves us. Thank you for a memorable match!

Both Trainers recalled their Pokemon and met at the center of the field to shake hands.

I lost, Tyson said, rueful but sincere. You're incredibly strong. You've shown me how much there is beyond what I know.

You're strong too, Ethan said with a smile. Without me here, you could have won this year's Ever Grande Conference.

Tyson took it for polite encouragement. He smiled bitterly, shook his head, and let it pass.

After they parted, Ethan waved to the stands and left through the competitors' passage. Misty, Ash, and the others were waiting. They gathered around him, applauding and offering congratulations.

Congratulations, Ethan!

You were amazing! I didn't think you'd beat Tyson so easily!

Where is Tyson? someone asked. Hasn't he come out yet?

He's already gone, Ethan told them. He's setting off on another journey right away. There wasn't time to tell you himself, so he asked me to apologize.

They exchanged startled looks. The six-to-zero loss had affected him that badly? Apart from Ash, most of them would have struggled to accept such a result. Going somewhere quiet to recover before continuing his travels was understandable. They respected his decision and said no more.

Ethan turned to Ash. I've made the top sixteen. You're next. Don't stumble now.

Don't worry! Ash clenched a fist and grinned. I'm not the Trainer I used to be. Just watch. I'll be in the top sixteen soon too!

Ash's match was that afternoon. His opponent was Katie, a Trainer from Lilycove City. She had impressed everyone during the preliminaries. However confident Ash sounded, his friends expected a difficult battle.

At noon they shared a hearty meal at a nearby restaurant, both to celebrate Ethan's win and to send Ash into his match in good spirits.

...

The sacred flame still burned brightly. At two in the afternoon, the Ever Grande Conference's main stadium filled with noise.

As the announcer introduced the competitors, Ethan, Misty, May, and the others made their way through the crowd to their reserved seats. They had spent time cheering Ash on in the competitors' passage, so he had already entered the arena when they reached the stands. They had missed the long warm-up and arrived just as the battle was about to begin.

Ethan sat down, set aside his popcorn, and looked up at Katie on the big screen. She was a beautiful woman in her twenties with long green hair. Standing with her arms folded, she looked calm.

Paper rustled beside him. Brock had produced a small notebook and was flipping through it. Without lifting his head, he recited, I keep notes on every beautiful older woman. The people of Lilycove call Miss Katie the Cool Beauty. She loves music, plays the clarinet, and enjoys spicy foodespecially spaghetti with Tamato Berries...

Stop! Misty cut him off. Where do you even find all that? And why is it all trivia? What about her battles? What types does she use? Which Pokemon is her ace?

Of course I didn't write that down. Brock smiled. It's my hobby notebook. I record what interests me. By the way, she's one of the three most beautiful women in this Conference.

That was Brock for you. Ethan could only admire the effort. These details must have been harder to gather than information about Katie's Pokemon. When a beautiful woman was involved, nothing could stop Brock.

Ethan looked at Katie again. She was certainly striking. If she was only in Brock's top three, what did the other two look like? He was curious in a purely appreciative way, of course. Nothing more.

The wheel settled on the Rock Field for Ash and Katie's first half. Gears raised a battlefield crowded with rocks of different sizes. At the referee's signal, both Trainers released their opening Pokemon.

Ash chose Torkoal. Katie chose Venomoth.

Torkoal's Fire type should have given it the advantage against the Bug and Poison-type Venomoth. Yet Ethan and Ash's close friends exchanged uneasy smiles. They had noticed a strange pattern: when Ash faced a bad type matchup, he struggled but sometimes won. When the matchup favored him, he often lost.

Perhaps it was only chance. Even so, seeing this pairing gave them a bad feeling.
