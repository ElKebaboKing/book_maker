Chapter 198: Danny Is Stunned

"For the first round, use an Ice-type move to freeze the water from a geyser," Danny explained. "Whoever freezes theirs fastest wins!"

As he spoke, he sent out his Pokemon. A Nidoqueen appeared on the snowy summit. Danny pocketed the empty Poke Ball and glanced sideways at the Great Ball in Ethan's hand, trying to guess what Ethan would use.

Ethan took a look at Nidoqueen. Like the Nidoking he had seen before, it was a large, two-legged Pokemon. Its hide was blue, though, and Nidoqueen were always female, a counterpart to the purple, male Nidoking. They were said to be fiercely protective of their young.

To freeze a geyser, a Pokemon that could use an Ice-type move would be the obvious choice. Nidoqueen was Poison- and Ground-type and could not learn such a move on its own. Danny must have taught it one by some other means for this event.

But why choose Nidoqueen? The Orange Islands were surrounded by water. Seel, Dewgong, Cloyster, and Lapras were all Water-type Pokemon that could learn Ice-type moves and combine them with their natural strengths. Ethan could not see what Danny hoped to gain from a Ground-type instead.

He did not spend much time puzzling over it. Whatever Danny's reasoning, this Nidoqueen would have no chance against the Pokemon Ethan had chosen.

"Come out, Articuno!"

At first Danny thought he had misheard, or that Ethan had called out the wrong name. Then Articuno spread its wings and appeared before him in all its beauty. Danny's mouth fell open.

"Ar... Ar... Articuno?!"

He rubbed his eyes and stepped forward for a closer look. Articuno flapped its wings, startling him into retreating to his former spot.

"Mr. Vale... Where did you get this Articuno? Is it the God of Ice?"

The God of Ice? Ethan stared for a moment, then shook his head.

"Sorry, this isn't the God of Ice from the Orange Islands. I happened to encounter this Articuno during my travels."

Danny breathed a little easier. The God of Ice, like the Gods of Thunder and Fire, was a sacred bird to the islanders. For generations, the three had protected the Orange Islands and maintained the region's delicate ecological balance. Nothing could be allowed to happen to them. Danny had grown up hearing their legends.

Then again, Articuno were a species. They were exceedingly rare, but the Orange Islands were not the only place they lived. And the legendary God of Ice was so powerful and so difficult to find that it could hardly have been captured by a human.

Even so, Danny now looked at Ethan differently. He had taken him for an ordinary challenger. With an Articuno before him, that was impossible to believe.

"Mr. Vale, who are you?"

Danny did not even notice how much his tone had changed.

The Orange Islands were close to Kanto, but Danny seldom followed news from beyond the islands. He lived in his own small world. Even Ethan's Indigo League victory and the attention it had drawn had escaped him.

"Who am I?" Ethan gave a small shake of his head. "Here in the Orange League, I really am just an ordinary challenger. But if you look up what's been happening in other regions lately, you'll find out soon enough."

Danny wanted to pull out his phone and search at once, but the contest was underway. It was no time for that. He set the question aside, and both trainers turned back to their Pokemon.

Articuno and Nidoqueen stood side by side, each facing one of two fissures. Both fissures were fed by the same geyser; rock above its outlet divided it into two openings. When the geyser erupted, it would send up two streams at once.

Danny glanced at Nidoqueen. He had always been proud of it, but beside Articuno it looked painfully ordinary.

They waited. The ground trembled beneath their feet, and the geyser erupted. Two neighboring jets of water surged from the fissures into the air.

Now!

Ethan and Danny gave their commands almost together.

"Articuno, Ice Beam!"

"Nidoqueen, Ice Beam!"

The steaming jets rose three or four meters. When the Ice Beams struck them, great clouds of vapor billowed out. The water was so hot that freezing it solid would take tremendous power in a very short time.

For Ethan, that was no obstacle. Articuno was already exceptionally strong, and the Never-Melt Ice it wore made its move stronger still. Its Ice Beam was visibly wider than Nidoqueen's.

Almost instantly, Articuno froze its stream into a pillar of ice. Nidoqueen needed another five or six seconds to finish freezing its own.

The winner was clear. Articuno's pillar was solid through and through; it would not mostly melt before the final round, as the ice had during Ash's challenge. It was even harder and colder than the pillar Danny's Nidoqueen had made.

Danny stared at the result with envy and resignation. "It was over in an instant... So this is Articuno's power. Incredible!"

The snowfall had eased a little. After so long outside, a thin layer of snow had settled on them.

Sabrina stepped up to Ethan and carefully brushed the snow from him. He smiled at her and accepted the gesture, then turned to Danny.

"Looks like I won the first round. What are we doing for the second?"
