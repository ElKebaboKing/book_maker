Chapter 413: Infamous

With Ethan present, Jimmy naturally would not have to fight alone.

In truth, Ethan and Team Rocket had long since become bitter enemies, each wanting to eliminate the other. Unless these people suddenly found a conscience, abandoned evil, and began doing good for the world, Ethan would cause trouble whenever he encountered them. Even if they were merely attending Giovanni's birthday, he would still ruin the celebration!

However...

Ethan looked to either side. Apart from Gengar hidden in his shadow, only Eevee, which had remained outside its Poke Ball, and Dragonair, whom he had just sent out to battle Typhlosion, were currently beside him.

Both Pokemon faced a disadvantage against Steel types, so Ethan naturally could not send them into the fight.

After a moment's thought, he released Gardevoir and told her to help Typhlosion battle Steelix and Skarmory.

Blue light flashed, and Gardevoir appeared beside Typhlosion.

Only when they saw Gardevoir did the silver-haired man and Attila finally turn their attention toward Ethan.

"So it's you! I never imagined we would encounter someone so famous here!"

The word famous was clearly meant sarcastically. They had recognized Ethan.

Images of Ethan and accounts of his actions had already spread throughout Team Rocket. Few members did not know him, so the men had recognized him at first glance.

Ethan studied the crystal curiously, then let his gaze rest on Raikou for a moment. "Team Rocket, did you come here specifically to capture Raikou?"

"It's only one of our targets. After this, we'll capture Entei and Suicune as well. Eventually, we'll even set our sights on Ho-Oh."

Perhaps because he acknowledged Ethan as a worthy opponent, the silver-haired man concealed nothing and openly revealed their objective.

"Ah, after all this talking, it's time we introduced ourselves. My name is Hun, and he's Attila. Like Vicious, whom you defeated before, we belong to the Pokemon Hunting Unit."

"Pokemon Hunting Unit?"

"That's right," Hun continued. "It's a unit devoted to hunting Legendary Pokemon. Every member is among the finest elites in Team Rocket. Of course, if the term is unfamiliar, you can use another name for us: Pokemon Hunters!"

Ethan thought of Vicious. He had no idea whether the Dragon Tamers had managed to capture him. Since neither Clair nor Lance had sent any news, they had probably found nothing.

"Perfect." Ethan nodded in understanding, then smiled. "Elites are exactly who I beat. Small fry aren't even worth my time!"

The statement was extraordinarily arrogant and instantly drew the men's full hostility.

Hun stared at Ethan for a long moment before his brow tightened. "I see. You truly are arrogant. Within Team Rocket, even the four Executives regard you as our greatest enemy. I want to see exactly what you're capable of!"

The brief but fierce verbal clash ended there. Both sides knew a battle was unavoidable.

Jimmy had long since grown impatient. Every additional second meant more suffering for Raikou inside the barrier. Now that the adults had finished trading words, he immediately waved a hand and gave his command.

"Typhlosion, use Flamethrower!"

A huge mass of flame erupted from Typhlosion's mouth. Facing the attack, Hun calmly ordered, "Use Sandstorm."

Steelix's lower body began rotating rapidly. Its enormous body crushed the loose stones and soil beneath it, instantly throwing a vast spray of sand toward Typhlosion.

The sand and flames collided in midair.

Empowered by Steelix, the sand not only extinguished the flames but continued onward, filling Typhlosion's eyes!

That was not the end. Attila issued an attack command as well.

"Skarmory, use Wing Attack."

White light covered the flying Skarmory's wings. It plunged downward and knocked Typhlosion to the ground.

"Typhlosion!"

Jimmy hurried forward and caught his Pokemon.

Seeing that this was all Typhlosion could manage, Attila could not resist mocking Jimmy after his successful strike.

"You're still too young, kid. A Fire-type Pokemon may seem to hold the advantage against Steel types, but what can you do against my Rock-type move?"

Ignoring the shaken Jimmy, the two men turned their attention back to Ethan.

This man was the greatest enemy they needed to guard against!

Steelix and Skarmory shifted their targets to Gardevoir and launched their attacks.

The two Pokemon's assault was exceptionally fierce. These men truly deserved to be called the best of Team Rocket's elites. Ordinary grunts could not compare with them.

Although Gardevoir faced two opponents alone, her exceptional strength allowed her to hold her ground for the moment.

Recovering his composure, Jimmy released a Beedrill to join the fight.

This time, however, he had learned his lesson. Realizing that he might not be a match for Team Rocket directly, he changed targets and focused his attack on the mechanical shark.

"Beedrill, use Pin Missile!"

Light erupted from Beedrill's twin lances. The next moment, hundreds of steel-like needles streamed toward the mechanical shark.

Thud-thud-thud-thud!

The barrage struck the machine, causing explosions across its outer structure. As its armor broke apart, thick smoke swept over the battlefield.

"What!?"

Hun and Attila immediately frowned.

They had never expected this seemingly single-minded brat to think so quickly.

Now the mechanical shark had been damaged. Without the machine, even defeating Ethan would not allow them to capture Raikou by force!

At that exact moment, the men's distraction created an opening for Gardevoir. Her Psychic struck Skarmory squarely in midair, sending it flying backward into Steelix.

After weighing their options, Hun decided at once. "There's no helping it. Attila, we'll retreat for now."

Attila, the man of action, was clearly unwilling to leave, but he had no choice but to agree. After he worked the controls, more than a dozen smoke bombs launched at once, instantly engulfing the area in a thick cloud.

"Cough, cough..."

Jimmy had been standing too close and choked several times on the smoke.

By the time the white cloud dispersed, Team Rocket and the crystal had vanished!

Seeing this, Ethan said quietly, "Gengar."

Gengar emerged from his shadow.

"What do you need?"

Ethan quickly instructed it, "Search for their trail. They can't have gone far. If you can, follow them and find out where their base is!"

"Understood. Leave it to me!"
