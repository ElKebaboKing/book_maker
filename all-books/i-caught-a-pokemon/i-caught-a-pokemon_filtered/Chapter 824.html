Chapter 824: Already Over

Jessie. James turned to her with a bright smile. Don't you understand? The operation is over!

Operation? Jessie stared at him. What operation? What's over?

Why, the James drew out the word, then swept an arm dramatically. Several grappling lines dropped from the ceiling in front of them. great Pokemon snatching operation!

Pokemon snatching? Jessie looked bewildered. Since when was that the plan? And my battle isn't even finished!

As she spoke, the last seconds of the five-minute clock ran out. Wobbuffet was still standing, but its Contest score was far below Squirtle's. By the rules, Jessie had lost.

No! Three red Xs appeared on the judges' displays. Tears streamed down Jessie's face as she reached toward the prize. My Saffron Ribbon!

Lilian had caught on by then. At first, she, the judges, and much of the audience had assumed this was an unscheduled part of the show. The director's alarmed denial in her earpiece told her otherwise.

You she began, ready to stop them. Then several children ran onto the stage. Lilian recognized May's companions from yesterday's rehearsal. Why were they up there too?

Ash reached Team Rocket first. What are you doing here? he demanded.

Twerp, Meowth said, this has nothing to do with you. Stay out of it, meow!

While he spoke, Meowth scanned the audience for the man they feared. The disruption had gone on long enough for Ethan to appear, but there was still no sign of him. Meowth breathed a little easier. He dared not wait any longer.

He flipped several smoke bombs into his paws and hurled them down. They burst with sharp pops, spreading thick white smoke across the stage.

Even Harley now understood that he had been tricked. He leaped onto the stage and plunged into the smoke. Team Rocket were already rising on their cables.

Wait! Jessibella! Give me back my Pokemon!

Meowth only laughed. You were stupid enough to hand them over, meow! Don't blame us!

He pressed a switch. A tremendous blast tore a hole through the hall's heavy roof. Cold air rushed inside, blowing everyone's hair about. James and Meowth laughed wildly as the cables carried the trio up through the opening and out of sight.

The gust scattered the smoke. It also blew something from Harley's pocket, sent it spinning through the air, and slapped it against his face. He pulled it away. It was his Kanto Contest pass, bearing Jessie's ugly autograph.

Damn it! he shouted. He wanted to throw it away, but he still needed the pass. He stuffed it back into his pocket and ran for the exit.

He hated those swindlers. So much for the fairy Jessibella! He had trusted her to avenge him and had handed over his Pokemon. It was humiliating. When he caught the trio, he would tear them apart.

At the doors, Harley looked back and shot May a hateful glare. Somehow this was all her fault. Next time they met, he would make her pay.

May could only stare after him. She had no idea what had happened. Why did it feel as though she was being blamed for it? Ash, Lilian, and the spectators were similarly confused.

One moment Jessibella had been battling; the next, she was gone. Who were the people with her? Why had Harley chased them? It had all happened too quickly for anyone to piece together.

Lilian recovered first, a professional to the end. She smiled toward the crowd. It seems we've had an unexpected interruption. But the clock has run out, and under Contest rules, the winner of the Saffron Contest is May! Let's congratulate her!

The lights came back on. Staff hurried onto the stage to clear away pieces of the damaged roof. The odd finish did not keep the spectators from standing and cheering for May.

The final battle had not been especially exciting or beautiful, but a win was a win. May lifted Squirtle high and waved to the crowd, smiling. Her excitement at earning the Ribbon was plain to see.

Once the stage was clean, Mr. Contesta personally presented her with the Saffron Ribbon. May had won her first Ribbon since arriving in Kanto.

That evening, Ethan treated the group to a generous dinner in celebration. Afterward, it was time to move on. This Ribbon was only a beginning. To enter the Kanto Grand Festival, May still needed four more.

Perhaps May's victory spurred Ash on. Perhaps he felt behind Ethan, having earned only one Frontier Symbol. Either way, he did not linger in Saffron City. With Max and Brock, he set out after dark for the nearby Battle Arena.

Ethan thought Ash was rushing. Given the strange turns Ash's journeys often took, setting off into the night might cost him time rather than save it. But it was Ash's journey to choose. Ethan and May had an adventure of their own, and he had no need to interfere.

After seeing the three off, Ethan and May returned to the Saffron Gym. Ethan spent more time alone with Sabrina. They rested there one final night. Then he left her warm company and set out with May again.

Their next destination was the Battle Palace, on an island in the Kanto sea near Cinnabar Island. The facility stood on Metallica Island. Ethan knew little about the island beyond its position among the islands along a sea route. He had chosen it next so that later Frontier challenges could follow a sensible route without unnecessary backtracking.

Ash might walk until he got lost. Ethan preferred to make a rough plan before leaving. Neither way of traveling was inherently better; they simply suited different people.

The following morning, Ethan kissed Sabrina goodbye. Her reluctance to see him leave showed in her eyes. After breakfast, he and May packed and headed for the nearest port city.

The first part of their route overlapped with Ash's. Only farther along would the roads branch in different directions. If Ash had stayed one more night in Saffron, they could have traveled together a little longer.

Then again, too many people traveling together made an adventure feel more like a picnic and offered fewer chances to learn. Perhaps Ash had been wise to go ahead.

...

The sky was clear and the sun bright. It was a fine day to travel.

Outside Saffron City, Ethan and May walked side by side along a riverbank levee, then crossed a broad stretch of grassland toward the sea. The soft grass underfoot smelled fresh. A light breeze made the walk comfortable.

May glanced across the fields and stopped. A farm stood in the distance.

Look, Ethan! She pointed to it with a smile. Isn't that farm beautiful? I used to imagine living quietly in a place like that. It must feel wonderful.

Ethan nodded absently. A moment later, something came back to him, and he stopped too. Would you like to see it?

Huh? May clearly wanted to, but she hesitated. Could we? Aren't we in a hurry?

A journey doesn't end in a day. It's good to take a break sometimes, and we can spare the time. Ethan smiled. If you'd like to visit, we can ask the farmer whether we may have a look around.

Really? That would be great!

When he nodded, May eagerly hopped down from the levee and started toward the farm. She had taken only a few steps when two Voltorb appeared from nowhere and fired electricity at her.

May had not seen them. She cried out and watched the two flashes race toward her. They struck something like an invisible wall and dispersed in midair.

She turned and saw Ethan's Gardevoir lowering an arm. He had released it without her noticing. It must have saved her.

But why had Ethan brought Gardevoir out at just that moment? Had he seen the danger beforehand?

May could not have guessed that the scene had reminded Ethan of an anime episode, prompting him to prepare. Without him, the Voltorb would have given her a painful shock. He had recognized the risk in time to prevent it.

Still shaken, May put a hand to her chest and backed away from the Voltorb. Why did they attack me?

They probably took you for an intruder, Ethan said. We're close to the farm, and you were walking toward it. They may be guarding the property.

That's right! a girl's voice called nearby.

The grass rustled, and a young girl emerged. She held a broom and wore denim overalls. Freckles dotted her face, and her untidy red hair was tied in pigtails. She looked Ethan and May over.

Who are you? Why are you coming onto my family's farm?

May glanced down and realized she was already standing on someone else's grass. Oh, I'm sorry. I didn't notice

Before she could finish, the girl recognized her face. She gave a surprised cry and ran over, stopping directly in front of May.

Are you the one who won the Saffron Contest yesterday?

Huh? May was surprised to be recognized so soon. Yes, that's me.

I knew it! The girl cheered, her cheeks flushing with excitement. I saw your performance and your battles on television. You were amazing!

The commotion drew a man and a woman out of the farmhouse. They looked toward the group.

Nicolette, what's going on? Do we have visitors?
