Chapter 287: Turning Down the Offer

Professor Elm turned his attention to Ethan.

"You said your name was Ethan Vale?" He adjusted his glasses, sounding excited. "I know that name!"

He knows my name? Ethan was not surprised. Pokemon competitions were enormously popular. Television, short videos, and websites had carried stories about him more than once. People even talked about his unbeaten record in schools, workplaces, and shopping centers.

Kanto and Johto shared a landmass and kept in close contact. The excitement over the Indigo League had not entirely faded. Ethan might not be known in every household here, but most people had probably heard of him.

Then Elm said, "You're the young man who published a paper with Professor Oak and Professor Ivy recently, aren't you? I read it. Your theory about the butterfly effect was especially good!"

Oh. That's how he knows me.

Of course a Pokemon professor would follow academic work more closely than tournaments. Ethan knew his own level of expertise. He smiled awkwardly and shook his head.

"I'm embarrassed to take credit. I have no research experience or qualifications. I only benefited from working with two professors. I made an offhand comment, and I never expected them to put it in the paper."

"You're too modest," Elm said with a laugh. "Two leading professors wouldn't both value the opinion of just anyone. Your idea really did offer a fresh perspective."

Grateful that they had recovered the Poke Ball, Elm warmly invited them into a reception room for tea.

As they talked, Ethan learned that Elm had studied at Celadon University and had been one of Professor Oak's outstanding students. He regarded Oak as both a great teacher and his most respected rival.

Elm set down his cup. "I also heard that you're an assistant to Professor Cerise?"

"I'm not really an assistant." Ethan gave another rueful smile. "I'm a field investigator at the lab. I mostly run errands. Do you know Professor Cerise too?"

"Of course!" Elm's expression grew nostalgic. "Our field is large in some ways and small in others. We work in different regions, but I've had many academic exchanges with Professor Cerise and Professor Ivy."

Ethan nodded. Come to think of it, Oak, Cerise, Ivy, and Elm all had names linked to trees or plants, despite working in different regions.

He sipped his tea. He knew little about tea leaves and could only tell that it was some kind of green tea.

Looker asked, "Professor Elm, what is your field of research?"

Elm straightened in his chair. His eyes lit up. "Pokemon Eggs!"

"Pokemon Eggs?"

What was there to study about them? Before anyone could voice the question, Elm continued.

"That's right. From what I've observed, all Pokemon hatch from Eggs! The shells usually have distinctive patterns, and those patterns can help you guess what kind of Pokemon is inside."

"All of them hatch from Eggs?" Looker was startled. He had never heard that before. He had learned something new already.

"Surprising, isn't it?" Elm had clearly expected that reaction. "Some Pokemon look like mammals, but they hatch from Eggs too. And here's something remarkable: a Pokemon inside its Egg can already see the world outside before it hatches!"

Elm grew more animated with every word. Professors seemed unable to stop talking once they reached their own specialties.

"Oh, that reminds me!" He turned to Ethan, his eyes brightening again. "Since you recovered my Pokemon, would you like to choose a Johto starter? Unlike Kanto's, ours are the Grass type Chikorita, Water type Totodile, and Fire type Cyndaquil. They're all excellent Pokemon!"

Ethan declined politely without hesitation. "Thank you, but please save that chance for a new Trainer. I already have Kanto's three starters, and their roles would overlap. With too many Pokemon, I couldn't give each one the care it deserves."

"The three starters. That's an interesting way to put it."

Elm did not mind the refusal. Ethan's willingness to pass up a free Pokemon showed him that this Trainer was different from those who only wanted to catch more and more.

They stayed a little longer. Assistants kept coming in to report to Elm, and it was clear he was busy, so they said their goodbyes. Elm insisted on walking them all the way to the entrance before returning to his work.

Watching him go, Looker said to Ethan, "You made a good impression. I could tell he liked you even more after you turned down the starter."

"Did I?" Ethan rubbed his nose. "I didn't notice."

Looker patted his shoulder and leaned close. "Being on good terms with him could benefit you. Someone in our organization told me Professor Elm may have a Master Ball. That must be precious to a Trainer."

Professor Elm has a Master Ball too? Ethan's eyes widened. He had not expected the scholarly professor to own an item Trainers dreamed of having.

He wanted one himself, but he barely knew Elm and had no real hope of obtaining it. With a wistful sigh, he shook his head.

"A Master Ball can catch a Pokemon without fail. It doesn't just tempt Trainers. Anyone would want one."

They chatted a little more. Then Looker checked the time and said goodbye.

"I'd better go. Silver was lucky to get away this time, thanks to Suicune. Team Rocket won't be so lucky next time. In ten days, we plan to attack their base in Mahogany Town. I hope you and Sabrina will join us."
