Chapter 368: Two Legendary Beasts

Inside the crystal tower, Entei raced continuously up the stairs while Ethan and his three Pokemon pursued close behind.

Attacks formed from flames and psychic power flew toward Entei from time to time, but it evaded every one with ease.

The two sides maintained a distance of more than ten meters as they raced onward. Entei could not shake Ethan, but Ethan could not catch it either.

Even from this brief observation, Entei's movements appeared exceptionally agile and powerful. It took enormous strides, and every effortless leap carried it five or six meters upward. Its mane streamed in the rushing air, giving it a majestic appearance.

Only now did Ethan realize that Entei did not merely resemble a Tibetan Mastiff. It truly possessed features reminiscent of a male lion as well.

As the chase continued, however, he gradually sensed that this Entei differed slightly from the one he had seen before.

The two looked identical, and Ethan knew little about them, so he could not immediately explain what felt strange.

Still...

While climbing, he glanced down.

They had already put considerable distance between themselves and the Unown. Why did Entei refuse to stop? Even after being attacked, why had it never retaliated?

Was it trying to lure him somewhere else, or did it have some other purpose?

This was far too strange.

Despite his doubts, Ethan knew he could not allow it to keep running.

"Charizard, cut it off!"

At Ethan's command, Charizard suddenly accelerated, flew over Entei, and landed on the staircase ahead, blocking its path. With Charizard in front and the others pursuing from behind, Entei had no choice but to stop.

"Gardevoir, use Psychic!"

Gardevoir caught up and raised both arms, gathering psychic power. Before she could attack, Entei turned, opened its jaws, and launched a burst of flame.

The fire arrived in an instant. Gardevoir hurriedly leaned aside, and the flames passed within inches of her head.

That was close!

Now that they had cornered it, Entei seemed to have accepted that it could no longer avoid battle. At last, it struck back.

With time running short, Ethan had no intention of fighting fairly. He ordered all three Pokemon to attack together.

Figures crossed and moves flew in every direction as they surrounded Entei, yet it held its own against all three opponents.

Fireballs and Shadow Balls roared overhead...

Explosions from the battle shattered the nearby crystal formations one after another. In only a short time, the area had become a complete wreck.

This Entei truly was powerful.

Ethan had never expected a Pokemon created by the Unown to possess such strength. No wonder Brock and Misty had been unable to defeat Molly!

But Ethan's three Pokemon were equally formidable. As the battle dragged on, the cramped surroundings restricted Entei's movements, and several attacks finally struck it.

Entei roared and smashed through a nearby crystal wall, escaping outside.

Without hesitation, Ethan led his Pokemon through the opening in pursuit.

He looked up and discovered that dusk had nearly arrived. The sun was about to set.

After spending so long inside the crystal tower, Ethan finally breathed fresh air again. The cool breeze sharpened his senses.

Now outside, Entei raced upward along the tower's outer surface.

Ethan's three Pokemon continued attacking as they pursued. Without the restrictions of the interior, Entei appeared far more composed than it had during the battle inside.

But with Charizard's advantage in the air, Ethan's Pokemon once again surrounded Entei. This time, it had nowhere left to retreat.

Entei surveyed the threatening Pokemon around it, opened its mouth, and growled at Ethan.

Unable to understand it, Ethan turned toward Gengar. "What is it saying?"

"Nothing in particular." Gengar shook its head. "It's just threatening you!"

At those words, Ethan became almost certain.

As he had suspected, this Entei had not been created by the Unown. It was a real Entei!

Although both were Entei, subtle differences separated the genuine Pokemon from the imitation. No wonder he had sensed something unusual between them.

But Ethan remembered that no real Entei had appeared during this part of the movie.

Something must have changed.

Perhaps the strange events here and the appearance of the false Entei had attracted the real one's attention, causing it to investigate.

Its authenticity made the situation even better. Ethan could capture it!

Entei was gradually falling behind. Just as Ethan prepared to order another attack, a thick stream of water suddenly shot in from the side and struck Charizard.

A blur crossed Ethan's vision. Charizard was blasted away and crashed through more than a dozen crystal pillars.

The blow seriously injured Charizard. The Water-type move had looked tremendously powerful, and it possessed a type advantage. That single attack nearly left Charizard unable to battle.

Huh!?

Ethan raised an eyebrow and turned in surprise. A distinctive Pokemon stood nearby.

That familiar form belonged to Suicune!

Why had Suicune appeared too?

Ethan was astonished.

The effects of the crystallization had spread farther than he realized. It had alarmed not only humans, but even Johto's legendary guardians.

But...

Had Suicune launched that attack to save Entei?

As fellow members of Ho-Oh's guardians, they even came to one another's aid. The situation was becoming increasingly interesting...

Ethan did not know whether any other members of the trio existed elsewhere in the world. If they did not, this Suicune had to be the same one he and Looker had encountered while pursuing Silver.

With two Legendary beasts drawn here in succession, Raikou was probably nearby as well and had simply chosen not to reveal itself.

If Raikou was included, all three of Johto's Legendary beasts had gathered in one place!

Looking at the two Legendary Pokemon, Gengar turned toward Ethan, its voice brimming with excitement. "What do we do now?"

As a Pokemon that loved mischief above all else, Gengar found greater chaos even more entertaining.

Ethan shook his head. "The plan has changed. This is now a capture battle!"

"What about the Unown?"

"How can we worry about them now?" Ethan chuckled. "We'll leave the Unown to Ash and the others! Entei and Suicune have appeared together. This is a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity. How could I possibly let it pass!!"
