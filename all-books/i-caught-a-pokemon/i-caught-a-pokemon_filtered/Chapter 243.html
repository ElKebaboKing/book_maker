Chapter 243: Luana's Request

Bang!

Two flashes crossed, and Scyther's X-Scissor struck Alakazam. Bug-type moves were strong against Psychic types. The hit dealt twice the usual damage.

"Ahh!" Alakazam cried out in pain.

Luana frowned. "Alakazam, get away from there!"

Alakazam looked up to see Scyther raising its blades again. Alarmed, it used Teleport and reappeared on the other side of the field.

It had no time to catch its breath. Scyther had already turned and was rushing at it once more.

Alakazam used Confusion to keep its attacker at a distance, but Scyther repeated its earlier move. Quick Attack carried it around the psychic force and rapidly closed the gap.

Scyther's stubborn fighting spirit had impressed Ethan back in the Bug Pokemon forest. Now Alakazam was learning what that determination felt like from the other side.

Luana grew frustrated as Alakazam struggled. Scyther just would not let it go. She was not about to stand by and watch it lose.

"Alakazam, use Reflect!"

Alakazam raised both hands. A barrier of psychic power formed before it just as Scyther's X-Scissor came down again.

Despite its name, Reflect did not send attacks back at an opponent. It raised a protective wall that weakened physical blows. Scyther's blades passed through the barrier and struck Alakazam. Reflect blunted the attack, but the type advantage still made it hurt.

Luana sighed. Only moments ago, she had mocked Ethan for being unable to command Scyther. After he had hit his own Pokemon, the battle had turned sharply against her. Did Scyther need a beating to make it behave? She could not understand it.

Whatever the reason, she had to find a way to recover. She turned toward her other Pokemon.

"Marowak, help Alakazam!"

Marowak broke away from its fight with Charizard and ran toward Alakazam. It had gone only a few steps when Charizard beat its wings, flew ahead of it, and blocked the way. Its fierce grin made its meaning plain: Marowak was not getting past.

"Marowak, we can't waste time! Use Double-Edge!"

Marowak accelerated and drove its hard skull straight at Charizard. As a Ground-type Pokemon, it was skilled at Ground-type moves, but those would not affect a Flying type like Charizard. Luana had chosen a Normal-type attack instead.

Neither of Luana's Pokemon was in a good position.

Marowak charged without holding back. Charizard gave one light flap and rose into the air, avoiding the blow. Marowak tried to use the opening to continue toward Alakazam, but Charizard swooped down with Wing Attack. Marowak had to dodge.

"Good! Keep it there!" Ethan was more at ease now that Charizard had the upper hand. He called to Scyther, "Finish Alakazam! Use Fury Cutter!"

Scyther swung its arms in a rapid series of five or six slashes. Alakazam evaded two, but the remaining Fury Cutter strikes landed. It could take no more. It fell onto its back, unable to battle.

The referee raised a hand. Under Kumquat Gym's rules, a side lost as soon as one of its Pokemon went down. With Alakazam defeated, the result was clear.

"Alakazam is unable to battle! The winner is the challenger, Ethan!"

At last it was over. Ethan breathed out. He was happy to win, but this match had tired him more than an ordinary Gym battle. In a Double Battle at this level, he had to keep track of two opponents and two of his own Pokemon at once. That took far more concentration than the uneven battles he sometimes found on the street.

Still, he had won. He called Charizard and Scyther back to him, then looked at Alakazam with a little sympathy. For the next few nights, it might have the same nightmare over and over: a scarred Scyther pursuing it with gleaming blades and refusing to stop.

It had lost the battle, and its imagination might go on punishing it. Poor Alakazam.

Luana came over. With the match finished, she had regained the booming manner Ethan had seen when they first met.

"Congratulations!" She seemed entirely untroubled by her loss and gave his shoulder a hearty slap. "You beat me with a Scyther that wouldn't even listen to you. That's impressive!"

Ethan had heard plenty of compliments from Gym Leaders and could answer politely without thinking. Luana's hand was another matter. She had almost knocked the breath out of him.

"Not at all. Your Alakazam and Marowak are well trained. I was lucky to win."

"You're modest too. Here, take your reward. You've earned the Jade Star Badge."

Luana took a small badge from inside her clothes and handed it to him. Sabrina came over as Ethan looked down at it. Like the other Orange League badges, it was made from a shell. This one resembled a gray conch marked with a six-pointed star.

Ethan thanked Luana and started to leave with Sabrina. They had gone only a few steps when she called after them.

"Please wait!"

He turned. "Is there something else?"

Luana hesitated. "Once you've finished your Orange League challenge, are you going to travel to another region?"

"Yes. After I defeat the Supreme Gym Leader here, I plan to go to Johto and challenge its League."

"That's wonderful!" Luana smiled. "In that case, could I ask the two of you for a favor?"

"A favor?" Ethan and Sabrina exchanged a glance. "Tell us what it is. If we can help, of course we will."
