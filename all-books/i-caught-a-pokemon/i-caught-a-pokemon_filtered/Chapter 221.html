Chapter 221: The Morning After

Ethan slept until the sun was high before he opened his eyes.

He rubbed his temples. The fruit wine had a stronger aftereffect than he had expected. He had thought a night's sleep would clear his head, but even mild wine could leave him dizzy if he drank enough.

Half awake, he reached across the bed. The space beside him was empty. Sabrina was gone.

Where did she go?

He yawned, sat up, and looked around the room. She was nowhere in sight.

Looking back, he had been impulsive the night before. With wine clouding his judgment, he had gone much further with Sabrina than he had planned.

After washing, Ethan folded the bedsheet and put it in his bag. He would find somewhere to dispose of it after they left. He did not want to leave evidence of last night behind for anyone to find and laugh about.

He went to Sabrina's room and knocked. No one answered. Puzzled, he headed for the banquet hall.

Ash, Misty, Tracey, Delia, and the others were eating a leisurely lunch. They greeted him as he entered.

"You're up late, Ethan!" Ash said around a mouthful of food. "We've been awake for ages. We're leaving after lunch to challenge the next Gym. Misty said that if you didn't get up soon, we should pull your blanket off!"

Misty's face flushed. "Don't make things up!" She glanced shyly at Ethan, then gave Ash a sharp knock on the head, Delia's presence notwithstanding. "You're the one who said that. Don't blame me!"

Their bags were packed and bulging beside Ash's feet.

Ethan smiled. "I've been exhausted these last few days. I didn't want to get up. And I wouldn't recommend barging into my room."

"Why not?" Ash asked.

"I might be sleeping without pajamas," Ethan said with a grin. "You'd both regret the surprise."

Ash and Tracey fell silent. Ethan let the joke stand and approached the table.

Several people were missing. "Where are Professor Oak and Professor Ivy?"

Misty studied him. Perhaps she was imagining it, but something about Ethan seemed different today, as though he had matured overnight.

"They took diving equipment and went to the three islands with Carol," she said. "They're studying the undersea currents for their paper. I think they want to test a theory."

"That sounds like hard work," Ethan said. "I thought professors like them spent all their time in laboratories. I didn't expect them to go into the field themselves."

Tracey remembered something and lowered his voice. "Carol said that when she went to bring Maren back last night, she also searched Lightning Island. She found wreckage everywhere, but no sign of the collector or his body. Maybe he slipped away."

The collector had survived Lugia's attack and escaped? Ethan raised an eyebrow. He was surprised, though the man had never struck him as someone who would die easily.

Had the ordeal changed the collector's mind? Would he still try to capture Lugia? Ethan could not know. Once he left the Orange Islands, he doubted they would ever meet again.

He still had not found Sabrina. As he looked around, ready to ask about her, footsteps sounded behind him.

She entered the hall with Maren just behind her. Sabrina's hair was damp from a recent shower. Maren yawned and looked as though she, too, had only just woken.

Maren spotted Ethan first and widened her eyes. "You have some nerve! I kindly took you to Fire Island, and you left me there without a word. I waited all night in the cold!"

She had heard the full story from Carol and did not truly blame them. The complaint was meant as a joke to ease the awkwardness. Her voice was hoarse, though, and she sounded congested. Ethan felt guilty.

"I'm sorry. The collector captured us, and we couldn't get word to you."

Misty and Tracey apologized too. Seeing their serious expressions, Maren realized her joke had misfired.

"It's all right," she said with a wave. "It's just a cold. I've taken medicine, and a few days' rest will see me right."

Maren sat down at the long table. Ethan finally turned his full attention to Sabrina. Her face and manner seemed normal. He knew her too well to assume that meant he could read how she felt.

"Sabrina..." He cleared his throat. "Are you all right?"

She understood what he meant and gave him an amused look. There was a new warmth in her eyes.

"I'm fine. The bed was hard, and I worked up a sweat last night, so I took a shower."

The others heard an odd exchange without making anything of it. Ethan was relieved she did not seem upset with him. They could talk properly in private later.

There was still something else on his mind. In the heat of the moment, he had given no thought to restraint or protection. Remembering it now made him uneasy.
