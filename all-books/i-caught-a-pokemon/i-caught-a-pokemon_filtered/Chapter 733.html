Chapter 733: Lying Low as the Stakes Rise

Once they had agreed on a plan, Ethan, Shelly, and Misty left the lakeshore and slipped up a small hill nearby. From there they could watch the lake from a safer position.

The view was excellent, and the hill stood well outside Team Aqua's working area. It was the best observation point on the island, somewhere they could conserve their strength and wait to see how events unfolded.

Team Aqua had patrols moving around the island to prevent interference. Shelly, however, had obtained their routes and numbers from her informant in advance. Knowing where to avoid them made the three much harder to find.

At the hilltop, Shelly hid half her body behind a tree and watched the lake through a monocular. Misty released Horsea. It went with Ethan's Gengar to gather information closer to the action, one traveling through the water and the other moving unseen through the ground.

Gengar could pass beneath the patrols without being seen. Horsea was common in the sea, so its presence around an island would attract little notice. Better still, Team Aqua and Team Magma were focused on Ethan and Shelly. Both teams knew what Pokemon those two owned, but had paid little attention to Misty. They did not know her team well enough to suspect Horsea was working for them.

Shelly and Misty had their tasks. Ethan leaned against a tree to save his strength. His part of the fighting would come later.

With trees and brush blocking his view of the lake, he could not watch it himself. He couldn't send another Pokemon to scout either. His gaze wanderedand came to rest on Shelly's back.

She had a slender, well-shaped figure. The cut of her outfit showed her lower back and the outer sides of her thighs. Her long black hair fell over her back, a little disheveled, and her lightly tanned skin lent her a wild beauty.

As if she had sensed him looking, Shelly lowered the monocular and turned sharply. Her eyes met his.

She gave him a cool, knowing smile. Even now, you're thinking about that? I thought you'd at least wait until we were alone. Planning to make a move on me already?

Ethan sighed. His eyes had landed on her almost by accident, yet she had caught him at once. Just his luck.

But what did she mean by waiting until they were alone? Had she already decided he had designs on her? Thankfully, Misty was too far away and busy with her own work to hear. Explaining this to her would have been difficult.

What are you talking about? Ethan looked away. I wasn't looking at you. I was looking at that dead piece of wood beside you. It's flowering even though the tree is dead. Strange, isn't it?

The change of subject was painfully obvious. Shelly glanced down at his trousers and smiled with unmistakable implication. She said nothing, but he felt she had said plenty. Ethan looked down too and instinctively shifted his stance.

His phone chimed, breaking the silence. He welcomed the interruption, took it out, and checked the message. Shelly snorted and turned back to the lake.

Or so he thought. She still had her back to him. Perhaps it was his imagination, but she seemed to arch it a little more now, her waist drawn in and her hips raised in a way that made her even more distracting.

Was she testing his patience? Did she know what she was inviting? Ethan shook his head and focused on the phone.

As expected, the message was from Courtney. Team Magma was nearby and would reach the island soon. They had arrived quickly.

Ethan put the phone away, went over to Shelly, and took the monocular from her hand. She rolled her eyes while he raised it toward the lake.

Archie and Matt had finished preparing. Team Aqua members spread out around a heap of rock. In Archie's hand was the Red Orb, the one blown away during their previous chaotic battle.

Fate had played a strange trick on both leaders. Archie wanted Kyogre, yet he stood before Groudon holding its Red Orb. Maxie wanted Groudon, but held the Blue Orb and had been following Kyogre instead. Each coveted the other's orb and ancient Pokemon. Neither dared abandon the one within reach and give the other an opening.

They're about to begin, Ethan said.

As if on cue, several excavators started digging into the rocks. Perhaps Groudon had not fallen fully asleep, or perhaps it had only been underground for a few days. Either way, Team Aqua soon woke it.

A violent tremor spread from beneath the island. Ethan felt it clearly on the hill, kilometers from its source. Even the sea grew rough. At the excavation site, Team Aqua members were thrown off their feet.

Then Groudon emerged. Magma rose first and spilled over the ground. Through that searing flow, the immense red Pokemon slowly climbed out of the earth.

Roar!

Its first cry carried a savage power Ethan could feel even from this distance. The Ultra Balls and Great Balls at his waist trembled faintly. His Pokemon sensed it too and were eager to act.

Not yet, he murmured to them. It isn't our turn.

When he looked back at the lake, Team Aqua was already fighting Groudon. Even without the monocular, he could tell how fierce the battle was. Archie and Matt led the attack while their grunts surged forward. Pokemon were knocked out by single hits, but the next wave kept coming.

They were in a hurry. They knew they had to secure Groudon before anyone interrupted. Its power was immense, but Team Aqua had numbers, and their Pokemon held some type advantage against a Ground-type opponent. Their attacks were having an effect.

At some point Misty had joined them. She watched anxiously. Could they actually catch Groudon?

That depends on how fast they are. Ethan tilted his chin toward the sea. Look. The third player has arrived.

A large ship of unusual design, red from end to end, was speeding toward the island. Ethan did not need to see an emblem to know it belonged to Team Magma. Its arrival drew Team Aqua's attention as well; their formation wavered for a moment. They had expected interference, perhaps, but not so soon.

Matt took a group toward the shore to meet them while Archie intensified the attack on Groudon. He held the Red Orb ready, apparently waiting for Groudon's strength to drop far enough for him to control it.

Ethan lowered the monocular. Both players are here. They'll clash soon. We can move a little closer.

Shelly and Misty nodded gravely and followed him toward the central lake. Gengar and Horsea met them along the way. Together they took cover in a dense thicket.

Archie and the remaining Team Aqua members were reaching a decisive moment against Groudon. Team Magma's ship had reached the island too. Ethan could make out Maxie and Tabitha standing near its bow.

Maxie stood with his hands behind his back and looked down at the battle, satisfaction flashing in his eyes.

Archie, stop wasting your strength! You're too late to catch Groudon. Kyogre is already mine. Hand over the orb while you still can!

Archie glanced back, his face dark, but gave no answer. He clenched his teeth and kept directing a fiercer assault on Groudon.

Maxie had no more patience for him. He waved a hand. A door opened at the front of his ship, and a Pokemon floated out, its eyes glowing red.

Kyogre! Misty whispered sharply from the thicket.

It was not as she remembered it. Kyogre moved on its own, but its red eyes made it plain that someone was controlling it. Archie had apparently thought Maxie was bluffing. Instead, his rival had gained control of Kyogre first.

Even Ethan was surprised. Courtney had not mentioned it. Perhaps her information was incomplete, or perhaps Team Magma had subdued Kyogre only moments ago. Either way, they controlled it now. The timing was earlier than Ethan expected, but he had prepared for the possibility. As long as Kyogre had not undergone Primal Reversion, matters were not yet at their worst.

Shelly was no longer content to wait. We should act, she said. They're both here now. They can't simply vanish. If Archie gains control of Groudon as well, this will be much harder to contain.

Ethan thought it over and nodded. Plans never quite survive events. But wait a little longer. Once the two sides are fighting each other, we move.

While they spoke, Maxie sent Kyogre over the island. Matt saw the Pokemon Team Aqua had dreamed of commanding in someone else's control and felt the pressure mount. He had to buy Archie enough time to take Groudon. Only then could they stand against Team Magma.

Matt led his grunts into Kyogre's path and sent out their Pokemon. They could not hold it. Kyogre surged forward, and its great body broke through them at once.

Damn it! Archie glared at Maxie, then turned back to assess Groudon.

After Shelly's betrayal in the Seafloor Cavern, he had reconsidered whether an orb could safely control an ancient Pokemon. Reports from his people had confirmed her concern: trying too soon might fail and drive the Pokemon into a frenzy, even turning it against its would-be master.

He had studied the problem and found what he believed was a safer method. First wear the Pokemon down to a certain threshold; then use the orb. That should give him a much better chance of controlling it.

Groudon was almost at that point. Almost. And now, at the worst possible moment, that arrogant man with glasses had come to interfere. Archie could hardly contain his anger.
