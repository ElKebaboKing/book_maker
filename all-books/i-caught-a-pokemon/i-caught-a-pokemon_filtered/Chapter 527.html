Chapter 527: The Thieves and the Museum

At the silver-haired woman's command, Ariados opened its mouth and attacked without hesitation.

The two were famous thieves, after all. Once they decided to act, they did so at once. Ash had never imagined that pulling off a little webbing and saying a few words would make them attack a stranger.

Caught off guard, he took Ariados's Psychic head-on and fell into the canal.

Ethan had no time to react. Even Pikachu could only watch as its Trainer was struck.

"What was that for?" Ash scrambled out of the water and shouted at the silver-haired woman. Soaked from head to toe, he looked miserable.

The thieves still seemed utterly unconcerned. Ash's anger rose. He clenched his teeth. "Fine. If that's how you want it, we won't hold back either! Pikachu, go!"

At Ash's signal, Pikachu charged headfirst into Ariados. The two Pokemon began fighting at once.

When the blonde woman's Espeon looked ready to join in, Ethan took out a Poke Ball and released Gardevoir.

The two Psychic-type Pokemon exchanged several bursts of power. Their attacks were invisible, but the force of the collisions could be felt in the air, which seemed to warp around them.

The blonde looked from one member of the group to another. "So you're all determined to interfere."

Erika, Misty, and Brock said nothing. They released their Pokemon too, showing Ethan and Ash where they stood.

The thieves' Pokemon were certainly strong compared with most Trainers' partners. Against Ethan and his friends, however, they were outmatched.

After a short fight, the women sensed it too. They exchanged a glance. The silver-haired woman started the speedboat and steered away, while the blonde gave a sharp whistle. Ariados and Espeon broke off their battles at once, leaped aboard, and vanished with the women down the canal.

"What's with them? Why'd they stop?" Ash was baffled by their sudden retreat. After being knocked into the water, he wanted an explanation, and their escape left him frustrated.

Then he turned to check on the girl and found that she, too, had disappeared.

"Huh? Where did she go? When did she leave?"

If Ariados's silk had not still been lying on the ground, he might have wondered whether he had imagined the whole thing.

At Ash's cry, Ethan and the others looked to where Latias had stood beside him. She was nowhere in sight.

Everyone had been focused on the battle. None of them had noticed when she slipped away.

"Maybe she's shy, so she left quietly," Ethan said with a shake of his head. Then he grinned at Ash. "Still, look at you. You've learned how to rescue a damsel in distress!"

"He really has," Misty said, spreading her hands with a theatrical sigh. "I never thought I'd see the day. Too bad she left without a word. She didn't seem all that grateful."

"Stop making things up!" Ash's face reddened as they teased him. "II didn't help her for that! She helped Pikachu, and when I saw Pokemon chasing a girl, how could I just stand there?"

"Of course," Misty said, giving him a sidelong look and waving a hand. "We understand. You don't have to explain."

Once Ash had wrung the water out of his clothes, the incident soon slipped from their minds. They left the canal and continued toward the city center, arriving at Altomare's largest square.

Their destination was Altomare Cathedral, which stood beside the square. It was both a church and the city's only museum. Inside were historical records and artifacts from Altomare's founding onward, as well as fossils on display.

There were many other visitors. When the group entered the main hall, its solemn atmosphere made them lower their voices.

Fine paintings hung on the walls. Set into the floor was a huge Aerodactyl fossil, fully embedded in the marble and protected so visitors could walk over it without causing damage.

"Amazing," Brock murmured. Then he looked down uneasily. "But is it really all right for us to walk on it?"

He had been speaking to himself, but an elderly voice answered from behind them.

"Don't worry. There are other Pokemon fossils farther inside as well, and they're all protected the same way. The surfaces have been treated, and a layer of clear tempered glass covers them. It may feel as though you're stepping on the fossils, but you can't harm them."

Ethan and the others turned. A short, slightly plump old man with a broad bald head and a thick white beard walked toward them.

He looked kindly and round-faced. A badge hung on his chest. Ethan read it: Lorenzo, Guide.

Ignoring their surprised looks, Lorenzo smiled. "This is your first visit to the city, isn't it? Come with me, then. I'll show you around."

He led them deeper into the cathedral. As he had said, they passed more fossils set into the floor, including one of a Kabutops. Each had been protected like the Aerodactyl fossil. Walking above them felt novel and gave the group a vivid sense of the ancient world.

Sunlight filtered through stained-glass windows and spread across the marble.

They reached a circular room. Before them stood a towering machine whose purpose was impossible to tell at a glance.

Colored light fell over Brock. For some reason, he suddenly spread his arms and cried out, "Basking in this sunlightcould it be a sign from the heavens that I'll meet some beautiful older girls in this city?"

Ethan walked past Brock and stood beneath the machine, looking up at it.

It was made entirely of steel and rose more than ten meters high. Four enormous supports surrounded a strange sphere at its center. The whole structure looked both peculiar and mysterious.
