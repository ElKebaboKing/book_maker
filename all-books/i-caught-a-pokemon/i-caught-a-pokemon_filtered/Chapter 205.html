Chapter 205: Setting Out for Fire Island

"Melody!"

From the next table, Carol gave her sister a helpless look. Melody had always been the darling of the family, and their parents spoiled her. As the older sister, Carol had little hope of keeping her in line.

Melody waved off Carol's objection and turned back to Ethan and Ash.

"Proving yourself isn't hard. There are three little islands nearby: Fire Island, Ice Island, and Lightning Island. Bring back one treasure from the shrine on each island, place all three on the altar here on Shamouti Island, and I'll play the flute of offering to the gods. That's the whole ceremony!"

"Where do we find the treasures?" Ash asked.

"In the shrines, of course!" When she saw him frown, Melody thought he was worried about reaching them. "Don't worry. There's a boat to take you to the islands, and the shrines even have stairs. It's easy."

Ethan exchanged glances with Tracey and the others. The islanders had plainly turned an old legend into an attraction for visitors. They must have placed the treasures in the shrines beforehand. It sounded like an easy task.

Ash was not afraid it would be difficult. He hesitated because he knew he was an ordinary person, not some commander from a prophecy. But Melody's reassurance made him feel as if she had underestimated him. He stood at once.

"Got it. I'll go and see for myself right now!"

Melody's closeness had something to do with his sudden enthusiasm. Looking at her face brought back the kiss she had given him when they first landed.

"There's no hurry," Melody said. "The festival lasts until tomorrow. You can go in the morning. Relax." She caught Ash by the arm and pushed him back into his seat.

Once Ash made up his mind, though, it was hard to change it. He pulled free and stood again.

"If something can be done today, it should be done today. That's how Ino, that's how this humble servant lives!"

Ethan, Misty, and Tracey stared at him. This humble servant? Even his choice of words was affected. Was this really the Ash who was usually so oblivious to romance? A pretty face had gone straight to his head.

His sudden conviction impressed Melody despite herself. It also won Maren over.

"All right, I'll support you!" Maren came over with a smile and tapped her chest. "I'll pilot the boat and take you to Fire Island. It's the closest one."

"Thank you!" Ash said. Then he looked at Ethan. "Are you coming too?"

"Sure." Ethan shrugged. He had nothing else to do here, and staying near Ash seemed the surest way to find himself in the middle of the story.

Sabrina and Misty wanted to come once they heard Ethan was going, but he stopped them.

"It isn't far, so we should be back soon. It's late, and there won't be much to see out there. Wait here and get comfortable."

They did not insist. Sabrina and Misty stayed in the banquet hall with Tracey.

Ethan picked up an apple and ate it in a few bites. Then he left with Ash and Maren for the harbor.

Instead of taking a boat from Shamouti Island, they set out in Maren's speedboat again. As she put it, another person's boat might be better in every way, but her own was the one she knew how to handle.

The engine roared, and the speedboat cut through the waves, racing south from the harbor.

A chill night wind raised goose bumps on their skin. Even within the reach of the boat's lights, the sea around them looked dark. Ethan glanced up. He could see neither moon nor stars; clouds had covered the sky again.

That isn't a good sign.

Before long, thunder sounded overhead. A bright flash of lightning split the sky, and rain began to pour. Heavy drops pelted the boat, driving Ethan and Ash into the cramped wheelhouse to stay dry.

At least the waves had not risen this time. They were spared the violent rocking and the danger of capsizing.

When the rain showed no sign of stopping, Ethan asked, "Miss Maren, how far is Fire Island?"

Maren thought for a moment. "The round trip should take about half an hour, if you're only counting travel time."

Half an hour? Ethan nodded. It was close, then.

Thump!

A faint sound came from the bow. The boat gave a small jolt, as though it had struck something and ridden straight over it.

"Did you hear that?" Ethan asked.

"Hear what?" Maren looked around. "All I heard was the rain."

"I didn't hear anything strange either," Ash said. "Are you sure you didn't imagine it?"

Had he? Neither of them had noticed anything, but Ethan was certain the boat had run over something. He thought he had even heard a few cries. He looked back into the darkness. He could see nothing where they had just passed.

I hope I was mistaken.

The speedboat continued south, leaving a long wake behind it.

Not long after it disappeared, a man, a woman, and a Meowth struggled onto a floating board. It was Team Rocket's trio, still following Ash. Their Magikarp submarine had been smashed to pieces.

Jessie glared after the departing boat and cursed. "Are they blind? They ran right over our submarine!"

James spat out a mouthful of seawater. "Does it matter? It was leaking already and we couldn't use it. If it's broken, it's broken."

"Idiot!" Jessie snapped. "Broken or not, we worked and saved for that submarine, then put it together ourselves! How are we supposed to get across the sea without it?"

"Will you two stop arguing?!" Meowth raked them with his claws. Then he narrowed his eyes and lowered his voice. "I think that was the twerp and his Pikachu on the boat. And the man called Ethan was standing beside him!"

Jessie and James, their faces scratched, stared at him. "Those two met up again? And they're traveling together? Does that mean something big is about to happen?"
