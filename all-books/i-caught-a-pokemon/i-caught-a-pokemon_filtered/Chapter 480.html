Chapter 480: Rising Water and Underwater Breathing

Ethan had taken only two steps forward when the current nearly knocked him down.

He had no choice but to abandon his pursuit of Dr. Namba. Turning to Erika, he said quickly, "Put on your mask and respirator!"

Fortunately, neither of them had discarded their equipment. They hurriedly put it on, and by the time they were ready, the water had already risen past their waists.

It was rising far too fast!

Watching the level climb, Ethan took out a Poke Ball and recalled Gardevoir, then released Dragonair.

They were already beneath the sea, so Gardevoir could no longer Teleport them away. Dragonair, on the other hand, could move freely underwater and would be far more useful in their present situation.

Only a dozen seconds passed before the water reached their chests. Moving became difficult, and the pressure against them made even breathing a struggle.

Unable to advance, they reached for anything they could hold to keep the current from sweeping them away.

The only handhold nearby was a pillar. Erika wrapped both arms around it first, leaving Ethan to hold onto the pillar and her at the same time. That kept him from being knocked down.

Whoosh, whoosh...

Thick torrents of seawater continued pouring into the room.

With the flood raging around them, they could not worry about the propriety of holding each other so closely. They pressed together and endured the current in silence.

They were so close that, through Erika's soaked clothes, Ethan could feel the warmth of her body!

Erika, in turn, could feel the breadth of Ethan's chest. Even in the middle of this danger, it unsettled her slightly.

Neither made any attempt to separate.

They both knew that if they let go, the current might hurl them against something hard and knock them unconscious.

Whoosh...

Water continued to rush in. Tables, chairs, and other objects were swept from adjoining rooms and hurtled toward them.

Dragonair placed itself in front of the pair and knocked the debris away with its tail before it could strike them.

The situation finally began to change when the room filled completely with seawater.

Ethan felt the force of the current weaken. Knowing their chance had come, he tapped Erika on the waist.

She understood and nodded. One behind the other, they began swimming back the way they had come.

They had walked these halls only minutes earlier, but now every corridor was flooded. The continuing current made each stroke harder and demanded far more strength.

They swam through a long passage and room after room. Erika led the way, and from behind, Ethan had a clear view of her figure and her long legs moving through the water.

Well... At least there's something pleasant to look at while we're escaping...

Even with that distracting view, swimming against the current made the journey seem exceptionally long.

By now the base had sunk, the person responsible had escaped, and their situation seemed incapable of becoming any worse. They both expected to continue swimming until they were safely outside.

Once Ethan adjusted to the effort, he even stole another glance at Erika's graceful figure ahead of him.

They were almost out when a pipe beside Erika burst as she rounded a corner. The blast sent a shock wave through the water and hurled her toward the opposite wall.

It happened too suddenly for even Dragonair to intervene.

Fortunately, Ethan reacted quickly. From where he swam, he was able to seize one of Erika's lower legs before she hit anything. The blast had knocked the respirator from her mouth, however, and the current carried it out of sight in an instant!

Glug, glug...

Bubbles escaped Erika's mouth. Although she could not dodge the shock wave, she managed to close her mouth in time to keep from choking on seawater.

Even so, the air she had been holding escaped at once!

Ethan did not hesitate. He swam to her, removed his own respirator, and handed it over. Erika immediately put it in her mouth and drew several deep breaths, finally regaining her composure.

Once she had enough air, she tried to return it. Ethan waved her off, indicating that he was fine for the moment.

Erika assumed he had taken one last deep breath before giving it to her, so she thought little of his refusal.

As they approached the exit, however, she began to realize something was wrong.

Several minutes had passed. How could Ethan still be holding his breath?

Could his lungs really hold that much air?

Puzzled, she offered him the respirator again. He shook his head and refused once more.

Worried that he was merely trying to act tough, Erika tapped him on the chest.

Her gesture made Ethan realize something was unusual too.

He knew his own body. He should not have been able to hold his breath for so long, yet he still felt no tightness in his chest!

He had gone without breathing far longer than usual. Focusing on the sensation, he could even feel a little oxygen in the seawater being absorbed through the skin around his throat and entering his windpipe!

The discovery immediately reminded him of the blessing he had received as the Sea Hero.

Could this be its second effect?

Curious to discover his limit, Ethan declined the respirator again and continued swimming with Erika toward the exit.

As soon as they emerged from the base, they encountered Misty's Corsola and Ash's Totodile.

After seeing the base sink, Ash and Misty had worried about Ethan and Erika and sent their Pokemon to look for them. The searchers had found them just outside.

Holding on to the Pokemon, Ethan and Erika quickly swam toward the surface with their help.

Then Ethan sensed something. Looking down, he saw an enormous dark shape sweep beneath them.

The view underwater was blurred, but he recognized Team Rocket's submarine immediately!

Dr. Namba and the researchers were probably aboard it.

Sigh. They got away after all...

The Team Rocket members who had fled through the surface exit had been a distraction. This was the real escape route!

Still, the virus research facility had been damaged. That counted for something. At least their trip had not been entirely in vain...
