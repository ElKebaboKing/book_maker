Chapter 605: Cheering for the Challenger

At the referee's signal, the Gym challenge began.

Geodude raised its enormous fists and came toward Eevee, looking eager for a fight.

Perhaps some magnetic force inside it was responsible. Though it had a round body and no legs, Geodude could move forward in little hops and even float briefly in the air. It was a strange sight.

As a Normal type, Eevee had no advantage against Geodude. In fact, Geodude's Rock typing halved the damage it took from Normal-type attacks, which was bad news for Eevee.

Geodude's hard body was both reliable armor and a source of considerable force when it used attacks such as Tackle.

With one Pokemon's attacks weakened and the other's blows strengthened, Eevee seemed to be at a clear disadvantage. Roxanne found Ethan's choice puzzling.

As a Gym Leader and Pokemon Academy teacher, she understood type matchups better than most Trainers. She would never willingly send out a Pokemon at such a disadvantage, and a two-time conference champion like Ethan should know better too.

Why, then, had he chosen Eevee? Was he trying to train it to battle through an unfavorable matchup?

Before she could think further, the two Pokemon engaged.

Geodude used Mega Punch. Each swing of its fist seemed to carry tremendous weight, cutting forcefully through the air.

Eevee was not suited to trading close-range blows, and Ethan had no intention of letting it do so. He used Eevee's speed and the rocky terrain to approach from different directions, keeping Geodude occupied while looking for an opening.

But Geodude's fists guarded it so thoroughly that Eevee could find no gap.

Roxanne's students watched without blinking. Both Pokemon were unevolved, and neither was using especially advanced moves. Still, this was a battle between a champion and a Gym Leader. Ethan's name alone made it worth their full attention.

As the Pokemon exchanged attacks, Ethan considered how to break through.

His gaze passed over the large boulders on the field, and an idea came to him.

Eevee, jump onto those rocks!

Eevee leaped up. Geodude immediately followed so it could keep attacking. The short delay gave Eevee time to prepare.

Use Sand Attack!

Eevee turned its back to Geodude and kicked off the rock with both hind legs. A spray of grit and chips rained down into Geodude's face.

Geodude raised its hands to shield its eyes. For a moment it could neither continue its attack nor see where Eevee had gone.

When the spray cleared and Geodude looked up, Eevee's large head was already rushing toward it. Eevee slammed into its body.

Geodude had been climbing toward the rock where Eevee stood and was still suspended in the air. Without firm footing, it was knocked straight toward the ground.

Before it could recover, Eevee turned and kicked backward with both hind legs. Its Double Kick struck Geodude twice.

Bang!

Geodude flew back and did not stop until it hit a boulder behind it.

Its toughness deserved praise. An ordinary unevolved Pokemon might already have been knocked unconscious, but Geodude's stony body gave it remarkable resistance to physical blows. It looked battered, yet it could still fight.

Good work! Roxanne called. Geodude, hang in there! Use Rollout!

Geodude shook its head to clear the dizziness, wrapped its arms around its body, and rolled toward Eevee.

Ethan studied it. Geodude had already used up much of its strength. Rather than have Eevee evade again, he decided to meet it head-on and settle the round.

Eevee, use Take Down!

Take Down could inflict heavy damage, but it also hurt the Pokemon using it.

Ethan would not have taken that risk at a critical moment. Eevee, however, had come through the battle almost unhurt and still had plenty of energy.

Geodude rolled faster and faster, gathering formidable force. Eevee was no less determined. It burst forward and threw itself at Geodude without holding back.

Before everyone's eyes, they collided head-on.

The impact boomed through the spacious battlefield. Both Pokemon stopped short, then fell away to opposite sides.

It had been a brutal collision.

Fortunately, Eevee suffered little beyond a dizzy head and some aches. It was first to rise and immediately took a fighting stance again.

Geodude rolled backward a dozen times.

Ethan thought the round was over, but to his surprise, Geodude shakily pushed itself up once more.

An ordinary Trainer's Pokemon would have been unable to continue after a hit like that. Roxanne's Geodude could clearly take an exceptional amount of punishment.

Some of its endurance might be due to its resistance to Eevee's Normal-type attacks, but even so, it looked close to falling.

At Ethan's command, Eevee used Swift. The stars struck Geodude, and this time it finally went down.

That was one tough Pokemon...

Ethan smiled helplessly. Without a favorable type matchup, Ground- and Rock-type Pokemon were hard to bring down. They could absorb punishment and stubbornly keep fighting.

In her Trainer's area, Roxanne smiled as she recalled Geodude.

Although her opponent had used an Eevee, the result did not surprise her. The Trainer mattered as much as the Pokemon in a battle, and Ethan belonged among the very best.

Even Roxanne's students, whose Gym had just lost a Pokemon, showed no disappointment. Instead, they whispered excitedly about Eevee's performance.

Neither side had done anything particularly dazzling, but watching Ethan battle up close thrilled them all the same.

It was like seeing your favorite athlete perform a simple warm-up in front of you. Even that could set your heart racing.

Some of the students had already switched their loyalties in spirit. They were actually happy to see the challenger win!
