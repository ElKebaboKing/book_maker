Chapter 307: The Unstoppable Black Light

The controlled Dragonite opened its mouth and attacked again. Sabrina stopped the blast just as she had the first.

Only then did Vicious take the woman in front of him seriously.

"Long black hair, that icy face... You're Sabrina, aren't you?" He looked her up and down and snorted. "I heard you and that Ethan Vale caused Team Rocket a great deal of trouble. You stopped Dragonite's Hyper Beam, so clearly you have some skill. But even with your unusual psychic power, how many times can you do it? You might stop the first attack and the second. Can you stop the tenth? Beside a Pokemon, a human is as fragile as a child!"

He was about to order another attack when footsteps sounded nearby. Ethan and Clair had arrived.

Before approaching, both had recalled their Pokemon to keep them away from the Dark Balls. Only Gardevoir remained visibly at Ethan's side, while Clair kept Charizard with her for protection.

Vicious glanced at them, crossed his arms, and regarded them coldly. He showed no sign of panic.

"Good. All the pests are here. Now I don't have to waste time hunting you down."

His voice was confident and cruel. He did not spare his defeated followers so much as a glance, as if their safety meant nothing to him.

Ethan walked unhurriedly to Sabrina's side and studied Vicious. Unlike ordinary Team Rocket members and executives, the man did not wear their usual uniform. His outfit was entirely his own.

A thick tuft of beard covered his chin, and his hair was tied in a short ponytail. A yellow mask hid most of his face, leaving only his nose and mouth visible. Ethan could not make out his features or judge his age, though his voice suggested a man in at least his thirties or forties.

He wore a black, sheer, stocking-like bodysuit, with a spiked black vest over it resembling something a biker gang member might wear. A red cape marked with an R hung behind him.

Altogether, it was a strange look, especially for a man of his age.

After taking it in, Ethan curled his lip in the sort of smug smile a domineering company president might wear.

What terrible taste. My transformation outfit and Gengar mask are leagues better. His disguise isn't even in the same class as mine!

After that brief moment of self-satisfaction, Ethan turned his mind to the fight.

Dragonite was plainly not itself. The Dark Ball had seized control of its mind, and it obeyed only Vicious. Lance could not bring himself to hurt his own Pokemon, and sending out another would only give Vicious another target to capture. He was caught between two bad choices.

There was one small piece of good news. Lance had trained Dragonite so well that its abilities were already near their peak. The Dark Ball had therefore added little to its strength, leaving its attacks within a range Sabrina could still withstand. The fact that she had blocked them proved as much.

Clair glared at Vicious. "What do you want in the Dragon Holy Land?"

"Isn't it obvious? The Pokemon here, of course!" Vicious pointed at Lance, Ethan, and Sabrina in turn. "Thanks to them and the International Police, most of Team Rocket's bases in Kanto have been destroyed. We've lost people and Pokemon, and we need fresh recruits."

Clair was furious. "So you came here? What a vile organization!"

"This is only one of our targets." Vicious took out several more Dark Balls and rolled them in his hand. "If you have any sense, stop wasting time and hand over your Pokemon."

"You can dream on!"

Vicious shrugged and gave an exaggerated sigh. "Then show me how you'll face the Pokemon that used to be yours. Come on. Struggle like insects for Lord Vicious to watch!"

It was an outrageous boast before an Elite Four member, a tournament champion, and two Gym Leaders. With the Dark Balls in his hands, though, he had reason to be confident.

At his gesture, two Dark Balls flew out together, aimed at Ethan's Gardevoir and Clair's Charizard. Black light surged from them toward both Pokemon.

Clair ordered Charizard to dodge. Ethan tried something different.

At his thought, Gardevoir raised both hands and pushed back with psychic power. A blue wave of energy met the black light.

It had no effect. The psychic force passed straight through the darkness. Neither disturbed the other in the slightest.

That's a problem... Ethan sighed. His idea had failed.

As the black light closed in, Gardevoir used Teleport and reappeared at a safe distance. Fortunately, the light did not track its target, and Gardevoir escaped.

The Dark Ball reminded Ethan of the strange black Poke Balls Mewtwo had used during their confrontation on New Island. The two kinds were similar in color and purpose, but their abilities differed.

Vicious's Dark Balls forcibly captured Pokemon and turned them evil. Mewtwo's black Poke Balls did not strengthen the Pokemon they caught, but they could track a target and even take a Pokemon directly from someone else's Poke Ball.

Each had an advantage. Mewtwo had escaped from Team Rocket, so Ethan suspected it had taken the technology behind its balls from them and modified it. If so, the two devices shared an origin. That would explain both their resemblance and how Vicious had come to possess Dark Balls.

Understanding that much did not give Ethan a way to stop Vicious. For now, he could guarantee only one thing: whatever happened, he would not bring out the three Legendary birds and turn this battle into a nightmare.

While Ethan and the others faced Vicious, Lance narrowed his eyes and thought rapidly. At last he addressed his companions.

"We can't keep dodging forever. Let's attack together and focus on the Dark Ball in his hand. If we break it, Dragonite should come back to its senses!"
