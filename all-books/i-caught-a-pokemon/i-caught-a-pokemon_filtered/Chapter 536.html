Chapter 536: A Massive Tsunami

The hall was quiet. Even Misty and Brock's faint footsteps were lost beneath the wind and rain outside.

They met no resistance. Feeling their way through the dark, they reached the room with the machine unhindered.

It was the only room still lit in the cathedral. They had been anxious all the way there, but found no fossil Pokemon standing guard. Peering inside, they saw the machine still running. Latios remained trapped in the iron rings beside it, his power continually drained away. He was plainly in pain.

Latias could not wait to save him. She rushed toward Latios before Misty or Brock could stop her. By the time they reacted, she had reached his side.

Her movement gave the two intruders away early, costing them their chance to take Oakley by surprise.

"What rats have crept in here?"

Oakley, the silver-haired thief operating the machine, snorted. She drew on the Soul Dew to revive several fossil Pokemon that had already been defeated, sending them against Misty and Brock. Under her control, the waterspout raging in the square also turned back toward the cathedral.

Ethan and the others outside noticed at once. Certain something had gone wrong with Misty and Brock's mission, they followed the waterspout toward the building.

Inside, Misty and Brock knew they had no time to lose. They released their Pokemon and ordered them to attack the huge machine beneath Oakley.

Ancient builders had made it entirely of steel, but it still proved vulnerable to Pokemon attacks.

After barely a dozen blows, the machine began to fail. It tilted badly, throwing Oakley off balance inside its controls.

"You little pests!" Oakley shouted. "Why do you keep interfering?"

She tried to draw on the Soul Dew again. Instead, the console inside the sphere flickered and spat sparks.

The situation spun out of control. The sphere carrying Oakley began rotating over and over. The rings holding Latios drained his power even faster, like a pump pulling up water, and he cried out in agony.

Latias grew frantic. She slammed into the rings again and again, ignoring the electric shocks that struck her.

Before she could free Latios, a flash of black light came from the sphere. Misty looked up. The once flawless Soul Dew had somehow turned dark purple. A deep violet glow pulsed inside it, ominous even from a distance.

"Oh no!" Misty remembered Lorenzo's warning. "The Soul Dew has been corrupted!"

When they first saw it in the Secret Garden, Lorenzo had explained that Latias and Latios had used the jewel to repel an ancient monster and that it stood for purity. If someone with evil intentions used it, the Soul Dew would become twisted and corrupted. It would lose its original power, and the island itself might disappear. Misty had thought it was only a story. Now the darkened jewel looked as though it might explode at any moment.

Bang!

With the machine's hold weakened, Latias finally broke through the rings and freed Latios. He was in dreadful shape, badly hurt and utterly drained.

Misty looked at Brock. "Latios is free. Let's get out of here!"

Brock nodded. Supporting Latios between them, they headed for the cathedral doors. Annie, the blonde thief, came toward them as they left the room.

She had no time to deal with them. She saw the machine still spinning behind them and ran toward it in alarm.

"Oakley! Are you all right?"

The groups passed with little more than a glance. Neither tried to stop the other.

Misty felt some relief as Annie hurried away. She and Brock helped Latios outside, where Ethan and the others were coming to meet them.

Bianca brightened when she saw Latios. "You saved him?"

Misty nodded. Then she noticed how strangely quiet it was. "Wait. Where's the waterspout?"

"We don't know," Ash said. "It suddenly vanished."

The word reminded Misty of the jewel. "The Soul Dew looked corrupted. It turned black!"

"That's terrible!" Lorenzo's face changed. "Once the Soul Dew is corrupted, it will disappearand trigger a tsunami!"

Ethan frowned. "Can it be purified?"

Lorenzo shook his head. "The Soul Dew was formed from the souls of Latios and Latias. It is utterly pure. Once corrupted, it cannot be restored."

"Look around," Erika said suddenly. "Something else is wrong."

At her urging, they looked about. The rain had stopped, and the water in the surrounding canals was slowly receding.

It drained quickly. Small boats that had been afloat settled onto the exposed canal beds. Altomare was an island in the middle of the sea; even if the canals emptied, seawater should flow back into them. It should not all be withdrawing like this.

Lorenzo seemed to realize what it meant and hurried toward the shore beside the square. Ethan and the others followed his gaze. The sea beyond the city was pulling away too, as though an enormous tide were going out.

Broad stretches of reef and seabed appeared around the entire island. Countless shells and Pokemon were stranded by the sudden retreat of the water. Farther out, the ruins of a castle emerged.

The sea had covered those crumbling ruins for centuries. Only now could anyone see that such a building had once stood near the island.

Lorenzo watched the receding sea, a pale line moving steadily away. "This is bad. A tsunami is cominga massive one unlike anything we've ever seen."

"A massive tsunami?" Brock, who came from inland, did not entirely understand. "You mean there'll be a huge wave?"

"Yes." Lorenzo's face was full of worry. "The sea has never retreated this far. Even a conservative estimate puts the wave at a hundred meters high when it comes back."

"A hundred meters?"

They looked at one another. None of them thought Lorenzo was exaggerating.

"Grandpa..." Bianca's voice trembled as she asked what they were all thinking. "Can the city survive a wave that big?"

"No." Lorenzo sighed, his face ashen. "The whole city would be submerged. The people here would probably die as well..."
