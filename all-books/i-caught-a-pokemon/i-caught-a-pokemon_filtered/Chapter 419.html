Chapter 419: Turning Back to Help

After the initial chaos passed, Team Rocket's members sent out their Pokemon and began fighting back under the direction of their squad leaders.

The two sides clashed. As the finest unit in the International Police, however, these officers naturally possessed Pokemon far stronger than those of ordinary policeand far beyond anything Team Rocket's low-ranking grunts could muster.

Almost as soon as contact was made, the officers broke through Team Rocket's hastily assembled defensive line. They drove forward without pause until they reached the base itself.

Gengar led them to a hidden entrance. At Anabel's command, the Growlithe joined forces and breathed flames against the steel door, melting it with the intense heat.

Ethan and Erika watched the officers file inside but did not follow them. They needed to keep watch for the helicopter. Anyone who tried to escape aboard it would certainly be an important figure, quite possibly Professor Sebastian himself. Their task was to prevent anyone from fleeing!

Besides, there was no need for Ethan to involve himself in what came next.

The International Police officers who had come here were all elites. They were more than sufficient. If they still could not capture this base, Ethan would have to question their professionalism.

"Report! We encountered resistance in the corridor... The threat has been neutralized..."

"Continue deeper. Maintain coordination between teams!"

"Team C reporting. Team Rocket members discovered inside a room... Bzzzt... Successfully subdued!"

Anabel's commands and her officers' reports continued to emerge from the radio. Ethan could even hear explosions, the roars of Pokemon, and the screams of Team Rocket members.

The mingled sounds gave Ethan a rough understanding of what was happening inside, even though he remained outside the base.

Overall, the operation proceeded with surprising ease. The officers encountered no meaningful resistance.

With the map guiding them, they soon advanced straight to their target and successfully captured Professor Sebastian. The ambitious Team Rocket scientist did not even have time to reach the helicopter before they cornered him in the laboratory!

The fighting inside the base finally subsided, marking the raid as a resounding success!

Not long afterward, Professor Sebastian was escorted outside. Ethan once again saw the wicked scientist who had tried to use his research to alter Gyarados in Johto.

Leaning against the door of an armored vehicle, Ethan smiled at the defeated professor. "It's been a long time, Professor. Do you remember me?"

"You!?" Despite the officers restraining him, Professor Sebastian laboriously raised his head at the sound of Ethan's voice and recognized him immediately. "Hmph! I wouldn't forget you even if you were reduced to ashes. If you and Lance hadn't ruined Project R, I would have succeeded long ago!"

"You would never have succeeded." Anabel emerged from the base and stared coldly at Professor Sebastian. She refuted him in a grim voice, "Even if one or two experiments happened to work, evolution that violates the laws of nature is destined not to endure in this world!"

Professor Sebastian opened his mouth to argue, but before he could utter a sound, the officers forced him into the vehicle.

Anabel walked slowly to Ethan's side, looking relaxed.

She had been the first to lead her team into the base. Although Ethan had not witnessed her fight, the radio traffic had given him a fair idea of her impressive performance.

"The base has been taken. All that remains is the sweep and documentation." Anabel beamed. "I've already contacted Officer Jenny and asked her to bring in the local police. With their help, the remaining work should be finished quickly. I know I've said it several times already, but I have to say it again. Thank you so much!"

Unexpectedly, Ethan frowned and looked around after hearing her words.

"It's over?"

Sensing that something was wrong, Anabel asked in confusion, "What is it?"

Ethan rubbed his chin. "Two people seem to be missing."

Anabel's expression changed. "Who!?"

"The two I fought yesterday. One is Attila, and the other is Hun. They claimed to belong to Team Rocket's Pokemon Hunting Unit, and they're both very strong."

"What? I didn't encounter either of them..."

At that point, Anabel also realized something was wrong. She opened the vehicle door and confronted Professor Sebastian again.

"Heh, heh, heh..." Professor Sebastian had already overheard their conversation from inside the vehicle. When Anabel entered, he let out a strange laugh and taunted her. "You're too late! Those two already left with a squad. Even if you've captured me, Raikou will still fall into our hands!"

To his surprise, Ethan showed no trace of panic at the news.

In truth, Attila and Hun trying to capture Raikou again was entirely predictable.

Raikou had been within their grasp yesterday before someone rescued it. How could they possibly let the matter end there?

Ethan had simply never expected their groups to miss each other on the road to the base!

They must have taken different routes...

If Ethan's group had arrived a little earlier, they might have trapped both men inside the base!

Still, missing them was no disaster. Jimmy and Marina might be young, but neither was weak. With Eusine and Kudo there to help, capturing Raikou would not be so easy for Team Rocket this time.

Even so, Ethan really should head back and see what was happening.

Just as he was about to say goodbye to Anabel, his phone suddenly rang.

He took it out and looked at the name. Sure enough, Jimmy was calling!

The moment Ethan answered, Jimmy's urgent voice came through the phone.

"Something's wrong! Raikou just woke up, but it smashed its way out of the treatment room and escaped!"

"Have you encountered Team Rocket?"

"Team Rocket?" Jimmy sounded startled by the question. "No. We've been following Raikou and only just left the Pokemon Center!"

It seemed Team Rocket had not arrived there yet, though it was only a matter of time.

"Understood. I'm finished here and will meet up with you immediately. Be careful. I just learned that those two men from yesterday are approaching you with a squad!"

After ending the call, Ethan waved Charizard over, then turned toward Anabel.

"Miss Anabel, something has come up at the Pokemon Center. Please excuse me while I deal with it."

"Of course. We can handle everything that remains. I'm deeply grateful for all you've done for us!"

Ethan smiled. "Once the Silver Conference ends, I'll be heading to Hoenn for my next adventure. I'm sure we'll have more opportunities to work together!"

"That's wonderful!" Anabel shook his hand and thanked him solemnly. "With your help, I'm sure many more evil organizations like Team Rocket will be brought to justice in the future!"
