Chapter 286: Professor Elm

Suicune opened its mouth. Thick white mist rolled in from every side and covered them all.

It came so quickly and was so dense that Ethan could see less than a meter ahead. The others vanished from view. Only the faint outlines of Gardevoir and Gengar beside him kept him from panicking.

Both Pokemon drew close and took up protective positions, watching the fog for danger.

"Sabrina! Are you all right?" Ethan called toward where she had been standing.

"I'm fine," she answered steadily.

Her voice came from the expected direction. The mist had blocked their sight, but it had not confused their sense of where the others were.

Looker called from the other side. "I'm fine too, but I can't see Silver. There were a few flashes of red light just now!"

Red light? That usually meant someone was recalling Pokemon into their Poke Balls. Was Silver using the fog to escape?

Ethan nodded to Gengar. "Blow this mist away, quickly!"

"Got it!"

Gengar drew a deep breath and blew upward. A strange wind swept across the clearing, scattering the white mist.

As soon as they could see again, they looked toward the place where Silver had fallen. He was gone.

"Damn it! He escaped while we were blinded!" Looker's face fell. He searched the surrounding trees, but the forest was too dense to show which way Silver had gone.

For Looker, losing Silver meant his operation had failed. After all the effort of tracking him, they would leave empty-handed. The disappointment hit hard.

When they turned back to the lake, Suicune had disappeared as well.

"Why would Suicune help Silver?" Looker said bitterly, punching a tree.

Ethan shook his head. "I don't think it meant to help him. It wanted to stop the fight. It would probably have done the same no matter who was involved."

He addressed the Pokemon around them. "Don't just stand there. Spread out and search. Find Silver or Suicuneeither one."

Gardevoir and Gengar went in different directions. Sabrina's and Looker's Pokemon joined the search. More than half an hour later, they returned without finding a trace of either one.

"Forget it," Looker said with a sigh. "They're long gone. This operation was a complete failure."

"Not entirely." Ethan glanced at Sabrina. She still held the Poke Ball she had taken from Silver. "At least we recovered Professor Elm's Pokemon."

Looker paused, then nodded. That made him feel a little better.

The three recalled their Pokemon and retraced their way through the forest to New Bark Town. It was past eleven at night. The town was silent, its streets empty.

Professor Elm's laboratory looked peaceful as ever. Apparently no one inside had discovered the break-in.

Looker yawned. "The professor must be asleep by now. There's no rush. We can return the Pokemon in the morning. Let's find somewhere to rest."

Ethan agreed. They chose the Pokemon Center near the laboratory instead of walking farther to a hotel.

Nurse Joy had been about to sleep. She came out to greet them in pajamas, with a face mask on.

"Sorry to bother you so late," Ethan said. "Were you already in bed?"

Seeing her dressed like that, he wished they had gone on to a hotel.

"It's all right," Nurse Joy said with a wave. "Pokemon Centers are here for Trainers. Plenty arrive late at night. I'm used to it."

She arranged two dormitory rooms for them. Staying at a Pokemon Center was convenient and free, but the rooms were separated by gender. Ethan had no choice but to share with Looker while Sabrina slept elsewhere.

Since Ethan and Sabrina had begun sleeping together, they had not spent a night apart. He had grown used to her presence beside him. Lying alone now felt strange.

One hour apart from Sabrina. I miss her.

One hour and five minutes apart. I still miss her.

After the lights went out, Looker talked with him for a long while. They moved from Pokemon to work, then from work to battles. Looker shared stories from his time with the International Police. Ethan learned things he had never heard before, and the conversation brought them closer.

He still slept poorly. The space beside him felt empty all night. When morning finally came and he saw Sabrina again, the hollow feeling vanished.

The three ate breakfast at the Pokemon Center, then visited Professor Elm.

By daylight, the laboratory was much livelier. Researchers moved busily through its halls. They found Elm in a workroom.

He looked about twenty-seven or twenty-eight. He wore a white lab coat and semicircular glasses, and his unusual hairstyle stuck out like a sea urchin.

They introduced themselves. Only after they explained what had happened did Elm realize that a Pokemon had been stolen from his own laboratory.

Ethan handed him the Poke Ball. Elm looked at it and sighed. "Totodile's Poke Ball. What kind of professor am I? I didn't even know a Pokemon was missing."

"Don't be too hard on yourself," Looker said with a smile. "You've been working so hard that you hardly stop to eat or rest. New Bark Town is usually safe, and this thief had clearly studied the laboratory and your routines. You had no reason to be on guard."

Despite the reassurance, Elm still looked troubled. "It was my responsibility, no matter how it happened. Thank you for stepping in. If you hadn't, the children starting their journeys from here in the next few days would have been disappointed."
