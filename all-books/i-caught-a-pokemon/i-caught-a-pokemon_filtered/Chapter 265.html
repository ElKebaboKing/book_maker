Chapter 265: Friends Tune In

In the competitors' lounge, Carl explained the remaining procedures to Ethan: which entrance to use, what to do after a victory, where to leave in defeat, and the other rules around the match.

When he finished, he looked out the window. "To be honest, the Winner's Cup always draws an enthusiastic crowd, but today is exceptional. Every seat is taken, and people without tickets are still waiting outside the stadium. In all my years working here, I've never seen anything like it."

"Really?" Ethan smiled. "Then the Winner's Cup and the Orange League must be growing more popular."

"No." Carl gave him a long look. "Attendance has usually stayed much the same from match to match. This is different. I think we have you to thank, Mr. Vale."

"Me? I haven't done anything. You're the ones who publicized it so heavily."

Carl sighed. He knew that Ethan's fame was the real draw, but did not press the point. He checked the time and moved toward the door. "There's still over an hour before the match. We've set out food and water. Please rest here. I'll leave you in peace."

After Carl left, only Ethan and Sabrina remained. Ethan closed the door and looked around. White walls and a polished floor made the room clean and orderly. It had a large screen, a tactics board, and a tablet computer, along with a massage chair, refrigerator, and air conditioning. The equipment made it a serious preparation space; the comforts softened it.

Even through the thick walls, he could hear the low roar of tens of thousands of spectators. Soon he would walk into their sight and compete in one of the world's most popular and exciting sports. He had experienced big matches before, but the thought still stirred his blood.

Unexpectedly, arriving here had also quieted his nerves. Distractions fell away, leaving him focused. Ethan smiled at himself. Perhaps he was the sort of Trainer who came alive at a major event.

He released several Pokemon from their Poke Balls so they could adjust to the room and the tension before the match. Then he reclined on the sofa and shut his eyes.

He had gone to sleep late and risen early. Both his energy and stamina were low. He needed to rest before the fight. Sabrina did not disturb him. She propped her cheek on one hand and watched him quietly as the minutes passed.

Meanwhile, on Shamouti Island in the central Orange Islands, Melody sat with Carol, the town mayor, and other islanders in a banquet hall. Their television showed the live broadcast from Pummelo Stadium. The same two presenters from the previous day were giving their final preview, with the bustling stadium behind them.

Melody sat cross-legged on her chair. As the start time drew closer, she called toward the kitchen. "Carol, hurry! It's almost starting!"

"Coming!" Carol emerged carrying several platters of fruit. She set one before her sister and smiled fondly. "Look at the clock, Melody. There's still plenty of time."

Melody grinned, plucked a grape, and popped it into her mouth. "Who do you think will win?" she asked around it.

"You mean, who do you want to win?" Carol asked, giving her a knowing look.

"Oh, stop teasing me." Melody was unusually flustered. "Of course I hope Ethan wins. He saved the world and helped us so much."

"Is that the only reason? It couldn't be because you like him?" Carol said. Then she grew thoughtful. "I don't know much about Pokemon battles, but I've heard Drake is formidable. He's beaten dozens of challengers since becoming Supreme Gym Leader."

Melody knew little about battling herself and had never paid much attention to tournaments. That did nothing to shake her faith in Ethan.

"So what?" She lifted her chin. "I know Ethan can beat him, whoever he is."

The mayor shook his head. "It won't be easy. Drake is called the strongest Trainer in the Orange League. Some say he could win a championship if he challenged a major League. Ethan faces a serious opponent."

Melody's expression fell. Realizing what he had done, the mayor hastily added, "But..."

"But what?" Melody perked up.

"Ethan is strong too. He stopped Lugia and won the Indigo League. Drake won't defeat him easily."

"Exactly!" Melody brightened. "If he sends out the three legendary birds, no one could beat him!"

"I doubt he'll use them," Carol said. "I haven't known Ethan long, but I don't think he'd reveal those three in a match like this. They'd draw too much attention and bring trouble."

Melody thought for a moment, then sighed. "You're probably right."

At the same time, another group was gathering around a television in Vermilion City, far away in Kanto. At Cerise Laboratory, Professor Cerise and his family sat with his assistants, Ren and Parker, to watch the broadcast.

Chloe had learned about Ethan's match the night before and told her parents at once. The professor's workload happened to be light, so he gave his assistants half a day off and invited them to watch with the family.

Elsewhere, Ethan's most devoted supporters, the biker gang, had gathered in a newly opened clubhouse. They were following the match too.
