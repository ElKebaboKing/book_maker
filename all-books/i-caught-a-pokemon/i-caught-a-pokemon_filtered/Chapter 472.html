Chapter 472: Lugia, Mother and Child

The arrest operation soon entered its final stage.

As more and more Team Rocket members were captured, only a few stubborn holdouts remained across the beach. Unable to escape, they continued their desperate resistance, but their struggle was destined to fail. The officers subdued them almost instantly.

The officers confiscated the defeated criminals' Pokemon, then bound them together in small groups with rope and escorted them into the helicopters.

While the operation proceeded in an orderly manner, Ethan led Ash and the others to Anabel, who was directing the officers, and told her about their next plan.

"You intend to raid Team Rocket's secret base?" Anabel did not immediately agree. Instead, she first voiced her concerns. "I'm sorry, but we don't have enough personnel to lead another operation. Our highest priority should be taking these captured Team Rocket members back to the station. The round trip will consume a great deal of time, so we truly can't clear out their base as well..."

Unlike Looker's steady but daring approach, Anabel's higher position had made her more cautious. She clearly would not rush into an operation without adequate preparation.

"That's all right." Ethan shook his head. "We plan to handle this ourselves. We may need you to take care of the aftermath when we're finished, though."

Anabel knew how strong Ethan was and how many people were helping him, but the plan still left her uneasy.

"Team Rocket's base must be hidden somewhere extremely well concealed, and it will certainly be heavily guarded. Wouldn't it be too dangerous for only the few of you to go...?"

Ethan glanced at his companions and replied with a relaxed expression, "We should be fine. We aren't going there to fight them head-on. If the situation becomes too difficult, we'll simply escape. Besides, Team Rocket attacked us first. We're only returning the favor!"

Seeing that he had made up his mind, Anabel considered the matter briefly and could only agree.

"Very well. I trust your strength, and I know you wouldn't attempt something you had no confidence in. I can't join your operation, but I'll arrange for several boats to support you as quickly as possible!"

Ethan did not particularly mind that the International Police would not participate.

In fact, this suited him perfectly. He, Ash, and the others would be enough for the operation. Quality mattered more than quantity, and unless they encountered a truly massive confrontation, he would rather not have the International Police accompanying them. Their presence would only restrict what he could do.

His only reason for telling Anabel had been to ensure she could handle the cleanup after they destroyed the Team Rocket base.

Before long, every Team Rocket member on the island had been arrested. Ethan's group waved as Anabel departed aboard a helicopter.

The roar of the rotors faded beyond the horizon, and the entire island became completely quiet.

Once all the outsiders were gone, Oliver dove into the water again. He soon returned with Silver.

Everyone sat together as they waited for Poliwhirl to return. They laughed and chatted cheerfully about how each of them had performed in the recent battle.

Every participant, including Oliver, had done well. Sharing such an experience had also brought Richie and Oliver closer to Ethan's group.

Now that the two knew everyone better, they clearly had more to say. Oliver in particular was no longer so reserved around them and had begun to regard them as true friends.

Looking at the battle scars covering the beach, Richie and Oliver could not help reflecting upon what had happened.

It was the first time either of them had participated in a battle on such a massive scale.

That was understandable for Oliver. He was still young, was not a Trainer, and had spent his entire life on Ogi Isle. Naturally, he had no experience fighting other people.

Richie, on the other hand, had traveled for a long time. He had competed in the Indigo League and faced many powerful opponents, so he had certainly seen something of the world. Even so, this truly was his first chaotic group battle. It made his blood race and left him profoundly shaken!

So these people regularly encounter situations like this, and they even have connections with the International Police...

Seeing how accustomed everyone else appeared only strengthened Richie's determination to stop pursuing League Conferences for now and continue traveling to hone his skills!

As the conversation continued, the subject eventually shifted to Silver.

Silver had become the center of attention. Ash, Misty, and the others all watched it, while their Pokemon surrounded the young Lugia and occasionally reached out to touch it as though observing some rare animal.

In truth, Lugia was an exceptionally rare Pokemon. For ordinary people, the chance to observe one at such close range was a scene beyond anything they could imagine in an entire lifetime!

As Oliver looked at Silver, he suddenly remembered something. He slapped his forehead and exclaimed, "Oh no! I almost forgot! Silver's mother should be coming to pick it up soon!"

"Its mother!?"

Misty's eyes widened. "You mean a real Lugia... I mean, an adult God of the Sea?"

Oliver nodded and rose to his feet. He urgently told the group, "You should hide somewhere. Its mother really dislikes humans. If she sees all of you gathered here, she might attack!"

"Huh? Is she really that bad-tempered?"

Ash scratched his head. Although his expression showed little concern, he obediently stood and prepared to hide with everyone else.

Before the group could move aside, the surface near the shore suddenly exploded. Amid the surging spray, an enormous silver-white figure rose from the sea!

Its appearance was nearly identical to Silver's, allowing everyone to recognize it at oncethe God of the Sea, Lugia!

Compared with the young Silver, the adult Lugia radiated overwhelming pressure!

It spread its arms and looked down upon the humans before it, making no effort to conceal the hostility in its eyes!

At the sight, Oliver hurriedly placed himself between Lugia and Ethan's group.

"God of the Sea! Please don't misunderstand! They're all good people. They just saved Silver!"

Silver also called to its mother several times. Even so, Lugia remained unmoved, and its gaze toward Ethan's group stayed filled with vigilance!

Ethan had initially intended to explain that someone had entrusted him with protecting them.

On second thought, however, even Maya and the other priests of the Temple of the Sea were probably no different from ordinary people in Lugia's eyes.

With that realization, he simply remained silent. He motioned for everyone around him to withdraw slowly and leave the beach to the two Lugia.

Sure enough, their actions caused Lugia's expression to ease slightly, and the tense atmosphere immediately vanished!
