Chapter 722: Too Bad I Wasn't There

Someone caught it? Sid exploded at the impossible news. How? Who could beat a monster like that?

After facing Deoxys himself, he understood the gulf between them. His Pokemon were like fireflies beside the moon. The difference was crushing. Other than Rayquaza, he could not imagine any Pokemon that might match it. Could a Trainer truly catch such a creature?

He would rather believe his Poke Ball was broken than accept Rafe's explanation. Yes, the ball had to be faulty.

Ash, meanwhile, thought of Ethan at once. In his limited experience, an Elite Four member like Lance might be able to defeat a Pokemon that strong. But if he had to name someone who could catch it, his first thought was Ethan. It could only be Ethan.

Ethan's powerful team and the many Legendary Pokemon he owned had left a deep impression on Ash. He alone seemed capable of capturing Deoxys. Could this one belong to him?

Ash looked at his own defeated Pokemon, then across the river at the Trainers under attack from the copies. He hesitated. Ethan wouldn't order his Pokemon to attack us, would he?

While Ash and Sid stood stunned, Rafe recalled his Pokemon and strode toward the river. Sid came to his senses and hurriedly recalled his team as well.

Seeing Ash still standing there, Sid shouted back as he ran. What are you waiting for? Get out of here!

Huh? Right! Ash recalled his other Pokemon, ran to Pikachu, and picked up his unconscious partner. Then he raced to the riverbank and jumped in.

Splash! The cold water cleared his head. Pikachu stirred and opened its eyes.

Pika...

Ash felt the knot in his chest loosen. He swam with one arm, holding Pikachu above the water with the other. Pikachu, you're awake...

Pika... it answered weakly.

Ash looked at it with concern. Why did you push yourself so hard? Pikachu did not have the strength to answer. Its eyes slowly closed again.

Hold on. I'll get you to a Pokemon Center. Ash held it close and swam after Rafe and Sid toward the city.

All three were dispirited. Only minutes earlier they had crossed the river on Water-type Pokemon, glad that the other Trainers were tangled up with the copies. Now their whole teams had lost, and they had to swim back on their own. It was a miserable reversal.

After defeating them, Deoxys swept over their heads toward the Trainers still fighting on the water. At first Ash, Rafe, and Sid flinched, afraid it meant to finish them off. It did not even glance down. Being ignored brought a different kind of defeat: they had never mattered to it.

The remaining Trainers had not seen the battle on the far bank. The copies kept them occupied, and they had no idea how badly the three boys had lost. Only when Deoxys appeared among them did someone finally notice it.

Fear was not their first reaction. They were delighted. They had struggled for so long to get past the copies and reach Rayquaza or either Deoxys. Now one of their targets had come to them.

Before they could act, Deoxys attacked.

Its strange voice sounded again. It brought both hands to the core in its chest and sent a tremendous beam of energy across everything around it.

Every Pokemon caught by the blast fell immediately. Those in the air and those above the water alike dropped into the river. In one attack, Deoxys had brought down more than half its opponents.

The Trainers stared as though the scene had stopped. Some did not even react when their own Pokemon hit the water.

Then a woman's scream broke the silence. Pelipper!

Her Pelipper had taken the blast without a chance to move and plunged into the river. It had taken her several seconds to grasp what she had seen. Her cry seemed to restart the world around them.

What kind of monster is that?

How can it be so strong?

Run!

The Trainers fell into chaos. Some jumped into the river after their Pokemon. Many more instinctively fled from Deoxys.

Misty's group watched from a safer stretch of bank. Apart from Ash, the others stood with wide eyes, worried for the people caught in the attack.

So that's Deoxys's power, Brock murmured. One attack beat so many Pokemon. He was so startled that even his usually narrow eyes opened wide.

Misty alone seemed unsurprised.

May glanced across the river and whispered in her ear, Will Ash and the others be all right?

They'll be fine. Misty checked that Brock and the others were too absorbed in the fight to hear them. Ethan knows what he's doing. He won't let this get out of hand. You saw it yourselfDeoxys had plenty of chances to hurt those Trainers badly and held back.

As they spoke, Deoxys attacked a second time. The remaining Trainers broke. It was less a retreat than a rout.

They had forgotten every thought of catching a Legendary Pokemon. After a single real exchange, they fled toward the city bank faster than they had come, as though wishing for another pair of legs.

Within moments, they were all ashore. Feeling safe for the moment, however, they stopped there to watch what happened next. They still had not learned their lesson.

Ethan had no patience for that. He gave Deoxys another order through telepathy. As it approached the bank, the Trainers scattered again and fled back into LaRousse. Even Misty's group withdrew to a safer position.

With the intruders gone, Ethan could give Rayquaza his full attention.

High above the river, the wild Deoxys and Rayquaza were still locked in a close fight. Both had taken some damage, but neither had a decisive advantage.

Once Ethan's Deoxys rejoined the battle, there was little doubt about the outcome. The two worked together until Rayquaza roared in anger, shot upward, and vanished into the clouds.

At last it was over. Ethan relaxed in the forest. He had only directed Deoxys through a few maneuvers, but the choices had taken a toll on his concentration. For now, the crisis had passed.

...

That night, Ethan entered the LaRousse Pokemon Center and found its dining room unexpectedly full.

Trainers sat eating in silence. Their troubled faces and occasional sighs betrayed their thoughts. For a room with so many people, the quiet was strange. It felt more like a library than a dining room.

Behind the counter, Nurse Joy hurried back and forth with treatment equipment.

Ethan spotted Ash's group in a corner, with Rafe and his companions at the next table. Ash's table was more at ease than the others, though they too spoke softly in deference to the mood.

Misty saw him and waved him over. He sat beside her.

Ethan, where did you go? Max asked at once.

For some reason, Max had started calling him by the same familiar form of address as Misty and Ash. He was the youngest of them, so Ethan thought nothing of it. The question drew every eye at the table, especially Ash's. Ash seemed to be testing a suspicion.

Where else? Ethan shrugged. I heard the evacuation order and went to a shelter with the people nearby. I only just got back.

Ash looked surprised. Simple as he was, he took the answer at face value. So whose Deoxys had that been?

Brock was less certain. Ethan's face gave nothing away, so he looked at Misty and May. Neither showed any sign of doubt either. That was odd. Did they know something?

Max pushed up his glasses and spoke as though Ethan had missed out terribly. That's too bad! Do you know what we saw by the river while you were hiding? Rayquaza and two Pokemon we'd never seen before! They were so strong. They beat Ash and...

Max, stop, May said quietly. Can't you read the room? Look at everyone's faces.

Max was clever, but still young and not always sensitive to other people's feelings. He finally noticed how grim the nearby Trainers looked and fell silent.

Ethan, however, had no such hesitation. He rubbed salt into the wound.

Oh, that fight! he said with a smile. I heard about it. The local television station broadcast it live, so the whole city knows why we evacuated. Everyone saw what happened.

He turned to Ash, still smiling. You lost badly. Too bad I wasn't there. If I had been, I definitely could have beaten that Pokemon!
