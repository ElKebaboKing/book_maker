Chapter 500: Challenging the Azalea Gym

That afternoon, Ethan and Erika walked out of the gate and left Charicific Valley. Another day and night of travel brought them back to Azalea Town.

With Charizard staying behind, Ethan now had ten Pokemon with him: Gardevoir, Gengar, Crystal Steelix, Jolteon, Dragonite, Scyther, Pupitar, Suicune, Raikou, and the Eevee he had received from the Kimono Sisters.

Two open places gave him room to catch more Pokemon in the future.

That did not mean he would catch any Pokemon he came across. He would rather leave those places empty until he found ones he truly liked.

After the long but enjoyable journey, they stood outside the Azalea Gym again on the morning of the third day.

Fortunately, the notice was gone and the doors were open. Bugsy had clearly returned.

Peering inside the round building, they saw something like a botanical garden. Tall trees rose everywhere, with fragrant grass underfoot and shrubs scattered throughout.

From the entrance, Ethan called, "Hello? Is anyone here?"

His voice echoed through the Gym, but no one answered for some time.

He exchanged a look with Erika, then stepped inside.

A narrow path ran through the grass. As they followed it deeper in, they looked around and found that the place was much like an ordinary forest.

To fit a small forest inside a building, the Azalea Gym must have been a huge undertaking. No wonder it stood outside town!

One difference was the number of Bug-type Pokemon hiding therefar more than in most forests.

In only a short walk, they saw a whole crowd of Caterpie, Weedle, Metapod, and Kakuna. The Pokemon hid among shrubs and behind leaves, then poked their heads out when they heard footsteps and watched the newcomers warily.

Erika sighed softly. "It really is a Bug-type Gym. They're everywhere."

The setting was much like the greenhouse at her own Gym, but its Pokemon were entirely different.

Ethan glanced at her and smiled. "As a Grass-type Gym Leader, you must feel uncomfortable surrounded by so many Bug-types."

He was not teasing without reason. Bug-type Pokemon were natural enemies of many Grass-types and had a type advantage over them. A dedicated Grass-type Trainer could hardly avoid feeling that tension.

Erika nodded slightly.

Misty was simply afraid of Bug-type Pokemon. Erika did not like them at all.

They walked a few dozen steps farther into the garden. Then a youthful voice sounded from the side.

"Who are you?"

Ethan and Erika turned. A purple-haired boy in a grass-green shirt and matching shorts was crouching in a thicket a short distance away, watching them.

Ethan stepped forward. "Are you Bugsy, the Gym Leader here?"

Bugsy brushed dirt from his hands and stood. Only then could Ethan and Erika see that he had been tending to a Metapod.

Bugsy looked Ethan over. "That's right! I'm Bugsy, the Azalea Gym Leader and a walking encyclopedia of Bug-type Pokemon!"

A walking encyclopedia?

Ethan hardly knew what to say about the title.

It sounded like one Bugsy had given himself. Did people really introduce themselves with their own nicknames?

"Are you here for a Gym challenge?" Bugsy asked.

Ethan nodded. "We came by a few days ago, but you were away, so we missed our chance."

"Oh? Sorry about that," Bugsy said, looking surprised. "I went somewhere farther away to catch new Pokemon, so I wasn't here these past few days."

Now that he knew why Ethan had come, Bugsy led them deeper into the forest. They passed dense shrubs and an indoor stream before reaching a battlefield among the trees.

Bushes surrounded it. Sunlight fell through the glass roof, and the scent of trees and flowers filled the air. The battlefield felt close to nature and had a character all its own.

Bugsy looked young, perhaps around Ash's age or a few years older. To lead a Gym at that age, he had to be capable.

He was, in fact, an exceptional young man. Quick-witted and knowledgeable, he studied Bug-type Pokemon and hoped to become a Pokemon professor. People across Johto knew him as a gifted youth!

Ethan and Bugsy took places on opposite sides of the field, while Erika sat on a bench.

A staff member came out of a nearby break room to referee.

Seeing both Trainers ready, the referee announced, "The Gym battle between Bugsy, Leader of the Azalea Gym, and the challenger will now begin! Each Trainer may use three Pokemon. The battle ends when all of one side's Pokemon are unable to battle!"

As the defending Gym Leader, Bugsy took out a Poke Ball first.

He threw it hard. A flash of blue light revealed a Pokemon that looked like a green spider.

Bugsy smiled. "My first Pokemonthe silent warrior of Bug-types, Spinarak!"

Spinarak was a Bug- and Poison-type Pokemon less than half a meter tall. Its head and abdomen were pale blue-green, with a white horn on its head and black eyes. It had three pairs of legs marked in yellow and dark blue-green. A pattern resembling a sorrowful face marked its back.

Spinarak were said to be extraordinarily patient. After setting traps with their thin, strong silk, they waited for prey. One could hold the same pose in its nest for days. Even when prey was caught in its web, it might remain motionless until nightfall before acting.

Its silk was sturdy enough to hold a ten-kilogram rock without breaking. Some fishers even wove the silk into nets to catch fish Pokemon!
