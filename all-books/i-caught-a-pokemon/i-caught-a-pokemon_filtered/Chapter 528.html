Chapter 528: Ash Follows the Girl

Lorenzo continued his explanation. "According to the island's legends, this cathedral and the Tower of the Sun outside were built by the people of old in gratitude to Latias and Latios for protecting the city. The device before you seems to have been made for the sake of peace as well. Unfortunately, it is so ancient that no one knows how it works anymore."

As Ethan and the others listened and examined the machine, Ash suddenly seemed to notice something. He looked up at the gallery on the second floor. A red-haired girl was drawing at a drawing board there. She looked just like the girl who had disappeared beside them earlier.

"It's her!"

At Ash's low exclamation, the others looked up and saw her too.

She had apparently just finished her drawing. She packed up her supplies, turned, and left with her board. For reasons he could not have explained, Ash followed her out.

"Ash!"

Brock called after him. When Ash did not respond, Brock sighed and went after him with Pikachu.

"What's gotten into him?" Misty turned to Ethan. "Should we follow too?"

"Let's go." Ethan nodded. "We know Ash. He can be impulsive, but he has a knack for stumbling into unusual things. Maybe he spotted something."

Even as he said it, Ethan wondered whether the red-haired girl who had left was really the same one they had met by the canalLatias in disguise.

He vaguely remembered that Latias had taken on the appearance of a girl before showing herself to people. And they had only just arrived at the cathedral, while this girl seemed to have spent a long time drawing upstairs.

Whatever the answer, following Ash seemed sensible.

Ethan, Erika, and Misty hurried through a side door, taking the route Brock had used to leave the cathedral.

Outside, they saw Brock trailing Ash beyond the cathedral grounds. They quickened their pace, crossed the square and a broad bridge, and followed them a long way through the city.

The red-haired girl walked at an unhurried pace. Ash nearly caught up with her several times, but he did not know the streets. Each time a canal blocked his path, he had to circle around to another route to keep her in sight.

Brock and the others, despite leaving later, managed to catch up with Ash first.

When the girl reached the opposite bank once more, Ash called across the water, "Please wait!"

She turned toward him. "Are you talking to me? What do you want?"

"Why did you leave without saying anything earlier? And who were those women?" Ash asked.

"What are you talking about?" She stared at him in confusion, then gave a cold laugh. "If this is some new way of trying to pick me up, you've chosen the wrong girl."

With that, she turned and walked away without another glance.

Misty covered her mouth, unable to resist teasing him. "Ash, I guess rescuing her didn't have quite the effect you hoped. Now she's got the wrong idea about you!"

"I wasn't trying to rescue her to impress her!"

Red-faced, Ash hurried onto the bridge and set off after the girl again.

This time she entered a more tangled residential district. Ash lost sight of her after only a moment's distraction.

Ethan and the others found him standing there, dejected. Misty caught her breath and asked, "Seriously, we ran all this way. Why are you so determined to find her?"

"I don't know..." Ash scratched his head. "She just gives me this special feeling. It's like... like..."

He tried several times but could not put the feeling into words.

Ethan glanced at him and smiled. "Like when you meet a Pokemon you really like?"

"Yes, exactly!" Ash's eyes lit up as he nodded. "That's it!"

"A Pokemon?" Misty burst out laughing. "So that's what liking a person feels like to you, Ashliking a Pokemon!"

By now, Ethan was almost certain that the girl who had just brushed Ash off was an ordinary human, not Latias.

The two looked alike, but there was one clear difference: this girl spoke, while the one they had met earlier had remained silent.

Of course, calling her ordinary was not entirely right. Even if Ethan could not remember the plot, Latias had chosen her appearance for a reason. The two must have some close connection.

With the girl gone, the group could only keep walking. Altomare had struck them as beautiful since their arrival, but its crossing canals, little bridges, and alleys made its streets a maze.

As they discussed where to go next, they spotted the red-haired girl watching them from a nearby bridge.

They were astonished. She had just shaken Ash off so impatiently, yet now she seemed to be waiting for them.

Ethan alone noticed that the look in her eyes was entirely different.

This must be Latias in the girl's form again. It was hard to explain such a change otherwise.

While they stood there in surprise, Latias waved to them, then slipped into an alley beside the bridge.

Ash looked at the others. "What does she mean by that?"

"You idiot," Misty said with a sigh. "Isn't it obvious? She wants us to follow her."

"But..."

Ash hesitated. The other girl's sharp words were still fresh in his mind, and this sudden invitation left him thoroughly confused.

"What are you waiting for?" Ethan lightly tapped Ash on the head to snap him out of it, then smiled. "You chased her all the way here when she told you to leave her alone. Now that she's asking you to follow, you're suddenly afraid?"

"I'm not afraid! Come on, let's go!"

The tap brought Ash back to himself. He stopped hesitating and hurried after Latias, Pikachu at his side.
