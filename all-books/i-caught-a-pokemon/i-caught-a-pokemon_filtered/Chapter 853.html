Chapter 853: What Is She Up to This Time?

Harley took out two Poke Balls and handed his Cacturne and Banette to Jessie. At the same time, Jessie and James handed Meowth over to him.

Not that they had to hand Meowth over. He walked up to Harley of his own accord, smiling.

Although the exchange had gone smoothly, Harley was not about to let his guard down. These people had tricked him before. To keep Meowth from slipping away, he found a length of rope, tied him up tightly, and held on to the other end.

With that done, both sides nodded in satisfaction.

I'll be looking forward to your performance tomorrow morning, Harley said.

Leave it to us! I promise I'll beat that little girl!

Jessie thumped her chest in assurance. Harley clearly had confidence in his Pokemon as well. He waved goodbye and led Meowth away on the rope without further hesitation.

Jessie and James watched him go. With two Poke Balls in hand, they looked at each other and burst out laughing.

We didn't do a thing, and he gave us two Pokemon, Jessie said with a wicked chuckle. Today really is our lucky day!

It sure is, James said. They're that fool's main Pokemon, too. I never thought getting them would be so easy. What a bargain! Oh, but what about Meowth?

Don't worry. We worked that out with him already. He'll find a chance to escape on his own!

Are we still entering the Contest?

Of course. Jessie spread her mouth in her familiar, enormous grin. With an opportunity like this, I'll use his Pokemon to win the Contest first. Then we'll run off with both of them and deliver them to the boss!

While Harley and Team Rocket carried out their bargain, Ethan and May had left the front of the Mulberry Contest Hall for a broad open space nearby. They had registered and inspected the stage. Now it was time for some focused practice.

Unfortunately, the open space was packed with Coordinators training alongside their Pokemon. There was no room for the two of them. They walked much farther before finding an unoccupied spot.

Perhaps the number of entrants had put pressure on May. This time she chose not to perform with a newer partner such as Eevee or Squirtle. Instead, she brought out Combusken. Her first Pokemon was still the one she trusted most.

Ethan found a chair and sat down to watch her practice. Before long, he shook his head helplessly.

Her nerves were getting the better of her. The crowded field and her encounter with Harley had unsettled her so much that she kept making mistakes while working with Combuskenmistakes she normally would not make.

If this was how she performed in private, what would happen in tomorrow's Contest?

Let's stop for a moment...

He beckoned May over. She knew perfectly well that her state of mind was the problem and gave him an embarrassed smile.

Relax. Don't let the other entrants get to you. Ethan could do little beyond helping her look at things differently. Think about it. If they were that strong, they'd already have five Ribbons, like Harley. The fact that they're entering this Contest means they haven't been able to win enough. And if you make it through the first round of judging, I don't think any of them can beat you in a battle.

Ethan knew how to comfort her. Ash and the others would probably have cheered May on, but he offered another way to see the field. His words made her pause and think.

He had a point. A few entrants, like her, might have missed earlier Contests for reasons of their own. Most of the others had entered those Contests and been eliminated. If they had lost before, why should she be afraid of them?

Once she understood that, May felt noticeably calmer. Even her hands and legs stopped shaking so much.

Thanks, Ethan. I'm feeling a lot better now.

She took a deep breath and returned to the grass with Combusken. This time their practice went smoothly. There were no more mistakes; she seemed to have found the form she had shown at earlier Contests.

...

They became so absorbed in practice that the hours slipped by. When May finally looked up, the sky had gone completely dark.

After a good night's rest at the Pokemon Center, they returned the next morning for the Mulberry Contest. Spectators poured into the hall from every direction, and Ethan joined the stream of people heading to the stands.

Lilian was hosting as usual, and the judges were the same three familiar faces. Barring a surprise, the Grand Festival might well use the same team. No one minded. The host and judges were skilled, respected, and popular. Seeing different people in their places might have felt strange.

Lilian's energetic introduction ended, and the Contest began. Only then did the full size of the field become clear. As Ethan had thought, the number of entrants rivaled a major city Contest, even if the hall itself was smaller.

Their skill varied just as widely. Not everyone had a realistic chance of winning. Some Coordinators traveled from Contest to Contest and could go a whole year without earning a single Ribbon. Yet the dream seemed close enough to reach, even as it kept slipping away, and countless people continued to enter.

Since this was the last Contest before the Grand Festival, the contrast was especially sharp. The best entrants showed real skill and earned high scores. At the other end, repeated mistakes made some performances almost comical.

One Coordinator after another stepped onstage and left again. Whatever their results, they brought the audience to life.

Then it was May's turn.

As in practice, Combusken was her partner for the appeal. Perhaps she had truly settled her nerves, because she performed even better than usual. Her stage presence had plainly reached another level.

During the disc-catching portion, Combusken caught every disc perfectly and showed off its coordination. Then it used Fire Spin to create a huge storm of flames on the stage, displaying the move's beauty as well as its power. For the finale, Combusken sprang up out of the fire and made a magnificent exit.

The hall erupted in applause. The audience was amazed by Combusken's dazzling performance and gave May their loudest cheers.

Harley, sitting in the stands, looked nothing like the excited spectators. His face had gone dark. He had never expected May to be in such good form today.

That appeal would have earned a high score even at the Grand Festival. Compared with the entrants before her, she had raised the standard by half a level.

Damn it! He clenched his fists.

How could this happen? Wasn't a setting like this supposed to make her nervous? Why had she performed better instead?

All he could do now was put his hopes in Jessie. Perhaps she could use his Pokemon for an even stronger appeal and outshine May.

Something else bothered him. When he had spoken with Team Rocket earlier, he had discussed tactics with Jessie and James. James had thumped his chest and promised to interfere during May's performance. Yet nothing had happened. Had they forgotten Harley was holding a hostage?

Harley frowned, unable to make sense of it.

In a warehouse not far from the hall, the answer was taking shape.

Meowth was tied hand and foot to a pillar. Harley's Ariados hung quietly in a web opposite him, keeping watch.

Ever since Harley had bound him and brought him here the day before, Meowth had been deciding when to escape. The timing mattered enormously to their plan.

If he escaped too early, Harley would notice and go looking for Jessie and James. They would never get away with Cacturne and Banette. If he waited too long, Harley would realize Jessie was not following his plan to target May and Ethan, and would come after them anyway.

Whether Meowth could escape was another question entirely. As the slyest and cleverest member of the trio, he thought Harley had badly underestimated him.

He kept a placid expression for Ariados's benefit. Behind his back, though, the claws on his bound paws extended to sharp points.

A burst of cheering came from the distant Contest Hall. Meowth knew his chance had arrived. He whistled toward the outside of the warehouse, and within moments there was a sound at the door.

Ariados climbed down from its web almost at once and watched the entrance warily. The door opened, and a blue, harmless-looking Wobbuffet poked half its body inside.

Wobbuffet!

It gave Ariados an apologetic smile, as vacant-looking as ever, and rubbed its smooth head. It made no other move.

Ariados was puzzled. Something felt wrong. As it prepared to drive this unexpected visitor away, it heard a sudden rush of air behind it.

It whipped around. The talking Meowth had somehow freed himself from the rope and was already right in front of it, claws raised.

Meowth's steps had been so light and his movements so careful that Ariados had noticed him too late. His claws knocked it down before it could react. He did not press the attack. The moment Ariados fell, he and Wobbuffet bolted from the warehouse.

By the time Ariados recovered and chased them outside, they had vanished.

Harley knew nothing of what had happened in the warehouse. He was still waiting to see what Jessie could do with his Pokemon.

After May's appeal, more than a dozen Coordinators took their turns. At last Jessie came onstage. Harley straightened in his seat and held his breath.

You've waited long enough! Welcome to the fabulous stage of the world's greatest idol, Jessibella!

Jessie waved grandly to the audience. Her butterfly mask, shawl, red gown, and white gloves completed her Jessibella costume and gave her the bearing of a queen.

Her appearance alone stood out from Coordinators who had come in their everyday clothes. The spectators' eyes lit up. Most had never heard of Jessibella, but looking at her now, they could almost believe she was a world-famous idol.

Harley himself had believed it the first time he saw her. The convincing costume and Jessie's boundless confidence had sold him on the act.

Only Ethan applauded mildly, his face blank amid the cheering crowd. He was not trying to make a point or look mysterious. Knowing who Jessie really was, he found her appearance here equal parts exasperating and absurd.

Ash was nowhere nearby. How had she turned up anyway? Did a Contest stage draw her more strongly than Pikachu, enough for her to stop chasing Ash for a while?

Regardless of how Jessie competed, she was bound to cause some sort of trouble before the end. The Contest had been unpredictable already; her arrival added another complication.

Even so, Ethan kept clapping. He had to admit Jessie had a talent for the stage.

What surprised him more was the Pokemon she released. It was not one of her own. It was Harley's Cacturne.

Ethan had no trouble recognizing it. He knew Jessie and Harley well enough, and with both of them here, it was hard not to suspect they were plotting something together. Jessie's goal was easy to guess: win this Contest and claim the final Ribbon.

Harley's goal could only be one personMay.
