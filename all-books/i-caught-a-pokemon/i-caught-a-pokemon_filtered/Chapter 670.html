Chapter 670: Norman and Caroline

The helicopter settled slowly onto the ground. As soon as its door opened, Kennedy jumped out first.

Mr. Vale... He stared at Ethan in amazement. You really did it!

From the helicopter, Kennedy had seen that no part of the valley was untouched by battle. He had not witnessed the fighting, but he could imagine what Ethan had faced.

Ethan merely nodded. It took a few unexpected turns, but I got the job done. He had no need to boast.

And Deoxys...? Kennedy asked carefully.

The fact that you landed here safely should tell you. I've caught Deoxys. It won't cause any more trouble. You can recover the meteorite from the lake.

Thank you so much! Kennedy bowed deeply to them both. Mr. Stone will be delighted. On behalf of Devon Corporation, I sincerely thank you for your help.

Kennedy offered to fly them back to Rustboro City, but Ethan declined. After giving him a few details about the site, he and Misty climbed onto Charizard and headed straight for Petalburg City.

Ethan had already spent too much time away from the Gym he had passed several times without managing to challenge. He did not want to delay again. A return trip to Rustboro could bring any number of distractions. Better to finish the Gym match first.

Rustboro had a strange way of drawing him back no matter how many times he left. He did not want to test that pull again. Nor did he want another ride in a helicopter; the noise had been more than enough.

Would Mr. Stone thank him in person or give him another material reward if he returned to Devon? After capturing Deoxys, Ethan no longer cared. Deoxys was the greatest prize he could have wanted.

Charizard spread its wings and carried them past the clouds. Fatigue finally caught up with Ethan and Misty, and they said little during the flight.

By the time they reached Petalburg City, it was too late to challenge the Gym. They went to the Pokemon Center and took rooms for the night. Almost all Ethan's Pokemon needed treatment, and he wanted to adjust his team before the Gym match.

It was not that his current team could not win. With Pokemon strong enough to compete for a championship, though, a Gym challenge would lose much of its point.

At the counter, Ethan handed all his Poke Balls to Nurse Joy. Her eyes widened when she saw them.

Th-these Pokemon...

She had worked at the Petalburg City Pokemon Center for years and treated countless Trainers' teams. Never had she seen a roster quite like this one.

Ethan put a finger to his lips. Please keep this quiet. If possible, could you treat my Pokemon in a separate room? Some of them shouldn't be seen by the public.

Nurse Joy recovered her composure. Of course. We protect our patients' privacy behind the scenes at every Pokemon Center. Your secrets are safe here.

Thank you.

He watched her wheel the Poke Balls into the treatment area. Then he and Misty went to their separate rooms, showered, changed, and met again in the Center's lobby.

To celebrate their victory, they decided to eat at an upscale restaurant rather than have dinner at the Center. Before that, they stopped at the city's largest shopping mall. Ethan had promised Misty a gift to thank her.

Misty took her time deciding. In the end, she chose a fairly expensive set of cosmetics. Ethan paid, and she walked ahead carrying several bags, delighted with her purchase.

He found himself reflecting that he might not have expected someone her age to care about such things. Then again, people liked to look their best at any age, especially around someone whose opinion mattered to them.

...

Early the next morning, Ethan ate breakfast, collected all his Poke Balls, and began exchanging his team.

He sent the red Gyarados, Blastoise, Venusaur, and Jolteon back to Cerise Park. Charizard returned to the Charicific Valley to continue its training.

He sent the three Legendary birds back to Sabrina. After a moment's thought, he sent Jigglypuff to her as well. He had no intention of traveling with that disobedient Pokemon. Sabrina could deal with it.

Ethan was curious which would prevail: Jigglypuff's sleep-inducing song or Sabrina's psychic power. Would Jigglypuff draw a ridiculous face on her, or would Sabrina tame it completely? Either result would be entertaining.

He then retrieved Dragonite, Scizor, Tyranitar, Eevee, Combusken, Metang, and Feebas from Cerise Park. These were the Pokemon he meant to train on his journey through Hoenn.

Once everything was settled, he and Misty headed for Petalburg Gym.

They followed the main road there without difficulty. It was only a little past eight in the morning, but the Gym doors were already open. Norman was ready for challengers. His dedication was a marked change from Brawly at Dewford Gym.

In most respects, this visit felt much like the last one. The greatest difference was that May and Max were gone, leaving the Gym unusually quiet.

They entered and walked toward the battlefield. In the distance, a middle-aged man was training his Pokemon. He appeared to be in his late thirties, lean, with thick eyebrows and alert eyes. Even standing still with his arms folded, he gave the impression of strength.

Neither Ethan nor Misty had met him before, but they knew who he was: Norman, May and Max's father and the Leader of Petalburg Gym.

Norman noticed them almost at once. Before Ethan could speak, he smiled. You've finally come. I've been waiting for you.

Huh? Misty looked puzzled. You know us?

Of course! Norman laughed. May and Max have talked about you several times. This is our first meeting, but they've told me plenty over the phone.

Oh, I see.

Norman turned to Ethan, rubbing his chin. You've come to challenge Petalburg Gym, haven't you?

That's right. Can we begin now?

Norman shook his head with a smile. No need to rush. You're May and Max's friends. Come have some tea first.

Tea? Ethan and Misty exchanged baffled looks.

Norman turned toward the rooms inside the Gym. Caroline! We have guestsMay and Max's friends!

Coming! a woman called back. A moment later she walked out.

Caroline looked to be in her thirties. She wore her long reddish-orange hair in two sharp, zigzagging ponytails. She was pretty, fair-skinned, and fashionably dressed, with pearl earrings. She looked more like a stylish woman from the city than someone Ethan had expected to find managing a household.

She was Norman's wife and May and Max's mother. Ethan could see where May had inherited her good looks.

He and Misty had come to challenge a Gym. They had not expected to meet May's mother before the battle even began. It felt a little like an unexpectedly formal introduction to the family. They managed polite greetings.

Hello, Auntie, Misty said.

Hello, Miss Caroline, said Ethan.

Misty's greeting placed her among May's friends, but Ethan's choice of Miss delighted Caroline even more.
