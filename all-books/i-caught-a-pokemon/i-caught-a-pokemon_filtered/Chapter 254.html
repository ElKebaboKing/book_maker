Chapter 254: Stopping the Rampage

A shock wave as powerful as a hurricane swept across the campsite, flinging everything into the air. Tents, sleeping bags, equipment, and stones of every size scattered. Even the trees at the edge of the forest swayed as if they might be torn from the ground.

The wind whipped Ethan's bangs. Sabrina waved a hand, raising an invisible barrier before the group. It stopped the blast's aftershock from reaching them, though its force still jolted their bodies.

Misty, the lightest among them, lost her balance. Ethan caught her before she fell. She stayed close to him for a moment, sheltered against his chest.

"Ptooey!" Ash spat sand from his mouth. Dust covered his face and clothes. When he looked toward the explosion, he found that the gently sloping lakeshore had become a round crater more than ten meters across. Fresh earth, broken stones, and exposed roots lined its sides. Lake water was already pouring into it, and before long the hole would be full.

Tracey and the others stared. "That explosion was powerful enough to change the landscape!"

Ethan let out a shaken breath. "It was far too close. We only just blocked it."

As the dust settled, he looked past the crater toward their camp. Quackenpoker's equipment was there, along with all their luggage. Losing any of it would have been a serious blow. Fortunately, their belongings were merely strewn about. The campsite was a wreck, but the things themselves had survived.

The Gyarados had recovered from the pause after using Hyper Beam. They swam toward the shore. Quackenpoker, meanwhile, was intent on checking his instruments and data for damage. He showed no sign of leaving.

Ethan could see no way around it. If they would not abandon Quackenpoker, they would have to fight.

He stepped in front of the old man with Ash and the others, putting themselves between him and the lake. Their Pokemon moved forward in turn, shielding the humans. Everyone braced for the approaching Gyarados.

As the distance closed and battle seemed inevitable, Ash gritted his teeth. He took a few steps forward and held out a hand.

"Gyarados, listen to me! We're trying to help you. Team Rocket caught you, not us"

It was just like Ash to try talking to them. The enraged Gyarados were in no state to listen. They glared at him and opened their mouths, ready to attack again.

They could not wait any longer. Ethan waved to the Pokemon beside him. "Go! Stop them!"

More than twenty Pokemon belonging to Ethan, Misty, Ash, and Tracey surged forward. Some flew, some ran, and others swam out to meet the Gyarados. They showed no fear of their opponents' size. A moment later, the two sides collided.

"Rrragh!"

"Pika, pika!"

"Rrroar!"

Cries rang along the lakeshore as attacks crossed in every direction. With so many Pokemon fighting in such a small area, the scene grew too chaotic for the Trainers to direct each one. The Pokemon had to fight on their own.

Fortunately, their partners were seasoned battlers. The Gyarados had exhausted themselves climbing the waterfalls before evolving, and rage had clouded their judgment. They had no coordination or technique. They simply struck at anything that was not another Gyarados. Attacks of every color flew across the battlefield.

In the middle of it all, another voice rose.

"Psyduck! I didn't tell you to come out! Stop leaving your Poke Ball on your own and making things worse!"

Misty stepped away from Ethan, exasperated. He followed her gaze and saw Psyduck standing in place, its head tilted as it stared blankly at the Gyarados. It made no move to attack and seemed equally unaware that it should dodge.

Misty pulled out its Poke Ball and recalled it before it could get hurt. Ethan could not help laughing. Psyduck was as reliably oblivious as ever.

When he looked back at the battle, neither side seemed to have the upper hand. They were unlikely to defeat the Gyarados quickly. His hand went almost unconsciously to the Great Balls at his left hip.

Even now, he had no intention of sending out the three legendary birds. One Thunder from Zapdos might knock out every Gyarados still in the water, but those Pokemon were his trump cards. He would not reveal them unless the danger truly demanded it.

As the fighting continued, Pokemon on both sides took injuries. Ethan and the others watched anxiously. Then a stream of fire flew over their heads, forcing them to duck.

Ash's Charizard had just taken a painful Hydro Pump. It had lost its temper and was attacking without regard for anyone around it.

"Hey, Charizard! Watch where you're aiming!" Ash shouted. As usual, Charizard ignored him.

Ethan had thought their relationship might have improved by now. Clearly, they still had work to do.

Haunter emerged from Ethan's shadow. It had been one of the few Pokemon content to watch from the sidelines, expecting an entertaining fight. Now that the battle was slipping out of control, it stayed close to Ethan to guard him.

Sabrina might protect him as well, but Haunter was Ethan's Pokemon. It would not leave its Trainer's safety entirely in someone else's hands.
