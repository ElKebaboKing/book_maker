Chapter 404: The Umbrella Dance and Hospitality

After the tea ceremony ended, Naoko temporarily left Ethan and Erika to prepare for the tea party's final performance.

The two wandered through the courtyard for a while, stopping here and there to admire the garden scenery. When the time drew near, they made their way to a stage in the courtyard.

For the tea party's most important program, all five sisters would perform a dance together. It was also the part the visiting guests anticipated most.

The skill of Ecruteak's Kimono Girls onstage was beyond question, and the opportunity to enjoy their dancing at close range was certainly welcome.

Nearly two hundred people soon gathered before the stage, accounting for every guest who had come that day.

Ethan and Erika sat among the crowd. They had not secured places in the front and could only sit in the middle, but that was still an excellent location. The entire stage fit within their view. Although they heard a little noise from the surrounding audience, the visual experience was ideal.

Before long, music began and the five sisters stepped gracefully onto the stage.

They were once again dressed in splendid costumes and stage makeup, and each carried a dance umbrella.

As they took their positions, Naoko quickly scanned the crowd as though searching for someone. Her gaze soon met Ethan's and Erika's, and a faint smile appeared on her face.

When the music changed, the five sisters began their performance.

The umbrellas opened and closed, swaying gently in time with their slow steps.

Unlike the previous night's performance, this was simply an umbrella dance. With no story demanding his attention, Ethan could concentrate entirely on the dancers.

His gaze moved slowly across them.

How could he put it? Every one of them was beautiful. Although their styles differed greatly, each possessed her own charms and was an exceptional beauty.

In order of age, the sisters were Miki, Kuni, Zuki, Naoko, and Sayo, with exactly two years separating each of them.

The youngest, Sayo, was only fifteen, while the eldest, Miki, was already twenty-three.

They're all in the flower of their youth...

The moment the thought occurred to him, Ethan suddenly came to his senses.

Wait... I'm only twenty years old myself. Why do I feel as though my outlook has grown so old!?

When the music ended and the dance concluded, thunderous applause erupted below the stage. The five sisters stood in a line and bowed to thank the audience.

After they left the stage, the guests began dispersing in every direction, voices of praise filling the air.

"Today's performance felt even better than the last one!"

"Miss Miki is so elegant!"

"Zuki was wonderful too!"

"I still remember Sayo dropping her umbrella during the last performance. Fortunately, she didn't make any mistakes today!"

Listening to the discussions, Ethan and Erika found it inconvenient to leave from the middle of the crowd. They waited until most of the other guests had departed before moving away from the stage.

"We should go too."

They had not walked far when Erika suddenly remarked, "Miss Naoko and her sisters are all so beautiful. They looked radiant onstage. They truly seemed to shine."

Ethan froze, uncertain what Erika meant.

Assuming she envied the sisters and wanting to comfort her, he somehow found himself saying, "Really? I think you're even more beautiful than they are. They may shine, but only while they're onstage. Your stage is the Gym battlefield. In my eyes, you're far more dazzling when you command your Pokemon in battle!"

Erika looked at Ethan in surprise, and the tips of her ears turned slightly red.

She had not expected him to be so skilled at praising someone!

She naturally understood what he meant, but she had only spoken on impulse. She felt no envy or jealousy and had simply been struck by how popular the girls were in that moment.

Just as she prepared to respond, a soft cough suddenly sounded behind them.

They hurriedly turned and saw a woman in a kimono descending the steps from the stage.

It was Zuki!

Expressionless, Zuki walked past them. As she passed Ethan, her eyes seemed to sweep over him deliberately.

A fragrant breeze washed over him, but Ethan inwardly cried out in alarm.

She must have heard what he said!

His expression inevitably grew awkward.

There was nothing particularly wrong with privately discussing someone with a friend, especially when he had not spoken badly of the sisters. The embarrassing part was that the person herself had overheard him!

And Zuki had only just served them tea!

Ethan carefully reconsidered his words. Fortunately, he had not said anything terrible. He had merely praised Erika as more beautiful than the sisters. Surely that was not a serious problem... right?

Uh...

All right, appearance mattered deeply to women, and no one could remain entirely indifferent to such a comparison. Zuki had probably placed him directly on her blacklist.

Hopefully she was not the kind of person to hold a grudge...

Ethan and Erika watched Zuki leave, then exchanged glances. Before either could speak, more footsteps came from behind them.

They turned and this time saw Naoko approaching with the other three sisters.

They had been farther away and clearly had not heard the conversation, nor did they notice the stiffness in Ethan's expression.

Naoko smiled and asked, "What did you think of our performance?"

"It was truly wonderful!" Erika replied earnestly. "Everyone below the stage was praising you!"

Naoko was delighted. Only then did she notice that something seemed wrong with Ethan's expression.

"Huh? Mr. Ethan, why don't you look well?"

"Him?" Erika glanced at Ethan and shook her head in amusement. "Don't mind him. He's feeling a little uncomfortable right now."

Seeing Ethan stand silently to the side, Naoko found his behavior somewhat strange. Although she did not know what had happened, she did not press the matter.

They returned to the courtyard together. Perhaps their conversation became too enjoyable, because Naoko warmly insisted that Ethan and Erika remain for dinner.

It was difficult to refuse such sincere hospitality, so the two could only agree.

At Naoko's invitation, they entered the main house. The tea party had ended, and all the other guests were leaving.

Naoko remained to entertain them while her four sisters went to the entrance to see the guests off.

Dusk arrived before anyone noticed. The daytime bustle came to a complete stop, leaving the courtyard quiet and deserted. Maids began clearing away the traces left by the visitors, and everything proceeded in perfect order.

That evening, the five sisters changed into simpler kimono and removed their makeup before hosting dinner in the main house.

A small table stood before each person, holding several delicate side dishes and sake.

They knelt facing one another. Other than Ethan, the room contained six beautiful women. It should have been a delightful sight, but Zuki's meaningful gaze left him uneasy. He could not shake the feeling that something was about to happen.
