Chapter 678: Nothing to Do with Me

It was a strange feeling.

Ethan looked at his own body on the bed. His heart was still beating, yet from outside it he could no longer feel even his breathing.

Misty saw him lying there as though asleep. She knelt on the bed and nudged his arm. Ethan? Have you gone?

Gone? You make it sound as if I died... Ethan turned to Gengar. She can't hear me now, can she?

A spirit can't speak to ordinary people, Gengar said. She won't hear your voice. You could still play a prank on her, though.

Ethan shook his head. He had no interest in playing tricks on Misty. But then he noticed something else.

When he did not answer, Misty seemed to assume he had left. Wearing a curious little smile, she slowly reached toward his chest.

Ethan stared. What is she trying to do?

He frowned at Gengar. Tell her I haven't gone anywhere yet. She needs to behave herself.

Gengar had been grinning, eager to see what Misty would do. Now it sighed in disappointment, cleared its throat, and relayed Ethan's message.

Huh? Misty snatched back her hand like a startled bird. Embarrassment flooded her face. I was just going to straighten his clothes. They looked a little messy.

Even as she spoke, she berated herself. I'm such an idiot! Gengar hasn't even left. Of course Ethan hasn't either! Why did I try anything now? I got impatient and he caught me. I hope he believes that excuse...

Hovering above her, Ethan believed none of it. Her flushed face had told him enough. He should have let Gardevoir out to guard his body.

Before he could say more, Gengar urged him on. We don't have time for this. I told you a spirit is unstable outside its body during the day. Go and do what we came to do.

Ethan cast one last uneasy glance at Misty, then followed Gengar through the cabin wall. They drifted into the corridor. He could not properly control his spirit form, so Gengar pulled him along.

Where are we going? Gengar asked.

Back to the deck where we met them. They should still be there. Can other Pokemon see me like this?

Only Ghost types. Are you trying to avoid them?

This is supposed to be discreet. It's better to avoid people and Pokemon if we can. That will spare us trouble.

Then we'll approach quietly through the air.

Without being noticed, they reached the deck where they had met Diane. She and Butler were still there, reclining in deck chairs. Other passengers were nearby, and the pair said nothing to each other. They simply sunbathed.

Their behavior looked perfectly ordinary. Somehow, that only deepened Ethan's suspicion.

Gengar glanced up at the blazing, cloudless sky. They could stay here for ages. Are we going to wait? Spend too long away from your body and you really will die.

Then make them leave. Ethan thought for a moment. Find a way to drive them inside.

I knew that job would fall to me, Gengar muttered. It slipped to a spot no one was watching, transformed into a giant electric fan, and sent a powerful gust across Diane and Butler's chairs.

Whoosh!

Hair flew everywhere. Hats were blown from passengers' heads. People scrambled against the sudden wind. Diane and Butler could no longer sunbathe comfortably, so they got up and went inside.

They appeared to take the gust for an ordinary change in the weather. When Gengar returned, Ethan followed them discreetly into the ship.

He did not get too close. They might have a Ghost-type Pokemon that could see him. He stayed more than ten meters behind, far enough to give Gengar time to react if something unexpected happened.

Their cabin was not far from his, though it lay two decks higher and looked rather more expensive. A magician can clearly afford a fine room, Ethan thought.

Diane and Butler stopped at a door, swiped their card, and went in. Gengar passed through first to check for danger, then pulled Ethan through the wall.

The moment he entered, Ethan heard a man's voice. You shouldn't have risked approaching him.

Butler had removed his sunglasses. Ethan could finally see him clearly. He looked at least in his thirties, handsome enough and graceful in his movements. His elaborate hairstyle gave him a slightly frivolous air.

What does it matter? Diane went to the drinks cabinet, poured herself a glass of red wine, and sipped it. You've left Team Magma. He's no longer your enemy.

Team Magma? Ethan's suspicion had been right. Butler knew him and had once belonged to Team Magma. But why had he left?

You're inviting trouble, Butler said darkly. If he becomes suspicious, our entire plan could fail.

Would that be so bad? Diane laughed softly. Then you could give up that impossible dream and finally let it go.

Never! No one will stop me from taking revenge on the people who mocked me. Once we reach Forina and find Jirachi's crystal, my wish will come true!

Jirachi. Ethan understood.

Butler had been a member of Team Magma. Something had happened there that left him humiliated; he had departed and now sought Jirachi to fulfill a wish.

Jirachi was a Mythical Steel- and Psychic-type Pokemon. An old legend said that once every thousand years, a clear singing voice could wake it for seven days. Upon waking, it would grant any wish written on the tags attached to its head.

Most people treated that story as a charming legend. Few believed it literally. Butler seemed obsessed with making it real.

Now Ethan vaguely remembered where he had seen these two before. They were characters from the Jirachi movie, and Butler was its villain. Their names had been unfamiliar, but their faces had stirred a memory.

Having learned what they were doing, Ethan lost interest. He had no designs on Jirachi and no wish to travel all the way to Forina. He would leave the rest for Ash to deal with.

He signaled to Gengar. They left the cabin and followed their route back.

When they entered their own room, Ethan found Misty asleep beside his body, her head resting against him. Her Starmie stood nearby, bored but keeping watch.

Honestly... Ethan shook his head and slipped back into his body. He opened his eyes.

The movement woke Misty. She rubbed her sleepy eyes and stared blankly for a moment before realizing what had happened. Ethan, you're back?

He regarded her uncertainly. You didn't do anything to my body, did you?

She was instantly wide awake. Of course not! Is that really what you think of me?

He was still suspicious, but his clothes were where they should be. He kept his doubts to himself.

Eager to change the subject, Misty asked, What did you find out?

Nothing that concerns us. Those two do have another purpose, but I'm not their target. We don't need to spend any more time on them.

...

For the rest of the voyage, Ethan set the matter aside and continued training Feebas with Misty. It did not evolve, but it gained a great deal of experience and improved noticeably.

Diane came to watch one of their sessions and chatted casually with them. She seemed unaware that Ethan knew about her and Butler. He remained polite but kept his distance.

The voyage ended when the ship docked at Sootopolis City and Ethan and Misty disembarked.

Sootopolis was a mysterious city where history slept. It stood in eastern Hoenn on a large island apart from the mainland. Ethan had heard that its real-world model was Santorini in Greece.

The city lay inside a volcanic crater. Long ago, an undersea volcano had erupted and risen above the ocean. Rainwater collected in the crater and formed a lake. People found the climate warm and pleasant, and built Sootopolis within its walls.

Ethan came down the gangway intending to head for Sootopolis Gym. Before he could leave the harbor, he glimpsed someone passing in the distance. The sight was brief, but his sharp eyes recognized her at once: Shelly, the Team Aqua commander he had encountered several times.

What is she doing here? Planning another operation?

Ethan called to Misty and hurried after Shelly instead of going to the Gym. His conflict with Team Aqua had gone far beyond a passing rivalry. If he heard they were nearby, he would seek them out; when one walked right in front of him, he would certainly not let the opportunity pass.

Shelly seemed preoccupied. She did not notice she was being followed. No other Team Aqua members accompanied her; she was alone. Even Misty thought something was wrong.

What's happened to her? Did losing Deoxys last time shake her up?

For a senior Team Aqua officer to walk openly through a public port while looking so distracted, something must have changed.

I doubt she's that fragile, Ethan said. It must be something else.

They followed Shelly out of the passenger terminal and toward the cargo area. When they reached a deserted stretch, Ethan stopped trailing her. He and Misty moved ahead and behind to cut off her escape.

Whatever had brought Shelly here, he would not ignore a chance to confront an enemy.

She startled when Ethan stepped into her path. Recognition made her look even more shocked.

Didn't expect to meet me here, did you? Ethan smiled as he reached for a Poke Ball at his waist. It's a shame for you, but today you're the one with nowhere to run.

W-wait! Shelly's expression changed. She raised both hands to show she did not intend to fight. I have something important I must do. Could you let me go this once?

She seemed like a different person from the arrogant opponent Ethan remembered.

Oh? His smile did not quite reach his eyes. You look as if something has happened. I'm curious. Will you tell me what it is?
