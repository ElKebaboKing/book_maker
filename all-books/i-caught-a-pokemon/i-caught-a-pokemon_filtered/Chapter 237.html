Chapter 237: Mugsy

At the sound of the voice, the spectators all turned at once. As they parted to make a path, a handsome man and a cool-looking beauty with long hair came slowly through the crowd.

Every eye was on the man, but his stride remained steady and his gaze fixed ahead. It was, of course, Ethan.

He stopped before the young Trainer and repeated himself. "Since no one else wants to battle you, I'll be your opponent."

The Trainer glanced at Sabrina. Her beauty caught his attention, but he looked away almost immediately, his eyes clear. However beautiful she was, Pokemon interested him more, and she would not interrupt his winning streak.

Ethan could not know the young man's thoughts, but he was impressed by his composure. It was natural to look at someone attractive. Yet this Trainer had shown more restraint than either Danny or Rudy, the two Orange Crew Gym Leaders Ethan had met. He was more disciplined than Ethan himself, who would certainly have stolen another glance or two.

The young Trainer studied Ethan in turn. Beneath his handsome appearance, Ethan seemed extraordinarily calm. Anyone willing to step forward now could not be an ordinary opponent. He must have confidence in his ability.

Out of respect, the Trainer introduced himself. "I'm Mugsy. What's your name?"

"Ethan. From your accent, I'd guess you aren't from the Orange Islands."

"That's right. I came from Johto specifically to challenge the Orange League." Mugsy lifted his chin with some pride. "To be honest, the Gym Leaders here aren't much. I've been here only four or five days, and I already have two badges. I thought the Orange League would be tougher."

People around them frowned. Most of the passengers were islanders. They did not enjoy hearing someone disparage their home Gyms to their faces. But Mugsy had just beaten several Trainers in succession. Before arguing, each of them had to consider whether they could back up their words in battle.

"On that, we agree," Ethan said, adding to their discomfort. "Compared with Kanto and other regions, the Gyms here aren't very professional. Especially those challenges that have nothing to do with battling. They're a headache."

He glanced at Mugsy's Pokemon. It was a Quagsire with a dopey, endearing look, the one that had defeated five opponents in a row.

"Anyway, back to the match. Will you keep using Quagsire?"

"Better not." Mugsy folded his arms and shook his head. "You don't look like an easy opponent, and this one is getting tired. I'll use another Pokemon against you."

Ethan had no objection. Since they were both choosing anew, he proposed something else. "Let's make it fair. We can each pick a Poke Ball and send our Pokemon out at the same time, without seeing what the other chose."

"A blind matchup? Fine by me."

Mugsy recalled Quagsire and took another ball from his belt. Ethan walked to the opposite Trainer's position, scanning the makeshift battlefield before he made his choice.

The field had been set up on a ship's deck. It could not be as sturdy as one built on solid ground. A particularly heavy Pokemon might damage it. He would have to choose someone agile.

After a moment's thought, he settled on his third Poke Ball. The two Trainers exchanged a glance, then released their Pokemon almost together.

"Come out, Jolteon!"

"Go, Poliwrath!"

Two flashes of blue light revealed Jolteon on Ethan's side and Poliwrath on Mugsy's.

Ethan raised an eyebrow. "Electric against Water. Even with a blind pick, I've ended up with the advantage."

"You have," Mugsy said with a smile. "I didn't expect Jolteon. Looks like I drew an unlucky matchup."

His voice held no alarm. The type disadvantage hardly seemed to bother him.

"Would you like to change Pokemon?" Ethan asked politely.

"Of course not." Mugsy waved the offer away. "Don't underestimate me. Poliwrath is my ace, and I'm good at fighting through a type disadvantage."

Ethan noticed a gleaming gold belt around Poliwrath's waist. "What's that?"

Mugsy grinned proudly. "A championship belt. Poliwrath and I won it at a Pokemon fighting tournament back home. We beat one strong opponent after another. It's a rare keepsake."

A fighting tournament. Ethan admired the belt. Unfortunately, he had no Fighting-type Pokemon, so he could not enter a competition like that for now.

While the Trainers talked, Jolteon and Poliwrath took their stances. The battle looked ready to begin at any moment. The spectators whispered among themselves, guessing who would win.

Mugsy had offended many of them, but he had a winning streak behind him, and most thought he would extend it. A few noticed Ethan's composed bearing and suspected he might be strong enough to end Mugsy's run.
