Chapter 644: Sometimes You Don't Have a Choice

On their own, Ethan's Combusken and Metang weren't especially powerful yet. They were about as strong as an ordinary Trainer's Pokemon, which put them at a disadvantage against Team Magma's elite grunts.

But Ethan wasn't fighting alone.

Ash sent out Pikachu and Corphish. Misty added Politoed and Starmie, and Captain Stern had his Sealeo. Together, they matched the Magma members in both numbers and strength.

No one could dismiss what they could do as a team.

At their Trainers' commands, the Pokemon attacked in succession. A burst of multicolored moves flew at the Mightyena from several directions at once.

The Mightyena couldn't block or dodge every attack. Even when they countered one incoming move with another, a different attack slipped through. After a few exchanges, they were close to defeat.

Just as Ethan's group thought they could finish the fight easily, rotor blades roared overhead. A helicopter appeared high above them.

The prominent emblem on its body marked it as Team Magma's.

Reinforcements? Stern frowned. After a moment's thought, he called to the others, They've brought help. We should leave for now!

He had recovered the case. If they could get away safely, the thieves could do nothing more to him. Once back in the city, he could call the police and leave the intruders to them.

Ash and Misty looked to Ethan. They understood Stern's concern, but did they really have to retreat?

If they had been on their own, Team Magma's reinforcements might have forced them to withdraw. Ethan was here, though. Ash and Misty knew better than anyone how strong he was and what powerful partners he still had in reserve.

Ethan looked up at the helicopter and marveled again at the wealth of these regional organizations. Team Rocket, Team Magma, and Team Aqua all seemed to have endless money, plenty of members, and every kind of advanced equipment. They were equipped like armies, perhaps better even than the International Police.

Unfortunately for Team Magma, that wouldn't change the outcome. It was time for Ethan to get serious.

His fingers passed over the Poke Balls in his waist pouch. He selected two and threw them onto the field.

Blue light flashed twice. Dragonite and Scizor appeared!

There was no need to bring out a heavier force than the situation required. These grunts didn't warrant Gardevoir or Gengar, much less Raikou or Suicune. Dragonite and Scizor were more than enough.

At a gesture from Ethan, Dragonite beat its wings and charged straight for the helicopter. Scizor joined the fierce attack on the Magma members on the ground.

The grunts, who had been barely holding on, rallied at the sight of their incoming support. Each sent out another Pokemon. But Scizor's arrival left them no better off. Its bladed arms struck again and again, and their Pokemon fell one after another.

Damn it! How is this happening?

The grunt leading the squad stared at Scizor, then at the Trainer behind it. How had an ordinary theft brought them up against someone like this? Their intelligence had missed a serious threat. A commander, or even a leader, should have been sent to deal with him!

He glanced skyward. The support helicopter they had pinned their hopes on was trailing smoke under Dragonite's assault and looked close to falling. Its crew fought back as best they could, but they couldn't hit the Dragonite circling around them.

The squad leader gritted his teeth. The mission couldn't end as planned. Fortunately, he still had a way out.

He exchanged looks with the other grunts. They all pulled out smoke bombs and threw them down. Thick, choking black smoke flooded the area.

They couldn't even think of a new way to escape. Ethan shook his head. Scizor, use Air Slash!

Scizor swept its bladed arms forward. Two nearly transparent, crescent-shaped blades of air cut through the smoke.

The blades parted it, revealing the grunts recalling their Pokemon and sneaking away through the opening.

They thought they could run? Ethan stepped forward, ready to order Scizor after them, but Stern suddenly held him back.

Let them go. Don't chase them.

Ethan gave him a puzzled look. Seeing the worry on Stern's face, he understood.

Unlike Ethan and his traveling companions, Stern had a museum here. If they angered Team Magma further, it would be Stern's property at risk of retaliation after the travelers left.

Ethan sighed. People's lives were tied together in ways that made every action affect something else. No one could simply do as they pleased. Everyone had worries and obligations of their own.

In the brief delay, the Magma grunts escaped. Ethan lost interest in bringing down the helicopter as well. He whistled for Dragonite to return.

Dragonite didn't understand, but it obeyed faithfully. It let the helicopter go and flew back to Ethan's side.

Its rotors thundered as the smoking aircraft departed. Quiet returned. Without the marks of battle beneath their feet, it might have seemed that nothing had happened at all.

Thank you so much, Stern said, breaking the silence. He thanked the group, then took the case from Max.

When he opened it, he frowned. Max cried out, How could this happen?

Ethan, Misty, and Ash came closer and looked inside. The stone was gone!

Max could hardly believe his eyes. He removed his glasses and wiped them again and again, but the case was still empty when he put them back on.

That's impossible! His face turned red. I guarded the case the whole time. I never let it go! How did the stone disappear?

They fooled us, Stern said with a sigh.

Isn't it clear? Ethan shook his head. Their act convinced us the stone was still in the case. They must have taken it out earlier.

He regretted letting them escape. If he'd insisted on stopping them, they might have recovered it.

Stern was pained by the loss, but he didn't despair. He drew a black fragment no larger than a fingernail from his pocket and gave Max a reassuring smile.

Luckily, I cut off a piece earlier. We haven't lost everything. This should be enough for me to continue my research.

Stern really did have a good attitude. If Ethan had been in his place, he would have gone after the thieves by now.

Still, perhaps this could work in his favor. With the stone, Team Magma might narrow down Groudon's location. Ethan could follow that trail and see the magnificent ancient Legendary Pokemon with his own eyes.
