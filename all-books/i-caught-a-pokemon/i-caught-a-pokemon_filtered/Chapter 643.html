Chapter 643: Battle for the Case

The Team Magma members all had the same Pokemon: Mightyena.

They might have been standard-issue partners, but once the fighting began, it was Team Rocket's trio who came off worse.

Their strength was as consistent as ever. The museum intruders weren't commanders, only a few elite grunts, yet Jessie and James somehow couldn't beat them. Even Wobbuffet, whom some called the Wobbuffet God, couldn't keep them from falling behind almost immediately.

At Jessie and James's commands, Seviper and Cacnea attacked with Poison Tail, Bite, Pin Missile, and Needle Arm. The Magma members' Mightyena were stronger, however. Using only Tackle and Double Team, they stopped the attacks and countered. In a single exchange, they knocked out both Seviper and Cacnea.

The two Pokemon flew backward into the Team Rocket trio, sending all of them tumbling into the sea.

In moments, the water swallowed them. They vanished from sight, leaving only their screams echoing over the waves.

As useless as ever. When it came to disappointing people, Team Rocket never disappointed!

At least, however unintentionally, the trio had bought Ethan and the others time to get close.

The Magma members disposed of the strangers who had appeared from nowhere and turned to leave. Then a boy and a girl suddenly emerged from opposite sides of their path.

They frowned. There was no need to ask why the two youngsters were there.

Stop! Ash shouted. Leave the case behind! And what have you done with Captain Stern?

Ash had encountered Team Magma before. Not long ago, he'd fought both them and Team Aqua, and he knew something of what they were after.

The male grunt leading the group glanced at Ash and Misty without answering. He tried to step around them, only to see another man approaching head-on with a bespectacled little boy.

Tch. The grunt scowled. You keep popping up one after another. What a nuisance.

Ethan was about to speak when footsteps sounded behind him. He turned. Captain Stern was running toward them, breathing hard.

The curator looked disheveled. His suit was wrinkled and his hair singed. He had clearly been through something unpleasant.

Wait! Stern called. These people are my opponents!

Ethan noticed the Poke Ball in Stern's hand. Perhaps he hadn't had his Pokemon with him when the thieves took the case, making it easy for them to overpower him. Now he had returned with a partner, ready to settle matters himself.

Could he really defeat these Team Magma members? Ethan wasn't sure, but he held back and gave Stern the chance. The victim had asked to handle his own fight; for the moment, the rest of them could watch.

Go, Sealeo!

Stern threw his Poke Ball. Blue light flashed, and a plump blue Pokemon resembling a sea lion appeared.

Sealeo was an Ice- and Water-type Pokemon with a cream-colored front and a blue back. It had two short upper fangs, a pair of front flippers, a short flipperlike tail, and thick white whiskers on either side of its mouth. Those whiskers made it look like a kindly old grandfather.

Sealeo lived in groups on drifting ice and always seemed to be spinning something on its nose. It could distinguish a prey Pokemon's scent while swimming, locate it, and catch it. Round objects delighted it: whether a Poke Ball or a Spheal, it could balance one on its nose and spin it at will.

As soon as Sealeo appeared, Stern ordered it to attack. Use Aurora Beam!

Sealeo opened its mouth and fired an energy-filled beam of ice. It struck a Mightyena precisely and drove it back. The blast's shockwave knocked the Magma grunts off balance, and the case containing the stone flew from their hands.

The case arced high through the air. Max spotted it, jumped without a thought for his own safety, caught it, and hugged it tightly against his chest.

Ethan marveled at Max's agility. He also understood why Stern had insisted on fighting for himself. Sealeo was genuinely strong. Stern had clearly spent time training it, and it was certainly more capable than Team Rocket's trio. His confidence was justified.

But the elite Magma grunts weren't helpless. The sudden attack had surprised them, not frightened them away. At their urging, their Mightyena bared their teeth and advanced little by little toward Stern.

Team Magma! Ash demanded. What do you want with that stone?

I'm not telling you anything! one grunt snapped, pointing at the case. Hand it over if you know what's good for you, before we have to take it!

Not a chance! Max hid the case behind his back and glared. You criminals! This is important research material. We would never give it to you!

The grunts hadn't explained themselves, but their purpose was obvious. They must have heard about the stone and wanted to search it for clues to Groudon.

Their efforts were bound to be fruitless. They had spent years searching across the land, never realizing that Groudon had been hidden beneath the sea all along.

Ethan stepped in front of Max. There was no point talking further. They had recovered the case. If they drove off or captured the Magma members and made them reluctant to return, the immediate problem would be solved.

He threw a Poke Ball, and Combusken and Metang appeared before him. Ash and Misty also released Pokemon on either side.

The air grew tense. Seeing that the group meant to protect the case, the Magma members exchanged glances. Then their Mightyena rushed forward together!
