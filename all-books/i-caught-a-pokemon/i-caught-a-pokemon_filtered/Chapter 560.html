Chapter 560: Casey's Own Trap

Ethan and Casey walked to their respective Trainer's boxes amid a roar of cheers.

While the announcer introduced them to the crowd, they looked at each other across the field.

Casey had regained her composure since their meeting outside. Her face was bright with energy, and none of her earlier anxiety showed.

She had recovered quickly.

"Ethan!" Perhaps she had finally overcome her hesitation about speaking to him more familiarly. Like Misty, she addressed him without the formality of before. "I'm ready. Please give me a real battle!"

Ethan smiled and nodded. "Of course. Give me everything you've got."

Seeing that both Trainers were ready, the referee announced, "We'll now decide who sends out a Pokemon first!"

They looked at the large screen. The wheel spun through more than a dozen rotations before slowing to a stop, its pointer settling on Ethan's red section.

Ethan sighed.

His luck with the draw had been poor throughout this tournament. Every time, he had been chosen to send out a Pokemon first. He did not care much about the disadvantage, but drawing first again and again was still irritating.

"Red side, please send out your Pokemon first!"

At the referee's signal, Ethan took out the Poke Ball he had prepared and released his first Pokemon without hesitation.

A flash of blue light revealed Jolteon on the field.

Just as Ethan had studied recordings of Casey, she had reviewed his team before the match. Jolteon's appearance did not surprise her.

The announcer flipped through his notes and raised his voice over the stadium. "Ethan has sent out Jolteon! As everyone knows, it performed superbly in both the Indigo League and this year's Silver Conference. How will Casey answer?"

Casey looked at Jolteon, steadied herself, and took a Poke Ball from the pouch at her waist.

"Go, Meganium!"

She threw the ball far out. Blue light flashed, and Meganium appeared before JolteonCasey's first and most trusted partner.

Of the few Pokemon on Casey's team, it was a sound choice.

Meganium did not have a type advantage on offense, but as a Grass-type it resisted Jolteon's Electric-type moves. That improved its chances considerably.

Even from across the field, Ethan could see Casey smile. Her resolve and confidence were unmistakable.

That was more like her.

But...

Casey had grown quickly, yet Jolteon had trained longer and fought in many more battles. Beating Ethan would still be difficult.

He had no intention of giving her an easy chance, either.

Sure enough, Jolteon's performance surprised Casey as soon as the battle began.

The fastest of Eevee's evolutions showed extraordinary agility. Meganium's Magical Leaf and Tackle could not land while Jolteon raced around the field.

Even Razor Leaf, with its swiftly spinning blades, missed as Jolteon dodged each leaf.

"It's so fast..."

Watching from the sideline, Casey could barely follow Jolteon with her eyes.

Worse, Meganium could not use Vine Whip to restrain it as some other Grass-type Pokemon might. Its short, thick limbs could not keep pace with Jolteon. Jolteon repeatedly found openings and struck with Electric-type attacks.

Meganium's resistance to Electric-type moves was all that had kept it in the fight.

Seeing her partner pushed back, Casey searched anxiously for a way to reverse the battle. Then an idea came to her.

"Use Poison Powder!"

At her command, Meganium sprayed a cloud of purple powder. The move was too obvious to catch Jolteon.

But hitting Jolteon directly was not Casey's aim.

She ordered Meganium to keep releasing Poison Powder. Soon the purple cloud covered nearly half the battlefield. Jolteon dared not rush into the dense haze and could only pace outside it, giving Meganium a chance to catch its breath.

"A clever response from Casey!" the announcer praised her. "If Jolteon closes in now, it will enter the poison cloud and risk being poisoned. Meganium has time to recover. What will Ethan do?"

Ethan raised his eyebrows at the haze.

It did keep Jolteon from approaching easily. Yet the scene looked familiar. Had he seen something like this before?

He could not remember which battle it had been, but he remembered how to respond.

"Jolteon, use Thunder Shock!"

Jolteon leaped back to widen the gap, then fired a current from its body. The instant the electricity touched the suspended Poison Powder, a deafening boom sounded and the entire cloud exploded.

The blast engulfed Meganium. When the powder cleared and the crowd could see again, the Pokemon that had looked healthy moments before was barely able to stand.

Casey's face changed. She stared at the field in disbelief. "How...?"

An experienced Trainer would know that a cloud of powder could explode when it met a spark or an electric current. Casey had never encountered that danger before.

"Oh no!" the announcer cried. "Casey released so much Poison Powder that she gave Jolteon the perfect opening. Her own strategy has backfired!"

Ethan did not need to give another order. Seeing Meganium in that state, Jolteon dashed forward and knocked it out with a single Tackle.

The referee raised his flag, and Casey finally came back to herself.

She recalled the unconscious Meganium with a rueful smile, scolding herself for her mistake.

When she saw Ethan looking helplessly amused too, she stuck out her tongue at him and took a second Poke Ball from her clothes.

"You're next! Go, Pidgey!"
