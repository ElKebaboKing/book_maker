Chapter 197: The Snowy Mountain and Three Challenges

Danny had already climbed more than ten meters. Ethan set aside his hesitation and followed, using both hands and feet to make his way up.

Navel Island's volcano had looked daunting from below, but once Ethan put other thoughts aside and concentrated on the climb, he felt calmer.

His movements were clumsy compared with Danny's, and he was slower, but he took his time. He tested each handhold and foothold, determined not to make a mistake. With Haunter secretly guarding him, he did not have to worry that a fall would kill him.

The rumble of the cable car reached his ears. Sabrina glided past on her way to the summit. Through the window, Ethan saw her give him a small wave.

"You can do it. I'll wait for you at the top."

It was a simple word of encouragement, yet it filled Ethan with fresh energy. After his awkward start, he gradually matched Danny's pace. The gap between them shrank until they were climbing side by side.

More than twenty minutes later, they reached the halfway point. Danny stopped and suggested a rest.

Ethan still had strength to spare and wanted to push on to the summit in one go. Danny knew this mountain, though, so Ethan took his advice.

"Whew..."

He sat on a flat rock, let out a long breath, and looked into the distance.

White clouds drifted beside them in the breeze, so close that he felt he could reach out and touch them. This must be the cloud-wrapped part of the mountain he had seen before landing on Navel Island.

The air was fresh, and the view took in the faraway sea and the island's forest below. Better still was the satisfaction of having climbed here with his own hands. He could hardly put that feeling into words.

Danny smiled at him. "You're doing well for someone climbing without equipment for the first time. Be careful from here on, though. The slope gets gentler, but the temperature drops sharply. It gets very cold."

Ethan nodded. "Got it. Let's go."

After a few minutes' rest, they continued past the halfway point. Just as Danny had said, they no longer had to climb; they could walk uphill. The air grew colder with every step, and Ethan had to keep the biting wind from blowing straight into his mouth when he breathed.

After another dozen or so meters, patches of snow appeared underfoot. Snowflakes began to fall around them. It felt as though they had gone straight from summer into winter.

Ethan had no idea how high they were, despite all the climbing. When he asked Danny, Danny shook his head. He did not know either.

"The mountain isn't especially tall," Danny said. "It's the weather. Dark clouds cover the summit all year round, and that makes conditions change like this."

They trudged several hundred meters farther through thick snow before finally reaching the top. By then it was snowing hard enough to obscure their view.

Ethan's fingers and calves ached from the long climb. He had worked up a sweat on the way, and at first the cold had not bothered him. Now, with snow falling on him at the summit, he felt chilled to the bone.

Through the shifting curtain of snow, he could make out dozens of geysers erupting from the deep snow.

Sabrina had arrived ahead of them. She hurried out of a small wooden cabin beside the cable car, a thick red blanket draped over her shoulders. When she reached Ethan, she placed another blanket around him. Its heavy warmth drove off the cold at once.

Danny watched from the side, completely overlooked.

There are two of us here. Couldn't she have brought me one too? They're my blankets...

He shook his head, went into the cabin, and fetched a blanket for himself. By the time he returned to Ethan, his confident expression was back.

"Congratulations. You passed."

Danny expected the abrupt announcement to surprise Ethan and Sabrina. Neither of them looked the least bit startled.

"I figured it out a while ago," Ethan said with a smile. "You're the Leader of Navel Gym, aren't you?"

Danny fell silent, deflated. Then he gave a rueful smile. "So you knew already. Pretty sharp of you. That's right. I'm Danny, the Navel Gym Leader and the Star of the East of the Orange Crew!"

He turned to Sabrina hopefully. "Miss, did you guess who I was too?"

Sabrina merely glanced at him without speaking. Learning his identity had not impressed her as he had imagined it would.

After traveling with him for so long, she had picked up on enough small clues to suspect who he was. Even so, what difference did it make? She was the Gym Leader of Saffron City, one of Kanto's busiest cities. She could not see why the Leader of a Gym on a remote island in a minor league thought his title would impress her.

Danny opened his mouth, seemed about to say something, and finally sighed.

"Never mind. First, welcome to Navel Gym. You've passed the initial test, so I'll accept your challenge."

He held up three fingers. "A Navel Gym match has three rounds. Win two, and you pass the challenge. The first round is a contest to freeze hot water!"

As if on cue, a fissure nearby erupted. A column of water shot several meters into the air. Steam billowed from it; even on this frozen summit, the water was clearly hot.

"A freezing contest?" Ethan said.

Just as in the anime, the events of this Gym challenge had not changed. But there was a problem.

Ethan rubbed his chin. "What if I don't have an Ice-type Pokemon?"

Danny folded his arms and shook his head with exaggerated regret. "Then there's nothing I can do. You lose the first round."

"Just like that?" Ethan stared at him. "Can't you choose a different event? I can't be the only challenger who's come here without an Ice-type Pokemon. Do you make every one of them forfeit?"

"No substitutions," Danny said with a smile. "Those are my rules. You have to follow them."

At Danny's firm refusal, Ethan frowned. He still had too few Pokemon, with too many types missing from his team. That made situations like this difficult.

But giving up a round without a fight was not his style.

He took a Great Ball from his waist. "All right, then. I can't keep this one hidden any longer. It looks like I'll have to make an exception and send this Pokemon out."
