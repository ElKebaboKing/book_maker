Han Sen had used Turtle, the Sky Palace force, and Suppress Evil to modify his own skill Coin. He couldnt use it in front of others in fear of revealing his identity, so these results were pretty decent for the skills first use in real combat.

That coin was like a mountain attached to the giants right glove. Not even Kahns power could keep him from sinking.
