She closed her eyes for a moment, allowing the memories to return with complete clarity.

The moment she had read Rens name in Julius letter, something had broken inside her chest. All the time avoiding him, all the emotional defenses she had carefully constructed, had disintegrated in an instant.

The fusion had been instinctive, desperate, driven by a need that went beyond logic.

Her Greater Spiritual Fairy and her Horned Snow Spirit had combined and duplicated within her in a configuration she had never attempted before, forming a wing of light with each of the resulting clones.

But the wings of pure light that had propelled her through the air at extreme speeds hadnt been the most useful help for the journey.

Rather, her incredibly long jumps had been.

With Larissa elevated high in the air above the wall, visibility was excellent. Each jump had covered kilometers, her fused form harnessing mana with great efficiency to maintain momentum that would otherwise have been impossible.

She had been a blur of speed and light. Everything had happened incredibly fast, but paradoxically, in her light form her thinking that had functioned much faster, made it as if the entire world moved in super slow motion around her.

During those stretched moments, while flying and leaping toward the epicenter of the strange but familiar energy she had sensed, her mind had been a whirlwind of conflicting emotions.

Of course she wanted to know if Julius and the others were okay. Of course she wanted to help if they needed assistance. But what had stressed her most, what had dominated every accelerated thought, had been a much more personal and terrifying question.

What would she do when she finally saw him?

What would she say after years of silence?

What would she feel when standing before him for the first time since that terrible day when hed had to take away the fragment?

Would there be hatred in her eyes?

The thoughts had swirled in her mind throughout the entire flight, every possible scenario playing out at accelerated speed as she drew closer and closer to her destination.

But when she had finally arrived, when she had seen the scene unfolding below, everything had gone blank.

The image she had witnessed hadnt been anything she would have expected, even in her worst nightmares.

Was that really Ren?

Was Ren really attacking Selphira and Julius?

Her accelerated mind had processed the visual information at high speed, confirming what seemed impossible to believe. Ren, wrapped in corrupted energy, with eyes blazing intense violet light, raising his hand to attack two people who had repeatedly tried to protect him.

She didnt want to believe it, but...

Indeed, Ren was acting like the villain.

The perfect moment to hate him, to reproach him for everything that had happened, to attack him and confirm that it had been a mistake to trust him from the beginning.

All the pain she had been carrying, all the repressed rage, could have finally found valid justification.

But instead of hatred, she had felt pure terror.

Terror not of him, but for him.

She had seen Selphira preparing a final desperate attack. At the same time, Larissa had pushed her last spatial jump without thinking, driven by pure instinct, positioning herself directly in front of Ren.

She hadnt known exactly what Selphiras attack was, nor had she planned to use or amplify it upon arriving just afterward.

She had simply jumped...

She had been trying to take advantage of something happening, hoping that after Selphiras attack she could make some difference.

But unintentionally, she had arrived at the exact instant before the drop touched Ren.

Unintentionally, her luminous presence had amplified the attacks effect, her elemental nature resonating with the purifying energy and multiplying its potency.

The result had been that Rens face, freed from darkness, had ended up directly in front of hers as the light gradually cleansed the corruption from his features.

In that moment of time stretched to the maximum by her accelerated form, Larissa had looked directly into Rens eyes as a small part of the darkness slowly faded.

And what she had seen had destroyed her.

Even filled with violet corruption, even distorted by energies that shouldnt exist in a human body, his eyes had been charged with emotions that even corrupt mana couldnt lie about. Immense pain. Fear that went beyond the physical. Anguish that seemed to be tearing him apart from inside. And above all, regret so deep it seemed to be drowning him.

All of that exploding in him just from seeing her for a moment.

Her heart had tightened with an intensity that had been physically painful. Was Ren really feeling so much upon seeing her? Had her mere presence affected him so much even in his corrupted state?

She had known he was empathetic, that he would surely feel bad for her.

But to that extent? To being devastated by emotional pain...

Understanding had come like an avalanche.

She couldnt hate him. Maybe she already knew that... but it was much clearer after understanding that he had been suffering as much as she had, maybe more.

Deep down, she simply couldnt hate him.
