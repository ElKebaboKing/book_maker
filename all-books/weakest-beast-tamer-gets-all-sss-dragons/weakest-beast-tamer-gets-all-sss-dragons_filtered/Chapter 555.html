Chapter 555: Chapter 555 - Taming the Fourth Year: Lunas Date - 2

The kiss was sudden and intense, carrying all the frustration and rebellion she felt toward her father. Rens mind went completely blank, overwhelmed by the unexpected intimacy and the intensity of the moment.

When she finally pulled back, both were breathing heavily, staring at each other with wide eyes as the reality of what had just happened began to sink in.

Rens eyes widened.

After the awkward and somewhat rough kiss, Luna sat on top of him.

"You always caught my attention," she told him, "although were really not compatible."

Her voice had changed completely, becoming more direct and aggressive than he had ever heard it.

"Im the opposite of you. Ambitious, youve shown me several times that youre not. Introverted, when youre not afraid to express what you feel. Elitist, toxically competitive, ignorant, vengeful, and privileged in a bad way. I never would have achieved anything with a weak beast like yours, rising from your total lack of privilege... much less with a spore, even though it became such a cute little creature."

Her words poured out like a torrent, as if she had been keeping them for years.

"Based on our opposite foundations, we should be enemies like Klein or Jin were to you. I shouldnt be your friend or close in any way. But after so many things and thinking about it..." she paused, "no one has interested me more."

Ren was completely overwhelmed by this avalanche of confessions and self-recriminations.

"Although today I wanted to beat Liora... see if you wanted to conquer me too," Luna continued, "see if Id like that... but I realize that a relationship with someone like me only someone like me could accept or enjoy... since its based on all superficial interest, appearances, and tastes."

Ren forced himself to ignore the hormones that had awakened with the kiss and the position they found themselves in. He breathed deeply and looked at her directly.

"Youre lying," he told her softly.

"What!?"

"My mana eyes dont work very well at the moment, but at least I can tell you still have that strange habit of saying things backwards..."

"...Were not opposites," he told her firmly. "And especially, Im also very privileged. I wouldnt change what I had at the beginning, my loving family, for any other privilege in the world."

Luna looked at him with a face full of surprise and something more.

"Its okay to be whatever you are. You always interested me too, and you failed to mention very pessimistic, because Ive observed you and youre not even a tenth as bad as you think."

Luna blushed intensely, but seemed to remember something and got angry again.

"I just wanted to test you to see if I would regret it," she told him brusquely, "but in the end it wasnt like that. Im sorry, but now were enemies and Ill take the first place seat from you now that your power is reduced."

Ren was confused, sensing through his mana that this statement was completely true.

Enemies.

Completely blushed, Luna reiterated: "Forget the kiss. Its not a big deal for me, so it shouldnt be for you either. And I didnt like it either."

Then she melted into the shadows, disappearing completely.

Ren remained there, completely confused, touching his lips.

He reproached himself because he had liked it so much when she pushed him and kissed him. His heart had accelerated just thinking about it again.

That he could only enjoy it if he was someone superficial? Maybe then he was MUCH more superficial than he believed.

And worse still... his self-proclaimed "enemy" had lied two more times at the end. So, the kiss was actually a big deal for her? And she liked it too?

He sighed deeply, realizing his situation was now infinitely more complicated than before.

Even giving himself a long time to think...

Everything was completely tangled.

The situation had somehow ended up worse than before. The first apology had led him to recover his friendships, but now he had lost them again just like that.

Not exactly lost, but interacting with the girls like always after all this?

Impossible.

All because of... Selphira and Sirius?

Somehow they had turned what should have been simple extra apologies into a complex web of feelings he didnt know how to handle.

But now, beyond his own confusion, he began to feel genuine curiosity about the situation.

He had to do something. He had to find a way to help Lu... the memory of the kiss still fresh made him blush just thinking of Lunas name... help her solve her problems.

First he could try to understand them better.

She had looked really upset by whatever her father had done. There was a desperation in her crying that went beyond normal family problems.

To understand them...

Ren concentrated on sensing mana signatures as best as possible. His perception abilities were significantly reduced, but not completely lost.

He didnt manage to find them immediately, so he forced himself to cycle mana in his system as he had been practicing to improve perception with his fungus-emulating mana technique.

After a moment and a small headache that reminded him of his current limitations, he managed to detect her trail. Sirius was, fortunately, at the academy. But he wasnt alone.

He quickly headed toward the area where he had sensed the combined mana signatures: Sirius, Selphira and... Zhao? It was somewhat far, but Ren used all his movement abilities to get there as quickly as possible.

When he was close, he could sense the mana without forcing himself too much and realized they hadnt moved. They must be in a room, probably having a private conversation.

He activated his artifact to hide his mana and employed a new trick he had also developed trying to emulate his funguss abilities. He cycled his mana from outside toward his center, making his mana signature become even smaller, almost imperceptible.

He approached the door cautiously and listened, amplifying vibrations with his affinity for earth and air elements.

"Are you sure it was there?" he heard Sirius ask, apparently addressing Zhao. "And it was a tree that looked like a claw of this specific black color?"

He seemed to be showing him something, perhaps an object or image.

The conversation carried an urgency that made Rens instincts sharpen. Whatever they were discussing involved something significant enough to require Zhaos knowledge and Selphiras presence, and Lunas emotional state seemed connected to it in ways he couldnt yet understand.
