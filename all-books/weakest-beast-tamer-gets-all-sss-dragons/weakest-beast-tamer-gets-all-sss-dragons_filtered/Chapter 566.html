Chapter 566: Chapter 566 - Taming the Fourth Year: Journey - 4

The silence that followed was absolute. Even the forest sounds seemed to stop.

Liu was the first to speak, his voice carefully controlled.

"Are you telling me we have to... roll around in that giant beasts s...?"

"Or you could say bathe ourselves, whichever option you prefer," Ren decided to clarify, though he knew the other option wouldnt sound much better. "Just... apply it generously to our clothes."

Taro made a grimace of disgust that seemed physically painful.

"Please tell me theres an alternative..."

"There isnt," Ren shook his head. "Golden Noses can detect smells from kilometers away because they smell for their only prey underground, which are root sprouts and larvae. If we enter a cluster without the protection of the scent, theyll attack us en masse. And believe me, we dont want to face hundreds of furious Golden Noses when were trying to rest."

Min raised a trembling hand.

"Exactly how... aromatic... is the smell of a Giant Cat Bear?"

"Imagine the strongest smell youve ever smelled in your lives," Ren responded honestly. "Multiply it by 2. Thats approximately half as intense as the smell of a Giant Cat Bear."

The three boys looked at each other, clearly searching for some way to escape this responsibility.

"Couldnt we just... camp outside the clusters?" Taro asked with desperate hope.

"In Gold territory?" Ren looked at him incredulously. "Taro, here the other diurnal and nocturnal Gold Rank beasts could attack us outside the clusters while we sleep. The clusters are literally the only perfect protection available."

Liu sighed deeply, resigning himself to the inevitable.

"Fine. But after this, youre going to owe huge favors to each of us."

"Enormous favors," Min added with a worried face. "The kind that involves expensive food and costly gifts."

"And were never, ever going to mention this," Taro concluded firmly. "To anyone. Ever."

When they finally entered the Gold 1 ring completely, the landscape changed dramatically. The dense, continuous fire forest gave way to a flat, open zone, dotted with clusters of what Ren had described as "small forests" scattered here and there.

The Golden Noses rose like natural cathedrals, their multiple slender, golden trunks shining with an internal light that made the air itself seem precious. Each cluster created small sanctuaries for those who knew the secret.

"Its beautiful," Liu murmured, momentarily forgetting his anticipated misery.

"And deadly," Ren added pragmatically. "Those trees can coordinate attacks that..."

His words were interrupted when he felt a small manaprint, a bit later two figures emerged literally from the earth in front of them.

Mako and Shizu materialized from the ground as if they had been waiting for them, their forms appearing so suddenly that Min stepped back several paces with a choked cry.

A small bird with ethereal blue plumage immediately flew from Makos shoulder, heading toward the sky with an evident purpose.

"What the hell?" Ren immediately got into guard position, feeling betrayed and furious. "This isnt what we had agreed on!"

The two guards exchanged uncomfortable glances. Mako was the first to speak, her voice loaded with genuine apology.

"Were sorry, Ren. We really are. But we have direct orders from above."

Shizu nodded, clearly feeling as uncomfortable as her companion.

"Zhao is going to come down soon. The bird we sent is to confirm your location."

Ren felt his anger increase. They had come so far, had been so close, and now they would have to fight against...

"Very well," he murmured, turning toward his friends with a grim expression. "Guys, prepare to face Zhao and these two with everything you have."

Min, Taro, and Liu immediately adopted defensive positions, though it was obvious they were terrified. Zhao was legendary even among students, and Rens personal guards had formidable reputations as Gold rank tamers.

But Mako and Shizu sighed in unison, exchanging another meaningful look.

"Ren," Shizu spoke softly, but there was something in her tone that made Rens stomach sink even further. "Zhao and we arent the only ones coming to make you return."

Zhao materialized near the ground, emerging from his silent flight and landing with grace, his wings folding as he touched the earth without making the slightest sound. His expression was convoluted... frustration, concern, and some reluctant understanding.

Immediately, Min, Taro, and Liu fused with their beasts, their bodies transforming as they prepared to invoke their double fusions if necessary. Mins amphibian created camouflage patterns on his skin, Taros beetle generated protective plates, and Lius bat sharpened his hearing to supernatural levels.

"Please," Zhao raised a hand in a pacifying gesture, "dont force yourselves to use the double fusion. Carrying you back is going to be much more complicated if youre unconscious from exhaustion."

His tone was tired but not hostile. Like an adult who had dealt with obstinate children many times before.

"Were not going back," Ren declared firmly, though he could feel the difficulty of his situation being so exhausted. "Please, Professor Zhao. Let us go. I have to do something important."

Zhao sighed deeply, completely terrifying Min with the sound.

"I found Lin... Selphira deduced most of the situation thanks to some things Liora and Mayo told her." His gaze softened slightly. "Those monsters did not wake up because of you, Ren. And its not your fault that things ended up this way. We already know those beasts were already preparing from much earlier."

"No!" Ren shouted, feeling frustration accumulate in his chest like pressurized steam. "You dont know the full story! But surely something happened near my old house that made these creatures become different and evade Dragarions crystallization punishment. So yes, it is my fault!"

The words poured out of him like a confession hed been holding back too long.

"You dont know that for certain," Zhao responded with the patience of someone who had had this kind of conversation before. "Besides, you dont have to worry about something like that because youre still very young. You need to grow more. Thats the most important thing and the best you can do now."

The condescension in his voice, however well-intentioned, hit Ren like a slap.

"I dont want to grow up in a cage!" he exploded, gesticulating toward the city. "That way Ill only fatten up like the trapped pigs! Without doing anything real, without helping those who matter to me!"

Zhao saw the black lines on Rens skin growing. He was about to mention them, but shook his head, maintaining his patient expression. They seemed to grow more because he was getting agitated.

"Calm down a little, this is not like you, youre exaggerating. I promise Id love to make this trip with you... though Im not thrilled about the idea of covering myself with that horrible smell again." A small smile appeared on his lips. "But its not your time yet. You need more development, more preparation. I really want whats best for you."

The lines began to recede a little.

But then they stopped...

"Then whos going to defend whats best for her? What matters to Luna?" Ren asked, feeling like he was drowning without hope but searching for a small light in what could be Zhaos response. "Who can take Siriuss place?"

Zhaos expression became genuinely sad.

"There will always be sacrifices, Ren. Theres no one else in the Starweaver line who can carry the artifact theyre looking for... Still, thats part of an adults decision. I dont know how you managed to find out but... Its up to Sirius to decide whether to leave the seals territory or not."

The word sacrifices resonated in Rens mind like a funeral bell. Larissa. His fungus. And now Luna...

"If you dont care what she wants," Ren replied, his voice becoming dangerously low, "I do care."

The black tattoos began growing on his skin again, radiating from his chest like ink spilling in water. Energy accumulated around him, visible like heat waves distorting the air.

The transformation was both cool and terrifying to witness to his friends. Min, Taro, and Liu sighed almost in unison.

They all got into fighting positions to face Zhao.
