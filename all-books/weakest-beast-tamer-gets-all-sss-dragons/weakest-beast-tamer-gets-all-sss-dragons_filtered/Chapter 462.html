Chapter 462: Chapter 462 - Tamers War - Broken Threads

Klein looked up from his books, and immediately his expression hardened with the accumulated resentment.

"What are you doing here?" he asked, his voice letting out months of repressed frustration.

"Ive come to take you to the new home where you will belong," Kassian responded, slowly advancing toward the rooms center.

The small number of teachers and guards tensed... being silver or lower, since all school gold ranks participated in the war, no one dared move. There was something in the two visitors presence that promised instant violence to anyone who interfered.

"I dont want to go with you," Klein declared, remaining seated but with his jaw clenched. "Im sick of your lies and how you hog all of fathers attention."

He didnt say it all directly, but also the resentment for years of being deceived and manipulated hadnt been forgiven and was evident in every word... The accumulated grievances of someone who had been lied to, exploited, and systematically undermined by the person who should have protected him.

The auxiliary teacher, seeing tension escalating and instinctively recognizing Klein was in danger, stepped forward.

"Excuse me," he said with all the authority he could muster, "but students are under academic protection during this crisis. You cant simply..."

He didnt finish the sentence.

Ravenspire moved with speed that defied perception, and the young teacher simply stopped breathing. His neck so brutally destroyed that the echo of his pain was felt throughout the entire refuge.

The silence that followed was absolute.

"Does anyone else have academic objections?" Ravenspire asked with courtesy that made his violence even more terrifying.

No one responded. The guard girls visibly trembled, Han had completely paled, and remaining teachers had become statues of pure terror.

Klein slowly stood, processing what he had just witnessed.

"Did you just kill someone?" he whispered, incredulity mixing with horror.

"Yes," Ravenspire responded as he bowed. "Does it bother you?"

Kassian sighed with exasperation.

"You didnt have to do that so... dramatically," he told Ravenspire. "Now Klein is going to be more resistant."

He addressed his younger brother with patience that sounded practiced.

"Klein, you need to come with me. Its for your own good."

The tone was reasonable and caring, the voice of someone explaining necessary medicine to a sick child...

False concern.

"NO!" Klein shouted. "Im not going anywhere with you! Im sick of you!"

Kassian moved closer, his movements deliberate and threatening.

"Klein, dont make me do this the hard way."

"Go to hell!" Klein roared, fusing with his beast in a desperate gesture of defiance.

He had recently leveled up to Bronze 2, and his defiant roar resonated throughout the refuge. But neither his improved strength nor his new abilities were rivals for Kassian.

The power disparity was cruel and absolute... enough improvement to give Klein hope, not enough to give him actual chance against someone some levels higher.

Kassian hit him.

It wasnt a soft reprimanding blow. It was a frustration-filled punch from an older brother who had lost patience, connecting with Kleins face with enough force to send him to the ground.

The violence was personal and brutal, family dynamics expressing themselves through raw physical force.

Klein got up bleeding from his nose, his eyes shining with tears of pain and betrayal.

"Why?" he murmured, touching his injured face.

The question carried weight beyond the immediate moment... not just why the punch, but why everything, why the lies, why the manipulation, why the sudden change of everything he had believed about his family.

"Because you leave me no choice," Kassian responded, beginning to manifest something that made the air around him become visible with strange patterns.

Ravenspire, who had been observing the familiar scene with amusement, approached Kassian with a smile that tried to hide his worry... even if the family drama promised entertainment.

"Were on the dangerous idiots side, remember?" he interrupted, "this is taking too much time. Kassian, hurry up."

"Im on it," Kassian murmured, concentrating on something that required absolute precision.

Invisible threads began extending from his hands, seeking connection points in Kleins nervous system.

"I didnt want to do this by force," Kassian explained while working, "because its more painful for both of us in the neural connection. But you leave me no choice."

The other children, Han, and guards could do nothing. Despite having developed some empathy for the poor boy from the wrong family, Ravenspires terror kept them completely paralyzed.

"Youll be the best version of yourself," Kassian insisted while threads sought to approach Kleins head and neural connections. "Ill help you achieve your true objectives, like I did with father."

Klein retreated desperately, but didnt fully understand what his brother meant.

"Im sick of your lies!" he shouted. "Stop talking like you own him... Im going to get fathers approval on my own! I wont need to listen to you ever again!"

Ravenspire laughed with genuine delight.

"Oh, this is on another level," he commented. "I didnt know this part of you, Kassian. Youre truly ruthless."

He decided that Venmont was going strong and they had time to add his own entertainment to the situation.

"You know what, little Klein?" Ravenspire said, approaching the crawling child. "Let me show you something interesting."

He created an illusion so real Klein couldnt distinguish it from reality.

His father appeared, but with a crucial difference. Kharzans eyes were empty, controlled, and when he spoke, it was with Kassians voice.

"I havent existed for years," Kharzans illusion declared. "Your father was always Kassian. Every decision, every word, every moment of approval or disapproval... everything was him."

Klein didnt want to believe it, but when he examined his memories more carefully, he realized something horrible. Memories of a distant and cruel father coincided exactly with periods when Kassian had begun being strange and distant too...

Shortly after his fathers announcement of allying with the Starweavers to protect the gates, leaving behind rivalry... that alliance announcement that never was. A younger Kassian had murmured defeat and disappointment.

The timeline was perfect... every moment of paternal cruelty corresponding to periods of Kassians emotional distance, every family disappointment tracing back to his brothers manipulation.

"No..." Klein whispered, his resistance weakening while processing implications.

Ravenspire abruptly cut the illusion and showed something even worse.

Kharzans severed head appeared in his hands, lifeless eyes confirming reality Klein had been denying.

"This hasnt been your real father for years, youve been seeing a puppet," Ravenspire explained casually. "Whats left of him, anyway."

Klein screamed, a sound that combined pain, horror, and absolute loss.

The cry was primal and heartbreaking.

Kassian immediately scolded the crow.

"That wasnt necessary!" he snapped. "Now the connection will be more difficult for me. Youre going to make me feel my idiot brothers stress with more intensity!"

The complaint was purely practical, annoyance at having his work made more difficult rather than concern for Kleins psychological state.

Ravenspire shrugged and apologized with false repentance.

"Sorry for playing along... I thought it would be fun... I wasnt wrong though."

Kassian sighed deeply and addressed Klein, who was now sobbing on the floor.

"Im going to revive our true father... Father is going to be just like before that stupid announcement," he promised with gentleness. "Everything he was is in my head, and I can repair it. Ill even make it better than before."

Klein looked up, tears and blood mixing on his face.

"Thats not my father!" he screamed with absolute desperation. "Its just your stupid idea! How can you be so idiotic not to see it?"

His voice broke while asking what he most feared to know.

"Did you kill him?"
