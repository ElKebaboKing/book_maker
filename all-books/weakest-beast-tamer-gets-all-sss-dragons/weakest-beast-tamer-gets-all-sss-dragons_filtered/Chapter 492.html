Beyond their military applications, the crystals represented generational wealth that could transform not just individual lives but the kingdoms entire economic structure.

Lin had been unconscious for days after her transformation, a luxury of time they couldnt afford in the current situation.

"So Im going to give her the elixir," Ren decided.

Besides, now that my beast is more powerful and I have new knowledge of higher ranks, I can use a new little trick... he thought to himself.

When he administered the potion, Ren carefully directed its flow so it would combine with the energy runes of Selphiras Black Turtle. Her water control could significantly amplify the quality of her healing thanks to some properties of the elixir.

This way, the same elixir can heal several people more cheaply and specifically using Selphira grandmas control as an enhancer and dosifier.

Juliuss arm regenerated gradually, beginning with bone and basic structure, then muscles and tendons, finally skin and nerve endings. It was like watching something grow in fast-forward, but with precision that only someone with decades of experience could achieve.

"Its extraordinary," Julius murmured, flexing fingers he hadnt had moments before. "I can feel everything perfectly."

My fungus took the seed and thats more potent than the elixir. What he didnt mention due to his fear of being scolded was that his fungus had already appropriated four artifacts that seemed to be extremely important...

The jade seed, the rings, and who knew what else his beast planned to absorb during their adventures.
