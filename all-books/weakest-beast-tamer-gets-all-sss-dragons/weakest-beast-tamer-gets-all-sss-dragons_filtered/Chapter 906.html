Ren couldnt know with certainty if the exterior areas had already been attacked by these mutants that had penetrated the defenses.

He desperately hoped it hadnt been so. That the vulnerable population hed once belonged to wasnt facing threats they couldnt effectively repel with such low ranks.

Iron tamers fighting against Silver-equivalent mutants would be a slaughter.

No other word for it.

But he had somewhat elevated hopes that wasnt the case. Because the beasts would come from the surface if theyd already emerged once to attack civilians. At least that would be more probable and logical. Returning underground after emerging would be without obvious tactical benefit.

Why tunnel back down when movement was easy and targets were still available on the surface?

But it was hope based on logic that mutants didnt always follow. So he couldnt be completely sure.

Still, something directing them specifically toward the academy would be better but nevertheless didnt sit well with him in any way.

Were they following him again like in the first attack hed suffered when he got corrupted?

Or was it something else like an artifact? Some other factor drawing them here?

He had no definitive answer. And there was no time to analyze deeply when immediate threat demanded action instead of contemplation.

Silent communication of shared recognition. Understanding without needing words.

Lunas expression: grim determination mixed with fear and anger. Fear for what was coming and anger that it was happening.

Determination to fight regardless...

Rens expression: Similar... Plus guilt about not having paid attention sooner. Plus calculation about how to be most useful in a crisis.

The exam had ended for now, their battles would have to wait. Because theyd need every capable tamer. Every beast that could contribute to the defense.

They were students, but they were also among the strongest students in their year and that meant more than it ever had.

The corrupted mutant beasts began emerging.

Earth erupting upward in some places as something massive forced its way to the surface.

Whatever Julius and Selphira had been trying to prevent or counteract, whatever had prevented them from watching the final battle, had escalated to the point where it was no longer a distant conflict but an immediate threat endangering everyone in the facility.

"Luna!" Ren shouted across the arena. "We need to make time for the people with low ranks! Can you intercept with me?"

The time it would take to mobilize the amount of population in the arena toward the designated reinforced shelters was time they hadnt available before the first enemies emerged

Only a positioning strategy remained. Leave non-combatants at the highest parts of the stands where theyd have some distance from the initial conflict zone in the ground level.

Fortunately practically the entire academic population was gathered here. That made coordination simpler even if it also meant all the vulnerable people were concentrated in one huge arena.

A blessing and a curse simultaneously.

Tamers with earth affinity, particularly those with beasts specializing in rapid construction, reinforced the arena base structure. They created elevated platforms where the highest benches had been. Improvised defensive positions converting spectator seats into small fortifications from which ranged attacks could be launched with some protection against counterattacks.

Not elegant construction but functional. Speed mattered more than aesthetics when you had seconds before combat began.

Ren wasted no time in deliberation.

He invoked both Hydra and Wolverine simultaneously. A decision to bring both Gold-rank power beasts to the field immediately rather than conserving one in reserve.

This situation didnt permit the luxury of retaining power. Not when hundreds of enemies converged from underground. Not when every second of delay meant more mutants reaching the surface.
