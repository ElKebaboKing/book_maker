Less Ren.

Less anything except violence, hunger and darkness.

Starting to back away.

Holding a corrupted Gold-rank tamer whod been getting stronger through absorption. Who was controlled by a thing that wanted survival and would fight with everything it had for it.

But theyd try anyway...

Because Ren was worth the risk.

And thankfully they had the upper hand.

Elemental purity being almost an antithesis of the corruption that had been propagating without control.

Darkness fleeing light. Wrong yielding to right. Not because of some subjective "moral superiority" but because of fundamental incompatibility.

A web like living thing spreading across the battlefield. Connecting all corpses and draining them. Feeding power back to the host through channels that pulsed with dim purple light.

Recognition that she was the primary source of light causing trouble. It was a targeting having a solid tactical logic from a strange entity fighting for its survival. Major threat elimination being priority before dealing with secondary threats.

Smart even. Not mindless corruption anymore but something approaching intelligence.

Interception being executed with perfect timing coming from her attentive observation of how darkness moved. The shadow jaws had closed over the dark extensions and separated them from the main mass.

Win here or die, no middle ground.

Second beast manifestation compromising sustainability but being justified by the immediate necessity. The tiger added its own luminous projection to the effort. Amplification transforming the constant pressure into an advantageous assault, making it difficult for corruption to maintain appropriate form around Rens body.

Two light sources now. Bracketing Ren from different angles and denying shadow the space to maneuver. Creating a cage of brilliance that contained darkness even as it fought to escape.

One shot to save him or lose him forever.

Just a few more seconds. Just a little more power. Just push it to the absolute limit where it would burn away everything corrupt.
