Chapter 841: Chapter 841 - Taming the Fifth Year - Biggest Eater - 6

Bringing the Bashe down with 10 semi-simultaneous shots was a solid plan based on simple mathematics Ren trusted hed calculated correctly.

The numbers were sound, the theory was proven.

Three stakes had created a noticeable effect; 10 would be overwhelming.

The problem was that the same simple calculation also showed him he couldnt simply make 10 of those stakes and fire them in succession as rapidly as hed executed the previous 3.

It was a too costly attack in terms of mana expenditure.

Making 10 stakes required slightly more than 20% of the wolverines total internal mana to create with appropriate density and launch with the necessary speed to ensure the shot wouldnt be dodged.

And although it could recharge mana from its core during battle, it would have to do so mid-attack if it attempted to fire all 10 in an already somewhat slow sequence.

The wolverines internal + external reserves were still at almost 80%. But creating all 10 new simultaneously required drawing on reserves that simply werent there that readily since 20% was the max that could be used at once. Even with continuous regeneration during the construction process, thered be a gap.

And that meant losing the roots momentum.

Thered be a pause between the 5th and 6th stake where the roots from the first half would die under miasma assault before reinforcements arrived. It was a timing problem converting the 10-stake plan into something less effective than simple calculations suggested.

The network would begin forming then collapse as the regeneration pause allowed the Bashe to overwhelm the partial formation. By the time stakes 6-10 arrived, hed basically be starting over rather than building on an established foundation.

Failure through mana depletion in the end.

He could fire the stakes slower as an alternative, reducing power invested in initial propulsion and relying on precision instead of speed to ensure impacts.

That would save approximately half the mana per stake launch, reducing the cost to around 18% each instead of 22%. With that savings, he could maintain more consistent cadence without needing a pause for complete recharge.

10 stakes 1.8% = 18% total, the process would be manageable. No pause required. Continuous assault that wouldnt give the Bashe time to recover between hits.

But he risked the Bashe managing to dodge the slower projectiles, which would be worse than the momentum problem because it would mean complete waste of mana.

A stake that missed was a stake that accomplished nothing. No damage, no network formation, no pressure on the enemy. Just mana down the drain and anl opening for Liora to exploit.

And Liora wasnt stupid. Shed recognize the pattern after the first few stakes and would do everything possible to avoid subsequent impacts. The serpent could weave, could rise higher until the maximum permitted altitude, could hit back at the worst moment.

The strategy required all 10 to connect or it simply didnt work.

For which reason Ren decided he needed to make extra preparations before executing the final assault.

He needed to establish conditions guaranteeing the 10 stakes would connect regardless of evasion attempts, and that the roots would have sufficient time to establish the complete network before miasma destroyed them.

The problem required a solution. So hed build one.

Liora remained attentive from her position, watching the wolverine with care that came from knowing Ren rarely made moves without multiple purposes.

Every action served 2 or 3 goals simultaneously. That damned knowledge of his, never seeming to be out of options. It was admirable and infuriating in equal measure.

She knew she couldnt show her cards yet and that actively attacking still wouldnt help her.

The wolverine on the ground could hide easily using shadow jumps or simply digging deeply where the Bashe couldnt reach it effectively. The serpents flotation gave it superior positioning but the wolverines mobility in the underground made it nearly impossible to pin down.

Chasing it would be pointless. Worse than pointless, it would waste the Bashes energy and potentially expose it to attacks during the pursuit.

It was basically a stalemate where only Ren could attack without significant risk while Liora had to maintain a defensive position waiting for Ren to exhaust most of his mana or provide an opening that hadnt yet presented itself.

She had no mana to waste...

While she watched and waited, Liora recalled a conversation shed had with Mayo weeks back when they were discussing strategies for the final exams.

Mayo had asked why she didnt want to "preload the Bashe" before entering battle, feeding it sufficient mana so it could attack with more freedom from the start without needing to absorb enemy attacks first.

Entering with the Bashe full of mana would have been an advantage for offense. The serpent could have been 50% larger from the beginning, could have generated more miasma, could have executed techniques that currently required absorption buildup.

And she only needed the serpent to eat some beasts from already-defeated teams to accomplish that, a "resource" that would have been relatively easy to obtain.

Thered surely be tamers willing to feel a bit of pain in exchange for appropriate compensation, especially those eliminated early whose futures werent too promising.

Easy money for some desperate students.

Mayo had been genuinely confused when Liora rejected the idea. "Why not? Its perfectly legal. Youd be stupid not to take every advantage."

But Liora had told Mayo it felt like cheating to use that method.

Not technically prohibited by the rules, true. The regulations said nothing about a beasts state upon entering the arena. You could arrive fresh or exhausted, empty or full, wounded or healthy. All that mattered was what you did during the match itself.

Besides, it wasnt as simple as it seemed on the surface. There was a specific reason for not wanting to enter "big" into the fight that Mayo hadnt considered in her suggestion.

A reason Liora wouldnt explain even to close friends because it touched on the Bashes deeper capabilities, secrets she had decided to keep hidden until absolutely necessary.
