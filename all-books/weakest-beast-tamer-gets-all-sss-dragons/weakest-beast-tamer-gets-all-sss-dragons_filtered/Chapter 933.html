Stay or go. Choose now.

And they both knew what shed choose. Hed calculated this perfectly.

Finally Selphira couldnt resist anymore.

She transformed in an explosion of mana that made the air around her cool precipitously. The fusion activating in a fraction of second.

And she launched toward the entrance with a furious look communicating exactly what shed do to Orion if she managed to get out of there.

Not a threat but a promise.

After she survived this, and she would survive this, shed make him regret every choice that had led to this moment. All of it would be repaid with interest that would make his current confidence seem like a tragic miscalculation.

The movement being smooth suggested hed been expecting this still. Hed known exactly what buttons to push. Known how much pressure to apply before she snapped and did exactly what he wanted.

But she still covered herself with ice armor shed anticipated manifesting. The protection ended up absorbing the impact and giving her more forward speed.

The ice cracked, but held long enough.

Selphira reached Victor about twenty meters before he wouldve reached completely to the bottom of the stairs where the six dual-beast tamers Orion had sent ahead were waiting. Her speed was sufficient to intercept the fall with space to spare.

She had the option of exiting the stairs confined tunnel and fighting with the six tamers in the more open space where mobility would provide advantage.

But Victor was literally in his final moments. Life being sustained by a margin so narrow that any additional perturbation could end it permanently.

She had to heal him immediately before considering any confrontation with enemies.

Every second counted and every delay increased the risk that hed die before she could stabilize him.

And shed have come all this way, sprung all these traps, expended an irreplaceable potion... Would that be just to watch him die in her arms because shed been too slow?

Unacceptable.

Selphira decided to give absolute priority to healing.

The rescue didnt serve its purpose if Victor died during the extraction process. So she invested a good quantity of mana and covered the tunnel with ice from both sides, taking advantage of the moment between Orions first and second beam.

The ice wasnt simple frozen water. It was reinforced with tons of mana that made it very sturdy. Not impenetrable but resilient enough to buy time measured in seconds or even minutes rather than milliseconds.

For now the six tamers couldnt reach her easily. A separation providing precious moments she needed to stabilize Victors condition.

But this once was all she needed.

The water and light mana she controlled at expert level, additionally amplified by the potions effects shed just consumed, flowed toward Victor in a constant current beginning to repair the damage that captivitys abuses had caused.

Water mended torn tissues and reconnected severed vessels.Light flushed corruption from systems barely functioning,burned away the foreign energies trying to establish themselves in his mana channels.

And together they worked miracles. Taking a body that shouldve been a corpse and dragging it back from the edge of death through sheer force of will and centuries of accumulated skill.

Consciousness returned abruptly in a manner that was disorienting when the last memory had been pain and darkness without apparent end.

Then suddenly this, cold air, warm hands, someone shouting his name through what sounded like underwater echoes.

But still, he remained practically incapacitated despite recovering his consciousness. His system didnt stop being affected by crystallization, that was something perhaps only Ren could heal...

And probably only partially.

They werent anything like the small Gold and Silver-rank crystals that had affected Zhao during the previous incident that had been resolved by Ren relatively easily. Though they werent as impossibly difficult as completely de-crystallizing a Diamond energy statue like Dragarion, they also werent something achievable without considerable effort and risk of causing more damage during the removal process.
