Chapter 948: Chapter 948 - Taming His Core - 2

It was too good to be true.

Ren knew that. Part of him had always known it, with that same cold certainty with which he knew two plus two equaled four...

The sensation was false. It had to be.

But convincing the rest of him that knowledge mattered was a different problem entirely.

The power filling his core didnt hurt. It didnt burn the way that ball of spiritual energy had, that slow but intense heat hed felt seeping through his mana lines. This was nothing like that. This felt like pulling off a pair of boots he hadnt realized were too tight, like taking a full, deep breath after so long without one that hed forgotten what breathing right felt like.

Freedom.

The word rose in his mind without invitation. Like something that had been waiting for him.

And in that exact instant, another voice crushed it.

"You must fight against the sensation of freedom, its completely false! The complete opposite!"

Liora.

Not a soft or old blurry memory. It was her exact voice with that particular urgency she only showed when something truly mattered too much to her and there was no longer any time to pretend otherwise.

Sharp and desperate, but not the desperation of someone afraid, the desperation of someone who knew something the others didnt, and couldnt say it fast enough. freewebnovel.com

Ren held onto that.

It was the only thing he could do. He held those words like a fixed point while everything else kept filling up with something that felt glorious and wrong at the same time.

The white haired girl noticed before he opened his mouth.

Her hands stopped, just for a moment. Her eyes, too still and calm for someone her age, moved across him with the same focused attention Ren associated with experienced tamers fighting, not with one person looking at another.

"Dont do that," she said.

No rise in tone or threat behind it... Just the same way youd tell someone their hand was over an open flame, not to scold them, just because it hurts and because the result is predictable and because it makes no sense.

"If you start swimming against the current in here, its going to hurt quite a bit." She paused briefly, almost considerately. "And not because I want it to hurt you. Its just how this works."

Ren met her eyes. "Thanks for the warning."

She studied him for another second, parsing whether there was sarcasm and whether it was worth responding to.

She decided it wasnt.

"You lost something," she said instead. Changing angle with ease Ren found more disturbing than a threat. "Something thats been with you since the beginning of all this." Her partially crystallized fingers slowed as she spoke, as though giving the idea room to settle. "I dont know if you actually liked it, or if you just got used to having it."

Ren said nothing.

But she could see the answer. Read it in the silence that spoke louder than words because it was shaped by loss.

She continued. Her voice dropped one register, not to intimidate, but to make it feel closer. More personal.

"I can give you what it gave you. The function, what it did for you before... Except this time theres nothing holding it back, nothing deciding how fast you can grow and by which path and for what reason." A pause. "Just you."

She was talking about his mushroom... His first beast.

About the silence having occupied this space ever since the familiar presence that had been part of his development as tamer from the beginning had withdrawn behind a wall he himself had built and Ren didnt know how to overcome.

He wouldnt lie to himself saying he didnt miss it.

He missed it. That was the simple uncomfortable truth.

The mushroom had been there from the start. Had been a mentor, a guide and a partner. Almost a friend in a way that transcended normal tamer-beast bonds.

And now it was gone.

Sealed away... Silent.

Leaving a void that nothing else filled no matter how many other friends he had. No matter how strong he became or how many battles he won.

She saw it in his face before he finished processing it himself. A small smile crossed hers, different from the ones before. Less calculated... Like something genuine had surfaced, briefly, beneath everything else.

"There it is," she said quietly. "You dont even have to do much. Just stop resisting completely. Ill handle the rest. We wake it up and you get that back, but without the limits you had before."

The argument had a serious flaw...

It sounded too good.

And Ren knew, with that same cold, analytical part that never went fully quiet, that things which sound too good often carry a cost theyre not showing you yet.

And yet...

The offer was tempting. Dangerously tempting. Because it targeted the exact vulnerability hed been trying to ignore. The exact loss hed been having a hard time pretending didnt matter lately. The exact wound that hadnt healed despite time passing.

Getting his mushroom back. Having that presence again. That voice. That guidance.

Just had to stop resisting.

Just had to surrender.

Such a small price for something so valuable. Such an easy solution to a problem that had been tearing him apart.

Then he saw them.

A fresh vision... Luna, a few meters away, reaching toward him with that particular tension in her expression she normally worked hard to hide. Larissa, shooting some light the artifacts suppression field swallowed before it reached him. Desperation visible even from a distance. Liora looking at him from Lins arms with that expression she had when deeply worried.

And further back, that had been unable to break from the last defensive formation, his parents, pushing forward now that the mutants left them and being pushed back again by the pulses.

His friends, his allies.

"No," Ren said.

The girl didnt react with frustration. She simply turned her attention back to the crack and resumed.

"Fine. Id rather youd relaxed and let this be easy, but you dont have a choice either way."

She pressed forward, and she was right... He couldnt stop her.

That had been clear from the first moment hed tried to move his body in this space and found the paralysis was absolute. His resistance was exactly what shed said: swimming against a current that didnt notice him.

The crack widened under her pressure.
