The bear had no time to react to a threat it couldnt see coming.

The blades impacted with force that transcended normal physical attack and strength.

And the Silver 2 beast with all its robustness and natural tank resistance that should have made it basically invulnerable to Bronze-rank attacks...

SECOND BATTLE

The silence between matches was oppressing.

Team Twelves remaining fighters stood together, discussing strategy in hushed voices that carried undertones of desperation rather than confidence. The bears obliteration had shattered assumptions about what Bronze 2 rank meant when applied to Rens beasts.

Roran stood slightly apart from his remaining teammates, arms crossed, his expression thoughtful rather than defeated.

"I know what youre thinking," he said to the girl preparing to enter second. "You saw what happened to the bear."

"Hard to miss," she responded with dark humor failing at covering her fear. "One hit. Just... gone."

"So dont get hit," Roran suggested, though his tone suggested he knew how hollow that advice sounded. "Lets see... Underground! Make him work for it. I wanna see more."

He paused, meeting her eyes directly.

"Im not sending you thinking youll win. Im being honest about that. But maybe you can learn something about his limits before going down. Maybe tire his beast enough that the next fighter has a chance."

It was brutal calculus but practical.

She nodded slowly, appreciating the honesty even if it stung. "So Im the sacrifice to probe for ceiling?"

"We all are," Roran admitted with grim acceptance. "The question is whether that ceiling even exists at our level, or if were all just small pebbles trying to gauge the height of a mountain."

The second opponent entered the arena with visible caution that came from witnessing what awaited her. The Earth Beast materializing beside her.

Shed seen what happened to the bear with its vaunted tank defenses.

She wasnt going to commit the same error of underestimating the mantiss attack power just because of rank disparity after that...

"Underground, now," she ordered her beast the instant combat began, not wasting a second on probing or testing.

The beast didnt hesitate, sinking into the arena floor like it was liquid rather than solid stone. Earth manipulation at its finest...

Underground it would be practically invisible to normal senses, just like the mantis above. And the mantiss strong water element wouldnt affect it if it went deep enough, earth absorbing moisture without suffering damage.

It was going to be a stalemate strategy rather than victory-seeking. If she couldnt win, at least she could force the beast to exhaust itself trying. Or rather... It didnt move. But were talking about the earth... Not the mantis, since whether the mantis moved nobody could confirm since it remained invisible.
