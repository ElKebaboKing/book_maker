But it encountered an uncomfortable reality at that moment... One that should have been known but forgotten in the speedy reaction, in the heat of the moment.

The Terror basically had no true rear in the conventional sense.

With its multiple legs distributed around its body and multiple hairs sensing vibrations and mana in an extremely sensitive manner, in a configuration that allowed omnidirectional movement, the creature could respond to threats from any angle almost as effectively as from the front.

It wasnt a coincidence that some ambush predators developed that type of advanced sensory adaptation to detect prey they couldnt see, it was an evolutionary advantage that also converted the concept of "vulnerable back" into an illusion.

And Taro had used only three of the mineral balls instead of launching them all in the first wave, only the needed to make it jump. The collision between pure energy attack and the enhanced mineral projectile resulted in a detonation that eclipsed previous explosions in magnitude. But the beam was still more powerful and continued its trajectory after destroying the obstacle, power reduced by the expenditure of piercing through the ball but still sufficient to cause fatal damage.

The Terror attempted covering itself with its legs as well, multiple limbs moving to interpose their tips while adjusting the faces of their remaining transformed mineral to divert the beam, and if that didnt work, even using the additional mass of all the extra legs between the beam and its vulnerable body.
