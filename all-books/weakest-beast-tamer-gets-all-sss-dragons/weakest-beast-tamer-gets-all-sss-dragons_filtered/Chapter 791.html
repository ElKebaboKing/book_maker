And he saw the small knowing smile on Rens face.

He knew, Liu thought with a mix of frustration and genuine respect. He knew the sound attack wouldnt function. Thats precisely why he chose the hydra.

It wasnt just about selecting beasts of similar size for fair confrontation. Wasnt just about wanting to give spotlight to a beast that hadnt shined recently in combat.

It was a perfect tactical selection. Choosing the specific beast that possessed natural advantage against Lius complete arsenal.

Did Ren want to see what Liu could accomplish in this disadvantageous situation?

The hyena could still fight with what remained. Still had its claws, its fangs, considerable physical strength from its Gold rank.

But without its primary sonic advantage that had carried it through previous battles...

It was simply a Gold-rank beast with good defensive armor facing a Gold-rank beast with superior defense and the advantage of two independent heads.

The odds of forcing Ren to deploy his final and presumably most flexible beast, despite the slight lower rank, had just changed dramatically in the wrong direction.

And Liu understood exactly how much the situation had shifted against him.

In the stands above, the audience murmured with growing admiration that spread through the crowd.

Not just admiration for the hydras impressive defensive capabilities.

But for Rens tactical acumen.

Who had converted what appeared to be a balanced battle into an almost assured victory with a single decision about which beast to manifest.

Who had understood the strengths and weaknesses of both sides so completely that hed selected the perfect counter without even considering all the elemental advantages of his other beast most adapted to versatile combat... essentially winning the match before the battle had even truly begun.

Liu frowned while analyzing rapidly. Is he really waiting for me to try something more before counterattacking? Testing my adaptability?

But Ren didnt wait for Liu to formulate a new strategy anymore.

He simply raised one hand in a casual gesture that suggested the demonstration phase was complete and the hydra attacked with an immediate response.

The first head rose smoothly on its serpentine neck. Mouth opening to reveal the glowing interior. Light concentrating in the throat with visible energy buildup.

It wasnt a slow manifestation that telegraphed the attack well in advance. There was no prolonged charging time where the attack announced itself with obvious preparation.

It was almost instantaneous in its execution.

One moment the hydra stood motionless and patient. The next moment, a beam of pure light was shooting forth from the first head with huge devastating force.

It was the signature move that Ren had used many times throughout his academy career with varying degrees of success. The attack that had once cost him considerable effort to manifest properly.

But that had been years in the past. Before the cultivation had reached this current level. Before the second head had emerged. Before the hydra had achieved its present development.

Now Ren could deploy it casually without any significant strain.

The beam of concentrated light crossed the arena in a fraction of a second.

Liu barely had time to react to the threat. "Dodge!" he shouted through their bond with urgency.

The hyena threw itself laterally in a desperate movement. Barely sufficient to avoid a direct impact.

The light beam passed so close that it grazed the hyenas side, coming within centimeters of connecting. So close that Liu felt the searing heat radiating through their mental connection despite not being struck himself.

But the hyena had successfully dodged the attack.

And then Liu realized his critical error.

The first beam hadnt been a genuine attempt to hit at all. It had forced the hyena to move in a specific direction through threat of impact. Had blocked certain evasive options. Had created a predictable path of movement that any experienced fighter could anticipate.

And the hydras second head was already firing before that realization could help.

Another beam of pure light. This time without any warning buildup. Without time for the hyena to dodge because it was still landing and recovering its balance from the first evasive movement.

The second beam impacted directly where it wanted with devastating force.

It struck the hyenas exposed flank with power that resonated through the entire field. Light so brilliantly intense that several spectators had to avert their eyes to avoid being blinded by the glare.
