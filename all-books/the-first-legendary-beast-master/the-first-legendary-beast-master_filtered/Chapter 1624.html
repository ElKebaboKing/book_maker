Chapter 1624: Shades Bond

"Alright, I will teach the skill first, and then I will activate [Chosen Partner], so that Shade can bond with her directly."

"Why do you call him that?" Anilla asked.

"Thats what he said his name is."

The girl nodded, and the Elder smiled, seeing a solution to her problem in sight.

First, Karl activated [Follow Me Little One], and chose to give Anilla the [Beast Space] skill, hoping that it would work now that he was an Immortal.

{Pet Sitter Karl Grants Knowledge} Skill [Beast Space] has been transferred to Shades pet Anilla.

Karl chuckled both at the description, and the way that the [Follow Me, Little One] message had changed with his class change. No longer did it ask them if they wanted the skill, it simply gave it to them.

But the fact that the System already considered her the cats pet boded ill for her future as an independent being.

[Chosen Partner] activated.

{Shade has chosen his partner.}

Anilla was lost in the System Selection screen when the cat vanished, retreating into his new Beast Space to start the necessary renovations.

"Theyre going to need plants, possibly some bedding and other materials. The beast space starts out pretty bare, and needs to be mentally formed, but it works better when there are real objects in there.

What species is he, anyhow?"

"The cat? Hes an orphaned Divine Moor Cat that I picked up a few months back. His mother was hunted for resources, but he was hidden and the Sect doing the hunting didnt notice him.

Or if you mean the girl, shes half Elf."

Karl nodded. "I guessed that part. Its also why she is late to activate her System. Elves mature more slowly, even half Elves do, though not to the same extreme. Thats why she hadnt awakened yet.

She likely wouldnt have activated a class until close to twenty, thats the usual age for them, as I remember."

The Elder shook her head. "Youre still young. The competition between Immortal born is fierce. Here, even the Elves find a way to force early awakening so that their children arent vulnerable for decades.

Ten years is already a long time to be locked down, protecting the children. But for Elves, where their children dont usually awaken for fifty years? Its an eternity to prevent the news of a powerful members weakness from being used against them."

"Ah, so thats how it is. While the children dont have a class, theyre a target painted on your back, easy leverage to get an Ascended Immortal to do something unsavoury."

The Elder nodded. "Have you made plans for when your wife gets pregnant?"

Karl smiled. "Actually, those plans were made for me. You see, the beasts are very protective of their Dana Mage, and now weve got an Immortal Rank Behemoth in the group.

Hes also got a fresh clutch of his own to protect, so I suspect that not many will be able to get past him."

"When did you go hunting for a Behemoth?"

"He was mortal, Mythic Rank, and just advanced. He was actually the second member of my team, other than Hawk."

"Ah, so thats how it is. Well, I cant say that youre wrong to want a Behemoth on your side, assuming that you can manage to feed him. Thats always the tricky part with the larger species.

Even this Divine Moor Cat is a pain to feed, as they will often refuse anything but specific wild beast meats."

Hawk laughed at her comment. When you tried to keep a wild beast as a pet, what else could they do in the face of overwhelming power but annoy you with small things like being a picky eater?

After over a minute of standing immobile, Anilla finally returned to the land of the living, and ran over to hug Karl.

"Its amazing. My Class awakened, and I got to bond with Shade, and Ive got all sorts of Ranger type skills available in my skill tree. Even my Class abilities are good."

Karl patted her head, while the Elder questioned her daughter.

"So, what Class did it give you, and what were your Class Skills? I will arrange tutors and supplemental training to help you catch up."

The little half Elf looked horrified at the impending workload, but dutifully answered.

"My Class is Forest Stalker, and I have Bow Specialty, Silent Movement, and Shadow Meld as Class Skills. Well, then Ive also got [Shred] and [Ambush] from Shade. Bonding with a Divine Moor Cat grants me their innate skills."

That was better than even Karl had expected.

Most Rangers would be able to use a skill from their beasts, whether it was an innate one, or something that their Class granted. But getting to use both of the Divine Moor Cats innate skills was going to be amazing for her advancement.

Silent Movement, Shadow Meld and Ambush combined with a [Shred] enhanced arrow would be a huge first strike bonus for her.

As a hunting focused ranger class, there wasnt much better that she could hope for at the very start of her progression.

The Elder nodded slowly, making a tutoring plan for the girl as she processed the skill names.

"Alright, I know what you need, and I will get you started tonight. Evening lessons will be most beneficial to you, as a stealth-based hunting class with a Moor Cat bonded.

You should go rest now, youve got a long night ahead of you, and I need to talk to the Karl for a moment."

Anilla bowed formally, then ran back inside, eager to gloat to her brothers that she now had an even better class than they did. And it really was an amazing class.

"Alright, you managed to pull through and bound the girl with the best possible beast for a young Elf. So I wont be stingy with the reward.
