Chapter 1401: Convincing Fake

Mildred smiled as she saw Rues new form.

"You know, with that appearance, nobody would ever know that you were not born a pure-blooded dragon. If anything, theyll think your pointy ears are a quirk of your transformation.

I dont sense any sort of non draconic lineage in you at all while youre transformed." She informed the young Princess.

"Now, we just need to update your skills so that you can properly show off your potential, and youll be fit to take over the world. Or at least your own hometown or something." Cara joked.

Rue giggled at her. "Well, if we work on that meditation thingie, it should help, right? All I have to do is make it to Totem Ranked before adulthood, and Ill be considered a prodigy.

That sounds easy enough. Ive already come this far so fast. Just a little bit more, and Ill be a Totem Ranked juvenile, and everyone will have to fear my potential."

Lukas sighed. "I think that they already do. Thats why were here, remember? But if you can advance even one more time, youll start getting attention from our father, who will start wanting to arrange a good marriage for you.

All of our sisters have married into strong alliances, even the ones with mediocre potential.

Its not a secret that the King has always been fond of you, so he will definitely try to find you something good."

Rue nodded. "Thats probably inevitable. But if he sends me away, how am I supposed to finish my life goals?"

"You have life goals already? Im impressed." Mildred joked.

Rue nodded. "I want to be powerful enough to make everyone who bullied me when I was weak apologize. Not just the fake apologies that they do when they get caught breaking laws and want to get out of trouble, either."

The consort smiled. "I can understand that one. When we first got together, everyone was afraid that my Lukas was going to name me his wife, and that would make me the Crown Princess. That would have been a disaster for me, and for most of the ones who were still hoping to get the position.

So, they were horrible to me for months.

But after a while it became clear that my position was settled and that I didnt want the position of the main wife. Im happy where Im at, and I fully understand that the position of Crown Princess will go to a political wife, a marriage to make the Kingdom stronger."

"Thats ... surprisingly realistic of you." Karl noted.

Mildred giggled. "You just havent realized how much an official wife can hate the consort that her husband actually cares about. Nine times out of ten, they hate each other, but only a madwoman would actually try to harm her and risk her position as Queen.

Oddly, that doesnt seem to apply to King candidates. If they marry in to a ruling Queens family, they all tend to go power hungry and forget that theyre the live-in in-laws, not the real ruler."

Cara winked at her. "You need to keep them in their place, or they get uppity. Its a guy thing. I wish wed brought Sister Rae with us. Shes a master of making people behave."

Karl didnt miss that Mildred was clearly talking about the current Queen, who had most likely killed Rues mother. She might be politically strong, but from what he had learned so far, it was more like the grudging respect for a tyrant, than loyalty to a leader.

"I think that we should work on having Rue build up friendships among other Royals as well. Not just local ones, but some from neighbouring countries. If people respect her as a leader, then, no matter where she ends up, she will have some solid backing when she arrives." He suggested.

They were aiming to have her be the Queen of Gathuzan, but he wasnt going to say that outright.

Prince Lukas nodded. "That is a wonderful idea. If she has allies among the other nations Royals, she will be able to more easily advance. Now, youve taught her the basics of the methods needed already, and Im sure that you have some more resources for her, but there is always something that a dragon will need to help them grow, and its often in another country.

For a Royal to travel is a complicated issue.

We might have better access to resources, but a lot of that relies upon our ability to convince another nations leader to let us go get it.

Like when I advanced to Mythic Rank. I needed a particular potion to help cleanse some lingering damage that regular healing wouldnt touch. So, I had to ask the Hill Dwarves to help me meet with the Alchemist that could make it.

That sort of connection is everything for a Royal."

Karl nodded, and Rue smiled as she began to understand. "And, if the guests like me, then its impossible to bully me in public."

"Youre not wrong. But its so much more than that. Youll understand once you start making the connections. The ability to move freely among other Royals and know that they are allies makes social events so much easier. Theyre not part of the local power struggle, so you can talk to them and build political or business relationships, while ignoring the locals who are giving you headaches," Lukas explained.

"That sounds lovely. Alright, Mister Karl, do your best. Ill rely on you to help me make it through my advancements so that I can be that sort of Royal, while big brother Lukas teaches me how to make friends with Royalty." Rue laughed.

Karl gave her a thumbs up, while Cara wrapped an arm around her and stage whispered in her ear.

"Dont worry, weve got lots of tricks to help you advance. Now that we know youve got the potential, we can even push the first advancements so that youll be ahead of the others.

It might not be fun, but I am sure that we can do it."
