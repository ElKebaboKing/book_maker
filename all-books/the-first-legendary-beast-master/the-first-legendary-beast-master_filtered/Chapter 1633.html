Chapter 1633: Interesting Things

Cara had already flown away, looking for more interesting things. But the druid wasnt waiting, he transformed back into a squirrel and ran back into the trees, scrambling up the branches to the same spot she had snatched him from.

It was still within sight of the gates, so under normal circumstances, it was considered perfectly safe.

Nobody had expected Cara to simply snatch him out of the tree.

Not even him.

The group kept going, keeping up a steady jog down the road as Leo spread [Indomitable] to the whole group so they didnt get tired and slow down before they found the good stuff.

{Ooh, Karl, turn right. I can smell magical flowers to the right.} Remi insisted.

Hawk looked down at the area she was thinking of, but didnt see any flowers at all.

It wasnt even forest, just grass and shrubs, but he didnt see any flowers from where he was circling overhead with Dana. Perhaps they were too far away to see the little stuff?

That shouldnt be the case, his eyes were excellent.

Hawk flew low, looking for the plant, but he still couldnt see any flowers at all. However, Remi had slithered down off Karls shoulders, and she was digging under a bush using four shovels at the same time.

So, it was a subterranean flower.

Now Hawk didnt feel bad about not seeing it. Perhaps that was the trick to finding resources here? If they were too easy to spot, they would be gone already.

So, he couldnt rely on his eyes, he would have to focus on finding anomalies in the mana that might be a magical plant in hiding.

Remi emerged from the hole covered in dirt with a truffle in her hands and a smile on her face.

{This is going to be an excellent resource for later. I dont know if it works in Alchemy, but it definitely works with cooking. Ive definitely had truffle mushroom sauce for steak before.} She declared.

The other Clan members winced at the thought. Those truffles were a rare magical resource, worth multiple Clan Credits each, and she had a whole cluster of them in her arms.

Her find would fund their resource use for half a year.

But the Naga fully intended to find a way to eat it.

Remi put the plant into her swamp, on a bit of a hill, so that the water element plant was kept moist, but not submerged below the water line, where it would rot.

That should be perfect for its continued growth, and once it was settled, she would split it up to replant it in dozens of different spots.

{And on to the next one. I still smell flowers in the area.}

Mark gave her a suspicious look. Did Naga smell like that? Didnt they do the tongue thing like snakes?

But Remi was vanishing into the bushes again, nearly invisible when she was keeping low to the ground, thanks to her slender body. The only one that could easily track her was Hawk, whose senses were keenly attuned to finding things that moved in the grass.

This time, Hawk managed to find the plant that she was looking for. He couldnt see it, as it was below water level in a small pond, but when he circled it, the rest of the group came to see what they had found.

Remi vanished into the water, and then came back with a lotus plant that was out of flower.

Normally, that would mean that it wasnt ready for harvest, but Remi moved it to her space anyhow. She would keep it growing, and then she would get all the flowers for herself.
