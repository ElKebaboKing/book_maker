Chapter 434: Resonance Eclipse Devouring

I found myself back in the realm, sitting quietly in the garden of the floating ruined castle. The air was still, carrying the faint scent of old stone and flowers that had somehow survived among the cracks.

The Emperor’s words were still echoing faintly in my mind, but I had already let his words slip away. They didn’t matter to me right now.

My thoughts were locked on something far more important, the changes in my status. For the last five days I had pushed myself and I was admiring the changes.

As I looked at my status, a deep wave of satisfaction washed over me. It was the reason I honestly didn’t care about Lucien’s worries regarding Peanu. In truth, I felt certain I could fold Lucien, the so-called strongest man in our world in half if I truly wished to.

I could see through him completely. His body, his limits, even a rough outline of his stats floated in my mind.

Strength: somewhere between 1200–1500

Constitution: around 800–1000

Dexterity: maybe 700–900

Psynapse: between 1200–1500

My eyes then fell to my own Psynapse stat, the only one that had crossed into a completely new threshold—Omega. That single breakthrough had nearly destroyed me.

The day my Psynapse crossed 4000 was the day I went through the worst pain of my life. It felt as if my mind had been torn apart, disintegrating piece by piece. I was both present and absent at the same time, drifting in a state that felt alien. And then I understood why, because in that instant, I had glimpsed something far beyond human comprehension.

Essence itself had poured into my brain, reshaping it, enhancing it. But during that process, something else happened.

The world itself shifted. Colors changed. And then, for a single second, I saw the fundamental runes of existence. I witnessed the building blocks, as if I were staring at the structure of a single atom of Essence with my naked eyes.

That one second shattered my mind. The unbearable pain came from my brain failing to comprehend what it was seeing.

But when the process ended, I felt... different. My mind was no longer the same, it was like a machine I had never used to its fullest before.

The first thing I noticed was that six Psynapse fractures had opened within me, waiting to be used. And, of course, no law was beyond my grasp anymore. My minor laws advanced rapidly, almost naturally. Even the Minor Law of Space rose to level 5.

But that wasn’t the only thing. I also comprehended something entirely new, the Minor Law of Resonance.

The inspiration had come from Ragnar.

I remembered watching him fight, watching him unleash his so-called Sonic Fist. He compressed the air, and a cone of high-energy sound blasted outward, obliterating everything in its path. I had been fascinated.

I decided to replicate it. Of course, mine had always turned out stronger than his, sometimes too strong. If I controlled it too tightly, it never felt the same. But then, through trial and error, I stumbled upon something different.

I matched the frequency of his attack, perfectly.

The moment I succeeded, our combined strike didn’t just destroy. It shattered everything before it in a way that was devastating beyond words. That was when I understood what I had touched.

Resonance.

The Minor Law of Resonance was about frequency, vibration, and synchronization. At its core, it dictated how energies, elements, and even laws could interact when their frequencies aligned or when they clashed.

Where Assimilation was about absorbing and adapting, and Convergence was about combining, Resonance was about harmony and discord. It could amplify beyond limits by synchronization or cause collapse by creating instability.

Next, my eyes moved to my skills, and the sight brought a wide grin to my face. I had made massive progress there too.

Almost all of my skills had reached level 7, and that wasn’t by accident, I had pushed for it deliberately, grinding until I was certain each one crossed that mark.

But what excited me most were the two new skills that appeared on my list: Devouring Horizon and Eclipse Convergence. Just reading their names gave me chills. They sounded like disasters waiting to be unleashed, and I couldn’t wait to try them on Peanu.

The thought of tearing through our enemies in Peanu with those skills and their upgraded versions almost made my blood boil with anticipation.

And then there was the last change, the one I had been holding back on.

My Ability.

Apex Body – II (Passive).

The ability had already evolved several times before.

The first time had been when I reached 50 in every stat.

Then it had happened again at 200, and once more at 800.

From that pattern, I had assumed the next threshold would be 3200. So I forced my stats past 3200 except Strength which sat at 3190.

I pulled out the description of my ability once.
