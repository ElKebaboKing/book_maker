Chapter 21: Dodge, Punch, Repeat: How to Not Becoming Dog Food

The second was riskier but could be worthwhile, try increasing my talent to Level 2 and hope it unlocks something useful.

And finally, the last option... use Essence in a way I hadn't tried before.

After considering all my options, I decided the best approach would be to level up to 9, raise my talent to Level 2, and experiment with Essence to see if I could do something useful.

I was on a tight timeline, I had no idea how long the pillar and its lightning defenses would hold against the hounds. If they managed to snatch whatever Arkas had left on it, that would be a serious problem.

Keeping my eyes closed, I focused on the Essence swirling in my heart.

Then, I activated [Essence Manipulation], separated a chunk of Essence, equivalent to one unit and guided it through my body, starting from my left side and moving to my right. I continued circulating the Essence throughout my body for another hour, letting it seep into every muscle and fiber.

Our goal was simple: hunt whatever we could find. I knew finding high-level prey wouldn't be easy, but with the hounds moving toward the valley checkpoint, we could intercept them along the way and maybe squeeze out a level from the hunt.

"Power Up."

The Generator roared to life.

The first Level 5 hound was within range.

They were too weak.

Either way, I needed a challenge to progress from here.

And if my theory was wrong? Well, that would be a lesson learned the hard way.

I stood up and took a deep breath.

Jumping down from the tree, I started walking toward the cluster of hounds surrounding the lightning-coated pillar, each step measured and steady.

I checked my Essence reserves, 5/5, back to full. I had spent the last hour recharging before coming here.

"Power up," I muttered. My heart thrummed as the generator roared to life.

I activated my skill, [Essence Manipulation], and channeled a surge of Essence into my legs.
