Chapter 332: Dance Of Shadows And Death

The portal chamber roared with power.

The glow from the four massive teleportation circles pulsed faintly beneath the stone floor, casting flickering shadows across the vast hall.

Knight stood tall in the center, his obsidian fur rippling with dark Essence, horn gleaming faintly with a deep crimson hue. The air around him writhed, thick with shadows and streaked with the faint scent of blood.

Three old Masters circled him like vultures, their battle-worn robes tattered and stained. Lines of exhaustion cut across their faces, mingling with confusion and unease. Each had summoned their weapons, and all bore varying degrees of injuries from the relentless assault.

The oldest among them narrowed his eyes and muttered, "What kind of creature is this? I cant even see his level."

One of the others replied cautiously, "Im not sure. Hes a pantherclearlybut with space affinity. I remember overhearing something about an Abomination like this... but this one does not look like an Abomination."

Knight stood motionless before them, shrouded in swirling shadow and faint crimson mist. He made no move to respond, either because he didnt understand them, or more likely, because he simply didnt care.

He remained crouched, a low growl rumbling from his chest, claws tapping rhythmically against the floor. The shadows at his feet pulsed, alive, waiting.

One of the Masters moved first, vanishing in a blink and reappearing behind Knight, blade already swinging down toward his back. But the attack sliced only air. Knight had already vanished into the shadows.

The second Master turned instinctively.

Knight reappeared behind him, horn glowing with compressed energy. A black beam of concentrated force and shadows fired straight and, in the blink of an eye, vanishedteleported mid-flight.

The beam reappeared inches away from the third Masters chest.

BOOM!!!

"Stay together!" the oldest barked. "Watch each others backs!"

The man dodged, only to find Knight already behind him, stepping through a shadow vertex in space.

Blood spurted as Knights shadow tendrils shot up from the ground and wrapped around the mans arms and legs, pulling him down like a predator anchoring its prey.

Knights claws came down next, tearing through the mans chest in a single, fluid strike.

Blood gurgled from the Masters mouth as his eyes went wide with shock and pain.

Knight didnt pause. His claws dug deeper, slicing through flesh and bone until they reached the mans heart, then shredded it without hesitation.

The mans body jerked once, then went limp in Knights grip, lifeless and broken.

But Knight didnt flinch.

The shadows swirling around his body surged upward with a hiss, forming into sleek, black spears that launched from every direction, fifty in total, meeting the golden barrage midair.

The two storms of spears clashed with violent precision, flashes of light and darkness exploding in the air, shaking the very walls of the chamber.

And in the chaos, Knight vanished, swallowed by the shadows.

The Masters eyes widened.

The Master howled in pain, but Knight gave him no chance to recover.

He lunged forward, claws extended, and grabbed the mans head in one hand.

With brutal force, Knight charged, dragging the struggling body with him and then slammed the Masters skull straight into a nearby pillar.

The marble beneath them cracked, then shattered with a deep boom as the man crumpled lifeless in Knights grip, blood spilling freely down the broken stone.

Suddenly, shouts echoed from the halls entrance.

Knight turned his head, still holding the corpse, and watched as four more figures stepped into the chamber. New enemies, each radiating the strength of a seasoned Master.

The last remaining old guard barked a threat.

"Release him now, or you wont be leaving this place alive!"

He spat it out like trash.

"Form up! Hes alone!"

But Knight didnt need to dodge.

Instead, he lowered his body, shadows swirling tighter around him, and then

He vanished.

BOOM!
