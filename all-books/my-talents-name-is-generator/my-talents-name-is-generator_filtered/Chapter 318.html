Chapter 317: Atleast My Mind Is Alpha Plus

I grinned and spoke, "And you wont be the only one playing with space this time."

I transferred all the Essence I had in Psynapse.

The panther growled low, eyes narrowing as if it understood me and then it charged.

I held my ground.

This time, I wasnt just reacting. I wanted to test my new understanding. I was going to do exactly what it had done to me in our last fight and see if my level one space control could hold up.

As it ran, I raised my hand and pointed forward. My perception spread outward, covering the space between us in a heartbeat.

I felt the spatial particles around me, the tiny threads that made up the fabric of the air, the distance, the points in between. It was like sensing the invisible structure of the world itself.

At the tip of my finger, a faint glow formed. A narrow beam of light gathered, focused and clean.

I fired.

The beam zipped through the air toward the panther but before it hit, I reached out with my mind and shaped the space between us. I mimicked what the panther had done.

A ripple shot out from my finger and another from a fixed point just ahead of me. I pulled the space tight between them, bent it, folded it like a sheet and opened a linked tunnel between two fixed points. A single, directed transfer.

The beam that had flown straight suddenly bent unnaturally and reappeared at the panthers side, coming from the ripple point.

It saw it coming a second too late.

The beam scraped along its side, cutting a clean line through its fur. It hissed and rolled away, tail lashing, more alert now.

I grinned wider. "Oh yeah, this is gonna be fun."

The panther didnt slow. It came at me with renewed speed, vanishing mid-charge into a pool of flickering shadows.

But this time I was ready.

I didnt look for its form, I looked for the space. And sure enough, a moment later, I felt it. The tug at my position, the way the space particles trembled near me. My presence in the world was being challenged again.

I raised my hand and whispered, "[Space Lock]."

The world shimmered. I carved a cube of sealed space right above my head, just where it had appeared last time. The moment the beast flickered into view, it was already surrounded by the violet shimmer of locked space.

Its claws came out mid-leap, but they hit the cube and not me.

BOOM!

The panthers body bounced off the inner wall of the space cage. It twisted midair and landed back inside, growling furiously.

I let it out after two seconds, just enough to make a point.

"Lets call that... payback," I said casually.

The panther roared and rushed again, clearly angrier now.

I stepped back and activated [Sovereign Absorption]. The vortex formed behind my back, waiting.

I flicked my fingers and cast a weaker light beam, letting the panther see it. It dodged easily but just as it prepared to blink again, I manipulated the space in front of me. This time I didnt create a tunnelI compressed the distance itself.

Instead of traveling a full twenty meters, my beam only had to travel five. The rest was skipped over. The panther didnt expect the hit.

The beam hit it directly in the shoulder.

It howled and rolled across the ground again. Dust and stone flew as it skidded back.

"Come on," I said, waving it forward. "Lets see whos better at playing with space."

It came again, claws flashing. I didnt retreat.

Instead, I raised my hand....not to block, but to strike.

I channeled the Essence to my fingertips and willed space itself to split. The idea wasnt to throw a physical blow, but to cleave the space between us. Like a sword slash made not of metal, but of space...not existing.

A shimmer formed in the airbarely visible, like a ripple on glass.

I dragged my arm down through the air, mimicking a sword swing.

For a heartbeat, nothing happened.

Then the space between us cracked open in a sharp, distorted line. It wasnt clean, not yet. The edges trembled, unstable, and the pressure it gave off was weak. But it moved fast.

The distorted wave of cleaved space raced forward and hit the panther in its other shoulder.

The strike didnt cut it deeply, but it staggered, growling in surprise and pain as its paw buckled. It fell sideways with a thud, claws scraping at the ground to stay upright.

I kept my hand raised, eyes locked on the fading shimmer.

"That worked," I muttered. "Not well, but it worked."

The cleave wasnt powerful, it wasnt even sharp enough to cut anything solid. But it disrupted the panthers balance and position. A twist in space, not a wound.

I exhaled slowly, letting the new understanding settle deeper into my mind.

After that, I didnt move. I just stood there.

The angry panther roared and charged at me again. It began throwing everything it hadclaws, punches, dark beams from its horn, even those writhing shadowy tentacles but I remained still.

A faint violet vortex shimmered around me, and I absorbed the energy behind every blow. My body took the hits, but my core took the power.

The numbers in my Essence storage kept ticking up with each blocked or redirected strike.
