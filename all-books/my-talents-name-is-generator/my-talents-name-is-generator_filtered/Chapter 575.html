[System Chronicle Log - XXXX - Node Delta 5]

In a distant realm where almost no one ever went, the air was so thick with Essence that it blurred everything in sight. The entire place shimmered with color and mist, and the flow of time itself felt slow and uneven. It was here that Aurora, one of the Defenders, resided.

She was known throughout the ages as someone loyal only to the Prime Universe itself, not to any god or power. Many called her the most impartial of all Defenders, a guardian who only moved when balance was truly threatened.

For a long time, nothing had disturbed this quiet realm. But suddenly, the air trembled. Essence twisted violently, and a figure appeared out of the mist. The shape was hard to see, shrouded completely in light and shadow.

Aurora looked around calmly before speaking, her voice soft but clear.

"Hm... a new quest?"

There was a hint of surprise in her tone. "How long has it been since you issued another one?" she muttered.

She raised her hand, and Essence around her began to move, forming patterns in the air. Dozens of glowing screens appeared, each showing symbols, text, and moving images from the System. She read them slowly, her eyes tracing the flow of the quest that had just been given.

The air fell silent for a while after that. Then she sighed.

"Him again?" she said quietly. "Sometimes I wonder who is more dangerous: the Eternals, or him."

Her voice echoed softly through the mist before turning firm again.

"Still... why was no one told about this? A new Executor is not a small thing. It should not be kept from us."

The light from the screens slowly dimmed, one after another, until only faint streams of Essence drifted in the air like fog. Aurora stood still, waiting for a reply from the System but none came. The silence stretched on.

She exhaled softly. "So, things are getting worse, huh?" Her tone carried a quiet bitterness. "Of all the choices you had, you decided to gamble on the Executor? You understand, dont you? Even you cant predict Him."

Aurora wasnt speaking to anyone visible, but she knew she was being heard. The System never truly ignored her; it simply refused to answer.

The silence deepened, the only sound being the soft hum of Essence swirling around her. After a few moments, she gave a small nod.

"Fine," she said finally. "Ill accept the quest. But remember this, theres only so long you can hide that boy from the eyes of the Eternals... and from us. I hope youre ready for what follows."

Her voice lingered in the air for a moment before her form began to fade. The Essence folded back into stillness, and the realm returned to its usual quiet emptiness, as though she had never been there at all.
