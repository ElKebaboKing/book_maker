It was like taking a blade and cutting that truth out of the world, stripping it of all its lifelines. With this, I could shut down an enemys weapon, slice them away from their own power, or even cage a law effect until it collapsed on itself. Pure, silent execution.

The kind of thing you only flip on when youre sure the next few minutes decide everything. But, I had a feeling that it will change again in future. Maybe it will upgrade itself on the next rank up.

The raw numbers made me pause. Each stat jump here was enough to shift the balance in a fight on its own. Combined? It was a complete overhaul. The boost to Psynapse meant my control over Essence would be even smoother.

The rush of Essence into my body stopped, and I felt lighter, like invisible chains holding me back had finally snapped.

I turned my eyes toward the three of them.

Lyrate was still floating in the air, her hair glowing faintly. Her beauty had always been striking, but now it felt sharper, almost too perfect to be real.

There was an air of power around her, not just strength but grace, like she belonged on a throne. The black cape behind her fluttered without wind.

When her lips moved, I almost thought I imagined it.

"That hurt a little." she said, her voice smooth and calm. "Finally....some power."

My eyes widened. "You... can talk?"

Her brows furrowed as she brought her floating body down on the ground.

"What do you mean? I could always talk."

Now that made me even more confused.

Before I could respond, Knight stepped forward. His dark fur looked sleeker now, the runes along his limbs brighter, sharper. His frame had grown a little, his shadow stretching unnaturally behind him.

He stopped right in front of me, silent for a moment. Then, in a low, serious tone, he said, Billion.

I blinked, surprised by hearing the panthers voice directly in my head.

Why, he continued, did the lightning bolt break up with the cloud?

I stared at him. "...What?"

He tilted his head, completely deadpan. Because it found someone more striking.

I blinked, my jaw dropping.

"The first time you can speak... and you start with a dad joke?"

He gave a low growl, then muttered, Hmmm.

I did not imagine them being able to speak. Curious, I turned my head toward Silver.

Silvers body was visibly bigger now, his wings spread wide with a metallic shimmer running along the feathers. His eyes were bright and almost glowing with excitement.

Finally! he shouted, grinning from ear to ear. Do you know how boring it is to fight and not yell cool stuff while doing it?

I nodded. "I can imagine."

No, you cant! Silver shot back, laughing. But now I can talk and when we fight next, youre going to hear me shouting the names of my moves so loud the enemy will run before I even hit them.

I groaned and said.

"You know that other people will only hear you screech because you are speaking in my head."

Silver shook his large head and responded.

Doesnt matter, you three will hear me, thats enough.

Knight chimed in.

I like silence. Please do not shout.

Lyrate suddenly spoke up.

"Yes. Shouting is for uncouth, unrefined people. But considering you are a beast, I cannot expect much."

I moved my head between each of them. There were two voices in my mind from Knight and Silver, my own voice echoing, and then Lyrates calm, regal tones.

Silver laughed softly.

Cmon, Knight, lighten up! You cant be serious all the time.

Knights growl rumbled in my head.

I am never serious.

Lyrate rolled her eyes.

"I suppose the two of you will need constant supervision if you are to behave like civilized beings."

I chuckled, shaking my head.

"Great. Now I have four voices arguing in my head. This is going to be fun."

Silvers voice perked up.

Fun? Finally! I like the sound of that!

Knight muttered, Silence is good.
