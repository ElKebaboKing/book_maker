Chapter 728: The Eclipse Anchors Core

Layered slabs of blackened material fused together by laws rather than matter, pulsing faintly with a rhythm that felt uncomfortably close to a heartbeat.

The Eclipse Anchors core.

A sphere of condensed matter, suspended in an array of sealing constructs that rotated slowly around it.
