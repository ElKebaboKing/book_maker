**** [Random Demon PoV]

The battlefield had already been raging for hours.

Far out in the void, a massive tear in space shimmered like a wound carved into reality itself. The rift stretched wide across the darkness, its edges bleeding unstable colors that twisted and shifted between all kinds of colors. From within its depths came the constant hum of foreign energy pushing against the fabric of the Prime Universe.

A Grade Four rift.

In front of it stood the defensive structures raised by the invading force. Three massive layers of constructs floated across the void, each filled with thousands of abominations and phantoms guarding the entrance. Their grotesque forms drifted and lunged through the battlefield like living storms of corruption.

Closer to the rift itself rose a towering black structure that stood on the layer closest to the rift. The tower stood silent and unmoving while deathmist pulsed through its surface.

The Eternal commander waited inside.

Across the battlefield, a sea of demon warriors clashed with the creatures pouring from the defensive lines. Blazing weapons carved arcs of fire through the darkness while roars of battle echoed through the void. Abominations the size of warships lunged forward with claws dripping corruption while phantoms streaked through the demon ranks like flickering shadows.

High above the battlefield, the demon commander floated alone.

His crimson armor reflected the flashes of battle below as he watched the fight with calm patience. His hands were clasped behind his back, but his fingers had curled tightly into fists.

He was not watching the battle.

He was waiting.

Below him several Grandmasters and Transcendent demon warriors fought with measured restraint, focusing on defensive formations rather than pushing forward.

One of them glanced toward the sky while deflecting the claws of a lunging abomination.

"How long do we keep holding like this?"

Another demon beside him shattered an abomination with a sweeping strike before answering.

"They only told us he was coming today."

"That was hours ago."

"Well they didnt say when today," the second demon replied grimly. "Just hold the line."

The first demon growled under his breath.

"Easy for you to say."

A third voice cut in from nearby.

"Stop complaining and defend the formation. If the commander says wait, we wait."

The demons tightened their defensive perimeter as another wave of creatures surged toward them.

Above the battlefield, the demon commander suddenly lifted his head.

Something had changed.

A ripple spread across space.

At first it was subtle, like a stone dropped into a silent lake. But the disturbance expanded rapidly, rolling outward through the void in widening waves that distorted the battlefield itself.

Every warrior froze for a fraction of a second. The ripple grew larger and larger. Soon the disturbance spread across the entire battlefield, bending space itself like water.

Even the abominations hesitated. From the black tower near the rift, a tall figure flashed out.

The Eternal commander had sensed it too.

Then space split open.

A vortex formed above the battlefield, its swirling edges twisting with controlled spatial force. The massive gateway stabilized instantly, dwarfing even the largest warships drifting through the void.

From within the vortex, a single figure stepped forward.

A long dark jacket drifted behind him as he floated calmly into open space, its fabric moving like a banner in the void. The design was simple yet striking, flowing down to his knees.

On the back of the jacket burned a single emblem.

Order of Absolute.

For a brief moment the battlefield went quiet.

The demons stared upward.

Then the demon commander exhaled slowly.

"Hes here."
