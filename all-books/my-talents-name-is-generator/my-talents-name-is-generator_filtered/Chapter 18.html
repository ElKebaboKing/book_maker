Five days had passed since I started my routine, Six days in total since Arkas had randomly dropped us here to die.

I wondered what the old man was doing right now. Probably sipping tea, watching us struggle to survive, and laughing at the thought of us shitting in the wild.

In the past five days, I had located a water source, one of the rivers, but I hadn't found anyone else from my unit.

My self-training had been going well, especially the Essence training. Every day, I infused Essence into my right leg, let it wreck some part of it, and observed how the damage played out.

At this point, I was pretty sure my right leg was screaming discrimination. And no, I wasn't a masochist.

The reason I focused only on my right leg was to see if repeatedly exposing it to Essence would lead to any long-term changes. But with such a small amount of Essence, the experiment would take time before showing any results.

Until then... my right leg would just have to deal with it.

I stood on a thick tree branch, peering down at my next prey.
