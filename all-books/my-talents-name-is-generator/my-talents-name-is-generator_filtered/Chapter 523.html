Her hand slid to the back of my head, pulling me closer, deepening the kiss until the world itself seemed to slow down around us. Her lips were soft and full of care.

We spent the next hour together, talking about everything. She told me about her training, her progress toward reaching Grandmaster rank, and the quest she had received.

Before I left, I promised her again, this time more seriously that I wouldnt go off alone on my new adventure. She didnt say anything, just gave me a look that said shed believe it when she saw it.

Once I stepped out of the hut, I reached out through my Psynapse, connecting with the world core, and checked on my summons.

Next was Knight. He was pure motion, darting between abominations so fast that even my senses through the world core could barely track him. By the time one creature fell, he was already behind the next, slicing through them with terrifying precision. He was already on his fourth zone, leaving trails of shredded remains behind.

And finally, Silver. I found him soaring high above his battlefield, two colossal tornadoes spinning at his sides. Wind roared like thunder as the abominations below were crushed and torn apart. The few that survived barely had a second to react before Silver dove down, ripping through them effortlessly.

Watching them all, I couldnt help but smile. It was hard not to feel a strange sense of pride.

Once I was sure they were working enthusiastically, crushing everything in their paths without hesitation, I let my senses drift outward, searching for Dante. But his presence was like smoke; no matter how hard I tried to pin it down, it slipped through my grasp.

I wasnt worried, though. Dante had a way of appearing exactly when he wanted to, and I was certain hed find me once he finished his preparations.

Taking a steady breath, I stepped forward, then launched myself upward, tearing through the sky.

The air roared past me as I broke through the thin veil of clouds, ripping across space until the land beneath me turned into endless blue.

Far below stretched the ocean. A small piece of land floated at the heart of it all, a lonely island thick with trees and vegetation but devoid of life.

I descended slowly, letting the wind fade from my ears as I landed at the center of the island.

With a quiet exhale, I sat down cross-legged.

I ignored everything else as soon as my eyes locked onto the Laws section. That was where the real problem lay for me.

To cross into the next rank, the requirements were simple on paper, reach level 300, complete the advancement quest, and comprehend a major law.

For most people, this was a linear path. They would start with a smaller aspect of a law, like space compression or thermal conversion and then refine it, deepen their understanding, and evolve it into a major law.

But my situation was... different. For me, each so-called minor law was already vast, complex, and layered, almost as if I had taken bites out of entire laws themselves.

The deeper I went, the more it felt like I wasnt learning fragments but shaping the entire structure from the ground up.

That meant pushing one of them to the level of a major law wasnt just hard, it was like trying to build a mountain from the dust of galaxies.

Still, I wasnt planning to stop.

My focus drifted to another area that had been burning in my thoughts lately, the domain. The way the Phantoms domain had worked still lingered in my mind.

It wasnt just a manifestation of power; it was alive, flexible, and terrifyingly efficient. It didnt just suppress or expand, it rewrote the battlefield itself.

My own domain, Absolute, was born from my class and my comprehension of several laws. It existed as a passive authority that enforced my will subconsciously. But now, with time and the Law of Time itself resonating faintly within me, I saw a way to evolve it further.

I wanted a domain that wasnt just passive, but active, one that fought alongside me.
