Chapter 563: Against The Transcendent

Before the energy could even settle, three more detonations followed.

Knights shadow dome collapsed inward and burst open like a collapsing star, releasing a surge of condensed darkness that ripped through the Ferans trapped inside.

Ragnar raised his club, slammed it forward, and a repulsive shockwave of silver force radiated outward, breaking bones and armor alike.

Even Vynor and Princess Velaira were thrown off balance, their defenses cracked under the combined pressure.

All of them appeared beside me then a single blur flickered beside me. Dante appeared, wiping blood off his hand, and with a careless flick of his wrist, tossed away the severed head of a Feran grandmaster.

He smirked faintly. "Id say that got his attention."

I stared at Vaelix.

Even with the corpses of his kin floating around him, his expression didnt change. Not a flicker of emotion.

"I dont think he cares," I said quietly.

"He doesnt," Anjee muttered, his jaw clenched, fury simmering beneath his calm.

"Alright then." I lifted my hand, Essence surging at my command. "Lets see if he truly doesnt."

"[Sanctum of Judgement]."

The moment the skill activated, my major laws resonated in the void.

Light tore through the darkness behind me, blazing, divine and absolute.

Spears of radiant energy bloomed one after another, their heat bending the air, their presence burning the void white.

One. Then ten. Then a hundred. Then a thousand.

In a breath, the void shimmered with a thousand spinning lances of light, orbiting me like a storm of judgement.

"Go."

The spears shot forward like a rain of comets, each one finding its mark, each one aimed at the Feran grandmasters.

And only then did Vaelix move. He unfolded his hands, raising a single palm.

A roar erupted from him, ancient, primal, reverberating across the void. The sound itself seemed to cut through space, striking the oncoming spears instantly. They slowed, their momentum faltering under the sheer force of it.

Another roar followed, and a ripple surged outward, hitting the spears again, forcing them to stagger in midair.

My eyes narrowed. I could feel the laws he was bending: Sound and Vibration, fused together seamlessly, amplified by something far beyond grandmaster scale.

Then came a final roar, a perfect, earth-shaking crescendo.

The ripples struck the spears one last time. Each one shattered before it could reach the grandmasters, exploding in golden sparks that lit the void in brilliant, violent showers. Shockwaves rolled outward, shaking even our ship.

When the echo faded, Vaelixs voice boomed across the battlefield.

"You are an impressive human. The strongest grandmaster I have ever faced in my life... from our universe."

I remained silent.

He continued.

"Now, I have a proposal for you. Join the Feran race as an honorary member. Aid us in our fight against the Eternals. In return, you will gain the path forward to higher ranks, and your world will receive our protection."

I raised my hand and pointed at the mirror. "I want that."

Vaelix held a measured silence for a few seconds before replying.

"Is that your final answer?"

I nodded without hesitation.

"I understand," he replied.

Then, with a simple wave of his hand, the dead Feran bodies floating across the void suddenly exploded.

Red blood burst outward, droplets scattering in all directions, trembling as they floated. They didnt fall; they surged forward, moving as if drawn by an invisible force, rushing straight toward Vaelix.

He lifted his palm again, and the torrents of blood twisted, coiling into a single massive droplet. It merged seamlessly into his open palm, the crimson gleaming like liquid fire.

Then, without a word, he raised his hand, pointing directly at us.

"[Blood Art: Dormant Crimson Claw]"

The void itself seemed to shudder in response.

Far above us, the darkness tore open as a massive crimson claw materialized, descending like a falling mountain.

Its sheer size dwarfed our spaceship, reducing us to nothing more than ants beneath it. The Essence thickened with pressure, radiating from the claws enormous palm.

The force alone drove Anjee and Primus to their knees, their bodies straining against the invisible weight. Our ship groaned and vibrated under the oppressive energy.

Instinctively, I released my own presence, letting my Essence surge outward, countering the pressure and stabilizing the ship against the overwhelming force.

But the claw kept descending, slow but unstoppable. I could feel the blood and Essence fused within it, the stolen life of the fallen Ferans, amplified exponentially by Vaelix and woven seamlessly with his laws.

I raised my hand and pointed a finger directly at the claw.

My core roared as the Essence inside me churned violently. I pushed nearly eighty percent of all my stored Essence into a single, concentrated strike.

"[Devouring Horizon]"

A thin, razor-sharp beam of violet Essence erupted from my fingertip, striking the claw squarely at its center.

The claws bloody, law-infused Essence began to collapse inward, drawn toward the void created by my attack.

Within seconds, the massive claw imploded completely, vanishing as if it had never existed, leaving only the echo of its destruction in the void around us.

"Impossible," I heard Vyrons shocked voice echo across the void.

It was nearly complete, if my estimate was correct, just one more shard was needed to finish the artifact. As I studied it, I noticed Vaelix doing the same, and it seemed he had come to the same conclusion.

Then a heartbeat pulsed through the void.

"Defend," I said. In the next instant, Vaelix vanished.

Behind it, Knights shadow dome emerged. I opened multiple violet vortexes in front of each of us, preparing for impact.
