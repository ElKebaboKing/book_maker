Chapter 921: The Blue Cleansing

Days passed quietly as we waited for Vivi to complete her hibernation.

By then, everything we had set out to do was already finished. The entire operation had come to an end, and somewhere along the way, the general public had given it a name of their own.

The Blue Cleansing.

It spread faster than expected.

The name of the Order of Absolute echoed across planets, across systems, carried by stories, fear, and admiration alike. Each of my summons had become figures people spoke of openly, their titles known even among children who had never seen a battlefield. Primus had clearly taken it upon himself to ensure the narrative stayed in our favor.

He was doing a good job. Through all of it, I stayed away from the noise.

I was focused on improving my laws. With the sudden leap in comprehension after taking over the galaxy core, I could feel the imbalance clearly. I had too many fragmented laws, too many overlapping functions. It worked, but it wasnt refined.

So I began restructuring and along the way started merging my laws.

The first to merge were Stormfire and Froststorm.

Both carried the nature of violent elemental systems, just expressed differently. One burned, the other froze, but at their core, both represented chaos in motion. Bringing them together was natural.

They became the Law of Tempest.

Now, instead of switching between conflicting expressions, I could command the entire system as one. Fire, ice, lightning, wind... all part of a single, unified force. Not separate attacks, but one continuous, overwhelming storm.

Next came Devour and Assimilation.

This merge was even more straightforward. Taking something in, breaking it down, making it mine... they were always part of the same process. Separating them had only limited their potential.

They became the Law of Consumption.

Now, anything I devoured wasnt just absorbed. It was understood, integrated, and converted instantly. There was no delay, no inefficiency. Power taken became power owned.

Polarity and Resonance followed.

Opposites and alignment.

Force and frequency.

Together, they formed something far more stable.

The Law of Harmony.

With it, I could influence how things interacted at a fundamental level. Attraction, repulsion, amplification, cancellation... everything now followed a single principle. It was about balance, or the deliberate breaking of it.

Sealing and Dreams took more thought.

They were different on the surface, but deeper down, they shared a connection. One restricted reality. The other distorted perception.

When combined, they became the Law of Bounded Reality.

Now, what someone perceived could be enforced, and what was enforced could feel real. Illusion was no longer just deception. It could bind, restrict, and shape outcomes. And sealing was no longer just confinement. It could be applied through perception itself.

The merge of Space and Time came last.

It wasnt something I would have attempted before. But now... it felt natural. They no longer existed as two separate laws.

They became Spacetime.

Both responded together, forming a single, seamless control over positioning, motion, and sequence.

Some laws, however, I chose not to merge.

Blood remained as it was.

Elemental Convergence remained as it was.

Both still had paths I had not fully explored, and forcing a merge too early would only limit what they could become. Even after all this, I hadnt lost access to the individual expressions.

That was the key difference.

The merged laws gave me a higher form of control, but I could still break them down when needed. Use fire without the storm. Use sealing without illusion. Use time without fully engaging spacetime.

As I worked through these changes, a thought began to take shape. If laws could merge... they could evolve further. Not just into stronger versions, but into entirely new concepts.

Elemental Convergence, for example. Right now, it allowed me to combine elements.

But if pushed further...

It could become something else entirely.

Creation. Not just combining what already exists, but bringing something new into existence by defining its components through Essence and law.

The idea was still incomplete.

But it was there. And for now... that was enough.

After the laws I focused on skills and decided to develop something new.

An art.

Something that would define how I fought and will make use of my updated laws as well as my class.

Absolute Art.

I spent days within the Dawn World, observing how the laws responded to each other now that they were no longer fragmented. Spacetime no longer needed conscious coordination. Harmony adjusted interactions naturally. Consumption flowed without resistance. Tempest didnt clash with itself anymore. And Bounded Reality... it no longer felt like just a tool, but something that could shape the battlefield itself.

The more I worked with them, the clearer it became. freewebnovel.com

From there, the first technique formed.

Absolute Art: Void Severance.

A decisive strike and it was the upgraded version of Absolute Void Cleave.

Not just speed, not just power, but the collapse of distance itself through Spacetime, combined with the destructive force of Tempest and the absorption of Consumption. It wasnt meant to overwhelm an opponent through repeated attacks. It was meant to end things in one clean motion.

The second came naturally after that.

Absolute Art: Boundless Collapse.

Instead of attacking directly, this one shaped the battlefield. Bounded Reality locked the space, Spacetime distorted its structure, and Harmony ensured everything within it worked against itself. It wasnt a visible trap. There was no obvious sign.

But once inside, escape became meaningless. Everything would eventually collapse inward.

The third technique I formed was after realizing many times I did not fight alone.

Absolute Art: Eternal Consumption.

With Consumption at its core, supported by Blood and Harmony, it allowed me to take everything from the opponent, not just essence, but vitality, energy, and momentum itself. And the moment it was taken, it became mine.

It was a continuous shift of advantage.

The final one was a planet killer.

Absolute Art: Tempest Ruin.

A large-scale release of power. Tempest and Elemental Convergence fused completely, creating a unified storm that didnt act as separate elements, but as one overwhelming force. It wasnt just destruction for the sake of it. It was controlled devastation, something that could wipe out entire battlefields or be focused into a single point.

Together, the four techniques formed a complete system. Each one connected. Each one built on the others. I didnt need dozens of skills. I needed a few that worked perfectly.
