Chapter 2228: A Broken Treasure

"W-What about me?" Xiao Meilin suddenly asked.

Yuan looked at her and replied with a question, "What about you? I never even accepted you as my servant in the first place."

"No way! How can you say that to me now?! Thats too cruel, Master Tian! Are you really going to throw me away after using me for so long?!" Xiao Meilin exclaimed.

Yuan showed a stiff smile and said, "Dont make it sound so weird. Youre not a toy. Also, while youre not my servant, youre still my disciple. Do you intend on staying as a disciple for the rest of your life?"

"Of course!" Xiao Meilin answered without any hesitation.

Yuan rubbed his eyes and sighed, "In that case, I have failed as your teacher and dont qualify to be one anymore."

"Youre acting pathetic, Elder Sister," said Xiao Hua. "You should stop bothering Yuan."

"Thats easy for you to say since youre always following him around! Ive been wondering for a long time now, but why did you get to go with him while I am stuck here? And did you just call him Yuan?! What happened to Master Tian? Did you really regain all of your memories? Or did you hit your head somewhere after awakening?"

"Blame your own incompetence for not being chosen," Xiao Hua said with a calm sneer. "I was chosen because Im not only more talented and powerful than you but, more importantly, Im not annoying like you."

"You little bitch... are you looking for a fight with me now?" Xiao Meilins eyebrows twitched nonstop at Xiao Huas provocation.

"Stop it. Both of you." Yuan sighed.

Xiao Meilin gritted her teeth and fell silent, but it was only for a brief moment.

"You didnt answer my other question. Why are you suddenly calling him by his name? Thats disrespectful, and you should know that."

"Thats none of your business," Xiao Hua said, refusing to answer.

Yuan then said, "I told her to stop calling me Master Tian. Im sure I asked you to do the same when we first met."

"So not only is she annoying, but shes also disobedient? Why am I not surprised?" Xiao Hua chuckled.

Xiao Meilin trembled in anger, and her aura started to surge violently. However, before she could even say another word, Xiao Cangming hammered her head with his fist, instantly wiping away her killing intent.

"Your inability to control your temper is exactly why I never allowed you to follow Yuan," he said calmly. "Rather than be helpful, youd only cause more trouble for him."

"..."

Xiao Meilin fell silent again.

"Dont be so down," Li Zhenwu finally spoke up. "You can always temper your control, and now that well be leaving the Primordial Realm, youll have plenty of chances to"

"I know." Xiao Meilin interrupted, but she didnt say more and fell silent again.

Some time later, Yuan spoke, "I have been thinking about something. The Primordial Realm... Its no longer filled with criminals like it was in the past, correct?"

Xiao Cangming nodded, "Yes, since most of the first generation are long dead. Why do you ask? Dont tell me you want to..."

Yuan smiled faintly. "I wonder... would it be alright to open the Primordial Realm and let those who wish to leave do so?"

He had been pondering this ever since his encounter with Wu Qi, and that thought had only deepened after witnessing the cultivators desperation.

Xiao Cangming responded seriously, "Honestly, I have thought about this more than just a few times. While there are many who wish to leave this world, and we have the power to release them, the problem is what comes after."

"If the current Celestial Emperor wanted to release us, he wouldve done so long ago," Xiao Cangming said with a faint bitterness in his tone. "While its technically possible to leave through the Supreme Heavens Legacy, that so-called path is nothing but false hopea cruel game that dangles freedom before the desperate. After all my time in the Primordial Realm, I can count on one hand those whove ever succeeded in fulfilling the legacy."

"So what youre saying is, we have to deal with the Celestial Emperor before we can release these people? I figured it would be like this." Yuan sighed.

"After all, even if the people get to leave, as long as they have the exiles branding on them, they wont truly be free."

Yuan narrowed his eyes slightly and asked, "This branding... what is it? It cant be a curse since it didnt appear when I used the Sacred Chalice of Purification on you."

Xiao Cangming shook his head.

"Im not sure either."

"Its a curse."

"What?"

Everyone in the room turned to Mu Xuelian, whod just spoken.

"Lord Shiva said its a curse."

"Can you elaborate? If its a curse, why didnt my Sacred Chalice of Purification activate? Its a treasure that can remove all curses."

Shiva took control of Mu Xuelians body and beckoned Yuan, "Show me that little treasure of yours."

Yuan didnt question her and handed her the Sacred Chalice of Purification.

"Hmph."

Shiva took a quick glance at it before tossing it back to Yuan as if it were trash.

"The curse was created with the power of an Eternal. A broken toy like that wont have any effect on it."

"Wait a second..." Yuan rubbed his eyes in a stressful manner and continued, "So youre telling me that not only is an Eternal involved, but my Sacred Chalice of Purification is a broken treasure? Even though it functions properly and is powerful enough to remove most curses in existence?"

"An Eternal also created that treasure, and I happen to know its creator," Shiva revealed.

"What? An Eternal creating treasures?" Yuans eyes widened at this information.

"I didnt even think an Eternal would show such interest."

Shiva snickered, "Not even Gods are impervious to boredom, you know? And while that thing might be a treasure to you, its nothing but a toy in our eyes. In fact, most of the treasures that appear naturally in this world are our creation."
