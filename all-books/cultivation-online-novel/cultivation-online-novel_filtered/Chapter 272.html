Chapter 272 - The Three Names

Early in the morning shortly after sunrise, Long Yijun summoned all of the Core Disciples within the Dragon Essence Temple.

Once they received this summoning, regardless of what they were doing, even if they were in closed cultivation at the Immortal Caves, the Core Disciples gathered at the location of the gathering within an hour.

"I was wondering when theyll announce this years participants for the Mystic Realm."

"What about Liang Qichao? Hes always mentioned when one talks about Gao Dongya and Xue Jiye."

"Its possible, but who knows, really. Everyone here has a chance to obtain that third spot."

While the Core Disciples there made their guesses on wholl occupy the third spot, Fei Yuyan stood at the back of the crowd like a bystander with a nonchalant look on her face.

Even though hes not a Core Disciple, its already obvious that theyll pick him for the third spot... The image of Yuans handsome face appeared in Fei Yuyans head as she thought about him.
