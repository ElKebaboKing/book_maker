Chapter 2097: Visiting the Demon Sealing Grotto(2)

"The Demon Sealing Grotto will support me?" Yan Hara raised an eyebrow, genuine surprise flickering in her eyes. "Thats quite the unexpected offer. However, I must askwhat if I am not trying to challenge Qian Chu for his position? What if I am just trying to increase my position for my own benefits?"

Zhou Yanfei paused briefly before speaking, his gaze steady. "Thats difficult to believe. I may not know everything about you, but I know enough to say youre not the type to act so selfishly. And those youve been rallyingtheyre all individuals who are not under Qian Chus influence."

"If youre not aiming to challenge Qian Chu, then whatever youre planning must be something that will shake the very foundation of the Demon Sealing Clan."

"You sound very confident in that statement."

"Thats just the feeling I am getting from you."

"Well, unfortunately, I have no intention of challenging Qian Chu," Yan Hara said, lying without the slightest hesitation.

She couldnt afford to trust Zhou Yanfei so easilynot when there was no telling if his offer was sincere or if spies loyal to Qian Chu were lurking within the chamber.

Zhou Yanfei narrowed his eyes slightly, but he didnt continue to dwell on the topic and said, "Lets talk about something else then. During the Supreme Demon Sealer Qualifications... What happened?"

"Why are you asking me that again? I already told you everything back then." Yan Hara raised her eyebrows in a puzzled manner.

"My memories are getting a little hazy these days, so why dont you remind me?"

Yan Hara rubbed her eyes and let out a soft sigh. "As Ive said before, I was in the midst of battling the Demon Emperor when I was suddenly seized by the Demon Sealing Flag. At the time, I didnt understand what had happened, but I later learned it was Qian Chu who had saved me."

She paused briefly before adding, "Thats also why I have no intention of challenging him. I could never turn against the one who saved my life."

Naturally, shed already realized that it was Yuan who saved her that day, but it was true that she initially thought it was Qian Chu who saved her life.

"Ah, yes. I recall now," Zhou Yanfei said, his voice laced with curiosity. "Though, I find it rather strange that Qian Chu saved you, yet allowed the others to perish. Why is that? Are you two perhaps... involved in that kind of relationship?"

Yan Haras expression turned cold as she said with quiet fury, "Even if youre the Demon Sealing Grottos leader, I wont allow such disrespect."

Zhou Yanfei quickly offered a courteous nod. "My apologies if my speculation came off as offensive. But that only deepens the mysterywhy did he save you? To my knowledge, Qian Chu has never intervened personally for anyone before."

"Youll have to ask Qian Chu since I have no idea what hes thinking."

Zhou Yanfei let out a heavy sigh. "That day, three of our most gifted Demon Sealers lost their lives, while the Demon Sealing Clan suffered not a single casualty. We will not rest until the truth is unveiled, and those responsible are held accountable."

"..."

Yan Hara fell silent. There was nothing she could say to defend that incident, as she understood how guilty the Demon Sealing Clan looked. She had been there, but held no control over what had transpired. And even if Qian Chu had truly tried to orchestrate their deaths as an accident, they had no proof.

"I will ask you one last time. Do you know what happened on that day?" Zhou Yanfei asked again a moment later.

"No." Yan Hara instantly responded with an adamant face.

"In that case, theres nothing more to discuss. Should you choose to challenge Qian Chu or want to switch sides, you may contact us again. And before you leave, wed like you to pass a message to the rest of the Demon Sealing Clan."

Zhou Yanfei paused briefly, his expression turning cold and his gaze radiating killing intent.

"We, the Demon Sealing Grotto, hereby declare war on the Demon Sealing Clan. From this moment forth, any member of your clan who dares approach us will be killed without mercy."

Yan Haras eyes widened with disbelief. "What happened to not turning on each other because of the looming threat?! You called it foolish just minutes ago!"

Zhou Yanfei responded with a cold sneer.

"I was talking about you, Yan Hara. It would be foolish to eliminate a Demon Sealer of your caliber. But the rest of the Demon Sealing Clan?" He scoffed.

"Theyre nothing but dead weightfodder in the face of war against the demons. Purging them would be a mercy, not a loss."

"Th-Thats!"

"Why are you so shocked?" Zhou Yanfei said. "You of all people shouldve seen this coming. Our factions have been at each others throats for decades, and it was only a matter of time. This war didnt begin with us. Your Demon Sealing Clan lit the fire. We only sought to hone our Demon Sealing Aura and hunt demons, but your people forced our hand."

Although Yan Hara couldnt refute Zhou Yanfeis words, she still sought a path to reconciliation. A war between them now, with the looming threat of demons, would be nothing short of catastrophic.

Just as Yan Hara struggled to find a solution, Yuan finally broke his silence and calmly said, "If you dont mind, there are a few things I would like to say."

"Huh?" Zhou Yanfeis gaze snapped to Yuan, his eyes sharp with disdain. "This conversation is far beyond your station, boy. Do yourself and your master a favor: keep quiet and walk away before you make this any worse."

Despite the threat, a tranquil expression remained on Yuans face as he spoke, "I understand your fury over the loss of your Demon Sealers. However, both factions bear some responsibility for what transpiredand if blame must be assigned, Qian Chu bears the greatest burden. To condemn the entire Demon Sealing Clan for the actions of one man would be both unreasonable and shortsighted."
