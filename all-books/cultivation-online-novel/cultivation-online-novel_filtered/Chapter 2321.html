Chapter 2321: Overwhelming Bloodlust

Although Ren Xia had decided to see for herself whether the crimson dome had really vanished or not, she didnt immediately head there.

Instead, she returned home first to let Sun Rouxi know of the situation.

"What? The crimson dome has vanished?" Sun Rouxi was unsurprisingly surprised to hear this news.

"Its just a rumor right now, but I am going to confirm it with my own eyes. Although I would love to wait for Tian Yang to emerge from his seclusion before doing anything, we have no way to know when hes coming out, and disturbing him will be dangerous, so I can only do this without him."

"I understand what youre trying to do, but it sounds dangerous," said Sun Rouxi.

"Dont worry, I wont do anything reckless and will only be investigating from afar. Once I confirm that the crimson dome has truly vanished, I will immediately return to wait for Tian Yang before doing anything else."

"Should I come with you just in case?" Sun Rouxi offered to join her.

However, Ren Xia shook her head and said, "No, you should stay here in case Tian Yang comes out early. Moreover, youre being hunted by your family right now, correct? Unless you want to risk being caught, I suggest you stay here, where its safe."

"If you say so."

Despite being older than Ren Xia, Sun Rouxi couldnt help but view and respect her as an elder sister. Not only was Ren Xia Tian Yangs first partner, but she had also played a crucial role in bringing Sun Rouxi and Tian Yang together.

Ren Xia left shortly after, traveling straight to the Giants Continent.

"Heavens..." Ren Xia muttered in a daze upon arriving at her destination and witnessing the situation with her own eyes.

"Its really gone..."

The crimson dome that had sealed the Giants Continent for nearly a hundred thousand years had suddenly vanished without warning. However, that wasnt all.

"Not only did the barrier vanish, but this place... is overflowing with bloodlust!"

I have already accomplished what I wanted to here, so theres no point staying here. Lets get out of here before something goes wrong...

However, right at that moment, Ren Xia suddenly felt a pair of eyes gazing directly at her.

Fuck!

I knew it! That troublesome bastard is still alive!
