Chapter 2199: Confronting the Chaos Elders

"So you knew we were tracking you, yet you still decided to leave the city alone? I cant even imagine whats going through that mind of yours," remarked the female Chaos Elder, her tone full of mockery.

"Its nothing complicated. Theres just no reason for me to worry. After all, youre not a threat to me," Yuan calmly responded.

"Still trying to act tough? Not only are you just an Immortal, but we outnumber you."

Yuan chuckled. "So what? You may be God Ascension cultivators, but you only have slightly more spiritual energy than I do. Right now, youre no different from mere Immortals."

"This little bastard is very talkative..." mumbled one of the other cultivators.

"Elder Pan, can we kill them already?" said another.

The male Chaos Elder, surnamed Pan, glared coldly at Yuan as he spoke. "I dont know what hole a bastard like you crawled out from, nor what background you havebut no one who insults the Chaotic Sect walks away unscathed. Still, being the generous man that I am, Ill grant you one final chance."

"Be a good boy and hand over everything in your possession, and I will grant you a swift and painless death."

Yuan smiled and replied, "If you want my stuff, youll have to work for it. However, the results might not be what youre expecting."

"What a pointless struggle."

"If you want to die a horrible death, allow me to help you!"

One of the experts suddenly lunged at Yuan with a Divine-grade spear in his grasp.

Yuan didnt even bother dodging the attack and stood still as the spear struck his chest.

"What?!"

To the wielders shock, the spear slammed into Yuan and stopped dead, unable to push forward.

In the midst of the wielders shock, Yuan grabbed the spear and casually yanked it from his grasp.

"This is how you use a spear."

Yuan surged with Chaotic Essence as he lunged the spear, the thrust detonating with cataclysmic forcesplitting the air in a deafening boom and rending open the very fabric of space as it hurled at the shocked expert.

"What are you doing?! Dodge it!"

The spear expert snapped out of his daze at the sound of Elder Pans voice, and he hastily summoned his spiritual energy in an attempt to block the incoming strike.

However, due to their environment that forced everyone to be mindful of their spiritual energy usage, the expert subconsciously held back the amount of spiritual energy he used.

Due to this mistake, the spear ripped through his spiritual energy like paper before carving a gaping hole through his body. That being said, even if the expert had used all of his spiritual energy to defend, it wouldnt have been enough to block the strike.

Before the spear expert could recover, Yuan closed the gap and flicked his arm that was surging with Supreme Sword Aura, striking the mans head and sending it spinning skyward.

With his physical body destroyed, the man immediately tried to flee with his soul. However, Yuan extended his arm in the direction the man was fleeing and mumbled, "Shivas Eternal Frost."

In the next instant, the temperature plummeted as if theyd been thrust into the ninth region of White Hell, and everything within ten thousand miles was sealed in ice.

The other four cultivators did not freeze outright, yet they felt their blood beginning to solidify. The fleeing soul, however, was sealed entirely, entombed in eternal frost.

That bastards using my powers! Shiva exclaimed inwardly after witnessing the scene.

Though the power itself was pitifully weak, it was not something mortals could wield, making Yuans ability to unleash it a feat in its own right.

Meanwhile, Yuan was surprised by how demanding the technique was. A single strike had emptied his reserves in an instant, and that was only enough to allow him to barely activate it. Fortunately, his energy recovered just as swiftly.

As expected, a technique that surpasses human limits is quite demanding... Yuan smiled inwardly.

Instead of disappointment, excitement stirred within him at the thought of the day he could wield Shivas power to its fullest potential.

Yuans gaze swept over the remaining four, his eyes like ice as he murmured, "One down... four to go."

Elder Pan and the others tremblednot from Yuans overwhelming might, but from the unfathomable aura that still lingered from Shivas Eternal Frost.

"Whats wrong? Dont tell me youve lost all will to fight this quickly?"

Yuans words jolted them from their daze. Elder Pan barked, "Fool! You may have slain one of us, but that technique must have drained nearly all your energy!"

Yuan only smiled. "Youre not wrong. But unlike you, I can simply replenish it whenever I wish."

"Youre really stupid if you think that bluff would!"

Suddenly, one of the cultivators vanished from sight and reappeared behind Yuan, his saber already cleaving toward Yuans neck.

This time, Yuan did not stand idle. He lifted his arm and caught the incoming saber between two fingers, stopping it cold in an instant.

"You feeling cold? Let me warm you up a bit," Yuan murmured, unleashing the Primordial Fireflames so searing they twisted the very air as they consumed the man.

"Ahhhh!"

The mans screams tore through the sky as he plummeted to the ground, thrashing desperately in a futile attempt to smother the flames. But the Primordial Fire would not relent, and it devoured him piece by piece until only his soul remained.

Once again, the cultivator attempted to flee with his soul.

"You think you can strike at me and leave as you please?" Yuan sneered, summoning the Primordial Fire once more.

Flames engulfed the mans soul, and since it was far more durable than flesh, the fire gnawed at it slowly, wringing screams of unending agony.

Yuan didnt bother waiting for the Primordial Fire to finish devouring the soul. Instead, he turned his gaze upon the remaining three, their faces ashen, as though they had just seen a ghost.

"Not looking so confident anymore, huh?" Yuan remarked with a cold smile.
