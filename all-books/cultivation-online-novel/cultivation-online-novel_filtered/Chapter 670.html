Destiny? Youre telling me that the Stairway to Heaven can tell someones destiny?

That is correct.

Whatever. Let me continue my trial then.

Yuan nodded and said, I will need every ounce of spiritual energy I can get.

He sat down the next moment and began recovering his spiritual energy.

Wow! I shouldve noticed this sooner! The spiritual energy in this place is so pure! I can feel my cultivation base soaring towards the next level as it recovers! Yuan was shocked when he absorbed the spiritual energy in the Stairway to Heaven.

Even if they move 1,000 miles an hour, it would still take 100 hours to complete the trial! Yuan sighed out loud.

Im only a Spirit Grandmaster, but I can defeat a Spirit Lord... Hopefully, they wont mind it...
