"I was told that if someone could play this zither, they would get to keep it. Is that true?" Yuan asked Senior Zou sometime later.

Yuan nodded his head and removed the black jade mask without hesitation.

Hes much younger than Id expected! A true prodigy! Hes a true prodigy! Senior Zou cried inwardly.

"By the way, I have been wondering this, but since youre in this city at this time, do you plan on participating in the zither competition?" Senior Zou suddenly asked him.

After all, Senior Zou has participated in the zither competition many times as a judge, so his judgment is highly regarded. If he says that Yuan could achieve first place, there was a very good chance that it might really happen!

Senior Zou turned to look at Fei Yuyan and responded with a smile, "Of course... That is if you also do your part as his partner. After all, this is a paired competition— not a solo."

Fei Yuyan nodded with a serious look on her face.

"Good. Then I shall see you all again tomorrow— as one of the three judges." Senior Zou said with a smile on his face.

Sometime later, after Yuan and the others left, Senior Zou stared at the sky and mumbled in a low voice, "At last... You have found your long-awaited partner..."

Normally, one would register for a competition weeks, even months beforehand.

Fei Yuyan nodded and said, "Lets go to the place where the competition will be held."
