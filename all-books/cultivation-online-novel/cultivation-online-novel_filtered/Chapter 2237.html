Although the Absolute Powers restrictions bound the Eternals prowess within its vicinity, they could temporarily exceed those limits as long as they were at the edge of the powers influence, where it was the weakest. Moreover, they had to pay a tremendous cost.

"Bear witness, mortal ant! This is Mighty Saaruk unleashing twenty percent of his true power!" he roared with manic laughter, hurling an attack that was twice as devastating as before.

"Twenty percent?! What nonsense is that?! What about the Absolute Power?!" Yuan exclaimed as he channeled as much Eternal Essence as he could muster to block the incoming strike.

"Argh!"

Moments after unleashing his attack, Saaruk suddenly screamed in agony, as though an unseen force were ravaging him, inflicting far greater pain than anything Yuans blows had caused.

Then, without waiting around to see the outcome, Saaruk immediately turned tail and fled, racing in a direction that took him farther and farther from the Nine Heavens until he was no longer within its vicinity.

Had he hesitated even a second longer, his ethereal form would have been annihilated by the unseen force. Though it wouldnt have killed him outright, the damage would have weakened his Eternal Essence, requiring trillions of years to recover.

However, that wasnt to say Saaruk escaped unscathed. By daring to defy the Absolute Power and drawing upon strength beyond what was permitted, hed forfeited roughly ten percent of his total strength. It was a heavy price, enough to drop his standing among the Eternals and cast him out of the top twenty.
