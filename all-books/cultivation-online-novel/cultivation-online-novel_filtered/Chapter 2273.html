Chapter 2273: The Method to Becoming a Giant

By the time Tian Yang finished recounting his experience inside Han Zexians cavern, the tea had arrived.

"These tea leaves are exclusive to this continentI cultivated them myself," Kulas said as he poured each of them a cup.

When Tian Yang took a sip, he immediately sensed a change within his body. His physique had been refined, as though he had just undergone an intense body-tempering session.

"My bodys getting a little sore... Arent teas supposed to help you relax? I feel the exact opposite right now," Tian Yang said with a stiff smile.

Kulas burst into laughter at his remark.

"Relax? That word doesnt even exist in the Giant Races dictionary!"

"This isnt going to give me a body as grotesque as yours, right...?" Ren Xia muttered as she hurriedly set her cup down, fearing that her body might become too muscular for Tian Yangs taste.

"Hey, heywho are you calling grotesque? You have no idea how much effort it took to achieve this physique!" Kulas protested, flexing his muscles proudly.

"This isnt something you can get just by drinking tea, so theres no need to worry. The tea only strengthens your foundation and repairs most of the damage in your bodyeven the kind of restoration pills cant heal. The reason youre feeling sore is because your muscles were just restored."

Kulas shot Ren Xia a look and sighed.

"I dont understand your taste. Whats so appealing about that bony physique of yours? Theres nothing to feel besides skin and bone."

"I dont expect someone built like a walking mountain to get it. Your brain is likely made entirely of muscle, too," Ren Xia retorted.

Kulas laughed and turned to Tian Yang.

"Why dont I introduce you to a few women here? Once you experience their muscles, youll never go back to that skeleton beside you!"

"You looking for a fight?!" Ren Xia snapped. "How dare you suggest my darling sleep with another woman right in front of me!"

"Whats wrong with that? Its not uncommon for a man to have several wives, especially if theyre powerful. I have over twenty wives right now. Or do you intend on keeping him to yourself?"

"I know," Ren Xia replied, folding her arms, a hint of defensiveness in her posture. "Im not fond of the idea of sharing him. But if he genuinely fell in love with someone else, Id rather accept it than let jealousy destroy what we have."

Tian Yang shook his head and said, "I doubt Ill ever love someone else, especially since I am no longer going on adventures alone, so you dont have to worry about that."

"Never say never," Kulas said.

"Alright, lets put this topic behind us. Why dont you tell me what youve been doing? Especially after we rescued you from the Immortal Confinement Dungeon."

Kulas leaned back, closed his eyes, and crossed his arms, sinking into a wordless silence.

He broke the silence a moment later.

"Truthfully, I barely remember anything after I was freed. My mind wasnt stable, so I must have wandered for some time. By the time I came to my senses, I had already ended up on this continent."

"Then you remember declaring yourself the ruler of this continent? Why did you do something that would only bring attention to yourself?" Tian Yang asked.

"I acted on instincts... probably," he replied.

"Probably?"

"Even after I regained my senses, I wasnt fully healed," Kulas said with a sigh. "My personality was nothing like it is now, and it took hundreds of years to recover. In fact, I didnt fully return to my original self until shortly before I reopened the continent."

"Then lets forget the reason behind your actions. How did you create the Giants Race? I heard from a giant that you bestowed your blood upon them. Ah, that giant was named Karan." Tian Yang didnt forget his promise to give the giants name to Kulas.

"Thats not entirely correct," Kulas said.

"My blood is exceptionally potent, to the point where even my children have difficulty refining it, let alone ordinary people. What they carry isnt my direct blood, but my childrensderived from my lineage, yet far weaker in comparison. The only time I gave my blood was in the beginning, when I hadnt fully realized the power of my bloodline."

"And how does that work? Surely, they werent born as giants."

He shook his head.

"No. Anyone not born of my direct lineage began as an ordinary human. It was only after they absorbed and refined my bloodline that they became giants."

"Your bloodline... then it wasnt a result of the Great Mammoth Body Refinement technique?"

Tian Yang muttered. "Our understanding of bloodlines is still limited. How did you come to possess yours?"

"Through trial and error, I suppose. And Id never share the Great Mammoth Body Refinement technique with ordinary people. Even within my own family, I only teach them half of the full method."

"Are the massive beasts your doing, as well?" Ren Xia asked.

"Yes. We giants possess extraordinary strength, and ordinary beasts no longer pose any threat to us, which is a problem, since we rely on them for training. So we strengthened them by giving them our blood."

He continued, "Of course, training isnt the only reason. Theyre also our source of nutrients. Tiny beasts wouldnt even fill the gaps between our teeth."

"Can anybody receive your bloodline and turn into giants?" Tian Yang then asked.

Kulas hesitated for a moment before shaking his head.

"Of course not. Many have died attempting to become giants. But we dont force anyone onto our path. Its no different from the sects in the outside worldexcept that instead of teaching cultivation techniques, we offer the chance to undergo the transformation into a giant. Anyone who desires strength may choose it."

"How would one even go about requesting such a thing? I didnt notice any form of recruitment."

"Youd be surprised by how many seek us out," Kulas replied with a smile. "Most ask about our methods out of simple curiosity, but some truly wish to become giants themselves, even if itll bind them to this continent for the rest of their lives."
