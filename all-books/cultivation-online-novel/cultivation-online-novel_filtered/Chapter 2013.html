Chapter 2013: Holy Sword Clan

When Yuan saw the situation, he decided not to interrupt but to spectate instead.

A few minutes later, the young man whod caught the attention of the old man from the Holy Sword Clan opened his eyes and stood up, seemingly unaware of the elders presence.

The old man cleared his throat loudly to announce his presence before speaking, "Young man, whats your name?"

The young man was startled momentarily, but he quickly recovered when he saw the old mans white and golden robes.

Then, he hastily lowered his body and bowed, "This junior is called Chu Ling. I apologize for not noticing you earlier, Senior."

The old man smiled and said, "Its my fault for sneaking up on you. Anyway, if you dont mind me asking, how old are you?"

"This junior is currently 3,291 years old," the young man responded.

"No, I do not, but I did spend a thousand years at the Gleaming Sword Sect."

The old man nodded before retrieving what looked like a slab of metal and said, "If you can leave even the slightest scratch on this plate using your Enhanced Sword Aura, Ill recruit you into the Holy Sword Clan."

Chu Ling swallowed nervously at the opportunity before him. The Holy Sword Clan was home to three Sword Emperors. Joining them was a dream pursued by countless swordmasters.

"Are you willing to accept this challenge?" the old man asked.

"I am willing!" Chu Ling immediately responded.

The old man nodded and placed the metal on the ground.

"Whenever you are ready," he said.

Chu Ling retrieved his sword and took a moment to prepare himself.

Before anyone could make sense of what had just happened, a cold, mocking laughter echoed through the air. All eyes turned toward the old man, who was now laughing hysterically, as though hed just witnessed the most absurd joke in the world.
