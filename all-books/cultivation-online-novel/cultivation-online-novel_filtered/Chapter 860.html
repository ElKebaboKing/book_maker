"H-Here's your token. It's red because you've achieved excellent results." The examiner handed Shi Lang his token and explained the purpose of it without being asked.

After exiting the portal, Shi Lang found himself standing in front of a massive lake. However, there was something special about this lake.

'Is this supposed to be hard?' Wang Ming wondered as he approached the 50th step.

One of the examiners there went to pick up Wang Ming before bringing him back to land.
