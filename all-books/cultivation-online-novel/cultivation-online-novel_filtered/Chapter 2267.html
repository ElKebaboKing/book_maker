Chapter 2267: The Immortal Clans Downfall

Ten years... fifty years... two hundred years...

Two hundred more years went by, and the Immortal Clans had been reduced to a state nearly unrecognizable.

They were no longer the haughty, untouchable powerhouses they had once been. Now, they were no different from ordinary familiesshunned by the rest of the world, largely because people feared the consequences of siding with them. After all, Tian Yang targeted anyone who remained close to the Immortal Clans.

With their power reduced to this pitiful state, Tian Yang could eradicate them whenever he wished. Yet he chose to let them linger, allowing the remnants of the Immortal Clans to spend their final days drowning in regret for ever offending him. As for Patriarch Gu and those directly responsible for torturing Kulas, Tian Yang showed no such mercy, and he crippled every one of them, stripping away not only their cultivation but their limbs as well.

By now, Tian Yang had ascended to the Golden Immortal realm, with Ren Xia trailing just a step behind at Silver Immortal.

Though his time within Han Zexians tomb had greatly accelerated his cultivation, Tian Yangs growth had slowed sharply afterward. Even after consuming countless treasures to refine his aptitude, his talent was still merely above average.

Ren Xia, on the other hand, continued to advance at a remarkable pace. Her natural talent allowed her to steadily close the gap between them.

The only reason Tian Yang even managed to reach the Golden Immortal realm was mainly due to plundering the Immortal Confinement Dungeon after learning about its vault and finding its new location, or he would still be a Silver Immortal right now.

That said, Tian Yangs cultivation was slightly deceiving, as he also possessed numerous powerful martial techniques that gave him a massive advantage over other cultivators, allowing him to defeat most around his cultivation with ease.

"Did you hear about the Desolate Continent? Its no longer sealed off." Ren suddenly started the conversation as they sipped tea in a private room inside a high-end restaurant.

Tian Yang slowly placed down his teacup and responded, "Of course."

Over the years, Kulas established his own family and empire within the Desolate Continent. Then, without warning, he sealed off the entire continent, forcing out all who were not part of the Giant Racea race he had personally created. Those who dared to resist were killed on sight. freewebnovel.com

Because of this, Tian Yang could no longer watch over him. However, since only Kulas and his family would exist on the continent, he wasnt too worried. That said, he was curious about the reason behind the sudden seclusion.

It had been over a hundred years since then, and just as abruptly as the Desolate Continent had been sealed, it was now being reopened to the public for reasons unknown.

"Now that the Immortal Clans are no longer an issue, do you plan on visiting him?" Ren Xia asked.

Tian Yang took a moment to respond.

"Im... not sure. Its been almost half a millennium since we last met. I wouldnt be surprised if he has forgotten about me."

"Are you really saying that after all this time? But if youre truly curious whether hes forgotten you or not, go see him."

Tian Yang raised his teacup and sipped on it before responding, "I guess so. Its not like we have anything else to do at this moment."

Since achieving their goal of bringing down the Immortal Clans, the pair embraced a tranquil, almost retired existence together, roaming the Divine Heavens at leisure while steadily refining their cultivation in peace.

With their next journey decided, they set off for the Desolate Continent, now called the Giants Continent.

Although the couple could easily fly to their destination, they decided to take the ship instead, as neither of them was in a rush to see Kulas.

The journey to the Giants Continent stirred the bitter memories Tian Yang had long buried, from his first encounter with the Huang Family, the true beginning of his path as a cultivator, to Han Zexians Tomb, where he had first met Huang Xiao Li, Kulas, and Ren Xia.

Even after hundreds of years, those memories remained vivid, resurfacing as sharply as if they had happened only yesterday.

"What are you thinking about?" Ren Xia asked Tian Yang, who had been quietly staring out the window of their deluxe room, one of the largest and most luxurious on the ship, ever since they set sail.

"I never told you about my first journey to the Desolate Continent... did I?" Tian Yang muttered, his voice tinged with distant nostalgia.

"Youve told me many things about yourself, but you have always avoided talking about the Desolate Continent."

Tian Yang looked at her with a warm smile.

"Are you willing to hear about it now?"

She quietly nodded.

Without further ado, Tian Yang began recalling his story to Ren Xia, from his first meeting with Huang Xiao Li, his first love, to their meeting.

After listening to Tian Yangs story, Ren Xia finally understood the depth of his hatred toward the Immortal Clans, something he had never spoken of outright.

His story not only revealed the reason behind his relentless pursuit of them, but also the exact moment Tian Yang had become the man he was todaythe man she loved.

It was a tragic story, yet Ren Xia couldnt help feeling grateful for the path it had carved. Had even a single detail of the past shifted, she might never have crossed paths with Tian Yang, condemned to remain nothing more than a tool of her family.

"Perhaps the real reason why I refused to visit Kulas before wasnt because I didnt want to disturb Kulas peace, but because I was simply not ready to return to the Desolate Continent," Tian Yang sighed.

"Well, how are you feeling now?" Ren Xia asked.

Tian Yang looked at her with a gentle smile. "If it werent for you, I wouldnt be here right now."

Ren Xias eyes widened, delight shimmering within them.

Saying nothing, she took his hand and pulled him to the bed, where they passionately embraced until the ship arrived at its destination.
