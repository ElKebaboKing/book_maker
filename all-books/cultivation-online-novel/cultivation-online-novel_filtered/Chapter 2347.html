Chapter 2347: Imposter

"Calm down, Rouxi!" Yuan shouted in a somewhat panicked voice as her overwhelming pressure descended on him like a mountain collapsing from above.

"I never gave you permission to use my name, you damn imposter!" Sun Rouxi snapped, her aura growing more violent and powerful.

Seriously, what the hell is going on?! Yuan cried inwardly, completely bewildered and puzzled by the situation.

Is this part of the trial? he couldnt think of another reason why Sun Rouxi was acting like this.

"I will give you one last chance! Identify yourself! And tell me what you did to Tian Yang!" Sun Rouxi shouted as she retrieved her golden wheel, her entire being seeping with immense killing intent.

"..."

After a moment of silence, Yuan replied, "My name is Yuan. I may not be the Tian Yang you know, but I am definitely Tian Yang."

"What bullshit is that?!" she sneered, refusing to believe him.

This left him perplexed, as he couldnt think of any other explanation that wouldnt sound even more crazy.

Eventually, Yuan sighed and asked, "How can you tell that I am not Tian Yang?"

"Your soul," she calmly replied.

"I have never told anyone, but I had developed a technique that allows me to see anothers soul, and your soul is completely different from Tian Yang. No... Its actually similar, but its too old to be Tian Yang."

"My soul, huh?" Yuan muttered.

"Then can you tell how old my soul is?" he then asked.

"I cannot, but I tell that its old. In fact, its much older than anything I have seen before. Just how old are you?"

"If were only talking about the age of my soul, which has experienced several reincarnations and multiple different eras... at least a few hundred million, I guess."

"Several hundred million years old?! Theres no way! I doubt this world is even that old!" she exclaimed.

Yuan shrugged, "I dont know what else to tell you."

"..."

Sun Rouxi was speechless, but she eventually asked, "What happened to Tian Yang? Is he still alive?"

"Well..."

Yuan wasnt sure how to explain further.

"Actually"

Right as he opened his mouth, the entire building shook violently.

"What the...? An earthquake?" Sun Rouxi looked around. "No, that shouldnt be possible. The entire continent is protected by formations that prevent these sorts of disturbances!"

Yuan spread his divine sense, but he couldnt see anything out of the ordinary.

A few seconds later, the building... No, the entire world trembled again.

Is this caused by an Eternal? Yuan wondered inwardly.

The shaking did not stop. In fact, it grew more frequent and violent.

Yuan turned to look at Sun Rouxi and said, "Lets postpone this conversation for later. Right now, we need to"

Before he could finish his sentence, Yuans vision blurred, and he found himself in a familiar place.

"What the...?"

He was suddenly back inside the Stairway to Heaven.

"Tianer, what is going on?" he asked out loud.

In the next moment, Tianer emerged from the void with a troubled face.

"I apologize for interrupting your trial, Master, but we have a situation," she said.

"Some troublemakers are attacking the Stairway to Heaven," she proceeded to explain.

"What?" Yuan raised an eyebrow.

"Under normal circumstances, this wouldnt be a cause for concern," she said. "But this situation is different. Were being attacked by multiple individuals simultaneously, across several realms. Moreover, these people are wielding a power that is similar to Eternal Essence."

Although it was forbidden to attack the Stairway to Heaven, this wasnt the first time someone had attempted to force their way inside. Of course, none had ever come close to succeeding.

"At this rate, they might actually be able to force their way in," Tianer said, worry etched across her face as she bit her nails.

"Is there anything I can do?" Yuan asked.

Tianer shook her head and said, "Ill do something about it. Unfortunately, this means youll be unable to continue the trial, and youll have to return later."

"Thats fine."

"Then Ill send you to that place right now."

"Alright. Stay safe."

Tianer snapped her fingers, creating a portal before Yuan.

Yuan had a lot of questions for Tianer, but with how urgent she seemed, he decided to save them for next time.

"Dont forget about me!" Kulas voice resounded as his soul appeared beside Yuan.

The next moment, Yuan stepped into the portal, disappearing from the Stairway to Heaven.

Once Kulas also entered the portal, Tianer immediately activated the Stairway to Heavens defensive features, something that had never been utilized until this moment.

Meanwhile, outside the Stairway to Heaven, the troublemakers across different realms were taken by surprise when the Stairway to Heaven suddenly vanished into thin air.

"What the? Where did it go?"

"Damn it! I was so close to cracking it open!"

With the Stairway to Heaven gone, the troublemakers did not linger and quickly left the scene.

The Stairway to Heavens sudden disappearance was quickly noticed, and word spread like wildfire throughout the Nine Heavens, plunging all nine realms into chaos.

"What?! The Stairway to Heaven vanished?! Thats impossible!"

"Its true! I saw it with my own eyesit was gone! And at the location where the Stairway once stood... There were corpses everywhere!"

"If the Stairway to Heaven is gone, how will people traverse between worlds now?"

"Itll likely be impossible for the majority of people, and only those who can traverse the starry sky will be able to move between realms."

"I heard from a friend that he witnessed from afar someone attacking the Stairway to Heaven right before it disappeared!"

"That must be why it disappeared! What kind of lunatic would attack the Stairway to Heavenand for what reason?! Its like burning the only bridge across a raging river!"

"My only concern right now is whether the Stairway to Heaven will return..."

In just a few days, news of the Stairway to Heavens disappearance reached almost every single ear in the Nine Heavens.

Of course, this included the Celestial Emperor, who was just as baffled as everyone.
