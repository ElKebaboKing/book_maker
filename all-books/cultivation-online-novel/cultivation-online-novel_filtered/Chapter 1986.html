"That doesnt make any sense!"

But Yuan didnt respond and fell silent after entering cultivation.

This left the others flabbergasted. None of them could understand why Yuan was waiting for his soul to be attacked.

If Yuans soul hadnt been fortified through his many reincarnations, the Celestial Emperors Curse wouldve instantly shattered his soul, killing him completely.

However, because his soul has existed since the Primordial Era and has been tempered for billions of years, not even the Celestial Emperor could easily destroy it.

Moreover, Yuan has been training Heavens Indomitable Soul for years now, which has strengthened his soul even more.

Yet, despite this, the Celestial Emperors Curse was relentless and extremely powerfulone of the most powerful curses in the Nine Heavens. The curse continued to gnaw at Yuans soul, causing him incredible pain, and no matter how powerful his soul was, it wasnt indestructible, especially since he hadnt even reached the God Ascension Realm.

Slow but surely, the Celestial Emperors Curse was destroying his soul.

Despite his soul on the verge of being destroyed, Yuan didnt do anything to stop it, allowing it to happen.

His puzzling actions greatly worried his companions. A destroyed soul wasnt the same as a destroyed body, and his regeneration ability had no effect on his soul, so if it were truly to be destroyed, hed die for sure.

Meanwhile, in the Celestial Emperors Palace, the Celestial Emperor could sense the activation of his curse.

So he managed to reunite his soul with his body, huh? However, as long as my curse is on you, you will not live long enough to return to the Nine Heavens!
