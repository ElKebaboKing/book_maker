Chapter 2219: Spirit Tree

"By the way, you said the Spirit Tree hasnt fully matured yet?" Yuan suddenly recalled Xiao Cangming mentioning this earlier.

He nodded and confirmed, "The Spirit Tree is still growing..."

After a brief pause, he continued, "...No, thats not quite right. To be honest, the Spirit Tree hasnt grown in tens of millions of years. No matter how much cultivation I pour into it, it remains unchanged. In the beginning, it responded, showing clear signs of growth each time I fed it my energy, but that ceased not long after it reached this form."

He sighed softly. "I suspect theres a unique cultivation method required to nourish it. Unfortunately, I lack that knowledge... so this may be as far as I can go."

"Is that so?"

Yuan stared at the small eternal tree before him with a pondering gaze, seemingly in deep thought.

Xiao Cangming and the others silently waited for his next action.

After a moment of stillness, Yuan raised his hand, palm facing downward above the Spirit Tree. Without warning, he drew a sharp cut across his palm, letting his blood drip onto the tree below.

The others watched in quiet intrigue, while Yuan himself felt strangely numb, as his actions were guided not by thought, but by pure instinct.

The moment Yuans blood touched the Spirit Tree, it was instantly absorbed, almost like a sponge with water. In the next breath, the entire tree began to change, its glowing green hue bleeding into a brilliant crimson.

"Oh?" Yuan muttered in a surprised voice after seeing the aftermath.

However, the Spirit Tree wasnt finished with its transformation.

Mere moments later, the ground started to shake, growing more violent with every passing second.

"This is... an earthquake?" Xiao Meilin looked at the ground with a dumbfounded look.

Natural earthquakes in the Primordial Realm were impossible, and the only time the ground would shake was when cultivators collided. However, there was obviously nobody fighting right now.

Then, right before their very eyes, the Spirit Tree started to expand in size. Seeing this, Yuan and the others hastily backed away from it.

In the blink of an eye, the Spirit Tree, which was only as tall as ones knees, enlarged until it rivaled the massive tree in Feng Yuxiangs Immortal Cave.

Moreover, it wasnt just its size that changed, as the amount of spiritual energy it produced drastically increased, too.
