Chapter 658: Evil God

How do you like the scenery? Does it make your heart pound with excitement? The handsome man suddenly asked him without looking away from the red sky.

Angry? Of course, I am angry! However, its not because you killed those people! I am angry because you possessed my body without my permission!

First it was the Divine Paragon, now its you!

Possessed? You think we possessed you? That is incorrect. Were not ghosts.

Then how do you explain what happened?!

The Divine Paragon took over your body because you wanted to defeat the demon. I took over your body because you wanted to kill those ants. Its as simple as that.

...
