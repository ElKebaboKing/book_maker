Although the vast majority of participants were humanand more than half were eliminatedless than one percent of those disqualified were giants.

Kulas spoke of things that he wouldnt even tell his own children without any hesitation, almost as if he had no restrictions when it came to them.

Ren Xia frowned at his words and said, "So... youre going to use your children for your own political gain. As expected of someone from the Immortal Clans. Even if you claim to have left them behind, your nature remains the same."

Despite her harsh words, Kulas wasnt offended. He knew she had always despised her family because of their arranged marriagehow they had used her for their own benefit. Of course, Ren Xia had never told him the truth. He had merely sensed that her seemingly unreasonable hostility came from somewhere, and as he grew older and more experienced, he came to understand it better.

In the end, the final victory of the Tournament of Strength was a giant by the name of Grandis.

As the victor, Grandis was granted the title of Champion and the opportunity to fight Kulas.
