Chapter 2060: Primordial Phoenixs Blood Essence

"Of course. Like I said, whats mine is yours," Feng Yuxiang said with a calm nod.

She continued, "To be honest with you, Young Master, Ive always been a little envious that you possess so much dragon power but almost no phoenix power. It makes my bloodline feel a little inferior. Hopefully, this will help balance things out."

"Its not like I am purposefully picking dragon powers over phoenix powers. Its just that Ive had more encounters with dragons than phoenixes," Yuan said. "In any case, Ill accept it. Thank you."

Feng Yuxiang handed the Primordial Phoenixs Blood Essence to him the next moment. This then reminded Yuan that he still had the Dragon Ancestors Blood Essence.

"I will need a quiet and secure place to absorb this blood essence, especially since I dont know how long itd take..."

At first, he considered absorbing the blood essence in Senior Bais world within the Myriad of Techniques, since he was familiar with the place and it was accessible to only a select few. However, there was a chance that his cultivation might become violent, and he didnt want to risk causing damage to the place.

Eventually, Yuan decided to find a secluded place somewhere in the Eighth Heaven to consume the blood essence. However, he didnt know where to go.

This was when Feng Yuxiang spoke up again, "Young Master, if youre looking for a place to enter seclusion, why dont you visit my Immortal Cave? Its located in the Eighth Heaven."

"Oh? That sounds like a good idea. Where is it?"

"Ill take you there."

She retrieved her flying treasure and boarded it a moment later. Once Yuan was aboard, she guided it into motion.

"It might take some time to get there since its quite far away," she said.

Yuan nodded, and in the meantime, studied the Mystic Sword Tablet.

Several months later, they arrived at a place of searing heat, where volcanoes and rivers of flowing magma stretched in every direction, as if an apocalypse had recently swept through the land.

This sort of extreme environment wouldve been great for Yuan to train his fire resistance before, but he was already at the point where the heat didnt affect him in the slightest.

Feng Yuxiang finally brought the flying treasure to a halt and said, "Young Master, weve arrived at the location, but theres a problem..."

Yuan stowed away the Mystic Sword Tablet and stepped to the edge of the flying treasure to see for himself.

In the distance, he spotted several groups of cultivators encircling a particular area. Judging by their distinct robes, each group appeared to hail from a different faction.

"Is that where your Immortal Cave is located?" Yuan asked Feng Yuxiang.

However, Feng Yuxiang shook her head and said, "No, my Immortal Cave is a little further ahead. However, with so many people here, I dont think itll be a good idea to use it anymore."

Yuan then said, "Theres no need to come to a conclusion so quickly. Lets check out the situation first before we decide what to do next."

Yuan leapt from the flying treasure and flew toward these cultivators, who were all within the Immortal Realm.

There were about seven hundred cultivators gathered there, most of them ranging from Bronze Immortal to Silver Immortal. Within each group, a single Golden Immortal stood at the forefront, acting as their leader.

When these cultivators noticed Yuan, they immediately became alert.

"Stop right there." One of the Golden Immortals demanded in a tone of authority.

Yuan slowly came to a halt, but before he could even open his mouth, the Golden Immortal continued, "Turn around and leave immediately. Were busy."

Because Yuans cultivation was only at the Bronze Immortal stage, the Golden Immortal didnt even bother to show him any courtesy and brazenly ordered him to leave.

"That would be difficult, as I have some business ahead," Yuan said.

"That business will have to wait. If you know whats good for you, leave while Im still patient enough to let you!" the Golden Immortal snapped, his voice rising sharply.

"This bastard... Who the hell do you think you are to speak to my Young Master in that tone? Have you lost your damn mind?!" Feng Yuxiang snapped as she caught up with Yuan.

Instantly, all eyes shifted to hera peerless beauty with flaming red hair and golden eyes, unmistakably marking her as a beast in human form. Yet what truly seized their attention was the terrifying aura of her True Immortal cultivation.

Over the past several years, her cultivation had recovered enough to surpass Yuan once more, now just a hairs breadth away from stepping into the God Ascension Realm.

The Golden Immortal who commanded Yuan tried to respond but was quickly interrupted, "First of all, youre all in my territory. If anyone should leave, its you lot."

"Your territory...? Im sorry, but weve traveled through this region for the past hundred years, and this is the first time weve ever seen you. Who exactly are you people?" another Golden Immortal asked, his tone far more respectful now that they were standing before a True Immortalsomeone who could easily kill them all.

"A mere hundred years?" Feng Yuxiang scoffed with a cold sneer. "To an Immortal, a hundred years could easily pass in the blink of an eye. You might as well admit youve only just arrived with such a trivial span of time."

What Feng Yuxiang said made sense, but it was not like they could just leave, especially after pouring so many resources into their business.

"Ah, it seems weve forgotten to introduce ourselves. I am the Second Elder of the Blazing Orchid Sect, and" The Golden Immortal tried to reveal his background, but Feng Yuxiang couldnt care less and said.

"I dont give a hot damn about your identity or where you came from. If you dont apologize to my Young Master and get lost within ten seconds, Ill turn you all into ashes."

Feng Yuxiang threatened as she revealed some of her flames and unique aura.

"A phoenix!" These Immortals quickly realized her identity and swallowed nervously.
