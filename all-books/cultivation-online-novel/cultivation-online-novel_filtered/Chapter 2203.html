Chapter 2203: The Chaotic Sects Revenge

"I am aware of how ridiculous it sounds, but it is the truth," said Sect Leader Zu.

"Hmm..."

After a moment of silence, Leader Yang responded, "I understand your situation. Unfortunately, I cannot just hand him over to you."

"Why are you protecting him?"

"First of all, he is our guest. If we hand him over simply because you demand it, our reputation will be tarnished. Secondand more importantlythat man may have ties to the Asura Clan. I trust I dont need to spell out what that means," said Leader Yang.

"Hahaha!" Sect Leader Zu suddenly burst out laughing. "Dont tell me that man actually managed to fool the Inferno Alchemists with such an obvious lie!"

Leader Yang narrowed his eyes slightly and replied, "Are you saying that hes lying about being connected with the Asura Clan?"

"Do I really have to answer such an obvious question?"

"Do you have any proof to back up your claim? Although we dont have proof that hes connected with the Asura Clan, we also dont have evidence that hes lying to us. Its better to be safe than sorry."

"Proof? I dont need such a thing! Even an idiot can tell that hes lying!"

"Then I may be an idiot. Can you enlighten me?"

"..."

Sect Leader Zu fell silent, as he did not have a proper response.

"Enough of the chitchat. Regardless of whether hes related to the Asura Clan or not, its a fact that he killed four of my elders. This will be my last time asking. Will you hand him over to us or not?"

Sect Leader Zus aura suddenly started increasing.

"And what are you going to do if we refuse your demands? Attack us? Do you not fear the Asura Clans wrath?"

"Dont worry, we wont harm the Inferno Alchemists. However, we will drag him back with us even if we have to be a little forceful."

"..."

Leader Yang fell silent. The Inferno Alchemists numbered barely a hundred; if the Chaotic Sect truly wished to annihilate them, resistance would be futile. However, due to their connection with the Asura Clan, the Chaotic Sect wouldnt dare to do such a thing. Moreover, so long as no harm befell the Inferno Alchemists directly, the Chaotic Sect believed there was a good chance the Asura Clan would overlook the matter.

If we hand Tian Xian over to them, we would look like pushovers, and if he happened to really be connected to the Asura Clan, we would have no excuse. However, if we refuse and they forcefully take him away, our reputation would still take a blow, but at least, we can tell the Asura Clan that we had no choice since were no match for the Chaotic Sect.

After weighing the choices, Leader Yang gave his answer, "My answer is no. We wont hand him over to you. If you want to take him, youll have to do it with force."

This way, the Inferno Alchemists wouldnt be held responsible for Yuans death.

Sect Leader Zu sneered before commanding the hundreds of disciples that came with him, "You heard him. Search every nook and cranny and bring that bastard back to me!"

"Yes!"

At his command, the Chaotic Sects disciples invaded the Inferno Alchemists territory to look for Yuan.

"Leader! What should we do?! Are we really going to just stand here and let them invade our territory?! Even if theyre not here for us, theyve crossed a line they shouldnt!" The elders of the Inferno Alchemists asked as they watched the sea of disciples fly past them.

Leader Yang shook his head and said, "Theres nothing we can do but let the Asura Clan deal with them."

He turned to glare at Sect Leader Zu and spoke angrily, "Youll regret this, Sect Leader Zu."

In response, Sect Leader Zu merely sneered with disdain.

Meanwhile, Yuan stopped studying the Mystic Sword Tablet and sighed out loud, "Looks like I have guests."

"Need my help?" Mu Xuelian asked as she watched him stand up and approach the exit.

"Ill be fine."

"They sure brought a lot of trash..." Yuan sighed.

"H-Hey! I think thats him!"

One of the disciples shouted when he saw Yuan.

"You might be right. He matches the description."

Yuan smiled and calmly spoke out loud, "If youre looking for the one who killed your elders, youve definitely found him."

"I-Its him! Someone let the elders know that we have found him!"

The disciples immediately surrounded Yuan and his building.

"No, I figured you would come after me. However, I still held onto some hope. Anyway, lets kill some time before your elders arrive, shall we?"

Before the disciples could even react, Yuan struck first. With most of them being at the Immortal Realm, it took him little effort to cut them down. Of course, he didnt wield any weapon and used only his Supreme Sword Aura.
