Chapter 2384: A Black Mountain

Unlimited blood?! Lev nearly fell from the sky in shock after hearing her once-in-a-lifetime offer. Lev and Supreme Ruler Dena frowned when they sensed this aura, and they came to a complete halt.

He turned to look at Supreme Ruler Dena and asked her, "What are you going to do? Want to stay here, too?"

However, she shook her head and said, "As a Supreme Ruler, my resistance to this sort of energy is much higher."

Yuan raised an eyebrow at this revelation.

"Is that how it works? Does that mean Supreme Rulers cannot turn into Forsaken?"

"Not necessary. It just means were less likely to do so. However, weve never had a Supreme Ruler turn Forsaken, as were essentially the Demon Gods envoy."

"But I am not sure that I want to risk it. If you turned into a Forsaken... even I wont be able to stop you."

"If I stay here, that means youll be with the Queen Wraith of Demise by yourself..." Supreme Ruler Dena said with a slight frown on her face.

"Ill be fine," Yuan said with a confident smile.

"If you say so."

Yuan continued following the Queen Wraith of Demise, while the others stayed outside the Chaotic Essence. However, it wasnt just Lev and Supreme Ruler Dena who were forced to stopeven the ordinary Nether Wraiths halted their advance, clearly against their will, as they released wails unlike anything heard before, as if declaring their concern for their queen.

So these beings are weak against Chaotic Essence too, huh? Yuan noted to himself.

He had no idea that Chaotic Essence was capable of such a thing. However, this wasnt necessarily a bad thing. In fact, it was quite advantageous for Yuan, who could wield its power.

Of course, the Queen Wraith of Demise wasnt aware of this and unknowingly revealed their weakness to the worst individual possible.

Moreover, it was clearly the source of the Chaotic Essence, and the amount it emitted was unlike anything Yuan had ever experienced beforeeven during his time as Tian Yi, the Immortal Monarch, who was most familiar with Chaotic Essence.

In fact, even if he combined all of the Chaotic Essence that he absorbed from the Chaos Coins hed amassed inside the Primordial Realm, it still wouldnt come close to the amount contained within the mountain.

Just how much stronger would his Chaotic Essence become if he were to absorb all of it? Yuan almost started drooling at that mere thought.

"Isnt it about time you tell me what you need my help with? I can assume it has something to do with that mountain, right?" Yuan said as they got closer.

When it first appeared, it was barely larger than a boulder. The energy it emitted wasnt particularly remarkable at the time, but it grew stronger and larger after absorbing some of my children who got too close out of curiosity."

"Let me guess... the Corruption Shower," Yuan said.

"Correct." The Queen Wraith of Demise turned to look at Yuan and spoke with a serious expression. "If this continues, this object will eventually take over our territory and threaten our existence, not to mention those living outside."
