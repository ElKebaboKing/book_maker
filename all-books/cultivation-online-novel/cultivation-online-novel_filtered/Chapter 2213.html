Chapter 2213: Li Zhenwu

"Well, what do you think?" Feng Yuxiang asked as she showcased her treasures with a prideful air around her.

Xiao Cangmang took a moment to look over the treasures before responding with a surprised expression, "Ill be honest with you. I cant identify even half of the treasures here, but judging from their intense aura, Im sure theyre all priceless treasuresbeyond anything I anticipated. If we have this quality of treasure, we can definitely make a top-grade vessel."

"In that case, allow me to introduce them to you," Feng Yuxiang said as she began identifying the treasures and explaining their uses.

"This right here is the Exalted Divine Crystal, a treasure used to store a massive amount of spiritual energy. It can be used to create the Dantian."

"The item right next to it is the Nine-Flame Heart Pill, which can be used to create the heart. Not only will it give her vessel improved resistance to fire, but it will strengthen any fire-element techniques."

"Next is the Primordial Essence Root, which will be used for her spiritual veins and give her superior cultivation speed."

Feng Yuxiang pointed at a bottle of red liquid and continued, "This is phoenix bloodmine, to be precise. However, we will need more blood, preferably human blood from the same family."

"I can give my blood," Xiao Meilin raised her hand.

Feng Yuxiang nodded and continued explaining the treasures, "This is the Heavenly Marrow Essence. Its usually used for crafting treasures, but it can also be used to forge bones."

"These are the main ingredients for her vessel. The rest are mainly used to enhance their effects, but I will still explain them."

A few minutes later, Feng Yuxiang finished.

Not just Xiao Meilin, but even Xiao Cangming was left speechless by Feng Yuxiangs resources.

"Are you sure we can use such precious ingredients? Honestly, even I would want to move my soul into a vessel made with these ingredients," said Xiao Cangming.

Feng Yuxiang looked at Yuan and said, "Originally, I planned on using them to strengthen the Young Master."

Yuan smiled and said, "Just your thoughts are enough. Moreover, at my current level, these treasures will have minimal effect, so its better to use them for Xiao Hua."

"Feng Yuxiang, right? Ithe Asura Clan will never forget this debt," Xiao Cangming said with his head slightly lowered.

Feng Yuxiang rubbed her nose and responded in a stiff tone, "Honestly, I am only doing this for the Young Master, so you dont owe me anything."

"It doesnt change the fact that youre the reason Xiao Hua will have a new vessel."

"So, is this enough to craft her vessel? If so, how long do you think itll take?" Yuan asked a moment later.

Xiao Cangming nodded and replied, "This is more than enough. In fact, we can make a vessel with only half of it. The rest is just a bonus. As for how long it will take... I havent attempted to craft a vessel since the Primordial Era, so it might take me a couple of weeks just to be safe. I dont want to mess up these precious ingredients, after all."

"Alright, Ill leave it in your hands then."

Yuan placed Xiao Hua back inside his Dantian shortly after.

"Ill need a few days to prepare myself. Make yourself at home until then."

Xiao Cangming left to prepare a moment later.

Meanwhile, Xiao Meiling brought Yuan to Mu Xuelian, who was waiting in the guest room.

However, when they arrived, Mu Xuelian was not alone, and there was another beautiful woman in the room with her.

The two were quietly sharing tea, but the moment Yuan saw the womans face, his body went rigid, his eyes widening in utter shock and disbelief.

"Fei Zhener...?" Yuan muttered.

The beautiful woman greatly resembled Xiao Cangmings dead wifethe one Tian Xian had personally killed in the past. The resemblance was so uncanny that he wouldnt be surprised if this woman were actually Fei Zhener.

Seeing Yuan, the woman stood up and gracefully bowed to him, "You must be Master Tian. I have heard a lot about you from my husband. I am Li Zhenwu, and its an honor to finally meet you."

Yuan turned to look at Xiao Meilin, who was standing right behind him and chuckling at his reaction.

"I knew youd be surprised when you see her. The resemblance is uncanny, right?" she remarked.

Li Zhenwu smiled and said, "I was told that I greatly resemble my husbands late wife, but I never really took it to heart. However, seeing your reaction... I cannot help but wonder just how much I resemble her now."

Yuan swallowed nervously and replied, "Youre a splitting image of her. If you claimed to be Fei Zhener, I wouldnt have doubted you in the slightest."

"If you dont mind me asking, are you busy right now?" Li Zhenwu suddenly asked.

"Me? No, I am not busy."

Li Zhenwu clasped her hands together at his words and spoke with an excited smile, "In that case, can you tell me some stories about my husband? This might sound weird, but he only ever talks about you, so its not an exaggeration to say that I know you better than my own husband."

"I dont mind... but wouldnt it be better if you just asked him?"

Li Zhenwu shook her head and sighed, "I have tried, but the topic always ends up shifting to you."

"Alright, Ill tell you everything I know about Cangming." Yuan nodded.

"Thank you! Let me grab some more tea first!"

Yuan rubbed his eyes and sighed.

"I know youre joking, but..."
