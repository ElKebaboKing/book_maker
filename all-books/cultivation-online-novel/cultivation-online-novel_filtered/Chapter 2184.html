Chapter 2184: Asura Clans Location

The mans expression darkened after hearing the name Asura Clan.

"What did you just say?! The Asura Clan?! Are you insane? How dare you utter their name before me! You must be trying to get me killed!"

Yuan narrowed his eyes slightly and responded, "What are you so afraid of? Youre not the first person Ive asked about the Asura Clan, but you all have the same terrified reaction, almost as if youll die just for talking about them."

"What nonsense are you spouting? Anyone living in this forsaken world would tremble at their name! Unless... youre not from the Primordial Realm? No... that cant be."

For a brief moment, the man considered the possibility that Yuan had come from the outside, but he quickly dismissed the thought as impossible. After all, the Primordial Realm has been closed since the Primordial Realm.

"So what if I am from the outside world?" Yuan suddenly said.

The mans eyes widened with shock and disbelief.

"You... are you serious?" The man swallowed nervously, as he couldnt imagine what sort of heinous crime Yuan had committed to force the Celestial Emperor to throw him in an abandoned prison like the Primordial Realm.

Yuan silently nodded in acknowledgement.

No wonder his appearance is so clean... but hes only a True Immortal. What could he have done toNo, I shouldnt involve myself in his business. Hes too dangerous.

"Why are you looking for the Asura Clan?" the man then asked.

"I just have some business with them. Why does it matter to you?"

The man frowned and said, "I want no involvement in your search for the Asura Clan if youre trying to fight them. If they kill you and discover I was the one who pointed you their way, Ill be as good as dead myself."

Is that why everyone was afraid to tell me the directions? Because theyre afraid that I might fight them? Yuan wondered to himself.

He then smiled and said, "Dont worry, Im not going to fight them."

The man pondered for a moment before pointing toward the south and saying, "You can find the Asura Clan at the border of the Spirit Tree."

"Spirit Tree, right? Thanks for the information."

Yuan stopped absorbing the spiritual energy.

"Before I leave, I have one more question. Where is the nearest city?"

The man then pointed west and said, "Theres one a week away in that direction."

Yuan didnt linger and left with Mu Xuelian shortly after.

Once Yuan left, the man sighed in relief.

"For the first time since the Primordial Era, someone new has been tossed inside the Primordial Realm... I can only hope this is just a one-time event."

The man worried over the implications. If the current Celestial Emperor were to make use of the Primordial Realm again, it could plunge the realm into chaosdisrupting the fragile society and norms its residents had managed to build through many eras.

"Lets not overthink it and hurry up and consume the spiritual energy before someone else comes..."

Meanwhile, Yuan and Mu Xuelian sped toward the nearest city, arriving at their destination in under a day of travel.

Although the man said it would take a week, he was talking about walking distance since cultivators in the Primordial Realm only fly when its an emergency, as it would be a waste.

Once they arrived in the city, Yuan used his divine sense to scan the city. He wanted to look for a teleport formation, but he didnt find one even after searching the entire city.

"Looks like we will have to go to another city," Yuan said to Mu Xuelian.

Yuan shifted his attention to a nearby pedestrian, who was a young man and a pure mortal.

"Hey, can I ask you a question?"

The young man turned to face Yuan, and the instant he saw his appearance, shock overtook his face.

"How may this lowly one serve you?!" he cried, dropping to his knees and kowtowing before Yuan.

Yuan raised his eyebrows in a puzzled manner at the young mans unexpected reaction and questioned, "Do you recognize me?"

The young man, not daring to raise his head, responded, "No, I do not!"

"Then why are you reacting like that?"

"Because you are a cultivator!"

"..."

"Hes probably like that because cultivators are rare in the Primordial Realm. In a world where spiritual energy rarely exists, I highly doubt many would even attempt to become one," Yu Ning remarked.

"That makes sense..." Yuan mumbled, finding Yu Nings words very logical.

"Anyway, I would like to ask you... Do you know which city has a teleport formation?" he then asked the young man.

"A... what?" The young man responded in a confused tone, almost as if hed never heard of this term.

"I dont think teleport formations exist in this world," Yu Ning suddenly said.

"If you think about it, teleport formations can only be activated with spiritual energy. Even if they somehow manage to activate it, theyll still need spiritual energy to teleport."

"..."

Yuan became speechless when he realized that hed overlooked this fact.

"Senior Immortal?"

The young man spoke when Yuan didnt say anything for some time.

Yuan snapped out of his daze and asked, "Then do you know how far away the Spirit Tree is?"

"The legendary Spirit Tree, where only the most powerful cultivators go?! Im sorry. While I have heard of its name, I dont know where its located. I reckon that only powerful cultivators will know since its not something us ordinary mortals even dream of."

"Is that so..."

Yuan pondered for a moment before speaking again, "Hey, I have another favor. Can you tell me more about this world? Ill treat you to a meal for your assistance."

"Really?!"

Yuan nodded, "Ill even let you choose the restaurant. Lets talk while we eat."

"Okay!"

"..."

After a moment of awkward silence, the young man asked, "Is it okay for me to stand up now?"

"Of course. You dont need to be so formal, either. Just treat me like a friendly neighbor or something," Yuan said.
