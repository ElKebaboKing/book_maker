Chapter 2349: Crafting Yu Nings Vessel

Upon reaching the golden door, Yuan inserted the sword-shaped key into the lone keyhole. As expected, it fit perfectly.

The moment the door opened, a profound ripple swept through the darkness, transforming the void into a massive chamber filled with treasures.

The storage room was unimaginably massive, resembling a limitless space with no visible walls, filled with a sea of treasures that extended beyond sight.

"This is... a lot more than I anticipated..." Yuans eyes widened at the sight, his jaw slightly loose from shock.

"Heavens... Ive never seen so many treasures gathered in one place before!" Yu Ning exclaimed, hardly daring to believe her eyes.

"This is the first time the phrase sea of treasures truly feels appropriate."

There were so many treasures that they formed the very ground beneath Yuans feet.

"Rather than worrying about not having enough materials, Im now more concerned about finding them," Yuan sighed. "Lets hope it doesnt take too long."

However, just as he was about to scour the storage with his divine sense, information about every single treasure abruptly flooded his mindmuch like when he became the master of the Nameless Tomb and could view all of its contents through the system.

A smile immediately tugged at Yuans lips, and he said with enthusiasm, "Not only will I be able to craft Kulas a new vessel, but Ill also be able to craft yours, Yu Ning."

"Eh?"

Yu Nings mind blanked out for a brief moment after hearing Yuans words. Shes been so accustomed to and comfortable living inside his Dantian that she completely forgot about their promise.

"Honestly, I wouldnt mind living inside your Dantian as nothing but a soul forever," she chuckled.

"Thats a little..."

"Its just a joke."

Although he had a list of all of the treasures, Yuan still needed to choose which ones to use for their vessels, and that alone took several days.

Eventually, Yuan acquired all of the materials needed to craft not two but three vessels.

"Cant forget about Ji Ran," he mumbled as he tossed the materials into his spatial ring.

"What are you going to do with the rest of these treasures?" Yu Ning then asked.

"Its not like I can take them all with me. And even if I could, I doubt Ill use most of them in my lifetime. If I ever need something, I can simply return here for them," he said.

"That said, Ill take a few treasures that might come in handy soon."

Of course, he didnt forget about Xiao Hua and the others, selecting several treasures he believed would benefit them as well.

Once he was done, he left the storage room and retrieved the sword-shaped key.

"Believe it or not, this is not the only stash I have," Yuan suddenly said. "In fact, this is where most of the trash is kept."

"Seriously?" Yu Ning was speechless.

Some time later, Yuan returned to Kulas side.

"I have the materials to craft your vessel, but Ill need some time to master the technique Ill be using to craft them and practice inside my head. Also, Ill craft your vessel last since its going to be the most difficult out of the three."

"Youre going to craft three vessels? What are you going to do with the other two?" Kulas asked.

Yuan gave a brief explanation, telling him about Yu Ning and Ji Ran.

"I see. Well, take your time."

Yuan nodded and immediately went to work.

First, he retrieved a particular technique he had acquired from the storagea method for crafting vessels. Although he could imitate what Xiao Cangming had done when creating Xiao Huas vessel, that approach was outdated, and attempting to replicate such a technique carried far too much risk.

Now that he possessed a more advanced techniqueone he could properly study and masterthere was no reason to rely on Xiao Cangmings method.

A few hours later

<You have learned Karmic Vessel Creation Scripture>

<Karmic Vessel Creation Scripture>

<Rank: Supreme>

<Description: A unique technique created by the 7th Sect Leader of the Karmic Soul Sect, who was said to have crafted the perfect vessel using it.>

Although learning the technique took only a few hours, mastering it was a different story, and it took Yuan an entire week.

<Your comprehension of Karmic Vessel Creation Scripture has reached max level>

Once he had mastered the technique, Yuan began running simulations in his mind, crafting vessels within his thoughts just as he once forged treasures mentally as Tian Qiyuan, the Divine Blacksmith, before working on the real thing.

Naturally, this process took far longer than mastering the technique itself. Striving for perfection, Yuan lost track of time, and in the blink of an eye, half a year had passed.

"Okay, I am ready." Yuan declared as he emerged from his training.

"Ill craft your vessel first, Yu Ning."

He immediately retrieved the necessary materials and began working with flawless precision, his movements so practiced that it was as though he had done it millions of times before.

Unlike Xiao Cangmings method, Yuan didnt require a cauldron. In addition to alchemical flames, he employed array symbols and intricate formations to further temper and refine the vessel.

Although the technique was far more advanced, it was also significantly more difficult to employ and demanded much more time. In the end, it took Yuan nearly two weeks to complete Yu Nings vessel, which took the form of a peerless marble.

"Your new vessel is ready, Yu Ning. Youll need to complete the rest yourself."

"Okay."

Without hesitation, Yu Ning left his Dantian and entered her new vessel.

In the next instant, the marble trembled violently and began to expand, limbs rapidly forming as the vessel took shape.

Within moments, the fist-sized marble transformed into a peerless jadelike beauty with large breasts and round buttocks.

"What do you think?" Yuan asked. "If you dont like it, I can make you another one."

Yu Ning looked at him with her large emerald eyes. Then, without warning, she leaped forward with her arms wide open and wrapped him in a tight embrace.

"I love it!" she said, her voice brimming with joy.
