The Supreme Seal bestowed a unique blessing upon the Divine Supremes, enabling them to grow alongside Yuan. This bond ensured that, despite Yuans monstrous talents and rapid advancement, they would not lag too far behind, maintaining their relevance and strength as his companions.

However, this came at a costYuans cultivation progress would be slowed down even further. Yet, he was fully aware of this drawback and willingly accepted it, prioritizing Lan Yingyings growth.

Fortunately, the Supreme Seal had no negative impact on Yuans overall prowess. While the Divine Supremes inherited 25 percent of his strength and talents through the seal, this did not mean Yuan himself became 25 percent weaker. Instead, the seal functioned as a conduit, allowing them to draw upon his power directly.

She nodded and turned to look at Elder Qian.

The intensity of her presence was so imposing that even the Midnight Armored Talon, previously unfazed, instinctively recoiled, feeling a genuine sense of threat for the first time.

"Fine," Elder Qian said a moment later. "If you wish to continue, we can do that. However, this time, we wont hold back anymore."

The Midnight Armored Talon let out an eager, piercing cry, as if shaking off its earlier fear. Its aura erupted like a storm, surging to its peak as it fully unleashed its cultivation base, preparing to confront Lan Yingying with all its might.

Lan Yingying leaped gracefully into the air, her movements swift and fluid, before soaring toward the Midnight Armored Talon. The two clashed mid-sky, their battle unleashing shockwaves of energy as they exchanged ferocious blows at each other.

"Impossible! Shes actually fighting on par with my beast?!" Elder Qian exclaimed, her eyes wide with disbelief.

Despite the surge in her aura and presence, Lan Yingyings cultivation level hadnt changed; it remained at the peak of Spirit Enlightenment. Yet, she was somehow able to hold her ground against the Midnight Armored Talon, a beast operating at the full power of the seventh level of Divine Sovereign.

Moreover, Lan Yingyings prowess seemed to intensify with each passing moment, her attacks becoming more powerful and deadly. The sight defied logic, leaving Elder Qian struggling to comprehend the reality unfolding before her.

What is this overwhelming power? Lan Yingying thought, feeling just as astonished as Elder Qian, if not even more so.

I see... this is Yuans power, Lan Yingying quickly realized. And I can feel it growing stronger as my body adapts to it...

As Yuans energy coursed through her entire being, it felt warm and reassuring, almost like she was being embraced by him, and she couldnt help but feel a profound sense of unity with him.

Eventually, Lan Yingying started to push the beast back.

While this replenished some of its depleted reserves, it was only a temporary measure. Against Yuans seemingly endless well of spiritual energy, Elder Qians efforts were no match. Her spiritual energy could only sustain the Midnight Armored Talon for an additional twenty minutes before she, too, began to run dry, leaving her beast vulnerable once more.
