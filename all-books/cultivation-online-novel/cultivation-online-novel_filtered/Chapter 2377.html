Chapter 2377: Demon Gods Wrath

After leaving the Deprived Crimson Valley, Supreme Ruler Dena looked at Yuan and asked, "What now?"

"Before we decide what we want to do, I need to know what you plan on doing now, Lev." Yuan turned to face Lev, who stood still with a dumbfounded look on his face, almost as if he was still having trouble believing that he was outside of the Deprived Crimson Valley.

However, he quickly snapped out of it and said, "Although I am grateful to you for helping me leave the Deprived Crimson Valley, it wont change the fact that I am still an exile, and that my soul is still branded as one. But that wont stop me from avenging my family!"

Yuan then asked Supreme Ruler Dena, "Can you do something about the branding with your Authority?"

She calmly nodded and pointed a finger at Lev.

"..."

Lev swallowed nervously, wondering what she was going to do to him.

The Authority symbol appeared on Supreme Ruler Denas forehead again, and the next second, a single strand of her crimson hair suddenly shot toward Levs forehead, penetrating his skin like it was wet paper and striking his soul.

"Its done," she calmly said as she retrieved her hair.

"What...?"

Lev wore a blank look on his face, as if he didnt even realize what shed done to him. In fact, it happened so fast that he wasnt even able to react!

"Congratulations, Lev. Youre no longer an exile," Yuan said with a faint smile.

Before Lev could respond, Yuan continued, "I wont tell you how to live your life, but I wouldnt recommend dedicating it entirely to revenge. Ive lived that way before, and it leads nowhere good."

Lev lowered his head to stare at the ground for a moment before responding in a low voice, "Then what else am I supposed to do?"

"I survived the Deprived Crimson Valley for so long because of my desire to avenge my family. However, now that I think about it, with how much time has passed, I doubt I will even be able to find the person who killed themif theyre even still alive."

Yuan then spoke, "If you dont know what you want to do, why dont you continue following us for now and think it over? Perhaps you might find a new purpose in life. That said, I cannot guarantee your safety."

Lev didnt need to spend much time pondering about Yuans offer and quickly nodded, "If you dont mind having me, itd be an honor to continue being by your side."

"Now then..." Yuan looked at Supreme Ruler Dena and said, "Lets figure out why you were exiled first before we decide on anything."

"How are we going to do that?"

"We can ask around"

"Please dont do that." Lev suddenly interrupted.

"Have you forgotten? We are prohibited from mentioning her name outside the Deprived Crimson Valley," he reminded Yuan.

"So what? Its not like such rules have never been broken before."

However, instead of Lev, Supreme Ruler Dena spoke, "No, hes right. If the current Supreme Ruler prohibited my name from being mentioned, anyone who utters iteven if nobody is listeningwill be struck by the Demon Gods Wrath."

Yuan raised an eyebrow at this information, wondering if this Demon Gods Wrath was akin to Heavenly Tribulation in his world, where those who defy the Heavenly Laws will be punished.

"This is my first time hearing about such a thing," he remarked.

"What? How is that even possible?" Lev stared at Yuan in disbelief. "The Demon Gods Wrath is basic common sense. Even a newborn knows about it."

"Oh, right." Yuan glanced at him and smiled. "Since youll continue traveling with us from now on, I should probably catch you up on a few things."

"First of all, I am not from this world."

"Huh?" Levs eyes widened as he tried to comprehend Yuans words.

However, Yuan wasnt done, and he continued to bombard Lev with more shocking revelations.

"Not only do I not belong to this world, but Im also not a demon. Im actually a human. Thats why there are many things about this world that I dont know," Yuan revealed.

"Although I visited this place before, its been so long that most of my knowledge is probably no longer reliable."

"..."

After several moments of silence, Lev finally spoke in a nervous tone, "I knew you were strange, but to think youre actually from the world of countless legends... the Divine Heaven!"

He continued, "I read in some ancient history books that the Forsaken used to be sent there to save resources and space, but for some reason, that practice stopped."

"Ahem."

Supreme Ruler Dena suddenly cleared her throat loudly before speaking. "Thats not correct. We didnt send the Forsaken to the Divine Heaven. At the time, we werent even aware of its existence. We only believed we were sending the Forsaken somewhere into the boundless void."

"It wasnt until a certain individual appeared in our world and began wreaking havoc that we realized the truthand the consequences of our careless actions," she continued with a soft sigh, her crimson eyes staring directly at Yuan with a somewhat apologetic gaze.

"Is that so...? I was born after that period, so I had no idea."

"Hm? If youre not from our world, then what were you doing inside the Deprived Crimson Valley? In fact, how the hell did you even get inside?" Lev turned to look at Yuan and asked.

Yuan shrugged and said, "I came here by accident."

"Seriously...? Then youre incredibly lucky that Supreme Ruler"

Lev abruptly sealed his mouth with his hands when he realized that he had nearly committed a serious offense and invoked Demon Gods Wrath.

"That was close. I almost uttered something I shouldnt have."

Yuan raised an eyebrow at his reaction. Then, after a moment of silence, he said, "Lev, move away from me. You too, Dena."

"What are you trying to do?" Lev asked with a puzzled face.

He smiled and calmly replied, "I want to see for myself the prowess of the Demon Gods Wrath and whether its truly threatening or not."

"You want to purposefully invoke Demon Gods Wrath?! Are you insane?!" Lev cried out loud.

Then, without hesitation, he distanced himself from Yuan, moving so far away that he turned into a tiny black dot in the distance.

"Are you really going to do it?" Supreme Ruler Dena asked.

Although her words sounded concerned, her face remained completely calm.

"The Demon Gods Wrath is unfathomably powerful, and depending on the severity of your crime, it could even exterminate a True God," she explained.

Yuan nodded, "I will be fine."

Without another word, Supreme Ruler Dena distanced herself from Yuan. However, unlike Lev, she moved only several meters back and remained very much in view.

Yuan took a deep breath before mumbling in a low voice, "Supreme Ruler Dena."

He immediately prepared to confront the Demon Gods Wrath after uttering her name.

"..."

However, nothing happened even a few seconds later, prompting Yuan to try again, this time louder.

"Supreme Ruler Dena," he spoke in a normal tone.

When nothing happened again, he shouted loudly, "SUPREME RULER DENA!"

Yet, even after waiting for many moments, the Demon Gods Wrath never occurred.

"What the hell?" Yuan cursed out loud before turning to look at Lev. "Are you sure its forbidden to say her name?"

Levs head popped out from behind a tree as he replied, "Of course! I remember very clearly when the Supreme Ruler publicly announced it, as well as the hundreds of Demon Gods Wrath that followed!"

"Then why isnt it coming after me? Is it because I dont belong to this world, so its rules dont apply to me?" he wondered out loud.

"Seriously...?" Lev mumbled in a dumbfounded voice, suddenly feeling an urge to test it out himself.

However, before he could do so, Supreme Ruler Dena beat him to the punch, speaking in a calm voice, "Supreme Ruler Dena."

The very next moment, Yuan sensed a sudden change in the surrounding atmosphere, sending a faint shiver down his body.

"This feeling..."

He subconsciously lifted his head to look at the sky, and sure enough, there was a reaction above. The clouds had darkened and twisted into a swirling vortex that resembled the eye of a hurricane.

Then, from the very center of that eye, a massive crimson beam suddenly descended.

Unsurprisingly, its target was Supreme Ruler Dena.

The beam was so massive that Yuan had to retreat to avoid being caught in it.

And the moment he exited its range, the beam struck Supreme Ruler Dena without mercy, completely engulfing her figure in its massive crimson light.

The Demon Gods Wrath lasted only a little over a second, yet in that brief moment, it carved a massive crater into the groundso deep that its depths were swallowed in pitch-black darkness.

Meanwhile, Supreme Ruler Dena was nowhere to be seen, as if she had been completely vaporized by the attack.
