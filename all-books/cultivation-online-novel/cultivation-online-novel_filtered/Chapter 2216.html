Chapter 2216: Saaruks Threat

When Yuan heard this voice, he immediately delved into his mind, as this voice seemed more than just his imagination.

Within his consciousness, he sensed that a fragment of the Saaruks power had transformed into a kind of link, forging a connection between them. It functioned much like a communication jade slip, allowing their minds to intertwine and exchange words directly.

"Hm? Wait a second... this presence... youre not an Eternal!" Saaruks voice returned before Yuan could respond to it.

"And what are you going to do about it?" Yuan responded in a provocative tone.

"This mighty Saaruk has memorized your scent! The moment you leave the safe zone, this mighty Saaruk will devour you!"
