Of course, Gu Tan wasnt willing to give up so easily, as his parents had assigned him to recruit Yuan with his head on the line.

Thus, he proceeded to follow Yuan and continue to try and recruit him.

"What do you desire, Disciple Yuan? Do you want wealth? Fame? Power? The Gu Family will do everything in their power to fulfill your dreams. If needed, well even get the Gu Family from the Spirit Heaven to help."

Looks like I will have to give up for now and ask him again later. Maybe hes just in a bad mood. Gu Tan tried to soothe himself.

About ten minutes after Gu Tan left Yuan alone, another individual blocked his path.

"Im Xue Yixin, and I was wondering if you have some time to spare so we can talk." This young lady spoke with an enchanting expression on her face.

"What do you want to talk about? Im busy right now, so well have to do it later."
