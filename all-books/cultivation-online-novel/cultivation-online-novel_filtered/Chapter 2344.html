Chapter 2344: Becoming Heavens Envoy

"Y-You... I forbid it!" The Enlightened One bit his lips to resist his fear and shouted at Yuan.

"This position belongs to me! I worked nonstop for over 100,000 years to reach my current status! Who the hell are you to just appear and try take it away from me?! You dont deserve it!"

Yuan chuckled and replied, "You think you deserve this position? If it werent for the Eternal, you wouldnt even be alive right now, you idiot. And all you do is order people around and relay the Eternals message, pretending to be heavens envoy. If you think about it, youve really done nothing deserving of your position, hence why your Master wants me, someone more competent and useful, to replace you."

The Enlightened Ones face drained of color. Even though Yuan had yet to begin usurping his position, the mere weight of his presence was enough to make him feel threatened. Worse still, Yuan had Bahrahs backing, while he himself was forbidden to actforced to stand by and watch as Yuan slowly replaced him.

"Be a good little boy and stay here. I have a lot to do." Yuan turned around and began making his way toward the exit.

Seeing this, the Enlightened One rushed to block the door with his own body, arms spread and everything.

"How cute." Yuan chuckled before walking around him. "Theres nothing you can do to stop me, so just sit back and enjoy whatever time you have left in that position."

The Enlightened One trembled.

"No! I havent even achieved my dream of being the supreme ruler of this world yet! Theres no way I will let you take it from me!"

The Enlightened One then spoke with a determined look on his face, "I may not be able to directly interfere with you, but its not like theres nothing I can do!"

Meanwhile, Patriarch Sun returned to the Celestial Hall after dealing with his business.

"What the...? Hes gone?"

He was dumbfounded to find Yuan missing from the Celestial Hall.

"Did he leave... or did he enter the Celestial Chamber to meet with the Enlightened One?"

Patriarch Sun pondered for a moment before abandoning his latter thought, as there was no way the Enlightened One would invite someone to his chamber.

"After all, even I have not been inside the Celestial Chamber..." Patriarch Sun shook his head.

Just as he was about to turn around and leave in search of Yuan, the double doors swung open, and two figures emerged from the light.

Patriarch Suns eyes widened in disbelief as Yuans face came into clear view. Yet his shock did not end there. The Enlightened One stood right behind him, his veil and hood gone, his face fully exposed.

"Your Majesty!"

Despite his shock, Patriarch Sun subconsciously greeted the Enlightened One.

Yuan raised an eyebrow at the way Patriarch Sun addressed the Enlightened One as if he were already the Celestial Emperor.

"Your Majesty?" Yuan looked at the Enlightened One and poked fun at him. "Even though you have not unified the world yet?"

"S-Shut up!"

Patriarch Sun lifted his head to stare at them with a bewildered expression.

"T-Tian Yang... Y-You..."

"Hm?" Yuan quickly realized why Patriarch Sun was staring at him in disbelief and smiled. "Dont worry, weve become best friends."

Yuan suddenly wrapped his arm around the Enlightened Ones shoulders as if they were best buddies.

"Get your filthy hands off me, you crazy bastard!" The Enlightened One swiftly pushed Yuans arm away.

"Tian Yang! What have you done?!" Patriarch Sun exclaimed in anger. "Touching His Majesty is forbidden and punishable by death!"

"Oh? Are you going to execute me for touching you, Your Majesty?" Yuan asked the Enlightened One with a cold smile.

Go ahead and execute me. Ill kill you before that happens.

Yuan suddenly spoke using voice transmission, causing the Enlightened One to tremble in fear.

"Your Majesty!" Patriarch Sun glared at him.

After a moment of silence, the Enlightened One spoke, "Its fine."

"What?!" Patriarch Sun almost couldnt believe his ears.

"What did I say? Were best friends." Yuan chuckled. "After all, I have also become Heavens Envoy after speaking with Heaven itself!"

"WHAT?!" The Enlightened One and Patriarch Sun exclaimed.

This sly bastard! I didnt think hed use me to turn himself into Heavens Envoy! The Enlightened One cried inwardly after realizing his mistake.

"Why are you so surprised?" Yuan asked the Enlightened One. "We just finished speaking with Heaven. Or are you saying that I am lying?"

"..." The Enlightened One gritted his teeth.

If he were to invalidate Yuans claim, hed be interfering with Yuan and denying Heavens Will. In other words, defying Bahrahs orders.

"I-Its true..." he spoke in a trembling voice. "He has spoken with Heaven and received its approval..."

"T-This is...!" Patriarch Sun was speechless.

Yuan couldnt help but laugh inwardly.

Oh, Celestial Emperor... youre too young and naive right now, making it incredibly easy to manipulate you!

Having spent most of his time in isolation within the Celestial Chamber and rarely interacting with others, the Enlightened One lacked real experience. Moreover, a lifetime of absolute authority had left him surrounded by a fragile wall of perceived safety. Yet that same fragility meant that once someone shattered that illusion and stepped into his personal domain as a threat, he had no idea how to respond and would panic easily.

Furthermore, Yuan had already instilled fear in the Enlightened One with a genuine threat of death, making it even harder for him to think clearly.

Yuan took advantage of this situation and spoke again, "Not only have I received Heavens approval, but I have also received many divinations from Heaven that will allow me to unify the world within ten thousand years."

"What?! Thats impossible!" The Enlightened One exclaimed.

Yuan looked at him with a puzzled face and said, "Didnt Heaven tell you? I guess Heaven is really disappointed by your underwhelming performance, hence why it asked me to take charge."

"Y-You...!" The Enlightened One gritted his teeth and trembled in anger, but his mind was in too much of a mess to think of a proper response.
