Chapter 2215: Saaruk

Sometime later, Xiao Meilin brought Yuan to the Spirit Tree, which was located only a few miles away from the Asura Clans residence.

Shortly after they left the residence, Mu Xuelian, who followed them, spoke, "Yuan, Lord Shiva has something to tell you."

"Hm? What is it?" Yuan turned to look at her.

"She said that man doesnt have long to live."

"What?" Yuan raised an eyebrow.

"That first-level God Ascension cultivator we were just with."

"Cangming? What is her reason for saying such a thing?" Yuan immediately stopped moving and glared at her with a serious frown.

Mu Xuelian closed her eyes and, after a moment of silence, responded in a different tone.

"That man has been afflicted with a particular technique by a certain Eternal," said Shiva, who just took control of Mu Xuelians body.

"You mean his illness? So an Eternal was involved, after all..." Yuan sighed.

"What are you talking about?" Xiao Meilin asked.

Yuan explained calmly, "The beings we know as Outer Gods refer to themselves as Eternalsand your fathers illness was caused by one of them."

"And how does she know that?" Xiao Meilin glared at Mu Xuelian with a suspicious face.

"Long story short, theres an Eternal living within her."

Xiao Meilins eyes narrowed at the revelation. "I knew there was something off about her the moment I saw her," she muttered. "Now I understand why."

"Master, why are you traveling with someone like that? Arent you enemies with these Outer Gods?"

He nodded, "Yes, but there are many of them out there, and I am not at war with all of them."

"Is that so? Well, this is news to me," said Xiao Meilin.

"Anyway, what was that about father? Hes going to die soon? Can you elaborate on that? Hes managed to survive tens of millions of years with this illness, after all."

"Thats because the technique used on him wasnt meant to kill him quickly," Shiva said. "It was designed to inflict pain for as long as possible. Tens of millions of years may seem long to mortalsbut to us Eternals, its nothing. In fact, I have taken naps far longer than that."

"Since you know what it is, do you know how to cure him?" Yuan then asked.

Shiva calmly nodded, "Of course. Its actually very simple."

"Im listening."

"You just need to absorb it."

"What?" Yuan raised an eyebrow.

"Have you forgotten the essence of our powers alreadythe power of your physique?"

"You mean I can just refine the illness into essence and absorb it, turning it into my power?" Yuan asked with a somewhat dumbfounded face.

Shiva silently nodded.

"In that case, lets return now," Yuan said to Xiao Meilin.

"What about the Spirit Tree? Were about to arrive."

"That can wait. Your father will be crafting Xiao Huas vessel soon, and I dont want anything to go wrong when that happens."

Yuan didnt want to risk Xiao Cangmings illness affecting him during the creation of Xiao Huas vessel and decided to take care of it as soon as possible. Moreover, Xiao Cangmings life was on the line.

"If you say so."

Xiao Meilin didnt argue and immediately turned around.

Sometime later, they returned to the room where Xiao Cangming and Li Zhenwu were.

"Youre back already? Did you forget something?" Xiao Cangming asked.

"No. I came back because I learned about the cause of your illness and the method to cure it," said Yuan.

"What?" Xiao Cangming stared at him with a bewildered face.

"How did you acquire this knowledge in such a short time?"

After all, theyd only been gone for a few minutes.

Yuan looked at Mu Xuelian and explained, "Actually, there is an Outer God dwelling within my friend here, and she was the one who told me about it."

"Thats..."

Xiao Cangming was left speechless. As far as he was aware, all Outer Gods were enemies.

Seeing his reaction, Yuan quickly explained, "Just like humans, not every Outer God is friendly with each other, and they even fight amongst themselves. In other words, not all of them are our enemies."

"I see... So, what about my illness?"

Mu Xuelian then spoke, "According to Lord Shiva, its a technique used by the one known as Saaruk, meant to torment you with unbearable pain."

"Then what about the treatment?"

"I just need to absorb it," Yuan replied.

"Didnt you try that already?"

"Thats different, since I treated it like a poison. What I am going to do this time is entirely different."

"It sounds risky. How risky is it?" Xiao Cangming asked with a slight frown.

"Thats... it shouldnt be too risky, right?" Yuan turned to look at Mu Xuelian.

"Lord Shiva said its not risky, but it will be very painful for you, the absorber, since youre essentially consuming the power of an Eternal."

"You heard her. Its not risky." Yuan said with a calm smile.

"When do you plan on absorbing it?" Xiao Cangming then asked.

"Right now."

"Right now? But were about to start crafting Xiao Huas vessel."

"Thats exactly why we must do it now. I dont want anything to go wrong, and since theres a chance the illness might strike during the process, I want to deal with it before you start."

Xiao Cangming couldnt refute his reasoning. Since it was true that the illness could surface at unpredictable intervals, Yuans concern was entirely justified.

"Alright, lets get this over with." Xiao Cangming nodded.

Hearing this, Yuan gestured for Xiao Cangming to take a seat before him.

Once Xiao Cangming was seated, Yuan took his place behind him and rested both hands against his back.

Yuan closed his eyes and started trying to sense the illness in Xiao Cangmings body.

Li Zhenwu, who had been focused on the ingredients, paused and watched them in silence, a faint trace of worry flickering in her eyes.

A few minutes later, once Yuan found the source of the illness, he said, "I am going to start now."

Then, he started to refine its very essence.

Shortly after Yuan started to absorb it, a loud and unfamiliar voice thundered inside his head.

"Which bastard dares to covet this mighty Saaruks power?!"
