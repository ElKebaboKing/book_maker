Chapter 2106: Stolen Treasures(2)

Seeing Zhou Yanfei and the other Demon Sealers kneeling on the floor, their faces twisted in grief as if they had just witnessed the slaughter of their entire families, Yuan spoke, "By the time I defeated the traitors and reached the vault... the treasures were already gone. Im sorry."

"..."

None of the Demon Sealers responded.

"Though I did encounter the thief, who was on his way out. He was at the fourth level of God Ascension, so there was nothing I couldve done to stop him," Yuan added.

Upon hearing this, Zhou Yanfei slowly turned to face Yuan, his eyes narrowing sharply.

"You encountered the thief... and survived? No, somethings not right. You dont even look injured, not in the slightest. Its almost as if he let you go without even trying to kill you."

He paused, voice hardening.

"No thief in their right mind would go through all that trouble to infiltrate our vault... only to leave an eyewitness behind."

"Thats"

Before Yuan could say another word, Zhou Yanfei suddenly lunged from his kneeling position, his hand shooting toward Yuans throat, overflowing with uncontrolled killing intent.

"You!"

His actions were too sudden, and Yan Hara failed to react in time. By the time she even realized what was happening, Zhou Yanfeis fingers were already at Yuans neck.

As for Yuan, he stood still, almost as if he also failed to react. However, his eyes were following Zhou Yanfeis movements perfectly. It wasnt that he couldnt react. He simply didnt move on purpose so Zhou Yanfei could hit him.

In the very next instant, Zhou Yanfeis hand reached Yuans neck, fingers tightening as if to rip his throat out. But just as he made contact, his movements froze.

However, it wasnt because Zhou Yanfei had a change of heart, or that hed suddenly snapped out of his subconscious assault.

"What in heavens name...?" Zhou Yanfei muttered, staring at his hands frozen around Yuans neck, eyes wide with disbelief and confusion.

Despite pouring nearly all of his strength into the strike, he couldnt harm Yuan in the slightest. In fact, it felt as if his fingers had slammed into solid diamond.

Zhou Yanfei was currently at the third level of God Ascension, and even though he didnt use Celestial Qi in his strike, it shouldve been more than enough to slaughter a Golden Immortal like Yuan with extreme ease.

Yet, he couldnt even damage the skin on Yuans body, much less kill him.

"Haa..." Yuan released a soft sigh before speaking, "You should really let people finish their sentences."

"I"

"You old bastard! What do you think youre doing?!" Yan Hara kicked Zhou Yanfei in the face right as he opened his mouth, sending him flying into the empty treasure pedestal.

"Master Zhou!"

"Leader!"

The Demon Sealers exclaimed in shock after seeing their leader sent flying.

"I-Im okay..."

Since Yan Hara only used her natural strength, Zhou Yanfei only sustained minimal injuries.

"Anyway, as I was going to say, while the thief tried to kill me, he simply didnt have the capability, and not wanting to risk being captured, he fled after failing to kill me for a few minutes," Yuan said a moment later.

"What the hell is your body made of?" Zhou Yanfei asked after a long moment of silence.

"The same things as most normal people. Flesh and bone."

"An ordinary person wouldnt be able to escape unscathed after being attacked by a God Ascension cultivator while only being an Immortal."

Yuan casually shrugged.

Yan Hara then spoke, "What the hell was even stolen for you to have acted like that?"

Zhou Yanfeis face darkened after being reminded of the stolen treasures, and with great difficulty, responded, "The Demon Sealing Scroll and the Demon Sealing Talisman."

"WHAT?!" Yan Hara screamed, her voice so sharp it nearly pierced their eardrums upon hearing the names of the stolen treasures.

"The Demon Sealing Scroll and the Demon Sealing Talisman? Arent those the Divine Paragons treasures?" Yuan asked, pretending to be ignorant.

"Precisely," confirmed Zhou Yanfei.

Snapping out of her daze, Yan Hara stormed over to Zhou Yanfei, seized him by the collar, and began shaking him violently.

"Precisely, my ass!" she shouted. "How the hell could you let the Divine Paragons treasures be stolen?!"

Zhou Yanfei slapped her hands off him and shouted back, "Do you think I want them to be stolen?! We locked them in formations that were even stronger than the ones protecting the entire vault!"

"That doesnt say much when your vault was cracked this easily!"

"Lets stop this pointless argument and try to figure out a solution," one of the Demon Sealers suddenly said.

He turned to look at Yuan and asked, "Do you have a description of the thief? Any information about him is fine."

Yuan immediately recalled all of the thiefs features, everything from his ordinary facial features to his foot size.

"What about his techniques? Did you notice anything when he tried to kill you?"

Yuan pondered for a moment before responding, "No, he didnt use any techniques on me and tried to kill me with brute force."

"I understand. Since its essentially impossible to find him with this little information, well have to interrogate the Demon Worshippers we captured, as well as the traitors."

"Master Zhou, please get yourself together. Two of the Divine Paragons treasures were stolen. We dont have the time or energy to waste on bickering."

Surprisingly, the Demon Sealers lectured him.

Even more surprisingly, Zhou Yanfei wasnt offended by it and even thanked them.

"Youre right. My apologiesI acted in a manner unworthy of my position."

Once he had regained his composure, Zhou Yanfei turned to Yan Hara and said, "I will take full responsibility for this incident. An investigation will begin immediately. I hope youll consider staying a while longer, but if you choose to leave now, I wont stop you."

Yan Hara looked at Yuan, who nodded and said, "I have a few months."

"Then I will also stay," Yan Hara said afterward.
