Chapter 2030: Sword Emperor Qualifications

"Ill let Overlord Bai know that youre here," Kelan said as he sent a message to Senior Bai through his communication jade slip.

Meanwhile, Yuan began recalling his experience at the Holy Sword Clan and the Holy Sword Temple. The others didnt have much to talk about since they spent all of their time training.

"Although were spending all of our time training now, well be leaving the sect to train outside and gain real experience soon," said Wang Ming.

Kelan nodded and said, "Dont worry, Ill make sure to have some experts accompany them."

"Im not worried about them," Yuan said with a smile, and he continued, "But I am worried about the trouble they might cause."

"Youre one to talk!" Shi Lang immediately said.

"Right? Youre always getting into trouble, Yuan. At least thats what it seems like from the stories you tell us," said Wang Bingbing.

"As much as I love you, I have to agree with the others." Chu Liuxiang shook her head with a smile on her face.

Meixiu quietly nodded her head.

Seeing this, Yuan burst out laughing.

"Youre right, but its not like I am purposefully looking for trouble. They just somehow come to me."

Some time later, a familiar figure approached from a distance, descending gracefully and landing right in front of them.

"This makes it our first time seeing each other outside of the Myriad of Techniques," Senior Bai said to Yuan with a warm smile on his face.

Yuan returned the smile and said, "Yes, it does. Thank you for always taking care of me."

"Since youre here... does that mean youve learned Supreme Sword Aura?" Senior Bai then asked.

Kelan had only mentioned Yuans arrival and nothing else, so Senior Bai could experience the same surprise hed felt.

Yuan nodded.

"Yes, I have already qualified for the Sword Emperor Qualifications."

He revealed the medallion he received from the Sect Elder.

Senior Bai chuckled and said, "I heard. I also know that you broke the crystal, causing us to delay the event for a whole month."

"Sorry..."

"Its fine."

Yuan then asked, "Is Sister Xu here?"

Senior Bai shook his head.

"No, shes too busy to come down to the Seventh Heaven, but shell be watching it."

"I see..."

"Anyway, I have to return to work now. Lets talk again later. If you need anything, just ask Kelan," said Senior Bai before flying away.

Shortly after he left, Senior Bai made a quick announcement to the people there.

"Due to some unexpected circumstances, we have to delay the Sword Emperor Qualification for a month. We apologize for the delay and any inconvenience this may have caused and hope for your understanding."

Surprisingly, there were no complaints from the audience, or there were so few that it was unnoticeable.

"The crowd is more calm than I had expected," Wang Ming commented, as hed anticipated some complaints."The Sword Emperor Qualifications can easily last several years, so everyone here is already prepared for a long stay. An extra month is nothing," Kelan said.

"Several years?! What about our training?" Xi Murong exclaimed in shock, as hed only anticipated spending a few weeks or months at most at this event.

Kelan shrugged and said, "Youre free to leave at any time you want. The whole thing will be recorded anyway, so you wont miss anything but the experience."

"..."

A few years may seem like nothing to Immortals or those who have already lived thousands of years. However, for Xi Murong and the others who have only lived for a few decades, a few years was a lot of time, and they were still at a point where their progress could be seen quickly.

"You dont have to force yourself to stay here," Yuan suddenly said. "Just stay as long as you want."

"Im staying for the entire event," Chu Liuxiang said firmly. "A few years mean nothing in the grand scheme of thingsbut missing such a pivotal moment in my partners life? Thats unthinkable."

"Me too," Meixiu added. "But you dont have to force yourself to stay because we choose to stay. We all have different priorities, after all."

"I guess well see how things go," said Wu Zao. "If its entertaining enough, Ill stay. Otherwise, Ill just return to my training."

"Me too," said Hong Xiuquan.

"You cant even take a small break? And you call me a training addict?" Li Jinxi shook her head.

Wang Bing chuckled and said, "Were all training addicts."

In the end, everyone but Li Jinxi, Meixiu, and Chu Liuxiang decided to watch until they couldnt anymore.

Time passed, and two weeks later, while Yuan was chatting with everyone, a figure descended onto their area.

Kelan raised an eyebrow after seeing this individual, and he asked, "What are you doing here, Sword Empress Huang?"

"I dont have business with you," she said before pointing to Yuan.

"Since we have some time, lets spar."

"No." Yuan immediately refused, which took her by surprise.

"W-Why not?!"

"Why should I? The Sword Emperor Qualification is about to begin, and you want me to waste my energy sparring with you? If youre trying to help those two, dont bother." Yuan shook his head.

"What? Are you talking about the Holy Sword Maiden and the Holy Sword King? Im not asking for a spar because I want to help those two. I just want to experience your Supreme Sword Aura myself," she defended herself.

However, Yuan refused again, "If you want to spar, we can do it after I become a Sword Emperor."

Sword Empress Huangs eyes widened.

"You speak as if youve already won. Do you really think the Sword Emperor Qualification is that easy?"

"I hope not, or it wouldnt be fun."

"Fun...? You think this is a game?" Sword Empress Huang frowned, clearly offended by his words.

For the rare few swordmasters granted the chance to participate, the Sword Emperor Qualifications represented the pinnacle of their livesan opportunity to carve their names into history. Yet Yuan spoke of it as if it were nothing more than a casual pastime.

Any swordmaster would have taken offense, as this was a sacred trial, not something to be taken lightly or treated as entertainment.
