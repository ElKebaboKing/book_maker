Chapter 2202: The Chaotic Sects Response

"What Alchemist Shen said is logical. If those two are truly connected to the Asura Clan in some way and we kill them, itd spell the end of our faction," one of the elders said, breaking the silence.

"Then what should we do with them? The chances of them being friends with the Asura Clan are slim to none, and if we bother the Asura Clan, it might affect our relationship with them," said another elder.

"Why dont we hold off on contacting the Asura Clan and examine them for a little while before we make any decision?"

"Thats a good idea. We can just stall this for a little. If they turn out to be troublemakers, well kill them."

Though the Inferno Alchemists stance might seem excessive, it revealed the depth of their fear toward the Asura Clan. In the Primordial Realm, even something as simple as approaching them to confirm an identity was anything but simple.

"Ill let them know," said Alchemist Shen, who guided Yuan and Mu Xuelian to their headquarters.

While the elders continued to contemplate the situation some more, Alchemist Shen returned to Yuans guest house.

"We will be sending someone to the Asura Clan to confirm your identity soon," Alchemist Shen said to Yuan.

"So, how long is this going to take?"

"Im not sure... but well let you know immediately when we receive an answer."

Yuan narrowed his eyes slightly and said, "Theres no way it should take more than a few hours, a few days at most. Dont you have something to contact them directly?"

Alchemist Shen looked at him with a dumbfounded face and said, "Only our leader has direct communication with the Asura Clan, but even then, he can only use it during emergencies."

Yuan sighed out loud, "Why are they so hard to contact?"

Alchemist Shen remained silent.

"In any case, we just need to wait, right? Alright, but dont take too long."

"Thank you for your understanding."

Meanwhile, at the Chaotic Sect, the cultivator who managed to survive revealed the situation to the Sect Leader and other elders.

"What did you say?! A mere True Immortal killed four God Ascension cultivators?! Do you really expect me to believe that?!" The leader of the Chaotic Sect angrily shouted after receiving this news from the sole survivor.

"I swear on my soul its true!"

The room fell silent for a moment.

"Sect Leader Zu! We must avenge Elder Zi and the others!" One of the elders suddenly exclaimed.

"How dare that bastard kill people from our Chaotic Sect! Our elders, no less!"

Hearing that they wanted revenge, the cultivator quickly said, "No! We shouldnt!"

Everyone in the room turned to look at the cultivator with deep frowns on their faces.

"Elder Jiang, what are you saying? Are you telling us not to avenge Elder Zi and the others? Are you out of your damn mind?!"

"Did you get brainwashed by that bastard or something, Elder Jiang?"

"Y-You dont understand! That guy wasnt human! He killed everyone in the blink of an eye, and he did so with immense ease! He might also have connections with the Asura Clan!" Elder Jiang exclaimed.

"T-The Asura Clan...?"

The atmosphere in the room instantly changed at the mere mention of the Asura Clan.

"Elder Jiang." The Sect Leader suddenly spoke in a chilling tone, causing him to tremble. "Putting the issue with the Asura Clan aside, are you claiming that a single Immortal is stronger than the entire Chaotic Sect, so we should not offend him?"

"Thats..." Elder Jiang could not find a proper response.

After a moment of silence, he shook his head and responded, "No, unless hes as powerful as the head of the Asura Clan, theres no way a single person could threaten our Chaotic Sect. I apologize. It seems I havent been thinking straight from fear."

"Very well. Now, lets get back to the other subject. You said he has a connection with the Asura Clan. How certain are you on that?"

"Thats... Honestly, Im not very certain. He had me bring him to the Burning Tree. If he truly had a connection with the Asura Clan, he wouldnt need me to take him there. However, theres something about him that reminds me of those from the Asura Clan."

The Sect Leader stood up and said, "Regardless of whether hes related to the Asura Clan or not, I will not let someone who killed four of our people get away with it. Gather all of the elders. Were going to avenge the others."

"As you command!"

A week later, at the Inferno Alchemists headquarters.

"Master! We have an emergency! The Chaotic Sects leader and many of their members are currently at our gates!"

"The Chaotic Sect? What do they want from us?" The leader of the Inferno Alchemist was immediately alarmed by this news.

"Theyre demanding your presence, and thats all we know!"

"What a bunch of arrogant bastards. Are they trying to start a war with us or something?"

The leader of the Inferno Alchemist went to meet with the Chaotic Sect shortly after.

"Sect Leader Zu, what is the meaning of this? To show up unannounced with so many of your elders... are you trying to invade us? Do you not fear the Asura Clan?"

Sect Leader Zu quickly responded, "Dont worry, Leader Yang. We have no business with the Inferno Alchemists today. Were here for that bastard who came to your place recentlythe one whos trying to go to the Burning Tree. Is he still here?"

Leader Yang responded, "Yes, hes still here. Why are you looking for him?"

"That bastard killed four of our elders, and were here to make him pay for his crimes."

"What...?" Leader Yang wore a dumbfounded look on his face as he was fed this information.

"Are you sure you have the right person? That man is only a True Immortal, and your elders are all at least God Ascension cultivators. Yet you claim he killed four of them?" he asked.
