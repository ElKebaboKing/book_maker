Chapter 2074: Sealed Outer God

After a brief silence, Yuan spoke again, his voice calm yet tinged with amusement. "Well, Outer God or not, theres nothing I can do about this situation. And if you really are an Outer God, you wouldnt be trapped behind this door by a few formations. Arent you lot supposed to be all-powerful?"

BANG!

In response, the Outer God slammed against the gate, and in the very next instant, every formation protecting it shattered like fragile glass.

Yuan was jumpscared and immediately distanced himself from the gate.

However, nothing happened even after many moments, almost as if the formations had no effect on the gates in the first place.

"It appears that the formations werent holding it back in the first place, but something else," Yu Ning muttered.

"The hell? Then what was the point of that?" Yuan remarked.

"It was probably made as an assurance, but who really knows..."

"If its not the formations keeping it inside, then what is...?" Yuan mumbled as he stared at the sealed gate.

Suddenly, Yuan noticed a trail of red smoke seeping through the narrow gap between the gates. His body instinctively shifted into a battle-ready stance, preparing for whatever was about to emerge.

The smoke began to twist and swirl, slowly forming words until an entire wall of text hovered in the air before Yuan. However, the words werent written in any human language and they closely resembled the strange inscriptions carved into the gate itself.

Since Tian Yang had deciphered the inscriptions in Han Zexians Cavern, he could somewhat understand the words before him.

"This is... a technique," Yuan muttered with raised eyebrows.

"A technique?" Yu Ning repeated.

"Im not certain, but I think so."

Yuan sat down and closed his eyes after memorizing the entire.

A few hours later, he opened his eyes and spoke, "Its definitely a technique, but I dont understand their language enough to decipher it right now. It will take some time."

Yuan stood back up and spoke out loud, "Why cant you just use my language? You clearly understand me, so why cant you use it?"

"..."

After a brief moment of silence, the red smoke began to shift once more.

Then, two massive words formed before him.

"OPEN SEAL"

This time, Yuan could read the words.

"Are you telling me that the technique just now would open the seal to this gate?"

Bang!

The Outer God acknowledged with a loud bang.

"So the gate can only be opened from the outside, huh? Anyway, why should I help you escape? Youre an Outer God, and as far as I am aware, youre nothing but bad news. It would benefit me and the entire world if you remained in there for an eternity."

The red smoke changed again, this time forming into a single word.

"FATE"

"..."

Yuan silently frowned at this word. Despite its vagueness, Yuan knew what the Outer God was trying to saythat they were connected in some way and that fate would lead him to opening the gate eventually.

Although Yuan did not like the idea of unsealing this unknown Outer God, he knew that not all Outer Gods were evil, as hed recieved help from Shura.

If its true that were connected by fate, I will definitely remove the seal and release this Outer God eventually... Yuan sighed inwardly.

Who knows? Maybe I have another Outer God as an ally and just dont remember. Whatever. Ill worry about it once I actually decypher the technique.

"Can you not tell me the technique using my language?"

"..."

No response.

If he cannot translate the technique, then its likely because the technique would not make sense in our language. A technique used by Outer Gods, huh? How intriguing...

Then, he spoke a moment later, "In that case, I can only return here later once I decipher the technique and learn it, so wait a little longer."

Bang!

The Outer God acknowledged.

"Then Ill leave now."

Yuan turned around and left. This time, the Outer God did not try to stop him.

Once Yuan left the cavern, Yu Ning asked him, "Are you really going to release whatever is inside? I mean, it was probably sealed for a reason, right?"

"I doubt it was humans who sealed it. If its truly an Outer God, then it was likely sealed by another Outer God. Im also not sure what I should do. I guess Ill figure it out as I try to decypher the technique."

"By the way, whats an Outer God?" Yu Ning suddenly asked.

Though shed listened to his conversation with it just now, she didnt understand its existence.

Yuan shook his head and said, "I would tell you, but it might destroy your soul."

"So theyre an existence beyond my understanding, huh?" Yu Ning remarked.

"Youre quite perceptive." Yuan smiled.

"Probably because Ive already experience this before."

"You what?" Yuan suddenly stopped moving.

Yu Ning explained, "During the Primordial Era, shortly after cultivators gained the ability to traverse the Starry Sky, some claimed to have seen things out thereentities so incomprehensible that those without sufficient soul strength would perish just from hearing about it. Some even began to revere them as gods. I believe they were also called True Rulers of the Universe."

"True Rulers of the Universe..." Yuan mumbled.

"What else do you know about them?"

"Not much. Since no one could prove their existenceand those who claimed to witness them couldnt truly share what theyd seenthey eventually faded into myth. Some even consider it nothing more than a hoax," she said.

"Then, once the war with the Celestial Emperor begun, it became irrelevant to most people."

"I see... Well, in that case, theres a good chance these True Rulers of the Universe are Outer Gods," said Yuan.

"Theyre basically beings that exist exclusively within the Starry Sky, so I cant fathom why one would be sealed here in the Nine Heavensor what it must have done to be sealed away. Theyre also unimaginably powerful. Even a Cultivation God would be like an ant in their eyes."

"A Cultivation God... akin to an ant...?" Yu Ning whispered, swallowing nervously at his words.
