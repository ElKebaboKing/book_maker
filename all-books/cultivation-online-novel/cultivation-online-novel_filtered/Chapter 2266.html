Chapter 2266: The Great Mammoth

"Theres a peculiar rumor going around," Ren Xia started a conversation as she rested beside Tian Yang on the bed.

"Is the rumor related to the Immortal Clans?" Tian Yang asked.

"No, not exactly."

"Then it doesnt concern me," he said in a blunt, indifferent tone.

"Are you sure youre not interested? You might regret it."

Tian Yang narrowed his eyes slightly. Knowing Ren Xias character, she wouldnt be so persistent in a subject if it wasnt serious or if it was related to him.

"If theres something you want to tell me, just tell me. No need to beat around the bush," he sighed.

Ren Xia suddenly lifted herself and positioned herself on top of Tian Yang, sitting on his body. Then, with an entertained smile, she spoke, "According to this rumor, someone claiming to be the Great Mammoth recently declared himself as the ruler of the Desolate Continent."

"Great Mammoth?" Tian Yang raised his eyebrows at this familiar name.

Ren Xia gave a calm nod and continued, "And from what witnesses say, hes a complete monster, standing almost four meters tall with an unprecedented physique."

Tian Yang shot up from the bed after hearing the details, his eyes wide with shock.

"Oh!" Ren Xia, who was sitting on top of him, fell backwards but gracefully caught herself.

"Doesnt that sound like someone we know?" she chuckled.

"It has to be Kulas!" Tian Yang exclaimed.

He finally realized why the name Great Mammoth was familiar. It was the name of the technique hed given KulasGreat Mammoth Body Refinement!

"What are the Immortal Clans reaction? Have they done anything yet?" he then asked.

Ren Xia curled her arms around his neck, letting out a soft sigh.

"I wish youd show me even a little of the passion you always have for Kulas."

"Are you saying that youd rather be in Kulas situation rather than what you currently have right now?"

Ren Xia fell silent for a moment before speaking again, "I dont think the Immortal Clan is aware of his identity."

She completely changed the topic. Clearly, she was satisfied with her situation after giving it some thought

"What makes you think so?" Tian Yang asked.

"Well, this rumor has been floating around for two weeks already. Theres no way the Immortal Clans havent heard it, but they havent shown the slightest response. Theyve been behaving as if nothing happened."

"Moreover, unless they learned about the Great Mammoth Body Refinement technique from Kulas, they wont immediately make the connections just by his size. With that said, even if they realize his identity, I doubt theyll do anything to him now, especially how much weve weakened them."

It had been over three hundred years since the fall of the Immortal Sword Clan, and throughout that time, the two of them relentlessly chipped away at the Immortal Clans strength. Once untouchable, those clans were now mere shadows of their former power.

Even if they were aware of Kulas return, he would be at the very bottom of the priority list with Tian Yang and Ren Xia at the very top.

Naturally, Tian Yang and Ren Xias infamy wasnt confined to the Immortal Clans alone. Across the whole Divine Heaven, their names resounded with fear and awe alikeTian Yang, the Immortal Slayer, and Ren Xia, the Sword Goddess.

While their names struck fear into the hearts of many, even becoming a warning used to discipline unruly children, just as many revered them. After all, they had done the unthinkable by defying the Immortal Clans and driving them to near ruin. freewebnovel.com

Even if they were to die tomorrow at the hands of the Immortal Clans, the clans reputation would never recover, and the shadow of Tian Yang and Ren Xia would forever loom over them.

In fact, after so many years of failure, it wasnt just the ordinary peopleeven the Immortal Clans themselves no longer believed they could defeat Tian Yang and Ren Xia.

"I guess so..." Tian Yang muttered with a pondering look on his face.

"Are you going to see him?" Ren Xia suddenly asked.

Tian Yang closed his eyes and remained silent for a moment.

"No," he replied in a clear voice.

"Really?" Ren Xias eyes widened with surprise, as she had already prepared to travel to the Desolate Continent with him.

"Why not?" she inquired.

"He essentially vanished the day we rescued him. Now that I know hes alive and well, I wont endanger him by dragging him into our mess. If Im going to meet him again, itll be after weve completely erased the Immortal Clans from existence," he said, his voice filled with resolve.

If they were to meet Kulas right now, the Immortal Clan would definitely go after Kulas, too.

"That said, I should still keep an eye on him just in case anything happens," he added a moment later.

"How are you going to do that without getting close to him?"

"Ill hire somebody to watch over him."

"Youre acting like a stalker." Ren Xia chuckled.

"If thats how it seems, then so be it. As long as I can protect him properly this time, nothing else matters."

With that resolve, Tian Yang and Ren Xia intensified their assaults on the Immortal Clans, moving with a slightly greater urgency than before, as though something had spurred them forward.

Time continued to pass, and as the Immortal Clans grew weaker and lost influence, Kulasnow known as the Great Mammothrose in the opposite direction. He became a powerhouse and an authoritative figure within the Desolate Continent, eventually establishing himself as its undisputed ruler.

Although Tian Yang sought the downfall of the Immortal Clans, he never confronted them through outright slaughter. He wasnt looking for massacres. If he had truly wanted to destroy them, he could have easily wiped out every member of their clans besides the Patriarchs.

Instead, he chose a different approach and targeted their businesses and allies, undermining their influence and shattering their reputation, only killing when absolutely necessary.
