Chapter 2238: Unknown Eternal

After reading all of Saaruks memories, the unknown Eternal withdrew its glowing string from his body and fell silent.

"!!!"

"B-But Saaruk has already killed that mortal!"

"Thats correct, but theres no way he couldve survived twenty percent of Saaruks power! Its simply impossible!"

Saaruk refused to believe that Yuan had survived his attack, especially after hed sacrificed a portion of his own strength to ensure the mortals destruction.

"Impossible, you say? That mortal has already defied logic and done the impossible multiple times. Even if theres only a slight chance that the mortal is alive, Im willing to bet on that chance. After all, its not like theres anything else for me to do right now."

"..."

Saaruk was speechless.

"I was going to punish you for destroying my resources, but since you have made such an exciting discovery, Ill forgive you this time," said the unknown Eternal.

Saaruk sighed in relief after hearing this. There were very few thingsenough to count with one handthat Eternals feared in this universe, and this unknown Eternal was one of them.

As for the reason, it was pretty simple. This unknown Eternal possessed such overwhelming strength that it could instill fear in other Eternals.

"Saaruk is grateful," he said.

The unknown Eternal no longer spoke, almost as if theyd left. Sure enough, Saaruk could feel the unknown Eternals presence getting further and further away.

While one might assume that the unknown Eternal could reach Yuans location swiftly, in truth, it would take many years to traverse such an immense distance, even with its prowess and the Power of the Void. The only reason Saaruk had found Yuan so quickly was because hed already been nearby, and the only reason he returned to his true body so fast was due to being in his ethereal form.

As for the unknown Eternal, Saaruk could tell that they were traveling with their true body.

Meanwhile, after Saaruk had left, Yuan poured all of his power into defending against Saaruks attack, which contained 20 percent of his true prowess.

Unfortunately, there was simply no way he could completely defend it, and the most he could do was weaken it and hope for the best. As for trying to avoid it... the attack was as massive as a star, making evasion impossible unless his Power of the Void reached a certain level that allowed him to travel through the void.

Right before the attack reached him, Yuan activated the Robes of Destructions ability.

<Dragon of Destructions Ultimate Chaos activated>

When Saaruks attack finally struck, the Robes of Destruction absorbed as much of the energy as it could. Afterward, the unthinkable happened.

Despite being said to be indestructible, it shredded into countless pieces and was reduced to nothing.

<Robes of Destruction have been destroyed>

Although the treasure was said to be indestructible by the system, it was a system that did not take into account Eternals, godly beings with unfathomable prowess.

However, even though the Robes of Destruction were destroyed beyond repair, it still managed to do its job, absorbing as much of Saaruks attack as it could, even rebounding what it absorbed as it was destroyed.

Because of the Robes of Destruction, Saaruks attack was weakened even further.

But even with that, Saaruks attack remained immensely powerful and still a threat to Yuans life.

Suddenly, Number One Under Heaven trembled, and Zi Xuans spirit emerged from it.

Without uttering a word, she swung her arm at Saaruks attack and unleashed a powerful aura that was similar to Supreme Sword Aura but was clearly different at the same time.

The sword energy clashed with Saaruks attack and managed to endure for a full second before being destroyed.

After unleashing her attack, Zi Xuan quietly returned to Number One Under Heaven and immediately went into a deep slumber after exhausting all of her energy.

Although Zi Xuan couldnt stop Saaruks attack, she managed to weaken it some more.

After Zi Xuan disappeared, Dragon Gods Vengeance appeared of its own will, and Goddess Yeyou emerged from it.

She cast a quick glance at Yuan before turning toward Saaruks attack, her mouth opening wide. In the next instant, a radiant beam erupted forth, piercing through Saaruks assault like a needle tearing through silk.

Saaruks attack dispersed like mist for a fleeting moment before reforming, but its strength had drastically weakened. What once carried the might to annihilate even a Cultivation God with ease was now weak enough for Yuan to defend against it.

"Thank you all," Yuan said as Dragon Goddess Yeyou disappeared alongside the Dragon Gods Vengeance.

Now that Saaruks attack was weak enough, Yuan faced it with what little Eternal Essence he had left before leaving the rest for his physique to deal with.

<Eternal Constitution(Incomplete) activated>

<Through Saaruks Essence refinement, your understanding of Eternal Essence has improved drastically>

Since hed already refined Saaruks powers once, he was able to do so again with much more ease.
