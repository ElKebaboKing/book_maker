All the previous times that he had been in the Earliest Folds, he did not last too long!

As he walked around and felt the warmth of the blistering storm of snow all around... he smiled as he thought that if he had remained in that terrifying battlefield of Aethelgard, he may have just had this journey end abruptly as well.

And yes, it was correct when he said he felt the warmth of the snow all around. Everything here was filled with concentrated Early Existential Authority that he continued to absorb passively into his existence!

Even if the complexity and purity that he absorbed per second was not so immensely significant to cause any grand changes unless he was here for thousands of years, it was a constant, gentle infusion of power, a reminder of the sheer, untamed potential of this ancient, terrible place.

But yes, he, Noah Osmont, was walking around the Earliest Folds without worry! Well, part of the reason that he could do this was because...

As for the Early Creature... from its remains, he had gotten his hands on a Living Astral Codex that held four Astral Arcana.

Three common ones and one uncommon. They had all been utilized to further understand the system, and RUIN/EDEN had even requested to break down one of the Astral Arcana Glyphs... for the sake of research, of course.

Noah smiled as he continued to walk across the vibrant, snowy lands. Here, he had time. Here, he had peace.

He couldnt freely walk around like this if he were back in his current Era, where he knew things were barreling towards destruction.

He was thinking of the many ways he could go about things as he came to a stop.

He felt a movement, a faint, almost imperceptible stirring in the air before him.

And he saw it. A vibrant blue butterfly.

It was a thing of breathtaking, impossible beauty. Its wings were not of flesh, but of pure, crystalline ice, their surfaces etched with intricate, fractal patterns of frost.

It fluttered through the swirling, snowy winds, not fighting against them, but dancing with them. It landed on a glacial flower, its petals frozen in a state of perpetual, perfect bloom, and with a delicate, almost reverent, touch, it began to drink.

Not nectar, but the very essence of the cold, the pure, untainted authority of this frozen world.

Noah observed it with a sense of profundity.

This creature, in its simple, beautiful, and utterly focused existence, was a perfect expression of its own Way.
