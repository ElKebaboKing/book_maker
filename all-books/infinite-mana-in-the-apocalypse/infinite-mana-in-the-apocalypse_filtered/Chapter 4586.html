Chapter 4584: Is A Foundation Ever Complete? II

A foundation was never finished.

This statement alone seemed ridiculous on its surface, but why the hell not?

As they continued to move deeper into this vast shore through forests of impossible scale, Noah looked toward the figure of Ulmoreth with eyes shining with determination.

|I will give what you said a try,| he said with a voice carrying conviction.

|Go ahead, Little Anomaly. I will try not to overwhelm you with too many bundles of information all at once. It is good to put into practice what you learn before the knowledge fades |

The entity shone with obsidian gold light as his vibrant and thick hair pulsed with concentrated authority.

Noah smiled as he closed his eyes while continuing to move forward through the ancient landscape, trusting his enhanced perception to guide his steps.

He thought of the Phoneme of Fire and its activation sequence. But instead of allowing it to manifest externally as he had done before, he would have that terrifying fire actually course within his own existence. He would have The First Tongue bathe and scorch his nerves, synapses, cells, organs, tissues, every single component of his physiological and existential structure.

He did not even take a moment to consider whether he might accidentally burn himself or cause catastrophic internal damage.

He simply proceeded to do it with absolute confidence.

Who the fuck was he to have any hesitation, doubt, or fear about his own capabilities?

HUUM!

The Phoneme began.

Rising frequency. Compression. Accent. Expansion.

He spoke The First Tongue internally with the same structural precision he had used for external manifestation, and immediately felt the response as terrifying blue gold flames ignited within his existence.

These were not normal flames confined to physical space. They were Fire itself at a conceptual level, empowered by multiple foreign fire based Principles that existed throughout Ginnungagap and beyond. Even a Singularity Principle responded faintly to his internal utterance, lending its incomparable weight to the manifestation.

The flames began their journey through his body with systematic precision.

First, they flowed through his cardiovascular system. His heart became a furnace of blue gold fire, each chamber burning with concentrated intensity as the flames coursed through arteries and veins. The blood itself carried Fire through every capillary, delivering the conceptual flame of The First Tongue to every tissue.

Then his respiratory system. Lungs filled with flames that did not consume oxygen but instead burned with the principle of transformation itself. Each alveolus became a tiny inferno, exchanging Fire of The First Tongue with his bloodstream.

His digestive system next. Esophagus, stomach, intestines, all bathed in blue gold flames of The First Tongue that refined rather than destroyed. The organs responsible for processing matter were now processing the concept of Fire itself.

His nervous system lit up like a network of burning pathways. The brain, that magnificent center of consciousness, became illuminated with such intensity that his entire head shone with radiant light visible even through closed eyes. Each neuron fired with flames of The First Tongue instead of electrical impulses! Each synapse transmitted Fire across its gap. The spinal cord became a pillar of blue gold combustion!

As he felt all of this, he had to affirm that truly, Mana did not directly equate to The First Tongue!

The First Tongue and Mana were one and the same, and yet at the same time not as The First Tongue truly felt like Mana...but how it was always meant to be.

It was glorious.

The First Tongue continued to course across him.

His muscular system. Every muscle fiber, from the largest in his legs to the smallest in his eyes, contracted and relaxed with flames flowing through them. The Fire of The First Tongue refined the proteins, elevated the cellular structure, made everything more efficient and capable.

His skeletal system. Bones that formed the framework of his body became infused with Fire of The First Tongue at a fundamental level. Even the bone marrow, where blood cells were produced, burned with blue gold intensity as it created new cells already marked by The First Tongue!

Oh!

His endocrine system. Glands throughout his body released hormones that carried Fire with them, spreading the influence of The First Tongue to every regulatory system.

His lymphatic and immune systems. The very defenders of his body learned to recognize and embody Fire of The First Tongue, integrating the principle into their operation.

Then the flames went deeper, flowing from the largest components toward the microscopic.

Tissues broke down into individual cells. Trillions of cells throughout his body, each one containing its own universe of complexity, became bathed in blue gold flames. Cell membranes, cytoplasm, organelles, the nucleus containing his genetic information, all refined by The First Tongue!

Even deeper. The flames reached the molecular level. Proteins, lipids, carbohydrates, nucleic acids, all the fundamental molecules of life were touched by The First Tongue and elevated beyond their previous state.

Deeper still. Individual atoms that comprised those molecules felt the passage of The First Tongue. Electrons orbiting nuclei carried the blue gold flames with them. Protons and neutrons at the atomic core resonated with the principle of transformation.

And once the flames of The First Tongue had burned around all cells and atoms throughout his entire body, they flowed toward a final location with purposeful direction.

THE Organ of Civilization.

The surrounding area where his brain was located felt completely aflame as his entire head shone with radiant light that would have been blinding to outside observers. But all the distributed flames throughout his body began flowing systematically toward this single point.

They converged on THE Organ with concentrated intensity.

The flames coursed across the Parenchyma, that functional tissue responsible for processing civilizational complexity. Every component of this extraordinary structure was bathed by The First Tongue and refined to a higher state of existence.

The Desmoterion glowed brilliantly and became reinforced!

The entire Organ became ever so brilliant, shining from within with blue gold radiance as it integrated the principle of Fire into its very foundation.

The Phoneme of Fire, which could scorch a Pre-THE when called out by him, pulsed one final time throughout his entire existence before the flames began to fade gradually.

Noah felt his entire existence operating at a level that could only be described as terrifying and glorious simultaneously. Everything had been switched to overdrive. He felt more alive than he had ever felt before in any previous moment of his existence.

In fire, one truly felt alive!

One needed to burn themselves to really experience what being alive meant!

Well...
