Noahs response came after a moment of deliberate control.

Then she shook her head.

Not enough.

The words were delivered without negotiation, without room for counter-offer.

Three more Fragments of similar quality, or something of genuinely equivalent value, might convince me to shift my plans and move against THE Weaver. Her dark eyes met his with absolute finality. Until then, focus your other body on retrieving my Disciple as previously agreed upon. That arrangement remains in effect regardless of whatever additional requests you wish to make.

The golden light of the Full Moirai Thread pulsed around Noahs form as the simulation continued processing potential futures.

In Muspelheim, his physical body remained motionless. Blue-gold plasma still swirled around him like the corona of an angry star. The Muspeli Lifeforms watched with mixture of awe and concern, none daring to interrupt whatever profound process had claimed their unlikely allys attention.

Within the simulation, Noahs consciousness grappled with the fundamental problem that had defined his entire existence since awakening to Infinite Mana.

How did one who hadnt yet reached the apex stand against those who already resided there?

How did a Scale Breaker break scales that existed beyond his current reach?

It was a heavy question to ponder on at this moment!

But as Noah grappled with this, he also paid close attention to the workings of the Full Simulation itself.

Because it actually had many benefits!

He learned what would happen from small, simple things in these interactions. Like what happened if he shared news of his victory with Eon.

The answer was clear now.

He needed to not tell her about collapsing THE Living Spirit. The moment he did, shed ask for her Fragment back.

He could keep that for as long as possible.

And the simulation... remained a simulation. Nothing that happened within it was real. He inherently did not offer his newly found Fragment of THE Axiom of Differentiated Tongue in actual Existence, but was there any harm in doing so in a simulation just to see if it could move Eon?

The answer was no!

His eyes shone brightly as he discovered a key point in these simulations!
