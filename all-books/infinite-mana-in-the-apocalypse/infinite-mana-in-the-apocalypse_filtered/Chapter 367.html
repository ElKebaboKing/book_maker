Chapter 366 - A Breathtaking Golden Door

A grand Prime Warship streaked across the vastness of space, having been a few days since it first left the 7th Celestial Star.

Noah and Athena were on a uniquely designed rectangular dining table that had a white cloth draping over it, numerous dishes that glistened in the light filling this table as the two ate while talking.

"Its disheartening to find out that even your cooking skills are out of this word."

The Senior Sister that was gradually opening up to Noah said these words as her clear eyes looked at Noah inquisitively as she threw out a question. Noah replied as he grabbed the fat golden thigh of a large bird as he took large bites.

"My time in the Profound Astral Sect wasnt just spent cultivating, I made sure to at least get the skill of cooking down when everyone was still looking down on me."

Athena nodded as she carried on with light conversation. They had been on the ship passing through numerous stars over the past few days that after training in their separate rooms, they routinely got together to eat. Noah used the time to show off his cooking skills as he slowly gained more and more trust from the valiant War Princess, as well as the old Grandmaster that simply shook his head after realizing the disciple he considered a genius had another thing he excelled in.

"Oh? It seems like we are finally nearing the location."

The War Princess that was licking the oil on her ring finger called out, her senses reaching a region tens of miles from where they were. Noah spread his own senses out as he came to observe the appearance of numerous Prime Warsh.i.p.s much larger than the one they were on.

These Prime Warsh.i.p.s only increased in number as they neared, Noah noticing the protective formation that the numerous sh.i.p.s took as if they were protecting the most treasured thing possible.

Each of these sh.i.p.s was at the same level or higher from the Prime Warship they were on, meaning there were numerous sh.i.p.s capable of destroying entire stars spread out all over the location that held the entrance to the Celestial Realm!

The Prime Warship they were on continued to get closer as it smoothly passed the circles of protection, its identity being known long in advance as the profound weapons of numerous other sh.i.p.s did not even turn towards it.

The further in they went past the initial perimeter, the more powerful sh.i.p.s that contained horrendous auras they could feel as they realized how heavily protected this location was by the Celestials!

Noah could already feel numerous auras at the Soul and Domain Expansion Realm overlooking these many sh.i.p.s, but his gaze stayed up front as he began to sense the appearance of a shining light.

This shining light only became brighter and brighter as their ship went closer, continuing to grow in size as it soon came to overshadow the large vessel they were on.

His figure flashed away from the dining area and into the front of the ship that held a clear glass screen, his eyes landing on this brightening light as it gradually became more and more clear, his eyes finally registering the appearance of a humongous Golden Door!

It was a breathtaking scene to observe as this door stood serenely in space, gold particles of light ever so slowly coiling around it as it portrayed a sense of majesty Noah had not come across before. The only thing that slightly took away from this majesty was the numerous Interstellar Sh.i.p.s surrounding it on all sides, the eyes of the Celestials protecting this door as if it was their lifeline.

"The Celestial Door, a breathtaking sight to come across for the first time as it is nothing compared to the gates leading to the Infernal Realms you have come across before."

Grandmaster Vredral appeared next to Noah as Athena joined their position, his wizened gaze deepening as he stroked his beard and continued.

"One can look at the names of the two and say they are similar, but the Celestial Realm and the known Infernal Realms are not even capable of being at the same stage of comparison. The entire size of the First Infernal Realm cannot even compare to one-hundredth of the minuscule amount of lands that we have managed to explore in the Celestial Realm."

The words of the Grandmaster revealed the immensity of the Celestial Realm more and more as their ship came to a stop not too far away from the Golden Door. The more than 2000 meters in size Warship seemed tiny compared to this grand door floating in front of them.

"Now, lets go in and have you two take a first look!"

The Grandmaster voiced out with a happy expression as a wide door opened up on the side of their Prime Warship, their figures stepping into the void of space as they began to head towards the Golden Door.

None of the numerous Interstellar Sh.i.p.s even batted an eye towards them as they all knew their identities to be important figures from the 7th Celestial Star!

Many of the beings that were stationed in the numerous vessels to protect the perimeters of the entrance to the Celestial Realm were beings that were average in talent, using many of the years on missions of securing the perimeter as they cultivated for tens of years in these vessels before they were assigned to other missions.

Some of them had not even been given the chance to enter the Celestial Realm yet until they completed their missions!

Noahs party smoothly bypassed all of this as they neared the door with the Grandmaster at the lead, and under the eyes of everyone, they came to float just a few meters from the Golden Door. The simple l.u.s.ter and brightness of the gate caused them to squint their eyes as the Grandmaster spoke once more.

"The moment anyone touches this Golden Door, they will be transferred to a specific region that we have long ago colonized and made our own, and it will be this region that you all will spend quite some time to get acquainted in before you begin to fully explore the dangers and opportunities of the Celestial Realm."

Noah and Athena nodded as their figures came ever so closer to the Golden Door.

"It will be my honor to welcome the newest geniuses of the Celestials into the first region of the Celestial Realm, the Sanctum!"

The voice of the Grandmaster reached a crescendo as they fully came in contact with the Golden Door, their figures disappearing in a flash of light as Noah Osmont, a nobody from a Small World had finally stepped into a Realm that would allow for unpredictable changes for the entire Light Expanse!
