2569 The Incursion of Dissolution! III

Emperor Shao gazed at the devastating battlefield.

Battles with Hyperversal Extremities were always unique as in their stage, it didn't matter too much in terms of statistical values when accounting for who was stronger.

A lot of the time, it was the concepts that one took to Extremity that were determinants of power- and whether one could win against another!

Of course, unique concepts taken to Extremity also greatly altered one's Soul and granted them a tremendous Hyperversal Extremity Authority that would make other beings feel weak in their knees.

The concept of Adjudication...was something all 7 Adjudicators of the Seven Havens held as one of the tools under their belt- something they were granted when they were elected to the position of Adjudicator!

[Why would one ever seek this thankless job?]

His body was covered by profound layers of authority in the hidden folding of space as he looked at the Extremity Expansion that Svetlana had released.

Truly powerful Extremity Expansions released a domain that rejected any other Extremity Expansion from being expanded in that region, with Adjudication being one of the concepts that was especially deadly as it made Adjudicators at their peak of power at Tier 3 Hyperversal Extremity!

Even with this as a boon, Emperor Shao never saw the appeal of becoming an Adjudicator.

[A glorified position that if you take seriously, you only make enemies that make plans to hunt you. If you turn the other way, a stream of Extremity Treasures flow to your side as you forge connections across the Seven Havens.]

...!

It could be seen which path Svetlana had taken by the actions today as there were many hidden pieces here today!

One was the fact that somehow, the Avernus Clan and others had found out or theorized that the Adjudicator of Dissolution was influenced by the faction behind the Extremities adorned in white gold masks, and they already planned to smoke out just how far the reach of these beings extended past an Adjudicator!

This Apex War just became a perfect chance to do so as many audiences from multiple factions were here, and they would achieve their goals of saving their disciples as the ruse behind it if any were caught or questioned.

And the consequences of this incursion into Dissolution today would be the names of multiple factions having been verified or suspected of having their Extremities connected to the veiled organization moving for their own gains...

these suspected factions would face scrutiny and begin to be ostracized ever so slowly!

Isolation, growing conflicts, more enemies than Allies, until ultimately...a faction begins to dissolve as its core powerhouses are picked off one after another.

The Seven Clans had been established for eons, but a few had changed names over the vast Recorded History.

The Aeonic Inheritances of Extremity were numerous, but some giants had risen and fallen in the past!

Because the Seven Havens were never short of conflict and conflicting goals.

And the beings that stood at the peak of power...constantly played games of life and death that granted the winner even more pieces of Extremity!

[But this isn't the way to go.] Emperor Shao shook his head and sighed.

He didn't block the expansion of Svetlana's Extremity Expansion as with its emergence, he moved multiple pieces into the Battlefield for them to achieve their secondary aims.

One was into the pocket of Vacuous Hyperversal Authority where a supposed genius Avernus Scion was under the threat of death, and the other was the remnants of the Avernus and Singularity Overseers on the battlefield being whisked away from the deathly authority spreading across the battlefield!

It was a calculated move that also caused more of the selective existences on white gold masks to come out as they achieved their goal of probing, but this would come at a cost.

The Extremity Expansion of Adjudication was not something to take lightly, especially when cast from a Zenith Tier 3 Hyperversal Extremity!

Someone was bound to feel some pain as Emperor Shao would only make a move to clash against it when their goals were accomplished, using it as the chance to have them pull out their forces entirely away from Dissolution after that.

[Let's see, who shall be the unlucky piece...]

His eyes spun endlessly with grandeur as even though others couldn't see anything in the Extremity Expansion below them, his eyes covered by layers of Unique Absolute Dynamis of Extremity saw too many things.

And at this moment, they flashed to see one of the attacking Extremities covered by a Crimson light forcefully be shifted across space as they appeared on one of the two massive golden scales meant to judge others!

The veiled Extremity was none other than Destruction as the voice of Svetlana thundered out.

[Did you think this meager disguise would mask you? When I gave you a warning not too long ago?!]

The thundered out as shockingly, the massive golden gavel above the two scales swung down.

As of the decision on the Extremity on the scales was already decided!

...!

Thereafter, shockingly potent Hyperversal Extremity Authority converged on all sides of the Scale Destruction was on as the further down the gavel swung, the deadlier the power would be exerted as escaping this would not be something done lightly!

The figure of The Hyperversal Extremity of Destruction swayed with a gloomy light as shocking brilliance erupted from him to withstand someone he already knew he wasn't a match for!

Uniquely, he would be one of those who paid a heavy cost for this incursion...

Zeitgeist had half of his body eradicated as his soul barely hung by a thread, a few dozen Singularity Overseers around him having used their Astral Leviathans as sacrifices to save themselves as the laughing of a Lich could still be heard in this darkness that gave them a sense of fear!

At such a critical junction, he felt the warm aura of his Master actually appear as the Hyperversal Extremity of Devouring passed over this region- his authority descending to wrap around the remaining Singularity Overseers as they disappeared from the battlefield.

Amidst the cover of darkness of Adjudication, he moved and swiped any Avernus Scions he also came across as the battlefield was rapidly being cleared- with those who had made critical moves today beginning to achieve their aims!

Their plans came at the expense of ruining the golden path of the True Emperor of Quintessence as he was also covered by the immense darkness of the Extremity Expansion of Adjudication, his gaze luminous as his will sent out multiple commands!
