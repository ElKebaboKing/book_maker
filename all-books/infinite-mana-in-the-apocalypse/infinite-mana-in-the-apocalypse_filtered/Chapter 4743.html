THE Weaver.

Not a Half-Step embarrassment like THE Living Spirit. A genuine, categorical Absolute operating at THE Absolute Depth of Existence itself.

Noahs mind raced.

He had one body here in Muspelheim. Another in The Early Veiled Shore of THE Creature. A third moving through THE Corrupted Vault of Ginnungagap to retrieve Eons Disciple as part of their tenuous arrangement.

Existence post-Fallout was heavily resistant to clones. The corrupted fabric of reality itself seemed to reject the multiplication of singular existences, as if the laws governing identity had been rewritten specifically to be a pain in the ass.

But Noah continued to defy restrictions.

He should be able to deploy another body to the Wastes of THE Fallout. Should be able to seek the location of Lumivara and Ulmoreth despite barriers that wouldve stopped anyone else cold.

His eyes blazed with Tyranny!

The newly forged Seals threatened to spill out with his immense weight, each one pulsing beneath his skin like caged beasts demanding release. Twenty-seven concentrated expressions of everything he had become, everything he represented, everything he would continue to build.

And what was unique here, what set him apart from any reasonable existence facing such circumstances, was that he knew an Absolute was bearing down on his people...and he was still planning to go there anyway.

Not fearful or hesitant!

Fear was for those who had something to lose and no way to protect it. Noah had everything to lose and the absolute conviction that hed find a way regardless.

But...

He had to have a plan.

It was great and all not to be fearful. Tyranny demanded nothing less than absolute confidence in the face of impossible odds. But if he simply threw a body into an Absolutes hands without preparation, without strategy, without understanding what he was actually facing...

How catastrophic would the results be?

And yet Ulmoreth and Lumivara couldnt be collapsed!

Ulmoreth had given him knowledge of The First Tongue that fundamentally transformed his understanding of Existence itself.

Lumivara, that little fox, also could not die!

They were pillars of his Civilization.

He would not allow them to fall.

So he had to come up with a solution against an Absolute.

He looked like an angered celestial fiend whose wrath could unmake countless folds.
