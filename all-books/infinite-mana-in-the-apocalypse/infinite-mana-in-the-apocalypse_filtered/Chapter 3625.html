Chapter 3623 Impossibilities! VI

He smiled and laughed freely at the words of the Trace Will of the Great Beyonder as this whole time, part of him expected tremendous disappointment from the legends whose names were mentioned across eons.

The Great Beyonder. The Omnifather. The Stellar Weaver!

They and other unknown entities were mentioned in hushed whispers as when it came to the possible power they could hold, Noah reserved his expectations after what he observed Ashmole doing.

The Advancement of the Wandering Alchemist was not one that showed him a truly deep complexity even as he stated he was going towards a place that those similar to the Great Beyonder had arrived at.

But at this moment, the Great Beyonder stated that she and others had attained their True Source!

He felt happiness at this as he also felt like...he was no longer alone.

In Existence, he was not alone in attaining a True Source.

Well, he still was an exception as he would not just have a single True Source of Existence!

Facing the perplexed gaze of the Trace Will of the Great Beyonder, Noah shook his head and looked towards her with an interested smile.

"I was just happy that I would not be disappointed by the complexities that came before me. You were talking about True Sources?"

There were different True Sources.

Ones that were far too profound and complex like that of the Wheel of Existence, and then there were Existential True Sources that entities could obtain.

The Great Beyonder attained her True Source a long time ago as she mentioned True Frequencies of Existence.

She looked towards him with a cold gaze as she continued.

"There isn't much time to waste. If that Dead Thing has recovered even a bit of its complexity, it will try to brute force its way into the True Frequencies of the Wheel. Even if it taints Common Frequencies and Prime Frequencies of the Wheel, it would still not be able to do much..."

She was speaking thoughtfully when right after, Noah stopped her as he spoke out.

"Oh, that so called Dead Thing has already obtained the Primal Flame of the Wheel of Existence from Hell Terra Firma."

"..."

The Trace Will of the Great Beyonder blinked as her eyes became sharp.

"What?" She asked as if she wanted to make sure she heard correctly.

BZZT!

Her figure then rose up with a sense of anger on her face as she looked towards Noah with even greater coldness, and he continued calmly once more.

"I'll rephrase that- he has obtained less than half of the Primal Flame of the Wheel of Existence from Hell Terra Firma. He is currently going around sealing the connection of Channelers to the Wheel of Existence across different Frequencies."

...!

He gave more information regarding what was happening as the hands of the Trace Will of the Great Beyonder clenched, and she took deep breaths as her blessed chest heaved up and down.

She then closed her eyes and enunciated each of her words clearly as if she was almost at the absolute end of her patience.

"Where...is the remaining portion of the Primal Flame of the Wheel?"

Her stellar waves of dark hair pulsed.

Her fair skin shone with resplendent as Dark Matter was vibrant around her.

A unique sense of beauty surrounded her as Noah looked at her with a silent smile while he replied. "I hold the remaining part of the Primal Flame of the Wheel. Safely tucked away in a place he cannot reach."

"...!"

Her eyes flashed sharply as the gaze she gave him gradually changed.

It still maintained its coldness as she uttered out through her teeth.

"The Primal Flame of the Wheel is one of the keys one could use to enter the True Frequencies of Existence. Within, the Dead Thing would have gained true access to the Wheel of Existence to do as he saw fit. If the Dead Thing is shutting off the connection of Frequencies to their existence, then he should be seeking to lower the influence of Existences across the Wheel just enough to perceive where any of the myriad True Frequencies are, and he would then gain access. This is the most roundabout way he could use, though, as I'm curious why he didn't just destroy multiple Frequencies easily..."

...!

Her gaze was contemplative.

Opposite her, Noah waved his hands as he thought of Bob and spoke out.

"That so called Dead Thing is a sentimental and emotional one. They have said that they apparently care for the many lifeforms out there."

He spoke words even he found laughable as the Trace Will of the Great Beyonder became stern as she shook her head.

"Do not even speak to Dead Existences. Those with Dead True Sources can taint you. Alter Existence in ways you cannot fathom. Even their words are poisonous and filled with deception as some can have their own discrepancies, but they are all ultimately the same. As for you...since you hold the Primal Flame of the Wheel and destroyed the safeguards I personally put in place, you will have to be the one who stops this Dead Thing. I will also relay words to others to see if any of their safeguards are active or capable of being activated. But you...you need to enter a True Frequency of Existence. You need to follow the Flow and attain your True Source. Only a True Source can eradicate a Dead True Source."

WAA!

Only a True Source could eradicate a Dead True Source.

The Trace Will of the Great Beyonder stated such words with absolute confidence as Noah maintained his composure.

He...already had the True Source Atlas of Existence.

And he was already halfway to attaining another True Source.

And there were many more on the way!

But he didn't say any of this.

"You could potentially use the Primal Flame of the Wheel if you hold the more significant portion to access True Frequencies. Or you can use the Singed as another Key.
