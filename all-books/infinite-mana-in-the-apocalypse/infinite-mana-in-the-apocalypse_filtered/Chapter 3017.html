3015 The Initiative! I

Too many things worthy of note occurred.

One was the fact a new entrant actually had such shocking levels of Dignity to the extent it seemed to match someone as terrifying as Sanctus Princess Sona!

The other was of course the expression of Dignity from Sona herself, which only further amplified the shock regarding the being she faced.

The ritual of Knighthood was flipped over.

The stairs were never ascended.

The whole test of dignity was changed!

And as the two came face to face, their Dignity pulsed and emanated off of each other as all existence around them trembled.

At that moment, the Elder Queens in the far distance reacted and moved.

Who was this new entity?

How could his Dignity match someone who nearly wrestled the Source of Dignity away from the Elder Queen Mother? This had to be ascertained first and foremost!

Yet the moment they began to move...

The eyes of Sona flickered as she turned to gaze towards their direction, feeling like something critical was about to be sidetracked.

Her intent weaved out calmly as a unique power erupted from her, forming into an icy blue bubble in this region.

|Let's filter out what isn't important first.|

...!

She didn't care for the rituals or what reaction those within the Sanctum of Dignity would have towards what had just occurred- and she needed to truly understand this being before her if the reality she had affirmed over and over again could change!

So she let him know of her intent.

And the icy bubble enveloped everything around them with a speed even Teracosm Stage Entities wouldn't easily grasp.

And...

POP!

With a flash of blue light, they disappeared.

|...|

Sanctus Princess Emilliana was astounded.

Elder Queen Ariana arrived on the walkway that now began to crumble along with a few other Elder Queens, her body trembling with visible anger as she couldn't sense Sona and the newly appearing entity with any of her weavings- her capabilities showing they far outstripped hers!

|SONA!|

Her bellow of anger was the only thing that reverberated out as a proof of one's Digbity turned out to be something far more than anyone imagined.

A moment after her eruption of anger, Elder Queen Ariana turned her blazing gold eyes towards the figure of Sanctus Princess Emilliana sharply.

The moment the icy blue bubble popped, the figures of two Entities disappeared from the Sanctum of Dignity that was contained with the source of an Existential Extremity Authority...and they actually went towards the Source of another Existential Extremity Authority!

Being capable of containing immense amounts of existence within the source of a concept showed how far along one was, with the prerequisite being someone powerful enough at the Teracosm Stage of Existential Dominium Realm.

Yet Sona defied this once more as they warped across existence and extremes to arrive at a new place.

All around, an icy blue expanse could be seen as stellar freezing galaxies rotated above and below, an immeasurably icy coldness spreading into one's Dominium naturally as they may feel a biting cold if they couldn't withstand it.

This...was within the Source of one of her main existential extremes.

The Source of the Existential Extremity Authority of Psychros!

Her gaze was studying the Weavings of many things as she turned to really look at the being she had brought here after asking him about joining her initiative.

An Initiative to stop what she could already see with her own eyes since eons ago.

It wasn't some ridiculous Apocalypse that she saw the weavings of.

The Omniverse was existentially stable.

Its workings too pristine for others to even fathom as a possibility of destruction for it was truly an impossibility.

It was them.

The Sanctus Populi.

Others.

The Shadow of Damnation covered them as this seemed to be their Omniversal fate! One that she couldn't even begin to change.

If she were to truly succeed, she would need to have enough power to do so. If she wasn't enough, there would have to be others.

Now, if there was someone who didn't even have this Shadow of Damnation on them, then she simply had to attain his services among others!

She may actually begin to follow the past weavings she foresaw and look for other anomalies- other Entities that may hold a similar uniqueness to her.

This was the Initiative.

Her initiative.

But first...Noah Osmont.

|You seem to freely be doing whatever you want.|

His voice echoed out as he moved around the Source of Psychros freely- as if its coldness didn't even have the chance to permeate into his existence!

Sona looked at this scene as she nodded with a reply.

|Yes. When you know what I know, you have to move as you wish. Otherwise, you get bogged down by unnecessary things easily.|

As she spoke, she looked at him up and down as if he were some interesting case study, nodding her head here and there as Noah raised his brows at such a demeanor!

Sona then stopped doing such actions and started straight at Noah, her icy gold blue eyes seemingly calculating the best way to move forward with this being as after a while, she continued.

|I asked you about joining an Initiative, but that may have been too many steps forward. |No. The Omniverse is far too stable- something that very few can even comprehend. But it is an unchangeable path of Damnation that I've seen for the Sanctus Populi.|
