Celebrations were curious things in the grand weavings of existence.

For most beings, celebrations marked achievements both small and grand, moments where they could pause and acknowledge that they had accomplished something worthy of recognition. A battle won. A threshold crossed. An enemy defeated!

These were all occasions that warranted raising a glass or singing songs or simply taking a moment to breathe and appreciate what had been done.

But for others, for entities like THE Genesis Monarch of Infinite Lexicons, celebrations were luxuries that could not yet be afforded.

How could one celebrate when the peaks still loomed so far above? How could one pause to acknowledge achievement when THE Creature and THE Living Paradox and THE Primordial Chaos still stood at heights that made current accomplishments seem like the first steps of a long journey? How could one rest when every moment spent resting was a moment that enemies spent growing stronger?

For such entities, true celebration could only come when they stood at the apex of existence and gazed down below at the masses.

Only then, when there was no one left above them, when every peak had been conquered and every rival surpassed, could they truly pause and acknowledge what they had built.

Until then, there was no celebration.

There was only much more to be done!

A great man once said that the self is an illusion, that one should relax and stop striving and simply realize that they were one with everything already.

To relax.

Fuck that guy.

Noahs eyes shone brightly as he did not dwell on the fact that he had just decimated one of the bodies of THE Living Spirit. He continued to forge ahead and strived forward as at this moment, he closed his eyes while grasping the Fragment of THE Axiom of Differentiated Tongue.

The sensation that he had while holding onto this object was a unique one, as if it was a baseball bat or an object that he used to play with when he was young on that tiny blue planet. Of course, he knew that this was just the familiarity of sensation brought about due to The First Tongue and this being an object intimately related to it.

But its capabilities were ridiculous compared to the fact that they came with absolutely no cost at all!

Since entities like THE Creature and THE Living Paradox likely had either completed Axioms or multiple Fragments, the things they could do were absolutely ridiculous. Noah had to play a lot of catch-up to match what they had accumulated over eons.

But at this moment, he was advancing.
