Chapter 1315 - An Uprising! I

The beginning of untold chaos.

This was the sensation that Destiny told Halcyon as she looked at the unfolding scenes in the Cosmic Battlefield. The Edict she had managed to put together under his help unfolded before her eyes as it showed her unique things even at this moment in time.

Kairos. It held a meaning akin to 'a fateful moment' or 'a critical point in time', being something abstruse to understand unless one already had experience in seeing unexpected things like Halcyon!

At this moment, the essence of Kairos weaved in her eyes as when she looked at the scenes of unfolding chaos, she saw a different sight compared to everyone else.

As different existences moved, she saw golden illusions of all of them moving- these illusions being things that were going to occur in the immediate future! Her Edict could show her all of this as she could utilize the information she was receiving to enact her own critical moment if she chose to act!

This was just one of its many abstruse uses, and Halcyon could see that from the enacted golden gates that had bloomed from the blobs of blood released by Stormdust...multiple golden illusions of Primordial Beasts bloomed out of them after the entrance of three powerful Grand Primordial Beasts.

Illusions of dozens of Primordial Beasts that flooded out in a constant fashion from a vast hidden Primordial Sea!

'Is this what is required to stop the Enemy of Reality?' She found herself questioning what she saw as the image of her own father appeared in her mind, a myriad of emotions crossing her heart when she thought of this being!

Her fear and hesitation towards him was evident as it had strained their relationship, but this man was still one of the reasons why she was here today. Now, she was fully standing against him and working with the unique being that might actually be an answer for the tumultuous chaos to unfold.

'Yes. There can be no hesitation!'

RUMBLE!

Her eyes shone a light of affirmation as she rose just like Springforge beside her, their bodies erupting with the essence of Nomological Edicts as these two women and all the powerful existences in the verdant green seats at the edge of the Cosmic Battlefield rose for battle!

Far from them, a sharp light crossed the golden eyes of Havenbreaker as she looked towards their direction and noticed the profound aura of an Edict around her daughter, her eyes shining dangerously as her body also rose with no hesitation.

"To arms!"

WAA!

Against the three auras of Grand Primordial Beasts that had stepped out from the three golden gates, the reaction of the Protectors of the Bastion of Reality was instantly to be ready for battle. Some of those situated in the red seats of the House of Stormdust were in an awkward position as those who did not know in depth information questioned everything they knew!

But time waited for no one. Chaos was chaos because during it, many things were undecipherable for others! So while the majority of existences rose the moment they saw the appearance of three Grand Primordial Beasts, these existences actually did not bother with any of them as they turned into blurs of golden lights towards only the glorious figure of Aegon.

BZZZT!

Vibrant bursts of color erupted out as following them, dozens of titanic Lesser Primordial Beasts and even slightly weaker Grand Primordial Beasts compared to the first three erupted out!

What increased the level of chaos was that all of these Beasts shot out in different directions, going towards the edges of the Cosmic Battlefield while not throwing out any true attacks, but they instead released a form an even deadlier attack as these existences opened their wide jaws...and began to suck in the surrounding layers of space.

They began to use their unique trait as Primordial Beasts to devour the pieces of the Cosmos they were in, with many of them still being bursts of light as their aim was to break out of this Cosmos and wreak havoc outside of it!

For Primordial Beasts to truly bloom out from the center of the Primordial Empire as they laid out a devastating attack!

Towards such a sight, the Defenders of the Bastion of Reality moved as momentarily, the shock of the Writ of Challenge that had bloomed all of this was put on hold.

"YAA!"

Halcyon and Springforge flew out as they shone with scintillating gold and green light, forging a path as Forefathers and Daolords followed their lead and erupted out with power!

Amidst all of this...

"You all actually dare?"

RUMBLE!

A voice akin to a clap of thunder erupted out across the Cosmic Battlefield.

From his pristine white gold throne, the grand figure of the dark haired Aegon rose. His face held a trace of anger as his cold and imperceptible eyes shone with utmost dignity! He stared at the three blurs of light that represented Grand Primordial Beasts as his chest lit up brightly.

BZZT!

The light silk shirt he had on was ripped apart as his prominent muscles filled with Runic Dao Lines bloomed out gloriously, these Dao Lines uniquely snaking across his body as they lit up brightly and even spread to his face!

An ancient and primal aura leaked out from him as primal tattoos settled terrifyingly on his face, his chest shining brightly as there, in all of its wonder and glory...was a Cosmos filled with multicolored 100 Billion Splendiferous Universes.

A Realm that outclassed all other beings here!

"It seems I have to remind you all what the Cosmic Realm means." The space around Aegon cracked and splintered as he spoke, his left eyes swirling in a dazzling fashion as his aura overtook everything in the Cosmic Battlefield.

Higher than Noah. Higher than Stormdust. Higher than the Primordial Beasts!

It was the aura of the Great Conqueror, the aura of the Champion of Reality.

"Let me show you all...the concept of Invincibility."
