Chapter 3213 Outerversal Morphon Ozymandias! III

The Royal Existential Omniversal Simian Nuntius Eshkeri gazed towards Noah with a stern gaze, his body still simmering with golden brilliance.

"If it is deemed so, then I will take on the role of a Protector of the Commonwealth."

His voice held no grandiose authority as he replied, but Nuntius Eshkeri nodded with solemnness as he came beside him and raised his hand while amplifying his voice towards the rousing Arena that still had their hands raised in the air.

|From the ranks of this recently discovered Omniverse, the first entity who shall join the ranks of new Protectors after this grand Celebration of Omniversal Discovery is none other than Prime Citizen Emperor Osmont!|

WAA!

A moment to show utter awe and wonder.

|As the one to unveil an Agent of Anti-Life among us, his actions are those of a role model to follow as from this day forth, from the ashes of Prime Citizen Emperor Osmont...comes the rise of Protector Osmont! A brethren of the Royal Existential Omniversal Simians, a Defender of the Commonwealth!|

...!

A Protector of the Commonwealth.

A grandiose title as with the simple motion of raising Noah's hand in the air, Nuntius Eshkeri actually dared to discern Noah's body as a form of assurance that he didn't have any threads of Anti-Life within him!

Noah felt the intrusive authority of this Apogee Autarch as of course, it wasn't able to discover anything about the Atlas of Existence nor the Authority of the Laborer, and instead...

|A new authority stemming from a uniquely different Source of Existence has sought to analyze your weavings.|

He instead gained a glimpse of another wonder as he looked towards Nuntius Eshkeri and smiled.

He was having a hard time finding a sufficiently different System of Existence these past days, and yet in the span of minutes, he came across two.

This Apogee Autarch saw Noah's gaze but didn't care, his intent coming out only for Noah to hear at this moment.

|That was a preliminary test to ensure that your Existence is pure. I will transport you to an area where you will be received and proceed as a Protector. From here onward...we will be colleagues.|

HUUM!

A burst of golden brilliance came with his words and wrapped around Noah's body, his voice now echoing out to everyone as the newly ordained Protector disappeared.

|As the fruits of the Commonwealth, we will not fear and let this incident interrupt our rituals- so how about we continue to showcase the strength of the Commonwealth with more battles on this Arena?!|

OOOH!

The crowd cheered for more blood as the threat to their lives that had just been taken care of had all but been forgotten.

Below the Arena, the throne where Prime Citizen Emperor Osmont used to be was all but empty.

In the distance, seated on his throne, The Imperium Autarch and the Eonic Uterra locked gazes with each other as out of the three of them- one had managed to make the jump and become a Protector!

Who else would follow?

Across weavings of things not capable of being fully understood at this moment.

Part of Noah's Existence was traversing across an Unknown road that he didn't know the end of nor the direction through it.

No vision.

No feeling.

Nothing.

He would occasionally smash into an obstruction before he was steered forward as the Authority protecting him lessened more and more.

The Authority of a young Omniverse that had been expanding itself constantly with the process of Existential Terraforming!

BZZT!

His body felt like it grazed past a high frequency oscillation of something that ripped apart Existence itself as another portion of authority was shaved away.

Within him, his Authorities were burning with everything they had.

|The Ascendant Existential Extremity Authority of the Protagonist steers any possible weavings to ensure your survival and success as befitting a Protagonist.|

|The Ascendant Existential Extremity Authority of Quintessence states there can only be a single Quintessential result!|

|The Ascendant Existential Extremity Authority of Entropy feels like the path being traveled is one that leads to the most basal and earliest form of chaos.|

|Entropy seeks the freedom of utmost chaos. No direction. No expectation. No reaction. Let the path being formed be the one of true and unfettered entropy.|

...!

Freedom of chaos.

The moment this prompt came, Noah felt an inkling of a sensation that this might be the answer!

He felt his existence visibly relax.

And he went with the flow.

No sooner did he do so, his existence once more smashed into an interference that felt like a wall on the road he was on, and as per precious times, he would direct himself away from this interference to return to the road itself.

But this time, he didn't take any action.

He just went with the flow.

BZZT!

The Authority of the Infinite Omniverse protecting him rapidly decreased as it went towards critical levels!

But she still did nothing.

BOOM!

And so, the interference smashed into him fully without any sense of protection as everything about him disappeared!

He was bounced around.

He still couldn't feel or sense anything as the interference he ran into exponentially increased, the Authority protecting him falling into dangerous levels as very soon, his Outerversal Seed that had yet to sprout would be unveiled and decimated!

And yet...he still did nothing.

|Entropy burns with exhilaration.|

A flash of a prompt.

And thereafter...

PAH!

The last defenses of Noah's Existence were shredded apart by an interference as it seemed like the only path thereafter was erasure.

From all around, a prickling sensation surrounded him as they would decimate him right away!

And he still did nothing.

And so, the interference smashed into him fully without any sense of protection as everything about him disappeared!

"..."

Failure.

Or so it should be as every part of Noah seemingly flashed out of Existence instantly, his consciousness fading entirely.

And yet another instant later...

SHAA!

As if his consciousness was waking up from a long slumber.

It could have truly been an instant, or it could have been millions of years.

He couldn't say for sure.

The only thing he could say with surety...was the sensation he currently felt.

The most apt description he had for it...was like he was being born!
