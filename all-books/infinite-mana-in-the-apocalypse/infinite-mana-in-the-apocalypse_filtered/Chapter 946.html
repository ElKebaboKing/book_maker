Chapter 944 - You Thought!

The rules of the Bloody Battlefields Galaxy Cl.u.s.ters that surrounded the Universal Construct- Slaughter Star Monolith, were absolute.

No being outside their respective levels could enter Galaxies that were not designated for their level, and this rule could be broken as the galaxies with the Bloody Battlefields Galaxy Cl.u.s.ters were the ones used to anchor the Slaughter Star Monolith in the Animus Universe!

As a Universal Construct that could act to promote Slaughter and influence multiple Universes, it came with its own limitations as the division of power was one of them! When the rule was broken, it would interfere with the Construct, and this was why it had been kept strong without ever being broken since the placement of the Slaughter Star Monolith.

So very few beings could ever break the rule of this Universal Construct and do so with very little if any repercussions at all...and that was the maker of this construct himself.

THRUUM!

The magisterial figure and aura of a Universal Hegemony spread out wildly within the Galaxy as his glorious white hair and beard shone with glimmers of light, an old man that looked like the true representation of a wizened expert appearing in front of the eyes of many!

His eyes were as deep as black holes as they glimmered with the light of stars, his appearance causing the Bloodline Races and their reinforcements from other Universes burst into extreme happiness as they called out.

"The Master..."

"The Master!"

RUMBLE!

Their gazes were reverent as it seemed like they were saved, nearly all of them coming to halt to look reverently up to the starry space! Yet...the beings they were fighting did not seem to feel the aura that descended down at all, their gazes those of contempt as they looked at their enemies that had suddenly come to a stop and threw down even more devastating attacks that snuffed out their lives!

"Ah!"

Some finally came out of their stupor as they continued to fight back, the eyes of the Universal Hegemony flashing with intense light at this scene as his mouth opened to utter a single word.

"Stop."

OOOOOM!

With the mere mention of this word, not just the Bloodline Races, even Noahs Summons and the Chaos Dragons behind them found they could not move- every single person within this area of the Galaxy finding themselves stopped in place as they couldnt even get their essence to revolve around their bodies.

They all came to a complete halt as shockingly, the single word from this Universal Hegemony was capable of stopping all the actions of everyone around, no matter if they were Sages or Great Sages!

...!

Shock and horror appeared in the hearts of the Chaos Dragons as they recalled the terrifying being that they heard of from stories, while the reverence in the hearts of the Bloodline Races only increased as this reality.

In this environment where the figures of every one were brought to a halt, the eyes of the Universal Hegemony went towards a single location- and that was the place where a tinge of movement in all the surrounding miles could be seen.

It was the figure of a Tyrant Dragon shining with an obsidian crimson hue, its eyes glimmering with beams of red light as its body seemed to be vibrating at high speed amidst the shocking pressure!

Against the command that A Universal Hegemony had released where others could not even move, the body of this being was vibrating as it threatened to break out of such shocking influence!

This scene was being displayed to the parties watching the unfolding events within this Galaxy, causing an endless amount of shock to the Paragons and Monarchs of the Bloodline Races as well as causing the expression of a particular Architect of the Dao to turn cold.

THRUM!

"Dont."

RUMBLE!

Similar to Ambrose, Noah said a single word as Valentina turned towards him with sharp eyes, his words continuing thereafter as he spoke with a domineering light!

"Universal Hegemonies cannot act against those of the lower levels, merely influencing them somewhat! Even now, he couldnt use his power to stop just my forces as he had to stop both sides in order for it to not be counted as him acting against a specific group."

...!

"Im curious to see how this scene will play out, so lets just continue to watch."

"..."

Valentina stared towards Noahs figure in the golden mirror with a difficult expression. Many thoughts flashed through her mind as she wondered how a being like him would be so comfortable as to think of all of this and not even be fearful when he faced against a Universal Realm Hegemony!

She then thought about the Marks of Antiquity and the birthing of a Dao that occurred right in front of her as her eyes flashed, turning her gaze towards the illusory screen showing the events with the certain Galaxy.

Yet...amongst all the beings, there was one that seemed capable of resisting the immense pressure, his body vibrating as it seemed like he wanted to break out of the Influence of a Universal Hegemony!

The voice of one of the powerful Monarchs rang out as they stared at this scene.

"The commands of the Master are inviolable..."

The Paragon nodded his head while looking at the scene with shining eyes, a regal light shooting from them as he replied.

"Yes, especially when he isnt acting against a specific group of beings. Nobody should be able to move until he allows them to so aga-"

THRUM!

Like a bolt of lightning, their minds buzzed as before the words of the Paragon could even finish, they watched a fantastical sight of the body of the Tyrant Dragon pulsed with a domineering light, his roar resounding out as the red beams of light released from his eyes lit up the starry space!

Under the eyes of many beings, Noahs Tyrant Dragon form broke free of the chains that a Universal Hegemony had placed on everyone, its bright eyes releasing a light of tyranny as they glanced towards the magisterial figure of the being above fearlessly.

They thought the mere aura and pressure of a Universal Hegemony could hold the body of a Primordial Ruination Clone?!

RUMBLE!
