Chapter 4466: Immense! II

Noahs words emerged with commanding finality.

"Only a single Vessel of THE Living Paradox has been collapsed. The Avatars have secured and concealed the others, distributed them across existence to prevent easy acquisition. Find them. Hunt them. Collapse them one by one until none remain."

His crimson visor swept across the assembled forces.

"Meanwhile, I shall summon you sequentially to undergo Civilization Specialization over the next day. When you return to combat, you will return transformed...optimized for specific functions that will make our Civilization exponentially more formidable."

The Primus Inevitabilities accepted the order without hesitation, their formations breaking as they dispersed in coordinated groups.

Thousands streaked away in different directions, beginning systematic search across the Folds for remaining Vessels!

Noah looked at those still present...entities fused with Civilizational Armor who would return with him rather than continuing the hunt. Riya, Henry, Sigrid, Protos in his separated form, and numerous others.

His command was simple.

"Let us depart."

The Light of Mana and Hunger wrapped around all of them simultaneously...crimson-obsidian radiance that distorted space as they began disappearing one by one!

What remained behind was decimated, decayed shore.

In distant corner far from the primary battlefield, all those who had been frozen and paralyzed during the engagement began waking gradually, their consciousness returning as the overwhelming pressure of concentrated Civilizational Authority finally dissipated.

They would find their domain utterly transformed. Their reality shattered. Only dazzling blue sparks floated around them as extended hands for a chance.

In the wake of war, only devastation persisted!

---

After warfare concluded, there existed few viable courses of action available to participants.

The defeated held no voice in determining what followed...their opinions rendered irrelevant through systematic loss of capability to enforce preferences.

The victors could indulge in celebration...reveling in triumph, distributing spoils, commemorating fallen comrades.

Or they could recognize they remained in perpetual state of conflict, understanding that failure to advance immediately would result in them becoming the defeated when next engagement occurred.

And because Noah understood this fundamental principle with absolute clarity...

He stood now in the Early Veiled Shore as this vast continental landscape, rapidly enlarging and transforming through accumulated harvest, currently bore three massive Sovereign Spires of Osmont standing impossibly tall near each other along the Shores expanding edges.

These three represented the available Specialization paths.

The first Spire gleamed with obsidian-gold coloration and bore defensive architectural characteristics- massive layered walls and redundant structural supports.

Above it, floating designation proclaimed...

THE IRON CONCORDAT - DEFENSIVE SPECIALIZATION

The second Spire was sleeker, more predatory in design- sharp angles, aggressive protrusions, surface covered in blade-like formations that suggested violence barely contained.

Crimson light pulsed through obsidian framework. Its designation read...THE CRIMSON HUNGER - OFFENSIVE SPECIALIZATION

The third Spire radiated gentle luminescence...green-gold-blue light emanating from its structure in rhythmic pulses.

Circular rather than angular architecture, flowing curves rather than harsh edges. Its designation proclaimed...THE VERDANT CYCLE - SUPPORT SPECIALIZATION

At this moment, Noah stood on golden sands alongside Khor and many others, all watching the entrance of The Iron Concordat Sovereign Spire with focused attention.

They were witnessing the emergence of the first entity to undergo Civilization Specialization transformation.

The massive obsidian-gold doors of the Spire opened with resonant sound that seemed to vibrate through existence itself as...

Titano walked out.

His figure was adorned in Civilizational Armor of Mana and Hunger that now shone with pure obsidian-gold brilliance rather than the mixed coloration others displayed.

The armor had transformed...becoming far more massive, far more imposing, covered in layered crystalline plates that overlapped with sophisticated precision.

He looked absolutely magnificent, a walking embodiment of impenetrable defense.

On his chest, prominently displayed where all could observe, was inscribed massive obsidian-gold shield...the mark distinguishing him as member of The Iron Concordat, as one who had chosen the path of The Infinite Bulwark.

The armored head opened with mechanical precision to reveal Titanos giant features, his expression holding a mixture of satisfaction and solemn recognition of responsibility he had accepted.

He looked directly toward Noah and spoke with a voice that resonated with newfound authority.

"Brother! Titano can protect everyone now. No one will fall while Titano stands. This...Titano promises!"

...!

He could protect everyone now.

Noah smiled genuinely at such an earnest declaration as he nodded with approval and pulled up Titanos current status information through his Undefined Potentials analytical capabilities.

Noahs eyes burned with brilliant intensity at a single number as everything else wss pushed aside.

8 Sextillion.

8!

That was not a calculation error or display malfunction!

Titano had originally possessed 300 Quintillion Complexity and Purity as recipient of the elevated Boon of Major Mana and Hunger Bulwark. Adding the 100 Quintillion from his fused Primus Inevitability brought him to 400 Quintillion baseline.

After selecting his Specialization and undergoing transformation, that 400 Quintillion had been multiplied by 20x through his Sovereign Potentials enhanced benefits...resulting in effective defensive rating of 8 Sextillion!

For defensive purposes, Titano now effectively possessed protection exceeding Noahs own Architects Bulwarks threshold of 3 Sextillion...and yet Titano was considered a bastion of his Civilization- a wall as no damage would flow to him unless it bypassed Titano!

And when there were tens of millions of tanks in his Civilization...the enemy would need to be ridiculously powerful to ever even hope to touch his grandeur and immensity.

This was a glimpse of how his terrifying Civilization Specialization could utterly change things from here on out!
