Chapter 3024 The Sanctus Sea of Rebirth!

They truly didn't understand the thing named Sona as the day she was born and almost caused the Source of Dignity to change owners, it wasn't because the Elder Queen Mother had managed to reign it in and stabilize it.

It was because Sona herself grasped the weavings of existence seconds after being born, and she grasped what she was doing as she simply stopped.

The Elder Queen Mother did not have the power to keep her own Source of Dignity in front of a newlyborn chick!

This was terrifying in itself, but it was merely one of the things others didn't know about the Entity distinguished as Sona.

The Elder Queen Mother raised her head while observing the thousands of plants holding tens of thousands of Fruits of Existence across the fields before her.

|I know her...so what does that make you, Noah Osmont?|

...!

She knew Sona and her capabilities. So what exactly did Osmont have to make her begin moving like this?

The eyes of the Elder Queen Mother fluctuated with brilliance.

The scene of the garden she saw all around her very quickly became obscured as hazy illusory screens appeared in front of her.

At the very center was a massive screen that showed two figures arriving in a plane of existence also towards the center of the Sanctum of Dignity- not too far from the one that the Elder Queen Mother was in!

But they were in a plane of existence rich in vitality and life- and this was where the Sanctus Sea of Rebirth was located!

The Elder Queen Mother's eyes focused on the figures going towards this Sea as both emanated immense immense waves of Haki that pulsed with Dignity.

In a Plane known as the Sanctus Rebirth Plane.

The surroundings were filled with verdant pathways floating in space, with lustrous greenish black holes floating around these paths like ornaments.

Many of them seemed to come from one central direction and converge downwards towards a vast green sea down below!

On one of these descending verdant paths, Noah was about to arrive at a location with the lead of Sona who simply warped them away from the cluster of Elder Queens, Sanctus Princesses, and Dragon Knights who were shocked at her actions.

At this time, Noah's senses that were freshly beginning to borrow the influence of Omniversal Authorities felt a tug as he raised his head and looked behind him, his eyes sharp as he felt like there was a pair of eyes watching him even though there was nobody behind them for light years!

|Don't worry about that...| Without even turning around, Sona waved her hands dismissively as she continued forward.

|...it's just an old lady who likes to peep at others since she has so much time on her hands. But I'm glad you're already beginning to expand your senses to merge with the weavings of the Omniverse after influencing an Omniversal Authority not too long ago. This little pool here should also help.|

...!

|...|

The first portion of her words affirmed Noah's intuition that there was a gaze behind them, and the latter portion spoke about a 'pool'!

The place she referred to was the dazzling green sea below them as the closer towards the center they went, the greater the concentration of existential authority Noah felt.

And at this moment, their steps halted as they arrived at a junction where all the stellar verdant paths converged- a circle forming at the center where they met as down below...was the absolute center of the Sanctus Sea of Rebirth!

And right at its entry was a woman adorned in a full verdant gold armor emanating heavy sharpness- a golden aura of the Teracosm Stage blazing around her.

To her sides, three stern faces of Dragon Knights also at the Teracosm Stage could be seen- staunchly guarding her sides as they emanated a profound aura of Dignity!

They were the ones guarding the Sanctus Sea of Rebirth.

A unique type of an Omniversal Treasure that was in the form of a sea, continuously generating Fruits of Existence over the years as its waters were filled with potent Existential Authority.

And then, all of it was magnified and further enhanced over the years!

The Sanctus Populi would have its Sanctus Princesses undergo a transformation within a treasure like this once in their lifetimes- with the concentration of existential authority only growing over the years.

It was one of the reasons why Sanctus Princesses were a cut above the rest!

Far above the sea, Noah could already see its verdant viscous waters filled with densely swirling Fruits of Existence.

And the only way to truly access it was to be given the right to do so, and pass the four Teracosm Stage Entities standing at this central area!

The face of the Elder Queen adorned in an Armament was hardened as in front of her, Sona freely walked forward with Noah in tow while her intent weaved out.

|A Sanctus Prince is here to undergo his rebirth. I assume that won't be a problem?|

|...|

The Elder Queen remained silent.

And an instant later, the verdant spear in her hand smashed down in a signal, the three Teracosm Stage Dragon Knights beside her making way and allowing a passage to form towards the center point that looked like a deep abyssal hole towards a dazzling sea!

Directions had already been given by the Elder Queen Mother, and all others obeyed!

Sona nodded at this scene and nodded towards Noah who at this moment breathed out while unbuttoning his sleek white dress shirt.

Within moments, his bare muscular chest and toned abdominal muscles were visible, his steps calm and filled with Dignity as he walked towards four Teracosm Stage Entities!

They gazed at him with sharp eyes filled with pressure and suffocation that may have crushed the Dominiums of weaker beings, but Noah bore it all until he came to stand on the path they had made.

A Teracosm Stage Entity to his left and right.

A terrifying Elder Queen nearby!

In such an enclosure, Noah merely threw his white shirt back as Sona caught it with raised brows, and he then dove towards the deep hole leading towards the center of the exceedingly verdant sea!

A sea thick with Existential Extremity Authority. A sea filled with dazzling Fruits of Existence like shimmering galaxies.

It was the sea that told Noah he had found the resources he needed to devour to get past the Primordium Stage!
