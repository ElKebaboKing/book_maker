Chapter 3338 Osmont's Decree! II

"Young Master!"

The cries of Nazal rang out behind him as all around this space, Megalonian were looking around frantically as before they could do anything, the visage of Quintessential Megalonian Nu could be seen in the skies making a snatching motion down below as if she were plucking stars, and hundreds of Megalonians disappeared on this Megalos Mountain as they turned into glimmers of light that were contained in cerulean crystals around the waist of Quintessential Megalonian Nu.

She seemed to see the intent radiating from the distant figure of Pyrrhus atop the golden sea as her gaze flickered, but she continued to take all those she could with her.

Atop the golden sea, Pyrrhus seemed to have come to a decision.

At the same time, Noah seemed to have come to a decision.

The Will of Pyrrhus should have long been extinguished over the vestiges of time and shattered records, and yet at this moment...

HUUM!

Noah felt a buzzing within his very soul as in its depths, he felt something rising.

Within the depths of a Reconstruction done by unfathomable means!

This buzzing...came in the form of an extremely regal voice.

[That day, they attacked.]

...!

BOOOM!

After this voice buzzed within him, the golden skies of the Megalos Mountain here split open.

The sound was heavy and unfathomably loud as the decibels reached a level where even those at the stage of One With Existence felt their existence buzzing as a sense of confusion would dawn on them!

From the split skies, two massive golden hands pulled on either side and ripped it further open, and from the opening...an extremely majestic face popped in.

A face shimmering with golden splendor as the moment it peeked into the Megalos Mountain and saw the Megalonians within, its eyes radiated profound glory!

Its pupils were shaped with an animalistic serpentine glow, and they shone with magnificence at this moment as when they observed the Megalonians, they smiled with ecstatic brightness.

|So it was true!|

BOOM!

Along with these words, the skies were split apart even more as the titanic entity that seemed to be spanning the size of an Omniverse tore open the golden skies of an entire Megalos Mountain, his upper body squeezing itself in as it fully radiated golden splendor.

Seeming like a celestial entity descending from the skies, the steely hands of this entity moved as their palms radiated a shocking suction force- such a force instantly caused Megalonians to forcefully rise to the skies at the speed of light towards them!

At the same time, the serpentine eyes of this being flashed as they spared a glance towards Quintessential Megalonian Nu who had a horrified expression on her face.

|Existence Sealing.|

...!

The terrifying power emanating from Quintessential Megalonian Nu instantly began to falter and reduce as if it were nothing.

The serpentine eyes of this golden entity the size of an Omniverse radiated beams of light that focused on the cerulean crystals around Quintessential Megalonian Nu as if it could see everything.

The cerulean crystals instantly cracked as thousands of Megalonians spilled out from it, astral light leaking all around as their bodies also began to be pulled towards this entity!

At such a time...

|THEODOSIUS!|

A booming bellow echoed out from the heart of the Megalos Mountain as the visage of the Outsider who was brought here injured just days ago appeared while staring dreadfully at the entity the size of an Omniverse above, the visage of Vayan by his side as the hands of this outsider chopped up!

HUUUM!

Existence trembled.

The hand of the Outsider chopping out enlarged and stretched maddeningly until its size became comparable to the massive invader above, and it went on to smash onto its body as the pull of Megalonians towards it was disrupted!

...!

It was a shocking show of power.

It seemed to exceed the bounds of what they knew to be Existence Severing.

|You...you truly thought you could keep this to yourself?! Against the vastness of Existence, this is one of the few viable options! You stupid fool, we should have never taken you in!|

The Invader voiced out with rage as the hands chopping towards him was rebuffed, his serpentine eyes looking down below as he pointed around him.

|Look around you, it is already too late. Stop this inconsequential interference.|

Around them.

On the other Megalos Mountains, scenes similar to this and even worse were occurring.

The skies were torn apart as entities brimming with heart shaking power descended, some the size of Omniverses as only half of their bodies peered through the cracks as they gazed down with looks that felt like they were observing untapped treasure.

They reached out towards the Megalonians as if they were dangling fruits, their eyes filled with an insatiable need!

Seeing this, the eyes of the Outsider showed tremendous pain as beside him, his beloved was trembling as her very existence seemed to age from the sheer emotion of understanding the consequences of what she had done.

The Outsider felt all of this closely as he couldn't help but release his pain upwards!

|CHOP!|

...!

An unbelievable battle bloomed.

On the golden sea below, the visage of Pyrrhus remained calm and unfettered as, at this moment, its eyes didn't seem to be one.

It was as if two beings were looking through the same lens, the shocking will of the complex entity who was born while attaining the state of One With Existence, and attained his Eye Of Existence a day after being extremely active at this moment.

[That day, they came and took everything. They treated us as if we were mere objects to be taken, and we...did not have the power to stop it. We were too weak. We were given too little time. Even I...had only been alive for days.]

He couldn't effect any change.

He couldn't do anything against those who came into his home!

The emotions of Pyrrhus were extremely active at this moment as his existence began to seethe, countless versions of himself appearing as he prepared a decree.

[What has happened cannot be changed, but I can't help but feel stifled.]

WAA!

[If I had more time.]

[If existence was not so cruel.]

Regrets.

They filled Noah's weavings as while Pyrrhus moved, he also moved!

His eyes radiated an endless blue light as he...also prepared his decree.
