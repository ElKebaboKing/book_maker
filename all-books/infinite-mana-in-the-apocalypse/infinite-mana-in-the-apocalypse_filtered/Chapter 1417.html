Chapter 1415: Indomitable! II

The Antiquities could not follow the speed.

Daolords could not follow the speed of the battle! Even the Family Heads who had comprehended Edicts...none of them could actually follow when Noah made a move within the domains of apocalypse as he instantly materialized before Cecilia's eyes!

"Boo!" In a show of utter calmness and power, Nion had a devilish smile as he spoke out the moment he appeared- the Primordial's eyes squinting while her Ascendancy Halo instantly brightened up with immense power!

BOOOM!

Not a single move was made as the crimson halo merely spun and smashed into the white Ascendancy Halo. Yet...

RIIP!

Reality itself was torn as the impact was akin to a spark of billions of nuclear bombs that smashed the figure of the Primordial from where she stood!

Like a star made of pristine white light, Cecilia's figure was smashed back at such speed and fervor that horrifically, her body smashed onto the Walls of Cardinal Sins of one of the Domains of Apocalypse as she felt a sense of boundless and unshaking pressure envelope her.

...!

Her hazy eyes brightened up as she gazed at the point she just received the impact that was countless light years away, but she didn't find the primal and archaic looking figure of the existences wreathed in snaking Flames of Edicts there as she very quickly sensed something to her side!

"I am right here, O Primordial!"

RUMBLE!

Shockingly, the crimson Ascendancy Halo materialized along with the figure of Nion once more at a speed that even this debuffed Primordial that had her Realm brought down a level could not catch up to, the crimson halo weaving in a majestic manner as it smashed onto her like a cosmic hammer!

BOOM!

Barely capable of reacting, Cecilia could only bring her own Halo to rise up and meet the terrifying Ascendancy Halo that she had called false as an instant later, her figure was smashed back again as she smashed across one end of the meaty wall where Cardinal Sins were budding off of to another end!

One must know....each of the Domains of Apocalypse were vaster than a Cosmos.

In one attack, she was thrown countless light years from the ends of one Cosmos to another as her figure was only stopped when she smashed onto another section of the terrific walls of the domain.

Tentacles of Cardinal Sins erupted around the Wall of Cardinal Sins around her as they passively tried to drown her, but her Ascendancy Halo brightened up to burn them all as after getting smashed twice, Cecilia's eyes flashed with immense anger as a blinding light echoed from her!

WAA!

Her figure became utterly bathed in white as nothing could get near her, her Ascendancy Halo spinning so much around her that even her figure could not be seen.

Yet even against this blinding light that burned everything, her senses rang out madly as she was barely able to turn around to find the figure of Nion calmly withstanding the burning light from the pulsing on the crimson halo around him, his devilish smile etched onto her wrathful eyes as he spoke out calmly once more, the golden horns that looked like tusks above his head shining with a murderous light at the same time!

"Tell me more about how my Halo is false, O Primordial!"

BOOOM!

An unshakable impact as for the third time, the Primordial was knocked away.

"...."

Domination!

Absolute domination!

Nion was absolutely indomitable as after the fusion of Noah and Hyperion, the power displayed was utterly mind-numbing.

The combination of two Cosmic Realm existences who were at the peak of power within their own Reality!

Apart from this, there was the advent of the Ascendancy Halo that when he utilized with this new body, he achieved power and speed that even the Primordial could barely follow!

It showed the difference in the quality of the Ascendancy Halos.

It showed what a glorious future was coming as the forging of this Halo would have consequences echoing out across Realities!
