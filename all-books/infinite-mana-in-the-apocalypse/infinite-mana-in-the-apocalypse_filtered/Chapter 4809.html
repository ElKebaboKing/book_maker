Chapter 4807: Everything IV

Even just perceiving it made Noahs foundations tremble with the effort of containing its weight. It pressed against the boundaries of his existence as if testing whether he was worthy of housing it!

|Absolute Everything Formed|

|You have cleansed a large portion of Existence and formed your first genuine Absolute Everything of your existence, which represents the currency of Absolutes and marks your first step on a path that only Absolutes can walk.|

|An Absolute Everything is condensed potential that can be utilized in ways that normal authority cannot, as where lesser beings accumulate Depth and Weight and Civilizational Authority, Absolutes accumulate Absolute Everythings that are formed through an expression of their own power, or when an Absolute contributes significantly to Observable Existence in ways that lesser beings cannot.|

|Purifying corruption, stabilizing Weavings, and maintaining the fabric of reality itself are all actions that generate Absolute Everythings due to the net positive gained from such actions.|

|Absolute Everything Properties and Utilizations|

|Depth Elevation: Absolute Everythings can be consumed to directly elevate Depth without the need for conventional advancement, as where a normal being must accumulate authority over eons to reach Absolute, one who possesses sufficient Absolute Everythings can simply forge an unquestionable Foundation to act as a stairway to Absolute by converting this currency into pure Depth.|

|Axiom Utilization: Absolute Everythings can serve as fuel for Axioms, and where THE Axiom of Absolute Denial currently resists your usage due to insufficient Depth, feeding it an Absolute Everything would allow temporary activation regardless of your classification, with the more complete the Axiom, the more Absolute Everythings required for full utilization.|

|Artifact Nurturing: Absolute-tier Artifacts like the Golden Scissors of Fate require Absolute Everythings to reach their full potential when wielded by non-Absolutes, as an Absolute Everything fed to such an artifact creates a temporary bond that allows usage and even forging of similar items.|

|Existence Manipulation: Absolute Everythings can be expended to perform actions that would normally require Absolute Depth, allowing for Embodiments and other forms of expressions.|

|Absolutes maintain Observable Existence with such diligence because they are not being altruistic but rather gaining Absolute Everythings through large cleansings of Corrupted Growths.|

|The gap between Absolutes and non-Absolutes is not merely one of Depth but one of access, as Absolutes have access to Absolute Everythings while non-Absolutes do not, at least until now.|

|You, THE Weaver of Existence, have bridged that gap by forming your first Absolute Everything without being an Absolute yourself, accessing the currency that fuels the most powerful beings in Observable Existence while still being below their classification.|

...!

Noah stared at the cluster of mobius strips rotating within his existence.

His first Absolute Everything.

It was something unique to feel as around the same time that he was analyzing the cluster of mobius strips within his existence, another set of prompts came down with information that made his foundations pulse with recognition.

|Absolute Depth Absorption Complete|

|The recoverable Depth of an Absolute has been fully absorbed and distributed across your recovered Fundamental Depth foundation, or really, within your Formless Depth that defies conventional classification.|

|Although it may be inaccurate due to the limited data available on the foundations of THE Four during their earlier stages, it has been estimated that devouring the recoverable Depth of an Absolute like THE Weavers has reinforced your Fundamental Depth weight and immensity to be approximately 25% comparable to that of THE Creature or THE Primordial Chaos when they crossed this stage.|

|Little information is available regarding the foundations of THE Four, but it seems that the foundations built by these beings sought to make up for all the deficiencies of weight in their prior Depths before they took a step to become Absolutes with unquestionable foundations.|

|They spent eons at Fundamental Depth, building weight that would support their eventual ascension.|

|You have achieved 25% of that weight in a fraction of the time through the consumption of an Absolute.|

|Civilizational Status|

|Primary Civilization: THE Primordial Civilization of Mana(THE First Tongue)|

|Classification: Formless Depth (unplaceable within conventional categories)|

|Status: Primary vessel for all authority, containing foundations that exceed THE Creature at Surface and Intermediate classifications while approaching meaningful comparison at Fundamental|

|Derivative Civilization: Hunger|

|Classification: THE Fundamental Depth|

|Status: Fully integrated, contributing to Perpetual Harvest and consumption-based advancement|

|Derivative Civilization: Undifferentiated Fate|

|Classification: THE Fundamental Depth|

|Status: Newly inherited from THE Weavers, provides access to fate perception and manipulation across Observable Existence|

|Derivative Civilization: Elemental|

|Classification: Pre-THE|

|Status: (Will you continue your encroachment on your most hated enemy in existence?)|

...!

The vibrant pressure around him calmed.

He remained on the throne as he was still receiving waves of information from different sources, but at this moment, he raised his hand and clenched his fist as...

BZZT!

A terrifying pressure emanated from him!

It radiated outward from his existence with weight that would have crushed any Fundamental Depth entity into paste. It pressed against the walls of THE Absolute Existential Palace with authority that made even this domain of a dead Absolute tremble slightly.

He could clearly feel that it was as oppressive as, if not a bit more oppressive than, the Half-Step Absolute Depth of THE Living Spirit!

More immense and grand than someone who was Half-Step Absolute!

|Comparative Assessment Confirmed|

|Your current Depth and Weight exceeds the Half-Step Absolute classification as demonstrated by THE Living Spirit and similar entities, and if you were to meet THE Living Spirit again, you could potentially curb stomp him without utilizing any Axioms or special artifacts. It may be time to say hi to THE Living Concept.|

|You have devoured an Absolute, and this simple action alone will make it extremely hard to find any enemy below Absolute Depth capable of going against you.|

|At most, Half-Step Absolutes, depending on who they are and what type of treasures or Axiom fragments they carry, could give you a challenge, but even then, your Formless Depth and the foundations you have built that exceed THE Creature at multiple classifications would provide advantages that most Half-Step Absolutes simply cannot overcome.|

|You are no longer prey for anything below Absolute.|

The affirmation was a grand one as Noah gave a light nod while looking at another distant door.

The Tomb of THE Weavers had many things as he sensed that what remained were her memories locked in the Memory Sanctum, but he would come back for these later as right now, he wanted to do a few things.

So he rose up from the throne as golden strings followed him like loyal pets seeking their masters attention.

But his eyes flashed with authority.

The strings dimmed and disappeared completely, retreating back into the fabric of the Palace to await his return.

The Tomb of THE Weavers could be considered half cleared with many things still in it, including the Golden Scissors of Fate and THE Axiom of Absolute Denial that still resisted his grasp, but it was still his treasury as he would come back to it when his Depth rose further.

For now...

He took a step and disappeared.

HUUM!

He could sense the fates and Mana of all those connected with him, threads of blue-gold that stretched across distances and told him exactly where his people were and what state they were in.

He appeared in the shattered stands of THE Palaestra, right behind Ulmoreth and Lumivara and Skoll who remained where they were, but at this moment, the heavy and interminable auras of Absolutes shockingly pressed around them!

The situation had changed since he had entered the Tomb.

The visage of Paradoxos shockingly floated a distance away from his followers, their dual form radiating menace that would have crushed any Fundamental Depth entity into submission. Four eyes burned with fury that had not diminished since watching Noah collapse their factions Absolute.

And Glossikos, this dazzling Dryad, stood in front of Ulmoreth and Lumivara with an imperious expression, her eyes pulsing.

When Noah appeared beside Ulmoreth and Lumivara, both Absolutes gave glances to him that he completely ignored as he got down on one knee and began to play with an excited Skoll who saw his Master return!

The massive dogs tail wagged with enthusiasm that seemed entirely out of place given the tension filling the air. His neon flames brightened. His tongue lolled out in pure canine joy.

While Noah rubbed his ears and scratched behind his head...

"I wonder what the great Strategoi of THE Agora of Primordial Judgment would be gracing their presence for little things like us."

His voice came casual and unbothered, as if he was not surrounded by beings who could reshape reality with a thought.

"Is there anything we can help you with?"

HUUM!

He did not even gaze at them.

He spoke while playing with his dog as the Absolutes turned over with shining eyes!

The way they looked at him had changed completely!
