Chapter 3624 Sources! I

She spoke and came up with a plan so easily.

She mentioned the name Ezekiel as she said this Singed knew the methodology left behind in order to open the True Frequency of the Singed!

But...

" Ezekiel has already had his Embers of the flames returned to the Wheel..."

No one spoke with a straight face as he told Trace Will of the Great Beyonder that Ezekiel had long been dead!

"..."

The face of the dark-haired woman paused slightly at such words while Noah continued.

"His weavings were among the first collapsed by the so called dead thing, that is Bob. At this moment, the Singed are led by Emelline of the First Flame."

...!

Emelline.

He just mentioned her name and nothing else as the Trace Will of the Great Beyonder closed her eyes thoughtfully with a silent sigh before she opened them and continued.

"Even she should still know the methodology left behind for the Singed. Connect with them and see if you can get through to the True Frequency of the Singed. As for the Dead Thing...Bob, you said?"

Bob.

The name became amplified and scrutinized in her eyes as she held her chin thoughtfully as if she were trying to remember something critical. "Bob...there were whispers of this name many, many years ago among the Dead Things that we were fighting. Some...were cautious and fearful of this name alone..."

WAA!

She pulled from the last vestiges of her memory as she uttered such words and caused the atmosphere to become even over here.

Around them, the weavings of the Amarantos Dream pulsed with a heavy ebb and flow as Noah gave a light nod.

"As much as I would like to discredit the Dead Thing that you call Bob, he is a creature with a unique level of complexity who seems like he will make things a tad bit difficult for us. I never give compliments to my enemies, but this one...has caused me a bit of a headache..."

...!

The Patience.

The schemes.

The lack of rambling about his grand plans like a psychopath!

None of it fit the mold of the many enemies he had come across in the past as he had to make a new mold for this being.

The Trace Will of the Great Beyonder shook her head eventually as she replied.

"I will ask around more regarding this to make sure that I did not miss anything. In the meantime, do what you can to locate and enter a True Frequency of Existence. I will leave behind scattered weavings of myself within this Avatar as a trace of my cognizance should still remain, and if I need to return, I will."

This Great Entity made plans to leave already as Noah remained seated while asking in a low tone.

"The Infinite Ravines where you all are fighting...where are they located?"

...!

He wanted more direct answers without leaving too many mysteries behind as when he asked, the Trace Will of the Great Beyonder looked at him coldly and only answered a moment later.

"When a True Source has grown complex enough to be capable of living independently from the Wheel of Existence, it will be capable of following a pull towards an exit point filled with endless weavings. The only place to go in and out of the influence of the Absolute Complex True Source. Easiest to reach them through a True Frequency of Existence. The Infinite Ravines. Without a sufficient enough complexity, you will not be able to make a trek there. As for you..."

She looked at him closely before shaking her head.

"You're still a ways off, so keep at it."

WAA!

She truly said he was still ways off!

He smiled calmly while the Trace Will of the Great Beyonder clasped her hands together, millions of Amarantos Runes returning to her as her eyes lit up with a dark light of brilliance, and she shook her head right after.

"I had spread out my weavings across nearby frequencies to see if I could locate this Dead Thing myself, but I have come back with nothing and I cannot stay here any longer than I need to. They have already begun their assault in the region I protect and with much grander intensity. All of this..."

She spread her hands out within this Amarantos Dream as she looked towards Noah.

"All of this is existence. It's ups and downs. It's good and bad. Sometimes it is fair; sometimes, it is unfair. All of it is still existence. I don't know what your motivation is for doing all the things that you have done so far, but..."

Her stellar black eyes filled with countless Runes locked with his.

"Keep at it. Don't be like those who gave everything up in the end. Don't let up. There won't be some grand reward waiting for you on the other side. There won't be groups of people waiting to congratulate you. It will feel like thankless work as you even ask yourself what you're doing everything for..."

She had been talking to him as if she was giving a speech to tell him not to give up, but halfway through it, Noah could see she became half here and half not as the same words she was giving him...felt like they were for her!

"..."

How many years had she been fighting? At a certain point, did all these great beings start to question and grow tired?

Noah sighed internally as he knew there had to be a more permanent solution than this, and he rose from his pulsing mountain peak as he looked at the complex weavings of the Trace Will of the Great Beyonder, and his deep voice echoed out.

"At the end of the day, we continue to protect the weavings of Existence, yes? There won't be others to recognize or congratulate on the work, but we all have each other. I'll be the first to congratulate and acknowledge you, the Great Beyonder, for all that you have done for the Wheel. Your contributions mattered and saved many for countless cycles, and they will save even more for the years to come.:

In front of him.
