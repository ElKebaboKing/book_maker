Noah contemplated this absurd arsenal while stroking Sigrids hair, his consciousness split between the intimate moment in the Abode with many others and his main body standing in the Ninth Level.

The contrast between peace and danger, between victory and challenge, defined his existence now.

The previous eight levels had presented terrifying existential hazards....environments where paradox itself attacked, where concepts became hostile, where the mere act of observation could unmake the observer.

These hazards had slowed their progress more than any enemy could have.

Until now, they had encountered no other Remnant Lifeforms besides Kelgar. Only hostile environments and the endless need to break through crystallized reality to continue forward.

The Ninth Level changed that.

They stood now in an environment that defied simple description. Waves of Living Conceptual Authority saturated the space with pressure that made breathing an act of will.

The realm stretched vast and broken...grey-bronze earthen fragments and crystals floated everywhere, filling space with chaotic patterns.

Each piece, from pebble to boulder, carried lethal conceptual authority. A single impact from even the smallest fragment could injure an Honored Living Existence, each one containing concepts that shouldnt be weaponized.

At the center of this maelstrom of hostile geology stood their second Remnant Lifeform.

But this one made them all become somber, because the power emanating from it was unmistakably that of a Duke.

Exceedingly so!
