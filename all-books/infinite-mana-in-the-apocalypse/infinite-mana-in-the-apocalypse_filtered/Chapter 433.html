Chapter 432 - The Magus World

Noah's clone was covered with the aura of [Unique Origin] as well as the concealment techniques of <<Herald of Fate>> and <<Herald of Chaos>>. He was covertly moving and hidden so well that nobody could find out anything about him, whether they were looking for it or not.

His body seamlessly pierced through the layers of earth in the Magus World as he went deeper and deeper, his abilities being so adept that he seemed to be flitting through the grains of sand. A few minutes passed as he streaked through the earth, soon breaking past numerous layers as he entered the innermost layer of the Magus World and came face to face with the core of the powerful Prime World.

[...]

The World Core was unexpectedly taken aback and did not even speak, Noah being the one to take the initiative as he glanced at the circular rotating mass of mystical essence that stood as the center of a Prime World.

"I apologize for intruding, but I came to reclaim something that I made possible to still be living today."

He spoke out curtly as he neared the World Core, the immense mystical power that was bound by universal laws which it released not affecting him one bit as he heard the reply of the World Core.

[What exactly are you talking about? Do you even know what you are currently standing in front of-]

"Yes yes, it will be easier if I simply share something with you. Can I get some help?"

Noah spoke nonchalantly to the core of the Magus World as his last sentence was directed to his Planetary System. From Noah, a manifestation of lights that were actually memories were released, nearing the mass of rotating core as a surprised voice came out.

[Such a thing should not be possible for an entity like you, what is-]

[...!!!]

The information that was uniquely passed from the Planetary System and onto the core of the Magus World as a shocking level of silence followed, an entire 5 minutes passing as Noah stood calmly in front of the core that did let out any more words!

This was because the information that was just passed to it was about the plans of the Celestials to fully destroy the Magus World, and the intervention of Noah that allowed for this world to continue living, even though it had to lose its most powerful forces!

[It is you that massacred the beings I raised for so long.]

"Yes."

[It is you that saved everything else, even me.]

"Yes."

[...What do you want to do?"

A sly smile appeared on Noah's face at these words, giving a speech he had given to many worlds before as this one did not hold any sort of threats.

[A connection?]

After a while, the core of the Magus World ascertained while thinking carefully, voicing out something that would be the biggest worry for the undertaking that Noah was about to carry out.

[If everything you say is correct, you would be putting yourself and this entire world into even more danger, as Celestials can simply come and do what they wish even if this connection is successfully established. There are also a few on them on this world at this very moment.]

"Let me worry about that, I have your agreement?"

[...Yes.]

With the agreement of the World Core of the Magus World, Noah's eyes shone as he stretched his neck and prepared to begin!

The first thing he did was use the abilities of <<Herald of Fate>> and <<Herald of Chaos>> to discern his own fate and the fates of the many beings in the Magus World. A large inflow of information rushed to his brain as with the use of both absolute skills that involved the Supreme Laws of Fate and Chaos, he felt like he was seeing even more, the simile of seeing both sides of the same coin appearing in his mind as he sensed the bright fate and chaos auras located in the Magus World as many plans flowed in his mind.

He also pulled out the Dagger of Time as it rotated rapidly in front of him, using the ability of [Insight] as he foresaw many possibilities of the next few minutes, nodding his head as he breathed out slowly, getting himself in the right mindset as he was about to pull off a bold heist!

"Let's do this silently, why don't we?"

He sent his thoughts to the Planetary System and the core of the Magus World as a bright light that signified a connection shot from him and into the core of the world, a fantastical process beginning as from Noah himself, a beautiful circle of blood began to spread out with him at the center.

Both [Blood World], [World Veil], and [Unique Origin] were cast simultaneously as powerful covert techniques went on to begin wrapping around the entire Magus World with extreme speed and precision!

The beings on the world felt only a slight tremble at first, and the more powerful ones felt an overpowering essence pass through them as the cast Blood World and World Veil very quickly covered the entire planet.
