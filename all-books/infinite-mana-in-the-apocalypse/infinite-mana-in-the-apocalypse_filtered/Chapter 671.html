The wings made of plasma erupting behind her seemed to make her unique charm and power even more lustrous as they did not harm her one bit, the control this woman had over the minute atomic particles being extremely frightening .

The other being that wasnt shitting his pants amidst all of this was naturally Noah!

Currently, he was sitting over 16,000 stars as his Nebula felt extremely robust and vibrant, and the intrusion of these beings made him look around in surprise as he complained within .

These were extremely powerful enemies, with two that were shockingly two Realms above him!

He wasnt fearful as he knew he could run away whenever he wished, and none of these beings were even aiming or looking at someone like him as he seemed to be a small figure in this expansive galaxy .

But was running ever part of his skill set? The answer was no!

He also could not turn tail and teleport away since the person with a singular identity in the Nexus Galaxy had already connected herself to him, and he would be taking an easy path if he continued to stay near her as he went on to try and complete the Protagonist Trait that required him to play a central role in the Nexus Galaxy .

An ability stemming from the [Blood Lord] skill tree continued to be just as useful as ever, making Noah keen on continuing this Skill Tree and even the ignored Law of Chaos skill tree .

At these high Realms of Power, one needed to accumulate a large amount of essence before they could proceed forward, and Noah had yet to even confirm the path forward after establishing his Black Hole .

But that would not prevent him from trying to take down experts of such a level as he tested exactly what they would give .

Cores! Skill Points! Whatever other loot they brought with them!
