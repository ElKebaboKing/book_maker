Chapter 827 - Tyrannical!

In the Illusory Unbreakable Realm, the situation was actually ridiculously looking dire for the many Entities that were grouping up against a single one!

The Primal Emperor saw this scene as he observed the battle while clashing against a relentless Noah, his vibrant voice ringing out again as he gave a painful command to the forgotten 100 Peak Tier GALAXY Realm experts that were surrounding the fighting Entities and watching apprehensively as they threw out attacks that could heavily injure any Peak Tier GALAXY being who didnt have the protection of a Dao essence in an instant.

"Join together and fight! Keep the freaks at bay while we take them down!"

He continued teeming the unbelievably powerful Noah and his summons as freaks, his command ringing in the ears of the Peak Tier GALAXY experts as they moved apprehensively!

They actually did not want to enter the frightening battlefield, but Entity after Entity kept falling from their side as they knew if the unbelievable and impossible thing of all them falling occurred...death would also come for them.

So even though they were unwilling, they joined the fray!

BOOOM!

Resplendent long distance skills overflowed as they tried to keep their distance, but their abilities only bounced off the bodies of the summons as the moment they entered...the eyes Noahs pets honed in on them.

They saw the pristine loot of GALAXY Cores, Law Assimilation Crystals, and Dao Comprehension Crystals when they looked at these beings that hadnt yet fully comprehended their Lesser Dao, the option of them dropping Dao Assimilation Crystals not even being a possibility.

But Noahs target when creating the loot drop skill was always to increase the rate of hid law assimilation, so these 100 Peak Tier GALAXY experts were simply gift wrapped for him!

He gave the command to his pets that while they matched and took down the Entities, they shouldnt forget to send an attack or two to reap the lives of the Peak Tier GALAXY experts!

...!

Ah!

Such a domineering thought process came naturally to Noah. The thought that his summoned pets could simply take a few seconds of their time to butcher Peak Tier GALAXY experts for their loot...

How far he had come!

How short the period of time!

THRUUUM!

The Golden feathers of the mystical wings overflowed with blood as the Aether sword found its target once more.

The gaze of the Primal Emperor had turned solemn as he gave a command to the 10+ nearby Entities that were attacking Tiamat and the Blue Slime in conjunction.

"Support me!"

RUMBLE!

The words were strong, but painful to hear!

It was the Ruler of the Primal Winged Expanse asking for help from his allies...he was expressing that he alone could not keep the Noah that continued to rise in oppressive aura at bay.

How shameful!

The Primal Emperor could nearly feel his face burning red as two Entities appeared beside him, these two giving their brethren Entities a much harder time as they left them alone with Tiamat and the Blue Slime that comprehend many Grand Daos!

They reinforced the Primal Emperor as Noah now faced three powerful Entities by himself.

"Hmph!"

His domineering snort rang out as he moved like a flash of light, avoiding the attacks of the new Entities as his body finally materialized for more than a second once more.

His hair danced wildly as it didnt even have the texture of normal hair anymore, seemingly looking like tendrils of blue mana dancing on his body as the arrogance and disappointment on his face was apparent.

"The weak always huddle and gather together when faced against an oppressive force!"

...!

The faces of the Primal Emperor and all other Aileron Entities in the Illusory Unbreakable Realm turned red at these words that stabbed deep into their hearts!

Those watching from afar that were in parties looked at their friends in shock as expressions of incredulousness were apparent on their faces.

They seemed to be asking themselves, did this f.u.c.k.i.n.g guys really just say that?!

The affix of creating a Ruination Shield if he hadnt taken Damage for 10 seconds was taken off.

He didnt choose the affix for Smiting his enemies with 10,000% Ruination Damage that had a 10% chance to activate.

He further made his base offensive boosts even crazier as he activated the fairly simple [+10,000 to All Damage] Affix!

Simple, straightforward, and for his current state that was already enhanced by numerous percentages and support skills...it was simply deadly!

THRUUUUUUM!

The moment the affix was activated, the faces of the Primal Emperor and the 2 Entities behind him drastically changed.

The Golden hammers shimmering with the essence of fate that were floating behind Noah disappeared and reappeared above their heads smashing down.

Noahs body also turned into a flash of light as beside him just attacking with his Law Sword, he now also began casting active skills.

[Sever].

ZIIING!

[Portals of Terror].

[Fireball].

BRRRRRRRR!

Enhanced by ridiculous amounts, skills were cast as they bloomed in the midst of the 3 Entities.

"No...!"

WAA!

"Call some more."

...!
