Chapter 1067 - Not Following The Plot! II

Above Noahs main body, the golden seas of Destiny churned as his vast Universal Fortune began to erupt out wildly!

He had undertaken many actions in the past weeks, the growth of his Universal Fortune as an Architect of three Daos that were being utilized by the beings from over 4 Universes having grown to an extent that most Hegemonies could not even compare to.

Those with immense Universal Fortunes were the types of beings who could somehow overturn a horrendous and deadly situation to their own favor at the most crucial junctures, somehow finding hidden opportunities at a time when they would normally face death or suffer a major set back!

Noah...had no major risks of death as he had truly prepared too much, but he still had ways experience set back.

At this moment, the vast Fortune he held churned as for the first time ever, this fantastical concept sensed a coming set back that would befall on the being it coiled around. As there had been so few set backs, this Fortune churned wildly as it flew towards the vast rivers of Destiny, assimilating together before shooting into Noahs head as he was filled with boundless information!

Information about a coming calamity! Information of what he could possibly do to bypass this Calamity!

WAA!

Glorious waves of gold covered Noah at this moment as the hundreds of beings in the theatrical stadium around him who were watching the illusory screens showing the scenes of 4 different Universes looked towards him in shock and wonder.

.

He didnt say a single word as his eyes released golden beams of gorgeous light, a tremor going through his body as his eyes turned towards the screen of the Chthonian Universe!

The rest of his people also turned their eyes in this direction as they saw a blinding crimson light beginning to stretch out from 9 locations.

Noah looked at this light as he understood it represented a great calamity.

A calamity...but also a grand opportunity!

His Universal Fortune told him this much.

There were certain things he could do as if his Primordial Ruination Clone came out of this terrifying situation alive...there would be something beautiful waiting for him.

There was a possibility for a certain light to bloom.

There was a possibility of the glorious light of a Universe to bloom!

A possibility for Hegemony!!!

The Great Old Nazzagath floated outside the boundary of the Chthonian Universe as he looked at the vastly changing situation within, other Hegemonies that had found out the Apex Paragon being here staying a few miles away from him.

This being was titled a [Great Old] within the Chthonian Universe, being a powerful Hegemony that was only slightly behind Chronos, Oathkeeper, and the Goliath in terms of power. His figure was that of a crimson star shaped monstrosity, purple flames constantly burning throughout the wriggling mass that formed into the shape of a star!

In the Primordial Cosmos, he was known as the Great Old One, the Star of Destruction, and the Hegemony of Venerable Chaos.

Aside from the Goliath...he was the oldest being in the Primordial Cosmos!

He knew very well the renowned and powerful Paragons of the Chthonian Universe he was born in, even the ones that fell under the influence of the only other Great Old One that had now sided with Chronos.

So he recognized the three planetary sized Chthonians that came towards the Apex Paragon, even though it seemed their Original forms were greatly changed and the very air they released had become something else.

These were the very same Ancient Old Ones that had forever been taken from the collective consciousness connection of the Chthonians a few hours ago.

Two were very similar, their bodies being those that a majority of Chthonians took. They had the octopi-draconic features as their bodies leaned more towards the spherical shape, looking very similar to the purple Chthonian(Cthulhu) that the Apex Paragon had brought with him!

These were known as the Trepidities of the Deep!

They had two abyssal black eyes the size of small stars peeking out of the wriggling tentacles that covered their bodies that were over 50,000 miles long!

Recall that a small planet was only a few thousand miles long in diameter.

These two Chthonians had grown to the size that was tens times that of an average planet after their transformation, even the form of the Apex Paragon and his allies looking 4 times smaller in size!

"Oh Varaxx and Kabulzan...what have you all done...?!"

The Great Old Nazzagath couldnt help but shake his head sadly, glancing at the third Ancient Old One that actually led the party of 9 terrifying beings.

The Chthonian Paragon who was most likely to become a Hegemony in the next 100,000 years. The being known as the Three-Faced Old One, being a unique Chthonian that was also circular in shape as its body had three pronounced faces that stretched for thousands of miles!

The Three-Faced Old One was currently over 60,000 miles in diameter, the star sized eyes on its three heads releasing a terrifying sense of loss as they didnt actually release any light. They released a deep sense of loss as when Nazzagath looked at them...he couldnt see the light of a soul within these eyes anymore!

The eyes were the key to every being, the light within them giving a glimpse of just who they were.

The Trepidities of the Deep had naturally abyssal black eyes as Nazzagath didnt notice anything, but when he looked deeply into the eyes on the three faces of the Three-Faced Old One, he saw its utter blankness as his heart trembled in pain.

There was no soul there!

Whatever these transformed beings were...not just the three Chthonians but also the 6 other ones releasing a terrifying light of power- they werent who they used to be!

They were something else entirely.

They had become Subjugations of the Usurper!

Their bodies swirled with such immense and terrifying essence as even the watching Hegemonies rose up in shock.

It wasnt power that any Paragon could wield.

It wasnt a type of strength that any Paragon could stand against!

It was a strength that they truly believed had already neared if not reached the bounds of a Hegemony!

Such strength...emanated from 9 beings as they didnt go one by one.

All of them and without a warning...thundered towards the singular Apex Paragon that had the form of a Chthonian shining with a colorful aurora of galactic light!
