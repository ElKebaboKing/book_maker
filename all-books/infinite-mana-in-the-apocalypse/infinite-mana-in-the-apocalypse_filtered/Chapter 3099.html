Chapter 3097 Dialogue! III

An authority that many didn't recognize!

Even the eyes of the massive Two-Legged Draconic Crow blazed with black flames as it seemed about to erupt with even grander fervor, especially as coilings of its Paracosm Stage Authority was actually being rebuffed against the massive tendrils of serpentine like Authorities.

And yet in the next moment... its eyes glazed crimson.

Its figure came to a stop.

The Authorities raging around it came to a stop.

And in the next moment...

|Interesting.|

A guttural and utterly thick intent boomed out.

It was not an intent belonging to the Two-Legged Draconic.

And yet it came out of the Two-Legged Draconic Crow!

Hearing this intent, the figures of Little CaCuLaTan and Asterias Virgo behind Noah trembled.

The other Omniversal Pillagers behind the Two-Legged Draconic Crow trembled and subsequently bent their bodies with respect and obedience, with such a reality continuing to flow across the Sacred Natal Omniversal Sea of Pompeii in the far distance where other groups of Omniversal Pillagers absorbing the silver waters all seemed to stop what they were doing and pay their respects right away!

To a singular intent.

One they had only heard through the weavings of Greater Omniversal Authorities every now and then!

The intent of the great Eonic Uterra!

|...!|

Sona's visage was tense as along with Noah's careful eyes, they both gazed at the visage of the Two-Legged Draconic Crow that had changed drastically in aura in just an instant.

The eyes of the Paracosm Stage Entity had now taken a deeply crimson hue, the weavings of authorities fading from it as these eyes came to lock onto Noah's visage, a calm and brutal intent leaking out slowly at this time.

|You hold the last traces of my Deorcnysse, and now you show this...what a unique one you are.|

....!

The words were heavy ones as Noah's eyes became sharp!

His Deorcnysse? It couldn't be his.

At most, maybe this Entity had nearly grasped the majority of the complex weavings of Deorcnysse as Noah saw Omniversal Pillagers utilizing it before, but this Greater Omniversal Authority should not have been theirs entirely!

After this Entity spoke such words, the head of the Two-Legged Draconic Crow turned up and gazed around before they continued.

|I experienced Deorcnysse being eaten up firsthand. I know the enemies that are causing the flies of the Sanctus Populi to tremble and the oh so great Imperium to churn.|

...!

|I experienced Deorcnysse being eaten up firsthand. I know the enemies that are causing the flies of the Sanctus Populi to tremble and the oh so great Imperium to churn.|

...!

His words caused the figures of Martial Kings and the Martial Emperor of the Imperium to blaze with intent, but he paid no attention to them as his intent continued- this time the gaze of the Two-Legged Draconic Crow coming to lock onto Noah again fully.

|Come to me. Since you hold the full threads of Deorcnysse, it can be weaved again. Its resurrection will hold some clues against the Worms that took it from me as after that...we can cleanse everything and go back to the times of controlled perilous destruction.|

WAA!

A call towards Noah.

A call towards the Weaver of Existence!

There was much to unpack regarding what the Intent of the Eonic Uterra had just weaved out, but one of the major things that Noah stuck with was...

'Worms?'

The identity of the Unknown enemies he couldn't even observe on the illusory screens of Existential Omniversal Editor.

The Eonic Uterra had managed to merely glimpse at their figures at least as Noah...he had only seen multiple destructive and horrific scenes in the last three seconds- or really, in the last three days within the Desecrated Existential Chronos Domain!

The scenes constantly shifted, but when they came to a stop, these Unknown Enemies- these entities that the Eonic Uterra had referred to as Worms...would only cause utter and complete destruction.

Two other Imperium Outposts that were filled with Teleios and Sequor Stage Entities, and even the brilliance of a few Teracosm Stage Entities. And yet even they fell without a fight.

Three Nests of Omniversal Pillagers- one led by a terrifying Five Winged Turtle who had nearly grasped the multicolored brilliance of Paracosm.

Noah saw how within a short period of time, an unseen force arrived in these locations as moments thereafter, there was nothing else alive in those regions.

Every ounce of Existential Extremity Authorities was devoured while any form of life was erased!

And towards all of this...a possible answer may lie in the decimated Greater Omniversal Authority of Deorcnysse?

Noah's eyes blazed with ever grander waves of brilliance as he looked at the Two-Legged Draconic Crow and the unfathomable Entity currently taking over its body.

He then remembered the new Prophecy that the Ascendant Existential Extremity Clone of the Protagonist had weaved out along with his support in the last three days within the Desecrated Existential Chronos Domain as this molded his answer towards this being.

So when it came to the proposition of the Eonic Uterra...

|I have a lot to do as I won't be going to anyone. As for the Greater Omniversal Authority of Deorcnysse...you must be mistaken!|

...!

He denied the advances of the Eonic Uterra!

And all eyes watched with rapt attention as nobody knew what would happen next.

But uniquely...the figure of the Two-Legged Draconic Crow bathed with obsidian flames and crimson eyes that were sharing the sight of another only looked at Noah calmly in silence.

And the Will within it didn't utter another word as after the passage of a few nanoseconds, it disappeared entirely.

|...!|

The eyes of the Two-Legged Draconic Crow stared towards Noah again as if it had returned to its old self, its coldness only becoming denser as Noah didn't care for it!

The Prophecy shed the path for him for the future to come.

Trust and cooperation would not be given so easily.

Especially not so towards the Omniversal Pillagers!

If the lost weavings of the Greater Omniversal Authority of Deorcnysse truly held an answer as they were the first things these Unknown Enemies- these Worms devoured, then Noah would bring back Deorcnysse himself and see just what clues it held!
