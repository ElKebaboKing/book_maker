Chapter 1436: Changes!

At this period of time, many phenomena occurred without rhyme or reason!

Within another Reality.

In a vibrant Primordial Sea, a huge golden structure the size of a Cosmos was enacted as it was reminiscent of a holy shrine with countless statues of different races surrounding it.

Each of these statues held ferocious expressions filled with power as all of them had their hands raised to hold an expansive bronze bowl!

At the very bottom of the statues, golden steps wound down from the shrine-looking structure as a single step was as vast as a Universe, and there were currently two titanous beings walking calmly atop these steps while talking to each other.

It was a man and a woman, their features being unique as the man had extremely long sharp ears and glimmering wings on his back, while the woman had dazzling four pairs of eyes and golden flames rising in waves for her hair!

The sharp-eared man gazed towards this flame-haired woman with a calm gaze as he spoke teasingly.

"Just how many Insuperable Edicts are you trying to include in your next Ascendancy Halo, Sis Belle? Even then, it might not be enough to reach the Sixth Firmament of Ascendancy!"

...!

His words seemed to be edging her on as she harrumphed, continuing to walk up the vast steps as a light of confidence shone on her eyes while she replied.

"Whether through Edicts or other Treasures...I'll do whatever it takes to not be the weakest one guarding this holy place!"

Her eyes were filled with conviction, the man nodding towards her resolve as just when he was about to continue talking...

THRUM!

Their surroundings began to tremble and quake in a shocking manner!

It wasn't their surroundings, but the golden steps they stood on that were attached to the cosmic-sized shrine! The man and woman raised their consciousness out as to their shock, they saw the countless golden statues holding on to the bronze bowl tremble as rivers of blood began to leak out from their stony eyes, the bowl they held also trembling as from it...golden liquid began to bubble out and spill over!

"This...!"

The pair of experts were shocked at the scene as their scalps tingled, multiple auras rising up from within the shrine at this time as a myriad of existences even more powerful than these two stepped out to look at this phenomenon.

They varied in shapes and sizes as they were all of different races, but many came to cluster around a single powerful aura that was more powerful than anyone here, and this aura belonged to a hunched old man that walked out with a walking gray walking stick that seemed dismissed of all life.

Yet, the body of this old man was shining with an iridescent stellar color as throughout his body, one could see an endlessly spanning Reality!

Filled with countless Cosmos, it was an illusory reality that caused all of the beings in the surroundings to gather towards this old man as they stared at the phenomenon of golden liquid overflowing from the bronze bowl and bathing the shrine a shimmering gold color, while the statues bled rivers of blood as they seemed to hold a foreboding feeling for what was to come!

"Master, this is...?" The woman with flames for hair was the only one able to raise her voice to ask as she noticed the gazes of many beings around her to be completely grim, and she didn't know what the phenomenon they were seeing meant as she sought answers from the hunched old man.

His eyes seemed capable of seeing through the veils of everything as they shone with a Cosmic light, his ancient voice coming out slowly as it resonated in the very hearts of those in the surroundings.

"Destiny overflows as blood shall bathe the Infinite Realities."

RUMBLE!

Blood shall bathe the Infinite Realities as destiny had begun to overflow!

It was a shocking statement as the eyes of many beings turned even darker, the flame-haired woman not being able to help but ask another question!

"What...what could have triggered this phenomenon at this time after countless eons?"

In the eyes of the existence at the Fifth Firmament of Ascendancy, a trace of fear could be seen as she thought of the possibilities of what was to come!

"The birth of something or someone...or simply the weaving of destiny has deemed it so. If it is the former...we must find and destroy whatever has risen at the swiftest time possible."

WAA!

The body of the hunched old man glimmered with light as his eyes became exceedingly cold, his voice turning authoritative as he spoke out the next few words.

"Stretch out across the vast Realities. When you meet what we seek, you will naturally know. Strike without reservation and obliterate anything on your path...we must prevent the destruction of Realities at such a cataclysmic scale."

...!

"The other shrines should be seeing similar phenomenons as they will also send word, with many of us needing to be mobilized to face the perilous eons to come..."

OOOM!

The surroundings shook as the golden liquid overflowing from the bronze bowl reached the golden steps, but it wasn't alone as mixed within it were rivers of crimson blood that made for an extremely contrasting scene.

The hunched old man gazed at the golden and red mixture of liquid flowing between his feet as it went on to cover the whole shrine, making for a unique scene in the Primordial Sea of an enormous structure that was actually dripping red and gold!

This type of phenomenon occurred within this shrine, and as the hunched old man mentioned- it occurred on many other shrines similar to it spread out across the Infinite Realities as those that guarded these shrines quickly relayed the message!

The unique thing about the existences overseeing these shrines was something they all shared even though they were of different races.

Their bodies carried a regal glow of a certain Bloodline that made them extremely special.

It was the Bloodline of Primordials!
