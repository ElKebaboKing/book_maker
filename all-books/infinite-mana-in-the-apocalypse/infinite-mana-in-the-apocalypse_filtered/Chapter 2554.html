2552 Madness! III

The shocking scenes of the Apex Battlefield flowed like films on an illusory screen as multiple areas were focused on and seen with utmost clarity- the clashing of Dynamis of Extremity and Boundaries felt as if the watchers were right there in the midst of the battle.

The illusory screen showing the scene of the clash between the Vacuous Hyperversal Authority of Ahpuch and Alexander had taken center point, while the Haven Lich Ra'Zan who had begun to release waves of deathly authority over the whole Battlefield took another major focus on his own screen!

As for those watching...

[The battle has begun for a little while now. Can you see what the outcome would be?]

The voice of an aged Hyperversal Extremity thundered out.

He was adorned in a light red and white robe that held the burning symbol of infinity behind it, a horn rising on his forehead that looked quite similar to the one of the woman sitting before him.

Around the illusory screens they were viewing, a blazing flames seethed as molten mountains and burning suns were spread out all around, all of them pulsing with tremendous Authority of heat that was directed and focused on the single figure of Ayame's Main Body!

She was adorned in a light white gown as her expression was hardened, tremendous heat flowing over her skin as multiple Boundaries were forcefully being cycled through her body to try and achieve nothing else other than the Convergence of enough Boundaries to form an Absolute Seed of Extremity.

Her Grandfather stood behind her as with the aid of a being like him who held too many Absolute Dynamis of Extremity, it was only an inevitability!

What she was doing now was the same as many other powerful Scions- sending their Aeonic Soul Clones to observe the changes of the Extremity Vein of Dissolution to watch the Apex War unfolding while their main bodies accepted the support of their Clan or Inheritance to undergo a thorough advancement in power.

At this moment, Ayame's Grandfather had asked her the very hard question of whether she could already see the outcome of this battle!

Sensing a bit of hesitation from her, the Hyperversal Extremity with a brooding aura that burned vigorously among any Tier 3 Hyperversal Extremities laughed and spoke lightly.

[Little Ayame, do not let the veiled records of a single being break your confidence. You can see the strands of all reality and read them through the Aeonic Annals of Extremity. So now...I want you to provide me with a thorough analysis of the battle. Of all the pieces involved.]

WAA!

The words roused Ayame out of her stupor as she nodded.

She could read and see many things about reality as after gazing at the many moving pieces, she began.

"In any battle with Apex Aeonic Lifeforms, victory is truly decided by the strongest commander leading a side. So long as their Boundary Layers are higher, their closer ties to Extremity will ensure that they oppress the enemy commander as their authority will also spill over to their soldiers. In this case, this Apex War's winner should be Ahpuch as he holds multiple Boundaries at the 16th Layer, holds Vacuous Hyperversal Authority, and very likely had his own Absolute Seed Extremity forged!"

...!

Ayame spoke calmly as she felt her Grandfather smile behind her, and she continued.

"But...this Apex War is different. The enemy Ahpuch and the forces backing him are facing...my eyes tell me that he shouldn't have exceeded the 13th Boundary Layer. And yet, the Commander wields an Absolute Dynamis of Extremity and Vacuous Hyperversal Authority as well, and multiple people on his side also wield Absolute Dynamis of Extremity. This acts to balance the scales somewhat, but then...there comes the effectiveness and uniqueness of one Absolute Dynamis of Extremity that was shown on the side of the True Emperor of Quintessence."

Her words came out rapidly as her Grandfather's deep voice rang out behind her.

[Nice catch. Yes...from the Records he did not hide, he termed it the Thanatos Absolute Dynamis of Extremity. This is likely not a normal Absolute Dynamis of Extremity, but one that has the Convergence of at least 20 concepts. Something that only pristinely talented Apex Aeonic Lifeforms who have achieved the 17th Layer and are on the cusp of Extremity can ever fathom to achieve...something that you can more easily do as a Tier 2 Hyperversal Extremity.]

As the two of them spoke, their eyes came to focus on the visage of Alexander King and the Haven Lich Ra'Zan, as well as the 7 cracks in the skies that showed off another eruption of this Thanatos Absolute Dynamis of Extremity "Now, the outcome of the battle has become centered on the pieces connected to this unique Absolute Dynamis of Extremity. I could barely grasp the Recorded History of the First Commander to be Alexander King as the Entity he called out should be known as a Haven Lich. Their abilities will cause the favor of this War to tip towards them if Ahpuch and the truly powerful Elder Apex Aeonic Lifeforms do not stop their abilities from spreading..."

As she spoke, they observed the dense authority of death cast by the Haven Lich to descend on a wide area.

It washed over the visage of Ahpuch and then spread onto the 200 Avernus Clan Elders, going even further to also land on the 300 Singularity Overseer Elders as their bodies lit up with their own defensive authorities to resist it!

When reading the deathly authority with the Aeonic Annals of Extremity, Ayame's eyes felt troubled as she continued.

"The authority is concentrated as plagues and curses that lower and restrict the authority of their targets. This Haven Lich should have spent a lot of its Soul Values to cast such wide ranging debuff abilities, and yet..."

KA KA KA!

They heard the cackling laugh through the screen as this Lich continued to wave its staff badly, illusory totems cackling just like it erupting out by the hundreds as they went to settle above the heads of anyone who couldn't resist the authority of the Absolute Dynamis of Extremity it held!

The Wills and souls of the affected Avernus and Singularity Overseer Elders then faced constant mental attack abilities imposing feelings of inferiority and madness, their eyes becoming somber as they released their own authority to be rid of this negative effect!

Their essence flowed slower as they faced horrific deathly plaques and curses, and the activation of their abilities could at times fizzle and not unfold.

"The authorities the Haven Lich is releasing are working to lower the capabilities of their enemy forces, while there are also the Prince Tier and King Tier Thanatos Vassals I saw before making their moves..."

As she spoke, Ayame centered on another screen that showed the Void Dracolich releasing a Poison Breath towards the Apex Astral Leviathans carrying Elder Apex Aeonic Lifeforms, the Chaos Nightwalker motioning with his hands as massive glacial sword formed in the skies and also rained down death!

Meanwhile, the Legions of Prince Tier Thanatos Vassal moved like Undead waves of a sea as they tried to drown much more powerful Elder Lifeforms by simply overpowering them with numbers!

[Yes.] Her Grandfather nodded behind her as he accepted her analysis so far.

[The ability to control the battlefield is extremely crucial, and so far, the side of the True Emperor of Quintessence has taken the reins of control, even if the other side has more powerful Lifeforms. And since they didn't stop another capability of this distinctive Thanatos Dynamis of Extremity being expressed...]

As he spoke, he focused on the 7 points of light that came to be after Alexander King called out [Chthonian Sinners of Thanatos].

From the cracks in space, heavy auras of Entities slightly exceeding the power of common Grand Elders erupted out as a mere glance of their bodies showed grotesque-looking Lifeforms of massive size rush out with mad intent for battle!

It caused everything to seem utterly chaotic as their appearance made the whole battlefield descend into even more chaos and madness.

This wasn't to mention that behind the Haven Lich Ra'Zan, portals multiplied and bloomed as Legions of Prince Tier Thanatos Vassals began to surge out like endless tides!

[The Authority of Death and Destruction...is a distinctive one as something at this magnitude has only been exhibited by the Hyperversal Extremity of Necromancy in the Haven of Penumbra. To have a Rising Junior express its authority like this already...]
