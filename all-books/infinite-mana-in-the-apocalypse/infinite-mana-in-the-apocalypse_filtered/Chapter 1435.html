Chapter 1433: The Dwindling Flames of the Champion of Reality

Just as he was about to erupt with his might and flames of Edicts to confirm this location and how to break out of it, his mind became dazed when his awareness came across a shining crystal of immense grandeur just a million light-years away.

The crystal was barely the size of a World and seemed heavily reduced from its original size, but it contained within it the glimmer of Cosmos as it seemed to be a portion of Reality!

"This..?!"

Aegon had never come across such a thing as his figure flashed towards it rapidly, his mind that was filled with wrath against those that schemed against him now focusing on this pristine Treasure that his Origin seemed to be calling out to with fervor.

He came before it as it reflected before his eyes, unable to stop himself from laying his hands on it as the moment he did...his Origin quivered in joy as it was telling him to devour the object before his eyes right away!

So against his usual careful judgment, the Champion of Reality didn't even fully think about his current situation as he simply absorbed the laid out and nearly used up Shard of a Seed of Reality.

"I can do more than just reach your feet, Little Champion."

With his voice smashing down like a clap of thunder, Noah appeared not too far away from Aegon as he gazed at this being with eyes of pity.

This being that held grand aspirations and destiny as he was a chosen being- a Champion of Reality! And yet right now...he lay in the palm of another being's hands as the bright flame of Destiny that burned brightly around him...it had begun to dwindle!

The illustrious flame of a chosen being that meant he would go extremely far and reach unimaginable heights began to flicker as the Destiny of this being was the first to notice what was coming.

"You...!"

His eyes gazed towards Noah as his newly forged Origin that was now a few hundred Cosmic Filament Cores thrummed with power.

He wanted to rip this being before him apart but at this moment, his heart inexplicably skipped a beat as he felt his destiny come to a halt!

His eyes became confused as he didn't understand this feeling, and the next thing he saw was the calm face of Noah as he thrust out his palm.

He didn't know!

The palm arrived the moment that Aegon saw it as it was bathed in the black essence of Apocalypse along with a glimmering golden essence of an Insuperable Nomological Edict!
