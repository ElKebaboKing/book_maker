3246 Change! IV

The weavings of the Wheel were their own.

They could not be so easily grasped and understood.

The ebb and flow was something that common life forms would forever stare at in the distance, endlessly out of reach.

Countless things came and went, countless interactions weaved to and fro over cycles and eons unknown.

Could a single stone cast into all these cycles and chaos manage to effect any significant change?

Across the weavings of Existence.

Inside of an Omniverse undiscovered by the Commonwealth.

The weavings of Existential Extremity Authorities were prevalent here, but they seemed distorted and altered compared to normal.

They flowed like endless threads towards a single direction- a direction where a vibrant obsidian stellar hue pulsed and gave all the color in the surroundings.

The deeper one followed the threads of distorted Existential Extremity Authorities, the more suffocating a sensation of power they would feel as soon enough, they would come across titanic Entities with skeletal forms wading about.

Their numbers ranged by the trillions, from different species of all walks of life.

Without fail, their skeletal structures shone with a terrifying black and white hue- almost metallic in nature as they looked unbreakable.

Past these trillions of skeletal life forms, the converging distorted weavings of Existential Extremity Authorities flowed into an extremely heavy obsidian singularity that was surrounded by shattered remnants of millions of Domains of Existence.

It was a Dark Matter Core!

A complete one at that, not one stemming from a Half Tainted Omniverse.

In its very depths, a titanic skeleton the size of a cosmos bowed towards certain darkness, very little being visible in the depths of the massive Dark Matter Core.

|Any orders for me, Master? I hunger to conquer even more under your name...|

The voice was a powerful one, burning with Outerversal Authority and a fearsome sense of destruction!

The place it spoke towards was filled with boundless darkness, and yet the pure brilliance of an obsidian glinting skeleton was barely observable from the sockets that burned with white flames.

|Nothing on the horizon yet on this side, Bellerophon. One of my Chosen is going to put on an interesting show for me as she heads to take one of the heads of Savitar. If she is capable enough and succeeds, I will feed off of Savitar and reward her. If your blade seeks blood...follow up on these rumors of the Sign. I am getting whispers of Rain on the other side, Bellerophon. Rain! Haha, This is bound to make things more interesting as it means the weavings of the Great Beyonder are about to light up with activity. And once his weavings are lit up...|

...!

He spoke with great respect when talking of the Great Beyonder.

And when he spoke of the 'Other side' that referred to the Megalos Beyond, it was almost like he was speaking about one side of a single coin!

|Once his weavings are lit up, they will even affect things here in the Megalos Ynnere. Then, the already active Megalos Nihilos will get even wilder. In this wildness, Bellerophon, we will make grand moves.|

A dense amount of information was unveiled all at once as this being spoke of grandiose things.

He referred to where he currently was, within the space holding this Tainted Omniverse, as the Megalos Ynnere!

There was the Megalos Beyond where this entity stemmed from, and he also mentioned Megalos Nihilos.

Three inexplicable wonders.

Three bodies.

And each seemed as mysterious and endless as the one before it!

Inside of a Vessel the size of a small world.

Under the banner of the Commonwealth, there were many dazzling technological advances that allowed for the forging of fantastical vessels, but the ones carrying powerful Protectors of the Commonwealth more often than not were on the smaller size.

Something smaller wasn't seeking attention. freewebnovel.com

Something smaller could more easily bypass the attention of the horrors out there.

Inside of this vessel termed as the Omniversal Voyager, Noah walked across a crystalline white floor- his direction being towards the curvaceous visage of Protector Lucretia who was staring out through the transparent glass made of unknown materials.

The Equestris Protectors worked fast after being given clearance, with their leave from the Obelisk of the Protectors being quick while delving into this personal Vessel of Command Protector Valeria that instantly shot out of the Omniverse of the Protectors.

The figures of Command Protector Valeria and the three Royal Existential Omniversal Simian Protectors were at the very front of the vessel, Protector Lucretia being the only one in this area as Noah came to stand beside her while staring out into the endless dark brilliance with the occasional flashes of white around them.

The moment he arrived, Protector Valeria spoke out in a serene tone.

"Do you know how the Outerversal Morphons refer to this endless expanse of Existence we are currently crossing? The Megalos Ynnere. We've heard them mention it many times over the eons as they truly believe in giving us such a distinction, as if we are only a part of something, and not everything. Where we are is the endless expanse of Existence itself, but they seem to indicate otherwise. What do you believe, Protector Osmont?"

Her question was a thoughtful one.

Noah took in the information she offered while replying.

"I don't think we encompass all of the stretches of Existence itself. On this, I would agree more with the Outerversal Morphons."

"Oh? You would side with a viewpoint of Outerversal Morphons?" The brows of Protector Lucretia were raised with interest as her crimson hair danced on her shoulders like flaming snakes, her face showing a playful smile.

Noah smiled with a sharp gaze as he nodded.

"Yes. After all, apart from being able to unravel and expose Outerversal Morphons with my Authorities, my eyes can at times recognize an agent of an Outerversal Morphon with just a mere gaze if they ever happened to stand right before me."

...!

Calm words.

Yet the playful smile on Protector Lucretia faded as her eyes became immeasurably sharp while staring right into Noah!
