Chapter 3353 Infiniverse Megalos Seed! I

Over endless stretches of the Expanse.

Over the Megalos and the countless light years they covered.

There were innumerable entities within as whenever every single one made a move that would tremendously alter their existence, there was always a shred of caution and doubt.

They always knew that something could go wrong.

But, it was different for the Infinite Omniverse.

It had planned and calculated.

From its countless calculations, it had even gone above and beyond what it considered enough- devouring more Tainted Omniverses and Dark Matter Cores than it even needed for its complexity!

The boundless weavings of the Megalos from Ozymandias that it had only stopped just now to ensure that there would be no interruptions.

The Existential Terraforming that greatly expanded its weavings.

It had already surpassed all the scopes it set for success in transcending existence by a terrifying margin as the possibility of failure...was infinitely close to zero!

This was because the Infinite Omniverse knew that it couldn't afford to fail.

|My Extremity Vein.|

HUUUM!

The fabric of existence that made the Infinite Omniverse was buzzing as the Extremity Vein was stimulated.

The Manifestation of the Infinite Omniverse had a calm expression, but one could see immeasurable defiance in its eyes as it called out again!

|My Aletheian Havens.|

...!

Surrounding the Extremity Vein, the dazzling Aletheian Havens of Dreams, Apollyon, Elysium, Genesis, Helios, Oceanus, Prana...all these Havens pulsed with shocking valor as they contributed.

They were Havens, but they also had other identities.

|My Ordinances.|

WU!

One of the tools it had as a domain that others did not have.

Its Havens...were declared Ordinances under its Master!

And not just its Havens.

But also what made up the very foundation and pillars of the Infinite Omniverse were declared Ordinances!

Their level of Complexity was more than enough to induce transcendence within any Entity.

And yet the Infinite Omniverse held dozens of such Ordinances as its Pillars of support, along with their complexity!

So it asked again!

Who decided it would forever remain an Omniverse?

BOOM!

|Rise.|

Its voice boomed out across the Aletheian Havens and Extremity Vein.

All the countless areas of complexity it had so intricately prepared were ushered and moved at once, the connection towards Noah being focused on the most!

The connection towards a part of him that was not within these Megalos.

The connection towards the part of him within a Blancverse Domain inside the Expanse!

|Transcend.|

Commandingly.

Unquestioningly!

The Infinite Omniverse stated so as all the prepared Complexity...was to be used to directly traverse into the Expanse where Noah was!

This was what all the preparations were for.

For a single journey.

For a single jump!

The moment this journey was complete, the Infinite Omniverse would have transcended its past weavings of existence and be something else entirely once it arrived in the Blancverse Domain in the Expanse!

BOOM!

An unfathomable light wrapped around the Infinite Omniverse entirely- this construct buzzing as at this moment, within Omniverse X-999, an insignificant singularity blazed with terrifying brilliance.

A light so bright that the Omniversal Ankyra, who was currently in the midst of becoming One With Existence after his ventures across the Primalwater of the Primaltree of Existence, was shocked and blinded, barely catching a glimpse of this brilliance as an instant later, 99.999% of it disappeared.

A mere fraction was left behind within Omniverse X-999 as in part, this Omniverse that contained the Vacuous and Vitalis Domains were roots that were not abandoned!

But the entirety of the Infinite Omniverse itself had long since disappeared!

At this moment, the Manifestation of the Infinite Omniverse was gazing out as if it could see absolutely every grain of sand and any changes it experienced within this domain.

The whole Infinite Omniverse felt like a rapidly traversing body as it was surrounded by multicolored light, and a terrifying pressure was bearing down on it the further ahead it traveled!

But the more distance it covered, the closer it got to something grand.

To the Expanse.

And the closer it was to the Expanse, its complex weavings of Existence across its Aletheian Havens and Extremity vein began to undergo fundamental changes.

The complexity of the Infinite Omniverse began to rise above Terrestrial!

The closer it got to the Expanse, the closer it would be towards the next demarcation of complexity.

The closer it got to the Expanse, the more terrifying the pressure that was pressing down on it became!

As if the pressure was stating again and again, and with even more ferocity each time that the Infinite Omniverse should not do this.

It couldn't do this!

And yet...

|What are you to decide that?!|

The expression of the Manifestation of the Infinite Omniverse gained more emotion as heavy, unfathomable majesty erupted on it, livid defiance flashing on its face as it kneeled down near the multicolored throne of authorities and roared!

HUUUUM!

A deep, ancient roar of authorities.

It activated waves of countless weavings of existence of the Infinite Omniverse as instantly, the distance it covered and crossed exponentially increased as it only became faster!

Within the Expanse.

In an unknown region, between two golden strings, a miniaturized Blancverse Domain could be seen.

Within its depths, a golden robed young emperor with astral brilliance surging around him opened his eyes wide as he...heard a distinct roar.

A roar of endless defiance and regality.

He didn't hear it from the Infinite Omniverse, but from the depths of the Blancverse Domain that he had loosened the bindings of the most to welcome a new entrant!

For him to hear this roar...

HUUM!

His eyes turned down with a sense of ecstasy.

He looked at the center of the Blancverse Domain where a spinning white gold singularity had begun to bloom.

As if a pathway was being opened for something connected to him to arrive!

All this time, the Infinite Omniverse had calculated and planned as when it moved into action, there was only assured success!

BOOM!

The Blancverse Domain trembled

With the control and understanding that Noah had gained recently, he loosened its restrictions towards what was coming as the task of the Infinite Omniverse became even easier!
