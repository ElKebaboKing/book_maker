Chapter 3301 The Weavings of the Omnifather! III

A great deal of questions, and yet no viable answers!

It meant that they had to be unearthed for themselves as Noah went ahead to do just this.

He sent his people into the square white portal as his will lay within all of them.

It coursed through their blood.

At this moment, the Blood of the Omniversal Archontes Engineer burned within his son as whatever Henry could see...so could he!

A distortion of weavings of existence later, his son and others very quickly came to as they opened their eyes.

And all around them... were incandescent, sparkling, cerulean waves of water.

They were underwater entirely, but everything was extremely lit up and clear as these waters did not show an end in sight!

Around them, figures of entities from a variety of races and lineages stood, with Humans, Simians, Antediluvian Leviathans, and Draconic Entities making up the vast majority. All of them ranged within the Existential Dominium Realm...all the way to the Omniversal Realm.

But undoubtedly, nobody within these waters had Transcended their own weavings of existence.

In here, Noah's will located Imperium who was gazing above at dazzling golden words that shone brilliantly above the surrounding waters.

[After countless Eons, the Tresorverse of the Omnifather has opened to all those affiliated with Matter.]

[The Tresorverse of the Omnifather is a Bridge that can take one from where they stand to merely a step away from the Wheel of Existence. Wade across the Tresorverse Primalwater used to nurture the Tresorverse Primaltree of Existence, and you will gain the capability to ascend the Tresorverse Primaltree of Existence itself.]

...!

The words were only slightly different from the ones that appeared within the part of the Tresorverse of the Omnifather that Noah and other powerful entities appeared in.

The Primalwater utilized to nurture the Primaltree of Existence itself!

Just by standing within these waters, Noah could feel the blood within his son and others begin to burn with activity.

Their existence passively began to increase in complexity while their Extremes surged with ever greater intensity.

And in the midst of all of this...

BOOM!

A commotion broke out below as heavy weavings of combat echoed out.

When they looked below, the extremely clear waters showed sparse globules of radiant light that were stretched out across the distant waters, and an Antediluvian Leviathan that was much smaller than those Noah had come across could be seen holding a Royal Existential Omniversal Simian in his hands.

The body of this Antediluvian Leviathan was humanoid and simmering with dazzling tendrils of blue lightning, his very skin crackling with waves of lightning as the shattered golden wheel that had initially attacked him lay broken!

The Omniversal Stage Simian within his hands struggled with terror as never in his life would he have believed that his power- the System of Existence of Kuklos, would fail against mere Lightning.

But this lightning seemed to carry such unfathomable complexity that it even overcame the weavings of his Wheel, and at this moment...

|Spare-|

BOOM!

A clap of thunder and lightning boomed out as the hand of the Antediluvian Leviathan closed fully.

The Fabled Omniverse of the Simian was scorched by trillions of complex lightning bolts that seemed to carry their own omniversal authority- the faint makeup of an illusory omniverse made of pure blue lightning briefly flashing on the Antediluvian Leviathan as he turned his eyes back on the globe of radiant light before him- grasping it and bringing it to his mouth right away!

...!

The globe was devoured entirely as moments later, the power of this Antediluvian Leviathan began to rise up with shocking speed, their existence becoming increasingly complex.

This...

All those who watched this scene instantly looked towards the distant globes within this sea below them with boundless greed, their bodies shooting out in different directions as chaos ensued!

|Move together, and steer clear of others!|

Henry's intent echoed out commandingly as the entities of the Infinite Omniverse moved as a singular group, delving into the waters below as around them, more and more figures were teleported in!

They were among the weakest.

Most of them weren't even at the Autarchical Stage!

And here, they had just seen an Omniversal Stage Entity perish.

In an area of shocking mystery, a mad scramble began as very few actually knew what was happening!

In a Sector unmapped by the Cartographer of Existence.

A white robed man sat calmly as hundreds of Omniverses had stably stopped spinning, and there were floating like dazzling stars around him as his gaze was that of utmost calmness.

"The Omnifather spoke of the methods of the Great Beyonder that were invoked early and allowed the Morphons to advance greatly over the past cycles. Over all these Cycles, the mechanisms he put in space have collected and concentrated great deals of wonder from the Expanse in one place...but will it be enough? Neither the Great Beyonder nor the Omnifather knew what that Entity from the Megalos Nihilos had planned..."

...!

The mechanisms that the Omnifather had put in place for eons!

They had been harvesting and concentrating countless unknown wonders by means that even this entity did not understand.

He only followed the weavings of the Omnifather to trigger everything when the time was ready.

When the Wheel made a certain turn.

As for what results would come from it all...

"Will it be enough?"

With hundreds of Omniverses filled with ancient Antediluvian Leviathans and other Lineages calmly floating around him, this entity turned his head up and gazed at the endless dark waves of the Megalos Ynnere.

His gaze carried ancientness. His gaze carried interminable power and complexity.

His gaze carried longing.

His gaze carried a yearning as he thought of the Omnifather.

Why did he not take me, he wanted to ask.

Why allow things to play out this way?

He had many questions too, but he would follow the Will of the Omnifather regardless.

For he was the Watchman of the Megalos Ynnere.

And his purpose was a heavy, heavy ordeal!
