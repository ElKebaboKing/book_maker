Chapter 1123 - NOAH! II

The reply from Noah caused an eruption of fortune both from him and a certain Antiquity that was located far across Dimensions, both of these beings monitoring this situation closely as the consciousness of the Antiquity spoke out.

"Well need a way to communicate. This consciousness will fade away entirely soon, and I wont be able to reach out into this Dimensions again unless there is a medium."

The voice seemed to be searching for something when it was speaking, changing entirely to a quizzical tone next.

"The medium in the past was Chronos...where is that guy?"

"Oh?" Noah raised his head towards the question of the Antiquity as he smiled lightly.

"That guy? I locked him in a time prison since I have some plans for him later. For him to be a medium across Dimensions, did it have something to do with his Dao of Reincarnation?"

...!

Noah hit the nail of the head as the Antiquity was surprised, shaking his head wryly with his real body as his consciousness in the Abyssal Universe looked pitifully as the Oathkeeeper and the 12 locked Hegemonies beside him.

"These guys and Chronos were really fighting against a guy with a cheat-like system and didnt know it...no wonder everything was such a mess."

The voice of the Antiquity was that of pity as when Noah picked it up, his brows perked up as he spoke out quizzically.

"Dont forget you were above them, it was your loss too."

...!

The Antiquity nearly choked from this as his rumbling voice shot back. "Well...just a small setback for me as I lost a Subjugated Antiquity. But yes, if you can somehow obtain The Dao of Reincarnation, just enter a half dead state and you should be able to see your timeline."

"My authority has spread into this Cosmos and timeline enough that Ill be able to sense you with my unique abilities when you do, and we can discuss more things then."

Gradually the voice of the Antiquity was filled with conviction, this being having figured something out during their conversation!

Noah nodded to his words as he saw his Destiny and Fortune settling down, where he had already sown certain seeds as he would wait for them to bloom and see exactly what would come out.

"Until then...I have some work to do."

His voice reached the Antiquity as their conversation came to an end, a possibility being planted at this moment as the consciousness of the being Noah shared a similar name to began to fade away.

"Ill be waiting to hear from you soon...Noah!"

THRUM!

With a transparent flash of light, Noah felt a pressure be lifted as the consciousness of the Antiquity was now gone!

Far across Dimensions, the scene that this Antiquity could see also faded away, his eyes only seeing the expanse of white coldness around him as his cerulean blue eyes flashed beautifully.

The existence made of ice beside him saw he was done as she spoke with a smile.

"How long will you be staying this time?"

The voice seemed to be tinged with happiness and sadness, causing the Great Usurper to let out a light smile at this as he patted her head and replied.

"I wont be going anywhere for a while. Well take some time to reinforce our strength and plan. Maybe we wont be alone in this battle...."

With such words, the red haired woman made of particles of ice smiled brightly as she nodded.

"Un!"

This was because he was thinking further ahead about a few things, his mind focused on the Primordial Cosmos and the task of the Quintillions of beings he had brought into the Dark Universe.

RUMBLE!

This being had not wasted his millions of years, especially so with the fact that he was one of the strongest beings in the Cosmos!

WAA!
