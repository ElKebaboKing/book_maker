Chapter 3014 Osmont's Proof of Dignity! IV

3014 Osmont's Proof of Dignity! IV

Her existence was a unique one.

She wasn't apathetic to everything because she thought herself above others.

She didn't participate in the workings and rituals of the Sanctus Populi not because she couldn't be bothered to, but because she had seen thighs others simply couldn't fathom!

The day she was born, she felt such immense pain that in trying to eliminate it, she nearly stripped away the source of the most powerful Existential Extremity Authority in the Domain she was in.

She saw too many things as on the Elder Queen Mother, on the Elder Queens, on the Sanctus Princess...all those she came across- their Dominiums were marked with an unbearably heavy darkness of oblivion and damnation.

They were all marked for destruction.

Bearing such knowledge the day she was born was a weight others couldn't even fathom, but it was her first years of life when she moved madly to prevent this fate, grasping weavings others couldn't even think of, connecting even to Omniversal Authorities...even with all of this, she found that nothing changed.

She looked further ahead.

She saw some possibilities.

Of people like her.

Those referred to as blessed by the Omniverse.

Those known as Omniversal Anomalies.

She theorized weavings of existence with such entities included, and tried to foresee far into the eons to come...but nothing changed.

Thus, she grew despondent.

She had all this power.

She had capabilities that this Sanctum of Dignity didn't even know half of.

And yet even with all of this power...she was essentially powerless.

No matter how much she tried to change things, it didn't matter!

So what use was all this power?

It grew to the point that she even stopped annealing her Dominium and let it be.

Whether she remained in the Teleios Stage or Teracosm Stage or further above, nothing changed.

So why bother?

This was the reason for her nonchalant and apathetic demeanor as to the Elder Queens, this was an affront to her blessings and power as they wanted her to do something- not Knowing she had tirelessly tried to do many things for them to no avail.

And now, here she was.

Doing something.

A process of Knighthood.

The light of apathy in her eyes was gradually drowned out by a light of interest the moment her expectations were defied- the moment this being made it clear he would not climb up!

He instead forcibly altered the weavings of Dignity she had laid down and made a pathway from the descending stairs- Dignity that Sona could see he was expressing for the first time truly!

And when he began walking towards her grandly as his intent told her to express her own Dignity...she finally understood something.

She finally realized what the thing she just couldn't put her thoughts on...the thing that caused her confusion when she first saw him.

All entities she looked at across this Dominium and those she studied the weavings of outside of it had a darkness.

A Shadow of Damnation.

She had seen it so much all of her life that she had actually blanked out...when she saw a being that didn't have this shadow.

...!

Yes.

He didn't have the Shadow of Damnation!

Such a reality could mean many things as the moment she ascertained it...

BOOM!

Her figure flung forward with horrific speed and power across the golden path of Dignity.

BOOM!

From her body, solidified waves of golden Dignity erupted out in shockwaves that spread all around, the figures of Sanctus Knights feeling their Dominiums shuddering as they were flung back!

Only Sanctus Princess Emilliana and Dragon Knight Alder were among those nearby in this region who remained still even as their gazes changed.

In the distant space above the Nine Dragons statues, the visage of The Elder Queen Ariana that had just left reappeared, her gaze stern as moments later, other Elder Queens began to appear beside her!

All entities at the Teracosm Stage of Existential Dominium Realm.

One was shrouded in a purple light as she gazed down while her intent weaved calmly.

|That child actually did something for a change?|

...!

The weaving was only meant for the Elder Queens who had come to observe the shocking fluctuations they felt.

And yet wondrously, far down on the golden path of Dignity, Sona heard the whispers of these intents easily as she felt all sources of Dominiums nearby.

Yes, she was doing something for a change.

Because she had to confirm.

She had to see if this Entity before her was an entirely new Omniversal Anomaly she hadn't come across before, and whether anything would change with the weavings she had seen before if he was incorporated.

The first test she sent out was uniquely still a test of Dignity.

Any Anomaly had to have an extremely high value as this entity before her had to be distinguished from a common existence to an uncommon one.

And as Teleios Stage Entities in the surroundings were knocked back from the shockwaves of pure Dignity she released...

HO!

The bustling visage of the entity opposite to Sona continued on the golden path of Dignity as if the shockwaves weren't even felt by him!

It didn't mean he was more powerful than Teleios Stage Entities- it only meant that when it came to Dignity, he far surpassed such beings!

'Good.'

The light in Sona's eyes began to shine even brighter.

She had shot forth with such blinding speed that she reached the halfway mark of the golden path of Dignity, and the Entity opposite to her had also reached the halfway point as they came inches away from each other- their dignity buzzing and warping the surrounding space as it felt like two fields were trying to establish their rulership!

Around her, shockwaves of Dignity burst constantly as from the entity opposite to her, snake like tendrils of Dignity supported by royal purple and colorless lights raged madly- some of these tendrils even beginning to form a throne behind him as if to show off his majesty!

She looked at this being closely.

And he seemed to be looking at her closely.

Sensing the deep waves and reserves of Dignity before her, she nodded to affirm that he at the very least had passed this benchmark- her eyes grasping as much of his weavings as she could as her intent weaved out.

|The name is Sona. You are?|

The weavings within this intent were simple.

And the one she asked pulsed with brilliance as he replied just as simply.

|Osmont, Noah Osmont.|

Behind him, unfathomable reserves of Dignity raged as Sona looked at them calmly while nodding, her next words being anything but simple.

|Osmont, do you want to join an Initiative?|

A question.

A question about something that no Elder Queens or Sanctus Princesses would even understand.

This was the result of Noah's Dignity being tested as around the two of them who were nonchalantly clashing with heavy waves of Dignity, Dragon Knights, Sanctus Princesses,and distant Elder Queens were gazing with calculative eyes carrying varying levels of shock!
