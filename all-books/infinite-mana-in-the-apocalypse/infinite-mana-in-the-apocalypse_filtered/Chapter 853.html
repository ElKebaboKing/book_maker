Chapter 851 - With a Swing, A Sword King Falls! II

One might come across a myriad of shocking and incredulous events throughout their lives, but nothing would ever be as incredulous as watching a single Entity with his pets fearlessly match 73 other Entities.

Traverser was watching the scenes with an agape expression as he observed the Sword Kings to be moving slower as if they were in a quagmire, the effects of ||Ashes to Ashes, Dust to Dust|| fully settling in as they had to use their essence to prevent against the corrosion of a horrendous area of attack skill that was now backed with the immense power of multiple Daos and Laws!

The Animus Summons moved in furiously as the Sword Kings did not give them an ounce of fear, their resplendent attacks raining down mercilessly as they attacked multiple Entities at the same time.

The Blue Slime was the most terrifying thing as it seemed to be expanding continuously, its enormous body very soon being able to occupy the surrounding starry space as any that entered the viscous mass would have an extremely horrendous ending!

As for Noah himself...this guy had already closed his eyes as the swords of Aether and Death vibrated in his hands.

He wasnt using his eyes as his aura was spread out wildly, sensing the Universal laws supporting him as well as the Dao essences he was freely releasing from his body with his endless mana.

He had yet to figure out the unique state he entered when he utilized all laws at the same time, but he knew that every single part of him felt extreme exhilaration as his senses were magnified by a large extent.

So with his eyes closed, he waved his Aether Sword as a starry river of swords was formed from the use of [Starry Flow of the Sword].

THRUUM!

His Death Sword waved to the other side as a rain of sharp swords in the thousands tore through space through the use of [Sword of Revelation].

From above, mountainous swords had begun raining from all around as at this moment...a spamming session of ultimate proportions had begun.

Traverser felt his heart grow cold as while he observed, he was shocked to find out that every time Noah waved a sword, one of the Sword Kings on the battlefield would fall!

Other times it was due to the attacks of ferocious Animus Summons that moved with extreme ferocity while using the gaps of defense created by Noahs relentless spamming attacks on the Sword Kings.

But the truth remained the same...every time the being with his eyes closed waved his swords while he continued to spam countless numbers of skills, an Entity fell!

RUMBLE!

The figures of the Sword Kings never reached Noah to even land any attacks on him due to the relentless nature he was spamming his skills, where they could not even get near him.

If they could not even reach him while he constantly attacked and decimated their defenses with abilities that held too high of an attack power...what could they do but fall?!

And so with every swing of the Tyrannical Entitys swords, the battlefield had one less soul present.

"This..."

Traverser felt his scalp tingle at such a scene as he reached towards his forehead to wipe perspiration and sweat. The actions occurring in front of his eyes were so shocking that they made an Entity break into a cold sweat!

In the mystical Realm filled with plains of green, the Sword Emperor looked at the scene where his Sword Kings had reduced by more than 10 in less than a minute.

Is this his current Peak state of power? Extremely powerful, but...

Even as the Sword Emperor saw Entity after Entity fall, his eyes only shone brightly as he remained placid.

"...but it is still not enough!"

RUMBLE!

Yes!

Even though it was a tremendous show of power, the Sage still shook his head as he knew it wasnt enough!

"Against the suicidal Sages from the Spirit Race...it is not enough!"

The Sword Emperor had a thoughtful look as he thought about the incorporeal beings that were the Spirit Race.

When taking into account every powerful being in the Dark Universe, they were the only ones that deviated from the common goal of trying to prevent the apocalypse of their Universe.

At this juncture, Morgana had flown into a rage as she nearly declared a war, the Sword Emperor being the one to pull her back as they digested what just occurred.

The answer had been so shocking that the two of them could not make heads or tails of why.

Why would anyone not want to save their own home?!

As he thought of this, the eyes of the Sword Emperor shone like resplendent lanterns as he decided...the last step truly had to be taken.

"For the Dark Universe."

THRUM!

RUMBLE!
