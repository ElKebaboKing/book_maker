Chapter 3309 The Trunk of the Tresorverse Primaltree of Existence! I

The moment that Arguros smashed into the Trunk of the Tresorverse Primaltree of Existence, a few other observable entities on other roots did the same.

But...the Tresorverse Primaltree of Existence was vast as those in this space couldn't even observe the other side of this massive tree.

And even if they could...would they have been able to even observe entities who had exceeded the stage of Ordinance of Existence and were already in the stage of Ordinance Amalgamation?

It could not be said with surety that Arguros was the first to enter the trunk of the Tresorverse Primaltree of Existence.

A distance away, Noah also closed in as he broke down bundles of information that this Antediluvian Leviathan released.

Ordinance Amalgamation. Existence Severing.

Once complexity arrived at a critical enough juncture, one had to cut themselves away from Existence entirely- they had to Sever!

Yet the fatalities for this and the stage before it were high as the most critical thing was a single, pure source for one to rely on.

Noah...was taking a different route entirely as he had his Atlas of Existence.

Nine different Sources of Existence could fill it as within his first System of Existence, he had the overarching umbrella of concepts as within it- hundreds of concepts mixed in!

Purity of sources was not under his banner. But even after hearing all the things that Arguros stated, his heart never faltered regarding the path he was taking!

Not a single thing would be left behind.

Not Infinity.

Not Tyranny.

Not Quintessence.

He didn't just want a single source, he wanted them all!

HUUM!

His very existence vibrated as if it were agreeing with these thoughts, his figure becoming a pure blur of light as within moments, the trunk of the Tresorverse Primaltree of Existence was before his eyes as well.

PAH!

His figure felt like it smashed into a comfortable body of plasma and not the solid exterior of a tree, disappearing into it entirely as he...was among the first set of Entities to step into the Trunk of the Tresorverse Primaltree of Existence!

Alongside those who had formed their Ordinances- and theoretically, some who had even Amalgamated it.

It was unknown just what level of power was the highest among those who had entered the square white portals that appeared within the vast stretches of the Megalos Ynnere.

The moment that Noah stepped into the Trunk of the Tresorverse Primaltree of Existence, his figure found itself transported across...a string.

A string reminiscent of the countless golden strings within the Expanse- but this string was verdant in color as he crossed uncountable distances to finally arrive...in a small domain that was surrounded by 9 circular layers of verdant rings of light.

The rings were spinning calmly around each other, the largest one occupying the most space on the outside as there were smaller rings towards the center.

And Noah appeared in the very center as not too far from him was a flickering golden star.

This star was radiating an intent that bloomed into understandable words soon after.

[You have stepped onto the Trunk of the Tresorverse Primaltree of Existence and accessed the Tresorverse Sacred Domain that was sculpted by the Omnifather from a segment of the Expanse and the Megalos Ynnere.]

...!

[The Tresorverse Sacred Domain has its own Existential Continuum, with no entry allowed for any Entity unless it is through the possession and assimilation of the existence of those already living within it.]

[The lifeforms within the Tresorverse Sacred Domain are unique entities that only existed for a few cycles many eons ago within the Expanse, their Weavings of Existence being so potent that they could smoothly traverse across the States of Existence in an extremely short period of time. Known as the Sacred Primal Megalonians, the potency of their weavings of existence also made them targets as when devoured by others, tremendous levels of complexity of existence can be gained that make it easy to attain the more intricate latter states of Existence and even greater weavings of complexity.]

[These Sacred Primal Megalonians were hunted, eradicated, and devoured entirely the moment that the potency of their existence was discovered by others, never gaining the chance to illuminate across the Expanse. The Omnifather has pulled onto unfathomable weavings to reconstruct the existence of these lifeforms within this Tresorverse Sacred Domain, and you can possess and assimilate a random Sacred Primal Megalonian depending on your level of complexity of existence.]

[In the time it takes for this dying star to fade, release as much of your complexity of existence into this isolated domain, and depending on the quantity and quality of complexity, you will be granted the possession and assimilation of an Average Sacred Primal Megalonian, a Rare Sacred Primal Megalonian, a Quintessential Sacred Primal Megalonian, or even an Origin Sacred Primal Megalonian. Depending on which Megalonian you assimilate while entering the Tresorverse Sacred Domain, the level of talent and true source of the reconstructed Megalonian will vary tremendously.]

[The timeline of the Tresorverse Sacred Domain is set, the events to play out within are set, and the eventual destruction of the Sacred Primal Megalonians is set. The gains made in complexity and states of Existence will be retained once the set events have come to pass, and the pathway up the Tresorverse Primaltree of Existence will open up once more.]

...!

A flood of waves of information surged out from the dazzling star, Noah processing it extremely quickly as great surprise reverberated through him!

A domain with a set timeline, set events, and set entities.

Accessible only by possession and assimilation, but the gains to be made within were tremendous as they were based on...what type of entity you could possess.

An Average Sacred Primal Megalonian, a Rare Sacred Primal Megalonian, a Quintessential Sacred Primal Megalonian, or even an Origin Sacred Primal Megalonian!

And the selection of such an entity was based on the complexity and amount of existence one could release within this space filled with 9 rings of verdant light!
