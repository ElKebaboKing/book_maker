Chapter 3365 Corpse! I

A body that was ten thousand miles long!

It looked slender with tout muscles, its skin gray and cracking as the head showed long white hair and pointed ears.

The corpse floated in the Expanse with its eyes entirely dimmed, but one could see an expression of defiance and resignation written on the face of this being even in death.

It's eyes.

The pupils were cracked and splintered as all they left behind were residues of what this being used to be.

This was the body of an entity that had failed Existence Severing!

The Ancient Phrike, Vujun, became contemplative as he looked at it.

|Its alignment is matter, but...look at its expression.|

...!

He couldn't help but observe that gaze of defiance.

That look of a being that had strove for success and toiled away for who knew how many cycles

|Even in its collapse of existence, it was defiant. And yet it still could Sever its Existence. What happens when we arrive at such a point ourselves? If we face the collapse of our own Existence?|

|...|

The Nihilos Shelled Snail and the Necrotic Nest-Slug looked at each other in silence before the eyes of the slug-like entity flickered with brightness.

|What the fuck, Vujun? We invited you here just to combine our complexities and get near this body to devour it. Why the hell are you becoming a philosopher? Worry about your own Existence Severing when you get to it. You have even fewer worries than us being connected to that honored personage.|

The voice flickered with emotion towards the end as the many eyes of Vujun blinked as he nodded.

|Let's go.|

Their bodies flickered with heavy radiance as the light of chilling banners wrapped around them seamlessly, staying close together as they shot towards the floating body!

HUUM!

The moment they even came close, crescent waves of a terrifying authority lashed out towards them- an authority that did not want its owner to verify desecrated even in death.

It radiated a verdant allure as it was the Ordinance of Vitality.

It still brimmed within the corpses that had tried to sever itself away from Existence- where even though it failed, this Vitality was still potent enough to hang on until now!

BOOM!

It smashed onto the bodies of the three Anti-Matter Ordinance Amalgamation entities as they withstood it easily from this distance.

But the closer they went to the corpses.

CAAA!

The more verdant storms erupted and struck out against them, but they withstood and made their way over slowly!

Around this same time.

Many light years away, a distance of about one Omniverse.

A white space silently flickered into existence.

Within it, a man floated in a meditative position while the figure of a multicolored woman was finishing an infusion of a shocking process, her hand having moved from his chest and on his back.

When one took a closer look, one could see the very reality surrounding this man vibrating as his skin rumbled and roiled as if giant snakes were coursing underneath!

An unfathomable crimson gold radiance flashed over his skin as it seemed like quintillions of sheets of singularities were stacked on top of each other to reinforce it.

Colorless gold wings stretched out behind his back as the feathers made of pure colorless gold light seemed to be sharper than the blade of a common Ordinance Amalgamation entity.

Golden tattoo like bands covered his body, the largest one being on his chest as it seemed to hold within it the reserves of endless weight and tonnage.

At this moment.

|The Infiniverse Megalos Physiology has attained a state of awakening within your weavings of Existence.|

A prompt flashed over Noah's eyes.

Behind him, the Infiniverse Megalos Body released her hands from his back as her tremendous eyes stared at him- and within their depths, one could almost see a flash of pride.

Almost.

Feeling the unique complexity coursing within his body, Noah moved from the meditative position and stood up as this simple motion...

HUUM!

Caused the surrounding space to tremble.

As if something far too heavy was moving about!

"Hmm..." He raised his hand and clenched his fist as he felt the equivalent force of millions of singularities imploding just from the motion of his fingers.

It was all purely a complexity of his Physiology as even he felt extremely heavy, as if every step to take would be effortful!

But once he acclimated and was able to move freely...he could easily wield this tonnage of complexity that was the equivalent of the complexity of the Infiniverse Megalos Seed.

||Any force you apply is now equivalent to the Infiniverse Extremity Vein, the Infiniverse Aletheian Havens surrounding it, and the vast complexity of the Infiniverse Megalos Seed itself. Its advancement in complexity has allowed you to utilize it for a fraction of the previous cost, to the extent that only a tenth of your reserves of authority will be kept at bay to manage the utilization of the Infiniverse Megalos Physiology.||

The Infiniverse Megalos Body released its intent and looked over Noah up and down as if she was looking at a weapon born from an unfathomable Forge.

Before, Noah could only borrow the strength and weight of the Infiniverse Megalos Seed at a high cost and only utilize it a few times, but its jump in complexity to become a Constellation now made it possible for him to utilize its sheer tonnage and complexity constantly, at a cost he could bear with ease!

The higher up in complexity one became, the simpler things seemed for them.

As he felt the complexity and weight of the power coursing through him, he knew he had to get acclimated to it as wondrously enough, there were targets not too far from here!

He came for the Ancient Phrike, Vujun, and yet this spider did not seem to be alone as alongside it, there were two other Anti-Matter entities!

Their power was not small as the moment that Noah appeared even a distance of an Omniverse away, he saw them, and they saw him!
