Chapter 613 - Outsiders! II

The Novus Galaxy was in an extremely peaceful era where many of the creatures in the Light and Dark Expanse were forging new lives as they recovered from the terror of constant war with the disappearance of the Celestials.

Not many knew the truth that the leader of this organization had actually exterminated this enormous force himself, and they lived in blissful ignorance as they continued on with their lives! Even at this moment as for the first time in the history of this Novus Galaxy when Outsiders were about to enter, only a few creatures in this Galaxy knew or felt it!

RUMBLE!

In the Letalis Expanse where the boundary of the Novus Galaxy could be seen, an Imperial Vessel could be seen squeezing through the miles long boundary with ease as it was covered by a unique energy.

This energy stemmed from a single being- the one at the forefront who was a Grand Controller of Chaos, and actually had the essence required to freely travel through the Chaotic Void as well as easily pass through the boundaries of galaxies, or even shatter or portion of such boundaries if they wished!

But the Grand Controller was moving with caution this time as they were undertaking a fairly dangerous task of entering another Galaxy. The problem at this moment lay in the unknowns, where even though they knew that this Galaxy should only be a Millenium Galaxy with at most a few Controllers, they still had to move carefully to make sure nothing went wrong.

SAAA!

The mystical chariot that was the Imperial Vessel passed through the boundary as it appeared in the Novus Galaxy for the first time, the three Law Masters and Blessed within the chariot stepping out and floating alongside it as the Void Beasts breathed in the new atmosphere with vigor!

"This..."

The eyes of the Outsiders from the Nexus Galaxy shone with l.u.s.trous light as they sensed the surrounding space, their awareness confirming with them the truth of this galaxy as they affirmed it really was less than fifty thousand years old!

They knew this because this was not the only outside Galaxy they were stepping in aside from their Nexus Galaxy. They had been sent on missions before that required them to step into fearsome galaxies of close stature to their own, and a few that were at lower levels.

But during all this time, they always explored Galaxies where their predecessors were there before them! This new Galaxy they stepped in...they saw that they were actually the first as they sensed the peaceful nature and movement of essence!

RUMBLE!

At this point, essence rumbled out from the body of the Grand Controller, the unique essence of chaos permeating out as his voice resounded.

"Continue to be careful and do not celebrate yet. This Galaxy must have its own protectors, and as all Galaxies sense foreign objects and beings entering them, they will be sending their protectors over to us at any moment."

OOOM!

His words carried immense weight as the others nodded and their bodies began permeating with power, the Fire Law Master and the Space Law Master beside the Grand Controller actually lighting up as their bodies released powerful Authorities to surround the Imperial Vessel in a defensive manner!

They had to do this because as mentioned before, a Galaxy could always sense foreign beings that did not belong to it, causing any outside forces entering any galaxy to be sensed right away as if it contained powerful enough Protectors, these Outsiders could be defeated and thrown out with ease!

"I am confident after entering this galaxy that they have a few Controllers or powerful Blessed, and at most, two or three Grand Controllers. We will test whatever is sent our way first before we decide to act."

The eyes of the Grand Controller were shining with greed as they stared at the foreign space around them, all he could feel from it being its young age as his heart looked forward to the treasures of this Galaxy!

RUMBLE!

Before his voice could even settle in the ears of others, they felt a strong aura propel towards them rapidly as the first thing they could see were its extremely large star-sized eyes.

WAA!

This strong aura rushed towards them as they could feel intense emotions of anger from its eyes, its enormous size stopping hundreds of miles away from them as the red eyes locked onto their party atop the Imperial Vessel.

The Cthulhu had made an appearance as the six beings looked at it carefully, their eyes not changing as they simply floated and kept their energy ready to go without moving!

This was because they were waiting...they were waiting to see just how many protectors this Galaxy could afford to send towards them!

RUMBLE!

A second later, another two auras appeared, their sizes just as large as they were in the thousands of miles. The Ancient Terror and the Terror of the Void appeared with cold looks as they also stared at the outsiders from a distance of a few hundred miles away.

It was an extremely tense situation as the Outsiders from the Nexus Galaxy waited a few seconds longer, the frosty Blessed who held the Trait of [Ice Man] not being able to hold himself back as an ecstatic expression actually appeared on his face!

"Haha, this is all? It looks like this venture will be extremely worthwhile!"

Shocking words were heard from the Outsiders by the three Terrors as they were filled with immense disdain. This was because the Outsiders could sense the auras of Grand Controllers on the three Protectors, with this aura being strongest around the Cthulhu that wriggled with baleful tentacles filled with Aether.

Even the face of the Grand Controller of Chaos was filled with light as he waited a few seconds longer before confirming they had really struck it rich to be entering a Galaxy that had so few Protectors!

"Outsiders, you are not welcomed here!"

RUMBLE!

The voice of the Terror of Aether rang out as it was filled with anger and unwillingness, the thing it did not want the most to happen actually taking place at this moment! But the Outsiders he spoke paid him no need as the frosty Blessed laughed while stepping forward, the authorities of the two Law Masters still actively surrounding the force of six Outsiders.

"Boorish creature, has nobody ever told you size is not everything?"

The words of the Blessed were filled with confidence as even while sensing that the creature in front of him was a Grand Controller of the Law of Aether, he stepped forward fearlessly and spoke down towards it!

OOOM!

A unique feeling of power released from this Blessed as he freely activated his Trait, [Ice Man].

SHAA!

For the surrounding thousands of miles, the starry space instantly became a realm of ice as the being that was the cause of it all flung his arms out with a wide smile and spoke.

"A galaxy in its infancy just waiting for us to explore...ahh!"

RUMBLE!

An enormous area of complete white permeated for thousands of miles in the Letalis Expanse near the barrier of the Novus Galaxy, six outsiders appearing as they disdainfully looked down at the natives sent to stand on their way!
