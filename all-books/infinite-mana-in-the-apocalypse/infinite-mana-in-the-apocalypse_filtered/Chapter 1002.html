Chapter 1000 - A Goal of Universal Conquest! II

The arrival of Chronos had even made Valentina who was showing off terrifying battle prowess to pull back.

It had filled the watching Hegemonies with apprehension as they looked at his heavy weight in the same tier as Oathkeeper and wondered just what plans he had within this group of Hegemonies!

Yet in the midst of all this, a Paragon came and stole the spot light as he appeared in the middle of the intense stand off, finding the restriction that was present in all the 9 Universes after the process of Universal Amalgamation was initiated.

"..."

Ambrose looked towards the figure of Noah with intense wrath, this being accounting for many of his plans being knocked out of order as his entrance at this time was like getting slapped multiple times!

What was worse was that he couldn't even move against such a creature that he thought would be dead, expecting Dark Shadow to have killed him first even before the Amalgamation began. But he now watched as this being domineeringly stood in front of them, his figure smiling as he began to float towards the Universal Construct that was behind Ambrose and the others.

All eyes were on this nonchalant Tyrant Dragon as he came upon the crimson barrier enacted by Ambrose, his body lighting up with the essence of destruction as he released attacks upon attacks at this barrier!

BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!

The explosive force of galaxies dying emanated from his body as he attacked a barrier enacted by a Hegemony to no avail, only stopping seconds later as he let out a sigh.

No Hegemonies could attack those in the lower Ranks within these Universes, but those in the Lower Ranks didn't stand a chance of stopping their plans as they couldn't break the barriers of protection that would no doubt be enacted across all 9 Universal Constructs!

This was why even though Ambrose and the others were maddened by the disrespect of this Paragon, they weren't the least bit worried about him.

THRUM!

The purple clock behind Chronos faded away as he gazed at Noah with a somber light of curiosity, this being not appearing in the past lives he lived as he took him as one of the many changes he was experiencing in this life.

Any changes that deviated from what he experienced before...he had to pay attention to!

So he watched with utter clarity as the Tyrant Dragon gazed towards the direction of the boundary of the Animus Universe where he knew Oathkeeper and the others were watching as he spoke once more.

"Paragons and below cannot be harmed by this group of Hegemonies, but we cannot get past their protection of their Universal Constructs. Unless...a strong enough Paragon who has the power capable of contending against a Hegemony while in the lower ranks appears!"

...!

This mere Paragon...was he boasting that he would get the power of a Hegemony at his rank as he worked to stand against Chronos and the others?!

He seemed to go at his own pace as his words continued for one more time before he disappeared in a flash of light, only to reappear in another location within the same Animus Universe.

"You all figure out other ways to stop their Universal Constructs- they are gathering the essence of the Universes for a reason, and I think none of us want to wait to find out for what!"

With that, the figure of the Tyrant Dragon disappeared as it left behind multiple Hegemonies that looked at its position in shock.

In front of the Bloody Battlefields Galaxy, Chronos turned towards Ambrose as his eyes gazed towards the direction of the Draconic Holy Lands where he picked up the aura of the same domineering Tyrant Dragon reappearing.

"Tell me everything that has happened since the appearance of that Paragon."

...!

Past the boundary of the Animus Universe where Oathkeeper and a few other Hegemonies watched.

Many of them stared at Valentina as the archaic gaze of Oathkeeper locked onto her as he posed out a question as well.

"Tell us about this Paragon and the things that happened within the Animus Universe..."

As the powerful experts tried to find out their enemies and allies amidst the dreadful actions taken by a certain group of Hegemonies, the identity of one particular Paragon popped up throughout as both sides sought to understand his role!

As for the being in question?

He appeared on the battlefield of the Universal War where 9 Bloodline Races were still fighting, the state of this battle already edging towards completion as his Animus Summons backed by his own power were ripping apart any remaining Paragons of the 5 Bloodline Races.

His appearance was the nail in the coffin as with his return, the Legions being led by a few remaining Paragons felt utter despair as the mere presence of Noah was that suffocating!

A Hegemony had acted against him, and this being didn't die! What would they be able to do?

Noah gazed at these despairing eyes with a sharp expression as his main body continued to sense his continued rise in power, as well as what his influence and actions within the Animus Universe were doing to contribute to his continued forming of the Marks of Antiquity.

For his goal to obtain 50 Billion Marks of Antiquity and forge 50 Billion Dao Galaxies that would enable him to have complete connections of all of his Galaxies...he had told himself he would have to explore other Universes and expand his influence!

In order to birth his Universal Seed and go towards becoming a Hegemony, he saw the fastest way to be the one he just did with the Universal War of the Animus Universe.

A little bit of bloodshed. Obtaining Vassals. Standing as the being with the strongest influence within that Universe.

Such a reality caused Noah's heart to bubble with an intense longing.

A longing for Marks of Antiquity.

A longing for Vassals that could provide him with such marks in the millions.

A longing for the many beings within each of the 9 Universes currently affected by the Universal Amalgamation.

A longing...for Universal Conquest!
