Chapter 3604 Alteration! I

A frequency filled with endless waves of water.

A serene blue covered everything as in a certain region, there was an Aquahaven holding an Empire stretching out for light years.

Towards the center of this Aquahaven, a humanoid Greater Source with the upper body of a human and the lower body of a fish floated grandly- an Atlantian of powerful stature as he carried a blue gold trident in his right hand that was currently shooting out rays of blue light onto Primaltrees of Atlantis- nurturing them and causing fantastical fruits to begin blooming!

Around him, massive multicolored fish swam serenely, many bowing their heads towards him reverentially as they passed.

A distance away, brilliantly lit walls stretching out for miles could be seen surrounding vibrant Aquacitadels of an Empire.

The Greater Source Atlantian continued his work as normal when all of a sudden...

WAP!

The light shooting out of his Trident instantly ceased!

"Huh?"

Bubbles left his mouth as, in the next moment, he felt the heavy pressure of the water all around him, and the gills on the side of his neck began to breathe as he was shocked!

He did not need to breathe.

Nor should he feel any pressure at this moment.

What...

His mind was buzzing as he reached out to weave his Greater Source when...

'Huh? Where...'

He was terrified to realize he couldn't...feel anything.

He couldn't sense anything!

As a Greater Source, his mind could even observe the makeup of his atoms as at this moment, the only thing he could observe was what he could see with his own eyes.

A form of 'inner vision' where he could see inside his body seemingly ceased to exist!

He also felt his hand sinking and being pulled down as the Trident made from the Core of an Omniverse slowly sank down, his hand feeling a tearing force as he let go!

This...

His heart was filled with trepidation and confusion as it felt like his Greater Source and everything he had worked eons for...had just silently disappeared!

As he faced such a terrifying situation.

Behind him, a school of predatory fish- Bluehaven Orcas, who were just reverentially swimming in the distance when they sensed his regal power...one blinked and turned towards the direction of the Atlantian as its eyes showed confusion first, but after that, they became covered by a predatory glint as it sped forth!

Its aim was the Atlantian that released no waves of power or regality, and the Greater Source Atlantian...could only see what was ahead of him with his eyes as he could not even sense the predatory fish silently coming behind him!

...!

This was one being in this vast frequency of waters.

There were many like him, and an uncountable number who were below him in power.

On this day.

Inside the Frequency of Aqua.

Every single one found themselves unable to sense or utilize any form of authority.

All they had...was what their bodies were born with!

It would be the first of many as a Unique Existence moved right after- warping away from this frequency along with a silver white flame bound by black and white Wheels.

He warped away from a frequency where even though every single entity should not be able to access and channel to the Wheel of Existence, he was still able to use authority freely.

It was not because he was an exemption.

It was because he did not draw or channel from the True Source that all other beings here channeled from!

In the Prime Frequency of the End.

The new changes continued to settle over Noah as he monitored everything, this entire scene being one of utmost wonder as a Prime Source Theodosius kneeled quietly in the distance, Ruination had her hand behind his back with great focus, and the Queen of Matter had squinting eyes of excitement and expectation as if she was waiting for something!

But during her wait, what she came across was not something she wanted nor expected by a long shot.

The color drained from her eyes.

Her expression distorted and became ashen as Noah saw this drastic change and became serious, the Queen of Matter speaking before she could even be asked!

"The...the communication of all weavings of Matter with the Frequency of Aqua...are gone! Gone! Has...has an entire Frequency been erased like this?! If it was an action done by someone, it would have at least been gradual as I would see what was behind it. But this...it all just instantly gone!"

Her expression was heavy as she imagined countless dead in a matter of an instant.

Only a single being could possibly do such a thing.

Only a single one had the power to do so!

"BOB!"

She yelled out his name, her lips quivering with a sense of fury as the books connected to strings around her buzzed.

Bob.

Noah's gaze was heavy as he thought of the words of the Queen of Matter, and if there was anything true regarding Bob...he shouldn't have collapsed and killed an entire Frequency of Existence after his last failure!

So...

WAP!

Another body of his adorned in a white Emperor's Robe appeared in this place as he grasped the hand of the Queen of Matter.

"Send me the coordinates of the Frequency of Aqua."

His voice was thick and filled with undeniable command as if what he thought was right, Bob should have just been in that frequency or was still there!

The Queen of Matter held a face of sternness for the first time in a while as bundles of weavings left her and flowed into the body of the newly appearing Noah.

And the moment these weavings were ascertained...

HUUM!

Swirling weavings of existence flashed instantly around them akin to circular multicolored radiance which took the Queen of Matter by surprise, and they disappeared right after!

Foreign.

Noah's weavings...felt foreign and distinctly different from what the Queen of Matter knew as the normal weavings of Existence!

And when it came to the slow process of crossing into different Frequencies that took time and a great deal of Authority, she would come to find this jump Noah made from one frequency to another...took less than a millisecond!
