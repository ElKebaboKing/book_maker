Chapter 437 - To Be Matched Against Special Existences

"The deciders of the direction of the war are the strongest beings, with players at the World Rank or the ranks above being the ones that will ultimately decide which battles we win or lose "

Noah listened closely as the Grandmaster continued, his mind going towards the cultivation ranks the Grandmaster mentioned as he already knew what was above the rank of World.

After a being established a Domain to cover their origin, the next stage was the World Realm as they fused their Domain to their core, being able to bring out power that could destroy Small or Large Worlds!

After the world stage, one would embark on the path to strengthen their origin as if they were forming a world itself at their very center, the realm above this being called the Star Forging Realm!

Experts at this realm legitimately had the force of a star thrumming at the very center of their strength, Noah knowing that the middle ranked Infernal Lords occupied this level, as well as some of the more powerful Grandmasters.

Above the Star Forging Realm was the realm that the experts standing at the top of power in this expansive Novus Galaxy, it was the realm where one had to shatter the very Star they forged which stood at the center of their origin, allowing the explosion of the dying star to form what was the next stage after this- Nebula!

A Nebula was a giant cloud of dust and gas in the vastness of space, and it was this cloud of dust and gas where new stars formed. Experts at the Nebula Realm were those standing at the peak of power as any one of them could have a Nebula that already birthed multiple stars, the levels of strength being wide ranging as the fire power of such a realm was shocking to even understand.

As far as Noah knew, the strongest First Infernal Lord was at the Nebula Realm, along with the Founders that stood atop the Celestials!

But this was only what his knowledge led him to, he could always be wrong. He stopped dwelling in his thoughts as he continued to listen to the Grandmaster before him.

"Celestials at the World Rank or higher are held with great esteem, being one of the powerful weapons that could even match against the weakest Infernal Lord if multiple of them work together. Thus, any treasure that can possibly help a Celestial step towards the World Rank is treated with utmost importance!"

"Such a treasure...does exist in our hands. It is something that is completely out of my reach, and will only be given to the most promising Special Existence or a Celestial holding the title of Noble Fate. This treasure is something that we found in the southern region of the Spiritual Land where Fate Behemoths are abundant, a treasure that I only know by the name- Luminescent Rainbow Dew!"

The eyes of the listening students shone as a treasure was mentioned, something that even Grandmaster Vredral himself could not get his hands on.

"You two have the chance of obtaining this Luminescent Rainbow Dew, but only if you can prove that your potential and power is greater and more deserving than the other beings at a similar standing as you. The way to do this is simply...through battle. In order for you all to experience a rapid rise in strength similar to the past few weeks and enter a Realm that will grant you the power to make a difference in this war, you will have to fight for it."

Noah thought of many possibilities as the abundant treasures of the Celestials continued to help him greatly, and this treasure that was being hyped up by the Grandmaster was one that could possibly bring him to yet another stage that would allow him to catch up to some of the monsters he was facing.

"Who do we expect to be facing, Grandmaster?"

Noah replied with the conviction that seemed to say they would do whatever it took to increase their power, the Grandmaster nodding with a received smile as he continued.

"In a soon to be specified location, a Battle of Geniuses will be held where those joining will be faced with beings holding Noble Fates and Special Existences that have immensely powerful innate abilities. Coming out on top of this tournament will grant you some of the most valuable treasures that we have collected over the last thousand years!"

A resounding reply rang out in the training hall they were in as they received the information on just how they could advance their strength further, Athena turning to look towards her Junior Brother with a determined look as the both of them nodded towards each other, turning their gazes to the Grandmaster as they replied.

"We will not let you down, Grandmaster!"

Noah continued to play the part of a genius disciple well as even more opportunities landed on his lap.
