Chapter 4025: Do Nothing! I

Noah made his way toward the indicated plot, his movement careful and deliberate. He was acutely aware of being observed with the kind of attention given to children who had wandered into an adult workspace. The other Fold Dwellers didnt stop their work, but he could feel their ancient gazes tracking his progress.

The Seventh Contemplation Grove rose before him like a monument to impossible agriculture.

The trees here were different from the stellar giants elsewhere...more refined, more purposeful. Their trunks were white as fresh paper, inscribed with patterns that might have been words if words had been invented yet.

Fruits hung from their branches like condensed thoughts. Each one was perfectly spherical, about the size of his fist, and contained within their translucent skin swirled galaxies of compressed potential.

Noah reached out to touch one, and information flooded his consciousness.

Noahs hand pulsed slightly as he plucked the fruit. It came away from the branch with a sound like a perfectly resolved musical note. The weight in his palm was negligible physically but profound conceptually...he was holding the crystallized meditation of someone who had spoken directly with THE Living Spirit!

He bit into it without hesitation.

The taste was indescribable as it was simple in a way that nothing in modern existence could achieve. It tasted like the moment before decision, like the space between heartbeats, like the pause before understanding dawns.

He looked at the tree before him with new understanding.

Another fruit found its way into his hand. Then another.

As Noah consumed his fourth fruit, feeling his complexity soar beyond anything he could have achieved in years of normal cultivation, a shadow fell across the grove.

The shadow that fell across the grove turned out to be something slightly new.

A curious Fold Dweller stood before him, her presence commanding attention through sheer physicality rather than any display of power.

She was burly, humanoid in general form but crowned with the head of a wolf.

Gray fur, streaked with silver that might have been age or might have been wisdom, covered her lupine features.

Her eyes, amber gold and ancient, regarded Noah with particular intensity.

"You are such a weak thing," she observed, her voice carrying a growl beneath words that somehow made them more genuine rather than threatening. "Yet you seem to be the least worried being here while others prepare for potential death."

It was true. All around them, Fold Dwellers who had existed since before time had proper measurement were taking defensive positions, their ancient faces showing fear. Yet Noah stood calmly, still holding his fourth fruit, juice running down his chin from where hed bitten into it.

He swallowed the piece hed been chewing, feeling the surge of complexity flow through him before responding. His voice emerged steady.

"Worrying," he said, meeting those amber eyes without flinching, "is the act of suffering twice. Once in imagination, once in reality...if the feared thing even comes to pass. It changes nothing except to steal from us the present moment, which is the only time we truly possess."

He took another bite of the fruit, the casual action emphasizing his point.

"I could spend these seconds fearing what might come, rehearsing deaths that may never arrive. Or I could use them to grow stronger, to experience what is rather than what might be. One should simply move forward while trying their best. The outcome will be what it is, but at least well meet it having used our time rather than wasted it."

The wolf-womans eyes blazed brighter, her muzzle pulling back in what might have been a grin if wolves could truly grin. "Good mentality!"

The approval rang genuine. "I am Silvara, Guardian of the Contemplation Groves for eight hundred cycles."

She tilted her massive head, studying him with renewed interest. "And who might you be, young thing who speaks old truths?"

"Noah," he replied simply, seeing no reason for elaborate titles in this place.

"Noah." She tested the name, rolling it around like tasting new fruit. "Since you are a bit weak, I can help you out by sharing some of my Authority."
