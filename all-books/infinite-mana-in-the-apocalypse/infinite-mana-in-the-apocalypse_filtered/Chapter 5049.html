Chapter 5047: Hadean! III

The transformation was unique in ways Noah hadnt anticipated.

His Organ of Civilization, which was becoming THE Hadean Organ of Civilization, kept moving across his body in patterns that defied prediction. From his head to his heart to his limbs and all extremities, the organ traveled as if searching for its proper home. He felt like a pinball machine, the transforming organ bouncing between locations while leaving trails of multicolored warmth wherever it passed.

He had to rise up from the golden sands.

The warmth of both the Pluvial Epoch of Existential Infinity and THE Hadean Emergence bathed his body on all sides, two Miniature Causes working in harmony to saturate his existence with authority hed helped create. The golden sands of the Early Veiled Shore around him had risen up and were floating like glimmers of golden stars, each grain suspended by forces responding to the transformation occurring within him.

Inside his body, as all of this took place, he continued to generate endless Quintessence Infiniforce.

All of it was a flood of sensations that he frankly had to say felt better than some of the grandest sensations hed experienced so far in existence.

The pleasure wasnt merely physical. It ran deeper, touching aspects of his foundations. Every moment of the transformation brought waves of something approaching bliss!

It even exceeded his euphoria during his spar with THE Creature.

That spar told him a great deal of things about existence itself. There was no need to say who was the victor of that spar, of course. After all, it was just a spar, right?

Right!

There was no need to declare a loser or a winner when both parties walked away with what they wanted!

What he did learn was that THE Creatures power was unique beyond measure. Even though their confrontation occurred eons in the past from his perspective, THE Creature had felt just as strong if not stronger than the current version Noah had talked to in Alfheimr. That ancient monster had been playing with Adversity for longer than Noah could properly calculate, experimenting with concepts that most beings couldnt comprehend.

But after fighting against him, Noah could effectively say that in all of existence, there likely wasnt a single entity at THE First Scale who could possibly push him around.

If there was a competition for who the strongest entity below THE Proterozoic Scale was, he was fairly confident hed be taking that title. With his control over Infinity externally, and now his control over Quintessence Infiniforce internally, paired with the budding and terrifying force of THE Infiniverse, the only thing he was currently looking forward to was punching upward toward entities on an entirely different Scale.

It felt like the change of the Organ of Civilization would aid with that endeavor.

HUUM!

As he was thinking this, he gained a profound new sense from the organ.

It felt like an extension of his being in ways his previous Organ of Civilization never had. Something within it had shifted, gaining an invisible force that pressed against his awareness with intensity that demanded acknowledgment. At this moment, he felt it begin to shine with multicolored crystalline light that pushed outward from within his chest.

The organ had stopped at the very center of his chest.

It had found its home.

The moment it completed its transformation...

BOOM!

Noahs figure rose into the air involuntarily, lifted by forces responding to what had just emerged within him. The golden sand that had been floating around him began to spin in configurations that placed him at the absolute center, each grain orbiting his form as if he was the core of a galaxy being born. Light poured from his chest in streams of multicolored brilliance that painted the Early Veiled Shore in hues existence rarely witnessed.

As utmost glory spun around him, his chest began to shine even brighter.

From it began to rise a weapon.

A multicolored resplendent crystalline spear emerged from his sternum, pushing through flesh that parted without pain, materializing into physical form as it left his body. The weapon was a Kontos spear, its shaft stretching longer than cavalry lances, designed for reach and devastating strikes from positions of superiority. The crystalline material comprising it shifted through every color contained within Quintessence Infiniforce, never settling on a single hue, constantly flowing through the spectrum in patterns.

The spearhead itself was leaf-shaped and broad, edges sharp enough that looking at them seemed to cut perception. Runes he didnt recognize covered the shaft in spiraling configurations, each one pulsing with light that matched the rhythm of his heartbeat. The butt of the spear ended in a crystalline counterweight that balanced the weapon perfectly despite its impossible length.

After it emerged fully and floated above Noahs chest, he raised his hand to grasp it.

Everything around him began to tremble.

The Early Veiled Shore shook. The Three Thousand Realms beyond shook. THE Infiniverse itself seemed to recognize what had just been born within its creators existence. Noahs fingers closed around the shaft of the spear, and the moment contact was made, prompts bloomed before his vision.

He could sense what was happening inside of it now, perceive aspects of his internal existence that had been hidden before the transformation. There were even grander changes occurring within, configurations that the spears emergence had only begun to reveal.

THE Infinite Desmoterion.
