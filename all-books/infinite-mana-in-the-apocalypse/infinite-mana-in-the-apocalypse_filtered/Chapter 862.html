Chapter 860 - A Sage's Sacrifice II

Crimson red swords shot forth like missiles towards the Sword Emperor, each of them screaming resonantly as they would not stop until they smashed into him!

Wherever he teleported, wherever he disappeared to...the crimson Cardinal Swords of Ruination would be there!

BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!

Three of them exploded out in succession as the sharp auras of the Daos of Withering and Sword were bearable, but the essence of Ruination was deadly.

It was a much higher tier essence that even while just being at the stage of comprehension, Noah could command this essence to burn through the defenses of the Sword Emperors fully assimilated sword essence!

"Good!"

The vibrant shout of the Sage emanated out while he was in sword form, the coalescing of sword essence occurring as he remained in one spot and allowed all the chasing Cardinal Swords of Ruination to smash into him.

BZZZZT!

Space quaked and trembled as when the scene cleared, it showed ravaged sword essence as the bright blue sword that represented the Sage...had lost some of its l.u.s.ter!

Yet when the Sage expected recompense, he found out to his stupor that multiple more Cardinal Swords of Ruination were already heading his way. Noah had not stopped casting them for a single second as they seemed like homing missiles constantly rushing towards the Sword Emperor.

At the same time, the roar of an emotional Tiamat rang out as an enormous door appeared in the skies, this door opening rapidly to show the illusory form of a terrifying dragon head!

Its jaws opened as from them, purple red flames erupted out that filled the skies. The other summons moved with vigor while enhanced by Ruination, Jormungandr opening its jaws wide as the illusory form of a Galaxy Devouring Serpent appeared behind him- its terrifying force temporarily holding the Sage in place.

The purple red flames in the skies were joined by scorching golden flames, the Golden Crow looking at the Sage domineeringly as it released its fury!

All the other pets moved with vigor as the Sage was besieged on all sides, the homing missiles that were the Cardinal Sword of Ruination continuing to smash towards him and dealing constant damage.

But in the midst of all this...the Sage laughed!

"Haha! Very good! With this, there is a chance!"

As he felt the terrific power of this being that now had the strength of a beginner Sage even though he had not yet become one, the Sword Emperor felt like his Masters words were close to fruition.

He knew that this being grew ever stronger from devouring the strength of others, and he attributed the current rapid rise in Noahs power to be due to the Sword Kings he had been fed in the prior floors of the Immemorial Sword Formation.

He actually wasnt too far off as this was actually the truth!

But the Sword Emperor knew that for the strength of this being to be even more stupendous, he had to be the last sacrifice!

Even at this point while he was besieged on all sides, he had a variety of ways with the Strength of a Sage to erupt with deadly strength and reap the lives of Noahs pets or even possibly Noah himself, using his flames of life to choose mutual destruction.

But he didnt do so as after experiencing the shocking strength and taking on the Cardinal Swords of Ruination that caused tiny cracks in his sword form, he finally stayed still as he was ready to make the ultimate sacrifice!

He knew that his Origin would be preserved due to the Immemorial Sword Formation, that he wouldnt truly due. But still! He would be losing a large majority of his power as for the events to come...he would rest them on the hands of this being!

Capable of comprehending a Cosmic Dao while not even being a Sage yet, how can I compare?

BOOM! BOOOM!

Cardinal Swords of Ruination continued to smash into the sword form of the Sword Emperor as the attacks of the pets threatened to tear apart his strong layers of defenses unsuccessfully. His figure was like a lone sword swimming amongst the waves of destruction, peacefully passing through unhindered!

It was a truly wondrous scene as in the next moment, a Cardinal Sword of Ruination smashed into the Sage and threw him towards a certain direction.

It was a fold in space where a Blue Slime had been waiting patiently for the entirety of the battle.

WAA!

Its magisterial essence was released at this moment as its body ballooned to cover up the surrounding 20 meters instantly.

Hmm?

The Sage had a smile on his face as a brilliant blue light shone, his sword form evaporating as he turned back to his devilishly handsome 4 meter tall body the instant before an Absolute Skill was activated.

When he returned to his body, his serene eyes gazed towards Noahs main body far off in the distance as they seemed to give him a singular message.

I let you defeat me!

It was the pride of a Sage that did not wish to fight to the death, where even while using his life and power to further his goals, he still let it be known that he was truly powerful!

<<Universal Devouring>>.

WAP!

The devilishly handsome figure of the Sword Emperor...then vanished.

"..."

It was the sacrifice of a Sage.

It was a selfless action that would be the reason for something wondrous to bloom, for the rise of a shocking being that would etch his name in the annals of history!
