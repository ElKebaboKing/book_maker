Chapter 1439: The Royal Blight Serpent Dragon- Yacucoatl! III

Information that nobody should know about the Royal Blight Serpent Dragon Yacucoatl was revealed by Noah as he caused shock and wariness to reverberate across the Origin of this powerful being!

How could Noah know?! The memories of Cecilia were abundant and told him of the history and information about many races, Bloodlines, and Realities as some of these memories naturally contained information on the Bloodline of Royal Blight Serpent Dragons. But it didn't have anything about the identity of this being before Noah's eyes, nor about the Edicts he had and when he had forged them.

The answer was of course, through the Authority of the Dictum Emperor!

Even more so, it was apt to say it was through the Ascendancy Halo of Catastrophe rotating around Noah at this moment!

He had quantified yet another one of just uses as he found that the ultimate skills given to him by the Flames of the Nomological Edict could passively be activated with the Ascendancy Halo, their cost being entirely minimal- especially if they required the essence of Reality.

The moment that Noah neared Pyxis and the Royal Blight Serpent Dragon Yacucoatl- Author's Viewpoint was activated as the status sheets of these powerful existences appeared before Noah's eyes.

The status sheet of Pyxis was naturally interesting, but Yacucoatl...that was something else entirely!

Noah's figure floated forward as he perused through it while continuing to speak.

"You spent over 10 million years in the Primordial Battlefields to forge yourself, managing to gain something in the end as you forged your Insuperable Edict of the Virulent Poison Emperor...granting you essence that can poison even other concepts and Nomological truths, much less existences!"

WAA!

These words instantly caused the wariness in Yacucoatl's eyes to increase, his body showing hints of hesitation before the royal blood within him churned as he spoke out with power.

"You...you are one of those Seers?"

Seers!

Noah heard this term as he couldn't help but smile, being familiar with it now due to Cecilia's memories as it was a term given to unique individuals across the vast Realities that had the talent of forging Nomological Edicts that allowed them to peer into unknown truths and tear apart the veils of secrets!

Such a task was extremely hard to do for most beings as it delved into the workings of destiny...and yet Noah had done it so easily!

Coming across Seers was extremely rare as even if one managed to form an Edict that could peer into the veils of secrecy, it would at most be a minor or Greater Edict, or at most at the Quasi-Insuperable level. To be considered a genuine Seer, one needed to have forged an Insuperable Edict that allowed them to peer through the veils of secrecy! Defending against such beings from obtaining information about you was hard as you needed an opposing Edict of a similar concept to prevent them from seeing through you- or you had to have an Edict of a Higher level that even if it wasn't one that a Seer would have, it's authority would still prevent others from piercing through one's veil of defenses.

Yacucoatl had such an Edict as he forged the Insuperable Edict of the Virulent Poison Emperor, and the authority of this Edict should have prevented a Quasi-Insuperable Nomological Edict that a Seer had as the only way this being bypassed him...is if he held an Edict of a Seer at the INSUPERABLE level.

WAA!

This was why Yacucoatl turned careful.

Yet...

He was wrong as Noah didn't have an Insuperable Edict that a powerful Seer would have, but he did have the authority of another Edict and an Ascendancy Halo that had not even been categorized! He layered the authority of the Resplendent Treasure Emperor and the Quasi-Insuperable Nomological Edict of the Dictum Emperor as with his unique Ascendancy Halo of Catastrophe...he tore across the protection of another being's Insuperable Edict and obtained all the information about them!

"A Seer or not...it does not matter. I know all of this about you and I dared to be here right now. What do you think that means, O Royal Blight Serpent Dragon Prince?"

RUMBLE!

With unmatched tyranny and dominance, Noah walked forward with his body brimming with boundless strength and power.

"Daring!"

WAA!

The deep voice of the Royal Blight Serpent Dragon echoed out as his body began to glimmer with incandescent light, but one could see his oppressive might had lost to the being made of Cosmos before his eyes!

There were many stories about Seers.

Yacucoatl had his confidence shaken by such a being as an instant later...

THRUM!

His three Ascendancy Halos shone and receded into his body as stupendously, he fused with all of them right away to enter his true form and battle with everything he had!

The surroundings tumbled out of order as the enormous body of the coiling Serpent Dragon underwent a change, multiple pairs of wings sprouting from its scaly back as eight more heads colored green and black protruded outwards.

A sea of deadly poison bloomed around its body as anything that even came near would suffer, and this virulent green sea actually continued to expand wildly as it threatened to cover everything in the surroundings!

It seemed like an ancient Serpent Dragon of the primal ages was being born on a vastly expanding sea, even Pyxis and Hyperion from afar moving their battle even further not to come in contact with this sea!

The new 9 heads of the Royal Blight Serpent Dragon Yacucoatl stared coldly towards Noah as they screamed of ferocity and regality.

"The Virulent Poison Sea backed up by my fusion of Ascendancy Halos, Insuperable Edict, and bloodline will make it hard for Peak First Firmament existences to even stay within it for a few seconds...much less come near me! Give up your foolish aims and die...no matter if you are a seer!"

WAAA!

Renewed confidence rose within Yacucoatl as he watched the sizzling green sea quickly cover the area that Noah was at...but this being actually did nothing as the sea washed over his body and covered him completely.

Yet, the expected scene of pain and horror did not occur.

"...???"

The 9 heads of the Royal Blight Serpent Dragon squinted in a deadly fashion as they watched with extra eyes the figure of Noah simply raise his hands to play with the poisonous sea that would normally be melting the body and Origin of any existence in the First Firmament of Ascendancy in a matter of seconds!

Yet...boundless life-force seemed to emanate from the human within the Virulent Poison Sea as at this moment, an Edict he forged not too long ago fully showed its oppressive might as it covered him head to toe.

Noah had not been able to even display the full might of many of his Quasi-Insuperable Edicts because the enemies simply weren't powerful enough to allow for it. Now, his body bloomed with green-gold light as immense life force surged from him as while a Virulent Poison Sea forged from a fusion of three Ascendancy Halos, an Insuperable Edict, and a unique bloodline of a Royal Blight Serpent Dragon tried to break down his defenses...the domineering Edict of Antediluvian rose up with vengeance!

Antediluvian!

The Edict that granted him the Bloodline of a Primal Titan and the abilities of <Unshaken>, <Physique of a Titan>, <Primal Authority>, and <Antediluvian Tree>...this Edict was not to be looked down upon by itself as now that Noah had fused it into a stupendous Ascendancy Halo...even a deadly attack that could melt Peak First Firmament experts dealt absolute no damage to him!
