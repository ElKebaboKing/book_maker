Chapter 361 - Fantastical!

The last words of the Grandmaster smashed into Noah powerfully as his thoughts rapidly moved, the meaning and possibilities behind these words being so immense that he could not even imagine!

In the last thousands of years as the Celestials expanded their power while exploring this Celestial Realm, they had only explored a measly 10% of the entire Realm!

In this area they had explored though, they were able to come across ridiculously powerful treasures that could destroy stars, as well as obtain techniques that gave them the prodigious positions that they held in the Light Expanse today.

Noahs heart was shaking as his eyes looked forward into the unlimited possibilities of the future.

If...just if he was given access to this realm that even the Celestials had not fully explored...just what would he be able to pull out when the Celestials already received so much from just covering 10% of it?

"There are still numerous secrets to uncover when it comes to the Celestial Realm, but the one thing we have confirmed is that the rewards are abundant. This was also the reason why knowledge of it has been kept very scarce, the privilege of exploring it given only to powerful Celestials or disciples that show overwhelming potential over the years."

The solemn gaze of the Grandmaster fell on the figures of Noah and Athena at this moment.

"This is where you two come in! In the near future, you will be exploring this vast realm as you learn to grow your power even faster. With your status as those who hold a Noble Fate, who knows what you might discover, and if it would come close to the level of things that we have discovered over the years, or something out of our wildest dreams!"

The eyes of the Grandmaster were shining as he looked at them, Noah reading between the lines as he realized that the Celestials did not just permit them entrance into the Celestial Realm for the purpose of rapidly increasing their strength, but also to use their titles as those with Noble Fate to see if they can chance upon anything at the level of the treasures they have come across over the last thousands of years.

The deep thoughts behind the actions of the Celestials made Noah more careful as the Grandmaster continued to speak.

"Although this time it is going to be different. As I oversaw the mission in the Magus World and observed the changes in the workings of fate in real time, an idea struck me that I have passed on. We are now just awaiting confirmation but for the near future, for the first time in history- we should be opening the doors of the Celestial Realm to select individuals that do not hold the identities of Celestials!"

His words carried an unknown meaning as he did not fully delve into what idea he had proposed and passed on, but Noah knew something huge would be occurring in the Light Expanse in the very near future.

At the center of all of it were the Celestials, and now, he had stably instituted himself in their ranks as he would be given access to a fantastical Realm he never knew existed, to the realm where the immense power of the Celestials came from!

He hid his deep smile as he continued to listen closely to the solemn Grandmaster, his eyes set on the future that seemed to be calling out to him brightly.
