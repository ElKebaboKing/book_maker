Chapter 3238 Origins II

A thread connecting to another Outerversal Morphon!

Their body was smaller than most as they had the form of a rotund creature with stellar fur and limbs, and it wasn't the only one connected to Aspasia as when Noah observed other lines extending from her, they stretched out towards the vast stretches of the Megalos Beyond itself.

Of course. An Outerversal Morphon could also bind those within the Megalos Beyond.

Just like they could reach out to weavings of Matter, they could also connect to the weavings of Dark Matter similar to them!

But the former seemed to be much easier.

'Hmm...'

Noah had a pondering expression as he wanted to know more.

So he sensed the resonance of the Existence he was connected to and delved even deeper.

...!

It was an action that caused a strong reaction, the visage of Aspasia below him radiating a blinding brilliance of obsidian light that caused half of his consciousness to be taken somewhere else entirely.

And when this consciousness opened its eyes, it found itself waking up above a calm obsidian body of water that stretched out with no end.

His reflection could be seen on it as he rose, his bare body shining with obsidian radiance reflecting back along with his powerful wings and nine Halos spinning behind him.

Even though it shone with stellar radiance, the muscular humanoid form could still be seen on its curves as his eyes, which were vibrant singularities, looked ahead of him to see the bare figure of Aspasia that seemed to be trembling slightly as she acclimated.

Her stellar obsidian skin outlines a unique blessed chest different in its finer details than what Ozymandias was normally exposed to, with her slender waist pulsing with stellar radiance as in the next moment, she waved her hands to cause a stellar obsidian robe to wrap around her figure.

She looked towards Ozymandias with a sense of modesty while breathing out calmly.

|You went a bit overboard there, but at least it brought us to this space. To the Nexus of Megalos Records.|

WAA!

|The Nexus of Megalos Records?| Ozymandias looked around them while he asked lightly.

Apart from the obsidian body of water stretching out with no end, there was only a similar hue of obsidian stellar space above them akin to the Megalos Beyond itself as it didn't seem all that different.

Aspasia nodded lightly while gazing at Ozymandias' blessed visage and the tool he had used to allow them to achieve this profound state of resonance they were currently in- her hand rising and tapping towards him as an obsidian robe came to adorn his body as well.

|Yes. It is moreso a Nexus between you and I, but the Nexus of Megalos Records exists for all Outerversal Morphons out there with a high level of power. It is a place to obtain knowledge and spread knowledge. Currently, we are in a Nexus that most closely resonates with something at the depths of my being. Something relating to the Great Beyonder. It is here that I will be able to rely on his weavings to come to any confirmation about who you are.|

|I see...|

Ozymandias scratched his stellar chin as he wondered, was he about to get exposed here? He could already do everything that these entities believed as he passed this Aspasia before him, everything would be set in stone.

He looked at her figure as he asked.

|Just who is the Great Beyonder?|

A name that rose ever since his entry into the Megalos Beyond!

A name with tremendous influence as its words were believed with damn near certainty.

Aspasia looked at him with analytical eyes as she waved her hands above and caused the stellar space to change- the outline of a blurry figure appearing as his sheer outline alone caused the space above to tremble.

|The Great Beyonder is one of the Oldest Outerversal Morphons known.|

...!

|Is?|

Aspasia spoke in the present tense.

And she nodded in affirmation as she continued.

|Is. He has disappeared for many cycles now, but he is not dead. We may be in a place called the Grave of the Great Beyonder, but this is simply a distinction and a place to honor him that Outerversal Morphons converge in. There are other places like this stretched out across the Megalos Beyond. Think of them like Shrines of the Great Beyonder as we await his return. If he chooses to return.|

An Entity surrounded by tremendous mystery.

Noah thought about his standing deeply as he probed.

|From the weavings I've heard, he disappeared after he made the revelations that put us where we are now. About Signs and an Instrument that would either expand or annihilate the Megalos Beyond. Why would he...disappear?|

A question seeking to get to the crux of the beliefs of the Outerversal Morphons.

And Aspasia took it in strides as she began to walk on the calm black Sea towards him while replying.

|Heretical Morphons like to believe that he paid a great cost to see the possible end or expansion of the Megalos Beyond. That his weavings unraveled and he spread out across the Megalos Beyond- with some areas converging clusters of his weavings as these became known as the Graves of the Great Beyonder.|

She arrived in front of him as her radiant obsidian skin pulsed with pure glory, her palm rising in front of him as Ozymandias raised his own hand to reflect his stellar palm.

|We believe something else entirely. We believe the Great Beyonder achieved a level of Existence that exceeded all of us so much that we cannot even perceive him anymore unless he wishes for us to. A different level of dimensionality entirely. To him, time moves differently. Space moves differently. For him to have been capable of seeing the possible expansion or destruction of the Megalos Beyond...he had to have transcended any common concepts we understand and seen things we cannot see. We very much believe that the Great Beyonder is right here.|
