Chapter 828 - Surrender in not an option!

THRUUUM!

Outside of the Illusory Unbreakable Realm, silence reigned in the expansive chaotic void.

Even though many experts were hidden in the folds of space while watching the shocking battle, not a single vibration of sound could be heard!

Many of those watching were Entities from powerful Ancient Powers, and even they had solemn expressions as they watched Noah dispatch 2 Entities in a matter of seconds, only the more powerful Primal Emperor escaping his grasp.

Their solemn eyes were because they felt like they were facing a great enemy, the power that this single being showed being something they had not expected to see when they came to check out the war between the Expansive Dragon Cl.u.s.ter and the Primal Winged Expanse!

But among those watching, there were few that were only observing with cold eyes as a myriad of thoughts crossed their minds.

Sister Gold, the stupendous being from the fearsome Spirit Race, was among these Entities.

Ever since she had asked the question of whether they would treat this tyrannical Entity in front of them as an enemy or ally, the voices in her mind had quieted down!

This was because they knew the full weight of this decision after observing the power of this being along with the high possibility that he was the one that was connected to the Ruination Cores.

Thus, they calculated greatly before they even brought forth a thought.

The silence did not last too long, as an aged voice rolled out extremely slowly in the mind of Sister Gold.

"Strength is king."

RUMBLE!

"Enemy or ally, it does not matter. It only matters whether our goals can be achieved. If he furthers our goals he can be an ally, if he stands against us...there shall only be destruction."

THRUUUM!

A shocking statement was uttered in Sister Golds mind as her eyes shone brightly.

The being that spoke was someone extremely special among the Spirit Race, one of the beings that they termed Ancestor!

Even after watching and analyzing the horrendous strength that Noah displayed as he matched numerous Entities, he had still uttered a statement that made it seem that if they were to become enemies...it wasnt them that would perish.

Even as they saw how he butchered Entities in the Illusory Unbreakable Realm.

Even as they saw his seemingly inexhaustible mana reserves or the terrific Animus Summons that were no less powerful than him!

Those from the Spirit Race were confident!

"Use your body as the conduit to create a doorway. We shall reinforce you just in case."

"...Yes, Ancestor."

...!

Even though Sister Gold was a terrifying being that was studying and assimilating many Daos, someone who looked down on the Entities studying a single Lesser Dao that Noah was butchering right now...even she was being sent reinforcements just in case!

Those of the Spirit Race did not take risks unless they knew they would win!

Essence silently erupted in a corner of the chaotic void near the Illusory Unbreakable Realm, no other Entities being any wiser on what was to come...

Back in the Illusory Unbreakable Realm, over half an hour had passed as the realm only had 30 minutes remaining.

This 30 minutes...felt extremely grueling to the Aileron Entities as with every passing few minutes, another Entity would fall!

From the side of the Colossal Helios Leviathan and the fearsome Jormungandr, only 2 Entities remained as 3 were taken down.

The Golden Crow and Calamity Bear were left with a single Entity that had a fearful look on his face as his defenses were slowly ground down!

Where the main force of 15 Entities used to be around the Primal Emperor who faced the most terrifying Blue Slime, Tiamat, and Noah...only 5 Entities remained alive including the bloodied and battered Primal Emperor.

The results of this battle were extremely shocking to look at as time passed!

Many considered if it was a show, the unbelievable sight of Entity after Entity falling from the hands of a single being and his Animus Summons being something purely out of fantasy.

The face of the Primal Emperor remained cold and wrathful, nobody being able to understand the thoughts coursing through his mind as he pushed his body further and knocked away the vibrantly shining figure of a winged Noah that swirled with the essence of all laws!

WAA!

Every attack or skill coming from the shining being in front of him felt like a hammer of death smashing towards him, the damage they dealt being inconceivable.

The +10,000% All Damage increase from the Ruination Core was something overpowered as it felt like a train smashing into the body of a defenseless being every time!

With his body bleeding profusely on all sides, the Primal Emperor kept his cold gaze as his remaining wings shimmered, streaking towards Noah once more as he attacked.

Throughout all this time, he never uttered any words of weakness or called for the battle to stop while claiming misunderstanding like others in a deadly situation like this would do.

The remaining Aileron Entities looked at this scene in despair as they finally came to the conclusion that no matter what happened today, the Primal Emperor would not stand down!

Some felt emotional as they fought faster, while some had looks of hesitation as they felt like their lives could be taken at any second from the ridiculously powerful pets that seemed to have boundless strength.

The remaining Entity facing the the Oculothorax and Obsidian Panther could not hold on any longer as he evaded the rays of [Eye of Doom], his expression ashen as he called out!

"I surrender!"

THRUUUM!

The cry wasnt particularly loud, but it reverberated throughout the Illusory Unbreakable Realm and the surrounding chaotic void.

"..."

Those that had it had somber expressions as they could understand the reason why!

The Aileron Entities hearing it were angered as they looked towards their struggling brethren with faces of betrayal, but the beings pitiful cry only rang out again, this time with more determination.

"I surrender!"

...!

Ah...

Many shook their heads at this scene as the shockingly golden and white winged eye that was the Oculothorax stopped in front of the Entity in his last seconds of life.

Off in the distance where Noah continued to clash with the Primal Emperor, his bright and oppressive voice resounded out for all to hear.

"Surrender is not an option that I can offer."

RUMBLE!

With his body flashing with innumerable lights from the essence of all laws and dao essence, Noah looked like a cruel archaic being as his bright eyes brought down judgment.

"I shall offer you all salvation...as my loot!"
