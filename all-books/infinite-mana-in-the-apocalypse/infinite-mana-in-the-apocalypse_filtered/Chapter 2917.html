2915 The Fruit of Existence III

Translucent.

Radiant.

The glow was initially blinding and covering everything, only to slowly start dying down to reveal the source that had begun to cause the surrounding weaving to pulse with a sense of excitement.

The Dowager of Quintessence gazed at it with surprise as Noah, Ruination, and the Manifestation of the Infinite Hyperversal Haven had sharp eyes!

The Fruit held an ever-present glow as its texture was closely resembling that of an apple.

It let out a translucent glow, with light passing through it, and yet at the same time...one felt like they could see their reflection in this fruit as well!

Its appearance was unique as the moment it came to, it radiated a pull that all four entities here felt.

It pulled on Noah's and his mother's soul.

It pulled on Ruination...and it even radiated an undeniable pull towards the Manifestation of the Infinite Hyperversal Haven!

[A Quintessential Achievement!]

HUUM!

Waves of glory seethed around Noah as his will went on to wrap around this dazzling fruit.

He had to know.

He had to grasp exactly why this object before him was at the center of why the Legacies of Al-Abalem and Ignatius were present in the Vitalis and Vacuous Hyperversal Domains!

Like a hungry tide, his soul cascaded down on the translucent wonder.

[The object before you is something far and above your current grasp, and yet you have attained it.]

[To understand it truly, one needs to have scaled the nine Gradations of Extremity and achieved a special weaving of their authority!]

...!

Yes.

It was something far out of his grasp. But since it was before him...

HUUM!

Vermillion flames burned as he reached out.

Like a tyranny moving to hold something pristine only for himself, he reached out and came to hold this glorious translucent fruit in his hands.

[The Weaver of Extremity stares at the Fruit of Existence.]

[The Weavings of the Fruit of Existence begin to speak to you!]

...!

Brilliance unfolded as Noah used a large amount of his Will and Existential Extremity Authorities to understand what he held in his hands.

And...

[A Boon of the Fruit of Existence is gradually becoming clear to you.]

Information began to flow.

Weavings began to flow as Noah uncovered a single usage of the Fruit of Existence so far!

[Once devoured, the authority within the Fruit of Existence will reduce and reverse the deterioration of one's existence, going even further to expand the breadth of one's existence over time.]

...!

Noah's eyes opened wide.

The latter part of the prompt that spoke of the expansion of the breadth of existence was definitely terrifying enough, but Noah's pulsing will couldn't help but be shaken by the first part of the prompt.

As one lived, one aged. All things fell to states of more and more disorder.

The Fruit of Existence...reversed disorder.

The deterioration of one's existence was reversed as Noah could feel that these words alone were not this simple!

The effect of such words...should be something utterly maddening as they would affect everything about the one who devoured this fruit!

But before he could even focus on this...

[Another Boon of the Fruit of Existence is gradually becoming clear to you.]

[Once devoured, the authority within the Fruit of Existence can be directed towards a chosen concept within one's existence. The light of the Fruit of Existence can bathe this concept to cultivate it towards the Existential level, and go further to increase the authority of an Existential Authority.]

....!

[Another Boon of the Fruit of Existence is gradually becoming clear to you.]

[Those with means and capabilities of advanced utilization can take the authority of the Fruit of Existence and allow it to nurture selected Records or Domains. This Existential Nurturing can allow a selected Existential Extreme to be bound to a Record or Domain, with further elevations allowing the chosen domain and extreme to grow into a construct of Infinite possibilities. When the purity and refinement of an extreme bound to a record or domain reaches a critical level, it may even be capable of generating wisps of the authority of the Fruit of Existence that once empowered it.]

...!

The weavings around Noah came to a halt.

Vermillion flames burned brightly around him as he stared with burning eyes towards the Fruit of Existence in his hands!

He had only barely managed to unearth three uses, and each one was more maddening than the last as this most recent prompt...it held too many implications.

The Fruit of Existence could be used to nurture domains themselves.

It could be utilized to nurture the Infinite Hyperversal Haven.

It could be utilized to nurture Noah himself.

And it could be utilized to nurture concepts!

But the most astounding thing was the nourishment of a domain and binding of an extreme to it as it meant the Legacies of Al-Abalem and Ignatius strengthened Vacuous and Vitalis- bringing them to such a shocking extent as along with the Seeds refining these very same authorities within...they were able to extract Fruits of Existence.

It didn't seem like there was a limit.

There weren't too many details Noah had uncovered as there still seemed to be many mysteries!

And even after unveiling three uses, Noah knew that he was only scratching the surface as under his gaze, he couldn't see everything right now.

To the extent that maybe, the best way to uncover any more weavings...was to devour this Fruit of Existence before him and observe the changes within his existence!

His very existence was crying out for it as his Extremes had already expressed their intent.

Every single part of him wanted this unimaginable treasure as after arriving at such a juncture, Noah breathed out and controlled himself.

The first entity within the Infinite Hyperversal Haven to get to enjoy the unfathomable wonders of the Fruit of Existence had to be a special one.

So Noah held the fruit within his hand and turned to gaze towards an entity ahead of him who was looking at everything with curiosity.

"Mother..."

He called out to the one who went through tremendous adversity for him in his early days.

He offered the honor of the first Fruit of Existence converged from the Loot of the Existential Apokalypsis Beasts...to a single person whom he could never repay!
