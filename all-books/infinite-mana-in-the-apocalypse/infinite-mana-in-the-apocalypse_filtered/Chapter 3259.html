Chapter 3257 The One Who Walks! I

The One Who Walks.

It was a unique distinction to hear, and the entity who stated it could not be taken lightly as his form was grandiose.

A massive purple skull that cried out utmost glory, with thousands of limbs protruding from behind this skull!

Dazzling purple flames burned in its sockets as around it, debris of decimated Existential Domains lay.

They pulsed weakly with an obsidian light of Corruption as behind this skull, a swirling mass of obsidian light that seemed to be absorbing everything for many light years stood.

The glimpse of the Dark Matter Core.

Command Protector Valeria looked at it all with a calm gaze as her body swirled with the grandeur of a Fabled Omniverse.

She stared deeply into the eyes burning with purple flames as she voiced out.

|I wouldn't aim at any part of you if you just stayed away from our Lands. Must you all be here?|

Her voice held no fear.

She had built up her existence to such an unfathomable extent that even though she knew the enemy before her was a terrible one, this was just one of his bodies.

She could handle a single body.

The purple flames within The One Who Walks sneered as its wide jaws crackled.

|How can we not be here, puny thing? Your Omniverses are akin to untapped fruits waiting to be plucked. This one is almost ripe as your interruption is very much unneeded.|

...!

His voice held unmatched confidence and might.

And yet Command Protector Valeria was undaunted as from behind her, an illusory cerulean wheel rose to her left, and a crimson wheel rose to her right.

They both pulsed with intricate interweavings of lines between them as they emanated ecstatic waves of power.

They released a potent glow of existence that covered her body with celestial auroras of red and blue radiance that easily kept the obsidian radiance of Outerversal Authority at bay!

The purple flames within the socket of Savitar flared at this scene as his booming voice echoed out once more.

|Do you know why they call me the One Who Walks?|

He asked.

And before him, Command Protector Valeria scoffed with a reply.

|Many of your bodies within Tainted Omniverses have asked this question. You seem to really like this accolade.|

|Kaka...!| Her reply caused booming cackling laughter from Savitar as his many limbs clapped and made horrific sounds.

|Yes! If you have heard of me from those who managed to survive my assault, then you may know. But first and foremost...the One Who Walks makes it so that others...will no longer be able to do so.|

WAA!

Obsidian radiance flashed immeasurably quickly.

It covered the whole Half Tainted Omniverse in an instant as wondrously, invisible black lines stretched out and covered everything.

Lines pulsing with Dark Matter that imposed an undeniable Authority.

|Of all those here, none will be able to walk away, O Peon of the Commonwealth. I do not sense from you the quality of existence my other bodies feel from those above-average Peons I have faced before. As sad as it is for you, this is likely the end of your path. Do not worry...I will take from your body and change it to something far greater. This, I promise you.|

HUUUM!

From behind, Noah's figure decimated rows of Existence Reapers to see the glorious scene of shattered Domains of Existence and the figures of Outerversal Morphon Savitar and Command Protector Valeria.

He arrived just in time to hear the words of the One Who Walks as he also turned his flames towards him!

|Oh?|

Seeing the interminable destruction and ease he held, the flames of Savitar were aroused.

|You are...an oddity. You are with her and smell so foreign...and yet familiar at the same time. Have you also come to die?|

Words that could very easily be misconstrued echoed out as Noah arrived gloriously.

Existence Reapers who even neared him with their unfathomable speed exploded before they even neared, their brilliance being pulled towards his wings right after as he floated in with his hands behind his back!

Seeing him, Command Protector Valeria didn't even turn around as her will already captured everything, her intent surging towards him.

|I won't be able to protect you much, so you best have tremendous confidence to be here. If you have any designs or plans, it's best you share them now, otherwise, let me take care of it all.|

Just like the Outerversal Morphon, she blazed with confidence.

It didn't just stem from her Omniversal Stage of power.

It was something deeper.

Her System of Existence!

Noah could now feel it closely as within him, his Third Mundi of Power moved Innately.

Ingeniare was already breaking down the System of Existence that Lucretia utilized, and it was the same for Command Protector Valeria.

Noah smiled as he replied while floating closer.

|You don't worry about me and face him as you would. I will provide support to ensure his fall.|

He spoke confidently as prompts began to fall before his eyes.

|The System of Existence utilized by the core forces of the Commonwealth has been broken down and quantified.|

The System of Existence that the most prominent Entities of the Commonwealth followed.

|The System of Existence of Kuklos is being documented.|

Kuklos!

A word closely related to the weavings of the Wheel!

In the heart of the existence of all Protectors of the Commonwealth, there was a single similarity.

At the base of their Existence, there was the illusion of a Wheel.

|With the mimicking of the Wheel of Existence at their core, the Existence of the followers of the System of Existence of Kuklos face a refinement and elevation of existence at every turn of the Wheel. After their Existence has been raised to a sufficient level, they can utilize the weavings of the Wheel to bend Existence to their will with half the effort and twice the results.|

The dazzling blue and red wheels that appeared behind Command Protector Valeria caused her white skin to flicker with gem-like radiance.

Paired with the spinning Fabled Omniverse coming to life around her, she turned into a radiant beam of light that surged fearlessly toward Savitar!
