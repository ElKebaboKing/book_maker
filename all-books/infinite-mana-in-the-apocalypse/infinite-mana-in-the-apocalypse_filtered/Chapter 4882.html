A path to victory against Absolutes!

Not through matching their power directly, but through exploiting weaknesses that their power couldnt protect them from!

Your Infinities create a shroud of endless variation around your Manifestation, making it impossible for observers to perceive any single configuration long enough to identify weaknesses. What they see changes faster than they can process.|

So he could see himself as clearly as he could see others, but others couldnt see him in return.

His Infinities protected him from the very capability he now possessed, creating a one-way mirror where he could observe without being observed.

Unfocused attacks carry no structural targeting benefits. If you cannot even get near your enemy, even if you see their weakness, you will be unable to do anything.|
