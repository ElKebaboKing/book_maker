Chapter 386 - Grandiosity

A world shaking battle was taking place below a nondescript volcanic mountain that nobody was privy to!

A world of blood had expanded all around to hide anything that was happening within, while in the underground space of this mountain where the fight was taking place, a red moon hung at the highest point!

At this moment, the Draconic Fire Behemoth roared out as the very air trembled, its eyes shining as flames coagulated in front of it.

[Immolation Pillars].

RUMBLE!

A barely discernible whisper was heard. But this whisper was the command of something terrible as tens of blazing fiery pillars of flames appeared without reservation around the Dragon, each one heading towards the clone and the summoned forces.

SHAA! SHAA!

They streaked towards them menacingly as Noah put the golden shield on his left hand out, withstanding the fiery attacks as they exploded in a shower of sparks and flames. The summoned Abyssal Undead that felt no pain were struck head on, numerous parts of their bodies being set aflame as they still continued forward valiantly, the Abyssal Revenant shining the dark coffin it was carrying towards the Dragon as it opened to release a viscous cloud of poison and death! The Abyssal Jiangshi carried the deathly pillars in their hands as they weaved their large bodies forward and threw the pillars like spears, chains being seen attached to these pillars that they could pull back whenever they wished!

The eerie Ancients that were summoned were the most spectacular, their white eyes shining as the pillars of flames snaking towards them simply dissipated as they neared.

Thereafter, their gazes locked on the enormous beast as their dark wings flapped, their figures disappearing into particles of light as they appeared near the Behemoth, clenching their fists as grey air wrapped around them and let out a strike towards the Dragon!

DING!

The sound of metal deformity rang out as shockingly, only a few dents appeared on the body of the Draconic Fire Behemoth. The even more shocking thing though, was that the indented portions of the Dragons skin actually began to decay as an eerie energy began to spread from their point of contact.

ROAR!

A wave of power released from the Draconic Fire Behemoth as it actually felt pain, using its high achievements in the Law of Fire to wrap around the weird energy trying to enter his body as his wrathful head glanced at the many beings ganging up on him.

"Do you think you are the only one that can summon things to aid you, ugly worm?"

A voice filled with wrath resounded as the body of the Draconic Fire Behemoth pulsed with power, another set of words being heard all around as the fire energy around them went berserk!

[Dragon Summoning]. [Rejuvenating Flames]. [Elemental Soul Reinforcement]...[Domain of Fire].

WAA! WAA! WAA!

The very space trembled as it squeezed in upon itself, the Draconic Fire Behemoth using its expansive mana to cast many skills!

All around it, the incorporeal forms of deadly dragons around half his size began to appear en masse, their reddened eyes locking on the many summoned beings around Noah as they began moving right away.

On the body of the Draconic Fire Behemoth, golden flames appeared as they healed the decaying strikes caused by the three Ancients, an even more mystical set of flames appearing deep inside its origin as they protected its Soul. This was because the Behemoth had seen a tinge of the Law of Fate around the enemy in front of it, and prepared to counteract it as well as protect itself.

The last thing cast by the Draconic Fire Behemoth was a domain...and this was a domain that only those in the Domain Expansion Realm, or those half a step into it like this Behemoth, could actually utilize!

OOOONG

An air of majesty spread out as the domain of the Draconic Fire Behemoth went on to expand just as wide as the Blood World, covering everything within it as it heavily bolstered the strength of the Behemoth and the numerous dragons it summoned, while Noah and his forces felt a heavy weight placed on their shoulders as if the very air around them had become viscuous and hard to move in!

ROAR!

A resounding roar was released yet again as fiery attacks flew, the many summoned dragons finding their targets as the Abyssal Undead began fights of their own, the Arch Lich RaZan waving its golden staff as more and more Bone Dragons, Undead Phoenixes, and Specters came to life.

The mystical Ancients floated in the air as they sensed the heavy pressure of the domain, their blank white eyes shining as they pointed towards Noah and his forces.

[-----]

A blank set of sounds appeared in Noahs mind once more as he noticed a change on his body, the three Ancients initiating a skill that caused for a shimmering grey shield to appear on his body and the bodies of the numerous summons, the oppression they felt from the [Domain of Fire] gradually lessening as they actually began to feel their strength be bolstered!

The moment this finished, the three Ancients went back to duking out terrible attacks towards the Draconic Fire Behemoth and its summoned dragons, these mystical and yet eerie beings actually being the ones that were dealing the most damage in this battle so far.

Noah looked at these hard to understand beings called Ancients as he thirsted for more knowledge about them, the shimmering grey shield wrapping around his body causing him to feel weightless as he smiled and flapped his pairs of wings forward.

He felt the [Domain of Fire] that the Draconic Fire Behemoth had cast just now, finding the extreme difference between it and the first ability that the Dragon had used to actually break apart his time stop and even stop the activation of [Emperor of Greed]. The ability the Dragon had used, which he called a type of [Authority], was much stronger and was one of the bigger causes for worry as this battle continued!

Yet from all his observations as he spammed numerous skills and began cornering this powerful beast, this Authority was not used again, making him believe there were certain restrictions to such a fearsome power that could even go as far as canceling enemy skills. He would confirm his thoughts later after this battle finished though, for now, he had to focus.

He placed the Glaive of Simmering Ember behind him as he carried the golden blade and shield from the abilities of [Fates Guardian], his sword tip pointing towards the many incorporeal dragons summoned in the surrounding as he called out vibrantly.

[Portals of Terror]. [Sever]!

RUMBLE!

ZING!

Numerous dangerously colored portals began to appear on the sides of the Draconic Fire Behemoth as they trembled to release destructive energies, the cast [Sever] going on to bisect the incorporeal dragons as a few were cut in half!

"Haha, come on!"

Noah couldnt help but call out as the thrilling battle continued!
