Chapter 4526: Predator! IV

The Infinite Unfurling had to be one of the most unique places that Noah had ever been in.

One of the more striking characteristics was how there was nothing weak existing in this place. Truly nothing weak could survive the ambient pressure and dangers that permeated every corner.

An hour passed as he moved through the mountainous landscape that he found himself in, surrounded by rolling mists that obscured vision and created an atmosphere of perpetual twilight. He effectively mapped out the region and cataloged what could be lying within its boundaries.

In the surrounding Gigaparsecs, he was actually able to discover multiple entities ranging from Upper Nonillions to Decillions in Complexity and Purity. Each one followed their own unique Way of existence, carving out territories where they hunted and grew stronger.

He tested his concealment by observing them closely and even entering the perimeter of a few light-years near them.

Gloriously, none of them could sense his presence at all!

If he went too close and his ambient integration wore off from proximity to their concentrated authorities, they might detect the disturbance. But for now, he had left them alone while continuing to scout the region.

Within the surrounding Gigaparsecs, his figure could not be observed by others as he moved with purpose. He traveled rapidly like a flicker that was almost impossible to track.

And after he continued to scour this entire region methodically and confirmed that he could not sense any visible power at the level of THE, he felt reasonably secure. Even though if such a being was present and they hid their aura similar to how Tor concealed his presence, Noah would not really be able to sense them anyway.

But after cautiously mapping this entire place with systematic precision, there was no signature of any THE entities apart from the creatures that he had marked.

After all of this was confirmed through careful observation, he appeared a few light-years away from one of his first targets.

It was a creature that was chewing on the bones of another entity it had recently killed. Its body was surrounded by crackling arcs of purple lightning that danced across its form in chaotic patterns.

The creature looked like a massive salamander, easily the size of several mountains. Its body was covered with jutting sharp crystals all over its surface that refracted the lightning into prismatic displays.

It was another entity following the Way of Lightning, its power stabilized at 955 Nonillions of Complexity and Purity.

Noah looked at it from his concealed position as his eyes pulsed with calculating tyranny. His method of attacking had to change somewhat from what he had done with Tors protection.

He needed the battles to be quick and seamless. The longer he spent in conflict, smashing around the environment and destroying mountains while fighting, the more risks he took. The more chances there were for other entities to be drawn to the commotion.

So he asked himself a critical question: Could he not skip all the booming impacts between his Way and the Way of his enemy? Could he simply settle everything in a single strike, as long as this strike made the outcome of whose Way was stronger absolutely clear?

With this tactical consideration in mind, he breathed out slowly and raised his left hand toward the enemy that he intended to kill.

His Primus Mana could become anything he desired. He had a vast array of offensive capabilities under his belt after devouring multiple Sovereignties.

Utilizing Nonillions of Primordial Fireballs and condensing them into concentrated points of his Way was one method of attack. He had used it frequently because he was most familiar with that approach.

But he had endless variations available because his Primus Mana could accomplish everything he conceived. He could design new Mana Arcana Glyphs at any time to suit any situation.

When he thought about an attack from long range that could potentially snipe an enemy and decimate them in a single strike without giving them time to react or defend...

He smiled with cold satisfaction.

The weapon materialized with perfect balance and deadly intent, composed entirely of compressed authority.

And with his right hand, he pulled back on the bow strings with smooth motion. A terrifying arrow began to form between his fingers as he filled it with endless seas of his Way of existence.

As he drew the string back further and further, he whispered silently to himself with absolute certainty.

"Mana is the foundation of all change in existence," he said with analytical precision. "For any sparks of lightning to form, they need Mana at their foundation to provide the energy for that manifestation."

His eyes gleamed with tyrannical confidence.

"So if I deem that lightning is unable to stop my Mana, if I declare that my Way supersedes the authority of electrical discharge, then it simply cannot resist!"

The arrow that was forming shone with an even more intense green light as he spoke these words. His Civilizational Authority of Mana and Hunger was pumped into this single arrow endlessly.

He meant for it to take the life of an extremely powerful enemy by collapsing what their Way stood for. By proving in a single shot that his Way superseded theirs in quality and depth.

His invisibility would vanish.

And the very instant that happened, the enemy also needed to be dead before they could react or call for help.

Noah breathed in deeply, gathering his focus to a single point.

And then...

His figure flashed into existence as he lost his invisibility, becoming visible for the first time in over an hour. The distant enemy he was targeting raised its head instinctively to sense what had just appeared in its territory.

All of it happened in the span of an instant that was almost too fast to perceive!

But the arrow arrived with inexorable purpose.

BOOM!

Its weavings and Way had actually been collapsed entirely by the single strike!
