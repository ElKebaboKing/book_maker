Chapter 3406 Inevitability! II

Nothing worked.

No matter if Noah Severed all the hundred plus Sources of his existence, no matter if they were all amalgamated with him- he had the distinct feeling that even with such complexity, he couldn't escape that blooming gray brilliance.

He recognized it to be the brilliance of a frequency of the Wheel.

But it was far too terrifying.

Once wrapped up in its light, it was inevitable to flow into it as this held many implications!

And the ones to cause all this...

|...You will not be missed, Infiniverse Dowager! You can fuck off all the way, Origin Sacred Primal Megalonian!...|

The intent of the newly appearing entities from a distance rang out clearly across the Blancverse as the culprits were made clear.

Infiniverse Dowager.

Origin Sacred Primal Megalonian.

They knew them.

They knew of them!

But...

'From other weavings of existence?'

Noah's eyes were bright as he thought of the only possibility where these beings could have met them or known of them.

He had many questions and things to think of, but the terrifying gray light above didn't give any recompense as with a brilliance of inevitability, it touched him, the Infiniverse Megalos Seed and Body, and Ruination!

Instantly, his gaze became even more somber.

Far from the Expanse.

In The Megalos Ynnere.

Opposite a chess board, a figure of Noah seated in front of Old Man Eudemus instantly changed his expression as a terrifying gray light wrapped around him, and he disappeared right away. ...!

On the other side of the chess board, Old Man Eudemus scrambled back with a horrified look as his weavings of existence continued to shake from merely being near the gray light!

In another region of the Megalos Ynnere.

Within an Omniverse where the Great Ponderor had taken refuge in.

Nietzsche looked utterly terrifying as the new Master that had just bound his Megalos Regnum was warped away by a gray light that made him feel like a newly born Morphon!

Within the weavings of the Tresoverse Domain of the Omnifather.

Inside of a Golden Tower of Existence, Noah's brilliance was only just beginning to unfold when in the next moment, blinding gray light that caused the Golden Titans and those within other Towers of Existence to feel trepidation and fear erupted out.

It consumed the brilliant Golden Tower Noah was in as anybody near scrambled away with fear, and when they looked back...they only saw a split tower.

Lifeless and dim, it was decimated as the weavings of the one within it were far gone.

Nobody moved in the next few seconds as they feared the reappearance of the gray light that had just flashed!

Any weavings of Noah's.

His clones.

His other bodies.

Anything related to Noah Osmont...was not spared as it was ensnared by the terrifying gray light that only left behind terrified observers.

All of it was brought back and superimposed on his body within the disappearing Blancverse!

Back in the Expanse, Noah stared at the terrifying gray light somberly as he spoke to the Infiniverse Megalos Seed.

"Be ready for anything. Entanglement is guaranteed, but death is not."

He couldn't feel a sensation of death from this terrifying gray light.

But the feeling it did give seemed to be even worse than death! What could be worse than death?

He didn't know, but he would be ready for it as he watched the gray light that was enveloping everything.

Within the Expanse.

Surrounded by complex weavings of Existence, a shadowy figure confirmed a few things before he nodded.

"Alright, let's go. The target is ensnared in the weavings of that frequency as we have ensured the success of this event. Mission parameters stated not to be near whatever blooms here."

Amalthos uttered out calmly as his figure began to flicker with silver black radiance.

What others found unfathomably hard to do- the traversal of different weavings of existence, was merely something that required a decent amount of effort to achieve.

Since they were done here, and were not supposed to peer and see any details regarding those involved in this event, their mission was entirely completed as the only thing remaining was to ensure that none of this could be traced back to them!

"Yes, yes. Let's go." Beside him, Barbatos was wrapped up in the same brilliance as her shadowy body began to flicker away.

Within the span of an instant...

WAP!

The shadows of these two beings entirely disappeared as if they were never here to begin with!

And silence reigned in this area as slowly, veiled complexity began to die down.

It began.

But it didn't take long before space flickered with radiance as a single dark shadow returned!

"Haa, escaping the senses of that fool is hard."

The shadow of Barbatos flickered as a part of it remained!

Going against mission parameters, a part of it silently came back as...

It was curious.

For the first time, it wanted to see the contents of the mission.

"Hmm..."

Her hands moved haphazardly as thousands of terrifying Runes of Existence burst around her.

She had to do a few things to ensure no blowback or entanglements by observing those of other weavings of existence- especially those she had a hand in completely altering their future weavings.

From her shadowy brilliance, her figure slowly became clear.

Dark hair and eyes that pulsed with appalling complexity. A somewhat small figure, but with curves that showed a gorgeous sculpted visage. The chest and waist were plentiful as her skin shone with a caramel-colored radiance!

Her eyes held an inquisitive light as she questioned and wondered.

Who was involved? Why was it best for the Wheel of Existence for them to be sent away to that place? Why was it deemed this was the path to follow to ensure the smooth flow of the Wheel? Who deserved to not just be killed, but locked away like this?

"Who, who, who...." She spoke with curiosity as she freely looked at this moment, the veil she and others always set up when conducting their missions being broken down as she was able to see down below for the equivalent length of over 100 Omniverses of distance.

She saw the Immovable Sacred Legions and the Pioneer amidst them who should be the one they had been subtly aiding and influencing to find the frequency of that place.

She then saw the Blancverse being 97% pulled into that place as her inquisitive eyes rapidly delved into it, intent on seeing just who was deserving of this treatment!

She saw the inside of it.

She saw multiple figures.

But her attention and all weavings of existence were pulled towards and locked onto a single figure.

A face.

A man with the visage and demeanor of an Emperor.

With devilish handsomeness and profound valor.

At this moment, he was bellowing out with defiance and calm rage as he still burned with shocking complexity against the inevitable pull nobody could escape!

Seeing his face, her heart shuddered.

Her mind buzzed as tears rapidly began to flow down her face.

"Ah..." She let out a silent scream as she felt heart-searing pain- as if something within her was being pierced by trillions of needles!

Subconsciously, she reached out towards that figure that valiantly stood in defiance even while wrapped in the light of inevitability!

"No...!"

She didn't know why.

She didn't know why it hurt so much!

But she had to...

WAP!

Like a flickering singularity, the Blancverse entirely disappeared.

Along with everything inside of it.

"NO!"

Barbatos screamed out as she felt like her own heart had disappeared!

Her body was trembling uncontrollably as her mental state buzzed, unable to make heads or tails about what was going on.

Who was that? Who was that?!

Why did her heart break so much just seeing him? Why did she feel such an appalling sense of desolation right now as he was forever taken away?!
