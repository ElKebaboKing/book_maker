Chapter 4646: THE Farewell of Ginnungagap I

Noah delved into The Infiniverse while thinking of the many things that had to be accomplished.

The idea of The Fallout was something he could look at in different ways.

If he looked at it like a blade hanging over his neck that could fall at any moment, he might never get anything done.

Others might feel a sense of pressure to the extent that things would feel suffocating.

But he put it in the back of his mind as he got ready to do the countless things that needed to be done.

Because why worry when he had no control of when Midnight came?

So for now...

HUUM!

His body flickered and divided into multiple bodies, each one disappearing into different domains.

One pulled back to the region of collapse with the imprisoned Schrodinger and The Disciple of THE Creature and others.

Others went elsewhere.

Moments later, he appeared with everyone in The Early Veiled Shore that was now unfathomably vast!

The brows of THE Living Origin rose as she looked at Noah up and down while staring at The Early Veiled Shore before she spoke.

"Are you sure you arent THE Creature?"

Noah didnt even glance at her as he moved forward while speaking.

"Im sure Im not THE Creature. Welcome to my Osmontian Shore. The bastion that will eventually stand against THE Loom. Settle in for now and acclimate. There are Sacred Herbs and Sacred Mead for everyone to enjoy. I have a few things to look at first. Malphas, can you take care of everyone?"

The moment he finished speaking, the butler-like figure of Malphas appeared and bowed as he took over care of the new entrants!

Sigrid also sent a message that she would momentarily keep THE Living Origin company.

Noah nodded at this as he turned his gaze towards the visage of Elyndra and The First Farmer, who was currently gazing around with shining eyes!

The entity who planted Seeds of Principles.

He wanted to see what would happen if he actually had knowledge that he was playing with The First Tongue without knowing it.

Noah approached Elyndra and The First Farmer, who both turned their gazes towards him with shining eyes.

He put his hand around Elyndras waist and gave her a kiss on her forehead as he spoke softly.

"Let me borrow your father for a second."

Elyndra smiled shyly while nodding with her head down.

The First Farmer had his mouth twitching as light wrapped around him and Noah.

And the two of them disappeared.

They reappeared far from The Early Veiled Shore, inside a budding Transcendent Helios Fold filled with crimson-gold plasma and lemniscate singularities.

Energy pulsed around them in rhythmic patterns.

In this space, The First Farmer scrubbed the image of Elyndra and Noah from his consciousness as he calmly spoke.

"It seems much has changed since we last talked. I didnt know you were a farmer as well."

Noah nodded lightly as he looked at this being while raising his hand.

He formed a concentrated bundle of knowledge regarding The First Tongue and the eighteen Core Phonemes.

Blue-gold light coalesced into a perfect sphere of information.

"Causality is a unique thing. In the past, you forged the Seed of The Principle of Perpetual Harvest and failed. I completed that Seed for you. And now, I will expand your knowledge as I infuse you with understanding of The First Tongue that makes up all Principles. All the workings of Existence. The equations that explain how Existence flows the way it does. The fundamental linguistic structure underlying reality itself."

As he said this, The First Farmers eyes opened wide in shock.

But Noah moved swiftly to throw the singularity of knowledge forward.

It flowed into The First Farmers existence!

The eyes of The First Farmer trembled with shock, then recognition, then pure ecstasy.

His entire body began to glow with building radiance.

His breathing became rapid as eons of effort and failure suddenly made sense.

BOOM!

From his body, a Phoneme of Fire began to be constructed!

Brilliant green-gold flames erupted around him as linguistic structures manifested in the air.

The ancient patterns of The First Tongue bloomed from his Weavings.

Prompts descended before Noahs vision.

|Anomalous Factor of Existence Detected|

|Harvest Assessment:|

|A Harvest of significant Infinite Potential has been collected from The First Farmer due to the effect of your actions|

...!

Noah watched the scene before him with shining eyes as he thought about the information regarding Anomalous Factor of Existence.

Would him awakening to Infinite Mana all those years ago be considered his Anomalous Factor?

Or could he not even put himself on the same scale as others?

He couldnt say for sure.

But at this time, he sat down while watching the dazed First Farmer erupt with ridiculous talent to design his first Phoneme.

The ancient entitys body blazed with green-gold flames as linguistic structures formed and reformed around him.

And while The First Farmer did this, this body of Noah here would have a simple job.

"Focus the Infinite Potential on getting my own personalized Phonemes completed and finalize the analysis of which connected Logos make up my Principles so we can take them to Genesis level."

BOOM!

|Understood, Master.|

The reply from Ruination came swiftly as his body began to explode with power.

At the same time, his chest began to shine as he pulled out THE Farewell of Ginnungagap!

The glorious obsidian-gold box materialized in his hands.

It was filled with unfathomable Weavings of ancientness and majesty.

Complex patterns covered every surface, pulsing with preserved power.

The craftsmanship alone spoke of mastery that transcended normal understanding.

Noah looked at it with shining eyes.

The farewell gift from his teacher.

He sighed as he moved to open it, wondering just what might be packed within.

What would Ulmoreth, THE Primeval Titan, consider important enough to give as a final gift?
