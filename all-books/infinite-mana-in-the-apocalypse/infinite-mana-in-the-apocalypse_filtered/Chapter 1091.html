Chapter 1089 - Delivered On A Platter! I

Out of the 9 Universes that were affected by the Universal Amalgamation of the Antiquity, the Microbial and the Chthonian Universes were the only ones to be freed as it left behind 7 Universes that still had Universal Constructs standing strong within them.

From these 7 Universes...Noah had completely conquered three of them as they didnt even have any existence living within their vast stars, only having the Universal Constructs and the Hegemonies that protected them!

These three were the Animus, Necrotic, and the Automaton Universes. They left behind the Liberated, Euthenia, Omicron, and the Abyssal Universes as for Noah, these Universes that contained Universal Constructs and existences that he could have pledge fealty to him to gain the remaining half of the Marks of Antiquity he needed...he considered these Universes to be the fat sheep that he wanted to milk.

All of them would involve battles with Hegemonies, but the latter mentioned 4 would also grant Noah billions of Marks of Antiquity as it was within these 4 Universes that Noah sent a clone of the Blue Slime in each.

THRUM!

Simultaneously, a glimmering Blue Slime appeared within the Liberated, Euthenia, Omicron, and the Abyssal Universes as the Hegemonies protecting the Universal Constructs within them were alerted en masse.

One might wonder, what happened to the authority of Antiquity that prevented any Hegemonies aside from those that branded their Origins onto the Universal Constructs or were already within the Universe when the authority descended? How could the Blue Slime at the level of a Hegemony so seamlessly enter 4 vastly different universes?!

The answer was of course, the seamless assimilation of the Universal Construct devoured through <<Universal Devouring>>.

Aside from the gift of 10 Billion Marks of Antiquity for the Blue Slime, it also took in and assimilated the aura and core of the Universal Construct as it understood them inside and out, with even more depth than those that merely branded their auras onto them!

So the 4 clones of the Blue Slimes appeared seamlessly in the Universes with Quintillions of beings and Universal Constructs, their auras reverberating out wildly as their shining cerulean figures shot towards the Universal Constructs where the defending Hegemonies were located at with fervor.

Noahs other subordinates that had entered these 4 Universes to begin the conquest before continued unhindered, the Seven Deadly Sins, Legions of Undead, Automaton Sentinel Legions...all of the forces went forward from galaxy system to galaxy system as they already took the first steps to spread Noahs domination!

The Blue Slimes were the topping on the top as they would face the defending Hegemony at each of the Universal Constructs, the defeat of these Hegemonies meaning Noah could widely spread his essence of Fealty out across the Universe like he did for the Chthonian Universe and bring the conquest to a close on these Universes as well.

This was the mission of the 4 Clones and Noahs subordinates that were given for the sake of efficiency.

You then had Noah himself and the 5th clone of the Blue Slime that appeared...in the Animus Universe!

WAA!

When he teleported away from the Oathkeeper and others in the Microbial Universe, the Chthonian figure of Noah seated upon a Blue Slime appeared in the Animus Universe as he was soon joined together by an alluring figure of a Hegemony that had this same Universe as her home.

The vibrant clone of Valentina looked towards the mountainous figure of the Blue Slime at the realm of a Hegemony in a daze as she questioned everything she knew about cultivating the power of Realms, sighing endlessly in her heart about how such a thing could even become a reality!

The three of them appeared not too far from the Universal Construct, the figure of Ambrose coming to attention and looking towards them with cold eyes as he wasnt alone. The Hegemony that had broken the Oath and initiated the chase across the Universe while holding onto Noah was also there, Hegemony Dark Shadow looking towards them with baleful eyes that shook with the aura of Tenebrosity.

But even this wasnt all.

It was because at this very moment, Chronos received information on all the happenings of the 5 affected Universes that Noahs forces arrived in as his expression changed, and he spoke towards the many Hegemonies around him and gave out commands!

Numerous figures flashed and disappeared, a particularly jubilant Cerulean Hegemony being one among the party of Hegemonies that went towards the Animus Universe while others went to reinforce the defenders in the other 4 Universes.

As for Chronos and the Goliath...their eyes were on the Oathkeeper that could make his move at any moment, these powerful beings also conserving their power as they got ready to fight with everything on the line to achieve their dreams.

Their actions meant grand changes in the Animus Universe as Noahs eyes raised up when he began to see the light of teleportation call forth multiple Hegemonies to stand beside Ambrose and Dark Shadow.

There was Solerno, two other Hegemonies Noah hadnt yet met, and the Cerulean Hegemony!

The two that Noah was seeing for the first time was the caped Heroic Hegemony and the Hegemony of Fire, a being from the race of Flame Spirits that shone brighter than many suns.

But even with all of these new faces...Noahs eyes perked up when they landed on the Cerulean Hegemony.

The eyes of the Cerulean Hegemony also landed on him and the Blue Slime as this Hegemony with the blue crown brilliantly floating on his head looked towards them with immense hidden greed and fervor!

Enemies that had known each other months ago met again on this day!

"Haha!"

Noah couldnt help but let out a boisterous laugh that shook the surroundings as he looked at the Cerulean Hegemony that was delivering himself to him on a platter.

The figure of the Cerulean Hegemony also buzzed with power, this being seemingly not being able to wait amidst his excitement and greed as he was the first to vibrate with essence before he shot out towards Noah and the 2 Hegemonies surrounding him- as he shot out to what he considered a free Cosmic Treasure!
