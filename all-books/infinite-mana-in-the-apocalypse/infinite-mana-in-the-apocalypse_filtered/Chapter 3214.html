Chapter 3212 Outerversal Morphon Ozymandias! II

A show.

This was how Noah could best describe it as a titanic illusory image of none other than the Viceroy of the Commonwealth was displayed far above, his hand pressing down radiating the golden light that was stopping the advancement of the Declaration of the Outerversal Morphon Ginnungagap!

Golden brilliance enveloped everything around the Gladiators' Arena as the obsidian domain became constricted and smaller.

It was being pushed back towards the center of the Arena where the Shrine of the Outerversal Morphon was enlarging with grandiosity, the thrones of the audiences finally being taken out of what seemed an endless age of darkness where they were all about to be massacred in.

"Ah..."

The Citizens of the Commonwealth that visited Omniverse X-999 raised their hands up to the visage of brilliance that saved them.

Towards their Viceroy!

Feats of his brilliance here would undoubtedly be spread to the other Omniverses that the Commonwealth oversaw- his brilliance that was going against the influence of an Outerversal Entity personally!

|Profane!|

...!

His appearance bellowed out towards the obsidian Declaration as it wriggled as if it were an animal.

The seething golden brilliance converged together above as it shockingly took on the form...of a Wheel.

A golden wheel of interminable brilliance as it looked like a collection of many small circles contained in one massive circle their center point showing another illusory image of the Viceroy seated in a meditative position as, at this moment, this wheel began to slowly spin.

HUUM!

A slow spin.

Yet the moment this spinning of the Wheel began, an unimaginably heavy pressure thundered down that reduced the size of the Declaration of the Outerversal Morphon Ginnungagap by half.

At the center of the Arena, the rising Shrine of the Outerversal Morphon Ginnungagap that was gradually becoming more real cracked and became more illusory.

And the Wheel continued to spin.

As if it were grinding down something.

As if with every turn the wheel made, an inviolable weaving of power came down.

When it completed a full turn...

CRACK!

The illusory Shrine of the Outerversal Morphon Ginnungagap was cracked as the Declaration that had turned the golden sands dark was fully constricted mere miles around the Shrine!

The tentacles that were spreading out with shocking fervor and devouring everything screeched as if they had met their natural enemy before they began to shrivel and fade away into nothingness.

The singularity that was spitting out the emergence of the weavings of the Outerversal Morphon Ginnungagap shrieked upwards towards the Wheel.

From it, a massive obsidian pupil opened along with an overlap of thousands of massive tentacles!

The pupil seemed to be etching its gaze with the entity stopping its entry, locking its gaze above as the apathetic expression of Viceroy Gilgamesh looked down.

And under him, the golden Wheel turned once more.

As it was about to complete the turn, it seemed to be meeting resistance- but this small point of resistance faded away the moment that Viceroy Gilgamesh pressed his foot down, the rings within his wheels pulsing at the turn was completed!

RIIP!

The sound of something being ripped apart echoed out as gloriously, the Shrine of the Outerversal Morphon Ginnungagap was ripped apart as it folded into itself.

As if everything about it was being compressed and erased!

The myriad of obsidian tentacles that were spreading out were compressed into a fading singularity as in an instant...

...!

All Sources even emanating traces of the Authority of the Outerversal Morphon Ginnungagap was eradicated.

Above, the image of the Viceroy was the only thing that remained.

Unfettered.

Brilliant.

Omniversal!

The golden Wheel he had called out only turned twice before the enemy was eradicated, the Royal Existential Omniversal Simians in the thrones of the audience instantly dropping to their knees while they raised their hands towards their Viceroy!

They raised their hands to him as they wanted to express their fervor. Their loyalty. Their awe and respect to the Viceroy of the Commonwealth.

Down below on the golden sands, Noah gazed up at the massive intricate golden Wheel that Viceroy Gilgamesh recovered with a wave of his hand, his gaze looking imperious as he looked over the masses, his voice echoing out.

"This is who we are fighting against."

WAA!

"We do what we do day in and day out to ensure that Existence is protected. That life is protected. The glory of the Commonwealth will continue to spread to all corners of known and unknown Omniverses as we ensure that none will fall to our enemies!"

...!

A heartfelt speech!

One that the citizens of the Commonwealth heard and resonated with their hearts, their hands lifted up as they bellowed out in affirmation!

"OOOH!"

"Glory to the Commonwealth!"

"Long live the Commonwealth!"

"Long live the Viceroy!"

"Long live!"

An abundance of shouts of valor and honor.

Noah observed it all on the sands of the Arena as the visage of Nuntius Eshkeri floated down to stand before him.

Other Protectors floated in the space above as they looked respectfully towards the figure of the Viceroy, and only when he began to disappear did they begin to move.

Nuntius Eshkeri's expression was solemn as he spoke while walking toward Noah, his brilliant monkey tail behind him pulsing with golden arcs of light.

|Prime Citizen Emperor Osmont, you were able to unveil an Agent of Anti-Life today and prevent them from entering deeper ranks of the Commonwealth- this already distinguishes you as someone who does the job of a Protector. Thus, it is deemed for your status to be elevated to become a true Protector of the Commonwealth!|

...!

There was supposed to be an evaluation of the Gladiators as they battled each other in the Arena for a chance to become Protectors of the Commonwealth.

Something that came to be because a Protector was greatly hurt in an Eonic Omniverse Record!

And now, another incident occurred where someone who was part of the Commonwealth actually turned out to be an Agent of Anti-Life, and Noah incidentally revealed this.

And as a result...the position of a Protector was now being offered to him.

"..."

Cause and effect.

Noah looked at the visage of Nuntius Eshkeri looking at him with stern brilliance as his eyes curved into a devilish smile!
