Chapter 3050 War!

Within the vast stretches of the Imperium.

Past innumerable Existential Domains, under the glorious golden banner of the Source of the Imperium, there was a halcyon colored Plane of Existence.

Inside such a plane of existence, there was a golden floating pathway that was comprised of a single golden block- this block constantly moving around a space that was littered with thousands upon thousands of dazzling golden stars.

Stars...that one could easily grasp in their own hands.

And yet these stars held austere brilliance and wonder as the regal aura of a Teracosm Stage Entity emanated from each of them!

Atop the golden block floating amidst these stars, there was a woman sitting peacefully while she wrote on a dazzling Codex.

The Codex radiated a multicolored light that put all others to shame, her fingers acting akin to a pen that was writing something in the Codex!

Her hair was a mixture of gold and white strands as her skin was tan, grace and unfathomable beauty rising and falling from her as her pure white robe hid the extensive majesty underneath.

A small dark mole that only added to her exotic demeanor could be seen on her lower right cheek.

Such a being didn't radiate any aura at all as one could think she was a normal entity who hadn't even grasped the workings of mana.

And yet such a being was watching over an unfathomably large number of Dominium Crystals- with the word watching only being a very small part of what she actually did!

At this moment...

CRACK!

The light within a distant Dominium Crystal instantly dimmed and cracked, the eyes of the seated woman fluttering as she blinked, and the golden block she sat on instantly disappeared.

The next moment, it was before the cracked Dominium Crystal that was leaking golden blood.

A crystal that the moment she saw, she furrowed her brows and tapped on Its remnants.

And in the next moment, illusory screens shining the last moments of the owner of this Dominium Crystal began to play out.

The illusory scenes showed...a scene that caused the beautiful woman to blink in surprise as it wasn't one that you would expect to see.

It was a a scene of Alderon surrounded by rough looking manifestations that jumped him as if it were a gang beatdown!

But the most unique thing the woman caught was how Alderon's Authorities were not even able to put up an ounce of defenses!

|An Omniversal Organ...|

CRACK!

Another sound that caused her gaze to change bloomed as she had just gotten to the part where a few words stated that Archon Basileus Alderon did not leave alone!

The name on the page caused her gaze to change as she once more disappeared along with her block, coming to appear in front of a second bleeding crystal.

At this time, her eyes blazed with multicolored radiance as she seemingly grasped the weavings of everything within an instant, her expression changing yet again as this time, she sighed!

|Why is it you...?|
