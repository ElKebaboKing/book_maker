In the Dark Universe.

Noahs main body was floating in the skies while still holding on to the figure of the Blue Slime. But...his attention was entirely elsewhere as his eyes were fully covered with a layer of gold, with his Universal Fortune vibrantly swimming around him in lustrous waves.

When it came to the crucial moment of the clone of the Blue Slime reaching the only remaining Universal Construct and destroying it or not being able to reach it in time as the descent of an Antiquity was all but guaranteed...Noah was gazing upon his Destiny while waiting for the results of this exact moment.

If the Antiquity descended, he expected this to be a setback as his Destiny would momentarily reduce and turn dark, where even his immense fortune would only be able to help slightly. But if the clone of the Blue Slime reached it...it meant he would come out on top as the entire Primordial Cosmos would be free of any apocalyptic threats as he could slowly grow his power.

"..."

RUMBLE!

His heart was still calm even with such an outcome and setback, his glazed eyes observing his Destiny to see just what changes would occur to it as the expected reduction and darkening of his Destiny...did not occur.

Instead...

BZZZZT!

His eye shook and couldnt help but squint as a second later, Noah fantastically observed his golden Destiny and fortune that were as vast as seas...to become an even more wondrous gold color as they began to churn and expand wildly, their size propagating outwards crazily as they nearly doubled!

...!

With the action of them failing to stop the descent of an Antiquity in time, his Destiny and Fortune had actually not reduced as they instead increased even further.

Just what was in store to cause this?!

THRUM!

To simply cast [Deus Ex Machina] and see what would come out of it!
