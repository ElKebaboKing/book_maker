Chapter 1486: The Fantastical Forsaken TreasureReality! V

An existence that was a native of the Forsaken Treasure Reality sat atop a mountain of Soul Crystals as the eyes of Noah and all other Golden Titans honed on it!

The first thing one would think about when they looked at this creature would be the word grotesque as even though it shone with a sheen of gold- the creature was a mass of flesh and shining metals and Crystals as it took the form of an enormous golden slug.

Wings made of golden flesh like meat could be seen behind its back as its expression was that of utter derision and greed, but the waves of power it released were no less than that of the Sixth Firmament of Ascendancy!

SHAA!

Noah's eyes flashed as he felt the authority of the Destiny Goblin King echo out, information about this being coming into his mind as its description was pulled up before his eyes.

[Amalgamation of Greed]:: A creature born within a Destiny Domain Space as it acts as its guard. It lives and dies within the Destiny Domain Space as if the treasures within are ever threatened to be taken by others, it will fight to its last breath...

An existence only born to protect the Treasures born within Destiny Domain Spaces!

In this case, the Destiny Domain Space was the periphery of this mountain of Soul Crystals- this domain space producing all of these wondrous resources over the years as the Amalgamation of Greed before them...had been enjoying the Soul Crystals this whole time.

Noah was only able to get the description of this creature as the {Light of Fortune} that everyone receives when they entered this Forsaken Treasure Reality did nothing to aid them, nor did his Treasure King Authority that he obtained as a Destiny Goblin King!

The creature before him was not a Destiny Goblin so naturally, Noah could not control it as there was only one way for them to get past it while obtaining the mountain of Soul Crystals before them.

Seeing them nearing, its slug-like body rose in attention ever so slightly as it let out a guttural growl.

"OOOH!"

Its meaty flaps waved about as the Soul Crystal mountain shook, the Sixth Firmament Golfen Titan within Noah's group nearly stepping forward before Natalya raised her hands ever so slightly.

"The Forsaken Treasure Reality is vast as we have yet to even come across Materials worthy enough to be used as ingredients to forge a Primordial Relic...I will take care of the enemies and allow us to continue moving in haste!"

WAA!

Her words were inviolable as she moved, the Golden Titans nodding with fervor as even Noah didn't step forward. As powerful as he was, he still didn't compare to a Sixth Firmament existence that had an Ascendancy Value of 1,000,000!

His own Ascendancy Value had continued elevating up higher as in the past hour, it has reached the Boundary of 7,000 as the boosts from the effect of the Emperor of Old Bloodline fully settled in.

He was only 3,000 Ascendancy Values away from the Fourth Firmament, and the gulf towards the 1 Million Value of even the Sixth Firmament was much too high for him as the most he could do with the ridiculous 90% True Soul and Physical Damage Negation along with his own resistances...was to maybe survive the blows of a creature at the Sixth Firmament!

So Natalya stepped forward with ease as her figure rose up towards the Amalgamation of Greed on top of the Soul Crystal Mountain, the temperature around her beginning to rise as the existence atop the crystalline mountain roared out threateningly.

Dozens of glorious Flames of Edicts rose around it along with the illusory light of Ascendancy Halos, but even such a scene only caused The Overseer to release a harrumph.

She was Natalya Rostova. The Scorcher of Realities!

While a Sixth Firmament existence had a 1 Million Ascendancy Value, she had over 10 Million as an Ascendancy Value as her might could not be denied!

Even against two other Seventh Firmament experts, she was able to send them back heavily injured with a single attack as the power of this Overseer was unfathomable.

Noah having attached himself to such an existence that had even brought him into this Forsaken Treasure Reality was an extremely bountiful event as there were many possibilities for the future!

BZZZT!

Her figure had flown to the peak of the Soul Crystal Mountain as the Amalgamation of Greed roared out towards her, its body crackling with power as in comparison- Natalya looked like a reserved beast about to break out.

And it was truly so as from what she stated- she was going for speed!

WAA!

Her fingers flashed as an instant later, a soul shaking Great Bow appeared within her fair hands.

It released the wondrous aura of a Peak Tier Quasi-Primordial Relic as with a swift motion as if she had practiced it for countless years, Natalya drew on the bowstring as the essence of white flames formed a heart rending arrow- the figure of the Amalgamation of Greed sensing something terrifying as the Flames of Edicts around it began to fly out!

But in the span of less than a millisecond.

SHAA!

A flash of white light.

"..."

POOOOF!

The viscous muscular body of the Amalgamation of Greed was razed into mincemeat an instant later as a pristine arrow of white flames had pierced through it faster than even Noah's eyes could catch it!

...!

The bow within Natalya's hands came down as she didn't even break a sweat, the strength of a Seventh Firmament expert being affirmed in everyone's eyes at this moment as they saw the Overseer being capable of sniping a Sixth Firmament enemy without even utilizing her Ascendancy Halos!

OOOM!

An instant later her fingers moved as wisps of essence landed on the Soul Crystal Mountain that the Amalgamation of Greed used to be on, this mountain of treasure being divided into multiple portions as she sectioned two big portions- one that flew towards her Voidforged Treasure Pouch and the other into Noah's. The other sectioned portions were over a dozen as they went to Grandmaster Monos and the others, their eyes shining brightly as wondrous Loot landed on their hands.

Noah was given the same portion as a Seventh Firmament Overseer because of his identity, his eyes also smiling brightly as he adored any Loot that could strengthen his soul!
