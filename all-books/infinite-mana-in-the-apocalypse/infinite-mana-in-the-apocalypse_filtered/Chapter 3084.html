In the Sacred Natal Omniversal Sea of Pompeii.

Was he okay?

He was more than okay!

He just had a change of perspective as there were a few things that he needed to get done from this moment on.

So he nodded towards her with a smile while pointing to the heavy weavings of Entropy behind him that were gradually becoming mixed with thousands of other matching extremes stretched out across the Omniverse.

|A blooming Omniversal Authority showed me a few things. Let's finish up here and delve deeper soon, time is not on our side.|

...!

Time was not on their side.

But ...

|Mmm, you have another body, yes? Let me take that somewhere for a bit.|

He spoke calmly, but Sona's eyes squinted as if she could sense the change and weight of his words.

At the same moment that Noah spoke about time, he also felt the extremes of Chronos among others begin to move as even if time wasn't on their side, they could always influence it!

While such a thing began regarding the weavings of time along with the continued construction of the Greater Omniversal Authority of Entropia, Noah had his other bodies make a move to usher in a few changes.

Far away from the Greater Omniversal Domain of Pompeii.

Within an Existential Domain of the Imperium.

Henry was floating above golden skies as he watched the reality around him be pulled towards a single point of singularity- all of it being devoured by the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed as in the surroundings, sounds of battle still rang out against the remaining Imperium Knights that were stationed within this Domain!

At such a moment...

WAP!

Henry's pupils trembled as he saw the visage of his father appear in front of him, his figure coming to attention as he bowed while calling out.

|Father!|

He kept his head bowed respectfully as many possibilities crossed his mind on why his father would be here.

Had a great enemy they had not foreseen about to appear?

Had-...!

Little Henry's thoughts came to a stop as he saw his father's figure float in front of him until he was inches away, his face showing the same majesty as from the viewpoint of his Will, Henry could see a sense of melancholy within his father's eyes even as he smiled!

Melancholy.

Even traces of sadness!

Had something drastic happened? Had the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed been compromised? Had..?!

As he was raising his head, his thoughts truly had to come to a halt as his father...opened his arms and embraced him.

...!

A simple embrace.

A simple hug!

But even this caused Henry's mind to buzz as he couldn't fathom what was going on!

As for Noah, his mind was filled with his own thoughts as he felt a sense of sadness at the reality that his own Son had to bow to him and not gaze up just because of his Glory.

Even the embrace he put him in right now...when was the last time that he even held his son close?

When did he not impose a heavy and unbearable pressure on his own son for him to improve and be just a fraction as powerful as he was?

He couldn't remember.

So he embraced his son as at this moment, Little Henry was so shocked that he didn't even know what to say.

|I am sorry, my Son.|

WAA!

|For any heavy expectations I placed on you. For any pressure you felt in the months you came to be. I am sorry.|

...!

Was the erasure of many possible paths he and the others had felt this massive of an issue? Was an apocalypse that even they couldn't escape coming?
