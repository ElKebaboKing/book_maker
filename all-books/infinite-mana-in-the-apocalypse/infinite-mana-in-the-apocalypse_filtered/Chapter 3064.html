Chapter 3062 Entropic Fervor! V

Within the Source of Dignity.

Brilliant verdant and golden lights fluctuated like seas all around as the figure of Sanctus Princess Emilliana could be seen standing straight amongst other Sanctus Princesses, with the figures of Elder Queens standing ahead of them as all of them...gazed up front.

On a throne of pure Existential Extremity Authority that was also surrounded by Tendrils of the weavings of Omniversal Authorities.

The figure of an utterly gorgeous entity sat with calmness and serenity as she was the Elder Queen Mother!

The one who currently controlled the Source of Dignity!

Her expression was currently stern as surrounded by verdant and gold lights, her voice rang out.

|Destruction and genocide is coming as the Imperium wishes to enact yet another campaign similar to eons past that eradicated countless Lifeforms. This time, the Sanctus Populi will not sit idly by and defend until our forces are overrun. We will bring the war towards their own homes!|

They fought for the good of the people.

But they were not weak.

They would not be etched in the eons of history as another force that the Imperium had erased or enslaved!

They could not.

|Prepare your Dragon Knights and set off as soon as possible to your set coordinates...Salus populi suprema lex esto!|

WAA!

In bellowing tones that seemed like drums of war.

|Salus populi suprema lex esto!|

|Salus populi suprema lex esto!|

The good of the people reigned supreme!

The good of the people was the supreme law.

|Emilliana, any word on your sister?|

|...|

Sanctus Princess Sona and the Prince she had set off with.

As if it was heavily expectant on what was to come and how it would benefit!

Amidst such a fervor...

HUUM!

...!

Across the Omniverse, many wondered about the mystery of Omniversal Pillagers.

Whether they moved only to pillage and destroy, and why there would be times of silence and inaction, and times of chaos.

Every. Single. One!

And the passage of hours...came with a great deal of changes.
