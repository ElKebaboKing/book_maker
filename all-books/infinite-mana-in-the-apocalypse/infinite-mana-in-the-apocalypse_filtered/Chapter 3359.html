Chapter 3357 Infiniverse Megalos Seed! V

Incredulity.

Such an emotion flashed past the eyes of the Silver Horned Ordinance Amalgamation entity as it knew its power well.

Currently, its Ordinance of Dignity was covering this whole region as for him, it was akin to an extension of his body.

As if this whole region was now a part of him.

And Dignity could be expressed thoroughly and absolutely in this space!

For it to be denied, an equally imposing authority had to be laid down as even then, the two Ordinances would fight for supremacy.

The better and more unique Ordinance would win.

The more complex Ordinance would triumph!

As someone who did not only express his Ordinance, but also amalgamated with it entirely through his existence- denying him was that much harder.

Another Ordinance Amalgamation entity would have to be more complex than him by a factor of two or more to be able to even deny it!

This was the truth.

And yet...

Without using an Ordinance.

Without any superfluous means.

This woman shimmering with multicolored radiance actually denied and broke his Ordinance by what seemed like sheer mass of complexity alone!

|Mass? Your Amalgamated Ordinance is mas-|

HUUM!

The Silver Horned Ordinance Amalgamation Entity had begun to speak with an ugly expression, but the Infiniverse Megalos Body paid him no heed as she moved towards him.

With every step she took, the dazzling purple chains laid out shattered and disappeared- her figure looking like a runaway train that could not be stopped no matter what as in an instant, she arrived before her enemy!

Such an action caused alarm to rise on all those here as hastily, the Silver Horned Ordinance Amalgamation Entity waved his hands as his figure became encased in a crystalline purple exoskeleton of dignity- unbreakable in nature.

Above him, a banner of purple Dignity unfurled as it burned with endless majesty, and behind him, the outline of a grand celestial palace began to form imposingly!

With all of this unfolding, a terrifying feeling of undeniable royalty flowed out as the Daughter of Existence in the back, along with her other Attendants, nodded and breathed easily once more.

Yes.

This was how their might should be.

In front of this endless grandeur of Dignity, all enemies would find the complexity of their existence to not be enough as their Ordinance would gain a feeling of inferiority until it was ultimately suppressed!

Yet in the next moment.

The dazzling figure of the Infiniverse Megalos Body balled her hands into fists before she swung them down like hammers.

BOOM!

Appallingly, the radiant exoskeleton and forming Manifestation of a scared palace instantly shattered, the fists of the Infiniverse Megalos Body shattering the swirling Dignity around her enemy as her hands actually smashed on top of the head and caved it in!

Purple gold blood gushed out from the eyes and nose of this regal Ordinance Amalgamation Entity, his eyes fully bulging out of their sockets from the blow!

The expression of the Infiniverse Megalos Body was cold as her right leg was flung out right after, going up towards the groin mercilessly as any shred of complexity was destroyed by sheer mass alone, a horrific sound booming out as her leg deformed the groin to the extent that her leg extended all the way to his stomach.

...!

Unbearable, unfathomable waves of pain echoed out within the bulging eyes of the Silver Horned Ordinance Amalgamation Entity, his existence feeling as if the heaviest thing in its whole life had knocked into it as at this moment- he couldn't even retaliate.

All of his complexity felt dazed and denied entirely, his Dignity utterly shredded as it couldn't find itself rising again from the sheer tonnage pressing down!

And in such a state...

WUU!

The Infiniverse Megalos Body raised her hand up and chopped down as if she was a sharp sword, purple gold blood bursting in abundance in the next moment as like a jagged blade, the unfathomable complexity of her sheer weight alone tore the body of an Ordinance Amalgamation entity top to bottom.

It wasn't just the tearing of the body.

It was also the decimation and tearing of the weavings of Existence of this entity fully as in the next moment, no manifestations of purple chains remained active anymore.

Only the golden strings of the Expanse surrounding a small Blancverse remained, along with the bloody and wild figure of the Infiniverse Megalos Body fully tore in half an Ordinance Amalgamation entity, their deep and dense weavings of existence being turned dim as she grabbed the halves of his body and flung them towards the Blancverse Domain.

Her figure then vibrated as purple gold blood and pieces of organs drifted off her body, her cold eyes staring ahead towards the Three Eyed Goldflame Steed, the one atop it, and those around it who looked towards her with rage and small traces of fear!

This was because they just watched an Ordinance Amalgamation expert be literally ripped apart in seconds.

What kind of monster was this?

What-

BOOM!

Her figure was relentless as it flung itself forward, being even faster with no laid out Ordinances around as she actually reached the front of the Three Eyed Goldflame Steed in an instant, this horse jumping back in fright as the figures of the other two Ordinance Amalgamation Entities appeared in front of it while crying out.

|Protect the Daughter of Existence!|

|You dare to do this?! Understand that behind us, there is the Immovable Sacred Palace of the Expanse!|

Two shouts.

All with self-explanatory purpose, but there was a sense of fear in each shout!

After all, they had just observed their colleague literally be ripped apart.

Their bodies had begun to erupt with signature complexities of Dignity, a massive palace beginning to manifest behind each of them when...

PANG!

The hands of the Infiniverse Megalos Body swung left and right like hammers, smashing into their bodies and shockingly causing their emerging Ordinances to forcefully retreat in their bodies, and the head of the Infiniverse Megalos Body flung itself forward to smash into the Three Eyed Goldflame Steed that the Daughter of Existence sat on!

BOOM!
