Chapter 1114: A Heart! II

In the void within the Abyssal Universe, the figure of a pristine woman could be seen as she gazed down above multiple Hegemonies.

From her, one could feel immense coldness and apathy, and no one could feel it more than Noah himself!

This was because the existence was currently addressing him, where even with the veil covering everything about her, Noah found her words to be filled with immense apathy even though she was mentioning something like a reward.

It was extremely hard to explain the feeling he was currently experiencing as he came out of his thoughts when he saw the unique existence above them wave her hands, causing for the appearance of a sparkling and pulsating...heart.

Yes!

It was a pulsating heart that shone with a rainbow colored aura, its appearance causing a fantastical fragrance to descend down as it made the minds of the Hegemony buzz while Noah himself found his Origin becoming entirely focused on this heart.

The veiled gaze of the existence also went towards this pulsating multicolored heart as her sonorous yet apathetic voice continued to ring out.

"Across the vast and nigh endless Ruination Sea, there are terrifying creatures known as Primordial Beasts roaming free."

...!

The veiled figure seemed to be explaining the origin of the shocking object she pulled out, Noah and the others listening carefully as their hearts trembled.

"These Primordial Beasts are unique creatures that require the sustenance of Universes and Cosmos to survive and reproduce...and so they traverse across the Ruination Sea while devouring the Universes within the vast Cosmos they come across."

RUMBLE!

Shocking new information was revealed from this being as she said it so simply and without any emotion, her hands waving forward as the multicolored heart began to float over to Noah!

"It's normally very hard for them to locate Cosmos, the chance being nearly impossible as the only time when that chance increases...is when Antiquity appears from a Cosmos."

...!

"The appearance and aura of the Antiquity that violated rules and entered your Dimension, that Apocryphal Antiquity around you, and even my appearance here...they have all increased the chances of a Primordial Beast crossing countless light years from the Ruination Sea to possibly come in contact with your Cosmos."

Yes!

Once a Cosmos had advanced far enough to produce the emergence of an Antiquity, it was put in a whole new tear as if it was invisible before...it became a light that began to flicker in utter darkness at that moment.

And the more power and might it displayed...the more Antiquities and even the possibility of a Cosmic Realm Existence coming to be would mean a Cosmos would be blinking gloriously across the dark and crimson Ruination Sea!

Such words and knowledge caused the hearts of Oathkeeper and the others to tremble as Noah listened with shining eyes to the new concepts displayed before him.

Primordial Beasts that crossed the Ruination Sea!

To him, it meant they were very unique existences as they had to have the Dao of Ruination or something like the Primordial Dao to be able to do such things.

He had many questions to ask amidst all of this but the most important one was of course...about the heart before him.

"When the Primordial Beasts find a Cosmos and begin Devouring, they can be repelled by the Antiquity or Antiquities within as when defeated...all of their essence and Origin are condensed into a Primordial Heart..."

WAA!

As these words finished, the multicolored heart arrived before Noah's eyes as his hands reached out to it, grasping onto it as he felt the pulses it released as these alone caused his body to vibrate with excitement!

"A Primordial Heart is extremely unique as with it, one can rapidly increase the number of Galaxies within their Universes or utilize the Heart to birth Dao Galaxies...making it possible for Antiquities to birth Universes fairly quickly."

Even while talking about such wondrous and fantastical realities, the voice of this being remained apathetic as she looked towards Noah grasping the heart and focusing on it with all of his mentality.

"That is only if the Primordial Beast is defeated though. If it succeeds in Devouring many Universes and Cosmos...it can rise to become something that even those in the Cosmic Realm struggle with."

"Use this Primordial Heart and prepare yourselves in the case a Primordial Beast ever comes across your Cosmos."

RUMBLE!

With such shocking and wild information laid out, the figure of the veiled existence began to shine with fantastical light as a crack unfolded behind her again.

Noah came out of his stupor at the incredible looking pulsating heart before his hands as his eyes once more focused on this figure.

He wanted to ask where she was from! He wanted to know what force she belonged to and why exactly she had been able to be influenced by Deus Ex Machina! He wanted to know how this existence could just come and go like this as he understood the reasoning behind her actions.

But when he looked at her apathetic air, he didn't say anything as he only spoke calmly.

"Thank you."

WAA!

A simple gratitude, and that was it!

The veiled figure nodded with the same apathy as when the crack behind her began to gush out Ruination and Primordial Essence, she delved into it as she pulled everything in and closed it soon after.

"..."

Gone just as quickly as she had arrived.

With her departure, a pervasive silence descended onto the void of the Abyssal Universe!

The sequence of events caused every Hegemony's mind to buzz in a stupor as none of it was as expected.

This was more so for the Goliath of the Primordial Cosmos and the Hegemonies on his side, the dreams of these beings taking the method of Antiquity from an Antiquity being dashed as when they all sobered up, their gazes unconsciously landed on the figure of Noah.

On the sparkling multicolored Primordial Heart that from the words of the veiled existence...it seemed like something that only the most powerful of Antiquities that could take down the terrifying Primordial Beasts could get their hands on!

Now, it was given to the Hegemony before them as their eyes glimmered brightly with intense light of wanting.

"You all...!"

RUMBLE

The bellow of the Oathkeeper reverberated out at this moment!
