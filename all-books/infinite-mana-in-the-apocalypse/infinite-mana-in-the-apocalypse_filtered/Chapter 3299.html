Chapter 3297 An Unfathomable Emergence! III

The Cartographer of Existence!

Cartographer.

A word that related to an entity that designed maps and generally drew in countless waves of information and laid it out in a geographical manner.

Such a distinction was given to Noah at this moment as truly, he felt like he was gazing at a portion of an endlessly vast map, with many twinkling lights representing Omniverses strewn about.

His vision was centered around the Omniverse he was currently in, its depth spreading around it as even at this moment, it couldn't see everything.

After a certain distance, he only saw endless darkness without any glimmer of stars as there, his vision could not yet extend to.

|The Region your Cartographer's Vision extends to currently has been defined and designated the distinction of Sector 1.|

|The location of Omniverses and pertinent weavings within Sector 1 of the Megalos Ynnere are being further defined.|

...!

It was unique.

A male generally had a sense of direction and what the distance was between two points, but Noah could barely get a gauge on even distance between Omniverses at this moment.

In a corner of what was deemed Sector 1 of the Megalos Ynnere purely because this was where Noah's native Omniverse currently was and where his current vision was limited to, Noah was also able to hone in on another Omniverse where his body was in.

The Half Tainted Omniverse...and even the Omniverse of the Protectors!

These two were on opposite ends of Sector 1, with Noah currently not being able to judge their distance even though he knew their relative positions.

It was a unique experience as with this, Noah could effectively begin mapping the vastness of the Megalos Ynnere...so long as he continued to extend his vision past his current limitations.

And with such a tool, he could easily traverse from one Omniverse to another without wandering aimlessly!

But he knew this wasn't all.

This was a boon under his new System of Existence of Kuklos.

Brimming with a profound connection to the Wheel of Existence itself, there were still other wonders in store.

|On Omniverses where your weavings are present, the Cartographer's Gateways can be formed, with each one laid down in an Omniverse allowing instantaneous passage to all other connected Omniverses.|

Cartographer's Gateways!

It was a passage from one Omniverse to another.

But even though this weaving seemed dazzling, Noah felt like there was another, even more dazzling weaving in this terrifying new distinction.

|As the Cartographer of Existence, the Existential Displacement of Omniverses or other bodies of existence at a similar level is possible for an extremely high cost that your weavings of Existence will have to bear.|

...!

Existential Displacement.

Now that...seemed like a grandiose capability.

Noah focused on the weavings of such an ability as his vision of Sector 1 of the Megalos Ynnere only became clearer, his intent beginning to test things out right after.

'Begin the formation of the Cartographer's Gateway within Omniverse X-999...and the Half Tainted Omniverse.'

He began to test things out.

At the same time, he blinked- his vision changing from one landscape to another as within an instant, his main body opened its eyes within the depths of the Infinite Omniverse.

Another flash of light echoed from him and disappeared as outside of the Infinite Omniverse, within a section of the red and blue waters of Cappadocia, a golden robed Noah appeared.

His figure seemed ethereal and filled with radiance as in front of him, space began to distort while a colorless rift began to tear apart the surroundings- seeming as if existence itself was being torn!

At the same time.

Within the Fourth Mundi of Power.

A newly formed golden wheel was spinning while radiating pulses of golden light, waves of potent authority being burned by it as around this body of Noah, a colorless gold Quintessential Fabled Omniverse expanded around him to support the terrifying expenditure of the first Cartographer's Gateway!

A door that could lead from one Omniverse to another instantly.

This...was just part of Noah's boons as he added a fourth System of Existence to his Atlas.

He had made tremendous progress in the past days as after the Commonwealth made their entry into this Omniverse, he had since then filled up multiple new Sources of Existence, condensed a Fabled Omniverse and escaped the Existential Dominium Realm, and he even had another part of himself stirring waves within the Megalos Beyond!

His power...had spilled within the Fifth Echelon of Existence.

It was a good measure that seemed equivalent across the board, with the body of Savitar within the Half Tainted Omniverse having been at this stage, and Command Protector Valeria being at the same stage.

But Savitar's main body within the Megalos Beyond was likely on the Seventh Echelon of Existence as his power was a potent one!

But the traversal of this power across the Megalos was difficult as in a different Megalos than the one that you were born in, it seemed like one's power had to be raised again.

The disconnect between Matter, Dark Matter, and Anti-Matter was very real.

But even this...Noah was already beginning to merge Matter and Dark Matter to produce shocking effects as he was on his way.

Many more days from now, who knew what ridiculous height he would have reached?

When the rune of the Amarantos Dream of the Megalos was active again, what level of power would he gain even though this time, a single dream wouldn't last an instant?

The weavings of Invictus Maximus pulsed as even though Noah may not fully know, he could see possible glimpses.

And to arrive at the most pristine ones...causality would be twisted and distorted under his banner!

With an expectant gaze, he watched the formation of the first Cartographer's Gateway.

Within the same Sector 1 of the Megalos Ynnere.

An unknown distance away from where the first Cartographer's Gateway was forming, another one of Noah's bodies floated into the vessel termed the Omniversal Voyager as the eyes of Command Protector Valeria and Protector Lucretia never left him.

The Three Simian Protectors of the Equestris Protectors were looking towards him with scrutiny and disbelief as they saw the scene they just saw of this man before them melding with the surrounding Existence!
