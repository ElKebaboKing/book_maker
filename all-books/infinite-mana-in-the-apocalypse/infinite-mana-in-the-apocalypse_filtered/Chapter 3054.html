Chapter 3052 Greater II

The Records of the two Omniversal Pillagers he had already thoroughly analyzed!

This Entity.

As if these trillions of tendrils of light were coursing through the Omniverse and spreading a message.

HUUM!

An unfathomable Greater Omniversal Authority was on its way to recognize nobody else other than Noah as he couldn't be interrupted!
