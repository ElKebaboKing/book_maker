Chapter 3184 The Commonwealth! I

It was not still.

The massive Wheel spun gloriously as it radiated pulses of golden light towards the surroundings- Noah feeling these waves even from a distance as they seemed to be enriching all surrounding forms of Existence.

|You have entered an area of an Existential Domain with Absolute effects.|

|The Existential Ruling of the Wheel of Weaving seeks to impose itself on your Existence.|

|Your Source of Existence will circulate 20% faster in this domain, its expansion and strengthening further increased by 50%|

|In addition to this, the marking of the Wheel of Weaving will be applied to your current Existence- its location and status being constantly recorded.|

A boon and a tracking feature to come along with it.

Noah's eyes shone brightly as he began to analyze everything while floating closer towards the incandescent Wheel.

The massive pillars of crimson gold light that seemed to be like torches lit up everything as even from afar- Noah could see the monkey like figures in the millions floating towards the center of the Wheel.

The moment he neared.

Or truly, it was the moment the waves of golden light pulsing from the Wheel touched him.

Many auras gazed towards his direction as if they knew.

This was because moments later...

WAP!

A figure warped before his eyes.

White gold fur. A humanoid monkey face. A vibrant gold tail. The features weren't as regal or oppressive as Protector Antonio's, but this being held their own charm as they smiled and spoke toward Noah!

"You must be one of the Prime Citizens of this Omniverse. I shall be your Guide to the Commonwealth and this glorious Celebration of Omniversal Discovery. Please..."

The Guide pointed towards the pulsing Wheel of Weaving as Noah floated in freely while asking.

"Prime Citizen?"

"Mmm, any leader of the Factions of the newly discovered Omniverse gets this perk that allows you much more leeway and freedom across the workings of the Commonwealth. Observing your figure and based on the information passed down...you must be Prime Citizen Emperor Osmont?"

An air of profound confidence burned within the Guide as he spoke, Noah sensing tendrils of Authority equivalent to the stage of Autarchy from this being as his Source of Existence was still not easy to discern!

"That would be me. Is it protocol for millions of entrants to come into a newly discovered Omniverse" Noah's eyes were locked onto the scenes around the Wheel of Weavings as he asked.

Beside him, the Guide let out a smile as he shook his head.

"Only for large Omniverses filled with high levels of Existence and potential. More join in with the festivities of the Celebration of Omniversal Discovery as every time this occurs, a show gets to be put on for Entities from a variety of different Sources of Existence to express themselves. The main attraction of the Celebration of Omniversal Discovery- The Gladiators of the Omniverses!"

WAA!

The Gladiators of the Omniverses.

Literal sparks and waves of golden brilliance burst from the mouth of the Guide as he said these words.

"Those who are victorious and achieve high rankings as Gladiators of Omniverses...get to enjoy and revel in the Vaults of the Commonwealth as they can further propel their Existence forward. But to attain any high ranking and distinction, one must attain victory over multiple Entities with different Sources of Existence. Conceptual Systems of Existence like yours make up the majority, but then you have the variety of Existence coming in, as there are those with different Sources of Existence that you can only experience to understand. Overcome them and prove your System of Existence...and glory lies ahead."

...!

A chance to fight against other Entities from different Sources of Existence!

If outstanding enough, one could receive a high ranking that allowed them access to the Vaults of the Commonwealth.

Noah gazed at the Guide and the auras of many entities far ahead as he had to say it.

The Commonwealth were invaders, but they were truly good at what they did.

They invaded and began taking taking over and Omniverse, and yet every part of their approach made it seem like they were saving the Omniverse from a grave danger as thereafter...they were doing everything they could to include it in a more massive civilization!

"Please..."

They arrived at the massive entry point of the construct in the form of a wheel, this entry point alone being the size of a Cosmos as hundreds of thousands of uniquely designed vessels were already floating and moving around it.

Within them, Entities adorned in regal dresses and clothing at the Teracosm and Paracosm Equivalent Stages could be discerned, their eyes bright as if they were about to set off to visit a zoo.

Many rose their wills to observe Noah as if he had a marking above him that stated he was a native!

Seeing this scene, the Guide smiled as he took them in while explaining.

"The Citizens of the Commonwealth love nothing more than connecting with others of different Existences. Of course, matches of the Gladiators Omniverses also draw in a lot of us..."

As the Guide spoke, Noah fully entered the Wheel of Weavings as within it, it felt like one had stepped into an entirely different reality.

All sound seemed cut off as in the far distance, a massive golden portal could be seen to the left where Vessels constantly streamed out of- its weavings immeasurably stable as it was an established connection between this Omniverse and another.

And to the right...was something radiant with a crimson and golden glow that could only be described as a coliseum from pure dreams and fantasy!

"The Gladiators' Arena."

The Guide spoke succinctly as the aura of Autarchical Equivalent entities...could be felt towards that direction the most.

"The place that will be the center of all activities. Come, Prime Citizen Emperor Osmont, and let me show you the wonders of the Commonwealth."

Their figures warped towards the Gladiators' Arena, Noah keeping a close eye on everything as amidst all of this showy exterior, his inquisitive mind could see many things that were never explained!
