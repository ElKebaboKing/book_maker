The journey would be long as there were multiple stops to make.

The Pure Blooded Domains of the 5 Cardinal Royal Bloodlines stretched out with the Main Reality at the center, where one would progressively find more and more clusters of Realities as well as Grotto Sanctums the closer they were to the Main Reality.

This would also be accompanied by increasingly powerful forces, especially in the Main Reality where the past Bloodline Rulers of the Pure-Blooded Cardinal Royal Bloodlines took positions as Representatives of their Bloodlines in the Primordial Alliance!

In the past Era, there was the Olden Alliance that contained Representatives of Ancient Bloodlines among many others.

In this new Age and Era- there was the established Primordial Alliance that held the true power of the Primordials, with the past Bloodline Masters of Primordial Bloodlines being the Representatives who enjoyed great status in the Main Reality!

These beings ascended their duties as mere Bloodline Masters when they became LEGENDS, their pathway smoothly going forward to give them positions as Representatives.

Past these Realities...one came across Unexplored lands and Realities that over the countless years- the Primordials had continued expanding their reach into!

The Purple Endeavor that Brightborn currently led wasn't going towards the Main Reality where the core power of Primordials was concentrated, and it was actually making its way towards the opposite direction where unexplored Realities stood.

The purpose of this were the so called 'Guests' that Brightborn mentioned.

HUUUM!

"It is time."

OOOM!

His words reached to the depths of this Unstable Aether Domain that had snaking tendrils of aether, something actually reacting and beginning to float out towards the ship as a shockingly animalistic aura of a deadly beast pervaded outwards!

He was a monstrous genius from the Olden Era that had repressed himself until now, ready to explode with a shocking foundation and power as a mere wave of his hands seemed capable of releasing arcs of Aether that were comparable to the might of a LEGEND!

His gaze was apathetic as he walked past Brightborn and into the Purple Endeavor, his eyes landing on everyone here as they screamed of arrogance and power- and he could truly back up such a gaze!

He gazed at everyone as if they were mere children before his eyes, about to flash towards the descending purple gold stairs to isolate himself in the lower decks until this vessel arrived at the Unexplored Grotto Sanctum when his eyes happened to pass over the visage of none other than Adjudicator Katelyn.

HOOONG!

Lustrous light bloomed from his eyes as a trace of shock appeared, which then turned into greed and excitement as this shocking being turned into a streak of light towards her in an instant!
