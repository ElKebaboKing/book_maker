Chapter 3376 The Wheel Turns As The Wheel Turns! V

|I brought you to this Primal Eldritch Palace of the Expanse not just to localize you here and lead you to different regions of the Megalos Beyond from here, but also to see if it would be viable for you to grasp an opportunity.|

The Great Mother radiated her intent as Ozymandias listened closely, delving into a Blancverse shimmering with obsidian radiance as massive pulsing tentacles stretched throughout, and obsidian Nests and singularities could be seen anchored in different regions as terrifying auras of Outerversal Morphons were contained within.

In the short while that they had been moving, a figure of an old man appeared in front of them as it was none other than the Observer of Old and Young.

He nodded towards Ozymandias and the Great Mother as he came to follow beside them calmly.

As if he merely wanted to continue being an Observer!

The Great Mother laughed at this and continued.

|In this Primal Eldritch Palace of the Expanse, there is an Existence Severing Great One whose body allows for unique means of increasing complexity. Inside her, the shackles of the slow grind of complexity are loosened greatly as she severed them to a tremendous degree, and any methodology of increasing complexity carried out inside her for the first time will yield terrifying results. If she allows you to step inside of her Severed Existence, we may be able to give you yet another bump in your elevation of complexity to welcome you to the Expanse.|

...!

Her words were shocking ones as Ozymandias listened closely while moving forward.

The murky obsidian region of this Blancverse was slowly showing the massive tentacles flowing like roots at this moment, and the Great Mother floated down to land on a particular tentacle while the rest followed.

Thereafter, a tentacle emerged from her body to form into a horror inducing palm- a palm that touched the massive tentacle below as she spoke!

|The Great Mother seeks an audience with the Great Segregator.|

WAA!

A simple intent that traversed across the tentacle!

And a few seconds after...

HUUM!

An obsidian light flashed that wrapped around their bodies and began pulling them forward- their figures sinking deeper into the pulsating Blancverse thereafter.

Weavings of Outerversal Authority embedded and flowed like a deluge of a black sea as within seconds, their figures slowed down and appeared in an isolated domain.

At the center of this domain, an oscillating obsidian flower floated calmly, the bud of this flower showing an ancient face with filled solemnity as millions of tentacles budded off of it like rose buds.

Its aura was a heavy one as it seemed to exist on its own volition of existence, with its ancient eyes gazing forth at those before it.

|You rarely seek others out, Great Mother. For you to be here...this is for the supposed Instrument of the Beyonder you have found?|

...!

News traveled fast.

Especially when it was related to myths and legends as from one Pantheon to another!

The tentacles of the Great Mother swirled with power as they bent down in slight respect.

|Great Segregator, Netherflower, I came to bid for the Instrument of the Beyonder to increase the level of his complexity for a time within your Severed Domain. Given his accomplishments, I have no doubt that the Great Segregator will also benefit greatly from it.|

...!

Yes.

Her Severed Domain was a unique one as after she tested it cycles ago, it was found that any other entity undergoing great change in their complexity within her Domain faced much fewer restrictions within their existence, and there was a feedback of complexity to the Great Segregator as well!

All she had to do was allow entry and provide the space, and she even stood to benefit from it. And yet...

|What if it is not what you say? What if the gains are minimal for me having to spend a decent amount of effort to open up that Severed Domain?|

A cruel light passed in the eyes of the swaying obsidian flower as the Great Mother answered as if she had expected this part of her nature.

|If I am wrong, the Great Segregator can devour half of my Complexity of Existence.|

...!

A terrifying cost!

But the Great Mother was that confident as beside her, the face of the Observer of Old and Young changed while Ozymandias became stern.

A jagged, cruel laughter erupted from the flower ahead as its face began to open, revealing an endless obsidian stretch of radiance as its intent echoed out.

|I will take that offer. But I am not cruel- I look up to the Great Beyonder as well. You can both enter- if his weavings are as explosive as you say, then even you will be able to benefit from his rise in complexity. If it is nothing to talk about...then I will move to honor your offer.|

Half of her complexity was on the line.

And yet the Great Mother was unfettered as she beckoned to Ozymandias, the two of them being pulled towards the unfurled obsidian flower and flowing into it as they entered a terrifying space.

The moment Ozymandias passed through the mouth of the flower, he felt as if a buzzing weight and noise that was there the entire time faded away.

He felt lighter.

As if there were many things that bogged him down, and this space he was in isolated all of that!

Not entirely, but by a great deal.

|The unique Severed Domain of the Great Segregator. Here, a rise in complexity is many times more potent and faster than outside of it. Just do what you would normally do without worries.|

Beside him, the Great Mother seemed to be smiling with her tentacles as she spoke, Ozymandias gazing at her and the great lengths she was willing to go as he began to think.

When it came to a rise in complexity of the weavings of existence, there were a few means.

But at this moment, he could only think of one thing.

Inside of him, a certain rune was shimmering with brightness once more as, after all this time, the Amarantos Dream of the Megalos was ripe once more!

He could dream.

But he didn't know how terrifying the dream would be if it was being done in a space that Severed the weavings of the outside existence entirely!
