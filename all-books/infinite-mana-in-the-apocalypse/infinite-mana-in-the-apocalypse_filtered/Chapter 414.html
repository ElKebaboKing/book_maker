Chapter 413 - Agent of Chaos

The description of the <<Herald of Chaos>> skill was similar to the opposing skills that he obtained some time back from the Celestials, only having a few differences from <<Herald of Fate>>!

<strong><<Herald of Chaos>> :: An inheritance from the Disciple of Chaos that holds tremendous power. The mysteries of the Supreme Law of Chaos can be slowly understood while this technique is in use, understanding what true chaos is as the user moves to not just create more chaos, but to control it. Abilities include Chaos Discernment, Chaos Traverser, Chaos Concealment, and Chaos Harvester.</strong>

<strong>[Chaos Discernment] :: The clouded and abstruse movements of chaos of different entities or beings became easier to see. The Agent of Chaos can then decide how to use this information. Strict conditions must be met for any usable information to be discerned.</strong>

<strong>[Chaos Traverser] :: As one that is proficient in the workings of the law of chaos, you have more of a leeway over the chaos around you and those you are connected to . This ability allows you to traverse towards the optimum chaos that will grant you the most power.</strong>

<strong>[Chaos Concealment] :: The users chaos can be made hard to decipher, where others will not be able to find exactly where chaos will lead you.</strong>

<strong>[Chaos Harverstor] :: An ability given to the Herald of Chaos that allows them to grant chaos to others while reducing chaos from themselves or others. One must use it sparingly as the distribution of chaos is harder to control than expected. </strong>

The description of the abilities were very similar to the ones from the <<Herald of Fate>>, where one could even argue they seemed like a simple copy and paste! But the two laws were counter parts of one another, and having equivalent abilities would only allow Noah to see both sides of the equation, being able to discern both Fate and Chaos.

But his gaze was on the offensive and defensive skills similar to [Fates Guardian], these ones called [Agent of Chaos]! This was where he had obtained the skill he just used to incapacitate the movements of the huge Fire Behemoth, the skill called [Essence Disruptor]!

<strong>SOUL [Agent of Chaos](0/60) :: The title of an Agent of anything is a rare one. This skill tree gives its user the ability to confidently call themselves an Agent of Chaos. Abilities include Essence Disruptor(0/5), Chaotic Mandate(0/5), Chaos Orbs(0/5), and Void of Impending Doom(0/5).</strong>

<strong>[Essence Disruptor](0/5) :: The Chaos Particles under your control heed your call as they go on to bind and disrupt the essence and movements of the enemies that fall upon your Chaos Field. </strong>

<strong>[Chaotic Mandate](0/5) :: The user is covered with a mandate of chaos, the Chaos Field surrounding them transfusing with their skin to adorn them in a Chaos Armament. This mandate allows the Agent of Chaos to stand as a true being capable of reigning down unmatched retribution. +100% Chaos Damage, +100% Chaos Resistance, +50% Elemental Damage, +50% Defense, +50% Attack, Cast, and Movement Speed, +50% of all Damage converted to Chaos Damage. </strong>

<strong>[Chaos Orbs](0/5) :: Destructive orbs of chaos are called upon, being released upon your enemies as an area of attack ability. Each of the orbs carry with them the explosive force of a disordered star. Affected area- 50 meters.</strong>

<strong>[Void of Impending Doom](0/5) :: A void of chaos is focused on one particular enemy, this void installing the Fear and Oppressed Effect onto the enemy and disordering their mental state before deadly Doom Spikes rain down.</strong>

A set of beautifully described skills were laid out in front of his eyes, and he started by testing out [Essence Disruptor] first, the more than 45,000 Chaos Particles under his control permeating through the body of the Fire Behemoth as it tried to will it essence and muscles to move, but it was rendered unable to!

The Emperor Penguin watched this scene where his master incapacitated a monstrous Soul Forging Behemoth the moment he entered, being taken out of his stupor when he heard the calm command ring out in his ears.

"Attack."

OOOH!

The Harbingers of Sin and the Pureblood Vampyres became pumped at the show of power, moving with even more vigor as their attacks smashed into the Fire Behemoth who could not even use its essence to defend itself, Noahs clone remaining on top of the huge iguana looking beast as he continued to pour mana into the [Essence Disruptor] skills to allow the Chaos Particles in his Chaos Field that wrapped around the entirety of this huge beast to continue disrupting its essence.

With his power, this was the extent of what he could do currently, which was a terrifying thought to imagine as he cooly stopped the movements of a powerful Normal Behemoth!

He did not cast the other terrifying sounding skills like [Chaos Orbs] or [Void of Impending Doom] which would most likely wipe away everything on site, choosing to let his subordinates finish off this Behemoth and not steal their kill.

ROAAR!

A weakened roar of unwillingness was let out from the Fire Behemoth as it faced a situation where it could not even move, horrible attacks reigning down that soon took its life!

BOOM!

Its body slumped to the ground as the Order of Blood cheered, the many team members glancing towards Noah with shining eyes as they dawned a Behemoth so easily with his intrusion of a few seconds.

"Master!"

The eyes of the Emperor Penguin were shining as he and the fervent looking Elena were the first to step forward, Noah nodding towards them as he waved his hands to the right of the Behemoth, essence converging as a portal slowly began to form.

The sleek penguin came to the side of the Fire Behemoth as it used its spatial abilities to put away the Cores and Skill Books that dropped from it as loot to be divided for later, waving its flippers towards the mountainous body of the beast itself thereafter as it also disappeared into the Emperors Penguins own spatial inventory skill.

OOOM!

The portal finished forming as silently in the explored Northern Region of the Celestial realm, Noah came to collect the rest of his subordinates for them to begin learning the Inheritance of the Disciple of Fire, the closest plans he currently had dealing with the beasts of the Spiritual Land the most as he prepared for something that he considered a practice run for his future plans.

The thing he considered a practice run was fulfilling the requirements of upgrading the Spiritual Land to the 9th Tier, where he needed to get his hands on the core of a Small World!
