Chapter 1432: A Reforged Ascendancy Halo! II

Within the Cosmic Holy Land.

The figure of Hyperion could be seen as with a gaze of slight disbelief, he watched as his own Origin stepped onto the path of the Cosmic Filament Realm with utter ease.

A single wave of a certain being's hands had given him a single Universal-sized crystal as now, he elevated himself onto a stage he wouldn't have thought possible if not for the passage of a significant number of years!

All of this...was made possible by a single being. As the one-thousandth Cosmos formed within his Origin, Hyperion gazed towards the titanous figure of Noah as his emotions towards this existence were complex, but ultimately a single emotion won out of all of them. This emotion was admiration.

At a certain point, anyone who had closely seen Noah's work could only stare in admiration as they had to admit- this being was just built different!

Yet even amidst the admiration, abundant shock would always come as while gazing towards his direction, Hyperion saw Noah's eyes open with a domineering light as his illusory Ascendancy Halo began to pulse with light.

"He didn't..." Hyperion muttered under his breath as he watched with widening eyes as Noah's incomplete Ascendancy Halo pulsed with increasing frequency as in the next second, an illustrious crimson light flashed as another section was added to this massive ring that was made of complex clusters of runic lines!

In shock, an Ender of Reality watched as a being younger than it by millions of years went forward to complete their Ascendancy Halo, the means he used being known only to him as it was a stellar sight to see nonetheless.

BZZZT!

With wild abandon and fervor, Noah called upon the Flames of the Sanguine Emperor to fuse the Bloodlines of the Resplendent Treasure Emperor, Unbridled Emperor, Golden Emperor, Savanna, Vestige....all of the newly gained Bloodlines as in consequence, their Flames were drawn into the incomplete Halo!

To this day, Noah did not know the full extent and use of this Halo as it was something he had little information about, the memories of Cecilia helping shed the light on its true usage as it wasn't merely something to fuse with.

Even the description he read before mentioned that its uses were still being quantified, with the details of the Ascendancy Halo ever changing in some aspects as something like the number of passively forged Splendiferous Universes daily had already changed from the 1 Billion Noah saw before to an even higher number!

The Halo was a collection of the Flames of Edicts- unique authorities that provided Reality altering capabilities that one could open with the Essence of Reality. The connection of such Flames into a Halo should give a fantastical effect far more than what Noah had found so far, and even more so for his Halo as it was the complete fusion of those Edicts through their Bloodlines.

OOOM!

One after another, he observed his vast and expansive Origin that contained the Bloodlines fuse them serenely under the authority of the Sanguine Emperor, their Flames joining to complete his Ascendancy Halo as the crimson light it released only became more and more oppressive!

A Halo's strength relied on the Edicts that made it, and nobody could deny the quality of Noah's Edicts as a single Insuperable level Edict and 8 Quasi-Insuperable Nomological Edicts were now dwelling at the base of this Halo. Its power was still unranked as after its completion, Noah could freely optimize it and fuse more Edicts into it while even removing lesser ones that didn't contribute much to its power.

There were many grand plans for the future as Noah watched on calmly while the Flames of Edicts weaved into the unique Halo of Authority, the incomplete crimson ring gradually closing in on itself as in the next moment- the last piece of the puzzle came into place!

CLANG!

As if a gate was closing, the metallic sound of an indomitable Halo becoming complete resounded out as momentarily, it released a blinding crimson light that turned the entire Cosmic Holy Land red.

"..."

An austere silence descended down as a complete halo pulsed around Noah calmly, spinning and releasing waves of crimson light as it caused an oppressive pressure to be felt all around! The authority of the Reality Overseer was mostly clustered around this halo at this moment, Hyperion's incomplete halo barely having any of this authority left as one could see what the passive will of this Reality had chosen!

Noah himself stared closely at this mysterious halo as bursts of information entered his mind, his gaze going towards the number of his Ascendancy Value that was currently updated in real-time on his status panel, seeing a beautiful 85 at this moment as he was ridiculously approaching the Second Firmament of Ascendancy in such a ridiculously short period of time!

OOM!

The changes were many. He needed time to adjust to them and quantify them!

His origin was also coming to settle down as the number of his forged Cosmos approached 2 million, Noah having already begun on a second Shard of a Seed of Reality as for this one, he didn't completely devour it.

He absorbed it slowly and for a good reason as only a small section of the fantastical crystal that looked like a piece of Reality remained, Noah stopping his absorption at this time as he stared towards the direction of a bound Champion of Reality.

This existence was still unconsciously bound by flames of Quasi-Insuperable Edicts as Noah's close had his hands analyzing the Origin and soul of Aegon, this process coming to a stop as Noah had obtained as much information as he needed on Primordial Epitaphs and how to forge his own System!

If he had the required Essence of Reality right now, he would even forge it right away! But he barely had any of this unique resource as the answer to all this...was the figure of Aegon before him and the two existences currently waiting to see an injured figure of Cecilia for them to intercept.

For this, Noah schemed and planned as he looked at the nearly used Shard of a Seed of Reality as he smiled devilishly, a Domain of Apocalypse slowly erupting from him and covering a large portion of the Cosmic Holy Land as the Flames around the Champion of Reality began to be loosened!
