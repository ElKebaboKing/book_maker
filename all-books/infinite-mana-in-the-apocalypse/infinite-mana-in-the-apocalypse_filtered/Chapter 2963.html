Chapter 2961 Legacies and Seeds III

Were any of their Existential Authorities Quintessential?

This question came and went just as quickly as the Basileus of the Legacy of Al-Abalem didn't put it in their hearts.

Each of those here were of the Neonate Stage of the Existential Dominium Realm as unlike the Corrupted Seed above them, they had been lucky enough to devour and assimilate Fruits of Existence over the years!

Their Soul Dominiums had annealed and grown much more as they knew they were much stronger than this being above them, but Basileus Moraine had still sought to prevent a flash because eventually...they knew that even a weak entity at the Neonate Stage of the Existential Dominium Realm was not to be so easily dismissed.

A Dominium was a heavy thing as anyone that had reached this stage had exploded with an accumulation of extremes and existence, and if they burned their Dominium when pushed to the brink...the power they could unleash before they perished was dreadful.

So, Basileus Moraine already knew what to do as she stared coldly at the spreading light of the Colorless Haven.

|Spread out and drain his Dominium. When he starts to burn his existence, increase the proximity and finish him off without any injuries.|

Her intent spread out at an extremely fast speed that was akin to the span of an instant as the Basileus around her had already begun moving- this single instant not even passing yet.

Basileus Moraine waved her hands thereafter as within them, weapons that would make one pause appeared.

They seemed like...firearms. Sleek and white with a fervent Existential brilliance, they exceeded any High Grade Armament of Extremity as they seemed entirely identical- with Basileus Moraine holding onto the grip of two handguns!

The magazine was already in as it held heart shaking weapons within, with the barrels of both of these guns inscribed with the markings that read {A Boom Box Production}!

Gripping them tightly in her hands, Basileus Moraine already raised both hands to aim ahead as the visage of the Corrupted Seed was within her gaze, the Colorless light reaching their surroundings at this moment as her intent moved to direct the flow of battle once more.

|Keep him at bay and finish him off cleanly. Show what it means to make an enemy out of the Legacy of Al-Ab-|

SAA!

Space fizzled.

Her words didn't finish as she instantly felt her Dominium Soul retreat around her body, her eyes no longer seeing the figure of the Corrupted Seed in the distance.

Instead...a voice shockingly rang out beside her very ears.

|You don't seem to understand.|

...!

Her pupils dilated.

The Existential Armaments in her hands quickly began to turn as shockingly, to her left, the visage of the Corrupted Seed with his form of an Infinity symbol actually appeared, somehow bypassing their spread out Dominium Souls!

This was because similar to her, even those in the surroundings felt their Dominiums forced to retreat around their bodies, only covering them inches all around as this allowed their enemy to get close!

An entire Haven radiating an aura of a Dominium seeking to invade her protective Dominium Soul!

And yet...
