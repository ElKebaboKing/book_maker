Chapter 777 - Nothing better than counting loot!

It was a shocking scene to come across in the isolated Life Realm as only 4 beings floated in the skies, all around them bloody chunks of beings and scintillating lights of loot being the only things one could see.

Anna was mounting her Four Winged Unicorn as she stared at the scene around her in a stupor, where even after she watched it play out in real time she still could not help but be shocked at it!

The three PRISMATIC Summons were just now reducing in size as Noah stopped pumping them with Dao essence, their bodies having terrifying looks as they seemed to be bathed in blood.

This was especially so for the Obsidian Panther that actually had the highest kill count, this speedy cat taking down 7 GALAXY rank experts itself as it was simply too fast and deadly!

Even Noah had only taken down 3 more Aileron Royals after reaping the life of Prince Kirigan, his Animus Summons doing the rest as the Colossal Helios Leviathan and the Void Serpent acted in a brutal fashion to crush or devour their enemies.

The end result was the entire party from the Primal Winged Expanse being wiped out, where only Noah remained in the skies with his body bathed in the blood of enemies he pulverized by throwing around a Galaxy!

He stared at the surroundings as his eyes shone while looking at all the loot around him. After the death of Kirigan, he let the loot of the defeated or exploding enemies remain in the skies as a magical scene very few would come across was playing itself in front of his eyes at this moment.

It was the scene of numerous loot items shining with immense l.u.s.ter all around him, ranging from cores to skill books and even Dao Crystals!

The Dao Crystals were even more eye catching as they seemed to be releasing a wondrous red pillar of treasure light, seemingly screaming it was high tiered loot as it could be distinguished clearly from the blue glimmering GALAXY Cores.

As his gaze passed over them, he sighed with emotion as he waved his hands to count and collect everything he had obtained.

The boon this time was simply enormous, with the targets delivering themselves to him being 20 beings in the GALAXY Realm, with one third of them being in the Middle Tier!

This meant that he had obtained a great deal of Low and Middle Tier GALAXY Cores, and his mind was already working overtime on how to best use these cores.

For him to go from QUASAR into the Low Tier GALAXY Realm, he had to absorb the cores of all the Elder Fiends he took down as well as cores from multiple Low Tier GALAXY Realm Wild Animus in this Secret Realm! So when he thought of advancing to the Middle Tier, he truly wasnt sure if even the cores of the 20 GALAXY Realm experts would be enough to do so.

But he was still in the Secret Realm, and there were a great deal of Wild Animus he could still farm for cores to be used by him and his subordinates, so he didnt stay on this thought too much!

Aside from the many skill books that he would go through later for anything unique, it was the Dao Crystals that occasionally erupted from the dying enemies that made him even more excited.

From the 18 experts he had killed after he changed the Affix to [Slain enemies have a 10% chance to drop Dao Crystals based on the Dao they cultivate], only 5 had triggered the affix!

Even this was extremely lucky as it was a 1 in 10 chance of it ever occurring, where if it happened twice it would be considered great.

But the glimmering Dao Crystals that he collected made him truly smile as from his calculations, he saw that if one were to absorb all of them...they would be able to gain over 30% in the Lesser Dao of Withering!

That was the same gain from a Protagonists Bookmark, the capability to learn one third of a Dao in an instant...

And this time, it wasnt just limited to Noah as he could actually choose to have others absorb the Dao Crystals and gain the comprehension instead.

And if he were to lets say...come in conflict with the Primal Winged Expanse after killing their 20 Aileron Royals, would he not get a chance to take down more of them as he farmed even more Dao Crystals?

Didnt this mean he could quickly farm a type of loot that would allow him to gain 100% Comprehension of a Dao in a relatively short period of time? Loot that also wasnt just restricted to him?!

Many thoughts crossed his mind at this moment as he simply waved his hands and put everything away.

He then turned to Anna on the side as he spoke with a light smile.

"So, we just killed your future husband and the entire delegation of the Primal Winged Expanse. Its going to be wild when we get out of the secret realm."

"..."

Anna looked towards him as her eyes shone with a sharp light, seemingly thinking about a lot as she replied with a strong voice.

"Grandfather will have to make a choice. And...I feel like you would enjoy the outcome of conflict anyways..."

"Then lets go deeper!"

RUMBLE!
