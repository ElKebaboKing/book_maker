Chapter 4647: THE Farewell of Ginnungagap II

Who didnt love opening gifts?

Ever since entering dungeons on the Blue Planet and opening treasure chests, obtaining the Concept of Loot and gaining access to Loot Crates, Loot Caches, and other wondrous forms of treasure, Noah felt giddy whenever opening any box of rewards.

The anticipation of what might be inside never got old.

The possibility of something extraordinary always made his heart race just a little faster.

And this particular gift was uniquely special as it stemmed from THE Primeval Titan, Ulmoreth himself!

The one whod taught him The First Tongue.

The one whod sacrificed his Way of Primordial Continuity to save Noah from THE Primordial Chaos grasp.

Obsidian-gold light shone from the top of this chest as Noah placed his Primus Mana into it.

And shockingly, the box began to react with building radiance.

The light intensified as complex Weavings activated across its surface.

Gradually, an illusory obsidian-gold image began to emerge.

The form solidified into the towering figure of THE Primeval Titan himself, Ulmoreth!

WAA!

Eight hundred meters tall even in this manifestation, his body constructed from living hairy stone and pulsing with ancient power.

His eyes glowed with the same warm intensity Noah remembered.

Noah looked at him with shining eyes as this manifestation looked back and smiled sadly.

The expression held both pride and regret.

|I wish I could have given you the rest of the lessons in person, but... circumstances must have proven to be impossible, so here we are.|

The manifestations voice resonated in The First Tongue directly, each word a pulse of structured Mana.

|I talked with you a great deal about Phonemes and a bit about Logos during our brief time together, but I didnt really get a chance to delve into Philology. I taught you the letters and the words of The First Tongue, but I didnt teach you the sentences. I didnt teach you the grammar that allows those sentences to reshape Existence itself.|

Ulmoreths manifestation raised one massive hand as blue-gold light began gathering around it.

|Let me explain what Philology truly is. Youve learned the eighteen Core Phonemes. Youve formed Logos by combining those Phonemes with intent. But Philology... Philology is the deepest grammatical structure that governs how Logos themselves form.|

The manifestation began pacing as if this were still a classroom lesson.

|Think of it this way. Phonemes are individual letters. Logos are words formed from those letters. But Philology...Philology is the syntax. The underlying rules that determine what is possible within The First Tongue. Not individual words, but the fundamental structure that allows words to exist and have meaning.|

Noah listened with absolute focus as his teacher continued.

|I wont ramble on too much but...when you form a Logos, you combine Phonemes in a specific order with specific intent. But the order matters tremendously. The same Phonemes arranged differently create entirely different effects. This is syntax. The relationship between the Phonemes determines the ultimate meaning.|

Ulmoreth manifested several Phonemes in the air.

Fire, Water, and Beginning floated before Noahs vision.

|Watch closely. If I arrange these as: Beginning, Fire, Water...|

The Phonemes aligned and pulsed with specific rhythm.

A small flame appeared that gradually transformed into steam.

|The meaning is: What begins as fire becomes water. A transformation from one state to another.|

Then the Phonemes rearranged themselves.

|But if I arrange them as: Fire, Beginning, Water...|

The same Phonemes now pulsed with different rhythm.

Steam appeared first, then condensed into water, which erupted into flame.

|Now the meaning is: Fire initiates waters beginning. The causal relationship has completely reversed. Same components, entirely different effect. This is the power of syntax.|

Noahs eyes widened as he absorbed the lesson.

|Logos can also be conjugated to change their properties. You can modify tense to make effects occur in past, present, or future. You can adjust intensity to make them weak or devastatingly strong. You can alter scope to make them affect a single target or the entire scope of Existence.|

BOOM!

The manifestation demonstrated by taking a simple Logos of Fire and showing how subtle variations in the pulse patterns changed it from a small flame to an inferno, from a current blaze to a future ignition, from a localized effect to an existential one.

|And just as words combine into sentences, Logos can combine into compound structures. Multiple Logos woven together create complex sentences that achieve sophisticated effects impossible with single words alone.|

Ulmoreths expression became more serious.

|What you need to understand is that each Sovereignty youve encountered - Storm, Hunger, Order, Chaos - these are essentially dialects of The First Tongue. Specialized vocabularies optimized for specific expressions but inherently limited in versatility. You...someone like you and what you stand for as master of The First Tongue...can speak all dialects fluently and even create new ones.|

He paused before continuing.

|Ginnu Lifeforms like myself instinctively know The First Tongue because we emerged when it was being formed. Were native speakers. Others must consciously learn it like a foreign language. But you, Noah... you can surpass all of us. You could reach the same heights as...them.|

The manifestations eyes glowed brighter.

|The progression path is straightforward. First, you must recognize that Mana is language, not just energy. Youve already accomplished this. Second, you learn Phonemes by understanding individual Mana pulses as distinct units. Youve mastered all eighteen Core Phonemes. Third, you form simple Logos by combining Phonemes with intent. Youve been doing this.|

Ulmoreth raised four massive fingers.

|Fourth, you master Logos structure to achieve what we call Singularity Principles. These are refined expressions that embody complete concepts. Fifth, you comprehend Philology to fully access Genesis Principles. This is what Im teaching you now. And finally, sixth, you learn to speak in complete sentences to reshape Existence at its most fundamental levels.|

The manifestation gestured grandly.

|Genesis Principles are different from Singularity Principles. A Singularity Principle is a perfected expression of a single concept. But a Genesis Principle... a Genesis Principle is the underlying structure that allows entire categories of concepts to exist. Its the difference between understanding fire and understanding the nature of transformation itself.|

Noah felt his mind expanding as he processed this information.

|I will now teach you the Philology of my three Genesis Principles. I will pass down what I hold to you. Watch carefully and listen with your entire existence, not just your ears.|

The manifestation of Ulmoreth raised both hands as THE Civilizational Authority of Primordial Continuity blazed around him.

He began writing in the air with blue-gold light.

Complex Logos formed, far more intricate than anything Noah had seen before!
