Chapter 4859: The Crack Of Observable Existence I

THE Mouseion of Fated Artifacts was a unique place.

When Noah arrived, he found himself standing on a golden river that seemed to be flowing forward endlessly. The current moved beneath his feet without disturbing his balance, carrying him nowhere while simultaneously carrying him everywhere.

All around him was a stellar environment that held sparkling multicolored lights etched across the expanse as if they were stars scattered by a guiding hand. They burned with colors that shouldnt have coexisted, crimson beside azure beside gold beside shades that had no names in any language.

Each of these supposed stars had strings they were connected to.

These strings extended outward to the edge of perception itself, gossamer threads of fate that linked artifacts to destinies, treasures to those worthy of claiming them. The entire Mouseion was a web of predetermined connections, a place where what was meant to be found would be found by those meant to find it.

Behind him, the figure of Glossikos looked at him with an inquisitive gaze.

"All of us study and revere The First Tongue."

Her voice was musical.

"It is rare to find someone like you who calls themselves THE Tongue Whisperer. One who has mastered what we spend eons merely attempting to comprehend."

She gestured toward the stellar expanse around them.

Her wooden features held something like pride.

"If anyone is fated for anything in here, they release a glimmer of their Civilization. If they are fated with anything, it comes down for them. The strings part. The barriers dissolve. What is meant to be claimed descends into waiting hands."

She paused.

"We tried to contribute ourselves and found that we gained merit points whenever we added things to this place that are claimed. The method to do that is right there."

She pointed toward an area to the side of the golden river.

An elevated pedestal rose from the flowing current, its base somehow anchored despite the endless movement beneath it. Atop the pedestal was a swirling golden platform that churned with authority similar to the river itself but concentrated, focused, purposeful.

It looked like an altar.

An altar where offerings could be made to fate itself.

Hah!

Noah walked up to it as his treasure chest followed behind him, the blue-gold container hovering faithfully at his side. He came to stand before the elevated pedestal on the edge of the golden river, and the moment he stood before it, information flowed into his existence.

|THE Mouseion of Fated Artifacts is a place where merit can be granted depending on anything contributed|

|The merit is awarded only when what has been contributed is claimed by the one that is fated|

|If nobody fated claims it, no merit will be awarded|

He didnt waste too much time.

He proceeded to wave his hands, and the treasure chest behind him came forward at his command. It floated over the golden river, past his position, and landed on the golden platform with weight that made the pedestal tremble.

The Antinomia Prismatica.

The uninfected core of THE Primordial Paradox.

Hidden inside layers of Undifferentiated Fate and Philologies and Archai of The First Tongue.

Waiting to be claimed by the one fated to receive it.

Waiting to be claimed by Noah himself, eons from now!

Everything shimmered in golden light.

The platform blazed with radiance that painted the stellar environment in shades of pure authority. The strings that connected the countless stars above trembled as if acknowledging the arrival of something significant. Something that would change the nature of fate itself within this space.

A golden string fell down from the endless skyscape above.

It descended with purpose, with inevitability, with the weight of destiny given physical form. The string connected with the treasure chest, wrapping around it like a gentle embrace from existence itself.

Moments later, the chest got pulled up.

It rose from the pedestal, following the golden string toward the stellar expanse above. Higher and higher it climbed, becoming smaller and smaller until it was just another point of light among countless others.

Then it disappeared into oblivion.

Gone!

Hidden among the infinite artifacts of THE Mouseion of Fated Artifacts.

Noah didnt feel any sense of panic as he watched it disappear.

He knew where it was going. He knew who would claim it. He knew that the fated recipient was himself, and that the fate binding it to him was a fate he had created through his own choices.

He turned around calmly.

The figure of the future Absolute, Glossikos, raised her brows. She looked at him with an expression that mixed curiosity and confusion in equal measure.

"Do you not wish to release the light of your Civilization to see if you have anything fated in here?"

Most beings who contributed to THE Mouseion immediately checked to see if anything was fated for them in return. It was common courtesy to oneself. A chance at claiming treasures that destiny had prepared.

At such words, Noah smiled.

"I will come claim what is fated for me later on."

His voice held knowing that transcended what should have been possible.

"Who knows. Perhaps at that point in time, you will also be there to witness it."

BOOM!

He said these words cryptically.

He knew his other body in the distant, distant future was with Glossikos at this very moment. That version of himself was entering THE Mouseion, preparing to claim something that he believed destiny had prepared for him.

And it had.

Just not in the way anyone would expect.

But the Glossikos here shook her head as if she was staring at a crazy person.

Noah didnt explain.

He took a step backward, leaving the domain of THE Mouseion without even bothering to look around. The stellar environment faded. The golden river disappeared beneath his feet. The countless strings of fate retreated from his perception.

He returned to The Thermae of Cyclic Refinement!
