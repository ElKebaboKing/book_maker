Chapter 5173: Hey, Look At Us! I

While the fires of his retribution claimed the floating landmasses of THE Wyld and the Primordial Architects who had sought a Civilizational Holy War were reduced to insignificant drifts of ash, the focus of Noah remained divided across the vast distances of his reach.

Around this same time, he was focusing a significant portion of his consciousness that had traversed the profound connection toward Ubergulden Adelheid, and he was currently flooding her existence with a tidal wave of Quintessence Infiniforce that defied the previous limits of their shared bond.

Inside the intricate and golden weavings of her existence, the manifestations of Ubergulden Adelheid and Noah stood at the center of a surging multicolored flood, the density of the Infiniforce so great that it threatened to drown the ambient Observable Force of her domain.

Ubergulden Adelheid gazed at him with a look that remained heavy and cold, yet her expression shifted into a silent and narrow focus as she observed a fundamental change in his presence.

She witnessed the transition as the cold and imperious gaze of the Absolute began to thaw, the crystalline blue light of his lemniscate pupils receding into the depths of his eyes to be replaced by the sharp and dark calmness and amusement.

As the last remnants of the clinical void vanished from his features, Noah looked at her and offered a brief and measured cough to clear the heavy silence of the chamber before he finally spoke.

"Apologies, I had... a little episode there and initiated a process that required a certain degree of cognitive exclusion."

Ubergulden Adelheid did not immediately soften her posture, and her gaze remained clinical and cold as she looked him up and down while processing the return of his vibrant and tyrannical personality.

"I put a lot on the line for you, and I have risked the status of my House and my own continuation," she said, her voice carrying the sharp edge of a being who had been genuinely unsettled by his recent transformation.

"Can you not be trusted to remain himself? Has the weight of Infinity already begun to affect you in this manner, and will it happen randomly during the most critical moments of our ascent? I need to know if we are still able to have our arrangement, and perhaps more importantly, I need to know why...you are still flooding my existence with this intensity of Infinity if you have truly returned to yourself."

Noah met her flood of questions with a calm and unhurried composure, his figure radiating a sense of grounded power that felt even more dangerous than the cold apathy that had preceded it.

"Our...arrangement can certainly continue as this was not some episodic madness that will happen again, but rather a singular reboot of my system. I just made a major jump in power and had to reconfigure my foundations to account for the new architecture, and in the end, this development is quite good for you because my current standing should likely exceed the Transitional Cambrian stage between THE Second Scale and THE Third Scale."

"As for why I have not stopped this flow of Infinity, the answer is simply that since the process has begun, there is no...logical reason to stop it.
