Why was it so hard to replace an Absolute?

THE Civilizational Scale of Existence, or THE Level One Scale of Existence, held entities whose Ways held a level of differentiation.

Someone following the Civilization of Elemental, at THE Fundamental Depth more often than not, could also expand their existence to encompass the Way of Fire or the Way of Air. They could branch out, diversify, build subsidiary paths that complemented their main journey.

But ultimately, if and when they attained Absolute, it would be with their singular Civilization of Elemental.

If they were profound enough, they would have Fire or Air as Derivative Civilizations that remained at Fundamental Depth, while their main Elemental Civilization stayed at Absolute Depth.

They had to embody that Civilization absolutely in order to be an Absolute, after all.

The word itself implied totality.

Complete dedication to a single path.

Now the question came which asked... can an Absolute not have two Civilizations at Absolute?

They had such a complex weight of their Depth. Surely, such a thing may be possible?

The answer to this was a very, very difficult one.

And it was why the statement was made that replacing an Absolute was nigh impossible!

To replace an Absolute, one would have to take on their Civilization at the Absolute level. But if one already had their own Civilization at Absolute, the two would conflict.

Two Absolute Civilizations in one being?

It may be considered fundamentally impossible.

But of course, as always, there were exceptions.

At this moment, Noah chose to be one particular exception as he remained attuned in that state where he was seeing a great deal of things.
