Chapter 4571: The First Tongue II

Everything froze instantly in response to that ancient voice.

Noahs eyes opened wide as he observed how the surroundings had entered a unique state of temporal suspension. His Mana had integrated with the surrounding region so profoundly and uniquely that it felt like waves of an endless ocean had formed across the landscape.

Yet it was now all completely frozen in place as if time itself had stopped flowing. His existence vibrated with recognition of overwhelming power as he looked ahead toward the source of the voice.

In the distance, he could see both the massive wooly mammoth and the arthropod-like creature that had examined him earlier. They were looking in his direction now, but they clearly were not the ones who had spoken.

They actually stayed back at a distance with seemingly annoyed expressions on their alien features.

And far ahead of both of them, Noah saw a massive creature that possessed a vaguely humanoid shape but seemed to be completely overflowing with dense hair covering every surface.

This ancient-looking entitys entire body appeared covered by obsidian-gold hair so thick that none of his actual features were fully visible beneath the coverage. Even his face was completely obscured behind the flowing strands.

The being radiated presence that spoke of incomprehensible age and accumulated wisdom. When he spoke, his words seemed to carry weight like ancient proclamations of fundamental truth.

Noah observed with cautious analytical eyes as in this environment of frozen time and space, a single hairy hand emerged slowly from the dense obsidian-gold hair covering the entitys form.

This hand reached out and touched the frozen seas of Primus Mana that Noah had released. And even though Noah could not actually see the face of this tremendously powerful entity beneath all that hair, he could almost tangibly feel a sense of genuine surprise emanating from it.

He looked at this being with cautious but shining eyes as he opened his mouth and spoke with deliberate simplicity.

"Hello," he said clearly.

A single word. A simple greeting.

But the response was unexpected.

An immense silence followed his spoken word before moments later, the hairy entitys voice echoed out again through the frozen space.

|What rubbish are you trying to communicate with that crude method?| the voice said with tone mixing confusion and instruction. |You were just speaking perfectly clearly with The First Tongue moments ago. Your intent was filled with hope, unbound confidence, and wonderful novelty. I can still feel those qualities resonating now. Use your proper Voice to speak. Use The First Tongue as you were doing.|

As Noah heard these instructive words, his eyes opened even wider with sudden realization and understanding.

The voice was not actually coming from the head region of this entity covered completely with hair. Instead, it manifested as a pulse of obsidian-gold light surrounding them, emanating directly from their Way of Existence itself.

Their Way of Existence that seemed overwhelmingly grand and incomprehensibly refined. But within its fundamental structure, Noah could distinctly feel clear traces of Mana at its absolute core.

The First Tongue, this ancient being had called it.

Naturally, the only method of communication he had just been using before speaking aloud was his glorious Primus Mana flowing freely.

And so with this understanding clarified, he closed his mouth again. Pure blue-gold Primus Mana gushed forth from his existence once more as he deliberately spoke using Mana itself as the medium.

As he spoke with The First Tongue directly.

|Hello,| he communicated through pure Primus Mana expression.

The glorious hairy humanoid entity moved closer in response as their body pulsed with terrifically concentrated power that made the environment resonate.

|Yes yes, hello indeed, Little Anomaly,| the ancient voice responded with what almost seemed like satisfaction. |What is it specifically that you are searching for in these malformed weavings of Existence to be expressing yourself so loudly and freely here?|
