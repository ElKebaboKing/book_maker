Chapter 3250 A Higher Form of Existence! II

Across what the Outerversal Morphons referred to as the Megalos Ynnere.

Within the Omniversal Voyager, Protector Lucretia thought of an uncertain future as she sprawled on a throne opposite to Noah.

Her mind was calculating many possibilities based on any actions she took as after a certain point of time...

|...|

She felt something off as she blinked and looked towards the throne where Noah was.

And...

|Huh...?|

He was no longer there.

As if he had never been there.

He was just...gone!

Protector Lucretia rose from her throne with incredulity as she blinked and spread out her authority, but she felt nothing nearby as she even slowly spread her authority across the Vessel they were in.

And...nothing.

|What the fuck?|

He disappeared as if he had never been in this vessel since the very beginning.

Flashes of interminable brilliance.

This was what Noah felt alongside the intents of prompts that allowed him to know exactly what was happening.

|A change irreversible across the weavings of the Wheel of Existence has been brought to fruition.|

|You have transcended the limitations of your designated form of life and existence.|

|You have become a unique and never before discovered form of Existence|

|Your Existence has gained the distinction of the Omniversal Archontes Engineer.|

|Your Existential Omniversal Lumen Emperor Lineage is transitioning to the Omniversal Archontes Engineer.|

|The weavings of Invictus Maximus have reached full saturation and begun their emergence.|

...!

A robust array of changes.

His very makeup was altered as the very core of his being changed.

His very lineage and form of life changed!

And yet uniquely...no changes were observed or felt by anyone in the Infinite Omniverse.

Under his own gaze, all the parables that made him were buzzing and interacting full of life and existence.

The introduction of the Psioneutrino Particles caused everything to act as if it were supercharged, the breadth of his existence tripling right away as it continued to rise even now.

The concentration of his Authorities. The level of power he could wield as a lifeform. Everything was scaled upwards in an unfathomable way.

When he opened his eyes, he could see the weavings of the Infinite Omniverse around him, and yet none of its weavings reacted to him.

Instead, his eyes felt like they were observing countless golden strings glowing calmly and with utmost stability all around him.

Golden strings.

Countless of such golden strings as from them, tendrils flowed out serenely and infused themselves unto his existence.

This was...

A higher form of Existence.

Something he gained access to by changing his own Existence!

The golden tendrils of light flowing through him were so pure and stable that the moment they touched him, they instantly traveled across all the trillions of cells that made up his existence.

The quintillions upon quintillions of quarks, leptons, Psioneutrinos, and other particles that made him- these golden tendrils of brilliance were absorbed into all of them nigh instantly before they felt ever so enriched!

He didn't fully understand it.

But at this moment, he had to put it aside as his Existence felt like it was exploding.

HUUM!

On his chest, nine brilliant singularities shone as they cried out from the very base of his existence.

His Mundis of Power.

Those that held his Systems of Existence!

They were crying out with emotion and fervor at this moment as they advanced far ahead!

|The Atlas of Existence has advanced from the Echelon of Laborer.|

[The Atlas of Existence has entered the Echelon of Administrator.|

|You have become the Administrator of the Atlas of Existence.|

...!

From a Laborer to an Administrator.

It was a fantastical change, but Noah felt a sense of oddity within his Atlas of Existence.

When the weavings of his existence came together, there was a foreign one that converged within his Atlas!

And he stared at this weaving of existence that came to be clustered...in the Third Mundi of Power.

|The weavings of a sufficiently different System of Existence have naturally flowed their makeup into the Third Mundi of Power.|

|The System of Existence of the Ingeniare has taken the place of the Third Mundi of Power.|

Ingeniare.

A word closely related to the distinction and type of existence he had just become- an Engineer!

An engineer was someone who designed and built things, and Noah had gained such a distinction by placing a newly found particle that came as a product of Matter and Dark Matter into his existence- achieving a state even he didn't fully grasp.

Noah stared closely at the fantastical change blooming within him as at this time, he felt a tug.

It was a unique tug that described...emotions of worry of those he deemed close, his existence blinking its eyes as the origin of this worry was actually right next to him.

In the Haven of Helios.

One of his many bodies was still lying on the wide golden bed, its visage much more radiant than before as unique obsidian bands wrapped his fair skin like ancient tattoos.

He could see Adelaide and Barbatos, and yet they couldn't see nor sense him as they tried communicating with him through their Lineage!

Their hands passed right through his body as if their very existence could not grasp anything about him.

Uniquely, Adelaide was still staring at the same spot on the bed where he lay.

As if her senses and connection to him were so deep to the extent that they told her he was there, and yet her authority and everything else said otherwise!

It was a shocking finding as under Noah's eyes, he gazed at the countless golden threads and strings only he could see stably moving around him...and blinked.

The vibrant bands of tattoos around his body then flashed with a golden brilliance.

And he felt like he moved his vision from a well-lit room and towards a slightly darker one, but the moment he did so...

"Ah..."

Barbatos screamed out as Noah reappeared in front of their eyes right where he was!

But...her small fair feet were standing and passing through his sculpted abdominal muscles.

Adelaide's vibrant blue hairs phased through his shoulders and chest as even though he was right in front of them, their hands and bodies passed right through him as if he weren't entirely real!

And yet there he was.

He smiled at them as his brilliance caused them to be dazed.

"Did I worry you?"

...!

It was the first sentence that the Omniversal Archontes Engineer spoke since his conception!
