The words were unfathomably calm, and Origin found herself searching within them for emotion that matched her own. She wanted to see pain. She wanted to see remorse that burned as hot as her anger. She wanted to know that her suffering had meant something to him beyond mere acknowledgment!

But she couldnt find it.

Not the emotion she wanted, anyway.

THE Creatures apology was sincere, but it was also the apology of a being who existed on a level where individual suffering, no matter how profound, was just one thread in an infinite weaving. He meant his words, but he couldnt feel them the way she needed him to feel them.

She breathed out and controlled her existence, forcing the trembling of her form to still as she asked the question that had haunted her since her reconstruction.

"The more I think of it, the more I can see that Paradox shouldnt have had the power he had. Even if he supposedly took your heart by using Anaximander, even if you were chained, you are powerful. You were powerful."

Her eyes narrowed.

"So really, why...did I have to be collapsed? Why did you allow it?"

There were many whys she wanted to ask, but this was the critical one. This was the question that would determine whether anything between them could ever be salvaged.

THE Creature looked at her with a gaze that truly wanted to portray how he felt, the multicolored flames parting slightly as if to allow her to see more of his expression. What she saw there was not cruelty. What she saw there was not indifference.

What she saw there was the certainty of someone who had made calculations that others couldnt understand.

"At that time, I mistakenly believed that it was all...for the sake of adversity."

For adversity?
