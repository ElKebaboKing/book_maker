Chapter 1282 - The Ordeal Of Achieving The Cosmic Realm! II

How exactly did Noah plan to deal with a monster that had the backing of a Primordial and something as ridiculous as the Invincible System?

It was a question that Springforge posed, but it was one that Noah had asked himself in the past day many times!

Of course, he had gotten many different answers as he had even seen a pathway where in the future, he lay atop the fractured body of Aegon- so he knew the possibilities were endless! There were futures where he lost, and futures where he became a slave of the Primordial, and then there were other grand futures that Noah had only now begun to see as he amassed more information.

He continued to utilize <Destiny Negation> after he obtained all the information he was getting from <Reader's Foresight>, and many more paths unfolded as even though Noah's confidence only grew from it, he was also shown futures where he lost as he knew to be careful!

And when he collated all of this information and looked for a way forward with <Path to Victory>...Noah was shown many possibilities. All of the futures he saw were possibilities and not entrenched in certainty!

But he was confident as he looked towards Halcyon and Springforge and replied calmly.

"I'll need a few weeks or months at most and I should be at the Cosmic Realm. After all, it is only 100 billion Splendiferous Universes."

"..."

Springforge raised her eyebrows as she looked at him inquisitively, her voice echoing out sharply.

"A non-answer. What exactly can you do? You do not have the Invincible System that allowed Aegon to achieve the Cosmic Realm with ease. You have an Edict, and he has multiple.... there are so many ways I can mention where I just don't see how you can climb over such an insurmountable wall."

Springforge didn't have the absolute belief that Halcyon had from the memories she saw of the future as she continued to question Noah for exactly what he had! Towards this woman, Noah's body that was brimming with golden with the essence of Kismet this entire time began to be tinged green.

"Who said I only have a single Edict?"

RUMBLE!

Springforge found herself pulling back in shock as she then pointed her hands towards Noah with her words stuck in her throat!

"That! That...!"

The essence of Amelioration- the Edict that Springforge herself had designed...it could shockingly be seen wrapping around Noah's body as he gave off an indubitable life force.

The sight caused Springforge to not be able to talk as Halcyon looked towards the scene with her heart easing, finding that even amongst all the events occurring and the possibilities of the future- the being before her truly could find a way forward.

"Even if I don't tell you the specifics of what I will do. Believe me when I say I will do what is required." Noah spoke simply as he floated towards the shocked figure of Springforge while his body still brimmed with Amelioration.

"Now, Stormdust and Havenbreaker are Tritus and Elera. You are..." Her eyes shone with dazzling light as the scene of him using her own Edict out of nowhere entirely overwhelmed Springforge, her lips moving before she realized as she voiced out absentmindedly.

"Estella. Estella Holmes." Her absentminded voice showed her shock as Noah's right hand lifted her chin to align her verdant eyes with his.

"I look forward to working with you, Estella." The scene of one of the Family Heads that owned one third of the Cosmos within the Primordial Empire having her head raised by a man played out before Halcyon.

The golden haired Foremother looked at this scene with raised eyes as one could see an emotion akin to dissatisfaction as a stifling feeling settled in her heart!

It was only after a second did Estella come to, her eyes flashing sharply with green light as she definitely looked into Noah's eyes while her body began to brim with power!

"Let me know when you achieve the Cosmic Realm then. Until then, I looked forward to seeing how you handle Stormdust."

The aura of Amelioration vibrated on her body as she still couldn't accept seeing another person freely use it, the two of them staring at each other as neither side backed down, but of course...who could compete with droves of endless mana?

Pure mana was interchanged to become the essence of Amelioration as it released from Noah's body in waves, the Architect of this very edict actually finding herself losing in terms of pure essence of Amelioration as she continued to be filled with disbelief!

Noah saw the pride of this being hurt as she didn't want to back down, laughing as he stopped the waves of Amelioration releasing from him and stepped back, turning his head towards the golden haired woman who genuinely held a light of jealousy while staring at them!

"Halcyon, tell me about all the things you've seen. The more detail the better."

Noah controlled the pace of the whole talk as he made himself at home in this verdant realm, waving his hands as a huge portion of a torn wing of a Primordial Beast appeared, a fireball silently being cast with the essence of Kismet as it bloomed below the huge wing that spanned over a mile.

"Meanwhile, I'll grill us some good food..." The essence of pristine Nomological Edicts were used to cast unique skills, Noah activating [Five Star Chef] as he began to cook something wondrous!

Springforge looked at such an abrupt change with an annoyed expression as she couldn't help but think exasperatedly. 'Who the hell thinks of grilling food right now? How can he use Amelioration?!'

She was exasperated, but little did she know the food being prepared by Noah's Five Star Chef was enough to even cause those in the Cosmic Realm to shed tears due to how good it was!

Of course, this was on top of the 100,000% boost to All Parameters, Realm Progression, and many other temporary boosts for all who consume any foods Noah made under the effects of [Five Star Chef]!

As his Primordial Ruination Clone collected information while dining with two powerful women, his main bodies were carrying out the even more important tasks as towards the question from Sprinforge of how he would exactly catch up towards Aegon...Noah was carrying out one such action right at this moment.

It was an action made possible after he had fused multiple new Cosmos that his clones reached in the past day into the Infinite Cosmos. It was something he could do with an overabundance of the Light of Conquest!
