They should be able to find something matching this event as they would observe how it was solved in other Frequencies and resolve it here as well!

It was a truly minuscule amount of change.

But he sensed it.

The concentration of the weavings of authority themselves reduced, and they were already at a level far below when the event itself had unfolded!

If this continued...

"When this process reaches an irreversible point..."

Around Bob's body, maddening waves of silent authority began to bubble and seethe as his circular head glinted with danger!

"When this process reaches an irreversible point, I will wage war with all of your Logos of the Wheel for your hand in this. You can prepare for that in advance for the hand you laid in this."

|That will only cause a propagation of more chaos. We have no need nor intention to fight and are only looking after the welfare of the Wheel, just like you.|

The voice was stern as it spoke.

As if it wanted to convey a threat, and yet at the same time knew that it was hard to threaten this being!

"What you look for are benefits and profits for yourselves. The Wheel does not need us to look after it. It matters not, just know and prepare."

...!

TING!

Another string of existence snapped.

But this time, he was ready for it as he moved extremely quickly, his hands shooting out at speeds many times faster than light as his limbs shot forward and grasped onto the two sides of the snapped string!

He reached out and actually held them in both hands, the force of this single string being far too heavy as his arms shook from holding the two sides!

They wriggled like snakes in his hands as he tried to bring them back together, but this action itself seemed to be absolutely impossible.

As if he was trying to bring the skies and earth together.

BOOM!

An explosive force boomed out as the two sides of the string slipped across his hands, his palms burnt with the skin on them shredded as he looked on with calmness.

Everything began to heal rapidly as he spoke again after failing in his intervention.

"Remember, when this reaches an irreversible point, your Logos of the Wheel and I will be at war."

...!

A singular point was reinforced as immense silence settled into the surroundings, only to be broken when...

TING!

Another string snapped.

And the hands of Bob shot out again to grasp onto the split ends, only for things to end the same way as before!

But this didn't mean he could stop.

So he continued!
