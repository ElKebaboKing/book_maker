Chapter 1131 - A Cosmos Bound To Me! II

At the depths of the Universal Core, Noah had no knowledge of the movements of other beings as he was fully focused on the task at hand!

Currently, his main body was pulsing with immense light as he released droves of mana outwards, and there were clusters of millions of Marks of Antiquity that had begun to form around him as they descended endlessly.

The rewards of the Assimilation of Universes had already begun as the process was currently occurring at a rapid speed, a Universe becoming covered by the light of Assimilation every few seconds as they were dissociating their boundaries and becoming linked with the Dark Universe quickly!

The 9 Universes that were affected by the Universal Amalgamation were the first as after that, the light of Assimilation began to cover Universes that even Noah hadnt had the chances to fully explore yet.

The Cerulean Universe where an old enemy came from.

The Light Universe, The Stone Universe...the dozens of Universes that could fill up the requirements and allow a cluster of Universes coming together to be called a Cosmos were all gradually becoming covered by the light of Assimilation as their nature was being changed, coming in contact with Ruination Essence as their centers were shifted to all join the Universal Core of the Dark Universe!

The effects of such actions were stupendous as the Hegemonies within all of these Universes looked at what was occurring in shock, but they didnt have to wonder of what was going on for too long as from their Wings of Primordial Destiny, the somber voice of the Oathkeeper came out.

"The Primordial Cosmos is undergoing a change currently. Do not try to interfere!"

...!

A statement that seemed very overbearing, but the Hegemonies had seen Oathkeepers rise to Antiquity in the Abyssal Universe as they could only nod to his words.

There were 52 Universes within the Primordial Cosmos, this number actually being a fairly small one when one looked at a Cosmos!

Now, all of them were bound to the Dark Universe as one key factor remained.

That last remaining factor...was the very core of the Primordial Cosmos that supplied Primordial Essence to all Universes.

<It seems like it is time.>

An ancient voice reverberated out in the roiling Primordial Sea that Noahs Primordial Ruination Clone and Oathkeeper were in.

Noah nodded his head with shining eyes as even though the core of the Primordial Cosmos was hidden within layers of space in unreachable depths...he could sense its location at this moment as he made a move to allow another consciousness to go towards it!

RUMBLE

From the pristine white Primordial Essence around them, a point of red began to bloom and permeate outwards.

This blooming red Ruination Essense brought with it a terrifying consciousness, and it was the consciousness of the Universal Construct under Noahs control!

The Universe that aspired to be a Cosmos was right now standing at the cusp of it as it just had to complete the final process and merge with the core of the Primordial Cosmos...completing its goals in an efficient and stupendous manner!

<For survival.>

The ancient consciousness of the Primordial Cosmos spoke out as it stated the main reason behind its actions and acceptance of what was currently occurring.

But the consciousness of the Universal Filament Core that was about to become a Cosmic Core said something entirely different.

<For power.>

WAA!

For power! The consciousness reflected its Masters thoughts as its intent was made clear, the Ruination Sea blooming out vigorously to fill the space they were in as a grand light began to shine out!

It was the light of two consciousnesses merging, a light of Ruination and Primordial Essence coming together!

Even though they were entirely opposed, they swirled around each other in an interconnected manner as the result of it left Noah and the Oathkeeper with a sight they wouldnt be able to forget for the rest of their lives.

As all of this occurred, Noahs main body was almost overwhelmed by the vast amounts of Marks of Antiquity rushing onto him, the number having long since passed billions every second as the effect of coming under the control of 52 new Universes and being on the cusp of being under the control of a Cosmos were too stupendous!

The number of Marks of Antiquity had actually toppled 148 Billion at this moment as they were enough to birth another Splendiferous Universe, but they continued to come relentlessly.

Mind you...Noah hadnt yet used his Dao of Fealty on the other Universes he had yet to visit!

His vast seas of fortune and destiny were churning crazily as the actions he had done were truly too significant, carrying a level of importance that even Noah did not fully comprehend.

Never before had a Cosmos come under the control of a being.

Noah was nearly overwhelmed with the boundless information coming in, but this was when he heard the voice he was used to hearing for many times in the past months reverberate in his mind.

Not a Universe or Universal Filament....but a Cosmos!
