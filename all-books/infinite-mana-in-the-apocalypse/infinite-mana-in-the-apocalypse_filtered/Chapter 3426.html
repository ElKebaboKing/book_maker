Chapter 3424 Giving a Monster Wings! IV

It felt as comfortable and light as if he were adorning silk robes, and yet their might could likely smash away a normal human.

"Hmm..."

He looked at his visage, which was now covered by two layers of armor, as he felt a sense of protection.

As a normal human, he could die here at any time.

Having protective measures would greatly ease his mind!

He looked at the other items piling up into a mountain before him as he took two bottles of water, throwing one towards the dazed Pioneer Nysa while he drank the other.

Relishing the clean taste, he spoke out valiantly.

"Our cooperation will be a beneficial one. You let me know of any others like you- Pioneers, whether in this Settlement or others. Keep providing blood daily...and you will live a comfortable life here that others can only dream of."

...!

His eyes burned with ambition.

He had arrived in a place where force was contained.

Strength ruled, and nobody had an exorbitant amount of it.

It was all equal.

The only differences came in body types as massive animals like Titans or Dragons who formed their own Settlements would still be formidable foes!

Among them, there may be Pioneers, or even Source Complexity existences.

And Noah wanted them all.

Ambition coursed across his body as he grabbed another set of armor and threw them towards the first Pioneer to offer her blood to him, the dazzling gold armaments landing on her feet as she stared at them dazedly!

In a few more seconds, the sound of steps echoed out from behind as Infiniverse came out, her hand holding a piece of paper that listed a catalog of every single thing sent over so far.

Noah accepted it and read through everything as his eyes lit up even more.

Behind him, Ruination appeared while carrying a massive obsidian case twice her size- and she laid it down on the gray earth while pressing a button at the very center that caused this case to open up its compartments!

Within, a neon crimson gold body suit could be seen, the clothes adorning Ruination falling off as an exquisite naked figure briefly showed itself with an ample chest and waist before seconds later, it was wrapped up in the neon crimson gold battlesuit.

The case seemed to hold multiple compartments as at the very center, a glistening obsidian purple sniper rifle lay shining with a dangerous glint as Ruination picked it up and checked it before she swung it behind her back.

In the compartment, there were also two golden desert eagle pistols that she grabbed and checked before they were holstered on straps on the sides of her thighs.

She then picked out two katanas with golden handles and white blades, their sharpness even being felt from afar as they were also sheathed on preset components of her battle suit on her back!

She seemed to be pulling one terrifying weapon after another and placing it on her body as by the time she was done, she could mutilate and cut up ordinary men in a manner where they wouldn't even know what had just happened.

She then came to stand behind Noah like a hardened general as she stared forth coldly.

Noah was staring at the catalog of all things brought over as he voiced out to the Infiniverse.

"Let me have the Miniature Polyacrylate Housing Unit #1."

...!

Miniature Polyacrylate Housing Unit.

Something designed by the Infiniverse Megalos Seed herself that was made up of preset constructed structure through materials that were known as hydrophilic polymers or superabsorbent polymers!

All they needed was to absorb water, and they would rapidly expand to their preset shape.

The Infiniverse modified them to also have a sense of hardness as at this moment, she brought the Miniature Polyacrylate Housing Unit #1 to Noah.

By all means, it looked like a small eaten castle that a child could play with.

But Noah took it and walked to the clearing behind them where the old house of Warlord Maxus used to be.

"Move back."

He gave a simple command to the others as he set the miniature castle on the ground, using the half-drunk bottle of water in his hands as he poured it onto the Miniature Polyacrylate Housing Unit #1!

His own figure dashed away right after as in a few seconds...

WUU!

The miniature castle absorbed every drop of water on it, and without the usage of any authority...it instantly and rapidly began to expand.

The ground shook as if a giant had stepped down, and before anyone could even blink, a glistening silver castle with three pointed peaks appeared before them.

...!

Pioneer Nysa's jaw opened wide.

The other inhabitants of Hell Terra Firma, who had been here for months or years, looked at this scene in utter shock and horror as they wondered what the hell was going on!

They couldn't use any authority, right?

So what exactly was this being doing?!

None of them had an answer as Noah looked at the dazzling work of science before him, walking to it and touching its walls that felt like concrete at this moment.

He opened its silver doors and looked within as he saw a long hall leading into it, and other doors that led to other rooms.

A place fit for an emperor to sleep in, not these snacks within this Settlement.

He smiled at this progression of events as he spoke out other sets of commands.

"Set up the Miniature Polyacrylate Defense Walls around this whole area. And let us get ready with all the means to raise and equip an army..."

...!

His words betrayed his wild ambition.

He had to get his hands on more rare entities that were weak and powerless in this place.

Their blood and very being would serve as fuel for power as in the future to come, he would go across Hell Terra Firma while finding a way out of this!

As for those who put him here?

The figures within the Immovable Sacred Palace of the Expanse?

They would face the wrath and gratefulness of Ozymandias alone as they may not even be alive by the time he got out of here!
