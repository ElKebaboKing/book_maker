Nine massive statues arose all around the Peacemaker and Seraphina!

They were magnificent, terrible things, their muscular forms etched with an eternity of sorrowful judgment, their faces a perfect blend of profound disappointment and deep, paternal sorrow.

Their terrifying white eyes, devoid of pupils, radiated a light of pure, unyielding law.

All nine turned their gaze upon Alexander and Seraphina, a silent, collective judgment that seemed to weigh their very souls.

Then, one of the massive statues began to speak, its voice the sound of mountains grinding, of eons passing.

"You two were supposed to be here many, many years ago. If you had taken the inheritance of THE Living Law at that time, you may have had a chance before being laid to waste in the Fallout of what is coming. But now... you are far, far too late."

HUUM!

The words were heavy, each one a death knell. Alexanders face, once so calm, became a mask of stern resolve. "What do you mean?"

All nine statues of law and judgment looked at him, and another spoke, its voice a chorus of echoing pronouncements.

"All existences that were not granted entry into THE Loom countless eons ago are those destined to fall in the coming collapse and Fallout. THE Living Law left behind an inheritance for those who would nurture parts of its shattered will. You two hold the two major components of that will. Had you been here when you were meant to be, you may have had the chance to cultivate your Law to the extent of gaining the chance to pursue an opportunity for survival. But now, even that chance is gone. Now, there is nothing."

...!

Now there was nothing!

The words were so despotic and numb that one could go mad hearing them!

The statue paused, and the silence it left was more terrifying than any sound.

"You would need, at the very least, a few million years. But based on what we have sensed is occurring out in the Folds... not even a million years will pass before the Fallout. It might be a hundred thousand years, or one thousand years, or even one year... the numbers dont truly matter. Whether it happens tomorrow or a million years from now, you two, and all others present across the Folds at this moment, are doomed."

...!

The words were a final, absolute judgment. Seraphinas face paled, the blood draining from it, her eyes wide with the sheer, existential horror of it all. The collapse of everything. Certain doom!

But the visage of Alexander Asmodeus, the glorious visage of the Peacemaker, shone with an unruly, defiant light.

He looked up at the nine colossal statues, and his voice was a commanding roar that defied their pronouncements of doom.

"I will be the one to make the decision on whether it is too late or not! For now, we came to claim the Tool and the inheritance of THE Living Law. So forget about certain doom and bring it out, so that we may work towards a solution and not wallow in decimation!"

HUUM!

The nine marbled statues looked at him, their cold, sorrowful eyes seeming to gauge the depth of his defiance. Then, as one, they turned back into still, silent stone. The surroundings began to tremble, and their nine synonymous voices echoed out, a final, resigned pronouncement.

"...So be it."

So be it.

Words of cold resignation, but also the words of a possible beginning.

But they were ones sorely needed!
