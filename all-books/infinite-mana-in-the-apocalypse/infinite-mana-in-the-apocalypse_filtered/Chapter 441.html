Chapter 440 - Materials

In a brightly blue lit up underground area, sparking crystal stalagmites exposed the brilliance of the area as the brightest one that stood at the center had more descriptions rise up gradually in front of Noahs eyes.

<strong>[Unique Material- Aquamarine Mesrandite] :: A material that has collected an abundant amount of the essence of the law of water, granting it the unique title. Can be used to refine Supreme Treasures or in the creation of one in the Blacksmiths Forge of the Spiritual World.</strong>

Noahs Ancient Blood Clone had actually come across a material of great value in his search for an inheritance, something that thrummed with the power of the Law of Water, a material that he could use to either refine the Trident of the Sea, or possibly create another Supreme Treasure!

His eyes shone as he waved his hands towards this enormous [Unique Material- Aquamarine Mesrandite], watching it disappear from his eyes as his gaze turned towards the numerous other crystals that werent nearly as bright as the one he had just pocketed.

<strong>[Water Crystal] :: A crystal holding the essence of the Law of Water, can be used as a material to refine normal Items or further refined with the locked feature of the Spiritual World, [Alchemy Hall], into Soul Jades.</strong>

The less bright crystal stalagmites were Water Crystals that were similar to the Fire Crystals he had obtained as materials before from his first Fire Behemoth, but he noticed the additional descriptive words added to the item as the Planetary System hinted to a feature he could not even use yet.

"You are able to foresee the possible uses of the future features of the Spiritual World already?"

He asked the Planetary System as a reply came a millisecond later.

[The veils of secrecy for the natural order and existence of things is gradually becoming thinner as we gather more information and connect to more worlds, your elevation to an Infernal Lord granting us access to a profound bank of knowledge that allows for fairly accurate possibilities.]

Noahs clone nodded as he was reminded of the information that would make many tremble to their cores, the powers of the beings standing atop this Novus Galaxy being so terrifying that nobody would ever think of matching themselves against them.

When faced with beings that had established a Domain and then entered a realm where they could destroy worlds, and then they went even further ahead to forge an entire star at their core before they shattered this star to form a Nebula that could birth even more stars...many would simply run away!

But Noah received this knowledge as it made his fear at first, yes! But he reminded himself of his own power, and just how rapid he could rise through the ranks compared to anyone else.

An example was how in just a period of the past few weeks, he had gone from the Void Realm and into the Soul Forging Realm, and just now he had stepped into the Domain Expansion Realm!

What speed!

What genius!

With such an overpowering power behind him and his own confidence for the future, he saw himself in no way lesser than the beings that already stood near the top.

He shook himself out of his reverie as he glanced at the many Water Crystals around him, waving his hands as the wide expanse that was the size of a football field began to be emptied until the brightness was gradually gone. Every single shining crystal that had even the tiniest l.u.s.ter was taken into the Spiritual World as the place where a treasure trove had been laid barren, Noahs figure bringing out the Spiritual World thereafter as he used [Treasure Hunter] once more, another golden arrow appearing as he was led to another direction once more!

He disappeared from the barren underground area as he teleported to the skies, having finally attained the Domain Expansion Realm that allowed one to fly freely in the Celestial Realm. But, he liked the feeling of flying on an enormous Draconic Fire Behemoth even more, so Draco reappeared in the skies with a shaking presence as Noah landed on its head, a slight smile on his face as he patted the enormous dragon that began leaving embers of flames as it began shooting through the skies!

In another location, his main body was in an expensive lodging of the Celestials in the Sanctum, having returned from the far region where Chaos Behemoths were abundant in order to consolidate his realm and in preparation for the battles between Special Existences and beings similar to him who held noble fates, or so the commands from Grandmaster Vredral stated.

His main body was planning on how to best move within the ranks of the Celestials to maintain the most benefits, especially this treasure known as Luminescent Rainbow Dew that even Grandmasters at the peak of the Domain Expansion Realm wished for. He was thinking of how to move while gaining all of these benefits, but he was also thinking of the overall results he wanted to obtain from this organization!

His thoughts went towards Athena, another figure that would play a central role in the events to come as at this moment in time, he saw her as a viable scheme to play. He had already placed himself in the War Princesss life until her guard was completely down around him, and he recently obtained even more information that pertained to her from the Grandmaster Luo that he took down in the Magus World.

The information was something that was only a bit of history, but it could be used to create an amazing opportunity for the future. So while his clone continued to move in search of treasures and the continuing cultivation of laws, Noah was currently scheming, and at the heart of the scheme was the valiant War Princess!

In the expansive room he was in, Noah turned his head towards the door as he waved his hands to make the Dagger of Time that was slowly rotating in front of him disappear. The past few hours, he had been using the [Insight] feature of the Dagger to look at a few possibilities of how his plan could play out, and was confident in it somewhat as he heard a knock on his door soon after.

He went over the plan a few times, changing what he was wearing as he also used [Unique Origin] and then walked towards the door and opening in as in front of him was the War Princess herself, her face holding a vibrant expression as Noah saw on her body, she was adorned by a unique golden robe that only signified one thing, and it was a type of status that very few Celestials held in the Light Expanse.

"Senior Sister, theyve already given you a title of Grandmaster?"

Noah smiled as he spoke to the War Princess in front of him, a scheme that would be one of the deciders for a few major events in the future beginning to play out at this moment. He had not done anything these past weeks as he was around this Senior Sister, but he would be making his move today!
