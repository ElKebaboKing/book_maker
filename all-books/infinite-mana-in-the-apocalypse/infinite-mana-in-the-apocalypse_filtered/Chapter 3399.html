Chapter 3397 Unbound Obsession! III

The scene was a disastrous one.

Merely one of the Quasi-Existence Severing Golden Titans made a move, and yet an Ordinance Amalgamation Antediluvian Leviathan and the Tower of Existence he protected was shattered alongside a dozen Towers of Existence that Ordinance of Existence entities occupied, including a few dozen towers manned by those less powerful!

By all means, it was a scene of devastation.

And yet!

Among this devastation, there was a single tower that was untouched!

Around it, a dazzling golden wheel formed and spun as it radiated a light of Severed Existence.

It was the only thing capable of moving in this domain of frozen time!

When it bloomed, many found themselves questioning with emotion.

Was there actually an Existence Severing entity on their side hiding this whole time?

It was a question for many- the wonder if there were multiple Existence Severing entities that entered the Tresoverse Domain of the Omnifather.

At this moment, they would truly be needed as these terrifying Golden Titans could become genuine Existence Severing entities at any moment!

If their weavings didn't collapse.

If many were successful in their Severing.

The Tresorverse Sacred Domain of the Omnifather would turn into a land of bloodbath!

HUUM!

The Severed golden wheel of Kuklos spun with heaviness and indisputability.

The Golden Titan looked towards this wither with crazed eyes as he bellowed out.

|Break for me...Break for me! Temporal Decimation!|

His words were those of a maddened entity.

But his power was very real as all around, the purple brilliance brought with it a light of extinction that made its descent on the golden Tower of Existence!

But...

WU!

The wheel continued to spin heavily as around the Tower of Existence, the hazy illusion of a devilishly handsome Emperor manifested.

An Origin Sacred Complexity Rune of Existence spun rapidly at his center as 81 Meridians lit up brightly, his gaze currently exceedingly cold!

But his eyes.

His eyes stared towards the Golden Titan as they held...a hint of an undeniable obsession. An obsession that showed complex rage and hatred that didn't entirely fit the calm face!

But such eyes looked at the Golden Titan as if they already considered him dead.

When his figure made an appearance, many were astonished as some had seen his face before when they were climbing the roots of Primaltree of Existence.

Those belonging to the Commonwealth were even more astonished as they had seen the face and details of this being after the Viceroy asked about him- even more so when he was able to attain one of the three Golden Origin Tresorverse Towers of Existence!

His figure was shrouded in mystery as he should be a Protector of the Commonwealth, and yet he wasn't. He should be weak, having barely entered the states of Existence, and yet...he wasn't! The only reason he wasn't labeled an enemy was that until this point, Old Man Eudemus vouched for him.

And now, he showed a Severed Source- and it was Kuklos nonetheless?!

In a far distance, the Viceroy of the Commonwealth was in his tower as he looked towards this direction with blazing eyes!

Amidst the Antediluvian Leviathans, there was one burning with waves of Entropy around a multicolored Tower of Existence at this moment who stared over in shock.

Wasn't this...that young friend he had been racing on the roots of the Primaltree?!

There were many gazes and reactions.

But the man in question didn't bother with all of this as he focused on the enemy before him, the weavings of the Origin Sacred Primal Megalonian buzzing as if that scene of decimation all those cycles ago was playing in front of its face again and again.

His very blood burned as his Meridians pulsed.

His weavings were amplified as through them, he carried the weight of the Infiniverse Megalos Physiology and cycled it through the buzzing weavings of Kuklos!

"Time is merely a part of the weavings of the Wheel."

HUUM!

His voice reverberated out with seemingly just as much hatred as the Golden Titans. "Why would your time hold any significance before the Wheel?"

...!

A blinding light of gold pressure and weight blasted out of the spinning wheel that seemed far too solid.

The light blazed and actually fractured the purple brilliance and the illusory clock that had formed into a domain that could cover an entire Omniverse, and it formed into trillions of smaller golden wheels that surged to smash onto the massive Golden Titan like a halcyon sea!

BOOM!

Like an unstoppable tide, it smashed forth and illuminated everything as it was far too fast.

It was heavy, and yet so fast!

The countless golden wheels smashed onto the body of the Golden Titan mightily, imposing a tremendous weight and pressure that seemed to be asking again and again...

|What is Time before the Wheel of Existence?!|

|What is Time before the Wheel of Existence?!|

|What is Time before the Wheel...!|

Again and again, his body was bombarded with such an intent along with the sea of golden wheels.

|OOOH!|

The reason why many would seclude themselves when Severing their existence.

Any heavy interruptions that could sow any seeds of doubt to what one was Severing could be deadly!

And after facing a bombardment of trillions of Wheels while having his Ordinance questioned...his weavings now trembled with instability and began collapsing!

Collapsing!

The gazes of all Quasi-Existence Severing Golden Titans and those behind them!
