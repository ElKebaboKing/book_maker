Chapter 609 - Peace

As Noah completed the terrifying Trait of Infinite Mana, the beings connected to him simply experienced the huge changes of the Infinite Realm that continued to get denser and denser atmospheric essence as it further strengthened their resolve to get stronger.

In the core area of the Infinite Realm where the central continent of the Spiritual World used to be, an arrogant penguin had his beak raised high as he faced thousands of beasts, beings that the penguin considered his brothers and sisters!

The area they were currently in was filled with the universal essence of space as it was very conducive to cultivating the Law of Space, and the Emperor Penguin was currently acting as the teacher as he faced the crowd of beasts.

This arrogant Penguin was floating at the forefront as one could see that in order to fit into the role of a teacher more, this penguin had adorned a nice fitting white suit that shone with great contrast to his sleek black feathers, the appearance of a bow tie being even more prominent as it gave the Emperor Penguin an appearance that would make many beings stop on their tracks!

"RAA!"

He released a bellow that relayed his intentions to the many beasts, each of them activating [Disciple of Space] as they tried to comprehend this law around them, some of them beginning to display their skills as they bowed to the Emperor Penguin and sought guidance.

It was a carefree and peaceful scene of these beings only striving their best to get stronger, because even though the Overseer of the Infinite Realm had not relayed them any information that made them feel any sense of danger, many of them were still striving to improve their strength as they saw the continued changes of the Infinite Realm every day!

Noah had not yet told anyone else about the events that occurred at the boundary of the Novus Galaxy and the Signal sent out, or that a force could be making its way towards here at this moment. He knew that his subordinates would grow to be extremely powerful in time as they used the resources of the Infinite Realm and the Novus Galaxy, but they were currently nowhere near being ready to face beings that could traverse the Chaotic Void.

He knew the knowledge that because the Novus Galaxy had finally granted him the first Trait of this Galaxy, it could now begin to awaken Traits on those that met the qualifications or those who were destined for them as many beings would gain even greater forms of power in the coming future!

But the battle to come, Noah would most likely be fighting it alone, only supported by the fearsome Terrors that held the titles of Controllers. Thus, his subordinates had no idea of what was coming, but they still instinctively continued to train with even more vigor.

Aside from the Emperor Penguin who was working to improve the strength of the high ranking beasts of the Infinite Realm, there were also Barbatos, Athena, Elena, and many others that were doing just the same!

In one region of the Infinite Realm that was filled with the essence of Chaos, Barbatos was sitting on a throne of bones releasing a distinct black hue that continuously strengthened thousands of high ranking Undead of a powerful Legion that she herself was creating, this legion being reinforced by the power of Chaos as she chose to comprehend this law.

Her gaze was distant as her fingers unconsciously released essence, her thoughts rotating around a single being as she felt the continuing changes of the Infinite Realm and sighed!

"Ah, Little Fish..."

She thought of a single man who seemed to be elevating himself to greater and greater heights each day, her resolve of getting stronger only increasing as she sought to go towards the same level of power as this being! To just be able to stand alongside him while raising her head high!

Barbatos was not the only one with these emotions as in another location, Athena was actually facing the three remaining Mythical Beasts of the Beast World, some of the Demon Lords from the Demon World, and various other beings all over the Infinite Realm that sought to learn the law of Fate.

One could also see Old Man Inuit and Fritz by the side giving their own guidance, these old beings looking at their surroundings in shock and awe still as the fantastical sights continued to play around them.

Athena was currently following the routine where she aided those needing to see the techniques of Fate in the Infinite Realm for half of her day, using the other half of the day to traverse the Light and Dark Expanse with Old Man Inuit and Fritz as they continued to oversee the healing period for these two Expanses.

Her goal of seeking Order was still just as steadfast as she also had another reason to seek for strength at this time.

ROAR!

In another location of the Infinite Realm, one could observe a fearsome Draconic Fire Behemoth nearing 100 meters in size, the pet known as Draco having grown to the powerful peak of the World Rank as it sparred with Elena and hundreds of other Vampyres.

Scenes like these were abundant all over the Infinite Realm as many beings strengthened themselves while feeling a period of peace.

This sense of peace was enhanced even more when they looked at the nearly endless resources of the Infinite Realm!

Anytime they wished, they could walk to a nearby field that would be filled with wondrous medicinal herbs or fruits that were potent with essence, aiding them in regenerating their strength or even advancing through ranks as they enjoyed an atmosphere that very few could come across!

Even while feeling this peace, many of the top commanders under Noah were nearing the rank of Star Forgers as time passed while they also began traversing in the path of laws. Being in an environment where they could dedicate all of their time to training while keeping calm hearts, it was actually the perfect recipe to create powerful soldiers that would grow their strength at terrifying speeds.

Even if they would be nowhere near as close as Noah when it came to their breakthroughs and even with comprehending laws, it would not take them hundreds of years like their predecessors for them to achieve high levels of strength!

Just a few years would be enough to produce multiple experts, and Noah even expected to have multiple Controllers in around 10 years or less as he thought about his subordinates and all the opportunities they had access to!

But for all of this to come to fruition, he had to make sure that the plan of the Novus Galaxy was carried out seamlessly, that whatever they invited would be taken care of with no problems as he would then continue on this grand journey!
