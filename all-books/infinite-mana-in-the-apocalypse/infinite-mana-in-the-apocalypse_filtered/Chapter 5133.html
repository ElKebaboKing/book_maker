Chapter 5131: Greed! III

The laugh rolling out of Octavius was an ugly one.

It wasnt the laugh of a being who had lost control. It was measured, scholarly, and almost delighted! He waved his hands upward as if surrendering to a tide he couldnt turn back, the gesture so disarming it nearly sold itself as sincere.

"Alright, big guy."

His smile held the brilliance of a host welcoming a long-anticipated guest.

"You are far too complex for us little things. Which table would you like my body on so you can take a look? Whichever arrangement suits your research methodology, Im happy to oblige. I have preferences myself, but Im willing to be flexible on my preferences today."

...!

Something shifted in the weavings around the island.

|Warning: Civilizational Weaving of Unity has been detected.|

|The weaving is infused into the ambient Infinity and is attempting to propagate along contact vectors. Targets: Noah Osmont. Naldine Manthon. THE Living Emotive.|

|Effect Goal: Infiltrate existential foundations and establish infectious Unity bond with source entity.|

Noahs eyes sharpened.

That was why Octavius was smiling. The surrender was theater. The welcome was bait! He had already embarked on an attack the moment the words left his mouth, threading his Civilizational weaving through the ambient Infinity that was already saturating the island, using the density of the environment as camouflage for a strike that would have rooted itself into the existences of all three of them before any of them had time to notice.

It wasnt even a bad attempt!

Against a lesser party, it would have worked. Against Noah alone, it might have given him pause for a fraction of a moment. But the weaving had reached not only for him. It had reached for Naldine too.

His eyes turned unfathomably cold.

It wasnt the attack itself that did it. What cooled his gaze into something glacial was that the reach had extended toward Naldine, toward the Hadean woman floating beside him whose radiant white hair caught the filtered light of THE Wylds strange sky.

Grand and tyrannical and calculating though he was, there were weaknesses he had never bothered to hide from himself.

Women were one of them.

His women, specifically.

Once a bond settled between him and someone he counted as his, he became overly protective in ways that his pragmatic self couldnt quite discipline out of existence. If there was a single emotion associated with him that could have been amplified into an Ego that made him immune to the madness of Infinity, it probably wasnt pride.

He wasnt overly prideful. Rage and wrath came when they came, but they didnt last long enough. Lust, though. If one wanted to make a case, Lust might have been it! Even though he knew for him and how he played with infinity, emotions shouldnt be the answer of why he never went mad.

Such a thought crossed his mind in the space between heartbeats.

He shook his head once and focused down on the island.

"Okay," he said calmly. "Second option, it is."

...!

He wanted to test things.

Here were powerful Primordial Architects whose engineered capabilities he couldnt properly gauge from observation alone. The seas of Infinity inside them told him they were stable. The amplified Egos told him they were designed to resist madness.

But the actual combat ceiling of each being, the exact pressure they could withstand, the point at which their engineered stability began to crack, none of that could be derived from inspection. It required contact.

So he struck with an attack that would have decimated someone like Grimvault.

He stayed where he was. He raised his hand, and with a simple motion he pushed it down through the air.

HUUM!

In the next instant, identical visages of his body appeared in front of each Primordial Architect gathered below.

Each copy held the same posture. Each copy pushed its hand down with the same slow deliberateness. The air between him and every single target collapsed into a Geodesic Weaving of infinity, a sea of infinity, one attack distributed across multiple points simultaneously, one movement propagating through the ambient Infinity until it arrived at every target in the same instant it left him!

Because Infinity was pervasive, his attack became pervasive. One gesture. Infinite vectors!

Oh!

Behind each pushing hand, a concentrated sea of Infinity pressed downward with the full weight of his Hadean physiology riding its current.

BOOM!

The island rocked on its foundations!

Every Primordial Architect below was hammered backward. The Rhyacian Tier scholar at the rear of the cluster was flung across the length of the island before her body even registered being hit. Two Calymmian peers slammed through their own apparatus, flasks shattering around them, bone towers toppling onto their crumpled forms. The research grounds that had stood for eons finished their rearrangement in less than a second.

Two figures remained standing.

Octavius and Valeria held their ground.

They had both been struck. He had seen the impact land on them, had felt the resistance his weavings encountered. But neither of them had been knocked back. Neither of them was even visibly strained!

Valerias crimson braids swayed once from the force and settled. Octavius adjusted the cuff of one emerald sleeve with the fingers of the other hand as a faint sea of infinity swirled around him.

|The weight of Infinity and Quintessence Infiniforce distributed equally across all hostile targets.|

|Against all engineered Primordial Architects on the primary island, single-strike termination(one hit, one kill) was not achieved. Engineered stability exceeds standard Primordial Architect thresholds.|

|Result: Five of seven targets incapacitated or displaced. Two targets unhindered.|

|Octavius Kraethos and Valeria Morne have sustained negligible damage.|

...!

Noahs smile turned dangerous.

"You try sending your infectious weavings of Unity to me or those around me again," he said, his voice drifting across the ruined island with the patience of a decision already finalized, "and I really wont leave your corpse in one piece."

Octavius smiled brilliantly.

He rolled his shoulders. Cracked his neck once, a small sharp sound that shouldnt have carried across the distance and somehow did.

"Big guy. Infinity-bearer. Infinite Conduit. Whatever name you want to take for yourself."

His pale gold eyes shone with a hunger that had finally found its proper subject.

"I do wish to tell you that we are not like any Primordial Architects youve encountered before. We are the Chosen. Our Egos burn brightly with Greed that radiantly resists the madness of Infinity, the same madness that would have you drowning if you werent whatever exception you happen to be. If you thought this was going to be easy, Im sorry to disappoint. The disappointment...will be educational, I promise."

...!
