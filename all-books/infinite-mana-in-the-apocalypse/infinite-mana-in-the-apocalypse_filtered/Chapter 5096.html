There exists a term for those who find pleasure in pain.

Algophilist!

From the word algos, meaning pain, and philos, meaning lover. One who loves suffering. One who seeks agony not as means to an end but as the end itself. The sensation of hurt becomes indistinguishable from the sensation of joy, and they pursue both with equal fervor because to them, both are the same.

Noah Osmont...was not an algophilist!

THE Silurian Light touched him, and in that instant, he understood what Alexander had felt when Beowulf tortured him across millions of subjective years. Every Existential Nociceptor within his existence activated simultaneously, every pain receptor screaming with immensity and entered territories that most beings couldnt survive experiencing.

Pain.

Real pain. Not the cycles of destruction and rebirth that Grimvaults weapon had provided. Not the shattering and reforming that THE Crucible of Observable Rebirth had transformed into fuel for advancement. This was something else entirely, agony that existed at the level of causality itself, suffering that came from having your very existence told it should never have been.

And Noah defiantly felt all of it.

He didnt defend or retreat. He didnt channel Infinity or Observable force to resist what was happening. He wanted to feel this. He wanted to understand what a Pulse truly was, what it meant to have something derived from a Cause deployed against your existence. So he opened himself to the pain and focused through it with clarity that his previous transformations had provided.

His existence bellowed toward the Pulse in defiance.

What are you? What do you stand for?

HUUM!

The immensity of THE Silurian Cause.

And it wanted him gone.
