Chapter 4045: Variation! I

Limitations are peculiar things. They exist not as universal truths but as boundaries defined by perspective, capability, and understanding.

What one being calls impossible, another accomplishes with a casual gesture. What requires eons of preparation for some happens in heartbeats for others!

The wall that stops a child is merely a step for an adult. The ocean that drowns a man is home to a fish. The void that kills most beings is where others are born.

It was known and established...written into the fundamental understanding of existence itself, that one could not teleport in or out of the Doorways to the Loom.

This wasnt merely difficult or dangerous; it was deemed impossible. The Doorways existed in states that rejected standard spatial manipulation, their paradoxical nature creating barriers that even Dukes couldnt breach through normal means.

Everyone knew this. Everyone accepted this.

But Noah had never been everyone.

---

Seconds ago, while Duke Gwendolyns panic painted the air with urgency, while she urged him to make a quick exit so they could flee properly once outside the Doorway, Noah had done something simpler.

He hadnt asked whether it was possible. He hadnt considered the established limitations.

He had simply connected to his Early Veiled Shore.

The connection flowed through him like water remembering its source, natural and inevitable.

His brilliant energy...containing eleven Living Existential Authorities that shouldnt coexist within a single being, pulsed with quiet certainty. The terrifying Kleos Glyph on his forehead, The Creatures First Lesson, blazed with light that made reality pay attention.

He looked towards Sigrid and Titan!

"Are you ready to visit my home?" he had asked calmly, as if inviting them for tea rather than attempting the impossible.

Titanos massive head had nodded with his characteristic blank expression, processing the question at his own pace.

Sigrid, still radiating the overwhelming presence of newly awakened Order, had simply nodded, her trust in him absolute despite not understanding what he intended.

Brilliant light had erupted around them...not harsh or violent, but encompassing, like being wrapped in starlight.

The illumination contained within it the authority of someone who didnt just bend rules. He wrote new ones! Noah, Sigrid, and Titano were enveloped in radiance that made the Doorways paradoxical nature irrelevant.

Then they were simply... gone.

He had left Thessaly and Altheon behind without a second thought. Their baggage wasnt something he needed to carry!

Let them explain to Duke Gwendolyn how the impossible had occurred. Let them wrestle with questions that had answers they couldnt comprehend!

The transition was seamless, like stepping from one room to another when both rooms happened to be separated by impossible distances and fundamental barriers.

Noah, Sigrid, and Titano materialized on the golden sands of the Early Veiled Shore, the light of this impossible space welcoming them with warmth that transcended temperature!

The body of Noah that had traveled with them, one of his many simultaneous existences...became a streak of light that merged with his primary form.

He rose from where he had been seated on the sands, his upper body bare, muscles glistening with the particular sheen that came from existing at frequencies reality barely acknowledged.

His lower body was covered in loose obsidian-gold pants that seemed to be woven from twilight itself.

The Goad rested beside him, that simple stick that could educate existence itself about its own nature!

Sigrid looked at him, then at their surroundings, her newly enhanced perception taking in details that should have been impossible to process!

Her eyes widened with recognition.

"Dont tell me this... is an Early Veiled Shore?!"

The words emerged with the weight of someone recognizing they stood in a place that shouldnt exist, that most beings would never even know could exist!

Early Veiled Shores were myth to most, legend to some, and death to nearly all who tried to reach them without proper understanding.

Noah walked up to them with confidence, his bare feet leaving perfect impressions in sand that remembered every step ever taken upon it.

"The one and only."

HUUM!

Titanos reaction was more visceral. The giants head swiveled in all directions, trying to process everything at once.

He leaned down to touch the sand, letting the golden grains run through his massive fingers.

His eyes tracked to the crystalline waters that lapped at shores that extended into infinity!

The Aquarium with its impossible architecture. The trees bearing fruits that could elevate existence itself.

Then he saw them...the cluster of glass Woks that Noah had given him before, each one still filled with the soup of vibrant Marine Lifeforms. They sat arranged neatly near one of the trees!

Titano looked at all of this, his gaze moving back and forth between Noah and the wonders around them. His mouth opened and closed repeatedly, but words wouldnt form!

He pointed at everything with an expression of amazement and shock that transcended his simple nature.

For once, the giant who could wrestle Inevitabilities into submission was completely speechless.

Noah smiled and patted his massive back, the gesture requiring him to reach up considerably.

"Come on, Brother. Let me introduce you to some people."

Noah kept things very straightforward and simple!

They moved toward the center of the Shore, where a gathering had naturally formed.

Amelia Osmont stood with a particular dignity. Henry was beside her, his complexity still surging from giving away his Everything of Fear.

The Emperor Penguin waddled about with self-importance that seemed comical until one remembered he had helped build an Abode.

Liora, Kazuhiko, Erikson, and others had assembled or were already nearby.

Noah brought the gigantic Titano over first, his massive form casting shadows that existed in multiple dimensions.

"Mother," Noah said with warmth, "this is Titano, an adopted brother Ive taken on."

...!

Titano, showing surprising grace for his size, bent down and extended one massive finger for Amelia to shake.

His simple mind was buzzing as even this simple interaction...being called a Brother...was something he never had during all the years with Duke Gwendolyn.

Honored Living Existences and lesser ones feared him.

Dukes looked at him like a chess piece. Nobody...had called him Brother!

Nobody had done what his brother was doing for him now!

So he put his massive finger out while making sure in his sinple thoughts that he would not cry.

Titano did not cry!

Amelia Osmont took his massive finge with the calm acceptance of someone who had long ago stopped being surprised by her sons choices in companions.

But her eyes werent on the giant. They had fixed on Sigrid, who stood slightly behind, still processing everything she was witnessing.

Amelias expression shifted to something knowing, something that mothers throughout existence would recognize.

"Did you already find another daughter-in-law for me? How are you managing to do this on top of everything else?" she asked with a smile that contained layers of amusement. "Which one is this?"
