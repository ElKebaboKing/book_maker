Chapter 3305 Terrific! III

An entity burning with glorious Entropy.

He had spoken to Noah once before as after being prompted by another Antediluvian Leviathan in the far distance, this being sent his intent towards Noah's figure that was blazing across the golden root and getting ever closer to his current position!

|Why are you in such a hurry with such pure weavings of Existence, young friend? Pace yourself, and you will enjoy the grace of the Omnifather even more.|

...!

The intent was extremely quick.

At this stage of power, entire conversations could take place within the span of an instant as the exchange of thoughts was that quick.

In this span of an instant, Noah's currently burning blue eyes glanced up towards this being and smiled.

|I know nothing of the grace of the Omnifather, so I must struggle and hurry ahead to understand. Are you able to tell me what we can expect ahead?|

The Antediluvian Leviathans!

Entities who seemed to know about the owner of this Tresorverse as they moved with terrifying power and calmness- and a level of fervent light Noah had only seen among Morphons when they spoke of the Great Beyonder.

The entity burning with Entropy chuckled at Noah's words as his strides pulled ever further ahead as he replied.

|I know a little. I reach out to you because the purity of your mana has resonated with a brother of mine a distance from here. He is sentimental of those similar to us in purity as he has asked me to look out for you. Pacing yourself is one such advice as if you are drained and can no longer interact with the Tresorverse Primaltree of Existence with your weavings of existence, you will lose all your progress.|

...!

When the constant drain of authority could not be met, one could no longer stand on the Tresorverse Primaltree of Existence as they would fall off.

The Antediluvian Leviathan brimming with Entropy said such things as Noah nodded towards this being and smiled.

|Thanks for the heads up, Brother Leviathan. And give my thanks to the other Brother Leviathan as well. Say, what is your name? You can refer to me as Engineer Osmont.|

He was holding this conversation seamlessly without stopping as an instant passed, and...

SAA!

He continued surging forward with what seemed to be incrementally increasing speed and even more output of his weavings of Existence as he never stopped!

Around him, the illusion of two blue Fabled Omniverses bloomed as they looked like two clear cerulean boundaries.

But with the passage of time, these boundaries seemed to gain more and more lines of weavings as they became more intricate.

More complex.

It was the product of the much more complex weavings of authority returning to Noah!

|The Infinity Fabled Omniverse and Manadynamics Fabled Omniverse are growing with exponential complexity.|

This prompt flashed wondrously as Nkah didn't hold back.

This entity who gazed at Noah inexplicably as he replied.

...!

Arguros.

His words carried majesty and intrigue as they emanated out.

By the time the words of Arguros finished, Noah had shockingly come to be beside him.

Side by side with the terrifying titan whose strides alone covered tremendous distance, and calm waves of Entropy steadily left him while even more complex ones returned to him!

The former possibility didn't make sense, and the latter seemed even more off.

Many kept their gazes on the leading figures of Noah and Arguros as they also expended more of their authority to pull forward faster.

He effortlessly held the conversation as he kept pace with this leading titan!
