Chapter 3032 Expansion IV

The Illusory Weavings under Noah's banner were invisible, yes, but it didn't mean they couldn't show themselves if they wished.

At this moment, a pair of invisible hands clamped on the Imperium Royal Decimara and his Guards at the Sequor Stage, none of them being able to move an inch as the pressure of three Teleios Stage Entities bloomed out!

The visage of the Illusory Commander Augustus appeared behind Decimara, with the Illusory Weavings of Archon Basileus Ignatius and the Royal Observer Xavier pressing down on the guards as from the Illusory Commander...

|No need to be so hasty.|

A biting intent filled with unsurpassable power boomed out and shook Decimara's will.

His will gazed at the Entity at the Teleios Stage surrounded by blazing purple flames behind him as too many questions arose.

Who were they? How could they have possibly found this place? And were they...friends or foes of the Imperium?

Decimara's head buzzed as before any answers could come...

|You can get all the resources lying behind this Existential storm, but before you proceed...you simply have to pledge fealty.|

...!

Decimara felt like his throat had become extremely dry as he weaved out his intent.

|Pledge fealty to...who?|

He felt an unbelievable sense of danger.

A danger that told him if he said the wrong thing, it would very likely be the last his Dominium would see!

Behind him, the Illusory Commander let go of the body of the Imperium Royal as he walked forward, a smile on his face as he spoke callously.

|To the Weaver of Extremity. Pledge your fealty to the Master of the Illusory Weaving Legions!|

HUUM!

A thick tendril of Tyranny erupted out along with slivers of Conquest.

Before the eyes of Decimara, this thick concentration of existential authority came towards his Body as if his Dominium rejected it, he knew the full pressure of the Teleios Stage Entity before him would eviscerate him!

So Decimara closed his eyes and shuddered while the tendrils of Authority flowed deep into his Dominium.

His pudgy body filled with fat and brilliance trembled as he felt the source of his existence be penetrated, a new authority settling within him that would control his existence for the eons to come!

He didn't fight it.

He was too used to life. He didn't want to end everything because of stubbornness.

And he saw the possibility of taking another path in the next moment as behind him, one of his guards roared out in defiance as the Exclusive Existential Extremity Authority of the Imperium exploded out from him!

|Glory to the Imperi-|

BOOM!

The hand of the Illusory Weaving of the Archon Basileus Ignatius smashed down with an interminable existential authority that was colorless, causing the body of the guard to crack and fold into itself before it shattered into fine dust within an instant!

A Sequor Stage Dominium...turned to fine dust in an instant!

And the more terrifying thing was that within the next few seconds, a light shone from the dust that then coagulated to begin forming...an illusory figure.

A figure that when its humanoid body fully formed looked extremely like his dead Guard!

|...|

The pudgy visage of Decimara trembled even more as he suppressed the Exclusive Existential Extremity Authority of the Imperium as far down as possible, not wanting its arrogant quality to rise up and cause him to be killed as within his mind, a voice rose.

A voice telling him to pledge fealty!

It was the last and final straw for him as the moment he did so, his Dominium would forever be bound by another.

And so...

His visage kneeled as he and his remaining guard voiced out at the same time.

|I pledge fealty to the Weaver of Extremity!|

HUUM!

Waves of brilliance shone.

And his weavings completely became bound to another as slowly, he felt the Exclusive Existential Extremity Authority of the Imperium leaving his Dominium!

But in its place...multiple Existential Extremity Authorities shining with a Colorless, Cerulean, and Golden lusters began to present themselves to Decimara with prompts as there would be more choices for him to make.

While this occurred, the Illusory Commander nodded his head and left the dazed Decimara behind, walking towards the chaotic violet storm of a natal Existential Extremity Authority as he also kneeled down and thrust his hand forward.

|I serve the Master.|

...!

His hands were raised up as he was used as a conduit.

From his raised hands, a singularity in the form of a Seed bloomed with a gorgeous blue light of Manadynamics.

It was known as a Miniature Existential Omniversal Seed of the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed!

It was part of Noah's construct that the moment it was released into this Unstable Anomalous Existential Domain...it anchored itself in space as Manadynamics began to flow out freely, latching on the tendrils of violet existential authorities ahead and beginning to change and devour them!

Manadynamics.

It stated that essence could neither be created nor destroyed, only changed in different forms.

At this moment, it altered the form of an unknown natal Existential Extremity Authority while a devouring light began to radiate from the Miniature Existential Omniversal Seed- the very reality of this Unstable Anomalous Existential Domain beginning to be devoured even before the deadly storm that prevented Decimara from attaining clusters of Unique Fruits of Existence, Metalloids, and other resources within place was cleared!

The weavings of the Unstable Anomalous Existential Domain seemed to tremble and roar as it began to be eaten up, tendrils of heavy Existential Extremity Authority beginning to gather here.

At the same time, the Automaton Workers flapped their wings and floated towards different areas of this Unstable Anomalous Existential Domain, intent on releasing the power of the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed across it as after the Vitalis and Vacuous Existential Domains...this Unstable Anomalous Existential Domain would be the third Domain to be devoured.

It was the true beginning of the expansion of the power of the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed- a construct that was adding dozens of Existential Extremity Authorities even at this very moment from the pillars of authorities that made it!
