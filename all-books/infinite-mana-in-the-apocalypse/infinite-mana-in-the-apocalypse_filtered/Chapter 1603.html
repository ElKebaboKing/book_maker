Chapter 1601 An Ushered Era of Chaos! ll

A party of terrifying Enders of Reality moved as they started off a maddening assassination attempt on a Future Candidate Ruler of a Pure-Blooded Cardinal Royal Bloodline!

But...this was not an isolated event.

Not by a long shot!

Across the vast Realities, many tumultuous events were occurring around major domains of power and areas where immense authority was concentrated under Primordials.

Across the vast Realities, outside the Boundary of a prominent Prime Reality of the Royal Wolf Bloodline that had an Eighth Firmament Overseer protected by many Royal Wolves at the Seventh Firmament.

Outside the Boundary of such a Reality, the Matriarch Belladonna could be seen along with the Blight Serpent Dragon Emperor as they had silently brought an army of Royal Blight Serpent Dragons outside the doors of a Prime Reality under Primordials, their armies not being alone as they were surrounded by terrific variations of Primordial Beasts releasing guttural roars of power!

Belladonna was bounded to her left by an Eighth Firmament Ender that had the body of an enormous Iridescent Purple Cosmic Serpent, its beastial eyes shining with malice as it roared out before descending unto the Prime Reality before them.

OOOOOH!

Surrounded by power on all sides, Belladonna had a sneer as she gazed at the unfolding chaos and slowly responding Primordials in the Reality below.

"It is time for the winds of change to ravage across Realities...it is time for the beginning of the end of the authority of Primordials!"

RUMBLE!

A declaration of war!

A war that would begin on this day and go on for a long, long time as the ushering of a new Era from an old one could not be done in a day.

Those acting to try and bring about a new Era knew this.

It was why they moved the way they moved as their actions spanned across countless Realities!

In another infinitesimally small region of the vast Realities.

The figure of a Draconic Elephantine Ender was watching thousands of Enders surrounded by Enlightened Zombies surge into a Prime Reality under the Cardinal Royal Solaris Simian Bloodline as his trunk bellowed out with excitement.

"I see."

Hyperion spoke out with fervor as he glanced at his destiny to see it rolling around with golden splendor.

"A war filled with Enders for me to devour...this is a glorious stage I cannot step off of!"

It was a selfish thought as a tumultuous and bloody conflict began. But in these vast Realities, one had to be cold-blooded and selfish if they ever wished to attain anything. So while seeing his very Bloodline declare war on Primordials and move while allied with other Bloodlines, Hyperion began to move and plan on how to best devour as many of these very same Enders to raise his own power and rise at the fastest possible pace during such a time!

In another region- this time near a Blue Tier Continuum Apocalypse Reality.

"You fucking dare?! Do you know who I am?!" With a look of rage and seriousness, an existence known as Europa stared at an Eighth Firmament Chthonian Telos backed by dozens of existences at the Seventh Firmament surrounding her and all those nearby as their eyes shone of malice and apocalypse.

"It is precisely because we know who you are that you must die, Princess. You and many others!"

The Eight Firmament Telos that released shocking waves of apocalyptic essence spoke coldly as it looked like a dead sea whale with a sparkling set of blue aurora wings, its eyes beginning to power up deadly lights thereafter as Europa's own eyes began to crackle with profound power while her body transformed into something else!

As she did so, her voice reverberated out to those around her.

"Eckert...bring out everything you have with your weak little Origin and try not to die. If you stay alive after all this...you might just have the bodies of many Seventh and Eighth Firmament fools under your Legions!"

WAA!

Her voice reached a blue eyed young man whose body was surrounded by waves of intense darkness, his eyes releasing tendrils of light as his face brought out into a devilish smile when facing enemies that would be certain death for others!

Such shocking events similarly unfolded across many regions of the vast Infinite Realities.

The Enders moved with shocking unity and timing as their targets were predetermined, whoever set up and planned such movements that spanned across different spacetimes being someone truly terrifying as their understanding of Destiny had to be entirely obscene!

This shock did not just pervade across the Infinite Realities though.

Across spaces and Dimension not seen by many, across Grotto Sanctums that powerful beings only knew about...golden blood spilled as a shocking cataclysm had begun at this time.

In a unique Dimension of Space that those of the Ninth Firmament of Ascendancy and below could not even reach.

"A Telos will still be a Telos at the end! We let you be our compatriot for all these eons and you dare to raise your eyes towards us?!"

RUMBLE!

A Reality shattering battle was unfolding in unseen planes of existence as the form of a creature the size of a Prime Reality was constantly flashing in and out of existence as his raucous laughter came out with ancientness and vicissitude.

"Haha...no need to be surprised, Little Vine Legend. This is simply my nature. The cycles of Continuum Apocalypse Realities satiated my hunger for Apocalypse briefly...but why would I not participate in Apocalypses of even grander scales? I would be going against my very own nature if I just stood by and let these Enders have all the fun!"

...!

Shock and horror bloomed across the explored Realities and Explored Unique Dimensions as very few areas were untouched by the ushering of a New Era.

An Era of war and Conquest that would benefit some while harming the vast majority of others!

The vast Realities moved at their own pace as they had countless billions of years of history.

No single being could think themselves to be a chosen one of these vast Realities!

No single being could think that everything revolves around them!

When the times of chaos came and the Age of Emperors fully bloomed, nobody knew who would survive and come out on top as an Emperor to be respected and revered!
