Chapter 3394 Tyrannical Oppression! II

As Noah gained another Megalos Regnum Crown in one area within the Megalos Ynnere.

The Selection here was the fastest compared to the Quintessential, Rare, or Average Tresorverse Towers of Existence.

They left behind weaker call Ordinance of Existence beings who gritted their teeth and scrambled away to fight for the lesser Tresoverse Towers of Existence!

Within this vast space where all those that had entered the Trunk of the Primaltree of Existence were sent to, the throne Noah was on rapidly shot out into a distant golden tower.

The golden tower itself looked like a massive monolith that opened up and swallowed him whole- his body direction through layers of weavings before the crown settled itself in the center of the tower.

From the inside, the walls were transparent and vast as Noah could see what was happening outside, but the most important thing was the dazzling allure of gold light that was spinning in the very center of this tower!

As he arrived, so did the monotone intent that was ringing out this whole time arrive.

|Familiarize yourself with the Origin Sacred Complexity Modus Operandi and prepare to defend against hordes of prisoners put in a position just below you, with their freedom contingent on defeating you and destroying the towers that you and others occupy.|

The spinning golden singularity was the promised Origin Sacred Complexity Modus Operandi.

When its hazy glow finished, Noah clearly saw an extremely complex rune that held a profound complexity similar to the Amarantos Rune of Existence!

Similar- but not all the way at its level as it still seemed a level off.

But this Rune of Existence was a terrifying one that allowed one to increase their output of weavings of Existence by 500%!

Noah didn't like that it was another person's Rune of Existence, and they would know every single entity who was utilizing their Rune- but he knew this was a temporary measure.

Once he broke down the components of each Rune of Existence and understood it entirely, he could engineer his own and make it even better than before.

HUUM!

He reached out and grasped the golden Rune of Existence as it settled into his weavings- coming to settle near his chest as it pulsed with a dim gold light, asking to be activated!

Above Noah, a dazzling Sacred Primal Eye of Existence opened as he observed the intricacies of this Rune of Existence while allowing a dense amount of his weavings to flow into it, instantly causing it to spin and radiate circuits- meridians all across his body as there were 81 in number.

81 dazzling gold meridians that looked like Ethereal rivers of gold stretched out across his weavings of existence!

When his weavings flowed into them, they increased in power and potency every time they completed a cycle across all these Meridians.

The more of his weavings he put in, the more this output of power increased as his body began brimming with pressure many times more powerful than before!

This...was what a single powerful Rune of Existence could achieve.

The Great Beyonder had put out Crucibles of the Beyonder that contained things like the Amarantos Rune of Existence, the Defy Rune of Existence, and the Sword Rune of Existence.

The Omnifather took a different route and put out different variations of Runes of Existence that increased the output of one's weavings of existence!

Different methodologies, but the same results of strengthening their respective Megalos in the end.

Noah knew there had to be an equivalent entity similar to the Great Beyonder and the Omnifather on the side of Megalos Nihilos, and he felt sour missing whatever boons it had prepared for the entities within Nihilos!

'Hopefully, that guy is enjoying some of it at least...'

His eyes twinkled as he thought of the Great Usurper who had crossed Megalos Ynnere and into the Megalos Nihilos before the boons of the Omnifather had bloomed.

Alongside his thoughts, he sat down and analyzed the Origin Sacred Complexity Rune of Existence, being lost in his own weavings for some time!

Unknowingly, a few hours passed.

In the surroundings, other selections were completed as those who had enough strength to obtain ownership of Quintessential, Rare, and Average Tresorverse Towers of Existence received them. Noah's attention only came to when he felt the buzzing of the Tresoverse Domain and a booming voice that spread out all around.

|The selection of the Towers of Existence is completed. Within the next few moments, the Prisoners dwelling in this Domain will be released. Defend against them and hone the complexity of your Weavings of Existence. Be defeated, and be lost in the cycles of endless weavings.|

...!

The voice was cold as if it was letting a dangerous reality be known- that if they faltered, they could very well perish here!

Almost as if they truly were in defensive positions and they needed to ensure that nothing bypassed them!

They had their own battles to fight as the region a single Ordinance of Existence entity covered where they could exert their power may just be the equivalent of a single Omniverse or less.

It would be a war of expansive scale!

WU!

Existences that were very reminiscent in the eyes of any entities that were lucky enough to enjoy the Reconstruction of the Megalonians!
