Chapter 348 - Unmatched Judgment!

Everywhere that these tremendously large Blades of Liberation passed, numerous beings no matter their power levels were sliced through, the blades sliding down as if they were cutting through butter.

<strong>[Judgement](5/5) :: A declaration of death for all those that stand against you is called forth. [Judgment].
