Chapter 1137 - Antiquity Of Mana! II

Cosmic Primordial Beasts!

Enders of Realities!

Existences that even those in the Cosmic Realm would be hard pressed to fight, existences that more often than not- Primordials had to take care of.

Primordials...or the special and unique champions that they raised across different realities.

The existence that the Primordial was watching wasnt matching an Ender of Reality, just a Primordial Beast that was a level lower. But if anyone within dozens of Cosmos claimed they could do what this being was doing...they would be lying!

THRUM!

The innumerable colorful lines and runic circles rotating on the skin of the ones known as Aegon thrummed powerfully, his body shining like a treasure as he moved across the Ruination Sea fearlessly.

ROOOAAR!

The Grand Primordial Beast also bellowed out as the Ruination Sea around them fell under its control, its entire body suffused with Primordial and Ruination Essence as it seemed like it could change its entire nature into these very Daos at any second!

SHAA!

A glorious light flashed as the figure of Aegon and the Grand Primordial Beast passed each other.

BOOOM!

A sound akin to a Universe shattering exploded out, golden blood from the Grand Primordial Beast erupting out as two of its wings were actually ripped apart, the golden blood from them still freshly flowing from the hands of the dominantly colored Aegon.

The Ruination and Primordial Essence flowing through this creature couldnt stop what had just occurred, as Aegon didnt use any flashy moves and simply utilized his very own hands to somehow rip apart a pair of wings from the terrifying Primordial Beast!

...!

His eyes were without any enthusiasm or happiness as he looked at such a result in utter calm, the angered roars of the Beast not even being registered by him as he moved forward again, the many runic lines and circles moving colorfully across his body being the reason for his shocking power as he moved without any flashy skills to tear apart a Beast with thousands of Splendiferous Universes and completely assimilated Ruination and Primordial Daos.

Just what was the mystery behind his strength? How could he so easily bypass the stupendous might of fully assimilation Ruination and Primordial Daos?!

The mystery would remain unsolved as in the turbid Ruination Sea, golden blood overflowed.

The regal and sacred looking Grand Primordial Beast continued to release shocking power and awe, but it was still being ripped apart endlessly by this single being that had his body shining gloriously with an array of rainbow colored lights!

ROAR!

The cries became weaker and weaker as minutes passed, the Ruination Sea nearby becoming dyed gold as in a short period of time, the weakening roars ultimately ceased.

"..."

The majestic figure of the multicolored Aegon shone gloriously in the Ruination Sea, glancing at the lakes of golden blood around him as within his hands, a colorful heart thumped powerfully.

A Grand Primordial Heart pulsed with glorious power while still dripping golden blood, this existence doing something stupefying in the next moment as he opened his mouth and threw this heart into his mouth, golden blood spurting out amidst his chewing!

This Primordial Heart was nothing like the one Noah had received as it was light years in sheer size alone, the stupendous existence actually chewing this heart and devouring it raw as in this process, his sharp eyes gazed towards a certain direction.

A few trillions of miles away in the Ruination Sea, it was the spot where a certain Primordial was watching the events unfold, her veil shaking slightly as she raised her head, a hidden smile appearing that nobody could see as she tore through the Ruination Sea to appear out grandly!

---

As a meeting occurred after a grand battle countless light years away, another one formed in the Microbial Universe of the Infinite Cosmos.

It contained a majority if not all of the Hegemonies that spanned from the 54 Universes, all of them converging here amidst their shock as this was the place where from its boundary, they could see the thin veil of another Universe that had not been here for the millions of years that even the oldest of them had been living in the Primordial Cosmos!

They also gathered here for answers as after Oathkeeper had sent a message for them to not interfere when they sensed changes occurring across many universes, he also told all of them to gather in the Microbial Universe if they wished for answers.

And so they did.

These powerful beings were clustered around each other with somber expressions as they were very aware of what had been occurring the past few minutes, noticing the wondrous light of assimilation that passed through many Universes as well as the appearance of this new Universe.

As for the being responsible? All of them had an inkling of who it was as the Great Old Nazzagath was the first to turn his head from the direction of the newly formed Universe!

WAA!

From there, the figure of a devilishly handsome young man appeared, his brows sharp as his eyes glimmered with a golden and crimson light.

His body released the powerful aura of an Antiquity as Great Old Nazzagath and the other Hegemonies found their Origins shaking when they noticed the manifestation of 4 wondrous Universes, 3 of them being completed Splendiferous Universes that shone with a fantastical glow!

All of the powerhouses of the Infinite Cosmos gazed towards Noahs figure with a myriad of thoughts as a white crack expanded above each of them soon after, Primordial Essence gushing out of it as the figures of Oathkeeper and Noahs Primordial Ruination Clone that looked exactly the same as his main body that just stepped out of the newly formed Novus Universe.

Oathkeeper glanced at Noahs main body and then back to the Primordial Ruination Clone that had been with him all this time in the unique Primordial Sea of the past Primordial Cosmos, this ancient being sighing out slowly as his gaze landed on the Hegemonies of the now Infinite Cosmos.

"I guess now is a good time as any for introductions."

RUMBLE!

The voice of Oathkeeper reverberated out as it carried with it the unique aura of Antiquity, catching the eyes of all Hegemonies as they felt very familiar with Oathkeeper! He was someone they knew very closely as even though he had achieved a grand stage before them, they still saw him as their own.

They paid attention to him as he gazed and pointed towards Noahs figure that just stepped out of the Novus Universe.

"You knew him as the Apex Paragon just days ago. He achieved Hegemony in the past day as hours later...he achieved Antiquity."

...!

Even though they knew the sequence of events, the Hegemonies still had their Origins tremble in a stupor at this incredible reality being real as they also felt ashamed of themselves for having spent hundreds of thousands if not millions of years in the stage of Hegemonies!

But the Oathkeeper paid no attention to their emotions as this ancient being had already learned many lessons recently, thinking of how to introduce Noah now that he had achieved Antiquity.

He could base it off of the most unique Dao this being comprehended as he introduced him as the Antiquity of Ruination, but the golden eyes of the Oathkeeper shone as he truly thought about another unique feature that he had seen with this being over and over again since the appearance of the Universal Constructs and this whole ordeal!

The unique feature of this being that as a Vassal, he felt very closely at this moment as it had made him overly powerful.

It was the feature of mana!

"I introduce to you all the being responsible for our current state of affairs, the Antiquity...of Mana!"

RUMBLE!
