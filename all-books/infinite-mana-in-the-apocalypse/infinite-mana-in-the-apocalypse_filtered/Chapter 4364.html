Chapter 4362: The Glorious Evolution! II

"The First Tier represents baseline achievement," RUINEDEN explained. "Those who enter the Chamber and survive the anchoring process will emerge with power that would make common Early Creature seem like children."

The second representation blazed brighter.

|TIER TWO: ENHANCED EVOLUTION|

|Requirements: Withstanding increased Mana Haki pressure + Demonstrating exceptional compatibility|

|Results:|

|Complexity: 2 Quintillion|

|Purity: 2 Quintillion|

|Enhanced Mana-Born Heart of an Early Creature|

|Designation: Advanced Mana-Born Early Creature|

|Additional Capability: Direct Mana manipulation at scales that First Tier cannot achieve|

"The Second Tier requires more," RUINEDEN continued. "Not just survival, but excellence. Not just endurance, but demonstration that the entity can bear weights that would crush weaker beings. Those who achieve this evolution emerge twice as powerful as First Tier, with capabilities that extend beyond simple power increase."

And finally, the third representation blazed with radiance that made the previous two look dim.

|TIER THREE: THE GLORIOUS EVOLUTION|

|Requirements: Withstanding maximum Mana Haki pressure + Perfect compatibility with Way of Mana Architect + Existential resilience that transcends normal limits|

|Results:|

|Complexity: 3 Quintillion|

|Purity: 3 Quintillion|

|Prime Mana-Born Heart of an Early Creature|

|Designation: Prime Mana-Born Early Creature|

|Additional Capabilities: Mana regeneration that approaches infinite, Authority over Mana itself within localized areas, Capability to grant temporary Mana augmentation to allies|

|Note: This tier represents peak achievement possible within current Chamber configuration. Those who emerge from Glorious Evolution are beings who could stand against Prime Dead Early Creatures and survive.|

RUINEDENs voice carried reverence as she explained the mechanism.

"When an entity steps into the Chamber," she said, "they will be anchored through those blue vessels you see extending throughout the structure. And once anchored, their existence will be forced to bear the Mana Haki of your... Infinite Mana."

Her expression grew more intense.

"Mana Haki is weight made manifest," she explained. "Not physical pressure, but existential weight...the crushing force of unlimited power pressing down on consciousness that has limits. Its like asking finite being to hold infinity in their hands without being crushed by the impossibility."

The diagrams showed entities being subjected to this pressure, their forms straining under burden that exceeded their capacity to comprehend.

"Depending on how much of this force they can bear," RUINEDEN continued, "depending on how long they can withstand pressure that increases exponentially with each moment, depending on whether their existence proves resilient enough to be augmented rather than shattered... the entity will gain different tier of evolution."

She paused, making sure Noah understood the significance.

"The pressure is real. The danger is genuine. Those who cannot withstand even First Tier pressure will collapse, their existences unmade by weight they attempted to bear. But those who survive..."

Her smile widened.

"Those who survive will emerge as new species of Early Creature. Mana-Born. Forged in your Chamber. Carrying your authority. Walking your Way."

Noahs expression remained cool, but internally, satisfaction blazed!

This was his power.

This was what he could accomplish through combination of impossible Principles, reality-breaking cheat codes, and collaboration with entities who understood his vision.

He held the Incarnaut Sanctis...a Chamber that existed simultaneously within his Heart and manifested externally on his Shore. And ridiculously, with help from RUIN/EDEN and The Infiniverse, with Perpetual Harvest weaponizing effort into immediate reward...

He could now produce up to 9 Mana-Born Early Creatures.

Beings who could potentially possess power at 1, 2, or 3 Quintillion depending on their resilience and compatibility.

It was ridiculous.

It was broken.

It was his Civilization taking shape according to principles that current-era existence couldnt comprehend.

RUIN/EDENs final words carried weight of profound acknowledgment.

"This is the harvest and results of your effort, Master," she said. "Everything youve accumulated, every Principle youve integrated, every impossible achievement youve accomplished...it all culminates in this capability. The power to elevate others not through slow cultivation but through transformative evolution that compresses eons into moments."

Noah smiled.

He looked around the Early Veiled Shore, his gaze tracking across the beings who had chosen to follow his Way or been bound to it through various circumstances.

Sigrid was already a given...THE Living Order would undergo evolution, would emerge with power that matched her conceptual significance. She would be first, or among the first, because her role was too critical to delay.

But who else?

The limit was 9 currently, though he knew that as he continued completing his Heart, as the Incarnaut Sanctis matured and integrated more deeply with his existence, that number would rise steadily.

And when it came to effort, when it came to beings who had demonstrated loyalty and capability...

Noahs eyes swept across the Shore, cataloging potential candidates with calculating precision.

Henry, his Nepotism burning brightly.

Moiraine, whose paradoxical nature pulsed. The Emperor Penguin, Malphas...

Even beings like the Arch Lich RaZan, who commanded Death itself.

And bouncing in the distance, occasionally phasing through solid matter with cheerful disregard, a particularly enthusiastic Blue Slime.

But as Noahs gaze swept across these potential candidates, it stopped on a figure that made him pause.

Titano.

The simple-minded giant stood near the edge of one of the Sacred Water pools, his massive form somehow managing to project contentment despite his size.

He was looking around with expression of pure, uncomplicated happiness...just enjoying being surrounded by unique beings in a space that welcomed him.

No schemes. No hidden agendas. No complex motivations.

Just Titano, happy to be here, grateful for inclusion, content with simplicity.

Sometimes, the best candidates were the ones who didnt overthink things.

"Titano!" Noah called out, his voice carrying across the Shore.

The simple-minded giants head turned, his expression brightening immediately into sheepish smile.

He scratched his head, a gesture that suggested embarrassment at being singled out, then lumbered over with movements that somehow managed to be both massive and gentle.

"How can Titano help Brother?" he asked, his words carrying that particular simple-minded cadence.

Noah looked up at the giant and smiled.

"Go in there," he said, gesturing toward the Incarnaut Sanctis. "I want to test something out. Your existence will undergo another change."

He paused, making sure Titano understood.

"If you can withstand the pressure inside as long as you can, you will gain tremendous power. And if you gain power..."

Titanos eyes lit up with understanding, his expression transforming into excitement that transcended his usual contentment.

"More power means more riches!" he declared, the logic absolutely sound in his simple worldview.

Noah nodded, satisfaction warming him at the giants enthusiasm.

"Yep. Go on."

Titano didnt hesitate. No questions about danger, no concerns about transformation, no complex analysis of risk versus reward.

Just immediate, trusting obedience based on belief that Brother wouldnt lead him wrong.

The giant approached the Incarnaut Sanctis with steps that made the golden sands tremble, his massive form dwarfing the Chambers entrance despite its substantial size.

But as he crossed the threshold, as he entered the space that existed simultaneously in multiple dimensions, Titanos form seemed to shift, accommodating itself to interior that operated on different spatial principles than exterior suggested.

The entrance sealed behind him with pulse of crimson-gold light, and suddenly, every being on the Early Veiled Shore could sense what was happening inside!
