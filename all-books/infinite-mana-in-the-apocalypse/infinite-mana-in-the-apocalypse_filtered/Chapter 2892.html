Quintessential clarity boomed in him.

What felt foreign and difficult to understand and do before became easy.

Quintessence. Tyranny. Infinity. Protagonist.

Their roots within him entrenched themselves ever deeper as what they stood for- their desires, their intent, their weaving...it grew ever more.

{Your authority as the Weaver of Extremity has risen to such an extent that in addition to being capable of grasping the weavings of Extremes, Records, and Existence, you can now begin to experience their weavings as if you were there.}

{What a wonder you are...Relativity...}

His voice was deep and full of power.

Not as his normal self...but as Emperor Delbert.

He was Emperor Delbert. It wasn't a possession or an experience, he truly and succinctly felt that at this moment, he was truly this being!

{The Weaver of Extremity has unearthed and stepped into the past weaving of Existence of Emperor Delbert.}

The Extremity of Relativity that under the hands of Emperor Delbert was about to achieve the Existential level!
