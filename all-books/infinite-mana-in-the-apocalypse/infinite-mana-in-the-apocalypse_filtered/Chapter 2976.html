Salus Populi Suprema Lex Esto!

Those who stood to reclaim the dignity of the masses.

Those who put the welfare of people above all else!

A dance began to ebb and flow.

An Emperor born and reborn through countless fires of Quintessence as now, he could finally gain a brief moment of respite to understand the power he held, and just what existence meant for him who had pioneered into the Existential Dominium Realm, forged an Omniversal Lineage, and had his construct take the steps to become an Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed!

An area so endlessly vast that even with the passage of eons, it was hard to say if one had ever caught a glimpse of its end.

Throughout the Omniverse, across the swathes of non-

existence, there would always be points of existence stretched throughout.

Existential Domains.

Massive pockets of existence that held the weavings and records of an uncountable number of creatures in each one, with the lives of trillions sometimes not knowing about the quintillions of others stretched out in the same Existential Domains!

Even the Gradations of Extremity they climbed may not be the same!

Even the Dominiums they formed...may not be the same.
