Chapter 4638: Actualization III

An entity at THE Surface Depth of Absolute Sovereignty.

But her Depth was fractured, compromised, fundamentally weakened.

Noah gazed at the details with calm analytical focus.

At this moment, THE Dead Order finally reacted to his intrusion.

Her body exploded with terrifying power as her eyes burned with glory and grace.

"Do you think it will be the same as last time you came here?! You have no flames of THE Living Elemental to protect you now! You are now fully within my domain, my space of absolute authority! This time you will NOT leave! I thank you for your utter stupidity and hubris to think that after defeating a few Pre-THE entities and metal constructs of Paradox that you would somehow be ready for the power of a genuine THE entity! I thank you for delivering yourself to me!"

As she spoke, her Way erupted with devastating force.

THE Civilizational Authority of Order surged from every direction to surround Noah completely.

White-gold light flooded the space as authority sought to lock him down from all angles.

The veil behind him became utterly covered and sealed to ensure he could not retreat back to Sigrids side.

"You are now locked in here with me, and I will take my time with you. Slowly. Methodically. Until there is nothing left."

At such words, THE Civilizational Authority of Order thundered down onto Noah to bind his existence completely.

And yet shockingly, the crushing authority seemed to stop mere inches away from his skin.

As if striking an invisible barrier it could not penetrate.

He raised his hand calmly and formed a single simple stick.

The Goad.

The First Lesson.

THE Potentates First Lesson, which had mainly been used to discipline THE entities.

THE Genesis Monarch of Infinite Lexicons First Lesson!

Before its authority, what was a THE entity with a fractured Depth?

Noahs voice emerged with grand certainty that filled the entire space.

"I am not locked in here with you, O Order. You are locked in here with me. And what a terrible place it is for you to be trapped."

The next scene unfolded in glorious slow motion.

The eyes of THE Dead Order opened wide in mounting shock as she witnessed her authority accomplishing absolutely nothing.

And then Noah struck his stick outward.

The movement appeared slow and deliberate, each increment of motion visible and trackable.

Yet paradoxically, the speed exceeded Planck Speed by incomprehensible margins.

The moment he completed the strike, the stick arrived before THE Dead Orders head.

It enlarged explosively as it approached.

Time seemed to stretch impossibly as the Goad made contact.

Multiple Logos of The First Tongue flashed brilliantly along the sticks surface with the impact.

The Phonemes of Beginning, Division, and Ending arranged themselves in declarative structure of Logos that most closely translated to: "What begins whole becomes divided and ends fractured."

The stick smashed into the forehead of THE Dead Order with devastating precision.

Her skin, which should have been inviolable for an entity of her classification, ruptured instantly.

The flesh tore open as bone beneath fractured audibly.

White-gold blood began seeping from the wound as her entire head rocked backward from the force.

Then Noah took a single step forward.

His glorious body adorned in that imperious blue-gold robe moved with fluid grace.

The spinning crown above his head blazed with Fragments of Phonemes as he moved.

He appeared instantly above the split forehead of THE Dead Order.

And with calm deliberation, he sat directly on her head.

His expression remained utterly cold and analytical as both hands reached down.

His fingers gripped both sides of the torn forehead firmly as Logos began manifesting across his arms in intricate patterns.

The Phonemes of Earth and Division combined to grant tremendous physical strength and the ability to separate what should be unified.

Solid bronze radiance reinforced his muscles and bones.

Silver fractures of Division ran along his fingers, creating cutting edges that could tear through conceptual bonds.

The Phoneme of Void opened within his palms, creating spaces that pulled matter apart.

The Phoneme of Ending manifested as deep violet dusk energy that concluded the integrity holding her skull together.

These Logos glowed with blue-gold radiance as they enhanced his capability exponentially.

Using the power of these combined Phonemes, he held THE Dead Orders struggling form completely still.

And then he pulled with both hands.

To further split her torn head apart.

The bloodied eyes of THE Dead Order showed utter horror and complete disbelief.

Her entire majestic presence seemed to be getting literally torn apart by this Pre-THE entity.

She tried to scream but found her voice trapped by the Logos binding her.

She attempted to unleash her Civilizational Authority but found it suppressed by linguistic superiority.

She sought to call upon her protection but discovered it took time to manifest.

And through all of this, Noah continued pulling steadily and methodically.

The terrible ripping sound echoed through the space as her skull began separating.

White-gold blood flowed more freely as the wound deepened.

In the next moment, Noah truly split the head of THE Dead Order completely.

Her skull separated down the middle as he tore it open like breaking fruit.

And the moment the split was complete, he formed another complex Logos using multiple Core Phonemes.

The Phoneme of Water for adaptation and flow.

The Phoneme of Beginning to initiate transfer.

The Phoneme of Unity to connect what was separated.

The Phoneme of Division to separate what should not remain together.

The Phoneme of Space to create proper distance and channel.

These Phonemes wove together into a Logos of tremendous complexity that most closely translated to: "What flows from one beginning divides from unity and unites across space with another."

THE Civilizational Authority of Order contained within THE Dead Orders fractured existence surged outward like a white-gold tsunami.

It flowed across the space toward the distant Sigrid in unstoppable waves, following the channel created by Noahs Logos.
