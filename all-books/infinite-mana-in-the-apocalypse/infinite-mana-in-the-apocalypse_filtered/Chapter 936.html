Chapter 934 - A line up of Champions from afar! I

Galaxy B-41.

Within this galaxy, Noahs terrifying Nether Lich Emperor that was using a Cosmic Dao as the base of its strength moved unhindered across the vast space!

OOOOH!

Behind him, an Undead Legion that filled the nearby space for thousands of miles could be seen, each of them with red flickering flames that made it seem like billions of red lights were moving across the starry space.

In the periphery of this Undead Legion, forces of Storm Dragons could be seen moving with them as in all the Galaxies that Noahs subordinates were teleported into, Tiamat had used her main body or clones to descend down and give orders to the Dragons within these galaxies to work with Noahs forces!

"Kekekeke!"

The wild laughter of the lift resounded across the stellar bodies of space, the flames within its sockets brightening up as it sensed many life forms ahead of them.

They neared the core location of their enemies, but the laughter of the Lich wasnt caused by it just sensing the bloodline races spread throughout...but the aura of another being just like it that should not be here!

WAA!

Like a tide, the Undead Legion that numbered billions thundered forward, the Sagely Necrolords leading them as the scene in front of them cleared.

It was a cl.u.s.ter of stars and planets that surrounded an enormous red crack in space, this crack glimmering with splendorous stellar light as it seemed extremely inviting for anyone that neared it!

But the cl.u.s.ter of stars and planets were surrounded by figures from 2 Bloodline Races and...the deathly aura of 3 Lich Emperors!

"Kekekeke! It shall be a dance of death on this day. What brought my brethren here?"

The Nether Lich Emperor ignored the forces of White Tiger Race and the Qilin Race, focusing his eyes on the Lich Emperors that should not be here.

"The same reason why you are here, young lich. Our Masters have merely called us all here."

RUMBLE!

The eyes of the Nether Lich Emperor blazed brightly as the red flames that contained a cosmic essence rose, its teeth clattering as it spoke domineeringly.

"Then let us not disappoint our Masters! Kekekeke!"

...!

A shocking battle was about to unfold within this Galaxy as in another, the forces of Dragons being led by Barbatos, Morgana, the Sword Emperor and a few others also arrived at the core region of their galaxy where the Bloodline Races controlled the gateway that led to the Slaughter Star Monolith.

In front of them, there was the force of the Nine-Tailed Fox Race, Phoenix Race, and the Golden Cyclops Race!

They held tens of Great Sages that looked towards them with dangerous eyes, but the more shocking thing was that amongst them A Lich Emperor could be seen along with...caped beings!

Beings that all held powerful strength at the Realm of Great Sage, with their figures sporting ridiculous getups as they seemed out of place when looking at the fantastical Undead Liches and Phoenixes in the surroundings.

"This just became much, much harder..." Morganas voice rang out as her eyes shone with a destructive light, her body seemingly trembling in excitement while looking at the force of beings displayed in front of them!

"We might need extra forces if we come across this many of them..." The Sword Emperor analyzed everything carefully as he spoke, a terrifying battle set in place within this Galaxy as even more shocking ones were about to occur across all the galaxies Noahs forces were currently moving in.

They passed word to Noah who was already actively analyzing the situation, but even more wondrous scenes were occurring in other Galaxies at this very moment!

Within Galaxy B-35.

In front of the stronghold where the bloodline races occupied another entrance into the Slaughter Star Monolith, terrifying star sized creatures stood while facing an even larger tentacled being that pulsed with the power of Aether!

It was the creatures called from the Chthonian Universe, their eyes glimmering with light as they came face to face with Cthulhu and the other Terrors that were flanking its left and right.

"What...are you...?"

OOOOM

The worm shaped creature from the Chthonian Universe had its voice reverberate out as Cthulhus star sized eyes looked towards this worm like being that neared his size coldly.

"I dont make a habit of talking with creatures about to die, so lets just do this quickly."

Aether palpated majestically from the innumerable tentacles that the star sized Cthulhu released, dying the starry space a shade of purple as even the Dragons that followed this Terror of Aether stayed far away, with their Great Sages only honing their eyes towards the bloodline races that could barely be seen as blips behind the Chthonian Worm!

Beside them, the enormous figures of the Terror of the Void and the Ancient Terror stood to the left and right of the Cthulhu, their gazes on the enormous monstrosities beside the speaking Worm that seemed like deformed Chthonian horrors!

"A fallen Chthonian...any of those that deny the Master...shall perish!"

RUMBLE!

The body of the enormous worm rumbled as it barreled forward, its tentacles outstretched as a shocking battle erupted, the other majestically enormous creatures moving towards the Ancient Terror and the Terror of the Void while the Bloodline Races thundered towards the Dragons!
