Chapter 439 - Growth, Treasures

As Noah made his way south, his forces propagated various areas of the Celestial Realm as their increased power only allowed for more efficient and effective cooperation that took down behemoths with ease.

Not to mention their own recently upgraded ranks and cores from Soul Rank Behemoths continued to flow towards their hands, the boon from the [Golden Guider] skill was so overpowered that when they faced the Fire Behemoths to the north, they could take them down much faster as each of them held a hint of the law of fate in their attacks.

Most of the forces were now actively moving as the things around Noah became more and more expansive, even the lazy Barbatos joining the Order of Blood Exploration Team as her own specialized legion of undead joined the fights.

Others were either put on tasks to manage the newly added worlds or hunting Behemoths for their resources, with these resources being transferred into the Spiritual World through the Emperor Penguin that held ever so powerful spatial skills.

These abundant materials would then be processed by the System at the center of the Spiritual World as they were forged into Items in the Blacksmith's Forge, powerful Sets of armors and weapons that had overwhelming effects being created with ease.

Currently, the number of Blood Stained Infernal Oath Sets made from the bodies of Infernals were the most numerous as Noah had simply hunted too many of them to obtain his Infernal Lord title. The Incandescent Flameborn Set was next in production as more and more materials from Fire Behemoths were obtained, these powerful Sets that had a 6 piece bonus of an Immolation ability that allowed the wearer to deal 1000% more damage being something extremely deadly.

As Noah's forces gradually became equipped with these terrifying Items and the boosts from the Golden Guider, their power was spilling upwards exponentially as they were growing to become a powerful force!

It was not just Noah that was currently rapidly advancing through ranks, his forces were also following along!

His Ancient Blood Clone continued flying through the mystical forest of the Celestial Realm, the young dragon Draco enjoying the return to the overflowing essence of the realm as it broke the sound barrier to shoot towards the direction that its master directed it towards at high speeds.

"Slow down."

Noah sent his thoughts forward as he felt the golden arrow from the Spiritual World beginning to blink, the dragon slowing down and reducing its altitude as Noah came to view the environment to the south of the Celestial Realm with more clarity.

Huge ancient trees with blue colored leaves were abundant, with numerous lakes that sparkled clearness and serenity spread out all around. As he spread his senses further out with the dragon slowly moving forward, he could see rushing rivers, and even further locations being areas of frozen lands where he even spotted an Ice Behemoth.

In the South of the Celestial Realm, one could come across Behemoths proficient in the law of water, whether they were plain Water Behemoths or the specific Ice Behemoths.

ROAR!

The arrival of a Draconic Fire Behemoth in the skies drew the attention of a nearby Ice Behemoth, but Noah ignored it as Draco continued towards a certain location, Noah following the blinking arrow closely as it pointed towards a sparkling and expansive lake that was surrounded by trees.

Draco hovered over the pristine lake as Noah saw the arrow to be pointing down, sending his thoughts to his pet as Draco was transferred back into the Spiritual World, the figure of the clone shooting down towards the clear lake speedily as it went to see just what exactly the [Treasure Hunter] feature had found now.

SHAA!

His body pierced through the waters with ease as he began descending down, the lake being very deep as he spent a few minutes diving down and watching the area around him.

His body was filled with covert skills after he sent Draco away so that he did not get discovered too easily if there was something powerful down the lake, and his senses already caught one Water Behemoth happily swimming a few miles away.

After achieving the Domain Expansion Realm, Noah felt an astounding level of control of his energy as the Domain that wrapped around his origin allowed him to feel a sense of stableness that he never came across before. He saw that his progress in the Fire Origin Core even increased after achieving the Domain Rank, seeing that higher realms held their own secrets and surprises to unfold as he continued on in the future.

His figure continued shooting down the lake for another few minutes before he neared the bottom the area around him becoming colder as his eyes came across a cold hard floor that shone with a blue crystalline l.u.s.ter!

His senses tried spreading through this layer of earth to see what lied below it, but he surprisingly found that he could not spread his aura past it, whatever the Spiritual World was pointing down towards still not known.

His fingers tapped onto the earth as he found it to be extremely hard material, but its uniqueness lied in the fact that even a being at the Domain Rank could not spread his aura past it. Noah chose the less destructive way as he became extremely small and minute through [Unique Origin], using his very small size and immensely boosted strength to drill through this unique earthen layer.

It wasn't even that thick, his body being able to pierce through the other end in a matter of minutes as he entered into the hidden space that lied below the frozen floor at the bottom of the lake.

His eyes looked around as they shimmered a color of blue, this color being reflected by everything he was seeing as the place he had entered...looked to be a huge stalagmite mine!

Numerous Crystals littered the area, his eyes seeing that the space was not even that huge, maybe the size of a football field. But this space held an array of Crystals Stalagmites that shone with a deep blue hue, Noah sensing a congregation of the Law of Water deeply entrenched into every single one of them.

Though the blue crystals were many, they were not the most eye-catching thing in this hidden cavern. The most eye-catching thing was that at the center of this place, there laid an even more enormous crystal in the shape of a spiked crown, most of the energies around this place congregating towards it! Noah's gaze landed on this vibrantly dense blue crystal as a few words popped up in front of his eyes.

<strong>[Unique Material- Aquamarine Mesrandite].</strong>

His eyes shone with understanding as he laid his eyes upon the first-ever Unique Material that he came across, his goal of searching for an inheritance leading him to another treasure entirely as he actually came across something which could be used to refine Supreme Treasures, or even possibly create one!
