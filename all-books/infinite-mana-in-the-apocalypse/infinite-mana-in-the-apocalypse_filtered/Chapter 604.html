Chapter 602 - Into the Nebula Realm!

On another side of the Chaotic Void, a singular being in the Novus Galaxy continued to advance forward as multiple milestones were achieved.

The first milestone was the continued upgrade of his subordinates, the Harbingers of Sin and other powerful beings overseeing the Novus Galaxy actually being granted entrance past the golden barrier of the Central Realm!

Using the ability of Pocket Realm, Noah used it as a simple transfer portal as his subordinates came into the depths of the Central Realm with ease, their minds being shaken once more as they sensed an abundant atmospheric essence that was even greater than the Infinite Realm that was still expanding even now.

Noah had brought his subordinates over to the mystical clearing that had numerous Inheritance Crystals which contained the techniques of comprehending universal laws, and he gave them the option of choosing what law they wanted to follow!

Unlike him, none of them could afford to focus their attention on multiple laws, with his subordinates choosing the law that fit them best as they also embarked on their path to become future Controllers.

Kazuhikos eyes shone when he glanced towards the Crystal containing the techniques of the Law of Chaos, looking at the dark glint as he rushed towards it right away. To him, he would finally be achieving his dream of releasing dark rays of chaotic light from his blade once he became proficient in the law of chaos!

Athena had only come into the Central Realm briefly as she sought to learn more of the truth of this realm that many Celestials fought for, being one of the last few remaining Celestials who had even been able to enter past the golden barrier. She remained on the path of the Law of Fate as her resolve became even stronger when looking at the level of strength her Junior Brother displayed, knowing that she had to work that much harder.

Others like the Kraken were humble as they remained comprehending the normal laws of Fire, Air, Earth, and Water. One particular Emperor Penguin was even bolder and more arrogant than the rest as he chose to study both the law of Time and Space, making it his goal to increase his spatial abilities and combining it with time to be even more deadly!

"RAA! RAA!"

Excitement was abound in the hearts of Noahs subordinates as they learned these techniques as well as the treasures of the Central Realm. Things like Luminescent Rainbow Dews that could upgrade one into the World Rank were free and many, with other treasures of similar potency or even stronger being able to be found in these deep areas of the realm.

Their goals of rapidly increasing in strength to catch up to Noah seemed to be possible at this moment as the next few days were spent with them making rapid progress!

The second milestone was the bringing another Skill Tree into the Star Forging Realm, Noah getting ready for whatever was coming as best as he could, and he chose the goal of bringing at least one more skill tree into the Nebula Realm before whoever received the released Signal came.

The skill trees he chose for this task were the Fates Guardian skill trees! He chose Fates Guardian naturally because the law of Fate was currently one of the only laws he had at 100%, and the skill tree would benefit greatly from its backing.

Over the past few days, the product of this skill tree at the Star Forging Realm had been produced as it added even more to his abilities, their skills seeming subpar at this point compared to Terror of Cthulhu skill tree currently at the Nebula Realm.

He gained another pair of skills that passively increased his comprehension of the law of Fate, as well as a skill termed Celestial King that was very similar to what Aldrich had used in the past battle!

But this skill tree was just one of the major things in Noahs mind, because he was currently focused on absorbing the Nebula Realm cores that he had obtained from the defeat of Aldrich.

He had chosen not to convert the Origin of Aldrich into skill points in order to obtain these cores, as the strength and resiliency of his level would matter just as much as the perks being given by becoming a Controller as he advanced to further stages!

From the stage of a Star Forger, one would have to destroy their very Origin that was in the shape of a star as they later used the shattered dust of this star to try and form a Nebula, an Origin that was so strong that it would contain multiple miniature small stars.

As Noah absorbed more and more Nebula Realm Cores, his Origin had continued vibrating at a high speed as it even caused the artificially formed Origin Cores of laws to bounce in excitement.

Within Noahs body, there existed not just his main Origin Core, but also the Fire, Water, Air, Earth, Light, and Dark Origin Cores!

He was shockingly walking around with multiple cores within his body, and his main one was about to dissolve into dust as it served as a base to build even more miniature stars in the future!

RUMBLE!

His main body was situated in a beautiful scenery of green as enormously tall ancient trees surrounded him, his position currently being in the depths of the Central Realm past the golden barrier as an overabundance of essence surrounded him.

Much of this essence was currently swirling towards him rapidly as he had already started the process of entering into the realm of a Nebula!

His Origin had been vibrating at a high frequency as a pained expression continued to deepen on his face, the process of essentially shattering ones own core before reforming into something grander being extremely dangerous.

CRACK! CRACK!

Lines of cracks spread throughout the Origin Core as Noah felt the other origins representing multiple laws rotate even faster, bringing a sense of stability as in the next millisecond...his main Origin shattered!

RUMBLE!

It felt as his Origin had been completely ground into dust, leaving behind a cloudy mass that began to circulate rapidly. This cloudy mass was the initial formation of a Nebula, and it needed enormous reserves of essence as it was circulating faster and faster while trying to birth multiple stars within.

Yet it turned out that at this time, the nearest sources of essence was the unique energy that the Fire, Water, Earth, Air, Light, and Dark Origin Cores that had been even more actively rotating and releasing essence as this multicolored assortment of energies rushed forward to build the stars within this Nebula!

The unique essence of Universal Laws themselves! Going towards building a single beings Nebula!

Such a thing was unheard of, and it would be the beginning of a shocking set of events as far from the Novus Galaxy where Noah was trying to condense his first stars, an entity that was freely floating in the Chaotic Void of the expansive Universe multiple light-years away opened his eyes the moment that Noah began establishing a new Core that was being built with the essence of Universal Laws themselves.

RUMBLE!

"After countless eons..."

WAA!

The body of the entity took a seated position as this moment as a shocking event played out light years away!
