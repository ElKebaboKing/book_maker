Fear was a curious thing.

In many people, fear gave way to recklessness, weakness, and madness. Fear was a gateway for many, many bad choices.

Three entities, in particular, feared the identity and very being of what they knew to be a Calamity.

So as they watched with their own eyes as one of them- the most powerful among them, almost decimated by one of the calamities they escaped from, their fear reached unprecedented levels!

|We must leave!|

Palace Master Nisa bellowed out.

At the same time, the bloodied figure of Palace Master Antiope looked towards the Silver Feathered Phoenix as she pleaded.

|O Pioneer of Dimensionality, please release us from this domain. If you all must stay, that is more than okay, but please let us return to the Immovable Sacred Palace!|

...!

They plead.

Existence Severing Palace Masters pleaded to be taken out of the same enclosed domain as the instrument of the Beyonder!

The silver eyes of the Pioneer of Dimensionality flashed with disdain and distaste as she harrumphed with her intent booming out.

|You all sit on the sidelines as you will be questioned later, and we will get to the bottom of whatever intricacies of connections there are between you and this invader!|

...!

The faces of Palace Masters Antiope and Nisa became ashen as they realized their pleading would get them nowhere.

They wanted to say that for all of them here, there may not be a later. When it came to calamities that defied existence, a future was never guaranteed!

The situation had rapidly changed as at this moment, the Pioneer of Dimensionality was moving alongside the Pioneer of QI and the Pioneer of CHI.

The Pioneer of CHI burned with white flames as a dazzling white Rune began to bloom on his chest, the circulation of his weavings reaching unprecedented levels as he called out heavily.

|This invader has laid down a fairly powerful domain that seems to be moving alongside him. A domain capable of even affecting Pioneers as entire paths can be nullified within this domain. But as always, for something so powerful, there is a cost. As we have seen, we were able to seal his usage of Outerversal Authority for him to choose the seal of the Pioneered Path of Time under Heracles. Theoretically, he should now be powerless, and yet he isnt- meaning that this entity is an anomaly that may have another one or two Severed Ordinances. An impossibility, but they may appear from time to time. All that we need to do is to ensure that we seal his remaining paths, and this fight will no longer be a fight!|

A terrifying white eye of existence was burning on top of the Pioneer of CHI as he analyzed many things and laid out a plan.

Ahead of them, the Immovable Sacred Guard remained calm with the same expression on his face as he stared towards Ozymandias carefully.

He stared at the seemingly increased level of complexity around him, his gaze a pondering one as he spoke out.

|The Instrument of the Beyonder, yes? If I can express anything before there is more bloodshed to be carried out, it is that all of this is meaningless in the grand scheme of things. What you do here will not change much. Whether you manage to kill the three that you came to kill- these intruders from different Frequencies of Existence. Whether you are an intruder yourself that has chased them here. Whether you manage to hurt me or any of the other Pioneers of the Immovable Sacred Palace of the Expanse who are currently here...it will change nothing in the grand scheme of the Wheel of Existence.|

His gaze was utterly serene. His eyes pulsed with a verdant light of brilliance as he seemed to gaze at everything with apathy and interest at the same time!

He smiled towards Ozymandias and continued.

|Do you know of the countless records just in our Frequencies of Existence? Of the powerful entities within this frequency that are more powerful than me or you? Do you know what they have managed to change in regard to the Wheel of Existence over the eons? Absolutely nothing. The Wheel turns as the Wheel turns, all of us being mere pawns in the grand scheme of things as the most that we can do is sit back, relax, and enjoy.|

...!

His words even caused the other Pioneers and Palace Masters to change.

Ozymandias stared at this unique being with calm eyes as he analyzed the rapid change of his complexity with all of the newly Severed weavings of existence in addition to Cheats continuing its Severings.

He looked at this being as a colorless light of Quintessence and Loot began to burn around him, his intent echoing out in reply.

I make my choices, then my choices affect the outcomes around me. Just like I gave you all the choice to not get involved and simply give me the three that I came here for- you all made a choice, and now here we are.|

Choices.

Causality!

Someone to question when the Watcher gets here. He is half dead anyway with a major part of his weavings taken by you. How does this sound?|
