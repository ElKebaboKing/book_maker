In the grand Nexus Galaxy, a wide array of Realms and Worlds peacefully stayed within .

All of these Realms and Words were in a period of peace and conquest as they were unified under the rule of the Imperials residing in the Brightgold Monarchy Realm .

This realm had the name Brightgold Monarchy because it was founded by a being known as Golden Monarch Brightgold!

Though currently, the ones ruling the Central lands of this Realm and residing in the Imperial Citadel were the Nobles of the Dannicus Household, with the one known as the Imperial Adjudicator at the lead .

The Dannicus Household ruled with an iron fist as much of the success of the Nexus Galaxy could attributed on a whole to this powerful family, the prosperity many beings were seeing today coming from their conquests and actions .

This household was also the one to call themselves Imperials, with different ranks of this name given out to different beings depending on their power!

At the lowest rank, you had the Nobles who could freely claim the singular Imperial Title .

Above it, you had those who could traverse the stars and Galaxies, the High Imperials!

After High Imperials, you had the esteemed Imperial Lords who managed different regions of the Nexus Galaxy, and above them you had the Imperial Rulers that controlled the forces sent through the Universal Rift as well as command posts on other Galaxies .

Standing above all these Imperials was the singular Imperial Adjudicator himself, the Golden Conquer that resided in the Imperial Citadel!

This Imperial Library was open to very few beings, and it was restricted only to those holding the title of Imperials!

The first and second floors of this vast Imperial Library could be accessed by High Imperials, with the lowest rank of Imperials only being able to access the first floor .

Only Imperial Lords could access the mysterious Third Floor, and only Imperial Rulers had access to the Fourth Floor!
