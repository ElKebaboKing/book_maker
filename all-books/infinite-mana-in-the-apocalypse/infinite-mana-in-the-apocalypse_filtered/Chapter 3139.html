Chapter 3137 What is known and not known! IV

Illusory Quintessential Legions of Lumen.

This was the term that flowed across the mind of the Son of Osmont as he gained the access to view them and command them!

The Light of Lumen had passed through his Father and throughout all of their blood, but even more of it coursed through the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed.

The changes his father has undergone were as stellar as always, but the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed...the Son of Osmont believed that if all of its data were to be broken down, its advances and qualitative changes may even exceed his father!

So when it appeared above and released the Illusory Quintessential Legions of Lumen that were made from the weavings of his father's defeated enemies and now an integration of Lumen, he knew that this wasn't all.

And moments after...

HOONG!

A horn of war was blown as neat rows upon rows of white gold Automatons appeared.

Blazing metallic gold wings behind them, and humanoid muscular bodies that made them look like glistening ancient titans!

Their hands seemed malleable as they already shifted into an array of armaments ranging from swords, spears, flails, hammers, guns, cannons, and more!

Above their heads, A Halo of blue, gold, and colorless luster rotated as the profound glow of Lumen covered every part of their bodies.

The Quintessential Automaton Legions of Lumen.

Each of them emanated a pressure equivalent to something at the peak of the Teracosm Stage- but even this outburst of power shouldn't be entirely trusted as what they could defend against and dish out in damage was not something anybody understood yet!

Beside them, the majestic figure of Hyperion could be seen along with thousands of Enders of Extremity- their bodies blazing with white gold flames as parts of their figures seemed experimented on- a metallic sheen emanating from their limbs as they boomed with ferociousness.

These were the products of the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed.

Products that it churned out itself within its magisterial Aletheian Havens, with resources under its own banner and the millions of Fruits of Existence it held access to as Causality was constantly distorted.

And now, Henry gained access to command them as well.

The Illusory Quintessential Legions of Lumen and the Quintessential Automaton Legions of Lumen.

At this moment, The Son of Osmont brought his hands down commandingly as he felt the connection and weavings of command linked to all of these Legions.

And they were let loose across the changing battlefield!

HUUM!

Brilliance unfurled.

But this wasn't all.

The dazzling Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed remained floating above everything in the crimson skies as at this time, its size began to enlarge by thousands of miles.

It expanded more and more until its shadow covered nearly half of the whole battlefield, its shadow bringing with it immense pressure as in the eyes of Henry...

In the eyes of the one granted access as the Commander of this battlefield...prompts began to rise.

|Offensive features of the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed are being activated.|

|The Ascendant Existential Extremity Phaser Kanons are being activated.|

...!

Around the circular and now massive Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed, massive cannon-like cylinders instantly formed all around and pointed themselves toward the Faeburned Legions.

Each one shone with either a majestic cerulean brilliance or a murky crimson radiance, the glow of Manadynamics and Entropy running rampant!

|Existential Ammunition Generation has been scaled infinitely.|

|Ballistokinetic Combat has been activated, with every released projectile of the Ascendant Existential Extremity Phaser Kanons containing a portion of the weight of the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed.|

|The Ascendant Existential Extremity Bombardment will now begin.|

HUUUM!

The massive cerulean and crimson cannons hummed, and in the next moment, catastrophe unveiled itself.

BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!

Unfathomably heavy shots of massive singularity looking projectiles began to shoot out of the Ascendant Existential Extremity Phaser Kanons, each one containing a mixture of the colors shot out of the cannon along with a blinding white gold radiance!

They began to rain down a bombardment, and they never stopped shooting as the first one landed right in front of a Legion led by a Teracosm Stage Entity, and...

WAP!

The thrown out projectile exploded with glorious radiance, expanding out in a gorgeous circular shape before retreating and converging into nothingness soon after.

And the area of effect its simple expansion and retraction covered...left behind only glimmers of ash and twinkling condemned Sources of Existence that were drawn towards the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed in the distance!

...!

The bombardment rained down everywhere as in front of a Legion led by a Paracosm Equivalent Faeburned Existence...

WAP! WAP!

A dense number of projectiles swerved directly above him and around him with purpose.

The weavings of existence protecting the terrifying Paracosm of this being vibrated as the projectiles expanded and contracted- each impact eating up the authority of existence as the Paracosm Faeburned Existence could only defend and be pushed back while roaring out with saddened fervor!

Around him, any Teracosm Equivalent and lower powered Entities were being utterly shredded, the only thing left behind for them being twinkling Sources of Existence that were pulled up towards the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed!

|...|

It was a scene that caused even Entities like Ayame, Aurelia, Erikson, the Cimmerian Architect, and many others to pause and gaze upwards in shock as they thought to themselves...

'The Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed could do this as well?!'

Even those who had been living inside of it were shocked as at this moment, across the shimmering boundary like layer of the circular looking Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed, more Ascendant Existential Extremity Phaser Kanons arose like deadly cylindrical mountains, some of them beginning to shine with the allure of Dreams, Avalon, Gravitation, Relativity, Continuity, Genesis, Penumbra, Helios...and even more.

A dense number of Phaser Kanons of different authorities rose and began an endless bombardment- causing even the inhabitants of the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed to question the land they lived in as when they stared at the now frantic legions of the Faeburned that were scrambling in shock, they couldn't help but pity them!

Each projectile carried with it a percentage of the weight of the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed.

What did it feel like to be smashed by a portion of the weight of an Omniversal Seed? The answer to this question...was one that nobody would seek to know!
