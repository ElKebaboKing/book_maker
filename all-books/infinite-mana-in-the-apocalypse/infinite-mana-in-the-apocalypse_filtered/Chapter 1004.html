Chapter 1002 - The Strongest Influence in the Animus Universe! II

The gazes of the Hegemonies still using their power to observe from a large distance turned somber.

They saw the scenes in the chaotic void near the Holy Draconic Lands that was filled with the rivers of blood- they saw the scene where a single being told billions of creatures that before him, they would either have Annihilation...or Fealty!

They all chose fealty as seconds later, the auras of these beings began to crazily rise up as their eyes that were filled with despair and fear a second ago turned into those of shock, and when they looked up towards the figure of the Tyrant Dragon once more, their eyes had a fervent light aside from the fears.

This was because of the power they felt coursing through their bodies!

Yet...the gazes of the Hegemonies were still somber.

This was because they remembered this scene from not too long ago.

The eyes of Oathkeeper and others turned towards Valentina.

They realized the answer to their question of just who she had pledged Fealty to that caused her situation to entirely change as she turned the tables against 2 Hegemonies!

Valentina felt her face heat up at the questioning gazes of the Hegemonies, her figure beginning to waver as she spoke out.

"I have to go into seclusion and heal. Let me know how we decide to deal with these guys!"

WAP!

Without another word, she disappeared from the eyes of the Hegemonies as she didn't stay to answer their questions!

Their eyes had to turn back towards the incredulous scene of the Tyrant Dragon as at this moment, the many watching Hegemonies placed him on their watchlist as an important existence to keep track of.

This was because at this moment, he was directly placing his influence on an entire Universe at a rate even Hegemonies didn't dare or had the capabilities to- and these knowledgeable beings knew that when such an existence broke through to the Universal Realm, their Universal Fortune must be great as they would quickly rise in power!

But even these Hegemonies could never expect that Noah's fortune was already more massive than many Hegemonies, the number of Marks of Antiquity he had collected being something that many of them could only ever dream of.

WAA!

The scene at the bloody chaotic void of the area near the Draconic Holy Land turned quiet as Noah's eyes gazed at all the Billions of beings with the Legions of both sides.

Both the allied 4 Bloodline Races and the 5 Bloodline Races that were enemies.

All the forces of these Bloodline Races that had participated in the Universal War had pledged Fealty under his words of Fealty of Annihilation!

His fortune and destiny were swirling crazily around his main body in the Dark Universe, but the eyes of his clone remained utterly cold as with the wild power it showed, a command released from it once more.

"The strongest of your Races have pledged Fealty...but their Trillions more back in your Holy lands that need to do the same. Only with this path can peace be achieved."

"Set off and spread the word. You've tested the wonders of pledging Fealty...let all your people do the same!"

...!

The hearts of many beings trembled as they couldn't help but nod, the Legions of many creatures beginning to move as the cluster of forces around Noah began to spread to different directions.

8 Animus Summons shot towards the cluster of 8 Bloodline Races, each of them being Noah's representatives as they would spread Noah's influence to the trillions of creatures spread out across the Holy Lands of these Bloodline Races!

Noah's Tyrant Dragon figure, Tiamat, and the Blue Slime remained in the chaotic void as their eyes turned towards the Draconic Holy Land not too far from them, Noah's command ringing out as the Dragons that were watching this scene in a stupor like many others began to move.

Paragon Quinnie's heart was still shaking as she watched her husband move towards the Serpentine Holy Land with Noah's Galaxy Devouring Serpent in the lead, her gaze turning back towards the Draconic Holy Land that all dragons were heading to as it was time.

Time for them to reclaim the homes stolen from them long ago!

The Queen Dowager was leading the procession of Dragons as a fantastical scene of rows of majestic Dragons making their way towards their Holy Land played out, the figures of the bloodline races that had remained behind within these lands feeling a shocking force come towards them as they would soon begin quaking where they stood.

Tiamat's goal of revenge and reclamation of her home after over a hundred thousand years...Noah would achieve it for her merely days after he arrived in the Animus Universe!

It wasn't the two Hegemonies that were fighting for influence over the last tens of thousands of years...but a Paragon that had risen up in mere days taking the seat of the most influential creature within this Universe!

Waves of change continued to spread out from this moment as many powerful beings moved to understand exactly what had occurred, even more on them focusing on a few figures they had to pay attention to.

Near the Bloody Battlefields Galaxy Clusters where a Universal Construct stood.

Chronos watched as the figures of Oathkeeper and a few others faded away, leaving behind only a few clones from the Hegemonies that had cloning abilities as they kept watch over the events within the Animus Universe.

Chronos stared at Ambrose and the other Hegemonies around him with eyes shining with ancient lights as he spoke, the aura he emanated making these powerful beings all turn towards him as he was the center of attention.

"Assess the status of all the other Universes and how the rate of Accumulation is proceeding. We need to get a timeline of when everything will come together...and when the Antiquity will be born!"

RUMBLE!

With a gaze full of relentless might, Chronos forged forward with an unknown goal that the powerhouses of the Primordial Cosmos were currently moving to stop!
