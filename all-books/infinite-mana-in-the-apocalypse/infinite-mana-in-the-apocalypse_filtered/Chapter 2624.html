2622 Havenly Aurora! III

The first Underdweller to arrive in the Supernal Haven of Dissolution!

Noah claimed this title as when he landed atop the floating landmass of pristine fantastical grandeur, the voice of a Hyperversal Extremity echoed out as he saw the verdant and obsidian grass around him make way and form a path towards the depths of the island.

The Smilodons around this area only gazed at him briefly before they went back to their prior actions- many of them lying down with their eyes closed as the aura of Dissolution formed into tendrils of light around them.

With his body as a black and cherry-colored Smilodon, Noah went deeper into the island as he began to come across more Apex Aeonic Lifeforms, passing through the groves of thick and ancient trees pulsing with Dissolution until a clearing began to open up ahead of him.

A small mountain rose within this clearing as a stream of stellar obsidian water could be seen circling around it- the small mountain divided into floors as the bodies of Apex Smilodons were sprawled all around it, with the aura of a Hyperversal Extremity Smilodon at the very peak of this small mountain!

It was the only thing in this clearing as the mountain was surrounded by heavy concentrations of Dissolution, and Noah could see that far above this region, the halos of circular bubbles holding profound Aeonic Treasures of Extremity floated majestically!

[My, I really can't tell you apart from other Smilodons...just what did you use to even fool the senses of a Hyperversal Extremity?]

The feminine voice of a Haven Saber Smilodon thundered out as it stemmed from the regal red-furred existence at the top of the small mountain.

Watching it was akin to a habitat of a pride of lions, with the eyes of the Haven Saber Smilodon locked onto Noah as a smile formed on her head!

Noah's visage was calm as he responded.

[Just a trick I came across not too long ago. You mentioned an introduction?]

An introduction. If one didn't want to stumble in these new lands, this was invaluable!

The Hyperversal Extremity before Noah did not release an aura anywhere as powerful as the three managing the Vein Rift as she seemed to be Tier 1 at most, with her serene utterance continuing to echo out at this moment.

[Mmm...you've just arrived in the Supernal Haven of Dissolution and stepped on one of its Havenly Archipelagos. One of the more common Golden Havenly Archipelagos that holds the most basic Havenly Treasures.]

...!

Havenly Treasures!

As she spoke, she pointed her paw upwards and tapped as from the glorious glimmers of light above, one of the halos of circular light surrounding a treasure floated down until it came just below the mountain in front of Noah.

[Reality may be entirely different in your Nether Haven of Dissolution, but the reality in the Supernal Havens of Extremity is one where above the Havenly Archipelagos, Havenly Authority gathers and mixes with the Authority of the Supernal Haven to produce a myriad of Havenly Treasures. And to attain them...well, why don't you take a look and try to take the one before your eyes? It is something that an Underdweller like you would need the most right now.]

WAA!

An entirely different reality.

The Havenly Saber Smilodon talked of the Havenly Authority gathering above the Havenly Archipelagos as she referred to Vitalis Hyperversal Authority, with Noah's mind putting all the information together as he floated up to the halo of light that when coming closer, one could see a dazzling black and white scroll contained within!

Noah's paws went towards the halo of light containing this black and white scroll as...

BZZT!

His hand was rebuffed.

And thereafter, streams of information surged out that for him- they transformed into gorgeous prompts.

[Insufficient Units of Havenly Aurora to acquire the Golden Aurora Accumulation Modus Operandi. 100 Units of Havenly Aurora are required.]

...!

The usage of the Units of Havenly Aurora!

Noah had attained 10 from defeating the Earth Saber Smilodons as it seemed all the Havenly Treasures atop the Archipelagos of the Supernal Haven of Dissolution required such a form of authority in order to be obtained!

[Unique, isn't it?] The voice of the Haven Saber Smilodon continued as Noah stared at the black and white scroll before him with blazing eyes.

[Reality revolves around the Units of Havenly Aurora one has as with a sufficient amount, one can even acquire Havenly Treasures that can take them to Extremity on Obsidian or Crimson Havenly Archipelagos. The bodies of the Havenly Lineages across the Supernal Havens of Extremity have slowly collected Havenly Aurora since they were born, with the Challengers that you Underdwellers are facing having been prepared as they already utilized most of their Havenly Aurora to redeem Treasures before stepping out- just in the case they lost against any of you, they wouldn't lose much of their accumulated Havenly Aurora!]

Even more dazzling words came out as a few truths became clear.

One was that it was possible to slowly build up the Havenly Aurora over time while in the Supernal Havens of Extremity.

Another was that a method to attain Havenly Aurora was to overpower and defeat another being- this in turn transferring their Units of Havenly Aurora to the victor!

And of course, there was the Golden Aurora Accumulation Modus Operandi floating in front of Noah and unattainable by him at this moment.

His eyes were fiery as he gazed at it, the voice of the Haven Saber Smilodon continuing with its smile becoming even wider.

[Ah yes, the faster way to build up Havenly Aurora. Even though it's the lowest tier of the Aurora Accumulation Modus Operandi, the Golden Aurora Accumulation Modus Operandi grants you the wisdom on how to pull Units of Havenly Aurora into your existence faster- this being the major way existences accumulate Aurora apart from Challenges!]

...!

[Challenges...you can participate in these on any Havenly Archipelago you land on as if you step on the Challenger Arena, you can battle others. So long as you win, their Havenly Aurora is yours. And the biggest introductory lesson I can give to an Underdweller like you is if you start battles anywhere else apart from Challenger Arenas and commence massacring Havenly Beasts to acquire their Aurora...you would not live long as just like you massacre others, someone more powerful will arise to do the same to you.]

...!
