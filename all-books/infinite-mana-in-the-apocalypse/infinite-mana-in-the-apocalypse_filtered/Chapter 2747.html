Amidst the swirling storms of authority, Noah felt his mind buzzing as he received the details of the most prominent Boundary Layers.

[The Seventeenth Boundary Layer of the Protagonist]:: The sublime control of the journey of the Protagonist begins to fall in your lap as the stages of your progression- namely the stages of [A Call to Adventure], [Aid and Cheats], [From the Known to the Unknown], [Challenges and Ordeals], [Point of Death and Rebirth], [Transformation], [Ultimate Boon], [Return], and [Master of Path] are now freely accessible to you up to the point of the highest stage of the cycle you're currently in. The rankings of Items, Concepts, or Entities who are embroiled and connected to you in any of these stages will have their Records and Information available for you to view for a set cost, no matter what height they have reached in the Gradations of Extremity- they will not be masked from the eyes of the Protagonist. The further an Item, Concept, or Entity is on the Gradations of Extremity, the less information is readily available, but information will be there nonetheless. If you can exceed this stage and attain Extremity in the concept of the Protagonist, it may then be possible for you to actively make alterations in the stages of Progression you're in, and even become capable of affecting the Items, Concepts, or Entities embroiled with you within the set stages...

[The Seventeenth Boundary Layer of the Hero]:: The definition of a Hero can vary depending on where you stand. This Boundary Layer of the Hero stands to reward you Quintessential Extremity Loot of your choice whenever you gain enough recognition as a Hero by the vast majority of the Conscious Entities of the Reality around you, or when you perform acts deemed heroic by the vast majority of the Conscious Entities of the Reality around you.

[The Seventeenth Boundary Layer of the Villain]:: Similar to the Hero, one can be defined as a Villain depending on which stance they take across their lifetime. This Boundary Layer of the Villain also stands to reward you with the Quintessential Extremity Loot of your choice whenever you gain enough recognition as a Villain or when you perform acts deemed villainous by the vast majority of the Conscious Entities of the Reality around you.

[The Seventeenth Boundary Layer of the MacGuffin]:: A MacGuffin is a plot device utilized to propel the intricate reality around the protagonist forward. It is usually the cause of conflict or what sets off the journey of the Protagonist, and it could be an item, a concept, or an Entity. There may already be a single or multiple MacGuffins that are currently propelling your reality, with this boundary layer allowing you to choose one item, concept, or Entity and designate it as a MacGuffin. Once selected, unknown and unforeseeable alterations of reality will occur around this item, concept, or Entity. If you can achieve Extremity in this concept, multiple MacGuffins may be available for selection as their existence could cause the stories of all conscious entities around you to resolve around this plot device.

[The Seventeenth Boundary Layer of Harems]:: You have a Harem of distinguished and beautiful Existences, but where are they? Why do they not share the spotlight with you and are left to collect dust in the background? This Boundary Layer of Harems seeks to change that as since no Entity can have a fast rate of progression that will ever come to match yours, this Layer goes a different route to provide a solution. At the current point in time, up to 9 Entities that are considered members of your Harem or the products between you and any members of the Harem- they can be selected to take the place of one of your Reified Sources of Boundaries as they can exhibit 50% of the total power of these Clones. If your concepts have attained Extremity or are much higher compared to them, this selection to have their Soul utilize the power of your Clones will rapidly aid them in going towards and past the Gradations of Extremity as they will feel like the tools they're wielding are their own. With this push, if the members of the Harem or their products between you and them still get left out of the spotlight, the Boundary of Harems can only give up and take a back seat...
