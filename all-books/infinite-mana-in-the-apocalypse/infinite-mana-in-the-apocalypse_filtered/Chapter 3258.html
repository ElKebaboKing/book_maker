Chapter 3256 Tainted! IV

What was this being's power?

He said he wasn't a Delegatus of an Outerversal Morphon.

But why?

Why did he act the way he did?

And he...

|...!|

'What the fu-'

But...

SAA!

SAA! SAA!

...!

"You cannot even touch me."

The level of his power.

The place he had reached.

Interacting with him.

They were on a whole different level of Existence!

His wavelengths though...could reach all of them with surety.

All tens of thousands of Existence Reapers who were closest nearby!

WU!
