Chapter 4633: Infinite Potential III

The pieces did not fit together into a coherent picture in his understanding.

And yet the moment he arrived back in his current era, he appeared directly in the region of collapse where an ongoing battle was occurring.

Nine creatures burning with paradoxical authority and inscribed with the Phoneme of Paradox had been attempting to suppress and induce despair in his Civilization.

So he had struck down with his Goad.

But his Goad now held multiple Phonemes arranged within its structure in complex linguistic patterns.

And when paired with his current level of power, his new lineage as THE Genesis Monarch of Infinite Lexicons, and his terrifying Foundation reinforced by the Nine Pillars of Infinite Expression...

These entities at the Pre-THE Existence level were absolutely nothing before him.

Even if they were approaching the power of a THE Surface Depth of Absolute Sovereignty entity through their unique construction.

After all, he had faced genuine Surface Depth of Absolute Sovereignty entities in direct combat!

He had battled and imprisoned 14 of them in THE Infinite Desmoterion.

So nine paradoxical weapons, however impressive their construction, could not stand against what he had become.

His action had caused immense silence to descend across the battlefield.

He didnt pay attention to the shocked reactions rippling through all the gathered entities.

Instead, he turned around to face his people directly.

The moment he did so, he came face to face with none other than The Infiniverse.

Her figure was majestic and commanding, adorned in a blue-gold battle dress that seemed woven from compressed reality itself.

The dress was filled with and surrounded by countless Omniverses and Folds, each one orbiting her form like satellites around a planet.

Her radiant multicolored hair cascaded down her shoulders in waves that seemed to contain flowing information itself.

Each strand appeared to shift through different hues depending on the angle of observation.

Her eyes seemed filled with infinite information and possibilities, containing depth that suggested she processed reality at speeds beyond normal comprehension.

They typically held a type of analytical coldness, the gaze of one who viewed Existence as data to be understood and optimized.

And yet at this moment, as she looked at Noah and saw the defiance and lingering lividity within his eyes, something shifted in her expression.

The Infiniverse floated forward with deliberate movement.

And shockingly, she embraced Noah directly.

She actually placed her head against his chest and closed her eyes, her arms wrapping around him in gesture that seemed utterly foreign to her normally emotionless demeanor.

She spoke only for him to hear, her voice carrying warmth that few would believe she possessed.

"Welcome back, Master."

Welcome back.

They were simple words, only three syllables combined.

But they were filled with immense relief and genuine warmth that transcended her usual analytical nature.

Because she was actually one of only two beings who had been watching what happened to Noah in Ginnungagap in real time.

The Infiniverse and Ruination had observed through their deep connection to him.

They watched as THE Primordial Chaos appeared and things turned horrendous.

They watched as THE Living Temporal arrived and the situation seemed to deteriorate beyond hope.

They watched as THE Creature manifested and the future became utterly unclear.

The outcome had been uncertain at that terrible moment.

Ruination and The Infiniverse were the only ones who lived through the terror of that convergence alongside Noah himself.

So at this moment, the normally emotionless Infiniverse actually embraced her Master and welcomed him back with genuine feeling.

The burning glory and defiance within Noahs eyes lessened somewhat as he held the head of The Infiniverse gently.

He patted her hair with careful affection.

"Im back," he said simply. "Im back."

The words were straightforward enough, carrying weight of reassurance.

In the next moment, glorious and terrifying prompts bloomed before his vision as time itself seemed to freeze around him.
