Chapter 766 - A Fantastical Holy Land! II

Within the belly of the draconic shaped Realm that was the core of the Expansive Dragon Cl.u.s.ter, Noah was surrounded by powerful personages as he stared at a crack in space.

On one side, the Holy Emperor was standing behind 20 young experts from the Expansive Dragon Cl.u.s.ter. All of them were powerful and talented Animus Trainers that were all in the Low Tier GALAXY Realm, with five of them even being in the Middle Tier!

Looking at this scene, Noah couldnt help but send a telepathic message towards Anna.

"Surrounded by all these experts while at the Black Hole Realm- arent you falling behind, princess?"

He couldnt help but tease her as her clear eyes turned towards him slightly, her voice ringing out in his mind soon after.

"Hmph, that old man has already given me a ton of treasures that will allow his dear granddaughter to breakthrough into the GALAXY Realm fast. I just havent stooped down to use them!"

The Princess shot back as Noahs eyes shone at her words, sending her another message telling her that she shouldnt turn down free things that would raise her strength! He then continued to observe the surroundings of the crack that would lead into the Primal Holy Land as his eyes turned to the party of the Aileron Race.

They also had 20 beings, with the levels also all being in the Low Tier GALAXY, with 7 being in the Middle Tier. Within this party, the figure of Prince Kirigan could be seen standing majestically at the very center as the 4 meter tall stature shone with dominance.

"The Primal Holy Land is a grand opportunity to explore the home of the Esteemed Great Sage, a Realm whos time flow is 10 times faster than our own as a day out here is 10 days in there! Since the passing of the Great Sage, the Realm has developed restrictions to those in higher Realms, with Peak Tier GALAXY Realm beings not being able to remain inside for longer than a few minutes before they are ejected. Use this opportunity wisely as you raise your strength!"

The boisterous voice of the Holy Emperor rang out as his figure moved forward the moment that everyone arrived, his eyes grandly shining out beams of light as a single word was uttered while he stared at the crack in space.

"OPEN!"

THRUUM!

Space tumbled and quaked as a horrendous pressure descended, the forehead of the Holy Emperor splitting apart as a crack appeared on it, a blood red light shooting from this crack on his forehead as it went to connect to the crack in space!

The moment this occurred, the crack in space began expanding rapidly as it reflected back a red-gold light, this illumination getting brighter and brighter as in a matter of seconds, a gorgeous red gold portal began to form in front of everyone.

Noahs eyes shone as he stared at this portal that would lead into a Secret Realm, the Primal Holy Land of the Great Sage Aoin!

"You all can now enter!"

RUMBLE!

The grand voice of the Holy Emperor woke many up from their stupor as the Aileron Race Royals glanced towards their Primal Emperor as he gave a slight nod, the Holy Descendants from the Expansive Dragon Cl.u.s.ter already leading the way as Noah and Anna joined them.

The party of 40+ beings all in the Low and Middle stages of the GALAXY Realm all entered as only a few beings stayed behind to watch this scene!

Off to the side, Anastasia was watching all of this with worried eyes as she was not actually standing beside her father, but a woman that was veiled in the same manner as her- someone that was rarely seen even in the most prestigious events of the Expansive Dragon Cl.u.s.ter.

"Mother, why dont you step in and talk to father? He might still listen to you."

Anastasias voice was mild as she said this, the one beside her own mother who stayed enclosed in a castle the majority of the time. If one could see beneath her veil, they would notice that she had a sickly appearance even though her body released an aura at the GALAXY Realm! This veiled woman glanced at Anastasia with a light smile as she shook her head.

"You know him just as well as I do. After your father plans, all we can do is follow."

Words that Anastasia expected but didnt want to hear rang out, her head shaking in sadness as she glanced hopefully towards the disappearing figure of Noah that followed closely behind her daughter disappear into the wondrous red gold portal.

---

For those entering the red gold portal, they felt immense spatial disorientation as they were shuttled into an entirely different region, their sight only clearing up after a second as they appeared in colorful skies of a brand new realm.

Noahs eyes lit up as he gazed around him, noticing the skies to be tinged with an aurora of colors as a cloudy mist seemed to blanket above them with shimmering splendor!

Below them, an expansive mystical forest filled with lush greenery and vegetation lay, huge trees of all types with vibrant fruits being seen as the most fantastical thing of all...were the distant cries of beasts.

SHAAA!

Not too far from them in the skies, Noah observed a golden feathered bird streaking through, golden tongues of flames dancing behind it. Below, he could sense the movements of multiple beasts of the ground as the Realms of the nearest ones he could sense...ranged from Nebula and all the way to the Middle Tier GALAXY Realm!

"CAAA!"

A piercing screech resounded out at this instant as the golden feathered bird towering over 10 meters in height turned its fearsome gaze towards the newly appearing parties, its figure leaving behind only a flash of light as it thundered towards them.

Noah gazed at this creature that seemed to be in the Low Tier GALAXY as the Holy Descendants around him began summoning their pets, but a figure stepped out to meet this creature first as their body shimmered with a hazy grey l.u.s.ter, a swirl of dao essence rumbling from them as they threw out a fist to meet the coming golden ray of light!

BOOM!

In a magisterial fashion, the golden feathered bird was knocked back after a golden flash of light, everyone observing that half of its golden face was rapidly turning deathly as the fist that was thrown out to meet it contained the Dao of Withering!

WAA!

The 4 meter tall figure of Prince Kirigan floated grandly as he withdrew his fist, the light of the dao still swirling around him as he gazed towards this creature without any regard!
