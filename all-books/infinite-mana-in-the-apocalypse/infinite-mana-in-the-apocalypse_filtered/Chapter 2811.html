Chapter 2809 A Heavy Choice! I

2809 A Heavy Choice! I

As the Cimmerian Architect's voice reverberated within Noah's mind, he felt a surge of profound brilliance mingled with waves of uncertainty.

Her words echoed the doubts that had been lingering at the edges of his consciousness- especially the doubts of possible dangers!

'Why am I not dead?' Noah mused silently, his thoughts racing as he considered the implications of the Architect's question.

He stared at her figure shrouded with power and mystery as he could erupt with Quintessence and Infinity at any time if he wished, but there was no harm in obtaining more information that others were not privy to!

From the records of the Annals Inquisitor he had devoured, he gained a semblance of knowledge on The Ones Above Extremity.

Mere rumors and scattered records, but they were there.

He already theorized for himself the many possibilities and probable dangers, but the entity before him was much more equipped with knowledge as she was on a whole other Tier of importance in comparison to the Annals Inquisitor.

But she floated close to him right now without the intent to battle as she seemed intent on a discussion.

Should he just...kill her and devour her records entirely to obtain all of this knowledge and not have to go through a back and forth conversation?

Such a dangerous thought arose as the brows of the Cimmerian Architect quickly flashed with brilliance.

[Yo, what the hell were you just thinking? Were you thinking of trying to fuck me up when the things I could tell you could possibly save your existence? Because you wouldn't be waving around another type of Existential Extremity Authority freely if you fully understood just what it implied.]

...!

Her perception was a profound one as the slight eruption of killing intent was felt by her, Noah's mind calculating the risks and benefits as her voice echoed out again.

[I'll give you the respect as the most recent Honored One to try and hold a discussion, and you can try to reciprocate that same respect and see what we gain from this. Otherwise, we can go back to trying to kill each other- it's fine for me either way.]

WAA!

Her words were resonant ones as at them, Noah smiled.

He analyzed the risks and benefits as he waved his hands.

[The Extremity of Chronos exerts its Authority.]

[The Extremity of Continuity emerges with indomitable brilliance, basking in the light of Infinity and taking this instance of time and expanding it endlessly!]

Their surroundings slowed down even more until the flow of time entirely ceased in this domain.

Even the visage of the Quintessential Extra who was the Annals Inquisitor seemed frozen in time as only he and the Cimmerian Architect could move freely.

With a wave of his hands, a solidified cerulean crystalline throne appeared behind him while pulsing with glory.

He sat down with majesty on such a throne while waving his hands toward the Cimmerian Architect.

[Alright, Cimmerian Architect...let us see what will come from this discussion.]

...!

The air congealed as the veiled figure of the Cimmerian Architect became serious.

She was here as the Primogenitor of Talismans. Her identity as the Cimmerian Architect was not guessed even by the Prime Devourer!

Yet this being spoke about her so freely as she harrumphed and conjured a dazzling obsidian crystalline throne, sitting atop it grandly as the veil over her faded, her true form and face being revealed.

Black and gold strands of hair flowed freely to her shoulders.

A runic ecriture of indeterminate origins was inscribed on her forehead as her fair skin pulsed with a light of Extremity- the beauty she expressed having been refined for innumerable years across the Gradations of Extremity.

She was exquisite in all accounts as putting her right hand on her chin, she began to speak calmly while waving her left hand to conjure a dazzling ball of light.

[Since you can at least figure out who I am, you may not be an entirely lost cause. Here- unlike what others may believe, I'm not that much of a talker, so peruse this present of mine for information.]

The ball of light from her left hand floated the few feet between them as the colorless light of Quintessence flowed to receive it, the appearance of this light causing a piercing light to flash in the depths of the eyes of the Cimmerian Architect as after Quintessence found nothing wrong, Noah's will absorbed this bundle of light.

A light...that was more like an intricate database of information regarding things that were of utmost importance for Noah right now!

This ball of light eliminated a back and forth dialogue of at least two pages as Noah attained more background information on the Ones Above Extremity, prior records or mere whispers of anything to do with them, and the eons of History of entities who had sought to elevate their Concepts to the Existential level...and their sudden disappearance.

Noah perused through this bundle of information as the question of the Cimmerian Architect buzzed in his mind once more!

Why wasn't he dead?

His eyes flashed with solemnity as he came to realize the answer right away!

He wasn't dead...because of the Quintessential Annals of Extremity.

He wasn't dead because his records were sealed and pulled into their own separate database as were he to still be getting Recorded in the Vitalis or Vacuous Annals of Extremity, he truly couldn't say for sure if he would still be functioning and alive right now!

The Empire he had built all these months.

The Infinite Hyperversal Haven and all of its intricacies.

His Family. His Vassals!

It was all hidden within the Recorded History within the Aeonic Annals of Extremity.

It could have all been eradicated in less than a nanosecond!

Such a thought was a solemn one to come across as such a threat should have never been this close to bringing an end to his story without him even knowing it.

It was too close for his comfort!

And even now, the danger was much greater than even what the Cimmerian Architect believed.

Noah didn't just forge an Existential Extremity Authority- he forged two.

And he had gone even further and enacted something that the Ones Above Extremity- the unfathomable force behind Vitalis and Vacuous Existential Authorities...he had enacted something that they would consider blasphemous and utter overarching into their Authorities!
