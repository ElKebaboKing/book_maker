Chapter 1288 - Searching For Pandemonium! II

In the Universe Noah had termed Blacksmith's Forge.

Countless Galaxies with no life in them floated while releasing waves of immense heat, even being in the stages of Hegemony finding the environment of this Universe stifling if they ever stepped foot in it.

Noah floated into the vast Forge of this Universe itself as this structure shaped like a hammer contained millions of stations designed with crimson gold colors, showing a workbench that held pristine gold hammers, anvils, and glimmering furnaces that held within them condensed crimson galaxies.

Noah could choose to pick up a hammer in any of these stations and heat up the materials before hammering them down to the shape he wanted, or he could simply allow the automatic process of forging the Items as he gave the Cosmic Core the specifications of what he wanted!

He continued past the countless stations as he came to the center of the Forge, seeing a vast clearing a few hundred thousand light years in diameter as here, materials that had been passively collected over the days by the Cosmic Core from the newly added Cosmos could be seen here.

Unique Crystals condensed from Cosmic Core essences made up a vast majority of the materials as they were collected from naturally born mines where the essence of Cosmic Daos clustered. There were many more things that didn't interest Noah as he waved his hands to add on to this pile the special material he would be using- and also the material that he was currently seeking with his clones!

THRUM!

Space fluctuated as Noah took out the dismembered pieces of Primordial Beasts he held with him- this being a precious resource as Noah actually erased the Primordial Beasts before with the deadly effects of Cessation.

But there were still many that weren't entirely wiped out as Noah took the chance to obtain their remaining bodies as in the near future, he would be using the skin and bones of Primordial Beasts to forge his items!

"Not any Quasi-Cosmic Treasures though...these ones will be born with the essence of an Edict!"

Yes! Noah had seen the quality of Quasi-Cosmic Treasures, seeing them not enhance his power all that much as they even held limited boosts if a Daolord used them.

Most Quasi-Cosmic Treasures held the essence of a particular Dao similar to the Cosmic Treasures like Ruination, with the Cosmic Treasure having more grand effects such as allowing one to have a Primordial Ruination Clone or a 1000:1 time ratio Realm in the case of RUINATION.

Noah's Quasi-Cosmic Treasures would be approaching the power of average Cosmic Treasures without their uniqueness alone if things went as he planned!

As his thoughts ran rampant, they were evicted up by the consciousness of the Cosmic Treasure deeply connected to him as the voice of RUINATION stretched out into Noah's mind.

<Master, you'll have to make sure you don't leave me behind when you achieve the Cosmic Realm and even forge past it. You can upgrade me to a Cosmic Filament Treasure and even a Primordial Relic if you raise the level of the Cosmic Core and enhance the Blacksmith's Forge some more.>

The practical and gradually more emotional Cosmic Treasure spoke out grandly as Noah laughed, sending waves of the essence of Amelioration into this treasure as he reassured it not to worry. There were many things he was planning for in the future as this would be among many!

As for the forging of powerful Quasi-Cosmic Treasures nearing the power of genuine Cosmic Treasures? This was something that the next Edict he was designing would help with.

He already knew the name of the new Edict he was waiting for, and since it wasn't as intense as many others, it would be completed fairly soon!

"The Nomological Edict of the Forgemaster...I look forward to it!"

Forgemaster! Noah could already see this name as in the Ruination Realm, the mixtures of laws and daos were nearly complete.

"Let's see what types of Quasi-Cosmic level items we can make first..." He familiarized himself with the unique types of weapons and armors as a glimmering Blue screen of many designs appeared before his eyes.

Base designs of weapons, and even blank blueprints that he could design the shapes of his own unique items!

There were many things he could do as apart from the materials of Primordial Beasts he already had, he was about to obtain many more at this moment as six of his clones had managed to find something that seemed like an entrance into a cluster of Pandemonium!

His Universal Body lifted a hammer as his stellar eyes weren't even seeing the forge before him, the scenes of an Edict nearing completion and his clones locating dens of Primordial Beasts reflecting within his eyes.

"Let's get things rolling..." His eyes released beams of light as the river of destiny churned wildly...but it was being steered every so slightly at this moment!
