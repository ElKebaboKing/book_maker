Little Entity...Little Entity...Little...

"One With the Dao! [Vitality Reversal]!"

RUMBLE!

At this moment, it seemed like all the surrounding space and stars lost color, everything gradually turning grey and lifeless as they were all taken towards a singular being.

Even the remaining Fiends that the Calamity Bear had not taken care of became lifeless husks as in the surrounding thousands of miles, it became a lifeless world!

Everything in the surroundings became lifeless...except the Entity that had cast the terrifying skill, and the regal Calamity Bear that actually gazed towards this Entity with disdain.

"So damn annoying!"

These were the only words left behind as the Entity looked on in a wrathful stupor as the Calamity Bear disappeared as well.

This...

Numerous thoughts crossed the Entitys mind as she tried to make sense of the situation in front of her.

I cannot sense anything that would make such a thing possible! A Grand Dao? A protective Dao Weapon?!

Yet no answer came as the Entity could only continue to chase the attackers besieging the Burning Domains Galaxy to no avail!

She threw out an attack that drained the essence of an entire star the moment she neared another one of the attackers, and it was the Variant Oculothorax this time around.

The planet that the Oculothorax was rampaging around in was drained entirely with everything on it as one could shockingly see chunks of it turning to earthen dust, yet the Oculothorax wasnt harmed one bit by the attack as the huge eye stared at the Entity.

A light of curiosity twinkled as before teleporting away, the eye was buffed by Intents as the ability of Sin of Envy was also utilized, [Eye of Doom] being cast a second later as a shocking ray of light erupted out and struck the body of the Entity!

OOOM!

Noah affirmed his thoughts that he could not defeat this creature no matter what he did at this moment, teleporting the body of the Oculothorax away as he continued his Siege to collect more loot.

He couldnt take care of the most powerful being in this Galaxy, but his goal of obtaining a large number of cores from Nebula, Black Hole, and Quasar rank experts was proceeding smoothly!

None of the Fiends at the Realm of QUASAR could stand against him, and he had already taken down two at the Low Tier GALAXY Realm as the three remaining Elder Fiends were among his targets before his [Invulnerability] effect was taken away.

So while being chased around by a furious Entity that could do nothing to him, he continued to amass more and more loot that would for sure bump him to the QUASAR Realm while keeping his Grand Completion, and it still remained to be seen whether it would be enough to rise to GALAXY!

<strong>[Vital Life Water] :: A treasured vital water that was drenched in the Dao of Vitality. To those delving into it or drinking it- Enhances Realm Progression by 200%. The Vitality Essence will gradually fade away after 1 year.</strong>

Yet another treasure lied in front of him as he waved his hands and used an enormous amount of mana to place all of it in his [Expansive Space]. He also made it his goal to steal the rest of the Vital Life Water that was in the other Domains- the thing that caused the battle driven Fiends to elevate themselves and create many experts over the last hundreds of years!

"Wretched creature!"

BOOM!

The Entity appeared before him again as she continued to released attacks without giving up, with his reaction being to ignore her as he knew he couldnt defeat her at this moment, only collecting information on all the skills and abilities she used while trying to observe the powerful Dao she wielded.

There was a powerful Entity still recovering from injuries chasing away four attackers, while each of them continued to release terrible skills that reaped the lives of Fiends over and over again.

In front of [Plot Armor] that granted the carrier of the Protagonist Trait [Invulnerability], what could an enemy do but despair?!
