Chapter 541 - Her ability makes her fit!

Across the vastness of space, a few Golden Commanders, Celestials that had achieved the Star Forging Realm- were sitting with serious faces as a cold atmosphere over took the command room of the Realm Dreadnaught they were in.

This enormous Vessel that very few Star Forging Commanders got the chance to pilot was actually not alone, another Realm Dreadnought of exactly the same enormous size stationed not too far from it. The size of these vessels were nearing those of Continents, with them being packed by hundreds of thousands of Celestials with their exterior shimmering with deadly weapons that could easily decimate Prime Worlds.

Currently, this second Realm Dreadnaught was actually not occupied with any Commanders, only masses of Celestials moving within it as the Commanders that normally took its reigns were the two Star Forging Celestials that had been chosen to assassinate Noah!

Now with their death, a new Commander had to be chosen for this monstrous vessel of death, and it was why this meeting was taking place.

"I can take over to avenge our brothers. I will see to it that Verittas Fortress falls!"

A strong voice erupted from a Star Forger that had sharp green hair, his eyes wild with power as the elements of the Law of Earth and Fate shimmered around him.

The surrounding Star Forgers nodded at his words as they saw no issues, the green haired Star Forger looking at a single Celestial at the forefront of the command table they were situated around.

All the eyes of the room turned towards this being who was known as Golden Child, the Celestial that currently acted as the right hand man of Aldrich!

This being had eyes that seemed to constantly be shining with a golden light, his gaze calm as he voiced out.

"No."

Unexpected gazes locked onto the Golden Child as they wondered for the reason of the denial, silence overcoming the room as his face seemed to show a certain level of uncertainty before he spoke.

"The one to take control of the Realm Dreadnaught shall be the upcoming Commander Athena."

...!

Shocked faces were the only thing that met him as the powerful Celestials looked around the room in confusion, the green haired Star Forger known as Commander Verdant speaking out carefully.

"The War Princess? But...that makes no sense. Not to even talk of her merits, but she has only just bega-"

"This is not coming from me, but from the Founder himself."

RUMBLE!

Waves of essence tumbled out at this as everyone shut up, any misunderstandings or grievances they had wiped away instantly as they nodded!

Even though they had many questions, even though many of them did not follow the train of thought of why an upcoming Commander who was still at the World Rank would be given command of one of the few powerful weapons of the Celestials- they still agreed because it was the words of the Founder!

The Golden Child looked at the many faces before he sighed and spoke once more.

"It is because of her unique ability that allows her to see the movements of the enemy even before they happen- {Precognition}."

...!

"Our enemies are veiled in chaos where we cannot discern their future even with our techniques of Fate, which led to us losing two brothers today. This unique War Princess actually has an ability that will help counter that 9th Infernal Lord with all his surprises becoming things we can anticipate."

The Golden Child spoke of the ability of the War Princess as the listening Star Forgers adopted astonished expressions, their gazes agreeing as time passed while the Golden Child continued to speak.

"Brother Verdant and Lyle, you two will accompany Athena on the Blazing Realm Dreadnaught, along with the company of the other three geniuses that participated in the recent tournament. The Founder plans to create an enormous legion to specifically counter the forces stationed at the Verittas Fortress, and you will be at the forefront of it!"

RUMBLE!

A golden glow erupted from his eyes at these words as the surrounding Star Forgers nodded.

"For order!"

"For balance!"

Waves of change continued to spread out as the effects of the recent battle continuing, a being that should have taken months to show great battle merits actually being flung to the center stage in a short period of time!

Was this the meaning of one with a Noble Fate, or something else entirely? Nobody could say they knew as the preparations were made, the commands being related to Athena, Crixus, the Three-Eyed Fate Child, and Grandmaster Light.

It was at this time that Noahs main body received the telepathic commands, and he was actually performing a change at this time as his first clone that had been moving to acquire prime world cores all this time teleported into the expansive Prime Warship of the Celestials he was on, both these two bodies appearing at the same veiled location as Noah curiously stared at himself.

As this clone teleported into the Prime Warship, Noah teleported out as [Unique Origin] was initiated to make the first clone seamlessly adopt the identity of Crixus. This ability stemming from the Progenitor bloodline being something that even the eyes of the Founder had not bypassed!

His main body was switching positions with his clone as during the momentous event of upgrading his Spiritual World and his many connected worlds into a Realm, he wanted his main body that held the actual connections and his full Origin to be presiding over it.

He always veiled himself with a Blood World whenever he was making breakthroughs or making progress in the laws, and he did not know how wild the effects of this coming upgrade would be. Thus, his main body would now assume the identity of the 8th Infernal Lord while presiding over everything else, his figure teleporting is the quadrant where his home world and many of the connected worlds stood.

He absorbed the news of Him and Athena being thrusted into the forefront of the coming battles as his eyes shone with many ideas, seeing another bright opportunity open up in front of him as he pushed aside these thoughts for now.

This was because he was ready to get started on the project to transform many worlds into a singular Realm!
