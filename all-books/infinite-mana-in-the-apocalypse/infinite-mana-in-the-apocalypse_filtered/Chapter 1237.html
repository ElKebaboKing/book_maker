Chapter 1235 - A Blur Of Multicolored Light! II

SHAA!

Like a blur of multicolored light, Noah crossed the Cosmic Battlefield as he erased 7 of the 8 remaining Daolords with a mere wave of his hands, none of them even being capable of defending due to how fast he was!

The last Daolord was Flamebright, the figure that was wielding a Quasi-Cosmic Treasure and had a disbelieving expression of wrath and incredulity at this moment.

Noah released the terrifying light of Cessation around him momentarily as he appeared before this being that had his lower body missing already, his hands moving in the slowed town to seamlessly unfurl the fingers holding the flickering crimson sword with this Daolords hands and taking it himself as he cast Understanding and Eyes of Truth onto it!

It was utterly disrespectful to this Daolord as he was disarmed without even knowing it nor being able to react to it as Noahs speed was that much faster than his!

The details of the Quasi-Cosmic Treasure appeared before Noahs eyes.

<Maddened Quasi-Cosmic Thundersword> :: A failed Cosmic Treasure that didnt quite reach the level it should have, but it still retains a powerful will and abilities. The user gains access to a unique essence of Thunderflames applied to all abilities cast with this treasure, this essence ignoring half of all enemy defenses while dealing 100,000% more damage. The abilities of [Thunderflames of the Maddened Cosmos], [Righteous Indignation]....

The details of a somewhat unique treasure appeared before Noahs eyes, but it didnt excite him all that much as he put it away in his Expansive Space and reignited his body with Cessation, barely sparing a glance to Daolord Flamebright as he released a wave of Cessation and wiped this being away an instant later!

Just continued utter disrespect for the most powerful enemy opposing him as he wasnt given any special treatment and was wiped away just like a common mob.

And before Noahs eyes, there wasnt really any difference between him and the other Daolords as neither of them could even react to his movements right now!

But with this, all the Daolords were cleared as Noahs eyes turned to the Antiquities that under his eyes felt like they were even stopped in time.

SHAA!

He flashed in front of them as their gazes were still locked towards where he used to be while he was powering up with apprehension, Noah counting 2369 Antiquities remaining alive as his body lit up brightly with Cessation- choosing to cast a skill that would instantly wipe out all of them away at the same time.

After the continuous use of [Fireball], he felt nostalgic as he took out another skill he had spammed many times in the past to clear out droves of enemies.

It was [Sever] from the Cthulhu skill tree that had a ridiculous amount of support skills like [Multiplicative Ray Support] or [Aether Chain Support], where from initially shooting out a ray of Aether- it gained the ability to chain from one enemy to another to even allowing a Portal of Terror to bloom everytime an enemy was hit with the Aether ray!

After the Cthulhu skill tree was elevated to Universal Filament Realm...this ability had become even more insane as it could chain between hundreds of enemies and allow even Galactic supernovae to bloom every time they came in contact with an enemy!

Simply said, it was a very good instant clear of enemies apart from something like the Endless Rays of Time that wouldnt just illuminate the enemies before him if Noah cast them, but they might go even further and erase the trillions of audiences watching at the edge of the Cosmic Battlefield.

"Sever."

Noah uttered lightly as from his fingers, multiple rays of light that seemed to have Aether at their base bloomed, but they were backed by the essence of Cessation as the void of space itself was torn apart, their speed ridiculously being even faster than Noah as they smashed into the group of Antiquities!

...!

Noahs luminous eyes watched as the purple crimson rays hit the first Antiquity, their body erupting into a mass of colorful light as they were instantly vaporized, and the ray of Aether and Cessation then chained onto a few hundred other Antiquities in the next instant!

A single ray did this as it eradicated every single Antiquity it hit, the extra effects of Galactic Supernovae not even blooming yet as because Noah released multiple rays of light...each one covered a few Hundred Antiquities as all them were chained instantly and became vaporized under the terrifying might of Cessation.

"..."

Then, the Supernovae of destructive essence bloomed from the locations where these existences were erased as an ear shattering explosion resounded out!

BOOOM!

The burning of Splendifirous Universes was stopped.

The slowed down time Noah had been experiencing all this time was seemingly returning back to normal, and those watching were finally able to follow what had happened as under their eyes, something drastically different had occurred.

They had been watching Noah as he powered up crazily with his aura flaring out madly, and then an instant later he turned into a multicolored light that zipped towards Daolord Flamebright and those around him, the moment this multicolored light appeared being followed by a rocking explosion that left billowing waves of heat spreading out as before they could even blink...they found that they couldnt see the figures of any Daolords or Antiquities opposing Noah anymore!

"..."

None! Not even half a second had passed, and they saw the inexplicable scene of a mere multicolored flash moving as an instant later, everything disappeared!

Daolords and Antiquities erased at a speed they couldnt even follow, their hearts turning utterly cold as many knew they would not be able to fare any better if even while watching this being move, they couldnt even see his figure.

THRUM!

The incandescent light of the Primordial Light of Authority at the peak of the Cosmic Battlefield beginning to shine gloriously and going towards Noahs lone figure was the only thing that affirmed that the battle had ended, but it had ended so terrifyingly that apart from the buzzing energy sounds released from this glowing light, everything else was starkly quiet!

So pervasively quiet as Noahs sole figure was the only thing remaining at the center of the Cosmic Battlefield, his arms outstretched as he accepted the Primordial Light of Authority that grant him an additional 10 Cosmos to oversee as well as....the notifications of the Loot he obtained blooming in golden prompts before his eyes!
