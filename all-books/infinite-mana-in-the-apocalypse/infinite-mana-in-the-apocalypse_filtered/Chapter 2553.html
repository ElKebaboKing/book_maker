The prompts cascaded one after another before Noah's eyes as he smiled.

And he could only smile as this was something he had caught glimpses of!

Now, he wanted to see just how stupendous it could grow to be.

Ahpuch.

This was the name he proclaimed for himself when he was born.

A name that meant death, disaster, and destruction.

And in the region he was currently in, there was a dense amount of death and destruction!

Within this atmosphere and the enemy Lifeforms rising beside the True Emperor of Quintessence, Ahpuch could feel the chains of the unreachable 17th Boundary Layer of Destruction loosening!

It told him that so long as he destroyed everything here...there was a chance.

And this was a chance he wouldn't miss.

He gazed at the massive form of a Draconic Elephantine Ender, and three Apex Aeonic Lifeforms that were bright in their own right, one of them being among those he had half-heartedly chased earlier as he saw they would lead him to a grander path.

A meeting of the moment this Apex War unfolded and the visage of the True Emperor of Quintessence!

Now, they stood before him against him as they made grand moves.

Now he...would make his own grand move and show them how far out of their depths they were.

That the one to face him had yet to step up!

That when he merely moved toward them, they would be forced to move back!

He took a step forward.

And...

HOONG!

Amidst the destruction, a terrifying authority of nothingness shot forth from him like an expanding bubble.

It was none other than Vacuous Hyperversal Authority!

The moment it erupted from him, the four beings standing before his path had their gazes change as their bodies glimmered with defensive brilliance and forcefully moved back.

And how could they not? Their very Souls screamed danger from being enveloped by the authority outside of space, time, and reality itself!

"Fight."

His voice rose out across the battlefield for the first time as it thundered with brilliance.

Fight.

Was he telling the battalions of the Apex Aeonic Elders behind him this?

He took another step, and the area of effect of the Vacuous Hyperversal Authority became ever grander and vaster as the four beings standing before him were pushed further back!

The visage of Aurelia had an enraged and distasteful expression while Hyperion and the others were somber.

"Fight!"

Ahpuch called out again, this time with even more ferocity as he took a large stride forward!

The enemies before him were pushed back past a certain dazzling multicolored throne and the figures of the self-proclaimed First Commander of the Infinite Hyperversal Haven, as well as the Undead Entity he had called out.

They were the only ones that didn't move a single step backwards as Ahpuch's eyes shone with brilliance.

His current armaments burned into crimson flames as the authority of Destruction transformed into a heavy armor that covered all parts of his body apart from his head.

His hair now burned crimson and black as his eyes shone like burning suns.

The immense aura of Destruction burned brightly around him as with the periphery of the Vacuous Hyperversal Authority about to spill into the Armies of the True Emperor of Quintessence, he called out for the third time.

"Fight!"

...!

Before his word even ended, the visage of Alexander King raised his skull staff and pointed out.

And from it, a similar eruption of nothingness erupted in a grand show of glory!

It was another being who could use Vacuous Hyperversal Authority- or at least had access to it as Ahpuch could tell its form was a bit different!

A circular domain of nothingness tinged with destruction barreled down as another domain of nothingness tinged with brilliance erupted out to stop it, a shocking clash erupting out as reality began to break down!

BZZZT!

The domains didn't even touch.

Just nearing each other caused the erasure of reality between them as this junction of space seemed like a massive fissure erupted- the crimson silver skies above being parted and seething out while the earth below that had begun to be covered by the verdant obsidian mist of death did the same.

It felt like the battlefield was divided into two halves at such a moment as the masses of Apex Aeonic Lifeforms stood up with their mouths snapping open.

They couldn't sit still for this.

They couldn't not focus and observe this grandeur!

"Vacuous Hyperversal Authority..."

The Priestess Persephone uttered out in a far corner away from the battlefield as she wasn't the only one.

Many eyes watched the clash of Vacuous Hyperversal Authority unleashed by two beings as how frequently seen was such an authority?

How important were the personages that held it?

A clash of this authority alone divided the battlefield in half as two central forces that shone like burning suns gazed at each other, the terrifying First Commander of the Infinite Hyperversal Haven adorned in his bone Armaments spoke out with majesty.

"Fight, you say...but this will not be a fight. This will only be a trampling."

WAA!

The voice was filled with confidence as why was this? The enemy was powerful. The Apex Elders were veterans of the battlefield with a myriad of Dynamis of Extremity under their banner!

So what gave the forces of the True Emperor of Quintessence so much confidence?

SHIING!

On the side of the True Emperor of Quintessence calmly sitting on his throne, 7 points in space had long since ruptured into massive obsidian portals as at this point, blazing auras in titanic forms began to step out.

And before anyone else could make a move, a raspy voice that had its own portals formed cackled as its voice sought to dominate the battlefield!

[Death.]

WAA!

[There shall only be death.]

The voice was raspy as the Haven Lich Ra'Zan moved.

A lich controlled a myriad of things to do with death, but did this only mean summoning legions of undead?

The answer was a resounding no.

Death came in many forms.

And the Thanatos Haven Lich Ra'Zan sought to show these uncultured beings just how many forms death could take as his staff began to wave amidst the clash of the Vacuous Hyperversal Authorities, his voice ringing out multiple times over!

[Haven Curse of Corruption.]

...!

[Unrecorded Plague of the End.]

[Aura of Fear.]

[Sea of Agony.]

One after another!

Utterly debilitating abilities bloomed as a billowing smoke of death began to surge out like waves as at this moment, the two buzzing domains of the Vacuous Hyperversal Authorities were canceled out as the waves of death thundered down upon the masses like an endless sea!
