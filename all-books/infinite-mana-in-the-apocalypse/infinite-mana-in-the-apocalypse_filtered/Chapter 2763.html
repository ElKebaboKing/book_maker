2761 Honorarium! II

Honorarium!

It was a type of dignity that transformed one's existence in imperceptible ways as their achievements actually affected the flow of Reality around them.

As she spoke about this, the Esteemed Havenly Empress before Noah's eyes reclined on her crimson gold throne of Helios as the golden wings on her forehead fluttered, her eyes gazing up above serenely.

[There are Primogenitors who have accumulated so many Honorariums that the moment they make a move, the whole reality unfolds in such a way as if they were at the very center of everything, and everybody else was just playing a minor part in their Records.]

...!

As he heard this, Noah raised his brows. It seemed that people with a great deal of achievements were actually trying to make themselves the centers of attention- possible main characters of the unfolding reality as those around them with lesser accomplishments would only serve as their extras!

It felt...like others were intruding on his playing field.

But he knew that before he appeared, in the eons of History that passed, there were of course Emperors and Entities who defined their Eras. Beings that shone brightly and held power to shift the very working reality of these Havens.

But now that he was here...

'You can all take a backseat...'

...!

His thoughts alone raged with tyranny as he looked expectantly into the future!

Extremity Solarium then refocused her gaze onto Noah as she continued.

[I see no falsehood in your words, even with the fact that you knowing something like this raises a lot of questions. A major one is what my Extremity Clone has been figuring out by going into the Supernal Haven of Dissolution and glancing at the newly enacted Records there. The Underdwellers are abuzz about you. Noah Osmont...let me ask you about something that has yet to reach my attention, but would have been brought to me soon enough. This Infernal Havenly Commerce...what do you plan to do with it?]

WAA!

A probing question that Noah expected down the line has brought itself much earlier as finally, a key figure in the Supernal Havens of Extremity locked onto his Infernal Havenly Commerce!

The Blood Sources sold from his Infernal Havenly Commerce were already transforming the Underdwellers from the Nether Havens as of course, the Havenly Extremity Lineages had gathered information about this and kept an eye on the Archipelagos he set up within Dissolution.

[The Records of the buzzing Underdwellers seem to think you are merely connected to the Infernal Havenly Commerce, but from what I'm gathering, you should be at its very core. So, you can obtain knowledge of the movements of Vacuous Entities of the Fifth Gradation of Extremity in advance, with you being critical enough or having something that would force them to make a move, and you have a Vitalis Lineage contesting with even the Four Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Lineages of these Supernal Havens. Everything about you points to an Entity that should be supported by pillars of Honorariums...you would greatly benefit from following the path of your predecessors and others like you by joining factions of Primogenitors.]

...!

Her dual pupils radiated waves of beauty and grace as she continued!

[Behind every one of the Four Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Lineages are Primogenitors. Even the prominent Lineages of the Nether Havens have their own Primogenitors among them. Join their cause and be under their light. Their Honorariums will mask yours. Enemies will not be able to easily discover your Records nor be able to plan to do whatever they're planning to do to you in the next day. Under the banner of honorariums and records of Primogenitors, you will have the time to cultivate your power until you rise to a sufficient level in the Gradations of Extremity where you can finally move alone, unhindered.]

Her words were like claps of thunder filled with simmering calmness as she proposed a pathway for Noah!

And yet the moment he heard it...he shook his head with a smile while denying such a path vehemently.

[I do not plan to bask under anyone's light. Such a thing would seek the budding Quintessence and Tyranny within me entirely!]

Tendrils of multi-colored light erupted around the crimson gold throne of Helios he sat on as he said such words.

And he continued.

[Even now, I move alone, unhindered. Many forces have tried to topple me before, and yet they were ground down to become ink that filled the pages of history. Their legends and honorariums faded into nothingness as in this unfolding eon, history will only repeat itself.]

...!

Quintessence freely blazed around him as he spoke.

The Entity at the Fifth Gradation of Extremity gazed at him calmly, her dual pupils flashing with light as she ultimately sighed in the end and shook her head.

[Another crazy guy chasing dead paths. Even if you manage to overcome that wall, do you know how difficult a path you have ahead of you? Ah....no matter. If Infernal Emperors are entering the Vitalis Hyperversal Domains for something you possess or you yourself, then of course we shall partake in an opportunity to derail them. Of course, you won't be left empty handed as depending on the results, you can take 25% of the gains obtained from this opportunity.]

Her eyes flickered as she instantly got down to business.

25% of the gain.

Noah himself wouldn't be doing anything but provide the information, but he would attain 25% of the gains that may stem from this ambush!

He nodded lightly at such words as Extremity Solarium took his affirmation and continued.

[I will include two or three others to share in this opportunity right before it occurs- this leaves less of a chance of the Infernal Emperors being warned or alarmed of this ambush. Once something is Recorded and pondered about, it is easy for others to grasp it. But apart from this...you're supposed to be stepping into the Havenly Hyperversal Gauntlet of Gradation. I recommend you do this after you've grasped at least a single concept to Extremity and can freely utilize your own Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Rune Scriptures. Apart from your clone dying within the Gauntlet, there is even a threat of complete eradication of your Records if you aren't careful enough. You will only truly benefit if you have already embarked on the Gradations of Extremity.]

Her calm words were inviolable as she leaned forward from her crimson gold throne, her eyes sharp as she spoke once more.

[Now, just how many more details can you give me about this? When and exactly where? And if you somehow have a grasp of anything to do with their identities, this will go much smoother...]

...!
