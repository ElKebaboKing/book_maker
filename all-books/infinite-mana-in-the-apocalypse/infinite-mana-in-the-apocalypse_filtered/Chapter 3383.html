Chapter 3381 Appaling Complexity! I

Inside a certain Dream.

BOOM!

The moment the lids of Archimedes closed, a unique weaving was forever lost within him.

Inside that moment, an expression of relief and sadness overcame Archimedes as near him, the globule of blood of a Pioneer shockingly warped and disappeared from existence entirely!

And an instant later, the terrifying will of the Great Instrument of the Beyonder wrapped around Archimedes- an unfathomable light of pressure and complexity cascading down as the body of Archimedes became crushed into obsidian paste.

The Will within it was decimated, with the weavings of the Great Instrument of the Beyonder seemingly searching for something inside these collapsed weavings as it couldn't find it!

HUUM!

Its weavings shook with interminable complexity as in the distance, the Nihilos Marburg, a Pioneer of Existence became stern as ever so cautiously, it backed away.

The Nihilos Marburg held terrifying weavings of power as a Pioneer, and it regarded the visage of the Great Instrument of the Beyonder with Great caution- even gazing around it as it kept its eyes peeled!

This creature had to have the support of other Pioneers to be able to do what it did with Ynnere, and now the war it was waging against Nihilos.

So the body of the Pioneer of Existence, Nihilos Marburg, looked around cautiously while a large part of its intent was still focused on the veiled visage of the Great Instrument of the Beyonder!

The aura of that entity that was for some reason enraged with someone on its own side was stifling, and it didn't even pay attention to the Nihilos Marburg at this moment as above its massive veiled obsidian visage, the barely observable body of the Great Instrument of the Beyonder vibrated as a terrifying rune swelled up above it.

It was a rune radiating intense fervor and power that read...AMARANTOS!

Amarantos.

But it was extremely solid and filled with complex smaller runes that constantly shifted and changed, with its radiance being so luminous as if it could be used to dream of a terrifying number of things!

It radiated terrifying complexity above the Sword of the Beyonder as at this time, barely a glimpse of its intent could be discerned.

|I will find you.|

|I will find you!|

BOOM!

The massive Rune of the Amarantos Dream of the Megalos flashed again and again rapidly as if it were seeking a specific direction- for a specific dream, but it wasn't able to find it.

Terrifying weavings of complexity erupted as a certain Dream...supposedly continued to unfold long after the weavings of Archimedes perished!

|Ah...|

The Great Segregator felt her Severed existence trembling as it continued to support the storm of weavings around the Instrument of the Beyonder these past few minutes.

With every passing second that she stabilized her Severed Domain, a burst of complexity erupted from it that reinforced her weavings again and again in ways she didn't know were possible!

The reinforcement felt entirely unique and new as if she had never come across it before in all her cycles of life.

She could feel her degree of complexity increase substantially with every passing second, her emotions unable to jump from alarm to euphoria as she felt like if she didn't focus for even a moment, everything would collapse.

And now...

HUUM!

A burst of obsidian light flashed as the dazzling rune of Amarantos above the Instrument blared with brilliance.

The Great Segregator wanted to curse as she could feel complexity building up again- almost as if for one last burst.

What the hell was happening now?!

Within the Severed Domain.

Eyes alongside a Will fluttered with brilliance as an array of words rose.

|Even with the initial low level of proficiency of the Amarantos Dream of the Megalos, an unsurpassable dream of unique weavings of Existence was experienced due to a convergence of multiple factors.|

|This Unsurpassable Dream has been brought to an end after weavings potentially harmful to you made themselves present.|

|Your proficiency in the Amarantos Dream of the Megalos has been elevated to the Fourth Echelon, with your Proficiency at 88%.|

|Based on this, the weavings of the Amarantos Dream of the Megalos you can obtain are somewhat exceptional.|

|All memories of the Unsurpassable Dream are retained.|

...!

The memories of Archimedes flashed within the mind of Ozymandias.

His weavings.

His love for the tragically lost Lacana!

His ambition.

All of it came and went far too quickly. And in the end, all of it collapsed.

Thinking of this, Ozymandias couldn't help but feel melancholic as he knew that Dream...was far too real!

There was a Lacana. There was an Archimedes. And now, their weavings were forever collapsed and gone.

His weavings vibrated brightly as the prompts continued.

|The Weavings of this single Dream can be retrieved, with two out of three components viable for retrieval due to the current Proficiency of the Amarantos Dream of the Megalos.|

|The Components of the Dream capable of being chosen are as follows.|

|Outerversal Physiology Echelon VIII, Megalos Regnum Echelon VIII. 3 Billion Pure Delegatus Channels.]

[Weavings and Research of dozens of Source Complexity of Entities from the Megalos Nihilos. The Full Sword of the Megalos Transcendent Megalos Ability.]

[A Single Globule of Blood of a Pioneer.]

...!

Three dazzling singularities wondrously rose before his eyes as he had to choose between them.

Ozymandias only glanced at them for a moment before the choice was made.

[The full weavings of a Single Globule of Blood of a Pioneer have been retrieved]

[The Full Weavings and Research of dozens of Source Complexity of Entities from the Megalos Nihilos and The Full Sword of the Megalos Transcendent Megalos Ability have been retrieved.]

[Part of the Dream of Outerversal Morphon Archimedes, the Great Titan, has been forever lost.] ...!

Choices were made.

And from these choices, came consequences!

BOOM!

Within the Severed Domain, the voice of the Great Segregator rose up as it felt tumultuous waves of complexity erupting out- a terrifying aura briefly flashing within her space that caused even her to feel trepidation!

It was an aura that far exceeded Existence Severing- its brief flash of brilliance causing her to shudder as just as quickly as this brilliance appeared...it faded!
