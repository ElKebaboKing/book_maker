Chapter 3666 Ashes I

The True Frequency of the Singed.

Inside it, Noah remained atop the blistering True Heart of Existence.

The same True Heart of Existence that was considered the Source of the scattered Flames of the Wheel of Existenceand only a fraction of what made up the Absolute Complex True Source that was the Wheel of Existence.

Its gloriously pure white flames and blood continued to surge into his body as he sought to harness that unparalleled purity to elevate his other pathways, testing if they, too, could ascend to the level of True Sources.

The effectiveness had dropped significantly after he attained the True Source of the Singed, yet he remained here nonetheless as he did not stop.

He could feel the distant gaze of the Origin Singed. And at that moment, his expression shiftedeyes narrowing as he turned toward a distant region within the vast True Frequency of the Wheel. There, the brilliant white-gold space split apart and tore open as a shattered white Wheel of Existence pierced through, and it was carrying none other than...

Bob.

BOOM!

He crashed into the True Frequency of the Singed, having done the impossible.

He had shattered an Absolute Complex True Sourceeven if it had already been dead, just to brute force his way into a True Frequency of Existence.

...!

Noah's body, still draining the purity of the True Heart of Existence, now only contained the residual power of the True Source of the Singed. At most, he could express a fraction of its might through his form here.

But even that, magnified by the effects of the System of Existence of Ordo Ab Chaowasn't enough to stop Bob.

And now, Bob was in a True Frequency of the Wheel, exactly where he wanted to be!

The moment he arrived, tears spilled from his eyeseyes that shone like boundless singularities. Behind him, the other body of Noah followed, trailing an obsidian Pillar of Concordance and spinning halos of dark brilliance.

Bob clutched the remnants of a shattered half of a white Wheel of Existencedim, fading into glimmers of ash. He'd used it not just to find the True Source of the Singed, but to bypass its protective foldsa feat that would've obliterated anyone else.

Half of an entire Wheel of Existence began to crumble into pure white ash. With its collapse came the release of countless souls.

Wailing entitiesburning within the weavings of a dead True Source for what could have been centuries now perished.

Their cries ceased.

As if they had been released.

Towards the end, the ceasing cries of some even seemed to be those of relief as if they were thankful they did not have to live in death any longer!

A silence came.

And with that silence, Bob's face twisted with grief.

His bald head began to regenerate, flesh knitting back together as his eyes filled with the weight of what he had just done.

For so long, he'd heard those screams. And now...gone. Silenced.

The absence was unbearable.

He nearly screamed.

But he held it in. Because what he'd sacrificed... couldn't be in vain.

His will surged outward across the white-gold landscape of this True Frequency.

He saw the remnants of fallen wheels. He saw a terrifying, silver-white Wheel of Existenceits countless Spokes gleaming like spears.

And he saw the Origin Singedits expression livid, its body seething with rising power.

The defender placed here within this sacred frequency to ensure that this very scenario never happened.

And yet Bob cast only a single glance toward the Origin Singed before shifting his gaze toward a distant horizon.

A direction where waves of white-gold brilliance stretched on endlesslyseemingly empty.

But Bob felt it.

An entrance.

One that led directly to the Absolute True Source of Existencethe core of the Wheel itself.

BOOM!

He surged forward.

But Noah moved just as fast.

His obsidian form, blazing with the splendor of the True Source of Existence, blinked into Bob's path. He collided with him, driving him back as a Pillar of Concordance sent Bob spiraling across the skies.

Noah turned his gaze toward the distant Origin Singed, hand outstretched, voice roaring:

"Don't you fucking dare bring back your True Source!"

His presence thundered with command. He could already feel itthat sliver of power within the Origin Singed was rising rapidly, nearing the Nascent Source-Bearer level of complexity.

That meant one thing.

The Origin Singed was preparing to recall its True Source from the Infinite Ravines and forcefully reintegrating it into the Wheel of Existence.

It was the same blasphemous action the Omnifather once tookan act that brought Bob, the Dead Thing, into being.

And now, it was happening again.

This ancient being, who lived for logic, purity, and precision, heard Noah's words.

But his expression remained unreadable.

His body launched forward, inserting itself between Bob and the distant entrance.

His voice, ancient and resolute, echoed like a verdict.

"The most important priority is to stop the immediate threatthe one that seeks to disrupt or collapse the Wheel of Existence. That threat is here. It has pierced this sacred place. In its presence, all other possibilities are irrelevant."

...!

Pure logic radiated from him.

From his perspective, if Bob reached the heart of the Wheel, nothing else mattered.

What use was it to protect the Infinite Ravines if a Dead Thing had already broken into a True Frequency of the Wheel itself and was about to taint its Core?

If the Origin Singed's main body remained anchored within its Gateway Domuswhat difference would it make?

What was he defending if the core was already compromised?

So, in the next moment

From a Nascent Source-Bearer Complexity, a terrifying grandeur erupted.

The power of Harmonized Sourcebound Icon Complexity surged from the Origin Singed.

His form warped, appearing before Bob.

A massive Pillar of Concordance, glowing with pure white and gold, bloomedslamming into Bob and enveloping him completely.

The True Origin Singed had returned.

Abandoning his position. Bearing the weight of unspeakable consequences.

But he returned with his full True Source of Existence...
