Chapter 4155: Order! III

The words were sweet honey poured over bitter medicine, heavy with the weight of obligation dressed as opportunity.

Sigrids expression shifted through several complex emotions, her frown deepening as she processed the manipulation wrapped in maternal concern.

At this point, Noahs figure, which had remained seated with tyrannical calm throughout the entire exchange, finally made a move.

And it was a terrifying one in its casualness.

His hand moved with speed that transcended speed, reaching out to grasp Sigrids waist with the certainty of someone claiming what was already theirs.

Before anyone could process the motion, before thought could become reaction, he pulled her back and...shockingly, impossibly, with audacity that redefined audacity, and seated her on his lap.

The action was so quick and so unexpected that reality itself seemed to need a moment to catch up!

Duke Gwendolyns mouth actually fell open!

Origin Amas white eyes widened fractionally!

Even Duke Whisker on Noahs side squeaked in surprise!

Sigrid, THE Living Order, was sitting on Noahs lap like a queen on her throne, except the throne was a person who was treating the gathering of existences most powerful beings as a minor inconvenience to be managed.

Noah gazed up at Origin Ama with calm that transcended calm, his eyes carrying the kind of sharpness that could cut concepts!

When he spoke, his voice carried the tone of someone explaining something that should have been obvious to a slow child.

"Some people dont hear the word No in all their lives. When they are told exactly that, they act like it doesnt exist. No comes in many forms. No. Nope. Nah. Never. Fuck off... it can have its varieties. But no is no." His gaze grew even sharper, if such a thing was possible. "I do believe this young lady here said no."

...!

The words landed like the final ingredients being added to an already volatile mixture, the last element needed to transform potential into catastrophic kinetic reality!

HUUM!

The auras of all the Dukes present blazed upward simultaneously, their authorities erupting like volcanoes that had been dormant too long.

Power that could reshape Folds pressed against the barrier Origin Ama had created, testing its limits, searching for weakness.

"You... you!" Duke Gwendolyns voice cracked with emotion she couldnt properly express, her carefully maintained composure shattering like ice under summer sun.

The words wouldnt come...there were no words for this level of disrespect, this casual dismissal of everything the Living Origins represented.

But more than all of this, Origin Amas white eyes began to pulse with light that suggested violence being very carefully considered.

Her body didnt emanate raging power...that would have been too simple, too crude.

Instead, she contained it, compressed it, refined it into something that would be absolutely decisive when released.

Sigrid observed the volatile scene developing around them.

The Living Origins behind Gias burned with their authorities even higher, reality itself beginning to buckle under the combined pressure of so many Duke-level existences preparing for violence.

"Order," she said simply.

The word should have carried weight, should have commanded attention.

But nobody paid attention to it. What Noah had just done, the casual claiming, the dismissive words...was too heavy, too insulting to process alongside anything else.

Behind Noah, his assembled Dukes began their own preparations.

Titano moved forward with a sheepish expression that didnt match the Duke-level power radiating from his massive form.

Erikson, still in his beach attire that had somehow become even more inappropriate for the occasion, smiled wide as he pulled out the Slipper of Existential Humiliation...a weapon whose name alone should have been warning enough.

The sight of Titano, her former guard, her weapon that she had cultivated and traded away, actually stepping up to stand against her pushed Duke Gwendolyn past her breaking point.

Her eyes showed profound lividity, the kind of rage that came from being betrayed by something you had never valued enough to think could betray you.

Chaos was about to unfold!

Violence that would reshape this entire section of the Folds was heartbeats away from manifesting!

Sigrid looked at all this, at Order itself preparing to dissolve into its opposite, and called out again with more force: "Order!"

WAA!

Her voice echoed with authority that should have been impossible to ignore.

Yet still, nobody listened.

The momentum toward violence had its own gravity now, its own inevitability that even Order struggled to redirect.

After having called for Order twice and being ignored both times, something shifted in Sigrids expression.

The patience evaporated. The diplomatic approach ended. THE Living Order decided to live up to her title.

"I SAID... ORDER!"

BOOM!

The third time was the last.

Her entire figure instantly blazed with a terrifying pillar of authority that redefined what authority meant!

A white beam of Order shot upward with such force that it shattered the barrier Origin Ama had created...not through superior power but through something more fundamental.

How was such a thing possible when Origin Ama was so much more powerful in raw numbers?

The answer was elegantly simple and terrifyingly complex.

The light of Order had flowed into the barrier, seen its structure and stability, understood the order that held it together, and then rearranged that order to include a directive to break when touched by her light.

The barrier shattered because Order had decreed it should shatter!

Not through force but through reorganization of what the barrier believed about itself.

That was a glimpse of the true power of THE Living Order...not to impose order through strength but to reveal that everything already had an order that she could see, understand, and adjust.

The light spread even further, its brilliance spilling out across the nearby Concordat Amphitheater like dawn breaking over a world that had forgotten sunlight existed!

Every being present, from the mightiest Duke to the most recent arrival, felt Orders touch...not forcing, not commanding, but simply revealing that chaos was a choice, and she was offering an alternative.

The image was glorious beyond description!

Oh!

Sigrid, while seated on Noahs lap, using him as her own makeshift throne, had transformed into something that transcended her previous manifestations.

Her eyes were aflame with resplendent white flames that burned without heat, illuminating without blinding.

On her forehead, a stellar white Eye of Order had opened...not metaphorically but literally, a third eye that saw not just what was but what should be!

Her entire figure was bathed in inviolable authority that made the concept of opposition seem quaint.

She was grand in this moment, terrible and beautiful, the promise and threat of Order given form!

Her voice echoed out deeply, addressing not just those immediately present but everyone within range of her expanded authority.

"Before me, there will be Order!"

The declaration didnt request or demand.

Oh, it simply stated what would be, with the certainty of gravity declaring that things would fall!

The scene was glorious in its composition...the elevated beings frozen in tableau of interrupted violence, Sigrid crowned with glorious authority while seated on Noahs lap as if he were her throne, the shattered remnants of Origin Amas barrier falling like snow made from crystallized impossibility!

Magnificent in its implications!

THE Living Order had just demonstrated that raw power meant nothing if she could simply reorder its purpose.

Quintessential in its perfection...this was what it meant to truly embody a concept rather than merely wield it.

Oh, what a moment to witness!

What a declaration of independence through the very force she was supposed to use in service to others!
