Chapter 2741 Their Rise Tips the Scales! IV

Her words caused the brows of many to rise as they already knew what she was talking about!

The pressure of the Rhetorician of the Senatus pulsed as he probed calmly.

{Oh?}

{Yes, Rhetorician.} Seer Amestris nodded calmly as she waved her hands to show many illusory screens that displayed the woes of the Avernus Clan over the last days!

{Isolation, chaos, deaths. All of these are things the Avernus Clan has faced over the last days as we have the highest death toll when counting all Seven Clans, with our lands currently burning in chaos. All of this stems from a Minor Conflict with this man here...}

She waved her hands once more as the visage of none other than Noah painted an illusory screen!

The eyes of 80 Tier 4s and the Tier 5 Rhetorician of the Senatus focused on this image as Seer Amestris continued.

{It is nothing new. The conflict began on the lower rungs of our forces and gradually grew, with the subject here- Noah Osmont, not revealing he was part of a force of supremacy connected to the Supernal Havens of Extremity as he wasn't treated like an associate or an Entity of the same standing as us. This has led to an unneeded conflict during this war that weakens all of us, with the forces he is connected to cutting off access to the rising Extremity Treasure known as Blood Sources, and our Extremities being butchered as if they were pigs for slaughter.}

WAA!

The sharpness in her voice wasn't contained as it spread out freely!

She gazed at all beings here on their respective thrones as she continued.

{The Avernus Clan is prepared to admit our initial fault in this matter as we seek to bring Noah Osmont to the table and discuss an end to this needless conflict. The woes of any members of the Seven Clans are the woes of the Senatus, and the butchering of 100 Tier 2 Hyperversal Extremities by those from our own Havens is not something we can turn our eyes away from. All of them...were on the front lines fighting against Vacuous Malignancies for the Senatus when they were butchered by those backing Noah Osmont, some who may even be in this very domain.}

...!

A stark silence descended after her words as many of those situated on the thrones gazed at each other.

The Rhetorician of the Senatus of Extremity was calm as his domination figure was revealed.

It was a man built like a mountain, his size massive while sitting on the throne as halcyon threads of light swirled around his magnetic face.

Strands of golden hair fell freely as he spoke calmly.

{The Source of these Blood Sources are from the Supernal Havens of Extremity, none of us can aid you in that. But if the crux of it all is with this Noah Osmont, then we simply need to bring him to the table. Who here has a line of connection with this being?}

His authoritative voice echoed out as many beings silently looked at each other!

Nobody's voice rose for a moment as Seer Amestris spoke once more.

{He shares a connection with the prominent Scions of the Infinite Extremity and the Sima Clan. Some of his bodies have been verified to be within these domains.}

{Oh?}

Her words caused many eyes to turn towards multiple thrones that held the Extremity Clones of Entities from the Sima Clan and the Inheritance of the Infinite Extremity!

Receiving all of these gazes, a Throne representing a Tier 4 Ancestor of the Sima Clan vibrated before the voice of an old man emanated out.

{The one known as Noah Osmont is currently within one of the Domains of the Sima Clan as a guest of my grandson. I can pass on whatever message is decided on here towards his direction at most.}

These words caused a nod to bloom on the Rhetorician of the Senatus as he didn't seem to want to spend any more time on this issue, his voice thundering out.

{Very well, bring this Noah Osmont to the table and we will have the Avernus Clan and him bury the hatchet. What is next on the Agenda?}

The issue was brought up and resolved so quickly that the eyes of some Entities flashed with their own myriad of thoughts.

Days ago, a Representative of the Cult of the Endless Shadows had sought rectification from the Senatus of Extremity for the blatant attack and seizing of their Armament of Extremity, yet none of their complaints were brought to the floor as everything seemed to have been forgotten after the Vein Rifts bloomed in the Haven of Dissolution.

Yet now, the Avernus Clan brought an issue forward as it was already going towards a resolution!

What a difference in response.

What a difference in treatment!

One was an inconsequential Inheritance that didn't even have a single Tier 4 Hyperversal Extremity, while the other was one of the Seven Clans with a deep history and multiple of such Entities within their ranks.

Yet when watching such a reality play out, it was hard not to see the unfairness in this situation!

It is the Havenly Hyperversal Emperor Blood Sources that the Havenly Moon Clan has obtained in great abundance. We...are finding the power of those who assimilate with these Blood Sources go so far as to double or even triple, and even further advancement is occurring right now as the issue we bring forth is the procurement of these Blood Sources for the armies of the Senatus of Extremity in the front lines.}

...!

Her words were astonishing as Seer Amestris and other representatives of the Avernus Clan couldn't help but turn somber!

They didn't know that the Blood Sources they didn't have access to actually augmented one's power so much.

Other Clans were actually enjoying such boons? And their clan continued to be barred from them? This couldn't continue!

{The Infernal Havenly Commerce of the Supernal Havens of Extremity distributes these Blood Sources to...all who would pay a sufficient price, barring the Avernus Clan. Since their conflict with Osmont leads them to believe he may have a pull with the Infernal Havenly Commerce, we can also communicate with him and see if we can obtain more information on these Blood Sources, and what the larger plans of the Infernal Havenly Commerce are. Obtaining them for all of our forces...is equivalent to multiplying our power many times over.}

WAA!

The words held deep meaning to those who could truly understand the workings of things as the gaze of the Rhetorician of the Senatus of Extremity flickered with brilliance.

This was the second time the name of Noah Osmont was brought up in the agenda of the Senatus of Extremity.

This Entity had to be looked into!
