Shock!

This was the emotion that many of the watchers felt as for some, rousing anger exploded forth!

They were all powerhouses of the Seven Havens as their actions represented the Wills of major factions and powers whenever they moved!

For them to move like this now...

Erikson's soul buzzed as either a somber gaze, he sent out a message.

"Uncle, we cannot remain idle as this situation unfolds..."

His words traversed into the mind of one of the Sima Clan Extremities in this Haven as the scene he saw was something that breached everything they stood for!

Those with more power very clearly committing wrongs regardless simply because it served their best interests.

Even though it wouldn't make any difference, Erikson had already grabbed his slippers and held them tightly into his hands as if he would jump into the battle himself, but what could a recently advanced Apex Aeonic Lifeform who was merely distorting probability to converge his first Dynamis of Extremity do against the grandeur of Hyperversal Extremities?

[Be at ease, Young Master. I have already made a move.]

...!

The words were unexpected ones as Erikson's eyes flashed with brilliance.

His Uncle had already made a move.

It meant that he was one of the Hyperversal Extremities on the battlefield as Eirkson's heart didn't waver on which side he would have been standing on, his intuition ringing as he came to focus on the figures of the three Extremities wearing masks that had the inscriptions of [II]!

[I've told you before that you will be officiated and begin missions after you become an Apex Aeonic Lifeform. You haven't been officiated yet, but you can observe how we move and why we move now. Because reality is not black and white, because the Seven Havens are not perfect Vaults of peace and prosperity as they may seem on the surface.]

WAA!

Her grandfather's face was filled with an age and interminable power at the same time as he adorned the white gold mask on his face, everything about him changing and becoming different as he turned into a man of short stature with a bulky build- where if she hadn't seen this body of his change before her, she wouldn't have known it was him!

Her expression was grim as her grandfather continued.

[It is not yet time for me to move yet, because if the words of Guardian Recorder are true, they have yet to fully reveal themselves.]

...!

Words of mystery and wonder erupted out as some of the prominent Scions saw what was happening and didn't sit still, reaching out to those above them to make a difference.

But it didn't always play out the same way for all.

Besides Erikson and Ayame's Soul Clones, the Soul Clone of Atlas of the Plum Sword of Extremity also reached out to those above him when he saw the shocking scene unfolding and remembering the favor he owed Osmont from the Initiation Step.

BZZT!

Shock that was replaced by outrage as when he turned back on the ever changing battlefield, it turned out that those above him...were on the side of the veiled Extremities attacking!

It was a devastating reality for him to affirm as the Apex Aeonic Lifeforms gazing in the surroundings tried their best to follow what was happening before them.
