Chapter 1250 - Analyze And Copy! I

Something wild that would involve him silently teleporting his main body away and bringing in his clones...and then spamming and utilizing his crazy percentages to collect an astound amount of loot that would make the strongest of beings shocked.

All of these were possibilities.

As for what Noah would do?

His liquid form of Primordial Essence nearly formed into a devilish smile as the moment he came across the first clusters of Primordial Beasts, he already knew what he would be doing!

Further ahead in the Primordial Sea as Noah weaved across the bodies and auras of too many Lesser and Grand Primordial Beasts, he could feel a singular overpowering aura waving out maddeningly as it was uniquely different from any of the Lesser of Grand Primordial Beasts.

This aura caused Noahs consciousness to churn as a terrifying thought crossed through them.

Has one already risen? An Ender of Reality?

WAA!

A Cosmic Primordial Beast! Ender of Reality!

This was one of the few things Noah could attribute to the vastly terrifying aura he had begun to sense as he went deeper into the Primordial Sea while being surrounded by countless Primordial Beasts all around him!

When such creatures arose, the fall of a Reality wasnt far away as Noah could not truly confirm that this aura was one of these creatures, or if it was simply a Grand Primordial Beast that was about to become a Cosmic one, or something even more unique.

There were too many things he didnt know, so he would continue to go deeper as he found out more information first before he did anything! And he did not have to wait long as he came across something fantastical.

A few billion light years to his right, across a cluster of countless Primordial Beasts- his awareness actually found an area of the Primordial Sea that was utterly clear, the Primordial Beast seemingly making a path as 5 enormous Grand Primordial Beasts billions of light years in size had white chains erupting from either side of their mouths- each of the chains clasping onto the barriers of a glimmering Universe as it was being dragged behind them!

A Universe separated from its Cosmos as it was 100 billion light years in size, and yet 5 Grand Primordial Beasts that looked different and released auras more terrifying than all other Grand Primordial Beasts were dragging this Universe in a solemn manner, the action even seeming sacred before Noahs eyes as he saw it wasnt just a single universe being pulled forward.

He shockingly saw that it was like a procession of Universes, each one having 5 Grand Primordial Beasts with pristine white chains attaching to their boundaries and dragging these Universes along towards a single location.

A location that Noah couldnt help but now gaze towards as his Primordial Essence form moved rapidly to appear in the surroundings where other Primordial Beasts were watching this sacred procession in solemn silence.

The procession of Universes were seemingly like glimmering circles plucked off a Cosmos, Noah sensing quintillions of life forms within it as he found his currently nonexistent heart shaking as he knew even if he made a move...the ensuing destruction would shatter all of these Universes! He counted 12 of them as he made his heart firmer while following them, spending a whole hour as the procession went to the depths of the Primordial Sea, Noah actually finding that fewer and fewer Primordial Beasts could be found as they went deeper in.

As for the reason why there were fewer Primordial Beasts? It was due to the fact that the terrifying aura he had felt this whole time was becoming more and more prominent, and Noah was finally able to see what it was as the process of 12 Universes pulled by Primordial Beast finally arrived before a vast open space within the Primordial Sea, this space having a single existence within it as this existence caused Noahs heart to turn cold!

Woah...

How could he even describe it?

Floating to be ten times as large as any of the Universes being dragged towards it, a beast of utter regality that had golden white scales could be seen. Five pairs of multicolored wings erupted on its back, and it had four ginormous scaled limbs shining in gold as uniquely, this beast was floating upright as it showed mannerisms reminiscent of humans!

Yet it was anything but that as on its face, scaled draconic eyes dripping with majesty and power could be seen along with two pairs of pristinely white tusks and a trunk that made Noah think of a war elephant! Its head truly seemed like a terrifying mixture of the oldest dragons and ancient war elephants.

Something that caused Noahs consciousness to vibrate was that when he looked at all the Primordial Beasts he came across in this Primordial Sea, he was able to see them and their wondrous composition as well as the movement of essence within their bodies, but he could not see this same sight for the terrific beast before him!

He couldnt see any of its inner essence movements nor find out what type of Origin it had, such a thing leading to a terrifying conclusion that one could easily affirm when they looked at the slit on the forehead of the creature that showed a hidden third eye that was currently closed.

An eye that even while closed was releasing bright rays of pristine white light, none of the secrets of this existence being revealed to Noah as in the next second, its trunk lifted up to reveal a bottomless abyss that was its mouth as it was directed to a Universe that was brought before it.

The 5 Primordial Beasts pulling it flashed away with haste as below the raised golden trunk where an endless void could be seen, an accumulation of dao essences gathered in a unique fashion as Noah in his Primordial essence form felt his left eye throbbing!

It was throbbing as shockingly, it observed the activation of a skill from this magisterial existence- a skill that was so profound that Noah was forced to pay attention and watch as after the trunk of this creature opened and its abyssal jaws were seen, the Universe directly before it was instantly fractured as it turned into a mass of light that was directly absorbed into the mouth of this creature.

As if its mouth was a quasar or black hole capable of swallowing everything!

It was a devastating sight, but Noahs consciousness was buzzing as after seeing the utilization of daos to enact a stupendous skill, something fantastical was arranged in Noahs mind as the mere words for it floated before his eyes seconds later.

<Esurient True Devouring>.
