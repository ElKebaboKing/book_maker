Chapter 1824 A Damsel in Distress? l

In a different region of the Unexplored Grotto Sanctum.

The fantastical scene of a mesmerizing young woman adorned in a silver dress could be seen, one of her fair hands gripping tightly onto the cylindrical end of a weapon that only became larger and larger towards the opposite end that was the form of a crystalline silver mirror like hammer!

This being was adorned with a crown that had the words on it, grasping the massive hammer that was many times her size as it even seemed unnatural.

It caused the surrounding space to buzz as even though the cold seas were extremely stable with the space of this Grotto Sanctum being made extremely stable, it still crackled and buzzed as she swung this hammer effortlessly downwards- bringing with it terrifying swirls of Dimensional Essence!

The poor target of this strike was a badly malformed Abyssal Undead Anaconda who seemed to have already formed its Reality, its body covering nearly a fourth of the barren and cold landmass as much of its bones were currently broken or turned to dust.

BANG!

The hammer thundered on this Abyssal Undead Anaconda once more as its serpentine draconic head began to cave in, this Undead at the level of a LEGEND screaming out unfairness as it felt its very Dimensional Bloodline be oppressed by this young woman before it.

BANG!

As she swung the Mirror Hammer effortlessly with her gaze calm, the blue flames on the sockets of the Abyssal Undead Beast turned dim with unwillingness as its Origin broke apart!

'Why is a Dimensional Royalty like you here bullying little old me? Ah!'

A melancholic thought passed through its mind as it was the last thing, the ravaging Mirror Essence that had permeated the surroundings sinking into and shattering its origin into countless Mirror Shards at this moment.

"..."

The Undead Reality of the Anaconda shattered and faded as the culprit to do all this- the Adjudicator Katelyn, floated across the cold waters calmly as the Mirror hammer faded away.

She came to float above the pulverized Abyssal Undead Beast as she patted its shattered skull, her expression showing complex emotions as she sighed and moved forward to the dome shaped golden barrier at the center of this landmass that swirled with a treasured light of destiny.

Her fair finger landed on this tough barrier for a few seconds as she felt its indestructible defense, but she uniquely didn't start infusing the barrier with essence or attack it as instead- an illusory authority of a black Halo flashed while she tapped onto the golden dome once!

BZZZT!

In the next instant, a unique scene manifested as a thin silver line appeared above the golden dome, this line showing a reflection of the dome barrier as it shockingly recreated the whole nearby area to be reflecting onto itself!

Katelyn's eyes flashed with a terrifying silver light as her hands reached into the crystalline silver mirror like reflection of the golden dome, her eyes lighting up as she pulled out a cluster of glimmering Grotto Nacres from it.

Nacres...that were clustered into the ecriture of that when Katelyn saw, her gaze became even sharper.

CRACK!

The Mirror line she had drawn faded as the reflection of the golden dome faded- but the treasures within were already gone as a silver gateway appeared beside Katelyn where all the Grotto Nacres disappeared into!

It was a wondrous and unique power, and the existence to wield it was even more unique.

She gazed around the barren land around her thoughtfully as the words flashed over her eyes that soon turned up to see the Chains of Reality leading to unknown areas forward, about to continue her ascension when all of a sudden...

"Oh?"

She couldn't help but speak out as she felt terrifying malice and Essence rising from behind her- from the Chain of Reality that should be leading all the way down to the first region of the Unexplored Grotto Sanctum!

SHAA!

With valor and power, two terrifying auras rose up from the Chain of Reality as the oppressive might of two LEGENDS appeared.

They flashed with utter wildness and malice as at the very forefront, the figure of Xian could be seen as his whole surroundings were bathed by storms of Aether.

Beside him was a brown skinned young man with an air of poisonous allure, two sickles with a deathly green light buzzing in his hands as his eyes released a green hue!

"Haha!"

Xian let out a wild laugh as his gaze landed on Katelyn, the concentration of Aether around him getting even denser as he walked forward fearlessly.

"You haven't forgotten me yet, right? I couldn't freely make moves back then with Brightborn there, but you...you're really a fruit that I truly cannot pass up."

BZZZT!

His words were powerful as 30 dazzling Ascendancy Halos erupted and were ready to merge into him, the other monstrous genius from the Olden Times beside him doing the same as he released a horrific verdant light when he spoke.

"You really weren't lying Xian! Why don't I take first dibs with her? I'm sure you can feel how dangerous this will be."

...!

Yes!

These two existences appeared here with their own aims, and they affirmed their target as well as the fact that their destinies told them it would be an extremely difficult task to achieve their goal!

"I might have had to run away with nothing if I came alone." Xian nodded his head as his Will tried to discern destiny and Karma.

He had lived in the Olden Era as he knew how important understanding destiny was, and he wouldn't normally take risks that showed the level of danger that he was feeling right now when facing this calm young woman before him.

"But if she was easy to take down, then she wouldn't be a Dimensional Royalty. Let's not tarry too long...make your move, Dion!"

WAA!

His lips curved in a devilish smile as he spoke, his figure shooting to the left while Dion shot to the right, circling Adjudicator Katelyn from different directions as they prepared for a tough battle!
