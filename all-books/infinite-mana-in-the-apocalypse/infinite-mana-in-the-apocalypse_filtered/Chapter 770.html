Chapter 768 - GALAXY! I

Across the Dark Universe, a great deal of realms and Galaxies propagated as one could come across truly wondrous and dangerous locations that could take your breath away.

Secret Realms were among the top most wondrous places, as more often than not a great personage used to reside in such a realm or they even built it themselves!

The Secret Realm known as the Primal Holy Land was one such a Realm- a legacy left behind by the Great Sage Aoin as within it dwelled fantastical Animus of all types, its mystical environments not being any lesser to any other unique environments that one could stumble upon in the dark universe.

At this moment, multiple beings had entered this Realm as each of them had their own aims. The party of 20 Holy Descendants from the Expansive Dragon Cl.u.s.ter wished to train themselves as they battled terrifying Wild Animus and search for the legacy of the Great Sage, while the party from the Primal Winged Expanse had their own designs!

At this moment, the multi winged Aileron Royals were flying through the misty multicolored skies as in the very forefront, Prince Kirigan was leading with a sharp expression as within his hand, one could see a glimmering dark red page.

"Prince, still no reaction from the Page of Ruination?"

A voice from an Aileron Royal sounded out as the group moved forward, Prince Kirigan shaking his head as he replied.

"If the Ruination Core was freely located within this Secret Realm, this page should have sensed it. It seems that the Core is either on the Holy Emperor himself, or he hid it in a very special realm somewhere in the Expansive Dragon Cl.u.s.ter!"

Words tinged with coldness and frustration rang out as the others nodded, another Royal looking off in the distance as he chimed in.

"Then why dont we have some fun first? I want to see the face of that disrespectful human genius despair, and you could play with your soon to be wife a year early, Prince."

The Royal was full of malicious intention as he said this, the multi winged beings looking at each other as Prince Kirigan put away the page from the Book of Ruination and commanded out.

"Track them. Lets see how that fly can withstand even a single strike from me..."

RUMBLE!

---

As some beings made their way with malicious intent, Noah and Anna were moving through the Secret Realm of the Great Sage Aoin with way too much ease.

This was because in the outer perimeters, mostly QUASAR and Low Tier GALAXY Wild Animus could be found! When it came to such levels of beings and Noahs own Summons...well the fights were simply brutal, to say the least.

The figures of Noah and Anna remained atop the Colossal Helios Leviathan as it floated very close to the verdant green earth of the secret realm, its wide jaws occasionally opening as horrendous purple-blue beams of light erupted out.

OOOONG!

The purple-blue plasma beam was from an ability known as [Heliotic Energy Beam], a unique skill the Colossal Helios Leviathan could utilize as it could be shot out rapidly and at high speeds. The only issue was that it requires immense mana to utilize, and this became a nonissue as the trainer of this animus had infinite mana to reinforce it.

BOOM!

The plasma beams utterly eviscerated any QUASAR ranked Wild Animus as they injured those at the Low Tier GALAXY Realm, and this was where the other two summoned PRISMATIC Pets came into play.

First was the Mercurial Obsidian Panther that acted like an assassin in the dark as the Wild Animus who survived the plasma beams of the Colossal Helios Leviathan and prepared to retaliate were met with glistening dark claws that came from nowhere, their eyes only seeing a black flash swirl around them as their limbs separated from their bodies!

SHAA!

The Panther would then arrogantly look over their decimated bodies before it disappeared in a blink! This was an animus summon that already embarked on the Lesser Dao of Alacrity and had speed as its biggest tool, and such speed was utilized fully as a black flash of death was the only thing this summon represented on the battlefield.

Next up was the future Jormungandr, the Void Serpent that was still in the QUASAR Realm but could match other Wild Animus in the Low Tier Galaxy Realm because of its powerful Bloodline! Its serpentine body slithered below in the lush greenery as it erupted with the ability of [Annihilatory Death Devouring] on any injured Wild Animus as their attention would be on defending against the horrendous plasma beams from the Colossal Helios Leviathan above them.

It was truly a horrendous team that only wreaked havoc and death as they mowed down any Wild Summons they came across!

Noah had no worries about wiping away these creatures in a Disordered State, as he could not wipe away this status effect at this moment and if he did not retaliate against them, they would just reap his life and make him waste a perfectly good Save Point or Plot Armor.

So he sat above the Colossal Helios Leviathan as his job...was to collect and absorb all the loot that dropped from the kills of his pets!

Anna was sitting beside him absorbing cores similarly as she shook her head with disbelief while she glanced at Noahs pets while comparing them with her very own Four Winged Unicorn. She could only sigh as she continued to elevate her strength, sensing that she would very soon step into QUASAR as she was absorbing cores at this level and even those at the Low Tier GALAXY Realm.

Meanwhile, Noah was fully taking in GALAXY Core after GALAXY Core without stopping as he sensed the unique feeling of Grand Completion, knowing that the moment this was achieved he could break through while maintaining such a state in the Low Tier GALAXY Realm!

He first finished the cores he had left over from the Elder Fiends of the Burning Domains Galaxy that his Harbingers would soon be besieging themselves, and he then ate up the cores of any defeated Wild Animus that fell under the teamwork of his three PRISMATIC Pets.

He hadnt brought out the Variant Oculothorax, Calamity Bear, or the future Golden Crow as they were still in the QUASAR Realm, and he wouldnt reveal them yet until he stepped into the Low Tier GALAXY Realm and he brought them up as well.

As for entering such a realm...well, it would only take minutes as he continued to absorb the glimmering GALAXY Cores while his pets ravaged the beautiful Secret Realm below him!
