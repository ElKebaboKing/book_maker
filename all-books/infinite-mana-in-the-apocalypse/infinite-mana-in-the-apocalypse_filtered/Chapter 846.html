Chapter 844 - Picking Up a Grand Dao is Easy I

In the mystical realm filled with lush greenery, the Sword Emperor was still situated among the verdant grass as he peacefully breathed in and out.

A few meters away from him, an illusory screen could be seen that showed the scene of Noah looking towards one of his Sword Kings, the body of the Emperorslayer beginning to bubble with power as things were about to begin!

Many thoughts crossed the Sword Emperors mind as he looked at this scene, his essence constantly making sure the Immemorial Sword Formation that was set up was working perfectly.

He had no regrets from his actions, the possibility of the identity of this Master of Ruination simply being too important as he had to act! His senses told him that the Cataclysm was truly near, and if they wanted to stand against the suicidal Spirit Race...they had to move.

THRUM!

Essence was now erupting freely on the First Floor of the Immemorial Sword Dungeon as his starry eyes locked in to pay attention.

In another location, Traverser was left behind as the voice of the Sword Emperor had reached him to explain the ongoing situation, an illusory screen also present on the outdoor dining area they were in that showed the scene of Noah and the Sword King.

All the other floors of the Immemorial Sword Dungeon also had similar illusory screens as Noahs battles would be analyzed by the very same beings he would face, his mission becoming increasingly harder as he climbed up.

The pieces were set.

The board was made.

Now, it was time to play.

On the first floor of the Immemorial Sword Dungeon, Noah directly activated [Automatic Cast of All Support Skills] as his hair danced wildly, his body being wrapped by the multicolored essence of all Universal Laws as glorious wings erupted behind him while he was adorned in a magisterial armament.

Vibrant golden hammers swirled on his back as two sharp swords appeared in his hands, the Sword King in the distance glancing at the Swords with a light smile as he wielded his own.

"You really do dare raise a Sword against someone that has wielded it their entire life."

His voice was soft and placid, Noah sensing many emotions in it as the moment the being finished talking, he began walking towards him.

THRUM!

Each step he took, his aura became even wilder as the Sword he held hummed, Noah feeling the power of the Grand Dao of the Sword as he moved himself.

He was truly curious what exactly this Grand Dao has that created fanatics.

"The Will of the Sword Emperor must be followed...and he told us to go all out as if death in here was truly final!"

BOOOM!

This object was naturally the Sword of the Arch Human Entity, Noahs colorful eyes watching things as if in slow motion as he saw the swirling coagulation of the essence of the Sword Dao on the descending weapon.

This essence was truly unique as it was filled with immense power, this single strike making it seem like it would bring down the weight of an entire star when it met its target!

The Dao of Withering had essence with a destructive nature that deconstructed and eroded the enemy, the Dao of Vastness has expanding essence that granted the user unique properties...every single dao had its own unique essence.

The essence of the Dao of the Sword...gave its Entities extremely sharp and destructive attacks that carried the force equivalent to or even greater than the weight of stars or the even heavier stellar bodies, causing for the Sword Kings to be capable of shattering planets with a single swing of their sword!

RUMBLE!

Such was the strike that was coming towards Noah, his arms rising up to bring his two Sword that palpated with the essence of Aether and Death to stop the advancement of the Sword Kings strike that was smashing towards his face.

....BOOOM!

The impact was horrendous, the sheer weight the strike carried smashing into Noahs Swords as it passed through them and descended onto his body, smashing him backwards as his body was thrown a few miles back!

WAA!

So this was the power of the Sword Dao!

"Good!"

THRUM!

His body exploded back towards the Sword King miles away, the pressure he released becoming even wilder as the Ruination Cores Affixes were activated.

[+10,000 to All Damage] and [+10,000 to Attack and Cast Speed] were activated as his figure turned into a streak of light, the swords he wielded moving at speeds that the watching Entities who were lax on the other floor failed to catch!

...!

They rose up from their nonchalant gazes as they focused on the illusory screen, only then being able to see the path of Noah that had truly become too fast out of nowhere!

BOOM!

It was Noahs turn to strike back as the Sword King he faced barely raised his sword in time, the clash this time causing space to shatter as neither side was moved back!

Horrendous sounds were rusting out as the eyes of the Sword King focused feeling the force that Noah released now to be on par with the enormous strength that his Dao provided him.

"...!"

It was a chilling truth that only came by due to [+10,000 to All Damage] and [+10,000 to Attack and Cast Speed], the effects of the Ruination Core Affixes being the same if not more than what boosts the fully comprehended Daos provided to Entities!

"Haha!"

It was a shocking reality as Noah moved to strike again, enjoying the feeling of his body moved at shocking speeds and releasing tremendous power as he had yet to even call forth his summons.

As for the affixes, he would be replacing the Attack and Cast Speed affix the moment his foes were about to be defeated and replace it with the [Slain enemies have a 10% chance to drop Dao Crystals based on the Dao they cultivate] Affix in order to collect the Dao Crystals of this resplendent Sword Dao himself!

His strikes gaining the force equivalent to the weight of stars? He wanted it!

RUMBLE

The clash against the two Entities continued wildly as the eyes of the Sword King turned bright, his mouth moving in a barely discernible whisper.

...

The skies turned quiet as an ability was cast.

Hundreds of Sword began materializing above Noah, each of them carrying the weight of stellar bodies as they tore through space and thundered down.

"Ah..."

Towards such a shocking ability, Noah had a bright smile as around his body, innumerable miniaturized stars, suns, black holes, and quasars began to form.

The abilities of the Void Terror and the Terror of Aether- Cthulhu skill trees that were brought up had many abilities that conjured up stellar bodies as the method of attacking.

They eventually reached the stage of being able to throw out even black holes and Quasars as attacks, the only thing Noah could not do at this moment being the fact that he couldnt conjure up a miniaturized Galaxy to throw at someone...but he had a real Galaxy to do that with if he wished!
