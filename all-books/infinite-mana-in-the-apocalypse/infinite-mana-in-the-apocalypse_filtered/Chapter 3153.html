Chapter 3151 The Atlas of Existence! VI

Three Entities with different Sources of Existence came together for nothing else but a single purpose!

Himself.

They wanted what Autarchs could obtain a glimpse of before, but something they could no longer see right now!

His Base of Existence.

Since it took the center of the Atlas of Existence, everything about it became veiled as even the Sanctus Empress was no longer able to peer through it unless they were fused.

The Faesinged Queen had taken the path of seeking aid with the other Omniversal Ankyras of this Eonic Omniverse Record, and now they all sought Noah's base that was the size of a grain of sand in a small corner of the Var-El Eonic Omniverse Record.

His Invictus Dominium buzzing and showing him the scene of these Autarchs meant that they would soon find him- and he had to prepare!

"Hmmm..."

His eyes were filled with many thoughts as his Distinctions as the Weaver had risen, and his eyes saw things very differently from others.

What he saw was a breakdown of data and weavings coming together, and he could at this moment see the weavings of the very Source of Existence of these beings!

How different were their weavings compared to each other and him!

Their Systems of Existence...Noah felt like he could almost peer at them fully.

"The Faesinged Queen...your System isn't sufficiently different to deserve its own spot in the Atlas of Existence."

His gaze was a pondering one as he stroked his chin while gazing at the trio.

His face showed immeasurable calmness as if everything was under his grasp, and as the one with the Atlas of Existence...he was truly given this astounding sense of confidence!

Not arrogance.

Not pride.

But a sensation as if he could have everything under control for the vast variety of Existence if he really put his mind to it.

This was the effect of merely having the Atlas of Existence, as its shocking potential and sheer existence alone was still something that even Noah felt like he didn't fully grasp!

"And you..."

His gaze turned towards the massive figure of the Sunwinged Leprachanus.

"Overflowing with the light of Loot, the System of Existence you cultivate is also not sufficiently different."

...!

Everything he was seeing so far indicated either low complexity or simply not too different from the Conceptual System of Existence!

The only caveat of the System of Existence of these beings was how focused and singular they were in themselves.

The Faesinged Queen and her focus on the mind.

The Sunwinged Leprachanus and his focus on wealth as the Source of power.

And lastly, the Satyrus Nymph who focused on Vitality and Life.

"None of you are sufficiently different enough..."

Noah shook his head with gradually blazing eyes as he rose from his seated position.

He remembered the visions that the glimpse of Invictus Maximus had given.

The Nine portals of light that seemed to be reflections of the Atlas of Existence, but each of them were leading to different Eonic Omniverse Records!

He was only within the Var-El Eonic Omniverse Record that he reached with the aid of Lumen- which he had now incorporated into the First Mundi of Power within his Atlas of Existence as he better understood how it and the weavings of the Protagonist allowed him to enter this Eonic Omniverse Record.

But this was merely just that.

A single Eonic Omniverse Record.

"I will not allow it to take too much of my attention..."

His eyes buzzed with a profound sense of confidence as his Ascendant Existential Extremity Clone of Quintessence rose.

Quintessence.

Days had passed since he began and continued sparring with his people, and it could still not be considered to have arrived at the point where it could freely generate and utilize Autarchical Authority.

One of the reasons why even though Quintessence was at the forefront in Autarchy but had yet to achieve it...were the Invictus Dominium Wings.

Noah was urging them towards what he had only caught a glimpse of before- Invictus Maximus!

His Wings were focused on the breakdown of Aitia and the Autarchical Omniversal Authority it led to, its Four Components being focused on as apart from Efficiency, Noah had been grasping and utilizing the other Three Components as well.

This, and his Atlas of Existence...gave him an utterly profound sense of confidence.

So much so that at this moment...

"Let me have a taste of your Sources of Existence."

HUUM!

He only spoke while gazing at the illusory screen showing the three Omniversal Ankyras of this Eonic Omniverse Record as in the next instant, his figure turned into a streak of light that disappeared into this illusory screen!

...!

He didn't tell any of his people.

He didn't even alert the Sanctus Autarch who was refining her own Authority and getting ready for a major battle.

He only disappeared by himself as when Noah was given plenty of time...utterly disastrous things could occur.

In the area where the last battle had occurred between Noah's forces and the Faeburned Existences.

|This guy is playing hide and seek with us? The Var-El Eonic Omniverse Record is not that big. We could cover every single corner in under a day. I have to say, I'm a bit let down...|

The voice of the Sunwinged Leprachanus released a vibrant echo in the surroundings, his pressure alone causing the Legions of the Faesinged Queen and Satyrus Nymph to feel suffocated!

Near him, the Faesinged Queen seemed to be pondering while the Satyrus Nymph with an anatomy that seemed to be a mixture of a goat and plants closed their eyes, the vibrant luster of life buzzing from them.

Their eyes were filled with murky green galaxies as their intent weaved out at this moment.

|If that is what we must do-|

"I must apologize for letting you down."

...!

The intent of the Satyrus Nymph never finished.

An audible voice echoed out in front of them that caused its intent to cease, its figure erupting with Havenly authority as it looked ahead!

HO!

The Sunwinged Leprachanus and the Faesinged Queen were the same as the Legions behind them also went on high alert!

They had to.

A devilishly handsome man in a colorless robe that had a white tinge to it, a Halo of brilliant light above him, and ecstatic colorless wings on his back and feet!

His gaze was immeasurably calm as he stared at three Autarch Equivalent Existences- And this wasn't to mention the Autarch Equivalent Faeburned leading the Legions of the Faesinged Queen, and another Autarch Equivalent Nymph under the banner of the Satyrus Nymph.

And such a thing was extremely terrifying!
