Chapter 3389 Runes of Existence! III

The most recent words to be uttered out from the Omnifather didn't cause a whole lot of tumultuous waves on the vast majority of Antediluvian Leviathans.

But within the weavings of Noah, his eyes pulsed with a terrifying light.

The Rune of Existence carried the signature of the one who designed it- to the extent that their forger of a Rune could know just who was utilizing something they designed!

Noah looked within the depths of his weavings where the Amarantos Rune of the Great Beyonder and the Quintessential Sacred Complexity Rune of Existence of the Omnifather lay.

Both...were not his.

The moment that he began using the Transcendent Megalos Ability of the Amarantos Dream of the Megalos, did his name get added to a long list that the Beyonder could easily see as the one who designed this Rune?

The thought didn't entirely sit right with him as he already began to think about how he could change this in the future!

|I tell you this because from now on, I have shown you enough variations of Runes of Existence that you can design your own unique Runes. There is a region in the Expanse that holds dense variations of scattered weavings of Runes for you all to gain more ideas from, and that will be the next location of study. Come.|

...!

The words of the Omnifather echoed out as many Antediluvian Leviathans rose with expectant eyes to follow the Omnifather!

They trusted him with their very weavings of existence as they would follow and do anything he told them.

Boros rose up as well, but at this moment...

|Not you.|

HUUM!

Silently.

Other Antediluvian Leviathans didn't even sense anything!

But the voice of the Omnifather boomed within Noah's ears as he found his body anchored in space, unable to move!

As others began to be teleported, his figure silently disappeared as well.

A flash of an instant.

Thereafter, he found himself standing on a starry blue sea of plasma, dazzling Omniverses spinning in the far distance as their sheer size and stature made one feel unfathomably small.

Above this starry blue sea of plasma, the figure of Boros appeared as behind him, the visage of the Omnifather was adorned in a multicolored robe- his arms folding behind him as he stared out at the spinning Omniverses!

|Traversing into different weavings of existence is dangerous. Unnatural. For you to appear in the weavings of Antediluvian Leviathans must mean you are connected in some way within your own weavings, thus, I allowed you to move freely and learn in the last decades. But were to keep following any further, it would become dangerous for you.|

...!

The Omnifather spoke to Boros.

No, not Boros, but the will residing within him!

Noah turned his head to stare at the terrifying entity that was the Omnifather as his will was calm, his mouth opening calmly.

"When did you find out?"

|The moment I first laid eyes on you. Anyone above Existence Severing may be able to notice some things if they are focused on you, with more complex beings sensing even if their attention didn't just happen to fall on you. I tell you this while keeping hope that you will heed my warning. One's own Frequency of existence is more than sufficient. You can spend your entire lifespan and not be able to scour every single bound of Existence in the Weavings you were born in. Delving into others could aid in your complexity in the short term, but you will start causing changes and fluctuations not meant for those weavings of existence. And if you begin affecting a big enough change...you will fall under the eyes of those beings soon enough. Beings who can more clearly see the Wheel of Existence more than anything else as they ensure their steady flow!|

...!

Unfathomably complex words echoed out from the Omnifather.

Words that caused Noah to turn silent as he mulled over them again and again!

The biggest thing being falling under the eyes of Entities that ensured the steady flow of Weavings of Existence.

He had just heard of this, and yet he had a great deal of questions!

Who? Why?

He had many questions, but he knew that he would very likely not receive any answers here as he looked at the Omnifather and asked about something else entirely.

|What do you know of the Megalonians?|

HUUM!

The Megalonians.

Were they destroyed in these weavings of existence as well? The moment he asked this question, the gaze of the Omnifather flickered with a tremendous amount of emotion as his gaze changed, and he waved his hands with a booming voice!

|Leave!|

...!

The Amarantos Rune of Existence within him blared with light as he instantly felt unfathomably sleepy.

Gazing at the visage of the Omnifather and the brilliance around him, Boros closed his eyes and fell into a deep slumber- a unique complexity leaving him as an Amarantos Rune of Existence faded away!

Yet even after this occurred, the Omnifather continued to stare silently as the figure of Boros woke up soon after.

|Huh? Omnifather...!|

Seeing the visage of the Omnifather, Boros instantly rose to his feet with reverence as the Omnifather merely nodded and waved his hands, Boros disappearing from his eyes right after.

|...|

His gaze was a unique one as he seemed to be faced with a great deal of difficulty and confusion.

At a certain point...

|Why did he not appear in the other weavings of himself? Why possess another if traversing the weavings of existence?|

...!

A query that the Omnifather had not been able to answer.

The Wheel of Existence was unfathomable, but more often than not, if there was a being capable of traveling across its myriad Spokes of Existence, they were likely to be of renowned stature and complexity across the Spokes of the Wheel.

So why not do the easy thing and traverse into another version of oneself, and instead go for the much harder possession?

Unless...a version of himself did not exist in these weavings.

But this...was highly unlikely, right?

Not receiving any answers, the Omnifather's gaze flickered with many emotions before he also disappeared!
