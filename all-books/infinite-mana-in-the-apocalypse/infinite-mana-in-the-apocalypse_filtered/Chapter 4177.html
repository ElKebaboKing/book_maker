Chapter 4175: You called them! II

Golden arcs of loot light erupted across Noahs body, wreathing him in radiance that suggested wealth about to be forcibly extracted from reality!

His eyes pulsed with glory that made several nearby beings step back instinctively.

He rose from his throne with movement that was both gradual and instantaneous, existing in the space between rest and violence.

His heart...the Heart of an Early Creature that beat within his chest, began pounding like a war drum announcing the beginning of harvest!

The sound resonated through dimensions, each beat a promise of violence transformed into opportunity. He opened his mouth, and what emerged wasnt quite a word but pure expression of desire!

"COME!"

HUUM!

The howl boomed across the Transcendent Origin Folds like a glorious commandment.

Every Duke in the vicinity turned toward him with expressions of incredulous horror. What did he mean, come?!

Did this madman actually want whatever lurked behind those tears to emerge?

This fucker!

As if responding to his invitation, change erupted from the largest tear directly above Concordia.

A massive mummified head emerged first, wearing a golden crown that had tarnished with age that predated the proper invention of time.

The skull was wrapped in bandages that might have once been white but had yellowed and blackened with accumulated death. Eye sockets that should have been empty blazed with unholy light that made existence itself want to look away!

Mummified hands followed, each finger the size of a buildings foundation, moving with speed that large things shouldnt possess.

They grasped the edges of the tear and pulled, ripping reality wider with the casual strength of something that had been waiting too long to be gentle!

The full creature that emerged made several Dukes actually pale...a physical response that beings of their level shouldnt have been capable of experiencing.

Noahs enhanced perception immediately began calculating, measuring the waves of power that radiated from this first arrival.

Five hundred trillion. At minimum!

Fuck!

The first Dead Existence to fully emerge already eclipsed many of the assembled Dukes in raw power.

Its presence pressed against reality with weight that made lesser beings simply cease, not from attack but from proximity to something that shouldnt exist.

Across the Transcendent Origin Folds, the tears continued disgorging their contents.

Eidolons emerged...beings of pure death-force that had forgotten they once had bodies.

Wights crawled through, their forms constantly shifting between what they had been and what death had made them.

Each tear revealed new horrors, new impossibilities, new opportunities for those brave or foolish enough to seize them.

Then, from one particular tear that seemed deeper than the others, something different emerged.

A winged head poked through first...not attached to a body but floating independently, supported by wings that had too many joints and moved in directions that space didnt normally permit.

Tentacles surrounded it like a living crown, each one tipped with eyes that saw in spectrums existence hadnt named.

Noahs gloriously burning figure was forced to focus on this particular emergence because from it, he felt something that made his blood sing with recognition and challenge!

The aura of an Early Creature!

Among The Dead, a Winged Head of an Early Creature had actually spilled across The Veil!

Not a Duke, not a mere powerful existence, but something that had walked in the Earliest Folds before walking became a concept that needed definition.

Oh!

The exclamation escaped him as pure expression of delighted recognition.

This wasnt catastrophe!

This was opportunity wearing catastrophes mask!

This was Existence delivering exactly what he needed, wrapped in packaging that would terrify anyone sensible enough to refuse the gift.

The Dead had come in force, and among them, something worth devouring.

The overflowing aura of The Dead pressed against reality with weight that made existence reconsider its structural integrity!

Noah gazed at the unfolding catastrophe with eyes that shone with both gravity and possibility...the particular gleam of someone doing math that would horrify accountants.

Twenty trillion in complexity and purity. It was nice, certainly.

The kind of power that could reshape Folds, that made him a Duke among weaker Dukes.

But when faced with Dead Existences measuring in the hundreds of trillions, when an Early Creatures winged head was emerging with power approaching a single quadrillion markers... it wasnt entirely up to the task.

But he wasnt alone. He had another body...Ozymandias, whose purity had been refined to impossible heights! And he had something else, something that most here couldnt imagine.

A Legion of The Dead himself.

The masses of Aeternitas Concordia had gone pale...an achievement for beings whose complexions ranged from crystalline to void-touched.

Many turned toward Noah with expressions of expectant anticipation in their fear.

He had bellowed "COME!" with such magnificent authority.

Surely now he would charge forward, leading the defense against these monstrosities?

Noah met their gazes without changing expression.

Then, with the casual certainty of someone who had just remembered hed left something behind, he floated back down beside Sigrid.

...!

He settled into a comfortable hovering position with his arms crossed, wearing the particular expression of someone waiting for a show to begin!

"..."

The silence that followed was deafening.

"What in the collapsed Folds are you doing?!" a Duke of Living Origins actually shouted, dignity forgotten in confusion.

"You called them here!" accused another, pointing at the emerging horrors. "You literally invited them!"

Noah continued watching with polite interest, as if the accusations were happening in a language he didnt speak.

Sigrid beside him had to suppress her expression as she was also somber...she knew that expression, had seen it before when he was about to do something that would make everyone reassess their understanding of possibility.

What they didnt know, couldnt know, was that Noah wasnt retreating. He was simply allowing another part of himself to shine.

In the next moment, as terrifying Dead Existences began fully emerging from tears that shouldnt exist...

"Ka! Ka! Ka!"

The cackling cut through the chaos like a scythe through wheat.

From a blooming portal of obsidian light and bones that materialized with theatrical precision in the same above Concordia, Arch Lich RaZan made his entrance!

His skeletal form was magnificent in its terrible glory...bones of obsidian-gold that caught light in ways that suggested they existed in more dimensions than were strictly necessary.

His skull blazed with green-gold flames that danced with genuine amusement. Tattered robes that seemed woven from the final breaths of dying Folds billowed around him despite the absence of wind!

He stood atop the emerging Primordial Stone Ape, whose undead form had grown even more terrifying since its transformation.

And leading them both, moving with the inexorable certainty of hunger given purpose...

Ozymandias!

The Purest Hunger had arrived, and reality itself held its breath!
