Chapter 3421 Giving a Monster Wings! I

Hell Terra Firma.

The Purgatory Lands!

An unknown number of entities lay within as with the rejection of Mana and any Natal authority, all life forms were the same.

They lived and died.

But there were lifeforms who had long lives even without the influence of Mana.

Turtles who could live for a few hundred years.

Tree lifeforms that could live for a few thousand years.

Dragons, Phoenixes, and other unique variations of lifeforms also had lifespans exceeding one thousand years or more even without Mana!

"But I am just a lone little human being, you see. My life is extremely short as I have to make the most of it."

Noah's voice echoed out as he looked at five humanoid entities suspected to have been Pioneers in this Settlement!

This was the task that Ruination had been given charge of carrying out the moment that the first test succeeded.

She commandingly asked for any information regarding Pioneers in this small Settlement, and in this population of a few thousand- 5 possible ones were identified and found.

From rumors. From something they had said before. From their disposition and gazes.

After knowing the hardships of Hell Terra Firma, very few Pioneers made their past position of power public knowledge as those who were most powerful...were those targeted the most in these lands.

A human woman that used to be an Ordinance Amalgamation entity? Not worth much. A human woman who used to be a Pioneer? Some Warlords of Settlements made it a goal to find them as they felt like they were collecting entities of extremely noble lineage and esteem!

Even male entities at the level of power of Pioneers were prized as in the Purgatory Lands, past complexity still played a role in status.

Noah looked at the five humanoid entities before him and asked thoughtfully while behind him, Ruination gazed on imposingly.

"I have no ill will towards you all, nor are your lives in danger. You will even be compensated for your services. So...would any of you step forward if you were a Pioneer before you arrived here?"

...!

He asked calmly as he chewed on a chicken drumstick with his right hand.

Hunger was very real here, and he was a voracious man.

He ate and gazed at the five beings before him- two holding animalistic features as one had the head of a lion, and another had white fur and a tail as she closely resembled a variant of a simian.

A single woman, and five men.

When asked, they looked at each other wryly as a man with fair skin and red hair spoke gruffly.

"Compensated how?"

"Food, water, whatever one needs for survival." Noah turned his head towards this being as after he asked such a question...

He stepped out of the lineup and spoke with a gaze of solemnity and power- as if to relive his past life!

"I reached the heights of a Pioneer before being swept up in this place. What do you need from me?"

...!

What could he need from a Pioneer?

Noah smiled and brought a few things forward.

"Just fill this little bowl with your blood."

He threw the jagged blade he obtained from an inconsequential extra as well as a small clay bowl, the man looking at this with disturbed eyes, but he made a cut on his palm and did what was asked of him!

Noah watched on and then passed the small bowl filled with crimson blow towards the Infiniverse Megalos Body who was waiting in the distance, and she took it while disappearing into the complex and massive Infiniverse Megalos Seed behind them!

He looked at the red haired man and nodded.

"This may be the beginning of a long relationship. I'll only ask for a little volume of blood from you each day, and you can live the life of a Warlord."

He spoke while also looking at the other four humanoid beings as if he were nudging them on!

None of them moved forward.

And after a few seconds, the visage of the Infiniverse climbed out of the Infiniverse Megalos Seed, her gaze frosty and filled with a cold light as she spoke out.

"Merely Existence Severing."

WAA!

There wasn't a burst of pressure or haki as there would normally be since all authority was rejected.

But the air in the surroundings felt a tad bit colder just from the calm smile fading away from Noah's face, with Ruination making a move behind him as she threw out a punch onto the chest of the red haired man and flung him back like a ragdoll!

"Do not lie to me, for it will not end well."

...!

The eyes of the other 4 vibrated at such a scene and unnatural strength displayed by Ruination.

They looked back to see the wheezing visage of the red haired man who had only claimed to be a Pioneer to try and get resources as in their situation, how could they lose anything?

With all authority here rejected, if one said they were a Pioneer, then they were a Pioneer. After all, there was no way to check.

And yet...this being before them seemed to be able to do just that!

"So..." His gaze, now icy, turned towards the remaining four humanoid entities.

"Any chance that you all are Pioneers?"

Immense silence reigned in.

Nobody else dared to step up until...

The white furred simian woman rose up with a cold gaze.

Her eyes were radiating a dim light of coldness as she spoke out somberly.

"These three are useless things, but you can have a bit of my blood every day if you want. All I want to know is how that woman behind you can access such power. Is she of another lineage? A Miniature Titan? Progeny of Dragons? Are all able to access a smidgen of authority unlike everyone else?"

...!

She rose and asked multiple questions.

Noah raised his brows at her as he affirmed.

"You are a Pioneer?"

White hair all over her body. Simian in nature!

This woman came forward and took another clay bowl that Noah put forward, taking out her own knife within her robes and making a cut on her palm as she slowly filled up a small bowl with blood!

"You can check for yourself."

Her gaze was firm.

Noah took the clay bowl and passed it to Infiniverse once more who disappeared into the Infiniverse Megalos Seed, his eyes expectant as this may just be the real deal!
