Chapter 2876 The Existential Symposium! II

They knew the script!

They could foresee how it would end!

They had seen time and time again how the Ones Above Extremity were simply that- above Extremity entirely as they laid down their heel against any who went against them. The Primogenitor of Steel gazed at the distant throne housing a powerful woman at the very peak of the Seventh Gradation of Extremity.

His reply was a languid one.

{We are in this meeting because for the first time in eons, it may not end in death and destruction. The most recent Honored One has gotten further than any of us in the past, and it is foolish to think that the rest of us will not be involved and embroiled in what is already occurring. So we are here to understand just what side we should take when that time comes...}

...!

His words caused a great commotion as the majestic entities seated across the Existential Arcadian High Hall felt their intents buzz!

Would there actually be something so significant?

From the Celestia Extremity Lineage of the Supernal Havens of Extremity, a figure of a Tier 5 Extremity similar in expression and stature akin to Emperor Shao silently listened.

On a distant throne, the Bard of Vitalis could be seen as his monkey-like figure shone with lustrous white fur, his hands holding a guitar that he strummed with a sense of loss and desolation.

In another direction, a group of Vacuous Entities led by a humanoid male in the Seventh Gradation of Extremity were quiet this time as this man...this Primogenitor of the Extremity Lineage known as the Crimson Pitohui- he began to speak.

{Your words mean an extended conflict with someone like us against the Ones Above Extremity. Your words give this most recent one a lot of credit. From what we know, he only just recently stepped into the Gradations of Extremity. Against those exceeding Extremity...do we truly need to set such high expectations that his actions against the Ones Above Extremity would devolve into a long extended conflict that would draw all of us in?}

{...}

This was a forum of discussion.

Naturally, beings could and should share their opinions and ideas!

But the more people that spoke, the more that the expression on the Primogenitor of Steel became cloudy as a sense of ire rose in his eyes.

Before any more rising clamor could continue, his body buzzed with tendrils of power that drew in all eyes as he spoke powerfully!

{The Ones Above Extremity are swift and decisive in everything they do as it is taken care of instantly. Up to this point, a foreign Existential Authority has appeared that they have not been able to erase. More of this authority is spreading as not too long ago, the words of a prophecy burned across existence- you all saw this!}

WAA!

The Prophecy!

The golden words that appeared and rang out across Vacuous and Vitalis before Undefined Existences erased everything about them!

At this moment, the Primogenitor of Steel glared at all the entities seated on their high thrones as there was still a trace of hesitation in his eyes this whole time, but at this moment..he let this hesitation go and moved forward.

He spoke the golden words he had seen above the Veil of Arcadia!

{Waters crossed with unimaginable speed.

Immeasurable extremes all brought under a single creed.

One by one, Legacies shall fall.

White and black will come under the heel of the call.

Only one Extreme will be left at the end of it all.

Unshackled lands will rise above Recorded Havens.

Annals will fall as an endless weaving awakens!}

...!

{White and black will come under the heel of the call- this is clearly Vitalis and Vacuous! With all this capable of being done right under the nose of the Ones Above Extremity, do you all really think this will be swept under the rug like always? Can you all not assume that truly, we are witnessing a moment at the precipice of a great change that may truly alter everything?!}

His tanned visage shone gloriously as flames burned within his eyes!

But the moment he finished speaking, multiple figures rose from their thrones as another robust voice rang out.

{You speak the same words that I saw the visage of the One Above Extremity appear to erase minutes ago. Such words are dangerous as just speaking them jeopardizes our lives...I will not be a part of this forum if you all are already seeking death!}

...!

Even though they were Extremity Clones, they knew they may be targeted by association as they were fearful of the Ones Above Extremity linking all of them together.

So they sought to make their exit quickly as they knew even in the veils of history, the eyes of the Ones Above Extremity were pervasive!

Steel saw the scene of a major cluster of Entities rising from their thrones the moment he repeated the golden words as anger rose within his heart.

{You all spend your lives traversing your extremes...do you not have any backbone-}

PA! PA! PA! PA!

His words came to a halt.

They had to.

From one of the thrones where a being remained seated, a cruel and sharp smile became etched on their face as they clapped resonantly, each clap causing trepidation in the hearts of those who heard it as their extremes began to weave with a sense of shock!

And this shock further progressed to a sense of fear when in the next moment, their souls buzzed as they understood an intent being weaved from the entity clapping.

|It's great to see that there are some Seeds who still hold common sense.|

...!

Those who had risen up from their thrones felt a sense of trepidation as those who had remained seated felt an even grander pressure!

The expressions on Steel and Curagulus changed rapidly as they felt a shocking authority settle into not just the Existential Arcadian High Hall, but all of the Veil of Arcadia at this moment!

And from the seated throne, the humanoid figure that had spoken began to have their visage twist and distort as akin to a caterpillar emerging into a butterfly, the shell broke apart as a massive and brilliantly milky white Entity appeared!

A single eye on the face and chest.

Wide spanning butterfly like wings with serpentine eyes at their center.

And a sense of suffocation and undeniability that all those in the surroundings could feel!

Such an Entity appeared amongst their ranks as his serpentine eyes came to lock onto the Primogenitor of Steel while his intent continued to weave.

|As far as Seeds go, I actually favored you, Steel. It is...such a pity.|

...!
