Chapter 1154 - Exploring The Vast Seas Of Ruination! I

The expansive and endless Ruination Sea that contained a myriad of hidden existences and Cosmos floating within!

Very few beings got a chance to explore such vastness, and such privilege was left against unique beings that comprehended the Dao Ruination or the Primordial Dao, and those with unique power that they could survive against the ravaging of Ruination in one piece for a long period of time.
