Chapter 1170 - Stellar Bridges Secure A Cosmos!

No Cosmos, no hidden watchers, nothing.

Just the vast Ruination Sea and the vast secrets that lay within it!

A single gigaparsec equated to 3 billion Light years...and this is the scope of numbers that we are currently working with.

It was enough to cause weak beings to shake as their souls broke from the sheer size of everything!

THRUM!
