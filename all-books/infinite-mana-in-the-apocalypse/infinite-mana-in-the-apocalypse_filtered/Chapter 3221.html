Chapter 3219 A Sign! I

A sign.

These were the ravings of a powerful Outerversal Morphon as she observed the casting of lines and instant luminosity thereafter that indicated an extremely rapid process of Alieia!

An extremely rapid process of wading one's weavings across the Megalos Beyond into other parts of Existence and somehow being able to locate and bind a Delegatus right away.

How else could this be explained?

In the vast knowledge of the Outerversal Morphon Tabatha, there was a shred of ancient words that many Morphons that had lived over the cycles knew.

It was so shocking to the Outerversal Morphon that she only gazed on in shock while repeating herself.

"The sign...when a newly born Morphon will bind Existence freely the moment they deemed it. The sign...!"

She recalled what it all meant as she seemed to be in a trance.

And the entity she was looking at, the one causing her to repeat her words like a broken record....he also seemed to be in a trance as more and more tentacles transformed into endless strings that were cast down.

Moments later, these strings shone with a glorious luster as the size of Outerversal Morphon Ozymandias only increased exponentially.

How could such a phenomenon be explained by the Outerversal Morphons?

Were they supposed to suddenly think that somehow, the Outerversal Morphon Ozymandias before them was actually a part of an existence who ruled over an entire young Omniverse, his Lineage spread out to quintillions upon quintillions of beings that he was connected to all across the Aletheian Havens of this Omniverse?

Were they supposed to make a jump and think that somehow, such a being had reverse engineered a pathway into the Megalos Beyond after watching the entry of Outerversal Morphon Ginnungagap, and this was what led to the current reality?

Such thoughts would be utterly outlandish to make any jump towards as of course, the first and only thought in their minds was something they knew for many cycles now.

Legends spread across the Outerversal Morphon.

As Outerversal Morphon Tabatha repeated herself again and again, the Young Light Morphon glowed brightly as she weaved her authority to interrupt while asking.

"Great Mother Tabatha, what does such a Sign mean?"

What did this sign mean?

The words woke Outerversal Morphon Tabatha out of her reverie as her wormlike figure buzzed while she echoed out.

"A sign that for better or worse, the Megalos Beyond is about to change. It has been said that when such a Sign presents itself, it means the instrument of the final destruction or the final expansion of the Megalos Beyond across all of Existence will have been born!"

Heavy words filled with oddities echoed out as the Young Light Morphon squinted her singularities while asking carefully.

"So...is he the instrument that will bring about destruction or expansion?"

Her words were straightforward.

But the visage of Outerversal Morphon Tabatha was not as straightforward as her many jagged teeth swirled with twists and turns.

"Many Morphon across the cycles disagreed and argued about this as it was never explicitly stated. It is unknown as the Origin of the Instrument was never clarified! But what is clear is this sign that is unfolding right before us."

Words that didn't make things any easier to understand echoed out as the Young Light Morphon watched the ongoing scene carefully.

She then came across a critical issue that she turned to Outerversal Morphon Tabatha and asked innocently.

"Great Mother Tabatha, who spoke of these signs initially? Why were they not asked to clarify?"

...!

A simple question.

But it was one that caused the worm like visage of Tabatha to vibrate as she uttered out as if she were in a trance.

"That ancient being...has disappeared since then. They-"

"Hush now."

HUUM!

The booming and thick voice of the Observer of Old and Young echoed out as Outerversal Morphon Tabatha became quiet.

The tendrils of his millions of tentacles had inadvertently covered everything around them- and they only came to a realization now as they realized everything around them was covered by the wriggling tentacles of the Outerversal Morphon Phulax as if they were endlessly twisting snakes.

"You have spoken out a great deal and likely alerted others. Alas, that is how it was always meant to be."

His deep voice held power and misery, weakness and contentment, and even more in between as it was truly complex.

But it was solid and without any doubts as it continued.

"Let the child finish what he is doing. We will see then."

...!

The child.

The Outerversal Morphon Ozymandias who continued to cast out strings across the Megalos Beyond at this moment- all these strings crossing thin gaps left open by the Observer of Old and Young as they stretched out across Existence!

---

In another area across the vastness of the Megalos Beyond.

An Entity of approximate size to the Observer of Old and Young had millions of lines cast down to the Megalos Beyond, her gaze filled with ancient brilliance as she had three crimson eyes shaped like nine pointed stars.

These eyes were attached to a massive visage that had the upper half of a humanoid entity, and a lower half that seemed to be a long trunk extending into countless roots across the Megalos Beyond!

These roots pulsed at this moment as some of them captured in distinct weavings that could only be picked up by those with unique dispositions and power across the Megalos Beyond.

|The Sign!|

...!

|Bind Existence the moment they seek it! The sign!|

Words that should have in no way reached such a vast distance did so.

The weavings of terrifying authorities that were always keeping a look out for anything to do with certain subjects picked it up.

It was so terrifying that it could be something occurring an infinitely far distance within the Megalos Beyond, and yet if certain authorities were set up to pick something relating to a specific issue...so long as something related to it arose, those who set up these authorities would know!

At this moment, heavy words spread that caused the countless roots of this massive Outerversal Morphon to flutter.

Her three eyes burned with brilliance and began to spin.

It wasn't entirely clear what she was about to do, but what thing was abundantly clear.

Certain words were uttered.

And these certain words had begun to, ever so slowly, permeate across the Megalos Beyond!
