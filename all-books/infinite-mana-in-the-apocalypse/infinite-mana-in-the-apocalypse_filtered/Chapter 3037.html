Chapter 3035 Causality! II

Incredulity.

This was what Al-Abalem felt as he floated in the midst of Imperium Legions while looking at the distant figure of Noah!

Was...was that really the Seed that caused havoc within his past Vitalis Domain?

Someone who should have only been in the Gradations of Extremity?

So why was it now that in mere days, his body shone with the distinct purple grandeur of a Teleios Stage Existential Dominium? How...

Al-Abalem wanted to understand. He wanted to know the root cause of how things ended so spectacularly for him!

He wanted to, but at this current stage...

|Move!|

He was only considered a small piece of the puzzle.

The only players deserving to even speak were two terrifying old men at the very forefront who vibrated with waves of Omniversal Authorities while glaring towards the distant visage of Noah, and by the look of green gold hair...a Sanctus Princess with him in tow.

They stared towards the encirclement with sharp eyes as in the hands of the Sanctus Princess, a freezing icy sword appeared freely.

BZZT!

Tendrils of opposing light seemed to clash between their line of sight.

The eyes of the Sanctus Princess and Noah Osmont were locked onto the aged eyes of Martial King Reperiel and Archon Basileus Alderon!

Two young looking entities with unfathomable beauty, against two old men whose golden locks of hair ran over their shoulders like ancient hermits.

...!

The Legions that had moved to the surroundings were all for this purpose.

Alderon didn't say, but all Knight Commanders were holding their Codexes of Extremity and connected to the Imperium Knights within their Legions to also allow the flow of an Omniversal Authority to further reinforce the Imperium Blockade!

And for it to even be broken, all Knight Commanders would have to somehow be killed before it could be opened.

And even then, there were the two of them to deal with.

WAA!

Archon Basileus Alderon began succinctly as beside him, Martial King Reperiel smacked his lips while shaking his head, his intent bright as his body began to buzz with brilliance.

|You get more talkative in your old age, Alderon. Is it because you're seeing the pretty face beside him? Brats like these need to be killed first, and then have the questions asked later. Their records will tell the story.|

...!

Martial King Reperiel was much more straightforward.

He already began to gather the influence of Omniversal Authorities as beside him, Archon Basileus Alderon languidly began to float towards Noah and Sona as his eyes showed countless veiled weavings!

...!

As she spoke, her body began to pulse as she raised the icy sword in her hands, golden tendrils of Dignity booming out as this time, they were supremely fast.

Her eyes became cold as they gazed at the mocking look on Archon Basileus Alderon and the careful visage of Martial King Reperiel.

|And also...to keep me in any cage, those holding it up must ask themselves- do they have more dignity than me?|

HUUM!

Her sword hummed.

HUUM!
