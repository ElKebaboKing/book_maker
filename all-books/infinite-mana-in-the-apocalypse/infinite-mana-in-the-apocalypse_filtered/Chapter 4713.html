Chapter 4711: Muspelheim III

For Surtra, it was very unique to observe the emergence of this blue-gold monolith as she was extremely perceptive to The First Tongue.

She had spent eons studying its nuances, learning its rhythms, understanding how Phonemes flowed into Logos and Logos wove into Philologies. She knew The First Tongue the way a master calligrapher knew brushstrokes, the way a composer knew the spaces between notes.

And what she was seeing right now didnt make sense.

The entrance of this monolith came with it grand Weavings of change, and the moment that it descended, she was shocked and infuriated to see the surrounding atmosphere of Muspelheim begin to be drawn towards this monolith. As if it was being absorbed. Devoured. The primordial flames that had burned across this realm since before the great differentiation, the very essence of Muspelheim itself, was flowing toward that blue-gold construction like water drawn toward a drain.

Someone was drinking their realm.

But alongside this audacious consumption, the imperceptible changes shed been trying to figure out from The First Tongue fully arrived in her domain. The blue-gold light radiated for gigaparsecs as if it were trying to cover as much surface area as possible, washing across the flaming lands and the golden pillars and the temple at the center of her domain.

In turn, Surtra instantly felt the new elements of The First Tongue permeate across her Way and Civilization.

And the seas of obsidian flames that moved like sand in the surroundings of the domain she protected, the very foundation of her defensive authority, they gained an even more immense quality to them as something clicked into place that she hadnt even known was missing.

BOOM!

The change was immediate and devastating.

Her Dissipating Shores of Infinite Whispers had always operated on principles she understood intimately. Absorption. Dispersion. Reformation. The qualities of sand elevated to linguistic authority. But now, new Phonemes she had never encountered were weaving themselves into her existing Logos, and instead of disrupting her constructions, they amplified them.

It was as if she had been speaking The First Tongue with an accent her entire existence, and someone had just shown her the proper pronunciation.

The seas of obsidian flames that surrounded her domain surged outward with renewed force. Where before they had held the line against THE Living Spirits assault, now they pushed back. The white-gray fog that had been pressing inward suddenly found itself facing resistance it hadnt anticipated.

The fog recoiled.

THE Living Spirits forces, tens of thousands of Spiritual Abominations that had been maintaining constant pressure against her defenses, were actually pushed back at this time. Not by much. Perhaps a few kilometers. But after minutes of stalemate, any movement at all was significant.

And Surtra hadnt done anything differently.

The blue-gold light had simply made her existing Way more effective. More complete. As if her understanding of The First Tongue had been operating at ninety percent efficiency her entire existence, and now it was finally reaching its full potential.

Surtra was stern and shocked as she kept her gaze on that radiant blue-gold monolith. Her obsidian flame eyes burned with intensity as she voiced out silently, the words barely a whisper even to herself.

"Is it you? Have you arrived?"

It was only a thought in her head. A hope she and many others had carried for eons, nursed through countless cycles of waiting and wondering if the claim theyd sensed would ever be fulfilled.

She blinked in the next moment as if she was seeing things.

But she could swear that in front of the endlessly rising blue-gold monolith, she saw the appearance of a blue-gold humanoid hologram with piercing eyes that began to gaze at the vastness of Muspelheim radiantly.

The hologram was there for only an instant. A flicker of presence. A suggestion of awareness.

And then it was scanning the realm, taking in everything, processing information at speeds that made Surtras own considerable perception seem sluggish by comparison.

Someone was watching!
