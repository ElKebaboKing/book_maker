At this juncture, one had to look at a distance in terms of light-years!

For the knowledge creatures, there was an understanding that a single light-year was equivalent to 6 trillion miles, a distance that the majority of beings would never even dream of traversing throughout their lives.

Yet in this vast Universe, at a distance of an unknown number of light-years, a Signal was traveling along the waves of universal laws!

The unique thing about this signal was that it never traveled in one direction, the chaotic and deadly nature of the Void outside of galaxies throwing it around millions of miles of random distance every second.
