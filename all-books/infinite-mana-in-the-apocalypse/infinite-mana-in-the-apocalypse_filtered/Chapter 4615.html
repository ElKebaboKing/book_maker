Chapter 4613: A Period of Quintessential Progress! II

Noah stepped into the vastly expanded arena with calm deliberation, his blue-gold Goad held loosely in one hand.

Across from him, Astrape, THE Maelstrom Thundermane, stood with a heavy expression carved into features that seemed perpetually surrounded by crackling white lightning.

The entitys eyes blazed with fury and profound resentment.

When Astrape spoke, his voice carried the rumbling quality of distant thunder as he looked at him up and down.

"All of this. Every single consequence that has befallen me. All of this happened because of YOU. And now, after everything youve caused, you have the audacity to step into a cage with me?"

HUUM!

White lightning began crackling more intensely around his form as killing intent solidified into almost physical pressure.

"Regardless of what The Ginnu Lifeforms do to me afterward, regardless of what punishments they devise, I will kill you today. Your existence ends here, Anomaly. I swear it upon my Way."

The arena trembled with the weight of that vow.

Noah looked at the enraged entity with complete calm, his expression revealing nothing.

And then he spoke with quiet finality.

"You should not have given me time to utter."

THE Maelstrom Thundermanes expression shifted to confusion.

What could that possibly mean?

In the very next instant, he understood with horrifying clarity.

Multiple Logos descended onto his position from positions all around the arena where Noah had already inscribed them while walking forward.

Seven Logos of Imprisonment, identical in structure to those that had bound the cages of captured THE entities, wrapped around Astrapes form with irresistible authority as he found this power familiar!

Additional Logos designed specifically to weaken and suppress lightning-based authorities activated simultaneously.

Shock exploded across Astrapes features as he realized Noah had prepared the battlefield before even beginning the confrontation.

The battle that unfolded was less a clash of pure power and more a deadly dance between opposing forces.

Above the arena, Ginnu Lifeforms continued their ritual celebration, dancing around the massive flames of Phonemes with building fervor.

Their movements seemed to mirror and accentuate what occurred below.

THE Maelstrom Thundermane erupted with his full authority, his Way of Fulmination expressing itself in overwhelming fashion.

Bursts of brilliant white lightning exploded outward, each discharge containing enough concentrated power to annihilate entire realities.

The lightning smashed against the descending Logos with devastating force, shattering several of the linguistic constructs instantly.

But Noah simply reformed them faster than Astrape could destroy them, his Fluent Speaker mastery allowing rapid reconstruction.

Then Astrape released something far more terrifying.

A Genesis Principle.

The Genesis Principle of Absolute Conduction!

The moment he unleashed this profound authority, everything within the arena transformed fundamentally.

The ground, the air, the very fabric of space itself, even the Logos Noah had constructed, all of it converted instantly into tendrils of pure white lightning.

Existence itself became an ocean of electrical discharge under Astrapes complete authority.

Noahs body was smashed backward violently as reality itself pressed down with crushing force.

The four Logos inscribed across his form flared with protective authority, filtering out approximately half of the devastating pressure.

But even with that resistance, every ounce of transformed reality around him sought to force his existence to convert into pure lightning under Astrapes Genesis Principle.

Astrape came to float above Noah like a titan formed entirely from condensed electrical fury.

At that moment, this enraged being chose not to speak or waste breath on declarations.

He simply wanted to kill.

What happened next seemed to play out in slow motion, each instant stretched to painful clarity.

Astrape raised both hands high above his head.

For Gigaparsecs behind him, stretching to the very edges of the expanded arena space, absolutely all of existence turned into vibrant white lightning under his authority.

A sea of electrical annihilation waiting to be released.

He brought his hands down with finality, seeking to thunder all of that accumulated devastation directly onto Noahs position and erase him from Existence entirely!

At such a critical moment, the scene continued playing in that strange slow-motion clarity.

Noah simply raised his stick with calm precision and spoke words that carried quiet satisfaction.

|Thank you for coming so close.|

Even though the might and Depth of Astrape was so terrifying that his Genesis Principle had transformed absolutely everything in the arena, even the Logos Noah had constructed, into white lightning, there remained one fundamental truth.

That Genesis Principle itself was ultimately a complex form of Logos written in The First Tongue.

And even though the visible Logos Noah had utilized were transformed into lightning alongside everything else, his Way could sense every single Phoneme comprising those linguistic structures.

All around him, hidden within the ocean of white lightning that now permeated the entire space, Noah had reformed the seven Logos of Imprisonment.

He had masked them perfectly to appear identical to the lightning surrounding them, making them completely undetectable.

At this moment, where Astrapes descending hands would bring annihilation crashing down to ensure Noahs death, Noah activated the veiled Logos that were now mere inches away from the thundering entity.

HUUM!

The terrifying waves of pressure and overwhelming power that Astrape was releasing instantly began to dwindle and be systematically repressed.

The seven Logos of Imprisonment activated simultaneously, wrapping around the shocked entity with binding authority.

Additional Logos manifested in rapid succession.

Multiple Logos of Absolute Separation cut Astrape off from his Civilization and Way.

Logos of Predetermined Failure ensured that any attempt to draw upon external Existence or utilize his Way would conclude in immediate collapse.

Because of the extreme closeness and proximity, Noahs mastery expressed itself with devastating efficiency.

Tendrils of white lightning that had surrounded Astrape instantly transformed back into visible Logos and began binding him with multiplying restraints.

Noah himself took his Goad and began striking with serpentine speed.

The stick lashed out multiple times in rapid succession, each strike landing on different points across Astrapes massive form.

With each precise impact, another portion of Astrapes power was locked away and sealed.

The strikes looked like an intricate dance of Existence itself.

In the surroundings, the vibrant music of the festival permeated even into this expanded arena space.

The rhythm of drums, the melodic tones of Phoneme-based instruments, the harmonic chanting of The First Tongue all created an atmosphere that transformed violence into performance!

Above, the Ginnu Lifeforms were dancing with even more fervor as if everything had reached a critical precipice.

Their movements became faster, more passionate, more celebratory.

The more times Noah struck Astrape with his Goad, the dimmer and dimmer the authority of this once-terrifying being became.

No matter how desperately Astrape tried to express his Way of Fulmination, the Logos already binding him made such expression impossible.

And even when he managed to break through individual Phoneme connections in the linguistic chains through sheer force, Noah reformed them even faster than Astrape could shatter the links.

For the final strike, Noah took a batting stance with his Goad held in both hands.

He swung upward with explosive force, the stick connecting with Astrapes bound form and launching the massive entity high into the air.

Noahs figure flickered and appeared far above at the precise trajectory where Astrape would arrive.

He swung downward with equal devastating power, smashing the helpless entity back toward the arena floor with meteor-like velocity.

BOOM!
