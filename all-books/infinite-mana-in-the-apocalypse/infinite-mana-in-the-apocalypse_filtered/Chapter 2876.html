Chapter 2874 The Weaver of Extremity! IV

Noah didn't just set his eyes on the weaving of the Veil of Arcadia, but from his main body bathed in vermillion colored flames, a clone arose and disappeared as it followed the weavings of Existence and non-existence.

It crossed the Records of the Quintessential Annals of Extremity and into the Aeonic Annals of Extremity, delving with a paradoxical light as along the way, the air of Tyranny and Infinity he gave off gradually changed as a white glow began to surround him!

The white glow of Vitalis.

The Weaver could project the weaving of any Extreme outwardly.

He masked his Existential Extremity Authorities and made it seem that his whole existence was bathed in and stemmed from Vitalis, and his clone followed the weavings of the unfolding reality within the layers of Records hiding towards the Veil of Arcadia.

A veiled Record where powerful entities that had lived through eons lived in- and one where Legends from the Vacuous and Vitalis Hyperversal Domains would come to meet!

What were the Concepts they nearly brought to the Existential level? {The Weaver of Extremity seeks to unearth the weaving of Existence and Extremes purposefully lost and eradicated in eons of passing records.}

Back to his main body, the Haki around him only became more intense as he felt like he was flipping through countless pages of absolutely nothing- and in this endless field of nothing, he was trying to hone in on something that had been erased by the Dikastes of Extremity a long time ago!

{In the era before the spurred wars between Vacuous and Vitalis, the Vitalis Hyperversal Domains held a unified rule under the banner of a being who had risen from obscurity to become the Emperor.}

{All scattered factions fell before him as he focused on a single Extreme that he understood the weavings of to a tremendous level- the Extremity of Relativity.}

...!

Relativity! A concept Noah already grasped as it was a past Natural Law!

|Why do you all have such an overabundance of greed? Even with the whole Vitalis Hyperversal Domains under you, you still seek for more and more...|

Greed!

The Dikastes of Extremity had actually called it greed as Noah's eyes closed with a sharp light, as if he could see the day that this risen Emperor suddenly fell without even knowing why, and everything about him disappeared soon after.

It was a detestable thing for the Dikastes of Extremity to do.

And as Noah gazed at the weavings of the lost history of Emperor Delbert...

{The weavings of Relativity as seen by Emperor Delbert become prevalent to you.}

Such a phenomenon may just pave the way and indicate that Relativity and the other governing Natural Laws Noah held...may indeed have the capability of attaining the ranking of Existential!

The weavings of Relativity from a long gone Honored One flowed into Noah's mind as outside of it, fundamental changes continued to bloom throughout the Infinite Hyperversal Haven!

And yet all of this was just the tip of the iceberg.

Within the Veil of Arcadia.

A long spanning ancient metropolis stretched out towards the horizon as monumental auras continued to appear towards its very center- all entities floating towards a floating circular structure that had a cloud of Vitalis gloriously shining below it, waterfalls of authority serenely washing down any falsehood.

Whether their true forms of the Entities being seated were ancient cosmic sized wonders, whether they were lineages of Animalia, Celestia, Yog-Nyarvax, Chthonian, or any nature...they would all appear in humanity forms to be seated on these thrones!

This was the sacred meeting place at the center of the Veil of Arcadia.

The Existential Arcadian High Hall!

Here, no matter if you were of the Vacuous or Vitalis Hyperversal Domains, all were welcomed.

Here, an Existential Symposium would take place!

Within it, powerful auras could be felt by the dozens as they ranged from Entities at the Fifth Gradation of Extremity all the way to the Seventh Gradation of Extremity.

There were even some unique areas that made one unsure where they stood on the scales of Gradations- even possibly inching into or being within the Eighth Gradation of Extremity!

Uniquely, two entities were standing left and right by the wide golden doors, their hands calmly behind their backs as they were the Extremity of Steel and Curagulus.

Their auras were hard to ascertain as they gazed at those coming in.

His body emanated calm waves of Vitalis that didn't particularly belong to any Gradation, with his face showing devilish handsomeness and valor as he passed through the wide golden doors while giving a nod of recognition towards the burly figure of the Primogenitor of Steel!
