Chapter 3388 Runes of Existence! II

A new language!

The methodology to communicate with the Wheel of Existence was that of Runes of Existence, with entities of higher power utilizing them to achieve unimaginable feats.

The Great Beyonder and his Transcendent Megalos Abilities.

The Omnifather and his Sacred Complexity Modus Operandi.

All of them and more had delved into the Runes of Existence as now, Noah began to study their shocking complexity as well.

In the dazzling dream, a year passed relatively quickly under the perception of the Antediluvian Leviathans who lived for cycles.

And as promised, the figure of the Omnifather blazed with brilliance and reappeared on the midpoint of the trunk of the Primaltree of Existence, many Leviathans rousing to look at him while his multicolored figure smiled!

|A year has gone by as the gauge of whether you have any degree of ease in the Runes of Existence is done. Embarking on grasping this language seems to have many unknown factors around it, but one's own complexity of existence is one thing, and the type of Source of Existence is another, with some even saying the purity of existence allows one to forge Runes of Existence with greater success, ease, and complexity. Do not despair if you did not grasp too much in the last year- we will put things into practice today.|

...!

The Antediluvian Leviathans were roused as they looked ahead with anticipation.

|Let's start off with the lowest Terrestrial Rune of Existence, the Rune of Blanc that can change to take on any weavings. Its complexity isn't as much as the Quintessential Sacred Complexity Rune due to what it seeks to achieve, and this is how one would draw it.|

Everyone watched with bated breaths as they saw the potent weavings of the Omnifather leak from him and transform into small, intricate fine strokes that showed off the construction of a Rune.

It wasn't easy.

With every fine stroke, a sense of balance and harmony had to be maintained. With every stroke, one had to converge a tremendous amount of complexity in an extremely small area and continue to do so for another thousands of strokes.

The Rune of Blanc- one of the lowest Terrestrial Runes of Existence...held more than a few thousand strokes simply to bring to existence as after the Omnifather was done, a blinding white rune floated before everyone's eyes.

Whoever looked at it would see it read- Blanc!

After many intricate weavings of existence made it, one could now take it and choose any weaving of existence to fill this Rune of Blanc.

A few thousand strokes.

The Quintessential Sacred Complexity Rune of Existence they had been studying this past year had at least a million strokes that all came together intricately to form this rune, while the Amarantos Rune of Existence...held at least 10 million unique strokes all around.

This was a guess from Noah based on what he had seen so far as he had not managed to understand all the strokes contained in the Runes he studied- just roughly an approximation!

But the Rune of Blanc...seemed much simpler as every Antediluvian Leviathan here tried to duplicate what the Omnifather had just done- to follow all the few thousand strokes he made.

Nobody was able to succeed on their first try.

Not the Existence Severing Entities, and certainly not the Ordinance of Existence Entities!

For many, even starting a few strokes with their vibrant and heavy complexity didn't allow the strokes to remain still as everything fizzled out even before a hundred strokes could be made.

|Failure results in the depletion of your weavings of existence, but with every failure, your understanding of the Runes of Existence will increase.|

Nobody had succeeded on their first try, but over the next day...

HUUM!

Multiple blinding white singularities bloomed on the periphery where the Existence Severing entities were, a few of them managing to complete a single Terrestrial Rune of Existence due to their own sheer depth of complexity!

When they did so, their weavings pulsed with a serene light of brilliance as the complexity they held this whole time seemed to increase slightly just from this recent practice of Rune formation.

The Omnifather nodded towards the Existence Severing Antediluvian Leviathans and continued.

|If successful, continue forming as many more Runes of Existence as you can and increase your rate of success and understanding. You will come to see that the familiarization of Runes of Existence and even forging them is a tremendous practice in increasing the complexity of your own weavings of existence. But for variation, here is another Terrestrial Rune of Existence, The Rune of Erasure...|

Vibrant lights formed around the Omnifather as he constructed another Rune of Existence under the watchful eyes of all.

With their spirits invigorated, the Antediluvian Leviathans delved into the practice of Runes fully!

In the ninth week, Boros was the first Ordinance of Existence Entity that managed to successfully construct a Rune of Blanc.

Not just the first Ordinance of Existence entity to do so, but also the first Antediluvian Leviathan after the Existence Severing entities who managed to form a Rune of Existence!

And how could he not when even though the talents of Boros were poor, he held the Rune of Amarantos that was even more complex than anything that the Omnifather had shown them so far for him to study?

His accomplishment caught the eyes of the Omnifather in the distance who nodded towards his direction, an imperceptible light flashing briefly in his eyes as he locked onto Boros before he looked away.

He continued to show more and more simple Terrestrial Rules of Existence as he systematically taught the powerful Antediluvian Leviathans, and the years passed.

Five years.

Ten years.

Fifty years!

The surroundings of the Primaltree of Existence were now filled with a shocking array of complexities as thousands of Runes of Existence floated around it, causing the concentration of weavings of Existence nearby to be enriched and pulse with activity.

A few dozen Ordinance Amalgamation Entities had also managed to embark on the path of Runes of Existence, with very few Ordinance of Existence entities succeeding in any Runes so far!

Boros remained one of the astonishing ones as at this moment, many different Terrestrial Runes of Existence surrounded him.

In the 50th year, the Omnifather gave another demonstration as he looked at the progress around him.

|Those with the ability have managed to forge a few Runes of Existence, and from them, you should feel a unique link. Every rune is unfathomably unique as it shares the signature of the one who made it, with its complexity and design being so intricate that it is forever bound by the one who forged it. Other people can be given access to the complex workings of a certain rune- a certain word that they can then utilize, but it will always belong to the single being who forged it. They may even be able to know every person utilizing the word, the rune that they forged.|

...!
