[Domain Expansion]:: When sufficient strides have been taken towards Extremity, the resonance of one's Soul with advanced Boundary Layers gives way to the expansion of the Domains of Boundaries and Dynamis to an unfathomable level. For the cost of 80% of your current Soul Values coursing through and putting a strain on your Heart as the Domain Engine, the domain of a Dynamis can be further expanded by adding the definition of a concept to it- or vice versa. It is a closer interplay between concepts and Dynamis as one gets closer to Extremity, with the resulting domain allowing your Will to be expressed anywhere within it instantly for 1 minute. The expanded Domain allows you to use all skills and abilities of the chosen Dynamis and Concept of Extremity at no cost of Soul Values or any other resources, with your Will remaining anchored onto the expanded Domain to the extent that so long as it is not destroyed, your soul cannot entirely perish to any attacks. The true power and extent of the cast Domain Expansion truly depend on the Dynamis and Concept of Extremity put together as something with great resonance can give birth to a domain that will cause one's enemies to only despair...

80% of one's Soul Values to cast. It was a heavy cost, and the truly powerful Apex Aeonic Lifeforms paid this cost in order to win difficult battles.
