"But light has an intimate relationship with time that you have not fully grasped. Light travels at a fixed speed through space, which means it is bound by temporal constraints. When you observe distant stars, you are seeing light from the past because time was required for that light to reach you."

But they were not as powerful as this being, and under the threat of complete collapse or potential elevation, they followed her without question. They progressed their Sovereignties while weaving connections with time along the way, hoping the benefits would outweigh the cost.
