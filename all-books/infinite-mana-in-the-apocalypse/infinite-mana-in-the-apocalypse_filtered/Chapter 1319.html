Chapter 1317 - A Show Of Invincibility! I

In the glimmering Primordial Sea, the figure of the grandiose Draconic-Elephant Cosmic Primordial Beast that was Hyperion could be seen!

His three eyes swirled with the light of mysticism as he oversaw many scenes, his current focus in this millisecond being a scene not within the depths of the Primordial Empire, but outside of it.

At this moment in time within the Cosmic Battlefield that was located in the depths of the Primordial Empire, Primordial Beasts had bloomed as they would ravage and begin to fracture the vast cluster of Cosmos from within. But this was just a single factor in Hyperion's eyes.

If he was going to make a move, he would make it in a grand fashion as he wouldn't just stop there!

"Move from the Clusters of Pandemonium..." His dominant voice echoed out as it rang into the minds of a number of beasts located differing distances away from the Primordial Empire.

Some were billions of light years away or even trillions, but a flood of dozens of Beasts erupted from Clusters of Pandemonium as they made a beeline towards the grandiose Primordial Empire! Grand Primordial Beasts led the way from each cluster, their eyes hungry for the allure of Universes and Cosmos as their jaws already pulsated with the Cosmic Essences of Ruination and the Primordial Dao. Their aim was the vast Empire before their eyes as it revealed one of the many issues such a conglomeration of Cosmos had.

The Cosmos within the Primordial Empire were many as even though it was labeled the Bastion of Reality, it was also a location that was widely known by the existences that devoured Cosmos! They didn't attack under the orders of a single being- not because the Great Conqueror of this Empire was too strong for them to handle.

Aegon was a fantastic deterrent for any normal Primordial Beasts as they would not dare to approach his Bastion of Reality, but it was not a strong enough deterrent for the existence that had reached the Cosmic Realm before him!

So this being plotted and planned as he installed many Clusters of Pandemonium that held a number of his pieces, and this occasion today was one of the many that Hyperion planned to use these pieces!

Apart from peeking into the existence that had reality bending abilities apart from the Champion of Reality, Hyperion used this juncture to do nothing else but cause a single action that was always planned for the advancement of his future plans.

"Chaos...let it bloom and muddy the waters. It will make it all that much easier to set a stage for the fall of a Primordial...all that much easier for the Edict of Anarchy to elevate me to even greater levels."

WAA!

A terrific set of words left Hyperion's mouth as from this Chaos, there was much for him to benefit from! As the holder of the Nomological Edict of Anarchy, any destruction and chaos caused by him elevated his levels of power tremendously, the destruction of many Universes and Cosmos placing him where he was today.

This was just a part of his power, and it was now being elevated with every passing millisecond as from his actions, Anarchy unfolded in a glorious fashion.

His third eye that split apart his forehead swirled with intense light as it reflected the figure of a certain battle blooming in the Cosmic Battlefield- the scene of the figure of Aegon clashing with three Grand Primordial Beasts as the result...actually wasn't the decimation of the defenses of these beasts!

The three Beasts at the Universal Filament Realm actually withstood the Champion in the Cosmic Realm, and the billions of Splendiferous Universes within the Origins of these Beasts were not enough to explain it. It was the protection and effectiveness of a unique authority that stemmed from the unique Primordial Eye that held an immeasurable number of swords circling its iris!

"The Primordial Eye of the Kaiser..."

...!

Hyperion uttered such a grand name as this fantastical eye released a cascade of deadly light, a light that was enough to support three Grand Primordial Beasts proficient in Edicts to momentarily withstand an existence in the Cosmic Realm!

The light of the Primordial Eye of the Kaiser remained on Aegon for a few more seconds before its focus shifted to another existence.

"As for you..." Hyperion's gaze turned towards the image of Osmont sitting upon a throne with one of his arms raised, a glorious golden hammer the size of multiple galaxies forming above him in a stellar fashion.

This hammer bloomed from the sundered cracks of space as it blazed with a scorching golden light that melted the boundaries of broken galaxies and universes, being inlined with red pulsating runic lines as it bordered over dozens of billions of light years in size, being larger than any of the currently moving figures in the Cosmic Battlefield!

This terrifying Hammer formed as it then thundered towards the roaring figure of Stormdust, the Primordial Eye of the Kaiser within Hyperion flashing once more as a unique light went on to wrap around the body of the Betrayer.

Hyperion stared at this unfolding scene as his eyes flashed with a sharp light, his words coming out slowly.

"To display this power, I feel like you are hiding things from me and intentionally making me see others...for you to be able to do that, you would have to have that, hmm?"

...!

The intuition of this Cosmic Primordial Beast was terrifying as a possibility closer to the truth popped up within his mind!

"Intriguing...let's see what this chaos shall call forth."

With a domineering light of sharpness, the Origin of this existence vibrated strongly as scintillating waves of light erupted all around him!

In the Primordial Empire, its defenders rose in valiant fashions as they sought to stop the rampage of Primordial Beasts that seemed terribly more powerful than normal, a large majority of these defenders being stuck in the depths of the Empire that was filled with a cluster of Cosmos as at this moment...even more Primordial Beasts began to appear from the tumultuous Ruination Seas that surrounded the vast Empire.
