Chapter 1136 - Antiquity Of Mana! I

In the vast Ruination Sea.

Far away from Noah observing his Infinite Cosmos, the prominent figure of a certain Primordial that he had come in contact with not too long ago could be seen!

She had crossed untold distances and deadly Ruination and Primordial storms to arrive at a certain location in the Ruination Sea, and she was currently hidden in the folds of the Ruination Sea as she watched a certain spectacle play out with interest.

Her figure was actually located a few trillion miles away, but her eyes crossed this shocking distance to gaze upon a battle that was taking place between two existences.

The battle was something that caused the vast Ruination Seas to churn and tumble as when the Primordial gazed upon it, one couldnt find any awe or surprise on her veiled expression, but simply a sense of interest!

"Little Aegon has truly grown up since I first met him. To be handling a Grand Primordial Beast like this..."

...!

Yes! The Primordial was shockingly watching a battle between an existence that was set to be the champion of an actual reality against a Primordial Beast that could devour entire Cosmos!

RUMBLE!

The Ruination Sea trembled as the figures of the two existences became clear.

One was a valiant looking human, his figure being nothing as such as it towered many light years in size, being larger than any Galaxies based on his form alone! The eyes of this being shone with utter darkness as they seemed like black holes that could draw in all light, his eyes and jaws looking so sharp that they could cut apart those that looked too closely.

His hair was even darker as it draped over his shoulders, giving this being a wild look as this wasnt the most impressive thing to look at on his body. The most shocking thing was that below the neck of this being...his entire body was glowing like a pristine and sculpted Cosmic Treasure, every single portion of his skin shining beautifully with a rainbow glow of light!

An innumerable number of lines and runic sigils seemed to be revolving on his skin constantly, his body rumbling with power as he even caused the surroundings Ruination Seas to vibrate due to his sheer power.

With his dark eyes and hair cascading down his shoulders and his body glowing with multicolored light, he was the brightest thing in the Ruination Sea for thousands of light years as the enemy he faced...was a Beast that was actually five times his size.

A Beast that eclipsed the sizes of multiple Galaxies as its eyes were filled with an adorned majesty and power, these eyes shimmering a beautiful gold as they reflected the colors of this Beast gloriously! Its entire body was filled with golden white fur that draped over it majestically, this Beast having 5 pairs of white and gold wings erupting on its back as it floated on four legs that glistened gloriously!

The head of this Beast was extremely unique, looking like a mixture of the most pristine dragons and phoenixes as paired with the 5 pairs of white and gold wings, it had an air of inviolable majesty that screamed sacredness and honor! As if this Beast could be the protector of some holy land that graced the lands with love and protection...but this was a Primordial Beast.

A Primordial Beast that devoured entire Universes and Cosmos as they had the goal of erasing all the Cosmos within a branching reality and returning it to the Original Reality!

This Primordial Beast was even termed a Grand one at that, thousands of Splendiferous Universes shining brilliantly on its chest as it stared fearlessly at the titanous human before it. The human stared back with even less fear as the multiple colors all over his body rotated even faster, and one would be able to notice something shocking sparkling on the chest of this being.

There, hidden amidst the brilliance of moving lines and runic circles on his skin, one would be able to see a grandly shimmering Cosmos.

A Cosmos!

Such a being was standing against a Grand Primordial Beast fearlessly, but the more profound thing was that the Primordial Beast didnt fear him as well! The Beast being in the upper tier of the Universal Filament with thousands of Splendiferous Universes actually didnt relent when facing against an existence in the Cosmic Realm, and this was due to a truth that very few beings knew that even allowed Grand Primordial Beasts to stand against regular existences in the Cosmic Realm.

It was the terrifying power that all Primordial Beasts were given at their birth that caused them to be so terrifying that normal Antiquities would only find themselves facing death if ever they came across one. It was...

"Lets see...this one has already entered its second stage in life as it has complete comprehension and assimilation of both the Primordial Dao and the Dao of Ruination. Should have already devoured a few cosmos..."

RUMBLE!

The Primordial watching from afar spoke out with no emotions at the mention of this beast having already devoured a few Cosmos- that was an uncountable number of lives! But her apathetic words contained shocking information about the nature of Primordial Beasts.

At their birth, they either held complete comprehension and assimilation in the Dao of Ruination OR the Primordial Dao.

It was something that even the existences who were lucky enough to comprehend these Daos would struggle to achieve until the Universes within their Origins dried up and they died, and yet they had it at birth!

The complete assimilation of Daos such as these brought about profound strength that would almost make an existence unrivaled across myriad of Cosmos.

Yet this was the first stage of life of Primordial Beasts.

Their second Stage consisted of them gaining the complete assimilation of BOTH Ruination and the Primordial Dao, and this was when they were termed Grand Primordial Beasts.

The third stage of life was truly the most terrifying one, and it was these Primordial Beasts that more often than not accomplished their purposes of returning things to how they once were and destroyed entire branching realities.

The stage that allowed them to be termed Cosmic Primordial Beasts, the Enders of Realities!

RUMBLE!
