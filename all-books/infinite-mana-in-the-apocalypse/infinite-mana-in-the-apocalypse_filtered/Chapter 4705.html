Chapter 4703: I Am!

What does it mean to be oneself?

Many across the ages have grappled with this question, and none have arrived at an answer that satisfies all. Some say the self is merely the sum of ones experiences, a collection of memories and traumas and joys that accumulate like sediment at the bottom of a river.

Others argue that the self is something innate, a core that exists before experience, before memory, before even the first breath of consciousness.

There are those who believe the self is an illusion, a story we tell ourselves to make sense of the chaos that surrounds us. And there are those who believe the self is the only thing that truly exists, with everything else being merely a reflection of our inner truth projected outward onto the canvas of reality.

But perhaps the most profound truth about the self is this: everyone will tell you who your self is, and who to be.

Parents will shape you in their image. Teachers will mold you according to their ideals. Friends will influence you with their expectations. Enemies will define you by opposition. Society will demand conformity.

Power will demand submission. And through it all, countless voices will whisper and shout and scream about who you should become, how you should act, what you should believe, and why you should change.

But in the end, one can only be themselves.

Because nobody knows how you can be your true self apart from you. No sage, no ancient entity watching from the shadows of existence can tell you who you are at your core. They can offer counsel. They can share wisdom. They can warn of dangers and promise rewards. But the fundamental question of selfhood can only be answered by the one asking it.

And sometimes, the answer comes not through contemplation, but through assertion, through defiant declaration. Not through becoming what others say you should be, but through embodying what you know yourself to already be.

Noahs defiance was clear as at this time, he felt his Existence and Phonemes buzzing while he looked directly at the calm figure of THE Secretive Eon.

I am realizing more and more that power comes in different forms. His voice was steady, contemplative, yet underlaid with something that pulsed with growing conviction. If I look at my power now, I could strictly designate myself as a THE Surface Depth or THE Intermediate Depth of Absolute Sovereignty entity, and that would be accurate. But it is also inaccurate, because I can do things no Intermediate Depth entity could do. I was able to go to the Earliest Folds. To the Infinite Unfurling. To Ginnungagap itself. I was able to evade the imprisonment in THE Belly of Existence and THE Loom when others could not. I was able to do things that no THE Intermediate entity could do, without ever stepping into THE...

BOOM!

His eyes shone with an unfathomable light of immensity. A profound and unique air filled with ancientness and immensity began to radiate from him, and the very Stoa of Dogmata seemed to tremble in recognition of something taking shape.

So when you say I am not at the level of THE Creature or you, perhaps in some aspects that is true. But there are things I can do that THE Creature cannot do. There are places I have been that THE Creature has not reached. I have done with The First Tongue what THE Creature never did. There are truths I have touched that THE Creature has not grasped. His gaze burned brighter. And if that is the case, then in those aspects, I am more powerful than THE Creature. In those aspects, I exceed him.

BOOM!

Blue-gold light surged around him with profundity, his eyes shining like blazing suns as if he was extrapolating and expanding a glorious epiphany that had been building within him since the moment he first touched The First Tongue.

You say I do not have any comparable power to them. That I am weaker than them. That I should hide until I am stronger, should crawl like a worm until I earn the right to stand. His voice did not rise, did not fall, but each word landed with the weight of absolute conviction.

And yet I believe I can move freely just like them. Because what Surface or Intermediate Depth of Absolute Sovereignty entity can sit here and talk to you without worry? THE Creature could talk to you as I am talking to you now. THE Living Paradox could do so. And now, I am doing so.

He let that truth settle into the air between them.

Because in some ways, my standing, my glory, my distinction, the very weight of my name, in some ways I am already like you and all others. That is my Way. That is my Immensity. His eyes blazed with finality.

I am not strong. I am not weak. I simply am.

BOOM!

...!

His blood buzzed. His Grimoire of Infinite Lexicons buzzed. His Organ of Civilization buzzed!

His mind buzzed as he felt like he had grasped and asserted an inner truth about himself that nobody could deny, a truth that Existence itself was forced to acknowledge, to the point that he embodied it!
