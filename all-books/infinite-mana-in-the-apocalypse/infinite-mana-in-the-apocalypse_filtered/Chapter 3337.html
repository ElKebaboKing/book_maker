Chapter 3335 Progression! III

BOOM!

The heavy force reverberated out as this small part of this Sector within the Megalos Ynnere was lit up white.

The force was so heavy that even Noah was forced to move further away, his eyes bright as the Shockwaves of three Tzar Versepiercer reached him even during his retreat!

But at such a distance so many light years away...

The reverberations were only booming flickers on his body.

Even from this far distance, he still felt the shocking interactions of unstable Matter and Dark Matter as now, what was left to see were the results of his actions.

His retreat stopped as his Sacred Primal Eye of Existence looked ahead while he began to advance towards the Tainted Omniverse where the Tzar Versepiercer was deployed.

A distance away, the rough figure of Savitar could be seen as his outer body layers trembled with instability- his gaze ashen as he seemed to be traumatized while he thought of many things.

Feeling the attack that Noah had released and what effects it had from this far distance, Savitar felt relief.

But he also felt a great deal of rising fear towards this Entity who had bound his Megalos Regnum as he couldn't help but think what would have happened if such a weapon had been unleashed on him!

SAA!

Noah's figure passed by Savitar with a mere glance as he went toward the center of the explosion.

The whole area held a burning sensation of existence, with this sensation only becoming stronger the closer one came.

Noah was able to continue seamlessly as the unstable waves of Matter and Dark Matter did not cause him too many issues due to having both such types within the makeup of his body, but even he felt the distortion of heat!

And when his eyes landed on the Tainted Omniverse...

"Oh my..."

He drew a sharp breath.

His Sacred Primal Eye of Existence captured everything from afar as the circular obsidian Tainted Omniverse that used to stand grandiosely just moments ago...was splintered into multiple pieces as it burned with seething white flames.

These flames seemed to be interactions of unstable Matter and Dark Matter as they burned heavily, and Noah could ascertain that within this space...there were absolutely no weavings of existence or signs of life!

The only thing left behind apart from the splintered Omniverse was a Dark Matter Core that was ablaze with white flames- and it was even knocked light years away from the location of the Omniverse itself as the impact was that heavy.

The only other thing was an illusory scorching white visage that burned where the Tzar Versepiercer was initially detonated.

It was an innate design Noah told the Infinite Omniverse to add that would act as a marker- and it currently showed an illusory image of himself clasping his hands in front of his chest in sorrow as his face...held an expression of mocking prayer for all those affected!

If there were any survivors at all, this illusory image of himself would be the last thing they saw before their Existence continued to erode and became wiped out.

Apart from these aforementioned things, nothing else was left.

No bones.

No weavings of any existence apart from sparse tendrils of Outerversal Authority.

It was the result of a ridiculous weapon that there were only 10 more remaining at this moment until the Infinite Omniverse designed some more!

And seeing the horrific results they could achieve as well as how vast the stretch of its influence could be...

"Take the Isolated Aletheian Haven Testing Site to the opposite of the explored Sector. As far from the Infinite Omniverse as possible."

|The process is already underway, Master.|

...!

He spoke towards the Infinite Omniverse itself to move the Isolated Aletheian Haven where these Tzar Versepiercers were being designed and held to an area far from anything else!

If any accidents were to happen and 10 or more Tzar Versepiercers were sparked to bloom...the catastrophe was one that even Noah did not want to bear witness to.

But the power of these new constructs expanded Noah's possible style of fighting as with them...he didn't have to be entirely fearful of enemies a stage or two higher than him as if they messed around...they would come to find out.

HUUM!

His weavings went past the scorching white flames of the Tzar Versepiercer as they wrapped around the remnant Tainted Omniverse and Dark Matter Core that were burning with white flames- all that remained of them being fuel for the Infinite Omniverse itself as they would be devoured and assimilated!

As Noah did this here, another one of his bodies was led by a reluctant Savitar towards another Tainted Omniverse known to be under the banner of the Great Ponderor.

Only a matter of minutes passed after the detonation of the Tzar Versepiercer in another Tainted Omniverse and when they arrived at a close enough proximity towards another Tainted Omniverse, and by the time Noah's gloriously gold Sacred Primal Eye of Existence began to open once more as he seemed about to repeat his actions...

"Master, can I leave first before you attack? I don't want this body to be affected as well..."

The One Who Walks, Savitar, wanted to walk far from this place before another Tzar Versepiercer was deployed!

But....

"No need."

Noah's golden eye squinted as when he looked into the Tainted Omniverse in the far distance that should have been another base of the Great Ponderor...it was utterly empty.

It had been emptied out entirely as it seemed that in a matter of minutes since the last Tainted Omniverse was decimated, The Great Ponderor had already cleared himself and all those who belonged to him out!

He left behind an empty Tainted Omniverse for Noah to simply walk into.

Noah smiled at such a reality.

What was this?

It was power. It was a deterrent!

It was something that even a Great One feared as Noah could only laugh gloriously as he simply strode in towards another Tainted Omniverse!

Very likely, any other Tainted Omniverses that Savitar knew the locations of would have a similar presentation.

All of it...would be more than enough for the uses of the Infinite Omniverse to be ready for its unfathomable ordeal!
