Chapter 4871: The Evolutionary Dead End

And for him right now, it was actually easier to navigate the idea of looking for treasures and resources than it would have been for anyone else in Observable Existence.

Because at this moment, he held Infinite Undifferentiated Fate.

...!

Noah saw faint lines of golden light stretching out in three separate directions from where he currently was, the threads of fate made visible through his Derivative Civilizations influence on the surrounding proto-matter.

Two of them were surrounded with a deep hue of crimson light that pulsed with warning, the color of blood and danger and almost certain death.

Only a single one shone bright orange.

The two shining with crimson light emanated a great level of danger for him, the kind of danger that even his newly infinite existence might not be able to survive. Perhaps Undivided Ones guarded those paths. Perhaps Formless Terrors lurked along their routes.

The one pulsing with orange light seemed to be potentially manageable but still very dangerous, the kind of danger that would test him without necessarily destroying him. The kind of hardship that Vakochev demanded for progression rather than the kind that simply ended existence.

But...

Everything in Existence was dangerous.

Hardship and adversity were a must for growth, werent they?

THE Primordial Paradox had said it himself when discussing the Vakochev Scales: if ones foundation hadnt weathered through waves of Immensity and hardship, if ones Way hadnt been established through genuine adversity, then the doorway to the second Scale would remain uncrossable regardless of how powerful one became.

So while feeling the immense growth and Immensity of his existence expanding with every passing moment, and seeking to grow it even more while he had the opportunity in this place, Noah began to move once more.

He took a step toward the orange-designated path.

And even though he did so, what actually moved was a swirling storm of proto-matter that concealed his existence within its undefined currents. The storm shot forward at speeds that made the proto-matter around it blur and distort, covering distances that should have taken considerable time in mere moments.

His speed was approaching the speed of his very good boy, Skoll.

His speed was approaching the speed of an Absolute.

...!

He had grown so much in such a short time, and he was just getting started.

As he moved silently through THE Prima Indifferentia, his concealment holding even at these tremendous velocities, Noah thought about the words of THE Primordial Paradox and this place as potentially the origin of THE Entity.

The one thing that stuck with him was what THE Primordial Paradox had said about THE Formless Terrors and THE Undivided Ones, the entities that represented the greatest dangers within this undifferentiated expanse.

THE Formless Terrors especially haunted his thoughts, because they had lost all definition of their existence and now moved around seeking to attack anything that possessed definition. They wanted to make others like themselves, to drag differentiated beings down into the formless state they had been trapped in.

And the words that THE Primordial Paradox had spoken resonated with something he had heard before.

THE Primordial Paradox had said exactly:

"It is not malice. It is something more fundamental than malice. It is the natural behaviour of undifferentiated existence encountering differentiated existence."

It was not malice.

When THE Secretive Eon had talked with him, she had mentioned how she, or they, were not doing anything overly malicious. They were simply returning things to their truest nature, helping existence achieve a state that they believed was more fundamental and more pure. And the more that others joined them, the less conflict there would be, as they would collectively focus on moving across the Scales of Existence together.

THE Secretive Eon had been confident that the second Scale would be achieved rather rapidly once enough of Observable Existence had been unified under THE Entitys influence.

The philosophy was almost seductive in its simplicity.

Join us. Become one with us. Stop fighting and start ascending together.

But now, with what Noah had just learned from THE Primordial Paradox, a fundamental flaw in that philosophy became abundantly clear.

The Vakochev Scales of Existence demanded hardship for progression.

They demanded adversity and conflict.

They demanded that foundations be built through genuine struggle. freewebnovel.com

So THE Entity seeking to infect everyone and have all of Existence collectively move toward the goal of elevation might truly be an evolutionary dead end. For those who had been unified under THE Entitys influence, there would be no genuine adversity because they would all be working toward the same goal without opposition. There would be no hardship because hardship required something to struggle against, and when everyone was the same, struggle became meaningless.

THE Secretive Eon had spoken of ascending together.

But The Scales of Existence didnt care about collective ascension.

The Scales of Existence cared about individual hardship woven into individual foundations.

The actions of THE Entity, if successful, would actually halt all progression across Observable Existence.

No one would reach the second Scale.

No one would advance beyond where they currently were.

Everyone would be unified, yes, but unified in stagnation rather than growth.

"..."

The thought was a heavy and deadly one as Noah moved across THE Prima Indifferentia, following the lead of his Derivative Civilization toward what should be a resource or treasure that also held a level of danger.

THE Entity might not even know about this flaw in its philosophy.

Or perhaps it did know and didnt care.

Or perhaps the goal was never actually ascension but something else entirely, something that used the promise of collective growth as a lure while the true purpose remained hidden behind layers of infection!
