Chapter 3078 Chaos Theory I

Time.

It was something that others had a great deal of, and also the thing that determined much about the power of an Entity.

Those who had more time in existence generally had more power.

Noah had not gone through a great deal of time, and yet he found himself always embroiled in the midst of things that occurred just as he was moving.

|The Existential Extremity Authority of the Protagonist ponders on why wars and apocalypses break out the moment of your arrival into a new Dominium.|

Time to simply and in a laid back manner explore, get stronger, farm Fruits of Existence, develop and understand millions of Existential Extremity Authorities, and observe the continued growth of the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed.

If he was given a month or even a year with no conflict, given the rate of the production of Fruits of Existence by him and the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed, he may just have grown strong enough to topple the strongest Entities here!

But no.

Wars and apocalypses bloomed left and right as soon as he arrived, and in this manner, the progress of months or years could be obtained in a short period of time by burning and devouring the hard work of the enemies that threw themselves at him.

|The Existential Extremity Authority of the Protagonist affirms that this reality only further proves its distinction.|
