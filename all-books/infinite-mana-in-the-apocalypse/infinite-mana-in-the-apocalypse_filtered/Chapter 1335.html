Chapter 1333: The Birth Of The Infinite Empire! I

Two lovers floated peacefully amidst the wreckage of shattered galactic and universal boundaries.

'I will never leave you, my Champion...'

These words echoed out in the minds of all those watching with their heads bowed down low off the edges of the Cosmic Battlefield, and they echoed in the mind of the man that had just seen his daughter and friend leave his side and protection for another being!

Even if their relationship was strained. Even if they had things to work on...they still chose to leave him regardless.

But one person stayed by his side. The person he had sought when he was weak, and the one he had obtained after he became the champion of Reality.

The golden haired beauty that at this moment...fully settled into his heart as her words rang out clearly!

'Me and you...against the enemies of Reality!'

Just them against all of Reality.

And who could stand in their way?!

WAA!

With such a thought and his heart settling, Aegon held Havenbreaker closely as his cold eyes were filled with a light of infatuation, glancing at the Cosmos around him as his voice reverberated out.

"We have always been the Bastion of Reality...and we will continue to be so!"

His voice was like cascading sunlight on a dark stormy day, the remaining Forefathers, Daolords, Antiquities, and many more existences privileged enough to watch today's scenes looking towards the pair of existences with fervent eyes.

"Today, we were reminded of the power of Primordial Beasts as after this...we will redouble our efforts as we won't just protect the Primordial Empire- we will actively hunt these creatures until we wipe them from existence!"

"...!"

Glorious words rained down.

There was a second of a delay before...

"OOOOH!" The masses listening to him roared out in fervor!

So what if nearly a third of their Cosmos were lost? What if there were Betrayers in their camp? As of today, all those who didn't stand with them were purged as after all this, the Primordial Empire was bound to be stronger than ever!

"My Champion..." In Aegon's arms, Havenbreaker smiled in a dazzling manner as her voice entered his ears and continued to shower him with utter sweetness.

The two lovers held each other closely as on this day, apart from the Emperor of the Primordial Empire, an Empress would also rise!

A time for restructuring and healing had come as the Primordial Empire rose in another direction, destiny continuing to flow as it wished as far from the sight of two lovers in the middle of the Cosmic Battlefield, there was a single being from far away that was smiling at this scene!

In the pristinely white Primordial Sea, the grand figure of the brutal and magnificent Hyperion glanced at a line up of Daolords and Antiquities from the House of Stormdust that were salvaged and brought over here with him, golden blood flowing into these creatures as their Origins and souls were changed into chimeric products.

While Hyperion oversaw this, the image of two lovers appeared in his mind as he smiled, his ancient voice echoing out.

"The seeds are already sprouting as all there is left now is for them to bloom into something beautiful..."

...!

The essence of Anarchy and a myriad of other completed Edicts rotated madly around this being's Origin as his ancient eyes shone with a devilish light, his plans only known to him at this moment!

In the Ruination Sea some light years away from the Primordial Empire.

The angry figure of Springforge could be seen surrounded by the Forefathers, Daolords, Antiquities, and Hegemonies that chose to accept her light.

She was gazing angrily towards a calm looking Halcyon, and then her eyes turned towards the materializing Tyrant on the Throne of Tyranny as her voice snapped out.

"This is the result that leads us to save our Reality? Having division occur in the only Empire that can withstand the assault of even a Cosmic Primordial Beast?!"

Her voice was filled with anger as even though she believed Halcyon and in extension Noah, she was still someone that had just lost her possession of 1,000 Cosmos and countless individuals under her!

She rightfully had the right to anger as the newly teleporting Noah gazed at her and those around her as they didn't consist of any of the beings that were already Noah's Vassals- these existences having already been transported to a certain fantastical location.

"And now we are wandering in the Ruination Sea! Where shall we go? What shall we do?! Back to the Cosmos you stemmed from...is that the answer?" She ground her teeth while she spoke as she only saw Noah looking at her with a curious smile, realizing this being wasn't even looking at this situation as seriously as she was as it annoyed her to no end!

But before she could continue any further, Halcyon stepped forward and glared at Noah upon his floating golden purple throne.

"Before we proceed, you wish to bind us to you...yes?" The Essence of the Nomological Edict of Kairos danced around her eyes as she spoke, Noah smiling lightly towards this unique being as the essence of the Dao of Fealty waved out from him in glorious cascading waves.

"There is an answer to all your questions, and a beautiful place for you to see...but you will first have to pledge Fealty!"

WAA!

His voice echoed out across the Ruination Sea as the millions behind Springforge and even this past faction leader herself looked towards him in shock while staring at the prompt appearing before their eyes.

Yet before any of them could reply, their eyes watched an unfolding and utterly shocking scene of the elegant and beautiful golden haired Halcyon trying to bend towards the being on the throne with her voice echoing out.

"I pledge Fealty to the..."

...!

Before she could kneel, the strong hands of the Tyrant lifted up her chin as his resounding voice echoed out.

"You do not need to kneel to me, Halcyon. Just your Fealty is enough!"

A light of power and destiny burst out as Fealty bound one being to another, Springforge and the others watching the scene of the powerful and unobtainable figure of Halcyon having her chin elevated to look at a single man on a throne as her eyes...her eyes didn't seem to have space for anybody else!
