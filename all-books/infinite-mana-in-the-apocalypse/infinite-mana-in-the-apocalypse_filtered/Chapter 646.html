Ultimate Laws!

Laws that even his own galaxy did not have the techniques to cultivate, but this Nexus Galaxy that had lived for countless eons held it!

Noah listened to the words of the Imperial Princess as he nearly sighed, repeating the fact that Ultimate Laws were located in the Third Floor, and his title as a High Imperial at this moment only allowed him to access up to the second floor .

The Imperial Princes noticed his forlorn expression as he stared up the Imperial Library, her voice ringing out in a surprised manner .

"Youre already trying to jump to Ultimate Laws? You have access to the floors that hold the techniques of all other laws up to Supreme, you should even find great techniques for your comprehension in the law of Aether there . "

Noah nodded at her words as he seemed to be thinking of something else .

"Well you see, I always wanted to learn an Ultimate Law since I am such a genius in lower-tier laws . Now that Im here, I cant help but feel like it is within my reach but still so far away . "

"Haha!"

A slight laugh escaped from the Imperial Princess as she shook her head while staring at the man who seemed to have missed out on a huge treasure right in front of his eyes, his words speaking about himself like some kind of a genius that could easily comprehend laws . The maids Maya and Raya looked towards him with slight contempt as they thought he was showing off again .

Hmph, wanting to embark on an Ultimate Law when you havent even mastered normal or supreme ones? Hes definitely a boaster!

It wont be so easy for you to show off in front of the Princess . A genius in laws? Ha!

The thoughts of the two sisters were in sync as they looked at each other, shocking words coming out of the mouth of the Imperial Princess soon after .

"Well if you are so curious about Ultimate Laws and are the genius you say you are, I can grant access to the Third Floor for you just once . "

...!

Noahs eyes opened wide as he looked towards the veiled figure that he could distinctly feel was smiling with an expression of challenge . It seemed she was curious to see if he was just talking big about wanting to learn Ultimate Laws, and she would even use her status to grant him access so could see exactly what he was made of!

If things were really like that...

"Ill owe you a favor if you can make something like that happen, Princess . "

His eyes twinkled as the veiled figure nodded, the two maids shaking their heads full of complaints as they knew not to speak out once their Princess had made her mind .

With out minding the gazes of the many beings around them, they ascended up the steps of the grand Imperial Library as its golden arched doors were wide open, a thin film of transparent white light being the only thing present!

Noahs new Imperial Emblem shone as he neared and passed through the barrier into the First Floor of the Library with no problems, following the leading Imperial Princess and her maids forward as his eyes looked around him .

All around him, rows and rows of bookshelves could be seen as he could not even ascertain their numbers . Far off in the distance of this first floor, he could notice a glimmering blue portal surrounded by multiple Imperial Guards .

"The Inheritances of normal Laws can be found once you go through that protected blue portal, the same thing being present on the upper floors . "

Maya explained with a cold voice as instead of staying on the first floor, the four of them had begun taking the spiral stairway leading to the second floor . A similar scene as the first floor repeated as Noah saw the essence of space vibrantly permeating through this floor, its size somehow expanded to be even larger than the first floor as it nearly doubled in size even though it did not look like it from the outside .

This second floor should have been the last place he could access, where one would come across Supreme Laws! But he had the powerful Imperial Princess leading the way as her very presence caused murmurs to spread out in the quiet library, many people committing the figure of Noah who was accompanying her to their memory as they planned to find out who he was!

The four of them came upon the spiral stairway leading to the third floor as Noah saw it was protected by a single Blessed, this protector having a vibrant aura at the realm of Black Hole as he blocked the way towards the third floor where only Imperial Lords could access!

He saw the Imperial Princess that came ever so closer to him as his eyes shook, standing aside quickly as he bowed, but his aura also locked towards Noah at the same time .

"Princess..."

His gaze was questioning as he glanced towards Noah, the Imperial Princess waving her hands as a cold voice rang out from her .

"Librarian Shaun . This is my guest, and will be allowed access just this once . "

Her words came out cold and domineering, seeming more like a command to this expert at the Realm of Black Hole as Librarian Shaun nodded while bowing even deeper!

The barrier that only Imperial Lords could cross using their Emblems opened up in front of Noah at this time as he and the three women made their way to the third floor . Shocked gasps and envy could be seen in the eyes of many as they watched this scene, the information systems of many Nobles being put in play on this night as they all went to figure out the identity of the being taken to the 3rd Floor of the Imperial Library by the Princess .

Majestic and ancient were the only words to describe the third floor, being nearly empty as Noah could not sense a single soul on it!

It did not even have any guards stationed at the glimmering portals that shone with a pure green and oppressive dark luster, each of the two portals signifying a different Ultimate Law as they lay on opposing sides of the floor .

"I dont want to make my old man angry so Ill only give you a chance for one or the other . Death or Life, what will it be?"

The voice of the Imperial Princess rang out sonorously as her veiled figure turned towards Noah, nobody being able to see the expectant eyes under this veil that wondered what surprise this being would have in store for them!

If his words were just lies and he was just boasting in front of her this entire time, she would be very disappointed as she very quickly cut off her connection with this being .

But her eyes were expectant because . . . this persons figure seemed to have an unbounded sense of confidence that simply could not be faked!

As the eyes of the maids and the Imperial Princess honed in on him, Noah turned his face towards the vibrant green and the intense dark portal that led to two different Ultimate Laws .

Life or Death . . . which would it be?!
