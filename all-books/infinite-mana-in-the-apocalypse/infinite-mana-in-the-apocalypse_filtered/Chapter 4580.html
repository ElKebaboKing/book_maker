Chapter 4578: The First Tongue IX

The liquid mass generated silver white flames that shifted between solid and ethereal just like its own form!

All three pillars converged on THE First Hunger and THE Living Origin with devastating force.

Tors eyes widened with genuine alarm as he felt the concentrated power bearing down on them. This was by far the strongest attack they had faced in this entire miserable encounter!

THE Living Origin was already moving, her Way of Origin manifesting as defensive chains that attempted to bind the incoming flames to their foundational state before combustion. White gold barriers formed in intricate geometric patterns.

The collision created spectacular explosions of conflicting authorities as fundamental concepts warred against each other.

Fire tried to consume. Hunger tried to devour. Origin tried to reduce everything to its source state.

The landscape around them scorched and cracked from the sheer intensity of power being exchanged. Reality itself groaned under the pressure of so many high level Principles activating simultaneously.

But despite the terrifying nature of the attack, both THE entities proved their competence and vast experience. Through coordinated effort and masterful application of their Ways, they managed to defend against and systematically disperse the converging pillars of flame.

"Okay. Okay! Thats it!" he roared with voice shaking the environment. "I tried to hold back somewhat in this damned suppressive place, but no more restraint!"

They were holding back somewhat. Because they knew...one should not easily kill a Malformed Ginnu Lifeform! One Ginnu killed would have one branded and hunted down by all other Ginnu as they would not survive Ginnungagap!
