Chapter 1112 - You Shouldn't Be Here! II

In the extremely tense unfolding situation, the Great Usurper continued to gaze up with anger as his eyes flashed coldly, his voice continuing to ring out.

"I shouldnt be here...and nor should you!"

RUMBLE!

Against an existence that caused shock and awe just from her appearance alone, the Antiquity didnt back down as in reply to this as at this moment, the vast Universes within his bodies vibrated immensely as he silently cast a certain skill.

It was a skill that would come at a great cost, but he used it nonetheless as he somewhat understood the extent of the ridiculous being that had appeared before him. As for how this existence had appeared?

A golden light of fate and destiny crossed through the eyes of the Antiquity as he discerned a few things, his eyes ultimately giving a barely discernible glance to the figures of Hegemonies below him as he silently cast the skill.

Yet, it seemed as if the existence above could have nothing escape from her eyes as the moment the Antiquity made this move, this existence also moved!

DING!

A gloved hand rose from the newly appearing figure as she tapped a few times in the void of space.

DING! DING! DING!

With every tap, resplendent white and crimson lines erupted out by the millions, these thin white and crimson lines causing Noahs eyes to widen as they vibrated with Ruination and Primordial Essence!

Millions of these white and crimson lines bloomed as they went on to instantaneously wrap around the figure of the Antiquity, beginning to circle around his figure rapidly as the entire area turned utterly colorful.

Yet even with such a move being made, the Antiquity only looked towards the newly appearing being with anger as he didnt make a move.

This was because he had no need to.

Just as Noah and all the others knew they couldnt do anything to the being above...so did the Antiquity!

Even though he seemed angered by her appearance, the extent of his actions was to silently cast a certain skill! His Blue eyes were palpable with anger as he watched the veiled figure that released their voice again.

"You do not belong in this Dimension. For your violation, you shall be sent to the Primordial Battlefields for 10 Million Years."

WAA!

The voice was as impassive as ever, causing a chill to run down everyones spines when they heard it!

But the angered Great Usurper actually smiled at this time, his blue eyes leaving the veiled figure above as he stared down at Oathkeeper once more.

At the Cosmic Treasure he had sacrificed many Universes to try and obtain.

Then, his eyes couldnt help but wander over to Noah and the Apocryphal Antiquities surrounding him.

Then...his voice rang out!

"I think not."

...!

After gazing upon the beings of this Cosmos he raised his head again as from his deeply blue eyes, one could see them beginning to emanate shocking cracks that very quickly spread out to cover his face!

"Among the many things I will do...falling under the control of you hypocrites is not one of them."

The moment his words finished, Noah and everyone else watched in shock as a spectacular sight unfolded before their eyes. The beautifully blue eyes of the Antiquity had begun to crack, but the more shocking things were the Splendiferous Universes and large numbers of normal Universes that became manifested at this moment.

Every Hegemony looked at such a sight with envy and greed as this was what they searched for!

The sight of multiple Universes within an Origin was what they all sought, as such a thing represented Antiquity! Yet they were shocked to find that these wondrous manifested Universes from the Origin of this Antiquity...were actually burning.

Burning luminously in the dark void as they caused for the appearance of a beautiful scene!

The Hegemonies that had regained control of their bodies scrambled away at this moment as they thought this brazen Antiquity was about to self destruct with an attack to wipe them all, but no such thing happened as everything continued silently.

His Origin continued to burn as the Universes within it were being used up of all of their pristine essence for a use that nobody knew!

Only the existence above them seemed to have an idea as her fingers tapped out again.

DING!

The millions of lines made of Primordial and Ruination Essence stretched out as after circling the Antiquity, they shot into him like sharp needles as they sunk into his skin and Origin seamlessly.

BZZT!

It should have been an action that caused this being immense pain, and yet he remained silent as his Origin burned away silently, the cracks stemming from his eyes stretching to his as he looked like a porcelain figure that was about to break.

"Hmm?"

Even the existence seemed to find this unique as the lines of Primordial Essence and Ruination she sent out should have stopped this being from doing anything, yet whatever he had done seemed unstoppable!

He instead only silently gazed up towards this existence as he focused on the twelve-pointed star on her dress, his words ringing out calmly.

"Hypocrites are all that you are...sitting upon your high pedestals and gazing upon everything currently happening as if you are above it all, even though you are the ones responsible."

RUMBLE!

"I know the secret you all hide so well...I know what you all did!"

...!

His cold and decisive words shot towards the veiled figure as this existence finally reacted when such things were mentioned to her.

WAA!

Behind her, flowers of Ruination and Primordial Essence bloomed as the millions of thin lines that bound the Antiquity shone brightly and began to move this being up, a majestic aura descending all around that even caused Noah and the Apocryphal Antiquity that was the Blue Slime to feel stifled!

At this moment, the bitingly cold voice of the newly appearing existence thrummed down gloriously.

"What can a failed creature like you know? You have outlived your purpose as of this moment...and can cease to exist."

RUMBLE!
