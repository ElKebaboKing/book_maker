Chapter 3858: Early Veiled Shore I

The Crucible shuddered. The entirety of the Early Living Transcendent Infiniverse Wheel of Existence shuddered!

The prompt repeated again!

|The Living Origin, Ruination, has made a Declaration of Living Existential Proliferation targeting the emergence of the shred of the musings of an Early Veiled Shore.|

The Hymns paused, as if to make room for something older the moment that Ruination joined hands with the Infiniverse and enacted a Proliferation!!

|Shreds have accumulated.|

|Shreds have been multiplied.|

|Shreds have bloomed and turned into actualizations.|

The sky above them cracked with golden threads of forgotten purpose. Veins of Paradox, Origin, and more!

|Through the hands of the Living Origin, Ruination, the Early Transcendent Infiniverse Living Wheel of Existence, and your aura and distinction as an Early Creature, the most rudimentary and natal version of an Early Veiled Shore, a Budding Shore, shall bloom at the center of the Infiniverse.|

HUUM!

One explosive string of information after another came!

At the center of the Infiniverse Wheel of Existence, he felt the shift as something subtle and massive rooted itself- like the planting of a World Tree without trunk or seed.

Noahs breath was steady. But his heart... his heart sang with quintessence.

He looked at his companions- Ruination glowing, clasping her sisters hand, and the Infiniverse, floating gloriously, her gaze flicking toward him as if to say...

Witness us!

Because the two of them working together made way for the rapid emergence of something unfathomable!

At the center of the Infiniverse Wheel of Existence, where Spokes once merely represented Frequencies, now stood embryonic Towers covering them.

And from those rising bones of Existence, light- raw, unfiltered light of Continuity, of Dreams, of Genesis and Relativity and so much more- gathered toward the Wheels very heart.

It was not a gathering of stars.

It was not even a singularity in the traditional sense.

It was something... older.

Noahs breath pulsed as he felt his Early Living Towers of Origin resonate. Their hymns bled out in reverent chorus, pouring endless torrents of Living Origin Authority toward that spiraling point at the Wheels center. The call was not forceful, but inevitable.

Drawn forward without resistance, Noahs body vanished from the Crucible.

Ruination followed him with a pulse of crimson gold.

The Infiniverse Body glided like a comet streaking behind, multicolored rivers of existence dancing in her wake.

They left the Infiniverse Crucible!

Together, they arrived at the center of the Wheel of Existence!

Before it.

The singularity had bloomed the moment they appeared.

But it did not expand in a show of grandeur.

It compressed into something impossibly refined.

Only a thousand miles in diameter!

In a place where omniverses were considered rooms, where galaxies were mere verses in a longer song... this was small. Humble. Quiet.

And yet, the power emanating from it churned every fold of their being.

The air was thick with Living Existential Authority, but twisted through it all was mist.

White-gray fog, dense and slow-moving, forming lazy spirals around the new-formed land. There was no thunder. No cataclysm. Just this mist...and its scent. Primal. Ancient.

Foreign.

Even to him.

|The Budding Shore, an early stage of an Early Veiled Shore, has begun emerging.|

|It awaits for an Early Creature to step into it to unlock its full emergence and wonders.|

...!

What would it mean?

What would a land belonging to beings like him truly offer?

What did the memory of home feel like for a race unknown and unheard of?

He floated forward.

The mist parted for him, not with resistance, but reverence. Every tendril of fog curled around him like it had known him once, long ago, and had been waiting patiently for his return.

He went past the impenetrable fog.

And then his feet met the sand.

Warm.

Unyielding.

Alive. Very much alive!

The moment he stepped foot upon the shore, it was as though time hiccuped, then rearranged itself to welcome him.

|You have unlocked an Early Veiled Shore.|

|A consecrated land where anyone stepping on it will become Living Existence (Randomized to the types of Living Existences currently bound to you).|

|Current Maximum Population Occupancy: 25.|

BOOM!

Noah didnt move as he absorbed the shocking prompt!

He let the grainy softness of the golden sand shift beneath his boots. The wind here was gentle, not oppressive. It did not howl or shriek like the storms in many other Domains of power. It sang. Whistled. A quiet lullaby, carried by threads of primal magic embedded in the air and land alike.

He looked up.

The coastline curved gracefully, embraced by wide calm currents of water that shimmered crystalline blue.

The sea here did not stretch to the horizon. It ended abruptly at the mists edge- an outer rim of fog that bordered the entire Budding Shore. It was not water that claimed its boundaries, but ambiguity.

Beyond that fog? No one knew.

The land was untamed. Simple. No architecture. No cities. No structures left behind from ancient builders. Just untouched grasslands, the color of deep summer, and vibrant green vegetation sprouting in graceful waves across the interior.

At the very center, three trees stood. Just three.

Not tall.

Not wide.

But distinct.

Each one shimmered with strange resonance, their bark lined with faint glowing runes, their leaves tinted with shades that mimicked Living Origin Authority. They stood in a small clearing, rooted in soil that pulsed slightly underfoot as though the land itself breathed.

Noah felt the breath and air of this place that caused his Living Existential Authorities to churn and cycle faster than even the deepest concentration of authorities in the Transcendent Paradoxical Folds!

Denser authority than...even the Paradoxical Dyson Sphere!

He felt such dense authority rise to meet his own.

Behind him, Ruination touched the sand and gasped.

The Infiniverse floated beside her, pausing mid-step as her radiant toes brushed the coastline.

Noah moved forward, and as he walked, his gaze fell to the clear waters nearby. The coast sparkled with light that should not exist in such simplicity- pale blues, iridescent whites, faint golds all dancing beneath the tide like stardust fallen into the sea.

He knelt.

Touched the surface.

The water rippled away, serene and cool.

|You have observed the Early Sacred Water of the Budding Shore. Drinking it aids in rising ones Purity, but one must not drink too quickly, else the Early Intoxication will slowly erase their sense of self and existence. One drop of the Early Sacred Water is enough to raise ones Purity from nothing to Originus Venerant.|

Noah froze.

One drop.

A single drop, and a normal human could attain the purity of an Originus Venerant entity!

And the cost if one drank too much...was the loss of self. The fraying of who you are in pursuit of what you might become.

His fingers remained dry even while playing with these waters.

He turned his back to the sea and faced the heart of the Budding Shore. The trees. The soil. The mist. A sanctuary born not just from authority or power, but from a convergence of far too many quintessential possibilities.

He felt the weight settle on his shoulders.

Responsibility.

Possibility.

Belonging.

The Budding Shore pulsed again.

And the mists thickened ever so slightly...as if something unseen within the fog had begun to stir!
