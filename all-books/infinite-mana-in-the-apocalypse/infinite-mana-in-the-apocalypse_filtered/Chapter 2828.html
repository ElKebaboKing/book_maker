2826 The Dungeon System of Extremity! IV

Reality warped and twisted around them as they entered the Obsidian Difficulty of the Existential Raid Rift and found themselves teleported into a vast and ancient Record.

Around them, shattered Royal Elysium Cosmos floated all around as in the distance, a golden sea seethed with glory!

Atop this Sea, the auras of hundreds of Enders of Extremity at the First and Second Gradations of Extremity could be seen, with there being dozens ofEnders at the Third Gradation of Extremity that uniquely took on the forms of the Reality Ursidae, Infinite Willow Trees, Dragon-Phoenix, and the Astral Race.

And at the forefront of these Enders at the Third Gradation of Extremity, there was actually a man.

A man that currently seemed to be steeped in darkness as his hair flowed with an obsidian light, and her his eyes shone with a resplendent golden hue of Destiny.

His stature was made to be larger than even the Enders of Extremity themselves as he was a few thousand miles in size- being equivalent to a Small World in stature.

When Henry, Kazuhiko, Emperor Penguin, and others saw him, their gazes changed to ones of shock as they remembered the extremely handsome face of this entity.

They...had even hunted down this Entity before!

[How befitting for you to be the first one I come across...O Son of Destiny.]

The voice of this Entity echoed out resoundingly as it ridiculously stood at the Fourth Gradation of Extremity.

The Extremity of Prana flowed across his Veins as with any Raid Boss, his Vitality Values were greatly inflated as killing him would require blood, sweat, and tears!

Tendrils of destiny swirled around him as seeing the shock of his opponents, this entity continued.

[I am despondent and even more surprised than you all are. I came back merely as a Record of my old self. A glimpse of what I used to be after my greatest Enemy yet...merely sent his son to kill me as he couldn't even be bothered. He had grown so powerful that even if I considered him a great enemy, to him...I was merely an Extra. And now, I truly am a Quintessential Extra that has merely taken the place of an Ender of Extremity. But even though I am not truly me...even though I am merely a collection of Records...

the Extremity of Destiny courses through and resonated with me.]

HUUM!

As the man spoke, the golden seas below him seethed as the Enders of Extremity behind him roared.

His gaze came to lock onto the Son of Destiny in the distance as a massive golden spear appeared in his hands.

[I never reached the Extremity of Destiny, but it flows across every single part of my Record due to the Will of Infinite Hyperversal Haven- due to your Father's Will. And I wish to see whether I truly deserved to be killed by you. I wish to see whether you have simply been riding on the Destiny of the one responsible for all of this, or if you can actually forge your own.]

WAA!

His presence was massive and oppressive as nobody had expected to ever see this being again, nor in such a position of power!

Even if he was just a Record and not the past being himself...this was still the past True Emperor that had reigned in the Age of Destiny.

The Past True Emperor of Destiny.

The Empyral Deceiver!

Oppenheimer!

HUUM!

Infused with the authority of an Ender of Nature, 3 Natural Laws and 3 Decreta flowed through him as at this moment, a heavy prompt rose.

[Prove your concepts of Extremity and take down the Obsidian Raid Boss- Oppenheimer. Failure to do so will result in a complete wipe out and restriction of using the Existential Raid Rift for 12 hours.]

...!

Henry gazed at this prompt as he felt his blood burn.

He felt as if his Father- no, he felt as if the very Infinite Hyperversal Haven was testing him.

After all, this couldn't be random.

And if this was a test...they it wasn't one that he could fail!

[On me!]

He bellowed out commandingly as those behind him erupted with auroras and blazing auras that glimmered with Quintessence or Infinity.

RAA!

An Emperor Penguin enlarged as with a bellow, it and many others followed the lead of the Son of Destiny and charged towards a golden sea filled with Enders of Extremity and a supercharged Oppenheimer in the lead!

This...was the inside of one version- one record of an Obsidian Difficulty.

One by one, other parties had begun to enter the Golden Difficulty as they tested their own power and luck as at this moment...the usage, refinement, and elevation of the Existential Extremity Authorities under Noah began in full swing.

The means of the Extremity of Systems were shown to be terrifying as it worked intricately with the Infinite Hyperversal Haven to show off a glimpse of its current power.

It's shocking means regarding Alchemy and Blacksmithing weren't even unveiled as currently....just its sheer capability of producing terrifying Enders of Extremity advanced in the Gradations of Extremity through its infusion of Natural Laws and Decreta...this alone was enough to make any construct terrifying.

But the Infinite Hyperversal Haven wasn't any construct.

It was something supported by an Extremity Vein quire literally with the shape of an infinity symbol as its pillars of support that were the Natural Laws and Decreta...were all pulled towards Extremity.

All at the level of Quintessential Zenith.

It then held Existential Extremity Authorities flowing through it and constantly altering its very Existence.

It then had the Quintessential Annals of Extremity basking it and elevating it to an even greater level!

Its origin was a mere Spiritual Land, yes.

But currently...it was something that even Noah may have not yet fully ascertained as what it could currently do was already obscene to ridiculous margins!

And in the midst of all of this...

[MacGuffin rises from its slumber.]

...!

Outside of the Existential Raid Rift he had just called out, Noah saw this prompt appearing as he felt a part of himself behind to pulse wildly.

[The Extremity of MacGuffin has made its choice.]

[The Dungeon System of Extremity shall be the first idea- the first construct that future plot will revolve around.]

...!

The first MacGuffin was chosen in such a shocking manner as even though this was his own Will, even Noah was surprised!

But in the next moment...

[Even more Concepts of Extremity surge to support the Dungeon System of Extremity.]

[The Extremity of Conquest, Plot, Apocalypse, Devouring, Gluttony, Armageddon, Wrath, and many others infuse their Extremity Authority into the Dungeon System of Extremity.]

WAA!

[The methodology of stripping away the authority of the Vacuous and Vitalis Hyperversal Domains begins to unveil itself.]

[The Existential Dungeon Apocalypse feature is coming to fruition!]
