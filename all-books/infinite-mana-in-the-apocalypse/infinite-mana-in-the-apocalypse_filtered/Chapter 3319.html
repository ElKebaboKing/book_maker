Chapter 3317 What an Eye! I

[Seventh Body of Savitar]:: State of Existence- Quasi-Eye of Existence. Source of Existence- Outerversal. Lifespan- 18 Megalos Cycle/70 Megalos Cycles. Records of Weavings- The Seventh Body of Savitar that was reconstructed through the hands of a great being. Its power exceeds any of its other bodies apart from the Main One....

...!

An extrapolation of records of Savitar unfolded under Noah's gaze as his weavings underwent a tremendous change!

He looked at the obsidian golden skeleton visage of Savitar who currently had a dark expression as he uttered out.

|White doorways have appeared within all the Tainted Omniverses I oversee...and likely here as well. But these gateways reject anything relating to Outerversal Authority!|

His voice carried great frustration with it as he looked towards Noah inquisitively as if he was seeking for more information.

And Noah laughed lightly as he waved his hands.

"It's not an opportunity for the Outerversal Morphons. We had the weavings of the Great Beyonder stretch out and empower us; the white portals are simply an opportunity that someone of equivalent power, if not greater than the Great Beyonder, had placed for the masses of the Megalos Ynnere."

...!

His words caused the flames within Savitar to blaze heavily as he asked in a low tone.

|Were you successful in taking a Delegatus across it? The Outerversal Authority within them was masked?|

The One Who Walks asked some critical questions that Noah did not answer.

He only waved his hands dismissively.

"Who knows. Forget about that. You've actually lived for 18 Megalos Cycles, and you've only attained this stage of power? Even the Eye of Existence, you have only stepped into it halfway? Just what exactly designated you to be a Great One?"

...!

Word after word that caused the flames within Savitar to flicker in shock and then subsequent horror echoed out.

|You...you...!| He was too baffled by the things Noah knew!

How could he know how many cycles he had been alive? How could he know his exact state of power?!

HUUM!

"Let's see..."

Noah went even further as above him, a terrifying golden eye opened.

Along with it, prompts cascaded down.

|The Sacred Primal Eye of Existence- Pyrrhus, has turned its gaze onto the target before it.|

|The weavings of the selected target are laid bare.|

Savitar trembled as he felt like a terrifying gaze had landed on him, and everything about him turned extremely clear as if it was all beings perused!

And he...couldn't do anything to stop it.

"Born near the Megalos Pantheon of Zeitland, you were only a little cub all those cycles ago. But look at you now..."

WAA!

His voice seemed to hold traces of interminable power.

His mere voice caused Savitar, a supposed Great One, to shudder as he felt like if he kept listening, his whole history- all of his records would be told right in front of him!

|Who...no, what are you? What do you really want?!|

Savitar asked with a sense of trepidation as he realized he truly didn't understand this entity before him.

Looking at the terrifying golden Eye of Existence that caused his very weavings to shudder with pressure, he realized that he truly knew too little about him!

At this moment, the golden eye of this being pressed its heavy pressure on him as his voice echoed out passively.

"I don't need partners. Only followers. You, O One Who Walks, get to decide at this moment whether you wish to be a follower."

The power within his voice was potent.

But it was that eye.

That terrifying golden Eye!

That was the thing that caused Savitar the most fear as he felt like everything about him would be discovered sooner or later.

|I...I don't...|

"DECIDE!"

A single word boomed out as shockingly, the illusory golden eye above Noah's head opened its pupils wider than ever before golden lightning moving many times faster than the speed of light smashing into Savitar instantly as his figure froze.

|The Sacred Primal Eye of Existence- Pyrrhus, has imposed one of its capabilities on the target named Savitar.|

|Existence Suppression has been utilized for the first time.|

|Due to their lower complexity of existence and state of being compared to you, the target before you was not able to evade the effects of Existence Suppression. You can now choose to seal the complexity of Existence of this body of the selected target, and establish a link towards other parts of its weavings. If your gaze lands on their other Records as well, the effects of Existence Suppression can be applied freely.|

...!

The...sealing of the complexity of existence?

Sealing.

Sealing!

It was too profound to see as Noah couldn't help but wish to give it a go!

Instantly, Savitar felt his existence...begin to forcefully shrink.

His power.

His weavings. His records.

His Outerversal Authority.

Everything about him felt like it was becoming smaller- as if it was being shut behind a door he could never open no matter how he wished to in the future!

He looked around him in sheer terror as he noticed the weight of an undeniable golden light- a light that didn't just affect this body, but could eventually go on to affect all his other bodies- even his main one.

...!

And his existence inherently felt the end result of all this would be...his entire weavings of existence sealed to the point that he would be even more powerless than the day he was born.

He would be like the entities here considered less than nothing- the tiny humans who lived within the depths of Cosmos and Universes who didn't even know how to wield authority!

This...this filled him with such terror that couldn't even be brought by death as his golden obsidian body instantly bent itself down.

His aurora of wings that had recently sprouted behind him and his thousands of limbs all bent down in sheer terror as his voice groveled out!

|Follower! I will be a follower!|

HUUM!

With a face filled with terror, a Great One yelled out.
