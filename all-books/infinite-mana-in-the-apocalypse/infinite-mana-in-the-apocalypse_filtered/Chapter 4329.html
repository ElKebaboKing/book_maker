"Okay," he said, his voice a quiet, satisfied hum. "The Codex is coming along nicely. Let us go and show others what the Way of Mana truly means, okay?"

Being overpowered.

Many...misunderstand the very idea of this concept itself!

Its like the difference between imagining being rich (yacht parties and endless vacation) versus actually being rich (mostly tax documents and people asking for money).

Most believe being overpowered means winning every fight, solving every problem with a gesture, having Existence bend to your whims. They imagine it as freedom from consequence, the ultimate power fantasy where youre basically playing life with cheat codes enabled.

This...was a fundamental misunderstanding of the burden of being overpowered!

Some say that truly overpowered beings understand something the power-seekers dont: strength isnt freedom- its prison. The stronger you become, the smaller your world gets. Every increase in power is a decrease in things that can matter to you.

This is why truly overpowered beings often become either utterly detached or desperately attached to tiny, trivial things. THE Creature playing with Early Creatures and Fold Dwellers like pets. THE Living Paradox obsessing over THE Creature!

Some overpowered beings cling to whatever can still provide meaning in an existence that has become tissue paper to their touch.

What it truly means to be overpowered is to be disconnected from the very fabric of existence you sought to master.

It was winning so thoroughly that theres no game left to play. Its being so strong that strength itself...becomes meaningless.

Some believe that those who are genuinely overpowered dont seek more power. They seek limitations, weaknesses, something...anything, that can make them feel connected to existence again.

The goal of becoming overpowered is to never have to struggle again.

But struggle...is what gives existence meaning.

So when someone says they want to be overpowered, what they really mean is they want to be just powerful enough to overcome their current problems. Actually being overpowered? Well...this was hard to conceptualize!
