2531 The Curtains Draw Close! IV

In a blinding stellar region filled with churning liquified essence of Dissolution distorting space constantly.

This essence felt vile and majestic at the same time as any being with weak Aspects of Existence nearing it could be corroded in a short period of time, and rivers of this essence seemed to be extending from all directions like endless branches and joining together into a vast sea- a sea that formed an interminable wall before the eyes of all beings who had managed to make it this far as it had no end in sight.

The wall seemed liquid and solid at the same time as it was akin to countless branches intersecting together...as if it was the junction between an outer layer of the Vein of Dissolution and an inner layer!

Within the veins of Existences, they were composed of the tunica adventitia which was the outer layer, the tunica media which was the middle layer, and the tunica intima which was the innermost layer lined up by smooth endothelial cells.

This region...seemed akin to the junction from the outer and into the middle layer!

In between the surging conjoining rivers and branches of Dissolution, Obsidian Extremity Panaceas could be seen floating serenely as they dared any beings brave enough to attain them- with massive Leviathans of Dissolution nearing 1 Apex Aeonic Soul Values roaring out in this region as powerful Scions like Erikson, Atlas of the Plum Sword of Extremity, and Lodovico among others could be seen.

Their bodies released blinding radiance and power as they had forged their Extremity Foundations, beings like Atlas having even attained a Dynamis of Extremity as the power they released was enough to rival Leviathans of Dissolution who were almost nearing a singular Apex Aeonic Value!

They still fought as apart from Obsidian Extremity Panaceas, there were also blinding singularities of Aeonic Treasures of Extremity that were either precious loot that allowed one to traverse towards the 10th Boundary Layer with ease, aid in forging a Dynamis of Extremity, or Panaceas that were useful in reinforcing one's foundation.

They fought tooth and nail as all of them felt the urgency that others had begun to openly mention the last few hours.

That the Initiation Step of Extremity Sanctification was close to an end!

That the Extremity Panaceas were becoming rarer to find unless one came in this region where a massive wall of a sea of dissolution stopped the advancement of any beings forward!

The reason for this could all be attributed to a single being.

BOOOM!

While others were struggling or grouping up against the Leviathans of Dissolution nearing the power of Apex Aeonic Lifeforms, any single one that he came across was bullied into oblivion as the visage of the Quintessential Emperor of Extremity had just ripped apart five Leviathans of Dissolution who were guarding a group of Loot filled singularities right before the endless wall of Dissolution.

The one to bring everything to an early end was none other than the True Emperor of Quintessence as he had his Reified Sources of Extremity sweep away all obstacles easily while obtaining an overabundance of Loot himself!

It was to the extent that apart from the Loot stemming from the two Apex Aeonic Lifeforms he had killed, Aeonic Treasures of Extremity that were conducive to elevating Boundaries, forming Dynamis of Extremity, and the accelerated Convergence of Dynamis of Extremity were within his hands in abundance.

Each of them held a myriad of names like the Blazing Hyphae of Extremity, Glacial Perennial Hartwig, Radiant Moon Grass, Singularity Torn Bud...and many more.

When categorizing them, Noah was able to quantify 233 Aeonic Treasures of Extremity geared towards helping the progression of Boundaries up to the 13th Boundary Layer, many others sharing this quality like the Blazing Hyphae of Extremity!

There were 198 Aeonic Treasures of Extremity geared towards increasing the chances of the successful Convergence of Boundaries into a Dynamis of Extremity- these beings things utterly useless to Noah as he would actually have to try really hard to be capable of failing in forming a Dynamis of Extremity.

But they would be invaluable for his people, so even they held their importance.

Lastly, he had scoured and attained 174 Aeonic Treasures of Extremity geared towards increasing the rate of Convergence of already formed Dynamis of Extremity!

It was a heavy haul for a single being, and this wasn't counting the Extremity Panaceas he collected for the Shadowlings of the Cult of Endless Shadows and others he kept for his own collection.

All in all, he had dominated the Scions that had risen up in this stage of the Initiation Step of Extremity Sanctification, and he now stood before a huge demarcation of a barrier that seemed to be something only Apex Aeonic Lifeforms could cross!

His eyes were full of calm as he had utilized the feature of [Adumbrative Viewpoint] greatly these past moments with the might of Alexander, finding out many more things in the unfolding future as he understood that this wall...would only begin to buzz and initiate a profound new process when the last set of Crimson Extremity Panaceas were plucked.

Yes.

A set!

He had seen this with his own will as his body in this intersection of a space filled with Dissolution turned away and gazed towards an entirely different direction.

The hidden space far away from here when the set of Crimson Extremity Panaceas would bloom...and where the visage of Ayame of Infinite Extremity was hidden.

The horn on her forehead shone with a Crimson gold glint at this moment as she seemed like a priestess of flames and honor, her figure hidden from others as the region she was in had been enacted with veils that masked the very Records of this area!

If others were to look- even Scions like Lodovico may be fooled into seeing an Altered Record than was currently happening.

Because as fewer and fewer Extremity Panaceas remained with tens of thousands of Scions struggling for even a smidgen of an edge in order to stand out on the stage of Apex Aeonic Lifeforms about to unfold, a heavy aura and a blinding crimson light had begun to blare from this region!

It was a light that would normally tell others and cause them to rush here to fight for one of the ultimate opportunities, but Ayame's fearsome means contained it all as she gazed towards a blooming Singularity with great expectations.

That is until...

WAP!

Her eyes flashed as her gaze changed, turning towards her left side as she saw the Altered Recorded she had set up be twisted as an existence calmly stepped in!

An air of majesty emanated from him as he was none other than the Reified Source of Dreams.

The Dreamer of Extremity carrying Noah's devilishly handsome visage and adorned in sandy gold robes appeared at this position that nobody else should have been able to discern!
