Chapter 5117: THE First Infinite Scale! II

Was his Infinity different compared to others?

Who could say. He had touched Infinity when he was nothing but a human throwing fireballs. He had integrated it so thoroughly into his existence that separation seemed impossible now. Perhaps the very act of claiming it so early, before he understood what he was grasping, had changed both him and what Infinity meant within his existence.

Noah rose up and stretched, his loose robe shifting around a body that blazed with Hadean authority.

"After cycling through my existence, maybe I tame the Infinity you all know entirely and make it something else. Maybe thats why I can fill myself and others with it, and no madness comes."

He looked toward Naldine with eyes that shone like singularities.

"But forget that. Use your Hadean Infinity of Development. See if you can rapidly transform your Proterozoic Bones to Hadean Bones."

...!

He was initially going to ask her what she knew about going from Calymmian to Ediacaran. That knowledge could have been valuable, would have given him insight into how Primordial Architects structured their progression through the tiers of THE Second Scale.

But he didnt want to be influenced too much on his own progression.

He was choosing to go in a direction that was entirely his own. Vakochevs Scales had served as a useful framework for understanding where others stood, but they werent his framework. They werent designed to account for what he was becoming. They werent built to measure someone who held Hadean classification while still technically at THE First Scale.

What he wanted to do now was complex and simple all at once.

"Ruination, let me see an outline of the ninth potential First Infinite Scale progression weve designed."

...!

Yes.

He had actually made blueprints of nine potential systems of progression and tweaked things as he went. Each iteration refined the concepts, eliminated redundancies, and brought him closer to something that felt right. He wanted to stop at the ninth one because otherwise he felt like he would have continued going until the eighty-first potential system design, and that much deliberation would have consumed time he didnt have.

The declaration of Civilizational Holy War pressed against the back of his mind. Twenty-four hours. Less now. Every moment spent designing was a moment not spent preparing his people for what was coming!

But this design would determine everything that followed.

|Displaying the ninth iteration of THE First Infinite Scale of Existence.|

Ruinations voice emerged as golden light coalesced before him, forming diagrams and notations that outlined his vision.

|You have structured your First Infinite Scale into three major demarcations, each representing a distinct phase of development before officially stepping toward what you will design later as THE Second Infinite Scale.|

|The foundational pillars of this progression are Infinity through Quintessence Infiniforce, Observable Force, and Civilizations as actualized building blocks through THE First Tongue.|

|First Demarcation: Infinitas Corpus, The Infinite Body.|

|This is where you currently stand at the peak. Your entire skeletal structure and organ systems have achieved Hadean classification. Your grasp of Infinity exists at the level of Uncountable Infinity, which saturates every aspect of your being without limit or boundary.|

|The requirements for completing this demarcation are: Full Hadean transformation of all 206 bones and 78 organs, stable integration of Uncountable Infinity throughout existence, and the capacity to channel both Quintessence Infiniforce and Observable Force simultaneously without conflict.|

|You have fulfilled all requirements. The First Demarcation is complete.|

|Second Demarcation: Infinitas Architectura, The Infinite Architecture.|

|This phase focuses on the construction of a singular internal structure that will serve as the foundation for everything that follows. You will build a Hadean Pillar of Infinity within your existence.|

|This single Pillar differs from conventional cultivation foundations in a critical way. Rather than constructing multiple separate pillars for different authorities, you will construct one Pillar that can be built up by as many Civilizations as you can actualize within it.|

|Through THE First Tongue, you can currently access and embody any Civilization temporarily. Infinitas Architectura requires you to move beyond temporary access toward permanent actualization. Each Civilization you congeal into stable form becomes another layer of your Hadean Pillar of Infinity, another foundation stone supporting the structure you are building.|

|There is no upper limit to how many Civilizations can be integrated into your Hadean Pillar. The more Civilizations you actualize within it, the grander and more stable the Pillar becomes. Observable Force serves as the binding agent holding these actualized Civilizations together. Quintessence Infiniforce serves as the animating principle that allows them to function as a unified whole rather than separate fragments.|

|THE Infiniverse can contribute to this process. Her own Hadean transformation provides templates for how internal structures can be organized. As her backbone becomes Hadean, resonance between your existences will accelerate the construction of your Pillar.|

|To complete this demarcation and progress to the third, you must achieve Absolute Infinity. This represents Infinity that has transcended mere uncountability to become self-referential and complete. Absolute Infinity can observe itself, can contain itself, can become both the container and the contained...well, potentially, we still dont know. Only when your grasp of Infinity reaches this level will the transition to your third demarcation become possible.|

|Third Demarcation: Infinitas Causa, The Infinite Cause.|

|This is the final phase of THE First Infinite Scale.|

|Having constructed your Hadean Pillar of Infinity and achieved Absolute Infinity, this demarcation focuses on the full comprehension and actualization of THE Infinite Cause that defines your existence. The chain of your Cause runs from your birth through your Infinity through the Seed of Observable Existence. This chain must be understood completely.|

|Your Hadean Pillar of Infinity must be saturated with the authority of your Cause. Every Civilization actualized within it must resonate with the fundamental truth of your emergence. The Pillar becomes not merely a structure of power but a monument to THE Infinite Cause itself.|

|Upon completing Infinitas Causa, you will stand at the peak of THE First Infinite Scale.|

|However, transition to THE Second Infinite Scale requires one final advancement.|

|Transcendent Infinity.|

|This level of Infinity lies beyond Absolute Infinity and can only potentially be attained during the transition itself. Transcendent Infinity represents Infinity that has become indistinguishable from Causality.|

|This advancement cannot be achieved while remaining at THE First Infinite Scale. It is the bridge between Scales, the transformation that marks the difference between standing at the peak and stepping beyond it.|

|I should note that this ninth iteration represents significant refinement over previous versions. Earlier designs attempted to incorporate too many separate structures, creating frameworks that would have been powerful but fragmented. This version maintains elegance through unity. One Pillar. Unlimited Civilizations. Infinity as the foundation for everything.|
