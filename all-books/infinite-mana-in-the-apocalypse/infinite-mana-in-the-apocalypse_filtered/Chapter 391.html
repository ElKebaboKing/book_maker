Chapter 390 - The First Treasure from the Celestial Realm

Noahs clone was staring blankly at the set of words that described something out of his wildest dreams!

<strong>[Origin Core- Behemoth Zygote] :: An incubating Origin that will eventually give rise to a pet that the user can bind to themselves. Caution is required as the position of pet and master can change if there is a large gap in Origin strength. Incubating time varies depending on location and amount of essence available.</strong>

Aside from the numerous Cores and Skill Books, the other thing to come out of the loot from the Draconic Fire Behemoth was an incubating egg that would actually gift him a Behemoth!

This was something unique that Noah was coming across for the first time, sending a message to the Planetary System as a reply was quickly sent back.

[The Celestial Beasts have extremely potent Origin Cores that are fused with their souls, making it capable to extract their origin and reproduce it into this form of being.]

Shocking words were transferred into Noahs mind as something huge was said so simply, nodding as he looked at the Incubation Time for this huge egg to pop. The time varied by location and the amount of essence, so he would see if it was possible to achieve some results today. His thoughts transferred from the loot as he now placed his attention towards the memories of the devoured behemoth that were slowly made clear, the hundreds of years worth of this monstrous beings life playing themselves out rapidly in his mind as he sat down on the back of an Abyssal Revenant to absorb all the information.

The life of this Celestial Beast was extremely unique, its first memories beginning while it was still in an egg when it first came to be in this same volcanic mountain! It very rarely left this home it found itself in, but it did move around over the last hundreds of years, which granted Noah a large amount of information on some of the nearby geography.

But the thing that made the Draconic Fire Behemoth stay in this volcanic mountain and continue to guard it all this time...was the resplendent red crystal that was attached atop the mystical ancient pedestal.

The eyes of the clone opened briefly as he looked at this pedestal not too far away from him that he could grab as the victor of the last battle, but he held himself back as he carefully perused through the memories to learn everything he could first.

Shockingly, the treasure in front of Noahs eyes was actually some type of Inheritance, something that very few beings would manage to come across in the Celestial Realm, but these Inheritances were some of the most valuable treasures!

It was this particular Inheritance that the Draconic Fire Behemoth found himself with since it was born that allowed it to reach the heights it had in the Law of Fire. When the memories came to this point in time, Noah finally got the bits of information that explained the unique {{Flame Lords Authority}} that the Dragon had managed to use to cancel out his Time Stop and the already cast [Emperor of Greed]. This ability that was called an authority...actually stemmed from achieving high comprehension in a particular Universal Law!

The Inheritance in this volcanic mountain had been an extremely powerful one from the Law of Fire, the staunch Behemoth choosing to practice it for hundreds of years as it advanced rapidly and got the strength that it had until today. To obtain this {{Authority}}, the Draconic Fire Behemoth had to actually advance and comprehend close to half of the Law of Fire.

This was something crazy to think about, as from the knowledge that Noah had gathered, very few beings in the Light Expanse were proficient enough to actually have comprehended even half of any laws. The only ones he could think of were the Celestials themselves, their power being boosted as they obtained a set of techniques in the Celestial Realm that taught them to be extremely proficient in the Law of Fate, with Noah still being unsure at the level they had reached after hundreds of years. But he did not doubt that there were many in this powerful organization that held some form of {{Authority}}!

Yet, this Inheritance in this volcanic mountain and the stern hard work of the smitten dragon that practiced it for years allowed this Behemoth to actually come close to attaining an {{Authority}} in the Law of Fire, which brought about the spectacular ability it used against Noah!

THUMP!

He could feel his heart beating faster as his gaze locked on to the glistening crystal atop the ancient pedestal, rising up from his position as he slowly pushed the newly flowing memories aside and neared this wondrous piece of treasure himself.

The ancient pedestal let off a mystical air of blistering flames as it was still standing calmly at the corner of the cavern, the crystal on top of it becoming larger and larger in Noahs eyes as he neared.

His eyes felt a searing pain as he came closer upon it, tears almost falling out as he came within a meter of it, and then an inch of it as he reached out and laid his hands upon it!

RUMBLE!

A brilliant red light shot out as extreme calm returned in the surroundings, Noah opening his eyes as the Planetary System worked to decipher the treasure in front of him, a radiant description appearing in front of Noahs eyes soon after.

<strong><<Inheritance of the Disciple of Fire. Includes techniques that allow one to inscribe the essence of fire into their bodies, as well as offensive and defensive techniques only those cultivating the Inheritance can use>></strong>

A set of beautiful words flowed in front of Noahs eyes as his excitement shot off to extreme levels. In his hands, his first treasure from this mystical Celestial Realm was giving off a powerful light as he placed more of his essence into it, keen to know exactly what awaited him from this <<Inheritance of the Disciple of Fire>>!
