Chapter 3161 Momentary Peace! II

Sitting side by side with their legs dipped into the golden waters of a pool, the figures of Lilith and Ayame remained filled with power and serenity as, at this moment, Lilith gazed at the suns of Helios far above while asking slowly.

"When was the last time you talked with him? From the Records you've shared with me, you moved together with him across Dissolution and Helios..."

A light of interest was flickering in her eyes as what she asked caused Ayame to shake her head while replying with a smile.

"I haven't personally spoken with him these past days. He feels like the same man I got to know briefly back then, but now...he also just feels too far away. I don't think I could look at or speak to him like I used to. It's...hard to explain. But..." As she was answering, she turned her eyes to the Daughter of Hatred as she asked sharply.

"...this is the third time you've asked about him. Are you...interested in our great Emperor? He's already got his Harem around him, and I don't know how messy it is right now...or if he's just as capable in handling the emotions of everyone in that whole web as he is in terms of his power."

The collection of women around the True Emperor of Quintessence!

Who knew just what emotions they felt on a day-to-day basis and exactly how the Existential Omniversal Lumen Emperor took care of them.

'Well, he does have a lot of Clones...and dilated time so...'

Ayame had this thought in her mind as beside her, the Daughter of Hatred laughed while waving her hands.

"I was his enemy all the way back then. It truly just seems like yesterday- I was stronger than him, held Legions under my banner, and so on. The grandiose Dimensional Hellion Ruler! The Dimensional Deceiver! Haha, it all seems worthless now. He came out of nowhere and ate everything up."

...!

Her gaze seemed reminiscent as she thought about the Desolate Mausoleum and the Dimensions under her banner.

She shook her head as she answered Ayame's question.

"No, I don't see myself entering his grand Harem, but of course, I've never stopped being curious about the man who bested me and is currently leading this whole ridiculous circus of freaks and monsters. Like...what the hell is even going on in his mind? Even this tournament he's starting tomorrow- how is he able to just think of doing something like that while around the Omniverse, the threat of the Devouring Worms that treat every ounce of power we have built so far as useless is waiting."

...!

"Is he just that confident that he will overcome this just like everything else? Is he planning everything for what is coming in the future...or does he make it up as he goes?"

"..."

Ayame calmly watched Lilith continue to speak as both of their feet waded across the golden waters.

Her gaze was far as Ayame couldn't help but shake her head while looking at her friend.

She may be telling the truth regarding the Existential Omniversal Lumen Emperor, but she didn't see the fascination she showed just while speaking about that man.

So she tried to offer a solution as but on another Quintessential Fruit of Existence.

"You could always just ask him. Send your thoughts across your Lineage and he'll probably bring a Clone of his before you."

...!

Lilith turned to stare at her as if she were crazy as she shook her head while rising from the golden pool.

"Why would I do something like that? What would I even say to him? We are currently at entirely different places. No...I'll do what I set off to do not too long ago. I'll seek for a seat of power that would distinguish me as a Ruler once more. For this...let's put our all into the coming tournament."

HUUM!

"Yes, yes. Come on, The Healthcare Branch of Dr. Hall's Practice of Life now has Students capable of giving Existential Massages and Pedicures. Let's relax a little tonight before the business of tomorrow..."

Amidst all the wars.

Amidst all the conflict, upgrades, and elevation of the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed.

Dr. Hall's Practice of Life had multiple branches extending to different sides of medicine that many entities came under his banner to work on.
