Chapter 426 - Crixus Shows Off

A unique phenomenon spread out across a few stars as the beings within unexpectedly came in contact with something the vast majority of them would have never had the chance to even glimpse nor understand.

A distinct aura of fate wrapped around them as they felt their bodies strengthened and reinforced, those that were actively dungeon diving or fighting seeing this strength increase first hand.

Because of this, shockingly something that was a norm on Prime Worlds was occurring throughout the planets that Noah was connected to. This phenomenon was the one where a vast majority of the beings on Prime Worlds would be considered Awakened individuals in the terms of the Planetary System, where a vast majority of beings on Prime World would have a tinge of essence running throughout their bodies that granted them a longer life span and the possibility of getting stronger in the future.

This was only possible because of the powerful atmospheric essence that was abundant in Prime Worlds, making most of the beings in it be considered Awakened. Such a phenomenon was actually occurring right now in the numerous Planets that Noah was connected to, where those that could be considered normal beings were slowly awakening because of the powerful energy of fate that wrapped around them!

In a small apartment located at the banks of the cities in the Blessed Empire, a young man was watching the TV with shining eyes as he saw the scene of Rank S Hunters valiantly fighting against each other in the Coliseum. A thought was running through his mind as he looked at this scene longingly.

When would I be able to cast a skill or do anything remotely as powerful as them?

It was the thoughts and gazes of longing that many felt when they watched their phones and TVs while observing powerful hunters obtain riches beyond their wildest dreams! This young man had the same, but he shook his head as he placed his focus back on the TV, when he suddenly felt a unique energy wrapping around his body, this energy going on to unlock something within him as it directly made him awaken, his shocked eyes finding themselves watching a materializing blue stat panel in front of his eyes there after!

Shocking scenes like these continued to occur wide and far as a spectacular phenomenon continued across the few connected worlds, bringing immense joy to unsuspecting beings as this was only the beginning of it all.

Noah himself had no knowledge of this yet, until a few seconds later he heard the words of the Planetary System ring in his mind.

[That Golden Guider ability is more ridiculous than you expected it to be.]

Noah, who had already begun moving forward to another task heard the occurrences around the planets he was connected to as he glanced at the description of this skill in shock. Those considered his subordinates were simply too many...right?

Realizing the extent that the effects of this Golden Guider traveled, a variety of thoughts passed through his mind as the Planetary System affirmed and voiced out his thoughts.

[If there are other Guider type abilities in the future Skill Trees, the effects are bound to be something unique.]

A shocking thought passed through the mind of one being and the Planetary Systems of a few worlds as countless possibilities lay for the future!

Noah shook his head at this ridiculous boost that almost every being on these stars had received and what it could mean for the future, his main body that was moving with the Righteous Fire Exploration Team and Grandmaster Vredral continuing to move as they had located yet another Chaos Behemoth.

He would observe the full effects of the Golden Guider ability later as he focused on what he was about to do currently in front of these Celestials. After the upgrade of the [Fates Guardian] skill tree that granted him the advancement of [Unruly Guider], he planned to show off some of his genius in the next hunt for the Chaos Behemoth!

Grandmaster Vredral was slowly pulling back in the fights more and more to have Noah and Athena work together with the Righteous Fire Exploration Team to stand against these Elite Behemoths, their bodies that continued to receive numerous Origin Cores of Behemoths and their materials making them near the peak of the Soul Rank in just a matter of days.

The hand of the Grandmaster moving in front of them came up as they received the signal to stop, his voice ringing out thereafter.

"A fairly average Elite Chaos Behemoth lies in front of us. Crixus and Athena will lead the charge, with the rest of you backing them up. I will jump in if any life threatening situations occur!"

The calm voice of the Grandmaster allowed them to nod with serious faces as the War Princess and the Destroyer took the lead, their bodies beginning to light up as they cast [Empyrean Guards] and [Empyrean Armament].

ROAR!

Their use of these skills caught the attention of the Chaos Behemoth as the earth below them began to tremble, the mountainous beast most likely reaching their positions in a matter of seconds!

4 Empyrean Guards appeared around Athena as they soon turned into streaks of light and gave her the usual golden armor and two golden hammers with shining light wings.

Around Noah, a similar scene repeated, but the summoned Empyrean Guards did not stop at 4, or the normal 6 that the Celestials had seen him use before!

With the addition of [Empyrean Ruler] from the Unruly Guider skill tree, he could summon an additional 12 Empyrean Guards! What exactly would come out of him absorbing this many Empyrean Guards?

Grandmaster Vredral watched carefully as his eyes began to shine an intense light, watching the appearance of exactly 12 Empyrean Guards around Crixus before they all turned into streaks of light and rushed to attach to his body.

A blinding light shot out as a brand new transformation came from doubling the amount of Empyrean Guards, Noah not even using the extra 6 that he could as he only portrayed 12 overall to the Celestials around him!

ROAR!

The Chaos Behemoth broke through the resplendent foliage of the Celestial Realm as the blinding light that was Noah moved to clash with it first, his spectacular adorned figure finally appearing in front of the eyes of the Grandmaster, Athena, and the members of the Righteous Fire Exploration Team as they stood with agape expressions, Noah himself clashing with the Chaos Behemoth head on!

BOOOOM!
