Chapter 475 A Sense of Pressure!

Noahs main body continued moving as the cores and the skill books that he placed in the Expansive Space were then quickly accessed by his clone and transferred into Spiritual World for his subordinates to use right away.

The ones prioritized were the Harbingers of Sin, as well as beings like Elena. The domain expansion rank cores and skill books would be even more useful to them as they didnt have the ability to very rapidly max proficiencies of their skill trees and upgrade them as quickly as Noah could. So even with the rapid power leveling that he continued to do with them, their skill trees almost always lacked behind their ranks because of how fast they rose.

He would keep a sufficient amount of Domain Expansion Realm skill books to use as sacrificial skills for his skill trees and give the rest to his people for them to be able to use powerful skills at the level that they enter. He planned to strengthen his subordinates well and have a few of them enter the Domain Expansion Realm and actually participate in the coming conflict of the Celestial Legions and the Legions of Mages, Wizards, Vampyres, and Infernals waiting for them at the Verittas Fortress.

He had also prepared numerous sets of armors from Flame Behemoths and from Infernals for them to equip, along with some completed Chaos Sets made from the many chaos behemoth materials that he had been collecting while hunting with Athena and the Righteous Fire Exploration Team before the tournament.

Only three of the Chaos Sets were able to be completed, and the three strongest combatants that entered into the Domain Expansion Realm before the war began would be given for them to wear.

His main body continued forward in this Celestial Realm as he hunted more behemoths and kept an eye out for any treasures. His gaze was bright as he waited for his clone that could now move with 100% of his strength to clash with the coming Celestials!

---

The first thing that was eye-catching was the scintillating colors that now surrounded his body, the passive emission of the purple Aether from the Fledgling Aether Terror abilities and the sparkling blue water elements constantly given off by the Hydromancers Mantle giving off an eerily beautiful contrast when also putting into account his devilishly handsome looks.

The Hydromancers Mantle had a particularly unique style as one could almost feel as if waves of clear blue water were constantly moving at the edges of the blue mantle.

His body shone with an aura of a boss monster that he would only find in late game content of the games he used to play in the past, this type of boss monster being able to drop the most valuable loot!

Noah found himself smiling as his thoughts briefly went back to times of extreme ignorance and bliss, times when he could just play games and read novels while he went on with his life.

Those were the times when his mind was free with no responsibilities whatsoever on his shoulders, his life simply going along with the flow as he enjoyed the things around him.

A slight look of reminiscence appeared on his face as he quickly shook himself out of it and breathed out, adopting a serious expression as he thought about the current him.

He was still going along with the flow of things, even moving himself to steer their flow and the flow of fate to the direction he wanted. And now, the things he would have considered to only be in his wildest dreams and as just fantasy were actually right in front of his eyes!

So he shook himself out as he stopped reminiscing and made himself focus on the task at hand, his heart set on continuing forward.

As expected, while wearing the Hydromancers Mantle, the progression of the Law of Water had nearly doubled, with the water particles rushing towards him much faster than the fire particles.

Even though the Seaborn effect was only for the Law of Water, the faster progression also propelled the opposing fire elements to run slightly faster!

The clone nodded its head as he continued on for another few hours before the tests came to a stop, his figure disappearing as his main body had placed yet another bountiful loot in the Expansive Space.

He waved his hands to take it out as his figure reappeared next to powerful auras of numerous beings training in the Time Space of the Spiritual World.

The Harbingers of Sin and the powerful Spiritual World Beasts were all here training to break past the Soul Rank and step into the Domain Rank as this was the prerequisite that Noah had set for anyone that wanted to participate in the war at the Verittas Fortress!

He was unconsciously becoming more and more protective as he saw that anyone in the Soul Forging Realm only had a chance of surviving in the coming war, and he didnt want any deaths that could be easily prevented.

A particularly arrogant Emperor Penguin raised its beak towards its master as it swallowed a Domain Rank Core, its flippers sticking out with no shame as Noah waved his hands for another similarly ranked core to fly towards him.

He pulled out more and more Domain Rank Cores that his main body continued to place in the expansive space as he hunted down Behemoths, all of them going towards his subordinates as he could sense the auras of many of them about to spill over into the next realm soon.

The Spiritual World Beasts entering into the Domain Rank was an even better situation as it signified that one of the requirements for the next upgrade of the Spiritual World was complete, the only thing remaining being the obtainment of a Large World Core.

This was something Noah would worry about for later as he focused on the current events, continuing the preparations for the war as besides giving his subordinates numerous Cores and Skill Books, he put the task of designating the Item Sets to the Spiritual World System whenever one of them stepped into the next rank.

Among the subordinates currently training, a few others were not in the Time Space as they were busy stabilizing the newly connected Underworld and moving around the Spiritual World thereafter, with others still looking over the many new worlds that were now connected.

He was not the only one busily moving, the forces he was connected to all feeling a sense of pressure as they collectively moved towards a brighter future!
