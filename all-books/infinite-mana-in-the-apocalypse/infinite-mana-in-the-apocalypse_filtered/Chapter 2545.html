2543 Violating Common Sense! III

Extremity Marcellus.

He held many disciples in the Genesis Institute of Extremity as Titus was among them.

When he had perished, he noticed and talked to a being that was devouring Titus' memories, even understanding that the whole squadron Titus led was about to die!

The being he had talked to then and the one he was looking at now...were one and the same.

He remembered that he had told this being the last time they met that if their paths crossed in the future, he may even discuss destiny with him.

And now, this being gave a warning to make the whole Genesis Institute of Extremity his enemies should they participate in this Apex War.

So he spoke towards the three other bodies of Hyperversal Extremities that came with him to the Aeonic Haven of Dissolution.

[What irreconcilable enmity do we actually have with this being before us?]

What great enmity did they have?

[He massacred some of the squadrons of Scions we sent during the Initiation Step-]

[He also killed Scions of many other Inheritances, but I do not see them here in this Apex War. I'm asking what this being did against our Inheritance that we have a smidgen of our forces, however small it is, supporting a War where from his perspective, he is fighting for his very livelihood and the lives of his whole Lineage.]

[...]

The other Hyperversal Extremities didn't reply as they could see where Extremity Marcellus was going with this.

On the third feathery throne, a woman squinted her brows as she spoke cautiously.

[Extremity Marcellus, you couldn't possibly be thinking...]

[Why not? What do we lose from it? Do you think our Inheritance would look weak from such an action?]

WAA!

His white hair danced with authority.

The other Hyperversal Extremities were quiet for a while as the female one sighed again.

[You've lived longer than all of us and are known as the Wise Extremity of Genesis. If you believe this is the best course of action, I have no qualms with it. But this Osmont's mouth...]

[Live and let live...] Extremity Marcellus waved his hands as he came to a shocking conclusion.

[For this Apex War of Annihilation, the forces of the Genesis Institute of Extremity will not be involved.]

...!

The seething skies palpated with the aura of Hyperversal Extremity Authority even now as a moment after the voice of the Hyperversal Extremity of the Avernus Clan ended, another one came down with an equally shocking message.

A message that was simple in nature, resonantly sinking into the ears of all here.

[The forces of the Genesis Institute of Extremity will not be involved in this conflict.]

...!

Words that caused the minds of many to buzz.

The mind of Vladic and the other Noblesse Commanders buzzed even more so than others as this faction...was their own backing!

They were about to lose the very backing they had allied with before the Apex War could even begin?

The Genesis Elders were just as shocked as them as the figures of Grand Genesis Elder Meiying and others began to disappear with a glimmer of light from the battlefield as if they were never there.

"This...!"

Commander Vladic who had just experienced euphoria when 200 terrifying Apex Aeonic Lifeforms began descending from the crack in the skies felt his smile fading as his eyes burned with shock and rage, but he gritted his teeth when he looked at the terrifying figures from the Avernus Clan while steeling his resolve.

Thereafter, his gaze went towards the beings stemming from the Singularity Overseers.

What would they do?

Before the choice came down, the figure of Noah floating before the transparent wall gazed above at the distinct Hyperversal Extremity Authority from the Genesis Institute of Extremity that had yet to fade, his hand waving upwards and sending out a portion of his volition in a vibrant ball of light.

A light merged into space and disappeared along with the Hyperversal Will that had spoken on behalf of the Genesis Institute of Extremity!

Noah's eyes were vibrant as this single action was him being intrigued by whoever was bold enough to actually reverse the course of the Genesis Institute of Extremity, and he established a line of communication with this single move as thereafter, he continued gazing above while voicing out.

"What shall be the path that the Singularity Overseers take?"

WAA!

He ushered forth a reply from the last remaining faction supporting the Noblesse as in the crimson silver skies, there was only silence so far.

[Devouring, you made a move on your own when this was a small scuffle, what shall you do now?]

In an isolated domain close to the lands of the Planeswalkers, a voice entered the mind of the Hyperversal Extremity of Devouring as he healed the visage of Extremity Kabul who was in a sorry state!

His eyes flashed with a vicious brilliance as he gazed before him where illusory screens showed the Apex Battlefield, his voice decisive as it thundered out.

[I shall redirect a portion of the Singularity Elders here for the Culmination Step for this War. I do not fear a creature who has merely forged an Absolute Seed of Extremity, nor will the Singularity Overseers fear such a being. This is still a minor and insignificant skirmish as we shall treat it as such...and cause as much chaos to tarnish the reputation of Svetlana!]

His eyes throbbed with both viciousness and age.

Truly, in the viewpoint of beings who had lived billions of years and held the profound power of Extremity...would they fear the words of a single existence in the face of the power they wielded, the power their Inheritance wielded, and the very Aeonic Havens themselves that they had founded themselves in?

[...]

The Hyperversal Will that had reached out to Devouring was silent with recognition as it instantly acted.

And above the recently calm skies of the Apex Battlefield...

BZZZT!

Space was torn apart once more as if it were paper, and the answer of the Singularity Overseers came with actions instead of words.

Blazing auras of exactly 300 Apex Aeonic Lifeforms descended with stern expressions, all of them riding on variations of massive stellar beasts whose bodies seemed to be formed of constructs of Cosmos, Realities, or even pulsing Singularities!

ROOAAR!

The Tamed Apex Astral Leviathans roared out with majesty as the 300 Lifeforms riding them...at least half held pulsing majesty akin to a Grand Elder of an Inheritance!
