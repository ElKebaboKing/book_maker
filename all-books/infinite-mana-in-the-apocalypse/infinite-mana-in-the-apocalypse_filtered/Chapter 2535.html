2533 The Extremity Vein Rises! I

The Culmination Step which held unknown wonders and boons was one that received the most attention in any Extremity Sanctification.

Even the Extremity Step where only Hyperversal Extremities could truly reign did not receive as much attention- and this had its own profound reasons that many would come to find out in the future to unfold.

The Culmination Step was so favored because of many points, but one of the more common ones was the fact that Aeonic Treasures of Extremity that could aid with Boundaries past the 13th Boundary Layer appeared. During this time of the Initiation Step, Noah had only ever gathered loot similar to the Blazing Hyphae of Extremity that aided one up to the 13th Boundary Layer.

The Culmination Stage was one that forged a generation of powerful Apex Aeonic Lifeforms and future Hyperversal Extremities as Dynamis would be more easily converged here, and the hope of forming an Absolute Dynamis of Extremity...was actually a Recorded History that had come to be in the previous Extremity Sanctifications!

It was a crucial stage.

And Noah had ushered it forth as when the space around them began to tremble, deeply black light began to cluster above Noah's visage as a pure source of Dissolution seemed to be drawn from all around and converging here- this dense authority snaking towards Noah's Dreamer of Extremity and sinking into his body as it tried to sear itself into his soul.

Ayame watched quietly as she knew what was happening.

For the being to have attained any astonishing Recorded feats in the Initiation Step of Extremity Sanctification, they would be branded with a profound authority that would aid them in the future Steps of Extremity Sanctification.

For the being to obtain the First Extremity Panacea.

For the being to kill the most Leviathans of Dissolution.

For the being to attain the most Aeonic Treasures of Extremity.

For the being that obtained the Last Extremity Panacea!

Normally, all of these feats would be spread out across multiple different beings. But as Ayame gazed towards Noah...she knew that he may just pull in all of these achievements for himself and attain an even grander threshold for the future to come.

The authority of an Aeonic Haven of Extremity.

It was something that any prominently powerful being with grand aspirations would seek to take for themselves, or at the very least- expand what such a Haven stood for as a concept.

But many would come to realize over the years that passed that either their expanded concepts would veer ever further away from what the Haven stood for, and that nobody could ever truly reach Extremity in what the concept of the Haven signified.

Many in the Aeonic Haven of Genesis expanded the concept of Genesis- with unique existences like Orion and Noah also expanding their version of Genesis as none of them truly had a link to what the Aeonic Haven of Genesis stood for.

One would know they weren't on the proper path of Genesis or any other concepts of an Aeonic Haven they expanded when in their Boundary Layers...

nothing relating to this Haven itself would be found.

Noah and Orion's concepts of Genesis had no link to the Aeonic Haven of Genesis as they did not correlate with the authority of this Haven.

But if a being was accomplished enough and was able to enact one Recorded History after another within a Haven...they may just accumulate enough understanding and access to the extent that they would be able to truly step on an endless path of the authority of a genuine Aeonic Haven of Extremity!

On this day, Noah Osmont was receiving pure streams of Dissolution as they were forming a brand into his soul- a brand that read with brilliant obsidian grandeur...[DIS]!

The beginnings of Dissolution.

One of the Seven Aeonic Vaults of Extremity. The Havens of Extremity!

There were the Vaults of Genesis, Helios, and Penumbra.

There were also Vaults of Andromeda, Elera, Oceanus, and Dissolution.

Noah was currently gaining a more distinct connection with the Aeonic Haven of Dissolution as anyone gaining such a distinction would only have an easier time while following the path of Extremity Sanctification!

Ayame watched the scene of such grandeur as above her head, minute traces of such authority formed as they paled in comparison to what Noah had surging into his soul, but they were there nonetheless to show her accomplishments.

With this, she knew that what her Grandfather had predicted would now come to fruition.

Extremity Sanctification would truly begin!

HOOONG!

The skies and land in the Endless Abyssal Thalassic Caverns of Nullity trembled and shattered for countless light years as if a cataclysm had arrived.

The endless Abyss hidden within it roared out with fervor as the Abyssal Sanctimonious Lands that were deeply entrenched in the underside of Dissolution...they began to rise!

BOOOM!

Space warped and twisted with this area as the epicenter, Sources of Chaos running rampant all around as fantastically, thick tendrils of Dissolution began to rise from the depths of the Abyss that were light years in length and diameter- these tendrils smashing onto the skies and terrain as the moment they did...the areas they touched seemingly became malleable and began to expand.

The very earthen glacial floor expanded as if countless tectonic plates were shifting below it, all of this change prompted by the tendrils of Dissolution writhing out and expanding from this region and continuing throughout!

Alongside this...the glacial floor split apart with utter glory as from the depths below, one could see the rough yet glorious sheen of a Vein begin to rise.

The pulsing Extremity Vein of Dissolution was rising from its depths ever so slowly as the very Haven of Dissolution split apart and expanded due to it- the expansiveness of this whole Haven about to be increased by an unknown amount!

As this occurred, the thick tentacles of Dissolution caused sparks of anomalies to bloom- singularities that sometimes formed into Planar Seeds ready to bud into Planes of Existence, while other singularities pulsed with a light of Aeonic Treasures of Extremity as they became concealed in the folding and expanding space- some becoming veiled deeply while others floated out grandly in the open!
