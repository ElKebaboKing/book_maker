Chapter 571: 8 Million!

In the starry space above the Verittas Fortress, all the attention was on the appearance of two enormous star sized forms of a being with wriggling tentacles that thrummed with the power of Aether!

Just from a conservative estimate on the effect of the Fate Stealer ability taking away 12% of the amassed Fate Lines of these beings, that meant Noah would gain more than 600,000 Fate Lines from just a single one of them.

Just what could have gone wrong? Where did everything begin to fall apart?!

But it truly was not sustainable. "You...will never succeed...!"

8 million!!!
