Chapter 387 - A Budding Economy

As a spectacular battle took place in the underground cavern of a volcanic mountain, elsewhere in the Celestial Realm, wild adventures of numerous Exploration Teams continued!

Tens of thousands of Void Ranked beings continued to pour into the Celestial Realm, with a few hundred at the Soul Realm making their entrance as well as more and more hunting teams to take down Behemoths and explore the wonders of the Celestial Realm were formed.

The Celestials were the biggest winners as always and as planned, watching as many beings perished while others learned from their mistakes, and ultimately create a smooth process where they received the resources of numerous materials from the bodies of Behemoths, and even further increased their exploration of the Celestial Realm. The benefits were slowly opening up as a bright future showed itself in front of their eyes.

The more powerful forces of some Prime Worlds guessed the intentions of the Celestials, but the rewards were simply too enticing so they went along with it!

This was because they found out the immense uses of the bodies of Behemoths that were drenched in universal laws, finding that the consumption of these beasts would rapidly propel their own comprehension and increase their power, not to mention that swallowing their Origin Cores where most of the universal laws were condensed gave one years worth of comprehension in that Law!

This made for a very competitive atmosphere as many beings formed powerful teams to gain access to even more resources. On this particular day, the newly formed Order of Blood was conducting its first hunt, the Royal Vampyre Elena at the lead as they faced a powerful Ice Behemoth.

This particular Behemoth had huge tusks that made it seem like an oversized Mammoth, its large amount of fur acting like a thick coat in the freezing and snowy region of the Celestial Realm. Numerous spears of ice continued to form and shoot out from its body every second as it faced its enemies!

The curvaceous body of the Royal Vampyre twisted as her hands flew forward, initiating a sea of blood that formed into powerful lances that smashed towards the Behemoth and kept its attention. This powerful Royal Vampyre was actually acting as the tank while the Emperor Penguin and others adorned with weapons pulsating with the fire element released their attacks towards the Behemoth.

ROAR!

The Behemoth roared out as Elena sent a telepathic message to the others.

"Ready?!"

Focused looks appeared on the other Vampyres in the surroundings, the Emperor Penguin stretching its flippers as it replied and nodded, the Calamities of the Sea and other Harbingers that were not staying and training in the Time Space of the Spiritual Land ascertaining as they carried out a plan they discussed beforehand.

The body of the Emperor Penguin teleported as it disappeared and reappeared far into the skies, feeling the strong gravitational pressure begin to pull its body downwards already as it did not have the capabilities to fly in this Celestial Realm like many others. Its goal was something else though, as its beady eyes locked in on the Ice Behemoth still letting out wild attacks towards its teammates, a shimmering silver light appearing over its body as it cast a particular skill it unlocked from its skill tree!

[Spatial Displacement]!

RUMBLE!

Attacks from above, attacks from below, and all while disorientingly falling from the skies!

ROAAR!

BOOM! CRUNCH!
