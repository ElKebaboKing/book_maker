Chapter 803 - Dreadful! I

Fear was a curious thing.

There were many types of fear, but the more prevalent one at this time was the all consuming fear of death.

Rarely would a being ever feel such a fear, and it might only be once or twice in their lifetimes where one could see their life flash before their eyes.

At this moment, millions of Ailerons felt a deep and intense fear, a dreadful type of fear that guaranteed death for them the moment that the singular being holding on to a wiggling Blue Slime turned his eyes towards them!

Their hearts were enveloped in an intense fear as just from this beings gaze, they saw that they were not even considered living things anymore! From his eyes, they saw that Noahs gaze already considered all of them dead!

This thought actually had some truth in it as at this moment, Noah considered them as nothing more than loot- and it was loot of such great quality that he would never let it escape his sight.

"Aaaah...!"

"Run...!"

"Run...!!!"

THRUUUM!

Figures began to twist and tumble as the natural response to their all consuming fear was to naturally run.

RUMBLE!

Ignoring the Holy Descendants and any other enemies in front of them, the Aileron Army began to scatter as the Peak Tier GALAXY experts were the first to turn into streaks of light!

But the being targeting them was truly unforgiving and much faster than them, the void being something he could consider his home because of the [Void Devouring Blood Terror] skill tree that stemmed from Blood Lord. The moment his words finished and just as they began running, his figure melded into the chaotic void as he appeared right in the midst of the Aileron Army, uttering a single ability that would be feared by many in the future to come.

[Life Realm].

"..."

WAA!

Faster than a flash of light, a unique green light expanded out for hundreds of miles as it passed through every single Aileron and even included Noahs subordinates and some of the Holy Descendants from the Expansive Dragon Cl.u.s.ter.

This flash of green light washed over every single being on the side of the Aileron Army as the moment it receded...they all disappeared!

"..."

Many did not know what had just occurred, but some truly knowledgeable people did as back in the heart of the Primal Winged Expanse where the Primal Emperor was surrounded by Entities, one of them uttered with a solemn expression.

"Instant Realm Creation..."

The words made the expressions of the powerful beings in the surroundings much graver as they knew the intricacies of what this meant!

It wasnt just the fact that it was a unique and rare seen ability, it was that utilizing it would truly require too much essence, especially when it came to forcefully creating an isolated space and dragging other beings into it.

An Entity had a somber expression as he voiced out what everyone else was thinking.

"Unless they work together to very quickly break the spatial locks of that Realm, that entire Aileron Legion...is lost."

RUMBLE!

The body of the Primal Emperor trembled as his eyes were filled with wrath, but he came to the same conclusion!

All those caught in the newly created Realm would be facing the horrendous creature that devoured the Entity Vast...and what could they do against such a creature?

"Good! Good! Good!"

The Primal Emperor uttered indignantly as he continued to look at the scene with wrathful eyes!

...

Back in the Rising Stellar Peaks, the Old Man Khan stared at the enormous green door that represented the entrance into the Realm that Noah had locked millions of experts in, his hand landing against this door as he found that he wasnt granted entrance!

It was a realm that locked everybody in and kept everyone out!

The other defenders of the Expansive Dragon Cl.u.s.ter didnt know if they should cheer or celebrate as the war truly did not follow the norms, many of them simply feeling a tinge of fear when they thought about the being carrying a blue slime and the fearsome 10 headed dragon that he commanded.

Even they did not want to know what was happening inside the locked Realm!

But inside of the [Life Realm], Noah stood above scrambling Aileron Forces as his gaze was cold.

In the past few seconds, the GALAXY experts from the Aileron forces had pushed down their fear as they tried to work together to break the confines of the Life Realm!

When they found to their despair was the thick roots being nearly impermeable to damage, looking towards each other in dismay as they then grouped together and stood in front of their entire disorganized Aileron Army.

"If you want to get out, its only my death that will allow it."

RUMBLE!

Noahs voice reverberated out as the Ailerons looked towards him with solemn faces, their hearts not necessarily fearing his figure the most, but the figure of the Blue Slime he carried in his hands!

This singular Blue Slime was currently releasing an oppressive aura of a being that had fully comprehended a Dao- an aura unique to an Entity! To their dismay, they found that they were actually imprisoned in a Realm with a dreadful enemy Entity that had defeated Vast in two attacks.

Even further behind Noah were a few tens of thousands of the defenders of the Expansive Dragon Cl.u.s.ter that got caught up in his Life Realm.

But the scene was that grand! Millions of beings on one side, and it was actually this side that was looking fearfully to the opposing side that had less than 50,000 beings!

As Noah stared at the 8 Peak Tier GALAXY Ailerons, the tens of them that were in the Middle Tier, and the over 100 in the Low tier GALAXY Realm...his eyes shone with palpable beams of light as all he could see was loot!

"Lets see what this stage of Entity is all about. Dont devour them, just kill them."
