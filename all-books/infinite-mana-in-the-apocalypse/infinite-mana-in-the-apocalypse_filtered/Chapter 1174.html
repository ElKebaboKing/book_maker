Chapter 1172 - Roll The Fuck Out! II

"Hmph, Daolord Ilyad...why would they bother accepting a Writ of Challenge of a lesser House?"

The two Daolord that looked like twins from the House of Havenbreaker floated forward as their auras vibrantly matched those from the House of Stormdust, the female Daolord turning and locking his gaze towards Noahs direction as he spoke out with a ridiculous amount of pride.

"Little Daolord, if you wish for an easy life and a future of great prospects...accept the Writ of Challenge from the House of Havenbreaker. Well be able to change even aboriginals like you into those worthy of being Protectors of the Bastion of Reality."

...!

When these powerful existences said these words, they genuinely thought them to be of help and mercy as they didnt notice the heavy insults they came with, the hearts of Augustus and others shaking in anger as Noah only had a smile on his face as he waved his hands to pull in it Primal Crystal holding information on the Primordial Empire.

"Then Ill thank Daolords for your stupendous hospitality."

Noah had a light smile as the Daolords from the House of Stormdust and Havenbreaker nodded approvingly, seeing this as the perfect demeanor that bumpkins like him should have when meeting with such graceful and powerful existences like themselves!

Under the weight of the powerful forces inhabiting the Primordial Empire, what could new entrants do but bend the knee and grovel at their feet for resources and influence?

Azazel looked at the scene occurring with sharp eyes as he didnt expect the Daolord he was careful of to actually have no backbone, but the situation rapidly changed in the next moment as the voice of this Daolord continued to ring out.

"Since the kind Daolords have made the trip over to our Indigo Cosmos and acted their part as the welcoming committee...why dont you all roll the fuck out of this Cosmos for now?"

...!

RUMBLE!

The surrounding void began to tremble the moment Noahs words finished, the Daolords that heard them not sure if they had heard correctly!

But they registered very clearly as all of their gazes changed and turned utterly frosty, their Runic Dao Lines swirling majestically as they locked their gazes onto Noah that was playing with the Primal Crystal containing the information of the Primordial Empire on his palm, his voice continuing to echo out!

"We have a week grace period before we have to accept the challenge, yes? No moves can be made against this Cosmos and the existences within it before then, so I suggest the great Daolords and the little Antiquities they came with to fuck off for now until that time. Ill give you all the answer then."

THRUM!

His words became more and more domineering as time passed, the smile on his face gradually wiped away as it became one of utter coldness!

Tiamat looked at this scene from her master as the heart of this dragoness fluttered.

She wasnt the only one as even the ambitious Lexis Marcus Tiberius saw herself turning towards Noah in shock and awe as her body felt lightning course through her!

King Augusts was the one most shocked as he never expected this side from what Noah had shown before, but the Daolords and Antiquities from the Primordial Empire were even more in a stupor as they never expected a being they considered lesser to ever talk to them in such a way.

"IMPUDENT!"

WAA!

The twins from the House of Havenbreaker released the word in synchrony as their eyes blazed out rays of golden light, ancient Runic Dao Lines beginning to swirl on their bodies as the pristine suits and golden dresses hugged them tightly!

"A little thing like you dares?!"

The Daolords and Antiquities from the House of Stormdust merely blinked as the void above them changed color, turning into a metallic red tinge that threatened to devour and overwhelm every single thing right then and there.

"Haha...haha!

Meanwhile, Azazel had begun to laugh in a raucous manner after seeing Noahs reply, his side being the only ones that seemed to be simmering in anger amidst the laughter.

They were the prestigious existences of the Primordial Empire! The Protectors of the Bastion of Reality! They were existences that had lived for a very long time and knew very well of their qualities of arrogance and pride...but they deserved to be so based on what they had accomplished!

Who was this being before them to disparage that and talk to them in such a manner?

RUMBLE!

The surroundings quaked and tumbled as if everything could shatter into nothingness at any moment, but Noah simply continued to play with the Primal Crystal within his hands as his now cold voices continued to echo out.

"Under the rules of the Great Conqueror, no harm or moves shall be made against any existences of a newly joining Cosmos for the period of 1 week. I want to ask the Daolords before me...are you all planning to reject the rules of the Great Conqueror? Are you all planning to rebel against the guidelines of the Emperor?"

His voice was cold and steady, perfusing into the minds of all those in the surroundings as their wild and flaring power came to a halt as they looked at this being incredulously.

Was he...was he actually using the rules of the Primordial Empire against them? After barely arriving here for a few minutes?

The void froze at such a development as the smiles were wiped off from the faces of the Daolord Azazel and others from the House of Springforge. The beams of golden light shooting from the twin like existences from the House of Havenbreaker calmed down as they stared coldly, while those from the House of Stormdust gazed on coldly as the tinge of metallic red disappeared from the skies.
