Chapter 614 - [Fireball]!

Going back a few seconds, Noah had been talking with a not so pleased Terror of Aether when they felt the abrupt change in the barrier they were observing as well as the appearance of 6 new auras of power.

Three of them felt familiar to Noah, as they each had a hint of law where Noah knew it was two Controllers and a single Grand Controller that also seemed to have a unique aura included with him! The other three were even more unique as the aura Noah felt from them was unlike anything he had come across before!

RUMBLE!

The enormous body of the Cthulhu began to move the moment the auras appeared, leaving behind wrathful words that showed its thoughts at this moment.

"The result of the actions you condone has come, let us hope it is not the doom for us all!"

Noah gave a light smile at the strong words of the Cthulhu, sensing the other two Terrors far away from them also converged towards six newly appearing signatures as he also made his way.

"A galaxy in its infancy just waiting for us to explore...ahh!"

He appeared just in time as the Outsider at the forefront had an ecstatic look on his face while observing Cthulhu and the other two Terrors, Noahs body finally become visible in their eyes as they also looked towards him, his appearance actually making them look even happier as it apparently furthered their belief that this galaxy was nothing much!

Noahs eyes shone as he looked towards the Grand Controller letting out an aura of Chaos, as well as the other two Law Masters released auras of Fire and Space. These beings he could sense the auras of and understand, but the other three gave him a unique feeling of strength that he could attribute one thing- Traits!

He glanced at the being at the forefront who had wavy frosted hair as his body released extremely cold elements that turned the surrounding miles into a frozen space. He observed this unique being using his power, realizing that this being was utilizing a trait while his body only gave off the essence of someone at the Nebula Rank, but no law masteries whatsoever!

Yet this being was able to cause the surrounding thousands of miles to be filled with cold elemental particles that actually reduced the movements of Controllers and even slowed them down.

This was a being with a Trait, this was a Blessed!

Noahs small figure stepped forward amidst all of this as the three Terrors had their essence ready to erupt at any moment, his eyes shimmering with strength as he appeared fully in front of the six Outsiders.

The Outsiders squinted their eyes as they actually could not observe any sense of danger from Noahs small body, their confidence levels only rising as they confirmed these beings in front of them really were the only Protectors that this Galaxy could send out!

"It seems like our journey will be extremely rewarding this time around."

The other two Blessed holding the traits of [Supernova] and [Ability Mimic] spoke to each other with smiles as they were actually protected by the Authorities that two Law Masters released as well as the field of frozen ice that the strongest Blessed laid out, not treating Noah as anything much!

Noah did not mind this as he simply wanted to open up a new chapter and fully start the terrifying plan that the Novus Galaxy had concocted, and he wanted to start off this new stage in the most spectacular way possible.

He did not want to exchange any words or play games with these Outsiders, he only wanted to start off his new path in one way! He wanted to do so in the same way when he first awakened...and it was with a single skill!

After becoming a Controller of the Law of Fire, Noah had carefully read the description that said how any rank of skills could display power at the level of Controller, and he had many ideas when he saw this!

The first thought that came to his mind was his first skill, the skill that actually started his path as an Awakened until he reached the position he did today.

It was a skill with an extremely fast casting time because of it being the lowest ranked skill- it was the Rank F+ Fireball!

His hand stretched out in front of him towards the unbothered Outsiders that had only begun releasing their energy more rampantly, preparing to see what the native of this Galaxy would throw before they fully committed to doing as they wished.

It would be an utter surprise for them when they heard a simple word!

"[Fireball]."

.....

BRRRRRRRR!

Like the sound of a gatling gun, tens of balls of fire bloomed out from Noah as events had to be described in a manner as if time was slowed down!

When describing the balls of fire being released from him, it did not really capture what they were fully because their sheer size was simply too enormous! These so called Fireballs were actually tens of miles large, where each one was larger than Noahs current human form by thousands of times!

They were not really fire balls, but genuine scorching suns that were nearing the size of stars! The heat they released as they were utilized by a controller caused undulations in the surrounding space as they threatened to melt anything in sight, and it was these genuine suns that were shooting out in the tens just from Noah casting [Fireball] once.

It was a skill maxed in proficiency as Noah even added to it 5 Skill Points, this simple beginner skill at this moment actually being able to shoot out multiple scorching suns at a speed unbefitting of their sheer size and stature!

"..."

OOOM!

The Blessed with the [Ice Man] trait had his eyes dilate as he discovered hundreds of miles long suns rushing towards them at ridiculous speeds, his body glowing in a powerful blue light as he screamed out while wiping away the unbothered expression.

"Ice Age!"

RUMBLE!

He was not alone as the face of the Fire Law Master hardened, his body also stepping forward as he actually utilized |Intent| using his power as a Controller.

|Protect|!

BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!

Sun after sun smashed towards them in a shower of searing heat and molten flames, each explosion causing the frosty Blessed to tremble as the Ice Age he was spreading out was rapidly eaten up!

BOOM!

Yet another sun smashed and exploded as a hazy red barrier formed from the Intent of the Fire Law Master stopped it, their faces finally becoming serious as they stared at this being in front of them.

Waves of heat washed over to them as the hand of the Blessed with the [Ice Man] trait was trembling, the eyes of the Fire Law Master cold as he stared at this being that was apparently just like him!

Even the Grand Controller nodded with a careful expression as it seemed this was in the bounds that he expected this Galaxy to send out, beginning to send messages to the other 5 Outsiders at this moment, but his words would get drowned out soon after!

RUMBLE!

His words were cut short as they consecutively heard the same words for the skill that had just made their expression serious, the words of the Grand Controller veering off as they looked towards the Protectors of this Galaxy with wide eyes.

BRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRR!!!

Time seemed to be moving extremely slow as near Noah, the Terrors tumbled away!

A shocking and majestic sight erupted as tens of suns began to appear in front of Noah.

On the other side, the Grand Controller of Chaos had an incredulous face as his powerful essence finally moved and swept out as he sensed this overpowering appearance of an ability used too many times, an Authority of Chaos emerging out as it held a hint of something else- it was the aura of someone that was a Beginner Assimilator!

But even this Assimilator had a gaze full of shock as he stared at the appearance of hundreds of suns that covered the entirety of their sights from all around them, each of these suns carrying with them a power of a peak Fire Controller as they thundered towards them at speeds not matching their miles long sizes!

It was shocking!

It was ridiculous!

It was the spamming of a low rank skill that had an extremely fast casting speed while carrying the immense prowess of a Controller!

It was the rank F [Fireball], and it opened up yet another stage in Noahs life!
