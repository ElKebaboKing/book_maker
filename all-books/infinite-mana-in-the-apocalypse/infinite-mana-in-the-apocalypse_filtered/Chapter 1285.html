Chapter 1283 - An Unachievable Effect! I

Over the past day, Noah had his clones stretch out to locate more Cosmos as after they were found and the conversation with the Core consciousnesses overseeing these Cosmos occurred, the dozens of Universes of these Cosmos were fused into Noah's Infinite Cosmos as he then spread his essence of Fealty over them!

Currently, over 23 new Cosmos had undergone the process of fusion into the Infinite Cosmos, this shocking number increasing the population of Noah's Cosmos countless times over as with them falling under his banner, the Light of Conquest he gained spread out brilliantly across his millions of Splendiferous Universes.

The newly added Cosmos ranged in their own uniqueness as Noah found most of them to have Hegemonies at the very peak of power, the ordeal of breaking into Antiquity that was easy for some also being an insurmountable wall for others. He obtained only three Cosmos that uniquely had a single Antiquity that ruled the entirety of their Cosmos, these existences being accomplished beings that had achieved such a task against all odds as there were no factions to divide their power!

Yet now, they all fell under Noah's banner- no matter how grand their achievements were. The Infinite Cosmos now held over one thousand genuine Universes within it as the Cosmic Core was dramatically elevated in size and power, but no new changes had occurred with this as when Noah looked into this with the Nomological Edict of Kismet, he was given the number of 100.

On the 100th fused Cosmos, there lay a possibility for Noah to experience a chance in the features and capability of the Infinite Cosmos!

At this moment though, these recent actions had granted him an overwhelming amount of Light of Conquest as he could finally enhance the latest Cosmic Dao he had designed that boasted ridiculous capabilities!

In the Ruination Realm, his Universal body was engrossed in reading the golden book that was transcribing a hidden meeting between Stormdust and the Cosmic Primordial Beast. One of his main human bodies was watching the soon to be completed mixture of daos and laws for another Minor Nomological Edict as a Grand Primordial Heart was being devoured of all of its essence, and then there was the third human body that was adorned in the verdant effects of [The Ameliorator]...it was this body that was taking the job of the next task as he voiced out grandly.

"Let the Light of Conquest flow into the Cosmic Dao of Loot."

...!

Ah!

A brilliant white light shone within Noah's origin as it drilled into the golden river that represented the Cosmic Dao of Loot, this grand show of lights continuing for a few seconds before Noah's body released a wave of powerful white gold light!

His stellar eyes being washed over by the mesmerizing essence of the Ameliorator glanced at the status Panel as he observed the new and improved authority of the Assimilator of the Cosmic Dao of Loot.

Before this, he always had the +1,000% Increased Loot Quantity and +100% Increased Loot Rarity, but after the enhancement with the light of conquest...

{Authority of the Assimilator of the Cosmic Dao of Loot}<EMPOWERED>: An authority that only appears around those who have fully comprehended and assimilated the Cosmic Dao of Loot. It has been empowered by the Cosmic Essence of Conquest as its Rank is further elevated. It grants +150,000% to All Parameters, and the unique modifiers of +2,000% Increased Loot Quantity and +200% Increased Loot Rarity are also obtained. Adversely, the essence of this Dao and its effects can only be activated by the essence of 10,000 Splendiferous Universes per second. Due to the Empowerment, all loot will now have their rarity ranked based on colors of white, blue, purple, and gold.

A stellar description of Noah increasing the amount of loot he could get from a factor of 10 to 20 appeared right away as wondrously...if he killed a single Lesser Primordial Beast, he would get 20 Resplendent Lesser Primordial Hearts! This also wasn't to mention whether the effect of 200% Increased Loot Rarity would still give out loot with the affix of <Resplendent>, or if he could finally elevate the rarity of the treasure from one rank and into the next!

But...he didn't have to wonder too much about this because he wasn't done.

He had been shown with <Path to Victory>...and this was one of the methods that would allow him to do the impossible- the method to allow him to catch up to a Champion that had simply been given too much! His eyes flashed with intense excitement for what was to come as he voiced out grandly.

"Deconstruct and reconstruct the Cosmic Dao of Loot."

RUMBLE!

Yes!

The turning of the Cosmic Dao of Loot into a Runic Dao Line that granted a 100 fold boost to the current boosts of the selected Dao!

This definition of "current boosts of the selected Dao" was extremely important as it was different from the definition of the Runic Dao Line Enhancers that elevated the "base boosts" given by a Runic Dao Line.

The base boosts included things like [+150,000% to All Parameters] or [+100,000% Increased Annihilation Damage], but it didn't apply to extra effects like [50 Random Debuff Effects to enemies] that stemmed from the same Annihilation.

When the Dao of Annihilation was turned into a Runic Dao Line, all effects, whether base or extra effects- were multiplied by 100 times as Noah gained 10,000,000% to Increased Annihilation Damage and 5,000 Random Debuff Effects to enemies. But when he utilized Resplendent Runic Dao Lines, only the base effect of 10 million percent was enhanced as the extra effect of 5,000 Random Debuffs was left unchanged!

From the Cosmic Dao of Loot, [+150,000% to All Parameters] was the base effect that would be elevated by Resplendent Runic Dao Line Enhancers, but the extra modifiers of the enhanced [+2,000% Increased Loot Quantity and +200% Increased Loot Rarity] would not be affected.

Resplendent Runic Dao Lines would not affect these ridiculous modifiers...but the initial step of turning the Dao into a Dao Line held a certain 100 fold increase to all boosts of the Dao- base boosts and even extra boosts.

This was Noah's aim!

A 100 fold increase to +2,000% Increased Loot Quantity and +200% Increased Loot Rarity!

Even if they couldn't be enhanced with Resplendent Enhancers after that, so what?! A 100 fold increase of 2,000% was 200,000%. Of 200% was 20,000%.

"..."

Even Noah was breathless as he watched the deconstruction of the Cosmic Dao of Loot, knowing that even the possibility of such a thing was utterly balance breaking as nobody should even be capable of obtaining such a thing.

And he was correct.

He already knew what was coming as he had seen it- where for the first time ever...he actually wouldn't succeed in branding a Runic Dao Line onto his body.

He would actually fail in branding the Runic Dao Line of Loot as the effects such a thing would provide were simply not possible!

Yet...he still did it regardless as what awaited on the other side...was something just as glorious!
