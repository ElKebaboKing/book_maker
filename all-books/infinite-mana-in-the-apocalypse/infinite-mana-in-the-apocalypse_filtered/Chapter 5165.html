Chapter 5163: You Seal Nothing! You Bind Nothing! II

He could perceive the image so unfathomably clearly.

Because she truly thought she could bind him? Him?

"Me?! Haha!"

BOOM!

As he bellowed the question out across the ruined balcony, around his body boundless multicolored Infinity surged in response to the bellowing, and his Quintessence Infiniforce continued to generate endlessly within his existence at the elevated rate the Superbius amplification had enabled hours earlier, pouring outward from his foundations in rivers of layered color that did not show any sign of having been sealed or suppressed by Elzyanas work.

The brilliance of what he was putting out pressed against the gold-saturated sky above him and found the sky unable to contain it, and small flickers of his multicolored Infiniforce began to push upward against the golden canopy with the patient persistent assertion of a living current reclaiming its territory!

Noah continued to laugh while his Quintessence Infiniforce surged around him and commenced painting the surrounding regions of the mountain and the sky with his own preferred colors, and he voiced his thoughts aloud with the tyrannical amusement of a being witnessing the failure of a process that had presumed to address him.

"THE Infinity Seal?!"

His voice carried the clean dry cadence of genuine amusement.

"You cannot bind or suppress my Infinity. You cannot even begin to! My Quintessence Infiniforce is generated internally, from my own existence! Your seal passes through my foundations like nothing. It finds nothing to seal. It closes nothing!"

HUUM!

His Quintessence Infiniforce surged around him ever more intensely, and the boundless Infinity began to coil around his body and skin like an endless network of coiling serpents, each coil carrying its own layered colors, each coil weaving through and around the coils adjacent to it in patterns that suggested the Infinity itself had been waiting for permission to express itself this freely and was now taking full advantage of the permission being granted!

Noah closed his eyes while surrounded by the boundless Infinity, and his figure stood gloriously at the center of the surging currents as his Hadean Pillar of Infinity bloomed around him in its full manifested form rather than the faint background construct it had been earlier.

THE First Tongue crystallized into its blue forest at the base of the Pillar. The multicolored flames of Existence licked upward through the mid-tiers with renewed intensity. The mirrors of Paradox reflected their impossibilities across every direction. The storm of Chaos roared at the apex with the weight of his refusal to accept the conclusions.

Every actualized Civilization throughout the Pillar rose in rank alongside his spoken refutations, each one contributing its weight to the broader assertion his existence was now making against Elzyanas distributed authority!

He spoke to his very existence and to his Civilizations within him and within his Pillar, and his voice boomed outward from within his foundations as it refuted each of the bindings in sequence.

"THE Scale Limiter?"

His voice held the dry amusement of a being pointing out an obvious miscalculation.

"You limited me to THE Fundamental Depth of Vakochevs First Scale. I am not on Vakochevs Scales! Your Limiter has applied itself to coordinates I do not occupy. It sits there, compressing a portion of Vakochevs framework that is not mine to be compressed. The binding is technically functional. The binding has technically done what it was designed to do. The binding has simply done it... to a space I was never in."

BOOM!

His Pillar rose higher in response to the refutation, and more of his Quintessence Infiniforce poured outward from his existence to replace what the refutation had just consumed.

"THE Observable Force Lockout?"

He felt the continuous feed from Ubergulden Adelheid still flowing through their shared bond, the golden current pouring into him through the wormhole of Quintessence Infiniforce they shared, unaffected by the Lockout that had sealed the ambient channels around him.

"You blocked my access to the ambient currents. You did not block the private channel I share! The Lockout has closed a door I was not using. The door I actually use remains open."

BOOM!

"THE Unobservable Force Lockout?"

His laughter continued beneath the words.

"I have not been drawing on Unobservable Force in any meaningful way yet."

BOOM!

"THE Civilizational Dormancy?"

He observed within himself the way his Civilizations were behaving under the pressure of the binding, and he perceived the specific shape of what the Dormancy had attempted to do. His Civilizations flickered slightly under the settling of the binding. But...they did not fall asleep.

His Hadean Organ of Civilization held them against the pressure, and his Pillar reinforced the hold by bearing down on the storage configuration with its full accumulated weight. The Civilizations continued to operate. The Dormancy continued to insist that they should not be operating. The disagreement between the binding and the actual state of his Civilizations was simply a disagreement that the binding was losing.

"You forced my Civilizations into dormancy. My Organ refused the force. My Pillar refused alongside the Organ. My Civilizations continue to hum within me at their active intensities, and your binding continues to sit within my foundations issuing instructions that my foundations are not obeying. It is a conversation the binding cannot win because my foundations have already decided the outcome...backed by my Infinity!"

BOOM!

"THE Voice Restraint?"

He laughed fully at this one, because the laughter itself was the rebuttal, because he had been speaking freely and defiantly for the entire duration of his refutations and the binding that was supposed to prevent such speaking had found no purchase with which to prevent anything.

"I am speaking. I am speaking loudly. I am speaking in direct opposition to Sororis authority."

BOOM!

"THE Deferential Posture?"

He stood tall at the center of his Pillar, his head lifted toward the gold-saturated sky above him, his spine straight, his shoulders relaxed into the posture of a being who had never been inclined to defer to anything across his entire cultivation.

"I cannot defer. I will never...defer."

BOOM!

"THE Memory Seal?"

He remembered every detail of the afternoon with the clarity of his Hadean Mind of Infinite Calculation, remembered THE Creatures strike, remembered Aurelius Maximuss carving, remembered Elzyanas descent and her clinical cruelty and her distribution of the trillions of constructs across the Observable, remembered every word of her extended analogy about ants and soil and gardeners, remembered the exact sequence in which his bindings had been applied to him.

Nothing was sealed. His memory was entirely available to him.

The more Noah refuted, the more his body generated Quintessence Infiniforce boundlessly, each refutation pulling additional reserves of his internal substance outward into the ambient field around him, and the surge of Infiniforce around his figure began to expand in magnitudes that exceeded any surge he had previously produced across his existence.

It felt boundless.

The multicolored Infinity surged outward from him like a vibrant rising sea, and the sea commenced rising upward through the gold-saturated sky with the patient persistence of a tide that had decided to claim the territory a prior tide had been occupying.

The gold of Elzyanas and Aureliuss combined wrath began to be displaced. Small fractures appeared in the golden canopy above the mountain as Noahs multicolored Infiniforce pressed against it from below, and the fractures widened into gaps, and through the gaps his own colors began to pour outward into the upper reaches of THE Wyld.

The multicolored Quintessence Infiniforce spread across the upper atmosphere of THE Wyld in rolling waves that overwrote the golden wrath with Noahs own preferred palette. Where the gold had claimed the sky as Sororis Prima Elzyanas domain, Noahs Infiniforce now commenced claiming it back as his!
