"The enemy that caused his death?"

The rising force calling themselves Celestials? Noah thought about these words that would have been said more than a thousand years ago as he pondered.

Theyre not just a rising force anymore, are they?

The boosts and skills are so ridiculous that Ill have to spend quite some time just to understand how to best use them all.
