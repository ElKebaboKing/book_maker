Chapter 4463: Civilization Specialization VI

Noahs eyes pulsed with immensity as he observed the formation of the Sovereign Spires of Osmont while prompts descended to explain exactly what Civilization Specialization represented.

The prompts materialized alongside radiant illusory holographic displays of the Sovereign Spires...towering structures that appeared simultaneously within his manifested Civilization and as conceptual diagrams floating before his vision.

|The Civilization Specialization System represents evolutionary refinement of The Primordial Civilization of Mana and Hungers membership structure.|

|This system allows for the diversification of roles and capabilities among Members, transforming your Civilization from monolithic force into sophisticated organism with differentiated functions.|

|Through the Sovereign Spires of Osmont, Members of your Civilization can voluntarily transform their existence into specialized path that provides substantial benefits to both individual and collective framework.|

|Current Available Specializations:|

| The Infinite Bulwark Specialization|

| The Hungering Maw Specialization|

| The Vital Chorus Specialization|

|Additional Specializations Currently Under Development:|

| The Architects of Infinity Specialization: 17% completion|

| The Sovereign Heralds Specialization: 13% completion|

|Access to additional Specializations will unlock as your Civilization advances and as harvest continues flowing into the Sovereign Spires of Osmont.|

...!

Noahs eyes flashed as he processed these prompts.

So the Civilization evolves from everyone possessing similar capabilities toward specialized roles with distinct functions.

Three Specializations currently available, two more under development...

In the next moment, he observed one of the massive Sovereign Spires of Osmont within his Manifested Civilization rise up prominently and be displayed before him with enhanced clarity...as if the entire conceptual structure was being pulled forward for a detailed examination.

The Spire was magnificent in its architectural expression. A towering structure composed of layered obsidian-gold crystalline formations that suggested absolute solidity and permanence.

The surface was inscribed with countless defensive Arcana Glyphs that pulsed with rhythmic intensity.

Above it, floating labels materialized with clear identification.

SOVEREIGN SPIRE OF OSMONT - THE IRON CONCORDAT

The blue-gold holographic figure of RUIN/EDEN appeared beside the displayed Spire, her form coalescing from scattered light into coherent representation.

She bowed gracefully toward Noah with expression mixing professionalism with barely suppressed excitement...as if she had been waiting eagerly to present this new capability.

Her voice emerged with precision, though underlying enthusiasm colored her typical analytical tone.

"Master, allow me to present the first available Civilization Specialization...The Infinite Bulwark Specialization. This path can be obtained when any Member of your Civilization, with the sole exception of yourself, enters a Sovereign Spire of Osmont bearing the distinction of The Iron Concordat and consciously chooses to undergo the transformation."

As she articulated this introduction, the obsidian-gold coloration of the Spire intensified...the defensive architectural structure becoming more pronounced as if physically responding to being discussed.

RUIN/EDEN began explaining the Specialization through systematically presented prompts!

|THE INFINITE BULWARK SPECIALIZATION|

|Faction Declaration: "We are the wall upon which existence breaks. We do not fall. We cannot fall."|

|Primary Focus: Absolute Defense and Endurance|

|This Specialization represents commitment to protection above all other considerations. Members who choose this path sacrifice offensive capability entirely in exchange for defensive power that transcends conventional limitations.|

An illusory figure manifested beside RUINEDEN...showing how a humanoid figure who chose this Specialization would appear after transformation.

The figure was massive, imposing, covered in layered crystalline plates of obsidian-gold that seemed to absorb light rather than reflecting it.

The entity moved with deliberate slowness, but each step radiated absolute certainty and caused minor stabilization effects in surrounding reality.

Additional prompts continued with detailed specifications.

|CORE TRANSFORMATION PARAMETERS:|

|Total Offensive Sacrifice: Members of this Specialization lose 100% of their offensive capabilities. They cannot deal direct damage through any conventional or unconventional means. All attacks, abilities, and techniques designed to harm enemies are systematically removed from their framework. Any attacks towards Members of The Civilization are automatically drawn to them.|

|Multiplicative Defensive Gain: In exchange for complete offensive sacrifice, their Complexity and Purity is multiplied by 15x for all defensive calculations. This multiplication applies to Durability, Resistances to Living Existential Authority, Principles, and Civilizational Authority, and all other protective measurements.|

|Immovable Presence: Cannot be forcefully moved, teleported, displaced, or otherwise repositioned without explicit consent. Their existence becomes anchored to their chosen location through fundamental authority that resists manipulation.|

...!

The illusory Bulwark figure demonstrated these capabilities...absorbing devastating attacks without visible damage, remaining perfectly stationary despite tremendous force being applied, radiating defensive authority that created visible protective fields around nearby allies!

|UNIQUE CAPABILITIES:|

|Civilization Fortress Formation: Multiple Bulwarks can merge their individual defensive fields to create unified impenetrable barriers protecting designated allies. The more Bulwarks participate in formation, the stronger the collective barrier becomes, scaling exponentially rather than linearly.|

The visualization showed three Bulwarks combining their defenses...their individual protective fields merging into single cohesive barrier that appeared capable of withstanding apocalyptic assault.

|Damage Conversion: All damage absorbed by Bulwark can be systematically stored within their framework and subsequently channeled to the offensive specialists within the Civilization. Nothing is wasted...every attack they endure becomes fuel for allied counter-assault.|

The illusory figure absorbed barrage of attacks, and visible energy accumulated within its form before being transferred through golden conduits toward offensive-oriented allies who held different Specializations!

|Existential Anchoring: Their presence stabilizes reality itself within substantial radius. Prevents spatial tears, dimensional collapse, reality manipulation, or existential alteration within their vicinity. The more powerful the Bulwark, the larger the stabilization radius.|

The visualization demonstrated reality becoming more solid around the Bulwark...attempts to tear space, manipulate dimensions, or alter fundamental principles simply failing within the stabilized zone!

|Regenerative Resonance: Healing effects directed toward Bulwarks are amplified by 500%. Their defensive specialization creates synergistic relationship with restorative capabilities, allowing healer Specializations to restore them far more efficiently.|

|Armored forms appear as living fortresses, massive entities covered in layered crystalline plates of obsidian-gold that shift and reconfigure to optimize defensive coverage.|

|Movement is deliberate and unhurried, but conveys absolute certainty.|

|Eyes glow with steady, unwavering silver light that suggests immutable commitment to their protective purpose.|

|IDEAL CANDIDATES: Those who value protection above personal glory or offensive capability.|

|Beings who have experienced devastating loss and refuse to allow such loss to occur again.|

|Entities with naturally defensive psychological dispositions who find fulfillment in shielding others rather than destroying enemies.|

|Members who understand that sometimes the greatest strength lies not in capacity to harm, but in capacity to endure.|

...!

Noahs eyes pulsed with tyranny as he absorbed the comprehensive presentation of this first Civilization Specialization!

He ignored everything else and focused on a number.

15x.

15!

Did this mean, truly, 15x the Complexity and Purity of any members who chose this defensive Specialization? 100 Quintillion to 1,500 Quintillion(1.5 Sextillion)?

Truly?!
