Chapter 545 - [Skill Creation]!

As a ridiculous amount of essence continued to be sucked into a wondrous process of a Realm Formation, a single being was floating in the skies of the realm as his body was surrounded by a column of blue light!

His arms and legs were outstretched as his muscles spasmed, his eyes shimmering as they shot out beautiful blue arcs of light.

Even though this being looked to be in pain, a smile could be seen on his face as the surprises he was getting were too many!

First was the Inherent Ability of mana manipulation, and then came a feature of his System that many others could already do- the capability to create skills! The feature of [Skill Creation] was added on top of such things as [Skill Combination], Noah taking half of his attention from the forming of the realm and into his panel as he observed the details of this feature.

<strong>[Skill Creation] :: A feature that allows the user to create skills or skill trees. The tier of a skill or skill tree is dependent on the stage of the user. Multiple conditions are required for the successful creation of a skill or skill tree: The user is required to describe the intended ability in detail before a skill model can be computed for its feasibility. To bring the skill or skill tree into fruition, [Skill Points] are required. The amount of [Skill Points] needed is dependent on the type of skill being created.</strong>

The description of a feature that seemed to actually be shining bright gold in Noahs eyes appeared, this feature being a much-awaited one as he theorized all he could do with it! The only thing that put a damper on his thoughts was the requirement of Skill Points that he had very limited amounts of remaining, much of them being cl.u.s.tered and spread out into numerous Skill Trees.

Unless he found a new source of Skill Points in the near future, he would have to soon inactivate some skill trees as he took the Skill Points within to focus them onto a few specific ones, as he would soon be upgrading the stages of skill trees into the Star Forging Realm!

He would be faced with the situation where Skill Points were gradually becoming a scarce resource, and this was made especially so by the fact that he would need to spend them to create new skills or skill trees.

Thus, he looked forward to the day that he could obtain new Skill Points, but he also abhorred it because it would come with its own terrifying connotations!

[The Realm Formation is 80% completed.]

He locked this thought in the back of his mind for now as the voice of the System rang out in his mind, his awareness focusing on the expanded channel of mana that could intensely feel as well as the changes occurring in the expansive Realm before him. He would play with [Skill Creation] later as he now finished overseeing this realm upgrade.

The circular planets had all inexplicably unfolded into wide and flat land masses that would make for a grand paradise for anyone that believed stars were flat, these landmasses connecting together like pieces of a puzzle that had just been waiting to be fitted this entire time!

The vibrancy of all sorts of colors of essence was abound in this spectacular realm formation that even Noah did not know how to describe it all.

He could see the different regions that used to be the Spiritual World, his gaze seeing those that shone with colors of blue, red, gold, or dark depending on their different Law Spaces. In the location that used to be the Spiritual World, he could see the figures of beasts led by the Emperor Penguin, this arrogant penguin actually standing at the forefront as one could see its body trembling, tears actually coming out of its beady eyes!

This was because this event was simply too important for a few beings much more than others, and the Spiritual World Beasts that were first born when this was still just a small floating landmass in a corner of the Beast World were those affected the most! Thus, one could see the scene of emotions that would never be expected of the face of this arrogant Emperor Penguin as this achievement was simply too much for him!

The Harbingers of Sin that were nearby simply looked on with unbelievable expressions at the grandiosity of everything, Kazuhiko being one of the many that was pinching themselves multiple times to make sure what they were seeing was not a dream.

Noah moved his awareness out further as he could see the blood red Ancient Blood World that was now a landmass which was thousands of miles long, the figures of minuscule Vampyres and beings from the Dark Expanse that he had saved in prior battles looking around them with immense shock.

Even closer to the past Spiritual World was the place that he came from, the blue planet he called his home. Its land and seas were already situated peacefully as the Humans, Atlanteans, and Merfolk within were looking at all the splendor.

Noah gazes at all of these regions as he observed the completion of the Realm formation, sending his thoughts to the System of this new Realm.

"Can we already modify the geography?"

[Yes. What would you like?]

"Spread the different Law Spaces out, let every past world and land have access to fate, chaos, aether, fire, water, earth...all the currently available elements."

RUMBLE!

His words were followed with vast and spectacular changes as a flash of multicolored auras rose, particles of all colors literally washing over this soon to be completed realm as the function of law spaces was spread out everywhere!

It did not matter if it was the Supreme Law of Fate or the Law of Air, every land in this realm would have a region that would be populated with an abundant amount of numerous elements, such a thing making way for an unbelievable realm that was packed with not just dense essence, but also giving the beings dwelling within even more abundant elemental essence of any laws they wished!

The gorgeous display of colors only further made the already spectacular formation to become even greater in the eyes of all those watching, those who did not think they could be shocked anymore being proven wrong time and time again...
