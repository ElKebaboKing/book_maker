Chapter 1106 - The Undeniable Descent Of The Great Usurper! I

The folds between the two remaining universes connected with the Universal Constructs were extremely thin.

So thin that when certain beings made their advancement, it could closely be felt by others!

There was a certain being that felt this feeling even more than others as the Cosmic Treasure he wielded reacted to another.

Oathkeeper was within his Sword of Primordial Essence against the Goliath as he weaved in and out of the attacks of over ten other Hegemonies, but he couldnt help but momentarily raise his head towards the direction where terrifying auras arose from!

One was an aura that far surpassed Hegemony, while another was the one that Oathkeeper felt really closely, as it was something directly opposed to the Cosmic Treasure he held!

"Impossible..."

He uttered out in shock as a thought passed through his mind, but he wasnt the only one spacing out as even the Goliath and the Hegemonies around him were looking in this direction while quizzically wondering...

Had the Antiquity already descended?

...!

Yes! Sensing the aura that spilled above the level of Hegemonies, these beings actually wondered if the Antiquity was already descending, but they continued to protect the Construct behind them with their very lives!

---

In the artificially created Ruination Sea within the Liberated Universe.

As the Hegemonies saw the figures of Noah and the Blue Slime emanate power and begin to move, all Hegemonies aside from Chronos cried out fearfully as these powerful beings...actually began to call out weakly!

They didnt call out to the emotionless Blue Slime, but the being that had just stepped into the Universal Realm at this moment.

They couldnt call him the Apex Paragon anymore.

Sensing the aura around his body and the terrifying crimson blade the size of a planet grasped by his thousands of miles long galactic Chtulhu figure...these hegemonies had shameful and despairing looks in their eyes as they began to call out!

In their fear and shame, they addressed this being respectfully for what he was most renowned for, with a few of them uttering out different names.

"Tyranny...Hegemony of Tyranny, please...spare us!"

RUMBLE!

From the title of Tyrannical Emperor they saw this being use over and over again, a few Hegemonies bent over backwards amidst their fears as they called out the Hegemony of Tyranny!

But others took the Cosmic Daos that existences comprehended with more importance as after hearing Chronos shout out the name of a certain Cosmic Dao as well as the stifling environment they were in, they were able to put two and two together and realize the being before them could utilize Ruination.

So naturally, when they wanted to save their lives from being wasted in the Ruination Sea, they called out while trying to hide the shame and pain in their eyes.

"Hegemony of Ruination...please stay your hand!"

WAA!

"Hegemony of Ruination....!"

The glorious calls of certain titles began for the first time as a terrifying Cthulhu grasped onto a planetary sized Sword of Ruination, his figure flickering coldly with the glimmer of a Universe spinning in his eyes as he disappeared in a flash of red light!

RUMBLE!

At this moment, Noah actually tuned out the calls of the Hegemonies as he moved seamlessly in the Ruination Sea while grasping the Sword of Ruination. Its pulsing power coursed through his body as he appeared silently behind a planetary sized Hegemony and swung the terrifying sword.

RIIIP...BOOOM!

"Ah...!"

A terrifying explosion and scream rang out simultaneously as when the eyes turned towards a certain direction, they could see a Hegemony bisected cleanly in half as a rupture ran through his body! An explosion of Ruination essence erupted out with the hit as it caused the split apart body to fly out in separate directions, its aura immensely weakening as this existence was on deaths door right away.

"..."

A single swing of the Sword of Ruination, and Noah had nearly killed a Hegemony!

THUMP!

He could feel his heart beating wildly at such a power, emotions flooding into his mind for having arrived at such a juncture in his life.

To be capable of seamlessly cutting apart Universal Realm experts!

Of course, many factors came to make it possible as Noahs Primordial Clone was at this moment having its power enhanced by +1,000% just by being in the Ruination Sea, and then you had the Sword of Ruination itself that had a 1 million percent enhancement of Ruination Damage.

That wasnt to mention the Affixes attached to this Sword that could trigger Ruination Novas , Strikes, and Ruptures with every swing!

Such a power was so overbearing that the Hegemonies only struggled with their lives with even more fervor.

But Noah wasnt alone.

As he held his blade after cutting apart a Hegemony with a swing of a Sword, two clones of Blue Slimes appeared where the two halves of the body shot out to as they devoured them seamlessly!

The other clone had already wildly spread out its essence to establish its authority as within the Ruination Sea, the other Hegemonies could not even move at this moment. This was why they had called upon the Hegemony of Ruination to spare them!

WAP!

A flash of blue light illuminated the crimson darkness.

WAP!

Yet again, this flashing blue light repeated!

Flashes of blue light were glimmering every millisecond at this moment, this light representing the tremendously fast speed the Blue Slime was moving at this moment in the Ruination Sea as it devoured the Hegemonies within it.

In but less a second, the power of an Apocryphal Antiquity showed itself as even without another point of comparison at this moment, it was terrifying enough as in this single second...the cries of all Hegemonies stopped!

Every flash of blue light was a Universal Devouring, stopping every desperate cry!

All the cries stopped because these pleading Hegemonies that could only struggle madly in the Ruination Sea had been devoured within the span of a single second by the terrifying Apocryphal Antiquity.

"..."

It was such incredulous speed that even Noah could barely follow it with his eyes, and this was due to his clone being fused with the Blue Slime!

At this moment, only Chronos remained.

His eyes were bright with a luster of purple and greenish white, the Daos of Chronos and Reincarnation swimming beautifully within them as he came face to face with the existences that would ruin his goals in this lifetime!
