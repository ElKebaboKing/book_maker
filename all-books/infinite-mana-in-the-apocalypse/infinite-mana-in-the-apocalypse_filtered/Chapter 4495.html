Chapter 4493: You Sacrilegius Savage! II

It fucking worked?

Noahs eyes shone with brilliance as he watched the impossible unfold before him.

It was a long shot, a gamble based on a split-second analysis through the Lens of Civilization. He had looked at Aethon and peeled back the layers of magma and storm to see the core truth of the entity, and what he found was simple and profound. Aethon cared for his people more than his pride.

Was this the first enemy that was actually reasonable?

Noah watched the blue-gold snowflakes settle on the skin of the Elemental Legion like blessings descending from an alien heaven. He watched them close their eyes in reverence. He watched them pay their respects to his Primus Mana, to the Civilizational Authority of Mana and Hunger that wore the mask of Elemental supremacy with such perfect conviction.

The moment they accepted, his existence lurched.

It wasnt a stumble or a misstep but an expansion, as if the boundaries of his being had suddenly grown to encompass territory that had never belonged to him before.

He felt a profound sense of immensity wash over him, a validation that vibrated in his very marrow and sang through his bones. He had made the right choice!

In all things, Noah debated the angles and calculated the trade-offs. Every action was a transaction with Existence. To spare Aethon was to forsake the immediate loot of tens of thousands of Sextillion-level entities, to leave a harvest un-reaped in the traditional sense of conquest and consumption.

But as the immensity settled into his bones and spread through his consciousness, he knew the trade was not just fair.

It was a magisterial robbery.

Because the prompts that bloomed before his vision were not of simple experience or loot, not the mundane accumulation of power through violence.

They were of Glory.

|UNFATHOMABLE ACTION CONFIRMED|

|You have embarked further upon your Way of Existence.|

|You have actively utilized the Unique Quality of The Way of Mana.|

|Analysis: Mana is present and pervasive. It is the basal authority, the fundamental building block of countless weavings. No fire burns, no ice freezes, no earth hardens without the catalyst of Mana.|

|By proving your Mana is the supreme form of their authority, you have initiated:|

|THE CIVILIZATIONAL ENCROACHMENT OF MANA|

|Target: The Primordial Civilization of Elemental.|

|The number of entities paying respect is small relative to the whole, but their acknowledgment represents a conceptual breach. You have opened a pathway to siphon the immensity and harvest of a Primordial Civilization directly.|

...!

Noah read the words and his eyes blazed with concentrated intensity.

Around him, the blue-gold snowstorm became maddeningly serious in its purpose. It spun faster, the flakes growing heavy with accumulated authority, turning the battlefield into a domain of absolute Mana Supremacy.

He felt it then, a terrifying influx of harvest crashing down toward his existence like the endless waves of a celestial sea threatening to drown him in power.

|Quintessential Harvest Descends.|

|Source: Civilizational Encroachment|

|Target: Primordial Civilization of Elemental|

|The harvest of the laid claim has begun. The concentration of this authority exceeds all prior harvests.|

|The foundation of the Elemental Civilization stretches far outside THE Loom and expresses itself even more immensely within it. You are siphoning from the totality.|

|Under your Primordial Civilization of Mana and Hunger, a new feature has risen- The Derivative Civilizations of Mana and Hunger|

|You can now enact: The Derivative Civilization of Elemental under the Brilliance of Mana.|

...!

Holy shit.

What?

Noahs mind raced as he processed the implications cascading through his understanding.

A Derivative Civilization?

He thought that power belonged solely to THE Creature, that only THE Primordial Civilization of Existence could spawn derivatives like The Way of Idle Existence or The Civilization of Effort. These were privileges reserved for the absolute pinnacle of existential authority.

But now... his own Civilization could do it?

Because Mana is fundamental. Because Mana is everywhere. Because he proved his Primus Mana can become any Element!

Oh!

He felt the harvest slamming into the Architects Bulwarks with the force of continental collision. The walls of his civilization were growing thicker and taller and more absolute with each passing moment. He was eating the foundation of a rival Primordial power and using it to pave his own streets, to build his own monuments, to fuel his own ascension!

It was a moment of profound and glorious change. It was a victory that would be marked in the annals of history! Well, however short that history would be.

But choices always have reactions.

Every action has an equal and opposite reaction, whether intended or not, whether welcomed or feared.

Noah had reached out and touched the foundation of a Primordial Civilization with grasping fingers. He had grabbed a pillar of the establishment and shaken it with the audacity of a thief in the night.

And the owner of that house, of course, noticed.

It was akin to opening the doors of a bedroom and taking anothers wife right there...of course they would see you do it!

"You Sacrilegious Fucking Savage."

BOOM!

The voice was deep and terrifying and absolute. It did not come from the air or from any physical source but from the atoms themselves, vibrating with fury.

"How dare you?"

...!

Everything froze in that instant.

The snow stopped falling mid-descent. The wind stopped howling as if cut by an invisible blade.

Existence itself trembled under the weight of attention suddenly focused on this single point in space and time.

DUM. DUM. DUM.

Drums began to beat with terrible rhythm. Not drums of skin and wood but drums of frozen fire and elemental voids, instruments forged from the fundament of reality itself. A symphony began to spread across the battlefield, a rising crescendo that screamed a single word across every frequency of observable existence.

ELEMENTAL!

ELEMENTAL!

ELEMENTAL!

Noahs eyes snapped wide open as recognition slammed into him.

He looked past Aethon and past the submitted legion kneeling in the snow. He looked to the rear, to the distant scattered members of the Elemental Civilization who had not yet paid respect, who had remained uncommitted in their uncertainty.

Their eyes ignited with crimson-gold light.

The glow blazed from their sockets with intensity that burned away their individuality and their choice. They were no longer independent soldiers making their own decisions but vessels, hollow containers for something far greater and far more terrible as their existence could not handle it and already shockingly began to melt their bodies.

A terrifying consciousness was descending upon them. It was forcing its way into the fabric of the Injunction Sanctuary with brutal inevitability, tearing through the barriers of distance and dimension as if such concepts were merely polite suggestions.

The booming voice came from their mouths now, spoken in perfect horrifying synchrony that made the words echo and multiply across the battlefield even as their bodies broke down. This entity did not care.

Just as THE Living Paradox had descended to protect his Vessels when Noah and Khor were about to collapse it.

THE Living Elemental...was trying to descend.

Unlike the others, he was not here for a game or a philosophical debate.

His Civilization, his very foundation, had just been encroached upon by an upstart thief. A burglar had broken into his vault and was actively stealing from the treasury, and he had come to catch the perpetrator red-handed and make an example of such audacity!

Noahs gaze flashed with fierceness and lividity mixing into something dangerous.

He did not cower before the descending presence. He did not apologize or attempt to explain or beg for mercy.

He prepared.

An unfathomable opportunity had opened before him like a door to paradise.

And an equally dangerous predicament had descended to meet it!
