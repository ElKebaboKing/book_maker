Much of these types of worlds came from the memories of the Demon Emperor Baal who was very active in targeting middle and large worlds as he expanded his forces. Some Celestials were opposed to this as they wished for a longer preparation period to orderly move all forces, but even more were for it as it meant they could begin their conquest that would lead to more influence over many beings fates much sooner!

The request of Grandmaster Ramiel would most likely soon be given a green light after it was passed to the Founder, what happened thereafter only being etched in history.

Something new that came at the center of this was the prepared command that would be given to the powerful beings of the Prime Expanse that were moving in the Celestial Realm. They would be told that for them to continue exploring and enjoying the boons of the wondrous realm, they had to send a specific number of beings from their worlds as soldiers to fight alongside the Celestials! They had to fight for the peace and order of the Light Expanse that was being disrupted by the Dark Expanse!

This command would cause for the influx of millions of beings to be in the front lines in the coming war, this conflict of attrition eventually benefitting the Celestials as they would be losing very little of their own forces as time passed.

Thus, many waited for the okay to get things started, Noah absorbing all of the information himself as he continued preparing in his own ways. The Spiritual World continued to bubble with essence, numerous things being accomplished every day by the System that stood as the center of the Spiritual World.

This created the unique environment where two opposing Law Spaces had an inch of a barrier that stopped them from flooding into the other. This was all in preparation for the obtainment of a new inheritance in the Law of Water that would allow Noah to perform Dual Cultivation with these two laws as well!

This time, it wasnt the North where the Fire Behemoths and Chaos Behemoths resided, but the South where one could find Water Behemoths and Ice Behemoths, his goal being to look for the treasures and inheritances here as he increased the efficiency of his cultivation even more!

His rapid rise in Chaos Particles and Fate Lines was going in the right direction, but his Fire Origin Core had remained with a slow progression where even the Supreme Laws could soon surpass it.
