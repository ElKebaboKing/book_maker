Chapter 1090 - Delivered On A Platter! II

A beautiful sight was opening itself up at this moment in the Animus Universe.

On one side was an Apex Paragon and two Hegemonies, the other side actually having 6 Hegemonies standing against them as their very aura caused the surroundings to distort and fracture!

The Heroic Hegemony shone with a glorious aura as his caped costume shimmered gold and red, looking like a valiant hero that was standing against immense evil.

You then had Ambrose bounded on both sides by Solerno and Dark Shadow, with the Hegemony of Fire floating beside them as he raised the temperature of the surrounding hundreds of miles.

Then there was the Cerulean Hegemony!

His body had actually turned into a vast Sea at this moment, building up immense momentum as it expanded to become bigger than 50,000 miles, and it continued to stretch out endlessly!

One must recall that when the Cerulean Hegemony was first introduced within his Universe, this being had been sitting down on a Galaxy as he could expand his body to vast sizes.

This was an innate nature of his body that seemed to be made from an endless sea, the color of this destructive sea shining an illustrious blue as it was fighting for supremacy against the glimmering color of the Blue Slime!

Such was the fantastical scene currently in play! A vast Sea filled the essence of a Hegemony rolling towards a planetary sized Blue Slime that had a starry galactic Cthulhu sitting atop it, a beautiful Hegemony of Summoning expanding her body as it also began to thrum with power!

"Hoo..."

Towards such a scene, Noah breathed out slowly as his fused clone that was with the Blue Slime resonated with each other, their connection becoming crystal clear.

The chaotic undulations of the void around them seemed to slow down as they nearly came to a stop, Noahs mind being the only thing that was moving millions of times faster than anything else!

The Star of Conquest above him shone brightly as it cascaded down waves of the essence of Conquest.

He had gone through multiple other Universes since he last used the essence of Conquest, and it had built up immensely again as when faced with the shocking number of 6 Hegemonies, Noah was actually unfazed.

Against 6 Hegemonies, he wasnt the least bit worried!

His eyes glimmered brightly as in this slowed time, he turned towards the glimmering Star of Conquest as he thought of enhancing another one of the strongest skill trees he held at this moment so that he could carry out a certain fantastical plan.

It was the skill tree that allowed the smooth and simplified usage of the Dao of Chronos...the Dao that he could now use to play with these Hegemonies in a myriad of ways!

It was a Dao that was only a level below the Primordial Dao and the Dao of Ruination, and the Blue Slime at the level of a Hegemony could now utilize it to call forth a shocking array of results against other Hegemonies.

With the descent of an Antiquity on the line, Noah was planning on going entirely crazy for the battles to come as just too many things were in play.

His fight for his own Universal Realm, the fight for the Universal Constructs, and the fight for the Fealty of multiple Universes!

With such things in the back of his mind, Noah gave the command for the pure essence of Conquest to flow into a skill tree.

"Fill the [Wielder of Chronos] with the Light of Conquest."

RUMBLE!

His main body that was floating in the skies above the huge stadium that the main bodies of his subordinates were observing everything in gave the command.

A pristine white color shone as the Star of conquest buzzed, half of its current essence rushing into Noahs soul and Origin as it searched for the Wielder of Chronos skill tree that was entrenched into Noah!

Thereafter, Noahs body shone a glorious cacophony of colors as a powerful skill tree of a Cosmic Dao was enhanced, its effects becoming crystal clear in the next instant.

[Wielder of Chronos] <EMPOWERED>:: A skill tree geared towards a basic application of the Cosmic Dao of Chronos. Abilities include [Temporal Looping] (5/20), [Instantaneous Age Regression and Progression](5/20), [Temporal Manipulation Perception](5/20), [Temporal Halt](5/20), [Temporal Inversion](5/20)

...!

First was the fact that after the empowerment of this skill tree, the cap on its skill points for how strong its abilities could be was enhanced from 5 to 20!

Such an enhancement allowed for crazy possibilities, and Noah would have to undertake many tests just to know the full quantitative effect of such a thing.

But at this moment, he just clicked the plus button as he filled all the skills to their new max skill point cap before viewing their details once more!

None of the abilities of the skill tree, whether it was Temporal Looping or Temporal Halt- had any numerical quantifiable number for the resultant of their skill effects.

So when the Light of Conquest filled them, these skills simply all gained a percentage of the enhancement they received attached to them, as well as a few unique boosts of their own!

[Temporal Looping] 20/20) <Enhanced>:: Depending on the mana used, the user can place a certain area or being on a temporal loop that is extremely hard to escape from. Only those capable of releasing strength to shatter the effects of time itself of beings proficient in the workings of time can seamlessly escape Temporal Looping. Mana requirements are reduced by 90% for this ability while its effectiveness is increased by 20,000%

20,000%.

That was an effectiveness increase of 200 times, and it was written like so for the rest of the skills under the Wielder of Chronos!

Noah felt the strength of these skills scream out from his soul as he finally came out of his reverie.

He looked at the vast Sea that was the Cerulean Hegemony rushing towards him, this being seemingly not caring about the fact that Hegemonies could not hurt those of the lower Realms under the rules of the Universal Constructs!

The Cerulean blue sea should have been extremely beautiful to look at in the darkness of the chaotic void, but it was actually tinged with the green of greed and envy as this being only had one thought in mind.

That was the thought of a Cosmic Treasure!
