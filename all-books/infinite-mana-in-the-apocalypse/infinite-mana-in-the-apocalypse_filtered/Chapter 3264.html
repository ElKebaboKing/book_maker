Chapter 3262 The Oriole! II

The thoughts of a Great One buzzed.

Yes. A Great One!

The one holding the string of Protector Lucretia was a Great One as naturally, only someone of this caliber would dare to make a move on an entity like Savitar.

But for all of his efforts to go to waste- for someone else to reap the rewards of his efforts...

|...|

The Great One mulled silently.

Noah Osmont was a Protector. From the records of Lucretia, a newly added one at that, as it meant he had to be adorned and cleared to not be on the side of Anti-Matter or Dark Matter before he was given such a distinction.

And yet...he was an Outerversal Morphon!

|Interesting...who are you? You cannot be a nobody, and you should have sensed my intervention. Yet you did what you did regardless. You dared to steal from me...|

The Will of a Great One formed a chilling smile as its intent weaved.

For this Entity, he always had the tendency to know everything about his enemies before he made a move.

He found the process of information collection the most thrilling, as after all possible knowledge about his target was known, he could easily dismember them, their power, and everything else under their banner!

Savitar had fallen to be one of his many targets as he knew a great deal about him.

Like where one of his bodies was hidden, and how he could expose it to the Peons of the Commonwealth to have Protectors sent to kill this body of Savitar on his behalf- only for him to reap the rewards.

He planned, learned, and schemed, and when he made a move...he may even use others as he wouldn't make the move himself!

He had lived far too long.

'Too many eons and cycles can make anyone paranoid...'

He had seen again and again entities of Unfathomable power be toppled, their weavings of existence only ever showing shock and disbelief when they perished.

An Outerversal Morphon staring in shock towards him as they believed him to be their closest ally, and yet he killed them.

An Outerversal Morphon who knew nothing about him, but had gained an opportunity of a Grave of the Great Beyonder that held Pure Dark Matter Crystals. He lost his life even before any of this could be enjoyed.

A Protector of the Commonwealth who had a bright future ahead of her as her Sister had attained the Omniversal Stage and she herself wasn't too far off...only to find herself forced to be a Delegatus of the very enemies she was fighting again!

There were many examples of those who had fallen to his whims and plans.

And even with all preparations, things could still fail unexpectedly as they did just now due to something he couldn't have even accounted for.

Due to someone.

'Mmm...'

The Great One felt a thrill rising within him as he found himself a new target.

With the current Will he had here, he could even choose to make a move against this being right now.

But the chances of success were unknown since he did not know much about him.

And even if he destroyed a single body, so what?

He would have other bodies out there.

Some on Megalos Ynnere. Some in Megalos Beyond.

He would find out where every piece of him lay, and only then would he make a move!

'Ahh, I can already taste it...this will be a long hunt!'

His will buzzed with tremendous weavings of existence.

Down below, Savitar had mentioned a State of Existence.

One With Existence.

This was only the first State of Existence that entities who had greatly forged their Existence could attain.

For those who were even grander, their Existence would begin to change and be distorted.

Until they developed something...utterly unfathomable to others.

HUUUM!

Silently, around the veiled Will of a Great One that was filled with endless darkness melded with existence...the outline of an Eye formed!

The Eye of Existence!

A piercing eye that could get past endless lies and discover countless truths of Existence!

This Eye opened wide at this moment and locked onto none other than Noah Osmont.

'Let us see...'

At this stage of power, Entities could veil weavings of their existence very well, even to the extent that their records would be things others could not find out.

Utilizing the Eye of Existence, this Great One was going about it a different way.

He didn't alert his new target, but his Eye of Existence pulled on the records of his surrounding Existence.

Where he had appeared before.

How he had interacted with the Existence of his surroundings.

The terrifying authority of this Great One focused entirely on the interactions his target had with surrounding weavings of Existence and nothing else!

And the Eye saw.

Interactions within this Half Tainted Omniverse.

Interactions leading to a Vessel.

Interactions tracing all the way back to the Omniverse of the Protectors.

It was glimpses- mere pieces, but it gradually formed a story.

The story of his target!

The Eye continued to see as from the Omniverse of the Protectors, it grasped the weavings of another vessel that traversed Great distances.

And this vessel originated from another Omniverse.

'Oh, a newly discovered one?'

...!

Omniverse X-999!

The Great One traced the interactions of Existence all the way back there as in this place...there were too many interactions!

'Is this...one of your homes?'

The Great One that had reached the state of attainment of the Eye of Existence smiled with unfathomable brilliance.

'Let's probe around and discover more, shall we?'

The information he was getting was too sparse.

Only interactions.

They told him a lot, but also very little.

'Let's see, how about using others again?'

His weavings moved.

A newly discovered Omniverse.

This was a prize as what if he led some Outerversal Morphons there?

'Hmm...hmmm!' The Great One hummed a tune as he realized he was enjoying this particular hunt a great deal.

This was because in all the interactions he was uncovering with his powerful State of Existence, he still didn't uncover any truly personal details about his target.

He wanted to know more!

He was a Great One.

He was the Great Ponderor, Nietzsche!

And he would come to know.

He would come to understand.

Only after he understood would he make a move.

And by the time he made a move, his enemy would already be defeated!
