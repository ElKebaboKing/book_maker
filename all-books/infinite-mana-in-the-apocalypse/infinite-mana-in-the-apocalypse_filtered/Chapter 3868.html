With his other hand, Noah called more meat into his mouth. Another bite. Another shock of glorious clarity. The rhythm of chewing, of swallowing, of refining- each one brought him closer to the completion of what even he had once thought unattainable.

A familiar one.

Noahs eyes darkened.

And so... the echoes of Bobs voice rested against the edge of his conscience.

So small a request. So heavy its weight.

He exhaled.

A hand rose to his forehead as the golden glyph shined brighter.

Then...

He vanished.

The Early Veiled Shore stood still.
