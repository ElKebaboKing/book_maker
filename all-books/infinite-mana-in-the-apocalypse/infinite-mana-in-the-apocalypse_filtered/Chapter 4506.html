Chapter 4504: The Infinite Unfurling! I

In the Cultivation Citadel.

Noah sat on the ground with his legs crossed in a meditative posture as crimson-gold flames continued to burn all around him in their endless dance of torment.

Even while withstanding such excruciating pain that would have long since collapsed lesser beings, his expression remained calm and analytical. His eyes were even showing an expectant light.

The reason was none other than the fact that at this moment, after almost an hour of continuous feeding of resources into THE Organ of Civilization, he had managed to form nine Unquantifiable Units of Everything.

Nine Mobius strip objects floated in his head at this moment, each one a masterwork of impossible geometry and concentrated authority!

And his entire existence buzzed with immensity for it.

After the ninth one formed, no more appeared even after he poured in seas of Primus Mana and Civilizational Authority of Mana and Hunger for double the amount of time. The Organ simply absorbed the resources without producing anything new.

So it seemed that nine was the maximum capacity at this time.

Nine could be considered a low number, depending on what terrifying usage these Unquantifiable Units of Everything actually held when applied to problems.

And Noah considered them terrifying from the moment the second one had proceeded to double his 7 Septillion Complexity and Purity, bringing it up to 14 Septillion just from its appearance!

Sadly, the doubling effect did not continue thereafter. With the third appearance and every other appearance of the Unquantifiable Unit of Everything up until the ninth one, his Complexity and Purity only increased by seven Septillion each.

After the ninth Unquantifiable Unit of Everything, his total stood at 63 Septillion Complexity and Purity.

The jump was staggering. From 3.5 Septillion to 63 Septillion in the span of an hour!

Seeing the ridiculous effects that they had just from being formed and remaining inside his existence passively, his expectations were immensely high for what they could produce when actively utilized.

At this moment, he gave a simple command.

"Come out."

HUUM!

The first Unquantifiable Unit of Everything floated out of his existence with graceful motion. It caused the surrounding air to shimmer as if reality itself was bending around its presence. The atmospheric authority in the surroundings instantly began buzzing and increasing in concentration by at least tenfold!

The Cultivation Citadel responded to its presence, the weight of existence pressing down even harder as if acknowledging something of profound importance.

When it came to how he wanted to test it, the description had stated that it could be applicable to countless things.

But for now, he would start with something he understood well.

"Use the Unquantifiable Unit of Everything as the authority to activate Perpetual Harvests Harvest Festival Endowment on The Infiniverse."

A simple command.

Yet the moment that Noah gave such a directive, the Unquantifiable Unit of Everything began to spin with silent glow and intensity.

And then it warped out of existence in the next moment.

Just disappeared completely from his perception!

And a moment later...

BOOM!

Noahs vision became covered by verdant-green seas of brilliance that erupted from nowhere and everywhere at once!

The prompts that bloomed were unlike anything he had seen before.

|The Unquantifiable Unit of Everything disdains its immensity being used for such a simple task.|

|Note: Its breadth is capable of empowering your entire Civilization.|

|Perpetual Harvest has been fully activated with the Unquantifiable Unit of Everything stemming from THE Organ of Civilization as the catalyzing authority.|

|The Infiniverse has gained an endowment of 5,000 Sextillion Complexity and Purity (5 Septillion).|

|The strongest amongst your budding Civilization have also been empowered by 500 Sextillion Complexity and Purity each.|

|Recipients include: The 54 Million+ Primus Inevitabilities, all those who have visited the Early Veiled Shore, and those who share a significant enough connection with your existence.|

|Introductory access to The Seed of the Grand Principle of Mana Genesis has been granted to all recipients mentioned above.|

|The Infiniverse has gained the capability to form The Primordial Primus Mana Folds of Existence.|

|Effect: The Infiniverse can now create self-sustaining Folds of reality that generate Primus Mana perpetually, serving as resource nodes for your Civilization.|

|Current Formation Limit: 10 Primordial Primus Mana Folds of Existence.|

|The Infiniverse has gained the architectural blueprint for The Eternal Forge of Existential Advancement.|

|Effect: A grand structure that can be built to continuously forge equipment, treasures, and existential enhancements for all members of your Civilization. Items produced will scale and continue to scale with the Civilizations overall power.|

|Construction Requirements: Available resources sufficient. Construction can begin immediately.|

...!

After Noah read over the prompts and felt the terrifying waves of changes brought about by the usage of a single Unquantifiable Unit of Everything, he was genuinely astonished.

His eyes flashed with immense brilliance as he analyzed the boons given with careful attention.

The primary empowerment alone was unfathomable. The Infiniverse gaining 5 Septillion that easily was ridiculous.

But the secondary effects were what truly caught his mind.

The Seed of the Grand Principle of Mana Genesis meant that his entire inner circle, millions of Primus Inevitabilities, would soon be cultivating and nurturing a Principle. How hard was it to gain access to a Principle?

And yet now...oh!

More than this, +500 Sextillion Complexity and Purity to all the Primus Inevitabilities and others!

Oh!

The Primordial Primus Mana Folds of Existence was another ridiculous boon to rise.

And The Eternal Forge of Existential Advancement...ah! Equipment and treasures that scaled with Civilization power meant that as his people grew stronger, their tools would grow with them.

All of this from a single Unquantifiable Unit of Everything.

He still had eight more.

In the distance, he felt The Infiniverses presence surge with newfound power. Through their connection, he could feel her wonder.

But again, Complexity and Purity was grand, but it did not mean everything!

Noah looked inward at the remaining eight Unquantifiable Units of Everything floating in his consciousness and asked himself the only question that mattered.

Just how terrifying was this Unquantifiable Unit of Everything stemming from THE Organ of Civilization?

He had to remind himself that THE Organ of Civilization bloomed as something completely new in Observable Existence.

It meant that even in The Earliest Folds, THE Creature and all others with the distinction of THE did not have this as part of their pathway towards such power, or as a component of their current selves!

This was novel and unique to him.

This was his own path being forged, and it was proving to be more terrifying than he had dared to dream.

The crimson-gold flames still burned across his existence, but now they felt different somehow. Not less painful, but more purposeful.

They had forced him to evolve. To undergo a Glorious Evolution. They had pushed him to discover this Organ. They had inadvertently given him a weapon that he really wanted to use to cut down THE Living Elemental!

Noah allowed himself a small smile despite the pain.

"Lets see what the rest of you can do," he murmured, looking at the eight remaining Units!
