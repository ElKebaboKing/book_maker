Chapter 4497: At The Darkest Hour II

In the Infiniverse.

As Noah burned with immense pain that transcended comprehension, Elyndra stood before him with shaking eyes. She could see it clearly, the excruciating agony he was going through written in every tremor of his form, in every flicker of the crimson-gold flames that wrapped around him like chains of suffering.

But Noah kept his calmness even as his body shook. His voice was steady despite the cost it took to speak.

"I wont be able to continue infusing you with Perpetual Harvest so..."

Before he could finish, the space beside them rippled with authority.

The humanoid figure of The Infiniverse appeared with deliberate precision. She stood tall and ethereal, her form composed of countless spinning Omniverses and flowing rivers of verdant-gold light. But her eyes, those infinite pools that normally held warmth for her Master, were particularly cold.

One could see that she was enraged and simply containing everything with titanic effort, holding back a fury that could unmake Folds!

Noah nodded to her with understanding passing between them, and then he disappeared from this domain entirely.

He left The Infiniverse, who had full access to Perpetual Harvest, to continue the infusion with Elyndra in his place.

As this scene played out, something extraordinary began to happen.

Existence itself began to buzz with strange resonance. Drums, deep and solemn, began to bloom from nowhere and everywhere at once. Pianonic sounds followed, ethereal and haunting, creating a melody that seemed to emerge from the fabric of reality itself. Then voices, wordless and beautiful, echoed out across this space. Across Existence.

When the stolen flame descends

When the ancient powers demand their toll

Does the fire cleanse or break

Does it forge or consume whole?

It was as if Existence itself was buzzing with a melody for this momentous occasion, acknowledging what was transpiring.

The Infiniverse unfurled her hands with mechanical precision and began to release verdant-gold brilliance into Elyndra. The light was beautiful and terrible, carrying the weight of Perpetual Harvest within its threads.

But The Infiniverse seemingly didnt care much for the task at hand. Her focus was elsewhere, her rage barely contained.

Elyndra looked at her with concern. "Is he going to be okay? What did that to him?"

The Infiniverse remained cold and silent for a long moment, as if this stillness was the only thing she could do to keep back the tsunami of rage. When she finally spoke, her voice dripped with loathing and carried an immensity that made the surrounding space tremble.

"THE Living Elemental."

The drums grew heavier in the background, the piano more insistent.

The Infiniverses eyes blazed with barely contained fury as she continued, her words a solemn vow wrapped in absolute certainty. "The pathetic flicker of primordial authority that thought it could mark my Master and walk away unscathed. The entity that believed its age and power made it untouchable."

Her voice dropped to something far more dangerous, a whisper that carried the weight of inevitability.

"I will personally grind THE Living Elemental down to existential dust. I will erase every particle of its authority. I will unmake its very concept until not even the memory of its existence remains to pollute reality. This I swear on the foundations of my being."

This is the Brand of sacred violation

The price of touching what was never his

Crimson-gold immolation

Where mortal will meets the abyss

The chorus swelled!

Existence continued a grand symphony!

Elsewhere, panning out across various regions of The Infiniverse.

In an isolated domain of The Abode of The Early Creature, Riya stood motionless. She wore simple blue bloomers and a blue tank top.

She watched with those teary eyes as Noah, excruciatingly burning in crimson-gold flames, turned to leave the domain.

The pain in his movements was visible.

But he was leaving to protect her from what he had become.

The shield steps before the sword

The leader takes the blow

While armies scatter, terrified

Of pain theyll never know

In a different isolated domain, this one decorated with obsidian furniture and dark elegance, Barbatos stood trembling. She wore an obsidian sundress that flowed like liquid shadow, and her normally composed demeanor had shattered completely. She shook with rage and sorrow mixed together in equal measure as another one of Noahs bodies, equally wreathed in flames and equally trembling, also prepared to leave her presence.

"Dont," she whispered, "Please dont."

But Noah could not stay. The risk was too great. The contagion too dangerous.

He left without responding.

Seconds break the strongest minds

Minutes shatter souls

But time keeps passing, relentless

As the burning takes its toll

In yet another isolated domain, this one filled with gently falling snow that never seemed to accumulate, Adelaide stood in a flowing blue dress. The snowflakes caught in her hair like diamonds, but her eyes held only sorrow as she looked toward Noahs burning form.

"Let me..." she started. "Let me feel what you feel. Let me feel the same pain."

Noah shook his head with agonizing slowness, each movement sending fresh waves of torture through his frame. For the sake of his loved ones, he could not even be near them at this moment!

He wasnt afraid of them accidentally coming in contact with him and being branded with the same curse. What he couldnt bear was being around when one of them inevitably came close or felt like they wanted to share that burden.

It was causing him this many issues already. He would not be able to watch his own people suffer THE Immolation of the Sacrilegious Savage as they eventually collapsed their own existence just to escape the pain!

So he simply shook his head lightly, and even from Adelaide, he left.

The symphony continued to play from Existence itself, solemn and heavy.

What is pain but a teacher?

What is agony but proof?

That something still exists to feel

That theres still a will to move

The scene shifted again to show something unprecedented.

All of Noahs bodies throughout The Infiniverse, every single iteration that had been spread across countless domains and separated to prevent the spread of the Brand, began to gather in a single isolated area.

This space was filled with sandy golden dream authority that swirled like desert winds frozen in time.

And then, with excruciating slowness, they began fusing into each other.

He compressed himself back into singularity, consolidating his existence despite the torture it caused.

Until finally, only a single Noah floated in that golden space, trembling but whole.

The humanoid form of The Infiniverse appeared before her Master with silent grace. She looked at his trembling form with eyes that held infinite sorrow and infinite determination.

"Can Master follow me?" she asked quietly.

Noah looked at her briefly and nodded once.

The two of them warped through space with The Infiniverse guiding them, and they appeared above a radiant blue world that pulsed with concentrated authority.

The moment Noah saw it, his eyes buzzed.

This was the Cultivation Citadel!

The feature The Infiniverse had designed where one would be imposed with the weight of existence many times over, where if they adapted they would gain an explosive increase in Complexity and Purity.

But even moving for Noah was painful at this moment. If he added an elevated weight of existence on top of what he was already bearing...

...!

Here the answer to pain manifests

In trembling, burning form

Pain becomes a currency

And endurance, the new norm

The Infiniverse looked at her Master with an expression that mixed love and necessity and terrible resolve.

"Ive been elevating it as we increase in power," she said softly. "The Cultivation Citadel is even grander and harder than before. I know it will be excruciating for Master. I know it seems like you cannot adapt to this pain."

She paused.

"But... I need you to go in, Master."

...!

Noahs body shook with a solemn tyranny.

He looked at the Cultivation Citadel that would make his pain hundreds of times worse, and he truly did not want to enter it.

But this was a calamity as much as an opportunity. He needed to make use of it. He needed to do something to adapt to it even if adaptation seemed impossible.

So even though he did not want to, even though every fiber of his being screamed in protest...

Noah flew toward the Cultivation Citadel.

His body, wreathed in the curse of THE Living Elemental, descended like a falling star wrapped in chains of agony.

Lesser beings would have long since perished from what he faced!

But he persevered!

In the space above, The Infiniverse watched silently as her Master descended. Beside her, RUIN/EDEN materialized with an expression that was pained and heavy.

"We could have waited," RUIN/EDEN said quietly. "For him to adapt a bit more, to see if the pain gets any less..."

And yet, The Infiniverse shook her head decisively.

"It will not get less. It will not get any less."

The surrounding existence continued the solemn symphony as another chorus swelled across reality.

This is the Brand of sacred violation

The mark that even Sovereigns cannot erase

Crimson-gold immolation

Burns across time and space

RUIN/EDEN listened silently, and then her expression shifted to something enraged and terrible.

"THE Living Elemental..." she started.

But The Infiniverses focus was entirely on the scene below. Her eyes reflected her Masters burning figure as he reached the earth of the Cultivation Citadel and tried to stand with excruciating effort.

His knees buckled as his hands pressed against the ground. Every muscle trembled.

But he stood.

As she watched this impossible display of will, golden lines of terrifying wonder began to leak from The Infiniverses infinite eyes as she had never seen her Master like this!

They fell like molten stars, each one carrying the weight of her fury and her pride and her absolute devotion.

"I am going to rip THE Living Elemental apart," she whispered, but her voice carried across dimensions. "Atom by atom. Particle of authority by particle of authority. I will dissect every weaving of its existence. I will make it experience pain beyond what it inflicted. I will make it beg for the mercy of non-existence. I will make it wish it had never experienced existence at all. And then, when it has suffered for eons... I will grant it oblivion and erase even that mercy from the memory of reality."

...!

The symphony and chorus resounded even heavier now, the voices of existence itself acknowledging the vow.

Let THE Ancients tremble

At what their curse became

Not a weapon of destruction

But a forge for his ascensions flame

HUUM!

On this day, The Infiniverse shed a tear.

On this day, the entirety of The Infiniverse, the countless Frequencies of Existence, the Wheels of Existence, the Omniverses, the Domains of Existence, all of it trembled in resonance with her emotion.

And oh, for the entity that caused The Infiniverse to shed a tear... bless their poor souls.

The Infiniverse turned to RUIN/EDEN, and when she spoke her voice was cold and tyrannical and absolute.

"Mobilize the Primus Inevitabilities into all Elemental Folds immediately," she commanded with the force of inevitability itself. "Convert every single Elemental into The Way of Mana, whether by choice or by force. I do not care which. Erase every single signature of The Primordial Civilization of Elementals outside THE Loom. Convert every single iota into The Derivative Primordial Civilization of Elemental under the banner of Mana."

Her eyes blazed brighter.

"Afterwards we will begin devouring and incorporating all Transcendent and Minor Elemental Folds without exception. After that... I shall begin devouring every single remnant of the Shattered First Folds, all the Transcendent Folds and Minor Folds across Observable Existence. I shall take it all. Every piece. Every fragment."

Her voice dropped to something even more dangerous.

"They want to play? Let us fucking play."

...!

The command was heavy and absolute. RUIN/EDEN nodded silently, her own expression grim with determination, and then she disappeared to carry out the orders.

The flames dont cease their hunger

The pain knows no reprieve

But standing in the inferno

Is the choice that sets him free

The final chorus and symphony swelled across existence as The Infiniverse stood alone above the Cultivation Citadel.

She overlooked Noah, her Master, at seemingly his most pained state. She would watch over him. She would guard him. She would feel everything he felt.

After all, they were connected in a Quintessential and irreversible manner.

If she wanted to, she could feel what he felt through their bond.

And this whole time, from the moment THE Living Elemental had branded him, she had subjected herself to feel the inescapable pain her Master endured!

She trembled alongside him and she suffered alongside him.

And she would have her vengeance for it.

Below, Noah took his first step into the deeper gravity of the Cultivation Citadel, and the weight increased tenfold.

And The Infiniverse watched with golden lines still falling from her eyes, each one a declaration of war against those who had dared to harm what was hers like this!
