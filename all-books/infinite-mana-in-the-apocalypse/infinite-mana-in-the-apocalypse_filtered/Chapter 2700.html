Around the same moment in time, within the Nether Haven of Helios.

In a plane of existence located amidst trillions of burning suns, within a domain termed the Royal Infinite Scorching Chambers.

A crimson gold Aurora of lights covered this whole region as behind her floating figure, her grandfather could be seen.

And far ahead of them, 9 crimson gold flaming thrones radiating intense might and grander floated as they were the ones analyzing everything about Ayame after she had taken a Blood Source!

[This Havenly Lineage is one of the most conducive things to seek an Aspect of Infinity that I've ever come across. Hidden within it...I can even sense a thread of Infinity!]

WAA!

A thread of Infinity!

[This thread of Infinity...was successfully taken to Extremity? The one to enact this Lineage has actually made Infinity no longer be a Dead Path?!]

If an existence ever took a concept that was considered a Dead Path and elevated it to Extremity, its Hyperversal Extremity Authority would be born.

Thereafter, it was akin to a forefather taking the steps forward and giving a chance for those behind him to follow, with the chances of others opening up this dead path also increasing if they were given access to the pure Hyperversal Extremity Authority of the Dead Path!

Infinity...was something that nobody in the Inheritance of the Infinite Extremity had achieved.

They only sought Aspects of Infinity in other concepts as a vast majority focused on Heat. Others called it Incandescence, or Calefaction. Others focused on entirely opposing concepts of Coldness!

But at the end of the day, they sought Infinity.

[No, it shouldn't have been taken to Extremity just yet. Otherwise, this Lineage would be something that may change the fate of our Nether Havens. But...we do need to obtain more of these Blood Sources, as well as any connections they hold. You said Ayame obtained these from a...Noah Osmont?]

Another authoritative voice thundered out as it clasped a Blood Source within their hands.

Ayame's grandfather nodded as a moment later, another voice echoed out from a far left throne.

[Invite this Noah Osmont over. In the meantime, the completed Hyperversal Bow of Helios does not yet have an owner...are any of you opposed to using this to obtain everything our Inheritance needs?]

[Nay.]

[Nay...]

No opposition!

[Very well, make the trade with the Infernal Havenly Commerce. Let us see if this is the key that shall finally let us grasp the ever distant Infinity...]

A breath of billowing heat from the speaking Tier 4 Hyperversal Extremity boomed out as Ayame was finally awoken from her slumber, her will gazing around and not knowing just what monumental choices were made!

In the Supernal Haven of Dissolution.

Towards the depths where one would find Supernal Vein Rifts leading to the other Supernal Havens of Extremity, within one of the largest Crimson Havenly Archipelagos.

Across its vastness and quintillions of Lifeforms moving about, towards the very center of this Archipelago- there was a Restricted Domain that only some of those with the purest Celestia Extremity Lineage could enter!

In one such restrictive domain, streams of Vitalis Hyperversal Authority flowed serenely as crystal white trees rose majestically, glistening white grass sewing in the wind as it surrounded a lake of Vitalis Hyperversal Authority.

At the center of this lake, the figure of Emperor Shao's main body could be seen seated in a meditative position as in his hands...he grasped the tool that made it possible for him to attain his First and Second Vitalis Awakening successively.

His tan gold skin reflected its crystalline white luster as it was a smooth round crystalloid object the size of his fist, and yet its weight alone was hard for the past him who was at the Tier 3 Gradation of Extremity to even hold onto!

It was something that could only be forged by Tier 4 Hyperversal Extremities with a strong inclination to Vitalis Hyperversal Authority- and those who also had a Billion Units of Havenly Aurora to buy a Modus Operandi that allowed one to refine Vitalis Hyperversal Authority and forge such a treasure!

It was known simply as...a Vitalis Planalith Krystallos.

A Havenly Extremity Treasure that contained within it the densest and most concentrated forms of pure Hyperversal Extremity Authority as even though it was the size of his fist, it contained inside of it enough Pure and dense Vitalis Hyperversal Authority to fill an entire plane of Existence!

Vitalis Planalith Krystallos!

A single one was equivalent to the baptisms of Vitalis Hyperversal Authority he had received for his grand achievements.

And he...had already devoured 10 Vitalis Planalith Krystallos that were refined by the very same being who threw him out of these lands long, long ago.

They were part of his inheritance that cost Billions of Units of Havenly Aurora as even among the Four Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Lineages, getting one's hands on any Vitalis Planalith Krystallos was nearly an impossibility.

Shao breathed out serenely as tendrils of Vitalis Hyperversal Authority burst around his body, continuing to slowly take in the essence of the Vitalis Planalith Krystallos in his hands when his gaze changed.

He waved his hands to pull out a communication medallion that showed the illusory visage of Seer Amestris rising up before him, her figure unveiled before his eyes as she even smiled!

[Emperor Shao, we are ready to redeem the last favor.]

WAA!

His dark hair shone with a glorious luster. His now golden-colored skin pulsed with incandescence as he spoke with sharp eyes.

[You know my methods. Send over all the information.]

All of the information.

The background, who it involved, their Recorded Histories, affiliations, recent events and conflicts- everything!

He always wanted a clear picture before he ever made a move as information was critical.

The medallion he held wavered and flashed with light as soon after, multiple Singularities containing information floated out as Shao's will permeated into them.
