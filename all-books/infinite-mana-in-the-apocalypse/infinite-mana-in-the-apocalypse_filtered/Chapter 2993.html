2991 Glory! III

The bowstring vibrated.

An immense sense of exhaustion dawned on Noah as instant after, his Dominiums creaked as they could no longer bear the weight of the Existential Omniversal Shrouding!

The dazzling Shrouding began to fade from him as his visage receded further from this area, but his eyes never left the unfolding scene ahead!

Inside of him, Extremity Passages began to surge and seethe as they sought to fill up his Dominiums with power once more.

The Shrouding of the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed was a terrifying one as its results were about to show themselves. But its cost was just as heavy, and with Noah in the beginning sky of the Primordium Stage of Existential Dominium, he could barely handle it while releasing such a concentrated version of its power like he just did.

But for such a high cost...the effects were even more dazzling.

In the blooming battlefield of existential authority and even something relating to the very Omniverse itself being stimulated by a Codex of Extremity, an attack descended on each entity here at the exact same time.

And yet the reactions to them were all entirely different!

Knight Akalusia seemed to sense the attack the moment it appeared, her gaze placid as she pressed her hand behind her- a sea of boundless purple light of Royalty blooming as the heavy arrow disappeared into it.

Sanctus Princess Emilliana was even more obscene as while facing both Knight Akalusia and Knight Palmino at the same time, she didn't even turn her head towards the attack as she called out!

|Dragon's Dignity.|

HUUM!

Brilliant verdant gold light bloomed as the dazzling multicolored arrow was forced to a stop a distance from her, never even coming close to her or her Dominium!

|...|

Noah gazed at this scene unfolding as truly, it was hard.

It was truly hard to bridge the gap of the stages of power across the Dominium Realm as even a tool like the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed could only just barely give a bridge towards this idea!

For the two strongest entities, his attacks never even came close to their Dominiums.

They were too cautious. They were too powerful to allow any variables!

But then, there were others who heldjust a hint of more pride and arrogance in their own power.

Facing the instantly appearing attack, Knight Palmino was in the middle of swinging his sword and cutting apart the weavings of existence itself before him, his eyes flashing as he discerned this attack and affirmed it wouldn't have a major impact on him.

His Dominium could rebuff something like this!

And so...

BOOM!

The multicolored arrow splintered into a heavy singularity of power as his Dominium withstood it easily.

...!

He continued on as per normal.

But he didn't know.

Oh, he didn't know!

[Loot Marker has been applied to a Knight of the Imperium- Knight Palmino!]

Noah's eyes pulsed with brightness as one way or another, Loot Marker was applied to one of the stronger beings here.

And it wasn't over as in the midst of their attacks, the other Imperium Knights handled the newly appearing attacks on their own ways as some swung their Existential Armaments to rebuff them, others allowed their Armors to withstand it, and in the case of the Imperium Royal Basileus Observer Xavier in the far distance not participating in this...

BOOM!

His expression was unsightly as shockingly, half of his shoulder was blown off.

Half!

It was a reality that ascertained Noah's power and the power of the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed- that even at its current stage, its output was actually capable of skipping the Neonate Stage and Sequor Stage to be capable of injuring someone at the Teleios Stage of Existential Dominium Realm!

[Loot Marker has been applied to the Observer Xavier!]

[Loot Marker has been applied to a Knight of the Imperium- Knight Rudiger!]

[Loot Marker has been applied to a Knight of the Imperium- Knight Viola!]

Only a single Teleios Stage entity was injured by it.

But others underestimated the nature of this attack as they shook it off with their Dominiums and Armaments alone, half of the Knight Legion moving a distance away while Knight Palmino began to shout vividly.

|A second enemy hidden in the dark- find them and bring them to the light!|

His words boomed commandingly as 6 Knights turned into lights that spread out towards the surroundings.

Knight Akalusia had a stern expression as she controlled the Codex of Extremity, her left hand waving to reveal the endless purple sea containing the shot out arrow as at this moment...

the arrow began to disappear.

But she already felt something from it.

She affirmed there was a third party besides those from the Sanctus Populi here!

'Perhaps...'

A glimmer of a Weaving flashed over here as at this moment, the battle entered a new phase as the different forces shifted.

She was about to begin utilizing the Codex to uncover everything when...

|What?|

The intent of Knight Palmino drew her gaze.

His golden hair flowed brilliantly as at this moment, he was touching his face with a twisted expression.

This was because gradually, in the past instant after withstanding the arrow that was just shot, a marking began to bloom on his face!

It was a pair of eyes and a devilish smile forming on his forehead as a moment later, boundless golden light gushed forth from the devilish eyes as if they were a passage- Knight Palmino feeling the very essence of his Dominium Soul beginning to leak out senselessly!

...!

The devilish smile seemed to be cackling in joy.

The eyes looked like punctured points of his Dominium that gushed out his authority into massive rivers that disappeared into nothingness!

And he wasn't the only one as apart from him, four other Imperium Knights and the Observer Xavier also had similar markings blooming on their foreheads!

Rivers of golden Loot light leaked from them senselessly as a terrifying extreme began to truly express itself...
