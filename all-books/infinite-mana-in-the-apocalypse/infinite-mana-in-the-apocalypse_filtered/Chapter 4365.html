Chapter 4363: The Glorious Evolution! III

Inside the Chamber of Blood, Titano stood in space that defied his understanding.

The interior was vast...impossibly vast, given exterior dimensions, and filled with those blue vessels hed seen on the outside. But here, they werent decorative. Here, they pulsed with purpose that made the air itself hum with potential.

Thousands of blue conduits surrounded him, each one thick as his arm, each one carrying concentrated Mana that made his skin tingle with proximity.

And then, without warning, they pulsed.

The vessels suddenly blazed brighter, their blue radiance intensifying until sharp needle-like ends emerged from their tips.

The needles shone with green-gold light...the unmistakable radiance of Perpetual Harvest integrated into every aspect of the Chambers function.

They shot out.

Hundreds of them. Thousands of them. Mana-infused needles moving faster than Titanos enhanced perception could track, converging on him from every direction simultaneously.

SHINK! SHINK! SHINK!

The needles pierced his skin.

Titanos eyes widened with shock.

Something had pierced his skin.

His skin, which had been augmented by Lost Forgotten Principle to withstand attacks that could shatter Folds. His skin, which had never been broken by anything in his memory. His skin, which was supposed to be his ultimate defense.

And these needles just... went through it. Like it was nothing!

He tried to move, to pull away, to resist...but the needles were already anchored. Already connected. Already beginning their work.

His entire body was elevated into the air of the Chamber of Blood, suspended by thousands of conduits that had pierced through his defenses as if they werent there.

RUINEDENs voice echoed through Noahs consciousness, providing real-time analysis.

|EVOLUTION PROCESS INITIATED|

|Subject: Titano|

|Current Status: Anchored|

|Mana Infusion: Beginning|

"The process has started," RUINEDEN stated, her tone carrying clinical precision mixed with evident excitement. "Immense concentrations of pure Mana are being poured directly into the very core of Titanos existence."

The diagrams showed Mana flooding into Titanos being, not just filling reserves but transforming the container itself.

"Perpetual Harvest is nurturing every aspect of the transformation," she continued. "Every moment of endurance is being harvested and converted into additional augmentation. Every second he withstands the pressure is effort that compounds into greater results."

Her voice took on more serious tones.

"And alongside all of it, the weight of Mana Haki is beginning to be imposed."

...!

Inside the Chamber, Titano felt it.

Weight.

This was weight of infinity trying to fit into finite space. This was the crushing burden of unlimited power pressing down on consciousness that had limits. This was Mana Haki...the existential pressure of Noahs Infinite Mana made manifest as test of worthiness.

Titano roared.

Not from pain, though pain was certainly present. Not from fear, because Titano didnt really understand fear in complex ways.

He roared because thats what you did when something heavy was pressing down on you...you pushed back with everything you had.

His simple mind didnt complicate the situation with analysis of whether he could withstand this pressure or how long he could endure or what the strategic implications of failure would be.

He just... endured.

Moment after moment. Second after second. Breath after agonizing breath.

The weight increased. The pressure intensified. The Mana flooding into his existence grew thicker, more concentrated, more potent until it felt like liquid metal being poured directly into his soul.

But Titano withstood it!

HUUM!

Through sheer, stubborn, simple-minded refusal to give up because Brother had asked him to do this, and Titano didnt fail Brother.

The pressure continued building. The weight continued increasing. And Titano continued enduring, his consciousness narrowing to single point of determination...withstand, withstand, withstand!

And then, suddenly, something changed.

His forehead began to glow.

Blue light emerged from his skin, concentrating at the center of his brow with intensity that made the Chambers own radiance seem dim.

The light coalesced, taking shape, forming symbol that pulsed with authority that transcended normal measurement.

An infinity symbol.

Brilliant blue. Perfect in its execution. Marking him as something new, something that hadnt existed before this moment.

|THRESHOLD ACHIEVED|

|Subject has withstood sufficient Mana Haki pressure to qualify for Second Tier Evolution, and cannot proceed further.|

|Initiating Enhanced Transformation Protocol|

WAA!

Blinding blue light exploded outward from Titanos form, flooding the interior of the Chamber with radiance so intense it leaked through the structures walls and blazed across the entire Early Veiled Shore.

Every being on the Shore felt it...that pulse of transformation completing, of evolution achieving success, of new species of Early Creature coming into existence!

RUINEDENs voice buzzed.

|EVOLUTION COMPLETE|

|Subject: Titano|

|Final Classification: Advanced Mana-Born Early Creature|

|Tier Achieved: Second - Enhanced Evolution|

|Current Statistics:|

|Complexity: 2,000,000,000,000,000,000 (2 Quintillion)|

|Purity: 2,000,000,000,000,000,000 (2 Quintillion)|

|Heart Type: Enhanced Mana-Born Heart of an Early Creature|

|Additional Capabilities Granted:|

|Direct Mana Manipulation (Advanced Tier)|

|Mana Reinforcement (Can augment physical capabilities beyond baseline through active Mana circulation)|

|Mana Sense|

|Authority Integration: Lost Forgotten Principle has been preserved, is being analyzed and enhanced through transformation|

"This," RUINEDEN said, "is the first Early Creature created after you, Master. A new species never before seen in the Earliest Folds. A Mana-Born entity whose existence proves that your Way can produce wonders that traditional cultivation never imagined."

The blue light began to fade, and the vessels binding Titano retracted their needle-like ends with synchronized precision.

The giant descended slowly, his form touching the Chambers floor with gentleness that belied his mass.

For a moment, he stood there, processing transformation that had reshaped his existence from foundation upward.

Then he moved toward the exit, each step measured and controlled in ways that suggested he was still adjusting to capabilities that exceeded his previous limits.

The Chambers entrance opened, and Titano emerged.

Noah stood waiting, his expression calm.

Because the being who walked out of the Incarnaut Sanctis was... magnificent.

Titano had always been large, but now his form seemed refined by transformation. He appeared as golden giant...not just golden-skinned, but radiating golden light that suggested internal power made visible.

His entire body was inscribed with brilliant blue and white lines that pulsed in rhythm with his heartbeat, patterns that werent tattoos but actual manifestations of Mana circulating through enhanced physiology.

His head was bald, and at the center of his forehead, shining with authority that made reality pulse, was that brilliant blue infinity symbol...mark of his achievement, badge of his new status, proof that he had withstood weight of infinity and emerged stronger!

He came to stand before Noah, his movements controlled despite obvious eagerness to share his experience.

"Brother," Titano said, his eyes shining with wonder, "Titano feels... big! Free! Titano feels... magisterial!"

HUUM!

Magisterial.

The word resonated nearby!

Noah smiled.

"Yes," he said, "Magisterial indeed."
