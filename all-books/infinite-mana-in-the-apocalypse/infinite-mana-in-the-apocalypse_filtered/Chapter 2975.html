Such a thought repeated within the shaky Dominium of Anaine as the weavings of Existence...were ultimately all made up of choices.

And for others like Al-Abalem...they had just lost the home to their Legacy.

One's legend and the legend of their Legacy was determined by their capability to generate wealth.

Existential Domains were one of the main methods to generate wealth as a Legacy that had lost their Existential Domain...may as well lose Tier status as a Legacy!

Unless they could accumulate their wealth again through more dangerous means than when compared to Existential Domains.

Or if they had the capability to start up an Enclave and produce niche products to be sold in the Existential Imperium Exchange.

Ultimately, it was all about wealth and resources.

He still held and felt this Existential Extreme, but he could no longer feel the sense of absolute control with it to the extent that any others using this authority could be suppressed by him.

He had lost the right of its control as he now shared no connection to Vitalis itself, the Codex of Extremity above it, or any of its weavings!

He now had to depend on his other Existential Extremes, and not the one he had focused so many wins of resources into.

|...|

A tremendous loss.

A distance away from this area.
