Chapter 3235 Fabled! I

The next Target of the Equestris Protectors was a body of the Outerversal Morphon Savitar that reigned over a Half Tainted Omniverse, and after this target was defeated, they would be able to destroy the Dark Matter Core that began forming the moment the Omniverse was first Tainted.

Destroy the Dark Matter Core, and the Half Tainted Omniverse itself would follow!

Noah repeated these words to himself as he looked at the calm figures of the five Protectors around him and asked.

"Why just destroy this Dark Matter Core? Why not study it since we are looking for the solution for the Three Body Problem- for understanding Dark Matter, Anti-Matter, and Matter itself?"

"..."

He asked as he received raised eyebrows from Protector Lucretia who was a hidden Delegatus of an Unknown Outerversal Morphon, and Command Protector Valeria replied while shaking her head.

"We've tried many times to study the Dark Matter Cores we come across. An Omniversal Protector even tried absorbing into his own Fabled Omniverse, and the only thing that happened was everything within it becoming Tainted, his Fabled Omniverse acting as an Anchor that the Dark Matter Core resided in as from it came the Descent of an Outerversal Morphon that went on to decimate a dozen Omniverses before he was pushed back."

...!

Terrifying words echoed out from Command Protector Valeria as Noah gained another burst of history and information.

She was about to continue, but he stopped her with an interjection.

"Fabled Omniverse?"

The Omniversal Realm.

He wanted to peer more information on the stage of power of these beings, and Command Protector Valeria humored him as she smiled wryly for the first time and replied.

"I guess you wouldn't readily know or have access to this information yet. To combat the weavings of Dark Matter and Anti-Matter over the eons, the Commonwealth looked at the best ways to purify existence to reach a terrifying pinnacle that could not be easily nullified or negated against them. The answer was the construction of one's existence into the smallest unit of Matter. A Quark."

HUUM!

A Quark!

"Something that could possibly be traced back to the theories of Matter and Anti-Matter colliding. That everything was composed of Quarks or clusters of quarks, and from them...everything came to be. So the Commonwealth devised of a way to purify one's existence into a Quark, and from this single Quark...birth an extremely pure form of Existence. Something containing everything about you- your records, your power, your personality, and all else, but purified to an unfathomable level as at the end, you can establish your Fabled Omniverse."

As she spoke.

Around her body, Command Protector Valeria began to emanate a heavy pressure as a crimson illusory circle of unfathomable weavings began to circle around her.

The thrones they say on trembled and began to move as if pulled by an undeniable gravitational force.

Noah felt his Atlas of Existence erupt with colorless brilliance as it was barely able to resist being affected- his eyes looking towards Command Protector Valeria with blazing brilliance!

"Before this Realm of Power, nothing else matters."

Her eyes were currently aflame with crimson brilliance.

They didn't seem like the eyes of any normal Life Form- but akin to a being that had crossed over some unexpected and unfathomable stage!

"From a single Quark blooms countless others of immeasurable purity, everything about you being gathered to form a Fabled Omniverse. This Fabled Omniverse...transforms your existence in a way that makes it capable and extremely efficient in withstanding Dark Matter and Anti-Matter. Almost as if you have taken yourself back to a time before any omniverses, when only Quarks smashed into each other endlessly. This...is the level you should all be seeking to obtain as only when you arrive here can you truly enact any sort of significant change on the larger weavings of Existence. On the Wheel of Existence!"

WAA!

Protector Lucretia looked towards her sister with a gaze that briefly showed mixed emotions, while Noah looked at her as if he had found a piece of a treasure he wanted to dissect!

Her red hair danced wildly as she continued.

"A Fabled Omniverse is one of the purest and most stable forms of power, and yet when one took in a Dark Matter Core, it became Tainted. So as strong as we have become, Devouring or Assimilating Dark Matter or Anti-Matter into ourselves has never worked. Only destruction or Annihilation provides any leeway."

Destruction was the only path.
