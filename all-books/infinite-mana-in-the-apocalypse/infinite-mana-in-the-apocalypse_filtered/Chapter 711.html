Chapter 709 - Merely an Entity!

In life, many beings have many different ways where they carry themselves.

Some are servile and some are overbearing, with many more staying in between these extremes as they simply pass through life. Some may confidently raise their heads to the skies while strutting about while others hunch over and keep their heads very close to the earth!

Those who can confidently raise their heads and those who can be overbearing- a lot of the time there is something within their psyche that grants them such a disposition. Something that is there to back up their confidence in the face of anything.

Some had wealth or high societal position to back them up, others simply cultivated a strong belief in themselves as they raised their heads high through pure hard work. When it came to the being holding the Trait of Protagonist though...his assurance to act high and mighty in the face of a powerful Entity was his fear of Death being removed!

Backed up by [Plot Armor], Noah in the form of the Void Serpent glanced at the Entity with his serpentine head held high.

The Void Serpent towered over 700 meters with all the buffs, its red eyes glancing towards the Entity who towered over 20 meters. This Entity had a humanoid shape with a face similar to that of human females...yet at the same time, it seemed different!

The face seemed like a mixture of human and devilish features as at this moment, it was filled with wrath at having its authority violated, and violated by someone at a much lower status than her!

"I have traversed the Dark Universe more than many can even dream of, seen things that you cannot even imagine. I have fought Entities proficient in terrifying Daos and come out alive, conquered multiple Galaxies and destroyed even more with ease! Yet today, a creature at the Lower Tier of the GALAXY Realm dares to be wild under my gaze?! You dare to kill when I say you cannot?!"

RUMBLE!

Each of her words were filled with wrath as the oppressive aura she released rose higher and higher, the space around her trembling as the unique essence of the Dao swirled around her.

One must recall that this was an Entity! A title that was given to a being that the prerequisite alone was for them to be in the Peak Tier of the GALAXY Realm. The power of this being...was immense!

Yet Noah glanced towards her with even more calmness and arrogance as the rising pressure she released seemed to not make him falter a single bit.

"I dared! Will you continue talking and spreading out that vile aura, or will you act?!"

SNAP!

His words made all hell break loose as a grey-green light covered the Entity and caused her to instantly disappear, her titanic body reappearing right above the head of the Void Serpent as she pushed out her palm.

Only a palm, but it was filled with a terrifying power that would wipe away a Middle Tier GALAXY expert and heavily injured normal Peak Tier ones!

Her eyes shone with innumerable lights as her gaze became cold, this palm of her seemingly eclipsing everything as it expanded to be even larger than the enormous head of the Void Serpent.
