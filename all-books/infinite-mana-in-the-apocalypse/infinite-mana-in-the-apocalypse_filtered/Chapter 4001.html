Chapter 3999: Be The Scariest Monster! I

The sharp light of transportation dissolved around them like morning mist, revealing the familiar golden expanse of the Early Veiled Shore.

Noahs feet touched the warm sands with the satisfaction of one returning home after a journey through existential chaos, but what greeted his eyes made even his enhanced perception pause in appreciation.

The massive corpse of the Primordial Death lay compressed upon the golden shore like a monument to impossible victory!

Its skeletal form, which had towered over armies of Living Existences mere moments ago, now rested in defeated stillness.

The bones themselves seemed to pulse with residual deathly authority, creating distortions in the air above them where existence itself remained uncertain about accommodating such concentrated ending.

But it was not alone in its repose. Scattered across the pristine sands lay thousands of bone fragments, pieces of otherworldly flesh, and crystallized segments of what had once been Dead Existences.

They gleamed under the Shores light like macabre treasures, each piece radiating the particular wrongness that marked beings who had existed in states of perpetual termination.

These were the fruits of his Festival of War, the one percent that had been automatically claimed from every elimination enhanced by his orchestrated blessing!

Moiraine stood beside him, her eyes sweeping across the grotesque bounty with an expression that shifted from amazement to concern. The golden light caught the worry lines forming around her eyes as she turned to face him.

"Sir," she began, the honorific carrying its usual warmth despite the seriousness in her tone, "was it... truly okay to reveal yourself in such a manner? Entities like Schrodinger..." She paused, seeming to search for words adequate to convey the magnitude of what concerned her. "They are extremely terrifying. Beyond what most can comprehend."

HUUM!

Noahs lips curved into a smile as he began walking toward the massive corpse, his bare feet leaving glowing impressions in the sand.

Each step felt like a declaration of intent, a refusal to be cowed by the scope of powers that existed beyond his current reach.

"I stopped living in fear many, many months ago," he said. "Fear is a luxury I can no longer afford, not when every moment of hesitation could mean countless possible futures lost." He paused beside a particularly large fragment of Dead Existence bone, nudging it thoughtfully with his foot. "But I am curious about this Schrodinger. What makes him so particularly terrifying among beings who can already reshape existence with their thoughts?"

Moiraines expression grew serious, her gaze drifting toward the vibrant waters that lapped at the Shores edge.

In the distance, she could see Ruination seated in deep meditation, her form wreathed in crimson-gold radiance as she processed the gifts Noah had bestowed.

The Infiniverse floated nearby, multicolored jewels across her skin pulsing in harmonious patterns.

The Sacred Totems rose like monuments to achievement, their crystalline surfaces catching and reflecting light in ways that created new colors with each observation.

She took in all of this...this sanctuary of power and potential, before beginning to speak, her voice taking on the cadence of someone sharing secrets that few were privileged to know.

"Schrodinger," she began, "is not merely powerful in the way other Dukes are powerful. His connection to Living Paradoxes runs so deep that some theorize he predates the current understanding of what Paradox even means. There are whispers...never confirmed, always denied when asked directly, that he used to live as an attendant of an Early Creature inside an Early Veiled Shore." She paused, letting the weight of that possibility settle. "Not visit, not observe, but actually lived within the Shore of an Early Creature."

...!

The wind picked up slightly, carrying with it the scent of the sacred herbs growing in their plots, and Moiraine continued, her voice growing more animated as she warmed to her subject.

"His habits make no sense to anyone who tries to understand him through normal logic. He frequently takes on the visage of a beggar...not as disguise or infiltration, but seemingly because he genuinely enjoys it. Hell go to mortal worlds, worlds so insignificant they dont even register on most maps of existence, and simply... live there. Sometimes for years, sometimes for centuries, just existing as a homeless wanderer beside muddy roads."

She shook her head, hair catching the light as it moved. "Most think hes insane, or playing games beyond their comprehension. He rarely makes moves...eons can pass without him taking any action that others can perceive. But when he does act..." Her voice dropped to barely above a whisper. "Every time Schrodinger has made a deliberate move, it has resulted in profound changes across existence itself. Entire Folds have been restructured. The balance of power between Living Existences has shifted. Things that were thought impossible suddenly become inevitable."

...!

Noah absorbed this information with the careful attention it deserved, nodding slowly as pieces of a vast puzzle began arranging themselves in his mind.

"In existence," he said after a moment of contemplation, "there can indeed be some scary monsters. Beings whose power and understanding transcend every category we use to measure capability." His eyes blazed with inner fire as he continued. "All that I have to do... is become scarier than them. And slowly, gradually, Im gathering the resources to accomplish exactly that."

...!

Yes!

There were many monsters out there. He simply...had to be the bigger monster!

He turned toward the massive corpse of the Primordial Death, reaching out to tap its crystallized bone with casual familiarity.

"Yoshinami," he called out, his voice carrying the authority of one addressing a bound companion, "you can have your half."

...!

The response was immediate and dramatic. The air beside him shimmered with crimson-black radiance before Yoshinami materialized in her human form.

Her pale features were transformed by an expression of genuine joy, her crimson eyes blazing with happiness that made her usual cold demeanor seem like a distant memory.

With grace that spoke to eons of practiced ceremony, she dropped to one knee and took his hand, pressing her lips to it in a gesture of gratitude.

"Masters generosity exceeds all expectation," she murmured against his skin, her voice carrying vibrations that seemed to resonate with the spider-essence of her true nature.

Then, without warning, her form exploded outward. The transformation was both beautiful and terrifying to witness. Her human shape dissolved into expanding darkness that reformed as the massive spider, her true form standing taller than the Sacred Totems themselves.

Eight legs, each one thick as ancient trees, positioned themselves around the Primordial Deaths corpse with surgical precision.

One of her forelegs, sharp enough to cut through dimensions themselves, descended with deliberate care. The limb sliced through the massive corpse with a sound like reality tearing, dividing it perfectly in half!

No resistance met her blade-like appendage...even in death, the Primordial seemed to recognize a predator of superior nature.

Webs began flowing from her spinnerets, wrapping her portion of the corpse in silk that gleamed with otherworldly properties.

Each strand contained within it preservation techniques that would maintain the corpses potency while she processed its essence. With movements that made the ground tremble, she lifted her wrapped prize and began moving toward the distant trees where she could consume it in privacy.

A prompt materialized in Noahs consciousness as she departed!

|Yoshinami has begun devouring half of the Primordial Deaths corpse. The profound nutrients contained within this Duke-level Dead Existence will elevate her capabilities significantly. Upon completion of consumption and digestion, the Crown of Inevitable Retribution will be available for use twice within any 24-hour period rather than the current single application.]

Noah watched her massive form disappear among the trees, each step making the Shore tremble slightly, before releasing a long breath.

The casual brutality of her efficiency, the eager joy in her service...it was simultaneously comforting and unsettling to witness.

"Quantify all remaining corpses to equivalent nutrients," he commanded the Shore itself, his voice carrying the weight of authority that expected immediate compliance.

The response came as cascading prompts that made his eyes widen with each successive revelation.

|Analysis complete. All scattered pieces of Dead Existences obtained through the Festival of War boon have been evaluated. Total nutritional value equivalent to: 1,250 corpses of Eminent Early Living Marine Lifeforms. This represents concentrated essence of ending that, when properly processed, can be converted to nutrients through the Shores unique digestive capabilities.|

|Additional analysis: Half of the Primordial Deaths corpse (portion retained for Shore processing) has been re-evaluated. Nutritional value equivalent to: 1 complete Kleos Early Living Marine Lifeform. This represents the highest grade of nutrients ever introduced to your Early Veiled Shore.|
