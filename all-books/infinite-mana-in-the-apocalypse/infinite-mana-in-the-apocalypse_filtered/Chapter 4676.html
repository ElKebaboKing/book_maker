Chapter 4674: We All Fall Down III

The words uttered at this moment finally caused Noah to become somber and serious.

The eyes of Schrodinger which contained the gaze of THE Living Paradox pulsed with the terrifying sense of Paradox as he looked up at the surrounding collapse, flames of contradictory authority flickering around his borrowed form.

|What many dont understand is that countless small, seemingly insignificant things had to happen for us to arrive here,| Paradox continued, his voice growing grander with each word. |Countless things. I heard of your arrival in Ginnungagap. The things that happened there actually had an effect and contributed to what is happening right now. The things that happened in the Earliest Folds, whether you have already been there and done whatever you have done, also contributed to what is happening here. No good deed goes unpunished. No bad deed goes unpunished. Everything that everyone did is the reason why we are all here.|

He paused, letting the weight of his words settle.

|And Existence is far too vast and far too immense, even for me to claim that I know everything. So yes, even I have no confidence that I will come out on top in the Weavings of what is to come. Amidst all of this, it might actually be THE Creature or THE Primordial Chaos or someone else. Hah, I could even think wildly enough and say it could be somebody like you or someone entirely unknown to us. Who knows? Existence is unpredictable like that.|

His voice grew harder, more resolute.

|But in all this uncertainty, I take it a step at a time. I put one foot forward, and then I put the next. And it is why even with all of this uncertainty, I still believe in my Way so much that for me, there can only be victory or there can be death. No in between. As the symphony you have uttered continues to repeat... Existence is a fleeting song, and weve been singing it all along. Oh, Ive been singing.|

HUUM!

At this point of his dialogue, a terrifying aura erupted around Schrodinger as Existence began to buzz all around. It felt like the massive weight of THE Living Paradox himself was descending through this borrowed vessel, pressing down on reality with authority that shouldnt have been possible through such a limited conduit.

The cold and calm eyes of THE Living Paradox continued to stare straight at Noah as his words continued.

|I have been singing, Osmont. And my song shall spread gloriously across Existence as all of this unpredictability, I will do everything in my power to make it predictable. You ask me what The Fallout will be like? Whatever you are imagining right now, imagine ten times worse, and then on top of that, imagine another octave higher. Existence is a fleeting song, and truly... we all fall down. We all... fall down.|

HUUM!

The surroundings of the cage turned terrifyingly tumultuous as flames of Existence descended all around, burning with colors that contradicted themselves and heat that was simultaneously freezing. But the body of Schrodinger continued to keep its eyes on Noah as he floated further and further away with a somber gaze.

The brilliant blue-gold stick appeared in Noahs hands as multiple Logos and Philologies wrapped around him to cause his body to emanate a sense of glorious brilliance that pushed back against the descending weight of Paradox.

THE Living Paradox stared at him as a terrifyingly devilish smile appeared on his borrowed face.

|We all fall down, Osmont, so be ready. And good luck. It was actually pleasant to welcome the end of Existence with you.|

At this moment, Schrodinger tipped the cup that Noah gave him as if he was giving a toast.

|May we meet again in a playing field where there will be no suppression of Existence. Stay alive if you can. Struggle. Fight. Prosper. At that time, whatever power you have and whatever power I have will decide who leaves that meeting alive. If I were to come across you... ah, who knows what I would do. But until then... Au revoir!|

...!

With such words, the immensity that the body of Schrodinger released reached unfathomable levels. Existence pressed down even more as contradictions began manifesting physically around the cage, reality folding in on itself in ways that hurt to perceive!

And then in the next moment...

BOOM!

The body of Schrodinger exploded.

Not just exploded, but was collapsed purposefully, as if THE Living Paradox did not wish to leave any sign of him here. The explosion was controlled and absolute, every atom, every Weaving, every trace of Schrodingers existence being unmade with surgical precision.

It was a message.

I can do this whenever I want. To anyone I want. Remember that.

...!

Noah observed all of this while holding his Goad, his expression somber but unshaken.

His prison looked deformed and almost broken from the force of the explosion, its once pristine blue-gold bars now warped and bent. On those bars, the meat that used to be Schrodinger melted and sizzled, dripping down in grotesque rivulets that dissolved before they hit the ground.

The cage that had held one of THE Living Paradoxs most useful pawns was now empty.

A beggar had died, and existence mourned.

The symphony continued to play in the background, its melancholic notes seeming even more appropriate now.

"Existence is a fleeting song, and weve been singing it all along..."

Noah looked at the ruined cage for a long moment before he turned away.

THE Living Paradox had given him more information than he probably intended.

Even that ancient being wasnt certain of survival.

Even that ancient being acknowledged that the outcome was unpredictable.

And that meant there was room. Room for someone else to claim victory. Room for THE Anomaly to write his own ending.

Noah smiled grimly as he gripped his Goad tighter.

Let whatever wss coming come.

He was ready to fall down with everyone else.

And then get the fuck back up when the dust settled!
