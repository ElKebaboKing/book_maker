Chapter 2862 The Protagonist! II

The Protagonist did not play dead!

With his eyes radiating a profound light of tyranny, he turned around to stare straight into the obsidian brilliance of the Undefined Existence.

He looked into the serpentine eye on its face and chest, and the serpentine eyes on its outstretched wings freely without fear!

At the same time, his existence was undergoing subtle changes as his Colorless Hyperversal Quintessential Extremity Rune Scriptures of the Protagonist began to attain their own glorious color.

A color of triumph and achievement.

A color and glory and brilliance!

Out of all the colors it could have chosen, the Protagonist did not hesitate to radiate a heavy halcyon light as one after another, the Aurum Hyperversal Existential Extremity Rune Scriptures of the Protagonist began to proliferate.

Aurum!

A color of brilliance and glory!

This had barely begun to blaze within Noah's eyes that were staring at the figure of the Undefined Existence when after all this time, this entity seemed to finally come out of its stupor of actually finding Noah as within it, an Intent was about to begin a weaving.

About to begin.

But even at the point of its inception, before the weaving even took place...

WAP!

Like a golden bubble popping, the visage of Noah disappeared!

It was too fast.

Faster than speeds that even Entities in the Seventh or Eighth Gradation of Extremity could explode with!

But this wasn't the speed and power of the Extremity of Quintessence, but the speed and power of his enemy that was weaving an intent against him!

Noah's construct of Infinite Log On and Log Off was activated the moment the intent began to weave and took him to a state of non-existence instantly.

At the same time as the Undefined Existence became stern at such a scene, it gazed down as it felt like somehow, however impossible it was...a minuscule shred of its authority- an insignificant portion truly, had been taken away.

|...|

The situation was astonishing as the Undefined Existence knew...so long as the Insurgent was on the Gradations of Extremity, they could not leave it.

So, its existence blazed as it first sent word that it had located the insurgent, and its intents exploded out as it deeply looked at the surrounding waters nearby!

In this same span of an instance, light years away from the position of the Undefined Existence, Noah's figure reappeared with a flash of Aurum Hyperversal Existential Extremity Rune Scriptures as his visage buzzed with majesty.

{Infinite Log On and Log Off was activated automatically after sensing your Existence being targeted!}

{A miniscule shred(0.0001%) of the Authority of your enemy was successfully devoured!}

{You Existential Extremity Authorities are refining and purifying the foreign Existential Extremity Authority.}

Quintessence blazed with colorless flames.

Tyranny and Infinity surged while the Protagonist itself released a glorious halcyon brilliance- a point of darkness that Noah had taken in quickly becoming reduced and transformed into something pristine that acted akin to fuel.

A fuel that caused his Hyperversal Existential Extremity Rune Scriptures to explode by hundreds of thousands as in addition to all this...

{The Weaving of Extremes is gradually becoming clearer to your Existence.}

{Your understanding of the Undefined Existences is slowly increasing!}

His soul felt the pristine resonance and brilliance as the terror of his Infinite Log On and Log Off construct revealed itself.

Not an instant passed, and he found himself returning to a state of existence in another region of the waters of the Sixth Gradation of Extremity, the visage of the Undefined Existence nowhere to be seen as beside him, the stern figure of a Vitalis Extremity with a stern expression could be seen- a variety of chains around him.

{...}

The first test was a tremendously successful one as it showed Noah a new reality where he didn't have to be fearful of meeting Undefined Existences, and it would be of great benefit if he did come across them as every time, his Existential Extremity Authorities made it so that he would be able to siphon and devour a portion of their- however insignificant this portion was!

If it occurred hundreds or thousands of times, it would add up to a percentage that may rapidly elevate the level of his existence across the Gradations of Extremity.

It would rapidly open up his eyes to better understand the Weaving of Extremity!

It meant that there were many more paths that he could now take. HUUM!

His figure buzzed with even greater power as he continued ascending the waters of the Sixth Gradation of Extremity, albeit ever so slowly and not even close to how rapidly he rose in the lower Gradations. But he was making progress nonetheless as within his Existence, thousands of Aurum Hyperversal Existential Extremity Rune Scriptures of the Protagonist began to proliferate and cause subtle changes that were slowly making themselves prevalent to him!

But, seconds after he began his ascent, vibrations spread across the waters of the Gradations of Extremity as in the far distance, the glorious figure of an obsidian Undefined Existence flapped its butterfly-like wings and surged over.

Its serpentine eyes were deadly as it began to weave an intent, and...

POP!

Noah's grand visage disappeared once more.

|...|

The serpentine eyes of the Undefined Existence became stern.

Once more, it felt a minuscule amount of its authority slip away along with the visage of the Insurgent.

The two were intricately linked as they gave a glimpse to a complex utilization of Existential Extremity Authority that the Undefined Existence did not at all like seeing in the hands of the insurgent!

It showed one of the terrifying truths of Existential Concepts.

They could bridge the gulf of power between different entities across the Gradations of Extremity or beyond it if elevated to a certain level, and this Insurgent...was showing signs of increasingly more adept utilization of his two Existential Concepts!

This couldn't be allowed to continue.

The intent of this Undefined Existence weaved as he sent communications to alert even the Whitehats of his findings, the eyes on his wings opening like brilliant suns at this moment and causing a circular blast of vibrations to spread out across the nearby waters as he felt for something.

And before long, he sensed a blinding brilliance far, far away in these endless waters of the Sixth Gradation of Extremity- a brilliance of a being who seemed unfettered as he continued to rise freely!
