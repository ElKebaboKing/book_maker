Chapter 3345 Disregard! I

The Tresorverse Prison Domain.

This was the distinction given to the area they were currently in as it was meant to hone the way they utilized the Complexity of their Existence!

The Megalonians were mentioned as one had to have lived through the reconstruction to truly understand what this was about.

But Noah knew that the weavings of that Reconstruction would never rise again within the Tresorverse Primaltree of Existence.

It was a one time opportunity as this Tresorverse Prison Domain should be the major playing field for all those who reached the Trunk of the Primaltree of Existence!

A buzzing clamor of majesty echoed throughout as the entities here were those filled with extreme power and positions.

Among the throngs of thousands of Protectors of the Commonwealth, apart from those paying their respect to the Shield of the Commonwealth and the Sword of the Commonwealth who brimmed with shocking power, there were also many centered around another figure.

The distinguished Viceroy himself had made it into the Reconstruction who knew when, and he now stood amidst other Simians who protected his majesty.

His visage...radiated a unique power of the Ordinance of Existence level as there was a veiled Ordinance Amalgamation Simian standing behind him protectively!

The Shield and the Sword of the Commonwealth were also not far away from this entity.

The Symbol of Power of the Commonwealth.

Someone Noah had seen act within an Arena.

The Viceroy!

The Simians treated his status highly as they all continued gazing above at the blooming intents.

|The Tresorverse Prison Domain is a domain of combat, a domain that can be lethal to those weak or unlucky.|

...!

|Once the true Tresorverse Prison Domain is revealed, you will come across Tresorverse Towers of Existence the size of Omniverses. Each tower may only be led and occupied by a single Lead Existence who will get their hands on a Modus Operandi that shows unique means of weaving Complexity.|

|Different rarities of Tresorverse Towers of Existence flow abundantly, with ownership of the Towers decided by strength. The one who can occupy a tower for the time it takes a singularity to disperse will gain its ownership, with heavy gains and responsibilities falling upon them depending on which tower they occupy.|

|Each State of Existence will have their own unique Tresorverse Towers of Existence as only the strongest within a state of Existence will determine ownership. After a period of acclimation, those leading these Towers will have to withstand the assault of a Lineage that has been imprisoned here for the past 10 Cycles. For them, killing you is a condition for their freedom. Life and death will rely on your own Existence.|

...!

A Lineage...that had been imprisoned for 10 entire cycles?

Noah's gaze turned sharp at this as at this moment...

HUUUM!

The surroundings began to tremble as the golden bark they stood on fluctuated.

There were tens of thousands of powerful Entities standing atop it as at this moment, these golden lands seemed like they moved as all around them, reality began to change.

As if a veil was dropped, stellar astral obsidian radiance was seen all around, waves of golden light burning as massive wonder bloomed around them and stretched out for lightyears!

In comparison, their figures on the golden bark seemed like a grain of sand.

Radiant, different colored structures that were the size of Omniverses stretched out, and they seemed like huge monoliths suspended in space!

Each one pulsed with a unique aura of power as the majority didn't feel that oppressive, but there were 81 Multicolored Monoliths stretched out within this Trunk of the Tresorverse Primaltree of Existence that felt extremely oppressive.

There were 9 that felt suffocating.

And then...there 3 that seemed to innately make others want to kneel in reverence.

There were all Tresorverse Towers of Existence!

At the same time that space unfurled around them, everyone also saw different colored strings appear above each of their heads.

|The Tresorverse Towers of Existence are free to contend for, simply choose the difficulty based on the complexity of your Existence and the confidence to withstand and rebuff others for the time it takes a Singularity to distinguish entirely.|

...!

A choice had to be made.

When Noah looked up at the strings, he couldn't help but think of the endless golden strings within the Expanse.

A momentary thought.

After it passed, his eyes that were looking closely at the strings saw some details.

|White String of Existence]:: Pulling upon it will designate one to vie for an Average Sacred Complexity Modus Operandi within an Average Tresorverse Tower of Existence. Of Average Quality, it can at most allow one to increase their output of weavings of Existence by 50%. Many of these towers are available.

|Blue String of Existence]:: Pulling upon it will designate one to vie for a Rare Sacred Complexity Modus Operandi within a Rare Tresorverse Tower of Existence. Of Rare Quality, it can at most allow one to increase their output of weavings of Existence by 100%. There are 81 Rare Tresorverse Towers of Existence.

|Purple String of Existence]:: Pulling upon it will designate one to vie for a Quintessential Sacred Complexity Modus Operandi within a Quintessential Tresorverse Tower of Existence. Of Quintessential Quality, it can at most allow one to increase their output of weavings of Existence by 150%. There are 9 such Quintessential Tresorverse Towers of Existence.

|Gold String of Existence]:: Pulling upon it will designate one to vie for an Origin Sacred Complexity Modus Operandi within an Origin Tresorverse Tower of Existence. Of Origin Quality, it can at most allow one to increase their output of weavings of Existence by 500%. There are 3 such Origin Tresorverse Towers of Existence.

The Omnifather very clearly drew inferences from the tiers of Megalonians in designing this unique domain!

Average, Rare, Quintessential, or Origin Tresorverse Towers of Existence!

Only a single Lead Existence could take hold of a single Tower- and they were exclusive to a single state of Existence!
