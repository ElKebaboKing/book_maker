Chapter 4960: The Spread of Infinities! IV

Noah watched as a lotus began to bloom with petals that unfolded in perfect sequence.

The first petal opened, revealing blue-gold light within. Before that petal finished settling, the second petal began opening. Then the third. Then the fourth. The progression continued endlessly, the lotus never finishing its bloom but simply continuing to produce.

It was beautiful.

Not beautiful like conquest was beautiful, or like power was beautiful. Beautiful like...watching something grow. Beautiful like patience rewarded.

Beautiful like the simplest truth in existence: plant something, tend it, and it flourishes.

Oh!

|This herb grows in spiraling patterns that mirror the sequential nature of Countable Infinity. Its petals unfold one after another in endless progression, each petal containing the seed for the next. The lotus never finishes blooming. It simply continues producing petals in sequence.|

Noah stepped forward and reached toward the lotus!

His fingers touched one of the completed petals. The warmth exceeded physical temperature. The weight exceeded the delicate appearance. Within it, he could sense Bounded Countable Infinity already formed and waiting.

A Seed ready to be planted in whoever consumed it.

He plucked the petal carefully.

The lotus didnt diminish. It simply continued blooming, producing more petals to replace what he had taken, the sequence uninterrupted by his harvest.

"One petal," he said, examining it. "One follower elevated to Bounded Countable Infinity. Without spending my own reserves."

He looked at the field of lotuses. Dozens of them. Each one blooming endlessly.

|Effects Upon Consumption: A follower who consumes petals from THE Bounded Lotus of Sequential Eternity receives Bounded Countable Infinity directly integrated into their Civilization. |Second Classification: THE Convergent Root of Density Absolute.|

The roots didnt spread wide. They dove deep, following the shortest paths through the earth to find and compress every fragment of Infinity within their reach.

|This herb grows downward rather than upward, its roots extending into the soil along geodesic paths that curve toward concentrations of Infinite authority. Where it finds such concentrations, it wraps around them and draws them inward, compressing Infinity into density that exceeds its original configuration.|

Noah knelt and reached into the soil, his authority allowing him to perceive what was happening beneath the surface!

The roots had wrapped around units of Infinite Observable Harvest he had buried. They were squeezing those units, compressing them, forcing sequential expressions closer and closer together until gaps began filling.

|What enters the root as sequential Countable expressions emerges as something denser. Something approaching the spaces between sequences.|

One root had completed its compression!

Noah grasped it and pulled, feeling resistance that spoke of weight exceeding what the roots size suggested. When it emerged from the soil, it glowed with density that made his eyes narrow.

This was... what he needed for his own advancement.

Advancing others was great and all, but he needed something for himself!

He held the root, feeling the concentrated Infinity within it pressing against his perception. Years of saturation, compressed into something he could consume in an instant.

"The lotus elevates my followers," he said slowly. "The root elevates me."

He looked between the two fields.

|Effects Upon Consumption: You experience saturation acceleration at whichever Depth you direct the roots compressed authority toward. The root has already performed the work of filling gaps between your sequential Infinite expressions. Consuming it introduces pre-densified Infinity directly into your foundations, aiding your advancment towards your progression from Countable toward Uncountable Infinity.|

First, it was a Lotus, and second, it was a root!

And the third and final wonder to bloom in his farming fields of infinity?

|Third Classification: THE Divergent Bloom of Infinite Variation.|

Noah moved to the third field, where chaos reigned in beautiful form!

Plants grew in every direction simultaneously, their branches splitting endlessly as they extended toward the horizon.

No two branches followed the same path. Flowers bloomed at random points along each branch, and no two flowers held identical colors or shapes. Some were blue. Some were gold. Some were colors that shouldnt have existed.

Each one unique.

Each one different.

Each one a possibility he hadnt conceived.

|This herb grows outward in all directions simultaneously, its branches splitting endlessly as they extend toward the horizon. No two branches follow the same path. No two flowers that bloom upon those branches hold identical properties. The herb embodies divergence, spreading its Infinite nature across every possible configuration.|

Noah reached toward a flower that caught his attention!

It was crimson and gold, its petals arranged in spirals that seemed to twist in directions his perception couldnt quite follow. When he plucked it, he felt something shift in his awareness.

A glimpse of possibility that his current state couldnt normally access.

A preview of the Boundary he was working toward.
