Chapter 1124 - A Universe Aspiring To Become A Cosmos! I

A uniquely shining Primordial Heart floated above Noahs palm as the Oathkeepers gaze inadvertently locked onto it.

He wasnt the only one as the Hegemonies of the Primordial Cosmos were watching these scenes from afar, none of these beings having even had the chance to help to contribute anything with how things had rapidly progressed!

Currently, the most astounding moment had come as the being that was a Paragon just moments ago had now become an Antiquity and had just appeared with the pulsating Heart he was given not too long ago, seemingly about to hand it to the Oathkeeper!

But at this moment, a miniaturized Blue Slime just a few inches in diameter appeared beside the valiant figure of the Oathkeeper, Noah giving a light smile at this guy as the Slime made sure to call dibs first on the remaining Primordial Heart before anyone else!

Noah nodded towards the true body of the Blue Slime as a blue tentacle shot from it, wrapping around the Primordial Heart as a resplendent glow erupted out from the process of absorption beginning.

The Blue Slime was already an Apocryphal Antiquity as he had begun to form a second Universe, but it seemed the true authority of an Antiquity was always given after one completed the first full Splendiferous Universe!

After the constant Devouring of the Universal Constructs with the authority of the...being that shared a similar name to Noah, the Slime now only had 5 Billion Galaxies to be changed into Dao Galaxies, the soft glow of the Primordial Heart flowing into him as this process ensued.

THRUM!

A cerulean glow flashed out as it drew the eyes of the Hegemonies from all around, the unique phenomenon of a being stepping into the ranks of Antiquity actually playing out right before their eyes as they couldnt not pay attention!

The Origin of the Blue Slime shone with an iridescent glow in the next few seconds as a few billion Dao Galaxies bloomed, the Origin vibrating in resonance soon after as a full 100 Billion Dao Galaxies now flowed within it.

WAA!

The aura of the Blue Slime gradually changed as it became even more oppressive, but its cerulean eyes flashed with light as it didnt get drunk on the feeling of absorption of the Primordial Heart, the tentacles it released letting this treasure go as it floated towards Noah.

Noah himself watched the scene of the birth of another genuine Antiquity with a smile, simply nodding towards the Oathkeeper to absorb the Primordial Heart next as after some slight hesitation...this ancient being hardened his resolve and went forward!

---

The vast numbers of Hegemonies were looking at the incredulous scenes occurring within the Abyssal Universe in shock as they didnt even dare to approach at this moment!

The sequence of events just had too many shocking results as from an Antiquity descending because of Chronos and the Goliath along with many others Hegemonies seeking a method to achieve it themselves...all the way to this moment where these Hegemonies were missing or dead along with the Antiquity they called down.

Instead, the Apex Paragon that had shown shocking prowess was the one to inexplicably rise to Antiquity along with his Blue Slime, and now the aura of the Oathkeeper was changing as well as this being also went towards the same direction!

It was a truly ridiculous sight as the ones who were trying to prevent the descent of Antiquity were the ones to actually obtain Antiquity in the end, those searching for it like Chronos and The Goliath missing out...

The Great Old Nazzagath looked at this scene with eyes that flashed with intense light, recalling the words of the Apex Paragon that was now an Antiquity.

Fealty...is that what Oathkeeper has now done? Just that and given the chance to advance to Antiquity?

The thoughts of the Ancient Chthonian were his own as he and many others watched the scene before them in wonder!

They watched as the golden wings and aura around the Oathkeeper expanded rapidly after he began to absorb the Primordial Heart, his eyes shooting out majestically beams of light as he bellowed out like the primal ancient being that he was.

"OOOOOH!"

This bellow was followed by the continued rapid conversion of normal Galaxies into Dao Galaxies as every few seconds, billions would be added as this being put together the less than 40 billion that he needed for Antiquity.

The scene was one of utter wonder and awe as nobody could take their eyes off of it, even the Hegemonies counting the number of converged Galaxies as after a few minutes...they neared the complete 100 Billion just like the Blue Slime moments ago.

The surrounding space shook as a golden glow washed over the Oathkeeper, his entire body being covered seemingly with liquid gold when the 100th Billion Dao Galaxy was reached, rays of light stretching from him as if he was breaking out of a cocoon and becoming something else!

RUMBLE!

After the golden waves of power settled down, the figure of the Oathkeeper became visible again as the eyes of the Hegemonies trembled when they saw him.

He didnt have the usual pair of wings, this existence currently having three pairs of shimmering golden wings behind him as he had grown to be nearly 4 meters, his body fully suffused with a golden glow as he looked like a primal creature gracing lower worlds with his presence!

Noah looked at such a scene and nodded as he sent multiple mental messages to the Oathkeeper, the figures of his Primordial Clone and the Blue Slime disappearing soon after.

All that was left behind...was the newly risen Antiquity and 12 white eggs that contained Hegemonies that worked with Chronos, the Oathkeeper glancing at these prisons made of Primordial Essence as he then glanced towards the Hegemonies that didnt dare approach before.

"Come. It is time for healing and restructuring!"

THRUM!

His commanding voice reached the Hegemonies as it raised them out of their stupor, Great Old Nazzagath and the others moving into the Abyssal Universe at this moment!
