But even while feeling immense wrath, nearly all of them were hesitant to make a move as they didnt know the depth of the Fiends strength!

So the Imperial Adjudicator focused on reestablishing order, and as soon as this was done he moved on to the next important task .

One would be able to notice that among many of the names, Alexander would be popping out with frequency as he was discussed with much fervor!

"A High Imperial at the Realm of Black Hole who can match Quasar?"

"Dont forget that hes a Life Law Master as well, and apparently also dabbling in Fate and Aether..."

"I heard he is always with the Princess, is there...?"

"Dont even finish that sentence if you want to keep your life! Do you know who she is?!"

Words and rumors spread around as the situation gradually settled . "Little Anna, we need to go now . "

"Tell him to come here, quickly . "

Anna hid her surprise in her veiled face as she quickly sent a message to Noah, who had just finished obtaining the techniques for the Law of Death in the Imperial Library!
