2825 The Dungeon System of Extremity! III

A chance for the inhabitants of the Infinite Hyperversal Haven to elevate their existence.

A chance for them to attain glory and honor within the Existential Raid Rift as it served multiple purposes all at once!

[The Extremity of Loot relishes in the forging of the Dungeon System of Extremity!]

[Loot Caches shall flow in overabundance for those worthy!]

The scene expressed within the massive rift the size of a Haven flashed as above the figures of millions of Enders of Extremity, Golden, Obsidian, and resplendent Crimson colored treasure chests and treasure caches appeared.

They gave off an enticing look as the very souls of Entities quivered when they looked at them!

Thereafter, the scene within the Existential Raid Rift disappeared as a cerulean radiance bathed everything, its interior a mystery until those who sought to enter approached it.

[The Extremity of Quintessence expresses that there is no need to worry about any forces outside of the Infinite Hyperversal Haven- their influence cannot reach here.]

[Focus on seeking greater power and glory of your Existence, focus on Extremity...and everything else will follow.]

...!

They had no need to worry about Vacuous or Vitalis Malignancies entering this domain and causing any wars.

No distinction of Clans or Inheritances!

No worry of the Ones Above Extremity!

There was only...the search for Extremity.

The millions of Extremities from different factions looked at each other with heavy breaths at such a scene.

Each one held their own thoughts as at this moment, their souls were pulling them towards the dazzling cerulean Existential Raid Rift.

In a corner of the Infinite Hyperversal Haven, the visage of Lilith had left the Cerulean Instance Dungeon Portal of Infinity as she gazed at other Portals and the even more massive Existential Raid Rift.

There were many choices to choose from, but her soul told her that undoubtedly, the Existential Raid Rift would have the most rewards!

With its size being akin to that of a Haven, anyone could be near its light and majesty.

Lilith only floated close to it before a cerulean bordered box rose in front of her eyes.

[You can now enter the First Existential Raid Rift of the Infinite Hyperversal Haven.]

[The Difficulties available to select are Golden Difficulty, Obsidian Difficulty, Crimson Difficulty, and Tyrannical Difficulty.]

The glimmering test box in front of her was a dazzling one as just for the sake of seeing more about this System, Lilith went ahead and selected the Tyrannical Difficulty!

[You have selected the Tyrannical Difficulty.]

[A Minimum of 9,999,999 Participants are required for entry into the Mode of Tyrannical Difficulty, or one can bypass this by expressing power equivalent to the Sixth Gradation of Extremity and test the might of their Concepts by going Solo.]

[Possible Loot that can be obtained in the Mode of Tyrannical Difficulty]:: Zenith Middle Grade Armament of Extremity, Middle Grade Quintessence Planalith Krystallos, Middle Grade Infinity Planalith Krystallos Three Volumes of the Quintessential Breath of Extremity Modus Operandi, Quintessential Set Middle Grade Armaments, Infinity Set Middle Grade Armaments, Tyrannical Set Middle Grade Armaments...

[Based on your Existence, it is recommended for you to start on the Mode of Golden Difficulty. The chances of meeting a False Death within 1 Millisecond in the Mode of Tyrannical Difficulty are 99.99%.]

[You can join the Queue if you still wish to participate in the Mode of Tyrannical Difficulty.]

[Current Queue]:: 0/9,999,999

[...]

Lilith was speechless at the prompts before her as she gritted her teeth before selecting the Golden Difficulty.

[A Minimum of 9 Participants, up to a maximum of 9,999 Participants are required for entry into the Mode of Golden Difficulty, or one can bypass this by expressing power equivalent to the Third Gradation of Extremity and test the might of their Concepts by going Solo.]

[Possible Loot that can be obtained in the Mode of Golden Difficulty]:: 10% Chance of a Low Grade Armament of Extremity, 10% Chance of a Low Grade Loot Planalith Krystallos, 10% Chance of a Low Grade Protagonist Planalith Krystallos, 10% Chance of a Quintessential Set Low Grade Armament, Early Entry Pass....

[Current Queue]:: 6/9,999, 3/9,999, 1/9,999...

The possibilities of Loot were endless, and it seemed like one had to successfully enter, clear, and repeat this Raid Rift Difficulty multiple times in order to gain the truly precious things it offered!

With a more powerful party...

Lilith gritted her teeth at this as she joined the randomized que.

Connections were a form of strength, and she had not forged any.

She couldn't be a part of a powerful party with Entities like Barbatos, Kazuhiko, or the Emperor Penguin that were always close to the side of the Extremity of Quintessence.

But...she could still try and see how far she could rise!

With her eyes blazing with Flames of Hatred, she began a new Queue as the party leader as she would decide how many random Entities joined in before she began the raid!

Realities like this began to play out all across the Infinite Hyperversal Haven as nobody wanted to be the last.

Those who understood things a little bit more knew that for the greatest achievements, they had to be first!

[Let's go.]

In a certain region, the Son of the Extremity of Quintessence came to bathe in the light of the Existential Raid Rift as behind him stretched out hundreds of the core powerhouses of the Infinite Hyperversal Haven.

RAA!

A bellow of pride came from the Emperor Penguin as Kazuhiko, the Kraken, Sword Emperor, Guinevere, and many others followed behind him!

He knew his own limits and the limits of those who followed him as the Son of Destiny chose the Mode of Obsidian Difficulty.

[A Minimum of 99 Participants, up to a maximum of 99,999 Participants are required for entry into the Mode of Obsidian Difficulty, or one can bypass this by expressing power equivalent to the Fourth Gradation of Extremity and test the might of their Concepts by going Solo.]

[Possible Loot that can be obtained in the Mode of Obsidian Difficulty]:: 50% Chance of a Low Grade Armament of Extremity, 50% Chance of a Low Grade Apocalypse Planalith Krystallos, 50% Chance of a Low Grade Harems Planalith Krystallos, 50% Chance of a Quintessential Set Low Grade Armament, 10x Early Entry Pass....

This information flashed in the mind of Henry and those that followed him as...

[You are the leader of a preformed Party of 588 Participants.]

[You have met the minimum requirement to enter.]

HUUM!

The eyes of the Extremity of Destiny flashed brightly as with a daring glimmer, he made the decision for all those behind him.

[Enter!]

...!

A blinding flash of light boomed out.

And a party exceeding 500 Entities delved into the Obsidian Difficulty of the Existential Raid Rift!
