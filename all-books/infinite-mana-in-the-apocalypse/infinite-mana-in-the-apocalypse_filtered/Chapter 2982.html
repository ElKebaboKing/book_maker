Chapter 2980 Imperium Knights Dispatched! I

As the Existential Omniversal Emperor began to produce Fruits of Existence within his own Dominium, weavings of his actions were spreading outwards- their effects beginning to unfold at this moment.

Across the endless expanse of the Omniverse.

At speeds unfathomably faster than light, in a manner that couldn't be fully captured due to the Existential light covering it...a massive circular vessel crossed the Omniverse towards one of the many, many renowned domains of the Imperium.

Within this vessel, a space the size of a plane of existence was contained within as at the very center of this space, there was an area surrounded by an expanse of a golden sea that currently had a single being and his attendants lined up to his left and right.

Around him, a dense number of illusory screens filtered out weavings of information, his glorious visage and golden hair simmering with a serious expression at this moment as he suffered a loss that he had to report!

|Master, we have received permission to warp in.|

To his right, an attendant at the Neonate Stage of the Existential Dominium Realm bowed slightly while sending the news.

The golden haired observer nodded as he twisted his hand, a command being activated as the whole vessel disappeared right away.

By the time it reappeared, the blank darkness around everything had begun to be lit up as waves of blue and red auroras began weaving here and there.

It was the glory of an extremely potent Existential Domain under the banner of the Imperium.

As they came closer, the bright red and blue auroras only became more brilliant as far ahead of them, an unfathomable concentration of power and might was gathered.

The golden haired observer sighed with both relief and grimness after returning here, the voice of his vessel echoing out as it began to slow down and come to a stop.

{We have arrived at Cappadocia.}

...!

Cappadocia!

A bustling Existential Domain of the Imperium that held one of the three Existential Lineages of the Imperium!

The vessel of the Observer came to a stop in an empty area as not too far from him, other uniquely shaped vessels were also anchored.

The surroundings were entirely filled with auroras of red and blue light that seemed to stretch out endlessly, with the area ahead of them shining with the most blinding light as this was their destination!

The Observer warped outside of his vessel with his attendants, their figures disappearing soon after as they became wrapped in an existential light.

They were guided by a specified weaving, their bodies transported across the vastness of this Domain as they were directly taken into the foldings of a plane of existence.

At the center of such a plane of existence, an advanced fantastical citadel floated grandly, its exterior pulsing with existential authority as initially, the golden haired observer and his attendants arrived within the walls of this citadel that were bustling with tens of thousands of entities.

But a moment after his arrival, his figure alone became wrapped by a light and disappeared, his attendants being left behind as he was taken towards a central castle atop this floating citadel!

When the golden haired observer appeared again, he found himself standing in a blue hall lined with Planalith Krystallos as decorations, his gaze directed towards the end of the hall that held a red throne behind a crystalline table filled with golden pages.

On the throne, an old man took off his glasses while raising his brows towards the golden haired observer, becking with his hands as a shocking attractive force pulled the Observer forward to stand directly before him!

|I perused through the weavings you've already sent over. An entire Existential Domain perished, and the culprit is a Seed controlled by Omniversal Pillagers...does that make fucking sense to you, Xavier?|

HUUM!

A terrifying pressure descended as a blazing transparent golden light of an utterly heavy Dominium seemed to be emanating from the old man.

It only pressed down lightly on the transparent purple light of the Dominium of the Observer called Xavier, but this Royal kneeled down with a somber expression as he couldn't say anything!

|Hmph, and now you request for the mobilization of the Cappadocia Imperium Knights to track down this...Noah Osmont?|

|Yes, Imperium Martial King. I believe this will be suitable to confirm exactly who is behind this Seed...|

Observer Xavier replied with his head bowed down as the entity he referred to as the Imperium Martial King sneered while shaking his head.

Observers, Knights, Martial Kings!

These were their rankings within the Imperium as Observers traversed to different areas of the Omniverse and were tasked with overseeing the workings of the countless Existential Domains out there.

Knights enforced the rules of the Imperium as it was the highest honor of an Imperium Royal to be chosen as a Knight!

And Martial Kings...one like this entity before Observer Xavier controlled important locations of the Imperium, with Cappadocia Enclave only being one of them as this Entity could actually command and direct Legions of Knights.

This seemingly harmless old man was an amalgamation of terrifying power that Xavier had to bow down to, waiting for the final command as this Entity's voice came out calmly while he stamped a golden page on the crystalline desk.

|The excuse of Omniversal Pillagers behind a Seed is useless. Next time, at least place a suitable blame on something as critical as the disappearance of an entire Existential Domain- this is something the Imperium demands an answer to as due to this...The Red Legion of the Cappadocia Imperium Knights will be mobilized, you're lucky they're stationed here for their vacation.|

...!

Observer Xavier's body trembled at such words!

The Red Legion of the Cappadocia Imperium Knights!

Wasn't this the Legion in charge of overseeing the protection of certain dangerous regions where Omniversal Pillagers frequented?!

Knights of the Imperium.

Their bodies blazed with Dominiums of purple brilliance as each one could be the head of a Legacy.

And yet they gained much more in their position as Cappadocia Imperium Knights- an entire Enclave standing behind them!

|Hunt down and capture this supposed Seed- Noah Osmont. His location is approximately...|

HUUM!

Towards an Entity that had yet to become well known within the Imperium!
