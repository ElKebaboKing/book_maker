Chapter 784 - Successor of a Great Sage!

On the base floor of the Great Sage Aoin Tower, the Holy Descendants were watching in pure shock as a monstrous being actually faced an enemy at the Peak Tier GALAXY Realm without being killed right away.

"That is the essence of the Law of Life? It can be used to stand against a Lesser Dao like this?!"

"None of that matters, how the hell has his essence not run dry while supporting all of his pets with Dao Essence and using all these abilities..."

Discussions ran rampant as they watched the shocking scenes, these beings not being able to explain what they were seeing as they could only speculate!

Anna watched the entire battle with shining eyes as she looked at this figure with many mixed thoughts.

Appearing as a genius in front of her just weeks ago, and finding out he was the Ruler of an entire Galaxy- along with the one that used to be her home, not too long ago!

Yet he didnt act high and mighty as many other Rulers, aside from being overbearing and domineering towards his enemies of course. When she looked at his figure, she only saw the wild eyes that seemed to always be filled with a myriad of thoughts, his figure getting increasingly bigger in her eyes as her pride made her strive to achieve the level of strength she was seeing.

She was a genius too. She held a shocking Trait of a Vector Controller. She embarked on Dao while using the bland technique from the Expansive Dragon Cl.u.s.ter in a short period of time! So why could she not do what Noah was doing right now?!

RUMBLE!

Her thoughts remained hers as she only watched while the Peak Tier GALAXY Wandering Void Tarantula had its numerous legs begin to be devoured, a flash of an Obsidian Panther rupturing its eyes every now and then as the deadly plasma rays from the Colossal Helios Leviathan thundered down.

It was the first battle that actually took Noah some time, having to spent over half an hour to get through the defenses of this being that was utilizing the Dao of Fortification!

ROAAR!

The Wandering Void Tarantula let out an unwilling bellow as the light of teleportation covered it, the entity overseeing the Tower observing that its life was actually in danger as Noah...cleared the last fight in the combat test!

[Legacy Disciple #1(Alexander) Test of Combat, Exceptional!]

The cold voice reverberated out as Noah unmerged himself from the Void Serpent, giving a command as all his three pets returned into his Origin. He waited expectantly as he wondered how many points he would receive, and which Peak Tier GALAXY Animus Summons he would choose with them.

[Legacy Disciple #1(Alexander)- Points...Null]

RUMBLE!

Noahs gaze shook at these words as he looked around him incredulously!

On the base floor, the screen that showed Noah disappeared as the Holy Descendants and Anna looked towards each other questioningly, not knowing what had exactly occurred.

All the eyes turned on the screen where the name of the first Holy Descendant who had taken the Trial first was listed as they hoped to see an extra name and points, but nothing appeared!

"What happened..."

Even Anna had a worried look as she looked around pensively!

Yet at this moment, Noahs figure was teleported away without any explanation from the room he was in, his senses spreading out as he found himself in a mystical area that was surrounded by colorful rolling clouds.

His senses could not spread too far away as they seemed contained within this area, the nearby cloud rumbling as they formed into the shape of a being Noah had seen in the skies above the Tower before.

[The number of points are null because you will not need them.]

The strong voice of the bearded being made of clouds floated into his mind as he listened with great focus!

[Given your Exceptional talent and aptitude, you more than meet the conditions to be the True Successor of Aoin, the Great Sage that founded the Expansive Dragon Cl.u.s.ter!]

RUMBLE!

The words came down shockingly as Noahs eyes shone, this being that was the consciousness of the Tower- a mere sliver of a soul that the Great Sage Aoin left behind...this being wanted to make him the True Successor of a Great Sages Legacy!

[The only condition is for you to bear the weight of protecting the Dragonoid and human race of the Expansive Dragon Cl.u.s.ter. Can you bear this responsibility and become my Successor?]

Not even a second had to pass as Noah would have promised this consciousness whatever it wanted to hear, his mind already imagining what he should choose from all the Peak Tier GALAXY pets.

"I will bear the responsibility and be the True Successor!"

[So it shall be.]

RUMBLE!

Waves of essence raged out as the multicolored clouds raged towards Noah, most of them going towards his forehead as a unique sigil was burned into his mind.

This sigil was truly unique as it held an abundant amount of information, many of them being skills and abilities!

[With me coming to be at this moment, it means my main body and soul have perished in the mission to isolate our Dark Universe from the Primordial Cosmos. But coming across someone like you...]

The cloudy form of the residual consciousness of the Great Sage continued to ring out as Noah let the Legacy freely flow into him, his attention on this former big boss that was now monologing.

[Coming across a being with such ridiculous talent means I can grow a powerful Great Sage to defend my home and fight any future battles with those thieves! For this...I am truly happy!"

The light of melancholy could be seen on the Great Sages face as his eyes honed in on Noah.

[The abilities I used to traverse and assimilate my Daos, you shall have. The treasures of this Tower, you shall have. Whatever you wish to achieve...you can have as long as you put in the effort!]

RUMBLE!

At this moment, the runic sigil etched itself onto Noahs mind as he sorted a great deal of information, turning his starry eyes to the illusory figure of the Great Sage as he spoke out with shining eyes.

"Strength is everything, and I wish to increase mine! Can I see the 10 Peak Tier GALAXY PRISMATIC pets? I would like to start with them!"

He made his wishes blatantly as the figure of the Great Sage had a light smile, waving his hand as the cloudy area around them parted to reveal one of the most beautiful scenes Noah had ever laid his eyes on in his entire life.

More beautiful than any exotic foods or gorgeous men and women!

More beautiful than even a father watching their first child be born!

As the clouds parted, what was revealed was a line up of PRISMATIC pets at the forefront, each humongous in stature as their bodies let off horrendous auras.

Behind them were rows and rows of other Animus Summons of all species and colors, Noahs eyes lighting up with beams of golden light as he laid his eyes on all of this!

The sight of a magisterial Galaxy Devouring Wolf, of a dreadful Tiamat, of a glorious Monkey King, of a resplendent Blue Slime...

It was truly too beautiful!
