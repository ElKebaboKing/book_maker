He nodded appreciatively and proceeded to add the leaf to his teacup with the casual air of someone adding sugar.

Be careful, Outsider. This Schrodinger is not simple.

"Existence has many things to ponder and discuss, but the most important one is how... nobody seems to know what is really coming." He paused, swirling his tea in a cup that showed different contents from every angle.

Did he know?

"I know many things. And I always prepare for what is potentially coming. As for the Living and the Dead, they are simply two sides of the same coin. I will move how I need to in order to ensure my safety and the safety of those under me."

Noah took another sip of tea, the liquid helping him see patterns in Schrkdingers words.
