Chapter 4099: The Purest Hunger! III

Ozymandias stood complete, his form radiating the terrible certainty of something that would never stop hungering. Yet through their bond, Noah could feel something else...control.

His will still commanded this Inevitability, though now it was less like controlling a limb and more like directing a force of nature.

How does it feel, Khor asked with genuine curiosity, to be both predator and prey? To hunger for everything while being everything others hunger for?

Noah opened his eyes, meeting both Khors gaze and Ozymandiass simultaneously.

Like understanding why Inevitabilities terrify many Existences, he replied.

Khor laughed with delight that made the Shores light flicker.

Now you begin to understand, Outsider. Hunger isnt evil. Its just honest about what everything else pretends not to be...desperate to continue, willing to consume others to persist, inevitable in its need.

She looked at Ozymandias with something approaching pride.

Take care of him. Inevitabilities are like children...they need guidance, boundaries, and occasionally someone to tell them no when they try to eat something important.

...!

The humanoid tentacle form of Ozymandias opened its eyes fully, and reality took a step back.

Seas of stellar obsidian light surged around him like tides that had decided gravity was optional. His presence made the air itself reconsider whether it wanted to exist so close to something that might devour it.

Prompts cascaded before Noahs vision with urgency that suggested even the system was struggling to process what had occurred!

|All Everythings Stripped Simultaneously from Ozymandias|

|Processing ramifications...|

|Everything of Limitations: REMOVED|

|Effect: No maximum capacity for consumption|

|Effect: No physical boundaries defining form|

|Effect: No temporal restrictions on existence|

|Warning: Without limits, growth has the potential to become cancerous|

|Warning: May consume self if external food depleted|

Noah walked closer to his transformed other self, gazing upward at the terrible beauty of what Ozymandias had become.

He read the prompts with fascination and gravity!

Khor stood nearby, her tiny form radiating satisfaction as she sighed and shook her head with the particular exhaustion of an artist who had just completed a masterpiece.

More prompts materialized, each one detailing a loss that was also transformation.

|Everything of Joy: REMOVED|

|Effect: Cannot experience a true sense of satisfaction from consumption|

|Effect: No pleasure delays the hunger|

|Warning: Endless consumption without reward|

|Note: Efficiency increased by 400%|

|Everything of Rage: REMOVED|

|Effect: Cannot be provoked to rash action|

|Effect: Consumption becomes mechanical, inevitable|

|Warning: No anger means no passion for protection|

|Note: Emotional volatility eliminated|

|Everything of Life: REMOVED|

|Effect: No biological needs or functions|

|Effect: Cannot be killed through conventional means|

|Warning: No growth through living experiences|

|Warning: Existence becomes static hunger|

|Everything of Death: REMOVED|

|Effect: Cannot cease through normal ending|

|Effect: Persistence beyond reasonable termination|

|Warning: No rest, no peace, no conclusion|

|Warning: May continue consuming after existence ends|

The implications stacked upon each other, each loss revealing itself as both liberation and curse.

Ozymandias had become pure in the way a blade was pure...excellent at one thing, not excellent for others!

|Final Assessment Complete|

|With all Everythings removed except Hunger|

|Through monumental effort witnessed and blessed|

|The Weavings of Ozymandias have achieved unprecedented purity|

|Current state: Unbearably pure existence|

|Option available: Convert all Complexity to Purity|

|This would create the purest Inevitability possible|

|Proceed? This action cannot be undone|

A question came.

And...

Noah didnt hesitate.

Proceed.

HUUM!

The stellar obsidian sea around Ozymandias erupted with radiance that transcended light and dark. It became pure statement, pure existence, pure hunger given form.

|Conversion initiated...|

|All Complexity transformed to Purity|

|For Everythings given: +100 Billion Purity|

|For effort of the full transformation: +200 Billion Purity|

|For advancing to new Way of Existence under the guidance of the First Hunger: +200 Billion Purity|

|Total gained: 500,000,000,000 Purity|

|Complexity gained: 0|

|Ozymandias has become: The Purest Hunger|

BOOM!

The final form revealed itself as the light settled.

Ozymandias stood as architecture more than anatomy...a humanoid form crafted entirely from tentacles that had learned to pretend they were flesh.

Each tentacle moved with its own hunger while contributing to the whole, creating a body that was simultaneously singular and infinite.

Behind him stretched a single massive wing of obsidian-gold, not for flight but for statement...asymmetry that declared he needed no balance because hunger only moved forward.

From his head rose a crown of tentacles that formed a halo, each one reaching toward different concepts of consumption, creating a corona of need that cast shadows in dimensions that shouldnt exist.

Noah felt through their connection the profound absence of everything except want.

No joy to color experience. No fear to provide caution. No hope to sustain through difficulty. No despair to acknowledge failure. Just hunger, pure and endless and honest.

There was something terrible and beautiful about such simplicity. No confusion, no doubt, no complex emotions to navigate. Just the eternal question of what to consume next and the certainty that something would be consumed.

The Ways of Existence of many beings are unique but messy, Khor said, her voice carrying the weight of having seen existence from its beginning.

They want contradictory things. They hunger but feel guilty. They consume but apologize. They need but pretend they dont.

She gestured at Ozymandias with something approaching maternal pride.

Inevitabilities are some of the purest Ways of Existence out there. We were pure. We were first. We knew what we were and never pretended otherwise. Her expression grew more complex. Everything that came after simply tainted this purity. Added complexity that made existence richer but less honest.

As she spoke, she began twirling the Everythings she had stripped from Ozymandias.

They manifested as small orbs of condensed concept...Joy glowing warm yellow, Rage burning red, Life pulsing green, Death shimmering grey. They danced around her tiny fingers like candies made from fundamental forces.

They thought they were improving on us by adding all these complications. Emotions to make consumption meaningful. Limits to make growth sustainable. Life and death to give existence rhythm. She popped Joy into her mouth, chewing thoughtfully. But all they did was make existence harder. More confusing. Less pure.

She consumed Rage next, her expression suggesting it was spicy.

Your Ozymandias is now what we once were...honest about existences fundamental truth. Everything consumes everything else. We just admitted it.

Life went down with a satisfied hum. Death followed with a expression of finality.

Hell be magnificent and terrible. Efficient and empty. Perfect and broken. She looked at Noah with eyes that contained eons of experience. The question is: can you handle having part of yourself be that pure? Most beings go mad when they realize what existence looks like without all the pretty lies we tell ourselves.

Noah felt through his connection to Ozymandias and found... nothing but hunger.

Not happiness...that required joy. Not satisfaction, that needed fulfillment. Just the hungering peace of absolute clarity, of knowing exactly what you were and accepting it completely.

Hes... perfect, Noah said quietly.

Khor smiled, swallowing the Everything of Limitations last.

Of course he is. Hunger always is, until it runs out of food.
