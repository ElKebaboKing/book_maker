Chapter 4823: Big Stepper! II

In Observable Existence, there is a chasm called the Divide of Becoming.

It separates what beings are from what they could be. Many stand at its edge across their entire lives, staring into the vast emptiness between their current selves and their potential. They measure the distance. They calculate the odds. They prepare endlessly for a jump they never intend to make.

And they die at the edge, having never learned what waited on the other side.

The Absolutes whisper among themselves that greatness is not earned through accumulation alone.

You can gather power for eons. You can build foundations until they stretch across Observable Existence itself. You can prepare and plan and perfect every aspect of your Way until nothing seems capable of stopping your advance.

But the final step cannot be walked.

It must be leaped.

There is a moment in every beings journey where the ground simply ends. Where no amount of preparation can guarantee success. Where the only path forward requires releasing everything safe and familiar and trusting that what you have built will carry you across the void.

Those who hesitate at this moment, who decide they need just a little more time, just a little more power, just a little more certainty before they commit, find that the edge crumbles beneath their feet anyway.

The chasm does not wait for readiness!

Those who cannot find themselves capable of taking that single leap, who cling to safety when Existence demands courage, will always remain in the dredges of nothingness.

And it is because potential means nothing to those unwilling to risk everything for its realization.

A being who dies mid-leap has lived more truly than one who dies having never jumped at all!

A step!

Ulmoreth took a heavy and ridiculous step with all of his existence as the moment he did so, his foundation, his Immensity, his glory erupted!

Noah looked at the scene with heavy eyes.

What he saw was the emergence of an unfathomable Depth that blazed with authority he had not expected to witness so soon. The weight that Ulmoreth carried had been building across eons within THE Belly of Existence, accumulating foundation after foundation while being suppressed from ever reaching this final threshold.

And now, with the Absolute Everything serving as the catalyst, all of that accumulated potential was exploding outward at once.

Noah estimated that what he was witnessing equaled perhaps five to ten percent of the Depth he had currently forged after swallowing the recoverable authority of THE Weavers. It seemed small in comparison to his own foundations, but this was an Absolute being born who was not one of THE Four. This was a being crossing the threshold that separated those who shaped reality from those who merely existed within it.

Five to ten percent of his current foundational weight, concentrated into a single being who would now stand among the most powerful!

That was no small thing.
