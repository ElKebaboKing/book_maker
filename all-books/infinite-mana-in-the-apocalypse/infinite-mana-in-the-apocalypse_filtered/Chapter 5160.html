Chapter 5158: Golden Wrath! I

From above, figures began to appear across the already shattered gold of the sky that THE Creature had overwritten moments earlier.

Noah observed them emerge with the same clinical attention he had been paying to every detail of the battle, watching as six Superbius Gilded Ones manifested in the air above THE Gilded White Mountain at varying altitudes and distances!

Each of them carried the same refined gold luminescence of Paleozoic-tier Superbius engineering, each of them was robed in the layered ceremonial colors of their respective houses, and each of them radiated basal Existential Radiation at the steady passive intensity that had pressed Noah down to the cracked balcony when the Gerousia had first arrived.

And then, after them, a seventh figure descended!

She arrived with even less announcement than the six who had come before her, simply resolving into visibility at the center of their arrangement as if she had always been there and had merely been waiting to be perceived, and Noah registered her presence with an immediate sharpening of his attention because the radiation pouring off her was of a different register than the six Gilded Ones flanking her.

Her Existential Radiation did not announce itself with the brash golden luminescence of the Superbius. Her radiation was quieter, denser, older, a pressure that settled rather than pressed, and the settling of it felt more absolute than the pressing of Aurelius Maximus had been.

She was a woman of terrifying beauty.

Her form was tall and slender with the long refined lines of a being engineered for presence across very large chambers of influence, her skin the pale pearlescent gold of a Gilded Lifeform, her hair a flowing river of molten silver-gold that fell past her waist in patterns of arrested motion that did not quite obey the ordinary rules of falling.

Her eyes were a deep amber-gold that held no swords in their pupils the way Aurelius Maximuss had, but her eyes instead held something that looked like a slowly rotating mandala of interlocking geometric shapes, each shape moving at its own independent rate against the others.

Another brilliant light bloomed near her!

Beside her, floating close at her right shoulder, was a massive muscular Gilded One around whose body crimson red light surged in steady aggressive currents that never quite settled into stillness.

His frame was built along the proportions of an ancient warrior rebuilt at Paleozoic scale, his chest broad and armored in red-gold plate that seemed to have been forged from his own refined Wrath rather than worked from external materials. His eyes were pure unbroken red, the red of arterial blood held at the moment before it spilled!
