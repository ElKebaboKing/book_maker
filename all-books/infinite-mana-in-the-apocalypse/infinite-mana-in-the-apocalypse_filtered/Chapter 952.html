RUMBLE!

Scenes of their terrifying battles were being displayed for all to see, with Noah finally being able to match the memories he had obtained from the enemies he had defeated with what he was currently seeing! When his eyes focused on any of the innumerable red stars on the Slaughter Star Monolith, the scenes of the battle occurring within became even more clear as it was like he was watching the battle right in front of him.

Above this were stars expanded to be 10 times larger, with many of them being empty but a few actually held genuine Paragons battling in a shocking manner.

Paragons!

This was the Universal Construct filled with mysteries, the Slaughter Star Monolith where slaughter prevailed within as many sat back to watch the ongoing battles and spread the fame of the powerful beings within!
