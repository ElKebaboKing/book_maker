The Challenger Arena felt a sense of suffocation unlike anything they had come across before.

It was the feeling a tiny little rabbit felt when a lion locked onto it and hunted it down.

A feeling of prey being stared at by a predator!

The gazes of Extremity Blue and other Avernus Extremities changed at such words as in the center of their encirclement, Noah remained utterly calm.

His eyes were gazing above.

There, cracks split across space and time as from within, the figure of a middle-aged man carrying the grandeur of an unparalleled Emperor appeared!

His body was adorned in an obsidian full body Armament of Extremity as on his head, an obsidian crown lazily hung down.

His hair was a simple dark color that faded on the sides while his fair face carried an enticing charm, his eyes seeming like endless black abyss singularities that emanated infinite power!

This illusion was caused by the status and grandeur of this being as an Existence that had reached the Fourth Gradation of Extremity.

Tier 4 Hyperversal Extremity!

His eyes carried a chilling and regal light as when he appeared, the masses of Apex Havenly Animalia Extremity Lineages and Extremity Havenly Animalia Lifeforms all became filled with respect as they bowed their heads silently.

This includes Laeta and Ruby who gazed at this scene from a distance!

This was the Havenly Master of this Saurischian Archipelago!

His true form was something they had only witnessed once during his coronation- a Titanosaurus that stood at the very peak of the Saurischian Lineage within Animalia.

The Abyssal gaze of this being was piercing as it moved from Extremity Ariana, Gayacus, Blue, and all the other Avernus Extremities as his mouth opened.

[The doors of the Supernal Havens of Extremity opened for others to seek Extremity, and for beings like you to ascend the Gradations of Extremity at a faster pace. Your purpose should have only been this as even at this moment, enemies are knocking at our doors. And yet supposedly esteemed beings like yourselves are here being roused and excited to burn down the records of an existence that has yet to even achieve Extremity?]

...!

The Culmination Step of Extremity Sanctification of the Last Haven!

It was meant for Apex Aeonic Lifeforms and Extremities alike all for the sake of exceeding or going past Extremity!

His words were admonishing the Avernus Extremities for using such an opportunity to focus on hunting an Apex Aeonic Lifeform.

His words were telling them to quickly take a step back, and yet Ariana and the others were put in a difficult spot as they couldn't do this!

They knew the threat of this being.

They knew the orders from the Avernus Extremity Overlord!

On the other hand, Extremity Blue was eerily silent as her vibrant and talkative demeanor was nowhere to be seen, her figure continuing to gaze at Noah silently.

It was as if she could feel something was very, very wrong- and yet she couldn't put her finger on it.

Since she didn't speak, Extremity Ariana's eyes vibrated as she spoke slowly towards the Havenly Master.

[Esteemed Extremity...this being before you has a death feud with our Clan as it is either us or him on the road of Extremity. Our history extends far from the Nether Havens of Extremity as we simply wish to close things off and put this feud to rest.]

...!

Her words were very careful as she felt her skin prickling and tense at every moment. The sheer pressure the Tier 4 Hyperversal Extremity above them displayed was just that obscene!

And his words were even more domineering.

[I don't give a damn if your history extends to the farthest reaches of Extremity. Drop it, or I will end the feud myself by eradicating all of you here...because it will definitely not be a Soldier of Vitalis that will perish from whatever inconsequential feud you all have.]

WAA!

A Soldier of Vitalis.

As he spoke, the Havenly Master's gaze came to lock onto Noah with scrutiny while the gazes of the Avernus Extremities changed!

They gazed towards Noah with complaints in their hearts as what had this being done in the last few hours that granted him the stout protection of a Tier 4 Hyperversal Extremity?

But they truly couldn't step back as their silence caused the suffocating pressure to only increase.

But a moment later, a smile bloomed on Extremity Ariana's face as she gazed up.

Her gaze was followed by a descending voice that was just as astounding as the Tier 4 Hyperversal Extremity above them, this one feminine and filled with endless mystery and charm as it was someone who put ease in Ariana's heart!

[I will offer you the Millennium Nexus Records of a Zenith Tier 4 Hyperversal Extremity if you simply look the other way on this matter.]

...!

The voice was astounding as it didn't make a threat or begin by fighting, it instead began with an offer that caused the eyes of all Tier 3 Hyperversal Extremities to flash with a shocked light!

It was a set of words that even caused the Havenly Master to raise his brows as he looked up.

Beings at their stage of Extremity had very few resources to aid their path forward.

There was a major reliance on Vitalis Hyperversal Authority among a few other things- one of these being the Records of Existences grander than them who had arrived at the place they wanted to go to!

It was basically akin to looking at a path another being had successfully walked on and making inferences on how to get there themselves.
