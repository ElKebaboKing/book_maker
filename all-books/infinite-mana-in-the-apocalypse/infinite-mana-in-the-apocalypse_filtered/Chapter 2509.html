Chapter 2507 Alexander King! III

Aurelia's eyes twinkled as they shone with an exuberant light of gold, excitement visible within them!

Such a genuine expression of excitement was not something commonly seen on an Apex Aeonic Lifeform who had gone through years of life as it gave her an otherworldly sense of unattainable beauty and wonder.

"An Extremity Foundation based on Loot? That's all you had to say.

Mmm...what about my Boundaries and Dynamis of Extremity?

Layers above 9th cannot exist without Meridians as..."

Even with her giddiness when it came to Loot, her rationality was still there as Noah waved his hands while they began to shine with the lights of Realm Reconstruction and Aspect Configuration.

"Your Concepts and authority as an Apex Aeonic Lifeform will be set aside and utilized during the end of your Reconstruction. Nothing will be lost."

His abilities were ready to unfold.

Aurelia gazed at his hands pulsing with the authority of two different Absolute Dynamis of Extremity as she breathed out as if she prepared herself, shaking her head while voicing out.

"Alright, let's do it. My forge better be at least the same level as yours if not more pristine after all this!"

Aurelia of the Ancestral Lernaeans became the first Apex Aeonic Lifeform to choose which path she followed.

And Noah didn't delay as his authority moved like unstoppable tendrils of light of cherry and emerald lights that caused her golden hair to move wildly.

They sank into her body as this Apex Aeonic Lifeform didn't put up any defenses, allowing the essences of two Dynamis of Extremity to flow into her Aspects of Existence- wrapping around dazzling Extremity Meridians that supported her existences like pillars of supporting light.

They intricately wrapped around her very being as she granted Noah a profound level of access and control to the extent that he could shatter her very existence at this moment!

'How curious the fates of living beings are...'

Noah had such a passing thought as he thought of how just days ago, he was just grasping the 5th and 6th Boundary Layers while stepping into the Aeonic Havens of Extremity.

And now, here he was. He had eradicated two Apex Aeonic

Lifeforms not too long ago, and now he held the very Aspects of Existence of another such lifeform in his hands as...

BZZT...CRACK!

The undeniable authority of an Absolute Dynamis of Extremity caused the Extremity Meridians of an Apex Aeonic Lifeform to crack along with the Origin of this being.

It wasn't so much a matter of Damage or Defense Values as it was the authority of [Realm Reconstruction] being carried out on a more powerful willing party!

"Ah..." Aurelia felt her life's work being broken at this moment as it was something any being would despair at, yet there was never even a thought that this was a mistake in her eyes as she watched the authority of the Broken Extremity Meridian that had her Boundaries coursing through them...she saw that even though they were broken down, everything about them was preserved and put aside!

The abundant authority of her lifeforce. Her Apex Aeonic Soul Values that had long since crossed the threshold of 200s. Her Forge in the very depths of her Soul.

Everything that made her was put aside to be utilized for later as her Realm was degraded to the lowest possible point!

Her vision slowly began to fade.

She didn't know how much time had passed, but after a certain point, she could only hear the voice of a single being.

"Fortune shall be at the very basis of your makeup, and your Realm shall be composed of Fortune, Loot, Providence, Genesis, Conquest, Forgemaster, Destiny, Kismet...and Quintessence."

His voice determined the new Planes of her Realm.

Planes that would go on to determine her Extremity Foundation!

[The Master of the Infinite Hyperversal Haven is Reconstructing your Realm and Aspects of Being ever closer to Extremity.] [You have been set on the path of the Quintessential Loot Extremity Foundation.]

...!

The words before her caused her eyes to widen as at this time, she felt the portion of her past self that was put aside begin to return to her.

They felt...foreign and not as pure as her current self.

She almost wanted to tell the being who was doing all this not to return the things from her past self when her will saw that everything returning back was being refined and reconstructed as well, her Realm beginning to rapidly reforge itself as her past accomplishments as an Supreme Aeonic Being were used as one of the many ingredients!

She then...felt the Forge that was locked away in her soul once more.

It seemed like a massive mountain of Loot as at its very core, her greatest treasure pulsed and radiated light at this moment.

Her Riviere of Fortune.

Her utmost treasure that she didn't even pull out on the deadly battle of an enemy more powerful than her and Orion who had chased them towards the Noblesse battlefield as she would only utilize it when she felt like there were no other options!

She felt the relic she had anchored into her Workshop since before she even became the True Emperor of Luck radiate wondrous light as...

[Your greatest creation resonates with the forming Quintessential Loot Extremity Foundation.]

[Your Workshop has begun basking in the light of Quintessential Spoils as it is transforming into a tool worthy of the Quintessential Loot Extremity Foundation!]
