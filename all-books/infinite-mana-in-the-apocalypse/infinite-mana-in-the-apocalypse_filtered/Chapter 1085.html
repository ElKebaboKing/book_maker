Chapter 1083 - Merely The Universal Realm! III

"OOOOOH!"

A majestic scene of a Chthonian horror bellowing out could be seen, the planetary sized worm like body having its pierced abyssal scales vibrate as its millions of teeth and jaws that made its head shone with multicolored luster.

From this figure, a manifestation of a Universe bloomed as this Hegemony had to actually resort to this if he was to obtain even a hint of survival against 5 other Universal Experts!

BZZZT!

The glorious 100 Billion galaxies of his Universe tried to stretch out wildly to withstand against the cerulean blue Galaxies that numbered 500 Billion from the 5 Blue Slimes.

Over a billion of them were actually vibrant Dao Galaxies as they shone with a rainbow colored luster, these ones being the only thing that even allowed his Universal Authority to be able to display itself briefly under the pressure of 5 other manifested Universes!

But...this was all it did.

The universe of a Hegemony that had the moniker of Great Old was actually only able to display itself for a brief few seconds before the 5 slimes that had the spear like extensions of their bodies still stabbing into this being harrumphed.

RUMBLE!

The 500 Billion Galaxies of the manifested Universes of the Blue Slimes shone with a terrifying cerulean glint as in the next second, something terrifying occurred.

With the Great Old Kubo trying to manifest his Universe outward, its many galaxies were flickering in and out as in the next second, it was dominated by the 5 already manifested one and this wondrous Universe...faded!

WAA!

The Hegemonies outside of the boundary of the Universe watching this scene were breathless as they now saw the unhindered pressure of the 5 Blue Slimes fall upon the single Chthonian Hegemony, even more spear like extensions releasing from their bodies and skewering this being left and right.

When few seconds passed, the scene became clear as the enormous worm like body of the Hegemony was pierced on all sides, a dazzling array of light displaying itself thereafter as all those watching were able to see...an innumerable number of blue spears surrounding the deeply hidden and protected Universe within the body of the Great Old Kubo!

The spear like extensions of the viscous slime had actually entrenched themselves into the Universe of this being, into his very Origin as in the next second...

"Universal Devouring." "Universal Devouring." "Universal Devouring....

5 synonymous voices rang out from the splendid Blue Slimes.

After the advancement to fully become a Universal Emperor Slime, these existences could even swallow genuine Universes s when enough of such a thing happened- they might be capable of becoming Apocryphal Antiquites and forming their Universal Filaments in this manner.

Even though it was not the natural process of Antiquity and was even given a word that meant false, it was still Antiquity! So...what would happen when a Universal Emperor Slime didnt devour a genuine Universe, but the Universe that an existence had built within their Origins?

The answer....

"AHHHHHHH!"

Was the resonating and loud cry of an aged being that could visibly feel its Universe being devoured.

The worm like figure of the Great Old Kubo trembled with such intense pain and fear at this moment as his screams reached the ends of the Chthonian Universe!

Meanwhile, Noah provided all the mana required as the bodies of the 5 Blue Slime caused the few light years around them to turn a beautiful cerulean blue, the screams of a Hegemony being drowned out as their spear like projection sucked dry a Universe.

The Galaxies within it went dark one by one as in a matter of seconds, the appalling sight of a dimming Universe that eventually cracked and began to fall apart played out!

It was bright red and black to begin with, but in a matter of seconds it turned gray and lifeless as it began to disappear as if it were dust.

"..."

It was a breathless scene as the screams thereafter stopped.

The millions of eyes and jaws that were vibrating fearfully dimed as they lost all luster.

The enormous planetary sized body of the worm like Chthonian then began to crack and splinter as without the support of a core, it faded away!

"..."

Like this, a Hegemony was shockingly put to rest.

If one kept track of the time, they would also be able to notice that it didnt take longer than a minute!

Ah!

To elevate the matter even further, a single humanoid Chthonian that shone with a starry galactic luster with his Cthulhu form just sat on top of a Blue Slime while watching the scene playing out with eyes shining with brilliant lights.

At such a reality, Noahs voice resounded out as he shook his head in a dissatisfied manner.

"What a disappointment!"

BOOM!

His words were like strikes of thunder to the watching Hegemonies that just observed an ancient existence ganged up on by 5 Blue Slimes and then decimated in less than a minute, their own Origins shaking at such a scene as a pervasive feeling entered their hearts when they looked at these beings!

"That...the Universal Emperor Slimes couldnt do that before, right?!"

The voice of the Hegemony of Smithing resounded out, seemingly at a higher pitch than his usual deep voice as a trace of stupor and fear was hidden within!

Of course, he was referring to the fact that the Blue Slimes were actually capable of devouring the entire Galaxy of a Hegemony and reap their life in a matter of seconds! It truly wasnt something they came across in the past.

But...there was nobody to answer his question as the rest of the Hegemonies had extremely somber expressions after witnessing the harsh reality. Many of them were asking themselves- if they were the ones to face the descent of 5 Universal Authorities...would they fare any better than Great Old Kubo?!

What caused them to become somber was the fact that each of them wfound themselves answering no to this question! None of them could see any other pathway except for death if they were in the same situation.

This was why they were so somber as at this moment, the glimpse of fear of the cerulean light of a Blue Slime had begun to bud within their hearts and Origins!

But things were not over.

There was the fact that the Blue Slime had just devoured the Universal Core of a genuine Hegemony just minutes after it entered this Realm, and the fact that a Universal Construct lay unprotected at this very moment!
