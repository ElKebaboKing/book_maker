Chapter 3172 Existential Colonization! I

A flag was pulled out by the glorious entity with the features of a humanoid monkey, this being thrusting the tip of this flag onto the dark space right away!

DU! DU! DU!

It pulsed three times before it became fully anchored in this space, the insignia of a wheel on this flag beginning to spin as a fantastical weaving of light began to unfurl in the distance.

At its base, a flat golden surface began to spread as from it, golden Pillars pointing above bloomed.

As this occurred, the blue device this being had taken out continued to vocalize with an autonomous voice.

|The Undocumented Omniverse boasts great integrity as it has been deemed to be a type of Conceptual Omniverse governed by specific rules. It is determined that Authorities scaling up to Tier 9 of Existence hold the make up of this Omniverse. Much of their weavings of Existence have not been spurred by any catalyst, nor have their Products of Existence been procured.|

...!

Everything was being analyzed rapidly as the smile on the humanoid monkey Entity only became larger, his voice echoing out in kind.

|We have found another treasure trove for the Commonwealth. A link will be established soon enough...what about the inhabitants?|

He asked with shining eyes as the Pillars that had risen from the golden flat surface burst into golden flames that illuminated everything, with these flames beginning to hum and resonate as above them- the outline of a massive portal began to form!

|An Infection of Neonatal Worms of Existence has been detected, but it is presumed to be in its early stages. The System of Existence of the Inhabitants may hold some effectiveness against them as it is estimated that there are over a dozen Tier 9 Existences within this Undocumented Omniverse. Contact will soon be made-|

|No.|

...!

Before the device could continue, the humanoid monkey entity denied its request as he voiced out.

|I will make contact with the natives myself. For the Infection...those critters are extremely perceptive against us, especially when they are still in the Neonatal Stage. Begin the procurement and deployment of the Synthetic Existence Legions.|

|Understood, Protectors Antonio.|

The process was fast.

Following the commands of Protector Antonio, a flourish of blue and golden brilliance bloomed above the flaming golden Pillars; the outline of a portal gradually became more distinct as from it, space began to fluctuate as a massive avoid vessel floated out!

Colored crimson and gold, this vessel bore the same insignia of an incandescent wheel, and it floated out of the portal with interminable glory as moments after, its center opened to show a flurry of Entities stepping out from it.

In the lead was another Entity adorned in gold robes and with the likeness of a humanoid monkey, this one boasting dazzling gold hair and a feminine charm as she smiled toward Protector Antonio.

|Protector Antonio, it seems our positions will soon shift after your finding.|

She gazed around her as she spoke, a blue device flowing out from her as well.

Behind her, dozens of Entities adorned in blue robes followed- their robes holding an ecriture of a hammer as they looked around with shining eyes while awaiting for orders!

Protector Antonio smiled while waving his hands dismissively.

|A finding is a finding, no matter how big or small- it is all for the benefit of the Commonwealth, Protector Nia. For now, establish a base of operations while I track down the strongest natives and ensure no hostility forms. I will bring them the good news and let them know they no longer need to fear the Worms of Existence. I shall start the spread of the glory of the Commonwealth and prepare them and this Omniverse of the glorious times ahead.|

...!

The glorious times ahead.

Glory for some, may not be glory for others.

But change was coming nonetheless.

Whether it was good or bad- well, this was another point entirely!

HUUM!

With a flash of golden light, Protector Antonio's body split into multiple parts that shot out in different directions.

He left behind another Protector and a massive vessel as at this moment, Protector Nia gave out a command that made the Entities behind her with the ecriture of hammers to begin moving.

|Get started. We have to prepare many things...|

A simple command.

And soon after, the glow of structures and massive constructs began to bloom in the surroundings as the weavings of a whole new civilization began to form!

Across the Omniverse.

A line of crimson gold light crossed unfathomable distances in a short period of time.

It was none other than Protector Antonio as at this moment, he was going towards the signature of the strongest life form that happened to be closer to his point of entry.

It was through veils of weavings of concepts, and yet his body tore through them like nothing as the blue device on his hand beeped constantly.

Within moments, he broke through a veil of a high Tier Authority of Existence as he came to observe a nest filled with titanic life forms!

Their existence buzzed as they turned up towards him in shock, some of them having their bodies glimmering with golden or Multicolored radiance.

Protector Antonio ignored all of them while flashing deeper into this area, heading straight for the signature of the strongest life form here as past massive mountains of authority, there was a throne made of the bones of Authority of Existence.

On top of this throne, the figure of a lone man sitting on it could be made out as he raised his head calmly at this moment, watching the arrival of Protector Antonio whose device beeped with a set of words only for him to hear!

|The observed life force holds a unique identifier that may not be entirely in line with this Omniverse, but much of his existence has already been influenced to be part of this Omniverse.|

It was a quick assessment made about the entity before them as after a moment, Protector Antonio nodded while voicing out.

"Hello, Native of this Omniverse. I am Protector Antonio bringing you the weavings of glory of the Commonwealth. How may we call you?"

The monkey-like visage bathed in the golden brilliance of Protector Antonio smiled as he ignored the tendrils of colorless authority that had erupted since his entry.

He didn't even bat an eye towards the rising sense of suffocation of multiple Autarchical Authority!

He only smiled while looking ahead as the man on the throne had his eyes flash sharply while he replied.

|Uterra...The Eonic Uterra.|
