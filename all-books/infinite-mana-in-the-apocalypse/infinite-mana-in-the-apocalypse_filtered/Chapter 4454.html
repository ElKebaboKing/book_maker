Chapter 4452: Duty V

When Protos observed this deterioration, he knew the time had come for next phase.

"The enemys defenses falter. His paradoxical barrier consumes itself. Be ready for a response. Maintain formation. Maintain duty!"

And the product that came out...

"If touched by even one of these pillars, your existence will be forced into quantum paradox. You will become simultaneously dead and alive in the same state until reality is forced to choose one outcome. His crimson eyes blazed with unwavering conviction.

"This attack is lethal. This attack is devastating. This attack seeks to eliminate us before we can fulfill our purpose. But I ask you now, Brothers and Sisters: does this attack exceed our duty? Does this assault surpass our purpose? Can paradoxical death prevent us from serving our Master?"

...!

In that instant as the attack was coming, as the millions of golden pillars were racing toward them with lethal intent, in the communication channel only for the Primus Inevitabilities, all those present roared their reply with unified conviction!

"NEGATIVE! Our duty is absolute! Our purpose is infinite! We do not falter! We do not fear! We devour all obstacles! Death itself cannot exceed our duty to the Master!"

Void Appetite.

The glorious scene that unfolded was breathtaking in its significance.

The light of Paradox was being devoured by the jaws of Infinite Hunger.

In single nanosecond that felt like eternity, the millions of golden pillars of Paradox... were devoured by a gloriously endless appetite!

In fact... the light around them seemed to be getting stronger?

This was why Protos would serve until his existence ceased.
