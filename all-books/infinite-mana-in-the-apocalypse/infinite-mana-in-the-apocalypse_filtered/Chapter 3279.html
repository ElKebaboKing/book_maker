Chapter 3277 An Endless Dream! I

Dream!

The very weavings of Ozymandias echoed such a command as within him, the forming rune of AMARANTOS burned with a fearsome obsidian gold light as from the Infinite Omniverse, the weavings of the Dream Aletheian Haven tunneled into it endlessly.

The Transcendent Megalos Ability, Amarantos Dream of the Megalos, was something unfathomable that allowed one to experience weavings of existence that were Dreams...and yet the gains of these dreams could be retrieved and assimilated into one's existence.

The time of the Dream varied as a single dream could last seconds, days, or years, and it could be equivalent to seconds, years, or even entire cycles.

It was all unknown.

But from the few who had attained this technique, it was known that the first Dream would only ever take an instant, no matter how much time passed within the Amarantos Dream of the Megalos.

At this moment, Ozymandias started his first Amarantos Dream of the Megalos as the forming rune of AMARANTOS exploded out to cover him with a storm of sandy gold brilliance.

And thus...an instant began to tick.

BOOM!

His eyes snapped open.

His form was that of an obsidian worm that rose in a dark earth, his segments wiggling around to observe his surroundings as he was just born.

And yet he knew who he was.

He was Ozymandias, and this was but a Dream!

He didn't have his power.

No state of Transcendence.

No state of Existence.

No Infinite Mana.

No connection to the Infinite Omniverse!

He rose up to look around him and found a few other worms like him waking, their bodies as Morphons looking feeble and sumptuous.

And so...

SUU!

His body zapped towards the nearby wriggling Morphons as he began to devour them one by one, his Outerversal Physiology and Regnum beginning to rise maddeningly.

In this Dream, he didn't feel any connection to the Infinite Omniverse or anything else as he could only depend on himself.

After devouring all the newly born Morphons near him, he bid his time and recalled the sensation of Alieia, of the Innate nature of Morphons spreading their weavings to connect to others.

He didn't go too far as he began to fish for connections in the dark earth he was on.

A month passed before he was able to find his first Delegatus- a weak flower tentacle that grew from the dark earth he was on.

With a single Delegatus bound, he was able to feel as if his vision had greatly expanded, his will spreading out towards nearby Flower Tentacles as his Delegatus began to increase rapidly!

His Megalos Regnum slowly strengthened. His Outerversal Physiology began to ebb forward steadily.

Slowly, years passed in this glorious dream.

The small body of a worm was no more, and a renowned Outerversal Morphon had begun to rise in the land where the dark earth was contained in- a small portion of the Megalos Pantheon of Gama!

Under him, his Delegatus extended not just within the Megalos Beyond, but even within the Megalos Ynnere.

His body was now a massive segmented worm with thousands of tentacles pulsing eerily around him, and the Morphons under him reached thousands!

He obtained a great deal of knowledge regarding Outerversal Morphons, and also about the Crucible that stood within the Megalos Pantheon of Gama- the Crucible of the Existence Defying Physiology of the Megalos.

He didn't have anything relating to Noah Osmont or Ozymandias, but he had the Will.

He had the memories.

He held the Dignity!

A hundred years passed.

His power grew to attain the Fifth Echelon of Outerversal Physiology as at such a time, he delved into the Crucible within the Pantheon of Gama.

Given the power he had cultivated all these years along with astounding techniques he designed from previous weavings of existence, he challenged the Crucible!

But it was truly too arduous without the power he commanded outside of this Dream, and he was only able to complete half of the crucible.

Thus, he was awarded with half of the Existence Defying Physiology of the Megalos.

But even this half, when it began to be cultivated...required tremendous amounts of Nectar of Existence among other resources!

But the results...allowed his power to scale tremendously.

He began to gain some true renown on the Megalos Pantheon of Gama, his power at unprecedented levels as hundreds of years passed!

He became known as the Worm of the Deep!

On the 2,500th year.

His power reached a point where all of a sudden...his weavings buzzed as they felt unfathomably sleepy.

Unfathomably so.

And endless slumber overcame him as he fell asleep and began to dream.

|GAH!|

Inside of an Omniverse.

A golden furred Simian arose from a verdant pool filled with terrifying waves of existence, 9 Apogee Autarchs and an Omniversal Entity looking at him at the center of the pool as they breathed a sigh of relief!

|You survived the ambush of the Morphons, Protector Alexei...you had us worried for a second!|

...!

Protector Alexei opened his eyes wide and stared at his hands.

The golden fur on them.

His reflection in the verdant waters that looked like a Simian.

It was not who he truly was. Because he knew...that he was in a Dream!

But this Dream had gone from the Megalos Beyond and stretched into the Megalos Ynnere.

At this moment, he closed his eyes as he felt at the Base of his Existence, there was a massive Wheel turning.

A system of existence based on the Wheel of Existence- one seeking to emulate its wonders and be close to it!

Inside of him burned an astonishing bloodline and System of Existence as he rose from the verdant pool while smiling devilishly.

"I was truly worried for myself too, my Brother!"

He spoke jovially towards the Omniversal Stage Entity as the endless dream continued!

As a Protector of the Commonwealth- one so close to the Omniversal Stage, he was privy to many secrets.

And he became so unfathomably in tune with the System of Existence of the Royal Simians.

The Existence of the followers of the System of Existence of Kuklos held a refinement and elevation of existence at every turn of the Wheel.

This...he attained seamlessly in this Dream!
