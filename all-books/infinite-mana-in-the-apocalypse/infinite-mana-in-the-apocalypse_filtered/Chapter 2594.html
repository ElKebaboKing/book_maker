Chapter 2592 A Cryptic Future!

{Vault of Curagulus}was the most dazzling one as when this feature was expanded, a myriad of fantastical borders of different colors showing a vast array of different items unfolded!

Some were labeled {Hyperversal Extremity Treasures}, {Extremity Relics}, {Extremity Circulation Modus Operandi}, {Aeonic Treasures of Extremity}, {Unidentified Relics}, {Vessels}, {Armaments of Extremity}...

There were many, but Noah focused on {Armaments of Extremity} as over a dozen square tabs shining with gold light made themselves apparent to him.

Each one shone with utmost radiance as these were Armaments of Extremity!

An image along with a brief description was all that was present for each one as Noah breezed through them.

{Record Cleaver- Amattul}:: An Armament of Extremity forged by the Extremity of Steel. 10 Billion Curagulus Coins to buy, or trade an item of equivalent value...

{Hyperversal Pursuer}:: An Armament of Extremity forged by the Extremity of Flames. 8 Billion Curagulus Coins to buy, or trade an item of equivalent value...

{Genesis Sword}:: An Armament of Extremity forged from the apocalypse and Genesis of one hundred Planes of Existence. 10 Billion Curagulus Coins to buy, or trade an item of equivalent value...

{Vacuous Moonshadow Scythe}:: An Armament of Extremity found in the stretches of Vacuous Hyperversal Domains. 11 Billion Curagulus Coins to buy, or trade an item of equivalent value...

{Leviathan Spear of Calamity}:: An Armament of Extremity forged from the bones of ten Hyperversal Extremity Leviathans from the Extremity Step of Andromeda. 11 Billion Curagulus Coins to buy, or trade an item of equivalent value...

The Relics shown were mind-numbingly glorious as each one had a high cost attached to it!

A cost in terms of Curagulus Currency- something one could attain from completing missions of the Curagulus of Extremity or by trading items into its Vaults.

When he arrived here, Noah's eyes shone with a profound brilliance as many possibilities rose in his mind.

The Relics he mass produced like the Quintessential Loot Seeker's Regalia Sets were considered Quasi-Extremity Relics. If he mass-produced more relics like this and traded them into the Vaults of Curagulus alongside Aeonic Treasures of Extremity and Spiritual Herbs from the Infinite Hyperversal Haven, would the sufficient currency required to obtain some of the more astounding things here be obtained in a smooth and quick manner?!

The idea had only just risen as there were still many things to consider, but Noah took his eyes away from the Vault of Curagulus and went towards the Missions tab before him.

He would first choose a mission to complete for the Curagulus of Extremity to gain an official tank in the coming days before the Culmination Step of Extremity Sanctification fully unfolded.

With the focus on {Missions}, waves of new information rose.

{Missions for Guardians are filtered on current location, connections, and difficulty based on your current power.}

{Golden Tier Mission}:: Surveillance- Observe and report the movements, conversations, and observable Histories of the Avernus Clan Scions and Elders for the next three days without being seen...

{Obsidian Tier Mission}:: Information- Rumor has it that the Cult of Endless Shadows has luckily attained an Armament of Extremity. Infiltrate and obtain information on this Armament by observing it personally...

{Golden Tier Mission}:: Achievement- obtain 10,000 Curagulus Coins through exchanging for treasures or relics...

A whole page of missions unfolded before Noah as they were filtered specifically for him, with the second one clearly being pointed towards him as the Curagulus wanted to obtain the details of the Shadow Sword that the Cult of Endless Shadows had attained!

These introductory missions were easy enough to complete as he would be considered an official Guardian thereafter, with numerous more boons becoming accessible to him after that as he would use the Curagulus to further his own gains.

As he became familiar with the Curagulus Mask and its features, the voice of the Adjudicator of Dissolution rang out in front of Noah.

{After you're acclimated, the inclusion of your First Commander of the Infinite Hyperversal Haven will be next. Thereafter, missions will be issued depending on how the Culmination Step begins to unfold.}

"Oh?"

Noah took off the Curagulus Mask as the Adjudicator's words as he asked lightly.

"And does the Curagulus know anything more about what is to come in the last Culmination Step of the Seven Havens?"

The Curagulus of Extremity was a massive organization spanning several Inheritances and even beings from the Seven Clans.

A Behemoth such as this should have compounded a dense amount of information as with its motto being the inclusion of prodigies across the Seven Havens, there should be numerous Hyperversal Extremities proficient in traversing the Aeonic Annals of Extremity among them!

{...}

Noah's words caused the surroundings to become utterly silent as it wasn't the Adjudicator of Dissolution who replied, but Extremity Marcellus who adorned his Curagulus Mask and spoke in an ancient tone.

{We know of a single sentence relating to the Culmination Step of Dissolution. And that is...As Above, So Below!}

...!

As Above, So Below!

A cryptic sentence about the Culmination Step that immediately caused numerous new options to rise in Noah's mind, with him giving a simple nod as the Adjudicator of Dissolution continued.

{Many seem to have forgotten or dismissed the mystery surrounding Extremity Sanctification. Why did it occur for every Haven? Why in sequence from oldest to youngest Haven? The objective of Extremity Sanctification is Extremity, but for what purpose? The existences within the Seven Havens are being ushered towards Extremity for a purpose, and the Extremity Sanctification of Dissolution is the last chance for this purpose. It will not be simple as even what we have discovered from the Aeonic Annals of Extremity...may not come close to understanding just what is about to happen!}

"..."

Words of utmost wisdom echoed out as Noah's eyes shone, countless thoughts passing through and going towards the Wealdian Council of Extremity to discuss as far down below from this location, the figure of Alexander King stepped out of the pocket of Vacuous Hyperversal Authority and looked up towards the endless skies tinged with death.

The power emanating from his body...was utterly brutal!
