Chapter 4726: Against A Legend! III

Each sound was a Civilization ending, a Way shattering, an existence that had accumulated power over eons being reduced to nothing more than harvest waiting to be claimed.

And then the arrow warped one final time to appear before the eyes of a scrambling GalThurak in the skies!

The demon spirits seven eyes went wide with horror and fear that he had not felt in eons, his chains rattling uselessly at his sides as he recognized that death had come for him in the form of a blue-gold arrow that had just murdered his colleagues without slowing down.

"PLEASE!"

His roar was desperate and pathetic, the sound of someone who had spent his existence subjugating others suddenly finding himself on the other end of absolute power, and the terror in his voice was so raw and so genuine that it might have moved a lesser being to mercy.

Noah was not a lesser being.

But in the next moment, as the arrow surged toward GalThurak with the inevitability of a death sentence already signed, a massive white-gray claw appeared in its path!

THE Living Spirit moved with speed that defied even the slowed perception of this moment, one of his countless limbs extending across the distance between himself and his subordinate as he refused to lose another...well, actually, the last follower to this impossible enemy!

The claw was massive and terrible, composed of compressed spirits that screamed silently as they were forced into service, and it caught the arrow with a grip that released gray bursts of Immensity in all directions.

"SPIRITS CANNOT BE SEVERED BY MORTAL WEAPONS!"

BOOM!

The Declaration crashed against Noahs Thesis with the weight of someone who had been ancient when THE Fallout was merely a possibility rather than a reality, and the arrow holding the terrifying Thesis buzzed in THE Living Spirits deformed and radiant claw as two authorities contested for dominance.

The arrow stopped.

THE Living Spirit looked toward Noah with eyes that blazed with fury beyond anything he had shown before, with rage that spoke of losses that could not be calculated and insults that could not be forgiven.

And then he looked past Noah, toward the surroundings of the citadel where his forces had been stationed.

All of them had slumped down like dolls whose strings had been cut.

The Cetus. The tentacled horror. The eight Intermediate Depth spirits. The thirty-six Surface Depth entities. All of them lay still and silent, their Ways shattered and their Civilizations collapsed, nothing remaining but corpses that had once been legends in their own right.

An instant later, those corpses began to move.

They flew out toward Noah as if drawn by invisible strings, rising from where they had fallen and streaming through the burning air of Muspelheim toward the entity who had killed them, and one by one, they sank into his back with sounds like stones dropping into still water.

As each one was drawn into him, Noahs immensity and weight erupted higher and higher!

The first corpse, a Surface Depth spirit, added its essence to his foundation.

The second and third and fourth followed, each one making him burn brighter.

THE Intermediate Depth spirits came next, their accumulated authority flooding into his existence with force that made the air around him shimmer and distort.

And finally, the Cetus, the former Fundamental Depth entity whose Way of Spirit Water had been shattered by a single arrow, sank into his back and elevated his foundation in ways that made everything before seem like appetizers before a feast!

Blue-gold flames surrounded Noah in a corona of power that seemed to grow with every corpse he absorbed, making him look glorious and tyrannical in ways that defied easy description. He had just collapsed dozens of Civilizations in a single blow. He had collapsed a Fundamental Depth entity in a single blow! Even if he was utilizing a Fragment of THE Axiom of Mayhem to reduce their Depths, even if he had built his attack on an already established Thesis, the achievement was staggering!

What a terrifying wonder he had become!

He carried out such a ruthless action with the casual ease of someone who had long since stopped feeling guilt for doing what needed to be done, and in the heavy silence that followed, prompts bloomed before him with comprehensive detail!
