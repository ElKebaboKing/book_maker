Chapter 444 - Water Origin Core!

The Water Law Space located on the Northern Continent of the Spiritual World was an area filled with blue, the plants fitting to become aquamarine vegetation and the land itself holding vast expanses of lakes and rivers, with numerous waterfalls created from the different elevations of multiple areas as the water flowed in a circular fashion throughout the continent.

Elemental particles of water danced freely in the surroundings as Noah went towards the very center of this Water Law Space where the Spiritual World System had already placed down the [Prismatic Aurora Pearl] that released spring water filled with water element essence.

The pearl had been placed on an area of depression that would contain the spring water it would release, Noah already seeing more than half of the half a mile area already filled with the sparkling Prismatic Aurora Spring Water as he dipped his body into it.

He would cultivate at the very center of this Law Space, inside the valuable Spring Water from the treasure he recently obtained!

He prepared himself as he settled deep within the area, feeling the overpowering essence of water all around him as he smiled...and then activated {Disciple of Water}.

RUMBLE!

It was like a match had been lit as the instant the skill was initiated, all of the surrounding essence filled with the law of water rushed towards Noahs clone, making him feel a tinge of pain as it rushed too fast towards the Origin of his main body!

The energy flowed in freely as he soon felt the appearance of a small clear blue dot to the right side of his main Origin Core, the Fire Origin Core that lied to the right of his main core still sitting stably.

Essence filled with the law of water rushed in, and so did his own mana as it all converged towards the ever so enlarging blue dot. Noah knew that the formation and rise of the Water Origin Core would be much faster as he already had 11% in the Law of Water, but he didnt expect it to be so vigorous.

SHAA!

In the Prismatic Aurora Spring, there was a swirl of essence forming as it rushed towards Noahs clone like a black hole, passing through him and going towards his main body through the unique channel they shared, the clone simply acting akin to a limb as his main body kept [Unique Origin] ability active with even more care these days to make sure that none of the changes that were brought upon by his clone cultivating the laws would ever outwardly show.

OOOM!

In a matter of moments, the first stage of the Water Origin Core formed as a shimmering blue circular core formed near his origin, beginning to rotate even faster as his mana continued rushing in.

After achieving stage one, just half an hour later, it doubled in size as it reached Stage 2!

Similar to the Fire Origin Core, whatever energy the Water Origin Core drank, it used it to get bigger while also releasing a unique energy that went on to wrap around every single cell in Noahs body, the energy once again permeating though the clone before it rushed towards Noahs main body in the Celestial Realm.

He could feel the dual strengthening of both the unique energy that the Fire Origin Core released, as well as the new one from the blue shimmering Water Origin Core.

The fiery one made his body feel more explosive, like his speed and power just continued to break through all bounds!

The calm blue energy permeating within him now slightly calmed the fiery energy, even slightly bolstering it, as Noah felt an overabundance of defense and resiliency with every passing second that his two bodies were washed by this energy. The process continued for quite a while, Noah enjoying the effects of it fully!

The essence of water continued to pour into the core for thereafter, only settling down a few hours later as Noah observed it to be slowing down its rate of energy absorption similar to what the Fire Origin Core had done, but it was still faster as he was located in an area extremely dense with the law of water, the Prismatic Aurora Spring only enhancing the environment more.

His eyes wandered to the shining blue box only he could see as he observed the stat panel for the new addition.

[Bloodline(s): Vampyre Progenitor(Awakening)]

[Fate Line(s) : 174,274]

[Chaos Particle(s) : 146,164]

[Origin Core(s) : Fire-2, Water-2]

[Domain(s) : Water, Space, Fate, Fire, Chaos]

[Vitality: DOMAIN]

[Focus: - ]

[Strength: DOMAIN]

[Law(s) : Water-19%, Space-8%, Fate-1.7%, Fire-18%, Chaos-1.4%]

The number of Fate Lines and Chaos Particles continued their rapid growth as he broke through any established norms to amass even more of them. His eyes focused on the Water Origin Core that had reached the same stage 2 as the fire counterpart, its percentage even catching up as he did not completely see through the Stages and how they correlated with the percentages. Stage 2 didnt simply translate to progressing 20%, and it seemed he had to break past this stage to see exactly how the percentages would move thereafter.

To do this, he shifted his body as he teleported from the Prismatic Aurora Spring Water and went towards the area where both the Fire and Water Law Space met.

His gaze landed on these areas that portrayed two different worlds with just a movement of his eyes, where to his left was a blazing earth filled with fiery outbursts, and to his right was a calm blue shimmering earth filled with water.

It was an interesting contrast to observe as he went forward and positioned himself right in between where these two areas met, his body standing right in the middle as half of it was in the Fire Law Space, and another half was in the Water Law Space.

He sensed the two opposite elements as he closed his eyes, the slowly rotating Origin Cores of Fire and Water slowly continuing to circulate by taking in his mana and releasing vibrant essence that went on to strengthen him. His eyes never left these two origin cores as he activated both of the unique skills, {Disciple of Water} and {Disciple of Fire} simultaneously!
