Chapter 1019 - The Burning Light of Conquest! I

As the group with Chronos monitored and planned, another group was also observing Noah's movements carefully!

The group of Hegemonies that had all sworn an Oath under Oathkeeper were keeping watch over the happenings of the 9 affected Universes, and two Hegemonies were doing so for the Animus and the Necrotic Universe.

Oathkeeper looked at this as he raised his Wings of Primordial Destiny and spoke into it.

"Is this your work in the Necrotic Universe?"

The message crossed the vast distance as it reached a singular being...Noah himself!

He had stashed away the Wings of Primordial Destiny into his Expansive Space as it rang out and he heard it clearly, his brow rising up at the straightforward manner of this powerful being as he replied with but a single word.

"Yes."

RUMBLE!

He hadn't particularly tried to hide what he was doing, and he had no need to do so as with the current reality, no Hegemonies could touch him!

The ones in the 9 targeted Universes could not, and he used his clones for any time he traversed in the Primordial Cosmos. His main body was stashed away in the Dark Universe that was floating peacefully in the Sea of Ruination, and he even had a Save Point active in case of any mysterious or sudden deaths.

Simply put...he wasn't afraid!

But he also didn't want to border on the side of arrogance and shoot himself in the foot, so he took things as they came and evaluated them while changing plans along the way.

The plans of Conquest...they would in no way be affected by the fact that others knew it was him doing it.

It would actually be even better if his notoriety spread, where even the Universes he targeted next would know of him so that his might can be something as magisterial as beings like Oathkeeper, Chronos, and the Goliath of the Primordial Cosmos!

With a mention of their names...even Hegemonies turned somber!

So he replied simply towards Oathkeeper, and as for the reason he was going from the Animus Universe and then the Necrotic Universe in a campaign of Conquest?

He would just bullshit his way across as they didn't know he was doing it to collect Marks of Antiquity!

"Conquest..."

"Share this type of information with others earlier as we're all going for the same goal. Also, the natives of the 9 Universes...they are existences with homes and families as well, I hope you can bear the responsibility of your actions depending on how far you go with your Conquest!"

...!

Conquest leading to Marks of Antiquity.

Marks of Antiquity leading to a Universal Seed that would bloom into a Universe!

From a Universe and into a stage nobody had reached in the Primordial Cosmos...it was the Universal Filamanets that would pave the way towards Nirvanic Antiquity, towards the Cosmic Realm!

RUMBLE!

That was an uncountable number of trillions!

WAA!
