Chapter 4085: Losses and Gains! I

There are existences accustomed to winning...beings for whom victory is not achievement but expectation, for whom success is the baseline from which all other experiences are measured.

When such beings experience loss, the sensation transcends simple defeat.

It becomes existential crisis, a fundamental questioning of identity itself!

If you are defined by winning, what are you when you lose?

There were those existences, and then... there was Noah.

At this moment, he lay upon a vast white sea, his body carried by gentle waves that had once been cerulean but now shone with stellar radiance.

The light of Order had bombarded these waters so thoroughly, so completely, that they had forgotten their original color and adopted a new truth.

The waves moved with purpose that transcended physics, supporting him with the particular care of water that had been organized into knowing exactly how to hold someone.

He stared at a ceiling inside an isolated pocket dimension in the Abode of the Early Creature, and for perhaps the first time in his rapid ascension through impossibility, he had to admit something that sat strangely in his consciousness...

He had actually lost.

During his Dual Cultivation with THE Living Order, despite all his complexity, despite his multiple Seed Glyphs, despite being The Early Creature, Osmont..he had lost!

Not devastatingly, not humiliatingly, but definitively. Sigrids complexity and purity had exceeded his own, and he had learned firsthand what that distinction of [THE] truly meant when attached to a Living Existence.

Basically, he was manhandled by his girl!

Sigrids voice cut through his contemplation with the particular mix of affection and exasperation that had developed between them.

She stood nearby, tying her white hair into a ponytail with movements that were efficient yet graceful. The pure white robe she wore seemed to glow with its own light...or perhaps it was her light, Order itself radiating through simple fabric.

Noah rose from the stellar waters, droplets cascading down his bare torso like liquid starlight seeking return to the sea.

Each drop caught light that shouldnt exist, creating momentary prisms before rejoining the vast white expanse. His lower body was adorned with pure white pants that seemed cut from the same conceptual fabric as restraint itself.

Because if anything feels off...if you sense something foreign trying to assert itself, tell me now. I can always take matters into my own hands.

The implication was clear. He would rather stop her awakening, rather limit her power, than lose her to something wearing her face.

Sigrid moved toward him across the stellar white sea, her steps creating ripples that organized themselves into perfect concentric circles. She sat beside him, then shifted to lay her head on his lap, looking up at the ceiling with eyes that contained depths of contemplation.

Her arm reached upward, fingers grasping at something invisible...perhaps trying to capture certainty in a situation that offered none.

I was just a girl who lost her entire family, my whole lineage, based on the orders of those above me.

I didnt ask to become the resurgence of THE Living Order. I still dont understand how its possible. But what happened, happened. Now Im here.

She paused, her hand still reaching toward something beyond the ceiling.

I may not have my family anymore, but Ive found something else. Someone else. Her gaze shifted to meet his. I was terrified you would be like the Living Origins...someone who only wanted to use me for what I represented. But then you brought me here. Her expression softened with vulnerability that THE Living Order probably shouldnt show.

She placed her hand on her chest, closing her eyes as if listening to something within.
