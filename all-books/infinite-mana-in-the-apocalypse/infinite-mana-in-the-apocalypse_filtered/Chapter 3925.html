Through the center of this magnificent chaos ran a single golden pathway that led upward toward the ascending heights of Concordia, known as the Aureum Ascendant!

Along the sides of this golden road, peak Originus Venerant Fold Dwellers had established elaborate stalls beneath massive banners that proclaimed their wares in terms designed to capture the attention of the desperate and ambitious.

"Wonders from the Loom!" declared one such banner, while others advertised "Treasures of the Loom" or "Items from the Loom" in letters that pulsed with their own inner radiance.

It was, Noah realized with growing interest, an elaborate fiction.

Ridiculous lies!

None of these traders had actually reached the Loom itself.

Instead, they dealt in treasures recovered from the Doorways that led toward the Loom, artifacts torn from the grasp of Inevitabilities or discovered in the unique locations of thr Doorways.

The variety was staggering.

Scattered among these wonders, treasures that had been wrested from Inevitabilities themselves stood!

Most of these treasures could aid progression across the Originus Venerant stage, while others promised the potential to transcend that realm entirely for those who had already reached the theoretical maximum of 99,999,999 in both Complexity and Purity.

Within the pristine towers that rose above the marketplace, even more remarkable treasures could be found- even the rites that could grant the opportunity to undergo the fundamental transformation required to become a Living Existence.

It was this possibility and many more that drove many Fold Dwellers to brave the dangerous Doorways of the Loom, following the instructions of their sponsors while simultaneously seeking to accumulate enough contribution and funds to...trade for their own transcendence!

This was the culture of the Empyrean Bazaar, as this section of Concordia was called!

A place where desperation and ambition mingled, where the impossible became merely expensive and dreams could be purchased by those willing and rich enough to pay the ultimate price.

Deeper into the citys heart, areas reserved primarily for Living Existences housed golden tower-like buildings that contained even more fantastical treasures like artifacts that could propel ones complexity toward the exalted status of Prime Living Existence or even hint at the terrifying grandeur required to achieve Honored status.

Everything could be found here, for those with the means to acquire it.

Everything!
