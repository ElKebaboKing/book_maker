Inside THE Loom.

While apocalyptic horrors raged outside...

Ambessa uncomfortably pulled on the collar of the obsidian-gold suit she was forced to wear by the forces of THE Living Elemental as she moved from one section of THE Loom to another.

Around her were a few Pre-THE and False THE entities who were surrounded with vibrant golden lights as shockingly, their Sovereignties were being elevated. They naturally and forcefully had their Depths taken to THE Surface Depth in a process that should have taken eons but was being accomplished in mere moments.

It truly fucking paid to be in THE Loom as while it razed down Existence, everything devoured was being swallowed up by it and distributed to a specific few.

But did they really need these ridiculous suits?

Ambessa would rather be wearing her bloomers and something free, not this full body coverage bullshit that constricted her movements and made her feel like a decorated puppet on display. The obsidian-gold fabric clung to every curve and left no room for comfort, designed more for presentation than practicality.

"Can you fucking relax? It is Liberation Day, and we have been chosen."

"Youll be standing in front of THE Living Elemental and THE Living Emotive soon as they prepare us for the emergence to the outside Existence once THE Fallout is done. We have been chosen. Show that fucking graciousness. You see that Depth of Paradox building inside you? That is eons of work erupting today, and it is not your own. Come on."

A THE Surface Depth Elemental Early Creature who had already been given a high-sounding title that didnt matter to shit to Ambessa what it was. Something like "Vanguard of the Elemental Emergence" or some equally pompous nonsense.

About how another one of her Avatars had been decimated while she had gone to seek the forces of Noah Osmont.

And because of that name, because of Osmont, she was chosen among those to receive the bigger boons of THE Loom during THE Fallout. She was having her Depth built regardless of how tough it was to go from False THE to actual THE.

All because of the little connection she had with Osmont.

Frankly, couldnt they leave him alone? He was probably dead from THE Fallout anyways. The flames that were consuming Existence outside were not something that could be easily survived, and from what she last saw as her Avatar fully perished, Osmont had been outside when it all began.

Why did THE Living Elemental have such a grudge against him to be forming a task force of THEs that would be looking for Osmont after THE Fallout?

It seemed excessive. It seemed obsessive. It seemed like a waste of resources that could be better spent elsewhere.

But no one asked for her opinion, and she was smart enough not to offer it.

Right.

And THE Loom had already separated into multiple Shelters that would flow across Existence. When THE Fallout ended, they could observe and explore the changed inhabitable Existence while maintaining their rule from different bastions of protection.

But...

Ambessa raised her head and looked up, seeing waves of auroras descending down from the crystalline ceiling of this domain. They wrapped around her and elevated her power with warmth that felt almost nurturing despite its artificial nature. Other waves spread to different parts of THE Loom, reaching chosen individuals across the vast structure.
