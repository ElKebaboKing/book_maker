2566 A Flicker of Brilliance! II

[Hyperversal Plundering].

The expanding explosive waves of the Corpse Explosion.

She was the Adjudicator of Dissolution!

A single finger!

The three other Hyperversal Extremities continued to extend their authority towards the Apex Battlefield as they reached out to the remaining Avernus Clan Elders and Singularity Overseers!

This sudden progression of events was utterly shocking as it caused all beings to rise in shock and cry out, the scene of the Adjudicator of Dissolution being occupied by an immensely potent authority of an Unknown Hyperversal Extremity while 5 other Hyperversal Extremities appeared to interfere with the battle being imprinted in all minds.

Reality was never fair.

He didn't expect to have a fair fight by any account, and so he prepared!

His Vassals all had clones representing them here as they couldn't be traced even in death. And he himself was unkillable as he could leave at any time!

He just did all this because he wanted to expose these grand Clans and Inheritances for what they ultimately were.

Beings that moved with their own goals and ideals- ideals they would achieve no matter what rules lay before them.

He depended on his own power to get past even the interference of these beings as the two Hyperversal Extremities- one of them undoubtedly being Destruction, would at most arrive to find Ahpuch halfway through being Plundered!

And the vast majority of Avernus and Singularity Overseer Elders were nearly torn to pieces under the grandeur of his forces.

So let them interfere.

Because in all this, it seemed he would get to see a reality play out that revealed much more of the Seven Havens!
