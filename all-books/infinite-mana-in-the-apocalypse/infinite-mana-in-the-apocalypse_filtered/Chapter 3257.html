Chapter 3255 Tainted! III

Possibilities of the future unfolding in many possible directions already flashed before Noah's eyes as his job was a simple one.

He had a future in mind, and he simply had to fill in the gaps for it to come to fruition.

The first gap was delving into this massive Half Tainted Omniverse where an Outerversal Morphon had made his home.

Getting past droves of lifeforms it had bent to its will, and eventually getting past the Outerversal Morphon itself!

WU!

A low scream echoed out a light year away as they delved deeper into the Half Tainted Omniverse, Command Protector Valeria turning towards this direction as there, they could see a few black beasts moving on four limbs, their bodies covered by a shrouded of stellar darkness as they only had jaws at the front of their bodies.

They looked massive, and yet...

SAA!

One of them flashed to instantly a pear a few miles from them as a slimy red tongue lashed out from its jaws to taste the surrounding atmosphere.

The first thing to observe was its shocking speed.

A speed that very few Apogee Autarch could even reach as it was a terrifying sight to witness.

The hand of Command Protector Valeria rose as their figures came to a stop, the obsidian wheel spinning below them calmly as their figures remained masked.

The only thing one could sense or feel when gazing in their direction were more tendrils of Outerversal Authority.

SAA!

In a flash, the massive yet speedy beasts disappeared as they resumed their movements once more.

Softly, the voice of Command Protector Valeria rang out in their minds.

|We have come across Agents of Outerversal Morphon Savitar in other Tainted Omniverses where his most powerful bodies reside. We've come to call them another common moniker for a few Agents of Anti-Life- Existence Reapers. They are fast, and their bodies are capable of rapidly corroding existence if they ever get their hands on you. Your tasks once we get discovered will be to disrupt their weavings of existence without letting them get close.|

DUM!

The Wheel below them pulsed as if it was alive and breathing.

The body of Command Protector Valeria had begun to vibrate with concealed power that seemed to be contracting and ready to be released at any moment.

A heavy bloody ax swirling with trillions of crimson galaxies emanating an Omniversal light was grasped by Protector Actaeon.

Protector Orpheus and Ajax grasped a long multi colored spear and a bow respectively, their bodies swirling with intense concentrated weavings of existence as they were ready to go!

Only Noah and Protector Lucretia seemed to be on the nonchalant side as their group delved deeper and deeper into the Half Tainted Omniverse.

Miles away from them, more and more Existence Reapers appeared and disappeared, their massive bodies and wide jaws moving on four limbs pulsing with heavy Outerverversal Authority!

These were past lifeforms who used to thrive in this Omniverse, and they were now turned into other things entirely as a single one could be made from the existence of one powerful Entity or the horrific product of millions of other existences forcefully fused together.

But undoubtedly, each one carried the power of the Outerversal Morphon Savitar.

Each one was a Delegatus!

DUM!

Their surroundings entirely became tinged with obsidian radiance.

The spinning wheel they stood on moved silently as below it, an endless stretch of shattered black glass began to appear.

Tentacles of darkness grew out of them akin to an infestation as a few dozen Existence Reapers were perched on them.

And the deeper they went...

The clusters of Existence Reapers became denser.

Hundreds.

Thousands.

Hundreds of thousands!

DUM!

The breathing of Protector Lucretia could be felt as the multicolored battle robe on her hugged her body ever closer.

The three Royal Existential Omniversal Simian Protectors grasped their weapons ever tighter as they were on high alert!

At this moment, from where they came from, it looked like thousands of Existence Reapers covered everything.

And yet they fearlessly delved deeper as if they were in a nest!

Noah smiled at this scene.

Why were Protectors required to be powerful?

This was the reason why.

They delved into an Omniverse tainted with Outerversal Authority as they were surrounded by millions of Entities that could hurt even an Autarch with a mere touch!

And yet they delved in without fear.

|Be ready.|

Filled with power, Command Protector Valeria's voice echoed out once more.

Even bigger Existence Reapers with tendrils of fur floated around aimlessly as one who was the size of a world passed just miles away from them!

Its hairs vibrated as if feeling the surrounding Existence, but they continued to remain veiled and passed it to go deeper.

Until...

DUM!

Massive tendrils of darkness entered their vision.

Titanic Existence Reapers floated as they radiated sparks of obsidian radiance, their very existence warping nearby space.

Below them, stretches of shattered Domains of Existence lifelessly pulsed within thousands of other Existence Reapers releasing a cacophony of roars.

In the far distance, the pulse of what seemed to be like a breathing living organ could be felt.

The Dark Matter Core that was actively altering this Omniverse- or what remained of it!

Reaching this point was already an astounding enough result as at this moment, Command Protector Valeria came to a stop.

The wheel below them stopped moving forward.

In the next moment.

WUU!

In front of them, a massive furred Existence Reaper opened its jaws and roared out as a crimson tongue radiated waves in front of it!

Waves that instantly disrupted Existence and revealed the five figures floating above an obsidian Wheel.

|...|

A buzz of Outerversal Authority began to rise. From all around, millions of Existence Reapers turned their jaws towards their direction.

All the cacophony of noises died down as immense silence filled with blood curdling horror came in.

And at such a time...

|Do not die.|

Command Protector Valeria spoke out coldly as akin to an arrow knocked from a bow, she blasted off!
