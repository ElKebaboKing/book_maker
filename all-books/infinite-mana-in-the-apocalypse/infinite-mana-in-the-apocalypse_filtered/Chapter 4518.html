Chapter 4516: Brutality! III

Noah clenched his fist and continued feeling the immensity of his Way as he looked around and felt a wave of scalding heat wash over him.

The region they had arrived in was dramatically different from the poisonous swamp.

This domain was filled with countless layers of volcanic smoke that rose in towering columns stretching toward the sky. Churning volcanic mountains released extremely complex and pure volcanic materials in continuous eruptions.

Molten brilliance flowed like rivers between the mountains, glowing with crimson-orange-gold light that illuminated the perpetual twilight. The lava was condensed authority given liquid form, each drop containing enough power to obliterate Folds.

Geysers of superheated plasma erupted at irregular intervals, creating fountains of stellar fire that arced across the sky before raining down as meteoric destruction.

The entire region was surrounded by crimson mountains that pulsed with internal heat, their peaks glowing like forge fires. Floating meteorites spun in orbital patterns overhead, each one burning with such intense heat that space itself warped around them.

A single spinning meteorite colliding with an unprepared entity could collapse anyone with mere hundreds of Septillions in Complexity and Purity instantly.

No weak Living Existences or Early Creatures could survive here!

Noahs gaze did not even need to be directed before he found the target that Tor had brought him to.

His eyes flashed with a light of tyranny as he observed another glorious monstrosity.

The creature was a nightmare given flesh and fire.

Its body was serpentine and elongated, stretching for what looked like light-years in length. But it had no eyes, no visible sensory organs at all. Its head was smooth and rounded, featureless except for a massive circular mouth lined with rings of crystallized flame teeth.

The skin appeared pale and translucent, almost sickly, but this was deceptive. The flesh burned with crimson lava flowing just beneath the surface like blood in veins. Flames erupted from pores across its entire body in rhythmic pulses.

Its neck could extend to impossible lengths, and the creature moved with disturbing fluid grace despite its titanic size. The tail ended in a whip-like appendage that left trails of fire in the air.

With a mere flash of light, Noah saw its details.

The Way of Fire.

The floating meteorites began to glow brighter, responding to Pyrokhans rage.

And yet, while surrounded by the glorious weight of his Way manifested fully, Noah did not even feel a change in temperature.

His protective aura of blue-gold and crimson stellar light simply negated the heat as irrelevant.

He spoke with imposing authority as he approached the eyeless creature.

"You play with the Way of Fire," he said with cold certainty. "What do you think allows your sparks to flicker and bloom? When the first flame formed in existence, what do you think it was composed of?"

His foot swirled with endless concentrations of his Way, visible authority coiling around it like dragons made of fire and light.

He was truly learning how to manipulate his Way of existence, testing out different methods of exerting his influence.
