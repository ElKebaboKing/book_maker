Chapter 4517: Brutality! IV

Pyrokhans entire body coiled and then lunged with apocalyptic force. Its movements were cataclysmic, each motion forming flaming mountains and volcanic eruptions in its wake.

PAH!

But Pyrokhan was relentless.

The creatures body coiled around the battlefield, creating a ring of volcanic mountains that all erupted simultaneously toward the center where Noah floated.

Hundreds of Decillion-level volcanic projectiles converged on his position from every direction!

Noahs limbs blazed brighter as he spun in place, his arms and legs trailing arcs of blue-gold and crimson light. Each limb that struck an incoming volcano completely scorched away the fire authority that composed it.

His movements were precise and economical, wasting no energy.

But some attacks got through.

A stream of molten plasma struck his left shoulder, burning through his protective aura and charring flesh down to the bone. Another volcanic boulder crashed into his back, creating a crater in his torso.

Noah did not even make a sound.

His expression remained calm and analytical as the injuries rapidly healed, his Way flooding the damaged areas with regenerative authority.

His enemy was still much more powerful than him in raw numerical terms, but the quality of his Way was too grand for Pyrokhan to overcome.

The battle raged across the volcanic region, their clashes creating permanent scars in the landscape.

Noah continued gathering high concentrations of his Way of Mana and Hunger onto the extremities of his limbs. More and more authority compressed into smaller and smaller spaces.

Noah simply raised his arms, which now looked like endlessly radiant blue-gold suns, and pushed outward.

CRACK!

Pyrokhans body was forced away, sections of its flesh scorched and burned by contact with Noahs limbs.

The creature shrieked and created a volcanic apocalypse, summoning thousands of erupting mountains simultaneously!

Noahs legs, also glowing like miniature suns of concentrated authority, kicked out repeatedly. Each kick generated a blade of compressed Mana and Hunger that sliced through the volcanic formations and scorched away their fire authority.

His arms punched forward, creating shockwaves that erased entire mountain ranges.

The radiance from his limbs grew brighter and brighter until it actually began to scorch away the scorching flames of Pyrokhan itself!

"Your Way of Fire," he said without emotion, "is a subset of Mana. Fire cannot exist without Mana providing its foundation. Fire cannot burn without Mana fueling its reaction. Fire cannot spread without Mana carrying its authority."

His limbs blazed even brighter.

"And Hunger consumes all things, including the very concept of heat and combustion that you have tried to claim. Your Sovereignty is incomplete. Your claim is weak. And ultimately, your entire existence can only be devoured."

The creature shrieked and bellowed with desperate fury, unleashing everything it had accumulated over eons!

A final volcanic cataclysm erupted, creating a sea of molten destruction that should have obliterated anything in the Decillions of Complexity scale!

And Noah had just stepped in the Nonillions!

Yet...

Noah simply walked forward through it, his sun-like limbs scorching away all fire authority they contacted.

He reached Pyrokhans main body and grabbed it with both hands.

The creature tried to burn him, tried to melt his grip, but Noahs hands had become something beyond simple flesh.

They were manifestations of his Way made solid.

And then, brutally, he began to rip Pyrokhan apart.

His fingers tore through flesh that should have been impervious. His hands scorched the creatures Sovereignty, burning away its claim to Fire piece by piece.

Pyrokhan writhed and screamed, but Noah was merciless.

He proceeded to brutally eat the creatures Way of existence piece by piece, consuming its Fire Sovereignty bite by bloody bite.

His own Way shone ever brighter with each consumption!

The end scene was apocalyptic and destructive. Blood boiled and evaporated. Flesh charred and disintegrated. The surrounding existence buzzed with resonance and melted from the sheer intensity.

And when it was finally over, Noah stood alone amidst the devastation, his body still glowing with solar radiance as the last traces of Pyrokhans existence were absorbed.

The message was clear: Noah had exceeded expectations again!

And they were just getting started.
