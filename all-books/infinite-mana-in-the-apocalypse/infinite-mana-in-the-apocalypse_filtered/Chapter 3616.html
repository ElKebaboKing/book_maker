Ashmole!

"I know very well who you are. Did you forget about me, Alchemist Ashmole?"

"Haha! Osmont? Osmont! I thought you were dead and banished after I stopped messing around with the Commonwealth. So you are alive and well! But..."

Ashmole looked around at the terrifying bursts of tentacles that were supported by Absolute Unidemic Existence Pathogenesis.

"...this type of power here is excessive even for you, don't you think? How about we-"

"What did you do with my drop of blood?"

WAA!

Noah interrupted the ramblings of the old man.

They had made an exchange what seemed like many, many days ago when Noah was first exploring the weavings of the Commonwealth.

Who knew that all the way back then, Noah had met a Prime Source Complexity who was veiling himself to be so weak?

Hearing the question posed to him, Ashmole stopped and smiled.

"Oh, that?"

"I truly do study Existence. Especially anomalous Existence. You, other creatures in separate frequencies who hold a massive flow of the Wheel around them, Singed, and now...even those Faceless Lifeforms."

WAA!

HUUM!
