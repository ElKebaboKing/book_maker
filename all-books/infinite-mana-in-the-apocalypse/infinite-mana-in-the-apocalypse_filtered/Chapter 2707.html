2705 Deterioration! Refinement! I

It had been over a day since Noah's initial display of power, with his control over Vitalis Hyperversal Authority being shown as at that time- it was enough to make people think he had undergone his Second Vitalis Awakening.

Though at that time, he only had Primitive Vitalis Manipulation and was of the First Vitalis Awakening!

But it was still enough for the Havenly Master of the Obsidian Havenly Saurischian Archipelago to pay attention to him.

He was officially stripped of his identity as an Underdweller as he emanated the glow of the Havenly Animalia Extremity Lineage, and any existences in the Supernal Havens of Extremity who had undergone Vitalis Awakening were a precious resource!

This was even more so for those who had undergone their Second Vitalis Awakening as they were commonly genius Tier 2 Hyperversal Extremities, or those at the stage of Tier 3 or 4 Hyperversal Extremity.

An Apex Aeonic Lifeform being in this state though? It was unheard of!

[You've had some time to think it over...what will your choice be? Even the forces of the Supernal Havens of Extremity will soon set off and be embroiled in the ensuing chaos. I can write a Recommendation for you to enter the Havenly Hyperversal Gauntlet of Gradation in the Supernal Haven of Helios. It is a treasured domain born under the warping of Vitalis Hyperversal Authority and the nearby spacetime continuum. It allows Entities and Treasures from Recorded History to appear within as it is also filled with great peril and unknown dangers, but the Genius Extremities of our Supernal Havens are sent there when they seek to traverse the Gradations of Extremity at a faster pace. It is truly meant to cut down on hundreds or thousands of years of hard work...so long as you can survive the Gauntlet of Gradation. Spots are limited in order to keep the shocking concentration of authority and treasures within, but you will be able to take a slot so long as you prove yourself.]

...!

The Havenly Hyperversal Gauntlet of Gradation!

It was a place that eliminated the factor of time- reducing it by a great deal and allowing genius Existences to more smoothly traverse the Gradations of Extremity.

These were the words that the Havenly Master of this Archipelago was telling Noah before a moment later...

They both turned their heads up towards a certain direction. And soon after...

BOOM!

The space above the gorgeous obsidian island with its flowing waterfalls of Dissolution split as a massive spinning Crimson Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Rune Scripture of LUNACY appeared.

It held a crimson halo around it, and yet the scripture itself was filled with a majestic blue glow as even though it threatened to cover the whole Archipelago, its authority was focused like a needle towards a single being!

The Havenly Master of the Archipelago saw this as his eyes opened with brilliant anger, a harrumph coming from him as he pressed his index finger up.

A single finger.

And yet in the next moment, a crimson light of Extremity flashed as from a swirling Crimson Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Rune Scripture, an illusory finger of Titanic stature and size bloomed as it pressed on the descending Scripture of Lunacy!

The single illusory finger was endlessly light years wide as it instantly shattered the blinding glow of Lunacy, continuing to go further up as the expression of Extremity Blue changed rapidly.

[Ya!]

She bellowed out with fervor as shockingly, the massive illusory finger continued to barrel towards her and arrived on moments- with her body erupting and releasing an endless sea of blue that the massive finger sank into.

BOOM!

The endlessly blue sea of Lunacy churned as it was split apart and destroyed into streams of innumerable rivers, the figure of Extremity Blue manifesting far off on the side from one cerulean stream as crimson gold blood flowed freely from her eyes.

She looked to be in a terrible state after facing the counterattack of the Tier 4 Hyperversal Extremity of the Obsidian Havenly Saurischian Archipelago as his voice boomed out at this moment!

[You dare? A measly Clan from the Nether Havens of Extremity dares to be so bold as to attack with the intent to decimate a whole Archipelago?!]

His voice boomed out like a thunderclap as in the region above, Extremity Blue's bleeding eyes still burned with utmost hatred as she didn't falter.

The light of Lunacy bloomed again as this time, her whole body radiated the spinning of a myriad of Crimson Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Rune Scriptures as a moment later, she exploded into billions of strands of water droplets that spread out to cover the whole Archipelago, this water drops coming down like an endless rain as even though there were billions of water drops...Extremity Blue's soul and existence was only hidden in a single one!

And the billions of other drops of water all carried the terrifying corrosive power of a being on the precipice of Tier 4 Hyperversal Extremity.

To try and pick her out amidst all of these water drops...should be impossible as it should give Blue some time to get closer to the one she wanted to kill.

[OSMONT!]

The billions of water droplets all bellowed out as they thundered down with utter disregard for the Havenly Master.

And this being turned exceedingly cold as his body glowed with authority, his two palms rising in front of his chest as he brought them together.

In the space above where innumerable water drops were raining down, two massive illusory palms formed as they pressed from left and right, squeezing together and evaporating all the water drops they came in contact with in a shocking show of power as they crashed towards each other speedily, eradicating every single drop of water until...

BZZT!

A single drop of water remained as it came to struggle between the incoming pressure of two palms!

[Ya!]

Extremity Blue's raging bellow came out as she transformed from the water drop, her eyes unwilling as she was outclassed by this Tier 4 Hyperversal Extremity on all sides!

And now, his massive illusory hands threatened to crush her between them as she burned every ounce of authority she had to stop them from clamping her out of existence.

This gave birth to a figure of a dazzling gorgeous crazy woman stuck between two illusory palms, her hands stretched left and right as her bountiful chest trembled from the shocking pressure!

And the Havenly Master didn't relent as he brought his palms ever closer, intending to squash this being and decimate their records!

[Sigh.]

...!

At such a time, the reality playing out and being recorded fluctuated.

The gaze of the Havenly Master became stern as at this moment, the blaring aura of another Tier 4 Hyperversal Extremity appeared!
