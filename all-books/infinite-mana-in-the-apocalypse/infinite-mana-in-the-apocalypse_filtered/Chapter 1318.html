Chapter 1316 - An Uprising! II

Invincibility!

Such a word was called out as Aegon's body flickered and it turned into multicolored light, shooting towards the three blurs of golden light that were heading towards him in a fearless fashion!

The eyes of these Grand Primordial Beasts swirled with the light of Edicts as they were truly powerful, but they couldn't be more powerful than an existence in the Cosmic Realm. So what exactly gave these existences the confidence to face Aegon?

Unless they held multiple GREATER Edicts or one ranked even higher, what gave them the confidence to stand against the Great Conqueror?! The answer was simply the existence behind them as if one were to look closely at the beastial eyes of these Primordial Beasts, they would be able to notice the light of countless golden swords shining within.

Golden swords that uniquely bore the structure of a unique type of Primordial Eye that only one existence had in this Reality!

The golden swords reflected in their eyes hummed as they released a unique power that surrounded the bodies of the three Grand Primordial Beasts along with the essence of their Edicts, and they fearlessly went to clash against Aegon as the impact was something that even the supernova of a Universe could not replicate.

BOOOOM!

An ecstatic battle unfolded in this corner as back in the center of the Cosmic Battlefield, the aura of Stormdust finally stabilized as it released waves of unbelievable power. His form was that of a titanic Dragon with distinct elephantine features like golden tusks on either side of his spiked jaws, pairs of colorful wings on his back as his eyes shone with a similar light that was present in the eyes of the three Grand Primordial Beasts!

The influence of a certain existence was very much presently inserting itself at this moment as amidst the chaos, Stormdust shot towards Noah while his body became suffused with dangerous lights.

"Whether real or false, there is one thing that will always remain true about destiny." Stormdust's voice echoed out brutally while the vast new and dangerous aura emanating from him clustered onto his crimson gold claws that tore across the boundaries of galaxies and universes towards the figure of a single situated being.

"Destiny is not always guaranteed...it is something foretold as before it has come to be, anything can happen!"

WAA!

"Even those with grand Destinies can fall! Even promised Champions can fall!"

...!

Stormdust's voice seemed to be filled with booming emotions as he accepted his new situations while getting used to the ever expanding sense of power! He said these words with conviction as they had to be true. As he had to believe them if he wanted to overcome the huge walls before his eyes.

His newfound power carried his words forward as instantly, his humongous form appeared above the existence sitting on the golden purple throne, his now draconic claw thundering down as it contained the compression of not just his Edict of Burning Destiny- but also another power that he borrowed from none other than Hyperion!

The claw carried his hopes for the future as he wanted to see it rip apart the smug gaze of this single being at least!

Yet...

BZZZZT!

An impact of indescribable waves of light erupted out the moment that his claw neared the Throne of Tyranny, being stopped by the appearance of an illusory golden shield that seemed to be capable of withstanding even the ravages of time.

From the Nomological Edict of Bulwark, it was the Shield of the Bulwark that granted +50,000,000% Increased True Defense, blocked 50% of All Incoming True Damage, and had a Thorn effect that returned 100,000,000% of all received Damage back to the enemy!

It shone with utter splendor as it was filled with a unique array of swirling runic lines, and behind it...the Throne of Tyranny could now be seen shining with multiple colors as beside this unbreakable shield, the [Aura of Vigor], [Aura of Defense], [Aura of Mana], [Aura of Grace]...all of the auras contained in the <Aura Master> ability from the Edict of Bulwark were displayed gorgeously.

On top of all this, the < Warder> ability was cast as Noah encased all of the few hundred Primordial Centurions closing in on Stormdust's forces and the now blooming Primordial Beasts- all of the damage they received being taken by him as he could release this in the form of an attack whenever he wished!

Abilities like these and others from the Edict of Amelioration and other Edicts bloomed, surrounding Noah in a glorious fashion as he gazed up at the stopped claw of Stormdust that held terrific power that might have injured him just days ago...but not today!

"Others with astounding destinies may fall..but not me."

...!

While surrounded by the incandescent glow of multiple stacked defensive auras that made him look like an ancient existence surrounded by primal circular veils of light, Noah spoke domineeringly.

"This is not hubris or pride, little Betrayer...but it is fact!"

WAA!

Others can fall, but not him!

These words rang out for all to hear as they caused their Origins to tremble from the sense of surety the being that said them had!

Amidst the chaotic battle, the eyes of others could not help but lock onto him as they saw the majestic image of this being situated seamlessly on a throne with waves of colorful circular auras surrounding him, and yet his eyes and the strength they released seemed to be the most illuminating thing stemming from him.

OOOH!

Essence surged around him as while situated on the throne of Tyranny, Noah raised his right hand.

A mere raise of his hand while gazing up towards the figure of Stormdust...and he then called out four words in a magisterial fashion!

"Hammer...of Cosmic Glory!"

...!

The only offensive ability from the Nomological Edict of the Forgemaster was utilized.

The profound space of the Cosmos tore and sundered.

From the cracks, something terrific formed as a fantastical scene worthy to be inscribed into the annals of history bloomed!
