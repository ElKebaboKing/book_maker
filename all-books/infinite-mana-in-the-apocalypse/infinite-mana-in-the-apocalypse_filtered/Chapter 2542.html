Those from truly powerful Inheritances or those part of the Seven Clans were gaining even more boons as they basked in the aura of their Hyperversal Extremities, being given unique resources to aid them in forging their Dynamis of Extremity.

The Wall of Dissolution did not crumble down as the Extremity Vein rose ever so slowly even now, the Culmination Step not yet unfolding as a decent number of the myriad factions came to the periphery of the Apex Battlefield to watch.

Some Inheritances considered it beneath them as their forces already entered the Extremity Vein of Dissolution and stood before the massive wall that stopped all others!

But the mere presence of renowned names from factions like the Sima Clan, Infinite Extremity, and Plum Sword of Extremity being here observing caused a few more eyes to be drawn over.

"It seems the suspense and questions are about to come to an end."

His exceedingly handsome face was apparent to all as he was adorned in a light blue Emperor's Robe, seeming like he was going on a simple stroll as he gazed into the Battlefield ahead of him that instantly blazed with a terrifying intent for battle the moment he had appeared.

"Osmont!"

The being who had crushed Euryale's soul right before him.
