Chapter 4475: Pathos III

Space cracked violently as Noah emerged from the dimensional tear with grace.

His descent was measured. Controlled. Alexander and Seraphina rose from their thrones immediately.

Noah smiled calmly and proceeded to sit on another throne opposite them without requesting permission or waiting for invitation.

He looked at both Alexander and Seraphina as his smile widened.

"It seems you two were successful in your endeavors? I was rooting for you, Seraphina."

Noah articulated this with knowing amusement.

Because in the past, when he had encountered these two in Aeternitas Concordia, he had advised Alexander how one mans heart could be tied to multiple women without betraying any of them.

It seemed his philosophy had infected The Peacemaker successfully!

After his words, Seraphina Valorheart visibly relaxed, as if confirming he remained the same entity they had met all those days ago.

Alexander also breathed out with relief, his gaze affirming Noah held no connections to THE Loom despite the terrifying power he and his forces displayed.

Alexanders words emerged with measured directness.

"I did not anticipate the absolutely terrifying power that your forces and you personally seem to be wielding. This creates certain possibilities that I believed would only become viable weeks or months down the timeline after my own substantial personal advancement."

His eyes gleamed with calculated hope.

Noah looked toward Alexander as he nodded.

"Oh? What possibilities?"

Alexander continued.

"In the Earliest Folds during the initial conflicts, when forces of THE Living Emotive and THE Living Paradox were systematically deployed... The Primordial Civilization of THE Living Law was targeted specifically by combined armies of Emotive and THE Living Elemental."

He paused deliberately.

"Some of the more powerful components of Laws forces were scattered during systematic assault. The major battle had already occurred, THE Heart of Law itself was decimated and fractured through combined assault from THE Living Paradox and THE Living Emotive working in coordination."

His expression grew somber.

"In that catastrophic engagement that followed, the armies of Emotive and Elemental pursued the surviving vestiges of Law who were desperately carrying a shattered fragment of THE Heart of Law itself, attempting to preserve it from complete annihilation."

Alexander leaned forward.

"For the sake of saving one of the remaining vestiges of his collapsed Civilization along with that precious fragment... THE Living Law instituted an Existential Injunction and froze everything and everyone."

His voice intensified.

"The vestiges of his own collapsed Civilization holding the shattered Heart fragment... and the pursuing armies of Emotive and Elemental that would have systematically eliminated them... all were frozen across eons of time and contained in whats called the Injunction Sanctuary of Law."

He placed his hand on his chest.

"Countless powerful Living Laws have been frozen there across incomprehensible duration, still holding the shattered fragment they were trying to preserve. But frozen alongside them are the terrifying armies that would have annihilated them completely."

At this critical point, Alexander looked toward Noah with eyes shining.

"I have obtained the inheritance of THE Living Law. This Injunction Sanctuary represents something I was planning to reclaim after growing sufficiently powerful over coming weeks and months."

His voice dropped.

"THE Remnant Will of THE Living Law itself expressed profound regret that we lack time. That if we possessed sufficient duration to systematically reclaim the shattered Heart fragment and other scattered components of the collapsed Civilization... there existed genuine possibility of reconstituting power close to or exactly at THE entity level."

He shook his head.

"Power sufficient to withstand The Fallout entirely. But THE Living Law was woeful that time remains insufficient. That I wouldnt reach Sextillions and Septillions in Complexity fast enough through conventional advancement."

His gaze fixed upon Noah with intensity.

"But you... it appears your forces alone already operate at those power levels. If we assault this Injunction Sanctuary together... the terrifying armies of Emotive and Elemental frozen within...forces that should range within Sextillions in Complexity...we may actually prove capable of systematically collapsing them."

His voice carried weight of proposal!

"We could save the collapsed Civilization of Law. I could obtain the shattered Heart fragment and potentially begin to reconstitute THE Living Laws power. What do you think?"

...!

Beside him, Seraphina Valorheart looked visibly tense as this monumental proposition was said.

The Dukes of Law scattered throughout the coliseum appeared even more strained...several possessed ashen faces as if they desperately hoped this conversation would proceed differently.

As if they didnt want the weavings of Law to be shared with others!

Noah maintained his focused gaze on Alexander as he eventually nodded with calm finality.

"I have armies being forged just waiting to be directed at appropriate targets. Acceptable. I will join in on this fun."

...!

His simple agreement caused Alexanders face to relax substantially, though caution remained.

"It will be extraordinarily dangerous. The forces of Emotive and Elemental frozen in that Sanctuary represent genuine Primordial Civilizations before most withdrew into THE Loom. The engagement will be absolutely brutal."

Noahs smile only widened at such warning as he responded with tyrannical satisfaction.

"I appreciate brutality above most other approaches. Come. Let us prepare for the assault."

...!

In the Abode of The Early Creature, Osmont.

Inside an isolated domain of this abode.

Different layers of seas composed of blue-gold Primus Mana stretched across the space endlessly. Radiant rays of concentrated blue-gold light permeated everything...filling the domain with ambient glow that pulsed rhythmically.

At the very center of such magnificent space, Noahs figure could be observed lying atop the surface of the radiant sea. His toned physique was bare as a veil of concentrated Primus Mana covered him and the figure of Riya snuggled contentedly in his arms.

As if this moment stripped away some of the many burdens weighing on her existence.

Noahs eyes remained clear as he contemplated the conversation another instance of himself had just conducted with Alexander.

"An Injunction Sanctuary of Law..."

He raised his hand upward as he looked at the infinite seas of Primus Mana stretching above and below...thinking about how the Specialists of his Primordial Civilization would receive opportunity to be deployed against forces from genuine Primordial Civilizations.

The same Civilizations whose leaders I just witnessed discussing THE Looms construction through Malphass recovered memories...

Glorious!
