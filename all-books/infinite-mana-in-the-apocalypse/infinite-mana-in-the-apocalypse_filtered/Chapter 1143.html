[Automatic Planting and Harvesting] was utilized yet again, this time on a crimson Galaxy that was designated as a Ruination Law Space and held an uncountable number of resplendent Ruination Strawberries.

He could elevate his Comprehension and Assimilation with ease for all of his Cosmic Daos!

Such a thought caused his eyes to release radiant rays of golden light as they landed on the vast number of Primordial Apples and Ruination Strawberries floating before him.

Some would wonder, was he about to start chowing down on all these hundreds of billions of sacred fruits until he reached the pristine 100% in both of the Cosmic Daos?

Maybe if it was another being, that would be occurring. But this...was Noah Osmont!

Among the many cards he had to play, he had the Sin of Gluttony that he could utilize the abilities of, this Sin capable of Devouring things and putting them in the [Large Stomach] that acted akin to Noahs own.

[Devouring].

The hard to raise proficiency of these Cosmic Daos...had actually begun to shoot up at this moment as Noah devoured truly ridiculous fruits that should in no way exist.

"More! More!"

The dense aura of the Sin of Gluttony erupted from him with even more fervor as it spread to cover hundreds of thousands of the Primordial Apples and Ruination Strawberries, the dense essence around Noah only exploding in a terrifying manner at this moment.

Before Noah could even blink, the interface before his eyes seemed to have almost glitched as in the next second, he saw complete 100% Comprehension in the Cosmic Dao of Ruination as a moment later, this same number appeared next to the Primordial Dao!
