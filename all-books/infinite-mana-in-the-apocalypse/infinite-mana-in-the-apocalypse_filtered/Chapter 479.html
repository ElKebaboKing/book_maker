Chapter 478 Simultaneous Breakthroughs

In the Time Space of the Spiritual World, a particularly arrogant Emperor Penguin raised its beak high as it felt the course of a new Supreme energy travel through his body.

The aura of chaos that normally destabilized the essence of those it entered felt like a docile stream as it passed through his vessels and core, his breakthrough into the Domain Expansion Realm that was at the cusp of occurring actually happened right away with the addition of this new energy.

RAA! RAA! RAA!

He let out fearsome battle cries, but he wasnt the only one as many of the other Harbingers and beings that were receiving Domain Rank Cores from his Master the past few days were all experiencing breakthroughs into this realm simultaneously!

RUMBLE!

Essence rapidly converged around the numerous beings stepping into the Domain Rank as they all felt the newest boost from the addition of the new Guider Type Ability- Dark Guider.

Apart from the golden energy of fate coursing through them, a dark scintillating energy of chaos was now vibrantly coursing through them as well, their bodies feeling the boost in power once more.

They could be considered bonafide beings at the Domain Rank, but their power was unlike any others at this realm as they were receiving huge buffs from two Guider Type Abilities!

The Emperor Penguin rose to the skies as he felt this fearsome power, his fanaticism towards his master only growing as he sought to prove his strength in battle.

[Come to the Forge.]

His thoughts were cut short as he received a command from the Spiritual World itself, his arrogance being cut short as this was the only entity besides his Master that could push him around.

A silver light flashed as the figure of the Emperor Penguin appeared outside of the Blacksmiths Forge, entering it thereafter as he was met with a floating item set that shone with the luster of chaos!

[As the first beast of this Spiritual World, you represent all of your brothers and sisters whenever you move. Master gave me the right to choose who will be wearing the created Chaos Set Items, and Im going to be biased and give you one of them! You must live up to your name as the Emperor Penguin.]

The voice of the Spiritual World that seemed more commanding when communicating with the arrogant penguin rang out, the beak of the penguin nodding up and down as it received the item set that nobody else had gotten their hands on yet.

[Baleful Chaos Bringer Set] :: An item set made from the materials of Chaos Behemoths. Contains a scimitar, breastplate, briefs, boots, arm guards, and helm. Varying levels of boosts are given when the set items are worn simultaneously. 2 Set Items- Reduces the cooldown and mana cost for all Chaos Abilities by half, as well as doubling their effects. 4 Set Items- Grants the wearer a Chaos Barrier that reflects 30% of all incoming damage back to the caster. 6 Set Items- A passive Chaos Storm is initiated by the resonance of all 6 Set Pieces. This Chaos Storm swirls around the wearer as it tears down the defenses of their enemies while boosting their own attacks. +250% Fate Element Resistance, +250% All Element Penetration, +250% All Element Resistance, +100% Chaos Damage applied to all abilities.

The set bonus of the spectacular [Baleful Chaos Bringer Set] became apparent in the eyes of the Emperor Penguin as he glanced at the smaller descriptions of the items themselves and all of their boosts. His eyes shone with brilliance as he adorned the Item Set, his fluffy body being covered by the dark chaos armor as it was soon fully hidden, only a fearsome shape of an armored penguin wielding a Scimitar that shone with a deadly edge becoming visible.

RUMBLE!

A Chaos Storm erupted from him as he enjoyed the feeling of strength the Item Set provided, but this didnt last long as he felt an immense pressure descend down on him a second later as the Chaos Storm continued to spread out in the Blacksmiths Forge.

[Dont get conceited and damage the things in the Forge. Remember that all of this strength is made possible by the Master. Just try to be more useful now to him now.]

The cold voice and pressure of the Spiritual World descended on the Emperor Penguin as his beak pointed down in a docile manner, stopping the advancement of the ability given the 6 Item Set boost while also being reminded once more that beside his Master, the entity that was the Spiritual World was the only other one that could take him off his pedestal no matter his strength!

This was because he was a beast born from this unique World, his Origin being tied closely to it.

"I shall go acclimate to my new Realm and Items while waiting for Master to call us..."

The Emperor Penguin spoke out with a tone of someone bullied but it still held a tinge of arrogance as he flashed away from the Blacksmiths Forge, going on to test his new strength and abilities in the Time Space of the Spiritual World.

Besides him, numerous beings in the Spiritual World broke into the Domain Expansion Realm thereafter as they were also adorned with their own Set Items!

The remaining two [Baleful Chaos Bringer Set] went to Elena and Barbatos, their realms breaking through the fastest while also holding their own unique strengths.

Aside from the limited Chaos Sets, the Spiritual World System was working to rapidly produce Set Items from the Fate Behemoths that Noahs main body continued to take down in the Celestial Realm.

Even though standing against Celestials with the opposing Chaos Sets was ideal, having armors made from Fate Behemoths also provided a level protection from the element itself, thus other beings would be adorned with newly created Fate Item Sets that were being created right at this moment. They had to be lucky enough to first enter the Domain Expansion Realm, and for the war to not break out too soon before the creation of the Fate Set Items were finished!

But just on Noahs side, numerous beings were stepping into the Domain Rank as they were fitted with fearsome Items. All of them were waiting...waiting to participate in the first battle of many in a war that would later be known in history as...the Last War!
