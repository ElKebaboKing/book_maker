2573 Overpowered At Its Best! II

The 14th Boundary Layer of Quintessence was simply maddening!

Once a Dynamis of Extremity was forged, it was set in stone.

If one wanted to create something similar but with additional concepts or different concepts, they had to converge a brand new dynamis and examine out its compatibility!

But Quintessence looked at such a reality and chose to give it the middle finger.

It told Noah on its 14th Boundary Layer that he could add and eliminate Concepts from Perennial Yggdrasil of Extremity as he wished- the only thing being a high cost as to him, the cost didn't matter!

His Quintessential Hyperversal Mana Value took care of nearly all of his asset costs instead of the required Soul Values.

He would be able to freely add and remove multiple concepts even if the cost was stated to be 75% of his total Soul Values Per concept as the main thing here...was that he could enhance his Absolute Dynamis of Extremity into Quintessential Absolute Dynamis of Extremity!

All of these surprises...stemmed from a single Boundary of Quintessence.

What others may consider a dead path because it was simply too grand.

A path that could not be taken to Extremity!

It fell along the same lines as the concept of Infinity.

The Boundary Layers of this concept...were part of the core reason that Noah's visage of Alexander was currently shining with translucent and crystalline light as if his very body was a young Armament of Extremity being forged!

His fantastical changes had only just begun to unfold.

But outside of this, events continued to spill out around the Apex Battlefield as the blinding sword light that cut open Adjudication continued to blind all beings for a few seconds, and when it finally died down...

The scene revealed was one to stare at in wonder and wonder.

Fantastically, the only figure to be seen in the skies was the Adjudicator of Dissolution who currently had tendrils of profound authority raging around her- the auras of other Hyperversal Extremities nowhere to be seen.

It was as if they were never here to begin with!

As if a shocking battle had not just taken place.

The ones veiled with Hyperversal Extremity Authority who attacked were nowhere to be seen, and the ones wearing white gold masks who had appeared were also nowhere to be seen!

The Apex Battlefield below showed rows of Enders of Nature standing fearlessly, valiant Apex Aeonic Lifeforms adorned with white gold Armaments floating in front of them with valor as around them...deathly wheelies ran rampant as a certain lich collected corpses nonchalantly as if nothing else was happening around it.

A few massive Emperor Tier Thanatos Vassals could be seen in the background along with the sorry figures of Primeval Elder Silas, Roan, and other remnants from the Cult of Endless Shadows as they stared around them breathlessly.

The spectators who were blinded by darkness and then light this whole time could finally see again as they drew sharp breaths when the scene became clear!

On the battlefield, the only thing left behind of the Avernus and Singularity Overseer Elders were corpses that a cackling Haven Lich was collecting with glee, all others being whisked away as approximately 15-20% of the forces of powerful Apex Aeonic Lifeforms were saved.

But all others faced a devastating loss as the eyes of many beings couldn't help but focus on the man still seated on the multicolored throne in all this.

He hadn't moved a muscle.

He had grandly sat and watched over everything as even now, his eyes seemed to gaze around their audiences with scrutiny as if he was looking for the attackers that had come and gone from among them!

Far in the distance, the figure of Erikson grandly fell back on his seat as...

"Fucking hell..." He put his slippers back on his feet while rubbing his brows, this whole reality being an utter headache as too many significant things happened.

Ayame's eyes shone brilliantly as beside her, Atlas had a clouded expression as moments later...

"You guys will have to excuse me for a while."

...!

He rose from the vessel of Erikson and disappeared into a burst of Plum petals as too many things seemed to be on his mind!

CAA!

And at this time, a fearsome cry of authority rang out as the figure of the Adjudicator of Dissolution gazed at the masses.

Her piercing eyes seemed to cross across all veils and land on the souls of all beings here as her fearsome voice reverberated out with a hint of clear anger!

[Today, multiple parties from a Clan and Inheritances violated the Investiture of Extremity as they believed their power and standing would mean there would be no consequences!]

WAA!

[You may disguise yourselves and use the darkness as a cover, but you know who you are! You know...since today, I stripped the very defining Concept of Extremity from one of you. Since you do not seem to hold the decorum of a Tier 3 Hyperversal Extremity, you can back down to Tier 2!]

...!

Her words were utterly callous as the skies seethed at her taunting!

She knew who the culprits were.

The culprits knew exactly what she was talking about.

In the distance, one could almost hear the howls of a maddened Hyperversal Extremity who seemed to be getting held back from appearing due to the taunts of the Adjudicator!

As for the audiences across the myriad of Inheritance and Clans here...

They drew sharp breaths at the words of the Adjudicator.

She had stripped the authority of a Hyperversal Extremity from Tier 3 to Tier 2.

Very few beings even knew the true distinction between Tier 1 Hyperversal Extremities and those who were Tier 2 and Tier 3!

But it was broadly known that the requirement for one to be elected as an Adjudicator of a Haven was to be at the Tier 3 at the very least!

Such beings were the cornerstones of the Seven Havens.

And for one reason or another...the Seven Havens had deprived a being of Tier 3 Hyperversal Extremity!
