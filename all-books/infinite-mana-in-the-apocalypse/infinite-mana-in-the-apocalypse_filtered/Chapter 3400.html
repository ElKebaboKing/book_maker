Chapter 3398 Unbound Obsession! IV

Golden Titans on one side.

A single Golden defense Tower on the other.

The image of an illusory emperor within this tower staring at the shocking number of forces of the Prisoners all by himself etched itself in the minds of many- as if he alone would shoulder this burden!

But they knew.

The moment he fell, a disaster would bloom.

So in the next instant...

|Charge!|

|Defend!|

The Tresorverse Towers of Existence stretched out across this space buzzed and moved, with the Ordinances of the Ordinance Amalgamation Entities shooting out across the equivalent length of 5 Omniverses or mortuary forming a tide that surged towards the Golden Titans.

They saw that they didn't necessarily have to have the power to overcome and defeat the Golden Titans- they only had to disrupt their weavings significantly enough to ruin their Severing or Ordinance Amalgamation and cause the collapse of their Existence!

So they moved.

The Golden Titan that had initially faced Noah and now held collapsing weavings of existence roared out, turning into a streak of purple gold brilliance as even while his Ordinance and weavings collapsed...there were still waves of purple radiance trying to reverse everything.

|Death, I do not fear!|

He roared maddeningly as he came before Noah's massive tower once more; Noah's eyes honed in on him and the other auras that now moved alongside him as he let out a harrumph.

In terms of Complexity, he wasn't far off.

In terms of Runes of Existence, he was even putting himself above them!

And to take care of this burning obsession of Pyrrhus...bathing in the Blood of these Golden Titans didn't seem bad as he once more pulled upon the heaviness of the Infiniverse Megalos Physiology!

In the surroundings.

|Defend!|

Other Towers of Existence arrived as in one, the illusory visage of a seated old man could be seen.

It was none other than Old Man Eudemus who was looking towards Noah's Tower of Existence with an extremely somber gaze that seemed to hold millions of questions and no answers.

Alongside it, dozens of other multicolored Towers of Existence holding the terrifying weavings of Ordinance Amalgamation also arrived and erupted out towards the Golden Titans!

It felt like two roiling seas of weavings would soon clash, and no side wanted to lose as every single entity joined in.

Whether they were at the Ordinance Amalgamation stage or merely One With Existence.

They all released their attacks that joined in together to form a startling sea of brilliance!

And Noah, at the center of all of this, let loose entirely to satiate and give way to an obsession for the last time.

After this, he could keep this obsession repressed as he moved without listening to its blazing wrath, merely having it in the background as a tool of recognition of the enemies around him!

But at this moment, it was free.

He could feel the unbound obsession within the very blood of the Origin Sacred Primal Megalonian as time seemed to slow down to a minute, incomprehensible level.

The Severed golden wheel of Kuklos that was spinning slowly began to speed up as countless waves of authority flowed into it.

Infinite waves of authority!

The weight of the Infiniverse Megalos Physiology was terrifying, yes, but there was also the weight of infinity.

It may not carry as much tonnage, but it had the genuine weight of endlessness that at this moment, Noah let loose freely.

Countless concentrated waves of immense complexity flowed into the rapidly spinning wheel- his whole Tower beginning to radiate a terrifying Golden light as at such a moment...his massive illusory body channeled all of this power into his fists.

An obsession wanted carnage.

He was granting carnage.

In slow motion, his right fist struck out once as his hand burned like a scorching Golden sun.

His left fist then followed with a similar presentation as it seemed unfathomably slow!

But in this slow pacing, his hand pulled back as he punched out again, and again, and again!

The stopped time only seemed to be for him as if the weavings of Kuklos deemed it so.

And his golden fists that seemed like slow bombs of power...gradually began to seem extremely rapid as they turned into a barrage of golden fists composed of trillions of small golden wheels.

The moment the span of an instant passed, what bloomed in front of Noah's tower were thousands of massive golden outlines of his fists shooting out, each one equivalent in size to a tenth of an Omniverse as they covered absolutely everything.

They caused the sea of attacks on their side to shine with golden brilliance as many were only able to catch a glimpse of it before the impact bloomed on both sides.

...!

BOOOM!

It was an unfathomably heavy cluster of complexity coming together all at once as every single entity was swept up in it!

As this heavy impact of two seas occurred.

Outside of the Tresorverse Sacred Domain of the Omnifather.

Within the Megalos Ynnere.

An unkempt entity that was floating in the blankness of the Megalos Ynnere with multiple Omniverses circling around him raised his head and looked far above- as if there was something only he could see that others could not.

His eyes were starry and filled with brilliance, with an aura far exceeding that of Existence Severing silently bubbling under his skin!

The entity that had disappeared from the throngs of the Commonwealth just before the portals leading to the Tresorverse Domain bloomed.

An entity that Old Man Eudemus and others had never been able to truly understand or grasp the true extent of his power.

At this moment, his gaze seemed to be crossing untold distances to see what was happening within the trunk of the Primaltree of Existence, his eyes filling up with ever increasing brilliance as he sighed.

"Is this truly the right path, Omnifather?"

...!

Words of doubt and longing at the same time.

This being wondered of such weavings as he rose up- intently gazing up and ready to move in case he needed to!
