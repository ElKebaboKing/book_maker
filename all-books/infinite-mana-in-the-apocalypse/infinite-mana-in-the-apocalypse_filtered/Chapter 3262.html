Chapter 3260 State of Existence! II

At the same time, the figure of Noah below Savitar exploded with authority as his colorless white and black wings stretched out brilliantly.

Invictus Maximus.

They foresaw the assurance of a favored result.

Under his gaze, his Third Mundi of Power pulsed as Ingeniare blazed like flames that were only just beginning to burn.

They clustered in his eyes as he saw...the foreign appearance of an unbreakable Outerversal Authority covering everything like a ceiling.

And this ceiling was akin to a blanket of Dark Matter that effectively cut off a large part of Savitar's power from the Megalos Beyond where his main body should be!

A foreign interference that would go to help them- of course, it wasn't for no reason.

'Interesting...'

His eyes twinkled as his own movements didn't stop. The wings turned into dazzling colorless white and black feathers that formed into lines that began to bind the Limbs of Savitar, some even seeking to wrap around his massive skull to anchor this being in place as two wheels rotated above and below him.

Noah was within this same space, yet the terrifying power of these Wheels was not directed at him- but even if they were, he had interminable confidence to withstand them.

But the power of Command Protector Valeria was not to be scoffed at as she directed her authority to achieve her goal here, Savitar roaring out as the honor and grace of a Great One was being spat on at this moment.

|Impudence! You think that you are taking on an opportunity when you're falling into the schemes of others. Someone else has just made a move and you're only acting as a sword on their behalf! Take a step back and think before doing someone else's bidding, O Peon of the Commonwealth!|

His authoritative voice boomed out as if he still controlled everything.

Yet neither Noah nor Command Protector Valeria stopped their actions as the expansion of Noah's wings began to light up with the Authority of the Administrator of the Atlas of Existence!

The colorless hue was much deeper.

It almost seemed liquefied and more real as the power of all three of his Systems of Existence were pulled upon to momentarily aid in holding down an Entity who far exceeded the stages of Existential Dominium Realm- and should have been someone only those in the Omniversal Stage could handle.

Yet Noah's Existential Veil of Transcendence made many things possible.

|Fools!|

The cruel voice of Savitar boomed out as its authority could not suppress both Noah and Command Protector Valeria- his skull beginning to gain visible cracks as the hundreds of spinning Pillars of obsidian radiance stopped being anchored in space and converged towards his body like splicing beams of Existence.

Noah knew his words were not false.

His mind already worked out all the possibilities as he already had an idea of what the foreign interference was!

Since it was a Veil of Dark Matter, the culprit was another Outerversal Morphon.

And out of many possible Outerversal Morphons, who else would know precisely where they were and what they were doing?

He smiled silently as the face of Protector Lucretia rose up.

'Is this the influence of the one who bound you?

He asked such a question to himself as he then placed himself in the perspective of the foreign Outerversal Morphon who leaked Savitar.

What could he gain from this? From a body of Savitar being destroyed?

...!

Noah's eyes radiated unfathomable radiance.

'You are here doing exactly the same thing as me?'

What was he doing right now? He was also interfering and keeping the weakened body of an Outerversal Morphon in place so that after his death...

The Delegatus he had bound would be his!

Now, it seemed there was another seeking to do the same thing.

So who would come out on top? Who would claim the ultimate prize?

|I will not sit here and be disgraced by a bunch of kids being used by others.|

The deep voice of Savitar echoed out as he stopped struggling.

His purple flames gazed above at Command Protector Valeria who looked like a celestial controlling parts of the Wheel of Existence, and he then gazed below towards Noah and his wings that had turned into countless threads encapsulating him!

|A Great One cannot be disgraced by those who are not. You all have managed to earn my ire today, and I will come to collect.|

The words were intense as the expression of Command Protector Valeria instantly changed, her intent booming out towards Noah.

|Release him!|

It was a shout for him to let go.

For him to move as far away as possible.

But even in this situation, Noah did not move a muscle and remained right where he was.

His wings were telling him that this was where he had to be if he wanted to achieve his aims!

The sockets of Savitar instantly extinguished all radiance as his intent rang out for the last time.

|Feel a glimmer of my wrath.|

...!

BOOOM!

He did not wait for his existence within this body to be extinguished.

He ended it himself- and he did so in the most terrifying way possible.

He detonated everything this body stood for!

An Entity with the Distinction of a Great One would rather detonate one of his bodies than face the disrespect and schemes of others!

A blinding obsidian light that unraveled all weavings of Matter exploded out Command Protector Valeria grit her teeth as her figure flickered, stepping on the crimson wheel above as her figure disappeared and was teleported on the blue wheel below- her figure rising up and seeking to pull Noah away from the point of the explosion!

At the same time, dozens of vibrant illusory wheels manifested around them like bastions of unbreakable shields.

...!

The impact was heavy.

Everything felt like it was broken.

The wheels called out shattered and reformed rapidly as the obsidian light was so blinding that Command Protector Valeria had to close her eyes.

The very weavings of matter felt like they were decimated!

Any form of existence would feel a burning sensation as if they were being scorched out of existence!

But in the midst of all this, as she began to feel her existence burning and her Omniversal Armament become torn....Command Protector Valeria felt a body cover her.

...!

She felt her head pushed into a strong chest, and her back wrapped by strong hands filled with unfathomable steadiness.

And the burning of her existence stopped right after.

All weavings of existence were in chaos as even she couldn't easily see what was happening.

But above her, millions of dazzling threads connecting to Delegatus nobody else could see but a single man flashed ever so briefly.

And within this period of time, all of these threads were absorbed into colorless white and black wings as they traversed rapidly across a Second Mundi of Power!
