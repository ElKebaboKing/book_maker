Chapter 3413 Purgatory or Paradise? I

A small piece of the Infiniverse Megalos Seed.

The moment he arrived in it, Ozymandias released waves and waves of Mana into it as if he was providing a source of power to light it up brightly.

His signature was taken in seamlessly as he went through its very center while wondering.

When the gray brilliance of the Frequency of inevitability hit him, every part of him, whether it be a clone or will, was affected, pulled, and merged back into him by the time he was whisked away!

Only Ozymandias, who shared an entirely different Source of Existence- Dark Matter, was not recognized and ensnared.

But the Infiniverse Megalos Seed...had a part of it not wrapped up in the Entanglement as he could only attribute this to its unique qualities!

What the Infiniverse Megalos Seed actually was.

A form of planetary domain that had risen so far above, to the extent that it became a Megalos Seed.

It had not bloomed into a Megalos yet, but it was on its way there.

When Noah became entangled, his body and the region they were in seemed to all be taken away as he saw the Tower of Existence he was in fracture with a large section of it taken when that clone was pulled back, and the same phenomenon occurred in other places where he was.

Small sections of the land and space around him were also eaten away.

But just because these small sections of land and space became entangled and eaten away into that terrifying Frequency of that place, it didn't then cause all billions of miles of land and space of that domain to also be devoured!

The Infiniverse Megalos Seed was akin to such domains.

It was entangled in the entirety with the Blancverse it was in, but it was akin to a piece of a Megalos being eaten away as it wasn't treated like a normal lifeform!

Thus, a part of it was left behind.

An isolated Dream Aletheian Haven.

It held the workings of the Infiniverse Megalos Seed, but such a small residual weaving of it was left behind as even now, after Ozymandias arrived and began nurturing it with endless waves of Mana did a sense of cognizance and will slowly begin to form.

As it did, Ozymandias had a cold expression as a devouring light erupted from him- the surrounding weavings of Omniverse X-999 also beginning to be pulled in and assimilated into this small isolated Haven to help it grow!

Weavings buzzed as such a process began to take place, the terrific visage of Ozymandias multiplying into two moments later as one left.

Outside of Omniverse X-999, the other body of Ozymandias gazed at the figure of Savitar who held an expression of stupor still as he voiced out coldly.

|Other domains of Outerversal Morphons apart from you and Nietzsche. Let's go.|

He needed fuel currently.

And there were many Omniverses out there as he continued to map sectors- especially Tainted Omniverses under the banner of Outerversal Morphons!

Coming out of his stupor, Savitar nodded brisket before their figures disappeared.

Back in the small isolated Haven, Ozymandias nurtured the connection he held with the Infiniverse Megalos Seed as he currently cradled a golden singularity that had formed after all this time- this singularity containing a budding will as with enough nurturing, it would bloom into enough complexity a sliver of the Infiniverse Megalos Body.

Or so he hoped!

Half an hour passed.

And...

HUUM!

Outside of the isolated Haven, within OmniverseX-999, three terrifying singularities tens of times larger than the Isolated Dream Aletheian Haven itself appeared- these being nothing else but three Dark Matter Cores!

The other body of Ozymandias appeared alongside them as the moment they were transported over, the Isolated Dream Aletheian Haven began to devour and assimilate them greedily!

The singularity in the hands of Ozymandias became denser and denser.

More and more complex.

After a certain point, an emergence of fantastical brilliance erupted out in front of Ozymandias that formed into complex lines of a humanoid figure!

Simmering with multicolored radiance. A level of complexity much, much lower than before!

But undoubtedly...

|Infiniverse...|

Ozymandias called out as the silent visage of the newly built Infiniverse Megalos Body opened its eyes, its pupils hazy and foggy as if there was a tremendously powerful barrier preventing its operations.

Outerversal Authority leaked from Ozymandias among much more, the light of many Ordinances flickering and flowing into the small body before him until...

|Ah...| A voice as if one was taking a breath after a long time came through.

It was followed by light filling the multicolored pupils of the Infiniverse Megalos Body, its eyes landing on Ozymandias as they showed such great surprise and relief to the extent that the moment this body formed, she threw herself towards the towering obsidian stellar body of Ozymandias and hugged it tightly!

|Master!|

A single word.

But it expressed relief. It expressed happiness. It expressed the potential beginning of the end of a terrible nightmare.

Ozymandias looked at her and wondered- just what place was his other self sent to that the monotone Infiniverse Megalos Body now expressed such emotions?

He cradled her head on his stellar chest as he asked lightly.

|Is everyone okay? Those within the Infiniverse Megalos Seed?|

A critical question to put his heart at ease.

And the newly formed Infiniverse Megalos Body blinked while calming herself.

|My state of being has been altered due to that place, and I will work on processing the overflow of emotions, Master. Everyone is okay, but my memories and records of that place are scattered and even delayed in getting to me here, it seems. Here is what I could recover...|

Her forehead glowed as she brought it in contact with his giant chest, bundles of information flowing out from her and into Ozymandias as he became clear on exactly what had happened and where his other self was!

Hell Terra Firma.

The Purgatory!

This was the place his other half was in as at this moment, he began to analyze everything about it!
