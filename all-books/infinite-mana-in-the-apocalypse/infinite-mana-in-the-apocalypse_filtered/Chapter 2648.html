2646 Isn't it unfair? II

The Shadow Extremity Overlord gazed at the blinding singularity in front of him as his soul pulsed with power.

He was an Overlord.

A title granted to any leader of an Inheritance or Clan who had at the very minimum reached Tier 3 Hyperversal Extremity Authority.

There was a heavy responsibility on his shoulders as unlike other Extremities who moved carefree, he had quintillions of Existences to think of!

Recent events had made his heart heavy.

Recent events had made him question.

He had denied joining any of the Seven Clans and Major Inheritances in order to elevate Domains of Shadows, and yet he found himself questioning if he made the right choice!

Now, Vein Rifts pulsed in the distance holding immense opportunities they couldn't even grasp.

And then the first being to enter these Vein Rifts came out to dangle an opportunity of profound proportions as the Shadow Extremity Overlord once more found himself at a crossroads!

Everything that was uttered from the mouth of this existence surrounded by hundreds of these dazzling singularities was not a lie.

And yet...

[What do you gain out of all of this? Why would you freely give off something that makes all of that possible?]

He couldn't help but question this existence surrounded by a light of mystery as at this, he tilted his head calmly.

"Does it matter?"

...!

He didn't deny about any benefits he would receive. And he didn't need to as did it truly matter to them what he received out of all of this when they were in such dire straits and such a profound opportunity lay before their feet?

The gaze of the Shadow Extremity Overlord became stern as he looked around to see his forces gazing at the Singularities around Noah with a greedy light, his voice coming out as he focused on an Apex Aeonic Lifeform in the distance.

[Silas, come forward.]

The Primeval Shadow Elder who connected them all to Noah Osmont.

The one who made an oath with him for the Shadow Sword!

This being stepped away from behind Extremity Laos as the Shadow Extremity Overlord waved his fingers, the Blood Source within his hands rushing over to him as his intentions were clear.

Silas would be the first one to test this.

Surrounded by ever-increasing extracted Blood Sources, Noah smiled as he looked at the being he had an oath to elevate to Extremity.

This Apex Aeonic Lifeform could no longer look at him with the same gaze as before as with complex emotions, he took the extracted Blood Source in front of him and swallowed it!

"..."

THUMP!

He felt the beat of his heart.

And then...for the first time in his life, he experienced Quintessence.

BOOM!

And this Lineage...had blood that flowed with Vitalis Hyperversal Authority.

It had tendrils of the Authority of Dissolution coursing within it!

And it came with shocking elevations as Silas felt his soul and boundaries cry out in euphoria, prompts beginning to flash before his eyes as waves of information emerged in his mind!

[You have devoured the Havenly Quintessential Hyperversal Emperor Lineage Blood Source.]

[Your Aspects of Existence are being transformed as all lesser bloodline records are erased.]

[The level of purity of the Havenly Quintessential Hyperversal Emperor Lineage you have attained is less than 5%.]

But the prompts that came afterwards...caused Silas's figure to tremble as all eyes were on him for what would happen!

"What the hell? This...!"

"How is this possible?!"

BOOM!

A pristine authority radiated from him as he screamed out incoherently while gazing towards the Shadow Extremity Overlord who was using him as a test, his skin gaining a stellar hue as his eyes pulled with multicolored radiance.

"A Lineage! An Ancient Lineage! 10,000% increased rate of progression towards Extremity and past it! An Absolute Dynamis...I can form as many as my soul can handle with a 100% chance of success! Ah...there's more!"

His words were explosive.

The hearts of other Apex Aeonic Lifeforms trembled.

The wills of Hyperversal Extremities wavered as they looked at each other!

A heavily injured Extremity Gabriella who barely kept a sliver of her life after Extremity Ariana's attack felt her heart pulse with life and possibility.

Flashing Shadow and the Shadow Extremity Overlord looked at each other as countless waves of information were exchanged in an instant!

And a moment later...

A scramble began as Extremity Laos, Silas's Master, was the next and first being to move the fastest and appear in front of the extracted Blood Sources that had reached a few thousand around Noah and still continued to increase.

They could currently only see thousands of the extracted Blood Sources.

They didn't know how many this being ahead of them could provide as they didn't want to miss out!

Blood Sources that caused Extremities and Apex Aeonic Lifeforms to flock towards him on all sides in a scene that deserved to be recorded in the Aeonic Annals of Extremity.

The Shadow Extremity Overlord once more had another singularity in his fingers as while staring at the image of Noah with his arms outstretched as tens of thousands more Singularities proliferated out...he moved his hands in what seemed like the heaviest choice he would make in his life.

And then...he devoured the magisterial singularity.

One hundred. One thousand. Ten thousand!

Thousands of existences from the Cult of Endless Shadows devoured the Blood Sources and attained the Havenly Quintessential Hyperversal Emperor Lineage.

Some had already grasped Extremity, some had only arrived up to the 15th Layer of Shadows, and others were at the 17th Layer with Extremity forever out of their reach no matter how much they tried to grasp it these past years.

Out of thousands of the Apex Aeonic Lifeforms, there was one such being among those who devoured a Blood Source and underwent an unbelievable phenomenon at this moment- a phenomenon that never in her wildest dreams would she have thought would come on this day!

She had been stuck at the 17th Boundary Layer of Shadows for the last 10,000 years.

10,000 years, and not a single step forward could be taken!

As if her existence was now more geared to head towards a certain far away prize.

Towards Extremity!

CRACK!
