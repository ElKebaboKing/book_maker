Chapter 1070 - Inconceivable! I

BOOM!

Outside the boundary of the Microbial Universe.

BOOM!

Explosive sounds of utter immensity rang out as a being glimmering white and gold wielded a Cosmic Treasure and released droves of Primordial Essence that actually tore apart a Universal Boundary!

The being carrying out such shocking actions had an exceedingly cold expression at this moment, but when one looked at his eyes while he did such shocking actions...they would be able to see a tinge of pain coursing through with every hit.

As if he truly didnt want to do what he was doing, but he was left with no choice!

His entire life believed he had the purpose of bringing order to these very Cosmos. And yet at this moment, he was ripping one of its Universes apart!

BOOM!

The Primordial Essence had disassembled over 80% of the Universal boundary in the distance of a few miles, the Oathkeeper raising his hands once more to land even stronger attacks to finish everything when...

"What a hypocrite you are!"

WAA!

The essence of Chronos and Extinction bloomed from the inside of the Universe as they seamlessly passed through the now thin boundary that had nearly broken, rushing towards the figure of the Oathkeeper as he simply looked at this coming destructive essence while wrapping himself in Primordial Essence.

BZZZT!

The attacks from the Goliath and the Hegemony of Chronos landed on the vibrant Primordial Essence as they were eaten up, The Oathkeeper staring daggers at Chronos that had begun disparaging his name as soon as he appeared.

"The great protector who does nothing but establish order is actually tearing apart a Universe at this moment...just how far have you fallen?"

...!

An enormous regal purple clock erupted from behind Chronos with these words, the Goliath saying nothing as his chaotic body began to tumble around with essence of Extinction, a shocking illusory image of a planetary sized eye beginning to form behind me as he uttered calmly.

"Primal Eye of Extinction."

WAA!

Maddening essence rushed out as Chronos and The Goliath moved to stop the Oathkeeper from tearing apart the boundaries of the Microbial Universe.

The gaze of the golden glimmering being was still impassive, but as he stared at the two beings he had considered those alongside him, the wrath in his eyes was palpating as at this moment, another crucial junction for the Primordial Cosmos occurred.

Seeing the mocking expression on Chronoss face, seeing the unrecognizable form of the Goliath that was even older than he was...seeing these two beings accompanied with the myriad of recent actions and loss of purpose actually caused the Oathkeeper to snap!

The reserved and calm pillar that stood among the peak of power in the Primordial Cosmos bellowed out at this moment.

"OOOOOOOH!"

With his bellow, the Primordial Essence around him raged as it burned furiously, coalescing together as it formed into a glorious righteous Sword of Primordial Essence.

Pristine white in appearance but carrying such shocking power that even the hearts of Chronos and The Goliath tremble!

"Break for me!"

Oathkeeper bellowed out as if he was giving a command, the pristine white Sword of Primordial Essence thundering down as the thin film of a Universal Boundary remaining for the few miles around him...

CRAAAACK!

Shattered!

BOOM!

As if a bubble had popped.

As if an unbreakable piece of treasure had been defamed.

A Universal Boundary was broken as a being gallantly shining gold and white put his hands forward, grasping onto the pristinely white Sword of Primordial Essence as with its tip in the lead, he plunged into the Microbial Universe!

The figures of Chronos and Goliath were in shock as their bodies glimmered with intense light, their shocking attacks being released to stop this terrifying being from advancing.

The 9 Universes were still under the authority of an Antiquity that repelled any foreign Hegemonies from entering.

At this moment, the Oathkeeper was forcing his way in as with the Sword of Primordial Essence in the lead, he tore apart the pressure that repelled Hegemonies from entering the Universe!

His gaze was locked onto the far away Universal Construct, where even the attacking Chronos and the Goliath that wanted to stop his advance did not enter his eyes.

The gold irises within them shone as they only focused on the far away Universal Construct and the pristine white Sword of Primordial Essence within his hands.

Filled with determination and rage, the monstrous Oathkeeper continued forward!

---

"My turn."

WAA!

The voice was extremely chilling to hear, the Hegemonies watching the unfolding situation adopting solemn looks at this time as among all of them currently on the boundary of the Chthonian Universe, The Great Old Nazzagath was the one to speak.

"These 9 monstrosities...their power is on the border of a Hegemony!"

RUMBLE!

His voice caused the eyes of all others to shake as they looked at each other in affirmation, but one particularly grim Hegemony shook his head as he replied back with an even somber tone.

"The power that a single one of them can release nears a Hegemony. When all 9 collectively release their power on a single being together...that strike should be no less than the might of a newly advanced Hegemony."

"..."

Silence reigned in the surroundings.

The reality set in as all the Universal Experts in the surroundings understood the meaning of the words.

The Apex Paragon in front of them...could actually withstand the force of a Hegemony and not perish.

How wild!

How outlandish!

How inconceivable!

Utter shock and wonder spread out as after such a thought was realized, next up was naturally whether such a feat could be repeated twice or three times.

To be able to stand against a force comparable to a Paragon once was an astonishing feat. But it must have taken everything he had as thereafter...would he really still be able to do the same?!

A great question! And one that wouldnt take too long to be answered....
