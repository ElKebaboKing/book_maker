2631 Unfathomable Depth! I

His conundrum.

Within the Supernal Havens of Extremity, Four Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Lineages were reigning over the masses as when it came to the truly critical Havenly Treasures- one couldn't even near the Archipelagos they were in unless they held the right status!

Noah gazed at the visage of Extremity Laeta before him as he asked lightly.

"Just to what extent do the Four Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Lineages hold sway in the Supernal Havens of Extremity?"

How grand was their position?

How unshakable was their might?

Noah asked simply to grasp a bit more about the structure of these new lands, but the reply was a heavy one as Extremity Laeta smiled brightly.

[They hold sway over everything.]

...!

[Their influence has reached an unfathomable depth within these Havens as they...hold the most Havenly Aurora in their hands. And since Havenly Aurora is the most crucial thing in these Havens, they hold the most power. And this power...is centered around something forged and built by all Four Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Lineages- the Havenly Vaults.]

Something enacted by the most powerful entities whose sheer influence across the Supernal Havens of Extremity was unfathomable!

As she spoke about it, Laeta's voice was calm as if all of this was simply the way of life.

When she spoke about the Havenly Vaults, her feline eyes lit up brightly as she continued with enthusiasm.

[Something you may not know yet is when one collects a great deal of the Units of Havenly Aurora, let's say beginning by exceeding 100,000...your body will start emitting a rich aura of Havenly Authority that actually acts like a beacon for others. It invites Challengers whether you like it or not. If you somehow overcome and accumulate 1,000,000 Units of Havenly Aurora for a pristine Hyperversal Extremity Treasure, even Tier 3 Hyperversal Extremities will come to Challenge you no matter your stage of power as such a number is extremely enticing!]

[So at any point in time while you're working hard to collect tens of thousands or hundreds of thousands of Units of Havenly Aurora, you can be challenged and defeated as you lose everything- years worth of accumulation. This is why a single being can never truly save up millions of Units of Havenly Aurora over their lifetime as the rich aura they automatically and uncontrollably emit will only become more powerful, inviting terrifying Challengers until they lose everything. And this...was where the Four Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Lineages came in. They forged the Havenly Vaults as they allowed the Haven Races...to willingly transfer their Units of Havenly Aurora to be stored and kept safe as in exchange, one is given...a Havenly Note of Extremity.]

As Laeta spoke, she waved her hands to reveal a gold piece of paper.

It was rectangular in shape as it pulsed with the Authorities of multiple Supernal Havens of Extremity, Noah seeing this gold note inscribed with a myriad of runic markings as it held a runic ecriture of [I] at its very center that pulsed with the authorities of all 7 Havens!

A Havenly Note of Extremity!

Laeta waved it in front of her serenely as she continued with a smile.

[A currency. This single Havenly Note in my hand is worth 10,000 Units of Havenly Aurora as this is the method Existences use to amass large units of Havenly Aurora over the years. They store what they accumulate before it reaches alarming levels to emit a rich aura of Havenly Authority and transfer it into the Havenly Vaults run by the Four Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Lineages for a fee, and whenever they have accumulated enough Units of Havenly Aurora for whatever they wish to attain, they exchange all their Havenly Notes for the equivalent Units of Havenly Aurora within the Sacred Crimson Havenly Archipelagos where no Challenges are accepted.]

...!

[That's how the Havenly Vaults came to be as under the control of the Four Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Lineages, they have amassed Billions of Units of Havenly Aurora- charging fees for all who use their services to store and keep their Units of Havenly Aurora and even Havenly Notes safe before they can be utilized. The fees of Units of Havenly Aurora they rack up alone on a daily basis are more than a lifetime's work that hundreds or thousands of Extremities can accomplish as those in the upper echelons of the Four Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Lineages are the wealthiest of Existences. And the wealthiest...are naturally the most powerful as they can access the most glorious Hyperversal Havenly Treasures!]

Noah's eyes radiated brightness as this being before him spoke, with the things she currently said making Noah think of these Havenly Vaults she was mentioning as some form of a banking system utilized by the upper echelons of power in the Supernal Havens of Extremity.

He even scrutinized the paper currency that seemed to be forged airtight and with profound authority as on the four corners of the Havenly Note of Extremity that Laeta had pulled out, Noah saw four symbols that should represent the Four Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Lineages.

It was a fantastical system as at the crux of it though...lay the question of how.

A question that Noah raised to the Extremity before him.

"These Havenly Vaults of Extremity that the Four Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Lineages enacted...what exactly are they? Isn't the transferring of Units of Havenly Aurora done person to person?"

When an existence began exceeding tens of thousands of Units of Havenly Aurora, an uncontrollable scent of Havenly Authority emitted from them that would invite Challenges!

How did the Havenly Vaults remain active and unbothered over the eons and allow the Four Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Lineages to amass the power that they now held?

When arriving at this question, Extremity Laeta became serious as she caused the Havenly Note in front of her to disappear, her words succinct as she stared at Noah closely.

[Because the ones who forged and stand guard over the Havenly Vaults...are those who have undergone Havenly Awakening.]

...!

[For them, an immense authority has been grasped as unlike all of us, the scent of Havenly Authority is able to be controlled and even hidden!]
