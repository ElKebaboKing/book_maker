Chapter 4621: Hello! II

The figure of another powerful Ginnu Lifeform stood beside him.

A being that Noah immediately noted carried a heavy sense of oppression similar to Ulmoreths presence.

If not slightly denser and more profound.

This being looked toward him with calm observation, purple eyes studying him with interest that felt both curious and calculating.

Ulmoreth waved his hand to form an isolated domain of obsidian-gold light around the three of them.

As if he didnt want what he was about to discuss to be overheard by everyone at the festival.

He looked toward Noah and nodded with visible appreciation as he spoke with building enthusiasm.

|It seems THE Surface Depth classification is not far from your grasp at this rate of advancement. I was just telling this old friend here that I have come to a profound decision after watching your performances. To give you my complete support and backing, and to lobby actively on your behalf to others like me across Ginnungagap.|

His voice carried weight of genuine conviction.

|If multiple entities of our caliber throw our full weight behind you, if we combine our resources and authority to support your advancement, then with what you already represent and your mastery of The First Tongue, what would possibly stop you from wielding the same level of power as THE Creature or any of The Others who stand at the apex?|

Ulmoreths words were tremendously heavy.

Noahs eyes shone brightly at the implications.

The full support of multiple THE Fundamental Depth of Absolute Sovereignty entities?

Such a terrifying boost seemed almost too good to be true.

He had been wondering how much longer he would even be able to remain in Ginnungagap.

His timeline was to attain THE classification in the next few hours at the exponential rate that things were currently progressing.

And after grasping that level of power, he could move with genuine confidence to explore THE Vaults of Ginnungagap and claim whatever treasures they held before making his way back to his current time.

If he also had the backing and support of beings like Ulmoreth, entities who stood at the very peak of what Existence produced...

His eyes flashed as his paranoia returned with full overwhelming bloom.

That would mean things continuing to go ridiculously smooth without interruption.

The sense of profound unease in his existence grew even stronger as he looked around cautiously.

He raised his head upward as if literally expecting the fabric of Existence to shatter in the next moment and bring catastrophe crashing down.

When he did this, Ulmoreth smiled wryly at his students excessive caution.

Beside him, the one he had introduced as a friend looked at Noah with growing amusement and spoke with a voice of genuine curiosity.

|After being told of such grand possibilities and offered tremendous support, why is the very first thing you do looking upward with such extreme caution? Most would be celebrating.|

Noah brought his eyes down to focus on this being and Ulmoreth before he replied with calm seriousness.

|Fortune and disaster come hand in hand throughout Existence. There has been so much concentrated fortune falling upon me that my intuition is screaming warnings about potential disaster arriving to balance the scales.|

At these words, Ulmoreth laughed jovially as he clapped Noah on the shoulder.

|Being careful and cautious is genuinely good practice! But this entity here is Mammon, an old friend of mine. As long as you have him as another backer alongside a few others with our Fundamental Depth of Absolute Sovereignty supporting you directly, entities like THE Creature will not be a genuine danger to you in the future!|

After Ulmoreth made this introduction, the being known as Mammon stepped forward with a warm smile.

He raised his hand casually toward Noah in offer of a handshake.

His voice carried friendly warmth as he spoke.

|I go by many names across different eras and regions. Ains knows me by one particular name, Ulmoreth knows me by another. Mammon is a longtime name of mine that Ive grown quite fond of...|

As he said this, Noah looked at this being with close attention.

Toward the outstretched hand, he returned the handshake as their two hands made contact.

While this physical connection occurred, Noah also activated his Lens of Civilization to examine whether this being was veiled like Ains had been or freely displaying their true capabilities.

A status sheet materialized before his vision.

|Analysis Report|

|Classification: ???|

Noah blinked as his eyes became razor sharp.

His mind buzzed with accelerating thoughts as his heart beat faster, and THE Organ of Civilization began to howl!

The Eldest of The Oldest Paradox of Existence?

The Oldest Paradox of Existence referred to the fundamental question of what came first in the origin of reality.

The chicken or the egg. Existence or The First Tongue.

Which preceded which, and how could either exist without the other already being present?

The paradoxical nature of this question gave Paradox itself tremendous power as a concept.

If someone was deemed The Eldest of The Oldest Paradox of Existence, that would mean...

His entire existence howled with alarm as realizations struck at Planck Speed.

|I go by many names, yes. So many names accumulated across eons. Mammon works perfectly well for casual interactions. THE Arbitrium serves for certain purposes. But the name I personally like the most, the one that represents what I truly am...|

HUUM!

Noahs expression transformed to heavy somberness as his entire existence buzzed with alarm.
