Chapter 1287 - Searching For Pandemonium! I

How could [Aura Master] be best described? To Noah, the moment he finished the cast of the skill, he observed one circular aura after another blooming from his origin as they layered themselves around him and overlapped gloriously!

A blue Aura of Serenity, a red Aura of Determination, a green Aura of Defense, a gold Aura of Grace...a myriad of colorful auras surrounded him as he looked like an existence encased in glowing circles of light that released calm waves of their respective essence unto his body.

"Haa..." He breathed in deeply as he felt like all of his cells were screaming in joy- the feeling of this skill and even the essence of Amelioration being something that caused one's very cells to feel a sense of happiness.

The many auras surrounding him undulated with waves of power as he could leave them in this flashy fashion, or he could choose to use his Primordial Eye and make the many auras invisible to others!

He enjoyed the vast array of colors around him as he then tested <Ephemeral Fortress> and <Shield of the Bulwark>, nodding his head with appreciation as he looked at this completed Minor Nomological Edict.

After the design of this Edict, Noah effectively covered himself Offensively, Defensively, and even had multiple support type Edicts!

Now, he would finally spare some of his focus on an Edict that didn't just strengthen him alone, but also strengthened those important to him.

"We'll finally put the features of Blacksmith's Forge to use if all goes well..."

Noah smiled at the thought of the possible next Edict, his two bodies already being surrounded by Resplendent Grand Primordial Hearts again as he went back to reading! At the same time, his clones moved as half of them overtook the important mission of locating the Clusters of Pandemonium hidden across the Ruination Sea where clusters of Primordial Beasts could be seen.

Noah would target these in the next following days before the glorious Writ of Challenge against Stormdust went down as after this event...the forces of the Primordial Empire were going to get extremely busy as things became utterly chaotic!

From Noah being teleported to a cluster of Pandemonium before during an assassination attempt and even the one he observed Stormdust be teleported from to reach the Primordial Sea, he had come gained some exposure to these unique locations as he considered them dens of treasures.

As for how to locate more of them? This was an easy thing to do as since he knew there should be some around the trillions of light years around the Primordial Empire, he just had to silently traverse the vast Ruination Sea around this area while utilizing the Primordial Eye to look through the folds of space until he found what he was looking for.

He could do this...or something even simpler as since he designated and looked at what contained in clusters of Pandemonium as loot, he could use a feature of the Cosmic Core that had sat in the dust for too long.

"Utilize [Treasure Hunter], focus on the aspect of Entities that can give out treasures more than anything else."

Noah spoke out grandly towards the Cosmic Core as he called for the feature of Treasure Hunter to be used!

<Yes, Master.>

THRUM!

Essence coagulated around 28 Clones Noah had deployed across the Ruination Sea, where above each of their heads...a shimmering golden arrow only they could see appeared as this arrow began to shoot towards a certain unknown direction!

The golden light of each of the Clone's left eyes shone as they turned into pure Ruination Essence, turning into crimson blurs of light as they followed these golden arrows towards places that should have things considered treasures for the current Noah.

He could find the clusters of Pandemonium he sought for, or other things entirely!

While his clones did this, his Universal Body appeared in the vast and now nearly endless Infinite Cosmos.

He appeared first in the Novus Universe as he watched his people assimilating their new strength through battles with each other, his voice stretching out towards the Cosmic Core once more.

"Is the new Universe ready?"

<Yes, Master. A Universe with its galaxies built to be the furnaces and engines for the Blacksmith's Forge has been completed.>

One of the many boons of Noah adding new Cosmos into the Infinite Cosmos was the tremendous increase in Vital Essence that could bore new singularities and form new Universes, with many more possibilities opening up as for the future to come, Noah made a request for an entire Universe to be designed into the [Blacksmith's Forge]!

An entire universe for him to build new weapons and armors at the level and power that others could only envy! These new items would also go hand in hand with the new edict that was in the works of being completed- an Edict geared towards this field that should further elevate Noah's power along with his people.

"Let's see it."

His body that shone with colorful incandescent bubbles of auras disappeared before he could disrupt the training of his people, teleporting into a new Universe that if it could be described in one word...it would be scorching!

The structure had the glorious shape of a hammer as it spanned millions of light years in diameter, this being the center of this Universe that was known as the Blacksmith's Forge!

Noah himself didn't have to personally carry out the act of forging as he could designate it to the Cosmic Core that would take his essence and utilize the materials he chose to form the weapons and armor, but Noah liked the feeling of starting out the new series of weapons after all this time himself as he wanted to enjoy the unique process of using the forge.
