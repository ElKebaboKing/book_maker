Chapter 4430: Derivative I

The heavy Symphony continued building in surrounding atmosphere as for the first time in a while, Noahs smile transformed into actual laughter!

"Haha! Oh man, the delicious irony! An unkillable entity perishing! You may become the first of your kind to actually perish...and at hands of creatures you dismissed as imbecilic, no less!"

...!

His words carried tremendous weight mixing revelation with cruel amusement, and THE Dead Orders expression of volcanic rage gradually... calmed.

She sat back even more deliberately upon her throne, forcing composure to return through sheer disciplined will.

Her features settled into careful neutrality as she processed everything.

Eventually, a calculated smile appeared across her face.

"This would represent the only unique situation where such outcome might be theoretically possible, wouldnt it? A singular vulnerability in otherwise absolute defense. But..."

Her smile took on predatory edge.

"What makes you genuinely believe you will be successful? That this siphoning will continue unimpeded until completion? You fundamentally do not comprehend how massive my Civilization truly is...how infinitesimal the amount youre currently taking represents proportionally."

"And her, the one you desecrate Order with through your base physical relationship...she resembles a delicate porcelain container that cannot hold substantial volume at present developmental level. She can easily break, this fragile thing. That would constitute genuine tragedy, wouldnt it? It would be such a shame."

Her voice intensified.

"You would also require time..moh, so much time that you simply do not possess! Me, Paradox, others... just which will reach you first?"

"THE Living Paradox doesnt know about her yet, correct? He would have taken immediate action if he possessed such intelligence. What if I simply share this discovery with him? Enemies of enemies are what again? Haha!"

HUUM!

She turned toward Sigrid with deadly smile and an expression mixing disappointment with cold calculation.

"And you. You are systematically siphoning my Civilizational Authority, yet you have barely articulated single word in this entire interaction. Do you not possess your own standing? Your own independent will?"

Her voice became scathing.

"You permit him to speak and act on your behalf for everything. Did he truly reduce you to merely his plaything? A toy existing solely for his desires and amusement? Can you not aspire to become something more as THE Living Order? He will diminish your light systematically. He will make you feel small and worthless as he appropriates all your brilliance and claims it as his own achievement."

...!

"I have only been made to shine brighter since encountering him. So Order need not concern itself with my wellbeing or autonomy. And he holds my Everything willingly given...you dont need to worry about that arrangement either."

"I will speak when I feel genuine need to contribute. Right now, I have been rather occupied with stealing from you. That requires considerable focus, as you might imagine."

...!

Her words emerged with grand simplicity, and THE Dead Order regarded her as if observing absolute fool...someone who couldnt recognize their own exploitation and diminishment.

Noah looked between both of them as his mind filled with grand possibilities, that devilish smile still present across his features!

"You two truly do not even understand what you have inadvertently touched. That little Symphony of Existence you just manifested? You likely dont even comprehend what that phenomenon actually represents."

"When ones existence becomes unique enough to affect all surrounding reality in supportive resonance...when your Way aligns so perfectly with fundamental principles that Existence itself acknowledges your authority, manifestations occur across spectrum of intensity."

"At the weakest threshold, existence performs modest buzzing and trembling...barely perceptible recognition. Slightly above that level, rhythmic sounds emerge as surrounding reality resonates with your cause, your Way of Existence harmonizing with Existence."

"Those who are truly strong, however..."

"ORDER! SUPREMACY! ORDER IS SUPREMACY!"

HUUM!

...!

Oh!
