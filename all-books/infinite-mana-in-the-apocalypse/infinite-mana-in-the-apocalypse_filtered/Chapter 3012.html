Chapter 3010 Sanctus Populi! II

3010 Sanctus Populi! II

The nine Dragons each shone with a different color, the central one being bright gold and brimming with immense dignity.

Each of them were the size of Planes of Existence, and behind these massive statues of Dragons, clusters of thousands of Planes of Existence could be seen!

Some contained in small points of singularities akin to grains of sand.

Some outwardly expressing their grand stature.

And at the very center of the statues of nine dragons, there was an equally titanic golden arena that looked much more like a Coliseum- and this was the location where an entity exceeding the Teleios Stage had been thrown out of to smash onto one of the dazzling statues of Dragons!

In the surroundings, Entities in the forms of Dragons or Humans were abundant as they all seemed to be observing what was occurring in shock, the identity of the being who was smashed onto the Dragon statue being someone they all had to revere.

Someone of much higher status than some Sanctus Princesses!

Emilliana's sigh became stern as she also realized this, her intent weaving out towards those far ahead to grasp just what had occurred.

Moments later, a Dragon Knight appeared near them as they made their way over towards the massive golden arena, bowing towards Emilliana as he voiced out.

|Sanctus Princess...it seems your younger sister has angered the Elder Queens so much so that Elder Queen Ariana sought to discipline her. But this is the result...|

...!

The rankings within the Sanctus Populi were simple.

While the Imperium had Observers, Knights, and Martial Kings, the Sanctus Populi held Knights who served under the Sanctus Princesses, and the Sanctus Princesses who answered to the Elder Queens!

The power of Elder Queens was utterly terrifying as they were at times equated to the Martial Kings of the Imperium- with all of them having begun influencing or grasping Omniversal Authorities one way or another.

They all had long since exceeded the Teleios Stage as they had stepped on the Teracosm Stage of the Existential Dominium Realm.

Teracosm!

One's Dominium would gain a heavy golden sheen at such a stage as the night they wielded delved more so towards the Omniverse.

And yet...an Elder Queen of the Teracosm Stage currently had her visage imprinted on one of the Nine Sanctus Dragons depicted within this endless stretch of the Sanctum of Dignity.

The culprit...was Sanctus Princess Emilliana's younger sister.

A sister even she had difficulty with as her power was something very few truly understood.

|Unfilial!|

BOOM!

A screech boomed out from the Dragon statue at the center as a beautiful older woman in her prime floated out, blazing golden flames wrapping around a unique Dominium Origin as the Existential Extremity Authority of Dignity burned much too bright around her at this moment.

Those at the Sequor Stage couldn't even handle looking at her as she seemed like a burning sun, only those at the Teleios Stage being able to glance at her figure without feeling their retinas burning!

Noah's eyes brightly locked onto her as he felt a slight burning sensation, but he moreso locked onto the target of this Elder Queen.

A young woman who seemed to share some features with Sanctus Princess Emilliana could be seen at the very center of the vast golden arena, her expression calm and devoid of emotions currently as her beauty far exceeded all others here.

Her head held gorgeous locks of green and gold hair, her nose sharp as her eyes seemed capable of piercing the secrets of the Omniverse themselves.

She had a single white blue sword in her hands that coursed with liquefied Dignity and another Existential Extremity Authority that seemed capable of freezing one's Dominium at a glance!

|You won't take any Dragon Knights under you! You won't take any missions as a Sanctus Princess! What good are you just staying here and wasting away?!|

The roar of the Elder Queen boomed out as all of the surrounding weavings of existence seemed to be supporting her.

Yet the gorgeous young woman on the golden arena didn't pay her any heed, the shocking sense of oppression bearing down on her actually being withstood as if it were nothing...and she flicked her sword up once again.

...!

The horrific power of the Elder Queen pressing down all around was actually cut through with such a simple stroke, existence becoming frozen as the Teracosm Stage Elder Queen was smashed by an unseen force once more, Singularities of frost and utmost coldness exploding around her as she was flung back to the same impression she made before on the dragon statue!

|...|

Silence reigned in the surroundings briefly before a billowing golden mass of power filled with wrath rose up from the indentation of the statue, the power of an enraged Teracosm Stage Entity about to run wild when at such a time...

|Elder Queen Ariana...please lessen you anger.|

The voice of Sanctus Princess Emilliana rang out.

Her arrival was quickly caught by others as they paid respects- the men in the surroundings particularly paying their loyalty by bowing as this was also part of the critical structure of the Sanctus Populi.

Sanctus Princesses. Elder Queens. Sanctus Elder Queen Mother. Sanctus Empress.

All positions were held by female entities as the men supported them as their Knights!

As Sanctus Princess Emilliana was acknowledged, the Elder Queen who was actually getting whooped by her unruly younger sister harrumphed, her power gradually calming as her intent continued.

|Nobody else can talk sense into her...if you fail to do so too, I will fucking get the Empress involved if I have to. All this power, and what does she do with it? Useless!|

With a glimmer of light, the Elder Queen finished her words and disappeared.

In her place was utter silence, as well as the nonchalant and calm figure in the arena holding an icy blue sword.

Noah went on to stare at her as his Dominium buzzed.

Sanctus Princess Emilliana stared at her supposed younger sister as she held her own complex emotions!

Just like her, her sister was also at the Teleios Stage of the Existential Dominium Realm.

And yet...she could freely battle against Teracosm Stage Entities as she was someone famous amongst the Sanctum of Dignity!

Famous as one blessed by the Omniverse.

Famous as one with great destiny, with a power so unique that others could not even compare.

An Omniversal genius.

And yet...she also came with her own bundles and stacks of problems!
