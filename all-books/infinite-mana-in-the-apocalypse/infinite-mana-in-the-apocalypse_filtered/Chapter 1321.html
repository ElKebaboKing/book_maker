Chapter 1319 - A Show Of Invincibility! III

Invincibility.

A word that couldn't be used lightly, and the Champion of Reality did not use it lightly in any way!

He told the three Grand Primordial Beasts that he would show them what this word truly meant, and he wasn't playing when he said so.

BZZT!

The protection of an Edict suffused over the Primordial Beast as even though it couldn't react to Aegon's speed, its base defenses were still there to protect it! Yet...

"ROAR!"

A single word that seemed like a command.

BOOM!

Invincibility. This was a sense of it as he ripped apart a Grand Primordial Beast with an Edict in less than a millisecond! As for the other two beasts? The Champion that would always stand in their way!

All that was left was his figure as he ridiculously wiped out 3 Grand Primordial Beasts with Edicts and the support of a unique authority of a Cosmic Primordial Beast!

This was Invincibility!

Others didn't have to understand how he did it, he would just show them a performance worthy of the Champion of Reality.
