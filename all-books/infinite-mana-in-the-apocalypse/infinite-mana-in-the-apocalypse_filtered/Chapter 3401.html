Chapter 3399 Carnage! I

Within the Megalos Ynnere, a being wondered whether the path he was on was the right one.

Around him, dozens of Omniverses rotated as the complexity required to keep them all miniaturized and in such a state was utterly unfathomable.

Even the strongest of entities with immeasurable complexity of existence experienced doubt, much less those who were weak.

It was general. It was unavoidable.

Across countless weavings.

Outside the scope of the vast Megalos Ynnere, Megalos Beyond, and even Megalos Nihilos.

Within a unique frequency of Existence.

Within an extremely unique Spoke of Existence!

Inside a region of the Expanse filled with a mixture of black and white strings.

There was a shadow of a woman who was sitting on a white string, her hands holding an Omniverse as she seemed utterly engrossed in it.

Her entire figure seemed to be made of a cluster of shadows that existed and didn't exist at the same time, her complexity being utterly frightening as it was not at the level of a Terrestrial Entity nor a Constellation Entity!

Even a Pioneer of Existence that stood above Existence Severing...was still a Constellation Entity.

Above that...was a Source Complexity.

Source!

The complexity of this entity could only be at this stage as, at this moment, another shadow flashed near her.

She didn't even turn towards it as she flipped the weavings of the Omniverse in her hands and asked lightly.

"What is it now? At least tell me it is local."

Her voice was sweet and commanding at the same time, its intent firm and undeniable.

The other shadow was a man with an equally terrifying aura who spoke calmly.

"Sadly not local. A mission of high priority in another Spoke of the Wheel."

"Oh?" The shadow of the woman frowned at such words as she looked up. freewebnovel.com

"Yes. A few kids are planning on carrying out an action in a unique frequency of the Wheel. We just need to ensure it succeeds."

"Oh? Any more details? Any reason why?" Using the same word, the shadow of the woman replied again.

The shadow of the man was still calm as he paused at these questions momentarily before he replied.

"The only details I was given are that a little Pioneer and two Existence Severing Constellations seek to impose the weavings of...THAT Spoke of the Wheel to ensnare a target. No details were given on who they are or why they are doing what they are doing. We just need to ensure they succeed. As for why...we do not question orders. We merely follow. You know this by now."

"..."

The shadow of the woman was briefly silent before it continued.

"Who is the target that even deserves our attention to ensure they end up in that place?"

A heavy question.

Yet the reply was heavier!

"Mission parameters did not include their details either. Nor are we to probe the weavings of the target. "Yes, yes."

The shadow of the woman waved her hands dismissively.

"I know. We only exist to ensure the flow of the Wheel. I just...find it odd that we are supposed to never question. Who are they to say that supporting or denying a certain event in some frequency of the Wheel is what allows the smooth turning of the Wheel to continue? We move covertly to make sure our actions aren't observed by THEM, who also stand to watch and ensure the flow of the Wheel. So really...who is right, and who is wrong? As if she was about to say something truly dangerous and profane.
