Chapter 434 - A Prime World Disappears

The instant the Grandmaster on the Magus World perished, any other Celestials on the Prime World faced similar fates as Noah wordlessly located their positions and took them down.

He was keeping emotions out of it as he moved with a single goal that was extremely time sensitive!

The few powerful beings of the Magus World at the Soul Rank that still remained alive already felt the shocking waves of power spreading out where the Celestial Grandmaster was located, their figures only appearing thereafter as they saw the scene of destruction.

Noah had already teleported back to the inner layer where the core of the Magus World resided, his sight faced with the newly established connection as the Will of the Magus World underwent a similar process to the past worlds. From shock at their boldness to violating universal laws to acceptance as they were now all on the same boat, this process went by quickly as Noah pulled out the compartment containing the World Engine.

"Ready?"

He sent his thoughts towards the world core as the mystifying World Engine began floating in front of Noah, taking a position in the middle of him and the core of the Magus World.

[Lets do it.]

An affirmation came from the core of the Magus World as pure world essence began to leak from it and into the World Engine, Noah simply watching as he did not really need to provide any energy, this planet now connected to the reserves of many and having more than enough! Still, his mana flowed towards the World Engine as the treasure still needed the location of where it was warping this Prime World if they did not want a random movement of the star.

RUMBLE!

His eyes closed as he thought back to the void of space not too far from his home world, the World Engine that was receiving enormous amounts of essence having blue runic lines seamlessly moving across it as it expanded and released a unique energy that began spreading throughout the inner layer of the Magus World and further out!

It was a new type of energy that Noah was not entirely familiar with, only noticing the spatial essence within it as this energy went on to wrap around the entirety of the Magus World, a few quakes spreading out as the inhabitants felt an enormous change occurring.

If a being that could pierce through a [World Veil] and a [Blood World] were to be standing in the void of space watching the Magus World at this very moment, they would notice an eerie spatial energy wrapping around the entire world as it began to look as if it was folding in itself, and then instant later it fully disappeared from the location it was at!

Seamlessly!

Silently!

Powerfully!

An entire Prime World had disappeared out of nowhere, powerful beings only finding out about this shocking information some time later...

On the 7th Celestial Star, the being that oversaw a majority of the operations on this star, Grandmaster Ramiel, shot up from his meditative position as his eyes shone dangerously.

An overabundance of Fate Lines erupted on his body as his eyes shone gold, his usage of <<Herald of Fate>> that was exceedingly done in an expert fashion confirming the shocking revelations in his mind as his face turned sour. He opened his mouth as he released a powerful voice that rolled throughout the 7th Celestial Star.

"A Grandmaster has been murdered in the Magus World! Leave all tasks behind and get there as soon as possible. Be prepared for battle!"

WAA!

A shocking command reverberated in the ears of every Celestial Grandmaster of the 7th Celestial Star as many golden figures of Grandmasters moved without a second of delay, their bodies either teleporting onto huge Warsh.i.p.s that were powered up instantly and began shooting from the shimmering star, or others jumped onto established portals that connected to worlds near the Magus World that the Celestials held sway over.

From the 7th Celestial Star, numerous golden lights shot out as Prime Warsh.i.p.s of varying sizes erupted out with maximum speed, Grandmaster Ramiel being the first one to move the moment he finished his rallying call as he was shockingly moving through the void of space alone and without the use of a Warship, but his speed was multiple times faster as he shot through at speeds that seemed to be surpassing light! In the void of space, ruptures continued to spread out as he shot forward.

This monstrous Grandmaster was shooting through the void of space so speedily that in only 10 minutes, he had traversed from the far away 7th Celestial Star and at the exact location where the Magus World was...or where it used to be!

His face was colored black as he swept his energy all around, turning even darker when he found that where the Magus World used to be, fluctuations of spatial energies were freely flowing, but the world itself was missing. It was missing along with Grandmaster Luo who was stationed here a few days ago with a few disciples.

His gaze became sourer and sourer as golden waves of fate began spreading around the area, his essence utilizing <<Herald of Fate>> even more a monstrous number of fate lines rolled around his aged body. They seemed insurmountable as one could barely make out that their number most definitely passed a million, if not higher!

He utilized the Absolute Skill to try and decipher exactly what had happened here and who was involved, but he shockingly found out that he could not get even a semblance of knowledge on the being involved, nor could he locate anything about their fate.

All that met his peering gaze was a void of darkness, where he also saw spurts of shining light!

His gaze continued to get more and more chilling as the time passed, soon enough sensing the arrival of numerous Prime Warsh.i.p.s and other Celestial Grandmasters as they all surrounded where the Magus World used to be, their eyes almost popping out of their eye sockets as the location where one of them had fallen...was actually missing.

They glanced towards the leading Grandmaster Ramiel as they did not know how to move, because simply, something like this had never happened before!

In the past 1000 years after the War of the Righteous, not a single Grandmaster had fallen to enemy forces, much less assassinated.

So who exactly was bold enough to kill a Grandmaster, and how powerful were they that this entire group of Celestials could not find out anything about them?!
