Chapter 4924: Infinite Harvest! I

Noah set THE Transcendent Paradoxical Inscriptiones Dormientes (Defensive) beside their offensive counterparts on his desk.

Twenty elevated Inscriptions now burned before him, arranged in two groups of ten, their twin radiances illuminating THE Interstices with authority that exceeded anything this space had contained before.

The light they cast was almost warm against his skin, almost like sunlight!

Offense and defense.

Both augmented to levels that transcended their original classifications.

Both capable of affecting beings that would have seemed untouchable before THE Numerical Sovereignty had been integrated into his weavings of THE First Tongue.

After he was done testing this out, Noah sat back on his throne of Absolute Seals. The crystallized authority pressed against his back, solid and reassuring. His blue-gold pen still swirled with Infinities pulsing around its form, still ready to write more Inscriptions if he desired.

The Phonemes had resumed their endless dance around his existence, galaxies of infinite authority that now carried numerical values alongside their semantic meanings.

The void of THE Interstices stretched out around him, patient and immense as when he thought about the capabilities of what he had just designed...

Oh, what he just forged?!

He could not help but lean his head back against the throne.

And then he used his hand to cover his face.

But through the gaps between his fingers, an utterly terrifying devilish smile could be seen rising across his features. His resplendent white teeth showed gloriously as the smile widened, as the implications of what he had just created settled fully into his consciousness. His shoulders shook once, twice, with laughter that he did not permit to escape his throat!

With THE Transcendent Paradoxical Inscriptiones Dormientes, he could erase standard Absolutes, wound THE Undivided Ones and Formless Terrors, and potentially gain a tiny footing even against Primordial Architects.

With their defensive counterparts, he could become nearly invulnerable to attacks from beings across all classifications of existence.

And these were merely his first tests of THE Numerical Sovereignty!

What would happen when he applied these operations to his Harvests? To his Archai of Existence? To his Perpetual Harvest that already claimed resources from enemies across Observable Existence?

The smile behind his hand grew even wider.

THE Genesis Monarch had just done something that Observable Existence was not prepared for, and he was only getting started!

After his tests designing and elevating Inscriptions with Multiplicative Amplification and Exponential Expression, Noah turned his gaze inward.

The Inscriptions were merely the beginning. Weapons and shields were useful, certainly, but they were tools.

What he was about to do was different.

What he was about to do would transform the very nature of how he grew!

Noah straightened on his throne of Absolute Seals, feeling the crystallized authority press against his back as he drew a breath that seemed to pull the scattered energies of THE Interstices into his lungs.

He called out grandly and unequivocally.

"Apply Multiplicative Amplification and Exponential Expression to all incoming harvest from the spread of my Absolute Seals across Observable Existence. Every single thing benefiting, every area covered, every harvest turned into Infinite Potential that further amplifies my existence and foundation..."

His voice dropped to something that pressed against reality itself.

"Multiply it and exponentially elevate it."

BOOM!

The sound was...well, not truly a sound. It was Existence acknowledging that something fundamental had shifted in the relationship between Noah Osmont and the resources that flowed toward him across all of Observable Existence!

He knew this would be one of the heaviest actions he could take. Such a configuration was not merely heavy. It was the kind of thing that would have overwhelmed foundations unprepared for the volume of power about to surge through them.

But he had Infinities!

So he could withstand it! He could pay the price!

Noah felt his Depth and Immensity moving in response to his words.

The sensation was grand. This was not external manipulation of Inscriptions that existed outside his existence. This was internal restructuring, his very being rearranging itself to accommodate the mathematical operations he had imposed upon his harvest. He could feel the numbers taking shape within him, not as abstract concepts but as physical structures that wove themselves through his foundations like new organs being grown.

His Absolute Seals surged around him with intensity.

They had always swirled. They had always danced in configurations that reflected his state of mind and authority. But now they moved with purpose. They flowed outward and inward simultaneously, streaming from his existence in rivers of blue-gold light that curved and bent and folded back upon themselves.

And they formed something.

A massive blue-gold infinity symbol materialized around him, its loops stretching across THE Interstices until they seemed to encompass the entire space here. Noah floated at its center while seated on his throne, the crystallized authority beneath him now simply another part of the greater configuration. The infinity symbol burned with light that pulsed in rhythms matching his heartbeat, and he could feel it not just as a visual phenomenon but as an extension of his own existence.

All incoming harvest flowed into this churning infinity symbol!

The first streams arrived within moments of the configuration completing itself. Harvest from his Absolute Seals spreading across Observable Existence. Harvest from the corrupted paradoxical proto-matter that THE Living Paradox was flooding everywhere, carrying Noahs Infinities alongside his own Information. Harvest from every being who used THE First Tongue and paid the Existential Tariffs he had established.

The harvest flowed into the infinity symbol like rivers joining an endless sea.

And then the Multiplicative Amplification took effect.

Noah gasped as the incoming harvest was multiplied by ten!

The sensation was overwhelming in its immediacy. What had been a steady flow of resources became a torrent, a flood, an ocean of potential that surged through the loops of the infinity symbol with force that made his foundations buzz. He could feel the potency of each unit of harvest increasing tenfold, and simultaneously, the volume multiplied as well. Not just stronger harvest, but more of it, ten times as much crashing against his existence with every passing moment.

The infinity symbol churned with increased intensity, the blue-gold light brightening until Noah had to narrow his eyes against its radiance. The harvest surged along the loops, compressed and concentrated, building toward something that felt inevitable.

And then the Exponential Expression activated.

Harvest multiplied by itself.

And then multiplied by itself again.

And then multiplied by itself a third time!

Harvest times harvest times harvest!

The already-multiplied harvest didnt merely increase in power. It compounded upon itself with mathematical precision that produced results exceeding comprehension. What had been tenfold became something else entirely, something that existed at scales where normal measurement lost meaning.

He watched as the churning harvest within the infinity symbol began to condense.

The streams of raw potential that had been flowing through the loops started to concentrate, to crystallize, to take on form that they had not possessed moments before. The blue-gold light shifted, became something more golden, more defined, more singular in its purpose!

And Noah understood what he was witnessing. He even came up with a name for it!

"THE Infinite Observable Harvest," he breathed.

The name emerged from him without conscious decision, pulled from some deep understanding that recognized what was forming before his eyes!

This was what he felt when he looked at the condensing power. Not mere harvest. Not simple resources being accumulated. Something elevated beyond normal classification through the mathematical operations he had imposed.

A single unit of THE Infinite Observable Harvest completed its formation.

It looked like a golden infinity symbol, smaller than the massive configuration surrounding him but burning with intensity that exceeded its size. The unit floated within the greater infinity symbol for a moment, beautiful and terrible and filled with potential that made his foundations ache with anticipation.

Then it flowed into him.

BOOM!
