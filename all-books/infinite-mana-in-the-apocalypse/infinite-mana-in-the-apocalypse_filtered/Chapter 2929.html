Chapter 2927 Jumper! I

Before the distinction of those behind the Dikastes of Extremity, Noah only gazed coldly as he calculated many things!

Noah felt like he was just stepping on the cusp of these organizational forces at this moment, and he had to proceed carefully to make sure that he didn't alert any Entities that he could not handle yet.

But he also had to be ready.

And understanding what lay in front of him, as well as being capable of fully countering it...would be ideal.

So he gazed at the half devoured Vitalis Dikastes of Extremity as his eyes flared with brilliance!

And his extremes answered.

{Quintessence states that nothing can fall outside the scope of its understanding.}

{The Extremities of Loot, Devouring, and Greed state that they have made sufficient gains from breaking down the body of the current Dikastes of Extremity!}

...!

The replication of the Dikastes of Extremity they were seeing before them- the reconstruction of something that was likely a product traded between Legacies to take care of Existential Domains!

{Cheats smiles with mischief and initiates the start of Project Existential Automaton.}

WAA!

Project Existential Automaton!

It was a grand undertaking as before Noah's eyes, a cerulean blueprint of the massive visage of a Dikastes of Extremity formed- this being all the parts that were just devoured and broken down.

As the rest of this body was devoured, this blueprint would be even more complete as thereafter, the results of this recently enacted project would be something that others would be dumbfounded to even look at.

His extremes moved with their own weavings as they decided on the materials, the core, and the very weaving of something new- Project Existential Automaton kicking off hours after Noah's entry into the Gradations of Extremity.

At the core of such an Automaton...

His extremes buzzed as a moment later, a Colorless light bathed the blueprint.

Quintessence.

From what Noah could tell, the constructs of the Dikastes of Extremity began in a neutral state before they were influenced to match the Existential Domains they would be residing in.

The most appropriate core material for such a construct for him...would no doubt have to be Quintessence.

Other ingredients and materials would soon follow as Project Existential Automaton kicked off with a flurry of Existential Extremity Authorities!

After affirming this, Noah's eyes blazed with brilliance once more as he called out.

{Existential Proofreader.}

HUUM!

HUUM!
