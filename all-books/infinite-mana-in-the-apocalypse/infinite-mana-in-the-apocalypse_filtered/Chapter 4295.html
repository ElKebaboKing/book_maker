Then, they incorporated their Principles, and the effects and capabilities of both the Living Existential Armors and the Astral Arcana Glyphs became unfathomably grander!

Noahs eyes kept their tyrannical luminosity.

"All of these different systems of existence are ones that either THE Living Existences formed for Early Creatures, or simply the innate powers of Early Creatures. Whether it be the Astral Arcana Glyphs, Justiciars, Glyphs, or Haki..."

"All of it was made and designed by others."

A new, more profound, and utterly tyrannical thought took root.

He was not just a user of systems. He was a creator. His Way of Existence was not to follow, but to forge.

He did not have to utilize these strict means and systems of existence of others. He could use their skeletons, their backbones, as mere references to make his own, grander system of existence!

HUUM!

He watched the war, at how, no matter what system was being used, all of them were being strengthened by Principles.

A system that incorporated the burst potential of the Astral Arcana Glyphs, the sustained power of Haki, the symbiotic growth of a Living Existential Armor... and he would bring it all together under the grand, glorious, and absolute light of his own Principles.
