Chapter 3051 Greater I

History will tend to repeat itself.

If she could alter them, she could.

If she could change the nature of the Imperium, she would.

But she was bound by too great a purpose.

A certain Dragon Knight Emperor of the Sanctus Empress.

Another Martial Emperor similar to her in the Imperium.

'Who are you?'
