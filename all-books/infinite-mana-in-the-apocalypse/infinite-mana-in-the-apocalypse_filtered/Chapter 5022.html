Chapter 5020: Euphoria I

Noah breathed in and out as he came to realize a problem.

Every time he breathed, every time his existence continued endlessly generating Quintessence Infiniforce, that feeling of euphoria that had built up until he almost brought his hands together to unintentionally cause a Miniature Cause was returning. It crept through his foundations with warmth that made his cells sing and his awareness sharpen to edges that cut against perception itself.

It was as if his existence naturally entered that profound state of euphoria the more Quintessence Infiniforce he generated.

While it was not bad per se, Noah could not quite put his finger on exactly what it meant. Was this THE Gamaidjan that Naldine had warned him about? Was this the madness of Infinity creeping into his consciousness through internal generation rather than external conduction?

Or was this simply what it felt like to be a finite existence producing infinite force, the natural high of something unprecedented occurring within his foundations?

He was not averse to this euphoria.

It caused his existence to buzz in an always ready state, prepared for action at any moment, eager to demonstrate what Quintessence Infiniforce could accomplish when properly directed. The sensation was addictive in ways that his cautious mind recognized as potentially dangerous, but his tyrannical nature embraced as simply the feeling of power that belonged to him.

To the extent that in the next moment, when Naldine raised her hand slightly as if pointing to existence itself, Noah felt ready for absolutely anything.

"Freeze."

HUUM!

A single word from the Second Scale Primordial Architect.

But that single word carried weight that pressed against reality itself with authority accumulated across eons of cultivation. Her Civilization of Limitless Existence declared that this moment would not end, that time would not progress, that everything within her domain would remain suspended in an instant that had no duration.

The color white permeated over everything.

It spread from Naldines extended hand like frost across glass, racing outward to touch every surface and every existence within The Primordial Genesis Shore. Ginnu Lifeforms froze mid-motion, their expressions locked in whatever emotion they had been displaying when the effect reached them. THE Living Temporals past body became still, purple rivers of time crystallizing into motionless streams. THE Primordial Chaos himself froze with expression of frustrated amusement locked onto his features, his chaotic authority suspended in configurations that should have been impossible to halt.

All bodies became frozen in that Limitless Instant.

All except one.

In the far distance, the multicolored flames surrounding THE Creature were about to be frozen when they blazed brighter with defiance that exceeded even Naldines Second Scale authority. The massive titanic form remained in motion while everything else became still, those burning eyes looking down at the scene below with awareness!

Noah affirmed this with shining eyes as he moved.

He appeared instantly before the invisible figure of THE Secretive Eon, crossing distance that should have required measurable time in a moment that existed outside of time entirely. She stood frozen like everything else, her dark hair cascading around features that held natural beauty and grace despite the weight of what she would eventually become.

Her dark eyes were locked on the distant confrontation between THE Primordial Chaos and THE Living Temporal, unaware that she was being observed by someone who knew exactly what infection would be within her foundations eons from now!

Noah pushed his hand forward.

It held a vibrant multicolored singularity filled with Quintessence Infiniforce, internally generated authority compressed into a point of density that exceeded what most beings could produce through any methodology. The singularity sank into her existence silently, passing through her frozen form without resistance, embedding itself deep within foundations that did not yet know they would need restoration.

A seed planted in the past that would bloom in the future.

He then looked over to the other direction where Temporal stood close to THE Primordial Chaos, the past self frozen in proximity to the apex entity who had been grasping Ulmoreth moments before. Noah pointed toward Temporal as vibrant blue weavings of his conducted Infinity flowed from the frozen Absolute, forming a dazzling singularity that floated through the air with purpose that defied the frozen state of everything around it.

The singularity entered the body of THE Primordial Chaos without disturbing his frozen form.

Both singularities now burned with Infinities within their hosts, albeit two different types. The multicolored Quintessence Infiniforce within THE Secretive Eon. The blue conducted Infinity within THE Primordial Chaos. These Infinities could easily mimic the authority of their hosts Civilizations and remain dormant for eons, undetectable until the moment Noah chose to activate them.

Until he completed this attack in the present by awakening seeds planted in the past.

Naldine looked over in his direction as if to confirm he was done, her hooded form barely perceptible even to his enhanced awareness. He nodded with bright eyes, mission accomplished, objectives achieved, another closed loop in the making.

But that euphoria from endless generation continued building within him.

It demanded release. It wanted to flourish. It pressed against his finite foundations with force that felt irresistible, similar to how his hands had been drawn together before Naldine stopped him from causing a Miniature Cause.

He wanted to unleash this euphoria.

He wanted to allow Quintessence Infiniforce to flourish and demonstrate what it could do when properly expressed.

But how?

His eyes were bright with possibilities as Naldine sent him a message through their established connection.

"Alright, let us go."

She was saying this.

She was right to say this.

They had accomplished their objectives. The temporal traversal could end now. This could be the shortest journey through time he had ever undertaken, a surgical strike that planted seeds without disturbing the soil of history.

But Noah continued to feel that budding euphoria building within his existence.

With bright eyes shining beneath his disguise as THE Tongue Whisperer, he looked toward THE Creature in the distant skies. That massive titanic form surrounded by multicolored flames, the only existence besides themselves that remained unfrozen in this Limitless Instant.

"Everything that happened in the past has already happened."

His eyes shone with multicolored light of Quintessence Infiniforce as he began to rise from his position, ascending toward the skies with purpose that had nothing to do with their mission objectives.

Naldine frowned as she sensed what he was about to do.

Before she could say anything, before she could warn him about destabilizing temporal weavings or causing unpredictable results, Noah listened to his euphoria. He looked toward the distant figure of THE Creature with eyes blazing and smile spreading across his disguised features.

"Hey! Can we spar?!"

HUUM!

Incredulous words echoed from him across the frozen landscape of The Primordial Genesis Shore.

THE Creature looked down from his position in the skies, those burning eyes analyzing the figure rising toward him. A young existence challenging an apex entity whose passive pressure alone could collapse weaker beings into unconsciousness.

After an instant of consideration, THE Creature gave a light nod.

Noah smiled brilliantly and laughed with genuine delight.

The endlessly generating Quintessence Infiniforce churned within him even more wildly as he shot upward like a bullet, his disguised form blazing with multicolored light that pressed against the frozen reality around them!

Naldines expression became colder beneath her hood as she watched this young lifeform ascend. At this time, the effect of freezing everything ended, her Civilization of Limitless Existence releasing its hold on this moment as continuing to maintain it while Noah engaged THE Creature would have been pointless.

The Limitless Instant concluded.

Time resumed.

And to the perspectives of all others present on The Primordial Genesis Shore...

BOOM!

An entity with multicolored brilliance instantly emerged in the skies, shooting out with a heartshaking aura toward THE Creature himself. The frozen moment they had not known they were experiencing simply became a moment like any other, and suddenly there was something new happening that demanded their attention.

Ginnu Lifeforms across the settlement looked upward with shock and confusion.

THE Primordial Chaos turned his gaze skyward with expression shifting from frustrated amusement to genuine curiosity.

THE Living Temporals past self looked up with awareness that his future consciousness still occupied, watching events unfold that he now understood had always been part of this moment.

THE Living Origin and THE First Hunger, who had been rushing forward moments before, paused to observe the new development with expressions of complete bewilderment.

Many focused on the ascending figures form, on the wings unfurled behind his back, on the letters of THE First Tongue orbiting his existence in patterns that declared ancient authority. Recognition sparked across multiple faces simultaneously.

A Fundamental Depth Ginnu Lifeform voiced out with shock that pressed against his words.

"Is that... THE Tongue Whisperer?!"

...!

THE Tongue Whisperer had appeared again after so long, that mysterious entity who had been immortalized in THE Agora of Primordial Judgment as the one who reached the level of Polemarch as soon as he entered. The being whose origins remained unknown, whose power exceeded his apparent classification, whose presence here made no sense given the circumstances.

And astonishingly, his brilliance smashed toward THE Creature with intent that bordered on madness!
