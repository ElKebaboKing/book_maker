Chapter 410 - Dual Cultivation

But it was only by this much, as he observed himself getting a few hundred more Chaos Particles before the atmospheric converging chaos began to stall and reduce. He was about to accept this result when he thought of Borus words on how they increased their Fate Lines rapidly through defeating Chaos Behemoths and using their materials.

Through using their body parts and purifying the enormous amounts of chaos entrenched in their bodies, one would be able to gain Fate Lines much faster!

This thought crossed Noahs mind as he chose to become bold briefly, testing the waters and this theory as while {Disciple of Chaos} was initiated, he also activated {Fates Disciple}!

If this same technique could be applied to Fate Lines, it meant he could possibly catch up and even pass a being like Grandmaster Vredral in the span of a month!
