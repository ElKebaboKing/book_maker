Chapter 2822 Eons of Change! II

2822 Eons of Change! II

Multiple theories flowed in the mind of Seer Amestris as she didn't know the right answer.

Whether it was just a reward they received, or whether there were many strings attached to it...did not matter.

[We have received a Havenly Mandate...a Mandate! Our thoughts are not wrong!]

Her voice echoed out grandly as beside her, the figure of the Avernus Extremity Overlord felt the overflowing power at the Fifth Gradation of Extremity that continued to rise as his pupils radiated purple white flames of majesty and regality.

[Seven Nether Havens...one Avernus Clan!] freewebnovel.com

WAA!

An unexpected elevation bloomed in this region and continued, the landscape of the Seven Havens about to change greatly due to it!

Away from this region, in the Supernal Havens of Extremity within the Extremity Vein.

In the skies where the Obsidian Saurischian Archipelago used to be.

A visage of a shockingly beautiful woman filled with a sense of wildness and craziness could be seen, Tendrils of Lunacy vibrating around her as she was starting at the destruction of the land she was near of when it was smited out of existence.

But now, she stared above as thick snake like tendrils of milky white Vitalis Hyperversal Authority cascaded down and came to bathe her body, her unstable realm at the Fourth Gradation of Extremity that she had just stepped into beginning to rise!

She didn't understand why.

But she didn't have to.

She held her obsession with the man who had infected her soul with Virions that nearly cost her life.

Inside Lunacy, this obsession didn't die.

It also meant that Noah Osmont...did not die!

After hearing his adulations and Honorariums of his name ring out across the Vitalis Hyperversal Domains, her pride and honor had been shaken as Lunacy went even crazier at the fact that she may not be powerful enough to take care of her obsession!

But now...

HUUM!

Tendrils of white grandeur surrounded her as Polychrome Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Rune Scriptures of VITALIS bloomed in her soul, Awakenings beginning to unfold one after another as she went towards a grand destination.

[Ah, Little Osmont...I know you're still alive. Come out, come out!]

Maddening waves of Lunacy echoed out as even more changes occured here!

In another region.

In the isolated domain where the Bard of Vitalis used to be, and where dozens of terrifying Entities at the Sixth Gradation of Extremity or higher had appeared momentarily Milliseconds ago.

The Main Body of the Prime Devourer stood alone in this region, still in shock at how things had played out as one way or another...he had actually succeeded in his goal.

But in his puzzlement and wonder...

BOOM...CRACK!

His soul shuddered as he felt the appearance of an obsidian cluster of unidentifiable Rune Scriptures above, followed by tendrils of pristine obsidian light smashing into him as....

He blinked in shock and alarm as he felt his stagnant progression at the Fifth Gradation of Extremity shoot up indiscriminately, coming to arrive at the uncrossable gates of the Sixth Gradation of Extremity as in the milliseconds that followed...the gates holding him back from a shocking power began to break!

...!

It was a shocking scene reminiscent of what was occuring in the Vitalis Hyperversal Domains.

It was hard to accurately say why it was happening, or what the True intent for those behind these actions were.

But undoubtedly, it ushered in an era of change as after all of this, things would never be the same.

Far away from the Vitalis Hyperversal Domains, an even grander scene was playing out within the Vacuous Hyperversal Domains.

In a corner of the Haven of Perdition.

Seas of obsidian Perdition rose and fell chaotically as the aura of Vacuous Hyperversal Authority was particularly potent, the whole Haven seeming like a domain devoid of reality and time.

In a space surrounded by tendrils of Perdition.

Everything moving in its chaotic manner when suddenly....

SHAA!

Tendrils of deeply cerulean light suddenly emerged- these tendrils of light covered by a silent power and authority as within moments, it transformed into an illusory figure of a handsome young emperor.

He gazed around carefully with eyes burning with tendrils of blue light as he spoke with a hint of caution and serenity.

[Are you able to feel me?]

[...]

His gaze turned up as if he was speaking to the space around him, and yet there was no answer.

The blue light within his eyes became even more concentrated as the longer he waited and heard no reply, his lips began to curve into a devilish smile as he was ever so certain...that he had succeeded!

[Good...you came for me, so I'll be coming for you now.]

WAA!

With a flicker of cerulean grandeur, the illusory figure of the Great Usurper disappeared- almost as if he were melding into the reality around him.

Rumors and whispers of his death were greatly exaggerated.

He returned from a perilous point in his life once more as this time, he would ensure such a type of peril never presented itself again!

Inside the Infinite Hyperversal Haven.

The Cimmerian Architect felt the new Existential Extremity Authority flowing within her as she was comparing it to Vacuous Hyperversal Authority.

Vacuous always held a cold regality and power as with it, one could power and expand upon the state of non-existence, non-reality, and the absence of time and space.

When she felt the endless blue light of Infinity coursing through her...she could sense an overabundance of existence.

Existence that seemed to be coursing endlessly as with it, she could sense the possibilities of endless time and space, of possible realities that stretched out with no end.

It gave her the illusion that she could continue following Infinity endlessly, that even if she somehow reached the Ninth Gradation of Extremity...she could still go further on an endless path of power!

[Ha....]

She let out a light gasp and came back to herself as she stepped out of the stage of rich Infinity.

The feeling she just had felt like an illusion, and yet real at the same time as it caused the Cimmerian Architect to compare Infinity to Vacuous Hyperversal Authority!

If Infinity was elevated greatly enough...she felt that it may actually be possible for it to overcome Vacuous Hyperversal Authority!

...!

This possibility alarmed her as before she could think any further...

WAP!

The space around her warped as with a cerulean flash, she disappeared.

When she came to again, the scene around her was a glorious one as she was surrounded by intensely burning authorities that caused her heart to palpitate.

Her eyes flashed as she turned her gaze ahead where she felt a heart shaking aura, expecting to see the visage of the man responsible for all this.

A visage of Noah Osmont.

And yet...

Her pupils dilated at the scene of a woman covered by waves of multicolored light similar to the massive Havens she had seen in the domain she was just in.

She sat on a glorious blue throne taking the form of an Infinity symbol, with her gaze cold and callous as if she never held a trace of emotion in all her life!

This...was not Noah Osmont.
