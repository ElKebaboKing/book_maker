Chapter 4360: The Farmers Greeting V

The words buzzed between them.

Noah continued staring at the tree ahead, his expression thoughtful as he processed everything shed said.

Then, he smiled with genuine curiosity.

"What is wrong with all of us becoming The Dead?" he asked.

"We could all survive being collapsed and return again a day later. Sounds like elegant solution to surviving The Fallout, doesnt it?"

Elyndra blinked, her expression cycling through confusion before settling into something resembling disbelief.

She studied his features, trying to determine if he was joking or if he genuinely didnt know.

"You do know," she said slowly, carefully, as if explaining fundamental truth to someone who somehow missed it, "that the Curse of The Dead, and anything to do with THE Creature, means that all Dead Existences are completely cut off from ever reaching the next Scale of Existence, right?"

Her voice grew more emphatic.

"That was the cost for such a terrifying capability. THE Creature granted The Dead the ability to return from collapse, to survive what should be absolute ending, to persist through conditions that would unmake Living Existences. But in exchange, they can never advance beyond their current Scale. They are trapped, forever, at whatever level they possessed when they crossed The Veil."

She paused, making sure he absorbed the information.

"Becoming The Dead isnt just transformation...its permanent limitation. A ceiling that can never be exceeded. An end to all potential growth in exchange for impossible survival."

...!

Noahs eyes turned immensely sharp at this revelation.

He looked at Elyndra up and down.

But her expression carried no deception...just certainty born from knowledge that she considered fundamental truth of existence.

The Dead couldnt reach the next Scale of Existence.

They were trapped, forever, at whatever level theyd achieved before crossing The Veil.

Which meant that if he became Dead, if he crossed to the other side, if he chose survival through that method...

He would never reach THE.

Never advance beyond his current Scale, no matter how much power he accumulated, no matter how many Principles he integrated, no matter what achievements his Way produced.

It would be ceiling disguised as salvation, trap baited with immortality, permanent limitation masquerading as ultimate survival technique.

What of his Arch Lich though? Did his type of Death align with THE Creatures Curse? Or was he purer?

Noah simply stared out toward the tree silently, his thoughts racing through implications!

So that probability stream RUINEDEN had shown him...the one where THE Dead Order captured him and turned him into battery for her Civilization, wasnt just horrifying because of the loss of freedom.

It was catastrophic because it would permanently cap his advancement. Would lock him into current Scale of Existence forever, removing any possibility of reaching heights he was apparently already walking toward.

The Dead couldnt reach THE.

Which meant THE Dead Order couldnt either, despite her immense power. She wasnt fully...THE...Unless she unified with Sigrid!

She was trapped on that side of The Veil, unable to cross over or advance further, limited by the very method that had allowed her to survive when THE Living Order had collapsed.

Once someone crossed The Veil and became Dead, they were no longer competition. They were permanently locked out of ever challenging THE Living Existences positions.

Was that why Khor and others were potentially collapsed?

Elyndra took his silence as confirmation that hed been joking, her expression relaxing slightly as she sighed.

"So," she said, returning to her earlier question, "do you think a collaboration is possible? With your Perpetual Harvest, cultivating what even father could not bloom, we could accomplish goals that many outside THE Loom seek to accomplish."

She paused, her blue eyes finding his with intensity that carried genuine hope mixed with determination.

"As I said before," she continued, her voice softening, "I would like to be your friend."

...!

Noah looked at the figure of Elyndra...at her astonishing beauty, her nine converging Principles, her genuine desire for collaboration mixed with hidden agenda he suspected but couldnt confirm.

Then his gaze shifted to the distant figure of The First Farmer, who was gazing at groups of countless beings in his sanctuary with expression that seemed calm and benevolent.

Finally, Noah smiled.

"Collaboration is possible," he said simply, his voice carrying certainty. "And I would like to see the weapons of The First Farmer. If were going to work together, I should understand what resources are available."

Elyndras smile blazed brilliant enough to make existence in the entire area seem a shade brighter, her blue eyes shining with joy.

She stretched out her hand toward Noah, her thumb and two fingers raised in gesture he didnt recognize.

Noah raised his eyebrows. "What are you doing?"

Elyndra blinked, then turned her head slightly, confusion flickering across her features.

"Oh," she said, realization dawning. "You dont know the Farmers Greeting? Its a gesture- three fingers waved at a distance, or connecting like a handshake when up close."

She smiled, moving closer.

"Its akin to blessing and wishing a good harvest and prosperity to the other party. Here, let me show you."

She motioned to Noahs hands, and he brought his thumb, index finger, and middle finger forward as shed demonstrated.

Their fingertips touched...three points of contact forming triangle of connection between them.

Elyndras smile became radiant, her blue eyes shining brilliantly with genuine warmth.

In that moment, she seemed to have forgotten the heavy task laid upon her by her father, forgotten the conflict between duty and principle, forgotten everything except the simple joy of teaching someone about her cultures traditions.

"There you go," she said, her voice carrying delight. "Your first Farmers Greeting. I wish you boundless harvest and prosperity, Friend."

...!

Noah looked at this astonishingly gorgeous being, at her vibrant presence, her genuine warmth...and calmly smiled.

"And a boundless harvest to you as well," he said coolly.

Their fingers remained connected for moment longer than strictly necessary, the gesture lingering in space between them.

Then they separated, the moment passing as Elyndra began explaining details about the Sanctuarys weapons and resources with enthusiasm.

---

Noahs consciousness shifted, pulling away from the Wandering Territories, from the golden tree and Elyndras complicated presence, returning to his Early Veiled Shore where something magnificent was reaching completion.

He stood on the golden sands, his form perfectly still as he observed the final stages of construction happening in spaces that existed simultaneously within and beyond normal reality.

The Incarnaut Sanctis.

The Early Chamber of Blood.

The space where genesis of new Early Creatures would occur, where beings of sufficient potential could undergo transformation that would elevate them to tier of existence that current reality insisted was closed.

He thought about his interaction with Elyndra, about the collaboration she proposed.

In his mind, RUIN/EDENs voice emerged with tones that carried amusement mixed with professional assessment.

"The Daughter of The First Farmer might pounce on you before you pounce on her. Masters charms are unbeaten as always."

Noah shook his head at such words.

"Stop playing around," he said, his voice carrying command wrapped in fondness. "Finish Incarnaut Sanctis, the Chamber of Blood. Let us bring the newest Mana-Born Early Creatures to fruition."

He paused, his eyes blazing with tyrannical light.

"Let us usher in...The Glorious Evolution."

HUUM!

RUINEDENs response was singular, her voice carrying certainty that transcended artificial origin.

"Yes, Master."

And on the golden sands of the Early Veiled Shore, the Chamber completed its formation with pulse of authority that announced to existence itself that something new and impossible had just achieved manifestation.

The genesis was ready to begin!
