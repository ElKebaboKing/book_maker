Chapter 2984 Buying and Selling! III

Regions that held Unstable Anomalous Existential Domains were extremely dangerous to delve into as one would not normally use themselves, or in the case of an Imperium Royal, they wouldn't even send their Knights out first.

This was because eons of experiences showed how there could be Existential Distortion in regions such as these that could warp someone into distant foreign spaces, with the weavings shared by the Dominiums of some Imperium Royals before they died showing them being warped into the hunting grounds of Omniversal Pillagers, regions under the domains of other forces, or even areas where Existential Extremity Authorities were nullified!

If such a thing occurred, one could only wait for death.

So when it came to exploring any of these regions, many Imperium Royals would have constructs pave the way for the exploration, and they would map out the dangers of an entire Unstable Anomalous Existential Domain before sending out their Knights or Attendants if need be to collect unique treasures!

|Do I really have to purchase Existential Dominium Constructs? The profits from this whole venture will be greatly reduced after that...|

As he entered the Existential Imperium Exchange and began to sort through the countless items listed there, the golden haired Imperium Royal sighed while questioning his next moves.

His hair shone with a golden lister as his body was round and filled with fat, but his face still gave off a sense of profound beauty and power as he stood at the Sequor Stage of Existential Dominium!

He was known as Imperium Royal Decimara!

The two Knights he was granted from his Enclave were of the same stage, with barely a third of the Attendants having achieved the Neonate Stage as the powers he controlled were very little, and yet he was trying to eat up an Unstable Anomalous Existential Domain that he discovered before having to report it.

His will rapidly perused thousands of products every second as he looked for alternative solutions.

|Come on...any junk that can at least withstand a minute before being destroyed. If I can just map and confirm the safety of the entrance ahead, I can attain droves of Fruits of Existence!|

A sense of urgency was welling up within as he looked through the affordable constructs below the Existential Dominium Realm, and as thousands passed before his eyes...

|Huh?|

He stopped his scrolling due to something that stood out.

Among the listed constructs, many had prices of ? or even ? a Fruit of Existence, and yet there was actually a Construct listed with a cost of 3 Fruits of Existence!

|Hah? Who the hell is bold? What kind of things would you list for such a price? Unless it shits existential gold...|

His curiosity got the better of him as he clicked on the listing, its details flowing out before him.

{Unbound Automaton Worker}:: 3 Fruits of Existence. Peak Gradation of Extremity, approaching Neonate Stage of the Existential Dominium Realm in power. Useful in the management of Existential Domains, or as fodders of exploration, this Unbound Automaton Worker was produced by the Infernal Enclave under the direct supervision of the Weaver of Existence...

...!

Approaching the Neonate Stage of the Existential Dominium Realm?

These words were extremely bold as the demarcation of constructs between the Gradations of Extremity and the Dominium Realm was an uncrossable one.

For any Entity to say they were about to bridge this with their constructs...

|Hmm....|

Decimara had a pondering gaze as he knew anything listed under the Existential Imperium Exchange underwent review, and it was hard to push out defective or false products.

He could either continue buying multiple more Dikastes Workers for a fraction of Fruits of Existence, or he could take a risk and buy a single one of these...Unbound Automaton Workers.

As for whether its power truly approached the Neonate Stage of the Existential Dominium Realm? This would be the risk he had to take!

|Bah, fuck it. Come on!|

He clicked on the outline of the golden blue figure and paid the price of three Fruits of Extremity, the space in front of him buzzing as a runic magic circle of immense spatial authority bloomed!

And a moment later...

SHAA!

A glorious muscular Automaton revealed itself before him.

|Ho...|

He whistled at its visage as it was bathed in a glorious gold and blue light.

A pair of blue wings stretched out behind it as the moment it appeared, its eyes blazed with nine pupils of glorious light as Decimara nodded his head.

|Nice new design...the Infernal Enclave, is it? Let's see how your product is.|

He waved his hands to take this Unbound Automaton Worker outside, a Fruit of Existence flowing from him and towards its chest as this Automaton absorbed its light and came to life right away.

It appeared inside his Vessel as his attendants and Knights gazed at it with question marks, wondering what their Master had wasted resources on now!

But Decimara paid no heed to any gazes as his Existential Extremity Authority flowed outside of his body and into this Unbound Automaton Worker right away...instantly changing its blank nature as with his eyes, he saw it take on the brilliant purple hue of his Existential Extremity Authority.

The very Extremity of Royalty!

|Good...let's go.|

His eyes flashed with a purple light as the vision of the Bound Automaton Worker became his, its figure that was standing tall pulsing with light a moment later as it was warped outside of the Vessel.

Violet Tendrils of light smashed down all around as the moment the Bound Automaton Worker appeared, its massive muscular body weaved left and right to evade them!

|Oh?|

The eyes of Imperium Royal Decimara lit up as he could see the clouded violet storm ahead and the unstable mirrored planes of existence cracking nearby, his Intent going towards the Bound Automaton Worker to delve deeper into the violet cloud to see what lay ahead.

Yet a second later...

BOOM!

A heavy tendril of Violet light with existential authority smashed onto the gold and blue Automaton as it wasn't able to evade it in time, causing the gaze of Decimara to flicker towards anger as he expected another purchase to be destroyed!

But...

|Oh...?|

When the violet light faded, the Automaton could be seen...uninjured as tendrils of violet existential light covered its body with its muscles spasming, but it seemed fine.

It even looked like the violet light was absorbed into its body before it disappeared into the weavings of its authorities.

Then, unhindered, it continued.

|Haha! Good Automaton! From the Infernal Enclave eh? Very well!|

With a sense of excitement, the Imperium Royal forged forward.

At the same time.

Unbeknownst to him.

Within the supposedly 'Bound' Automaton Worker, a brilliant colorless light of Quintessence arose!
