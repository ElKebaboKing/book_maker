The Queen of Matter.

In a region of Malformed Megalos of Existence, dangerous layers mirror like shards stuck out all around as even a Lesser Source would be skewered by these surroundings!

Yet her body was covered by a unique hue of power that protected not just her weavings, but the weavings of the books she carried.

Books attached to golden strings of existence or held tightly in her hands.

Such a being had spoken of many shocking things, but the latest one related to Bob!

"Bob?"

Noahs eyes flashed dangerously as he asked.

"Bob!" And the Queen of Matter nodded multiple times over seriously as she affirmed!

Noah gazed at the flickering singularity she had come through as when it lingered there for just a few seconds, the Queen of Matter became shocked while shaking her head as she stood between him and its line of sight.

"Woah, your next thought isnt, Since Bob is in those weavings of Existence, then I will merely go to those weavings of Existence! right? Right???"

She spoke once more in a manner as if she knew him.

An action that only made Noahs eyes flash at her with more and more coldness as he replied while moving towards the singularity she came from.

"Stay here."

BZZT!

The weavings of a grandiose Regnum erupted around Noah as he delved into the collapsing singularity that the Queen of Matter had come from right away!

"Woah...he really is the Tyrannical Emperor! How could he not be scared of that monster? Hey, Miss Ruination..."

The Queen of Matter turned around to observe the figure of Ruination with vibrant eyes, while Ruination, on the other hand, stuck her hands out while speaking coldly.

"The book. Let me see it."

She pointed to the thick golden book labeled Noah Osmont that the Queen of Matter grasped even tighter.

"This..." She seemed conflicted by such words as if she never wanted to say no to Ruination, but the book she held to her chest was truly too important for her to just give away!

"What if...I give you another one? Ive got the Records of the Ruination Cores right here!"

She tugged upon a golden string of Existence as a crimson gold bordered book appeared.

The Records of the Ruination Cores.

Ruination tugged and brought this book into her hands while opening it, seeing pages that were filled with Records of a very, very distant past.

A past of the Primordial Cosmos. Of Universes brought to Ruin. Of Noah discovering her and bringing about the emergence of Ruination.

After these records, there were a few more as she flipped across the pages, quickly going through the book as when it came towards the latter part, the pages were empty.

Her eyes flashed with questions at this as she was about to raise her head to ask the Queen of Matter when somehow, this woman had already appeared beside her, her eyes showing pride and excitement as if she had achieved a certain dream of hers to read a book about someone with that someone.

"Is it not crazy how far youve come? I was only able to find out about and record your early life. Before your existence became increasingly complex to the point that your weavings became your own, and your Records could not be easily discerned. But the Flow of the Wheel is tremendous, and anything that Matter has an ear to, I was still able to work hard to note down. What sucks is that I dont know much about the details of what you and the True Emperor of Quintessence have been up to these past weeks. Do you know how hard it was? I had to listen and record other Fables that werent nearly as tyrannical as his. I-...!"

Her words stopped as while holding her Book in one hand, Ruinations other crimson gold hand shot out to wrap around the neck of the Queen of Matter!

Ruinations eyes were sharp as she did not like to see weavings of her past written, even if it was before her Records were made her own.

"Do you know what I am now?"

She spoke coldly while coming to really look at the Queen of Matter.

The woman who, even with her throat grasped, showed an excited expression as she shook her head!

"No, but seeing how you can bypass a natural barrier of a Greater Source easily...my gosh, what has the True Emperor of Quintessence fed you?!"

"..."

She took none of this seriously at all as it caused a sense of dissonance, and at this time...

BOOM!

The decaying singularity where Noah had just disappeared to opened again as he returned.

His gaze was chilly as he looked at the scene of Ruination holding the Queen of Matter by the neck as he voiced out.

"No sign of Bob anywhere around your weavings. What did you say he took?"

He floated over while the Queen of Matter hummed as if she was truly enjoying herself today, still hugging a certain book on her chest tightly.

"He stole the Book of Existence. It holds everything that Matter has laid witness to across the Frequencies- weavings of important Nodes of the Flow of Existence. Where any weavings of the Weaving might be hidden, and other forms of knowledge relating to the Wheel that could be very dangerous in the wrong hands. I knew he was sketchy when he came to meet me before and was curious about the Fables I was recording, but I didnt think he would steal like this! But hey, at least he left some important books behind!"

She said this while clutching the book with the title of Noahs name closer, the other books around her connected to the strings of Existence shining brilliantly as Noah began to reach out and flip through them.

"You seem to know who we are, so you should know about my nature. I will give you a chance to explain thoroughly and clearly right now, O Queen of Matter."

His voice was heavy and serious!

And yet when he uttered her name and looked at her seriously, the visage of this Greater Source entity brimmed with excitement as she hummed and began to speak- her showing how she absolutely enjoyed this whole interaction!
