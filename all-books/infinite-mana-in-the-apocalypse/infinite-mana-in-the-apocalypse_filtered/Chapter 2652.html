Chapter 2650 Change in the Vacuous Domains! II

Noah had actually come across the fringes of Legions of Vacuous Malignancies as they were marching.

Just with his perception, he could feel billions of Apex Vacuous Malignancies stretched out in countless legions as this...was just what he could see.

The clusters and forces of Vacuous Malignancies continued to spread out even further than where his will could currently cover on this domain of Vacuous Hyperversal Authority as there were likely billions more there.

And since Noah was on the fringes of these vast legions...he was seeing their weaker forces now as towards its center and forefront should be maddeningly powerful existences!

As he joined the ranks of the Apex Vacuous Legionnaires, the ones he came across uniquely made space for him after gazing at his body that gave off a calm glow of power.

Noah's Vacuous Havenly I Entity Form held a status among others as one who had achieved their First Vacuous Awakening, and his figure saw the treatment in status as he was able to pull in deeper into the Legions until he came across another existence giving off a similar glow to him.

Others like them seemed to be stretched out from here in larger numbers as Noah stopped going inward when he arrived at this position, joining the march as the Vacuous Malignancy that had achieved their First Vacuous Awakening gazed towards him with eyes of power and brutality as he spoke.

"Did you get cold feet, Comrade? Why fall out of ranks for such a Holy Mission?"

The words were carefree as if one family member was talking to another, with Noah's bright eyes showing annoyance as he harrumphed and replied.

"I'm lazy by nature, I just don't understand...why now?"

Why now?

Why were endless legions of Vacuous Malignancies marching?

Noah didn't have to wonder where these endless legions of Vacuous Malignancies were marching towards.

He could feel the direction they barreled towards as the other parts of his soul were moving within those lands freely.

The place where he could feel the tug of Vitalis Hyperversal Authority.

The endless legions of Vacuous Malignancies were marching towards the Seven Havens of Extremity!

"Why now? The passed word from the Commander Legionnaires is that something happened within the Vitalis Hyperversal Domains. Something significant enough to spread their forces and spur us to move in a way we never have before. Even if you are lazy, this is a time for glory, Comrade. Imagine ripping into the soul of an Extremity who cannot defend against Vacuous Hyperversal Authority...imagine assimilating their soul onto your own concepts. By devouring and assimilating a single one, Extremity arrives right at your doorstep!"

WAA!

Something significant had occurred within the Vitalis Hyperversal Domains!

The Vacuous Lineages knew full well what was happening within the Seven Havens as the Culmination Step of Dissolution was a match that had triggered too many things.

As Noah thought of this, his eyes radiated immense brightness as the Apex Vacuous Legionnaire beside him laughed.

"Bahaha, I see that hungry light in your eyes. Be patient, Comrade...for we are close. I am Igor, you are?"

Igor was a burly humanoid Apex Vacuous Lifeform, his body built like stones as his face had eyes aflame with obsidian light that formed complex tattoos across his whole body- his whole figure seemingly set aflame.

Noah looked at such a being as after an instant of pondering, he smiled while replying gloriously.

"Aleksandr, comrade. I am Aleksandr!"

Noah hit it off with another Vacuous Havenly I Entity as he talked freely to obtain more information, an ancient rhythm coursing through the Legions around him as they matched forward with purpose!

They didn't consider themselves Vacuous Malignancies just like those from the Seven Havens didn't consider themselves Vitalis Malignancies.

And yet both sides called each other Malignancies who wanted to spread their cancerous authorities into the other's homes.

Both sides thought they were fighting a holy war to save their dominion and home from extinction!

Amongst the Vacuous Lineages, Awakenings were widespread and greatly supported, with institutions built within the Vacuous Hyperversal Domains that directed and taught newly born Vacuous Lifeforms towards Vacuous Awakening.

Those talented enough like Igor and many more would achieve it even before grasping Extremity, with the only possibility for the Second Vacuous Awakening truly only rising after one became a Hyperversal Extremity.

As for the Third Vacuous Awakening? It was too far for many Extremities to even dream of!

Waves of such information entered Noah's mind as with the absence of time, it wasn't entirely clear whether they had quickly arrived and taken a long period of time to do so, but the endless nothingness all around them began to change.

The Legions stopped moving.

Because ahead of them, in the midst of all of this darkness and emptiness...

The allure of a blinding white light could be seen as even getting near it for most Vacuous Malignancies was unbearable!

OOOM!

The rhythm of the Legions reached a crescendo as they stayed far in the distance.

Their glare towards the blinding white light far ahead was sharp, with perceptive existences being able to appreciate that as the obsidian of Vacuous and the transparent white of Vitalis neared each other, a hazy field of misty gray light was formed in between as the two authorities seemed to dim when they got close to each other!

But one thing was clear.

The transparent white light of Vitalis was ever radiant as more of it blared out with glory, with Vacuous not even coming close as left and right....the ends of the transparent white light couldn't be seen.

And similarly, the ends of the obsidian Vacuous Hyperversal Domains also couldn't be seen as it felt like two endlessly vast walls trying to encroach on each other in a constant and endless battle that would never end!

Vitalis was more potent and powerful as it reigned in Vacuous.

And yet Vacuous was vastly grander in quantity as even though the transparent white domain seemed endless all around, it stretched towards an illusory horizon and seemed to make a turn.

The Vacuous Hyperversal Domains were much larger and vaster than the Vitalis Hyperversal Domains.

And on this day, after countless past skirmishes and battles...an untold number of Legions of Vacuous Lineages were knocking on the door of Vitalis!
