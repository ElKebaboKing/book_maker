Chapter 3266 Advancement! II

After he stated these words, Bob seemed to have come out of a reverie as he realized over half of the Hearthraes between him and Noah were missing, his hands having been fast to eat them all as he coughed at this moment while pulling his hand away.

He had taken just a little bit too much for the sheer taste.

|Alright, that will be all from me. This space here can act as a refuge for you if you need it. Good luck, Little One. You're the Ninth one I've come across...and likely the last.|

His circular head shone as he began to hum and rise up, floating upwards.

Unnaturally, his head twisted as he looked above while voicing out calmly.

|You like peeking at others, don't you? Why just hide over there and watch in silence.|

He left the blank white space he brought Noah in, the space he stated that it could be a refuge for him if he needed it.

Far above, where countless golden lines flowed, existence fizzled in and out as a hazy figure stared toward Bob with caution and calmness.

|Sir...we are not supposed to intervene.|

The words from this hazy space were respectful and yet reproachful at the same time.

Bob heard them while waving his hands dismissively.

|What intervention? I just met someone by chance, and his weavings followed me around for a bit. Are you trying to make trouble for me?|

...!

His mouth, which was always smiling, turned cold as his eyes flashed.

Above, the hazy existence instantly began to tremble while shaking their head.

|I wouldn't dare to, sir.|

|Mmm, then let's go.|

Without looking back, Bob flashed away and disappeared entirely.

Above, the hazy figure stared towards the translucent visage of Noah briefly, almost as if they were debating something, but after an instant, they also disappeared!

All that was left behind was a wide white space surrounded by endless golden strings around it.

Inside of it, a translucent figure encompassed by waves of radiance could be seen- an illusory Primordial Hearthraes Lotus Primaltree above him and a Primordial Mountain of the Expanse below him!

In a Half Tainted Omniverse.

Command Protector Valeria had been looking towards Noah with caution after his last words that brought about great shock.

He stated that he could break off the connection between her Sister and the Outerversal Morphon who had bound her!

She wanted to question such a statement, but in the next moment...

HUUM!

His figure began to radiate a shocking amount of golden and purple radiance.

|...Why are you glowing?|

She asked such words while squinting her eyes, but the man before her waved his hands dismissively while gazing behind him.

Past the scorched space.

Towards the tendrils of Outerversal Authority converging into a massive central singularity- the Dark Matter Core!

He could also sense that far in the distance, the figures of Protector Lucretia and the other three Royal Existential Omniversal Simian Protectors were wrapping up the Delegatus of Savitar who had lost their master here- the Existence Reapers who were scrambling out while crying like children- many of them escaping towards the shattering waves of Outerversal Authority in the distance.

Among the connections Noah had sent towards Ozymandias, many also included these Existence Reapers among others as Ozymandias would have to grasp them all and identify them!

And alongside all of this, ridiculous changes that he was trying to keep up were initiated by one of his bodies that was within the Higher Weavings of Existence, their boons stretching towards his Atlas of Existence and everything else that made him.

All of it happened at once.

But even now, he had to move quickly to get something else he wanted!

He began to drift towards the space with scorching obsidian radiance where one of Savitar's bodies had perished- going towards the tendrils of the distant Dark Matter Core as he voiced out towards the Command Protector Valeria.

"I'll help with your sister, and all I ask in return is to be the one that destroys the Dark Matter Core. I can gain something from it that will even help with cutting off the link from your Sister to the Outerversal Morphon she is connected to."

...!

Lies were mixed with truth as Command Protector Valeria gazed towards him with hesitation.

Why did she continue to ask for missions of hunting down Morphons? Why did she feel such hatred for them?

It was all for the sake of saving her sister from their grasp.

If this entity could truly be true to his words...

Her gaze shifted multiple times over as she felt the auras of the three Royal Existential Omniversal Simian Protectors turn towards this direction along with Lucretia.

|...|

Between them and Noah's figure that was going towards the Dark Matter Core, her body flashed as she turned into a streak of light that went to intercept the rest of the Equestris Protectors!

Within moments, she crossed the heavy folds of Outerversal Authority and appeared in the space where Existence Reapers were running rampant just moments ago, seeing the figures of Protectors clearing any remaining ones away while coming forward as they stopped and smiled with happiness when they saw Command Protector Valeria.

|Haha, Commander, Savitar has perished? I thought we would have to come aid you after we cleared his Peons!|

Protector Acteon radiated his intent wantonly as they gazed towards the distant interminable storms of Outerversal Authority.

Lucretia also drew near her sister while controlling her buzzing existence, her eyes cautiously staring towards the direction where Noah had also surged towards.

|And Protector Osmont?|

A question she threw towards her sister.

One that Command Protector Valeria nodded calmly to as she began to summon crimson and blue Wheels to wipe off any remaining Existence Reapers.

|He is observing the complete destruction of the Dark Matter Core. Come on- let us rid this broken Omniverse of these Agents of Anti-Life entirely!|

Her valiant Command echoed out as the others...only nodded and followed.

In the distance, Noah was coming upon a buzzing Dark Matter Core!
