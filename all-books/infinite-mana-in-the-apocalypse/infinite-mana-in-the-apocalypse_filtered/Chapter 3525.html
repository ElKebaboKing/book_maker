It was unbelievable.

They looked at it and truly thought that they were witnessing the Wheel of Existence itself before it shattered in the next moment

That was how ancient it was.

That was how archaic and Singed the Tzar Versepiercer was made under the banner of the Infiniverse Megalos!

When it exploded, Yoo Jin and the Sages instinctively wrapped their sources around them.

Instinctively.

Because the breaking of the Wheel spread its effects all around them.

It threatened all of them!

It felt like time stopped under the brilliance of the breaking of the Wheel, and Yoo Jin was furious as she thought she was about to die from something a newly gained ally threw out.

Her father had been right. He had advised her not to be involved and to remain isolated as it had worked for eons when she told him about Osmont.

Now, because she didnt listen...

Ah...

Under the brilliance of the unraveling complexity exploding out, she expected grievous injuries to her Source as...?

She blinked as she didnt feel any pain.

She opened her eyes and spread her senses out as she observed the most beautiful and devastating scene of destruction at the same time.

She saw white gold flames that felt older than her many times over.

She saw the breaking of existence as if she were there in primordial times when existence was first breaking and forming.

She saw how all of this came together to form a ravaging explosive force that should be utterly shredding even the defenses of her Source, and yet...

Nothing.

It didnt affect her.

She looked around as she saw the Sages carrying similar expressions to her, but theirs were much more reverential!

As if they had witnessed something holy.

Behind them, Tam of the Singed had spread his arms out as if to welcome this explosion, and he basked in its white gold flames as he had a joyous expression on his face!

As if he has experienced something he only saw once in his life before.

This heavy unraveling and destruction of existence itself also did not affect him!

The only one it did affect...

|OOOH!|

It was the Faceless Lifeform who was currently surrounded by countless obsidian pillars stacked on top of each other to form dark towers- and there were so many that in the next moment, existence in that area began to distort and disappear!

As if nothing could be allowed in to change it.

But...the white gold flames of unraveling from the Tzar Versepiercer were too pervasive as they still entered through the gaps of existence, breaking down the dark towers and coming to land on the Faceless Lifeform as his body was soon aflame!

CRACK!

Horridly, in the next moment, the weavings of existence nearby collapsed, the white gold flames all surging to become concentrated into a single point of singularity near the Faceless Lifeform before this singularity underwent one final blast of incinerating explosion.

This time, the flames hung around and released a heavy light of radiation.

The sandy gold makeup of this domain was aflame, along with the shattered dredges of wheels stretched out all around.

"..."

The Sages and Yoo Jin were silent as they observed the body of the Source Complexity Faceless Lifeform simmering in these flames, its body holding streaks of burn marks all over as its right hand...was placed on its chest- covering a small scorched gash that nearly cut open the obsidian suit of this being!

|...|

It didnt have an expression.

It didnt have a face.

And yet at this moment, they could feel its palpable rage amidst the scorching waves of radiation that remained burning all around.

|You. Insignificant. Dredges. Of. COMPLEXITY!|

BOOOM!

Maddening black radiance erupted from this truly complex creature.

Noah looked at it from afar as the biggest thing he confirmed was the utility of the Tzar Versepiercers- the fact that they could actually affect a Source Complexity with their power!

The other thing confirmed was that truly, any Source Complexity entity was not someone easy to take down.

Along with its enraged intent, the Faceless Lifeform brought its hands in front of its chest as if it were praying, a hymn beginning to resound from it that caused a sensation of unease to spread out!

"Continue!"

The Sages and Yoo Jin no longer sat still at the emergence of such a complexity as their Sources struck out.

And yet behind the Faceless Lifeform, an illusion of a black flame began to form and gradually become more real.

A black flame.

The moment such a thing arose, even Noah felt a sense of revulsion as if he smelled something that had gone bad, his expression turning serious as he waved his hands to reveal another three Tzar Versepiercers.

Tam of the Singed saw this scene as he trembled, falling onto his knees as he gazed towards the three Versepiercers with fanaticism.

"Let everything be bathed in scorching light of fire..."

He uttered out the words reverentially as by the time others observed the Tzar Versepiercers, it was when Noah was throwing them towards the Faceless Lifeform!

...!

This time, the Sages and Yoo Jin did not falter with trepidation, using this chance to unleash their heaviest weavings as unique complexities lit up the nearby weavings of existence.

They were followed by the simultaneous emergence of three massive wooden Wheels of Existence.

Wooden wheels that then vibrated and shattered into multiple explosions, their focus entirely centered around a creature that was praying at this moment as he was drowned in scorching white gold flames!

Alongside this, the Sources of 6 other entities of the same stature came down to bombard this being as everything joined together to take down the grandeur of a single Source.

This alone was enough to show the indefatigability of a Source- the difficulty in ever getting past its defenses and actually wounding it!

But one man had the means to make it possible.

And he didnt even have to wait before he could hear woeful screeches amidst the raging flames!
