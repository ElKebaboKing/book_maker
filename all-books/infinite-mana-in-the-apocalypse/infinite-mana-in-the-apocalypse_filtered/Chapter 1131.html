Chapter 1129 - The Glorious Light Of Assimilation! II

Noah observed all of this closely with all of his bodies as there was a terrifying thought in his mind as he was looking at the grand process about to begin.

But the prospects of it...Noah couldnt help but think of this over and over again.

Not just the myriad of Cosmos within this branching reality, but what of the innumerable number of realities out there? What of the Original reality that all of this stemmed from?!

Bind this Primordial Cosmos, then the myriad of other Cosmos, then this branching reality, and then...the myriad of infinite realities out there...!

THUMP!

Slow down. Slow down.

Everything else would fall into place.
