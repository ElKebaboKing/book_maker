Chapter 3293 The enemy of my enemy I

In red and blue waters that stretched out mystically, with the allure of stellar brilliance shining all around.

The Omniversal Archontes Engineer cooly sat on these waters as he talked to a trace will of Savitar, his gaze firm as he continued.

"Since he led you here, you must know a great deal about them. Tell me who he is so his schemes can be put to rest."

...!

Who was the Outerversal Morphon behind Protector Lucretia?

Who had managed to bend a Protector of the Commonwealth, and how many more Protectors were there that were his Delegatus?

Noah observed the buzzing figure of Savitar that was pulsing between wrath and hesitation, this being keeping his intent in without revealing anything else as Noah raised his brows and continued.

"Will you not even reveal him? Are you fearful of him? From his actions, he treats you as a joke. He helps kill one of your bodies one day, and then goes to use and manipulate you the next day as if nothing happened. How low must he think of you? You are a Great One, yes? For a Great One to be treated like a joke-"

|There are ranks to the Great Ones!|

A bellow of wrath and humiliation came through.

The skull of Savitar bobbed and fell on the waters as weavings of wrath and shame circled around him, and he looked spitefully towards Noah while his purple flames burned.

|He manipulated me, and you think you can sit here and speak of what you think to be my weakness to manipulate me too? You will not get anything from me, O Manipulator.|

...!

The One Who Walks was firm.

He reigned in his rage and insecurities as Noah gazed at him calmly.

Then, the hands of the Omniversal Archontes Engineer arose as interminably pure waves of Outerversal Authority began to swirl around him.

"Is my identity what is causing you reservations? We stem from the same source, Savitar. I have waded the Megalos Beyond for too many years for one of my peers to treat me as an outsider. This is something we can discuss between each other Morphon to Morphon, without any reservations."

WAA!

Savitar stared at the extremely pure waves of Outerversal Authority as his purple flames burned brilliantly.

|The Sword of the Megalos...Outerversal Authority...you aren't a mere Delegatus. Who are you?|

His flames flickered subtly.

Noah looked at him and shook his head with a smile as he replied.

"I have not intertwined my weavings with others for too many cycles. I do not know much of you all, and you all have likely not heard of me, or I may have been forgotten entirely. But you can call me..."

Noah's mind churned as he decided on a new distinction for himself.

After a second, he laughed internally while continuing.

"...The Dreamer of the Megalos."

HUUM!

Words held power.

Choices held power.

Distinctions even more so!

|The Dreamer of the Megalos...|

Savitar uttered these words as he looked closely at Noah and the silent waves of pure Outerversal Authority.

"We are of the same breed. So let us discuss the enemy seeking to use us as chess pieces. I have confidence in my own power to strike back against them...I just need the target to be made clear."

|...|

His words caused silence to descend as Savitar only stared at him with flickering purple flames.

It was only after a few seconds...

|The Great Ponderor.|

WAA!

|He is a Great One who has managed to survive for far too many cycles. Others have said that he was alive all the way back to when the Great Beyonder still moved across the Megalos. He is a Great One that other Great One steer clear of...Nietzsche!|

...!

Nietzsche!

Noah carved this name into his memory as he nodded, letting out a light sigh.

"The Great Ponderor...I have only heard whispers of him since I woke up. The Great Beyonder though...ah, he was something else."

Cryptic words echoed from him that shocked Savitar as this Morphon looked towards him quizzically.

The way he spoke made it seem as if he had lived in the cycles when the Great Beyonder was active too!

|You...did you know the Great Beyonder? Did you observe his feats?|

He asked cautiously.

And Noah smiled as truly, he had dreamed of the cycle when the Great Beyonder was just constructing Crucibles.

He smiled with a sense of mystery and replied.

"I know a lot, and I still know very little. Recently though, I observed some changes in the Megalos Beyond where a young Morphon overcame the Crucible of the Amarantos Dream of the Megalos on the Pantheon of Umbra...the so called Instrument, is it?"

WAA!

His words instantly shocked Savitar as his skull moved much closer!

|You...your main body is currently within the Pantheon of Umbra?! The Great Ponderor is there as well! Even one of my bodies is...|

"I am not there..." Noah raised his hand to stop this being who had inexplicably thought of him much differently than before.

"I told you, I am the Dreamer of the Megalos. The amount of information I can obtain is astronomical without ever having to be in that place myself. But it is great to know where one of the bodies of my enemy is. Where else is he in the Megalos Beyond? Which Tainted Omniverses is he feeding from within the Megalos Ynnere?"

Savitar heard his questions clearly as he calmed down.

He seemed to come to a decisive point as his flames flickered firmly.

He was hooked.

Without a shadow of a doubt, he believed Noah to be another terrifying Great One.

After all, he even knew of something as recent and intricate as what was occurring on the Megalos Pantheon of Umbra!

|I...know of at least 9 of the Tainted Omniverses under his control. As for the locations of his bodies across the Megalos Beyond, he is scattered. Nobody knows where his Main Body lies. You...are you sure you are able to go against him? He...he alone is powerful. And he has many, many allies.|

...!
