Chapter 4714: Archai II

The implications alone made his mind race with possibilities. Thesis meant that Fundamental Depth entities from before differentiation didnt simply declare their truth into existence through overwhelming authority. They proposed it. They invited challenge. And if their logic held, if their framework was coherent, their truth became more real than anything a modern Declaration could achieve.

Because it had been tested. Because it had survived scrutiny. Because existence itself had examined it and found no flaw.

And Apophasis was even more terrifying.

Modern Embodiment meant becoming what you were, fully and completely merging with the concept you represented. But Apophasis was the opposite approach achieving the same result through a different path. It meant unbecoming what you were not. Systematically removing every limitation, every constraint, every definition that could be used against you until you existed outside the boundaries that trapped other entities.

An entity using Apophasis didnt need to be stronger than their opponent.

They simply needed to not be something their opponent could affect.

...!

Noah twirled the Fragment of THE Axiom of Mayhem as he read these prompts, feeling the pulsing power within his existence as he envisioned all the possibilities before him.

The main differences were becoming clear to him now.

Declaration versus Thesis. Both created truth, but through fundamentally different mechanisms. Declaration created through authority, through the raw weight of ones Civilization imposing its reality upon existence. Thesis created through proposition, through logical coherence and philosophical framework. Declaration was harder to challenge directly but easier to overpower if you simply had more authority. Thesis was vulnerable to logical attack but became incredibly stable if left unchallenged.

An entity from Ginnungagap or Muspelheim using Thesis could potentially be defeated through debate rather than combat.

But an entity using Thesis that went unchallenged would have truth more solid than anything a modern Declarer could achieve.

And Embodiment versus Apophasis represented the same dichotomy at the highest level.

A clash between them would create fundamental paradoxes.

One entity saying "I am this" while another said "I am not that" in direct opposition could reshape local reality entirely.

At such a time, beside Noah in the sea of blue-gold Mana, the figure of the holographic Ruination appeared with an excited expression on her features.

|Master, Ive managed to break down what your Principles could be distinguished as. I finished integrating the last few Phonemes into them.|

Her face was brilliant and filled with enthusiasm as she spoke, and illusory diagrams began to form around her as she explained.

|Your action of altering them and adding Phonemes into them elevated them to an ancient form of Principles that even Ginnu Lifeforms or Muspeli Lifeforms wont necessarily have access to. So much has changed since before differentiation. The knowledge was lost. The methodology was forgotten. But from pushing The First Tongue to permeate freely in the Corrupted Vault, more of this information has been obtained.|

The diagrams shifted and reformed, displaying complex linguistic structures that pulsed with power.

|The designation given is: The Undifferentiated Principles. Archai.|

Noah looked at all of these prompts with eyes that burned with blue-gold light.

Archai.

Undifferentiated Principles that had not yet decided what they were.

His Perpetual Harvest was becoming something that could be Growth or Decay or Evolution or Destruction depending on how he chose to express it. His Cheating Architect was becoming something that could bend rules in ways that hadnt been defined yet. His Undifferentiated Mana was becoming the Arche of Mana itself, the raw building block from which all Mana-based Principles could potentially emerge.

Against modern opponents who used rigid, differentiated Principles, he would be able to shift and adapt in ways they couldnt anticipate. Against ancient opponents who might recognize what Archai were, he would be speaking a language they understood but hadnt heard in eons.

Either way, he had advantages that no one else, or very few, currently possessed!
