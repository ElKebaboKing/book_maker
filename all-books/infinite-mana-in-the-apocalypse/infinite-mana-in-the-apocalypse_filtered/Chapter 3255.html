Chapter 3253 Tainted! I

Unconquerable was the goal.

The end result after countless possible initial conditions and changes that could be made.

And after attaining this current stage of power...

|Hello!|

...!

All weavings came to a halt.

All scenes faded.

In the midst of countless golden threads, Noah turned to his right as there, shockingly, a figure of an Entity could be seen.

An entity he didn't sense the entrance of, nor warned about by his recently appearing Invictus Maximus or any other forms of his power!

It meant that it was an Entity far above him in leagues.

He knew that in this place he termed the Higher Weavings of Existence, there were bound to be other terrifying beings that had appeared before him, and he would slowly explore to see just what was out there!

What he didn't expect was to be found within minutes of his entry.

He looked at the entity where the intent had stemmed from.

Humanoid in nature by all accounts.

Legs and arms that were covered by a white robe, but his head...was entirely bald and far too circular as it only had two white singularities for eyes and a mouth!

No nose. no ears.

The mouth was currently curved into a friendly smile, the white singularities actually showing enthusiasm as they looked towards Noah.

Having such a terrifying entity before him, everything around Noah was retracted as he replied with a calm nod.

|Hello.|

His reply only caused the smile on the entity ahead of him to deepen as his fair skin shone with brilliance.

Around him, Noah could see the golden threads change their movements and gravitate around him in unique motions, entirely different from how they ignored him entirely.

|Hello friend! I haven't seen you around here before. I am Bob, who are you?|

...!

Bob.

A ridiculously simple name was given by this entity as Noah carefully looked at it while reciprocating its demeanor, a smile appearing on him as he replied.

|I am Noah, Noah Osmont. Are you familiar with these parts, Bob?|

Since this entity didn't make a move, neither did Noah.

And this entity, Bob, nodded his head vigorously as he neared Noah.

|Yes, my friend! I've traveled all around these parts. Did you just arrive? When I arrived here, it was hard to find lots of friends to talk to, but now, there are so many! I don't want you to be bored like I was when I first arrived, so how about I take you somewhere special? There's been something I'm itching to get my hands on for a while, and you might just be perfect to help me get it!|

|...|

His enthusiasm was unmatched.

He spoke of many things as a great deal of details could be discovered under his calm words, with Noah choosing to go along with it as he nodded.

|Sure.|

He didn't know much about the place he was in.

But he was curious just what oddities and wonders had already transcended their level of existence to arrive here.

|Haha, great! This way, Friend!|

Bob's smile covered all of his face as he came forward and...grasped Noah's hand.

...!

He didn't phase through or miss, Noah feeling the pincers-like grasp as thereafter, Bob squinted his eyes as his other hand tugged on a golden string a distance away from them.

And...

HUUM!

The golden strings vibrated as their figures instantly disappeared- covered by a golden light as Noah felt himself traversing unfathomably vast distances in an extremely short period of time!

As if they were utilizing the golden string that Bob pulled on and taking it to an extremely far away place.

Within moments...

WAP!

Their figures reappeared.

Everything here felt richer.

Golden strings pulsed in abundance.

Noah's eyes were bright as he gazed before them to see a dense cluster of golden strings that formed into the shape of a massive tree.

But this wasn't the most astounding thing.

It was the fact that surrounding this massive tree, golden dew drop shaped fruits hung gloriously- radiating an enticing glare that caused his very own existence to tremble.

Bob had long since released his hand as he crossed his own hands on his chest while speaking with a smile.

|I've taken you a little deeper into the Expanse, Friend. The tree you see before us is one of the many fun things you will come across here, but when I near it...|

His body rapidly floated towards the dense cluster of golden strings that formed the massive tree, but the moment he neared...

HUUM!

His figure became increasingly wrapped with golden strings that stopped his advancement until he couldn't move forward anymore!

|It is very sensitive to complex weavings of existence as someone like me cannot get close. But if it's you, Noah, you should be able to get close no problem! Just grab me a few, and you keep the rest. The taste of the Hearthraes is impeccable, and it can also solidify the weavings of your-|

ROAAR!

In the midst of his words.

A booming roar suddenly echoed out in the distance as Noah's Invictus Maximus Organ pulsed, showing him endless darkness!

In the distance, traveling on thousands of golden strings, a cloud of stellar purple light roared out as it came towards them with immense malice.

The sheer pressure its existence exerted was so terrifying that Noah knew this was not an entity he could face right now.

It was likely in the Omniversal Stage if not higher, with its intent filled with a devouring light of greed hungrily staring at none other than Noah and Bob!

But just as it neared them...

|Tsk...|

The smile on Bob faded, his hand rising as he tapped out.

A tap.

A single, simple, tap.

And yet...it felt like existence itself had turned its back on the entity formed of purple clouds, the golden strings around it briefly turning black as they released an interminable obsidian wave that instantly caused the whole body of this Entity to break down and disperse!

...!

With a mere tap.

Moments later, the golden strings returned to their normal self as Bob shook his head while smiling again.

|I hate interruptions the most. Ah, so how about it, friend? Can you try and get us some of those Hearthraes? After that, we can have a feast and share some stories.|

|...|

Bob.

This terrifying being spoke nonchalantly after eradicating an immeasurably powerful entity with a tap of his fingers.

Noah's figure took everything in strides as he began walking towards the unfathomable cluster of golden lines forming into Authorities as he replied.

|Sure, Bob. Let's try and get these Hearthraes.|

He spoke succinctly as he went forward, his weavings of existence working overtime as he wondered and sought to analyze.

Who the fuck was this Bob?
