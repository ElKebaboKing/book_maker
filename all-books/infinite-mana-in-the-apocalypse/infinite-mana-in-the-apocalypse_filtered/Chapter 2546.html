2544 Violating Common Sense! IV

The Apex Battlefield became rowdy.

200 pristinely powerful Apex Aeonic Lifeforms from the Avernus Clan and 300 from the Singularity Overseers were added as the force supporting the Noblesse became utterly massive!

Commander Vladic's dark expression became many times lighter when he saw this ever changing scene as his fists tightened.

So what if they lost the support of the Genesis Institute of Extremity?

Their number of Apex Aeonic Lifeforms on their side alone had increased by more than 5 times since just minutes ago!

Meanwhile, the opposing side, however surprising it was, held a bit over 200 exceptional Apex Aeonic Lifeforms.

Would they be able to stand against the enraged forces of the Avernus Clan and the Singularity Overseers?

This was a question that some believed they had the answers to as at this moment, the silent clamor in the surroundings rose as those watching realized just how much bigger this War had become.

But while their discussions began amongst themselves, the visage of the True Emperor of Quintessence remained unfettered while staring at the myriad of beings before him.

His voice was even calmer than before, a smile forming on it as very few would be able to understand why he was smiling!

"At times, the smallest of decisions can forever change the course of one's destiny."

WAA!

"Today, the destiny of the Avernus Clan and the Singularity Overseers has diverged into another path entirely."

As he spoke, he waved his hands again.

A simple motion.

But all beings who were watching aptly remembered this motion.

When he did it before, 200 utterly powerful Apex Aeonic Lifeforms adorned in Relics far exceeding Apex Aeonic Relics had appeared.

What exactly was he calling forth now?

Time felt like it was crawling by extremely slowly.

Besides the True Emperor of Quintessence, space fluctuated as an instant later, an aura of utmost brilliance bloomed as an Entity appeared.

His mere presence caused the surrounding space to buzz and grind on itself heavily as on his very skin, a pristine authority of an Absolute Dynamis of Extremity could be felt!

His hair shone black, gold, and green. His pupils shone with an obsidian verdant sheen that seemed to be filled with both life and death as any weak Apex Aeonic Lifeform gazing into them may have their Soul shattered!

His body was adorned not in the Loot Seeker's Regalia Set...but with wondrous armaments that shone with a stellar obsidian and green crystalline light as they seemed to be forged from bones.

In his right hand, a verdant staff was grasped as at its tip, a skull with its sockets inscribed with a deathly green infinity symbol could be seen.

Armaments that were forged just for him.

Relics that came together to forge a Set known as the Raiments of the Quintessential Lich Emperor!

A bony helm of this set stably adorned the visage of none other than Alexander like a crown as it blazed with horrifying green flames.

All of this was stunning, but nothing was more so stunning than his aura and the fact that from him, an aura of a fully converged Absolute Dynamis of Extremity could be felt.

Not an Absolute Seed that had yet to bud, but a genuine Absolute Dynamis of Extremity!

At his appearance, the far-away Ahpuch that the forces of the Avernus Clan had gathered near rose as his body throbbed with grandeur.

His heart beat loudly like a drum as if it was telling him...his destiny had arrived.

Before any surprise and alarm could be expressed, the motioning hand of the True Emperor of Quintessence was not done as behind his stellar force of 200, dazzling lights of titanic stature began to bloom.

The figures of Regal Beasts began to unfold by the dozens as even with their massive size, there were dozens of them who were led by none other than a titanic Draconic Elephantine Ender of Extremity!

All around him, the images of Beasts that seemed like massive celestial creatures only born in ancient times proliferated as their forms varied a great deal, taking on statures of Qilins, Phoenixes, Serpents, Crows, Dragons, or a myriad of combinations of such creatures with even more Beasts!

They didn't bellow out or roar to show their power as their eyes held interminable brilliance, gazing around at everything coldly and with precision as even average Apex Aeonic Lifeforms watching in the distance felt their blood run cold.

Their numbers and sizes filled the scanty battlefield as a count had 300 Enders of Extremity pulsing with Extremity Foundations and a dazzling aura of the Apex Aeonic Lifeform stage.

"..."

Wondrous dread and power unveiled itself as the myriad of watching gazes each held their own thoughts, each one observing a different part of the unveiled forces.

Roan could not believe the scene before him.

The figures of Oriona and Aurelia felt utterly unreachable as even though it was hard to believe, he could somewhat go through the phases and bring himself to accept it.

But when he looked at the other Apex Aeonic Lifeforms he knew very well to be weaker than him just days ago, and their auras were many times more powerful than him right now...he couldn't accept it!

This was...until his eyes fully focused from his shock as he blinked when his gaze came to land on a figure he recognized very well.

A being with broken meridians.

A being that had failed Apexification.

Maximilian...was he...was he seeing things or was this past Lineage Emperor an Apex Aeonic Lifeform with an aura just as maddening as all the others?!

"..."

Roan's will couldn't formulate any thought as at this moment, his soul felt like it had ground to a halt from utter incredulity!

He and many others found themselves unable to even discuss as they truly never expected the True Emperor of Quintessence to call forth a force that would make an Inheritance jealous!

And before any of the disbelief could be expressed...

BZZZT!

The skies seethed once more with Hyperversal Extremity Authority.

An authority more insane than any that had come before it.

It was the authority of the Adjudicator of Dissolution as above the Apex Battlefield, space cracked open while a hidden visage stepped out!
