Chapter 3278 An Endless Dream! II

As Protector Alexei, hundreds of years passed as he battled against the forces of Outerversal Morphons and Worms of Existence.

In one Omniverse, he faced the dredges of an ancient Outerversal Morphon who had spread out his influence over dozens of Omniverses.

In another, he was exposed to the terrifying Worms of Existence stemming from the Megalos Nihilos!

Every time he massacred them, the Wheel in his existence turned ever faster.

He could almost feel the forging of his Fabled Omniverse get closer and closer.

In this Dream, over these hundreds of years that passed, he never looked for Omniverse X-999.

This was because of a critical piece of information he had received early on.

On a certain day hundreds of years ago, his Brother on the Omniversal Stage had told him something.

|The Omniverses our Commonwealth has discovered? Hmm, the most recent one is Omniverse X-577. Why?|

...!

Yes.

Within this Dream, it was unknown exactly how many years ago it was before Omniverse X-999 was ever discovered.

In addition to this, another terrifying piece of information was uncovered during another discussion with the Brother of Protector Alexei.

He had just returned from a campaign, and his expression was a cold one.

Seeing the gaze of the golden-furred Protector Alexei, the Brother had sighed and spoken.

|As you get stronger, it will not just be Outerversal Morphons you will have to fight off. Eventually, our own people will fall under your scrutiny.|

...!

|Today, I tracked down Protector Voidsurge who had recently made tremendous advancements in a short period of time. He wouldn't let me Scour the Weavings of his Existence as ultimately, he exploded out with Outerversal Authority...and the illusion of a massive, tenebrous, tentacled Wheel. It was not the Wheel of Existence we all follow, but the Wheel of Enticement! The Great Wheel! The Wheel of the Distorter!|

...!

A terrifying hidden issue was revealed within the bounds of the Commonwealth with those who sought the glory of the Wheel of Existence.

Some were led astray as when they sought the Wheel, they found something else entirely.

This...was something to be mindful of.

Life continued.

Until, on a certain day, Protector Alexei led an incursion against a Half-Tainted Omniverse that had a new player who had never appeared before.

An Outerversal Morphon that looked like a massive snail.

When Protector Alexei and this Morphon came face to face, terrifying weavings of existence flourished as a great battle ensued- and in this battle, his Fabled Omniverse actually began to form as he condensed all of his existence into a fine point!

But before the transformation could be completed.

In the middle of battle.

Protector Alexei began to feel a heavy pull of sleep.

And darkness overcame him entirely.

|...|

And he continued to Dream!

PAH!

His consciousness arose once more.

He found himself staring at endless obsidian radiance all around, with his surroundings filled with auras of terrifying creatures

Another dream.

In this dream...Ozymandias was something else entirely.

|O Great Deceiver, are you sure of this? The Instrument has garnered a great deal of power. Multiple Great Ones stand to support him...with the Great Mother being the one who is the most fanatical.|

...!

Near him, an entity that looked like a star formed from Tentacles spoke towards a terrifying Outerversal Morphon that had the head of a bull and the body of a human.

A Great One. The Great Deceiver!

At the intent shown his way, this entity harrumphed as he replied.

|The Great Beyonder has far exceeded all of us as his means are unfathomable. He left this astonishing Legacy not just for the Instrument. Attaining it...will also aid us in our Incursion across the Expanse. We need more to become One With Megalos. More to Transcend their old shells and become something else. So yes...I am sure!|

...!

The area they were in was hazy.

But he stood behind this terrifying Great One as he looked at himself at this moment.

An Outerversal Morphon who also held the head of a bull and the body of a Human.

Someone known as Outerversal Morphon Lexo!

An attendant of this Great Deceiver.

He held terrifying waves of Megalos Regnum, and the connection he had under him...spanned hundreds of millions.

'Fantastic...'

His thoughts were vibrant as he prepared for a long dream.

In front of him, the Great Deceiver turned his will unto him as he spoke out.

|Lexo, contact the Great Ponderor and-....huh?|

The Great One stopped in the midst of his words.

His crimson eyes blinked before a second later, they burned with flames as pillars of crimson stellar light actually erupted from them- these pillars scorching everything on their path as they went to ground Morphon Lexo into nothingness!

|Fucking hell! A Dreamer! A Dreamer actually stood beside me this whole time? Who? WHO?!|

The shouts were filled with rage and waves of wrath, the third Dream being stopped extremely short as this time, the pull of a deep slumber was not felt.

Ozymandias opened his eyes once more, but he found himself in an infinitely vast stellar obsidian space.

And in front of him...three blinding singularities could be seen.

|Due to the erasure of your Existence, the Amarantos Dream of the Megalos has been brought to an end.|

|Your proficiency in the Amarantos Dream of the Megalos currently stands in the First Echelon, with your Proficiency at 66%.|

|Based on this, the weavings of the Amarantos Dream of the Megalos you can obtain are extremely limited.|

|All memories of the Dreams are retained.|

|The Weavings of a single Dream can be retrieved fully, and half the weavings of another Dream can be retrieved.|

|The Three Dreams of the Megalos capable of being chosen are as follows|

[The Worm of the Deep- Outerversal Physiology Echelon V, Megalos Regnum Echelon IV. 155,000,000 Pure Delegatus Channels. Half of the Existence Defying Physiology of the Megalos Transcendent Megalos Ability.]

[Protector Alexei- System of Existence of Kuklos Echelon 4. Musings of the formation of a Fabled Omniverse.]

[Outerversal Morphon Lexo- Outerversal Physiology Echelon V, Megalos Regnum Echelon V. 277,000,000 Pure Delegatus Channels.]
