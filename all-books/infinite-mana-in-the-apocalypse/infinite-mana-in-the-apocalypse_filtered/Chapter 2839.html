Chapter 2837 What is Extremity? I

It was unique.

Noah's vision always saw more things than others, and this was elevated to a ridiculous level when he was capable of attaining the state of Quintessential Unity of Existence.

His Existential Extremity Authorities also quite literally allowed him to 'see' and be cognizant of the Gradations of Extremity!

Now, he came to the realization that from the First Gradation and through the Fifth Gradation of Extremity, one could barrel through them so long as they held enough quantity or quality of concepts of Extremity.

But when stepping into the Sixth Gradation of Extremity....this wasn't enough!

Just a swirling mess of Extremity Rune Scriptures was not enough as one had to make sure their Extremes...were intricately flowing across their existence.

So theoretically, someone like Noah who held over 100 Extremes in his body...would have an extremely difficult time to bring them all together.

And an Entity who only pursed a single concept to Extremity...may be able to attain a unique flow that didn't allow for any gaps around their existence! But would a single concept be strong enough to take them across the initial Gradations or even simply withstand the pressure of the Sixth Gradation? The answer was likely no.

Thus, entities pursued Extremity in multiple fields.

And they then sought to converge their extremes into Dynamis of Extremity!

Noah already had a skeleton of convergence of his concepts before they reached their Extremes as now...he only had to bring it all together.

His first convergence though, ended up involving an Existential concept as the very first one.

Who in the Vitalis and Vacuous Hyperversal Domains could say their Dynamis of Extremity held an Existential Concept of Extremity and multiple Quintessential Zenith Concepts alongside it? The answer was no one!

HUUM!

And this first convergence caused his vision to see something else as for the first time, Oraculum was truly coming to fruition.

After Quintessence began everything, Providence, Destiny, Karma, Fate, Fortune, Elysium, Wisdom, Dreams and multiple more Concepts of Extremity that had their Scriptures affected by the Colorless light of Quintessence all began to flow in together.

And the scene Noah saw...wasn't just the fact that above him, the gaps were becoming slowly covered as singular circles of Rune Scriptures began melding into each other.

He could see at the very center of the flow that had begun to form...the concepts themselves.

Extremity itself.

It seemed to be an ability of the forming Oraculum, but it caused his mind to enter a daze as if he was looking at his very own concepts for the first time!

It wasn't just that he could see them more clearly, but he saw that even though their Rune Scriptures were affected by Quintessence and made Colorless, at their core...each of his Concepts of Extremity held their own color.

Their own identity. Their own...emotion, desire, longing, weaving, and intent.

Weaving!

Intent!

What their extremes stood for and sought to do!

Noah saw that even though they were majorly affected by Quintessence, each concept of Extremity held its own profound longing and intent.

And once Noah fully realized the intent of the Concepts of Extremity, he shockingly found that he wasn't just able to see their current flow...but illusory golden pathways of where they were intending to go!

This scene caused his mind to buzz and illuminate as among the melding concepts entering under the umbrella of Oraculum, he came to focus on the concepts of the remaining 9 Decreta as he grasped onto their intent- their very sources entrenched in his soul...and he pulled them towards the flow Quintessence controlled as he altered their current pathway and flow to match the other concepts already converged into Oraculum.

And...

SHAA!

All remaining concepts relating to Decreta seamlessly joined the flow as over 25% of his body was now entirely covered by a Colorless light with absolutely no gaps.

His body stabilized in the Sixth Gradation of Extremity.

He saw the source of Ascendancy blare with a deeply hidden golden light as the clear waters of the Sixth Gradation of Extremity burned and turned into flows of authority that came to further elevate his existence and cause the propagation of even more Extremity Rune Scriptures.
