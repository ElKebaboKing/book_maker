Chapter 4435: The Scales of Law II

Far from their current location within The Collapse, in a domain of Law filled with brilliant silver-white grandeur of absolute order and systematic justice, an extraordinary scene was unfolding.

Alexander Asmodeus spun silently while suspended in mid-air, surrounded by potent Living Authority of Law that resonated with his existence, and additionally...concentrated Civilizational Authority of Law that marked him as legitimate inheritor rather!

Below him on silver marbled ground that reflected his spinning form with perfect clarity, Seraphina Valorheart panted with evident exhaustion.

Her existence seemingly could not handle the complete inheritance being offered, and her framework was lacking sufficient breadth to contain such concentrated authority.

Around Alexander, within his consciousness, the voice of THE Living Law rang out with a mixture of satisfaction.

"Different beings possess fundamentally different Breadths of Existence, the capacity to contain and express authority varies dramatically across individuals. Some are naturally born Leaders like Elderborns, possessing innate capability to coordinate vast resources."

"Others have uniquely expansive landscapes within their existential architecture, allowing them to house multiple competing authorities simultaneously."

The voice continued with analytical assessment.

"You could handle my full inheritance without catastrophic destabilization. That girl could not...her framework lacks necessary dimensional breadth, though she possesses other valuable qualities."

Alexander looked towards Seraphina Valorheart calmly as he heard these words echo out in his mind.

"She will be of substantial help to you in future endeavors, so do not abandon her merely because she cannot match your capacity. Loyalty has value beyond simple power."

The tone then shifted.

"But as of now, you hold a leading administrative position within my Primordial Civilization of Law. Even though a large portion of my Civilization was systematically collapsed by a coordinated assault, a fundamental truth remains: A Primordial Civilization can never be truly collapsed. Only suppressed, only diminished...never permanently eliminated."

Anticipation entered the voice as THE Living Law continued!

"After The Fallout and THE Loom opens, I will possess opportunity to rise again toward previous heights. But if you prove sufficiently exceptional in upcoming trials... you might be capable of surviving long enough to become a False THE before The Fallout even commences. That may give you the power to save others and whether through The Fallout. Maybe...maybe."

The voice became more practical.

"You wanted to be given genuine chance at significance rather than remaining scattered individual. This is it. You possess the Scales of Law innately within your existence now...whenever you encounter anyone, their true affiliations will be weighed."

"Anyone who was or currently is affiliated with forces from THE Loom will be revealed in your enhanced perception, allowing you to judge everyone with appropriate justice rather than making blind assessments."

Additional information followed as Alexander listened closely!

"To ensure your optimal success probability, you should also have gained the Compass of Law. It will guide you toward others who genuinely seek to impose and establish law as structured principle."

"Out there in broader existence, there should be remnants of other Collapsed Civilizations...survivors maintaining vestigial identities while lacking unified purpose or coordinated strategy."

The voice intensified with urgent conviction.

"If you can bring them together... collaboration among Collapsed Civilizations would dramatically increase the collective chances of survival and eventual restoration. Alone, we Living Existences were splintered and methodically picked off one by one by THE Living Paradox, THE Living Emotive, and others who joined their coalition."

"They collaborated with systematic coordination...and that unified approach was precisely how they forged THE Loom and achieved dominance!"

BOOM!

The final words boomed with emphasis.

"We must do the same. Collaboration. Collaboration. COLLABORATION!"

BOOM!

As these words echoed with tremendous weight, the surrounding brilliance of Law reached critical precipice as this entire domain began flickering!

Stability was failing as THE Living Laws consciousness prepared to fully withdraw or collapse from here.

Alexanders expression hardened with determination as he spoke with a voice of absolute conviction!

"Thank you for everything you have provided. Your efforts will not be wasted."

...!

With such a solemn declaration, the surrounding domain began fracturing comprehensively as Alexander and Seraphina Valorheart were enveloped by spatial light of tremendous intensity.

They disappeared from the collapsing realm!

WAP!

Moments later, they materialized with disorienting abruptness.

They had been directly transported to the Transcendent Law Folds.

Their appearance occurred in a region filled with expected silver-white brilliance of Law suggesting this was appropriate territory...

But alongside this established authority, there also seemed to be unusual influence of blue permeating the atmosphere!

Blue!

Alexander raised his hands to feel the essence as he tilted his head with confused assessment.

"Mana? Why would such extraordinarily pure Mana be covering the Transcendent Law Folds so comprehensively..."

He looked around while articulating such a question as Seraphina Valorheart frowned while also surveying their unexpected arrival location with tactical vigilance.

A moment later, Alexanders head snapped toward specific direction as above him, The Scales of Law manifested visibly and began being tinged with crimson coloration...indicating detection of an entity affiliated with a potent force from THE Loom!

He perceived what had been concealed: a

Crow in the distance observing them with unusual intensity!

The Crow stared back at Alexander with crimson eyes as if realizing it had been discovered despite its stealth measures, but rather than fleeing, the avian form transformed smoothly into devilish butler who regarded them with cold analytical eyes.

"Who are you? Why do you possess... such unusual authority radiating from your existence? That signature doesnt match any known Living Laws currently operating here..."

Alexanders eyes also turned cold as he responded with voice carrying judgment.

"The Scales of Law deem you... among those connected to THE Loom. Let me see... Emotive? What specific connection do you maintain to Emotive that would relate to THE Loom?"

HUUM!

The surroundings pulsed with sudden tension as the devilish butler, Malphas, began radiating power that caused Seraphina to gasp in shock at the magnitude, while Alexander Asmodeus maintained cold observation despite recognizing the genuine threat.

They just came out, and they meet someone this powerful?!

Malphas deliberately donned white gloves with a theatrical flare as if this white would soon be painted red, and he said with a voice mixing threat and curiosity.

"Ill be asking the questions today. No ordinary Living Laws would emanate the Civilizational Authority of Law you are displaying. You may be the ones actually connected to THE Loom. What are you truly?"

...!

Two powerful entities faced off at this critical moment as whether they would actually come to violent blows or find diplomatic resolution... remained uncertain!
