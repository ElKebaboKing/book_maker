Chapter 4921: Language II

The brilliance that unfolded around Noah in THE Interstices exceeded anything he had witnessed from his own existence before.

The swirling storm of Seals of Absolute Tongue transformed with every passing moment as numbers integrated themselves into the very fabric of his linguistic authority.

Archai began operating as equations that could describe and alter reality through calculation as much as declaration.

And prompts bloomed before his eyes with weight that pressed against his consciousness with the significance of what he had just achieved.

|Unprecedented Integration Achieved|

|As THE Native Speaker of THE First Tongue, you have successfully integrated numerical expression into your unique variation of linguistic authority. This integration does not alter THE First Tongue itself but creates a personal methodology exclusive to your weavings that allows mathematical operations to augment your existing capabilities. This new dimension of expression has been designated to be...THE Numerical Sovereignty.|

|THE Numerical Sovereignty operates through the following core principles:|

|Principle One: All expressions of THE First Tongue now carry inherent numerical values based on their complexity, authority investment, and foundational weight. These values can be perceived, manipulated, and amplified through mathematical operations that you apply during composition or activation.|

|Principle Two: Mathematical operations applied to your weavings do not consume additional authority proportional to their effect. Instead, they restructure how your existing authority expresses itself, creating amplification without equivalent expenditure. This is possible because numbers describe relationships rather than create substance.|

|Principle Three: THE Numerical Sovereignty augments your existing capabilities rather than replacing them. Embodiments remain Embodiments. Inscriptions remain Inscriptions. Archai remain Archai. But all of them can now be modified through numerical operations that multiply their effects in ways that would otherwise require exponentially greater authority investment.|

...!

Noah read over these foundational principles with eyes that burned brighter by the moment.

His own mind led the way, directing Harvests and Infinite Potential to flow across his existence as he maintained this glorious state of epiphany, his mind designing more and more things!

He thought how complex numbers could be as he forced them to be simple, to be compressed into something easily applicable for him!

More prompts unfolded as what he designed within himself became actualized.

|Core Operation One: Exponential Expression|

|You may now apply Exponents to any expression of THE First Tongue that you produce. When an Exponent is applied, the effect of that expression is multiplied by itself according to the value of the Exponent available. An Exponent of 2 causes the expression to multiply by itself once. An Exponent of 3 causes the expression to multiply by itself twice. And so forth.|

|Example Application: An Inscriptio Nominata targeting an enemys foundation would normally cause deterioration proportional to the authority invested. With an Exponent of 2 applied, that deterioration multiplies by itself, causing the effect to grow not linearly but geometrically. The target experiences not twice the deterioration but the deterioration multiplied by the deterioration, a compounding that far exceeds simple doubling.|

|Critical Understanding: Exponentiation is not multiplication. When you apply an Exponent of 2 to an attack that would deal 100 units of damage, you do not deal 200 units. You deal 100 multiplied by 100, resulting in 10,000 units. When you apply an Exponent of 3, you deal 100 multiplied by 100 multiplied by 100, resulting in 1,000,000 units. The scaling is not additive or multiplicative but exponential, growing at rates that quickly exceed comprehension.|

|This operation can be applied to: Embodiments, Apophasis, Inscriptions of all types, Archai of Existence, Harvests, defensive constructs, foundational weight expressions, and any other weaving of THE First Tongue you produce. At your current level, an Exponent of upto 3 may be possible. Higher expressions will require a qualitative change.|

...!

Noah felt his foundations tremble as he successfully set up the weavings of Exponential Expression.

An Inscription that would normally cause moderate damage to an enemys foundation could, with an Exponent of 3 applied, cause damage that exceeded what most shitty Absolutes like THE Living Elemental could survive. A defensive Embodiment that would normally block attacks from beings of equivalent foundation could, with an Exponent of 2 applied, block attacks from beings whose foundations exceeded his own by margins that should have been insurmountable.

And Harvests!

If he applied an Exponent to his Perpetual Harvest or his Rite of Paradoxical Inheritance, the resources he claimed from defeated enemies would multiply by themselves according to the Exponent chosen.

An enemy whose collapse would normally grant him theoretical 1000 units of foundational weight would grant him 1,000,000 units with an Exponent of 2 applied, or 1,000,000,000 units with an Exponent of 3.

THE Absolute Existential Palace of The Weavers, if he applied an Exponent of 2...would not be as if he doubled the rewards for killing an Absolute, but he effectively would be multiplying the rewards of a fallen Absolute...by themselves.

So not an Absolute times 2, but an Absolute times an Absolute!

"..."

He...really wanted to see what the result would be.

The scaling was terrifying!

More prompts continued.

|Core Operation Two: Multiplicative Amplification|

|You may now apply simple Multiplication to any expression of THE First Tongue that you produce. Unlike Exponentiation, Multiplication scales linearly but can be applied with values that exceed single digits. A Multiplier of 10 causes the expression to be 10 times more effective. A Multiplier of 100 causes it to be 100 times more effective. A Multiplier of 1000 causes it to be 1000 times more effective.|

|Multiplicative Amplification is less dramatic than Exponential Expression but more predictable and controllable. It is recommended for situations where precise scaling is desired rather than overwhelming force.|

|This operation can be combined with Exponentiation for compound effects. An expression with a Multiplier of 10 and an Exponent of 2 would first be multiplied by 10, then that result would be multiplied by itself. The compounding of operations creates amplification that exceeds either operation alone. Currently maximum Multiplication Value: 10|

...!

|Core Operation Three: Divisive Reduction|

|You may now apply Division to expressions of THE First Tongue that target you or your allies. When Division is applied to an incoming attack or effect, its potency is divided by the value chosen. An incoming attack divided by 10 retains only one-tenth of its original effect. An incoming attack divided by 100 retains only one-hundredth.|

|This operation provides defensive applications that complement the offensive applications of Exponentiation and Multiplication. Attacks that would normally devastate your existence can be reduced to negligible inconveniences through sufficient Division.|

|Note: Division cannot reduce effects to zero. The minimum result of any Division is an infinitesimally small but still present effect. Complete negation still requires Apophasis declaration rather than numerical reduction. Current maximum value for Division: 10|
