Chapter 2904 Acceleration IV

Change.

It was something hard to accept, but the truly powerful entities were those who accepted change without too much rejection or regret.

After seeing the brutal and dominant power expressed by the Extremity of Quintessence where they were forced to observe a reality where they couldn't even express their extremes, the Bard of Quintessence became the first to accept change and pioneered the way forward.

Other Primogenitors watched this scene as the renowned visage of Steel who had forged uncountable legends...sighed before his own existence began to emanate an intent.

It was a silent one, but moments later, vermillion gold blood flowed above him and wrapped around his visage as along with it...a light of Quintessence bloomed.

Quintessence!

Steel simply followed the weaving of his existence and it less him to this as at this moment, he imagined something grand.

He was an architect.

He was someone who built Armaments of Extremity with his own two hands.

At this moment, he imagined for himself what he could do with the Existential Extremity Authority of Quintessence.

He imagined what the production of Quintessential Armaments of Extremity would be!

So his pride and reservations were placed aside.

The pride and reservations of all Primogenitors would have to be placed aside if they wanted to move forward at this moment as akin to falling dominoes, one by one...they began to choose their Existential Extremity Authority.

Above this space, Noah floated grandly and looked at this scene as he felt the subtle changes that were coming as a result of all of this.

Vitalis and Vacuous Hyperversal Authorities were constantly being made powerful by these beings before him in the past, and now, he had brought the vast majority of them into the Infinite Hyperversal Haven as the refinement and strengthening of his own Existential Extremity Authorities...was about to reach another level.

He would now wait to see just what critical level they had to reach before the distant and unachievable dream of Fruits of Existence would actually come to fruition.

And the knowledge of exactly what wonders this fruit entailed to the point that it was made the central motivation of the Legacies of Al-Abalem and Ignatius who were overseeing these lands!

For this, the progression of power and refinement of Existential Extremity Authorities had to occur even faster.

And with the unfolding reality at this very moment...this pace would only accelerate!

At this moment of brilliance and wonder as many more entrants were truly branded inhabitants of the Infinite Hyperversal Haven, a prompt rose only to Noah's eyes that caused his eyes to shine with glory.

{A critical threshold has been reached after the assimilation of the obtained domains of Vitalis and Vacuous along with the inclusion of more Existences into the folds of Quintessence.}

{The possibility of forging High Grade Planalith Krystallos and High Grade Armaments of Extremity had opened.}

...!

High Grade.

This level was opening up before Noah minutes after his entrance into the Gradations of Extremity.

Krystallos and Armaments that matched the latter Gradations of Extremity!

His Infinite Hyperversal Haven pioneered the way forward ceaselessly every construct connected to Noah worked speedily to ensure their survival.

None of them truly knew what was coming, but they could all feel a sense of urgency as they moved faster and faster!

Basileus Anaine stood atop paradisiacal clouds of white, rivers of Vitalis swirling around her as they seemed to be extremely active and agitated.

She twirled these tendrils of Existential Extremity Authority in her hands as her pupils contained uncountable thoughts!

The purity of this authority around her was unmatched across the Aeonic Annals of Extremity, and today, it held a sense of wildness as if it were affected by something.

Something...that was about to be put in motion at this very moment.

|Mmm...| Her gaze was a pondering one as she thought about everything.

There were a variety of ways to go about resetting Existential Domains.

One could spend a somewhat significant amount of Fruits of Existence to activate a Legion of Dikastes of Extremity, an even more costly option was the descent of Basileus themselves.

Both of these were good, but they held a hint of unpredictability as there have been instances where the weavings of the reason a reset was required in the first place remained.

So if one truly wanted to have a complete reset and uproot any so called corruption within the Existential Domains, a more costly but definitive option was carried out through the Codex of Extremity.

The governing construct of any Existential Domains!

Everything that occured within it was recorded within the Codex as the methodology of Mors Apokalypsis was utilized to definitively wipe out and reset everything within a domain.

Nothing would be left behind as that which was in Existence, that which was in Non-existence, and everything in between would be erased.

Nothing escaped the game of the Codex of Extremity.

'So, if your Existential Extremity Authorities somehow overcome this...if you are able to survive a cleansing at such a fundamental level...then we shall see of the opportunities opened up with this, Noah Osmont.'

...!

The thoughts of Basileus Anaine were her own as at this moment...

HOONG!

A blaring sound reverberated from the center of the Archaeos Vitalis Pylos as all streams of Vitalis Existential Extremity Authorities changed directions and converged towards a single point.

All massive cylindrical pillars of glorious white light released tendrils and changed directions.

Basileus Anaine became stern at such a moment as she couldn't imagine what Archon Basileus Al-Abalem was facing as he initiated such a process, the resources he had taken from Archon Basileus Ignatius and his own being the key to starting all of this!

RUUUMBLE!

The Dikastes of Extremity that weren't hibernating rose and gazed at the changes as they knew.

Basileus Anaine knew.
