Chapter 2659 The Ash Tree of Gradation! I

A grand seed!

A Quintessential Hyperversal Sporos of Extremity that for the feat of attaining the 17th Layer of Quintessence, Noah gained an entirely matured one.

Its purpose was twofold as through it, he could go towards a Quintessential Hyperversal Extremity Soul. And through it, the hopes of building 9 Foundations to achieve Extremity became more realistic!

He could choose any of the Havenly Authorities corresponding to Havens of Extremity as enough of such authority to forge a complete Haven would then be granted.

And this was only half of the essence contained in the Quintessential Hyperversal Sporos of Extremity.

Noah couldn't take his eyes off of it as even though he held it within his palm, it felt denser and heavier than anything he had ever held onto before!

His soul cried out for it as he didn't outline it just yet.

He was waiting for a confirmation of something critical.

His luminous eyes that didn't look away from the Sporos of Extremity reflected the scenes within two different locations.

One was in the Vaults of the Sima Clan, and the other was within the Infinite Oni Haven.

Here, his two clones still held their hands on the Iron Heart and the Hyperversal Bow of Helios as the extremely Pure Hyperversal Extremity Authority flowed into him and coiled on the Sources of Foundation and Helios.

And yet...

They only flowed and cycled, but did not grant any changes.

Pure waves of Helios and Foundation flowed into him as they resonated, but that was it!

No further advancement.

Extremity was not so easy that only by continuing to absorb Pure Hyperversal Extremity Authority would take one towards it!

If this was the case, Clans and Inheritances would have produced millions of Extremities.

But even with this reality, Noah still wanted to test his limits as his hand that was touching the Bow of Helios moved- his intention being to take hold of the Armament of Extremity!

To wield such an Armament, one had to have attained Extremity.

So if someone who did not have this authority try...

KAAA!

A piercing cry rang out from the Bow of Helios as if it were a living thing, a burst of molten Helios Authority erupting out as an instant later, Noah's right hand was entirely missing- burnt and scorched out of existence!

It was a shocking scene as up to his shoulder, immense waves of heat simmered- and he couldn't even heal this hand fully as it felt like minuscule records of it were almost erased!

"..."

His gaze was brilliant as he gazed at the terrifying Bow of Helios.

Behind him, a shout came from Ayame who witnessed this scene.

"Are you okay?"

He turned back with a smile as the opportunity of the Infinite Oni Haven had granted what it could, and it showed a harsh reality that to go from the 17th Layer and reach Extremity...was an unscalable wall that wouldn't be breached so simply.

The same applied to the Iron Heart that radiated Foundation!

After Noah affirmed this, the eyes of his main body blinked.

The scenes showing his two clones within Helios and Genesis faded as his gaze was only on the Sporos of Extremity in his hands.

His ticket towards Extremity...if he could utilize it well!

If he had the resources for it.

As his eyes gazed at the completely matured Quintessential Hyperversal Sporos of Extremity, he released silent waves of Quintessence of this seed and uttered out simply.

"Genesis."

HUUM!

An aurora of multicolored light began to blaze around the Sporos of Extremity.

This blinding light rose along with the seed from Noah's hands as it came to float in the space before him, beginning to spin like a singularity that would birth a whole Haven as from it, a milky white light of utmost purity began to pulse!

Genesis.

Noah had many options to choose from as with the imbalance of the Fifth Aletheian Haven of Extremity being entirely composed of Helios so far with a corner of it being Dissolution, he could have chosen the Havenly Authority of Dissolution to bring back a sense of balance between the authorities making up this Haven. But the grander option was to open up access to his Third Havenly Authority.

He had the Boundary of Genesis from the musings of Orion that he made his own, but he didn't have the Havenly Boundary of Genesis.

He didn't have Genesis as it related to the Authority of the Haven!

And so...

THUMP!

Like a flourishing organ coming to life, the Sporos of Extremity thumped while radiating pure milky white light, a sprout gloriously rising from the seed with seven leaves that all shone with a pristine light of Genesis.

The sprout then became larger and larger as even though it didn't cause any surging waves of essence in the surroundings, reality itself creaked at the sheer pressure and grandeur that was contained in this emerging sprout!

A sprout that grew to be over a light year in an instant, the Sources of Chaos in the surroundings being calmed as within moments, the thousands and tens of thousands of light years around Noah within this Apollyon Aletheian Haven where he was discussing Extremity with Xiaomi...they all became utterly still and docile.

The Sporos of Extremity sprouted as the Ash Tree of Gradation came to be.

And during its first appearance, the entropy of reality and everything else was brought to a halt.

The active and fiery Apollyon Aletheian Haven was calmed and became docile starting from this point and throughout the whole Haven, a shocking scene arising for those worthy enough to feast their eyes on it.

It was a scene of an Ash Tree of Gradation shimmering with illusory milky white light as its trunk rose throughout the Apollyon Aletheian Haven, its innumerable branches and leaves spreading out across the very peak of this Haven!

With a contained pressure and power, its innumerable branches and leaves moved as they dropped down towards a single being!
