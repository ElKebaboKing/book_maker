Chapter 5177: Ego III

The capabilities of THE Absolute Superbius Ego of Infinity continued to unfold before him!

|THE Infallible Aegis of Pride: Damage directed at your figure is reduced by ninety-nine percent if the source of the attack is deemed lesser by your Ego, for the concept of Pride refuses to acknowledge the validity of a strike from an inferior being, rendering their efforts into nothing but wasted motion.|

|THE Monarchs Pulse of Infinity: Your Infinite Light is amplified by the weight of your Superbius, causing the regression of targets to occur ten times more potent and faster while simultaneously inflicting a state of existential despair that prevents the activation of any defensive contingencies.|

|THE Absolute Supremacy Radiation of Infinity: Your passive Existential Radiation has been refined through the forge to broadcast your supremacy as an immutable fact of existence, ensuring that any being within range involuntarily perceives their own foundations as fundamentally lesser while their Egos find themselves unable to maintain their engineered amplifications in your presence. As the radiation permeates the local substrate, the confidence of your enemies collapses and their Civilizations and skills operate at a drastically reduced efficiency because the beings wielding them can no longer believe fully in their own capacity while you occupy the same coordinate, and even other weak Gilded Ones of the Superbius classifications may feel their very Existence shrink as your existence dictates the terms of the encounter through the sheer weight of your singular sovereignty.|

Oh!

The capabilities were grand as Noah couldnt help but smack his lips!

Okay.

Okay!

Noah then turned his gaze toward THE Absolute Humilitas Ego of Infinity, which stood in stark contrast to the first, appearing as a figure of quiet and terrifying simplicity with its hands folded and its head bowed slightly in a gesture of total release.

This manifestation radiated a stillness so profound that it felt like a void in the atmosphere, a state of existence that had discarded every layer of self until only the core of his Infinity remained as a silent and watchful observer.

|THE Absolute Humilitas Ego of Infinity is the inverse of Pride, and it grants the following capabilities:|

|THE Void of Surrender: Your existence becomes imperceptible to the intent and perception of others, for a being with no Pride provides no surface for enmity or detection to find purchase upon, allowing you to move through existence as a phantom of the Absolute. It is estimated that those on Vakochevs Third Scale and below may entirely not be able to perceive you.|

|THE Reflective Mirror of the Humble: Attacks directed at you are not resisted but are instead accepted and reflected back toward the source with double their original intensity, utilizing the lack of self-assertion as a catalyst for a perfect and effortless counter that requires no movement.|

|THE Absolute Erasure of the Self: By surrendering your ego, you can phase through any barrier or binding that relies on identifying the target, for the bindings of the Gilded cannot find a person where no person exists, permitting you to navigate the most restricted layers of the Braneworld as if they were empty air.|

|As of now, the Egos of Superbius and Humilitas are the only ones available for your immediate transformation, with their abilities capable of expansion and addition as more infinity is infused into them, and the remaining twelve are still being designed and infused with the necessary concentrations of Infinity to achieve their Absolute states within the forge.|

Noah read the prompts as his eyes blazed with a dark and calculating brilliance, for he could already see how these two states would allow him to navigate the dangerous waters of the Braneworld with a versatility that no other Gilded One could match.

He looked at the two available transformations and could not help but nod his head as a sharp smile crossed his features, his mind already busy selecting which face he would show!

"Good. Good! Good!!"

HUUM!

As Noah was looking at the nondescript form of Humilitas, a piercing scream echoed out to ruin the calmness of this space as like a meteor, a manic existence thundered down into the forge where her Heart was!

"AAAH!"

BOOM!

The impact sent ripples of multicolored light across the crystalline floors of this grand workshop, and the air itself groaned under the sudden weight of a presence that was as chaotic as it was undeniably powerful.

Noah sighed lightly while the remnants of the impact settled around the intruder, and he whispered to himself as if he were reciting a mantra intended to preserve his patience.

"She gave her Everything for you," he murmured, his voice barely a breath against the humming of the Absolute Forge. "She gave her Everything...she gave her Everything..."

After repeating this three times and ensuring that his pragmatic temper was sufficiently anchored, he turned to see her.

The figure that rose from the center of the impact was THE Living Emotive, but she was no longer the skittish being who had crumbled at the base of the mountain, for her infusion with his Quintessence Infiniforce had triggered a transformation that made her power burn with a shocking brilliance!

She now stood firmly within THE Transitional Cambrian stage between THE Second Scale and THE Third Scale, her aura radiating a density of authority that would have made even a high-tier Primordial Architect tremble in her presence.

She looked around with the skittish and frenetic intensity of a rabbit caught in a high-tier snare before her focus finally sharpened onto the figure of Noah, her eyes lighting up with a manic brilliance that caused her body to instinctively surge forward.

She appeared as if she wanted to climb toward him and anchor herself to his existence as she had done on the mountain, yet she managed to hold herself back by gripping her own shoulders while her manic violet hair cycled through a strobe light of emotional colors.

She breathed in and out with a visible effort to control her own manic impulses before she finally rose up grandly, her gaze sweeping across the vast and immaculate expanse of the forge while her eyes eventually settled on her own pulsing Absolute Amaranthine Heart at the center of the room.

Her eyes began to sparkle with a renewed and terrifying fanaticism as she took in the fourteen massive illusory Egos of Infinity that surrounded her heart, and she voiced her question with a voice that was thick with wonder.

"Sir, where are we?"

Noah looked at her for a moment while noting that her eyes were shining far too brilliantly for his comfort, and he simply offered another heavy sigh before waving his hand toward her.

A single, concentrated drop of multicolored Infinity surged from his finger and entered her body, carrying with it the partial weight of the information regarding this place and the architecture of his internal reality, for she had her heart in this place and deserved to understand the significance of where she stood!
