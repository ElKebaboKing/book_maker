Chapter 622 - Ruination Core!!!

"What is that...?"

Noah asked with trembling words as he sensed this unknown feeling of pressure coming from the thing floating out of the obsidian core of the Novus Galaxy, being able to notice its shape being square as it seemed to be filled with multiple holes that led to nowhere!

A square item with countless holes, shining with a fearsome red and black light!
