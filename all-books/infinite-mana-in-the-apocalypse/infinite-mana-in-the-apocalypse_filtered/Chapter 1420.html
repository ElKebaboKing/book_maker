Chapter 1418: You Are Already Dead! I

Cthulhu's crimson eyes the size of stars watched the ecstatic scene in the Domains of Apocalypse as immense emotion burned within him.

It was the scene of a single existence wreathed in snaking Flames of Edicts standing against a golden green Celestial Vine that was vaster than a Cosmos!

His clones in the forms of terrifying Telos moved in the surroundings as the domains were held up by four glimmering Towers of Ragnarok. Towers that released arcs of origin destroying essence of Apocalypse in the forms of unstable Universes constantly! Towers that released the repulsive essence of Pandemonium that seemed to want to devour everything around it!

Within the Domains that these Towers of Ragnarok were centered around, Cthulhu could see the unique essence of Eldritch Terrors that made his bloodline burn with excitement- as if this essence was the final direction he should be heading towards! As he gazed at everything, the feeling of wanting to grasp such a power within his own hands was extremely pervasive as he wasn't the only one.

Barbatos had the same gaze.

Halcyon had the same gaze. The Oathkeeper. The Emperor Penguin...many beings had fervent gazes of wanting to experience such a stellar battle, but they were too weak to do so! And yet at this moment...each of them felt a calling.

A calling from the Domains of Apocalypse as one of the features of the Towers of Ragnarok was to call upon the vassals of the Seeker of Apocalypse and grant them the identity of Heralds of Apocalypse, their lives being tied to the Seeker of Apocalypse as if Noah does not perish, the Herald of Apocalypse will not!

They could die over and over again within the Domains of Apocalypse as while they were reinforced by the essence of Apocalypse and the support of the now fully materializing Eldritch Terrors...the battle power they could display was rather remarkable.

OOOM!

One by one, they began to answer the call as their figures disappeared in a flash of light!

When they opened their eyes again, they felt their bodies budding off from a pristine black tower, surrounded by the essence of a Quasi-Insuperable Nomological Edict as boundless power coursed through them! When their eyes gazed at everything at this close range, they sucked in shocked breaths as they saw a terrifying Outer Eldritch at the Cosmic Realm wading through the Domain of Apocalypse on an illusory old ship the size of a Universe, this Eldritch shrouding his humanoid form with a cloak of darkness as one could see feelers and tentacles blooming horrifically from its face.

In another direction, another Unique Outer Eldritch also materialized as he released scorching waves of heat akin to a sun, but the light released was entirely obsidian in color as it caused increasing darkness.

This was the battle they were given access to help in and experience! A total of four Outer Eldritchs at the Cosmic Realm were fully materialized as they supported the back of a single being who had a crimson halo surrounding him- a halo that was currently trying to suppress an untouchable figure of the Celestial Vine Race!

"OOOOOH!"

Such an apocalyptic scene and battle caused all of those that had answered the call to be Heralds of Apocalypse to move out fervently as they acted like small Universes containing the essence of apocalypse to smash towards the vibrant Celestial Vine. Death was of no consequence to them, so they might as well say they battled against a Cosmic Filament Rank Primordial with the help of their Emperor!

BZZZT!

The uncountable number of vines sprouting around the Celestial Vine went to smash towards the Telos, Outer Eldritchs, Heralds of Apocalypse, and the countless unstable universes filled with apocalypse sent forth by the Towers of Ragnarok.

The two central vines of the Primordial that were armed with two sharp Quasi-Primordial Relics...these weaved towards Noah with utter malice and fervor as they actually ruptured apart reality itself where they crossed!

Nion gazed at such a scene with clarity as under his collective six eyes, it was coming in at a very slow motion.

He watched the two Quasi-Primordial Relics with a fiery gaze. These were Quasi-Primordial Relics! Just the Eonic Bell of the Unguarded allowed Hyperion to bring down the Realm of a Primordial. These Circlets that Cecilia was using...Nion knew he could not take on their offensive damage!

Or it was more correct to say that he couldn't take them on...yet.

"Big guy, it's time for you to go to sleep."

As a terrific Primordial returned to her True Form and threw a spacetime rupturing attack towards him, Noah uttered such a sentence to the existence that shared his origin and soul currently.

The Primordial had merged with her Ascendancy Halo to return to her most powerful state- reverting to an elevated version of her Celestial Vine race as the power she wielded could erase existences at the level of time and space as she wielded her Quasi-Primordial Relic Circlets! Now...Nion had to also fuse with the Ascendancy Halo of Catastrophe and revert to an elevated version of his own True Form.

But due to a myriad of factors that made Noah the main contributor of the Ascendancy Halo, fusing with it to grant the True Form only allowed a single consciousness to remain active in order for one to truly obtain the True Form! For this, Hyperion's consciousness had to sleep.

OOM!

Within Nion's six eyes, a trace of Hyperion could be seen as his voice rang out in the collective consciousness.

"I will wake up free of the shackles of this reality?"

A grandiose question that Noah's consciousness answered simply!

"You will wake up free."

With these words, one of the consciousnesses of the two fused beings dimmed. The Eonic Bell of the Unguarded remained powered up and attached to Nion's body as its effects continued on its target.

RUMBLE!

The bloody Ascendancy Halo released a burst of excited light as while watching in slow motion the coming of a terrific attack of two Quasi-Primordial Relic Circlets that were cutting apart Reality itself, the crimson Halo instantly receded and fused into the body of its Master!
