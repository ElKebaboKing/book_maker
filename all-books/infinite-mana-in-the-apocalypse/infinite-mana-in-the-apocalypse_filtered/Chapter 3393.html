Chapter 3391 How to Kill an Existence Defying Being? I

Palace Master Antiope stared at the scene of the Infiniverse Dowager and the Origin Sacred Primal Megalonian with extremely stern eyes.

They had, at times, been seen together.

They had worked together multiple times as they were believed to be extremely complex entities whose goals aligned, and so they worked together!

But...they actually had such a history? Even before they Severed their Existence and Pioneered their own paths, they were always together?

This information caused different parts of her mind to light up as the Infiniverse Dowager was terrifying enough.

The Origin Sacred Primal Megalonian...this Tyrant one one that many beings feared!

|The one responsible for your daughter is truly no ordinary being. If Palace Master Evrin holds trust in me, we will continue doing this my way, where we observe and follow for the next few days. After that...we will make a move that will ensure to give you closure.|

She kept her voice calm as she spoke to the Palace Master whose murdered Daughter of Existence was the root cause of this whole unfolding reality.

And Evrin only nodded as even though he knew there was something much more there, he didn't ask!

---

Within another area in the Immovable Sacred Palace of the Expanse.

Palace Master Antiope portrayed the same scene she observed from the Ordinance Amalgamation Entities currently observing a certain Blancverse- the illusory image that showed the Infiniverse Dowager and the Origin Sacred Primal Megalonian!

|They obviously have a greater history than we came to know, but yes. He is here too. In these weavings. This chance...|

|It is not a chance as much as what we need to do has become twice as hard- nay, ten times as hard. One Infiniverse Dowager is terrifying enough, but adding the Origin Sacred Primal Megalonian?|

...!

Before Palace Master Antiope.

The only Pioneer of Existence amongst them spoke out as his gaze was one full of thought and caution.

Heracles!

Palace Master Antiope gazed at him and the only other entity to come here with them- Nisa, her gaze currently a somber one.

Palace Master Antiope breathed calmly as she spoke once more.

|I am not underestimating them, really. But again, we have a Pioneer and Existence Severing entities on our side while they have the power of Ordinance Amalgamation currently. Ordinance Amalgamation! Besides fearing so much of who they might become in the future, let us come up with ways to kill these beings that seem to defy Existence itself!|

WUU!

Her words were resonant.

Around the three of them, dazzling green leaves flowed and wrapped around everything as above Pioneer Heracles, a vibrant green rune rotated calmly.

A Constellation Rune of Existence that read- Quarantine!

It was spinning with a vibrant glow, but the level of complexity held within this single rune exceeded the complexity that all the weavings of Existence Severing could radiate.

It isolated everything about themselves and what they talked about here as with it, even a terrifyingly perceptive entity wouldn't be able to feel an inkling of a bad feeling or sensation about them being plotted against, or even the threat of death.

All possible weavings of existence were quarantined!

Pioneer Heracles gazed at the fluttering stellar green leaves around them as he spoke out with an ancient voice.

|The Wheel turns as the Wheel turns. Maybe...we shouldn't do anything at all. Maybe this is how events are supposed to flow. Our entry into these Weavings of Existence already violates enough. If we make truly significant moves...|

|We are in these weavings of existence because we ran away! From beings like them!|

Palace Master Antiope felt immense rising frustration as she pointed towards the illusory pictures of Infiniverse Dowager and the Origin Sacred Primal Megalonian.

|Please, Heracles. We have already gone through this and arrived at this step. And when it comes to killing...there are many ways for it that are not just purely killing.|

...!

|Oh?|

The brows of Heracles arose as beside him, Nisa looked towards Antiope questioningly.

|If these are the Source Weavings of Existence of the Infiniverse Dowager and the Origin Sacred Primal Megalonian, we simply have to ensure they do not continue. Apart from seeking to kill them- something we are taking many precautions for, we can also do something a step below in difficulty while achieving the same result. We can...imprison them.|

WAA!

Imprisonment?

This word caused the eyes of Nisa and Pioneer Heracles to gradually shine before they radiated a terrifying light!

Nisa even rose up as the leaves around them fluttered while she asked.

|You don't mean...that place?|

That place.

Something they had only heard about, and their biggest fear before they gave up a tremendous amount of their power to take the risk of jumping into a different frequency of Existence!

The eyes of Heracles pulsed with brilliance as he also seemed to be thinking. He thought of legends from cycles past that were inherited as horror stories regarding the vastness and immensity of the Wheel of Existence! Horrors not meant to terrify children, but any entities who dared to think of traversing the different Weavings of the Wheel of Existence.

|A place feared by even the most complex beings that have communed with the Wheel of Existence. Any entities who sought it forever disappeared from any records...you think that Frequency of the Wheel we nearly fell into was it?|

...!

Something that made even the most complex of entities fearful.

At their level of power, very few things, if any, could make such entities fearful!

Yet those who truly knew spoke in hushed whispers about a place that was part of the Wheel...and also not.

About a frequency that existed and did not truly exist as there were no weavings of it. And yet when those with weavings fell into it...they never returned.

Antiope looked at her two compatriots as her eyes didn't hold any hesitation.

Before all of this, in the past, they were all equals. She looked at Pioneer Heracles who had pulled ahead now as she spoke with confidence!
