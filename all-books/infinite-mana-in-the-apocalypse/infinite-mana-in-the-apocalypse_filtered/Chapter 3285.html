Chapter 3283 The Wheel Turns! V

Something that had only ever appeared in the Megalos Beyond.

Something that the Great Beyonder put in place for the Outerversal Morphons to overcome the complexity of their existence to reach higher and higher stages!

At this moment, it truly landed in the hands of someone from another place entirely.

At this time, Noah's visage of the throne closed his eyes as he fell into a long dream.

As if he had been asleep for far too long.

When he woke up, he found himself in the middle of a bloody battlefield.

Around him, all kinds of sounds blared as rivers of crimson gold blood flowed freely on the shattered domain of existence he was on.

Far above, stellar auroras of multicolored light converged as battles were still erupting out, his eyes that belonged to a Paracosm Stage Entity observing Protectors of the Commonwealth facing off against horrific Worms filled with obsidian radiance.

As they fought, they radiated chants that seemed to give them power- illusory wheels appearing around them with terrifying brilliance as they stopped the advancement of the Worms of Existence!

|The purity of existence is what keeps the Wheel turning! O natives of Omniverse X-1038, make sure your existence is pure and seeking a single thing!|

...!

Omniverse X-1038.

A number exceeding X-999.

This Dream...was shockingly a dream of a time that had not even come yet.

It was terrific to come across as Noah felt his body that held a mess of a Conceptual System of Existence filled with a myriad of concepts.

Death. Regality. Destruction. Water. Chaos...

There were many concepts making up this body of a Paracosm soldier.

But after hearing the words of the Protectors above, he wanted to see this conjecture of purity he had heard before that kept the wheel turning.

So he looked at the mess of concepts inside of him...and destroyed them all entirely as from their remnants...

'Quintessence.'

WAA!

His soul remembered Quintessence clearly.

He had lived it. He had forged it.

Like a lost seed, it quickly began to bloom as in the future, he would only focus on Quintessence for all his weavings of existence.

The Purity of existence kept the Wheel turning.

He would put this to a test as within Omniverse X-1038, life moved on.

A year passed, and the Paracosm body he occupied became an Autarch. He was adorned with the distinction of the Quintessential Autarch!

The Protectors of the Commonwealth guarded this Omniverse as he found that at a certain point in the past, changes had occurred that allowed Omniverses to be discovered rapidly by the Commonwealth as their reach only became stronger.

They pushed back the Worms of Existence within this Omniverse, but they seemed unkillable as they always came back and ravaged this Omniverse and many more.

Their numbers propagated much more rapidly than before, and their power was even grander!

The Omniverse he was in turned into one of constant wars and death as in such a state, hundreds of years began to pass.

The distinction of an Apogee Autarch was attained rather quickly as in these hundreds of years, Quintessence was solely focused on to the extent that its complexity...even became capable of affecting the Worms of Existence that tore apart this Omniverse!

In all these years, he was finally able to join the ranks of Protectors as the first thing he sought to do was to track down Omniverse X-999. Would it have himself? Or did he not exist in this Dream entirely? The day he became a Protector, he expressed curiosity towards the older Protectors of the Commonwealth stationed here about expanding his knowledge and visiting other Omniverses, expressing curiosity about X-999 especially!

The Simian Protector looked at him with a sharp gaze at such words before he replied.

|Omniverse X-999...is an anomaly that is still unsolved by the Commonwealth. It has disappeared within the Megalos Ynnere as even now, it is nowhere to be found.|

...!

Shocking words.

Ones that acted akin to a trigger as at this point, the Quintessential Protector felt his mind become hazy.

He almost felt like he was slumbering, and yet at the same time he was awake...but somewhere else.

His gaze became divided.

This dream still continued, but he felt a part of himself opening his eyes within another Dream entirely.

A dream still continued, but answer one started!

This was...was in the Megalos Beyond.

A star shaped Outerversal Morphon opened its single eye as around him, the auras of terrifying Morphons pulsed heavily.

Among them, multiple intents echoed out.

|Is it true? That the Great Beyonder has constructed an unbeatable Crucible on our Pantheon?|

...!

The words instantly caused Noah's single eye to shine brilliantly as he awoke fully.

Another Dream.

And in this Dream, it seemed to be countless cycles ago...before the Great Beyonder had disappeared!

Memories flooded into his mind.

He was Outerversal Morphon Starbright, a native of the Megalos Pantheon of Xerxes.

The groups of Outerversal Morphons he was with were entities who frequently moved with him within this Pantheon, and they were currently in the Undersun Sanctuary that was akin to a citadel ten times the size of an Existential Domain.

Massive obsidian structures and statues filled this place as currently, it was abuzz with the news and weavings of the strongest Outerversal Morphon.

The unfathomable Great Beyonder!

|The Crucible...apparently our Pantheon is not the first. In the Pantheon of Umbra, the Crucible of the Amarantos Dream of the Megalos was built- nobody knows anything about it as any who have tried it have failed.|

|Oh? And the Crucible here?|

The voices and intents rose with deep power and curiosity as a single answer came.

|The Crucible of the Sword of Megalos...|

...!

Noah's weavings buzzed as he came across the mention of a third Crucible.

All of it was mixed with unknown mysteries as the being responsible for it all...was present in his current Dream!

Who...was the Great Beyonder?

A dreamer questioned as he delved fully into another unfathomable Dream.

Present time, outside of the Dream a certain Emperor traversed in.

Within the Megalos Beyond, in a Megalos Pantheon.

Within a large obsidian deluge of a river that snaked across a massive mountain, hundreds of small squid like Outerversal Morphon trembled as they gazed up to look at an amalgamation of black flames above them.

They trembled not just because of the power he radiated, but also because this was the being who had forcefully bound them as Delegatus not too long ago!
