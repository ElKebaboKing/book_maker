In the Transcendent Origin Folds.

Above where Aeternitas Concordia used to be.

Above the white gold space...

The Living Origins in the surroundings, who had been rebuilding Concordia while systematically erasing any Dead Existences that attempted revival, looked up with shock.

And they began to hear it.

A harmony. A chorus singing with the descent of these unknown entities!

"These are the weapons existence created,

Born from pressure that could not be contained..."

The melody accompanied their arrival like a soundtrack to an existential event, and the Primus Inevitabilities waved their hands with synchronized motion to allow hundreds of thousands of Sparks of The Way of Mana to flow outward.

The Sparks surrounded Living Origins like twinkling stars...each one carrying invitation to join the Primordial Civilization of Mana and Hunger.

With the terrifying level of power and aura these entities portrayed, each one radiating 100 Quintillion Complexity and Purity, and how they silently looked upon the Living Origins with crimson eyes...

Regardless if they were Dukes, Honored Living Origins, Royal Living Origins, or mere Fold Dwellers, all of them felt tremor in their existence.

Fear. Recognition.

They reached out to grab these Sparks.

What choice did they have? When beings of such overwhelming power offered opportunity rather than destruction?

The Primus Inevitabilities were that terrifying. That compelling.

"Why couldnt things be left alone?

Why couldnt existence remain free?"

In the Transcendent Law Folds.

The Heart of Law.

An expanse of silver-white citadel stretched out many Gigaparsecs in size.

Billions of Living Laws dwelled within its structured boundaries, countless Transcendent Wheels of Existence orbited in perfect formation, and just an overabundance of ordered life proliferated throughout.

They could sense an extremely pure aura of Law around her!

How grand were the Living Laws about to become from here on out? How great would their progress be into the secrets of THE Loom?

As they were contemplating these possibilities with eager anticipation...

"Now blades are echoing across the void..."

In such grand place filled with structured magnificence, thousands of the Primus Inevitabilities manifested grandly above the Heart of Law itself.

Each one burned with terrifying presence of 100 Quintillion Complexity and Purity...power that exceeded anything the Living Laws had witnessed in their carefully ordered existence.

Their appearance was followed by the melody and chorus echoing out all around this place too.

"Now vast swathes will bleed with mana and hunger,

You asked for weapons, you forged them in pain..."

The titanic Primus Inevitabilities descended with synchronized precision, their obsidian-blue-and-gold forms casting shadows across the silver-white citadel that seemed to stain its perfect order.

Seraphina Valorhearts expression changed as she looked upward...her face turning ashen.

These entities possessed power that made her recent inheritance from THE Living Law seem modest by comparison.

Alexander Asmodeus maintained stern expression as he and the Living Laws before him felt the overbearing sense of power that seemed capable of eclipsing everything they had built.

His Scales of Law pulsed with readings that made no logical sense...beings that powerful shouldnt exist outside THE Loom, especially in auch large numbers as...there seemed to be thousands of such beings here?

Where could a terrifying force of thousands of such powerful beings stem from?!

There was no answer.

But...

All Primus Inevitabilities possessed the capability of Civilizational Resonance.

The grandness of this ability manifested gloriously as while the Primus Inevitabilities floated above the Heart of Law, the authority of their Primordial Civilization began painting over the established authority of Law itself.

Blue-gold light washed across silver-white structures.

Obsidian tendrils of influence wove through ordered formations.

It was as if a new existential order was being instituted...one that could subsume or overwhelm the existing framework through sheer weight of accumulated power.

"These... are simply the Blades and Shields of The Master. They come bearing gifts rather than threats, though I understand the confusion."
