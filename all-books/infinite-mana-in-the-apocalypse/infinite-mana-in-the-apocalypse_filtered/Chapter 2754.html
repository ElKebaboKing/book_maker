2752 The Demon of the Seven Havens! I

A promise.

This was what the True Emperor of Quintessence brought down as at this moment, the thrones representing Esteemed Havenly Emperors of the Avernus Clan burst into righteous purple flames of indignation.

The surroundings buzzed with power as multiple other wills and pressure from entities at the Fourth Gradation of Extremity descended on Noah!

But this was merely pressure.

They had yet to make a move on him with their authorities. And yet even under this pressure, Noah was undaunted as his Extremity Soul buzzed in response.

His soul was unique. It stood far apart from normal Extremity Souls as it was Quintessential!

So how could the pressure emanating from a Soul that had stepped on the Fourth Gradation of Extremity make him stand down? The only one he was cautious of was the Entity that had yet to make any moves or say anything towards him- the Rhetorician of the Senatus of Extremity.

A second passed.

The illusory scene Noah conjured stayed up for this second before it faded away as in this period of time, all Entities here computed and had millions of thoughts cross their minds.

The promise of this Entity seated on an illusory throne as well as the Blood Sources and his connection to the Infernal Havenly Commerce that allowed him to forcefully cause whoever the Primogenitor of this Lineage was to exclude the Avernus Clan...it showed that this being's standing and position in the Supernal Havens of Extremity was not low!

Seeing his Soul give off waves of Hyperversal Extremity Authority when they couldn't see any Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Rune Scriptures was another piece of the puzzle.

All in all, his demeanor and words, and whatever force stood behind him were one of the main things holding back an eruption of authorities at this moment.

Instead, the gaze of the golden haired Speaker became ever chillier as as her voice rang out.

[There are many paths we can take in our Records. Taking on a path of internal fighting and conflict...is not a wise decision. Those behind you should know this. The Primogenitors of the Nether Havens and the Primogenitors of the Supernal Havens have been protecting these lands over the eons that have passed together. Forces of the Supernal Havens never fought against our forces...nor did we kill each other based on vendettas that the other side is seeking to bury. The Avernus Clan seeks to bury this vendetta, this war ongoing between the both of you, and you would rather choose Annihilation?]

...!

She mentioned great secrets and the identities of beings far greater than any of those who sat here.

Of Primogenitors that Noah only knew a few of from the names of Legends they left behind.

Of Entities like Extremity Steel!

At her words, Noah shook his head with a smile while replying.

[How do you forget that this began as an Apex War of Annihilation? Before that War began, I warned all forces trying to exterminate me and my people. Some forces were wise and listened, and other forces chose to push forward. They made a choice then as this...is my choice now. The complete disbarment and Annihilation of the Avernus Clan.]

WAA!

His words were irrefutable.

Immense silence descended as the Speaker's eyes now pulsed with liquified golden light, her voice once more sounding out!

[Taking on this path of conflict with our own people makes you no better than the Demons we face every day. And yet you come from among us as even with all your self-proclaimed titles and distinctions...the only thing you will be known by will be as another Demon.]

Her voice was becoming piercing as she continued.

[Is that the Legacy you wish to leave behind as we start yet another great war with our true enemies? A Demon who is even worse than them- A Demon of these Seven Havens?!]

...!

Her piercing words clapped out like thunder!

And before any other voice could continue...

[Enough.]

HOOONG!

The booming voice of the Rhetorician of the Senatus of Extremity who had been silent this entire time rang out.

From his throne, a terrifying pressure rose as reality twisted and reformed from its mere eruption.

An interminable number of Crimson Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Rune Scriptures swirled around this Entity like an endless sea as under his thundering voice, the Speaker pulled back and sat down on her throne.

Nobody else uttered a word as at this moment, Noah raised his brows while coming to gaze at this being.

Emanating a sense of immense vitality and handsomeness, his golden hair danced on his head like rivers of reality as his eyes swirled with Vitalis Hyperversal Authority!

His eyes fully came to lock onto Noah at this moment as he spoke out slowly.

[Who are you to the Infernal Havenly Commerce? To the Primogenitor of the Havenly Hyperversal Emperor Lineage?]

...!

He didn't speak regarding the Avernus Clan, but on Noah's own Identity!

And Noah only smiled at this as he shook his head.

[Does it truly matter in the end? One way or another, I have the authority that I have, and I do what I want.]

His Will could not be swayed.

He made this known as in their minds, they still didn't ascertain his identity to the force that he himself has enacted in the Supernal Havens!

The gaze of the Rhetorician remained calm at Noah's reply as he spoke again.

[You can select the terms and price you wish with the Avernus Extremity Overlord to end this feud. The Senatus will guarantee whatever terms you have stated will be enforced.]

WAA!

A shocking set of words left his grand visage.

Words that caused the faces of the Avernus Esteemed Havenly Emperors to turn dark as they turned towards the Rhetorician in shock!

What was this? The True Emperor of Quintessence alone would be allowed to set the terms? The Avernus Clan alone would pay the price? And the Senatus would enforce it?!

Why did these words make it seem like the Rhetorician of the Senatus was conceding to this being after seeing his connection to the unfathomable Infernal Havenly Commerce?

[Esteemed Rhetorician, this is not-] Seer Amestris quickly rose to object as before she could even finish, the right hand of the Rhetorician rose as she was silenced.

His gaze remained on Noah.

He waited for see what choice would be made at this point in time!
