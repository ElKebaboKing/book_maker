Chapter 5171: Ashes to Ashes, Dust to Dust! I

Noah stood at the center of THE Infiniverse, his gaze lingering on the vibrant, multicolored seas that churned with a restlessness he now recognized.

He looked at his hands, sensing the profound and terrifying weight of the power coursing around him, a current of Absolute Infinity that still hummed with the cold, efficient geometry of the state he had just narrowly escaped. reewebnovel.com

With a slow, deliberate exhale that carried the weight of a man reclaiming his soul, he finished thr process and actively began to draw that boundless pressure inward, compressing the staggering density of the Absolute into his Hadean Organ of Civilization.

The transition was not a simple storage of energy, but a structural rewriting of his very foundation as the organ buckled and then hardened under the influx, its purpose evolving to match the paradoxical nature of its new contents.

"Ruination."

He called out the name with warmth, acknowledging the one he had nearly blacklisted during his descent into the apathetic void. Ruination stepped forward from the shimmering air, a soft, relieved smile gracing her illusory features as she waved her hand, causing a cascade of prompts to bloom before his eyes.

|Due to your active rejection of the clinical completeness of Absolute Infinity, your existence has compressed the influx into THE Hadean Organ of Civilization.|

|THE Hadean Organ of Civilization is undergoing a permanent structural metamorphosis to also become THE Absolute Forge of Infinity.|

|A paradoxical state has been achieved: Nearu Absolute Infinity.|

|As a finite being, your own scope and perspective act as a natural ceiling to the boundless; you are the limitation that prevents Infinity from being absolute. Until a form of solution can be attained to bridge this existential gap, Nearu Absolute Infinity shall be utilized and mediated exclusively through your Organ, THE Absolute Forge of Infinity.|

Noah read the prompts and sighed, the sound echoing through the vastness of THE Infiniverse, for he understood the irony with the crystalline clarity of his Hadean mind.

His own being was a limitation to the power he wielded, yet even with that knowledge, he felt no regret for his choice. He did not wish to become a cosmic automaton, a robot of progression for whom the journey held no value beyond the destination, because what he loved about existence was the raw and unadulterated wonder of it all!

He lived for the exploration, the crushing adversity, the searing pain of growth, and the exquisite pleasure of victory, and other...forms of pleasure, and to lose those things was to lose the very reason he had reached for the stars in the first place.

He realized now that he could still carry the efficiency and ruthlessness of that Absolute state within him, utilizing it as a tool when necessary without sacrificing himself.

And when it came to sacrifices...

He felt something deep within the newly formed Absolute Forge, the manic engine of emotions that was THE Heart of Emotive, which had served as the signal flare to bring him back from the brink of total self-extinction.

He thought of the sacrifice she had made, a woman he had historically viewed with a mixture of distaste and suspicion, yet who had given her everything to ensure his return regardless of the cost.

"Ill be back," he murmured to the two manifestations beside him.

His body flickered and disappeared, warping through the dimensions with a speed that left the multicolored seas rippling in his wake, and he reappeared at the base of the mountain in THE Wyld with his feet settling onto the pale and cracked stone as he looked down at the figure on the ground.

Emotive was there, her manic violet hair muted and her eyes filled with a desolation that seemed to have aged her eons in a single hour, but when she looked up and saw him, her expression changed with a staggering and visceral intensity.

Her eyes opened wide, tracing the contours of his face and searching the depth of his pupils for the return of that vibrant and tyrannical spark she had feared lost forever.

When she confirmed it, when she saw the return of the Noah who could still look at her coldly and tyrannically, she did not hesitate!

She surged from the ground with her body practically becoming a blur as she threw herself toward him, wrapping her arms around him in a grip so tight it would have crushed a Rhyacian Proterozoic Scale entitiy!

"I thought your brilliance was gone forever!"

She wailed, her voice thick with tears and a manic relief that shook her entire body!

Noah looked down at the woman currently clinging to his body, and his internal reactions were a conflicted.

As he had seen her coming, his first thought had been to instinctively smack her away, much as he had done in the mountain of Alfheimr, and he had even begun to raise a hand to stop the collision, but he found himself unable to carry it out!

Despite her faults and her history and her irritating manic nature, this woman had plunged her hand into her own chest and surrendered her very heart to him when she truly did not have to.

He sighed, the movement causing her to grip him even tighter, and instead of a strike, he found his hand settling atop her head in a slow and patient pat.

Beside them, Anaximander rose from the stone and shook his head incredulously.

Emotive blinked, her manic emotions blooming wildly at the touch, and she slowly let him go while moving back with a sudden and flustered start.

"Ah, my apologies, Sir! I was just taken away by... huh?"

She stopped mid-sentence, raising her hand in confusion as she began to make small and bewildered sounds, for multicolored Infinity began to rise from her form in terrifyingly dense concentrations and her existence radiated a profound aura that seemed to mirror Noahs own.

Noah had to sigh again as he looked at her, his Hadean perception already tracing the source of the anomaly.

Just how the hell was this woman so lucky?

Because her Heart, the very core of her Civilization and her claim to Chaos, was currently residing within his Absolute Forge of Infinity, it was being bathed in the boundless currents of the Absolute, and by extension her existence was being dragged upward along his own path while forced into a metamorphosis that neither of them had anticipated.
