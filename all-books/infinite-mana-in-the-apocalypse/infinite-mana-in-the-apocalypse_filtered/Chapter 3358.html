Chapter 3356 Infiniverse Megalos Seed! IV

Seeing his frowning gaze and even disregard, the three Ordinance Amalgamation Attendants of the Daughter of Existence became stern as their power roiled out, causing the surrounding weavings of Existence to vibrate!

|You dare not show respect to a Daughter of Existence? Are you not aware of what she represents? Are you not aware of the Immovable Sacred Palace of the Expanse?!|

HUUM!

Each of his words were like shocking decrees as all of surrounding existence near the Blancverse Domain rapidly changed color.

Instantly, purple chains bloomed and covered everything- thousands of them covering all surrounding landscape as they only curved and did not apply their oppressive effects on the other Ordinance Amalgamation Entities and around the Three Eyed Goldflame Steed behind them!

But everything else.

All weavings outside of the Blancverse Domain were under his control.

"The Immovable Sacred Palace of the Expanse?"

Noah raised his brows at such words, the obsession of Pyrrhus further buzzing as if it were telling him one of the culprits...were either within this Immovable Sacred Palace of the Expanse, or made up this power entirely.

Seeing his nonchalant expression, the Attendant of the Daughter of Existence let out a laugh of surprise.

|You really do not know of the Immovable Sacred Palace of the Expanse? Did you just recently begin roaming the Expanse? And you even attained a Blancverse Domain?|

Such a phenomenon was rare as this had to be a person of great luck!

On the Three Eyed Goldflame Steed, the Daughter of Existence smiled as she tapped on the gold fur beneath her.

|Young Man, come out and give up ownership of this Blancverse Domain to me, and I will expand your gaze to the wonders of the Expanse. You won't even know what to truly do with it anyway. Do this, and I can even consider making you my Prime Concubine. You will enjoy resources within the Immovable Sacred Palace of the Expanse that will allow to to fearlessly amalgamate your Ordinance- and even Sever your Existence!|

...!

Her eyes were bright as she couldn't help but look at this defiant man with favor.

She didn't know why, but his very existence emanated an attractive force that made her want to put him under her!

|The Daughter of Existence, Sentara, Third of her Name, has shown you have favor. Are you still not coming out to receive it?|

The Attendant with the silver horn on his head had an ugly expression at this moment.

A Prime Concubine!

The Daughter of Existence actually favored him so much to offer him this in exchange for the Blancverse Domain!

This was something that even he could not attain as if he did, his status within the Immovable Sacred Palace would be cemented for cycles to come.

The status of the Daughters and Sons of Existence were that high!

Yet this buffoon before him didn't even seem to know favor from calamity as he shook his head.

"I do not need to be a Concubine, and I think I will keep this little Blancverse to myself."

...!

|You...!| The Silver Horned Ordinance Amalgamation Attendant turned furious.

But Noah waved his hands dismissively towards these beings as he waited for the transformation of the Infiniverse Megalos Seed to finish, and then-

||Master.||

HUUM!

Beside him, the Manifestation of the Infiniverse Megalos Seed stepped forward, her illusory form pulsing with multicolored radiance as ever so slowly, it began to become very, very real.

The curves of the figure became distinguished.

Multicolored strands of hair formed from terrifying authorities cascaded past her shoulders.

Fair skin that shone with a bronze golden luster formed, and eyes filled with endless light of trillions of singularities came to being!

A multicolored robe pulsing with shocking authority draped over her as she looked like an ancient queen gracing the common lands with her presence, her bright eyes illuminating everything!

She didn't seem like a Manifestation any longer- she seemed unfathomably genuine.

||I wish to try something, Master.||

...!

Noah gazed towards the Manifestation- nay, could it still be called a Manifestation?

"This is?"

He asked with bright eyes as the reply came while this dazzling visage of a woman raised her scintillating hand up, seeing the trillions of layers of reinforced authority underneath.

||My Infiniverse Megalos Body. With it, I wish to test some things.||

Authority crackled around her madly.

The Infiniverse Megalos Body!

Noah looked at this wonder with bright eyes as prompts flashed over his eyes.

|The limitations and bindings of the past are being rewritten.|

|The potential and possibilities of the complexity of existence of the Infiniverse Megalos Seed and what weavings it can accomplish are unknown and endless.|

Such prompts flashed within Noah's mind as he gave a nod of dazed affirmation, and in the next moment...

PA!

The Infiniverse Megalos Body took a single step and instantly appeared outside the Blancverse.

Outside!

Any Ordinance Amalgamation or Ordinance of Existence Entity did not need to personally use their bodies to attack their enemies.

Some could attack the distance of an Omniverse away, and terrifying Ordinance Amalgamation entities could fight and exert their Ordinance the distance of multiple Omniverses!

By all means, the Infiniverse Megalos Body had no need or reason to appear outside of the protection of the Inviolable Sacred Blancverse Domain.

And Noah knew she was far from being stupid with its calculations.

So he didn't call her back or doubt her, only watching with a sense of amazement as the moment she arrived outside the Blancverse, she was faced with the terrifying altered existence filled with layers of purple chains!

All of the surrounding Existence- under the banner and control of the Silver Honed Ordinance Amalgamation expert.

Yet when she arrived, her body shimmered with a seemingly appalling radiance- a radiance of weight.

Of Mass.

Of Tonnage!

Her body seemed to carry ancient and unbearable weight that tyrannically pressed down over everything at this moment!

The dense purple chains in the region she appeared...vibrated and quaked before they were actually shattered an instant later- leaving behind a gold region of golden strings of the Expanse once more.

...!

|...!|

The eyes of the Silver Horned Ordinance Amalgamation Entity opened wide with incredulity!
