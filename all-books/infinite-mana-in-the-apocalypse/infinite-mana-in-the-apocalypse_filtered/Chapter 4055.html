Chapter 4053: Records Without Limitations! II

Noah checked her condition with genuine concern. Are you okay?

Ruination smiled...an expression that transformed her entirely.

Her fair skin now seemed to glow with inner darkness, her figure wrapped in a deep red-obsidian dress that appeared to be cut from ruptured folds of existence itself.

The fabric was absence given form, endings made wearable!

I have never been better, she said, her voice carrying waves of termination. For you, Master, I will chase endings all the way to the Earliest Folds...where I need to.

HUUM!

Noah shook his head with a mixture of pride and concern, then raised his hand to release multiple rays of white-gold light.

The Guidance Seals shot out like stars being born, seeking every existence within the Early Veiled Shore...Henry, Kazuhiko, Emperor Penguin, Adelaide, Athena, Anna, Barbatos, his mother, everyone who had chosen to follow him into impossibility.

Scintillating lights landed on all present, each seal adapting to its recipients nature. Some blazed bright, others dimmed to match quieter natures, but all carried the same fundamental blessing...the guidance of one who could lead Living Existences to transcendence!

Noah turned to the Infiniverse, whose form had been shifting with increasing calmness since witnessing Ruinations transformation.

Youre up next.

...!

The being that was the consciousness of the Infiniverse Wheel itself trembled with anticipation.

What would it give away? What would it become? The possibilities were infinite, which was perhaps the problem...when you contained all possibilities, choosing just one path seemed almost impossible.

But that was why Noah was here. THE Living Existential Guider didnt just mark beings for growth...he would help them find their paths when the options were too vast to comprehend.

He had already been discussing with the Infiniverse on what could be given as she was similar if not grander than him in what she could give!

The entrance to the Primordial Excavation Depths yawned before them, promising treasures and dangers from eras that predated current understanding. But first, his people needed to be prepared for what they might face.

After all, you couldnt mine the bones of Existence without being ready for Existence to object!

Noah gazed at the Infiniverse.

The manifestation of the Infiniverse Wheel of Existence that held the Early Veiled Shore at its very center. She stood before him as the consciousness of structure itself, the awareness that had developed within the framework that housed countless existences.

Her Spokes were all Frequencies of Existence containing countless domains of Matter, Dark Matter, and Anti-Matter. She was the architecture within which countless beings lived, loved, fought, and died without ever knowing their universe had consciousness, had will, had dreams of its own.

The moment the Guidance Seal touched her, her body radiated waves of profound complexity that made the air itself reconsider its properties!

Her entire existence pulsed.

A pulse.

Such a simple thing, a pulse.

Hearts did it billions of times without thought. Stars pulsed with radiation across eons. Even atoms vibrated with their own pulses!

But this pulse was different.

From this single pulse, the massive Infiniverse Wheel of Existence itself...hidden away in the Nullvein Gravewake Folds, shuddered with recognition.

The tremor passed through every Spoke, every Frequency, every particle of matter and antimatter that comprised its incomprehensible bulk.

And the Early Veiled Shore, housed at the center like a heart within a chest, also pulsed in response!

The Infiniverse rose from where she had been seated, and her ascension was revelation itself.

Her dress seemed woven from existences own dreams...multicolored jewels that werent merely decorative but functional, each one representing a different Frequency she contained. They caught light that didnt exist, reflecting possibilities that might never be, creating an aurora of potential around her form!

Her face carried the kind of beauty that transcended physical features. It was the beauty of music finding harmony after discord, of understanding dawning after confusion.

When she spoke, her voice carried the resonance of billions of voices speaking in perfect unity!

I give...Everything of my Limitations.

The words fell into the Shores atmosphere with weight that made reality reassess its rulings!

Everything of her Limitations.

Not some, not most, but Everything.

Noahs eyes blazed with glory of what she was attempting. A prompt materialized before his vision with urgency that suggested even the system that generated it was shocked.

|The Infiniverse has chosen to give an extremely fundamental part of her Everything|

|Limitations define structure, boundaries, possibility itself|

|Without limitations, infinite expansion becomes possible|

|Without limitations, infinite collapse becomes possible|

|Warning: Outcome cannot be predicted|

...!

The tentacled jaws of the Seed of Inevitability flashed into existence above her, summoned by her declaration of sacrifice.

But something unprecedented occurred...they didnt immediately chomp down.

Instead, they hovered, almost hesitant, as if recognizing that what was being offered transcended normal feeding.

The Infiniverse gazed up at them with regal certainty, no fear in her expression despite what she was about to lose. She raised her hands slowly, formally, as if conducting a ceremony that had been waiting eons to be performed.

From her palms, something began to emerge...and it was not light or darkness.

It was possibility itself crystallizing into form.

It was every boundary she had ever possessed.

Every rule that kept her Frequencies from colliding. Every law that prevented her galaxies from occupying the same space. Every limitation that made her finite despite containing infinity.

BOOM!

The moment the Inevitability accepted what she offered, everything shook.

But this wasnt the trembling of destruction or the shuddering of fear.

This was Existence reorganizing itself around a fundamental change, like the universe clearing its throat before speaking for the first time!

The Early Veiled Shores golden sands rose in spirals that defied gravity.

The Early Sacred Waters formed geometries that shouldnt exist.

Every tree, every plot, every construct began resonating with frequencies that had been locked away since before time was properly separated from space!

|Due to countless limitations being lifted, the Infiniverse is undergoing fundamental transformation|

|Scattered records and memories from the origin of the Infiniverse are unfolding|
