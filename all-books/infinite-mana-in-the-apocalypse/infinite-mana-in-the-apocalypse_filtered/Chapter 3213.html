Chapter 3211 Outerversal Morphon Ozymandias! I

On the same golden beach within a corner of the Aletheian Haven of Helios.

Standing In front of the Grill of Helios that lashed out tongues of golden flames, Noah was putting the Extremity of Cooking to full use as he waited for the results of a heavy investment.

Until now, all feeling and sensation had been cut off from the other part of him traversing across an Unknown path that the Outerversal Morphon Ginnungagap had taken, so he didn't know whether it was still on its way, or already entirely destroyed!

But he didn't place too much emphasis on it as he continued to take care of things he could control.

Being present with this Ascendant Existential Extremity Clone of Helios with his family in the midst of all of this was something he could do.

To his right, his son could be seen with blue swimming trunks, his built figure showing the toned muscles that all the men had on this golden beach.

In his hands, two chalices containing the squired juices of Quintessential Fruits of Existence could be seen- two golden plates in front of him as he came to grab finished pieces of the dragon cutlets his father was grilling!

"Rare, medium, or well done?"

His father asked him with a nudge as Henry smiled while bringing the floating plates forward.

"Rare for me, well done for her. If she sees any red on the meat, it'll stay on the plate. And she's too shy to ever send anything back to you."

...!

As Henry spoke, he was looking towards an area of the beach where a young woman could be seen sitting on a blue beach chair- her figure adorned in a white sundress as her blue hair calmly waved in the wind.

A young woman from Atlantis.

Last time, Noah had told Henry to be honest regarding who he was as this young woman he had a relationship with didn't know about his illustrious identity as the Son of the Existential Omniversal Lumen Emperor!

He had taken the next step and revealed all of this as it would have overwhelmed any common woman, but this young woman with a royal mermaid Lineage from Atlantis held a stronger soul than most as she was slowly introduced to the family.

Noah smiled as he placed pieces of meat radiating golden oils on the plate they were put on, potent life force brimming within them as he filled the plate of well done dragon cutlets a bit more.

"We'll have a more private affair soon. A small banquet. Bring her and introduce her to everyone officially there."

He spoke calm words to his son- a Foundation Autarch, and this being actually nodded with nervousness as he walked towards a Young woman on the beach with vibrant plates in tow!

It was a simple glimpse into the slightly normal side of Entities with power that could distort destinies and Existence itself.

Noah reflected on this scene and the many still playing out across this golden beach of helios as this was it.

Part of the happiness that he was protecting.

This was why in the face of any larger than life enemies or mysteries.

In the face of the Commonwealth, Concepts of Anti-Life and Anti-Existence, and now even the concept of Outerversal Morphons. He didn't falter in the face of any of this as he kept on moving forward.

Until the day came when he had seen all the wonders and scales of power.

Until the day came when he could sit back and not have to worry about an Apocalypse, or a being stronger than him by many levels appearing and dictating how his life would be from there onward.

If and when such a time ever came, he would sit back and enjoy retirement as a farmer.

But until then...the brilliance and Fable of the Existential Omniversal Lumen Emperor would continue to spread out far and wide, his tendrils of influence seeking to now stretch across Omniverses as he prepared for the integration and slow dissemination of the Commonwealth!

The Commonwealth that was on full display of its attitude and standards at this moment as in the Wheel of Weavings.

In the Gladiators' Arena, where a Shrine of an Outerversal Morphon was becoming ever so real, its Declaration caused a sense of powerlessness on all weavings of Existence as obsidian tendrils erupted out to devour anything they came across!

It was an unexpected situation, yes, but there were many powerful entities of the Commonwealth here.

Noah remembered the Nine Thrones holding entities who very likely exceeded the Existential Dominium Realm and were within the bounds of the Omniversal Realm!

Yet they did not make a move.

Was it to inspire a sense of fear? Show others how hopeless they were against these powerful enemies?

SAA!

Noah cut through breaking weavings of Existence as he dodged thousands of sleek obsidian tentacles that only increased in number, his gaze going towards the distant seats of the audience as there, tentacles were about to reach as those who couldn't even muster up a semblance of their Authority would perish en masse!

Would the Commonwealth truly go as far as allowing for deaths of their Citizens before stepping in?

Just as this thought flowed from Noah.

HUUM!

A brilliant golden light shone from above and began to constrict the undeniable obsidian Declaration that was spreading out.

When Noah saw this golden light, his eyes couldn't help but constricted as it wasn't like any familiar light of authority he had come across!

It was encompassing. It was solid and without any gaps. What is sought to accomplish would be achieved with surety.

It was...Omniversal.

It pressed down from all directions as the expanding Declaration of an Outerversal Morphon actually began to shrink!

Not an Outerversal Morphon- but the infinitesimally small glimpse of the power of an Outerversal Morphon that had begun to seep out of the shell that used to be Zimo.

But at the center of the golden light pressing down on the Declaration of the Outerversal Morphon Ginnungagap, the image of a grandiose Simian showed itself with unfathomable glory- his visage amplified and expressed to all!

Almost as if it was a show.
