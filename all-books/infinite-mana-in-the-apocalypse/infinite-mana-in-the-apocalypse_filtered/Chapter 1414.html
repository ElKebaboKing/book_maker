Chapter 1412: The Descent of a Bloody Halo! IV

A promise was broken and rescinded!

Under it, a unique possibility arose as Noah's destiny surged steadily, Hyperion standing right next to the terrifying Telos form of his main body as when he gazed at the boundlessly powerful white Halo descending down, Noah calmly called upon the most recent authority he had gained access to.

BZZZT!

With Hyperion by his side, they stared down a blazing white Halo smashing towards them while around them...a crimson ring of light pulsing with unique power began to form!

A crimson ring of light that seemed to be dripping with its blood as its outline was eerily similar to an Ascendancy Halo!

A unique authority only forged by the connection of 15 Flames of Nomological Edicts! It was something out of mere fantasy with many conditions required to be met, which begged one to question exactly how a Halo was currently rising around Noah and Hyperion- and why it was so starkly different from Cecilia's Halo that was the color white.

How exactly did they manage to form an Ascendancy Halo across two different beings? How did they consolidate Hyperion's Edicts and Noah's Edicts into a singular thing for them to utilize?!

The answer was simple as if there were two beings...they just had to become a single one!

WAA!

Two beings just had to fuse together and bring their Flames of Edicts as the answer to this...actually came in the form of a Lesser Dao.

The Lesser Dao of Summoning!

Within this unique Dao, there was a feature that allowed one to merge with the existences considered their Summons under this Edict as this was the solution that Noah had proposed...a solution that nearly caused a rift to form between the two beings at first.

Hyperion had his own unrelenting will and pride that when it came to be bound by another being- it wasn't even a possibility under his eyes!

He was unruly and unwilling to be put down on the feet of another as Noah could only think of another solution- and he went ahead and simply devised and forged a new Cosmic Dao that took the concepts of the Lesser Dao of Summoning and Cosmic Dao of Fusion to produce something that allowed two existences to form a temporary fusion of Origin and soul, one side not being more in control than the other!

Even with this solution that Hyperion accepted, he was still unhappy with it as this was why his expression was cloudy as he stood beside Noah.

A resonance of origin and soul.

A fusion of two separate existences into one.

This...was made possible by the newly forged Cosmic Dao of Unification!

There were many perks within this Dao just like many others, but the biggest one under use at this moment in time was a singular skill contained within after one achieved complete assimilation in this Dao.

"Unity."

RUMBLE!

Noah called out lightly as while the bloody ring of light around them was only hazy, it began to materialize more and more into fruition as in the next instant- his body and Hyperion's shimmering with light as they mixed and fused into each other!

Unity!

<Unity> :: An ability that utilizes the essence of the Cosmic Dao of Unification to allow for two or more beings to merge two bodies into a single paramount being. The effects of increase of power through the fusion are dependent on those merging together, with there being possibilities of the strength of the product ranging from 10 to 100 times more powerful. Unity allows for the fusion of Origin and Soul momentarily, and it is recommended to only utilize it with those who have earned your trust as it is common for memories of the merging beings to overlap...

An utterly unique ability that took the concepts of merging from the Lesser Dao of Summoning, with the augmentation of strength for the fused being ranging depending on many factors!

But for Noah, it was the factor that the ability allowed for the Unity of Origin and Soul temporarily as this...met the conditions to call forth an Ascendancy Halo from the Edicts of two different beings!

BZZZT!

The void shook from the descent of a pristine white Halo, but the hazy bloody Halo that formed around the figures of Noah and Hyperion solidified rapidly, brightening up at a fantastical rate as in no time....

RIING!

It buzzed with utmost power and awe as one could see a shimmering coagulation of ancient weaving of runic lines that connected together to form a glorious crimson Ascendancy Halo.

A Halo that seemed to be surrounded by a cloud of blood as it roared out with pride and power, rushing up to meet the descending white Halo as it seemed like two disks capable of destroying Reality itself went to clash with each other!

BOOOOOOM!

The void that even the Ruination Sea ran away from was razed and shattered as wondrously, tears and breakage occurred in reality itself!

Two blazing rings of light smashed into each other as one carried the stench of blood and power, while the other carried prideful arrogance that roared against destiny itself.

WAA!

If a powerful enough being were to watch the impact, they would notice that after the appearance of the crimson Ascendancy Halo, the color red began to occupy more than half of the surrounding space as the white Ascendancy Halo actually lost out in this exchange!

BZZT!

The two Ascendancy Halos pulled apart from each other as the white Halo returned to spin around Cecilia- the eyes of this Primordial currently releasing rays of sharpness as within them...one could actually see surprise and shock.

And how could she not be shocked to see the glimmering crimson Halo before her?!

"What is..." Her sharp eye focused intently as she uttered out with utmost confusion- this confusion stemming from the fact that the mere appearance of a red Ascendancy Halo was something that entirely defied all her knowledge.

This was because she had never before seen or heard about a red Ascendancy Halo!

Among their Tiers commonly known by Higher Existences, red was not one of them!

So what exactly was this?!
