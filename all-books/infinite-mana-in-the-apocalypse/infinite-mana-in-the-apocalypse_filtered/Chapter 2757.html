2755 The Demon of the Seven Havens! IV

The fall of any Entity at the Zenith Tier 4 or at the stage of the Fifth Gradation of Extremity was a rare and insurmountably difficult reality to come across.

When Entities of such levels did fall, their advanced progress in the Gradations of Extremity allowed what remained of their Aspects of Existence to form Hyperversal Gradation Fruits of Extremity- holy Panaceas that others only knew of in Recorded History but rarely came across!

They were extremely rare to even form as they required an impossible reality to occur, and their usage...could at the very minimum birth a Crimson Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Rune Scripture on any Entity.

Whether they had achieved Extremity or not.

A single Hyperversal Gradation Fruit of Extremity passed down a portion of the Inheritance of an existence that was extremely advanced in the Gradations of Extremity as after taking it, any Entity could be elevated to the Third Gradation of Extremity.

If their souls were unique and their existence was powerful enough, they could achieve an even greater result!

Such a Fruit formed above the shattered body of Ancestor Renee as a portion of the soul that possessed her was destroyed along with her- a heavy casualty occurring on this day as Ancestor Renee was one of the few who had their Main Bodies here.

The Rhetorician of the Senatus gazed at this scene coldly as his will surged to gaze at ten Senatus Members floating around him, and the fractured bodies of other Senatus Members who were caught in the battle that had bloomed here.

His will gazed at all of this as his voice rang out coldly.

[It is time to cleanse the Seven Havens of anything to do with Vacuous Malignancies. All Entities in seats of power will bring themselves before me and have their Soul analyzed for any connections with Vacuous Malignancies. Anybody who disagrees...will be executed on the spot!]

WAA!

His figure radiated a profound light of power as he spoke words of undeniability!

The gazes of the Tier 4 Hyperversal Extremities around him changed at such a drastic response, but they couldn't refute it.

The golden hair on the Rhetorician of the Senatus waved with glory and majesty as he continued while gazing into the far distance.

[We are in a precarious time of war that may decide how the Vitalis Hyperversal Domains live for the eons to come. Drastic times require drastic measures as first, we will secure our own borders and forces. Evelyn...you first!]

The Rhetorician called out.

And from behind him, the Speaker whose visage emanated great beauty and grace stepped out and came to bow before him.

His massive hand reached out and grasped her whole head as a golden light flashed, the Records of a Tier 4 Hyperversal Extremity being glanced at as an instant later, the Rhetorician nodded lightly as the Speaker moved to the side.

[Melchior.]

One name after another started to be called.

Great change began as the leadership of the Senatus of Extremity began to ensure that among their ranks, only those they trusted would be among them!

And in the midst of all this...

[What about my Avernus Clan?]

The voice of Seer Amestris rang out as billowing purple flames raged around her.

Grasping the head of Melchior, the Rhetorician glanced at her without a word as he called out another name!

Seer Amestris saw this scene as her gaze changed greatly.

[There is a Primogenitor stemming from the Avernus Clan out there. He would not sit still knowing that the Senatus that the Primogenitors set up did not defend the Avernus Clan in their time of need. That the Senatus allowed a Demon with unconfirmed backing and power to destabilize one of the Seven Clans!]

...!

Seer Amestris asked the Rhetorician directly.

And after a pause as another Tier 4 Entity was analyzed and cleared, the Rhetorician turned towards her and spoke coldly.

[Amestris.]

He didn't answer her question.

He only called out her name as this beautiful Tier 4 Entity gritted her teeth and warped right before the Rhetorician, feeling his hand land on her head as Records were scanned!

He looked down at her figure a moment after as he spoke coldly.

[The Senatus has already intervened on your behalf and did their duty. Seek to resolve this issue yourselves. Get on your knees and beg Noah Osmont or the Infernal Havenly Commerce he is connected to if you must. Your people began this conflict, your people will resolve it.]

...!

The eyes of Seer Amestris opened with incredulity as the Rhetorician continued.

[The Annihilation that Osmont seeks of the Avernus Clan is not death and destruction of all its Extremities- he wants to destabilize and break the identity of your Clan itself as some of your Extremities are already leaving the clan to assimilate the Blood Sources that are the cause of your chaos. For the overall picture, the Vitalis Domains will fairly have the same numbers of Vitalis Hyperversal Extremities protecting it, even if Osmont targets and kills a few more Avernus Extremities, those that do leave your Clan by assimilating Blood Sources will be more powerful than their prior selves as they will fill any gaps made. This..is the verdict of the Senatus of Extremity. The fate of the identity of the Avernus Clan lies in your own hands.]

WAA!

The words shocked many of the Entities here as their minds moved at extremely high speeds.

Was the capability that Osmont had displayed of the Havenly Hyperversal Emperor Lineage so obscene that it altered the mind of the Rhetorician so much? Or was there something else at play? The Infernal Havenly Commerce?

Many questions buzzed in their minds as by the time the majestic hand of the Rhetorician left Seer Amestris' head, her eyes scowled with clear anger as she talked back right in the face of this Entity without fear.

[The decision of the Senatus is to leave one of the Seven Clans to fend for itself? What use is it for us to be a part of the Senatus? And you all...]

Her gaze turned to the other Senatus Members who represented the other Seven Clans and Major Inheritances.

[What is to prevent Osmont from declaring war on any of your Clans and Inheritances and denying access to the Blood Sources for you all as well? What will the Senatus do for you then?]

...!

Her words were chilling as even before a second passed, Melchior of the Sima Clan sighed as he gazed up at the stellar chaos around them.

[The rest of us didn't start a War of Annihilation with him...]

Silence descended in the Chambers.

And a moment later...

[Dawn.]

The Rhetorician continued to call out the name of another Senatus Member as reality propelled itself forward.

The results of this meeting...were ones that nobody had expected going into it!

Time passed.

Seconds turned into minutes.

And with the mere passage of minutes, many changes and events took place as information was spread across the Seven Havens rapidly.

The new fronts of war in front of Extremity Bridges had their states determined, and what occurred in the pristine Chambers of the Senatus of Extremity became known by other members of the Seven Clans and prominent Inheritances.

From this event, the name of the Entity who was systematically destabilizing and destroying one of the Seven Clans spread.

Noah Osmont.

His identity was an enigma as the connections he held to the Supernal Havens were a mystery, and he held many distinctions as currently...the most pronounced one that was making its rounds was the one that the Speaker of the Senatus had called him!

A Demon of the Seven Havens.

Getting into a conflict with him meant facing Annihilation of your very Clan or Inheritance as even if the Senatus intervenes on your behalf, he would not stop his ways.

This was Osmont.

This rise of a new name and Entity that they had to be careful of, and yet from their intelligence and all the information they had recorded...this being had yet to even achieve Extremity!

As for the being in question himself...he remained in the Infinite Hyperversal Haven as he prepared.

Before his eyes at this very moment, prompts continued to cascade.

[Your distinction as a Villain is rising quintessentially!]

[A Quintessential Extremity Loot has begun to be forged at the core of your Origin.]

[Your distinction as a Hero is rising greatly!]

The Seventeenth Boundary Layers of the Villain and Hero activated.

Noah gazed at the prompts serenely while feeling singularities blooming at the top of his Aletheian Havens.

He then placed the Hyperversal Bow of Helios in his hand down as he gazed in front of him where a Sporos IV of Extremity floated serenely.

It was a Sporos that now needed maturity after drinking the nutrition of over half of the 100 Tier 2 Avernus Extremities and over a thousand Vacuous Malignancies.
