The next prompt contained requirements that made even Noah shake his head at the level of difficulty associated with maintaining an Early Veiled Shore.

|A vital catalyst to allow for the anchor and growth of the Auric Vitae Grass is the Blood of an Early Creature. For the amount of Auric Vitae Grass on hand, approximately 18 Drops of Blood would be required.|

Noah read these prompts with the resignation!

But the benefits provided by the Early Veiled Shore were far too critical to abandon over concerns about cost- costs which werent truly costs as he could recover them in a short period of time!

So he went ahead.

"Let us proceed with the planting of the Auric Vitae Grass," he voiced aloud, his words carrying the weight of Existential command.

The palm of his right hand opened like a flower blooming in reverse, and eighteen drops of dull crimson-gold blood flowed forth with the stately grace of liquid starlight.

Each drop pulsed with its own inner radiance, containing within its molecular structure the compressed essence of what it truly meant to be an Early Creature.

These drops moved through the air with purposeful intent, flowing toward an empty area close to the crystalline waters of the Shore but positioned further inland where the golden sands met the roots of the ancient trees. They arranged themselves into a perfect square formation before sinking into the ground with the weight of Existence being fulfilled.

The moment his blood touched the earth, transformation began.

The golden sands of that region shifted and changed, their very nature being rewritten by the influence of Early Creature essence.

A glorious plot of fertile land rose from the Shore itself, its surface shining with white and gold radiance that spoke to potential beyond normal understanding.

|The Early Sacred Living Farming Tower of Osmont has fully formed due to the unique circumstances of this planting. Your connection to the agricultural mysteries of Early Creatures has deepened beyond normal parameters.|
