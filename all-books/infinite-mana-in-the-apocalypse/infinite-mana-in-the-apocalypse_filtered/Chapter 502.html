Chapter 501 Three is Better Than One!

In front of Noah, a drop of blood revolved rapidly before it soon bloomed into the bloody image of himself, a brand new presence appearing as a devilishly handsome face soon reflected on his eyes.

He smiled while looking at the face of someone that was becoming more and more mature as the months passed, always gaining extra charms aside from the effects of [Unnatural Beauty] from the Vampyre Progenitor passive abilities.

The clone breathed out while glancing around the Time Space, this being the place that this second Ancient Blood Clone would spend the most time in as he did something shocking.

For now, Noah would have this clone remain in the Time Space as he maxed the proficiencies of his other skill trees while also training in the four laws he currently held inheritances of!

One must know that the significance of this additional clone was not just to further accelerate his rate of growth, but it effectively meant another combatant at the same level as himself that he could choose to deploy on the battlefield.

When the time came that he was standing against a truly powerful enemy, what would this enemy do when two more of him appeared to join the battle?!

It was a thrilling scene to imagine as Noah grandly looked forward into the future! His main body teleported out of the Spiritual World as he anchored it to himself and disappeared from the Celestial Realm, briefly leaving this wondrous place for the first time with his main body as he reappeared in an empty expanse of space. He needed this level of privacy as he was about to do something that was simply too big!

His clone remained in the Time Space as he was about to begin the task of laws and raising proficiencies of skill trees. Even though he could not use the loophole of the Time Space to quickly master the laws, his regular rate of growth for Chaos Particles and Fate Lines was already crazy, being able to amass more than 25,000 of each in a single day, or 50,000 if he focused on just one.

If one did the math, this meant that even if Noah chose to do nothing else and just simply brought up the percentages of laws, he would be able to master the Law of Fate to 100% in just 9 months, and add more than 7 million Chaos Particles around that same time period!

This was plainly ridiculous, rule breaking, and something that would make the hard-working beings comprehending laws for hundreds or thousands of years simply go mad.

Yet he would not be sitting still while this clone worked on increasing the percentages in Laws and upgrading the skill trees. He would embroil himself in battles that granted him a stupid amount of Fate Lines and Chaos Particles, as well as numerous other things that would each have their own effects.

The future seemed even brighter as Noahs main body left the Time Space right away, the new clone sitting down as it sent words to the Spiritual World System.

Soon after, in a small area of the Time Space, a portion of the surrounding land turned fiery and another turned into a sparkling blue sea- the Water and Fire Law Spaces being built in a small area of the Time Space as the clone sat at the junction where they met and began cultivating.

Water and Fire Elemental particles began rushing towards him, while the appearance of thin Fate Lines and Chaos Particles expressed themselves in the air. At the same time, golden swords of liberation, spears of judgment, and the reappearance on an Arch Lich RaZan took place as the clone trained the proficiencies of the next skill trees for an upgrade at the same time!

It was multitasking to an insane degree, but Noah still felt in his heart that something was still missing. The missing piece was naturally the fact that he could train in a Fire and Water Law Space, but the more crucial Fate and Chaos Law Spaces had yet to be unlocked.

Thus, after letting the Spiritual World bloom freely to its full size in the starry expanse of space he had teleported to in the Light Expanse, his main body appeared above Central Continent of the Spiritual World, glancing at the four surrounding floating continents as he waved his hands and caused the appearance of enormous World Core.

Thats right! His first clone had already obtained the core of a Large World as he connected more and more planets, taking the time to locate a fairly weak Large World that had a few billion creatures occupying it. It was nearly impossible to find a Large World that was uninhabited, or it would even make more sense to say that a world could not get to the Large stage if it did not have any beings dwelling in it.

This world had contained in it humanoid and beastmen creatures, Noah simply conducting mass teleportations as he painstakingly moved all the beings of this world into the wide Ancient Blood World where they were placed under the care of the Blood Guardian.

His heart had not yet grown so ruthless as to cause the deaths of billions just to obtain his own aims, only using [Devouring] after every single creature was transported away and watching the crumbling of this world thereafter. His heart had to be cold at this as the only victim was the entity that was the core of this world, but even that he had no need to do as this consciousness would simply get assimilated into the Spiritual World System!

Thus, while his first clone continued to move and add more world connections from the abundant memories of the dead beings he had obtained at the Verittas Fortress, his main body waved his hands as the Large World Core placed in the [Expansive Space] appeared, this core trembling instantly thereafter as it shot down towards the Central Continent.

This was the last requirement for an upgrade from the 9th tier and into the 10th, a tier that would unlock the Fate and Chaos Law Spaces, as well as numerous other functions!

RUMBLE!

The many beasts, humans, and any other creatures living in the Spiritual World felt the fluctuations once more as they opened their eyes wide and spread out their auras to observe.

The Central Continent of the Spiritual World, as well as the enormous four connected floating continents, had begun to tremble as essence was washing over them freely, the knowledgeable beings shocked senseless once more as they realized the already wondrous Spiritual World was getting yet another upgrade!
