Chapter 2996 Existential Destroyers! II

The words the Manifestation of an entity uttered out in front of Noah were heavy ones that may make any layman shocked!

The Imperium held another name on their banner- but it was not one they took on themselves.

Existential Destroyers.

The destroyers of existence itself.

Entities who incarcerated an entire Omniversal Lineage and every possible branch that was a part of such a lineage to be Seeds inside Existential Domains!

|If you are truly what they term a Seed from one of these Existential Domains, then somewhere in your blood...there may lie trace amounts of an Omniversal Lineage long forgotten through time. The distinction of a Seed is demeaning to your existence- and the existences of countless others. You are no Seed, nor am I some filth that should merely be something to be butchered under the swords of the Imperium.|

HUUM!

As she spoke, the adornments around her became more and more pristine, waves of verdant golden light wrapping around her figure as she seemed an entity who held honor and dignity deep in her bones!

An entity steeped in royalty!

She actually told Noah that he may have a trace of an Omniversal Lineage inside him- with his blood running the possibility of being descended down from eons upon eons...or he may also be entirely disconnected and simply stemmed from a natal lineage within his natural Existential Domain.

At such words, Noah couldn't help but smile.

And the distinguished visage of a royalty continued.

|I am Emilliana- one distinguished as a Sanctus Princess from the Sanctus Populi. A Bastion for the people. A Bastion for all of those who cannot defend themselves. You may already know much of what I speak of, or you may not know in a limited fashion. I know not the extent and truth behind what happened to the recently destroyed Existential Domain, nor do I know much about you. But for being capable enough and even more crucial- brave enough to strike against the Imperium in the way you are doing now, I have to do my duty as a Sanctum Princess and offer you the grace of protection. You are aware of what they will do regarding you now?|

Her words ended with a question.

An important one as she was insinuating the gravity of the situation Noah found himself in by being brave enough to actually target and attack Knights of the Imperium!

Injuring a Knight alone was enough to condemn one to death and destruction with surety.

Having the Dominium Blood of a knight on One's hands? Actually killing an Imperium Knight or important members of the Imperium within their Royals, Enclaves, or Legacies? There was nowhere one could hide in the Omniverse to escape their pursuit after such actions.

|Nowhere under the light and darkness of the Omniverse will provide a shield or shade for you. Sometimes, it will even feel like the Omniverse itself is against you. As if it favors and bends under the will of the Imperium. That is how much authority they have gained over the eons due to their cruel actions, to the extent that under the banner even Omniversal Authorities become their tools.|

WAA!

She uttered words that minutes ago may have caused Noah to have a genuine surprise.

Before he attained years' worth of memories and weavings of the Knights of the Imperium.

Before he began draining the existence of Knight Augustus and the lifeblood of those choosing to support him at this moment.

Omniversal Authorities.

The inkling he had seen from the one holding a complete Codex of Extremity- Knight Akalusia!

The one who made moves to the extent that it seemed even the surroundings of the Omniverse themselves were inflating and enhancing her will and existential authority!

The memories of Knight Augustus flowed into his mind as they carried weavings of information relating to this.

And Sanctus Princess Emilliana spoke of such matters while extending her hand towards Noah.

|I know not your circumstances, but I can always carry out my duties as a Sanctus Princess and offer you a shade amidst all of this. Any power we have that stands against the Imperium, all the closer we come to liberating all those that have been oppressed over the eons. The Sanctus Populi is able to obscure the gaze of the Imperium and take you under our banner- to protect you from the persecution and death before it fully comes raining down. But...I seem to have spoken a great deal here. What say you, Noah Osmont?|

...!

The Sanctus Princess finished her monologues and gazed towards Noah for a reply.

She offered shade from a threat that was about to get immeasurably worse.

A threat one couldn't so easily escape across the Omniverse!

And yet towards such a reality, Noah only smiled as he was about to weave his intent.

He...was about to.

HUUM!

His manifested Will in this space buzzed in the next instant though, a heavy air of ancientness suddenly emanating from him that caused the weaving of the Manifestation of Sanctus Princess Emilliana to be bewildered before her eyes opened in shock!

Noah only captured a glimpse of this as he was focusing on himself- moreso on his main body that after beginning the devouring and assimilation of thousands of Fruits of Existence coming from the lifeblood of Teleios Stage Knights of the Imperium....

{Your Existential Dominium Soul, Existential Dominium Body, and Existential Dominium Origin have attained enough substance to truly embark on the theorized pathway of your Realm.}

Such a prompt came as his Main Body in the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed became surrounded by a refulgence of brilliance.

{Your three Dominiums resonate and attain an existential convergence that gives way to the Existential Primordium Dominium Core!}

{A culmination of your achievements.}

{A culmination of your existence!}

Inside Noah's existence- his Origin, Body, and Soul became superimposed as they enacted Noah's Primordium Dominium Core.

In the center of his existence.

Between all of his three Dominiums!
