The information Noah was reading in the form of prompts was grand, but the images he saw were even grander!

His eyes blazed gold as even though this was only a possibility, he saw it.

THE Scorched entity appeared in his minds eye with terrifying clarity.

It was a serpentine creature of impossible grace and horrifying beauty, its body long and sinuous like some ancient leviathan adapted for land. Fins that should have belonged to an aquatic being fanned out along its spine and tail, but instead of membrane and scale, they were composed entirely of crystallized Fallout flames that shifted through colors.

Its fur, or what might have once been fur, had become living fire that flowed across its form like water made of destruction. Multicolored flames of violet and crimson and gold and colors that had no name rippled across its serpentine body with each movement, leaving trails of scorched reality in its wake.

The creatures face was elongated and almost regal, with eyes that burned like twin suns trapped in sockets of ash. Where its mouth opened, one could see that even its teeth were flames given solid form, each fang a concentrated point of existential annihilation.

Most disturbing were the cracks across its existence, the markers of the cursed that glowed not with the usual residual Fallout energy but with active, hungry fire. The cracks were not healing. They were spreading. And from each crack, more flames poured forth.

It moved with a dancers grace despite its size, each step leaving behind footprints of burning reality that took moments to repair themselves. Bubbles of superheated existence rose from its form like foam from turbulent waters, each one a tiny pocket of destruction waiting to pop!

Oh!

Even though he was only viewing scenes of it in his mind, Noah could see the ferocity of THE Scorched as its Depth burned with heavy Intermediate immensity.

This was not a creature that could be reasoned with.

This was not a creature that remembered what it once was!

This was flame given purpose, and that purpose was consumption!

The golden haze around Noahs vision flickered as the Partial Simulation continued to feed him glimpses of what might come. He saw himself facing this creature in the gray Wastes, saw the clash of blue-gold authority against multicolored annihilation, saw the dance of death that would unfold between them.

And he saw that this was merely the first of many such encounters!
