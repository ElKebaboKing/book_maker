Chapter 428 - Fame

Among the networks of the Celestials moving in the Celestial Realm, two names had begun popping up and shining with vigor as the days passed.

The Normal and Elite Exploration Teams had been passing around the names of the two newly arriving Celestials with Noble Fates that were rising rapidly through the ranks, their names now being placed in the same places as renowned Celestials that were moving in Elite Teams!

"They arent the only ones with Noble Fates though. There are others that shine even brighter like Grandmaster Xander and Grandmaster Lila." freewebnovel.com

Two normal teams were enjoying a feast of simmering behemoth meat as they talked within the special locations that were exclusive to Celestials in the Sanctum.

The beings they mentioned as Grandmasters were not some old fogeys that had been living for thousands of years, but young and upcoming Celestials that had passed the point of 100,000 Fate Lines and obtained the title of Grandmaster!

They were beings that were similar to Noah and Athena, holding the title of those with Noble Fates.

"Well, I know we place a lot of importance on those with Noble Fates, but what about the Three-Eyed Fate Child and Grandmaster Light? They dont hold such titles, but because they are unique existences in our galaxy, they hold tremendous power!"

A burly celestial took a large bite of the glistening meat that shone with essence as he spoke out, the topic of conversation not disparaging any of the powerful beings they were talking about, where they were simply sharing their opinions on who else was out there.

This was the atmosphere of Celestials, who were under the doctrines of maintaining Order and Balance, and thus were happy as long as powerful figures were growing in ranks to be able to better reduce the ever so increasing Chaos!

The two other beings they mentioned as the Three-Eyed Fate Child and Grandmaster Light were not those holding powerful titles, but simply beings born in unique environments which gave them innate strength that was one of a kind in the Light Expanse.

The Three-Eyed Fate Child was the last of its species, a being that was found by a roving Grandmaster in an uninhabitable planet that was filled with destructive storms. This Three-Eyed Fate Child had been able to survive alone in this star for years before the Grandmaster found him, his unique Three Eyes giving him immense talent in the Supreme Law of Fate and the Supreme Law of Time-Space. This gave birth to a being that was proficient in two Supreme Laws!

The other was a Celestial known as Grandmaster Light, a being that was born on a planet made of light, this planet going on to shatter the moment that Grandmaster Light was born. She was an existence that held a unique composition, with much of her body being made up of particles of light! There were rumors that only her heart was the same as everyone else, possibly being her only weakness as well.

As she was a being of the Light Element, she had been able to cultivate the Supreme Law of Light and Supreme Law of Fate with ease as she moved through the ranks of Celestials over the years.

Now, each of these figures were moving on their own Elite Teams as they sought to increase their power and be the ones that brought down the most order and balance.

"Our organization is extremely united, but I feel like there will be some rifts and competitiveness soon as the number of beings with powerful talents appear. We have large amounts of treasures, but is it enough to raise multiple beings with Noble Fates and even Special Existences to the peak levels of power before the next war begins?"

A low voice erupted from the corner of the table, a fairly intelligent robed Celestial speaking out as he caused a silence to descend onto the area, those that heard his words being made to think.

The words of this wondering Celestial were actually fairly spot on though, as when the moment that Grandmaster Vredral applied for more materials and Cores for the powerful students under him that he knew to be showing immense promise, he was soon faced with an awkward situation where he was told to wait, as much of the other resources were already diverted to other beings holding titles of those with Noble Fates or Special Existences!

This caused the Grandmaster a bit of unhappiness as he knew that the disciples he had were extremely talented, knowing they would be able to bring them the most help when standing against the coming chaos in the future.

Grandmaster Vredral had thought about this issue as he went ahead and contacted his connections in the Sanctum and the 7th Celestial Star, letting his thoughts be known similarly to when he brought forth the idea of influencing the beings of the Light Expanse more by letting them into the Celestial Realm.

Because of his recent contributions, his words held a significant influence and value as even though he did not stand near those that would be considered at the top of power when observing the large scale of the Celestials, his influence was still wide as he helped oversee a significant portion of the Light Expanse with other Grandmasters in the 7th Celestial Star.

He had sacrificed his rise in power in the past as he chose to leave the Celestial Realm and take part in the overseeing mission of the Light Expanse, where the friends he knew and had in the past who had remained in the Celestial Realm were powerful Grandmasters that had already entered the World Rank, with some of them even having the task of looking after Special Existences or those with Noble Fates!

His influence over some of the regions in the Light Expanse allowed him to easily amass hundreds of thousands of Fate Lines even while he wasnt actively hunting Chaos Behemoths as the other Grandmasters over the years.

Thus, his words were taken seriously as the names Crixus and Athena were brought forward to the upper echelons of power of the Celestials, these two beings being put on the list among the brilliantly shining Celestials that were being groomed with the most resources for the war to come.

But this brought the problem where the exceptional Celestials were too many, with the materials and treasures needing to be spent very carefully as they were only focused on to beings that had the ultimate potential to create powerhouses that could sway the direction of the war in the future.

So how would those that were truly deserving and with most potential be chosen among the geniuses in their organization as they neared the precipice of a war?

This question would bring forth a spectacular event in the near future, an event that would be a show for the ages!
