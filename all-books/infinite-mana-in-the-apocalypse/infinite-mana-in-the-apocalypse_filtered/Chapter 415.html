Chapter 414 - Herbs and Pills are Good for Breakthroughs

As Noah erased the portal which led to the Spiritual Realm in the location where a Fire Behemoth just fell, he was the last to disappear as he joined everyone else beneath the volcanic mountain where the Spiritual Land stood next to the Ancient Pedestal that held the Inheritance Crystal for the Disciple of Fire.

The extremely hot environment was one where only those at higher realms could withstand, with all the newcomers that were just battling the Fire Behemoths being those who could easily learn the inheritance on their own.

He looked towards Elena as he gave out multiple directions to her and the others.

"Gather all those that havent learned the Inheritance and oversee the whole process. You.."

He pointed towards the Emperor Penguin as he continued speaking.

"...gather your brothers and sisters. We have another power leveling session to go through."

He released multiple commands towards many beings, those familiar with Noah noticing the slightly colder tone as they nodded with serious faces and each went about their tasks.

The normal smile that Noah carried months ago was fading away slowly, his disposition becoming more serious when he obtained the Vampyre Progenitor bloodline, and now becoming even colder as he was hit with some of the truth about the true reality they were all in, and just how outclassed they were!

Those paying attention would recall his disposition becoming more cheerful or feeling a sense of excitement only when he explored new areas, obtained new skills, or got his hands on powerful new treasures. His emotions were gradually settling down on the more impassive and calmer side, the cheerful and excited side of himself slowly being tucked away in a corner!

Many beings went about their tasks as most were simply learning a powerful inheritance and then cultivating in the Fire Law Space to form their Fire Origin Cores, with the Spiritual Land Beasts having a different task as they were brought down to the Time Space of the Spiritual Land for more intense training.

Noahs Ancient Blood Clone looked at the many resplendent fields of medicinal herbs and fruits spread out across the Time Space, this region that should have been one where people trained their hardest and surpassed those in the outside world slowly turning to become a region filled with farmland. He planted as much of the Spiritual Land Plants that had long growing and maturing times but gave out amazing herbs of medicinal fruits that held overflowing essence in the Time Space, using the increased ratio to grow these plants in a short period of time.

When the Spiritual Land was last upgraded to the 8th Tier, he had planted the newly available plants that were called Athramire and Kings Collard among others, where the products of these magical plants were being observed in front of him right now as he looked at farmlands overflowing with shimmering plants that dangled rainbow-colored fruits, while some were growing as scintillating herbs.

A profound aroma was released from these many fantastical fields as Noah waved his hands, collecting many of the fruits and herbs as he prepared to make pills once more while using the {Forge of the Silver Flame} that he received from a being that was shrouded in mysteries, but Noah already had a slight indication of a few possibilities!

This being that called himself the Karmic Sect master had also left him something he had not been using all that much since he received it, the item called [Lawful Nourishment] that helped one with cultivating laws. This was something that he planned to take out and use during this time as he was finally getting into the time when he was focusing on the comprehension of laws.

He put these thoughts aside as a cauldron materialized in front of his, a shining silver flame beginning to burn as the shimmering medicinal fruits and herbs that were filled with essence began to be refined and turned into pills.

He was utilizing these Spiritual Land Plants that were heavily conducive to the rise in strength of the Spiritual Land Beasts in order to achieve the goal he currently held of pushing the ranks of more than 10 beasts into the Soul Forging Realm.

He had a set of Soul Rank cores from the many Soul Ranked beings he defeated these past few weeks, but they would not be enough to push the ranks of 10 beasts from the Void and into the Soul Rank, with these medicinal herbs and fruits acting like a buffer to continue propelling the levels of these Beasts forward!

The Emperor Penguin, Arctic Wolf, Flying Spider, and 7 other of the most powerful beasts in the Spiritual Land stood respectfully near Noah as they saw the appearance of shining pills that flew towards them, their jaws spreading wide open as they swallowed and began digesting the treasures they were receiving.

On top of the pills filled with abundant essence, Noah waved his hands to bring out the Soul Rank Cores in his [Expansive Space] as they were also thrown towards the beasts, the process of raising the ranks of 10 beings from Void to Soul beginning in the Time Space of the Spiritual Land!

It was a busy time in this fantastical floating landmass as many beings were moving rapidly within it, many of them cultivating the Inheritance of the Disciple of Fire, with others excitedly being fed herbs and pills on top of Cores to push their ranks from one stage to another.

During this whole process, Noah was thinking about the other condition he had to fulfill if he wanted to bring the Spiritual Land from the 8th and into the 9th Tier.

Upgrade Conditions: 10+ Spiritual Land Beasts at the Soul Rank, A Small World Core

He looked under {Spiritual Land Management} as the words A Small Word Core shone with a blue l.u.s.ter, his thoughts moving rapidly as he thought of exactly what his actions would mean for him to obtain a World Core.

This momentary thought was quickly thrown out of his mind as his heart became firm, knowing he would face this problem head on as the time neared, and he would accomplish without fail no matter what he had to do!

His mind was already perusing through many memories of the beings he had devoured before as he searched for possible small worlds, whether it was those occupied and filled with life, those that did not have any civilizations or even those that were simply uninhabitable planets filled with unlivable environments. He had many possibilities, and did not necessarily have to destroy an entire world and its beings in order to achieve his goals!

He watched the progress of the Spiritual Land Beasts as their power continued to grow, biding his time as his mind-enhancing skills worked rapidly, his main body undergoing an entirely different ordeal as he had begun receiving even more treasures from the Celestials.
