3003 Attacks on Cappadocia! I

Being the Illusory Weaving of Knight Augustus, the Illusory Commander Augustus knew very well all the protective measures within Cappadocia and how to bypass them.

All he did afterward was remain with his Innate weaving of not even being visible in existence to other entities and overseeing authorities!

And then, he followed the weavings of his Master to arrive at those whose extremes and records he was entangled with.

Silently, he observed the remnant forces of the Legacy of Al-

Abalem on the 899th floor of the Pearl of Cappadocia around a luxurious golden table where drinks made of Fruits of Existence flowed within golden chalices.

Only Basileus Anaine and other members of the Legacy of Al-

Abalem were here, with the Archon Basileus Al-Abalem himself not being present!

But the Illusory Commander Augustus was not worried about this.

A man had to start somewhere.

And when he moved, he had to guarantee results!

He closed his eyes as he remained invisible while surrounded by swathes of Basileus from different Legacies and Enclaves, most being in the Sequor Stage of Existential Dominium Realm as the second most common was the lowest Neonate Stage, and there were a few prominent heads of Teleios Stage on the leading seats of some clusters.

The moment a move was made, an exit had to be made right after!

The Illusory Commander Augustus closed his eyes at this moment as he felt the weavings of his Master.

Within the great stretches of Cappadocia, he could sense the few Knights affected by the Loot Marker and the Observer they dragged along with them.

The intent of his Master's prophecy rang in his mind as he sensed the progression of these marks.

[Robbed Honor and glory become commonplace, their process undeniable once in place.]

The process was undeniable.

His former self had met the inevitable fate as he was reborn under a grand new light under the glory of the Weaver of Extremity!

He could feel that even with the help they came to Cappadocia to seek, another Teleios Stage brethren would be joining him soon enough.

But the power of the Imperium clustered here, there were those who could still reverse the terrifying power of his Master!

So the Illusory Commander Augustus waited patiently to strike.

At the most opportune time.

While waiting...

|Come forth to aid in accomplishing the commands of the Master.|

His weavings traversed across space and time to reach the Illusory Weavings of Neonate Stage Basileus his Master had already killed- those who joined the ranks of his Master first but he now led.

All of them would answer his call as the result of their mission was to grow the numbers of their Legion even more!

But since they couldn't hide as well as him at his current stage, their mission would be elsewhere.

There was also the Vacuous side of Entities, the side where his Master had granted additional advice due to the complex weavings of the Great Usurper mixed in with these forces!

Within Cappadocia.

In a restricted area filled with gloriously pulsing seas of existential authority.

The auras of powerful entities in the Sequor and Teleios Stage could be felt as they belonged to the powerful Legions stationed within Cappadocia, with the eyes of many Knights currently gazing towards the Existential Panacea Sea where one of the prized Imperium Knight Healers could be seen.

The Existential Panacea Sea was verdant gold and filled with an overabundance of life, and a massive Willow Tree rose from its very depths as some of its many branches were currently wrapped around three Imperium Knights and the Royal Observer Xavier.

The figures of Knight Palmino and Akalusia were standing in front of this massive tree as within moments, a terrifying aura far exceeding the Teleios Stage of Existential Dominium Realm appeared in the surroundings!

It was the appearance of an aged man with white gold hair and a pair of glasses, his demeanor of an old man entirely destroyed by his absolutely shocking might.

It was the Imperium Martial King overseeing many of the operations of the Existential Domain of Cappadocia!

The moment he arrived, Knight Akalusia and Knight Palmino bowed down along with the rest of the Knights in the surroundings, but the gaze of the Martial King wasn't even on them as he was locked onto the three Imperium Knights and the Observer Xavier.

His eyes instantly lit up with wrath as he saw the looks of agony on the Imperium Knights who were getting their Dominiums actively devoured, the efforts of the massive tree and its massive life force behind them not even being able to help.

Then he also saw the visage of the Royal Observer who had his face split and caved in the middle already, the rest of his body seemingly about to follow as when the Imperium Martial King appeared, Xavier and the others adopted looks of great hope- they hands reaching out to be saved!

They all wanted to be saved.

But looking at their state, the Martial King instantly moved his body towards the three Imperium Knights as Observer Xavier instantly despaired, the wavering of his intent instantly becoming critical as his Dominium instantly began fracturing and caving into itself, the cackling of the devilish smile and eyes on his forehead flaring victoriously!

It seemed like the ultimate insult before these Imperium Knights as their Dominiums buzzed, with the might of the Martial King erupting on his aged body as his hands descended on the first two Knights who were branded- blinding light erupting out and beginning to erase these brandings as the authority seemed undeniable!

Those who would be saved would be saved.

And those who would perish as the Protagonist prophesized would perish!

But at such a moment when one of the major powerhouses within Cappadocia was making a move to resolve an issue, and while many eyes were drawn towards the return of the Red Legion and the death of an Imperium Knight...an Illusory Commander made a move!
