Prompt after prompt came.

A Colorless and Cerulean radiance also flashed and spread thereafter whether within the Infinite Hyperversal Haven or within Recorded Histories or outside of it.

A few entities connected to Noah began to experience an unimaginable change as they came across a sensation and power that only a single being truly understood at this moment!

50% of the power of the Extremity of Harems.

It was unchanged from when this Concept had yet to attain Extremity, but the sheer fact that it was kept while Noah's power rose so much had shocking implications.

What was 50% of the power of an Entity that has just devoured the Records of a being at the Fifth Gradation of Extremity?

Adelaide, Barbatos, and a few others were about to find out as the edge they were about to gain...would heavily propel them towards the top of the Banner of Quintessence!

And thus, a bustle of busyness ensued within the Infinite Hyperversal Haven as the Quintessential Annals of Extremity neared its completion.

Located outside of the Aeonic Annals of Extremity, Noah and his people were in an extremely safe position as all they had to do at this point was grow stronger as a force that others could not even fathom.

And the terrifying concepts of Extremity that he held...would take them there.

Along with the entities he had here, there were also pathways of inclusion of other beings that at this very moment- Noah was already working on establishing.

Within the vast stretches of the Domains of the Sima Clan. freewebnovel.com

In an isolated space filled with an Aurora of purple liquified light, Noah's clone gazed at the figure of the Seer of Sima, Extremity of Foundation, and Erikson as he replied to the Seer who realized she couldn't feel him anymore!

[The next few days will be extremely critical as many choices will have to be made. The choice I present to the Sima Clan now...is for you all to truly join the banner of the Havenly Quintessential Hyperversal Emperor Lineage. Once this choice has been made, it is irreversible.]

WAA!

As he spoke, his nine pupils radiated a profound authority as behind him, a rift began to tear open in space as it emerged with a Colorless light.

It was a Colorless Instance Dungeon Portal of Quintessence...but it was one that led into the Infinite Hyperversal Haven!

Noah was confident of leaving this here as with his Records and the Records of the Infinite Hyperversal Haven isolated from the Aeonic Annals of Extremity, only those who held his lineage could enter as if any Entity at the Fifth Gradation of Extremity or above tried to enter- they would come to find their pathway denied as they could not follow any records back into the Infinite Hyperversal Haven.

The rift portal blared with light as the aged figure of the Seer of Sima stepped forward, gazing at it while sighing as she spoke out.

[Primogenitor, you're not trying to put an old lady like me to work, right? If I go in there, is it akin to me finding a place to retire in old age and just enjoy my time- or is there another Holy War we're joining the cause of by stepping in?] Her uniqueness and majesty emanated out as only the Extremity of Foundation and Erikson were filled with somber seriousness as they gazed at the portal before them.

They were part of the Sima Clan.

One of the Seven Clans of the Seven Aeonic Havens!

When making a decision like this, they had to consult the Sima Extremity Overlord as it involved too many things.

Their history was simply too deep with too many lives involved.

And they couldn't just...

The thoughts of the Extremity of Foundation and Erikson came to a halt as in the corner of their eyes, they suddenly realized that the Seer of Sima was walking forward serenely after saying what she said.

"Granny, you can't just-!"

Erikson shouted out towards her as she waved her hands with a harrumph.

[I've lived for too long and seen too many things. I'm curious about what this Primogenitor here has in store...so I'll see you all on the other side.]

...!

With wanton recklessness and a laid back demeanor, the Seer of Sima stepped into the massive Colorless Instance Dungeon Portal of Quintessence under the shocked gazes of a father and son!

A Tier 4 Hyperversal Extremity began to traverse across records towards the Infinite Hyperversal Haven.

She was the first...and it was unknown just how many would follow after her as while Noah was living up to his name of a Demon of the Seven Havens by destroying one of the Seven Clans, he was also setting up the pathway to begin devouring the rest of them!

And the destruction of the Avernus Clan...was entering its end stages as in addition to the ongoing chaos and desertions of their forces within the Vitalis Hyperversal Domains, their Legions outside of it were now entirely destroyed as an appalling reality played out outside of the Vitalis Hyperversal Domains.

The vast majority of those belonging to the Avernus Clan renounced them after they felt unprecedented danger.

As for those who went against their intuition and tried to apply their common sense to the being threatening them...

POOF!

Their fate was a deadly one of ash and dust as all those at The Third Gradation of Extremity and below faced such a deadly fate!

There was immense and deafening silence in this region from the Vitalis and Vacuous Hyperversal Legions alike.

Such a catastrophic chain of death had not occurred since moments ago when an unknown Esteemed Emperor also reaped the lives of 100 Tier 2 Avernus Extremities.

But now, all of those who had not renounced from Tier 3 and below perished as following the terrifying words of the True Emperor of Quintessence, the wills of all nearby Entities came to lock onto the two Tier 4 Avernus Extremities who now floated without their Legions.

Their bodies were trembling as if their very existence was facing an unfathomable and terminal threat! Their faces were ashen as they wavered on the space they floated on, but they didn't fall and turn to dust as some more powerful beings noticed...there was a foreign buzzing of over a hundred thousand Crimson Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Rune Scriptures surrounding each of them.

This was what was keeping them alive.

This...was not their own power.

HUUM!

Near the visage of the Extremity of Quintessence, the glorious figure of Emperor Shao appeared as his expression was a serious one.

The power stabilizing and keeping the lives of the two Tier 4 Avernus Extremities...was also not his.

[Sigh.]

An ancient voice echoed out like booming thunder across the battlefield as for the first time, the blaring aura of an Entity at the Fifth Gradation of Extremity emerged in this Holy War.

With it, the visage of an old man shrouded by blinding white light appeared.

His might was ever rising as with his appearance alone, a flood of Vitalis Hyperversal Extremity Authority spread out for light years as the Vacuous Legions were blinded and forced to move back ever closer to their Savior's Arks!
