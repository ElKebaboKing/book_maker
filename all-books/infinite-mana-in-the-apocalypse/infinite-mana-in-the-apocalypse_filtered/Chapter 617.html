Chapter 615 - Assimilator!

Have you ever come across tens of exploding suns?

Deep in the center of these explosions as well, each of the scorching heat of these suns actually being directed at you as they seemed to endlessly rush forward? It was a terrifying sight to say the least!

BOOOM!

The Grand Controller of Chaos was taking the central position of defending as he was backed on his right by the Fire Law Master and on his left by the Blessed with the [Ice Man] Trait.

Their gazes were finally serious as the essence of the Grand Controller of Chaos expertly moved to create a thick black barrier, this barrier being covered by freezing ice in front and a layer of a flaming barrier in the back as the three beings worked together to withstand a barrage of exploding suns!

BOOM! BOOM!

The suns seemed nearly endless as the face of the Grand Controller became extremely serious, his voice ringing out to the others as they made their moves.

"Sajar, Johnny Boy, youre with me in taking on this small being. He should be the strongest protector in this Galaxy, yet seems to not understand the concept of preserving ones mana with such reckless attacks."

His words were careful as they were directed to the Fire Law Masters in his party and the Blessed holding the [Ice Man] Trait. All the meanwhile, he slowly analyzed the situation before them while the barriers they erected were able to stop the barrage of suns that continued to explode with searing heat in front of them!

"You three, take on the oversized creatures behind him, and dont hesitate to use your life-saving Talismans in case something unexpected happens, I will rush in to resolve anything else."

He then spoke towards the two other Blessed beings and Space Law Master as the one holding the Trait of [Ability Mimic] nodded with vigor.

The words of the Grand Controller were filled with strength as he breathed in after finishing his commands, the other 5 Outsiders nodding in understanding as they saw the unique power bubbling on his body!

This was a power that they did not expect this Grand Controller to have, a power that showed he had actually already embarked on the path of an Assimilator!

This made their resolve even stronger as in the next moment, two words filled with intent left the mouth of the Grand Controller of Chaos.

|Chaotic Dispersal|

RUMBLE!

A star shaking might erupted out with these words, the intent of a Beginner Assimilator rushing towards the endless suns as it carried with it a unique authority, this essence wiping away all the coming suns as if they didnt exist in the first place!

This intent continued forward until it found the source of the enormous balls of fire, only being stopped as it clashed with another intent that Noah released!

BZZZT!

An impasse came to be as the two sides were able to see each other once more, the Outsiders seeing the figure of Noah calmly observing them as a distinct smile was visible on his face, apparently very pleased at the way his opening attack had gone.

The Grand Controller of Chaos locked onto Noahs figure as the party he was with followed his words!

The Space Law Master became a white streak of light as he shot towards the Terror of the Void, the one holding the Trait of [Supernova] going to match Cthulhu, and the one with the [Ability Mimic] Trait actually locking eyes with Noah first as her body shone with an Incandescent light before she went over to match the Ancient Terror.

Others might not have known, but the incandescent light on the Outsiders body when she locked eyes with Noah was her actually using her trait as she became able to mimic the terrifying skill he had used, the Outsider wanting them to have a taste of their own medicine when she went to match the Ancient Terror!

This left the Grand Controller of Chaos, the Blessed holding the [Ice Man] trait called Johnny Boy, and the Fire Law Controller to face Noah himself, these beings moving carefully as they did not want any mistakes after finding such an opportune galaxy.

"You dont seem to have even embarked on the path past a Controller yet, Native. Why dont we have a talk where I might even show you the way?"

The voice of the Grand Controller who could also be called a Beginner Assimilator rang out towards Noah, the unique essence he used to completely wipe away tens of scorching suns being extremely potent as his right hand pointed forward!

The other two Outsiders backed away from him as one moved with their Authority and the other released terrifying coldness, this Beginner Assimilator continuing to talk as his aura only shot up higher.

"You see, after completely comprehending a Universal Law and becoming a Controller, one must then Assimilate their law comprehension where the ultimate goal..."

OOOOM!

His hand released a milky black aura as Noah could see it was an extremely potent essence of Chaos, and it seemed to be moving in conjunction with the Beginner Assimilators intentions as it wrapped around his body before he completely disappeared in a flash of chaotic light!

"...is becoming one with the very Universal laws themselves."

The Beginner Assimilator actually appeared instantly behind Noah, his fist covered with this extremely potent chaotic essence as it smashed into Noah before he could even react!

BOOM!

The light of chaos overflowed as a blow was dealt by the Outsiders for the first time, even the eyes of the Terrors looking towards Noahs figure that had been shot forward like a cannonball towards the other two Outsiders.

WAA!

Halfway through before Noah even reached the Law Master of Fire and Johnny Boy that were already preparing their attacks, his body came to a stop as a tinge of golden light began to cover him, his face shining in the dark space as one could actually see an expression of joy on his face!

This was because he had affirmed the path forward that he knew of after becoming a Controller, and he had felt the strength of this stage as the strike that had smashed into him from this Grand Controller that had begun Assimilating his comprehensions was enough to injure him!

But that was all- just enough to injure him as the two Outsiders behind him could observe his back that was a gory mess of blood rapidly healing as good as new, the distinct gold light that represented fate beginning to fully release from him.

His eyes were full of joy as he stared at the Beginner Assimilator as if he had found a rare treasure, his mind already seeing the path of laws laid out in front of him.

First, you comprehend the laws, and then you become one with them!

Assimilate them!

This was the stage above a Controller, this was the stage where beings sought to become intertwined with the Universal Laws as they freely used them to do their bidding!
