Chapter 4884: Native!

"The Everythings of what was collapsed are mine and not mine. They belong to me and they do not belong to me. They must be given to me instantly and they must never be given to me at all."

BOOM!

Noah uttered THE Rite of Paradoxical Inheritance while consuming pieces of THE Hollow Echo, the paradoxical words flowing from his existence with practiced ease as the carved portions of colorless flame began to dissolve into streams of pure authority.

In the next moment, the Immensity and Depths of THE Hollow Echo surged into him like rivers finding the ocean.

The weight was heavy. Even diminished by the destruction of its Manifestation, even weakened by the systematic targeting of its structural vulnerabilities, THE Hollow Echo had still been an echo of THE Creature himself. Its foundations held density that lesser Absolutes could only dream of, and all of that density was now flooding into Noahs infinite existence with a force that made his blue-gold flames roar brighter!

And where its cut pieces had floated moments before, something else began to form.

Colorless light coalesced into shape and substance, the remnants of THE Hollow Echos existence crystallizing into an artifact that pulsed with authority.

Noah looked at all of this calmly.

For this action of momentous achievement, decimating an Absolute without any tricks or external help, without THE Primordial Paradoxs intervention, without relying on anything but his own transformed existence, he breathed in deeply to celebrate the sheer magnitude of what hed accomplished.

Then... he got over himself.

He didnt need to be overly prideful or happy. That kind of ego was how beings stagnated, how they convinced themselves theyd reached heights that didnt require further climbing.

"Just a Hollow Echo of THE Creature. An Echo of an Absolute."

His voice was deliberately dismissive even as the power continued flooding into his foundations.

There was much more to be done, and he wouldnt let his ego go wild just on this. THE Hollow Echo had been a test of his new capabilities, a confirmation that THE Civilizational Instinctus worked. But it wasnt the ceiling of what he needed to achieve. It wasnt even close!
