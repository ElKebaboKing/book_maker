Chapter 4794: History! I

As he said such terrifying words, an unfathomable Immensity that nobody could sense or see passed down.

It was the weight of all of THE First Tongue across Observable Existence pressing down on an Absolute...

And snuffing out absolutely everything!

Someone had failed their Claim on one of the four aspects of the Oldest Paradox of Existence.

And the penalty for failure... was annihilation.

Before Noah, the bodies of THE Weavers stood still.

"..."

No words.

No Immensity.

Nothing.

Even Noah breathed hard as if he couldnt half believe it had occurred. As if he couldnt believe it had worked!

He beckoned to his Heart and Grimoire.

They floated back to him.

His Heart reinserted itself into his chest, muscles closing around it as if welcoming it home. His Grimoire sank back into his existence, its pages closing with finality.

And a moment later...

His expression became heavy.

His existence buzzed with pain.

Crimson-gold blood began to leak from his nose and mouth.

...!

Noah felt breathless.

It had... worked?

An Absolute... had fallen?

The moment such a thing was truly ascertained...

BOOM!

From where THE Weavers floated, cracks appeared in reality itself!

These cracks glowed with obsidian-gold brilliance that shattered Existence itself.

The cracks spread outward from the point of death, spanning across The Palaestra in branching patterns that looked like lightning frozen in space!

From all around, Hoplites scrambled away from the spreading fractures.

Lochagoi fled toward the edges of the Arena.

Polemarchs erected barriers to protect themselves and those near them.

The Reckoning Stone beneath the point of collapse began to crumble!

The Four Pillars trembled!

The Nexus of Truth blazed with light it had never shown before.

Glossikos and the other Absolutes... stood where they were with a myriad of expressions and incredulity.

Glossikos looked at Noah with something that transcended shock. Her figurr had gone completely still.

Paradoxos wore expressions of fury and disbelief on both faces simultaneously.

Khaotikos had stopped laughing entirely, his features frozen in something that might have been awe.

Ontikos... for the first time in recorded history, Ontikos moved. He leaned forward on his throne of uncarved marble.

As such a terrifying thing occurred, the region where THE Weavers stood collapsed entirely!

It became a terrifying vacuum!

The bodies of THE Weavers disappeared into the void created through their failure!

Nothing remained where they had been!

Only an absence!

Only a wound in reality where something that should have existed no longer did!

BOOM!

Noah stood at the edge of the vacuum that had once been THE Weavers.

Blood continued to leak from his nose and mouth.

What truly happened when an Absolute died?

It was such a rare and impossible thing.

At this moment, at the point where the vacuum bloomed where THE Weavers used to be...

BOOM!

The vacuum collapsed in on itself again and again.

The cycle repeated with increasing intensity, reality itself unable to process what had just occurred! The absence where an Absolute had existed moments before was too profound for normal space to accommodate! It was not merely empty. It truly and unimaginably was the negation of fullness. The opposite of presence. A wound in Existence that refused to heal.

The entire scene felt tense and sacred.

As if something sacred had fallen.

As if reality itself was holding its breath, uncertain how to proceed.

And at such a moment...

An obsidian-gold pillar of Paradox instantly shot up and down.

It pierced through the ceiling of The Palaestra, through the artificial sky, through barriers that should have been impenetrable! It drove downward through the Reckoning Stone, through the foundations of the Arena, through layers of reality that descended beyond normal perception.

Other pillars of Paradox shot out in all other ordinal directions.

North and south! East and west! And diagonal trajectories that shouldnt have existed in normal space! The pillars spread outward like the death throes of something fundamental, announcing to all of Observable Existence that one of its Absolutes had ceased to be.

Noah observed all this while his body floated gloriously and illustriously.

His chest had sealed. His Heart and Grimoire had returned to their proper places. But blood still leaked from his nose and mouth, evidence of the price he had paid for this victory.
