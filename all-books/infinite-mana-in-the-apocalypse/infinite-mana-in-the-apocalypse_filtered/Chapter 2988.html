Chapter 2986 The Eyes of Vassals! II

She was adorned in a black veil that covered her from head to toe, her fair skin only visible through her eyes as she felt like she was back during her tenure as a Shadow Guard.

It was a position that benefitted her even now as since she was a close aide to the foremost Queen of the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed, she was blessed with many things.

Her Frozen Queen had given her Fruits of Existence freely as her own Gradations had risen heavily, where even though she wasn't among the die hard fans of power, she arrived at the Seventh Gradation of Extremity!

And now, she was observing the visible change and alterations of the weavings of Entities who had lived for billions of years as they came to understand just which lands they were currently in.

One of the foremost case studies was the Primogenitor of Steel who was among those that reigned supreme in the Vitalis Hyperversal Domain.

After the battle with the Lesser Mimeisthais and the subsequent Loot that was granted to him and many others, the stage of power of this old entity...finally came to reach the Ninth Gradation of Extremity.

And when the scribe watched his expression, he seemed more dazed and lost for the most part as if he was questioning what he was doing for millions upon millions of years before this!

Apart from him, there were others whose expressions were filled with determination as the choices they took led them to a similar stage.

The visage of the Young Emperor Prince Henry was bathed in the golden luster of an Existential Extremity Authority as he also stood at the Ninth Gradation of Extremity, his gaze far above as if he were looking at a shadow of his father that he could never overcome.

Sis Barbatos, another Queen of the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed, held a stage of terrifying power that seemed to be half in the Gradations of Extremity, and half outside of it.

Her power was unique as she and the other Queens shared a great deal of strength with the Master, and since his Realm was only becoming more unfathomable, so would theirs!

Then, there were other new entrants like Ishtar, Sargon, and the Cimmerian Architect who had defeated Mimeisthais of their own and attained enough Fruits of Existence to touch upon the Ninth Gradation of Extremity...along with one unlikely contender that The Scribe had been watching closely over the last few days.

The Demon that had fallen from her throne a long time ago...her fair crimson hands now rose as she seemed to be looking at who she was without believing it.

Among those who had made it.

Among those who stood at the peak of the Gradations of Extremity.

The Daughter of Hatred questioned with her own eyes whether she really stood at the Ninth Gradation of Extremity!

Around her, those who had been with her through the past battles were a stage before her but just as happy, with the figure of Ayame coming and hugging Lilith from behind while releasing a bellow of happiness- the weavings of Infinity pulsing around them!

Yes.

Joy.

A rarity in this rat race of more and more power, but some beings could bask in their accomplishments- even if momentarily as at this moment, only half of their Wills were here.

The other half...even the Scribe's will included, were all pulled towards another unique location that seemed to exist between the weavings of Existence and Records of the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed!

The Scribe closed her eyes as she sensed her other half in a place recently designed by the Existential Omniversal Emperor.

In this space, an endless clear sea could be felt all around them as when they opened their eyes, there wasn't any sign of the chains they knew wrapped around them before.

Only an endless blue sea all around as when they gazed far above, they seemed to barely catch a glimpse of an endless sky!

|The Quintessential Gradations of Extremity...|

The Scribe called out with her intent as in her hands, a white book appeared along with a Colorless pen that began to write down everything she could see!

She was in the waters of the Seventh Gradation of Extremity, but the seas were so clear that an innumerable distance above her, she could almost make out the figures of the Young Master, the Primogenitor of Steel, Barbatos, and many others far above her in the next Gradations.

There were hundreds of other Vassals of the Master all around her as they found the blue waters all around them...continued to passively flow into their Existence as they reinforced it and the Concepts within them.

As if even if they didn't do anything, they would continue to be elevated until they eventually reached the Ninth Gradation of Extremity!

Who else but the Master could do this?

The Scribe smiled at such a thought as she wondered, how different was it for the Young Master and others who were swimming in the waters of the Ninth Gradation of Extremity? How much time would pass before they reached the endless skies?

She didn't know for sure, but she knew she had to have this level of power herself in order to continue her work as the Scribe of the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed.

So she also became filled with determination as she began to ascend the Quintessential Gradations of Extremity, the white book in her hands closing as she held it tight!

On the cover of the book, it read...{The Quintessential Fable of the Existential Omniversal Emperor}!

In the dazzlingly vast Omniverse.

|Where the hell is it...|

The voice of a certain Sanctus Princess rang out as she continued to search atop her Draconic Knight for something she knew was in this region to no avail!

Her body was already emanating a heavy Existential light that spread out all around, but she became stern as she began to spread it ever further at this moment.

Unbeknownst to her though...a dazzling cerulean vessel in the form of a Ruler had crossed great distances and was arriving towards the same region she was in to search for the same thing she was seeking!
