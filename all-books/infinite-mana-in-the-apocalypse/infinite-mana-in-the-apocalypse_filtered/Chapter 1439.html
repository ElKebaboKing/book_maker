Chapter 1437: The Royal Blight Serpent Dragon- Yacucoatl! I

Within an infinitesimal region in the endless Boundaries Between Realities that held an infinite number of Realities.

The Tyrant Beast Pyxis was watching a certain direction very closely as he waited to see a scene of an injured Primordial flying out, his Origin brimming with energy as he was full of focus and ready to lash out at any moment!

Silent waves of destiny echoed out as just at this time, waves of unstable power echoed out as the luminous eyes of Pyxis sharpened, focusing on a newly appearing aura that was traversing across the Boundary Between Realities at an extremely high speed.

Of course, as experts at the peak of the First Firmament of Ascendancy, Pyxis and the Royal Blight Serpent Dragon Yacucoatl.....could easily see the rushing figure!

With streaks of golden blood on her white dress and a torn veil that revealed a breathtaking face, it was the figure of Cecilia that they had been waiting for this whole time!

'Finally!'

BZZZT!

Crackles of light surrounded Pyxis as his vibrant pair of wings pulsed with immense light, two profound Blue Tier Ascendancy Halos erupting from his body as they screamed of terror and regality.

His body instantly turned illusory as he shot forward at such a high speed that would make him a mere blur of light to most beings in the First Firmament of Ascendancy, and he arrived before the 'distraught' Cecilia in an instant as he could just make out her shocked expression that he had seen too many times.

It was the expression of terror and coming death that the Tyrant Beast Pyxis had seen too many times, his heart screaming in glee as his right claw thundered down with the support of two Blue Tier Ascendancy Halos- the claw manifesting wrathful white flames of edicts that coalesced to form an illusory claw of this Ender of Reality!

The blood of a Primordial! It was a unique delicacy that very few existences could claim to have tasted, with even fewer daring to go against this Bloodline. But throughout history, there were always those that wanted to try! And Enders of Reality that had broken their restrictions and could move freely across Realities...there was an enticing glow around Primordials that they could not deny.

The enormous figure of the Winged Tiger Ender of Reality shot down with an illusory claw made of white Flames of Edict, intent on heavily wounding the Primordial before him in a manner that she could not even react to!

The surrounding void itself trembled as at this highest and most crucial moment, the eyes of Pyxis fluttered and were startled as he saw the terrified expression on the figure of 'Cecilia' fading away.

Immense coldness replaced her face soon after as a domineering aura erupted from him, an aura that came with a blinding crimson light!

It was so fast and the two beings were so close that there was no time to react or make a move, the speed that 'Cecilia' displayed being even more horrific than the speed Pyxis showed earlier as the attack released was even faster! The crimson glow released shockingly came in contact with the illusory flames of edict as in the next instant...the white flames were wiped out entirely as the red glow that had now manifested into a glimmering Halo went forward to smash onto the body of Pyxis!

SHHIING...BOOOM!

It was too fast.

Even a being at the peak of the First Firmament of Ascendancy could not react as even the far away Royal Blight Serpent Dragon Yacucoatl only saw a brief flash of crimson light before a resounding explosion echoed out!

"GAAH!"

A Winged Tiger's limb flew as golden blood surged out like rivers, the terrifying crimson halo also smashing into its body as it actually cut apart the Blue Tier Halos surrounding Pyxis to smash into his body!

...!

A single crimson halo actually cut apart two blue ones! Could this even be done? Was it even a possibility?

Golden blood overflowed as the expression of Pyxis was twisted in pain and disbelief, leaving behind a genuine river of blood as he was knocked back.

BZZT!

His Origin trembled painfully as he was dealt a heavy injury, and even more so- he couldn't stop dazedly staring at his two Halos that even now...they had a smooth cut as they didn't connect anymore!

"Pyxis!"

The bellow of the Royal Blight Serpent Dragon Yacucoatl shook him out of his stupor as when he stared forward and tried to wrap protective essence around him, he gazed up to see the figure of 'Cecilia' changing to a devilishly handsome man that glimmered with the light of cosmos throughout his whole body, a crimson Ascendancy Halo surrounding him as this being...wasn't even gazing at him at this moment.

What Pyxis saw was a mere ploy!

A ploy that heavily injured an existence at the Peak of the First Firmament of Ascendancy as thereafter, the eyes of this being actually dared to gaze elsewhere.

He was actually gazing towards the direction of Yacucoatl as if Pyxis was now insignificant, the mesmerizing voice of this existence ringing out as he turned into a blur of light towards the Royal Blight Serpent Dragon!

"Heavily injured with his battle power heavily reduced...you can wear him out now, Hyperion!"

OOOOM!

The moment his words finished, the figure of Hyperion appeared in utter grandeur as his trunk bellowed out a horn of madness and power, a half-filled Ascendancy Halo fusing into his body as he shot towards Pyxis like a blazing pillar of light!

Wear him down. These were the domineering words spoken out as when it came to finishing Pyxis, this job would fall back to Noah himself.

With his Origin creaking in pain and his Ascendancy Halo literally cut, Pyxis faced a wrathful Ender of Reality as the Royal Blight Serpent Dragon faced the oppressive pressure of a being who could somehow even injure Ascendancy Halos.

The murky green clouds and shadows around Yacucoatl became ever denser as they lashed out poisonously, the imperious eyes of this serpent the size of a Cosmos brightening up with immense power as wondrously- three Ascendancy Halos released from it!

Not a single one was white.

Two Blue Halos could be seen as the third and most powerful one...it was another color entirely!
