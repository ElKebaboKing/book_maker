Chapter 1423: The Might of the Resplendent Treasure Emperor! II

The Nomological Edict of the Resplendent Treasure Emperor was an Edict that at its base granted 100 Billion percent to All Parameters- something that was only seen in the crimson Ascendancy Halo of Catastrophe that was still unranked!

This was why Noah's power was so oppressive.

This was why events had undertaken the route they had!

Noah drew in the concepts of an Ultra Rare Bloodline along with the capabilities unique to Primordial Beasts and the Universal Emperor Race- this being the key that when coupled to many Daos and laws...granted him an INSUPERABLE tier Edict.

It also elevated his Bloodline of Destiny Goblin further as he became the Destiny Goblin King, a development that truly made it set in stone that he had to scour the Forgotten Treasure Realities!

But...none of this would explain his choice to smash a Cosmic Filament Core in his hands instead of devouring it. After all, a single Universal Devouring of such an Origin would grant him said Origin itself. Yet he was about to destroy it because what he could gain from its destruction...was far more than what its devouring could grant!

For this, one had to turn to the new abilities stemming from the Nomological Edict of the Resplendent Treasure Emperor- <Existential Devourer> and <Loot Multiplier>.

<Existential Devourer>(PASSIVE):: Due to the unique essence of the Resplendent Treasure Emperor, all defeated existences are devoured of a large aspect that makes them as the Resplendent Treasure Emperor gains their Memories, Laws, Daos, Bloodlines, Abilities, Fortune, and Destiny. The user can inevitably devour Cosmos to gain the Essence of Reality as achieving full completion in the Edict of Resplendent Treasure Emperor will even allow them to devour Realities in exchange for the Essence of Reality and obtain Bloodlines of Edicts from fallen enemies.

Existential Devourer! This ability alone was oppressive enough from this Insuperable Edict as with it, Noah could snatch the fortune and destiny of his enemies as even more than that...their Bloodlines!

So Cecilia's Celestial Vine Bloodline...would be granted to Noah if he killed this existence. Furthermore, the Primordial Bloodline this being carried...it would also be taken out by Noah just through this ability.

The Bloodline of a Primordial! Something supposedly sacred that others could not touch...Existential Devourer would grant it.

And if Noah achieved full completion of this Edict- even obtaining the Bloodlines of Edicts his fallen enemies had was possible.

This was uniquely a second method Noah could utilize to obtain the Edicts of others, where he also had a similar option with the Nomological Edict of Apocalypse as it could break down Ascendancy Halos to grant unique rewards. But the lower Tier Apocalypse would only grant Edicts- while the Resplendent Treasure Emperor granted the Bloodlines of Edicts which meant Noah would obtain the completed Edicts and their Flames!

All he had to do was complete this Edict, which might prove to be hard as even now, it still lay at 1% as Noah didn't have any residual Essence of Reality left to play with.

But all these ...were rewards from a single skill stemming from this Edict.

Then you had <Loot Multiplier>, which actually had a fairly simple description!

<Loot Multiplier>(PASSIVE):: The Resplendent Treasure Emperor is unwilling to receive the bare minimum of Loot as his essence passively multiples the rewards from defeated enemies, granting him 500,000% Increased Loot Quantity and 50,000% Increased Loot Rarity.

500,000% Increased Loot Quantity and 50,000% Increased Loot Rarity!

In comparison, the empowered Cosmic Dao of Loot with its base stats boosted through being turned to Runic Dao Lines only granted 50,000% Increased Loot Quantity and 5,000% Increased Loot Rarity. A factor of more than ten times as the results that could come from this were utterly fantastical. The single skill of Loot Multiplier alone granted such rich rewards that it could have been a System for others!

This was why at this moment, Noah's hands were grasping onto a Cosmic Filament Core as he crushed down, the essence of Apocalypse and the Resplendent Treasure Emperor flashing out as the closing of his hands was akin to the opening of a pandora's box!

BOOOM!

Under the gaze of all the important existences within this Reality, a myriad of Cosmos in a Filament were crushed as a blinding light was released.

CAAA!

The moment the Origin was crushed, a fearsome cry echoed out from a portion of it as it stemmed from a unique golden bloodline- one of extreme ancientness and oppressiveness as it disdained everything around it. Such a bloodline was wrathful and unbridled that a being that contained it...actually had its core destroyed!

It cried out with power as it located and lashed onto the culprit right away, its golden blood splashing onto Noah's hands as uniquely- it bypassed the many layers of defenses- even from the INSUPERABLE Nomological Edict of the Resplendent Treasure Emperor! It didn't cause any damage, but the moment after this blood landed on Noah, the area above his head began to distort as an illusory golden skull began to form.

An illusory golden skull that continued to change forms from human to a dragon, to a tortoise and then to a phoenix...it constantly shifted into the skulls of many races that weren't even known or seen before as all of them were staring down with immense wrath towards Noah.

The constantly changing golden illusory skull staring down angrily towards Noah signified nothing else but an existence becoming a Marked One!

Someone that could be located uniquely by Primordials and hunted down without end until they perished!

Whether Cosmic Filament Rank Primordials had to be mobilized. Whether Reality Rank Elder Primordials had to be mobilized! Anything and everything would be done as those that touched the bloodline of a Primordial would have nothing else to do but perish.

"Haha..you fool..."

Amidst her unwillingness and shock, the croaking voice of a dying consciousness of Cecilia echoed out as she gazed towards Noah's cosmic-sized figure!

"The Blood of Primordials...is not to be taken lightly."

BZZZT!

Her golden green figure of a Celestial Vine could no longer support itself as she fell back, Noah waving his hands to stop the horrendous essence of Apocalypse spread out across the Domains from beginning to devour this vast body that was in itself a treasure.

Even as her consciousness faded, she still left behind shocking words to not dare take lightly the bloodline of a Primordial!

"Even now...you cannot escape death...as a Marked One!"

Her fading voice was filled with mockery as if she got the last laugh, and yet Noah saw the shattered Origin dripping out stellar objects within his hands as he gazed up towards the wrathful skull above him, his voice echoing out calmly.

"It is merely the title of a Marked One, nothing much under my eyes!"

RUMBLE!

The surrounding void quaked as an illustrious golden light shone!

Under Noah's domineering words, even the lone figure of Eckert holding his dull golden sword paled in comparison! He wasn't worried about becoming a Marked One because naturally, he had a solution for it.

A solution that of course...stemmed from the INSUPERABLE Nomological Edict of the Resplendent Treasure Emperor!
