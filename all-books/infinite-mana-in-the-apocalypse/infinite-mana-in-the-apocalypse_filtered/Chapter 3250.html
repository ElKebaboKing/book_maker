Chapter 3248 Revelations II

Protector Lucretia was silent as she didn't say anymore.

She gathered herself as she still held the dagger in her hands, her authority beginning to rise once more as her face showed hesitation.

This being now truly knew her secret regardless of how they arrived here. She was having internal turmoil on how to take care of this if he truly wasn't involved with Outerversal Morphons!

But she didn't reply.

So Noah pushed even harder.

"Is the one you are bound to Savitar? Or someone else? Are we walking into a trap right now as we head towards the Half Tainted Omniverse?"

...!

His words caused her face filled with difficulty to change as she shook her head vehemently.

|What? I have nothing to do with Savitar. And he...that Entity...he promised safety for me and Valeria. He never goes back on his words.|

"Safety for you and your sister. So the rest of those around you might die."

|...|

Noah nodded thoughtfully as he processed many things.

Looking at his nonchalant demeanor in the midst of all of this, Protector Lucretia seemed to have figured something out as she put the dagger down and sighed.

|This is just another one of your bodies, isn't it? Without the power of someone similar to my Sister, I cannot eradicate you along with the root of all of your possible Existence wherever you are.|

She stated something wholly and true as Noah gazed at her and nodded with a smile.

She looked at him deeply once more as if she were trying to figure him out.

|Who are you, really? What do you want?|

...!

Noah smiled.

He had been asked this question many times before.

And his answer always differed.

Towards this red haired entity before him...

"Just think of me as a helper. Just like Old Man Eudemus, I seek to solve the same problems you all face."

|...|

His words caused Protector Lucretia to turn placid as she sighed and put her dagger away.

|You didn't say anything when you found out, so you won't say anything now, is that it? I have been tightly carrying this stain on my existence for many years now. Another person knowing of it fills me with great anxiety as if you suddenly decide to flap your mouth...|

She sat on another throne as she massaged her temples and thought of many possible paths she could take.

She made a ridiculous mistake today by being rash as now, she was in a predicament with someone she didn't know all too well.

Someone who, at this moment, was looking at her as if he were observing a certain exotic animal.

"You won't tell me who the Outerversal Morphon behind you is? I may be able to help."

Lucretia turned her gaze towards him with a deprecating look.

|It doesn't matter, does it? What would someone like you even do? This isn't something that can be solved even if you attain a Fabled Omniverse. I am bound. I will remain bound. Until...|

Her gaze was filled with longing.

As if she wanted to be free.

But she could only shake her head and sigh.

She had tried far too many times.

|No. I cannot even say his name. If I do, he will use it as an excuse to gaze at me. I've already made many mistakes today, so I'll make another small one by saying that among Outerversal Morphons, he is granted the distinction of a Great One. Great...that single word means and stands for something more grandiose than you can ever imagine.|

Protector Lucretia shook her head as she seemed tired.

On the other side, Noah sat on his throne and nodded as he gained one piece of information after another.

Uniquely, he raised his head and looked at a high point inside of the miniaturized domain they were in within this Omniversal Voyager Vessel.

At the ceiling of this Domain, he was staring at something.

He was surprised about another thing this whole time.

Because even though Protector Lucretia felt the gaze of her sister fade away, the means of Omniversal Entities were profound.

Only Noah who had begun to look closely at Matter and Dark Matter, and had his status as the Weaver of Extremity and Existence elevated, was able to notice the thin pulsing of an interaction that was as small as a Quark dancing above them like an eye.

An eye that Protector Lucretia didn't see, but Noah saw clearly for it to still be the gaze of Command Protector Valeria.

She saw everything.

And yet...

There was only calmness in the surroundings as nothing happened.

After her sister making a mistake and revealing that she was a Delegatus of an Outerversal Morphon, nothing happened even though she witnessed it all!

"..."

Noah's eyes turned ever brighter.

The sister knew?

The Omniversal Entity of the Commonwealth leading a powerful team of Protectors knew that her Sister was an Agent of Outerversal Morphons?!

"This gets better and better...."

He stroked his chin while looking at the small unseen point above, making it clear to this gaze that he could see it!

This gaze that was originating all the way towards the front of the Omniversal Voyager where the Command Protector Valeria was, along with the three Royal Existential Omniversal Simian Protectors.

The three Royal Existential Omniversal Simians were talking amongst themselves as they reclined on radiant thrones while eating vibrant Fruits pulsing with Existence.

|This is the first time I see Lucretia sneak around with someone else since she was with The Great Luminary Protector. Wouldn't he be mad that his replacement is a newbie she met just a day ago?|

One Royal Simian, Protector Actaeon, had a gaze of interest as they sent a mental message to the other two Protectors- Orpheus and Ajax.

|An Omniversal Star of our Commonwealth or a newbie who has not even come close to a Fabled Omniverse...haha. Mmm, I heard they clashed and disagreed on something a while ago, so she can do whatever she wants. But hey...we've been with her for a while, but she didn't go for any of us. Do you think she prefers them furless? She doesn't know the pleasure of a Simian full of fur, or she wouldn't go bac-|

"Are you all little girls who have so much time to gossip when we are heading to a Half Tainted Omniverse right now? Review the physiology of our enemy if you have so much time!"

The booming voice of Command Protector Valeria shook them as they cleared their throats and rose straighter on their thrones.

Command Protector Valeria gazed at them coldly before she continued her sharp focus on steering the Omniversal Voyager while protecting them and veiling everything.

Her power was vast.

Her might was unfathomable.

She was even fearlessly going to hunt down a body of a Great One at this very moment!

But when she recalled what she just observed between her sister and Osmont.

When she recalled what her own little sister had never dared to even come to her for help.

When she recalled all of this...she felt so powerless.

But such a stifling feeling was temporary.

Inside her, a power that very few entities understood swirled.

With this power, she would cut down all Morphons herself if she had to.

The one they targeted now. The one behind her Sister.

She would eradicate every single one.

No matter the cost, she would find freedom for her sister!

As for this newly entering Protector Osmont that was an extreme unknown that could change many things...

Valeria's eyes became sharp as a part of her will stared straight into his eyes that were turned up in a distant room of this vessel.

As if he could see her clearly, and he was smiling freely as if none of that mattered.

While Noah stared fearlessly into the will of an omniversal entity in a corner of what the Outerversal Morphons considered the Megalos Ynnere, his Existence was about to undergo an unfathomable change within the Infinite Omniverse!

Inside of the Infinite Omniverse.
