Chapter 3407 The Erasure of Noah Osmont! I

"Ah...!"

A scream that wasn't all scream echoed out for nobody to hear.

Barbatos lay on a swirling field of abundant complexity as her face was filled with pain and confusion.

She felt like she had lost every single thing important to her- when she didn't even know she had it!

Tears freely streamed down her face as her heart...

"Why? Why?!" She didn't understand it.

She clutched her chest and felt the pain in her heart as if she had wronged her most precious person.

All her life, she had lived with privilege and power within her weavings of Existence.

When she had Pioneered her path long ago, she was trained for the complex missions through different weavings of Existence and the one critical thing to do during traversal.

To avoid Entanglements.

To not observe who or what was within those weavings of existence, as unforeseen results could occur during Entanglements!

She didn't expect that the first time she dropped the veil to see just who she had helped throw into that unforgivable frequency was- this would occur.

She felt a sense of desolation even though she didn't know who the person she was looking at was. It could only mean...

A version of herself here was connected to that man in an extremely unfathomable way!

She raised her tanned hands as she looked at herself. She wiped the tears and stood up, feeling the aching in her heart as she knew she couldn't just sit here.

She knew she had to leave and not entangle herself even deeper, but...she just couldn't!

So she looked around.

She saw the figures who were responsible for initiating this event and the ones she and Amalthos had aided to ensure its success.

She saw them, and she flickered away from existence!

In a region filled with golden strings.

Palace Master Antiope looked towards Pioneer Heracles with excitement and trepidation as she asked.

|How will this affect those weavings of Existence? Those frequencies were the future Infiniverse Dowager waded through? With her source sent to that place, would events rewrite themselves? Will entire Frequencies of the Wheel correct themselves? Will This present action that we took change past results concerning us?!|

She bombarded question after question as she became increasingly excited with each.

The change.

The result of their actions!

If the Infiniverse Dowager and the Origin Sacred Primal Megalonian had their Source Weavings locked away in that place, then many things should change...or have already changed in the past.

As she asked this, Palace Master Nisa beside her also had a complex expression as she thought of many things.

Pioneer Heracles on the other hand, had a difficult expression.

|This...is hard to say as nobody truly understands the weavings of different Frequencies of Existence. Due to the paradoxical factor and uncertainty of time in some Frequencies- we can't truly tell the results of our actions in the past. The best possibility I can think of...is for us to experience the results in our current future.|

...!

It was confusing.

Anything dealing with the Frequencies of the Wheel took extremely extraordinary minds to truly understand!

|So...nothing that we went through in the past has changed? We won't suddenly revert back to our original Weavings of Existence since we never would have had to leave due to the Infiniverse Dowager that is no longer there?|

Palace Master Nisa asked hesitantly.

And Pioneer Heracles massaged his temples as he replied.

|If everything was to be changed like that and we were returned to our previous weavings, it would also mean that we never arrived in these weavings to find the young Infiniverse Dowager and the Origin Sacred Primal Megalonian to begin with. If we weren't here, then they wouldn't be locked away right now. It...is truly difficult and paradoxical to understand as we can go in circles thinking about it, but we should see the change ourselves in the coming future, and not the past. We will know soon enough the initial results of locking away the Source Weavings of Existence of the Infiniverse Dowager and the Origin Sacred Primal Megalonian.|

Convoluted. Hard to grasp.

But Pioneer Heracles made it clear that changes would likely occur forward in time! From now on.

But what possible changes could bloom from what they just did?

Palace Master Antiope's mind was buzzing with possibilities as she was about to speak again when...

TING!

A booming and utterly terrifying sound that caused their weavings of existence to buzz emerged above them.

A sound akin to something that was tightly wound snapping.

|What...?|

Their gazes were stern as they looked up, and there, within the Expanse...

TING!

Their wills vibrated as they saw a golden string snap.

A golden string.

A String of Existence!

There were countless of them stretched out across the Expanse as those truly powerful knew how to utilize them to traverse tremendous distances of hundreds or thousands the equivalent length of Omniverses in a short period of time.

They held even more complex meanings that very few knew about, but at this moment...

TING! TING!

With horrific snapping sounds, the tightly wound Strings of Existence were breaking one after another.

The halves disappeared on opposite ends as if one didn't observe it closely, they wouldn't even be able to know that it happened as even now, you had to look extremely close to see that there were larger gaps between some strings of existence compared to others!

It was an eerie unfolding of reality as Pioneer Heracles was ashen.

|What is this?|

He had to ask. With this phenomenon occurring as soon as they successfully sent the Source Weavings of Existence of the Infiniverse Dowager and the Origin Sacred Primal Megalonian into that place where they could never return, they could not say that it wasn't connected!

But...why did such a phenomenon occur in response to that?

HUUM!

Amidst this shocking unfolding of reality, all of nearby surroundings for the span of the equivalent length of ten Omniverses turned silver black and froze.

All weavings froze as in the next second, the visage of Barbatos appeared before Pioneer Heracles and the two Existence Severing Constellations beside him!
