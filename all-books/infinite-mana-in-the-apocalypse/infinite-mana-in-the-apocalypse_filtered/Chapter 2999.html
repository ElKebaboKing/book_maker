Chapter 2997 Primordium! I

The essence was so palpating that Noah's figure rose from his resplendent throne- tendrils of existential authority following his rise and flowing into him as his arms and legs spread out.

HUUM!

Tremendous waves of existential authority converged around him as a circular Singularity formed with essence coursing in from all directions, everything directed towards a point that was Noah's absolute center!

This was the location of the Existential Primordium Dominium Core!

The very extreme of his Body converged towards it, along with the extreme of his Soul and Origin. It seemed like three sharp points converging towards the very center of himself, and the circular Singularity surrounding him showed a wondrous manifestation of three equal triangles coming together.

All of it accumulated together into a dazzling core.

It shone with a dazzling milky white luster.

It was the Existential Primordium Dominium Core!

{An Unfathomable Achievement!}

{Your Dominiums have formed a type of Omniversal Singularity at the very core!}

{The Existential Primordium Dominium Core is a pathway not yet entirely understood with your current weavings and the weavings of the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed, but it currently seems like a starting point for the grander Omniverse.}

...!

It was something Noah was stumbling onto that was so fantastic that his weavings did not fully understand it- nor did the weavings of even the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed.

But how did it truly come about? What factors contributed to its fruition? His choice of elevating all three Dominiums since the very beginning? Other people in the past eons may have done so too!

The Quintessential Fruits of Existence? His Foundation? Or something else entirely?

The answer did not rise yet as at this moment, weavings of what this buzzing Omniversal Singularity began flowing into Noah's mind.

{The Existential Primordium Dominium Core will absorb all incoming Existential resources and distribute them across your three Dominiums- elevating them to the same extent as if they were a single collective Dominium.}

{The cost of elevating the Existential Primordium Dominium Core is still much higher than other Dominiums of the Soul, Origin, and Body, with your stage of Primordium being tremendously different from the Neonate Stage of Existential Dominium Realm.}

The wonders of this unique Omniversal Singularity continued to flow within Noah's mind as both he and the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed had to unveil the weavings of its mystery.

While this occurred, a unique aura was released from Noah that even his sectioned wills and clones expressed!

An aura that in a proxy Haven stretched out somewhere in the Omniverse, a part of his will that was meeting with the intent of a certain Sanctus Princess also emanated.

And at that moment, her gaze had changed.

She was stern and earnest before as even with her high position as a Sanctus Princess, she didn't consider herself of insurmountable importance compared to others.

She didn't show a hint of arrogance towards Noah.

She spoke to him as an equal regardless of their positions!

But she still has her power. She still had her backing.

Even if she was trained not to be arrogant towards others and to practice a sense of humility, there was still a certain sense of royalty and wonder entrenched deep in her bones.

Yet at this moment, within the depths of her soul, she found this royalty being shaken by the aura that Noah radiated!

This...wasn't this an aura that the Elder Queens of the Sanctus Populi emanated? A sense of awe and power of those getting intimately close to the Omniverse itself?!

How was...?!

Her eyes found themselves vibrating as her words leaked out.

|You...| And yet before she could even finish, the unique aura Noah emanated was retracted and faded away a moment later as if it never existed.

He looked at her and released a devilish smile as he nodded.

|Sorry about that. We were talking about the shadow that can be provided by the Sanctus Populi...although the thought is a nice one, I do not think I will need it.}

HUUM!

Boundless tyranny rose with his very being as he spoke such words.

Anyone hearing them would have to bend down and understand that he was saying the wonders of the Imperium- the might that countless others feared and could not even escape...he was stating that he wasn't among those who were fearful.

He wasn't among those who would worry and tremble over the swords of the Imperium!

His words shook Sanctus Princess Emilliana from her trance as she refocused.

She wanted to ignore the aura she just felt, she wanted to alter the words she wanted to say, she wanted to even address it further!

But seeing his dismissive demeanor, she swallowed most of her words and proceeded carefully.

|I won't call you foolish, nor will I question how you can confidently say such things, but what say you regarding the aid and knowledge of the Sanctus Populi? You will have to undergo a process of verification, but the means and power of the Sanctus Populi are for the people. For the good of the people. Salus Populi Suprema Lex Esto!|

WAA!

The good of the people was the supreme law!

The Sanctus Princess Emilliana uttered this as Noah squinted his eyes and nodded.

|While I may not necessarily need protection, there is no harm in the power of numbers and expanding my horizons.|

His words were simple!

But what they would come to mean...what they would come to signify for this Omniverse...nobody had yet even fathomed!

And as such words were stated, far away from this proxy of a Haven, there was still the ongoing battle between the Red Legion of the Imperium and the main body of Emilliana and her Dragon Knight.

Facing her, the eyes of Knight Akalusia suddenly shifted and became somber as even though she had the upper hand, the situation of Knight Augustus in the distance and the intent of the enemy before her caused her heart to feel a great sense of unease!

What had just happened?
