Within the vast Primordial Empire.

In a Cosmos deep in the cluster of Cosmos within this Empire, an existence that was floating in a unique environment filled with the Primordial Essence and the Essence of Ruination opened her eyes.

Her verdant green hair washed over her shoulders as this existence looked extremely pristine, not a single layer of clothing on her as the glorious blessings were open, but there was nobody there to see!

All her proportions were so pristine that she was more than fully worthy of titles such as Miss Universe, if not Miss Cosmos!

Ruination and Primordial Essence wrapped around her tightly as her languid eyes gazed towards a certain direction, her hand raising as her fingers tapped the air and allowed the entrance of multiple Primal Crystals that contained bouts of information.

Much of it were things she expected and knew, the situation of the shifts of power within the Primordial Empire and which House was able to expand their influence across other Cosmos displayed before her. But her eyes raised as she came across a Primal Crystal from Daolord Azazel- one that included a certain scene of a new Cosmos being welcomed into the Primordial Empire!

"Oh?"

Her green eyes shimmered in the wondrous environment of the calm Primordial and Ruination Essence as her eyes locked onto the domineering figure of Noah on the scene playing out. "Is there actually something there, or is it just some hot air?"

Her voice was like bells ringing throughout as she spoke with a smile, her hands waving the Primal Crystal containing this information and scene away as she moved on to other issues that pertained to someone like her!

Someone who was a king above many kings, someone that was the head of the House of Springforge!

In the unique space, her pristine body could be seen as it was utterly spotless, not a single Runic Dao Line in sight being seen on her body as it seemed to ring with another source of power.

Someone who oversaw the myriad of Kings within her House and controlled the expansion of their power into the territories of other Cosmos through the Writs of Challenges...she was a truly powerful individual, and it was a wonder as to why she didnt have a single Runic Dao Line inscribed on her stark naked body!

But her glimmering green eyes continued to move from one Primal Crystal to another as she sifted through droves of information, her fingers tapping the air every now and then as strings of commands were released.
