Chapter 3323 Existential Displacement!

Within Omniverse X-999.

Inside a sea of golden brilliance, Imperium had a gaze of fervor as with his power, he had already attained three Artificially Constructed Hearthrae Imitations that he devoured- and he could feel that a Fabled Omniverse was not too far away!

It was a fantastic boon for him to enjoy, but in the back of his mind, he continued to wonder.

He entered with Osmont, and yet he never came across him again.

Were they in different places? And the power this being expressed, truly, what was his identity?

He, as the Omniversal Ankyra, actually didn't know nor could he comprehend much regarding an existence that lived in this Omniverse!

It was hard to grasp as in the midst of all this...

HUUM!

The Autarchical Omniversal Authorities told him that...the whole Omniverse was shifting!

Shifting.

Becoming displaced!

WAP!

Imperium felt a flash of white light.

And thereafter, everything settled.

Rapid. Efficient. Seamless!

Whatever Displacement had just occurred, these were the words that Imperium would use to describe it!

Displacement.

Did this mean that their Omniverse was no longer in a location that the Commonwealth could locate?

As for what could be responsible for this...

Was he...truly the Omniversal Ankyra?

Nothing.
