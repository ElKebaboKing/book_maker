Chapter 395 - A Scholar, Chaos and Fate

In the region immediately south of the Sanctum, frigid winds ravaged the snowy environment as a large Ice Behemoth was rolling in the snow peacefully.

Not too far from this Ice Behemoth, a hidden figure covered head to toe with a dark robe was crouching beneath the sparkling snowy vegetation as he wrote a few lines in his note book.

Both the Northern and Southern regions have a similar level of elemental particles in the atmosphere, but their levels are still much higher than any other realms.

The being was carefully doc.u.menting his findings of the Celestial Realm as his vibrant power at the peak of Soul Realm hid its aura from the nearby Behemoth.

This was someone that had managed to enter the Celestial Realm with the aid of the Pureblood Vampyres from the Underworld, a powerful covert operative from the Scholars Guild that boasted great power in the Dark Expanse!

The supposed Celestial Beasts are even more unique, their bodies holding an abnormal amount of universal laws.

The being continued placing more findings, pushing up his glasses as his eyes remained on the Ice Behemoth rolling in the snow with its enormous body.

The structures of the Behemoths I have managed to get my hands on shows that their physiology is similar to their species counterparts in other worlds and realms, but their Origins seems to have been influenced greatly by the force behind this Celestial Realm

Numerous possibilities passed through the mind of the Scholar, comparing his abundant experience and knowledge with the new land that the Celestials had managed to explore for the past thousands of years. With his tenure in the Scholars Guild that only the most genius Wizards and Magicians had a chance to take positions in, his experience was wide and vast!

The biggest possibilities so far is that this Celestial Realm is one of the highest tiered realms to be found in our Novus Galaxy, or the even more unbelievable possibility is that this Realm could be what we have all been searching for...the place where universal laws are abundant and the atmospheric essence is immense, this could just be the C-

ROAR!

The man from the Scholars Guild stopped taking his notes as his gaze went towards the mountainous Ice Behemoth, seeing an Exploration Team from a Prime World nearing the beast and beginning a hunt as the Scholar used his power to veil his body even more, sinking into the earth as he continued observing. What was on his mind was the same thing that many beings were recently trying to figure out, and it was the question of what exactly was this mystical realm that the Celestials had been enjoying for so long?!

Near the center of the Sanctum itself, Athena and Noah were meeting with a serious Grandmaster Vredral as they discussed their recent ventures, the Grandmaster approving of their fast progress as he adopted a careful expression and spoke.

"Youve gotten used to the pace of Normal Behemoths and learned how to best take them down, and we can move on to the next stage of your training as you tackle even more fierce Behemoths, and these ones will actually be of great aid for you to increase the speed at which you obtain Fate Lines!"

Grandmaster Vredral spoke with a powerful Voice as Noah and Athena looked forward respectfully, many thoughts passing through Noahs mind as he looked forward to anything that could enhance the speed he could raise the Supreme Law of Fate.

"Past the Fire Behemoths in the Northern region, the danger increases as you start getting into the dangerous regions where mainly those at the Soul Forging Realm explore, with the highest tiered teams like the Righteous Fire Exploration Team and Elite Teams coming into play as the enemies you might come across...are those proficient in Supreme Laws!"

A powerful light shot through the eyes of the Grandmaster as he said this, using his power as he waved his hands and created the illusory image of a dark Behemoth in front of their eyes.

"The ones that are the most dangerous to us as well as the most profitable...are the terrible Chaos Behemoths that are proficient in the opposing element that we cultivate. This means that our attacks backed with the law of fate are especially harmful to them, but it also means that their attacks filled with the Law of Chaos are especially dangerous to us as well!"

"But with danger comes opportunity, where the hunting of Chaos Behemoths has shown to rapidly cause an increase in the number of Fate Lines one can condense, rapidly gaining strength through this dangerous path."

The Grandmaster finished his words as the two disciples in front of him had shining eyes, Athena being the first one to speak as her body pulsed with a gold light, her expression that of excitement.

"We are willing to brave the dangers, Master!"

Noahs eyes also showed an enthusiastic expression as he nodded towards the Grandmaster, their next steps to increase their already rapid rise in power being set in stone as they prepare to delve further into the mysterious Celestial Realm, and this time it was to search for Elite Behemoths that were proficient in a Supreme Law!

Preparations went underway as the leader of the Righteous Fire Exploration Team became excited at the news that their team was granted clearance to delve past the Fire Behemoths and into the more dangerous regions further away from the Sanctum, the boisterous Boru bringing Noah and Athena to celebrate the entire night as a feast was carried out.

To them, another milestone was coming up as they would get the chance to brave dangers and increase their strength, but Noahs mind was filled with other possibilities as he had entirely different thoughts when he thought about the Chaos Behemoths and their proficiency in the Law of Chaos.

His mind continued to wonder what Items and materials would come out of them, what treasures or Inheritances he could find if he delved into this region, and exactly where his power would be as he amassed more Fate Lines while increasing the Stage of the Fire Origin Core slowly growing inside him!

As others looked at one or two possibilities in front of their eyes, Noah was seeing a bright future filled with endless opportunities that were all just waiting for him!
