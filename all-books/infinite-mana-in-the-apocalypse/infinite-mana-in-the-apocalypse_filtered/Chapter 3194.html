Chapter 3192 Fat Monkey! I

A testing location.

Noah never thought he would arrive at such a point in his existence where he could design something so disastrous that he needed an entirely different setting just to observe its effects.

Due to many unknown factors regarding the scope of its effects and the permanent damage left behind thereafter, the testing grounds had to be within an entirely different Omniverse.

And given the reality that even though the Infinite Omniverse was actively looking for other Omniverses right now, no results had come from this as the only thing left...were the much easier to access Eonic Omniverse Records.

Records reachable via unique weavings of Existence- one that Lumen made possible along with the alteration of the weavings of Existence through the Ascendant Existential Extremity Authority of the Protagonist!

When the Sentinels of Lumen remained active between the three-way battle before, more of them had died to the Worms of Existence that enough shards to formulate a gateway to a few Eonic Omniverse Records became possible.

And there was something else even more pristine in connection to Eonic Omniverse Records and the Atlas of Existence that was formed with one!

It related to the Sources of Existence obtained and assimilated into the First Mundi of Power within his Atlas.

|The assimilated weavings of all new Systems of Existence are recorded and kept for further analysis within the Atlas of Existence, with these weavings capable of being traced across Existence to other Sources similar to them, within Eonic Omniverse Records similar to them.|

|If the connection and similarity of Existence is strong enough, a gateway could potentially be formed toward another Eonic Omniverse Record with enough Authority of the Laborer of the Atlas of Existence.|

There were just some of the prompts that had risen over the last day after Noah's busy movements, which gave him two ways to go about entering Eonic Omniverse Records- but both weren't entirely usable as currently, there was an active search and surveillance for what caused the eradication and implosion of the Var-El Eonic Omniverse Record!

The entry into another Eonic Omniverse Record set up by the Commonwealth would almost certainly draw the gaze of eyes currently monitoring and making Noah predict unfavorable future weavings.

But...if he entered an Eonic Omniverse Record ever so briefly just for the purpose of deploying and understanding the effects of his Nuclear Omniversal Weapons...

|There is a 70% probability of observing the full effects of the deployment of Fat Monkey in their entirety. There is a 40% Probability of being discovered at entry, with this probability rising over time. With the veiling of the Authority of Laborer, it is estimated that only if the enemy exceeds the quantified values of Autarchy by a large margin would they be able to discover you right away. But if only a portion of your Will is sent, it could be destroyed with all traces erased thereafter.|

The sonorous voice of manifestation of the Infinite Omniverse echoed out beside Noah as the forming idea became more and more cemented.

A blinding light shone from Noah's body as part of himself was sectioned off, three dazzling shards of light also being brought out as they converged into a singularity beside them that began to balloon into a small blue portal!

In his prior visions under the banner of Invictus Maximus, he had seen 9 portals of light surrounding him that each led to different locations- and he still wanted to bring this vision to fruition after his Authorities continued to be elevated in power to the extent that he didn't have to worry about anyone watching or seeking him.

But for what he needed to do now, one was enough.

|The Three Fragments of the Wasser Existential Omniversal Map have joined together and formed the gateway into the Wasser Eonic Omniverse Record.|

|The stability of the gateway has been purposefully reduced to ensure that nothing outside the bounds of Autarchy is capable of passing through.|

"Let's put Fat Monkey in its intended home before deployment."

HUUM!

The weavings of Manadynamics shone.

The Authorities flowing in the surroundings converged around the Weapon of Mass Existential Omniversal Destruction as an outline of a fat monkey began to form, the material coming out being finalized with a golden metallic sheen that showed a statue of a monkey that had simply eaten too much over the years.

The statue showed a big round belly that the hands of this monkey held onto, its face that of contentment as it was smiling with its eyes squinted!

Noah nodded with contentment at this Weapon of Mass Existential Omniversal Destruction, his authority carefully wrapping around it as he took it within the folds of his existence...and disappeared towards the blue portal before him.

Wasser.

It was an ancient word closely linked to nothing else but...water.

As he felt the distortions of Existence itself, his small sectioned will was further veiled by the Authority of the Laborer that even Ashmole was only able to sense, but not know exactly where he was!

Within moments.

WOOSH!

He warped into an entirely new weaving of Existence.

Not a genuine Omniverse, but an Eonic Omniverse Record that was somehow made through the hands of the Commonwealth. It was here that Protectors placed Existences whose Omniverses were destroyed...among others!

The reason why wasn't entirely clear. The weavings of Existence became extremely clear within moments as his sectioned Will found itself in a body of water.

All around him was an endless sea stretching out in all directions, and the moment he arrived...

HUUM!

Countless chains of golden brilliance instantly blazed with gold flames all across these blue waters.

Alongside it, a voice that seemed to be a few light years away boomed out jovially!

|Haha, do you know how much of my Existence I've had to stretch out across different Eonic Omniverse Records waiting for you, O Follower of Anti-Life?!|

An authority that felt capable of suppressing the colorless brilliance of any normal Autarch as it coursed through the countless chains that were no doubt spread out across this Eonic Omniverse Record!
