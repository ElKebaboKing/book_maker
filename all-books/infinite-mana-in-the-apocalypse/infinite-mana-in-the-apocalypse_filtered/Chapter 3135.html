Chapter 3133 Integration! VI

While the Existential Omniversal Lumen Emperor observed the weavings of the battlefield and made many decisions based on it, he wasn't the only one.

There was an Entity at the forefront of the battlefield whose hair shone with a colorless glimmer.

Her eyes seemed like an infinitely spinning colorless singularity as her entire figure looked like a porcelain treasure that had to be protected!

Yet she was the one with the most power here, and even though her eyes faced ahead...her Will was spread throughout as everything fell under her senses.

There were a great deal of things to pay attention to on this battlefield. The new types of Existences ahead of them.

An extension of her Lineage- one of her many daughters, Sona, and the changes she had undergone.

Those granted the distinction of Quintessential Legions.

All of these were unique things to pay attention to, but none of them more so than the man responsible for why they were even in this place.

As if she had eyes in the back of her head, the Sanctus Autarch stared straight at just some of the bodies of the Existential Anomaly she had come across.

And the same question she had asked herself multiple times since she saw one of his Ascendant Existential Extremity Clones rose in her mind once more.

|Does he really not know?|

...!

Did he not know?

Truly?

As an Entity that had touched upon the minute specs of Autarchical Authority and what it meant, she was able to see from this being...an Autarchical Base had already formed.

Colorless in brilliance.

Unfathomable in the number of possibilities it could unfold.

And yet...his own strength and power was unique, but it did not utilize any Autarchical Authority!

His Existence did not probe or pull on his already formed Autarchical Base as it simply lay there...doing nothing!

Initially, the Sanctus Autarch had thought that maybe this being was purposefully not utilizing his Autarchical Authority to test himself.

So he was finding unique ways to alter the surrounding Omniverse as without utilizing Autarchical Authority, he transferred a large number of people into a Record of an Omniverse where tike would flow freely for 30 days while outside of it, only an instant would pass!

Such an action was feasible by expending a certain amount of Autarchical Authority.

But even if she did this, there was nothing she could gain within 30 days that would change the current state of affairs.

Nor could she jump onto an Eonic Omniverse Record that held Entities just as powerful as her.

This was something one had to gain access to through unique means as this being had seemingly done just that through his unearthing of Lumen!

And yet, his Autarchical Base never moved.

As more time passed, as she talked to him as if she were talking to a peer, she became more and more baffled as she came to the conclusion that truly...he did not even seem to know that his Autarchical Base was already there!

But this was so unbelievable that she continued to ask herself.

Did he truly not know?!

|...|

Yet he continued to achieve things that Autarchical Authority could accomplish, the latest one being the alteration of Existence and Lineage in a manner that formed a halo of defensive brilliance above all of his people!

Looking at it, the Sanctus Autarch could understand its weavings, but it wasn't something she could accomplish easily.

She could see tendrils of Lumen and a heavy concentration of his own Bloodline forming a barrier of Existence, and she could straightforwardly do the same thing with her Autarchical Authority as even though the enemies were of a different Source of Existence- she was able to defend the Legions of the Sanctus Populi by spreading out her authority.

But this being has achieved such a feat passively, and based it on his Lineage.

All without the utilization of any Autarchical Authority.

Frankly, it frustrated her as she found that at this moment, she couldn't sit still without this question being answered.

If he knew and he was only playing around, that was that.

If he didn't know...as unfathomable as this was, he had to know.

|Mmm...|

The Sanctus Autarch waved her hands to clash against ten Paracosm Equivalent auras, her Elder Queen Mothers defending against multiple other entities with this power as the Sanctus Autarch...blinked.

A blink of her eyes.

And in the next moment, another version of herself appeared in the distant crimson skies- right in front of two of the bodies of the Existential Anomaly in question.

Both of their brows rose in synchrony as if to show how a single Will controlled them, with one showing a hint of surprise while the other had a look as if this was to be expected!

The Sanctus Autarch stared at both of them, her eyes showing immeasurable calm as she asked.

|Do you truly not know?|

HUUM!

A simple question.

One that the Sanctus Autarch had been asking herself again and again, and she simply asked it out loud this time!

Yet Noah stared at her quizzically as he replied while tilting his head.

|That is as obscure as one can be...what don't I know?|

|...|

His reply only caused the eyes of the Sanctus Autarch to squint and look at him even closer, especially the figure of the Ascendant Existential Extremity Clone of Quintessence as she spoke out succinctly!

|You already have an Autarchical Base of Existence, yet it remains untouched and untapped. Do you truly not know?|

HUUM!

The question was explosive, causing Noah's mind to buzz as his Ascendant Existential Engine of Cultivation stirred!

He stared silently at the Autarch as he could see she was utterly serious, his gaze becoming stern as he replied with a question.

|What do you mean?|

...!

The always placid expression of the revered Sanctus Autarch showed incredulity as she pointed all around Noah.

|Its brilliance is gushing out all around you as it is not directed. If any other Autarch were to see it blatantly like this, they may be tempted to obtain such an Autarchical Base of Existence themselves as your fortune is unfathomable for not having come across the Imperium Autarch and his Advisors or the Eonic Uterra and his Abominations. Any other Autarch seeing what you have wouldn't even bother to wonder and ask questions like me as they would have already devoured you whole...|

...!

The words were succinct.

Noah's mind worked fast.

There were many things among her words he didn't know full well about, but the latter parts of her words were clear!

Any Autarch that had seen him would want something that even he didn't know he had.

And which Autarch had seen him recently?

...!

The skies above shattered.
