Chapter 3358 Infiniverse Megalos Seed! VI

The two Ordinance Amalgamation Entities to the left and right had their Ordinances suppressed and flung away, one being smashed on the chest as he heaved, while the other had their head fully twisted with a huge dent.

They were Ordinance Amalgamation entities.

The Three Eyed Goldflame Steed...was merely of the Ordinance of Existence.

It faced the terrifying complexity of the Infiniverse Megalos Body head on- literally, as when the head of the Infiniverse Megalos Body smashed into its own head, the appalling scene of the horse-like head being pulverized into a cloud of golden blood emerged.

Up to the long neck, everything was turned into a cloud of blood.

A terrifying power and complexity seemed to be carried along with this strike as the brightness of any Ordinance and life of the now headless Three Eyed Goldflame Steed dimmed greatly, its weavings entirely shutting down!

Such a scene...now caused waves of rising horror.

The Infiniverse Megalos Seed moved rapidly and savagely.

She seemed like a celestial child of the wilderness who tore everything with brute force, her body flinging her arms and legs as their force alone tore everything apart!

The Three Eyed Goldflame Steed dead, the Daughter of Existence atop it was utterly livid and filled with trepidation.

At this moment, the angered Daughter of Existence finally moved from her dead steed and appeared right before the Infiniverse Megalos Body, her hands rising up as behind her, the manifestation of a purple mountain the size of half an Omniverse already appeared.

It supported her small figure as she exploded with terrifying strength, her hands swinging like hammers and with tremendous speed to smash onto the head of the Infiniverse Megalos Body as she wanted to overpower just like it- give it a taste of its own medicine!

...!

BOOOM!

The strike and force was heavy, and yet...

The radiant head of the Infiniverse Megalos Body was only forced to turn sideways slightly.

Her hands reached out to grasp both arms that had swung down on her face- slight redness on her cheeks slowly fading away as she came to stare at the Daughter of Existence coldly- horror rising on the face of this woman.

When she stuck down, she didn't even feel like she had hit a living, complex being! What was this?

What was this?!

RIIP!

The Infiniverse Megalos Body savagely pulled down the arms it held, muscle and bone tearing apart as all of these physical actions carried heavy implications of the weavings of existence.

It was like the very complexity of existence of the Daughter of Existence was being denied and ripped apart, her arms actually becoming torn from her shoulders as golden blood overflowed!

The hands of the Infiniverse Megalos Body then swung towards the neck of the Daughter of Existence like scissors as...

SNIP!

The head was cleaved clean off.

The gorgeous head of the Daughter of Existence that was filled with appalling horror.

It wasn't just her head, but it felt like all of her existence was being cleaved off by the terrifying complexity the Infiniverse Megalos Body possessed!

The shadow of death came for the Daughter of Existence.

|No...!|

The two flung-back Ordinance Amalgamation entities cried out in shock and horror as their Ordinances whipped out again from afar, but it seemed like everything was all done!

At such a moment.

HUUM!

A unique power rose within the headless body of the Daughter of Existence- bursting with a terrifying radiance that shockingly emitted a Severing light that struck out towards the source of the deathly damage dealt to the Daughter of Existence.

BOOM!

The Infiniverse Megalos Body was flung back like a ragdoll, only coming to a stop a distance away as her hair whipped around her head maddeningly.

A huge gash split open her body top to bottom as if it was seeking to bisect her, but her body was shockingly far too sturdy!

In the gash that was rapidly healing, one could only see multicolored astral radiance within- not any blood or organs.

As she healed, she looked ahead to observe the scene of the bisected head of the Daughter of Existence going back towards her neck along with her ripped hands- her dying existence instantly returning to its previous state due to a shocking power!

A power that wasn't hers.

Likely a safeguard placed on her by her people!

In an instant, she was healed and back to her prime as she had a savage look on her face, her eyes and intent betraying her fear as her two remaining Ordinance Amalgamation Attendants appeared beside her.

|Escape!|

...!

Escape.

They could do nothing else as their enemy was too dominating!

Behind them, the Ordinance of Existence attendants scrambled away rapidly as all of them turned into streaks of light.

HUUM!

The Infiniverse Megalos Body had finished healing as her gaze was unfathomably cold.

She raised her hand and formed a fist.

Instantly, for the distance equivalent to three Omniverses, a sandy gold domain emerged rapidly.

Its sands were filled with the authority of the Ordinance of Dreams, and it wrapped around everything while making it clear that nobody would leave!

The hearts of these terrifyingly powerful Ordinance Amalgamation Entities began to fill with terror as at this moment, they began to burn their existence, going all out!

But the Infiniverse Megalos Body was relentless.

With the emerging sandy gold domain, she finally stayed still as her body...began to shimmer with the grandeur of Ordinances.

Plural.

Instantly, a violet purple light flashed in her eyes as dazzling flames of Entropy burned around her.

In the far distance, sandy gold waves surging around the two Ordinance Amalgamation Attendants instantly changed to become filled with heavy Entropy- its imposition causing their own Ordinances to tremble as the Complexity of their existence slowly began to erode!

And an instant later, in addition to the light of entropy, a blazing white banner of Genesis flashed above the Infiniverse Megalos Body.

Instantly, above the fleeing figure of the Daughter of Existence, a sharp white sword manifested and cleaved down as if it sought to return everything to its initial, basal state!
