Chapter 931 - Unknown Forces Descend Unto the Bloody Galaxies! II

As many forces were sent out, a single clone had an important task that required him to go towards a certain location in the Dark Universe.

It was within the vast Universal Core, within the area that used to be the past Novus Galaxy!​​

In an area of space where the element of Aether permeated throughout, Noahs clone flashed in as its arrival caused two eyes the size of stars to open and lock on to him!

RUMBLE!

The eyes seemed to hold a myriad of emotions, tendrils of aether and shockingly large tentacles that palpated with the essence of destruction. They locked on to Noah as a tyrannical voice reverberated out.

"What do you want?"

OOOM!

The voice was booming across space as Noah only smiled at it, waving his hands as he took out duplicated Galactic Filament Cores and multiple Dao Crystals from his [Expansive Space].

"Im bringing gifts once again, this time with a mission for you to finally move out."

The eyes of the enormous Terror shone with arcs of Aether, the creature that was larger than most planets staring at Noah with a nonchalant look.

It was the Terror of Aether, Cthulhu!

The Terrors were unique existences from Noahs original Novus Galaxy, the galaxy that had utilized a Ruination Core to birth Noahs Infinite Mana and Protagonist Traits among many things as it bent universal laws to achieve it!

Noah having the Cosmic Treasure of Ruination himself at this moment and utilizing its universal breaking capabilities understood the most that anything birthed from Ruination was bound to be special.

Thus, while he raised the strength of his subordinates and himself, he always made sure to share the loot with the Terrors of the past Novus Galaxy as he continued to elevate their strength even if they didnt step into the limelight.

Noah hadnt come across many creatures that were naturally the size of stars like the Terrors, knowing their bodies could release shocking battle power as different from people utilizing skills to change into such huge forms that would require an immense amount of mana to keep active, the Terrors themselves had no problem with this as it was merely their original bodies!

Granted Noah and the Terror of Aether had not begun on the right foot during the events that led to the fusion of the Novus and Nexus Galaxy, Noah was still the strongest protector of the Galaxy the Terrors were born in and the larger Universe!

His clone threw the Galactic Filament Cores and dao crystals over to the enormous figure of the Cthulhu as he continued to speak.

"Were currently breaking the influence of a Universal Realm Hegemony by stopping a cycle of slaughter in a certain region of the Animus Universe. Well need as many hands on deck to efficiently take over the cl.u.s.ters of galaxies to wipe out enemy Races while helping the Dragon Race..."

"Why should I go to another Universe when Im perfectly fine here? This doesnt concern me at all!"

RUMBLE!

The voice of the Terror was resonantly spreading across space, Noah only smiling lightly as he waited, a voice even more domineering than his or the Terrors ringing out a second later.

[Do not lazily slack off here anymore, go and contribute.]

...!

The voice of the Universal Core thundered into the mind of Cthulhu as he went quiet, letting out a harrumph before he looked towards Noah!

"...where to?"

WAA!

Spatial essence went on to cover the body of the Terror of Aether as in other areas of the vast Universal Cores, Noah went to the Terror of the Void and the Ancient Terror as well, giving these unique existences Galactic Filament Cores and Dao Crystals to bring them to the level of a Great Sage before they went through the Universal Gateway leading to the Pocket Universe that was currently anchored to the Animus Universe.

It didnt take too long as within an hour, the Three Terrors entered a Galaxy where many powerful Great Sages of both Dragons and other Bloodline Races roamed, their mission being to aid the Dragons while wiping out those that opposed them!

RUMBLE!

The larger than most planets bodies of the Terrors shone majestically as they appeared within the Galaxy. Cthulhu was the biggest among them as an innumerable number of tentacles and tendrils of Aether began to spread out, an apocalyptic scene of a Great Sage over 20,000 miles in size playing out!

For comparisons sake, the size of Noahs home world in the past merely had a radius of 4000 miles.

Such a creature set off as one would wonder- when it faced beings at the same level as it...how would they even fight?!

---

With many pieces set in place, Noahs mission officially commenced as within the Galaxy he had stepped in, he followed the party of Chaos Dragons to where the core Chaos Dragon Clan of this galaxy was located.

They arrived as a star system that held a great deal of planets and realms hidden within, the auras of the pure bloodline of Dragons emanating from Tiamat and Noah causing the Chaos Dragons hidden within their abodes to come out in stupor!

Noah looked at the many forces of Dragons that were coming out to greet them, giving a nod to Tiamat to release her heavy bloodline power that would be mistaken at the level of an Ancestor for now.

THRUM!

"The Dragon Race will not bow to the Bloodline Races within this galaxy any longer...with all of us moving to wipe out the core powerhouses of the Bloodline Races that reside around the gateway to the Slaughter Star Monolith!"

...!

The hearts of Dragons palpated as they felt their bloodline power riled up, Noah smiling at this scene as his eyes turned towards a far away direction within this Galaxy.

There, he could almost see the gateway that he had only seen from stolen memories, the gateway that led into the Universal Construct that was present in a few universes...the Slaughter Star Monolith!
