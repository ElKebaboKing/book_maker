A Seed.

Something they had known all their lives as they always had their own perceptions of such entities- noen of them being positive!

Seeds were ignorant Lifeforms living in Existential Domains, their lives always capped to a certain level they could never rise above of.

Their records were boring to look at, they lived, they died, and they were quickly replaced.

They contributed to the purity and accumulation of Vitalis and Vacuous as this was the main purpose of their lives.

To be Seeds that would eventually make way for more Fruits of Existence.

From the standpoint of a Basileus belonging to any Legacy, a Seed was akin to an ant they could step on if they so chose.

Akin to the clouds beneath their feet.

They could be stepped on, they could be sat on, and they forever remained beneath them.

So to actually have a Seed appear in the Archaeos Vitalis Pylos...

Truly, it was a violation of existence itself!

So Basileus Amelusa called it Blasphemous!

But before the two sides could even meet...

EEEE!

A fearsome cry came from the illusory Codex of Extremity above.

And the mass of white and black that had been blazing and converging this whole time flashed with brilliance as a shocking Dominium spread from it.

Filled with apocalyptic grandeur.

It was the last product of Mors Apokalypsis!

And it...was just a core.

Because within the Archaeos Vitalis Pylos and the Archaeos Vacuous Pylos, there were millions of Hibernating Dikastes of Extremity that were inactive this whole time.

Like throngs of puppets, hundreds of thousands of hibernating Vitalis Dikastes of Extremity burst from the clouds and white structures within this domain as they came to fuse and stack together with the core emanating a profound Dominium!

And from the obsidian gash above the white flames, hundreds of thousands of Vacuous Dikastes of Extremity also flew down akin to puppets without any strings attached, all of them joining together into a single swirling Dominium.

It all began to form into an utterly massive Dikastes of Extremity with a Dominium so heterogenous...so transfigured and unstable, and yet undeniably there.

Its Dominium Soul expressed a profound Dominium Rule as it was an amalgamation of creatures that shouldn't be possible, and yet when the distant figure of the Weaver of Existence gazed upon it, he began to peel apart its weavings!

[Oh well, an ugly thing remains so no matter the size. Come on!]
