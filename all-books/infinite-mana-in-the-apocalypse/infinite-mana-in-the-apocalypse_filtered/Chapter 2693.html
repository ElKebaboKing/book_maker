2691 Second Awakening! V

A stunning revelation of reality!

The Avernus Clan members seemed to have a shocking history and an unfathomable enemy as when a Tier 3 Avernus Extremity tried to swallow a Blood Source, a terrifying message appeared before everyone's gaze.

His expression was bold as he bellowed out.

WAA!

He took the Blood Source again as he flicked it towards a Tier 2 Hyperversal Extremity behind him, this being gazing at the source with a somber expression before he also swallowed it.

And a moment after...his expression became exceedingly sour as the Blood Source appeared outside of his body, flashing a similar message soon after!

[For any Avernus Clan Members seeking the boons of the Havenly Hyperversal Emperor Blood Source, an Oath of Extremity of them cutting off all ties to the Avernus Clan and barring their involvement in any of their Clan matters is required.]

[...]

Immense silence descended in the surroundings as the forces of other Clans and Inheritances here looked on with astonished gazes, instantly beginning to transmit scenes of what was happening to their forces as this was the first time they came across this!

As far as they knew, nobody else from any inheritance or Clan had ever received such a treatment in front of the Blood Sources.

Now, the biggest question was...why was the Avernus Clan different? Just who did they offend to have the current reality playing out?!

[What is this? How come it is like this?]

The Tier 3 Avernus Extremity was enraged as he turned his might and pressure onto the Tier 2 Hyperversal Extremity of the Extremity Cicada Inheritance.

The Employee of the Infernal Havenly Commerce who had sold him the Blood Sources!

Facing the pressure of an existence that had condensed Crimson Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Rune Scriptures, the soul of this Infernal Havenly Employee couldn't help but tremble as he spoke while gritting his teeth.

[I am merely an Employee conducting business, I know not of any other matters!]

His eyes shone with unwillingness as he noted in his heart that Avernus Clan members would never again be his customers!

And yet the enraged figure of the Tier 3 Avernus Extremity still remained. But apart from releasing his terrifying pressure, no other action could be made in this Independent Oceanus Marketplace, much less before the steps of the Oceanus Iridescent Auction House.

The wills of multiple Tier 3 Hyperversal Extremities had already begun to rouse because of this commotion!

Feeling despondent and stifled from the gazes of others, the Tier 3 Avernus Extremity waved his hands angrily as he and his group began to retreat.

And soon after, the surroundings buzzed as information rapidly began to circulate!

It of course circulated even faster with the force directly involved, all of that had occurred being sent to the higher echelons of the Avernus Clan as everything became known within seconds.

In the Nether Haven of Dissolution.

In a spatial pocket carved out by a Tier 4 Hyperversal Extremity, the astonishing scene of dozens of Extremities in seated positions with their faces constantly twisting in pain could be seen, with only a few having blank expressions filled with bloody murder!

Such Extremities included Ariana, Gayacus, and Extremity Blue.

Above these Extremities, a group of a few others floated behind a wondrous Tier 4 Hyperversal Extremity.

The Seer Amestris!

Beside her, an illusory mirror showed the grandiose and majestic visage of the Avernus Extremity Overlord who was gazing at the scene ahead with utmost calm.

[Someone is trying to destabilize our Avernus Clan from within. Have you come up with some possibilities?]

They received the news of the message of the Blood Sources all others were assimilating and receiving shocking boons, and yet this wasn't the case for the Avernus Clan members!

Naturally, this had to be by the hands of someone.

An enemy!

The Avernus Clan had made many enemies over the years.

They were at odds with the Reality Plucking Pavilion Inheritance, the Havenly Moon Clan, and other individual Extremities that they had conflicts with in the past.

More recently, they just added the Cult of Endless Shadows as another enemy!

The Seer Amestris was silent for the following seconds before she carefully opened her mouth, pure Authority continuing to leak from her and flow into the bodies of the Avernus Extremities fighting against an infection of their Existence.

[Four possibilities come to mind, but I've eliminated two of them due to the timing. If I add origination as well...then the possibility of the one who played a hand in this may be the same one that did this to a portion of your forces.]

WAA!

A daring possibility.

[I'm not entirely confident in this theory, but the Blood Sources that have been spreading recently are originating from the Supernal Havens of Extremity. They are a form of the Havenly Lineages similar to the Four Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Lineages. The last time I left Osmont, he had the backing of a Tier 4 Saurischian Titanosaurus as he revealed his Vitalis Awakening. He may have utilized his influence to ask for the powerful force to do something like this!

Even their current theory was a speculation!

[Verify this as soon as you can, pay a visit to this Infernal Havenly Commerce if you have to. As for this Osmont...to do this to Hyperversal Extremities, it is all the more reason for us to quickly erase this feud before it continues any further. But if he has joined the Havenly Animalia Extremity Lineage and is afforded their protection that scales up to Tier 4 Hyperversal Extremities...

how should he be handled?]
