Chapter 4923: Immensity II

Oh!

Exponential Expression!

The fucking Interstices flinched!

He could feel them now in a way he had not felt the originals, a constant awareness at the back of his mind like the presence of dangerous predators that had chosen, for the moment, not to attack.

But the transformation went deeper than mere power increase.

The edges of each Inscription began to shimmer with dual lights that should not have coexisted. Blue-gold Infinities wove alongside something else, something that held the nature of paradox itself.

Differentiation and undifferentiation merged within the infinite constructs, creating weapons that could harm through definition and through dissolution simultaneously!

Prompts bloomed with weight that exceeded anything Noah had witnessed from RUINATION before.

Noah read over these prompts with eyes that blazed with understanding. His hands had curled into fists without his conscious decision. Ten Inscriptions!

Each one capable of erasing standard Absolutes with single activations. Each one capable of genuinely wounding THE Undivided Ones and Formless Terrors that were currently flooding across Observable Existence. Each one potentially capable of harming even Primordial Architects themselves.

He set THE Transcendent Paradoxical Inscriptiones Dormientes aside carefully. The Inscriptions resisted separation from their creator, wanting to remain close, wanting to be used!

And oh, he would use them!

Their golden pages burned with dual lights that made the other Inscriptions on his desk seem like childrens toys by comparison, like drawings of weapons next to the actual blades.

Now for the defensive application.

Noah rolled his neck, hearing the faint crack of tension releasing, and took another golden page in hand. The Phonemes swirled around his form with increased intensity, responding to his intent before he had fully formed it, eager to participate in another act of glory.

He began to write once more, his movements carrying the same deliberate precision that had produced the offensive Inscription. The sensation of drawing from that infinite ocean returned, familiar now but no less demanding.

"I am not vulnerable to those whose foundations cannot match my own. The protection I receive is not static but scales with the difference between my weight and the weight of the one who seeks to harm me. The greater my foundation exceeds theirs, the more impenetrable my defense becomes."

The words settled into place as another Inscriptio Dormiens completed itself. Where the offensive Inscription had felt like a blade being forged, this one felt like armor being fitted, like walls being raised around something precious.

Noah looked at the completed Inscription and allowed himself a moment of satisfaction before beginning the augmentation process once more. The defensive application would follow the same pattern, but he found himself curious whether the sensations would differ.

"Multiplicative Amplification. Factor of ten."

HUUM!

The defensive Inscription blazed with light as the numerical operation took effect! The warmth that washed over him was different this time, not the hungry heat of a forge but the comfortable warmth of a hearth, of safety, of walls that nothing could breach.

As with the offensive Inscription, the single page divided and replicated, becoming ten identical defensive Inscriptions that burned with amplified protective authority!
