2571 That Sword!

In the hidden folds of space near the Apex Battlefield.

The visage of Ahpuch was spat out of purple flames with an ashen gaze as the figure of Extremity Ariana appeared soon after, her gaze ferocious as she spoke.

[Did you manage to track all the Extremities that made a move?]

The question was not for Ahpuch, but towards a communication channel that held the will of another Hyperversal Extremity who had not even appeared on this Battlefield!

And the answer Extremity Ariana received was a special one.

[For the most part. The Genesis Institute seems to be tainted along with an unknown portion of the Sima Clan, Infinite Extremity, Immeasurable Mystery, and two more that I need to verify the signatures to be 100% sure.]

...!

The reason for their actions.

The purpose of their probe!

Extremity Ariana's eyes flashed as she heard these words and she nodded while replying just as quickly.

[Then we can begin pulling out now. Remember, our goal isn't to fight today!]

Mind you, this was a Tier 3 Hyperversal Extremity.

His authority was raging around him and barely hanging on as the Gavel of Adjudication swung down, but if it was a Tier 2 Hyperversal Extremity that was placed on the scales...Svetlana would have been able to easily crush them and erase their Recorded History by now!

But...

[Continue to be weak...]

How grand would such an occasion be?

How fantastical of an opportunity would it be! They could have utilized such a reality to actually shake her seat of an Adjudicator as thereafter, someone they favored would be supported to step up and be elevated to this grand pedestal!

But Svetlana moved carefully as she chose one of their stronger combatants and pressed down Adjudication unto them, this authority penetrating directly into the Soul of Hyperversal Extremities as its damage was directly on the Concepts one expanded to Extremity!

This was the type of power awarded to Adjudicators- one that made them powerhouses nobody wished to go against in the Seven Havens.

Because of their granted Hyperversal Extremity Authority that didn't touch one's Vitality Values, but they simply attacked one's records and scorched even Concepts that had achieved Extremity to oblivion.

It was utterly dreadful.

Even Extremity Ariana felt a sense of apprehension when she gazed at it as at this moment, she reached out to the person moving behind the scenes.

[Emperor Shao, can you move to provide an opportunity for retreat? Destruction seems to be in a tight spo-]

OOOOH!

It was because at this moment, The Hyperversal Extremity of Destruction felt the Gavel of Adjudication swinging down targeted none of the Concepts he had achieved Extremity in...but a single one.

The concept he defined himself with.

The concept that became his moniker.

The concept that was his name!

BOOOM!

The Gavel of Adjudication actually had its unstoppable authority attack the very concept of Destruction within him!

[You all seem to think I will not act as you move rampantly...so let me show you.]

...!

Svetlana's voice was cold as even while her figure couldn't be seen, the gavel above seemed to press down with even more gravity as Destruction screamed out!

Destruction's body was bathed in crimson splendor as he roared out maddeningly, but he seemed to be anchored onto the scale he was on as he could not leave- being forced to watch as the Authority of Adjudication seeped into his soul and burn his most prized concept.

It was stably entrenched in Extremity as he had elevated this concept to stand far above any others at the same stage.

Yet now, it seemed to be forcefully burnt from his very soul while the defenses he set up continued to be bypassed!

This was the terror of a Zenith Tier 3 Hyperversal Extremity.

Once one made their mind and faced another existence that was also Tier 3...

their victory would be a certainty!

[OOH!]

Destruction's roars continued to resound as in the next moment...

All sound was canceled.

There, in the midst of all this darkness, a point of light appeared.

the vast and endless domain of Adjudication laid out...was cut.

Cut!

The cornerstone of an Adjudicator!
