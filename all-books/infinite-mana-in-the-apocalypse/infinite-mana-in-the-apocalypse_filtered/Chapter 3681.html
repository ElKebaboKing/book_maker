He would take it from here.

His silence felt ancient, as if it carried the weight of millions of years spent drifting through the Nullvein Gravewake Folds and the Wheel of Existence. He simply floated, silent, as the obsidian Bob reached out and grasped the black Absolute Complex True Source of Existence.

No blood spilled. There was none left to give.

Bob pushed the black wheel of existence into the gaping wound, inch by agonizing inch, until the entirety of the Dead Absolute Complex True Source of Existence was sealed inside him.

Into his being.

Even if he found what he was searching for- the one thing whose presence was rumored to be hidden deep in the Wheel of Existence- even if he discovered The Loom, this act made certain he could never reclaim the status of the living.

"I will ensure her weavings remain untouched. I will find THAT at all costs, and I will bring her back from the fate of a Dead Thing. I will see her smile again. No matter what."

For her.

He would give up everything for her.

Even this- the irreversible union with a Dead Absolute Complex True Source of Existence.

He had passed the threshold.

A point of no return!

There would be no going back.

But this...this was only the beginning.

The obsidian Bob turned to the white Bob, his complexity rising in terrifying bursts of light as he spoke, his voice cold and resolute.

"Rest."

WAA!

"You've worked hard all these years," he added, softer now. "You can rest."

...!

...!

Because the one he spoke of- that entity- was something else entirely. A being that had laid waste to entire Frequencies with nothing but a thought in his Wheel of Existence.

A creature that, when disappointed by what lay at the core of an Absolute Complex True Source of Existence, had simply devoured its Coressence and shattered it.

What if that being entered the scene?

Another irreversible point.

And

"You! Have you been active in the Wheel all these years? What have you done? What have you done?!"

BOOM!

Versality exploded into existence- a brilliance of infinite possibilities and cascading variations.

A single tap.

As if the very Folds themselves- the paradox, the collapse, the unknown- answered to him.

A wave of terrifying complexity surged forward, wrapping around the Omnifather's radiant power.

In a blink...

Possibilities vanished.

Variations shattered.

...!

"Existence is but a stage. All the living and the dead are merely players. Each has their entrance, and each- an exit. Each has a role before they fade. It's not a matter of if. Only when."

Heavy. Ancient.

His form flickered, and then he vanished, a streak of light swallowed by the immeasurable depth of the Nullvein Gravewake Folds.

Partly because of the terrifying transformation Bob had undergone.

Partly... because the Folds themselves favored the Dead.

WAP!

"..."

Until...

BZZT.

And a man stepped forth.

His face was peaceful- but troubled.

Instead, he had witnessed a force he could not comprehend.
