Chapter 424 - Just a Glimpse of True Genius!

Noahs Ancient Blood Clone opened his crimson eyes in the void of space, his figures devilishly handsome look being enhanced in this environment as he floated in darkness and had an aurora of golden colored lights and fate lines all around him.

Breaking the 100,000 milestone would allow one to begin using Fate Lines and Chaos Particles in a new way, granting them the ability to release pure Fate and Chaos Energy in their attacks that were even deadlier to the opposing element!

The War Princess Athena would soon break past this point, but Noah would not be far behind as he would soon even surpass her at a rate that would shock them if they knew.

But whatever he showed them would simply be just a glimpse of what he could truly do!

His expression was determined as he glanced around the void of space around him, the golden light having died down majorly as things went back to normal. His figure turned into particles of light as he disappeared from the location and returned to the Spiritual World.
