Each space bore a massive Sovereign Spire of Osmont at its center, architectural anchors around which the factions organized themselves.

The three carved domains represented The Iron Concordat, The Crimson Hunger, and The Verdant Cycle!

Those who had undergone Civilization Specialization could be observed moving together within their respective territories, working systematically to understand their newfound capabilities.
