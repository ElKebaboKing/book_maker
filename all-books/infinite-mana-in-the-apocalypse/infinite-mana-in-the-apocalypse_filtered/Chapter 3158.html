Chapter 3156 Systems of Existence! V

The Authority of the Laborer of the Atlas of Existence.

It was unique in nature as Noah saw Autarchical Authority melding with it at the depths of his existence- the Duru of Autarchy flung wide open as a colorless Authority constantly surged from it. The designation remained colorless, but the authority felt thicker and more robust.

It felt heavier and even more immovable in nature- as if normal Autarchical Authority could not compare!

As it gathered within his Existence, The trillions of Focus Points of Existence on the Sunwinged Leprachanus blared as they seemed to sense the perilous situation, the expression of this Entity maddening as he roared out while gathering all remaining Authority of Opulentia.

|Who do you think you are?!|

BOOM!

|Do you know who I am? What I've done?! Do you think you could possibly silence me? Take my wealth? Rubbish!|

WU! WU!

Like a blinking star at the end of its path, his existence blared with the brightest light it had flourished with than any other time in his life as he was directing it all towards Noah.

Noah who gazed at this scene while grasping a few tendrils of the new Authority of the Laborer, his existence directing it towards Causality and Loot while looking at the Sunwinged Leprachanus!

"For your final moments, you can go out with an overabundance of wealth."

...!

His voice retained its calmness as carrying a hint of solemnity, he spoke out.

"Laborer's Fatality- Opulentia."

HUUM!

A flash of colorless and gold brilliance unfurled.

The gaze of the Sunwinged Leprachanus changed as he instantly raised his hands up, finding an unfathomably large and heavy stone of dark gold wealth appearing above his figure as if it were trying to crush him.

Below his feet, a river of gold gems and armaments bloomed as it seemed like a flat disk anchoring him where he stood, his figure not moving down even an inch while the dark gold stone made of unknown waves of authority pressed down to crush him!

|OOH!|

He bellowed out with maddened fervor as the pierced rings and gems on his hands ripped and shattered at the sheer weight, his hands instantly caving in as the stone became supported on his back.

A stone of infinite wealth crashed down, its core supported by an authority distinct from Autarchy and yet carrying a sense of undeniable oppression!

CRACK!

The weight of the massive stone of wealth cracked and splintered not just his body- but his existence itself.

Within it, strands of the will of the Sunwinged Leprachanus flowed in as he sensed the wealth contained within.

'This...'

A different type of wealth.

He saw a glimpse of the wealth being used to crash his existence as within it, he saw the outline of an utterly glorious Seed.

Within it, boundless wealth was contained!

'Ah...'

Was that wealth? Was that-

CRACK!

His back supporting the weight of the massive stone of wealth was broken as the rest of his body followed, his eyes popping out of their sockets as this time, he used the last shreds of his authority to direct his loose pupils to gaze at his compatriots.

'You dumb fuckers just stood there until the very end? Stupid, dumb, motherfu-'

BOOM!

Like an unstoppable meteor, the massive stone pressed down entirely as the visage of the Sunwinged Leprachanus became crushed under it- the gold sea below his feet becoming painted with crimson gold blood!

A gush of the Authority of Opulentia spread out amidst such a devastating scene.

Loved by wealth, and living his whole life with the standing and accumulation of wealth, the Sunwinged Leprachanus was ultimately given a Fatality of Wealth!

Crushed under the sheer weight of Wealth that he couldn't even comprehend!

"My goodness..."

Noah's voice echoed out amidst such a terrifying moment of reality, his hand tapping out as the remnant weavings of the Sunwinged Leprachanus gathered together to form a single point of singularity that came towards him.

He grasped and looked at it closely while his voice continued to ring out.

"Now, that was the most powerful one among you, and he has met such a fate. I wonder...what will the two of you do now? Are your Sources of Existence sufficiently interesting enough for what is to come?"

|...!|

The Faesinged Queen and the Satyrus Nymph had immeasurably somber expressions as their Authorities burned like a tide.

While waiting for their answer, Noah gazed at the singularity within his hands.

|Seed of Existence|:: A Seed containing the very essence of Existence that has been refined to the utmost. With it, an Existence with a sufficiently strong soul could refine it to take them towards the distinction of Autarchy.

...!

A ridiculously simple definition.

But it was an insane one as it stated one could go to the stage of Autarchy by assimilating this Seed of Existence!

What was that!

What was Autarchy? Could one reach it so simply as they wished? Now, under Noah's banner, one could!

It was a ridiculous reality as Noah put away the Seed of Existence while turning to look at the Faesinged Queen and the Satyrus Nymph who were now inches from each other, standing together protectively as their Authorities burned in a way it had never burned before.

At such a time, the Faesinged Queen opened her pink lips while emanating boundless charm.

|We were wrong.|

WAA!

She looked like a damsel admitting her mistakes.

Like a delicate flower surrounded by colorless and crimson radiance who needed to be carefully cradled and forgiven!

|We should not have coveted something we did not understand, and for doing so- I will personally pay the price with my body. You can do with it anything you will.|

...!

In an utterly mesmerizing tone, the Faesinged Queen uttered this while showing a Maiden filled with defeat who only wanted her conqueror to take her!

Noah nodded towards this being as her eyes lit up.

"Using your Authority of Illudere even now...ah, you are right- I will do anything I wish with your body."

His words caused the eyes of the Faesinged Queen to brighten up as she saw an ounce of possibility- a possibility that was shattered within the next moment as Noah continued!

"Among the things I wish to do, granting you death is one of them."

WAA!

A flicker of brilliance unfurled.

And a newly emerging authority began to be tested even more as Noah was clearing up the Artificial Omniversal Ankyras of the Var-El Eonic Omniverse Record by himself!
