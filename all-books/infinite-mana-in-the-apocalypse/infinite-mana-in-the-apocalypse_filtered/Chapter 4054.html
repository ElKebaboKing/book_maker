Chapter 4052: Records Without Limitations! I

In the Early Veiled Shore.

Noah...wouldnt describe himself as a person who could be smitten. He had many beautiful existences around him...beings he cared for deeply, loved in ways that transcended simple emotion.

But smitten suggested something uncontrolled, overwhelming. As he looked at Sigrid standing near the entrance to the archaic mine, he wouldnt say he was smitten.

Marveling, however...that word fit perfectly.

He was marveling at this creature purely because of the fact that her progression...was ridiculous!

She radiated the terrifying aura of an Honored Living Existence, but even that classification seemed inadequate for what she was becoming.

The white light of Order that emanated from her organized everything it touched, making the chaotic patterns in the mines entrance stones arrange themselves into perfect harmony.

They stood together making final preparations for their first delve into the Primordial Excavation Depths, but Noah found his perception continually drawn to her transformation.

When he focused his enhanced sight on her details, what he saw was utterly terrifying!

|Sigrid Ivanov THE Living Order|

Primary Classification: THE Living Order(63% Awakened)

Secondary Classification: Honored Living Existence (Transcending), Living Origin

Distinctions:

Vessel of Primordial Order

The Restored Foundation

Bearer of Ordered Chaos

Chosen of The Early Creature, Osmont

Current Statistics:

Complexity Quotient: 157,892,473,621

Purity Quotient: 156,903,892,517

Current Increase Rate: +500,000,000 CQ/PQ per second (Stabilizing)

Unique Traits:

Order Incarnate: Her presence naturally organizes chaotic systems

Existential Anchor: Prevents reality degradation in a Fold-meter radius

THE Living Order Authority: Commands absolute obedience from Order-aligned entities

Noah shook his head at these numbers and distinctions.

His own complexity was just approaching five billion...impressive by any standard, revolutionary for someone who had started from nothing.

But compared to Sigrids explosive growth, he might as well have been standing still.

Near them, Ruination stood. The Infiniverse floated nearby, its form shifting between states as it continued processing its own evolution.

Moiraines clone had arrived from the Abode where her primary body was currently... experimenting with other cultivation methodologies alongside another of Noahs bodies. The implications and results of those experiments and how they would be replicated, and with who... would have made Duke Gwendolyn faint!

The five of them...Noah, Sigrid, Ruination, the Infiniverse, and Moiraine...had gathered to explore what lay within the Primordial Excavation Depths. But first, preparations needed to be completed.

Alright, Noah said, his voice carrying the authority of decision. Let us finish preparations. Sit.

They arranged themselves on the golden sands near the mine entrance, the glorious light of the Shore casting no shadows despite the depth of the opening before them.

Noah turned to Ruination first, raising his finger with deliberate purpose.

A vibrant white-gold light shot from his fingertip, wrapping around Ruination like chains made of possibility itself.

This wasnt an attack or restriction...this was a Guidance Seal, the mark of THE Living Existential Guider claiming another existence to nurture toward transcendence!

The moment the seal took hold, Ruinations body erupted with stellar white-gold light that made reality buzz.

|Ruination has been granted a Guidance Seal|

|Analyzing potential changes...|

|The nature of Endings within Ruination has achieved existential resonance with the Seed of Inevitability|

The distant Seed of Inevitability, planted in its plot, suddenly pulsed with recognition.

Obsidian tentacles erupted from the soil, existing in the space between real and unreal!

They stretched across the Shore with impossible speed, coming to hover above Ruination like judgment waiting to fall.

Ruination opened her eyes, gazing at the tentacles with an expression of cold evaluation. Without hesitation, without fear...she spoke.

I give Everything of my Beginnings.

BOOM!

The tentacled jaws clamped down with violence that transcended physical action.

They passed through her body while taking something fundamental...white brilliance was torn from her being, leaving behind only ruinous blackness!

The extraction was visible to all, streams of light that represented every new start she might have had, every fresh beginning, every reset...all of it devoured by the Inevitabilitys hunger.

Ruinations expression showed terrifying serenity as the process completed.

Her being now emanated only endings...no possibility of starting over, no chance of new beginnings, only conclusions and terminations!

Yet her eyes looked not at what was happening to her, but into the distance where her Master sat.

He was right there, close enough to touch, yet somehow infinitely far ahead, pulling away at speeds that demanded everything to match.

He was too glorious.

To keep up, sacrifices were necessary!

I give my Everything of Order.

HUUM!

Noah frowned, beginning to rise with concern, but Ruinations gaze held him in place. This was her choice, her evolution. The tentacled jaws descended again with obvious glee, biting through reality to claim another fundamental aspect.

The phenomenon that followed was heavier than the first. Ruination trembled with transformation so profound her existence struggled to contain it!

|Ruination has given Everything of Order and Beginnings|

|All that remains: Endless chaos, endless endings, endless ruin|

|Ruination has tapped into Quintessential potential through the authority of THE Living Existential Guider|

|Catalyst: Seed of Inevitability + Deviating Early Veiled Shore|

|A grand nature of endings has been unveiled from her existence!|

|Ruination has begun walking the path of an Early Living Ruin|

HUUM!

The surrounding existence shook tumultuously!

Heavily!

|This weaving has potential to create entirely new type of Living Existence|

|Early Living Ruin Capabilities:|

Gains complexity from every ending witnessed or caused

Purity increases through perfect destruction

Can end concepts, not just physical forms when powerful enough

Immune to resurrection or reversal effects

Each ruin created strengthens her foundation

Ruinations body burned with a sense of profound glory!

Waves of crimson and gold bathed everything as her figure floated gloriously, but it still seemed like she was not done.

Because on her forehead, a glorious glyph began to form.

|Due to the nature of her transformation, Ruination is ending all of her complexity and previous Glyphs to the task of forming a Kleos Glyph of Ruin|

|Effect: Touch brings inevitable ending to all things|

|Growth: Exponential based on significance of endings|

|The Early Living Ruin, Ruination, is completing a glorious and Quintessential transformation, and is looking forward to embarking on path of endless Ruin!|

The waves of crimson and gold slowly subsided, but they were no less grand!
