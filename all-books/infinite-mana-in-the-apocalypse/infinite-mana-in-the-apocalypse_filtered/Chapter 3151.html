Chapter 3149 The Atlas of Existence! IV

Sunwinged Leprachanus.

This was a title, as well as a type of Existence that was afforded the highest peak of power for the Source of Existence of Leprachanus.

Their very existence cultivated wealth; with the more wealth that one held and accumulated, the stronger one became!

Their Authority was known as the Authority of Opulentia!

The Faesinged Queen hated many things about the Var-El Eonic Omniverse Record, but she hated this Entity the most purely because of his power.

Being stronger than both her and the Satyrus Nymph, this Entity wantonly did what he wished since they were locked in here as apart from his wealth, everything else about him was horrid!

She looked at him seated on his bejeweled throne, his massive figure looking short and stout as behind his back...the dark gold glow of countless Suns formed his wings.

Sunwinged.

He genuinely had trillions of suns making up the structure of his wings as within these suns, The Faesinged Queen knew there were countless mountains of treasures hidden within those miniature suns as she kept her eyes straight into the two Luminous suns that were his eyes as she replied.

|An Intruder in the depths of this Eonic Omniverse Record. He doesn't seem to be one of them as he seems to be an Explorer or something that inadvertently arrived here.|

...!

|Oh?|

Her words roused the attention of the Sunwinged Leprachanus as he moved his face out of the dark gold veil. The features of his face could be seen as golden rings hung from his ears and nose, his bright lips laced with light as they smiled ear to ear as he replied.

|Why is this of importance? They're likely not bound to this Eonic Omniverse Record like we are as they can leave at any time. Why waste any breath on this? What makes you come to see me about them?|

His eyes swirled with a treasured light of brilliance as he asked, and the Faesinged Queen silently simmered in her thoughts before she replied.

|One of those who entered has an unblemished Base of Existence. Untouched. Unused. They know it as an Autarchical Base as their System of Existence leans more on the Conceptual Manipulation of Existence.|

WAA!

The moment these words were uttered, the visage of the Sunwinged Leprachanus flashed and appeared inches away from the Faesinged Queen.

...!

An action that caused the Faesinged Queen to tremble slightly, but she controlled herself and staunchly looked ahead as the Entity opposite her smiled ear to ear, his hot breath blowing over her face.

|Autarchical...a Source of Existence one arrives after advancing Conceptual extremes to the utmost. If there really is an Unblemished Autarchical Base, an Unblemished Base of Existence...you could have used that against your Base of Existence to escape this Var-El Eonic Omniverse Record. If that is the case...why are you here? Don't tell me a doll like you was too weak for him...|

His hand rose as his rough fingers caressed her smooth cheeks.

Veiling her disgust, the Faesinged Queen continued calmly.

|He has enough means as someone who was able to randomly appear within this Omniverse Record. I tested him, but he proved to be difficult even though he has yet to touch his Base of Existence. He also has an Autarch beside him. A fair dainty female...someone you could get your hands on.|

...!

She waved her hands as she spoke, showing the image of Noah and the Sanctus Autarch as seeing them, the massive Sunwinged Leprachanus only became brighter as a raucous laughter boomed from him!

|Haha, you know me well, but this still doesn't...|

|His Autarchial Base is so unique that it would be big enough for you and I to share, and even have left over for the Satyrus Nymph.|

|...|

Immense silence descended.

The bejeweled rings all over the face of the Sunwinged Leprachanus bobbed and weaved as they made a unique sound, his rough face shining with a splendor of gold as seriousness set on it. freewebnovel.com

He seemed to have thought a great deal about the words of the Faesinged Queen before he replied.

|You believe even the Nymph is necessary to take down these two?|

His eyes lingered on Noah and the Sanctus Autarch.

And the answer from the Faesinged Queen was one of heavy affirmation.

|Yes. When you see him for yourself, you will understand. But his Base of Existence...even if there were five of us in here, it would still be enough for all of us to share and use to get out of here.|

...!

The reality was set.

The visage of the Sunwinged Leprachanus nodded.

|Well, even if we were to escape and get out there again, we would likely have to hide from the eyes of those wankers again. Those so called Protectors of Existence...Oh well. A life on the run is a fun one. All right, I'll need a few days to prepare; you go ahead and tell the Nymph.|

|A few days?|

The brows of the Faesinged Queen rose as the massive figure of the Sunwinged Leprachanus turned around and smiled.

|Yes, I shall increase my wealth even more and have my Opulentia Authority at its peak before breaking out of here. To do that, it seems my Lineage will have to be reduced once again...but this time for good. Ah!|

He shook his head as if he felt great sorrow, and yet his face had a deep smile of amusement and greed!

|Oh don't be such a hypocrite. Only the three of us could possibly leave as if we succeed, all others have to be left here. And after we are gone, so will our roles be gone as artificial Ankyras as this Eonic Omniverse Record will perish soon after. Why does it matter if I kill them all now for greater wealth or let it all rot in here over time? You have to be smart Little Fae, otherwise...|

HUUM!

His body buzzed as it shone with an incandescent dark gold authority.

In such a heavy atmosphere, he smiled again while waving his hands!

|Go on now, I'm over here getting giddy at going against Autarchical Authority. My Opulentia has not had something like that in so long...I wonder how this Source of Existence compares?|

Greed buzzed around him as with a glimmer of dark gold brilliance, he disappeared!

|...|

A few days.
