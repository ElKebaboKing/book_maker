Chapter 476 Ahh! My Progenitor!

Elena was floating in the skies of the Spiritual World as her face was that of immense wonder and infatuation.

A few days ago, her Progenitor- nay, her Master, had brought her into this wondrous land once more after saving them from the clutches of the Celestial Founder.

Her Master had actually duplicated his body as he ingeniously still kept his identity as a Celestial while also working against them simultaneously to save her and many others from certain death!

It was something beautiful and resplendent, something that only her Progenitor would be able to do!

She had great worries about her little brother in the Underworld the moment the Celestial Founder called for its destruction, and her Master had somehow been able to do something as ridiculous as teleport the entire Prime World away while dominating its core at the same time.

It was at this moment that Elena realized that she really did not know her Progenitor as much as she thought she did!

What type of being could subjugate entities that were the centers of entire worlds? Who could then even teleport such an enormous entity to an entirely different quadrant in the Light Expanse?

What energy was required?! What grandeur! What magnificence!

Ahh! My Progenitor!

She found her hands clutching her chest as her thoughts went in this direction while she floated in the skies of the Spiritual World.

She had already stabilized the newly added Underworld as she gained the knowledge that her Progenitor already had numerous worlds like this connected, each of them with their own set of beings that were all being taken care of by him.

She flew along the paradisical worlds as her gaze traveled to the Central Continent of the Spiritual World and flew towards a certain location there.

Not to just mention the four floating Continents that were beautifully seen all around her, she actually found the Central Continent to be more wondrous as within it, she found the unbelievable land known as the Time Space, as well as the location where her Progenitor created fearsome armors and weapons that nobody else had ever seen.

But even more than this was the collection of portals that one could find at a particular area of the Spiritual World.

Her eyes gazed upon different colored portals that were ordered in a circular manner on a large snowy clearing.

There were names floating above these portals that would make one tremble when they realized exactly what their meaning was. She could see the titles above the portals as such- [Demon World Portal], [Ancient Blood World Portal], [Beast World Portal], [Underworld Portal]...

Her shining eyes saw many of such portals spectacularly laid out, figures of beings of different colors and races occasionally seeing coming in and out of these portals as they actually transferred from these many worlds and into the Spiritual World.

Almost all of the beings coming in and out of these portals had one singular thing on their faces- wonder and amazement as they came and went with their own assigned jobs.

Many of them did not even have jobs perse, as what they were doing was simply coming into the Spiritual World to be granted paradisiacal Medicinal Herbs and Fruits that were spread out all across the Spiritual World.

This was another thing that caused Elenas heart to beat even faster. In the Spiritual World, she found that across all the continents, the majority of their lands were covered by farming fields that had shimmering fruits and herbs filled with essence growing within!

Aside from the Spiritual World beasts that enjoyed these medicinal herbs and fruits the most, the beings entering and leaving the Spiritual World were coming and going mostly for these resources as they took them back to their own worlds.

She had seen her Masters figure travel through the fantastical floating landmasses of the Spiritual World as he went to each of these fields and waved his hands above them. Every time he did this, a green light would descend down and wrap around the medicinal herbs or trees, causing them to have rapid growth and achieve maturity in a matter of minutes compared to the days or months that were required.

He had such a wide array of abilities that Elena wondered, could she be able to find something that her Master could not do?

Even something as simple as cooking- the one time that she tasted it she nearly blacked out from pleasure! She even found herself wondering why he would even excel in something like cooking, but it just added to the charm that he had! But the wonder of him being a farmer on the side overtook her mind momentarily as her thoughts wandered even more.

Her Progenitor was so magnificent that he actually took the time to be a farmer and plant all of these wondrous things for them, things that were actually filled with so much essence that one could consider them prized treasure gardens of powerful sects.

Yet all of them could freely be seen spread out in the Spiritual World as the followers of her Master enjoyed them to the fullest while also using them to bolster the strength of the many worlds her Master was connected to.

The more she learned about her Master, the more reverence and infatuation Elena had as she found her fangs unintentionally coming out while she licked her lips.

She closed her eyes in the skies above the portals where many beings went in and out, ignoring their gazes as she recalled the sensation of her Progenitor sucking blood from her weeks ago. That amazing feeling she was able to feel then...she had not gotten the privilege to feel it again ever since!

She reminisced as she wondered.

My Progenitor, just when will you feed on me again? My blood is quite good you know? Ahhh...

The matter of feeding war paramount, especially to the Vampyre Progenitor. She felt like he had been suppressing his urges for far too long, and the day that he chose to get lost in them would be the day to remember.

Her eyes were closed as her body wiggled curvecously in the air, her mind filled with her Progenitor as her thoughts soon went towards those closest to him.

Her face instantly turned hard as she thought about the women around her Master. The closest ones she could see was the one called Barbatos- all she saw in her eyes was a small woman with somewhat decent looks, but she would never question the tastes of her Master. This Barbatos was simply too lazy in her eyes, only training and accompanying her Master when he had the time, but the way she referred to him was not the way one should address someone such as him!

The other was a fair little queen known as Adelaide who always came to her Master with a shy expression that just asked to be spoiled. Her royal countenance was always reserved around others, but one could see a much rosier appearance whenever she was around the Progenitor. This one seemed to at least fit Elenas requirements, but there were still many things to be worked on.

All of these women surrounding her Master seemed gorgeous and perfect to others, but to her- they werent enough!

Does nobody not know the majesty of her Progenitor? Do they really think his desires would be satisfied by the presence of merely two women?!

Her Progenitor was someone grand that was already taking over numerous worlds, with a future that many of them could barely even imagine!

Soon enough, even these women would start to find themselves inadequate in the presence of her Master, and that was when she would go in to allow her Master to do whatever he wished with her. If it was high quality blood- she had it! Anything else- she had it!

She would accompany him to her death as she carefully chose the beings deserving to be around him and experience his grandeur with her.

This was the future she awaited for with her Master, her cloudy eyes clearing up as she thought of this, her figure disappearing from the area of the portals as she went on to join the others that continued to be spoiled with treasures from her Master.

She did not even have the patience to reply to the figure leading the Vampyres in the Dark Expanse, the Vampyre Ruler Vladivostok, as she focused on her own strength. His message was releasing commands that were filled with bloodline power to ask her to reply or return, but this Vladivostok did not know that someone way above him had already given separate commands to her. If a subject received the command of the King and then received another set of commands from another subject of the King, who would she listen to?

Hmph!

The powerful figure overseeing the Clans of Vampyres in the Dark Expanse at the Star Forging Realm was just like another subordinate of her Master. So what if he was a whole rank above the World Realm? What if he had lived for thousands of years?

As her Master was now playing the Dark Expanse, she knew that the entire faction of Vampyres that made up the Dark Expanse would soon fall into his hands. After all, who could deny their Progenitor? Who would be able to look at the being with the purest bloodline of Vampyres alive and say no to him?

She was one of the few permitted to feel this reverence, so she knew she had to work hard. She also had to increase her strength if she wanted to be around him!

In the skies above the portals that she just left, the beings moving below finally breathed out again as they went on with their tasks once more, whispering words only a few of them could hear.

"Who was that demoness wriggling her body in the skies?!"

"You...shut up and move faster. You dont want to offend any of the experts under the Spiritual World Master, do you? It doesnt matter how weird they are!"

"Stop gawking and hurry you fools!"

Hushed words passed between them as their bodies brimming with strength from Noahs all around buff caused by the Golden Guider coursed through them, their gazes bright as they moved across the Spiritual World while also seeing a bright future ahead of them!

Many of the beings from the numerous connected worlds that were granted permission to enter the Spiritual World only knew of Noah from those that stood at the upper echelons of their worlds, with very few of them actually coming in contact with him.

But they all knew of his grandeur and power as they worked diligently, grateful to be placed under such a persons wings!

As these numerous beings continued to have their lives changes affected, their fates steered towards directions entirely different than what was ordained, the one responsible would continue to benefit immensely as time passed.

The Founder was able to increase his power and Fate Lines by indirectly influencing and changing the lives of many worlds in the Light Expanse, and his power had risen to the peak of many in the Novus Galaxy in the past thousand years. What of someone like Noah who was directly and actively steering the fates of numerous planets and their inhabitants then? How long would it take him to scramble to the top as he continued to expand his scope of influence?!
