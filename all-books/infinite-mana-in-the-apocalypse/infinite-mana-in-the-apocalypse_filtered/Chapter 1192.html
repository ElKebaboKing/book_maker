Chapter 1190 - Dumb Bitches Die First!

A grand golden throne the size of a Galaxy that was meant for only a single being, yet Noahs gaze was stuck on it the longest before he shook his head and looked at the beings before his eyes.

"Are you so tongue-tied that you cannot even reply, little thing?"

Ilyads taunting voice continued to ring out as her body undulated with powerful levels of power, her Runic Dao Lines swirling atop her scene as she looked like a valiant queen of war!

Daolord Ray was beside her with eyes that flashed with a light of cruelty, his gaze seemingly only waiting for the veil between them to be torn down.

At the constant taunting, Noah didnt even have to reply as not too far from his, Barbatos who was adorned with a dress inscribed with cackling black skulls spoke out with a domineering tone.

"Can a bitch like you shut up for a second? We cant even take in the sights with your constant yapping."

...!

RUMBLE!

It wasnt the powerful Daolord that replied, but a mere Antiquity behind him as her voice stretched into the ears of Ilyad and the millions of powerful beings from the three factions watching this Writ of Challenge at the edges of the Cosmic Battlefield!

"..."

BZZZT

Daolord Ilyads body began to burn with an obscene amount of power at such a juncture, her eyes becoming locked onto the small yet curvaceous figure of Barbatos who was still staring towards her with a sneer that could topple kingdoms.

A Daolord insulting her was already demeaning and unbelievable enough, but now even an Antiquity did the same?!

A lesson! These new bumpkins that didnt know the rules and honor of Daolords that had to be respected in the Primordial Empire that even Antiquities thought they could talk back to someone like her.

She gritted her teeth as it sounded like multiple worlds exploding, her voice resounding out.

"Ray...start the challenge. Ill rip apart that little bitch first before anyone else"

WAA!

She called for the Writ of Challenge to be started right away as beside her, Daolord Ray raised his hand and called out majestically.

"I put the Anthracite Cosmos and every existence within it on the line for this Writ of Challenge!"

RUMBLE!

The moment his words finished, a bundle of white light shot forth from him and rose to the very top of the Cosmic Battlefield, this light signifying the authority that would be relinquished if he lost the battle of this Writ of Challenge.

Noahs figure also shone at this moment as he felt a pull, uniquely feeling a type of essence wrap around the far away Indigo Cosmos and connecting itself to him, this essence becoming clustered in a bundle of light that was bubbling with his body as he also smiled whole calling out lightly.

"I put the Indigo Cosmos and every existence within it on the line for this Writ of Challenge."

...!

THRUM!

A single sentence.

Yet it was all that was required to initiate the first ever Writ of Challenge Noah would face as the bubbling white light within his body also shot out, coming opposite to the one that Daolord Ray shot out as when these lights intermingled...the crimson barrier that was holding all of them back instantly disappeared!

WAP!

The two sides became utterly exposed to each other amidst the apocalyptic destruction of entire Galaxies.

ZAP!

Amidst this lull, the angered eyes of a particular Daolord moved first, Ilyads fiery figure tearing through the folds of space as she didnt go for the Daolord of the opposing side, and she instead went to the small curvaceous figure of Barbatos that had insulted her!

Runic Dao Lines rotated throughout her right hand and shoulder as they covered a majority of her body, her figure appeared before Barbatos in but a blink of an eye as her hand that palpated with enough strength to shatter a Universe thundered forward.

It went forward to completely disintegrate the figure of this curvaceous woman, and it went forward to give a lesson of how the authority of a Daolord could not be denied!

Yet before it could arrive and achieve its intended purpose...

RUMBLE!

The space between Daolord Ilyad and Barbatos seemingly shook as a figure materialized right in front of her, her eyes unconsciously being sucked into this beings gaze as she felt alarm bells ringing crazily in her mind!

PAH!

In her daze, she barely noticed as her fist that was filled with enough might to shatter a Universe was stopped by the palm of a hand, the force behind it becoming dissolved into nothingness as the figure before her eyes seemingly covered all her sight, becoming larger than anything she had ever seen.

"OOOOOH!"

A bellow erupted from the mouth of this being that had lived for millions of years, her figure breaking into cold sweat as she shook herself out of her unconscious fear and gazed towards the figure of nobody other than Daolord Osmont beside her in utter somberness, feeling like there was danger from all around her!

His eyes seemed to glimmer with the light of billions of Galaxies, staring like at her like she wasnt even worth more than a fly.

Amidst this intense situation, the figure of a curvaceous Necromancer came forward.

Barbaros peered from behind the broad shoulders of her man as with an enticing and provocative expression, he spoke out.

"Dumb bitches like you always die first because you dont know who you should and should not provoke."

RUMBLE!

Ilyads eyes trembled out of pure rage, fear, and wrath as she heard such a tone directed towards her, but she couldnt keep her attention from the Daolord that had stopped her punch so calmly!

But she was actually told she was someone that didnt know who she should and should not provoke.

She didnt know this, but who was she to threaten one of Noahs women? Who was she to even make a move against her?!

Her actions may have seemed small, but they had grand consequences!
