Chapter 4913: Echoes! II

At the center of THE Infiniverse, in that realm of concentrated authority where boundless seas of Infinite Seals of Absolute Tongue permeated everything, THE Infiniverse worked with focus!

The singularity before her had grown beyond anything she had originally envisioned.

What had begun as a budding domain filled with proto-matter and Undifferentiation had transformed into something else entirely. Her Masters Infinities swirled through the corrupted paradoxical proto-matter that THE Living Paradox had sent flooding across Observable Existence. The two substances that should have been hostile to each other now danced together in configurations of forced harmony, of power being merged regardless of origin.

She had scattered her soil.

Like Ongatala descending into the waters of nothing, she had distributed pieces of her existence across the corruption and transformed it into something that served her Master rather than his enemies. And now that transformation was reaching its culmination.

THE Infiniverse observed the singularity with eyes that burned with her Masters borrowed Infinities. The concentrated point of authority before her pulsed with rhythms that seemed to echo across dimensions she couldnt fully perceive.

Definition and Undefinition swirled within its depths. Differentiation and Undifferentiation merged and separated and merged again in patterns that defied conventional understanding.

And then...it began to unfold.

The singularity didnt explode or expand or release its contents in any violent manner. It simply opened, like a flower that had been waiting eons for the right moment to bloom. Petals of multicolored authority peeled back to reveal something that existed at the intersection of everything her Master represented and everything that THE Prima Indifferentia contained.

A domain was forming! A Quintessential, Magisterial Domain!

THE Infiniverse watched with seriousness as the proto-matter and Infinities crystallized into something stable. Something permanent. Something that would exist within her structure as a territory unlike any other she had ever contained.

When it finally took shape, when the last weavings settled into place and the domain achieved completion, something happened that THE Infiniverse had not anticipated.

Silent... Echoes released from the newly formed domain.

And they spread far.

They carried no sound. They produced no vibration. They traveled across her and Observable Existence and THE Prima Indifferentia alike without anyone feeling their passage.

But THE Infiniverse felt them because they emerged from within her own structure, and what she sensed made her foundations tremble.

The Silent Echoes...may have carried glimmers of a Cause.

Maybe.

Maybe!

The uncertainty itself was part of their nature, for they had emerged from a domain built upon paradox and Infinity, where certainty was merely a suggestion rather than a requirement.

BOOM!

THE Infiniverse reeled as her existence suddenly faced an increasing concentration of Civilizational Authority that exceeded anything she had experienced before. The weight pressed against her foundations. It concentrated within her structure, drawn toward the newly formed domain like rivers flowing toward an ocean that had just appeared in their midst!

The concentration increased exponentially.

Ten times what she had contained before!

Her foundations groaned under the weight as she stood grandly to accommodate authority that exceeded her designed parameters. But she was THE Infiniverse. She was the domain of THE Genesis Monarch himself. And she would not fail her Master simply because the burden had grown heavier than anticipated. This was fucking nothing!

She stabilized herself through force of will and sent prompts toward her Masters consciousness.

|Master, unprecedented changes have occurred within my structure.|

|The domain I have been forming through the merger of your Infinities and the corrupted paradoxical proto-matter has achieved completion. Upon its emergence, Silent Echoes were released across Observable Existence and THE Prima Indifferentia.|

|My concentrated Civilizational Authority has increased by a factor exceeding ten times my previous capacity. This increase continues to grow exponentially as the newly formed domain draws authority from ongoing events and their Harvests.|

|I am entering the domain now to assess its nature.|

With those prompts sent, THE Infiniverse turned her attention fully toward the newly formed domain that hovered at the center of her existence.

It appeared as a multicolored crack in the fabric of reality itself, its edges burning with blue-gold flames of Infinities that danced and swirled with life that seemed almost autonomous. The crack was neither large nor small, neither stable nor unstable. It simply was, existing in states that shifted depending on how she observed it.

THE Infiniverse stepped through.

HUUM!

What she witnessed on the other side... defied everything she had expected.

The domain stretched endlessly in every direction, a vast expanse that held within it echoes of Primordial Realms!

Seas of crimson-blue stellar fire reminiscent of Muspelheim lapped against shores made of twilight that could only have come from Alfheimr.

Mountains of impossible ice rose in the distance, their peaks wreathed in mists that spoke of Niflheims frozen depths. Forests of nature so pure and ancient that they could only have been inspired by Vanaheimr grew alongside glaciers and volcanic plains in configurations that should have been impossible.

And woven through all of it, the light of Jotunheims chaotic grandeur, that realm of giants and primordial forces, pulsed with authority that made the other elements seem small in comparison.

But this was not merely a glimpse of a merger of Primordial Realms.

This was something new.

Terrifying seas of definition and undefinition crashed against each other throughout the domain, waves of meaning and meaninglessness colliding in endless cycles that produced foam made of pure potential. Differentiation and undifferentiation existed side by side without destroying each other, the corrupted proto-matter from THE Prima Indifferentia coexisting with the structured authority of her Masters Infinities in harmony that should have been paradoxical.

And it was paradoxical!

THE Infiniverse looked upon this place with eyes that shone with recognition of what she had accidentally created. This was a domain of transformation!

A domain where beings could be broken down and rebuilt. A domain where the boundaries between what something was and what something could become dissolved into pure potential.

A domain of hardship and adversity.

THE Infiniverse smiled...just a bit.

She knew exactly what this domain was for and exactly what to call it.

"THE Yggdrheim."

WAA!

The name emerged from her with authority that made the domain itself seem to resonate in acknowledgment.

THE Yggdrheim.

Where definition met undefinition.

Where differentiation merged with undifferentiation.

Where hardship waited for those brave or foolish enough to enter!

THE Infiniverse sent one final prompt to her Master.

|Master, I have created a domain of hardship and adversity for you and your people. THE Yggdrheim now exists at the center of my structure, a realm where the elements of multiple Primordial Realms merge with the corrupted proto-matter of THE Prima Indifferentia and the authority of your Infinities. Those who enter will face trials that strip away weakness and force evolution upon survivors. I have designed this place to ensure that all who follow you will not forever be stuck in THE First Scale of Existence.|

...!

The prompts hung in the connection between them as THE Infiniverse stood within THE Yggdrheim, surrounded by the terrifying beauty of a domain that would reshape anyone who dared to challenge its depths.

This was her gift to her Master.

This was her contribution to his grand design.

And it was only the beginning of what she would create in his name!
