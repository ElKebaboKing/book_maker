Chapter 1370: Higher Existence! I

His entire body that was made up of billions of Universes entirely vaporized itself as what was left behind was a vibrant golden sea!

Not just his body, but his very Origin that lay at his core had been extinguished into nothingness as one would think they were facing certain death. But it was not so!

His soul was perfused throughout the sea of golden blood that had begun to expand crazily as it contained everything about himself- his identity, abilities, laws, daos, edicts...even the flames of his Edicts could be seen stretched out across the golden sea as only concepts remained while he himself faded away!

The Cosmic Treasure RUINATION floated mesmerizingly in the fantastically expanding golden sea as apart from all of this, even more terrifying changes occurred in other locations. Within the Infinite Empire, Noah's clones that were spread throughout were disappearing into dust as it caused a great shock to spread on anyone he was around.

In the Forgemaster's Universe, Steel Mikhail was with one of Noah's clones as he watched a huge tower shield he especially requested be forged, but his heart shook as he found the Noah he was standing to turn into gray dust in the next second! In the Novus Universe, the Blue Slime was comfortably situated atop the head of one of Noah's clones as in the next second...it felt weightlessness as the being it was on also turned to dust!

Through the Infinite Empire, scenes like this occurred for all of Noah's clones as it brought a huge shock to all those who were around him, their minds not even connecting to the possibility of death as it was something that seemed so far away from Noah.

And before this shocking thought could permeate any further, a resounding mechanical voice rang out in the minds of everyone that was affected by the recent actions!

<He is okay.>

The Cosmic Filament Core that still felt the unbreakable connection between it and Noah's soul spoke out, settling the hearts and surprise of all those affected as if we go even further- it wasn't just Noah's clones that disappeared.

In the Ruination Realm, one of Noah's three main bodies disappeared as whatever origin and soul was contained within it...it was shuttled through space and back to the nondescript location in the ruination sea where a golden sea of True Blood was expanding!

In the Novus Universe, the other original human body of Noah also disappeared as he was watching the progress of those granted the Bloodline of Edicts, all the collection of his Soul and every concept that made it- whether it be skills or Trait unique to this body, it also disappeared in a flash of light to rejoin the mass of expanding True Blood in the far away region.

All his bodies and clones perished as they were connected to the same Origin, and this Origin was about to undergo a tremendous and unshakable change as everything was recalled into the mass of True Blood!

All the concepts that made Noah were contained within his soul that was stretched out across the bundle of the golden sea, everything about him still being there as when it was all collected...

BZZZZT!

The golden sea crazily stretched out in four different directions for millions of light years, and then they began to contract back as when the golden pass was pulling into itself, the surrounding itself seemed to be getting squeezed and pulled!

Space fluctuated unstable as the ruination sea bent and twisted in a unique space-time phenomenon, Noah's budding consciousness that was watching this in shock finding that when the golden sea of True Blood contracted and squeezed the surrounding space...it eventually became torn as from four different directions, Reality itself tore apart.

Reality tore apart as from its tears, pearls of multicolored Essence of Reality stretched out to sink into the golden sea of blood as it went to its very center where it began to shine with utter resplendence!

The magisterial Essence of Reality itself released rays of light in the center of his True Blood as from this light...his new Origin began to manifest.

Going from a Lower Existence to a Higher Existence actually had such a process where one's very Origin...it was molded by the Essence of Reality!

Ah!

Furthermore, this process was even more unique to Noah as his three bodies had all of their qualities brought into the golden sea of True Blood where the end product of it would be a single Origin of a Higher Existence. As for what changes this would bring, what gains or losses Noah would have...he had yet to find out.

Hundreds of millions of Splendiferous Universes began to bloom as they formed a new Origin, each of them shining a multicolored brilliance as since Noah was an Existence with the Universal Race that had its Origin be its actual body...his consciousness began to see the process as this body became reforged by the fantastical Essence of Reality!

Why were Primordials so powerful? What made them stand out since the beginning of time as the strongest beings as countless eons later, they still stood on top of every other existences and races across the myriad of Realities?

What gave this race of beings across the multiple races the capability to split apart the Main Reality itself into countless ones to bloom into limitless possibilities?!

Well, for one- their Origins were molded by the very Essence of Reality as something that Noah did not yet know was that every single Primordial since their birth...was a Higher Existence.

RUMBLE!

Majesty crackled and suffused the surroundings as the golden sea of True Blood began to recede calmly around the reforging Origin and body, entering its interconnected system of veins and arteries as this resplendent sea enclosed in on itself to form an enormous golden cocoon the size of a galaxy!

THUMP!

The cocoon released a constant and resounding beat that caused the surrounding ruination essence to tremble, an oppressive and mystical aura leaking from it with every thump as every now and then, pearls of the Essence of Reality sank into it to complete its transformation.

THUMP!

Eventually, the cracks in Reality faded as the golden cocoon continued to pulse before within seconds....everything became calm and silent.

"..."

The waves of ruination sea resumed their activities while staying away from this location, creating a region of blackness that even the essence of Pandemonium would fear.

In this region, only a golden cocoon remained!
