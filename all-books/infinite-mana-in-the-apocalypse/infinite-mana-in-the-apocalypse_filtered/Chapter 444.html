Chapter 443 - Disciple of Water

Noah glanced at the ancient pedestal holding the resplendent crystal, expecting for the skills and descriptions of the Inheritance of the Disciple of Water to be very similar to the counterpart of Fire. His thoughts were affirmed as he placed his hands on the huge crystal and the enormous flow of information began rushing towards him.

The first thing was the technique that would help him cultivate the law of water itself, it was the unique skill, {Disciple of Water}!

<strong>{Disciple of Water} :: A technique geared towards cultivating the Law of Water. Grants the user the ability to form a unique Water Origin Core.</strong>

It was very similar to the Disciple of Fire, Noah not needing to comment anything on it as he moved on to the Absolute Skill contained in the Inheritance.

<strong><<Herald of Water>> A technique coming from the inheritance of the Disciple of Water that strives for the aspect of extreme in the Law of Water. Abilities include Ice Age, Incarnation of Water, Elemental Soul Reinforcement, and Summon Tsunami.</strong>

<strong>[Ice Age] :: The Herald of Water had the right to call forth an entire field of unstable and frigid water elements onto a wide ranging area. Enemies within will have extreme cold seeps into their bones as the Slow and Frozen Effects are applied while the user gets increased speed and power within the world of ice. Current Enhancement- +0%</strong>

<strong> [Incarnation of Water] :: You continuously release particles of water from your body, creating large bodies of water rapidly as it can be manipulated to form varying shapes depending on the comprehension of the user. The Stage of the Water Origin Core will determine the enhanced damage. Current Enhancement- +0%</strong>

<strong>[Elemental Soul Reinforcement] :: A unique shield of water rich in the elemental law of water wraps around the soul of the user, granting increased protection from any sources of Soul Damage. The Stage of the Water Origin Core will determine the enhanced reinforcement. Current Reinforcement- +0%</strong>

<strong>[Summon Tsunami] :: A disaster materializes in front of the Herald of Water as a tsunami made up of water particles whose only purpose is to drown their enemies descends forth. Current Damage Enhancement- +0%</strong>

The abilities of the Disciple of Water were laid out clearly in front of Noah, a few of them being similar to the abilities from the Disciple of Fire, with some distinct ones. One thing that could be noticed now was the fact that for their enhancements, they were all at 0% as currently, he had not even formed a Water Origin Core yet.

Once he formed one at the First Stage, the enhancement would go to 100%, with the second Stage granting him 200% and on and on.

Noah watched the new skills and abilities settle in his already expansive Stat Panel as he used [Anchor] to place the Spiritual World near the Ancient Pedestal, entering within as he appeared in the skies.

He watched the mystically floating continents on all four sides and the central one that he could see large scale changes on, the Spiritual World System already beginning to renovate and use the [Architecture] feature to fully build a magnificent city of Noahs wildest dreams.

He thought he was creative, but his thought did not stand a chance when compared to the System that lied at the center of the Spiritual World!

The Central Continent of the Spiritual World had futuristic buildings that were reminiscent of the dreams of the people in Noahs home world on what a future would look like, with huge skyscr.a.p.ers and exquisitely styled buildings that would take ones breath away.

Not all the areas of the Central Continent were changed like this, a majority of it still left in a natural state as huge trees rose up, the Spiritual World Beasts still living in the habitats they enjoyed.

Of course, the higher leveled Spiritual World Beasts like the Emperor Penguin preferred the quality of comfort that could be found in the exquisitely built buildings, especially the places with hot springs and beds that made one feel they were sinking down endlessly.

Noah put the spectacular advances of the Spiritual World out of his mind as his figure teleported to the Northern Continent of this wondrous treasure where both the Law Spaces of Water and Fire resided, his figure entering the Water Law Space first as he wanted to form the Water Origin Core similarly to how he first formed the Fire Origin Core without trying anything out just yet.

While he did this, he sent telepathic commands to the Harbingers and his other subordinates to tell them that yet another Inheritance waited for them!

Those that were moving within the Spiritual World simply needed to go out to the luminescent hidden space where Noah anchored the Spiritual World right next to the Ancient Pedestal holding the Inheritance of the Disciple of Water, while those who were not on the Spiritual World would simply have to use the portals in whatever world they were in to return and partake in yet another unbelievable opportunity.

Many of them were already growing numb at all the treasures and opportunities they were receiving, and in such short periods of time at that as they had not even fully devoured the gains of the previous treasures.

For many of his subordinates, they were still just finishing forming their Fire Origin Core at the First Stage, where even the second Stage might take them months or years to achieve. Yet now another Inheritance that would allow them to create another Origin Core landed on their laps!

Many beings in the light expanse would only dream of being subordinates that received such a treatment, but it was not even a possibility for them!

Noah would allow his people to learn the inheritance in the next few hours while he himself began forming the Water Origin Core by using the {Disciple of Water}, where he would try the Dual Cultivation method thereafter to see exactly what stage he could reach with the laws of water and fire.

After this task finished, he would check on exactly how he wanted to use the other treasure he obtained today, the [Unique Material- Aquamarine Mesrandite] that he could use to either refine the Trident of the Sea or possibly create a brand new Supreme Treasure.

As always, he felt like there were so many things he had to do, and just too little time!
