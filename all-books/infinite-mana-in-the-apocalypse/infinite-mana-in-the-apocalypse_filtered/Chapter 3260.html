Chapter 3258 The One Who Walks! II

A beam of unfathomably powerful light that rejected the Outerversal Authority in the surroundings and made way to achieve a single purpose.

Savitar looked at this brilliant light as he scoffed.

He was the One Who Walks.

He walked across the Megalos Beyond and attained a power that allowed him to be distinguished as The Great Savitar.

A Great One.

Many didn't truly understand what this distinction meant in the grand scheme of things.

Even if Savitar was on the lower rungs of a Great One, he was still a Great One!

Even if his Existence was spread out across the Megalos Beyond and Megalos Ynnere, he was still a Great One!

His Body in this Half Tainted Omniverse may only carry a portion of his power, but even this was enough.

Because what he saw under his gaze and what his enemies saw were entirely different.

His flames twisted into a cruel smile as he let this be known.

|Gahaha, your Existence is powerful, but it is crude. Unlike those other Peons of the Commonwealth, you have not attained that State of Existence.|

...!

A State of Existence.

No matter how much power one held over existence, if they did not flow with it as if they were one. If they were not capable of melding with existence and using it to further their alteration of reality, they would not be placed in the rungs of experts.

|Kaka! Let me show you.|

WAA!

His flaming sockets became sharp as for the first time, his body moved forward.

A massive skull filled with thousands of outstretched limbs, and yet its speed was so unfathomable that it disappeared entirely in the next moment!

Command Protector Valeria kept her calm as the potent glow of existence around her only solidified further, but her gaze changed in the next moment as she turned around.

The thousands of limbs of Savitar were like spears as they bloomed half an inch away from Noah's figure that was floating calmly.

Too fast.

No matter her power, Command Protector Valeria would not be able to reach her on time!

A single limb of Savitar was faster than all others as like a sharp spear, it thundered above Noah's head and crashed into him instantly!

The only thing that would be left would be decimation.

|That is but a glimpse of the pain to come for you, Little Peon. You have other compatriots just like him, yes? They will follow you in deat-...?|

His words didn't finish as he noticed something ecstatic.

His limb smashing onto Noah's figure didn't cause the complete erasure of existence, and wondrously, it was like his spear-like limbs were hitting a very stretchy net- much of its force becoming dissipated as the impact that did arrive only smashed his target downwards rapidly as he smashed onto a shattered glass Domain of Existence in the distance!

BOOM!

And even on this shattered Domain of Existence, his body bounced as if it were a balloon.

His figure was still intact.

On his face was a profound expression and a smile as he confirmed a single thing!

The Existence of Savitar was too profound as he was still able to affect his Existence.

And yet the extent of this effect showed that much of the power of this being would be dissipated, and what remained...

"Ah..." Noah brought his hand to his forehead where the sharp point of the limb of Outerversal Morphon Savitar had hit.

There, a drop of incandescent golden blood dripped, his fair skin split open slightly as it already began to heal.

Potent obsidian waves of light sought to ravage this wound and make it even more disastrous, and yet they were devoured seamlessly under the skin as they disappeared.

"You have caused me to bleed."

His finger traced the closing wound as his words caused the One Who Walks to gaze down at him blankly!

Above him, his thousands of limbs rose instinctively as two massive crimson and cerulean wheels smashed towards him while spinning.

The fur on his limbs buzzed like a rough hide as they grasped the intricate structures within the Wheels, bearing their heavy weight as the purple flames within its sockets kept their gaze locked on to Noah!

But the weight of the Wheels was unfathomably heavy- the existence they carried being undeniable as a dozen limbs were twisted and broken from the pressure.

Yet Savitar didn't bat an eye.

|Interesting.|

HUUM!

Instead, obsidian singularities filled with Dark Matter began to appear en masses all around.

In this whole space and as far as the eye could see.

And from these singularities, cylindrical beams of scorching obsidian radiance erupted out in two directions- forming what seemed like two Pillars of pure Outerversal Authority connected to a singularity anchored in space as they began to rotate and spin!

SHIING!

They covered all nearby surroundings.

They constantly spun like milestones that would crush anyone on their path!

Command Protector Valeria's red hair fluttered gorgeously as her body twisted sideways to evade the swing of one of these obsidian Pillars of pure Outerversal Authority, her figure shooting back towards Savitar as she kept part of her gaze on Noah.

Below her feet, a golden wheel bloomed and expanded, smashing into and stopping the spins of nearby Pillars of obsidian radiance while her hands twisted- the crimson and cerulean wheels that Savitar held off with his limbs bearing down with even greater pressure as she voiced out coldly.

|Your opponent is me, O One Who Walks.|

Her might was indomitable even in the face of this Outerversal Morphon!

And yet even at such a time...

"What State of Existence are you talking about, Outerversal Morphon Savitar?"

Noah's voice echoed out in its midst as the wound on his forehead had already closed.

The State of Existence that Command Protector Valeria had yet to reach.

The Flames of Savitar flickered after receiving such a nonchalant question in the midst of all of this, and in the next moment, his massive figure disappeared into the surrounding space once more before hundreds of his limbs turned into spears that bloomed in front of Noah!

But now, they were painted with thunderous flames of obsidian brilliance, Savitar's voice echoing out as these spears came in contact with Noah instantly.

|You are interesting. It is a shame only this small body is here, I would love to inspect you with my main body too...how about you make a trip to the Megalos Beyond with me? I will answer all your questions there.|

...!

Even as he made a horrendous attack, he spoke so simply.

And Noah, with his Existential Veil of Transcendence, got a chance to test exactly what the limits of his defenses were!
