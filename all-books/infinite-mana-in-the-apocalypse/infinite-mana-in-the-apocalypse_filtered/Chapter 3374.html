Chapter 3372 The Wheel Turns As The Wheel Turns! I

Most of all, it was the sense of protection.

Because in any Palace of the Expanse, there was likely a Pioneer.

Someone that had gone ahead step further after Existence Severing!

Someone who could walk across the Expanse with a level of confidence.

With entities like these behind them, there was a level of confidence in surviving the calamity to come.

|Hmm? Palace Master Evrin? What brings you here?|

Inside the domain as small as a world.

Thousands of white lotuses bloomed, a terrifying Existence Severing entity seated atop one Lotus as she raised her eyes calmly towards the descending golden figure of Palace Master Evrin.

The woman seated on the lotus carried a terrifying sense of charm, her eyes being pure white with no pupils as they held unfathomable waves of complexity.

Even Palace Master Evrin always felt a sense of trepidation towards this being, and it was why he left her for last.

Palace Master Antiope...she did not seem like any other Existence Severing entity!

Palace Master Evrin gave a light nod as he spoke.

|A minor matter. A Daughter of Existence I sired was killed today as the enemy is a peak Ordinance Amalgamation creature. I do not wish to have any others fall to them as I wanted to send off a few select forces under us that would ensure quick and smooth success. I just wish to borrow one of your Immovable Sacred Legion- or a few combatants from it.|

...!

He wanted to ensure success and that no others in their Palace fell to this enemy.

Palace Master Antiope raised her eyes at such a request before she replied while tapping on the white lotus below her.

|Which creature is so bold as to not care about the identity of a Daughter of Existence?|

|Ah, just a minor character that may not even know about the Immovable Sacred Palace of the Expanse...| Palace Master Evrin waved his hands and conjured the hazy image of the woman surrounded by multicolored radiance.

Her face was hazy, but one could barely see the flash of apathy and utmost confidence within.

Palace Master Antiope raised her eyes languidly as they came to rest upon the illusory figure shown by Evrin, and...

BOOM!

Her white eyes instantly blasted open as a shocking complexity bloomed from it, Palace Master Evrin becoming stern as his body that was entirely severed from any outside existence but himself floating amidst this eruption of complexity calmly while probing what caused such a sudden change!

He saw...the figure of Palace Master Antiope covered by trillions of compressed sheets of complexity all over her body, a terrifying white eye blooming behind her as if she was ready for battle!

Her whole figure emanated a terrifyingly sharp light that even caused Palace Master Evrin to turn somber, and he looked around as he thought they were under attack.

But...

|...|

There was nobody here.

The only thing that Palace Master Antiope was still staring at while shocked and panting was...the illusory image of the target he sought to kill to avenge his daughter.

This...this illusory image caused Palace Master Antiope to react in such a way?

|Palace Master Antiope?|

Evrin called out slightly.

And...

Antiope only continued to stare at the illusory image ahead, her eyes finally regaining their focus as her body, which had moved impulsively based on cycles of intuition, finally calmed.

It was just an illusion.

Just an illusion!

But...

|How could she be here? This is...|

Her eyes held a trace of fear.

An Existence Severing entity- fearful!

Palace Master Evrin only looked at this scene with greater and greater shock as he didn't know what was going on!

Did Palace Master Antiope know the veiled entity that had killed his Daughter? And why would she be reacting this way?

|Antiope.|

He called out again.

And Palace Master Antiope snapped out of it and stared at him, her gaze then returning towards the illusory image ahead as she waved her hands and caused it to disappear.

|Hold on. Don't do anything yet!|

Her voice held a sense of clear alarm and shock within it as shockingly, a lotus bloomed where she was as she instantly disappeared.

Before she did, in a whisper nobody could hear, she seemed to be talking to herself as she asked.

|How could she be here? How could the Infiniverse Dowager be in these Weavings of Existence?!|

...!

With a flicker, she had disappeared, leaving behind a dazed Palace Master Evrin!

In the depths of the Sacred Palace of the Expanse.

A lotus flickered in and out of existence as it went deeper and deeper into this Blancverse, entering a region that even Existence Severing entities were barred from entering most times.

She flickered through such areas rapidly before she came to slow down as she neared a domain filled with a blue sea, multiple serene floating islands anchored above this blue sea as she went towards the central one.

On such a floating island, in a field of green gold grasslands and trees, a man was talking with a woman as they both raised their heads up at this moment to observe the frantic entrance of Palace Master Antiope.

The woman emanated a terrifying air of another Existence Severing entity, while the man...his dark hair and blue eyes would cause trepidation even in an Existence Severing entity as he seemed to have exceeded such a stage.

A Pioneer!

Existence Severing were Greater Constellations in terms of Complexity.

Pioneers of Existence...were Elder Constellations as they could walk across the Expanse without fear!

Palace Master Antiope landed on the green gold grasslands as her frantic voice echoed out while she waved her hands.

|She is here. Within this timeline of the Wheel! Within these Weavings of the Wheel- she is here!|

...!

The image of the veiled multicolored woman that was just shown by Palace Master Evrin bloomed once more.

The moment her image was shown, the blue eyes of the Pioneer became stern as the Existence Severing Entity beside him took a step back instinctively.

As if a fear of this being was deeply ingrained within her!

|The Infiniverse Dowager...the Existence Devourer!|

...!

They all recognized her.

The slight veiling, her multicolored radiance- they recognized it all too well!
