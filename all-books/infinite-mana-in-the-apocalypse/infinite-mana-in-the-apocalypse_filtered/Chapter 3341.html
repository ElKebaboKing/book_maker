Chapter 3339 Osmont's Decree! III

Regret!

As Entities appeared and picked off his people and his home, the unique Origin Sacred Primal Megalonian that was Pyrrhus felt boundless waves of such an intent.

It was brazen.

His Sacred Primal Eye of Existence was open as he could see all the 81 Megalos Mountains, and on some of them, Invaders at the Existential Severing Stage and Ordinance Amalgamation stage appeared- their weavings mixed.

Some radiated waves of profound Matter.

Others pulsed with incandescent Dark Matter.

While others pulsed with glorious Anti-Matter.

Each held their own uniqueness as not all of them were the massive Omniverse sized entities who split open the golden skies above the Megalos Mountains.

But the entities smaller in size were even crueler as Pyrrhus saw.

He saw an Ordinance Amalgamation entity grab a Rare Sacred Primal Megalonian who was moving others away while exploding with power; this Ordinance Amalgamation Invader...opened his jaws unnaturally as tentacles erupted from it that pulled this Megalonian in.

They devoured him whole.

Thereafter, the jaws of this entity closed as an ecstatic expression appeared on his face- his existence buzzing with resonance as he actually showed great astonishment and shock!

For a brief second, thousands of illusory versions of himself exploded outwards as they exploded in complexity, and all of this complexity merged into the central body as all others faded a moment later.

But the complexity of this Ordinance Amalgamation Invader...was at least 25% more.

...!

From devouring the records and weavings of existence of a single Megalonian!

The Ordinance Amalgamation Invader felt this change personally as his eyes turned crazed, his figure howling as he madly shot out towards other nearby Megalonians.

Others say his shocking eruption in complexity after devouring as even though they were given certain orders to mainly capture, they began to devour Megalonians on sight.

It was brutal.

On all 81 Megalos Mountains, such horrors only became more intense as on another mountain, three Existence Severing Invaders besieged a Quintessential Honored Megalonian, their power erupting out heavily as they cut the very existence of this Megalonian apart into thirds before they each swallowed any piece!

And their bodies instantly exploded with complexity as thousands of illusory versions of themselves appeared- their bodies burning with ever brighter complexity.

[They did it so freely.]

Pyrrhus continued to buzz within Noah's weavings.

[They did it without a care. As if they were plucking fruits or flowers that naturally deserved to be plucked by them, as if they were on a farm and simply enjoying the livestock there. Days after I was born, I had to watch all that I came to know be taken away. My existence was unwilling. My Source was unwilling. My Ordinance...was unwilling.]

HUUM!

The golden sea below Pyrrhus seethed.

As destruction and chaos unfolded all around, he seemed detached from it all as a third of the golden sea was instantly turned into streams that disappeared into his body.

[I know that none of this is real. I know that what has come to pass cannot be reversed. I know that I am dead, and merely part of the Reconstruction of the guilty conscious of an Outsider. But nonetheless, it is a Reconstruction of who I am. Of my Origin. Of my Decree.]

...!

An Origin Sacred Primal Megalonian.

[And even if it is a Reconstruction, in this, there lies a possibility.]

BOOM!

The body of Pyrrhus exploded with radiance as above him, a terrifying Banners that spanned across this Megalos Mountain and extended to the others bloomed.

It was so blinding that the Invader called Theodosius by the Outsider turned his gaze towards Pyrrhus, his eyes showing shock and elation thereafter as he moved his arms with fervor towards the golden sea.

|THEODOSIUS!|

A move that caused the Outsider to fight back with even greater fervor!

Pyrrhus turned his gaze up to stare at the eclipsed golden hand as his banner unfolded.

As his decree became clear.

As his Ordinance was made!

[I detected the Megalos that made these Invaders. But I was born out of the Megalos themselves. Gaps within the Expanse, but it was the Megalos regardless. So my decree...was a decree of the Megalos themselves.]

BOOM!

Megalos.

The Ordinance of the Megalos!

It stretched out madly all around as it seemed far too vast.

Far too glorious than if it was even declared by any other Megalonian.

This was because...

[My Megalos contains everything about me. What made me. My Origin. With it, the one capable of binding me in this Reconstruction should be able to withstand it. To obtain it. If you do, I simply ask...for carnage.]

WAA!

[I do not seek justice. I do not seek for wrongs to be made right. I seek the same carnage and destruction imposed on the Megalonians...by the Invaders.]

HUUM!

Pyrrhus' body radiated pure, unfathomable waves of wonder.

They stretched out to cover all Megalos Mountains with a blinding light that seemed to empower even the remaining Existence Severing Megalonians- their bodies burning ever brighter.

[I seek destruction and cataclysm on all those across the Expanse who rose ever higher from the Blood of the Megalonians. I seek carnage on these Invaders...and the one who brought them here, whether inadvertently or not.]

[I seek Carnage on the Outsider that made this very Reconstruction.]

...!

Noah's will pulsed at such words.

Pyrrhus sought the destruction of the Omnifather himself!

[With my Origin, you will not even need a cycle. With my Megalos, many things are possible.]

After he said these words, the will of Pyrrhus simmered and turned silent.

And at such a moment, Noah sighed as his eyes turned unfathomably bright.

Pyrrhus was making his decree.

He had to make his own.

But he couldn't make just a single decree, he had multiple decrees.

And he could not call even a single one before the others, otherwise it would all be for naught.

If he did this, he knew that when the time came for Amalgamation, he would very likely face the collapse of his existence!
