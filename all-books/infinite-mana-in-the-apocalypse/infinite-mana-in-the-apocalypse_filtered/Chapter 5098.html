"And now...youre not needed anymore."

His right hand rose slowly, index finger extended, flames of THE Silurian Light still wreathing his form in magma-like brilliance. The gesture was simple, almost casual, nothing like the complex somatic movements that Naldine had demonstrated for THE Silurian Light or THE Cambrian Word.

He drew the lemniscate.

His finger traced the symbol of infinity in the air before him, a horizontal figure-eight that looped back upon itself endlessly. The motion was elegant in its simplicity. Where his finger passed, blue gold light remained, the symbol hanging in the air with brilliance that pressed against everything within range.

The somatic component of THE Infinite Light required nothing more than this. No complex gestures. No sequences of movements that needed to be memorized. Just the symbol that represented his Cause, drawn with authority that came from being the product of that Cause.

"Infinite Ember."

"First Spark of my emergence."

"I am the Thread. I am the Cause. What exists without the fire of painful birth shall know that fire now."
