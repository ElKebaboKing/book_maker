A meeting of insurmountable importance!

This was exactly what was occurring at this moment in time as the Archon Basileus Al-Abalem and Archon Basileus Ignatius stared at each other.

Each of their bodies radiated a shocking amount of brilliance and majesty as the meeting of these beings was not a normal one.

They were Rulers of their Legacies- they were Entities who stood atop domains other beings could only dream of.

They were existences with immense power and wealth as the names of their Legacies were etched across uncountable eons.

When they met, something critical would occur thereafter!

The shining bald head of the Archon Basileus Ignatius draped in black and violet glinted with even grander brilliance as he spoke.

"We seem to have allowed a disease to fester to such an extent that it has now jumped onto many prominent Seeds."

His words were light and full of ease as towards them, Archon Basileus Al-Abalem merely nodded with a reply.

"Yes. It may be a first for us while managing these lands, but these things happen. Now, we don't need to allow the disease to fester even more as I can initiate 'that' with the Codex of Extremity. We each just have to bear the cost but..."

The eyes of Archon Basileus Al-Abalem shone as he continued with a level of authority.

"Since the usage of the Codex of Extremity falls under my heel, I will be gracious enough to provide 60% of the required Fruits of Existence."

...!

The brows of even Basileus Anaine arose at such words, not to mention Archon Basileus Ignatius and the entity beside him.

Since when has Archon Basileus Al-Abalem been such a gracious being?

Such a question arose as every move of entities of their level was measured and filled with purpose, and they couldn't yet see why the Archon would take a greater loss than what he already had to currently pay!

But no answer came as faced with his stoicism, Archon Basileus Ignatius simply nodded while replying.

"Alright. Let us proceed with enacting that."

That.

Many of those who spoke about it didn't prefer to call it by its name as it was truly a distasteful thing.

But it was also a necessary thing.

Something to bring it all to an end and start anew.

That.

It was...

"Mors Apokalypsis."

HUUM!

Archon Basileus Al-Abalem spoke sufficiently as the surroundings trembled at the mere mention of it.

Mors Apokalypsis!

We won't be able to know what happened, nor will we be able to even gaze at the authority unleashed by the Codex of Extremity. But...will we really need to?"

WAA!

Ones that did not bode well for the Existences within the Aeonic Annals of Extremity!
