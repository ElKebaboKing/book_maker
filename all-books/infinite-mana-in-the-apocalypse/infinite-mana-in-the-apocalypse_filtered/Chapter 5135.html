Chapter 5133: Greed! V

Valeria spun once through the air, hit the stone of the island, skidded, and came up into a crouch twenty paces away. Her braids had come loose along one side. A thin line of golden blood traced down her cheek from the corner of her brow. She straightened with measured calm, her shoulders rolling as she reset her posture, and then she drew both blades of infinity again without a word.

Above, the abomination completed its spin.

It came back down toward Noah faster than it had gone up!

Its jaws didnt open this time. Its hands extended forward in front of its body, and from those hands erupted long jagged talons made of concentrated madness, THE Gamaidian given edge and purpose!

Octavius launched new weavings from behind the abomination.

His Unity reached through the air in coordination with the abominations strike, not trying to bond with Noah now but trying to bond with the talons themselves, trying to graft them into Noahs body once they connected so that the embedded weapons would root and spread and turn Noah into new soil for Primordial Mycelia.

Valeria came in from the left again at the same instant.

Three angles. Three attacks!

Three different methodologies converging on his standing position with the precision of a rehearsal that had been waiting!

Oh!

Noah watched all of it.

His eyes drank in the weavings as they approached. The mad Infinity of the abomination, wild and beautiful in its chaotic saturation. The Unity of Octavius, patient and infinite in its approach!

Valerias cutting Infinity, clean and scholarly in its geometry. Three different expressions of what Infinity could become in the hands of beings who had been engineered to hold it, and all three of them reaching for him at once.

He found himself genuinely interested.

The madness especially. THE Gamaidjan was rare. Most beings who fell into it were consumed too quickly for any meaningful study, their weavings dissolving into incoherent pressure before anyone could trace what the madness was actually doing to them at the fundamental level. But here it was, harnessed, walking, attacking with something that passed for strategy!

Octavius had achieved something grand.

Noah smiled brilliantly.

He raised both his hands upward into the descending convergence of attacks.

HUUM!

Seas of blue Infinity erupted outward from his body in concentric waves, the blue deepening through cobalt and into something closer to void as the density compounded against itself.

Multicolored Quintessence Infiniforce bloomed between the blue currents, golds and violets and purple and silvers braiding through the blue without mixing with it, each color holding its own distinct weaving. The ambient Infinity of the region bent toward him. The rivers of Observable Force on the island curved upward to feed his expanding authority!

Multicolored flames of Existence licked through its mid-tiers. Infinite mirrors of Paradox reflected impossibilities at every angle. The eternal storm of Chaos swirled at its apex, and beneath that apex the additional actualized Civilizations layered themselves in concentric halos of authority!

His voice rolled out across the island with calm grand finality.

"Freeze."

HUUM!

On the broader island, the Primordial Architects who had been struggling to rise stopped rising.

The ones who had been knocked unconscious remained unconscious, their Infinity reservoirs held still within their stalled forms. The ambient rivers of Infinity threading through the island paused in their golden flow, their currents held motionless as if the islands entire circulatory system had been told to wait!
