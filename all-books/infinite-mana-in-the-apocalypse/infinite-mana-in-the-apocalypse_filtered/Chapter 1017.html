Chapter 1015 - The Rise of the Tyrannical LiChapter Emperor! I

A skill tree that cost 12 Billion Skill points- and one that held a skill that seemed to make it possible for Noah to call forth more than 1 Lich Emperor.

But was it truly so?!

...Yes! It was so!

The mere descriptions of the skills within the [Tyrannical Lich Emperor] skill tree were absurd, to say the least.

[Summon Lich Emperors](5/5)(ACTIVE):: The user is capable of calling forth a maximum of 5 Lich Emperors at the same stage, their Parameters increased to be 200% of the caster's maximum power.

[Undead of Ruination](5/5) (PASSIVE):: All of the summoned Undead the Tyrannical Lich Emperor are branded with an Undead Ruination Aura. The Undead Ruination Aura grants +100,000% Ruination Damage, +100,000% Poison and Vitality Decay Damage, +100,000% Increased Resistance to All Laws and Daos, 20% of All Damage is Converted to Health, and it allows for the collective bearing of all damage dealt to be distributed to the entire Legion.

[Unstable Necrotic Ring of Death](5/5) (PASSIVE):: A constantly swirling ring of death filled with the essence of the Daos of Ruination, Annihilation, Decadence, Destruction...and the Dao of Withering begins to rotate around any Undead Legion that the Tyrannical Lich Emperor called forth. The greater and more powerful the number of Undead, the more potent and deadly the Necrotic Ring of Death will be.

[Endless Torment](5/5)(PASSIVE):: Any poor soul that manages to fall within the bounds of the Unstable Necrotic Ring of Death is burned to fuel the power of the Ring of Death even more, every additional being that dies within it adding 10% Increased Damage and area of effect for upto a maximum of 1,000,000% for 60 seconds.

A gorgeous skill tree!

A single Active Skill with the rest of them being Passive, one that didn't just give Noah the capability to call forth 5 more PARAGON Level Lich Emperors- but also brought to life something that Noah considered a method that would put to shame most killing machines.

Without a delay, he started by casting [Summon Lich Emperors].

THRUM!

KEKEKEKE!

Eerie sounds began to permeate throughout, Noah waving his staff madly as he lost himself in the wonders of playing with fearsome Undead creatures, constantly pouring his mana out as after the Lich Emperors at the level of Paragons rose up, their bodies lit up with the glorious light of death as they smashed their staff into the chaotic void- causing the auras of 100 Monarchs to rise from each of them and below them, thousands of Abyssal Undead Great Sages and tens of thousands of Undead Sages began to rise.

It was the grand Symphony of Death ringing loudly once more as Abyssal Skeleton Kings, Dullahans, Undead Dragon Emperors, Unholy Phoenixes...a myriad of Undead rose from the newly summoned 5 Lich Emperors and the initial one that stemmed from the skill tree of the Nether Lich Emperor!

Hundreds of thousands turned to millions as very soon, millions turned into hundreds of millions as a truly large number of undead permeated around Noah as he cackled wildly.

This was because as the Legions of Undead formed, the skill of [Unstable Necrotic Ring of Death] passively came online as a storm of essence containing coagulated forms of blood and bones rose around the Undead Legions like a rotating cloud of death!

It truly and majestically was a Necrotic Ring of Death forming, the essence of all of Noah's currently comprehended Daos beginning to swirl in a circular motion as with the dense aura of death, bones and skulls could be seen within the swirling ring of death that threatened to drown out any enemy it touched.
