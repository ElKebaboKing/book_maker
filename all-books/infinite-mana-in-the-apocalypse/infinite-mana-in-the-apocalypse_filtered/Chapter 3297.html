Chapter 3295 An Unfathomable Emergence! I

Has that time come.

A single sentence.

A single glance up.

Yet this was all it took to cause blaring alarms to ring out within this idyllic paradise and across the entire Wheel it was connected to.

Within this idyllic space the size of a small word, there were small twinkling circular orbs that floated around like stars, but each one seemed to hold a terrifying weight that could collapse the surrounding space if another force wasn't holding them up.

The disheveled man waved his hands towards these unfathomably heavy orbs spread out across this idyllic space as they all twinkled before coming to circle around him, forming into a cluster of dazzling orbs.

The disheveled man then took out a worn out looking treasure chest, the orbs circling around him beginning to move into this treasure chest slowly!

As they came, they reduced in size before they disappeared entirely.

If one looked closer towards these orbs, they would shockingly come to notice that they held vibrant Omniversal boundaries, with countless complex weavings of existence buzzing within each one as these were...Omniverses!

Omniverses.

And the ones circling around the disheveled man and going into the worn out treasure chest numbered in the hundreds.

This entity wasn't hurried at all, and he truly paid no attention to the alarms that had rang out.

He didn't even bother to raise his head to see that around the idyllic landscape the size of a world, over a hundred Royal Existential Omniversal Simians at the Omniversal Stage were surrounding him, and another blinding flash of light bloomed as...The Sword of the Commonwealth himself arrived!

The moment he did so, space around him distorted as he fused with the surrounding existence, a massive Eye of Existence opening above him while a golden staff appeared in his hands that he swung down towards the isolated world below.

...!

The staff smashed into an invisible boundary, and not even a decibel of sound was made.

The Sword of the Commonwealth stared threateningly inside this small world as truly, it wasn't a prison in the truest sense.

It was a prison as the being inside had never come out for eons now.

But...the Simians didn't make this prison, nor could they enter it. They had only constructed the tight Wheel of Weavings around it within this Exalted Omniverse!

And no matter what they did over the eons, they have never been able to enter that small, isolated space.

The worn out treasure chest closed calmly as the disheveled man waved his hands and caused it to disappear, his figure slowly straightening up as a clear white robe appeared on him, his hand waving over his head and causing vibrant dark hair to bloom down to his shoulders wildly.

His fair skin glinted with untold brilliance as he gazed up again, ignoring the Omniversal Stage Simians staring at him with wide eyes, some even showing apprehension and shock as his gaze was looking at something extremely far away.

"It is time. As the Omnifather had stated..."

A slight sigh seemed to escape him as in the next moment, he took a step.

A single step.

And his figure entirely disappeared!

Along with hundreds of Omniverses, the white-robed man disappeared under the eyes of the terrifying forces of the Commonwealth.

|...|

Stark silence descended in the surroundings as an instant later, beside the Sword of the Commonwealth, and spatial light flashed as another entity appeared.

It was none other than the Shield of the Commonwealth- Eudemus!

His messy dark and white hair seemed out of sorts as he sighed while looking at the empty idyllic world below them that remained untouched.

"Well, this is something I did not anticipate. In all these eons, he stayed still. For him to move now...it seems that shit is about to really hit the fan."

...!

His words were calm and yet filled with a sense of somberness!

He was the only human in the midst of Simians as the Sword of the Commonwealth had his fur tinged with golden light as he gazed towards his direction before speaking out coldly.

"He was a prisoner this whole time, and we simply grew complacent thinking he was broken. He will be found again."

These words caused Eudemus to raise his brows as he shook his head.

"Was he ever truly our prisoner? In all these past eons, nobody was able to get into that small domain, and he now left it just as freely. We haven't been able to lock him down here, nor have we been able to find him in the Expanse. I really think...we should just leave things as is..."

The words were those seeking peace.

In the past, Eudemus had told Noah that he sought a solution to the Three Body Problem- whether this was through peace or Annihilation!

At this time...

"How can things be left as is? After what we did to them. You know this more than anyone else, Eudemus...there can be no peace."

...!

There can be no peace.

Eudemus remained quiet after such words as he thought about the past.

The rise of the Commonwealth was not a seamless one.

The rise of the Commonwealth was a bloody one!

Beneath the rubble of their rise...the force of the prisoner that had just left lay!

But were they truly defeated all the way back then?

With their power, why did they not fight back? This whole time, why had this being not made a move?

And with him moving right now, just what did it say about the coming future?!

|...|

There were no answers. Only the stern faces of Protectors gazing at the empty idyllic space with uncertainty. The Sword of the Commonwealth looked around at all the powerful Protectors nearby as he waved his hands towards them.

|You no longer need to stand guard as for now, spread out. If you come across Morphons, Worms, or anything else- spread the word. Anything not belonging to the Commonwealth...eradicate it!|

WAA!

A command was given.

And it was then followed.
