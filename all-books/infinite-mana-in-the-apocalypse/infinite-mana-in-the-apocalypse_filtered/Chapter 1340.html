Chapter 1338 - Halfway Towards The Cosmic Realm! II

His eyes shone with boldness and power as during this time, his Origin had become even more vibrant as with the Devouring of so many Shards of a Cosmic Core and Shards of a Cosmic Primordial Heart, his Origin was now saturated with an unbelievable number of Universes as it still rose higher.

If one counted them all, they would be able to see the fantastical number of more than 30 Billion Universes, with this number changing to 31 and 32 billion within seconds as Noah rushed towards the halfway point of the Cosmic Realm in mere seconds!

He made it look too easy when others were still stuck with a few thousand Universes all their lives, and even prominent destined existences like Halcyon and Springforge had only formed a few million in all these years.

Yet he...ah!

Brilliance and glory leaked out of him as with every passing of a second now, a billion Universes bloomed as his body alone palpated with magisterial power!

As one being forged billions of Universes while also designing Cosmic Daos, events continued to unfold elsewhere as there was a particular one worthy of note atop the healing Primordial Empire.

The figure of Aegon was floating above the vast Empire that now had erased the crimson wound running across it, the area where Hyperion had plucked out Cosmos being filled with others as Aegon merely closed up the huge rift of Ruination with the remaining Cosmos!

His calm gaze still carried a trace of wrath as even though the cluster of Cosmos was still as brilliant as ever, he still felt that it now had some imperfections.

"I leave for such a short time and so many things happen?"

...!

A sonorous voice resounded behind him as without his knowledge, a veiled being clad in white appeared in a mesmerizing fashion!

Adorned in an exquisite dress and a veil covering her head, she released an aura of power and purity as even the essence of Ruination in the surroundings didn't bear to ever approach her.

Aegon turned towards her direction as he still held his impassive expression, and yet those with extremely discerning eyes would be able to see a flash of a hidden emotion hidden deeply within the gaze of this Champion!

"The Ender of Reality has risen and made his first actions, that is all."

...!

The brows of the veiled Primordial before Aegon lifted as the one that Noah knew only by the name Cecilia- the being who had given him a minuscule portion of a Primordial Heart a long time ago...this being made her return into this Reality at this time!

Aegon's words caused her to raise her head to the side as one could imagine a calm smile being etched on her face.

"When I left, you should have been nearing an average power of a Cosmic Primordial Beast, now that you have come in contact with one, the boosts of your system should have kicked in as it is now an inevitability for you to surpass it!"

...!

Her words were calm and filled with surety, speaking of something that only Aegon was privy to as he nodded calmly!

"I will be the only one unrivaled and Invincible...with my rate of growth rising to whatever is required to reach the level of the strongest enemy from my Reality..."

Yes! One of the perks of the Invincible System was the fact that Aegon would have his rate of growth and power scale upwards crazily whenever he met stronger enemies, with it continuing to rise until he was the only one who could call himself Invincible within his Reality.

'Within my Reality...' Such a thought crossed Aegon's mind as his eyes flashed with an unknown light, focusing on the figure of the Primordial before him.

"Yes. I will be more proactive in clearing out the smaller Beasts and will soon approach the Primordial Sea and drag it out of its whole when my strength has risen sufficiently. I want to complete more Edicts first..."

His eyes were filled with confidence and strength as he said this, with the veiled Primordial coming before him as slender white gloved hands reached out from her pristine white dress and came forward to tap on his forehead.

Aegon floated in the Ruination Sea calmly as if this was normal, the veiled Primordial nodding her head as she spoke out calmly.

"Everything is fine with your Soul Record...you're even exceeding many of the parameters that other Champions have a hard time passing with the Invincible System."

...!

Aegon's eyes flashed as he nodded as if this was to be expected, his eyes turning towards her questioningly as he asked.

"What came from the incident we experienced before that you left to investigate?"

He recalled an event not too long ago where a consciousness had actually hidden from them and been around them without their notice, questions on exactly this as the veiled Primordial pulled back while gazing upwards to a place only she could see!

"The culprit stems from the Bloodline of Samsara. It might be another Primordial or an Anomoulous existence that has latched onto this Bloodline."

...!

She replied calmly as Aegon's eyes opened wide. Why would such an existence have been listening to their conversation?

"After finding out about this, I visited Old Man Cthal'ithost who designed the Edict of Samsara to know who exactly was so bold as he was about to tell me when all of a sudden...that being achieved full inclusion into the Bloodline of Samsara."

WAA!

"Old Man Cthal'ithost considers all those with full inclusion into one of his Bloodlines to have certain rights and protection, so he then chose not to tell me...that Old fool."

...!

Terrifying words left her mouth as the complicated weaving of destiny was revealed ever so slightly at this moment!

Just within the last hour during the battle in the Primordial Empire, Noah had completed the Nomological Edict of Samsara as it just so happened to coincide with the same time his identity was about to be known by the veiled Primordial Cecilia!

Yet the first Edict he chose to complete was Samsara.

Through coincidence or Destiny, a Calamity was averted through this single action!

"So I simply returned here to check on how things are going after that. After all, I cannot change the mind of an Elder Primordial like Cthal'ithost."

Her words carried a trace of annoyance and respect as she spoke about this being, the Primordial and her Champion continuing to talk as waves of unseen destiny reverberated out.

Far from them, in a shining white sea.

A certain Ender of Reality raised his head as his three eyes opened sharply, waves of destiny and fortune surrounding them in a storm as the surroundings became tinged gold!

Even further away in a newly founded Infinite Empire, Noah was surrounded by the essence of Kismet as his entire body glowed gold...and this essence began to flow around him madly at this time.

Destiny and fortune....they interweaved around certain existences as the product that would result from this weaving was still not known!
