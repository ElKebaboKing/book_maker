Chapter 448 - Supreme Treasure

Noahs Ancient Blood Clone teleported deep in the Central Continent of the Spiritual World where the Blacksmiths Forge lay. He had spent a significant amount of time cultivating the four laws that he currently had the cultivation techniques of, and planned to get to the task on how to best use the [Unique Material- Aquamarine Mesrandite] that he obtained from the southern region of the Celestial Realm.

It was a treasure that could either be used to forge a new supreme treasure, or be used to [Refine] an already existing one. freewebnovel.com

His current mindset was leading him towards creating another one, as he had very few to begin with, and using the material to refine his only existing one would only give a boost, himself having no knowledge of exactly how big this boost would be.

He was thinking of using the Unique Material to create a Supreme Treasure in the form of an armor since he already had one in the form of a weapon. One weapon, one armor, two Supreme Treasures!

His gaze was clear as he went towards the [Hearth] of the Blacksmiths Forge and communicated with the Spiritual World System.

"Is the process of forging a Supreme Treasure any different from the Item Sets weve been creating?"

[Yes. It will take a significant amount of time to create one depending on the unique material and expected treasure.]

Noah gave a light nod as he waved his hands and pushed the [Unique Material- Aquamarine Mesrandite] towards the hearth, a blue light shining as the large crystal material that was double Noahs own size was scanned thoroughly.

[This unique material is fully capable of creating a complete Supreme Treasure, with a possibility of a few scraps being left to perform a refinement in the future. Do you have any specific requests for the design of the treasure? The [Recipe Design] feature can be used in this instance.]

The Spiritual World System that understood everything happening on it asked in a monotone fashion, Noah smiling as he knew this entity which was connected to many planets through his own connection with it as its master had access to enormous vaults of information.

He had access to this information as well, but his mind was nowhere near as close to what the Planetary Systems could contain and process, thus he left a majority of the minute small details on all the planets he came across and their abundance of information to the Planetary Systems. When it came to items, he had some ideas on what he could design himself based on his imagination and his own experiences, but he knew that the Spiritual World Planetary System could come up with something multiple times better than him just because of its special identity!

"Use the information of all the suits of armor weve come across recently from the different worlds and organizations. Design something that is a combination of all of their strengths to create a Supreme Armor."

Thus, he left the design of the powerful treasure to the entity with enormous reserves of information to make something at the peak of quality and usability.

[Understood, Master. Any specific name for this Recipe Design?]

Noah thought closely at this question before he replied, a smile spreading across his devilishly handsome face as he glanced at the water elements dancing around the [Unique Material- Aquamarine Mesrandite].

"Staying with the theme of water, lets call this Supreme Treasure...-[Hydromancers Mantle]."

[Understood, Master. It should take approximately 72 hours.]

RUMBLE!

Essence began to converge around the Blacksmiths Forge as the huge [Unique Material- Aquamarine Mesrandite] was taken further into the Hearth, a blue flame beginning to wrap around it as it shimmered strongly under the heat.

While the material was being broken down, the Spiritual World System was compiling large reserves of information on all the armors that the numerous worlds Noah was connected to held, a database of information that even the strongest information groups would envy freely flowing into it from the connection it shared with Noah.

At this moment, a resplendent armor that very few would know the name of but closely remember its unique design was being created. The [Hydromancers Mantle] would have its design started today, with its creation being completed some days later!

With this process underway, Noahs clone continued forward as there were simply too many things that fell on its hands. He was currently at the Domain Realm, but his skill trees were at the Soul Forging Realm. On top of increasing the percentage of laws, he had to increase the proficiency of his skills while preparing for a busy future.

His main body had been using the abilities of {Herald of Fate} and {Agent of Chaos} to foresee many things with more clarity these past few days, grasping a few abstruse pieces of information that dealt with the Infernal Lords. His title as the 9th Infernal Lord would not all be for naught, and he foresaw that he would be called upon sometime soon in the future for him to begin carrying out his duties!

The clone continued forward as the main body was moving side by side with Athena and the members of the Righteous Fire Exploration Team. Boru was still as boisterous as ever- he talked towards the two beings holding titles of Noble Fates with more respect as they were now an entire realm above him.

"You two have gathered the information on your possible opponents and rewards?"

The gaze of Boru was shining as he mentioned the rewards, just the rumors released on exactly what was at stake in the upcoming tournament making the hearts of powerful Celestials quiver!

Noah smiled as he replied to Boru with shining eyes.

"One of the biggest rewards in the Luminescent Rainbow Dew, something that was found in limited quantities by the Founders more than a thousand years ago, a treasure that guarantees a being an advancement to the World Realm!"

Yes! Just one of the rewards up for grabs for battling Special Existences and beings with Noble Fates was something that would create an expert capable of affecting the direction of the future war. Experts at the World Realm were rare, where even the lowest leveled Infernal Lords stood at the peak of this level! Treasures that allowed for one to enter such a realm...were nearly nonexistent in the hands of others. In this coming tournament, just what other treasures lay in store? Just who would stand against them vying for these same treasures?!
