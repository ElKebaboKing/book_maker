Chapter 3210 A Moment in Time

Inside of the Infinite Omniverse.

Within the Aletheian Haven of Dreams.

Out of the many Aletheian Havens out there, it was Dreams and Helios that Noah and his people found themselves frequenting the most.

Dreams held a certain sense of awe as with a little input of their own imagination, the surroundings would take on this shape and continue to change how they wished.

At this moment, in a small portion of the Aletheian Haven, Noah's Ascendant Existential Extremity Clone of Dreams was sitting on the edge of a golden waterfall, the waters passing by below his feet and thundering down below.

When one looked at them, one would notice the concentration of the water within the river extending from the waterfall rapidly increasing while the river itself continued to enlarge and expand.

And if one looked closer to the surroundings, everything else was also expanding as the process of Existential Terraforming continued unabated!

"It's truly marvelous..."

Beside Noah on the golden waterfall.

The figure of the golden haired Dr. Hall could be seen, his white coat adorned on his body as he observed this process along with Noah.

In the Infinite Omniverse, there were very few who could genuinely say they were a friend to the Existential Omniversal Lumen Emperor who lorded over these vast lands, but there were still some who managed to be themselves around him and form a stable friendship even with the complex relationship that made him seem grander than life.

For Dr. Hall, his love for understanding the profundities of Existence and all the wonders out there made it so that he would frequently come and talk with Noah.

Over the days in the Desecrated Existential Chronos Domain and even more days in the Var El Eonic Omniverse Record, Dr. Hall only found himself more and more around Noah as more wonders unfolded all around them.

Wonders of possibilities. This was how their interests aligned!

Dr. Hall looked above them to an incandescent gold sky as he continued.

"I sensed the concentrations of all viable forms of energy dip in this place and in all the places my other Clones are into inside the Infinite Omniverse...what is doing now that requires so much energy? Can I assume it is half of all the Authority that was collected within the Infinite Omniverse?"

He asked because he was curious.

And Noah smiled and nodded in a reply.

"Over half of all the authority of the Infinite Omniverse. But it was all for my purposes. The images I just showed you all across the Lineage- this one deals closer to the new enemies we've just come across. The Outerversal Morphons. I'm trying to go to the same place they are in, to do something they're currently doing."

...!

The words caused Dr. Hall's eyes to widen with golden light, his glasses pushed up as countless thoughts buzzed inside of him.

Among the many people inside the Infinite Omniverse, he was spreading his reach out far and wide in regards to health and wellness, with many of his Practices and Business stretched across the different Aletheian Havens.

Anything new, he loved to understand and see if he could use.

But this concept of Outerversal Morphons...just seemed too grand.

"What will that make you as an individual? If you succeed, would you still be considered a native of this Omniverse, or an Outerversal Entity outside of it? Which distinction trumps the other?"

What exactly would Noah be?

Far from the positions of Noah and Dr. Hall.

Inside the Aletheian Haven of Helios.

The figure of Sona sat on a sandy gold beach, her body adorned in a yellow gold swimsuit that showed much of her radiant skin and icy green gold hair that drifted below her shoulders.

She sat on the beach and stared out into a golden sea burning with flames of helios, and yet they held a rejuvenating effect for anyone swimming within it as the figures of Emilliana and other Sanctus Princesses were freely swimming inside of it currently- taking what seemed to be a long overdue vacation after fighting wars for so long.

On Sona's head, Bubbles was bouncing happily in his blue slime form, while Little Caculatan shone with an obsidian gold radiance as its round furry body rested on Sona's lap.

Her eyes gazed ahead as she seemed to be thinking of far too many things.

"You haven't gotten used to it? Sitting back and letting Noah do everything?"

From behind, the blessed figure of Barbatos came by- her small but curvaceous visage adorned in a black two piece.

Her beauty only seemed to be getting more refined as she held in her hands two skewers of Dragon Meat grilled in the fires of Helios!

Behind them on this beach, the figure of Noah's Ascendant Existential Extremity Clone of Helios could be seen standing before burning flames of helios that roasted meat, his Son beside him learning the Extremity of cooking.

To their left and right, numerous figures could be seen as Erikson's bronze body was sunbathing in his proper beach attire, and Ayame and Lilith were seated nearby talking.

Even closer to Noah, the figures of Adelaide, Katelyn, Eowyn, and all the other Queens of the Infinite Omniverse could be seen seated on golden beach chairs surrounding an extended vertical table that held an overabundance of Quintessential Fruits of Existence and grilled meat from the most exotic fantastical animals.

At the head of the table, they all surrounded and happily talked to the figure of Amelia Osmost- their Mother-in-law!

Apart from them, the figures of Kazuhiko, Emperor Penguin, and many others could be seen doing their own things across this golden beach.

All of this was happening in the background and more as Barbatos came to sit beside Sona and offered her one of the skewers in her hands.

Towards her question, Sona could only shake her head while accepting the skewer and taking a bite.

"It's not even that. I had these grand plans of an Initiative, and even when I think about it now, that doesn't seem to be it. It's just..."

A sense of frustration was on her face as she didn't seem to be able to put into words what she was feeling.

Barbatos smiled towards her as she spoke like she understood.

"Do you feel a sense of disconnect? Like you shouldn't be here doing this? Relaxing while it feels like out there, the whole world- well I guess Omniverse now; while it feels like the whole Omniverse is burning?"

The words from Barbatos caused Sona to go entirely silent as the Necromancer of Existence smiled with gorgeous brilliance.

"What I've come to learn over the months and years is that there is never a time when the outside Worlds, Cosmos, Existential Domains, or Omniverses aren't burning. But that can't stop us from enjoying the simple joys of life. Should we be serious and worried all the time? Ponder in silence for whatever is to come? I would tell you to put your trust in Noah and just be there for him when he needs you. Until then, enjoy yourself. The Fruits of his labor inside this endless Infinite Omniverse. And of course, don't forget the Dual Cultivation sessions..."

She laughed while stating her last sentence, her figure rising as she patted Bubbles above Sona's head- turning back to where the party was brightest as she left behind words of advice.

No sooner had she left did another figure appear beside Sona, this being none other than the Sanctus Autarch.

She was on the more moderate side as she had not gotten used to things fully just yet, and she was adorned in a light green robe while on the beach as she looked somewhat out of place.

But when she looked at the Queen Mothers nearby and the Sanctus Princesses and Dragon Knights in the water, she felt a sense of security she hadn't felt in a long time as she came to sit beside Sona.

She didn't have to say anything.

One old.

One young.

Two women sat on the beach inside a grandiose Aletheian Haven of Helios, their emotions similar as all around them, Entities from different walks of life took a moment to just breathe and relax.

In difficult times, moments like these were rare to come, but were often times the most important.
