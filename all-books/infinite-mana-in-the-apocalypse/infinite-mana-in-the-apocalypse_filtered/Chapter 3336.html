Chapter 3334 Progression! II

Such a scene was observed by Noah in the far distance as in a place only he and a few could enter, he smiled while observing it.

Atop the multicolored throne that all authority within the Infinite Omniverse seemed to be coursing through.

Noah watched his son and others attain the Omniversal Realm as he sought to have them gain an introduction to Psioneutrinos- to potentially increase their complexity with this mixture of Matter and Dark Matter as he sought to induce within them a sensation of at least becoming One With Existence.

None of them could be like him in transcending their existence as they did not have what he had to make it possible.

So they had to be creative.

If they could attain One With Existence before the reconstruction of the Omnifather ended, they would be able to make even more rapid progress with their complexity!

If possible.

Noah continued watching their celebration as he tapped his fingers at the tune of the Bard of Quintessence while speaking to the Manifestation of the Infinite Omniverse.

"How is it?"

|Six total Dark Matter Cores and Tainted Omniverses are being assimilated, with half of these already being fully melded with the Infinite Omniverse. All calculations show that complexity itself should be sufficient to attempt everything, but to increase the degree of certainty by another margin, the assimilation of even more Tainted Omniverses and their Dark Matter Cores would be beneficial.|

...!

The Infinite Omniverse felt like it was there.

But it wanted a higher degree of certainty.

So more complexity was needed!

And when it came to obtaining Tainted Omniverses after all of Savitar's were swallowed up...

"Mmm, how about we take the established homes of our plotting enemy? We can also use them as the testing grounds for the next generation of the Weapons of Mass Existential Omniversal Destruction."

His voice was heavy with malice as a cold smile hung on his face, Savitar having exposed all the Tainted Omniverses he knew The Great Ponderor to be a master of as Noah could unleash a set of wonders of his enemy before he went in to assimilate the scraps.

|Understood.|

And the Infinite Omniverse was fully on board.

|Beginning the deployment of the successor of Fat Monkey, the Tzar Versepiercer.|

...!

The Tzar Versepiercer!

A daring name!

A daring weapon developed and improved by the Infinite Omniverse from its tests as it became more and more proficient with Dark Matter, to the point that it packed over a million highly unstable miniaturized Fat Monkeys into a single Tsar Versepiercer.

A million!

Even Noah was entirely scared of this as a single improved Fat Monkey could even decimate the weavings of an early Omniversal Stage Entity.

A million of such horrors packed into a single one?

Very thin layers of weavings separated them as the smallest trigger could set them off.

And once they were set off...

Oh boy.

It honestly felt like an overkill.

Currently, only 11 Tzar Versepiercers had been built so far as they took a tremendous amount of authority for the Infinite Omniverse to focus, direct, and build them to ensure nothing imploded and decimated everything.

The first ones weren't even built in the Infinite Omniverse as the experiments were carried out in an Isolated Aletheian Haven that was thrown out into an empty area of Sector 1 that the Cartographer of Existence had mapped just in case!

And now, one would be put to the test.

"Let's go."

The target had to be ascertained with his own eyes.

|O Dreamer of the Megalos...are you planning to act against the Great Ponderor right away? He has many friends...|

Across the Megalos Ynnere, Savitar led Noah's body across the relative positions of his now devoured Tainted Omniverses and towards a Tainted Omniverse under the banner of the Great Ponderor.

"You don't worry about that. We should be close enough..."

He stopped in a region of complete darkness as Savitar floated behind him akin to a loyal follower.

Above Noah, a dazzling golden eye unfurled that caused the calm Savitar some trepidation, but it wasn't even staring at him!

It stared off into an extremely far distance that they weren't remotely close to, and yet within moments, the Sacred Primal Eye of Existence covered this distance as a pulsating outline of a Tainted Omniverse became clear.

One of the Omniverses under the banner of the Great Ponderor!

Noah's eyes looked at it from afar as he waved his hands and caused the appearance of an obsidian gold statue that looked just like him.

It was adorned in royal purple Armaments over its golden sheen, and his hands were clasped on his chest while the face was one of sorrow as if he were saying a prayer for the poor souls about to be exposed to its wonder!

The Tzar Versepiercer- a Weapon of Mass Existential Omniversal Destruction.

"Go...and let me see your fallout."

WAA! Noah gave the command, and with it, an obsidian gold light flashed speedily!

Speed was one of the main components of this unstable construct, its sorrowful expression slowly flickering to cruelty as it thundered towards a Tainted Omniverse even faster than light.

Within the Tainted Omniverse.

A massive Outerversal Morphon bowed towards an obsidian veiled cluster that his master swam in as his deep voice echoed.

|Master, Bellerophon does not understand. What enemy could possibly make you wish to make a temporary retreat into the Megalos Beyond? Your might is endless, and your power is unfathomable...|

A Follower asked their Master, and their master shook their heads as if they wouldn't understand

|Sometimes, one must mak-...!|

The intent stopped.

It couldn't help but stop.

Because in the next moment...

BOOM!

A white light that seemed like it was erasing anything it touched flashed, the last thing this body of the Great Ponderor saw being the skeletal structure of Bellerophon literally melting as his Outerversal Authority was erased as if it didn't even exist.

And then, this blast of white light covered him as thereafter...there was nothing!

Inside the far distance, Noah was ready to observe when his eyes squinted, and his figure turned into a flash of light as even from this far distance, he retreated away rapidly!

Seeing such a scene, Savitar was shocked as he scrambled away as if his very life was on the line!
