Chapter 3346 Disregard! II

The numbers of Towers Noah gained information about seemed to only be prevalent to entities at the Ordinance of Existence, and he didn't know how many of such towers there were for those who only attained the Eye of Existence, or those at Ordinance Amalgamation.

It was a System that was meant to have the strongest entities of the same state of Existence take the lead!

Naturally, there was only one tier of a Tresorverse Tower of Existence that Noah could go for.

After all, his type of Existence had just inherited such a level of Complexity.

It could only be Origin!

With steady hands, Noah moved his hand up as he tugged on the golden string.

An instant later, this string came down and wrapped around him as a golden robe was adorned on his body, and on his back- runic words stating [Origin Pass] were inscribed!

Out of what seemed like hundreds of Tresorverse Towers of Existence, many were White and Blue, with nine massive Purple Tresorverse Towers of Existence and three largest Gold Tresorverse Towers of Existence that were floating on the fringes of the stellar obsidian astral domain they were in.

Compared to these Towers that were the size of Omniverses, the tens of thousands of transcended entities here on the golden bark were truly like insignificant grains of sand.

As more and more entities pulled on the strings above them, they were divided and split based on their level of power as with the vastness of this space, those at the state of One With Existence were sent towards the leftmost side of this region, those at the Eye of Existence level coming after them, those at the Ordinance of Existence following, and then to the rightmost side of this domain, the few terrifyingly powerful entities at the Ordinance Amalgamation Stage could be seen.

There were less than a hundred of these terrifying entities as the Shield and Sword of the Commonwealth among other Ordinance Amalgamation Protectors were sent here- along with Arguros of the Antediluvian Leviathans.

The Leviathans...who made up the majority of the forces of Ordinance Amalgamation entities as the scattered Existences not belonging to any group followed.

The four different groups demarcated by different states of Existence were divided with the length of over 50 Omniverses between them- being equivalent to the distance of an entire Sector of the Megalos Ynnere!

They could not interfere with others.

And as far as anyone knew, unless there was an exception made for them...nobody could see Existence Severing Entities here!

It was hard to believe that there weren't any in the Megalos Ynnere.

But nobody truly knew as such existences were too distant. Too pristine!

So their focus remained on themselves at this moment.

After the demarcations made due to the States of Existence, Noah found himself in a region surrounded by a thin golden barrier, with a few hundred terrifying auras of Ordinance of Existence entities actually contained in this domain.

Gold!

All of them were competing for the Origin Tresorverse Towers of Existence- of which there were only three!

Outside of the thin golden barrier, thousands of entities at the Ordinance of Existence stage watched on with caution and interest- none of them having selected the golden string.

|The selection of the Origin Tresorverse Towers of Existence shall be carried out first.|

HUUUM!

Within the golden demarcated area, three dazzling golden thrones appeared in the distant space- a calm singularity floating atop each one!

|The Ones who can remain seated on the throne for the length of time it takes for the singularity above to disappear shall gain ownership of the Origin Tresorverse Towers of Existence.|

...!

Three thrones.

The powerful entities who had already transcended their existence all carried extremely deep records, history, and power as none made a move right away!

There were a few hundred of them here.

Each one at the Ordinance of Existence.

Whichever three were bold enough to try and claim the three golden thrones first...would become enemies of all others here as they had to defend for an unknown amount of time!

Just who would have such tremendous confidence in their power to be among the first ones to step forward?

As such a daring question arose, Noah's brows raised as among all Ordinance of Existence Entities here, two stepped out right away.

One was a cerulean Antediluvian Leviathan, his body vibrating with ecstatic power.

The other...was the Viceroy of the Commonwealth- the young leader of this power as behind him, there were close to 20 Protectors at the stage of Ordinance of Existence that followed behind him protectively!

He sought to take one of the three golden thrones while being protected by his forces!

His simian fur shone with a regal golden luster as he looked around all Ordinance of Existence entities here with a gaze full of majesty and power- his figure magisterially floating towards one of the thrones unhurriedly!

As if he was so confident in his power that he wasn't even worried that others here might shake him.

With a few steps, he arrived before the central golden throne and sat grandly- Protectors circling around him protectively as above the throne- the singularity began to burn!

On the other throne, the cerulean Antediluvian that had also stepped out arrived in no time, his body adorned with a golden robe similar to all others here taking a seat on the right throne with tremendous speed.

A flaming blue crown swirled above this Antediluvian Leviathan as he seemed like an ancient Emperor who could erupt with an unfathomable Ordinance at any moment- giving many here a sense of oppression!

Now, only a single throne was left.

Who would be bold enough to sit on it before chaos ensued?

Who-?

|Huh?|

Many gazes blinked as they only saw the golden robe of a devilishly handsome man step forward, his demeanor carefree and without any worries as in a few steps, arrived and sat on the leftmost throne.

After he sat, he stared at the throngs of Protectors, Antediluvian Leviathans, and other unique entities from across the Megalos Ynnere as he did not at all seem worried!

Who was that?

He dared to actually be among the first seated on one of the golden thrones?

Many eyes looked between his figure that they couldn't sense much threat of, then towards the seated Viceroy of the Commonwealth who was surrounded protectively by over 20 entities of the same stage, and then they gazed at the cerulean Antediluvian Leviathan who emanated extremely heavy and stifling complexity of existence.

And...their eyes returned back to Noah's visage as their expressions seemed sour, showing complete disregard.

Who was this complete nobody that actually dared to be among those seated up there?

His demeanor was filled with confidence as the dignity he carried seemed high enough, but so what?

Did he actually consider himself an unshakable power at the Ordinance of Existence stage?!
