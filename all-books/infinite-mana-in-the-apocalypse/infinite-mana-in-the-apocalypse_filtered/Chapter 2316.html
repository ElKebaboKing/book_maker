Chapter 2314 Destiny Makes a Move!

They could not do much else amidst such chaos!

It was just that he knew his Destiny lay elsewhere.

And...a critical time was actually coming as he became exceedingly calm.

His gaze remained on the cerulean Emperor in the distance a bit longer as he nodded, affirming the faint connection with his soul as soon after, the golden light of Destiny around him flickered.

His reason for being here was about to unfold.
