Chapter 3386 Appalling Complexity! VI

The weavings of all listening Antediluvian Leviathans began to tremble as they felt like they had arrived at a precipice.

The reason that the Omnifather gathered them all here today!

This multicolored being smiled as he looked around the Primaltree of Existence, gazing at the myriad of individual Antediluvian Leviathans as he posed a question.

|Runes...what do you all know about his concept?|

...!

Runes.

The moment he asked this, many Antediluvian Leviathans were confused while some Ordinance Amalgamation and Existence Severing Leviathans began to radiate a terrific light of brilliance.

Receiving no answer, the Omnifather continued.

|Runes...actually became a solution to the unsolvable problem of an uncrossable chasm. Over time, many came to learn that it was entirely a barrier of communication that existed between them and the Wheel of Existence, where no matter how far they traversed across the states of Existence, they still felt far away. The Wheel of Existence seemed to have an entirely different methodology of communication as, after eons, a few Great entities came to understand a type of language to breach that chasm and better communicate with the Wheel of Existence- Runes.|

WAA!

As he spoke.

His hand twirled as within it, a singularity filled with extreme heaviness bloomed.

It looked like the strokes of ancient letters coming together as just its appearance caused a stifling sensation to those in the surroundings!

|That is what Runes are- what they were discovered to be. A type of language. Something that made it easier to communicate and grasp the profound weavings that keep Existence flowing. They can seem small, but they are unique because they can compact a tremendous amount of weavings and complexity...into a single small letter. A Single Rune.|

...!

As Boros listened, the will within him was pulsing with brilliance as Noah thought about many things.

Runes.

The Transcendent Megalos Abilities designed by the Great Beyonder.

After one inherited the Transcendent Megalos Ability, a rune formed within their existence as any time they sought to activate it, they simply had to infuse complexity of existence into it!

This...was even what Noah had done to arrive in this Dream.

He had activated the Amarantos Dream of the Megalos by infusing terrifying droves of complexity of existence...into the AMARANTOS Rune.

Paired with the Severed Source of Existence of Kuklos and the terrifying Complexity of the Infiniverse Megalos Seed, he arrived here.

In this supposed dream.

In these different Weavings of Existence.

All from the utilization of a single Rune!

A single rune made many things possible. The Rune of Amarantos Dream of the Megalos made it possible to attain the Blood of a Pioneer by someone who was only in the Ordinance of Existence stage. And the utilization of this Blood to sever a part of himself!

A drop of blood...to sever a part of oneself.

Wait.

"..."

His weavings buzzed for a small moment of realization.

As the Omnifather showed the terrifying complexity of a single rune he conjured up, Noah took a moment to reflect on a single rune and the terrifying thing he had done due to what he obtained through it.

He had Severed a Small Part of himself.

A small part for him was a single concept- Kuklos.

A single Source of Existence.

This was small for him, and he had been able to Sever it with the drop of Blood of Pioneer that was somehow able to be brought back to him from Archimedes and cross unknown distances to enter his weavings of existence through the Amarantos Rune.

And then, a small part of him- a single Source of Existence which he held multiple of, was Severed due to it.

But this small part of himself...if Old Man Eudemus was put forth on his behalf, this was everything to him!

He only had the single Source of Existence of Kuklos as, at this moment, Noah had managed to sever it even before he underwent Ordinance Amalgamation with Kuklos.

He Severed it before his existence Amalgamated with it.

He hadn't even stopped to ask whether it was possible, and simply did it after taking the boon that was the Drop of Blood of a Pioneer that stemmed from a journey in other weavings of existence...initiated by a Rune.

A rune designed by the Great Beyonder!

|Being such an intricate language, Runes of Existence can be utilized to do a great deal of things, with one only truly able to speak this language and understand it themselves when their complexity gets close to Ordinance Amalgamation. And even then, if one seeks to design their own Rune for any unique purpose, they may only be able to design the lowest tier of a Rune of Existence- like the one in my hands. It is merely a type of a Terrestrial Rune of Existence.|

...!

The words of the Omnifather continued as he looked around and then pointed towards an Ordinance of Existence Antediluvian Leviathan who was looking towards him with awe.

|You. Strike against this broken Pillar of the Expanse with as much strength as you can.|

WAP!

The surroundings flickered as instantly, a massive gray stone-like pillar appeared and floated before their eyes. The Antediluvian Leviathan picked by the Omnifather adopted a solemn expression as his body was aflame with a crimson light of none other than the Ordinance of Destruction- and it struck out heavily while crossing Great distances to smash onto the broken Pillar of Existence as...

BZZT!

A single white scratch appeared.

...!

The eyes of the Antediluvian Leviathan trembled as in the distance, the Omnifather nodded.

|Mmm, sufficient. That can be considered a peak power of an Ordinance of Existence entity. Now, Destruction is not my Ordinance, nor is it a path I've been following. But...|

HUUM!

His hand tapped out once more.

And with the tap, utterly appalling waves of complexity of existence erupted as a blinding white singularity bloomed- its outline being hazy to many as it was a Rune!

|Even without truly understanding Destruction, if I can lay out a single rune that is able to communicate with it, I can command this Weaving of Existence to act on behalf of my complexity and...|

The White rune flashed with radiance as it instantly burst into crimson brilliance that shot towards the pillar that an Ordinance of Existence entity had only managed to leave a scratch on, and....

BOOM...CRACK!

The entire pillar was decimated as debris flew about everywhere.

|Runes.|

The voice of the Omnifather was heavy.

|If you can learn this advanced language. If you can begin communicating with the Wheel of Existence in a frequency actually acceptable towards it, you will come to find many things deemed impossible...a mere reality.|

...!
