Chapter 1311 - Not Worthy To Stand Up For! II

A Cosmic Resource Point!

Noah didn't look at the description of the [Resplendent Shards of a Cosmic Core] or the [Transcendental Runic Dao Line Enhancers], and he instead focused on this new concept within the passage of a mere millisecond that was akin to the outside world not even moving.

At this time, the battle in the Cosmic Battlefield was like it was paused as Noah's thoughts were moving so fast that he could loot at the loot he was receiving and formulate a myriad of grand plans without the passage of even a millisecond!

[Cosmic Resource Point] :: A Unique accumulation of essence in the form of an Authority. A single resource point allows one to gain the complete comprehension or assimilation of any Dao, or the advancement of a single percentage of any Minor or Greater Edicts. A single resource point can also be used to fully upgrade a single skill tree and all of the skills contained within to a numerical cap of 50/50 Resplendent Skill Points. Improbable and reality-bending skills can now be made from [Skill Creation] through the utilization of Cosmic Resource Point. If consumed, a resource point will grant +1,000,000% to All Parameters permanently. Current Cosmic Resource Points: 15

"..."

A fantastical new concept born from Skill Points being enhanced multiple times by Increased Loot Quantity and Rarity!

A single point could increase a percentage of an Edict or fully comprehend or assimilate any Dao, but Noah already knew he would be using the Cosmic Resource Points to get his Edicts to 100%- and he would be beginning with the only GREATER Nomological Edict he held. Then there was also the birth of new skills that would have been impossible to bring forth with mere skill points alone, and this was another avenue Noah had to explore.

Of course to strengthen himself even further, some Cosmic Resource Points would also be utilized on skill trees in the future to come. He only held 15 at this moment, but when every single enemy from the House of Stormdust fell, this number should shoot up many times over!

"And the two other Loot…" His gaze then went over to the Shards of a Cosmic Core and the Enhancers that had gained the moniker of [Transcendental]!

[Resplendent Shards of a Cosmic Core] :: A unique portion of a Cosmic Core born under special circumstances. The absorption of a single one can grant 10,000 normal Universes. Current Resplendent Shards of a Cosmic Core: 144,000.

[Transcendental Runic Dao Line Enhancers] :: The elevated version of the amalgamation of branded Runic Dao Lines that normally fade back into nature after the death of their owner, but the unique power of Noah Osmont has pulled upon this cycle of return and caused the formation of Dao Lines that can enhance the power of branded Daos to form. The Transcendental Dao Line Enhancers will elevate the base effect of a selected branded Runic Dao Line that stems from Lesser Daos by 60%, Grand Daos by 40%, and Cosmic Daos by 20%. Currently owned Transcendental Runic Dao Line Enhancers- 60,500.

Transcendental Enhancers that elevated his base percentage of Cosmic Dao Lines by 20%!

Then there were Shards of a Cosmic Core that with the death of a portion of the army from the House of Stormdust, Noah gained enough to obtain 1.4 Billion normal Universes!

Of course with the Duplication under RUINATION that was already beginning in Noah's origin, the actual result would come out to be close to 3 Billion normal Universes. And this…was just the beginning.

His thoughts moved swiftly as he ascertained everything he could, his focus fully returning back to the Cosmic Battlefield where his subordinates and summons in the form of the terrifying Primordial Centurions continued to constrict their circle around the army, Forefathers trying to fight back in a futile manner as more and more fell every millisecond!

Noah stared at all this as he sat upon his throne, everything proceeding as he had read about as his cold gaze locked onto the figure of Stormdust that was coming out of his stupor.

"Impossible…" 𝑓𝘳𝑒𝑒𝓌𝘦𝘣𝘯ℴ𝑣𝘦𝑙.𝘤𝑜𝑚

The words of this Family Head echoed out in a raspy voice as his Origin began to tremble in an unstable fashion!

"How can you…how can you have such a Destiny? What are you?!"

The scene Stormdust saw was shocking enough that it stopped him in track as thereafter…he only held disbelief as he could truly not understand why.

Try to cut Osmont's String of Destiny? That was practically suicide as it was even larger than the golden scissors he could utilize with <Destiny Cleavage>!

'No…' Stormdust's heart froze over amidst his confusion as his thoughts eerily turned crystal clear.

'My goal was Aegon. My aim was Aegon. He is the one I have to surpass! This existence before me…'

THRUM!

"Must die no matter what."

…!

His thoughts finished out loud as wrathful resonant words that reached the Throne of Tyranny that was far away.

His crimson hair waved slowly as his eyes attained a strong resolve even after he saw the unbelievable destiny of the enemy before him!

"If he cannot be defeated through Destiny, that he will be destroyed through pure force…is that what you are thinking?"

WAA!

The domineering voice of a Tyrant rang out in Stormdust's ears, Noah's words actually whispering what his own thoughts were as the eyes of the Family Head constricted in shock amidst their resolve!

"No need to forge another resolve or boost up your confidence. I passed your stage of power days ago as your mere half a billion Universes cannot even make me rise from my throne to take care of you."

…!

Not a threat or a promise, but a mere statement of the current Reality!

Stormdust gazed towards the being situated upon the Throne of Tyranny with blazing crimson eyes as his muddled mind intensified in power.

"The Family Head controlling over 1,000 Cosmos…reduced to a mere coward that cannot even move another step against his enemy."

The taunting words of Noah echoed in his ears over and over again as Stormdust's eyes fully turned red! He saw nothing but red within his eyes as deep within his Origin, the hidden blood provided by a terrifying existence began to leak out like an endless sea to fill his Origin!

After such a significant psychological damage with the Edict he believed in so much being nullified into nothingness as he saw the Destiny of his enemy be something that even a being like Aegon could not compare to, Noah's words were sharper and deadlier than anything that Stormdust could hear at this moment.

His heart and origin screamed out unfairness as a beam of white-gold light shot forth with him at the center, a unique aura permeating the surroundings as an unresigned bellow of frustration and disbelief echoed out grandly.

OOOOOH!
