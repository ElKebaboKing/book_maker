Chapter 1026 - Fine, I'll do it myself! I

A shocking conversation that landed on the ears of many powerful Hegemonies of the Primordial Cosmos was playing out, one that would be etched into the recesses of all their memories for the years to come!

"Well, after the first one, it gets easier."

WAA!

Noah's reply to Oathkeeper caused essence to grind to a halt in the Origins of the powerful Universal experts who heard it.

Not a single reply came towards Noah from the other side as those listening had the area around them shaking as they unconsciously released their terrifying auras!

"...You've birthed more than a single Dao before you established a Universe within your Origin?" Oathkeeper's voice was the only one to return as this powerful being kept searching for more information to understand this newly rising power within the Primordial Cosmos.

"Naturally, I couldn't stop at the first one after seeing how smoothly it went. The second one was even more expertly designed, but the third one...Ah!"

THRUM!

Noah felt an intense wave of essence release from the Wings of Primordial Destiny within his Expansive Space, a devilish smile appearing on his face as he continued.

"The third one is the glimmering white star you all can see above my head in the Necrotic Universe. The Dao of Conquest…"

…!

The angered and envious eyes of many hegemonies trembled as those that had arrived near the boundary of the Necrotic Universe honed in on the figure of Noah that had a rotating Star of Conquest above him!

A Paragon had actually gone ahead and birthed 3 Daos- something that should in no way be possible for a being at his rank to do, much less to do it three times! Even most Hegemonies birthed 1 Dao, or at most two if they had lived long enough and been just as lucky.

Yet this being…

They felt like they shouldn't listen to his words anymore or they would find themselves not capable of finding the will to live, many of them stopping their input of essence in the Wings of Primordial Destiny as they stopped listening!

"I created this Dao of Conquest for the occasion of the 9 Universes, maybe I'll design a fourth one when the time comes to tear down the Universal Constructs that Ambrose and the others are protecting…"

RUMBLE!

"..."

Even more Universal Hegemonies stopped inputting their essence into the Wings of Primordial Destiny as soon enough, only the Oathkeeper was left behind listening to Noah's ruthless words.

But to his words, nobody replied! At a certain point, Noah stopped his train of thought as he sent his voice into the Dao Treasure to see who was on the other side.

"Hello?"

"..."

"Is anyone still there?"

"..."

"Alright, I'll head back to the mission on hand."

"..."

THRUM!

A Paragon that could create their own Dao...what could they even call him?

Normally, when a Universal expert birthed their own Dao, they were referred to by that name as Valentina was known as the Hegemony of Summoning, or a being like Ambrose was known as the Hegemony of Slaughter.

But...they couldn't really term this Paragon the Hegemony of Conquest as he had not even reached the title of a Hegemony yet!

As for what his power would signify when he reached the Universal Realm? The many powerful Hegemonies were actually expectant, but at the same time they didn't hold too many feelings attached to Noah.

This was because they needed to solve the problem of Universal Amalgamation and whatever Chronos and the others were calling forth now. It was a time s?ns?t?v? issue! To become a Hegemony, it took the brightest geniuses over 150,000 years- and an example of this was Oathkeeper himself.

The youngest being to reach the stage of a Hegemony in history, that title was held by the glorious Oathkeeper!

For such a time s?ns?t?v? issue, could they really rely on a powerful Paragon that had limitless potential which could only be unlocked tens of thousands of years into the future? To them, the answer was no!

So while they looked at Noah as a unique existence to appear in their Primordial Cosmos, they still didn't think he would be a deciding factor for the challenges to come...

---

At this moment though, they could not even put their focus on him as something was happening within the Animus Universe!

It was...the attack of the Hegemony of Summoning that had returned into the Universe as one of the few beings who still had access to it as she was within its boundaries when the process of Universal Amalgamation began.

BOOM! 𝙛𝒓𝓮𝙚𝔀𝒆𝒃𝓷𝒐𝓿𝙚𝓵.𝙘𝒐𝒎

Valentina's face was that of utter coldness as a planetary sized phantom of herself struck down towards the somber figures of Ambrose and Solerno. Similar to before, hundreds of hands seemed to bloom from the body of this phantom as they all struck down towards the two Hegemonies, threatening to smash into the red barrier protecting the Universal Construct if they weren't stopped.

"Foolish woman!"

Ambrose bellowed out strongly as his Cosmic Essence waved around him, Solerno also moving with a grim expression as his body ballooned up to a planetary size as well.

But...similar to before, we knew the end result of this battle!

Against the enhanced Valentina that had her mana reserves expanded by over a hundred times, these two Hegemonies could not stand her! Last time, Chronos had appeared and saved the Universal Construct and the Hegemonies protecting it, Valentina running away before she fell into his grasp.

This time, a clone of Valentina was making the attack as she had no worry for her life, her hands ever closer to reaching the crimson barrier protecting the Universal Construct!

"Sigh."

An ancient sigh reverberated out from the Animus Universe.

The purple aura of a certain disastrous Hegemony bloomed.

But this time...Valentina didn't run.

Soon after, a brilliant white star began to shine brightly on top of her!
