Chapter 4477: Iskhus I

Noah watched as the grave trembled and pulsed with mounting intensity.

Immensity could be felt radiating from within!

So profound that moments later...

Prompts materialized before his vision.

|A tremendous accumulation of effort and countless failures that have been systematically cultivated across eons are finally reaching ripening stage.|

|Conditions for unprecedented manifestation have been satisfied.|

...!

Noah absorbed this information with eyes shining brilliantly.

Inside his existence, the Principle of Perpetual Harvest buzzed with sudden intensity, beginning to permeate its green-gold radiance across his entire framework as if resonating with what was occurring below.

Through that resonance, even more detailed information flooded into his consciousness.

Additional prompts emerged with profound implications.

|Effort and failure exist as inseparable companions across all cultivation paths.|

|No entity in Observable Existence has invested comparable quantities of sustained effort while experiencing proportionally equivalent systematic failure as The First Farmer.|

|His most recent failure involved deviating from his established path, attempting alternative approaches despite accumulated expertise suggesting otherwise.|

|And yet, even in this unprecedented effort at innovation... he failed absolutely.|

|The culmination of an existence defined by cycles of immense effort and continued failure has concluded in ultimate failure, the complete collapse of his existence itself.|

|But his effort persists across eons regardless of physical dissolution.|

|His failures persist across eons as accumulated weight.|

|Boundless and unfathomable effort now blooms paradoxically from complete failure.|

|This is the fruit that effort bears when invested without surrender across incomprehensible duration.|

BOOM!

From the grave below, a radiant seed erupted upward with explosive force!

It appeared as extremely pristine celestial seed, almost resembling cocoon in its perfectly symmetrical construction. It shone with terrifying green brilliance that illuminated the entire Sanctuary with concentrated authority.

Noah felt immense Complexity and Purity emanating from such object as he observed it spinning gloriously in the skies above the Sanctuary of The First Farmer.

As it rotated with hypnotic precision...

It stopped suddenly.

And sank back down into the ground with deliberate motion.

The vast earth of this endlessly expansive Sanctuary trembled violently. A moment later, something began emerging from the area that had previously been simple grave.

A tree.

An utterly massive tree that vibrated with the concentrated light of an incredible Principle!

When Noah examined it with calmness, he could perceive its fundamental nature clearly.

A Singularity Principle!

Prompts emerged with clarifying details.

|From endless effort and systematic failure, the weavings of The First Farmer bloom him once more into existence.|

|This represents unprecedented emergence through accumulated cultivation rather than external intervention.|

|The fruit of his effort manifests as transformation of his entire existence into living embodiment of what he pursued across eons.|

The emergence of this terrifyingly vast tree representing Singularity Principle continued with dramatic progression.

At the base of the massive trunk, bark composed of concentrated green authority covered with golden leaf patterns, a hand suddenly erupted outward!

A figure began emerging from the glorious tree itself as if being born directly from accumulated effort given physical form.

Fair hands emerged first, gradually followed by entire form of middle-aged man who pulled himself free from the tree’s embrace.

His physique was laid bare initially. His skin was covered with countless green Glyphs that appeared like intricate tattoos painting elaborate patterns across every visible surface.

His eyes...

His eyes were terrifying golden beams of concentrated light that gazed around with intensity that illuminated vast lands surrounding the Sanctuary.

Those eyes eventually landed on the figure floating above, on Noah who had been silently observing this entire emergence.

They paused on him.

Blinked once.

Noah looked back with completely calm expression that rapidly became stone-faced as he processed what he was witnessing.

The entity who had just emerged blinked again with apparent confusion before looking down at his own body.

He seemed to gradually recognize that it remained completely bare.

Noah’s cold-faced gaze conveyed unmistakable sentiment without requiring verbal articulation.

As if he was saying...

Dude. Why are you walking around with your junk hanging out?

In the next moment, The First Farmer waved his hand with a slightly embarrassed gesture.

Green robes materialized to cover his form, flowing garments that seemed woven from living plant matter and concentrated authority.

His figure began floating upward as he examined himself with expression mixing confusion and gradual comprehension of what had just occurred to his existence.

When he came to float directly before Noah, he simply looked at him.

Noah looked back with identical calm assessment.

The two did not speak. They simply analyzed each other with mutual recognition that words would be premature until proper evaluation concluded.

Noah’s eyes flashed as he systematically observed the specifications of the entity before him.

A status sheet materialized with comprehensive details.

|The First Farmer|

|— The Cultivator of Endless Cycles|

|— He Who Failed Forward|

|— The Persistent One|

|— Architect of Ten Thousand Unsuccessful Harvests|

|— The One Who Would Not Surrender|

|Civilization Affiliations: An Unknown Budding Civilization)|

|COMPLEXITY AND PURITY: 100 Sextillion (Exactly)|

|CORE PRINCIPLE: The Singularity Principle of Perpetual Effort|

|PRINCIPLE DESCRIPTION: This Singularity Principle represents the accumulated weight of effort invested across eons regardless of outcome. It does not require success to generate power, only sustained commitment to continuous cultivation. Each failure feeds this Principle as much as each success. The more effort invested, the more powerful the wielder becomes regardless of whether that effort produces desired results.|

|UNIQUE CAPABILITIES:|

|— Effort Conversion: All energy invested toward any objective, successful or failed, converts to accumulated power|

|— Failure Immunity: Cannot be permanently collapsed as long as effort was genuinely invested|

|— Resurrection Through Persistence: Will manifest again after any collapse through accumulated effort across existence| 𝒇𝙧𝙚𝓮𝙬𝙚𝓫𝒏𝓸𝓿𝓮𝒍.𝓬𝙤𝓶

|— Unfathomable Endurance: Cannot be discouraged, demoralized, or convinced to surrender regardless of circumstances|

| This entity has undergone transformation from standard existence into living embodiment of his cultivated Principle. He IS Perpetual Effort given conscious form.|

...!

Noah absorbed these specifications with his expression remaining deliberately neutral.

100 Sextillion Complexity and Purity!

A Singularity Principle born from accumulated failure!

And as Noah looked at the Principle he embodied...

’Is this fucker just copying and pasting?’

...!

The details his new Principle, Perpetual Effort, were very much similar to Noah’s Principle of Perpetual Harvest!

But at the end of the day, even Perpetual Harvest stemmed from The First Farmer...

The First Farmer continued floating there, golden eyes examining Noah with equal analytical focus as if performing identical assessment.
