Chapter 4216: Weavings of Power! V

The thousand Noahs folded back into one, a profound, almost chilling understanding settling into his mind.

This was the power of Quantum. Not just to be in many places, but to choose which reality would be the real one.

As he marveled at the terrifying effectiveness of this new methodology, the battle around him began to shift.

The great turtle, its nine heads now blazing with the amplified authority of Noah’s Haki and Sigrid’s Order, moved with a new, terrible purpose.

Beams of pure Origin, now focused and precise, lanced out across the Fold, striking not only at The Dead, but at the Tears themselves with concentration.

Where the beams hit, reality was rebooted.

The space was returned to its original, pristine state, the memory of the Tear and the death it had brought completely erased!

The arguments between the factions died down, replaced by a grim, unified focus.

Legions of hundreds of thousands of Living Origins, Laws, Quantums, and others, whose individual complexities were less than a trillion, began to work in concert. 𝐟𝕣𝕖𝐞𝐰𝕖𝚋𝐧𝗼𝚟𝐞𝕝.𝗰𝐨𝐦

They moved not to the front lines, but to the skeletal remains of Concordia. A great, somber quiet fell over them as they began the heartbreaking work of clearing the bodies of their fallen comrades, of cleansing the ground that had been the home of Origins and a few other Living Existences!

It was a scene of profound, collective grief, a reminder that even for them, the cost of this war was measured in friends and family lost.

Noah watched this for a moment, then his focus shifted. He had seen what Existential Artifice could do in battle. But what of its other applications?

His gaze flickered to the Early Veiled Shore.

He stood upon the golden sands of the Radiant Shore, the air here clean and sweet with the scent of impossible growth.

His main body, which had been observing all these events from afar, now became the center of his focus.

He looked out at the vast, beautiful expanse of his domain. The herbs were maturing, the trees were heavy with fruit, the Aquarium teemed with life. It was a paradise, but it could be more!

He began to test the Weaves, not as weapons, but as tools of wonder.

He turned to a plot where the Transcendent Veilmoss was growing, its fronds shimmering in and out of existence. He ignited the Crucible of the Protagonist.

Plot Advancement, he wove, his will a command to the very story of the plant.

Skip the tedious growth phase. We’re moving to the harvest.

|You have exerted the Weave of Narrative Acceleration.|

|The Transcendent Veilmoss has decided its character arc has been fulfilled. It is ready for its dramatic conclusion.|

|Maturation: INSTANTANEOUS.|

The moss flared with a brilliant, ethereal light, its growth cycle completing in a single, glorious instant!

It was ready.

Next, he turned his gaze to the Leviathan’s Eternum Cage. The Aquarium was a marvel, but its collection was still limited to the Eminent-level creatures it had managed to ensnare. He wanted more. He wanted... Kleos.

He ignited the Crucible of Life, and the Crucible of Spirit.

Sanctuary’s Call, he wove, a complex, two-part harmony of authority. He did not bait the waters with his blood; he baited them with a concept.

He wove the very idea of a perfect, ultimate sanctuary, a place of endless food, absolute safety, and profound peace, and cast it like a fishing line across the dimensional boundaries!

|You have cast the Weave of Irresistible Sanctuary.|

|This conceptual bait is now propagating across adjacent Folds, whispering promises of paradise to all Marine Life.|

|High-level entities are notoriously cautious, but even they are not immune to the promise of a perfect home.|

|A Kleos Early Living Marine Lifeform has taken the bait.|

|Incoming...|

A golden fishing line, one of the Cage’s own constructs, snapped taut with a force that made the entire Shore hum.

Something vast and powerful was being hauled across the dimensions, something that had heard his call and found it too compelling to resist.

He smiled, a deep, profound satisfaction filling him!

But his work was not done. His gaze settled on the Seed of the Principle of Perpetual Harvest.

It was a source of unbelievable power, but he knew it could be more.

He ignited...the Crucible of Infinity.

THUMP!

His Heart beat heavily. The Ascendran Nodule beat heavily!

This would take more than a gentle Weave; it would require a torrent of his power, a great and terrible cost!

His existence blazed, hundreds of trillions of Complexity and Purity pouring from his Heart into this single, monumental effort.

He was not just accelerating the Seed’s growth; he was using the Weave of Expansion to multiply the very effort the Seed was already exerting, creating a recursive loop of exponential maturation!

HUUM!

His body trembled under the strain, the Shore itself resonating with the sheer, overwhelming power he was unleashing. He felt a deep, conceptual exhaustion, a weariness that went beyond the physical.

His Heart, that raging furnace of Tyranny, began to ache, a warning that even its power had limits.

But he pushed.

|You are performing an act of monumental, reckless effort.|

|You are attempting to force the maturation of a Principle.|

|[The Weave of Infinite Effort Multiplication] is active.|

|The Seed of the Principle of Perpetual Harvest is... receptive.|

|Maturity: 25%... 35%... 48%... 50%!|

BOOM!

A wave of verdant, golden light exploded from the Seed, washing over the entire Shore, over the entire Infiniverse!

It was a light so rich, so full of the promise of endless bounty, that it felt like being drowned in pure, liquid hope.

Noah staggered, his body finally reaching its limit. He came to a halt, a deep, profound exhaustion settling into his very Existences!

His Heart needed to rest.

But as he stood there, catching his breath, his Infinite Mana surging to replenish and reenergize his Complexity and Purity used as fuel... the prompts bloomed, and they were worth the cost.

|Every Weave cast generates a harvest. The more complex the Weave, the richer the yield.|

|Total Harvest from recent efforts: +250 Trillion Complexity and Purity.|

|[The Seed of the Principle of Perpetual Harvest] has reached 50% Maturity.|

|For a short period of time, the Seed will be resistant to further forced elevation. It must now grow naturally.|

|At 50% Maturity, your connection to the Principle has deepened. You are now capable of forging up to 50 Seed Glyphs of Perpetual Harvest.|

|Cost per Glyph has been elevated to: 100 Trillion Complexity and Purity.|

|Effect per Glyph: The effects of all Weaves and Endowments are now amplified by an unknown factor. Could be 10, could be 100.|

WAA!

He observed and analyzed these terrifying effects, a slow, devilish smile spreading across his face.

Had he...become unfair enough yet?!

The cost was immense, but the returns were astronomical. And as he stood there, basking in the afterglow of his achievement, he took a moment to observe his own, transformed existence.

|Noah Osmont – The Early Creature, Osmont (Age 1)|

|Complexity: (1.8 Quadrillion)|

|Purity: (1.8 Quadrillion)|

|Systems of Power: Early Atlas of the Folds, Infinite Mana, Glyphs, Principles, Existential Artifice (The Weavings of Power)...|

|Distinctions: The Early Creature, Osmont; True Emperor of Quintessence, Tyranny, Infinity; The Living Existential Guider; Master Chef of the Verdant Eternia Tribe...|

|Heart: The Heart of Tyranny (Fourth Part: The Ascendran Nodule – 27% Complete)|

|Principle Access: Perpetual Harvest (50% Matured Seed|

...!

He had gloriously done it.

He had taken a battlefield that Schrodinger likely initiated and turned it into a forge.

He had taken a moment of crisis and turned it into a crucible for his own evolution!

The path ahead was still long, the threat of the Fallout still loomed, but for the first time in a while, he felt like he was returning to a level of terrifying magisterial control and power in a field where he had been weaker than many!
