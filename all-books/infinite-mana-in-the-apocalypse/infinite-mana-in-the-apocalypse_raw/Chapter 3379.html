Chapter 3377 A Dream of a Different Tune! I

The Great Segregator.

An Existence Severing Eldritch entity in the form of a massive flower.

Currently, the figures of the Great Mother and Ozymandias were within her unique Severed Domain as she had many musings flowing through her.

Many had always paid a great cost to utilize her Severed Domain, with this space being something she uniquely discovered years after she Severed her own existence to be free and unfettered.

After devouring a few entities into this space, an Ordinance Amalgamation creature that struggled and tried to make a breakthrough before his weavings were ground down and collapsed, and this entity was shocked to find that his progress that always seemed stalled and heavy became light and free-flowing!

The last bouts of complexity that evaded him before he could sever his own existence seemed to be coming to him rapidly as he began to increase his complexity, seeking to sever his existence and break out of the Domain of the Great Segregator.

When this entity did this, the Great Segregator discovered her own slow ebb of weavings beginning to move- as if catalyzed by the new weavings advancing within her.

Even though they weren't as complex as hers, the variations and uniqueness in weavings allowed her own to experience a significant advancement.

But of course, she couldn't have an Ordinance Amalgamation entity actually sever his existence with her, no matter if he would be successful or not, so this being who allowed her to initially see the usage of her Severed Domain was decimated by her moments later as he never stood a chance.

Since then, she had made her weavings known as for a cost, genius Outerversal Morphons could step into her Domain and cultivate!

She made gains from it in complexity, and made even further gains once a cost was made for use.

But out of all beings to utilize her Severed Domain so far, only three were truly memorable to her for the extent of advancement they provided.

The first entity who almost Severed his existence.

A Great One that had risen cycles ago whose name was now feared across the Megalos Beyond- the Great Ruler.

And an Outerversal Morphon who advanced from attaining their Eyes of the Megalos all the way to Ordinance Amalgamation in a single year.

A year.

Such an event had caused a shocking commotion to the Primal Eldritch Palace of the Expanse, with that Entity having long exceeded the Great Segregator in complexity as he had even Pioneered his own path!

A legend among Outerversal Morphons.

The Great Nexus.

These three beings were the main memorable ones as the Great Nexus cultivating within her Severed Domain had allowed her to cement her position as an Existence Severing expert- very few Outerversal Morphons being able to shake her within this same stage. 𝒇𝙧𝙚𝓮𝔀𝓮𝒃𝙣𝓸𝒗𝒆𝒍.𝙘𝒐𝒎

After all these cycles, she could tell the moment an entity started increasing their complexity whether they would provide a significant enough boon for her, or whether it would be minimal.

Her own Severed Domain would show a tell purely depending on how much complexity and the degree of freedom an entity gained after escaping the outside weavings of the Expanse or Megalos Beyond. If her Severed Domain vibrated, she was always in a good mood as this meant she could expect a somewhat significant boost in her own complexity.

If an obsidian radiance of the megalos shone within it, even more so!

The Great Nexus had even shown the Manifestation of the Primordial Mountain of the Expanse the moment any sense of shackles were unbound from him as the Great Segregator had made tremendous gains then.

So…what would the supposed Instrument of the Beyonder bring?

'If half of the things they've said about him are true, then I should make suffic-'

RIIP!

A massive, interminable tear instantly bloomed and caused her Severed Domain to vibrate and tumble chaotically.

|Ah….!|

Her intent began to rise in incredulity and confusion as above Ozymandias, a rune of Amarantos flashed with shocking radiance before it released Shockwaves of brilliance- a phenomenal reaction occurring as the simple reality that this rune was activating in a space free from the weavings of existence of the Expanse and Megalos seemed to have triggered a shocking reaction!

Free from the weavings of existence. Free...from the weavings of the Wheel, by a certain extent.

A dream was initiated in such an environment.

And the Great Segregator did not know this, but for the initiation of this dream, the terrifying complexity of an Infiniverse Megalos Seed quite a distance from here was allowed to flow freely to power up this dream!

The complexity of something that had never before emerged.

The ability passed down from the Great Beyonder.

The unique environment brought forth by the Great Segregator.

And Ozymandias himself.

Multiple factors came together to produce an unimaginable result as above Ozymandias, the rune of Amarantos blared with heavy radiance- this entire space fluctuating as stellar obsidian weavings of the Megalos Beyond unfurled around it!

Below, a massive obsidian Primordial Mountain of the Expanse bloomed as above, a Primordial Hearthraes Lotus Primaltree emerged and gushed down waves of complexity!

But this wasn't all.

From all around, illusory golden strings of the Expanse itself began to manifest.

Uncountable in number, brilliant in their Manifestation.

Strings of Existence. Strings of the Expanse!

A terrific convergence and recipe for disaster seemed to be blooming here as the rip that had bloomed within this Severed Domain became even more massive, as if it wasn't meant to isolate and sever such a shocking degree of complexity as it was almost collapsing!

Shocked and infuriated, the Great Segregator bellowed out as terrifying droves of her own unique Severed Existence erupted out, flowing voraciously into the Severed Domain and holding it steady as if it collapsed, a large part of herself would collapse as well.

Steadiness came into the domain as she looked with rising rage and incredulity within, wondering if this was a plot from the Great Mother to kill her in their very own Blancverse!
