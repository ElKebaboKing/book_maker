Chapter 1790 The Unnamed Kainos Emperor Gazes Upon the Masses! ll

Ezekiel was actually looked down upon as Helen seemed lost in her memories when she spoke of him!

"Mmm…" Juliette nodded after a while as her eyes moved onto the brown-skinned beauty that was Nefertiti.

"This one is actually interesting as she's the sister of that woman who actually turned down the invitation of a Potentate of her Bloodline from the Main Reality-...oh?"

Her words stopped as she turned towards the direction of the Pure-Blooded Cardinal Royal Human base at this moment.

From the gaps of the protection of LEGENDS, a purple gold ship gradually neared as it held swirling golden waves of destiny that most beings proficient in Destiny and Fate could see!

A cluster of such dense destiny was paid attention to by the monstrous beings here as another region that was like this was near the entourage of the Young Emperor.

A smile rose in Juliette's eyes as she saw this.

"Speak of the devil…"

…!

All entering vessels that neared the Domain Rift had to pass the tight defenses of the LEGENDS overseeing everything, and at this moment- the vessel they was the Purple Endeavor appeared.

OOOM!

It was subtle.

But the atmosphere changed!

The fee Chosen Emperors and monstrous geniuses building their foundation couldn't help but gaze towards the Purple Endeavor and see just who was on it as their destinies vibrated and reacted in that direction!

Juliette's eyes sparkled with a stellar light as she watched this TABOO Vessel anchor, figures beginning to rise from it that were led by the small visage of a smiling Brightborn towards the cluster of Royal Humans.

4 Candidate Emperors whose bodies screamed of wildness that Brightborn had picked up, the regal visage of Genevieve whose aura stood above even her sibling Namur, and then behind them and floating soundlessly alone…the visage of a silver white haired beauty whose crown glimmered with the word .

A word more robust than many more beings here as even the four Alpha Strata LEGENDs only had swirling around their crowns as they didn't have anything attached to this!

"Sacralized? Are you kidding me?"

Even Juliette was stumped at this as her figure bubbled with power, seemingly wanting to fly over in that direction even now!

"I mean a guy is good but…" She tapped her chin as a devilish smile appeared on her exceedingly beautiful face.

"...maybe I can also see if I can subjugate her to be a hubby under my heel."

WAA!

Dreadful words echoed from her as Helen shook her head again on the Lotus Flower TABOO Vessel, floating a few more emitters away from Juliette whose body was releasing a terrifying air at this moment!

"Eh?" Yet the eyes of this being briefly stopped as they locked on to another being coincidentally.

He wasn't moving with pride and mixing with etiquette among the others, with his gaze being sharp as his eyes seemed to be brightening up as he gazed at everything around him!

Over the vast Domains of the Pure-Blooded Bloodlines. Over geniuses and LEGENDS as the look in his eyes…didn't show any apprehension or wonder within them as Juliette could swear she could see something akin to hunger!

But she shook her head and got back to staring at Katelyn as which being would gaze upon the Scions of the Pure-Blooded Bloodlines like this when they themselves didn't even have a solidified crown?

—

Noah stepped off the Purple Endeavor as his eyes took in the vast wonders around him.

Dense auras of powerful LEGENDS and existences with astounding Destiny permeated throughout- Noah getting a chance to meet a section of the few exceptional individuals across these vast Realities!

His main body back in the Cosmic Holy Land had pulled out a glimmering golden book as he utilized the Dictum Emperor to try and peer into what could possibly happen with the tightly coiling destinies of all beings here- but it was very hard to get back any information as he prepared to let this go and begin focusing on the Third Infinite Dream that would grant him yet another edge in this Unexplored Grotto Sanctum.

His Clones had entered the Dream Dimension as they sought to find Dream LEGENDS and Nightmares, while another one of his clones with RUINATION in the Mirror Dimension was finally making headway as the eyes of the Gazer had grasped onto what he might need across the hard to navigate spaces filled with endless Mirrors.

And now, this clone of Alexander had arrived at the pulsing Domain Rift of the Unexplored Grotto Sanctum as Noah couldn't help but greedily gaze at all the existences here, trying to contain himself as best as he could as the authority of the Unnamed Kainos Emperor was crying out with fervor!

OOOM!

His three Aspects of Existence that were filled to the brim vibrated intensely as his destiny could actually feel a level of danger from a few spots around this reality sized Domain Rift, such a Reality not making him sullen as it actually made him more excited.

The Realities were vast, and he had still only been moving for just over a year. The true geniuses and anomalies that rose and lived for millions or billions of years…it would be disappointing and unrealistic if there weren't a few that were utterly exceptional in their Realm!

He could feel the auras of such beings here as even with the current level of power as a Triple Limit Breaker he had achieved…

'I might still have to break some sweat.'

WAA!

Work. It seemed Noah truly had to put on work this time around as his eyes were dazzled by the many TABOO Vessels stretching out across the 5 mini domains made by the Pure-Blooded Bloodlines, and he was dazzled more than anything by the massive figures of 5 LEGENDS that represented the Bloodlines here today releasing undeniable waves of power.

When Noah merely compared his current self to them…he felt like a mere palm from these beings would eradicate all Aspects of Existence of his Clone with the damage even extending towards his main body if he were to ever face them! Their power was currently too high as this may be the case now…

'But it won't be so in just a few months…or maybe even weeks!'

…!

His will swept out silently and madly as he made this resolution, walking towards the forefront of the Legions of Royal Humans as Genevieve and Emperor Namur went to mix and mingle with the Royal Humans from the Main Reality. Adjudicator Katelyn remained behind with one of the Adjudicator Legions that normally followed her as those invited by Brightborn and many others in the surroundings began to spare glances at her- especially the crown spinning above her head that read SACRILIZED.

She seemed to stand above many beings even here as for now, Noah didn't make any moves to stand out or cause any eyes to land on him.

He waited for the entrance into the Grotto Sanctum to fully unfurl his wings and let loose.

He waited until he was locked in a room with a few of the geniuses of these vast Realities as there…the battle for supremacy would show who excelled among the rest!

Why could he feel a level of danger from a few beings that he could only gaze at and not spread his will over at this moment? How many Limit Breakers were there and what oddities of these vast Realities had they attained?!

Noah wanted the answers to all these questions as his gaze spread over the majestic Royal Humans, Reality Ursidae, Infinite Willow Trees, Dragon Phoenixes, and the shimmering Astral Bloodline that uniquely looked very close to his Samsara Dao Origin forms.

His gaze was just about to travel towards one of the areas where he felt a level of danger- an area where a certain entourage was calmly talking amongst each other while floating behind a Young Emperor when…

HOOONG!

A blaring horn sounded as a flash of golden light actually bloomed at the very center of the temporary domains set up by the 5 Pure-Blooded Bloodlines- a light so fast that many beings only saw it when the existence behind it formed instantly before their eyes!

Before the eyes of many LEGENDS and powerhouses, an existence actually appeared with a flash of golden light as one could shockingly see a pair of purple gold wings extending from her feet, an even more pair of radiant gold wings stretching from her back as she was adorned in a sleek battle armor.

From this existence…a maddeningly sonorous commanding voice echoed out for all to hear- without even an ounce of fear!

"Why are we still tarrying around here? Let us enter!"

WAA!

With an unblemished visage and valor, an existence with no sign of a crown on her head appeared as destiny began to weave madly in the surroundings, Noah's ears picking up a few voices of beings from the Main Reality who spoke with shock when gazing at the new entrant.

"Akaris!"

…!
