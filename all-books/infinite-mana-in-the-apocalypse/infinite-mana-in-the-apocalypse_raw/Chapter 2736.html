Chapter 2734 An Unchangeable Destiny! III

2734 An Unchangeable Destiny! III

Not so early.

Not yet!

These were the words she uttered as they could hold any myriad of meanings, but at this moment, the erupting singularity caused her expression to shift from shock to sorrow, to unwillingness, and ultimately indescribable resignation!

The singularity of Vacuous Hyperversal Authority only became more potent and immense as in front of her, Noah rose with a serious expression.

She stared towards him with a gaze of desolation as she clutched her chest and continued.

[I thought and calculated that I had more time. I thought I could alter my fate. I sent my main body into the Supernal Havens of Extremity to seek something to change my fate. To change my destiny…but it seems that for some of us, there always exists an Unchangeable Destiny.]

…!

Around her figure, a blaze of obsidian flames of Vacuous Hyperversal Authority rose as the Singularity on her chest caused it to cave in before it began to split wide open.

The unique Havenly Authority of Dissolution was shockingly then pulled into the Singularity on her chest as an unfathomable resonance bloomed!

Seeing this scene, her gaze became even more forlorn as she shook her head in desolation!

[In life, some Existences are given the opportunity to make their own choices. To choose how they want to live their lives. To pursue what makes them happy. Some of us…never had that option. And it seems that no matter how we try to change it…]

Her last words were left unsaid.

An abstruse phenomenon bloomed around her as in front of her, Noah's eyes that now burned with his Extremity Soul could see many things, and even he was astonished at what he was observing as he saw the Extremity Clone of Svetlana become anchored in space, the Singularity on her chest actually mixing with Dissolution before it began to split open- her very Lifeforce and Extremity Soul Values being used as ingredients for a shocking reaction!

At this scene, Oraculum buzzed in Noah's mind as it actually wasn't able to light up any paths for him to follow regarding this.

There was one possible flickering path that rose and fell as if even after pursuing it, the results would simply be unknown.

[I isolated it to my Extremity Clone in case it was ever activated, but my Main Body will follow soon enough to keep it steady and active.]

Whatever was occurring was becoming more and more irreversible as she spoke, her gaze coming to lock on Noah at this moment as she smiled with despondency and unbound unwillingness.

[You know, your eyes are truly similar to my Master.]

WAA!

[You gaze at me now as I'm sure you are thinking and discerning what you can do, but behind your eyes…I can see a level of apathy. You don't really care what happens to me…so I don't know why I even shared my woes with you. The most I see is you wishing to pay back the karma for the little help I've given you…but at the end of the day, I am nothing to you. Just like the Master had said over and over again, I will never be anything to anyone. Just a tool…

just a tool…]

As she said her last words, obsidian tears fell from her wide eyes that were becoming unfocused, Noah observing an intrusive foreign authority rising and encroaching upon her very soul as the Adjudicator of Dissolution was put in a numb state of existence!

Seeing this development, Noah's gaze became stern as with shining eyes, his figure shot forward at this moment as he stepped on the only observable path whose results remained unknown!

As he moved, his hands reached out as his palm split open, serene and pure waves of his Havenly Quintessential Hyperversal Emperor Lineage Blood flowing out freely.

Not just a contained Blood Source.

But waves of his Blood!

If there was a foreign interference seeking to influence and usher forth something from Svetlana, he would see if his own interference could counter this and what result could be ushered forth.

But even as he did this, the reaction already initiated was unstoppable as after achieving a set level, the Singularity that was splitting her chest open entirely exploded as a brilliant light shot above them.

A light…that formed into a serene illusory bridge.

Yes- a bridge!

Its structure pulsed and was formed with Dissolution layered over Vacuous Hyperversal Authority, its mere appearance causing roaring booms to erupt in the surroundings as the very Haven itself seemed to reject this abomination.

But the mixture of Dissolution held the rejection at bay as thereafter, in the span of nothingness above them…a brilliant white light began to shine as another illusory bridge was seemingly reflected opposite to the obsidian one!

One bridge was anchoring itself within the Nether Haven of Dissolution, while another bridge was anchored elsewhere.

At this moment, these two bridges were brought together as Noah's will buzzed while taking in all of this information, many records being unveiled before his eyes as what he saw was quantified and stated!

[Havenly Bridge of Dissolution]

This was the distinction of the illusory Bridge that stemmed from the split chest of the Adjudicator of Dissolution, and the milky white bridge that was now burning with hellish white flames…

[Infernal Bridge of Anarchos]

…!

Anarchos.

An Infernal Authority from the Vacuous Hyperversal Domains!

Noah had observed Acheron, Perdition, and now he was gazing at the flaming white authority of Anarchos that represented an Infernal Haven within the Vitalis Domains that was bathed in hellish white flames.

The moment it appeared, the Extremity Soul Values and Extremity Vitality Values from the Extremity Clone of Svetlana became drained at a shocking speed as these two bridges pulsed, gradually turning from illusory to becoming real and filled with structure.

And the moment they gained even a semblance of stability…these two bridges actually had tendrils of Vacuous Hyperversal Authority connect them- the Infernal Bridge of Anarchos seemingly in its own spacetime continuum while the Havenly Bridge of Dissolution was within the Haven of Dissolution.

And at that moment…

HOONG!

A resonant horn boomed out as from the ends of the Infernal Bridge of Anarchos bathed in white flames…the aura of Vacuous Hyperversal Extremities rose as on the very end of the bridge, Noah's will saw Legions upon legions of Vacuous Malignancies stepping into the Bridge and making their way over.

…!
