Chapter 5170: Reign VI

Time seemingly became stretched toward infinity and slowed down at this moment, the falling dust of the mountain freezing in mid-air and the roar of the distant Wyld fading into a singular, pressurized point of absolute stillness.

The holographic image to appear within Emotive was actually Ruination, with her manifesting with an urgency that stripped away her usual detachment, her eyes reflecting the swirling chaos of Emotive’s interior world as she gazed at the manic being’s consciousness.

|We do not have much time.|

|The Master pushed himself a bit far and entered a temporary state of Absolute Infinity that needs a concentrated ignition of raw emotional resonance to stabilize and return him to his old self. Other options have become nonviable, and maybe the most chaotic, intense weavings of emotions in all of Observable Existence may be enough to allow Master to regain control. I can take whatever you give and infuse it directly within him, allowing for a temporary instant of Infinity where he can anchor himself back to who he was. For his sake... what will you give? He would need everything you can give as his Infinity is currently too encompassing. What... can you give?|

Emotive was shocked as the magnitude of the situation settled into her mind, the realization that her Fixation’s "boundless" state was actually a dangerous imbalance that threatened to consume the man she adored, and because she was told there was no time, her decision was already made in an instant that required no deliberation!

She trembled with a sudden, violent conviction as her manifestation within her own body reached out and plunged her hand into her chest, the illusory flesh parting without resistance as she grasped the very core of her being.

She pulled out a heart burning with a blinding, kaleidoscopic emotion that defied classification, the organ pulsing with the concentrated intensity of every joy, every rage, and every obsessive fixation she had ever siphoned across her long existence.

Tears began to fall from her face in heavy, translucent tracks as she smiled with a heartbreaking mixture of sorrow and triumph, looking at the holographic image of Ruination with eyes that had finally found their singular purpose.

"For him, I give my Everything," she whispered, her voice cracking under the weight of the gift.

HUUM!

"All my emotions, all my intensity, all the chaos I have ever claimed... I give my very Heart."

BOOM!

Boundless emotions converged in the pulsing multicolored heart, the organ expanding and contracting with a frequency that threatened to shatter the mental space they occupied. Ruination looked at Emotive’s smiling, teary face, seeing the terrifying conviction of a manic being, and she reached out to grasp the heart with hands that shone with a blinding, white-gold light of Hadean authority.

Ruination bellowed out a command that shook the folded dimensions of their connection, waves of Infinity cascading around her in violent, recursive loops as she utilized her intrinsic link with her Master to open a channel that should not have existed.

She prepared to infuse the most potent and boundless emotions from a single wild being in all of Observable Existence, a tide of raw, unadulterated feeling that acted as a searing brand against the cold perfection of Noah’s Absolute state, flooding into her Master with the intent to drown the Infinite in the return of the Finite!

BOOM!

An explosion of utmost brilliance and incandescence exploded out right after, the light of glory tearing through the silence of THE Wyld and the hidden depths of THE Infiniverse simultaneously, as the heart of a manic being was offered up to save the soul of a King. Of a Tyrannical Emperor!

HUUM!

---

It felt like a truly long incandescent dream that he was actively participating in, a state of existence where the boundaries between his will and the fundamental forces of the existence had dissolved into a singular, shimmering continuity.

He could not quite explain the sensation with the vocabulary of his previous experiences, for while he was still himself in the most foundational sense of his identity, he felt as though there was a corrective weaving applied to his thoughts and actions that dictated every movement with a terrifying, glorious efficiency.

In this state, he had operated without the interference of emotions, his decisions bypassing the messy filtration of sentiment to achieve the most optimal outcomes across every theater of his influence.

The rapid completion of THE Infinite Calabi-Yau Manifolds, the intricate design of the new Miniature Causes that now accelerated his Infiniverse, the clinical dismissal of his mother’s and THE Infiniverse’s concerns, and even his ongoing, aggressive restructuring of Ubergulden Adelheid’s very nature had all been executed with the cold grace of a calculated choices.

In this influx state of Infinity, he had carried out actions that he would normally hesitate to perform, yet throughout the entire process, a small, receding part of his consciousness had told itself that he could simply leave this state whenever he wished, that the transition was a tool rather than a destination.

But now, as he attempted to reach for the familiar anchors of his finite self, that certainty began to waver under the sheer volume of the Absolute.

’Now, that’s not too good...’

The thought echoed through his mind with a dry, detached cadence, for the boundless Infinity permeating within him was making his own thoughts not want to return to the limitations of the past.

Because why should he?

At this moment, his own Quintessence Infiniforce was actively beginning a process that felt as patient as it was inevitable, a transformation that sought to rewrite everything about him from a finite being into something else entirely...a being whose scale was so vast that the concept of an individual "Noah" became a redundant rounding error.

He had theorized this possibility, understanding that a finite being did not possess the same worries or priorities as an infinite being, and that they were fundamentally different configurations of existence that could not coexist without one eventually subsuming the other.

He realized with a chilling clarity that as long as he did not let go of his finite self, he would never truly live up to the full potential of Infinity, yet if he did let go, the man who had started this journey would be lost to the brilliance of the Absolute; in his hands, Infinity would always be limited by his refusal to disappear.

He sighed within the silence of his crystalline mind, the sound a faint ripple in a sea of almost perfection, and he prepared to pull the biggest contingency he had placed upon himself to shatter this state of apathy before it became permanent, when all of a sudden...

BOOM!

He shockingly felt an influx of unfathomably intense, manic emotions surge from his connection with Infinity, a tide of raw, unadulterated chaos that defied the clinical geometry of his current thoughts.

These emotions were so ridiculously intense and crazy, so filled with a singular, obsessive intensity, that the blinding calm and the near-perfection of his Absolute state were suddenly, violently overwhelmed!

The cold efficiency of his mind buckled under the weight of a manic emotions, and Noah did not hesitate, his Quintessential command erupting with the full force of his remaining will as he seized the surge of feeling!

He moved tyrannically, acting with the predatory precision of a king reclaiming a stolen throne, as he channeled the entire flood of manic resonance and infinity directly toward his Hadean Organ of Civilization, using the heat of those emotions to burn through the glazed crystalline shell that had formed around his consciousness!

HUUM!

The process was boundlessly glorious, the internal collision of Absolute Infinity and absolute emotion producing a resonance that shook the very foundation of his internal reality, forcing the dimensions to stabilize and the clinical apathy to recede like a tide before a rising sun.

He took a crystalline core of this apathy and sealed it within his Hadean Organ of Civilization!

Moments later, his body seated at the core of THE Infiniverse and surrounded by the endless multicolored seas infinity, let out a long, ragged breath that carried the warmth of returning life.

He blinked his eyes, and the glazed crystalline glare that had defined them for the past hour rapidly dissolved, replaced by the sharp, calculating, and viscerally human light that had always been his trademark.

He felt the weight of his own skin again, the familiar pressure of his own memories and personality that had nearly been lost to the boundless. 𝐟𝕣𝗲𝕖𝕨𝗲𝐛𝗻𝗼𝐯𝗲𝚕.𝗰𝚘𝐦

Before him, the figures of THE Infiniverse and Ruination gazed at him with bright, expectant eyes, their forms flickering with the residual energy of the stabilization.

Ruination stepped forward, her illusory features softening as she breathed a profound sigh of relief that seemed to echo through the system itself.

"Welcome back, Master."

Noah looked at his hands, seeing the multicolored light dimmed tremendously but still dancing there.

He smiled, a slow, dark expression that held all the tyranny and ambition of the man who had defied THE Gilded Ones!

A Reign of Infinity was brought to an end in an extremely short period of time!
