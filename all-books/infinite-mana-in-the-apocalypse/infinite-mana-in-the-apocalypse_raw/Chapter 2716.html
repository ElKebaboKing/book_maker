Chapter 2714 A Twist of Fate! I

The astonishing scene that had just occurred has been branded into the souls of all, with astonishing effects of course being felt by Xiaomi, but Noah also made great gains!

And the gains only continued to pour in as he began dual cultivating with a genius Zenith First Gradation of Extremity.

When he had met The Adjudicator of Dissolution and bathed in her residual aura of Hyperversal Extremity Authority, he had promised himself to experience Extremity in an even closer sense.

Now, he had done just this as while he discussed Extremity with Xiaomi and felt her Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Rune Scriptures of Ruin closely, the prompts that showed his own gains from her Advancement showed themselves.

{An existence under your banner has achieved Extremity mainly through your aid and guidance!}

{You are the Third in Recorded History to elevate another being to Extremity before achieving Extremity yourself.}

{You have earned the distinction of Guider of Extremity.}

{The concept taken to Extremity by your Vassals is closely connected to your Source of Ruin. Under the authority of Guider of Extremity, all concepts you aid others in traversing to Extremity can have their False Golden Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Rune Scriptures forged by you through the utilization of pure Soul Values and Mana Values. Only 100 False Golden Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Rune Scriptures can be built from any one concept, with the capability of building False Obsidian Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Rune Scriptures only becoming possible if the aided concept in your Vassal attains the ranking of Crimson Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Rune Scripture.}

…!

Noah's eyes had opened wide at this prompt as it thundered down an explosive and heavy ability!

When he attained Extremity II Despoiler, he could forge False Obsidian Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Rune Scriptures of DESPOILER in addition to the Golden Tier ones from before, but all of these required heavy reserves of Vitalis and Vacuous Hyperversal Authorities as it took a long time to make them, and they always drained him at the end of the day.

But due to the distinction of Guider of Extremity and the fact that he aided his Vassals to achieve Extremity in a concept…he could utilize Mana and Soul Values to enact up to 100 False Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Rune Scriptures!

He could enact a hundred from Xiaomi currently, and if he guided multiple other concepts on other Vassals to Extremity…he could stack more and more.

When he had hundreds of False Golden Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Rune Scriptures ready to be deployed on the battlefield to Tier 1 Hyperversal Extremities affected by his Virions…what would they be able to do but tall en masse?! 𝓯𝙧𝓮𝓮𝒘𝓮𝙗𝙣𝒐𝒗𝒆𝓵.𝓬𝓸𝒎

HUUM!

His Aspects of Existence shone brightly as his body was filled with multiple wild and utterly reality breaking authorities, with the discussion occurring with Xiaomi also coming with its own boons.

{You are closely experiencing the Hyperversal Extremity Authority of Ruin within your Dual Cultivating partner.}

{In this state, you can forge the False Golden Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Rune Scriptures of Ruin with 80% less resource requirements.}

{Increased forging of the False Golden Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Rune Scriptures of Ruin and close interaction of the Pure Hyperversal Extremity Authority of Ruin through Dual Cultivation will eventually allow you to take the infinitesimally small step towards the Extremity Layer of Ruin. Such an action is a pathway of achieving Extremity, but it is recommended that you stop its advancement if it does occur as this pathway isn't enough to trigger Extremity in Quintessence- no matter the authority expressed by your Extremity Meridians.}

"..."

The requirements of the concepts considered Dead Paths continued to be just as restrictive.

The elevation of a concept much lower than them- even if they were the same Crimson Consummate Boundaries…

would not trigger their advancement to Extremity with all 81 Boundaries in Noah's Meridians!

Even at the Crimson Consummate level, Quintessence was much, much greater than Ruin.

And then…there was Noah's task of taking them to the ranking of Quintessential Zenith which would theoretically make these concepts even harder to elevate to Extremity, but the rewards and power from it would be that much more obscene.

Quintessence…had to be the first concept that Noah brought to Extremity.

He couldn't do it any other way, or this pathway would forever remain a dead pathway!

But even with such a massive wall before him, Noah was unfettered and free as he knew in his heart what he could and could not achieve.

And Extremity in Quintessence…would be in his grasp soon!

BOOM!

Immense Authority erupted from him as during his discussion of Extremity with Xiaomi, he raised his right hand up and caused the emergence of dense threads of Ruin that when mixed with his Quintessential Hyperversal Mana and Soul Values- vibrant False Golden Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Rune Scriptures of Ruin began to be forged.

His partner in the discussion of Extremity refocused her eyes at this scene as she blinked at the scene of Extremity Rune Scripture being forged by him, questioning whether she was the one who achieved Extremity or him!?

Waves of authority converged and bloomed in abundance as more tools were added under Noah's belt.

Meanwhile, in addition to the Records of Extremity and Writs of Extremity, he had still been getting thousands of prompts of him receiving the Golden Hyperversal Bones of Gradation, Obsidian Hyperversal Bones of Gradation, and the Hyperversal Marrow Soul Singularities every time his Virion ravaged the affected Extremities and brought them towards true death.

All this loot piled up into a mountain and then became an even bigger mountain after being processed by Ruination, and it was then directed towards the Sporos III of Extremity as this maddening seed surged towards full maturity.

The time for the next tier of the Ash Tree of Gradation to be born was nearing as at that juncture- Apexification would be underway! While this time became ever closer, Noah continued increasing his authority as he currently held 23 complete Writs of Extremity in his hands that represented the true deaths of 230 Tier 1 Hyperversal Extremities.

His Reified Wealdian Source of Vitalistic held them while looking at his people near the light years domain of the Andromeda Cleaver, a bright light crossing through his eyes as he was about to select a few specific beings that through the twist of fate- were one of the few existences in the Infinite Hyperversal Haven who held Planar Boundaries!
