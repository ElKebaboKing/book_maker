Chapter 1742 The lnfinite Dream Physique! ll

Noah had many types of Essences, with the vast majority of them being Infinite due to the Infinite Resources of his System!

Only Destiny, Fortune, and a few other unique types of resources couldn't be made Infinite as a mere Construct of a System made through the Essence of Reality wasn't enough for this.

Maybe similar to the Infinite Dream Physique Construct, something that would grant True Infinite Resources would have to be made with Lavalliere through the Absolute Dream Authority!

<The Uses required for that isn't something that even a future you some years from now can pay.>

"..."

But the idea was shot down just as it arose as Noah was unfettered, simply affirming that it was a possibility!

But the resources that could be made Infinite were those Noah had attained through his concepts, with the Essence of the Sanguine Emperor and all his other edicts simply being transformed from Mana and also being Infinite as they could freely be generated within Noah's body.

But…he couldn't generate the Essence of a Dimension yes even though he could pull upon it a little with Infinite Dream and <Sandman>!

Similar to how he gained other types of essences, he had to do something else to be capable of generating the Essence of a Dimension.

The simplest method and what Noah would be doing was to form an Edict geared towards this essence, granting Noah Flames that could bloom with this essence and he could generate it within his body!

Another way to obtain a Bloodline of a powerful existence in the Dream Dimension…and there were a myriad of other ways even Noah wasn't privy to.

But even though he couldn't generate it within his body currently, he could still control it as to access it…he had sent back a clone into the Dream Dimension.

OOOOM!

Swirling golden sand surrounded it as Noah made sure to discern with Destiny that he was exceedingly far away from the Dream Hellscape that the Ancestral Beowulf and the Emperor of the Golden Eye might still be in, this clone gaining the task of pulling in nearby Dream Essence and transferring it to the main body for the purposes of refining the Infinite Dream Physique!

This would be many times slower in comparison to him generating and using it himself, but he could do what he could now until the design of a concept that could generate this Dimensional Essence was made.

He had a feeling that it would at most have to be Quasi-TABOO if not TABOO since it was a Dimensional Essence, and his main body was making sure to complete the current mixture of essences to form a TABOO concept!

As for the Essences of Destiny and Fortune, the Essence of Reality and Mana…these were what Noah could provide himself as he was about to begin the moment things fell into place.

And this was when the words of RUINATION reached his mind.

<Master, I can edit a viable Affix of Reality termed <The Unanimity of the Parsimonious Emperor> that allows a level of harmony between the three Aspects of Existence, but it will require the authority of three uses of the Affixes of Reality to bring such an Affix to light.>

RUINATION posed a unique possibility as Noah didn't hesitate to give the go-ahead!

"Do it."

BZZZT!

After the Affix of Reality that granted Noah a percentage of passively daily generation of the Essence of Reality, this newly designed Affix contained within it the authority of 3 total Affixes as it would act to alleviate the imbalance Noah had with his Body, Origin, and Soul!

Noah sensed the changes in his soul closely as when the new reality-altering Affix was laid down, his Soul, Origin, and Body began to be suffused with a glow of an authoritative light.

<The Affix of Reality- The Unanimity of the Parsimonious Emperor, has been obtained.>

BZZZT!

<The Unanimity of the Parsimonious Emperor>:: The three Aspects of Existence are by themselves, and they eventually lean towards harmony as one elevates themselves to higher stages. The Unanimity of the Parsimonious Emperor is a construct that alters the reality of your Aspects of Existence to make it so that the strongest Aspects will propel the weakest ones forward and not leave them behind, with the weakest Aspect receiving reinforcement and passive strengthening from the strongest Aspect. This Affix is only capable of correcting major imbalances between the three Aspects of Existence as its effect wanes after a slight correction to the discrepancy between them, with it not being capable of achieving complete harmony…

The wondrous Affix of Reality would be many things for Noah, but he currently needed it to alleviate any extreme discrepancy within his body as the moment it was applied, his Origin began to feel a tremendous sense of pressure as it bubbled with pulses of power!

Apart from a level of harmony, The Unanimity of the Parsimonious Emperor would actually serve as a weapon for Noah to utilize to break his limits of another Aspect of Existence, where the number of 10 Billion Cosmos might eventually be exceeded soon enough as his Origin received passive strengthening due to how much more ridiculous his Soul and Body were!

But at this moment, the already vibrant Royal Cosmos were suffused with an incandescent glow as they seemed to be getting refined- getting larger and larger as the stellar bodies within them seemed to increase in abundance.

If a single Royal Cosmos stood for 2 normal Cosmos before, they would soon be standing for more than that as Noah's Body and Soul pulled his Origin slowly forward!

<What a unique little treasure that RUINATION is to be capable of instituting small level Reality Altering Constructs just from its authority. It seems to fall in line with those types of relics from the Age of Light…you should raise that one past TABOO soon if you wanna have an even easier time.>

Lavalliere spoke out freely at this time as Noah felt RUINATION'S body freeze in the Mirror Dimension, the words of Lavalliere having actually been passed through for her to hear as a sharp light crossed her crimson eyes!

She seemed to have been praised and also insulted as the terrific light illuminated the mirror like space for miles.

Noah's will and soul controlled everything occurring expertly as he asked.

"What type of relic from the Age of Light does RUINATION fall under?"

<Such knowledge is bound by Karma and will require the remaining Half a Use to learn, you want it?>

"...No. Everything will naturally fall into place with time." Noah replied lightly as he observed these small interactions, and as everything was in place with his current goals…his visage began to release a wondrous authority as he called out lightly.

"Infinite Dream Physique Refinement."

HUUUM!

The Golden blue skies of the Cosmic Holy Land trembled as the endless golden steps bounced off pearls of immense light and authority!

From the Dream Dimension, a True Sanguine Clone slowly began to pull in the surrounding Dream Essence with the little authority he currently held over it- with Noah already beginning to work on a concept in tune with the Dream Dimension and Essence so that his future refinements would be even easier.

Then, the cost of the Essence of Reality, Destiny, and Fortune surged next as thousands of Units of Essence of Reality began to burn every second, the Units of Destiny, Fortune, and Mana being so abundant that their current usage was inconsequential!

BZZZT!

After all the conditions were met, incandescent white sand surged around Noah as it swirled mystically around him like a storm.

A terrifying storm that caused his body to withstand a great pressure, this being the process of refinement as his Physique would be tempered by what seemed like unique Sands from the Dream Dimension, with this first stage required White Sand!

Eventually, the raging storm around Noah that he would have to withstand would go to Blue, Purple, Gold, and so forth as he rose through the Cycles of the Infinite Dream Physique.

HOOONG!

His main body stood within the eye of the raging storm of incandescent white sand while seated on a Throne that was currently burning Edicts and elevating his Soul even higher, his Body now joining the fray as while the raging storms surged and refined the Physique- white dew drops shining with radiant light formed as they went to sink into his Body!

The refinement of the Infinite Physique began at this moment as not even the first Cycle was completed…and nobody knew just how many Cycles Noah would go through on his path of power!
