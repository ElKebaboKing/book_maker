Chapter 3378 A Dream of a Different Tune! II

But the Great Segregator could see the shock and confusion on the Great Mother herself as her body began pulsing with obsidian light, her form of an infinity symbol beginning to glow as if even she was affected by this rising complexity.

After the brief period of impending doom of her Severed Domain, the moment of stability allowed the Great Segregator to feel a sense of rising horror and shock as from the depths of her weavings, she felt ridiculously complex weavings of Existence arise and begin to proliferate in a way she could have never imagined.

A type of complexity of existence that she seemed far away from.

It rose and began to seethe within her as the moment she stabilized her Severed Domain, she began to feel the boons that came with the ridiculous weavings unfolding within her.

At this moment, she felt like she finally understood as her Severed Domain…seemed to only be getting utilized as a conduit.

It should have merely allowed others to explode with complexity outside the influence of the Expanse and Megalos, but the Instrument of the Beyonder within had enacted enough of a change that now, her Domain was used akin to a passage to allow something that should have theoretically not been possible!

As for what it was, even she didn't know.

All she knew was the rousing complexity that didn't seem to only benefit her, but even the Great Mother within her Domain seemed to be benefiting as her body in the form of an Infinity symbol seemed to be coursing with boundless obsidian waves of brilliance- influenced by the shimmering rune of Amarantos spinning above Ozymandias as at this moment, it let out a blinding light that almost seemed like it wanted to pause everything!

As if this moment would forever be halted in its weavings of time.

Above the blaring rune of Amarantos.

Ozymandias was only able to stare at it for an instant before his vast consciousness buzzed into oblivion, feeling his existence pulled in a way that never occurred before as he came to understand the Dream he initiated this time around…would be an utterly incomprehensible one!

Within the Primal Eldritch Palace of the Expanse.

On this day, many auras within were aroused as near the center of the Blancverse this power belonging to the Megalos Beyond was situated, a glaring obsidian light filled with heavy complexity bloomed for an instant.

Only an instant!

But its complexity was enough to alarm even Existence Severing entities.

—

A dream.

An Outerversal Morphon seemed to be waking up from an endlessly long dream, his weavings feeling fractured and out of sorts as he gathered himself and set his will outwards.

As if he was regaining his vision, he saw that he lay on a field of swaying black gold grass.

He rose as his hands passed through this grass, seeing a humanoid obsidian stellar skin as when he looked at himself fully, he found that he was entirely humanoid in nature- but his body was simply made up of the vastness of the Megalos Beyond itself as he glowed with a dim obsidian stellar radiance.

He rose as his hands passed through this grass, seeing a humanoid obsidian stellar skin as when he looked at himself fully, he found that he was entirely humanoid in nature- but his body was simply made up of the vastness of the Megalos Beyond itself as he glowed with a dim obsidian stellar radiance.

A single wing burning with obsidian flames stretched out behind his back, and a halo of obsidian radiance floated above him.

Soon, he regained more of his cognition as he realized he was not alone.

To his left, there was an entity letting out serene waves of Ordinance Amalgamation and those of Matter, her skin tanned and shining with a bronze hue as her hair glowed with bright gold radiance.

She seemed to be sleeping at this moment, with his gaze on her waking her up at this moment.

Her eyes fluttered to show gold pupils that looked towards him with a happy smile, her exquisite figure rising up at this moment and coming closer to him as she came to rest her head on his lap.

"We can't keep hiding in this little dream of ours, Archimedes. The Great Instrument of the Beyonder has already made a Decree for all those at Existence Severing and below to gather and prepare for the Incursion into Megalos Nihilos. We cannot delay this any longer."

…!

An Entity made of Matter. A human.

She currently looked towards him, Archimedes, an Outerversal Morphon, with love and care.

She wasn't bound to him.

She wasn't a Delegatus.

And yet, they were together.

His head felt a splitting pain as if weavings of memories were about to erupt, scattered and everywhere all at once!

"Are you okay? I told you not to mess around with the techniques of the Great Beyonder. Only his Instrument is able to use them to perfection as the rest of us cannot compare."

In his scattered state, the woman before him grabbed his head tenderly and asked as he blinked and cleared his weavings, some memories returning as he looked around.

He turned his gaze above and spread his will out as he found himself in a massive Omniverse, and outside of it…he could see the start of an even more massive Pantheon in the distance!

An Omniverse and a Pantheon close together.

When his will spread further out, he saw weavings of both Matter and Dark Matter leaking into each other as it seemed like the Megalos Beyond and Megalos Ynnere were mixed together chaotically!

"Archimedes?"

The woman before him grasped his head with concern as he shook himself out of his reverie and smiled.

Some memories came back.

Memories of this place.

He forced a smile as he floated up.

"I'm okay, Lacana. Come on, let's get going."

His voice was magnetic as Lacana nodded with hesitation, their figures rising up and shooting out of the small planet within the calm Omniverse where Outerversal Morphons and Lineages linked to Matter could all be seen mixed together.

The two of them went towards a certain sector where the Great Instrument of the Beyonder was gathering all forces under his command as he sought to stretch out his influence across all Megalos!

'The Great Instrument of the Beyonder…'

Archimedes thought this as scattered memories came and went, a rune of Amarantos shining brilliantly within him as his sense of cognition slowly returned.

What he knew of the Great Instrument of the Beyonder also flowed in freely.

An entity of great reverence and power that had managed to expand the Megalos Beyond into the Megalos Ynnere.

He had only glimpsed his brilliance once when this entity was decimating Existence Severing Antediluvian Leviathans.

Moving on four limbs, a massive body radiating brutality and brilliance.

The Great Instrument of the Beyonder, Kurai!

It was not Ozymandias.

It caused Archimedes, who was accepting many fractured memories to wonder.

Exactly where was he?
