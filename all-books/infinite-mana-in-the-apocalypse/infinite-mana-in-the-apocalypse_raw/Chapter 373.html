Chapter 372 - A Battle With a Fire Behemoth!

Waves of power erupted out as Boru’s Soul Forging Realm was revealed gloriously, the heavy armor made from materials of a Fire Element Behemoth shining an arrogant red color as they went on to push back the raging flames being propelled forward by the mountainous beast in front of them!

The long BroadSword letting out palpating waves of the water element went on to further disperse the powerful attack coming towards them as the members of the Righteous Fire Exploration Team spread around the Fire Behemoth, each one knowing their positions as their grasped weapons let out colorful attacks.

BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!

The moment the wide area attack of the Behemoth ended, attacks layered with the strength of thrumming Fate Lines from 5 Soul Forging Realm Celestials began to land on the large beast.

"Heave HO!"

BOOM!

Boru took the center point as he kept the attention of the maddened beast, swinging his large blade downwards as it released a spectacular blade light of gold and blue, the unique energy provided by his Fate Lines passing through the weapon he held as this blade light went on to leave a scratch on the thick skin of the Behemoth.

That was right, a scratch!

That was all that the numerous attacks did to this terrible creature!

Noah and Athena were observing the ensuing battle from the back to get a grasp of how the team normally handled these types of creatures, their own bodies aching to join the battle themselves as the splendors of colorful skills bloomed in the area.

"Zeke, left flank! Reiner, get behind it! Sylphy..."

Boru was giving numerous commands as multiple Empyrean Guards began to appear around the raging Behemoth, these Empyrean Guards soon turning into streaks of light that went on to wrap around the bodies of the members of the exploration team.

OOOM!

The immense aura of fate spread out as the five figures of the exploration team became golden armored and winged beings, tens of thousands of Fate Lines on each of their bodies pulsating as they rained down even more attacks with vigor.

ROOOAAAR!

The Behemoth roared out as it felt dents beginning to appear on its armor, the numerous spiked crystals attached all over its huge back and tail shining a murderous red light as they erupted from its body and flew to the skies, very quickly raining down in the form of dangerous spiked flaming lances that attacked the entire perimeter around it!

BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!

Boru and his teammates weaved in and out of the area as they evaded the flaming lances that exploded on impact, their armored bodies taking the brunt of the explosive forces as one of the teammates had to even pull back after cracks began to appear on her armor.

"Sylphy, pull back! Everyone else, get [Judgment] ready!"

Boru shouted out as he oversaw the entire area closely, his Soul Rank strength bubbling with power as he dual wielded a golden hammer in one hand and the long jagged water element BroadSword in the other.

"Hooo..."

A hot breath released from him as Noah shockingly counted more than 80,000 Fate Lines thrum within his body, his body coiling like a spring as it propelled itself towards the head of the Behemoth in a straight line!

Boru’s body began spinning in the air as wave after wave of golden blue blade lights began to erupt from him and smash onto the large head of the Behemoth, the monstrous beast itself going on to reply by releasing a horrid breath of fire that disintegrated the very air itself towards him.

SHAA! SHAA! SHAA!

The numerous golden blue blades of light met this attack as Boru continued spinning, his team mates moving to the sides as they prepared a skill Noah was very much familiar with, [Judgment]!

"Ready!"

"Ready!.."

The four of them called out as Boru used the force of his weapons to push himself out of the way of the destructive fire breath, the Behemoth choosing to pursue him with more attacks angrily before it came to a stop, its senses telling it that danger was coming.

RUMBLE!

Essence converged around the Behemoth as an enormous barrier of colorful flames appeared around it, but would the attacks of 5 Soul Forging Realm Celestials be that easy to block?

[Judgment]!

[Judgment]!

...

Four calls resounded first as Boru’s teammates released their abundant mana to release this powerful skill, numerous golden spears appearing in the skies as they aimed straight down towards the enormous Behemoth.

BOOM!

The wide and colorful shield of flames it had erected was pierced through after the first and second round of spears rained down, the third and fourth casts going on to pierce through its thick hide as a squeal of pain was finally released from this monstrous beast.

ROAAR!

A roar of pain erupted out as the beast felt enormous damage in its soul from the numerous attacks layered with abundant Law of Fate, but its pain was not done as the one who had been peskily running around it had the time to collect himself and prepare an even more powerful attack with his abundant lines of fate and something else that Noah could not yet figure out.

[Judgment], [Smite of Liberation].

OOONG

The air trembled as Noah used his senses to observe the being called Boru call upon every drop of energy in his body, his senses observing this energy pass through his tens of thousands of fate lines as it was released in a unique manner to initiate the cast of two abilities from [Fate’s Guardian].

BOOM!

The numerous sharp golden spears arrived first, piercing the wide body of the mountainous Behemoth that had actually begun to bleed bubbling red blood and going on to skewer its body even deeper!

The golden spears were followed by the sky clearing up as a large incorporeal golden Blade of Liberation that was more than three times the size of the blades Noah normally cast descended down.

This single blade was half the size of the more than 50 meter Fire Behemoth as it shot down at high speeds, the Behemoth sensing this danger as a destructive fire storm raged around it, but the damage it had received to its Soul affected it too much! 𝗳𝚛𝚎𝚎𝘄𝕖𝕓𝕟𝕠𝚟𝚎𝕝.𝗰𝕠𝐦

SPLUCH!

The abilities of [Fate’s Guardian] could be vastly different depending on who was using them, the enormous Blade of Liberation being cast by the Soul Forging Boru being phenomenally powerful as it went on to pierce the hard hide of the mountainous Behemoth and sink deep into its body.

ROAAAR!

"Haha!"

A defiant roar rang out from a deadly beast inflicted with immense Soul Damage, while a spent Celestial hoisting a large BroadSword laughed as he knew the battle was as good as over.
