Chapter 610 - [Controller](Fire, Water)

As Noah repeated the words of Novus Galaxy in his mind over and over again, he was staring at the description of his Trait while continuing to sense the confounding sense of power from his Origin that had birthed a Nebula.

<strong>[Infinite Mana] (Unranked) :: Its uses are pure and simple, granting exactly what the trait says. The holder of this Trait can cast as many abilities as he wants for however long he wants, with mana never being an issue for them. It is currently unranked as there is very limited knowledge of where it might stand amongst the numerous Traits in the outside galaxies.</strong>

Noah glanced at the details of the Trait as he smiled, knowing that on this day, he could full well look down upon all others when it comes to the reserves of mana as he can confidently ask- Mana depletion? What’s that?

His smile was wide as he glanced at the description of this trait with vigor, only being taken out of this trance when he felt his Origin begin to vibrate with power!

He sent his awareness in as he found the now over 50 stars in his Nebula floating in a mystical formation, each of these stars contributing to granting him stupid levels of attack and defensive power.

If beings in the Star Forging Realm were powerful because they had a star supporting them in their Origin, one would only have to think a little to understand the realm of power where a being at the Nebula Rank had multiple stars that were doing just the same!

This created for a vast gulf for beings in the Nebula Rank, where one could have tens of stars while others only had two or three- the former being able to trample on the one holding fewer stars with ease!

But currently, the reason that Noah’s origin was shaking was because extremely pure streams of essence continued to come from the law Origin Cores surrounding his Nebula, but the current flow of universal essence was even stronger from two particular Origin Cores- the Fire and Water Origin Cores.

This was because the comprehension of these two laws...were about to hit 100%!

RUMBLE!

Tremors spread out because of the majestic energy as the size of the red and blue cores were the second largest after Noah’s Nebula, where they were glowing even brighter than the Light and Dark Origin cores that were a level higher in terms of laws.

Their rotation only increased as the seconds passed, and Noah felt their vibrations reach a breaking point as the two Origin Cores came to halt instantly...thereafter exploding into a shower of colors as they were both ground into dust!

Similar to how his Nebula formed, the Origin Cores of Fire and Water had dissolved into milky red and blue particles as they actually formed the base of a Nebula, extremely small red and blue stars beginning to erupt from these Nebulae as Noah felt an even more intense rush of universal essence washing over his own Origin.

This was an extremely interesting sight to come across as in the vast Galaxies, Nebulae were huge cl.u.s.ters of gas and dust in space that come about from the supernova of a dying star, and they are also the regions where new stars begin to form as they were actually termed the Nursery of Stars!

Seeing the Origins of two laws forming into a Nebula at this moment represented a grand future that showed the path of these laws was only just beginning.

The two newly appearing Nebulae that represented the comprehension of law of Fire and Water vibrated with essence as they began to float around Noah’s main Nebula in a circular motion, following the same path as the four other Origin Cores of Air, Earth, Light, and Dark that were all still singular circular origin cores!

But the essence they released towards Noah’s Origin was ten times what the other cored released, Noah feeling this awe-inspiring sense of strength as he looked at his Stat Panel.

[Noah Osmont][Title(s): Infernal Lord(Variant), Grand Controller(Fate), Controller(Fire, Water)]

[Bloodline(s): Vampyre Progenitor]

[Aether Particles(s) : 6,294,563]

[Karma Line(s) : 924,249]

[Fate Line(s) : 10,000,000]

[Chaos Particle(s) : 6,624,983]

[Origin Core(s) : Light-2, Dark-2, Fire-10, Water-10, Earth-4, Air-4]

[Domain(s) : Aether, Karma, Fate, Chaos, Light, Dark, Space, Time, Fire, Water, Earth, Air]

[Vitality: NEBULA]

[Focus: - ]

[Strength: NEBULA]

[Law(s) : Aether- 62%, Karma-9%,Fate-100%,Chaos-66%, Light-21%, Dark-21%, Space-38%, Time-27%, Fire-100%, Water-100%, Earth-38%, Air-38%]

[Authority(s) : Aether Lord, Fate Lord, Chaos Lord, Flame Lord, Water Lord]

Shockingly, he had just seamlessly advanced forward as he became a Controller in another two universal laws, where he now stood at the level where he had complete comprehension of 3 laws!

He focused his attention towards the two newly appearing titles of Controller.

<strong>[Controller](Fire) :: A title granted to those with complete comprehension of the law of Fire. All fire-related skills will exhibit their power at the level of a Controller regardless of their rank, the user beginning to embark on truly understanding the essence of Fire. The essence of a Fire Controller allows them to be immune to any fire-based attacks of those at lower stages of the Law of Fire.</strong>

<strong>[Controller](Water) :: A title granted to those with complete comprehension of the law of Water. All water-related skills will exhibit their power at the level of a Controller regardless of their rank, the user beginning to embark on truly understanding the essence of Water. The essence of a Water Controller allows them to be immune to any water-based attacks of those at lower stages of the Law of Water.</strong>

Nearly identical descriptions with the only changes being whether it was fire or water were displayed in his eyes, Noah gaining two more Laws that granted him the title of a Controller as he could actually claim Immunity to any beings with less advancement in the two laws!

Another even more ecstatic thing was the fact that being a Controller of the laws of Fire and Water allowed one to display the immense power of such a level on all possible cast skills of the user.

This made Noah’s eyes shine with l.u.s.ter as he imagined a shocking sight of him defeating powerful enemies with skills of the lowest tier!

"Haha!"

He gave a boisterous laugh as he was not the least beast fearful of whatever was coming, his main body letting his clones continue their advancement in the laws and proficiencies of a particular skill tree that would soon enter into the ranks of Nebula as he went off towards the Letalis Expanse.

His 100% comprehension in the law of Fate told him that whatever was coming would be upon them in just a day or two, thus he went towards the only location that any Outsiders could enter- and that was the Letalis Expanse where three Terrors were stationed!
