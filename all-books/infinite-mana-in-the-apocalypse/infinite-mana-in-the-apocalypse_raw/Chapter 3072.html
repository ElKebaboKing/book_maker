Chapter 3070 Pompeii! II

Within the bounds of the Greater Omniversal Domain of Pompeii.

First, one had to have a semblance of understanding regarding an Omniversal Domain to truly understand where the Legions of the Imperium and Omniversal Pillagers were currently in.

A Greater Omniversal Domain.

Its root cause or source was the Greater Omniversal Domain of Pompeii- which meant that this was the authority of the Seed of the Omniversal Authority that was currently budding within this space!

Its complex weavings were many.

Millions of Existential Extremity Authorities flowed as Pompeii…seemed to either be at the same level if not more majestic that the Greater Omniversal Authorities of Oneiros and Realis that Noah had just come across.

And all of them were being analyzed by the terrifying Quintessential Codex of Extremity in his hands while within his Dominium, Manadynamics seethed with cerulean splendor to freely give out the resources required to allow him to influence the Greater Omniversal Authority of Realis to protect them.

Yes.

Mana was still his forte.

He still had an infinite amount of it!

And as he acclimated to the new realm of Existential Dominium, this truth was becoming re-established more and more as the tools that got him here gained free reign.

None more so than Entropy and Manadynamics.

None more so than Infinity.

Since even before the construct of the Codex of Extremity, the fervor of the Existential Extremity Authority of Entropy was only rising within the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed as it seemed to resonate with the larger Omniverse itself.

And it still wasn't done.

But along with his endless Mana that could power up his Dominium, his Omniversal Organ that could distort Causality, Quintessential Codex of Extremity, and the Innate Existential Causality Defense wrapped around him…Noah moved with confidence.

When they came across the massive visage of an Ox like Omniversal Pillager and a battered Legion of the Imperium, Noah's body continued to freely move forward!

The Omniversal Pillager was the most dangerous as it could deal 1,000% more damage to others under the Illusory Sleeping Omniverse of Oneiros, but Noah didn't stop his momentum and came directly in front of it while holding the bow multicolored Codex of Extremity in his hands.

ROAAR!

A bellow was heard as at this stage, moves occurred within the span of an instant.

A massive limb formed like a crimson axe was before Noah's small figure right away, and yet he looked immensely calm and did not retaliate in any way.

BZZT!

Inches away from smashing into him, auroras of Tendrils of light buzzed as his Innate Existential Causality Defense flourished with brilliance!

Causality was passively twisted and distorted.

Noah didn't face the debuffs of the Greater Omniversal Domain of Oneiros as the weavings of Realis surrounded him, so this Teracosm Stage Entity was only that to him!

Seeing the baffling situation where its massive limbs were supposed to smash onto this being and yet they didn't go any further, the Omniversal Pillager was about to make another move while the Imperium Legions opposite it got a chance to breathe.

Yet in the next moment…

|There is a high probability of your death…|

The intent of Noah weaved out as on the oxen head of the Omniversal Pillager, its pupils vibrated.

Noah wanted to see how powerful he had gotten without utilizing the complete Causality effect of the Invictus Dominium Wings.

So weavings of his Extremes moved alone as the Extremity of Probability shone with a deep purple hue!

|And since nothing on you is Quintessential…how can your authorities even operate?|

…!

A simple question.

Yet the body of the massive Omniversal Pillager further trembled as a Colorless light of Quintessence came to surround it!

Alongside it, the weavings of the Greater Omniversal Authority of Realis surged around it and dismembered the buffs of Onerios this being received- the full effects of Noah's extremes landing on it.

Its circulation of extremes began to waver and slow.

The defenses of its Dominium slowly came to a halt in a shocking show of power, its crimson eyes buzzing as it stared at the small being before it!

Shrouded in a multicolored Emperor's Robe with multicolored rivers of light swirling around him.

A pair of purple gold wings on his back and feet. 𝑓𝑟ℯ𝘦𝓌𝘦𝘣𝑛𝑜𝓋𝑒𝓁.𝑐ℴ𝓂

And at the end, the Oxen Omniversal Pillagers looked at this being's eyes.

His eyes…were innumerably bright.

They were currently shining with an endless blue hue of wonder.

|You dare to stare straight into the eyes of Infinity.|

HUUM!

An eruption of cerulean light bloomed.

How powerful was Noah now?

After devouring multiple Teracosm Stage Entities and even the dual cultivation with someone as unique as Sona…

His Existential Extremity Authorities alone held a tremendous amount of power.

Especially after he tapped into one of the Four Components of the Greater Omniversal Authority of Aitia and increased the efficiency of these concepts!

Infinity was one of the concepts whose extremes were now heading towards a place even he didn't know fully well.

|A target has stared into the endless sea of Infinity.|

|Unable to comprehend it and filled with an endless flow of weavings, its existence becomes increasingly overwhelmed until everything about its Existential Dominium breaks down.|

|From the weavings of Infinity, the Existential Omniversal Pupils of Infinity have been born and activated for the first time.|

…!

|So long as any Entity designated as a target stares into your eyes, they will be blinded by the glimpse of Infinity as depending on their level of power, everything about their existence may be overwhelmed entirely as they cease to operate, or they could combat the endless weavings of Infinity until they ultimately fall.|

A shocking blue hue shone within Noah's eyes as it seemed to expand towards the horizon.

And when this light faded, the massive body of the Oxen Omniversal had frozen as its crimson eyes…were now bathed with an endless blue.

Yet it was not moving.

Its Dominium was no longer circulating!

The golden glow of Teracosm had entirely stopped moving around it as it seemed to have stalled and solidified!

And against such a being, Noah waved his hands as its massive body twisted and came towards him, disappearing into nothingness as within the next moment, it appeared within his Infinite Forge.

|...|

It was a shocking scene as from behind, Sona whistled while surrounded by icy brilliance.

After his recent upgrades…Noah's power was ascertained!
