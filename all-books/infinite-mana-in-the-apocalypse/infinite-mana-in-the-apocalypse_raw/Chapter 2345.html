Infinite Mana In The Apocalypse

Chapter 2343 Catastrophic! II

After Titus's glorious transformation, all the others had remained at bay.

But at this moment.

BZZT!

Radiant golden and silver wings blossomed on the 11 other beings as one of the most dazzling women in the silver gold crystalline armament squinted her eyes while reading out what was on the Apex Aeonic Relic.

<Osmont, with an Aeonic Soul Age of less than 100 Years. And Quantitative Aeonic Soul Power…unable to be defined at this moment. Titus, this is likely a genius affiliated with the Aeonic Haven of Penumbra for him to be able to mask even his Quantitative Aeonic Soul Power against the Haven Seeing Mirror. He may be similar to those fostered monsters from the Vicissitude Extremity Grotto with mutated souls for him to be this young…>

WAA!

The Haven Disciples of the Genesis Institute of Extremity gazed towards Noah as if they were seeing an equal for the first time, many assumptions being made by them as Titus didn't lose his smile.

<You may be right, Diona. His Consummate Boundaries are hidden as we can assume he had as many as us, and he must have been bathed in Aeonic Treasures of Extremity within the first few seconds of being born and infused with them for the next decades…haha, we've actually come across one of the Engineered Entities!>

…!

More and more assumptions were made as Noah's sharp eyes took in all of this information and expanded what he already knew.

He gave the enemies the time to freely give him more information while his transformation also finished, where he allowed them to continue making wrongful assumptions about him as much as they wanted!

A genius affiliated with the Aeonic Haven of Penumbra?

Akin to the fostered monsters from the Vicissitude Extremity Grotto?

Engineered Entity?

He was already wrongfully placed into so many destinations when all he was…was someone with Infinite Mana that continued to utterly break the laws of Reality!

<But…even if he is a being engineered since birth to speedily advance towards Extremity, I am turning 900 in a few days as our age discrepancy is not that large. I am curious what such a being will be able to show me as even with all this…my Destiny still thinks that he is not complete.>

WAA!

The silver gold glow around Titus became even more intense.

Those around him shook their heads while sighing and becoming serious, Diona pursing her lips as she radiated multiple Boundaries at the Consummate level.

<We'll have to finish this once we start it, and we also can't allow it to delay our search for the Extremity Panaceas. Those similar to us from other participating Havens are not too far off from our position.>

<Mmm…> Titus nodded as liquid silver and gold began to drip around his body, focusing strictly on Noah as he voiced out with a smile.

<Pretty boy, you ready? I want to see what mysteries the Haven Seeing Mirror couldn't unveil from you. I haven't yet met someone younger than me exhibit power at this level apart from those ridiculous beings from Extremity Lineages…and they have Extremity quite literally coursing in their veins. What will you show me?>

…!

Terrifying waves of Haki and battle intent spread out.

Noah's visage in the skies was unfazed as he spoke lightly towards Ophelia.

<Will you be okay? I'll take on as many of them as possible.>

<Mmm…> Ophelia nodded with a serious gaze as she continued. <My Aeonic Soul Clone shall not perish here today.>

WAA!

Their Aeonic Soul Values rose to unprecedented levels.

The beings before them posed terrifying power as they knew their current selves weren't their match!

But she was Ophelia, the one who deeply understood Wisdom.

And he…was the True Emperor of Quintessence. Battles were his Forte. Battles were where he thrived.

So long as he could overcome his enemies in battle, he would come out many times stronger than before.

Haven Disciples?

The Genesis Institute of Extremity?

Their distinction and standing didn't matter so long as at the end of it all, he triumphed over them and turned them into Loot.

BZZZT!

His newly risen Consummate Boundaries pulsed as they roared out to be released, the many tools under his belt ready to unleash their glory!

His soul…his soul that had undergone so many transformations was entirely different from what any soul should be.

The recent one of the changes brought about through the Entropic Entity of Extremity caused him to feel the Sources of Chaos from afar as even though the Haki and radiance released from Titus had pushed away the raging tendrils of RUIN and NULLITY, his body attracted new tendrils freely as just his existence in this space…increased the disorder and chaos.

Streaks of silver and red emerged from all directions and surged towards Noah as the smile on Titus expanded even more, his body instantly disappearing a moment later!

As for the 11 Haven Disciples behind him, they also turned into points of light that instantly disappeared as their speed was something that only Unequaled Reverend Emperors could even have a chance of releasing!

Unequaled.

Reverends who had no enemies apart from those at the same stage, and those above them who were Apex Aeonic Lifeforms.

These were the beings they became entangled with as facing such a situation, a catastrophic amount of cerulean singularities the size of atoms blossomed from Noah to cause his surroundings to turn into a hellish torrential flame storm, tendrils of RUIN and NULLITY reforming as he released area of effect attacks to keep the coming enemies at bay.

Ophelia instantly released her Planar Domain as a golden light covered her body, Words of Wisdom spewing out freely!

While the Mad Reverend Emperor Caesar…

<I have no time for this foolishness!>

BOOM!

His figure bellowed out with utter rage as he was on a race against time, and these beings actually dared to stand in his way!

So what if they were from one of the older Havens?

What if they were on the same stage of power as him?!

He just had to erase them like all those that confronted before him in the past, and he would be ever closer to achieving what he wanted.

So he turned into a shining cluster of terrifying Quasars that seemed akin to trillions of black suns that could each burn weaker Reverends, the gravitational temperature he released being something that nobody could freely even come close to!

With such an surge of power, the eyes of the Mad Reverend Emperor entirely disappeared as when he thought about his dying daughter, he bellowed out fearlessly.

<LET'S GO!>

BOOOM!
