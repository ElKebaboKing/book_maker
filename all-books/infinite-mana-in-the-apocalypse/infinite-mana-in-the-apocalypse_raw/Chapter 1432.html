Chapter 1430: Domineering Advancement!

A phenomenon of utter fantasy played out as while forging Cosmos within his Origin, unique resources moved to complete his Nomological Edicts as five raging Flames bloomed at once!

In the Cosmic Holy Land, even Hyperion had to stop his advancement to gaze towards Noah's location, seeing the body of this existence bursting with thunder and multicolored light as it seemed like an ancient being reforging every part of himself after waking up from eons of slumber.

Every time a Cosmos was formed in his expanding Cosmic Filament, a burst of unique essence was released from it to bathe his body as those perceptive could see the muscles and bones being reforged and strengthened in real time!

Thereafter, countless Universal Singularities would bloom as even more Cosmos would be born, and this process would go on to repeat for a shocking number of 1 Million times as this…was the number of Cosmos that would be forged from a single Shard of a Seed of Reality.

From the Cosmic Realm to the Cosmic Filament Realm. From a single Cosmos to hundreds in a matter of milliseconds as the number continued to increase even faster, racing towards 1 million with intense fervor!

At this same time, five glorious Flames bloomed as a single one outshine the rest and and oppressed them to bow towards it with fear- this being a natural suppression of Tiers as the vastly gold glimmering Flames of the Resplendent Treasure Emperor came to bathe over the center of Noah's Origin while the other four Flames stayed in the periphery.

These four Flames all stemmed from Cecilia, all her work becoming Noah's as he obtained the Flames of the Golden Emperor, Unbridled Emperor, Savanna, and Vestige!

The Flames of the Golden Emperor stemmed from Cecilia's concept of a being with robust luck, Fortune, and Destiny- giving herself this title as it now became Noah's. With it, his destiny and fortune were further elevated to greater levels with ridiculous boosts like +500,000,000% Increased Reserves of Destiny and Fortune, and the same number for the Increased Rate of Resource Regeneration!

Of course, the resource Regeneration didn't apply to something as pristine as the Essence of Reality as this resource continued to remain distinguished by itself.

The Flames came with abilities that also took the Essence of Reality to activate, but as Noah read through their details…he was frankly disappointed as they didn't match the domineering effects of abilities that stemmed from Flames of Edicts he designed himself!

'Living for so many years and not even being able to creatively make overpowered Edicts…' Disdain was thrown to a defeated existence as Noah only breezed over the details of the Flames of the Golden Emperor, his gaze going towards the other three Quasi-Insuperable Nomological Edict stemming from Cecilia as even though they were of this tier, they were lackluster when compared to his own Edicts of the same tier.

The Flames of the Unbridled Emperor focused on the aspect of one being wild and unrestrained, granting abilities that allowed one to enter a berserk state in exchange for Essence of Reality. In this berserk state, they would gain a semblance of Invincibility as it was something similar to Plot Armor, with the only thing capable of breaking the boons of the Flames of the Unbridled Emperor being the Flames of another Quasi-Insuperable Edict!

Cecilia didn't even get to use this as she focused all her reserves of the Essence of Reality rotating complete her remaining Edicts and forge her second Halo, but even if she had…Noah would have been able to bypass the state of Invincibility with the undeniable essence of his Insuperable Edict.

The Flames of Savanna were unique as they stemmed from concepts of Cecilia's Bloodline of Celestial Vine, granting one Increased Life Force and defenses as this was her defensive edict similar to how Noah had The Nomological Edict of Antediluvian.

But of course…Noah's Antediluvian was much more oppressive in its effect as the addition of Savanna only made him even more unkillable against beings with authorities of only Quasi-Insuperable Nomological Edicts.

Then lastly, there were the Flames of Vestige that had the concepts of remnants or remains- this being another life saving Edict Cecilia tried to establish where even if her Origin and soul were shattered, she could always come back so long as even a cell of her remnants were left behind. Of course, Noah nullified the effects of this Edict when he struck her Origin with the effects of Apocalyptic Erasure that eradicated her across spacetime while backed with the authority of the Insuperable Edict of the Resplendent Treasure Emperor!

Even though her body the size of a Cosmos remained, she would never return as her Vestige would only be used for Noah to elevate RUINATION!

These were the four Quasi-Insuperable Nomological Edicts just completed into Flames as Lastly, there came Noah's very own design.

<Flames of the Resplendent Treasure Emperor> :: The Flames of the Resplendent Treasure Emperor bloom in your origin as they usher in the Bloodline of the Resplendent Treasure Emperor. The tier of this Edict is extremely high as for the sake of purity, not a single follower is allowed. A natural suppression effect also exists with this Edict against any QUASI-INSUPERABLE, GREATER, or MINOR Nomological Edicts due to its higher rank, with complete nullification being achieved against the essence and abilities of Greater and Minor Edicts and only 20% effectiveness with the essence of Quasi-Insuperable Edicts. When the Flames of the Resplendent Treasure Emperor descend, the destinies and fortunes of all those below it are temporarily absorbed as Reality itself will begin to conform towards the result that the holder of these flames seeks. As the architect of this Bloodline Edict, you are granted the abilities of <<Absolute Looter>>, <<Resplendent Treasure Cache>>and <<Reality Looter>> that can be utilized with the Essence of Reality in conjunction with the Essence of the Resplendent Treasure Emperor. With the Flames of the Resplendent Treasure Emperor, the glow of treasures surround you as even Realities can be sacrificed for Loot. As the Progenitor of the Bloodline of the Resplendent Treasure Emperor, you are granted +200,000,000,000% to All Parameters, +200,000,000,000% to True Defense, and +200,000,000,000% to True Annihilatory Damage. All abilities that utilize the essence of the Resplendent Treasure Emperor also have their resource requirement cut down by half, and your Origin produces an extra 10 Billion Splendiferous Universes daily…

The details of the incandescent golden flames revealed themselves as the mere words alone were enough to burn one's eyes!
