Chapter 3175 Existential Colonization! IV

The arms of the Protector were spread out wide as he spoke with a smile!

"Let me be first to welcome you, and we will soon be in contact again as the Commonwealth sets up Bases to protect you all against any threats in the future. You will also have the privilege of enjoying the boons of the Commonwealth soon enough, so be on the lookout for more news, O Glorious Citizens!"

…!

His golden tail weaved maddeningly.

His smile was deep as his audience remained stern.

His figure began to flicker with radiance as he rose upwards, his sharp eyes coming to gaze at Emperor Osmont as he spoke out his last words.

"You really do have a nice home here, Citizen Emperor Osmont. You should do your best to protect it."

"..."

With a thundering boom, he was gone right after. 𝕗𝗿𝕖𝐞𝐰𝗲𝕓𝐧𝕠𝕧𝗲𝐥.𝚌𝐨𝚖

As he left the small Mobile Plane of Existence, Protector Antonio spoke to the device on his neck.

"See if this Omniverse has any more Mobile Planes of Existence. They're nice trinkets to have for storage…"

|Understood, Protector Antonio.|

Brilliance flourished.

And with a glimmer of brightness, Protector Antonio was gone!

Back on the makeshift round table.

HUUM!

Around Noah's Ascendant Existential Extremity Clone of Quintessence, his brilliant Halo returned along with his colorless wings as he breathed out heavily- the interminable Authority of the Laborer of the Atlas of Existence burning around him as his plans and contingencies came to a great success!

|All traces of the Intruders are gone, Master. A Unique Tool of unknown ranking and origins aids the so-called Protector as its capabilities to analyze Existence are extremely high.|

The voice of the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed echoed out as around him, the gazes of his people were filled with anger and solemness.

But Noah…had a profound expression on his face as he had achieved a major victory!

Sensing that the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed would be something hard to conceal under the gazes of these beings, he left it out in plain sight.

But only in a way that he wanted to. He left it out in plain sight, but when the Protector discovered it, the only thing they were able to discern was that it was a Plane of Existence!

The grandiose Aletheian Havens…we're able to escape the gaze of these terrifying beings that he wasn't ready for.

"These are the enemies we're going to be dealing with for the foreseeable future."

His voice was calm as he spoke towards his Son and all others here.

His Existence was analyzing the results of how effective the Authority of the Laborer was in veiling the weavings of Existence as he continued.

"They are currently more advanced than us, and they hold much greater numbers as just that single Protector alone is not someone I can guarantee the death of at this time, much the hundreds, or thousands, or tens of thousands of others that may be just like him."

They had very little information as of right now.

They didn't know much about the behemoth that was the Commonwealth and what its forces entailed, but one thing was clear!

"They hold sway over Sources and Systems of Existence as one of their stipulations…"

Noah waved his hands.

He recreated the Contract of Existence that Protector Antonio had brought before them.

There was a class among many that stated.

|Attempts to study other Systems of Existence or to emulate them are expressly forbidden. Should anyone be discovered even attempting the adoption of other Systems of Existence apart from the one belonging to their Native Omniverse, they will be stripped of their Citizenship and judged in the Courts of Existence for Execution. The purity of separate Sources of Existence must be maintained to ensure the equilibrium and longevity of all Existence- this being one of the core missions of the Commonwealth.

A single stipulation among many.

But this stipulation showed the terrifying reality of something Noah was already breaking! Within his Atlas of Existence.

Within his First Mundi of Power, there were the weavings of three other Sources of Existence apart from his own Conceptual System of Existence!

But it wasn't discovered.

"Everything we do will be in secret as we learn more about these Entities entering our Omniverse. The vastness of their Commonwealth, their numbers, their power. We will learn, adapt, and in time…"

HUUM!

His eyes burned brightly.

He could see a grand future ahead even with the challenges that would come alongside it.

"For now, continue seeking recognition of the Atlas of Existence. Until you all gain access to the Authority of the Laborer, you cannot sufficiently veil yourselves and move about freely in front of these beings."

…!

He spoke out simply, yet the gazes of all those here changed as if they could feel the injuries they had suffered before rising up again.

Erikson cleared his throat as he spoke out hesitantly.

"Is it…possible to tell your other self to go a bit easier on us?"

His other self.

The Atlas of Existence itself!

Noah smiled while shaking his head at this, his Will expanding and entering the dazzling Atlas of Existence within him.

His vision changed gradually as he found himself standing on top of an endless blue sea.

Above it, Nine glorious circles of light flashed as at the very center, a colorless singularity spun with unfathomable brilliance.

In the center of this singularity, a figure that looked just like him with white hair could be seen.

His eyes opened just as Noah arrived, rising from the center of the Atlas of Existence as by the time Noah took a single step, he was right before this manifestation!

A manifestation that kneeled before him an instant later.

"Master."

HUUM!

His Atlas.

His wonder.

It came out of his wildest designs as it was something under his control, but it didn't mean he understood everything about it.

It didn't mean he could predict all the surprises that would come from it in the future!

Just the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed, what lay ahead was a wonderful waiting to unfold.

Noah looked deeply at himself- at his Atlas as he commanded out.

"Let's see what analysis can be made regarding this Protector Antonio and his Source of Existence."

With steady steps and a careful weaving of his Will, Noah continued to prepare for a future full of possibilities and perils.

As he looked into the entity known as Protector Antonio, grand changes were beginning to be enacted within the Omniverse starting at a small area!

The place where Protector Antonio had appeared minutes ago had been dark and void of anything, but at this moment.. titanic structures had already begun to be enacted as from the major ones…a flag adorned with an interminable Wheel made itself present!
