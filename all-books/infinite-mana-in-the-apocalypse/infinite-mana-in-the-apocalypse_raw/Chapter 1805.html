Chapter 1803 The Tyrannical Emperor's Drastic Plan! l

The trekking into this new area seemed to be for the talented as in one region, a party of Dragon Phoenixes had occupied and began to ascend through one Chain of Reality.

Their bodies gave off a majestic glow of power and grandeur as their eyes reflected the majesty of destiny far above them, but as they were ascending…

BOOOM!

An explosion akin to a bomb shot out behind them as an existence smashed into the change of skies to water at shocking speeds, causing surging waves of sea storms to surge out and pushing even shaking the nearby Chains of Reality as alarm rose in the minds of all those in the surroundings!

When they turned their heads to observe who could act so wildly, they saw the visage of an existence clad in gold Armament all around, pairs of wings flapping below her feet and behind her back as unlike all others who sank when entering the dreary seas filled with oppression…she floated calmly as when the wings below her feet buzzed, she even began to fly upwards as her body released a unique authority.

…!

"This…"

The scalps of those watching tingled as the majestic Dragon Phoenixes on the Chain of Reality looked with unfathomable gazes and envy as one spoke out.

"Akaris…"

The existence that had spoken calmly to even Alpha Strata LEGENDS as her word had started the quick entrance into the Grotto Sanctum.

She didn't seem too horribly phased by the restrictions and oppression around her as her body gave off wave after wave of glowing might, the many pairs of wings on her buzzing with power as she slowly ascended upwards- but at a much faster rate compared to those nearby struggling up the Chains of Reality!

What authority did she have to be able to do this when even TABOO Authorities were not enough to allow one to move freely?!

The leading Dragon Phoenix gritted his crystalline sharp teeth as he continued up with even more vigor, telling himself there were some beings he shouldn't compare himself to as the identity of Akaris was too special even in the Main Reality.

In another region, the allure of a silver white haired young woman took light but quick steps up the Chains of Reality, her body giving off a silvery mirror like glow that buzzed with the chain below and allowed her a level of freedom- causing her beautiful expression to continue gazing at the chain with surprise as she continued to ascend upwards.

Mirror constantly bloomed and shattered to protect her from the heavy restrictions as her speed…was no less than that of Akaris as she went up towards the far away glowing land masses!

Countless Geniuses and Emperors began to arrive in a new region of the Grotto Sanctum as they faced a cold and brutal atmosphere, each one using their own means to ascend upwards as undoubtedly, there was one being in the lead that was moving with utmost haste!

—

In the Infinite Reality.

Vibrant rivers of Realities layered themselves atop the Infinite Reality and underwent Apocalypse and Assimilation, their Cosmos fusing into this wondrous domain as incandescent True Stars of Conquest constantly formed and rushed down towards the slumbering visage of a single being!

The veils of the Cosmic Holy Land seemingly didn't exist as the milky white stars rushed down with glorious purpose, Noah's slumbering figure having its eyelids constantly vibrating as more and more portions of Souls sank back into him.

His progress in the Third Infinite Dream could be considered astounding to say the least as Emerging Realities took mere seconds for him to reign in the Cosmos and then pull the Realities towards the Infinite Reality, and this record pace of progress gave birth to a ridiculous phenomenon!

Since over 10,000 Portions of Souls were sent out, those who were destined to find Emerging Realities found them relatively quickly as with the seconds of domination Noah did for each of these Realities…he did it all simultaneously.

It also meant that when they were returning back to the Infinite Reality with their dominated Realities in tow, a relatively short period of time would have passed! This created for a situation where hundreds of Realities were layering themselves atop Noah's domain and undergoing Apocalypse, a True Sanguine Clone gazing at the destruction and Assimilation with immense focus as within Noah's slumbering Origin…two concepts were bubbling with majestic fervor!

The Nomological Edicts of Apocalypse and the Tyrannical Emperor.

Every time a Reality perished and was assimilated, the concept of Apocalypse buzzed with a light of Obsidian glory.

Every time that the thousands of Cosmos of a Reality merged into the Infinite Reality and its inhabitants came under the will of the Tyrannical Emperor, this concept also buzzed with a light of glory!

These two evolvable concepts were currently buzzing wildly with their authority as from Quasi-TABOO, they began to make their way towards TABOO.

And they were forging towards TABOO rapidly as within minutes, the Third Infinite Dream had already proceeded to have over 3,000 Realities assimilated.

The Bloodlines of these concepts also burned brightly within Noah's Blood as they experienced a qualitative change, beginning to forge upwards in quality as they didn't seem to be staying at the Tier of Supreme anymore!

Under the banner of glorious Conquest, thousands of True Stars of Conquest also rained down as the milky white light of Conquest painted everything, making the area around Noah seem like a holy land that others should not even be near at this moment.

The more time that passed, the faster the shocking phenomenon of the assimilated Realities became as the 4,000th True Star of Conquest came down…then the 4,100th…4,500th…and within mere minutes that followed- over 5,000 True Stars of Conquest had accumulated within Noah.

"..."

An astounding stellar glow also began to bloom at this moment, but it didn't even come from Noah!

It came from all areas of the Infinite Reality.

This was because at this same time, the number of fused Realities into this fantastical domain had long since exceeded the halfway point of the requirement of 10,000 fused Realities before it could proceed forward to an entirely different level!

HUUM!

When this time came, Noah's will began to surge.

His main body remained slumbering, but a True Sanguine Clone rose up to take on an astounding task.

Noah's body occupied the Throne of Unnamed Tyranny as the True Sanguine Clone came to occupy the Throne of Arcadian Tyranny, these two multicolored thrones coming close while facing opposite each other until they came in contact!

The connection between Noah and his Clones was filled with vibrant clarity as at this moment, the stellar eyes of his Clone released beams of light as he spoke out.𝒇𝒓𝙚𝒆𝔀𝓮𝓫𝒏𝓸𝙫𝓮𝓵.𝓬𝙤𝙢

<Nomological Regime>.

…!

The Regime of the Tyrannical Emperor was pulled upon!

Then, the domineering Will spoke out grandly as an illustrious white gold milky light began to spread.

"Utilize 5,000 True Stars of Conquest to elevate…the Resplendent Treasure Emperor."

WAA!

Incandescent light began to explode out…but Noah's will was still stirring vibrantly as he pulled upon the authority of the Infinite Reality Passages!

A wild thought and destiny had bloomed within his mind these past days as he knew the elevation of the Infinite Reality was coming. And after the things he learned while in the Unexplored Grotto Sanctum, his thoughts and ideas became further solidified.

Now that the time was approaching…he got ready for it as the number of Realities would very soon exceed 10,000!

When that time came…

The blooming thoughts within Noah's mind alone began to cause bursts of Destiny, Fate, Karma, and Fortune to bloom as the actions that followed thereafter would be even more disastrous and unbelievable.

Lavalliere seemed to have sensed these changes as well, this vibrant treasure becoming roused up as in the next instant, genuine shock could be heard from her as she voiced out!

<You…what are you planning to do?!>

WAA!
