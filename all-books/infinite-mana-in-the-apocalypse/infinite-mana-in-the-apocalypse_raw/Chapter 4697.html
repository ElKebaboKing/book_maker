Chapter 4695: Moirai I

Noah looked at THE Emerged Devourer with a sense of pity.

A Malformed Ginnu Lifeform who was imprisoned this whole time, and at the time of freedom, they were too weak to fight against THE Fallout.

SCREE!

THE Emerged Devourer still continued to bash its entire body on the barrier, its Depth heavy and profound as it was rapidly eating away at the nearby Logos on THE Infinite Loom. Each impact sent ripples of destruction through the carefully constructed defenses, the creature’s maddened hunger dissolving the meanings that held the barrier together.

It was causing an unnecessary expenditure of his home as Noah began to float toward it.

Pity was pity, but he would not hold back.

He was on a path. He was streamlining his Way and abilities as at this moment, he wanted to feel his Depth freely.

Eighteenth of the Immensity of THE Creature at THE Intermediate Depth? Okay.

What did that translate to? His Way was at Surface Depth as it could Assert Mana or Hunger in unique ways, but if he held Intermediate Depth immensity, then his Assertions would automatically be elevated to Impositions.

He wanted to feel with his body closely as he expressed his immensity. How the Logos within him moved. How the Philologies flowed.

In Ginnungagap, he used The Goad and Logos of Imprisonment to gradually corner THE Surface Depth entities until they could no longer exert any power of their Depth, and while in such a state, he took them into his prison.

But this was when he had not even formed his Depth yet. He had the Depth at this moment, and even though he was at THE Surface Depth in his Way and Civilization, he wanted to see how he would measure up to this Intermediate Depth creature that was mutated from THE Fallout.

And so he took a step forward.

As he did so, his skin glinted with Logos and Philologies of The First Tongue. Every weight and immensity of his existence surrounded his entire body, held together tightly as blue-gold light permeated around him like a glow of brilliance. He looked like a humanoid entity that was set aflame with stellar blue-gold brilliance, each Phoneme burning across his form like stars embedded in flesh!

As if THE Emerged could sense a level of immensity from him, it actually backed away for him to come out of the barrier. Its gaze was that of fervor and mad excitement while looking at him, as if it were looking at a chicken that had come out to be cooked and eaten. Saliva that burned with primordial hunger dripped from its impossible maw.

The moment Noah left the confines of THE Infiniverse to appear in the landscape of THE Fallout...

HUUM!

His entire existence vibrated as he felt waves of resonance cascading all around.

|CRITICAL HARVEST DETECTION|

|For stepping out into the expansive, corrupted, and altered Weavings of Observable Existence, Perpetual Harvest has briefly sensed the boundless Infinite Potential that should have been waiting for you.|

|Sources detected:|

|- All the changes that The First Tongue underwent during THE Fallout|

|- All the advancements that those who followed your Way or backed you within THE Prison of THE Creature underwent|

|- The Shelters of THE Loom making themselves visible within Observable Existence, allowing their Weavings of Existence to run free|

|- All the usage of Mana and The First Tongue across the eons that was carried out to maintain THE Loom and by those within it|

|All of this boundless harvest is barely being sensed at this moment due to the altered Weavings of Observable Existence.|

|Note: If you did not have nine newly forged Phonemes reinforced in The First Tongue, this harvest would never have been collected.|

|The Philology of Perpetual Harvest, backed by your newly forged Phonemes of The First Tongue, promises you that it will ensure every scrap of harvest is accounted for and given to you.|

|Infinite Potential begins to be gathered, however slow and however rigorous, until every ounce that belongs to you is given.|

...!

Noah sensed and heard these prompts as he felt invisible waves of brilliance like an endless sea begin to surge above him and trickle down into his Foundation and Civilization.

He accepted this calmly as he trusted in the Philologies that he was advancing at this moment, that every ounce of what was his would be given!

But he kept his gaze on the Gulper Eel.

At this moment, an ashen color of its Depth erupted around it like heavy strikes of lightning as it looked toward Noah with an immense sense of greed and want. It radiated the immensity of its Depth toward him as if to bring him to submission so he could seamlessly be enjoyed and eaten.

Its mental state and Way seemed entirely far too gone. It could not even be saved. Anything that made it a Malformed Ginnu Lifeform had truly already been stripped away.

What a pity.

The creature’s Way expressed itself as Primordial Consumption, a regressed, bestial version of hunger that predated sophisticated concepts of devouring. It did not merely want to eat Noah. It wanted to unmake him, to return his complex existence to raw, undifferentiated sustenance.

As its weight and immensity surged toward Noah, he erupted with his own Way in response.

Blue-gold tendrils of lightning unfolded from his existence, crackling with the authority of Mana and Hunger intertwined. They clashed with the ashen bolts of the Gulper Eel’s Depth in the space between them.

HUUM!

The collision was cataclysmic.

Reality itself groaned as two immensities met in the corrupted air. The ashen clouds around them were blown back by the shockwave, revealing glimpses of the gray wasteland that stretched endlessly in all directions. Lightning of blue-gold and lightning of primordial ash intertwined and fought for dominance, each tendril seeking to overwhelm the other.

The ground beneath them cracked! The falling ash was vaporized in a sphere around their clash.The very Weavings of Existence trembled as two Depths contested for supremacy!

And shockingly, Noah’s Depth and the Depth of this creature were almost equal. 𝚏𝐫𝚎𝗲𝕨𝐞𝐛𝕟𝚘𝐯𝚎𝗹.𝕔𝐨𝗺

Both sides were dispersed.

Noah looked at such a scene and nodded.

At THE Surface Depth, he could disperse the Depth of an Intermediate Depth entity.

He looked at the Gulper Eel as it was not deterred. In fact, it looked at him with even more greed as at this moment, its Way began to twist. Noah sensed this creature was about to utilize Imposition that THE Intermediate Depth of Absolute Sovereignty entities had access to.

The Gulper Eel lit up with a sickly primordial glow as reality around it began to warp. Its maw opened impossibly wide as it sought to impose that the enemy in front of it was already cut up, already prepared, already ready for consumption.

Noah saw this and prepared to counter with his own Imposition when he became astonished.

This was not an Imposition!
