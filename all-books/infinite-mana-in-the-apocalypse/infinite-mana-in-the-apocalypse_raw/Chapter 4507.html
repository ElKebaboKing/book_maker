Chapter 4505: The Infinite Unfurling! II

When Noah thought about the terrifying capabilities of the Unquantifiable Unit of Everything and what it could do, many other possibilities flooded his mind.

His thoughts wandered to his Early Atlas of the Folds, to the feature that had always held immense promise and danger in equal measure.

Temporal Traversal!

He always knew how desperately he needed more knowledge regarding existence, and one of the foremost places to attain this knowledge was The Earliest Folds themselves!

If he used the product of THE Organ of Civilization to power up his Temporal Traversal, how far back could he go? How pristine would the environment be? What secrets could he uncover?

The more he thought about it, the more his eyes shone brightly.

And then, without his conscious command...

|The Unquantifiable Units of Everything deem this action worthy of use. One Unquantifiable Unit of Everything has flown into your Atlas.|

...!

Noah’s eyes opened wide with alarm as he tried to speak.

"Hey, hold on!"

He was only just thinking about this as an abstract possibility! He wasn’t ready yet to actually execute such a journey!

And yet, his existence seemed to have found something it could not let go of, as if his very being had made a decision independent of his conscious mind. Even his THE Organ of Civilization buzzed with urgent resonance!

And before he could do anything else to stop the process...

|THE Organ of Civilization unequivocally agrees with your line of thought. Unforeseen dangers have been detected with this Temporal Traversal due to your Existence being inherently different compared to all previous times. 2 additional Unquantifiable Units of Everything have flown into your Atlas. An Absolute Civilizational Temporal Traversal is beginning.|

...!

A rapid flashing of prompts appeared and disappeared in microseconds!

And right after...

WAP!

Noah disappeared from the Cultivation Citadel entirely!

A moment after he vanished, The Infiniverse appeared where he used to be. Her existence pulsed with newfound power from the recent empowerment, and the illusory figure of RUIN/EDEN materialized alongside her.

"Something unexpected occurred and he is going through Temporal Traversal rather early," RUIN/EDEN said with concern coloring her voice. "It seems to be unlike any other time before. His entire existence was pulled through."

The Infiniverse nodded at these words as she gazed at the empty space where Noah had been burning.

"Everything is as it can be," she said with calm acceptance. "Existence will continue to be unpredictable, and we can only continue to do what we can. Take care of him there."

After such words, The Infiniverse disappeared to attend to the countless tasks that required her attention!

---

A few seconds ago.

In the Injunction Sanctuary of Law.

Deborah stood before all remaining members of the Civilizational Legion of Law, her posture rigid with the weight of command. She gazed at the flaming bodies of Noah and Ozymandias with complex emotions.

At this moment, millions of Primus Inevitabilities surrounded them in protective formations alongside Aethon and his Civilizational Legion of Elementals who had sworn new allegiance.

All Emotives had been collapsed and mainly devoured by Ozymandias in this terrifying battle, and many still couldn’t believe how things had unfolded so dramatically.

At this moment, Deborah was about to take out the Shattered Part of The Heart of Law to give to Alexander when...

WAP!

Noah and Ozymandias’s flaming figures disappeared without warning! Ozymandias actually turned into a streak of light that shot across the space and disappeared into Noah’s body before both vanished completely!

Alexander and the others watched in astonishment as Noah simply ceased to exist before them.

Khor frowned for a moment with concern before she steadied herself as if she had received news through their connection. She turned to address the assembled forces.

"Continue with the mission," she said with absolute authority. "The Outsider is simply taking care of other matters."

Other matters.

Very few knew the ultimate truth about what had just occurred!

---

Unlike before, unlike previous Temporal Traversals where Noah could send a single body or clone and keep other instances behind to maintain presence...

This Temporal Traversal had taken every single ounce of his being.

As of this moment, Noah Osmont did not exist in this era at all!

Across weavings of existence.

Noah’s expression was solemn as the transition occurred, but he rapidly adapted his thinking. He understood that 3 Unquantifiable Units of Everything had been consumed to power up what must be a ridiculous Temporal Traversal!

An Absolute Civilizational Temporal Traversal at that.

He observed his surroundings with careful attention and found himself surrounded by an intense and immense storm of existence that felt much wilder and more primal than any previous Temporal Traversal.

New prompts bloomed before his vision.

|ABSOLUTE CIVILIZATIONAL TEMPORAL TRAVERSAL - ACTIVE|

|Previously, your existence did not contain even a single iota of THE. You were able to freely use the unimaginable feature of Temporal Traversal with your Atlas as this alone was an impossibility.|

|When paired with a fairly weak entity utilizing such a feature, you were able to pass through the temporal gaps without being noticed by THE Living Temporal.|

|Current Status: At this moment, you hold THE Organ of Civilization. Even though your power is heading towards the right direction, you are still considered weak while holding an aspect budding with THE.|

|Warning: It is significantly harder to pass through the gaps unnoticed. THE Living Temporal has a high chance of discovering your movements.|

|Solution Implemented: For the sake of caution, the utility of The Unquantifiable Units of Everything has allowed your existence to traverse much further back than before, to a time before THE Living Temporal had much influence on the Temporal Weavings of Existence. You have been dropped in a region that would grant you the highest chance of survival, near Weavings of Existence that match your Civilization.|

|Current Location: You have arrived before The Earliest Folds. Extreme caution advised.|

...!

The prompts were heavy with implications. Moments after they bloomed and faded, Noah’s existence pulsed with terrifying luminosity. His Sovereign Spires resonated with each other in harmonic frequencies until...

BOOM!

He felt space break and reform around him before darkness and light folded and collapsed in patterns. And he instantly found himself elsewhere entirely.

...!

The moment he arrived, waves of ancient authority began to flood into his existence like water filling a vessel.

He found himself standing on what appeared to be an extremely tall crystalline obsidian mountain peak that stretched upward for what might have been miles. Misty clouds that even obscured his enhanced vision permeated all around in thick layers.

In the far distance, other massive crystalline obsidian mountain peaks could barely be seen through the mist. Some seemed to be growing upside down, defying conventional understanding of gravity and orientation!

The skies above and the supposed earth below were both filled with rising obsidian crystalline mountains in configurations of utter glory. Some mountains floated freely in the air, rotating slowly. Others grew in spirals that twisted through multiple dimensions.

The domain around him felt like his existence could continue drinking in the atmosphere forever and never be satisfied. It felt ancient beyond measure, old in a way that transcended simple time!

The air itself carried weight, each breath filling his lungs with authority that had existed since before existence learned to organize itself.

Between the mountain peaks, he could see what looked like rivers of liquid light flowing upward and downward simultaneously, defying direction. Crystalline structures that might have been buildings or might have been natural formations dotted some of the peaks in the distance.

The mist itself seemed alive, pulsing with patterns that suggested consciousness or at least awareness.

And everywhere, everywhere, there was the sensation of potential. As if this place existed before things had been decided, before reality had committed to specific rules!

New prompts rose a moment later.

|You have arrived in a place and time that THE Organ of Civilization has quantified as The Infinite Unfurling.|

|Definition: The period before The Earliest Folds solidified into distinct realities.|

|Critical Warning: The entirety of your existence is present in this location. You have no backup of yourself remaining in your home era.|

|Extreme Caution Required: You must move with immense care. The littlest thing has the potential to unmake your existence completely.|

|The rules of reality here are not yet fixed. What is impossible in your era may be commonplace here, and vice versa.|

...!

The tiniest thing had the potential to unmake his existence completely!

And he was here with all of his being. Ozymandias was merged inside this body, and as he stood on the obsidian crystalline mountain peak, his figure was like a candle flame in the vast darkness.

Crimson-gold flames of THE Immolation Of The Sacrilegious Savage still burned around him, marking him as something foreign in this ancient place!

How should he go about exploring this environment without triggering catastrophe?

"Ho? What’s this?"

...!

A booming yet calm voice filled with profound immensity echoed out across the mountain peak.

Without Noah noticing any approach or movement, a humanoid figure peeled back the layers of existence in front of him as if he had always been standing there this whole time and Noah simply couldn’t perceive him until now.

He showed himself fully and looked at Noah up and down with analytical interest burning in eyes that held abyssal folds!

...!

Noah’s expression became unfathomably heavy as he stared at this humanoid being whose voice continued to echo across the mountains.

The figure was tall and lean, his form seemingly composed of shifting shadows and hungry voids. His skin looked like night sky filled with dying stars. His eyes were black holes that consumed light rather than reflected it.

And around him, space itself bent inward as if reality wanted to be devoured by his mere presence.

"I was about to take a bite of you, you know," the entity said with casual menace. "You feel like something that does not belong here. Something... displaced."

He tilted his head with curious intensity.

"Who are you, Outsider? And why do you give off the scent of my Hunger?" 𝘧𝑟𝑒𝑒𝘸𝘦𝘣𝑛𝑜𝘷𝑒𝓁.𝘤𝘰𝓂

...!

A profound and terrifying sense of suffocation spread out from the entity like a physical force. The pressure made Noah’s newly elevated 63 Septillion Complexity feel insignificant!

The entity before Noah burned with the unmistakable allure of THE.

He had met a THE Existence mere seconds after his entry into a place before The Earliest Folds, into The Infinite Unfurling itself!

But for good or bad, he could say with certainty that he knew this being!
