Chapter 1334 - The Birth Of The Infinite Empire! II

A scene of a kingly enthroned figure and a woman trying to kneel towards him, her gaze glancing at him and him alone as such an image etched itself into the minds of all those watching for the rest of their lives!

Springforge looked at this scene as she sighed with frustration, her gaze going towards the mesmerizing being on the throne as she gritted her teeth.

They were already here.

There was no going back at this point!

With her head raised, the past Family Head with millions of Universes in her Origin and a self discovered Edict called out elegantly.

"I swear Fealty to the Tyrannical Emperor!"

THRUM!

Waves of fate and destiny coalesced as those behind her looked at their own leader speak such words, Daolords like Azazel who had appeared before Noah when he first arrived at the Indigo Cosmos looking at this scene in a surreal fashion as after some hesitation…they too followed.

"I swear Fealty to the Tyrannical Emperor…" Hegemonies, Antiquities, Daolords…and even the few Forefathers in the surroundings all gritted their teeth as they followed the example of their leader and pledged Fealty to a single man!

Destiny and fortune flew around wildly, especially on the figure situated on the purple golden throne as he raised Halcyon to look at him straight, his voice echoing out.

"Now I can invite you all to my home…"

BZZT!

Mana colorfully waved around and wrapped onto every one of them, their figures disappearing from this region of the Ruination Sea as they were transported vast distances towards another area!

When they all came to be and gazed at the new sight around them, they found that even though they were still on the Ruination Sea, there was a nigh endless object that reminded them of something they were used to seeing for much of their lives before their very eyes.

It seemed similar, but it had a fantastical beauty as before their eyes, they saw a cluster of glimmering transparent Cosmic Barriers that seamlessly contacted each other, the myriad of Universes within each of these Cosmos being able to be clearly seen as around all of these Cosmos…a vast transparent barrier wrapped around them protectively!

It was a cluster of dozens of Cosmos as it reminded them of the Primordial Empire on a smaller scale, but the scintillating white light released from its glimmering barriers that were so transparent between the Cosmos made them think they were looking at something else entirely.

"I welcome you all to my Infinite Cosmos…or more aptly now, we should call it the Infinite Empire!"

WAA!

The booming voice of Noah took them out of their stupor as he waved his hands once more, the figures of everyone flashing away as they were sucked into the unique floating treasure that had grown enough to be termed an Empire!

They were sucked into the main Cosmos of the newly titled Infinite Empire, the Primordial Cosmos that Noah's Dark Universe stemmed from as all of them were transported into the Novus Universe that held Dao Galaxies of verdant Dao Fruits and Herbs among many other things.

"Soon to exceed 100 Cosmos in total, it's the place I call home as well as the place that will be the true Bastion of Reality! The place that if all else falls on our Reality…it will be the last line of defense!"

The booming voice of Noah shook all the new entrants out of their reverie as they looked around them, the eye catching scene of Noah situated on a throne taken in by them as around him, they could see the figures of stupendously shining women gazing their way- the one at the lead being Adelaide as Barbatos and others flanked her left and right.

Behind them, the figures of Noah's summons, Oathkeeper, Kazuhiko, Steel Mikhail, the Emperor Penguin, Cthulhu…and many more could be seen on top of the newly transported King Augustus Marcus Tiberius and his family from the Indigo Cosmos!

The new and old met each other as Springforge and Halcyon took in this scene with shining eyes, their auras billowing out to wrap around this Universe and the Cosmos it was in as well as the numerous other Cosmos surrounding it as they learned about their new environment.

Noah gave them a few seconds to adjust as within this time, he also reflected on the closure of the Nexus Event and what it meant for him from here!

He had obtained enough loot that he had to absorb and achieve a halfway mark towards the Cosmic Realm, he had completed two Edicts at the same time as he opened the pathway of Bloodline of Edicts that allowed him to generate the True Blood of Higher Existences to elevate himself to this stage…he had obtained a small clump of the essence of Reality through the Blue Slime as from the Bloodline of Kismet, he had unlocked <<Author's Jurisdiction>> as he could quite literally edit aspects of Reality as long as he obtained enough Essence of Reality!

He had to consult with the Edict of Kismet for the best path forward for him to take…on whether he would utilize the small clump of the essence of Reality later when he collected more or at this moment to complete more Edicts or edit other aspects of Reality for himself, Hyperion, or even Aegon.

There were many things for him to do as many boons were gained from a short period of time, and Noah had to now absorb them and elevate himself higher while still making plans for obtaining even more!

As for the next Treasure chest that he wanted to dip into?

'An Ender of Reality with reserves of the Essence of Reality trying to forge a Primordial Epitaph and bring forth a System…'

It was an expansive and dangerous treasure chest that Noah would more than likely still poke in the future!

He thought of all of these things and more as after a few seconds of Halcyon and Springforge recalibrating, the Primordial Ruination Clone tapped his finger upon his throne as he gathered the attention of all, his nearby surroundings flashing with a spatial light as his Universal Body appeared.

…!

The body that only those in the Infinite Cosmos had seen- the body that Halcyon and Springforge as well as all others from the Primordial Empire had yet to see showed itself in all of its glory!

It was the size of multiple galaxies as it looked extremely stellar and ancient, weaker beings seeing his Universal form for the first time nearly blanking out in awe as Springforge gazed towards him with her jaws nearly falling off the void of space.

Halcyon's eyes glimmered with intense light as her golden eyes reflected this scene, everyone watching this shining stellar being with billions of Universes flowing through his body as his voice reverberated out.

"I welcome you all onto a place that began as a mere island floating in a blue sky."

THRUM!

Noah recalled the Spiritual Land of a treasure he had obtained from the Beast World, his eyes filled with millions of Universes releasing rays of glorious light as he continued.

"A place that began as a mere island and then became a world, a Galaxy, a Cosmos, and now…a cluster of Cosmos! I welcome you all today for the proclamation and birth of the Infinite Empire!"

WAA!

With his body suffusing multicolored light all around and in a purely fantastical form that drew the awe of all, Noah started off a new chapter of a complicated journey with the proclamation of the Infinite Cosmos into the Infinite Empire!
