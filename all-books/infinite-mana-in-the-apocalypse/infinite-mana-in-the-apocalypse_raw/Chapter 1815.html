BZZZT!

The blue flames of the Abyssal Undead Lioness gazed on in a stupor as it felt like it hit an impenetrable wall that it couldn't pass through no matter what!

This was an Innate Primogenial Defense Barrier that only LEGENDS normally had, but Noah had attained it after exceeding his Limits as it passively wrapped around him with half of the maximum Defense Values he could reach with his strongest form.

Currently…even this half had long since exceeded 10 Million Defense Values as now, it was even tainted by True Dimensional Defensive Values!

BOOM!

The body of the Undead Beast was rebuffed as its silver gray bony aurora of wings kept it floating in the cold seas, gazing at its sharp bony claws and back to the small Royal Human below that was still gazing up with a smile.

"..."

An eerie silence came as even the two hiding Abyssal Undead Tigris didn't come out, the most powerful Abyssal Undead just staring dazedly at Noah as it didn't seem to know what to do!

"Hmm…"

Yet the Tyrannical Emperor nodded as 30 clear white Ascendancy Halos erupted around him- causing the wills of the Abyssal Undead Beasts to swirl with utter disbelief at seeing the weakest type of Halo erupt from this being- and all 30 at that!

The Abyssal Undead Beasts wanted to roar out to this being a unanimous set of words….

'As if we'd believe that!'

Yet Noah didn't even call upon the 10 extra Halos from LETHE as he fused with the authority of 30 Halos. 𝙛𝒓𝓮𝙚𝔀𝒆𝒃𝓷𝒐𝓿𝙚𝓵.𝙘𝒐𝒎

"Let's call out something simple to test some things out…the Infinite Willow Tree True Form should do."

…!

An incandescent light short from Noah as faced with an Abyssal Undead Beast with traces of the Niflheim Dimension that could rival a beginner LEGEND, he brought forth a portion of his power to see just how much he had grown!

—

As the Unnamed Kainos Emperor moved to quickly and efficiently terrorize monstrous Undead Beasts, other beings were also making progress towards the floating landmasses that Chains of Reality stretched down from.

Most had their goals to ascend upwards towards the depths of these fantastical cold seas, but others had their own aims and way of doing things!

Midway up one of the metallic Chains of Reality.

A figure was shrouded with a dark gray cloak that melded with the surroundings, but it couldn't hide her thick curves as one could see the outline of a great deal of things beneath this cloak!

This existence was uniquely lying flat on the Chain of Reality, her hands letting out a stellar sheen as she was of the Astral Bloodline…and at the same time not as a blue glow surrounded the Cosmos and stellar bodies shining throughout her body. This blue sheen had a unique signature that could only be found…from the Spirit Race.

A bright solidified crown with the ecritures of <DESO> shone brightly over her head as glimmering radiant hair was tied into a stellar bun.

Her vibrant hands were currently holding on to a shocking weapon that released waves of TABOO, a weapon that seemed to be towards the pinnacle of Technomancy as it glowed with incandescent purple and blue Runic ecritures etched onto an obsidian metallic weapon that had a long cylindrical barrel releasing a terrifying light- this Relic most closely resembling a sniper rifle as it held a vibrant tactical rifle scope that shone with a golden light!

The stellar blue eyes of this unique genius were looking through this scope as she locked on to Pure-Blooded Primordials climbing up the Chain of Reality, the golden scope shining a beautiful white light when it locked on to some beings while realizing a dreadful dark light when landing on others.

The meaning of these lights were only prevalent to this being as the barrel of her TABOO sniper gun focused on the existences that shone with a dark light.

"Little Barrett…let's kill some baddies."

WAA!

She stroked the rifle as it buzzed with terrific power, her finger beginning to squeeze the trigger as a light pull from her actually caused dense TABOO authority to surge into the gun as it aimed at a Royal Human from the Main Reality who was adorned in a solidified crown and climbing up with a cold expression!

The weapon in her hands released a terrific light as in the next instant…

SHAA!

<Nuclear Firmament Piercer>.

A burst of dark and red light flashed.

The curvaceous flesh of this woman trembled from the recoil as her blessed parts trembled.

A genius Emperor below then felt a tingling sensation of doom as before he could even raise his head…

WAP!

A terrifying bullet filled with TABOO authority entirely erased the whole upper half of his body, utterly eradicating his Aspects of Existence in a mere instant!

…!

Chaos bloomed in this region of the Chain of Reality as geniuses from behind looked at this in alarm, their Halos blooming out to fuse with them as in the next instant, the Empress far above the Chain of Reality pulled the trigger another two times as she targetted another two existences that were covered by a dark light on her scope.

PA! PA!

Like bubbles popping, the chest and Origin of a Dragon Phoenix on the same Chain of Reality was eradicated as a large hole appeared, and the majestic visage of a Reality Ursidae with a shining solidified crown had half of his body blown away before he could even react.

"..."

Essence exploded out as all those who had witnessed this shocking scene jumped off the Chain of Reality and used the oppressive force of the surroundings to carry them towards the earth as soon as possible, their faces grim as they didn't wish to die from an unknown enemy!

"Mmm…"

The visage of the existence carrying Astral and Spirit Race Bloodline rose as she breathed out lightly, hoisting the terrifying TABOO Relic behind her back as her will gazed at her solidified throne that was condensing the Ecriture <L>.

With a spring to her step, TABOO authority and Essence of Reality wrapped around her as she continued upwards- no competition behind the Chain of Reality she was on as nobody dared to ascend it!

This existence and a few others were some of those distinguished in this Unexplored Grotto Sanctum…and those that destiny was weaving around them most as each one moved separately at this moment, but those with astounding destinies were bound to meet and clash very soon!

—

On another one of the dreary Chains of Reality in a different region.

A figure clad in arcs of Aether walked steadily up the Chains of Reality, his body releasing dangerous waves of power at the stage of a LEGEND as he didn't seem like any normal beginner LEGEND!

There was actually someone beside him releasing similar fluctuations of shocking power, this being a man with a brown complexion and devilish handsomeness as he carried two sickles that dripped with a poisonous light.

"You're sure she has that type of body?"

The voice of this genius LEGEND that was in the Firmaments of Ascendancy not too long ago rang out towards Xian who replied with a smile of cruelty and power.

"Yes. And she's actually untouched even now as the only thing I've come to learn as a reason for this is that her father is an Alpha Strata LEGEND from the Royal Humans. But with the established Rules of Engagement here...we can move freely!"

WAA!

A devilish expression pervaded his face as he continued.

"Can you imagine having a Dimensional Royalty under you? After Dual Cultivating with her enough, you can attain the Mythical Meretricious Mirror Physique! Seeing her abundant power...you should have no problem draining more of her vital essence to attain this as well. Since she might be problematic, working together is naturally a solution..."

Mythical Meretricious Mirror Physique! Not a Natural Born Physique, but something that one gained when they took the vital essence of unique existences for themselves.

A look of carefulness and ingenuity crossed Xian's mind as he had learned from his last encounter with this being and how she had reflected his attack easily.

Thus, he sought to work with another being as after finding traces of Katelyn in this region, everything was all but a matter of time.

His eyes released a ferocious flash of Aether as he imagined the silver white hair and the glazed eyes of the arrogant woman below him, his heart already buzzing with excitement as he couldn't wait!
