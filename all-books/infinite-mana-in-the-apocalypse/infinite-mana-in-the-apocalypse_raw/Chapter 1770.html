Chapter 1768 Those Seeking the Throne! ll

"You think she'll be okay in that mysterious Grotto?"

The stellar LEGEND held on to the arm of her partner as she asked while looking at the vibrant young woman from afar.

"If our daughter is not okay among those going in, then nobody will be okay. Do not worry too much, we have given her everything as it is now time for her to forge her own destiny…with someone like her holding a genuine shot for the seat of the True Emperor!"

WAA!

Wondrous words came out from this husband and wife pair that didn't let out any waves of power- their stage belonging far above the Primogenial Strata as they should be a Strata or two Strata above this.

Yet their solidified crowns…didn't hold any spinning ecritures as they showed their Candidacy to become a True Emperor of this Age was lower than even their daughter!

The authority of an Emperor of an Age.

All beings fought for it as the higher in power one was, the harder it was to be distinguished!

Below the stage of Reality, one could forge TABOO Edicts, Exceed the Limitations of the Aspects of Existence, forge a Bloodline unique to that Age or Era…and many other profound things that would allow them to become distinguished and increase their authority.

But within the stage of Reality and for those standing in higher Strata…the things they had to do to become distinguished were insurmountable harder.

This was why it became an unwritten understanding that if you lived through an Age and rose to power without becoming a True Emperor…you had more than likely lost your qualifications to do so forever!

This was why those who had been suppressing and building their foundation waiting for the First Age of the Kainos Era to begin…were those who had the highest chance of holding the seat of the True Emperor.

Of course, you had the powerful existences in higher Stratas who wanted to defy everything as even though it was much harder for them…they could still attain authority so long as they targeted rising Emperors and stole what they had built up so far!

It was a terrifying reality to think about as this was one of the reasons that reaching the seat of the True Emperor was insurmountably difficult.

Yet the eyes of the couple watching a Limit Breaker rip apart a genuine LEGEND shone with utmost confidence as the father repeated.

"She can do it. She holds a genuine shot!"

WAA!

The weaving of destiny pulsed vibrantly as this scene faded as well.

In a location that Noah's True Sanguine Clone of Alexander was actually near.

In the depths of the Pure Blooded Domains of the Royal Humans- across multiple layers of restrictions and Bloodline tests that required one to hold the authority of the current Lineage of Bloodline Master and the authority of one…it was the domain space that held the Royal Treasury!

Ta. Ta. Ta.

The light steps of Genevieve could be heard as she walked along a stellar golden road floating in space that was lined up on all sides by floating glimmering treasures.

Crystals, gems, jewels, weapons, and many more things filled with terrifying waves of energy could be seen as this was one of the treasure vaults of a Pure-Blooded Cardinal Royal Bloodline!

One of the Treasure Vaults of a Bloodline that had lived for eons- it was something multiple times larger than Noah's Expansive Space as if he could get his hands on something like this...

Items releasing waves of Sui Generis Relics, Quasi-TABOO Relics, and even hidden TABOO Relics could be felt the deeper one went as those gaining access to this place would be sending their wills out to attain the Quasi-TABOO or TABOO Relics.

But Genevieve had her clear eyes closed as she didn't sense any of this, but instead was silently resonating with something that wasn't in this Royal Treasury- but adjacent to this at an even deeper level that she wouldn't have easy access to unless she was the Bloodline Master or a powerful LEGEND of the Royal Humans!

It was in this space that her body and Origin were kept and drained of all of its resources since the past Age- picked apart and used to refine Relics and Elixirs as if she was a mere source of materials!

OOOM!

Anger rose within her mind when she thought of this, but her gaze refocused on the resonance of her old Body and Origin. She had many restrictions for the forbidden technique that allowed her to reincarnate after death, with the shackles binding her being heavy as if she had access to her old self- things would be a million times easier.

THUMP!

In the deeper Treasury she had no access to, the Origin of a Sacred Beast began to pulse silently as it resonated with its future self, Genevieve feeling a tug in her soul as the heavy chains binding her loosened ever so slightly…her will surging out as she pulled upon a vestige of her old self!

'More TABOO concepts…more of my Physiques!'

HOOONG!

Like a fisherman throwing out a line, Genevieve pulled and prodded as she returned back with terrifying concepts- a price still being paid for this even though it was minimal compared to her calling back her old concepts by herself.

She also had to be careful as even though vibrant authorities were silently surging into her, the visage of Brightborn wasn't too far away monitoring everything as she also had to put on a show and be quick about it!

TABOO authority leaked from her as she masked the incoming concepts with it, her hand pointing towards the depths of the Royal Treasury as a cry of a TABOO Relic resonated with her and began to come forward. It was normally a hard task for one to seek TABOO Relics within the Treasure as they would fall to those destined…but when the relics were made by her very own bones- she knew where every single one was.

WAA!

A resplendent white battle armor flowed out from the depths of the Royal Treasury as it flew towards Genevieve, the LEGEND Brightborn watching all this from afar while nodding his head- not realizing that this being had snatched multiple TABOO Concepts from her old Origin that they had locked away at the same time!

She repressed the realization of these concepts at this moment, or her solidified crown would have begun filling up with Ecritures due to her accomplishment. She only held a silent smile as an existence with multiple TABOO Concepts and Relics rose at this moment- and she had her own grand plans for how things would play out in the Unexplored Grotto Sanctum!

All of these beings felt a pull calling them to this Grotto Sanctum as if they wanted to explode with fortune and destiny, they felt like this was the place to be in.

A pull of destiny drew them all in.𝑓𝑟ℯ𝘦𝓌𝘦𝘣𝑛𝑜𝓋𝑒𝓁.𝑐ℴ𝓂

A weaving of destiny concocted for an event that the results of were unknown!

—

In the Cosmic Holy Land.

Noah's body was surrounded by surging essences as things calmed down, his solidified crown shining gloriously as his body was surrounded by pulsing Runic Ecritures from all his Physiques.

In addition to MANA, PURE…and many others, IDYLL was now added as the Ecriture for the Limit Breaking Arcadian Physique- this ecriture washing Noah's body with regenerative boosts constantly for his vitality as he always felt like he was brimming with life!

His sparkling eyes opened and closed wondrously as he watched the last prompt that came with the boons of breaking all of his three Aspects of Existence.

WAA!

!

It was a concept Noah only saw on the prompt as he couldn't discern anything about it- with the word lying within its name as it reflected the wonders of the Realm of Reality.

Noah was marveling at it when the sonorous voice of Lavallier rang out.

…!

She spoke out nonchalantly as Noah's eyes turned to the glimmering cerulean page screaming of wonder and ancientness not too far away from him!

A Modus Operandi designed just for him. Something that reflected the rotation of the Infinite Reality he held- and would continue to evolve along with the Infinite Reality.

When Noah was dreaming of this Infinite Runic Ecriture of Modus Operandi, there was one aspect aside from Reality Passages that he wanted to actualize to become reality the most.

It was a concept closely interconnected with his Resplendent Treasure Emperor that allowed him to loot his enemies and break them down into Bloodlines, Concepts, and Cores based on the level of their Origin.

This concept stemmed from the very workings of the Infinite Reality where at its base, it could bring down apocalypse and assimilate other Realities onto itself infinitely!

What Noah wanted to test and bring to reality was the idea that if his future Reality rotated and worked in a similar manner to the Infinite Reality…would he be capable of pairing this up with his current means of bringing about loot and be capable of swallowing the Reality level Origins of other beings seamlessly to elevate himself and not only be limited to things like Grotto Nacres?!

That apart from swallowing their Edicts and Bloodlines, he took their entire Strata as he assimilated it onto himself?!

…!!!
