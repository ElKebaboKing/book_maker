Chapter 4146 Who Is He?! I

When it came to what path Noah would take, it was actually a very simple one.

The complex analysis, the strategic considerations…all of them were interesting exercises in possibility, but Noah had never been one to overthink when his instincts pointed toward an obvious solution.

As both he and the Infiniverse shimmered with seventeen ready-to-use Seed Glyphs of the Principle of Perpetual Harvest between them, the path forward crystallized with perfect clarity.

"An Endowment for Ruination."

The words had barely left his lips when the response came. The green-gold clouds that still roiled above the Early Veiled Shore seemed to concentrate, condensing above Ruination's position like reality itself was taking aim!

A pillar of light descended with the force of recognition demanding expression.

Ruination stood within that pillar with her characteristic stillness, the crimson light that usually emanated from her form now mixing with the green-gold in patterns that suggested endings being transformed into beginnings.

Her complexity and purity surged upward with violence that would have shattered lesser beings, but she simply accepted it, absorbed it, made it part of her nature to ruin and remake.

But even as this process continued its magnificent course, Noah was already turning toward another section of the Shore, his gaze finding a figure who stood watching with that particular mixture of pride that only mothers could perfect.

"Two Endowments for my mother."

WAA!

Yes, he could do anything he wanted. The power was his to distribute, the choice entirely within his authority. He didn't need to do this for his mother…there was no strategic necessity, no systematic reason why Amelia Osmont needed to stand at Duke level.

But remembering how she had stood beside him during that attack, how she had faced impossibility with nothing but maternal determination and a will that refused to let her son face danger alone... he simply wanted to.

And so he did.

Amelia Osmont's eyes widened with surprise that quickly transformed into affectionate frustration that parents had when their children did something simultaneously wonderful and excessive!

She shook her head helplessly toward Noah even as waves of green-gold light began to surge around her form like reality itself was acknowledging what should have always been true.

The prompts that materialized before Noah's vision carried heavy weight!

|Amelia Osmont, The Mother of The Early Creature, Osmont, has carried out unfathomable effort to produce an equally unfathomable anomaly|

|The act of raising what cannot be raised, of nurturing what defies nurture|

|Of believing in impossibility until impossibility became inevitable|

|First Harvest Festival Endowment grants: +10,000,000,000,000(+10 Trillion) Complexity and Purity|

|Second Harvest Festival Endowment recognizes compounded effort|

|The exponential difficulty of maintaining normalcy while raising anomaly|

|Second Endowment grants: +11,000,000,000,000(+11 Trillion) Complexity and Purity|

|Total elevation: +21,000,000,000,000(+21 Trillion) to each parameter|

|Note: Highest possible boon from any Endowment at current stage achieved|

|Maternal effort has been recognized at fundamental level|

Noah nodded toward these prompts with satisfaction.

He hadn't known a single Endowment would grant her ten trillion in one sweep…the recognition of her effort as a mother apparently warranting rewards that exceeded what combat or cultivation could achieve.

He smiled toward his mother, the expression carrying all the things that didn't need to be said between them, before turning to survey the assembled beings across his Shore!

His gaze moved across faces familiar and faces newly arrived, each one representing a thread in the grand musings he was weaving.

Then he began calling out names.

"Titano!"

The massive titan looked up with eyes that widened as a green-gold pillar descended upon his tremendous form.

"Kazuhiko! Emperor Penguin! Erikson!"

Three more pillars descended simultaneously, each one finding its target with unerring accuracy!

Kazuhiko accepted the power with an expression that couldn't quite hide his shock.

The Emperor Penguin's characteristic arrogance and pride actually seemed justified for once as Duke-level power flooded through his form.

Erikson, dressed in his beach attite and slippers, had his mouth wide in shock as if he would have never expected this!

"Queen of Matter!"

Another pillar descended, this one carrying particular intensity as it recognized the accumulated effort of someone who had been documenting impossibility, turning observation into its own form of power.

The Queen of Matter nearly tore the book she had been recording in as she pointed to herself to ensure she heard righht!

He did not chose them randomly. These were the beings who would cause the most interesting disruptions in the Kleos Concordat!

And he wanted chaos.

For every name he called, pillars of green-gold light descended from those impossible clouds.

Some needed only one Endowment to cross the threshold of ten trillion. Others required two, their starting points lower but their potential no less significant!

---

While transformation blazed across the Early Veiled Shore, the Transcendent Temporal Folds buzzed with activity of an entirely different nature.

The amphitheater that had been constructed for this gathering defied conventional architecture by existing in multiple temporal states simultaneously.

Past, present, and future intersected in its structure, creating a space where beings could sit in yesterday while observing tomorrow, where conversations could conclude before they began, where significance preceded event!

If one was at the level of a mere Living Existence, they may find themselves disoriented to be here!

Each section had been crafted to accommodate the unique nature of its designated Living Existence.

Terrifying auras already filled many of these sections.

Royal Living Existences sat with bearing that suggested they ruled concepts rather than territories. Honored Living Existences radiated power that made local spacetime reassess its stability!

Each one represented heights of achievement that most beings couldn't even conceive of, let alone reach.

But at this moment, many eyes were focused on a small sliver of the amphitheater that had been specially built but didn't belong to any of the Ten Living Existences.

Between the sections reserved for Living Origins and Living Laws, in a space that seemed to have been added as an afterthought that was anything but thoughtless, stood a collection of white-gold thrones.

They lacked specific Living Existential Authority surrounding them, existing in a kind of neutral territory that nevertheless demanded recognition.

Of the many thrones that had been constructed in this unusual section, only one was currently occupied.

Noah sat with the kind of calm that suggested he was exactly where he meant to be, his form relaxed but ready, his presence neither aggressive nor submissive.

On his shoulder stood Duke Whisker, the mouse's tiny form containing power that would have been comical if it weren't so genuinely terrifying!

Duke Whisker's shining eyes blinked as he continued the conversation they had been having while everyone gathered. "I've been curious about those you would bring," the mouse said, his voice carrying despite his size, Living Origin Authority making his words resonate through multiple layers of existence.

"They would give clues to who you are, what you represent. Shouldn't you bring them over soon?"

The mouse's tiny paws moved with practiced precision as he waved, revealing a block of stellar cheese that he began to nibble on with the air of someone snacking while watching a show that was about to begin.

Noah smiled.

It was not the gentle smile of diplomacy, not the warm smile of friendship, but the tyrannical smile of someone about to flip a table that others had spent eons carefully setting.

He looked out at the gathering Dukes, Royal Living Existences, and Honored Living Existences, each one representing power structures that had existed since before current existence knew how to structure power.

The show was about to be grand indeed for all those gathered here!
