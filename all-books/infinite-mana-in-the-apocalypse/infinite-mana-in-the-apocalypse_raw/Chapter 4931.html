Chapter 4929: Ganging Up On Bazuman! III

Noah’s eyes flashed brilliantly at these words from THE Primordial Paradox!

One mustn’t forget.

THE Primordial Paradox was powerful.

Whether as powerful as THE Creature or not was unknown, but he was up there among the most terrifying beings in Observable Existence.

And he was offering to hold Bazuman down.

So Noah simply gave a light nod to show his confidence.

At this time, THE Civilizational Instinctus activated fully.

The sensation was akin to a door opening behind his eyes. Noah’s perception shifted as the capability he’d gained from the Spark Fragment of THE First Cause revealed what lay behind Bazuman’s physical form.

The Secondary Function of Manifestation Observation showed him the Civilization that this Undivided One possessed, the architectural construct that represented everything Bazuman had accumulated across eons of consumption.

What he saw was heavy!

Behind Bazuman’s churning physical form existed a structure that defied conventional description. It appeared as an infinite stomach-archive, a vast cathedral of digestion where countless chambers held the preserved memories of everything Bazuman had ever consumed.

Pillars formed from crystallized recollections supported ceilings that stretched toward infinity. Walls built from accumulated authority separated sections dedicated to different categories of consumed beings!

And everything was connected to core at the center. A pulsing core of pure undifferentiation that served as the heart of Bazuman’s existence.

The Tertiary Function of Structural Analysis identified vulnerabilities within this horrifying Manifestation.

Noah saw cracks in the walls where different consumed memories didn’t integrate cleanly with each other, places where the authority of one devoured being conflicted with the authority of another. He saw pillars that bore too much weight, supporting structures that had accumulated more than they were designed to hold.

He saw fraying connections where Bazuman’s paradoxical nature, remembering while remaining undifferentiated, created stress points that his existence constantly struggled to maintain.

And most importantly, he saw the central weakness.

The core itself was surrounded by a ring of instability where the pure undifferentiation of Bazuman’s core met the differentiated memories that he maintained. That boundary was constantly shifting, constantly struggling to remain coherent, constantly threatening to collapse if sufficient pressure was applied from the right angle.

If Noah could reach that boundary...

If he could flood it with definition...

Bazuman would collapse from the inside out!

As he observed all of this, THE Primordial Paradox moved!

Well, yes and no.

He spoke.

"Who... gave you permission to have Undifferentiated Paradox?"

HUUM!

A few words.

Simple words, even.

And yet, in the next moment, Noah observed as behind THE Primordial Paradox, multiple Embodiments and Apophasis manifestations bloomed by the dozens!

They took on the forms of terrifying conceptual creatures. Paradoxical beasts that existed and didn’t exist simultaneously. Entities that were both predator and prey. Constructs that attacked by being attacked and defended by being destroyed. Lions made of contradiction. Serpents woven from impossibility. Eagles whose wings were forged from statements that canceled themselves out!

And then all of these multiple Embodiments and Apophasis actually flowed into the words THE Primordial Paradox had uttered!

A...stacking of Embodiments and Apophasis?

They merged with his simple question, empowering it, elevating it, transforming it from mere words into a truth that pressed against the fabric of existence itself. Noah felt the power rushing past him like wind from a hurricane!

THE Primordial Paradox truly asked the entirety of the existence of Bazuman...

Who gave it permission to have Undifferentiated Paradox?

The instant his terrifying question was asked, the instant the power of THE Primordial Paradox erupted...

Noah felt another immense pressure permeating all around!

This time it was Paradox. Obsidian-gold authority that pressed against everything with weight that declared contradictions to be law. The pressure of THE Primordial Paradox’s released power exceeded even Bazuman’s sea of undifferentiation, overwhelming the crimson tide with paradoxical authority that simply stated the undifferentiation both existed and didn’t exist, rendering it functionally inert.

And in the next moment...

The horrid body and limbs and echoes of THE Undivided One before them became frozen!

Bazuman’s countless eyes, which had been blazing with hunger moments before, now stared forward without ability to move. Its tentacles, which had been reaching toward Noah with desperate hunger, now hung suspended in positions that defied gravity.

Its churning surface, its Echoes, which had been constantly shifting and revealing faces of consumed beings, now held perfectly still as if time itself had ceased to flow for this entity alone.

Even its Civilization froze!

The Manifestation that only Noah could see, that infinite stomach-archive with its countless chambers and connected core, became still in ways that Civilizations shouldn’t have been capable of. The memories ceased their eternal churning. The pillars stopped bearing weight because weight itself had been placed in paradoxical suspension.

The core at the center continued to exist, but it existed in a state where it both pulled and didn’t pull, where it both consumed and didn’t consume.

It was a terrifying display of power from THE Primordial Paradox!

And the moment this being acted, Noah also made his move!

From the corrupted glimmers of Infinite undifferentiation that had been surrounding Bazuman, from the proto-matter that carried both THE Living Paradox’s corruption and Noah’s scattered Seals, golden pages began blooming!

How dare it take in glimmers of his Infinities when because of it, he could unleash attacks that effectively bloomed inches from his existence?!

THE Transcendent Paradoxical Inscriptiones Dormientes that Noah had moved within the corrupted proto-matter now activated!

They’d been sleeping within the very substance that Bazuman had been using, waiting for the moment when Noah chose to awaken them. The sensation of their activation was like feeling hounds finally released from their leashes, eager and hungry and straining toward their prey!

They emerged from particles that seemed too small to contain them, golden pages burning with dual lights of differentiation and undifferentiation that made them visible even against the frozen chaos of Bazuman’s form.

The Inscriptions flowed toward the weak points of the Manifestation that only Noah could see!

If THE Primordial Paradox wasn’t here, Noah would have had to fight with the terrifying Immensity and undifferentiation of Bazuman while trying to attack the weaknesses of his Civilization. He would have had to contend with echoes and tentacles and the gravitational pull of the core while simultaneously attempting strikes against vulnerabilities that only he could perceive.

But since THE Primordial Paradox had ridiculously caused every single Depth and undifferentiation and Immensity of Bazuman to freeze...

It was like Noah was basically holding a gun.

And his enemy was bound and shackled...and he simply had to shoot.

So he shot.

The first Transcendent Paradoxical Inscriptio struck a crack in the wall of Bazuman’s stomach-archive whereconflicting memories failed to integrate cleanly!

The golden page burned against the vulnerability with light that held both differentiation and undifferentiation, and Noah felt the impact reverberate through THE Civilizational Instinctus like watching a hammer strike glass.

The wall didn’t merely crack further. It began to forget that it had ever been a wall. The memories it contained began questioning whether they’d ever been memories at all!

Undifferentiation and differentiation working in harmony tore apart structure that Bazuman had maintained for eons.

The second Inscription struck a pillar that bore too much accumulated weight! 𝑓𝑟𝑒𝘦𝓌𝑒𝑏𝑛𝑜𝘷𝑒𝘭.𝒸𝘰𝑚

The golden page pressed against the stress point that Noah’s Structural Analysis had identified. The countless consumed beings whose authority comprised that pillar started separating from each other, their combined strength dissolving as the Inscription declared that they were and weren’t connected simultaneously. The pillar cracked. It wavered. It began to collapse under weight that it could no longer justify bearing!

The third, fourth, fifth, sixth Inscriptions struck fraying connections throughout the Manifestation!

Each golden page found a vulnerability that Noah’s perception had identified, and each activation caused cascading damage that spread beyond the initial point of impact. The sensation was exhilarating, like watching dominoes fall in patterns he’d arranged, like feeling a plan come together with precision that exceeded his expectations!

Connections that had been struggling to maintain coherence now failed entirely. Sections of the stomach-archive that had been integrated for eons now separated as if they’d never belonged together.

Bazuman’s Civilization was coming apart from the inside!

But Noah wasn’t finished.

He focused on the central weakness.

That pulsing core of pure undifferentiation surrounded by a ring of instability where undifferentiation met differentiated memory.

Noah took a step.

And appeared deep in the very center of Bazuman as another Inscriptio cut through like a blade of differentiation and undifferentiation!

THE Primordial Paradox’s freezing authority had suspended everything about this Undivided One, including the defenses that would have prevented such intrusion. Noah simply moved through the frozen tentacles, through the suspended echoes, through the churning proto-matter that couldn’t churn while paradox held it still.

He arrived at the terrifying center.

The entrance to core gaped before him. The belly that had swallowed him long ago during his escape through THE Prima Indifferentia. He’d run from this place then, had fled with everything he possessed to avoid being consumed by its endless dissolution.

But now he didn’t run.

Now he radiated.

His entire body began to emit terrifying blue-gold brilliance as Infinite Absolute Seals flooded out of him in numbers that exceeded anything he’d released before! The Seals didn’t attack. They didn’t need to attack. They simply existed, carrying definition that declared what they were with authority that couldn’t be questioned. And he amplified it all by releasing his Inscriptions here!

The sensation of releasing so much of himself was like exhaling a breath he’d been holding for eons, like finally letting go of weight he hadn’t realized he’d been carrying.

His light and brilliance began to encompass the entirety of the center of Bazuman!

He sought to infuse the endless definition of Infinity into an entity who only had undefinition and undifferentiation.

The only way to collapse something this deeply undifferentiated was to give it a profound sense of definition. And since Bazuman had Infinity within its corrupted weavings, since it had consumed a piece of Noah eons ago and absorbed more through the corrupted proto-matter, all other areas across its body had resonance with Infinite authority.

With Noah radiating Infinity at Bazuman’s very center, that resonance activated!

The Infinite Seals within Bazuman’s consumed existence began responding to Noah’s broadcast!

All other areas across its body began to radiate a strict outlined definition of Infinity!

The sea of undifferentiation that Bazuman had released was now filled with points of definition that declared themselves with absolute certainty! The echoes it had manifested found their consumed foundations suddenly becoming defined, becoming differentiated, becoming things that had names and boundaries and limitations that undifferentiated existence shouldn’t have possessed!

The entire area became filled with the definition of Infinity!

And Bazuman, for the first time in eons upon eons, found itself becoming defined.

This whole time the terrifying Immensity of THE Undivided One sought to move, but it was unable to. Its eyes were livid as they stared at both Noah and THE Primordial Paradox, hatred and hunger burning within crimson depths.

But the body of THE Primordial Paradox remained imposing and grand as it stared down with obsidian-gold flames burning steadily around his form.

And Noah, deep within the center of Bazuman, looked at the core being filled with definition as he knew it was over for this being.

The Manifestation he could see through THE Civilizational Instinctus was collapsing entirely now. The infinite stomach-archive was no longer infinite. It had boundaries, limitations, edges that could be perceived. The countless chambers were no longer countless. They had numbers, values, quantities that could be calculated. The core itself was no longer purely undifferentiated. It held definition that conflicted with its fundamental nature.

Bazuman was becoming something it had never been.

Something defined.

Something limited.

Something that could be consumed!

Noah’s eyes blazed with blue-gold light as he looked at the central core of this Undivided One, as he prepared to harvest everything that this ancient horror had accumulated across eons of consumption. His weavings were already activating, preparing to multiply and exponentially elevate whatever he claimed from this collapse!

And before he did it, he spoke words that echoed Bazuman’s earlier confession with cruel symmetry.

"I...want you, O Bazuman."

HUUM!

This time it was his own confession.

And he wanted to consume this Undivided One whole!
