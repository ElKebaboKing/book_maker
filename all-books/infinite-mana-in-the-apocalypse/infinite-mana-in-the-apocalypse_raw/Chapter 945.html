Chapter 943 - Shattering the Calculations of a Hegemony! II

None of the being from the Bloodline Races or the visitors from other Universes could have guessed that the enemy they faced nonchalantly created a ridiculous Dao that gave 5% of his power to all his subordinates, the boom of multiplying their Mana reserves by tens of times being the thing that freely made them move without fear.

Nor would they ever guess that during this time as well, their enemy obtained things that only Universal Realm Hegemonies could play around with, elevating his own Power to a large degree as this development caused the calculations of a certain being to be off!

Deeper in the Bloody Battlefields Galaxy Cl.u.s.ters, the Monarchs and Paragons of the Bloodline Races that were alerted by the foreign intruders and had even sent in their own Great Sages and Sages forward watched on with grim expression, a few of them pulsing with essence as they seemed ready to go!

"You know the rules. Only those at the level of Great Sages and below can enter these Galaxies. We cannot break this rule."

THRUM!

Three powerful Monarchs of the White Tiger Race were ready to set off as their eyes were red with anger and bloodl.u.s.t when watching scenes of their allies butchered under the hands of this new force. Behind them, a pristine White Tiger towering 1000 meters in size appeared while speaking, his white furred body releasing the powerful aura of a Paragon!

"But Paragon Neriliel...isn’t it a Rule that the Master set up? He can also choose to let us break it when it comes to defending our people!"

...!

The Monarchs looked towards the Paragon with blood red eyes, the enormous White Tiger shaking his eyes that seemed full of wisdom as he replied with a cold light while staring at the illusory screens showing the shocking events in the peripheral galaxies.

"This deals with the secret of the Slaughter Star Monolith that these Galaxies are cl.u.s.tered around, and the reason why the Rankings within are also isolated to the levels of Great Sages, Monarchs, and Paragons...If we want to interfere, we can only send more Great Sages! But even this seems futile..."

RUMBLE!

The l.u.s.trous fur of the White Tiger Paragon shone as he asked what many creatures of the Bloodline Races were thinking as they watched the unfolding events.

"Just who are they? What makes them so bold to disrupt the ecosystem created by a Universal Hegemony...?"

A grand question! One that would be answered soon enough as the forces moving in the periphery of the Bloody Battlefields Galaxy Cl.u.s.ters did not just surprise Paragons...but even the Universal Hegemony overseeing the whole thing!

The ridiculous developments that Noah had created truly caused the calculations of a Hegemony to be entirely off, a loss about to be taken by this being for the first time in many, many years!

In an isolated Realm somewhere within the Animus Universe.

The enormous figure of a regal being situated upon a star could be seen, his eyes cold as they stared at the many illusory screens showing exactly what was happening across certain galaxies.

Around him, a fantastical scene of swirling golden destiny and universal fortune could be seen, his increasingly cold eyes glancing at every single line of destiny and fortune as he came to a conclusion when he observed the minuscule amounts of reduction in his Universal Fortune!

It was the conclusion that what was happening now was just the beginning- that if he did not nip it in the bud...it would lead to an even greater loss in his fortune as everything he built would crumble!

This was similar to how when Valentina had met Noah, her proficiency in Destiny and Universal Fortune told her he would be the solution that would solve her problem and further increase her fortune!

Even though he found it hard to believe, Ambrose could not help but confirm the conclusion multiple times as his eyes focused on the central screen he was viewing that showed Noah’s figure.

A Hegemony facing a possible heavy loss in the future just from the single Tyrant Dragon at the Galactic Filament Realm and the forces he led against him.

A being that had at most forged a few billion galaxies, who had at most undergone 25% Purification of Primordial Essence...such a creature actually posed a threat to him!

RUMBLE!

Ambrose waved his hands as he ascertained this, his expression placid as the swirling fortune around him disappeared, a light of teleportation wrapping around him while his focus remained on the central Illusory screen showing the figure of the Tyrant Dragon.

He was a being that had lived for over a million years.

He had truly seen a lot, and knew what he needed to do when a problem like this arose! He...had to nip it in the bud before it bloomed to something bigger, and nothing would be more effective than moving himself!

WAA!

His body disappeared as he undertook a long distance teleportation, his destination...being a place that not even the Paragons he controlled would ever think he would be in.

---

The terrifying figure of the Tyrant Dragon that had ripped apart two planet sized Chthonians was reflected in everyone’s eyes, the Great Sages of the Bloodline Races feeling deep terror in their hearts as they saw this creature cash with the last Chthonian horror!

Meanwhile, his Summons moved with just as much terrifying might as they whittled down the forces of the Bloodline Races and their reinforcements like they were cutting through vegetables.

A feeling of despair welled up deep in their hearts as the only commands they heard from the Monarchs and Paragons above them was to continue fighting with all their power, their hearts shaking as they wondered if they had been abandoned!

Yet..

THRUM!

In the next second, a terrifying aura began to descend unto the starry space they were fighting on.

The eyes of this being opened as they billowed out with intense pressure, his gaze causing the bodies of many to tremble as it landed on a single Tyrant Dragon that was ready to tear apart another Chthonian Great Sage!
