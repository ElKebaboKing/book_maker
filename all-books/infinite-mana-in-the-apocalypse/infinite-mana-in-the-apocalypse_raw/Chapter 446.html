Chapter 445 - Fire and Water

Behind a rushing waterfall in a particular location of the Celestial Realm, there was a hidden space where an Ancient Pedestal stood, gloriously holding up a vibrant blue crystal that held the Inheritance of the Disciple of Water.

Near this pedestal, there was a barely discernible floating Spiritual World where few creatures came out of every so often, learning the inheritance before they went back into the paradisiacal world. Most of them rushed to the floating continent on the North of the Spiritual World, this continent being divided in half by a thin line that had fiery environments on one side and a watery environment on the other.

The beings rushed to the side where the particles of the Law of Water were abundant before beginning their cultivation, the continental floating landmass being so wide that they didn’t even near a particular position where an explosion of blue and red lights was beginning.

At the very junction between the Fire and Water Law Space, a singular clone was sitting meditatively as his senses focused on his body, feeling the minuscule changes as he watched the two Origin Cores for fire and water slowly begin to rotate the moment both of their unique skills were activated.

From one side of his body, the elemental particles of fire rushed in and went towards the Fire Origin Core, and the opposite side of his body had the elemental particles of water rushing into the Water Origin Core.

BZZZZ!

Friction seemed to occur for a millisecond as the opposing energies seemed to bathe Noah half and half, their point of contact causing a rapid repulsion as they began to vibrate in extremely close proximity to each other. Yet at the same time, there continued to be a sense of attraction as the two cores pulled this energy towards them, Noah feeling as if his body was becoming a battle ground.

Every time that the particles of the two opposing elements came in contact as they went towards his Origin, they became agitated and pushed against each other, making them aggressive as this reaction caused them to rush towards his Origin faster!

The moment that they entered, they rushed to their respective Cores, whether Fire or Water, before even more of the elemental particles continued to rush in as the reaction became faster and faster.

The colliding particles of water and fire continued to agitate each other and senselessly rush down Noah’s body, creating for a rapid progress in the rotation of the Fire and Water Origin Cores as they absorbed the essence rapidly while releasing its unique energy that coursed through his clone and then through his main body!

At this time, a smile of accomplishment would have passed over Noah as he performed the successful Dual Cultivation of the Laws of Water and Fire, but his current expression was one of grim pain as unlike Fate and Chaos, the reaction of the oncoming particles of Fire and Water used his body as the battlefield to become agitated before they rushed towards their respective cores speedily.

The reaction that occurred with his body at the center made for a very painful experience as he could feel the impact of the opposing elements damage him, observing his body being put under strain as even some muscles were torn!

It was at this instant though, that the unique energy being released from the Fire and Water Origin Core permeated through his body, washing over the torn muscles and aching areas as the millions of cells around these locations hungrily drunk the energy and were reformed right away, the strength they had increasing ever so slightly.

Noah observed this reaction as he grimaced in pain, but a smile came on his face nonetheless as he felt the minute strength gained from the unique energy released from the two cores repairing his torn muscles made them stronger than when it was absorbed by his cells normally while only cultivating a single law.

Thus, for the effects of the Dual Cultivation of both Fire and Water, Noah could conclude that it was bound to be an extremely painful experience, but it allowed him to bypass the rate at which energy entered into the Origin Cores at a turtles pace, the flow being constant and even increasing every so often as the energy the cores released went on to heal and even further strengthen his body that was acting like a battle field for the opposing laws!

RUMBLE!

The essence of water and fire from the two different sides of the Law Spaces cl.u.s.tered around Noah as they began forming a resplendent cloud of light, a unique cultivation session that had never before been done continuing in the Spiritual World.

In the wide Sanctum that Celestials controlled, there was a bustle of activities recently as among the ranks of the Celestials, word had been getting passed around that soon, the future powerhouses of their generation would be getting assessed on who among them had the greatest potential in the form of combat!

These words became a reality a few days later as it was announced to all that in the next few days, a spectacular tournament would be held at a specified location in the Celestial Realm. This tournament would involve beings that many rarely saw in their lifetimes as they were always moving with extreme efficiency deep in the Celestial Realm to increase their power.

It would be a tournament where beings holding Noble Fates and Special Existences took to stage, each of them fighting a battle of supremacy to see who exactly would come out on top!

This tournament was open for all to watch, being made into a huge festive event where the Celestials planned to reveal the beginning of a long paused conflict with the Dark Expanse. The Celestials wanted to use this tournament to announce the initiation of a grand campaign!

It would be located around a particularly clear field of land to the east of the Sanctum where the slightly docile Earth Behemoths resided, this wide expanse of land being perfect for the huge and spectacular battles about to take place.

Among the Special Existences and Celestials with Noble Fates, the upcoming geniuses known as Athena and Crixus would be making their grand entrance into the eyes of many who had only so far heard of them, or knew nothing about them...
