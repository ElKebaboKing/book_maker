Chapter 2510 Raising a Dreadful Army! II

Noah's words caused the Mad Apex Emperor to burn with a grand light as he turned up to stare into the verdant expanse.

Then...

"Do it."

...!

His resolve shone brightly as he made his choice!

And the light of Realm Reconstruction and Aspect Configuration shone as Noah's Soul blazed with brilliance.

The more existences continued to utilize his authority of Dynamis of Extremity on- especially Apex Aeonic Lifeforms, the faster they went towards complete Convergence as Aletheian and Vitalistic Dynamis of Extremity would soon follow the path of Indefatigable and blood with a Yggdrasil of Extremity!

In another region of space far from the one where yet another Apex Aeonic Lifeform had fallen into Noah's hands.

The figure of Xiaomi shone with stellar brilliance as she stared closely at Noah.

Her eyes had always been able to see things others could not easily discern as she was currently trying to formulate an understanding of this being before her!

Even though she was afflicted with a condition as her lifeforce was very faint, her beauty was unrivaled among many others as the way trillions of quasars came together to form her body made her seem like an innocent stellar flower that even Kings and Emperors could only gaze at from afar.

She knew that her father and herself had already fallen into the grasp of this being before her as she didn't need to take on any responsibility, but she wanted to understand him better, and thus she did what she did.

And she was the first one to speak.

"When the Elder of the Singularity Overseers landed his eyes on me, his whole demeanor changed as after learning to what extent they're still trying to go through for me, I've theorized with 99% certainty that he must have seen something within my body that someone even more powerful than him wants."

...!

"And it has to be someone more powerful than him, otherwise he wouldn't be able to mobilize all of the forces of Singularity Overseers in Dissolution to look for me. This means that whatever he saw was of interest to someone at the very peak of Apex Aeonic Lifeforms...or a Hyperversal Extremity. The only thing such beings would be interested in are possibly Mutated Aspects of

Existence...or an Innate Foundation that even its holder wasn't aware of."

WAA!

She spoke rapidly as Noah gazed towards her with an interested smile, nodding along the way as she continued.

"And connecting the dots to the whole incident with RUIN that put me in my current state, I can only assume there is a secret foundation within me I was not able to grasp and control in time.

Something that even a peak Apex Aeonic Lifeform or Hyperversal Extremity wants to devour as it means it is a foundation potent enough to easily or quickly attain Extremity. Now...nobody would stop trying to get their hands on such a thing as this is where you come in."

Her gaze was brilliant.

She began walking towards Noah as the verdant grassland below her swayed with happiness.

"Someone like you who hasn't attained Extremity...did not show the same devouring light of greed as the Singularity Overseer Elder.

Where even now as I speak of an opportunity of reaching Extremity, you are unphased by it."

"Why of course." Noah's resonant voice rang out at such a juncture as he spoke calmly.

"I have too many ways to reach Extremity, unlike others. What I do have though, is curiosity for what exactly it is that very likely...a Hyperversal Extremity wants from you."

The possibility that it was a very powerful Apex Aeonic Lifeform seeking what Xiaomi had was stricken out.

And...

"No. It is not just curiosity you hold."

Xiaomi's sonorous voice rang out as she came a few inches away from Noah.

"My eyes can see many things that others couldn't, and from your eyes...I see the light of possessiveness."

...!

"You may not want to harm me or devour what I have, but you have a light of wanting to make my very being yours. It may not be easy for others to notice, but I can."

Her eyes didn't hold any hint of fear as she spoke, Noah's lips forming a calm smile at them as she continued!

"Now, among the options of suitors in the Aeonic Haven of Extremity, there are some dirt poor options of being devoured by a Hyperversal Extremity for whatever I have or a Quasarian Prince with poor prospects. Or...there is an enigmatic being like you who can raise Apex Aeonic Lifeforms before even undergoing

Apexification yourself. I would like to get to know a being like you better as all I want is your help in me getting the strength to protect myself from the Hyperversal Extremity wishing to devour me. And then..."

Inches from Noah, weak waves of Essence coiled around her feet as her figure floated forward with her arms behind her back, her dazzling face coming closer as her small lips lightly landed on Noah's before she pulled back!

"...you can satiate that possessive light in your eyes by taking whatever you want from me."

Bravado and brilliance were apparent in her eyes, but the arms behind her back showed her fingers trembling with nervousness as they betrayed the strong front this being was putting up!

Noah glanced at the Quasarian before his eyes as his right hand flashed- bountiful lifeforce and an aura of Extremity bubbling from it as a celestial obsidian crystalline flower that was nothing other than an Obsidian Extremity Panacea.

His Reified Sources of Extremity in the Extremity Vein of Dissolution were making stupendous advancements from

massacred Leviathans of Dissolution and obtaining more Sources of Complete Crimson III Aeonic Pearls of Extremity among other burning quantities of the Light of Loot, and obtaining Golden and Obsidian Extremity Panaceas only came easier after that as the exploration of this wondrous location continued!

What was even more stunning was that in the deeper areas, the golden lights of treasures unlike Panaceas had begun to shine.

They were things that likely allowed one to converge a Dynamis easier or the progression of Boundaries past the 9th Layer!

Noah smiled as he thought of what was to come, focusing back on the young woman before him as he spoke out.

"We can leave everything else for later as we now have to understand why someone who has already attained Hyperversal Extremity Authority would want from you."

HOONG!

The light of the Obsidian Extremity Panacea began to blaze, turning into stellar black flames under Noah's hands that flowed into the body of Xiaomi.

Like many other healing panaceas in the past, Xiaomi's body was about to reject it as a pained expression rose on her face, Noah stabilizing her as the three authorities of his Dynamis of Extremity also surged out and coursed through her body!

Her vasculature was dried and filled with dead vessels that arched with the essence of Ruin.

Her very Foundation seemed feeble and about to die, and yet when the essence of the Extremity Panacea reached into it, it began to buzz and come back to life ever so slowly.

The Obsidian Extremity Panacea should have been a tool to aid one in forging an Extremity Foundation, and yet its essence was being drawn into the seemingly weakened Foundation in the depths of Xiaomi's body as it wasn't used to forge a foundation!

It seemed like it was simply going towards causing the current one to shine with even more brilliance.

BOOM!

An explosive boom of authority erupted as Noah's visage was nearly pushed away.

A dreadful and pristine aura began to ride from Xiaomi as her dead vasculature began to come back to life!

And it returned even greater and more immaculate than before as it had swallowed the burning light of an Obsidian Extremity Panacea!

More than this though, even Noah was surprised to find that from the aura blazing from Xiaomi, he could sense a similar grandeur compared to his own.

He could sense...

"Hyperversal...?"

WAA!

As soon as his words ended, prompts began to rise before him.

[You have unearthed the Innate Pellucid Hyperversal Foundation of Ruin!]

[Destined to disappear into oblivion after its holder failed to control its core essence, you have reversed its course and even directed it towards a grander path with the rejuvenation of a type of Natal Extremity Authority!]

Natal Extremity Authority.

This was the type of energy contained in an Obsidian Extremity Panacea!

So the Crimson Extremity Panacea Noah swallowed that aided in him obtaining the Unrecorded Aeonic Quintessential Hyperversal Extremity Foundation... maybe something even greater!

[Due to a confluence of factors, the Unrecorded Innate Pellucid Hyperversal Extremity Foundation of Ruined Chaos has been born in the selected target!]

[The sole purpose of the Innate Pellucid Hyperversal Foundation of Ruin was to elevate the concept of Ruin towards Extremity in the shortest possible time. The Unrecorded Innate Pellucid Hyperversal Extremity Foundation of Ruined Chaos can now elevate 8 other Sources of Chaos apart from Ruin towards Extremity in an incredibly short period of time.]

...!

Dazzling prompts appeared before both Noah and Xiaomi.

Her slender, bountiful, and gorgeous visage pulsed as around the verdant domain, dense origins of chaos began to gather!

Along with this...

[The Sources of Chaos within your own foundation begin to resonate with the Unrecorded Innate Pellucid Hyperversal Extremity Foundation of Ruined Chaos.]

[As the one who unearthed this Unrecorded Foundation from facing oblivion, you are granted a distinctive authority to benefit from the same Sources of Chaos the holder of this Foundation raises towards Extremity!]

[Cultivating near or with the Holder of the Unrecorded Innate Pellucid Hyperversal Extremity Foundation of Ruined Chaos will increase the rate of advancement of select Boundaries connected to Sources of Chaos by 10,000% to 100,000%!]

A dazzling notification appeared before Noah's eyes.

And before the stellar quasarian eyes of Xiaomi who felt her entire Aspects of Existence undergo a revolution...

[As an existence holding the Unrecorded Innate Pellucid Hyperversal Extremity Foundation of Ruined Chaos, cultivating near or with the Holder of the Unrecorded Aeonic Quintessential Hyperversal Extremity Foundation will increase the rate of advancement of all your Boundaries connected to Sources of Chaos by 100,000% to 1,000,000%.] 𝒻𝑟ℯℯ𝑤𝑒𝑏𝑛𝘰𝓋𝑒𝓁.𝒸𝑜𝘮

The 11th, 12th, 13th, 14th, 15th, 16th, 17th, and the final 18th Extremity Boundary Layer were immensely difficult to cross, each one being hundreds of times more difficult than the one prior.

Due to her Innate Foundation and the changes it had just gone through, connected with the being of a being that truly shouldn't exist with the Foundation that he held...Xiaomi was one of the few, rare existences that may just be able to traverse across the arduous upper Boundary Layers with utmost ease!
