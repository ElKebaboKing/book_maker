Chapter 1626 Building the Ultimate Defense! l

A throne made of multicolored light shot out across space to smash onto the scaled white claw of a White Dragon Emperor, the Unnamed Kainos Emperor seated the whole time as he wielded his spear forward and burst with trillions of Units of Mana!

HOOONG!

Like the blaring of a horn, the impact was deafening.

BZZZT!

The claw of the White Dragon Emperor carried the tremendous force of nine different Ninth Firmament Enders as their power easily exceeded 1,000 True Reality Damage Values and ranged upwards of 3,000, the moment of impact causing the many defensive multicolored auras around Noah to be eradicated one after another!

POP! POP! POP!..

Hundreds of True Reality Defense Values after another were wiped out even though Noah was spamming them constantly, the rate of replication of these abilities being exceeded by the damage that the White Dragon Emperor carried as it felt like every microsecond- the constant force of more than 1,000+ True Damage Values was being applied.

"Impudence!"

The will of the White Dragon Emperor roared out as its claw became wrapped in white lightning, putting Noah in further peril as even though his constant rate of spamming countered back with constant instant casts of Reality Piercer and Auras, he could not pierce the scales of this corporeal Dragon before him even though his will could withstand it!

The amalgamation of so many Enders under a singular formation with all of their Edicts and fused Ascendancy Halos all pummeling towards him…it was too much as any lesser existence at the Ninth Firmament would have fallen.

Yet he had shockingly faced the attack for a few microseconds without even being pushed back as when only 3 auras were left and the rate of reapplication could not keep up with the damage he was constantly receiving, he allowed the force of the White Dragon Emperor to push him back as the moment the last auras shattered, his figure was flung back across space in the same direction he came in.

BOOOM!

Like an arrow of light, he was shot back from the impact with such speed and haste that he smashed onto the opposite end of the locked down domain on the other side- his figure still seating on the Throne of Unnamed Tyranny when space broke and fissured as he smashed onto the walls of space!

Yet he was alive.

WAA!

His eyes flashed up gloriously as he looked at himself to see the Atlatl of Reality trembling as it pulsed with light, his hand holding it currently ruptured and bleeding as stellar Cosmos painted on it began to be repaired rapidly- these of his body in a similar state as fractures and split stellar skin could be seen throughout!

"Haha…"

He couldn't help but release a devilish laugh as even though he was pushed back, heavily injured, and outclassed on all levels- he didn't perish under the joined formation of nine powerful beings that called out the will of a past Emperor!

And this…was before he fused with his 10 Ascendancy Halos!

He was getting a better grasp of his limitations as well as how he should optimize his abilities, essence wrapping around his throne as after being shot back, he began making his way back towards the overpowering White Dragon Emperor that had burning white flames surging around its body.

Genevieve looked at this scene with somber light as seeing the state of Noah, her aged eyes flashed as her beastial figure opened its jaws yet again, releasing Resplendent rulers of Reality that almost seemed like stellar sharp bridges that made their way over!

"Fight!"

Her shout was resonant as the Pure-Blooded Cardinal Royal Humans joined the fray, their bodies releasing stellar waves of attacks as they tried to hide their shocks when looking at the magisterial figure of Noah seated upon his throne while fearlessly pointing his Spear of Reality forward.

Noah who at this moment felt his inadequacy when it came to defensive abilities as he began to make some changes to his concepts- his throne coming to stop in place as he didn't go into another close combat strike with the terrifying enemy, and he instead began spamming <Cosmic Thrower> from a distance as countless Unstable Cosmos carrying 200-400 True Reality Damage Values began to surge out endlessly!

His will blazed out with fervor as while this might seem like him overusing his resources to dish out crazy attacks, it cost him barely anything as the reason he did this was to keep the battle going as he collected information while also optimizing his abilities at the same time.

As for what he was optimizing?

'Take the Quasi-TABOO Tiers of the Nomological Edicts of the Beastial Reality Devourer and Sun Chaser…and transfer their tiers to the concepts of Bulwark and Antediluvian!'

HOOONG!

His dazzling Ascendancy Halos flashed as his own designed Minor Nomological Edict of Bulwark and Quasi-Insuperable Nomological Edict of Antediluvian that bolstered his life force and defense were forcefully taken from one Tier to another, the Quasi-TABOO Edicts Noah had just obtained being used as sacrifice to make this happen!

Stellar light exploded within his Origin as a verdant hue began to bloom out, the ceaseless life force of Yggdrasil surging out as the auras Noah had just been casting from Bulwark were fundamentally changed into something even more fantastical.

Prompts about the elevation of these edicts and their enhanced abilities flowed over Noah's mind as a few of them were of important significance!

<Due to the elevation of the Nomological Edict of Bulwark to Quasi-TABOO, the Flames of Bulwark have risen to a greater level as the ultimate abilities of Resource Shield Master and Runic Defender have been unlocked, along with the ushering of the Ultra Rare Bloodline of the Cosmic Armament…>

WAA!

All of his own specially designed Nomological Edicts granted stupendous Bloodlines so far as Tyrannical Emperor at Quasi-Taboo had given way to an Olden Bloodline while Sanguine Emperor had made it possible for Noah to forge his own Kainos Emperor Bloodline.

When his other Edicts were elevated to this level, even though their effects might not be as exaggerated as his most powerful Edicts- even they came with their own powerful Ultra Rare Bloodlines!

Bulwark was at a slightly lesser level as this was its result.

But Antediluvian…this Edict was made at the same time as the Dictum as Noah remembered calling out the words of these edicts hand in hand not too long ago!

Its elevation was even grander than Bulwark as it paved way for a stupendous type of Bloodline Noah had not gotten his hands on yet.

<Due to the elevation of the Nomological Edict of Antediluvian to Quasi-TABOO, the Flames of Antediluvian have risen to a greater level as the ultimate abilities of The Eonic Cosmic Yggdrasil and the Transcendent Plant Kingdom have been unlocked, along with the ushering of the Supreme Pure-Blooded Cardinal Royal Bloodline of the Infinite Willow Tree…>𝗳𝚛𝚎𝚎𝘄𝕖𝕓𝕟𝕠𝚟𝚎𝕝.𝗰𝕠𝐦

…!
