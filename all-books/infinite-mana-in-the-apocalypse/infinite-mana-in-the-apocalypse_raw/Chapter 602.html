Chapter 600 - Realm Expansion!

Hello.

It is your trusted Shadow Guard Night once more.

This is the 36th Journal Entry in the new Volume I’ve started since the day the Infinite Realm was established.

It has been an extremely busy period of time, with many beings moving around this wondrous Realm as they mostly harvested its abundant resources that did not seem to be ending.

Delegations were sent from numerous Lands that used to be Worlds, beings of different Races meeting as they began to work together towards a common goal. I was always present and protecting Queen Adelaide during these meetings, her delight as she handled any matters that would be helpful to the Esteemed Person being extremely visible every time.

During this busy time period, the Esteemed Person abruptly passed down the word that the biggest enemy was defeated. Just like that.

He didn’t need an army with him or millions in order to come out victorious in such an ordeal, and this knowledge caused many of us within this Realm to feel a burning desire to get stronger- just so that this Esteemed Person would not leave us all behind!

We wanted to participate in the battles, you know? Be of some help as we received so many benefits that we never thought possible, you know?

Yet not too long thereafter, on what should have been a period of relaxation and resting...a change began to occur.

RUMBLE!

Ahh, it’s actually just happened once more as I am writing this. Over the past few days, a tremor would pass throughout the Infinite Realm every now and then. Sometimes it came in a few minute intervals, sometimes just a few every hour. We didn’t know what these tremors represented at first, but every time a tremor finished the extremely perceptive beings could notice that the atmospheric essence had risen once again.

This was an easy thing if you had been following the Esteemed Person for a while and was there was the Worlds ascended to higher ranks before ultimately forming into a Realm, and I noticed it right away as a shocking truth was confirmed right after as the changes became more noticeable and word passed down from the culprit in question!

It seemed that every time this tremor occurred, it signified that yet another World was joining the expansive Infinite Realm, that another few billion creatures had joined us once again!

It was such a shocking thing as we expected the Esteemed Person to rest for even a little, but it seemed like he could not sit still as every few minutes, another World was fused into this grand Realm filled with wonders.

RUMBLE!

Multiple worlds have already been fused as I am writing this Journal Entry, the task we have at hand to continue reaching out to the newly joining worlds and establish communications and governance only continuing to increase as many beings would have to be mobilized.

But this entire unbelievable and shocking issue of the Esteemed Person actively moving as he combined numerous worlds into this Realm made many of us who could think realize that something big was coming.

He would not be moving like this unless another enemy we do not even know about had risen, or maybe the Esteemed Person just enjoyed achieving ridiculously higher realms of power that he didn’t wish to stop.

But either way, I was already preparing my mind to withstand any shock that the Esteemed Person wanted to send towards us next. Me being even surprised at the fact that a new world was being fused into this realm every few minutes was a loss on my mission, but I am now vowing with all my strength to never be shocked at the things this being did in the future.

I would wholly expect unbelievable and shocking things to continue to occur, and I will succeed in never having my jaw drop at the next unbelievable event.

Until then, there is an entire Realm to explore and numerous races to meet. I’ll update next time on the progress of my mission, whenever the time comes that the Esteemed Person chooses to break all common sense again.

---

RUMBLE!

Yet another world seamlessly fused into the Infinite Realm as Noah used the feature of {World Integration}. He had stopped counting the hundreds of billions after a certain point as many beings found their worlds suddenly changed, his focus working overtime in the Infinite Realm as they stabilized any newly appearing fused lands and spoke to their people.

The billions of Vampyres that were spread out all over the Dark Expanse were those at the forefront of wanting to take on this task, trying to be of some help to their Progenitor as they moved diligently to make sure everything continued smoothly!

But just like this, Noah was able to seamlessly integrate many worlds as he first teleported across the Dark Expanse where he only had to let beings like the Grand Mage Edrusim know what he was doing as they only nodded in a stupor, already sending him messages of when their own Worlds being integrated into this wondrous Realm that only a few higher of ups of the Dark Expanse knew about.

They were at the forefront of it as Old Man Inuit had told them the being who had so easily taken care of Aldrich and the billions of Celestials that nobody could find at this moment was the 9th Infernal Lord, a being that was moving for the peace of the Novus Galaxy!

All those with the tiniest bit of intelligence wanted to attach themselves to this being as Noah was able to bring in multiple powerful Prime Worlds in this manner.

Days passed like this as his main body integrated new worlds while his two clones continued cultivating multiple universal laws at once, the areas where these two clones stood turning into shockingly overabundant fields of essence as the mana of numerous laws freely swirled around.

RUMBLE!

His origin shook at this moment as he detected yet another change, reaching another stupidly grand milestone in a short span of time as one of his clones briefly opened his eyes to observe the changes in the stat panel.

[Noah Osmont][Title(s): Infernal Lord(Variant), Grand Controller(Fate)]

[Bloodline(s): Vampyre Progenitor]

[Aether Particles(s) : 5,526,932]

[Karma Line(s) : 502,492]

[Fate Line(s) : 10,000,000]

[Chaos Particle(s) : 5,192,265]

[Origin Core(s) : Light-1, Dark-1, Fire-8, Water-8, Earth-3, Air-3]

[Domain(s) : Aether, Karma, Fate, Chaos, Light, Dark, Space, Time, Fire, Water, Earth, Air]

[Vitality: STAR FORGER]

[Focus: - ]

[Strength: STAR FORGER]

[Law(s) : Aether- 55%, Karma-5%,Fate-100%,Chaos-51%, Light-8%, Dark-8%, Space-28%, Time-12%, Fire-83%, Water-83%, Earth-38%, Air-38%]

[Authority(s) : Aether Lord, Fate Lord, Chaos Lord, Flame Lord, Water Lord]

Beautiful numbers that were enough to make a grown man cry were displayed in front of him, the shocking speed of progress he had made in just a matter of days being visualized for the first time!

Multiple laws now littered his stat panel as he managed to break the 50% mark in the Law of Chaos, gaining yet another Authority aside from Aether that was just handed to him. Even more shocking was the rate of progress for the four laws that had amazing synergy- it was Fire, Water, Earth, and Air!

The synergy of cultivating these four laws at the same time allowed for him to simply speed run their advancements, the four Origin Cores of these laws spinning at such a rapid rate as they devoured all the essence they came across.

Yet as Noah stared at the new information on the laws of Karma, Aether, Light, Dark, Space, and Time...he couldn’t help but bring a smile to his face while thinking of the future!

The Novus Galaxy had a grand plan aside from Noah completing his Infinite Mana trait, and it was a plan that Aldrich had helped to initiate as he sent out a signal to the chaotic Universe! Whatever this signal would bring to them...Noah had to be ready for it as it would determine the success or failure of the plan that his own Galaxy had concocted, and whether it faced another cycle of destruction and rebirth because of it! 𝕗𝚛𝚎𝚎𝐰𝗲𝗯𝗻𝚘𝚟𝚎𝗹.𝕔𝐨𝕞

It was a shocking and cruel plan when considering the lives of the creatures within this wide Novus Galaxy...yet Noah could not help but think back to the grand goal that the entity overseeing the Novus Galaxy had discussed with him back then, his face becoming firm as he chose to continue advancing his strength and make sure nothing went wrong.
