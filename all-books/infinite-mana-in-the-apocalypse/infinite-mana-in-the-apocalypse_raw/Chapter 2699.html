2697 The Way to Scale the Unscalable! V

A brilliant half-formed scimitar.

It radiated glorious purple waves of Pure Hyperversal Extremity Authority of ANDROMEDA as similar to the Hyperversal Bow of Helios, it was an Armament of Extremity that was being formed with a Havenly Authority at its basis!

It now sat atop the Aletheian Havens of other Havenly Authorities as the Pure Hyperversal Extremity Authority blared out like an unfolding sea, covering the nearby 1,000 light years in diameter before the surging tendrils of ANDROMEDA pulsed and maintained this stellar domain.

This was the Andromeda Cleaver!

And by obtaining it, Noah could possibly do a great deal of things as first and foremost…

[My people…]

HUM!

His voice roared out with majesty and grandeur as his main body appeared in the periphery of the Armament of Extremity.

His voice echoed out to all Apex Aeonic Lifeforms under his banner as through the purity of his Lineage, Henry was the first to arrive as he was soon followed up by Aurelia and Orion!

Barbatos, Guinevere, Kazuhiko, the Sword Emperor, and many others appeared soon after that as their numbers only kept increasing.

All of their main bodies were in the Infinite Hyperversal Haven as only their Vitalistic Clones ventured into the Supernal Havens of Extremity, and they were currently stretched out across multiple Havenly Archipelagos as they lived and breathed in Challenger Arenas and collected Units of Havenly Aurora!

As soon as they neared the 10,000 threshold or exceeded it to the extent that even Extremities could begin paying attention, they transferred all the Units to Noah for safekeeping as he was acting like one of the Vaults of Extremity that the Four Hyperversal Havenly Extremity Lineages established.

These Units would then be utilized by them whenever they wished to purchase Havenly Treasures that met their needs, with his strongest Vassals being reborn through the baptism of battles and loot as they actually put their concepts of Extremity as their own!

And now, he called them here to grant them access to something he fought hard to get multiple times.

[A Half-Forged Armament of Extremity lies before you. The Andromeda Cleaver…if you can bear and absorb the Pure Hyperversal Extremity Authority it radiates, you will obtain the Havenly Boundary of Andromeda. If you are inches from it, you will achieve the 17th Layer. If you are able to wield it…

Extremity is yours…]

His voice was filled with Vitalis and Vacuous Hyperversal Authority as he spoke, his words causing clamor and shock to spread among his people- even those who thought they knew Noah better by now!

If one had to be an Extremity to wield it…then how did it get into their Infinite Hyperversal Haven?

Was Noah already….

Many gazed at his figure with astonished and questioning looks as in a corner, even the figure of the demoness Lilith could be seen observing with a difficult expression!

She seemed to bury many things in her heart at this moment as she was among the first to ask no questions.

She only walked forward.

Henry and the others were also at the forefront as with their Boundary Layers barely reaching the 13th or 14th as their highest, they came in contact with and tried to overcome the Pure Hyperversal Extremity Authority of a Half-

Forged Armament of Extremity! 𝗳𝚛𝗲𝕖𝚠𝚎𝚋𝗻𝗼𝕧𝗲𝐥.𝚌𝚘𝐦

At the same as Noah released this opportunity to his Vassals, the area surrounding him flickered as the crimson gold humanoid figure of RUINATION appeared from the depths of his soul.

Her gaze was fixated on the Andromeda Cleaver as Noah's voice rang out lightly.

"Can you deconstruct it and at the very least duplicate it? Or can you only devour it to gain its status?"

…!

His voice calmed and wasn't burning with authority as even after he asked…

[...]

Ruination remained quiet.

For the first time in many weeks and months, RUINATION didn't reply to Noah right away on whether she could do something or not!

She took her time as her eyes seemed to flash with glory and uncertainty at the very same time.

And soon after…

[I need to be close to be sure.]

Her words were resolute as he also began to walk into the 1,000 Light Year Domain!

At this moment, an Armament of Extremity was about to be put to full use by the whole Infinite Hyperversal Haven- the one to make this happen being the Infernal Havenly Commerce.

Its source…one of the Seven Clans.

The Havenly Moon Clan had traded a Half-Forged Armament of Extremity for millions of Havenly Hyperversal Emperor Lineage Blood Sources.

Such a reality and knowledge was spreading far and wide as it was easy to speculate…just what effect it would have on the current state of affairs of the Seven Clans? And what of the Aeonic Inheritances of Extremity?!

—

In the Nether Haven of Genesis.

The Seer of Sima was looking at a man shrouded in a billowing storm of golden white brilliance, his aura sharp and oppressive to all others as around him, tendrils of Vitalis Hyperversal Authority swan freely!

He was filled with power, and yet the Seer of Sima treated him normally as she spoke at this time.

[The Havenly Moon Clan has just used an Armament of Extremity to buy all of their most important forces the keys to unlock the Havenly Hyperversal Emperor Lineage. I've told you that if the Sima Clan is to survive with a decent number of us keeping our lives, something like this will be critical. I know you've had everyone collecting and filling up the Vaults of Sima all these years…it is time to empty them. If not an Armament of Extremity, then other oddities and rarities you haven't let go all these years..]

Her words were resolute.

The man billowing with white gold storms of brilliance was quiet before he gave a light nod a moment later!

This…was just the Sima Clan.

The other members of the Seven Clans reacted in varying manners after learning of the actions of the Havenly Moon Clan, but there were others with an even stronger reaction who held just as much power as the Seven Clans!

The most powerful Inheritance of Extremity!
