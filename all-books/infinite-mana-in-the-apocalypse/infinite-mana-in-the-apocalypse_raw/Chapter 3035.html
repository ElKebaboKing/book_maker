Chapter 3033 Expansion V

ROAR!

A violet existential authority raged as it began to feel an undeniable devouring.

Across its Unstable Anomalous Existential Domain, different anchors- different Seeds were placed that began to devour everything about it.

Its signature within the very Omniverse itself was being devoured!

This encroached upon its very source. Upon what it stood for.

Violet.

This was the color spread out across the Unstable Anomalous Existential Domain as even though it was a natal Existential Extremity Authority with nobody to command it.

It had reached this current stage even though it was born not too long ago, and if given eons to continue, even with the errors that caused its instability…it may have culminated to become a terrifying Unstable Anomalous Existential Domain!

Because its weavings were grand ones.

Filled with majesty and grace even amidst the chaos of instability, Noah had found the identity of this Authority through the Existential Automatons a while ago!

And at this moment as his Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed devoured it, the chest of an Existential Automaton opened up like a door as from it, Noah's Dominium Body stepped out.

Simmering with wild power.

Glorious Pairs of purple wings on his back and feet.

And his chest laid bare for his sculpted visage to bless the surrounding reality.

He appeared as the moment he did so, dense rivers of violet light seemingly found their target- the one behind all of this as they converged towards Noah's figure in anger!

Noah looked up at the perilous rivers of Existential Extremity Authority as he merely stood there, letting it smash into his body and in this surrounding space as…

CRACKLE!

His body glistened like a reinforced diamond that nothing could pass through.

And amidst this storm, his body began to give off tendrils of golden Dignity.

Of Tyranny.

Of Infinity.

Of Quintessence!

He gave off such brilliance as his intent weaved out!

|Do not be defiant, I will raise you to a level even you cannot fathom.|

WAA!

Surrounded by his own pristine authorities, Noah told the natal Existential Extremity Authority of this Unstable Anomalous Existential Domain to fall in line.

That he would elevate it to a level it couldn't even imagine!

The violet tendrils of light paused while staring at the boundlessly coiling Dignity.

At Quintessence, Infinity, and Tyranny!

And after it ascertained the state of the current reality…

WAA!

The raging rivers of violet light became calm.

It sensed the immeasurable Dignity from the entity before it as this natal authority fully felt like its distinction would not be wasted if it submitted to this being- and there didn't even seem to be much of an option anyway!

The hidden seas of Dignity it was shown just simply granted complete acceptance.

|Come.|

Noah beckoned to it as the violet authority seethed and began to surge its Tendrils of light into Noah's body.

It would do well.

Its extreme would be signified well with this being.

After all, it was the Existential Extremity Authority of Sovereignty!

Sovereignty!

HUUM!

The wings behind Noah's back flapped.

For a brief moment, they seemed to expand and cover the countless light years this Unstable Anomalous Existential Domain stretched out to.

And a moment later…

WAP!

All incoming violet authority of Sovereignty instantly disappeared into Noah's body, and the vast stretches of the Unstable Anomalous Existential Domain also disappeared into the Miniature Existential Omniversal Seeds!

|...|

Everything happened in an instant.

The Existential Automatons, Illusory Weavings, and newly conquered Imperium Royal Decimara and his guard were the only ones left behind to bathe in the shocking result of this brilliance!

A result that somehow within the span of an instant, the entire Unstable Anomalous Existential Domain was devoured.

Decimara trembled in shock while the Illusory Weavings kneeled with respect.

They didn't ask how.

While Noah himself…

{You have discovered another feature of the Existential Invictus Dominium Wings!}

{The Invictus Wings are capable of affecting, accelerating, and bringing to fruition the Existential Causality of an action or event that is ascertained to happen with the highest probable chance within the span of an instant.}

…!

Existential Causality.

Cause and effect!

If an event had the highest probable chance of happening, Noah could affect it, accelerate it, or even bring it to fruition in the span of an instant.

|...|

Noah's mind buzzed at such a finding as back in the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed, his Dominium Origin felt the influx of the whole Unstable Anomalous Existential Domain of Sovereignty bloom like a singularity atop the Extremity Vein, its authority being assimilated into the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed rapidly as everything within was enriched!

But even as this scene played out, Noah was still stuck thinking about the Invictus Dominium Wings and their capability to affect Causality.

It had just happened naturally without Noah directly putting his mind to it, but what if he focused on an event directly and utilized this terrifying power to bring it to fruition?

An event…

HUUM!

His mind buzzed.

His Dominium Origin was the only one remaining within the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed giving a lecture as the Existential Omniversal Guider, and at this moment, in his humanoid form of his Dominium Origin…he raised his hands up while focusing on an event.

His people only watched him unblinkingly as they wondered what he was doing now.

But Noah was unfettered as he thought about…the production of Fruits of Existence by the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed.

This was an event that would continue to happen for the foreseeable future.

It was something guaranteed with the highest probability….so could he affect Causality and bring to fruition the production of all the Fruits of Existence for some time frame in the future within the span of the next instant?

The production of Fruits of Existence that had its rate already rapidly increased?

…!

This was the idea.

And as soon as he focused on it, the dazzling purple wings behind his back shone, and then proceeded to enlarge with an illusory light as if their wingspan was covering all of the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed!

Even the wings on his feet fluttered and radiated a blinding radiance as…

{The Existential Causality Feature of the Invictus Dominium Wings has been applied on the production of Fruits of Existence within the Infinite Existential Omniversal Seed for the currently possible maximum time of 24 hours.}

…!

A single prompt.

And an instant later…

BOOM!

A glorious eruption of thousands of Fruits of Existence gushed out in front of Noah, twinkling over the astonished gazes of his people like stellar blinking stars!
