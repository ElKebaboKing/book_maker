Chapter 1157 - A Primordial Beast! II

A sound even louder than the implosion of a universe.

A sound that stemmed from a single being gloriously appearing before a Primordial Beast as he pushed out his hand that was glowing a myriad of colorful lights and smashed into the oversized creature in a devastating fashion!

BOOM...CRACK!

With a shatter and a crack of the very void of ruination, it seemed as if Noah’s small figure had rammed into a think and impenetrable balloon as his fist that was reinforced by Runic Dao Lines from a Lesser and Cosmic Dao actually tore apart the myriad of shields of ruination that the creature had conjured, smashing into its body as the impact sent it shooting back a few thousand miles!

"ROAAR!"

A painful cry ruptured out of the body of the Primordial Beast, its majestic figure stabilizing after a few thousand more miles as its eyes turned to look towards Noah in shock.

The eyes of King Augustus and the Antiquities around him also trembled as they could see the area where the Primordial Beast had been hit...ruptured muscle could be seen as golden blood sprayed out gloriously.

"...!"

Silence!

There was utter silence of shock not just from the Primordial Beast, but also the watching Antiquities as the Beast they could barely defend against was actually pushed back by a single punch from this being!

When their eyes locked upon his right hand that glimmered with a myriad of Dao Lines, their eyes brightened up as King Augustus voiced out with a vibrant and majestic voice.

"Daolord!"

OOOOOH!

A terrifying title was released by the leading Antiquity of the Indigo Cosmos as currently, Noah’s figure was experiencing waves and waves of power coursing through him!

The feeling of the Runic Dao Lines swirling within his right hand was to the point of it being euphoric, making him want to put in more and more essence into his hands as the Runic Dao Lines would only swirl faster.

Nomological Edicts were unique...but the Runic Dao Lines had something with them that Noah couldn’t put a finger on, but its feeling of power alone was purely stupendous as Noah’s figure undulated with waves of light, his eyes and mouths releasing rays of golden light amidst his bellow as his figure turned into a streak of light that arrived in front of the Primordial Beast once more, another punch was thrown out!

BOOM!

It was a fight of brutality and pure strength as with the Runic Dao Lines of Annihilation and Withering giving stupendous boosts as well as the 10 million percent increase in All Parameters from the Nomological Edict of Samsara... Noah received the perfect opponent to test the extent of his current strength.

It was against a magisterial Primordial Beast that looked like a combination of phoenixes and dragons, its golden white fur shimmering brightly in the crimson seas as at this moment, its golden blood had begun to leak out with fervor!

BOOM!

"ROAAR!"

Its furious bellows could be heard as it swiped at empty stops where Noah used to be, this existence not being able to catch Noah’s figure on speed alone as its pristine skin continued to get mutilated while golden blood flowed out.

The eyes of the Primordial Beast flashed with wrath and madness at such a scene, its body undulating with Ruination essence as Noah’s figure continued to weave around out and releasing terrific punches that were unavoidable!

The Antiquities of the Indigo Cosmos watched this scene in a stupor as the beast that they would have considered the ender of their Cosmos was actually matched against a single being. But these beings were not the only ones in a stupor.

From the direction their gazes had left, the enormous figure of the Colossal Helios Leviathan was floating forward powerfully. Above it, the humanoid form of Tiamat began to pulse with power as her curvaceous figure stood at the forefront of all the Animus Summons.

"We won’t just be here gawking...show your worth to Master and don’t be mere leeches!"

RUMBLE!

At her words, Noah’s summons flew out like streaks of light fearlessly, her own figure letting out powerful waves as a ten-headed dragon very soon erupted from it, rushing towards the enormous golden Primordial Beast that Noah was powerfully facing!

ZIP!

The cerulean figures of the Blue Slimes and Obsidian Panther were at the very forefront, their eyes shining brilliantly as they joined a battle of brutality to test their worth at the stage of Antiquity for the first time.

Noah felt his summons joining the battle as his smile only became brighter, the beat of his heart becoming even more excited as he rose above the figure of the Primordial Beast that gazed up at him with wrath. It had been trying to grasp onto him unsuccessfully in the past few seconds, the body of the beast currently vibrating with dangerous waves of power as at this time...Noah would go ahead and give it another head-on collision!

WAA!

In a magisterial fashion, his figure rose like a majestic Phoenix before descending down head-on towards the terrifying Primordial Beast

---

Augustus Marcus Tiberius! this was his name and title as the King of the Indigo Cosmos, this being having seen a great deal of things throughout his life.

He had seen much, but the scene of this being before him would forever be etched into his mind!

"Daolord..."

He called out a title again as he and the allies around him could only call the vibrant being this name after seeing the pulsating Runic Dao Lines coursing through his hand. 𝙛𝒓𝓮𝙚𝔀𝒆𝒃𝓷𝒐𝓿𝙚𝓵.𝙘𝒐𝒎

Daolords! Existences that had fully assimilated the abstruse laws of Ruination and the Primordial Daos as they crossed the vast Ruination Seas and lay waste to Primordial Beast using the tremendous power they obtained from the deconstruction of Daos!

Such beings were few and rare to see, with King Augustus only knowing of them as they were the Champions of the force he had already called for help from.

He also knew how to identify those beings as the one before him...didn’t stem from that force!

’Just who is....’

The thought of this being’s identity crossed his mind as the surrounding situation changed at this moment, all of them watching as his figure rose above the Primordial Beast before descending towards it in a fervor.

What was different this time around was that he wasn’t descending down with his shimmering multicolored right hand outstretched alone, with there being a myriad of Dao Essences extending behind him as the most potent one was the Primordial Dao!

From his small figure, they could hear a set of words ringing in their ears clearly.

"Primordial Wheel of Extinction."

THRUM!

The first ability of a specially designed skill tree for the Cosmic Primordial Dao made its first appearance at this moment!

A pristine white wheel larger than a galaxy bloomed in the Ruination Sea as the beautiful crimson in the surroundings began to be dyed utterly white!
