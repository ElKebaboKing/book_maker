Chapter 2580 Overpowered At Its Best! IX

Every time, his soul would heal as his Concepts and Dynamis backed him. And every time, he would be destroyed by Vacuous and Vitalis Hyperversal Authorities!

Fantastically, it took his soul longer and longer to be destroyed after every cycle as ever so subtly…traces of Vitalis and Vacuous Hyperversal Authority were being infused on the very soul they were constantly destroying.

The Vitalis Hyperversal Authority was originally only going to baptize Noah's soul and elevate Quintessentially due to his actions in the Unrecorded History.

And yet now, it continued to flow into Noah's soul and fight with the Vacuous Hyperversal Authority that had its source stemming from Noah's own foundation and the Indefatigable Dynamis of Extremity!

It was a form of Vacuous Hyperversal Authority as it differed from the potent authority of Vacuous Malignancies- the Vacuous Daimonikos who lived outside of reality, space, and time.

The Authority Noah was working with was barely at the level of potency that Vacuous Malignancies held before their First Vacuous Awakening.

And there were many others over the eons who had attained different forms of Vacuous Hyperversal Authority!

None of them had been destroyed the moment they did so. Noah himself wasn't rejected or destroyed when he first accessed Vacuous Hyperversal Authority as the major powers of Extremity even considered this authority the greatest tool to fight against Vacuous Malignancies!

None of them talked about Vitalis Hyperversal Authority.

None of them talked of it as very few even knew of it!

Frankly…Vitalis Hyperversal Authority was even greater than Vacuous Hyperversal Authority.

Whenever the two authorities met, they would repel or devour each other depending on the concentration as always, Vitalis would come out on top.

Rifts, spaces, or pockets of Vacuous Hyperversal Authority created within the Seven Vitalis Havens would always reduce and be erased naturally as they never expanded.

Vitalis Hyperversal Authority was so potent that Vacuous Malignancies could not survive within the Seven Vitalis Havens- within the Seven Aeonic Havens!

This was how distinguished and astonishing it was!

The reason for Noah in his current state…was him daring to have a foundation infused with Vacuous Hyperversal Authority, for his soul to hold a source of it in the form of an Absolute Dynamis of Extremity, and then coincidentally undertaking an action within an Unrecorded History that awarded him a baptism of Vitalis Hyperversal Authority.

It all concluded with these two authorities being enclosed within the core of a single Entity.

Within the soul of a single existence!

And these two authorities…would never mix. One would always drown out the other.

Like fire and water. Like light and dark…but to an even greater extent!

If ever in any Recorded or Unrecorded History where any soul tried intentionally or by mistake to put these two Authorities in the core of their existence…death was their only reprise.

Like the deaths that Noah was experiencing over and over again.

Normally, Vitalis and Vacuous Hyperversal Authority should have clashed as another astounding genius would fall in the pages of the Aeonic Annals of Extremity.

Yet…

{Your Quintessential Hyperversal Soul has experienced a version of True Death.}

The anomaly that was Noah came to be.

His soul would continue to be fractured and he would die, but this same soul would be reforged an instant later!

For him, death would only come when a massive Infinite Hyperversal Haven perished.

So during this battle where Vitalis continuously attacked and repelled Vacuous Hyperversal Authority, unimaginable changes were forced to occur over and over again!

The Vacuous Hyperversal Authority that Noah held a minuscule potency of…was encouraged as after every death, its source seemingly became stronger as it came ever closer towards true Vacuous Hyperversal Authority.

And the Vitalis Hyperversal Authority that should have been cut off from flowing into Noah's soul…continued to flow as it had yet to achieve its purpose!

{Your Quintessential Hyperversal Soul has experienced a version of True Death.}

Death after death.

Cycle after cycle within the span of instants that were carried into nanoseconds.

Every time, the potency of Vacuous Hyperversal Authority increased.

Every time, more of it was infused in Noah's soul as traces of Vitalis Hyperversal Authority were being infused the same!

During all of these cycles, Noah withstood the tremendous pain of his soul being torn apart before it was put together again.

He withstood it all while shouldering the pain because he could see the cycles taking longer and longer!

He could see his soul being slightly different after every cycle as this wasn't the only thing being forcefully altered.

During the cycles of True Deaths of his soul, many things were impacted.

The Perennial Yggdrasil of Extremity.

The 81 Quintessential Hyperversal Extremity Meridian Seeds in his Main Body.

The 81 Quintessential Hyperversal Extremity Meridians in Alexander's body.

His Hyperversal Forge.

And as these changes occurred within his soul…their effects to a lesser extent reached his body and Origin as minute traces of Vitalis and Vacuous Hyperversal Authorities made their way over!

The cycles of his soul being torn had been so quick that they had occurred many times within an instant, but gradually, they began to last as long as a nanosecond.

And then as long as 10 nanoseconds. 1,000 Nanoseconds. 1 Millisecond!

On every junction of time past the passage of an instant alone, the number of true deaths exceeded a million.

A million!

And they continued until…

BZZT!

A second passed entirely as the two authorities raged within his soul, but his soul was not destroyed.

It felt like a thick and viscous mass that absorbed the shocks of the two authorities, and every time they damaged him…he healed just as fast as his state no longer deteriorated!

His soul breathed a exhale of tyranny.

He gazed at the authorities still raging within his soul coldly as because of them, too many things had changed.

{An Unfathomable Quintessential Alteration has occurred to your Soul.}

His capabilities came back to life as the profundities around him were quantified, prompts beginning to appear once more.

He gazed at his current Soul as its state was startling to look at.

After millions of cycles of True Death, changes had occurred every time his soul came back together as the multiple Perennial Yggdrasil of Extremity in his soul…were nowhere to be seen.

Instead, a single utterly massive and incandescent tree that screamed out ancientness power rose from the depths of his soul!

The tree had multiple extending thick trunks that flourished into other fantastically massive trees- with the Indefatigable, Vitalistic, and other Perennial Yggdrasil of Extremity seemingly now coming from trunks of this massive tree!

The 81 Quintessential Hyperversal Extremity Meridians in Alexander's body underwent a astonishing alteration.

But more than that…the 81 Quintessential Hyperversal Extremity Meridian Seeds in his Main Body Were altered to such an disturbing degree that…

{The Quintessential Hyperversal Extremity Meridian Seeds within your main body have transformed into the Quintessential Hyperversal Extremity Overlord Meridian Seeds!}

…!

{The Authority of the 10th Boundary Layer of Quintessence is now being expressed within the Aspects of your Main Body!}

…!

{The Authority of the 11th Boundary Layer of Quintessence is now being expressed within the Aspects of your Main Body!} 𝘧𝓇ℯ𝑒𝓌𝑒𝑏𝓃𝘰𝘷𝘦𝘭.𝒸ℴ𝓂

{The Authority of the 12th Boundary Layer of Quintessence is now being expressed within the Aspects of your Main Body!}

He did not undergo Apexification with his main body.

And yet…!

{The Authority of the 13th Boundary Layer of Quintessence is now being expressed within the Aspects of your Main Body!}

With Quintessence in the lead, the authorities of Boundaries that his soul within Alexander had extended were being expressed in his Main Body in an disturbing turn of events!
