Chapter 1748 VENERABLE! I

"Sanguine Emperor…"

OOOM!

The concept that was the cause of the coming of the Quasi-Venerable Unnamed Kainos Emperor!

The concept that had granted him TABOO authority from his Ascendancy Halos after its last upgrade allowed him to pack 60 Flames of Edicts into a single Halo!

This…was a concept that would help him greatly when fighting against terrifying existences or even LEGENDS as at this moment, Noah's will surged out with a command as the Seed of TABOO trembled.

CRACK!

From his chest, an amalgamation of a concept actually flew out as his skin split apart, an incandescent crimson light moving from him and transplanting itself into the Seed of TABOO as this utterly black seed began to shine with a vibrant bloody light!

Along with this, Noah's very cells and blood cheered as they began to feel large-scale changes occurring beginning from a concept that weaved itself around Noah too much being elevated to a stage of TABOO.

As Noah felt the fundamentally transforming Sanguine Emperor, he also felt three types of his essences to be burning and also undergoing changes within him!

This was a passive process that had begun after he forged the Seed of TABOO, and it could be explained by the two prompts that had passed through his eyes before.

<Your Fortune and Destiny have begun to be refined to a higher level.>

<Your Mana has begun to be refined to a higher level.>

Refinement!

Three types of his essence had actually begun to undergo refinement as this was all Noah knew, not being able to find anything else about it or what it could lead to so he could only…

"After the achievement of making a Seed of TABOO, my reserves of Essence of Reality increased but fortune, Destiny, and Mana began to undergo refinement…what is the purpose of that refinement?"

As a bloody light of wonder raged around him and the Sanguine Emperor left behind the shackles of Quasi-TABOO, he asked Lavalliere calmly.

<Your essences becoming refined is normally a process that occurs when building your Reality. It is one of the things that allows for the Reification of abilities.>

WAA!

Noah's eyes blazed with astounding light at this answer!

REIFICATION!

Abilities gaining personification and turning into something astounding. Noah always wondered what Fireball or Sever would be Reified into, and it seemed there was a possibility for him to do this before he reached the stage of a LEGEND.

A possibility!

<I can only say that much without incurring Karma, if you wanna know more, I can eat up .25 Uses…>

"...No." This terrifying treasure tried to hook him into utilizing his very few uses for information, Noah's will remaining stable as he knew the mysteries would open up in time.

The only question was when the refinement of essence would finish, and what other factors were required that could possibly make REIFICATION possible before someone became a LEGEND.

HOOOONG!

The ranging essence became even wilder as the Seed of TABOO entirely turned a bloody crimson light and sank into Noah's body at this moment!

It went on to pervade throughout his whole body as while seated on the Throne of Arcadian Tyranny, a radiant black light flashed over Noah's visage.

THUMP!

The beat of his heart was clear as his figure began to vibrate with glorious authority!

A power seemed to be trying to burst out and let itself be known, with Noah containing it so far but within mere moments- it seemed too vibrant and unshakable as it caused Noah to bellow out and released a beam of bloody crimson light that turned the golden skies of the Cosmic Holy Land red!

<The first TABOO concept has been inscribed onto your Soul.>

<The Nomological Edict of the Sanguine Emperor has been elevated to TABOO.>

BZZZT!

Golden prompts that uniquely seemed to be painted with streaks of glimmering red bloomed within Noah's soul as his three Aspects of Existence vibrated powerfully, each of them being bathed by Noah's very own TABOO authority as it came with astounding significance!

So astounding that at this moment, the Bloodline that was taking in aspects of all Bloodlines Noah obtained was burning with utmost authority and luster as it released pulses of shocking authority.

<The TABOO Authority of the Sanguine Emperor is unwilling for its Unnamed Kainos Emperor Bloodline to remain lower after its advancement.>

HOOOONG!

Glorious obsidian light raged out as Noah's bellow that should have been ending soon became even more sonorous as the beam of bloody light he released only enlarged!

After being taken halfway past Quasi-Venerable and with Noah devouring the Bloodlines of many Primordial Legions, an undeniable authority descended onto the Unnamed Kainos Emperor as the first act of TABOO for the Sanguine Emperor was…

<The Unnamed Kainos Emperor Bloodline has been elevated to the VENERABLE Tier.>

BOOOM!

Noah's figure upon the Throne of Arcadian Tyranny exploded into a pure mass of blood.

It exploded out in a shocking fashion as a viscous crimson gold stellar bloody sea formed!

A stellar bloody sea filled with sparkling Cosmos and releasing a glorious light of power that could shake one's very soul pulsed and expanded to bathe over the whole Cosmic Holy Land!

They shone with utter magisterial light of true royalty as this Cosmos seemed to be closely intertwined and pulsing with the stellar bloody sea.

Kainos Primordial Mesenchymal Cells raged around this bloody sea in larger numbers as they transfused themselves onto everything- all of Noah's Aspects of Existence!

His soul became tainted by the authority of the Unnamed Kainos Emperor, and so did his Body and Origin as they seemed to be forcefully getting pulled towards a fundamental change.

As this sea seethed and birthed Noah's one and only VENERABLE Bloodline, more prompts continued to descend!

<All basic abilities under the Nomological Edict of the Sanguine Emperor have been greatly elevated, with the Ultimate Abilities under the Flames of the Sanguine Emperor undergoing the greatest change.>

…!

<Sanguine Aggregation has been commandeered by the VENERABLE Unnamed Kainos Emperor Bloodline as under it, the sub ability of [Sanguine Ascendancy Domain] is born.>

<The elevation to a VENERABLE Bloodline has perfected the seamless fusion of all Bloodlines as the True Form of Unnamed Kainos Tyrant has been changed and elevated to the Unnamed Kainos Emperor that can now be achieved when the authority of the Ascendancy Halos fuses with you.>

OOOOOM!

Boundlessly shocking and profoundly magisterial, a stellar bloody sea expanded and shone with ancient might as an anomalous existence continued on his path of insane strengthening and reinforcement of his foundation, magisterial prompts continuing to rise over his eyes as more and more boons descended!!!
