Chapter 4261: A Glorious Engineer! IV

The command was given.

The path was chosen!

RUIN/EDEN, the system architect of his new existence, unleashed herself!

Noah’s Existential Command Interface, the crimson-blue globe in his perception, lit up gloriously, its swirling authorities spinning into a maelstrom of focused, creative energy.

|Project Initiated: [Omnichalcum Core Modulator] - Osmont Specification.|

|Objective: Forge a growth-type Early Tool to serve as the primary interface for a symbiotic Living Existential Armor.|

|Primary Resource: [Early Omnichalcum Metal].|

|Primary Tool: Your Everything of [Infinite Mana] will be utilized as the conceptual ’limbs’ to command and mold the metal.|

|Analysis: An excellent choice, Master. Using an infinite resource to shape an indestructible material is the very definition of working smarter, not harder. Let’s begin.|

The blue flames of Mana that had wreathed Noah’s form now surged outward. They were no longer a chaotic, brilliant fire, but had coalesced into a thousand delicate, precise tendrils of pure, liquid energy.

They moved with a surgeon’s grace and an artist’s flair, each one a perfect extension of RUIN/EDEN’s will, a limb to shape the stuff of glory!

As the tendrils of mana began to caress the floating blocks of Omnichalcum, a projection of an intricate, holographic structure materialized in the air before Noah.

It was a thing of impossible, beautiful complexity, a design that seemed to defy the very laws of geometry.

He smiled, a silent question forming in his mind.

’What are you doing, EDEN?’

Her reply was instantaneous, a cascade of cool, confident logic.

|For the Modulator’s primary design, I am utilizing the macro-structure of your Infiniverse and the micro-structure of your Early Atlas of the Folds, Master. The overall form will be a miniature, stabilized version of your own domain.|

|I am also incorporating the energy transference and collection principles of the Primordial Lodestone that nearly unmade you. A bit of poetic justice, I think. We are turning the weapon of your enemy into the heart of your new armor.|

|All of this is being filtered through the authority of the Ingeniare Tower of Osmont. It is providing the core engineering and architectural inspiration for this grand task. It is quite pleased to finally be put to some real work.|

HUUM!

Noah nodded, his eyes shining. As he processed her design philosophy, the Omnichalcum Metals, under the gentle, yet irresistible, command of his Mana, began to change.

They lost their solid form, not melting, but becoming a single, cohesive mass of stellar white, liquid light.

This liquid metal then began to flow, to overlap onto the holographic structure in the air, a perfect, one-to-one translation of design into reality.

It was a glorious scene!

The Early Omnichalcum, a material that had resisted the will of beings for eons, was being twisted, shaped, and manipulated with an effortless grace that was a profound violation of its very nature!

Elara watched, her crimson eyes wide with a disbelief so profound it had silenced her.

"What the hell..." she finally whispered, her voice a fragile, broken thing. "Just how grand is your concentration of Everything to do this? Even I... I could maybe keep this up for a little while, but you... you are not even straining."

...!

Blue waves of brilliance radiated from Noah as he continued to pour his infinite, boundless Mana into the project.

The liquid metal, now shining with a network of blazing golden lines, continued its work, flowing with a perfect, architectural precision.

The prompts from RUIN/EDEN continued to flash, a running commentary on the birth of a new marvel.

|Structural integrity at 99.8%.|

|Conceptual resonance is stable.|

|Integrating the final layer of the Lodestone’s transference matrix... complete.|

|Forging Complete.|

HUUM!

The holographic design vanished, leaving in its place a single, magnificent, and utterly terrifying object.

The Omnichalcum Core Modulator.

It was a circular, wheel-like structure, about the size of a large shield, its surface a swirling, milky white that seemed to contain Folds of captured starlight.

Intricate golden lines, forming a pattern that was both a circuit board and a constellation, pulsed with a soft, steady light.

And from its center, a single, massive, needle-like white structure, a full meter in length, poked out, its tip impossibly sharp, humming with a contained, hungry power!

The moment it was completed, a wave of white-gold brilliance gushed out from it, a pulse of such profound, conceptual weight that it caused the very air of the laboratory to feel heavy, suffocating.

|[The Omnichalcum Core Modulator – Phase I] has been successfully forged.|

|Classification: Growth-Type Symbiotic Early Tool.|

|Qualities:|

| - The Creature’s Echo: Contains a minute, yet potent, weaving of [THE Creature]’s Everything. At its current stage, it grants a passive, permanent increase of +75 Quadrillion to your Complexity and Purity.|

| - Symbiotic Growth: This Modulator is a living tool that will grow alongside you. It is currently in Phase I: The Keystone. Its later stages, Phase II: The Symbiote and Phase III: The Zenith, will be unlocked as your own existence continues to evolve.|

| - Exclusive Interface: This Modulator is fused to the unique signature of your [Infinite Mana]. If any other existence were to attempt integration, their being would be instantly and catastrophically collapsed.|

|Primary Purpose: To fuse with your existence and serve as the primary amplifier and support structure for a personalized Living Existential Armor.|

...!

Noah gazed over all these glorious prompts as behind him, Elara, her voice a choked whisper, finally spoke. "Is that really...?"

Noah nodded, his gaze fixed on the magnificent, floating tool. "Yes. A Modulator to accept a Living Existential Armor smoothly. This one is specific to me, so..."

He turned, a fearsome, tyrannical light blazing in his eyes. He looked at the Modulator, at this new, beautiful, and terrible part of himself, and he bellowed, his voice a grand, irrevocable command.

"Integrate!"

In front of a shocked, horrified Elara, the Omnichalcum Core Modulator shot towards him. The spiky, needle-like end aimed directly at his back.

"You reckless fucker!" Elara bellowed, her professional composure finally, completely shattering.

"Don’t integrate with raw Omnichalcum without any tests or experiments! You’ll tear your own existence apart!"

But her words were just wind. He forged ahead. The needle plunged into his back with a sickening, wet crunch of bone and authority!

HUUM!

And then, the pain began. It was an excruciating, all-consuming agony, a fire that was not of heat, but of fundamental, conceptual rewriting.

|Integration Protocol Initiated. This may be... uncomfortable, Master.|

|The Omnichalcum Core Modulator is fusing with your spinal column and your central network of authority.|

|Your existence is resisting the intrusion of a foreign, primordial object.|

|Analysis: The pain you are experiencing is your own body trying to reject a part of a unique cluster of Everythings. I recommend... embracing it.| 𝒻𝑟𝘦𝘦𝘸ℯ𝒷𝑛𝘰𝓋ℯ𝘭.𝘤𝘰𝘮

Noah gritted his teeth, his entire body trembling with the strain. He could feel it, the cold, alien metal weaving its way into his very being, its golden lines connecting to his nerves, his Atlas, his Heart!

It was a violation of the highest order, but it was a violation he had chosen. He would not just wield this power; he would become it!

The process felt like an eternity. The blue flames of his Mana raged around him, not as a tool now, but as a desperate, protective cocoon, trying to soothe the agony of his transformation.

And then, as suddenly as it had begun, the pain stopped. A profound, humming warmth spread from his back, a feeling of completeness, of a missing piece finally clicking into place.

At his back, where the needle had entered, the circular, wheel-like structure of the Modulator now rested, its surface humming with a steady, powerful rhythm.

It looked like a strange, beautiful, metallic engine, and from its core, radiant blue lights pulsed in time with his own heartbeat.

|Integration Successful.|

|The Omnichalcum Core Modulator is now a permanent part of your existence.|

|Analysis: Your existential framework has been successfully and irrevocably altered. You are now ready to forge your own specialized suit of armor, Master.|

WAA!
