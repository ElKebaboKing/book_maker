Chapter 4930: Three I

When something brilliantly filled with undifferentiation was frozen and then gloriously defined infinitely, the result was astoundingly unique.

Noah observed the entire figure of Bazuman crystallizing.

It began at the edges, where his Infinite Seals had first made contact with the undifferentiated surface. Blue-gold light spread across the churning proto-matter like frost forming on glass, but this frost didn’t freeze. It defined. Every particle of Bazuman’s existence that the light touched found itself suddenly knowing what it was, suddenly possessing boundaries and limitations and names that undifferentiation should never have permitted.

The crystallization spread inward with speed that exceeded perception.

The vast network of Bazuman’s existence, that infinite stomach-archive filled with consumed memories and accumulated power, transformed section by section. Walls that had been made of undifferentiated authority became crystalline structures of defined Infinity. Pillars that had supported ceilings of pure consumption became solid formations that knew their exact height and weight and purpose.

Noah’s defensive Inscription burned against his chest with comfortable warmth, allowing him to remain at the very core of this being without being affected by the transformation occurring around him. His offensive Inscriptions carried their Embodiments and Apophasis through the frozen structure, finding weaknesses and exploiting them, collapsing the very foundation of this Civilization of undifferentiation filled with hunger.

In moments, Bazuman became crystallized into blue-gold.

The countless crimson eyes that had blazed with hunger were now solid gemstones that held no life. The innumerable tentacles that had reached for Noah with desperate desire were now frozen extensions of defined authority. The mouths filled with teeth of crystallized memory were now structures that couldn’t consume because they’d been given definition that prevented consumption.

Noah could feel his own existence buzzing with anticipation as he looked at the crystallization of an Undivided One that seemed like his to take.

He carried out the Rite of Paradoxical Inheritance.

"The Everythings of what was collapsed are mine and not mine. They belong to me and they do not belong to me. They must be given to me instantly and they must never be given to me at all."

HUUM!

The moment he uttered these words, the blue-gold crystallization of Bazuman began to shine with light that pulsed in rhythms matching Noah’s own heartbeat. It tried to come toward his existence as he was rightfully claiming it, choosing to forego the Absolute Existential Palace in favor of direct integration. The crystallized structure began breaking apart into streams of authority that flowed toward him like rivers finding their ocean.

But as this process began...

Noah activated THE Numerical Sovereignty silently.

The mathematical structures within his existence shifted, numbers flickering at the edges of his perception as he imposed operations upon the incoming harvest. He commanded for his weavings to multiply by ten. He commanded for the incoming harvest and weavings of Bazuman to be raised by an exponent of three.

...!

Prompts bloomed before his eyes with weight that pressed against his consciousness.

|Multiplicative Amplification Applied to Crystallized Undifferentiated Civilization|

|After 10x Multiplicative Amplification, the density and Immensity of the Crystallized Undifferentiated Civilization of Bazuman has been increased by a factor of 10 and amplified by a factor of 10. The harvest you are receiving now contains concentrated authority that exceeds what Bazuman originally possessed by an order of magnitude.|

|Exponential Expression Applied|

|After Exponential Expression with an Exponent of 3, the Depth of the Crystallized Undifferentiated Civilization of Bazuman has been transformed exponentially. The compounding effect has elevated this harvest from the classification of an Undivided One to something approaching the classification of beings that shaped THE Prima Indifferentia itself.|

|Final Assessment: The harvest you are now receiving is equivalent to One Fourth of THE Civilizational Harvest of a Primordial Architect.|

BOOM!

Noah’s eyes illuminated with glorious Immensity at these prompts!

A fourth of a Primordial Architect?! Bazuman’s loot had been elevated from an Undivided One to be equivalent to a fourth of a Primordial Architect?!

These were beings Noah hadn’t even met, the cream of the crop in THE Prima Indifferentia! Entities that had existed since THE First Cause itself, that had shaped the nature of differentiation and undifferentiation, that possessed power exceeding even THE Four!

His weavings buzzed with profound tyranny and quintessence as the harvest began flowing into his existence with force that made his foundations tremble with anticipation rather than strain. Before things could proceed any further...

More prompts bloomed.

|Unexpected Finding Observed|

|Due to the set corruption of Bazuman from the Corrupted Paradoxical Proto-matter of THE Living Paradox, 10% of your harvest has automatically been siphoned off by an Absolute Paradoxical Weaving of Existence supported by an Axiom. This siphoning occurred before your Multiplicative Amplification and Exponential Expression could be applied to the affected portion.|

|Assessment: Any corrupted Absolute killed in Observable Existence will grant a portion of harvest to THE Living Paradox.|

...!

The waves of glory and the slight smile that had been forming on Noah’s lips faded.

Fucking what?

10% of his Loot was gone?

Siphoned off to that philosophical peasant who had the audacity to steal from him?

In life, one could get angry many times. Absolutes weren’t above anger as many still had profound emotions when pushed enough. Noah had felt annoyance before. He’d felt irritation. He’d felt the cold calculation of vengeance being planned against enemies who had wronged him.

At this moment, Noah wasn’t angry.

He was furious!

So furious that his greatest enemy in existence might just change from that trash waste of existence known as THE Living Elemental to become THE Living Paradox!

And his fury only became grander and grander as he began receiving the tremendous boon of a percentage of the Immensity of a Primordial Architect. Because even as the harvest flooded into his existence with force that should have been glorious, even as his foundations expanded to accommodate power that exceeded anything he’d previously possessed...

He felt his Derivative Civilizations of Infinite Undifferentiated Fate and Infinite Hunger become full.

As if they’d taken in far, far too much.

With them being Half-Step Absolute, at this moment they were squeezing up and spilling past Half-Step as if they were saying... bro, we can’t take anymore!
