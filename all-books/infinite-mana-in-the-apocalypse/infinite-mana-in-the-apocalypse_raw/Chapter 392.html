Chapter 391 - An Inheritance!

<strong><Inheritance of the Disciple of Fire> :: Left behind to those fate intended, this inheritance grants the unique cultivation ability {Disciple of Fire}, as well as the Absolute Skill <<Herald of Flames>> that are meant to create the most powerful Incarnation of Flames.</strong>

The knowledge swiftly came into Noah’s mind as he hungrily looked at the abundant information stored in the crystal, the immense value of this treasure just now becoming clear in his mind!

The biggest value that could be gained from this <Inheritance of the Disciple of Fire> was not just its unique cultivation ability and the Absolute Skill, but the fact that this Inheritance was something that could be learned by others!

Similar to the treasure that the Celestials came across that they spread out to their disciples, Noah could bring his people into this area for them to gain this Inheritance themselves, or see if he could learn how to make Inheritance Crystals similar to what the Celestials were doing.

He had used his abundant essence to try and move this ancient pedestal from its spot as he began reading the information on this inheritance, but found that some sort of inviolable law was keeping it in place, where he could not even make it budge an inch! This would not be a problem though, as he would simply have to teleport his people here to have them experience this amazing boon as well.

His gaze went down towards the unique cultivation skill, {Disciple of Fire} first.

<strong>{Disciple of Fire} :: A technique geared towards cultivating the Law of Fire. Grants the user the ability to form a unique Fire Origin Core.</strong>

Only two sentences for the description of the skill, but he found it eerily similar to the {Fate’s Disciple} ability that cultivated the law of fate by inscribing Fate Lines on the body of the practitioner, but this {Disciple of Fire} instead allowed one to form a Fire Origin Core!

Noah put everything else aside as this unique skill that was extremely similar to the one dealing with the law of fate that he received from the Celestials took his central focus.

Far away from his position, the Righteous Fire Exploration Team had already returned after they finished hunting the last Fire Behemoth that also provided Noah with the last piece of the Incandescent Flameborn Set, with his main body going into the regal structures that served as the sleeping quarters of Celestials as he secluded himself.

A mini Blood World bloomed in the scintillating gold room that Noah was in as it covered him entirely, the knowledge about the unique skill {Disciple of Fire} filling his mind as he wanted to see how different it was to {Fate’s Disciple}!

RUMBLE!

It felt like an explosion was occurring inside his body the moment he activated {Disciple of Fire}, something new appearing deep inside him! At the position that could be considered the center of Noah’s body, there was an incorporeal blue colored core that held streaks of gold that was standing stably. This could be considered the center of his Origin, his Origin Core!

Besides this large Origin Core, a small dot that shone with a fiery red light began to come to life, not even being one hundredth the size of his Origin Core.

This could be referred to as the Fire Origin Core!

RUMBLE!

A storm of converging essence rushed into the room that Noah was staying in as the other Celestials in the exquisitely built structure noticed this wild convergence and looked forward while shaking their heads with smiles.

They knew this to be the dwelling of a new Celestial that was also a being with a Noble Fate!

They took this wild convergence of essence as a breakthrough that affected the surroundings of this being, choosing to be respectful and not even send their auras towards the dwelling.

Meanwhile, an enormous amount of essence continued to acc.u.mulate as Noah absorbed both the surrounding essence, as well as the reserves of the planets he was connected to and drove them all according to {Disciple of Fire} into this budding Fire Origin Core.

At the very center of his origin, besides the stably rotating blue core inlaid with a few golden stripes, the formation of an extremely small red core was completed as it was quickly wrapped up in a mantle of flames, a unique energy being released from it and into every single cell on Noah’s body as it began to rotate slowly, absorbing as much essence as it could with no reservation! 𝕗𝐫𝐞𝕖𝕨𝐞𝗯𝚗𝕠𝘃𝐞𝚕.𝐜𝗼𝚖

The unique energy that the Fire Origin Core released the moment it was fully formed went on to wrap around every cell it came across, having an arduous journey in front of it as there were trillions of cells to cover!

The process was slow, but Noah sent his awareness into his own body as he observed this unique energy being released from the Fire Origin Core begin to slowly strengthen the affected cells, each of them gaining a hint of the universal laws, this one being the Law of Fire!

RUMBLE!

It felt like his body was undergoing euphoria as his cells drank this unique energy being released from the Fire Origin Core with wild enthusiasm, Noah sensing his body gain yet another law, this one going on to boost him in a different way than even the Fate Lines that he continued to condense with {Fate’s Disciple}.

{Disciple of Fire} had nothing like fate lines, but it did create another Origin Core inside him that had begun to slowly rotate as it accepted the input of a large amount of essence and then released a unique energy that was passively going on to strengthen Noah’s body, the crazy effects only being able to be discovered with time.

The aura of fire began to slowly burn within him, his awareness closely monitoring this process as he asked himself- would this be similar to the cultivation of the Law of Fate where he could not condense a large number of Fate Lines continuously, or would this be something different?

Essence was continuing to be absorbed by the small Fire Origin Core that began slowly growing larger as seconds passed, showing no signs of stopping as it went on to release even more of its unique energy to bathe Noah’s body. So he wondered as this mystical scene played out inside of him, was there actually a limit?!
