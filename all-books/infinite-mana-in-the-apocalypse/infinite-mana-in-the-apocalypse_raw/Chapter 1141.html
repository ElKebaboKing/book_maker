Chapter 1139 - Comprehending And Assimilating Cosmic Daos Is Easy! II

A Grand Dao that would allow his Vassals to utilize 25% of his power when fully assimilated!

But...there were no Dao Crystals or any methods for others to ascend through the Comprehension and Assimilation of a Dao that Noah created not too many days ago, and even now there weren’t any beings that had surpassed the rank of Baron by having more than 50% Comprehension in Fealty.

Another point was that the increased percentage allowed Noah to influence his Vassals at an even deeper level, his vast fortune continuing to expand resplendently as when he released the Grand Dao Essence of Fealty to cover the Infinite Cosmos...

RUUMBLE!

The regal essence of the Emperor calling for Fealty suffused throughout powerfully, an unknown number of Quintillions of existences seeing the prompt that told them if they wished for power...to pay Fealty to the Tyrannical Emperor, to the Antiquity of Mana!

The Dao Essence was filled with memories and scenes of Noah’s stupendous authority, most existences throughout the Infinite Cosmos answering the call as they read through the shocking memories within the essence as well as the idea of gaining power.

With the masses pledging their Fealty, Marks of Antiquity had coalesced like never before around Noah as on the first day of the birth of the Infinite Cosmos...it’s Core had released such dense golden light from the Marks of Antiquity converging towards Noah that these rays of gold light had even suffused out of the hidden folds of space and into the outside Universes!

This...was just the first day.

The majority of existences pledged Fealty as the rest would follow for the days and weeks to come, where at the end of it all, the number of Marks of Antiquity that Noah had obtained were a truly obscene amount.

The number..had actually spilled over a trillion as over 1000 Billion Marks of Antiquity were surpassed, the number stabilizing over 1580 Billion- or 1.58 Trillion Marks of Antiquity at the end of the day!

The golden rays of light had gone to wash over countless light years as by the end of it, Noah had enough Marks of Antiquity to bring to fruition over 16 Splendiferous Universes...with the only thing left to do being for him to produce them within his Origin.

Thereafter, Noah also elevated the levels of the Lesser Daos of Subterfuge and Conquest to Grand levels, these Daos gaining a significant boost as well.

GRAND [Dao of Conquest] :: A Grand Dao birthed by the Cosmic Core of Noah Osmont... Conquered lands will always allow the user to quadruple their power while fighting within them, while their strength will always be tripled when they are in the process of Conquest... Full Assimilation of Conquest grants the user the capability to Empower not just Absolute Skills, but any Lesser and Grand Daos they Comprehend, along with their power being quintupled in Conquered Lands and quadrupled while in the process of Conquest...

It was a stupendous enhancement of multiple levels as for Noah who had fully assimilated it, he would always have his power quadrupled under the path of Conquest while having it quintupled whenever he was within conquered lands like the Infinite Cosmos!

This wasn’t to mention the fact that he could now even Empower Grand and Lesser Daos from using the Light of Conquest which after conquering the entire Primordial Cosmos...this Light was burning so brightly within Noah’s Origin and the glorious Star of Conquest above him that it now caused his body to always release a regal white aura constantly.

The base effects and boosts this Dao provided to Noah were above and beyond even some Cosmic Daos like Annihilation, making Noah look at the Dao of Conquest as a possible Dao to turn into a Cosmic one!

If he did, he could then gain the capability to Empower even Cosmic Daos as such a thought made his calculative mind imagine a qualitative leap in strength, especially if he could Empower Daos such as Ruination.

But Noah held off on doing this as of now as the 3 spots for the Cosmic Daos was extremely precious, and only after more deliberation and guidance from his own Dao and Fortune would he go ahead and do this.

The elevation of the Dao of Subterfuge to the Grand level on the other hand didn’t provide as much ridiculous boosts as Fealty or Conquest, only enhancing the capabilities of Subterfuge being seen through as Noah could hide in front of or fool an existence a whole realm above him and have no chance to be discovered!

The elevation of all of the first 3 Daos Noah designed only boosted his strength to another level as he now directed his efforts to see what Cosmic Daos he should produce, and it was during this same period of days that he oversaw the Universes of the Infinite Cosmos while taking up his role as...a farmer.

A farmer! As grand as Noah was, his scale wasn’t small as he devised a plan for him to heavily elevate the Comprehension and Assimilation of his Daos, and this could now be done with ease when utilizing the newly unlocked plants and herbs of the Infinite Cosmos such as Ruination Strawberries and Primordial Apples!

For this, Noah spared no efforts as when he acted, he acted in a major way.

With the help of {Cosmic Core Management}, he could expertly select which areas he wanted to turn into farmlands that would be designated as Dao Spaces, these lands having increased concentrations of specific Dao Essences as Noah would plant their matching fruits and herbs here.

For the area that Noah designated as farmlands where he planted obscene amounts of Seeds of Time, Primordial Apples, Ruination Strawberries...it was an entire Universe.

The blank and newly formed Novus Universe...had all of its tens of billions of Galaxies with all of their stellar bodies turned into cultivation fields and Dao Spaces where he planted a shocking number of fruits that when consumed- they granted an increase in comprehensive and assimilation of their respective Daos!

An entire universe!

The extremely slow paced Cosmic Dao of Ruination and even the Primordial Dao that Noah had yet to learn....when he began eating up the treasured Ruination Strawberries and Primordial Apples, what could they do but submit to him and rise up crazily in proficiency?!
