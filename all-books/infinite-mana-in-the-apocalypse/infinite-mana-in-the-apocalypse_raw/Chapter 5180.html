Chapter 5178: Ego IV

Her eyes lit up as she processed the boundless waves of data in an instant, and beside Noah, the illusory figure of Ruination could be seen with a disgruntled expression that suggested she was not a fan of the specific prompts she was currently being required to generate.

Ruination floated around the space while the data settled into Emotive’s mind, and she uttered the prompts with a cadence that carried a hint of annoyance.

|Due to the unique circumstances of the developmental transition, the subject, THE Living Emotive, has achieved an unprecedented synergy with THE Absolute Forge of Infinity.|

|Since the intensity of her emotions and the essence of her heart were utilized as the primary stabilizing anchor during the amplification of the engineered Egos to counteract the Absolute Infinity pouring into them, she has become an intrinsic part of the resulting architecture.|

|As a consequence of this existential overlap, Emotive possesses the capability to take on the forms of THE Absolute Egos of Infinity at fifty percent effectiveness compared to the primary wielder.|

Noah listened to these prompts with a calm and measured composure while he looked at Emotive again, observing her as she stood upright and stared back at him with an expression that was far too bright for its own good.

He could see that she was holding back boundless emotions within her foundations, her very existence vibrating with the pride of being allowed to share in the tools of her Fixation, and he could not help but reflect on the sheer absurdity of the situation.

Truly, she was far too lucky!

The woman had practically stumbled into a tier of power that most beings would have spent eons pursuing and not achieved, and she had done so by simply following her own manic obsessions to their ultimate and sacrificial conclusion!

Noah looked at the fourteen Egos and then back at the woman whose heart served as part of their engine as he shook his head and got ready to work.

He looked over at Emotive and beckoned.

"You, come here."

...!

"Yes Sir!"

She rushed over like a dog let loose as internally, Noah had a lot more intricacies and engineering to uncover.

---

In THE Infiniverse.

The Realm of Dreams stretched outward in an endless expanse of shifting golden sands that glittered beneath the multicolored storms of the Thermal Infinimum, the atmosphere of this domain now thick with a heavy and nutrient-rich humidity that caused massive structures of dreaming glass to sprout from the dunes like translucent towers of frozen light.

Within the heart of this frantic and accelerated development, the Nearu Absolute Clone of Noah stood with a quiet and terrifying composure, his figure radiating a soft multicolored brilliance that served as a stable anchor against the chaotic growth of the surrounding territory.

Beside him, Naldine and Midas could be seen!

Noah looked at them for a long and patient moment before he moved.

"THE Absolute Superbius Ego of Infinity."

...!

With a slow and measured movement of his hands, he reached into the depths of his Absolute Forge of Infinity and called upon the first of his completed iterations, the air around him groaning as the Nearu Absolute Infinity within his organ began to resonate with the manic resonance of the Amaranthine Heart.

HUUM!

The transformation was not a gradual shift but a total and violent overwriting of his current manifestation, for his skin suddenly became more opaque and radiant while shimmering with the luster of polished obsidian and molten gold!

A set of regal robes manifested around his frame, the fabric appearing to be composed of liquid shadow and burning starlight that flowed with its own independent rhythm, and his posture straightened into a configuration of such undeniable majesty that the dreaming glass forests nearby seemed to lean away in a gesture of involuntary submission!

His eyes, though no longer glazed with the clinical void, blazed with a pride so absolute that it seemed to dictate the terms of existence for everything within his reach, and the very cadence of his breath became a rhythmic assertion of his singular supremacy.

He adjusted the flow of his presence, acclimating to the staggering weight of THE Absolute Superbius Ego of Infinity, and every mannerism he displayed began to emanate a regal pride that made the previous Noah appear as a mere shadow of the monarch he had now become.

He looked at Naldine and Midas, and when he spoke, his voice carried the heavy and unhurried resonance of a king whose words were already written into the laws of reality.

"The bindings of the Sisterhood...of Sororis Prima Elzyana are merely that. Suggestions. As of now, I reject these flimsy suggestions."

BOOM! 𝑓𝑟𝑒𝘦𝓌𝑒𝑏𝑛𝑜𝘷𝑒𝘭.𝒸𝘰𝑚

Noah raised his hand toward the two Nascent Hadeans, and he proceeded to utilize THE Sovereign Overwrite to address the pyramidal constructs that still pulsed with a faint blue-gold brilliance upon their skin!

This terrifying capability did not manifest as a simple destructive force, but as a tidal wave of multicolored authority that treated the Gilded bindings as mere errors in a document that required immediate correction!

BOOM!

As his authority made contact with the bindings, the air around Naldine and Midas began to crackle with the sound of breaking glass, the blue-gold light of Elzyana’s wrath struggling to maintain its geometry against the overwhelming weight of Noah’s Pride.

The pyramidal constructs were fundamentally rewritten into the palette of the Absolute Forge, their golden-blue essence being unmade and rewoven into flickers of multicolored Infinity that were immediately absorbed back into the local environment!

The severance of the bindings was total and clinical, the Sororis authority being erased from their foundations as if it had never existed, for the Sovereign Overwrite permitted no disagreement and recognized no higher power than the one who had unleashed it!

Naldine felt the sudden and violent return of her own power, her singularity-dotted eyes widening as the forced dormancy of her Civilization was shattered by the intervention of Noah, and even the greed-personified Midas let out a low and resonant rumble of satisfaction as his golden rings began to orbit with a renewed and terrifying velocity!

The desolation that had hung over them since the afternoon was washed away by the radiation of Noah’s supremacy, leaving them standing within the golden sands as beings who were once again whole and unrestricted.

Noah lowered his hand while the last remnants of the rewritten bindings dissolved into the dreaming glass, his figure still radiating the suffocating pressure of his Superbius state as he looked out across the expanding horizons of his domain before he called out...

"THE Absolute Humilitas Ego of Infinity."

WU!

Noah breathed out and the majestic weight of the Superbius Ego began to dissolve, the molten gold of his robes and the suffocating pressure of his regal pride fading into a simple grey shroud that seemed to absorb the ambient light rather than reflect it, and his posture softened from that of a king into that of a being who had reached a state of total and absolute release!

His eyes, which had previously blazed with the fires of an unyielding sovereignty, now became deep and quiet pools of blue that held the stillness of an eternal void, and every mannerism he displayed transitioned into a glorious and terrifying lack of self-assertion that made him appear more like a natural feature of the dreamscape than a living entity.

HUUM!

The transformation into THE Absolute Humilitas Ego of Infinity was so profound that Naldine Manthon blinked while her singularity-dotted eyes struggled to maintain their focus on the space where Noah stood, for while his physical form had not moved, the very concept of his presence had begun to unravel within her perception until she was looking at nothing but the shifting golden sands of the Realm of Dreams!

Beside her, the engineered greed of Midas rumbled in confusion as his rings of Greed slowed their rotation, his instinct to track and covet power finding no purchase on a being who had successfully discarded every layer of observable ego, and for a long moment, the two Nascent Hadeans stood alone as if Noah had never been there at all!

Noah allowed himself to remain in this state of perfect invisibility for a heartbeat longer before he intentionally projected a flicker of his authority to show himself once more, appearing as a soft and nondescript shadow against the glittering dunes.

"Another me will come soon as we prepare for some grand things," he said, his voice now a quiet and resonant murmur that carried no trace of his former arrogance, "so for now, join the others in The Early Veiled Shore and wait."

Naldine and Midas gave a synchronized nod of understanding as the Humilitas manifestation of Noah dissipated into the golden air without leaving a single trace of his passage!

---

He quickly appeared and moved across the vast expanse of the Wastes with a terrifying speed, his figure a mere ghost weaving through the glimmering multicolored rain and the torrential storms of the Pluvial Epoch that continued to drench the transformed landscape in the essence of his developmental phenomenon.

He eventually reached the outskirts of The Wandering Territories, a region filled with inverted landmasses and floating valleys of multicolored brilliance where the laws of spatial orientation appeared to have been discarded.

It was a place of impossible geometries and shifting horizons, and it was here, within a valley where the light flowed upward like a reverse waterfall, that he settled into a patient wait for the Sororis from THE Magna Sorora who would serve as the bridge to his next grand objective.

THE Genesis Monarch sat upon a crystalline ledge while looking out toward, his presence so thoroughly erased by his Ego that even the ambient Infinity of the region seemed to flow through him without resistance, and he waited for the hour of his crossing to begin!
