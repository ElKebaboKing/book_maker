Chapter 781 - Aptitude, Quality, Battle!

Next to all the treasures listed on the illusory screen, a set of points could be seen as these were required if one wanted to obtain any treasures within the Great Sage Aoin Tower!

As for the points...the way to get them was given to every single being that entered the Tower.

At the very center of the expansive first floor of this tower, a contestant had to step in as they would go through a series of tests. These tests and trials are what ultimately granted the points that a being could use to claim the stellar rewards listed above!

To go in order, the one undertaking the trial of the Legacy would be tested based on their Aptitude, the quality of their pets, as well as their Battle Power.

Everything fell under the Dao of Summoning, where even though the Great Sage Aoin also mastered the Dao of Voidspace, the former Dao was the one that he had handed down to his people!

"Haha, truly our Forefather’s Legacy!"

The experts that were residents of the Expansive Dragon Cl.u.s.ter were ecstatic, especially the Dragonoid beings that were looking at the treasures with shining eyes.

The treasures deserved such looks as Noah himself could not take his eyes off the things numbered 1-20.

These spots all had a name and picture of a Peak Tier GALAXY Realm Animus Summons, with those in the top 10 all being at the PRISMATIC Rarity and the remaining ones all being Ultra Rare!

It was a truly shocking scene as from this list, Noah could see names such as Abyssal Black Tyrant Dragon, Monkey King, Galaxy Devouring Wolf, Blue Slime, Tiamat...and other shocking names that made him want to tear down this entire place and get all of these pets for himself!

"Princess, please allow me to be a test subject for the trials of this Legacy!"

Even the GALAXY Realm experts by the side could not help themselves as one spoke up with a devout expression to Anna, this Holy Princess merely looking towards Noah before she nodded as this Holy Descendant rushed towards the center of the expansive floor they were in.

THRUUM!

The moment he delved deeper in, runic circles lit up under his feet as essence raged, a strong voice reverberating out from the tower as an entrant took the legacy for the first time!

[Legacy Disciple #17(Axin) currently undergoing the Trial.]

The voice was monotone and calm, filled with depth as all who heard it could only think of an aged wise being speaking with a full white beard speaking.

[First Trial- The Test of Aptitude...]

The takers of the trial would first be tested with their aptitude as their comprehension of the Dao of Summoning were evaluated based on their age and time they spent studying it!

The gaze of the Holy Descendant who was termed Legacy Disciple #17 was ecstatic as he waited to hear of his aptitude. After all, he was a genius from the young generation of the Expansive Dragon Cl.u.s.ter.

[Legacy Disciple #17(Axin)- Average Aptitude!]

BOOM!

Like a strike of thunder, the words smashed into the Holy Descendant as he turned ashen, the blank voice not paying attention to him as it continued.

[Second Trial- The Test of Quality. Summon all of your Animus Pets!]

The Holy Descendant very quickly used his essence as four Animus Summons appeared, 2 having the lineage of dragons as the others were a Flaming Golden Lion and a Verdant Turtle.

Even with the blow of the Test of Aptitude, the Holy Descendant was at least proud of his pets of which he had two at the rarity of Uncommon, and the other two being Rare!

[Legacy Disciple #17(Axin)-The Test of Quality, Below Average Quality!]

BOOM!

The merciless voice rang out again as the Holy Descendant was nearly pushed to depression, Noah looking at this scene while chuckling inside as in the next second, the figure of the Holy descendant and all of his pets disappeared.

[Third Trial- The Test of Combat!]

The only thing the others heard was the resonant voice ringing out again as with the disappearance of the Holy Descendant, the upper area of the floor they were in had another illusory screen light up, this one showing the image of the teleported being on the 2nd floor of the Tower as directly opposite him, a fearsome Animus Summon at the Low Tier GALAXY Realm stood!

[Begin!]

RUMBLE!

Without a pause, the last trial commenced for this contestant as his pets had to face a Low Tier GALAXY Realm Summon of the Rare rarity, passing this level without too many issues.

On the next floor, he faced 2 Animus Summons of the same Realm as he struggled and barely passed, and on the third floor...his pets were incapacitated as he was stopped by 4 Rare Animus Summons of the Low Tier GALAXY Realm!

[Legacy Disciple #17(Axin) Test of Combat, Below Average!]

THRUM!

Essence waved as the Holy Descendant was brought back onto the base floor, his gaze ashen as he looked towards the others with his gaze downcast. A hint of shame could be seen on his face as he turned his gaze up to the ceiling of the floor they were in, yet another illusory screen appearing as it listed the Trial takers of this tower!

[Legacy Disciple #17(Axin)- 50 Points...]

A measly number of points was listed for this being as 50 was barely enough to get a Low Tier GALAXY Realm Animus Summon from the rewards ranked 500-2000, with 50 points only being able to get something in the upper hundreds!

A Legacy Disciple needed a few hundred points to even be able to claim a reward of a Middle Tier GALAXY summon, with 1000 points required to get one of the Rare or Ultra Rare rank.

Meanwhile, one had to have a few thousand points to even touch the pristine rewards of Peak Tier GALAXY Realm Animus Summons, with each of the PRISMATIC Summons in the top 10 being 10,000 points each!

THRUUUM

Noah looked at this scene with shining eyes as after the Holy Descendant came back, none of the others seemed to want to go forward as they lost their confidence.

At this scene while they were looking at each other hesitantly, Noah stepped forward without worry as he wanted to see just where he would stand as a Legacy Disciple of a Great Sage!
