Chapter 4612: A Period of Quintessential Progress! I

THE Ritual Festival of Existence in The Primordial Genesis Shore unfolded with tremendous glory, and Noah was a full participant in it all.

He observed with fascinated attention all the unique ways that these lifeforms touched by The First Tongue acted and expressed themselves.

How they utilized the individual letters of The First Tongue to manifest and produce sounds and melodies completely unique to their particular Weavings of Existence.

Dances and rituals unfolded next to a swirling storm of Phonemes combining Fire, Light, and Darkness into a constantly shifting vortex of linguistic celebration.

He experienced it all for more than an hour of external time.

And was actually profoundly appreciative of this seemingly brief hour.

Because it allowed him to get a direct glimpse of just how terrifying the utility of THE Infinite Desmoterion would truly be going forward.

This single passing hour in external reality equated to fifteen thousand hours cultivating inside THE Infinite Desmoterion.

More than six hundred days of accelerated advancement.

Prompts bloomed before his vision detailing the extraordinary collective progress.

|THE Infinite Desmoterion - Temporal Progression Report|

|External Time Elapsed: 1 hour, 14 minutes|

|Internal Time Elapsed: 18,525 hours (772 days)|

|Prisoners Currently Cultivating: 2/27|

|Maintenance Cost Paid: 20 Rudimentary Units of Everything|

|Mastery Advancement - The First Tongue|

|Your proficiency in The First Tongue has experienced dramatic growth through intensive practice|

|Current Classification: Fluent Speaker (Advanced)|

|You can now freely form Logos of 8+ Phonemes with minimal concentration required|

|Complex grammatical structures containing multiple Phonemes can be constructed with careful effort|

|Inscription speed increased by 340%|

|Error rate when forming Logos under pressure: Reduced to near-zero|

|Phoneme Comprehension Deepened|

|You have extrapolated and internalized the different variations of each Core Phoneme|

|Understanding achieved: A single Phoneme can carry dozens of subtle meanings depending on context, inflection, and combination|

|Example: The Phoneme of Fire can mean destruction, transformation, purification, passion, or creation depending on how it is expressed|

|This multilayered comprehension allows for more sophisticated Logos construction|

|Foundation for Perfect Speaker classification being systematically built|

|Prisoner Cultivation Report - Ozymandias|

|Days Cultivating: 772|

|Primary Focus: Perfecting The First Tongue and refining The Way of Mana and Hunger|

|Progress: Ozymandias has achieved near-perfect fluency in basic Logos construction|

|Advancement: His insights into The Way of Mana and Hunger have deepened your own understanding proportionally|

|Prisoner Cultivation Report - Raijin Katsuro|

|Days Cultivating: 772|

|Initial Status: Extremely resistant and defiant|

|Current Status: Disciplined and fully focused on The Way of Heavenly Lightning|

|Transformation: After 347 days of constant containment and Mana influence, Raijin ceased resistance and began genuine cultivation|

|Progress: The Way of Heavenly Lightning is being systematically influenced and enhanced by The Way of Mana|

|Notable Incident: On day 189, Raijin attempted rebellion and was subjected to thrashing and additional Logos of Imprisonment by Ozymandias. Resistance ceased entirely afterward.|

|Raijin now meditates precisely 18 hours per day and has developed what Ozymandias describes as "an unhealthy obsession with perfecting his lightning manifestation to prove his Way wasn’t fundamentally weak."|

|Current Relationship: Professional prisoner with excellent work ethic|

|The Way of Heavenly Lightning - Integration Complete|

|You can now express The Way of Heavenly Lightning through your own Existence|

|Authority Level: 67% of Raijin’s original mastery|

|This percentage will continue increasing as Raijin refines his Way further within The Desmoterion|

|Principle Analysis - Records Studied|

|The complete records of Raijin Katsuro’s Singularity Principles have been thoroughly analyzed|

|The Genesis Principle contained within his foundation has been examined|

|Understanding Achieved: The structural difference between Singularity and Genesis Principles and The First Tongue has been mapped|

|Projection: After approximately 2,000 additional days of study within The Desmoterion, alteration of Complex Logos representing Principles will become possible|

|Application: This will allow the systematic elevation of your current Singularity Principles toward Genesis classification|

|Timeline: Achievable within 5-6 hours of external reality at current temporal dilation|

|Exponential Advancement - Way of Mana and Hunger|

|The combined cultivation of yourself, Ozymandias, and Raijin within The Desmoterion has created synergistic effects|

|Your Way has expanded across multiple dimensional expressions|

|Stability increased by 410%|

|Authority density increased by 890%|

|Foundation depth reinforced beyond previous projections|

|THE Organ of Civilization - Development Surge|

|Current Development: 31.00%|

|Maximum Prisoner Capacity: 27 to 36 entities|

|Temporal Dilation: 15,000x to 18,000x faster than external reality|

|Note: Each additional prisoner will continue accelerating overall development|

|THE Foundation of The First Tongue - Critical Advancement|

|THE Civilizational Authority Resistance: Increased to 50%|

|Effect: When facing THE Surface Depth of Absolute Sovereignty entities, your Foundation will innately filter out and resist approximately half of their authority pressure|

|This resistance applies passively and does not require conscious effort to maintain|

Oh!

The advancements made were absolutely glorious.

With such exponential progress, Noah truly felt like the seemingly far-away stage of achieving THE classification himself could be reached much sooner than he originally anticipated.

At this moment, he reached up into the swirling cluster of treasures overhead and plucked a jelly-like quartz that pulsed with internal radiance.

He took a bite, and his eyes immediately flashed with brilliant obsidian light.

|Treasure Consumed|

|Name: The Void-Touched Essence Quartz|

|Classification: Naturally Formed Civilizational Treasure|

|Composition: Crystallized Void authority mixed with pure THE Civilizational Authority|

|Effect: Permanently enhances capacity to manipulate void and empty space|

|Secondary Effect: Increases resistance to spatial collapse and dimensional distortion by 15%|

|Tertiary Effect: Grants instinctive understanding of how to hide one’s presence within spatial folds|

|Origin: THE Vaults of Ginnungagap|

|Note: Synergizes exceptionally well with The First Tongue’s Phoneme of Void|

As Noah absorbed the treasure’s benefits, Ul’moreth floated beside him with visible joy radiating from his hairy form.

The ancient being spoke with earnest sincerity.

|Ginnu Lifeforms rarely celebrate like this, you know. We tend to be solitary and focused exclusively on our individual advancement. But I wish for all of them to collectively succeed and find genuine happiness in their existence. It saddens me that across all of Existence, the strongest entities seem to have completely forgotten that happiness is even possible. Most seem intent only on covering all of reality under their particular Way and controlling absolutely everything they can reach.|

He shook his head with what might have been melancholy before turning to Noah with renewed energy.

|Alright, I’ve made you enjoy the festival long enough! You are partly still like The Others in nature. Like THE Creature, THE Primordial Chaos, or THE Secretive Eon and similar entities. I know your fundamental nature is not to sit still for extended periods. So go ahead. While the festival continues to unfold, we can also enjoy dancing and singing and eating while observing another battle for entertainment. I’ve already placed the one you had your eye on in the arena.|

Noah blinked and looked toward the distant arena.

Surely enough, THE Maelstrom Thundermane could be seen standing in the expanded space, looking around with a somber and heavy expression as if contemplating what was about to occur.

Noah smiled as he looked back toward Ul’moreth and nodded with genuine appreciation before beginning to walk steadily toward the arena.

As he moved, he replied to Ul’moreth’s words.

|Sometimes, one needs to have a precise balance of both approaches. To maintain that insane drive and ambition to place all of Existence within one’s palm and control, while also finding one’s own belonging and genuine happiness within that same Existence. Because sometimes, if you dont have Existence in your palm...you happiness can easily be taken away...|

At these words, Ul’moreth’s hairy figure radiated satisfaction as he responded with amused warmth.

|You don’t always have to have the last word in every exchange, my student. Go on now, let us see how you deal with someone who has a slightly stronger Depth of Absolute Sovereignty than your previous opponent. Even though their Ways are similar in expression, this should prove a little more difficult. Because some THE entities will not simply sit there politely and wait for you to speak The First Tongue at them. And remember, all THE classification entities are inherently faster than you currently are!|

Noah nodded at such accurate warnings as he opened his palm.

A vibrant blue-gold stick materialized with familiar weight.

The Goad, ready once more to deliver lessons.

He jumped directly toward the arena, and Gigaparsecs of space expanded dramatically all around him as the battlefield prepared for another impossible confrontation.

---

Far from The Primordial Genesis Shore, outside of Ginnungagap entirely, deep within The Infinite Unfurling where reality operated according to different fundamental rules, a titanic humanoid entity moved with terrifying purpose.

Multicolored flames of raw Existence itself surrounded this being completely, making their actual form completely unobservable beneath the blazing corona.

One could only see pressurized steam blowing continuously out of what appeared to be a nose, creating visible distortions in surrounding space.

Billowing waves of crushing heat poured out of what seemed to be a mouth with each breath.

The steam emanating from this entity’s existence alone melted and healed the surrounding fabric of reality in a continuous cycle.

If a THE Intermediate Depth of Absolute Sovereignty entity faced even the passive steam radiating from this being, they would be systematically melted alongside their entire Way and Civilization!

Dissolved completely by ambient heat alone.

The entity moved forward with measured steps that covered impossible distances.

Behind them, multicolored chains swung with rhythmic motion.

When one followed those chains backward, a horrifying sight became visible.

Seven completely bound THE classification entities were being dragged along like captured prisoners.

Each one held eyes filled with sheer existential terror as they barely dared to even look at the entity who pulled them along so effortlessly.

As for the entity themselves, their barely discernible eyes behind the flames of Existence were currently focused ahead on something specific.

Illusory screens floated in the space before them, displaying past scenes with perfect clarity.

Scenes from inside a Primordial Genesis Arena.

A battle showing a Pre-THE Existence wielding a simple stick.

Causing a THE Surface Depth of Absolute Sovereignty entity to kneel through linguistic authority alone.

This titanic entity watched the recorded scene with complete calm, their expression unreadable behind the consuming flames.

After several moments of observation, they waved one hand casually and caused the illusory screens to disappear entirely.

And then they continued moving forward.

Calmly.

Gloriously.

Unfathomably!

Pulling seven terrified THE entities behind them like trophies.
