Chapter 4614: A Period of Quintessential Progress! III

The moment this occurred, the dance reached its conclusion.

Noah crashed down alongside Astrape like a falling star, his Goad positioned perfectly to drive into the massive chest of the defeated THE entity.

BOOM!

The impact shook the entire arena as Noah landed standing directly on the prone body of a THE Surface Depth of Absolute Sovereignty entity.

Astrape looked up at him with eyes filled with pure incredulity and absolute shock.

This could not be happening. This should not be possible!

And yet...

Noah simply raised his Goad one final time and swung it down to strike Astrape’s head with measured force.

The instant the blow connected, Astrape’s entire form disappeared.

He was deemed defeated.

And now he was drawn directly into THE Infinite Desmoterion as its third official prisoner.

Only now did the dance truly end.

Outside the arena, the Ginnu Lifeforms dancing around the ritual flames howled out in celebration, their voices creating linguistic harmonics that made reality vibrate.

The Phonemes of Fire surrounding them exploded upward and formed into towering pillars of glorious multi-colored flames that reached toward the transformed sky.

Noah stood in the center of the arena with eyes shining brightly as he breathed out slowly, processing what had just occurred.

And glorious prompts began descending before his vision.

|Unquantifiable Harvest Initiated|

|You have suppressed and imprisoned a second entity of fundamentally higher Scale of Existence|

|Harvest being collected and amplified exponentially by Perpetual Harvest and The Cheating Architect|

|Resource generation continues exceeding conventional calculation parameters|

|All gains multiplied by factors beyond standard measurement|

|Sequential THE entity defeats are creating compound amplification effects|

|THE Scale Breaker - Distinction Evolution|

|Your Distinction has grown stronger through additional THE entity suppression|

|Effect Enhancement: Gap reduction when facing higher Scale entities increased from base level to amplified tier|

|THE Infinite Desmoterion - Prisoner Acquisition|

|Second THE classification prisoner successfully imprisoned|

|Active Prisoners: 3/36|

|Prisoner 1: Ozymandias (willing cooperation, perfect synchronization)|

|Prisoner 2: Raijin Katsuro, THE Monk of Ten Thousand Storms (disciplined cultivation)|

|Prisoner 3: Astrape, THE Maelstrom Thundermane (newly captured, status: resistant)|

|Maintenance Cost: 30 Rudimentary Units of Everything per 24 hours|

|Two THE entities now cultivating within The Desmoterion under 18,000x temporal dilation|

|Ozymandias continues perfecting The First Tongue and The Way of Mana and Hunger|

|Raijin Katsuro refines The Way of Heavenly Lightning under Mana influence|

|Astrape will be compelled to cultivate The Way of Fulmination under containment|

|The Way of Fulmination - Integration Initiated|

|As Astrape cultivates and refines his Way within The Desmoterion, aspects of The Way of Fulmination will gradually become accessible to you|

|His Genesis Principle of Absolute Conduction will be studied and potentially replicated|

|Estimated time for meaningful integration: Variable based on cooperation level|

|Timeline for elevating your own Singularity Principles to Genesis tier: Somewhat accelerated|

|THE Organ of Civilization - Development Surge|

|Current Development: 31.00% to 38.00%|

|Major advancement triggered by second THE prisoner acquisition|

|Maximum Prisoner Capacity: 36 to 45 entities|

|Temporal Dilation: 18,000x to 21,000x faster than external reality|

|THE Foundation of The First Tongue - Ongoing Growth|

|THE Civilizational Authority Resistance: 50% to 62%|

...!

Oh!

The prompts were numerous as he needed to condense them somewhat.

But...

Noah absorbed all of these notifications with deep satisfaction.

Two THE Surface Depth prisoners now cultivating at twenty-one thousand times temporal dilation.

His progression was accelerating at rates that defied reasonable expectation!

And he was only just beginning to fill THE Infinite Desmoterion with captured entities who would fuel his advancement.

The dance continued.

Always forward.

He looked up and said with a smile.

|Teacher, this student wants more enemies...so send more THEs down!|

...!

Teacher.

Never did he think a day would come where he would say this, but Ul’moreth had given him far too much as saying this distinction at least should be nothing much. After all, he didn’t think of himself to be so arrogant that he could not learn from another!

So he had called out above.

And truly, moments later...

He saw a distant cage be opened, an obsidian gold light pull something from inside it and chuck it down to the Arena!

A THE entity who looked around with shock and confusion landed in the Arena right after.

And Noah raised his stick!

But...

Every THE entity held their own unique Way.

The words of Ul’moreth remained absolutely true that regardless of how much control Noah gained over The First Tongue, THE entities were inherently faster than him.

So he had to prepare multiple things in advance when he came to face them.

Astrape had talked in the beginning and given him critical time to set things in motion.

The next monster that he faced was an extremely cautious one.

The moment this entity landed and found itself in the setting of the expanded arena with an enemy it sensed was merely Pre-THE classification, it moved with ridiculously terrifying speed.

Speed that exceeded light by margins that made conventional measurements meaningless.

Well, even light couldn’t be used as an accurate comparison for what THE entities were capable of.

What they moved past could at most be accurately described by Planck Speed.

Planck Speed represented the theoretical maximum velocity at which any causality or information could travel through Existence.

It was the speed limit of reality itself, beyond which the very concept of "movement" ceased to have coherent meaning.

To move at Planck Speed meant crossing a Planck Length in a single Planck Time unit. In practical terms, it meant traversing the smallest possible meaningful distance in the smallest possible meaningful moment.

And even those words were hard to grasp as one might scratch their head!

For entities operating at this velocity, conventional space became irrelevant. Distance was no longer an obstacle but merely a suggestion that could be ignored.

In the context of their current reality where Existence itself was woven from linguistic authority and Civilizational power, Planck Speed represented movement so fast that it occurred between the individual Phonemes of a spoken Logos.

You could begin forming a word, and a THE entity moving at such velocity would cross the entire arena, strike you, and return to their starting position before the second Phoneme left your mouth.

THE entities seemingly moved even faster than this theoretical maximum.

The moment this particular entity arrived in the arena was the exact same moment it arrived directly in front of Noah with one hand already wrapped around his throat.

It was fucking terrifying!
