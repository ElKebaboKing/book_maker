Infinite Mana In The Apocalypse

Chapter 2504 A Setting Battlefield!

Silence.

"..."

The gazes of the Apex Aeonic Lernaeans were ashen as they looked at the scene ahead of them!

Xiaomi's eyes sparkled while beside her, her Father cursed inwardly at such a scene.

Because he very clearly remembered a promise he had made with the Man who had just forged an Apex Aeonic Lifeform that could dominate an established Apex Aeonic Lifeform- much less him who had just advanced!

Shock and incredulity spread all around as Noah's voice resounded out.

"This was a presentation to show some of you that Apex Aeonic Lifeforms even stronger than you can be produced at the wave of my hand."

Silence reigned as all eyes focused on him.

"And I can go much further to even reforge the Origin and Realms of Supreme Eternal Lifeforms."

...!

Words that caused even Aurelia's and Orion's eyes to open wide in shock rang out.

And his gaze was moreso focused on them as he didn't seem to care much for the others!

"Did you advance without an Extremity Foundation? Do you actually wish to have a genuine chance of attaining Extremity? To have an Apex Foundation of Extreme Power that makes a possibility for you to form an Absolute Dynamis of Extremity? It's all possible for a small price!"

WAA!

A small price.

A price of their souls.

A price of fealty!

Brilliance shone as the situation developed into a fantastical path in the Infinite Hyperversal Haven. 𝕗𝚛𝚎𝚎𝐰𝗲𝗯𝗻𝚘𝚟𝚎𝗹.𝕔𝐨𝕞

-

Meanwhile, in the area filled with tendrils of Ruin and Nullity where Noah had almost come to a full clash with the Noblesse Lineage.

This same location was determined to be where the Apex War would take place as it would be the Apex Battlefield!

Currently, there was an endless transparent veil that pierced up towards the skies and down towards a razed Crimson and silver earth as if it divided two sides. And it truly did so as above the skies of one side, an illusory blue runic character that read [LERNAEAN] was etched gorgeously as on the other side, an illusory black character that read [NOBLESSE] was carved wondrously.

And on the side shining with the blue light, one could see a cluster of 5 Primeval Shadow Ancients shrouded in darkness as their gazes were cold and somber.

This was because on the other side of the transparent barrier, they could see the figures of Apex Aeonic Lifeforms whom they recognized from their insignias and essence to be from the Genesis Institute of Extremity and Singularity Overseers!

They currently numbered 9 all together as their eyes were just as cold as theirs.

The parties that represented three Aeonic Inheritances of Extremity were all in a single location as before an Apex War began, any faction that wished to join in support of any side had to make it clear and have their forces stationed on the chosen side of the Apex Battlefield.

The 5 Primeval Shadow Ancients floated on the side of Lerneans as they had received much more information about the being that Grand Primeval Silas had made an unknown deal with- this buzz even reaching their Cult of Endless Shadows as their close allies had reached out to them to discern their relationship with the Scion that had forged an Ultimate Power of Extremity and massacred hundreds of other Scions in the ongoing Initiation Step of Extremity Sanctification of Dissolution!

And in the last few minutes, they had received a command through their shared communication channel as it didn't even come from Grand Primeval Silas- it stemmed from a Hyperversal Extremity of their Cult of Endless Shadows.

The command was singular in nature.

[Stand by the side of the Lernaeans and wait for the time when the Adjudicator moves to save his life. After the dust has settled, we will have a Scion who attained an Absolute Seed of Extremity before even undergoing Apexification in our Cult. This will further cement our rise as an Aeonic Inheritance of Extremity!]

...!

A command that revealed many beings had the mindset that the one who called himself the True Emperor of Quintessence- a being who had forged an Ultimate Power of Extremity even before Apexification...most beings believed he wouldn't perish in this Apex War even if he wanted to!

He was a critical tool that if he really died under the watch of the current Adjudicator, it would bring their capabilities to question!

Existences who had become Supreme Eternal Lifeforms were the ones who delved and combined multiple Boundaries to forge Dynamis of Extremity.

Only the most powerful ones who had achieved the 16th and 17th Boundary Layers were lucky and powerful enough to forge Absolute Seeds of Extremity- and this number was low even amongst such beings!

It required someone truly special.

It was commonly Mutated Entities or existences with unheard of Extremity Foundations that rose across the Extremity

Sanctifications of the other 6 Havens.

And any such existences were protected in powerful Inheritances or part of the Seven Clans as they were guaranteed future Hyperversal Extremities!

Now, you have a being who managed to condense his Boundaries that hadn't even reached the 10th Layer, and converge nine of them or even more to produce an Absolute Seed of Extremity?

As far the the Primeval Shadow Ancients knew, such a thing wasn't Recorded in any history that could be verified!

Maybe rumors.

Maybe Whispers across the eons.

But never verified as it was for the being who had one of his bodies accompanied by Grand Primeval Silas as they went towards their Cult of Endless Shadows even now!

"He seems difficult to handle though...will everything unfold smoothly as expected?"

One of the Primeval Shadow Ancients remembered how the True Emperor of Quintessence had proclaimed himself when he came back with Silas and took the reins of the Lernaean Lineage.

Beside him, a woman shrouded in darkness replied with a sharp gaze.

"It doesn't matter. We are the only Inheritance he is thus far connected to, with our Shadowlings reporting that he even denied an Invitation from the Sima Clan! Now...we're no Sima Clan, so the prestige of having a Scion that even such a pristine behemoth would accept...Mmm, we shall see how it all unfolds."

Expectations were high as an Apex Battlefield was beginning to be filled with its combatants, unknown forces with their own thoughts moving behind the scenes as each person sought for their own ideal future!
