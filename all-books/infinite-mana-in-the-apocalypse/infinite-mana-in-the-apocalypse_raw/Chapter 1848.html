1846 My Path l

A foundation that to other beings, millions of years worth of work would be required in order to forge it.

1 Trillion Royal Cosmos within a vibrant Origin as it was even after a Limit was exceeded!

Even the most astounding geniuses would waver before even thinking about taking on this step, but Noah had taken it with ease.

This was due to his own strong will and mentality…as well as the fact that he hadn't spent millions of years getting where he was today as he didn't form a great level of attachment. Another factor was the wondrous Nomological Edict of the Dictum Emperor reaching TABOO as it allowed Noah to more easily see his path forward, where even with the great level of danger involved…he saw a ridiculously golden possibility that exceeded anything he had come across so far!

So he sent down a command that his Origin obeyed, and 1 Trillion Royal Cosmos buzzed as they began to grow fissure until…

CRACK…BOOOOM!

Every single hard-forged Royal Cosmos burst and exploded into countless shimmering fragments as Noah's figure blew up like a balloon, his stellar visage fading as the only thing left behind were glimmering shards of Royal Cosmos!

The force was so disastrous and shocking that even his Body was nearly ripped apart, but the many Physiques he had forged tightened and held everything together akin to unbreakable ropes as everything was kept in place!

"...!"

Noah's eyes trembled in a ferocious expression as even he couldn't keep his calm, his very Aspects of Existence being racked with unbearable pain as even someone like him who had gone through millions of years of dreams hadn't come across anything like it.

But even while feeling this tremendous pain…Noah gritted his vibrating teeth as his eyes released beams of luminous light.

It was good that he felt pain!

It meant that even as he shattered one of the very foundations of his life, he was still alive.

But…it didn't seem like it would be this way for much longer.

HUUUM!

After nearly exploding but being contained, his body turned dim as one could see countless shards of shattered Royal Cosmos floating listlessly.

Dr. Hall stared at this scene as the Eye of Nature focused with intensity, his heart trembling as he saw the light within the body of the Emperor dimming!

The destruction of one's origin was equivalent to death- this remained to be true!

But just before he faced true death and utter destruction, Noah had to use his Aspects of Soul and Body that were listlessly seeking for a direction to keep the mere wisps of his Origin alive for him to cause something reality breaking to come to fruition.

For this, he needed a small amount of time…as well as an overabundance of Destiny, Fate, Karma, and Fortune.

The radiant body of the Unnamed Kainos Emperor that was always bright with the luster of stars was turning entirely dim at this moment when his Primogenial Soul mustered its will and called out.

"Flourishing Fate, Flourishing Fortune, Flourishing Karma."

WAA!

The Essence of Reality that was contained in the shattered Origin moved listlessly, the surging rivers of essence coming down from the Infinite Reality Passages inducing them to activity while providing even more essence- this being one of the few sources of light that still illuminated the visage of the Tyrannical Emperor as it allowed shocking abilities to be cast!

"A Touch of Fate, A Touch of Fortune, A Touch of Karma!"

…!

Utter glorious radiance flashed out as while targeting the particular fate of succeeding in forming a new Origin, Noah utilized the capabilities of the Nomological edicts of the Opulent Emperor, Pathfinder, and the Causal Nexus Emperor.

A radiance of white, green, and gold permeated out with vibrant wonder as the shattered remains of his Royal Cosmos began to buzz just slightly.

Slightly.

It wasn't enough!

To defy set laws of reality itself, just this was not enough!

So the Tyrannical Emperor recalled the words of his mother as he called out.

He wasn't alone.

"My people…"

RUUUMBLE!

The area Noah was in trembled as Dr. Hall felt his very Soul tugged upon as a brilliant green line erupted from him and connected to Noah. 𝐟𝐫𝕖𝗲𝘄𝚎𝗯𝕟𝐨𝕧𝐞𝚕.𝕔𝕠𝐦

He wasn't the only one as across the vast Gourmandizing Infinite Reality, countless existences felt their souls pulled upon as vibrant green strings of karma erupted out- causing countless stellar green lines to shoot out across this vast domain and have it all surge towards Noah!

Karma.

Cause and effect!

It was an abstruse concept that was hard to grasp and much less utilize, and Noah called upon it as even if he couldn't consider himself a complex user of it, he could still call upon the boundless karma his Vassals had incurred with him to push forward the success of what he wanted to do.

Boundless light blazed down on his body that was continuing to dim, the vibrating debris of Royal Cosmos oscillating ever faster at this moment as wondrously, their constant impact with each other caused them to be ground down to glittering stellar dust!

Dust that at this moment began to slowly rotate and converge towards a single point- a center that would be the birth of Noah's new Origin.

But…it was unbearably slow.

The slower it was, the dimmer the fine stellar dust of his past Royal Cosmos became as if it continued at this pace, everything would perish before it could even come together to birth something new from the destruction!

And this was when Noah made one more move.

Something that had granted him a level of confidence when the idea of the destruction of his Origin came to light- it was the wonders of the TABOO Nomological Edict of the Dictum Emperor.

"Author's Viewpoint."

…!

Noah's consciousness buzzed as everything seemingly came to a halt.

His vision changed.

His viewpoint became entirely something else as when his 'eyes' opened, he could see his body and its fading life force even as the stellar dust of his old Cosmos were converging towards one central point!

The authority of TABOO had propelled the authority of Author's Viewpoint to a ridiculous level as depending on what Noah did, the cost could go up to tens of millions of Units of the Essence of Reality.

But right now, he just needed one last piece after utilizing Fate, Karma, and Fortune.

Destiny would seal the deal as before Noah's eyes, a golden scroll unfurled along with an incandescent pen!

As his Tyrannical will watched on, the pen began to move- his voice coming out at the same time as it pulsed with shocking authority.

"I shall write and dictate from here on out…"

…!!!
