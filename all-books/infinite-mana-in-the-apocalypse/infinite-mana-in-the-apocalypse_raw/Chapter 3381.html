Chapter 3379 A Dream of a Different Tune! III

Archimedes!

An Ordinance Amalgamation powerhouses- an Outerversal Morphon that was known as the Great Titan.

This was his identity as among the Ordinance Amalgamation Entities, he was a renowned one.

Finding companionship in a human Ordinance Amalgamation Entity aligned with Matter had caused many to talk behind his back about his preferences, but none dared to say anything to his face.

Under the banner of the Great Instrument of the Beyonder, Kurai, both the forces of the Megalos Beyond and Megalos Ynnere now fell and moved under his command as Outerversal Morphons and Lineages of humans and beasts intermingled!

Gradually over the last thousand years.

And now, a thousand years after the conquest of Megalos Ynnere, the Great Instrument of the Beyonder moved for Megalos Nihilos.

All forces would answer his call and participate, or have their weavings marked for decimation by the Great Instrument of the Beyonder and his Champions.

This, Archimedes and his woman, Lacana, moved towards a Sector that held a portion of the forces of the Great Instrument of the Beyonder- led by an Existence Severing Champion!

"It's certainly not quiet…" Lacana raised her brows as they arrived near a bustling Pantheon.

On it, terrifying auras converged as with a single glance, Archimedes could sense a bit over 100 Existence Severing Entities here, a few thousand at the Ordinance Amalgamation stage, tens of thousands of Ordinance of Existence entities, and hundreds of thousands of others below them who had attained the state of Eye of Existence or One With Existence!

It was a terrifying cluster of forces, but it was only a portion of those under the banner of the Megalos Beyond and Megalos Ynnere.

Archimedes could see that at the center of the Pantheon, a massive obsidian Tiger Whale floated with a horrific aura of power, obsidian tentacles leaking from its body as this was the Existence Severing Champion leading forces from this Sector- The Great Demolisher!

Near him, the calm visage of a multicolored Antediluvian Leviathan could be seen as existence itself seemed to be parting ways from him.

Other Existence Severing entities converged around these beings as they waited for the command of the Great Instrument of the Beyonder.

Uniquely, as Archimedes continued to look around these beings, he found a very scant amount of Simians of the Commonwealth!

Not just scant, but none of them really.

He frowned at this as he and Lacana arrived near a cluster of Ordinance Amalgamation entities within a Bazaar, his voice unconsciously leaking out.

"The Simians of the Commonwealth…" He had scattered memories regarding this as Lacana stared at him in surprise before replying with a look of pity.

"Why mention that extinct race? They enraged the Antediluvian Leviathans during the early stages of the Megalos War which eventually led to their defeat. Under the Origin Leviathan, the Commonwealth and all Simians were decimated and erased across history."

…!

Simians- extinct!

Such a reality caused Archimedes' mind to buzz as he felt that this Dream…became more and more foreign!

It didn't feel right.

It didn't feel like what he knew.

Outerversal Morphons and Lineages of Matter passed around them in this Pantheon as more and more forces were gathered, until one day- the order came from above to set off.

Above the Pantheon, Existence weaved and split apart as if paper was being torn.

The Great Instrument of the Beyonder had inherited a shocking Transcendental Technique of the Beyonder as his means were too mysterious, allowing for him to open up pathways across the Megalos!

On that day, pathways were opened above multiple Pantheons and Omniverses where the forces of the Great Instrument of the Beyonder were clustered, and the war against Nihilos began.

Archimedes followed the throngs of forces as, with Lacana by his side, they delved across the torn pathway to arrive at a region of white-gray blank space.

A space seemingly filled with emptiness and nothingness, and yet the moment they entered…

SCREE!

From the white blankness, massive gray spiders appeared with spikes, legs, and thousands of eyes, Existence around them having been severed as they bore down towards the coming armies as if they knew exactly where they would appear!

Behind the Existence Severing gray spiders, Legions of obsidian worms also rose and surged out like an endless sea as a terrifying battle started.

A war with Existence Severing entities in the lead, Ordinance Amalgamation Entities taking on a somewhat critical role, and all those below them acting like cannon fodder!

It was long and bloody.

A year passed since the war began, and Archimedes gained a thorough grasp of knowledge on a few regions of the Megalos Nihilos and those that moved within it as he decimated Ordinance of Existence and Ordinance Amalgamation entities while studying their bodies and makeup.

Those who knew of the Great Titan, Archimedes, said behind his back that he had gone crazy after spending so many days in the soft thighs of a human.

The Great Titan merely continued with his research as the war waded on, coming to understand quite a bit regarding the Systems of Existence of those within the Megalos Nihilos!

Another unique point he came to grasp during all this…was his search for those he knew of before.

The Observer of Old and Young.

The Great Mother.

The Great Wheel.

The Great Ponderor.

The Great Segregator.

No weavings of such beings were discovered, and months ago he had grasped onto weavings of the Great Mother in another regiment as when he went to go see…he only observed the visage of a massive octopus with thousands of tentacles at the Existence Severing stage.

Not an entity who was countless tentacles in the form of an infinity symbol.

It was another oddity that made Archimedes realize this dream was far too different, along with the fact that unlike the other Amarantos Dreams of the Megalos he had before, this felt far, far too real!

As if even though it was musings of a Dream, it was actually weavings of existence that seemed to be undeniably occurring all around him.

It was not based on the Megalos that he knew.

It was based on something else entirely as the weavings he found himself in…felt like those of an entirely different frequency of Existence than what he knew!
