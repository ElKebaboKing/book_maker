Infinite Mana In The Apocalypse

Chapter 1820 Butchering Newly Risen LEGENDS!L

The Reality Ursidae had roared out the word twice as he bolstered his pride and power after being captured the first time, and Noah returned his word simply as under Emperor's Haki, his voice was sharper than anything in the surroundings!

The majesty of the Reality Ursidae trembled as the forming Quasi-Reified titanic swords trembled before being eradicated completely, the majestic visage of this Reality Bear also being torn apart as his Innate Barrier was shredded along with its body- cracks spreading throughout as stellar golden blood overflowed!

PAH!

The Shockwave of the Haki and the sound waves of the word 'Power' were shockingly the last thing this Reality Ursidae heard, its soul buzzing in disbelief that its newly formed Primogenial Strata could actually be eradicated in such a manner.

"..."

Calmness descended as an illusory river of Reality shattered, prompts beginning to shower Noah's eyes as he chose one out of the many methods he had to loot this being.

He could spend 2 Million Units of the Essence of Reality to cast Reality Assimilator as his Infinite Reality Passages would use a unique authority to actually rip out the Reality of his enemies and infuse it onto Noah- granting effects similar to Grotto Nacres!

For this, he wouldn't be able to obtain any loot in the form of Cores as he would still attain their Bloodlines, Concepts, and other forms of loot.

He could also spend 1 Million Units of the Essence of Reality to cast Reality Devourer as this ability erased any chances of loot, devouring everything about the Chosen target as it all went towards increasing the affinity to the Natural Laws of Reality.

Or he could choose the path of the Randomized Loot Crate route and hope his fortune and destiny grant him stupendous items!

Or he could kill them normally like this LEGEND and obtain their Bloodlines, Cores, and whatever other forms of Loot that LEGENDS granted.

Such was the situation now as Noah chose this last option after already experiencing Randomized Loot Crates, wondrous prompts of loot rushing over his eyes soon after.

[+10 Primogenial Blood Pearls have been obtained…] DING! The effects of Loot Multiplier are applied...[+1,000 Physique Refining Pearls obtained.]

[+10 Liquified Soul Dews have been obtained…] DING! The effects of Loot Multiplier are applied...[+10 Liquified Soul Seas have been obtained.]

[+10 Seeds of Reality have been obtained…] DING! The effects of Loot Multiplier are applied...[+10 False Grotto Nacres have been obtained.]

[+100,000,000Trillion Skill Points obtained…] DING! The effects of Loot Multiplier are applied…[+10,000 Reality Resource Points obtained.]

<The effects of Absolute Looter are applied.>

<...You have already attained the Edicts of the slain enemy.>

<The Racial Bloodline of the Reality Ursidae has been attained.>

"Hmmm…" 𝚏𝗿𝗲𝐞𝚠𝕖𝐛𝗻𝗼𝐯𝕖𝚕.𝚌𝗼𝗺

Noah's eyes shone with luminous lights as there were new forms of loot for him to go over, and there was also the unique phenomenon of the concepts of killed enemies!

He had already cast Ascendant Multilayered Possession on the slain Reality Ursidae and had copied all of its concepts- so he would just be handed the same concepts he already knew when he killed them! What he didn't have that he attained though…was the racial Bloodline of the Reality Ursidae that he could now take as a True Form when fusing with his Halos.

A racial Bloodline that would also have its Aspects pulled into the Unnamed Kainos Emperor Bloodline as it would just continue to be strengthened!

"Then there's the loot…"

Another string of mana broke as two more Reality Ursidae appeared, Noah beginning to peruse through the details of the new loot while his voice nonchalantly rang out towards the waking geniuses who began to gaze around them with a sense of fear and disbelief as Noah spoke calmly just like before.

"Go ahead and break through to Reality, maybe then you can live."

…!

His words were chilly as always as the two geniuses looked at each other with gloomy faces, feeling the restrictions around them in this region as their destinies saw only one possible path forward.

"OOOOH!"

They bellowed out their yells as they began the process of breaking through, Noah ignoring these two future loot sources as his eyes focused on the details of the Loot from LEGENDS.

<Physique Refining Pearl>:: A dense cluster of unique Reality altering essence that can refine the aspect of the Body beginning at the Primogenial Strata. The True Blood and strength of the Body will continue to be refined to a greater level as it can accept more and stronger Physiques…

<Liquified Soul Sea>:: A vast collection of soul power into a surging sea that is suitable for expanding one's soup towards crystallization, and it is even more usable after the crystallization of the soul…

<False Grotto Nacre>:: An imitation of the unique Grotto Nacre that is naturally formed from the densest Domains of Grotto Sanctums over time. It can refine two chosen Aspects of Existence beginning at the Primogenial stage at the same time while granting a slight increase to the Affinity to the Natural Laws of Reality…

The description of the False Grotto Nacres that also appeared from a Randomized Loot Crate finally showed themselves as they were tools that cultivated one's Aspects of Existence along with the Physique Refining Pearls and Liquified Soul Seas!

They were stupendous tools for Noah to attain as all this time, his Aspects of Existence flowed uninterrupted as they didn't accept anything else- truly saying they had reached the limits of all limits as nothing would happen to them.

Yet Noah still wanted to try as in the Dimensional Holy Land, the True Sanguine Clone near the main body took out a False Grotto Nacre that stemmed from a Quintupled one- RUINATION having already listened to Noah's Will to only multiple one Nacre before her Upgrade and subsequent capabilities to multiply things to an even greater level.

The False Nacre looked like a massive mineral forged from the stars holding within it a massively flowing river of Reality that constantly coiled around this mineral like a snake!

It had a rounded bottom and golden crystalline edges that shot upwards, Noah's True Sanguine Clone bringing this above the main body before crushing it with his will as stellar golden nectar began to leak out from this False Grotto Nacre and wash over Noah's body.

It sank and wrapped into his vibrant Origin and Soul as it sought to sink into the Trillion Cosmos within and the vast liquified blue sea that couldn't expand any further, but…

Nothing occurred.

HUUM!

The dreadfully vibrant energy that was full of life from the False Grotto Nacre could not pervade into the Cosmos nor cause the blue soul sea to expand or crystallize as it only slid into the gaps between Noah's Origin and lay calmly- telling him once more to simply resonate his Aspects of Existence and forge his Reality for him to use all this essence!

"..."

Once more, Noah was denied exceeding already exceeded Limits as even loot from LEGENDS that was usable for those who had forged the Realm of Reality…was not usable for him.
