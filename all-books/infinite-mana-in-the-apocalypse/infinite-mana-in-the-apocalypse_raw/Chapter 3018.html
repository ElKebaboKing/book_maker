Chapter 3016 The Initiative! II

3016 The Initiative! II

Her words were shocking ones!

She included the Sanctus Populi, The Imperium, and the Omniversal Pillagers all in one.

|I sense the weavings of Damnation on all of them without fail, and no matter what weavings I try to carry out, nothing can be changed so far. And thus…I came up with the idea of an Initiative.|

|An idea to create a force strong enough to overcome whatever it is that is set to bring damnation to all forces indiscriminately.|

…!

|Though even with that idea, nothing has come of it as of right now. And yet on this day, I meet someone that unlike all of those I have seen and peered into…you do not carry the mark of Damnation. Your demise is not a thing that has been set in stone. So naturally…you may be a critical piece that may finally change the results of the millions of possible weavings I've discerned so far. Thus, I asked you to join my Initiative.|

|...|

After speaking this far, she silently stared at Noah as if she were waiting for him to take it all in.

And yet…he didn't show her much of a reaction of shock or concern, even when she told him of such shocking things!

Seeing this unfolding reality, the fair visage of Sona actually smiled with satisfaction while continuing.

|Good, you're the pragmatic type- likely wondering what you can obtain out of even a situation like this. I like that better than if you had been someone with a sense of righteousness who directly jumps into saying they will do what they can to save everyone. So, what could an Omniversal Anomaly like you want?|

HUUM!

Her eyes pulsed with liquified waves of Dignity.

She waved her hands to reveal golden circles of light all around her that showed different things within them, her intent pointing to each one.

|Various undiscovered and unexplored Unique Existential Domains that will have their dangers, yes, but also their boons.|

She pointed towards multiple circles of golden lights that showed different locations across the Omniverse, untouched by others and bustling with unknown wonders!

|A Teracosm License for the Existential Imperium Exchange, A Carapace of an Existential Pillager, or bundles of Unique Fruits of Existence…you can take your pick.|

…!

The golden circles around her rotated like treasured circles of lights as among all of them, there was one blinding one that was clear and transparent.

It didn't show anything.

She brought such a circle to float behind her as in an instant, it was her face reflected in it.

|Even that may be possible for you attain- depending on how the weavings unfold.|

…!

On that treasured circle of light, there was only her reflection.

Noah looked at this with a smile as his intent weaved out.

|You would even put yourself as a prize I would want? Are you planning on offering yourself to every anomaly you come across in order to get them to join your Initiative?|

WAA!

His words were sharp as Sanctus Princess Sona shook her head as if expecting them.

|Across the Omniverse, many things vary and change, but the nature of a Man remains the same. In the very depths of your drive for power and wonders, there lies a deep desire to dominate the most beautiful things in the Omniverse. A weapon. An extreme. A Record. A Woman. Such a desire is even more intense in men who stand out amongst others- thus, I know this method may work for you. And among the many beautiful things out there, very few surpass me.|

|...|

She spoke such brazen words with utmost confidence that any common being would be left dumbfounded!

|And as for offering myself as a prize for others, just that question there shows your need for possessiveness. To have something be yours and yours alone. Regarding that, I've already discerned which methodologies I need to utilize to attain the services of other Anomalies and bring them into the fold. Whether they stem from the Omniversal Pillagers, Sanctus Populi, or the Imperium.|

A mighty confident Sanctus Princess voiced out.

And Noah blinked at her before replying with sharp eyes.

|Who's to say that you are something I consider an ultimate prize? I have many partners that I can confidently say are no less than you.|

…!

A challenging light arose.

And the dazzling figure of Sanctus Princess Sona placed her hand on her lips as she laughed genuinely for the first time.

As if this laugh alone answered the question regarding whether she was an ultimate prize!

|Look at me.|

She pointed to herself.

Her gorgeous visage.

Her dazzling existence.

Her icy gold aura!

|No, really look at me. Past the extremes, look at…this.|

HUUM!

Her body began to buzz in a shocking manner as the existence surrounding them…began to bend and distort around her.

The space within the Source of Psychros was seemingly bending to her will as she wasn't using any existential authorities to cause such actions!

She was pulling…on the weavings of the Omniverse itself.

|What Teracosm Stage Entities begin touching on.|

WAA!

|What I began to touch upon days after I was born.|

…!

|Omniversal Authorities…something that many only hear about but cannot grasp. I can also lead you towards that as yes…I am the ultimate prize.|

Her level of confidence was utterly astounding to the extent that within Noah's mind…

[The Extremity of Tyranny appreciates the nature of the Entity before you.]

[The Extremity of Cheats states that she seems broken enough.]

[The Extremity of the Protagonist sneers and states that she needs to know her place as an extra in his Fable- that the Weaver of Extremity cannot just come under someone else's fold!]

[The Extremity of Extremity questions whether this being is merely an extra.]

The weavings of his extremes were also jumping on board with their own opinion as Noah took a moment to lock his gaze with the incredulous being before him, his intent weaving out moments later as he thought of the subject of Omniversal Authorities.

|You have my attention.|

…!
