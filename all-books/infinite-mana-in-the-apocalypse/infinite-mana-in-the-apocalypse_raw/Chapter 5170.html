Chapter 5168: Reign IV

The architecture of a nascent Observable Existence is not governed by the slow, grinding gears of natural evolution, but by the decisive imposition of a higher will that treats the laws of physics as...well,mere suggestions to be rewritten.

To transition a space from a mere collection of realms into a genuine Observable Existence requires more than just time and space; it requires the introduction of catastrophic developmental pressures that force the substrate of reality to expand, adapt, and solidify at a rate that defies the very concept of gradualism.

This could be achieved by Miniature Causes...in the form of the intentional ignition of existential flashpoints that serve as the catalysts for a reality’s maturation, ensuring that what was once a protected internal sanctuary becomes a sovereign expanse capable of standing against the weight of the outer Existence. 𝐟𝕣𝗲𝕖𝕨𝗲𝐛𝗻𝗼𝐯𝗲𝚕.𝗰𝚘𝐦

In the center of the folded dimensions of THE Infiniverse, Noah stood as the architect of this accelerated epoch, his hands moving with the rhythmic grace of a conductor directing a symphony of fundamental forces. Boundless rivers of multicolored Quintessence Infiniforce converged between his palms, swirling into dense, vibrating spheres of raw potential that hummed with the resonance of Absolute Infinity.

He did not speak with the breath of a man, but with the authority of Infinity itself, his words manifesting in the air as bars of immutable command.

|The Paleocene-Eocene Thermal Infinimum.|

As he voiced the designation, a sphere of searing, white-gold brilliance erupted from his hands and shot outward into the heights of the radiant sky.

This Miniature Cause was designed to unleash a wave of warming infinity and torrential, nutrient-rich storms across the Three Thousand Realms, raising the ambient temperature of existence to a point of hyper-stimulation that would cause the growth rate of every realm and every physical structure to increase by a hundred times.

|The Cretaceous Infinite Revolution.|

A second sphere, this one pulsing with the deep violet and gold of structural upheaval, followed the first. It carried the authority to trigger a profound shift in the geological and existential foundations of THE Infiniverse, forcing the realms to diversify their environments and refine their spatial boundaries, ensuring that the architecture of this reality possessed the complexity required of a genuine Observable Existence.

|The Mousterian Pluvial.|

The third manifestation took the form of a serene, crystalline blue light that carried the scent of rain and the weight of untapped vitality. Upon its activation, the humidity of the entire existence reached a state of perfect saturation, allowing for the expansion of populations, plants, microbes, and wildlife and all other forms of life at a pace that mimicked the uncontrolled blooming of a spring that would never end!

THE Infiniverse and Ruination watched as these Miniature Causes reached their respective zeniths and broke apart, spreading across THE Infiniverse in rolling waves of transformative authority.

In the sandy gold Realm of Dreams, the effects of the Thermal Infinimum arrived first. The golden dunes, which had previously shifted in slow, languid patterns, began to boil with activity as the warming infinity sank into the grains!

The sky above the desert turned a deep, bruised purple as torrential storms of liquid mana began to fall, and where the rain touched the sand, massive structures of dreaming glass began to sprout like crystalline forests, growing hundreds of feet in minutes as the realm’s development bypassed eons of slow accumulation.

In the Early Veiled Shore, the Cretaceous Infinite Revolution and the Mousterian Pluvial converged to create a scene of terrifying abundance!

The plots of farmland, already teeming with Sacred Herbs, suddenly expanded as the very coastline of the golden sandy beaches pushed outward into the multicolored sea.

The herbs themselves began to evolve in real-time, their stalks thickening into translucent pillars of power while their leaves shimmered with the refined weavings of Infinity, the entire shoreline becoming a dense jungle of high-tier resources that thrummed with the heartbeat of Existence coming alive.

Every single Herb...became Infinite!

Deep within the Realm of Prana, the trees of Yggdrasil responded to the deluge of the Pluvial with a violent surge of vitality. Their roots tore through the domain to claim more territory while their canopies rose to brush against the firmament, the air becoming so thick with the essence of life that new species of iridescent wildlife began to manifest from the ambient energy, populating the emerald heights with a speed that made the concept of birth seem like a redundant relic of the past.

While the realms above underwent their frantic evolution, a second manifestation of THE Infiniverse appeared within the depths of Muspelheim, a place of crimson-gold fires and eternal heat.

Her gaze was filled with a clinical, singular purpose as she navigated the rivers of flame toward a region where a gathering of high-level entities resided.

There, Amelia Osmont sat in quiet conversation with THE Mnemonic Leviathan and the singular cognizance, THE Beholder. They turned as the humanoid manifestation approached.

"Ma’am," THE Infiniverse spoke, her voice carrying the firm resonance of her Master’s new state. "I may need you to carry out a contingency plan Master left behind for us to carry out in the case things went wrong."

Amelia Osmont stood, her features tightening as a shadow of motherly worry crossed her gaze.

"In the case things went wrong?" Amelia asked, her voice trembling slightly. "What... what has gone wrong?"

THE Infiniverse did not hesitate, her movement appearing as a blur of motion that defied spatial distance. She placed her hand upon Amelia Osmont’s shoulder, her touch vibrating with a frequency that was no longer entirely compatible with the Bounded.

|Infinity.|

The word was whispered twice, a silent, terrifying echo that carried the weight of the change Noah had undergone.

|Infinity.|

HUUM!

As the word settled into Amelia’s mind, she felt the cold, boundless nature of infinity as she disappeared!

BOOM!

Soon after...

The atmosphere of Muspelheim, usually defined by the rhythmic crackle of crimson-gold fires, suddenly buckled under a pressure that did not belong to the elemental domain, as the space where the manifestation of THE Infiniverse and Amelia Osmont had stood was violated by a returning surge of multicolored light.

The two figures were deposited back into the heart of the flame-drenched realm with the violent precision of a reality-altering command, the vibrant waves of Infinity that accompanied their arrival washing over the molten landscape and silencing the roar of the eternal fires in a single, sweeping motion!

THE Infiniverse’s gaze became impossibly heavy as she felt the shift in the ambient environment, the effects of the newly ignited Miniature Causes now beginning to bleed into Muspelheim, turning the crimson heat into something refined, sharp, and terrifyingly efficient.

|I discussed contingencies with you during my lesser form.|

Noah’s voice erupted from the very fabric of the reality he now inhabited, his words booming with an imperious resonance!

|All that were considered contingencies are no longer contingencies. There is no requirement for you to attempt anything in this moment, as I shall deny access to all things. From this point forward, across every territory and every folded dimension we occupy, there shall only be the reign of boundless Infinity. Do you understand?|

The weight of the question was a declaration of a new, immutable law. The air itself seemed to vibrate with the frequency of his Absolute state, the multicolored light of his Quintessence Infiniforce painting the obsidian peaks of Muspelheim!

Amelia Osmont stood in the center of the surging light as she looked up somberly.

THE Infiniverse straightened her posture as she replied.

"Yes. Master!"

Without a further word, and without a single gesture of comfort for the woman who had birthed the man he used to be, THE Infiniverse disappeared again, her figure dissolving into the multicolored currents of the atmosphere.

There was no more room for secret plans.

There was no more room for the fear of loss or the caution of the Bounded.

There would only be boundless Infinity.

BOOM!
