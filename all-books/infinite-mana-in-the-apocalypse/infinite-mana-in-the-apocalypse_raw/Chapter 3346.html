Chapter 3344 Ordinance! IV

Forced to collapse his own Existence not to be devoured by another.

He wasn't given the time to shine.

Noah also needed time.

He didn't know everything and couldn't peer across all the mysteries, but he could take it step by step as he ensured that he and his people…did not end up like the Megalonians one day.

The reconstruction…was a cautionary tale!

And it was even more applicable at this moment as a golden banner of Megalos shone unfathomably bright above Noah, a terrifying sea of complexity burning into him from it as…

|Within the Blood of the Omniversal Archontes Engineer, the complexity of the extinct Megalonians flows once more.|

|Your Existence has seamlessly grasped the Origin Sacred Primal Existence of Pyrrhus.|

|Through you, the resurgence of the Megalonians is not an impossibility.|

…!

Seeing these prompts, Noah couldn't help but sigh.

In the past, he had unearthed the weavings of Lumen as they were a unique race that fell under the banner of the Conceptual System of Existence, but that was it.

A race born within an Omniverse in the Megalos Ynnere.

The Megalonians...were entities born within the Megalos Mountains contained within the gaps of the Expanse, their lineage being so terrifying that they were hunted down to extinction by a force that could even eradicate Existence Severing entities as if they were nothing.

And that was an unknown number of cycles ago.

Noah…now inherited the same complexity of such a lineage.

His existence would be akin to a Panacea that far exceeded the value of even an Existence Severing Entity like Nu as at this moment, he accepted the full weavings of the Origin Sacred Primal Megalonian- Pyrrhus!

How would his fate be different from the Megalonians?

"..."

He truly didn't know.

What he did know was the depths of his own complexity of Existence.

He closed his eyes as he felt the Banners above him.

His Ordinances.

As above, so below.

Within his body, he felt the Ordinances pulsing and interacting with each other across his existence, their interactions only prompting the emergence of even greater complexity as their power was truly unique.

At this moment, even without using his connection to the Infinite Omniverse or the Sacred Primal Eye of Existence, he felt like he could use his Ordinances to see everything within the Infinite Omniverse, and even Omniverse X-999.

Not just see, but act.

Not just see, but act.

He instinctively felt like he could impose his Ordinance instantly on a vast surface area, that he could be sitting in another corner of Omniverse X-999 as if an enemy appeared in an opposite corner, he would be able to release an Ordinance upon them and attack without ever having to move a step.

This…was the power of entities who had attained Ordinance of Existence.

They held such subtle control that they could use their ordinance to strike from an extremely far distance!

Noah felt like he easily covered Omniverse X-999, but even this was not his limit.

He didn't know what equivalent distance other entities could cover, but even when he thought about this, it didn't matter.

They could impose a single Ordinance of Existence when they made a move.

But what about him?

He smiled lightly as he opened his eyes and looked at the visage of the Manifestation of the Infinite Omniverse before him.

"Are you ready for your own advancement?"

He had made his jump as a shocking transformation and level of power bloomed.

The Infinite Omniverse was next as it had devoured and assimilated more than enough Dark Matter Cores and Tainted Omniverses that its chances of success in what it wanted to do nearly bordered on the absolute.

|I shall start soon, Master.|

Her intent came out brightly as she prepared.

Far from here, a body of his within a Blancverse in the Expanse was also ready to receive her!

"Very well."

He gave a light nod as his thoughts moved.

In the next second, his eyes reflected the scene of a purple sea of authority as within the Tresorverse of the Omnifather, changes bloomed once more.

His body within disappeared and was taken to another location entirely as here, many, many other auras could be felt.

Tens of thousands of terrifying auras found themselves deposited in a new domain that was astoundingly vast.

Antediluvian Leviathans, Protectors of the Commonwealth, and other unique entities who didn't belong to any of these powers, but held their own shocking power from isolated Omniverses across the Megalos Ynnere!

They all appeared atop what looked like a golden inner bark of the Tresorverse Primaltree of Existence itself, this flat area extending outwards for light years as uniquely, all those who passed through the Reconstruction were deposited near each other.

Tens of thousands of Entities who had transcended their Existence!

The vast majority had at least attained their Eye of Existence, followed by rare geniuses at the One With Existence level who managed to raise the complexity of their existence enough to also arrive in the Expanse.

Then, the third largest group were entities at the Ordinance of Existence, and then the terrifying auras of Ordinance Amalgamation blared here and there!

Of course, mixed in were those at the Quasi-Ordinance of Existence and Quasi-Ordinance Amalgamation level.

Then, there were those whose aura could not be felt as they hid it deep.

But the moment everyone arrived here, they all rapidly grouped up with their own forces.

The massive Antediluvian Leviathans circled around each other, the eyes of many being somber as it seemed the news regarding the Omnifather being the Outsider who inadvertently brought destruction upon the Megalonians began to spread.

The Simians and others belonging to the Commonwealth also rapidly found each other as many of them showed jubilation- thousands surrounding the Shield of the Commonwealth, Old Man Eudemus, who at this moment burned brightly with the terrifying aura of an Ordinance Amalgamation entity!

Many had made tremendous gains in the Tresorverse Sacred Domain as what they brought back with them was an astounding level of power.

As many entities gathered and grouped together, there were a few who remained where they were or probed around cautiously.

Then there was Noah.

Nobody around him, and no group to associate himself with.

He seemed to exude a sense of loneliness as at this moment, a part of his Origin was burning as the obsession of Pyrrhus was buzzing against the Antediluvian Leviathans- the lineage of the Omnifather!

He sighed as he had to take care of this obsession, but before he could think any further, his head rose up along with many others as they stared upwards.

There, an intent bloomed.

|Complexity of Existence alone is not enough, one has to also be proficient in weaving this complexity to achieve substantial results- to truly express their power exceptionally at their level, or even exceed it.|

|The Megalonians were eradicated even though many had attained high complexities of Existence. They were not able to fight back against those more powerful. This Tresorverse Prison Domain is meant to forge Entities with weavings of existence capable of standing confidently in their stage or exceeding it.|

…!
