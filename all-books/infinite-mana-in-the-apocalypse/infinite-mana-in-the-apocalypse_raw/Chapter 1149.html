Chapter 1147 - Deconstructing And Designing Daos! III

The Dao of Archetypes of arguable one of the most prized Daos Noah had made, the past Dao of Conquest that was already elevated to a Cosmic Dao being the only thing ranking higher at this moment based on usage and versatility, with Noah even tying this Dao to be on the same rank as Fealty that allowed him to amass Marks of Antiquity quickly.

Which expert could say they could effectively produce 500 Hegemonies with a wave of their hands?! Now, Noah was about to do just this as he finished devouring a myriad of Dao Fruits, his mind currently buzzing with droves of Information after he gained the full Assimilation of Chronos from Devouring countless [Seeds of Time].

All he had to do now was to pick up more Cosmic Daos through the Cosmic Dao Essence Integration and Emergence ability, or be lucky enough to pick up a Cosmic Dao from the millions of Dao Fruits he would consume in the near future!

He would then turn these Daos into Runic Dao Lines as all of his Assimilations about them would be permanently gone, with Noah not being able to comprehend or assimilate these Daos ever again.

"Ah..."

Noah rose up with a happy smile as he saw his vast advancements, a few more things remaining on his to do list before he began moving across the Ruination Seas.

His bright eyes glistened ever brighter as he sent a message to a few specific beings with the information on the Dao of Archetypes, and what the possibilities there were for them to choose from as at the same time, Noah was spending a myriad of Skill points to elevate any Skill Trees that were low leveled to the rank of Universal Filament!

This moment was the dawn of an Era in the Infinite Cosmos that would be defined by a few hundred specific Vassals that outshine the rest, these Vassals getting the chance to major in a specific Class that stemmed from a Skill Tree of the Antiquity of Mana!

---

"He fucking didn’t...!"

Kazuhiko couldn’t help but curse incredulously as to what seemed like just a minute or two having passed after Noah left the Novus Universe and they began to enjoy the Dao Fruits, he sent them a message about a newly birthed Dao of Archetypes.

The surprise was of course due to the time Dilation of the Ruination Realm, causing his subordinates to shake their heads at his efficiency as information of this Dao and all the choices they would have flowed into the mind of Kazuhiko and a few other selected beings.

"He really made Classes...he wants to turn this already crazy reality into something akin to a game, haha!"

Kazuhiko was shaking his head with a huge smile as he sifted through the boundless information flowing in, Valentina and others also having their faces changing at this newly appearing concept as they all found themselves focusing on it entirely.

"Necromancer..." Barbatos’s eyes shone as her blessed chest heaved, looking at the Archetype that stemmed from the Arch Lich Ra’zan that Noah had now raised to the Universal Filament Realm!

The latest mini skill tree to have been added that brought the Arch Lich skill tree to the Universal Filament Realm was titled [Universal Lich Lord], with it having another set of 4 abilities as always that were brand new, similar to the mini skill Trees of [Galactic Nether Emperor] and [Tyrannical Lich Emperor] that were contained in the Arch Lich Ra’zan skill tree.

If Barbatos were to select the Necromancer Archetype that stemmed from this Skill Tree, she would gain the ability to use all the skills contained within such as [Summon Lich Emperors] or [Necrotic Ring of Death], and all the way down to [Delay Death] and [No Man’s Land] that were one of the few skills from the first stages of the Arch Lich Ra’zan skill tree!

She would get all of this and be able to display battle power at the Universal Realm- as a Hegemony!

"Haha!"

Barbatos couldn’t help but let out a peal of chilling laughter as she felt like Noah deserved a little something extra later on tonight.

She then acted resolutely as she was the first existence to select the first ever Archetype within the Infinite Cosmos!

She didn’t bother to check the other Classes like the Temporal Lord or Terror or even Annihilator that many would be choosing, and she followed her heart as she was already bound to death, choosing the class of a Necromancer.

THRUM!

An oppressive black light shone with Barbatos at the center, her aura gradually changing as the dense black light condensed on her Origin, something becoming inexplicably branded onto her as along with this, a pristine mark of a skull appeared on her palm!

Barbatos gazed at this mark with shining eyes as she felt the flow of information of a myriad of new abilities she could use...as well as the power she currently released that was irrevocably at the realm of a Hegemony.

...!

The Great Old Nazzagath and other newly added Vassals looked at this scene in a stupor, Noah having only granted the right to choose an Archetype to the closest beings from his Original Universe first- so these Hegemonies from the Primordial Cosmos only saw as out of nowhere, a woman at the level of a Paragon began relaxing powerful waves of power that seemed stronger than most Hegemonies!

When they glanced at her chest, aside from its huge blessings, they could still see that her Origin had not formed into a Universe as it was still a manifested galactic filament.

So what...

RUMBLE

Before their thoughts even finished yet another aura extremely similar to this one rose up, this time stemming from the figure of Kazuhiko as he was laughing madly while the essence of Chronos swirled around him gloriously!

Those selecting the Archetypes that utilized Cosmic Daos didn’t mean they would be able to comprehend such Daos right away, and they were only granted access to unconsciously utilize it from the Tyrannical Emperor they pledged Fealty to.

So Kazuhiko had chosen the class of a Temporal Lord as his Archetype, and shocking auras similar to his began to rise from here on out as it only caused the Hegemonies of the Primordial Cosmos to nearly lose their minds at what was happening.

Not too far away from Kazuhiko, the cry of an Emperor of a race rang out clearly as the beak of a certain being rose towards the boundless space.

"RAA! RAA! RAA!"

The cry rang out loud three times, a golden glow being seen cascading down on the Emperor Penguin as this leading Spiritual Beast chose Incandescence Guardian for his Archetype, more than likely due to it causing him to shine an even greater luster of gold than anything else as the essence of Fate swirled around its body!

Such shocking scenes began to unfold fully as the man causing all of it was detached from it, currently still in the Ruination Realm as he finished his own preparation and was about to face an ordeal of sneaking into the soul of a Primordial!
