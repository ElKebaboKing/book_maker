Chapter 2553 Madness! IV

A Rising Junior expressing the authority of Death so proficiently!

The eyes of Ayame's Grandfather shone brightly as Hyperversal Extremity Authority continued to flow from him and into Ayame.

{Continue.}

He beckoned for Ayame to continue her analysis as she focused whole gazing at the constantly changing battlefield.

"The different forces have fractured into their own battles, and now…it seems more casualties are about to appear- this result brought about by the wide ranging debuff abilities of the Haven Lich and the soldiers of the True Emperor of Quintessence not letting opportunity go to waste…"

As she spoke, her eyes came to focus on a screen that showed Commander Vladic and his Noblesse Apex Aeonic Lifeforms surrounded by cackling illusory skulls as they rotated their Dynamis of Extremity to hold back its effects, their figures trying to stay close to the Legions of Singularity Overseers with their Apex Astral Leviathans as at this time, an Apex Aeonic Noblesse erupted with power to prevent the deathly authorities of the Haven Lich from reaching him!

He breathed heavily as the moment he thought he could catch his breath…

CRUNCH!

A cerulean blue light flashed.

Over a thousand Apex Aeonic Damage Values flashed as a viscous blue slimy light instantly chomped onto half of his whole body, his Aspects of Existence buzzing and experiencing a disconnect as in shock, his soul was destroyed right after!

Half of his body was missing as on the side, a blue slime chewed with a giddy light! The lifeless body began to fall from the skies at this time when a moment later, the verdant black mist of death that had spread across the battlefield wrapped around it.

The authority that Alexander King had released!

It seemed to be ever present on the battlefield as from the dead body of the Noblesse, deathly energies erupted out as a roar followed next, the skeletal form of another Void Dracolich rising from it as a third King Tier Thanatos Vassal was born!

On the side, Commander Vladic and others were ashen as before they could even react…

RAA!

A cry boomed out beside them as a lustrous golden penguin used its flippers to swing a golden Greataxe down!

"Death, and more death."

Ayame spoke as she looked at the scenes before her.

"The more time passes and the more deaths that occur, the more favorable this War becomes for Osmont. Even if they are more powerful, they will get drowned by the Legions of Undead that hold no fear of death, and these massive creatures I could only find records of as Enders who seem to not fear death either. Currently, the ball is in Ahpuch's court if he wishes to reverse the dominance of his enemy before it is too late. The answer to this lies in him using his edge as someone who had achieved the 16th Boundary Layer, if he doesn't- oh?"

Ayame's words came to a halt.

They focused on the screen showing Ahpuch as he gazed at the unfolding battle with a smile.

And he then spoke.

"The more death and destruction there is, the more powerful I am too, you know?"

He spoke while bringing his right hand before his chest as if he wanted to grasp his beating heart, his eyes closing as he uttered out.

{Domain Expansion- Interminable Destruction}

…!

THUMP!

A beat of a heart was heard.

And the whole battlefield changed!

In a matter of an instant, the vast Apex Battlefield and even close to the edges of it where the Wills of watching existences were hidden- an astonishing domain was laid down!

It covered everything in an instant.

"Domain Expansion," Ayame uttered with a somber expression as Ahpuch had finally made a genuine move.

"Something achievable commonly at the 17th Boundary Layer, but some Concepts can attain it earlier if they are unique enough. It allows one to add the might of a Dynamis of Extremity onto an existing Boundary Concept and expand it to an alarming degree, granting a domain of Absolute Control where one can instantly display their power across it. Even the surrounding reality will bend and conform to the truth of the imposed Dynamis and Concept…"

As she spoke, the scene before them greatly changed as massive crimson pillars of flames manifested across the Apex Battlefield a moment after the Interminable Destruction domain was expanded!

Interminable! This was the Absolute Dynamis of Extremity that Ahpuch grasped. And Destruction was his core concept as his Domain Expansion was Interminable Destruction!

Endless Destruction.

So long as one was within it…

BOOM!

The dense appearing crimson pillars of flames pulsed as they released hellish torrential storms of destruction that washed over the Prince Tier Thanatos Vassals- their skeletal structure facing over a thousand Apex Damage Values as their bones cracked and melted under the banner of Destruction!

ROAR!

The two Void Dracolich bellowed out as a dozen crimson pillars of Destruction appeared around each of them, the torrential waves of power they released ripping apart their wings as it seemed soon enough, they would face Destruction.

And the 300 Enders as well as the 200 Battalion of Apex Aeonic Lifeforms adorned in golden Armament that truly seemed like Quasi-Extremity Relics faced their first setbacks as their bodies were forced to go on the defensive for the first time.

Golden grandeur pulsed from them as a vast majority were knocked back, their barriers destroyed as their Vitality Values took a hit!

And this was only the first wave of torrential flames of Destruction from the {Domain Expansion- Interminable Destruction}.

When the next one came…

"Ahpuch has finally made a correct move and placed his enemies in a predicament, if Alexander King or his summoned Haven Lich do not pull out something to-...!"

Her eyes opened wide.

Amidst the burning pillars of Destruction and wide range torrential flames of a not yet fully established domain, the cackling of the Haven Lich could be heard.

{Did I not say there would only be death?}

BZZT!

His aura soared.

The flaming bone crown atop him spun fervently as he commandeered his Master's {Quintessential Undead Rise}

From the ashes of bones of the dead Prince Tier Thanatos Vassals and King Tier Thanatos Vassals, the lush black light expanded in concentration as what was destroyed reformed again.

And this time, the deathly energies were doubled as instead of a single Thanatos Vassal rising…two could be seen from a single spot!

Two reforming Void Dracolich became 4.

Over ten thousand more Prince Tier Thanatos Vassals last appeared as the Battlefield became even more chaotic!

At the same time, 7 massive points of light emerged and made their presence known.

One was a particularly frightening one as while shaped like a circular plane of Existence- an Entity whose body was made up of pulsing crimson black meat vibrating with tendrils of the Absolute Thanatos Dynamis of Extremity moved.

Its circular meaty visage held no eyes, with one seeing a single structure forming from one end to another as it was…a mouth. On its backside, a burning symbol reading {GLUTTONY} could be seen as it radiated terror!

The mouth on the front side suddenly opened in a devilish grin to reveal horribly sharp teeth and an endless abyss, this single summoned creature out of Seven new ones beginning to inhale as shockingly…the crimson flaming pillars nearby began to be sucked towards its abyssal jaws endlessly.

…!

The battle was constantly changing.

Ayame and many others watched it and analyzed every move made as just when it seemed the dominance of the battle would shift from one side to another, magnificent changes bloomed!

But there were still the injured forces of the True Emperor of Quintessence that may very well start seeing some deaths due to the Interminable Destruction Domain laid out by Ahpuch in the next moment when it released another one of its torrential waves of Destruction.

At such a moment, a being stood up from the backside of a Draconian Phoenix as a white coat billowed gloriously in the wind.

His voice was barely discerned amidst the chaotic war, but it buzzed out nonetheless!

{All Aspect Hyperversal Heal}.

…!

A lush blue light of life and grandeur emerged amidst all the death.

And the troops of the True Emperor of Quintessence…were enveloped by such a thick aura of life that some weaker Apex Aeonic Lifeforms were blinded!
