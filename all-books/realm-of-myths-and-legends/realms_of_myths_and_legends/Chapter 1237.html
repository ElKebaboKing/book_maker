Chapter 1237: Harnessing Energy Pill

"Since everything has been settled, you should proceed with the promotion test. I have heard some interesting things. It makes me look forward to the practical portion." Varuu said.

The next moment, a dark fog appeared in a bundle near the judging area. Then, a few seconds later, the dark fog transformed into a dark blue chair that seemed to be made of a mixture of a crystal and metallic materials.

Varuu took a seat on the chair and said, "Do not mind me. I will be observing—silently."

As Varuu said those words, her gaze swept across the observers, who felt a chill run down their spines.

This was obviously Varuu’s warning to them that if they dared to disturb the promotion test, they would follow in Konpel’s footsteps.

"So that’s the Head of the Apothecary Guild. I expected some old man, but—how can someone be so pretty? I almost mistook her for a trephasia." Celia said as her eyes had long since lit up when Varuu made her appearance.

Many of the Apothecaries and observers were smitten by Varuu’s beauty. However, they were careful with their gazes after seeing how she handled Konpel.

"What a domineering person. She took care of everything and restored order the moment she appeared. Even those observing us have quieted down." Worldly Skies commented.

Rozomius cleared his throat as he stepped forward and said, "I will now announce the winner of the special rewards for the best answer given for each question. Since the same individual has given the best answer for all three questions, it saves some time. Apothecary Izroth—congratulations."

When the Apothecaries heard that Izroth’s name had been called to obtain the special rewards, they could only let out helpless sighs. If it were before Izroth took the spotlight a moment ago to explain his insights in detail and any take questions about it that went his way, there would have been some who questioned the decision. However, it was clear that Izroth’s level on understanding regarding the questions asked was on the same level of those who came up with the questions. Rather, it may even be slightly above that!

If that was the case, how could they call it a moment of favoritism? Of course, it also helped that Varuu had arrived. No one wanted to risk saying the wrong thing and offending the Apothecary Guild in front of its Head.

Swoosh!

Rozomius casually waved his hand as three small red boxes appeared in front of him before slowly floating over to Izroth.

Izroth accepted the red boxes that looked just big enough to house a single pill. And, after using his Energy Vision Sense and Divine Appraisal to view the contents of the boxes right before accepting them, Izroth quickly discovered that each box did indeed contain one pill each.

"What you have received is called the Harnessing Energy Pill. Although it is only a grade five pill, the ingredients needed to craft it are exceedingly rare. That is why the Apothecary Guild can only produce a total of five Harnessing Energy Pills a year. However, it provides a great helping hand when it comes to nurturing promising talents. Under normal circumstances, we would only give out two Harnessing Energy Pills for this part of the promotion test. But, seeing as how this is the first time we are introducing this particular method of promotion test, the Head was gracious enough to increase the amount given by one. As for the other Harnessing Energy Pills, well, it is left up to the discretion of the Apothecary Guild." Rozomius said in a meaningful tone.

He then continued, "Apothecary Izroth, as someone who has acquired the Natural Energy of Lightning, this pill will be particularly useful to you. While it will not solve all of your immediate problems, it can aid in building a sturdy foundation for the path you must tread. I suggest consuming it immediately in order to receive the full benefits. I will also take this moment to give all participants a five minute break before we move on to the final portion of the promotion test. Prepare yourselves well."

When Rozomius finished speaking, a string of golden text appeared in the air above as a timer that showed as 5:00 formed and started to count down.

At the same time, the participants all had their eyes on Izroth.

As for Izroth, he had his attention turned to the small boxes as he wasted no time opening one.

The instant the box opened, a powerful and refreshing medicinal scent filled the area and revealed a single deep blue pill with a tight helix of white markings around its surface.

’If taken alone, for a grade five pill, its effects seem somewhat lacking on the surface. But, when taken along with the Flow of Energy Pill, it becomes something that easily rivals a grade six- No, perhaps even a grade seven pill. Though that’s too much of a simplistic comparison. In truth, its value exceeds even that of a grade seven pill thanks to the hidden effect.’

Izroth’s current bonus crafting speed was 150%, while his bonus pill crafting speed was at 45%. This meant that, when it came to crafting pills, he would be 195% faster than normal.

But, once he consumed the Harnessing Energy Pill, his pill crafting speed would shoot up by a staggering 500%! Among all the players in RML, there would probably be no one who could craft pills as fast as him!

Izroth removed the Harnessing Energy Pill from the box and consumed it.

Izroth felt a refreshing wave of energy flow throughout his entire body. From his head, fingertips, all the way down to his feet—Izroth could clearly sense the Natural Energy that entered his body through the Guiding Light.

As Izroth was getting used to the new feeling brought about by consuming the Harnessing Energy Pill, the gazes of the participants seemed to sharpen as some filled with greed and anticipation.

Harnessing Energy Pills—which of them did not know of that rare pill even that Apothecary Guild could not make as they pleased?!

"Three boxes... If I can get my hands on one, then...!" One of the Apothecaries muttered.

Everyone present had the same idea in their mind. The Harnessing Energy Pill would not give any additional benefits if consumed more than once. Therefore, Izroth had no real use for the other pills.

What surprised them more than anything was that the Apothecary Guild actually handed over three precious Harnessing Energy Pills to a single Apothecary. Nonetheless, this was a chance for them!

"Apothecary Izroth, I would like to offer a trade for one of the Harnessing Energy Pills in your possession. I am willing to trade one grade seven pill personally crafted by my father. As I’m sure you know, consuming more than one Harnessing Energy Pill brings you no additional benefits. Therefore, it’s not a loss on your part. Plus, my Duvelle family will owe you a favor." Pierce was the first to make a move as he approached Izroth.

"One of those pills should have originally been mine, yet they all ended up in his hands. I have no choice but to momentarily lower my head if I want to get my hands on a Harnessing Energy Pill. Even my father, who spent years trying to gather the ingredients for my sake was unable to do so under the nose of the Apothecary Guild. I have no choice but to rely on him." Pierce thought to himself as he secretly clenched his fist in anger and frustration behind his back.

"I’m afraid these pills already have owners." Izroth said calmly.

Then, without hesitation, Izroth handed over both boxes—one to Worldly Skies and the other to Celia.
