Chapter 1737: Time Trap!

"Endless Lord, I really envy you. You crossed the Undying Dragon Rock Fire Sea without getting injured, and you're only a Half-step Transcendence Legendary Lord, while we top Transcendence Legendary Lords are heavily injured!"
Lord Jinbei said enviously.
"Just luck. It's just that I happen to not fear the Undying Dragon Rock Fire," Ye Tian said with a slight smile.
"Let's go and see what good things are here," Lord Yunzhong said.
Then, Ye Tian separated from them; otherwise, if they found something good, it would be difficult to distribute.
*Whoosh whoosh whoosh!!!*
Several people flew off in different directions.
Everyone flew very slowly, fearing they might encounter traps.
After all, this was Yanglie Lord's tomb, so they naturally had to be careful, or they wouldn't even know how they died.
Yanglie Lord's tomb was huge, like a new world.
As a Legendary Realm Lord, Yanglie Lord naturally didn't want his tomb disturbed after death, which was why he set up the Undying Dragon Rock Fire at the entrance. However, Yanglie Lord had set a restriction that lords above the Half-step Legendary Realm were not allowed to enter the tomb. The internal settings couldn't be too strong and couldn't threaten the level of a Legendary Realm Lord.
Moreover, Yanglie Lord must have anticipated that his tomb would eventually be discovered. Thus, the outer areas might offer some benefits, but the interior would inevitably have very dangerous traps that could cause even near-invincible Transcendence Lords to fall many times.
Therefore, Ye Tian also had to be careful.
Although he didn't care about a single duplicated body, if it fell, it would waste a lot of time.
So, if he could avoid falling, he would.
Soon, Ye Tian discovered a palace hall suspended in the void.
He then entered it, but the hall was wrapped in restrictions. To enter, the restrictions had to be broken.
*Boom!*
Ye Tian tried to attack it and found that the restriction's defense was decent. It would take several top Transcendence Lords working together some time to break it.
He could break the restriction alone, but it would waste a lot of time.
So, Ye Tian directly gave up on the items inside this hall.
After all, an item as precious as the Time River Bead couldn't possibly be placed in such a spot.
In the following time, Ye Tian found several halls, all with restrictions.
He also saw some Transcendence Lords breaking restrictions while healing their injuries.
Ye Tian didn't care about the benefits here; instead, he was looking for the passage to the interior of the tomb.
"I'll create a few duplicated bodies and search together."
Ye Tian began to duplicate his body.
Soon, three duplicated bodies gathered here.
The three duplicated bodies traveled in different directions, searching for the tomb's internal passage.
Finally, after spending quite some time, Ye Tian found it.
And not only did Ye Tian find it, but several Transcendence Legendary Lords also found it.
An unimaginably massive Void Gate appeared in the void. Anyone could tell that behind this Void Gate was the path leading to the interior of the tomb.
At this moment, Ye Tian arrived here, and three Transcendence Legendary Lords were hesitating whether to enter.
Seeing they didn't dare to enter, Ye Tian went in first.
Seeing Ye Tian enter, the three no longer hesitated and followed him in.
After passing through the Void Gate, the other side was a calm area.
In the distance, the shadows of palaces could be seen; that should be the internal tomb area.
"The treasures there are mine!"
One Transcendence Legendary Lord said excitedly.
He quickly shuttled across, but after traveling a short distance, he instantly froze, and then his body was annihilated.
"What happened?"
The other two Transcendence Lords were bewildered.
How did this Transcendence Lord suddenly get annihilated?
"A Time Trap!"
Ye Tian figured it out.
Just now, that Transcendence Legendary Lord had fallen into a Time Trap set by Yanglie Lord. His body appeared to be in the distance, but in reality, he had fallen into a Time Trap, which was extremely dangerous and directly annihilated the Transcendence Legendary Lord's body.
At this point, the other two Transcendence Legendary Lords didn't dare to move. They stayed where they were and observed their surroundings.
Suddenly.
One of the Transcendence Legendary Lords became unable to move, as if frozen in time, and was soon annihilated as well.
Now, Ye Tian and the remaining Transcendence Legendary Lord realized that the Time Traps appeared randomly and targeted intruders. Even if one didn't move, they would still be targeted by a Time Trap.
Suddenly.
Danger loomed, and Ye Tian immediately shifted away.
A Time Trap appeared where he had been standing, but he dodged it in time.
"I am about to master time. The moment a Time Trap descends, I can sense it in time and dodge it," Ye Tian thought.
Therefore, he wasn't afraid of the Time Traps here.
Yanglie Lord probably hadn't expected that among the Legendary Lords below the Half-step Legendary Realm, there would be someone about to master time. Thus, the Time Traps he set were essentially neutralized.
*Whoosh!*
Ye Tian rushed toward the distant palace. Whenever a Time Trap descended, he dodged it directly.
At this time, the other Transcendence Lord thought Ye Tian was just lucky and also braced himself to rush toward the distant palace.
However, after traveling only a short distance, a Time Trap descended.
"Why is he fine, but something happens to me as soon as I move?"
This Transcendence Legendary Lord thought before his clone was annihilated.
He felt it was too unfair; the Time Traps seemed to be targeting him specifically.
Soon, Ye Tian passed through the Time Trap area and arrived in front of a massive palace.
In front of the palace, there was a stele with some words inscribed on it, left by Yanglie Lord.
"Latecomer, if you have passed through here, it proves that your strength is likely among the most monstrous of the Transcendence Lord level. I know you latecomers are here for treasures. My main tomb does not store any treasures; please do not enter, or the tomb will trigger self-destruction. The treasures you seek are in that green palace. After obtaining the treasures, please leave this place."
After reading it, Ye Tian didn't think Yanglie Lord was lying.
Therefore, he would not enter the main tomb palace before him.
He headed toward the green palace nearby. If the Time River Bead was in this tomb, it was very likely inside there.
*Whoosh!*
Ye Tian shuttled across quickly and soon entered the green palace.
