Chapter 1735: Auctioning Slots!

The message from the Time Merchant Guild said there was news regarding a Time River Bead, but it wasn't that someone was auctioning one.
For a treasure of this caliber, very few people were willing to auction it off unless they were truly lacking a specific treasure and chose to auction it through a barter system.
This time, the Time Merchant Guild stated that a Time River Bead had appeared within a ruin, but the specific details could only be learned by going to the Time Merchant Guild.
Thus, Ye Tian immediately went to the Time Merchant Guild in the Boundless Sea.
...
Time Merchant Guild.
Ye Tian discovered that many Legendary Lords, and even Half-Step Legendary Realm Lords, had appeared at the Time Merchant Guild today.
After all, since it involved a Time River Bead, not only did Ye Tian need it, but others did as well. A treasure like the Time River Bead had too many uses; it didn't just help condense a Supreme Legendary Body, it even assisted in mastering time.
Soon.
Ye Tian learned the specific details at the Time Merchant Guild.
It turned out that not long ago, a ruin had been discovered in the Void Region, which could also be described as the tomb of a Legendary Realm Lord.
It was very difficult for Legendary Realm Lords to perish. They were either killed by someone, in which case there would be no tomb, or they suffered a fatal injury where their life force drained uncontrollably and could not be healed. Only when death was certain would they build a tomb for themselves before dying.
This was the case this time; that Legendary Realm Lord had built a tomb before perishing. In fact, news that the Legendary Realm Lord was about to perish had spread a long time ago, but no one had been able to find them.
And that Legendary Realm Lord possessed a Time River Bead.
A Peak Legendary Realm Lord from the Time Merchant Guild performed a calculation and deduced that the Time River Bead was still there and had not been used up by that Legendary Realm Lord; it was right inside that tomb.
Logically, once this tomb was discovered, a Peak Legendary Realm Lord should have been able to easily take the treasures inside, but in reality, they could not be taken.
This was because the tomb's restriction prohibited any lord above the Half-Step Legendary Realm from entering. Half-Step Legendary Realm Lords simply could not break through the restriction set up by that Legendary Realm Lord, and Legendary Realm Lords did not dare to forcefully invade the tomb.
Because once a forced entry was attempted, the tomb, which was connected to the River of Time, would trigger a Time Explosion. The power of the River of Time pouring down would be enough to even heavily injure a Legendary Realm Lord; only a Peak Legendary Realm Lord would remain unscathed.
However, a Peak Legendary Realm Lord would not destroy this tomb either. Once destroyed, the treasures within the tomb would be obliterated, and even the Time River Bead could not be preserved, making it pointless.
Moreover, a single Time River Bead was not enough to tempt a Peak Legendary Realm Lord into action.
At this moment, the Time Merchant Guild was filled with the sounds of Legendary Lords chatting.
"I heard that this time, the Time Merchant Guild is going to auction the location of that tomb ruin."
"The Time Merchant Guild really knows how to make money. A single slot will probably sell for a massive amount of wealth."
"There's no other way. Currently, this location is controlled by the Time Merchant Guild. If we want to enter that ruin, we can only purchase a slot."
"Someone probably discovered it and then entrusted the Time Merchant Guild to auction it. Otherwise, the Time Merchant Guild would have just sent their own Legendary Lords in to explore. With the Time Merchant Guild's strength, finding the Time River Bead wouldn't be a problem."
"Let's wait for more news. The auction time is bound to come out soon."
Ye Tian waited for news at the Time Merchant Guild. He didn't have to wait for too long before a manager from the Time Merchant Guild appeared.
This manager said, "Our Time Merchant Guild is about to begin auctioning slots for that ruin. Furthermore, those who obtain a slot must not speak of it to others and must swear an oath."
Black-hearted!
Many Legendary Lords thought to themselves.
If they could talk about it, they could at least tell their respective forces or sell it to others. But once restricted by an oath, they could only auction it for themselves and go themselves.
In this way, the wealth the Time Merchant Guild earned from selling slots might even be more than what could be earned from entering that tomb. After all, no one knew exactly how many good things were in that tomb.
"Has your Time Merchant Guild entered that ruin? If you've already gone in, then there's no need for an auction."
A Transcendence-tier Legendary Lord said.
"Rest assured, we only sent one Transcendence-tier Legendary Lord, and they withdrew very quickly without deeply exploring the ruin. Therefore, your worries about our Time Merchant Guild taking the treasures inside will not happen," the Time Merchant Guild manager said.
"That's good then."
The many Legendary Lords still had great confidence in the Time Merchant Guild's reputation. At the very least, the tomb of a Legendary Realm Lord was not enough to make them ruin their own credibility.
Not long after, the auction began.
Inside the Time Merchant Guild's internal void.
Legendary Lords arrived one after another, but there were very few Half-Step Legendary Realm Lords. These few were unlikely to participate in the auction because there was no need; even if they obtained a slot, they couldn't enter the ruin.
Thus, they were only there to spectate.
Basically, those who came were Transcendence-tier Legendary Lords and a few Domain-tier Legendary Lords. Such a ruin was bound to be extremely dangerous, and it would be easy for a Domain-tier Legendary Lord to perish inside.
Once they perished, they would have not only paid the price of the auction but also sacrificed an avatar.
If there were Legendary Lords who didn't even have avatars, they would be even less daring to enter.
At this time.
An auctioneer took out a card and said, "One card represents one slot. We will send the location information to the customers who obtain a slot at a certain time. We will now begin auctioning the first slot for thirty million Time Coins."
Time Coins were an internal currency issued by the Time Merchant Guild. A single Time Coin was of immense value, so thirty million Time Coins were comparable to the lifelong wealth of an ordinary Legendary Lord.
"Thirty million!"
A Transcendence-tier Legendary Lord shouted.
No one raised the bid!
They weren't stupid!
It was just auctioning slots, and it wasn't just one slot. The Time Merchant Guild wouldn't limit the number of slots, so they wouldn't spend extra money. Although the starting price was thirty million Time Coins, it was effectively the final closing price.
With no one raising the bid, the first slot was quickly finalized.
"Thirty million Time Coins... I really don't have that much left."
Ye Tian thought to himself.
Thus, he went to exchange for Time Coins midway through.
The Time Merchant Guild accepted many precious items, and these precious items could be exchanged for Time Coins.
Shortly after, Ye Tian exchanged for 30 million Time Coins and returned to the auction hall.
Auction after auction, the speed was very fast.
The number of Transcendence-tier lords in the Boundless Sea was limited, and the number of Transcendence-tier lords willing to purchase a slot was also limited.
Many Transcendence-tier lords would wait until the news had completely spread before going to that ruin; they weren't in a hurry to be in the first batch.
Therefore, they didn't want to waste money.
But Ye Tian was in a hurry to obtain the Time River Bead, so he naturally purchased a slot.
Soon, Ye Tian successfully was the first to call out the bid: "Thirty million."
A slot was obtained!
