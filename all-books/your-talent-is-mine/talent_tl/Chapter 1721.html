Chapter 1720: Blood Tissue of a Supreme Legendary Body Creature!

Ye Tian went through countless attempts. From having no results at all in the beginning to slowly seeing progress, once he achieved results, he would enter the Gray Mist Fire Sea to verify them, thereby identifying the flaws in his particle structure.
Consequently, three hundred thousand Eternal Years of research finally allowed him to modify his own particle structure to its most stable state.
On this day.
Each of Ye Tian's bodies began to reshape themselves using the brand-new particle structure.
Buzz!
Ye Tian's body transformed into endless will, Innate Eternal Rules, and countless body particles. Then, they began to reorganize. This reorganization had to be completed in a short time; otherwise, the body's self-healing and recovery capabilities would forcibly restore the body structure to its previous state.
In an instant, the particle structure was reshaped.
Ye Tian's body finished reshaping, and a large amount of source energy began to stabilize this newly reshaped body.
"The body reshaping is complete. My physical strength hasn't increased much, but my body structure is ten times more stable."
Ye Tian murmured.
Previously, he had always believed his body was perfect, but after experiencing the Gray Mist Fire Sea, he realized there was an even more perfect body structure.
If he hadn't encountered the Gray Mist Fire Sea, he wouldn't have thought about reshaping his body.
It could be said that this Gray Mist Statue opportunity allowed him to truly complete a transformation.
"It's time to see if I can cross the Gray Mist Fire Sea."
Ye Tian stepped into the Gray Mist Fire Sea. Endless flames surged into his body, attempting to dismantle it.
Although Ye Tian's body was weak, his Eternal particle structure was incredibly stable, allowing him to endure it through sheer force.
He continued to walk forward, attracting the attention of many Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords.
"What? The Endless Lord is actually advancing through the Gray Mist Fire Sea. How is he holding on?"
They were stunned.
Previously, Ye Tian had also entered the Gray Mist Fire Sea, but he had always come out quickly. This time, however, he was constantly moving forward.
They didn't dare to do the same; otherwise, if they couldn't withstand the flames, their bodies would collapse directly, leading to the fall of that avatar.
When Ye Tian had covered half the distance of the Gray Mist Fire Sea, the Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords present didn't know what to say.
If Ye Tian passed through the Gray Mist Fire Sea while they, a group of Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords, could not, wouldn't that be too embarrassing?
After a while longer, Ye Tian was about to pass through the Gray Mist Fire Sea. Although his body was already covered in cracks and seemed on the verge of collapse, his body's particle structure was too stable, so he was still far from truly collapsing.
Finally, the Gray Mist Fire Sea ended, and Ye Tian successfully crossed it.
On the other side of the Gray Mist Fire Sea, a group of Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords were filled with envy. At the same time, they were very confused. Ye Tian, a Domain Realm Legendary Lord who had just broken through, should be inferior to them in terms of cultivation, will, and mastery of the Eternal Dao. Why was he able to cross the Gray Mist Fire Sea while they could not?
Could it be that the Gray Mist Fire Sea was showing special favor to Ye Tian? But that seemed highly unlikely!
Just as Ye Tian crossed the Gray Mist Fire Sea, a reward came into his hands.
This reward was somewhat special, contained in a bottle. Inside was a piece of blood tissue.
The bottle itself was not simple either. Ye Tian couldn't tell what material it was made of, but even when he squeezed it with force, it was like an ordinary person squeezing a glass bottle—it wouldn't budge at all.
"Perhaps it's a bottle refined by an Eternal Controller," Ye Tian guessed.
The purpose of this bottle was to hold that piece of blood tissue, or perhaps it served as a seal.
Therefore, even if he truly could break the bottle, Ye Tian wouldn't do it.
Immediately, Ye Tian extended his will toward it. The bottle surprisingly did not block Ye Tian's will.
Thus, Ye Tian's will touched that piece of blood tissue.
Boom!!!!
A terrifying aura erupted from the blood tissue, nearly inflicting heavy damage on Ye Tian's will.
Ye Tian's will immediately withdrew from the bottle, his face turning slightly pale.
"What kind of creature's blood is this? It's so terrifying!" Ye Tian said in shock.
But soon, he became very excited.
Because this biological blood tissue was alive and contained the body structure of that creature. Just by looking at it for an instant, Ye Tian could judge that the creature's body far surpassed that of a Legendary Realm Lord; its body had definitely exceeded the scope of an Eternal Body.
From the Domain Realm Legendary Lord stage to the Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord stage, the most important part is transcendence. Not only must the Will Rule Domain transcend to reach the level of a Will Rule World, but the body must also transcend.
How does a body transcend?
It must be improved from the Eternal Body. Among those in the Half-step Eternal Realm, experts of the Eight Extremes Realm have already elevated their Eternal Bodies to the Transcendence Realm, but that is the classification and terminology used in the Source Sea.
In the Boundless Sea, the transcendence of a Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord refers to transcending the category of the Eternal Body itself. The body is no longer an Eternal Body but a realm above it.
The meanings of these two types of transcendence are different.
In the Boundless Sea, the physical constitution of a Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord is called a Legendary Body.
A Legendary Body is a constitution that stands above the Eternal Body. it belongs to legend—a realm that ordinary Legendary Realms cannot cross. It is above the Eternal Body, which is why it is referred to as having the meaning of transcendence.
Of course, the body of a Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord is not a true Legendary Body; it only possesses the characteristics of a Legendary Body. Only the body of a Legendary Realm Lord can be called a true Legendary Body.
A Legendary Body combined with a Will Rule World is the true hallmark of a Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord.
As for the creature to which this piece of blood tissue belonged, its body was a Legendary Body, and not just any Legendary Body, but the most top-tier kind—one that could be called a Supreme Legendary Body.
In the Boundless Sea, a Supreme Legendary Body also belongs to legend. The bodies of many Legendary Realm Lords are not Supreme Legendary Bodies; they haven't even seen a Supreme Legendary Body.
And now, Ye Tian had obtained the blood tissue of a Supreme Legendary Body creature.
"A Supreme Legendary Body creature is likely invincible even among Legendary Realm Lords. Only an Eternal Controller could possibly kill one," Ye Tian thought to himself.
He put away the bottle, then continued forward.
As for researching the Supreme Legendary Body, he would do that later; this was no place for research.
Soon, Ye Tian arrived at the location of the next trial.
Here, all those Legendary Realm Lords from the True Void Sacred Land were blocked; not a single one had passed.
Ye Tian's arrival left them all dumbfounded.
"Endless Lord, you passed through the Gray Mist Fire Sea!!!"
Lord Wushuang said in disbelief.
She had essentially watched Ye Tian grow all the way from an Initial Realm Legendary Lord. It was one thing for him to advance to a Domain Realm Legendary Lord so quickly, but how could he have also charged through the Gray Mist Fire Sea?
Those few Half-step Legendary Realm Lords had been blocked for a long period and only managed to charge through while heavily injured.
Yet Ye Tian's strength was many times inferior compared to a Half-step Legendary Realm Lord!
