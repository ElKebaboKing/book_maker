Chapter 1724: Time Eternal Gold!

Within the Purple Aurora Domain, the range of will detection was already small, and even the detection range of the Purple Aurora Beasts was not large. Thus, as Ye Tian tracked this Purple Aurora Beast from a distance using only his Copy Talent, the beast could not sense him at all and remained unaware that someone was following it.
Initially, Ye Tian discovered nothing, but he was not in a hurry; he had plenty of time.
Time slowly passed. One day, a Legendary Realm Lord encountered this Purple Aurora Beast, and the two sides fought a great battle. Ye Tian did not intervene. In a place like the Purple Aurora Domain, the Purple Aurora Beast had a very strong ability to preserve its life and was skilled at escaping.
Consequently, the Purple Aurora Beast managed to flee.
After approximately several epochs had passed, the Purple Aurora Beast seemed to grow bored and began to traverse rapidly in one direction.
"Could it be heading back?"
Ye Tian's heart stirred.
He immediately followed.
Possessing the Legendary-grade Eternal Technique, Void Step, his speed was many times faster than that of the Purple Aurora Beast. Moreover, it caused no fluctuations, allowing him to move silently as if walking through the void.
This Purple Aurora Beast traversed for a long time. It had already crossed this section of the Purple Aurora Domain in the Source Sea and was now heading toward an even more distant Purple Aurora Domain.
Clearly, its original habitat was very far from both the Source Sea and the Boundless Sea, which was why it had remained undiscovered for so long. It had likely migrated to the vicinity recently, and combined with the tendency of some Purple Aurora Beasts to wander, it had finally been spotted.
After a long time, the Purple Aurora Beast's speed began to decrease.
On this day.
Ye Tian's Copy Talent sensed other Purple Aurora Beasts.
"Copy!"
"Copy!"
"Copy!"
Ye Tian continuously copied the Time Talents of these Purple Aurora Beasts, his heart filled with immense excitement. The number of Purple Aurora Beasts was actually quite large; he definitely had hope of fusing a Legendary Ancestral Talent-level Time Talent.
Just as Ye Tian was following that Purple Aurora Beast and was about to approach the Purple Aurora Beasts' nest, a terrifying aura crossed the void.
He had been discovered!
"Flee!"
Ye Tian made a prompt decision to escape.
Although each of his copied bodies could now serve as his main body—meaning if one copied body obtained a talent, the others could replace their own talents to effectively acquire them all—he still did not want this particular copied body to perish. He did not want to develop a habit of disregarding death; if a day came when it became difficult for his Copy Talent to create new bodies, such a habit would be his undoing.
Therefore, if possible, he did not wish for any of his copied bodies to fall.
Boom!!!!
A purple light shot out from the extreme distance, piercing through time and space and pulverizing all matter. Even the Purple Aurora Domain was split open, creating a path of light.
The power of this attack was already very close to that of a Half-step Legendary Realm Lord. The opponent was likely an existence that could be called Invincible among Transcendent Realm Legendary Lords.
"Myriad Life Eternal Dao!"
"Gray Mist Eternal Dao!"
Various Eternal Daos were applied to Ye Tian's body, and various Legendary-grade defensive Eternal Techniques were also instantly activated.
But even so, Ye Tian knew he would certainly be heavily injured by a single strike, making it difficult to escape the opponent's pursuit.
Thus, Ye Tian activated an Eternal Supreme Treasure Fragment—the Azure Void Bell Fragment.
This kind of Eternal Supreme Treasure Fragment was no longer of much help to Ye Tian, and he had almost forgotten about it. However, in this life-and-death crisis, he remembered it, and this copied body happened to be carrying the treasure.
But activating the Azure Void Bell Fragment required time, so Ye Tian needed to withstand the opponent's strike with his own strength.
Puchi!
Under the purple light, Ye Tian's Eternal Daos were pulverized, and his many defensive Eternal Techniques were torn apart as the light pierced directly through his body.
At this moment, Ye Tian was heavily injured. After all, the opponent was an existence nearly Invincible among Transcendent Realm Legendary Lords, while his strength was at most comparable to a powerful Transcendent Realm Legendary Lord. The gap in strength was still significant.
However, during the time he endured that strike, Ye Tian had already activated the Azure Void Bell Fragment.
Buzz!
The Azure Void Bell Fragment enveloped Ye Tian's body, tearing through the void as he departed.
In the next instant.
A massive Purple Aurora Beast appeared at Ye Tian's previous location, roaring in anger.
It was not skilled at long-distance traversal or tracking, so it had no way to catch up to Ye Tian.
...
At the other end of the Purple Aurora Domain.
Ye Tian's body emerged, and the small Azure Void Bell Fragment appeared slightly dim.
"The aura of the Azure Void Bell Fragment has grown even weaker. It will probably only last for a few more uses before it is completely destroyed!"
Ye Tian thought to himself.
But he didn't care; the item wasn't worth much anyway. It was only somewhat useful against the Purple Aurora Beasts. If he were facing other top-tier Transcendent Realm Legendary Lords or Half-step Legendary Realm Lords, the Azure Void Bell Fragment would be useless, as the opponent could hunt him down in an instant.
True Void Holy Land.
Ye Tian was searching for information regarding the Purple Aurora Beasts. After searching for a long time, he actually found some.
The True Void Holy Land had records of the Purple Aurora Beasts, but they were only brief mentions because such creatures were truly rare.
The Purple Aurora Beast was an Eternal lifeform of the Time lineage. Its birthplace would inevitably produce an extremely precious material—Time Eternal Gold. This material was so valuable that even Legendary Realm Lords had some interest in it.
As for its specific uses, Ye Tian did not know.
"Perhaps I can cooperate with the True Void Holy Land. Otherwise, as soon as I get near the Purple Aurora Beasts' nest, I'll be hunted down before I can even get close, making it impossible to copy many Time Talents. If I have the cooperation of a Half-step Legendary Realm Lord from the True Void Holy Land, perhaps I can enter the nest directly and copy the Time Talents from all the Purple Aurora Beasts."
Thus.
Ye Tian went to find Lord Wushuang.
Lord Wushuang was in secluded meditation, but she still had a clone that was not in seclusion and was instead out adventuring.
Initially, when Ye Tian sent a message to Lord Wushuang, she indicated she didn't have much time.
But when Ye Tian mentioned the Purple Aurora Beasts, Lord Wushuang appeared.
True Void Holy Land, Ye Tian's residence.
Lord Wushuang arrived and immediately asked, "Endless Lord, you found the Purple Aurora Beasts?"
Ye Tian nodded. "There is a region between the Source Sea and the Boundless Sea called the Purple Aurora Domain. Purple Aurora Beasts have appeared there now."
Lord Wushuang asked seriously, "Are you certain they are Purple Aurora Beasts? In the void regions, there are many purple creatures. Purple Aurora Beasts are very rare."
"I am certain!" Ye Tian guaranteed. "Furthermore, I found the location of their nest, but before I could get close, I was hunted by a Purple Aurora Beast that could be called Invincible among Transcendent Realm Legendary Lords. I checked the records, and the birthplace of Purple Aurora Beasts is very likely to contain Time Eternal Gold. Perhaps there is Time Eternal Gold there. Lord Wushuang, what are the uses of Time Eternal Gold?"
