Chapter 1717: Cultivating Within the Gray Mist Long River, Fifth-Tier Eternal Will!

"The Gray Mist Long River!"
Ye Tian looked at the Gray Mist Long River before him. He did not attempt to fly across but instead silently sensed the mysteries contained within it.
As time passed, Ye Tian gradually discovered a few clues within the Gray Mist Long River.
This Gray Mist Long River was different from the Gray Mist Weird Bridge. The Gray Mist Weird Bridge tested one's mastery of Innate Eternal Rules, while the Gray Mist Long River did not test Innate Eternal Rules but rather focused on the aspect of will.
When crossing the Gray Mist Long River, if one's will was weak, they would be influenced by the river and subsequently fall into it.
Therefore, this stage tested the strength of one's willpower.
The wills of those Domain Realm Legendary Lords and Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords had actually reached the Fifth-Tier Eternal Will. The wills of Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords were incredibly powerful, and logically speaking, they should have been able to pass through the Gray Mist Long River. However, in reality, there were differences even among Fifth-Tier Eternal Wills.
Some Legendary Lords simply increased their Eternal Will blindly, using various resources to boost their willpower, but this willpower was very fragile. On the other hand, some Fourth-Tier Eternal Wills were tempered through thousands of trials; to a certain extent, the toughness of their will was comparable to those with Fifth-Tier wills.
For instance, the fact that Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords could not pass through this Gray Mist Long River undoubtedly meant there were flaws in their wills. Only by mending those flaws could they pass through the Gray Mist Long River.
Ye Tian felt that his own will was incredibly tough and stable, but since his will had not reached the Fifth-Tier, there was almost no hope of passing through the Gray Mist Long River.
For now, he had to promote his will to the Fifth-Tier Eternal Will as quickly as possible before he could pass through the Gray Mist Long River.
But how could the Eternal Will be promoted so easily? It required a long period of time.
While others were bitterly pondering how to pass through the Gray Mist Long River, Ye Tian was thinking about the purpose of the river itself.
The Gray Mist Weird Bridge served to improve Innate Eternal Rules and various Eternal Daos, so the Gray Mist Long River must also have great benefits; it wasn't just a simple checkpoint.
"Previously, at the Gray Mist Weird Bridge stage, one had to enter it to comprehend the benefits. Could it be that for this Gray Mist Long River, one also has to enter the river itself to comprehend the benefits?" Ye Tian speculated.
However, other Legendary Lords had fallen into the Gray Mist Long River before and had to pay a heavy price to break free. If they didn't come out for a long time, they would surely perish.
Even Transcendence Realm Lords didn't dare to enter the Gray Mist Long River. He was currently only a Perfected Realm Legendary Lord; could he survive inside the Gray Mist Long River?
"No, the Gray Mist Long River targets the will. If a Transcendence Realm's will is not firm, they won't be much better at resisting the attacks of the river's power than a Domain Realm Legendary Lord. My will is actually very firm. Even though it's only at the Fourth-Tier, I still have the Gray Mist Battle Armor, and since I've comprehended the Gray Mist Eternal Dao, there really is hope of persevering inside the Gray Mist Long River for a long time."
Ye Tian thought to himself.
Of course, if he tried, he would be taking a great risk. Once he truly couldn't break free, causing this replicated body to perish here, the Gray Mist Battle Armor might fall to the bottom of the Gray Mist Long River, making it very difficult to retrieve.
"Let's take a gamble!"
Ye Tian decided.
Consequently, Ye Tian charged toward the Gray Mist Long River.
"Endless Lord, be careful!"
The other Legendary Lords warned.
They saw that not long after Ye Tian flew into the Gray Mist Long River, his body uncontrollably plummeted into the water.
Soon, his body was submerged by the Gray Mist Long River.
Time passed bit by bit, and Ye Tian did not emerge from the Gray Mist Long River.
At this moment, a Transcendence Realm Lord sighed, "I'm afraid the Endless Lord's avatar has perished in the Gray Mist Long River. Perhaps the benefits he obtained on the Gray Mist Weird Bridge have also fallen into the river. This loss is far too great!"
"The Endless Lord was too impulsive. Even Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords find it difficult to charge through the Gray Mist Long River. As a mere Perfected Realm Legendary Lord, he shouldn't have tried to cross; it would have been fine to just give up!"
What they didn't know was that Ye Tian's replicated body had not perished but was instead persevering beneath the Gray Mist Long River.
It was just that the Gray Mist Long River imprisoned the wills of Legendary Lords, making it difficult for their wills to investigate beneath the surface; thus, they were unclear about Ye Tian's situation.
------------------
Wave after wave of power invaded Ye Tian's body, battering his will and physical form.
At this time, Ye Tian's Gray Mist Eternal Dao, in coordination with the Gray Mist Battle Armor, took effect, allowing him to persevere here.
Under the impact of the Gray Mist River's power, Ye Tian was able to discover the weak points of his will. The Gray Mist Long River's repeated battering of his will was equivalent to tempering and hammering his will at every moment.
As long as he could persevere, his will would improve at a terrifying speed. Furthermore, this improvement was not superficial but a solid advancement, as firm as if it had been hammered and tempered hundreds of millions of times.
"So this is the true benefit of the Gray Mist Long River. The mysterious powerhouse who built the Gray Mist Weird Bridge and the Gray Mist Long River must be an Eternal Controller, right?"
Ye Tian speculated.
As time passed, Ye Tian gradually found it difficult to hold on. If it were anyone else, they would likely have to find a way to leave the Gray Mist Long River and then slowly recover from their injuries before entering the river to cultivate again once they were healed.
But Ye Tian didn't need to do that. He directly created a complete replicated body and immediately transferred the Gray Mist Battle Armor to the newly replicated body. In this way, he could continue to persevere for a long time.
Just like that, Ye Tian worked hard to temper his will within the Gray Mist Long River.
As time passed, his will not only grew stronger but also became increasingly pure and stable.
He was also opening up one Will World after another. These Will Worlds surrounded the Will Source Sea, allowing his will to obtain a certain degree of improvement.
Finally, after nearly ten thousand Eternal Years of bitter cultivation, Ye Tian's will reached the limit of the Fourth-Tier.
On this day.
Ye Tian's will welcomed a breakthrough.
Boom!!!!
The Fourth-Tier Eternal Will was transforming into the Fifth-Tier Eternal Will. This transformation was a truly massive metamorphosis; his willpower soared continuously, and even his Will Source Sea was expanding.
After a long time, the transformation finally ended. Ye Tian's willpower had increased nearly a hundredfold in terms of quantity, and every bit of his will had become even firmer, purer, and more powerful.
After his will transformed into the Fifth-Tier, combined with the Gray Mist Battle Armor, Ye Tian felt that he could easily cross the Gray Mist Long River.
"It's time to leave the Gray Mist Long River!"
Ye Tian thought to himself.
However, he would leave one replicated body behind to continue tempering his will at this stage, while another replicated body would pass through the Gray Mist Long River to the next stage.
