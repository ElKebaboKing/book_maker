Chapter 1722: Leaving the Gray Mist Statue

On this day.
Ye Tian finally completed his breakthrough. His Hunyuan Dao was the first to advance to the Perfected stage, becoming Ye Tian's first Eternal Dao at the Perfected stage.
At the very least, regarding the Eternal Dao, Ye Tian began to narrow the gap between himself and other top Domain Realm Legendary Lords.
After several more Eternal Years passed, Ye Tian's Wansheng Eternal Dao also broke through to the Perfected stage.
Afterward, Ye Tian began to specialize in the Gray Mist Eternal Dao.
His goal was to cultivate the Gray Mist Eternal Dao to the Major stage within the Gray Mist Statue. If he did that, his strength would also receive an improvement.
In the blink of an eye, another several hundred thousand Eternal Years passed.
By this time, after entering the River of Time countless times, Ye Tian's mastery of time through the River of Time had reached a very profound level. He could even resist the power of the River of Time for several seconds, ensuring that he would not be regressed by time within those few seconds.
This was already a remarkable improvement.
"I've broken through; the Gray Mist Eternal Dao has reached the Major stage."
Ye Tian was very excited.
A Gray Mist Eternal Dao at the Major stage was many times more powerful than an ordinary Eternal Dao at the Perfected stage, almost comparable to an ordinary Legendary Eternal Dao.
However, the difficulty of raising the Gray Mist Eternal Dao to the Perfected stage was too great.
Therefore, Ye Tian did not expect to be able to cultivate the Gray Mist Eternal Dao to the Perfected stage within the Gray Mist Statue.
Subsequently, Ye Tian meditated as much as possible. He would take whatever benefits he could get and no longer cared about obtaining more.
Thus, time flowed by.
At this point, fewer and fewer Legendary Lords remained inside the Gray Mist Statue.
Some Legendary Lords had fallen at various trials, while others had left voluntarily, unwilling to take risks. If they fell, the treasures they had obtained would be lost.
However, the Half-step Legendary Realm Lords did not leave. They entered the River of Time again and again. For them, this was a land of immense opportunity. It wasn't for the rewards, but simply to gain more mastery over time.
After the Gray Mist Statue appeared in the Source Sea, the time it would remain was limited. Now, the deadline was drawing closer and closer.
...
Outside the Gray Mist Statue.
Lord Longgu opened his eyes, sensing the vibrations of the Gray Mist Statue.
"In another few tens of thousands of Eternal Years, the Gray Mist Statue will flee. I wonder if I can track its traces this time?"
Lord Longgu murmured.
In the blink of an eye, tens of thousands of Eternal Years passed.
On this day, one Legendary Lord after another was kicked out of the Gray Mist Statue, and Ye Tian was forced to move out of the Gray Mist Statue as well.
At the moment he was moved out, Ye Tian annihilated his duplicate bodies, leaving only one duplicate body.
Whoosh whoosh whoosh!!!
One by one, the Legendary Lords of the Zhenwu Sacred Ground appeared in the void of the Source Sea.
Ye Tian was also here, his gaze fixed on that Gray Mist Statue.
Suddenly.
The Gray Mist Statue moved, and the surrounding space-time began to become unstable.
Boom!!!
The River of Time appeared, and the Gray Mist Statue vanished into the River of Time.
"So the Gray Mist Statue leaves by fleeing through the River of Time. I thought it would leave by tearing through space!"
Ye Tian thought to himself.
By fleeing through the River of Time in the Void Area, even those in the Half-step Legendary Realm had no way to track it.
As for Legendary Realm Lords, they simply could not catch up to the Gray Mist Statue within the River of Time.
Whoosh whoosh whoosh!!!
One by one, Legendary Realm Lords appeared and entered the River of Time alone to track the Gray Mist Statue.
But soon, these Legendary Realm Lords returned one by one, their faces showing disappointment. Clearly, they had not caught up to the Gray Mist Statue.
"What a pity!"
Lord Longgu lamented.
If they could catch up to the Gray Mist Statue, they could catch up to it every time in the future, which would be equivalent to completely monopolizing the Gray Mist Statue. However, this attempt failed again. The speed of the Gray Mist Statue within the River of Time was too fast, and any tracking method would be erased by the River of Time.
Therefore, he could not catch up, and the same was true for the other Legendary Realm Lords.
These Legendary Realm Lords did not stay in the Source Sea; they turned and left the Source Sea.
The Legendary Lords of the Zhenwu Sacred Ground also followed Lord Longgu back to the Zhenwu Sacred Ground in the Boundless Sea. After all, the Gray Mist Statue had already fled, so there was no point in staying here any longer.
Only after these Legendary Lords and Legendary Realm Lords had left did Lord Shengfeng and Lord Binghan of the Source Sea Space-Time Territory dare to speak.
"Lord Endless, thank you for this time!"
Lord Binghan said.
"How were your harvests?" Ye Tian asked.
Lord Binghan said, "No matter what I did, I couldn't pass the Gray Mist Eerie Bridge. Although I didn't obtain any treasures, by meditating within the Gray Mist Eerie Bridge, I gradually understood the mysteries of Will and Innate Eternal Rules. If I return and go into seclusion for a period of time, breaking through to become an Initial Realm Legendary Lord should not be a problem."
For Lord Binghan, becoming an Initial Realm Legendary Lord was already her greatest desire.
"I also had some harvests, and my strength has improved quite a bit!"
Lord Shengfeng said.
Soon, Lord Binghan and Lord Shengfeng took their leave and went back into seclusion.
Ye Tian also returned to the Dark Forbidden Domain. He prepared to study the reward he had obtained from the Gray Mist Statue—the Supreme Legendary Body biological blood tissue.
Inside the Gray Mist Statue, he hadn't dared to study it.
But now that he was out, he could study it with peace of mind.
Ye Tian mainly wanted to perceive the body structure and mysteries of the Supreme Legendary Body. Even if it was just some blood tissue, it contained the various Legendary Eternal Daos of that Supreme Legendary Realm creature.
Therefore, such a portion of blood tissue, which was still full of vitality, was of unimaginable value.
He didn't know how the Eternal Controller had done it. Without this bottle, logically speaking, such a Legendary Realm Lord creature could definitely use this blood tissue to heal and recover to its peak state; it would be impossible for it to fall and die.
"If Legendary Realm Lords found out about such a portion of blood tissue, I'm afraid they would come to snatch it from me."
Ye Tian murmured.
However, he would not expose this treasure.
This would be of immense help to him in condensing a Supreme Legendary Body in the future, and this portion of blood tissue could also help him perceive various Eternal Daos.
As time slowly passed, Ye Tian gradually became immersed in this blood tissue. Every drop of its blood was an infinite world, containing endless rules and various Eternal Daos.
After meditating for a long time, Ye Tian discovered that the owner of this blood tissue had likely completely mastered space and time.
Completely mastering time or completely mastering space allowed one to become a Legendary Realm Lord. If one completely mastered both, then they had reached the Peak stage among Legendary Realm Lords. The next step would be to completely master the fusion of space-time, transcending the timeline and spatial dimensions to become an Eternal Controller who stood above space-time.
"Although this portion of blood tissue is very small, it is of too much help to me. I have hope of using it to cultivate the Gray Mist Eternal Dao to the Perfected stage."
Ye Tian realized.
