Chapter 1740: The Azure Palace is Exposed, Being Targeted!

After refining the Ruyi Void Sky Shuttle, Ye Tian began to organize the other treasures he had obtained from Lord Yanglie's tomb.
Many of these treasures were also of great help to him, such as some that significantly aided in improving his Will Rule World.
Meanwhile.
In the inner area of Lord Yanglie's tomb, a Transcendent Realm Legendary Lord had crossed the time trap.
This Transcendent Realm Legendary Lord was a legendary lord from the Ancient Domain Sea, the number one Transcendent Realm Legendary Lord of the White River Sacred Land—Yuan Sheng Lord.
Yuan Sheng Lord's strength was nearly invincible among Transcendent Realm Legendary Lords, but he hadn't come here immediately. He hadn't purchased a slot; instead, he became interested after the news of Lord Yanglie's tomb spread and came later.
He knew that going from the outer area to the inner area required passing through a time trap, and only by crossing it could one enter the inner area.
Thus, he came.
Fortunately, at the cost of being heavily injured, he successfully broke in.
Yuan Sheng Lord also saw Lord Yanglie's Stele. After a moment of hesitation, he walked into the nearby Azure Palace.
But when he entered the Azure Palace, he was instantly dumbfounded.
"Where are the treasures?"
Inside the Azure Palace, not a single treasure remained.
His first thought was that Lord Yanglie had tricked him, but on second thought, it didn't seem right. There was no need for Lord Yanglie to deceive them!
Because there were currently only two possibilities.
First: the Time Merchant Guild had sent someone in advance and taken the treasures here.
Second: a Transcendent Realm Legendary Lord had arrived here and taken the treasures.
But even if someone had come, they couldn't have taken all the treasures here at once!
A person could only take three treasures; one would be informed of this upon entering the Azure Palace. Therefore, he didn't believe a single person could take all the treasures here.
Soon.
Yuan Sheng Lord leaked the news, saying that there were no more treasures inside Lord Yanglie's tomb and that they had been taken.
When this news spread, no one believed Yuan Sheng Lord. Instead, they thought he wanted to monopolize the treasures.
Consequently, they became even more frantic to break into the interior of Lord Yanglie's tomb.
After a long time, other Transcendent Realm Legendary Lords finally passed through the time trap with the help of some secret treasures. They also saw the Azure Palace and naturally discovered the empty Azure Palace.
Only then did people believe the news that the treasures inside Lord Yanglie's tomb were gone.
Yuan Sheng Lord was targeted, as some believed he did it.
Ye Tian was also targeted because some Legendary Lords had seen Ye Tian coming out from the interior of Lord Yanglie's tomb.
Of course, some also questioned the Time Merchant Guild.
The Time Merchant Guild's reputation was good, but that didn't mean every Legendary Lord or Legendary Realm Lord within the guild was trustworthy.
Zhenwu Sacred Land announced to the public that Ye Tian had only taken three items and that there weren't many items left inside to begin with.
Although this was a lie Ye Tian asked Zhenwu Sacred Land to tell, it was true that every Legendary Lord could only take three items. Some Transcendent Realm Lords even tried to study the restrictions of the Azure Palace and concluded that no one below the Legendary Realm could violate this rule.
Thus, many believed Ye Tian had only taken three items.
Perhaps there weren't many items in the Azure Palace to begin with; Ye Tian took three, Yuan Sheng Lord took three, and they just happened to run out.
If that were the case, the Time River Pearl was very likely on Ye Tian.
……
"I still can't avoid being targeted!"
Ye Tian lamented.
The outside world believed he had the Time River Pearl, and the key was that he actually did.
Of course.
A single Time River Pearl wasn't enough to make other experts invade Zhenwu Sacred Land to take the Time River Pearl from him. Others could only be envious or wait for an opportunity to strike at him.
To ensure the safety of his family, Ye Tian brought them to an Infinite Island in Zhenwu Sacred Land to cultivate.
Now, he was considered one of the high-level members of Zhenwu Sacred Land, so he had this bit of authority.
Thus, even if some expert was shameless enough to launch a surprise attack on the Source Sea Dark Forbidden Zone, he wouldn't panic.
In fact, without being certain which of Ye Tian's bodies the Time River Pearl and other treasures were on, no one dared to directly attack one of his bodies; there was no point.
Time slowly passed, and the heat of the Lord Yanglie's tomb incident gradually faded. Yuan Sheng Lord and Endless Lord Ye Tian became quite famous. Especially Ye Tian, a half-step Transcendent Realm Legendary Lord who could cross the Undying Dragon Rock Sea of Fire and the time trap before even entering the Transcendent Realm—it was simply incredible.
Therefore, Ye Tian received even more attention.
One Eternal Year after another passed. Unknowingly, three million Eternal Years had gone by.
Ye Tian's cultivation still hadn't changed, but his willpower and Will Rule World were improving. Moreover, he had raised more of his Eternal Daos to the Legendary Grade.
Additionally, his attainment in Time had reached a higher level, bringing him closer to completely mastering time.
One day, good news came from Zhenwu Sacred Land.
The number one half-step Legendary Realm Lord of Zhenwu Sacred Land, the publicly recognized strongest half-step Legendary Realm Lord—whose strength had even surpassed the former Wushuang Lord and was considered the invincible Baiheng Lord.
Baiheng Lord's strength was much greater than Wushuang Lord's. He was the most mysterious and ancient half-step Legendary Realm Lord of Zhenwu Sacred Land, and what he comprehended was precisely Time.
However, becoming a Legendary Realm Lord was too difficult. Even for Baiheng Lord, mastering time was somewhat slow, so he had never broken through.
Now, Zhenwu Sacred Land had built the Bridge of Time, so Baiheng Lord entered the Long River of Time to cultivate bitterly. Finally, he broke through the last bit of shackles and successfully mastered time.
Thus, Baiheng Lord could strike for the Legendary Realm Lord stage at any time.
It hadn't been long since Wushuang Lord became a Legendary Realm Lord, and now Zhenwu Sacred Land was about to give birth to another. This shocked and envied other forces.
"Could it be that what Wushuang Lord mentioned was Baiheng Lord's breakthrough to the Legendary Realm? No, it shouldn't be that; this has nothing to do with the Void Sea Gravel," Ye Tian thought to himself.
However, he felt that after Baiheng Lord's breakthrough, the matter Wushuang Lord spoke of would be very close.
Thus, he waited silently.
Not long after, Baiheng Lord was about to enter the Long River of Time to charge into the Legendary Realm Lord stage.
