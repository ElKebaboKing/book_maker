Chapter 1743: The Shock of the Legendary Lords!

The Legendary Lords of the Zhenwu Sacred Land were all incredibly shocked.


If someone mastered time while still in the Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord stage, that talent would be far too monstrous. Basically, these Legendary Lords only mastered time or space after becoming Half-step Legendary Lords and undergoing countless years of bitter cultivation.


For example, Lord Baiheng possessed invincible combat power, yet he also spent an unimaginable amount of time before mastering time.


Now, hearing that someone might have mastered time while in the Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord stage, he was truly shaken.


Of course, there was no jealousy. After all, he had already broken through to the Legendary Lord realm. If he had heard this while still a Half-step Legendary Lord, he might have truly felt jealous.


“No, it's not a Transcendence Realm Lord either!” Lord Wushuang added.


“Not a Half-step Legendary Lord, and not a Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord.” Lord Longgu suddenly thought of something. “Could it be that one of the Legendary Lords has mastered time after already mastering space? Is our Zhenwu Sacred Land about to give birth to a Peak Legendary Lord?”

At this thought, he became incredibly excited.


Who was it?

Everyone looked at each other; it clearly wasn't any of the Legendary Lords present.


There were still other Legendary Lords who hadn't arrived. Could it be one of them?

“Of course it's not a Legendary Lord. Even if a Legendary Lord really mastered time or space, you all would be the first to know, not a newly promoted Legendary Lord like me,” Lord Wushuang said.


“True!” The several Legendary Lords felt Lord Wushuang's words made sense. “Lord Wushuang, stop being so secretive. What exactly is the situation? Tell us,” Lord Huanghe couldn't help but say.


Buzz! The surrounding space-time was frozen to prevent other Legendary Lords from spying on them.


Of course, this couldn't stop the spying of an Eternal Controller, but an Eternal Controller wouldn't be so bored.


“It is a Half-step Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord who has mastered time,” Lord Wushuang said.


“What? A Half-step Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord mastered time?” Lord Longgu and the others looked incredulous, wondering if Lord Wushuang was joking.

…“You should be able to guess who it is,” Lord Wushuang added. Lord Longgu thought back and realized who it was, as did the other Legendary Lords.


Because in the current Zhenwu Sacred Land, there was only one such outstanding Half-step Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord. “Lord Wujin!” Lord Longgu said.


“Yes!” Lord Wushuang said.


“Lord Wujin mastered time? How is that possible? How long has he even been cultivating?” Lord Longgu said in shock. “I remember he hasn't been in our Zhenwu Sacred Land for very long. He broke through all the way from an Initial Realm Lord to the Perfected Realm, Domain Realm, and now to a Half-step Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord. If it weren't for the Gray Mist Statue incident last time, I wouldn't even have known about this Legendary Lord.” Lord Wushuang said, “I know it's hard to believe, but he indeed mastered time. You should know how much of a sensation it would cause for a Half-step Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord to master time. Therefore, I propose giving the Eternal Treasure of the Zhenwu Sacred Land that can hide and change one's cultivation to Lord Wujin.”

…“If that's the case, we can give it to him!”

“I agree as well!”

“Agreed!”

The Legendary Lords said.


“Since everyone agrees, it's decided that the Eternal Treasure will be given to him!” Lord Wushuang said. At this moment, Lord Longgu said, “It will be very difficult to completely hide the fact that Lord Wujin has mastered time unless he never takes action and stays in closed-door cultivation forever. But a Legendary Lord cannot refrain from acting forever, and we cannot restrict Lord Wujin. He will eventually be exposed.”

”

“As long as Lord Wujin's true body remains in the Zhenwu Sacred Land, we don't need to worry too much about his safety,” Lord Wushuang said.


“With Lord Wujin's aptitude, it shouldn't be difficult for him to condense a Legendary Body. Why hasn't he broken through yet?” Lord Baiheng asked.


“He aims to condense a Supreme Legendary Body,” Lord Wushuang explained.


“A Supreme Legendary Body? How could that be possible?” Lord Longgu shook his head.


Lord Wushuang said, “Lord Wujin has already obtained the Time River Pearl. If our current opportunity goes smoothly, he might also have a chance to obtain the Space Sea Sand. By then, he will only be missing the Ancient Space-Time Leaf.”


“Lord Wujin has actually already collected the Time River Pearl? Then there is indeed hope,” Lord Baiheng said.


At this moment, Lord Longgu revealed some news. “As for the Ancient Space-Time Leaf, I do know a bit of information,” Lord Longgu said.


“What? Lord Longgu, you know about the Ancient Space-Time Leaf?” Lord Wushuang was surprised.


“I learned a piece of news by chance, though I'm not sure if it's true or false,” Lord Longgu said. “The news I received is that the White-Bearded Old Man has an Ancient Space-Time Leaf in his possession.”

The White-Bearded Old Man—many Legendary Lords had heard of him. He was a Peak Legendary Lord, incredibly ancient. Rumor had it that he was even older than some Eternal Controllers, and he had even discussed the Dao over tea with more than one Eternal Controller.


However, the White-Bearded Old Man had no faction; he was a loner without even a family or race. He once had a race, but during an ancient war, his race vanished, having been caught in the crossfire and destroyed.


“The White-Bearded Old Man is a Legendary Lord with no desires or needs. Trying to obtain the Ancient Space-Time Leaf from him would be too difficult, and we have no relationship with him!” Lord Huanghe frowned.


“That is Lord Wujin's business. We will pass this information on to him; whether he can obtain the Ancient Space-Time Leaf will depend on himself,” Lord Longgu said with a smile.


True.


It was already good enough that they were sharing this news; they couldn't possibly go to any lengths to secure the Ancient Space-Time Leaf for him.


Afterward, Lord Wushuang went to the treasury of the Zhenwu Sacred Land to retrieve the Eternal Treasure, the Hundred Illusion Pearl! Soon, the Hundred Illusion Pearl was brought out, and Lord Wushuang immediately went to find Ye Tian.


…“Greetings, Lord Wushuang,” Ye Tian said respectfully.


…“Lord Wujin, this is the Eternal Treasure, the Hundred Illusion Pearl. It can change your cultivation aura, so even Legendary Lords won't be able to discern your cultivation realm. Furthermore, since your strength is already very powerful, others will only think you are a top-tier Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord,” Lord Wushuang said.


“Thank you, Lord Wushuang!” Ye Tian took the Hundred Illusion Pearl and expressed his gratitude.
