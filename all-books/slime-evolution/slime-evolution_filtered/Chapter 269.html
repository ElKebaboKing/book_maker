Chapter 269: 269 - I really need to move

The hissing noise was the only thing the man could hear at that moment; it was so overwhelming and terrifying that the constant sounds of trucks and horns blaring 24 hours a day outside the window vanished in an instant.

Lohan activated [Efficient Digestion] and forced his Mana to saturate the pores of his palm, acting exactly like the molecular processing he used in Elysium.

In the real world, his skin functioned like the membrane of a Slime, while his sweat turned into biomass, isolating and breaking down organic matter at the molecular level.

Fortunately, this was the skill he had trained most frequently in the real world, since it was what allowed him to bathe for free in that world.

When he opened his hand a few seconds later, the henchmans finger had simply vanished, leaving only a clean, cauterized void where the bone and flesh should have been.

To the mans horror, not even the blood that should have been gushing uncontrollably from a wound like that was there!

The leader let out a choked scream, his face turning pale to a grayish hue. "What... what are you?! Did you eat him?!"

"What I am doesnt matter," Lohan replied, his voice even colder. "I can start with your fingers, or I can simply wrap my hand around your head and rip out your brain while youre still screaming, just as Ive done to hundreds of other victims."

Hearing that, the mans face paled even further, realizing he had walked into the house of a fucking serial killer psychopath!

Lohan reached out toward the leaders face, causing strands of [Magical Weaving], blue and nearly invisible, to begin sprouting from his fingers, slowly wrapping around the mans neck like tiny snakes.

He applied subtle pressure, not to kill, but to make it even clearer that the man was hanging by a thread while creating the sensation that the mans very life essence was being drained by the threads, which it literally was.

The psychological torture of watching a comrade permanently lose a limb right before his eyes, combined with Lohans oppressive aura, was the final blow.

The groups leader broke down.

"STOP! ILL TALK! IT WAS OSCAR!" the man screamed as tears of sheer terror washed the dirt from his face. "Your landlord... he came looking for us last night. He was possessed, screaming that you had humiliated him and that he needed to be put in his place!"

Lohan clenched his teeth, feeling his Mana boil. He remembered the impact of the punch hed landed on Oscars solar plexus and the threat to make him "disappear."

It seems the drug-addled landlord didnt take the financial and physical humiliation well.

"He offered a good sum for the job... and I could keep whatever we found of value here." The leader continued, trembling under Lohans gaze. "He didnt want to contact the Asphyxia Gang directly so he wouldnt have to pay the management commission. He hired us on the side, through a third party... said you were just a skinny kid who probably got lucky in Elysium and needed to be processed."

Lohan loosened his grip on the mans neck, feeling a cold satisfaction. Oscar had not only tried to extort him, but had now sent mercenaries to steal his Elysium Helmet and deliver it to clandestine laboratories.

I see. So the rat decided the handout wasnt enough. Lohan thought, feeling his long, dense muscles relax slightly. He looked at the leader with indifference. "Very well, you are no longer of any use."

Then, with a flick of his hand, Lohan increased the force with which the filaments clung to the mans face, and with a swift open-handed strike, Lohan struck the leaders temple.

The mans vision darkened instantly, leaving only his face dominated by shock and disbelief that even after telling the truth, he would likely still not make it out alive.

Silence returned to the apartment, broken only by the electric hum of a hanging lamp and the hiss of Lohans breathing.

Five men lay scattered across his room, unconscious.

Lohan didnt want to go to the trouble of killing these men, because of the complications that might arise, but considering how much of his own power he had had to display in this fight, he knew there was no other choice.

Unfortunately, Memory Resonance only allowed him to read the memories of creatures he had killed and digested, not allowing him to alter the memories of creatures still alive, so to ensure no one knew of his power and potential, Lohan would have to kill those men.

Fortunately, Oscar hadnt hired the gang directly, so it was possible that no one besides Oscar knew that this group had come to his house that night to cause trouble.

So if they vanished from the face of the earth, only Oscar would know who the likely culprit was.

In such an advanced world, it would be difficult for others to make corpses vanish in the city center, but for Lohan, this was an easy job, time-consuming, but easy.

As long as he had enough time, he could digest those corpses himself, completely erasing the evidence of the crime.

Maybe there might even be some unexpected benefits... Lohan thought excitedly, since this would be his first time testing the digestion of other living beings in the real world.

As for the guilt of killing other people, he had already lost that when he killed in Elysium. Sure, if they were kind and innocent people, Lohan didnt know if he could take the final step and end those lives, but for people who wanted to turn him into a lab rat and steal everything of value he had, there was no mercy.

Lohan looked at his own shoulder.

The cut was no longer bleeding; the skin was closing at a much faster rate than is common in human biology, leaving only a pink line that would disappear in a few hours.

He felt an incredible sense of satisfaction with the evolution he had undergone in the real world as well.

"I think Isabella was right... I really do need a new apartment," Lohan muttered, knowing that after today, he would no longer have any peace in this place. "But before that, I have one last debt to collect from a certain landlord."
