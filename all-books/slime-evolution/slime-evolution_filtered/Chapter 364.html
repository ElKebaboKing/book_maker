The Silver Lotus group started running after the fairy, entering the shelter of Mythlorien’s colossal trees.

As they moved away from Aethelgard’s zone of influence and dove into the magical density of the deep forest, the tension in their shoulders began to ease.

They kept glancing at Pip, who was now back to his cute self without the black armor, and thought about the stark difference between his current appearance and how he looked without the armor.

After a few kilometers of nonstop running, Pip signaled for them to stop at a small rocky plateau covered in bluish moss.

The fairy landed gently in front of the group, and Lohan decided that was the ideal moment for a full explanation.

He completely removed the slime armor from Pip, causing the biomass to condense into a small, glowing blue sphere that rolled along beside the fairy.

The shock in the Silver Lotus group was immediate and palpable.

Jenny let out a gasp, lowering her axe, while Lotus’s blue eyes went wide with utter disbelief.

Now free from having to worry so much about their own safety, with Pip standing so close to them, they could finally take a closer look at that little boy’s appearance.

Before them stood two tiny creatures, about the size of a human palm, one with short brown hair and translucent butterfly wings that gleamed in the evening light. Pip looked like the very picture of cuteness and fragility, the exact opposite of the armored god of war who had just torn apart a band of Apex mercenaries.

However, the real shock was yet to come.

[Halon: Welcome to Astralis, everyone. Sorry for not greeting you all in person at the gate, but to make sure you’d stay safe, we only sent Pip and a clone of mine.]
