Because he had to deal with the tedious bureaucracy involving humans in this world, Isaac didn’t have much time to spend killing monsters to level up.

Just as he shouldn’t assign anyone to deal with humans, since he knew how important first impressions were when dealing with people, if he had someone interact with the nobles, the prestige gained from his ideas and leadership would largely be shared with that person.

If it were someone he trusted, like his closest staff in the real world, Isaac wouldn’t worry, but the people around him were hired in his Vila Novata, so there was no one worthy of that much trust.

On the other hand, he also couldn’t let his level fall behind; even if he wasn’t the most powerful in the Guild, he couldn’t be weak enough for that to mean a major vulnerability.

The solution he found for this was to have his soldiers go off and kill monsters on their own while he dealt with the nobles; then, once they leveled up, they would use that increased strength to take Isaac out hunting and let him claim most of the experience by eliminating weaker monsters.

Each soldier would hunt a monster and bring it to Isaac, who would kill the monster and split the experience exclusively with that soldier; then another soldier would bring another monster that Isaac would kill and receive half of that monster’s experience, while that soldier would receive only half of the experience from that single creature.

That way, with 4 soldiers hunting, each would receive X experience per monster, while Isaac would receive 4X that experience, allowing him to reach their level much faster than if he were leveling up alone.

Sure, this slightly slowed down the soldiers’ leveling speed, but for Isaac, that wasn’t very important. These soldiers weren’t the special forces mercenaries he’d trained in the real world to act as the true vanguard warriors in Elysium; those would be Isaac’s warriors to advance through Elysium.

The soldiers he had now were just veterans he’d hired in the Novice Village to do power leveling for him.
