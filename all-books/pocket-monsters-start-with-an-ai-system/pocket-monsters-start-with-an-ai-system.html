Chapter 1: A miserable start

Chapter 1 A miserable beginning

“Sandslash, push harder, hold on! Just five more laps and you can rest!”

In a small courtyard not far from the outskirts of Viridian Forest, Qin Yang was shouting, cheering on the Sandslash training in the yard.

At this moment, Sandslash was drenched in sweat, large beads of sweat dripping down its fur, its chest heaving violently, panting heavily.

Yet, its four limbs still moved powerfully, each step stomping heavily on the ground, kicking up a circle of dust, its eyes showing a stubbornness, with no intention of backing down.

Qin Yang stood by, his eyes fixed on Sandslash, watching it train so hard, his heart filled with satisfaction, a smile unconsciously curving his lips.

But as he watched, his gaze gradually became somewhat distant, his thoughts like a kite with a broken string, drifting back to the past.

Qin Yang didn't know how it happened, but he somehow, inexplicably, was reborn into the Pokémon world.

Just like in those novels, he followed the law of transmigration across worlds, his background quite tragic; his parents passed away when he was young, leaving him an orphan.

When he was six, he was taken in by an orphanage in Viridian City.

This Viridian City was no ordinary place; it was the main base of Team Rocket' boss, Giovanni.

Logically, this place should have been very dangerous, but at that time, Giovanni hadn't revealed his identity yet and secretly put a lot of effort into maintaining Viridian City's public order, managing the city very stably.

Those low-level Team Rocket members were clueless, completely unaware that their boss was right there.

The higher-ups knew, so Viridian City, as Rocket Team Headquarters, surprisingly didn't suffer much harm.

Because of this, the orphanage where Qin Yang resided had an unusually good treatment.

Boss Giovanni didn't care about the small amount of money saved by cutting orphanage funds, so much so that benefits not enjoyed by orphanages in some legitimate League cities were all available here.

This also made Qin Yang realize that the League side was somewhat corrupt, and he secretly clicked his tongue in amazement.

Qin Yang, having a protagonist template, naturally also had a cheat.

When he first discovered he had come to the Pokémon world, he was so excited he jumped three feet high.

He thought to himself, 'Now I can personally raise a group of majestic and adorable Pokémon, how exciting!'

But after staying in the orphanage for a long time, he realized that becoming a proper Pokémon Trainer was too difficult.

If one only wanted to raise a Grass-type Pokémon as a companion, that would be simple; Grass-types eat little and are easy to raise, keeping a few for fun is very easy.

But if one truly wanted to make a name for themselves on the Trainer path, first and foremost, they needed money; without money, it was simply impossible to move forward.

Qin Yang, an orphan, relied entirely on the orphanage for support, so where would he get the money?

Just as he was worrying about this, the system popped out with a “whoosh.”

Qin Yang's hands trembled with excitement at the time, thinking, 'I'm saved now, will I be able to become a Trainer smoothly?'

But when he carefully studied the system's introduction, a basin of cold water splashed over him, chilling him to the bone, and all his expectations instantly vanished.

It turned out to be an AI system.

To say it was useless, it had some use; to say it was useful, it wasn't as divine as Qin Yang had imagined.

He used to read novels where protagonists got a sign-in system, and wow, legendary Pokémon and Pseudo-Legendarys were at their fingertips, the newbie gift packages were so rich they could blind you, and in the blink of an eye, they became Elite Four Level powerhouses,

dominating the world, tearing Ash apart, kicking Steven, with Mei on his left arm and Cynthia on his right, infinitely glorious.

Qin Yang stared longingly at his system, feeling incredibly depressed.

This AI system lost a lot of data when it traveled through time and space, and now it was like a blank slate, lacking everything.

However, the system also had a few impressive functions.

One was the knowledge recording function: as long as Qin Yang read a book or material, the system could immediately record it, like a super brain, available for him to retrieve at any time, giving him the ability to never forget anything he read.

The second was the information organization and analysis function: no matter how messy the data, the system could sort it out neatly and provide Qin Yang with the desired results.

The third was the scanning function: how powerful this function was depended on the knowledge reserves collected beforehand; the richer the database, the more accurate and detailed the scanning results would be.

But this made Qin Yang difficult again; as a child from an orphanage, where would he get so much knowledge for the system to collect?

He was filled with regret, thinking, 'If only I had been reborn in Pallet Town, where Professor Professor Oak is; getting close to the Professor would allow me to obtain a large amount of research data,'

'my system would surely grow by leaps and bounds, and by then, wouldn't I soar to the sky?'

Unfortunately, there's no potion for regret in this world.

Qin Yang weighed his options and felt that he was too young; without the orphanage's protection, venturing out would be like a lamb entering a tiger's den, with more bad than good outcomes.

This Pokémon world was not as beautiful as depicted in the anime; humans and Pokémon often fought to the death,

and every year, many people were attacked by wild Pokémon, with a considerable number losing their lives.

Qin Yang's little joy, with a “poof,” extinguished by more than half.

However, he quickly cheered up, thinking that having a system was better than not having one; he was already so miserable, not having a system would be truly over, and a glimmer of hope was better than despair.

Thus, Qin Yang lived a peaceful life in the orphanage.

In a blink of an eye, he was eight years old, and the orphanage arranged for age-appropriate children to go to school.

The school system here was three years of primary school, three years of middle school, and three years of university. The orphanage only covered the tuition fees for primary and middle school; university education had to be self-funded.

Qin Yang later learned about this and realized that he owed Boss Giovanni a considerable favor.

Without those six years of study, the system would not have been able to collect so much Pokémon data and knowledge.

Although most of the knowledge in the school library and database was basic content suitable for elementary and middle school students, quite common, thankfully, the school allowed free reading.

This greatly helped Qin Yang; the knowledge within was vast, and if he had to read it all himself, it would take him ten years.

Fortunately, the AI system came into play at this time. For books, Qin Yang would flip through them once, and the system would collect the knowledge, then analyze and organize it;

as for knowledge on computers, it was strange, as long as there was no firewall blocking it, the system could directly scan and acquire it.

With the help of the system, from elementary school to middle school, Qin Yang spent most of his time either working odd jobs to earn money or diligently studying in the school library.

By the time he graduated from middle school at fourteen, the AI system had already collected a large amount of data and had some practical utility.

Relying on the system, Qin Yang also became a junior Breeder and a junior potion maker; if not for resource limitations, his level might have been even higher.

However, Qin Yang didn't want to be too conspicuous and didn't go for these two certificates.

An orphan, with such abilities at a young age, could easily attract trouble and be targeted by ill-intentioned people, which would be dangerous. So he preferred to be poorer than to be coveted.

Although he had no certificates, his abilities were real; working as an assistant in breeding centers and potion shops, he earned more than other employees.

This became his confidence to leave the orphanage; otherwise, without any skills, he would starve on the streets.

Chapter 2: System Limitations

Chapter 2 System Limitations

In this world of Pokémon, it's not like in the anime where you can embark on a journey gloriously at ten years old, receiving a Pokémon to roam the world.

This is a genuine real world, and the rules are very strict.

To receive a Pokémon and travel, you must be at least fifteen years old; at most, you can be one year early, fourteen years old, to own your own Pokémon, but you are not allowed to travel, and the Alliance keeps a very close eye on this.

Why? Because even a Pokémon with a dozen levels of strength is a considerable threat to ordinary humans.

If it falls into the hands of an immature child, who knows what trouble they might cause, with unimaginable consequences.

However, there are always exceptions.

Children from prominent families have privileges from a young age, with their families arranging for Pokémon to accompany them as they grow up.

Although they aren't officially captured at first, the child and the Pokémon live together day and night, and when they grow up and complete the procedures, they are already far ahead of others when they set out.

This is also a key reason for the strong family influence within the Alliance; ordinary commoners are at a vast disadvantage compared to them, an unbridgeable gap.

Qin Yang was only fourteen when he graduated from school and left the orphanage.

Although he had saved some money, this meager saving was not enough to cover his own living expenses and also raise a Pokémon.

In his helplessness, he could only temporarily abandon this idea.

Actually, Qin Yang wasn't without ideas; he had considered that if he made and sold basic Pokéblocks and basic potion, he would surely make a quick fortune.

Unfortunately, he lacked the relevant certificates, so the items he made couldn't enter Alliance stores at all.

The Underground Black Market? He didn't even dare to think about it; he didn't even have a single Pokémon, so rushing in would be like a lamb entering a tiger's den, seeking his own demise.

Just like that, half a year passed.

During these six months, thanks to Qin Yang's system, working at the breeding center and pharmacy became increasingly smooth, and his savings gradually grew to fifty or sixty thousand.

Qin Yang calculated in his mind and had an idea: he planned to use this money to buy a Pokémon, and he was quite confident that he could find a good deal and acquire one with good potential.

Why? Because as the system collected more and more information, its scanning function also became stronger and stronger; now, for common Pokémon, the system could scan and clearly display their strength, potential, and detailed training and breeding methods.

But the system also had its shortcomings; limited by previous data, it could currently only provide breeding methods up to the Gym-Level stage.

For Gym-Level-level and above, it was completely in the dark, knowing very little.

Although he could slowly explore, that speed was like a snail crawling, far too slow.

Qin Yang knew this, but there was nothing he could do; most of the knowledge in the school library was basic, and breeding methods only went up to Superior-level at most.

Anything beyond that, like Gym-Level-level knowledge, was only available within prestigious universities, old Gyms, and major families, or could be exchanged for large amounts of resources from the Alliance, and it was rarely shared externally.

After all, in the eyes of a Trainer, training methods are their most prized possessions.

In the Underground Black Market, ordinary Gym-Level-level training insights could sell for at least a million, and Elite Four Level ones were astronomical, starting at tens of millions, and almost impossible to find.

Any Elite Four Level Trainer who was doing reasonably well wouldn't be foolish enough to reveal their trump card and sell it to others; that would just be asking for trouble.

Qin Yang looked at the fifty thousand yuan in his hand, his brows furrowed into a knot, filled with worry. He muttered to himself:

“I can't just buy one with ordinary potential, can I? What future would it have then? Its prospects would be bleak.”

Perhaps fate was kind to him, or perhaps it was purely good luck.

One day, on his way home from work, Qin Yang saw a Sandshrew lying unconscious on the ground.

At first, he didn't pay much attention; he had seen this situation many times.

He was about to walk away; in this day and age, who could guarantee that a fainted Pokémon wouldn't go mad and attack people once it woke up? Such things had happened before.

But just then, Qin Yang had a sudden impulse, and as if by some strange twist of fate, he used the system to scan the Sandshrew.

With that scan, his eyes widened instantly, and he almost jumped up in excitement; the system showed that this Sandshrew's potential was Superior-level!

It's important to know that an ordinary Sandshrew sells for only thirty to fifty thousand in a breeding center, while a Superior-level one would cost at least two hundred thousand; at that price, the money in Qin Yang's pocket wasn't even enough for a fraction.

Qin Yang stared longingly at the Sandshrew, practically drooling with desire.

Finally, gritting his teeth, he took it home, fed it, and meticulously cared for it, eventually waking the Sandshrew.

When Sandshrew woke up, seeing Qin Yang's devoted care, it felt grateful and also wanted a stable home; the two hit it off, and Sandshrew became Qin Yang's first Pokémon.

After getting Sandshrew, Qin Yang worked for a while longer.

But slowly, he realized that if he kept working like this, when would he ever become a true Trainer? It was not a long-term solution at all.

So, he stomped his foot, quit his job, and went home to focus solely on training Sandshrew, planning to train for a while before venturing into the outskirts of Viridian Forest.

In this world, prices for general daily necessities are quite affordable; food and such are not expensive, enough for ordinary people to live on.

However, anything related to Pokémon immediately skyrockets in price.

Only Poké Balls are considered a benefit provided by the Alliance to ordinary people; a common Poké Ball costs only one or two hundred, which is very cheap.

But beneficial berries and medicinal herbs for Pokémon, any single type, can cost hundreds to thousands, ridiculously expensive.

Why did Qin Yang want to take risks?

He had done the math; relying on his meager income from working, it was difficult to even feed Sandshrew.

Although Sandshrew ate Pokéblocks made by Qin Yang himself and carefully prepared Pokémon food, which didn't require extra money to buy, the raw material costs were not low.

Sandshrew's daily food expenses plus the nutrition needed for training would cost three to five hundred, and Qin Yang only earned over ten thousand a month from his work.

Just eating would make him poor.

If he didn't take risks, he and Sandshrew would eventually starve.

Of course, if Sandshrew were only raised as a regular pet, the cost would be ten times less, thirty to fifty a day would be enough, eating the same food as humans.

But in that case, Sandshrew's strength would basically stagnate, and even if there was some improvement, it would be extremely slow; this Pokémon would essentially be useless.

PS: Pokémon potential and strength levels are divided into: Ordinary, Superior, Gym-Level, Quasi-Elite Four Level, Elite Four Level, Champion-level, and Champion-level. Ordinary strength is only below Level 30, Superior is 31-50, Gym-Level is 51-60, Quasi-Elite Four Level is 61-70, Elite Four Level is 71-80, Champion-level is 81-90, and Champion-level is 91-100.

Chapter 3: Life and death

Chapter 3: Life and Death

In the days that followed, Qin Yang took out a portion of his hard-earned money to buy raw materials and make Pokémon food and Pokéblocks for Sandshrew.

Every day, he patiently accompanied Sandshrew in training, his eyes filled with focus and anticipation.

"Sandshrew, use 'Scratch' to attack this target!"

Qin Yang pointed at the training dummy ahead, his voice firm and strong. Hearing this, Sandshrew's eyes flashed with determination, and he quickly rushed towards the dummy, swinging his small claws with force, stirring up a gust of wind.

"Good, just like that, keep it up!"

Qin Yang's face revealed a relieved smile as he encouraged him.

As time passed, with ample food and careful training, Sandshrew's body gradually recovered, and his strength steadily improved.

His fur became smooth and shiny, his eyes filled with vitality, and his movements became more agile.

Qin Yang scanned him with the system again and found that Sandshrew had reached Level 15.

Watching Sandshrew's growth, Qin Yang felt a deep sense of accomplishment, but he knew that his financial situation no longer allowed him to live off his savings.

So, he gritted his teeth and decided to take Sandshrew to adventure in the outskirts of Viridian Forest. Although he knew this decision was full of danger, for the sake of the future, he had no choice but to take the risk.

Qin Yang stood at the edge of Viridian Forest, taking a deep breath. The air was filled with the fresh scent of trees and the fragrance of earth, mixed with a hint of mystery—the unique scent emitted by numerous wild Pokémon.

He felt both nervous and excited, filled with anticipation for the adventure that was about to begin.

"Sandshrew, let's go!"

Qin Yang clenched his fist, his eyes firm, and led Sandshrew into the forest.

The initial adventure went smoothly. Relying on the system's scanning function, Qin Yang was able to know the information of surrounding Pokémon in advance, cleverly avoiding many dangers.

They traversed the forest and discovered many berries and medicinal herbs.

Qin Yang excitedly picked them one by one, his eyes twinkling with joy, muttering repeatedly:

"This time, it's truly a bountiful Harvest!"

Sandshrew also ran happily around him, seemingly able to sense his master's joy.

In the next two or three months, their Harvest grew. Qin Yang's pockets gradually swelled, and after deducting his and Sandshrew's expenses, he had accumulated over a hundred thousand.

This speed of earning money made Qin Yang deeply appreciate the charm of adventure and understand why so many people longed to become Trainers.

During this time, Sandshrew, due to good food and careful training from Qin Yang, saw his strength rocket, directly increasing to Level 22, almost ready to evolve.

His body became even more robust, and his eyes revealed a sense of confidence and majesty.

However, an act of greed plunged them into a huge crisis.

That day, Qin Yang's gaze fell on a Beedrill's hive, and a bold idea suddenly surged in his mind—to obtain Beedrill honey.

A hint of hesitation flashed in his eyes, but it was quickly replaced by greed.

"Sandshrew, let's try to get some honey. It shouldn't be a problem."

Qin Yang said softly, his voice tinged with nervousness and excitement. Sandshrew looked at him with some worry but still obeyed his command.

They quietly approached the hive. Just as Qin Yang successfully obtained some honey and was secretly pleased, an accident occurred.

Perhaps their movements were a bit too loud, or perhaps the Beedrills' senses were too keen; a group of Beedrills discovered their actions.

In an instant, more than twenty Beedrills swarmed towards them like a dark cloud, their buzzing echoing throughout the forest, like the horn of death.

Sandshrew immediately stepped forward, blocking Qin Yang. He opened his mouth, revealing sharp teeth, and let out a low growl, trying to scare away the Beedrills.

But the Beedrills were fearless; they rushed towards Sandshrew like a tide. Sandshrew swung his claws, engaging in a fierce battle with the Beedrills.

Every one of his attacks carried immense power, sending several Beedrills flying.

However, there were simply too many Beedrills. They continuously circled and stung Sandshrew. Sandshrew's body was quickly covered in wounds, blood seeping out and staining his fur.

Qin Yang was frantic on the side. He tried to drive away the Beedrills with stones and branches, but to little effect.

Suddenly, several Beedrills broke through Sandshrew's defense and lunged at Qin Yang.

Qin Yang's eyes widened in horror, and he frantically dodged, but he was still stung several times by the Beedrills.

A sharp pain came, and he couldn't help but scream loudly:

"Ah! It hurts so much!"

His face instantly turned pale, and sweat continuously beaded on his forehead.

"No, if this continues, we'll both die here!"

Qin Yang endured the pain, his heart filled with regret.

He suddenly remembered the burrow he had Sandshrew dig earlier—it was the retreat they had specially prepared.

"Sandshrew, run to the burrow quickly!"

Qin Yang shouted loudly, his voice hoarse from the pain.

Hearing this, Sandshrew immediately ran towards the burrow, with Qin Yang following closely behind.

They finally reached the burrow with great difficulty. Qin Yang used his last ounce of strength to drag over the stone slab he had prepared beforehand, blocking the entrance.

Inside the burrow, it was pitch black, filled with a damp smell mixed with the scent of blood from Qin Yang and Sandshrew.

Qin Yang leaned against the cave wall and slowly sat down, feeling his body growing weaker and weaker.

His face was as pale as paper, his lips trembled slightly, and his eyes were filled with despair.

"How could I be so foolish? Why was I so greedy... I didn't even think to prepare some Antidote medicine in advance, it's not expensive, I can make it myself.

It seems I've been misled by the recent Tailwind and forgotten where this place is, it's a very dangerous Pokémon world."

Qin Yang muttered to himself in self-reproach, his voice so faint it was almost inaudible.

Sandshrew lay beside him, his eyes filled with worry and exhaustion.

His breathing also became heavy and rapid, and the wounds on his body constantly caused sharp pain, but he still endured it, occasionally rubbing his head against Qin Yang, seemingly comforting him.

Qin Yang felt the toxins spreading through his body, his vision gradually blurred, and his consciousness began to dissipate. He recalled his experiences over the past ten years,

From being an orphan in the orphanage to working hard after obtaining the system, and then the little by little moments after having Sandshrew, his heart was filled with unwillingness.

"I haven't become a strong person yet, I haven't properly explored this world, am I going to die like this..."

Qin Yang's tears welled up in his eyes, but he was too weak to shed them.

Just as Qin Yang thought he was about to die, suddenly, a powerful force surged within him.

This force, like a surging tide, instantly swept through his entire body. It emitted a soft glow, illuminating the dark burrow.

Wherever the light reached, the toxins in Qin Yang's body dissipated rapidly, like ice meeting warm sunshine.

At the same time, this force was also repairing his body, which had been damaged by malnutrition. He could feel the pain in his body gradually lessening, and the feeling of weakness slowly disappearing.

Sandshrew, who was beside him, watched this scene in astonishment, his eyes wide open. He didn't expect his master to have such a miraculous ability.

As the toxins were cleared, Qin Yang's breathing gradually became steady, and his complexion slowly returned to normal.

He seemed to have fallen into a deep sleep, his brows gradually relaxed, and his expression became peaceful.

Seeing his master out of danger, Sandshrew breathed a sigh of relief. He could no longer hold on and lay down beside Qin Yang, falling into a deep sleep.

Inside the burrow, only their even breathing could be heard, as if telling the end of this thrilling adventure and foreshadowing the beginning of a new chapter.
