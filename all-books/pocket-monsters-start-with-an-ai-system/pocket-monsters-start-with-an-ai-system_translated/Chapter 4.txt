Chapter 4: Awakening

Chapter 4 Awakening

In a darkness as thick as ink, Qin Yang slowly came to, his consciousness struggling to break free from the chaotic abyss, finally surfacing with great difficulty.

He slowly opened his eyes, only to be met with endless漆黑, a darkness so substantial it pressed against him, making his breath catch.

In that instant, a strong sense of fear and confusion surged into his heart. He instinctively thought he had crossed the line between life and death and fallen into a gloomy, terrifying hell.

“Am I already dead? Is this pitch-black place the world after death?”

His lips trembled uncontrollably, his voice dry and hoarse, the panic impossible to suppress, echoing softly in the silent space.

Fortunately, he calmed down a little and searched his mind, pleasantly surprised to find that the system was still firmly in place, its presence very strong.

“What’s going on? Can the system still follow me even after I die, never leaving?”

He was filled with doubt, his brows tightly furrowed, his eyes showing confusion and incomprehension.

Immediately after, he subconsciously moved his fingers. The tangible sensation at his fingertips and the familiar warmth of his body made him suddenly realize that he might still be alive.

“That’s right, this pitch-black place must be the underground hole I hid in earlier to escape the Beedrill. No wonder it’s so dark, without a single ray of light.”

He muttered softly, his voice containing the relief of surviving a disaster, but also deep confusion.

“But I was clearly on the verge of death earlier, almost at the gates of hell. Why am I now full of energy and vigor?

What strange things happened while I was unconscious?”

His eyes flickered as he desperately tried to recall the river of memories, hoping to find some forgotten clues.

“I seem to remember that when I was about to die, a strong will to live surged from the bottom of my heart, and then some mysterious energy seemed to erupt with it.

Could it be this energy that pulled me back from the brink of death?”

Qin Yang slowly closed his eyes, concentrating on sensing the situation within his body.

At first, he felt nothing; his body was as calm as a lake, with no signs of energy fluctuations.

He even doubted whether what he had experienced earlier was just an illusion created at the edge of life and death.

Just as he was about to give up in disappointment, suddenly, a mysterious energy, like a meteor streaking across the night sky, flashed through his body.

This energy seemed to come from ancient, mysterious depths, exuding a captivating aura and full of vibrant life.

With just that brief sensation, Qin Yang felt an indescribable sense of comfort and peace well up in his heart.

His heart stirred, and he cautiously tried to control this mysterious energy.

Unexpectedly, this energy was very obedient, like a docile Pokémon, slowly circulating within his body at his will.

As the energy flowed, he clearly felt his body being infused with a clear spring. The fatigue and stagnation in his heart instantly vanished, and his body became lighter and more comfortable.

“So, it was truly this mysterious energy that saved me. Is this perhaps heaven taking pity on me and granting me another heaven-defying golden finger?

However, I have no idea where it came from or what its miraculous effects are.

Nevertheless, it saved me from a disaster. Although the process was thrilling and fraught with danger, I ultimately survived.”

Qin Yang sighed silently in his heart. Thinking back to the previous danger, his body couldn’t help but tremble slightly, fine beads of sweat seeped from his forehead, and his eyes still held lingering fear.

Suddenly, as if struck by lightning, he shot upright and anxiously shouted at the top of his lungs:

“Sandshrew, Sandshrew, where are you?”

His voice, sharp and shrill from tension, echoed in the cave, its tail trembling.

In a panic, he fumbled around in the darkness with both hands, finally finding his familiar backpack. With a “zip,” he pulled open the zipper and frantically rummaged inside for a flashlight.

“Click,” the flashlight turned on, and the bright light instantly dispelled the darkness of the cave.

Qin Yang’s gaze was sharp as he anxiously scanned the cave, quickly spotting Sandshrew curled up not far away.

Sandshrew’s condition was terrible at this moment. It lay quietly on the ground, its once smooth and shiny fur stained and matted with blood—a grim reminder of the desperate struggle with Beedrill.

Its body rose and fell slightly, its breathing heavy and rapid. With every breath, its body trembled, clearly in great pain.

Its eyes were tightly closed, and its long eyelashes fluttered slightly, as if it were struggling against pain and unconsciousness.

Qin Yang’s eyes reddened with heartache, tears welling up. He slowly approached Sandshrew,

gently stroking its scarred body. When his fingers touched a wound, its body trembled slightly, and he said with a choked voice:

“It’s all my fault. I was too greedy and harmed you, Sandshrew.”

Qin Yang knew in his heart that their current situation was incredibly difficult.

This place was deep in the outer reaches of Viridian Forest. Although most of the Pokémon around were only around level 20, their strength wasn’t too great,

for him, who had little combat ability, it was a den of dragons and a tiger’s lair.

His previous smooth progress was entirely due to the system’s scan warnings and Sandshrew’s brave protection.

Now, with Sandshrew severely injured and unconscious, without its protection, it was simply a pipe dream for him to leave this dangerous place alone.

But if they continued to stay here, Sandshrew’s injuries would not receive timely treatment and would only worsen. At that point, no matter how good its physical condition was, it would be difficult to escape death.

Qin Yang thought about how long he had been in this world, mostly staying in safe cities, completely underestimating the dangers of the wilderness.

It was all because he had relied on the system’s help before, making his adventures too smooth, which led him to lower his guard. That’s why he impulsively provoked the Beedrill swarm, ending up in this predicament—he truly brought it upon himself.

If he hadn’t awakened a new power, he would have lost his life long ago. Now, he could only say he was lucky.

He secretly vowed that if he could get out safely this time, he would be careful and cautious in the future and never be so reckless again.

Although he had some Potion he made himself in his backpack, which should be somewhat useful for Sandshrew, they were all low-level potion, good for treating minor or moderate injuries.

For its current severe injuries, they could at most provide some temporary relief, allowing it to hold on for a little longer.

Qin Yang was as anxious as an ant on a hot pan, pacing back and forth in the cave, his steps hurried and chaotic, his shoes scuffing against the ground with a “shush” sound. He constantly pulled at his hair, muttering:

“What should I do? What on earth should I do?”

Large beads of sweat rolled down his forehead, trickling along his cheeks and dripping onto the cave floor, kicking up tiny specks of dust. His eyes were filled with anxiety and helplessness, like a lost lamb in the darkness.

Suddenly, his gaze fell on the unconscious Sandshrew, and a light bulb went off in his mind:

“I wonder if my newly awakened ability will be useful for Sandshrew?”

He gritted his teeth, a hint of determination flashing in his eyes:

“Fine, at this point, I can only go all in and try anything, even if it’s a long shot.”
