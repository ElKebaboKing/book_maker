Chapter 10: Safe departure

Chapter 10 Safe Departure

After Qin Yang and Sandslash played for a while, he gradually quieted down.

He sat on the ground, took out some dry food from his backpack, and slowly chewed it, a thoughtful look in his eyes.

A whole day had passed since he was attacked yesterday, but he didn't feel too strong a sense of hunger.

Logically speaking, he had suffered such immense damage before, and his physical and energy consumption were astonishing, so he should have been extremely hungry.

Qin Yang tilted his head slightly, his brows furrowed gently, and he pondered to himself:

"Could it be that Power of Nirvana is secretly at work?"

Thinking of this, the corners of his mouth unconsciously curved upwards, revealing a relieved smile.

At this moment, Sandslash was happily eating a beginner Pokéblock, looking incredibly content.

Its mouth chewed rhythmically, making a faint "crunch, crunch" sound, and every bite was extremely satisfying, as if it were the most delicious food in the world.

Its eyes narrowed into slits, and from time to time it comfortably shook its body, its spikes clashing against each other, making a subtle "clack, clack" sound.

Although this beginner Pokéblock was carefully customized by the AI system based on Sandshrew's condition, its effect was no less than that of intermediate Pokéblocks on the market.

Even though Sandshrew had now evolved into Sandslash, the taste of this cube still suited its palate.

However, Qin Yang knew in his heart that Sandslash had already evolved, and the Pokéblock also needed to be re-customized to better promote its growth.

After they had both eaten their fill, Qin Yang gently patted Sandslash's shoulder, his eyes full of anticipation and trust, and slowly said:

"Sandslash, we're leaving soon. You'll have to protect me well on the road ahead."

Upon hearing this, Sandslash immediately raised its head, its bell-like eyes sparkling with excitement and loyalty.

It quickly stood up, its massive body swaying slightly, and the spikes on its body gleamed coldly in the faint light.

It first affectionately rubbed its head against Qin Yang's leg, emitting a low, affectionate "woo woo" sound from its throat, as if responding to Qin Yang's entrustment.

Then, it suddenly raised its front claw, swung it forcefully in the air, and then slammed it heavily onto the ground, making a dull "thud" sound,

The sound echoed in the silent cave, as if proclaiming its determination to protect its master to the whole world:

"Sandslash... Sandslash..."

When Qin Yang heard Sandslash's firm cry, a warm current surged through his heart. He genuinely felt Sandslash's loyalty and courage, and his tense nerves relaxed considerably.

"Don't worry, Master, I will definitely protect you well. You can leave the safety of the road ahead to me, your little Sandslash."

Sandslash's voice echoed in Qin Yang's mind, its childish yet powerful words making Qin Yang nod slightly, a relieved smile on his face.

Qin Yang recalled the experience of being attacked this time, and he still felt a lingering fear. A hint of terror flashed in his eyes, his brows furrowed tightly into a "川" shape, and he took a deep breath, as if he could still smell the dangerous aura lingering in the air.

He knew that this Viridian Forest was by no means as beautiful and safe as depicted in the anime; his previous unharmed state was merely the favor of Lady Luck.

Although the system's scan could alert him to some dangers in advance, he had ultimately underestimated the perils of the wilderness, which led to this accident.

"I must be careful and cautious from now on, and not be careless again."

Qin Yang muttered to himself, his eyes showing determination and vigilance, his hands unconsciously clenched into fists.

Life is only once, and surviving this great disaster was entirely due to luck. He dared not hope that good fortune would always accompany him.

In this perilous Pokémon world, only by staying vigilant at all times can one survive better.

Qin Yang walked to the cave entrance, placed his hands against the stone slab, took a deep breath, and pushed with all his might.

The stone slab remained motionless at first. Qin Yang's face turned red, the veins on his arms bulged, fine beads of sweat seeped from his forehead, and he gritted his teeth, emitting a muffled grunt.

"Why is this stone slab so heavy?"

He wondered to himself, perhaps the last time he pushed it up, his potential was unleashed in a desperate situation. Although his body had been transformed by Power of Nirvana, he still found it difficult now.

He adjusted his breathing, exerted force again, bent his legs slightly, leaned forward, and used all his strength.

Finally, the stone slab slowly moved, emitting a dull grinding sound, and a ray of sunlight shot into the cave like a sharp sword.

Qin Yang did not rush out, but stood at the entrance, focusing his gaze and activating the system to scan the outside.

His eyes were fixed on the screen displayed by the system, his brows slightly furrowed, his expression serious, not letting go of any corner.

Only after confirming that there was no danger outside did he crouch down and crawl out of the cave entrance, followed closely by the majestic Sandslash.

As soon as he stepped out of the cave, warm sunlight poured down on him, and Qin Yang couldn't help but squint his eyes slightly,

He looked up, letting the sunlight caress his face. He saw a few white clouds drifting leisurely across the clear blue sky, as if taking a leisurely stroll.

A gentle breeze blew, and the surrounding leaves rustled, as if playing a soft melody. In the grass, insects chirped cheerfully, one after another, as if holding a grand concert.

Qin Yang closed his eyes, took a deep breath, and the fresh air, mixed with the fragrance of earth, the清香 of plants, and the sweet scent of flowers, filled his nostrils.

He slowly opened his eyes, a comfortable smile on his face, and sighed in his heart:

"It's good to be alive."

He raised his hand to look at his watch and found that the hands had stopped moving, presumably damaged during the battle with Beedrill earlier.

He looked up at the sky and judged from the height of the sun that it should be around noon.

"Let's go home first. I don't want to explore this forest for now."

Qin Yang muttered to himself.

Under Sandslash's escort, Qin Yang walked along the path, his steps light and cautious, his eyes constantly scanning the surroundings vigilantly.

The occasional prompts from the system made him even more careful.

Along the way, they encountered several Pokémon. The closer they got to the outskirts of the forest, the weaker the aura of these Pokémon became.

A few Pidgey chirped on the branches and flew away, flapping their wings, after seeing Qin Yang and them; a few Rattata scurried around in the grass and quickly burrowed into their holes as soon as they sensed movement.

As they continued to move forward, Qin Yang finally saw the road outside the forest. He breathed a sigh of relief, and his shoulders relaxed.

"Finally out."

He said softly, a hint of fatigue and relief in his voice.

Although he knew it wasn't absolutely safe here, it made him feel much safer than inside the forest.

He looked at the dense forest again, and for some reason, a strange fear welled up in his heart. The lush trees seemed to hide countless unknown dangers, capable of devouring people at any time.
