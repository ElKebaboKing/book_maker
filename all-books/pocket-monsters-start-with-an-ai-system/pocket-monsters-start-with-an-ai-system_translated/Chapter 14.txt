Chapter 14: Go to the city

Chapter 14 Going to the City

The first ray of morning sun gently spilled into the small courtyard, and Qin Yang slowly opened his eyes, stretching with a big yawn.

The Sandslash beside him was also woken by his movement, sitting up with a 'whoosh', his eyes still a bit blurry.

Qin Yang looked at Sandslash and said with a smile, "Little guy, time to get up! It's time for our morning exercise."

Sandslash shook his body, his spikes making a slight 'rustling' sound with his movements. He lifted his head and looked at Qin Yang with his dark, round eyes.

"Sandslash, Sandslash!" He seemed to be responding to his master, with a hint of excitement in his tone.

Arriving on the path outside the courtyard, Qin Yang began his warm-up exercises. He twisted his waist and rotated his wrists and ankles, moving as he spoke, "Sandslash, you need to train hard today and unleash all your potential."

Sandslash, meanwhile, bounced a few times beside him, his spikes gleaming coldly in the sunlight. He cried out loudly, "Sandslash!" as if cheering himself on.

The morning run began. Qin Yang's steps were light and his breathing even. His gaze was firm and focused, fixed on the road ahead.

Sandslash, carrying a weight, powerfully trod on the ground with his four feet, raising a small cloud of dust with each step.

He followed closely behind Qin Yang, occasionally letting out 'huffing and puffing' sounds, but his eyes were full of stubbornness, as if competing with Qin Yang, determined to finish the run before him.

After running for a while, Qin Yang felt a bit strained and his pace gradually slowed down.

Seeing this, Sandslash sped up and ran beside Qin Yang, rubbing his head against his leg.

"Sandslash, Sandslash."

The sound seemed to be encouraging Qin Yang, and also showing off his own stamina.

Qin Yang smiled and reached out to pat Sandslash's head, but accidentally pricked himself on a spike.

"Ouch, you little rascal, so spiky and still so mischievous."

Sandslash seemed to realize his "mistake," lowering his head and making a 'whimpering' sound, as if apologizing.

However, both of them still completed today's morning exercise.

Ever since Qin Yang arrived in this Pokémon world, he had deeply understood the importance of physical training for a Trainer.

Venturing into the wild without a strong body would make it impossible to withstand the harsh environment.

Recalling his days in the orphanage, despite having the system's help, the conditions were too poor, lacking even basic supplements.

Even though he had always insisted on exercising, his physical fitness was only slightly better than his peers.

However, ever since undergoing the transformation of Power of Nirvana, he clearly felt his physical strength instantly double.

Now, as he continuously used Power of Nirvana, his body continued to strengthen. He eagerly looked forward to the day when he could become a "Super True Human" like those legendary powerhouses.

The reason Qin Yang was willing to exercise with Sandslash was because there were benefits.

This not only stimulated Sandslash's enthusiasm for training but also deepened their bond, allowing them to cooperate more tacitly in battle.

Moreover, it could also enhance physical fitness.

Although Qin Yang's body was good now, compared to a Pokémon, the gap was huge.

Take Alakazam, which has average physical strength, it is many times stronger than him.

In the past, Qin Yang's morning run was five kilometers; now that his body has improved, it has increased to ten kilometers.

As for Sandslash, he had to run with a weight, previously ten kilometers, now increased to twenty kilometers, and he still had to finish before Qin Yang.

Fortunately, Sandslash had evolved; if he were still a Sandshrew before, it would probably be very difficult to complete such a training task.

After the exercise, they returned home for breakfast. Qin Yang, while eating bread, said to Sandslash, "I plan to go to the city's breeding house today. The city is safer, so you don't need to come along. You can train well at home."

Upon hearing this, Sandslash, who was slowly chewing his food, immediately stopped, his eyes wide.

"Sandslash! Sandslash!" He stood up, his front paws restlessly scratching the ground, showing strong dissatisfaction.

Qin Yang looked at him helplessly,

"What are you doing? I'm doing this for your own good. Training at home is great; it can increase your strength."

Sandslash didn't listen at all, running directly to Qin Yang, blocking his way with his body, and continuously calling out:

"Sandslash, Sandslash, Sandslash!"

He looked as if he was saying:

"I don't care, I'm going with you."

Qin Yang sighed,

"You insist on coming along? Alright then, but you have to be obedient."

Only then did Sandslash quiet down, his eyes sparkling with joy.

"Sandslash!"

He jumped up happily, almost knocking over the table.

As they prepared to depart, Qin Yang got on his bicycle and looked at Sandslash, saying, "You've evolved now, so you can't sit in the basket anymore. You either run along, or go into your Poké Ball.

Of course, you can also choose not to go."

Sandslash tilted his head, thought for a moment, his eyes darting around, and then said:

"Sandslash... Sandslash..."

Qin Yang smiled and said, "I know, you want to run for a bit first, then go into your Poké Ball, and come out when we reach the city, right? You really are a guy who loves to join in the fun."

Sandslash happily cried out:

"Sandslash! Sandslash!"

His voice was full of cheerfulness.

And so, Qin Yang slowly cycled forward. After Sandslash ran alongside for a while, he voluntarily entered his Poké Ball.

Qin Yang hung the Poké Ball on his waist and cycled towards the city, his heart full of anticipation for today's itinerary.

And Sandslash in the Poké Ball seemed to be quietly waiting for the excitement to arrive once they reached the city.

Qin Yang stepped into the city, and a bustling scene immediately greeted him. The streets were filled with traffic and pedestrians, and the shops along the roadside were dazzling.

He stood at the street corner, a hint of curiosity and exploration in his eyes, squinting slightly as he surveyed the city's prosperity.

"There are quite a few breeding houses in Viridian City."

Qin Yang murmured to himself, his voice low but clearly audible.

His gaze fell on the three distinct buildings not far away.

"Charmander Breeding House, Bulbasaur Breeding House, Squirtle Breeding House. They clearly look like official establishments."

Qin Yang said, nodding slightly, a thoughtful expression on his face.

He put his hands in his pockets, leaning slightly forward, as if wanting to observe the breeding houses more closely.

These three official breeding houses each specialized in Water, Fire, and Grass type Pokémon. Although other types of Pokémon were also present, their main features were clear at a glance.

"However, besides these three official ones, there are also some private breeding houses."

Qin Yang frowned slightly, a hint of worry flashing in his eyes. He looked up, his gaze sweeping across the street,

"Those private ones are not simple either; they are either backed by large companies or big families."

Qin Yang's tone carried a hint of helplessness; he sighed softly, his shoulders slumping slightly.

Suddenly, as if remembering something, a glimmer of caution appeared in his eyes:

"There's also Team Rocket's breeding house. Team Rocket hasn't been targeted by the Alliance yet, so they can openly operate."

Qin Yang's expression became serious. He pressed his lips tightly together, a hint of wariness in his eyes.

He stood there contemplating for a moment, then decisively shook his head,

"It's better not to provoke that breeding house. Giovanni is not easy to deal with, and I don't want to cause trouble for myself. It's better to stay far away."

As Qin Yang spoke, he took a few steps back, as if Team Rocket's breeding house posed some kind of danger.
