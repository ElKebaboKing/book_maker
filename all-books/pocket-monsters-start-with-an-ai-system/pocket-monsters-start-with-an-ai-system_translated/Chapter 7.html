Chapter 7: Sandslash King

In the darkness, Qin Yang was deep in slumber when a sticky sensation spread across his face, jolting him awake.

The surroundings were still pitch black, and he couldn't see his hand in front of his face. Qin Yang only felt something squirming uneasily beside him.

He instinctively reached out to touch it, but the sensation startled him; the familiar soft fur of his Sandshrew had become somewhat prickly, a texture that a Sandshrew should never have.

Qin Yang's heart suddenly quickened, and a feeling of unease surged within him. In this cramped underground cave, there should be no other creatures besides his Sandshrew, but this strange touch made him wonder if an unfamiliar Pokémon had intruded.

Just as Qin Yang was filled with worry, a cry of “Sandslash… Sandslash” broke the silence.

It was clearly Sandshrew's voice, but strangely, Qin Yang could clearly understand its meaning:

“Master, Master, you're finally awake! This Sandshrew was so worried.”

Qin Yang was filled with surprise. He knew this must be related to his previous experience and that mysterious energy, but he had no time to ponder it now.

Unable to see clearly in the dark, he quickly fumbled for his flashlight. As a beam of light cut through the darkness, Qin Yang finally saw the situation inside the cave.

When his gaze landed on the Pokémon in front of him, he couldn't help but widen his eyes, his face filled with astonishment.

The original Sandshrew had actually evolved into a Sandslash. Its once short and sturdy body had become taller and more robust, and the spikes on its back were even sharper, glinting coldly under the flashlight's illumination.

Qin Yang was completely stunned. He couldn't figure out what was going on.

He knew that Pokémon evolution usually required many conditions: good condition, no injuries, and being conscious were all indispensable. But Sandshrew had clearly been unconscious before, so how did it suddenly evolve?

Did it wake up and evolve while he was asleep?

Qin Yang was full of questions, his brows tightly furrowed, his eyes revealing deep confusion.

Sandslash, seeing Qin Yang awake, excitedly bounced a few times in place. Its spikes made a slight rubbing sound with its movements, and the ground trembled slightly beneath its stomping.

Its eyes were fixed on Qin Yang, filled with affection and reliance, and it occasionally let out joyful cries. The sound echoed in the cramped underground cave, carrying a hint of excitement and delight.

Qin Yang looked at the Sandslash in front of him. Although he was full of questions, he felt more joy and relief.

He slowly stood up, his body still a bit numb, seemingly stiff from sleep, and his steps were slightly unsteady.

He walked towards Sandslash, reaching out to gently stroke its hard shell, feeling the cold yet solid touch, his heart filled with emotion.

“Sandslash, you really evolved. This is incredible.”

Qin Yang's voice carried an undeniable tremor, a mix of excitement over this unexpected surprise and deep confusion about the unknown cause.

Sandslash seemed to understand Qin Yang's words, nuzzling Qin Yang's hand affectionately with its large head.

A low, contented purr rumbled in its throat, as if responding to Qin Yang's emotions, or perhaps charming him for more affection.

Qin Yang carefully examined Sandslash. He could smell a faint earthy scent emanating from Sandslash, mixed with its unique, slightly pungent body odor.

This familiar yet strange scent made Qin Yang feel Sandshrew's transformation even more vividly.

Qin Yang's mind kept pondering the reason for Sandshrew's evolution. He guessed that perhaps during the process of clearing Sandshrew's energy channels and healing its injuries, he had inadvertently triggered some hidden evolution mechanism.

Or perhaps that mysterious energy had caused a special reaction within Sandshrew, prompting it to complete its evolution while unconscious.

Although these were just his guesses, he wasn't sure if Sandshrew's evolution was due to him.

But regardless, Sandslash's evolution was undoubtedly a delightful event.

“Congratulations, Sandslash, you finally evolved! From now on, we'll have to work even harder, Sandslash.”

Qin Yang said with a smile, his eyes filled with anticipation for the future.

Sandslash, hearing Qin Yang's praise, slightly lowered its head, seemingly a bit shy.

In fact, it was also very confused; it couldn't understand how it had suddenly evolved.

It clearly remembered being severely injured before, feeling at the time that it was doomed and would surely die.

When it slowly woke up, it found that not only had it not died, but it had miraculously evolved, which left it bewildered and utterly confused.

However, it also recalled the light emanating from its Master before it fainted. At the time, that light felt warm and comfortable, but it still couldn't resist the invasion of its injuries and fainted.

Now, thinking about it, could it be that the light from its Master saved it?

And Qin Yang, looking at the evolved Sandslash, felt a burning fighting spirit rekindle in his eyes. He tightly clenched his fists, as if declaring his determination to fate.

A firm smile appeared on his face, a smile filled with infinite expectation and confidence for the future.

He thought to himself that this could be considered a blessing in disguise; with Sandslash's strong protection, he should be able to return safely.

Sandslash seemed to sense Qin Yang's determination, raising its thick, powerful front claws high and letting out a loud Roar.

The sound was powerful and resonant, as if it could break through the dark underground cave, filled with endless strength and fearless courage.

It seemed to be showing Qin Yang its determination, and also declaring its new life and strength to the entire world.

Qin Yang also noticed that Sandslash's condition was indeed excellent, but he was still very curious about the specific changes after Sandslash's evolution, so he decided to use the system to scan Sandslash.

Qin Yang remembered that the last time he scanned Sandshrew's information, it was like this:
