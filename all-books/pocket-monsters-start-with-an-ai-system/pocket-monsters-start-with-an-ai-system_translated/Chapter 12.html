Chapter 12: Experiment of the Power of Nirvana

Chapter 12 Experiments with the Power of Nirvana

As soon as the matter with the Hunter Guild was settled, Qin Yang didn't let himself idle.

He was full of curiosity and anticipation, single-mindedly beginning to experiment with the miraculous Power of Nirvana on Sandslash, hoping to use this power to clear the energy channels within Sandslash's body.

In the beginning, Qin Yang's brows were always tightly furrowed, his eyes filled with worry, and he kept muttering to himself, "Will doing this actually harm Sandslash?"

To ensure absolute safety, he specifically took Sandslash to the Pokémon Center and found Nurse Joy to help with a thorough examination.

Nurse Joy's gentle voice echoed in the Pokémon Center, "Qin Yang, your Sandslash is really well-trained! How did you train him? He's so healthy and doesn't have any hidden injuries.

Which breeder helped you take care of him?"

Qin Yang's heart skipped a beat, and he stammered a few vague sentences, finding a random excuse to gloss over the matter.

Fortunately, the examination results showed that Sandslash was completely normal, and only then did Qin Yang completely relax.

After that, Qin Yang began to clear the energy channels.

However, he was very careful, as this matter concerned Sandslash's physical condition.

Qin Yang discovered that the large energy channels were simply too big; with his current Power of Nirvana, there was no way he could clear them all at once.

What was even stranger was that if a channel wasn't completely cleared in one go, the next time he checked, that channel would be blocked again.

This puzzled Qin Yang greatly, and he scratched his head, muttering with a bewildered expression, "What on earth is going on?"

Originally, he had thought that if a large channel couldn't be cleared at once, he could just take his time, but now it seemed completely unfeasible; the speed of slow clearing simply couldn't keep up with the speed of the channels getting blocked.

In fact, Qin Yang had once hoped that he could use this Power of Nirvana to unblock those blocked channels one by one, allowing Sandslash's aptitude to be infinitely improved, making him an incredibly powerful Pokémon.

However, reality was like a heavy blow, mercilessly shattering his illusions.

He knew that if his own Power of Nirvana could not grow further, then at most he could only try to unblock some small channels and slightly smaller medium channels within Sandslash's body.

As a result, even if Sandslash could improve his aptitude to a certain extent, many large channels would still remain unblocked within his body.

According to Qin Yang's AI System estimation, if all small channels could be successfully unblocked, Sandslash's aptitude might be elevated to Quasi-Elite Four Level;

if all medium channels were also unobstructed, his aptitude was expected to reach Elite Four Level;

and if those large channels could also be completely unblocked, perhaps Sandslash could even enter the ranks of Quasi-Champion or even Quasi-Champion.

However, Qin Yang also knew in his heart that this was merely the calculation result made by the AI System based on the current condition of the channels within Sandslash's body.

Due to the extreme lack of experimental data, its accuracy could not be guaranteed, and at best it could only serve as a reference, not to be taken entirely seriously.

Not only that, but Qin Yang also secretly speculated that in this Pokémon world, apart from those divine beasts with extraordinary power, the aptitude limit for ordinary Pokémon might be Champion-level.

It was just that he had never had the opportunity to witness what the energy channels within a Champion-level Pokémon truly looked like, nor was he sure if they were truly all connected.

"If the channels within a Champion-level Pokémon are indeed all clear, then it means my conjecture is correct, that is, only when the internal energy channels are completely connected can a Pokémon's aptitude reach Champion-level.

If that's truly the case, then I will have a clear direction and goal."

Qin Yang mused silently in his heart.

But this was ultimately just his guess; before it could be definitively confirmed, everything remained unknown, and perhaps other unknown secrets and mysteries were hidden within.

Qin Yang knew clearly that his current Power of Nirvana was extremely limited, so he could only temporarily focus his energy on the small channels and slightly smaller medium channels.

Each time he performed a clearing operation, he would pay close attention to his own energy consumption, daring not to be careless in the slightest.

After all, he had a deeply memorable experience.

That time, he accidentally completely depleted his Power of Nirvana.

In an instant, his face turned as pale as paper, his lips were bloodless, large beads of sweat continuously rolled down his forehead, and his body felt as if all strength had been drained, weakly collapsing to the ground.

That uncomfortable feeling made him feel as if he was about to step into the abyss of death.

Although his Power of Nirvana miraculously recovered the next day, the painful taste was deeply imprinted in his memory, making him fearful of exhausting his Power of Nirvana from then on, daring not to easily try again.

Currently, Qin Yang unblocks approximately eight small energy channels for Sandslash each day.

This way, he can still retain about 20% of his energy, preventing himself from falling into an extremely uncomfortable state.

And the remaining energy has no small use.

Whenever Sandslash completes his training, Qin Yang uses this energy to treat him.

With a focused and determined gaze, he gently placed his hands on Sandslash's body, softly saying, "Sandslash, bear with it a little longer, it'll be over soon."

The gentle energy slowly infused into Sandslash's body.

Sandslash comfortably narrowed his eyes, letting out a low purr, as if expressing his inner comfort and satisfaction: "This feeling is truly wonderful."

After such treatment, the fatigue and soreness on Sandslash's body instantly vanished without a trace, and no hidden injuries or potential issues were left behind.

Since acquiring this Power of Nirvana, Qin Yang clearly felt that training Sandslash had become much easier.

In the past, he was always cautious, not daring to let Sandslash overtrain, even with the help of the AI System, he was very careful when training, fearing damage to his body.

Because once injured, treatment would be extremely troublesome.

Not only would it affect training time, but it would also cost a lot of money.

But now the situation was completely different, Qin Yang secretly thought, "Since I can heal him using Power of Nirvana, then let him train to his heart's content, without too many worries."

Looking at Sandslash, he was truly exhausted during this period.

After each training session, he would be panting, lying on the ground, his tongue hanging out, breathing heavily.

His originally incredibly sharp and majestic spikes now seemed to have lost their former spirit, drooping limply.

He constantly complained in his heart, "Oh, ever since Master returned from the forest, he's like a changed person, becoming so strict and terrifying.

He makes me, the little mouse, train desperately every day, it's practically killing me."

However, he also knew very well in his heart that his Master was doing all this for his own good.

He understood that only by training harder and continuously improving his strength could he better protect his Master in the future and guard their bond.

Moreover, whenever his Master used that miraculous and comfortable energy to massage and treat him, Sandslash felt incredibly happy and satisfied; that feeling of pleasure even surpassed the Nurse Joy brought by the improvement in his strength.

Therefore, even though the training process was exceptionally arduous, Sandslash never showed the slightest retreat or slackness, always gritting his teeth and persevering.

And sure enough, in just one short week, whether it was Qin Yang's Power of Nirvana playing a crucial role or the result of Sandslash's own hard training, his strength actually soared like a rocket, increasing by a full three levels.

This speed is almost comparable to the rapid growth in strength of a newborn Pokémon, truly astonishing and delightful, earning endless praise.

What made Qin Yang even more ecstatic was that through the system, he discovered that as long as he frequently used Power of Nirvana, it would slightly increase the next day, like a seedling breaking through the soil.

"This is simply amazing!" Qin Yang thought excitedly.

"If things continue to develop like this, my Power of Nirvana will inevitably grow stronger day by day.

At that time, I will have enough ability to unblock all the channels within Sandslash's body at once, and no longer have to worry about the awkward situation of giving up halfway and the channels becoming blocked again."
