Chapter 24: Training Sandsling

Chapter 24: Training Sandsand King

Qin Yang sat in the room, his brows slightly furrowed, a hint of weariness and contemplation in his eyes.

He gently rubbed his temples, thinking to himself:

"Although today's investigation has resolved some previous doubts, new mysteries have followed one after another. The mysteries of the Pokémon World are truly unfathomable."

He sighed lightly, his gaze distant,

"However, those overly profound questions are probably not something I can figure out in a short time with my current abilities and knowledge.

For now, it's enough to confirm that opening energy channels is indeed helpful for improving aptitude, and this aptitude is closely linked to base stats.

It seems that in the future, I will have to try my best to capture Pokémon with high base stats, only then can I unleash greater potential."

Thinking of this, a hint of determination and anticipation flashed in his eyes.

"As for subsequent data collection, it naturally cannot be interrupted.

I possess this unique condition; if I can research a method to change a Pokémon's aptitude and sell it after my strength grows, the profits obtained will surely be unimaginable."

The corners of Qin Yang's mouth unconsciously curved upwards, as if he had already seen the massive wealth beckoning to him, but in an instant, he gently shook his head again, a hint of helplessness in his smile,

"It's just that the difficulties and obstacles involved are probably far beyond my estimation, and cannot be achieved overnight.

Let's put it aside for now. The most pressing matter is still to improve my own strength."

He slowly got up and walked to the window, his gaze falling on the Sandslash diligently training in the yard.

At this moment, Sandslash was wearing a forced training device, and every movement seemed extremely strenuous.

Its small claws scratched hard on the ground, trying to maintain balance, its breathing rapid and heavy, and from time to time, it let out low growls, as if cheering itself on.

Qin Yang's eyes revealed a hint of heartache and relief, and he softly said:

"Little fellow, you've worked hard."

His voice was light, yet it carried an undeniable firmness,

"However, all of this is worth it."

He squinted slightly, activated the system scan, and saw that the potential within Sandslash's body was slowly being released, with much better training results than before, and he nodded inwardly.

"Although this process is extremely difficult for you, I have Power of Nirvana and will not let you suffer permanent damage. As long as you don't train to death, then train until you drop."

His eyes became even deeper,

"I know that the pain of this training is a huge test for you, but the belief in your bones to become stronger has never extinguished.

This is also an important reason why I am willing to fully cultivate you."

Qin Yang crossed his arms and fell into memories.

"I still remember our first encounter; I found a fainted Sandshrew, took it back to care for it, and later captured and trained it.

During the danger in that forest, you were still just a Sandshrew, but you disregarded everything and stood in front of me, desperately protecting me.

From then on, an indescribable bond formed between us."

A warm smile appeared on his face,

"Later, you evolved into a Sandslash, and this friendship deepened even further. How could I not cherish your obedience and loyalty?"

Qin Yang was well aware of the many weaknesses of a Pokémon like Sandslash.

Its base stats were only 450 points, which was not high; its resistances were also relatively lacking, facing double weakness against Water, Grass, and Ice types;

Its special attack and special defense values were extremely low, and its speed was a major shortcoming.

In this Pokémon World, just like the saying in the martial arts world, "All martial arts, nothing is unbreakable, only speed is supreme," the same principle applies.

One must either possess powerful offensive and defensive capabilities or have extremely fast speed, making it difficult for opponents to hit, only then can one stand invincible in battle.

And Sandslash is merely a Ground type, with relatively simple move variations, lacking diversity in combat.

Qin Yang slowly walked out of the room and came into the yard. He squatted down and gently stroked Sandslash's head. Sandslash looked up at Qin Yang, its eyes a mixture of weariness and determination.

Qin Yang smiled and said:

"Keep it up, Sandslash. I believe that as long as we persevere, one day, you will become a true Superior."

Sandslash seemed to understand Qin Yang's words, nodded vigorously, and then turned back to its arduous training.

Qin Yang stood up and looked at the sky, silently praying:

"I hope all our efforts will not be in vain."

Night quietly fell, and Sandslash's long and arduous day of training finally came to an end.

If Qin Yang hadn't repeatedly fed it a large amount of potion and Pokéblocks during the afternoon to replenish its energy in time, Sandslash would have probably collapsed from exhaustion long ago.

At this moment, Sandslash, dragging its leaden body, slowly moved back into the room.

Its limbs trembled slightly, every step seemed to exhaust all its strength, its breathing was rapid and disordered, and its eyes were full of fatigue and pain.

Seeing this, Qin Yang did not hesitate and immediately began to use Power of Nirvana to treat Sandslash.

He knew that although today's high-intensity training had significant results, the aura emanating from Sandslash indicated that it was about to break through its current level. He estimated that if it persevered for another day tomorrow, it would successfully reach level 27, getting closer to the Elite Class level.

However, this also caused extremely severe damage to Sandslash's body.

He thought to himself, if it weren't for the help of Power of Nirvana, over time, even if Sandslash wouldn't die from this, its potential would definitely be greatly reduced, and within a year, it would probably become mediocre.

Qin Yang stretched out his hands, and after a while, a soft red light emerged from his palms, slowly enveloping Sandslash's body.

He concentrated, fully controlling Power of Nirvana, gradually repairing the damaged parts within Sandslash's body.

As the treatment progressed, Qin Yang clearly felt that this treatment consumed much more Power of Nirvana than before, almost one-tenth.

He silently calculated that, given this, he couldn't be as aggressive as usual when opening Sandslash's energy channels today; he could only open five first, as he still needed to reserve some power to nourish the Pokémon egg.

Under the effect of Power of Nirvana, Sandslash's physical condition gradually improved.

Before long, the treatment was completed, and Sandslash immediately felt much lighter. It looked up at Qin Yang, happily chirping:

"Sandslash... Sandslash..."

The sound was clear and loud, completely different from its previous weakness. Qin Yang understood its intention as soon as he heard it and asked with a smile:

"Do you want to watch TV?"

Sandslash nodded vigorously upon hearing this, its eyes full of anticipation.

Qin Yang nodded slightly, thinking to himself that it was understandable for this little fellow to want to relax after training diligently all day.

After all, training not only causes physical exhaustion but also a great deal of mental depletion. Although Power of Nirvana can repair physical damage, mental fatigue can only be recovered through relaxation and rest.

If mental fatigue is not alleviated, it will inevitably affect the training results of the next day.

"Go, relax well."

Qin Yang said softly, with a hint of indulgence in his tone.

Sandslash, having received permission, immediately perked up. It quickly turned around and trotted to where the TV was placed.

It skillfully pressed the power button with its small claws, then plopped down on the ground, leaning forward,

Its eyes fixed on the screen, a comfortable expression on its face,

And from time to time, it let out joyful hums, as if it had completely forgotten the fatigue of training and was fully immersed in the joy brought by the TV program.

Qin Yang looked at Sandslash's back, a relieved smile on his face, silently hoping that its training tomorrow would reach an even higher level.
