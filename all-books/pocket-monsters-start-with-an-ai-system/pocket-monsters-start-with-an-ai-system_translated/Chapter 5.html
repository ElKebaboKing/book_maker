Chapter 5: Treating Sandmouse

Qin Yang took a deep breath, slowly squatted down, and gently placed his trembling hands on Sandshrew’s body.

He closed his eyes tightly, concentrating fully as he mobilized the mysterious energy within him, trying to guide it slowly into Sandshrew’s body.

Qin Yang’s expression was extremely grave, his brows tightly furrowed, veins bulging on his forehead, and sweat poured down like rain, dripping onto Sandshrew’s fur with a faint pattering sound.

His breathing became heavy and rapid, as if he were engaged in a tug-of-war with death.

Under his guidance, the mysterious energy initially seeped out from his palms like a trickling stream, flowing into Sandshrew’s body.

But because he had just acquired this energy, although it had been very obedient within his body before, once the energy was outside,

the energy transfer was not smooth. Qin Yang’s face showed a pained expression, and his body trembled slightly, but he never gave up, continuously muttering to himself:

“I must succeed, I must save Sandshrew…”

A damp scent permeated the cave, mixed with the smell of blood from Qin Yang and Sandshrew. The pungent odor constantly irritated Qin Yang’s nostrils, making him even more anxious and uneasy.

But his gaze remained firm, fixed intently on Sandshrew, as if he wanted to inject vitality and strength into it with his eyes.

The moment the mysterious energy slowly probed into Sandshrew’s body, Qin Yang’s body suddenly jolted, as if his soul had left his body. His consciousness was pulled by a strange force, plunging into an unprecedented microscopic world.

His face instantly tensed, his eyes tightly shut, and fine beads of sweat seeped from his forehead, slowly sliding down the contours of his cheeks and dripping onto the dusty ground of the cave, raising tiny specks of dust.

His hands were pressed tightly against both sides of Sandshrew’s body, trembling slightly, seemingly engaged in a difficult tug-of-war with an invisible force.

Qin Yang’s breathing became rapid and heavy; each inhale was like an effort to capture the trace of that energy, and each exhale was like injecting his will deeper into it.

He could clearly feel Sandshrew’s faint life force, like a candle in the wind—a nearly dissipated aura, so weak that it seemed it would extinguish completely in the next second.

“Sandshrew, you must hold on.”

Qin Yang silently prayed in his heart, but his voice was choked with tension, only able to form a low murmur.

In this strange inner vision, Qin Yang was surprised to find that the internal structure of a Pokémon was completely different from that of humans.

Deep within Sandshrew’s core, a glowing orb emitting a dim yellow light flickered indistinctly, like a dying star, its light faint and unstable.

“What exactly is this thing? I’ve never heard of it. I absolutely cannot mess with it.”

Qin Yang secretly warned himself, a hint of fear flashing in his eyes. His brows furrowed tightly into a knot, as if he was trying to resist his inner curiosity and impulse.

Surrounding the glowing orb were intricate energy channels, these channels resembling the labyrinthine alleyways of an ancient city, winding and mysterious.

Looking at these channels, Qin Yang couldn’t help but recall the meridians through which masters circulated their inner energy in wuxia novels.

“Although I don’t know what they’re called in this Pokémon world, their function should be similar.”

Qin Yang murmured softly to himself, a hint of contemplation and curiosity in his eyes. His brows slightly relaxed, as if he was trying to understand this strange phenomenon.

However, he quickly noticed that most of these energy channels were blocked, and energy only circulated with difficulty through a few wider main channels.

After each cycle, it would slowly return to the central glowing orb, as if trapped in an endless loop.

“Now is not the time to explore these things. Saving Sandshrew is the top priority.”

Qin Yang took a deep breath, forcibly suppressed his curiosity, and refocused all his attention on treating Sandshrew’s injuries.

Qin Yang slowly adjusted the output of the energy within him, like a cautious helmsman carefully maneuvering a ship through turbulent waves.

He guided the mysterious energy slowly towards the bee venom within Sandshrew’s body, his gaze firm and focused, as if the entire world consisted only of him, Sandshrew, and the energy flowing within its body.

As the energy gradually approached, the bee venom, as if encountering its nemesis, began to rapidly decompose and dissipate.

Qin Yang could genuinely feel that with each bit of bee venom cleared, Sandshrew’s body trembled slightly—a sign of its struggle against the pain.

“Hold on, Sandshrew, it will be over soon.”

Qin Yang gritted his teeth and said in a low voice, his voice trembling slightly, as if he were fighting alongside Sandshrew.

Sweat continuously rolled down his forehead, blurring his vision, but his gaze never wavered, fixed intently on Sandshrew’s body, as if he wanted to infuse it with strength through his eyes.

After clearing the bee venom, Qin Yang dared not slack off in the slightest and immediately directed the energy to repair Sandshrew’s other injuries.

He manipulated the energy like a miraculous craftsman, patiently and meticulously mending Sandshrew’s damaged body tissues.

The mysterious energy, as if it possessed life, rapidly moved through the wounds under Qin Yang’s command, promoting cell regeneration and healing.

It was astonishing that, under the effect of this energy, Sandshrew’s wounds healed rapidly at a visible rate. The originally bloody wounds gradually closed, fresh granulation tissue grew, and finally, only faint scars remained.

In just a few minutes, all of Sandshrew’s wounds were basically healed. If it weren’t for the bloodstains and disheveled fur still on its body, it would be hard to imagine that it had just been struggling on the brink of death.

“This energy is incredibly powerful, simply beyond imagination.”

Qin Yang looked at the miracle before him, his eyes widening in surprise and disbelief. His mouth was slightly agape, as if he wanted to say something, but was too stunned by the sight to speak.

However, what puzzled Qin Yang was that even though Sandshrew’s injuries had healed, it still hadn’t woken up.

“What’s going on? Why isn’t it waking up? Did I miss something?”

Qin Yang paced anxiously around Sandshrew, his steps hurried and flustered, each step accompanied by the sound of his shoes rubbing against the ground. He continuously tugged at his hair, his eyes filled with confusion and helplessness.

“It’s impossible, the bee venom has been cleared, the injuries have healed, so what exactly went wrong?”

His voice carried a hint of a sob, sounding particularly desolate in the small cave.

In his extreme anxiety and helplessness, Qin Yang’s gaze once again fell upon the unexplored energy channels within Sandshrew’s body and the mysterious glowing orb.

“That glowing orb seems crucial, and there’s only one. I absolutely cannot take risks with it easily. So, I can only start with these energy channels.”

Qin Yang took a deep breath, trying to calm himself down. He slowly squatted down, gently placed his hands on Sandshrew’s body again,

re-enabled his inner vision, and slowly injected energy into those energy channels.

Initially, to avoid causing any accidents, Qin Yang only injected a very small amount of energy, like a cautious explorer stepping into an unknown, mysterious territory for the first time.
