Chapter 16: Flying Home

Qin Yang slowly stepped into the breeding house named "House of Flight" with Sandslash.

As soon as he entered, he was immediately captivated by the unique scene before him.

Inside the house, various Flying-type Pokémon flew and circled freely in the air, their movements light and graceful.

Sometimes they glided elegantly, at other times they darted quickly, and the gentle flapping of their wings intertwined, resembling a vibrant aerial spectacle.

Qin Yang's eyes widened slightly, a glimmer of curiosity and excitement flashing in them, and his head unconsciously turned gently with the Pokémon's flight paths.

His steps also became exceptionally light, as if he was afraid of disturbing these little creatures playing in the air.

He softly praised:

"This truly is a heaven exclusively for Flying-type Pokémon."

His voice was full of awe for the scene before him.

Sandslash was also attracted by this novel scene, his eyes wide and round, his small paws restlessly scratching the ground, and he occasionally let out "Sandslash, Sandslash" crys, seemingly expressing his surprise and curiosity about these flying Pokémon.

Qin Yang slowly paced, while pondering in his heart:

"Flying-type Pokémon indeed possess a unique charm; even with ordinary aptitude, they can play a significant role in many aspects."

He nodded slightly, a hint of approval in his eyes.

His gaze swept over Pidgey, Spearow, Zubat, Hoothoot, Hoppip, and Murkrow, all common flying Pokémon, one by one.

Pidgey's small body flitted up and down nimbly in the air, like a dancing sprite;

Spearow occasionally let out clear and loud calls, piercing the air in the shop;

Zubat hung upside down in the corner, its eyes darting around, showing a cleverness;

Hoothoot flapped its wings leisurely, unhurriedly;

Hoppip was like a light feather, dancing gracefully with the wind;

Murkrow exuded a mysterious aura, quietly perching high up, like a stern observer.

However, Qin Yang quickly realized the problem; his brow furrowed slightly, and a hint of disappointment flashed in his eyes.

Most of these Pokémon's aptitudes were unsatisfactory, quite ordinary.

He sighed softly with a hint of helplessness, the sigh faint yet full of resignation:

"Alas, as expected, it's not that easy to encounter Pokémon with outstanding aptitude."

His shoulders slumped slightly, and his steps became somewhat sluggish and heavy.

He knew in his heart that if he wanted to find high-aptitude Pokémon, he would probably have to go to a more advanced place, or purely rely on luck.

At this moment, the employees in the breeding house were busy.

Some were meticulously organizing Pokémon food and various items, while others were fully focused on maintaining order in the shop, yet not a single person came to greet Qin Yang.

But Qin Yang didn't care about this at all; he had worked in a breeding house before and knew well how such breeding houses operated.

He whispered to Sandslash:

"It's crowded and chaotic here, and the employees are swamped; let's look around ourselves first."

Sandslash seemed to understand him and instantly quieted down a lot, though his eyes still curiously darted around.

Qin Yang walked over to a Pidgey, slowly squatted down, and carefully examined it.

His gaze was focused and serious, as if he wanted to discover some secret from Pidgey's eyes and subtle movements.

He gently whispered:

"Pidgey, it would be perfect if your aptitude could be a little better. It's just a pity that even if your aptitude improved, with my current situation, I'm afraid it would be difficult for me to buy you."

Pidgey seemed to sense his words, tilted its head to look at Qin Yang, and then gently flapped its wings a few times.

Qin Yang stood up and continued to slowly explore the breeding house.

His heart was full of entanglement and contradiction, harboring a glimmer of hope that he could find a suitable Pokémon here, yet knowing that such a possibility was extremely slim.

Qin Yang quickly made a round in the "House of Flight" breeding house.

Although the shop was quite large and filled with a dazzling array of Pokémon,

With the system's miraculous function, he quickly scanned the aptitude of all the Pokémon openly displayed in the shop.

But the result left him somewhat disheartened; the most outstanding aptitude was only Superior, and the price was exorbitantly high, making his heart ache.

With his current meager funds, buying it would truly be painful.

If he chose those ordinary aptitude Pokémon, Qin Yang knew clearly that it would be better to go to the wild and try his luck catching one, which would be cheaper and more cost-effective.

Just as he was about to give up completely, a Superior Zubat suddenly entered his sight.

This Zubat instantly moved Qin Yang; its price was only half of that Superior Pidgeot, just 200,000.

Qin Yang stood in front of Zubat's cage, leaning slightly forward, his eyes fixed on it, a mix of struggle and hesitation in his gaze.

His brows were tightly furrowed into a "川" shape, his lips unconsciously pursed slightly, and his hands instinctively clenched and unclenched.

"If I buy it, the subsequent financial pressure will certainly be considerable."

Qin Yang mused to himself, his voice so low that only he could hear it.

He slowly raised his hand and gently stroked his chin, his fingers slowly rubbing his chin, his eyes full of conflicted emotions.

"But to give up so easily, I am truly unwilling."

He shook his head slightly, a hint of reluctance flashing in his eyes.

His mind involuntarily brought up Crobat's various advantages:

Its two pairs of wings granted it high-speed flight, allowing it to dart through the sky like lightning, and it flew silently, like a mysterious ghost in the night;

Its excellent stealth allowed it to approach prey unnoticed, launching swift attacks by surprise;

Its characteristic of sustained flight enabled it to soar for long periods without frequent rest;

Its decent resistances and diverse abilities provided a rich variety of tactical options for combat;

And its ability to learn multiple moves, whether Poison-type, Flying-type, or other types of moves, it could master them with ease, being flexible and adaptable in battle, like a highly skilled dancer.

"With it, I'll have an extra layer of security when camping outdoors; it can help me keep watch at night and effectively guard against potential dangers."

Qin Yang murmured softly, a hint of anticipation in his eyes.

But in an instant, he recalled Crobat's many disadvantages.

"Its movement on the ground is so inconvenient, only able to crawl with difficulty. If it encounters an unexpected situation, wouldn't it be extremely dangerous?"

He frowned, sighed helplessly, and his shoulders slumped slightly.

"Its durability is also unsatisfactory; aside from resistance to specific types of attacks, it's like fragile paper against other attacks.

Once its speed advantage is suppressed, its combat power will be greatly reduced, its output ability is quite limited, and it's also afraid of spikes on the ground..."

Qin Yang muttered to himself, slowly pacing in front of Zubat's cage, his steps heavy and slow, each step seemingly carrying a thousand pounds.
