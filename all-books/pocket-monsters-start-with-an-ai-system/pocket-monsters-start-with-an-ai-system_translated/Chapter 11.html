Chapter 11: Hunter Guild

Chapter 11 Hunter Guild

Qin Yang stood in the small courtyard, his gaze Lock-On to Sandslash, who was training with all his might, his thoughts surging back like a tide.

Unconsciously, nearly a week had passed since he returned from that perilous forest.

During this week, he and Sandslash recuperated peacefully, their minds and bodies gradually recovering their vitality.

Qin Yang slightly narrowed his eyes, his brows lightly furrowed, his heart filled with introspection about his past self.

In the past, he had become arrogant due to possessing the system; although he knew the Pokémon world harbored hidden dangers, he always felt he was different.

It was as if with the system as his reliance, even if his current situation was unfavorable, he would definitely become a magnate in the future.

This blind confidence led him to repeatedly put himself in danger, knowing the tiger was in the mountain yet insisting on going towards it.

“Alas, I was truly foolish back then.”

Qin Yang sighed to himself in his heart,

“This trip to the forest was a resounding slap, thoroughly waking me up. In this world full of unknowns and dangers, life safety is always paramount. If I don't even have my life, what's the point of becoming stronger?”

However, he also clearly realized that although this experience was painful, it was also a rare growth and transformation, allowing him to shed his youthful recklessness and become more composed and cautious.

During this week of recuperation, Qin Yang did not waste his time.

He remembered his experience of registering as a member of the Bounty Hunter Guild, and a hint of determination flashed in his eyes.

In fact, the idea of joining the Bounty Hunter Guild had already sprouted in his heart.

In his opinion, becoming a member meant being able to obtain a large number of tasks, and behind these tasks were generous rewards. For him, who urgently needed funds to cultivate Pokémon, this was undoubtedly a very attractive way to make money.

However, previously, because Sandshrew's level had not met the standard, he had always been unable to fulfill his wish.

It should be known that the minimum threshold for registering as a bounty hunter member is to own a Pokémon above level 20; this requirement is by no means groundless.

After all, in this Pokémon world, strength is the basis of survival. If the level is too low, when performing tasks, let alone completing the task and getting paid, even one's own safety cannot be guaranteed.

Qin Yang was well aware of the special status and complex nature of the Bounty Hunter Guild.

It neither belongs to the righteous path, under the absolute jurisdiction and restraint of the Alliance, nor is it a dark path that goes against justice, committing evil deeds and all sorts of atrocities.

It is more like a gray area between black and white, a subtle balance.

As long as it does not touch the Alliance's bottom line and does not commit acts that seriously violate public order and good customs or harm people, the Alliance often chooses to turn a blind eye.

In Qin Yang's understanding, this was actually a helpless compromise made by the Alliance after weighing various interests.

In its early days, the Alliance did indeed rely on the strong financial resources, vast power, and rich resources of those large families to develop and grow rapidly.

But as time passed, these large families have gradually become unmanageable; their power within the Alliance is deeply entrenched, forming a huge obstacle and constraint to the Alliance's further development and reform.

To break this deadlock, the Alliance had to open up new promotion channels for civilian Trainers, and the Bounty Hunter Guild became one of the important breakthroughs.

By encouraging civilian Trainers to train and grow in the guild, accumulate experience and wealth, and then attract them to join the Alliance,

Inject fresh blood and vitality into the Alliance, thereby promoting the Alliance to develop in a better direction.

When Qin Yang successfully registered as a bounty hunter and smoothly submitted the Beedrill honey task, his heart was filled with joy.

Although he almost lost his life in the process of obtaining the honey, the results he harvested at this moment made him feel that everything was worth it.

Those three liters of Beedrill honey, like a key to open the door of wealth and opportunity, not only helped him successfully complete the task and gain three thousand precious points, but also instantly swelled his wallet, bringing in 300,000 in cash.

Qin Yang secretly calculated in his mind:

“Now with this money, plus my previous savings, it's a total of 420,000.

If it's only used to train Sandslash, with a more reasonable resource allocation, it should last for half a year to a year.

Of course, if I pursue extreme resources, it might be depleted in a month.”

Qin Yang knew the arduous and long road of cultivating Pokémon, especially for civilian Trainers like him, who came from ordinary backgrounds and lacked strong family foundations.

Without sufficient financial support, it is difficult to obtain high-quality resources, and without the nourishment of high-quality resources,

The growth rate of Pokémon will be as slow as a snail crawling, and they may even lose their potential due to malnutrition during the growth process.

And for rich people, as long as they are willing to spend a lot of resources, plus the Pokémon's aptitude is sufficient, their strength can generally improve very quickly.

The strength before the Gym-Level can be quickly reached; this is the power of the pay-to-win Trainer.

Although Qin Yang was also a civilian Trainer and an orphan, without the Help of his parents, his conditions should have been worse.

Fortunately, he not only possessed the system himself but also had the abilities of a junior breeder and a junior potion maker, which gave him a unique advantage in resource utilization.

He could be meticulous and make the most of limited resources; materials worth 10,000 yuan, through his hands, often achieved the effect of 50,000 yuan.

Qin Yang clearly understood that his current level in the Bounty Hunter Guild was merely D-rank, like taking the first step in a long march.

It was extremely difficult to advance to C-rank. It not only required accumulating 10,000 points but also demanded at least one Elite Class-level Pokémon.

And B-rank was even more out of reach, requiring 100,000 points and a Gym-Level-level Pokémon.

As for A-rank, that was simply a fantasy; not to mention 1,000,000 points, one's own strength must reach Quasi-Elite Four Level.

The top S-rank hunters were personally selected by the Hunter Guild, and those who received this honor were all Elite Four Level or higher powerhouses standing at the peak of the Pokémon world.

“These goals are currently too distant for me, like stars twinkling in the night sky, seemingly beautiful but difficult to reach.”

Qin Yang silently told himself,

“For now, I should focus my energy on cultivating Sandslash to the Elite Class level and striving to be promoted to a C-rank hunter. Step by step, steady and solid is the way to success.”

Only when the hunter level is raised can higher tasks be accepted, and the rewards will be greater.

The Hunter Guild divides task levels from high to low into S-rank, A-rank, B-rank, C-rank, and D-rank.

Five levels, generally only by reaching this level can one accept tasks of this level. One can also accept tasks of lower levels.

For example, an A-rank hunter can accept tasks below A-rank, but generally no hunter does this. After all, at their level, the cost of Pokémon is not small, and accepting low-reward tasks would definitely not be cost-effective.

Of course, there is also a type of task not restricted by task level, which are some resource collection tasks. Any member of the Hunter Guild can submit these and receive rewards.

The task Qin Yang previously had, collecting Beedrill honey, was one such example. Otherwise, in terms of task level, it would have been at least a C-rank task, which Qin Yang would not have been qualified to accept.

The reason the Hunter Association is so popular is not only because accepting tasks can earn money but, more importantly, because of the points.

Points can not only raise the hunter level, and of course, they are not reset after leveling up.

They can also be exchanged for materials at the Hunter Guild, all of which are relatively precious materials, including special Pokéblocks, Evolution Stone, and even high-aptitude Pokémon.

This is the real reason why the Hunter Association, although not established by the Alliance, can attract so many people to join.

Only these rare materials can attract those powerhouses to join; otherwise, relying solely on money would not be able to attract Trainers above the Gym-Level level.
