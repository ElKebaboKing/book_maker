Chapter 22: Collecting data

Chapter 22 Collecting Data

Time flew by like a white steed, and in an instant, the faint glow of dawn quietly sprinkled upon the window, signaling the arrival of a new day.

Qin Yang slowly awoke, but he did not, as usual, go out for morning exercise with Sandslash immediately.

At this moment, his heart was filled with concern for the Pokémon egg he had nurtured with Power of Nirvana last night.

So, he hurriedly walked towards where the Pokémon egg was placed, with a hint of nervousness and anticipation in his eyes.

When he carefully checked, he found that the Pokémon egg was perfectly fine, with vibrant life force seemingly leaping joyfully within the shell, and its aptitude was also slowly improving.

Qin Yang immediately felt a sense of relief, letting out a long sigh, and thought to himself, “Last night, I was a bit impulsive after all. Fortunately, everything is going smoothly now. This can be considered a good start.”

After Qin Yang and Sandslash completed their morning exercise, Qin Yang gently patted Sandslash's head and said warmly, “Sandslash, you can train by yourself at home today, and by the way, help me look after the Pokémon egg at home, alright?”

Sandslash blinked its intelligent eyes, seemingly understanding Qin Yang's meaning, and nodded obediently.

Qin Yang looked at Sandslash's appearance, his heart filled with satisfaction, and turned to prepare to head to the city's breeding house.

In fact, Qin Yang's visit to the breeding house this time was not to purchase new Pokémon.

He had a plan in mind; he already had three Pokémon that were about to hatch. Although Caterpie and Weedle consumed relatively fewer resources during their growth,

with Sandslash and the unhatched Zubat, his resources were a bit tight.

He secretly pondered that as long as he could hold out until Sandslash successfully advanced to Elite Class, and Caterpie and Weedle successfully evolved into Butterfree and Beedrill,

and possessed a certain level of combat ability, then he could embark on an adventure,

to complete the Hunter Guild's missions, thereby earning more resources to raise Pokémon.

If he were to blindly add more Pokémon at this time, even if they had excellent aptitudes, he would struggle to provide enough resources for their training.

Of course, if he really encountered some rare Pokémon, Qin Yang would still take them, after all, rare Pokémon are not common.

Qin Yang's visit to the breeding house this time actually had a more important purpose.

He intended to use Power of Nirvana to thoroughly investigate the physical conditions of the Pokémon in the breeding house, thereby collecting more valuable data.

Previously, he had only observed the situation within Sandslash's body, and some of his conjectures were inevitably one-sided.

He knew that to draw more accurate conclusions, he had to investigate more different types of Pokémon.

His heart was full of curiosity, wanting to know if the energy channels within all Pokémon were the same in number, and if there were other differences.

However, Pokémon that he could freely touch and investigate could only be found in breeding houses.

After all, although not all Pokémon sold in breeding houses are gentle, they have all undergone systematic breeding and domestication beforehand.

Under normal circumstances, they would not easily attack humans, so they would allow customers interested in purchasing to touch and observe the Pokémon.

This undoubtedly gave Qin Yang an excellent opportunity for investigation; otherwise, he would truly struggle to find so many different types of Pokémon to investigate.

Of course, Qin Yang was not a reckless person; he naturally would not touch every single Pokémon in the same breeding house.

He knew that if he acted that way, it would certainly arouse suspicion from others.

Thus, he very carefully selected only two or three different types of Pokémon for investigation in each breeding house,

and tried his best to act no different from other ordinary customers, so as not to attract attention from others.

In this way, Qin Yang tirelessly traveled between various breeding houses, excluding the Rocket breeding house and the three official breeding houses of the Alliance.

He had almost visited every breeding house in Viridian City.

During this process, all the Pokémon data he investigated were collected, tallied, and deeply analyzed by his AI system.

Although Qin Yang had already tried his best, he clearly knew that there might still be some shortcomings in this data.

After all, the Pokémon in breeding houses generally have lower strength and are mostly in their initial forms. He could not determine if the situation within these Pokémon would change after evolution.

However, Qin Yang still found a Sandshrew in a breeding house and conducted a detailed investigation and calculation, carefully comparing the differences between it and his Sandslash.

Qin Yang was not eager to check the data at this moment; he secretly pondered that he would slowly study it after returning home.

Subsequently, he turned and walked towards the store, preparing to purchase some necessary supplies.

In the store, he carefully selected some berries and medicinal herbs, which are crucial for Pokémon training and recovery.

Then, his gaze fell on a forced exercise device. After much deliberation, he decided to spend fifty thousand to buy it.

Fortunately, this exercise device had a specific category for Sandslash, so there was no need for custom ordering, which saved a lot of time.

Qin Yang happily thought that after putting this on Sandslash, its exercise effect would surely be twice as effective with half the effort.

A faint smile unconsciously appeared on his face, and he secretly praised himself, “I'm truly a good and responsible master. I don't forget to bring a gift for Sandslash even when I'm out running errands. I wonder if that little guy will be overjoyed when he sees it.”

At this moment, Sandslash, who was secretly watching TV at home, suddenly felt a chilly breeze blow by, and its body shivered involuntarily.

For some reason, a strong ominous premonition surged in its heart, and the TV program it was originally watching with great interest instantly became dull.

It was full of worry, fearing that its master had returned, so it hurriedly ran to the big tree outside, stretching its neck to gaze into the distance.

After confirming that it did not see its master's figure, it finally breathed a slight sigh of relief.

However, Sandslash knew in its heart that slacking off like this was not a long-term solution. It would be terrible if its master found out.

After much deliberation, it finally reluctantly turned off the TV and obediently ran into the yard to seriously begin training.

After Qin Yang had arranged everything in the store, he roughly calculated that he had spent another one hundred thousand today, plus fifty thousand from yesterday. He originally had four hundred twenty thousand in hand, but now only two hundred seventy thousand remained.

He shook his head helplessly, sighing to himself, “This money really doesn't last! Raising Pokémon is truly too expensive.

I can still make Pokéblocks and potion myself, and even then, I spend so much. What about those ordinary Trainers?

No wonder commoner Trainers, although there is no limit to the number of Pokémon they can catch,

generally only raise three or four Pokémon, and it's even difficult to fill a team of six Pokémon. The difficulty of raising Pokémon is evident from this.”
