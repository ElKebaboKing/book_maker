Chapter 23 New Discovery

After Qin Yang carefully put away the items he had purchased, he set off on his way home.

Before long, his figure appeared outside his yard.

The Sandslash in the yard had extremely keen hearing and instantly detected the movement; its small figure immediately became lively, scurrying happily towards Qin Yang.

It circled around Qin Yang nonstop, continuously calling out, “Sandslash, Sandslash…”

Qin Yang looked at Sandslash's excited appearance, naturally understanding its thoughts.

It must have been asking:

“Master, why did it take you so long to come back? Your Sandshrew worked out very diligently today. Did Master bring me any gifts?”

Qin Yang smiled, gently stroked Sandslash’s round little belly, and said softly,

“Yes, I specifically brought you a wonderful gift.”

Upon hearing this, Sandslash’s eyes instantly lit up, and it excitedly cried out,

“Sandslash… Sandslash… Really? Master, what kind of gift is it?”

Qin Yang said mysteriously,

“It’s definitely a good thing. I’ll put it away first, and then I’ll give it to you.”

Sandslash was filled with anticipation upon hearing this, excitedly bouncing in place,

imagining what kind of surprise gift its Master would bring it, becoming more and more excited the more it thought about it.

Qin Yang quickly entered the house, and soon placed the things he bought in his room, then came out holding a box.

Seeing this, Sandslash immediately moved closer impatiently, its small eyes sparkling with excitement and curiosity.

Qin Yang didn't keep him in suspense and directly opened the box. Sandslash quickly leaned forward to look, but what came into view were only some metal rings. It was instantly stunned, filled with confusion and disappointment.

It really didn't understand what use this so-called gift had; it couldn't be eaten or drunk. What kind of good thing was this?

Qin Yang looked at Sandslash's dejected expression and explained with a smile,

“Sandslash, don’t underestimate this thing. It can help you train better.”

As soon as Sandslash heard this, its excited expression instantly collapsed, and it felt even more disappointed.

It thought to itself,

“Sigh, I didn't expect it to be something related to training again. Why is my Sandshrew’s life so hard?”

Qin Yang saw Sandslash's unhappy appearance but did not heed its emotions. Instead, he directly put the metal rings on Sandslash.

Sandslash felt its body sink, and instantly felt that whatever movement it made became extremely awkward, as if an invisible force was restraining it,

requiring greater strength to move freely, and its body was far less flexible than before.

It felt very uncomfortable and instinctively wanted to take this thing off.

Just then, Qin Yang’s voice rang out sternly,

“Sandslash, you must wear this thing all the time. You are not allowed to take it off.”

Upon hearing such a strict command from its Master, Sandslash knew that even if it was unwilling, it could only obey obediently.

Its heart was full of grievance, and it couldn't help but cry out loudly,

“Sandslash, Sandslash…” Qin Yang naturally understood Sandslash’s meaning.

This little guy was clearly protesting, protesting that he had deceived it, promising a gift, but it turned out to be something that made it uncomfortable, not caring about its feelings at all.

Qin Yang couldn't help but find Sandslash’s cute and wronged appearance amusing.

However, he did not change his mind. Sandslash’s protest was ultimately ineffective, and it could only helplessly continue training with this forced training device.

Qin Yang watched for a while, and seeing that Sandslash had gradually adapted, he turned and went back to the house, preparing to check the valuable data he had collected today.

After Qin Yang returned to his room, he eagerly began to check the status of the Pokémon he had investigated today. His system had already summarized and analyzed all the data, waiting for him to review it.

Today, Qin Yang investigated many different types of Pokémon, and in this process, he discovered some quite interesting commonalities.

Firstly, every Pokémon, regardless of its attribute, had a ball of light within its body. Qin Yang speculated that this should be a gathering place for energy.

Through comparison, he found that powerful Pokémon often had larger and brighter light balls, while weaker Pokémon had relatively smaller light balls.

Furthermore, all Pokémon had energy channels within their bodies.

Pokémon of different attributes had different colors for their energy channels.

For example, Fire-type Pokémon had red energy channels, Ground-type Pokémon had yellow, and Grass-type Pokémon had green.

If it was a multi-attribute Pokémon, its internal energy would be a coexistence of two colors, roughly distributed in a seven-to-three ratio, with one attribute being primary and the other secondary.

Of course, there were also special cases where the proportion of the secondary attribute might be slightly more or less, but it would not exceed 50% at most, and not be less than 10% at least.

Based on the data collected so far, no exceptions beyond this range have been found.

Regarding aptitude, Qin Yang discovered during the investigation that most Pokémon had ordinary aptitude, with only a small number belonging to the Superior level.

He also noticed that there was indeed a certain correlation between the level of aptitude and the贯通 (penetration/flow) of internal channels.

Superior level Pokémon clearly had more贯通 (penetrated/flowing) channels within their bodies than ordinary level Pokémon.

Although Qin Yang dared not assert that this was the only factor affecting aptitude, his previous conjecture was confirmed:

The more channels that are贯通 (penetrated/flowing), the faster the energy circulates, and naturally, the faster the strength improves, which is undoubtedly closely related to what Trainers call aptitude.

Of course, he also understood that there might be other factors at play, but they just haven't been discovered yet.

However, these discoveries were enough to strengthen his confidence; he could more confidently clear energy channels for Sandslash in the future, helping him improve his aptitude.

However, the biggest discovery today was not the above, but that the number of energy channels within Pokémon was not consistent.

Some Pokémon had more channels, while others had fewer.

Initially, due to the limited number of Pokémon Qin Yang investigated, he did not discover the pattern until more Pokémon were investigated. After in-depth analysis, the system came up with a conjecture and informed Qin Yang.

The system found that this was actually related to base stats. Pokémon with higher base stats had more internal channels and a more complex structure.

In addition, through comparative research on Sandshrew and Sandslash, Qin Yang found that evolution also affects energy channels.

Perhaps it was because he didn't pay attention to the condition of Sandshrew's channels when he first cleared them, and after Sandslash evolved, he was also unsure if there were significant changes.

Now, after investigating the channels within Sandshrew at the breeding facility and combining it with Sandslash's situation, Qin Yang found that the number of channels within the evolved Sandslash significantly increased.

This might be related to the increase in base stats, as mentioned before, the higher the base stats, the more internal channels there are.

When Sandshrew evolves into Sandslash, its base stats increase, and its energy channels naturally also increase.

Qin Yang felt this was quite reasonable.

Among the Pokémon Qin Yang investigated today, Scyther had the highest base stats, and the number of channels within its body was indeed more than Sandslash.

This discovery further confirmed the correctness of the system's analysis.

As for whether there would be unexpected situations, Qin Yang believed there shouldn't be too much deviation, after all, he collected data from many Pokémon today, and their base stats varied,

and the number of channels was also different. The statistical data had a certain scientific basis, so he was quite convinced by this result.

However, regarding his previous conjecture that if all the channels within a Pokémon were cleared, would its aptitude become Champion-level, Qin Yang still couldn't be sure.

After all, up to now, he had never seen a Pokémon with Champion-level aptitude, nor did he know if all its internal channels were cleared. Lacking real data for reference, this could only be considered a speculation for now.

At this moment, a hint of curiosity arose in Qin Yang's mind. A Sandslash with 450 base stats and a Scyther with 500 base stats, both having different numbers of internal channels,

if all their channels were cleared, how should their aptitudes be defined?

Could both be considered Champion-level?

But Scyther has more internal channels, and if all of them are cleared, its energy circulation speed would definitely far exceed Sandslash, and its strength improvement speed would also be faster.

In that case, could they still be considered to have the same Champion-level aptitude?

Qin Yang felt deeply puzzled about this, as this was also just a conjecture without data support, and for now, he could only ponder it in his mind.
