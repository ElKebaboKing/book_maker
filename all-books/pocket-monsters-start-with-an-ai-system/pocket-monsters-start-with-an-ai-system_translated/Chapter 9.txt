Chapter 9: The Power of Nirvana

Qin Yang carefully examined the data on the light screen, finding that there didn't seem to be many significant changes compared to before.

Only in the item bar, there was the three liters of Beedrill honey he had risked his life for, and in the talent section, a new display of "Unknown Energy" appeared.

It seemed that even the system couldn't precisely know what to call this mysterious power.

Qin Yang frowned slightly, pondering to himself. Continuously referring to this energy as "Unknown Energy" or "Special Energy" felt too stiff and awkward.

After all, he had deeply experienced the miraculous effects of this energy; it not only allowed him to understand Pokémon speech without hindrance but also possessed the extraordinary ability to heal and cure Pokémon, and improve their potential.

Perhaps, beneath its mysterious veil, there were even more astonishing powers yet to be discovered.

How could such a miraculous energy not have a proper name?

Qin Yang fell into contemplation, his gaze somewhat distant, continuously thinking of various possible names in his mind.

Time passed minute by minute, and suddenly, his eyes lit up, as if struck by a bolt of inspiration.

"Power of Nirvana!"

He blurted out excitedly.

This name, in his opinion, perfectly matched the miraculous effects this energy displayed.

It was like a phoenix undergoing Nirvana, capable of bestowing new life and transformation upon Pokémon, allowing them to break through their original limitations and gain stronger power and broader growth potential.

"System, change the talent name to Power of Nirvana (Beginner)."

Qin Yang gave the command to the system without hesitation.

As his words fell, the information on the light screen instantly updated, and the talent section had already changed to "Power of Nirvana (Beginner)."

Qin Yang looked at the new name on the light screen, the corners of his mouth slightly curved upwards, revealing a satisfied smile, his heart filled with anticipation and longing for this Power of Nirvana.

Sandslash squatted to the side, its large, bell-like eyes staring unblinkingly at Qin Yang. Seeing the brilliant smile on his master's face, though it didn't understand why, its heart also felt joyful.

Its heart was full of gratitude for Qin Yang, secretly thinking:

"If Master hadn't brought me, weak and injured as I was, home and taken such good care of me, how could I have lived until now, let alone successfully evolved into a Sandslash?

Master is my savior, my most, most, most important existence."

Thinking these thoughts, Sandslash stood up, shaking its hard shell, emitting a faint clattering sound.

It walked steadily to Qin Yang's side, gently rubbed its head against Qin Yang's arm, and let out a low purring sound, as if affectionately using Charm.

Then, it majestically raised its front paw and heavily slapped the ground twice, seemingly declaring its determination to protect its master to the world, its demeanor as if saying:

"If anyone dares to harm Master, I will absolutely not allow it!"

Qin Yang saw Sandslash's appearance, his heart filled with warmth and indulgence.

Although Sandslash did not speak, Qin Yang could genuinely feel that strong attachment and deep affection just by its eyes and posture.

He subconsciously raised his right hand, intending to affectionately rub Sandslash's back as usual,

But just as his hand was about to touch, he suddenly came to his senses; what was in front of him was no longer the fluffy, excellent-to-the-touch Sandshrew, but the spiky Sandslash.

Qin Yang's hand hung awkwardly in mid-air, he secretly thought:

"If I touch it, I'll probably get a hand full of bloody holes, so I'd better not."

So, his hand slightly turned, intending to rub its head instead, but as his gaze fell on the top of Sandslash's head, he helplessly realized that it was also covered in sharp spines.

Qin Yang sheepishly withdrew his right hand, that hand, like a homeless child, swayed in the air before

Awkwardly dropping to his side. A slight blush appeared on his face, his eyes showing a mix of embarrassment and helplessness.

Sandslash, originally full of joy, was waiting for its master's petting. It habitually closed its eyes, tilted its head slightly, and even had a hopeful smile on its face, ready to fully enjoy that familiar comfort and warmth.

However, as time passed minute by minute, the anticipated petting never arrived.

Sandslash opened its eyes, full of confusion, and saw Qin Yang's bewildered, misplaced right hand.

Its originally bright eyes instantly filled with bewilderment, and its ears unconsciously drooped slightly.

Just then, Qin Yang's voice came, with a hint of awkwardness and helplessness:

"Sandslash, it's not that I don't want to pet you, it's just that you have too many spines on your body; I can't even begin to touch you."

Upon hearing this, Sandslash froze in place as if under a spell.

The images of the majestic Sandslash leaders in its clan instantly flashed through its mind; they indeed were covered in spines, and it seemed truly difficult for its master to pet it like before.

Thinking of this, a hint of sadness gradually appeared in Sandslash's eyes, as if it had lost something extremely precious. Its nose twitched slightly, as if it was trying to suppress the tears that were about to well up:

"Rat-rat be strong, rat-rat don't cry."

It silently cheered itself on in its heart, but its slightly trembling body still betrayed its inner sorrow.

Qin Yang keenly noticed Sandslash's emotional change. He quickly stepped forward, squatted down, and looked at Sandslash gently, asking softly:

"What's wrong? Are you uncomfortable somewhere?"

Sandslash looked up at Qin Yang, its eyes full of grievance. It did not hide its feelings, its voice choked with a hint of a sob:

"Master, will I never be able to be petted by you again? I'm so sad."

Qin Yang looked at this childish Sandslash in front of him and couldn't help but laugh softly.

He gently patted Sandslash's arm and said with a smile:

"You, you still have a child's temper. Actually, petting your belly is fine too."

Upon hearing this, Sandslash's eyes immediately lit up, and that hint of sadness was instantly replaced by surprise.

It unhesitatingly lay back, four paws in the air, revealing its soft belly completely to Qin Yang, and even gently swayed its body, indicating that Qin Yang could pet it as much as he liked.

Seeing its adorable appearance, Qin Yang no longer hesitated, reaching out and gently rubbing Sandslash's belly.

Sandslash comfortably narrowed its eyes, a satisfied purring sound coming from its throat, that sound seemed to say:

"Master's petting is still so comfortable, rat-rat is so happy."

Qin Yang was also amused by its reaction, his face beaming with smiles, his eyes full of tenderness and affection.

The originally awkward atmosphere was instantly replaced by joy. One person and one pet, in this small underground cave, enjoyed this warm moment.

However, while petting Sandslash, Qin Yang also thought about how long he had been in this underground cave. Now with Sandslash's protection, he should be able to leave safely.

Thinking of this, a desire to leave welled up in his heart. He really needed to leave quickly. This was still deep within the outskirts of Viridian Forest, and it was still dangerous. It was better to leave early.
