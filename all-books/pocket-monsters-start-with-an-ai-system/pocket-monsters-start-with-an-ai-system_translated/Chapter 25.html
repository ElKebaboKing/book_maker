Chapter 25: Extended incubation period

Chapter 25 Extended Incubation Period

After dinner, Qin Yang no longer paid attention to Sandslash, who was relaxing and resting nearby, and turned back to his room.

His gaze fell directly on the three incubators in the room, with a hint of anticipation and concern in his eyes.

He gently stepped forward, carefully took out the Caterpie and Weedle Pokémon Eggs, and held them in his hands, examining them closely.

After a detailed inspection, he found that the Power of Nirvana injected last night did not seem to have any adverse effects on the Pokémon Eggs.

Instead, it was all beneficial, which finally put his worried mind at ease.

At this time, the incubation period for these two Pokémon Eggs showed only three days remaining.

Qin Yang thought for a moment, without much hesitation, and decided to inject 10% of Power of Nirvana into each of them again.

After the injection was completed, he habitually checked again, but was surprised to find that the incubation period had turned into four days.

This sudden change stunned Qin Yang, a look of confusion appeared on his face, and he secretly pondered:

"What's going on? Does this mean that as long as Power of Nirvana is continuously injected, these two eggs will never hatch?"

He scratched his head, his brows furrowed, looking bewildered.

However, he quickly calmed down, thinking:

"Never mind, I'll observe for a while first, after all, no one can say for sure what will happen later.

Anyway, for now, Power of Nirvana is beneficial to them, so let's maintain the status quo for now."

So, he slowly put the two Pokémon Eggs back into the incubator, his movements gentle and cautious, as if afraid of disturbing the little lives incubating inside.

Next, Qin Yang's gaze fell on the Zubat Pokémon Egg, which was a precious Pokémon Egg with Gym-Level potential, worth a lot.

He silently estimated the remaining Power of Nirvana in his body, which was probably a little over 20%.

He took a deep breath and first tried to inject 10% of Power of Nirvana into the Zubat's egg.

After the injection, he found that this Pokémon Egg showed no signs of saturation and seemed to be able to continue absorbing.

Qin Yang's eyes lit up immediately, and a surge of curiosity and desire for exploration rose in his heart.

"Since that's the case, why not try increasing the energy?"

He muttered to himself, then carefully increased the energy transfer step by step.

After some attempts, the Zubat Pokémon Egg finally absorbed a full 20% of Power of Nirvana before reaching saturation.

Qin Yang looked at the three Pokémon Eggs in front of him, forced a wry smile, and was filled with distress:

"These are all big spenders! If this continues, I'll have to conserve Power of Nirvana for the next few days, otherwise, I'm afraid I won't even be able to afford these three Pokémon Eggs."

His gaze fell on the Zubat Pokémon Egg, and he remembered its original incubation period, which should have been nine days remaining at this time today.

However, after this injection of Power of Nirvana, the incubation period was extended by another day, becoming ten days.

This made Qin Yang feel a bit depressed, and he couldn't help but suspect:

"These three eggs aren't planning to stay inside forever, are they? Sigh, never mind, I'll take it one step at a time and see how things go later."

He sighed softly, his eyes revealing a complex emotion of helplessness mixed with anticipation.

In the following days, Qin Yang's life gradually formed a regular rhythm.

During the day, he often accompanied Sandslash, witnessing it sweat on the training ground, constantly honing itself.

Every time Sandslash performed strength training, Qin Yang would observe with full concentration from the side, giving encouragement and guidance at the right time;

And when Sandslash practiced skill techniques, Qin Yang would carefully use the system to analyze its movements, point out deficiencies, and personally demonstrate the correct way.

Occasionally, Qin Yang would also take time to visit the city's Pokémon breeding house, like a mysterious explorer delving into unknown territory,

There, he would carefully investigate the condition of various Pokémon, not missing any details, just to collect more valuable data.

As night fell, the lights in Qin Yang's home would turn on on time, and he would begin to focus on treating Sandslash.

He first used the system to comprehensively scan Sandslash's body, precisely locating every injury and blockage in the energy channels.

Then, his hands slowly condensed Power of Nirvana, and a gentle light, like a trickling stream, infused into Sandslash's body.

While treating, he also did not forget to clear Sandslash's internal energy channels; every time he pushed the flow of energy, he was careful, as if carving a rare treasure.

And after completing Sandslash's treatment and channel clearing, he would turn his gaze to the Pokémon Eggs lying quietly in the incubator.

Speaking of the Pokémon Eggs' nurturing, the Caterpie and Weedle eggs, after absorbing Power of Nirvana three times, showed an interesting change.

Their incubation periods each increased by three days, but fortunately, they no longer extended after that, which greatly relieved Qin Yang, and his worries dissipated.

"Finally, I don't have to worry about them endlessly extending their incubation period anymore, otherwise, I would really have to consider stopping injecting Power of Nirvana into them."

Qin Yang muttered to himself, a look of relief on his face.

However, the Zubat Pokémon Egg still went its own way; its incubation period remained steadily at ten days.

Each time it absorbed Power of Nirvana, the incubation period would correspondingly increase by one day.

Qin Yang roughly estimated based on the situation of Caterpie and Weedle that the Zubat egg might need about ten days to complete this special "Nirvana Journey."

As a result, its birth time would be postponed to twenty days later.

Although Qin Yang was somewhat helpless about this, he also knew that a longer incubation time, allowing the Pokémon Egg to fully absorb Power of Nirvana, was greatly beneficial for improving their potential.

Take the Caterpie and Weedle eggs, for example; after absorbing Power of Nirvana, Qin Yang was pleasantly surprised to find that their potential had increased from the original ordinary Elite Class to the valuable Gym-Level.

Although this acquired change did not bring them new inherited skills, it was undoubtedly an exciting and good start, almost like defying fate.

Qin Yang had previously used the system to conduct in-depth analysis of Caterpie and Weedle; they were originally mediocre in Elite Class potential.

If it were according to the normal Power of Nirvana clearing method, it would take at least one month to raise their potential to Gym-Level level.

But now, being able to achieve this goal by just waiting three more days filled Qin Yang with joy, as if he had found a huge treasure.

"It seems that in the future, it's most cost-effective to raise Pokémon from the egg."

Qin Yang secretly sighed in his heart, a hint of enlightenment and anticipation flickering in his eyes.

Time flew by, and five days had passed in a blink of an eye.

Now, the Power of Nirvana in Qin Yang's body, in addition to being used to treat the exhausted and scarred Sandslash after daily training, and to clear the complex energy channel blockages in its body,

Only 20% more needs to be reserved for the Zubat Pokémon Egg.

Because the Caterpie and Weedle eggs are already close to the critical moment of hatching, according to Qin Yang's speculation, they will most likely Hatching tomorrow.

Qin Yang stood in front of the incubator, his eyes full of anticipation and longing; although Caterpie and Weedle are not incredibly powerful Pokémon,

They excel in their fast growth rate; once hatched successfully, they can grow rapidly, adding a new combat force for Qin Yang, which will undoubtedly be of great help to him in his future adventures.
