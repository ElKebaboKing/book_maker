Chapter 17: Gambling Egg

Chapter 17 Gambler

His heart was fiercely struggling; one moment, it was an eager desire for Zubat's powerful abilities, and the next, a deep worry about financial pressure.

“Alas, in the end, it's just that I'm too poor. If I had money, why would I be so conflicted? I would have just bought it without a second thought.”

Qin Yang chuckled bitterly, his face filled with helplessness and a touch of sadness.

He looked up at the ceiling, a hint of confusion in his eyes. After a long time, he slowly lowered his head, his gaze gradually becoming firm.

“No, I can't keep hesitating like this. I must carefully weigh the pros and cons.”

Qin Yang took a deep breath, as if steeling himself. He turned his gaze back to the Zubat, his eyes now showing more thought and determination.

Qin Yang stood in front of Zubat's cage, his heart undergoing an intense struggle. Just as he was about to steel himself to buy this Elite Class Zubat, a tender child's voice suddenly rang out beside him.

“Daddy, what's over there? Why are there so many Pokémon eggs laid out?”

“And why do all these eggs look so old and worn out? And what are those uncles and aunties doing, picking and choosing over there?”

The little boy's curious voice was full of questions, his eyes wide, pointing a finger towards an area not far away. His small body also unconsciously leaned in that direction.

The father beside him smiled and patted his son's head, patiently explaining:

“Son, that's the shop's egg-gambling area. It's full of Pokémon eggs that are either flawed or unknown ones brought from the wild. The eggs here can be good or bad.”

“You might find a really great Pokémon, but you might also have bad luck and buy a dead egg.”

The father's voice was gentle and low, with a faint smile on his face.

Qin Yang's eyes instantly lit up when he heard the conversation between the father and son, as if a light had flashed in his heart.

A hint of surprise and anticipation flickered in his eyes, and his body involuntarily turned towards the egg-gambling area.

“That's right, I don't have enough money, so I can totally try my luck with egg gambling.”

He mused to himself, his voice so low only he could hear it.

He slightly raised his hand, gently rubbing his chin, his brows slightly furrowed, his eyes revealing a thoughtful expression.

“The Alliance's machines for detecting Pokémon egg potential are already prone to errors, let alone these eggs, most of which are damaged.”

“Moreover, the cost of detecting Pokémon egg potential is even more expensive. If they were to test the potential of every egg, the shop would probably go bankrupt long ago.”

“That's why the shop puts these special Pokémon eggs in the egg-gambling area. They're cheap, only ten thousand yuan each.”

“Although most might be eggs of Caterpie, Weedle, or similar, and there are quite a few dead eggs, I have the system! I can probe the egg's potential. Isn't this a golden opportunity?”

The more Qin Yang thought about it, the more excited he became, and the light in his eyes grew brighter.

He reluctantly glanced at the Zubat in the cage next to him, a hint of apology in his eyes, and softly said:

“Sorry, little guy, I'm going to go check over there.”

His voice was very soft, as if he was afraid of disturbing the Zubat.

With that, he and Sandslash quickly walked towards the egg-gambling area, his steps light and firm, his eyes full of anticipation for the unknown.

Sandslash followed behind Qin Yang, its small head full of questions. It tilted its head, looking at Qin Yang, its eyes filled with confusion.

“Sandslash, Sandslash?”

It called out softly, seemingly asking Qin Yang why he had suddenly changed his mind.

It had originally thought Qin Yang would definitely buy the Zubat, after all, Qin Yang had been staring at the Zubat with a conflicted expression, and Sandslash had seen it all.

“Master was so conflicted just now, why isn't he buying it anymore?”

Sandslash wondered to itself, its short claws moving quickly on the ground, following Qin Yang closely.

However, it didn't dwell on it for too long and quickly let it go. As long as it could always be by its Master's side, it didn't bother to think much about anything else.

“Never mind, wherever Master goes, I'll go.”

Sandslash shook its head, its eyes becoming cheerful again, and its cry became clear and loud, as if it was cheering itself on.

Qin Yang strolled into the egg-gambling area and, looking up, saw an impressive number of eggs, a veritable mountain of them.

However, the appearance of these eggs was truly not commendable.

The shells of most eggs were covered in cracks, as if they were old, weathered porcelain. The cracks, like fine spiderwebs, spread wantonly, making it clear at a glance that their condition was poor.

Some other eggs were thickly covered in earth and stone, like ancient artifacts just excavated from a muddy swamp, exuding an old and dirty aura. In short, most of the eggs here had various problems.

Qin Yang knew that even with his miraculous system, he couldn't instantly discern the potential of these vast numbers of Pokémon eggs.

However, the system had an extremely practical function: it could quickly determine whether there were signs of life within an egg.

He secretly planned to first eliminate the lifeless, dead eggs, and then analyze the potential of each living egg one by one.

After all, assessing Pokémon egg potential was quite difficult, and even with the system's help, it was much more cumbersome than probing already hatched Pokémon, requiring a lot of time and effort, so the progress was naturally much slower than before.

Qin Yang stood still, closed his eyes slightly, and silently activated the system in his mind, then initiated scan mode.

His expression was solemn, his eyes fixed on the pile of eggs before him, as if his gaze could penetrate each one.

Before long, the system completed its initial scan of over a thousand eggs.

Qin Yang checked the results and couldn't help but be slightly stunned, marveling to himself.

It turned out that only two to three hundred eggs showed signs of life; the rest were all dead eggs.

Some of these eggs looked perfectly intact from the outside. If one were to judge by sight alone, they would surely believe that vibrant life was gestating within, but in reality, they were empty inside, devoid of any life.

“This egg gambling truly relies entirely on luck. If not for the system's help, who would have known that this seemingly perfect egg was actually a dead one?”

Qin Yang murmured to himself, raising a hand to wipe the fine beads of sweat from his forehead, his face filled with apprehension.

At the same time, he also noticed some peculiar phenomena: some eggs looked old and dilapidated, as if they could break at any moment, yet life stubbornly gestated within, which was truly amazing.

After completing the initial analysis, Qin Yang began to feign selecting Pokémon eggs, slowly pacing among the egg piles.

He only picked up eggs that the system indicated had life, and once he held one, he had the system conduct a deeper probe. As for those that had already been determined to be dead eggs, he ignored them and passed them by.
