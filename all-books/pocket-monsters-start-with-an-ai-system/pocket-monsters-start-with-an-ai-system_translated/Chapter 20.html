Chapter 20: Going Home

Qin Yang stood on the bustling street, his gaze falling involuntarily on the three Pokémon eggs in his hands, thinking to himself:

"It is simply too risky to wander around with these Pokémon eggs. If something goes wrong, the loss would be immense."

He frowned slightly, his expression grave, and carefully pulled the Pokémon eggs closer to his chest, as if this could provide them with more protection.

"These eggs are quite valuable. Eggs of Elite Class Aptitude Caterpie and Weedle can sell for around 100,000 at the breeding house, not to mention that Gym-Level Zubat egg, which is worth more than two or three million."

Thinking of this, a flash of hesitation crossed his eyes,

"I once thought about selling the Zubat egg to make things easier, but I am just a child. Carrying such precious items for trade is like a sheep entering a tiger's den; it might even bring about fatal disaster."

Qin Yang sighed helplessly and shook his head,

"Safety comes first. At worst, I can try my luck at the breeding house's egg-gambling area later to find some Elite Class Aptitude eggs to sell. Although the money is less, the risk is much smaller."

Having made up his mind, Qin Yang quickened his pace, rushing toward his home in the suburbs.

Along the way, he vigilantly observed his surroundings from time to time, and only when the familiar small house appeared before his eyes did he breathe a slight sigh of relief.

Just as he entered the house and placed the purchased Pokémon eggs into the incubator, Sandslash rushed toward the TV like a gust of wind, its little claws excitedly pressing buttons on the remote control.

Seeing this, Qin Yang immediately raised his voice and shouted:

"Sandslash, your training for today is not finished yet. How can you be thinking of watching TV? Go train immediately!"

His voice carried unquestionable authority, his brows tightly furrowed, and he stared sternly at Sandslash.

Hearing Qin Yang's reprimand, Sandslash's originally joyful movements froze instantly, and its whole body slumped like a deflated balloon.

It turned its head and looked at Qin Yang pitifully with those big, watery eyes, making a grievance-filled sound:

"Sand... Sand..." The appearance seemed to be saying,

"Master, I just want to watch for a little while."

Qin Yang crossed his arms, unmoved, and glared fiercely at Sandslash, his gaze leaving no room for compromise.

Sandslash seemed to read Qin Yang's eyes and knew that watching TV was out of the question today, so it had to drag its feet dejectedly toward the yard.

It turned its head three times for every step, its eyes constantly fixed on the TV. This reluctant expression made Qin Yang both annoyed and amused.

Qin Yang watched Sandslash's retreating back, shook his head helplessly, and thought to himself:

"This ※6〓, it only knows how to play. But its potential is not small, so it must be trained well."

He walked to the window, looked at Sandslash in the yard, and began to plan today's training content.

"First, I must exercise its physical fitness. Once its physical fitness improves, it can fight for a long time in battle."

Qin Yang muttered to himself, his eyes focused and determined.

"Then comes the use of moves, especially that divine skill, Swords Dance. I must let it unleash its maximum power."

Thinking of Swords Dance, a hint of excitement couldn't help but surface in Qin Yang's eyes.

"There were three main reasons why I decided to train this Sandshrew."

"First, at that time, I was penniless and couldn't afford good Pokémon at all. This Elite Class Aptitude Sandshrew appeared on its own, like a god-sent opportunity, so naturally, I had to train it well."

"Second, I have studied Pokémon ※32〓 rates. Apart from the Bug-type, rodent-like Pokémon grow very fast. This Sandshrew has good Aptitude, and it only needs to reach Level 22 to evolve into Sandslash, which can allow me to improve my strength in a short time."

"Third, this Sandshrew inherited the precious Swords Dance move."

Qin Yang narrowed his eyes, recalling the experience of inquiring about the value of the Swords Dance move, and couldn't help but click his tongue.

"The move discs sold in the city are priced differently according to the move's power."

"Those with power below 60 are priced under 100,000;"

"Those between 60-90 range from 100,000 to 200,000;"

"Those between 90-120 range from 200,000 to 500,000."

"The shop didn't have any with higher power. From my inquiries, I learned that Swords Dance, a status-changing move that can increase attack by two levels, is worth at least 500,000 coins or more, and it's even priceless and hard to obtain."

"So, the fact that this Sandshrew knows Swords Dance is like finding 500,000 for nothing for me."

"Sandslash, run 20 laps around the yard first to warm up your body!"

Qin Yang opened the window and gave the order loudly to Sandslash in the yard. His voice was loud, powerful, and full of expectation.

Hearing the command, although somewhat reluctant, Sandslash still obediently began to run.

Its short legs alternated quickly. With every lap it ran, its eyes became a bit more determined, as if it were secretly cheering itself on.

Qin Yang nodded with satisfaction, turned back into the house, picked up a training notebook, and began to record.

"After it finishes running, I will have it practice the two moves: Swords Dance and Dig."

"Dig can attack the enemy by surprise, and combined with the attack power boost from Swords Dance, it will surely catch the opponent off guard."

He muttered to himself while writing, his eyes flashing with the light of wisdom.

"However, training cannot be rushed. It must be progressive. Even if I have Power of Nirvana, I still have to avoid Sandslash getting injured."

In the process of recording the training plan, Qin Yang looked out the window from time to time, paying attention to Sandslash's training situation. Seeing Sandslash's hardworking figure, a gratified smile appeared on his face.

Now, let's check Sandslash's information again.

Qin Yang carefully examined the information of these three eggs in the System and pondered secretly. He found that all three eggs possessed inherited moves, which was truly good.

Among them, the Gym-Level Zubat egg inherited two moves, while the Elite Class Aptitude Caterpie and Weedle eggs each inherited one move.

Moreover, judging from the hatching time, the Bug-type Caterpie and Weedle grow faster, taking only three days to hatch, while Zubat takes ten days.

Qin Yang pondered the System's suggestion to use Power of Nirvana to warm the Pokémon eggs.

He knew well that although his Power of Nirvana was powerful, he had never had the experience of using it at the Pokémon egg stage.

However, after careful consideration, he felt that with his level of control over Power of Nirvana, it shouldn't cause any harm to the Pokémon eggs.

Therefore, he planned to try it on the Caterpie and Weedle eggs first to see what kind of magical changes and improvements this Power of Nirvana could bring to these Pokémon eggs.
