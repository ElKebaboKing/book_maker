Chapter 18: Picking Up a Bargain

At this moment, the egg-gambling arena was bustling, with many people harboring hopes of finding a hidden gem weaving through the crowd.

Their identities varied; some, like Qin Yang, came empty-handed, without any auxiliary tools, presumably just to join the fun and experience the unique atmosphere.

Others, however, were well-equipped, with magnifying glasses, flashlights, and other tools readily available, looking exactly like professional archaeologists intently searching for treasure.

Qin Yang strolled leisurely, his gaze inadvertently falling upon a middle-aged man across from him.

The man was holding an egg, which was exquisitely shaped, with a smooth, round, evenly colored, and bright shell.

A confident smile adorned the man’s face as he meticulously examined the egg with a magnifying glass, muttering to himself, seemingly convinced that he had found a rare treasure.

However, Qin Yang, with the aid of his system, knew that this seemingly perfect egg was actually a dead egg. He chuckled to himself, “These so-called experts are nothing special; they have all the gear but can’t distinguish between genuine and fake.”

Qin Yang continued to wander, while simultaneously using his system to scan the Pokémon eggs.

Unconsciously, he had already examined nearly a hundred eggs.

But the results greatly disappointed him; most of the Pokémon corresponding to these eggs had mediocre aptitudes, with common Pokémon like Caterpie, Weedle, Bellsprout, Oddish, and Magikarp appearing endlessly.

“Alas, if I could find a Pokémon egg with Elite aptitude, even if I didn’t train it myself, selling it would yield a considerable profit.”

Qin Yang frowned, sighing helplessly with a face full of disappointment, his shoulders slightly slumped, and his steps becoming slow and heavy.

He looked up at the sky, silently praying, “Oh, Heaven, favor me just once. Grant me a high-quality Pokémon egg. I am willing to pay the price of the author remaining single for ten years.”

Perhaps it was fate’s trick, or a subtle coincidence, but just as Qin Yang was feeling utterly dejected, his gaze was drawn to an egg covered in mud.

He quickly had the system scan it, and upon checking, he was immediately overjoyed.

The system showed that it was actually a Zubat Pokémon egg, and its aptitude was extraordinary, reaching Gym-Level.

Qin Yang was first stunned, then his eyes widened, filled with surprise and shock, his expression changing rapidly and wonderfully.

He was about to exclaim about his incredible luck, but then he suddenly remembered his previous wish and couldn’t help but hesitate: “Oh dear, my luck is great, but what about the author’s ten years of singleness?”

However, this dilemma lasted only for a moment before he decisively reached out and picked up the egg, thinking to himself, “Who cares? Such a good opportunity must not be missed.”

Originally, Qin Yang intended to stop there and leave with this precious egg.

But then he thought of the dozens of eggs yet to be examined, and a hint of expectation arose in his heart: “If there are even better Harvests later, won’t the author have to remain single for even longer? Hehe, just thinking about it is amusing.”

Qin Yang’s lips curved slightly upward, revealing a sly smile, his eyes sparkling with excitement.

So, he roused his spirits and continued to have the system scan the remaining Pokémon eggs.

Hard work pays off; just as the scanning was about to be completed, the system made a new discovery—two Elite Class aptitude Pokémon eggs, and interestingly, these two eggs were for Caterpie and Weedle respectively.

Qin Yang was initially hesitant, as Caterpie and Weedle were too common.

But he quickly realized that although their evolutions, Butterfree and Beedrill, were not very strong, they had one advantage: they consumed very few resources, and combined, they might not even eat as much as Sandslash.

Most importantly, both grew quickly and could easily form a fighting force, providing considerable assistance in the early stages.

Even if they might be replaced by stronger Pokémon later, they were quite suitable as transitional companions.

Moreover, the thought of the author having to remain single for another twenty years made the matter even more delightful.

Finally, Qin Yang happily went to the cashier. He purchased three incubators, carefully placed the three Pokémon eggs inside, and selected some food needed for newborn Pokémon, spending a total of 50,000 yuan.

After paying, Qin Yang held the incubators tightly, as if holding the most precious treasures in the world, his face beaming with happiness and satisfaction, muttering, “Little ones, from now on, come with me to explore this wonderful world.”

With that, he and the somewhat excited Sandslash beside him walked out of the store with light steps, intending to go straight back.

After all, he had bought three eggs today, and in the short term, unless he encountered a very powerful and rare Pokémon, Qin Yang would not buy any more Pokémon.

After all, Pokémon don’t just grow after hatching; they require many resources and frequent training to improve, demanding a lot of time and effort.

On the way back, Qin Yang and Sandslash happened to pass by the three official breeding houses again.

Qin Yang couldn’t help but recall his previous thoughts; he had secretly wondered whether to purchase a few common aptitude Starter Pokémon Pokémon from these official breeding houses and then use his unique Power of Nirvana to enhance their aptitudes.

Qin Yang was well aware of the preciousness and strength of the Starter Pokémon.

Even on the Elite Four Level or Champion-level battle stage, Starter Pokémon could shine in their own right, not appearing inferior in the slightest.

What’s more, they could Mega Evolve, which was simply fantastic.

Furthermore, the price of common aptitude Starter Pokémon was relatively affordable, at most a few hundred thousand, and with Qin Yang’s current financial resources, buying a few was not a problem.

Unfortunately, reality is often not as one wishes.

Qin Yang, being an orphan, simply did not have the qualification to purchase them.

The primary use of Starter Pokémon in these official breeding houses was not for ordinary sales but for reservations.

And only those who had made significant contributions and rendered outstanding services to the Alliance were eligible for reservations.

Generally, from the age of ten, a Trainer needs to pay 100,000 Alliance coins annually for five consecutive years, totaling 500,000, to be able to claim an Elite Class-level Starter Pokémon Pokémon from these official breeding houses.

As for common-level Starter Pokémon, even though there are many every year, they are almost impossible to find on the market.

The Alliance is determined to firmly control the Starter Pokémon Pokémon, so even Starter Pokémon with common aptitudes will never easily flow into the market.

Nowadays, Pokémon like Starter Pokémon, after long-term cultivation by the Alliance, can basically have their aptitudes stabilized at the Elite Class level.

This is also the result of many years of effort by the Alliance and an important force for its foundation.

Of course, if someone could unexpectedly encounter them in the wild, that would truly be a stroke of great luck.

However, under the strict control of the current Alliance, encountering wild Starter Pokémon in the wild is as difficult as finding a needle in a haystack.

Moreover, the Alliance has not completely blocked all avenues for people to obtain Starter Pokémon.

Typically, if someone can become a Gym-Level-level Trainer and has no adverse records in their past experience, then he can apply to join the Alliance.

And the Alliance will generously grant him a precious opportunity to purchase a Gym-Level-level aptitude Starter Pokémon, which is one of the effective means for the Alliance to attract powerful civilian Trainers to join its ranks.

Furthermore, this purchase price will enjoy a substantial discount, basically only half of the normal price.

It should be known that a Gym-Level-level Starter Pokémon normally costs at least five million, and after a 50% discount, it can save two to three million.

This undoubtedly has an irresistible appeal for those civilian Trainers who are not wealthy and have limited funds.
