Chapter 21: Transmitting the Power of Nirvana to the Elf Egg

As the last rays of the setting sun quietly faded, Qin Yang and Sandslash concluded their day of rigorous training.

Sweat beaded on Qin Yang's forehead, but he didn't rest, immediately setting about treating Sandslash.

He closed his eyes slightly, concentrating, trying to guide the mysterious Power of Nirvana within him.

His hands slowly approached Sandslash, and a strange light gradually emanated from his palms, like agile threads, seeping into Sandslash's body.

However, today Qin Yang only opened five of the small channels within Sandslash, reserving the rest of his Power of Nirvana to nourish the Pokémon eggs tonight.

He wanted to see the effect, so he saved some Power of Nirvana today.

During this period, Qin Yang had been closely observing every detail of the training process, and his miraculous system, like a loyal assistant, meticulously recorded and tallied various data.

Based on the current rate of opening eight channels within Sandslash daily, if all goes well, the goal should be achieved in approximately two months.

All the small channels would be opened.

But Qin Yang knew that later on, he would not only have to allocate some Power of Nirvana to nourish the Pokémon eggs but also new Pokémon that hatched would need to be cultivated; thus, completing it within three months would already be quite good.

Once achieved, according to previous estimates, Sandslash's aptitude would very likely be elevated to Quasi-Elite Four Level, so he would not have to worry about Sandslash's aptitude improvement for the next one or two years.

After the treatment for Sandslash was complete, Qin Yang softly said to him:

"Sandslash, that's enough for today. Go rest."

Sandslash blinked his big, round eyes; he vaguely felt that today's treatment time seemed shorter than usual, and his heart was full of doubt.

However, as soon as he thought about watching his favorite TV show, he instantly cast his doubts aside, excitedly calling out:

"Sandslash, Sandslash!"

Then he happily ran towards the direction of the television. Qin Yang hadn't realized that this Sandslash was a complete TV fanatic.

After Qin Yang and Sandslash had dinner, Sandslash comfortably nestled on the sofa, his eyes glued to the TV screen, looking utterly content.

Qin Yang returned to his room alone, his gaze immediately drawn to the three incubators on the table.

He approached the table and unhesitatingly picked up the Caterpie egg, carefully placing it in front of him.

He took a deep breath, trying to calm himself down, as this was his first attempt to transmit Power of Nirvana to a Pokémon egg, and he was inevitably a little nervous.

He was worried that if he accidentally harmed the Pokémon inside the egg, leading to a handicapped Pokémon upon hatching, it would be troublesome. Whether to continue training it or release it, neither was the outcome he wanted, so he had to proceed with caution.

Qin Yang estimated that after treating and clearing Sandslash's channels today, he still had about 40% of his Power of Nirvana left.

He pondered for a moment, then carefully divided this 40% of Power of Nirvana into four hundred equal parts.

Next, he very carefully transmitted one portion of Power of Nirvana towards the Pokémon egg.

Qin Yang had originally thought that the Pokémon egg would have difficulty absorbing energy, but to his surprise, as soon as this portion of Power of Nirvana was injected, it was quickly absorbed by the egg before he could even react.

After all, Qin Yang was only a mortal, and his reaction speed couldn't compare to the system's.

Fortunately, the system detected the situation in time and informed Qin Yang:

The Power of Nirvana had just been absorbed by the egg, and the egg's vitality and aptitude seemed to have improved slightly, with no adverse effects.

Seeing the system's analysis, Qin Yang knew that there shouldn't be any major problems.

So, he plucked up his courage and transmitted nine portions of Power of Nirvana to the Pokémon egg at once.

This time, it took a little while for the Pokémon egg to absorb the energy.

Through the transmitted Power of Nirvana, Qin Yang saw the scene inside the egg: a softly glowing light cluster, surrounded by many channels, and a faint outline of a Caterpie slowly forming within the light cluster.

Qin Yang withdrew his thoughts, thinking that the light cluster and channels inside this egg seemed similar to those in Sandslash's body. It seemed this Caterpie was not far from hatching.

After the Pokémon egg absorbed these nine portions of Power of Nirvana, the system prompted that everything was normal, and its vitality and aptitude had further improved.

At this point, the system sent a message, informing Qin Yang that Pokémon eggs absorb Power of Nirvana more easily and improve their aptitude faster in their embryonic stage.

Qin Yang observed quietly for a while and found that the egg's condition was still good. He was completely relieved now and, without further hesitation, began slowly transmitting Power of Nirvana to the Pokémon egg.

Qin Yang's Power of Nirvana appeared to others like a vivid red glow.

He slowly transmitted it, and soon, after the Caterpie egg absorbed about one hundred portions of Power of Nirvana, it reached a saturated state.

The system prompted that this egg seemed to be "full" and could not absorb any more today, needing to rest.

Qin Yang checked and confirmed that the egg's condition was normal, then gently placed it back into the incubator.

However, the system immediately told Qin Yang another piece of news: Caterpie's incubation period had suddenly changed to four days.

Qin Yang was momentarily stunned, full of confusion, not knowing why the incubation time had lengthened.

Although he was puzzled, as long as the egg was fine, he no longer dwelled on it.

Subsequently, Qin Yang used the same method to transmit one hundred portions of Power of Nirvana to the Weedle egg.

The result was identical to the Caterpie egg: the Weedle egg also reached saturation after absorbing one hundred portions of Power of Nirvana, and its incubation period also changed to four days.

Seeing the same changes in both, Qin Yang gradually understood that this might not be a bad thing; on the contrary, it could be a positive change.

However, a hint of worry still lingered in his heart, so he decided to observe carefully for one night first.

If everything was calm and no abnormalities occurred tomorrow, then he planned to also transmit Power of Nirvana to the Zubat egg.

Although using the Caterpie and Weedle eggs for this experiment might seem emotionally inappropriate,

Qin Yang also knew in his heart that this might be an excellent opportunity for them to change their fate and achieve a comeback.

For these two Pokémon, how could it not be a rare and precious opportunity that is hard to come by?

After the transmission here, Qin Yang observed for a while and found that everything was normal. Qin Yang also breathed a sigh of relief.

Although the system had previously said that Power of Nirvana could be used for nourishment, he still had no idea what the actual situation would be like.

Now that everything was normal, he felt a little relieved, but he still needed to see if there would be any problems tomorrow. If not, he would continue everything; if there were any adverse situations, he would stop immediately.
