Chapter 15: Tangled

Chapter 15 Tangled

Leaving the Team Rocket breeding facility, Qin Yang took a Poké Ball from his waist and gently tossed it. Amidst a flash of light, Sandslash appeared beside him.

As soon as Sandslash emerged, he excitedly looked around, continuously making "Sandslash! Sandslash!" crys, his short limbs constantly bouncing, and the spikes on his body slightly swaying with his movements, emitting a faint "kaka" sound.

Qin Yang looked at Sandslash's appearance and smiled helplessly. He squatted down, stroked Sandslash's head, and said,

"Alright, alright, let's go for a walk together."

Sandslash seemed to understand Qin Yang's words and affectionately rubbed his head against Qin Yang's hand. Although the spikes pricked Qin Yang a bit, he did not pull away.

As Qin Yang walked, he pondered in his mind:

"The Pokémon in official breeding facilities have all undergone strict testing by the Alliance's specialized machines. It's too difficult to find one with incorrect test results."

A hint of disappointment flickered in his eyes, and his steps became somewhat heavy.

"Private breeding facilities, however, are different. Although they also have qualification testing equipment, they certainly can't compare to the Alliance's professional standards. Maybe I can find a good prospect that was overlooked."

A glimmer of hope reignited in Qin Yang's eyes, and his steps quickened slightly, a light of anticipation sparkling within them.

"Although I have Power of Nirvana to improve a Pokémon's aptitude, I also want to find one with a higher starting point. If the aptitude is too poor, it takes too much time to transform.

Currently, just transforming Sandslash consumes a lot of energy and takes a lot of time.

If I add another one with even worse aptitude, it would require even more Power of Nirvana and time, which would also delay my own development."

Qin Yang shook his head helplessly, a bitter expression on his face. He touched his pocket, feeling the limited amount of money inside.

"And I also have to consider the price. My current funds can't withstand much. I can't afford anything too expensive."

There was a hint of frustration in Qin Yang's voice. He lowered his head, looking at the road beneath his feet, lost in thought.

As he walked, Qin Yang's eyes suddenly lit up. He stopped, an excited expression on his face:

"If all else fails, how about trying to catch a Magikarp by the river? There are so many of them; I can pick one with better aptitude, and with the help of Power of Nirvana, maybe it can evolve."

Qin Yang's voice unconsciously rose a few octaves, his eyes filled with anticipation and longing.

"Although I haven't tried it, it's worth a shot. After all, it doesn't cost money, just takes a bit of time."

Qin Yang muttered to himself. He raised his head, looking into the distance, as if he could already see himself battling with an evolved Magikarp.

However, this matter would have to wait. Since he had finally come to the city, he should first check out these breeding facilities.

Qin Yang and Sandslash strolled leisurely down the street. The surrounding clamor seemed to be isolated by an invisible barrier, and his thoughts were entirely immersed in the dilemma of choosing a Pokémon.

Qin Yang's brows were tightly furrowed, his eyes filled with entanglement and contemplation. His steps became sluggish and slow, and he would occasionally stop and stand still, lost in thought.

"Pidgey, this little guy indeed has many advantages after evolving into Pidgeot."

Qin Yang murmured to himself, his voice so soft that only he could hear it.

He tilted his head slightly, his eyes half-closed, as if sketching the majestic image of Pidgeot soaring in the sky in his mind.

"Pidgeot's combat ability is considerable, and Mega Evolution is a major highlight. Crucially, it can help me quickly escape danger, making it an ideal partner."

At this point, a flicker of anticipation flashed in Qin Yang's eyes, but it vanished in an instant.

"But, that price... He didn't know before that an Elite Class-level Pidgey would be so expensive. Ordinary ones, no matter how expensive, are only thirty to fifty thousand.

But at the Elite Class level, the price directly increased tenfold. Qin Yang currently only has a little over four hundred thousand in total, so it's impossible for him to spend so much money."

He sighed helplessly, a long and heavy sigh, as if carrying all his troubles. His shoulders drooped slightly, and he looked somewhat disheartened.

Qin Yang also knew that the reason Elite Class-level Pidgey were so expensive was mainly because only Elite Class-level Pidgey had the potential to evolve into Pidgeot.

If it couldn't evolve into Pidgeot, then Pidgey would not be of much use. After evolving into Pidgeot, it is not only an excellent flying mount but also the preferred choice for survival in wilderness exploration.

Moreover, Elite Class-level Pidgey, even if they appear in a breeding facility, are usually bought quickly.

If Qin Yang really wanted a Pidgey, he would either spend thirty to fifty thousand to buy one with ordinary aptitude,

and slowly improve its aptitude with Power of Nirvana himself. Although it might take some time, this method is more cost-effective.

Otherwise, he could go to the wild to catch one himself. He could use the system to scan for aptitude, which would be much more convenient than what hunters do. However, the wild is so dangerous, and it's not that easy to go there.

Sandslash watched Qin Yang curiously from the side. He didn't understand why his master was so troubled.

He gently tugged at Qin Yang's pant leg with his small paw, making "Sandslash, Sandslash" crys, his eyes full of confusion and concern.

Qin Yang looked down at Sandslash, forced a smile, squatted down, and gently patted his head, saying,

"Little guy, I'm troubled about choosing a new partner."

Qin Yang's thoughts drifted to Zubat again.

"Zubat, in the anime, you can see them everywhere, Team Rocket carries them. Although they are common, don't be fooled by appearances."

Qin Yang frowned slightly, a hint of hesitation and struggle in his eyes.

"It can evolve three times into Crobat, and its base stats are not low, at 535 points. It's not like a typical Starter Pokémon; it's quite good. If it can successfully evolve, it would definitely be a powerful assistant."

Qin Yang's fingers unconsciously rubbed his chin, his expression appearing somewhat serious.

"But this friendship evolution is really a big problem; it all depends on luck. If it doesn't go smoothly and gets stuck at the Golbat stage, that would be terrible.

And it also needs to suck blood, which is troublesome, even though those stores provide it, it's still inconvenient."

Qin Yang stood up and paced back and forth, his steps hurried, continuously muttering:

"How to choose, how to choose..."

"And Ponyta..."

Qin Yang's eyes suddenly lit up, as if he had caught a glimmer of light in the darkness.

"Rapidash's handsome appearance and powerful strength, just thinking about it makes me excited."

A look of longing appeared on Qin Yang's face, and his voice unconsciously rose a few octaves.

"And it can also be a mount, helping me escape quickly, and its combat power is also good.

Simply perfect."

But very quickly, his eyes dimmed again.

"It's just that the price is really too expensive; I simply can't afford it."

Qin Yang smiled bitterly and shook his head, his face full of helplessness and self-mockery.

Qin Yang stopped in the middle of the street. Passersby hurried by, but he stood like a frozen statue. After a long while, he slowly released his tightly clasped arms, and his gaze gradually became resolute.

"Forget it, there's no use thinking about it anymore. Maybe I'll find a suitable one as I browse."

Qin Yang took a deep breath, as if bracing himself. He gently patted Sandslash's back, his tone carrying a hint of determination.

"Come on, let's go check out this breeding facility."

With that, he straightened his back and continued forward with firm steps. Sandslash followed closely behind him.

The figures of one person and one Pokémon gradually disappeared down the street, entering the breeding facility known as the House of Flying.
