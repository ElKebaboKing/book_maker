Chapter 13 Recent Plans

After the training, Qin Yang brought the exhausted Sandslash back into the house.

Sandslash's limbs were weak, its steps heavy, as if every step used all its strength.

Its eyes were half-closed, revealing deep fatigue, and it constantly gasped, making "huffing and puffing" sounds.

Qin Yang gently raised his hand and slowly injected Power of Nirvana into Sandslash's body.

Sandslash's tense body gradually relaxed; it comfortably narrowed its eyes slightly, letting out a contented "woo woo" sound from its throat, as if all its aches and pains were slowly dissipating under the soothing energy.

A moment later, Qin Yang withdrew his hand and said softly, "Alright, Sandslash, that's all for today's training."

Sandslash slowly opened its eyes, a hint of gratitude in its gaze, and nodded gently.

In the evening, after Qin Yang was busy in the kitchen, he brought out a simple dinner.

After eating, the man and the Pokémon huddled together in the room watching TV.

The small courtyard Qin Yang rented was close to Viridian Forest, a bit out of the way, and though not small, it was very affordable.

Although the TV programs in the house were not quite to Qin Yang's liking, the advantage was that there were no commercials and no membership required, so he could watch freely.

Sandslash, however, was incredibly excited; it lay on the carpet in front of the TV, its eyes fixed on the screen, its ears twitching occasionally, completely absorbed in the TV drama playing.

It was a story of an orphan growing up and adventuring with a Sandshrew; Sandslash seemed deeply captivated,

its eyes sparkling with curiosity and excitement, occasionally letting out a few low "squeaks" as the plot developed, as if interacting with the characters in the show.

Its tail wagged gently, but its body remained as still as possible, afraid of accidentally poking Qin Yang beside it.

Qin Yang sat beside it, but his thoughts drifted elsewhere.

His brows were tightly furrowed, his eyes filled with worry and confusion; his thoughts were tangled like a mess, and he was struggling to ponder his future path.

He knew that he was almost 15 years old, and according to custom, it was time for him to embark on a journey.

But when he thought about his own situation, his heart felt like it was weighed down by a heavy stone.

"The outside world is definitely not as easy as it's portrayed in the anime; those newcomers who receive their Pokémon and happily set off on their journey are simply too naive."

Qin Yang mumbled softly, a hint of bitterness in his voice,

"If you go out to explore with a newly caught, unfamiliar, and weak Pokémon without any protective measures, what's the difference between that and seeking death?"

He couldn't help but recall the scene in Pallet Town from the anime; among those Trainers who set off together, only Ash persevered, and even Professor Professor Oak's grandson changed careers to become a researcher.

Although being a researcher is good, he is not a true Trainer after all.

"How can it be so easy to become a strong person?

Although there are many strong individuals in the Alliance who have grown slowly through travel, compared to the vast number of Trainers, only a very small minority succeed."

Qin Yang sighed helplessly, his face full of frustration and dejection, his shoulders slumping slightly.

Qin Yang knew in his heart that he was 15 years old and theoretically could travel, but he had neither abundant resources nor powerful Pokémon to escort him.

"If I go out so recklessly, I will definitely encounter all sorts of accidents, and I might even lose my life."

He murmured to himself, a hint of fear in his eyes.

But then he thought that he now not only had a system but also awakened a new ability,

"Staying hidden here forever isn't a solution; I have to take the initiative and seek opportunities."

Qin Yang gritted his teeth, a flash of determination in his eyes.

After all, Viridian City made him worried.

"This is Giovanni's stronghold; I'm so weak now, it's a blessing he hasn't noticed me. If I'm exposed one day, then disaster will strike."

Fine beads of sweat appeared on Qin Yang's forehead; he wiped them away with his hand, his palm covered in sweat.

Therefore, he understood that even if he didn't want to leave, for his own safety, he had to find a way to escape Viridian City as soon as possible and find a safe place to hide.

Pallet Town was originally his ideal place.

"How good it is there; Trainers who come out can receive Starter Pokémon with good aptitudes, which is a special welfare the Alliance gives to Professor Professor Oak.

And with Professor Professor Oak, a former Champion, protecting it, even Giovanni wouldn't dare to provoke Professor Professor Oak now."

A hint of longing appeared in Qin Yang's eyes, but it quickly dimmed.

"But I'm just an orphan with no power or influence; how could they accept me?

Professor Professor Oak isn't like in the anime, someone you can just casually meet; he's a prominent figure in the Alliance. If everyone could go see him, how would he have time for research?

I probably wouldn't even get close to his research institute before being chased away."

Qin Yang smiled bitterly and shook his head, his face full of self-mockery.

After repeated deliberation, Qin Yang finally cleared his thoughts; he knew he had three crucial things to do next.

"First, I must do everything possible to improve Sandslash's strength and aptitude. It's my most capable partner now; only when it's strong can I have more security."

Qin Yang clenched his fists, his eyes filled with determination, as if cheering himself on.

"Secondly, I need to catch new Pokémon.

I can't always rely solely on Sandslash; if it gets defeated in battle, I'll be completely defenseless.

Considering my economic situation, I can't afford expensive Pokémon; I need to find those with low training costs that can quickly evolve into combat-ready forms.

It would be even better if I could find a Pokémon that can quickly take me away from dangerous places, so I can be safer on my journey."

Qin Yang thought while gently tapping the table, his eyes revealing a hint of anticipation and longing.

"Finally, and most urgently, is to quickly leave this dangerous place, Viridian City.

No matter how many difficulties lie ahead, I can no longer hesitate; I must bravely take this step."

Qin Yang stood up, took a deep breath, straightened his back, his eyes filled with determination and courage for the future.

He knew that his destiny had to be rewritten by himself; even if the road ahead was full of thorns, he would never shrink back.

Qin Yang looked at Sandslash, who was happily watching TV beside him, and thought that he would be almost fifteen years old in about three months.

During this time, he would follow the plan he just made: first, diligently train Sandslash, and while training, continuously improve its aptitude to help it grow faster.

Also, he would visit more breeding houses to see if there were any suitable Pokémon for him. Since he decided to continue raising a Pokémon, he had to start early.

He couldn't procrastinate, otherwise, when he set off, if his strength was too low and he encountered danger, would the Pokémon protect him, or would he protect the Pokémon?

The rest of the matters could be dealt with slowly. After understanding these things, Qin Yang felt a bit more relaxed, and he accompanied Sandslash to watch TV and relax.

He would be even busier in the future, and such leisure would be rare.
