Chapter 19: Limitations of the Quartz Conference

In this era where the Alliance holds absolute power, Qin Yang, as a commoner Trainer, knew that rising to prominence was harder than ascending to the heavens.

High-level training methods above Gym-Level-Level, exceptionally talented Pokémon, and various rare resources were all tightly controlled by the Alliance, forming a near-monopoly.

Qin Yang understood that if he didn't have that heaven-defying system and hadn't mastered the miraculous Power of Nirvana,

the hope of breaking through these layers of blockade and succeeding was minuscule, almost a pipe dream.

He sometimes couldn't help but fantasize that if he were truly in such a situation, he might indeed choose to join Team Rocket.

At this moment, Qin Yang even felt a hint of understanding towards Team Rocket.

Team Rocket, led by Boss Giovanni, certainly committed many evil deeds in the world and would remain a major thorn in the Alliance's side in the future, like a sharp刺 deeply embedded in the Alliance's heart.

But it must be admitted that the existence of Team Rocket indeed brought unprecedented impact and change to the somewhat rigid and corrupt Kanto League.

They opened up a whole new path of survival for those commoner Trainers who were desperate and at their wits' end under the Alliance system, offering them a different choice.

For this alone, Qin Yang felt a genuine admiration for Boss Giovanni's extraordinary courage and boldness.

It's just a pity that Team Rocket ultimately went astray, repeatedly losing to opponents with invincible protagonist halos, which is truly regrettable.

In fact, Qin Yang had considered leaving the Kanto region.

He had countless times pondered that the Kanto League's control was too strong; perhaps by going to other regions where the Alliance's power was relatively weaker, he could break free and gain more development opportunities.

Qin Yang was confident in his abilities, firmly believing that as long as he changed environments, with his strength and wisdom, he would thrive, rise rapidly, and forge a brilliant future for himself.

However, reason told him that it was far from the time to make such a major decision.

No matter where the future path led, if he truly wanted to go to other regions to start a new life, the top priority was to fully enhance his strength.

After all, in this world full of unknown dangers and challenges, without enough strength to protect himself, even daily travel would be fraught with peril, let alone pursuing dreams and distant horizons.

Furthermore, Qin Yang had a strong desire in his heart, which was to participate in the Indigo Plateau Conference.

He longed to step onto that renowned competition stage, prove himself with his strength, and see how far he could go on this thorny path.

Since coming to this magical world, if he didn't even participate in such a grand event as the Indigo Plateau Conference, he would surely be left with indelible regret and unwillingness.

Moreover, the Indigo Plateau Conference has strict participation rules, only allowing Trainers to send out Pokémon caught within three years, and there is a clear age limit for participants, not exceeding eighteen years old.

It is precisely because of this that, although the Indigo Plateau Conference is well-known and highly anticipated, to some extent, it is more like a “novice trial” held for young Trainers.

Those truly standing at the peak of strength rarely participate.

Because of this, Qin Yang's fighting spirit burned brightly, and he became even more determined to participate and test his skills, as this competition was, to some extent, relatively fair.

However, in this Pokémon world, Qin Yang was well aware of some hidden special tricks.

Those powerful large families often had unique methods for raising Pokémon.

They would carefully select Pokémon with extraordinary potential when their children were young, and then devote all their efforts to nurturing them, but they were not in a hurry to catch them into a Poké Ball.

Instead, they would patiently wait until the children in the family reached the age of fifteen before arranging for them to formally catch these Pokémon that had already grown.

This way, a startling scene could very likely occur:

a novice who had just entered the Trainer profession and only caught a Pokémon a minute ago could possess a powerful partner with Gym-Level-Level or even Quasi-Elite Four Level strength.

Such things were not groundless; they had actually happened in the past history of the Indigo Plateau Conference.

It was a highly anticipated Indigo Plateau Conference.

On the field, a Trainer who was only 15 years old and had just started his journey not long ago, to everyone's astonishment, directly sent out an Elite Four Level Pokémon.

As soon as this Pokémon appeared, it was like a dazzling giant star, exuding a chillingly powerful aura.

It swept across the field, and wherever it went, opponents were as vulnerable as ants, dominating the entire arena with absolute superiority, leaving other participants far behind.

After this incident, it was like a bombshell dropped into a calm lake, instantly stirring up huge waves throughout the entire Alliance.

The public discussed it animatedly, and various criticisms surged towards the Alliance like a tide.

The Alliance's reputation also suffered a heavy blow, and its public image instantly plummeted.

The Alliance's high-ranking officials were in a frantic state, urgently holding a meeting to discuss countermeasures.

After a heated discussion, they finally decided to introduce a series of strict new regulations specifically aimed at restricting the participation of large family members in League Conferences.

It was clearly stipulated that before the age of 18, large family members were strictly prohibited from directly catching Pokémon that had been raised since childhood to compete in League Conferences, and an extremely crucial additional restriction was added:

the age of the participating Pokémon must be within five years.

Once a Pokémon exceeded this age limit, its eligibility would be revoked.

The promulgation and implementation of this series of new regulations were like strong shackles, severely restricting the behavior of those large family members who shamelessly used family resources to cheat in competitions.

Since then, there has been no more farce in the League Conference where Elite Four Level Pokémon arbitrarily swept the field with their overwhelming strength.

Under such a strict set of rules, the current League Conference presents a completely different competitive landscape.

Due to the strict age limit for participating Pokémon, unless the Trainer himself possesses extraordinary talent,

even with the advantage of pre-training Pokémon, within a short period of five years, a Pokémon can at most grow to Quasi-Elite Four Level strength.

And those truly gifted and exceptionally talented prodigies, their sights are often not limited to the League Conference.

And even if they do participate, they would not resort to cheating, which is an unsavory tactic.

For them, the League Conference stage is a bit too small; they prefer to participate in higher-level, more challenging Elite Four Four challenge matches.

There, they can have peak showdowns with true powerhouses and compete, instead of vying for the Champion's throne in the League Conference with a group of opponents whose strongest are only Quasi-Elite Four Level.

As for the current League Conference, the existence of various restrictions makes the entry threshold clear.

Generally speaking, as long as a Trainer has six Superior-Level Pokémon, they basically have the strength to pass the preliminary rounds and successfully enter the top 64.

However, if one wants to continue advancing in subsequent matches, without a Gym-Level-Level Pokémon as a solid backing, it is highly likely that they will unfortunately stop at the top 16 hurdle.

And to ultimately lift the Champion trophy, which symbolizes supreme glory, one must have at least one powerful Quasi-Elite Four Level Pokémon to break through the fierce and cruel competition and emerge victorious.

Qin Yang also secretly set a grand goal for himself: he was determined to win a Champion title in the League Conference before he turned 18.

However, looking at the three Pokémon Eggs he held tightly in his hand, and then at the cheerful, carefree Sandslash bouncing beside him, he couldn't help but fall into deep thought.

His heart was full of doubt and anticipation, wondering which of these partners would be able to accompany him all the way through the challenges and ultimately stand together on that podium symbolizing supreme glory.
