During this period, Qin Yang prepared a large number of tonics for Sandslash, so that despite Sandslash's exceptionally hard daily training, his body still showed a relatively significant increase.

In the world of Pokémon, although it's not true that the larger the body, the stronger the combat power, for most Pokémon, a larger body often demonstrates greater strength in battle.

Qin Yang calculated in his mind that he must cultivate Sandslash to be as strong and tall as possible.

After all, Sandslash excels in close-range combat, and its physical attack and physical defense capabilities are relatively outstanding, so to fully utilize this advantage, a large physique is indispensable.

If Sandslash were small, he would inevitably be at a disadvantage in close combat and suffer many losses.

Of course, if Sandslash took the special attack route, acting as a long-range attacking 'mage', perhaps he wouldn't need to be so fixated on his physique.

However, his innate special attack ability is poor, so Qin Yang could only focus on cultivating his physical attack.

Recently, the moves Sandslash practiced the most were Swords Dance and Metal Claw.

Although there was no one to guide him during the training process, and he could only explore on his own, fortunately, Qin Yang had the system's assistance.

The system can analyze, summarize, and categorize Sandslash's training situation; as long as he keeps practicing, the system can compile more effective and efficient training methods, preventing him from going down the wrong training path.

Qin Yang sometimes couldn't help but fantasize that once these were organized in the future, they might become an excellent training secret manual, and perhaps he could even earn a sum of money from it and embark on a new wonderful experience.

Time flew by like a white steed, and in a flash, the second day quietly arrived.

On this day, Sandslash was training alone outside the house, while Qin Yang was not accompanying him as usual.

At this moment, Qin Yang's full attention was focused on the Pokémon eggs in the two incubators inside the house, because Caterpie and Weedle were about to Hatching, and he didn't want to miss this precious moment.

In the world of Pokémon, there is a widely known common sense: the Pokémon born from a Pokémon egg will often become the person they are most close to at first sight.

Thereafter, whether it's cooperation during training or cultivating deep emotional bonds, everything will become smoother, and unparalleled默契 can be demonstrated when commanding battles.

It is precisely because of this that, under normal circumstances, Pokémon of the same aptitude, the price of Pokémon eggs is often half higher than that of juvenile Pokémon.

However, a hint of doubt arose in Qin Yang's mind: Why does the Alliance primarily provide juvenile Pokémon?

He pondered secretly, feeling that there were roughly two reasons.

Firstly, not everyone has the ability and experience to properly care for newly hatched Pokémon, and accidents are extremely prone to occur during this period, which the Alliance must be concerned about.

Secondly, it still comes down to the aptitude issue.

As previously understood, the difficulty of aptitude testing during the Pokémon egg stage is extremely high, and it is difficult to accurately determine a Pokémon's aptitude without an extremely detailed testing process.

Moreover, even if tested, the probability of deviation in the results is quite high.

As a result, the Alliance cannot precisely know the true aptitude of the Pokémon eggs.

If Pokémon eggs sold hatch Pokémon with low aptitude, it will inevitably have a negative impact on the Alliance's reputation; and if Pokémon with extraordinary aptitude hatch, the Alliance will probably regret it endlessly.

Neither situation is what the Alliance wants to see.

Therefore, the Alliance usually does not actively sell Pokémon eggs, but mainly sells juvenile Pokémon, which is a relatively appropriate choice for all parties.

Of course, this is merely Qin Yang's personal conjecture, and as for whether it is accurate, he himself cannot assert.

However, he believes that even if other factors exist, they should be roughly similar to his conjecture.

Just as Qin Yang was immersed in thought, suddenly, a soft light illuminated from within the incubator.

He snapped back to reality, his gaze instantly drawn to the shimmering light of Caterpie and Weedle's Pokémon eggs.

The bright glow was like tiny lanterns, illuminating the surrounding space.

Qin Yang's heart swelled with joy; he knew this was a sign that the Pokémon were about to hatch.

He quickly stepped forward, his movements gentle and Swift, carefully lifting the two Pokémon eggs from the incubator.

At this moment, the light on the eggs grew even more dazzling, as if a brilliant magic was brewing within.

Qin Yang's eyes were filled with anticipation and excitement as he stared intently at the Pokémon eggs, even unconsciously slowing his breathing, afraid of disturbing the little lives about to be born.

As the light continued to flicker and dance, Caterpie's Pokémon egg was the first to show movement.

A tiny crack appeared on the eggshell, and then, like a spiderweb, the crack rapidly spread.

Under Qin Yang's unwavering gaze, a small Caterpie slowly poked its head out of the egg.

Its emerald green body wiggled slightly, and the antennae on its head gently swayed, as if curiously exploring this brand new world.

Its eyes were round and black, sparkling with pure innocence, and upon seeing Qin Yang, it seemed to pause for a moment, then slowly wiggled towards him, its tiny mouth opening and closing as if making a silent greeting.

Almost simultaneously, Weedle's Pokémon egg also began to hatch.

With a soft 'crack,' Weedle used its hard head to break through the eggshell.

Its body was pale yellow with black stripes, and the sharp horn on its back glinted coldly in the light.

After struggling out of the egg, Weedle first spun around a few times in place, seemingly adapting to its new environment, then also crawled towards Qin Yang.

Its movements were a bit clumsy but full of life and vitality.

Qin Yang looked at the two newly born Pokémon in front of him, a joyful smile on his face.

He gently extended his hand, allowing Caterpie and Weedle to slowly climb onto his palm.

Feeling their soft bodies wriggling in his palm, a warmth and joy surged in Qin Yang's heart.

He knew that from this moment on, a special connection had been established between him and these two little Pokémon.

Qin Yang looked at the Caterpie and Weedle in his palm with great joy, but he was genuinely surprised.

He had originally thought that even if newly born Pokémon were small, they should be roughly the same size, after all, normal Caterpie and Weedle can grow to about 0.3 meters tall.

But these two little guys in front of him, curled up together, couldn't even reach 0.1 meters in height, they were simply miniature versions of Caterpie and Weedle.

Their bodies looked extremely tender; Caterpie's emerald green color was like newly sprouted shoots, and the black stripes on Weedle's pale yellow body appeared fainter, as if a diluted ink painting.

However, Qin Yang quickly calmed down, and he swiftly activated the system to detect the two small Pokémon.

When he saw that the system showed their aptitude was indeed Gym-Level, his suspended heart finally settled, and a relieved smile appeared on his face.

"It's fine if they're small; there's plenty of room for growth in the future."

Qin Yang murmured to himself, his eyes filled with anticipation.
