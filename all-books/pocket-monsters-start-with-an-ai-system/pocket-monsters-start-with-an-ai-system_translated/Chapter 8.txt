Chapter 8 Qualification Improvement

Now, when Qin Yang checked Sandslash’s information again:

【Sandslash】

Category: Rodent Pokémon

Level: 23 (Normal)

Aptitude: Gym-Level

Attribute: Ground

Height: 1.1 m (Average 1.0 m)

Weight: 32.0 kg (Average 29.5 kg)

Ability: Sand Veil (When in a sandstorm, its evasion rate increases)

Skills: Scratch, Defense Curl, Poison Sting, Sand Attack, Rollout, Fury Cutter, Rapid Spin, Bulldoze, Swift, Fury Swipes, Dig, Swords Dance (Inherited), Metal Claw, Rock Tomb

Training Method: It is recommended to supplement more energy, as it needs energy replenishment after evolution.

It is recommended to increase Rollout skill training, the specific training method is as follows...

It is recommended to increase speed training, the specific training method is as follows...

At this moment, Qin Yang also realized that the biggest change in Sandslash was not its sudden evolution, nor its one-level increase in strength, nor the two new skills it gained.

Instead, Sandslash’s aptitude had evolved from Elite Class to Gym-Level. This discovery made Qin Yang uncontrollably excited.

One must know that this was an evolution of aptitude, a miracle akin to defying fate!

How incredible was this!

Although he had collected a lot of information at school before, there were also some methods introduced to change a Pokémon’s aptitude.

However, they were all vague and perfunctory descriptions, with no specific operational methods, only repeatedly emphasizing that changing aptitude was extremely difficult.

Generally, only when a Pokémon evolves, by using some special potion or special items, is it possible to improve its aptitude.

That is why Pokémon with three stages of evolution are more valuable.

When Qin Yang saw this at the time, he naively thought that if he could obtain a method for aptitude evolution, wouldn’t he become rich?

But later, after Qin Yang’s in-depth understanding, he realized that methods for changing aptitude were extremely precious and rare.

Only ancient Gyms of the Alliance, and those deep-rooted large families, possessed such methods, and they generally never passed them on.

This was the fundamental reason for the long-term prosperity of those large families; how could they easily tell others the methods?

At most, they would sell some potion that changed aptitude, although it only targeted specific Pokémon and still had a chance of failure,

But each time it appeared, it would cause a frantic scramble among people, which shows how difficult and precious aptitude changes were.

Of course, this change in aptitude cannot be infinitely improved.

Generally, it can only increase aptitude to Quasi-Elite Four Level at most. Going further up would require ten times the resources, and there’s a high chance of failure, which would not be cost-effective.

Therefore, although those large families might not lack Pokémon with Quasi-Elite Four Level aptitude, Pokémon with true Elite Four Level aptitude were still extremely scarce.

Qin Yang stood rooted to the spot, his body trembling slightly, his eyes fixed intently on the Sandslash before him, his heart swept by a storm.

His thoughts, like wild horses, galloped freely among various possibilities.

“Could it be… could it be that my special ability brought all this about?”

Qin Yang’s voice carried a hint of imperceptible tremor, as if he was asking himself, and also as if he was seeking the truth from this mysterious world.

His brows were tightly furrowed, his eyes filled with a fervent glow of tension and anticipation intertwined, and fine beads of sweat seeped from his forehead, glistening faintly in the dim cave.

He slowly raised his hand and subconsciously touched his chin, his fingers gently caressing, as if this could help him sort out his chaotic thoughts.

“Sandslash completed its evolution and improved its aptitude in such a short time, and no other special events occurred during this period.

It can’t be because it was injured by Beedrill, can it?”

Qin Yang murmured softly, speaking very quickly, as if afraid that his voice would disturb the chain of reasoning forming in his mind.

He began to pace back and forth in the small space, each step carrying his inner anxiety and unease, his feet making slight sounds on the ground, which were exceptionally clear in the silence.

“The only difference is that I previously cleared its energy circulation channels, which significantly changed both the speed and total amount of energy flow.

Could it really be because of this?”

Qin Yang suddenly stopped, his eyes widening abruptly, as if he had discovered a new continent, a look of joyful surprise appearing on his face.

But this joy was instantly replaced by a hint of worry,

“However, is this aptitude improvement truly only achievable during evolution? If so, then this ability, though precious, is not invincible. But if…”

Qin Yang’s breathing quickened, his hands unconsciously clenched into fists, and the veins on his arms protruded slightly,

“If simply clearing the energy channels can improve aptitude, then haven’t I mastered the divine brush that can rewrite a Pokémon’s fate!”

He exclaimed excitedly, his voice echoing in the empty cave, carrying an uncontrollable thrill and anticipation,

His eyes sparkled with a fervent light, as if he had already seen himself standing at the pinnacle of the Pokémon world.

However, Qin Yang quickly calmed down. He gently shook his head, as if to shake these overly beautiful fantasies out of his mind.

“It’s too early to draw conclusions now, after all, I only tried it on Sandslash, and the specific situation is still unclear.”

He sighed, his brows slightly relaxed, and a hint of rationality returned to his eyes.

“Let’s first check the condition of that energy of mine. If it’s consumed and can’t be recovered, then everything is over.”

Qin Yang closed his eyes, took a deep breath, and tried to calm himself, concentrating on feeling the flow of energy within his body.

A moment later, a look of ecstatic joy gradually appeared on Qin Yang’s face. He suddenly opened his eyes, the light in them like fireworks blooming in the night sky.

“Haha, excellent!”

He burst into laughter, his laughter clear and full of vitality, echoing for a long time in the silent cave.

“This energy actually recovered! It seems my luck is really good.”

Qin Yang excitedly waved his arms, as if celebrating a great victory, his body swaying slightly with excitement.

“And, it seems to have increased a little?”

He tilted his head slightly, with a hint of doubt and curiosity in his eyes, and gently tapped his temple with his finger,

“Is it an illusion? Who cares, I’ll know after letting the system scan it.”

Qin Yang took a deep breath and said to the void with firm eyes:

“System, scan me.”

His voice was steady and powerful, like a resounding bell, filled with an intense desire for unknown answers and a fearless determination to explore the truth.

In an instant, a translucent light screen slowly appeared before his eyes, clearly listing his various information:

【Qin Yang】

Gender: Male

Age: 14 years old

Skills: Junior Breeder, Junior potion Maker, Senior Chef

Talent: Unknown Energy

Owned Pokémon: Sandslash (Level 23)

Owned Items: 120,000 money, three liters of Beedrill honey, Sandshrew exclusive Junior Pokéblock, various Junior potion
