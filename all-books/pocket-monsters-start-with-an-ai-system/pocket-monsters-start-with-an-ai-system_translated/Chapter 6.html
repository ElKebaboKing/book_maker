Chapter 6: Magical energy

Chapter 6 Magical Energy

As his energy entered the channel, he was pleasantly surprised to find that his energy could actually fuse with the energy originally operating within Sandshrew, and even slightly accelerate its operation.

"It works! Although it's tiny, it's a good sign."

A relieved smile appeared on Qin Yang's face, and a glimmer of hope shone in his eyes; his body trembled slightly, as if suppressing his inner excitement.

As the energy circulated, when it returned to the light sphere, Qin Yang keenly noticed that the light from the sphere seemed to become slightly brighter.

"Could it be that my energy can truly help Sandshrew improve its energy operation, and even enhance the power of that light sphere?"

Qin Yang's heart stirred, and a hint of excitement flashed in his eyes, but he remained cautious, not daring to act rashly.

After much thought and consideration, Qin Yang decided to increase the energy delivery.

He slowly closed his eyes, concentrating all his mental power, carefully guiding more energy into Sandshrew's body.

As his own energy increased in the channel, Qin Yang was surprised to find that he gradually gained control over the direction of energy operation within Sandshrew.

"What's going on? I can actually control its energy operation?"

Qin Yang was both excited and nervous; his breathing became rapid, and fine beads of sweat once again seeped from his forehead.

Following the observed direction of the channel, he carefully guided the energy circulation, and the light from that light sphere became brighter and brighter with the energy's operation.

During this process, Qin Yang noticed that many channels within Sandshrew were still blocked, which reminded him of the masters in Wuxia novels who greatly increased their power by unblocking their meridians.

"Since that's the case, I might as well try to unblock these channels for Sandshrew; who knows, it might have unexpected effects."

A surge of adventurous impulse rose in Qin Yang's heart; he gritted his teeth, deciding to take action.

However, to be safe, he still maintained most of the energy in the original channels, only diverting a small portion to try and unblock those tiny, blocked channels.

As he manipulated the energy to slowly rush towards those congested small channels, he encountered unexpected resistance.

The blockages were like impregnable fortresses; no matter how the energy impacted them, they remained motionless.

"Did I think wrong? Are these channels simply impossible to unblock later on?"

A hint of doubt and disappointment flashed through Qin Yang's mind; his brows furrowed tightly, and a trace of frustration showed in his eyes.

But he was not willing to give up just yet; after a brief thought, he decided to change his strategy.

Instead of relying on the energy within Sandshrew, he would directly gather all of his own energy and make a desperate attempt to unblock the channels.

When Qin Yang's pure energy approached those congested channels, a miracle occurred.

Those originally impregnable blocking substances, in the face of his energy, slowly melted like ice and snow meeting the scorching sun, as if completely dissolved by this energy.

"It worked! This energy actually has such magical abilities!"

Qin Yang shouted excitedly; his eyes were wide, sparkling with agitated light, his face beaming with joy, and his body trembled slightly from excitement.

However, he soon discovered that in the process of unblocking this small section of the channel, one-tenth of his own energy had been consumed.

"This consumption is too great; if I continue, I don't know if my energy can recover. If it's completely depleted, that would be troublesome."

A hint of worry rose in Qin Yang's heart; his smile instantly froze, and his eyes revealed a trace of hesitation and conflict.

Just as Qin Yang was hesitating, he was pleasantly surprised to find that after unblocking this small channel,

the energy within Sandshrew began to flow smoothly from here, the energy operation speed significantly increased, and that mysterious light sphere also became even brighter.

"It seems all of this was worth it."

A relieved smile once again appeared on Qin Yang's face; his eyes were filled with surprise and anticipation, as if he saw the moment Sandshrew would awaken.

Although he still had concerns about energy consumption, at this moment, his heart was filled more with hope and confidence for Sandshrew's recovery.

Qin Yang stared intently at the still unconscious Sandshrew, and after weighing his options repeatedly, he made up his mind:

"I'll use half of my energy to unblock these crucial channels; I hope it can completely restore Sandshrew."

He slowly closed his eyes, concentrating all his mental power, carefully guiding that mysterious energy to travel within Sandshrew's body.

This time, the channels he chose, though as fine as silk, were in extremely crucial positions.

In Sandshrew's intricate energy network, these few channels seemed insignificant, yet they were like bridges; once unblocked, they could tightly connect several major energy operation channels that were originally independent.

As Qin Yang's energy slowly infused, the stubborn substances blocking those channels began to gradually melt.

Although these stubborn substances melted easily, it was Qin Yang's first time controlling this energy, so it was still a bit awkward, and the energy consumption also made Qin Yang uncomfortable.

Qin Yang's expression was extremely solemn, his brows tightly furrowed, as if enduring immense pressure.

His breathing became rapid and heavy, each exhalation accompanied by a slight tremor, as if he was fighting a desperate battle against an invisible resistance.

"It must succeed."

He silently prayed in his heart, his voice caught in his throat from tension, only turning into a low murmur.

That mysterious energy, guided by Qin Yang, slowly probed into Sandshrew's body.

Upon touching those tiny but crucial channels, the energy, like a sharp sword, began a fierce confrontation with the blockages.

Qin Yang's body also swayed slightly; his lips were tightly pressed, his teeth clenched, as if pouring his entire will into this energy.

As the energy continued to impact, the substances blocking the channels gradually disintegrated.

Suddenly, a faint energy fluctuation emanated from within Sandshrew, like muffled thunder echoing in a valley.

Qin Yang suddenly opened his eyes, his gaze filled with a mixture of surprise and exhaustion.

He saw that within the unblocked channels, energy flowed like joyful streams converging into surging rivers; the previously dispersed energy operation channels were skillfully connected, and the energy flow speed instantly doubled.

And the mysterious light cluster at the core of Sandshrew's body, at this moment, was like a lit lamp, shining brightly, illuminating everything around it with dazzling brilliance.

"It finally worked!"

Qin Yang couldn't help but cheer in a low voice, his tone carrying the relief and joy of having survived a great ordeal.

But soon, his smile froze, because he clearly sensed that only half of the energy in his body remained.

"Less than half left. How am I supposed to recover this energy? If it runs out, I'll be completely helpless if I encounter danger later."

His brows furrowed into a deep "川" shape, his eyes filled with worry and conflict, and his hands unconsciously clutched his hair tightly.

Qin Yang deeply understood the preciousness of this energy, recalling the scene of the Gym-Level Chansey treating Pokémon at the Pokémon Center in Viridian City; he secretly marveled in his heart:

"The healing effect of this energy is simply comparable to that of Chansey.

With it, it's like having a powerful backing, which is too important in this dangerous Pokémon world."

Thinking of this, he became even more determined to conserve his energy; as for the channels within Sandshrew, he would decide later whether his energy could recover.

At this moment, although Sandshrew was still asleep, its injuries were no longer serious, and its breathing was even and steady.

Qin Yang looked at it, and a strong sense of exhaustion swept over him; although his body was fine, the mental exertion just now was considerable.

His body swayed a few times, leaning against the cave wall like a drifting fallen leaf, and he slowly sat down.

"I'll sleep for a bit; I really can't stay awake any longer."

He murmured weakly, his voice as faint as a flickering candle in the wind.

After speaking, his eyelids gradually grew heavy, and he finally fell into a deep sleep in this dim, damp cave, leaving only faint breathing echoing in the silence.

But what Qin Yang didn't know was that as soon as he fell asleep, Sandshrew's entire body began to emit bursts of light, appearing and disappearing in the darkness, incredibly dazzling.
