Chapter 110: 110 - Predator

Lohan was in the center of the giant body of the Queen of Beetles as he dodged the attacks of the creatures who, despite being careful with the queen’s body, did not give up trying to remove him from there.

With the pain the queen was feeling, her mental attacks decreased considerably, allowing him to create two more clones inside her body and further accelerate his digestion.

With the two clones and the Slime’s main body there, the pain she felt became even more intense, causing even the beetles that were attacking that body with care to abandon any concern and desperately try to open the queen’s body to remove the intruder from there.

But at that point, Lohan was already too deep inside. 𝒻𝓇𝑒𝘦𝘸𝑒𝒷𝓃ℴ𝑣𝘦𝑙.𝒸ℴ𝘮

The disadvantage of having a large body is that the density of biomass per gram of weight is lower than that of small, powerful creatures.

This meant that the amount of flesh and organs from the queen that Lohan could digest per second was even greater than what he digested from other monsters!

Thus, in a few minutes, Lohan, who was completely covered in green, stinking beetle blood, managed to destroy countless vital organs of the queen and saw two notifications appear before his eyes.

[You Killed the Player: Brynnear]

[Congratulations on killing the First Player in the Open World of Elysium!]

[Generating a unique reward for this achievement...]

[Reward obtained!]

[New Title: Predator]

Lohan was shocked to see those windows appear in his vision!

"LISA, THAT QUEEN WAS A PLAYER!" Lohan shouted through their mental link, startling the fox who was looking confusedly at the frozen monsters around her, but who was just as shocked as Slime by this news.

"WHAT?!" she asked in shock, completely losing the composure and elegant air she had maintained the entire time.

"How do you know that?!"

Ignoring the corpses around her, Lisa ran to the Beetle Queen’s body and searched for Halon.

As she got closer, the stench of blood and destroyed organs from that giant insect flooded her senses, making her look at the huge hole in the queen’s chest and realize how Halon had ended that battle.

In a few seconds, a blue-green mass rolled out of the insect’s corpse, bringing with it a terrible smell, but at that point, despite her disgust, it didn’t affect Lisa in the slightest.

"Quick, tell me what happened!" She asked with a mixture of shock, excitement, and regret.

Realizing how powerful this Queen Beetle was, it was obvious that her potential was enormous!

If she could be invited to join their guild, they would have an unbeatable general to deal with their enemies!

Considering her growth rate, it was obvious that she had at least a Rare Base, with the potential to be an Epic or even Legendary Base like her!

But thinking about everything they had done so far, it was possible that instead of inviting a powerful general into their guild, they would bring in a treacherous viper to steal everything they had built at some point.

"I just received a notification from Elysium telling me that I killed the first Player in the Open World!" Lohan said excitedly, using his digestion to devour all the blood and dirt stuck to his body, in a few seconds returning to being a bright and pure ball of blue slime.

"You were the FIRST Player to kill another Player in the open world?!" she asked, shocked. "But why didn’t I receive a notification like that? Was the damage I caused not enough to at least get an assist in that elimination?"

"I have no idea..." Lohan replied, a little regretful.

If he had known that’s how it worked, he would have tried to let Lisa cause more damage so that the two of them could get the reward for being the first.

With the partnership they had formed, if the two of them became stronger, their combat power would become even more terrifying!

Lisa thought about it a little regretfully for a few seconds, until she shook her head in denial and finally asked what she wanted to know so badly. "Whatever, but tell me, what reward did you receive?"

"A Title," Lohan said.

"A Title?" Lisa was confused.

"Yes, I don’t even know what it does for me yet." He said as he opened the game menu.

"I didn’t even know titles existed in Elysium..." Lisa commented in a low voice, as if talking to herself, thoughtfully.

Looking at the status screen, Lohan was shocked by what he saw.

[Name: Halon

Race: Slime (Mythical)

Class: Devourer (Mythical)

Titles: Predator (When digesting a Player hunted by the user as Prey, the success rate for extracting an Evolution proportional to the Rarity of the Prey Base is 100%.)

Level: 08

Experience: 6,480 / 12,800

Mana: 07 / 38.75]

Reading the description of that title, Lohan was completely shocked!

’Isn’t that a perfect title for a Player PK?! Does Elysium want me to become a Player who specializes in hunting other Players? Or am I going to be the Predator of Players?!’ Lohan thought, both shocked and fascinated at the same time.

As someone who only watched gameplays in the other world through a screen, he really liked the idea of PVP in the MMORPGs of that world. The open world PVP between the Horde and the Alliance in WoW, the PVP to steal goods from Archeage, the madness of the open world of Albion, Tibia, or so many other games.

It all seemed so fun to him, and only now did he realize that if he wanted to, he could embark on a PVP journey in Elysium just like he had dreamed of in the other world!

Sure, he didn’t want to become a killing maniac who killed any player for the pleasure of killing, but considering the rewards he would get for killing other players... that idea didn’t seem so bad.

"Halon, what’s the reward?" Lisa asked eagerly, totally different from the cold and indifferent attitude she maintained 90% of the time.

Laughing, Lohan didn’t hide from her what he had received, and if he had been fascinated by it, Lisa showed an expression almost green with envy.

And it was understandable, since she had a class almost focused on murder, if she had a title like that it would be perfect!
