The first thing Izroth did was isolate the stem and roots of the Extreme Yin Evil Roots underground, cutting it off in a chunk of sorts. Afterward, Izroth started to peel away at the chunk with Molding Earth one layer at a time.

Izroth performed this action until the stem and roots of the plant became visible. And, the moment it did so, a great chill spread throughout the surroundings that instantly froze the ground and covered it with a thick layer of ice within five meters.

Before the ice could reach him, Izroth kicked off his back foot and dashed backward to a safe distance without halting his use of Molding Earth.

"That was it's final counterattack. From now on, as long as I don't damage the roots, the Extreme Yin Evil Roots will be secured."

A few moments later, Izroth finished removing the last bit of earth from the stem and roots. At the same time, the thick layer of ice receded and was drawn into the Extreme Yin Evil Roots.

The stem of the Extreme Yin Evil Roots was a deep ocean blue, while the roots were pure white and seemed more like strands of energy than actual roots.

After examining it, Izroth placed the Extreme Yin Evil Roots into his garden bag.

However, even if one possessed a high level of fire resistance, it was not easy to travel through molten lava while simultaneously searching for something. Though in the trial dungeon where fire resistance as set to zero by default, this made the task even more daunting.

'The mythical-ranked ingredients all seem to come with its own fair share of troubles. Of course, if one wants to gain something worthwhile, it's nearly impossible to not run into some troubles along the way.'

One of the easiest mythical-ranked ingredients Izroth managed to get his hands on was the Young Wing of the Ambrosia Tree. The tree itself was guarded by a being in the legendary realm; however, it was due to a great deal of luck and circumstances that he got it without a fight.

There was also the Blood of the Immortal Hydra. Which, nothing much needed to be said about how much trouble one needed to go through in order to collect that.

While there was some information about the Demonic Heart Lotus in the book Izroth read, there were still many missing details that he could only fill in with speculation.

Either way, if he wanted to craft the Demonic Suppression Pill, there was no choice but to fetch the Demonic Heart Lotus, regardless of the risk involved.

Izroth set his gaze into the distance. Earlier, when he briefly scanned at maximum range with his Energy Vision Sense, Izroth discovered a single area in the garden that had the perfect conditions to cultivate the Demonic Heart Lotus.

'Although it's called a garden, if one stumbled into it by mistake, they would think it's a real forest with unusual elemental phenomenon. One day, I will create an even grander garden in the Resplendent Landscape Pocket Dimension Stone.'

Izroth stood at the edge of a pool of molten lava. The pool was approximately ten meters in length and five meters wide. As for its depth, even with his Energy Vision Sense, Izroth could not determine how far down it traveled.

"This is the place with the best conditions for a Demonic Heart Lotus to grow. However, how did he plan for the average Apothecary or even Herbalist to collect this thing?"

From the way he set up the trials, Izroth could tell that Franz Winsor was a prankster. But, it was never done in a malicious sense. That being said, with the limiters in place, no access to one's inventory and the ban on collecting other materials to craft any kind of fire resistance pill, how was someone suppose to get Demonic Heart Lotus unless they were born immune to fire?

'Is there something I'm overlooking?'

'I see. That method—it can work.'

Name: Extreme Yin Evil Roots

Izroth reached toward the Extreme Yin Evil Roots through the protective bubble and gently plucked the flower at its top before returning the main part of the plant to the garden bag.

Name: Cold Yin Flower

Rank: Pseudo-Ancient(88)

Usage: The Cold Yin Flower grows in a place devoid of sunlight and other sources of heat. It contains cold energy and is often used in healing fire-related injuries. The Cold Yin Flower is often consumed by cold-type creatures to enhance the strength of their ice element.

Typically, the Cold Yin Flower was a superior-ranked material on the verge of becoming ancient-ranked. However, since Izroth was able to flawlessly remove the Extreme Yin Evil Roots from the earth, the Cold Yin Flower retained its peak condition and held the rank of pseudo-ancient.
