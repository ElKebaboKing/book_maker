Chapter 1244: An Unknown Family History, Recruiting Offers

’The Zhang family and Jiang family... There are too many things that align for it to be a mere coincidence. There is still too much information I lack. I still haven’t heard back from Mariposa about what I asked her to look into. But, maybe I can finally connect a few missing pieces.’

When Izroth met up with Mariposa in the real world to discuss a few matters, he also requested that she help him look into two people. One of those people was Jin’s biological father, Jiang Gui.

A vivid memory that came to Izroth’s mind was one that happened around six years ago. It was then that Jiang Gui paid a visit to Jin and ultimately got him to sign some papers to essentially sever all ties for good.

However, Izroth found Jiang Gui’s actions strange. Although Izroth only knew of Jiang Gui through memories, he did not seem like the kind of person who covered his tracks well. For Jiang Gui to go out of his way to get Jin to sign such a contract meant that there was a good chance someone instructed him to do so.

’No matter how much I try to remember, there was never a time when my mother talked about her family. If she is somehow related to the Zhang family from the Seven Great Families, then how did she end up in such a bad situation? Is it related to the scheme that led to the Zhang family’s decline? It’s too much of a stretch to say for certain with the current information at my disposal. But, if my mother was driven out of the Zhang family, then there must have been someone behind it.’

Reilei was someone who supported Jin in his dream to be a pro gamer despite his numerous failures. However, despite their struggling financial situation, never once did Reilei discourage him from pursing his dream. She even did her best to support him and tried to understand the games he played.

A natural beauty with such a bright, compassionate and gentle nature—no matter how Izroth looked at it, Reilei would have undoubtedly been a target of endless jealousy and envy.

"Does having a pseudo-divine pill core rank increase a pill’s overall effects to this extent? If so, what do the effects of a pill with a genuine divine pill core look like? Saying it’s incomparable to other grade five pills is not enough." Worldly Skies commented.

"Palace Master, can you show us how you crafted this later?" Celia asked eagerly.

"Sure. But, it might be a bit difficult for others to employ the method I used to craft the Seven Element Change Pill. I will have to think about some adjustments so that it’s possible for others." Izroth stated calmly.

Worldly Skies and Celia let out a helpless sigh simultaneously. Seeing that they had the same reaction, the two looked at each other before they playfully giggled.

It was not easy to make changes to pill recipes when you were not its creator. This applied even more so for something like the Seven Element Change Pill with an unclear crafting technique. But, if Izroth was the one saying it, Worldly Skies and Celia did not find the idea to be ridiculous nor arrogant.
