Chapter 760: Skill Orbs, Are We Going Or Not?!

“Hmph! You can come over, but she has to leave.” Zouren said as he pointed to Azalea.

“Your presence here raised questions and agitated an already delicate situation. If the balance is upset any further, you can forget about the Secret Realm.”

Izroth looked over at Azalea; however, before he could even speak, Azalea respectfully cupped her fists and said, “Teacher, there is no need to concern yourself with my thoughts. As for our previous arrangement—this matter can wait.”

Azalea did not know what a Secret Realm was or the benefits from going there, but she roughly understood its importance from the brief exchange between Izroth and Zouren. Knowing her teacher’s temperament, Azalea would not be surprised if he insisted that she still join the discussion even if the Secret Realm was off the table for her. But, there was a good chance this would lead to the entire situation being upheaved. In the end, Azalea decided to dismiss herself and ease Izroth’s worries. How could she let him miss out on such a rare opportunity because of her presence?

Izroth inwardly shook his head at Azalea’s actions. Initially, he planned on asking her thoughts on the matter, but it appeared that it was unnecessary.

“Since I promised to teach you some skills, I will keep half of that promise now,” Izroth said as he held his hand out.

The next moment, a transparent orb no bigger than one’s fist materialized in the palm of Izroth’s hand. As the orb finished forming, a gentle cerulean light started to flow within it.

‘It is unfortunate that I could not imprint its primed version, but… It’s not impossible for her to search for her own understanding.’

After reaching level 50, players could undergo a second class advancement. This change was enough to propel a player’s power to a level well beyond that of their peers. However, such a class advancement did not exist for combat masters.

Instead, combat masters unlocked a new class feature—the ability to create items known as skill orbs.

Skill orbs were similar to skill books in that they could be used by players to learn the skill that was contained inside. However, unlike skill books that players had no control over, combat masters were able to set requirements and conditions for the skill.

Whether it was lowering or increasing the requirement, it had the ability to change the overall potential of the skill.

This was a feature known only by Izroth. After all, even the beta testers never made it this far with the combat master class. This meant that Izroth was the first-ever combat master in RML to reach level 50!

While the ability to create skill orbs did not increase a combat master’s strength, in a way, it transformed the combat master into the ultimate support class.

A class that granted players the ability to create skills and teach them to others—if news of this were to spread, it would cause a huge shockwave to sweep throughout RML!

Even something like a C-ranked skill book could go for hundreds of gold coins. As for A-ranked skill books—they were not on the market often, but the last known A-ranked skill book that was put up for sale sold for 12,000 gold coins. That was more than 2,000,000 RMB!

When it came to S-ranked skill books, there was zero every released to the open market. If it did sell, it was something only the top guilds who were backed by wealthy companies could ever hope to afford.

‘It’s a shame the growth potential is one step short. But, it’s better than I anticipated.’

Izroth handed the skill orb to Azalea, who happily accepted it.

“For now, work on mastering this skill. Once I return, I will teach you another suitable skill.” Izroth said calmly.

“Yes. Thank you, teacher. I shall await your return.” Azalea responded. Even though a veil covered the lower part of her face, it could not completely hide the light smile that found its way to her lips.

Azalea glared towards Zouren and said coldly, “If anything happens to my teacher, I will not rest until your body no longer has a head.”

Without waiting for Zouren’s response, Azalea turned into a phantom as she disappeared from the Moonlit Dove Garden without a trace.

Hearing Azalea’s words without having a chance to respond caused Zouren’s blood to boil over. First, he had to make some concessions and invite this hateful person to a Secret Realm and now even some random nobody was looking down on him?!

“Good, enjoy your arrogance while you can—both of you!” Zouren internalized as he held back his rage.
