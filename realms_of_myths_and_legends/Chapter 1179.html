'O'Tohelm and Tempest have the most to gain from an attack on the Noble District. If it is O'Tohelm, the attack can just be a way to divert attention away from the forces gathering in Danaharpe that are set to reclaim Terraharpe. On the other hand, if Tempest can make it look like O'Tohelm was behind the attack, it would help in further turning Amaharpe and its allies against them, leading O'Tohelm right into the arms of Tempest. Either way, whether it's directly or indirectly, something tells me that the explosion has something to do with O'Tohelm.'

There was still also the fact that no one knew why O'Tohelm decided to attack and occupy Terraharpe. In the end, it was just an outpost city that safeguarded one of Amaharpe's mineral deposits. If O'Tohelm wanted to inflict damage to Amaharpe or obstruct them, there were several better ways than occupying Terraharpe.

'Mineral deposits... Is that really all Terraharpe was built to protect?'

There were too many unanswered questions. However, if Terraharpe was protecting something besides the mineral deposits that was unknown to the public, it would explain why O'Tohelm took this opportunity to invade the city.

'If Terraharpe is protecting something that we don't know about, its value must be worth O'Tohelm offending Amaharpe and its allied forces. I will have to get someone to look into it.'

"If it's a person in the Noble District, their status is far from normal. It seems that things will be chaotic for a while in Danaharpe." Izroth replied calmly.

The mission into the kingdom Tempest, the fending off of Pzenium forces and the raid on the Night Lord's Crypt. It had not been a long time since the 9th Division was first established, yet it had already gone through a lot of challenges.
