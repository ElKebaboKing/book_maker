Chapter 1255: New Property

Avia seemed taken aback by Izroth’s words. The Fourth Great Clan she knew would never willingly hand over a treasure that belonged to them. This was even more so to outsiders. So, why did it sound like Izroth was so confident that he could obtain the Fire Ice Heart from them directly?

The reason behind Izroth’s confidence was simple. It had to do with one of his ongoing quests, An Essence of Madness.

One of the rewards for successfully completing that quest was to gain temporary access to the Fourth Great Clan’s Treasure Fields.

Even if the Fire Ice Heart was not located in the Treasure Fields itself, Izroth’s fame with the Fourth Great Clan would be maxed out along with his favor with the Princess of the Fourth Great Clan, Tesselia. Under such circumstances, even if the Fourth Great Clan did want to give him the Fire Ice Heart for free, at the very least, Izroth believed that they should be willing to make a fair trade for it.

’It’s one more reason why I have to pay a visit to the Mother of All Forests soon.’

The SSS-ranked quest Awakening of The Lost Bloodline, the SS-ranked quest An Essence of Madness, the unknown situation with the Seventh Demon Clan and now the Fire Ice Heart required to restore Nomii’s arm—Izroth knew that he had to prepare well for what was to come.

’There’s also still the matter with Gear. Since I have no way of contacting him directly, I asked Nova to look into his whereabouts and send word to the Mystical Realm Palace.’

Gear was infected with a mark of corruption that had plagued him for many years.

Izroth planned to utilize the same method he used to cure Nova of her mark of corruption on Gear. There was even the possibility that Gear could experience a breakthrough like Nova. If so, another formidable ally would be on the side of the Mortal Realm.

More importantly, Izroth felt indebted to Gear. It was Gear who provided him with numerous opportunities that aided in his overall growth in RML. And, Izroth wanted to repay his kindness tenfold.

"There are still things that need to be done before we can depart from Danaharpe. You did not come alone, did you?" Izroth asked.

"Lady Nomii is somewhere in the Darzhan Forest. There was something there that caught her eye. And, given Lady Nomii’s strength, there is no existence in Darzhan Forest capable of threatening her. Not to mention, Danaharpe is currently in a fragile state after the recent attack on the Noble District. Since Lady Nomii does not hold any official rank and her origins are unknown, it would be difficult for her to enter this place. Though, I have already sent word through the relic she entrusted to me that I have met up with you. We can rendezvous near the outer edge of Darzhan Forest whenever you give the word." Avia stated calmly.

"I see. Then, it can’t be helped. I have been busy, so I had not given it much immediate thought. I will make sure you won’t have to concern yourself with something similar in the future." Izroth responded.

’At the moment, Astratis and Nomii have no identity in the Mortal Realm. While it’s good for their strength to be hidden, they still need some form of identity if they want to move through Amaharpe and the territory of its allies. In this matter, the quickest way of securing an identity for them is to have them join my Special Unit. It’s not ideal, but it’s still too early for their true identities to be exposed. That said, I have no idea where Astratis went off to. With his strength, he is more than capable of protecting himself. Still, I should at least have an idea of his whereabouts. If anyone can track his location, it should be Nomii since their ethos is a unique energy source in the Mortal Realm.’

"For now, there’s someone waiting for us by the generous gift we received. Let’s go." Izroth said as he made his way toward the Apothecary Guild building exit.

Avia followed behind Izroth.

"Is this the place you mentioned?" Avia inquired with furrowed brows.

"This is it. The location is good and the building is sturdy. It can use a bit of work if we want to attract attention. Thankfully, I have already contacted someone to take care of the details." Izroth replied.
