Chapter 1126: Ultima Sygrix

In the end, even though both were of the fire element, because of the attributes attached to them, they evolved into something beyond basic fire.

'Spatial Ebb and Flow? How interesting.'

This was Izroth first time of hearing of such an attribute; however, it was not surprising. In addition to being one of the hardest elements to master, Space was also one of the rarest elements in RML. It contained countless secrets that even the highest level of mages below the firmament had yet to fully unlock and comprehend.

Due to his experience as someone from the Seven Realms, of course, Izroth was familiar with a myriad of elements-most of which, to his knowledge, did not even exist in this world. Nevertheless, despite uncovering some of the deeper rooter secrets regarding its

fundamental existence, magic was still unfamiliar territory to Izroth. There was still much left for him to discover.

Name: Artificial Plant: Ultima Synthleaf

Rank: Ancient(32) -> Ancient(108)

*New -> Element: Space

*New -> Usage: An artificial plant made by the ingenious fusion of science and magic that has absorbed the <<space>> element and attained the attribute <<Spatial Ebb and Flow». This item's effect is greatly restricted in its current state. This material is highly sought after to use for crafting and synthesizing.

*New-> <<Spatial Ebb and Flow≫ - Precisely control the surrounding space in a small area.

'It has become more difficult to work with, but the effect is better than I anticipated. With this, it should be possible.'

Initially, Izroth's goal was just to receive an Ultima Synthleaf with the Space element. As for its attribute, as long as it was not completely terrible, what he had in mind would remain in reach. However, for the Ultima Synthleaf's attribute to be capable of precisely controlling the surrounding space was perfect considering his circumstances.

Nevertheless, Izroth knew that even with Spatial Ebb and Flow, it would be impossible for a single ancient-ranked material to tame the wild space, let alone creating a stable passageway long enough for Empyrean and Selene to travel through. At least, that would be the case if it was used in its current state.

Bang!

Suddenly, Izroth's Dark Abyssal Armlet transformed into the Dark Abyssal Cauldron and appeared before him. Without missing a breath, Izroth activated the Dark Abyssal Cauldron and increased the flames to its maximum output.

Shhhhh!

The flames from the Dark Abyssal Cauldron burst forth and roared as the cauldron trembled.

Izroth stared into the flames for a brief moment as he could not help but let out a small sigh of disappointment.

'As I thought, even with an A-ranked fire core, its flames are still not hot enough. Still, this much is within my expectations.'

The next moment, Izroth removed a certain item from his inventory. And, the instant he did so, an intense wave of heat swept throughout the entire room. Even the flames from the Dark Abyssal Cauldron's A-ranked fire core paled in comparison.

'If not for my Heavenly Golden Body and the Apparel of Insatiable Transcendence, something as simple as being near this item would be troublesome.'

The item Izroth held in his hand was a sleek feather roughly one meter in length. The feather was deep red in color and released a radiant light that accompanied the intense heat wave. This was one of the items Izroth won from a bet he made with a few celestials back in the Ancient Celestial Realm, the Blazing Griffin Feather.

Name: Blazing Griffin Feather

Rank: Mythical(161)

Usage: The feather of an adult <<Blazing Griffin>> that was plucked while it was alive, maintaining intense traces of its powerful fire essence. The flames contained within a single <<Blazing Griffin>> feather is said to burn hot enough to melt high grade metals.

Special Note: This item is highly sought after by blacksmiths due to its ability to melt some of the highest grade metals with ease.

'It feels like a waste to use a mythical-ranked item in this manner. Unfortunately, I do not have the time to be reserved.'

Izroth placed the Blazing Griffin Feather into his Dark Abyssal Cauldron. As he did so, all the heat in the room seemed to be pulled into the Dark Abyssal Cauldron. Then, not even half a breath later, a loud series of explosions rung throughout the room.

BOOM! BOOM!

The Dark Abyssal Cauldron violently shook and it seemed as if it would break at any second. If it were a normal cauldron, Izroth would have never dreamed of implying this method. However, the Dark Abyssal Cauldron was indestructible. Therefore, Izroth was confident that it could contain the heat from the Blazing Griffin Feather without exploding. Izroth's main concern was the A-ranked fire core. He was unsure whether or not it could hold up clashing against the Blazing Griffin Feather while within a cauldron.

'Since I don't have a furnace on me, I just have to create a makeshift one.'

If other players could hear Izroth's thoughts, they would look at him as if he were crazy. He was actually trying to use a cauldron made for crafting pills as a furnace! More importantly, why was an apothecary trying to turn his cauldron into a furnace when he wasn't even a blacksmith?!

Although both cauldrons and furnaces used fire cores and was capable of controlling the level of heat they displayed, they served two completely different purposes.

For one, a cauldron was designed to be portable, while a furnace was made to be stationary. Needless to say, it was much easier to reinforce something stationary. Furthermore, although there were some materials for pills that required a high level of heat to process, it was not on the level of high grade metals that could only be smelted in a furnace.

To most players, at first glance, there was not much of a difference between a cauldron and furnace. But, in reality, there were too many glaring factors that separated the two. One of the most important of these factors was the temperature management systems contained within a furnace. It not only maximized the heat provided by a fire core, but had several safety features in place to prevent the fire core from overheating and exploding. A furnace, on the other hand, did not have the same features. It was why the cauldron Izroth used when he took the Apothecary test exploded when he crafted a grade three pill by pushing the fire core too far.

A few breaths after Izroth placed the Blazing Griffin feather into the Dark Abyssal Cauldron, a dark crystalline ore appeared in his hand.

Name: Unrefined High-Quality Sygrix Stone

Rank: Ancient(60)

Usage: This dark crystalline ore can only be found in dense mana areas without the presence of monsters. It releases a gentle pulse at its core. The more gentle a pulse, the greater its quality. Adding this ore to items will cause them to exhibit powerful physical or magical

enhancements.

Special Note: In its refined state, it is quite beautiful and is often a highly sought-after decoration by nobles.

'It is a shame that I do not have the time to refine it. Otherwise, the effects it displays would increase several fold. Regardless, even in its unrefined stage, the Sygrix Stone is still an ancient-ranked

material.'

Izroth had the Ultima Synthleaf in one hand and the Sygrix Stone in his other. And, when he finally felt all the heat in the room gather into the Dark Abyssal Cauldron, without hesitation, he threw the Sygrix Stone into the cauldron and quickly followed it up with the Ultima

Synthleaf.

When Izroth performed this action, surprisingly, there was no intense reaction. In fact, the Dark Abyssal Cauldron even ceased its violent trembles. At this point, most people would believe that their attempt had failed. But, Izroth could tell with his Spatial Awareness what was truly taking place in the cauldron.

When he tossed the Sygrix Stone into the cauldron, it immediately melted and turned into a puddle of liquid. Then, after adding in the Ultima Synthleaf that held the Spatial Ebb and Flow attribute, the Sygrix Stone in its liquid state latched onto the Ultima Synthleaf, fusing with it. Upon doing so, the Spatial Ebb and Flow effect seemed to activate by itself, essentially closing off the intense heat within the cauldron from escaping outside.

System Alert: You have created a new substance, «<???». Would you like to name it?!

"Ultima Sygrix." Izroth uttered without thought.

Woosh!

'It won't be able to hold up for long, but since it has the fire element, it should suffice.' Izroth brandished the Scorched Inferno's Fang spear before holding it over the Dark Abyssal

Cauldron with one hand and its blade pointing toward the cauldron.

He then stabbed down with extreme precision, causing the tip of the Scorched Inferno's Fang

to collide with the center of the fused Ultima Synthleaf and Sygrix Stone.
