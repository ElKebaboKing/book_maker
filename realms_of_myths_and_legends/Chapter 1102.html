Chapter 1102: Soul Severing Fist

The Soul Severing Fist Demon, Baalgua-this was a name that struck fear into the hearts of those who heard it in the Seven Realms. Wherever that man went, disaster soon followed and only a trail of destruction was left in his wake.

There was once a disciple from a well-known sect that insulted the Soul Severing Fist Demon without knowing his identity. As a result, that disciple along with his entire sect was erased from the Seven Realms without a trace.

More terrifying, the bodies of the disciple and his fellow sect members were all found in a horrifying state that looked as if they had fought an actual demonic being. The most tragic part was that there were no traces of their souls. This meant that they could never enter the reincarnation cycle and would be doomed to roam the world in between the living and the dead for all eternity.

Naturally, the cause of this was the Soul Severing Fist Demon, Baalgua. His fist techniques were otherworldly. At its peak, it was said to be able to separate a person's body from their soul with a single strike.

The Endless Chained Strikes was one of the first techniques of the Soul Severing Fist. Its purpose was to get the opponent to lower their guard due to its weak initial attack power as well as to disrupt the opponent's meridians secretly. By the time one realized what was going on, it was too late and their defenses would be completely broken through. Furthermore, the true attack power behind the Endless Chained Strikes would start to show in full force.
