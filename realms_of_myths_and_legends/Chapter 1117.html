"The contents of that notebook were quite troublesome," Izroth muttered to himself.

Zitra's Research Notebook contained the trials and errors of the chimera creation process. One key point was that chimeras made using only monsters, no matter how intelligent the monster, always perished within a few days. However, by adding a lifeform like a human, skounae, trephasia, and so on, it improved the chimera's stability rate and lifespan hundreds of times over.

According to Zitra's Research Notebook, all the components to create a true chimera were there except for one-a stable chimera core. But, Izroth was not surprised after seeing the Failed Chimera Core for himself.

'If a Failed Chimera Core is already at the mythical rank, that means a complete core needs to be at least at the pseudo-divine or even divine rank. To successfully create such an object is no different than glimpsing into the divine realm. If perfect chimeras can be produced, there is no telling what kind of destruction will be spread throughout the Mortal Realm. This Polkentin... Is it scientific curiosity that drives the Mad Alchemist or something more sinister? Just what does he hope to accomplish?'

"I will have someone look into it. Whatever the case may be, this kind of research cannot be allowed to continue unobstructed."

For the time being, Izroth placed those thoughts in the back of his mind. He lacked too much information about Polkentin or any potential organizations that may be backing the Mad Alchemist. Until he knew more, there was not much to do.

"I still have to destroy the base along with the rest of Zitra's research, but before that..."

Izroth looked around the spacious room filled with containment units, research materials, tools, and other various things. Multiple pathways and separate room entrances lined up along the walls in the nearby distance. Some of the pathways led back to where Izroth came from; however, Izroth did not know where most of the others led.

At the moment, Izroth was looking for two things. The first was a way to destroy Polkentin's Secret Underwater Base.

'Perhaps I should have kept him alive to question him a bit. Though I doubt he would have been cooperative.'

Forget eight hours, even several weeks would not be enough. Therefore, Izroth concluded that there must be a more feasible way to destroy the underwater base in its entirety.
