With less than ten minutes left until the crafting portion of the trial was over, Pill Master, Blue Quill, and Iron Cauldron did not have much time to spare as they immediately started the crafting process.

As Izroth observed their ingredients and its quality, he inwardly shook his head.

'It's worse than I thought. They pulled it out without knowing therrect harvesting process.'

Even if one was not an experienced herbalist, they would be able to see how poorly some of the ingredientsllected by Blue Quill and Iron Cauldron were handled.

The worst of all was the Morning Flames. Its handling was so poor that its rank had actually changed from superior to rare!

This is exactly what Izroth thought what might happen after realizing that Haishe had forgotten one important role for this trial dungeon—a herbalist.

With his own experiences and having read thousands of books in RML, Izroth understood how easy it was to ruin an ingredient during thellection process.

That's why, whenllecting the Morning Flames, Izroth followed the method he read about in a book located at the Apothecary guild in Amaharpe's capital city.

When Morning Flames grow they release a byproduct in the soil around them. This byproduct is usually destroyed immediately upon forming when its too close to the Morning Flames. However, it can typically be found a few meters away from the Morning Flames.

This byproduct was the light blue soil that Izroth picked up and placed around the Morning Flames before picking it. In truth, the light blue soil was not actually soil, but miniature crystals fused with the essence of the Morning Flames, natural energy, and mana.

Under normal circumstances, these crystals should have been destroyed when set around the Morning Flames. But, once the crystals turns from dark blue into light blue, they can withstand being in proximity to the Morning Flames and even has the effect of locking the plants essence within itself until it was ready to be used.

Ofurse, in the eyes of those who had no idea the Morning Flames and its byproduct had such an effect like Pill Master, it would seem like Izroth tossed a worthless twig into the cauldron.

'I've never heard about a trial dungeon that tests more than one profession before. That being said, it's not as though everything has already been divered in RML. Perhaps there exists a trial dungeon out there that tests all the major professions. Whatever the case, for this particular trial dungeon, the lack of a herbalist is fatal.'

Due to the way trial dungeons functioned, it was impossible to attempt it again unless it specifically stated otherwise. This meant that it was not entirely Haishe's fault that he did not bring a herbalist along. After all, the main focus of this trial dungeon was clearly with an Apothecary in mind. This was another reason Izroth believed that Franz Winsor was a prankster when he was still around.

In the end, Franz's Legacy: Five Cryptics was a one time trial dungeon. As a result, while it was not entirely Haishe's fault that he did not bring along a herbalist, he was still to blame for this oversight. It wasmmon knowledge that the Apothecary and Herbalist professions went hand in hand.

While Apothecaries should have an in depth knowledge of herbs, it was not easy tollect high ranking or difficult to handle herbs without the system's aid. One wrong mistakeuld reduce a material's rank and quality or render itmpletely useless."

There was less than two minutes remaining until they had to repel the wave of Lava Beasts; therefore, the three wasted no time as first up to place their finished product in the jade tray was Iron Cauldron.
