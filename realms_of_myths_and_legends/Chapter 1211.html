Rygar frowned and scoffed, "Do you know how difficult it is to create your own pill recipe? He then continued, "The Golden Eternity Pill is a pill recipe that was created with the combined effort of talented Apothecaries of the Apothecary Guild. In terms of effect, it won’t necessarily lose to some lower grade five pills. One could overturn the entire Mortal Realm and they will only be able to find a handful of grade four pills that can claim to be its equal or superior. Apothecary Izroth, do you still dare to say that the pill recipe you created can stand against it?"

"The result will speak for itself. But, if Apothecary Rygar is so confident in my failure and his success, why don’t we make a bet?" Izroth said in a carefree manner.

"A bet? With you? A waste of time. It is beneath me." Rygar mocked.

"Well, if Apothecary Rygar is too terrified to bet, then I won’t pressure you." Izroth stated calmly.

"Who’s scared to bet with you?! Do you have anything worthy of betting against me?!" Rygar snapped back.

As a grade seven Apothecary, naturally, Rygar was not lacking when it came to money and resources. And, seeing as how Izroth was only a grade three Apothecary with no clear backing, Rygar figured the best he could put up were some grade four pills. Why would he waste his time on such trash?

"Have you ever heard of the Immortal Hydra?" Izroth brought up as a single vial appeared in his hand and he held it up.

He then continued, "Within this vial is its blood—enough to fill roughly thirty percent of the average cauldron. This is what I’m willing to bet. Do you still think I have nothing worthy of betting against you?"

Rygar’s eyes widened in shock as he stared at the vial in Izroth’s hand.

"Immortal Hydra’s blood?! There are some monsters that still possess very faint traces of the hydra bloodline, but true hydra blood was long thought to have been lost in the Mortal Realm. How did this brat manage to get his hands on something so precious?!" Rygar thought to himself as a look of greed flashed through his eyes.

What Apothecary worth their salt did not know about hydra blood and the miraculous applications of legend that surrounded it? A single drop was worth a thousand times its weight in gold. Or, rather, it was hard to put an exact price on hydra blood due to its extreme rarity.

"Enough to fill up thirty percent of a cauldron... If he’s not boasting, then that vial contains enough hydra blood to use as research material and for crafting purposes for at least ten years! Not to mention, when it comes to hydra blood, the Apothecary Guild Elders who have always ignored me will come to find me willing. No, perhaps even the head of the Apothecary Guild will personally pay me a visit! If I can use that chance to become his student, then...!" Rygar’s thoughts raced.

"I can accept your bet. But, first, I’d like to confirm the contents of the vial. If it truly is hydra blood, it will have an unbearable stench that causes side effects. Since we’re in the Apothecary Guild, there’s no need to worry about being affected. The magic formation in place will suppress it." Rygar stated.

"Since Apothecary Rygar has given his permission, then I won’t hesitate." Izroth said as he opened the top of the vial.
