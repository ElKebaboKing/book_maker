Izroth left the office with Niflheim and walked out before the members of the 9th Division. The moment they did so, Izroth was greeted by a familiar voice.

"Welcome back, Captain. There is much to be discussed once this situation reaches its conclusion." Menerva said as she walked up and stood next to Izroth and Niflheim.

"You've worked hard during my absence. If there is anything you require, feel free to let me know. I look forward to your report." Izroth said in a carefree manner before he returned his attention forward.

Izroth swept his gaze across the crowd of players and noticed that every member of the 9th Division had gathered. Even the ones who were offline rushed over without hesitation.

Of course, this was not too surprising as no one wanted to miss out on what they had all been waiting for—the rewards given by their captain, the owner of the Mystical Realm Palace.

"First, I want to say that everyone here has performed their roles well since joining the 9th Division. I have thrown you into many unfavorable situations since our journey began; however, not once did any of you complain about it. Though we haven't known each other long, you played your trust in me, Lieutenant Niflheim, Lieutenant Menerva and one another." Izroth started.

He then continued, "Alright, I have made all of you wait long enough. I'm sure that Lieutenant Niflheim has already informed you of the reason why I called here. Those who are a part of my 9th Division must be rewarded for their efforts. The rewards I prepared this time around are somewhat special."

Listening to Izroth's words, the members of the 9th Division could barely contain their excitement as an atmosphere of anticipation overtook the building.

"I will start with the three individuals who displayed the most outstanding performance. When I call your name, come up to the front. Bellum, Champion and—Love Mist." Izroth called out.

Upon hearing their names called, Bellum and Champion walked up to the front.

On the other hand, Love Mist stood dumbfounded with an expression of shock on her face. Did she hear that correctly? Was it really her name called just now?

"Hey, the Captain called your name. Hurry up and go up there." Revel whispered from behind Love Mist.

Revel's words caused Love Mist to snap out of it as she made her way toward the front.

Once all three stood arrived in front, a carefree smile found its way onto Izroth's face.

"Bellum, thanks to your skills, everyone was able to safely make it through the Cave Mortem Monsoon and Ferae Jungle. You also played a role in the success we had at the Night Lord's Crypt. As a reward, you will receive 250 gold coins, a Self Awakening Pill and this-" Izroth said as he held out the palm of his hand.

The members of the 9th Division could not believe their ears. There was still more? With the current conversion rate, the 250 gold coins alone was worth 37,500 RMB! To most of those present, that was more than what they earned over the course of several months!

Bellum observed the pill; however, this was her first time seeing something like it. But, just from the aroma alone, it seemed superior to that of the grade three Five Cycles Pill everyone obtained after joining the 9th Division.

"Could it be a grade four pill?" Bellum thought to herself as her brows rose in surprise.

"You will be the very first person to receive this pill. But, I can guarantee that you will not be disappointed with its effect. It is called the Latent Attainment Pill. As for its effect, it will be better if you see it yourself." Izroth said as he used the Law of Wind to send the Latent Attainment Pill before Bellum.

Bellum held out her hands and accepted the pill as she examined it in more detail.

"A pill like this is also possible...?!" Bellum exclaimed softly as she read over the pill's effects.

"I thought he was being generous with the 250 gold coins, but a pill like this—I'm afraid even if one had pay 10,000 gold coins to get their hands on one, they wouldn't hesitate." Bellum thought to herself with furrowed brows.

Bellum was the ex-vice guild leader of Broken Exile; therefore, she had a better understanding than most when it came to the value of things and what those at the top were willing to pay for it. In truth, even 10,000 gold coins may not be enough.

Name: Latent Attainment Pill(Unique*)

Rank: Unique*

Usage: The user who consumes this pill will permanently obtain beneficial 1 «Hidden Stat» set to its lowest default value. This «Hidden Stat» will possess an innate growth value and cannot be increased using stat points.

Special Note: Consuming this pill more than once will not grant any additional benefits.

Hidden stats—this was something that every player hoped to obtain. The advantages of having a hidden stat could not be underestimated. After all, most of those who managed to acquire hidden stats were able to make a name for themselves relying on that alone.

For a pill to exist that could guarantee someone a beneficial hidden stat, if news of this got out, there would be no short of requests flowing into the Mystical Realm Palace!

Not only that, but everyone knew that the most difficult part of having a hidden stat was finding ways to increase its value. But, the pill covered for that weakness as the hidden stat would come along with an innate growth value. This meant that as long as one used it, the hidden stat would increase over time!

"I have no words. Our Captain is really generous. If this were Broken Exile, then... No, forget it. I shouldn't compare the 9th Division to a place that chased me out. It looks like I won't be able to sit still for much longer. Otherwise, it will feel like I'm taking advantage of our Captain." Bellum thought to herself as she stared at the pill in her hands in deep thought.

"Thank you, Captain. I will do my best to ensure that my future performance does not disappoint you." Bellum said graciously as she expressed her gratitude.

"I will look forward to your future performance." Izroth replied casually as he also sent over a small bag that contained 250 gold coins and a single Self Awakening Pill.
