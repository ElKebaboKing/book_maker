'Oh? A relic? It's my first time seeing one in the Mortal Realm.'

From what Izroth understood about relics, they were similar to magic items; however, their production methods seemed to differ. Magic items also contained high levels of concentrated mana and magic, while relics did not use mana or magic as their source of power.

Additionally, relics were not separated by ranks in the same way that magic items were despite some effects clearly being more powerful or useful than others.

That being said, Izroth had not done much research into relics since it had not been that long since he obtained some for himself back in the Ancient Celestial Realm.

Whatever the case, he initially speculated that perhaps relics were items unique to places like the Ancient Celestial Realm. However, seeing as a relic here in the Mortal Realm caused him to question that thought.
