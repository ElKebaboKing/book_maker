This was the first official ability given by the system to combat masters. Which is why, outside of that, Izroth could not think of anything else that meant breaking through the first phase. That is, unless he himself had not yet reached that point.

'If there is a first phase, it is safe to assume that there is at least a second phase, perhaps even a third or fourth phase. Either way, paying a visit to those ruins doesn't seem like a bad idea.'

As Izroth stood silently in his thoughts, Haishe grew restless with every passing moment as his mind raced.

"Alright, I can let you have the exclusive rights to the Spiraling Flames Pill. However, I'm sure you realize what I want to say next." Izroth said with a meaningful gaze.

In the blink of an eye, Haishe felt as though a heavy burden had been lifted off of him.

"Of course, I understand." Haishe replied as he cupped his fist respectfully.

He then continued, "Since there are still many uncertainties surrounding what lies beyond the sealed stone door, how about we settle things in a straight manner?"

"I'm listening." Izroth said casually.

"Since the requirements are a little unique, there is a possibility that what lies beyond the sealed stone door can only be used or taken away by a combat master. If that's the case, my Fatal Touch would have invested a lot into operation while walking away empty-handed as Palace Master Izroth reaps all the benefits. Even taking into consideration the exclusive rights of the Spiraling Flames Pill, isn't it a little too much?" Haishe said as he controlled his nerves.

As the recently appointed leader of Fatal Touch, everything Haishe did was watched under a microscope. One wrong move and his enemies would not hesitate to take advantage of the situation to attack him in some way or another. No matter what, he could not walk away empty-handed after Fatal Touch invested so heavily in unlocking the secrets of those ruins. That being said, Haishe knew that he could not push Izroth too far; otherwise, not only would he lose the exclusive rights to the Spiraling Flames Pill, but he would have no other choice but to take a huge risk and go with the alternate plan.

"That's why, I want to make a deal with Palace Master Izroth." Haishe stated as he calmed his turbulent thoughts.

"Oh? It turned from compensation into a deal. You should think carefully about the next words that leave your mouth." Izroth voiced with clear intent.

After taking a few moments to fully collect his thoughts, Haishe spoke up.

"A trade of fair value—if Palace Master Izroth can help us unlock the sealed stone door and it turns out to be something helpful to combat masters, then you will have the right to claim it for yourself. All I ask is that you give us something of equal value in return. But, if it turns out that it is unrelated to combat masters, then I will offer Palace Master Izroth 15%- no, 20% of the treasure we obtain from that place. If it turns out there is nothing of interest there, then I will give Palace Master Izroth 10% of all profits earned from selling the Spiraling Flames Pill. You won't so much as have to lift a finger to do anything except to collect profit. I know Palace Master Izroth does not lack money, so if you wish, I can also temporarily hand over the ownership of the dungeon I mentioned earlier and even lend you the manpower required to collect the resources there." Haishe stated clearly.

He followed up, "It's true that without Palace Master Izroth, we might have to wait a long time for another opportunity to arise. But, if there is too much to lose, then I will merely have to accept waiting. But, I think this deal is fair for both sides involved."

In the end, Haishe decided to take a gamble and place all his cards on the table. Now, all he could do was hope that it was enough for Izroth to considerate it seriously.

'At the very least, he is several times better than the one called Silent Steps. While I am still not sure about Fatal Touch as a whole, this will also give me a chance to see if Silent Steps as acting alone as Haishe said, or if Fatal Touch as a whole has already been corrupted by the shadahi. If it turns to be the latter, then—I will make sure that Fatal Touch no longer has a place in the Mortal Realm.'

"Very well. I will send the details over to the manage of Mystical Realm Palace so that we can form an official contract. You can go yourself or send someone else to visit the Mystical Palace and settle on the final details. Of course, I will make sure that the information you shared with me does not go beyond that." Izroth stated in a steady voice.

Izroth found Haishe's offer to be fair. Rather, the offer could even be seen as being slightly favorable to Izroth. Since Haishe was willing to show his sincerity and Izroth held some interest in the ruins, there was no reason to turn away from things.

An expression of excitement quickly found its way onto Haishe's face. However, the excitement as fleeting as it was soon replaced by one of deep thought.

"Can it be that you're having second thoughts about our agreement?" Izroth asked.

"Ah, no! Not at all, Palace Master Izroth! I was just lost in my thoughts for a second about an unrelated matter!" Haishe responded with haste.

There was a trace of reluctance on Haishe's face and uncertainty in his eyes. But, after mulling over it for a few seconds, the aura around him shifted as if he had made a decision.

"Have you ever heard of a group called Sovereign?" Haishe inquired.

'Sovereign? I've heard of many groups, but it's the first time I'm hearing this name.'

"I haven't. But, seeing as how hesitant you seemed to bring them up, they must have quite a bit of influence." Izroth noted.

Haishe closed his eyes and said, "I can't tell you much about them since even I myself am left in the dark about it. But, the request to investigate everything related to the shadahi came from a member of Sovereign known as the Envisage Saint. Since that moron was still in charge back when the request was made, I don't know the exact details. However, that name is only their title, not their actual player name. As for their appearance or what they hoped to accomplish, I don't know."

'The Envisage Saint? If they have the word Saint in their title... I see. Is that why he was hesitant to tell me?'

"The Envisage Saint—are they perhaps related to the one called the Sword Saint in your Fatal Touch?" Izroth asked.

"That—I can't say... And, although it should go without saying, I hope that Palace Master Izroth forgets where and who he learned this information from. Otherwise, it really will be difficult for me to continue in this line of work." Haishe stated.

'Sovereign and the Envisage Saint... Is it just a deep interest or are they planning something with the shadahi in mind? I will have to ask Azalea to look into them.'

There were too many unanswered questions. And, while Haishe undoubtedly knew more than he was letting on, from his perspective, he had already reached the limit of what he could disclose. After all, he had taken a huge risk just by divulging this information to Izroth.

"Rest assured, this conversation is one that never took place." Izroth said with veiled intentions.

"Then, we will part ways here. I will personally visit the Mystical Realm Palace in a few days to settle things regarding the contract. I look forward to the day our paths cross again, Palace Master Izroth." Haishe said as he respectfully cupped his fist into his hand before taking his leave.

After waiting a few moments after Haishe left, Izroth said, "Come out."

Immediately after those words left his mouth, the Phantom of Harmony emerged from Izroth's shadow and stood before him.
