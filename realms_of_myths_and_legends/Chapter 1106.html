The Strength of the Ancient Colossus is also now the Strength of the Primordial Colossus, doubling its amplification effects with a much lower cooldown. Of course, the reason it's a unique item is undoubtedly due to the Might of the Primordial Colossus. This effect alone has taken my attack power to a new level.'

With his current bonus physical strength from the Earthly Golden Bones of his Heavenly Golden Body, might stat, and the Ring of the Primordial Colossus, Izroth's was 18 times stronger than the vast majority of players. If he managed to train his might and Heavenly Golden Body to their peak states, even with his physical body alone he would be able to face off against those in the legendary realm!

If he also mastered the Boundless Emperor Void Perfection on top of that and reached 100% completion with the Great Void Merging, the attack from earlier would pale in comparison to what he was truly capable of.

As Izroth swam deeper into the Ancient Karst Spring, several strong underwater currents emerged out of nowhere. However, the currents were unable to obstruct Izroth's pace. Because of the Breath of the Great Sea skill he received after clearing the final phase of the Great Sea Palace raid, he was resistant to Flow. Additionally, movement restrictions no longer applied to him when submerged in water; therefore, his speed was no different than if he was on land.

'Hm? That area... The flow of water changes around there.'

Izroth's Energy Vision Sense was still not functioning properly because of the infected mana; however, he still had his natural senses to rely on.

A bit more than fifteen meters away from his position, Izroth discovered a hole roughly three meters in diameter. The strong underwater currents seemed to originate from that hole. But, more than that, Izroth could feel it even without his Energy Vision Sense-the infected mana was strongest in that location.

'Is this what Peaceful Chaos and the members of Blue Oasis were trying to investigate? If so, it's no wonder they tried to keep this a secret.'

It would not be impossible for those of Blue Oasis to reach this place. The powerful energy that attempted to invade his body was most likely the infected mana. However, infected mana was only a genuine threat if one possessed a high quantity of mana. Even if one included his artificial mana pool, Izroth's total mana did not exceed 10,000 points. For someone like him, the "attacks" from the infected mana would be weak in comparison to Empyrean who had over 120,000 mana.

There were also numerous methods at one's disposal to get past the strong underwater currents. Certainly, for a top guild like Blue Oasis, employing such methods would be relatively easy.

That being said, Izroth did not believe that Blue Oasis had ventured into that place just yet. And, even if they did, it would not have been more than a small scout group seeing as how Peaceful Chaos was not involved.

Izroth arrived in front of the hole. He was greeted by darkness, accompanied by a chilling energy that leaked out and could be felt to one's bones. After briefly examining the entrance, Izroth wasted no time heading onward.
