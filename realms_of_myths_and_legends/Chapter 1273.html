Chapter 1273: The Grand Duke’s Daughter

"Since the Treasure Hunt is beginning soon, we will save the important matters until it concludes. For now, please accept this gift as a small token of my gratitude for giving me some of your time." Vexpela said as she snapped her fingers.

The next moment, a wooden box appeared on the table in front of Izroth.

Izroth opened the wooden box and saw a black card with a diamond embedded in its center. The card emitted faint traces of mana and despite its simple appearance, Izroth detected several layers of securities contained within the card. 𝗳𝐫𝚎𝗲𝚠𝚎𝗯𝕟𝐨𝘃𝚎𝗹.𝗰𝗼𝗺

"That card is proof that you are a Diamond-Level VIP of the Golden Treasure House. With it, you can receive a 30% discount on all purchases and have access to our exclusive VIP rooms. If you do not have any gold coins in your possession, as long as you show that card to anyone store associated with the Golden Dragon Association, you will be able to make any purchase on credit. You can also borrow up to 100,000 gold coins without interest or up to 1,000,000 gold coins with a 5% interest rate. Once you touch the gem at the center of that card, you will be its official registered owner. Even if you lose or misplace it, it will not work for anyone but you." Vexpela explained.

"This is quite a gift for merely giving you some of my time." Izroth stated as he pressed his finger against the gemstone at the center of the black card.

"If I am unwilling to part with this small amount, how would I dare show my true sincerity once we become partners? So that there are no misunderstandings, even if you end up declining my offer, that card will forever belong to you. At the very least, I hope that we can maintain an amicable relationship." Vexpela said with a warm smile.

〈System Alert: You have been registered as the owner of «Diamond-Level VIP Black Card: Golden Treasure House».〉

The 30% discount alone was already a huge benefit. But, to be able to borrow up to 100,000 gold coins at no interest rate—that was roughly 15,000,000 RMB with the current conversation rate!

Of course, Izroth had plenty of funds at his disposal due to how well the Mystical Realm Palace was doing. However, he did not carry around that many gold coins in his inventory. After all, as players became stronger and gained more opportunities, the price of gold coins in RML were slowly declining. As a result, it was better for one to convert what they could to RMB to gain the most benefit.

’This works out well. With this, I won’t have to worry about the conversion fee. Still, as expected of someone with direct ties to the Golden Dragon Association. The 100,000 gold coins is described as a loan since they’re a business. But, in the end, since there is no time limit to when you have to pay it back and it has zero interest, it’s simply a gift in disguise.’

"Since you have agreed to see me again after the Golden Treasure Hunt, I will not keep you any longer. Ah, there will also be an auction after the Golden Treasure Hunt that is not open to the public. However, since you are now a Diamond-Level VIP, you have the qualifications to attend, should you choose to do so. If it is not inconvenient, perhaps we can discuss our potential partnership before the auction and then head there together?" Vexpela said.

’An auction... I haven’t been to one in RML yet. Well, it’s not surprising considering that most auctions worth going to have strict entry conditions that most players simply cannot meet currently.’

"Very well. We will follow your arrangements." Izroth responded in agreement.

"Leave everything to me." Vexpela said gracefully as she stood to her feet.

Izroth stood up from the couch and said, "There is some information that I need. Is there anyone trustworthy that you can recommend?"

Vexpela’s brows rose in surprise. Not long after, a thoughtful expression appeared on her face.

A few moments later, Vexpela answered, "There is someone I have in mind. I can set up a meeting, but their verification process is rather long. It will take at least a couple of days."

’That’s too long.’

Izroth planned to ask them to look into the matters regarding the recent attack on the Noble District as well as the identity of the noble who was targeted. He also wanted to know if there were any strange or unusual movements in the underground scene of Danaharpe that may link to the League of the Eidolon.

Seeing Izroth’s reaction, Vexpela quickly followed up, "If it is information that you need as soon as possible, then I can put in the request through my own name. You can consider this another small gift."

"In that case, I won’t decline." Izroth replied in a carefree manner.

Izroth removed a scroll and writing utensil from his inventory. He wrote down the details of his request on the scroll before he handed it over to Vexpela.

"If I may?" Vexpela asked.

Izroth gave a small nod of approval. Afterward, Vexpela read over the contents of the scroll.

"You are in luck, Sir Sagarus. It seems that only half of your request will need to be sent through the person I had in mind. As for the other half—there is no need for you to wait for a response. The noble injured by the recent attack in the Noble District is Lady Amelia Harpe." Vexpela stated.

’Amelia Harpe? That last name...’

〈System Alert: The quest «Deadly Plot of Ignorance» has been updated!〉

As Izroth pondered, he received an alert from the system as he opened up his system window.

Quest Name: Deadly Plot of Ignorance

Quest Difficulty: S

Recommended Level: N/A

Recommended Party Size: N/A

Objective: You have discovered a hidden connection that involves the «League of the Eidolon» and «Danaharpe City». Find out what the «League of the Eidolon» are plotting and put a stop to it.

Time Limit: 12 hours

-0/1 Uncover the goal behind a recent attack that happened in the Noble District of «Danaharpe City».

-0/1 Locate «???» -> «Amelia Harpe» and acquire the information in their possession.

-0/1 Help stop the «League of the Eidolon»’s plot in «Danaharpe City».

Reward:

-???

Failure:

-Lose 2 levels

-Incur Debt: 750 gold coins

-Lose 10,000 favor with «???»

-Temporarily unable to leave «Danaharpe City» for 14 days.

Special Note: This quest cannot be shared.

’The name has changed. A noble with the last name of Harpe... As far as I know, there is only one noble family permitted to have the last name of Harpe in the Amaharpe Kingdom. Is she the Grand Duke’s only child?’

Having read through many books of the Amaharpe Palace Library, naturally, Izroth learned of the noble families in Amaharpe as well as the other kingdoms.

According to the family tree he viewed in one of the books, Izroth knew that the Head of the Harpe family, the only Grand Duke in the Amaharpe Kingdom, had a single child—a daughter. However, for some reason, the book he read did not include the daughter’s name.

"I do not know who was bold enough to go after the Grand Duke’s daughter, but they will not get away unharmed. That much, I am certain of. It is no secret that the Grand Duke cherishes his only daughter." Vexpela said in a meaningful tone.

Vexpela looked as if she were full of questions; however, as someone in her line of business, she knew what questions should and should not be asked. Therefore, she decided to tread carefully.

"From what I know, Lady Harpe has yet to regain conscious after that attack. If she does not recover soon, the situation in Danaharpe will become quite complicated for a while." Vexpela sighed.

’Given how much the Grand Duke treasures his daughter, she surely must have had suitable protection around her. Then, how were they able to get close enough to injure her to the point where she’s no longer able to regain her conscious? There are still some pieces I’m missing. Regardless, I finally found a solid lead. Amelia Harpe... If she’s still unconscious, it must mean that nothing is working. If there’s one thing the Grand Duke’s family does not lack, it’s resources. The easiest way to find out the identity of those who targeted her would be to help her regain consciousness and ask her myself. But, it won’t be easy to see her directly. I suppose I’ll have to rely on that.’

"I appreciate your help. I am someone who does not leave his debts unpaid. As long as you present me with favorable terms, I will agree to a partnership. As for the details, we can work that out later." Izroth said in a carefree manner.

Since Vexpela had shown her sincerity again and again, Izroth decided to trust his own judgment and also take a risk on her.
