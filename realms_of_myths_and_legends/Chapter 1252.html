Chapter 1252: Sacred Fire, Lost Pill Recipe

"The Path of Dragons differs from person to person. Depending on the challenger, it can be seen as easy or difficult. There is no set way to determine what kind of experience one will have upon setting foot on the Path of Dragons. In the end, it is a place that tests one’s potential in a rather unique manner. That being said, Apothecary Izroth should be careful regardless of the results you end up with. Even with the protection of the third young master of the Dragon Palace, those from the hardline faction will see you as a nuisance. If your results are poor, it will help strengthen their position. If you have good results, it won’t be a good look for their side, so they may end up targeting you." Varuu explained calmly.

’The information lines up. It’s a shame there is no method to face the Path of Dragons. It seems that I will just have to cross that bridge when I come to it. On one hand, at least I was able to learn something more interesting. Well, if anything does go wrong, I should be able to escape using Void Turning.’

The Dragon Palace would undoubtedly be filled with true dragons who had stepped into the legendary realm. In truth, Izroth was not sure if he would be able to come and go as he pleases even with Void Turning at his disposal. But, he did have a few trump cards in his hand if it came to that.

...

Varuu spent the next few minutes going over some details regarding the Path of Dragons. They were mostly minor things. But, most importantly, Varuu seemed to stress the importance of Izroth being wary of the hardline faction.

’Seeing as how she likes humans and her position among the true dragons as an Elder, it’s possible that she had a falling out with the hardline faction. Maybe she was chased out of the Dragon Palace? Normally, it’s not something I should concern myself with too much, but...’

"The information about the Path of Dragons alone seems lacking as a reward for your efforts, Apothecary Izroth. I would like to give you a small reward of my own to show my gratitude." Varuu said as she casually waved her hand.

In the blink of an eye, a silver box appeared on the wooden table before Izroth.

"That box contains something that will not be that useful to you at your current stage. However, it will surely come in handy one day. Until that day, it would be best if you did not go around showing it carelessly. Even those old monsters would find this item tempting to get their hands on." Varuu cautioned.

’Oh? An item that even legendary realm powerhouses would go crazy over?’

Izroth opened the silver box as a silver light immediately flashed and the loud roar of a powerful beast echoed throughout the room, accompanied by a bone-chilling aura.

RAAAAAAWR!

For a moment, Izroth could see the piercing gaze of a ferocious beast hidden within the depths of the silver box.

This all took place within a split second. And, by the time the second was over, Izroth’s eyes shot open and everything around him returned to normal.

’An illusion?’

Izroth had many defenses against illusions. Therefore, the fact that it was still able to effect him showed just how strong the illusion contained within the silver box was.

’That aside, this item—this alone makes it worth making a trip to Danaharpe.’

...

A few minutes later...

"It is a pleasure to meet you, Apothecary Head Varuu." Worldly Skies said respectfully.

Once Izroth and Varuu wrapped up their talks, Varuu called in Worldly Skies.

Initially, Varuu planned on holding a one on one conversation with Worldly Skies; however, Worldly Skies made it clear that whatever Varuu had to say could be said in front of Izroth.

Since the person in question was okay with it, naturally, Varuu had no objections.

A third chair along with a teacup appeared at the small wooden table as Worldly Skies took her seat.

"Apothecary Worldly Skies, I heard about your situation from Apothecary Rozomius. You are fortunate that Apothecary Rozomius was the lead proctor in charge of the grade five promotion test this time around. No, rather, I should say that we are all fortunate. Otherwise, things would not have ended so peacefully." Varuu said with a foreboding tone.

Worldly Skies furrowed her brows. She did not fully understand Rozomius’ strange actions at the time; however, her instincts told her that it was not to harm her.

"Is it because of the Natural Energy I received from the Guiding Light?" Worldly Skies mentioned.

Varuu nodded and replied, "Precisely. The Natural Energy Apothecary Worldly Skies received is not just some random variant of the Natural Energy of Fire. It is the Sacred Fire. Throughout the entire history of the Apothecary Guild, the only other person known to have obtained the Sacred Fire is the founder of the Apothecary Guild." Varuu stated.

"The founder..?!" Worldly Skies exclaimed as her eyes widened in shock.

She knew that there was some secret behind her Natural Energy; however, she did not expect for it to be so shocking.

’I thought the brief energy I detected from her Natural Energy back then was strange. To think it was related to the founder of the Apothecary Guild... Sacred Fire—I’ve never heard of it before. But, I can see why Apothecary Rozomius took the actions he did, even if they were a little suspicious in the eyes of others.’

There was not much information circulating about the founder of the Apothecary Guild; however, one thing was made clear about them. The founder of the Apothecary Guild was a legendary figure who became a grade ten Apothecary and created many of the pill recipes that were still used by the Apothecary Guild until this day.

They paved the way for many generations of Apothecaries and set the foundations for those that came after them. It would not be exaggerated to say that the founder of the Apothecary Guild was one of the forefathers of Apothecaries!

If word got out that there was someone who obtained the same Natural Energy as they did, there would be no shortage of people with all kinds of greedy or harmful intentions. This was even more so in a time where they were currently at war.

If enemy forces found out that such a person existed, they would do everything in their power to eliminate them and keep them suppressed.

"As the Head of the Apothecary Guild, I would like to apologize if our actions have caused you trouble. However, I can assure you that more trouble would have come your way if no actions were taken." Varuu said.

Worldly Skies released a heavy sigh as she shook her head and responded, "No, I am grateful to Apothecary Rozomius for helping me hide this Sacred Fire. I had a feeling it was unusual, but I never expected for it to be related to the founder of the Apothecary Guild. Since I have no thought of leaving the Mystical Realm Palace and joining other forces, it helps that it was kept under wraps."

"It is good that you understand the position you are in. I am sure it goes without saying, but it would be best if you did not carelessly display your Natural Energy in front of others. In its early stages, the Natural Energy of Sacred Fire is indistinguishable from the Natural Energy of Fire. However, as you grow, the differences between the two will become like that of night and day." Varuu warned.

Meanwhile, as he listened to Varuu and Worldly Skies conversation, Izroth took the time to go through the items in his inventory.

There was one item in particular that Izroth wanted to open in the presence of someone like Varuu due to its unique nature.

Name: Sealed Lost Pill Recipe Scroll(Unique*)

Usage: Breaking the seal on this scroll will reveal a grade four or higher «Lost Pill Recipe».

’A Sealed Lost Pill Recipe... Since it is uncertain what kind of pill recipe I will obtain, it is best to be fully prepared. After all, if there is anyone who would potentially know lost pill recipes, it would be Apothecary Head Varuu. Of course, it’s just an extra precaution to save some time.’

Izroth wasted no time breaking the seal on the scroll as a new recipe appeared on his pill recipes list.

’Oh? This is...’
