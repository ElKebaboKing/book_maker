A few moments later, the trees and bushes in the forest around the Ore Cave shook as several players walked out from the east, west, and south parts of the surrounding forest.

At first, there were only a handful of players; however, as time passed by, the numbers quickly increased. From ten to fifty, from fifty to one hundred, from one hundred several hundred.

In the blink of an eye, more than five hundred players stood menacingly several meters away from the Ore Cave's entrance.

There were six players who stood at the forefront of the large group of players, five males and one woman with varying classes.

"That bastard Defeaning Silence... If he just contacted me from the start, we could have been the ones to claim it for ourselves. Now, everyone else also has their eyes on this place. I'll deal with him after we settle matters here. How annoying." Denier thought to himself.

"Hmph, it's nothing new. Sleeping Gardenia, those arrogant women—they have always been full of themselves. This time, they will have to pay a proper price for looking down on our guilds." The female player at the front of the group who went by Snow Blossom said.

Snow Blossom was a core member of Mist Palace. The long standing feud between Sleeping Gardenia and Mist Palace was no secret. The two guilds were like oil and water. Naturally, Snow Blossom could not stand the sight of Sleeping Gardenia keeping such treasures all to themselves.

Stood next to Denier and Snow Blossom were four other players from their respective top guilds.

Hirogen from Broken Exile, Uraga from Hollow Tempo, Wipeout from the Headhunter Syndicate, and Ace of Saint from Blue Oasis.

The roughly five hundred players that accompanied them was a combination of individuals from their guilds stationed in or near the Kifa Karsts. There were even a few members of their subsidiary guilds in the mix.

The Ore Cave was certainly something to covet; however, the reason why the guilds gathered such a large force in response was due to the presence of Valkyria and the Lotus Guards. If not, why would they need to send such a large combined force just to deal with a few members of Sleeping Gardenia?
