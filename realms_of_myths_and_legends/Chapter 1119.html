'I can see why this is an SS-ranked quest despite its seeming simplicity. Most players would not be able to afford to keep a unigon tamed by the princess of the Fourth Great Clan fed, let alone happy.'

At a glance, the quest appeared simple enough. All one had to do was escort the Yilsia to Tesselia while keeping her fed and happy along the way. However, the problem came with a unigon's diet.

From what Izroth read about the creatures in the Amaharpe Palace Library, unigons diets mainly consisted of high quality herbs and ores. This meant that unigons would not even glance in the direction of something below superior quality.

For the average player, feeding something superior-quality or higher herbs and ores possibly multiply times a day every day for potentially up to a month was a near-impossible task. Unless one had the backing of a top guild or well-off player organization, completing such an SS-ranked was virtually unthinkable.

Fortunately, if there was one thing Izroth did not lack it was resources.

'Keeping her fed should not prove that challenging. I wanted to give her a mythical quality source of food to gain her trust more easily, but superior quality and ancient quality resources on occasion should more than suffice.'

After expressing her gratitude to Izroth, Yilsia seemed to calm down. One could tell that she was still extremely happy, but her prideful nature of a unigon and intelligence made it so that she understood her situation. She may have been free, but there was still the issue of making it back to the Mother of All Forests and her owner.

Yilsia looked at Izroth with a pitiful yet hopeful gaze. It was as if she were pleading her his assistance.

"Do not worry. I will help you find your way back home and reunite you with your owner." Izroth said calmly.

Nwarah!

As if understanding Izroth's words and intent perfectly, Yilsia perked up as she let out a cheerful noise.
