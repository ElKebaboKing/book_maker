Chapter 1718: A Treasure Map, Breakthrough, Domain Realm Legendary Lord!

Ye Tian emerged from the Gray Mist River. When those legendary lords saw that Ye Tian's duplicate body had not actually perished, they were all dumbfounded.
"Heavens, the Endless Lord's clone actually stayed in the Gray Mist River the whole time without perishing. How did he do it?"
"The Gray Mist River will constantly attack us. If there is a flaw in one's will or if the will is not strong enough, one simply cannot stay for long. Once the injuries become too severe, it is impossible to break free from the Gray Mist River. The Endless Lord's strength is only at the Perfected Stage Legendary Lord level; how could he stay for such a long period of time?"
"I suspect the Endless Lord must have a treasure that allows him to avoid the encroachment of the Gray Mist River, right?"
"Since the Endless Lord does not fear the Gray Mist River, why did he stay in it for so long before coming out?"
The legendary lords present were not stupid and quickly guessed the reason.
Cultivation!
The Gray Mist River could assist in cultivation, tempering the will and even mending flaws. This was the true reason Ye Tian had stayed in the Gray Mist River.
Many legendary lords had guessed the reason, but they did not dare to go down. For no other reason than they could not stay in the Gray Mist River for long, so it would not provide any cultivation effect at all.
However, some Detached Stage Legendary Lords flew into the Gray Mist River. they had a certain level of confidence in staying for a long time; as long as they rushed out of the Gray Mist River when they could no longer hold on, there would be no problem.
More importantly, their will power had long been sufficient; it only had flaws.
Mending flaws was too difficult. Now that they knew this Gray Mist River could potentially help them temper their will and mend flaws, they had to try even if the price was high. As long as they mended their flaws, they could pass through the Gray Mist River and obtain another astonishing treasure or opportunity.
……
Soon, Ye Tian passed through the Gray Mist River.
At this time, a treasure fell into Ye Tian's hands.
This was a map.
Ye Tian took a simple look, and his consciousness was quickly immersed in it.
In this map, Ye Tian saw the fusion of all things, the fusion of will and Innate Eternal Rules. The fusion of will and Innate Eternal Rules transformed into a Will Rule Domain, and the Will Rule Domain continued to grow, transforming into a Will Rule Heaven and Earth...
"This is..."
Ye Tian was shocked. A small map actually perfectly displayed the cultivation from a Perfected Stage Legendary Lord to a Domain Realm Legendary Lord and even to a Detached Stage Legendary Lord.
What level of realm was required to achieve such a thing!
It could be said that the level of such a map was simply too high. Even the True Void Sacred Ground would not be able to produce such a treasure map.
"Could it be that everyone gets such a treasure?"
Ye Tian wondered.
In reality, other people's treasures were not nearly as precious as his map, only because he had crossed the Gray Mist River with the cultivation of a Perfected Stage Legendary Lord. Only then was there such an astonishing reward.
At this moment, Ye Tian was extremely excited.
With this map, it would not be very difficult for him to step into the Domain Realm Legendary Lord stage, and even his cultivation in the Domain Realm Legendary Lord stage would be smooth sailing all the way.
Just this one map alone was of immense value.
To prevent this duplicate body of his from perishing in the next trial, Ye Tian condensed another duplicate body to carry this map and not go to the next trial, while this duplicate body continued forward.
Soon, Ye Tian arrived at the location of the next trial.
Here, he saw several Domain Realm Legendary Lords, some Detached Stage Lords, and several Half-Step Legendary Stage Lords, but Wushuang Lord was not here. Clearly, Wushuang Lord had passed this trial.
This trial was a sea of fire, a gray sea of fire.
After inquiring, Ye Tian learned about the situation of this trial.
This place was called the Gray Mist Sea of Fire. One had to cross the Gray Mist Sea of Fire to be considered to have passed. The Gray Mist Sea of Fire targeted the body. If the body was not strong enough, once one entered the Gray Mist Sea of Fire, the body would be burned to ash almost instantly.
Previously, the bodies of three Detached Stage Legendary Lords had already been burned to ash and could not be healed, essentially perishing here. Even if it was a clone, the loss was not small.
If even the bodies of Detached Stage Legendary Lords could not withstand the Gray Mist Sea of Fire, Domain Realm Legendary Lords had even less to hope for.
"This trial will likely be difficult to pass!"
Ye Tian frowned.
Of course, he would not give up.
On the other side, Ye Tian's duplicate body was using that map to fully comprehend and cultivate, striving to raise his cultivation to the Domain Realm Legendary Lord stage as soon as possible.
With two ordinary Eternal Daos at the Major Stage, plus the Entry Stage Gray Mist Eternal Dao, and his will having reached the Fifth-Tier, Ye Tian actually had the qualifications to charge into the Domain Realm Legendary Lord stage, provided he could condense a Will Rule Domain.
· ········· Seeking Flowers ··· ·····
The process from being a Perfected Stage Legendary Lord to condensing a Will Rule Domain was very long.
If he did not have that map to assist him, Ye Tian would have needed at least tens of millions of Eternal Years, or even longer, to condense a Will Rule Domain.
But now with that treasure map, Ye Tian was confident he could condense a Will Rule Domain within 100,000 Eternal Years.
"I won't attempt this third trial for now. I'll focus on cultivation and charge into the Domain Realm Legendary Lord stage. Only after breaking through to the Domain Realm Legendary Lord stage will my body undergo another qualitative change, giving me hope of crossing the Gray Mist Sea of Fire," Ye Tian thought to himself.
Time slowly passed like this, and in the blink of an eye, 100,000 Eternal Years had gone by.
..... ... .......
In such a long period of time, not a single one of those legendary lords who were originally blocked had passed; they were still hesitating here.
At this time, Ye Tian had already fused his will and the Innate Eternal Rules to a certain extent outside his body and was about to condense a Will Rule Domain.
On this day.
Buzz!!!!
A terrifying Will Rule power spread out from Ye Tian's body, permeating the surrounding endless territory and gradually forming a domain.
Within this Will Rule Domain, Ye Tian could make rules dissipate with a single thought and make heaven and earth be reborn with a single thought.
Within the Will Rule Domain, one heaven and earth after another began to be born, and some uninhabited heavens and earths turned to ash with a single thought from Ye Tian.
Time and space stagnated, and even the Source Sea stopped its operation.
This was the power of the Will Rule Domain!
Not only that, Ye Tian's body also began to transform. Endless Source Sea source energy poured into his body, helping him truly complete the transformation of his body.
At this moment, Ye Tian's main body was transforming, and his other five duplicate bodies were also breaking through and transforming.
Before the Gray Mist Sea of Fire.
Those Detached Stage Legendary Lords and several Half-Step Legendary Stage Legendary Lords all looked at Ye Tian with some surprise.
"I remember it hasn't been long since the Endless Lord joined the True Void Sacred Ground. He was only an Initial Stage Legendary Lord at the beginning. He promoted to a Domain Realm Legendary Lord in such a short time?"
Such a cultivation speed was too monstrous, completely shattering their understanding.
