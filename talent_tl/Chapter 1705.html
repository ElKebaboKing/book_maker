Chapter 1704: The Qin Family's Mobilization, Entering the Boundless Sea!

The Qin family ancestor Qin Yu wanted to see was naturally the ancestor of his own lineage, a Transcendence Stage Legendary Lord. In the Boundless Sea, Legendary Stage Lords were too mysterious, and Half-step Legendary Stage Lords were all in seclusion attempting to break through to the Legendary Stage and were few in number. In reality, Transcendence Stage Legendary Lords were the overlords on the surface of the Boundless Sea, sufficient to deter all quarters.
Qin Yu's ancestor was named Qin Wuyuan, and he was not considered weak even among Transcendence Stage Legendary Lords, possessing a certain level of fame within the Boundless Sea.
"Descendant Qin Yu requests an audience with the Ancestor!"
Qin Yu shouted while standing outside the great hall.
"Come in!"
Qin Wuyuan spoke.
Whoosh!
The hall doors opened, and Qin Yu walked inside.
Soon, he met Qin Wuyuan.
Clad in a black robe, Qin Wuyuan sat upon a throne, quietly scrutinizing Qin Yu as he asked, "Qin Yu, have you come to see me because you've caused trouble?"
Qin Yu immediately said, "Ancestor, I have lost the Fire Domain Sword!"
Boom!!!
The aura of the Transcendence Stage suppressed him, making Qin Yu's entire body tremble.
Between the Initial Stage and the Transcendence Stage lay the Perfected Stage and the Domain Stage; the difference in strength between the two was simply too vast.
Consequently, Qin Yu could not resist his ancestor's aura at all, and of course, he did not dare to resist.
"The Fire Domain Sword is an Eternal Treasure of our Qin family. Although our Qin family is very ancient, we only have fewer than twenty Eternal Treasures. You were only at the Initial Stage yet were able to obtain an Eternal Treasure because the Qin family valued you. As a result, you actually lost an Eternal Treasure. Do you know how great a loss this is to the Qin family???"
Qin Wuyuan was incomparably furious.
Had Qin Yu not been a genius of the Qin family and someone he held high hopes for, he would have truly wanted to slap Qin Yu to death.
Qin Yu hurriedly said, "Ancestor, although I lost the Fire Domain Sword, I found an extremely weak Source Sea. This Source Sea has only a handful of Legendary Lords. The most powerful one is an Initial Stage Legendary Lord, but the opponent's strength is much greater than mine, and they possess an Eternal Treasure. They have even created a Legendary-level Eternal Technique, possessing combat power roughly equivalent to a weaker Domain Stage Legendary Lord."
"A new Source Sea? Tell me its approximate location. I will check it. If it happens to be the territory of some powerful force, we won't be able to take it," Qin Wuyuan said.
"Yes, Ancestor!"
Qin Yu immediately reported the location of the Source Sea.
Qin Wuyuan then began to investigate. Not long after, he discovered that the Boundless Sea truly had no records of the Source Sea. This was indeed a brand-new Source Sea, and it was also extremely weak. The Qin family could completely and easily occupy this Source Sea.
How high was the value of a Source Sea? The Qin family might even rise completely because of it and become a Legendary-level family.
Compared to a Source Sea, a mere Fire Domain Sword was indeed not worth mentioning.
Unfortunately, Qin Yu had not entered the Source Sea but had gone directly to the Legendary headquarters of the Space-Time Territory. Thus, he had not gone to the Source Sea in time to calculate its space-time coordinates in the void area. Otherwise, the Qin family could now directly construct a Space-Time Staircase to head to the Source Sea.
Qin Yu also hadn't known such a thing would happen. Otherwise, he would have definitely entered the Source Sea at the first opportunity to calculate its space-time coordinates.
In order to let the Qin family take control of the Source Sea as quickly as possible, Qin Wuyuan dispatched three top Domain Stage Legendary Lords, plus six Perfected Stage Legendary Lords and many Initial Stage Legendary Lords to head to the Source Sea together.
With three Domain Stage Legendary Lords leading the way, their speed would be very fast.
As for Transcendence Stage Legendary Lords, it was not suitable for them to be mobilized. After all, the Qin family was not without enemies. If a Transcendence Stage Legendary Lord were mobilized, it would inevitably cause a stir. If the location of the Source Sea were leaked, other forces would compete with the Qin family.
...
The Source Sea.
Space-time continued to fluctuate as layers of space-time emerged around the Hunyuan Order. Then, under Ye Tian's Space-Time Staircase secret technique, they gradually transformed into layers of stairs, extending into the depths of space-time like a flight of stone steps.
At first, these stone steps were still somewhat illusory, but they quickly became solid, as if they were real stone steps.
These stone steps were formed entirely from space-time power and contained the origin power of the Source Sea, making them extremely stable. Even ordinary Legendary Lords would find it difficult to destroy such a Space-Time Staircase. Once the Space-Time Staircase connected to the Boundless Sea and received the reinforcement of the Boundless Sea's origin, even Ye Tian himself would find it difficult to destroy it unless he spent a very long time doing so.
Additionally, the Space-Time Staircase was one-way. This was also for safety. If it were a two-way Space-Time Staircase, who would dare to recklessly build one?
A Space-Time Staircase could only be ascended, not descended...
If one entered the Boundless Sea from the Source Sea and wanted to return, they could only construct another Space-Time Staircase from the Boundless Sea to come back.
Time slowly passed, and after a long while, the Space-Time Staircase was finally completed.
Hum!!!!
A wave of fluctuations arrived as the Space-Time Staircase connected to the Boundless Sea. With the reinforcement of the Boundless Sea's origin, the Space-Time Staircase became incomparably stable and sturdy.
"It's done!"
Ye Tian said excitedly.
However, in order to construct the Space-Time Staircase, the Hunyuan Order had been heavily depleted and would require a long period of restoration before it could be as good as new. This was perhaps the only price for constructing the Space-Time Staircase.
"It's time to enter the Boundless Sea, and I must find the Zhenwu Sacred Ground as quickly as possible and then join it."
Ye Tian murmured.
Then, Ye Tian stepped onto the Space-Time Staircase step by step, enduring the pressure from endless space-time. Fortunately, his strength was powerful enough, so he was able to finish walking the Space-Time Staircase. If it were those Immortal Stage lords, they truly would have been unable to pass through the Space-Time Staircase.
Ye Tian's speed was not slow. He stepped across layer after layer of the Space-Time Staircase, and only after a long time did he truly arrive at the end of the stairs.
Here was a Space-Time Gate. Ye Tian pushed open the Space-Time Gate and entered the Boundless Sea.
...
The Boundless Sea, a certain sea region.
The seawater here was actually the same as in the Source Sea, formed by the convergence of endless origin energy.
The surroundings were completely empty. Having just entered this place, Ye Tian was also unclear as to where he was.
Subsequently.
He continued to fly. After flying for a long time, he finally saw a vast and boundless island.
"This should be a Boundless Island!"
Ye Tian guessed.
The status of a Boundless Island was like that of the Space-Time Territory outside the Source Sea; any Boundless Island was a prosperous place.
Although Ye Tian was not clear about the situation of this Boundless Island, he soon prepared to enter. At the very least, he needed to understand his current location and then inquire about the direction of the Zhenwu Sacred Ground so that he could head there.
Whoosh!
Ye Tian entered this Boundless Island from the Boundless Sea.
