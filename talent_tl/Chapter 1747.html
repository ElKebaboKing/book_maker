Chapter 1746: Crushing Lord Starshatter with the Power of Time!

Lord Starshatter was surrounded by several half-step Legendary Realm Lords and was currently breaking through.
Clearly, this was Lord Starshatter's true body; otherwise, he would not be so heavily protected.
Even if it were not his true body, Ye Tian did not care. What he wanted was Lord Starshatter's Eternal Treasure.
Buzz buzz buzz!!!
One by one, the half-step Legendary Realm Lords of Zhenwu Holy Land pursued Lord Starshatter and the other half-step Legendary Realm Lords.
Along the way, the void was shattered.
Soon, a half-step Legendary Realm Lord from Guixing Tower sacrificed himself to open a passage for Lord Starshatter and the others, allowing them to successfully escape Guixing Boundless Island.
However, this half-step Legendary Realm Lord was likely a clone. If it were his true body, he certainly would not have foolishly sacrificed himself.
"Pursue!"
Multiple half-step Legendary Realm Lords from Zhenwu Holy Land gave chase, and Ye Tian followed them as well.
However, some half-step Legendary Realm Lords did not join the pursuit, instead focusing on breaking into the various treasuries of Guixing Tower.
...
"What should I do? No one is coming to save me, and the Ancestor is also surrounded by numerous Legendary Realm Lords."
Lord Starshatter was in a panic.
Had he known Guixing Tower would be invaded, his true body would not have stayed there.
But now, it was too late for regrets.
Although he was nearly invincible at the Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord level, he was no match for a half-step Legendary Realm Lord. Even if he went all out, he could only hold out for a short while against an ordinary half-step Legendary Realm Lord.
"Seniors, buy me one second of time. I will use a secret treasure to escape."
Lord Starshatter requested.
"Fine!"
Several half-step Legendary Realm Lords nodded helplessly. Although some among them were true bodies, stalling for a single second was nothing. They still had a hope of survival. If Lord Starshatter fell while Lord Guixing was still alive, they would not only be hunted by Zhenwu Holy Land but might even be hunted by Lord Guixing himself.
Thus, protecting Lord Starshatter to buy him a second was a small price to pay.
Immediately.
These half-step Legendary Realm Lords each erupted with their full strength, proactively engaging the half-step Legendary Realm Lords of Zhenwu Holy Land in battle to ensure Lord Starshatter remained undisturbed.
At that moment, Lord Starshatter took out a talisman. This was a talisman that could activate Void Sea Sand.
As the talisman burned, the Void Sea Sand fused into the tip of the Eternal Treasure sword erupted with spatial fluctuations.
In an instant.
Lord Starshatter's body was carried into the Void Sea by the power of the Void Sea Sand. After a brief stay in the Void Sea, he crossed a vast distance in an instant and escaped.
Under such circumstances, even half-step Legendary Realm Lords could not keep up.
The reason he had waited until now to use this move was that there were too many half-step Legendary Realm Lords from Zhenwu Holy Land at the Guixing Tower headquarters, and they had even sealed the void, making it very difficult to escape into the Void Sea.
Only Legendary Realm Lords could easily enter the Void Sea.
Therefore, he had to break out of Guixing Boundless Island before he dared to use this move.
"Trying to leave?"
Ye Tian silently activated an Eternal Treasure within his body—the Ruyi Void Shuttle.
This Middle-rank Eternal Treasure could also perform long-distance travel and could temporarily enter the Void Sea.
Shua!
Ye Tian's body vanished as he pursued Lord Starshatter.
In a void region outside the Boundless Sea, Lord Starshatter's body appeared.
"I finally escaped. Aside from a very few half-step Legendary Realm Lords, no one should be able to catch me."
Lord Starshatter murmured.
But in the next instant, his expression changed.
The void behind him twisted, and a figure emerged.
He originally thought the opponent was a half-step Legendary Realm Lord, which caused his expression to shift drastically.
But soon, he breathed a sigh of relief.
"Just a Transcendence Realm Lord. It seems to be a Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord who possesses long-distance travel capabilities. I'll leave after killing him."
Lord Starshatter thrust out his sword.
This was an Eternal Treasure containing Void Sea Sand. A single strike was enough to shatter the Legendary Body of a Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord. It was very difficult for a Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord to withstand the power of Void Sea Sand; just like entering the Void Sea, they would collapse instantly.
In the void region, space shattered as the sword strike arrived.
Abruptly.
Ye Tian smiled and waved his hand. Lord Starshatter's body froze, and even the Eternal Treasure containing Void Sea Sand could not advance another step.
"Time Control!"
Lord Starshatter's expression changed drastically.
"No!"
He could not even cry out before his body was directly crushed by the power of time, leaving behind that Eternal Treasure.
The Eternal Treasure became a masterless object, with layers of time and space stacking up as if it were about to enter an unreachable special state.
But Ye Tian reached out gently and grabbed the Eternal Treasure.
For someone at his level of Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord, capturing an Eternal Treasure was an easy task, provided they had sufficient control over time and space. Moreover, since he had completely mastered time, taking the Eternal Treasure was naturally effortless.
"Time to go. To extract the Void Sea Sand, I'll likely need the help of a Legendary Realm Lord."
Ye Tian murmured.
Then, he took a step across the distance and vanished.
Time passed—
One by one, the bases of Guixing Tower were destroyed, and even Guixing Boundless Island was reduced to ruins.
Throughout the Boundless Sea, the Legendary Lords of Guixing Tower were being hunted down.
Several forces of the Wanhai Alliance were also at war with Zhenwu Holy Land, but with the destruction of Guixing Tower, the conflict soon subsided.
After all, continuing the slaughter held little meaning, and the Legendary Lords of Zhenwu Holy Land were also retreating back to their sanctuary.
On the River of Time corresponding to the Boundless Sea.
Lord Guixing was still fleeing, but his body was already heavily injured.
At this time, one of his clones had fought to the death to rescue his true body, but it had also severely wounded the clones of two half-step Legendary Realm Lords.
Yet, it was still futile. Even with some Legendary Realm Lords from the Wanhai Alliance coming to help, they still could not break the deadlock.
Zhenwu Holy Land had meticulously prepared for this operation; it was unlikely he would escape.

Bang!
Multiple Legendary Realm Lords struck from across the distance, directly knocking him into the depths of the River of Time.
Next, he was knocked from the River of Time into the Void Sea by the Legendary Realm Lords of Zhenwu Holy Land, and his Legendary Realm body suffered further annihilation.
Suddenly.
News arrived that Lord Fanxing had fallen in battle, his true body slain.
At this point, Lord Guixing was the only Legendary Realm Lord left in Guixing Tower.
"Lord Longgu, do you really insist on killing me?"
Lord Guixing roared in fury.
"You once killed a Legendary Realm Lord of our Zhenwu Holy Land. Do you think we would let you go?"
Lord Longgu said coldly.
"Hahahaha!"
Lord Guixing laughed in despair.
Abruptly.
A fishhook appeared from the void and suddenly hooked Lord Guixing.
In the next instant, Lord Guixing's true body was fished away. The speed was so fast that Lord Longgu and the others could not even react.
