Chapter 1727: Deep Within the Eternal Time Gold, the Corpse of a Legendary Realm Lord!

Which of the eight half-step Legendary Realm Lords would go in?
At this moment, they were currently discussing it.
Right then, Ye Tian spoke up, "I'll go in. I still have a clone within my body."
As he spoke, Ye Tian's second copy body appeared.
This second copy body was naturally one he had just condensed. In any case, the cost of condensing a copy body was very small; for him now, that cost was almost negligible.
Since the eight half-step Legendary Realm Lords did not dare to take the risk, he would go in to explore.
"Endless Lord, you want to go in?"
Wushuang Lord was somewhat surprised.
Ye Tian explained, "My clone is somewhat special; the cost paid is not great."
Seeing Ye Tian say this, the other half-step Legendary Realm Lords agreed.
Whoosh!
Ye Tian's copy body shuttled into the Eternal Time Gold mineral vein. As soon as he entered, the power of time began to erode him.
However, Ye Tian's life-preserving abilities were still very strong, so he would not fall that easily.
At this moment, in the outside world.
Ye Tian's other copy body projected the situation inside the Eternal Time Gold mineral vein almost simultaneously, allowing the eight half-step Legendary Realm Lords to watch.
Deeper!
Deeper!
Soon, Ye Tian had delved a long distance, and a vast amount of Eternal Time Gold ore was presented before the eight half-step Legendary Realm Lords.
"This mineral vein contains quite a lot of Eternal Time Gold!"
Wushuang Lord said excitedly.
The other half-step Legendary Realm Lords were also very happy. With this much Eternal Time Gold, it was enough for the Zhenwu Holy Land to construct a Bridge of Time, and there would even be surplus Eternal Time Gold to sustain it for a long time.
Originally, Ye Tian and the eight half-step Legendary Realm Lords were all quite happy.
Suddenly.
Ye Tian saw a flickering, dreamlike River of Time.
Greeting him was a pair of giant pupils, staring at him like two worlds.
These pupils belonged to an unimaginably massive Purple Aurora Beast.
And his arrival seemed to have triggered some kind of change.
In an instant, a terrifying power erupted, directly annihilating his body.
This power extended outward, causing the expressions of Wushuang Lord and the others outside to change.
"Go!"
Wushuang Lord took Ye Tian and fled, and the other half-step Legendary Realm Lords also retreated quickly. However, a trace of the aftershock still struck, directly wounding the eight half-step Legendary Realm Lords.
However, this copy body of Ye Tian was only injured and did not fall, thanks to Wushuang Lord's protection.
Outside the Purple Continent.
Wushuang Lord and the other eight half-step Legendary Realm Lords felt a lingering fear.
"Endless Lord, what happened just now?"
Wushuang Lord asked hurriedly.
Ye Tian frowned and said, "I seemed to see a Purple Aurora Beast far larger than the others. Half of its body seemed to be in the River of Time, and half seemed to be in a special space. I locked eyes with its pupils, but I didn't sense any life force from it. It should be a corpse."
Subsequently.
Ye Tian described the specific images he saw, leaving nothing out.
But when he tried to manifest this image, it directly collapsed.
"Endless Lord, such an image cannot be drawn; it must have violated time-space forbidden secrets. Moreover, we suspect that Purple Aurora Beast should be a Legendary Realm Lord."
Wushuang Lord speculated.
"What, a Legendary Realm Lord!"
Although Ye Tian had some suspicions, he was still shocked to receive confirmation from Wushuang Lord.
"Our knowledge regarding Legendary Realm Lords is also very limited. The situation here is somewhat special; we must report this to the Legendary Realm Lords."
Wushuang Lord decided.
-------------------
Zhenwu Holy Land.
Wushuang Lord came to visit a Legendary Realm Lord—Longgu Lord.
Currently, the most active person in the Zhenwu Holy Land was Longgu Lord.
"Wushuang, why have you come to see me?"
Longgu Lord asked.
"We have discovered an Eternal Time Gold mineral vein!" Wushuang Lord said.
"Oh, you found an Eternal Time Gold mineral vein? Good luck. How is the yield? If it's enough to build a Bridge of Time, I will help you build it. You didn't come here just to ask me to help build a Bridge of Time, did you?" Longgu Lord said.
"No!" Wushuang Lord reported, "The yield of this Eternal Time Gold mineral vein is very high, but a special situation has occurred. We discovered the corpse of a Legendary Realm Lord inside, and it is a Purple Aurora Beast. Half of its body seems to be in the River of Time and half in a special space. The shockwave from its aura alone heavily injured us half-step Legendary Realm Lords. This Legendary Realm Lord is likely incredibly terrifying even among Legendary Realm Lords; otherwise, it wouldn't possess such power after death."
"A Legendary Realm Lord, a Purple Aurora Beast!"
Longgu Lord's expression turned solemn as he recalled something.
"Let's go. Tell me the location; I'll make a trip there," Longgu Lord said.
"Yes!"
Wushuang Lord stated the location of that Purple Continent.
After learning the location, Longgu Lord took a step into the River of Time, walking through it step by step, and soon stepped out of the River of Time again.
With this step, he arrived directly at the Purple Continent.
……
"Greetings, Longgu Lord!"
The eight half-step Legendary Realm Lords all spoke respectfully.
Ye Tian also followed suit.
"It's right here, isn't it?"
Longgu Lord murmured.
"Yes, right there!"
Wushuang Lord pointed to the location.
"I sense it. Let's go together. With me protecting you, do not worry," Longgu Lord said.
"Yes!"
Ye Tian, Wushuang Lord, and the other eight half-step Legendary Realm Lords followed Longgu Lord into that Eternal Time Gold passage.
Unlike the last time he entered, Ye Tian did not feel any suppression this time, as if the place had suddenly become safe.
But he was very clear that this was because Longgu Lord was single-handedly withstanding the suppression and erosion here.
Soon, everyone arrived deep within the Eternal Time Gold mineral vein.
"This Eternal Time Gold mineral vein is very good, enough to build a Bridge of Time and maintain it for a very long time. Later, I will directly take this mineral vein back to the Zhenwu Holy Land; otherwise, if we mine it slowly, other forces might come to snatch it," Longgu Lord said.
"We shall follow Longgu Lord's suggestion," Wushuang Lord and the other half-step Legendary Realm Lords nodded.
Finally.
Everyone arrived at the place where Ye Tian's copy body had previously been annihilated.
Here, everyone saw the corpse of that Purple Aurora Beast.
Boom!!!!
A terrifying power attacked, but it was blocked by Longgu Lord. Everyone only saw endless time-space storms colliding, but they did not feel any danger.
"It really is the corpse of a Legendary Realm Lord!"
Wushuang Lord, Umeng Lord, and the others said in shock.
At this moment, Longgu Lord sighed, "It really is him.".
