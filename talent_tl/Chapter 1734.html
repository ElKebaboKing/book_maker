Chapter 1733: Supreme Legendary Body, Three Treasures

"Endless Lord broke through?"
"He only meditated on five Wutong Fire Spirit Leaves!"
"This... I've meditated on over 3,000 Wutong Fire Spirit Leaves and still haven't broken through. Endless Lord has only been here for such a short time, and he broke through after meditating on just five leaves. This is too demoralizing."
"Perhaps Endless Lord was already on the verge of a breakthrough, and this bit of meditation was just enough to push him over. That must be it."
Regardless, these Domain Stage Legend Lords were incredibly envious of Ye Tian at this moment, hoping they would have such a day as well.
As time passed, Ye Tian's Will Rule Domain gradually transformed into a Will Rule Heaven and Earth.
A Will Rule Domain was far from comparable to a Will Rule Heaven and Earth. A Will Rule Domain could only control all the rules within its range, whereas a Will Rule Heaven and Earth could modify and reshape them, taking things to a higher level.
The Will Rule Heaven and Earth could easily crush a Will Rule Domain. Wherever his body went, that place became his heaven and earth. With a single thought, he could change everything within that heaven and earth; with a single thought, he could transform an ordinary place into a precious land by modifying its rules.
However, the greatest function of the Will Rule Heaven and Earth was still to impact the Transcendence Stage Legend Lord realm and enhance combat power; everything else was merely a secondary effect.
"The Will Rule Heaven and Earth is established. Now, as long as I condense a Legendary Body, I can break through to the Transcendence Stage Legend Lord realm."
Ye Tian thought to himself.
Comparatively speaking, a Will Rule Heaven and Earth was easier to condense, while condensing a Legendary Body was extremely difficult.
Many Domain Stage Legend Lords had condensed a Will Rule Heaven and Earth but remained unable to condense a Legendary Body. There were quite a few such Legend Lords, and they were referred to as Half-Step Transcendence Stage Legend Lords.
Ye Tian had obtained the blood tissue of a Supreme Legendary Body creature, and it was even active blood tissue. If he were to condense an ordinary Legendary Body, it truly wouldn't be difficult for him.
But he looked down on ordinary Legendary Bodies. After all, the Legendary Body affected combat power and even had an impact at the Legend Realm Lord level.
If one were a Peak Legend Realm Lord with a Supreme Legendary Body and had improved all other aspects to the Peak stage, they would truly be nearly invincible.
However, he also knew very well that condensing a Supreme Legendary Body was incomparably difficult. Relying solely on the active blood tissue of a Supreme Legendary Body creature was far from enough to help him condense one; it could only give him a sufficient understanding of the Supreme Legendary Body.
In the following period, Ye Tian continued to study the Supreme Legendary Body, but progress was slow, and he didn't gain much.
"It seems that studying in silence won't work; I must consult someone else."
Ye Tian thought of Wushuang Lord, but Wushuang Lord had now broken through to the Legend Realm Lord level. He didn't know if she would be willing to see him.
But he still had to try.
Thus, he sent a message to consult Wushuang Lord.
Not long after, Wushuang Lord replied to Ye Tian, telling him to simply come to her palace.
True Void Holy Land, Wushuang Palace.
Ye Tian arrived here and, without encountering any obstacles, went directly into Wushuang Lord's palace.
"Greetings, Wushuang Lord."
Ye Tian said respectfully.
"Endless Lord, your cultivation speed is truly fast enough. You've condensed a Will Rule Heaven and Earth so quickly. You didn't come this time to ask me about condensing a Legendary Body, did you?" Wushuang Lord said with a smile.
"I cannot hide it from Wushuang Lord; that is exactly my intention." Ye Tian spoke truthfully. "I wish to condense a Supreme Legendary Body, so I have come to seek guidance from Wushuang Lord."
"What? You want to condense a Supreme Legendary Body?"
Wushuang Lord was incredibly surprised.
"Yes!"
Ye Tian nodded.
"Do you know the level of difficulty required to condense a Supreme Legendary Body?" Wushuang Lord asked.
"I know!" Ye Tian nodded, then took out his portion of active blood tissue. "I had a bit of a fortuitous encounter and obtained this opportunity from the Gray Mist Stone Statue last time, so I wanted to try condensing a Supreme Legendary Body."
He wasn't afraid of Wushuang Lord being greedy when he took out this active blood tissue, because a Legend Realm Lord might not necessarily care for such a thing.
"Blood tissue from a Supreme Legendary Body creature, and it's still active. This should be the work of an Eternal Controller. For a Domain Stage Legend Lord, it is indeed a priceless treasure," Wushuang Lord evaluated.
"However, it is not enough to allow you to condense a Supreme Legendary Body."
Wushuang Lord still didn't think highly of Ye Tian's chances. It wasn't that the value of the blood tissue wasn't high enough, but no matter how high the value was, it was impossible for it to allow Ye Tian to condense a Supreme Legendary Body because the conditions were insufficient.
"I ask for Wushuang Lord's guidance."
Ye Tian requested.
Wushuang Lord spoke slowly, "Do you know if anyone among the many Eternal Seas in the Void Area can condense a Supreme Legendary Body?"
"I do not know."
Ye Tian shook his head.
"The descendants of Eternal Controllers!" Wushuang Lord revealed. "Eternal Controllers can help their descendants condense a Supreme Legendary Body when they attempt to break through to the Transcendence Stage Legend Lord realm. This is because condensing a Supreme Legendary Body requires not only a sufficient understanding of it but also three incredibly rare materials. These three materials are exceptionally scarce."
"The first is the Time River Bead!"
"The second is Void Sea Sand!"
"The third is the Spacetime Ancient Leaf."
Ye Tian didn't recognize any of these three treasures, nor had he heard much about them.
At this time, Wushuang Lord smiled and said, "Are you about to say that you don't know these three treasures? Very well, let me tell you about them. First of all, the True Void Holy Land does not have these three treasures, so you cannot rely on the True Void Holy Land. Even we Legend Realm Lords do not possess them."
If even Legend Realm Lords didn't have them, Ye Tian immediately understood how rare these three things were.
Wushuang Lord began to introduce them. "The Time River Bead comes from the River of Time. The River of Time does not naturally produce items; it is entirely filled with the power of time. However, if Eternal Controllers fight within the River of Time, it is highly likely that the time power in a certain section of the river will be condensed and compressed, resulting in a qualitative change. After endless years, a Time River Bead may be born."
"As for Void Sea Sand, it is born in the Void Sea, and its method of birth is similar to the Time River Bead. I don't know where a Time River Bead can be found, but I do know where there is Void Sea Sand."
Wushuang Lord said.
"Where is there Void Sea Sand?" Ye Tian asked hurriedly.
Wushuang Lord said, "The Eerie Star Tower. The Eerie Star Lord of the Eerie Star Tower possesses a special Eternal Supreme Treasure. He integrated Void Sea Sand into the Eternal Supreme Treasure, the Eerie Star Arrow, making that treasure incredibly terrifying. Furthermore, he definitely integrated more than one grain of Void Sea Sand; the exact amount is unknown. Therefore, it is very likely that Void Sea Sand still exists within the Eerie Star Tower."
Ye Tian nodded secretly, indicating his understanding.
"As for the Spacetime Ancient Leaf, I only know the name; I am unclear about anything else."
After saying this, Wushuang Lord asked Ye Tian again, "Knowing the difficulty of condensing a Supreme Legendary Body, do you still want to continue?"
"I do!"
Ye Tian replied.
Wushuang Lord was silent for a moment, then suggested, "Perhaps if you go to a certain place, you might find these three treasures or information about them."
