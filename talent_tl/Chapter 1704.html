Chapter 1703: Preparing to Build the Spacetime Ladder!

Ye Tian browsed the Dark Mist Lord's memories, focusing only on the ones he wanted, and soon learned the location of the Boundless Sea.
In reality, the Boundless Sea was not very far from the Source Sea, but reaching it required passing through two dangerous places.
The Purple Polar Light Domain and the Darkness Barrier.
The Purple Polar Light Domain was a special zone in the void region, completely covered by a purple light. It was a forbidden zone that even void beasts dared not enter. Without the strength of a Legendary Realm Lord, entering would result in being assimilated by the purple polar power and becoming part of the domain. It was said that the Purple Polar Light Domain expanded every Eternal Year, and the energy required for this expansion came from external organisms.
Furthermore, while Legendary Realm Lords could enter without dying, they would still be suppressed. Their speed would become extremely slow within the Purple Polar Light Domain, and once inside, they would lose all sense of direction, not knowing how long they would need to fly to cross it.
Facing the unknown, several Legendary Realm Lords from the Spacetime Territory had tried to cross the Purple Polar Light Domain, but they eventually gave up due to a lack of persistence or failing to find the right direction. After all, there were no opportunities within the Purple Polar Light Domain, so it wasn't worth their exploration.
If one crossed the Purple Polar Light Domain and found the right direction, they would enter another void region. After flying further, they would reach the Darkness Barrier—an endless barrier formed entirely of darkness power. Without sufficient strength, one simply could not break the Darkness Barrier to continue forward.
Basically, a Legendary Realm Lord had the strength to break the Darkness Barrier and enter the next region.
Passing through these two places led to the void region where the Boundless Sea was located. From there, flying to the Boundless Sea was very easy.
However, the six Legendary Lords of the Source Sea had never completely flown across the Purple Polar Light Domain because they didn't know what lay beyond; otherwise, they would have tried at all costs to fly through the Purple Polar Light Domain.
Now that Ye Tian knew this secret, finding the Boundless Sea was naturally very easy.
"Back then, the Dark Mist Lord likely staked everything on a desperate gamble to escape this region. He charged into the Purple Polar Light Domain and accidentally crossed it, then passed through the Darkness Barrier to find the Boundless Sea!" Ye Tian murmured.
Additionally.
He discovered from the Dark Mist Lord's memories that the lord wasn't captured immediately upon entering the Boundless Sea. He had lived there for a period before being accidentally captured by Qin Yu. The Dark Mist Lord also had some understanding of the Boundless Sea.
For example, the general situation of the Boundless Sea and the strength of the Qin Family.
The Qin Family was a top-tier power in the Boundless Sea, possessing one Half-step Legend Realm Lord, three Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords, six Domain Realm Legendary Lords, and over a dozen Perfected Realm Legendary Lords. As for Initial Realm Legendary Lords, the Qin Family had even more.
Qin Yu was highly valued in the Qin Family, which was why he was bestowed an Eternal Supreme Treasure.
An Eternal Supreme Treasure was not only incredibly precious to Qin Yu but also very important to the Qin Family.
It could be said that the Qin Family wouldn't let this go; they would inevitably come to reclaim the Eternal Supreme Treasure.
When that time came, it wouldn't be Qin Yu arriving, but the Qin Family's Domain Realm Legendary Lords, or perhaps even a Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord.
No matter how strong Ye Tian was, he couldn't contend with the experts of the Qin Family.
Of course, Ye Tian had several choices.
First, leave the Source Sea; then neither the Qin Family nor Qin Yu could find him.
Second, find a backer that the Qin Family would fear immensely, preventing them from acting against him.
At this moment, Ye Tian thought of a power—the True Void Sacred Land!
The True Void Sacred Land was a power truly at the Peak of the Boundless Sea, far beyond what the Qin Family could compare to.
Previously, Ye Tian had learned about the True Void Sacred Land from the Prisoner of Mount Mo, but that was a long time ago. Now, he found more information from the Dark Mist Lord's memories, as the Dark Mist Lord had specifically inquired about it.
The current True Void Sacred Land indeed lacked its former glory because a great war had broken out in the Boundless Sea long ago, and the True Void Sacred Land was involved. Many powers perished in that war. Although the True Void Sacred Land didn't perish, its losses were heavy, with five Legend Realm Lords falling.
Even so, the True Void Sacred Land remained the most top-tier Peak power in the Boundless Sea.
However, since that war, the True Void Sacred Land rarely recruited members, and the requirements had become much higher. Joining the True Void Sacred Land was extremely difficult.
"I previously gained many benefits from the ruins of the True Void Sacred Land, so perhaps I have a destiny with them. I could join this power. From the Dark Mist Lord's memories, the Boundless Sea is connected to multiple powerful Eternal Seas, and Legendary Realm Lords from other Eternal Seas have joined powers in the Boundless Sea. So, I am also eligible to join the True Void Sacred Land," Ye Tian thought.
Flying directly from the Source Sea to the Boundless Sea was too slow. By the time he arrived, the Qin Family's experts might have already reached the Source Sea, and it would be too late.
There was only one way.
"I must build a Spacetime Ladder!"
Ye Tian decided.
The Spacetime Ladder was a method used in the Boundless Sea to reach other Eternal Seas. It used Eternal Supreme Treasures to stack layers of spacetime and then applied the Spacetime Ladder secret technique to construct a passage to other Eternal Seas.
Since Ye Tian now knew the spacetime coordinates of the Boundless Sea's void region and was currently in the Source Sea, he could naturally calculate the Source Sea's position.
The Dark Mist Lord had accidentally stumbled into the Boundless Sea and didn't know the Source Sea's spacetime coordinates. Since this was an invention of the Boundless Sea, it could only be calculated slowly after returning. Otherwise, he could have helped Qin Yu build a Spacetime Ladder back then.
"The Dark Mist Lord and Qin Yu are truly my opportunities. Otherwise, how would I know the location of the Boundless Sea or about the Spacetime Ladder?"
The Spacetime Ladder was a common secret technique in the Boundless Sea. The only difficulty was the Eternal Supreme Treasure, which was rare. Not many Legendary Realm Lords possessed one, but Ye Tian had more than one, so he could build it.
On the other side, after Ye Tian took the Eternal Supreme Treasure, the Fire Domain Sword, from Qin Yu, he immediately killed Qin Yu and began constructing the Spacetime Ladder.
In the Boundless Sea, at the Qin Family.
Qin Yu immediately sensed that his clone had fallen and was furious.
He knew his Eternal Supreme Treasure, the Fire Domain Sword, was completely stolen by Ye Tian. He no longer planned to hide it and intended to inform the Qin Family elders. Even if he were punished, contributing the location of a strange Eternal Sea would be enough to make up for his mistake and would even be a great merit!
Thus, he went to the Qin Family's main hall to seek an audience with the Qin Family's ancestor...
