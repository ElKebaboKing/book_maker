Chapter 1732: Wutong Fire Spirit Leaf!

Ye Tian continued his secluded cultivation, enhancing his Will Rule Domain.
Finally, on this day, his Will Rule Domain reached the Extreme of the Domain Stage Legendary Lord level.
If he wanted to improve further, he had to transform his Will Rule Domain and promote it to a Will Rule World, which was one of the two necessary conditions for breaking through to the Transcendence Stage Legendary Lord level.
Breaking through to a Will Rule World had little to do with the control of time, so Ye Tian could not easily transform his Will Rule Domain.
Therefore, he still needed to rely on some opportunities.
Zhenwu Holy Land provided an opportunity to every member who cultivated to the Extreme of the Domain Stage Legendary Lord level, and Ye Tian could also obtain such an opportunity.
Thus, he immediately applied for the opportunity from Zhenwu Holy Land.
Soon.
Zhenwu Holy Land agreed.
"Endless Lord, you may enter the land of opportunity controlled by Zhenwu Holy Land—the Wutong Cave Heaven World."
Lord Wumeng came to inform Ye Tian.
"Thank you!"
Ye Tian expressed his gratitude.
The Wutong Cave Heaven World was a precious land of Zhenwu Holy Land, helpful for Domain Stage Legendary Lords to comprehend the Will Rule World.
Of course, it did not guarantee that the Will Rule Domain would transform into a Will Rule World, but any Domain Stage Legendary Lord who entered the Wutong Cave Heaven World basically had a ten percent chance of achieving a breakthrough.
A ten percent chance might not seem high, but in reality, for many Domain Stage Legendary Lords, this was already a very high probability.
The Wutong Cave Heaven World was located in the Boundless Sea, guarded by three Half-Step Legend Stage Lords. Of course, they were guarded by avatars, so even if it were invaded, the losses would not be too great.
Now that Ye Tian's application was approved, he didn't need any credentials and could enter the Wutong Cave Heaven World directly; those three Half-Step Legend Stage Lords would not stop him.
Soon.
Ye Tian arrived at the Wutong Cave Heaven World in the Boundless Sea. In the void near the massive fiery-red spatial gate, three ancient palaces floated, where the avatars of the three Half-Step Legend Stage Lords resided.
They were almost always on rotation, but there would never be fewer than three Half-Step Legend Stage Lords.
This also illustrated the importance of the Wutong Cave Heaven World to Zhenwu Holy Land. After all, throughout endless ages, it had cultivated many Legendary Lords for Zhenwu Holy Land who comprehended the Will Rule World, most of whom eventually became Transcendence Stage Legendary Lords.
Without this precious land, the number of Transcendence Stage Legendary Lords in Zhenwu Holy Land would be much smaller.
Ye Tian passed through the entrance gate of the Wutong Cave Heaven World and entered.
The entire Wutong Cave Heaven World consisted of fiery-red land and void. At this time, there were about eighteen Domain Stage Legendary Lords meditating and cultivating here.
There was no time limit for meditation and cultivation here, but the longer one cultivated without a breakthrough, the harder it became to break through later. Therefore, if they could not break through, Domain Stage Legendary Lords would leave and not stay for too long.
The reason the Wutong Cave Heaven World could help Domain Stage Legendary Lords comprehend the Will Rule World was because of the Wutong Fire Spirit Tree.
The Wutong Fire Spirit Tree was a very special Sacred tree, located at the intersection of endless space-time layers, similar to an Eternal Supreme Treasure—an untouchable existence.
However, every Eternal Year, a Wutong Fire Spirit Leaf would drift down from the Wutong Fire Spirit Tree. While a single Wutong Fire Spirit Leaf could not exist for long, it could perfectly fuse a person's Will and Rules, creating a type of Rule World.
Thus, when a Wutong Fire Spirit Leaf fell, one of the Legendary Lords at the Extreme of the Domain Stage could use it for meditation and cultivation to comprehend the Will Rule World.
One leaf per Eternal Year, and since there weren't too many Legendary Lords who could reach the Extreme of the Domain Stage, there were enough to go around.
Basically, if a Legendary Lord had not comprehended it after 1,000 leaves, the possibility of success was not very high.
Of course, many Domain Stage Legendary Lords would keep waiting and only choose to give up after failing to break through even after 10,000 leaves.
At this moment, there were only nineteen Legendary Lords at the Extreme of the Domain Stage here. Thus, in the distribution, Ye Tian would be allocated a Wutong Fire Spirit Leaf every nineteen Eternal Years, and the other Domain Stage Legendary Lords would not fight him for it.
Of course, meditating on the Wutong Fire Spirit Tree without the help of a Wutong Fire Spirit Leaf also had a similar effect, though it was much, much weaker.
In the void around the Wutong Fire Spirit Tree, Ye Tian arrived and began to meditate silently, waiting for his Wutong Fire Spirit Leaf to arrive.
"Endless Lord, you've arrived. Let's discuss which Wutong Fire Spirit Leaf will belong to you."
"I have already meditated on many Wutong Fire Spirit Leaves; I'm in no hurry for now."
"I'm not in a hurry either. It's fine to let the Endless Lord meditate on one first."
After some discussion, it was decided that from now on, the ninth Wutong Fire Spirit Leaf would belong to Ye Tian.
"Thank you, everyone!"
Ye Tian thanked them.
In the blink of an eye, nine Eternal Years passed.
On this day.
Another Wutong Fire Spirit Leaf drifted out from the Wutong Fire Spirit Tree.
"Endless Lord, it's your turn."
The other Legendary Lords at the Extreme of the Domain Stage said.
Ye Tian nodded and immediately guided the Wutong Fire Spirit Leaf over, fusing his own Will and Rules into it.
Boom!!!!
The two began to fuse, starting the transformation from a Will Rule Domain to a Will Rule World.
At the same time, Ye Tian was also using the treasure he obtained from the Gray Mist Statue to assist his meditation, because that treasure also contained the evolution of a Will Rule World. However, that treasure map only revealed the process of promoting a Will Rule Domain to a Will Rule World, rather than utilizing Ye Tian's own Will and Rules.
But combining the two provided immense help to Ye Tian.
Unknowingly, this Wutong Fire Spirit Leaf exhausted its power.
But by this time, Ye Tian had already touched the threshold of the Will Rule World.
"With a few more Wutong Fire Spirit Leaves, I will surely be able to break through!"
Ye Tian said with full confidence.
In this way, Ye Tian meditated on the Wutong Fire Spirit Tree while also studying that mysterious treasure map, silently waiting for his next Wutong Fire Spirit Leaf.
After more than a dozen Eternal Years passed, Ye Tian obtained another Wutong Fire Spirit Leaf and used it again to comprehend the Will Rule World.
Gradually, dozens of Eternal Years passed.
On this day.
Ye Tian began to meditate on his fifth Wutong Fire Spirit Leaf.
Halfway through his meditation, Ye Tian's insights accumulated to the Extreme, triggering a qualitative change.
Boom!!!
The Will Rule Domain began to transform into a Will Rule World!
And this scene completely stunned those Legendary Lords at the Extreme of the Domain Stage.
