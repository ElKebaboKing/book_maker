Chapter 1738: Three Treasures, A Startling Idea!

Inside the azure palace.
Ye Tian walked in and encountered no danger.
Clearly, the words on that stele were not false.
If they were a trap, even if it killed someone, it would only kill their clone. That person could condense another clone to retaliate and disturb Lord Yanglie's remains, which would go against Lord Yanglie's original intention.
Therefore, Yanglie would not cause trouble for himself after his death.
There were many treasures inside the azure palace, each suspended within a small pocket of space.
The moment he entered, Ye Tian knew he could only choose three treasures. Once he had selected three, he would be expelled from the azure palace.
Of course.
He could also choose to crack the mechanisms within the azure palace to take all the treasures, but that would inevitably take a long time. By then, other monstrous Transcendence Realm Lords would likely have crossed the time trap and arrived here.
Thus, the treasures here would eventually be emptied anyway.
There was no need to waste so much time.
"The Time River Pearl should be here."
Ye Tian searched continuously and soon locked onto a treasure box.
He felt the power of time emanating from this box. Moreover, its position in the center of the azure palace meant it was surely a very precious item, most likely the Time River Pearl.
Ye Tian spent some time browsing through all the treasures here, and the item inside that treasure box had the highest probability.
"I'll choose this one."
Ye Tian took the treasure box and opened it to see a gray-white pearl resting in the center.
"It really is the Time River Pearl!"
Ye Tian was overjoyed.
This might not be the most valuable item here, but it was definitely the rarest, and it was the item of greatest value to him.
Collected!
Ye Tian then began to select other items; he could still choose two more.
Many of the treasures here were excellent. Although there were some ordinary ones, even an ordinary item would be enough to tempt any Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord. Consequently, those common items were placed in the outer cemetery, within those outer palaces.
With so many good things, Ye Tian truly found it difficult to choose.
"I'll choose this!"
Ye Tian stared at one of the items and said.
This was an Eternal Supreme Treasure, and not just an ordinary one.
In the Void Area, ordinary Eternal Supreme Treasures have no rank divisions, but overall, Eternal Supreme Treasures are actually divided into Lower-tier Eternal Supreme Treasures, Mid-tier Eternal Supreme Treasures, and Upper-tier Eternal Supreme Treasures.
Among them, Upper-tier Eternal Supreme Treasures are almost all held by Peak Legendary Realm Lords. The one before him was a Mid-tier Eternal Supreme Treasure, and it was also Lord Yanglie's only Mid-tier Eternal Supreme Treasure.
However, this Mid-tier Eternal Supreme Treasure was damaged, likely caused during Lord Yanglie's battle with an enemy.
Nevertheless, Eternal Supreme Treasures possess self-repairing capabilities, so the current damage was no longer significant. Its value was still much greater than those top-tier Lower-tier Eternal Supreme Treasures.
It could be said that none of the Eternal Supreme Treasures on Ye Tian's person were as precious as this one.
Swish!
Ye Tian took out the Eternal Supreme Treasure stored in the small space. It was shaped like a shuttle.
Due to the special nature of this place, this Eternal Supreme Treasure was not in an untouchable state, and Ye Tian could easily reach it.
The moment he touched the shuttle, he learned its name—the Ruyi Void Shuttle.
The Mid-tier Eternal Supreme Treasure, Ruyi Void Shuttle, contained several powerful spatial Eternal Daos within it—Void Piercing, Void Rending, Shattering, Heaven Breaking, and Ruyi. All five of these Eternal Daos leaned toward space.
It could be said that if he cultivated all five of these Eternal Daos to the Legendary Realm, it would help Ye Tian master space in the future.
These five Eternal Daos were considered powerful, but they were still inferior to the Gray Mist Eternal Dao.
"Not bad. A Mid-tier Eternal Supreme Treasure's intrinsic value is even higher than the Time River Pearl; it's just that the Time River Pearl is rarer," Ye Tian murmured.
He spent a little time gaining preliminary control over the Eternal Supreme Treasure, the Ruyi Void Shuttle, before putting it away.
At this point, he could still choose one more item.
After a while, Ye Tian finally made his choice for the third item—
"Forbidden Sky Sea Fruit!"
The Forbidden Sky Sea Fruit was an extremely precious treasure, a natural resource that could be consumed. He had seen it in the treasure records of the True Void Sacred Land before. Its sole use was to strengthen Willpower, and it had no side effects.
Among treasures that strengthened Willpower, the Forbidden Sky Sea Fruit was ranked among the top ten most precious.
However, it was too precious and its effect too great. Even if the quantity wasn't too small, many people needed it. Thus, aside from a few Legendary Realm Lords, it was almost non-existent. Even if a Legendary Lord obtained a Forbidden Sky Sea Fruit, they would directly consume and refine it to strengthen their Willpower.
In fact, many Half-step Legendary Realm Lords yearned to obtain a Forbidden Sky Sea Fruit.
For Ye Tian, the effect of the Forbidden Sky Sea Fruit was also significant. It could help him break through his Willpower bottleneck and charge toward Tier 6 Willpower.
What did Tier 6 Willpower represent? Even for Transcendence Realm Lords and a few Half-step Legendary Realm Lords, their Willpower was only at Tier 5. Only some Legendary Realm Lords had Tier 6 Willpower, and most of those were at late-stage Tier 6 Willpower.
The Willpower of a Peak Legendary Realm Lord could reach Tier 7, while the Willpower of an Eternal Controller could even reach Tier 8 or Tier 9. That was far too distant for him.
In short, once Ye Tian possessed Tier 6 Willpower, his strength could increase once more, and his Willpower Rule World would become even more powerful.
Therefore, in terms of value, the Forbidden Sky Sea Fruit provided him with the most help.
"Taken!"
Ye Tian directly took the Forbidden Sky Sea Fruit.
At that moment, a surge of power arrived and directly teleported him out of the azure palace.
With three treasures in hand, Ye Tian breathed a sigh of relief.
"If only I could go back in again," Ye Tian murmured.
Suddenly.
He thought of something.
Perhaps others could not enter because their clones and main bodies were the same. Even changing to a different clone would not allow them to enter the azure palace. The detection of a Legendary Realm Lord was certainly something Legendary Lords could not bypass.
However, he might be able to bypass it.
For example, if he copied the appearance of another Legendary Lord and entered this azure palace, would the palace treat him as a different Legendary Lord?
If that were truly the case, he could sweep the azure palace clean of all treasures, leaving not a single one behind.
"It's worth a try!" Ye Tian said excitedly.
