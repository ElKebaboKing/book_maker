Chapter 1712: Secrets of the Source Sea, Gray Mist Stone Statue!

Time passed.
Ye Tian gradually gained some profound insights from this Guixu, obtaining more understanding of the fusion of his body, will, and Innate Eternal Rules, as well as more insights into the Eternal Primordial Force.
In fact, the power within Guixu was a more thorough form of Eternal Primordial Force.
Ye Tian estimated that if he meditated here for a few more years, he would have full confidence in charging toward the Perfected Stage Legendary Lord realm.
Just like that, Ye Tian cultivated in Guixu for a full thirty thousand Eternal Years.
On this day.
Ye Tian felt it was about time and began his charge toward the Perfected Stage Legendary Lord realm.
He mobilized the power of the Eternal Heart, and a massive amount of Eternal Primordial Force flooded his entire body. His body seemed to have completely fused with his will and Innate Eternal Rules, but in reality, there were still some places that had not thoroughly fused.
BOOM!!!!
Endless Innate Eternal Rules vibrated, coordinating with the nearly inexhaustible will to constantly vibrate his body, finally revealing those tiny areas that had not yet fused.
"Fuse!"
Ye Tian forcibly fused the final sliver of his body with his will and Innate Eternal Rules.
Finally, the last shackle was forcibly broken.
Immediately, Ye Tian's body began to undergo a transformation, and his aura became Perfected.
Within Guixu, many Legendary Lords looked toward Ye Tian one after another.
"The Endless Lord has broken through!"
"The Endless Lord only joined our True Void Sacred Land not long ago, right? He's only been in Guixu for a few tens of thousands of Eternal Years and has already broken through the shackles to advance to the Perfected Stage Legendary Lord realm. I've been in Guixu for three hundred thousand Eternal Years and still haven't broken through!"
"The Endless Lord truly is a monstrous talent who became a Heaven-tier Sacred Son as soon as he joined the True Void Sacred Land!"
......
On a certain path, the Silver Moon Lord also paused her meditation, her gaze turning toward Ye Tian, her eyes filled with a sense of defeat.
She had touched the threshold of the Perfected Stage earlier than Ye Tian, and her cultivation had even stayed at the Peak of the Initial Stage for a very long time. The last time she saw Ye Tian, he was only at the seventh step of the Initial Stage Legendary Lord realm.
But now, Ye Tian had actually stepped into the Perfected Stage Legendary Lord realm ahead of her.
Such cultivation speed was something she could not hope to match.
However, she quickly adjusted her mindset and continued her meditation, striving to break through the shackles and advance to the Perfected Stage Legendary Lord realm as soon as possible.
After some more time passed, Ye Tian completely stabilized his realm and left Guixu.
After breaking through to the Perfected Stage Legendary Lord realm, Ye Tian's true combat power was already comparable to an ordinary Domain-level Legendary Lord. If he encountered those Domain-level Legendary Lords who did not yet possess Eternal treasures, he could even suppress or even subdue them.
"Cultivation after the Perfected Legendary Lord realm involves continuously fusing will and Innate Eternal Rules. Once fused to a certain degree, a Will Rule Domain can be formed, at which point one can become a Domain Stage Legendary Lord!"
Ye Tian murmured.
Fusing will and Innate Eternal Rules seemed simple, but wanting to condense them to the level of a Will Rule Domain was not easy.
According to Ye Tian's understanding, a Fourth-Tier Eternal Will was simply not enough to condense a Will Rule Domain; one must reach a Fifth-Tier Will.
And the Innate Eternal Rules also had corresponding requirements. A person's mastery of Innate Eternal Rules lay in the Eternal Dao.
One Eternal Dao must be Perfected, or three Eternal Daos must be at the Major stage; only then could one ensure the Innate Eternal Rules reached the standard.
However, reaching the Perfected stage in one Eternal Dao was extremely difficult, and a Fifth-Tier Eternal Will was also very hard to achieve.
Ye Tian's current Eternal Will was still some distance away from a Fifth-Tier Eternal Will, so he had no way to advance to the Domain Stage Legendary Lord realm in a short period of time.
In the Boundless Sea, any Domain Stage Legendary Lord had cultivated for countless Eternal Years, their cultivation time even longer than the time since the Source Sea was born.
Therefore, becoming a Domain Stage Legendary Lord was still very difficult.
Source Sea.
Ye Tian was cultivating when, one day, the Bing Han Lord and the Sheng Feng Lord came to visit.
Ye Tian hosted the two and began to chat.
At this time, Ye Tian clearly sensed that the expressions of the Bing Han Lord and the Sheng Feng Lord were somewhat solemn, as if some major event had occurred.
"Bing Han Lord, Sheng Feng Lord, is there something on your minds?"
Ye Tian asked casually.
At this point, the Bing Han Lord spoke: "Endless Lord, we have a massive opportunity to tell you about."
"A massive opportunity?"
Ye Tian was quite puzzled, not knowing what massive opportunity the Bing Han Lord and the Sheng Feng Lord could have. Besides, if there really was a massive opportunity, would they tell him?
The Bing Han Lord continued: "In the Source Sea, the Sheng Feng Lord was the first Legendary Lord to break through, and I was the second, while the Tai Huang Lord was the third. But in reality, after I broke through, for a very long time, not a single Legendary Lord was born. Therefore, the later Legendary Lords and other Eternal Stage beings are completely unaware of some things that happened before then."
The Sheng Feng Lord followed up: "The Endless Lord should know the structure of the Boundless Sea. The resources of the Boundless Sea are concentrated within the Boundless Sea, while the resources of the Source Sea are concentrated in the Spacetime Territories. Perhaps some Eternal Seas have this situation, but in fact, a long time ago, the Source Sea did not have Spacetime Territories; it was in the same situation as the Boundless Sea. At that time, Eternal Stage beings all stayed within the Source Sea, and the Bing Han Lord and I actually achieved the Legendary Lord realm inside the Source Sea."
"What? You achieved the Legendary Lord realm inside the Source Sea?"
Ye Tian was shocked.
He truly did not know about this secret.
He had always thought that the several Legendary Lords of the Source Sea were beings from the Spacetime Territories. Now, hearing the Bing Han Lord and the Sheng Feng Lord say this, he realized that was not the case.
Then why did the Source Sea turn into its current state?
The Sheng Feng Lord continued: "A long time ago, the Source Sea was still normal. But one day, a stone statue appeared in the Source Sea. This stone statue was not complete and had a crack on its body. This stone statue was very, very large, even larger than a Spacetime Territory. The moment it appeared, the beings of billions of territories were completely annihilated. Even ordinary Eternal Stage Lords who approached it would be killed by a burst of power. Its appearance caused the Source Sea Origin to vibrate. That was the first time we witnessed the scene of the Source Sea Origin going all out to suppress an object. You can probably guess the subsequent scenes. The rules of the Source Sea changed, and some Source Sea Origin Fragments erupted into the void regions outside the Source Sea, turning into Spacetime Territories. Afterward, the Source Sea Origin seemed to be wary of that stone statue, causing its power to constantly dissipate toward the Spacetime Territories, thereby allowing the Spacetime Territories to give birth to a large amount of resources, while very few resources were born inside the Source Sea. At that time, the Bing Han Lord and I were both at the nine-step Immortal Stage Lord realm, still charging toward the Legendary Stage Lord realm. We risked approaching that statue and found that cultivation became easier, and we even found some good things, thereby breaking through the shackles and advancing to the Legendary Stage Lord realm. Later, this stone statue hid itself, and we could no longer find it. Those who came later were unaware of this matter, and we did not inform the later Legendary Lords. But not long ago, we went to investigate that place in the Source Sea and once again felt the fluctuations of that stone statue. Now, the fluctuations are becoming stronger and stronger."
The Bing Han Lord also added: "During this period of time, we have also been gathering information in the Boundless Sea to see if we could find any news regarding that stone statue. As it turns out, we really did find some news. If our guess is not wrong, it should be called the Gray Mist Stone Statue. it once appeared in an Eternal Sea but then disappeared again."
After listening to them, Ye Tian roughly understood their thoughts.
The Gray Mist Stone Statue was definitely about to emerge, and the two of them could no longer hide it. They were worried that the True Void Sacred Land would directly occupy the Gray Mist Stone Statue when the time came, so they revealed it first. This way, the True Void Sacred Land or Ye Tian would not stop them from entering again.
"Rest assured, I will definitely let you enter. Now, take me to see it!"
Ye Tian said.
Having received Ye Tian's guarantee, the Bing Han Lord and the Sheng Feng Lord breathed a sigh of relief.
Immediately, the two of them took Ye Tian to a certain place in the Source Sea.
