Chapter 1705: Zhenwu Archipelago!

This Infinite Island was massive, many times larger than even the ordinary Spacetime Territories within the Source Sea Spacetime Territory, and the cultivation environment here was excellent.
After Ye Tian entered this Infinite Island, he used his Copy Talent to investigate and sensed a large number of practitioners. Among them were many in the Supreme realm, but those in the Eternal Realm were still incredibly rare. It would be strange if Eternal Realm existences were everywhere.
After all, no matter how powerful the Infinite Sea was, it was still an Eternal Sea, fundamentally no different from the Source Sea. Therefore, within the Infinite Sea, becoming an Eternal Realm practitioner was also exceptionally difficult.
This Infinite Island might just be an ordinary Infinite Island within the Infinite Sea, so it was perfectly normal for there to be few in the Eternal Realm.
However,
After Ye Tian finished his investigation with his Copy Talent, he still discovered several Eternal Realm practitioners, but their strength was not very great. The most powerful Eternal Realm practitioner was merely an Immortality Realm Lord.
Ye Tian concealed his strength and gathered information on this Infinite Island, quickly learning about its situation.
This Infinite Island was called Thunder Turtle Sacred Island, and it was one of the Infinite Islands under the command of the Thunder River Lord. The Thunder River Lord was a Legendary Lord.
Ye Tian had no interest in the Thunder River Lord, so he only inquired about the situation regarding the Zhenwu Sacred Land. After searching for a long time, he finally learned the general direction of the Zhenwu Sacred Land.
Subsequently, Ye Tian left the area and traveled toward that direction.
Afterward, Ye Tian passed through several more Infinite Islands. After multiple inquiries, he finally learned the specific location of the Zhenwu Sacred Land.
The Zhenwu Sacred Land was located within an Infinite Archipelago. An archipelago was a super-large, prosperous area formed by a dense concentration of many Infinite Islands, including many Eternal Infinite Islands. Such a place was a territory that top-tier major forces would inevitably fight over.
The Zhenwu Sacred Land was very powerful, so it naturally had the strength to occupy such a treasure land.
This treasure land was also known as the Zhenwu Archipelago. However, a long time ago, the Legendary Realm powerhouses of the Zhenwu Sacred Land had drawn upon the Infinite Sea Source in the surrounding area to form a Source Barrier, similar to a water curtain. One had to pass through this water curtain to reach the Zhenwu Archipelago. Practitioners whose strength had not reached the Eternal Realm simply could not reach the Zhenwu Archipelago.
Consequently, many weaker practitioners had no understanding of the situation regarding the Zhenwu Archipelago or the Zhenwu Sacred Land.
On this day.
Ye Tian arrived at the Source Barrier outside the Zhenwu Archipelago. Countless amounts of Infinite Sea Source energy flew out from the Infinite Sea, forming a water curtain barrier that stretched across the horizon, blocking practitioners from entering the Zhenwu Archipelago.
Ye Tian lightly touched the Source Barrier; it was extremely resilient. Without the strength of the Eight Extremes Realm or the Eternal Realm and above, it was indeed impossible to break through this layer of the barrier.
*Splash!*
Ye Tian gently tore through this layer of the Source Barrier and stepped across into the area where the Zhenwu Archipelago was located.
"So many Infinite Islands!"
Ye Tian exclaimed.
He looked out and, with just a slight sense, perceived tens of thousands of Infinite Islands in the distance, including over a thousand massive Eternal Spacetime Islands.
As he approached these Infinite Islands, Ye Tian also saw Eternal Realm practitioners traveling across the Infinite Sea between the various Infinite Islands.
Eternal Realm existences seemed very common here. There were many, many Eternal Realm existences, and even Legendary Lords were considered nothing special in this place.
Ye Tian entered an Infinite Island at random and did not face any scrutiny. As a Source Sea lifeform, his aura was somewhat incompatible with the Infinite Sea, making it easy to see that he was not a native of the Infinite Sea. However, many lifeforms from other Eternal Seas would enter the Infinite Sea, so no one cared about this point unless a Legendary Realm powerhouse from another Eternal Sea arrived; only then would they be closely monitored.
An Initial Realm Legendary Lord would not receive such attention from the Zhenwu Sacred Land.
In the Zhenwu Archipelago, Ye Tian mainly inquired about how to join the Zhenwu Sacred Land and did not care much about other news.
Soon, he gathered the intelligence.
The Zhenwu Sacred Land itself possessed a massive number of disciples, and the native residents of the Zhenwu Archipelago provided a large number of disciple candidates. Therefore, the Zhenwu Sacred Land truly did not lack disciples. However, it was possible for outsiders to join the Zhenwu Sacred Land, though the requirements for outsiders were extremely high.
First, they must not have joined any other Legendary-level force in the Infinite Sea. Second, their cultivation must have reached the Eternal Realm.
As for practitioners below the Eternal Realm, no matter how high their talent was, it was useless. If they failed to break through to the Eternal Realm, no matter how great their combat power was, they would still be considered trash.
It was just like many Eight Extremes Realm Half-step Eternal Realm powerhouses who seemed very impressive, but if they couldn't break through, they would remain that way for the rest of their lives. The rules of the Infinite Sea were similar to those of Spacetime Territories; those who did not reach the Eternal Realm still faced a limit on their lifespan.
Therefore, the Zhenwu Sacred Land did not accept practitioners below the Eternal Realm; the minimum threshold for joining was the Eternal Realm.
These two requirements were the most basic, but they were not the standard.
To join the Zhenwu Sacred Land, one either needed a recommendation from the high-level members of the Zhenwu Sacred Land or had to pass through the Zhenwu Sacred Tower.
The Zhenwu Sacred Tower was a structure used by the Zhenwu Sacred Land to test combat power. The stronger one was, the more powerful the opponents they would encounter in the Zhenwu Sacred Tower. Thus, it wasn't simply that the higher one's strength, the higher the tier they could reach.
The requirement for outsiders set by the Zhenwu Sacred Land was that they must pass the Ninth-Tier of the Zhenwu Sacred Tower before they could join the Zhenwu Sacred Land.
Furthermore, when challenging the Zhenwu Sacred Tower, one was not allowed to use any powerful secret treasures or weapons; for example, Eternal Supreme Treasures were also prohibited.
The Zhenwu Sacred Tower was a very lively place in the Zhenwu Archipelago because outsiders frequently challenged it. However, very few Eternal Realm practitioners could pass the Ninth-Tier. They merely used challenging the Zhenwu Sacred Tower as a standard to verify their strength. If someone reached a high tier, and the news spread, even if the Zhenwu Sacred Land did not accept them, other Legendary-level forces would come to recruit them.
"There isn't much time left for the Source Sea. I still need to pass the Ninth-Tier of the Zhenwu Sacred Tower as quickly as possible and become a member of the Zhenwu Sacred Land. If I join the Zhenwu Sacred Land, even that so-called Qin Family will have to be wary of me and won't dare to move against the Source Sea,"
Ye Tian murmured.
*Whoosh!*
Ye Tian arrived at the Eternal Infinite Island where the Zhenwu Sacred Tower was located—Bawen Island. This was one of the primary islands of the Zhenwu Sacred Land.
When Ye Tian appeared near the Zhenwu Sacred Tower, there were already many Eternal Realm practitioners there, all watching the excitement. Clearly, there were quite a few people currently challenging the Zhenwu Sacred Tower.
Ye Tian was in no hurry to challenge the Zhenwu Sacred Tower and instead stood to the side to observe for a while.
After all, no matter how urgent it was, he wasn't in a rush for these few moments.
