Chapter 1728: Those Who Fail to Impact the Eternal Controller, Die!

"Lord Longgu, do you recognize this Purple Polar Light Beast?"
Lord Wushuang was surprised.
"Yes!" Lord Longgu nodded.
Lord Longgu then introduced, "This Purple Polar Light Beast is very famous, but he was an incredibly ancient Legendary Realm Lord. He is from a time too distant; I'm afraid only some ancient Legendary Realm Lords would recognize him. He is called Lord Ziji, and he was the second Purple Polar Light Beast of the Void Region. His strength was formidable, much stronger than mine. I was once just a junior before him, someone who had to look up to him. He mastered Time and also mastered Space, situated at the Peak stage of the Legendary Realm Lord stage. It can be said that as long as an Eternal Controller did not kill him, he would almost never die."
"Then this is..."
Lord Wushuang looked at Lord Ziji's corpse with some confusion.
Could it be that Lord Ziji was killed by an Eternal Controller? But why would an Eternal Controller want to kill Lord Ziji?
But then, she realized she had guessed wrong.
"No, no Eternal Controller would kill him." Lord Longgu shook his head and said, "He did not die at the hands of another, but died during the process of impacting the Eternal Controller. Impacting the Eternal Controller is extremely dangerous. Even if one has many clones, and the clones are like the main body and can cultivate freely, allowing a clone to impact the Eternal Controller, the result is the same. Once it fails, the clones and the main body will all perish. It is useless no matter where one hides; all will die. Therefore, those Peak Legendary Realm Lords usually return the power of their clones to their main body to impact the Eternal Controller. If they survive, they become an Eternal Controller; once they fail, they perish completely. Lord Ziji has not appeared for an incredibly long time. Many Legendary Realm Lords believed he perished while impacting the Eternal Controller, while others thought he had become an Eternal Controller. However, many Legendary Realm Lords are very clear that if he had truly become an Eternal Controller, the fate of the Purple Polar Light Beasts would not have been so tragic. Thus, I also leaned toward the theory that he had perished. Only now, seeing his corpse, can I be certain."
"Impacting the Eternal Controller can lead to perishing?"
Even Ye Tian was surprised.
The other Half-step Legendary Realm Lords were also somewhat shocked; this was indeed a secret none of them knew.
"Precisely because impacting the Eternal Controller is so dangerous, unless they have absolute certainty, those Peak Legendary Realm Lords will not attempt it. Currently, several Peak Legendary Realm Lords have given up on impacting it. Living like this is also quite good; at least, if they do not offend an Eternal Controller, they are Invincible existences in the many Eternal Seas of the Void Region and are very difficult to kill," Lord Longgu said.
The other Half-step Legendary Realm Lords nodded, agreeing with this statement.
Without certainty, there was no need to impact the Eternal Controller. Being an Invincible existence in the Legendary Realm was also quite good!
Truly, Legendary Realm Lords like Lord Ziji, who desired the Eternal Controller so much that they would risk attempting a breakthrough without certainty, were not many.
Suddenly.
Lord Longgu said, "This is the place where Lord Ziji failed to impact the Eternal Controller, so this Time Eternal Gold vein is actually condensed from Lord Ziji's own power. Therefore, I can no longer move this place; let this Time Eternal Gold vein remain here. In fact, this is a natural treasure land, a place of great opportunity. Although Lord Ziji failed his breakthrough, he created a spatiotemporal treasure land, which is beneficial for you to comprehend Time and Space. However, once you enter the interior of this treasure land, it cannot exist eternally. The spatiotemporal balance will be broken, and it will slowly collapse. Therefore, before it collapses, you have the hope of mastering Time or Space. If luck is on our side, it is possible that a new Legendary Realm Lord will be born in our Zhenwu Territory."
"This place actually helps us master Time and Space?"
Lord Wushuang was incredibly shocked, and then she became excited.
"To prevent this treasure land and this Time Eternal Gold from being leaked, you must not disclose the situation here. Do not even mention it to the other Half-step Legendary Realm Lords of the Zhenwu Sacred Ground. Once this treasure land collapses, you can bring the Time Eternal Gold back to the Zhenwu Sacred Ground. I will help you establish a Bridge of Time and then let the other Half-step Legendary Realm Lords enter it to cultivate. Giving them more time to practice can be considered compensation for them," Lord Longgu suggested.
Lord Wushuang and the other Half-step Legendary Realm Lords also understood the importance of secrecy. After all, such a treasure land was too precious; once leaked, even other forces would come to snatch it.
No one could be sure if the other Half-step Legendary Realm Lords of the Zhenwu Sacred Ground would reveal it.
Moreover, such a treasure land could not accommodate too many people; if there were too many, it would collapse rapidly.
"Lord Wujin, you must also keep this secret!"
Lord Longgu looked at Ye Tian.
"I will keep it secret!"
Ye Tian nodded.
"I need to stabilize this place before sending you into the treasure land!"
Lord Longgu said.
Time slowly passed, and Lord Longgu gradually stabilized the energy here, so at least everyone would not be killed by the energy impact.
The so-called energy impact was caused by the spatiotemporal power losing its balance, thus triggering an energy explosion. As for why it lost balance, it was naturally because Ye Tian had broken into this place.
Otherwise, it would not have lost its balance.
But no matter who it was, as long as they came here, the balance would be lost; this was an unavoidable situation.
"Alright, the energy has stabilized. I will send you in!"
Lord Longgu waved his hand, and everyone was wrapped in a force that tore through Time and Space, entering a spatiotemporal treasure land.
In the center of this spatiotemporal treasure land, Lord Ziji's corpse was still there.
This spatiotemporal treasure land was divided into two parts: one part for comprehending Space and the other for comprehending Time.
Most of the Half-step Legendary Realm Lords went to the area for comprehending Time, while only two Half-step Legendary Realm Lords went to the area for comprehending Space.
Lord Longgu also stayed here to perform comprehension. After all, Lord Ziji's experience of failing a breakthrough was still experience. If he were to impact the Eternal Controller in the future, the insights here would be useful to him.
Furthermore, Lord Longgu had only mastered Time and had not yet mastered Space, so he also wanted to comprehend Space here.
However, after mastering Time, mastering Space was extremely difficult.
Otherwise, it would not be the case that many Legendary Realm Lords only mastered either Time or Space, finding it difficult to master both Space and Time simultaneously.
Ye Tian pondered for a moment, thinking about whether to comprehend Time or Space.
"Comprehending Time... I have copied so many Time Talents. If I fuse them, I will surely be able to form a Legendary-tier Time Ancestral Talent. In that case, I will have a huge advantage in comprehending Time."
Ye Tian thought to himself.
Thus, he went to the area for comprehending Time.
At the same time, he began to fuse the Time Talents he had copied.
