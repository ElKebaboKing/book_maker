Chapter 1749: Meeting the White-Bearded Old Man!

"This is the place!"
Lord Shanshan brought Ye Tian to a set of stone steps on Xuyang Mountain and said, "Walking up these stone steps is the test."
Ye Tian looked at the number of stone steps; there were about a hundred tiers. "May I ask what result is required to pass the White-Bearded Old Man's assessment?"
"The requirement for the Half-step Legendary Realm is to walk to the end, which is the hundredth stone step. The requirement for a Transcendent Realm Legendary Lord is the fiftieth stone step. As for a Half-step Transcendent Realm Legendary Lord, the Master has never mentioned it, so if you want to see the Master, you must reach the fiftieth stone step," Lord Shanshan said.
The fiftieth step, then.
Ye Tian acknowledged this and walked toward the first stone step.
He didn't know what mysteries these stone steps held, nor could he see anything from the outside. If he could, it would be strange. After all, this was a test set by the Peak Legendary Realm Lord, the White-Bearded Old Man; it was bound to be incredibly profound.
Ye Tian stepped onto the first stone step and was immediately trapped within a world.
He didn't attack the world immediately but silently comprehended the mysteries within.
"This world is actually composed of many innate eternal rules, and it's also mixed with the power of will, space, and time. If one's comprehension isn't deep enough, it's very difficult to break this world. Forcibly breaking it is impossible; brute force cannot tear apart a world created by the White-Bearded Old Man."
Ye Tian quickly saw through the key to this world and pointed a finger.
Immediately, the world shattered.
Soon after, Ye Tian stepped onto the second stone step.
It was the same, though the world of the second step was slightly more complex than the first, but Ye Tian broke it quickly as well.
Ye Tian walked through world after world, while Lord Shanshan didn't pay much attention to him.
As time passed, Ye Tian gradually reached the fiftieth step.
At this point, he could have stopped because he had passed the test.
However, if he only did that, he wouldn't be taken seriously by the White-Bearded Old Man. Even if he met him, would the man give him the Ancient Time-Space Leaf?
No, the White-Bearded Old Man wouldn't.
Therefore, he had to show even more monstrous talent.
He wanted to go to the end.
When he reached the sixtieth step, he found that the subsequent worlds were getting closer to the essence of controlling space and time.
Suddenly, he noticed the steps ahead split into two directions.
One certainly represented space, and the other certainly represented time.
At this moment, Ye Tian understood.
This so-called test was to examine the degree of control Half-step Legendary Realm Lords had over time or space. If their control wasn't high, it meant they were far from becoming a Legendary Realm Lord and might never become one, thus failing to earn the White-Bearded Old Man's attention.
If one reached the hundredth stone step, it meant they had a very high hope of completely mastering time and space, naturally qualifying them to see the White-Bearded Old Man.
Without the slightest hesitation, Ye Tian walked toward the stone step representing time and continued forward.
The sixty-first step!
The sixty-second step!
The sixty-third step...
Ye Tian continued to pass step after step at a very fast speed, soon reaching the ninety-ninth step.
At this point, Ye Tian needed to spend a little time to crack the world. It was evident that for a Half-step Legendary Realm Lord to pass through here, their control over time had to be very close to complete mastery.
"The hundredth stone step!"
Ye Tian stepped onto it.
One second!
One minute!
Ten minutes!
One month!
One year!
Boom!
Ye Tian shattered the world and walked out, officially passing the hundredth stone step.
When Ye Tian passed the hundredth stone step, a golden path of light appeared before him.
At this time, Lord Shanshan finally took notice of Ye Tian and was instantly shocked.
"You passed the hundredth stone step?"
Lord Shanshan asked.
"Yes," Ye Tian nodded.
Lord Shanshan stared at Ye Tian in disbelief. He had only taken a short nap, and Ye Tian had already passed the hundredth stone step. He knew very well what this represented.
If a renowned Half-step Legendary Realm Lord had done this, he wouldn't be surprised, but Ye Tian was only a Half-step Transcendent Realm Legendary Lord!
"Lord Shanshan, can I see the White-Bearded Old Man now?"
Ye Tian asked.
Lord Shanshan pointed to the golden path of light beneath Ye Tian's feet and said, "Walk from here, and you will be able to see the Master. Remember, when you see the Master, you can ask one question or make one request, but do not make a request that is too excessive. Do you understand?"
"Understood!"
Ye Tian nodded. ..........
Immediately, Ye Tian stepped onto the golden path of light.
It felt as if he had crossed a time-space dimension in a single step, passing through layers of time and space, somewhat like approaching an untouchable, unknown existence.
After a long time, he finally reached the White-Bearded Old Man's residence on Xuyang Mountain.
To his surprise, the White-Bearded Old Man's residence was very simple—just an ordinary bamboo house courtyard with two stone stools, a stone table, an old man, and a pot of tea.
When Ye Tian saw the old man, he knew he was the White-Bearded Old Man.
The White-Bearded Old Man lived up to his name, possessing a long white beard.
In reality, upon reaching the Peak Legendary Realm Lord level, one naturally wouldn't age, but some Eternal Realm Lords preferred to appear as old men, perhaps because their mindset had aged.
"Greetings, White-Bearded Old Man!"
Ye Tian said respectfully.
"Sit!"
the White-Bearded Old Man said.
Ye Tian sat down, and a cup of tea appeared before him.
"Drink it and savor it carefully!"
the White-Bearded Old Man added.
Ye Tian did as he was told, drinking the cup of tea and savoring it.
Instantly, countless insights and flashes of inspiration emerged.
He had already mastered time, but this time, he comprehended space and gained significant insights and improvements in it. This single cup of tea saved him at least millions of Eternal Years of bitter cultivation.
"Excellent tea. Thank you for the tea, White-Bearded Old Man!"
Ye Tian thanked him.
"This is Great Dao Tea. The first cup has the best effect; you can drink at most three cups. After three cups, it becomes ordinary tea, though it's still quite good to drink occasionally," the White-Bearded Old Man said with a smile.
Of course, only a Peak Legendary Realm Lord like the White-Bearded Old Man would treat such Great Dao Tea as nothing special; other Legendary Realm Lords did not have such a foundation.
"You are quite monstrous. To have mastered time as a Half-step Transcendent Realm Legendary Lord... if this were to spread, even many Legendary Realm Lords would be incredibly shocked. You came to me likely for the Ancient Time-Space Leaf, right?"
the White-Bearded Old Man suddenly asked.
Ye Tian wasn't surprised at being seen through; it could be guessed from his passing the hundredth stone step.
Thus, he replied, "White-Bearded Old Man, I am indeed here for the Ancient Time-Space Leaf. I ask that you grant me an Ancient Time-Space Leaf; I am willing to pay any price.".
