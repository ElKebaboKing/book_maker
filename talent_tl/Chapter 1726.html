Chapter 1725: The Terrifying Lord Wushuang!

At the mention of Time Eternal Gold, Lord Wushuang's expression changed completely.
"Time Eternal Gold is an incomparably precious treasure. It can help Legendary Lords comprehend the mysteries of time. If one possesses a piece of Time Eternal Gold, they can use it to comprehend the Eternal Dao related to time, helping them reach the Perfected stage of the Eternal Dao. However, this is merely a secondary effect of Time Eternal Gold; its true use is to construct a Bridge of Time!"
Lord Wushuang introduced.
"What is the use of a Bridge of Time?"
Ye Tian asked.
Lord Wushuang said longingly, "A Bridge of Time is a bridge constructed to connect to the River of Time. You have been to the River of Time within the Gray Mist Stone Statue before, so you should know the significance of the River of Time to those of us in the Half-Step Legend Realm, right? A Bridge of Time allows us to directly construct a bridge to connect to the River of Time from any location, and then we can enter the River of Time through it. Of course, a Bridge of Time cannot exist eternally. Constructing a Bridge of Time requires Time Eternal Gold, and maintaining it also requires Time Eternal Gold. If there is enough Time Eternal Gold, those of us in the Half-Step Eternal Realm could stay within the River of Time indefinitely."
"So that's how it is!"
Ye Tian finally understood the use of Time Eternal Gold.
To Half-Step Legend Realm Lords, many treasures were useless, and they didn't care about them. Perhaps many treasures that Ye Tian considered extremely precious were beneath the notice of Half-Step Legend Realm Lords; they might not even spare a second glance at the treasure map in his hand.
However, Time Eternal Gold was enough to make even Half-Step Legend Realm Lords take notice.
Of course, a single piece of Time Eternal Gold would not mean much to a Half-Step Legend Realm Lord, but if they found the nest of the Purple Aurora Beasts, they might find a Time Eternal Gold vein.
Even if there was only a slight possibility, they would not let it go.
"Lord Wujin, take us there!"
Lord Wushuang said urgently.
"Alright!"
Ye Tian nodded.
This time, not only would Lord Wushuang go, but several other Half-Step Legend Realm experts from the True Void Sacred Land would also go.
In a short time, a total of eight Half-Step Legend Realm Lords arrived.
"Let's go!"
These eight Half-Step Legend Realm Lords followed Ye Tian, first passing through the Space-Time Stairs to reach the Source Sea, and then setting off from the Source Sea to enter the Purple Light Domain. This was the fastest route.
After all, they were not Legend Realm Lords, so their travel speed naturally couldn't be that fast. If they were Legend Realm Lords who had completely mastered space, they could travel from the Boundless Sea to the Source Sea or the Purple Light Domain with a single thought. If they had completely mastered time, they could travel through the River of Time, which was equally fast.
However, Half-Step Legend Realm Lords could not do this and could only travel by shuttling through space normally.
Time passed slowly, and after a long while, Ye Tian finally brought the eight Half-Step Legend Realm Lords to the nest of the Purple Aurora Beasts.
Here, Ye Tian sensed many Purple Aurora Beasts, and the Half-Step Legend Realm Lords sensed them as well.
Boom!!!
The Purple Aurora Beast that had chased Ye Tian before appeared and directly attacked them.
A terrifying purple light blasted toward them, but it was blocked by Lord Wushuang with a single hand.
"Come here!"
Lord Wushuang beckoned with her hand, and the Purple Aurora Beast—which was considered invincible among Transcendence Realm Lords—flew over, shrinking continuously until it turned into a miniature beast in Lord Wushuang's hand.
"So strong!"
This was the first time Ye Tian had seen Lord Wushuang take action, and he was truly shocked.
This was a complete one-sided beatdown!
Was the power gap between a Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord and a Half-Step Legend Realm Lord really this large?
Seeing Ye Tian's shock, Lord Wumeng couldn't help but say, "Lord Wujin, Lord Wushuang is easily ranked among the top three of the many Half-Step Legend Realm experts in the True Void Sacred Land. Her strength is many times greater than that of an ordinary Half-Step Legend Realm Lord. About several hundred million Eternal Years ago, Lord Wushuang fought three enemy Half-Step Legend Realm Lords alone, heavily wounding two and killing one. She even managed to escape after being heavily injured in front of a Legend Realm Lord. There aren't many Half-Step Legend Realm Lords who can escape from a Legend Realm Lord."
"Wumeng, stop praising me. My strength is still far from enough!"
Lord Wushuang said modestly.
"Let's go in first!"
The several Half-Step Legend Realm Lords and Ye Tian did not continue the discussion but walked toward the Purple Aurora Beast's nest.
Along the way, Purple Aurora Beasts kept seeking death and were all blasted to death by the Half-Step Legend Realm Lords.
The Half-Step Legend Realm Lords were not interested in these Purple Aurora Beasts. Only the one with Transcendence Realm strength had some value and was taken by Lord Wushuang; the other Purple Aurora Beasts were too weak, and killing them was no loss.
Ye Tian, however, was very interested in these Purple Aurora Beasts, but only in their time talents.
Thus, Ye Tian continuously copied the time talents from their bodies.
Unfortunately, the number of Purple Aurora Beasts here was not very large.
Soon, everyone arrived at the Purple Aurora Beast's nest.
The nest was an island; its area was not very large, but there were many resources on it.
Lord Wushuang stood on the island, investigated for a moment, and said, "This is not the true nest; they migrated here. These Purple Aurora Beasts should be losers who failed to compete for territory and were driven away. They moved this island to this area. It's impossible for there to be Time Eternal Gold here."
The several Half-Step Legend Realm Lords were not disappointed; instead, they had a strange sense of expectation.
"Let's interrogate them!"
Lord Wumeng suggested.
Whoosh!
The Half-Step Legend Realm Lord known as Lord Yinmeng casually grabbed a weaker Purple Aurora Beast. Her eyes turned gray as she looked at the beast.
In the next moment, the Purple Aurora Beast stopped moving.
After a while, the Purple Aurora Beast lost its life force, and Lord Yinmeng returned to normal.
"I've managed to glimpse a few time fragments. These Purple Aurora Beasts came from this direction. Let's keep shuttling through space to search for it," Lord Yinmeng said.
"Alright, let's search in that direction then!"
Lord Wushuang also nodded.
Ye Tian followed as well. After all, this was his contribution, and these Half-Step Legend Realm Lords would not leave him behind.
Just like that, they continued forward, searching for the true location of the Purple Aurora Beasts' nest.
Time passed slowly.
In the blink of an eye, hundreds of thousands of Kalpa Eras had passed.
Finally, on this day, Ye Tian and the others once again discovered traces of the Purple Aurora Beasts.
