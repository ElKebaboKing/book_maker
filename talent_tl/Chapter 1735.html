Chapter 1734: Time Trading House, News of the Time River Bead!

"Where is it?"
Ye Tian asked for guidance.
"The Time Trading House," Lord Wushuang said.
"Time Trading House?" Ye Tian did not know much about this trading house, so he waited for Lord Wushuang's introduction.
Lord Wushuang said, "The Time Trading House is a trading house established by an Eternal Controller. Of course, this is a rumor, but many forces have accepted it as fact because a long time ago, a Peak Legendary Realm Lord who offended the Time Trading House was killed silently after a long period of time. Therefore, the existence of the Eternal Controller is likely true. However, it is not yet clear which timeline that Eternal Controller is currently in. But even if they are in a distant future timeline, they will eventually come into contact with others. If one truly offends the Time Trading House, there will surely be no good end in the future. Consequently, the Time Trading House holds very high authority across many Eternal Seas in the Void Region, and no Legendary Realm Lord would dare to offend them. The Time Trading House spans many Eternal Seas, so if you go there, with some luck, you might find those three treasures."
"Thank you for the guidance, Lord Wushuang," Ye Tian thanked him.
Afterward, he left the True Void Holy Land, preparing to pay a visit to the Time Trading House.
The Time Trading House had branches distributed across many Eternal Seas, but those weaker Eternal Seas did not have branches because the Time Trading House followed a high-end route, trading items at the level of Legendary Lords at the very least.
In some weak Eternal Seas, there weren't even many Legendary Lords, so there was no need to establish a branch.
However, the Boundless Sea was still very powerful, so it had a branch of the Time Trading House.
Soon, Ye Tian arrived at the Time Trading House.
The Time Trading House was not built on the Boundless Island but was instead floating above the source energy ocean of the Boundless Sea.
The Time Trading House was massive, and upon arriving, Ye Tian discovered that it was constructed by combining multiple types of Eternal Supreme Treasures.
For a single branch to be constructed by stacking Eternal Supreme Treasures showed just how immensely wealthy and powerful the Time Trading House was.
Ye Tian entered the Time Trading House and saw many Legendary Lords. Most of the customers here were at the levels of Domain Realm Legendary Lords and Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords. Legendary Lords with low cultivation did not have enough financial resources to purchase treasures here, so there were very few Legendary Lords below the Domain Realm. Furthermore, the treasures needed by Half-step Legendary Realm Lords were also extremely rare, and even Legendary Realm Lords might not necessarily possess them.
Therefore, Half-step Legendary Realm Lords also rarely visited the Time Trading House unless a treasure of great use to them appeared, which could attract them to come.
The employees of the Time Trading House were all Legendary Lords. Perhaps many of them were just Ordinary Legendary Lords, but no one dared to look down on them. In fact, countless Legendary Lords wanted to become employees of the Time Trading House but had no way in.
Without any connections, it was impossible to enter the Time Trading House.
Once one joined the Time Trading House, it was equivalent to having a great backer; almost no one would dare to touch an employee of the Time Trading House.
However, the Time Trading House almost always recruited internally. After all, over countless ages, there were a large number of practitioners within the Time Trading House, and it was naturally easy for some Legendary Lords to be born among them.
When Ye Tian arrived at the Time Trading House, a female employee soon came to receive him. This female employee was an Initial Realm Legendary Lord.
"Dear guest, this should be your first time at the Time Trading House. You can register an account with our firm. Our accounts have extremely strong confidentiality, and even Legendary Realm Lords cannot interfere with your account," the female employee said.
Ye Tian was not surprised that his first visit was noticed. The Time Trading House had no data on him, so these employees would naturally know instantly. If they didn't even know this much, there would be no need for the Time Trading House to continue operating.
"Alright, I'll set up an account first," Ye Tian nodded.
When trading at the Time Trading House, it was very inconvenient not to have an account.
In the Void Region, only the Time Trading House was qualified to establish accounts for customers, allowing them to feel secure putting their wealth into it. If it were any other trading house, it could be destroyed any day; who would dare to put their things in?
Of course, no one would put incomparably precious items into the Time Trading House either.
Soon, the Time Trading House processed the account for Ye Tian, and he obtained a special card. This card was quite extraordinary, utilizing a very powerful rule that made it impossible to alter its material or the information within it.
It must have a connection to an Eternal Controller; otherwise, it would not have such characteristics.
"Does the Time Trading House have Void Sea Gravel, Time River Beads, or Space-Time Ancient Leaves?" Ye Tian inquired.
"Currently, we have none!" The female employee shook her head and said, "However, 300 million Eternal Years ago, a grain of Void Sea Gravel was auctioned at the Time Trading House in Beihe. 600 million Eternal Years ago, a Time River Bead was auctioned in the Ancient Domain Sea. As for the Space-Time Ancient Leaf, the earliest it appeared was 1.8 billion years ago, and no one auctioned it."
"If there is any news regarding these three treasures, I hope the Time Trading House can notify me," Ye Tian said.
"No problem. As long as the guest signs up for the notification service, we will inform the customer in a timely manner," the female employee said.
"That works."
Ye Tian immediately processed this service and then left the Time Trading House.
Since those three treasures were not available, there was no point in him staying at the Time Trading House.
Thus, he departed.
...
After returning, Ye Tian continued his secluded cultivation, meditating on the blood tissue of that Supreme Legendary Body creature.
No matter what, he still had to comprehend the Supreme Legendary Body. Only by having enough understanding and insight into the Supreme Legendary Body could he condense it in the future once he obtained the Time River Bead, Void Sea Gravel, and Space-Time Ancient Leaf.
Otherwise, if his comprehension was not deep enough, even if he obtained those three treasures, he would have no way to condense the Supreme Legendary Body.
Additionally, Ye Tian continued to cultivate the Eternal Dao, striving to cultivate a large number of Eternal Daos to the Legendary-level as soon as possible.
Furthermore, the Will Rule World could still be improved. Although the progress was slow, it was still a way to enhance his strength.
Not only that, but his Will, Rule comprehension, and Time Mastery could all continue to improve, as these were not affected by his cultivation realm.
After a long period of time, approximately 3 million Eternal Years later.
On this day, the Time Trading House sent a message to Ye Tian.
When Ye Tian saw this message, a look of joy immediately appeared on his face.
There was news of a Time River Bead!.
