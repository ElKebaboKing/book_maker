Chapter 1719: Changing Particle Structure, Ye Tian's Attempt!

After a long time, Ye Tian completed his transformation and stabilized his realm.
At this moment, Ye Tian was already a true Domain Realm Legendary Lord. Although he still had some gap in realm compared to those top Domain Realm Legendary Lords and could not be compared with Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords, he had already narrowed the gap between them.
Additionally.
Ye Tian's true strength was likely already comparable to the top Domain Realm Legendary Lords, second only to Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords.
However, the Grey Mist Sea of Fire had even blocked several Half-step Legendary Realm Lords; trying to pass this stage was still very difficult.
But if he crossed the Grey Mist Sea of Fire with the cultivation of a Domain Realm Legendary Lord, the rewards he would obtain would likely be quite astonishing.
Just at this moment, a Half-step Legendary Realm Lord was about to attempt the Grey Mist Sea of Fire, and Ye Tian could just happen to observe.
This Half-step Legendary Realm Lord was Lord Wumeng of the True Void Sacred Land. His fame was slightly weaker than Lord Wushuang, and now it seemed his strength should also be much less, but he was still a Half-step Legendary Realm Lord, an existence far surpassing Ye Tian.
However, in these stages within the Grey Mist Stone Statue, sometimes it was not just about being powerful to pass. For example, Ye Tian's strength was not as good as a Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord, yet he had arrived here, while some Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords had not even crossed the Grey Mist Long River yet.
Therefore, strength was only one part; having a perfect foundation without any flaws was the key to easily passing the stages.
Boom!!!!
Lord Wumeng stepped into the Grey Mist Sea of Fire, and endless fire power invaded the interior of Lord Wumeng's body, incinerating his form.
Ye Tian saw clearly that the fire power was not true fire; inside the fire was a special kind of power. This power could dissolve Eternal Source Force. If the physical body was not strong enough and the Eternal Source Force was dissolved, the physical body would directly collapse.
Soon, Lord Wumeng reached the halfway point of the Grey Mist Sea of Fire, but his body also showed traces of cracks. These cracks were difficult to repair, but the problem was not yet too large.
After another while, Lord Wumeng finally reached the end of the Grey Mist Sea of Fire, but his body was already covered in cracks.
Nevertheless, Lord Wumeng ultimately crossed the Grey Mist Sea of Fire.
Subsequently, several other Half-step Legendary Realm Lords also tried to traverse the Grey Mist Sea of Fire, and all of them crossed at the cost of being heavily injured.
After all, these Half-step Legendary Realm Lords were only a sliver away from crossing, and they had been perfecting their bodies throughout these years. If they still could not pass, it would prove their talent was too trashy.
However, to become a Half-step Legendary Realm Lord, none had trashy talent, so it was inevitable for them to pass the Grey Mist Sea of Fire.
As these several Half-step Legendary Realm Lords passed the Grey Mist Sea of Fire, the only ones remaining were the Transcendence Realm Lords present and a few Domain Realm Legendary Lords.
The Grey Mist Sea of Fire was not like the Grey Mist Long River where it was difficult to come out once inside, so Ye Tian prepared to give it a try.
After all, no matter how much he observed others crossing the Grey Mist Sea of Fire, it was not as profound as experiencing it personally.
Whoosh!
Ye Tian stepped into the Grey Mist Sea of Fire, and an inexhaustible amount of fire surged into his body. No matter how he defended, it was useless. Even the Grey Mist Armor was of no use against the invasion of such power.
This power targeted the body itself; no defense or treasure could stop it.
Of course, if one's strength was great enough, such as a Legendary Realm Legendary Lord, perhaps they could withstand these fires.
Soon, Ye Tian retreated from the Grey Mist Sea of Fire.
Through this personal test and his previous observation of several Half-step Legendary Realm Lords traversing the Grey Mist Sea of Fire, Ye Tian roughly understood the situation of the Grey Mist Sea of Fire.
The Grey Mist Sea of Fire tested the basic strength of the body. For example, even among Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords, their bodies might seem the same and their Eternal Source Force might seem the same, but some Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords had stronger body foundations because the Eternal Particles formed after the fusion of will, body, and Innate Eternal Rules were different. If the Eternal Particles within a Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord's body were not solid, they could easily be dissolved by the Grey Mist Sea of Fire, leading to the collapse of the body.
At that point, no matter how great your strength was, it would be of no use.
And just now, Ye Tian felt that his own body was about to collapse; the Eternal Particles in his body as a whole were not considered very solid.
Perhaps if his cultivation reached the Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord level, he could traverse the Grey Mist Sea of Fire, but now he was only a Domain Realm Legendary Lord, so he could not traverse the Grey Mist Sea of Fire, though he could hold on for a few breaths of time in the Grey Mist Sea of Fire...
"If I want to charge from a Domain Realm Legendary Lord to a Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord, it will likely take a very long time. The emergence of the Grey Mist Stone Statue is not permanent for people to cultivate here. When the time comes, even if the hundred lives in the Grey Mist Stone Statue refuse to leave, they will be forcibly kicked out by the Grey Mist Stone Statue. So I cannot wait until the day I break through to Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord; I must change my own Eternal Particle structure. This is the only path that will work!" Ye Tian thought to himself.
Changing one's own Eternal Particle structure was very difficult.
Because for a Domain Realm Legendary Lord, their body, will, and Innate Eternal Rules had completely fused, forming a new Eternal body. Eternal Source Force permeated the form, and the Eternal Particles were also completely stabilized, making it very difficult to change the body structure again.
However, it was not impossible to change; otherwise, the meaning of this stage would not exist.
Most Legendary Lords could only achieve local changes, such as changing the arrangement of Eternal Particles within the body. But at the Domain Realm Legendary Lord level, the arrangement of Eternal Particles in the body was nearly perfect, and trying to change it to a more perfect degree did not help much.
This was also the reason why many Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords were constantly blocked and did not know what to do.
To completely change this situation, one had to thoroughly change the Eternal Particle structure of the body. The consequences of doing so were very serious; a slight mistake would lead to the complete collapse of the body and direct death, just like the situation in the Grey Mist Sea of Fire.
But once successful, it would directly bring about a true transformation in strength, and the body would become incomparably solid, such that even the power of the Grey Mist Sea of Fire would find it difficult to shake.
Others did not dare to take this second step, but Ye Tian did.
"Then I will use other duplicate bodies to conduct tests!"
Ye Tian decided.
Subsequently, he began to use duplicate bodies to conduct tests, changing his particle structure from the root, and combining them into the most perfect particle structure rather than a random combination. Otherwise, changing the structure would be meaningless.
It could only be changed for the better, not for the worse.
Just like that, 300,000 Eternal Years passed...
