Chapter 1707: Heaven-Tier Holy Son Level!

The fifteenth floor of the Zhenwu Holy Tower.
Facing the Initial Realm Legendary Lord of this floor, Ye Tian had no choice but to perform a Legendary-grade Eternal Technique; otherwise, there was no way he could pass this floor.
Once Ye Tian unleashed his Legendary-grade Eternal Technique, he easily defeated his opponent on the fifteenth floor.
The sixteenth floor!
The seventeenth floor!
The eighteenth floor!
...
The strength of the opponents on each floor did not increase by too much, but by the nineteenth floor, even when Ye Tian performed a Legendary-grade Eternal Technique, he had to go all out to defeat his opponent.
Ye Tian estimated that he wouldn't be able to clear many more floors; perhaps the twentieth floor would be his limit.
Finally, Ye Tian arrived at the twentieth floor.
The opponents on this floor were very powerful. All three opponents could perform Legendary-grade Eternal Techniques. Ye Tian had to use multiple Legendary-grade Eternal Techniques, such as Void Step, Billion-fold Void, Will Wall Light, and Void True Body, to be able to contend with them.
Rumble!!!!
The entire twentieth floor seemed to transform into a land of Annihilation. The battle between the two sides tore apart all the rules here, but even so, it was impossible to cause damage to the Zhenwu Holy Tower. No matter how much the internal void was destroyed, it would slowly recover later.
After fighting for a long time, Ye Tian began to suffer injuries. But at this moment, he performed the Myriad Life Eternal Dao.
This Eternal Dao was something he had comprehended from the Eternal Supreme Treasure, the Jialan Leaf. Although he could not use the Jialan Leaf itself, using the Eternal Dao was not a problem.
Under the effect of the Myriad Life Eternal Dao, the injuries on his body recovered extremely quickly, while the injuries of those three Initial Realm Legendary Lords could not recover. After all, they were merely Legendary Lords condensed by the Zhenwu Holy Tower; if they could recover from their injuries, it would be too unfair.
In this way, Ye Tian won through sheer attrition.
The twenty-first floor.
Ye Tian entered this floor but found that he was no match for it. Consequently, he immediately chose to exit the Zhenwu Holy Tower, because being unable to win meant he simply couldn't win, and continuing the fight would only be a waste of time.
When Ye Tian walked out of the Zhenwu Holy Tower, he was immediately met with the gazes of numerous Eternal Realm experts.
At this moment, all the Eternal Realm experts were looking at Ye Tian in disbelief.
"What exactly is this guy's background? He actually cleared the twentieth floor of the Zhenwu Holy Tower!!!"
Even Yinyue Lord found it somewhat hard to believe.
If someone had told her before that someone could clear the twentieth floor of the Zhenwu Holy Tower, she absolutely would not have believed it—unless it was those Legendary Realm Lords, who might have a hope of doing so. However, Legendary Realm Lords wouldn't come to the Zhenwu Holy Tower to clear floors.
The Zhenwu Holy Tower was merely a building for introductory screening; the powerhouses of the Zhenwu Holy Land would not come here for testing.
Therefore, only Eternal Realm experts who wanted to join the Zhenwu Holy Land would come to test. Among these Eternal Realm experts who wanted to join, how many could have terrifying strength?
Not a single one!
But now, a monster like Ye Tian had actually come to participate.
Boom!!!!
A figure appeared above the Zhenwu Holy Tower. This was a woman, her entire body radiating a bone-chilling cold. Many Eternal Realm experts only took one look, and it felt as if their wills were about to be frozen solid.
"Greetings, Wushuang Lord!"
Numerous Eternal Realm experts bowed respectfully.
Wushuang Lord was one of the high-level members of the Zhenwu Holy Land, a Half-step Legendary Realm Lord. She was considered extremely powerful even among Half-step Legendary Realm Lords.
Under normal circumstances, such a Half-step Legendary Realm Lord would not come here. Her arrival now was clearly for Ye Tian.
"Two of you have passed our introductory assessment. Come with me!"
Wushuang Lord waved her hand.
Yinyue Lord and Ye Tian were both pulled in front of her, and Ye Tian did not resist.
After approaching Wushuang Lord, she led Ye Tian and Yinyue Lord toward the true sacred ground of the Zhenwu Holy Land.
In reality, this place was only the territory of the Zhenwu Holy Land, but it did not count as the actual seat of power for the Zhenwu Holy Land.
The true location of the Zhenwu Holy Land was on Zhenwu Island, which was also the largest Eternal Boundless Island in the Zhenwu Archipelago.
In an instant, they passed through a barrier and entered the Zhenwu Holy Land.
Soon, Ye Tian followed Wushuang Lord to an ancient palace within the Zhenwu Holy Land.
Here, Ye Tian saw several Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords as well as several Half-step Legendary Realm Lords.
As for Legendary Realm Lords, Ye Tian did not see a single one. Such existences were too mysterious to be seen just because one wanted to.
At this time.
Wushuang Lord spoke: "Yinyue Lord, you cleared the ninth floor of the Zhenwu Holy Tower. As for your background, we have already verified it. From this day forward, you are a Human-Tier Holy Son level member of the Zhenwu Holy Land. You may choose any position available to Human-Tier Holy Son level members."
"Yes!"
Yinyue Lord responded.
According to her own understanding, with her strength and potential, she could only obtain a quota for a Human-Tier Holy Son level member. Of course, she could continue to improve in the future, but that would require certain opportunities. Having just joined the Zhenwu Holy Land, she certainly wouldn't be able to upgrade quickly.
"What level of member treatment will Endless Lord receive?" Yinyue Lord wondered curiously.
However, Wushuang Lord did not immediately announce Ye Tian's member treatment. Instead, she asked: "Endless Lord, our Zhenwu Holy Land only knows that you come from outside the Boundless Sea, but the Zhenwu Holy Land has no way to investigate conditions outside the Zhenwu Holy Land's reach. You need to state your situation, and then the Zhenwu Holy Land will construct a Space-Time Ladder to investigate. Only after that can your joining the Zhenwu Holy Land be officially announced. If you feel this offends your privacy, you may refuse, but you can only become an ordinary disciple of the Zhenwu Holy Land, or you may choose to leave the Zhenwu Holy Land."
Ye Tian did not hesitate in the slightest and said directly: "I come from the Source Sea, an Eternal Sea that has not been discovered by the Boundless Sea. It is not too far from the Boundless Sea. I can give the Zhenwu Holy Land the space-time coordinates, and the Zhenwu Holy Land can construct a Space-Time Ladder to the Source Sea to investigate the situation."
A new Eternal Sea!!!!
Wushuang Lord and the other high-level members of the Zhenwu Holy Land were all astonished. The value of such an Eternal Sea was not small!
If word got out, many top-tier forces would likely want to occupy it and develop it into their own backyard.
Of course, the Zhenwu Holy Land didn't really care about a single Eternal Sea, as it wouldn't be of much help to them.
"Good, we will construct the Space-Time Ladder immediately!"
Wushuang Lord agreed.
Subsequently, after Ye Tian reported the space-time coordinates, Wushuang Lord was able to construct the Space-Time Ladder.
Soon, the Space-Time Ladder was completed, connecting to the Source Sea.
-
Somewhere in the Source Sea.
Wushuang Lord stepped out of the Space-Time Ladder and sensed the Source Sea.
"It truly is an unknown Eternal Sea, and a relatively young one at that!"
Wushuang Lord murmured.
Swish!
The void in the distance rippled, and one of Ye Tian's duplicate bodies walked over. Seeing Wushuang Lord, he bowed respectfully: "Greetings, Wushuang Lord!"
Seeing this duplicate body of Ye Tian, Wushuang Lord knew it was either Ye Tian's clone or his main body, and she roughly understood the situation.
However, Wushuang Lord still spent some time investigating the Source Sea and peering into the memories of some creatures in the Source Sea to understand the situation here.
As long as it was proven that Ye Tian did not belong to a certain Legendary-grade force, there would be no problem.
And the Source Sea only had a few Legendary Lords; naturally, no Legendary-grade force existed there.
Thus.
"Endless Lord, after verification, you are fully qualified to join the Zhenwu Holy Land and receive Heaven-Tier Holy Son level member treatment!" Wushuang Lord announced.
"What, Heaven-Tier Holy Son level!!!"
When Yinyue Lord heard of such treatment, she was instantly shocked.
To become the highest level of Holy Son immediately upon joining the Zhenwu Holy Land—this treatment was too good.
But when she thought of Ye Tian's strength, she felt it was normal again.
