Chapter 1721: A River of Time, Controlling Time!

"Greetings, Lord Wushuang..."
Ye Tian greeted them one by one.
"Lord Endless is truly remarkable!" Lord Wushuang praised.
Other Half-step Legendary Realm Lords also offered words of praise, their voices filled with surprise and disbelief.
After greeting the Half-step Legendary Realm Lords, Ye Tian looked toward this trial.
To be able to stop all the Half-step Legendary Realm Lords, the difficulty of this trial could well be imagined.
Before the many Half-step Legendary Realm Lords was a river. This was not a river of grey mist, but a River of Time.
Ye Tian was no stranger to the River of Time. Many worlds had them; for instance, the countless heavens and worlds within the Source Sea all had Rivers of Time, which were branches of the Source Sea's River of Time.
For creatures of ordinary worlds, breaking free from the River of Time allowed them to live as long as the heavens and earth.
For a Legendary Lord like Ye Tian, a River of Time was nothing; he could even create countless Rivers of Time with a wave of his hand.
However, the River of Time within the Grey Mist Stone Statue was no ordinary River of Time.
As for what kind of River of Time it specifically was, Ye Tian had not entered it yet, so he was not entirely sure.
"Lord Wushuang, is there some mystery to this River of Time?" Ye Tian asked.
Lord Wushuang said, "This River of Time is a branch of the Void Region's River of Time. It was forcibly intercepted by the Eternal Controller with great power and placed here, then modified. Once we enter this River of Time, our time will be reversed, and our cultivation will continuously decrease, regressing from Legendary Lord to Half-step Eternal Realm, then Supreme... step by step, until finally returning to being ordinary and being assimilated by the River of Time. The last time the Grey Mist Stone Statue appeared, a Legendary Realm Lord came here. He wanted to cross the River of Time but ultimately failed. We have known of this River of Time's existence for a long time, but so far, no one has been able to cross it. It is said that top-tier Legendary Realm Lords have entered, but there were no rewards. Furthermore, the rewards for Legendary Realm Lords passing the previous trials are very ordinary. The opportunities within the Grey Mist Stone Statue are only useful to Legendary Lords and newly promoted Legendary Realm Lords; they are of no use to those powerful Legendary Realm Lords, so they naturally won't come in."
"So that's how it is."
Ye Tian nodded slightly.
Since it was of no use to powerful Legendary Realm Lords, there was naturally no need for them to enter. Entering would just take up a slot and waste time; it was better to let the Legendary Lords of their own factions come in and try.
This was also why Lord Longgu did not enter.
"Only a powerful Legendary Realm Lord can cross this River of Time!"
Ye Tian thought to himself.
He estimated that he certainly wouldn't be able to cross this River of Time, but this River of Time must surely count as an opportunity.
Otherwise, these Half-step Legendary Realm Lords wouldn't still be staying here.
Sure enough.
In the following days, each Half-step Legendary Realm Lord attempted to enter this River of Time. Each time, they wouldn't stay for long. Once their cultivation fell to the Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord level, they would return and begin to recover their cultivation.
Ye Tian also consulted these Half-step Legendary Realm Lords, and they revealed some information.
In the River of Time, although time would be reversed, one could clearly perceive the power of Eternal Time during this process. This helped in the comprehension of other Eternal Paths, as many Eternal Paths were mixed with the rules of time.
The River of Time here was the source of everything. In the Void Region, one could not enter the River of Time at all, nor could one perceive its existence. Only here was there such an opportunity.
What the Half-step Legendary Realm Lords cared about were not those Eternal Paths; in fact, they had all comprehended many Perfected Stage Eternal Paths. What they cared about was completely controlling time.
Once they completely controlled time, they could use the power of time to become a Legendary Realm Lord.
There are two ways to become a Legendary Realm Lord: one is to completely control time, and the other is to completely control space.
Once time is controlled, the entire series of Eternal Paths related to time can surpass the Perfected Stage and reach the Legendary level. This is the so-called Legendary-level Eternal Path.
At that time, they would be very powerful even among the Legendary Realm.
But now, in the Void Region, many newly ascended Legendary Realm Lords have not controlled time or space; they forcibly stepped into the Legendary Realm through various Legendary-level Eternal Paths.
Back then, the Legendary Realm Lord who tried to cross the River of Time had not controlled time; otherwise, he wouldn't have been unable to cross it.
"I haven't even comprehended a single Eternal Path to the Perfected Stage; I'm too far from controlling time. My Grey Mist Eternal Path has hit a bottleneck at the Entry Peak; perhaps I can use this River of Time to comprehend the mysteries of time and help it break through the bottleneck to the Minor Stage."
Ye Tian thought.
Thus, he also prepared to enter the River of Time.
Boom!!!
As soon as he entered the River of Time, the time of this body was forcibly reversed. Even though Ye Tian resisted, he could not withstand it.
He saw scenes of the past, and even his other copy bodies were almost affected.
In desperation, he disconnected the link between his copy bodies to avoid being affected.
Time was regressing, and Ye Tian's cultivation was also regressing.
Domain Realm Legendary Lord!
Perfected Realm Legendary Lord!
Initial Realm Legendary Lord!
Just as he was about to fall out of the Initial Realm Legendary Lord stage, Ye Tian forcibly rushed out of the River of Time.
At this moment, the cultivation of this copy body was truly frozen at the Initial Realm Legendary Lord level.
If it were any other Legendary Lord, they would probably have to cultivate back step by step. Although it would only take some time, that time would likely not be short. However, Ye Tian did not need to cultivate slowly; he simply annihilated this copy body and then replaced it with another copy body.
And so.
This copy body of Ye Tian's left first, and then he returned with a new copy body.
When others saw Ye Tian recover his cultivation so quickly, they were somewhat surprised, but knowing it was Ye Tian's secret, no one questioned him.
In this way, Ye Tian practiced in the River of Time almost every moment, constantly comprehending the mysteries of time.
Finally, on this day, after making a breakthrough in the aspect of time, Ye Tian's Grey Mist Eternal Path directly broke through its bottleneck and ascended to the Minor Stage.
At the same time, Ye Tian began to comprehend other Eternal Path rhythms.
The Primal Chaos Eternal Path and the Myriad Life Eternal Path seemed to have little to do with time, but time and space extend into all Eternal Paths. Thus, by controlling more of time, combined with that treasure map and many other opportunities, Ye Tian made continuous progress in the Primal Chaos Eternal Path and the Myriad Life Eternal Path.
These two Eternal Paths were about to ascend to the Perfected Stage.
