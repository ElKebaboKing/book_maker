Chapter 1741: Please Throw Me into the River of Time, Lord Wushuang!

Lord Baiheng was about to charge toward the Legendary Realm Lord level. This was a major event for the Zhenwu Sacred Land, and many Legendary Lords were paying close attention.
Soon.
Lord Baiheng quickly began his charge toward the Legendary Realm Lord level, and this time, multiple Legendary Realm Lords acted as protectors for him.
The River of Time.
Lord Baiheng arrived here, allowing the waters of the River of Time to flow over his body. The infinite power of time was unable to affect his physical form.
At this moment, he had already completely mastered time. It was like a fish entering water or a bird entering the sky; having entered his own domain, the power would not harm him.
Since Lord Baiheng had mastered time, this masterless power of time would not inflict any damage upon him.
He was as relaxed and natural in this River of Time as a fish in water.
All around.
One Legendary Realm Lord after another stood guard, preventing any other Legendary Realm Lords from disturbing Lord Baiheng.
"Lord Baiheng, you may break through with peace of mind," Lord Longgu said.
"Mm, my thanks to all the Legendary Realm Lords this time," Lord Baiheng said in gratitude.
Subsequently, he began his breakthrough, and everything proceeded very smoothly.
Soon, the critical moment arrived. He was about to become an incarnation of time, completely merging into one with time.
At this time, the several Legendary Realm Lords of the Zhenwu Sacred Land each looked tense, but from beginning to end, not a single Legendary Realm Lord came to attack. Even Lord Guixing had given up.
"Lord Guixing did not come," Lord Longgu remarked.
"Lord Guixing attacked once before. This time, he likely fears we have an ambush. He fears losing a clone, and even more, he fears losing his Ghost Star Arrow. The Ghost Star Arrow can exert powerful might within the Void Sea, but its power is mediocre within the River of Time, and it might even be intercepted by us. Therefore, he does not dare to take the risk," Lord Longgu said.
"It is for the best that no Legendary Realm Lords attacked. Let us continue waiting. Lord Baiheng has already passed the most critical moment and has broken through to the Legendary Realm Lord level. It will be fine once the transformation is complete."
A moment later, Lord Baiheng completed his breakthrough. He only needed to return and stabilize his realm.
"My thanks to everyone!"
Lord Baiheng was incredibly excited.
He had cultivated for an unimaginably long period of time, and now he had finally broken through.
"Very good. Our Zhenwu Sacred Land has finally birthed another Legendary Realm Lord," Lord Longgu and the others said excitedly.
"Everyone, I shall return to enter seclusion for a period of time. As for that plan, we shall act together once I have finished my seclusion," Lord Baiheng said.
"Agreed!" Lord Longgu and the others replied.
Subsequently, Lord Baiheng returned to enter seclusion.
In a flash, another several million Eternal Years passed.
During this time, Ye Tian had made significant progress. Especially in the aspect of mastering time, he felt that he was truly about to completely master time.
"The Legendary-level Time Ancestral Talent has been of immense help to me. If not for this talent, it would have been impossible for me to make such progress in the aspect of time," Ye Tian thought to himself.
He prepared to charge toward mastering time recently, and the best place to charge toward mastering time was naturally the River of Time.
Half-step Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords were not qualified to enter the River of Time; this was a rule of the Zhenwu Sacred Land. Only Half-step Legendary Realm Lords were qualified to enter the Bridge of Time to comprehend time.
However.
He did not need to go through the Bridge of Time. He only needed a Legendary Realm Lord to throw one of his cloned bodies into the River of Time. With his level of mastery over time, even if his cultivation was weak, he could persist for a period of time.
He could also continuously clone bodies, and thus could continue his comprehension within the River of Time for a long duration until he completely mastered time.
"Which Legendary Realm Lord should I ask for help?"
Ye Tian thought it over and decided to seek help from Lord Wushuang.
Although Lord Wushuang mastered space, as a Legendary Realm Lord, entering the River of Time was naturally something she could do.
Thus, Ye Tian went to find Lord Wushuang.
......
"Greetings, Lord Wushuang!"
Ye Tian came to visit Lord Wushuang and spoke.
"Lord Endless, what is the matter?" Lord Wushuang asked.
"Lord Wushuang, I wish to enter the River of Time and would like to ask for your help," Ye Tian stated.
Immediately, Lord Wushuang’s expression changed slightly as she said, "Ye Tian, it is not that I will not help, but rules are rules. Although you have performed a great service by discovering the Time Eternal Gold, the Zhenwu Sacred Land cannot allow a Half-step Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord to enter the Bridge of Time. Otherwise, other Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords will be dissatisfied, and once a conflict is triggered, it would be a very serious matter."
Seeing that Lord Wushuang had misunderstood, Ye Tian said, "Lord Wushuang, what I spoke of was not entering the River of Time through the Bridge of Time. Rather, I am asking Lord Wushuang to simply throw me into the River of Time."
"Throw you into the River of Time? Without the protection of the Bridge of Time, you would be pulverized by the power of the River of Time in an instant. The true River of Time is not like those diverted and modified tributaries of the River of Time within the Gray Mist Stone Statues," Lord Wushuang said.
"I know, but I am confident!"
The aura of time emerged from Ye Tian's body; this was a Perfected mastery over time.
Lord Wushuang looked at Ye Tian and saw the surging power of time from his body. That power of time was nearly comparable to the recently ascended Lord Baiheng.
"Mastering time! No, it is still a bit short," Lord Wushuang said in shock.
Ye Tian was only a Half-step Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord and had not even condensed a Legendary Body, yet his mastery over time surpassed those Half-step Legendary Realm Lords. Was this cultivating across realms?
"How did you do it?" Lord Wushuang asked in disbelief.
"My talent is relatively good," Ye Tian said truthfully.
"Talent is relatively good?" Lord Wushuang rolled her eyes. Among those who could cultivate to become a Legendary Lord, whose talent was not good? Among those who could cultivate to become a Legendary Realm Lord, whose talent was not monstrous?
Even those Peak Legendary Realm Lords, or even Eternal Controllers, were not this monstrous when they were at the Legendary Lord realm!
She seriously suspected whether Ye Tian was some Eternal Controller experiencing life.
Of course, that was highly unlikely. Which Eternal Controller was not incredibly proud? How could they possibly disguise themselves as a Legendary Lord?
"Fine, I agree. Are you ready?" Lord Wushuang asked.
"I am ready!" Ye Tian responded.
At that moment, Lord Wushuang grabbed Ye Tian with one hand, then tore through time and threw Ye Tian into the River of Time.
