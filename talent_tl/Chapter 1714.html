Chapter 1713: The Eternal Controller Traversing the Timeline!

Somewhere in the Source Sea.
Ye Tian arrived here with Lord Binghan and Lord Shengfeng, while the other Legendary Realm Lords and the members of the Guard Legion were unaware.
Currently, Ye Tian did not want to reveal the news of this place.
"It's actually a Barren Area of the Source Sea!"
Ye Tian finally understood why no one had discovered the anomaly here.
A Barren Area of the Source Sea refers to a region where no Heaven and Earth, Eternal World, or Treasure Land has ever been born, and there are not even any so-called Forbidden Lands.
A Barren Area is almost the same as a Void Area; there is nothing, it is completely empty, and even the Source of the Source Sea is thinner than in other regions.
Many people once believed that Barren Areas must contain Treasure Lands or worlds and spent countless ages exploring them, but in the end, they found nothing.
After countless ages of verification, it was concluded that Barren Areas have no value, so no one ever comes here.
"It is precisely because it is a Barren Area that no one comes here, so even if a phenomenon occurs, it will not be discovered!" Lord Binghan said. "In fact, before the Gray Mist Statue appeared, this was not a Barren Area. It was only after the Gray Mist Statue descended that the Barren Area was born. Perhaps the Source of the Source Sea is wary of and repels the Gray Mist Statue, which is why no resources are born here anymore."
Ye Tian nodded, also agreeing with this explanation.
"It's right here!"
Lord Binghan pointed to a patch of the Barren Area in front of them and said.
In fact, even without Lord Binghan's reminder, Ye Tian had already sensed the anomaly in this area. A terrifying aura spread out from this region, an aura that made even a Perfected Stage Legendary Lord like him feel a sense of trepidation.
Moreover, he was actually unable to detect the source of this aura.
Immediately, he threw a punch toward the Barren Area in front of him.
Boom!!!
The void shattered, but nothing changed.
He still had not found the Gray Mist Statue.
According to the changes in the anomaly here, it would inevitably be discovered in about a hundred thousand Eternal Years, or perhaps even sooner. After all, there were many more in the Eternal Realm within the Source Sea now, and someone might stumble upon this Barren Area and discover it one day.
But Ye Tian did not want to continue waiting.
He pondered for a moment, then took out the Eternal Supreme Treasure, the Jialan Leaf, and activated its power, attempting to use the Jialan Leaf to sense the Gray Mist Statue.
The Jialan Leaf was an Eternal Supreme Treasure born from the Source Sea, and since the Source of the Source Sea repelled the Gray Mist Statue, perhaps the Jialan Leaf could trigger a reaction from the Gray Mist Statue, allowing him to sense its position in spacetime.
Buzz!!!
The power of the Jialan Leaf was continuously released.
At a certain moment, the Jialan Leaf was suppressed by a terrifying force and began to tremble violently.
"Found it!"
Ye Tian sensed the location of the Gray Mist Statue and seized the opportunity, instantly tearing through spacetime and using the Void Step to enter an unknown spacetime domain.
Lord Binghan and Lord Shengfeng were a step slower in their reaction and did not enter, leaving them to stare at each other outside.
Although they knew they could use an Eternal Supreme Treasure to cause repulsion with the Gray Mist Statue and thus enter the spacetime where it resided, they did not possess any Eternal Supreme Treasures at all.
Whoosh!
The void rippled, and Ye Tian appeared.
This was clearly another of Ye Tian's clones, so Lord Binghan and the others were not surprised.
At this time, Lord Shengfeng asked, "Endless Lord, did you see the Gray Mist Statue?"
"I saw it!" Ye Tian nodded.
Lord Shengfeng added, "Endless Lord, the interior of that Gray Mist Statue is hollow. The only place to enter is through that crack. However, that crack in the Gray Mist Statue is extremely dangerous. We were heavily injured as soon as we entered back then. But Endless Lord, your strength is immense; perhaps you can truly break through and enter."
"No rush, I'll observe from outside the Gray Mist Statue first!"
Ye Tian said.
Outside the Gray Mist Statue.
Ye Tian stood there, feeling the terrifying power radiating from the Gray Mist Statue. It was indeed difficult for those in the ordinary Eternal Realm to approach this place; once they got close to the Gray Mist Statue, they would likely be easily obliterated by this force.
However, this Gray Mist Statue was indeed an unimaginable treasure.
At this moment, Ye Tian cultivated here for a bit and discovered that the speed at which his Will fused with the Innate Eternal Rules had increased several times over.
Of course, the Gray Mist Statue offered more than just that; there were other opportunities as well.
Ye Tian continuously traversed around the Gray Mist Statue and discovered that many things had been born on its surface, just like on a landmass.
Ye Tian found that these things were all extremely precious to those in the Eternal Realm, and some treasures even had a certain level of use for Legendary Lords.
Thus, he collected all of these resources.
"These should be treasures nurtured here by the power of the Gray Mist Statue over the long ages. Just the treasures piled up here make this trip worthwhile."
A long time later, Ye Tian had collected almost all the resources on the surface of the Gray Mist Statue.
At this point, he arrived at the crack on the chest of the Gray Mist Statue.
He did not enter immediately but instead silently comprehended this crack from a short distance away.
In his perception, this crack had clearly been created by an attack.
He found it hard to imagine the strength of the person who had attacked; to be able to strike with such terrifying force was something he was far from achieving, and even ordinary Legend Realm Lords were likely far from it.
"I actually can't sense any Will or Innate Eternal Rules within this single attack."
Ye Tian frowned, feeling very puzzled by this attack.
Even with Eternal Origin Force, one could see the shadows of Will and Innate Eternal Rules within its power, yet this attack seemed to contain neither.
True Void Sacred Ground.
One of Ye Tian's clones went to consult Lord Wushuang, a Half-Step Legend Realm Lord.
· ·········Seeking Flowers··· ·····
"Lord Wushuang, I saw a mark in a certain ruin. This mark was caused by an attack, but I never saw any aura of Will or Innate Eternal Rules on it. However, it must have been an attack struck by a very powerful existence, yet it also lacked the aura of Eternal Origin Force. What kind of attack power is this?"
Ye Tian asked for guidance.
"Oh, an attack that contains no rules or Will, and also lacks the aura of Eternal Origin Force..."
Lord Wushuang was slightly surprised and then fell into deep thought.
After a moment, Lord Wushuang asked again, "Does that mark truly exist in the present spacetime? If you are at the location of that mark, you might as well try to continue comprehending it from the perspective of spacetime; do not try to comprehend its power."
From the perspective of spacetime?
Ye Tian then followed Lord Wushuang's method to comprehend this crack. Suddenly, he was surprised to find that the crack no longer existed; from the perspective of spacetime, he could not sense the crack at all.
..... ... .......
So, he spoke truthfully, "Lord Wushuang, I actually cannot sense that crack anymore, as if it no longer exists. Even if an Eternal Supreme Treasure cannot be touched, it wouldn't cease to exist, but that crack seems to truly not exist, as if the crack I saw was fake. Yet, I clearly sensed many things before."
At this, Lord Wushuang smiled and said, "I am fairly certain now. The mark you saw should be an attack from an Eternal Controller."
"An Eternal Controller, what is that?"
Ye Tian asked.
Lord Wushuang said, "The Eternal Controller is the end of everything, the ultimate extreme of cultivation, and the endpoint of all legends. The ultimate goal of Legend Realm Lords is also to become an Eternal Controller; they are existences that govern all of spacetime. It can be said that they have jumped out of the timeline and can even traverse between the past and the future. Because their attacks do not exist in any spacetime of the past or future, they possess this characteristic, just like themselves. For them, things like Will, rules, or physical strength have no meaning."
"Traversing the past and future?"
Ye Tian said in surprise.
Lord Wushuang nodded and said, "However, although they can traverse to the past, they cannot change it; otherwise, it would cause a spacetime collapse that even they themselves could not withstand, and the entire Void Area would suppress them. But they can arrive in the future in a single step without restriction. For example, if their gaze sees a certain treasure being produced in a certain place in the future, they can arrive in the future in one step to seize it. Therefore, if some astonishing, heaven-defying treasure is born in the Void Area, it is very likely that several Eternal Controllers will traverse the timeline to fight for it, thereby leaving marks. In the current Void Area, there are quite a few such marks; what you saw might be one of them."
"Thank you, Lord Wushuang, for clearing up my confusion!"
Ye Tian now completely understood.
