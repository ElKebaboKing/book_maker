Chapter 1729: Touching the Control of Time!

Ye Tian fused one Epic Time Ancestral Talent after another. To accelerate the fusion, Ye Tian began to accelerate time within his mind, prompting the process to complete sooner.
In this way, time slowly passed.
Initially, Ye Tian was busy fusing these Time Talents and had no time to meditate on Time itself. However, it was worth it; once the fusion was finished, his meditation speed would far surpass his current state.
After a long time.
Ye Tian fused a massive amount of Time Talents, finally triggering a qualitative change in his Time Talent.
On this day.
As Ye Tian fused yet another Time Talent, his Time Ancestral Talent finally began to transform.
Boom!!!
In an instant, Ye Tian's consciousness seemed to enter the River of Time to wander. This was no ordinary River of Time, but the River of Time of the Void Region, the source of all Rivers of Time.
But this wandering soon ended, and the transformation was completely finished.
Immediately, Ye Tian checked his Time Ancestral Talent.
[Time Ancestral Talent: Legendary-grade]
As expected, Ye Tian's Time Ancestral Talent had reached the Legendary-grade Time Ancestral Talent tier.
"Let's test the effect!"
Ye Tian began his first cultivation session in the Time Meditation Area.
Buzz!
Countless insights flooded in, causing Ye Tian's comprehension of Time to rise at an incredible speed.
This feeling was something he had never experienced before.
It had to be said, a Legendary-grade Time Ancestral Talent was indeed too powerful!
"Perhaps I can directly achieve Controlling Time here," Ye Tian thought.
Controlling Time!
What kind of concept was this? It was equivalent to possessing the qualification to enter the Legendary Realm Lord rank in the future.
Of course, he also knew very well that if he wanted to achieve Controlling Time, it wouldn't be that easy. After all, his attainments in Time were far inferior to those Half-Step Legendary Realm Lords.
The reason those Half-Step Legendary Realm Lords had hope of Controlling Time in such a treasure land was that they were already not too far from it. He, on the other hand, was essentially starting from zero, while the Half-Step Legendary Realm Lords were starting from seventy or eighty.
Therefore, the difficulty was different.
However, Ye Tian was still very confident in himself. At the very least, he had hope of reaching a level close to Controlling Time.
"In the years to come, I will exhaust everything to meditate on Time."
Ye Tian murmured.
Long years passed.
One Eternal Year!
Two Eternal Years!
As time passed, this treasure land continued to collapse, and its effects became slower and slower. The eight Half-Step Legendary Realm Lords, Lord Longgu, and Ye Tian were all meditating with all their might. After all, such an opportunity might only come once.
A Peak Legendary Realm Lord failing to break through to Eternal Controller—even if such events occurred, they were usually impossible to find!
Ye Tian's only disadvantage was that his cultivation was too low. If he were a Half-Step Legendary Realm Lord, the benefits he gained from meditation would be even higher.
Finally, two hundred Eternal Years passed.
At this time, Lord Longgu reminded them, "This place can no longer hold together. You should head out. Otherwise, once this place erupts, the power released will be comparable to a full-strength strike from a top-tier Legendary Realm Lord. Even I would be heavily injured."
"Out!"
One by one, the Half-Step Legendary Realm Lords stopped their cultivation, and Ye Tian followed them out.
Lord Longgu did not stay behind either and went straight out.
After waiting for several decades, the treasure land completely collapsed.
The terrifying power nearly destroyed the purple continent, but the Time Eternal Gold was not destroyed.
Whoosh!
With one hand, Lord Longgu directly grabbed the Time Eternal Gold vein. Then, stepping across the River of Time, he quickly returned to the Zhenwu Sacred Ground.
Meanwhile, Lord Wushuang and the other Half-Step Legendary Realm Lords began to chat about their gains.
"I've gained a lot. Although there's still no hope of stepping into the Legendary Realm Lord rank, at least my strength has increased significantly."
"I'm still a bit short of completely Controlling Time. This 'bit' might take countless years, or I might never be able to control it."
"Lord Wushuang, you specialize in Space. How were your gains?"
The other Half-Step Legendary Realm Lords looked at Lord Wushuang with curiosity.
Lord Wushuang was one of the two Half-Step Legendary Realm Lords who had been meditating in the Space area, and what she was aiming for was Controlling Space.
Her application of the Dao of Space and Ice had reached a terrifying level; her ice power could even shatter space. Thus, she chose to try and Control Space.
Of course, it wasn't that her mastery of Time was poor, just that it wasn't as good as her Space.
Lord Wushuang said indifferently, "The gains were great. I've touched the threshold of Controlling Space. Within a hundred million Eternal Years, I am confident I can break through..."
"Congratulations, Lord Wushuang!"
"Congratulations, Lord Wushuang!"
"Congratulations, Lord Wushuang!"
The Half-Step Legendary Realm Lords offered their congratulations.
A hundred million Eternal Years seemed like a long time, but for these Half-Step Legendary Realm Lords, it was actually a very short period.
Moreover, once the Zhenwu Sacred Ground established the Bridge of Time, this time could be further reduced.
Perhaps before long, Lord Wushuang would be able to completely Control Space, thereby easily breaking through to the Legendary Realm Lord rank.
"Is no one going to ask me?"
Ye Tian stood there, thinking to himself.
These Half-Step Legendary Realm Lords didn't think his gains would be very significant, so they didn't ask, lest they discourage him.
But in reality, Ye Tian's progress far surpassed these Half-Step Legendary Realm Lords.
He was also about to touch the threshold of Controlling Time. If 99% was considered touching the threshold of Time, then Ye Tian had now reached 98%, with only the final 2% remaining.
But even with only 2% left, if Ye Tian wanted to achieve complete control, he would still need a very long time.
However, his realm was now established. In the future, when meditating on Eternal Daos involving Time, he could easily cultivate them to the Legendary-grade level.
Once he mastered a large number of Legendary-grade Eternal Daos, how terrifying would his strength be?
Therefore, he was the biggest beneficiary of this opportunity.
"Let's go, let's head back. This place no longer has any value," Lord Wushuang said.
"Let's go!"
Ye Tian shuttled toward the direction of the Source Sea.
This time, the eight Half-Step Legendary Realm Lords did not take Ye Tian's speed into account. They directly left him behind and shuttled away.
Zhenwu Sacred Ground.
Lord Longgu began to construct the Bridge of Time. Upon hearing this news, many Legendary Lords appeared, all wanting to witness the scene.
Ancient Half-Step Legendary Realm Lords who had disappeared for a long time also appeared. After all, the Bridge of Time was a great opportunity for them as well.
Among the spectating Legendary Lords, Ye Tian was one of them.
Just as Lord Longgu prepared the materials and was about to build the Bridge of Time, a voice came from the void.
"Longgu, where did your Zhenwu Sacred Ground get so much Time Eternal Gold? You actually dare to build a Bridge of Time!"
