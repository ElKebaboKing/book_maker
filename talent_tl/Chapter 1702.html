Chapter 1701: A Battle Comparable to a Perfected Stage Legendary Lord!

Lord Alliance Headquarters.
Qin Yu was getting impatient. He gave a final ultimatum: "I will give you one last chance. Are you willing to submit to me? If you do not answer now, then you shall never answer again!"
After speaking, Qin Yu's aura erupted completely.
Boom!!!
The entire Space-Time Territory and the Lord Alliance Headquarters were nearly frozen under a terrifying power. Those in the Eternal Stage had no ability to move at all, as if everything had been imprisoned—even space-time itself was locked down.
This was the strength of a near-Perfected Stage Legendary Lord, far surpassing ordinary Initial Stage Lords and even further beyond ordinary Legendary Stage experts.
"This... such a terrifying aura. Can the Endless Lord defeat such a being?"
Bing Han Lord began to have doubts, her heart starting to waver.
Not only was she doubting, but even the other Legendary Lords were beginning to falter. After all, they did not want to perish!
Right at this moment, Ye Tian finally arrived on the scene.
If he hadn't arrived, and if these five Legendary Lords had all defected to Qin Yu, he would have been left to fight a lone battle.
"Young Master Qin Yu, this person is the Endless Lord, Ye Tian!"
Seeing Ye Tian appear, Dark Mist Lord spoke immediately.
Boom!!!!!
Qin Yu struck Dark Mist Lord with a palm, directly causing Dark Mist Lord's body to crack. Countless particles suffered annihilation, and he was instantly heavily injured.
This was his true body, not an avatar; he had nearly been killed in a single blow.
At this moment, Dark Mist Lord was furious inside, but on the surface, he displayed a look of complete innocence.
"Young Master Qin Yu, what did I do wrong?" Dark Mist Lord asked.
"Hmph!" Qin Yu snorted coldly and said, "Dark Mist Lord, you dare to toy with this young master? You previously said the Endless Lord was merely an Immortal Stage Lord, but is he an Immortal Stage Lord now? He is clearly a Legendary Stage Lord, and he hasn't just entered the Legendary Stage—he has reached the Initial Stage, and specifically the seventh step of the Initial Stage. He is the highest-cultivated Legendary Lord in this Space-Time Territory. This young master hates being deceived above all else. If it weren't for the fact that you had some merit in bringing me here, you would be dead right now!"
"What? A Seventh-Step Initial Stage Legendary Lord?"
Dark Mist Lord was dumbfounded.
He observed Ye Tian carefully. Ye Tian really was a Legendary Lord, and he had even stepped into the Initial Stage that Dark Mist Lord dreamed of. Furthermore, he far surpassed ordinary Initial Stage experts. As for exactly which step of the Initial Stage he had reached, Dark Mist Lord's own realm was too low to see through it.
But Qin Yu had no reason to lie to him. If Qin Yu said Ye Tian had reached the Seventh-Step Initial Stage, then he must have reached that realm.
For a moment, he didn't know how to explain.
After all, not much time had passed since his battle with Ye Tian. No matter how good Ye Tian's talent was, in a place like the Space-Time Territory, being lucky enough to break through to become a Legendary Lord would already be quite impressive. How could he have suddenly leaped to the seventh step of the Initial Stage?
This was not normal!
If he were in Qin Yu's position, he wouldn't believe it either.
"Young Master Qin Yu, I really did not deceive you!" Dark Mist Lord said anxiously.
Qin Yu simply waved his hand, signaling Dark Mist Lord to shut up.
He no longer cared what the truth of the matter was.
He only wanted to handle the matter of Ye Tian now; everything else could wait until later.
"You are quite good. To be able to cultivate to the Seventh-Step Initial Stage in a place like the Source Sea... are you willing to become my guard?" Qin Yu invited.
As for whether this invitation was sincere, only he knew.
But even if it were a sincere invitation, Ye Tian could not possibly agree.
Thus, he refused directly: "Zhen Yu Lord, I do not have the habit of being a guard for others. This is the Source Sea Space-Time Territory, and outsiders are not particularly welcome. If Zhen Yu Lord has no other business, please leave."
Whoosh!
A frigid aura erupted from Qin Yu's body, turning into an endless killing intent that pierced through infinite space-time and rushed toward Ye Tian.
"You dare speak to me like that? You seek death!!!!"
Qin Yu attacked, and from the very first move, he had no intention of holding back.
He directly erupted with all his power, performing one of his top-tier Legendary Eternal Techniques—Endless Fire Prison!
Buzz buzz buzz!!!!!
Seemingly infinite fire prisons stacked continuously. In reality, this involved space, forming a cycle. Without sufficient power, one could not break the Endless Fire Prison and would be trapped inside, burned to death by the fire prison's flame power.
"What a terrifying attack!!!"
One by one, the Legendary Lords retreated, not daring to touch Qin Yu's attack. The five Legendary Lords of the Space-Time Territory also dodged away.
Right then, a power of extreme strength erupted from Ye Tian's body.
Rip! With a tearing sound!!!!
The Endless Fire Prison was actually shattered directly by Ye Tian. Those terrifying flames were unable to harm Ye Tian at all.
Ye Tian walked out, bathed in the dissipating flames of the Endless Fire Prison. With a shake of his body, those flames suffered complete annihilation.
"These flames of yours belong to a certain Eternal Dao of fire, but unfortunately, their power is still a bit lacking," Ye Tian said with a smile.
Boom!!!!
The two collided and began to fight. After only a few exchanges, Qin Yu was sent flying back.
Abruptly.
A Will Slaying Void Sword, composed entirely of Eternal Original Force, pierced through. No matter how Qin Yu tried to block, his body was still pierced by the sword, and he was instantly significantly injured.
"A Legendary-grade Eternal Technique!!!!! Impossible! You are merely an Initial Stage Lord, how could you create a Legendary-grade Eternal Technique? Even many Domain Stage Legendary Lords aren't necessarily able to create a Legendary-grade Eternal Technique!!!"
Qin Yu roared.
At this moment, his mindset truly collapsed.
He had thought this would be a great opportunity, but instead, he had encountered a Legendary Lord like Ye Tian—a Legendary Lord who knew a Legendary-grade Eternal Technique. Even in the Boundless Sea, such a person would be a legendary peerless monstrous talent, far beyond what he could compare to.
"No, I haven't lost yet. I still have an Eternal Supreme Treasure!!!!"
Qin Yu thought.
Immediately, he activated his Eternal Supreme Treasure—the Fire Domain Sword!
This was the Fire Domain Sword, which could greatly amplify his combat power. In the Boundless Sea, the Fire Domain Sword was an Eternal Supreme Treasure that held a respectable rank, not belonging to the lowest tier.
Once Qin Yu held the Fire Domain Sword, his combat power was already comparable to a true Perfected Stage Legendary Lord. Meanwhile, the strength Ye Tian had just displayed was also only comparable to a Perfected Stage Legendary Lord.
"Kill!"
Qin Yu fought with all his might, refusing to accept defeat.
He also seemed to have forgotten that Ye Tian possessed several avatars, but in reality, Ye Tian only needed one replicated body to defeat Qin Yu.
Seeing Qin Yu use an Eternal Supreme Treasure, Ye Tian no longer hid his strength and activated his Hunyuan Command.
