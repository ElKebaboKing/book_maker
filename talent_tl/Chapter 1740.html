Chapter 1739: Breakthrough, Sixth-Tier Eternal Will!

Ye Tian first copied the body of a Domain Realm Legendary Lord from the Zhenwu Sacred Land, then walked toward the blue palace.
Whether he could enter or not depended on this attempt.
If he could enter, it would mean that even the abilities of a Legendary Realm Lord could not see through the copying ability of his Copying Talent; if he could not, it would mean the Copying Talent was not that impressive.
*Whoosh!*
Ye Tian's body entered the blue palace without being restricted.
"I really can enter. In that case, all the treasures in this palace belong to me."
Immediately.
Ye Tian randomly chose three treasures and then came out right away.
Next, Ye Tian annihilated this copied body and began copying the body of another Domain Realm Legendary Lord.
In this way, Ye Tian entered the blue palace time and again using the identities of different Legendary Lords.
Before long, nearly a thousand treasures from the blue palace had all fallen into Ye Tian's hands.
It could be said that with the value of these treasures, his current net worth was already enough to rival an ordinary Legendary Realm Lord.
With all the treasures in hand, there was no need for Ye Tian to stay here any longer.
Immediately, Ye Tian left the place, passed through the time trap, and returned to the outer area.
On the way back, he encountered other Legendary Lords, but he no longer cared.
After all, some Legendary Lords had already seen him pass through the time trap, so the news of him crossing it could not be hidden.
However, he would not return the treasures that had reached his hands.
Currently, while the other Legendary Lords didn't know how many treasures were in the blue palace, they would definitely know that the Time River Pearl was in his hands.
However, he was a member of the Zhenwu Sacred Land after all, and with a copied body staying there, he didn't fear any ambushes.
Soon.
Ye Tian rushed out of Lord Yanglie's tomb and returned to the Zhenwu Sacred Land at his fastest speed.
......
Back at the Zhenwu Sacred Land, Ye Tian didn't concern himself with what happened afterward.
Upon his return, he immediately began refining the Forbidden Heaven Sea Fruit. There was no need to keep this treasure; he would refine it directly to enhance his will.
His willpower was already very close to the Fifth-Tier Peak. If he refined the Forbidden Heaven Sea Fruit directly, he would 100% be able to break through to the Sixth-Tier Eternal Will.
Immediately.
Ye Tian took out the Forbidden Heaven Sea Fruit and swallowed it in one gulp.
*Boom!*
The energy of the Forbidden Heaven Sea Fruit erupted within Ye Tian's body, merging into his body particles and beginning to enhance his will. His Will Source Sea, in particular, began to undergo a transformation.
Soon, Ye Tian's will rose to the Fifth-Tier Peak Extreme.
At a certain moment.
*Crack!*
The shackles were broken, and Ye Tian's will rose to the Sixth-Tier Eternal Will.
*Buzz!!!*
A terrifying force of will emanated from Ye Tian's body.
At this time, the Legendary Realm Lords of the Zhenwu Sacred Land all sensed it. Because Ye Tian had just broken through and could not completely hide his willpower, they sensed a trace of it.
"Lord Wujin's will has actually reached the Sixth-Tier? He hasn't even broken through to the Transcendence Realm yet, has he? This rate of willpower improvement is too fast."
The Legendary Realm Lords were astonished.
After some time, Ye Tian completely refined the energy of the Forbidden Heaven Sea Fruit. His willpower steadily reached the Sixth-Tier and even improved a bit further within that tier, and his aura stabilized.
At this moment.
An avatar of Lord Wushuang descended and arrived before Ye Tian.
"Greetings, Lord Wushuang!"
Ye Tian said respectfully.
"Lord Wujin, I observed earlier that your will was not even at the Fifth-Tier Peak, yet it has suddenly reached the Sixth-Tier." Lord Wushuang expressed her curiosity.
"I had good luck and obtained a Forbidden Heaven Sea Fruit!"
Ye Tian said.
"Obtained from Lord Yanglie's tomb?"
Lord Wushuang guessed.
News of Lord Yanglie's tomb had already spread, so she knew Ye Tian had entered it. Since he returned so early, he must have had a great harvest.
A Forbidden Heaven Sea Fruit was indeed a startlingly great harvest.
"You found the Time River Pearl, didn't you? Otherwise, with your personality, you definitely wouldn't have come back,"
Lord Wushuang said with a smile.
"Indeed!"
Ye Tian admitted.
Lord Wushuang was a Legendary Realm Lord and wouldn't covet his Time River Pearl, so there was no need to hide anything.
However, he did not reveal that he had snatched all the treasures from the inner area of the tomb; otherwise, it would be difficult to explain.
Lord Wushuang did not ask further but instead revealed a piece of news: "Lord Wujin, now that your will has reached the Sixth-Tier, your strength should have improved significantly. You can prepare yourself. In the near future, there might be hope for you to obtain Void Sea Gravel. Of course, it's only a possibility; whether you can get it depends on your strength..."
"Please, Lord Wushuang, tell me more."
Ye Tian said excitedly.
"I cannot reveal it at the moment. That news cannot be spread. You will know when the time comes."
After saying this, Lord Wushuang left.
"What news?"
Ye Tian was incredibly curious. He felt that the Zhenwu Sacred Land must be planning something big; otherwise, they wouldn't be so secretive.
Moreover, Lord Wushuang said he had a chance to obtain Void Sea Gravel, which might be related to the Guixing Tower.
Of course, he didn't speculate too much, nor would he speak of it to others.
"No matter what happens, strength is the most important thing. I must try my best to improve my strength during this time,"
Ye Tian thought.
Immediately, he took out the mid-rank Eternal Supreme Treasure, the Ruyi Void Heaven Shuttle.
Time passed slowly, and after a long while, Ye Tian finally refined the Ruyi Void Heaven Shuttle.
The Ruyi Void Heaven Shuttle not only contained multiple Eternal Daos, but it also had several major functions.
First, it allowed Ye Tian to travel long distances. For example, between the Boundless Sea and the Source Sea, if he were to fly, it would take a very long time, and he could only return to the Source Sea quickly through the Space-Time Stairs.
But after mastering the Ruyi Void Heaven Shuttle, he could reach the Source Sea in just a few shuttles.
It could be said that in terms of traveling, he was now second only to Legendary Realm Lords.
Second, it could break through spatial suppression. For instance, if a half-step Legendary Realm expert who controlled space were to attack him, he could use the Ruyi Void Heaven Shuttle to escape. Without it...
...a half-step Legendary Realm expert who controlled space could kill him with a single thought.
Third, he could escape into the Void Sea and survive there for a period of time.
This was impressive!
Even half-step Legendary Realm Lords found it difficult to enter the River of Time and the Void Sea, yet he could escape into the Void Sea with the help of the Ruyi Void Heaven Shuttle. This was enough to prove the shuttle's power.
Furthermore, escaping into the Void Sea was also a way to evade enemy pursuit.
However, for Legendary Realm Lords, the mere Ruyi Void Heaven Shuttle was of little use.
But for Ye Tian, who was not even a Transcendence Realm Lord yet, the Ruyi Void Heaven Shuttle could greatly enhance his life-preserving capabilities.
