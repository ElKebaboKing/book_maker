Chapter 1747: The Borderland Lord Fishing for Lord Guixing

"This is..."
Lord Baiheng and Lord Wushuang were filled with doubt, not knowing what had happened.
What was with that fishhook?
At this moment, Lord Longgu spoke with a face full of wariness, "A Peak Legendary Realm Lord just took action!"
"Which one?"
Lord Wushuang asked.
She had only recently entered the Legendary Realm and was truly unfamiliar with some of the ancient Legendary Realm Lords.
"It is the Borderland Lord of the Purgatory Sea," Lord Longgu said.
The Borderland Lord?
Neither Lord Wushuang nor Lord Baiheng had ever heard of this Legendary Realm Lord.
Lord Huanghe introduced, "The Borderland Lord is an incomparably ancient Peak Legendary Realm Lord. He is extremely low-profile and likes to disguise himself as an old man fishing in various worlds, occasionally giving pointers to juniors. He is considered a Peak Legendary Realm Lord who does not involve himself in external disputes. Logically speaking, Lord Guixing and the Borderland Lord should have no connection. Why would the Borderland Lord go to save Lord Guixing?"
Lord Huanghe did not understand, and the other Legendary Realm Lords were also puzzled.
"Perhaps it is due to Karma!" Lord Longgu speculated.
Karma!
Many beings no longer have Karma with their original worlds after leaping beyond their scope, but as long as they are within the 570 Void Area, anyone who has not become a Controller will be affected by Karma.
Some Karma is minor and can be ignored, but some is significant. If a Peak Legendary Realm Lord wishes to strike for the Eternal Controller realm in the future, they must resolve these Karmic ties.
Karma is a factor that influences the fusion of time and space under the control of time and space. It can be disregarded, but it cannot be ignored.
If that were truly the case, it would be nearly impossible for them to kill Lord Guixing.
-
In the Boundless Sea, by the lakeside of a certain world.
An old man was fishing when suddenly, his hook caught someone from the void. This person was precisely Lord Guixing.
When he fished Lord Guixing over, all traces were erased by him. Ordinary Legendary Realm Lords simply could not track them, nor would they dare to pursue.
At this moment, Lord Guixing was somewhat dazed, having been suddenly fished away.
When he saw the old man, he did not recognize him; he was very unfamiliar.
However, he could guess who the old man before him was.
There were very few Legendary Realm Lords who could fish him over with a single hook, and only one Legendary Realm Lord liked using a fishhook: the Borderland Lord.
"Guixing thanks the Borderland Lord for the life-saving grace!" Lord Guixing thanked him.
"Do you know why I saved you?" the Borderland Lord asked with a smile.
"I do not know."
Lord Guixing truly did not know. He had no intersection with the Borderland Lord at all, and the Borderland Lord was truly low-profile, never participating in major events. He did not know why the Borderland Lord had saved him.
"Your master was my disciple. Although he did not become a Legendary Realm Lord, for his sake, I had to save you once. But I will only save you this once, do you understand?" the Borderland Lord said.
Lord Guixing was shocked.
He indeed had a master, a Half-step Legendary Realm expert who had gone to a Forbidden land to seek an opportunity for a breakthrough long ago, only to perish there.
He hadn't expected his master to have such a massive background, being a disciple of the Borderland Lord—and seemingly a true personal disciple at that, otherwise the Borderland Lord wouldn't have personally come to save his life.
"Thank you, Grandmaster!" Lord Guixing hurriedly tried to establish a connection.
"Do not call me Grandmaster. The relationship between us ends here. Your master was my disciple, but you are not my grand-disciple, understand?" The Borderland Lord knew Lord Guixing's thoughts. If he acknowledged it, Lord Guixing would use his name to stir up trouble in the Boundless Sea, and wouldn't he be annoyed to death?
"Yes!" Lord Guixing was somewhat disappointed.
Suddenly, the Borderland Lord vanished. He was almost invisible.
"What terrifying strength. The Borderland Lord has not only mastered time and space, but I fear he also has certain achievements in the fusion of space and time. Otherwise, he couldn't have fished me here so easily, nor could he have shielded my perception and left soundlessly."
Lord Guixing murmured.
It was a pity that he hadn't successfully established a relationship with such a Peak Legendary Realm Lord. Otherwise, he could almost walk sideways in the Boundless Sea in the future; who would dare provoke him?
Soon, he thought of his Guixing Tower.
The Guixing Tower was likely gone, as even Lord Fanxing had fallen in battle.
Thus, he contacted some Half-step Legendary Realm Lords.
Some Half-step Legendary Realm Lords had perished, while others were still alive. But even those who lived did not dare stay in the Boundless Sea anymore, fleeing directly to other Eternal Seas for fear of being hunted by the True Void Sacred Ground.
Some had even joined other forces seeking protection.
Soon, Lord Guixing learned the general situation.
The Guixing Tower was indeed gone, wiped out.
"What about Suixing?"
Lord Guixing hurriedly contacted Lord Suixing, but could not get through.
At this moment, a thought occurred to him.
Suixing had likely perished!
Suixing was the junior he favored most, the one he had poured all his energy into. He had even wanted to nurture Suixing into a Legendary Realm Lord and had bestowed a grain of Void Sea Gravel upon him, integrating it into an Eternal Supreme Treasure just to enhance his life-saving capabilities and combat power.
However, Suixing had still perished.
When Lord Fanxing fell, he only felt regret, but he wasn't that sorrowful. After all, Lord Fanxing could only be considered a partner and one of the founders of the Guixing Tower. But Suixing was his true blood descendant and disciple; the relationship was completely different.
"True Void Sacred Ground!!!!"
Lord Guixing gnashed his teeth, wishing he could go and fight the True Void Sacred Ground to the death, but he didn't dare.
"True Void Sacred Ground, who killed my Suixing?"
He could not retaliate against the True Void Sacred Ground, but retaliating against a single Half-step Legendary Realm expert of the True Void Sacred Ground was not difficult.
In his view, it must have been some Half-step Legendary Realm expert from the True Void Sacred Ground who killed Suixing.
This kind of thing could be investigated, but he had to wait until this matter blew over. Otherwise, if the Legendary Realm Lords of the True Void Sacred Ground were still watching him, it would be difficult for him to act.
True Void Sacred Ground.
Ye Tian returned. Once Lord Wushuang and the others also returned, Ye Tian went to find Lord Wushuang.
As for Ye Tian's copied body, it had naturally undergone self-Annihilation, fearing it would leave evidence that would allow other Legendary Realm Lords to peer into his existence.
This time, Ye Tian came to find Lord Wushuang only to retrieve that grain of Void Sea Gravel from that Eternal Supreme Treasure.
In Lord Wushuang's palace.
Ye Tian arrived.
"Lord Endless, we did not kill Lord Guixing, and we haven't found a grain of Void Sea Gravel yet," Lord Wushuang said.
Ye Tian spoke, "Lord Wushuang, I have found it!"
