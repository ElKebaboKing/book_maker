Chapter 1700: People from the Boundless Sea!

"I didn't expect to actually find another weak Eternal Sea!"
Qin Yu was quite excited internally.
The Boundless Sea was an Eternal Sea, and the Source Sea was also an Eternal Sea.
He knew of more than one Eternal Sea, but each was extremely powerful. It was his first time seeing an Eternal Sea like the Source Sea, where the strongest were only a few Legendary Realm Lords.
Such an Eternal Sea was bound to be very young. Although the resources born within might not be many, once such an Eternal Sea was controlled and his own strength developed, any precious resources born in the future, such as Eternal Supreme Treasures, could fall into his hands.
This was a great opportunity!
Moreover, there was a possibility of obtaining two Eternal Supreme Treasures this time.
As a member of the powerful Qin family in the Boundless Sea, they didn't have many Eternal Supreme Treasures. As a direct descendant young master of the Qin family, he had only been bestowed one Eternal Supreme Treasure. If he could obtain a few more, his strength would increase significantly.
"Luck is on my side this time. Fortunately, I captured the Dark Mist Lord in time; otherwise, such an opportunity wouldn't have fallen into my hands. Those Perfected Realm or even Domain Realm Legendary Lords would probably all want to come here," Qin Yu thought to himself.
From the Dark Mist Lord's mouth, he learned that the few Legendary Realm Lords in the Source Sea's Space-Time Territory were relatively weak, almost all being Ordinary Legendary Lords, with at most one Initial Realm Legendary Lord.
This time, he had brought eighteen Ordinary Legendary Lords, three Initial Realm Lords, plus himself—a top-tier Initial Realm Legendary Lord comparable to a Perfected Realm Legendary Lord. This was enough to occupy the Source Sea and make the many Eternal Realm beings submit.
Once he completely occupied the Source Sea, he would establish a Space-Time Staircase to connect to the Boundless Sea. By then, traveling between the Source Sea and the Boundless Sea would be very easy, and he could even dispatch experts from the Qin family at any time to guard this place, ensuring it wouldn't be taken by other forces.
Another period of time passed.
On this day.
The Dark Mist Lord led Qin Yu and these Legendary Lords to an area outside the Space-Time Territory, where they could sense various Space-Time Territories.
"It really is another Eternal Sea, but its structure is somewhat different from the Boundless Sea. The resources of the Source Sea are mainly concentrated in the Space-Time Territories, nourished by the Source Sea's origin energy, whereas our Boundless Sea birthed countless Boundless Islands that float upon it."
Qin Yu murmured.
Whoosh! Whoosh! Whoosh!!!!
These Legendary Realm Lords instantly crossed this void region and descended upon the Space-Time Territory.
At this moment, Qin Yu commanded, "Dark Mist Lord, take me to your Lord Alliance headquarters."
The Dark Mist Lord whispered, "Young Master Qin Yu, shouldn't we go kill Ye Tian? That man has five clones; if he escapes, it might be bad."
"Do you intend to disobey my command? Besides, a mere Immortal Realm Lord is not worth mentioning. Once I've handled my affairs, I'll kill him for you in passing. If you say one more word of nonsense, you know the consequences," Qin Yu said impatiently.
The Dark Mist Lord's expression changed drastically, and he immediately dared not speak further.
Although Qin Yu had broken his promise, his strength was too weak to resist Qin Yu. Even if Qin Yu really didn't help him, he was helpless.
In reality, Qin Yu didn't care about the so-called promise at all; after all, the Dark Mist Lord had little value to him now.
However, Ye Tian definitely had to be killed, as it was highly likely he possessed an Eternal Supreme Treasure capable of boosting strength; otherwise, he wouldn't have been able to defeat the Legendary Realm Dark Mist Lord while only at the Immortal Realm tier.
But first, he wanted to control the Lord Alliance before dealing with Ye Tian.
Additionally.
As for whose hands the Eternal Supreme Treasure born in the Source Sea had fallen into, once he controlled the Lord Alliance here, he could find out.
Right now, controlling the Lord Alliance was the most urgent matter.
Whoosh! Whoosh! Whoosh!!!
Qin Yu led the many Legendary Realm Lords and traveled to the headquarters of the Lord Alliance.
Boom!!!!
The auras of the Legendary Realm Lords spread out, instantly causing the many Eternal Realm beings in the Lord Alliance to tremble in fear.
At this time, Shengfeng Lord, Binghan Lord, Taihuang Lord, Jiguang Lord, and Liuyang Lord appeared one after another.
"So many Legendary Lords!!!"
Shengfeng Lord's expression changed drastically, looking completely incredulous.
When had the Space-Time Territory ever had so many Legendary Lords? Moreover, they were all unfamiliar faces, definitely not from the Space-Time Territory.
Furthermore, Shengfeng Lord saw a familiar face among these Legendary Lords—the Dark Mist Lord.
It could almost be guessed that these Legendary Lords were brought by the Dark Mist Lord and likely came from a place similar to the Source Sea, such as the Boundless Sea.
After all, there were several ruins from the Boundless Sea in the Space-Time Territory, so these people were very likely from the Boundless Sea.
"You must be the five Legendary Lords of the Source Sea Space-Time Territory Lord Alliance. From today onwards, your Space-Time Territory Lord Alliance will serve me. I come from the Qin family of the Boundless Sea. My name is Qin Yu, also known as the True Yu Lord, but I prefer people to call me Young Master Qin Yu."
Qin Yu spoke.
"What? Serve you?"
The faces of the five Legendary Lords immediately showed signs of anger, but due to the strength of Qin Yu and his group, they did not act rashly.
At this moment, Binghan Lord secretly notified Ye Tian.
The Space-Time Territory side was not without the power to fight. After all, these Legendary Lords who had arrived from the Boundless Sea had not brought their main bodies and clones; they must have only brought one body.
And with the five Great Lords on the Space-Time Territory side plus Ye Tian's duplicated bodies, they were not inferior to Qin Yu's side in terms of numbers.
As long as Ye Tian could defeat Qin Yu, they would have a say.
In short, serving Qin Yu was impossible.
As Legendary Realm Lords, Binghan Lord and the others did not want to serve anyone else.
-
Blood Bone Territory.
Binghan Lord's clone descended here, and Ye Tian sensed it immediately.
"Binghan Lord, you look a bit flustered. Has something happened?" Ye Tian asked.
Binghan Lord hurriedly said, "Something big has happened. A group of Legendary Lords has come from the Boundless Sea, including the Dark Mist Lord. We suspect the Dark Mist Lord found the Boundless Sea. We searched for countless years and couldn't find it, but the Dark Mist Lord actually did. Now, these Legendary Lords from the Boundless Sea were likely brought by the Dark Mist Lord. The leading Legendary Lord is named Qin Yu, with the title True Yu Lord. His strength is likely very great; we are no match for him."
"Stall for some time; I'll be there immediately!"
Ye Tian said.
This matter was related to him; he couldn't just ignore it.
"I'm afraid I'll have to unleash my true strength this time!"
Ye Tian thought to himself.
