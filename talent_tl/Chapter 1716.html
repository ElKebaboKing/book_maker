Chapter 1715: Legendary Realm Lord—Lord Longgu!

After tens of thousands of Eternal Years of bitter cultivation, aided by Eternal Supreme Treasures and the Gray Mist Bridge, Ye Tian's progress was immense.
On this day.
Ye Tian's Myriad Life Eternal Dao achieved a substantial breakthrough, directly stepping into the Major stage. Even his Hunyuan Eternal Dao had been cultivated to the peak of the Minor stage.
Not only that, Ye Tian had another gain: he had obtained some insights into the Gray Mist Eternal Dao. However, he was still some distance away from completely comprehending it, as the Gray Mist Eternal Dao was indeed too powerful. Consequently, the difficulty of reaching the Entry stage of the Gray Mist Eternal Dao was also incredibly high.
Source Sea, Desolate Area.
The fluctuations caused by the Gray Mist Stone Statue grew larger and larger, spreading across a vast area. At this point, any Eternal Realm expert who came to the Desolate Area would sense the situation here.
Thus, before long, the phenomenon in the Desolate Area was discovered.
At this time, Ye Tian reported the situation of the Gray Mist Stone Statue to the Zhenwu Sacred Ground. Otherwise, once the Source Sea attracted the attention of other powers, Ye Tian would have no way to withstand those powerful organizations. Therefore, he had to involve the Zhenwu Sacred Ground.
The person Ye Tian reported to was Lord Wushuang.
……
"Endless Lord, are you saying that a Gray Mist Stone Statue has appeared in the Source Sea?"
Lord Wushuang asked in great shock.
"Yes!"
Ye Tian nodded.
Lord Wushuang looked deeply at Ye Tian and said, "Previously, you asked me about the attacks of an Eternal Controller. Could it be that you were asking about that mark on the Gray Mist Stone Statue?"
"It was indeed that mark!"
Ye Tian admitted.
Lord Wushuang did not blame Ye Tian. After all, hiding information was normal. Now that it could no longer be hidden, he naturally chose to report it to the Zhenwu Sacred Ground.
At this time, Lord Wushuang said, "I have heard of the Gray Mist Stone Statue, but I have never seen it. The Gray Mist Stone Statue is indeed an incredibly astonishing opportunity, but it is also very eerie. It has many trials, and one can obtain some benefits after passing each trial. However, no one knows what the final trial is. Those Legendary Realm Lords have also entered the Gray Mist Stone Statue; perhaps they know more secrets. Additionally, the Gray Mist Stone Statue only allows 100 lifeforms to enter at a time. Once the number of lifeforms exceeds one hundred, others can no longer enter. When the 100 lifeforms emerge from the Gray Mist Stone Statue, it will leave that area and head to another."
"So that's how it is!"
Ye Tian understood.
No wonder the Gray Mist Stone Statue had ended up in the Source Sea.
"Lord Wushuang, I need two slots. The other slots will be given to the Zhenwu Sacred Ground,"
Ye Tian said.
"No problem!" Lord Wushuang nodded. "I will report this to the Legendary Realm Lords of the Zhenwu Sacred Ground. Then, the Zhenwu Sacred Ground will blockade the area around the Gray Mist Stone Statue. By the time other powers find out, they won't be able to enter anymore."
Soon, Lord Wushuang explained the situation of the Gray Mist Stone Statue.
Afterward, Ye Tian met a Legendary Realm Lord from the Zhenwu Sacred Ground.
Boom!!!!!
This Legendary Realm Lord did not use the Space-Time Ladder. He directly shattered space-time, crossing the endless void area to descend upon the Source Sea.
He was the Legendary Realm Lord of the Zhenwu Sacred Ground—Lord Longgu.
Lord Longgu was the ancestor of the Original Ancestral Dragon race of the Boundless Sea. However, there weren't many Original Ancestral Dragons in the Boundless Sea. Over the long course of time, many Original Ancestral Dragons had fallen one by one. Coupled with the fact that no new Original Ancestral Dragons were being born, there were now fewer than twenty Original Ancestral Dragons left. However, they had all reached the Legendary Lord tier, and several had even reached the Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord stage.
Ye Tian had heard of Lord Longgu, but this was his first time meeting him.
"Greetings, Lord Longgu!"
Ye Tian said with considerable respect.
"Mm, you are quite good!"
Lord Longgu looked at Ye Tian and nodded slightly before turning his gaze toward the Gray Mist Stone Statue.
He saw the Gray Mist Stone Statue with a single glance and then took out a scale.
This scale contained his power.
In the next moment, the scale was sacrificed, flying toward the area where the Gray Mist Stone Statue was located.
Buzz!!!!
Endless space-time was suppressed and sealed; no one could forcibly tear through space-time to reach the location of the Gray Mist Stone Statue.
Seeing this scene, Ye Tian truly recognized the strength of a Legendary Realm Legendary Lord. A single scale alone could easily suppress him.
Whoosh!
A portal was born, leading to the Gray Mist Stone Statue.
Soon.
One Legendary Lord after another appeared. The Half-step Legendary Realm Lords, Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords, and several Domain Realm Legendary Lords of the Zhenwu Sacred Ground descended. They all intended to enter the Gray Mist Stone Statue.
At this time, Ye Tian said, "Lord Longgu, these two Legendary Lords from our Source Sea also need to enter the Gray Mist Stone Statue. They were the ones who discovered it, so I promised them slots."
Lord Longgu looked toward Lord Binghan and Lord Shengfeng. That single glance made Lord Binghan and Lord Shengfeng tremble all over, not daring to move an inch.
"Their strength is too weak and their potential is average. They won't find much opportunity inside the Gray Mist Stone Statue. But since a promise was made, let them enter as well!"
Subsequently.
Everyone entered one by one, but Lord Longgu naturally did not enter. Perhaps he had entered it before.
Suddenly.
Lord Longgu's expression changed, and he said, "Everyone, hurry inside! Legendary Realm Lords are coming!"
Whoosh whoosh whoosh!!!
The Legendary Lords of the Zhenwu Sacred Ground quickly rushed toward the crack in the Gray Mist Stone Statue and charged inside.
Lord Binghan and Lord Shengfeng were too weak. As soon as they entered, they were somewhat unable to withstand the impact of the entrance's power and were quickly injured.
At the critical moment, Ye Tian protected them. This was the only help he could provide.
"Thank you, Endless Lord!"
Lord Binghan and Lord Shengfeng said gratefully.
If not for Ye Tian, they feared they wouldn't have been able to get in and would have wasted this opportunity.
It was also at this time that Lord Longgu clashed with several Legendary Realm Lords.
Rumble!!!!
The endless space-time of the Source Sea was shaking, and large tracts of territory were collapsing.
Fortunately, they only exchanged a few blows before stopping completely. Otherwise, the damage caused to the Source Sea would have been too great.
"Lord Longgu, your Zhenwu Sacred Ground is going too far! You actually sent in a hundred Legendary Lords all at once, hogging this entire opportunity!" a Legendary Realm Lord said angrily.
Lord Longgu sneered and said, "At most, you can wait until they come out, find the Gray Mist Stone Statue again, and then send your Legendary Lords in."
"If we could stop or catch up to the Gray Mist Stone Statue, would we still be here arguing with you?" another Legendary Realm Lord shouted in anger.
At this time, seeing that all 100 Legendary Lords had entered, Lord Longgu stepped aside and said, "The Gray Mist Stone Statue is right there. If you can enter, then go ahead. I won't stop you!"
"You're ruthless!"
The Legendary Realm Lords retreated. Since they were a step too late, there was nothing they could do. Who told the Zhenwu Sacred Ground to act so quickly? They had only just received a bit of news and immediately came to confirm it, only to end up being a step late.
Soon, all of these Legendary Realm Lords departed.
