Chapter 1710: Revealing Identity, the Qin Family Retreats!

"Finally arrived!"
Within the Qin family's team, a burly man spoke with excitement.
He was one of the three Domain Realm Legendary Lords in the Qin family's team—Qin Kuangfeng, known as the Violent Lord. His strength was terrifying, and he was considered a top-tier existence even among Domain Realm Legendary Lords.
At this moment, the Qin family members had already spotted the Source Sea Spacetime Territory.
This was a new Spacetime Territory. If the Qin family could occupy this place, they would definitely be able to rise in the future.
"After entering the Source Sea, immediately calculate the spacetime coordinates, then construct a Spacetime Ladder to thoroughly establish a connection with our Qin family," Qin Kuangfeng said.
"Naturally!" Another Domain Realm Legendary Lord, Qin Ming, nodded.

The Qin family's Legendary Realm Lords traversed toward the Spacetime Territory, arriving in an instant.

Just then, the Guarding Legion stationed at the Spacetime Territory discovered the Qin family's movements.
Ye Tian knew the Qin family would definitely come here, so he had the Guarding Legion keep watch over the Spacetime Territory and patrol continuously. Now, they had indeed discovered the Qin family's Legendary Lords.
Xiu xiu xiu!!!!
One by one, the Legendary Lords of the Guarding Legion appeared, blocking the Qin family's Legendary Lords.
"So many Legendary Lords?"
Qin Kuangfeng and the other Qin family Legendary Lords revealed looks of doubt.
Although these Legendary Lords were very weak, with the strongest being only Initial Realm Legendary Lords.
But the key was that according to Qin Yu's intelligence, there weren't many Legendary Lords in the Source Sea, only a few.
Furthermore.
Qin Kuangfeng sensed the aura and rules belonging to the Boundless Sea from these Legendary Lords.
Clearly, these Legendary Lords were not from the Source Sea, but from the Boundless Sea.
"A force from the Boundless Sea!"
Qin Kuangfeng thought to himself.
"Which force do you belong to?" Qin Kuangfeng released his aura as a top-tier Domain Realm Legendary Lord, overwhelming the scene. The endless territories trembled under his pressure.
The Legendary Lords within the Guarding Legion felt the aura of a Domain Realm Legendary Lord from Qin Kuangfeng, and they all trembled.
This was a Domain Realm Legendary Lord, an existence far beyond them. Even if they joined forces, they would be no match for a single Domain Realm Legendary Lord.
However, they were not very panicked because they had a backer behind them.
"We belong to the Guarding Legion under the command of the Guarding Commander of the True Void Sacred Land, the Endless Lord!"
One of the Guarding Envoys declared their origin.
True Void Sacred Land!
When the many Legendary Lords of the Qin family heard this, their expressions changed drastically.
Compared to the True Void Sacred Land, the Qin family was undoubtedly like an ant.
However, they did not retreat immediately. After all, just because someone claimed to be from the True Void Sacred Land, did that mean they actually were?
"What proof do you have?"
Qin Kuangfeng said.
Just then.
A voice drifted over.
"You must be from the Qin family of the Boundless Sea, right?"
The void area rippled, and a young man stepped out from the nothingness. It was Ye Tian.
"It's you!"
The Qin family's Legendary Realm Lords recognized Ye Tian. After all, the images provided by Qin Yu included Ye Tian.
The Source Sea's Initial Realm Legendary Lord—the Endless Lord.
Ye Tian spoke: "I am a Heaven-tier Holy Son-level member of the True Void Sacred Land, the Guarding Commander—Endless Lord."
After saying this, a seal appeared behind Ye Tian.
The radiance of this seal illuminated billions of territories, and at the same time, a projection of the True Void Sacred Land appeared upon the seal.
"The Guarding Commander seal of the True Void Sacred Land!"
The Qin family members all recognized it; this thing could not be faked.
Clearly, Ye Tian had joined the True Void Sacred Land and had even become a Heaven-tier Holy Son-level member, obtaining the position of Guarding Commander.
"Damn it, this Endless Commander actually moved so fast. He joined the True Void Sacred Land before we even arrived!"
Qin Kuangfeng cursed inwardly.
If Ye Tian hadn't joined the True Void Sacred Land, he could have killed Ye Tian at any time.
But now, Ye Tian was a Guarding Commander of the True Void Sacred Land, one of the high-level members.
They had no way to kill Ye Tian silently. Perhaps Ye Tian's clone was still in the True Void Sacred Land.
Once the Qin family dared to act against a Guarding Commander of the True Void Sacred Land, it would be equivalent to declaring war on the True Void Sacred Land.
Facing the True Void Sacred Land, the Qin family was like an ant and could not shake it at all.
If war were truly declared, the Qin family would perish.
"Congratulations to the Endless Lord for becoming a Guarding Commander of the True Void Sacred Land. We only came this time to retrieve our Qin family's Eternal Supreme Treasure, the Fire Domain Sword," Qin Kuangfeng said coolly, suppressing the anger in his heart.
"Fire Domain Sword? What is that? I don't have it here. This is now a guarding area of the True Void Sacred Land and also my territory. Please leave!" Ye Tian drove them away.
"You!"
Qin Kuangfeng was enraged and wished he could immediately strike to suppress Ye Tian, but he was held back by the other two Domain Realm Legendary Lords.
"Let's go first!"
Qin Ming said.
The Qin family members did not have a conflict with Ye Tian and turned to leave.
At the same time.
The Qin family also learned of the situation in the Source Sea and had no solution.
Upon learning of the situation, Ancestor Qin Wuyuan of the Qin family immediately issued an order to leak the location of the Source Sea. Although they could not occupy an Eternal Sea like the Source Sea, they wouldn't let the True Void Sacred Land develop the Source Sea in secret either. Causing some trouble for the True Void Sacred Land and Ye Tian was not a bad idea.
Soon.

True Void Sacred Land.
