Chapter 1744: Zhenwu Sacred Land's Action, Joining with a Hidden Identity

The Hundred Illusion Pearl only treated the symptoms but not the root cause; it could not hide the fact that Ye Tian controlled time.
However, at the very least, the shock and amazement from the outside world would be much less. If the outside world truly knew that Ye Tian controlled time as a Half-step Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord, organizations like Zhenwu Sacred Land would truly destroy Ye Tian at any cost.
At this time, Wushuang Lord spoke again, "Endless Lord, in the coming years, Zhenwu Sacred Land will gradually release signals proving that you have already stepped into the Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord rank and obtained some treasures that significantly enhanced your time control. We will build you up as a person of great providence with extremely good luck. The theory of providence is real; almost all Legendary Lords are Children of Providence. However, once one reaches the Legendary Lord realm, providence rarely has an effect. But if you still demonstrate extremely strong providence and truly good luck—for instance, the news of you gaining benefits in the Yanglie Lord's Tomb—the outside world will believe it if it's spread."
"Over time, your identity as a Child of Providence will be solidified. With super good luck and obtaining various opportunities and treasures, if you expose the secret of controlling time in the future, the outside world will only think you relied on luck to obtain many treasures to control time, rather than it being your innate talent. This way, your threat to other forces will be much less."
Wushuang Lord explained.
Ye Tian was shocked; Wushuang Lord had considered so much to come up with this.
However, for him, this was a good thing. Since Zhenwu Sacred Land would handle the operations, he didn't need to worry about anything.
Suddenly, Wushuang Lord added, "Endless Lord, Zhenwu Sacred Land was originally going to have a major action next and would even allow you to participate. But this time, I suggest you do not participate. If Void Sea Gravel is obtained during the action, I will take charge and grant you one grain."
"Wushuang Lord, what action? If I can participate without exposing my identity, would I be allowed to join?" Ye Tian asked.
"Not exposing your identity?" Wushuang Lord pondered for a moment and said, "If you do not expose your identity, I will allow you to participate in the action."
Ye Tian smiled; then there was no problem.
Not exposing his identity was simply too easy.
He could completely duplicate someone else's body and then use the Hundred Illusion Pearl to change his cultivation realm and even his aura. This way, even if a Legendary Realm Lord peered through time and space, they would definitely not see him and would think another Legendary Lord was participating.
If he didn't participate and still received a grain of Void Sea Gravel, he would feel uneasy. Moreover, what if Zhenwu Sacred Land failed to obtain any Void Sea Gravel?
Therefore, he had to participate personally to increase the hope of obtaining Void Sea Gravel. After all, others in Zhenwu Sacred Land might not put in their full effort to get it. Perhaps a certain Legendary Lord or Half-step Legendary Realm Lord might obtain Void Sea Gravel but not mention it, unwilling to hand it over. Who could say anything then?
Sometimes, one must rely on oneself. Thus, he would not rely entirely on Zhenwu Sacred Land.
Afterward, Wushuang Lord said, "Endless Lord, regarding the Space-Time Ancient Leaf, Longgu Lord has provided some information you might want to hear."
Space-Time Ancient Leaf!
Ye Tian's expression shook as he listened intently.
"In the Void Region, there is a Peak Legendary Realm Lord known as the White-Bearded Old Man. This person is extremely ancient and has even discussed the Dao with more than one Eternal Controller. He has a Space-Time Ancient Leaf in his possession, but getting him to give it to you will likely be very difficult."
Wushuang Lord revealed.
"I understand. Thank you, Wushuang Lord," Ye Tian thanked her.
Afterward, Wushuang Lord left. As for the action, she still hadn't specified it yet; he needed to prove he could hide his identity before she would agree.
Time passed slowly, and in the blink of an eye, another several hundred thousand eternal years had gone by.
On this day, Wushuang Lord arrived at Ye Tian's residence once again.
"Endless Lord, Zhenwu Sacred Land's action is about to begin. If you have a way to prove you can hide your identity—for example, by successfully hiding it from me—I will take you there," Wushuang Lord said.
"Alright!"
With a wave of Ye Tian's hand, a duplicate body appeared.
This duplicate body was not his own, but the body of another Half-step Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord.
This was a duplicate body of a fallen Legendary Lord he had found while wandering through various Eternal Seas over the years. That Legendary Lord was originally only a Domain Realm Legendary Lord, but he used this duplicate body to cultivate slightly, reconstructed the physique, and then directly promoted it to the Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord rank. It was a genuine Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord...
As long as he annihilated this duplicate body when the time came, it would not affect his other main bodies.
Consequently, the strength displayed by this duplicate body was even more powerful than his main body.
"This is..."
Wushuang Lord looked at Ye Tian's duplicate body and frowned.
"Endless Lord, which force does this Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord belong to? Why have you brought him into Zhenwu Sacred Land?"
Wushuang Lord asked.
"This is my avatar!"
Ye Tian said.
"An avatar? Impossible. The soul aura is different, everything is different. How could it be your avatar?"
Wushuang Lord did not believe him.
At this moment, Ye Tian used this duplicate body to demonstrate the power of time control.
Buzz!
The surrounding time fell completely under Ye Tian's control.
Seeing this scene, Wushuang Lord was shocked.
Time control!
If one were to say Ye Tian had found another Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord who also controlled time, that was unlikely. How could there be such a coincidence where two such monstrous Legendary Lords appeared at the same time?
Soon, Wushuang Lord truly believed that Ye Tian's Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord body was an avatar.
"Impressive. Even I cannot distinguish his identity; I would only take him for someone else. Are the appearance and aura of this avatar someone else's?" Wushuang Lord asked.
"Yes. That Legendary Lord has already fallen. I paid a great price to completely imitate him. Even if he is exposed, no one will suspect my identity," Ye Tian said.
"Then there is no problem. Have this avatar of yours follow me."
Wushuang Lord led Ye Tian's avatar to a void.
In this void, various Legendary Realm Lords of Zhenwu Sacred Land, many Half-step Legendary Realm Lords, and some Transcendence Realm Lords were gathered.
When Ye Tian arrived, it caused doubt among many of Zhenwu Sacred Land's lords.
Ultimately, under Wushuang Lord's guarantee, those Legendary Realm Lords acquiesced. The other Legendary Lords did not know Ye Tian's identity, and Wushuang Lord had no need to inform them.
At this time, Longgu Lord, acting as the representative, revealed the details of this action.
