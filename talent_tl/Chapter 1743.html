"Ye Tian, I hope you can control time," Lord Wushuang said after throwing Ye Tian into the River of Time.

However, she did not have much confidence in whether Ye Tian could control time during this cultivation session. Even if he was on the verge of controlling time, he wouldn't be able to stay in the River of Time for long. After all, Ye Tian's cultivation was still too low.

She also didn't have the time to keep throwing Ye Tian into the River of Time over and over again; she didn't have that kind of leisure.

Everything depended on Ye Tian himself. She would not do such a thing again.

After all, she didn't owe Ye Tian anything.

......

Within the River of Time.

Ye Tian entered this place, and the surging power of time rushed toward him. Ye Tian exerted his full effort to control the time here to avoid being harmed by it.

However, his body still couldn't stop collapsing.

Of course, the speed of the collapse wasn't very fast.

At this rate, Ye Tian could at least hold on for a while.

If it were any other Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord, even the most top-tier Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord, they would turn into nothingness the moment they entered the River of Time.

Even some Half-step Legendary Realm Lords, if they didn't have much control over time, wouldn't be able to last for more than a brief moment.

"Let the comprehension begin!"

Ye Tian stood in the River of Time, quietly comprehending the mysteries of time.

He ignored the collapse of his body, solely focused on comprehending for a bit longer. After comprehending for a period, he immediately began to duplicate his body, and a brand-new body in peak condition was duplicated.

Then, the body that was about to collapse was annihilated by the River of Time, while the new duplicated body continued to comprehend there.

In this way, Ye Tian relied on this method to comprehend in the River of Time time and time again.

As time passed, Lord Wushuang thought that Ye Tian's clone had been annihilated in the River of Time, but the reality was different from her guess. Ye Tian was still comprehending the mysteries of time.

One Eternal Year later.

For a Legendary Lord, a mere Eternal Year was nothing, but for Ye Tian, his progress during this Eternal Year was immense.

On this day.

The power of the River of Time flowed past Ye Tian's side without harming him in the slightest.

At this moment, he had already controlled time and could roam freely within the River of Time.

Of course.

His strength wasn't great enough to tear through the barriers of the River of Time, so he couldn't use the River of Time to travel like a Legendary Realm Lord.

But having controlled time, Ye Tian's strength experienced a rapid skyrocket.

Now, although he had not yet stepped into the Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord stage, he could instantly kill any Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord. He could even fight against some Half-step Legendary Realm Lords without being at a disadvantage.

This was the power of controlling time!

After controlling time, Ye Tian's duplicated body emerged from the River of Time.

Entering the River of Time was difficult, but coming out was quite easy.

When Ye Tian emerged from the River of Time, Lord Wushuang sensed it. Her gaze immediately pierced through space and saw Ye Tian.

"The aura of controlling time!" Lord Wushuang said in surprise.

This was because Ye Tian had just gained control over time, and the aura had not yet completely dissipated, which was why she could see it.

Once Ye Tian became completely familiar with the ability to control time, he would be able to retract and release it at will. Even a Peak Legendary Realm Lord would find it difficult to see through Ye Tian's realm.

"How is this possible? When he entered the River of Time before, he couldn't stay for long at all. Even if he could stay that long, it's impossible to completely control time in just one short Eternal Year!" Lord Wushuang thought to herself in shock.

At this moment, she truly realized what a monster was!

She wasn't a monster, and Lord Baiheng wasn't either, but someone like Ye Tian was a true monster.

From now on, Ye Tian would surely be able to reach the Legendary Realm Lord stage and could even become a Peak Legendary Realm Lord.

If Ye Tian could condense a Supreme Legendary Body, then he would be nearly invincible at the Legendary Realm Lord level in the future.

*Whoosh!*

Lord Wushuang arrived at Ye Tian's location.

"Greetings, Lord Wushuang!" Ye Tian said respectfully.

"Lord Endless, have you controlled time?" Lord Wushuang asked.

"Yes!" Ye Tian admitted.

At this, Lord Wushuang said solemnly, "Ye Tian, you controlled time while only at the Half-step Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord stage. If the outside world finds out, I'm afraid you will be targeted. After all, no one has ever controlled time at the Half-step Legendary Realm Lord level. Therefore, your original body must stay in the True Void Sacred Ground to ensure your safety. In the future, when you go out, use your clones; your original body cannot leave. Additionally, I will apply to the higher-ups of the True Void Sacred Ground to try and get you an Eternal Treasure that can conceal your cultivation realm."

"Conceal my cultivation realm?" Ye Tian was puzzled. Was there such an Eternal Treasure?

After all, Legendary Lords and Legendary Realm Lords could almost see through everything; any secret technique or treasure would find it difficult to hide one's realm.

"There are!" Lord Wushuang said. "However, they are almost useless because once you fight, your strength will be exposed. But you are different. You have controlled time. Even if others learn of your combat power, they will only think you have reached the extreme of the Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord stage. Even if a Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord at the extreme controls time, it won't cause such a massive sensation. After all, the next step for a Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord is the Half-step Legendary Realm Lord stage, and they could break through at any time, so the impact wouldn't be as great."

"Thank you, Lord Wushuang," Ye Tian thanked her.

Afterward, Lord Wushuang went to notify the other Legendary Realm Lords.

The True Void Sacred Ground was about to hold a meeting.

On this day.

Lord Wushuang, Lord Longgu, Lord Baiheng, Lord Zhenguang, Lord Huanghe, Lord Tengjiu, Lord Hemu...

One Legendary Realm Lord after another arrived, but none of them knew the purpose of this meeting.

"Lord Wushuang, why have you called us here?" Lord Longgu asked.

Lord Wushuang spoke, "Our True Void Sacred Ground has another person who has controlled time."

"Another who has controlled time? Who is it? Lord Purgatory? No, he's still some distance away from controlling time. Lord Chenfeng? That's not right either; although he's very low-key, he definitely hasn't reached the level of controlling time. But besides those two, there are no others among the Half-step Legendary Realm Lords who are more outstanding!" Lord Longgu said in confusion.

"It's not a Half-step Legendary Realm!" Lord Wushuang shook her head.

"What? Not a Half-step Legendary Realm? Could it be a Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord?!"

The many Legendary Realm Lords were all shocked.
