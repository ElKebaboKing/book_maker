Chapter 1711: Guixu Treasure Land!

After Lord Wushuang departed, Ye Tian went to the Heritage Treasury of the Zhenwu Sacred Ground to apply for the exchange of the secret technique, the Eternal Heart.
As a Heaven-tier Sacred Son member, Ye Tian naturally had the qualifications to exchange for the Eternal Heart secret technique, and he only needed to pay a small amount of resources.
Subsequently, Ye Tian received the inheritance of this secret technique in the Heritage Treasury, but he was unable to teach it to others; this was an inheritance restriction.
Before receiving the inheritance, Ye Tian did not know what the so-called Eternal Heart was, but after receiving it, he understood.
The Eternal Heart was a special crystal formed by condensing and compressing Eternal Primordial Force. It was similar to a heart and could boost one's own strength.
More importantly, the Eternal Heart could increase one's Eternal Primordial Force.
After the fusion of the body, will, and Innate Eternal Rules reached ten-tenths, one could charge toward the Perfected Stage. At this time, the body was equivalent to possessing ten-tenths Eternal Primordial Force.
At this step, it was almost impossible to increase Eternal Primordial Force further because the entire body was already filled with it. How could it be increased?
However, the Eternal Heart could compress and condense excess Eternal Primordial Force into a heart, ensuring that the Eternal Primordial Force within the body became even greater.
Additionally, Initial Stage Legendary Lords could also practice the Eternal Heart. Although it was very difficult to master, once mastered, one could use the Eternal Primordial Force contained within the Eternal Heart to temper the entire body. By breaking through that final sliver of shackles, one could reach the realm of a Perfected Stage Legendary Lord.
This was also why Lord Wushuang suggested that Ye Tian practice the Eternal Heart secret technique.
The Eternal Heart was a secret technique created by Legendary Stage Lords over a long period of time. It was extremely important to the Zhenwu Sacred Ground, as it had cultivated a large number of Perfected Stage Legendary Lords for them.
After Ye Tian obtained the Eternal Heart, he tried to practice it.
"Eh, it doesn't seem to be very difficult!"
Ye Tian murmured.
He speculated whether it was because his talent was too incredible, making it easier to practice the Eternal Heart, as the Eternal Heart tested one's understanding of the body, will, and Innate Eternal Rules, and these three aspects were related to talent.
His talent far exceeded that of any Legendary Lord, and even the talent of Legendary Stage Lords was inferior to his.
With such talent, he naturally possessed a huge advantage in practicing the Eternal Heart.
As time flowed in this manner, ten Eternal Years passed by unknowingly.
On this day.
Ye Tian's Eternal Heart was about to be mastered.
Inside his body.
Boom!!!!
Countless amounts of Eternal Primordial Force condensed within his Will Source Sea, gradually constructing the shape of a heart.
After a long time, this heart completely condensed into form, radiating a powerful aura.
"I've mastered it!"
Ye Tian could now sense the Eternal Heart's tempering of his body and the boost to his strength.
He could use the Eternal Heart to extract the Eternal Primordial Force within his body and then continue to fuse his body, will, and Innate Eternal Rules to form new Eternal Primordial Force once again.
In this way, the Eternal Primordial Force on his body would become more and more abundant, and it would even assist in the fusion of his body, will, and Innate Eternal Rules, accelerating his cultivation.
With the coordination of the Eternal Heart, he could charge toward the Peak of the Initial Stage Legendary Lord realm even faster.
In the following years, Ye Tian worked hard to charge toward the Peak of the Initial Stage Legendary Lord realm.
After a long period of time, some new faces appeared in the Source Sea. They were all lords above the Immortal Realm, and there were even quite a few Legendary Lords among them.
Ye Tian did not stop them, allowing them to appear and disappear within the Source Sea.
At this time, the Source Sea did not have many resources, and these powerhouses would not be greedy for such a small amount of things. Consequently, the number of powerhouses coming to the Source Sea gradually decreased, but there were still some Eternal Realm Lords who remained in the Source Sea and the Space-Time Territory.
At the very least, there were fewer disputes here, and there was hope of obtaining more resources in the future. If they were lucky enough to obtain an Eternal Supreme Treasure, they would make a huge profit.
Regarding these Eternal Realm Lords who came from the Boundless Sea or other Eternal Seas, as long as they did not endanger the human race, the Guard Army, or the sovereignty of the Source Sea, he practically ignored them.
In the blink of an eye, tens of thousands of Eternal Years passed.
On this day.
Ye Tian's cultivation reached the Peak of the Initial Stage Legendary Lord realm.
At this point, he was only a tiny bit away from perfectly fusing the ten-tenths of his body with his will and Innate Eternal Rules to step into the realm of a Perfected Stage Legendary Lord.
However, this tiny bit was very difficult to cross. Many Initial Stage Legendary Lords never broke through this shackle in their entire lives.
Ye Tian tried to use the Eternal Heart to break through this shackle, but he found that he was still a bit short.
"It seems I must go to the Guixu Treasure Land of the Zhenwu Sacred Ground!"
The Guixu Treasure Land was a very important treasure land of the Zhenwu Sacred Ground, and not just any Sacred Ground member could enter. However, Ye Tian could enter the Guixu Treasure Land for free.
Strictly speaking, the Guixu Treasure Land was not within the Zhenwu Sacred Ground, but the Zhenwu Sacred Ground had a space-time channel leading to it.
Zhenwu Sacred Ground, Guixu Gate.
Ye Tian arrived here and then stepped into the Guixu Gate.
The space-time channel behind the Guixu Gate was somewhat similar to the Space-Time Staircase, but also different. This was a flat space-time channel, and it did not lead to other Eternal Seas.
However, such a space-time channel was extremely secretive. Even Legendary Stage Lords would find it difficult to peer into this space-time channel from the outside.
Soon, Ye Tian passed through the space-time channel behind the Guixu Gate and arrived at the true Guixu Treasure Land.
……
The entire Guixu was not very large. In the center was a spring, and around it were numerous energy channels, with billions of paths connecting to the spring.
If one looked down from a high vantage point, they would discover that billions of paths converged from the void and poured into this spring. Or rather, billions of paths merged into one, returning to a single point. Moreover, after these paths entered the spring, they all transformed into the same kind of source energy.
This source energy gathered the power of billions of paths, yet it did not contain the power of any single path. It was as if it were nothingness, yet also the source of all power.
"So this is what Guixu is like!"
Ye Tian murmured.
He saw Legendary Lords sitting on those paths, and he even saw a familiar figure—Lord Yinyue. She had also come here, seemingly also charging toward the realm of a Perfected Stage Legendary Lord.
With these billions of paths merging into one, those in the Legendary Eternal Realm could find a path that was compatible with them, meditate there, and comprehend the profundity of energy unification.
The body was energy, the will was energy, and the Innate Eternal Rules could also be considered a type of energy.
There were billions of types of energy, and here, billions of energies merged into one. If one could comprehend even a sliver of the profundity, it would assist in a breakthrough.
This was also why many Legendary Lords at the Peak of the Initial Stage came here to meditate and cultivate.
Ye Tian found a Hunyuan Path that was somewhat compatible with him, sat upon it, and began to carefully comprehend the profundity of energy unification. At the same time, he coordinated with the Eternal Heart to search for an opportunity for a breakthrough.
Breaking through all at once was unlikely.
Therefore, Ye Tian prepared to meditate here for many years, striving to break through to the realm of a Perfected Stage Legendary Lord as soon as possible.
