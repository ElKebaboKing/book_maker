Chapter 1730: Guixing Tower, Mortal Enemy of Zhenwu Sacred Land!

"Who is it?"
The many Legendary Lords of Zhenwu Sacred Land were all puzzled.
The speaker was clearly not an expert of Zhenwu Sacred Land; otherwise, they wouldn't have used the address "your Zhenwu Sacred Land."
And this was the headquarters of Zhenwu Sacred Land; an outsider actually dared to break in.
*Sila!*
The River of Time manifested, and a phantom appeared.
This was surprisingly a Legendary Realm Lord using an incarnation to cross the River of Time and arrive at Zhenwu Sacred Land.
"Guixing, it's you. You are not welcome here," Longgu said coldly.
"Hahaha, I heard your Zhenwu Sacred Land is going to build a Bridge of Time, so I came to take a look. Can't an old friend come to visit?" Guixing laughed.
"It's him!"
Many Legendary Lords of Zhenwu Sacred Land recognized him.
Lord Guixing, a Legendary Realm Lord, an existence who controlled time.
Lord Guixing was the mortal enemy of Zhenwu Sacred Land, a Legendary Realm Lord of Guixing Tower in the Boundless Sea. Guixing Tower only had two Legendary Realm Lords, and its strength was far inferior to Zhenwu Sacred Land, but Guixing Tower had united with several Legendary-level forces to form an alliance.
Therefore, even Zhenwu Sacred Land did not dare to directly tear faces with Guixing Tower; otherwise, they might face the entire alliance behind Guixing Tower.
Many years ago, during the Great War of the Boundless Sea, several Legendary Realm Lords of Zhenwu Sacred Land had fallen. Those Legendary Realm Lords had their true bodies and clones all killed in battle, dying completely.
One of them had fallen at the hands of Lord Guixing and several other Legendary Realm Lords, which was why Lord Longgu loathed the Master of Guixing Tower.
"Lord Guixing, if you want to watch the excitement, come with your true body. You don't even dare to send a clone," Lord Longgu mocked.
"Hehe, I indeed don't dare. Your Zhenwu Sacred Land has many experts. If I came with my true body, I'm afraid I wouldn't be able to leave," the Master of Guixing Tower said with self-awareness.
"Scram!"
Lord Longgu snorted coldly.
The River of Time shook, and Lord Guixing's incarnation directly shattered.
Boundless Sea, Guixing Tower.
Lord Guixing saw his incarnation was destroyed, but he wasn't angry. It was just a mere incarnation, nothing significant.
"Is Zhenwu Sacred Land really going to complete the Bridge of Time? If it's truly built, perhaps another Legendary Realm Lord will emerge. This is unfavorable for our Guixing Tower!" the Master of Guixing Tower muttered.
But he had no way to stop it, nor could he convince the entire alliance to go to war with Zhenwu Sacred Land.
After all, in the last great war, the entire alliance also suffered heavy losses.
The main reason was that so many Legendary Realm Lords had fallen. Now, many Legendary Realm Lords were afraid and didn't dare to fight again, fearing they would be the next to die.
After all, in that war, no one expected it to be so tragic that even so many Legendary Realm Lords could die in battle.
"Maintaining the Bridge of Time also requires a lot of Eternal Time Gold. Perhaps not even a single Legendary Realm Lord will be born in Zhenwu Sacred Land," the Master of Guixing Tower comforted himself.
......
With the Master of Guixing Tower's incarnation destroyed, Lord Longgu no longer saw the person he loathed and continued building the Bridge of Time.
*Hua!*
The River of Time appeared. Lord Longgu took out a large amount of materials, the main material naturally being Eternal Time Gold.
A large amount of materials fused with the Eternal Time Gold, gradually transforming into a purple bridge under Lord Longgu's refinement.
This bridge was built within the River of Time, gradually stabilizing that area of the River of Time, while the other end was located at Zhenwu Sacred Land.
Next, Lord Longgu built the Sacred Temple of Time at the Bridge of Time to serve as the entrance.
After a long time, the Bridge of Time was finally completed.
After the Bridge of Time was completed, Lord Longgu announced, "The Bridge of Time is finished. Any Half-step Legendary Realm Lord can cultivate in the River of Time for one hundred million Eternal Years. Legendary Lords below the Half-step Legendary Realm have no need to enter."
Those Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords were very disappointed upon hearing this, but they also felt it was quite normal, because only Half-step Legendary Realm Lords could obtain many benefits in the River of Time, while Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords actually couldn't gain much.
Since the Bridge of Time needed to protect Half-step Legendary Realm Lords, the more Legendary Lords entered, the more Eternal Time Gold would be consumed.
Therefore, Zhenwu Sacred Land could not let all Legendary Lords in; it was limited to Half-step Legendary Realm Lords.
Soon, one Half-step Legendary Realm Lord after another passed through the Bridge of Time, entered the River of Time, and began their cultivation.
Ye Tian also wanted to enter the River of Time, but he was only a Domain Realm Legendary Lord. If he entered the River of Time, others in Zhenwu Sacred Land would surely be dissatisfied, so Lord Longgu could not let him in.
Ye Tian didn't mind. He had already gained enough benefits. By the time he reached the Half-step Legendary Realm, he would surely have controlled time long ago.
"Next is to cultivate more Eternal Daos and raise them to the Legendary-level Eternal Dao Tier."
Ye Tian thought to himself.
The Gray Mist Eternal Dao was very powerful, and it was somewhat difficult to step into the Legendary-level because it involved not only time but also space. Therefore, he had to have some achievements in controlling space before he could raise it to the Legendary-level.
Therefore, Ye Tian specifically looked for Eternal Daos that involved more time.
For example, the Traceless Eternal Dao, Evergreen Eternal Dao, Eternal Age Eternal Dao, Indestructible Eternal Dao...
These Eternal Daos were not weak, and Zhenwu Sacred Land had corresponding cultivation sites for Eternal Daos, and even specialized Eternal Treasures for Legendary Lords to contemplate and cultivate, though they could not take the Eternal Treasures away.
It could be said that Ye Tian did not lack places to contemplate Eternal Daos.
As time passed, Ye Tian comprehended more and more Eternal Daos, and his application of controlling time became proficient.
After several thousand Eternal Years, Ye Tian's Myriad Life Eternal Dao was the first to break through to the Legendary-level Eternal Dao rank, becoming his first Legendary-level Eternal Dao.
Afterward, other Eternal Daos also broke through to the Legendary-level one by one, and Ye Tian's strength grew increasingly powerful.
At this time, if Ye Tian faced that Purple Aurora Beast, which could be called Invincible in the Transcendence Realm, he would definitely not be without the power to resist. He might still be unable to defeat it, but he could at least contend for a few moves and then retreat.
Besides cultivating these Eternal Daos, Ye Tian had another goal: to raise his cultivation to the Extreme of the Domain Realm Legendary Lord as soon as possible, cultivate his Will Rule Domain to the Peak, and then impact the Will Rule Heaven and Earth.
Just like that, three million Eternal Years passed in a flash.
On this day, a major event occurred in Zhenwu Sacred Land.
Lord Wushuang, after cultivating with the help of the River of Time, had controlled space ahead of time.
Reasonably speaking, Lord Wushuang cultivated the control of space, not time, and could not control space within the River of Time. However, Lord Wushuang's purpose in entering the River of Time was not to contemplate time, but to use space to resist time, thereby tempering his control of space through the confrontation.
In this way, time could be shortened.
But this method could only be done by those infinitely close to controlling space; otherwise, it was seeking death.
However, after controlling space, impacting the Legendary Realm Lord was still a very dangerous thing, mainly due to external factors.
Therefore, Lord Longgu notified the other Legendary Realm Lords of Zhenwu Sacred Land to protect Lord Wushuang.
