Chapter 1702: Easy Crushing! Dark Mist Lord's Memories!

Buzz!!!
With the Hunyuan Token's blessing on his body, Ye Tian created his own Will Rule Domain. Every legendary-level Eternal Technique could exert terrifying effects.
At this moment, Ye Tian was comparable to a weaker Domain Realm Legendary Lord; his strength was far greater than Qin Yu's.
Boom!!!!
Qin Yu's physical body cracked under a single Eternal Extreme Dao Punch from Ye Tian. Meanwhile, Qin Yu's attacks landed on Ye Tian's Will Wall Light and were all intercepted. Only a small portion of power penetrated the Will Wall Light, but it was neutralized by the Void True Body.
Ultimately, none of Qin Yu's attacks could cause any damage to Ye Tian.
Conversely, every one of Ye Tian's strikes was enough to heavily injure Qin Yu. Thus, the outcome of the battle was already decided.
"Hurry up and attack together!"
Qin Yu grew anxious and immediately ordered.
Swish, swish, swish!!!
The group of Legendary Realm Lords under Qin Yu's command no longer remained silent and attacked one after another to help Qin Yu. The Dark Mist Lord also gritted his teeth and went up to help.
Things had already developed to this point; he could no longer reconcile with Ye Tian. He feared that even the other Legendary Realm Lords would not be polite to him.
Instead of that, it was better to choose to deal with Ye Tian directly. If they could truly turn the situation around, there might still be a slim chance of survival.
With so many Legendary Realm Lords, although their individual strength wasn't great, they would be a huge help if they coordinated with Qin Yu.
But at the moment they were about to act, Ye Tian's copy bodies struck. The other Legendary Realm Lords of the Spacetime Territory did not stand idly by either, choosing to step forward to block these Legendary Realm Lords from the Boundless Sea.
The Dark Mist Lord was also targeted by one of Ye Tian's copy bodies. Although this copy body did not have an Eternal Treasure, its combat power was comparable to a Perfected Realm Legendary Lord. The Dark Mist Lord was merely an ordinary Legendary Lord; how could he be Ye Tian's match?
In the past, the Dark Mist Lord might have had the qualifications to fight Ye Tian for a bit, but now he had no chance at all.
"Quick, come help me!"
The Dark Mist Lord panicked, pleading with the other Legendary Realm Lords to help him resist Ye Tian's copy body. But everyone else had their own opponents; who would help him?
Swish!
The Dark Mist Lord used Dark Mist Shadow Body, transforming into multiple shadow bodies.
At that moment, Ye Tian directly unleashed a legendary-level Eternal Technique—Billion-Tier Void!
Buzz!
Countless voids compressed into a single layer, instantly causing the Dark Mist Lord's shadow bodies to collapse. Just as the Dark Mist Lord was about to perish, Ye Tian stopped and merely sealed him.
For now, the Dark Mist Lord still had value, so he could not be killed.
Next.
Ye Tian's several copy bodies attacked, blasting and killing the Legendary Realm Lords one by one. In a short while, only Qin Yu remained; all his subordinates had been killed.
"Endless Lord, this is just my avatar. How about you let me go?"
Qin Yu conceded.
"No," Ye Tian refused.
"Endless Lord, I am a member of the Qin Family from the Boundless Sea. Do you really want to become my enemy completely?" Qin Yu roared in fury.
"An Eternal Treasure is worth becoming your enemy!"
Ye Tian said with a smile.
Qin Yu could not refute. The value of an Eternal Treasure was indeed high. Had he not thought the Source Sea was too weak and that he wouldn't perish, he wouldn't have let this avatar bring the Eternal Treasure, the Fire Domain Sword.
The value of an Eternal Treasure was enough to make two Legendary Realm Lords turn against each other. For an Eternal Treasure, Ye Tian would indeed kill him.
If it were him, he would definitely do the same.
As for threats? Heh, if threats were useful, there wouldn't be so much killing in the Boundless Sea or anywhere else.
Swish, swish, swish!!!!
Ye Tian's several copy bodies joined forces to attack Qin Yu, completely stripping him of his last hope of escape.
Soon, Qin Yu was on the brink of death. At this time, Ye Tian did not choose to kill him but chose to seal him instead.
This was to seize the Eternal Treasure, the Fire Domain Sword, within his body.
If he killed Qin Yu, the Fire Domain Sword would form a spacetime barrier and exist in an unreachable spacetime. At that point, Ye Tian would need to comprehend the Eternal Dao within the Fire Domain Sword and cultivate it to the Entry stage before he could touch the sword.
But if he forcibly stripped the Fire Domain Sword while Qin Yu was still alive and then forcibly refined it, the sword would be unable to escape into spacetime. Then, even after killing Qin Yu, he could still reach the Fire Domain Sword. This was the true method to take the Fire Domain Sword.
Previously, Ye Tian hadn't thought of this method, and since that place was a prison, there were many inconveniences.
But now, Ye Tian didn't need to worry about that.
Swish!
Ye Tian's copy body took the sealed Qin Yu and Dark Mist Lord and left, heading toward the Blood Bone Territory.
As for the corpses of the other Legendary Realm Lords, Ye Tian gave one to each of the five Legendary Realm Lords. The treasures on them were also left to the five Legendary Lords. This had been agreed upon beforehand; otherwise, the five Legendary Lords would not have acted so decisively.
The entire net worth of a Legendary Realm Lord from the Boundless Sea—the five Legendary Lords were very curious and tempted by how many treasures it contained. There might even be treasures or inheritances that could help them break through.
Immediately, the five Legendary Lords went back one by one to check their gains. As for the consequences and impact of this matter, they could discuss it slowly in the future; there was no rush for now.
-
One of Ye Tian's copy bodies handled Qin Yu's matter, while another handled the Dark Mist Lord's matter.
He wanted to obtain some information from the Dark Mist Lord, such as information about the Boundless Sea, how to get there, and so on.
Getting the Dark Mist Lord to speak voluntarily was almost impossible.
The Dark Mist Lord likely guessed his own end, so he wouldn't tell the truth. Even if he did speak, it would likely be a deceptive trap.
Therefore, to obtain real information, he could only forcibly dismantle the Dark Mist Lord's will to obtain his memories.
If they were at the same tier, it would be impossible. But Ye Tian was much stronger than the Dark Mist Lord, so there was a certain hope of obtaining his memories.
To ensure success, Ye Tian first copied the Dark Mist Lord's body and used this copy body for experiments. After a long period of experimentation, Ye Tian had enough confidence to seize the Dark Mist Lord's memories, and only then did he begin.
Buzz!!!
Ye Tian's extremely powerful will power penetrated the Dark Mist Lord's will. Because Ye Tian was so familiar with the Dark Mist Lord's will, he easily completed the memory seizure, though it still caused the Dark Mist Lord's will to collapse.
But since the memories were already in hand, Ye Tian didn't care.
Immediately, he obliterated the Dark Mist Lord.
