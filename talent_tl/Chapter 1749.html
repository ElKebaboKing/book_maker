Chapter 1748: Lord Hanshan's Strike

"You found the Void Sea Gravel?"
Lord Wushuang asked in surprise.
"Yes!" Ye Tian produced the Eternal Supreme Treasure from Lord Star-Crushing and handed it to Lord Wushuang. "A grain of Void Sea Gravel is within this Eternal Supreme Treasure, but as I am not a Legendary Realm Lord, I cannot extract it. Lord Wushuang, you are a Legendary Realm Lord and happen to master Space, so you should be certain of being able to extract it. Thus, I have come to request your assistance."
"Extracting the Void Sea Gravel is not that difficult."
Lord Wushuang began to probe the Eternal Supreme Treasure with his will, quickly locking onto the location of the Void Sea Gravel.
Buzz!!!
The Eternal Supreme Treasure vibrated, but soon, a grain of Void Sea Gravel was stripped away under Lord Wushuang's spatial control.
Whoosh!
The Void Sea Gravel flew out from the tip of the Eternal Supreme Treasure's blade and landed in Lord Wushuang's hand, who then handed it to Ye Tian.
"Thank you, Lord Wushuang," Ye Tian expressed his gratitude.
Now, he possessed the Void Sea Gravel and the Time River Pearl. Only the Space-Time Ancient Leaf remained. Once he had all three, he could condense the Supreme Legendary Body.
Fortunately, there was news of the Space-Time Ancient Leaf being in the hands of the White-Bearded Old Man, though Ye Tian was truly uncertain if he could obtain it. Even so, he had to try.
Ye Tian then departed from Lord Wushuang's residence.
...
"The White-Bearded Old Man."
Ye Tian had learned specific details about him from Lord Wushuang, though Lord Wushuang did not know much either, having only summarized information gathered from various Legendary Realm Lords.
This was of little help; if he wanted the Space-Time Ancient Leaf, he had to find the White-Bearded Old Man himself. However, many Legendary Realm Lords had likely visited or sought him out, yet none were ever heard to have obtained the leaf.
"I must at least try," Ye Tian murmured.
On this day, Ye Tian prepared to visit the White-Bearded Old Man. According to the information, he was at Xuyang Mountain by the Yongyang River.
Ye Tian soon arrived at the Yongyang River.
The Yongyang River was part of the Eternal Sea, an ancient part at that, and the White-Bearded Old Man was the only Peak Legendary Realm Lord there.
In the Void Area, Peak Legendary Realm Lords were extremely rare. Mastering Space after mastering Time, or vice versa, increased the difficulty by a hundred or a thousandfold.
Because of this, Peak Legendary Realm Lords who mastered both were scarce.
Normally, it was quite good if an Eternal Sea could produce one Peak Legendary Realm Lord. Occasionally, a powerful Eternal Sea might have two, but most had none.
For instance, the Source Sea, being a nascent Eternal Sea, had few Legendary Lords, let alone Legendary Realm Lords.
He reached Xuyang Mountain.
This was the number one divine mountain of the Yongyang Sea and the domain of the White-Bearded Old Man.
The White-Bearded Old Man kept a low profile, rarely appearing except to interact with old friends.
As a Legendary Lord, it would be very difficult for Ye Tian to see him; not just anyone could.
At the foot of Xuyang Mountain, Ye Tian was blocked by a Legendary Realm Lord.
Indeed, it was a Legendary Realm Lord who was once a servant of the White-Bearded Old Man and later reached the Legendary Realm, yet continued to guard Xuyang Mountain.
"Halt."
A burly man appeared out of thin air, staring at Ye Tian and exerting immense pressure.
"Greetings, Lord Hanshan!"
Ye Tian said politely.
"Xuyang Mountain is not to be entered casually," Lord Hanshan stated.
Ye Tian said, "Lord Hanshan, I wish to pay a visit to the White-Bearded Old Man."
Buzz!
Lord Hanshan's Legendary Realm aura surged downward, reversing time and space, making Ye Tian feel the wrath of a Legendary Realm Lord.
"You, a Half-Step Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord, wish to visit my master? Even ordinary Legendary Realm Lords are not qualified," Lord Hanshan said with ill intent.
"Please, Lord Hanshan, give me a chance."
Ye Tian said.
"A chance?" Lord Hanshan sneered. "If I gave everyone a chance, my master would be annoyed to death. If you want a chance, fine. If you can withstand one strike from me without dying, I will give you that chance."
"Fine!"
Ye Tian agreed.
"Hahaha, then this avatar of yours is about to perish."
Lord Hanshan pointed a finger.
He truly attacked!
Boom!!!
A strike comparable to the full power of a Half-Step Legendary Realm Lord came at him. Though only equivalent to an ordinary Half-Step Legendary Realm Lord's full power, very few Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords could withstand it.
Since Ye Tian was only a Half-Step Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord, Lord Hanshan believed he would be crushed.
Ye Tian did not use his time-control powers because he could endure it.
He manifested the power of several Legendary-grade Eternal Daos and instantly executed multiple Top-tier Legendary Eternal Techniques.
His Sixth-Tier Eternal Will, combined with his incredibly powerful Will Rule World, was fully deployed for defense.
Lord Hanshan's finger shattered many of Ye Tian's defenses but was ultimately blocked, leaving Ye Tian with only minor injuries.
"Lord Hanshan, does this count as withstanding it?"
Ye Tian asked.
"You are quite good." Lord Hanshan was surprised. A Half-Step Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord could actually withstand a full-power strike at the Half-Step Legendary Realm level. Even if it was just one strike, it was an extraordinary feat.
Furthermore, at his level, he had comprehended many Legendary-grade Eternal Daos and created so many Top-tier Legendary Eternal Techniques, which was incredibly impressive.
"Fine, you have passed my test. I can give you a chance, but only a chance. Whether you can see my master depends on whether you can pass the trial he set."
Lord Hanshan began explaining the White-Bearded Old Man's trial.
"My master prefers peace and quiet, so he established a trial. However, this trial is intended for Half-Step Legendary Realm Lords and some monstrous Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords. As for a Half-Step Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord, you are the first to attempt it. Come with me; I will take you to the trial grounds."
Lord Hanshan said.
"Thank you, Lord Hanshan," Ye Tian said gratefully.
He then followed Lord Hanshan toward the trial grounds set by the White-Bearded Old Man.
