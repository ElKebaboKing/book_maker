Chapter 1726: Found the Time Constant Gold!

"We should be close to the Purple Aurora Beast's lair!"
Lord Wushuang said.
At this time, Lord Yinmeng grabbed that Purple Aurora Beast and peered into its memory fragments again.
"It is indeed nearby. Just advance a bit further in that direction and we'll be there!"
Lord Yinmeng said.
"Let's set off!"
Lord Wushuang said.
The group traveled through space, and after a short while, they saw a purple continent—an incredibly massive continent.
This was the lair of the Purple Aurora Beasts.
"Be careful, I sense the aura of the Half-step Legendary Realm! The Purple Aurora Beast race possesses Half-step Legendary Lords."
Lord Wushuang said solemnly.
"I sense it too!" Lord Wumeng and Lord Yinmeng nodded.
They weren't surprised at all that the Purple Aurora Beast race had Half-step Legendary beings.
After all, even a branch had a powerhouse who could be called Invincible among Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords; it was only natural for the headquarters to have Half-step Legendary beings.
This was also why Lord Wushuang and the others had brought eight Half-step Legendary Lords. If only one had come, they might not have been able to do anything to the Purple Aurora Beast race.
Lord Wushuang and the others sensed the Half-step Legendary beings of the Purple Aurora Beast race, and the Half-step Legendary beings of the Purple Aurora Beast race naturally sensed Lord Wushuang and the others as well.
Buzz!!!
Within the Purple Aurora Domain, a will projection emerged. It was the will projection of one of the Half-step Legendary Lords of the Purple Aurora Beast race.
"Get out!"
There were no extra words, only a direct expulsion.
"Let's just start. The Purple Aurora Beast race won't discuss or negotiate with us,"
Lord Wushuang said.
Whoosh whoosh whoosh!!!
The eight Half-step Legendary Lords flew directly toward the purple continent. Before they could even fly inside, three Half-step Legendary Lords of the Purple Aurora Beast race flew out.
The great battle began!
Ye Tian could not participate in such a battle, but he had another task: to copy these Time Talents.
"Copy!"
"Copy!"
"Copy!"
Ye Tian continuously copied the Time Talents of these Purple Aurora Beasts, even entering the purple continent to perform the copying.
There were indeed quite a few Purple Aurora Beasts here, enough for him to copy.
Soon.
Purple Aurora Beasts came to hunt him down, and they were Transcendence Realm Purple Aurora Beasts, no weaker than the Transcendence Realm Purple Aurora Beast from before.
Just then.
A beam of light directly shattered this Transcendence Realm Purple Aurora Beast, killing it instantly.
"Thank you, Lord Wushuang!"
Ye Tian knew Lord Wushuang had acted; otherwise, he really wouldn't have been able to contend with this Transcendence Realm Purple Aurora Beast.
"Endless Lord, there are still many Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord-level Purple Aurora Beasts on this purple continent. Do not go too deep," Lord Wushuang reminded.
Ye Tian nodded and stopped entering. He would wait for Lord Wushuang and the others to finish the battle before going deep into the purple continent to slowly copy Time Talents.
Immediately, he focused his attention on observing the battle of Lord Wushuang and the others.
The battle was almost one-sided. Those three Half-step Legendary Purple Aurora Beasts were no match for the eight Half-step Legendary Lords like Lord Wushuang. They were completely overwhelmed, and soon these three Half-step Legendary Purple Aurora Beasts were heavily injured.
Suddenly.
One Half-step Legendary Lord-level Purple Aurora Beast was frozen into a block of ice by Lord Wushuang. This block of ice quickly shattered, and along with it, the body of this Purple Aurora Beast also shattered into countless fragments.
However, this Purple Aurora Beast had not died yet. Those countless fragments were frozen again by Lord Wushuang, turning into countless ice particles.
"Shatter!"
"Freeze!"
"Shatter!"
"Freeze!"
After repeating this dozens of times, this Purple Aurora Beast finally truly perished.
It is incredibly difficult to decide life and death between Half-step Legendary beings, yet Lord Wushuang could slay a Half-step Legendary Purple Aurora Beast, showing just how powerful her strength was.
Before long, the other two Half-step Legendary Purple Aurora Beasts also perished one by one.
If this were in the Boundless Sea, it would be extremely difficult to kill a Half-step Legendary being because many Half-step Legendary Lords have backers. Once in a desperate situation, a Legendary Realm Lord could descend with a single thought. They were too hard to kill.
But these Purple Aurora Beasts had no backers, only themselves.
If the Purple Aurora Beasts had birthed a Legendary Realm Lord, they would be enough to become a powerful race in the Void Region. Otherwise, once they encountered today's situation, they could only become lambs to the slaughter.
"Let's enter!"
Lord Wushuang said.
Ye Tian followed the eight Half-step Legendary Lords into the purple continent and began a new round of his copying journey.
The goal of the eight Half-step Legendary Lords was simple: to find Time Constant Gold.
Ye Tian did not go looking for Time Constant Gold. He was only a Domain Realm Legendary Lord and did not need Time Constant Gold for now. Moreover, even if he did find Time Constant Gold, he wouldn't dare compete with the Half-step Legendary Lords for it!
Of course, if the Half-step Legendary Lords found enough Time Constant Gold and established a Time Bridge in the True Void Sacred Ground, he would also have hope of walking upon the Time Bridge in the future.
Ye Tian's speed was very fast. After all, he could copy those Time Talents in an instant. Thus, in a short while, he had copied all the Time Talents on this purple continent.
"Found it!"
Lord Wushuang and the others finally found the Time Constant Gold at this time.
Upon receiving the news, Ye Tian immediately traveled over.
--------------------
In an underground location, the ground had been torn open, revealing a massive underground rift.
In this underground rift, streaks of purple light seeped out. These purple lights contained the power of time.
The eight Half-step Legendary Lords did not enter immediately. A Time Constant Gold vein was no ordinary place. Before knowing the output of this vein, they could not enter casually.
Because this place contained extremely powerful time energy, which could even corrode their bodies.
The time dimensions where some Time Constant Gold veins were located were unstable; one could accidentally fall into the River of Time.
In the Gray Mist Stone Statue, the River of Time was intercepted, allowing one to enter and exit.
But if one accidentally fell into the River of Time on their own, they wouldn't be able to come out.
If one could come out casually, wouldn't it be better to ask a Legendary Realm Lord to throw them into the River of Time?
In that case, the Time Bridge wouldn't be so precious.
The Time Bridge could not only send them into the River of Time but also make that section of the River of Time slightly more stable and safe, allowing for a free exit. This was where the value of the Time Bridge lay.
They did not want to lose an avatar due to carelessness.
At their level, every avatar was highly valuable and could not be easily lost; otherwise, it would take a long time to condense one again.
