Chapter 1706: Braving the Tower! Everyone is Shocked!

In front of the True Void Sacred Tower, a huge projection displayed the results of each challenger. Ye Tian saw sixteen Eternal Realm Lords displayed on the projection, and the cultivation of each Eternal Realm Lord was shown.
Currently, the best result was from a Legendary Eternal Realm powerhouse named Silver Moon Lord, who had already reached the Seventh-Tier.
Ye Tian watched for a while, and suddenly Silver Moon Lord reached the Eighth-Tier.
This immediately and completely shocked the many Eternal Realm Lords watching outside.
"Silver Moon Lord actually reached the Eighth-Tier. If she clears the Eighth-Tier and the Ninth-Tier, even if she cannot clear the Tenth-Tier, she can join the True Void Sacred Land. Moreover, joining the True Void Sacred Land by braving the True Void Sacred Tower means she won't be an ordinary disciple; it will be at least a very high position."
"It might be difficult for Silver Moon Lord to clear the Ninth-Tier. I have heard of Silver Moon Lord; it is said she is the daughter of a certain Transcendence Realm Lord. Her strength is decent, but she doesn't have any outstanding battle records."
"Perhaps she can clear the Ninth-Tier. After all, no matter how powerful someone is, they won't truly reveal everything. However, Silver Moon Lord's father is a Transcendence Realm Lord; she has no real need to join the True Void Sacred Land, right?"
"A Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord is nothing to the True Void Sacred Land. The True Void Sacred Land has Legendary Realm Lords. Joining the True Void Sacred Land gives you a backer! Never mind having a Transcendence Realm Legendary Lord father, even with a Half-Step Legendary Realm Lord father, one could choose to join the True Void Sacred Land to have an even more powerful backer!"
"True. To us, Transcendence Realm Legendary Lords are unimaginable existences, but to the True Void Sacred Land, they really aren't much."
……
"Time to brave the True Void Sacred Tower!"
Ye Tian thought to himself.
Whoosh!
He entered the True Void Sacred Tower. The True Void Sacred Tower directly detected his basic information, such as coming from another Eternal Sea and being named Endless Lord. This could all be deduced from the Innate Eternal Rules unless Ye Tian deliberately concealed it.
Soon, the name Endless Lord appeared on the projection screen outside. Regarding Endless Lord, the Eternal Realm Lords outside did not pay much attention. After all, Ye Tian did not have any results yet, so there was nothing worth noting.
In the First-Tier of the True Void Sacred Tower, Ye Tian encountered three Initial Realm Legendary Lords. Their cultivation was the same as Ye Tian's, at the seventh step of the Initial Realm.
Ye Tian only needed to defeat them to clear the First-Tier.
And the strength of these three Legendary Lords was naturally very, very weak. Basically, any Initial Realm Legendary Lord could easily defeat them.
As expected.
Ye Tian instantly killed them with one move and cleared the First-Tier of the True Void Sacred Tower.
Subsequently, Ye Tian cleared the Second-Tier, Third-Tier, and Fourth-Tier, gradually attracting the attention of some Eternal Realm Lords outside. In the Seventh-Tier of the True Void Sacred Tower, Ye Tian entered and quickly defeated the three Initial Realm Legendary Lords there.
In reality, the Initial Realm Legendary Lords of the Seventh-Tier were already very powerful, surpassing the normal level.
At this moment, Ye Tian cleared the Seventh-Tier, and his fame suddenly soared.
Even if Ye Tian did not clear the Ninth-Tier, his name would definitely spread.
At this time, the Eternal Realm Lords outside were all discussing Ye Tian.
"Who is this Endless Lord??"
"Didn't it mention above? A Legendary Lord not from the Boundless Sea. He should be a Legendary Lord from another Eternal Sea. As for which Eternal Sea, the True Void Sacred Tower has no way to detect it."
"We can't even recognize all the Legendary Lords of the Boundless Sea. We are naturally even less clear about Legendary Lords from other Eternal Seas."
"However, from today on, the name of this Legendary Lord called Endless Lord will spread throughout the Boundless Sea."
"This Endless Lord's speed in braving the tower is very fast. Perhaps there is still hope for him to clear the Ninth-Tier and join the True Void Sacred Tower!"
……
Soon, Ye Tian cleared the Eighth-Tier again, and before long, he cleared the Ninth-Tier.
This time, the Eternal Realm Lords outside were all shocked.
Endless Lord really cleared the Ninth-Tier and was about to become a member of the True Void Sacred Land.
However, Ye Tian did not leave the True Void Sacred Tower. Although he was not clear about what benefits there would be for continuing to clear the Tenth-Tier and Eleventh-Tier, the higher the Tier he cleared, the higher his status in the True Void Sacred Land would inevitably be. Only then would he be qualified to have the True Void Sacred Land protect the Source Sea.
If he were just an ordinary Sacred Land disciple, he might not necessarily be able to make the Legendary Lords of the Qin Family wary.
The Tenth-Tier still did not stop Ye Tian; he cleared it very simply as well.
When Ye Tian reached the Eleventh-Tier and was fighting the three Initial Realm Legendary Lords there, Silver Moon Lord cleared the Eighth-Tier and was charging toward the Ninth-Tier.
After a while, Ye Tian reached the Twelfth-Tier, while Silver Moon Lord was still at the Ninth-Tier.
At this time, more Eternal Realm Lords were focusing on Silver Moon Lord. After all, Ye Tian was certain to join the True Void Sacred Land, so there was no sense of anticipation. As for how many Tiers he could clear, they were simply shocked beyond words and didn't know what to say.
Time slowly passed—
"Boom!"
The Ninth-Tier was displayed after Silver Moon Lord's name, representing that she had finally cleared the Ninth-Tier.
Not long after, Silver Moon Lord emerged from the True Void Sacred Tower. Clearly, she had failed at the Tenth-Tier and could not continue, but she had still passed the entry assessment for the True Void Sacred Land.
When Silver Moon Lord came out of the True Void Sacred Tower, the Eternal Realm Lords congratulated her one by one: "Congratulations to Silver Moon Lord for successfully joining the True Void Sacred Land!"
Silver Moon Lord was also in a very good mood and said: "Thank you all for your congratulations!"
Suddenly, she noticed that many Eternal Realm Lords were still staring at the projection screen, so she also looked over curiously.
With one look, she was completely stunned.
Endless Lord, Twelfth-Tier!
This represented that this Legendary Lord named Endless Lord had already cleared the Twelfth-Tier.
While she had only cleared the Ninth-Tier, the joy in her heart was suddenly halved, and she was deeply stung.
"Who is this Endless Lord?"
Silver Moon Lord asked curiously.
At this time, an Eternal Realm Lord said: "Silver Moon Lord, we are also not clear who this Endless Lord is. He entered the True Void Sacred Tower after you. No one knows him, and no one knows which Eternal Sea he comes from."
Seeing that the other Eternal Realm Lords did not know him either, Silver Moon Lord stopped asking and instead watched silently.
She was very curious about which Tier of the True Void Sacred Tower Ye Tian could clear. Although the strength gap between opponents in each subsequent Tier was not wide, after the Twelfth-Tier, the difficulty of clearing each Tier became immense.
After another period of time, Ye Tian cleared the Fifteenth-Tier.
At this time, Ye Tian also felt some pressure.
